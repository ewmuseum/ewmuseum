NPWA Etc [1/1] (Aug.26/97)

26 August 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The studio lights rise and are greeted by the beautiful Lindsay Jackson and the dapper Bruce Lord. Each are dressed in a black "Etc.." sweat- shirt and black jeans.) LJ: Greetings, and welcome back to NPWA "Etc...". I am Lindsay Jackson and joining me, as always, on "Etc..." is Bruce Lord. Well... long time no see, NPWA! BL: Yes, indeed. Folks..as you can see, the "Etc.." studios have *just* been revamped and are now ready to go as "Etc..." now takes a new direction to keep up with the NPWA. LJ: Right. From now on, "Etc..." will be a segment of "Monday Night Mayhem", and we will still bring you the ever-popular "Challenge Matches" and, when circumstances allow, "The Round Table". BL: Unfortunately, the paint was still wet last night and we were unable to get in here in time for the show...*but*.....we are here now and we have a very interesting "Challenge Match" to bring you....one that indirectly involves our lovely and talented Lindsay Jackson. LJ: (Surprised) What? Me? BL: C'mon...you know...Dave Diamond.... LJ: Oh brother. Spare me...I have *nothing* to do with Mr. Diamond at this time. BL: Yeah...we will see about that later. Anyway, let's take another look at what *Mr. Diamond* had to say a couple of weeks ago. (Screen fades to black and then emerges with....) (Video plays of Dave Diamond sitting in the back of a limo with three beautiful women - a blonde, a brunette and a redhead] DD: So, let me get this straight, I can challenge anyone I want for this Face Off thing, right? Girls: That's right, Dave! DD: Alright, I'm issuing a challenge right now... and it ain't who you think it is... nah, I've beaten you enough for now Powell, I've got other fish to fry at the moment... Girls: Ooooh, who is it Dave? DD: I'm glad you asked... this guy struts into the NPWA a few weeks ago, and I thought I was looking in a mirror! The robes, the strut and even the hip gyration... yeah, I'm talking about you "Midnight" Michael Dane, you steroid-induced, Dave Diamond wannabe!!! You ain't me and you could never *be* me, no matter how hard you try! And big deal, you have Alexis Gold in your corner... hey pal, I've nailed her already and she wasn't that great. Blonde: I heard she dyes her hair! Brunette: I heard she stuffs her bra! Redhead: I heard she's over 40! Girls: Ewwwwwwwwwww!!!! DD: Dane, you march in here, thinking you're gonna be the next "Hollywood Heartthrob?" Forget chump, I ain't ready to give up the title. Strut your wannabe ass down to the ring and I'll show you how it's done, junior. It's like I said to the high school students when I starred in 'Dead Poets Society' - "Carpe diem - seize the day." And I'm going to do that if you have the balls to step into the ring with the Heartthrob - or has Alexis taken those from you already? WHHHOOOOOOOOOOO!!!! (Fade to black....) (....and a rise back to a grinning Bruce and a stern looking Lindsay Jackson.) BL: (Looking at Lindsay with that same sly grin.) Ummmm...LJ? What do you think of the ......errrr...*challenge* by Dave? LJ: (Not looking at Bruce) Again, I wish Mr. Diamond the best of luck in all of his matches in the NPWA. BL: (Sarcastically and still grinning) Nope...no trouble in paradise here..*at all*. (Back to camera and a bit more serious) Well..earlier tonite, I finally caught up to Michael and Alexis...here is what they had to say. (Again, the screen fades and illuminates the following.) BL: Standing by is the man that will take on Dave Diamond later on in the show, "Midnight" Michael Dane and his manager Alexis Gold. MD: Lordy lordy if it isn't the Lord-man! Hey Bruce, you're lookin' good tonight. Of course not as good as yours truly but hey, how can you top the best. Speaking of people that don't look as good as me, Davey Boy Diamond, get ready boy, this is gonna be the wrestling lesson of your life. There's gonna be no style and profile for you. After the match it's gonna be all denial. AG: Face it, the only one that is going to sparkle in that ring tonight is my man, Michael Dane. "The so-called Hollywood Heartthrob" is nothing but a fake. More like Cubic Zirconia than Diamond to be exact. MD: Davey, you better drink up because "The Last Call" is coming soon, and if that don't get you, just imagine how you'll feel "The Morning After". AG: It's going to be a party. All.... night.... long!! MD: Now don't be cutting us off there Bruce, we've got one more thing to talk about. Last Monday I came out and told the whole world what I thought about "The Franchise" and yet there's no word from him at all. Now I admit that I'm one imposing man but come on. This is the man that has beaten almost everyone in the entire NPWA and he's running scared. Stone you ain't the man, cause I got the plan. The outcome is clear. It's all about fear. Afraid of doin the dance with the Midnight Man. AG: That's because he's never faced you and never wants to. MD: The gauntlet has been thrown down, but watch out when you pick it us Franchise, it may hit you harder than you think. AG: Just one last thing, Lindsay. Girl, you don't have a hope in hell of living up to the Gold Standard. Just keep that tail between your legs and try not to cry too much. MD: You may now return to your Midnightless life. (The camera fades and reappears to a now visible upset Lindsay and a full-grinned Bruce.) LJ: (Angrily) *Wait* just a minute. Who the hell does she think she is? I have two things to say to that little tramp.....*One* is--- BL: (Not smiling anymore and interrupting) --ummm..LJ? I don't think-- LJ: (Gibing Bruce a *very* sharp look) --I don't think it would be wise for you to say *one* more word...dig it? (Bruce nods....) LJ: (To camera) Listen, Alexis. I have no idea how many people you boinked to get to where you are at....well....as a matter of fact I do--- *everybody*. But that is besides the fact. I don't think you want to be messin' with me. I don't think you realize who my bro--- BL: (Interrupting and nervously laughing) ---OKOKOK...enough's enough. You two ladies can settle this later. I think we have a match to get on with. Let's head down to Johnny B. Nastee and Ed Wailen for the call of this match. (As the camera fades, Lindsay is seen chastising Bruce for interrupting her.) (Camera switches to Johnny B. Nastee and Ed Wailen ringside, headsets on and they appear ready to go.) EW: Well Johnny, both men have made it to the ring and are warming up for this special "Challenge Match" that was set forth by the "Hollywood Heartthrob" Dave Diamond. JBN: This'll be a battle to see who is the sexiest guy alive, Puke, but they are battling for *second* place because *everybody* knows that the Nastee Man is THE best looking thing on this God-given land! EW: Oh brother...excuse my colleague, folks, as he is obviousl--- JBN: (Interrupting) Diamond attacks Dane with a double axe-handle to the back of the neck! EW: Wow. That sent Dane falling out of the ring as well as Alexis Gold and she landed as if she was doing the splits! JBN: That girl is used to having her legs spread-- EW: (Interrupting) --Hey now....Be nice. Back to the action as Michael Dane and Alexis Gold did take a tumble out of the ring and Dave Diamond seems quite pleased with himself as he---- DD: WWWWWWWWWWWWWWOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!! EW: Umm..as I was saying Diamond propped himself up on the corner post, swung his hips around and gave that tribal yell he adores so very much. Gold is back to her feet and is over caring for her love machine named Michael Dane. JBN: I'm sure she would rather be massaging something else besides his ncek right now, Puke. EW: Knowing Alexis, I couldn't disagree with you there. Anyway, Dane is back to his feet as Jamal Patterson has started the ten count. Diamond is still on the post and just taunting Dane and Gold with verbal insults as well as continuous gyrations in their direction. JBN: With the way Diamond is moving, it wouldn't surprise me if Gold gets all hot and steamy and tries to clamp on to the hips of Diamond! EW: (Laughing) Well, if that were to happen, I think Michael Dane would have something to say about it. JBN: Yeah....**THREESOME**!! Hehehe. EW: ...And with no complaints from Ms. Gold, I am sure. Back to the action as Diamond is allowing Dane to get back in the ring. Dane enters through the --NO! DIAMOND WITH ANOTHER AXE-HANDLE! That sent Dane flying out of the ring again. JBN: Diamond follows him, Puke. EW: Yes he does, as Diamond slides under the rope and with a big ol' grin on his face, he approaches the downed Michael Dane. JBN: Here comes Gold begging for some of Diamonds gyrations! EW: Alexis Gold has,if fact, has put herself in between Dave Diamond and Michael Dane in order to protect her prize pupil. JBN: She wants it from the rear and the front, Puke! EW: Enough with the smut, Johnny. Diamond is waving Gold off as he does not want Alexis to get harmed during this match. JBN: That's strange, I heard that Diamond likes it rough. EW: Last warning, Johnny. To the action as Diamond as now gently nudged the protesting Gold out of the way to get at her still downed man. Jamal Patterson has begun the "ten" count and has reached "three"--Wow! Diamond rams the head of Michael Dane into the steel barrier. It looks like Diamond is trying to disfigure Dane. JBN: Dave Diamond wants to be *the* hottest man in the NPWA. EW: I guess so. AGAIN! Another shot to the barrier and Michael Dane falls limply down to the cement as Alexis Gold stand in the backround just screaming obsenities at Dave Diamond. DD: WWWWWWWWOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!!! EW: I guess Diamond has responded to Gold's yelling with some yelling of his own. The crowd is giving it to Dave Diamond as he just laughs it off and scoops up the Michael Dane and brings him back to the ring. JBN: Little Michael is busted open. EW: Yes he is and he leaves a trail of blood behind him as he is rolled to the middle of the ring by Diamond. Again, The Heartthrob scoops Midnight to his feet...irish whip by Diamond...BIG KNEE TO THE MIDSECTION OF DANE! JBN: Well, Mikey's tummy is hurting him just as much now as when he eats Gold's cooking. Hehehe. EW: Why, have you ever eaten Gold's cooking" JBN: Have I ever eaten Gold? EW: What? JBN: Yes. EW: "Yes", what? JBN: "Yes", to your question. EW: You have eaten Alex's cooking? JBN: Oh...I thought you asked me something else, never mind, Puke. EW: (Not getting it) Whatever. Back to the ring and Diamond again picks the Midnightguy up..this time by the hair and another Irish whip....Dane ducks under the clothesline. And he ducks under it again.....back comes Dane....Dane slides under the legs of Diamond. RIGHT CROSS BY DANE STUNS DIAMOND! JAB! JAB! JAB! Diamond is stunned. JBN:: So what else is new? EW: (Ignoring him) Dane...*flings* Diamond into the corner and The New Wavian hits it hard and slumps down to the ground as Dane now moves in on the attack as blood is still oozing from the forehead of the man called Midnight. JBN: God...I love when Gold gets all happy and bouncey. EW: Me too. Dane has Diamond by the hair. DANE SLAPS THE FACE OF DAVE DIAMOND..and now seems to be lecturing the blonde haired man from where ever the heck he is from and whips Diamond across the ring INTO THE OTHER CORNER! Wow. Again, Diamond hits hard and arches his back after that impact and falls flat on his face as the momentum has swung to Michael Dane. JBN: Diamond needs to do something here or he is a goner, Puke. EW: Right again, as Dane approaches the pained Diamond and scoops him up and THROWS HIM THROUGH THE ROPES AND OUT OF THE RING! JBN: GOD! That sure mad Alexis happy! I may need to change my vote to Dane for this one, Puke,only because I just love to see Alexis jump up and down. EW: A fine way to predict a winner and Dave Diamond hit the mat hard as Jamal Patterson has come over to Dane and is now giving Midnight some lip. WAIT! WHAT THE---? JBN: POWELL!!! EW: Dante Powell has sprinted from the back and is making his way down the aisle as Patterson is still explaining the correct tossing form to Michael Dane. Powell has the dazed Diamond to his feet outside the ring! JBN: **RIGHT** into the post! That guy is gonna --*AGAIN*! Powell did it--AGAIN! THREE TIMES TO THE POST! EW: Wow..and the Heartthrob is a bloody mess as Dante Powell...Jesus.. AGAIN! FOUR TIMES TO THE POST! Dave Diamond is unconscious and just a pool of blood as Powell has gained a bit of revenge and slid The New Wave guy into the ring. JBN: Where is Patterson during this mess? I'll tell you where...talking over coffee and donuts with Dane! EW: Not anymore as Jamal Patterson has completed his lesson of the day and now sees that Diamond has made his way back into the ring...DANE COVERS! 1...........2.............3!! Michael Dane wins the match with some unsolicited help from Dante Powell. It will be interesting to see how this all pans out in the near future. JBN: A scab win for Dane and a deathwish for Powell, Puke. EW: We will see and that's it for now! (Camera switches back to the "Etc.." studios and Bruce and Lindsay.) BL: (Seriously) Umm..gosh..Diamond took a beating there. Are you OK, LJ? LJ: (Upset yet composed) Yes. I am fine. BL: (Unsure on how to proceed) Ok...Fans. Have we got a surprise for you! We had a very special delivery the other day and have been anxious to show you this piece of footage. Take a gander at this... (Screen fades and then rises to...) [The scene opens in front of the NPWA banner where the Cougar is pacing like a lion in a cage. He turns to the camera.] Cougar: Something troubles me greatly. Something troubles me enough...that I shun my usual locales...where I do my commentary. My cave...the woods of the North...those are places I go...when I am at peace. I am not at peace right now. Valtharius...I have been following your career quite closely. You are warrior...born...and bred. You wear your tatooes as trophies...of your many...many battles...as I wear my scars...as reminders...of my own conflicts. I had been preparing...to offer you...the chance to face me...in our chosen battleground. But then...your advisor...Karachel...commited a grave deed. He insulted my honor. You would dare...call the Cougar...a coward? I...who have faced the best that the NPWA has to offer? I...who has had his own arm broken...by a submission hold...and still not surrendered? I...who has befriended the master of death...the Coroner...and with him by my side have TWICE taken the NPWA gold? I AM TERROR INCARNATE! I FEAR NO MAN...BEAST...OR SPIRIT!!! You asked on Thursday Night Thrashings...who had the courage to face you in the ring...with no outside interference. *I* have the courage! *I* DARE to challenge the mighty Valtharius. My terms...are simple. Regular match...inside a cage. Victory by pinfall...submission...or death. Two referees...to prevent interference. One inside the cage...the other out. The cage...shall not be opened...until after one of our hands are raised in victory. Prepare yourself...my large friend. The war drums...are beating. The women cry their brace-heart songs. And the spirits...have lined the battlegrounds...to view the coming war. You shall...Mighty One...and when you do...you shall know the name...of the Cougar. GGGGGGRRRRRRRRROOOOOOOOWWWWWWWWLLLLLLLLLL!!!!!!!!!! (The screen fades to black and returns to Bruce and Lindsay) BL: Now **that** will be a mtch-up to see and ya' must wonder how the bigboy from Italy reacts to the direct challenge from the infamous Cougar. LJ: Bruce...that match will be sheer power against the unpredictabilty of the Cougar. It will undoubtedly be the best match-up to date. BL: Yes it will. (Sly grin) Now....we here at "Etc..." received this video a few days ago and I would like my beautiful colleague to pay extra special attention to it. Here it is. (Fade to black and then...) [The video opens with "Hollywood Heartthrob" Dave Diamond sitting inside a private Lear jet. A table is set up with flowers, Dom Perignon and two crystal glasses. Diamond is alone] DD: Lindsay, honey... I know you're mad at me, but believe me - those three bab... I mean, young ladies, meant absolutely nothing to me. In fact, Chyna, Bambi and Sasha are my cousins, and my auntie asked me to show them around the city. That's all, I swear. [Dave turns to the camera with a look of utter sincerity] DD: Look, this jet is waiting at the airport with me on board. After the show, if you'll just forgive me, we'll fly to Cancun for the rest of the weekend. And to show you my sincerity, go take a look outside the studio... you'll find a little token of my affection wrapped in a big red ribbon. I hope to see you soon. (Fade to black..then the camera rises to a now estatic Lindsay and Bruce) LJ: (Happily) I'll be right back. (She leaves the view of the camera as Bruce just sits there shaking his head.) BL: She is a very bright lady, but she needs to realize that Dave Diamond is nothing more--- (From offstage...) LJ: OMIGOD!! A JAGUAR! DAVEY SENT ME A JAGUAR!! (We can hear running footsteps and a door open and shut.) BL: (Sighing) She just won't learn. Well, fans. We will bring you more of this ongoing love triangle *and* the response from Valtharius and maybe even a little chat at The Round Table...next week on "ETC..." I am Bruce Lord and for my again absent colleague, Lindsay Jackson...so long! (Final fade to black) NPWA "Etc..." Written and Produced ...................................[name withheld] NPWA "Etc..."
