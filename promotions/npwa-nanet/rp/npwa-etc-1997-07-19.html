NPWA Etc. (Jul.19/97)

19 July 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The opening lyrics of "Paradise Theatre" sung by Styx's Dennis DeYoung gracefully prances through a darkened studio's speakers. Two silouettes in the foreground are leaning on stools and by the gestures they are making, one can assume the two are conversing in casual conversation. As "Paradise Theatre's" introduction ends and Styx kicks the song into full throttle, the studio lights rise to introduce the two figures to the public. The familiar face of Bruce Lord is on the viewers left and a female newcomer is on the right. Lord is wearing a black sweatshirt with the NPWA logo in bold letters across the chest with the abbreviation "ETC..." in contemporary font splashed across the federation's logo. The African American female whose smooth, light black skin is wearing the same as he. The opening music fades and a smiling Bruce Lord is the first to speak.) BL: Happy weekend to you, fans. I am Bruce Lord and welcome to the premiere edition of "NPWA ETC..." And may I introduce to you the newest member of the NPWA crew, the lovely Lindsay Jackson. LJ: (Speaking in a pleasantly lively and articulate voice) Thanks Bruce. And thank *you* fans for tuning in to "NPWA ETC..." (The camera zooms into her stunning face as she begins) This weekly show will bring you an additional splash of coverage and a different perspective of the events and goings on in the NPWA. We will bring you studio interviews, a match or two, updates and something else that *only* the producers of the NPWA were ingenious enough to develop... "NPWA Face-Off". (The camera switches so only Lord is in view) BL: "NPWA Face-Off," found right here only on "ETC..." We'll talk more about "Face-Off" later in the show. I am being told that we are going to switch over to our pre-recorded match. (Both Lindsay Jackson and Bruce Lord are seen) LJ: We have a very special non-title bout featuring the NPWA Champion Steve Sampson. His opponent is yet another member of what must be the *biggest* family in the world, Cosmos "Mir" Duttley. BL: I don't know if "biggest" is the right word, Lindsay, maybe "oddest".... LJ: (Flashing a beautiful smile) Bruce, perhaps we are talking about a little inbreeding in the Duttley's massive household? I certainly have noticed an odd resemblance between the thirteen or fourteen Duttley's that have been literally bounced around the NPWA. BL: (Smiling as well) Lindsay, you will fit in here just fine....Let's go to Ed Wailen and "The Nastee One" Johnny B. Nastee as we take a peek at the final moments of this match between The Hardline's senior member and Mr. "Mir" Duttley, or whatever his name is... (Lindsay Jackson and Bruce Lord swing around on their stools to face the wall behind them where hangs a huge screen that once displayed the "NPWA ETC..." logo. The logo has disappeared and the aforementioned match in progress is joined...) EW: Yet *another* suplex by the champion!! Oh my!! How many does that make, Johnny? JBN: Well, puke, by my count, ....twelve......fourteen of 'em!! And I know we missed a couple earlier on before we started the tally. EW: An amazing display tonite by Steve Sampson who is undoubtedly using this match as a tune-up for the hellhole he will be facing on August 3rd. JBN: Hey, puke, believe it or not, we agree. Sampson is just warmin' up for Armageddon!! Needless to say, the competition in Berlin is gonna be just a wee bit tougher than this Cosmos "Mir" Duttley... Look at that guy!! He's wearing one of those old fashioned tinfoily astronaut suits!!! EW: Bubble helmet and all. WOW!! Make that fifteen suplexes for Steve Sampson as he connects with a gorgeous vertical variety! JBN: Hey! "Mir's" helmet popped off with that one!! It bounced out of the ring and into the crowd. EW: A nice souvenir for a lucky fan, I guess. JBN: Yuk! This Duttley is uglier than the rest of them. Sheesh, that fan should throw the helmet back into the ring and the NPWA officials should demand that Ol' Cosmos put it back on!! Wow, that incest thing is *really* getting out of hand on the Duttley ranch. EW: Whoa!! That fan must have agreed with you because the helmet was just tossed back into the ring. Whooops!! JBN: HaHaHaHa!! The bubble helmet hit Cosmos right in the head on the fly, puke. EW: Down went Duttley. JBN: That fan has quite an arm!! EW: Yes indeed. Steve Sampson is a bit perplexed. I don't think he knows what to do right about now and Jamal Patterson just kicked the helmet out of the ring and it is rolling down the aisle. Those helmets sure do have a nice roll to them, don't they? JBN: Yup, puke, they do. Uh-oh!!! EW: I think Sampson has decided to put an end to this as he has scooped Duttley into his arms is going up top!! JBN: (Sniffing) I smell the Pacific Drop Suplex!!! EW: CONNECTED!!!!! What's the suplex count now, Johnny!!?? JBN: Aww hell, puke. The Pacific Drop Suplex should count as four. That makes an even twenty suplexes for Steve Sampson. EW: Good enough. Sampson with the cover. In slides Jamal Patterson... 1..........2............3!! What a show by Steve Sampson as he sent Cosmos "Mir" Duttley into orbit! JBN: Bad play on words, puke. But I wonder how much Duttley would sell that helmet for? After a long night out on the town, occasionally I could stick one of those bubble helmet things on the women that I bring back---- EW: (Interrupting) Thank you and goodnite!!! (The scene returns to the studios of "ETC..." where Bruce and Lindsay are facing the camera and each wears a sly grin) BL: Lindsay, and what were you saying about the Duttleys? LJ: (Slowly shaking her head) That is one *strange* family, Bruce. Poor Steve Sampson probably had never encountered anything quite like that before. BL: I think Sampson must have broken some type of record by hitting twenty suplexes in one match. LJ: (Nodding her head in agreement) And most of those were of different varieties as well. And in my personal opinion, I think Cosmos Duttley may be receiving a one way trip to Siberia. (Short Pause) While we're touching on the subject of Steve Sampson, what's the latest with Armageddon, Bruce? BL: Plenty Lindsay, and let's take a minute or two to rundown the complete Armageddon '97 Pay-Per-View lineup. This Berlin based whopper is going to be a Deutschland Delite because the main event will feature twelve wrestlers in a double ringed caged extravaganza. I am talking about War Games. Three representatives from four factions are gonna settle it once and for all and the only guy that crawls out of this bloody bunker will be considered the winner, but at what expense? Let's discuss the The Hardline, considered by many to be the creme de la creme of the NPWA is captained by none other than NPWA champion Steve Sampson. He is joined by another member holding the gold and fresh off his classic battle to obtain the belt - the NPWA Television Champion "Blackheart" Brian Masters. Rounding out the favorites is the extremely talented youngster Sebastian Jericho. These guys are odds on favorites to clear the ring come August 3rd. But it won't be easy. They must face a group that is admittedly in a rebuilding stage. I am talking about The New Wave of Wrestling. Their leader is the wiley ring veteran Colonel Buddy Lee Jarrett. He brings to the table two young and diversely talented individuals. The first possesses great all around athleticism, he is the "California Stud" Christian Steele. Finally, the confident, extroverted and ever dangerous "Hollywood Heartthrob" Dave Diamond. Even though these guys are rebuilding....*don't* overlook The Theatre Of Pain, also in transition but led by "The Lucha Lunatic" Reynoso Torres. Quite simply, if he can become airborne and if it will inflict pain, Torres will try it. He is joined by "The Wrecker" Mick McGreggor who a specialist at putting away an injured man. The third and last member of The Theatre of Pain has not been revealed. There has been plenty of speculation whom it may be but Torres and McGreggor aren't talking. I have no doubt that the individual will round out what I consider to be a real threat to win. The last team is a bit of a mystery. They are led by newcomer "Dreamboat" Dante Powell and he will be escorted by his wife, the beautiful Samantha Powell. He is joined by two masked men whom have made their presence known of late in their efforts to protect their captain from injury. The predictions by many are that a couple of legends have been...ummm.... *dreaming* of a comeback. We will soon see. Lindsay, *that* is War Games '97. What else do we have in store for the fans on August 3rd? LJ: You better take your fridge break before that one starts... Actually, fans, you may want to purchase a new mini- fridge and place it within arms reach of your sofa 'cause the whole Armageddon card is loaded. The tag team belts are up for grabs, but only in Berlin will you see a triangle free-for- all that features the champions-- the distinguished "Lords of the Ring" stepping into the ring to defend their belts against the truly bizarre and unpredictable Hell Razors. Those two will be joined by the high flying team of Akira Sasaki and Kenshin Nobunaga! BL: That's not all. The NPWA North American Belt is also on the line as the newest member of Chronos Triumverite, "Breakfast" Bobby Toast will hope he will be serving struedel through a straw to his challenger, the incomparable Silent Striker. With the recent behavioral changes in Toast and the consistent effort put forth by The Striker, you have my permission to eat breakfast in bed at dinnertime. LJ: (Pausing a moment to look at Bruce who grins back. She slowly shakes her head and smiles as she continues) ......"eat breakfast in bed at dinnertime...?"....OK....The winner of the next match is guaranteed a shot at the champ. Barring numerous stops at the food vendors en route to the ring, The Man They Call Nug will take on "Sadistic" Steve Stone. Rumor has it this match may be *very* entertaining. In a rematch, two of the NPWA's top wrestlers square off at Armageddon. The completely unpredictable and former multiple-gold club member Cougar battles another member of The New Wave; he is the dangerous Maltese Falcon. It would not surprise me at all if these two end up scraping it up in the cheap seats, Bruce? BL: Speaking of unpredictable, Tyr the Nightmare battles whom many feel is destined for a title shot, "The Real Deal" Steve Studd. Tyr is the master of deception and Studd is an all-around great talent. LJ: In other matches, The Mighty Boss is due to face "Ultimate" Mike Mitchells. Big John Law squares off against the ever-odd Gan Green, and two energetic exciting rookies face-off-- Bowa the Snake Man battles The Overgiver. (The camera zooms into the face of Lindsay Jackson...) LJ: Fans. That is Armageddon '97. We'll be right back with more of "ETC.." (The studio lights fade and "I'm Alright" by Kenny Loggins plays loudly over the studio speakers. The silouettes of Bruce Lord and Lindsay Jackson can be seen moving their shoudlers to the beat of the music.) (fade to black) "Hollywood" [name withheld] - NPWA Mogul & Booking Guy NPWA ONLINE: http://npwa.simplenet.com THE NEW WAVE OF WRESTLING: http://www.execulink.com/~azrael/newwave.htm ("I'm Alright" by Kenny Loggins starts to fade and the studio lights rise. Both Bruce Lord and Lindsay Jackson are somewhat laughing as the camera catches them.) LJ: This Duttley thing is too much. (Looking at Bruce) Dances with Duttley? Really? (Bruce continues smiling and nods his head) LJ: (Eyebrows raised..) Whoa. Welcome back to "ETC..." Earlier in the show, we told you about the concept that only the folks involved with the NPWA could create: "NPWA Face-Off." BL: "Face-Off" is quite simple, actually. If a wrestler in the NPWA desires to "face-off" against another wrestler, a challenge is issued to the NPWA offices and is usually granted. Any requests for a title shot must be agreed to by the titleholder. If the champion agrees to lay the belt on the line -- then we'll have a title match. If not, it becomes a non-title match. LJ: Fans, you will be able to view both the "Face-Off" request and the response right here on "ETC..." Chances are we will bring you an in-studio interview with both participants. Anything goes in "Face-Off" as President Tunney has advised the producers at "ETC..." that they may approve about EVERY request within reason. I have a sneaking suspicion that things will get very interesting very quickly around here. BL: With Armageddon just two short weeks away, I can imagine that next week's "Face-Off" will be quite exciting. (Camera zooms in on Bruce Lord's face) BL: Fans, right now we join Lindsay Jackson at "The Round Table". (Camera switches to Lindsay Jackson whom is seated alone at an oversized round cherrywood table. On the table in front of her lays a dozen or so pieces of paper and a couple of pens. The camera slowly zooms in on her eye- appealing face) LJ: Thanks Bruce. "The Round Table" is a forum in which YOU, the fan, gets to hear from the stars of the NPWA up close and personal. It is here that many of the "Face-Off'" requests and responses will be voiced. It is also here where we will be conducting one on one interviews with your favorite NPWA personalities. Our first guest at "The Round Table" is a member of The New Wave. He is "The Hollywood Heartthrob" Dave Diamond. ("Unchained" by Van Halen blares throught the studio speakers as Dave Diamond confidently struts onto the "ETC..." set. Diamond's long blond hair looks striking against the black Armani suit and purple floral tie he wears. As he approaches a now standing Lindsay Jackson, he abruptly stops in front of her and eyes her sleek body up an down. She attempts to shake his hand but he grabs it and plants a near passionate kiss on the back of it. She flashes a coy smile in Diamond's direction and the music fades out. They both sit down in the black leather seats at the round table. LJ: (Almost flustered) Well...Welcome to "Etc...", Dave. DD: (Speaking confidently he occasionally gestures with his hands) Y'know, Lindsay, honey, usually, I start off an interview by saying my presence is *your* pleasure and not *mine*. (Gazing at her face and chest) However... I am definitely going to make an exception in this case. This is *my* pleasure to be sitting next to a fine looking woman like yourself. WWWWOOOOOOOOOO!!!!! DD: (Quickly continuing before Lindsay could respond, he puts his hand on hers) And "The Heartthrob" has a fine idea. After we are done cooing at each other in front of the millions of people that have tuned in to watch me, I suggest we do the coo down the street at this little bar I know. After that....we coo all night long up at Dave's place!!! WWWOOOOOOOO!!!! LJ: (She pauses a moment before responding and removing her hand from underneath Diamond's. Her tone is half serious and somewhat flirtatious) Babe, you couldn't handle me that long. DD: (A bit surprised by that response) Lemme try, honey, lemme try!! The Heartthrob will prove to be longlasting and oh-so pleasing!! WWOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!! LJ: (Smiling, she shakes her head and continues) That conversation should not be taking place *here*. (Seriously) Dave, critics say The New Wave is spiralling downward and can't recover. How do you respond to that? DD: (Seriously) Y'know, Lindsay. The boys and I have heard this nonsense. When we travel to Berlin in early August and play these little Wartime Games...we will *prove* that The New Wave is *the* force to reckon with in the NPWA as the three of us emerge unscathed from the cage. LJ: And then what? DD: (The devlish smile and twinkle in the eye return) And then, my sweet darlin', (he again grabs her hand and kisses it) you and I will continue to coo like little----WWHOOAAAAa!!!!! HHEEYY arraagghhhgghhllumpfff!!! THUUUNNNKKKK!!!! LJ: Holy S<bleeeep>!...Dante Powell!!! The Masked Men!!! What the---?? (The camera quickly showed Dante Powell grabbing Dave Diamond by the hair and ramming the New Wave member's head into the hard cherrywood table. The masked men rush around to a shocked Lindsay Jackson and gently lead her away from the table and into the direction of an equally surprised and speechless Bruce Lord.) (Dave Diamond lifts his head off the table and realizes his nose has been bloodied by the impact. As Dante Powell climbs up onto the table the smaller masked man again rams Diamond's head into the table and he lifts the injured and suprised Diamond onto the table. Dante Powell manuevers Diamond's head into a inverted headlock and leaps off of the table) BL: (From out of view) JESUS!! The Snoozer!!! Where did they come from??!! (Powell performs The Snoozer to perfection and Diamond lays motionless until his body is met with the force of the larger masked man who had also crawled onto the table) BL: OH NO!!! Diamond was just nailed with a flying elbow!! Cut to commercial. Cut to a God-damned commercial!!! (As Diamond writhes on the ground in pain, Dante Powell grabs the tabletop microphone and speaks. The masked men stand on each side of him) DP: (Looking at each masked man) Paybacks are certainly sweet!! Diamond... New Wave... Consider this just a taste of the meal that my friends and I will serve to you you putrid bits of pondscum. (Dante Powell hands the microphone to the smaller masked man...) SMM: For some stupid reason, Jarrett and the boys decided to wake a couplea' sleeping bears!! The bears are awake now and they are *PISSED* off. New Wave...it is quite simple---You have more of a chance of finding good Chinese food in Berlin than you do surviving War Games. (He hands the microphone to Dante Powell) DP: (Looking at a shaken Bruce Lord and Lindsay Jackon)....Ummm...Sorry about the interruption, folks..... (Dante Powell and the smaller masked man leave the area while the larger one pauses for a moment then simply waves goodbye to Bruce and Lindsay) (fade to black) ******************************************************************** Next week on Monday Night Mayhem!!! The returning former law man takes on the man with law in his name... Dark Justice battles Big John Law The new man in the NPWA takes on the 'Ultimate' wrestler The Overgiver wrestles 'Ultimate' Mike Mitchells Last time they met it was supposed to be one on one. Now they know the rules and the winner will have a definite advantage at Armageddon as they go for the titles. Akira Sasaki and Kenshin Nobunaga declare war on the Hell Razors Only on Monday Night Mayhem!!! Only on WTN & Lifetime!!! ************************************************************************ (The studio lights rise again to find Bruce Lord alone. He is leaning against the stool.) BL: (A little shaken) Wow! I guess payback's are a bitch. Folks, obviously the interview with Dave Diamond will not be completed. The medics have taken Diamond to the local hospital and we are assured that besides a sore neck and a very bloody nose, he will be fine. Dante Powell and his boys have made a statement here tonight and the heat is rising in the NPWA just two weeks before Armageddon. (He looks down at his notes before he continues) BL: Umm. Lindsay was supposed to tell you about next week's show but she is a little shaken at the events that occurred on "ETC..." tonite. (Shrugging his shoulders and smiling)........She's a rookie...she'll learn. BL: It ain't gonna cool down any on next week's "ETC..." We will have another Armageddon '97 Update, another match or two, the premiere of "Face-Off", and a **VERY** special treat at "The Round Table"----a chat with the captains from each faction right here...LIVE......on "ETC....." See you next week, fans!!!
