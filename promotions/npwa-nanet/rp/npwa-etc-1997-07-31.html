As he speaks, his hands rest on the top of the leather chair. Before he begins, he again glares in the direction of Mick McGreggor.) Sampson: What my contemporaries fail to realize is that what happens on August 3rd is not a simple fight. It has become much, much more than that. We've come to want to destroy each other. That's fine and that's great. You all can cry and whine about how much you want to tear us apart. You can go to the bar and moan to some 2 dollar slut that you're going to break the Hardline in two. Doesn't matter. What _DOES_ matter is the fact that we're going to get the job done. What does matter is that you're wrestling against three of the baddest men in the sport. Three men that don't give a damn about your well-being, your safety, or your careers. What matters to the Hardline _is_ the Hardline. Sebastian, Brian, and Myself are only worried about kicking some ass. So, come on, New Wave. Let's see what you got. Bring your best fighters out. Bring them all in to the ring if you want. Come Sunday Night, you're going to get broken. You will be destroyed. And you will find out what happens to splinter promotions when you feel your arms getting broken. What about you, Powell? You said on Thursday that you once said I was a great. I have no respect for petty little <BEEP>s like you, boy. Don't come kissing my backside. But let me be a humanitarian. You're making a mistake having Sam around, boy. You're becoming Diamond's kicking boy, and you're starting to seem to accept the job. SO what do you do? You go out and get two of the biggest whiners this sport has seen, and you put them under hoods. Gosh, I think maybe, just maybe, my son Trey wouldn't be able to figure this out. You two fools should have stayed out of the NPWA. You both have reputations to live down, and fighting the Hardline isn't a way to live them down. Beating an old man up. Yeah, that's something to be proud of. Running away when you can't beat a fat, wallowing pile of s<BEET>. You're a big man #1. Have you called your woman yet? Free advice to the three of you. Take off. Go to Singapore or something. The life you save, may be your own. Finally for my favorite crew, the Theatre. Mick, Reynoso and friend, you three have bought yourselves into a fight that shouldn't have gotten as big as it has. You broke my arm. I got revenge. It should have ended at that. However, you decided to try to take my _wife_ out. You made this personal, and believe me that was the worst mistake you could have _EVER_ committed. Brian may come out there and tell you he's going to break his foot off in your ass. Sebastian can go out there and say he's going to split you from gizzard to gullet. However, there's one man that still hasn't shown his hand, and that's me. What am I thinking, ToP? Am I thinking of becoming a nuclear warhead towards you all? Am I thinking of going out there like Lash LaRioux, both barrels blazing? Well, you don't really know, and it's not my style to tip my hand. August 3rd, whatever you do, don't expect to leave that cage alive. For you will walk the Hardline, and it will lead you straight to the brimstone you came from. (The room remains deathly quiet after Sampson finishes. He calmly sits down as the camera switches to Lindsay) LJ: (Pausing) Our last speaker tonite is Dante Powell. (Powell leans forward and folds his hands in front of him before he speaks.) DP: (Looking around the table) Y'know, I have come across my fair share of----- (Suddenly there is a brief crashing sound as if.......) LJ: (Quickly turning around) Who the hell knocked down the studio door----??? AAwww, s<bleep>! I knew it! I f<bleeping> knew it!! VAL: ME FIND MASKED GUYS!! IT NO TRICK OR TREAT!! (Valtharius has barged into the studio leaving a broken door in his wake. He is seen holding the two masked men in each arm and they are swearing and trying, unsuccessfully, to escape the grips of the big Italian. Val stops a few feet short of The Round Table and those using it.) DD: (Looking at Powell) Hey Powell, planning another misguided venture? DP: (Looking surprised and shrugging his shoulders) I had no idea..... VAL: IT NO TRICK OR TREAT! (The two masked men still are trying to break free and all of the participants have stood up.) LJ: (Putting her hands up in a calming gesture and speaking the same) Val...put the two men down, OK? VAL: IT NO TRICK OR TREAT! WHY MASK?! ME NO WANT MASK!! NO TRICK OR TREAT! (In one clumsy motion, Valtharius begins to yank on each man's mask trying to remove them as the men deperately try and stop the big man...) DP: (Moving towards Valtharius) Hey!! DD: (Laughing) WWOOoooooo!!! Go big boy, go!! VAL: ME NO <<tug tug>> LIKE MASK IF <<tug tug>> NO TRICK OR TREAT!! <<tug tug>> (As Dante Powell approaches Valtharius, the big Italian is finally successful in removing the masks from the now exhausted masked men.....finally revealing their identities.....) DP: (Stopping just short of Val and the newly revealed twosome) Damnit!! LJ: GASP!!! DD: (Now laughing uncontrolably) This is better than the f'n movies.....gimme my popcorn!! WWWooooooo!!! SS: (Deadpanning) Gee....I'm shocked. VAL: WHO THIS?? MM: (With a smirk) It's the f'n Dream Team... LJ: Val...put the two men down now.... VAL: OK. (Valtharius nonchalantley tosses The Southern Rocker and Big Daddy Amoral onto the cherrywood table as one would throw dice. The Southern Rocker slides across the table and rams into Mick McGreggor knocking McGreggor backwards. Amoral ricochets off of Diamond and flops onto the floor. Seeing the opportunity, Diamond lays a boot to the head of Big Daddy.) LJ: Dave! DD: (Shrugging his shoulders as he speaks to Lindsay) Hey babe, he slid into me first. MM: (While getting up) Yeah, *they* hit *us* first... (McGreggor starts to punch the face of The Southern Rocker as Dante Powell moves toward Diamond) LJ: Dave! Oh my--- (The face of Dante Powell is met by the right backhand of Dave Diamond as Diamond swung swiftly around. With Powell momentarily stunned, Diamond grabs the head of Powell and performs a perfect Hollywood Death Drop.) VAL: (Confused, he looks at the masks in his hands) WHERE HEAD? (As McGreggor continuously pounds the head of The Southern Rocker, Sampson executes a beautiful running clothesline to the unsuspecting McGreggor sending "The Wrecker" sprawling backwards and hitting his head on the wall behind him. Sampson simply spits on the fallen McGreggor and heads toward the door.) LJ: (Looking around in a near panic state) S<bleeo>, s<bleeo, s<bleep>. I knew this was going to happen! (Diamond kicks the still downed Big Daddy Amoral...) DD: (He performs the Hollywood Strut en route to the side of Lindsay Jackson) WWoooooo!!!! The Heartthrob has spoken!! Let's blow this popstand, my sweet. (Dave Diamond holds his arm out for Lindsay to accept but she quietly shakes her head no. Diamond shrugs his shoulders and exits.) VAL: ME RIP HEAD OFF?? WHERE TRICK OR TREAT HEAD? LJ: (Still shaking her head slowly and viewing the four strewn bodies in the room) Let's cut to a commercial... (Quietly, a fade to black...) ("I'm Still Standing" by Elton John rocks through the darkened studio and the lights rise to the lone fugure of a smiling Bruce Lord.) BL: Poor Lindsay. Her innocent little show never turns out the way she wants...oh well....Personally, I thought it was a bitchin' show.
