[name withheld] < [detail withheld] > wrote in article < 3304DD...@snet.net >... > All league presidents post the most unusual or best storylines that they > ever came up with here. Well, once upon a time, perhaps the most bizarre concept for a match we've ever had came up on a total whim that got out of control. It all started when super-face "Breakfast" Bobby Toast was hosting his famous segment "Breakfast Time", as usual around his kitchen table, with an assortment of good, wholesome breakfast foods around so that all you kiddies can have a good, nutritious breakfast and have the energy to face the day! <SLAP!> Sorry... got stuck in BBT mode. Anyways, an arrogant newcomer named Laser hit the scene, talked about how he was going to make an impression on the league, and proceded to shove Toast face-first into a bowl of oatmeal, not letting go until tens of nameless, faceless officials hit the scene to break the whole mele up. And thus, the Oatmeal Death Match was born. A tub, a foot and a half deep, the size of a wrestling ring. Filled with oatmeal. Only way to win is to shove your opponent down face-first in the oatmeal until he passes out. In classic BBT form, Gary (BBT's handler), spent the three weeks leading up to the match looking forward to a "good, solid, clean wrestling match with lots of holds and counterholds". There were serious questions whether Toast understood what the heck was going on... and if he did clue in, would he be willing to DROWN the masked newcomer? Well, Toast managed to win the match, in typically clueless fashion, with the help of his not-so-nice ally (Mr. Nice Guy) and his scuba-gear-wearing bodyguard. And then there was In Your Mall, with its classic Falls Count Anywhere In The Mall match. Let me just say that you haven't lived until you've seen a World Champion piledriven down an escalator. <Thud, thud, thud, thud, thud...> -- [name withheld] - Journalism student a-go-go Reporter, Humber Et Cetera, http://sms.humberc.on.ca/etcetera.htm Visit the NPWA Online - http://npwa.simplenet.com/ <<<END o_WgO2ZrVHoJ>>>
