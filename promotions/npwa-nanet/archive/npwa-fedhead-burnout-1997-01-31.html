[ESSAY] Fedhead Burnout

31 January 1997

> My sympathies go to all the other fedheads out there who have hit this > feeling, and all I can say is it is to be expected. I suppose it`s kinda > like a mid-life crisis, and perhaps the best thing to do is to slow things > down and really not worry about your fed so much. There are many more > important things in life to do than e-wrestling. I think when it becomes > your life, when it takes over everything else and destroys everything else > you love and know, then you must really begin to worry. I hear ya, Jake. I've been with the NPWA (then NWC) since late 1993, and in that time, we've taken two-month hiatuses twice, but other than that, we've been GO GO GO, and I have to say that I'm on about my 12th burnout right now. It goes in cycles... burnout, when you almost don't wanna do it, but something drives you on... as you aptly described it... an obsession. But then, there are the up periods when everything's clicking, life is good and you can't WAIT to write your matches, advance your angles, and just go to town. Unfortunately, during that time, you add two new features to your web page, and a new show... and when the burnout hits next time. Yeah. You get the picture. ;-) I have to agree though... without the players, and without, in my case, three of the finest, most fun people I've ever had the pleasure of working with helping me put out our product, we'd have closed a bunch of times. In fact, they saved the league from going under twice, once when one joined the writing crew to stop me from giving up, and another time when we re-distributed the system to a more egalatrian four-man committee we have to day. I owe them bigtime. Not to mention, we have the ultimate solution to burnout with the serious day-to-day work in a wrestling league... A GOOD COMEDY ANGLE OR THREE!!! Just when you think you can't go on, someone suggests a Pancake-Eat-A-Thon which culminates with an arena being burned down, and life is good again. ;-) -- [name withheld] - Journalism student a-go-go Reporter, Humber Et Cetera, http://sms.humberc.on.ca/etcetera.htm Visit the NPWA Online - http://npwa.simplenet.com/ <<<END NkrveNDaaAcJ>>>
