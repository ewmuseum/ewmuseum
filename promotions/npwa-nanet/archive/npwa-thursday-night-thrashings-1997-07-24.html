Thursday Night Thrashings (July 24/97)

24 July 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The shores of Lake Geneva are lined with the many boats of the wealthy that are invited to dine at the exclusive Cafe Royale. The deck extending over this pristine lake is filled with it's usual upper echelon of local residents, each of whom are seated at umbrella covered tables adorned with white table cloths. As the camera weaves between the tables towards it's destination, white gloved waiters are seen carrying trays full of fine wine and champagne to their respected destinations. Finally, Dante and Samantha Powell are within sight of the roving camera. Each is dressed in white polos and khaki shorts and sport sunglasses to protect their eyes from the warm Montreux, Switzerland sun. They both look well rested and are each holding a glass of wine. An intimate laugh is shared as the camera comes to rest next to their table.] DP: (Speaking calmly and confidently as he puts down his glass of wine) Y'know, a lot has been said about this Armaggedon War Games that is just around the corner on August 3rd. A few people have stuck their faces in front of the camera and said this and said that about how they are going to beat this guy up and whip that guy's butt. [He pauses to wipe his mouth with his napkin before he continues. Samantha is looking at her husband as she plucks green grapes off of their vine and pops them in her mouth.] DP: It has come to my attention that my two friends and I have been called *darkhorses* by some, and that our chances for survival in The War Games are considered a longshot at best. [Shaking his head slowly, a small grin appears on his face] DP: God, I hope all of you feel that way. Nothing would make me happier than to see the looks on all of your cocky faces when the three of us are the only ones left standing come the evening of August 3rd. Ya' got twelve of the top talents in the top fed in wrestling in a cage goin' at it!! What else would you expect but to see blood guts and (smiling) heck, maybe even a nose or two. I believe I called you the greatest champion this sport has ever seen. I also said that you need not worry about me at that time. [Looking into the camera deeply, he continues] DP: As a matter of fact, I am now looking forward, more than ever, to seeing just how good our champion really is. [Smiling and loaded with sarcasm] Number two: Davey Diamond....I loved you on "Etc..." this past weekend, you were great. [Dante crosses his legs and speaks seriously] DP: Tonight, I face-off against "The Real Deal" Steve Studd. Steve, I don't know a hill of beans about you, my boy, all I do know is that you are an incredible talent.
