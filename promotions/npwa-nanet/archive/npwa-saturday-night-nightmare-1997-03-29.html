It's even funnier when you can fight back from death. God knows I gave the doctor a heart attack by being in Salt Lake City on Thursday. Enough about my lousy week. Tonight, I'm here to talk about two men. Both need a serious attitude adjustment, and both will get it at April Assault. First, let's talk Brian Masters and his holier than thou attitude. Yes, Brian, I took it upon myself to learn your La Majistral, because I respected you and the hold. Yes, Brian, I hold you in the highest regard as both a wrestler and a man. God knows you've been able to hold a marriage together when others have failed. However, make no mistake. For your turning this back into a personal war, you're going to pay dearly. Do I want to debilitate you? No. Do I want to hurt you? Yes, yes I do. For all your crimes by bringing my dirty laundry into the open, you deserve no less than to get some kind of retribution. So ask yourself, Brian, why did you make this personal? Why did you want me to work at the height of adversity? Is it retribution for what I did on the way to Gory? <Pauses for a minute>* Let me get something off my chest right here, right now. Annette, if ever I said anything to offend you, I deeply apologize. It was never my intention to offend you. Back to Brian, though. Masters, you come and you try to hurt me. You come and you try to end my life. Those two jackasses that tried to kill me didn't get the job done, and neither will you. Let me speak about Mark Bagwell for a minute, though. Bagwell, what in GOD'S NAME do you think you're doing? "Oh, I'm Mr. Primadonna, and I demand a match at Assault or I'm going to quit". You get matches when you earn them, and believe you me, Bagwell, you did NOT earn that North American Title match. That scum sucking Southern Rocker earned it, but because he wanted to be a baby, he got fired. You lucked into that match, Bagwell. You aren't going to be so lucky when Stone rips you a new bodily opening for the removal of solid waste. God knows I hate Stone with a deep passion, but I hate you even worse, Bagwell. Your career means nothing, because you've ended up being a follower to Porter and Malone. Now, you're out in the open, and with no one to watch your back, you're as harmless as a flea. Who's going to save you when I beat you into the mat, only for you to hear "God, Steve, please stop. You've proved your point"? Will it be Malone? Will it be Porter? No, because neither man ever gave a damn about you. TBS didn't stand for The Body Shop. It stood for Three Back Stabbers. Come on, Bagwell, if you're a man, you'll quit talking smack, and you'll get in this ring against me. You're entering the ring against a man who just doesn't care anymore. I'm not afraid of a damned thing anymore, except for maybe being near Nug when he's out of Ball Parks. As a great friend of mine once said, Bagwell, Welcome to Reality. Now, Nice Guy, I know you're all set to wrestle Bagwell here tonight, but I'd really appreciate the chance to show that punk a lesson. So, I guess what I'm asking you, is this: Will you step aside and let me wrestle Bagwell tonight?" BB: "Oh my goodness! We could have a new main event here folks! Mr. Nice Guy is crawling back into the ring. He's asking for Sampson's mic." If you're so desperate for a fight, why don't you try me on for size? Or would you prefer to go home a beat one of the little tramps you call your wife?!?" BB: "OH MY! Sampson just charged out of the ring after Masters! Masters shoves Barclay in Sampson's way. Sampson tosses Barclay aside and OH NO!" MS: "BLACKJACK! Dealer wins!" BB: "Masters was concealing a blackjack behind his back and just nailed Sampson across the side of the head with it. Now he's laying the boots to Sampson! Security is trying to control Masters. Meanwhile, Mr. Nice Guy is standing in the ring, looking totally confused. AND HERE COMES BAGWELL!" MS: "I guess the 'Bad Boy' got tired of waiting. His job tonight was to dish a little sumpin'-sumpin' out to Mr. Nice Guy, and that looks to be just what he intends to do." BB: "Bagwell attacks Nice Guy and that's good enough for Referee Jamal Patterson who calls for the starting bell. Bagwell now, with a reverse neckbreaker. He follows up with a flying clothesline. Jamal Patterson counts: One, two, shoulder up. Nice Guy whips Bagwell hard into the corner and Bagwell goes over the top to the outside. Plancha by Nice Guy onto Bagwell outside!" They're brawling by the Tibetan Broadcaster's Table. Nice Guy hops up on the apron, flying axehandle onto Bagwell who goes through the table! Bagwell is hurt!" What's going on?" Mr.S: "I must disagree with you Mr. Buchanan. That bag does not appear to be leather. I have only seen a substance like that once before, when reviewing an ancient tome called the Necronomicon. It was said to have been bound in human flesh." MS: "You mean to tell me that his bag is made outta..uh...my stomach... I'm glad I didn't eat anything before the show. After last week, I decided not to take any chances. Why did Tunney hire this psycho again?" BB: "I don't know, but he's pulled the table up to ringside and is rooting through that...let's just call it leather, okay? He's putting the bag under table and heading over to the steps! He's gonna get into the ring!" Mr.S: "Hardly, Mr. Smart. It appears our occultist friend has a taste for blood. He's dipping his finger in a splash of blood left by Bagwell's head on the steps and placing it in his mouth. Judging by the smirk on his face, I'd say he likes it." He whips Nice Guy into the ropes, but Mr. Nice Guy meets him with a clothesline." MS: "As much as I hate Mr. Nice Guy, he's gettin' the job done in there, man. Those are some phat moves he's got goin' on there. That clothesline nearly took Bagwell's head off." Mr.S: "Tis truly an example of how one small error can change the tide of battle. Had Bagwell not hit his head when he went over the top, we would be watching a different match entirely. It was the ripple in the pond." MS: "The Pond? I thought we were in Reno, not Anaheim!" He picks up a bench and nails Masters across the back with it! But Masters retaliates with a low blow. Now Masters sets him up...." MS: "YO! Bobby-B! Wrong match, bro!" BB: "Oh yeah, sorry. I forgot. Mr. Friendship comes over to help but is quickly knocked down by Wicked Lester. He running to the back now to find help." Sampson is out! Masters picks up what is left of the beer bottle. It looks like he's going to carve up Steve Sampson! WAIT! There's "Renegade" Rick Carrera! He nails Masters with a clothesline! Masters realises that the odds are against him and wisely bails out. Carrera is yelling for medical assistance!" Mr.S: "Gentlemen, if I might direct your attention away from the monitors and to ringside for a moment, it appears we have a problem." MS: "Whutzup Sandy?" Mr.S: "Well 'Marky', as you can see, while you were concentrating on what was going on backstage, Wicked Lester has set himself up a rather quaint little stage. He has Mr. Nice Guy stretched out on the table, candles burning, and has begun painting symbols on Mr. Nice Guy's torso with that blood he spit up earlier." BB: "What is he up to?" So, I'd say he's offering up Mr. Nice Guy's soul to his Dark Lord." BB: "How's he going to do that?" Mr.S: "By sacrificing Mr. Nice Guy, of course." BB: <turning pale> "YOU MEAN....?!?! Get security down here now! We have to put a stop to this! I've never had a wrestler sacrificed on my show, and I don't intend to start now! Where's security?!?" MS: "They're all unconscious from the Masters/Sampson fight." BB: "Carrera is backstage help Sampson up. He's got a towel for Sampson's head. He checking that Sampson is okay. THERE'S MR. FRIENDSHIP!!! He's pleading to Carrera and Sampson for help!" MS: "They better come soon, because Lester's got some kinda messed up knife out!" Mr.S: "That would be a kryss knife. The blade is somewhat wavy, like the tail of a slithering snake. It is only used in the most prestigious of ceremonies, even among human sacrifices. It truly shows Lester's respect for Mr. Nice Guy" BB: "I'm sure that comes as great comfort to Mr. Nice Guy as he has just regained conciousness and realises the predicament he's in.
