Xmas Gory '97 State of the Union

24 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*********************************************************************** DP: Ladies and gentlemen, the following match is the MAIN EVENT OF THE EVENING!!!!! [crowd pops in anticipation] DP: There are no rules, no disqualifications, no countouts, no pinfalls or submissions and the match can take place anywhere in Toronto. This match will be decided when one of the wrestlers takes his LAST DYING BREATH!!!! [the crowd roars at the only-slightly exaggerated hype from Perry] DP: Introducing first. from Seattle, Washington, weighing 225 pounds, here is "SUPERSTAAAAR" STEVE SAAAAAAMPSOOOOON!!!!!!! [Sampson makes his way to the ring as the Skydome crowd roars in approval. "Shooting Star" is barely audible over the noise. His right knee is heavily braced.] JBN: I'll never understand why these idiots love this guy so much. if only they knew the *real* Steve Sampson like the Nastee Man does. EW: Yeah right, like you know anything about Steve Sampson. JBN: I *do* know that Mick McGreggor is going to go right after that bum knee of his and cripple him for life. That's what I would do. EW: Why am I not surprised. DP: And his opponent, from Columbus, Ohio, weighing 255 pounds.. here is "THE WRECKER" MIIIIIIIIIIICK McGREEEEEEEEGOOORRRRRR!!!!! [Hendrix's "All Along The Watchtower" plays as Mick slowly makes his way to the ring to a massive heel pop from the crowd. He looks deadly serious as he makes his way down the aisle.] EW: This is it, Johnny, the match the world has been waiting for. JBN: Sampson is a dead ma. EW: SAMPSON ATTACKS McGREGGOR AS HE TRIES TO ENTER THE RING!!!!!!! He dropkicks McGreggor right off the ring apron, and he crashes into the time keeper's table!!!! JBN: Sampson is a big, fat cheater! EW: Just how do you cheat in a no-DQ match, Johnny? JBN: The uh. bell hadn't rung yet! That's it! EW: Sampson climbs through the ropes and stands on the ring apron. MISSILE DROPKICK!!!! JBN: DAMN! EW: McGreggor gets drilled by that kick. looks like Sampson's knee is holding up so far with two big dropkicks! Sampson is up and he grabs the bell that is now lying on the floor. JBN: HE BEANS MICK IN THE HEAD WITH THE BELL!!!!!! EW: Well, the bell has been rung now, Johnny. satisfied? JBN: Hardee har har. EW: Sampson pulls McGreggor up to his feet. looks like a cut has been opened up in Mick McGreggor's scalp. SIDE SUPLEX THROUGH THE TIME KEEPER'S TABLE!!! JBN: Come on Mick! Get up! EW: Sampson is looking around the ringside area. he's coming over to our table, Johnny! JBN: He must have heard all the bad things you were saying about him! EW: Stop it! Sampson is taking one of the monitors off of the table and stalks back over to where McGreggor is laying. JBN: HE'S GOING TO THROW THAT MONITOR DOWN ON TOP OF HIM!!!! THIS IS MURDER!!!! EW: McGREGGOR NAILS SAMPSON IN THE KNEE WITH A TABLE LEG!!!!!! Steve's leg folds underneath him and he drops the monitor, which crashes to the cement floor in a shower of sparks. JBN: Go Mick go! EW: McGreggor breaks that table leg over Steve's head! JBN: YEAH! EW: Sampson goes down hard! McGreggor struggles to his feet. he pulls Sampson up.. TIGER DRIVER ONTO THE WRECKAGE OF THE TABLE!!!!!!! JBN: Now *this* is the Mick McGreggor I was expecting to see tonight! EW: McGreggor walks over to the steel security barrier that separates the front row of this capacity crowd from the ring area, and he tips it over on it's side. He walks over and pulls Steve Sampson up again, positioning him for something.. JBN: This is gonna be great!!!!! EW: POWERBOMB ONTO THE STEEL BARRIER!!!!!!! Sampson's back must be on fire. JBN: Or broken, if we're lucky. EW: Mick is wasting no time! He picks Sampson up by the hair. AND DRAGS HIM INTO THE CROWD!!!!! JBN: If the fans are smart, they'll take shots at Stevie while they have the chance! EW: Luckily our crack security staff is right there to protect wrestlers and fans alike! Mick is punching Steve repeatedly as he drags him through the fans lucky enough to have floor seats. JBN: All of Dutt's lackeys, no doubt. EW: They've reached the walls that separates Level 100 from the "playing field" and McGreggor throws Sampson over the wall onto the stairs leading up to the Level 100 Mezzannine. JBN: That moron referee, Jamal Patterson, can barely keep up! EW: Security is having problems as well. the Level 100 fans are going crazy as McGreggor climbs over the wall and joins Sampson on the stairs. McGREGGOR SMASHES STEVE'S FACE ON THE CONCRETE STEPS!!!!!! JBN: It can only improve his looks. EW: Now he begins dragging him up the stairs. it's been all McGreggor for the last few minutes. They're nearing the top and Mick tosses Steve up the last few steps onto the floor of the promanade. JBN: WHAT THE HELL??!?!?! EW: A couple of fans are overzealous and are stomping on Steve Sampson!!!!! Where is security?!?!?!? JBN: THOSE AREN'T FANS!!!!! EW: YOU'RE RIGHT!!!! THAT"S COLD DEATH!!!!!!! "The Iceman" Paul Wright and The Executioner are stomping the hell out of Steve Sampson! JBN: Remember puke, they asked Mick McGreggor to join Cold Death, so they are trying to show Mick some solidarity by beating the crap outta Stevie. AND IT'S ALL LEGAL! HAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA!!!!!! EW: Stop it, will you? McGreggor has walked up and is looking at Cold Death work over Steve. McGreggor laughs. AND WHIPS THE EXECUTIONER DOWN THE STAIRS!!!!!!!! JBN: WHAT THE HELL???? EW: The crowd roars in approval. JBN: Thank god for that Jumbotron or no one would be seeing this match! EW: True enough. Wright is in McGreggor's face, asking him why he did that. JBN: HOLY SHI. uh, CRAP!!!!! EW: McGreggor just suplexed Wright down the stairs! Wright goes tumbling down and lands on Executioner at the bottom! JBN: Guess Mick wants to beat the stuffing out of Mr. Popularity himself! EW: Guess so. McGreggor picks Sampson up. Irish whip. INTO THE SOUVENIR STAND!!!!!! JBN: I see that almost all the shirts are sold out, puke, except that "Father And Son Reunion" shirt with you and Skippy on the front. EW: There's no such shirt, so just stop it! The souvenir stand has collapsed and so has Steve Sampson. McGreggor walks over, as does the official and the security guards. JBN: NOOOOO!!!!! EW: SAMPSON JUST POPPED UP TO HIS FEET AND SMASHED A BOX OF SOMETHING. JBN: CD's!!!! IT WAS A CASE OF "NPWA: THE ALBUM" CD's!!!! EW: . RIGHT OVER MICK'S HEAD!!!! Jewel cases and CD's are flying every which way, and McGreggor goes down! Sampson grabs him by the arm and starts dragging him down the promenade. JBN: This is absolutely insane! EW: What did you expect? Remember Dying Breath I between Sampson and Masters? JBN: How could I? The internet geeks are *still* talking about it! EW: So you admit you hang out on the internet, eh? JBN: Well. I. YOU TRICKED ME, WAILEN!!!!! EW: [smirking] Sampson has paused in front of the McDonalds. he scoops McGreggor up and slams him on the cement. Confident that Mick isn't going anywhere, he hops up onto the counter and heads into the McDonalds concession stand!!!! JBN: What, little Stevie is having a Big Mac attack? EW: I don't think so, he's looking.. OH MY GOD!!!! NO!!!! JBN: HE WOULDN'T DARE!!!! EW: STEVE HAS JUST PULLED A DEEP FRYER OF BOILING OIL AND FRIES OFF THE GRILL. HE CLIMBS UP ONTO THE COUNTER AGAIN AND THROWS IT AT MICK!!!!! [long, dramatic pause.] EW: THANK GOD!!!!!!! McGreggor rolled out of the way!!!!!! JBN: He got caught by the splash back though!!!! EW: McGreggor is howling in pain as some of that oil splashed onto his skin!!!! The hatred Sampson possesses for this man is unbelievable! JBN: I don't think he was that vicious even he and Masters were at the height of their feud! EW: Sampson hops down and grabs McGreggor. where the heck? JBN: You're allowed to say "hell" puke. this *is* Pay Per View. EW: Sampson and McGreggor are heading up to Level 200! JBN: Must be heading to the Hard Rock for a pitcher! EW: Somehow I doubt it. they've reached the second level of the massive Skydome. Sampson locks Mick up. FISHERMAN'S SUPLEX ON THE CONCRETE FLOOR!!!! JBN: He's trying to win with a friggin wrestling move! What the hell is the world coming to?!?!? EW: On the concrete, even a technical move is going to do a lot of damage. Sampson gets to his feet and heads over to the beer concession. He picks up a tray loaded with full cups of beer. HE WHIPS IT AT McGREGGOR!!!!!! JBN: God, I hope that wasn't Blue! EW: That's all they serve here at Skydome, you know that! JBN: Poor Mick! EW: McGreggor is drenched in lager, and Sampson walks over and picks him up. they're heading for the stairs that lead outside!!!!!! JBN: It's freaking cold out there! If they're gonna beat the living tar outta each other, they should at least do it where it's warm. EW: Sampson and McGreggor are at the top of the stair. HOLY MOLEY!!!!!! JBN: "Holy Moley," puke? That's lame even for you. EW: McGreggor grabbed Sampson by the front of the tights and propelled him down the stairs!!!! JBN: That's gotta be 40 or 50 feet of stairs, straight down! EW: Sampson is lying in a broken and bloodied heap at the bottom of those stairs, and Mick McGreggor, who isn't in much better shape heads down to meet him. JBN: We don't have to go out there with them, do we? EW: Of course not, don't be stupid. [whispering into this headset] Dave, we don't have to follow them, do we? Good. [aloud again] McGreggor picks Sampson up and they head out the door. the ref and the camera crew follow out into the cold Toronto night. JBN: Look at all the people out here, puke? EW: Fans who came but couldn't get in I suppose. JBN: Yeah, this has only been sold out for the last 2 months. idiots. EW: Be nice, Johnny. McGreggor heads out toward Front Street. he's heading for one of the omnipresent sausage cart vendors! JBN: Oh Christ, haven't we had enough frigging hot dog spots here in the last 2 years???? EW: Will you stop??? Besides, this is a sausage cart, totally different. IRISH WHIP BY McGREGGOR!!!! JBN: SAMPSON SMACKS THE CART!!!!!! EW: Steve goes down hard on the sidewalk, and Mick walks over. HE'S TEARING THE KNEE BRACE OFF!!!!! THIS ISN'T GOOD!!!! JBN: Au contraire, puke. this is great! EW: McGreggor tears if off and tosses into the crowd. JBN: What a great souvenir from Stevie's last match ever! EW: Stop it! Mick puts the boots to Steve now. Steve is almost out of it completely. Mick grabs that right leg and positions it on the sidewalk. what is he doing. SWEET JESUS, NO!!!!!!!! JBN: HOLY SH<BLEEP>!!!! EW: McGREGGOR IS RUNNING OVER SAMPSON'S KNEE REPEATEDLY WITH THE SAUSAGE CART!!!!!!!! THIS WILL END HIS CAREER!!! JBN: Even better, he may be crippled for life! EW: I can't believe you!!!! This is unbelievable!!! Sampson is screaming in agony. JBN: It ain't over til he's dead, puke. Or something like that. EW: It should be over right now as far as I'm concerned. McGreggor picks Sampson up now. he can't put any weight on that right leg now, and McGreggor has to hold him up as he heads down Front Street to. East Side Marios?????? JBN: Hey badda boom badda bing! EW: Mick holds Sampson up, slaps him in the face a few times. AND THROWS HIM THROUGH THE BAY WINDOW!!!!!!!!!!! JBN: Those diners by the window just got more than they bargained for! Steve Sampson is lying on their table!!!!!! EW: The diners are scrambling as McGreggor climbs through the broken window and up onto the table. He picks Sampson up and scoops him up into the air.. JBN: OH MY GOD!!!!!!!!!!!! EW: SCREWED DRIVER *THROUGH* THE TABLE!!!!!!!!!!!!! The table collapses into pieces and both men fall to the marble tile below! Sampson hits the floor head first. this is bad, there's blood everywhere. JBN: He's finished. EW: McGreggor gets to his feet and stands over Steve Sampson, barely able to maintain his balance. he calls the official over who checks on Sampson.. IT'S OVER!!!! The official declares the Steve Sampson cannot continue! JBN: Mick wins!!!! YESSSSSS!!!!! EW: I can't believe what we've just seen... these two men must be completely spent! JBN: *I'm* completely spent, just watching them! EW: Well, fans... as Mick McGreggor tries to regain his breath and his composure, that brings to an end the wrestling portion of our show. I am, however, told that NPWA President Billy-Joe Tunney is back at the NPWA Towers, and would like to make an announcement! JBN: Oh goody. Sleepy time. *********************************************************************** [The shot cuts to NPWA President Billy-Joe Tunney behind his desk at NPWA Towers in Brampton, Ontario.] BJT: Good evening. As most of you long-term NPWA fans likely know by now, it is my tradition to give sort of a "State of the Union" address at the close of Pay-Per-View shows, such as the one you've just seen. This is especially true after Xmas Gory, as the NPWA closes out one year, and looks forward to the year ahead. This year, we saw many tumultous changes in the NPWA. We rode a rollercoaster, and saw many large-scale changes. We enjoyed creative direction from a larger number of people than we ever expected to, and in the end, we started as we began. Strong, virile, competitive. At the very top of the wrestling world. The performances of the athletes tonight should tell you that. I wish to congratulate each and every participant, be they winner or loser, for the stellar efforts they showed. And we look to the future, we look first at the very exciting young Cruiserweight division. Many people have spoken of this in the past, but allow me to officially confirm it. The NPWA will introduce a Cruiserweight division, with a tournament starting on January 1st, 1998, and culminating at Valentine's Day Massacre '98. The brackets for that tournament are as follows: *********************************************************************** David Fulton-----| |-----------------| Tyr--------------| | |-----------------| Le Rebel---------| | | |-----------------| | Jet Mini---------| | |----------------- Chris Worral-----| | NPWA |-----------------| | Cruiserweight Greggy Cool------| | | Champion |-----------------| Trace Michaels---| | |-----------------| Mike Mitchells---| *********************************************************************** BJT: We certainly wish the best of luck to all these competitors. Now, in the near future, we'll see a slight refocus of NPWA programming. We intend to further strengthen our position in the Monday Night ratings by putting increased attention on Monday Night Mayhem as our flagship program. Thursday Night Thrashings will continue to provide the same level of quality wrestling you expect from the NPWA, we're just "upping the bar" a bit on Mayhem. But Thrashings will get one last big day in the sun, as we'll ring in the New Year in NPWA style! New Year's Day, the NPWA will travel to London, Ontario, and there, from the Ice House, we'll present a Thursday Night Thrashings special, entitled First Night, First Fight. We've already been working in the back to sign several matches for that card. First of all, after recieving an official complaint from "The Impact Player" Mustafa, the NPWA will sanction a rematch between him and The Cougar, for The Cougar's newly-won NPWA North American Title. In addition, Drama will meet "The Franchise" Steve Stone and "Fair Warning" John Hunter for the NPWA Tag Team Championships. Also on the card, Dave Diamond will defend his NPWA World Title against the finalist in tonight's Pinfall Royale, and native Londoner, "Big Daddy Cool" Amoral. From there, we'll continue our trip west, and our big cards, as on Saturday, January 10th, we'll present the first in what we hope to be a series of NPWA wrestling specials which will air live on Canada's offical broadcaster, the CBC! The show, which we call Wrestling Night In Canada for obvious reasons, will feature "The Impact Player" Mustafa taking on "The Hollywood Heartthrob" Dave Diamond, regardless of who wears what titles at that point. Also, Mick McGreggor will face Bowa the Snake Man in a Falls Count Anywhere Texas Death Match, and the Tag Team Champions will face Cold Death... appropriate, given that the card is being held in Winnipeg. And then, as our tour continues west, we'll arrive in Vancouver, British Columbia just in time for the middle of February, and for our annual Valentine's Day Massacre Pay-Per-View special, live on Valentine's Day, Thursday, February 14, 1998. Although we don't have much of a card for that as yet, I can tell you that Mick McGreggor will get a shot at the NPWA World Champion, whoever that may be, and Resident Evil will get their shot at becoming NPWA Tag Team Champions. Obviously, many more details will be available in the weeks to come. I'd like to discuss some other changes. First of all, with the NPWA Online. We've been thrilled by the great response the site has recieved since it went online a year ago, and we're proud to announcee that a new version of the site is being prepared and uploaded to the Web as we speak! Please, do not miss the chance to see this new site... we're very confident you'll find it to your liking. And my last announcement... will you come with me, please? [Tunney gets out, motioning for the camera to follow him, and walks to his window. Out the window, you can see a large construction job going on in a field behind NPWA Towers.] BJT: Oh, it might not be much right now... but over the next year, this site will become the mecca of the NPWA! What you see here, friends, are the beginnings stages of Terror Twins Arena, the soon-to-be-home of the NPWA. Set to open by this time next year, it will seat, in comfort, 25,000 screaming NPWA fans, with every seat being especially designed and positioned for the wrestling fan's needs! Words cannot express how proud I am of this crowning achievement for the NPWA... and when we move in, effecitve January 1, 1999... you can bet the NPWA will still be a dominant player in the world of professional wrestling. And we have no one thank for that, but you, our fans, and your incredible support. Without you, we'd be nowhere, and the organization would have collapsed months ago, to be sure. So once again, I'd like to thank you, from all of us, to all of you. And wish all of you and yours, a very very happy holiday season, and all the best of the new year! Thank you, and God bless. *********************************************************************** EW: WOW! As usual, a lot of ground covered by NPWA President Billy-Joe Tunney... JBN: Yeah! The galoot took up all our time! EW: You're right, Johnny... fans, we're unfortunately out of time. Before we go, we'd just like to provide you with a bit of an update... The Silent Striker, we have been told, has been rushed to Toronto General Hospital... JBN: Apparently, for once, Pain Train Memorial was actually out of the way! EW: ... and is in a coma, although doctors do not believe the condition to be too serious. Most likely, just a case of needing time off to... JBN: Wake his dead-to-the-world body up. EW: ... come out of the coma and recuperate. We certainly hope all is well with him soon, as... and it's only fitting at Christmas... it appears he was reunited with his one true love here tonight. JBN: Ahhh... isn't that cute. EW: So from myself and Johnny... JBN: HOLD IT! EW: Oh no... JBN: I do the signoffs on Pay-Per-Views, puke... and this one will be a masterpiece as usual. This is the man who knows if you've been naughty or nice... and girls, if you've been naughty, I'll be around later... for the puke, this is The Nastee Man, saying... HAVE YOURSELF A NASTEE LITTLE CHRISTMAS!!!! EW: Merry Christmas, fans!
