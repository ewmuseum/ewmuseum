Those words echo through the camera man's head as he follows the curved red path. The Giant Man had told him this, and then pointed him in the direction of a brick road. The road curved through the circus, past the ferris wheel and the haunted house, past the rows of games where you could win stuffed demon bears and voodoo dolls, dangerously close to the dreaded House of Mirrors... The camera man was surprised he had not noticed the road sooner. It was a shiney deep red, and it glistened in the moonlight. It looked like the bricks that made up the road were painted with fresh blood. He didn't want to travel down the road at first, but the Giant Man did not give him much of a choice. He had started to protest, but the giant man bellowed "GO!" and pointed again, and the camera man decided wisely not to erage the giant any further. As so he walked, stupified at the visions of the circus around him. There was a cotton candy stand off to his right, except the cotton candy was red and melting and looked to be smeared across the walls of the stand. The man who was in charge of the stand turned to look at the camera man, and he smiled and waved. Well, he smiled as best he could, anyway... half of the man's face was melted. His left eye apparently had slid down to his cheek, the left side of his mouth drooped down into a permenant frown. The camera man waved back, more than a little nervously, and hurried onward. The road twisted around behind the rows of games, and the camera man finally saw where he was headed. It was a stage, enclosed by a red curtain. There was one chair set out for him... his very own front row... and there was a sign nearby that said "Welcome to a very special theatrical presentation from the Circus of Pain!" The camera man went to the chair, sat down, and waited for the show to begin... Moments later, the curtain slowly opened, and the horrors began. On stage was a large cross with a man tied to it. The vision was complete with a white cloth wrapped around his body, and a crown of thorns placed neatly on his head. He had no face. His body writhed and jerked on the cross... to one onlooker it may have seemed as if he was in utter agony... to another it may have appeared the man was squirming in sheer ecstasy. The camera man just stared, his mouth hung slightly open, his eyes wide with disbelief. And then Drama made their way onto stage, dressed in their flowing black robes. Agony entered stage left, Ecstasy entered stage right. Together they met in the center of the stage, surrounding the cross. They embraced the man on the cross, then turned away. As they step away from the man, his head begins to bleed from the thorns... tiny rivers of blood trickle down his non-face. Drama dissappears offstage, leaving the bleeding man momentarily. The camera man has turned completely pale. Drama returns, their robes discared. They are now dressed in their black wrestling attire. They stand, one on each side of the cross. A moment later, two men run onto stage. They appear to be wrestlers, dressed in traditional wrestling tights. Agony and Ecstasy catch each man and give them a vicious chokeslam down to the theatre floor. Two more men run out, and the same fate happens to them. This process continues, during which the man on the cross continues to bleed and writhe. Bodies begin to pile up on the theatre floor. Finally the choas ceases, and Drama steps over the fallen bodies and speaks in unison: Come one come all To the end of time Destiny awaits you When you cross the line Our domain is everywhere Even in your mind Our power grows stronger And we will eliminate your kind Your weak pathetic lives Will be ground into dust Put no faith in your gods It is Us you should trust You are looking At the greatest team Future World Champions All others can only dream Heed our words Before you come to die We may stab you in the back But about these things we never, ever lie We Are Drama And the camera man faints...
