Very cute, Hunter... very cute. Not cute enough, though. If you want to unnerve me... if you want to annoy me more than a fly would... you're gonna have to do a lot better. You're gonna have to get off that barstool a little earlier to beat Brian Masters when it comes to matters of the mind.
