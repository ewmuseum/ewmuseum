************************************************************************ (The main conference room at NPWA Towers in Toronto, Ontario, Canada is filled to capacity with television crews and print journalists from all around the globe. Up on the stage, a podium is set up on the left with the NPWA logo emblazoned upon it. Attached to the podium is a large number of microphones, representing the major American networks - ABC, CBS, NBC, Fox - as well as ESPN, TSN and the CBC. On the right of the stage is a long table, at which are seated seven men. The two closest to the podium are immediately recognizable as NPWA CEO, Vince Dutt, Jr. and NPWA President Billy Joe Tunney. Next to them is a man wearing jeans, a black shirt, cowboy boots, silver shades with cocky smirk on his face. The others are unknown NPWA executives. President Tunney stands and walks to the podium.) BJT: Ladies and gentlemen, it is my distinct pleasure to be here today to make a major announcement regarding the future of the NPWA. As you all know, the NPWA already has the greatest and most talented superstars in professional wrestling today. And we are constantly striving to maintain our spot as the number one wrestling organization in the world. That is why we are here today, to welcome a man who really needs no introduction, and to witness his signing of an exclusive contract with the NPWA. This wrestler has been a champion in every organization he has wrestled in, and we are extremely pleased that he has decided to wrestle in the NPWA. He is going to come up here and say a few words, then take a few questions from the press. Ladies and gentlemen, here is "Bad Boy" Mark Bagwell! (The man in jeans gets up and walks over to the podium as cameras flash, ignoring the outstretched hand of President Tunney.) BBMB: Thanks very much Tunney, and thank you all for being here today... NOT! You all might be asking yourselves why has the greatest wrestler in the world decided to sign on with the NPWA? Well that's a simple question to answer. The greatest wrestler in the world needs to be in the greatest federation around, and in my opinion, that's the NPWA. As you know, I've gotten a rap recently as being a multi fed wrestler...well that's all changed now. All those other federations I've been in just don't have the competition that I want, and from what I've seen of the NPWA on TV, this looks like the place to be. All my other contracts with the other promotions are all up at the end of this month, and I'm not re-signing with any of them. The NPWA and the NFW...if that promotion ever gets started...will be the only two federations that I will be wrestling in. So now if outsiders want a crack at me, they got to come to the NPWA for it. (Bagwell takes of his shades and ever so flamboyantly leans against the podium) BBMB: You know, I've won a lot of titles in my career, some of which are worth mentioning, like the FoPEW Light-Heavyweight title and the ICW World title...others aren't. I hope...whoa! What am I saying...WHEN I win the NPWA World Title, it will be the greatest championship of my career. I was this close to getting into the Hall of Fame just a few weeks ago, but due to politics and my so called "Bad" behavior, I wasn't admitted in. I'm sure when I kick everyone's ASS in the NPWA, that will guarantee my induction.
