[NPWA] Attention Women's E-Wrestling Fans!

20 January 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Apologies for the multiple posts, but I blew the subject line the first time. - [name withheld]) ATTENTION: E-Wrestling handlers and bookers interested in women's wrestling! Women's wrestling, which often offers better quality action than men's wrestling, is a dying form in North America. However, the NPWA wants to do something about that. The NANet Professional Wrestling Association, one of the finest wrestling organizations on the Internet (at least in our own opinions), is looking for help to successfully launch a Women's division. Like we do everything else, we're looking to make this division the very best it can possibly be, and to that end, we want to find the best people to be involved. We're looking for a few good people to help in the start-up of the division. Specifically, we're looking for someone to "book" and write the division, at least at first. As a member of the NPWA Booking Committee, you will be supported as much as you like and/or need in joining into the NPWA Executive, and enjoy creative control over the division, which will likely start off small with 8 - 10 wrestlers. Where it goes after that will be in your capable hands, with support from us. Your role will be similar to that of current NPWA Tag Team Division Head, [name withheld]. You will be responsible for keeping in touch with handlers, making sure their experience is good and handling complaints should they occur. You will plan angles and pushes for the Women's division, and be responsible for writing two or three matches a week (two at the start) in full-commentary format for the NPWA's weekly Sunday night supershow, NPWA Mayhem. The successful candidate should be a mature, independent worker who functions well in a team environment. The longstanding tradition in the NPWA is that the organization runs better with the combined creative efforts of three or more dedicated, serious individuals. That's not to say we don't have fun. We are an organization run by young, energetic and fun-loving people, and that spirit shows through among the committee members. Preference will be given to people interested in the job who have experience in writing and booking e-wrestling, particularly with women's e-wrestling. A strong list of contacts among women e-wrestlers would be a serious benefit. If you're a fedhead running a small women's promotion, you may want to consider amalgamating yourself under the NPWA banner. You'll still have editorial control over the division, in consultation with the other members of the Committee, and you'll benefit from having to spend less time on "overhead" issues, such as building weekly shows, maintaining mailing lists and Web sites, which will be taken care of for you by the NPWA. At the same time, the NPWA will be searching to gather talent for the start of the Women's division, and to that end, we are, for the first time, accepting applications from Women wrestlers. If you handle a female athlete in this great sport of e-wrestling, please contact us if you're interested in starting a new tradition that will walk hand-in-hand with the existing traditions of the NPWA, which traces its roots back to 1993. Please be aware, though, that the plans to launch the division are still tentative. If we do launch the division, we want to launch the best women's division we possibly can, and if we cannot find the correct person to organize it, we will delay our plans to launch it until such time as we find the correct person. If you think you'd be right for booking the NPWA Women's division, handling in it, or just would like more information on the NPWA, please contact [name withheld] at [detail withheld] , or visit the NPWA Online Web site at http://npwa.simplenet.com/ . We look forward to hearing from you, and sincerely hope that in our next announcement regarding the Women's division, we'll be able to proudly introduce you as a part of what we hope will turn out to be one of the most exciting presences in women's e-wrestling, much as the NPWA is currently one of the most exciting presences in men's e-wrestling. Sincerely, [name withheld] Executive Vice President, Wrestling Operations, NANet Professional Wrestling Association
