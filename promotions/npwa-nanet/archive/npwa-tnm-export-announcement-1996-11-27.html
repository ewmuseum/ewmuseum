[TNM] New Export Files + More (NPWA roster distributed)

27 November 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
First of all let me thank all of you who participated in the Arena Contest. It will be closing on December 1, and the top three will be getting their complimentary version of the next TNM as promised. If you tried to visit the TNM Homepage at some point yesterday, you probably didn't get there. The reason for this is that our provider, DFN, shut off their main router for maintenance work. Sorry for any inconveniences this may have caused. Two new export files have been uploaded to the TNM Export File Page a few hours ago. Number 72 contains Steve McMichael, Kevin Greene, Mark Henry, Glacier, Billy Kidman, Rocky Maivia and a few made-up wrestlers, while file 73 contains the complete roster of the NPWA, one of the great e-wrestling leagues out there (check out http://npwa.home.ml.org for more info). A new bugfix for TNM V6.2 will be uploaded to the Download Page on Monday morning along with the Update File for December and January. I've had problems delivering mail to some registered users lately, so if you're a registered user of TNM V6.2plus, you'll be getting a free freshup in late December. It'll contain an all-new tournament editor in circuit mode with which making tournaments will be a breeze. Remember, if you haven't registered, don't e-mail me about this freshup, as it's only available to registered users. Finally, if there's anything you want to announce or that you're offering or searching for, I encourage you to use the TNM Billboard on the TNM Homepage. There haven't been too many postings lately, and if this trend continues, I may be forced to remove it soon. Thanks for your time. ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- [name withheld] TNMlyger on IRC e-mail: [detail withheld] TNM V7.0...
