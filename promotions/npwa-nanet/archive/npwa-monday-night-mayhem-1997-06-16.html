JBN: It can't be, puke... the stipulations say "there must be a winner!" EW: Casius has finished off Warfield and climbs into the ring... he grabs the ref and powerbombs him!!! This is sick! Gan has just knocked Lord Midheaven out and calls Jack Hammer into the ring, telling him to go ahead and pin Midheaven. Hammer laughs and climbs into the ring... JBN: Orange and Green are attacking Hammer! EW: Hammer's a big man, but Green and Orange are bigger and there's two of them! Now Hammer's out and Blade was already out! And look at the Perfect Role Models, hands raised in victory as the crowd pelts them with garbage. Kenshin is rolling Lord Midheaven on top of Blade while Akira dives off of the top turnbuckle down onto Green and Orange and begins pounding on them. Now Kenshin joins him and all four men are going at it. JBN: But the ref's still knocked out! There's no one to count! EW: Wait a second... someone's coming, maybe another ref... no! Here come Dave Diamond and Christian Steele! They slide into the ring, roll Lord Midheaven off of Blade then roll Blade on top of Lord Midheaven! This is crazy! JBN: Now they're climbing out of the ring and heading for the melee going on beside the ring. EW: Diamond jumps Nobunaga from behind while Steele joins Akira Sasaki in a double team of Gan Green! I knew the tag team situation in the NPWA was getting volitile, but this is ridiculous! JBN: It's about to get even moreso, puke, because here comes a couple of the big guns! EW: The Hellrazors, Valtharius and Wicked Lester are making their way down the aisle... Valtharius climbs into the ring while Lester, seemingly drawn by the blood being shed in the donneybrook on the other side of the ring race around and dives right into the middle of it! JBN: And look at Valtharius! EW: He's picked Blade up off of Lord Midheaven... Gladiator Heartpunch! He drops Blade like a ton of bricks and then pulls Midheaven *and* Warfield on top of him! JBN: I think every tag team in the NPWA is out here fighting it out, puke! This is great! JBN: I was wrong puke, not every tag team is out here... look who's standing in the aisle watching this chaos! EW: It's the Muslim Massacre! General Arain and Colonel Mustafa are staying in the aisle away from the action as all of the men who would like to get their hands on those gold belts they've got around their waists are involved in a brawl of epic purportions!
