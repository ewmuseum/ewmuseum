[NPWA] Saturday Night Nightmare (Jan. 18/97)

18 January 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
WARNING! WARNING! WARNING! WARNING! WARNING! WARNING! WARNING! WARNING! The following program contains action of a graphic nature. You'll want to have your VCR rolling so that you don't miss one second of the senseless violence, gore or mayhem...and occasional acts of athleticism. VIEWER INSANITY IS ADVISED ************************************************************************ <As the NPWA logo swirls onto the screen, a baritone-voiced voiceover speaks> "The NANet Professional Wrestling Association - The Revolutionary Force In Electronic Sports Entertainment For The Past Three Years!" <Screen shows two kids walking up to a rather spooky looking house in the middle of the night. The weather is horrible. Thunder and lightning clash in the sky as eerie organ music plays in the background. The camera switches to a face shot of the kids as they slowly turn the doorknob to the front door of the house. The door opens with a creak. The children turn ghostly white, and turn screaming and running away down the street. The camera turns inside the house to see the Saturday Night Nightmare logo materialize at the foot of an old staircase.> ************************************************************************ <Crowd shot of a packed arena with the words :"LIVE -LAVAL, PQ" flashing on the screen> BB: "Good evening fans and mutants! This is Bob Buchanan welcoming you to a second edition of Saturday Night NIGHTMARE, live from Laval in la Belle Province, Quebec! With me again this week are Mark Smart and Mr. Sandman!" MS: "That's right Bobby-B! And we are psyched after the success of last week's episode, ya know whut ahm sayin'? The fans were wantin' a little sumpin'-sumpin', and that's what they got, boyz!" Mr.S: "Your staunch misappropriation of the Englush vernacular is both aseptic and benighted, but perchance your lack of pedagogics above the basal peg would expound this quagmire." MS: "WHAT?!?" BB: "You silly twit! What he said was...um..he said...er...Sandman, what *did* you say?" Mr.S: "Precisely." BB: "I see..well anyway...Fans, we've got an awesome card tonight!" MS: "That's right! We're gonna see the good Colonel, Buddy Lee Jarrett in action against the Anabolic Warrior...provided the Warrior shows up." BB: "In our Moldie Oldie, we'll see The Ultimate Dragon take on Slice in a match that aired April 8th, 1994. Also tonight, we will see Mr. Freestyle take on Mr. Y, and Big Timber take on the Hutt Brothers." MS: "They're still around? Hell, I hope they BOTH kill each other!" BB: "Irregardless, our main event will feature Jurisprudence facing off against Gan Green, but first, let's go to some pre-recorded comments from Jurisprudence." ************************************************************************ [The setting, JP is playing a few holes in the Glen Abbey Golf club] JP: Tonight I will be facing that Jack Nicklaus wannabee Gan Green. He thinks that his "Hole in one" attitude will reign supreme over justice. Well he has another thing coming. [Tiger Woods approaches J.P. and observes the tee off] JP: Fore! [with a mighty swing with his 1 Wood, remarkably he whacks the ball over 500ft.] TW: Holy cow, what power, how did you ever do that sir? JP: Years in the gym, years of studying in the university, that's all it takes. TW: You hit it better than John Daly. JP: That bum, that no good arrogant drunk, he reminds me of you. TW: Excuse me? JP: Listen, stop hanging around me and being a social climber, I have no time for you. Why don't you hang around with losers like Gan Green. [Tiger Woods leaves furiously after JP's insult] JP: Bring on your golf clubs and caddy Gan Green, it ain't gonna make no difference. Bottom line is you will serving hard time for stealing golf clubs at the PGA shop and for putting my number one client Steve Stone out of commission. Nobody gets away from the long arm of the law and gets away. See these guns (flexes his muscles), I'm gonna drive your body 500 yards into the green mat, sink my putts by whacking you around the ring, and then make a special necklace out of your golfclubs and I will personally wrap it around your neck. This is courtesy from the ATM Enterprises. You are not safe from the Death Sentence, and nobody has survived its after effects. Once I get you in the ring, you'd better take up professional golf as your fulltime job and stay out of the ring. I hear that the Mighty Puke will be commentating at ringside. There is a fine line between professionalism and jealousy. You just crossed both of them and you have a serious problem with dealing on how justice is served in the NPWA. Just remember one thing, I am the law, the system and justice of the NPWA. You are nothing but small potatoes, a donut guzzling hick cop who has no clue on how to serve justice. You were sloppy and lacking enough evidence on the Akira hearings. Just to piss you off, I have given Akira Sasaki full pardon and have reinstated him back to active duties in the NPWA. As for you, I am charging you of the following offences: false arrest, fabricating evidence (fraud), 2 counts of assault: inflicted on Mr. Sasaki and yours truly a superior high court judge, insubordination to your superior, the list goes on. This doesn't look good for you Boss, if you give yourself up and turn in your badge, I may go light on you. If you don't comply, I will use necessary force to the full extent of the law to punish you. I am aware of your potential interference, just to warn you, as the old saying goes: "Do not play with fire, sooner or later you will get burned!" Just remember who you are dealing with. Gan Green, I will see you in the ring and make sure you score a +30 over par. [JP approaches the ball he just shot and scores an eagle] ************************************************************************ MS: "I don't think I want to be around when Jurisprudence lays it on Gan Green! And with Mighty Boss at ringside, you know it's gonna be trouble!" BB: "Well Mark, maybe you could hide under the ring again like last week. Fans, let's go to David Perry now for the first match...." ************************************************************************ DP: "Ladies and Gentlemen! This match is scheduled for one fall! Currently in the ring, weighing in at 325 lbs, from Warrior International Correspondence School, here is THE ANABOLIC WARRIOR! <Warrior starts running around shaking the ropes> His opponent, weighing in at 260 lbs, from Biloxi, Missouri, but currently residing in Las Vegas, Nevada, here is COLONEL BUDDY LEE JARRETT!!!!" <Jarrett comes down the aisle to the ring to "Viva Las Vegas", looking focused as always.> BB: "And they lock up as the bell rings to start the first match of our second show." MS: "Ahh..Bobby...you're such a softie...I think I'm going to cry...NOT!" BB: "The Anabolic Warrior whips Colonel Buddy Lee Jarrett into the ropes. Warrior goes for a powerslam, but Jarrett greets him with a lariat. And now the Colonel explodes on the juice pig...I mean fine competitor in the ring. Back suplex! Elbowdrop! Short lariat! And an early pin attempt. Referee 'Blind' Willie Feldman counts: One, two, kickout by the Warrior." MS: "It'll take more'n'that to knock that sucka down!" BB: "Jarrett nails Warrior with a short clothesline. Warrior is backing off. Now Jarrett whips the Warrior into the turnbuckle, but Warrior reverses it. Warrior charges in after him, but Jarrett lifts his leg and nails him with a short clothesline again. Jarrett takes him down with a lariat, and pick him up again quickly for a spinebuster slam. And another pin attempt by the Colonel." MS: "Are you sure he's not a General?" BB: "Don't even start...The Warrior kicks out and catches Jarrett with a suplex. Now he's hit him with a gutwrench suplex." MS: "TWO SUPLEXES IN A ROW?!?! The Warrior must have been watching the Moldie Oldie tapes!" BB: "The Anabolic Warrior throws Buddy Lee Jarrett out of the ring. Now he nails Jarrett with an elbow and throws him back into the ring. The Warrior places Jarrett on the turnbuckle...another superplex! Now the Warrior is shaking the ropes again." MS: "He better forget the ropes and concentrate on Jarrett!" BB: "For once you're right Mark as the Warrior turns around to meet a punch to the chops by Jarrett, followed by a rather low knee-lift. And the Colonel is back into control!" Mr.S: "'We must take the current when it serves, Or lose our ventures.'; William Shakespeare; Julius Caesar, Act: IV, Scene: iii, Line: 222" MS: "What are you talking about now? You are nuts man!" BB: "Jarrett has regained the upper hand and is laying the boots to the Warrior. He's kicking at the knee of the Warrior. He goes to the corner..elbow drop to the throat from the second rope. He picks the Warrior up. Swinging neckbreaker!" MS: "He's got 'im..." BB: "You may be right as Jarrett slaps on the Whistlin' Dixie. Warrior tries to hold on, but gives in. This one's over." DP: <Over the P.A.> "The winner of the match, by submission, COLONEL BUDDY LEE JARRETT." BB: "The match is over, but Jarrett isn't letting go of the figure-four! He's signalling for the mic!" CBLJ: "You see this Rocker?!? You see the pain this ol'boy is in?!? I'm comin' for you, son, and I'm gettin' my belt back. But I ain't gonna let go of your leg boy 'til I put you outta my misery!" BB: "And Jarret finally lets go as the EMTs rush the ring to carry the Warrior off." MS: "Maybe his doctor will no-show and we won't have to see his sorry face in the NPWA again!" BB: "That's horrid Mark! Fans, I'd like you to know that I'm sure the good physicians here at the NPWA will have The Anabolic Warrior back on his feet in no time." MS: "Yo, Bobby-B! Do you know the difference between a brown-noser and a poop-head?" BB: "I'm sure I don't Mark." MS: "DEPTH PERCEPTION!! And guy, you better get your eyes checked soon cuz you is DAMN close to stickin' it in there, you know whut ahm sayin'?" BB: "You are vile! Sandman, help me out here!" Mr.S: " You seem to be doing just fine on your own, Bob." BB: "Fans, let's take you know to the question of the week..." ************************************************************************ THE PEOPLE WANT TO KNOW... Last week, we asked several NPWA superstars the question: What is your worst NIGHTMARE? Here's their answers! The Cougar: "My worst nightmare...would be to fail my master...the great King Ramesses the Second. No mortal terror...could compare..to the wrath..of one who is...a god!" Colonel Mustafa: "I have had alot of nightmares, but the scariest moments were of reality for me. Standing guard in the night shift at a military camp. That's a nightmare come true. But as far as the NPWA goes, my worst nightmare was when those 2 Resident Evil punks attacked my dear tag-partner and best friend, the General Arain, and also our loyal teacher, the Shiek. After that, you don't get bad dreams. Those badboy wannabe's will go down, so I can get back to the wonderful patriotic dreams I used to have." Colonel Buddy Lee Jarrett: "Nightmare? Son, th' Colonel went through enough nightmares in the '80s, not to mention th' fashions. All I have now are dreams, m'boy, dreams of th' New Wave conquering th' NPWA, an' those dreams are about to come true." The Man They Call NUG: (Wiping face with back of hand, mustard everywhere.) "My nightmare? Once when I was little, I got snowed in the barn, and I mean, like SNOWED in. 15 feet of snow, all piled up in front of the door, and I couldn't get out! I had nightmares for weeks after that! (glazed eyes as he recounts the terror) Almost five days with no hot dogs... (snapping back in to reality) Anyway, I don't mind so much anymore, if I have a goal in mind. Like this Pancake Eating tournament. I'll probably stay off food for a couple of days so I'm REALLY hungry. (thinking) hmmm.... but, you all know that I start counting my meals after 9 am, so anything before that is "empty food". I hope the ToastyMan has lots of syrup... (thinking again) What if he has no syrup? (shudders) Now THAT'S a nightmare! (Powers down five hot dogs. Obviously, the interview is over.)" The Silent Striker: "My worst nightmare has already become a reality. My bride to be had vanished without a trace . . ." * DIRECTOR'S NOTE: Someone sent in an answer to the question, but unfortunately, it was misfiled and lost. Mr.S: "The fears of those mortals are petty to the nightmares conceived by a mastermind such as myself." MS: "Personally, being locked in a room with Sandy here would give me nightmares!" BB: "I would tend to agree with you on that one, Mark. Fans, let's take you back now to April 8, 1994 as the Ultimate Dragon faces off against Hall of Fame Nominee, Slice. It was at this time that Slice was recovering from a brutal beating at the hands of current NPWA World Champion, Big Daddy Cool Amoral. Amoral had decided to cash in on another Hall of Fame Nominee, the Fishmonger's offer of $500 per stitch that was put in Slice's face. Let's take you back with Ed Whailen and Johnny B. Nastee to Southern NWC Mayhem..." ************************************************************************ The following was the Main Event of a TV Taping in Weenie Hollow, Georgia for SNWC Mayhem. Your announcers: Johnny B. Nastee and Ed Whailen. EW: Well, here's our main event, and it's going to be a great one... JBN: Shut up puke! I'm the color man! It's gonna be a great one, Slice against wrestling's most dangerous mercenary, The Ultimate Dragon. I think Slice may have made a mistake here, going against the Dragon with a bounty on his head, I mean face. EW: Yes, and with Richie Rich's cheque bouncing, I'm sure the Dragon is in need of cash. It won't be an easy night for Slice. Let's go to our guest ring announcer, Dexter Manley. DM: Uh, too nite da... ma..in ee..v..n. Ah hell, I can't read this, just get on with it! Down the aisle walks the Ultimate Dragon. As he passes the announcing booth, he grabs the mike. UD: This is my kind of night! Two paydays, one from SNWC, and one from The Fishmonger! As Ultimate Dragon climbs into the ring, a roar rises up from the crowd, the camera pans back to the curtain at the dressing room area and out comes Slice. EW: He's wearing a mask! JBN: This bounty thing must have him really scared! He's wearing a protective mask! As Slice gets to the announcer's booth he grabs the mike. Slice: In case you're wondering, I'm wearing the mask until the stitches come out from that cowardly attack from the Amoral. Once I can again show my beautiful face, in all its glory, the mask comes off. Nobody is going to see me in public with a bandage. Slice makes his way into the ring, to loud mumurs of disappointment from the ladies in the crowd. EW: Slice feels pretty strong about not showing his face with stitches. JBN: Yeah, but if Bash and Fishmonger have their way, he'll be wearing that mask for a very long time. EW: And here's the bell, Dragon rushes him in the corner, and they lock up. The ref calls for a break, Slice lets go and Dragon nails him with a left hand. Dragon throws Slice to the middle of the ring with a hip toss... he slaps on a chinlock. And now he's trying to take Slice's mask off! JBN: Seems pretty obvious, Dragon wants to get the mask off and start collecting the bounty. EW: Dragon is pulling hard on the mask, and Slice is holding on! The Dragon has lifted Slice to his feet by the mask! Slice has gotten his hands around the dragon's neck and... WHAM! JBN: Wow, Slice just dropped to his butt, driving the top of his head up into the Dragon's chin. EW: Dragon falls flat on his back, Slice hooks a leg and the ref counts, 1-2-3 Slice wins! JBN: Looks like the Dragon should have worried more about the match and less about the bounty. Now let's go to Todd Peddle-Swill back in the dressing room area. TPS: Thanks Johnny, I'm here outside the dressing rooms with The Sayanorah Kid and his manager, The Aloha Kid. Well kids, that was some match we just saw. What do you think about that bounty Bash and The Fishmonger have put on Slice's face? SK: I think they went too far. Suggesting that they could pay tax free cash is just too much. Everyone should pay their taxes. TPS: Here comes Slice now. Slice! Can I get a word with you? OK, here he comes now. Slice: Hello Todd, Kid, what are you doing here? AK: We wanted to tell you that you have our support in this bounty thing. And Slice, I'd like to give you a big kiss for luck. Slice: Well thank you, here just let me lift my mask up a little. My bandage is on the forehead, so no one will see it. WHAM! TPS: Oh My God! The Sayanorah Kid has just decked Slice with his adding machine, and he's kicking Slice in the ribs repeatedly. SK: Use the shoe! The Shoe! TPS: Aloha Kid has taken off her six inch stiletto heeled shoes. Sayanorah has grabbed Slice by the hair, and is holding his head up. Aloha has the shoe in her hand and she pounces on Slice. Oh No! Slice is cut on the left cheek, can you get a shot of that! He's cut badly! Here comes the Mat Man out of the dressing room. Sayanorah takes a swing at him but misses. Mat Man grabs Slice and drags him into the dressing room and slams the door. AK: Alright! After that scum walked off with the Shotputter, he had that coming. And I can always use some tax free cash! SK: That's right! And here's the man that's gonna give it! TPS: The Fishmonger! FM: That's right! See what happens when you cross us? You pay big time Slice, and you're not through paying, not by a long shot! SK: Speaking of paying... FM: Of course, its $500 a stitch. We don't know the final count yet, but that was at least worth $2,500. After the hospital report, if there's any more you'll get it. Fishmonger rummages in the many pockets of his coat and hands a wad of bills to Aloha Kid. He also hands her a pair of red leather seven inch spike heeled shoes. FM: You broke the heel of those shoes, here's some new ones. Can I have the old broken one? I'm starting a collection, all the objects used to cut Slice. This will look nice in my trophy case right next to the Amoral's camera. AK: Sounds like a fair trade to me. Pleasure doing business with you. Fishmonger, Aloha, and Sayanorah walk off to their waiting limo, laughing all the way. TPS: We're out of Time! For Johnny B. Nastee, and Ed Whailen, this is Todd Peddle-Swill saying so long from SNWC Mayhem! ************************************************************************ BL: "Bruce Lord here, and what an exciting match that was.
