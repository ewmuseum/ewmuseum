[The MC introduces Vince Dutt, Jr. CEO, NPWA Inc.] VDJ: Thank you very much. The NPWA is very proud to be part of Superstar Summit II being held... where is it being held this week? Mongolia? Oh, London is it? I guess that memo will reach my desk sometime in the next year or so. The NPWA is a major power in the world of wrestling. Oh to hell with it - we *are* the major power. Since our creation in 1993 we have been on the cutting edge. With each and every event we secure a firmer hold on the top of the Professional Wrestling mountain. For those of you who haven't seen the NPWA in action before you are in for a treat. For those of you who have seen us, you ain't seen nothing yet! We are producing our own pay-per-view event for this occassion entitled "April Assault: Vendetta in Vegas...Warfare at Wembley." Yes for the first time in wrestling history a pay per view will be held in two different continents at the same time. The viewers of Superstar Summit will be able to see the matches that generate from Wembley Stadium. Of course the people that purchase the NPWA pay per view will see it all live! This situation arose when we spent considerable advertising and promotional dollars advertising the Las Vegas show only to NOT BE TOLD of this move to Wembley Stadium by Summit organizers. I guess it is true the rest of the Summit conglomeration are afraid of us. Appearing at April Assault will be almost every current NPWA star we have. We are currently looking at about an 11-12 match card previewed by our standard setting one hour preshow which will feature another 2-3 matches. Approximately 5-6 matches will take place at Wembley Stadium with the rest of the card live from Caesar's Palace in Las Vegas. At this time we are pleased to announce that the main event for April Assault will be for the World Heavyweight Title. A match featuring the Champion, Big Daddy Cool Amoral vs. the man who pinned the champ in that very controversial match, Locutus. Stay tuned to WTN and our fantastic NPWA programs for further details or hop on the web and find us at npwa.simplenet.com . As for the Summit Cup... well we really felt that it would be unfair to the rest of the competition to put one of our current stars in the competition so not only will you see the current and future NPWA stars at Superstar Summit II but one from the past as well.
