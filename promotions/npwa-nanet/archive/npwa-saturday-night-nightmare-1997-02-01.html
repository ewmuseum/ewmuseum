[NPWA] Saturday Night Nightmare (Feb. 1/97)

2 February 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
WARNING! WARNING! WARNING! WARNING! WARNING! WARNING! WARNING! WARNING! The following program contains action of a graphic nature. You'll want to have your VCR rolling so that you don't miss one second of the senseless violence, gore or mayhem...and occasional acts of athleticism. VIEWER INSANITY IS ADVISED ************************************************************************ <As the NPWA logo swirls onto the screen, a baritone-voiced voiceover speaks> "The NANet Professional Wrestling Association - The Revolutionary Force In Electronic Sports Entertainment For The Past Three Years!" <Screen shows two kids walking up to a rather spooky looking house in the middle of the night. The weather is horrible. Thunder and lightning clash in the sky as eerie organ music plays in the background. The camera switches to a face shot of the kids as they slowly turn the doorknob to the front door of the house. The door opens with a creak. The children turn ghostly white, and turn screaming and running away down the street. The camera turns inside the house to see the Saturday Night Nightmare logo materialize at the foot of an old staircase.> ************************************************************************ <Screen is filled with a crowd shot as "LIVE - HALIFAX, NS" flashes on the screen. Camera pans over to the broadcast position where Bob Buchanan and Mark Smart are standing.> BB: "Good evening everyone and welcome to another exciting edition of Saturday Night NIGHTMARE!!! We are live from the lovely city of Halifax, Nova Scotia. I'm Bob Buchanan, and with me as always is Mark Smart." MS: "Yo Bobby-B! Whutzup?" BB: "But one familiar face who normally graces us with his presence, but is absent tonight, is Mr. Sandman." MS: "That's right, the man from the Sands of Time is out strapping on his tights one more time to give the Coroner a little sumpin'-sumpin' in the ring, which means that all the brothers and sisters out there is gonna finally be able to understand what's goin' on, ya know whut ahm sayin'?" BB: "No, I don't. We've also got Ramesses II in action." MS: "And let's not forget the best team in the NPWA today, the Perfect Role Models!" BB: "If they were the best, they'd have a championship, now wouldn't they? We'll also have all our usual features, such as the Cranium Cracker, Question and Answer, and the Moldie Oldie match. But first, let's go to Dave Perry for our first match." ************************************************************************ DP: "This tagteam contest is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first, already in the ring, at a combined weight of 529 lbs, from Michigan, here is Rick and Scott, THE WHINER BROTHERS!!!!!" <Rick and Scott start posing in the ring with their ICS jackets on.> MS: "Man, those guys need to lay off the juice! They can barely move with all those muscles in the way!" DP: "Their opponents, from the Green Acres Country Club and accompanied by the 'legendary' Cheat-Cheat-Rodriguez, at a combined weight of 590 lbs, here are Mr. Wrestling and Gan Green, THE PERFECT ROLE MODELS!!!!!" <The Role Models come down the aisle to boos as the theme to 'Happy Gilmour' plays.> BB: "And the Role Models waste no time as Mr. Wrestling attacks Scott Whiner before the bell. Mr. Wrestling nails him with a double underhook suplex. Mr. Wrestling takes him down with the Cross-faced chicken wing." MS: "How can Mr. Wrestling even bend that guys arms with all that juice in 'em?" BB: "Mr. Wrestling drags Scott over to his corner and tags out to Gan Green. Gan Green and Mr. Wrestling hit Scott Whiner with a double kneedrop. Now they team up for a double Hotshot. Scott rolls over and tags Rick as Mr. Wrestling finally gets out of the ring." MS: "Enter juice-pig number two!" BB: " Gan Green kicks Rick in the head. Rick shakes it off and nails him with a piledriver. Rick Whiner goes for a flying kneedrop, but Green rolls away and tags Mr. Wrestling. Mr. Wrestling and Gan Green whip Rick Whiner into the ropes...double backdrop by the Perfect Role Models! And Gan Green leaves the ring." MS: "These guys are gonna get the tag belts yet! I mean, with double-teams like that, how can they lose?" BB: "Mr. Wrestling is yelling at a fan outside the ring and is quickly greeted with a clothesline from Rick Whiner. Rick tags out to his brother, Scott. Mr. Wrestling whips Scott Whiner into the turnbuckle, but Scott Whiner reverses it." MS: "What's Cheat-Cheat doing?" BB: "Cheat-Cheat is digging in that golf bag of his as Scott crushes Mr. Wrestling with a shoulder-block in the corner. Scott Whiner tags out to Rick Whiner, as Rodriguez pulls a driver out of the bag and places it beside the ringpost in Green's corner!" MS: "Actually Bobby-B, I think that's a 4-wood..." BB: "Rick and Scott whip Mr. Wrestling into the ropes...double backdrop by the Whiners! Now referee John Gibbons is trying to get Scott out of the ring." MS: "And Green decides to practice his golf swing!" BB: "Gan Green just came into the ring and nailed Rick with a low blow with that golf club. Rick's eyes are bulging out of their sockets and he's gasping for air! Green tosses the club back to Cheat-Cheat and gets back to the outside just in time for referee Gibbons to turn around and see Mr. Wrestling tag Green in." MS: "Like Green says, 'Cheaters ALWAYS win!'" BB: "Gan Green practically strolls over to Rick as he pick him up for the Double Bogey and the pin. John Gibbons counts: One, two, three, it's all over." <Dave Perry grabs the mic> DP: "The winners of the bout, THE PERFECT ROLE MODELS!!!!!" MS: "They ain't done yet! They're laying the boots and the clubs to the Whiners!" BB: "What a despicable display of cheating and poor sportsmanship! Fans let's go to the Question and Answer segment while we get security in here and prepare for the next match..." ************************************************************************ THE PEOPLE WANT TO KNOW... Last week, we asked several NPWA superstars the question: What do you think of the new NPWA mascot, Bucky the Beaver? Here's their answers! Coroner: "Bucky the NPWA Beaver...in his current form, he is nothing more than a fur hat waiting to happen, but give the Pyramid but 2 weeks with the rodent...our elixir will transform the creature into a mascot worthy of adulation and respect." Colonel Buddy Lee Jarrett: Weeeellll, ahhh think it's faaaar too Canadian. It was absolutely ridiculous t' choose such an ugly, smelly, mangy, flea-infested, wood-eatin' furball for a mascot. Jus' when th' New Wave is tryin' t' help make th' NPWA more international, Dutt goes ahead an' hires "Bucky the Beaver". Ahhhh hope that this damned beaver never appears again, but if he does, ahhh may introduce him to m' favorite mascot... "Pedro th' Pitbull". Hahahaha... Cougar: "In a word.....LUNCH!" Next week, we'll answer the question What are you going to get your loved one for Valentine's Day? NPWA wrestlers, please answer this question by mailing your response to [detail withheld] on the Internet, or via a private message in NANet Private Mail to [name withheld] ************************************************************************ BB: "Folks we are back with what should be a good singles match as Ramesses II takes on Jeff Jarhead." MS: "Yep, the Pyramid is in full force here tonight with two big matches on the card." BB: "Let's take you to Dave Perry for the introductions..." ************************************************************************ DP: "This match is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first, already in the ring, weighing in at 235 lbs, here is JEFF JARHEAD!!!!!" <Jeff Jarhead struts around the ring as his song, "I Ain't Great" plays.> DP: "His opponent, from the town of Luxor, in the city of Thebes, Egypt, and accompanied by his manager, Queen Nofretari, he weighs in at 325 lbs, here is RAMESSES II!!!!!" <Ramesses and Nofretari enter to the sound of the theme to "The Mummy"> BB: "They lock up. Ramesses takes him down with a forearm to the back. He whips Jarhead into the ropes. Jarhead nails him with a bulldog headlock. Jarhead follows with a snap suplex, and now throws Ramesses II out of the ring!" MS: "WHOA! The big man from the Nile is in serious trouble here!" BB: "Ramesses is shaking his head! He can't believe it himself! He turns around and is greeted by a plancha by Jarhead. They're brawling on the floor. Ramesses II whips Jarhead into the guardrail. He sets up Jarhead on a table. Ramesses tries to drive Jeff Jarhead through the table a flying somersault splash, but Jarhead moves out of the way. Ramesses crashes hard into the table. Now Jarhead nails Ramesses with a belly-to-back suplex on the outside. Jarhead rolls Ramesses back into the ring and follows in after him." MS: "Looks like Ramesses is still shaken by the loss to Nobunaga!" BB: "That may be the case as Jeff Jarhead executes a bulldog headlock on the big Egyptian. Jarhead runs into the ropes, and is hit hard by an elbow from Ramesses. Ramesses whips Jarhead into the ropes, but Jarhead nails him with a clothesline!" MS: "Nofretari doesn't look impressed!" BB: "Not at all as she gets up on the apron to yell at Jarhead. Jarhead goes for the bait and confronts Nofretari." MS: "He's forgotten about Ramesses!" BB: "He certainly has as Ramesses slowly gets up to his feet. He sees Jarhead and charges in for a clothesline.." MS: "LOOK OUT!!!" BB: "Jarhead moved out of the way at the last second and Ramesses ended up hitting Nofretari full force! Nofretari went flying off the apron and into the guardrail! Ramesses realizes what he's done and jumps outside immediately to check on his Queen!" MS: "The ref is counting him out!" BB: "Jarhead is prancing around the ring...OH MY!!! Tyr just shot up through the ring in a cloud of smoke! He nails Jarhead with a shot to the groin, followed by a fist to the throat! I think his fist was loaded because Jarhead goes down hard! And now he's gone again just as fast!" MS: "Here come the Cadavre Connection!" BB: "The Coroner is checking on Nofretari while the Cougar is shoving Ramesses back into the ring. Ramesses gets back in as referee John Gibbons counts nine." MS: "But it don't look like he's sticking around!" BB: "Apparently not as Ramesses tries to exit again to check on Nofretari. But Cougar's not letting him leave as he hops up onto the apron between his King and Queen and points over to Jarhead. Ramesses looks a tad confused, but fumbles over to the now prone Jarhead for the cover. Gibbons counts: One, two, three. It's over!" <Dave Perry grabs the mic.> DP: "The winner of this bout, RAMESSES II!!!!!!" MS: "Some win! He clobbered his own manager!" BB: "The Pyramid of Pain, minus Tyr, head back to the dressing rooms with the Cougar carrying Nofretari and the Coroner lecturing Ramesses. Folks, let's take you now to Bruce Lords for the Moldie Oldie match." ************************************************************************ BL: "Thank you Bob. As you recall from last week, we are in the middle of our complete coverage of the first ever tag team title tournament. We now go to the second first round match featuring the Kentucky Corndogs facing the Godzilla Warriors..." ************************************************************************ A quick recap from last week's NWC SuperAction: Billy Bob Bureau and Golly Gee Goldhar were giggling in the dressing room when the NWC cameras caught up with them. "Look at whut we got," said Billy Bob. He held a photo. "Know whut this is?" smirked Golly Gee. "It's a photo of Gamma when he wuz jest a lil' feller." "And thet's his dog next to him - yew know, the lil pooch whut got hisself launched into orbit?" said Billy Bob. "Don't yew mean the pup whut got hisself turned into eagle mix?" said Golly, as both men broke up with laughter. When they wiped away the tears and composed themselves, Billy Bob spoke agin - whoops, again. "Now I hear that this photo is the last whut wuz ever taken of Gamma's lil doggie, so I reckon it's got whut you call sent-ee-mental value for Gamma. Well guess whut? It don't have no value to us at all. Got a match, Golly?" "Yew mean, like mah butt and Gamma's face?" "Ha! Thet's a good 'un. Now let's git cookin'," said Billy Bob. Golly handed him a match which he lit and placed at a corner of the photo. The photo curled up and burned. "Whoo-ee! It shore burns swell enuff. Reminds me of Pappy's still back home in the mountains, it does," said Golly Gee. "Shore nuff," agreed Billy Bob. "Now yew stoopid Warriors. lissen up! Now we jest playin' with yore thick heads. Come the tourney, we're gonna kick yore butts!" TONIGHT: Match 2 in the NWC Tag Team Tournament: The Godzilla Warriors (Gamma and Diben) vs. The Kentucky CornDogs (Billy Bob Bureau & Golly Gee Goldhar) The CornDogs entered the ring first, wearing their full length coats with the Confederate flag on the back. Then came the Warriors. "Watch closely!" growled Diben. "Tonight you see what we do to morons!" "You destroyed my freakin' photo - I'm gonna destroy you!!" snarled Gamma. The Warriors raced into the ring and jumped the Dogs. Before they knew it, the Dogs found their coats yanked off and wrapped around their necks. Billy Bob's eyes grew wide as Gamma tightened the grip and spun him around the ring by the jacket. Diben pounded Golly in the head a bit before doing likewise. The fans howled with typical sadistic NWC glee as the Warriors tossed the Dogs about the ring, never releasing their grips on the jackets. With final growls of contempt, the Warriors flung the Dogs over the top rope. They followed. Each Warrior grabbed a Dog and lifted him high overhead. Each Warrior then walked over to the closest steel railings and let the Dogs go. The sound of jaw hitting steel railing echoed throughout the arena in stereo! The Warriors tossed the semi-conscious Dogs back into the ring. Each of them grabbed a Dog in a hammerlock and lifted his victim into the air. The Dogs shrieked madly with pain. After a few minutes of this torture, the ref shook his head. "Sorry guys, but I'll only accept ONE pinfall or submission!" he told the Warriors. The Warriors dumped their foes. Diben and Gamma grabbed Golly and held him high overhead. Walking over to the corner, they let him drop down on the turnbuckle. Golly's head hit so hard that he flew out of the ring. As Gamma made his way to the other corner, Diben grabbed Billy Bob and lifted him high overhead. Gamma climbed to the top turnbuckle. "This one's for my little dog, @#$%!" yelled Gamma. He jumped off the turnbuckle, driving his elbow into the back of Billy Bob's neck. The Dog did a 360 in the air before crashing into the mat. Gamma put one hand on the unconscious Dog. "Count!" he demanded. The ref did so. As the Warriors posed, the stretchers were rolled out for the dogs. "Who's freakin' next?" yelled Gamma. And so the Godzilla Warriors advance to the next round. Their opponents will be the Rat Pack! But there's more Round One action to come! Just look: The Terror Twins (Bash & Slice) w/ Fishmonger vs. Tom Zenk & Johnny Gunn Infernal Revenue (Sayanora Kid & H.R. Block) w/ Aloha Kid vs. Richie Rich & his Mystery Partner ************************************************************************ BL: "There you have it! A decisive win by the Godzilla Warriors. MS: "How long did it take you to come up with that segue?" BB: "Only a couple of hours....let's go to Dave Perry for the main event." ************************************************************************ DP: "Ladies and gentlemen! This is our main event, scheduled for one fall. Introducing first, the challenger, weighing in at 272 lbs, he hails from the Morgue and is accompanied by the Pyramid of Pain, here is THE CORONER!!!!!" <Coroner comes to the ring with the entire Pyramid of Pain in tow as "Don't Fear the Reaper" by Blue Oyster Cult plays in the background. Queen Nofretari has a bandage on her elbow from the fall earlier. Ramesses II is at the end of the procession with his head slightly bowed. They all enter the ring and wait.> DP: "His opponent, making a one-time return to the ring, hails from the Sands of Time. Weighing in at 245 lbs, here is MR. SANDMAN!!!!!!" <Mr. Sandman comes down the aisle carrying a black satin sack as "Enter Sandman" by Metallica plays. As he enters the rings, he stops to bow to Queen Nofretari. She bows in return, prompting the rest of the Pyramid to do likewise.> BB: "What an odd display in the ring! The entire Pyramid is bowing to Mr. Sandman!" MS: "Nah man, they ain't bowing! They're trying not to laugh at the guy, knowing that they are going to disassemble him in about 3 seconds!" BB: "Regardless, the bell has rung and the match is underway. Coroner and Sandman lock up, and Sandman is forced into the corner. Clean break by Coroner!" MS: "WHAT!?! Coroner is the third biggest cheat in the league, and he didn't cheap shot him?" BB: "Apparently there is too much honour between these two athletes to cheat. The Coroner now with a Russian legsweep. Mr. Sandman is up fast as he whips the Coroner into the turnbuckle, but Coroner reverses it! Coroner runs shoulder-first into the corner, but Mr. Sandman lifts his knee. Mr. Sandman whips The Coroner into the turnbuckle. He charges in with a clothesline, but the Coroner moves out of the way!" MS: "These two guys seem to be one step ahead of each other all the way." BB: "So far, but that may change as the Coroner executes a series of European uppercuts to Mr. Sandman. The Coroner runs into the ropes, and Mr. Sandman nails him with a reverse cradle. "Blind" Willie Feldman counts: One, two, kickout. Mr. Sandman goes for a bodyslam, but the Coroner blocks it and counters with a fireman's carry. The Coroner hits Sandman with a clothesline, but Sandman was too close to the ropes and is knocked to the outside!" MS: "And here comes the Cougar!" BB: "The Cougar starts towards Mr. Sandman, but the Coroner calls him off, leaving Mr. Sandman to re-enter the ring on his own!" MS: "That's it! This isn't the Pyramid of Pain! This is the Pyramid of Pansies! Where's the blood and guts, man? The Cougar should have disemboweled Sandy there!" BB: "The Coroner must really respect Mr. Sandman to play it this straight and narrow. They lock up again and Mr. Sandman hits the Coroner with a kick, followed by a vicious short clothesline. Now Mr. Sandman takes him down with a fisherman suplex. One, two, kickout by the Coroner." MS: "I'll give Sandman credit. The dude's 15 pounds lighter than when he last wrestled, and yet he STILL is taking to the Coroner!" BB: "The Coroner goes for a backspin DDT, but Mr. Sandman counters it with a belly-to-belly suplex. Another cover by Sandman...One, two, kickout again. Mr. Sandman nails him with a headlock takedown. Mr. Sandman goes for a fisherman suplex, but the Coroner blocks it and returns the favor with a forearm smash. The Coroner whips Mr. Sandman into the ropes and takes him down with a spinebuster slam." MS: "And he's headin' for the corner!" BB: "The Coroner places Mr. Sandman on the turnbuckle. He goes for the Post Mortem, but Mr. Sandman counters it with a belly-to-belly superplex! Feldman counts: One, two, in the ropes! Sandman follows quickly with a legdrop. Mr. Sandman starts to pick him up, but the Coroner catches him with a DDT. The Coroner nails him with a double underhook suplex, but Sandman is quick to return with a palm blow to the chest. The Coroner executes a ribbreaker, then a bulldog headlock. Now he's going for a sleeperhold, but Sandman gets to the ropes!" MS: "I'm getting tired just watching this match!" BB: "The Coroner executes a backbreaker, and heads to the top turnbuckle, but Mr. Sandman is up too soon and throws him to the mat. Mr. Sandman is going for the pin; one, two, kickout by the Coroner! The Coroner whips Mr. Sandman into the ropes. His flying clothesline is ducked by Sandman. Coroner quickly slaps on another sleeperhold, but Sandman counters it with a jawbreaker. Sandman is slow getting up." MS: "Those sleepers must of had some affect after all!" BB: "The Coroner nails him with a forearm smash. He places him on the turnbuckle...POST MORTEM!!! He covers...one...two...three! Coroner wins it!" <Dave Perry grabs the mic> DP: "The winner of the match, THE CORONER!!!!!" MS: "What's Sandman up to?" BB: "I don't know! He's going for that black bag he brought to ringside! The Pyramid see him and start surrounding him. He's waving them off and asking for the mic." Mr.S: "Coroner! You have proven yourself a worthy opponent by beating me in the ring. As a prize, I offer you...THIS!" <Mr. Sandman pulls a large hourglass out of the bag and tosses it to the Coroner.> Mr.S: "When the last grain of sand falls in this glass, you will come to me in my temple in the Sands of Time. There, I will give you a gift that is beyond your imagination. Until then, my friend, take care." <One of the arena lights suddenly explode in a shower of sparks, causing the power to flicker momentarily. When power is restored, Mr. Sandman is gone!> MS: "What the hell was that all about?!?" BB: "I have no idea! Let's go to commercial break and see if Dan Barclay can find Mr. Sandman and get some answers..." ************************************************************** * Hey sports fans!
