Gateway ([name withheld]) =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= I'm [name withheld], formerly of the NPWA (Nanet Professional Wrestling Association) which ran from about 1994 to 1999. Some of you may remember me as my character, Breakfast Bobby Toast. Those of you who role-played with the NPWA during its hey-day probably dealt with [name withheld], "Hollywood" [name withheld], and a host of others. We're all alive and well and have all reunited recently to talk about old times and wrestling. No, we aren't reviving the fed though.
We were supposed to do one final farewell show in 1999 but unfortunately things didn't work out as planned that summer. Oh well, that's the way it goes sometimes :)
*But* parts of the show did get written including my all "NPWA All-Jobber Royal Rumble" Match. [name withheld] (aka Gateway, Valtharius the Mad) suggested that this be posted to RSPWF. So here it is! I hope the former NPWAers still out there will get a kick out of this. For those of you unfamiliar with our fed, let this be a small sample of what we did. Comedy matches and angles were my particular specialty.
Here's some background info to set up the match: - It's basically the WWF Royal Rumble format - Announcers are play-by-play man Ed Wailen (our version of Stu Hart's Stampede Wrestling announcer Ed Whalen) and heel colour man Johnny B. Nastee. Johnny liked to Ed a 'puke' all the time :-) - The match is set in Brampton, Ontario, Canada, which is close to Toronto. Brampton is also the home town of [name withheld], the man who is largely responsible for bringing the e-wrestling world the NPWA.
The setting and the many members of The Duttley Clan in this match are a 'tribute' of sorts to Rob :)
- Some of the jobbers we featured in the NPWA were parodies of real- life wrestlers. Some jobbers were just made-up. This match was our way of paying homage to the most memorable ones to ever go down for the 1-2-3 inside an NPWA ring.
And that's it! Enjoy the match. If you have any comments about it write to me at [detail withheld] (remove the 'xxx' when e-mailing).
