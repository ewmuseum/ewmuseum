(--) The GrimmWhackers: Cousin Jason and Cousin Jarrod 8 ( 8) The Hutt Brothers: Jabber & Jobber THE SINGLES PICTURE This month sees a lot of huge changes in the NPWA Singles rankings. The TV Championship is vacant and the remaining men in the tournament are all in the top ten. We've seen departures too this month: one was by choice (Laser) and another was not (Road Kill Kruetzmann). Several wrestlers have found their way back into the federation: Midnight Rider, Mr. Nice Guy, Lethal Vern Lloyd, and Mr. Wrestling. As well several newcomers promise to make things quite interesting in the NPWA: Fair Warning, Locutus, El Corazon, and Squeegee Sid, just to name a few. THE TAG TEAM PICTURE The tag team rankings remain largely unchanged from last month. After their recent loss to the champions, The Muslim Massacre have enlisted the aid of a new manager known only as The Sheik. They may need all the help they can get if they want to topple the bizarre duo of The Coroner and Vlad from their number one spot. At the same time they need to protect their current position from the likes of newly formed teams such as Sadistic Steve Stone and Gan Green, and The Lumberjack and Big John Law. ************************************************************************* LETTERS TO THE EDITOR "To the Editor, "I was just sitting back, catching another fine NPWA show on WTN and came to a sickening realization. I've always enjoyed the NPWA for its fine programming and their unique wrestlers, but I've noticed an increasing trend with the relative newcomers. It seems that several new wrestlers in the NPWA, who will remain nameless, are imitating a rival organization's superstar. Some people call him stunning, some refer to him as a master of the ring, and others think he's just plain stone cold. "Whatever happened to the likes of Breakfast Bobby Toast, Ramesses II, Silent Striker, or Gan Green? Now, those are characters with distinct personalities. It just seems that there are too many guys who want to give an ass-whooping to their sons because they say so. It's getting to the point where I can't tell one guy from the next." - [name withheld] Austin, TX (Ed: Dear Rocky, my anonymous sources inside the NPWA tell me that action is being taken right now to prevent the spread of Stone Cold Fever. In addition to heavy fines levied by President Billy Joe Tunney, violators will be forced to eat Mrs. Ed Wailen's home cooking.) ************************************************************************* (C)opyright 1996 - NANet Professional Wrestling Association. **************************************************************************
