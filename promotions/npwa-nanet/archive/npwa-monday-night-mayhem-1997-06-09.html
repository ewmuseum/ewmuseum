Do you think anyone knows who Brian Masters is outside the NPWA? HELL NO! But the minute you mention "Bad Boy" Mark Bagwell, EVERYONE knows exactly who I am. I don't even compete anywhere else but in the NPWA, and yet you look outside this federation, and who's the wrestler that every federation and promotion wants? _ME_! SW: What about Steve Sampson? He's been in other feds besides the NPWA.. MB: He ain't better than Masters or whoever else has been in the NPWA. You see Skippy, Sampson was in other feds besides the NPWA, but those feds that he was in are members of this little thing they call the LOOP. A bunch of kiss ass leagues who think they are top stuff, and yet they are the most pathetic group of federations I have ever seen. The only way to even come close to winning is by either kissing ass to the promoters, or complaining to them after you lose. And your telling me Sampson was in other feds besides the NPWA? I don't consider the biggest ass kisser, and the biggest whiner of the LOOP feds to be considered coming from another federation. I've seen Sampson there, he's held titles there, but he didn't win them by earning them, he earned them by being the biggest egomaniac in ego central. Sampson in my opinion is the most over pushed wrestler, not only in the NPWA, but in those pathetic LOOP feds. A long time ago, I had a somewhat brief feud with a guy named Super Scott who was a part of the LOOP. But just a few months ago he realized what the LOOP was all about.. there all CRAP! He turned on those feds and saw the light. Scott, good going my man! SW: Where kinda getting off the subject here.. MB: No we are not Skip, you asked if you thought _I_ was over pushed. I'm saying that your NPWA World Champion Steve Sampson is the one who is over pushed. I deserve to be pushed because I am the best wrestler and athlete in the NPWA, and later tonight, the Mighty Boss will see exactly why I am the best. Because if you play with the best... your going to lose like the rest Mighty Boss.. And NPWA... I'm the F'N BEST! Wwwwwwwwoooooooooooooo!!!!!
