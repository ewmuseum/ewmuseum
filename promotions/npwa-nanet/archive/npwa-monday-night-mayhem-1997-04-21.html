Monday Night Mayhem (Apr.21/97)

21 April 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Camera opens to Steve Studd, in the gym working out. He is wearing a NPWA t-shirt with black and gold sweatpants with "Real Deal" running down the left leg in red letters. He is curling what appears to be a big amount of weight. He has some sweat on his face, and when he notices the camera, drops the weight and picks up a nearby towel to wipe his face with] "The Real Deal" Steve Studd: The NPWA. An outstanding wrestling organization with a great history. Since I came in contact with the NPWA Officals about them signing myself, I have been reviewing tapes, and watching everyone in this organization very closely. Some great cards have passed by in the past, and the cards are only going to get better now, with Steve Studd on the roster. Because even though I am very young and this will be the first "Big Time" wrestling organization that I will be joining, I think that in a very short while I will make a big difference, that will be felt by everyone in the NPWA. Now I am going to look at some of the top superstars in the NPWA, and how "The Real Deal" would match up against them. Well, the best place to start would be at the top, with the recently crowned NPWA World Heavyweight Champion, Col. Buddy Lee Jarrett. Jarrett, you have impressed me with your abilites as you beat what I considered to be a good champion in the NPWA, Amoral. Jarrett, like I said, you have impressed me, and Steve Studd doesn't impress easily. Maybe it would be in my best interests to stay on your good side. But then again, I am hungry for gold in my career, so you never know who's side I will be on. All I have to say is, Jarrett you had better watch your back. While we are on the subject of champions, lets look at the next prestigous title in the NPWA, the North American Championship, held by "Sadistic" Steve Stone. Stone, like Jarrett, has impressed me with his abilities, and I respect his parents a lot. I mean, anyone's parents have to be smart to name their kid Steve. Stone, maybe sometime in the future we can get together as well. But if you don't see an alliance in our future, maybe it would be beneficial for both of us to meet in the ring, and maybe I will relieve you of your 12 pound burden around your waist, known as the North American Championship. [Some one yells at him off camera] Well, time to wrap this up, trainer needs to get started again. Anyways, I am looking for some allies here in the NPWA, so if you are with me, good. But if you are against me, you are in for the biggest dog fight in your life. Because Steve Studd, "The Real Deal" is here, and he takes a back seat to no one! MONDAY NIGHT MAYHEM On WTN! ************************************************************************ [Ed and Johnny are standing next to the ring as fireworks are exploding near the roof of the arena. I exacted my revenge and triumphed at the end sending his carcass to the watery depths. Whether he is gone or not is uncertain. But the thorn has been plucked from my side. However, with the good comes the bad, and in this case, the amputation of my left ear. As you already knew, Leanne, it did not serve its intended function any longer. You could say that the ear was dead weight, and perhaps a magnet of sorts for my opponents. But the burden has been extracted, and the incident has not affected me at all. SS: Appearances can deceive, but let me assure you that I am the same Silent Striker as before, but with every battle I endure, I grow stronger. I may have a slightly different image, but the black and purple that I wear still remains as does my loyalty to the Asian Triad. LG: I recall seeing a tattoo of sorts during a video segment of yourself in your herbal shop..? SS: Yes, it is that of a white scorpion that adorns my black scar upon the left part of my face. There needs to be a reminder of my past and since the scorpion earring has been taken from me, I chose to get it done. LG: Cute. So what's in store for Silent Striker? What goals do you have in the NPWA? SS: As always, I plan to move up in the ranks of the NPWA. Performing in singles is slightly different than being in tag team competition that I have been a part of for most of my wrestling career. But for most of my life, I have relied only upon myself for survival, so surviving within the squared circle doing battle alone is no different. LG: How much longer do you think it'll be until you wear gold again? SS: Let me remind you and everyone else of the reason I am in this very federation partaking in this career. Ever since my Celina was taken from me, it has been my sole purpose in life to find her to end my deafening silence. By joining the NPWA, I have benefitted two-fold. First, my finances are taken care of by the purse money I win by defeating opponents. I use the funds to pay for my various expenses. Everything else I give to the Lee family for they are not from a wealthy class and it has been a tough time for them. Secondly, since the NPWA does extensive travelling from town to town, it gives me the chance to search through cities throughout North America that I would not otherwise be able to. LG: So you're telling me that instead of taking in the sights and sounds of the wonderful locations throughout this great continent of ours before and after house shows, you're constantly searching for clues to find Celina? SS: That is correct. When I am not training or involved in direct competition, I spend my time surveying the streets. I do take in the culture of the various places we visit, but not for the pure enjoyment as is the case for the others. I am on a mission, and it was thanks to Ramesses that I was sidetracked and unable to focus on what is most important to me. LG: So what happens if, I mean, when you find Celina Lee? SS: The two of us will wed and I will announce my retirement from the NPWA to restart my life with Celina as a married couple. And let me tell you, Ms. Grant, I do not plan on staying with the NPWA forever! LG: That day will certainly be a mixed blessing of sorts. Best of luck to you in your search and continued success Silent Striker!! SS: . . .
