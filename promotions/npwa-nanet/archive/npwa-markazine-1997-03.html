( 9) The Hutt Brothers (Jabber & Jobber) ANALYSIS The Singles Picture The question on everyone's mind is: Who's the World Champion? That's the question we were asking the head office at press time, and they weren't answering. So to be safe, we've decided to list both Amoral and Locutus as both Champion and Number One Contender pending President Tunney's announcement, which will be after press time. Not to be forgotten in this whole picture is Col. Buddy Lee Jarrett, who clearly is not the NPWA World Champion, but this well-planned New Wave scam of stealing the World Championship Belt has surely added attention, and ranking, to Jarrett, who moves up to the Number Two Contender Spot. The other big Title News is the North American Championship. "Sadistic" Steve Stone won the belt in the King Of The Hill match at Valentine's Day Massacre, and has since successfully defended it against Mr. Nice Guy. The Southern Rocker appears to be the ranking contender to his belt, and that's probably a good thing, because these two superpowers are aching to get their hands on each other. Jurisprudence held onto the Television Title all month, but now is looking at stern challenges from the likes of "Superstar" Steve Sampson and The Mighty Boss. If he can hold on, it will be an impressive feat. The Man They Call Nug is working his way up the rankings very quickly, as is Tyr The Nightmare. Other superstars looking like they're getting it together: The Lumberjack and Big John Law. They're no longer friends, perhaps, and they're definitely no longer a tag team, so their renewed concentration on the singles scene could change a lot of things. Luck isn't so good for Mr. Freestyle and Ramesses, who continue to languish due to lacklustre performances, and for Akira Sasaki, who's ranking is dropping more out of inactivity than anything else. Not that things aren't being changed up with the signing of "Bad Boy" Mark Bagwell, who promises to begin his career as a troublemaker in the near future, and start skyrocketing up the rankings. "Blackheart" Brian Masters countered his recent slip in the ratings by bringing back old friend "Fair Warning" John Hunter to bring more terror to the NPWA. Other new blood promises to raise hell too, literally. Wicked Lester and "The Wrecker" Mick McGreggor have already started their way up the rankings, and promise not to stop until they reach championship gold. The Tag Team Picture With the apparent breakup of The Dream Team, and the definite breakup of Big Timber, the tag team ranks may seem to have taken a hit, but really they haven't. While the number of teams vying for NPWA gold may have slipped, the quality of the teams left in the hunt has definitely gone up. The Muslim Massacre are now firmly installed in the top contender's position, and will be getting their turn at the Cadavre Connection in the near future. Also moving up are the ever-improving Perfect Role Models, who appear to be close to earning a Tag Team Title shot. Not so fortunate are the new Asian Strikers pairing of Shinya Sasaki and Kenshin Nobunaga, who've lost a step, and three straight matches to The Cadavre Connection. Resident Evil have also suffered a setback in their loss at Valentine's Day Massacre to The Muslim Massacre. Only a victory by one over the other, or a higher ranked team, may prevent further slipping. ************************************************************************* WORM HOLE By Remy A.
