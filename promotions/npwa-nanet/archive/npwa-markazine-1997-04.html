( 4) The Asian Strikers 4. (--) The Lords Of The Ring 5. ( 3) Resident Evil 6. ( 5) The Hutt Brothers ANALYSIS The Singles Picture With a victory over Locutus, Col. Buddy Lee Jarrett moves into the number one position, but Locutus uses his strong month to stay in the second spot, meaning that the number one and two contenders will face off against the Champion at April Assault. The NPWA is still realing from the loss of The Southern Rocker, who appeared primed to win the North American Title at April Assault. In his place will be "Bad Boy" Mark Bagwell, the highest ranked of the new stream of talent arriving in the NPWA. Mick McGreggor has made an impressive move up the rankings despite avoiding the most talented competition. The Television Champion enjoyed a slow month, but that's looking to change with Jurisprudence defending against The Mighty Boss at April Assault, and the winner of that match can expect to see stiff challenges from The Silent Striker, "Blackheart" Brian Masters and former TV Champ Breakfast Bobby Toast. April Assault will probably tell the tale of who will sit where in the rankings. The winner of the Four Corners match will be in for a big jump in the rankings, after defeating three ranked wrestlers in one night, and chances are that World Title shots will be granted to the winner of either the Masters/Sampson match, or the Tyr/Corazon match. The Tag Team Picture Not a whole lot of change from last month. The Lords of the Ring make their debut in the number four spot, one place above their Aprial Assault opponents, Resident Evil, who fall from 3. The Asian Strikers appear to be getting some momentum back, and are up one from 4. Rumor has it that new tag teams are on their way... either from the existing roster, or newcomers. ************************************************************************* What if....
