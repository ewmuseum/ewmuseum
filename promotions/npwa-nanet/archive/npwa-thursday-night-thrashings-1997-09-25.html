THURSDAY NIGHT THRASHINGS On WTN & Lifetime! ******************************************************************** [The camera shot opens with Bob Buchanan sitting behind the NPWA desk.] BB: Good evening everyone, and welcome to Thursday Night Thrashings! This is going to be a very different Thursday Night Thrashings than what you are used to. This is going to be the new wrap-up show for the NPWA. And we've got a lot to wrap up. First, let's look back at Monday Night Mayhem where a new Television Champ was crowned. Take a look. ******************************************************************** EW: Both men are locked together in the middle of the ring. Striker reverses and belly-to-belly suplexes Masters instead. The impact stuns Masters! Striker struggles up and gets behind Masters. He wraps his arms around the waist of Brian Masters. Silent Striker executes a German Suplex! JBN: Blackheart Brian Masters is in La-La Land. And so is Striker! Both men are out on the canvas. EW: Those suplexes took a lot out of the Striker. That last one was especially difficult given his one bad arm. Both men are struggling to get up. Masters makes it to his feet. He reaches for The Striker... JBN: But Striker is playing possum. He reaches up and jams his fingers into Masters' mouth! EW: It's The Silencer! The Striker's applied his mandible-claw submission move on Masters. Striker is getting up to gain more leverage. He caught Masters totally by suprise with that move. JBN: He's got it locked on good! Masters is down on one knee. EW: Striker is struggling to keep that applied. He's exerting more pressure by the minute. Masters could pass out at any second now. JBN: The fans are in total shock! Masters is going out like a light. EW: But the champion that he is, Masters is fighting this every step of the way. He goes for a thumb to the eye! JBN: It has no effect! Masters missed by several inches. He's passing out and can't see what he's doing. EW: Masters is down on the mat and the Striker is on top of him with that Silencer applied. The ref checks the arm of Masters. He lifts it once... his arm falls! He lifts it a second time... it falls again!! There's the third one.... it falls! It falls! JBN: No! Masters got his arm up! He's barely got it up in time, but it's struggling to stay up there. EW: There is no quit in Brian Masters! This is incredible. A lesser man would have been out a long time ago, and we'd have had a new TV Champion. The Silent Striker cannot believe that Masters is still fighting this. But it's only a matter of time. JBN: Masters is still hanging on! I've never seen anyone last this long against The Striker's Silencer hold before. EW: This is completely amazing. The pain Masters is going through must be unbelievable. Yet he's firmly holding his arm in the air to let the ref and the fans know he's still in this thing. The Striker is trying to apply more pressure. But I think he's giving it all that he's got already. JBN: Striker's trying something else now. He frees up one hand and pounds his fist into the chest of Masters. EW: Masters reels from the blow. Combined with the pain from that Silencer, he rolls back slightly. His shoulders are on the mat! Brian Masters' shoulders are on the mat! The Silent Striker is right on top of him with that Silencer applied. JBN: But Masters still has his arm in the air. You can see him fight and struggle to keep it up despite the pain! EW: But the Silent Striker did what he had to do. He pounded Masters where he needed to and now the champ's shoulders are on the mat! The ref is making the count: 1.. 2.. 3!!! Ladies and gentlemen, we have a new Television Champion! JBN: The Silent Striker wins the TV Title from Brian Masters!! EW: What an incredible accomplishment for the Striker! His singles career has been a long and difficult one, and now he wins his first ever singles title. Congratulations to The Silent Striker. But what an incredible struggle by Blackheart Brian Masters. He fought until the very end. JBN: You got that right, puke. The Striker beat Masters for the belt, but he did not make him submit. EW: I've never seen a display like that before Johnny. The end of the road for Masters' TV Title reign comes here tonight. He took on an incredible array of challengers during his stint. Let's go up to David Perry for the announcement. DP: Ladies and gentlemen, the winner of the match and NEWWWWWWWWW NPWA Television Champion, The Silent Striker!!!! ******************************************************************** BB: What a brutal match up that was. Neither men were available for comment after that incredible display of athleticism. And speaking of comments, it appears that during our hiatus, the stars of the NPWA were all reserving their comments for a later date. Very few had an answer for the Question of the Week. Here's the answers we did get. ******************************************************************** QUESTION OF THE WEEK Every week we will ask a question that the superstars of the NPWA can answer. Last week we asked: What movie title best describes your character? Here are their answers: Dark Justice: Dark Justice always considers himself close to the famous Judge Dredd. My favorite line in the movie was when Stallone yells "I am the LAW!" I know where his character is coming from because that character is based on me. After Stallone has consulted with me with the character, he really outdid himself and that is why it became a #1 box office smash hit. Stallone is a smart man because he likes to associate himself with greatness. I am the true meaning and representation of GREATNESS. Judge Dredd kicked major butts in the movie, arrested and sentenced all who opposed him and was not afraid of anyone. Not only that, he was strong in stature, convictions, morals and of course muscles. I do the same thing here in the NPWA cleaning out the scum and trash. Remember that crime doesn't pay and all will have to serve some serious hard time. Cougar: "Mortal Kombat" is by far...the best title to describe...the Cougar. Merely stepping into the ring with me...is to take one's own life into your own hands. GRRRRRRRRROOOOOOOOOWWWWWWLLLLLLL!!!!!!! Here is the question for this week: Aside from yourself, who in the NPWA is most deserving of an NPWA championship belt? (Tagteam and/or Singles!) Wrestlers, send your answers to [name withheld] at " [detail withheld] ". And fans, if you have any questions you'd like the superstars of the NPWA to answer, send it in and we might use it in this weekly feature. ******************************************************************** BB: Interesting question. I have my own answers, but I'll save them for next week. Another belt that changed hands recently was the NPWA Tag Team Championship. The Maltese Falcon shocked the world by joining up with Gan Green to become the New Perfect Role Models, and the new Tag Champs. Let's go back to last Thursday for the closing moments of that match. ******************************************************************** EW: The New Asian Strikers are double teaming while the original members are watching on the outside. And referee John Gibbons has finally restored some order and it will be Kenshin starting against Green. JBN: That's great, but Green is already injured! What's he trying to prove? EW: With Green, you never know. Kenshin now whips Green into the ropes. Tilt-a-whirl backbreaker by Kenshin. He hauls Green up... JBN: BY THE HAIR! Disqualify him! EW: ...and tags in Shinya. They both whip Green into the far turnbuckle. Now Kenshin whips Shinya into the turnbuckle after Green! Huge splash with help from Kenshin. Shinya picks up Green and whips him back into the Triad's corner. Another tag as Kenshin comes back in. JBN: Well, what do you expect? Green is going to get killed against these two! The ref should disqualify the Strikers for all the double teaming! Heck, it would be the first fair call Gibbons ever made! EW: Shinya whips Green into the ropes and drops to the mat. Green hops over Shinya, AND RIGHT INTO A CHOKESLAM BY KENSHIN! JBN: WAIT! Here comes the calvary! EW: The Maltese Falcon is coming down to ringside! He's heading to Green's corner! Could he be the new Perfect Role Model? NO! He's picking up his figurine and walking away from the corner! He's heading towards the timekeeper's table! He's put the figurine on the desk and has pulled up a chair to watch the match! JBN: The coward is waiting for the Triad to soften up Green before he gets his revenge! That wimp! EW: I thought for sure Falcon was the new Role Model! I bet Green is wishing that Falcon *WAS* his partner, as Green receives a vicious powerbomb from Kenshin Nobunaga. Kenshin tags in Shinya and heads to the top turnbuckle with Green in tow. He's going for his Kyoto Massacre! Shinya is going up right behind him! What's going on? JBN: I don't know, but it looks like it's going to be painful! EW: OH MY LORD!!!! JBN: Yeouch! I was right! EW: As Nobunaga went for his top-rope powerbomb, Shinya leaped over them and a sort of Bonzai drop to the maneuver! Green isn't moving! JBN: That move could break all your ribs twice! One break on each side! EW: Shinya goes for the pin! One...two...NO! Shinya pulled him up?!?! What's going on? JBN: Green must still be breathing! EW: Shinya must have decided that he wasn't going to put up with the nonsense that Green is always pulling anymore! He whips Green into the far turnbuckle. Springboard elbowsmash! As Green crumples into the corner, Shinya spits on him for good measure. What's this? JBN: Now what?!? EW: AKira has come down to ringside! The Striker is holding him back! Now Shinya sees his brother and is trying to get at him! Kenshin is keeping Shinya in the ring! JBN: Looks like a case of sibling rivalry if I ever saw one. EW: You don't have a brother! JBN: No, but I've had some sisters fight over me before. EW: Shinya is still yelling at his brother...what's Falcon up to? JBN: He's heading over to Green's corner! EW: HE TAGS IN!!!! Falcon pushes Shinya into the ropes and rolls him up on the rebound! Gibbons is counting! One...Two...THREE!!!! WE HAVE NEW CHAMPS!!! DP: The winner of this bought, AND NEW NPWA WORLD CHAMPIONS....GAN GREEN AND THE MALTESE FALCON!!!! ******************************************************************** BB: Well, Shinya was less than pleased with his brother and the two squared off on Monday for the right to be in the Strikers. It was a brutal fight, but Shinya eventually pulled off the win, leaving Akira to slip off into the shadows. BB: We've got a special treat tonight. We are going to break a few rules and talk about some other federations for a moment, in the form of the "REC.SPORT.PRO-WRESTLING.FANTASY TAG TEAM TOP 200"! This list was created from fan votes from around the world. And the NPWA certainly made its presence known. We trimmed the list down to highlight the teams of the NPWA, as well as the top ten. Here they are. ******************************************************************** 194: Muslim Massacre (General Arain / Colonel Mustafa) 190: Bed and Breakfast (Mr. Sandman / "Breakfast' Bobby Toast) 132: Resident Evil (Blade / Jack Hammer) 88: Hollywood Studs ("Hollywood Heartthrob" Dave Diamond / Christian "California Stud" Steele 87: Infernal Revenue Service (Bash / Sayanara Kid) 80: Lords of the Ring (Warfield / Midhaven) 68: Drama (Agony / Ecstasy) 21: Asian Strikers ("Asian Rush" Shinya Sasaki / Silent Striker / Kenshin Nobunaga) 18: The Cadaver Connection (Cougar / Coroner) 10: Cowboys From Hell. (Buck Boy Basham & "Texas" Mark Massacre) Two-time GIWF World Tag Champions (GIWF/LRPW) 9: Jingoists (The Narcissist, Lt. Liberty, The Wingman, Mjr. Hawk Hunter) GIWF---2 Time and Current GIWF World Tag Team Champions. Members of the Freedom Brigade. 8: Trailer Park Assassins. (Cooter Lee Roy & Orville Ray Bob Tucker) two guys from down home Weasel, Arkansas trying to make it in the big time, and they have. They held the NCWF Television Tag Team titles before the fed merged into NFW, where TPA captured the World Tag Team Titles. They are currently top contenderes in AAPW 7: Tokyo Blades (Kantana & Wazakashi) WWW (Former WWW Tag Champs). They've dominated the WWW Tag Team rankings since they arrived back in April, and were the first and longest running WWW Tag Team Champions. A new team with lots of talent and potential. Keep an eye out for these guys. 6: Crusher Tech (Kingston & Stephens) PCW Former PCW World TagTeam Champs. Became tag team champions for the second time with recent victory over Bigger than Hollywood, before losing them to the same team they beat. Earlier in the year, held CHAOS!!!, EWA, and PCW TagTitles simultaneously. Although they did have a drop off during the year,they were consistent when Jesse rp'ed them, and now that they are retiring they definitely deserve the honor of going out as number one. 5: Natural Born Killaz ("Downtown" Donovan Brown and D-Bone Johnson) Debuted in early January of 97, and since then, have teared through all tag teams put in front of them. These 2 mean Comptonv badboys have fueded with many people. Their first fued was with Nature's Fury in the Stone Cold Wrestling Fed. After never losing a single match with this team, they then moved on to fueding with the Cash Money Bros., who the won the belts from. After StoneCold Wrestling became defunct, they headed south to Southern Championship Wrestling. The first get into a fued with the Bad Crew. The Bad Crew slipped away with 2 victories, and a third match was ruled a double dq. After that, The Natural Born Killaz won a one night single elimination tag team tournament to crown the TV Tag Champs, called the Southern Cup. After winning the titles, they have fought with many great teams, but none have been able to dethrone them. The Natural Born Killaz are currently fueding with The Mafia. 4: Bigger Than Hollywood (Mark Cutter & Shaun Adams). PCW Former PCW World TagTeam Champs. For the past eight months, Bigger than Hollywood have probably been the most consistent team in e-wrestling, losing to only three different teams. Lost their third match in PCW against the Rednecks, but soon thereafter defeated them for the tag belts. Dropped to Crusher Tech just over a month later, and were defeated for a second time by CT at PCW Spring Showdown. BTH recently came back, however, to win the belts at the end of June, and have retained since then. Also held EWA Tag Team Titles until they forefeited them, and went undefeated in DPW before it closed down in April at 6-0. For the year, they are 37-4. 3: Coldspell (Icehawk and Edmund Fitzgerald) [IIWF] Titles: Former IIWF Tag Champs who were just defeated by The Syndicate. Event: Ended the Prophets Of Rages IIWF World 2: Siberian Express (Ivan Drago / Andrei "Russian Bear" Bolotov). This fairly new and still relatively unknown team has gained recognition in each fed they've competed in. They've held the HWL World Tag Team titles, HWL TV Tag Team Titles (twice), NIWF TV Tag Team titles, NAEC Global Tag Team Titles. Competed in the HWL, NAEC, NIWF, CHW, and currently compete in LWC(IEWF), UFWA. They're new still but have had some great matches with teams like The Prophets of Rage and Pain Inc. Look for good things in the coming year for this up and coming team. And the Number one team in RSPWF is........ 1: Prophets of Rage (Derek / Shadoe Rage). IFWF, IIWF, TOWL, FeWS, IEWF. This team is perhaps one of the most unknown but in demand teams around. Their reputation keeps growing as they win championship after championship. Their resume is this: IFWF (current world tag-team champions, former European champions), IEWF (current world tag champs),IIWF (former World and U.S. tag champs), TOWL (closed the fed aschampions), FeWS (current North American champions). They also participated in the closed GCW (champions), CWF (World and U.S. champions) ******************************************************************** BB: Congratulations to all the teams who made the list. And special thanks to [name withheld] for supplying the list. If any of you viewers are interested in seeing the entire list, drop us a line via this station, and we'll see what we can do. We've got men out in the field trying to get the singles rankings, and if we get them, you'll get them so stay tuned. BB: We go now from celebration to mourning. Several top stars have been put out of commission. A recent one was Dante Powell, after a brutal match on Monday Night Mayhem. After winning an all out war of a cage match, Powell was celebrating with his wife, Samantha, when his opponent that evening, Dave Diamond, decided he didn't like the way the match turned out. Let's see what went down. ******************************************************************** SK: Diamond nails a DDT on Powell. He covers! ONE...TWO...TH...Powell gets his shoulder up!!! I don't believe it! TH: Neither does Diamond! He's leaving! SK: Diamond is heading over to the cage. He's climbing up to the top. Samantha Powell is screaming at her husband to get up. Powell is stirring. He's crawling toward the door. TH: With the lead that Diamond has, even taking the long route he's going to win. SK: Powell is at the door, but Diamond is at the top! He swings one leg over...WHAT'S HE DOING?!?!?! [Dave Diamond shouts out "WHHHOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!" to the crowd and does a stationary strut at the top of the cage.] SK: They've opened the door. Powell is almost out and Diamond is dancing at the top of the cage! Powell's crawling through the doorway...HE GOT IT!! DPerry: The winner of this bout..."Dreamboat" DANTE POWELL!!!!! SK: And Dave Diamond can't believe it! He's furious! Samantha is hugging her battered and bruised husband...DEAR LORD WATCH OUT!!!!!! [Dave Diamond leaps from the top of the cage at the Powells. Dante sees him and pushes Sam out of the way at the last second, only to catch all of the weight behind Diamond's top-of-the-cage dropkick square in the chest. Dante goes flying backward and catches the guardrail with the side of his head on the way down, as a large cracking sound is heard. As Dante lies there, his head in an awkward angle, Diamond grabs the microphone from Dave Perry.] DD: WHAT?!?! IS THAT ALL YOU GOT?!?! COME ON YOU SACK OF &*%#! GET UP!!! I AIN'T DONE WITH YA YET!!!!! GET UP DAMMIT!!! [Suddenly a very large, bald, muscular black man comes running down the aisle with a chair. Diamond heads for the hills when he sees him coming. The black man checks on Samantha, who is going into shock at the sight of her injured husband. He then checks on Dante Powell.] Mystery Man: GET SOME HELP DOWN HERE NOW!!!!! MOVE IT OR I'LL KILL YA MYSELF!!!! [As the EMTs rush to ringside, the camera pans away to a rather stunned Sayonara Kid and Tobias Hall. Tobias sees the camera and motions to kill the feed.] ******************************************************************** BB: After a very lengthy surgery, the medical technicians in Australia were able to save Dante Powell's life, but he is now paralyzed from the neck down. I know all our prayers are with the Powells in this time of tragedy. We still do not know who that mystery man was, but he did sign in Powell to the hospital with the initials "XLJ". BB: Others to be put out of action include Valtharius the Mad, who is serving several consecutive life sentences on murder and cannabalism charges, and the Overgiver, who has retired to pursue other career options. Another injury occured when Reynoso Torres injured himself during a training session. We are still awaiting word on whether or not he will return to action. BB: One person who is still around is Tobias Hall. Let's take you now to Opinions of Tobias Hall. ******************************************************************** OPINIONS OF TOBIAS HALL [Tobias is sitting in a booth of an old English-style pub with a large dark ale in front of him. He takes a sip, then begins to speak.] TH: Well, this has been quite a couple of weeks for the folks here at the NPWA. Normally, I'd take this opportunity to discuss a wrestler or two, but in light of recent events, I thought I'd take it up a notch and talk about the head-honchos, the bookers themselves. About two weeks ago, the entire booking committee stepped down from office. Oh sure, that Coughler guy stuck around, but between you, me, and this mug, the guy's anal-retentive, and an idiot. He couldn't book a match between Nug and a pack of hot dogs fresh off the BBQ! Fraser was still there, but he had only been on the committee for a week, so he doesn't count either. I want to take a minute to talk about the ones that left, and the legacy they left behind. Now I never worked with Chris, "Don't Call Me HERBERT!" Hebert, but sources close to me tell me he was one hell of a class act. Comparisons to the Great Dutt-man abound when Hebert's name is brought up. I don't know when he first signed up for the gig, because my memory's as bad as Steve Stone's breath, but it seems forever. Most wrestlers figured they'd retire before Hebert did. Unfortunately, they were wrong. Hebert changed the face of the NPWA for the better, and the new bunch are going to have a hard time living up to the standards he set. Joel Geraghty was famous for having a name that no one could spell. Like Hebert, he was one of those guys who just always seemed to be around when you needed him. He's off of the committee, but he's still hanging around helping out with statistics and the like. Bakewell signed on originally to write a match or two, and was quickly promoted through the ranks to Bookerman. He was always there with a well-placed compliment, or some bizarre angle idea that you knew would be a gem. One of the classic matches of Armageddon '97, the Nug vs Stone match, occured largely due to his brilliance and wit. I would say that match was second only to the main event. To have one guy of the calibre of Hebert, Geraghty, or Bakewell on your committee is a dream. To have three, is an great ecstacy. To lose all three, is an equally great agony. We can only hope that the next generation of bookers are up to the task of attempting, not to replace them, but to make an equally great impression on this sport. I'm Tobias Hall, and that's my opinion. Deal with it. [Hall take a long drought of his ale as the camera fades to black.] ******************************************************************** BB: I know many of us echo those same sentiments.
