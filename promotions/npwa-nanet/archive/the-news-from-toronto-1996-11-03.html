THE NEWS FROM TORONTO For the week of November the 3rd --------------------------------------------------------------------------- --------------------------------------------------------------------------- The News from Toronto is available in the following ways: ş It is posted bi-weekly to the NaNet Pro-Wrestling Conference. ş It is sent out to all members on the NPWA Mailing List. (We're still working on it, though) ş It is available on the NPWA World Wide Web homepage, at http://www.ncf.carleton.ca/~dk589/npwa.html * We are in no way affiliated with the NANet Pro Wrestling Association, but we thank [name withheld] for his help in distribution of this newsletter. --------------------------------------------------------------------------- The News -- November 3, 1996 Welcome to the first ever edition of News From Toronto! I don't really have much to say, I just want to thank all my inside sources for the information I will be posting bi-weekly.. whose names I cannot divulge. Let's start off the first edition of NFT with a rundown of the bi-weekly Alexx Marvel wrestling column from the Toronto Daily News: * The top story this week concerns the NPWA. As reported in the Fishmonger's report on The Traction Zone, there indeed will be a special primetime special wrestling show during mid-November to fill the gap between the Armageddon '96 PPV, and the NPWA Xmas Gory PPV in December. * Several mid-carders in the NPWA will be soon heading out of the league, or will be re-packaged with a gimmick. We have already seen the shocking exit of Laser from NPWA on TNT two weeks ago, and more dead weight will be on their way out. Sources tell that Laser may have signed with the WCW, and will be joining the Dungeon of Doom, form a team with Billy Kidman and feud with High Voltage. * The higher-ups at the NPWA are rather unsure of who will be granted the World Title shot at Xmas Gory. Other news -- * The WWF will be giving a major heel-push to Bob Holly, who is scheduled to destroy his stock racing car at the next set of TV Tapings. He will soon be managed by none other than Jim Ross. * Akira Sasaki will be departing from the Asian Triad, he has decided to go as a "lone wolf" singles wrestler. However, his place in the Asian Triad will be replaced by newcomer Kenshin Nobunaga. Now, I have seen tapes of this guy in action from an indie league in Japan, him along with his brutal style will be a valuable addition to the Asian Triad. Chinami will be leaving with Akira Sasaki. A Pyramid of Pain/Asian Triad w/ Akira Sasaki feud would be sweet, but don't hold your breath waiting for it to happen. * WCW is trying to put a copyright on the New World Order. Nice try. * Jushin "Thunder" Liger's tumour has actually not been removed, and it has spread to the rest of his body. His doctors are giving him two weeks to live. He's planning to make a come back next week. Don't rush things, Jushin. That's it for this week's report, be back at y'all two weeks from now.
