[Hollywood, Cool and Da Gan Green Guy come out as that cheesy bagpipe music plays...Hollywood is carrying a golf bag filled with clubs.] DP: And their opponents are the NPWa Tag Team Champions of the World! [the arena suddenly goes completely dark...the crowd pops] DP: Being led to the ring by Mr. Sandman, and representing the Chronos Triumverate...first, weighing 307 pounds and hailing from The Morgue, The Coroner! His partner, from The Rocky Mountains, weighing 255 pounds, The Cougar! They are The Cadavre Connection!!! [A strobe light begins flashing in the darkness as Juno Reactor's "Control Remix" plays...Mr. Sandman comes out first followed by Cougar and Coroner... Coroner is wearing a hooded black robe that hides his features completely, while Cougar is wearing his usual tawny brown tights and clawed glove, he is also wearing the pelt of a grizzly bear. The bear's skullcap acts as a hood, while the bear's paws are tied in a single knot in the front to keep the pelt on him like a cape. Around Cougar's neck is a leather cord strung through bear teeth. When they reach the ring, the lights come back up.] EW: Quite the entrance by the champs, Johnny! JBN: You'd better believe it...Mr. Wrestling is yelling at The Coroner, Coroner turns around... EW: Mr. Wrestling just jumped back about 5 feet in fright! What is Coroner hiding under that hooded robe? JBN: We're about to find out, puke...he's taking it off! EW: Oh my word! The Coroner's entire head, including his face, as well as his neck, upper back and arms are completely covered in multi-coloured tattoos! JBN: Who would tattoo their face! That must have hurt like hell! EW: It looks like...yes, the tattoos appear to depict different scenes out of Greek mythology...I can see a Medusa, a three-headed dog...and a bunch of other things I don't understand. It's very disconcerting to look at him. JBN: And it must be for The Greensiders as well...it's a great psyche-out tool for The Coroner. EW: It looks like The Cougar will be starting off against Mr. Big Wrestling Cool. The bell rings...and Cougar leaps at Cool! He's gouging, punching, scratching and kicking like a wild animal! I don't think Mr. Wrest...I mean Big Wrestling Cool was ready for an onslaught like this! JBN: The Cougar looks like he's becoming even more like his namesake now that he's returned to his Rocky Mountain home...but what was the deal with the bearskin? I thought he was The Cougar? EW: I have no idea, Johnny. Hopefully we can ask him or Mr. Sandman what's going on there. Cougar has backed Cool back into the ropes. Irish whip...hey, the Irish Whip should be the official wrestling move for today, don't you think? JBN: Hmmmm....wha??? Wassthat? EW: Aren't you paying attention, Johnny? JBN: Sorry, I was thinking about bearskin rugs, a fireplace and the entire female cast of 90210. EW: You're sick...Cougar executed a beautiful high cross body off of that Irish Whip. He's got Wrestling Cool down and is pummelling him with a series of lefts to the head. He drags Cool over to his corner and tags in The Coroner. JBN: Here we go! EW: Coroner takes Big Wrestling Cool off of Cougar's hands and presses him high overhead...he drops him across the top rope, throat first! JBN: He's going to sound like a Leprechaun now, puke! EW: Coroner stays right on his man, and picks him up...he butterflies the arms...DDT! Coroner pins him...1...2...kickout! Coroner drags Cool over to the corner and tags Cougar back in. JBN: The champions are controlling this match perfectly. Quick tags, keeping Big Wrestling Cool away from his partner...great tag team wrestling. EW: Cougar picks Cool up...scoop slam, and a legdrop across his throat! He hauls Mr. Big Wrestling Cool back up to his feet...Irish whip... reversal by Cool...no! Da Gan Green Guy pulled down the top rope and Cougar went over and down to the floor! JBN: You've got to have eyes in the back of your head in a tag team match, as Cougar just found out the hard way. EW: Cool tags in Da Gan Green Guy who immediately jumps down to the floor and picks Cougar up. He whips Cougar into one of the steel security barriers! JBN: He's got to get Cougar back into the ring if he wants to win the belts! EW: Apparently he just realized that as he picks Cougar up, presses him over head and tosses him back in unceremoniously over the top rope. Gan Green climbs back in...elbow drop on Cougar! He picks Cougar back up...belly-to-belly suplex! A pin...1...2...Cougar gets his foot on the bottom rope. Da Gan Green Guy pounds the mat in frustration, he though he had him. JBN: That looked like a pretty slow count by "Blind" Willie there...The Greensiders should be the champions right now! EW: It was not a slow count! Da Gan Green Guy picks Cougar back up. Whoa! Inside cradle by Cougar! 1...2...kickout! JBN: That came outta nowhere, puke! EW: Cougar crawls over to his corner...he makes the tag! Coroner steps over the top rope and nails Gan Green with a flying clothesline! He picks Green up and grabs him around the throat! JBN: Chokeslam! EW: That's 350 pounds of Da Gan Green Guy...can he...yes!!! He chokeslams Da Gan Green Guy with ease! Coroner is without a doubt one of the most powerful men in wrestling today! JBN: Yeah, take away his so-called "magic elixir" and then see how powerful he is! EW: Coroner picks Green up and sets him on the top turnbuckle...I think he's going to give him a taste of the Post Mortem! JBN: Wait a minute, Mr. Big Wrestling Cool has grabbed Feldman and is yelling at him about something or other... EW: He's just distracting the ref! Hollywood Cheat Cheat pulls out a 5-iron and gets up on the ring apron...and he brains Coroner! Coroner drops like a stone...and Cheat Cheat runs over and nails Cougar in the head as well for good measure! Mr. Sandman grabs Cheat Cheat and executes a brainbuster onto the concrete floor, but the damage has already been done! JBN: I don't believe this!!! I think we're going to have new tag team champions! EW: Gan raises his hands in victory as Wrestling continues to distract Willie Feldman. JBN: Someone is coming out of the audience! EW: It's Resident Evil! They've got their trusty baseball bats with them...Gan Green turns around to pin Coroner as Blade and Jack Hammer slide into the ring behind him. Blade hits him high and Hammer hits him low with the aluminum bats! Green drops to the mat, and Resident Evil roll Coroner on top of Green then exit the ring! JBN: Why on Earth are they helping the Cadavre Connection?!? EW: I don't know, Johnny. Big Wrestling Cool let's Feldman go, expecting Green to be pinning Coroner...and he sees Coroner on top of Green!!! He tries to grab Feldman again, but misses! Feldman counts...1...2...3!!! The Cadavre Connection retains the tag team titles, with the inexplicable aid of Resident Evil! JBN: I guess they want the Cadavre Connection to be the ones facing their enemies, The Muslim Massacre, at April Assault, not The Greensiders. Why exactly, I'm not sure. EW: Fans, we're right out of time this week...be sure to tune into WTN on Thursday for Thursday Night Thrashings, Saturday for Saturday Night Nightmare and next Monday for another Monday Night Mayhem! I'm Ed Wailen for Johnny B.
