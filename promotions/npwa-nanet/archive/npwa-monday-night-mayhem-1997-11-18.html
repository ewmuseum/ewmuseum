Monday Night Mayhem (Nov.18/97)

18 November 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
EW: There you have it, the view from both combatants... but it's kind of strange to see two men so focused on other issues when they're going to be wrestling each other. JBN: I'll tell ya, puke... if they don't pay attention to business, this match is gonna go to whoever wakes up first and remembers that this ain't about Xavier Lee Jackson or Mustafa or Dark Justice... this is about Mitchells and Diamond, mano y mano. EW: And another interesting note, fans... Dave Diamond mentioned his desire for a rematch with Xavier Lee Jackson, but he's not going to be getting it under an NPWA banner. Fans, Xavier and NPWA Chief Financial Officer Derek Serkies met late last week, and Jackson expressed.... concern... JBN: Stop pussyfooting around... he basically said the NPWA front office wasn't stable enough for his liking, and that he felt he'd accomplished all he could here by surviving his match with Dave Diamond. In other words, he's a coward who's looking for nothing but job security, and we don't need him. End of story. EW: Ladies and gentlemen, the lord of tact, Johnny B. Nastee. The rest of the NPWA certainly wishes Xavier Lee well wherever his journeys in wrestling may take him. JBN: I hear there's a guy handing out money to dumb ham-n-eggers in Atlanta... EW: ANYWAYS... we've wasted enough time jawing, let's get down to ringside for this match... Dave Diamond meeting Mike Mitchells! *********************************************************************** DP: Ladies and gentlemen, this match is scheduled for one fall, with a 20 minute time limit. Your referee is Denny Larson. Introducing first... ["Train" by Quad City DJs begins to play] DP: Hailing from the City that never sleeps, New York, New York, weighing in at 201 lbs, here is "Ultimate" Mike Mitchells! [Mitchells makes his way towards the ring, confidently holding his head up and revelling in, at least for this one day, a fair bit of crowd support.] EW: I know it's a cliche, but sometimes it's the size of the fight in the man, and not... JBN: Yeah, I know. And Mitchells is gonna get plowed, just like every other match he's wrestled. DP: And his opponent... ["Unchained" by Van Halen begins to play, and the crowd responds with a long round of boos.] DP: Weighing in at 254 lbs, he comes from where dreams are made, where legends are born, Hollywood, California... here is "The Hollywood Heartthrob" Dave Diamond! [Diamond, ignoring his normal routine, charges to the ring and climbs in, motioning that he wants Perry's microphone.] DD: Get it right, Perry! That's... the sexiest man in pro wrestling, the Southern All-Star Wrestling HEAVYWEIGHT Champion.... The Legend... The Man You All Want To Spend One Night With, No Matter What Gender You Are... and based on what I've heard... The Man Who Ran Xavier Lee Jackson OUT of the NPWA... "The Hollywood Heartthrob"... The Incomparable and Unbeatable... DAVE DIAMOND! WHHHHHHHHHOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO! [With that, Diamond hands the ring mic back, and blows a few kisses to the crowd, looking quite happy about the situation.] EW: Here comes Mitchells and we're underway! Mitchells attacks Diamond before he can get his robe off! JBN: Who does this kid think he is? EW: I think he's a little bit confused by getting fan support... and let me tell you, given all that Mitchells stands for... Diamond has to be a pretty hated man to get fan support! JBN: Except from the ladies, puke.... except from the ladies! EW: Diamond now... whipped into the ropes.... hard flying lariat by Mitchells, and Diamond is down and appears to be a bit confused! JBN: I don't think he was expecting to be tested at all by this kid! EW: And right away, Mitchells is on him with the boots! JBN: Interesting that this Mitchells kid, a little guy... he's what? Just over 200? Anyway... interesting that he chooses to brawl it out with a guy 60 pounds bigger than him! EW: Indeed it is, but he certainly seems to have a tough reputation. His mere arrival in the NPWA caused his longtime rival, The Mighty Boss, a former TV Champion may I add, to leave the NPWA in no small hurry. JBN: And yet, the guy's... what... yet to win a match. He sucks! It's so obvious! EW: He certainly has... yet to find his niche here in the NPWA... and now he finally lets up with the boots, and he scoops up Diamond and slams him to the mat! JBN: That's not too shabby... that's like a 250 pound guy slamming a 310-pounder! EW: Indeed, not too bad. Now Mitchells heads to the top rope.... flying stomp off the top rope, and that connected directly with Diamond's sternum! JBN: Right now, Dave Diamond is learning a hard lesson... don't take ANYONE for granted! EW: Diamond half-way up now... Mitchells looking for a DDT... turned into a Northern Lights Suplex... 1... 2... kickout by Mitchells! Nice counter by Diamond! JBN: And now Diamond slows this down by going with the front facelock! All of Diamond's weight right on the upper torso of Mitchells! EW: Indeed, you bring up a good point there, Johnny... can Mitchells hope to compete with heavyweight wrestlers? JBN: Well you know, puke... I don't think it's a coincidence that the NPWA doesn't feature a light heavyweight division, and also doesn't have many guys under 240 pounds. EW: A very valid point... it might be that the NPWA hasn't exactly created a light-heavyweight friendly environment. Diamond is really working this facelock... and look at him smiling so broadly at the crowd. What an arrogant man! JBN: If I'd ran Xavier Lee Jackson out of the NPWA, I'd be pretty happy about it too, puke! EW: Oh come on! You know as well as I do that Xavier Lee felt he'd accomplished all he could, and that he and the NPWA parted on good terms! JBN: Uh-huh... and he only reads Playboy for the articles, right? Gimme a break! The man's scared of Dave Diamond... it's as clear as the nose on your face... and let me tell you... your nose is pretty clear! EW: Why... I.... nevermind. Mitchells... slowly trying to work his way out of this... JBN: Can't! Too much weight on him! EW: Now Mitchells gets twisted around... hooks the arms... crucifix.... can he get him over? It wouldn't appear that way from here.... he's got him over! But he can't get him down! He's still struggling... wait a second now... Diamond stands up... POWER BOMB! JBN: Now THAT'S a comeback! EW: Diamond just dropped Mitchells over his head like a power bomb... here's the cover... 1... 2... Mitchells gets a shoulder up! Diamond, however, is quick to his feet, and over goes Mitchell with a snap suplex... oh, and this is just uncalled for! JBN: Look at the D-man strut! Think he doesn't know he's just too cool? EW: Diamond with a backbreaker on Mitchells... cover... 1... 2... kickout by Mitchells. JBN: Give the Ultimate one this... he doesn't stay down. Well... at least he hasn't yet in this match. But he will. EW: Diamond now throws Mitchells out of the ring, and Mitchells is really pulling for his air out there... here comes Diamond... and Mitchells catches him with a punch to the stomach, doubling The Heartthrob over! JBN: This is a mistake for the technician, Diamond, to enter the turf of the street brawler, Mitchells! EW: Indeed, it would seem to be so as Mitchells WHIPS Diamond hard into the ringsteps! JBN: OW! That's gonna leave a mark on Dave's handsome face! EW: It would seem that it has, as Diamond is too busy checking his teeth for damage to notice Mitchells has a hold of a handful of camera cables... JBN: And there goes camera four as Mitchells chokes out Diamond with the cord. It would seem the only person more blind than cameraman number four this right now is referee Dennis Larson, who can't seem to figure it out. Let's see... his back is turned. He's holding on to a cable, and Diamond is flailing like he can't breathe. Duh! I thought ol' Feldman was bad... EW: You never seem to complain when it's your favorite boy who's being cheated by referee inaction. JBN: Shut up, puke! EW: Now Mitchells appears to be done with that particular interlude... and he throws Diamond back into the ring. Following him in... it looks like Mitchells is feeling quite confident right now... he picks up Diamond... fairly impressive given the size difference between the two... and now he follows him up, it looks like he's going for his Buckle Knocker belly-to-belly superplex! JBN: And if he gets it we could have a huge upset on our hands! EW: Mitchells in place... but Diamond stands up and leans forward! Thesz press off the top! 1.... 2.... Mitchells gets a shoulder up! What a surprising turnaround! JBN: Oh yeah, puke... just goes to show the intelligence of Dave Diamond! EW: And now Diamond picks up Mitchells and gives him a huge atomic drop! Scoop up.. and Diamond nails him with a shoulderbreaker! JBN: Despite a few small errors, Diamond has really dominated this matchup! EW: Diamond... getting an idea into his head that this match is just about over... whips Mitchells into the turnbuckle... here comes the California Crush! But Mitchells is out of the way!!! JBN: Butt-first into the turnbuckles! That's gotta hurt like... [quietly] damned censors won't let me have any fun! EW: Mitchells seeks to capitalize, and as Diamond rebounds, he grabs him and throw him in to the far turnbuckles... here comes hisown Ultimate Splash into the turnbuckle.... BUT OH! JBN: Nobody's getting lucky with the turnbuckles! EW: Indeed, "Ultimate" Mike Mitchells just splashed the turnbuckles hard! Now Mitchells stumbles out of the corner... grabbed by Diamond... Hollywood Death Drop!!!!! JBN: Out of nowhere!!! EW: The cover! 1... 2... 3!!!! "The Hollywood Heartthrob" wins this one! JBN: He might have missed his California Crush, but in the end he still got what he wanted... his opponent falling dazed out of a corner! What a tactician! EW: But a game performance by Michael Mitchells. JBN: Yeah, whatever. Easy match for Dave. Especially considering he suffered heavy burns two weeks ago. EW: A good point... Diamond has bounced back remarkably well from that would-be career ending injury. JBN: Well enough to beat this two-bit punk, at least! EW: And maybe, Johnny, you're right. Maybe a man as small as Mitchells just can't compete with big guys like Diamond. JBN: Imagine if that had been The Man They Call Nug. He would have FLATTENED him! EW: Point taken. And moving right along, we have a few words from a man who last Thursday made a successful NPWA debut... Cesar "The Great Dragon" Montano! *********************************************************************** <Camera opens inside a The New Cesar Montano Dojo in Toronto, Canada. The construction crew is still building parts of the Dojo like the roof and other stuff. Cesar Montano is standing outside and watching the constructors build the dojo. He is wearing a big coat, white pants, and a bandana. His handsome face looks palish brown because of the cold. Cesar is shivering in the cold weather. He turns around and looks at the camera> Cesar: Brrrr.. Good lord.. it's cold in Canada. Brrrr.. Ne..never knew it was gonna be this co..cold!!(rubs his hands together). But I love it here. Brrrrr.. I can't wait to get started in NPWA. Hope I will have the same luck as..as..I did in TCW. I looked around the fed and so far I like what I saw.. I have always wanted to make it in NPWA and NOW I made IT!! I have been training for awhile now and I know a lot of good wrestlers is in this fed and I hope to become on of the best in this fed. <A gust of cold wind blows past> Cesar: BRRRRRR!!! Whoa!! Now that was cold.. Anyways.. I heard a whole bunch of wrestlers came from this fed like Val the Mad, Sebastion Jericho, and more!! Most of the wrestlers here might not know me now..so let me tell y'all a bit about myself.. I believe highly in respect and honor and the CODE of the Warrior. So many has violated this code and I hope to teach them the right way. I had my first NPWA victory against 'SURFING' SAMMY DEAN. He fought a well respected fight and I gotta give him that. Hope one day we will meet again. NPWA... prepare yourself for the DRAGON!! And.. <A car stops near Cesar. The window rolls up. It's Master Tzu, Cesar's sensei] Cesar: Good day, Master.(bows his head) Tzu: Same to you.(bows) Cesar: Ummm.. Tzu: Aren't you gonna come with me to Niagara Falls?? Cesar: But what about the dojo?? And my training?? Tzu: You have trained to much. Need to have fun. Cesar: Ohhh..kay!! <Cesar opens the car door and enters it. The scene fades away> *********************************************************************** JBN: What is that guy on? "Surfing" Sammy Dean put up a good fight? Was he watching the same match I was? I saw Dean do a good impression of a fold-up chair, but that's about all he did well. EW: Personally, I think it's nice that he gives some kind words to the up-and-coming Dean... maybe it'll improve his performance in his next match. JBN: Doubt it. EW: Yeah... well... me too. But that's beside the point, because right now, we've got comments right now from Dark Justice on his match, coming up next, against the NPWA's favorite Satanist, Wicked Lester! JBN: Should be interesting. How exactly DO you train for a psychotic lunatic? *********************************************************************** [The setting, an empty Madison Square Gardens arena and at the centre the squared circle mat, coming down the aisle, an ominous figure dressed up in somekind of a magician/Houdini outfit. He climbs into the ring, it looks like Dark Justice, our freelance cameraman curiously follows him closely. Dark Justice spots our cameraman and graciously invites him to the ring] Looks like my quest for the titles will be challenged by the NPWA Sadistic/Magician Champion, Lester the Wicked. Yeah, you look tough and you think tough but are you tough enough to dish it out with the likes of me. You may have forces that surround you to lead you to many victories, but I too have forces that surround me that lead me to even bigger victories that only you can be jealous of. I've seen you make opponent bleed, rivet in pain and squeal like a pig, I can do that too, my style, watch! [Dark Justice snaps his fingers and three wrestlers run from the aisle and towards the ring to ambush Dark Justice, the first wrestler is about to clothesline DK but out of nowhere DK unleashes a superkick knocking teeth and making the poor loser bleed like a stump pig. The second wrestler gets a hold of DK from the back, but DK reverses it, applies a standing double chicken wing hold and cracks the shoulders in half making the poor sucker rivet in pain. The third wrestler seeing what happened to the first two is so scared and nervous he starts to scream like a pig and is all over the mat hysterical from fear] I've seen you make wrestlers disappear too, watch me do the same trick as well! [As Dark Justice starts to make his way towards his opponents, they start running for their lives and out of the ring towards the dressing room] We do have our similarities, except I do it with a lot more pain and more effective results. I didn't come in the NPWA just to walk in the park and get the title just like that. No I thrive on beating up on things like you and the more my opponents fight back, the stronger and better I get. It's easy to say that you are just another stepping stone in the NPWA hierarchy, I don't have to say it because it is a given fact already that you're demise is written and evident and imminent. There is nothing fancy about me, I'm nothing but all natural, no preservatives, 100% pure pain. And believe me, you will taste that all goodness and delicious pain from the top of your scrawny head and down to your fungus infested toes. Get ready to take massive doses of tylenol 3, morphine, and any of your other top 10 pain killers 'cause it ain't going to help you before, during and after the match. You will pay and repent for the sins that you have committed and I will exorcise your soul through my brand of justice. Bring your magic tricks, sorcery and wonder powder. Your circus act ain't gonna work on your biggest nightmare. Get ready to notch another loss and get ready to go back to hell where you belong. I will be the one to put you back into the Pandora Box and keep you there for good. Why? Because I am the law... this is what I do best. [The arena lights begin to dim and all of a sudden Dark Justice starts to disappear as well, could it be that he might be a....... nah! The damn batteries in this camcorder might be losing its charge] *********************************************************************** JBN: Heheheheh Hoohoohoho. That Dark Justice is just too much. He pretended he was a freak like Lester, but then revealed it was just the camera! What a WIT! EW: Alright, Johnny... enough sarcasm out of you, thank you very much. This upcoming match could be big for both men's careers! JBN: Oh yeah, puke... it may be. But then again, you never know with this business, do ya? Neither one of these guys is the kind who's likely to take his ball and go home just because he loses a match. In fact, I'd say both parties will come out of this match better.... EW: That which does not kill us only makes us stronger? JBN: Right... only... That which royally pisses us off makes us wanna beat the crap out of the next loser that gets in our way even more. EW: Only from Johnny B. Nastee, fans. Last minute predictions? JBN: Lester... Justice... Christ, I don't know. EW: Ladies and gentlemen, the voice of decision, Mr. Johnny B. Nastee. And now, back up to ringside for the introductions! *********************************************************************** DP: This contest is scheduled for one fall, with a 20 minute time limit. Your referee for this contest is Willie Feldman. Introducing first... [The sounds of a rattle getting progressively louder and softer is heard.] DP: He hails from Gotham City. Weighing in at 305 pounds. Ladies and Gentlemen...here is....Dark....Justice. [Very dark, ultraviolet lighting marks his entrance, along with the eerie rattle sounds as his music entrance. He walks slowly in a straight line to the ring almost trancelike.] EW: As always, a very ominous presence being shown by Dark Justice here, Johnny. JBN: I don't think he's been right in the head since he lost to the Mighty Boss back in the summer, puke. He's lost what little marbles he had, and he's here to take it out on Wicked Lester. EW: Indeed, Johnny. Let's get back up to the ring and the introduction of Wicked Lester. DP: And his opponent... ["Unholy" by KISS plays as some Lester fans begin to cheer wildly] DP: From Detroit, Michigan. He weighs in at 286 pounds. Ladies and Gentlemen....WICKED LESTER! [Lester walks to the ring slowly, and spews blood over the fans on the right side of the aisle. He then walks into the ring and flames explode from the cornerposts. He then drops to his knee and prays to his medallion.] EW: Johnny, did you ever have a feeling that you're going into a situation that could be pretty bad no matter what happens? JBN: Last night with Michelle and Diana, Puke. They had me tied up and were... EW: As amusing as that story is, Johnny, We have to wait until later. Right now, two of the most frightening individuals in the NPWA are about to wrestle here, and no one wants to have to deal with the consequences. [Ding, Ding] EW: And there's the bell. Lester comes charging out and knocks down Justice. He's just pounding away on him right now. Lester picks him up and slams him back down. Lester again down with those fists of his. What a way to incapacitate the big man, Johnny. JBN: You could tie him down like Michelle and Diana had me, puke. That'd be much more effective. EW: Please. Lester picks Justice back up and now whips him into the corner. Lester charges in, but gets a foot in the face by the Dark one. Justice now takes him down with a clothesline, and he begins to stomp down on Lester. Picks Lester back up, and Justice nails him with a backbreaker. Justice now coldly staring at the fallen Lester. Justice now driving his knee into the hamstring. Smart work by Justice. JBN: Yeah. You wanna make sure that Lester can't get up, since Lester's a power wrestler. He wants to pound the heck outta Justice, and for Justice to keep him off of his vertical base, puke, that's smart. EW: Justice now picks Lester up and delivers an impressive press slam. Lester doesn't look so domineering right now. Justice picks Lester up and tosses him outside the ring. Feldman over to count as Justice goes outside the ring to fight with Lester. Goes to suplex him on the outside, but doesn't. Lester goes downstairs with a low blow and Justice grabs his groin. JBN: He looks like an ancient Eunich right now, puke. Looks like Mrs. Justice isn't going to be too happy about what's going on. EW: Indeed. Lester now grabs a chair and lays out Justice on the outside. Where's Feldman during all of this? JBN: Look towards the aisle, puke. Mike Mitchells just made his way down to the ring. EW: Feldman's over there warning him not to get involved, and he seems to be taking a seat along the security railing. Meanwhile, Lester grabs a piece of glass and is attempting to stab Justice with it. Justice for his part is holding out. Justice with a boot the the privates...into a DDT. Lester just broke that glass with his forehead. Lester's bleeding now, but I think he likes it. JBN: This is a sicko ward, puke. Lester likes the glass stuck in his forehead. Justice is enjoying the beating. Mitchells is watching this now and is just as stunned as everyone else about how sick these two are. EW: Justice now back in the ring, beating the count by Feldman. Lester follows closely behind, but Justice picks him up and nails him with a back suplex. Justice now locks on a figure four, working on that hamstring once again. Lester, blood on his face, isn't writhing in pain. Rather, he's smiling...and he's hitting himself in the head. JBN: Call Bellvue. You thought that Sampson was a nut in 96? You thought that Jon Shock was a maniac? Ladies and gentlemen, meet the new lead nut. This dude freaks me out, puke. And when the ol' Nastee Man gets scared, you know you better be afraid, too. EW: Indeed. Justice lets go of the figure four and Feldman comes over to look at the fallen, bloody Lester. Off the ropes Justice goes....*WHAM* OH MY! Mike Mitchells rushed the ring and nailed Justice with that chair. Justice didn't fall down though. Mitchells is stunned. Justice goes for Mitchells. ROLL UP BY LESTER! 1....2....3!!!!! Wicked Lester wins this one in a tight, hard fought contest for both competitors. JBN: Yeah... and something tells me that if Mr. Ultimate wasn't sore after Dave Diamond beat him a little earlier, he's gonna sure gonna be sore when Dark Justice catches up with him and beats him... again. EW: That could well be the case. [A loud sound, like a mic being dropped is heard.] JBN: Hey... where are you going? EW: [Off mic] Gotta go up to the ring, Johnny. Some important business to take care of. JBN: Hey, what the hell is this? What's Tunney doing out here? [NPWA President Billy-Joe Tunney comes out and gets in the ring with Wailen, to a mixed round of reaction.]
