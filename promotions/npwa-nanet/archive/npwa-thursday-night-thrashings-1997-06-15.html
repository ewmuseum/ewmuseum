NPWA — Card Archive

Thursday Night Thrashings (June 15/97)

15 June 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
SK: I can't believe what's going on here. The New Wave is jumping everyone in the ring. The Theatre of Pain is attacking everyone outside of the ring. The Asian Triad is in trouble. So is the Chronos Triumverate. The Hardline is just getting started and all three of them were in the hospital after Inhuman. I guess Pain Train was right when he said Anarchy Rules in the NPWA. This is insane. AG: It's just plain ridiculous. The all out war has begun. If you don't have backup you better run for cover. Who knows what's going to happen. SK: Well one thing is for sure. We've got three matches still to go so let's get to the ring. ******************************************************************** DP: Ladies and gentlemen, the following match is scheduled for one fall. The referee is Willie Feldman. Introducing first, accompanied to the ring by Lady Chain, from the forests of New England and weighing 315 pounds... here is The Lumberjack! [Lumberjack and Lady Chain make their way to the ring to the sound of chainsaws cutting lumber.] SK: Here's someone we haven't seen in action for awhile, Alexis. AG: I've heard he's been off playing softball or something... SK: A wrestler who prefers playing softball over the squared circle? Preposterous! DP: And his opponent, accompanied to the ring by The Silicone Sisters, from Long Beach, California, weighing 289 pounds... here is "The California Stud" Christian Steele! [Nikki, Vikki and Steele make their way to the ring as "California Love" plays. The guys in attendance roar approvingly at the Sisters' skimpy outfits.] SK: This should be a great match. Lumberjack is probably more powerful, but Steele is no slouch in the strength department himself. AG: He definitely has Lumberhead beat in the looks department. SK: The bell rings and we're underway. The two men lock up... standing side headlock by Steele. Lumberjack throws him off into the ropes... shoulder block by the woodsman that sends the Stud crashing to the mat. AG: Steele also has Lumberjack beat in the brains department, so he should be trying to outsmart him instead of trying to match power with him. SK: How do you know Christian Steele is smarter than The Lumberjack? AG: Didn't you read those wrestler profiles in the latest issue of the NPWA Markazine? Lumberjack listens to country music for crying out loud! SK: Lumberjack picks Steele up and sends him crashing back to the canvas with a nice side salto. AG: Hey, that was a real wrestling move! When did he learn one of those?!? SK: Lumberjack goes for the pin... only a one count. AG: Way to early to put away a stud like the Stud! SK: Steele rises to his feet with a little help from Lumberjack's fingers entwined in his hair. Vikki is up on the ring apron, distracting the referee... lowblow by Christian Steele! AG: Those little tarts may contain more chemicals than the East River in New York City, but they are a definite advantage for Christian. Of course, *I'd* be the ultimate distraction for a wrestler to exploit. SK: You looking to go into managing a wrestler now, Alexis? AG: Why, you volunteering to be my first client, Kid? SK: Moving right along... Steele has taken 'Jack down to the mat with a nice Russian Legsweep. He rolls over on top of the woodsman and starts delivering a closed left fist to the head of Lumberjack in succession. The ref is telling him to quit using a closed fist, which he promptly ignores. Now he stops and picks Lumberjack up. Whip into the ropes, he picks him up by the waist, takes a few steps backwards then drops Lumberjack throat-first across the ropes! AG: Yay!!! SK: What are you cheering for, Alexis? Lumberjack may have taken some serious damage to the throat area! AG: I'm cheering because this means he won't be singing along with those damn Waylon Jennings 8-tracks for awhile! SK: And just how do you know what type of sound system the Lumberjack has? AG: [smiles coquettishly] A lady never tells, Kid. SK: You're right, a lady never tells, so what's stopping you? Meanwhile, "The California Stud" is posing for the fans at ringside. AG: Oooh, look at those pecs, those lats, those delts... SK: He is put together, I'll grant you that. AG: In more ways than one, honey! SK: I'll have to take your word for that one... Steele is turning his attention back to his opponent. He picks him up and lifts him up for a vertical suplex... no, it's a brainbuster! This one could be over! 1... 2... AG: Kickout by Lumberjack! I don't believe it! SK: Seeing is believing, Alexis. Steele is yelling at the official about a slow count. And the Lumberjack is rising to his feet behind Steele! He grabs Steele from behind and rolls him up in a small package! 1... 2... kickout!!!! That's what you get for not paying attention to your opponent! AG: The referee was deliberately distracting Christian! He should be fired! SK: You're incorrigible! Lumberjack is on his feet and picks up Steele. Spinebuster slam!!! He drove him almost clear through the ring with that one! AG: This doesn't look good for Christian! He isn't moving! SK: Lumberjack signals for the Stump Hug... he picks Steele up and locks it on in the centre of the ring! AG: But Vikki and Nikki are up on the ring apron!!! The ref is over talking to them instead of checking to see if Steele wants to submit! SK: You can hardly blame the official, they are quite a distraction... wait! Someone is racing down the aisle... AG: It's Dave Diamond! SK: The newest member of the New Wave, "The Hollywood Heartthrob" Dave Diamond has just slide into the ring... he chopblocks Lumberjack's knees from behind! Lumberjack has to let go of Steele who collapses to the mat... Diamond locks Lumberjack up - reverse DDT! AG: I've heard he calls that his "Hollywood Death Drop!" SK: Whatever he calls it, it was devastating! Diamond picks Steele up off the mat, shakes him a bit to wake him up, points at Lumberjack then quickly slides out of the ring. Steele looks confused but goes for the pin anyway. AG: And dumb old "Blind" Willie Feldman is still over talking to The Silicone Sisters! SK: The Sisters start jumping up and down to Feldman's delight, telling him to turn around! Feldman finally, and reluctantly I might add, does so and sees Christian Steele covering The Lumberjack! Feldman counts. 1... 2... 3! It's all over, the winner, thanks to Dave Diamond, is "The California Stud" Christian Steele! The Sisters have run into the ring and are congratulating their man on his tainted victory. AG: Was Diamond acting on his own or on behalf of the New Wave? SK: I think we're about to find out. Here comes the New Wave down the aisle! Colonel Buddy Lee Jarrett and the rest of his New Wave cronies are climbing into the ring where the California Stud is standing victorious over the Lumberjack. Christian Steele looks a bit apprehensive as these goons step through the ropes. AG: He sent those airheads, Nikki & Vikki, out of the ring. It looks like he might want to fight the New Wave, but that wouldn't be very smart. [Steele stands on one side of the ring, near where the Silicon Sisters are on the floor; Colonel Jarrett and the entire NWW crowd the other side of the ring. Fishmonger hands Jarrett a "NWW" microphone] BLJ: Weeeeellll weeeeellll weeeeellll. Look who we got here. The "California Stud", Christian Steele. One a' the most promisin' newcomers to ever come to the NPWA. Now ahhhhh don't know if y'all been payin' attention, son, but lately the New Wave a' Wrasslin's been scoutin' you. Y'all have a lot of potential, just as every one of us [looks at his NWW members] showed when we first entered the NPWA. Now son, I've got a little somethin' here for you to take a look at... [Steele readies himself for a sneak attack, but Fishmonger pulls out some papers and hands them to Steele. As Steele starts to read them, his eyes grow big. He motions Nikki and Vikki back into the ring, points out something on the paper to them and they start squealing in delight at whatever it is he showed them.] So as you read that more than generous contract, Mr. Steele, what I wanna know, in this upcoming war, are y'all with us... or against us? [Jarrett throws the mic on the mat in front of Steele and waits, hands on hips, as the crowd boos] AG: The tension is unbearable, Sayonara. Steele seems to be putting a lot of thought into this decision. SK: It's a big decision. If he decides to join the New Wave, he'll be branded a turncoat for the rest of his career. But if he turns Jarrett down and stays on the side of the NPWA, he'll undoubtedly be subjected to sneak attack upon sneak attack courtesy of the NWW. AG: I wish he'd hurry up and decide. The wait is killing me! [Steele finally bends over and picks up the mic; he stays bent over longer than necessary, as if inviting the NWW to jump him] CS: Jarrett, you want me to join your motley crew. You want me to turn my back on the promotion that has given me my first big break in this business. You want me to risk being blackballed from every other wrestling organization on the planet. I've got only one answer for you.... YES! I will be a part of the Next Revolution, the New Wave of Wrestling. [the fans start to boo] CS: That's right, you all heard me. But before I go any further, I would like to reflect on the recent NPWA pay-per-view event: Inhuman'97. First let me congratulate all members of the New Wave of Wrestling on their impressive performance. [Christian Steele gives two thumbs up to the entire New Wave.] CS: Second, I would like to recommend the Silicone Sisters' plastic surgeon to Steve Spudd, who got his face torn off by those tigers. Now I hate to say I told you so... but, I told you so. I told everyone that that punk Steve Spudd would become a scratching post for those cats one way or another, and it happened. I can thank the New Wave for that. Now, I may not have had my hand raised at Inhuman, but at least I wasn't turned into a little kitty entree. And there is no way anyone can convince me that the "Real Meal" came out a winner after Inhuman. [Christian Steele pauses for a moment and glances over the crowd.] CS: All you maggots out there can say what you like about the New Wave, but at least they can recognize a winner when they see one. So since the New Wave wants to reward my talent with a whole lot of my favourite dead presidents, I've decided to accept their offer and together we're going to turn this place upside down! [Crowd boos and throws their garbage into the ring.] CS: All of you can shut the hell up right now, I've never seen a bigger bunch of hypocrites. Who doesn't work for the all mighty dollar!? I know it! You know it! The NPWA knows it! Hell, everyone in the world knows that money is what makes the world goes round. So all you lazy bums out there can take your boos and hisses, roll them up nice and tight, and stick them where the sun don't shine. I never really gave a rat's ass about the NPWA or any of you stinkin butt ugly fans so long as the Silicone Sisters and I got what we wanted. So what do you say, girls? [The California Stud looks over at Nikki and Vikki.] Nikki: You're right, baby. They just don't want to admit it. Vikki: Even the name "The New Wave of Wrestling" is great because it reminds me of the beach. CS: Don't I know it! Now shake those fine little asses over to the Colonel and The Fishmonger and show them how happy we are to be in the New Wave. [Nikki and Vikki bounces over to Jarrett and Fishmonger and each gives them a big wet kiss on cheek. Steele drops the mic and embraces the entire NWW. Much to the NWW's pleasure, Nikki & Vikki join the group hug] SK: My goodness, the New Wave adds yet another member, or three if you want to count those Sisters. This group is quickly becoming incredibly powerful with the latest additions of the legendary Bash, the Hollywood Heartthrob Dave Diamond, and now the California Stud Christian Steele. AG: Watch out, NPWA. Now that the Colonel has declared war on the NPWA, he's buying guys and girls left and right, anyone that can help him achieve his goals. ******************************************************************** SK: The NPWA is at it's most volatile right now. Everyone is choosing sides and you know that only one side will be left when it's all over. Wait... I'm getting a report that there's something going on in the back. Let's go back to the dressing room area. [A camera crew rushes in to one of the warm-up rooms a bench press has been turned over. Weights and equipment are strewn all over the room. A lot of commotion is heard around the corner. As the camera crew turns around the corner they see the Theatre of Pain beating on 'Sadistic' Steve Stone. The Fishmonger is down in the other corner with a large cut on his forehead.] AG: Looks like Stevie's paying for his comments against the Pain Train over the last month. They're tearing him apart. SK: I never thought I'd see a day where one of the New Wave was getting ganged up on. The Hell Razors are holding down his legs. The other three are holding down the rest of him. Train has one of those big weights in his hands. Oh no!! He drives it into his back. He drives it into him again. AG: It looks like Fishmonger tried to stop the attack earlier by the looks of things. Where are the rest of the New Wave. Someone has to stop these jackals. SK: I don't think they've arrived yet in the arena or whether they're supposed to show up tonight. Pain Train is telling his men to turn Stone over. Oh!! Now he's driving that weight into Stone's ribcage. Someone get some help in there before they kill Stone! AG: Stone is being pummeled. I've never seen him take a beating like he's getting right now. The North American Champ is practically unconscious. SK: All five wrestlers are kicking and stomping on Stone repeatedly. This is... here comes security finally. The Theatre of Pain is leaving but the damage has been done. What happens with the North American title match now. 'Breakfast' Bobby Toast might be too injured to wrestle and now the champion needs some medical help. AG: I'd say broken ribs and internal injuries are the minimum that he has. I wouldn't be surprised if he's got some back problems too. This group hasn't even been around for even a week and they've taken out the World Champion and the North American Champion. If I were the Muslim Massacre I'd be watching my back. SK: Speaking of them, Dan Barclay is in the ring for an interview with them. Let's go to that while the EMT's look after Steve Stone. ******************************************************************** DB: Allright, Belfast. My guests at this time, are STILL the NPWA Tag-Team Champions... [The fans go berserk] DB: Obviously they don't need any introduction. Here are the Muslim Massacre!!! [The fans roar on their feet as "Firestarter" by The Prodigy plays & The champs walk down the isle wearing leather jackets with the Irish flag colours, much to the fans approval. The champs get into the ring] CM: HELLO BELFAST!!!! [Fans go nuts] GA: It looks like the Muslim Massacre put a new meaning to the term "world class athlete's!" DB: Hello, Muslim Massacre. GA: Can't you smell it, Mr. Lord? DB: Smell what? GA: Can't you smell the sweet aroma of victory? [The fans cheer loudly in approval] CM: We did it, Dan. We defeated Resident Evil. They are behind us now, we have other challenges in front of us. GA: First of all, we would like to address the "Asian Rush". Shinya, we know you're back at your home country fighting to get your brother back. Hey, we feel your pain. I had a situation just like that, only the enemy was another country, not a gang. My brother died, Shinya. I hope yours doesn't. I guess for his sake we're gonna keep the "we're gonna kick your ass next time we meet" speeches silent for now. DB: That's very sad, General. GA: It is. But we have a job to do here. CM: That's right, General. There are now more teams forming in the NPWA. The Hardline, the Theater of Pain, the more improved New Wave, the list goes on. They all want a shot at these titles. You see, the only problem is that they all think that they're the best in the NPWA. GA: You know, that's kinda funny, Colonel. They don't have the belts, do they? CM: No, they don't. And they don't have all the fan support that we do. RIGHT BELFAST? [fans shout in approval] DB: You are arguably the most popular team in the NPWA. GA: Arguably? Lets hear that cheer again, folks! [fans go bananas] DB: That was loud... CM: Enough about that, on to the task at hand. It's time to issue the challenges again. The Lords of the Ring are coming off a great victory. they deserve a shot. GA: That's right. And hey, we should give a rematch to the former champs.. well providing the Coroner is still alive after that plane crash. CM: And remember, there's the new team of the Striker & Kenshin Nobunga - The Asian Triad. They're in line too. GA: To get to the point, we're issuing a challenge to every team in the NPWA - again. We're not hiding behind the belts. Hey, we'll even give another shot to the Resident Bedwetters. CM: There is one team though... The Hell Razor's is it? Weren't they a renegade IRCEWF punk team? GA: Yeah Colonel, I remember that team. THEY were part of the reason we didn't have the belts way back in August or September of last year! Well, I guess they now have an NPWA counterpart. We'll go through them, too! CM: It's kinda funny. All these teams are gunning for us. US, General, the supposed oil drilling millionaire spoiled brats from Saudi Arabia, who couldn't make in the big leagues, the NPWA. DB: It certainly looks like The Muslim Massacre are alive & well right now, but how are you gonna go through EVERY team in the NPWA? GA: [Laughing], well, we ARE the Muslim Massacre. Isn't that enough? [fans cheer] CM: If defending these belts to the full extent means having to fight every team in the NPWA, then so be it. We will do that.... IN TRUE NPWA HONOR & SPIRIT!! [The fans go wild as "Firestarter" plays once more & the Massacre shake hands with Dan Barclay & the fans] ******************************************************************** BL: Hey ho!! It's the Lord-man comin' atcha with the NPWA Inhuman '97 Wrap and Review Part One. I'll have the rest of the show on Monday but for now let's get to the action starting off with D.C. Destruction. We had two matches on the Countdown show. The first one featuring the former World Heavyweight Champion who apparently is back wrestling singles full time against the former number one contender Locutus in a tug of war match. Let's go to the closing moments of the match. ********************************************************************* SK: I should have seen that coming... now Locutus is tossing the badly beaten Cougar back into the ring... follows him in... looks like it's time for another Assimilator! Locutus hoists him, and.... HURICANRANA BY COUGAR!!!! AG: Whoa! Cougar was totally playing possum... seems he likes impersonating wildlife... and now Locutus is dazed! SK: And look at Cougar! He's still in this thing! He springs up to the top rope quick as he can... Locutus is getting up, with his back turned to Cougar! THE POUNCE! THE POUNCE! OH MY GOD!!! Cougar just hit that flying bulldog, and Locutus landed face-first on that Coal Miner's Glove! Locutus is cut wide open, and he's almost out cold... Cougar covers... 1... 2... Cougar picked him up! AG: Mr. Sandman ordered The Cougar to pick up Locutus! I guess, in true Chronos Triumverate fashion, they're not done beating him up yet. SK: Mr. Sandman is pointing to the top turnbuckle... and then to The Cougar's clawed glove.... Cougar goes over and picks up the glove, putting it on... AG: Oh my God, no... this can't happen... this could KILL Locutus! SK: It looks like it's going to happen, though... The Cougar is up to the top rope... AG: Oh... don't get up Locutus... just don't get up! I know he's not my favorite or anything... but he doesn't deserve this! SK: Locutus, slowly now... trying to make his way to his feet... AG: I can't watch! [A loud rumble is head, assumedly as Alexis takes off her headset.] SK: Locutus is up... Cougar leaps! OH MY DEAR [CENSORED] GOD!!!!! CATAMOUNT WITH THE CLAWED GLOVE! He's got that claw, quite literally, dug into Locutus' face! Referee Patterson counts it... 1... 2... 3!!!! It's all over! Cougar wins it... and that was a sick display by the former Champion! Well folks, give him this... if he's returning to a singles career... he just sent a message to the other competitors. And, unfortunately for Locutus, but what better way to set the tone for the Inhuman card to come with this show of violence and depravity. ******************************************************************** BL: and man was Sayonara ever right. It certainly did set the standard that would be broken all through the show. Of course we all know that the match was the last one for Locutus as he goes off to find himself and after what happened he may have to find his head too. What a move with that clawed glove. Absolute gore. The second match on the Countdown show was originally supposed to be a Television Title match but with the Mighty Boss lost that title on the last TNT before the pay per view to 'Bad Boy' Mark Bagwell. So The Mighty Boss faced off against Buddy Lee Jarrett in a handcuff match. First one to touch all four corners would win. Let's go to the footage. ******************************************************************** SK: I believe it. Jarrett is going for the turnbuckles now. He tags one buckle..... there's #2. He's having a tough time making it to the third but he does. Three down, one to go, and he may make it. He's very close, only inches away, but the Boss is putting up a great deal of resistance. AG: Come on, Buddy! You can do it! SK: Way to remain unbiased. Jarrett has no choice but to break his momentum to go back over to the Boss, who grabs him by the throat! The Boss gets up and he has one huge hand around Jarrett's neck... CHOKESLAM!!! He planted him with that maneuver! AG: Buddy Lee is still convulsing on the mat from the chokeslam. This can't be good. SK: Indeed not for the Colonel. The Boss pulls Jarrett up and hooks him... SLINGSHOT DDT!!! That's the Boss' finishing move, and it looks like Jarrett is finished. Boss easily drags Jarrett to the first turnbuckle, the second, the third, OH! That masked man just grabbed Boss' leg, stopping him. AG: The idiot Beanpole is reaching out to get the masked man. What a moron! SK: And here comes the Maltese Falcon! He bashes the Boss' skull from behind with that Falcon figurine! Sadistic Steve Stone has entered the ring now too, as well as the masked man. What's going on here?? AG: You said it yourself earlier, Kid - there are no disqualifications in this match-up, and the New Wave is just taking advantage of it. This is Inhuman, after all! SK: You're not kidding. The entire New Wave of Wrestling is stomping and kicking the barely conscious Mighty Boss. Jarrett has been helped up by the masked man. Stone, the Falcon, and the masked man drag the Boss around the ring as Jarrett is touching each top turnbuckle. AG: Ha ha ha, you could say that the Colonel is "doing the rounds". Isn't that the Beanpole's catch phrase? SK: Jarrett has got three tagged, and there's the fourth! This one is over, but if it weren't for the New Wave the Mighty Boss would have won this one. What a miscarriage of justice. ******************************************************************** BL: Miscarriage of justice as it was. It's NPWA Inhuman and you have to be prepared for things like this when dealing with the New Wave. In fact this match pretty well set the tone for the New Wave for the rest of the night. Well after that great Countdown show we were just drooling with anticipation for Inhuman and from the start it was just what we were waiting for. First off, King of the Jungle and the animals did have an effect on the match. Let's go to the footage. ******************************************************************** EW: Now Studd rolls Christian Steele over on his stomach... JBN: Six minutes... EW: The Stunning Stretch!!! He has that modified STF locked on right in the centre of the ring! JBN: The official is checking on Steele... EW: Steele taps out!!! He submits to the Stunning Stretch! This one's all over! The winner is "The Real Deal" Steve Studd! JBN: What a drag... I wanted this thing to go over 10 minutes! EW: You would. JBN: Christian Steele quickly rolls over to the door and is let out of the ring while Steve Studd celebrates in the ring. EW: This victory should definitely move him up the... what the?!?!? JBN: It's the Masked Man and "Sadistic" Steve Stone of the New Wave!!! EW: They have no business being out here... the Masked Man has a length of chain and a padlock and he quickly smacks the NPWA official away from the door and padlocks it shut! JBN: I guess Steve Studd shouldn't have been shooting his mouth off about the New Wave, eh puke? EW: This doesn't look good at all... Studd has just realized that he's trapped in the ring... he's trying to find a way out, but the top of the cage is covered in barbed wire for that electrified cage match later on tonight! JBN: Steve Stone just powerbombed the guy controlling the gate!!! EW: And Stone is cranking it wide open!!! The cats are rushing into the ring... Steve Studd is attempting to climb the cage anyway... JBN: ...and is knocked off by a leaping tiger!!! EW: The cats have Steve Studd down on the mat and are mauling him... this is horrible! NPWA officials are coming out with boltcutters... here come more of the wranglers who handle the cats with prods and wire leashes... ******************************************************************** BL: Well at the time we weren't sure what the New Wave was doing but as we saw earlier on tonight it's now quite obvious. Christian Steele may not have been New Wave at Inhuman but he is now. Next up was the Marquis de Sade match between the Lords of the Ring and The Perfect Role Models. The Lords were certainly confused and wanted out but sometimes things just work your way. ******************************************************************** EW: He's set up that folding chair in the centre of the ring. Now he's sitting Lord Warfield in it. Warfield's barely conscious. It's a wonder he just doesn't slide out of that chair. Now Mr. Wrestling is going up to the top rope. JBN: He's going for a high risk move. Talk about sadistic. EW: He leaps off towards Warfield with a top rope clothesline. But look at this ! Warfield slumps out of the chair. Mr. Wrestling connects on the chair. JBN: He didn't just connect. He went into it head first ! EW: Mr. Wrestling is rolling around clutching the top of his head. He may have some severe frontal lobe damage after that one. JBN: Gan Green is freaking out. He goes over to help his fallen tag team partner. EW: Lord Warfield is stumbling to his feet. He has no idea what continent he's on. He's trying to head over to his corner for a tag. JBN: But there are no tags in this match. This British fruitcake is completely out to lunch. EW: Lord Warfield stumbles on that chair and he trips. He just fell back on top of Gan Green and Mr. Wrestling. Gan Green is trying to get free but his foot is caught in the chair. Mr. Wrestling is flat out on his back with Lord Warfield and Gan Green on top of him. JBN: And wouldn't you know it, the ref is counting this as a pin. EW: Well I guess technically, Lord Warfield is on top of Mr. Wrestling. There's the count: 1.. 2.. 3 !!! I don't believe this. Lord Warfield has been awarded the pin and the Lords of the Ring win this match. ******************************************************************** BL: Sometimes you just catch a break. In the next match the New Wave struck again. Just when you think there's no way for them to interfere they pull the wool over your eyes. John Law was the victim against The Maltese Falcon in the S.W.A.T. Team Match. ******************************************************************** EW: He picks Falcon up and powerbombs him! He picks him up again, whip into the ropes... Law Breaker spikeslam! JBN: I think it's all over but the crying now! EW: Not quite... Law heads up top to execute the Law Maker moonsault... he jumps... hey!!! JBN: One of the SWAT Team guys hooked his ankle with his baton, causing him to fall! EW: The ref didn't see it and Law lands hard short of his mark! Falcon weakly crawls over and drapes his arm over Big John Law. 1... 2... 3! It's all over and the winner is The Maltese Falcon! JBN: I knew the New Wave would be able to pay off one of these cops... who's more corrupt than an American big city police officer? EW: The SWAT Team is heading back to the dressing room... wait a sec. One of them stops to talk to Fishmonger who points at Big John in the ring! The SWAT officer slides into the ring, pulls out his tazer and <ZZZZAAAAAAAPPPPP!!!> JBN: Look at Law doing the Lambada on the canvas with all that juice flowing through his body! EW: This is sick! Now the cop grabs his baton and starts beating on Big John Law as The Maltese Falcon holds him down! He's going to break his bones or rupture some of his internal organs if someone doesn't... JBN: Here comes the cavalry! EW: The rest of the SWAT Team is racing back to the ring, and Fishmonger orders Falcon and the rogue cop out of the ring. JBN: That cop's helmet just fell off...it's some guy with long, blond hair! EW: I doubt hair that long is allowed on the force, so you can bet that he's some New Wave plant. ********************************************************************
