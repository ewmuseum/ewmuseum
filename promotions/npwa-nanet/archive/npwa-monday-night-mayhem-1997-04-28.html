He sits is a black jeep over looking a few young kids playing in a playground. The camera slowing zooms in on the Wrecker.] [McGreggor looks very confident, and cocky] MM: Steve Sampson, <smirks> so you have a problem that I aired your "dirty laundry". Well Sampson, I have a problem with you wanting to be everyone's hero. That's right Sampson, let's cut the bulls<beep> you are no hero Steve, you are a bad influence. How many of them will turn to drugs. How many of them will turn to the company of a wh<beep>e. Steve I aired your dirty laundry not for your or my sake...I did it for the children. <chuckles> Yea right. I would _never_ follow in the footsteps of a loser...yet you don't have a problem with that. You follow Master's lead like a child trying to mimic his idol. [Cross his arms with a grin] Now you want myself to be Steve Sampson to your Brian Masters...well it's not gonna happen. Sorry to burst your little bubble. You see Sampson, I don't need your endorsement to succeed in this or any other fed. I am my own man...I bet you wish you could say the same. It's time for you and Simon to spend more quality time together at the local hole in the wall and talk about the good old'days. If anyone should have been selected to compete in this match it should have been myself. I was never given a rematch for the TV title when that damn Judge took off hiding in his chambers. Big Boss, as far as I am concerned you have my title. And as long as the TV title, Steve Sampson, and Brian Masters is in the building Thursday night...
