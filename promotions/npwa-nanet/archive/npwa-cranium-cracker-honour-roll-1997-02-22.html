Cranium Cracker of the Week (title honour roll)

22 February 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
BB: "The Cougar exits the ring quickly and heads back to the dressing room. Folks, before we go to our Moldie Oldie match, let's go to the Cranium Cracker of the Week!" ************************************************************************ CRANIUM CRACKER OF THE WEEK The answer to last week's question was: There have been 5 wrestlers who have held two belts. In order, they are: (W - World Title, TV - T.V. Title, TT - Tag Team Title, NA - North American Title) 1) Bash (W - Sep.1, 1993; TT (w. Sayonara Kid) - Jun.8, 1994) 2) Southern Rocker (W - Apr.2, 1995; TV - Dec.24, 1996) 3) The Cougar (W - Dec.24, 1995; TT (w. The Coroner) - Dec.24, 1996) 4) 'Big Daddy Cool' Amoral (TV - Dec.28, 1995; W - Dec.24, 1996) 5) 'Sadistic' Steve Stone (TT (w. A.T.M.) - Aug.20, 1995; NA - Feb.14, 1997) A few interesting things to note: - The Cougar has won both of his titles at the X-mas Gory PPV. Perhaps it should be renamed "A Very Cougar Christmas" PPV - X-mas Gory '96 featured not one, but *3* wrestlers getting their second title reigns. Did anyone bother to mention this before? Of course not! - When Stone won his second title, none of the head office boys made mention of the fact. Good thing for you folks that Bobby Cranium is on top of the action and information here in the NPWA! Hey, ham'n' eggers, you wanna prove you're not quite a total humanoid like the boob beside you there? Try to answer this one... Who was the first NWC Women's Wrestling Champion?
