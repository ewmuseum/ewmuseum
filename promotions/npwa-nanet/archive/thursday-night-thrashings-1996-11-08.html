VIEWER INSANITY IS ADVISED ************************************************************************ The NANet Professional Wrestling Association - The Revolutionary Force In Electronic Sports Entertainment For The Past Three Years! ************************************************************************ EW: Good evening one and all and welcome to Thursday Night Thrashings, coming to you this week from Brandon, Manitoba! I'm Ed Wailen and with me as always is my co-host, Johnny B. Nastee! JBN: The end of the world is at hand - The Nastee Man actually has set foot in Brandon, Manitoba! EW: My apologies to the great people of Brandon. We have a great show tonight as always. We'll see "Sadistic" Steve Stone in action as he wrestles two men in a handicap match! JBN: Stone may have gotten in over his head this time...these aren't two slouches he's wrestling, we're talking former Television Champion, Mr. Wrestling and The Vigilante! EW: Of course, the U.S. presidential elections were earlier this week, and we will see how The New Wave, Senator McJoseph and Colonel Jarrett, feel about the outcome. JBN: They didn't do very well, did they? EW: No, they didn't, but we'll get into the details later. Todd Massengill will be on hand with a huge announcement about an upcoming NPWA event! And in our main event tonight, Akira Sasaki will take on Ramesses II in our last semi-final match in the Television Title tournament! JBN: The winner goes on to face Colonel Buddy Lee Jarrett in the finals! EW: Indeed! We will have many other surprises along the way as well. Fans, a few weeks ago we saw The Mighty Boss in action. He also vowed that he was going to launch a personal investigation into the use of dangerous weapons in the NPWA. JBN: Yeah. It looks like that donut-sucking renta-cop has a problem with people bringing squeegees and cereal boxes to the ring. He should bust you for what you bring here every week, puke. EW: I'm almost afraid to ask, Johnny, but what are you babbling about ? JBN: That hair-piece on your head, Wailen ! That thing's deadly. Did you run over a muskrat, scrape it off the road, and stick it on your noggin ? You should at least be busted for cruelty to animals. EW: Arrgghhh ! Let's take it away for this look at The Mighty Boss. It seems like he's come across something which has caught his attention. ************************************************************************* <The Mighty Boss is in a police department squad room reviewing some video tape. He's watching highlight's from Akira Sasaki's Trick or Treat Match against Locutus. The Boss slowly reviews the part where Akira is clubbing Locutus with his kendo stick> MB: This is exactly the sort of stuff I'm looking for ! Fans, it's the Mighty Boss here and this video footage is the proof I need. This Akira Sasaki used a dangerous weapon on his opponent, and could have caused some serious, serious injury. If this happened out on the streets, Sasaki would have been arrested and thrown into the slammer. But just because he did it during a wrestling match doesn't mean he's going to get away with it. I'm here to enforce the law of the NPWA. If Akira Sasaki or any other criminal offenders want trouble, then they're going to get trouble. I'm looking to make some arrests here, and believe me, I'm one cop who doesn't need back-up. Akira Sasaki, I'll be watching you very closely for more violations like this. If you wind up on the wrong side of the law, I'll be there waiting for you. Remember, Sasaki, The Mighty Boss is making his rounds here in the NPWA ! ************************************************************************ EW: It sounds like The Mighty Boss means business, Johnny! JBN: Who appointed him the guardian angel of the NPWA?! This is professional wrestling, not some pansy sport like polo or table tennis! EW: It may be wrestling, Johnny, but what some of our NPWA stars have been getting away with lately goes beyond what is reasonable inside the squared circle! We will see if the Boss' warning has any bearing on Akira's match later tonight. Right now, let's go up to the ring where Howard Tinkle is standing by for the introductions in our opening match! ************************************************************************ HT: This special handicap match is scheduled for one fall with a 30 minute time limit. First, from his autumn residence at Martha's Vineyard, weighing 230 pounds, here is Mr. Wrestling! And his partner, weighing 250 pounds and hailing from The Grand Canyon, here is Vigilante! (Mr. Wrestling and Vigilante make their way to the ring to the sound of Steve Stone's "ambulance sirens" HT: And their opponent, accompanied by Gan Green, weighing 298 pounds and hailing from Parts Unknown, here is "Sadistic" Steve Stone! EW: Steve is coming to the ring prepared, he's even bringing the gurney for his opponents to be carried out on! JBN: Stone may be leaving on that gurney himself! He's facing two devious and dangerous opponents in Vigilante and Mr. Wrestling! I'm not sure if the guidance of Gan Green will be enough to overcome this two on one situation. EW: It will be The Vigilante starting off against Stone. And Stone doesn't even wait for the bell! He's hammering away on Vigilante with those huge arms of his! JBN: That ref's a new guy, ain't he? EW: Yes, officiating his first NPWA match is Nick Cataract. JBN: And Gan Green is playing him like a violin! EW: The ref is paying attention to Green, ordering him off of the ring apron, and Steve Stone is choking Vigilante with both hands! Mr. Wrestling comes in to save his partner, and gets a kick to the, uh, lower midsection for his troubles! JBN: "Lower midsection"...that's a polite way of putting it! EW: Green hops down to the floor, the ref turns back to the action and sees both Vigilante and Mr. Wrestling in the ring, and he orders Wrestling out of the ring. JBN: Classic Gan Green! EW: Stone locks up Vigilante...DDT! He goes for the pin...1...2...and Mr. Wrestling makes the save with a double axe handle to the back of The Sadistic One's head! JBN: Gan Green is furious! EW: So is Steve Stone, who picks Vigilante up and tosses him into the corner, daring Mr. Wrestling to make the tag. Wrestling does so and enters cautiously. JBN: Stone looks huge compared to Mr. Wrestling! EW: They lock up...and Mr. Wrestling hits Steve Stone with a low blow! The he kicks him in the back of the knee, causing Stone to go down on one knee. JBN: I knew two men would be too much for him! EW: Now Mr. Wrestling, on the offensive, kicks Steve in the stomach, then moves around behind and takes him down to the mat face first in a full nelson. JBN: His arms barely fit around Stone's shoulders! EW: Nonetheless he has Steve Stone off of his feet, taking away the size advantage. He kneels on the small of Stone's back and puts some sort of claw hold on the trapezeus nerves of Stone. Referee Cataract asks Steve Stone if he wants to submit, which enfuriates Stone! Stone leaps to his feet! JBN: With Mr. Wrestling still hanging on to his shoulders...he's dangling in the air!!! EW: Stone runs backward into the corner, crushing Mr. Wrestling against the turnbuckles! He turns around and buries a series of rights and lefts into the midsection of Mr. Wrestling, who collapses to the canvas! Steve Stone turns away and raises his arms, and the crowd cheers him heartily! JBN: The fans will never forgive Mr. Wrestling for pawning the T.V. Title, so they are enjoying this beating at the hands of The Sadistic One! EW: The referee goes over to Stone, telling him to get back to the match, and...and Gan Green has snuck over to the corner, pulled Vigilante down to the floor and...Spinebuster! And right on that concrete floor! JBN: I bet Vigilante is wishing the NPWA used mats around their rings right about now! EW: Stone and the ref turn back to the action in the ring, and of course Gan has gone back to the other side of the ring with the official none the wiser. Stone hauls Mr. Wrestling to his feet... Irish Whip...powerslam! Gan Green is up on the ring apron again, yelling at Steve Stone! GG: Come on Steve, hit him in the family jewels! Choke him out! Here, use these! (Gan reaches into his pocket and starts pulling out brass knuckles, a roll of quarters, a roll of tape, a length of rope...) JBN: This guy is a walking department store! Wonder if he has any XXX videos in there? EW: Will you stop?!? Luckily, Steve is telling Gan he doesn't need any of that stuff, and a good thing too since the ref is right there watching Gan offer it to him! Steve pulls Mr. Wrestling to his feet once more, and he is motioning to the crowd that it is time for 9-1-1! JBN: Bombs away! EW: Face first power bomb by Steve Stone, and it is all over! Stone goes for the pin...wait a second! Someone just came out of the crowd and has slid into the ring...it's Grave Stone! He has a length of steel pipe and he nails Stone in the back of the head with it! Nick Cataract is calling for the bell! Stone shakes off the blow and gets to his feet... JBN: And man oh man, is he pissed! EW: Not quite how I would have worded it, but accurate nonetheless! But Grave Stone has pulled Mr. Wrestling out of the ring, gathered up Vigilante outside and the three men are making their way back to the dressing room area. The winner, by disqualification, is "Sadistic" Steve Stone, but you have to think that Steve won't be happy with the outcome. JBN: No, he had Mr. Wrestling beat for the 1-2-3, and he will not accept a victory any other way. We haven't heard the last of this one! EW: Well fans, for those of you who followed the US elections, you'll know that President Clinton got re-elected. What this means of course is that the NPWA World Champion: Senator Carthy McJoseph, saw his presidential dreams go up in smoke. JBN: McJoseph got ripped off. He should demand a recount ! EW: I doubt a recount would help him much. I understand the margin of victory was, er, quite wide, to put it politely. I think Pamela Lee Anderson got more votes than the Senator did.
