( 6) The Hutt Brothers ANALYSIS The Singles Picture Last month's number one contender, Colonel Buddy Lee Jarrett, managed to wrest the World Title away from Big Daddy Cool Amoral in a triangle matc= h. Amoral falls to the number one contender's spot, while Steve Sampson, Brian Masters and The Man They Call Nug are all poised near the top of the rankin= gs to provide a strong challenge to Jarrett and the other singles champions, North American Champ Steve Stone and new Television Champion, The Mighty Boss. The other participant in the triangle match for the World Title, Locutus, sees the biggest drop of the month, falling from the number two contender all the way down to number seventeen. Mark Bagwell, John Hunter and Mick McGreggor all made strong moves into the top ten, and are expected to start contending for singles gold. El Corazon de Piedra, seemingly poised to make a strong run for a title at number eight last month, was unmasked at April Assault, revealed to be Reynoso Torres, and has taken a nose dive in the rankings to number fourteen. Four new wrestlers make their debut in the rankings this month and should make things difficult for the top contenders to hang onto their spots next month. The Tag Team Picture There was a bit of a shakeup and an interesting development in the tag team ranks this month - The Muslim Massacre managed to defeat The Cadaver Connection at Assault (with a little help from Resident Evil) and became the new tag team champions. Normally, a team that has just lost the titles would automatically be the number one contender, but this month, both Resident Evil and the former New Asian Strikers, Sasaki and Nobunaga steamrolled right over the former champs to secure the number one and two positions, leaving the Connection flailing at the number three position. The rest of the tag team rankings stayed pretty much the same, with The Perfect Role Models and The Lords Of The Ring falling a bit due to the chaos surrounding the title change. Rumour has it some new tag teams may be appearing in May and should give the teams currently in the rankings a run for their money. ************************************************************************* LETTERS TO THE EDITOR Dear Editor, How is President Tunney doing? Worm Hey there! Ya missed me? Of course you did, but I'm back this month with my critique on the latest NPWA masterpiece, April Assault. I've improved my column a bit with more emphasis on the ramifications of each match, and some new features such as Squirm, which is an indication of how "hardcore" the match was, and my own personal reflection on who my expected victor(s) was/were. You might also notice that my Turnbuckles Rating System (Out of 4) will be much tougher. I guess I was taken in by the novelty of the wrasslin business and was impressed by most matches. But after being here for a while, and having had a PPV under my belt, I'm much more picky. So without further ado, let's take a look at the 1997 April Assault!
