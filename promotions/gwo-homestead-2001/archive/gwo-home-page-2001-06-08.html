GWO home page (8 June 2001)

8 June 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
CARNAGE CARD NOW UP The Card for our next Carnage, and the last show before Day of Reckoning, is now up. You may notice it's quite a short card, but I won't book anyone a match unless the Roleplay, and at the moment only a few of you have bothered. A huge thanks to those who have roleplayed though, and I'll try to give you all what you deserve. Commisioner Cable The Global Wrestling Organisation is a new E-Fed looking for as many new superstars as possible. We run two cards a week: Carnage on Fridays, run by Ex-Wrestler Commisioner Cable, and Whiplash on Tuesdays, run by President, and Co-commisioner of the GWO, President Benson. You can contact use by using the links on the left to e-mail us. Any requests for matches/interferences/creation of tag-teams or stables should be sent to the appropriate commisioner (for matches). We don't insist on huge Roleplays, but if you don't roleplay for your match, don't expect to win. We simply ask that you try to Roleplay when you can and keep your Roleplays and catchphrases original. There are no limits on joining the GWO, but please, no WWF or WCW rip-offs please. We don't want to see 'The Crock' or 'Rock Hard Steve Baustin'. Feel free to email us with any questions. Anyway, as soon as we get anough wrestlers on the Roster we will run our very first Carnage, and Pay-per-views will follow. Happy Wrestling!
