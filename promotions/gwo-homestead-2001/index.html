Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Global Wrestling Organisation, GWO on its own pages, introduced itself in May 2001 as a new e-fed looking for as many new superstars as it could find. It ran two cards a week: Carnage on Fridays, run by Ex-Wrestler Commisioner Cable, and Whiplash on Tuesdays, run by the president and co-commisioner, President Benson. It did not insist on huge roleplays, but a wrestler who did not roleplay for a match should not expect to win; it asked for original roleplays and catchphrases, no WWF or WCW rip-offs, and wrote: “We don't want to see 'The Crock' or 'Rock Hard Steve Baustin'.”

On 21 May 2001 President Benson reported 27 wrestlers in the federation and a plan of a Friday Night Carnage, a Tuesday Night Whiplash, another Carnage and then a first pay-per-view, with some massive title matches coming; wrestlers who wanted a specific match were to e-mail the right booker. By 8 June 2001 the card for the next Carnage, the last show before Day of Reckoning, was up. Commisioner Cable called it quite a short card, because he would not book anyone a match without the roleplay, and only a few had bothered.
