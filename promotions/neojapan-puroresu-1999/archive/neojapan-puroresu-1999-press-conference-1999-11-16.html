NJPW Press Conference (16 November 1999)

16 November 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Establishing Shot -- A large compound in rural Japan. Rolling hills, green trees, and picturesque mountains in the background. It looks like a cross between a college campus and a maximum security prison. A huge gated fence bears the ubiquitous MISHIMA logo.)
(Exterior Shot - The MISHIMA compound is perpetually in motion, scientists, businessmen, and corporate drones buzz through the scenery, all feeding Kazuya Mishima his metaphorical royal jelly. Most of the employees sleep there in large coffin motel style dormitories, built like Mariott hotels, but crammed in like sardines. It also seems clear that there are underground catacombs; vast tracts of laboratories and holding cells for the employees who cross the system.)
(Move to - The centerpiece of the compound: Mishima Tower. A monolithic structure that stands 20 stories. Not huge by the standards of the skyscrapers of Tokyo or New York, but certainly enough to stand out among the neighboring farming communities.)
(Pan down to the airtight security system of the main entrance. Guards armed with MP5 submachine guns are checking credentials of all who enter.)
(Interior Shot - A lavish press room where members of Japanese and
American press await the arrival of some VIPs. While they wait, TV cameramen try to fix their shots while remote producer feast of the opulent craft services table. Sake, crudite and copious shrimp cocktail can be procured.)
(Interior Shot - An austere boardroom where MISHIMA pawns are arguing in a very fast Japanese, throwing ideas out in an almost unrecognizable dialect, exclusive to this compound. Hiroshi Okasa walks in, barks orders at these 'boys in the back room', and demands a file.)
(Tracking shot - Over the shoulder of Okasa as he walks purposefully down a hallway where people are constantly moving quickly, like that old Federal Express commercial. Okasa busts into…)
(Interior shot - Executive boardroom. That's more like it. Okasa hands
Kazuya Mishima the file, who inspects it, nods and continues what he was saying.)
Kazuya Mishima: Have a seat, Hiroshi-san. We called this press conference for many reasons, but here's the skinny. Do not, under any circumstances, answer questions about the bio-genesis project. I'll handle everything regarding generation 12. Let's do this.
(Interior Shot - The pressroom. Flashbulbs erupt as a cadre of MISHIMA dignitaries make their way to a press table. People we recognize include CEO Kazuya Mishima, Vice President of North American Operations
Adam Moore, Vice President of Puroresuringu Operations Hiroshi Okasa,
J1 champion Shoichi Mishima and Neo Japan Pro Wrestling Commissioner
Genichiro Matsumura. They are flanked by a dozen or so guards.)
(Okasa steps up to the press box as the buzz from the peanut gallery settles.) Okasa: Ladies and Gentlemen, before we begin, I'd like to remind you all that our own NJPW J1 champion, [Generation 13] Shoichi Mishima is available for your voting consideration in the RSPWF 200. Obviously, he proved his worth at Yushosha-no-Saiten, and since capturing the crown has continued his dominance. Though his feud with RSPWF#1 cruiserweight
RAPTOR has yet to come to a head, already the feud has brought out the best in each competitor. And only a Top 200 caliber champion could have
RAPTOR on the run like this.
Reporter: But isn't this the kind of blatant self-promotion that RAPTOR is famous for? Okasa: Please, hold your questions. But so you're aware, that is the kind of promotion we taught our Generation 12, preparing them for..
Sports Entertainment. RAPTOR took it like a duck to water. One lesson we learned was that promotion was better left to the corporation than the individual.
Reporter: Are there generations after Shoichi Mishima? A 14th generation? Okasa: (ignoring him) And now I would like to present the CEO of MISHIMA, the honorable Kazuya Mishima. (Flashbulbs and crowd noise as he takes the podium.) Kazuya Mishima: Members of the press, I thank you. Today is what will be my last appearance on behalf of Neo Japan Puroresu for quite a while.
(Reporters buzz) Kazuya Mishima: My reasons are quite simple. NJPW is a wrestling company. We are not "sports entertainment." We have no place for that kind of nonsense. Corporations… powers that be… it's foolish. From this point on, I'll be adopting a "hands-off" policy. My VP of Puroresuringu
Operations, Hiroshi Okasa, will be acting as my liaison.
And although the company could benefit from my direct contact… God knows we need some worthy challengers for Gen 13… I'll be retreating to the sidelines for the time being.
Reporter: Does this have anything to do with Generation 14? Kazuya Mishima: No, let me state this once and for all… There IS no generation 14. One thing I will do with my time away is try to incorporate a new image for the corporation and it starts with a change in the company name. The unfriendly foreboding Mishima Financial Empire will be dropped in favor of MISHIMA. We feel this will give our corporation a fresh, family friendly image. Any other questions?
Reporter: Does this mean you'll halt your genetic experimentation? Kazuya Mishima: We have no comment on that at this time. Thanks you all for coming out here, enjoy the shrimp cocktail and have a good day.
(The rank and file of MISHIMA exits the room to shouted out questions regarding the genetic tampering inquest.)
