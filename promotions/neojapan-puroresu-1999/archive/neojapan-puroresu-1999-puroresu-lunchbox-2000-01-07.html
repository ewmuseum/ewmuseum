=== Tasogare no Prince === *From total blackness, Twilight slowly fades into view. Across a wide expanse of gently waving grasses, the sun sinks inexorably below the horizon, streaking the ever darkening sky above with oranges, pinks, and purples, as if God himself has dragged several brightly colored paintbrushes across it. The clouds in the Twilight sky blaze bright red, as they reflect the last bit of sunlight. Above the clouds, the first few stars, along with the dim outlines of the moon, are faintly visible.
The camera pans slowly across the grassy plain. A breeze blows across the plain, causing the waist-high blades of grass to ripple, as if a stone had been tossed into a vast green pool. Standing amongst the tall grass is Tasogare no Prince -- The Prince of
Twilight, and NJPW JI Middleweight Champion... His purple trenchcoat is open, the edges gently flapping in the breeze, revealing the gleaming J1
Middleweight belt with it's light purple strap around his thin, yet muscular waist. In his right hand, he holds a single white orchid, which he lifts to his face, and inhales deeply of its fragrance. His long, silvery hair moves all around his head and shoulders, seemingly given life by the wind which blows all around him. He lowers the flower from his face, opening his eyes as he does so, and looking directly into the camera...*
Once again, I speak to you from the Twilight, and once again, I find in it the peace and tranquility which I so need and prize. No matter how stressful or unpleasant the rest of the world may become, the
Twilight will always remain a source of solace and comfort. However, after the response I recieved on the last edition of Denkoo Puroresu, I am fortunate and honored to have found another source of inspiration in the fans of NJPW. For that, I can never be capable of showing my true gratitude.
Yet still, I must return to the Twilight, for it is here, that I am able to hear the call of Destiny. The same Destiny that has chosen you, Shinji Nakata, as the first man to face the Prince of Twilight as J1
Middleweight champion. And even though it is a non-title match, I can assure you, Nakata-san, that you can expect no quarter from me. I can only assume that you would expect and want no less.
I am aware of your accomplishments in the recent All-Asian Tag Tournament, Nakata-san, I am duly impressed. You have truly proven yourself worthy of bearing the name of your father. A compliment I do not give out lightly. I have had the pleasure of having seen your father wrestle in the ring, and he was truly a great competitor.
Nakata-san, you base your career on the concepts of honor and discipline. I am no stranger to these ideas. It was honor that called me to go to America and reclaim the J1 Middleweight title from dishonorable Chris Del Rio. My entire career, it has been my discipline which has allowed me to achieve all the titles and other accolades which have been awarded me. I know all about honor and discipline,
Nakata-san. But there is one thing with which I am intimately familiar with that you are not, and that is
Destiny... You see Nakata-san, it is my knowledge of Destiny that guides me. All that I do, all that I ever will do, is quided by it. It is Destiny that assures me that when we meet this week, you will fall before the Prince of
Twilight. Kore wa sakete toore me, anata no unmei -- You cannot avoid your destiny. Shinji Nakata, for you that Destiny is to be the first to fall before the new
NJPW J1 Middleweight Champion, and nothing can change that. As for this, "Black Friday," I do not yet know who or what it means, but whether it is a new enemy, or an old one launching some new scheme, it matters not.
For it is bound to fail. Such is Destiny. *He pauses, smelling the white orchid in his hand as he does so...* Shinji Nakata, Black Friday or not, this week, you will enter into the Twilight, and you will leave it...
*He sniffs the orchid once more, closing his eyes in pleasure as he does so...* Forever changed... *The Prince of Twilight turns his back to the camera, and slowly walks away through the tall grass as the camera pans slowly back across the top of the waving grass, to where the last bit of the sun is disappearing below the horizon, and fades to black...*
=== RAPTOR === [Setting is the Ryoanji rock garden of Shokokuji monastery in Kyoto; it is merely old boulders placed in a mystically aesthetic way, which causes hundreds of monks to journey there each year, to stare at the boulders in the sand and thereby gain peace of mind. Alex RAPTOR ambles into camera view, jabbering into a handset]
RAPTOR: …no, little brother appears to be out of the picture for the time being; and I almost feel bad about it… well, I wanted to do it myself.
Hai, he’s the least of our worries, comrade; there is an even bigger hurdle than he. Hai, I saw the writing on the wall long before I caught the vignette.. I almost couldn’t believe he was coming back; he may already be in Nihon for all I know.. That’s fine …whatever he has in store for us is going to be met with equanimity, I assure you of that.
[pauses, pursing his lips as he listens] He’s no moron, whatever plans he has in place have been cemented for some time now; he was never one to tread lightly into situations of this delicate a nature.
[Amongst this plethora of holy men, the rambunctious one stands out like a lone raisin in a bowl of oatmeal; a walking dichotomy to the awe- inspiring location and serene imagery, RAPTOR is always living his own part, sporting the all too familiar in-ring attire of blue Everlast boxing trunks, taped fists, bare feet. He paces the fringes of the monastery until he arrives at a mat of rice paper, where he seats himself in full lotus position. Items such as his backpack, hookah, shurikens, and canteen, are symmetrically arranged beside the rice paper, along with a burning stick of incense cased in a coiled holder]
RAPTOR: Black Friday? What’s that all about? Hai, well, he can market it however he likes.. What? No, I don't imagine now, in the turbulent promotion we call NJPW, that it would be prudent to start trusting any parties on the administrative side. The belly's broke, the goo is dry.
[call waiting tone] RAPTOR: Hold on for a moment, I’ve got another call.. [clicks over] Moshi-moshi? [The smoke of the burning incense forms an ascending trail that forms a thought bubble above RAPTOR’s head. This new phone call has him somewhat rattled; RAPTOR’s usual arrogant demeanor is usurped by a certain austerity to his posture that hints of fear and concern]
RAPTOR: Well, well, well, if it isn’t my old buddy.. NO! You listen to
ME, foolio! If you’re taking umbrage with something I did or said, then bring it up with me mano y’ mano.. step on up and get served, pally,
I’m not going anywhere. BLACK FRIDAY, huh? You’re real witty. [pauses]
Really now? I’m not a difficult man to find.. Oi! [many of the Buddhist crusaders are now distracted by RAPTOR’s boisterousness] HAI! Just look behind you, I’ll be the one breaking his LEG off in your ASS! Hey, HEY, you know what THIS sounds like? [takes his index finger and violently hangs up line 2, and resumes his first discourse]
RAPTOR: What a dick.. wha? No, NO, not you.. We need to get back to the drawing board, desu né? It’s time to circle the wagons. I got this basket case crank calling ME now.. who the f#*k does he think he is? That’s MY shtick..
I’m not yelling, who’s yelling? At any rate, I’m about ready to head back, I just want to load up a few hookahs and I’ll be on my way.. ja né!

=== Shinji Nakata === [Scene: The camera opens to a shot of Neo Japan Pro Wrestling’s Shinji
Nakata, clearly exhausted from his training, coming out of the sauna.
Wearing only a black and red towel covering his lower half and a matching towel around his neck, he leans his chiseled frame back against the wall of his relaxation room and pauses to meditate a few seconds before acknowledging the camera.]
Nakata: It has been an exhilarating few days since the aftermath of the
J1 Real Grade Tag League...to say the very least. What has come out of this is that I now stand alone once more, and I have a renewed sense of fighting spirit as well. And I owe all of this to you, Takao Goto. [He has an ironic smile on his face as he says this.] You see, where I had been doubting myself earlier, when you chose to walk out on me, I felt the burning vigor return to my warrior’s spirit, and the fighting blood of my predecessors flowed through my veins once again. And while I eventually lost, I lost with honor and dignity. [His face hardens now, his demeanor briefly darkening.] Which are notions that you obviously have no concept of. But for now, I will put your betrayal in the past. You reap what you sow eventually.
[*Telephone rings*] Nakata: Excuse me one moment. [The camera follows Nakata as he walks into another room of his house and picks up a black cordless phone off of a dresser.]
Nakata: Hello? Yes, but could you please call back, I’m cutting a promo for NJPW right – what??
[Nakata’s eyes are wide open as he intently listens to the other end of the receiver. His mouth is agape and wide open as he hears the news from the other end]
Nakata: Of course I’ll take it!! Domo arigatou!! [He enthusiastically hangs up the phone and whirls around to face the camera, apparently jubilant from the phone message he has received.]
Nakata: Now, where was I? Never mind, what’s important now is that I have a match on January 7, 2000 against Tasogare no Prince! This is my chance to prove myself as a true star, a legitimate contender for the
J1 Middleweight Crown! (It would be nice if I were getting a title shot now, but good things come to those who wait, I suppose...)
Tasogare no Prince, make no mistake about it, you are truly one of the top wrestlers in Japan, and I have a tremendous amount of respect for you and your abilities. I watched your match with Chris Del Rio at
AWMC Explosion along with everyone else in Japan hoping you would win back the J1 Middleweight Title for Neo Japan. But this is my chance to establish myself in the upper echelon of the ranks in puroresu, and I’m not about to take that chance lightly. You often speak of destiny working in your favor; however, destiny can be a double-edged sword.
It is my destiny as a member of the Nakata clan to achieve greatness. Prepare yourself, as I will be training and looking forward to our encounter in Kobe Bunka Hall Friday....
