Denkoo Puroresu

7 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(If you can't smell the sweat form the gym, than you're not close enough to the television. The gym is decidingly un-Japanese--very gaijin. A single light hangs about a wrestling/boxing ring. It is the only apparatus providing light in this squalid hellhole of a training center. There is at least one person here; his brown Caterpillar boots can be seen up to the ankle. The rest of him resides in the shadows.
The sounds of wrestling announcers calling a match can faintly be heard, more crowd noise then anything else.)
Voice: So I tried it. I took about three steps onto the island nation of Japan, had a few cups of sake with a man known as Tetsuo Jack Weston and promptly left for the United States for one of the most important matches of my life. I have been healing ever since.
You know, the human body was made to absorb only so much punishment before it ultimately flips you the bird and says "ENOUGH!." I thought mine had done that about six months ago. I have had so many knee surgeries I'm going to name my first kids Patella and Meniscus. I've got bone floating here, there and everywhere. Spurs, chips, pieces...and still I beg for more. Because...because I made a statement not too long ago and I was never able to make good on it. (The voice intensifies) And if I can say one thing, it's that I don't walk away from the conviction of my words without at least an attempt.
NJPW gave me that chance and I let them down by not living up to the billing. Hell, at least I could've gone out on a stretcher here in
Japan. Then I would know whether or not I could hack it in the land of puroresu. But I went out in America. Only I had a big slab of gold and leather clutched tight to my breast as they wheeled me into the ambulance.
So...I suppose this is the Resurrection, NJPW. I know that metaphor doesn't fly too fast in a land of Buddhists, but I think you get the picture. Somehow...someway...the technology and medicine of the New
Millennium put Humpty Dumpty back together one more time. I'm the next generation's bionic man.
And though you don't know me...though I choose at this time not to re-introduce myself, I assure you that when it's right, you'll remember this and me, even if my last appearance had the longevity of Hale-Bopp.
A combination of God, myself, some fine surgeons and knowledgeable rehab staff got me back to the point where I can compete again. Maybe for the last time. I've run bums and beasts out of arenas on three continents.
But never....(the voice pauses for an extended period)....here. Never in the o-n-e p-l-a-c-e that separates the gimmicks from the grapplers;
that worships this sport like the pot-bellied guy in the Shinto shrines. (The voice pauses and exhales deeply and slowly) One last chance. I hope Nippon is ready.
