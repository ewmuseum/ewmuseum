E-Wrestling Hall of Fame

9 August 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello, My name is [name withheld], or [name withheld] if you prefer, I USED to run the IFW before it collapsed. After trying my hands at the UIWC (A league where you must be a GREAT ROLE-PLAYER) and the ULWF (A Unified League of Federations) both fell through. I thought, that I could do something uiseful for the E-Wrestling World. I had the inspiration, and here it comes. the E-WRESTLING HALL OF FAME. I know there are one or two already out there, but this will be slightly different. There are thre things that each wrestler/tag team must have a good record in 1) Titles (they must either have had a lot, or held one or two for ages) 2) Role-Playing (They must be considered good Role-Players by at least two people - Me and a Fed Head.) 3) Gimmick (they must be original, i.e. They can be a blatant rip-off, but now it is hard to tell who they actually rip-off.) You have to be ABSOLUTELY BRILLIANT in one, and above average in two of those categories. I already have sent an e-mail to a group of people who are already allowed in, and they HAVE to fill out a Simulator 5.3 form to be fully acfcepted as members as I am also having an EXPORT file for WLS5.3 of the greatest, best and legendary wrestlers. What I need now is for Fed Heads, and Role-Players, to get in contact with me, especially Fed heads, who want wrestlers in their league admitted to the Hall of Fame. I will then reply, with instructions what do next, usually entails putting me on the Mailing List for a week or two. Then it happens from there. There will be a website up sometime in late August, early September, and good luck to everyone. [name withheld]
