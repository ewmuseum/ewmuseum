[IFW} Been renewed completely.

5 April 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OK, this is a rescue attempt by your former Prez, we will still have TWO cards a week, but this time, everyone who wants in, must refill out a form, get a flash or two by Sunday NIGHT, and hold on to your hats. On Monday, short notice I know, there WILL be a card from the IFW, exclusively written by yours truly. EVERY wrestler, except mine of course, will be involved in a Tournament for EVERY singles title. I'm lifting ALL bans, so anyone can come in, NO favoritism, and NO crass decisions. In fact, I`m looking for TWO very willing helpers, I know I have [name withheld], so that's alright. EVERY wrestler, the helpers can have ONE wrestler each though, and I may have Patty Babes Carter, but I've yet to decide a fair system whereby he could win some, lose some. You may not be on the OFFICIAL IFW list, but I'm restarting the IFW, but NOT quite from scratch. Here's the jist. EVERY wrestler will be involved in a Tournament or Rumble on Sunday, the winner gets the World Title, second gets the newly renamed Global Title and third the newly renamed Bronze Title. No specialist tag teams will be allowed, and ONLY one wrestler per person, unless there are extra spots, it will be a 40-man royal rumble. You are allowed THREE units, TWO wrestlers and a specialist Tag Team. You are not allowed three wrestlers, this is so that we can ake a truly great tag team division. If enough enter their total allotment, we will re-instate the Six Man Tag Division. Fill this in please. Your Name: E-Mail: Wrestler: Hometown: Weight: Height: Entrance Music: Manager (optional): Fibisher: Description: Style: Any notes you would like on the website: DESCRIBE an Icon you would like your wrestler to have on the website. ********** Cards ********** There will be three cards a week. Sunday Night Slam (7 Matches) - The second helper will write this card. Tuesday Night Terror (7 Matches) - [name withheld] writes this card. Friday Night Thunder (7 Matches + 10 man Battle Royal) - I write this card. ********** Titles ********** World Heavyweight Title - Defended against a TOP 5 Contender On PPV's and EVERY other Friday. Global Title - Defended against a TOP 10 Contender on MOST PPV's and EVERY Tuesday. Bronze Title - Defended against anyone on MOST PPV's and EVERY Tuesday and Friday. World Tag Titles - Defended against a top 5 team on PPV's and EVERY other Friday. Global Tag Titles - Defended against any team on MOST PPV's and every Tuesday. World Six Man Tag Titles - Defended against any team every Sunday. ********** Flashes and Challenges ********** The cards and flashes will take the style of several leagues, it will have my own personal style, as well as Jon's and my helper's. It will take the form of the UWA in card style, with a few differences. PLEASE try not to send too many flashes a day, any more than 5 and you may start getting penalised for irritating people with e-mail (Just kiding), I just don't want anyone flashing too many times a day, as it CAN get irritating. You will send flashes to the whole league, if you can, if not, nobble, me, Jon or our helper to send them on your behalf. You MUST have IFW somewhere in the title, a constant neglect will only irritate again, as I, and no doubt some others, will have a mail filtering system. Instructions will be sent in by the times mentioned on the Final card. They will always be sent to me, who will then send the relavant information to the card writer. Your language should be kept down, whilst we don't care whether you mask obscenity or not, I will, and my helpers will, keep an eye out for anyone who has a tendency to use it excessively. You will be punished for excessiveness however, by a suspension of two weeks. This is NOT a hardcore league, like the HCW or NIWF where weapons make and break (literally), but Hardcore gimmicks are welcome, as you could easily distract a ref, or in some matches, the ref will give latitude. ********** Sorry to bore you ********** I want a minimal role in running the IFW as I have other things to do, so for a couple of months, the fed will be above average, until July, when it will become AWESOME. I founded the league just over a year ago, and recently it hit trouble. Those wrestlers already with points in the rankings system will have a head start, sorry about that, but you'll soon get matches. Also, from the UWA brainchild of Zach Schooley, I am going to have several different arena's as well, with Tours of different Nations and continents every so often, and a weekly magazine, and daily rumours. Please, if you want to be the second Co-Vice-President please contact me, and [name withheld] ( [detail withheld] ) as we'll be glad to here from you. ********** The Website ********** Many of you have visited my website, well, from Friday and over the weekend until the Rumble, I will be deleting the whole WWF section. If you HAVE any of your wrestlers music in MIDI format, I will gladly put that on the site, as I have a 2MB quota. I also have TWO e-mail addresses so I have a TWO GeoCities areas (Greedy aren't I?) the second area is in SunsetStrip and will hold the music, If you know where on the web the music is in WAV, I will gladly link to it. ********** Final Note ********** Please, I will not tolerate Non-RP'ers, unless you can give a reason for not RP'ing for a week, you will be taken off the list IMMEDIATELY. I do not want anyone doing nothing, I worked hard to keep this open, and even harder to reopen it now, so please, have some respect for thew work I do. ********** AND FINALLY ********** Please, only send one e-mail with the application in as it will ease mai congestion, fill it in four tmes, stating clearly who is tag and not. If you want a Six-Man Tag Team, state that clearly as well. Each Wrestler, specialist tag team and six-man tag team is entitled to an icon. Please REPLY ASAP. THANK YOU. NB: Since this was written, the Rumble has been moved to Tuesday, http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/Field/1304/ifwhome.htm
