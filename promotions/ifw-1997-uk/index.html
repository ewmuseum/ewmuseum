Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

IFW dates its own founding to 12 February 1996, by its operator's own account in a first-anniversary post the following year: “I run the IFW, and for the next week, it won't be admitting new wrestlers... the great ones are involved in a mssive feud, and I want a side feud.” No card, roster, or title of its own survives from this period; only recruitment and administrative posts remain, including a January 1997 pitch for players closing, “email... to catch the drift!!!!”

On 5 April 1997 it relaunched as “a rescue attempt by your former Prez,” announcing a return to regular cards and a tournament, open to every wrestler with all bans lifted, to crown a renamed set of titles — World, Global and Bronze — by way of a forty-man rumble, its style drawn in part from the UWA. The same day it joined a new Wrestling Alliance alongside UWA, NIWF and HCW. A correction followed hours later, its operator clarifying that he, not the family member credited by mistake on a shared household account, had written the relaunch himself: “Sorry, I wrote it, not my mum, reply to this one.”

The relaunch didn't hold. On 9 August 1997 its founder posted, in his own words, “I USED to run the IFW before it collapsed,” describing two further projects he had tried and lost in between, and used the same notice to announce something new instead: an E-Wrestling Hall of Fame open to any promotion's wrestlers and tag teams, judged on titles held, role-playing quality, and gimmick — “ABSOLUTELY BRILLIANT” in one category and above average in two.
