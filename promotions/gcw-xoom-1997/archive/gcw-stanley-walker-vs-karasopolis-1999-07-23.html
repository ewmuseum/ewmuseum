GCW — Card

Ken Stanley and Derek Walker vs. Viktor Karasopolis

23 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
>>> Arena From Above <<< HAWKINS Well, we're awaiting the arrival of our next competitors here.. let's talk about this next match, Jim. Viktor Karasopolis, as we all know, is retiring from wrestling. But there were a few people that weren't going to let him do that in peace.
McMULLEN That's right.. these guys, the Suicidal High Flyers and Derek "Sky" Walker..they go back to the undatable theory of life.. HAWKINS The one about living life to its fullest every day? McMULLEN No, the one about kicking a man while he's down. That's exactly what these guys did. They sent Viktor Karasopolis on his way, right onto his butt. Now, the big scary "Dragon" wants to step in the ring again with a mystery partner..who is he going to get, Matt Kamakazee?
HAWKINS It very well could be! #"Assisted Suicide" kicks in on the speakers# [Out from the curtains steps none other than Ken Stanley. Almost immediately, the fans get to their feet to boo him.]
#BOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!# <CAPTION>: Ken Stanley and Derek Walker w/ Destiny [Close behind, is Derek Walker ... who is oddly in street clothing. However, on his arm is the lovely Destiny, looking as great as ever. Fans that bark at Stanley at ringside are quickly shouted at by the heelish SHFer and brushed off by the snide Walker. The trio make their way to the ring in the typical Mile High Club fashion: smug, rude, and crude.]
HAWKINS Here they come ... KEN STANLEY and DEREK WALKER ready for action against VIKTOR KARASOPOLIS and some mystery opponent.
McMULLEN Yeah, but WALKER doesn't look like he'll be doing any fighting! He's not dressed to wrestle!
[As the trio make their way inside the ring, the lights begin to dim red.] #The Serpent's Eye hits the PA system.# [An immense pop is let out for the introduction of Viktor Karasopolis.] #YEEEAHHHHH!!!# [Karasopolis, along with the ever-so-lovely, Ishira stand there at the entrance platform and waits ... and just then, as his music reaches it's explosive climax, he throws his arms up in the air in the usual fashion and incites the crowd further.]
#WHOOOHOOOO!!# [After saluting his fans, "The Sable Dragon" moves down to the ring.] <CAPTION>: "The Sable Dragon" Viktor Karasopolis w/ Ishira McMULLEN Hey, where's /his/ partner? HAWKINS Good question ... but an even better question is WHO is VIKTOR KARASOPOLIS' partner? [Finally after another long drawn out entrance by "The Sable Dragon",
Karasopolis walks up the ringsteps and steps inside.]
HAWKINS Well ... it looks to me like we're about to find out the where-abouts of this mystery man! [The crowd starts to murmur with anticipation as VK takes the mic from
Misssster Anderson and begins to pace the ring a little. Finally, after glaring down at Stanley and Walker, he stops in the center of the ring and lifts the microphone to his lips.]
{Viktor Karasopolis} You know, I've never seen so much speculation about one man since
Pete Rose was booted from baseball for placing side bets. Everyone seems to have an opinion on WHO my mystery partner will be....
[The crowd starts to get louder and VK smiles, looking around the ring and shaking his head.]
{Viktor Karasopolis} Some have said it will be Matt Kamakazee... [The crowd explodes with approval when VK mentions the Godzilla-loving
Icon.] {Viktor Karasopolis} Some people think its Johnny Ving.. [The crowd gives a mixed reaction to Ving's name They aren't sure where he stands after his shenanigans against Simpson on the last
SuperFights.]
{Viktor Karasopolis} Some people have even used a little logic to figure out that MAYBE it's Johnny's new found friend, Sphinx!
[The crowd openly boos. VK laughs and shrugs.] {Viktor Karasopolis} The truth is, I considered each and every one of them as a possible partner. [The crowd murmurs again. VK look over at Stanley, who waits impatiently outside the ring with Derek Walker. Viktor blows a kiss at
Stanley and waves, taunting him with a wink.]
{Viktor Karasopolis} But none of them ARE my partner....no, no, no. I figured that I needed someone with a bit of an edge, someone with a mean streak a mile wide, someone who could scare the life out of a man just by staring him down. A man with a fighting spirit and the heart of a champion and someone I could trust.
McMULLEN Who'll it be?! Who's KARASOPOLIS' partner?! HAWKINS Jim, I think we're about to find out! {Viktor Karasopolis} So without further ado, ladies and gentlemen, soon to be making his way down the ais- {Ken Stanley} Woah woah big fella. Hold it right there. [Stanley is standing before Karasopolis, his own mic in hand. The fans immediately boo his interruptive nature.]
{Ken Stanley} I'm sorry, but your partner can continue playing with his junk backstage because my partner, Derek Walker is injured right now. So ..
[Crowd starts yelling at Stanley.] #YOU SUCK DONKEY BALLS, STANLEY!# [Oh my.] {Ken Stanley} So, it looks to me like this is a singles match once again. [The crowd starts to boo Stanley alot, VK starts to get them into it, as Stanley gets pissed off.]
{Ken Stanley} You know ... ["You Suck" chant starts up in the crowd.] #STAN LEY SUCKS! STAN LEY SUCKS!# McMULLEN Heh. Those fans ... don't they know that without Stan Lee, there would have been no Incredible Hulk or X-Men or even Spiderman!?
HAWKINS What?! [Stanley can barely be heard over the heat-giving audience.] {Ken Stanley} You know I ... [The chant gets louder, as Stanley gets frustrated with the crowd.] #STAN LEY SUCKS! STAN LEY SUCKS! BOOOOOOOOO!# {Ken Stanley} Hey! You shut the hell up! Don't you got anything better to do then boo me!? Go change the tires on your house fat boy!
[The crowd gives him more heat.] {Ken Stanley} Alright, Alright. You know I was sitting at home, rehearsing the lines I planned on doing tonight in this very ring, and I came to the conclusion that I don't really need a good promo here to get by a crying has been like Viktor Carry-A-Bucket-Of-Piss.
McMULLEN Ha! That was a good one! I was sure Bruce had used 'em all too! [The crowd in the front row facing him starts to boo him very heavily, and Stanley's had about enough.]
{Ken Stanley} Go ahead, and boo me if you'd like, it's not my fault that everybody here in Minneapolis has to keep their zipper open just in case they want to count to 11. So I guess, we can start this thing since you idiots won't let me finish.
[He throws down the microphone. Karasopolis has already put away the microphone he'd been using and is rubbing his hands together in anticipation for another chance to rip Stanley's head off. But don't take it as vaulting ambition ... Viktor Karasopolis is all business here tonight. He's eyes are locked on the target. His trigger finger's itchings ...]
#DING DING DING!# HAWKINS And there's the bell ... BOTH MEN lock up! [Almost immediately, the better-trained Karasopolis slaps on a hip toss taking Stanley down like a sack of bricks. But some meager toss won't put
Stanley
down for a three-count. The high-flyer quickly gets back to his feet with a
kick-butt kick-up.] McMULLEN How'd all this mess start anyway? HAWKINS I believe it all goes back to the--STANLEY just /nails/ Karasoplis with a clothesline! "THE SABLE DRAGON" is down!
McMULLEN You were saying? HAWKINS Oh well ... this all dates back to the Suicidal Alliance over a year ago ... McMULLEN Ah. [Stompedy! Stompedy! Stompedy! Stomp! Stanley does a kick Riverdance number on Karasopolis' face. Naturally, the fans boo him like nobody's business!] #BOOOOOO!# [... only making the former fan-favorite more upset ...] {Ken Stanley} SHUT YOUR HOLES! [Stanley pulls the Grecian to his feet and sends him to the ropes; he hits the opposite ropes himself. As the two meet in the center, Stanley stops
Karasopolis' running with a swift kick to the gut. This is soon followed by,
none-other than a ...] HAWKINS DDT! McMULLEN WOW! HAWKINS STANLEY has potential ... but with a mouth like his, I can't see him going TOO far in this new-found singles career ...
McMULLEN Oh why not? Plenty of former champs have had big mouths! ROGERS! THORN! Hell ... even SIMPSON has a big mouth, man! HAWKINS True ... but STANLEY is really putting his foot /into/ that big mouth by messing with "THE SABLE DRAGON" VIKTOR KARASOPOLIS, one of many former
Television champs here in GCW.
[Stanley, after snipping at a few more loud-mouthed fans, rolls Karasopolis over and goes for a pin. Kazuhito Tokeshi (gasundheit!), the ref for this match, starts to do his job like he was born to do it ...]
{Kazuhito Tokeshi} Wah! Two! [... but Karasopolis is also doing HIS job like nobody can! An easy kick out keeps this Greek Hero back in the game!]
HAWKINS STANLEY has KARASOPOLIS up ... sends him to the corner ... NO! KARASOPOLIS catches himself, drops down and he's out of the ring! [He takes a breather. Stanley's a little tougher than he remembered. Shaking his head, he tries to regain some composure. However, he won't be gaining it for two long ...]
{Destiny} HEY! YOU SUCK! [Destiny, the melon-chested valet of Derek 'Sky' Walker, gets right up in the face of the Wild and Crazy Guy. Karasopolis raises a Grecian Eyebrow in response ... he didn't exactly EXPECT to be face to face with this once on the outside.]
{Destiny} bitchbitchbitchbitchbitch! [On and on she goes about how much better Stanley is than he. Karasopolis tries to walk away, but she keeps going.] HAWKINS DESTINY is trying to play mind games with KARASOPOLIS! I've said it once ... I've said it twice ... those valets are a distraction! McMULLEN But distractions aren't neccesarily a bad thing when you're as lovely as Destiny! Wow! [Things even up a bit as Karasopolis own "I threw this on today and almost missed" dressed valet walks over; Ishira spins Destiny around and she begins to jaw jack with Destiny!]
[The graphics to signify that Hour One is over come up at the bottom of the screen and we go into Hour Two without a break...]
{Ishira} HEY! LEAVE MY MAN ALONE! {Destiny} WHAT?!?! {Ishira} bitchbitchbitchbitchbitch! {Destiny} bitchbitchbitchbitchbitch! [Karasopolis just stands there, dumbfounded. However, the Minnesotian fans are just loving it.]
#WOOOOO HOOOOOOOOO!# [Well ... the male ones at least.] McMULLEN KARASOPOLIS better open his eyes up, 'cuz here comes STANLEY! HAWKINS I think that "THE SABLE DRAGON" sees him! [Very much to Stanley's suprise, Karasopolis spins around and socks his opponent right in the jaw! Stanley sells like Rena Mero's house after a nice, fat counterlaw suit (I just had to get that in ... stupid
Sable). Karasopolis grabs Stanley's arm and whips him to the far guardrail. But Stanley is fast and catches himself!] McMULLEN STANLEY caught himself! HAWKINS He may have stopped himself there, but he won't be able to stop the ever-persistant VIKTOR KARASOPOLIS! [Karasopolis charges over towards Stanley and grabs him around the waist just as he turns around. In one smooth motion, "The Sable Dragon"
lifts Stanley up and back, dropping him facefirst on the steel ringsteps. The fans pop like madmen there in the Target Center!] #YEEEEEEEEEEAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAHHHHHHHHH!# {Ken Stanley} ACK! MY FACE! MY FACE! [After hawking a big ol' loogie at his downed opponent, Karasopolis yells those three words that say it all ...]
{Viktor Karasopolis} OH ... DEAL WITH IT! [... inciting the Ventura-worshipping fans even more ...] #YEEEEEEEEEEAAAAAAAAAAHHHHHH!# [Making the quick desicion that they've been outside for far too long,
Karasopolis grabs Stanley up by his hair and rolls him back in. He follows.]
McMULLEN OH MAN! STANLEY is up! HAWKINS Yes he is ... kick to the gut of KARASOPOLIS! VK felt that one! McMULLEN And just when you think you've got the upperhand ... in comes a kick to the gut! HAWKINS OR NOT! KARASOPOLIS nails STANLEY right in the midsection with one HELL of a kick of his own! McMULLEN Man o man! [It's obvious that Karasopolis is far more skilled with kicks than
Stanley. Stanley grabs his gut and makes an expression like, "Oh dear ... I think I felt my bowels just release themselves." Looking up from his
doubled-over possition, he can see Karasopolis about ready to attack again ...] McMULLEN STANLEY charges right at KARASOPOLIS! HAWKINS But VK grabs him around the shoulders and whips him into the corner just behind him! Did you /see/ that impact?!
McMULLEN MAN! I think KARASOPOLIS needs to show some compassion! HAWKINS This is all payback, Jim! PayBACK! KARASOPOLIS grabs STANLEY by the arm and whips him into the far corner ...
[Stanley's back bounces off the turnbuckles. He stumbles aimlessly out of the corner ... Karasopolis charges right at him, throws his arm over
Stanley's shoulder, and jumps forward ...]
HAWKINS SOVIET SUPLEX! STANLEY has been put down like a rock! [Oh man. That was bad. That was sooooo bad. :)] HAWKINS And STANLEY's getting put down for 1, 2, 3! KARASOPOLIS with a pin! [The ref (I won't try to say his name, because I'm too lazy to cut and paste it from the assignment pad) starts to slap the mat once again ..]
{Kazuhito Tokeshi} Wah! Two! [Too bad for Karasopolis ... this match is going to be a little longer.] {Viktor Karasopolis} Damn! #BOOOOOOOOOOOOO!# McMULLEN STANLEY kicks right out at two! HAWKINS BOTH MEN are to their feet! KARASOPOLIS hits STANLEY in the face with a big right hand ... follows with a couple more ... and a whip to the
ropes--STANLEY's going for the ride! [Karasopolis gets to the center of the ring and hunches down for a backdrop attempt. Karasopolis soon finds out that today just ain't his day ..]
McMULLEN OH! STANLEY saw that coming a mile away! A kick to the face of
KARASOPOLIS dazes the GREEK!
HAWKINS STANLEY's got him ... picks him up ... a gutbuster! [Karasopolis grabs his stomach, jumps to his feet and stumbles away. Pain courses all over his abdominals, but Stanley doesn't seem to care. A big grin creeps up over his face. After turning Karasopolis back around,
Stanley snap mares him to the mat and runs over to the turnbuckle.]
McMULLEN STANLEY's going up top ... HAWKINS A high-risk manuever is in the works here! McMULLEN STANLEY's up ... [Stanley pumps his arm at the fans. They boo endlessly. Finally, as if he couldn't stall anymore, he leaps off the top-rope and swandives at the fallen Grecian ...]
{Ken Stanley} Oh shi- [... but there's no Grecian there, bud.] #FUMP!# {Ken Stanley} OOF! [Landing right on his head, Stanley completely misses his target.] HAWKINS KARASOPOLIS rolled out of the way! He hit nothing but canvas! McMULLEN Oh, good grief! [However, Karasopolis is still favoring his stomach. Laying on his back, he winces up towards the light. Tokeshi starts a double-countout, but not for too long ... Karasopolis and Stanley both slowly begin to stand.]
HAWKINS BOTH MEN are to their feet ... McMULLEN And man do the they BOTH look out of it! HAWKINS STANLEY charges with a clothesline! KARASOPOLIS ducks it! A JUMPING SPIN KICK knocks STANLEY back! [It was nice too! A quick spin-kick from the ducking possition let's Ken Stanley know who the boss is. Stanley falls backwards, hits the top-rope, and falls out of the ring. He'd tried to stop himself, but Karasopolis is one HELL of a kicker.] HAWKINS STANLEY is down and out on the outside of the ring! Close behind is KARASOPOLIS! McMULLEN Back to the outside already? Come on! I thought KARASOPOLIS was a
"mat man?" [Karasopolis, after about ten minutes of frustration from Ken Stanley, grabs a hold of his head and says ...]
{Viktor Karasopolis} I'm really sick of you ... #THUMP!# {Ken Stanley} YEE-OUCH! [The "THUMP!" came from the sound that Ken Stanley's head makes when slammed against the rough edge of the apron. Grabbing his head in pain,
Stanley stumbles away. Karasopolis quickly follows behind ...] {Viktor Karasopolis} Where do you think you're going?! ["The Sable Dragon" grabs Stanley's arm, spins and whips him into the guardrail. Stanley's chest hits the black protective barrier and bounces off. Before landing flat on his face, he spins and says ...]
{Ken Stanley} *urk* #THUMP!# [This time the "THUMP!" was him landing on his face.] HAWKINS KARASOPOLIS is really using the outside to his advantage here! McMULLEN STANLEY needs to get up if he hopes to beat KARASOPOLIS! [Karasopolis grabs Stanley by the hair and pulls him to his feet. Stanley looks dazed ... but not for too long. Immediately, he swings with a big fist, aiming to knock Karasopolis' lights out!]
#FWAP!# {Viktor Karasopolis} OW! [And he scores a big ol' punch to the side of Karasopolis head! Rearing back, Stanley tries for another punch ... but this time, Karasopolis is ready!] {Viktor Karsopolis} Not this time ... [And /he/ then nails /Stanley/ with a punch to the face. And again! And again! And again! Stanley and Karasopolis continue to fight on the outside, and where is that dern ref?!]
{Destiny} Hey there big boy ... {Kazuhito Tokeshi} Oh my! [He may wear the black and white stripes, but he's still a man. And
Destiny, showing off enough cleavage to make the Grand Canyon envious has him more than distracted. It has the man in love!]
{Kazuhito Tokeshi} I ruv you ... {Destiny} I know. [Meanwhile, Karasopolis and Stanley battle in front of the ring. However, Karasopolis is in total control and is effortlessly kicking the crud out of Ken Stanley. After one last kick to the ribs, Stanley crawls away to the other side of the ring. Karasopolis just stand there with a big smirk on his face ...]
McMULLEN Hey! Who's that?! [Who's who?] HAWKINS Where? [Yes, where?!] McMULLEN At the entrance way! >>> ENTRANCEWAY <<< [There, at the entrance way and on crutches, is Tommy Kain. Kain, after being out of it for a month or two, looks very upset that his partner still continues without him.]
HAWKINS It's TOMMY KAIN! KEN STANLEY's former partner! McMULLEN KARASOPLIS sees him! ["The Sable Dragon" turns towards the entrance way ... he sees Kain

HAWKINS Well ... KARASOPOLIS is placing STANLEY back in the ring ... I think he may go for a pin attempt!
McMULLEN But TOKESHI is still distracted! [Tokeshi can't keep his eyes away from Destiny's pair (and I don't mean Walker and Stanley). Karasopolis is on the outside just shaking his head at Stanley. He doesn't even see what's coming ...]
**WHACK!** [A crutch to the back of the head puts Karasopolis down like a sack of bricks!] **Groan!** #BOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!# [There, with a big grin on his face, fresh from not only GOING to ringside, but actually SPRINTING towards ring-side is Tommy Kain. He holds the two crutches high in the air and does a little one-legged dance, basically saying, "Lookie, lookie, I'm not hurt!" The fans are booing him to hell.]
#BOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!# [Walker, after staying more or less non-involved in this entire match-up, walks over, grabs a hold of Karasopolis and with the help of Tommy
