[The camera opens up to the the rough worn and torn streets of a long abandoned city. [Aroku Murakami walks out from a dark alley. He's clad in a black silk suit with white shirt and black tie. His short spikey jet black hair stands firm in the blowing wind. A thin wired pair of lavender colored gucci sunglasses conceal his eyes. He walks into the street surveying his surroundings.] MURAKAMI: They call this hallowed ground, ground that was stained red with the blood of thousands of men more than fourty years ago. The winners ran away. The losers were maimed and left to live the rest of their lives without arms and legs, limbs that were left to rot in this dirt. The dead, I guess you could just call them the lucky ones. Such is the casualties of war. Jordan Thorn, let me be the first to tell you that we are in a very different state of war in this federation and it's just like any conflict, it has it victims. Victims of ideology, victims of peer pressure, victims of foolish pride, victims of fate, victims of their own ego and victims of their own youth. Isn't it ironic how many of us entered this war with enthusiasm? Youthful arrogance got them girlfriends, youthful arrogance got them friends, youthful arrogance... [He stops dead in his tracks -- briefly to finish his thought.] ..... that ended their careers. You see Jordan, every man wants to test himself and every man thinks he's up to it, but I have seen too many crushed by defeat. The very second self-doubt creeps into their mind, they're better off dead. They can't accept that fate and deep down they know they'll never be the same again. Lately, I've been hearing all the same accusations lately. Too young, too in-experienced, too arrogant to become a legend, too everything and anything that doesn't fit the mold that was set to all the wrestlers before me. I'm hungry and I'm the one just itching to take someone's spot no matter if it's yours, Simpson's, Stanley's it doesn't matter anymore. I know what you accomplished 7 years ago Jordan and no matter how much you may not want to accept it you're no longer the same man. At one time Jordan you were the ruler of this establishment, the heavyweight champion ... this place was once full of you, full of your power, full of your ideals, full of the your thoughts, full of your influence and questionably, full of your immortality. Sooner or later you were no longer king of the mountain, all the time and energy you put forth into building this federation came crashing down on you as the young lions entered the playing field ... and to this day I know that deep down in your dark soul that it still haunts you by the way you walk, by the way you talk and by the way you move around hanging your head at every available opportunity... [Murakami continues his descent down the street looking at the hallowed remains of this once inhabited city. He stops at his white limo that's pulled off on the side of the road. Remember when you were there though, you thought yourself unbeatable, immortal and untouchable and in those moments there was one who robbed you of it and in 2004, I am that underdog. [MURAKAMI opens the door to the limo and climps into he limo and slams the door shut. The tinted window slowly rolls down and Aroku looks out and takes off his sunglasses.] MURAKAMI: Remember the war Jordan, a bullet does more damage when it doesn't kill and it knows exactly what it's doing. So Mr. Thorn what exactly are you going to do when this six foot three, two-hundred and twenty-five pound speeding bullet comes for your life? Just _WHAT_ exactly are you going to do?
