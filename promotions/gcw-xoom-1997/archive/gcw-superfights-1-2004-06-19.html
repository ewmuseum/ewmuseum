[The scene opens up with the inside of a townhouse. Thin beige carpeting lined the floors, while off-white paint covered the walls. A black leather couch was positioned against the far wall, closest to the staircase. A glass top coffee table was positioned in front of the couch. The coffee table was littered with an array of various wrestling magazines. The end of a remote controller could be seen jutting slightly from the edge of a magazine. Across from the coffee table was a cherry entertainment center with a DVD player, stereo system and a 32-inch television set. Loud thumps could be heard coming down the staircase as Heath Starr, the newest G.C.W. wrestler rushed down the stairs and into the living room. He was dressed in navy blue Skecher Sport shoes, slightly faded blue jeans and a white Superman t-shirt that had blue ringers around the end of the sleeves and the collar. His short dark hair was slightly out of place, his sparkling blue eyes looked to be pre-occupied.] STARR: Awww, man ... they’ve gotten be around here somewhere. [Heath began looking underneath the magazines on the coffee table, eventually tossing them onto the couch, to clear off the coffee table. It was obvious that Heath didn’t find what he was looking for as he piled the magazines back onto the coffee table and began to feel in between the seats on the couch. After several minutes, Heath slowly stood. He placed his hands on the sides of his head and looked about the room.] STARR: Where in the world could they be? [Heath took a deep breath and lowered his hands to his sides.] STARR: Ok, let me recap. I unlocked the door ... I placed the keys back in my pock... [Heath raced his hands through his front pockets, but withdrew them slowly.] STARR: Shoot! Ok, I placed the keys back into my pocket and then I fixed myself something to drink. Then I accidentally spilled some Tea on my pants and I had to chan.. [Heath bolted through the living room and the kitchen like a streak of lightning and rushed over to the spacious room designated for the washer and dryer, the washing machine was still active. Heath raised the lid on the washing machine and sifted through the clothes until he found his jeans. He rummaged through the pockets and retrieved his keys. A look of relief crossed Heath’s handsome face as he placed his jeans back into the washer and closed the lid.] STARR: Man, I can’t believe that I did that. This is my big day with the G-C-W and I’m pretty much already late. [Heath glanced down at his titanium Fossil watch as his eyes seems to bulge slightly.] STARR: Crap! [Heath’s old high school days of being on the Track team quickly revisited him as he rushed through the house and to the door. The sound of the door being locked from the outside could be heard and then silence.] [The next shot was of the outside of the townhouse. The parking lot was sparsely occupied with a wide variety of vehicles. Heath immediately rushed over to a bright yellow 2004 Ford Mustang. Heath pointed the key chain at the car and pressed a button on his keyless entry box. He quickly opened the door and ducked inside the car. He slammed the door shut and quickly cranked the car. Heath took time out to check the back seat for his gear bag, which wasn’t there. Heath killed the ignition and opened the door and headed back for his townhouse. He unlocked the door and disappeared inside. Several minutes later, Heath emerged with a royal blue gear bag, a silver star resting in the middle of each side of the bag. Heath re-locked his front door and proceeded back to his car. He didn’t realize that he had left his car door open, but at this point in time, he didn’t care. He could always do a theft check later on. Heath then buckled his seat belt and placed the car in reverse and sped carefully out of the parking lot. The scene slowly faded to black as Heath desperately headed for his destination, in hopes of not been overly late.] {Later that same day...} [The scene opens up just outside of the American Airlines Arena in Dallas, Texas. The air was thick with humidity as a bright yellow 2004 Ford Mustang pulled into the back lot of the arena. The Mustang found a suitable parking space and stopped, the engine died and the driver’s door opened. A young man standing barely over the 6’ mark stepped out. He was dressed in navy blue Skecher Sport shoes, slightly faded blue jeans and a white Superman t-shirt that had blue ringers around the end of the sleeves and the collar. A pass with this photo was suspended around his neck on a thin chain. Since he was new to the GCW, he didn’t want anyone mistaken him for a renegade fan. His short dark hair was neatly combed. He reached into the back seat and retrieved his gear bag. He quickly shut his door afterwards and looked out at his surroundings.] STARR: Dang, it’s freakin’ hot! [Heath pressed the lock button own his key chain and proceeded towards the arena. The humidity seemed to cling to Heath as he walked. He finally reached the doors and pulled them open. The cool air rushed against Heath’s face as he stepped inside, the doors closing behind him. Heath just simply stood still, allowing the cool air to flow over him. After he was cooled off, Heath began to hunt for a GCW staff member. Several seconds later, he found one.] STARR: Excuse me, sir. I’m Heath Starr ... a new signee for the G-C-W. After that, you’ll pass through a set of doors and the dressing rooms are there. The rooms should be labeled. STARR: Thanks, I appreciate it. [Heath Starr extended his hand and the crewmember did the same. After shaking hands, Heath headed off for his destination ... the dressing rooms. Heath followed the crewmembers directions and found his way to the dressing rooms. After several seconds of looking, he found the door with his name taped to it. Heath opened the door and proceeded inside. The dressing room was decorated rather nicely. Thin beige carpeting lined the floor; the walls were composed of white painted concrete. A large black leather couch rested against the far wall, a small oak coffee table rested several feet in front of the couch. A black leather armchair was angled several feet away, but facing the couch. A few metal folding chairs was propped up against the wall furthest from the couch. Heath walked over to the couch and dropped his gear bag onto it.] STARR: Not too shabby. I could get used to this. [Heath looked around the room, and taking in everything. It wasn’t that this room was elaborate, it was that Heath wasn’t really used to a dressing room this big, or this nice. Heath walked over to the couch and sat down, the leather squeaking. Heath looked at the coffee table and that’s when he noticed a small white envelope with his name written on it. He carefully opened it, trying not to tear the letter inside.
