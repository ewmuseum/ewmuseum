WWC has opened. A new concept in e-wrestling

http://sports.groups.yahoo.com/group/wwcdonaldjacobs/ 1. WHAT IS THE WWC? A few months ago, at the behest of Pay TV mogul Reginald Burgess
Montgomery, the 5 independent continental wrestling organisations met in London to achieve the unity of all organisations of the world to control the expansion of a sport that desperately needed it.
Montgomery recognised the worldwide appeal of professional wrestling and even though each organisation had its own champion they lacked one key aspect: a status of "International".
Montgomery's motivation was quite simple: he wanted to achieve a virtual monopoly in pay-per-view pro-wrestling.
The organisations could not overlook the big dollars that Montgomery was offering and so the London meeting has given birth to the WWC, the biggest and most powerful wrestling organisation of all time.
The 5 organisations that make up the WWC are: i) The North & Central America Pro-Wrestling Association (NCAPWA)-Karl Parker (USA) ii) AustralAsia Action Pro-Wrestling (Triple A Pro-Wrestling)-Alan
Tsung (Hong Kong) iii) South America Wrestling (SAW)-Juan Carlos Garcia (Argentina) iv) All-European Wrestling Federation (AEWF)-Herman Schultz (Germany) v) Africa Pro-Wrestling (APW)-Ali Zamir (Egypt) 1.1 WHO RUNS THE WWC? The General Secretary of WWC, that is, the head honcho, is Donald
Jacobs from Australia. Donald is 56 years old and was previously head of Triple A Pro-Wrestling for 12 years. He won with a majority vote with all 5 continental organisations and their corresponding national members voting.
The Board of Directors is made up of the heads of the 5 organisations listed above. You will hardly see the above men on a weekly or even monthly basis, but rest assured their decisions affect everyone in the Council and more often than not there is always a lot of politics involved in every decision.
2. WWC INFO The WWC is a BOOKED league, meaning that I alone decide who wins the matches by way of who deserves it most. I will always endeavour to be fair and as impartial as possible when choosing the winners. The votes are based on three (3) things.
* Participation. Being involved by either getting in an angle, sending out flashes or roleplays, writing a news article by developing your character, etc. The main way to participate is by creating angles. Creating angles is the most important thing in the WWC. Angles are storylines you create with other handlers' wrestlers or by yourself. If there's an angle going, perhaps we'll let you lose a match only to build up heat for your next match where you will win. Angles are what make e-wrestling fun, SO MAKE ONE UP!!
*Flashing. Probably the most obvious way to show you're participating is by flashing. Flashes can be sent out to either the weekly shows, house shows, or The Touring Main Event. There are plenty of opportunities for a handler to roleplay so there are no excuses for a handler not to do so.
* The match strategy should be sent in before the match (which includes sneak attack strategy, bribing a ref, etc). This is an account of how your character will approach his match, or if it is a sneak attack, how he will interfere, etc, etc.
You get the picture. I must point out, however, that a distinguishing feature of the WWC is that if a handler only wants to participate "part-time" that is fine with me. You choose your level of involvement. If you want your character to only participate on a continental level (ie. just on summary cards) then participation can be as little as 1 or 2 flashes a month. If you would like your character to go the full hog then, obviously, you need to be more active. Read on for more details.
3 HANDLERS Handlers are allowed one single wrester or one tagteam unless certain circumstances exist. This rule exists to promote interaction among players, which is highly encouraged and rewarded.
3.1 CREATING A WRESTLER The WWC is an international organisation and as such wrestlers have to reflect this. I will not have 20 American wrestlers (even if they participate in the NCAPWA league). Use your imagination and create a wrestler from anywhere in the world and place him in the corresponding league.
Name The WWC is an original wrestlers only fed. No real wrestlers will be accepted. Ok if you name your wrestler Desmond "The Hitman" Harris he will be accepted. But if he starts acting and talking like Bret Hart he wont be accepted... never has, never will be.
Age You decide however no wrestler younger than 18 will be allowed to wrestle (legal reasons), and if you create a wrestler older than 50 expect him to be a bit saggy and slow in his matches so take that in account when you're writing the strategies.
Height and weight. The height and weight limits for wrestlers are: Height - no taller than 7'3 , no shorter than 5'0 . Weight- No heavier than 450 pounds, no lighter than 150 pounds. The lower limitations exist because we try to make the fed "realistic" and as it wouldn't be realistic that a 120 pound wrestler would beat a 300+ wrestler they won't be accepted. The higher limitations exist because I personally think it's pretty boring and unfair to the other wrestlers if a handler should create a 8'0 700 pound wrestler. 7'0 foot wrestlers who are highfliers will also not be accepted. That's that: Wrestlers like this won't be accepted. It will also depend on what kind of wrestlers there are currently on the roster. if there are already 3-4 guys who are over 7'0 then I won't be allowing anymore. I would like a little variety.
Alignment. Decide if you're a good guy or a bad guy or neither. Heel, Face or
Neutral it's up to you! Make sure that if you create a face he acts the part and vice versa for heels and 'tweeners
Entrance Music In the form of title and Artist or Composer. Try to be creative. I don't want themes like "Mr.Perfects" theme etc. Once again originality, originality!
Ring Entrance Write your own wrestlers' entrance, complete with Ring Announcer
Intro, plus describe what he's wearing, etc.
Appearance How he looks. I want a detailed description. Describe him as you would to a blind man. Your opponents cant see your wrestler unless we are able to describe him.
Personality His background story. What makes him interesting? Why would you yourself like to watch him? Make sure that when you sign up there isn't already a wrestler similar to your character in the roster. I don't want 20 pretty boy wrestlers.
Type of wrestler What is your wrestler. A matwrestling expert or a top-notch brawler. We want to know. 15 common moves. What are his favorite moves? List them so we can get to know your wrestler better and by doing so, be able to write better matches with him.
Finisher If it's an existing move just write it else we want a detailed explanation. Foreign object Does he use one? If he does what and how. Manager Does he have a manager? Manager description Self explanatory. 4. OUR CARDS There are various WWC sponsored cards throughout the week. Here's a rundown on them: Each organisation (ie SAW, NCAPWA, Triple A Pro-Wrestling, APW & the
AEWF) has a summarised house show once a week from somewhere in their corresponding continent. The house show is a written internet account by a fan who was in attendance. They contain 3-4 jobbermatches
(handler vs jobber. There will be less and less jobbermatches the more handlers we get) and 1-2 handler vs handler matches. This is a chance for the "part-time" handlers to flash as these fans usually provide a transcript of what wrestlers say in these shows. These are NOT televised shows and are usually held in front of a few thousand people
(would not be more than a 5000 strong attendance). It should also be mentioned that the World Champion does NOT wrestle in house shows.
The WWC also holds 1 PPV per month which airs worldwide in Montgomery's SUN Network. The PPV is named "The Touring Main Event" and is always held in different parts of the world. A PPV holds 7 Play-
By-Play (PBP) matches and is made up of the top wrestlers in the world
(ie the "full-time"/top handlers).
A few things to be noted: i)A wrestler cannot wrestle more than once a fortnight. This is due to me wanting to make this as realistic as possible. You don't really see boxers or MMA fighters fighting every week as the physical toll would be too much, well it's the same for pro-wrestlers.
ii)PBP matches will NOT be monstrous affairs. They will be relatively short (8K or so) with a longer Main Event. The main reason for this is because I have better things to do than write 30K matches and there is no way I would be able to do deal with that kind of workload, the other reason is that I just don't find it realistic for a wrestler to withstand 2 piledrivers, a powerbomb, 3 DDTs, the other wrestler's finishing move a couple of times, etc, etc, etc and still be able to kickout of a pin attempt, let alone still be alive. Be warned that matches may very well end with a victory roll, a piledriver (whether it is a finishing move or not) or even a sleeperhold. Rest assured if your character loses in this way it is not necessarily an indictment on your wrestler, this is me just being realistic.
iii)A wrestler may go to another league for a fight (eg. an AEWF wrestler may go to Triple A, to fight a wrestler there), however only a wrestler that RESIDES in said continent may become its eventual champion.
4.1 OUR T.V SHOWS The WWC also has a weekly show airing in the Sun Network named "The
Faces of the WWC". This is where everyone will have the chance to flash. "Full-Time" handlers are required to flash at least twice a month, straight after a PPV and just before it, especially if they're participating in it. "Part-Time" handlers can also flash as there will be a brief segment every week on each organisation. Flashing for "Part-
Time" handlers is optional. On the week that a PPV is on the weekly show is a repeat of the previous week.
5. RANKINGS & TITLES The WWC has 2 World Championships The WWC World Heavyweight Champion This title is the most coveted and highest ranked title in our fed. To become the Heavyweight Champion is the greatest achievment you can make in the WWC.
The WWC World Tag Team Champions The title all the tagteams are competing for. Each organisation also has its own continental champion, for example, the AEWF will have a European Champion and Tag Team Champions and they will defend their titles against other European wrestlers.
5.1 HOW THE TITLES ARE DEFENDED Champions must defend their titles at least once every 3 months either in a voluntary or mandatory defense. A contender must be:
i) Ranked in the Top 5 in the world (Top 3 for continental champions). For the most part a champion will be able to choose who he defends his title against (as long as they're appropiately ranked), but at times the Board of Directors will designate a challenger for the title. The designation of an official challenger requires a majority vote approval by the Board of Directors;
ii) upon a majority vote of the Board of Directors, any wrestler rated from 6 to 10; iii) a former or retired world champion upon majority vote of the
Board of Directors; and iv) another wrestling organization's champion recognized by the WWC, upon majority vote of the Board of Directors.
5.2 VACANT TITLE If a title becomes vacant for any reason, the Board of Directors may in its discretion take whatever steps or actions it deems necessary or appropriate to fill the title, including but not limited to:
i) authorize and cause to be instituted a tournament or elimination contest among leading available contenders with the winner being recognized as the new champion;
or ii) authorize or cause to be instituted a direct bout between the two highest rated available wrestlers and recognize the winner as the champion;
or iii) recognize as champion the winner of a previous elimination bout. This list is non-exclusive.
Interim Championship. The WWC may order an interim title bout when a champion will be temporarily inactive and unable to defend his title for a period exceeding 3 months due to:
a) certified medical reasons; b) a legal impediment; or c) a reasonable cause strictly beyond his control. If the inactivity is estimated to exceed 6 months, the title shall be declared vacant. Upon the return of the champion, the WWC will order a mandatory bout for the undisputed WWC title with the interim champion. Both wrestlers are bound to participate in this bout and cannot participate in an intervening contest.
5.3 RANKINGS Rankings are mostly decided on a subjective basis. Your win/loss record will still be shown next to your wrestler but his place in the rankings will be decided by the Board of Directors (for WWC rankings)
and by each individual organisation (with the previously mentioned characters as their respective Heads). Namely it is just me who decides rankings and I will try to remain as impartial as possible when doing this.
The Tagteam wrestlers are a special case in the rankings, however. Whenever a tagteam wrestler competes in single action, the outcome is based in the single rankings. If he a tagteam wrestler fares well in single competition he can be a contender in both divisions. Basically, what he does in each division is totally independent of the other.
5.4 RANKINGS CRITERIA Record. Result and quality of performance in the recent fights. Level or competition. Significance of bouts. Experience in championship fights. Fighting at home or abroad. Activity. Decisiveness of victories. Style. Controversial decisions. So basically it is all up to you. Do you want your wrestler to be one that takes on all comers? Or do you want to slowly build up his record by wrestling jobbers? Will he be just a local fighter fighting solely regionaly or will he take on the world?
6. CONTACTS You may contact the other wrestlers in the WWC to get to know them, set up teams, stables, feuds, etc. Make sure that once you set something up, that both (or all) of the parties confirm it with me so
I know. There is a mailing list of the current WWC Handlers that goes out every once in a while through the mailing list, so you know how to contact everyone. In addition to that there will be homepages for every active wrestler/tagteam with e-mail links to the handler. The
WWC staff can be contacted in two different ways. Directly if they have an e-mail address listed on the homepage or via the president. If you want to contact someone via the President just write who the message is for, then I'll see to it that it gets to the right person.
If the message is in the form of a roleplay they will be treated as such. Well I guess that's it! If you have any questions e-mail me I will explain it to you! Well what are you waiting for? Go on and sign an application! The WWC wants you!
