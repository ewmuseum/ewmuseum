AOLWF Thursday Rumble #2

7 August 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
***** Camera cuts to the backstage area and focuses in on Triple Threat ******** The Franchise Shane Douglas: The time has come. The new era has began and now its time for The Franchise to run this damn federation the way he sees fit!! I heard Shawn Michaels running his damn mouth about how he is the true Franchise. Let me tell you something punk!!! The only damn reason Diesel is ranked higher than me is that I don't give a damn about McMahon, Bischoff or any other fucking head honcho in this federation! I am in this damn league to take it where it has never been before and your President doesen't want that to happen but its going to! I will get in that World Title Tournament and I will win My Belt!!! I really don't give a damn if Chyna doesen't have a belt. I really don't give a damn if Gunn doesen't have a belt. Everybody in Triple Threat knows that their purpose here is to watch my back and to be a part of the master plan. To make sure it goes smoothly without any delays. Now they can go and win titles if they want but thier true purpose in the Threat is to do what I say! They will do whatever I plan and thats the real reason they are here! Now if you want Triple Threat to come at ya full force then by god we will do it! We are without a doubt the most dominant stable in this federation!!!! You can go ahead and talk all your words Michaels because thats all your good for! You can go ahead and badmouth The Franchise if you want to Hunter but when we meet in the ring I am going to take your ass out!!!! As a matter of fact, I would like to be so kind and thank every one of you no good son of a bitches for opening my eyes. I can see that Triple Threat is going to have to open alot of your eyes and by God I'm gonna start with Paul Roma by ending his fucking career!!!! Its time to get vicious and the only man safe from all this is Bret The Hitman Hart. Now its time to come down to the ring and kick some ass because Billy Gunn is going to beat the hell out of your so called Mr.Perfect!!!!!! ************************************************************************** ******************** ( Perfect Strangers begins to blare over the loudspears ) Vince: I don't like that Shane Douglas guy too much. He's a disgrace to the AOLWF! Bobby: What?! What are you talking about McMahon? Shane Douglas has the most powerful stable in the AOLWF,......Triple Threat! Jim: Well I think The Kliq could argue with you there and The Heart Foundation too. Billy Gunn is being led to the ring by Woman and the AOLWF Television Champion Mr. Perfect is awaiting him in the ring. Vince: Gunn is in the ring and here we go. Collar and elbow tie up and Perfect shoves Gunn into the corner. Knife edge by Perfect and Gunn fires back with a right hand. Perfect connects back with a left hand and lands another chop and now Gunn is rocked. Bobby: Well Perfect isn't the Television Champion of the world for nothing. Now he's going for the World Title Tournament and a member of Triple Threat stands in his way. Jim: Perfect sends Gunn into the corner and charges in with an elbow to the jaw. Perfect brings Gunn to the middle of the ring and executes a beutiful standing dropkick. Vince: Billy Gunn is right back up though and Perfect runs at him with a clothesline but Gunn ducked and caught Perfect in a sidewalk slam. Bobby: Great counter by Billy Gunn. I think Shane has been teaching him. Vince: Now Billy picks Hennig up and sends him to the ropes. Hennig comes back across and is lifted way up in the air with a back body drop. Clothesline by Gunn, Another clothesline by Gunn and make it three clotheslines and Perfect is not getting up after that one. Jim: Billy Gunn is viciously stomping away on Perfect. He's like a ravid dog! Bobby: What? Did you have a bad childhood when you were young Ross? Jim: Gunn sends Perfect to the ropes and ducks down again but this time Hennig puts on the brakes and delivers a piledriver on Billy Gunn! Bobby: That had to hurt. Gunn isn't moving and Perfect makes the cover. 1!!!!!!! 2!!!!!!! He got his foot over the ropes. I thought it was all over. Vince: Perfect executes a snap mare and down goes Gunn. Perfect backs up to the corner and nails his rolling necksnap. Gunn gets up but Hennig puts him right back down with a punch to the jaw. Jim: Hennig is waiting for Gunn to get up. Gunn is up and leaning against the ropes and here comes Perfect with a running clothesline and they both went over the top rope from the momentum of Mr.Perfect! Bobby: I tell you what. Billy Gunn is young and has alot to learn but he has got major talent. Once he gets it all put together I think he will be one of the top superstars in the AOLWF. I mean look how he's giving Perfect a hard time. Everyone thought Gunn would be pinned by now but he's taking it like a man. Jim: Thats one of the reasons Shane Douglas picked him for the Triple Threat. Hennig picks Gunn up but Gunn nailed a low blow and that stopped Hennig in his tracks. Gunn just suplexed Perfect on the concrete floor and both Gunn and Perfect are hurting here. Vince: Billy Gunn is to his feet first and he has Hennig. Gunn sends Hennig into the, No!,.....Hennig reversed it and Gunn flew into the steel post and that laid him out flat! Perfect comes right over and throws Gunn into the ring and now whats Woman doing?! Bobby: She's doing her job and destracting Perfect. Woman is on the aprin and Perfect is walking towards her and Gunn just ran from behind and clipped Hennigs' knee! Jim: Gunn just slapped on the figure four leg lock!! He dragged Perfect to the middle of the ring and he's got the figure four locked in deep! Perfect is in bad pain as you can hear the screams of agony but Perfect is trying to roll it over. He's gonna counter the figure four. Bobby: He did it! Vince: And now Gunn has the pressure on him but he quickly broke the hold. Gunn picks Perfect up but Perfect scores with a knee to the stomach. Perfect-Plex!!!!! 1!!!!!! 2!!!!!! 3!!!!!!!! Perfect gets the win and Billy Gunn cannot believe it. Jim: Woman and Billy Gunn are in a state of shock. Curt Hennig slapped on that Perfect-Plex before Gunn knew what happened. Bobby: Look out,......Chyna is coming down the rampway and she just got in the ring where Perfect is but Hennig rolled out of the ring and that was a smart move because Hennigs' knee is in pain. Look out!!!!!! Jim: Shane Douglas and Barry Windham just charged the ring with chairs and they are beating the hell out of Chyna!!!!! What in the hell is going on here?! Now Gunn has Chyna in the figure four and Douglas and Windham are pounding the hell out of Chyna with those steel chairs! Vince: I can't believe this is happening!! Shane Douglas is low-life son of a......... Bobby: Wait a second. Shane Douglas is picking up Chyna. Gunn and Douglas are wrapping Chynas' arms in the top rope. She's crucified and cannot move because of the beating she has taken. ( CRACK!!!!!!!! ) Jim: Windham just ran across the ring and busted her on the head with the chair and the steel chair is benot over Chynas' head! Bobby: I think Windham just killed Chyna! Douglas is grabbing the mic!! The Franchise: You people can sit down and shut the hell up! The crowd: Booooooooooooooooooooo!!! Shane Douglas: What you just witnesses was all part of the plan. Hah hah hah hah! Do you morons actually think that I would keep a woman wrestler in the most dominating force in proffessional wrestling? If you do your all bigger schmucks than I thought you were! I brought her in this league to prove a point!! And that point was that The Franchise backs up whatever he says. I told all of you people when I came back here that I had the next Light-Heavyweight Champion and I backed up what I said. Her purpose is served and now she is no longer any use to us at all. So what I would like to do right now is bring out the man who was the third member all along. Hit the fuckin music!!!!! Loud Speakers: Simply Ravishing!! ( Rick Rudes' ECW theme music begins to play ) Bobby: Its Rick Rude!!!!! Ravishing Rick Rude is the third member of Triple Threat!! This is great! Jim: Shane Douglas recruited one of the toughest men in this sport to be in Triple Threat and Threat is getting stronger and stronger as time goes by. ( Rick Rude climbs in the ring. ) Ravishing Rick Rude: Cut the music!! What I'd like to have right now is for all you fat, out of shape, Columbus co**suckers!!! Keep the noise down while I tell you the way it is!! Vince: I can't believe this! Rick Rude: You see there was a man that called me and told me that he had a spot open in the most devastating stable in the AOLWF so I couldn't refuse. The Franchise Shane Douglas, Ravishing Rick Rude, Billy Gunn managed by Woman. Triple Threat!!!! We are gonna rule this damn federation with an iron fist and thats just the way its gonna be because there ain't nobody that can stop us! Honky Tonk Man!!!!!! You and I have a little bit of unfinished business to attend to. Your win over The Ravishing One was nothing but a fluke! The next time I get you in that damn ring I'm gonna annihalate your ass because I can! This brings me to the New World Odor! You guys think your gonna come in here and take over? Its not gonna happen that way Hogan. You see we're not in the WWF or WCW anymore. You ducked my ass left and right and now that the True members of Triple Threat have arrived, Your goin down!!!!!!!!
