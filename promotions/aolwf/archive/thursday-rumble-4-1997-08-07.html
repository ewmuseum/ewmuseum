AOLWF Thursday Rumble #4

7 August 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jim: Well lets get to our main event damnit! The Fink: The following contest is set for one fall and is a special First Blood Match up!!!! Introducing first.....from Billings,MT....weighing in at 343 pounds!! This is GAARYYY ALLBBRRIIGHHHTT!!!!!! And from Detriot, MI....weighing in at 345 pounds....this is DIEEESSEEELLLL!!! Vince: How about you explain the rules for this match up JR! Jim: Ok...its very simple......first man who bleeds loses!! Vince: This could be a very violent match up! Bobby: Well you know it all dont ya Vince? Vince: The match begins!! Albright and Diesel meet in the center of the ring......Diesel with a scoop and a slam!! Jim: Gary Albright right back up..collar and elbow tie up......and Albright pulls Diesel into a side headlock! Vince: But Diesel escapes with a side suplex!!!!! Bobby: Uh oh! There's those suplexes again! Vince: Both men up.......and now we have a test of strength! Diesel is overpowering Albright! Oh! Diesel lets go and lights Albright's chest up with a big chop!! Jim: Gary Albright fires right back with a kneelift!! Diesel retaliates with a big right hand!! Vince: Diesel with a scoop and a slam!!! Bobby: I dont think Albright knows what he has started!!!! He beat Diesel last week and now Diesel is angry!!! Plus he has been calling out Taz and calling him a coward! Now Diesel knows how to suplex? Hmmmmmmm Vince: He now lifts Albright back up and sends him into the corner! Jim: Diesel now whips him to the ropes.......and puts him down with a clothesline! Vince: He now throws Albright out to the floor and follows him out! Lifts Albright to his feet......gutwrench suplex!!!!!! Bobby: Theres another one! Vince: Lifts him back up..........vertical suplex!!!!! And Albright crashes down on the concrete floor!!!!! Jim: Scoop and a slam by BDC!! He lifts Albright up again...short arm clothesline by Albright!!!!!!!! Vince: Diesel falls to the floor and Albright crawls back into the ring! Bobby: Diesel rolls back in as well and he's gonna be mad!! Vince: Diesel comes in and shoves a elbow into Albright's face! Albright falls down but gets right back up to his feet! Jim: Albright pulls Diesel into a side headlock but Diesel quickly escapes with a side suplex once again!! Vince: Both men up slowly.....Diesel whips Albright to the ropes..no!! Albright revereses! Diesel starts coming back and Albright tackles him at the knees!! Bobby: Oh! And you know Diesel has had problems with his knees!! Vince: This isnt THAT Diesel. Bobby: What are you talking about? Theres only one Diesel and thats him! Jim: Well both up are up!! Albright whips Diesel to the ropes....NO!! Now its Diesel who reverses and nails a big clothesline!!! Vince: Diesel has Albright up.....theres a right hand fist!! Follows with a boot to the gut!!! And a open hand slap!!!! Bobby: Slap him like a dog BDC! Vince: Theres a lock up..... Jim: Oh! Albright reaches around and pulls Diesel up off his feet.....Sidewalk Slam!!! Vince: Great move there by Albright! Albright pulls him back up now and drives a knee in his face! Follows that with a knee to the mid-section! Albright now goes behind Diesel and sets him up for a belly to back! Bobby: But Diesel puts the brakes on that with a elbow to the face!!! Vince: But look at Albright!!! He just shakes it off and grabs Diesel again!!!!!! Belly to Back suplex and Diesel gets dumped right on his head!! Jim: Albright lifts Diesel up again......Vertical suplex coming up.....NO!!!! Diesel blocks it!!! Diesel with a vertical suplex of his own!! Vince: Albright goes over the top rope and to the floor!!!!!!!! Bobby: Hey whats Diesel doing? Vince: Diesel is complaining to the referee about hair pulling! Jim: Oh good lord! Somebody just jumped out of the crowd!!!! Vince: Who is it? Bobby: I dunno! Whoever it is..is short and is wearing a mask!! Vince: I KNOOOOOWWWW Thats right! Jim: Oh good lord!!!!! The short masked guy is gouging away at Albright's head with a can opener!!!!!!! Good lord!!!! Now he rolls Albright back in and jumps back into the crowd and takes off!!! Vince: The referee never saw a thing!! Bobby: HA HA!!!! He does now!! The referee looks down and sees the blood pouring out of Albright's head and calls for the bell!!!!!! This match is over!!! Vince: Yes it is!! Diesel has won the match!!!!!! Jim: What a crock!!! Vince: The only question now is this....who was that short guy???? Bobby: You mean you dont know?? Vince: No I dont! Jim: Neither do I, it could be a whole lot of people!! Vince: Well sorry folks, we're all out of time here on Thursday Rumble!! Till next week....so long!!!!
