The AOLWF.....for 2 years....the force is revolutionary force in E-Mail Entertainment!!!!!!!! The America Online Wrestling Federation is looking for some great Role Players. We've just opened the doors for new people to come in, and we'd love to have you join. This is a Real Wrestler fed as well as a Role Play fed. So we're not looking for the Role Players who write somthing once a week....we're looking for people who can hang with the best. We have two cards a week with about 10 matches on each card. Every month we have a PPV.....and we have 5 titles. They are.... AOLWF World Title AOLWF United States Title AOLWF T.V. Title AOLWF Lightweight Title AOLWF World Tag Team Titles You know a lot of people post up a lot of BULLSHIT about their fed....but we're only telling you the truth when we say that this is the #1 Real Wrestler fed on the net. We've got the best writers, and we've got the best Rp'ers. You're the ones that will be missing out if you don't join. Hey....it's no skin off my ass if you don't join...I'm just telling you if you're looking for the #1 fed on the net...look no further!!!! E-mail me for more info!!!!!
