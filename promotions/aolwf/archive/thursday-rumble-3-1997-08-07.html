AOLWF Thursday Rumble #3

The Franchise Shane Douglas: Speaking of taking over, Thursday Rumble now belongs to Triple Threat! As a matter of fact, It will now be known as Thursday Live and if I hear an announcer call it Thursday Rumble again I'm gonna kick some ass! We don't have to take time out of our training anymore to do little comments and carnages because we've got a whole damn live broadcasted show to play with now! Ha hah hah hah!!!!! Bigelow!!!!!! Look at what your copycat ass Triple Threat has to go against. Once we take your asses out its gonna all out fuckin war on the AOLWF and Bret Hart can do whatever in the hell he wants because I've got his back if he needs it. Next week right here on Thursday Threat your gonna see the beginning of the takeover! We control Thrusday Threat and we are gonna strike everybody we want too! Ha hah hah. We will have the number one ranked show because all the little smart marks are going to tune in to see what in the hell we're going to do next but I'm not so sure if they can stomach it. Now I will allow Thursday Threat to continue.......Hit the music!!!!!!! ( Perfect Strangers blares as Douglas, Gunn, Rude, Windham, and Woman walk off holding up the Triple Threat sign. ) Bobby: Good evening everyone and welcome to Thursday Live!!!!!!!!! Jim: You think he was serious about kicking our asses? Bobby: Well go ahead and call the show the old name and see what happens. Jim: No thanks Bobby. Vince: I hate to say it but I think Triple Threat is taking over the AOLWF. While Chyna is stretchered out of the building lets take a quick commercial break. ************************************************************************** *************************** ( Commercial begins to roll as the camera pans in on The Franchise Shane Douglas wearing the new Triple Threat shirt. ) The Franchise: Now wouldn't all of you Triple Threat Disciples just love to get a piece of the future. Ha hah hah hah hah!! Well here is your chance you pieces of shit because the new Triple Threat shirt is hitting the market and its only $39.95!! You get a Threat signal on the back and the members on the front. It just doesen't get any better than this. So don't spend your money on the that faggot Shawn Michaels merchandise when you can collect something thats important! ( Billy Gunn runs in ) Gunn: Buy the shirt!!!!! Buy the shirt!!!!!!! Buy the shirt!!!!!! ************************************************************************** *************************** Bobby: Somebody give me the phone! I got to order a shirt. Jim: Lets get to Finkle and our Light-Heavyweight Championship match up! Howard Finkle: The next contest is scheduled for one fall and it is for the America On-Line Wrestling Federation Light-Heavyweight Championship Of The Woooooorrrrlld!!!! ( Psicosis' music begins to play ) Finkle: Introducing first the challenger,....Here is Psicosis!!!! Vince: Psicosis was originally suppose to challenge Chyna for that Light-Heavyweight Championship but Hakushi defeated her this past weekend at the Rampage. Bobby: And we all just saw what happened to Chyna! ( Hakushis' music begins to play ) Finkle: And his opponent,......Defending his newly won AOLWF Light-Heavyweight Championship Title for the first time. Here is Hakuuuusssshhiiiiiiiiii!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Jim: This should be a spectacular match. Psicosis is one of the best high flyers around and he is challenging the modern day Comikozee in Hakushi. Hakushi is in the ring and we are just about ready to kick things off. Vince: Psicosis just jumped Hakushi in the corner! Psicosis is laying in fast and furious kicks to the mid-section and he just nailed Hakushi right upside the head with another one. Psicosis sends Hakushi through the middle rope and the champion hit the concrete hard that time. Psicosis is getting a run,........ Jim: Suicide dive over the top rope and Psicosis nailed Hakushi while he was getting up! Bobby: We're not even a minute into this match and Psicosis has already gone crazy. To tell you the truth, I don't even know why we spend the money to set up a ring. I mean its not like we use it. Vince: Now Psicosis is kicking Hakushi in the ribs and whips him into the guard rail but Hakushi reversed it. Hakushi charges in after the challenger but Psicosis got a foot up and Psicosis hits a moonsault press off the guardrail to the concrete floor! Jim: Psicosis has started this match at a hundred miles an hour and things are not looking good for the champion but we all know that Hakushi has a very high threshold for pain. Bobby: I seen him get hit by a semi one time and just walk away. Vince: Well I don't know about all that. Jim: The challenger sends Hakushi back into the ring and is once again on the attack. Psicosis picks Hakushi up and delivers a body slam and the challenger with catlike speed climbs to the top rope. He's waiting for Hakushi to get up and he just caught him with a hurricanrauna off the top rope!!!! It could be over already! 1.........2..........No! Bobby: Hakushi was well aware of his ring position and got his foot over the bottom rope. Psicosis thought he had him on that one and so did I. Vince: Psicosis has things going his way and now sends Hakushi to the corner but the champion reversed it. Hakushi rushes in and connects with a leaping spinning savate kick to the face of Psicosis but the champion continued to fly and went over the top rope down to the floor but Psicosis just got his head took off with that kick. Jim: The champion is quick to his feet and he's dragging Psicosis outside the ring with him. Bobby: I told you. They just kind of pass through the ring and go to the outside. Jim: Hakushi is telling the fans to move out of the way and he just delivered a belly to belly suplex that sent the challenger into the second row! Hakushi just grabbed a chair from one of the fans and threw it in the ring and thats where he is headed as well. Hakushi sets up the chair by the ropes and hits the opposite side of the ring for momentum..........Oh Good Lord!!! Vince: Hakushi jumped off the chair, Sprung off the top rope and nailed Psicosis with a double axe handle in the second row!!!!! The Crowd: AOLWF!!!!! AOLWF!!!!! AOLWF!!!!! AOLWF!!!!! Vince: The crowd loves it but I don't think this match can go too long with the pace that these two competitors are going at. Jim: I agree on that point Vince: Hakushi has Psicosis back in the ring now and executes a brainbuster! The champion is putting that steel chair over the face of Hakushi and going to the top rope. Hakushi leaps off and drops a leg right down across the chair!!!!!!!! Psicosis is hurt badly but the champion is holding his leg. He makes the cover. 1.........2.......... Bobby: Psicosis kicked out. I don't believe that. Vince: Hakushi is headed to the top rope again but he seems to be distacted by one of the fans. The challenger is getting up and he's got that chair........... ( CRACK!! ) Bobby: Psicosis just nailed Hakushi in the face with that steel chair and the champion fell all the way down to the floor. Jim: Psicosis is going right after the champion. I think Psicosis is mad because he's dragging a table out from underneath the ring. Psicosis has the table set up and he just planted Hakushi on the head again with the chair. The challenger has Hakushi laid across the table and he's going back in the ring and now it looks like the challenger is going to use the chair. Vince: Psicosis comes off the ropes,....He hits the chair, Comes off the top rope and drops a leg on Hakushi but the table didn't break!!!!! The table didn't break!!!!!! Bobby: What in the hell?! Do we have specially re-enforced tabled now or something?! Jim: But it doesen't look like Psicosis is quitting. He's back in the ring and hitting the ropes again. He jumps off the chair and this time he clears the table......... ( CRASH!!!!!!!!! ) Bobby: Psicosis delivered another flying legdrop and the table went that time! Jim: Psicosis sends the champion back in the ring and he goes for the cover! 1!!! 2!!! And the champion kicks out again!!!! Hakushi can take so much pain that its gonna be very difficult for someone to get that belt away from him. Psicosis is arguing with the referee. He thought the count should have been faster. Bobby: Psicosis has Hakushi on his shoulder and sets him on the top rope. Right hand by Psicosis....and another one. Vince: The challenger is climbing to the top rope, It looks like he's gonna try and finish the champion off with a hurricanruana off the top. Here it comes!!! Jim: Hakushi turned it into a powerbomb off the top rope!!! Powerbomb off the top and now both combatants are down and hurt. Bobby: Its gutcheck time now. Whoever wants it the most is gonna win this one. Jim: Hakushi is the first man to his feet and he stomps away on Psicosis.The champion just threw Psicosis over the top rope and once again they're taking the battle to the outside. Bobby: Oh boy!,....Here comes another table. Vince: Hakushi is putting one end of the table on the security railing and the other on the ring aprin. Hakushi now puts Psicosis on the table and gets in the ring but Psicosis is getting up and Hakushi fires a quick right hand that stunned Psicosis. Psicosis is still standing on the table. He's facing the crowd and he seems a little wobbly. Hakushi comes off the ropes and...... ( SMAAASSSSHHHHH!!!!!!!!!!!!! ) Jim: OH DEAR GOD!!!!!! OH MY LORD!!!!!!!!! I CAN'T BELIVE THAT!!!!!!!!!!! The crowd: AOLWF!!!!! AOLWF!!!!! AOLWF!!!!! AOLWF!!!!! Bobby: Hakushi jumped over the top rope and caught Psicosis in a bulldog that took him through the table to the cement floor!!! I think Psicosis is dead! The crowd: AOLWF!!!!!! AOLWF!!!!!! AOLWF!!!!! AOLWF!!!!! AOLWF!!!!! AOLWF!!!!! AOLWF!!!!!! Jim: The champion is somehow back on his feet and he sends Psicosis back in the ring. Hakushi is positioning Psicosis in the middle of the ring and he's going to the top rope,.........Moonsault!!!!!! 1.........2...........3!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Hakushi has successfully defended his Light-Heavyweight Championship Of The World in one hell of match! Bobby: Do these guys get hazard pay? Vince: Lets not talk about that Heenan. However, I would like to say that....... ( Perfect Strangers begins to blare over the loudspeakers as The Franchise Shane Douglas is escorted down the aisle by Woman and Beulah. ) Bobby: YES! Douglas is back and it looks like he's got his wrestling tights on. Vince: He's not scheduled for a match here tonight! Jim: I really don't think its gonna matter Vince. The Franchise Shane Douglas: Cut the fuckin music!! I have come out here tonight for many reasons. I unveiled the true Triple Threat. I ended Chynas' career before it even began. I brought in Rick Rude. And now I've got to deal with a piece of shit named The Sandman!!!!! You see I've been hearing all the things that you have been saying Sandman and now its time to fuckin respond!!!! When you beat me for the ECW World Heavyweight Championship, You did not do it cleanly so stop saying you did. You see the angel thats standing here beside me, Woman, She had a little something to do with it. She took your cane and cracked me in the back of the knee with it which made me fall back and you to get the pin. But thats all goo because Woman and I had a liitle spat earlier that night so she did something that was totally uncalled for and ever she will admit that now. So you see Sandman, This Woman right here is the only thing that can make me lose so I'm gettin ready to give you your chance! If you want a piece of me punk then get out here and I'll give you an ass whippin that you are not gonna forget!!! ( Enter Sandman begins to play over the loudspeakers and the crowd goes nuts!!! ) Jim: Shane Douglas just dug his own grave because The Snadman is running down the aisle and he's got that cane!! Bobby: Get out of the ring ladies! This guy has completely lost it!! Vince: Sandman just hit the ring and takes a roundhouse swing at him with the cane! Bobby: Douglas ducked!!! Shane boots Sandman in the stomach and nails the single arm DDT!! Jim: Douglas is fierce!!!!! He's stomping the hell out of The Sandman! He picks Sandman up and nails another Single arm DDT! Douglas is getting the cane. He whips Sandman to the ropes and....... ( Smack!! ) Bobby: He just cracked Sandman in the head with his own cane. Ha hah hah hah!!!!! Vince: Thier is no call for this!!!! Sandman came out here to wrestle Shane Douglas and Douglas is turning it into something much more serious!! He's continually smacking Sandman in the back with that cane. Bobby: Well Sandman brung the cane into the ring and Douglas just used it first. Since the cane was brought into the ring I guess the referee is letting this be an Extreme type match of rules. Jim: Good observation Bobby. Bobby: You talkin to me?
