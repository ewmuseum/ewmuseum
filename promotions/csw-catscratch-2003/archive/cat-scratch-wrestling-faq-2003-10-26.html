[7] Conclusion ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ [1] What is this? ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Cat Scratch Wrestling is a e-wrestling promotion feature an all female roster. based out of Las Vegas, Nevada. Started by Brittney Christopher, the mind behind such successful promotions as American Wrestling Promotions, Ringside Seat Promotions, and the current Super 8. Cat Scratch Wrestling will feature all the glitz and glamour of Las Vegas and the hard hitting action of the female superstars of wrestling. Coming to you live every two weeks from the Las Vegas Event Center. You will get all of the following and more.... CSW will feature 5-6 fully written matches, the top female stars, and a whole lot more!. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ [2] Who runs it? ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ In character Brittney Christopher is the figuare head of CSW. Brittney has been around the business for many years and has ran a couple of top federations. Out of character I'll be doing most of the work for CSW. My name is [name withheld] and I've been involved with ew since 1996. I've been involved with several successful promotions and I'm currently running The Super 8 and the website EReview. I hope to make CSW just as successful as the other promotions I've ran. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ [3] How does it work? ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Cat Scratch Wrestling will be ran like most other e-feds today. Cards will be on a bi-weekly schedule. there will be an update after each card, and a roleplay/hype show before every card. During the "off" weeks there will be a special summary show which will be used to review the previous week. This card will also feature several dark matches which will help generate stories for the upcoming card. The rundown for the roster, shows, championships are as follows. Roster----- I'm hoping to start with at least 8 active members. I will cap the roster off at 15 [hopefully in time CSW will be popular enough to get that many]. As a handler you can run at least 2 characters in the fed at any given time. Shows--- There will be two regular shows and every 3 months a special event. Cat Scratch Fever---Cat Scratch Fever will be the summary recap show that will feature dark matches and a rundown of the upcoming card. Cat Scratch Sunday's---CS Sunday's will be a fully writen card with up to 5 live matches. This is the major show for CSW and will be broadcast live from the Las Vegas Event Center. Vengeance--This is the special event CSW will hold every three months. This is where rivalrys will be settled and new battles started. Championships---- At the start there will only be one major title that will be the CAT SCRATCH HEAVYWEIGHT TITLE. This title will be defended at least every other show. When/If the roster gets big enough there will be another title added. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ [4] How can I get involved? ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ CONTACT ME AT [detail withheld] or IM: [detail withheld] If you're ready to join you can complete the app below and send to [detail withheld] APPLICATION WVWVWVWVW Handler's Name: E-mail address: AIM/IRC: Wrestler's Name: Hometown: Height: Weight: Appearance: Alignment:. Having done play-by-play for American Wrestling Promotions and Ringside Seat Promotions. Blackwell is no stranger to the pro wrestling world and uses his intelligance and broadcast talents to provide solid play-by-play. Vince "The Prince" Richardson on the other hand is the complete opposite of Colin Blackwell. Vince tries to add some light hearted and comedic levity to the shows. Vince also served with Blackwell in AWP and RSP and the two have grown a strong chemistry. Interviewer: Rumor Mills Rumor is the inexperienced young lion of the broadcast team. Trying his best to get the real story behind the scenes Rumor usualy finds himself in the wrong place at the wrong time. As he name suggests Rumor is the man that spreads the gossip in the locker room, which at times makes him the enemy of the wrestlers. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ [6] Debut Show ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ If all goes well the debut of the CSW will be at the Super 8 in November. Brittney Christopher will have a special announcement and hopefully put together a special attraction match for the first night of the Super 8. If you would like to be involved with CSW and this special match get your app in now!!! ~~~~~~~~~~~~ [7] Conclusion ~~~~~~~~~~~~ The overall object off CSW is to have fun!! The other aim of this fed is to bring some respect and attention to the "Divas of EW". I'm not going for anything big here just hoping to run a fed that gets back to the basics of the game.
