An all-female promotion pitched as a Las Vegas spectacle by an operator already running two other named promotions. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Cat Scratch Wrestling opened with a FAQ posted 26 October 2003: an all-female promotion based in Las Vegas, staged as the brainchild of an in-fiction figurehead, Brittney Christopher, and billed to run every two weeks, “live from the Las Vegas Event Center,” with five or six fully written matches a card.

Its schedule set two regular shows — Cat Scratch Fever, a recap broadcast carrying dark matches and a preview of the coming card, and Cat Scratch Sunday's, the promotion's own live main event show — plus a quarterly special event, Vengeance, “where rivalrys will be settled and new battles started.” One title, the Cat Scratch Heavyweight Championship, is named as its founding belt, to be defended at least every other show, with a second promised once the roster grew large enough. Its broadcast team — play-by-play man Colin Blackwell, color commentator Vince “The Prince” Richardson, and backstage interviewer Rumor Mills — are introduced by name and role alongside Christopher, the promotion's own on-screen figurehead.

The FAQ sets a planned debut for November 2003 and caps the roster at fifteen once established, hoping to start with at least eight. Whether it opened its doors, ran a single card, or crowned a first champion at all isn't in the surviving record: no later document survives here to say.
