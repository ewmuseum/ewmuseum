New fed needs wrestlers to get underway. I am not going to boast about anything yet, because things are in the building stages. But with your help and some effort from me and you guys we can make it a fun fed. All i really want to do is have fun, so whehter you are experience, a firs timer, or looking to hone your skills, check it out. The site and fed is a work in progress, but we really need wrestlers. The first 8 wrestlers will be entered into the World title tournament. If you are familiar with GFed the id # is 6206 and the password is cwf, check out the Championship Wrestling Federation. For those ofyou who are not familiar with Gfed its the future of e wrestling for people looking to run a fed. But this is how you get to me fed, it may be kind of a hassle, but i promise in time it will be worth it. Go to www.geeksoft.net and click on the gfed link, and follow that to the gfed 2.0 link. somewhere, you should see a link to search for fed, you might have to click around to find, i dont remember. But anyways, do a search, and in the search box put in 6206, that is the id number, and that should bring up a link for the Championship Wrestling Federation. That should bring up the link, and all you have to do is create a wrestler and your in. There is a simple form for creating a wrestler. You might need a password when you are creating a wrestler in which case the password is cwf. You may or may not have to have a gfed account. if you do its simple just enter a screename, email address, and pick a password, and you ahve an account. I have experience in running a fed, i ran a damn good one, and i want to do the same. With effort from me as well as you guys, we can make it great. Please check it out. [name withheld]
