Modern Wrestling Corporation Player's Guidebook

17 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Modern Wrestling Corporation http://mwcwrestling.hypermart.net Player's Guidebook ======================================================================== WHAT IS MWC? ======================================================================== Based out of Laguna Beach, California, Modern Wrestling Corporation is the brainchild of professional wrestler Byron Fletcher. Using his father's money to back him, Byron started a wrestling promotion which he thought could compete with the big guys in terms of entertainment value. Keeping the promotion relatively small, Byron chose Newport Beach, California for its fruitful background and location to the large Los Angeles, California area. With Byron's affliction to the beach, his father had built a wrestling arena located right before the Balboa Pier in Newport Beach. The arena holds nearly a thousand people at full capacity and is within walking distance to the nerve center of the Newport Harbor. In honor of his father's addition to the promotion, the arena is named the Patrick Fletcher Wrestling Arena. Hopefully, with the building of the Arena and a small corporate headquarters, Byron Fletcher can turn MWC into a very interesting and entertaining wrestling promotion housing some of the greatest wrestling that the industry has seen. ======================================================================== SERIOUSLY? ======================================================================== In real life, the MWC is run by [name withheld]. I was involved in e- wrestling a few years back, but quickly fell out of it after losing an Internet connection. After recently getting back online, I've found the interest once more to be involved, which in this case, means running a league. I don't have much experience running these things, but that's never stopped me from doing things before. With any luck, we'll create interesting, interesting cards that will be a fun read. My contact information is located towards the bottom of this guide. ======================================================================== HOW DO I GET INTO THE MWC? ======================================================================== Firstly, you need to read this document carefully. If you don't plan on agreeing with some of the rules, ideas, or principles within, there really is no reason for you to apply. Secondly, check out the wrestlers that we currently have. This will give you a pretty decent idea of what character we have and which types of character we currently lack. Choosing to submit a character which we don't have will greatly increase your chances of being accepted. Thirdly, make sure you submit a sample of your work. It is difficult to completely judge a person's ability to handle a character without a sample of their work. You might have a great character, but unless you can put it to words, there is very little that the wrestler will be able to do. ======================================================================== WHICH TITLES ARE THERE? ======================================================================== There are currently three titles in the MWC. We are a rather small promotion right now, not generally reaching outside of the southern California region. Within due time, the league may become bigger, depending on the interest garnered. MWC ALTA CALIFORNIA TITLE: The main title in the MWC, it is named after what the Spaniards originally called the region which is now the 31st State. The bearer of this title holds a great deal of respect. MWC GOLDEN STATE TITLE: The secondary title in the MWC, this one is a stepping stone to the Alta California title. The holder of this title is generally a workhorse, a person who defends the title week in and week out. MWC ELDORADO TAG TEAM TITLE: The tag team title of the MWC. ELdorado is an imaginary place of wealth and fame, something which southern California is well-known for. However, the tag team that can claim to be the Eldorado Tag Team champions can hold their heads high, knowing that they are the best tag team that MWC has to offer. ======================================================================== WHAT SHOWS DOES THE MWC BROADCAST? ======================================================================== MWC Heavy Metal: Every week, on Sundays, MWC presents Heavy Metal. This card is hand-written, play-by-play of 4-7 matches per week. Including the wrestling will be on-card segments from wrestlers and other interesting portions. Hosted by head broadcaster Dale Calder and his side-kick Jon Grim, the wrestling action is broadcast over local television stations from the Patrick Fletcher Wrestling Arena right before the Balboa Pier in Newport Beach, California. MWC Voices: Each week, on Friday nights, MWC Voices is broadcast. Voices is a collection of flashes from wrestlers put into chronological order and sent out. After further exposure from the league, the show is expected to become larger and with more depth. ======================================================================== HOW DOES THE MWC WORK? ======================================================================== First off, this is a closed-flash system, which means that every role- play is sent to me. I put them together and send them out on Friday night under the banner of "MWC Voices". Each handler is expected to submit a role-play per week, unless other circumstances occur and prior arrangements were made. I understand that things come up in real life and that people may miss a week here and there, but people who continuously don't participate will not tolerated. While on the subject of role-playing, the MWC is a booked league. This means that the direction in which your wrestler heads is based on several factors: quality flashes from the handler; sufficient activity from the handler; and angles. Each match decision may not be based on that handlers particular flash for that week. Instead, the whole picture is looked at when deciding who will win and who will lose. Often times, a loss will occur in order to further an angle. This should be expected. In order for a person to be successful in the MWC, they need to participate. This means by role-playing for their character, whether it be an on-card segment or a traditional flash segment, submitting strategies for their matches, and by producing interesting angles. We've already discussed a small portion of what is expected in a role-play -- although the next section discusses it in greater detail -- so we shall move onto strategies. A strategy merely is a mindset for your character during their match. These are not required but are highly recommended. They make the match writers job much easier and allow your character to be portrayed more as you would like it. A strategy should include these things: - Entrance - Mind set during match (psychology) - Short move set - Short list of suggested spots (3-6 will suffice) - Possible ending - Response to either win or loss These are not that difficult to create and make it possible for your character to not only be better portrayed but to be better used. Creating angles in the league is not just up to you. As the fed head, it is my duty to also create interesting story lines, but not without the help of the handlers. You are expected to come up with angle ideas and story line submissions in order to keep your character fresh and to get them over with the fans. A contact list is supplied at the end of the Update which is sent out after each match. You can contact other handlers about setting up angles and so forth. You can also contact me and ask about angle ideas. ======================================================================== HOW DO I ROLE-PLAY? ======================================================================== There are some ground rules when it comes to role-plays: - Do not, under any circumstances, attack, injure, or otherwise harm and other wrestler or MWC official without proper consent. This will not only get you in trouble with the fed head but will generally be frowned upon by your co-handlers. - Please refrain from using out-of-control language. Some curse words can be accepted, such a "damn", "ass", "hell" and even the occasional "shit", but the rest must be [beep]'d out somehow. This rule applies to the broadcasters as well when match writing. - Do not use racial, ethnic, or otherwise abusive slurs in your role- plays. These are frowned upon with a great deal. If verbal abuse is a problem, the character will be suspended from participation or thrown out of the league altogether. - Don't take things to heart. This is supposed to be a game, so treat it as such. Do not take things seriously, and try not to "shoot" on other wrestlers, promotions, people. This is not necessary and is not welcomed. - Do not break kayfabe. There is no need for your wrestlers to be using terms such as "jobber", "blade", "work", "no sell", or mention any real life promotions or wrestlers unless it is necessary to further an angle, which must be passed through me first. Kayfabe exists to create an appeal for the hobby. - Please don't submit novels or novellas as role-plays. It is not only unnecessary, but often times will be overlooked or skimmed. This league isn't about creating the longest cards. We are about creating fun, enjoyable cards which pack a punch. - HAVE FUN. This is said in nearly every fed's guidebook, but it's the truth. We are here to have fun and create a good read. If you don't do either, then you are not in it for the right reasons. Enjoy yourself and others. ======================================================================== WHAT ABOUT SUPERCARDS? ======================================================================== True to form, MWC does not have the size to hold pay-per-views. Instead, every eight weeks or so, we hold a supercard which is the culmination of all of the angles within that cycle. The supercards will generally be larger, and for good reason. There are no set dates for these cards, but rather, will be announced when the time is right. ======================================================================== WHO ARE THE MWC OFFICIALS? ======================================================================== DALE CALDER: The heel announcer of the two Heavy Metal broadcasters. He isn't really that much of a heel, truthfully. He has more heel tendencies though, than Jon Grim. In his late 40's, Dale is a former wrestling scout in Florida, sent packing after making several bad decisions on wrestlers. Slim and well-cut, Dale likes to dress well, often times seen in a suit and tie. Black hair which is starting to show signs of grey, much to the dismay of Dale. JON GRIM: In street-terms, Jon Grim would be known as a nut rider. He is often times completely agreeing with Dale in order to win over his acceptance. However, it usually doesn't work, inciting sarcastic -- and sometimes humorous -- remarks from Dale. Jon is in his late 20's. A college dropout who served four years in prison for racketeering charges while involved in organized crime in Philadelphia. Jon found himself working in the MWC after being contacted by childhood friend, and MWC owner, Byron Fletcher. Grim usually wears a pair of jeans and a sweatshirt. He has brown hair and a pale complexion. GEORGE OAKES: Another childhood friend of Byron Fletcher, they were basically best friends growing up. They learned to skimboard together and would often times walk along the beaches in search of that killer shoredump. Often referred to as Geo, Oakes has curly, shoulder length, dirty blonde hair and regularly sports black sunglasses. Heavily influenced by the surfer culture and southern California in general, Geo wears clothes made by companies such as Rusty and Quiksilver as well as Nike and various LA sports teams jerseys. Always interesting and always starting some sort of trouble, Geo is a backstage interviewer who loves to become involved with the action. JUDY CHEEK: The lovely blonde interviewer of the MWC, often causing trouble with her short skirts and tight shirts. The typical beach bum chick, it is sometimes rumored that she and Byron Fletcher had a fling years prior, which would account for her having this job without any prior experience in wrestling at all. MANUEL LOUQUE: A retired southern California wrestling legend, Manuel used to go by the name Caiman IV when he wrestled in the northern Baja and southern California. A short, Mexican-born, father of three wrestling sons, who can still move with cat-like reflexes. Often times lets matches go as far as possible. Manuel is the head official in the MWC. PAUL MCCLANE: McClane, former referee of a southern California wrestling promotion known as Souther California Wrestling, was offered the position of referee by Byron Fletcher. Calls the matches down the middle, not showing any bias at all. JIM TAPP: The final referee in the MWC, Jim Tapp was a former broadcaster in said SCW. Tapp was offered the position of broadcaster in the MWC but opted to be referee instead, citing it to be his true passion. ======================================================================== HOW CAN I CONTACT YOU? ======================================================================== In case you missed it, my name is [name withheld].
