Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Quantum Wrestling Federation announced itself opening its own webpage on 24 March 1999, offering twenty-one open roster spots in a “Hunt for The All Star Title” under a self-billed president signing off as “the Chris Jericho of E-Wrestling.”

Two days later, the final round of its first televised broadcast, Dimentionz, put the vacant QWF World Heavyweight Championship on the line between Shane Beck and a debuting giant billed only as Mountain. Beck won it with a chair-assisted Atomic Arabian Facebuster after burying Mountain under half a dozen chair shots — but the broadcast's own color commentator, QWF President Kevin Michaels, called the finish questionable on air, and the referee appeared to miss Mountain's foot on the rope.

Michaels confronted the new champion in the ring immediately after, dismissed the win as tainted, and booked Beck to defend the title the following week against Mountain in a Chairshot Survival Deathmatch, in which a wrestler who could not answer a ten-count after any chair shot would lose.

How that rematch, billed from the Kingdome in Seattle, played out isn't in its surviving record.
