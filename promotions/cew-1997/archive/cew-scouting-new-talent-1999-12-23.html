CEW — Recruitment

Scouting New Talent for CEW

23 December 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey guys what's up? Long time no see. It's [name withheld], co-founder and owner of CEW, along with [name withheld], co-founder and webmaster of CEW, inviting anyone interested in having fun with e-wrestling to join CEW.
It's the holiday season, which is the season for giving...why don't you give yourself some good e-wrestling fun and jump on board? We've got TNM7 for the text bodies of matches, we add commentary, do full commentary cards, and we determine who moves up the ladder as follows:
The more "over" your wrestler(s) get, the higher your wrestler goes on the ladder. You get over through gaining as much heat for your wrestler as you can. This can be attained through any combination of quality rp's/interviews, jumps (saved for cards, obviously), quality angles, in-ring interviews (saved for cards---we've got to keep them interesting somehow), and anything else that will get your wrestler over.
[name withheld], Tom, and I started Cyber Extreme Wrestling in March of
1997 and we've had a blast for almost 3 years with it. This is continuous.
We haven't shut down once. Right now, we're looking for some new blood. We need to keep angles fresh, keep the fun up, energy levels high, and fucking
A...I just want to see some new faces in the league. We're all friends here, man. It's just a game---no need to stab yourself or your neighbor's dog over it...we just want to have fun. If you do too, check us out. Give us a reply and we'll send you a form, answer all your questions, and pimp you the finest ho's nationwide...maybe add you to our Christmas list too!
But hey, enough about that...we all know Pimpin' Ain't Easy, but having fun in e-wrestling can be...let's collaborate...let's have fun in CEW, anyone that's interested.
Hey, guys, thanks for your time. From us to you and yours, we hope you and yours have a very Merry Christmas and a Happy New Year and kick off 2000 right. Take good care.
Your friends and resident party animals, [name withheld] CEW owner/chief promoter [name withheld] CEW Webmaster
