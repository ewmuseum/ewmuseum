GWL - New E-Fed - looking for Wrestlers(App - Inside)

19 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Welcome to my new E-Fed, Always when someone starts an E-Fed, you might think why? Well, I found out myself, that I'm not satisfied playing in an E-Fed, most
E-Feds nowadays only schedule matches every 7 days, I think to really get involved, you need to see wrestling twice a week, I prefer having less matches on a card, then having to read endless cards once a week. So what do
I offer:
I offer 2 Wrestling shows a week, Monday Bonanza and Thursday Slammin'. Also a schedule of PPV will be made, PPV will be about every 6 weeks. The league will start as soon as there are the small number of 8 wrestlers. The first cards will just be start up cards, to get the fed going, as soon as there are 16 wrestlers, a tournament will be scheduled, which will take place in the regular shows, and which will lead to the first PPV.
Roleplays will be sent every second day, either via E-Mail, or in the Newsgroup, The Fed wants active roleplayers, but you don't have to be a genius, I don't expect you to write 5 pages twice a week, just be active and develop your character.
Feuds/angles and whatever will start with wrestlers coming in, I'm open to every idea, and will help develop good storylines.
Starts, what I expect from a strat is simple, tell me how and why you will beat your opponent, describe the moves used, or what tactics you want to use, everything about the match helps writing the match.
Booking is done by me, this is a roleplay fed, but also Match Strats are important, sometimes a wrestler might lose a match despite he is the better roleplayer, to develop a feud or get a better storyline, but in the end, the better roleplayer will be awarded.
Titles: Will to start we will have only the GWL World Title. Other titles to come, will be a European Title, a T.V. Title and Tag teams
Titles, if there will be a certain number of Tag Teams.
So now, fill out the form and wait for GWL Wrestling to come round your corner. Send everything for GWL to [name withheld] at: [detail withheld] GWL- German Wrestling League Application Just a note - this App is taken mainly from WILD, I saw the App and think it looks great, hope you don't mind me using it.
