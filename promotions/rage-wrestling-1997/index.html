Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

RAGE! Wrestling's own creation package, dated 2 June 1997, introduced the promotion as the work of three co-presidents and set a debut card for 21 June. It called itself a spiritual successor to New Internet Championship Wrestling (NICW), “one of the greatest federations, in e-wrestling, and in my opinion, the best,” and promised to run in the same style with differences of its own besides.

RAGE! Wrestling billed itself as a strictly regional promotion, based out of New York City and northeastern New Jersey rather than the “world wide” scale it credited to most of its peers. Its cards ran from the RAGE!-Arena, seating 2,134, with its weekly RAGE! Night televised live on Queens Public Access; four annual supercards followed the calendar in turn — a New Year's Day thirty-man Rumble, an April tournament crowning “King RAGE!,” a Labor Day RAGE! Fest, and a Christmas Eve show closing out the year. Only two championships were ever named, a Heavyweight and a Tag Team title, with a Junior Heavyweight belt held in reserve should the roster need one.

The promotion described itself as affiliated with a second federation, the Internet Wrestling Federation/World Organization of Wrestling, whose own wrestlers were made eligible to compete for RAGE! Wrestling's titles and to appear on its partner's pay-per-views in place of a supercard of its own. Its rules barred any roleplay that would offend an ethnic group outright, on pain of removal after a single offense.

No card, roster, or result of RAGE! Wrestling's own survives beyond that creation package and a near-identical second printing two weeks later. Whether it reached its planned debut, and what became of it afterward, is not recorded.
