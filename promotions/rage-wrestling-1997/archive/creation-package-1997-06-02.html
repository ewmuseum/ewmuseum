Wrestling has a limit of ONE wrestler per handler, or ONE tag team per handler. This being because there is a limit to 30 WRESTLERS and 10 TAG TEAMS in the league.
(*) Interviews, and flashes -- Try to make your role plays interesting, and make sure, they have a great deal of quality.

If you're going to go on vacation, and are going to be unable to role play for a period of time, please tell us before hand.

This federation only holds cards in one small region. That being the Metropolitan area (New York City + Northeastern
New Jersey). And RAGE! Wrestling only has four super cards a year, none avalible on pay-per-view. A majority of RAGE! Wrestling's events take place in the RAGE!-Arena. This is an exclusive wrestling complex, that seats up to 2,134 individuals. Other events take place in various other recreation sites. RAGE! Wrestling is the first regional promotion of
IWF/WOW (Internet Wrestling Federation/World Organization of Wrestling). Unlike other world wide-regional wrestling promotion agreements, the deal between these two federations are more noticable. IWF/WOW wrestlers are eligible to win RAGE! Wrestling titles and tournaments. Since RAGE!
Wrestling doesn't produce any pay-per-views on its own, it will have some matches on IWF/WOW's pay-per-view events.
Cards: RAGE! Wrestling holds one weekly televised card -- RAGE! Wrestling RAGE! Night (RWRN). RWRN is held every Saturday night, from the RAGE-Arena, at 10 PM. The show is televised LIVE on Queens Public Access television. Brooklyn, Bronx, Manhatten, and Staten Island can all catch a taped version of the show on Monday's, at 6 PM. RAGE! Wrestling will also hold house shows, from time to time. Notifications for special events will be sent out.
