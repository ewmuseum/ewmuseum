A September 1996 e-fed whose surviving weekly broadcast crowns two finalists before its own operators announce a merger with a fed styled FGW. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

This promotion's surviving record is a single day, 20 September 1996: a weekly broadcast, “Wednesday Wipeout,” copyrighted “WWWF Productions,” and a second post the same day announcing the promotion had already merged into another fed. No founding statement of its own survives.

That broadcast opened with new announcer Eric Price joining BillyBob at the desk, and colour man Drexler needling both of them, before running a World Tournament bracket: Len Miller outlasted The Crippler in a back-and-forth main bout, while Damien Long — managed by two masked men who unmasked mid-entrance as Quartz and Fury of a group the crowd chanted as TFOD — fought Iceman to a double-countout draw outside the ring. President Kyle Arcin ruled Miller through to the final outright and ordered Long and Iceman to run it back that Friday to decide who joined him.

The rest of the card ran alongside it: the Bruise Brothers beat Over the Hill in tag action, Scott Williams stole a win from Rage with a Diamond Cutter, the Supreme Powers put away the Venoms on a Zeus thunderbolt, and the main event saw Purple “Hardcore” Nerple beat the Welsh Dragon by piledriver through a table — a match paused mid-fall when the assigned referee collapsed of a heart attack and a second official had to finish the count.

The Friday rematch, the tournament final, and any champion it crowned are not in the surviving record: the only other document held is that same day's announcement that the promotion had merged with a fed styled FGW. How the merged promotion carried on from there isn't in the surviving record either.
