Man Without a Country

16–19 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
EastPrez — 04-16-04, 05:31 PM (FADEIN: To 'Hot Property' EDDIE MAYFIELD, standing in front of a INTRUDERS logo, wearing a plain black t-shirt, Black running pants, and his CSWA PRESIDENTIAL CHAMPIONSHIP belt around his waist. MAYFIELD looks scuffed up, and is sporting a full beard, arms still wrapped up in tape ECW-Style around the forearms and hands. Written on his taped right arm in red marker is the word 'MUTINY'. In his left hand is a white laundry marker. Standing a few steps away and to the left is the big, bad, BANDIT, the Intruders 'Head of Security-slash-Enforcer. He's wearing biker leathers, and a Harley Davidson bandana made out of a 'If you can read this, the bitch fell off' T-shirt. BANDIT growls and looks straight ahead, arms folded, showing off totally tattoo-covered arms. MAYFIELD shifts his attention and turns around to look at the banner, then turns back around, sucking his teeth.)



MAYFIELD: "Well, you see where this was heading, huh? The CSWA thinks it 'WON' the War of Intrusion. Well, if you look at the facts, maybe it did. GXW? Fell the HELL off within moments of their invasion. Intruders? The hottest faction on television? We came, we saw, we raised a little hell and kicked a little ass. If I could remember the rest of the words to that song, I'd recite em, but screw it, you get the idea. Chad Merritt was wide awake at night, laying in a puddle of sweat because of what Eddie Mayfield and the I's were bringing to him. PRIME TIME - hell, we took that over. INTRUDER TEE VEE - yeah, don't lie - you LOVED it. We kicked in Merritt's skybox door and made it our base of operations. We put Bandit in the production room and scared those little old men senseless, dood - when Bandit says 'CUT TO CAMERA FOUR', Man, you had better DO IT. (CUTTO: BANDIT, who holds up a gloved hand motioning for the Chokeslam! MAYFIELD glances over and makes a quick 'I'm glad I'm not in YOUR shoes' look at the camera) And all was good in the land. EXCEPT... EXCEPT... That some people... couldn't HACK it. That's right. I remember the I's, and I remember the I's filling up a room, a wrestling ring, or filling up some ring rats back at the Garner, NC Hyatt after a show. But what is new to me is this. (Motions around him) WHERE THE F(BLEEEP!) IS EVERYBODY?! Yeah, I know - they all tucked tail and RAN. GUNS? Heh. I tried to cover for him - tried to carry his Gimli-sized ass through our alliance, but I could tell his heart wasn't in it from the Get-go. He thought that he would have someone to keep his ass in the limelight with a third of the work, and looked right to the packmule of CSWA - (Points to self) That's right, Eddie Mayfields strong back for a ride. he wanted to piggyback EDDIE MAYFIELD to the promise land - the demise of CSWA itself, or to another World Championship - not necessarily in that order. But when I came into that locker room from day one and I told him, 'Look man, I don't give a sh[BLEEP!] what you say - I'm not bending over for ANYBODY anymore, he swallowed hard reluctantly and accepted that. He didn't like it and finally buckled under the pressure that he wasn't getting his way. But there was a part of that equasion that I didn't hold to:



I lied.



I lied, because I bent over TIME AND TIME AND TIME AGAIN for 'management' when I said I wouldn't. I bent over because I saw how the veil of the Bug, the Temptation of Hornet and those other overdone a[BLEEP!]holes were gonna keep me crawling around the midcard so I played ball. 2 years ago, damn near, I CALLED OUT HORNET ON NATIONAL TV. Did he come? NAW. Did he come out there and put me in my place? Pssh - RIGHT. No, I got ignored. Merritt didn't want his boy getting one-upped by a 'clown' like me. The guy who, on EVERY OCCASSION HE GOT to get his hand on that CSWA Championship, got taken up the a[BLEEP!] by MANAGEMENT. Mayfield vs. Aho - MATCH OF THE YEAR CANDIDATE. Guess how that went down? The 145th Airborne and the coat check girls baby daddy ran to the ring to keep me from showing up the CSWA's boy and bring home that belt. The Unified Tagteam titles? yeah, almost a YEAR of BULL[BLEEP!] and getting tazered by a couple of BABYFACES couldn't keep them off of me and Craig Miles' waist, but DAMN they tried.



And lookit just a few weeks ago - DECISION REVERSED, as I pinned that dumb hayseed Shane Southern in the middle of the ring, after he answered my Casting Call (Smirks) and I got my hand raised, but NO NO NO NO NO! We can't have any heels winning major titles around here - give Southern the win. And this guy has the nerve to act like he whooped my ass in that match. Whatever, Shane. So where does that leave me? Well at ANNIVERSARY, me and my GOOD CLOSE FRIEND Craig Miles went out there and got set up by 'Mr. CSWA' and some freaking retard whos' gotten too many cheap shots over on me for my liking as it is, and they beat Craigs lazy ass and we lose the World tag straps. (MAYFIELD takes the cap off of the laundry marker, and starts drawing on his shirt... making an outline of a championship title on his shoulder) That's MY belt right there... (He awkwardly draws another outline on his right shoulder) and thats MY OTHER BELT right there... those titles are MIA now, because of Miles didn't feel like kicking out of a weak-ass finisher. And where is he now? Hell if I know, and you know what? GOOD RIDDANCE. (BANDIT glances over with a look of confusion) That's right Bandit, I said it. GUNS walked out, f[BLEEP!] him. And he took his lackeys with him. MILES walked out to go do god knows what, later for him too - Who's left? Shamon? (Smirks and shakes his head)



Shamon? Hey, I have an idea for you, Shamon, LOSE MY fu[BLEEP!] PHONE NUMBER. Better yet, if I run into you in the hall, moonwalk your ass out of my air, because I'm gonna DROP YOU ON YOUR HEAD JUST FOR GENERAL PURPOSE. As far as I'm concerned, the Intruders are DEE OH AY - DEAD ON ANNIVERSARY. (MAYFIELD turns around and RIPS down the banner, leaving an exposed brick wall and several clamps dangling on a wire) Exactly. This title I have around my waist? The Presidential Championship? I hear idiots in the back snicker when they talk about it, because it's not the BIG BELT. Because it's not the World title. this coming from a bunch of pu[BLEEEP!] who never got anything done in their careers, but they wanna try and tear Eddie Em down for spite. Player Haters, the LOT of you. From the top of the card down to that kid in the parking garage who tops off my gas tank - you ALL want to be Eddie Mayfield, and that's no Jack. From the laid-back Devil-May-Care attitude, to the 6-star mic control, down to the way I make it look EASY out there.



And all of this while leading the hottest thing on TV, that EYE created from day one, and EYE just destroyed. THAT, is power, THAT is control, you retards. That is Eddie Mayfield coming out on National TV and doing something unheard of on CSWA Programming - making people CARE about what was going on in the ring. Not about some scarred-up fake hardcore legend and his rat doing monolouges from As The World Turns, NOT some BS Sibling Rivalry crap that nobody but 5 people and the head office cared about, not about some GOD-D[BLEEEP!] Midgets and Muppets. What did they get wet for? They screamed and called their local cable providers in horror when we beat Dan Ryan like a government mule and dropped his ass through a table and left him laying for dead. We had Mr. and Mrs. Nielson up all night when you heard Voodoo Chile come up, because you knew SOMETHING was gonna happen. Even...



GASP! Even Chad Merritt paid attention finally.



He knew that for all of the noise I ran, that the sh[BLEEP!] was truth. That nobody since god-knows had the sand to step out of nowhere and take the fight to the front step of the owner. You wanna talk about history? Let's create something new, so I don't EVER have to hear about GUNS and his God-D[BLEEEEP!] knee and that stake again. Let's create something new, so I don't have to hear about Mike Randalls and his shroom-addled brain YET AGAIN. What I offer to you all ... (Stands back and opens his arms) IS EDDIE MAYFIELD. ready to accept his place atop the mountain. Because EYE have single-handedly changed the CSWA Climate. The Young Blood or whoever walks in here from now on should owe me a royalty for paving the road.

The next lion who decides he's gonna be the most vile heel in the history of the business or learn how to wield the mic WILL refer to my DVD. I am the Pioneer, here, and how fitting for it to be that EYE - heh... (Looks down and rubs the title) am STILL the President Emeritus of CSWA.



Those tag belts? They were an anchor around my neck. They held me down, and held me back in a worthless spot on the card where there were NO CHALLENGERS. Troy and that crackhead? Hey, have fun man, you can both go back to your rowhome and do lines off of them for all I care. Now I am free to make my PRESIDENTIAL decisions as I see fit. Steel Viper? Cameron Cruise? You want to come after me? I have NOTHING LEFT TO LOSE, and that's dangerous. I am a man without a country, and I'll fight this whole f[BLEEP!] league by myself if I have to, because I'm after ONE thing:



That CSWA World Title.



Shane Southern? You dumb hick, you were'nt ready, and EYE knew you weren't ready to step up. You can talk crap about the 'bafoon' role all you want, but to me, you look like a beaten dog who hasn't held a belt in over 6 months here. So you can Parties' Over till your head falls off, but I know one thing - it's time for you to get to the back of the line, because you blew it, Hoss. Your glorification by all of those dirt writers and such never could see you for what you are, and for all that you swear you're better than me - you're just a CHOKE ARTIST, dood, of the worst kind. I won't make that mistake. You can ignore me, hate me, come crown me with a chair or try to kick my head off my shoulders, and it won't matter, man - you'd be better off Superkicking your own shadow against a brick wall. Or better yet - ignore me like you've always claimed you wanted to do. I've waited too long for this, and the Pioneer ain't gonna stop blazing trails until it's me and Dan Ryan, or whoever has the belt, across from me.



So Merritt - you get your robots and your henchmen to come get me - f[BLEEEP!] em, and f[BLEEEP!] you too and your suprises to keep me down - because I'm gonna Heel my ass off to get to where I gotta be. You thought I was bad before? man, you don't know the half. (Pauses) Yunno what? I have more to say, but I'll stop now - I've blessed you all with too much already. I'm outta here. (EDDIE turns to BANDIT, catching him offgaurd) Oh yeah one more thing - Bandit? just like my man Dee Dot Trump says - YOU'RE FIRED. PEACE."



(FADEOUT as MAYFIELD turns on his heels and walks away, leaving BANDIT stunned and in silence!) SouthernBoy — 04-16-04, 05:41 PM I know how Bandit feels ...
