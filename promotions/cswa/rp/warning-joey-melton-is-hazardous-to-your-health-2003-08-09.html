Warning: Joey Melton is Hazardous to your Health...and Sanity

9 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
QueenOfTheRing — 08-09-03, 09:28 PM OOC: Ok, this is my first promo here (please...be kind ;)), and it kinda ties in to happenings in NFW with Joey Melton. Go here: http://www.fwrestling.com/fw/FWForum52/134.html and here: http://www.fwrestling.com/fw/FWForum52/135.html to get the set-up





Pavement beneath rubber, wind whipping through hair, music blasting through a high-priced system and even higher-priced speakers. It's the same road she's travelled for years now. I-75 hasn't changed much, save for the obligatory construction work that always needs to be done to keep Florida's highways running smoothly.



Today, however, something's different.



Lindsay Troy was never one to let things that had little to no significance in her life get under her skin. But, after a long day at a place she never enjoyed going to, something managed to irk her just a bit.



Joey Melton.



Just thinking about that name brings a snarl to Troy's lips.



Today could have been, quite possibly, the worst day of her professional life at Radicomm. Not only did that pigheaded imbecile and his little twit sidekick nearly send one of her personnel directors into a murderous fit because of the elementary school fiasco, but he insinuated events and embarrased her.



AND...he DARED to lay his hand on her.



She feels so dirty. That filth touched her hip. Sure, it was only for a fleeting moment, but still...



This dress needs to go to the dry cleaners. Immediately.



Troy presses down hard on the gas pedal, and nudges the black Ferrari 360 Spider into the 6th gear, whizzing along at a speed that would surely have gotten her pulled over, had the police not known the types of cars she drives.



The CD changes over to a new disc, and the car speeds off down the road



*************



The car finally comes to a rest on top of a circular, gravel driveway. A long garage is set off to the left, but Troy's not ready to put the car away.



She has to drop the dress off at the dry-cleaners before they close at 8.



She steps out of the driver's side, pocketbook and briefcase in hand, and shuts the car door. The camera pans upward to take in the magnificent mansion set upon what seems like hundreds of acres of land before cutting back to Troy. She walks around the side of the car and makes her way toward the house. As she walks, with the short black dress clinging to her in the hot summer air, she's met by two large Doberman guard dogs, who trot by her side as she approaches the marble stairs that lead up to the front door. Troy takes the stairs two at a time, an amazing feat given the shoes she's wearing. After fumbling for her keys, she enters the house. The two dogs turn around and sit by the door, facing the driveway, their ears pricking alertly.



Home is where the heart is. She throws her things on the large kitchen island and walks over to the refrigerator. She pulls a water out and leans back against the door, her face showing signs of weariness.



That pompous, arrogant, sonofabitch...



She unscrews the cap to the bottle and takes a long, slow sip before lowering the water from her lips. Troy brushes wisps of brown, curly hair from her piercing hazel eyes. In the background, she hears a television blaring from another room.



Alaina's home early. Great.



She leaves the kitchen and walks down a hallway and enters an enormous, lavishly decorated den. Sitting on an large black leather sofa sits Alaina Troy, blond hair up in a messy bun-type thing, with the remote in her hand. The elder Troy leans back against a column as the younger one speaks



AT: I got home before you for once. What gives?



LT: I had a few stops to make first.



AT: I'll bet.



pause



AT: I heard you had a visitor today.



One shoe drops



LT: You could say that.



AT: The guy that went after Grant's kid. Melton was his name?



Here it comes...



LT: Yep. The other shoe could drop at any second. In 5...4...



Alaina turns around on the couch and looks wide-eyed at her sister



AT: Is he half as handsome in person as he is on TV??



Bam. Shoe...meet floor



LT(curtly): I wasn't paying much attention.



AT: Oh come on Lindz...just because you don't have a ring on your finger doesn't mean you can't look



LT: Can we please not talk about this right now?



She rolls her eyes, exasperated, and flops down on the matching black leather loveseat, a few feet away from her sister, and crosses her legs. Alaina glares wide-eyed at her



AT: Why don't you want to talk about it?



LT: Because I'd rather forget that Melton ever bothered to show up. Do you know what a public relations nightmare his stunt turned out to be? Christ, Grant was ready to decapitate him, and he probably would have if security didn't stop him.



AT: How come I wasn't told?



LT: Oh please, you were probably in your office screwing a client.



Alaina nods her head, knowingly. She liked to use her lunch hour wisely...



LT: Why is it that whenever I have to make an appearance at the office, something bad always happens that I have to sort through and smooth out?



AT: You're just lucky, I guess.



LT: Oh yeah. Real lucky.



Is it possible to hate someone you don't even know more and more with each passing minute?



His name makes her want to spit.



Mr. Joey "I'm so goddamn high and mighty" Melton



LT: I just want to curl up and forget that today ever happened.



AT: Well, you can't. Not yet anyway. You've got that dinner thing to go to tonight.



Aw ****. She had completely forgotten about that



LT(dryly): Great.



She looks down at her watch



LT: Is he...



Alaina shakes her head



AT: No. He left about an hour or two ago, just kinda grabbed his briefcase and suit-coat and walked out the door. I think he yelled something about leaving you a note, but I don't really remember.



LT: Well, I guess I should go get changed and see if I can find this "mystery note".



She stands up, pulls her dress down as far as it can possibly go (which isn't very far at all), and exits the den. Alaina looks after her



AT: Well...EYE think Melton's good looking. Especially those eyes...



*************



On a different level on the mansion, Lindsay Troy is in her bedroom. The room is gigantic, and decorated even more nicely than the den. The triple king-sized bed is made, although more thrown-together than neat, and articles of clothing lay strewn on top of the spread. Black shirt, black pants...those don't belong to her, but the pink lace corset top and tight khaki pants do.



Troy notices a piece of paper lying on top of a pillow. She sits on the edge of the bed and unfolds the note, reading it silently. She smiles to herself, and refolds the piece of paper, returning it to where she found it



LT(to herself): MISTER MELTON might not know just what he's gotten himself into...



She stands up and walks toward the wall-to-wall closet, and the scene cuts to black...
