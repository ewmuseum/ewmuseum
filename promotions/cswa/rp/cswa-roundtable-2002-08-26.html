CSWA Roleplays

CSWA Roundtable

26–29 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GreggG — 08-26-02, 03:35 PM (CUT TO: The U-62 logo, followed by "Rodeo" by Aaron Copeland. Recent highlights are shown, and then after 30 seconds there is a desk shot of junior TV personality BILLY BUCKLEY.)

BILLY: Welcome fans to CSWA Roundtable here on U-62, The Wrestling Channel. In the wake of Fish Fund, a lot of fiery words have been said between some of the brewing rivalries here in the CSWA. Perhaps the most tense situation has come between members of the GXW, The Intruders and various unaligned CSWA wrestlers. At the forefront of that list is the enigmatic Troy Windham-- who has returned from injuries to try and take on the self-appointed mantle of "Mister CSWA" while also feuding with his brother, Mark, who once again was crowned CSWA World Champion. Recently, I had a chance to sit down and talk to The King Of All Media about his return, his future and what he plans to do in the present.

(CUT TO: A black screen reads "Injuries." CUT TO: A shot of Troy, in a sit-down setting.)

TROY: Y'know, Billy-- the thing about my injuries wasn't that they came all at once. I didn't walk into Anniversary health. I walked into Anniversary less than 50 percent, let alone 100 percent. I've been injury prone for as long as I can remember. Wrestling's quite a cut-throat sport. There's a lot of guys out there who will do anything to get ahead in this sport. I should know, dogg-- because I've been known to get down'n'dirty also. But being someone whose been in the spotlight, who has been a force in this industry-- I've had a target painted on my back for a long time. Eli tore my hand apart. Eli busted my neck up some more. But I took my time off, had my surgery and rehabbed myself back. The results are going to be clear the minute I step foot in the wrestling ring-- I'm going to be in the best shape in my life, all at the time in my career where I'm entering my athletic prime. If you thought you knew what I was capable of doing before... well, buhbuhbuhbaby... you ain't seen nuthin' yet.

(CUT TO: The black screen. The words "Mark Windham" appear on the screen. CUT TO: Troy.)

TROY: There's a lot of questions everyone wants to know about my relationship with my older brother. Well, let me spell it out for everyone-- I have no relationship with my older brother. For my entire pro career, I was the one who was labelled as selfish, as bratty, as someone who took the shortcuts. No one ever bothered to ask my side of the story about my relationship with Mark. No one ever bothered to find out about how my brother would only communicate with me when he chose to communicate with me... how he would only talk to me when he felt it was time to talk to me. Mark's always been the most self-centered, egotistical jerk in the world. When he was busy on his ranch trying to find himself, like some 15-year-old goth chick, I was busy being one half of Playboys, Inc. I was busy selling out arenas, headlining PPV's and was busy risking my future ability to walk to prove to Eli Flair and the rest of the world that Troy Windham ain't no quitter. Mark returned when he knew *I* wasn't there. He saw that I was broken down, physically, and he saw a chance to return without the man who would most obviously be his top rival. Well, Mark-- you played your cards wrong, dogg. I'm back and I'm going to show you and the rest of the world that I'm at the top of my game. All the tools I've shown in my career... they're all coming together as we speak. And the minute you wrestle me, Mark, I'm going to take you apart, piece by piece, and I'm going to show you, once and for all, that I am the best wrestler on the planet... making me the best Windham on the planet.

(CUT TO: The black screen. The words "GXW" appear on the screen.)

TROY: I'm a multi-millionaire. It's no secret what I've been able to do in my five years in this sport-- I've been able to take my fame and my good looks and I've been able to parlay that into an acting career, into endorsement deals and it's been able to afford me a lifestyle that most men can only dream of. My home in this sport has been right here in the CSWA. This was the first league to sign me to a contract, to promote me as a star, to crown me as World Champion. Pro wrestling wouldn't be what it is without this promotion. I've had my disagreements with the league brass here before. But no matter what goes on in the offices down in Greensboro, this place is my home. And that's why it angers me to see a bunch of lowlifes from another league come in here. The New PLR? A bunch of guys who are going to prove, once again, that my best friend Eddy Love carried the originals to their greatness. Dan Ryan? Kendall Codeine? Some other guys from the GXW that I don't know? These guys couldn't DREAM of getting a CSWA contract unless they crashed the party first. The GXW is a low-rent promotion that can't make its own stars, that can't promote its own stars, that has to attack OUR champions and OUR roster to pop a rating. That personally offends me, being that I am the man who is the foundation of this promotion.

(CUT TO: The words WICKED SIGHT. CUT TO: Troy.)

TROY: That name personally eats away at me. Wicked Sight had every opportunity to make a dent right here in the CSWA. He had every opportunity to rise up and take what was his-- like I did, like Eddy Love did, like Eli Flair did, like Evan Aho did, like so many did before him. You can love us or you can hate us-- but all of us fought to be the best, we didn't try to ruin the league if we didn't have what it takes. But Sight, you did. You signed with the GXW forces all because you didn't think the CSWA fans or its roster respected you. Well, bra, you made the worst decision in your career. You wanted attention? Well, son, you've got my attention. You turned your back on the CSWA and turned your back on Mr. CSWA in the process. You step foot ANYWHERE in the same arena I'm in, Sight, and I'm going to see to it that you walk out with some injuries. You step foot in the ring with me ever, and I'm going to see to it that you don't walk out.

(CUT TO: The words "The Intruders." CUT TO: Troy.)

TROY: Another group looking to get over at the expense of those that built this league from the ground up. Craig Miles has been a wrestler for over 10 years-- and for over 10 years, he hasn't been able to make a dime, he hasn't been able to sell a poster. Eddie Mayfield USED to be the president of my fan club and is nothing more than a piece of enhancement talent who, for the first time in his career, has gotten some heat behind him. They'll show their true colors in no time when they crumble and collapse once someone... once Mr. CSWA... steps up to them and shows them for what they are-- a couple of jobbers who've spent a lot of their time watching The Best of Eddy and Troy tapes in an attempt to get over? And Guns? Little Guns? I beat him when he was still a force in this sport. Five years later, he's lost a step. He's lost a lot on his game. The only reason anyone knows who he is is because he has a knack for finding CSWA cameras and responding to every single mic-spot that mentions his name. But like his compadres, what he says is irrelevant and a pointless exercise. Because like his compadres, he can't back up what he says. When I face him, I'm going to do what I did the last time I beat him... and that's send him out of the sport for another half-decade.

(CUT TO: The words "Tom Adler." CUT TO: Troy.)

TROY: (Pauses) That's an interesting question you asked, Billy. The reason I mentioned his name is a reason. Like I said, I respect the man for what he's done. And I know that deep down inside he's seething at the fact that he's not a player in this sport any longer. He's seething at the fact that Eddie Mayfield used him as an ashtray and he sat back and took it. What I'm doing is providing him an opportunity. I'm a smart man, Billy. I know that while I'm the best wrestler alive today, while I'm the consumate showman... I'm just one man. The Intruders have four bodies. The GXW has countless numbers. And I am coming off my history of injuries. And I still want to get my title back and I still want to face my brother. So I know that I need people to help me out here. Tom Adler's a smart man. He's a great wrestler. He has a lot of tricks still up his sleeve. And I can give him the opportunity that he wants-- I can give him Mayfield, I can give him his prestige back. All he has to do is realize the opportunity I can bring him. But if he refuses me... or if he slaps me in the face just one more time, like he did the last time he opened his mouth... he's going to regret what he did after I bury him once and for all.

(CUT TO: Billy Buckley.)

BILLY: Very interesting comments from Troy Windham there. Folks, we'll be back later! (FTB) KRobinson — 08-27-02, 01:16 AM Fade In....

(an exert from Troy Windham's latest promo is shown on the screen)

"....The New PLR? A bunch of guys who are going to prove, once again, that my best friend Eddy Love carried the originals to their greatness. Dan Ryan? Kendall Codeine? Some other guys from the GXW that I don't know? These guys couldn't DREAM of getting a CSWA contract unless they crashed the party first. The GXW is a low-rent promotion that can't make its own stars, that can't promote its own stars, that has to attack OUR champions and OUR roster to pop a rating. That personally offends me, being that I am the man who is the foundation of this promotion."

(gradually, the image gives way to a GXW backdrop, which Kendall Codine stands before and prepares to address a few of Windham's comments)

Well, I must say that it is quite the pleasure to actually see the "almighty" Troy Windham show his face on national television. Its odd, because I had always heard so much about you, yet I never actually saw the evidence which would justify the words which were offered on your behalf....

Nonetheless, you are here....for now....and I would like to take this opportunity to offer a little history lesson. Much like GUNS, you enjoy making up events which are not quite factual. It seems that your money, fame, and good looks have given you everything except....

Intelligence....

You see Troy, intelligence is a wonderful thing. It would allow you to know that I have had a CSWA contract. In fact, over that two year span, I competed on a regular basis. The ironic thing is that during that time, I heard so much about Troy Windham, yet I never actually saw the physical evidence which would support the stories....

Overall, if you choose to address me in the future, then be sure that your facts, unlike your thoughts, are straight. The fabrication of events, actions, and so on may work in GUNS' world, but they won't in mine. And you can be certain, that each time you choose to be "Mister CSWA", living in the fantasy world that you have created for yourself, I will be there to correct your wrongs....

And from the looks of it, there will be plenty more of them to come....

(fade to black) GreggG — 08-27-02, 03:25 PM (CUT TO: Troy.)

TROY: Hey Kendall-- I just mentioned your name! How's it feel to be the most famous you've ever been in your life?

Now that I gave you some of my charisma-packed rub... let me get a little something off my chest for ya. Two years you were on this roster? It took you two whole years to figure out that you couldn't quite make it in the CSWA-- a league where only the cream rises to the top? Well, kid-- I'm glad you decided to make a name for yourself by becoming a top competitor in a league that would do *ANYTHING* to have a star of my magnitude on its roster so it could actually make payroll. But you decided to come back, and you came back with the invading forces-- and as the man who is the FOUNDATION of this league, that means you came back against me. FACT! You mentioned my name without my expressed, written permission. That means, Kodiac, you owe me something for this blatant violation of my intellectual property. FACT! I'll be taking my payback soon enough and you'll go scurrying back to whatever hole you dug yourself out of to begin with. And when that happens, pal... you'll see that this will go down on your permanent record! (FTB) KRobinson — 08-27-02, 05:18 PM Fade In....

(scene: as in the past, we find ourselves viewing Kendall Codine who stands before a GXW backdrop....upon hearing Troy Windham's latest words, Codine decides to address the situation further)

I guess this is the part where I'm supposed to apologize....

Or perhaps this is where I should accept the fact that I'm out of my league....

I assume this is where I should turn and run, thanking the lord that Troy Windham allowed me to pass without punishment....

(Codine hesitates for a moment, offering a slight smirk and shaking his head in a non-agreeing motion)

I don't think so Troy. I am, nor will I ever be, intimidated by your name because it is just that....a name....

There is no need for me to sit here and recollect about my past in the CSWA, because it will simply bypass that miniscule brain of yours and exit your head more times than you've entered and left this company. You choose to look down upon me and my past, then so be it. However, do not think for one moment that I will take what you have to say and not offer a response in return....

You see Troy, I am a man who chooses to stay with an organization longer than a week at a time. There will be no question as to where I am because I always remain active and in the forefront. Can the same be said for you?

(Codine smirks once again)

I think not....

Therefore, if you choose to make idle threats, with no intentions of capitalizing upon them, then I suggest that you shut your mouth at this current moment in time....

On the other hand, if you choose to stick around for perhaps a week or two....a record breaking feat on your behalf to say the least....then be sure to come and pay me a visit because I'm always more than willing to pay people back....

(Codine stares into the eye of the camera)

At least those who actually deserve it....

(fade to black) GreggG — 08-27-02, 05:43 PM (CUT TO: Troy.)

TROY: It's getting hard listening to chumps come out here to try and make a name for themselves at my expense. Kodiak, my man-- I can't help it that you have 1/100th of my star power. I can't help it that while you were busy wrestling dark matches at Fish Fund '98, I was busy on the set of 'A Sad Day In Brookville'-- the movie where I played the lead romantic role opposite Natalie Portman, who has posters of me on her wall while you are nothing more than a piece of garbage.

But if you knew your history like you said you did... you would know that whatever Troy Windham says is gospel. FACT! I said I'd be a bigger star than my older brother. I don't see HIM making movies. I don't see his face on billboards. FACT! I said I'd be a CSWA World Champion. And I was. I beat Guns, and used Hornet to do so in the process. I brought the CSWA from one era to next. I created the modern era of the CSWA. For that alone, you should come out here on national television and kiss my feet and thank me for the money I made you. I imagine that even wrestling in dark matches or our "C" level house shows pays a lot more than does the honor of being GXW champion. FACT! I said I'd be here to turn back the ClaimStakers. Look at what happened. Hornet's a non-entity. Mike Randalls is a non-entity. And me and Eddy Love, the dastardly duo known as Playboys, Inc... well, we're still so hot that you need SPF 30 just to be in our presence. FACT! I said I wouldn't quit in a match against Eli Flair. And guess what? I didn't. I stood with him, toe to toe, and took the most vicious beating in wrestling history. He beat me so bad, I was in the hospital that past three months. But did I say I quit? No, I did not.

So, Kendall Bear... before you decide to enroll in class, you'd better learn what goes on in the Windham School of Hard Knocks. You mess with Mister CSWA, you're going to get permanent marks against you... that will forever be placed on your permanent record! (FTB) KRobinson — 08-27-02, 11:35 PM Fade In....

(scene: beginning to become commonplace, we once again find ourselves viewing Kendall Codine who stands before a GXW backdrop....just as before, Codine takes this opportunity to address Troy Windham)

Every person in life has had their fair share of poor experiences with teachers....

Now it seems that I have finally had my own....

(Codine pauses for a moment in order to collect his thoughts)

Troy, I appreciate these facts that you are offering me, but obviously our perceptions of events and life in general are completely opposite. Nevertheless, it is always a pleasurable experience to partake in Troy Windham's history, as only he sees it....

Luckily for you Troy, all of that time spent in Los Angeles on movie sets, rather than in the ring, has helped you with one thing....

That one thing being the fact that you are able to act like a champion, rather than actually be one....

After all, the only thing that I've learned from the Windham School of Hard Knocks is that you enjoy clinging to the past, while at the same time acting like a wrestler....

My suggestion is that you actually put all of those years of acting to work and....

(Codine hesitates as he smirks and offers his final address)

Act like a consistent competitor here in the CSWA....

That is, if it isn't too much for your busy schedule....

(fade to black) GreggG — 08-28-02, 02:38 PM (CUT TO: Troy.)

Look, Kendall-- I don't know what drugs you're on, but they are certainly ones that I never took. They look like fun, tho.

The past year, when you were busy setting up the ring before your match in Topeka, Kansas, only to have to drive home in your 1987 Nissan Sentra, carrying the ring in your trunk, I was busy main eventing matches right here in the league that you oh-so-desperately want to be a part of again. When you were busy taking on The Gothic Wizard or some other sub-human, I was busy main eventing in the greatest series of matches in CSWA history-- matches that, for the most part, I left with my hand raised in victory. The only reason I've been gone is because I refused to say I Quit to the most violent man this sport has ever seen. So, before you say one more word about me, boy, I want you to check the FACTS! and I want you to do some reading, because right now you're coming across like an ignorant piece of trailer trash, complete with homemade tribal art tattoo. It ain't my fault that when you here I wasn't. Because if I *WAS* here when you were, well... you'd be even further down the card than you were to begin with. This is the last time I'm giving you any more free publicity. You mention my name one more time, I'll have you pinned by JJ DeVille. Fade In....

(scene: Kendall Codine stands before a GXW backdrop as he once again prepares to address Troy Windham....although it has become quite the familiar scene as of late, it still continues to serve the purpose)

I find it quite odd that you know so much about my life and my past, yet you continue to label me as a no name, a non factor, a nothing....

Are we being hypocritical again Troy?

(Codine pauses for a moment before continuing)

Windham, you can offer me any moniker that you would like, however, do not be mistaken that I speak the truth. Do I blame you for not being in the CSWA during my tenure? No, but I did make a certain point that you fail to acknowledge. That point being that I was in this organization for nearly two years, yet I have no recollection of your physical being. To me, Mister CSWA should be a man who has been with this company for a prolonged span of time....

Names such as....

Flair....

Hornet....

Powers....

Come to mind, when a label such as Mister CSWA is thrown around. A moniker that you use much too loosely....

To me, when the name Troy Windham is mentioned, I immediately come across terms such as....

Inflated ego....

One-sided point of view....

Long winded....

Ideas that fit you much better than Mister CSWA....

(Codine hesitates once more as he collects his thoughts before offering his final address)

Overall, I do not plan on being ousted from my current location any time soon. As far as I'm concerned, I have one more chance at finishing the business which I left behind. I'm growing too old and weary to be concerned with people who talk a big game, yet never want to support it within the squared circle. If you would like to be part of my business, then so be it because much like I told GUNS....

I will not sneak attack you....

I will not stalk you....

I will not avoid you....

If I want to deal with you, then I will come to your face and offer my grievances. So, for the time being, continue to talk Windham because I'm more than happy to oblige and feed that inflated ego of yours....

Because I'm willing to bet that your words are much like your life....

(Codine pauses and stares into the eye of the camera)

An act....
