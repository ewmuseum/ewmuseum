New Blood.....Indeed

27 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
AUMedina — 04-27-04, 12:25 AM (The camera fades in to a dimly lit wrestling gym. No one seems to be around in the place, no life shown whatsoever. Then, in the middle of the ring, sitting silently is a man with hair covering the view of his face. The camera catches the back of this man, as a short glimpse of a neon sign that reads "RingRats" is displayed on the far wall. The camera zooms in closer, showing the man with his hands out in the open, as he looks at them.)



???????: New blood.....new blood indeed. This isn't where I wanted to go. They wanted this. They want it, but they don't realize how foolish they wish for something they can never wish back....



(The camera pans around slowly to the front of the man, with the hair covering most of his face, except his lips, as he continues to look down at his hands, which seem to be stained with blood.)



???????: Is this it? Is this all that you have? You really believe chairshot....after chairshot....after chairshot is what will keep me down? That by breaking flesh and spilling my blood serves as a warning? A warning for whom? Me? What am I to be warned about? That even after 10 to 20 chairshots, and to be honest, I can't remember the count, will serve notice to everyone within the CSWA?



(The man starts laughing lightly, still keeping his gaze upon his hands.)



???????: How foolish these kids believe that because they've been praised by Mr. Merritt as one of his "new blood" wrestlers, they have the right to do what they please. Oh sweet ignorance.....how the young are so oblvious to the fact....



(The man finally brushes the hair from in front of his face. Wearing an black eyepatch over his right eye, with a symbol on the front in white, it becomes clear it is none other than Suicide. He weras a humble look across his face, even smiling faintly.)



Suicide: .....to the fact you've only dug yourself into a bigger hole than you were digging yourselves out. Mr. Sebastian, war you say? Are we bringing the other three seasons in to cause a "natural imbalance" to the supposed struggle between new blood and old guard? You actually believe someone like Mr. Merritt respects someone like yourself.....someone who can come into the ring and wield a chair like it was an extension of his libido? I beg to differ. Do you think you actually accomplished what you set out to do? The only thing you succeeded in Mr. Sebastian is starting the process of your downfall.



(Suicide raises his open palms to the camera, with the dried, stained blood on them.)



Suicide: This.....this is nothing to me. I've bled before and I will bleed again. The only thing good sir you've "shaken" up in the CSWA is the hourglass. Each grain of sand pinpoints the exact time you have left. Time left until what you may ask Mr. Sebastian? Until you meet me. I know who you are aligning yourself with Mr. Sebastian and if you believe that by doing this "deed" for "him" or even for yourself will put you in "his" or even Mr. Merritt's good graces....think again. New blood.....new blood indeed was spilt. But spilling my blood will be the first and last mistake you'll ever make against me. I think at the next On Time Mr. Sebastian.....you'll be my first. First what you ask? My first example in what everyone else will come to know.....



(Suicide lifts a hand towards his eyepatch. He grabs for it and pulls it off slowly. Suicide reveals a long, vertical scar over his right eye. The eye is not like that of his left, with a white eyeball and blue pupil, but a black eyeball with red pupil.)



Suicide: I take this sport seriously. And you Mr. Sebastian.....you just spit on everything I covet. On Time.....you.....me.....blood spilt.....pain felt.....'nuff said.....



(The camera fades out on Suicide's stern face.)
