CSWA Roleplays

Presidential Assassination

12–16 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Mad Dog — 07-12-03, 05:51 PM (FADE IN to highlights from Eddie Mayfield’s Presidential Challenge are shown. Mayfield is beating up a George Washington look-a-like and finally hitting the Screwjob piledriver for the win. Merritt appears on the big screen in the arena. He presents a real challenge for Mayfield in the form of Steel Viper. Viper charges to the ring as Mayfield and company scatter away.



The crowd lets out a roar of approval for the martial arts monster, Steel Viper. He pumps his fist in the air and the scene cuts away to Viper standing in front of a brick wall, somewhere in the bowels of the Arrowhead Pond. He wearing a CSWA t-shirt with the sleeves ripped off, showing off the tattoos that are all over his arms. He appears to be bigger and badder than he was before he left the CSWA. The former Presidential champion mean mugs the camera and begins to speak.)



STEEL VIPER: I’m sure some of you new fans are watching this and haven’t a clue of who I am. (Slightly nods his head.) That’s fine, I don’t expect you to. But you WILL know who I am and for those that already know me…its time we get reacquainted. I made my debut here in 1995, after already being successful in AAWC and the SWA, capturing titles in both. I broke in here the same time Bandit and Eli were getting started. Everyone thought I had a bright future here, that the sky was the limit. But I lost interest, I wanted to devote more time to training and fighting in mixed martial arts competitions. It also seemed to me that the booking committee here liked to take too many chances on matches…basically rolling the dice to see who would be matched up with who. That coupled with a contract from a, at the time, rival organization. It gave me a limited amount of days to wrestle, so I had plenty of time train in Muay Thai and Jeet Kune Do. Its safe to say there was NO ONE and still is NO ONE quite like me. I’m the baddest muthaf*cka on the face of the planet and I won’t hesitate to prove it to whoever doubts me!



(Vipers looks down at the floor, searching for what to say next. He looks back up and begins to speak.)



But the industry has changed. The Big Three are no more and now the CSWA is the only thing left standing from back then. The only federation that has been a constant in the wrestling community for as long as I can remember. I might have been ciritcal of the CSWA in the past and taken a few shots here and there…but it was only to get the attention of Mr. Merritt so he would sign me back. I feel I have a lot of unfinished business that needs to be taken care of…namely reclaiming something that belongs to me…the Presidential title.



Phony challengers, having your lackeys bail you out, calling yourself the President. (Shakes his head.) You have done just about anything and everything to run that title through the mud. A title whose history can be traced back to me. A title I helped build and make credible, you turn into a joke. Mayfield, you make me sick. I hate verything you are about and when I get the opportunity I am gonna TEAR YOU APART!



(Viper grits his teeth in anger as his blood begins to boil with the thought of Mayfield holding the Presidential title.)



You can’t run forever. There is nowhere to hide. Do the right thing and face me in the ring. Stop being a coward by wrestling non-title matches. Stop being a little b*tch and hiring bums off the street for squash matches. If you have any ounce of self-respect you will grow some balls and for once in your life…BE A MAN! I’m not the type of person you want on your bad side and you definitely don’t wanna piss me off!



(Viper cracks his knuckles and rolls his neck, in an attempt to calm down.)



I guess it’s too late for that. But it’s not too late for you to prove me wrong. It’s not to late for Eddie Mayfield to prove his worth…to me and to the eyes of the wrestling public. You think I’m wrong? You think you can do something about it? Then by all means DO IT! Step up and get in my face! SHUT ME UP! Because until you do…until you show me otherwise…I will make your life a LIVING HELL! Don’t like it? Tough…deal with it!



(Viper gives an evil look into the camera and runs his thumb across his throat. FADE TO BLACK) EastPrez — 07-12-03, 08:22 PM (FADEIN: To what looks like PRESIDENT MERRITT'S personal executive Skybox Suite, with the door kicked off the hinges! Sitting in one of the high back leather chairs, feet on the table, is 'Hot Property' EDDIE MAYFIELD, wearing his same deal: Dark suit with a blue buttondown shirt, loosened tie, and a Camel hanging off his bottom lip, his 30% tint PRADA shades on his face, smirking. Laying over the back of the chair are the CSWA PRESIDENTIAL championship belt, and BOTH Unified World Tagteam titles. Behind the chair stands BANDIT, same secret-service biker outfit on, holding his jaw and growling. MAYFIELD rests his hands behind his head, and sighs)



MAYFIELD: (Exhaling) "AHHHHHHH, It's GOOD TO BE KING. (Smiles) CSWA, a few nights ago you witnessed the first episode of EYE TEE VEE - Intruder TV, and MAN, you can't EVER call me a liar - I showed you ALL what I always call myself, MUST-SEE TV, and MAN, you saw what I was talking about. The Intruders came, we saw, we do what we ALWAYS do - we stirred the mutherf(BLEEEP!) drink so bad on iTV, the sh(BLEEEEP!) spilled all over the place. My first successful Presidential Title Defense against a tough competitor in George Washington... And yo - give it up for him, the old fella put up a hell of a fight. (Golf claps) Me and Craig Miles.. the PROFESSIONALS that we are... had a SuperPower style summit on Dan Ryan's face, and we even punked out Triple X-Lax for the Twofer... and... AND! Our main man Bill Cartwright - (Whispers) you may know him as GUNS, the Strongest Arms in the World... he's a close personal friend of mine.. and he introduced WRESTLETHON TWO OH OH THREE... and all in all, it was a GREAT night for the I's... (Looks up at BANDIT, who's running his tongue around in his cheek) Oh... right.. and Bandit got kicked in the head and pinned in the middle of the ring by Shane Southern... (Looks away) Well... three outta four ain't bad.



And oh, check THIS out? Chad Merritt tries to rain on YOUR Presidents parade... he tries to ruin MY ceremony by re-signing some nine foot mutant with taped feet who looks like he jumped out of a Street Fighter II machine... Steel Viper... He comes out here... and Merritt tries to use stroke to make me wrestle in a match that EYE DIDN'T PREPARE FOR... and put MY Presidency on the line? (Snorts) Well guess what Merritt - it takes more than that to get Steady Eddie to roll over. (Smiles) You gotta roll your 20-sided and get at least a 16 To Hit to get to Eddie Mayfield, and I don't think you've got the dice to do it. So let's examine this.



Steel Viper? Look, I know your deal. BIG HUGE man. Master of martial arts stuff I've only seen on Saturday Afternoon KungFu flicks. Master of Moogoo Gai Pan and sh(BLEEP!). Whatever. I'm not scared of you. You think you got MY number? You better check again, because James Earl Jones is gonna tell you you've got the wrong one. The President of the CSWA will not deal with terrorist demands and even a Weapon of Mass Destruction like yourself can't faze Eddie Mayfield on your BEST day. You don't like the way I handle myself in the ring? TOUGH! That's right. Lump it, pal. Because I know THIS much - you're not EVER gonna get your hands around THIS throat, and you're never... EVER gonna get THIS Presidential title... (Points to one of the titles, then looks up, and moves his hand to the right title belt) sh(BLEEEEP) man... I got so many belts, I don't even know which one to motion to... (Smirks) Whatever. Point being - you ain't getting it. ANY of 'em.



Steel Viper? You can huff and puff and make all the noise you wanna, but you're not giving me ANY worry. I got a match with Cameron Cruise-Missle at ON TIME, and after that... if you wanna see me about something? I think you'll need to speak to Mike McGee before you get an appointment at the oval office. You still have some paperwork to fill out if I'm not mistaken... (Smirks)



See you in the funnypapers, Viper. Good Night... AND GOD BLESS."



(FADEOUT as Eddie gives the 'Victory' signs and smiles like an ass. BANDIT rubs his jaw and growls!) Mad Dog — 07-16-03, 09:20 PM (FADE IN to what appears to be a dungeon, location unknown. The camera pans it’s surrounding as an old dilapidated wall, with torches shedding partial light around the room is seen. Off in the distance a figure can be seen throwing kicks into a hanging punching bag, rocking the heavy bag back and forth with each blow. The figure slowly works into a few combos before stopping for a breather. The camera zooms in on the punching bag that has a tattered and torn 8 x 10 glossy of Eddie Mayfield on it. The figure stands back into the shot, with his back to the camera, as it stays focused on the ragged and frayed photograph. The figure begins to speak in a deep raspy voice, it is Steel Viper.)



STEEL VIPER: Everyone nowadays wants to be a comedian. Steady Eddie is on the fast track to making his dreams come true. He is one step closer to making his comedy career a permanent one. Because when I do get my hands on you Eddie and I put you through a living hell like you’ve never seen before…then you can go all out and set your sites on the world of stand-up. Because as far as I’m concerned you’re gonna need a NEW CAREER to fall back on once I cripple you and send you packin’ from this one!



(Viper throws a Muay Thai roundkick at the punching back, destroying the already damaged picture of Mayfield. Viper leans down and picks up the torn pieces and crumbles them into his hand and tosses it aside. He turns to face the camera, fuming in anger.)



This isn’t a game, Eddie, and as much as you would like to think I’m straight out of Street Fighter II…I don’t wear an eye-patch and I don’t do helicopter kicks. Unlike a video game…I HIT BACK! You can’t plop in a quarter for an extra man…because once this life is up, that’s all you get…and I’m just the man to TAKE IT AWAY! You’re chillin’ on the top with the high score…but I’m about to bump you down and put MY NAME up there! You wanna refer to me as a video game? The closest thing out there is Pac-man…because all I do is gobble up everything in my path. You’re no different, Mayfield and as soon as you get Bar Mitzvahed and BECOME A MAN…you will find that out.



(Viper rocks his neck around, popping it slowly. He rolls his shoulders and snorts, breathing deeply through his nose. All with a mean scowl on his face and an intense look in his eyes.)



Do something, Mayfield. Stop being a pansy and step up to the challenge. Quit making excuses and wrestling guys that don’t deserve a shot. Step the f*ck up…to get knocked the f*ck out! You might as well take this ass-whippin’ now…because the longer you make me wait…the severity escalates! I’m not the type to sit on the sidelines and tolerate your antics for very long. So enjoy your playtime with your flunkies and soak it in for all its worth…because soon school is gonna be in session…and I’ll be teaching you a LESSON IN PAIN! Go run and do your homework, Eddie, because you’re gonna need it!



(Viper mean mugs the camera as he breathes heavily through his nose.FADE TO BLACK)
