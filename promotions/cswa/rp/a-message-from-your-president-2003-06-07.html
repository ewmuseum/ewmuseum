A Message From Your President

7 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
EastPrez — 06-07-03, 12:01 PM (FADEIN: To an alley somewhere, in front of a cyclone fence, which is surrounding an old basketball court, where the rims were ripped down ages ago, and weeds spurt from the asphalt. Suddenly, someone throws a tarp over the fence, and drapes it down the front, to show a decently-airbrushed copy of the United States Presidential Seal, and the CSWA logo in graffitti block letters underneath the seal! In a beat, we see the huge BANDIT come on-screen, carrying a podium, and he places it square in the middle of the shot, and stands next to it, having folded his hands in front of himself, looking left to right. BANDIT is around Six-foot-ten, monstrous and looks like he's been in his share of biker bars, but has that look of a biker who shaved, and cleaned his act up, but still looks visibly out-of-place. Best description: Primetime Brian Lee, Version 7 Big Evil-Deadman Undertaker, Inc. with a suit on. Next to him comes SHAMON, whos wearing Y2K Michael Jackson livery - looking like a Brass band leader, with a red coat with tails, shiny pants and a surgical mask over his face and a straw boosters hat, carrying a boombox with one hand, and holding up a newspaper with the obviously fake headline on the front: "EDDIE WINS! EDDIE WINS!" in the other)



(CUEUP: (From the boombox) "Hail to the Chief", as 'Hot Property' EDDIE MAYFIELD makes his way onto the shot, wearing the same suit we saw him in in his last promo. He has the CSWA PRESIDENTIAL CHAMPIONSHIP BELT cradled in the crook of his arm, and both UNIFIED World Tagteam belts hanging off of one shoulder. He takes the tag belts and hands them over to BANDIT, who slaps them on either of his huge shoulders. There's a lot of gold in the place, and MAYFIELD smirks knowingly as he props the PRESIDENTIAL TITLE on the podium next to him, SHAMON throwing down the newspaper to work the boombox)



MAYFIELD: "Thank You, Thank YOU! (Points at the camera) for your votes, because now... WE DID IT! (Canned applause from the boombox) WE DID IT FOR YOU! And now... Heh... Eddie Mayfield is now the PRESIDENT of the CSWA. (SHAMON jumps up and down like an idiot!) You see, what went down at BATTLE OF THE BELTS was like having a pity f(BLEEEP!) with some chick just because you're drunk and bored, and there's nothing better to do... it's GOOD for what it is - but yunno... it's really not that satisfying. And that's just what our match at BOB was - unsatisfying. It's at least not as bad as blue balls... because I did get SOME release... I picked up another belt, and I cut that rattail off of Shane Southern, and just this morning, sent it on it's way to the lucky winner of my eBay Auction. (Smiles) Got a pretty penny for that thing, too - maybe whoever won it will work a root on you, Shane - because I know all of you Louisianiananaans are into voodoo and sh(BLEEP!) - I'm sure that's not sitting well with you. Well guess what? You can draw X'es on my picture and throw pennies all day, but I did that just to spite you. Heh. Felt good to me. The Intruders GOT you, Shane, but we didn't GET YOU, GET YOU. You're still walking. And for that, I'm a little upset, but I'll sleep through the night. And lookit you now, Shane, you got NOTHING, but a chalk outline around your midsection in the shape of the United States title. Trust me, If EYE were you (And thank God I'm not) I'd be REAL pissy right now, because you got clipped in our match together, and you got STRIPPED in the next match (Smiles wide. MAYFIELD takes out a Camel, and BANDIT is over in a flash with a light, which scares SHAMON! He backs up, eyeing the flame nervously as MAYFIELD accepts graciously, gets off some test puffs, then blows a jet of smoke out the corner of his mouth)



So let's talk about some things. First things first - congratulations, Tom (SHAMON golf-claps) - you've officially gotten in a time warp and pulled out the time honored AdlerScrew - Yunno, lose without REALLY losing, so you could win. Hard to debate, makes you look like a champ, and it's all water under the bridge, right? (smiles) Well, yunno me and you have crazy history, and no matter how you'll try to spin this one, to me, you looked like a straight-up PU(BLEEP!) because you basically dropped that belt because you didn't wanna get in that ring with Eddie Mayfield, and get outCLASSED by a PROFESSIONAL. I'll give you your propers, Tom - now you've gotten Shanes US title, and your piggybacking is as crisp as it ever was - but know, my man, JUST KNOW - that me and you are gonna be hooking up, sooner, than later. I got a knot on the back of my head from you and your mutant Lost Boys that run with you, and I ain't forgot about that. Pinning some idiot reporter is some bullsh(BLEEEEP!) but I did what I had to do, as much as I wanted it to be YOU I knocked seneless, and now this belt is mine, just like every other belt I own - it ain't going nowhere. That US belt is a mockery of MY belt, Tom - (Looks down at it, lovingly) the PRESIDENTIAL title, means that I am the PRESIDENT of this company, and Merritt hasn't figured that out yet - but we'll be talking soon enough, and when we do - man, there's gonna be some changes around here (Smirks).



I'm not satisfied. yeah, with more gold than an arab jewelry store on 125th street, more gold than Fort Knox, and I'm still unsatisfied. Sh(BLEEP!) didn't go down EXACTLY as I had planned at BOB, and as YOUR President, I plan on righting those wrongs, and fixing the broke things. I... Hot Property Eddie Mayfield... UNIFIED World Tagteam Champion, and YOUR PRESIDENT... decrees it. Good Night, America, and God Bless."



(BOOMBOX: "Hail to the Chief!" MAYFIELD looks down, and slowly raises both fists, and slowly raises two 'peace' signs, just like President Nixon used to do. SHAMON starts screaming like a banshee, and launches hands-full of Red white and blue confetti into the air! with the confetti floating in the background, the camera closes in on MAYFIELD, as he brings his head back up, with a devil's glare in his eyes! FADEOUT)
