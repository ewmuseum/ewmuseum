Burning in the Ashes (Birth)

11–30 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Off to Valinor — 09-11-03, 08:28 PM V/O: “The reddest of lights shine up on you young man, God be with you.”



(cueup: ‘How Much For your Wings’ – The Black Crowes)



“How much for your wings?”



(FADEIN: An overhead shot from the ceiling of a modest motel bathroom. Lying facefirst, arms and legs stretched out is MIKE RANDALLS, unconscious. He’s strewn across a tile floor covered in broken glass, shards of a wall-length mirror and a crimson red pool of blood slowly flowing more freely towards the door…)



”How much for your wings?”



(CLOSEUP: RANDALLS eyes shoot open…)



”Well it comes and it goes…”



(RANDALLS starts quickly sucking in air…)



”How much for your wings?”



(RANDALLS body jerks inward as he takes a desperate inhale…)



“And it's high and low…”



(RANDALLS slowly exhales, his eyes glazing over…blood starts seeping out from his mouth…)



”How much for your wings?”



(RANDALLS eyes slowly close, a slight smile forms on his lips…he stops breathing.)



“I can name a price…”



(CUTTO: Back to the overhead shot of RANDALLS, the white tile floor becoming a river of blood…)



”How much for your wings?”



V/O: “Sir, we were notified of some screaming followed by a crash around 20 minutes ago – I just wanted to make sure everything is all right in here…”



”Many forget their pride…”



(A motel manager walks into the doorway – wearing a white collared shirt, navy necktie, khakis, brown loafers and holding a clipboard…”



MANAGER: “Jesus, lord in Heaven…”



(The MANAGER runs out of the room, screaming “CALL 9-11! CALL 9-11!”)



”If I were you and and you were me…”



(CUTTO: Inside the back of an ambulance – RANDALLS is on a gurney, his arms heavily bandaged – he’s still unconscious. Two EMT’s are working vigoursly to keep him alive…)



EMT: “He should’ve been dead by now, this is crazy.”



“We'd know…”



EMT2: “Don’t let up your pressure – we’ve got to stop the bleeding!”



“And could it be what you perceive…”



EMT: “He’s not gonna make it!”



“Is wrong?”



EMT2: (looking down at RANDALLS) “We’re almost there buddy, keep fighting.”



“And what I mean what wrong could be…”



(CUTTO: The ambulance pulling up to the ER, the EMTs hop out and pull RANDALLS out and run into the hospital…)



”I don't know…”



(CUTTO: The EMT’s and a Doctor wheeling him into surgery…)



DOCTOR: “How long do you think he was lying there?”



“I'm not naïve but I believe…”



EMT: “At least 30-45 minutes.”



”So I may grow…”



DOCTOR: “That’s impossible.”



“How much for your wings?”



(SFX: The heart monitor starts beeping faster…)



DOCTOR: “Don’t give up on me now! Hold those bandages tight he’s going into cardiac arrest!”



“The soul you kiss good-bye…”



(SFX: The heart monitor’s beeps increase in speed…then goes flat, the DOCTOR goes into full CPR mode…)



“How much for your wings?”



DOCTOR: (while pumping RANDALLS’ chest) “You can make it – fight like you haven’t before!”



“Watch the stars collide…”



(CUTTO: An astronomical scene of star clusters, nebula systems, unknown planets. The scene shifts as if the viewer has gone into some sort of warp speed, causing spectrums of colors to be the only visible thing flying past. As the speed increases – the scene gradually turns into a blinding white light then blackness…)



V/O: “Michael, my son. Come to me…”



(A faint distant light fades onto the screen…)



V/O: “Yes, yes – Michael. Leave the darkness, behind and you will find me.”



RANDALLS: (V/O) “I…I can’t.”



V/O: “Michael, let yourself go from the pain you hold deep inside. The darkness no longer is apart of you, you are free.”



(The faint light gets brighter)



RANDALLS: (V/O) “Where am I? Who are you?”



V/O: “I cannot answer your first question, for I do not know myself. But fear not, I am not here to hurt you for I am your Father…”



(CUTTO: A stoic elder man stands by a creek in a white robe – he’s very tan, somewhat tall, athletic for his age and has a salt and pepper beard…he turns to face ‘The Wolf’ Mike Randalls – wearing a grey cloak…a meadow valley amidst a sunny day encompassing them.”



RANDALLS: (looking around in awe) “This cannot be, this is impossible.”



DAVID: “Did you not think you wouldn’t accomplish your goal?”



RANDALLS: (now looking at his father)“You mean…this isn’t a dream?”



DAVID: “This isn’t death, if that’s what you are asking. This isn’t life either, as you can tell.”



RANDALLS: (while looking at his clothes) “Then what is this? Why should I believe you?”



DAVID: “Because you have nobody else to believe in, not even yourself anymore. You are here because you are given a choice, Michael…if you still linger in your anger, then that right will be taken away from you.”



RANDALLS: “What right?”



DAVID: “To live, Michael. To breathe the air, once again…”



(FADEOUT.



CUTTO: A hand writing a letter…the camera pans up the arm, a crimson, jagged branch-like scar the focus – as the camera pans away, we see ‘The Wolf’ Mike Randalls looking down at his paper – he’s sitting in t-shirt and jeans by a candlelit desk. His voice speaks as he writes…)



“To those I watch from exile,



I am not writing this letter to use the recent events of all your lives as a hope that I can be forgiven for what I’ve done in the time you’ve known me. I am not writing this letter to relate to you in hopes of gaining your friendships back – for I never truly deserved them in the first place.”



“I am writing an explanation.”



“An explanation of who I was, where I’ve been and why I’m back. To the three of you, I owe this.”



“Understand that what I write isn’t attempt to gain your sympathies. This isn’t some way to feel your pity on the life I’ve had and what I’ve become…everything I’ve done in the past I’ve done by choice – nothing more, nothing less. Don’t mistake this as a plea to join my crusade – it is only insight to how it was conceived.”



“I just want closure from a world and thought process that I must leave behind in order to accomplish the one goal I have for the rest of my life. You were all part of that world for many, many years – and some scars you may hide to this day were caused by my wrongdoings and blind philosophies.”



“What I will say here cannot be repeated, not only for the safety of myself – but also for yourselves as I have learned a great deal of my legacy and my roots…and the dangers they possess.”



“I only wish that with what I write you all will find hope in the hours of darkness that will plague you for the months to come…for if my life can find its destined path, you won’t be afraid to find yours…”



(FADEOUT as RANDALLS starts writing about his father, his death, his stepfather, James Maurer...and his time of death at 6:54 PM on a night filled with thunderstorms…) Off to Valinor — 09-17-03, 08:35 PM (FADEIN: ‘The Wolf’ Mike Randalls sitting at his desk, his green eyes flickering in the candlelight. His head, tilted in thought and his pen resting near his lips.)



(cueup: ‘Parasite’ – Nick Drake)



RANDALLS: (while writing, V/O) “In my last hour of vengeance and anger, I renounced whatever was taking ahold of me. Deep in the darkness, I had vowed long ago to never let go of my anger nor my hatred, for it was what I would need to survive. They would not come for me, I would come for them. Darkness consumed me as laid there on the tile floor, not even willing to let my last breath of hatred go.”



“And I faded. It was as if one second on Earth was one million years in my mind.”



“Perhaps you have been there, Ivy. I do not know for I have not spoken to you since a couple of days before Coop’s. Ironic it would seem that within days of explaining the remedial tasks of a copier to me, something like this would happen. I have not heard from you, but I do understand what you feel…”



“For where I was, I do not know. But right in front of my eyes before me, cloaked in grey, stood my father. And to think, this was a man I had never known.”



“It’s much like what I said to Elijah in a postcard I sent him.”



“Faith is just the beginning.”



“He still lives in Greece, right? I gave him the address of a healer in the mountains of New Mexico. He’s a great Uncle of mine. I found some notes on him in my father’s box…”



“…that cursed box. Pandora without a top.”



“My faith as I had known it had been broken. Everything I had trusted had failed me. I sat silent for what felt like hours…cursing myself and cursing the existence I had failed to end.”



(CUTTO: RANDALLS sitting in front of a creek with clear blue water. His cloak lies by his side, RANDALLS now is in regular clothes – crimson t-shirt, jeans and barefoot. His father, DAVID, watches quietly being cautious of the moment in which they find themselves. DAVID takes off his cloak, he’s wearing normal clothes – an open collard shortsleeve shirt, jeans and leather cowboy boots. His hair is jet black, his complexion a dark tan. He’s in his mid-40's with the physique of a athletic man back in the 1950’s – 1960’s. Not as ‘cut’ as today.)



DAVID: “If you wish to sit silently here forever, you will. You are not in control any longer.”



RANDALLS: “Shutup.”



(DAVID cocks his head, a little taken aback)



RANDALLS: “You think I haven’t realized that?”



DAVID: “I was just trying to get you to talk.”



RANDALLS: “Congratulations, you win.”



DAVID: “Do you want me to leave? You spent so much time fu(BLEEP!)in’ time trying to find me.”



(RANDALLS’ head finally turns around, a “do you know who you’re talking too?” look on his face.)



DAVID: “You heard me.”



(RANDALLS just nods his head in disbelief…he puts his head in his hands in frustration)



RANDALLS: (slightly muffled) “WHY?! (he raises his head out exasperated) Why with you of all people?”



DAVID: “Maybe I’m the only one you’ll listen to.”



RANDALLS: (almost laughing) “Now WHY in the hell would I want to listen to you?”



DAVID: “Maybe somebody or something wants you to be saved?”



(RANDALLS smirk drops like an anvil – he coldly stares…)



RANDALLS: “Don’t mock me.”



DAVID: (sitting down next to RANDALLS, trying to keep eye contact) “I’ve been waiting to do this for longer than you could possibly imagine, Michael.”



RANDALLS: (looking away, trying to hide any emotion) “Well, its too late.”



DAVID: (smirking) “There is no amount of time here, Michael – if this is to be our existence, I will spend all of it trying to help you see that you can be saved.”



RANDALLS: (rolling his eyes) “I shouldn’t even be here.”



DAVID: (spreading his arms to point to the surroundings, smiling) “Here? How do you even know you’re ‘here’ at all? Don’t you see, Michael. You are being given a choice.”



RANDALLS: “Maybe I didn’t want one.”



DAVID: “You’re just saying that because you never had one.”



RANDALLS: “I never needed one.”



(DAVID sighs and lowers his head, he stands up – RANDALLS doesn’t budge.)



DAVID: (while pacing) “You read the box, Michael. I know you did.”



RANDALLS: “So what?”



DAVID: (stops and looks in RANDALLS’ direction) “Well, don’t you want to know?”



RANDALLS (looking over curiously): “Know what?”



DAVID: “What it felt like.”



RANDALLS: “How WHAT felt like?”



DAVID: “The very poison that’s been affecting your mind.”



(RANDALLS stands up and walks over to a tree – he wants to avoid this topic)



RANDALLS: “It’s not poison.”



DAVID: “Oh, what’s inside of us isn’t poison. I know that, you just don’t understand that. See Michael, there’s something very considerable you must understand about that box and what you hadn’t been able to read. My deposition on how I escaped stated that I killed one man, my jailor.”



RANDALLS: “I’m REAL proud.”



DAVID: “I killed thirteen other men that night, Michael. All thirteen of them were sleeping.”



(CUTTO: RANDALLS’ face – his father standing behind him, RANDALLS’ eyes close…)



DAVID: “You do not understand what you carry inside, but before you renounce your choice I ask you to hear what I’ve repented all my life silently with nobody knowing, but James Maurer.”



(CUTTO: RANDALLS’ eyes open, he turns around coldly staring at his father.)



RANDALLS: "Go on."



DAVID: “I don’t know how they tested me, it would just happen. When you’re starving, you don’t care what they feed you. Sometimes, I’d wake up still in the jail. Sometimes, on a cold steel table of some makeshift laboratory in the jungle. I don’t know what they gave me, but it made me stronger, faster, greater stamina. They’d put bets on me in fights, knowing with my training and the testing combined – entertainment would be a certain man’s injury against me. Hours afterwards, even once a whole 24, I’d lie in my cell – this stuff coursing through my veins, vowing upon my escape – I would kill each and every man in that camp.”



“Every time I came back from the lab, the longer it would take to leave my system. Until it wouldn’t leave, until it became a part of me.”



(RANDALLS shaking his head in disbelief…)



RANDALLS: “You’re lying…”



DAVID: “No, I’m not. This is WHAT happened, this is a part of who you are. I can see in your eyes that you know where this story goes for you have lived it since you were born. For months, they kept doing the tests – but I would lie to them about the effects wearing off and they would increase the dosage. It never made me more powerful from when it stayed, but I feared for my life if they knew to erase the evidence, they would have to kill me. Luckily, I went undetected.”



(RANDALLS sits down, a little in shock at the story thus far…)



DAVID: (bows his head, slightly in shame) “I told James what was happening. We came up with an escape plan. The jailer we killed that night was found…the other thirteen men were not.”



(RANDALLS looks up curiously, DAVID looks away shamefully)



“I couldn’t stop, I couldn’t control what was inside of me. I found the barracks, before James had known to where I had escaped – I had slit all their throats and James found me standing in front of their funeral pyre burning…”



(DAVID looks at RANDALLS, sadly)



“When they found us, I was a wreck. It took me years to reverse all the anger I had felt towards those men. But I had friends, Michael. But I had FAMILY to fall back on. I had a father there for me, all that could comfort you in the nights was the darkness.”



(RANDALLS bows his head down…)



“Once I had reversed the tides, once what was sown inside of me had been purged – I felt I had a gift. I got involved in the very sport, you did. James and I were quite successful. But I became arrogant, Michael. Very, very arrogant.”



“For that, I angered dangerous men and was taken from you. If I had known, that my gift would become your curse…I was just so young and naive.”



(DAVID’s eyes start tearing, he pauses)



“Every moment of eternity, I blame my mistakes for the person you have become. You were left with nothing, Michael. You were cursed and its MY fault.”



RANDALLS: (looking up) “Its not your fault. I could’ve stopped…I just never wanted to.”



(DAVID lets out a slight smile…)



DAVID: “Well, do you want to? Now that you know the truth?”



RANDALLS: “I—I—(RANDALLS looks down) I don’t know.”



(DAVID kneels beside him)



DAVID: “But you can forgive me?”



RANDALLS: (looks up, and shakes his head almost against his will) “Yes…yes, I can forgive you.”



DAVID: (relieved and smiling) “Then, perhaps you are strong enough to do what it takes to forgive YOURSELF…”





(CUTTO: Back to RANDALLS, writing the letter…)



“The Walls were crumbling.”



“You must understand that in the darkness, one is blind.”



“But now I could see.”



“Only the end was nowhere in sight.”



“Indeed, faith is just the beginning.”



“I believed in my father. But could I believe in myself?”



(FTB) Off to Valinor — 09-30-03, 05:25 PM (FADEIN: RANDALLS, writing the letter at his desk.)



"What I had dearly loved, exposed me as nothing more than a fraud. I had bestowed my trust in a lie. All my beliefs, my faith they held no water no longer. They were as empty as my heart, as barren as my soul. Everything that I had fought for, everything that I had sacrificed for - it was all in deceit, it was all in blindness. I was a FOOL."



"I knew no love. I felt no pain."



"I had achieved the goal promised to me - a life of misery, malcontent and apathy. I did not care who I would know, who I would hurt or the consequences of my actions. What was given to me shall be given to them, if it means the sacrifice of my own self."



"And why? Because I believed the lies of my mother. Because I believed the lies of my stepfather."



"In youth there is innocence they say, in my youth there was ignorance. And the path it led me to was the realization that for twenty years, all that fed my hunger was in fact poison."



"I had reached my crossroad and my father asked me to walk a different path...to face the consequences, to right what was wrong."



"How could he? For twenty years, I was a walking, living, breathing lie. I dared not turn back from the direction I had now chosen. Years ago, in the face of God - I would laugh. Now, in the face of all that's true I could not even answer...for I had no place to stand."



"And for the first time in my life, I had become fearful of what I had become..."



(FADEIN: 'The Wolf' Mike Randalls standing by the creek, the water is turning darker. In the distance, a dark grey/black smoke was covering the sky. A wind starts blowing...)



RANDALLS: "It's getting colder...darker here."



(CUTTO: Randalls' father DAVID is standing several feet away, his cloak back on. He's looking off into the distance as well.)



DAVID: "You were blind, Michael. Soon, you will see where you dwelt and what you saw. My time almost draws near, my son. As does your choice and with that, your destiny."



RANDALLS: "I am worth no more than a dead man."



DAVID: "Perhaps. You may already be dead, Michael - that may not be the choice given to you in the end. It is not that easy, there are other trials..."



RANDALLS: "There is no time for me to unlearn what I have now learned. It is too late. All I still feel is anger - all I've found out is that I was betrayed once more in my life. And you ask me to fight for that?"



DAVID: "All I ask is that you not deny this opportunity out of your own selfish ego. Our curse has done much wrong in this world, our poisoned blood has stained good men's hands. Why do you fear setting this straight? Do you fear the consequences of admitting that what you fought for was a lie? You'd rather deny yourself salvation than admit you were wrong?"



RANDALLS: "I was wrong, but I still hate those that wronged me. And they are the ones that spawned my hunger from the start."



DAVID: "Yes, but now that you know the start to this...Michael, you can FINISH it."



RANDALLS: "It is too late for me...I'm sorry."



(RANDALLS bows his head as the water has now dried up, the creek is nothing more than mud. The billowing grey/black smoke - like a thick mist or fog rising over the hills steadily is coming closer, no more than 10 miles away.)



RANDALLS: "What is happening?"



(DAVID paces forward and stands next to his son...)



DAVID: "The darkness is coming, Michael. You should realize that by now, all that you believed will now be your trial."



RANDALLS: "What do you mean?"



DAVID: "In your anger, you were blind. Now it is time to see what you believed and if you're willing to accept it."



RANDALLS: "Where will you go?"



DAVID: "I do not know. For my existence has led me here, do not think that you are the only one with an unseen path...with questions unanswered before your time expired. For the decisions I made in my life, I am here for a reason."



RANDALLS: "You hardly took the actions I have taken, why would you suffer?"



DAVID: "Look at yourself, Michael. Then look inside yourself and see what you would be if I were there to protect you from all those that deceived you. From all those that lied to you. From all those that made you...hate."



(RANDALLS stands up...his head bowed towards the ground)



DAVID: "Ego, Vanity - no sin is greater than another when its the cause of such destruction and malice. It could be a curse that was given to us, Michael - but you have one last chance to make it a gift."



RANDALLS: (looking up slightly angry) "Are you here for myself or yourself?"



DAVID: "I'm here for you Michael. I've not been sent to hell, if that's what you think...I am not here to save myself...nor do I know if such a place even exists. I am here to give you the truth and it is your choice what to do with it. Whether you continue to hate when the darkness surrounds you will be your choice. If that is your dependence, if that is your faith - so be it. Then you will still be the fool and you will be defeated forever."



RANDALLS: "I am not the man you think I can be...I keep telling you, its too late."



DAVID: "What are you afraid of?"



RANDALLS: "I am NOT afraid."



(SFX: THUNDER! Dark clouds start forming above RANDALLS and his father. The wind starts blowing stronger - both men look up and then at each other.)



DAVID: "You are afraid that you cannot survive without your hate. You are dependent upon it. I am telling you, in our last moments together - that if you see your anger for what it was, if you see your soul for what it can truly be...then you will be more powerful than ever before."



(RANDALLS looks to the west, where the grey/black fog has almost reached the creek. When he turns around, his father is gone.)



RANDALLS: "Father?"



DAVID (V/O): "I must leave you Michael."



RANDALLS: "Why?"



DAVID (V/O): "It is my time. Your trials await you. Your choice must be made with no influence from anybody, but yourself."



RANDALLS: "What trials?"



DAVID (V/O): "You will either accept what you have become, beg for mercy at what you believe - or choose to fight all that was told to you, defeat all that you fear and all that you were. Then and only then, will you be strong enough to face what you fear - LIFE."



RANDALLS: "What if I don't know what choice I want to make? What if I don't want to make a choice any longer?"



(...SILENCE...)



RANDALLS: "Father?"



(SFX: THUNDER! The fog moves across the creek, RANDALLS stands for a moment and looks east to where a black, jagged mountain stands. He grabs his cloak and puts it on and starts running towards the mountain.



CUTTO: RANDALLS, writing the letter.)



"I feared what awaited me. I dared not face these trials with such doubt in my mind. I knew of no way I should turn, for I did not want to move. But the darkness had come, all that I had wished to die in was approaching - just at the moment I had realized my life was only a lie."



"To find what you seek when you least want it, this was the first trial. And my first choice was to run."



"As I ran towards the mountain - the darkness chasing me, I saw the fog approaching from the other side. I saw the mountain before me and made my second choice - to climb. Slowly, I climbed...slowly the fog rose. I reached the top, a flat surface - no bigger than a 15x15 room. The fog slowly rose, mocking me - the thunder crackled, laughing at me..."



"I had nowhere to no, I had nowhere to run."



"There was no choice, but to make a choice..."



(CUTTO: 'The Wolf' MIKE RANDALLS standing on the top of a plateau-like mountain, the surface black crumbled ashes and granite. RANDALLS is beaded in sweat, dirty from the climb. He throws his cloak off and paces to each side, watching the black fog slowly rise..."



"When I claimed to be lord of the darkness, I was blind to what I had surrounded myself in. Now that the blindfold had been lifted, I would see all that I used to wish for...finally in frustration, I screamed to the sky above..."



RANDALLS: "WHAT DO YOU WANT WITH ME!?!!?!"



(SFX: THUNDER! Lightning flashes across the sky...)



"The hour was approaching, I could feel the trial drawing near. The fear grew inside of me, the questions piled on top of one another..."



(SFX: THUNDER! Lightning flashes again, it starts raining. RANDALLS looks up as it starts raining heavily, the wind now very strong...and now howling...)



"I began to feel a presence quite familiar...Someone was watching me, yet I did not know who it was...a presence I had no strength to fight..."



RANDALLS: "Who are you!?!? Why do you mock me!?!?"



(SFX: THUNDER!)



"For twenty years, my anger had grown to infinite proportions. My hate had unequaled strength. For twenty years, I believed nothing could stop the path laid before me...no man, woman or child would ever derail my destiny..."



(CUTTO: RANDALLS on the mountaintop, soaked in the torrential downpour, the howling winds lifting his hair into the air...the fog has now risen over each side of the mountain circling the top - nothing visible except the mountaintop, lightning, rain and the thick black fog.)



"The trial must come, I could wait no longer..."



RANDALLS (over Thunder!): "SHOW YOURSELF! SHOW YOURSELF TO ME!"



"And then he walked out of the fog...the one I did not expect...the one I feared to face."



(cueup: 'Nobody's Fault But Mine - Led Zeppelin)



(CUTTO: A much, different looking 'The Wolf' Mike Randalls - this one is stalking out of the fog, holding two long, wooden staffs. His face is painted all black with white streaks under his eyes, 3 white circles on his nose and white around his mouth. He's wearing no shirt...black drawstring pants. CUTTO: RANDALLS backing up in shock...)



"I was to either survive or die at the hands of my very soul of anger...my very essence of hate..."



(The 'black-painted' Randalls tosses a staff to the normal RANDALLS who catches it...the 'black-painted' one smiles evily - Lightning flashes above, his eyes reflecting the energy...)



"..and it was Nobody's Fault But Mine."



(As the song kicks into its first verse, the two RANDALLS' stand several feet from each other - the 'dark' Randalls starts methodically walking towards RANDALLS who is backing away and all of a the 'dark' one sudden swings his staff, which RANDALLS blocks then FADE TO BLACK.)



"Nobody's fault but mine,

Nobody's fault but mine.

Trying to save my soul tonight,

It's nobody's fault but mine."
