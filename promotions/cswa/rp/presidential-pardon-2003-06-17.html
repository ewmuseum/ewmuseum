Presidential Pardon

17 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
EastPrez — 06-17-03, 02:05 PM (FADEIN: To a Belt-holding President 'Hot Property' EDDIE MAYFIELD, who's standing in front of another Presidential Seal logo, now, this one has a black and white photo of his face in the middle of the oval, holding his chin with one hand while maintaing a cheesy smile, eyes glancing at something we don't see. Suddenly, The big, bad outlaw, the Unforgiven... BANDIT comes out in his 'secret service' outfit, same Harley Davidson 'If you can read this, The B*TCH FELL OFF' T-shirt-becomes-bandana pulled low over his eyes, T2 Shades, black mesh t-shirt under a black suitjacket that had it's arms ripped off, showing off two 22 inch arms full of prison-tats, and leather pants. He's also has the earpiece in, as he scans the area and plops that podium front and center in the shot, right in front of MAYFIELD. Soonafter, flashbulbs and canned murmuring starts up as MAYFIELD lights a smoke and blows a stream out the corner of his mouth. MAYFIELD is wearing a suit and tie, with the tie loosened, and his porn star shades on. EDDIE takes the PRESIDENTIAL CHAMPIONSHIP BELT and hands it to BANDIT, who slaps it over a shoulder, and starts growling at nothing in particular. EDDIE then reaches into the podium and pulls out the UNIFIED WORLD TAGTEAM CHAMPIONSHIP belts, and hands those over to BANDIT as well, which he doubles up and slaps on the opposite shoulder!)



MAYFIELD: "It's been a long time, I shouldn't have left you... without a strong rhyme to step to. (Smirks) A little Rakim for ya'll. What's going on? Eddie Mayfield here, your good President and Ruler, and I have some things to say. First off, your President... is SAD. (SFX: Canned 'AWWWWWW' from the "crowd") yes yes, it's true. I'm SO SAD that all of these mutherf(BLEEEEEEP!) with no gold keep acting like Eddie Mayfield and the Intruders ain't sh(BLEEEEEP!). We've got Lucky Charms himself, Danny Boy Ryan, talking all big and selling Wolf-Tickets about who-done-done-what-to-whom, when weeks ago, Ryan crawled out of a rats nest in the basement and decided he was gonna stop pulling on his mommas apron and go get noticed on his own. Ryan comes out here and talks (BLEEEEEEP!) about the I's, then gets all huffy and wants to drop the subject when we respond. F(BLEEEEP!) that. You know the I's HAVE IT, and we keep it, Everyday, ALL day. Ryan, you wanna come out here and bring a knife to a gun fight? (Makes dismissing gestures) Have at it, but listen fella - you ain't making my blue skies cloudy. And you can marinate on that until you realize you'll get outclassed once again by the Intruders, and suddenly, (GASP!) want to 'not be bothered' and change the subject, ONCE again. The addage goes: Don't start something you can't finish, and it looks like yeah, I'm redundant - once again, you've done just that. But as President, you don't bother me, bro - you're just another stack of papers on my desk, and all I need is a whisk of my pen to use my Presidential Stroke to make you go away. Don't make me have to Veto you, Ryan. (Smiles) You've gotten this far under the radar. Don't make me call the D.O.D. to cock back and shoot your ass outta the sky.



"And now, our good ol' Crispy Critter, our favorite (Bugs eyes out)WOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO-EEEEEE! RAJUN CAJUN, The Gambit to the CSWA's X-Men, Shane Southern wants to have a Barbara Walters special at ON TIME and spout on how The Intruders WON'T LEAVE SHANE ALONE! (SFX: Cannned sound of a baby crying) COME ON, Shane. You taking the same meds that Ryan's on? Jesus. "THOSE BAD MEN CUT MAH HAH'RE! THEY WON'T STOP SWEATIN' SHAYNE SUHTHURN! PA! DUMP OUT THE DISHES INNA WASHBASIN, AH WANNA TAKE A BATH NEXT!" Yunno, all of these moaning cats that claim to be 'CSWA SUPERSTARS' seem to be a bunch of no-gold having player haters. Shane? You want us to leave you alone? Fine. (EDDIE slaps his hands together, like he's done with the task) There. I'll leave you alone. Evidently, you can't take anymore, and I'm not the kind of man that takes unfair advantage of the dumb. That's just cruel. We did what we had to do to you, Shane. I cut your hair, because it was funny. you lost your strap, because Adler... Dammit, even I hate to say it - Adlers' SMARTER than you. So until you begin wearing trenchcoats and start throwing energy-charged playing cards at people, I'm officially done with you... until you GIVE me a reason to start back up. (Smiles big! CUTTO: BANDIT, who growls and changes shoulders with the PRESIDENTIAL CHAMPIONSHIP BELT, putting all three straps on one shoulder.)



"At PRIMETIME, I have a VERY special surprise for the CSWA faithful, ANNND for my VICE-PRESIDENT, (Even though he doesn't know it yet) Chad Merritt. Eddie Mayfield has some Presidential Pardons to dole out, and PRIMETIME will be the place and time to begin. For all of you haters? For all of you whiny voters who didn't cast your vote for Eddie Mayfield - people like 'Non-Partisans' like Dan Ryan and Shane 'Don't hit me no more' Southern? Just pay attention to what happens at PT. There's not much we can do here for you, because nobody likes a whiner, but EVERYBODY loves a WINNER. (Smiles) Thank you America. Good Night... and GOD BLESS." (FADEOUT as MAYFIELD flicks his cigarette aside, then slowly raises his 'Double Nixon's' peace signs over his head... smililing evilly!)
