Burning in the Ashes (Trial by Fire)

5 October 2003–17 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Off to Valinor — 10-05-03, 03:43 AM cueup: 'Green Manalishi' by Peter Green



(FADEIN: A close view of the ‘dark’ Randalls, an evil grin behind his mask of black facepaint. The camera pans around him striding towards ‘The Wolf’ Mike Randalls with a wooden staff. ‘The Wolf’ is cautiously keeping away from his attacker, who follows him closely with his eyes. Above them the black fog crackles with thunder and streaks of lightning flash across the sky. The rain torrentially beats on the two men without recourse or pause.)



“There was a letter James Maurer had once written to me after I returned to America known as ‘The Wolf’. It had to do with a story that my father’s great uncle once told him during one of his many visits to the desert.”



“James always had a way of alluding to my fate without ever divulging what it was.”



(CUTTO: The ‘dark’ Randalls catches ‘The Wolf’ with a vicious uppercut with the staff to his head. ‘The Wolf’ swings but misses the facepainted terror, who delivers a stiff jab with the handle of his staff to the ribcage of the ‘The Wolf’. Dropping to one knee, ‘The Wolf’ gets caught with a blow over his back knocking him to the gravel beneath him.)



“It’s a story from an old, noble Cherokee Warrior sitting amongst his tribesman. He said to them that a fight was going on inside of him, it is a terrible fight and it is between two wolves.’”



(The ‘dark’ Randalls attempts to drive his staff through his opponent, but ‘The Wolf’ rolls out of the way avoiding the blow. The painted enemy does not break his smile continuing his stalking of the fearful Randalls, trying to keep away with jabs.)



”One wolf represents fear, anger, envy, sorrow, regret, greed, arrogance, self-pity, guilt, resentment, inferiority, lies, false pride, superiority and ego.”



”The other stands for joy, peace, love, hope, sharing, serenity, humility, kindness, benevolence, friendship, empathy, generosity, truth, compassion and faith.”



(The dark man swings viciously at ‘The Wolf’ who manages a block and ducks an immediate backhand return. He quickly handsprings away, the ‘dark’ Randalls in a stalking manner turns around and charges at ‘The Wolf’.)



”This same fight is going on inside of you and inside of every other person, too. They thought about it for a minute and then one tribesman asked the Warrior which wolf would win? The old Cherokee simply replied, the one you feed."



“For my whole life, I decided to feed one side. Now I no longer felt that hunger, like I was past starvation – withering away before this man’s very eyes.”



“Eyes, which had strength in them. Eyes, which possessed power over me. His malice and overwhelming will resonating in his voice as he talked to me.”



(CUTTO: ‘The Wolf’ blocks a vicious charging swing, but catches an elbow to the jaw snapping his head back. ‘The Wolf’ blocks away, his head bowed to the ground.)



‘dark’ RANDALLS: “Look at our face, Michael. Don’t hide from me in our last moments together. We have time to set things straight. (an evil grin forms, his eyes light for a moment) Perhaps even an eternity.”



(‘The Wolf’ catches a vicious uppercut across the face, followed by a lightning fast sideswipe – his body crumbles to the ground, his mouth bloodied. His eye now cut open. ‘The Wolf’ grasps for his staff, turns around and just in the nick of time blocks an overhead blow aimed for his head – he struggles to push the ‘dark’ Randalls off of him, trying not to stare in his raging eyes.)



“I looked into his eyes, admitting I could no longer hide – I was no longer blind.”



“His eyes were so apathetic, remorseless in the purpose it was bred for. The means would not matter on this dark night, so long as I met my end at his merciless hands.”



“Twenty years of teaching oneself that I was the facelessness of war. Now, I would have to watch that very face torture me without care through any existence of time.”



“His eyes spoke pure malice…”



‘dark’ RANDALLS: “Do you see yourself my lost friend? Do you see what you were meant to be? (smiles) Do not renounce your destiny.”



“His voice felt like poison.”



“What cruel trick was being played? Why must I fight the Wolf I had always fed? The Wolf I had always believed!”



(‘The Wolf’ pushes the ‘dark’ Randalls off enough to roll to his feet. He quickly has to parry off a vicious combination of swings, ‘The Wolf’s eyes are now focused on the ‘dark’ Randalls’ eyes – only the ‘Wolf’s are wide with shock and fear…)



“It was within this moment of battle that I realized that I, myself had changed. For what was in front of me to what I felt inside – I knew I was no longer this man. I was changed forever within life and without.”



“All I had believed I was fated to suffer from.”



(The ‘dark’ Randalls catches ‘The Wolf’ unexpectedly with a thrust kick to the stomach. As he doubles over, ‘The Wolf’ catches a crushing roundhouse uppercut to his jaw knocking him upwards – where an evilly grinning Randalls hits a spinning swing to his head knocking ‘The Wolf’ to his knees – not even conscious of himself dropping the staff to his side. His face is puffed and bleeding now, his attacker measuring him with his lustful eyes…lightning flashes across the sky – illuminating the wild man’s eyes.)



“My fate, like many others I had given…was to be devastated under the will of darkness I had harnessed, dispensed and nurtured for twenty years.”



“There would be no glory in my death.”



(The ‘dark’ Randalls with all his might forehand smashes ‘The Wolf’ across the head sending him into a heap on the black, unforgiving gravel.)



“Just an endless reminder of how remorseless I was to the world I lived in.”



“This wasn’t a fight I could win. It was the only fight, I had never prepared for.”



(CLOSEUP: The ‘dark’ Randalls standing over ‘The Wolf’ coughing up blood, lying on the gravel broken and defenseless. He stands there smiling in pure satisfaction, unrequited lust for more…)



“To live and fight with hate & anger was natural, to fight against it was hopeless.”



“So much of my strength, so much of my will, so much of my heart and so much of my bitter soul. Without thought it had been taught to strike. Without feel, it had been taught where.”



(The ‘dark’ Randalls grits his teeth and bashes “The Wolf” mercilessly as he can’t even protect himself – only his groaning to comfort him…)



“And without conscious, it would deliver its hand of self-justice.”



(The ‘dark’ Randalls stops as the defenseless ‘Wolf’ can’t bring his arms up for defense.)



“I was a man broken at the hands of absolute vengeance. No mercy would be given for eternity if I could not fight back. Temptation to plead with what was against me became stronger within me…and he knew it.”



(CUTTO: ‘The Wolf’ trying to bring himself off the ground, he’s bleeding and scarred in several places. The ‘dark’ man watches in delight as his prey struggles in front of him, defenseless to its fate bound to him. ‘The Wolf’ uses his staff as an aid to get to his knees…)



RANDALLS: “I am a man without hope to live, why won’t you leave me?”



(The ‘dark’ RANDALLS smiles maliciously, his eyes gleaming)



‘dark’ RANDALLS: (while licking his lips) “I promised I would never leave you, Michael. In either life or death. Did you think I could go away? When I promised to never abandon thee?”



(‘The Wolf’ leans his head against his staff – the words a cold reminder to what he has bound himself to…the ‘dark’ Randalls grins and walks forward, kneeling down to his broken victim…)



‘dark’ RANDALLS: (whispering) “Join me Michael, come back to me…”



(‘The Wolf’ closes his eyes in pain…)



“It’s all I wanted to do. To give in, to not fear my end. To avoid the necessary means.”



“His breath felt cold on my face. He was inches away from me smiling, bent on having me suppressed under his indomitable will. He wanted me to recede from the light shown to me, from the truth.”



“All the lies I believed, the lies that had made the man now standing over me – the lie he wanted me to become once again...



“He stood there waiting for my renouncement of truth, or waiting for my fated execution.”



“And even then, I could not go back. I could not forget. I could not change.”



“I lived one life as a life without truth…to live another, while knowing the truth…it was not inside me, it never was.”



(‘The Wolf’ opens his eyes and looks to his grinning enemy…)



RANDALLS: “No.”



(The ‘dark’ man’s smile fades to a deadpan expression. His eyes lose whatever ‘gleam’ they had.)



‘dark’ RANDALLS: “No?”



(‘The Wolf’ struggles to his feet with the aid of his staff…)



RANDALLS: “I will not live this lie any longer, even if I may die from it.”



“His eyes lost all life at this moment – ALL remorse.”



(‘The Wolf’ throws his staff away and off the mountain edge…and turns to his attacker, staggering – but for the first time, without fear…)



RANDALLS: “Nor will I fight this lie, I can only ACCEPT it.”



“My eyes met his – inside I felt alive, all I saw was a dead man.”



‘dark’ RANDALLS: “So be it, Michael. Then don’t fight…”



(Without recourse, the ‘dark’ Randalls swings with blinding speed sickeningly crushing ‘The Wolf’ in the skull with his staff, making him fall immediately as his eyes fall to the back of his head…)



“The impact of the strike, I will never forget – for I lost all consciousness, I will never know how far I faded…”



‘dark’ RANDALLS: (gritting his teeth and smiling) “Then die, you FOOL.”



“I lay on the ground frightened, battered, barren and without any strength to recover. In giving up everything, I felt much of my energy leaving my body. I had accepted the fate with the lie. However, I would die rather than submit to it.”



“And then in that moment of clarity as I raised my head from the jagged rocks, off in the distance my emptiness filled with hope – for the sun was rising. The signal of dawn had arrived…”



“Light was breaking the darkness, proof I was starting to see through the lies. That I had not failed yet…”



(CUTTO: Looking from behind ‘The Wolf’s head as he struggles to raise it off the ground. For endless miles, the black fog covers the sky and surrounds the mountain itself. But off on the horizon, the sky is clearing up – blue cracking through the black and an orange sun starting to rise…the camera pans around to ‘The Wolf’ opening his eyes to this with a slight smile, before his head falls to the gravel in exhaustion.



FADE TO BLACK)



“It was at this moment that both of the men on this mountain knew the darkness could end. It was now a matter of it would.” Off to Valinor — 01-17-04, 01:49 PM (cueup: 'Fearless' - Pink Floyd



FADEIN: 'The Wolf' MIKE RANDALLS lying facedown in the dirt, groaning. His whole back is scarred, while blood falls 30,000 feet down from his face. The 'dark' RANDALLS is pacing around with a sadistic grin...)



'dark' RANDALLS: "It was never supposed to end like this, Michael. Believe me, this is not what I wanted nor what I had ever planned."



He paced around my fallen and broken body, drained of all hope to resolve this fight within myself. If only he would end my suffering...



'dark' RANDALLS: "But we are an arrogant breed, are we not? Unable to accept defeat, to know when we are wrong. But fear not, Michael...(he smiles down at his prey)...your final mistake WILL be our LAST forever."



His staff had been broken over my back and out of the corner of my eye, I saw him pick up one resembling a stake. I could feel his violent intentions, yet I welcomed my fate to die like this - for my sins of the past certainly earned the punishment I would receive....



'dark' RANDALLS: (touching the sharpened edge of the 'stake') "I can feel your remorse, Michael. It SICKENS me. You've become so weak, so fragile, so easy to understand. Yet, in your ignorance you believe you will find your bliss...your END. Don't think that will ever SAVE you. Nor will it stop me from what I must do, what I am compelled to do, after all - you are the creator, you should know. Yet, I find it pitiful that you actually believe those fools of the past didn't deserve their demise or destruction..."



RANDALLS: (coughing) "Fools? (rolls over onto back) You are the arrogant fool. I only made them suffer because I thought the world, that God himself had cast me aside to suffer eternally, myself. I was wrong, which makes YOU wrong -- so understand that no matter what you say, no matter what you do, however this ends...I know I deserved it as much as you will when my part of us ceases to exist shortly. So, do what thou wilst - you cannot turn me to your will."



'dark' RANDALLS: (pacing around, shaking his head disapprovingly, but with a smile) "So we were lied to. Its not such a surprise since our whole life is a lie. But whose to say this is the end, Michael? Whose to say that while you suffer here endlessly at my hands, that somehow...someway...our body WON'T SURVIVE?"



He knelt down, leaning close to my face, I smelled death...I had not thought of this possibility and it frightened me.



'dark' RANDALLS: "Maybe you will pass onto the fires of hell, where you can pay for your sins as you wish, while I SURVIVE...like I ALWAYS have. (smiles, licks lips) Perhaps, I'll call Hornet again..."



He laid down, his lips touching my ear...



'dark RANDALLS: (whispering) "Or better yet...maybe I'll call IVY. To watch her suffer has ALWAYS been my dream."



A feeling I can't describe started rushing through my body, I turned back over onto my face - I didn't want him to see my reaction...my weakness, my tears. I looked up to the sun getting stronger, the clouds separating for something in the distance I could not see. Hornet, I was not worried about - he had seen the worst of this man. But Ivy...



He could feel my thoughts and he started to laugh obscenely at me, for he knew like I did that I wanted to stand up and fight, to end this man's existence. To end mine...



But I had no strength to do so...
