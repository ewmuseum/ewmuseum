The Longest Night - Feedback?

Chad — 05-30-03, 06:21 PM Anybody have feedback on "The Longest Night" series? It's probably the longest series I've done in a long time, if ever.

I know parts of it can be confusing, since it pulls in things anywhere from a year ago to 10 years ago. It's also supposed to be written in a melange of styles and voices, so I hope that doesn't come off as too chaotic.

Anyway, just trying to get some chatter going before BOB17.

-C Off to Valinor — 05-30-03, 09:46 PM I dug it alot. It's great. Although I gotta admit I'm pulling for Hornet to snap and start wearing only black facepaint. :) Chad — 05-30-03, 10:14 PM LOL...he hasn't worn *any* facepaint in years, so that'd be something. Hex Angel — 05-31-03, 09:17 AM Considering I got to hear all about them before they went up, they had a lot to live up to.

And they did. Good stuff. Yeeeeey. Deacon — 05-31-03, 09:42 AM I loved the beginning... showing a wrestler who's suffering w/ an addiction to painkillers... or an apparent addiction is both timely and cool. I think I'd told you that on IM.

The scene w/ the 'wall' was ok. It was weird to see the kayfabe breaking style of it. If Alicia was killed, really killed by Hornet by the use of Randalls giving him drugs, then it brings up why Randalls and Hornet and Eli were all together - unless Eli was able to deal w/ that and since I believe I'm the only one w/ a wife amongst us... he wouldn't deal with it... ever. He'd hit the courts and someone would be in jail.

The ending was odd. The little boy seemed to snap Hornet out of his need for sleep? Wait, it's 7am or so, and he'd been sleeping throughout the day anyway. What's the "ending"? Is he returning to the hero? What did he decide? How was he changed? The main thought I had was that it didn't leave me w/ an ending... perhaps at BoB? Or maybe I'm just tired... Alex did keep us up and then get us up early!

Warren Chad — 05-31-03, 03:20 PM Alicia was killed in a car accident, but Hornet didn't have anything to do with it.

However... Hornet *thought* he did while he was under the influence of Mike Randalls and some mind-altering drugs that Randalls was feeding him. Keep him mind, that's stretching back to an angle from around 8 years ago!

And that's really the 'confusing' part of this whole thing. I'm trying to cover a lot of ground. Hornet's having to deal with decisions he made and guilt that he's held onto for years and years.

As far as the ending.... it's possible that one more post will go up before BOB. And then, yes, it concludes *at* BOB.
