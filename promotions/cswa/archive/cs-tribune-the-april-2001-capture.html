In the apparent final announcement for ELVIS LIVES, CSWA officials revealed what's on the line in the "High Stakes" match for the Unified Tag Team Titles. Whichever team has a member that gets pinned loses their stake. In addition, the team making the pinfalls becomes the Unified Tag Team Champions. It was also announced that former Unified Tag Champs ARROGANCE have been added, making this a four-way match. Unified Tag Team Champs Simply Stunning will obviously put the titles on the line as their share. ARROGANCE will put Joey Melton's CSWA career on the line: should a member of ARROGANCE get pinned, Melton is banned for life from wrestling in the CSWA. Adler and Tyler have their tag team future at stake: should one of them get pinned, they will never be eligible for another shot at the tag team titles. And if a member of Foreign Exchange is unlucky enough to go down for three, they must disband as tag team. Three's A Crowd While we could be referring to the ClaimStakers after the fireworks between Randalls and Hornet at SuperPRIMETIME, we're actually talking about another ELVIS LIVES announcement. Following the controversy in Cincinnati, CSWA officials have added Jean Rabesque to the Greensboro Championship title match coming up in Nashville. He will face Greensboro Champion Evan Aho and Sky Suicide as part of the three-way title rematch. In another twist, officials have ruled that to win the title, either challenger must pin Aho himself. If Suicide or Rabesque are pinned, they must leave the ring area while the match continues. Top Dog Tournament While the ladies have their catfight, the gentlemen will have a "Top Dog Tournament" to determine the next Presidential Champion. With ten competitors entered into the mix, CSWA officials have opted to go with a format the CSWA hasn't seen in years. The ten men have been randomly divided into five tag teams. Two matches will take place in elimination tag team format; if one partner is pinned, the team is out. The winning teams move on to a tag team 'final'. The 'team' that wins the tag final goes on to the Top Dog match, where the team is split up to face each other one-on-one, with the Presidential Title on the line. Just another huge event for ELVIS LIVES! Catfight! We may have to call the CSWA ring at ELVIS LIVES the "Jungle Room" after the latest announcement. CSWA officials have confirmed that Teri Melton and Poison Ivy have been signed to a match at the pay-per-view. And not just any match, but an Alpha Female Match. Apparently, the winner is the top cat in the CSWA, while the loser has to eat dog food. It's definitely one not to be missed. Other Recent Headlines: Gemini Rising? CSWA Turns 13!
