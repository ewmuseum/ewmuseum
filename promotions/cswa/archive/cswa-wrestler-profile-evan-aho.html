CSWA Wrestler Profile: Evan Aho Name: Evan Aho Hometown: Seattle, Washington Height: 6'0" Weight: 234 lbs Age: 26 Alignment: Neutral Entrance Music: "Blood, Milk and Sky" by White Zombie Manager: Biography/Profile: A true wrestling purist, few are as dedicated to their craft as Evan Aho. His commitment to the sport of wrestling is borderline obsessive and seemingly dictates his actions both in and out of the ring. The dark-haired six-footer holds a strict training regimen combining film-study, weights, stretching, cardiovascular conditioning and in-ring sparring every day he isn’t working at an event. The result is an entirely focused wrestling machine. Shunning crowds, media and all other things “celebrity” has long been Evan’s habit. Aho’s ring entrance consists of straightforward heavy metal music; no fireworks, no laser lighting. His ring attire consists of navy blue wrestling briefs, kneepads, wrist wraps and boots. No logos, all business. At all costs he has avoided the hardcore, high-flying and cheap-shot styles of wrestling, contesting that his vast array of holds and moves are far more potent than any chair shot, moonsault or low-blow. Evan has a vast repertoire of ground moves based around suplexes and high impact throws directed at an opponent’s neck. While he has used a variety of moves as "Ecstasy" (Tiger Driver '91)s in the past, the combo of an inverted DDT followed by the Tiger Driver ’91 seems to be his favorite. Coupled together he calls them the “Agony” and the “Ecstasy”, respectively. So consumed by his quest to wrestle, few have actually grown close to Evan as a person. Likewise, few have been able to garner his distaste. None have succeeded in getting under his skin. Aho currently lives in what is described as an “upper-middle class” suburb of Seattle on Lake Washington. As a point of interest, long-time rival Sky Suicide’s mansion is on the opposite shore of the lake, clearly visible from Aho’s house.
