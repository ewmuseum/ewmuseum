CSWA Grapevine:  The Rumor Mill's Cookin! Back to the TRIBUNE UPDATED 1/29/01 The war is getting dirtier. So are the heroes. This spat between the ClaimStakers and the Playboys is no longer a black and white issue. Eli Flair putting Melissa through a table and Troy Windham's intent to bring Ivy McGinnis' mother and daughter into the issue has painted the CSWA in shades of gray. Moreso than anyone else in the CSWA, I probably understand the position of the CSers in this thing. Hornet, Randalls, and Flair are arguably three of the biggest stars in the history of the CSWA, with Hornet by far being the biggest "homegrown" star to ever come out of Greensboro. And I can't honestly say I approve of every decision Commisioner Merritt has made in this company's history, as it relates to the wrestlers. On one hand, he has had a tendency to treat them like little more than property, and while he is fairly generous with the wrestlers who have proven their worth, it's not like they're making money at his expense. Truth be told, while Merritt may have provided Randalls, Hornet, and Flair with enough money for a comfortable lifestyle, he's also a billionaire several times over for it. Of course, it's not like Merritt is Scrooge and the wrestlers are Oliver Twist, asking for "s'more." It's in their job description to wrestle, wrestle well, and wrestle often. Looking simply at a job description, Merritt has done nothing legally or morally wrong to any of these three. How did this start? That's easy.... Eli Flair put Sweet Melissa through a table. Or was it when Hornet, Mike Randalls, and Flair told the world at A2K, to stay out of their way? Isn't that where Hornet took his businessman image and merged it with everything he hid from us as the Greatest American Hero, and basically told us all that he was betraying everything he had traditionally stood for? Say what you will about the period between ELVIS XI and ANN99.... everyone makes mistakes. But that was no mistake. Was it, apparently the year before ANNIVERSARY 2000, when this plan seems to have been set in motion? Is that where it started? It's become something of common knowledge that Eli Flair's involvement was a last minute decision, sprung at A2K by those two words, "I'm In." Is it Hornet and Randalls' planning that was responsible for Sweet Melissa's horrible ordeal? Maybe it's deeper than that? The animosity between Hornet and Commissioner Merritt was well documented in the seven months following FISH FUND XI, when Hornet was forced out of the CSWA via his loss to GUNS. Take into account that Merritt only called Hornet when his legal expertise was needed for his buyout of former co- Commisioner Thomas. There are too many levels to this story to simply point to one incident as the kicker. And, as Troy Windham said, this is war, and innocent people get hurt. On the one hand, I would be right there with Poison Ivy in calling for some form of reprimand toward Troy if he had physically assaulted either her mother or daughter. On the other, he <b>didn't.</b> And just as Hornet warned the world at ANNIVERSARY, the Playboys warned the ClaimStakers after Melissa took her table. I'd expect that from the ClaimStakers. Regardless of their justification, their rationalization, and their posturing, Hornet and Eli Flair have sold their integrity down the river in favor of their current reign of terror. Teamed with Mike Randalls, that pair goes from overpowering to simply unstoppable. But someone has to stop them. I just wish it wasn't Troy Windham, Eddy Love, and Cardigo Mysterian we were turning to. Troy Windham? The King of the Slackers? Only he and his doctor knows if he's clean and disease- free. He's responsible for innumerable attacks on all walks of wrestler, be it solo, with the Frat, with Generation X-Press, with the No Limit Soldiers, or now, with the Playboys. Eddy Love. The man who banned Deacon from the CSWA. The man who won the World Title on a wave of decisive victories, unmeasurable arrogance, a loaded glove, and a can of Raid. The man who many point to as the reason for the dissolusion of the Powers of Love, arguably the most dominant tag team since the CS Express. Cardigo Mysterian. Several years ago, Paul Westbrock killed his brother at the culmination of a death match. Do I need to say anything else? These are the men we've apparently put our trust into to combat the force that the ClaimStakers present. Are they up to the challenge? History would tell us that this fight can go either way. But for the average fan, it's hard to tell, sometimes, who the "good guy" is. Over a year ago, the CS Tribune posted a very small article with a very profound headline. We Need A Hero. You're damn right. Updated 1/29/01 I'm Rudy Seitzer, and welcome to CSWA Grapevine, where we track down the rumors running rampant here in the world of wrestling. As always, I have to start with a standard disclaimer: we cannot verify any of the rumors reported here. Some may be true, some may be outright false...but they've all been passed to me by various sources. So don't come crying to Rudy...got it? Thomas is gone...I guess that means I'll finally get my choice of cereal in the CS cafeteria. I've never seen a man that could eat more cereal in my life. So was the lawsuit all a gag? Appears that it was and it wasn't. Thomas and Merritt were actually in court to draw up the 'division of power' in CS Enterprises, as well as address concerns from the FTC. The court normally requires mediation in these types of decisions. So the Mike Randalls/Hornet/Eli Flair association has been in the planning stages for just under a year? And I didn't get a whiff of it? This may qualify as "best kept secret of the year"...and maybe longer. Speaking of the 'triumverate', Poison Ivy and Hornet have been relatively circumspect on where their 'after-hours' relationship stands. I've heard that things have been heating up, but no major announcements yet. Deacon may have been putting on a brave face. I've confirmed reports that Deacon has been having that right arm looked at by specialists. He entered the match at ANNIVERSARY with a tingling sensation in his arm. While his shoulder may not be at 100% following the match, doctors are trying to find out if the two are related. Commissioner Merritt has stated that he may not be able to fire or suspend Hornet, but that he can deal with him in the ring. I've heard rumors that he may actually start things *outside* the ring. The question is, who's on Merritt's side? Is a Powers of Love reunion in the works? One might think so after Love helped put Powers into the World Title match in Pensacola by laying down for him at ANNIVERSARY. But I don't think it's gonna happen. Sunshine's back...and she's bad. Did you get a load of the change from goth to hot? But where's Vizzack? He hasn't even been spotted backstage, which is odd for the rarely-separated two-some. Is he biding his time, or has Sunshine got a new managerial focus? After hearing his name invoked at ANNIVERSARY, could Ray S. Cornette be planning a restart of his CORPORATION? Cornette retired almost three years ago, and has been focusing his efforts on a small business in promoting entertainment events. Word has it that the old wrestling juices may be flowing. Does this mean that he might be able to coax Joey Melton out of the retirement home as well? Or even Wall? First JW Locke, then Erik Zieba, is another independent promotion exec about to show up in the CSWA? That's it for now, Fans... I'll keep you posted.
