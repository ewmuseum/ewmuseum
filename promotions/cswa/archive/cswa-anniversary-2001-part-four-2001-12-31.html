CSWA ANNIVERSARY 2001 -- December 31, 2001 -- Part Four ANNIVERSARY TRIVIA Can you name the two primary antagonists involved in the famous "SKYDOME SPLITS" matches? Well you don't know your CSWA history then, do ya? Mickey Benedict (yes, that Mickey Benedict) and Bill Parsons started their series of SKYDOME matches as a friendly feud. It quickly erupted into a Hatfield-McCoy type family feud, with many of the Parsons clan getting involved against Mickey and his two sons. As always, the feud centered around a woman, Mickey's wife Melba. Mickey eventually dumped Melba in favor of one of the FISH FUND's surgery recipients, Myra. More than you ever wanted to know, isn't it? ANNIVERSARY 1999 featured the fourth IRONMAN of CHAMPIONS... and the return of the Red Midget, of course. ANNIVERSARY 1997 saw a masked man vie for the World Title in the finals a championship tournament. Was it Hornet? ANNIVERSARY 1996 saw the feud between Randalls and Hornet pull in a new America's Team!
