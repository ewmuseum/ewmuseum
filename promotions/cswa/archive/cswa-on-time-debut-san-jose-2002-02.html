Card Lineup: CSWA: On Time Debut

11-13 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Chad Feb 11, 2002 at 7:14 PM Here's the lineup for the first CSWA: ON TIME in San Jose: Machine vs. JJ DeVille Kin Hiroshi vs. Carl Brigsby "Lone Walker" Paul Michaels vs. Wesley Paige Men of Adventure vs. Raw Deal GREENSBORO TITLE: Shane Southern vs. Cameron Cruise MAIN EVENT: PRESIDENTIAL TITLE: XXXstasy vs. Tom Adler Non-match 'segments' will either be announced later (after the MAIN for A2K1 goes up, or handlers will be e-mailed to let them know what they need to prepare.) Roleplay deadline: Sunday, February 17th Segment/Angles deadline: Thursday, February 14th TWhitefield Feb 12, 2002 at 9:52 AM Ummmm.... Ok, I'll be the first to admit that I am, at times, guilty of skimming matches that don't involve my own guys. So, I may well have missed something along the way. But, I gotta ask... Is "Men Of Adventure" somebody I oughta be familiar with? =] TW TWhitefield Feb 12, 2002 at 10:07 AM RE: Ummmm.... (nevermind) Nevermind... I figured it out. I just wasn't familiar with the name of the team. =] (patiently waits to be jobbed out) LOL TW Calamity Jon Feb 12, 2002 at 3:47 PM RE: Ummmm.... (nevermind) (FADEIN on BENJAMIN "BIG TOM" REMUS and "IRON" JOHN WAITS, in trapper regalia and beekeeper masks, waving big sticks in front of a blue-screen of a bee swarm...) WAITS: "Familiar with us? Familiar with US? My boy, you should be as familiar with us as the very LICE IN MY CROTCH are familiar with the crabs around LITTLE JOHN ... wait, did I say "Little?" I meant "ENORMOUS JOHN -- MY PENIS!!!" HO HO!" REMUS: "It's true, the little fellas are tight like this (crosses fingers) DAMN THESE BEES!" (FTB as Tom and John swing wildly at the fake background...) ============================== So who RPs to an OORP question? THE MEN OF ADVENTURE, that's who! We're just that hardcore! TWhitefield Feb 13, 2002 at 11:38 AM RE: Ummmm.... (nevermind) OORP: LOL. No, honestly, I have seen your stuff on this team. I even actually read the match against Brigsby & Page. Just, for some reason, "Men Of Adventure" didn't connect with me as the name of your team. TW
