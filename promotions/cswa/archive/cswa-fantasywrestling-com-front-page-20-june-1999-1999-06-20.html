FantasyWrestling.com -- Welcome to the CSWA! Updated June 20, 1999 CSWA SHOWTIME in Long Island is up! Click here to find out how to sign up for the new CSWA mailing list! Click Here to Visit our Sponsor The Road to ANNIVERSARY With the close of BATTLE OF THE BELTS XVI: Personal Business, the stage is set for CSWA ANNIVERSARY 1999: Revelation. With the announcement of the IRONMAN of CHAMPIONS, the march toward Anniversary Weekend takes on even more importance. The road continues down the East Coast to CSWA PRIMETIME in Tampa, as the CSWA comes off a huge event this past weekend with SHOWTIME in Long Island. The road tour will stop in Tampa and possibly one other location in Florida before heading back up I-95 toward Greensboro for the July 4th holiday weekend. (Note: The Awards and pre-Anniversary SuperPRIMETIME will probably be posted during the weekend; the full ANNIVERSARY card will go up early that week.) The join form is still down. If you are a friend of a current member...contact them regarding entering the league. Once again, thanks for your patience as I've both moved back to Greensboro in the last couple of weeks, as well as dealing with some family issues. Also, look for more major changes coming soon! "Eleventh Heaven" The CSWA has begun its ELEVENTH year here in 1999. Starting as a 'home league' between friends in 1988, moving to the PRODIGY® service in 1992, and finally staking a claim here on fantasywrestling.com just over one year ago. We want to thank all the members from over the years, both past and current. It has been a joy to begin establishing a new online community here on fantasywrestling.com. You are the ones that make it fun, and make it worth the effort. We're working to update the entire site, as well as make sure things keep rolling on to ANNIVERSARY 1999! You may already be familiar with fantasy wrestling or 'e-wrestling.' The CSWA helped to innovate many of the standards in this fantasy sport...and take it to a new level. The CSWA traditionally (and currently) has competition that no one else can match. Our goal is very simple.....to have fun. We can only do that with your help. With great roleplayers, we can only offer you a few things....top-notch cards, great 'wrestling', and some comic relief thrown in. (Can you say...midgets?) The CSWA is not a simmed league. We don't use real wrestlers, and we don't run our league based around what the NWO or Degeneration X are doing. It's all original, all the time. This is your place to develop a character....to have fun...and to enjoy wrestling the way it should be. Welcome to the CSWA......since 1988, over 2000 wrestlers have passed through the doors for hundreds of cards and thousands of hours of fun. Now it's your turn.
