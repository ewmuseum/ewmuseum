Steve Radder Kevin Powers Eddy Love Mike Randalls Havoc Lawrence Stanley None of the above View Results UPDATED 1/2/01 IRONMAN Scoreboard CSWA INTERACTIVE CSWA Mailing List Information Features Happy New Year! The CS TRIBUNE wishes all its readers a very merry New Year as we step into 2001. In latest CSWA news, CSWA manager Poison Ivy continues her discussion with CSWA Commissioner Chad Merritt in efforts to get the firing of Eli Flair overturned. Controversy continues to surround the IRONMAN of CHAMPIONS. With Mike Randalls suspended due to his post-match attack on Kevin Powers, news has recently come to light that IOC competitor Havoc may also be under doctors' orders not to wrestle. We'll continue to update you as we approach round two of the IOC at SHOWTIME, coming up later this week. Eli Flair FIRED! In a press conference held at 11AM EST, CSWA Commissioner Chad Merritt announced that former CSWA World Champion Eli Flair has been fired from organization. No reason was given, although the TRIBUNE speculates that Flair's recent SHOWTIME attack on Eddy Love's manager, Sweet Melissa, must be the cause. In firing Flair, along with the suspension of Mike Randalls, Commissioner Merritt has essentially halved the ranks of the group most interested in removing his power base, the "ClaimStakers." IOC In Shambles? Two major announcements were made concerning competitors in the IRONMAN of CHAMPIONS, completely changing the outlook of the tournament for the CSWA World Title. CSWA medical staff released additional information on the condition of Kevin Powers, currently one of the IOC leaders due to his win over Mike Randalls at SHOWTIME in Birmingham. Powers sustained bruised ribs, along with a bruised kidney from his post-match beating by Randalls with a steel-plated baseball bat. Powers has been advised not to compete in the ring for at least two weeks. If Powers is forced to sit out of his match in Memphis, he automatically sacrifices his lead in the tournament. Following the report by the medical staff, CSWA Commissioner Chad Merritt announced that former Unified World Champion Mike Randalls has been suspended through the end of the year due to his action at SHOWTIME. "The actions by Mike Randalls were totally abhorrent. I have no choice but to suspend Mike from the CSWA again," said Merritt. This apparently puts Randalls out of at least the next two rounds of the IRONMAN, which would almost certainly mathematically eliminate him from winning the tournament. We'll have more updates on how this will specifically affect the IOC as more information becomes available. PLR Stands Tall? They may not be a threesome anymore, but the former PLR composed of Powers, Love and Radder, are standing atop the IRONMAN of CHAMPIONS scoreboard at the end of the first round. Over 18% of CS TRIBUNE readers believe that Kevin Powers should be the next CSWA World Champion. Powers remains second in the online poll to Havoc, who has 21%. Of course, 26% of readers believe that none of the six IOC competitors should win the title. Sounds like 'sour grapes' from fans of some of the wrestlers not chosen to compete. IOC5 continues in Memphis, with all three current leaders having the chance to jump to a perfect forty points. Steve Radder faces Mike Randalls; Lawrence Stanley takes his shot at Eddy Love, and the top two INTERACTIVE vote-getters face off as Havoc and Kevin Powers meet.
