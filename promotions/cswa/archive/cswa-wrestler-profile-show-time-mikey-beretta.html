CSWA Wrestler Profile: "Show Time" Mikey Beretta Name: "Show Time" Mikey Beretta Hometown: Atlanta, GA Height: 6'6 Weight: 275 Age: 21 Alignment: Face Music: "I'm The One" by The Descendents Manager/Valet: Biography/Profile: Mikey is a handsome man, with fairly short, bleached hair. In the ring, he is always clad in plain white speedo tights, and white boots. He likes the fans, and because of that, he usually tries to give them a good show. That is the reason his technical style is tinged with so much high flying ability. Mikey is the current leader of the "No Limit Soldiers."
