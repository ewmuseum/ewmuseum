CSWA Wrestler Profile: John J. Battles Name: John J. Battles Hometown: El Paso, Texas Height: 6'4" Weight: 255 lbs Age: 26 Alignment: Face Entrance Music: "Eyes of a Ranger" by Chuck Norris Biography/Profile: Battles is half Apache, with a dark complexion and long black hair he keeps tied back in a tail. He is powerfully built, but in more of an athletic way, than a bulky way like Lex Luger. His ring attire is simply black jeans and a turquoise muscle shirt, black mocassin style wrestling boots, and a black buckskin jacket he removes before wrestling. His Texas Ranger badge is always pinned to his lapel. JJB grew up a loner. His parents were killed when he was two, and he was raised by his grandfather, Running Wolf. The Apche children generally ignored him for being white, and the white children ignored him for being Apache. But his Grandfather taught him the old Apache way, including SuChi, the Apache unarmed fighting method, a martial art most people have never heard of. He excelled and soon his Grandfather had him enrolled in traditional martial arts classes. As a youth, he spent time in Japan learning from the masters, and is a master of Kenjitsu, Karate and Aikido. But Texas was in his blood, and Apache was his heritage, so he returned home. Battles' strict honor code made him a natural in the police force, and it wasn't long before he became one of the elite 99 men and women that make up THE TEXAS RANGERS. While working undercover in a small wrestling promotion, he seriously beat a wrestler who was using his fame to "get close" to children. Rather than put the Rangers through a media spectacle, he excepted a demotion to reserve status. But the wrestling bug had bitten him, and although he still was included in many Ranger cases, he had time to pursue his new passion. Previous Titles Held: SWF World Champion (2) NAWF World Champion - undefeated WCWL World Champion - undefeated TCW TV Champion NAWF Tag Team Champion Moves: 1. Spinning heel kick 2. Outside crescent kick 3. Long Arm of the Law clothesline 4. Scoop slam 5. Roundhouse kick 6. Boston Crab 7. Dropkick 8. Rapid Hands (Kenpo Karate technique) 9. Back Breaker 10. Iron claw Finisher: Apache Deathlock -- The head is grasped just like a Scorpion Deathdrop, but instead of dropping to the mat, the knee is placed behind opponent's, forcing them to their knees.
