CSWA Grapevine:  The Rumor Mill's Cookin! Back to the TRIBUNE UPDATED 1/29/01 The war is getting dirtier. So are the heroes. This spat between the ClaimStakers and the Playboys is no longer a black and white issue. Eli Flair putting Melissa through a table and Troy Windham's intent to bring Ivy McGinnis' mother and daughter into the issue has painted the CSWA in shades of gray. Moreso than anyone else in the CSWA, I probably understand the position of the CSers in this thing. Hornet, Randalls, and Flair are arguably three of the biggest stars in the history of the CSWA, with Hornet by far being the biggest "homegrown" star to ever come out of Greensboro. And I can't honestly say I approve of every decision Commisioner Merritt has made in this company's history, as it relates to the wrestlers. On one hand, he has had a tendency to treat them like little more than property, and while he is fairly generous with the wrestlers who have proven their worth, it's not like they're making money at his expense. Truth be told, while Merritt may have provided Randalls, Hornet, and Flair with enough money for a comfortable lifestyle, he's also a billionaire several times over for it. Of course, it's not like Merritt is Scrooge and the wrestlers are Oliver Twist, asking for "s'more." It's in their job description to wrestle, wrestle well, and wrestle often. Looking simply at a job description, Merritt has done nothing legally or morally wrong to any of these three. How did this start? That's easy.... Eli Flair put Sweet Melissa through a table. Or was it when Hornet, Mike Randalls, and Flair told the world at A2K, to stay out of their way? Isn't that where Hornet took his businessman image and merged it with everything he hid from us as the Greatest American Hero, and basically told us all that he was betraying everything he had traditionally stood for? Say what you will about the period between ELVIS XI and ANN99.... everyone makes mistakes. But that was no mistake. Was it, apparently the year before ANNIVERSARY 2000, when this plan seems to have been set in motion? Is that where it started? It's become something of common knowledge that Eli Flair's involvement was a last minute decision, sprung at A2K by those two words, "I'm In." Is it Hornet and Randalls' planning that was responsible for Sweet Melissa's horrible ordeal? Maybe it's deeper than that? The animosity between Hornet and Commissioner Merritt was well documented in the seven months following FISH FUND XI, when Hornet was forced out of the CSWA via his loss to GUNS. Take into account that Merritt only called Hornet when his legal expertise was needed for his buyout of former co- Commisioner Thomas. There are too many levels to this story to simply point to one incident as the kicker. And, as Troy Windham said, this is war, and innocent people get hurt. On the one hand, I would be right there with Poison Ivy in calling for some form of reprimand toward Troy if he had physically assaulted either her mother or daughter. On the other, he <b>didn't.</b> And just as Hornet warned the world at ANNIVERSARY, the Playboys warned the ClaimStakers after Melissa took her table. I'd expect that from the ClaimStakers. Regardless of their justification, their rationalization, and their posturing, Hornet and Eli Flair have sold their integrity down the river in favor of their current reign of terror. Teamed with Mike Randalls, that pair goes from overpowering to simply unstoppable. But someone has to stop them. I just wish it wasn't Troy Windham, Eddy Love, and Cardigo Mysterian we were turning to. Troy Windham? The King of the Slackers? Only he and his doctor knows if he's clean and disease- free. He's responsible for innumerable attacks on all walks of wrestler, be it solo, with the Frat, with Generation X-Press, with the No Limit Soldiers, or now, with the Playboys. Eddy Love. The man who banned Deacon from the CSWA. The man who won the World Title on a wave of decisive victories, unmeasurable arrogance, a loaded glove, and a can of Raid. The man who many point to as the reason for the dissolusion of the Powers of Love, arguably the most dominant tag team since the CS Express. Cardigo Mysterian. Several years ago, Paul Westbrock killed his brother at the culmination of a death match. Do I need to say anything else? These are the men we've apparently put our trust into to combat the force that the ClaimStakers present. Are they up to the challenge? History would tell us that this fight can go either way. But for the average fan, it's hard to tell, sometimes, who the "good guy" is. Over a year ago, the CS Tribune posted a very small article with a very profound headline. We Need A Hero. You're damn right. Updated 3/29/01 I'm Rudy Seitzer, and welcome to CSWA Grapevine, where we track down the rumors running rampant here in the world of wrestling. As always, I have to start with a standard disclaimer: we cannot verify any of the rumors reported here. Some may be true, some may be outright false...but they've all been passed to me by various sources. So don't come crying to Rudy...got it? Could it be that Merritt's actually gone face? Who would've believed it? Take a look at the latest "Bottom Line" and see what you think. I guess the ClaimStakers really do have him rattled. Speaking of which...do the ClaimStakers really have a purpose? Eli's obsessed with Troy, Randalls seems obsessed with Powers (and himself, of course), and Hornet's obsessed with Merritt...but keeps getting pulled into whatever the other two are doing. Well, you know what they say about a ship with no captain. I think I made that saying up just now actually...or at least the reference to it. Looks like the CSWA is starting to get an influx of new talent, or at least talent that's relatively new to the CSWA. Guys like the EWI's "Gemini" and his updated "Three Faces of Schmid" gimmick could be difficult backstage, much like some are saying Jean Rabesque has been. Rumor has it that the Unified Title may be returning. Does that mean it's gonna be around Randalls waist as the last recognized champion? Or is that Hellion? I guess it depends on who you ask. Where the heck did Sunshine go? Wasn't it just the last Grapevine that I was touting her new threads and wondering where her partner in misery was? I mentioned Ray S. Cornette on the last column as well. No sign of life from the former manager of managers yet...but I've got reason to believe the ol' snake in the grass still has a few tricks up his sleeve. And reappearing while Merritt is involved with the ClaimStakers would be just what I'd expect from Cornette's corner. ELVIS LIVES is just weeks away, and I've heard tales that Eddy Love's obsession with "The King" runs as deep as any we've seen since Bill Parsons and his Search. You may think I'm kidding, but it seems like the former champion is a true Southern boy at heart. I wonder what Eddy would look like if he wore a rhinestone-studded jumpsuit to the ring. Speaking of a bit loopy, what's going on with Powers and his former compatriot Apocalypse. Those two are going off the deep edge as far as I can tell. Apocalypse has been vocal about his resentment of the role Merritt is allowing him to play, while Powers is obsessed with either winning the IRONMAN or dropping out of it entirely. Just what would happen if Powers dropped out right now? How would we finish the round-robin tournament? Would someone simply be substituted for him? And speaking of the IRONMAN, I've still got no idea who's going to come out of things the champ. The recent CSWA INTERACTIVE poll had Kevin Powers followed closely by Havoc as the fans' choices to the win the whole thing. Havoc's currently in the lead....Powers appears to be sidetracked by Mike Randalls. And whatever happened to JW Locke? We haven't heard much from the reigning (or is it former) CSWA chief of staff recently. A few well-placed inquiries suggest he's been doing some talent-scouting both in the States and the Orient. That's it for now, Fans... I'll keep you posted.
