Steve's New How To Speed The CSWA Up Rant

6–7 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Steve — 11-06-03, 11:54 PM Simple really. The CSWA needs two things.



1) A 20-25 man roster.



and



2) A 4-5 (min.) card writing team.





The idea for OT was really a shot at slicing off a bit of the FWO world for us. Low carb cards written by the CSWA roster.



What we've discovered is this. In our neck of the woods, people committ to FW when they have time, and when they want to. That's the way it should be. The deal is, we shouldn't try to be the FWO. They're a rarity.



AS a whole, our roster can't put in an A+ effort for weekly cards. Not even for OT, where we're just asking for one rp and a match. No complaints here....just stating fact. As people we've grown. Time and effort for this game has to squeeze through an increasingly shrinking window. When we have time and go for it, the game still thrills us, but right now....let's be honest. This isn't a Chad and Steve went on vacation problem :) In 1996 it was, but we have help now :)



In the NFW, [name withheld] is doing a lion's share of work and credit to him, but I know he'd love help. I know he's gotta worry that one day he'll wake up and say, "I ain't write'in this weeks' card. I can't." Look at the week to week rp counts of the NFW. My point is backed up there.



At least in our neck of the woods, you can't make it on a 8 man roster.



"So what are you going to suggest this time Steve?"



Two options.



1) We go to monthly cards. One PT and one OT.



2) We take a short break to bring in a butt load of help. Our roster can't be anymore than 10 active members now. I'm saying, we go out there and find 15-20 more. In addition to the card writing team. A team in charge of booking/writing/web content.



"15-20? Are you insane?"



Assurdly so. But...that's not the point. Going by myself, and I know there are others...I don't have it in me anymore to put up the effort I did against [name withheld], and then Brunk (including writing the Main Event) on a monthly basis.



With the second option what I'm proposing is this....we book a little long term. Not results, but talent.



"Steve you work in December, but not Janurary."



With a big roster...we could work around guys schedules. Include them when they want to roll, but ensure that the league moves on while we get the best from everybody.



It's a theory..one that I'm not quite sure how it'd even work, but I know this...to get this league moving...we're going to have to have a writing team. Gonna have to bring in guys who want to help out.



That's step one. Whether you think the roster has to expand or not, you really can't argue the point.



I'm just thinking outloud here..and as always I could be way off base. But IMO....no matter what the CSWA looks like a month from now.....we need help.



This league is a little bigger now than Chad and I. The history. One day it will end. But I'm not ready for that to happen. And I know Chad's not .



That's why I'm suggesting we loosen up a bit and actively search for new people.



Any ideas? Am I way off base?

PRONTO. I would've done something to finish it, if you needed me to.

Ryan, Adler, Southern, Mayfield, Viper, Cruise, Rabesque, Hiroshi, Aho, Randalls, Miles, Bandit, Hornet, Windham and I know there's more...and there's new guys too.



-Regarding the A+ effort and a writing team:



JN thought Eddie Mayfield was written as a black man. Now before anyone jumps on that comment, it's all good and water under the bridge. Its now laughed about as most CSWA mishaps are. And believe me, I know it wasn't near the gaffes I've committed here...and I know I probably hold the top two.



JN has alot of writing (always, I know) for this PT, including the main event. PT in Anaheim actually came out a little closer on schedule, but JN and I definitely got a certain label of being nuts about stuff...at the same time this is because of details getting lost when we don't write things.



The card in Anaheim from the reviews was a big success.



So there's a question that CSWA has to ask itself and its the same question NFW did.



What level of quality do you want your PTs to be at?



If its even close to what is now...



Can you find a writing team that will accomplish this on time?



No offense I hope is taken by this...but I don't think the CSWA has had a writing team that could capture all the characters without handler involvement since [name withheld] and [name withheld] were on the team.



-The idea of letting guys when they want to work is close to the fWo, which is the best idea in your post. I don't know if it would work and I don't know if you'd need a writing team for it. It'd also sound like CSWA shifting to more of an angle fed than an RP one, which is another question...and whole different matter.



It would give people like JN and Evan a more suitable timeframe to work their magic.



At the same time, who knows who we'd lose in that too...cause cutting out Promo RPing might have a negative effect on some of our more traditional members...and I can't help, but think that's way it has to work if you move to a longer-term booking solution.



-ACW, a league I know from ewplanet.com just closed. They may have members looking to join a fed. From what I've heard, they had great writers there. Thought I'd sneak this in here.



-Writing Team: Can you really expect anyone new you bring in to be able to handle the more traditional stuff? While you and Chad obviously are busy people, I don't think you guys can just be hands off. The league is bigger than you two, but abandoning it to being written by someone that's been here for a month...that could cause problems too.



This is your league and I mean that in a way from how everything is booked. Unless you're willing to give up a certain amount of control over that I don't know how any of this works. You two are too busy to write results, but still want to be the judges of the important ones...and I'm not saying you shouldn't be the judges...and obviously, I don't care about results...but others may feel slighted, I dunno. It just seems weird that you guys would still be booking everything, while not having the time to participate every week.



-Don't let JN write main event matches you need in a week. He should probably be your test for the long-term writing. I'm sorry brother. It's true. I know how busy this man is and there was just no shot for PT this go-around. He is the best, but also one of the busiest. I think the problem here...along with other people is that sometimes they just won't admit this until the very end.
