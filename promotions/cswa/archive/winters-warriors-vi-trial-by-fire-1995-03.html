ERIC JACKSON vs. VINNIE MORTOLLA ********************************************************** Jackson has been calling for a title shot, any title shot; and after this match, he may be well on his way. Jackson was all over Mortolla after the early-going. Mortolla had a few shots at Jackson in the beginning, but after an eye gouge by Jackson, Mortolla was in trouble. Jackson caught Vinnie with an atomic drop and followed with a german suplex. Jackson didn't let up there, however, he hooked in an STF. Mortolla quickly grabbed the ropes but found himself being caught in a sleeper hold instead. Again, Mortolla fought his way out, but after an irish whip followed by a back drop, most of the fight was gone out of Mortolla. Jackson pulled him to his feet and then caught him with his finisher, the side suplex! And that was the three-count and the win for Jackson. And tonight he's looking for the win to push him even further up the rankings. He grabbed the ropes, breaking the figure four the Hunter had on him. With that, Crippler caught The Hunter with a belly to belly, following up with a slingshot suplex.
