Unified World Heavyweight Championship History

Unified World Heavyweight Championship History TITLE HISTORY On the PRODIGY service, the Unified Title has a history stretching back almost as long as their have been fantasy leagues. In actuality, the PRODIGY Unified World Championship history stretches as far back as the beginnings of some of those leagues. The PRODIGY Unified Championship did not originally begin as a single title. It was originally created, merging a conglomeration of league and superleague titles in order to create the Unified World Title. Below, you'll find a list of Unified Champions, as well as other notable moments from the history of the Unified Title. Date Event 10/24/1993 The IRONMAN of CHAMPIONS is held at the CSWA's LAST CALL II pay-per-view event. Participating in the event were AAWC World Champion Hot Shot, CSWA Unified World Champion Hornet, Tom Adler, IWCCW World Champion Flic Rair, Steven Flair, and Joey Melton. In the finals, Hornet defeated Joey Melton, merging the IWCCW World, AAWC World, and the ten-title CSWA Unified Championship, which also included the EN Superleague Title. 1/3/94 On the first stop of ULTRATOUR '94, the tour celebrating the 1994 ULTRATITLE Tournament, Hornet reunited the other half of the UWL Superleague Championship and added it and its subsequent World Championships to the Unified World Title by defeating Polaris in Charleston, SC. 1/17/94 The AR Superleague Championship and its subsequent World Championship were merged into the Unified Championship when Hornet defended the title successfully against Billy Freedom, the AR Superleague Champion. June 1994 During a dispute with some of the member federations over the state of the Unified Championship, Hornet handed the title over to the authorities, retiring it and causing a tournament to be held. 7/15/94 The sixteen-man Unified World Tournament began to decide the new Champion. Mark Windham, the previous top contender, was banned from the tournament because of a vicious attack on Jack Roiter. The first two rounds were held. 9/5/94 The Tournament concluded on Labor Day weekend. The Final Four was comprised of Patrick W. York, Hornet, Mike Roiter, and a masked man known as "Mr. Magnificent." The night of the tournament, York was involved in a serious car accident which ended his career. Also, Hornet was viciously attacked by Zack Sirus, making him unable to compete in the finals. The semifinal match between Roiter and "Mr. Magnificent" became the final. "Mr. Magnificent" defeated Roiter and unmasked himself, revealing "The Living Legend" Mark Windham, whose contract dispute had been settled just minutes earlier. 9/24/94 Hornet reclaimed the Unified World Title in a two-out-of-three falls encounter against Mark Windham. In the third fall, Windham attacked Mickey Benedict, an old rival who was commentating at ringside. Benedict tapped Windham on the head with a microphone, while Hornet, unaware of what had happened, rolled Windham up for a three-count. 3/20/95 Two days after their meeting inside an electrified steel cage at Winter's Warriors V, Mike Randalls and Hornet meet again, due to a contract stipulation that Randalls pulled out of his contract. While Hornet barely won their title match by sending Randalls into the cage two nights before, Randalls had been able to hamper with injuries from the previous match. Randalls defeated Hornet in a relatively easy match, becoming the Unified Champion. 5/13/95 At the CSWA's Battle of the Belts XI, Mike Randalls loses the title to wrestling superstar JT Tyler, who announces his plans to take the Unified Title on the "Consummate Tour." July 1995 On the Consummate Tour, JT Tyler loses a match to Tsunami, who at the age of 20, becomes the youngest Unified Champion ever. July 1995 Just two weeks after winning it, Tsunami competes in the second IRONMAN of CHAMPIONS. He is mathematically eliminated early. In the final match, GUNS and Hornet compete, with the Unified Title on the line. During the match, GUNS sends Hornet over the top, changing the momentum, and almost securing the match and title for himself. But Hornet, unwilling to let GUNS win the title, tosses GUNS over the top himself, getting disqualified. GUNS wins the match, but does not receive enough points to win the IRONMAN. Alexandr Karelin wins the IRONMAN and the Unified Title. August 1995 Alexandr Karelin falls to former ally Steven Flair, who, along with the aid of Teri "Ms. Hooters" Melton, becomes the newest Unified Champion, adding to his trophy case one of the few belts he had not held. October 1995 In a grudge match between these two old rivals, Hornet becomes a three-time Unified Champion by pinning Steven Flair. Flair had defeated Hornet just months earlier at the IRONMAN of CHAMPIONS. Thanksgiving Weekend 1995 In a match full of controversy on both sides, Hornet defends the Unified Championship successfully, getting a pinfall win over "Mr. Magnificent" Tom Adler, and capturing the AAWC Universal Championship. January 1996 In a match with "Antichrist" Jim Williams, Hornet is beaten with a branding iron to his back, and then worked over by three men. Earlier in the night, he had been accidently hit with the EN World Title by former foe turned friend GUNS. Doctors rule Hornet out of action for six weeks due to inflamed and swelling discs in his back. He is forced to retired the Unified Title due to an announcement by the Unified Championship Committee. March 1996 The 1996 ULTRATITLE Tournament is concluded. "Golden Boy" Paul Pierce is named not only ULTRATITLE Champion but Unified Champion, after beating out 127 other men to win the title, including Jack Samson and Julius Godreign, among others. July 20, 1996 After having defended the Unified Title against top contenders in various leagues, Paul Pierce drops a bombshell announcement at Winter's Warriors VI. He announces his intent to retire the Unified Title and return to his 'roots.' Paul Pierce is not heard from again....at least not until near the end of the year. September 1996 The five-man Unified Championship Committee announces that the vacant Unified Championship will be put up for grabs in the IRONMAN of CHAMPIONS tournament. Twenty of the top contenders to the Unified Championship are offered a shot to battle each other and make the cut to five, where they will particpate in an IRONMAN round-robin tournament, with the Unified Title on the line. December 15, 1996 The IRONMAN of CHAMPIONS continues, narrowing down the twenty man field to five, including "Rookie of the Year" Mark Windsor, 1996 ULTRATITLE Final Four member Julius Godreign, former EN World Champion GUNS, and former Unified Champions Mike Randalls and Hornet. The five men begin the round-robin portion of the IOC. January 21, 1997 The IRONMAN of CHAMPIONS concludes with Mike Randalls becoming the new PRODIGY Unified World Champion by defeating the entire field except for Julius Godreign, including two pinfall wins over GUNS and Hornet.
