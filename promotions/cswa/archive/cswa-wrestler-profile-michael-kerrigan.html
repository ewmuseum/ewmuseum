CSWA Wrestler Profile: "The Dragon" Michael Kerrigan Name: Michael Kerrigan Hometown: Toronto, Ontario, Canada Height: 5'11" Weight: 225 Age: 25 Alignment: Neutral Music: 'Real Solution #9' by White Zombie Biography/Profile: APPEARANCE: Caucasian male, in good physical condition and with short black hair. BACKGROUND: Michael 'the Dragon' Kerrigan was born and trained in Toronto. Began wrestling, during his early school years and even made it to the National Finals. Got the name 'Dragon' his frist year of professional competition. When he began, using FIRE against his opponents. From that point he has wrestled in Canada, Mexico and Japan. He has always had an utter dislike, for the American wrestling scene. But now things have changed and he once again returns... CHARACTERISTICS: Solid mat wrestler, combined with a touch of hardcore daredevil. Not afraid to get 'extreme', but would rather wrestle. PERSONALITY: In the locker room, he is very friendly and easy going. Working with many charities, including Special Olympics and the Free Tibet Foundation. However in the ring and in front of the camera, he is all business. RING ATTIRE: Red and white pants, made to resemble the Canadian flag [with the maple leaf, on the left pant leg]. Or... Straight black pants, with red trim and a red dragon wrapped around the left leg. RING ENTRANCE: Very straight forward, nothing fancy [unless stated otherwise]. Previous Titles Held: Moves: 1. Dragon Suplex 2. Dragon Screw Leg Whip 3. Ocean Cyclone Suplex 4. Power Bomb 5. Tombstone Piledriver 6. Implant DDT 7. Death Valley Driver 8. Top Rope Elbow Drop 9. Shooting Star Press 10. Moonsault 11. Northern Lights Bomb Finisher: Dragon Sleeper -- Reverse Sleeper Hold, sometimes also adds a leg scissors. Handler's Name: [name withheld] Handler's Email: [detail withheld]
