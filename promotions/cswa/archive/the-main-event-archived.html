When Brothers Attack...Again Mark Windham vs. Timmy Windham IRONMAN of CHAMPIONS IV for the CSWA World Championship As part of CSWA Co-Commissioner Chad Merritt's possibly-final act, Hornet was stripped of the CSWA World Championship. After some wrangling between the two commishes, the IRONMAN was announced. It will feature three former CSWA World Champions, two former United States Champions, and a perennial top contender, as the best in the world collide in a semi-round-robin styled tournament. Each man will face four of the other competitors. Whichever man can come out at the end of the night, having wrestled four grueling matches, will be named IRONMAN and crowned Champion. The competitors are: Deacon Mark Vizzack Hornet Eddy Love Kevin Powers Eli Flair Major Events BATTLE of the BELTS 16 saw the crowning of new champions in the first PPV of 1999! ELVIS LIVES XI saw the crowning of brand new champions in 1999!
