"Corporate Raider" Ellis Jackson Name: "Corporate Raider" Ellis Jackson Hometown: New York, NY Height: 6'3" Weight: 254 Age: 29 Alignment: Heel Music: "Money Talks" by AC/DC Biography/Profile: Appearance: Ellis Jackson is in his late twenties. Always dressed in some expensive Italian suit, he could pass for any other corporate CEO if not for his huge neck. His hair is conservatively cut and his eyes are fierce and hawk-like. His smiles are not warm or reassuring, but rather like a lion assessing its prey. Personality: Ellis Jackson is a corporate predator. This makes him an extreme opportunist who is willing to exploit whatever situation to get what he wants. He has no qualms about cheating or passing bribes. Mentally, he views himself as superior to EVERYONE. HE is 99% EGO. Gimmicks: Jackson's presence in the ring is usually heralded by a large pyrotechnic explosion and a shower of one-hundred dollar bills from the ceiling of the arena. However, upon closer inspection, the face of George Washington has been replaced by the grim visage of Ellis Jackson, "the Corporate Raider". This usually upsets greedy fans quite a bit. History: Ellis Jackson was born the second son of Randall and Margaret Jackson. Being the son of an incredibly wealthy oil baron, Ellis became accustomed to having his way early on in life. If there were a toy he wanted, he'd get it. If someone picked on him at school, the bully's family would suddenly have to move away. If he failed a test, it would curved to the point of giving him an 'A'. Little changed as he grew older. His years at Harvard were a farce, going only to socialize and flaunt his endless wealth. He graduated at the top of his class with a B.A. in business which he had scarcely earned. He was then given a Vice President's position at JacksonCo, his father's now-booming mega-corporation. The years that followed were a daze of fast cars, fast women, and hard substance abuse. There was little his job offered him but huge amounts of money, and, though he excelled at spending it, he was left feeling empty and unfulfilled. It was only chance that led him to accidentally switch to a televised wrestling event while he searched fruitlessly for "Wall Street Week." It was amazing. He watched as "white trash" icons beat themselves silly on national television. He stood up as if possessed and called his personal trainer. The next day, he began a year-long regiment of weight-lifting, running, and nutrition supplements. He spent more time in the gym than he did the boardroom, and it paid off. Finally, in June of 1999, "the Corporate Raider" entered the W.I.W. as a contender for the World Championship belt. Weary with oppressing the under-class from the boardroom, he's taking a new "hands-on" approach: beating them senseless in the ring. Known for his crippling trademark move, "the Hostile Takeover," he's already found a niche in the World Internet Wrestling federation. He is the "Corporate Raider" Ellis Jackson.
