Cat vs. Henderson Bramble “The One” Phil Dynasty vs. Carl Brigsby Troy Douglas vs. Michael Gettis Suicide vs. Aelieas Fierte Cleaver O'Connor vs. Kalas MAIN EVENT: Nathan Cross vs. Shamon For the new folks, here's a quick rundown. You'll be responsible for writing your match (all of them are 'jobber' matches except the Main Event), as well as one on-card roleplay segment. The match should be in what we call short-form (novel, etc.).
All matches and segments should be sent to [detail withheld]. Questions can be sent there as well. Steve will probably send some additional pointers via e-mail.
We're aiming for a quick turnaround here, since six of the matches are jobber matches. Try to turn your info in by Monday at possible... if it's not possible, drop an e-mail to that [detail withheld] address and let us know what your timeline looks like, or whether you need help.
Some of the 'jobbers' above are listed on the 'non-public' profile section at http://cswawrestling.com/profiles/newindex.php. Paige, Bramble and Brigsby are standard jobbers, you can search the site and get an idea of how to play them if you need it.
That's all I can think of at the moment. Warren and Mad Dog, you have the Main, so figure out what you want to do and make it as involved as you want.
