Don't like the official CSWA rankings? Vote for your own. Each time the rankings are updated, the top 15 or so wrestlers will have a polling box next to their name. You vote on which order they should be in...and we'll keep score. To vote: 1) You'll be voting for your top ten. Choose your personal #1 contender, select the "1-Top" in his polling box, and click "Vote!" 2) You will be taken to the Rankings Interactive results page. Click "Back" on your browser to return here, choose your personal #2, select the "2" in his polling box, and click "Vote!" 3) Continue steps 1 and 2 until you have picked your personal CSWA Top 10. Note: You may vote as many times as you'd like. However, to keep things even, please vote for an entire top 10 before starting over at #1. (In other words, don't vote ten times for a number one or a number ten. You'll dilute the poll by doing so.)
