CSWA Archives CSWA Archives This page is your link to various pages dealing with the CSWA's recent history. We promised that this page would grow, and guess what, it now has! Below, you'll find some card recaps, as well as some entire card transcripts. The Archives are not inclusive, that is, we haven't included every card in the past year or so.....but there are enough to keep you quite busy! We just added some classic cards from the second half of 1994, and we'll be updating the Archives with more recent cards very soon. Also, if you have any old CSWA cards sitting around on your hard drive or in your BBNM that aren't transcripted here, let us know. With the death of Chad's laptop, we've lost quite a bit of what we had for the past year and a half. Also, if you have any ideas or suggestions for the Archives, mail us at [detail withheld] If you want to see more of the best of the past of the CSWA, check out the truly old stuff from the CSWA's eight year history by heading to the CSWA Classic Pages. Featured Archive Winter's Warriors VI: Trial By Fire You could easily say that many of the feuds and follies over the past year began at one card, March 1995's WW6. This card featured Mark Windham vs. Mickey Benedict in an electrified steel cage match, as well as Hornet vs. Mike Randalls in a steel cage for the Unified World Championship! For those shocking matches and more, including the 1995 ULTRATITLE Final Four, check out this transcript. CSWA SuperPrimeTime in Miami From 2/15/96, this great card featured GUNS vs. Joey Melton for the EN World Championship, as well as the Horsemen vs. the Cutters for the Unified Tag Team Titles. BATTLE OF THE BELTS XIII: Super Bowl Blast '96 This 2/3/96 supercard freatures some huge championship matches, including the Dream Team vs. Horsemen for the Unified Tag Titles, and the DEATH ISLAND EN World Championship Rematch between GUNS and Mike Randalls. CSWA Showtime at the Meadowlands The first SHOWTIME of 1996 featured great matches, including: Alexandr Karelin vs. "Stylin" Steven Flair, "Lethal" Lex Taylor vs. Scotty Michaels, Jim Williams vs. Mike Randalls, Disco Express and the Disco Midget vs. < > Cutters and the Red Midget! CSWA SuperPrimetime: I Have A Dream Some of the biggest matches of the year took place at this MLK card, with six huge championship matches, including a Triangle Match for the EN World Title, and the MAIN EVENT, Hornet vs. Jim Williams for the Unified Championship. And there's much, much more! A CSWA Christmas: Dreams Really Do Come True This final card of 1995 features the aftermath brawl from the EN IRONMAN, as well as Mike Chain vs. Lex Taylor, Mike Flair vs. Wild Asian and more! EN World Championship IRONMAN Tournament The first half of A CSWA Christmas is transcripted here. Watch as GUNS, Mike Randalls, Alexandr Karelin, Steven Flair, and Mark Windham battle for the World's Title! CSWA SUPERPRIMETIME in Decatur: Christmas At Ground Zero Featuring: a Return Grudge Match between GUNS and Mark Windham, Alexandr Karelin and Steven Flair in a roofed steel cage, "Poopman" Bob Praxis vs. Bandit, and a twenty-man battle royal for the CSWA Presidential Championship! 12/24/95. CSWA SHOWTIME in Chicago 12/20/95 SHOWTIME featuring: the end of MIDGET MANIA in the CSWA, with the 20-midget Under the Top Rope Battle Royal, Joey Melton vs. Steven Flair, the Dream Team vs. the Horsemen for the Unified Tag Titles and much more! CSWA SHOWTIME in Las Vegas 10/29/95 SHOWTIME featuring: NC-17 vs. Crazy Like A Slacker in a non-title matchup; Tsunami vs. Joey Melton; Scotty Michaels vs. Johnny Tropic. MAIN EVENT: Hornet vs. Mike Randalls. SuperPRIMETIME in Des Moines: Horror In The Heartland: Features Adler/Randalls in a Rematch "I Quit" Match, ARROGANCE vs. NC-17 for the US Tag Titles, Mark Windham vs. Troy Windham for the USN Title, Hornet vs. Scott Waters and more! BATTLE OF THE BELTS XII: The Midgets Are Coming!: This CSWA PPV featured ALL the belts on the line. Featured matches include Hornet vs. Karelin for the Top Contender spot, Flair/Waters for the Unified, ARROGANCE/NC-17 for the US Tag Titles, and Tom Adler vs. Mike Randalls in a cage for the EN/AAWC World Title. CSWA SHOWTIME in Baltimore: Here's the inaugural CSWA SHOWTIME from 10/4/95 in Baltimore featuring Steel Viper/Cyborgus, Chain/Windham, Flair/Bonecrusher, and Melton/Hornet. SuperPRIMETIME in Portland: The Wild, Wild West Here's the recap from the 10/3/95 SPT in Portland featuring Karelin/BC, Windham/Windham and much more! ELVIS LIVES CELEBRATION IX: Disney Days Be sure to check out the recap of the CSWA's biggest PPV event ever! You can get the summary of the 100-post event by clicking above. September 1995. CSWA SuperPrimetime in Washington: Until They All Come Home This August 8, 1995 SuperPrimetime was a tribute to all the United States Vietnam vets. It featured a huge card, including: the kickoff of "Magnificence Tour II" with Tom Adler vs. Mike Roiter; the continuation of the GUNS/Hornet feud, Mark Windham vs. Bonecrusher, Alexandr Karelin vs. Cyborgus and more! AAWC's Under New Ownership The AAWC's final card occurred on August 5, 1995, after the sale of the promotion to CS Enterprises by President Robert Schmid. Schmid, however, didn't count on having to wrestle in one final match....a Tuxedo Match against Teri Melton! This card also included: the Armed Forces vs. ARROGANCE, the CS Express vs. the < > Cutters, and the MAIN EVENT, Tom Adler vs. GUNS for the AAWC Universal and EN World Championships! FISH FUND X Recap Includes all the results from one of the CSWA's last PPVs, the huge 77-post FISH FUND X: Mine Eyes Have Seen The Glory... June 1995. SUPER BOWL BLAST '95 Before WW6, there was Super Bowl Blast '95. Before the Hornet/Randalls feud at Winter's Warriors VI, there was the WHEEL OF DEATH match. Click above to see one of the greatest stip matches ever and more! SuperPrimeTime in Atlanta This great kickoff card of 1995 features Mike Randalls vs. Tsunami for the EN World Title, as well as Mark Windham vs. Bonecrusher! Primetime in San Diego One of the final cards of 1994, occurring on 12/19/94 to be exact, saw an incredible battle between Timmy Windham and Mike Randalls for the EN World Championship. It also includes Hornet being HANGED! To find out whodunit, check out this great archive. Thanksgiving Weekend Spectacular '94 Mark Windham quitting the CSWA? Ray S. Cornette taking over the Board of Directors? All that, and some great wrestling action happened right around Thanksgiving in 1994. Check out this archive to see it all. 11/27/94. Battle of the Belts X: The Fall Classic BOB X, on 11/12/94, included the beginning of an incredible title history. The original Enterprise World Championship Tournament is included here in its entirety. And along with it, Hornet vs. Mike Roiter, in a battle that saw the Roiter/Windham feud escalate. FISH FUND IX: Battle On The Bayou Unfortunately, only the first 12 or so posts of this card were recoverable, from way back on 9/1/94. But even in that small amount, you'll get to see part of the Unified World Championship Tournament that followed Hornet being stripped of the title. Look for more here in the near future!
