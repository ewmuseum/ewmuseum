Thread starter Chad Start date Jun 1, 2012 Chad The Godfather Jun 1, 2012 #1 Slow down there, buddy. Are you sure? This is the place that dreamed up ridiculousness like the "Mighty Morphin Power Midgets" and "The Death Island Match." We let ELI FLAIR be the champ for a while of all people. But if you're sure, then it's easy -- just drop a PM, post here or whatever works for you. Once the new site is revealed, there will be an application form built in. For now we're just counting hands and trying to figure out who is in and out. If you were previously a member and want back in, just go post in the Member Discussion forum that you should still have access to. The CSWA is a "hybrid" league -- meaning that we value competitive roleplay but also mix it up with some pre-booked action to further angles and the broader story.
