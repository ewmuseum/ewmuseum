CSWA Wrestler Profile: Apocalypse Name: Apocalypse Hometown: White Sands, NM Height: 6'9" Weight: 312 lbs Age: 28 Alignment: Heel Entrance Music: "Wherever I May Roam" by Metallica & the San Francisco Symphony Orchestra Manager: Miso Biography/Profile: Little is known about the man they call Apocalypse, but as each day passes a small mystery continues to unfold. Coming from a early childhood which saw all of his bloodline die due to 'supernatural' causes, Apocalypse was basically on his own for much of his life. Taking the investments and funds that were set aside for him, Apocalypse managed to hit big with the stock market and mutual funds and from the average man's aspect...he's set for life. After his graduation from college he decided to run away from it all and bury himself within the Far East mainly of South Korea and Japan. During this time it is believed that is where he met up with The South Korean Vixen Nightmare Miso whom he is still with to this very day. Also during this time it is said that he met and helped 'Good God' Kevin Powers, then in the military, set forth in the wrestling ranks. After that it becomes common knowledge. Once back in the states he went to the SWWA in which he won his first title, the South West Wrestling Alliance World Belt, by defeating his opponents in a series of World Title brackets. His most challenging win was against his long time rival Ego Buster. Once the league closed he then moved to Mile High Wrestling where, in time, he won the National Title against Johnny Pyro. He also found his way to WoW where he defeated 'Icenam' Steve Radder for the World Title. Never able to defend those SWWA World Title, WoW World Title, HEW World Title, MHW National Title, TCW Regional Title, CSWA Tag Titles due to those leagues closing as well he then moved to HEW where he won the World Title in a stunning and vicious victory over HaVoC, but lost it a month later at the hands of The Unholy Father. Along with being in the NthWA and Total Championship Wrestling, Apocalypse also ventured to the CSWA where, to this day, he is still the CSWA Unifed Tag Champion with Kevin Powers. Dressed in a black body suit with the sleeves cut off he also wears black with blue lighting bolt incribed leather boots that come up past his knees and out. With his signature blue hair Apocalypse is one of the most feared wrestlers in the wrestling community and has earned the reputation of destroying those that dare to walk in his Path of Destruction.
