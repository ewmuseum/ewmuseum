Unified Tag Tournament Announcement Thread starter Chad Start date Dec 30, 2012 Chad The Godfather Dec 30, 2012 #1 The CSWA is shockingly back. Even more shockingly, it's announced the return of the tag division. The Unified Tag Championships are back... with an important twist. Once the tag champs have successfully defended the titles at least three times, they can claim a UNIFIED World Championship (singles title) shot for both members of the team. But to do so, they have to vacate the tag titles. This "express pass" to the UNIFIED title has already encouraged a likely duo to team up -- Justin Voss and Scott Riktor claimed the top seed in the tournament by joining forces and taking down Team VIAGRA at CSWA: BLUE MOON. Will other wrestlers team up to try and do the same? This is an open call for the tag tournament. We're working on filling slots -- once they're gone, they're gone. We're looking for 8 teams, but could expand it if needed. A few teams are already determined by current CSWA members. If you want in, please post here or PM me so I can get you on the list. The matches up to the final will take place on the first 3 or so cards as we get started back. -Chad Jesse Ramey New member Dec 30, 2012 #2 If you're accepting singles to be randomly paired with other people, then count Jesse Ramey in.
