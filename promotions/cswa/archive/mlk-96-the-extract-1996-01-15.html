But finally some help is coming down the aisle. Or is it? Alexandr Karelin is on his way down to ring side. He says something to Cyborgus, and these two former allies have a short conversation. It looks like Karelin is going to get his shots in on Hornet as well! He raises his foot...... BB: But instead he decks Cyborgus with a right hand! He follows up with a knee to the midsection, and then gets hold of the chair Cyborgus had and drops him with that! Inside the ring, Williams has draped Tropic over one shoulder and is skulking out through the crowd! Karelin motions for the paramedics, and Cyborgus takes off after his "leader," Williams. This is terrible. Hornet still hasn't moved since that bear hug from Cyborgus. He's taken a shot from a championship belt from GUNS, in addition to his match with Williams, an axehandle blow to Tropic, and an assortment of kicks and blows from a branding iron! The paramedics are finally on their way down with a stretcher, and two trainers are out examining Hornet. GUNS has finally made his way down the aisle, and he's talking to the trainers, as well as with Karelin. What a night of action we've had, new Unified Tag Team Champions, a new EN World Champion.
