Thanks For Waiting!

22 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Chad — 12-22-02, 02:28 AM Just a quick note to fans and members alike:

Thanks for bearing with me over the last two months or so. As most of you know, I was jobless for a large majority over the year. That's what happens when the company you work shuts down, and you live in an area where unemployment goes over 7.5%!

Thankfully, waiting for the right opportunity paid off, and I'm at a place that I've enjoyed immensely so far. But as many of you know, starting a new job takes some getting used to (not to mention trying to keep up with an eighteen-month old and everything else).

But with all that taken care of (and the checking account slightly recovered) it's time to get things going.

PRIMETIME is up, ON TIME in Charleston will go up in the next couple of days, and then we get back to major action. The lineup for SHOWTIME will go up soon, and challenges will open for ON TIME in Norfolk.

Let me know if you have any questions, and feel free to submit articles for the TRIBUNE, as well as Fan Reports.

-C
