Steve Radder Kevin Powers Eddy Love Mike Randalls Havoc Lawrence Stanley None of the above View Results UPDATED 1/19/01 IRONMAN Scoreboard CSWA INTERACTIVE CSWA Mailing List Information Features Details on Flair's Rehiring In much the same way as Commissioner Merritt dealt with Mike Randalls' contract, Merritt renegotiated Eli Flair's contract. In return for rehiring the former CSWA World Champion, Merritt has put in a clause that allows for the immediate release of Flair and non-payment of his contract if Flair is involved in an unprovoked physical confrontation with any CSWA staff or fans. Ivy has negotiated a similar clause to the one protecting Hornet and Mike Randalls, allowing that Flair cannot be suspended for action other than stated above. Randalls Salary Cut? The TRIBUNE has learned that as a result of his agent's negotiation, Mike Randalls' CSWA contract guaranteed salary was cut in half in return for lifting his recent suspension. This unprecedented move by CSWA Commissioner Chad Merritt will apparently allow the former Unified World Champion to continue competing in the IRONMAN of CHAMPIONS. Randalls currently trails in IOC5 with zero points and one loss. Confused Yet? PRIMETIME in Little Rock seemed to be an exercise in confusion. Was Randalls suspended? Apparently not anymore. Wasn't Eli Flair fired? He's not now. And did Eddy Love really see... nope, we're not even going to fuel such speculation here in the TRIBUNE. SHOWTIME in Memphis is scheduled to be aired on television later this week. If Randalls isn't suspended, does that mean Powers will show up, even suffering from reasonably severe injuries? And what about Havoc's latest injury report? I guess there's only one television cliche that fits... stay tuned for more... same Bat-time... same Bat-channel. Happy New Year! The CS TRIBUNE wishes all its readers a very merry New Year as we step into 2001. In latest CSWA news, CSWA manager Poison Ivy continues her discussion with CSWA Commissioner Chad Merritt in efforts to get the firing of Eli Flair overturned. Controversy continues to surround the IRONMAN of CHAMPIONS. With Mike Randalls suspended due to his post-match attack on Kevin Powers, news has recently come to light that IOC competitor Havoc may also be under doctors' orders not to wrestle. We'll continue to update you as we approach round two of the IOC at SHOWTIME, coming up later this week. Other Recent Headlines: Eli Flair FIRED! IOC In Shambles? PLR Stands Tall?
