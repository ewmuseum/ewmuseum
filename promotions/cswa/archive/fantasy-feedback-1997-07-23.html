Fantasy Feedback Updated July 23, 1997 Fantasy Feedback is your place to make your voice heard about going-on in Fantasy Wrestling. Below you'll find various questions that require your feedback. Whether it's just clicking on a dot, or writing an essay, we want to know what you think. Feedback will be updated as often as we can do it, with various issues. Once you've answered as many of the questions as you'd like, please be sure to click on the "Send My FEEDBACK" box below. On various issues, we'll post the results the next time the questions are updated. I don't want to throw all of you into shock, but FANTASY FEEDBACK has been updated, in a timely manner even! Below the current questions, you'll find the results of last month's two big questions. Be sure to vote for the current questions so that we can have your opinion! The two questions at the very bottom, regarding Special Events and the FW Web Pages will remain there, so that we can continue to receive your great ideas. The goal in Fantasy Wrestling is to build a character. To the right, fill in your choices for the superlatives listed. Be sure to think about 'character' rather than just personal likes and dislikes? (For example, on "Most Popular", put which character on the boards in your opinion is most well-liked and received by the fans and *Professional Wrestling overall. For "Most Hated", who draws the most heel heat and gets everybody's blood boiling?) Most Popular Most Hated Favorite Announcer Best Manager/Valet Who's your pick to win the Robert W. Ryder Invitational Tag Team Tournament? (Be sure to check out the official web page for the Ryder Tag Tournament Unified Rankings page available here.) Your pick for the first Ryder Tag Tournament Winners? Results of Previous Feedback Questions From June 30th "Devastating" Mike Randalls is the newest Unified World Champion. In his current second reign as Unified Champ, how would you rate Randalls' performance so far? The majority of you who answered chose "The best yet" or "As good as any champ around" as your opinion of Randalls' title reign. In fact, the overwhelming majority of 75% chose Randalls as above average or higher. Who's your pick for the top contender to the Unified Title? Who should Randalls face next? There were really only three wrestlers who stood out in this one, receiving over half the votes alone. The first two were Randalls' latest opponents for the Unified World Title, GUNS and Rocky Ford Meloneer. The other man, and the only one not to have received a title shot at Randalls yet, Julius Godreign. Over the past year, various Fantasy Wrestling Special Events have been held. They included a special "real" wrestler tournament last October, the 128-man 1996 ULTRATITLE Tournament in January through March, a failed attempt at a board-wide Hall of Fame this summer, the IRONMAN of CHAMPIONS that concluded early this year, and, of course, the Robert W. Ryder Invitational Tag Team Tournament which is about to begin. What other SPECIAL EVENTS would you like to see in the future? Also over the past year, the Fantasy Wrestling Web Pages have continued to grow by leaps and bounds. FW Central, the Unified Championship Pages, replete with graphics and updates continue to be the backbone of the FW Pages. In the box below, give us your feedback on the pages. Are they informative? Are they fun? What other types of pages and information would you like to see? Would you like to see a Fantasy "want ads" page? In depth looks at leagues or wrestlers? Let us know your comments below. That's all for the current Fantasy FEEDBACK. Get your votes in so we can hear your opinions. We'll post a new set of questions after we get a significant response from this one. If you have any ideas for questions or topics on the next FEEDBACK, let us know. If you have an idea for an Interactive question, let us know by clicking one of the mailbox icons above and writing a message to us. Click here to return to the Fantasy Wrestling Web Page.
