Cards: 1988 1988 Note: In 1988, 'cards' were set up for 'major events.' This was before the CSWA had PPV events, etc. All were held in the "Merritt Auditorium" (i.e., the dilapidated warehouse). Keep in mind that many other matches were held, just not as a part of official 'cards.' For more information, be sure to check out "The Real Story" and other info. Be sure to check out the very first CSWA card ever. It's been put into written format and posted as part of the CSWA History pages.
