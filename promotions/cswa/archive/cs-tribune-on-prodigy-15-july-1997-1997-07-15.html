CS TRIBUNE: The CSWA On The Web Last updated on July 15, 1997 Best viewed with and That’s right, peons, your favorite wrestling announcer has his own column. Sammy Benson here, and for those of you who have gotten this far with your limited reading skills, hold on, ‘cause it’s gonna be a bumpy ride! First of all, let me say I'm shocked, no dismayed at what happened to me just a week ago! I, Samuel L. Benson, was cuffed and dragged out of the arena like an animal. Now, under different circumstances I'm not opposed to being cuffed. But, this..this was uncalled for! I am not a crook! I didn't kill RED, sure I hated the little guy, but I didn't kill him! For the past week, I've been stuck here, already convicted of a crime I didn't commit! I'm risking a lot for even trying to get this story to you, but I know how much you depend on my column. The worst part of it all, is that I'm pretty sure I took a shower next to Doc Silver's mother today! My lawyers are working around the clock to insure that justice is served. And Buckley, I got your gift today. Soap?! That's hilarious... I'm not sure how or why this happened. But, I can't stay here! I'm innocent! The fat woman!!! I tell you, she's out there! I was with her, no I'm not happy about it, but when my face is plastered everywhere with signs that read, "Do not serve" what am I suppose to do?! Please..for the love of all that's good and pure someone help me... (DISCLAIMER: The views of Sammy Benson do not necessarily represent the views of the CS TRIBUNE, the CSWA, or those of CS Enterprises.) New World Champion CSWA SUPERPRIMETIME in San Antonio crowned a new World Heavyweight Champion! In a match that will no doubt go down as a classic, "The King of Slackers" Troy Windham defeated GUNS. Windham, although hated by many, may have just ushered in a new era of wrestling. The end of the match, saw just about everyone under contract try their best to effect the outcome. While he hasn't even been back in the CSWA for a full week, 3-time UNIFIED World Heavyweight Champion HORNET has already made his presence felt. If you didn't see SUPERPRIMETIME the first time, be sure and catch it later this week on U-62. Unified Champ Comes Home JT Tyler, a former Unified Champion, shocked the wrestling world by returning to the CSWA. Although, Commissioner Merritt did not release contract terms, he in his own words said "I gave him what he wanted". The former Navy Seal, didn't have much to say about his whereabouts over the last two years, but one thing is for certain he's ready to make up for lost time. Tyler wasted no time in returning to action, as he and close friend HORNET got it on. The match resulted in the return of the Alliance. Has the case been cracked? Sammy Benson was arrested during the card in Orlando. Is he guilty? Was he framed? Is he going to jail? Click the headline above for the latest information. Key West--The CSWA heads to Key West, Florida for the return of SHOWTIME! One of the most popular shows in CSWA history, is back after a little retooling. "Dream Match" votes from fans around the world have been pouring in! Stay tuned to Bill Buckley's CSWA Update for the entire lineup, but the Main Event has already been signed. "The Untouchable" Julius Godreign defends his US Heavyweight belt against number one contender Shane Southern. FRIDAY NIGHT FURY! The debut of Friday Night Fury, was a tremendous success! Merritt has made a few moves that have come under question, but U-62 executives were thrilled with the 18.8 rating of Fury. The eight-man tournament to crown the new Intracontinental Champion, has been cut in half. This week's show will feature the Final Four, along with other marquee matches.
