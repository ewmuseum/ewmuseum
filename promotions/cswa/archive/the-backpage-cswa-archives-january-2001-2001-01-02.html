The Backpage: CSWA Archives Welcome to "The Backpage," the home of the CSWA's world-famous archives, featuring a small part of the CSWA's eleven-year heritage. While much of the CSWA's cards from pre-television days have been lost, now existing only on paper, much of the storied-tradition is available here! Starting with cards from 1994, check out the CSWA from yesteryear all the way up until yesterday. For the most recent cards, be sure to check out the TRIBUNE's Main Event.
