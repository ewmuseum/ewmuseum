BRET HART INJURED Reports are circulating that Bret Hart has suffered a knee injury that will require immediate surgery. Hart plans to work the "In Your House" PPV and will be at RAW this Monday, but will have the surgery immediately following. The knee surgery will keep Hart out of action in the WWF for a minimum of 6-8 weeks. Other reports indicate that Hart may also take this opportunity to have a long-standing wrist injury surgically corrected as well. The World Wrestling Federation has issued no official comment. The reported main event matchup for Slamboree continues to be announced as the nWo's Kevin Nash, Scott Hall, and Syxx against the team of Ric Flair, Roddy Piper and Carolina Panthers linebacker Kevin Greene. ECW PPV Buyrate The debut event of Extreme Championship Wrestling posted a surprisingly strong buy rate results as announced by Request, which exclusively distributed the wrestling event on Sunday, April 13. Despite being scheduled on one of the biggest sports television weekends of the year, up against boxing, golf and more, the ECW event is projected to produce between 50,000-60,000 buys in a universe of only 17 million pay-per- view homes, encompassing the cable and DBS universe.
