Steve — 06-09-03, 09:35 PM I'm still a co-owner here in spirit. So i'll throw up a post here. Feel free to walk around the body. Oh and just as a general alert to everyone. Haley Joel Osment has a little sister working in the film business. Be afraid. Be very afraid.



Since the league is making an effort to pick up speed and try to get on a roll, it wouldn't hurt to get everyone's ideas on what they'd like to see.



In the past the DREAM MATCH(s) have been fitted into a SHOWTIME lineup dependent on their sanity. I'm not about to open a SHOWTIME subject and write the card myself. But, maybe give Merritt a few of the matches and/or fueds you'd like to see develop. Might be fun to get a fresh take on who a character should be fueding with.



Me...the matches i'd most like to see:



1) Joey Melton vs Eddie Mayfield



2) HORNET vs Troy Windham.



It's probably been done 10 times already, but for some reason I just get the feeling it's ever been done right. Or on a large enough scale. If I"m wrong. Correct me as always. I'm not talking through tag team partners. I mean Hornet/Troy 1-on-1.



3) GUNS vs HORNET



Enough time has passed. Stop wasting more. Do it again.



4) Melton/Powers vs The Professionals



5) Adler vs Eli Flair



6) Mark Vizzack vs Stephen Thomas.



Grudge match for using Sunshine without permission. Doh!



7) Wildstar/Tsnumai/Eli Fair/Troy Windam. 4-corners match.



Or...take out one of the above and stick in Aho for a wicked change of pace.



8) GUNS and Sammy Benson vs Eli and Poison Ivy





I'm hesitant to suggest anymore. Number Nine would have been, "um dude that was on the last card."



If anyone's character wasn't brought up here, don't take it personally. It's not that you're not worth top billing. It's just I won't win any CSWA recent trivia. :-)



Looking forward to getting more comfortable with the newer guys.



Anyway, thoughts on these picks? Or your own?
