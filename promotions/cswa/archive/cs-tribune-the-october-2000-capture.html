Features INSIDER Don't miss "Breaking Kayfabe" below History's Over The aftermath of CSWA ANNIVERSARY 2000 was unprecedented. The history between Eli Flair and Mike Randalls said it shouldn't be probable. The history between Mike Randalls and Hornet said it shouldn't be possible. But it was. In the same night he took sole control of the CSWA and re-signed a superstar to a two-year contract, CSWA Commissioner Chad Merritt apparently lost control of the ship's tiller. An apparent triumverate of Randalls, Hornet and Flair in place along with Poison Ivy would be news anywhere. Add to it all the fact that their apparent target is Merritt, and you have something even bigger. The story of ANNIVERSARY may have been the emergence of the 'trio', but there were other emergents as well: Merritt was able to exercise some control during the evening, ousting Famine aka Cardigo Mysterian from the building in the middle of his match. Famine's wrestling history is well known on the independent scene, even encompassing a short run in the CSWA in the early 90s. Merritt's plan to keep The Dark Carnival out of the CSWA ended win Eddy Love gave assistance to his former tag team partner Kevin Powers, giving him the next shot at the CSWA World Championship. Aaron Douglas has already benefited by his association with the trio, winning the CSWA Presidential Title from Cameron Cruise when Hornet himself made the three-count. Wicked Sight may have inadvertently got the rub from the 'trio' as well. Instead of a match with newcomer Johnny Fizzbin, Sight once again held his own against a former World Champion, Eli Flair. Sight's stock continues to rise coming off the loss of the Greensboro Championship. Simply Stunning finally take the titles, making them the undisputed tag champs. Havoc turns a six-man loss at SHOWTIME into the beginning of a US Championship run at A2K. Nemesis returns to the ring to win his shot at the Greensboro Championship, while Sky Suicide makes a name for himself by breaking up the Cruise-Devon wedding. The CSWA enters a new 'era' following A2K. While the names of storied veterans like Love, Powers, Radder, Randalls, Hornet and Eli remain near the forefront of the talk, a whole new 'crop' of young superstars is determined to make sure their names are in lights. Swimming Upstream With CSWA ANNIVERSARY 2000 in the books, the CSWA begins its "Swimming Upstream 2000 Tour" sponsored by U-62 and "The Incredible Edible Egg." The Tour kicks off with PRIMETIME in Pensacola, where Kevin Powers and Deacon face off for the CSWA World Title. Also announced, A2K battle royal winner Nemesis and runner-up Sky Suicide will step into a three-way for the Greensboro Title against Evan Aho, whose A2K match with Cardigo Mysterian (Famine) was interrupted. CSWA's Drug Problem? The internet has talked for months about the apparent prevalence of drug use by some members of the CSWA locker room. Followed by the infamous "Sack on a Pole" match recently, major media outlets have begun delving deeper into the issue. Most recently, CSWA wrestler Nemesis was brought up on drug-related charges. A warrant for his arrest was issued when he failed to appear in court recently. Rumors have also run rampant regarding the strange actions of Unified Tag Team Champs Simply Stunning on their way to the titles at A2K. In a statement issued by CSWA Commissioner Chad Merritt earlier today, he states that all wrestlers will be subject to random drug-testing in the future, per their contracts. The results of these tests will be made available publicly. Merritt also advises that the CSWA and CS Enterprises' drug policy states that employees may be terminated immediately upon the finding of two positive drug tests. A positive result during any company-sponsored drug test is immediate followed by a second test. CSWA precedent shows that after one such incident, wrestlers are usually fined and suspended until a drug treatment program can be completed. If a second incident occurs, there is usually immediate termination. The only CSWA wrestler ever publicly fired for violating the drug policy was Alexis Vann in 1994. The current firestorm of controversy is the first to hit the CSWA in its twelve-year history.
