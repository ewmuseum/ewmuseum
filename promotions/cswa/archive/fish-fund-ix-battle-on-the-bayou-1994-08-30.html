FISH FUND IX: Battle On The Bayou September 1, 1994 Featuring: First two rounds of the Unified World Heavyweight Championship Tournament NOTE: This includes only the first 12 posts that were recoverable. :< =============================================================================== Time: Just before FISH FUND IX Place: 6th Floor, New Orleans City Hospital Director: OK, I want Camera 1 down the hall, Camera 2 in the room, and Camera 3 across the hall. Are we ready to start? Alright, ACTION! Cueman: OK people. "Jack Roiter: Tale Of An American Hero," take one! Reporter: Ladies and gentleman, this is July 15, 1994. We are here in a local hospital in New Orleans in order to see a true hero in action. Just less than two weeks ago, Jack Roiter was savagely attacked by a wrestler. Mr. Roiter already had neck problems before the attack. After the attack, the doctors told him he would never walk again. However, today, Jack Roiter is going to walk. Because he's what legends are made of. Because he's made of the stuff of heroes. And here he is, folks. (An orderly wheels Jack into the hall.) JR: Well, here goes nothing, folks. (He stands.) (The orderly tries to help him.) JR: I don't need help, youngster. I can do it. (He slowly begins to take a step.) Reporter: Here he goes folks. A man that doctors said would be confined to a wheelchair for the rest of his life. (Down the hall, two men are making loud noises as they come down the hall in wheelchairs.) Reporter: Slowly but surely, Jack Roiter is taking the first steps since his accident, steps that no one but himself thought he would ever take. (Down the hall a voice says): I'm gonna beat you, Timmy. This race is mine! Another voice: NO! ME will win! (The two wheelchairs crash into each other and begin spinning out of control.) Director: Hey! What the....? (The wheelchair with a rather large man in it come careening towards the set in the hall.) Director: Hey, we're making a documentary here, bub! Voice: Sorry! (The wheelchair keeps coming, cameramen dive out of the way.) Director: Look out, Jack! (But it's too late, the wheelchair with P. Vicious in it sideswipes Jack, sending him to the cold hard hospital floor, where he hits his head and hip.) JR: Somebody get a doctor, please..... (P. Vicious and the wheelchair go flying by, trying to catch up with Timmy Windham in the other down the hall.) Timmy: HA! ME win! ME get to end of hall first! PV: If you hadn't sideswiped me and made me hit that old man, I would've won! Doctor: Excuse me! What are you doing playing in wheelchairs on my hall!? Wait a minute! You! YOU! You're coming with me! I told you I'd find you! PV: NO! What are you doing here? I thought I left you at Green Valley?! You'll never catch me! At least not alive! Doctor: I never said it had to be alive, you lunatic! (P. Vicious takes off down the hall in his wheelchair, he ends up going down a stairwell and disappearing) BB: Hello wrestling fans!!! This is Bill Buckley here along with Sammy Benson. And this is: FISH FUND IX: Battle On The Bayou. We're here in the newly-built FISHDOME in New Orleans, Louisiana! SB: N'awlins! BB: Um, please excuse Sammy, he's been on Bourbon St. a little too long. But let's not forget the real reason we're here! The FISH FUND itself! SB: Yeah, the big fat women who need butt tucks and liposuction. BB: Basically, yeah, that's it. FISH FUND was started in 1989 to give less-than-perfect women some advantages through plastic surgery. And thus, the FISH FUND was born in Sweetwater, Texas, now home of the Windhams and the FISH FUND PARK. So, here we are in the FISH DOME to raise some money for these women and to see some great wrestling action! We've got the first two rounds of the Unified World Heavyweight Championship Tournament, as well as almost all singles and tag teams championships are up for grabs! SB: And of course, we just saw P. Vicious end Jack Roiter's chances of ever walking again! BB: We'll see OJ Simpleson and HEAT battle in their Barbed Wire match, the Dark Wizard and Dark Warrior rematch, the debut of a couple of tag teams! And that's not all! Remember, this is a two-day event! We've got Muppet Kid vs. Joey Melton for the IC belt. Bonecrusher will meet Troy Windham for the Greensboro Title. The CS Express returns to the ring as World Tag Team Champions. Crazy Like A Muppet will meet again in another rematch against ARROGANCE! The Parsons and the Benedicts will feud for the CSWA Presidential Tag Team Belts. Also, the debut of "Nirvana" Rodney Lawrence! We've also been told that CSWA officials have a few announcements to make! We'll return with them after this! It's not the biggest ever, but it has many of the best. Hornet, Melton, Tyler, Flair, Degadeth, York, Roiter, and many others are all gunning for their places in the Great Eight after tonight. They are all hoping to become only the second man to hold the Unified World Heavyweight Championship; the championship that has been two years in the making and includes 34 different titles from other premiere leagues. Of course, the one thing on everyone's mind is: how can it determine the best without Mark Windham being included? The simple answer is: it can't. Mark Windham is simply one of the greatest wrestlers in the world today. To answer some of your questions, or attempt to: we have been negotiating with Mark Windham. However, we will not back down on trying to collect the fine, and Windham still will not be competing in the tournament. We do not know if Mark will be returning to the CSWA. As for other news: the Board of Directors has decided that the runner-up in the Tournament will be the new United States-National Championship. There are two final announcements: First, the next CSWA Summer Supercard to close out the Bloated Fish Tour '94. In upcoming weeks, the CSWA will have: ELVIS LIVES CELEBRATION VIII: Takin' Care of Business! It will be held on the grounds of Graceland in Memphis, Tennessee. We've obtained special permission from the Presley Family and Elvis' estate to make this happen. The Final Four of the Unified Tournament will be held there and a new champion will be crowned. Finally, we have decided that also at ELVIS LIVES, the tag team situation will heat up. We have spoken to CS Express and they have agreed to put their titles on the line. They will be one of many teams competing in the CSWA's John W. Boozer Invitational Tag Team Tournament. The winner of the Tournament will not only win the World Tag Team Championships but one million dollars.
