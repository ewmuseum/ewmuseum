CSWA PRIMETIME in New Orleans -- November 3, 2001 CSWA PRIMETIME in New Orleans November 11, 2001 MAIN EVENT The ClaimStakers vs. The Playboys and Lawrence Stanley (CUE UP: A classical rendition of "God Save the Queen." From behind the screen, holding the small of his back, limping somewhat is "The English Gent" Lawrence Stanley. As he walks out, the crowd gets up in a united eruption and ovation. Stanley walks a few steps then stops, his eyes somewhat welled up from the response, then stops and turns around at the fans, letting out a scream in return.) BB: And there he is fans. A man who came seconds away from winning the CSWA World Title earlier tonight... and a man who is out to gain some revenge in this match against.... THAT DAMN HORNET... the man who stole his girlfriend! (The lights dim and the crowd starts to let out another scream. The introductory moog riffs of "Let's Go" by The Cars cues up, and on the video wall the word "PLAY" flashes followed by the world "BOYS". The lights come back on, and under the flashing wall of light are TROY WINDHAM and EDDY LOVE-- both frozen in a dramatic pose. Troy and Eddy are wearing matching one-piece European singlets-- black based with the gold Playboy Bunny insignia's on the front and on both cheeks, with gold fringe and tassles coming off the perimeter of their body suits. Troy and Eddy both start to Fargo strut down the aisle, then both run and slide under the ring.)
