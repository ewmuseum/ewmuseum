Chad — 06-07-05, 01:22 PM I'm posting this here because I believe some of the folks involved in the Presidential Tournament don't have access to the Member Discussion forum.

By now, many of you have seen the PRIMETIME in San Francisco show that you put significant effort into. Again, my apologies on the delay -- it all belongs on my head. Contrary to popular opinion, it didn't have as much to do with me having to write (I didn't write much on the card) as it did with just not having *any* time over the last three weeks to sit down and focus on things. To those of you who have been involved with the Presidential Tournament, you have my sincerest apologies since this is the second time I've poked you with sticks only to drop the ball.

Many of you know that I was gone on business trips half that time, and the other half was spent catching up at work, home, church, etc. I think I've only logged on to FW Central twice in that time. So, as I said earlier, Strike One in the "Rebirth" goes to me.

The good news is that I've found a workaround to the one technical problem that was stopping Steve or Pete from being able to post cards. I still haven't solved the sidebar and title issues, but I'm working on that as well, along with some minor changes.

So, my hope is to get the schedule reworked and posted on the Main Event page (http://cswawrestling.com/tribune/mainevent.shtml) today. I'm debating whether to try and go ahead and run the next show next Thursday (the 16th) or hold off until next Monday (the 20th). I'd prefer the earlier date, but would like your input on whether it works for you. If we go with the 16th, then we'd go back on on our Monday schedule with the next show being the 27th.

Anyway, post your thoughts...let me know you're still alive and kicking and all that fun stuff.
