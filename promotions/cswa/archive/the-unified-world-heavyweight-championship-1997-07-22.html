The Unified World Heavyweight Championship

22 July 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Unified World Heavyweight Championship Welcome to the PRODIGY Unified Championship Web Pages! Here you'll find info about what the Unified Title is, some of its recent history, as well a list of board-wide contenders, and where the title is scheduled to be defended. The Unified World Heavyweight Championship is composed of over 40 World, Superleague, Unified and other championships merged by generated leagues on PRODIGY over the last four years. The Unified Champion competes in various generated leagues, defending the title against top contenders. The Unified Championship is sanctioned by PRODIGY. Current PRODIGY Unified World Heavyweight Champion: "Devastating" Mike Randalls The Unified Championship was vacated last year when former Unified World Champ "Golden Boy" Paul Pierce announced his intent to retire the belt at the CSWA's Battle of the Belts XIV supercard. Pierce won the Unified Title by becoming the 1996 ULTRATITLE Tournament Champion in the first week of March earlier this year. Pierce beat out 127 other competitors in the six-week long tournament, including the likes of former Unified Champion Mike Randalls, Heartbreaker, and Jack Samson. That left PRODIGY's Unified Title vacant. The Unified Championship Committee put together the top twenty contenders to the title and began the IRONMAN of CHAMPIONS in late October 1996. From twenty, the field was narrowed to five in small four-man tournaments. Those five men competed in round-robin competition. And the winner? Mike Randalls, winning the title over the likes of GUNS, Hornet, Julius Godreign, and Mark Windsor. Randalls continues his second reign as Unified World Champion with everyone gunning for him. He's already taken on GUNS, Rocky Ford Meloneer, and others...and somehow, he's held onto the title. Whether it's the AAWC, the CSWA, the EFW, UWA, MHW or elsewhere, Randalls knows the wrestling world like the back of his hand. He's taken on the best the world has to offer.....but the question remains, how can long can he fend them off? Or maybe the better question is: can anyone take Randalls down from his position as king of the mountain.
