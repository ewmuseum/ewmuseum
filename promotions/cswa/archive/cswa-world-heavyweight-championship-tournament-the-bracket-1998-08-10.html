CSWA World Heavyweight Championship Tournament After a month-long tournament, the conclusive FINAL FOUR of the CSWA World Heavyweight Championship Tournament was held at FISH FUND XII on August 10, 1998. It featured Hornet, Michael Sparks, Eddy Love, and Billy Starr. If you missed any of the action, be sure to check it out under the CS TRIBUNE's Main Event. The first was in CSWA SuperPRIMETIME in Dallas, while rounds 2 and 3 took place at CSWA SuperPRIMETIME in Chicago. Championship "Hurricane" Eddy Love def. Billy Starr Semifinals Billy Starr def.
