Fantasy News: CSWA: Doubleshot Wknd The CSWA prepares for two cards this weekend, PRIMETIME in Paducah and SHOWTIME in Louisville. It's the third straight back-to-back doubleshot for the CSWA as it continues the IRONMAN of CHAMPIONS Tournament. PRIMETIME features a no-disqualification bout between former World Champ Eli Flair, Cardigo and Johnny Lang. The Unified Tag Team Titles are on the line in a Double Dog Collar match as Foreign Exchange defends against Simply Stunning. The Greensboro Championship features the first CSWA meeting between Sky Suicide and Evan Aho, who know each other from around the Fantasy Wrestling circuit. SHOWTIME includes the third round of the IRONMAN of CHAMPIONS. Former CSWA World Champion Eddy Love looks to break the logjam in the standings by taking on former US Champ Steve Radder. Former US Champions collide as Lawrence Stanley take on Kevin Powers, who is looking to end talk of choking in the big one. Current US Champion Havoc meets former Unified World Champion Mike Randalls in a battle that could definitely shake up the standings! CSWA: IRONMAN Continues The CSWA's fifth IRONMAN of CHAMPIONS continued into its second round as SHOWTIME in Memphis aired to finish out the CSWA's broadcasting year. SHOWTIME saw the entire field even things up at twenty points as the six men continued their round-robin tournament battle to win the vacated CSWA World Heavyweight Championship. An online on-demand replay of SHOWTIME can be viewed by visiting the CS TRIBUNE's Main Event feature at http://fantasywrestling.com/cswa/mainevent.htm. CSWA: PRIMETIME Airs CSWA PRIMETIME aired from Little Rock, Arkansas as one of the final cards in the CSWA's "Swimming Upstream Tour 2000". PRIMETIME concluded with a Hornet vs. Cardigo Main Event inside a steel cage, featuring the first meeting between the two veterans turned enemies. The matchup played a major part in the ongoing feud between the ClaimStakers, made up of Hornet, Mike Randalls, Eli Flair and Poison Ivy, against the Playboys Inc., Troy Windham and Eddy Love, with their recent partner, Cardigo. It seemed as if the tide had turned against the ClaimStakers; Mike Randalls was recently suspended for his actions against Kevin Powers in the first round of the IRONMAN of CHAMPIONS. Eli Flair was fired by Commissioner Merritt after putting Sweet Melissa, Love's valet, through a table weeks ago. But as happens so often, things change quickly. Merritt entered into negotiations with representatives for Flair and Randalls, and apparently a compromise was reached, allowing both men back into active competition, while reportedly shaving quite a bit off their salary structure. As the Playboys looked to take some revenge by going after Poison Ivy, Randalls and Flair revealed their positions in a preconceived ClaimStakers ambush, leaving Cardigo handcuffed and severely beaten in the middle of the steelcage, and the Playboys laying on the outside. The next chapters in this ongoing feud are almost certain to take place at the CSWA's next event, SHOWTIME in Memphis, featuring the second round of the six-man round-robin IRONMAN of CHAMPIONS tournament for the CSWA World Championship. Also signed for PRIMETIME in Paducah, Eli Flair and Cardigo will face off in a triangle match including Johnny Lang, who may be an innocent bystander that is in the wrong place at the wrong time.
