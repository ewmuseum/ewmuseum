Isn't it nice to know, however.... that my opinions, the TRUTH, will never change? May 16, 2001 It's been awhile, hasn't it? I figured I'd say something when I had something to say. With the recent dissention between Hornet and Mike Randalls, a lot of people are calling for the death of the Claimstakers. A lot of people think that Mike Randalls is simply not a team player. A lot of people think that Eli Flair bit off more than he could chew by hooking up with two men who have played his biggest enemy at some point in his career. And there's a hell of a lot of people who say that Hornet's ego and demeanor are the only thing keeping the ClaimStakers from completely dominating the CSWA. It's the smarks on the message boards, and even a few 'net geeks in the CSWA offices have all but posted a huge headline on the front page of the TRIBUNE: "Hornet's Ego Destroys ClaimStakers." Tearing down Hornet is one of the only things the Internet community at large can agree on. The internet community at large can F'n bite me. How dare you criticize this man. How dare you take a holier- than- thou approach to this man's career and what he's gone through? Sure, he might've 'sold out' his dear beloved fans.... but where were they when he was buried under a ton of brick and metal in Sweetwater? Where were they when he was stripped of the CSWA World Title for actions that took place three years before, whose witnesses were a Cuban refugee who can't speak english, a disgruntled, fat, unemployed color commentator, and a drugged out goth? I'll tell you where they were. They were chanting someone else's name. He's been accused of bringing it on himself. That he attacked Mark Vizzack at ELVIS LIVES and went against everything he fought so hard for for over a decade. Have any of you ever stopped to think what that decade consisted of, and how many obstacles he overcame? He was the ultimate hero, only to find that his ideals were getting walked all over, time and time again. It was the UCC, stripping him of the UNIFIED title because they wanted things to be fair, only for him to come back and defeat Mark Windham for the belt two weeks after the Living Legend won it. It was Ray S. Cornette and his power play in the Board of Directors that pushed Hornet and Randalls together to begin with, for a price. But it was Randalls, Windham, and HORNET who won the WHEEL of DEATH match, not Cornette's team. It was Hornet who went into a cage with Randalls at less than a hundred percent to give the fans what they wanted. It was Hornet who teamed up with GUNS to give the fans what they wanted, and Hornet who dumped GUNS at WINTER WARRIOR'S to once again give the fans what they wanted. And it was Hornet who was shoved out of the CSWA following the career match, because he had the audacity to play by the rules. What was the final straw? Eddy Love. Fish Fund 12. Eddy Love came in and the fans loved his spiel. They forgot who fought for them, and embraced a man who verbally abused them and aligned himself with Ray S. Cornette and the CORPORATION, of all people, as their new hero. Fine. The people are fickle. They want the guy who's so bad he must be cool, fine. Hornet gave you people what you wanted. But by that point, you wanted the hero again. You wanted the cool, insulting- yet- witty Eddy Love to take out the Greatest American Hero, Hornet. Then you wanted the 'poor man's Hornet,' Mark Vizzack, to take out the insulting- yet- witty 'Businessman,' Hornet. He gave you what you wanted. He gave you everything you wanted.... but it wasn't enough, was it? Is it any wonder he told Merritt that he was staking a claim on the CSWA? Is it any wonder that he refuses to do what he's told anymore? You want to boo Hornet, fine. You want to hate Hornet, fine. You think he's too bitter about the business for his own good, you need to take a step back. He's earned it. He's paid for it. Paid with back injuries, leg injuries, head injuries, burns, spotted title histories because people who can't stand to see how dominant he can be want to strip him of various belts. And he's paid for it with the only thing that was truly his. His love of the sport. This is Ivy. Ivy League Archives: 1/17/01 12/4/2000 October 15, 2000 September 16, 1999 August 4, 1999 June 20, 1999 April 23, 1999 April 15, 1999 February 1999 Gentleman's Club by Lawrence Stanley 22 January 2001 Welcome to the latest article penned by me, “The English Gent” Lawrence Stanley. I write this shortly after my victory over Eddy Love in Memphis, and the bruises I suffered at his hands are just starting to show themselves! We are now two rounds into the IOC and things appear to be balancing themselves out nicely, all six competitors have twenty points each and needless to say the CSWA Heavyweight title is anyone’s for the taking. I cannot emphasise how disappointed I was to lose in the opening round to Steve Radder, I had the match won and somehow I allowed myself to drift away and was punished accordingly. I have been lucky that results have deemed that the loss hasn’t been a greater one. I feel confident that I have enough in me to ward off the other three opponents I am due to face, and with that hopefully will bring with it the greatest prize of all. The keen-minded of you out there will no doubt be in a quandary over the relationship between myself and the radiant Teri Melton. Indeed, in the first round of the IOC I took the microphone and told the world how I planned on winning the IOC and the title on my own, and that I was so determined that if anyone interfered on my behalf I would deal with them personally. Thus I was in a difficult situation when looking back at the Memphis card I saw Teri help me by locking up Eddy Love’s leg, allowing me to deliver a German suplex for the win. I expect you all think I should take her to task, but the reality is…..I won’t. Teri and I had a heart-to-heart after the match and she made me see sense, she told me that behind every great man is a great woman…and she couldn’t be more right. While I need the help of my manager Alfred I also need the help of the MNC….and especially Teri. She has been a rock for me the past few months and without her I don’t know how I would have coped, she has focused me as of late, and with the support of everyone in the MNC there isn’t a man out there who can stop me. Enough about me though, onto other matters in the CSWA. I feel I must comment on the escalating feud between my fellow MNC members and CSWA tag team champions the Foreign Exchange and Simply Stunning. This war has been raging for months and both teams have taken turns to dish horrendous amounts of punishment out to each other. I’ve been pulling for my stablemates naturally, particularly when I listen to the wretched comments made towards Teri by my fellow Countrymen. How they could say that to a lady of her standing is quite remarkable and it offends me as much a sit does her. I hope that Tsunami and Wildstar beat some respect into their worthless bodies. Despite being wrapped up in IOC and ‘personal’ business I have been looking round the rest of the CSWA and as always there has been plenty happening, many new stars are rising up and making quite a name for themselves. I have been impressed with the likes of Evan Aho, Sky Suicide, Jean Rabesque, XXXtasy to name but a few. Never has a federation held some many talented wrestlers under one roof, the future certainly looks rosy as far as Merritt’s shares are concerned! Add to that the list of established stars and it is easy to see why the CSWA is the envy of every other wrestling federation owner. Well, that is all for me at the moment, be sure to check this space out after the next card where I shall dispense more pieces of priceless knowledge to you all. Till then, I bid you all….a good day. Column Archives: Kneel, and bow before me. Don’t be shy. My name is Sammy Benson, your constant example of morality. I understand, you’ve waited a lifetime. Come my son, and walk with me through the gates of Happy Hour. Updated 7/12/00 I just want to start by saying how much I despise Canadians. I know I've mentioned it before in this column...but after some 'time off' due to the CSWA's internal bickering between the big C and S, I thought I'd remind you. And that includes Steve Radder. The Canadian of Canadians. While Canadians babble on about his clean air and water...while they try to convince us that their Canadian beer is a true import and better than the American stuff...while they goes on and on about providing health care to everybody by taxing everyone excessively...I know the truth. It's all a gimmick. Radder and Canada both. They're all gimmick. Just like Radder sticking his nose into what could have been the greatest combination since America's Team...the Powers of Love. But no, Powers had to let the Canadian stick his nose into things... and so we get "PLR"...and the rest, as they say, is history. Then the Canadian proceeds to turn on Powers, the guy that got him *in* the in-crowd, while ragging on Eddy Love. Typical Canadian blather. He talks about how great he is...how he deserves a title shot... how he deserved better from Powers and Love. Please. You want some respect, Radder? Get up off your behind and show us that you can hold a candle to the former CSWA World Champion Eddy Love and the Unified World Tag Champ Kevin Powers. You didn't deserve to be with them then...and you're not in their league now. Get over it...and go back to Canada to raise your kid in the socialistic little society your crave. Like I said before: "Canadian: Australian for Gay". (Comments expressed in "Happy Hour" do not necessarily represent the CSWA or CS Enterprises On-line, Inc.) Carnival of Knowledge Updated 8/11/00 Controversy. Something that everyone experiences at least once in their lives unless you compete in the CSWA. If you compete in the CSWA then you experience it everyday in your life. So can be said about the events that occurred at Indy 2000 held by the CSWA. With three teams fighting for survival in the Stairway to Hell match, it was Kevin Powers who reached for the titles and claimed victory ... or so The Dark Carnival though. It is true that Kevin Powers and Michael Hardy of Simply Stunning grabbed the belts, but it was Powers who had control first. Yet the CSWA ... Merritt ... had turned a blind eye to the situation and has created controversy yet again. Now nobody knows who the tag team champions are. Perhaps if they asked Michael Hardy the would get a true answer cause deep down inside he knows he is the false hero to the cause. So would that match explain the assault of Steve Radder? Perhaps it would, but never look into the situation when the answer stares you right in the face. Kevin Powers attacked Steve Radder because he felt like it. Radder has had an unseen headlock on the CSWA which cannot be explained and now Kevin Powers will release that once and for all. In Phoenix Kevin Powers and The Dark Carnival will prove that only one team runs this league .... we do and nothing .... nobody ... can stop us from our complete and utter control. So when you, the fans of the CSWA, buy your tickets to the next event be ready for The Dark Carnival and ... Enjoy the Ride. Point/Counterpoint Updated post-TWS "Factions.....From one extreme......." These days, everyone and their mother is banding together as a group. Personally I don't see the point to some of them, but you can't argue with the logic. Back in the day, the old saying "A real man stands alone" held true, in fact, it was admired, now, it's just stupid. True, you have more room for debate in a group. You know, "Who's the leader?", "So and so would have lost if he didn't have his group", and so on. However, today, it isn't necessarily "What" you know....it's "Who". Take a look at some feds outside our realm. You have "Triple H", if he wasn't involved with Shawn Michaels at one point, he would still be genuflecting in the ring. Jeff Jarrett, if he didn't make goody good with Kevin Nash, he would still be talking about the stroke he wasn't getting. These days, you NEED people around you. There is a flip side to this as well. Say for instance, you get jumped, you think people are gonna back you up, just because "It's the right thing to do"? Back in the day, maybe that would happen. Nowadays, the only ones helping you are the referees, and they don't help at all. There are also those friendships that can happen in a group. No one can get a tag-belt by themselves you know. Don't get me wrong, I'm not saying there isn't a kindred nobility in standing on your own. Just let everyone else know when you stop living in the past, we'll bring you up to speed. You don't like it? Live with it. ".....To the other." Why is this culture so enthralled with bandwagons, and fads? One day it's cool to wear JimmyZ, the next day it's cool to wear FUBU, clothes are clothes. It's "Cool" to be a Yankee's fan, so, you know what, everyone is, it's "cool" to have an S.U.V. so every car manufacturer is involved, whether they should be or not. I mean, really, does an offspring of a Jeep, REALLY need to be luxurious? A few years ago, you would see a faction here, a stable there, nowadays, I swear they should form their OWN fed. somewhere, why? Because it's "Cool". Just like the plight of a teenager, come on, you know the words "Iwant to be judged for what I do, not what everyone my age does." Meanwhile the kid who says that is wearing the EXACT same kind of clothes as the three kids next to him. I know all about "fitting in", so don't say it. I'm not saying that if you are following the trend, and jumping into a faction, that you are a sheep. Not at all, what I AM saying, is, where's the challenge in it? Isn't that what life is supposed to be about? Isn't that why we have extreme sports? Because the average athlete wanted to be challenged beyond his known limits? Sure, a stablemate is good in a pinch, he's got your back, but can you say any accomplishment you make is REALLY yours? Take a look at some of the legends that this sport has to offer. Mike Randalls, now when have you seen him with a faction? Hornet, maybe a buddy here, a buddy there, but that's about it. There are many more, but I'm not going to get into too much detail, it's not necessary. Do you have any inkling as to why these men are of legend? They know that "A warrior without his weapons, is a warrior still". What that means is, they don't need anybody around them. Sure, they may get their butt handed to them by a group of thugs, but that doesn't change things. Take a look at the returning Swat Team, they knew that they were becoming nothing more than lackeys, so what are they doing? They're going on their own. These four men are warriors, no I'm not plugging an upcoming pay per view, this is coincidence. Does your being in a faction make you a wimp? Not necessarily, but you will have to prove that alot more than a man on his own. If you have yet to realize that, then wake up, this dream is over.
