CSWA Wrestler Profile: Kevin Powers Name: "Good God" Kevin Powers Hometown: Chicago, IL Height: 6'10" Weight: 318 lbs Age: 29 Alignment: Face Entrance Music: "Play With Me" by Insane Clown Posse Manager: 'Risque' Roseanne Fairhurst Biography/Profile: Hailing from Chicago, Illinois, 'Good God' Kevin Powers has been dubbed 'The Emperor of Hardcore' for good reason. Much is not said about Powers past. There is a rumor that he was a prior military service member and got out of the service in South Korea where he was down on his luck. This cannot be confirmed. Since his 'return' to the states he met up with Gina and Susan and he got his act together and decided to enter a wrestling school where he was noticed quickly. He first started his career in the now defunct SWWA where he was introduced into the league by Apocalypse. After the league closed down he soon found himself in Total Championship Wrestling where he won the Television Title, Multinational Wrestling Corporation where he won the first ever Intercontinental Title, and ChampionShip Wrestling Alliance where he captured the United States Title in a tournament final. The most memorable of the three belts is the reputation he gained when he battled 'Total Elimination' Eli Flair, thus earning the respect of the Wrestling World. After dropping the belts he soon tagged up with former friend 'Hurricane' Eddy Love and captured the CSWA Unified Tag Team, MWC Intercontinental Title, CSWA US Title, CSWA Tag Team Titles, TCW Television Title, HEW Hardcore Title in a tournament final. Also, during that time, he found his way to Hardcore Extreme Wrestling where he captured the Hardcore Title there as well only to drop it later on down the road. As of now Kevin Powers is still the Tag Team Champion of the CSWA, but now with a new Apocalypse in the form of .... Apocalypse. Powers manager, 'Risqué' Roseanne Fairhurst, is a former Mile High Wrestling Ladies Champion. She came to Powers side when Gina and Susan, Powers' old valets, left when times got tough. So now it's Fairhurst that makes sure that nothing happens to her man. Her normal attire consist of torn blue jeans with matching jean jacket and a red halter top. She also has blonde hair that comes down the the upper part of her back and also brown eyes. When his music starts up Fairhurst comes out and stands right in front of Powers and that's when the pyro effects go off from behind. After the pyro, which is a white fountain like effect, is done he takes a cool stride towards the ring where he lets Fairhurst get in first and then he follows there after. His ring attire consist of Black leather pants with a leather tank top. It's all in black except on one side of the leg says GOOD and on the other side of the leg it says GOD, both in red. Outside the ring, je wears black boots with black kneepads and a red right elbowpad.
