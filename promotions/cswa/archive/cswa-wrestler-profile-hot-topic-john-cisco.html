CSWA Wrestler Profile: "Hot Topic" John Cisco Name: "Hot Topic" John Cisco Hometown: San Diego, CA Height: 6'3" Weight: 247 lbs Age: 26 Alignment: Face Entrance Music: "I Dare You" by Black Rob Manager: Terra Lopez Biography/Profile: Appearance: John has long brown hair that usually dangles in his face, he's pretty well built, he will give the ladies something to stare at. He comes to the ring wearing a button up Nautica shirt which he leaves open so that the ladies can check him out, as well as a pair of khakis shorts. For in ring action however, John comes with black pants that say "Hot Topic" down each side and black and white knee high boots. He has two tattoos...Hot Topic on his right bicep and on his right forearm he has a tattoo of a rottweiler with a diamond collar. History: John Cisco knew from the time he was a young boy that wrestling was in his genes. Cisco grew up in San Diego which isn't a well known wrestling town, but he took them by storm, he quickly was named All-State wrestler of the year his first year n the wrestling team. After high school, Cisco's grades weren't good enough for College, however, some wrestling recruiter saw him and was amazed, so he told Cisco to tour with him and join some wrestling tournaments. Cisco agreed to go because he'd always wanted to get out of Cali. Even though Cisco lost nearly all his tournaments he still enjoyed the wrestling. To his name Cisco has only one tournament victory...ICWL Strive-2-Survive Cup. After his tour of the US, he went to Japan but discovered hardcore wrestling just wasn't his thing. He came back and upon his return met an interesting women who has been watching him and told him that with her help she can turn him into a winning machine. Terra Lopez took Cisco under her tutelage and taught him quite a bit. Now they journey to the wrestling world to try and wreak havoc upon it. Cisco tags with Mark Davis as "Strictly Business."
