CSWA Interactive! CSWA Interactive is back on the Web! The CSWA's patented poll format returns here on the CSWA Web Pages. The concept is very simple: we list the question and a series of possible answers, and you pick the one you agree with! There's also a way to write-in votes. You may vote as many times as you like, and you may vote for as many different answers as you'd like. We'll post the results for each question in the near future. NOTE: There are two different kinds of Interactive questions. For those listed below with a series of choices and links following them, click on the link to cast your vote. It will be recorded on the counter of the page you head to. For those questions below with either a fill-in-the-blank or a series of dots to choose from, those questions will be sent to us via e-mail when you choose the Submit My Answers box below. Question #1: What was the biggest surprise of FISH FUND XI? a) Randalls' appearance on the America's Team WAR GAMES team b) Hornet's loss to GUNS in the Career Match c) Power Unlimited winning the Unified Tag Titles d) The return of Tsunami and Wildstar as Dawn of a New Day e) Other -- write in your vote. Question #2: Should GUNS: a) be allowed to have a match with careers-on-the-line with Mark Windham b) be forced to fulfill his contract by wrestling opponents c) be hit with another bottle of piss d) Other -- write in your vote. Question #3: Is Anarky: a) the greatest champion of all time. b) nothing more than a glorified jobber. c) verging on superstardom d) almost ready to come crashing down e) Other -- write in your vote. Well, FISH FUND XI is over. It shattered the record for number of posts, and included some tremendous matches. But what rating do you give it? Note: If you want to give a rating such as 3.5, 2.5, or 1.5, simply type it in the large box below and send it with the rest of your choices. Should the Career Match decision be reversed? Yes, Hornet should've won. No, he's gone for good. Hold a rematch between the two. What was the best match on the card? Pick one from the box below. Hornet/GUNS Career Match Windham/Adler USN Match Triangle Match (Anarky, GUNS, Windham) WAR GAMES CSWA Challenge Tag Match Japanese Assault/Troy Windham Japanese Assault/Power Unlimited Wild Asian/Mark Windsor match Eliminator/ATTAXX IC match Xavier Virile/RF Meloneer G'boro Title match CSWWA Title Tournament Other--put choice in box below In this cute little box below is where you can include any other comments about FISH FUND XI, or CSWA Interactive in general. That's all for the current CSWA Interactive. Cast your votes as many times, and as many different ways, as you'd like. We'll post the results in the near future, and then come back with new INTERACTIVE questions. If you have an idea for an Interactive question, let us know by clicking one of the mailbox icons above and writing a message to us. Click here to return to the CSWA Home Page.
