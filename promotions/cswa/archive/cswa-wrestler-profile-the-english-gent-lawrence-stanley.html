CSWA Wrestler Profile: Lawrence Stanley Name: "The English Gent" Lawrence Stanley Hometown: London, England Height: 6'4" Weight: 320 lbs Age: 28 Alignment: Heel Entrance Music: English national anthem, "God Save the Queen". Manager: Lord Alfred: Stanley's friend and manager, a master tactician and strategist who is not scared to interfere in a match to help Stanley be victorious, often with the help of his steel cane. Biography/Profile: Lawrence Stanley is clean shaven and likes to have a smart appearance, although don't let this confuse you, he is a tough guy who uses his height and weight to his advantage with his aggressive brawling tactics when he fights. He is generally very calm and collected and likes to put Lord Alfred's tactics into operation when he enters the ring, some say the easiest way to defeat Stanley is to incense him so much that he loses his cool and loses concentration. Stanley is very patriotic and feels that anyone who is not English is below him, which means he has few friends, a view shared by Lord Alfred (not really a lord, he adopted the title to impress people.). Stanley shows a cruel, sadistic streak which probably can be blamed on his history. Abandoned as a child, Stanley grew up in a foster home where he kept himself to himself, he took out his frustrations on the other children which landed him in trouble. Lord Alfred, 20 years his senior walked past the home one day and saw Stanley 'at work' on the other children and saw potential. Alfred was a small time wrestling manager who at the time had a stable which was little more than a group of walking punch bags. He took Stanley under his wing and taught him how to wrestle and use his frustration to good affect. Alfred took Stanley to underground fights where Stanley acquired a reputation and Alfred acquired a lot of money from Stanley's success. In his late 20's Stanley wanted some recognition of his ability so Alfred saw the opportunity to wrestle in the premier federation - the CSWA.
