BATTLE OF THE BELTS X: The Fall Classic November 12, 1994 Featuring: the EN World Championship Tournament and Roiter/Hornet =============================================================================== Rhubarb Jones: The following is a special presentation from U-62 TV in conjunction with the CSWA. BB: Hello, CSWA fans!!! I, as you know, am Bill Buckley, your host here tonight in Chicago, Illinois! My partner in commentating is Sammy Benson! We are here in the United Center in Chicago to bring you... BATTLE OF THE BELTS X The Fall Classic SB: What a stupid name! BB: Shut up, Sammy, I was the one who thought of it! That's right folks, the CSWA is having its TENTH Battle of the Belts! SB: Of course this time there are no belts to be defended! BB: That's not QUITE true, Sammy. The CSWA has vacated all its titles except two, and those two, the Unified Heavyweight and Tag Team Championships are on the line tonight! Also tonight, the fate of the Enterprises World Heavyweight Championship will be decided in a 16-man tournament. The winner will become the EN World Champ, recognized by the CSWA as its World Heavyweight Champion. The runner-up in the tournament will receive the United States/National (USN) Title. That final tournament matchup is part of tonight's MAIN EVENT. The other half will see the Unified Championship Title Defense when Unified Champion Hornet takes on a retiring Mike Roiter! SB: So shut up and let's get the action underway! BB: For once you're right, Sammy. Tonight's first matchup sees another man who is in the hunt for Hornet's Unified World Championship. The 23rd-ranked Loki takes on former ABWA World Champion Henderson Bramble. (Some matches will be in short form, since we've got a whole Tournament to post! <G>) LOKI vs. HENDERSON BRAMBLE The former multi-time World Champion Loki was taken aback early by Bramble's combination fisherman's suplex and royal octopus hold. But finally, the 7'2" giant caught Bramble with an armbar that he followed into a german suplex. Then there was no stopping Loki. He caught the former ABWA Champ with a neckbreaker followed by a DDT. A final spinning elbow to the jaw put Bramble on the mat for the last time in the match. Loki's grapevine cover ended the match with a win for the big man.
