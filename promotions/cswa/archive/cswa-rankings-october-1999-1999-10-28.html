CSWA Rankings Welcome to the o-fish-al CSWA rankings. Rankings are usually updated every two or three cards, or after major events. Back to TRIBUNE Archived Rankings 9/22/99 8/18/99 5/22/99 4/23/99 Tag Team Ranks? With the Unified Tag Team Titles up in the air due to the Powers of Love split, the CSWA Championship Committee declared a lay-off to the rankings until the titles are decided as CSTV: First Edition! Rankings Plus The rankings expand to the big 3-0 this week as a list of series newcomers (and some old ones) make their debut on the big countdown. Exiting the rankings this time are two of the sport's top names and former World Champions. Mark Vizzack has been removed until he returns from his time off for injury. Hornet has been removed, having apparently announced his retirement following SHOWTIME in Negril. Steve Radder heads off the rankings as he steps away from the wrestling circuit. Jesse Solomon also exits the rankings, having been shelved due to injury following SHOWTIME.
