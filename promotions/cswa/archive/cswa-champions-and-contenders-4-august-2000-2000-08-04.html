CSWA Champions and Contenders Back to TRIBUNE Title Histories In a special feature below, we'll be giving you some of the history behind the belts. While the entire 11-year history would take pages, we'll give you looks at recent history. CSWA World Heavyweight Championship (since ANNIVERSARY 1998) From its return after Merritt's 'unfortunate incarceration,' the CSWA immediately looked to crown a new World Champion. Unfortunately, every opportunity was met with interference. From the ANNIVERSARY 1998 match between Hornet and GUNS for the title; to the later four-way between Troy and Mark Windham, and Hornet and GUNS, no one could claim the title. CSWA officials finally announced a tournament, with the Final Four commencing at FISH FUND XII. Eddy Love FISH FUND XII (8/10/98) --defeated Billy Starr in the Tournament Finals Mark Vizzack ELVIS LIVES XI (1/24/99) Hornet SHOWTIME in San Antonio (4/23/99 - 5/13/99)* Eli Flair ANNIVERARY 1999 IRONMAN of CHAMPIONS (7/25/99) Deacon THANKSGIVING WEEKEND SPECTACULAR '99 (Dec. 1999) *Hornet was stripped of the title at BATTLE of the BELTS XVI by Co-Commissioner Chad Merritt.
