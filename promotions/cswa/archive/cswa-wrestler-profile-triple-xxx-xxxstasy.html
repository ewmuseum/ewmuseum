CSWA Wrestler Profile: "Triple X" XXXstasy Name: "Triple XXX" XXXstasy Hometown: Orlando, FL Height: 6'3" Weight: 236 lbs Age: 24 Alignment: Neutral Entrance Music: "Bullet in the Head" by Rage Against the Machine Manager: Biography/Profile: Appearance: XXXstasy has long golden hair, that he wears in various styles (ponytail, two ponytails, etc...), a slight beard, and an average physique (similiar to Shawn Michaels). His ring attire consists of black tights w/ white outlining, and t-shirts with one of his many catch phrases on it... Background: XXXstasy's wrestling career was nothing more than fate...XXXstasy (aka Sean Stevens) was a normal kid with normal grades and a normal background...One day he and a bunch of friends decided to attend a Xtreme Wrestling League (XWL) event at the Orlando's own O-rena and while the event was taking place was dared to jump the guardrail...Well, never one to back down from a challenge, he did, but when he jumped the rail, instead of jumping right back over to join his friends, Sean got an adrenaline rush, and entered the ring before being carted off by the local police to spend the night in jail. The next morning, while in his cell, a guard came and opened the cell telling him somebody had paid his bail, and he was free to go...That somebody ended up being XWL president [name withheld], and he made a deal with Sean, that if he spent a year in the XWL he didn't have to pay him back, apparently the ratings went through the roof when XXXstasy jumped the railing and Greg took notice... Sean was then given the name XXXstasy, later shortened to Triple X. At first, he was a mid-carder at best... who played the role of sidekick, to one of the leagues top guns, the AMERICAN HEARTTHROB... Soon, he grew tired of being in AHT's shadow, and went out on his own, doing things his way. While XXXstasy was in the XWL he soon became the most hated wrestler in the fed by the other wrestlers...They didn't understand how somebody with no wrestling experience could just jump a guardrail, and get a job in the XWL, when they worked so hard to get there's. XXXstasy spent a year in the XWL, but experience would play a big factor in his career for XXXstasy never held a title... After the year was up XXXstasy left the XWL and headed for the EPWL, an independent league with far less competition, which was proven after XXXstasy nabbed it's world title a week after joining up.... Seeking more competition, XXXstasy left the EPWL and ended up in Soul City Fantasy Wrestling (SCFW). There he found all the competition he could handle, winning it's International Championship in the process. Although, he didn't reach the goal he had in mind due to the fact that the SCFW closed down, because of financial problems... Seeking a new home, XXXstasy recieved a call from Hardcore Extreme Wrestling owner, [name withheld], and immediately joined the HEW with high expectations...He immediately recieved numerous World title shots against then, undefeated champion, Randy Harders...Although never successful in any of those matchups, XXXstasy looked at those matchups as learning experiences, which proved necessary because XXXstasy later captured the HEW's International title twice, and it's North American title once...soon after the HEW closed down...which lead XXXstasy to Superior Championship Wrestling, but after about a week of being in that fed, it too closed down...leaving Triple X without a home...that is...until he recieved a call from a couple of friends telling him about Total Championship Wrestling and it's competition...Immediately XXXstasy packed his bags in pursuit of the TCW, with one goal in mind, and that's to be it's World Champion.... After some "backstage" controversy... TCW also closed down after about three months into XXXstasy's tenure'. Although, XXXstasy came within a hair pin, of becoming WORLD CHAMPION... he single-handedly tossed NINETEEN other individuals over the top rope... thus, proclaiming himself RECKLESS RUMBLE 2000's champion, as well as granting him a shot at NEWLY crowned TCW WORLD CHAMPION, Shawn "Superman" Stevens... As you could probably guess... the match was filled w/ controversy... FIVE wrestlers from the previous night's RUMBLE interfered on Stevens behalf and cost XXXstasy the one shot he claimed he needed. About, two months later XXXstasy won TCW's Regional Championship... but, before he EVER got the chance to defend it... backstage heat, got caused the fed to close down. Which leads XXXstasy to now. For as long as Triple X can remember, the CSWA was the one place he's always wanted to be... It was where the "big boys" hung. For far to long, he's been denied. One day, while sitting at home... he received a call from the office of CSWA owner, Chad Merritt... ecstatic from the conversation... XXXstasy once again packed his bags... in pursuit of his BIGGEST challenge yet. With one particular goal... still in mind.
