Bobby Watson vs. Page Michaels BUCKLEY: These two have been waiting for this one for a while. But while the edge in brutality most certainly goes to Bobby Watson, Michaels showed that his almost-win against GUNS was no fluke. He powered Watson to the mat, negating the edge in brutality. Michaels continued to go after Watson, keeping the smaller man on the mat with a series of suplexes, and finally, a tombstone piledriver, that gave Michaels the 1...2...3! 'Sexy' Steve Hotbody vs. Spanky Richards BUCKLEY: Fans we saw the debut of a new wrestler tonight as a surprise guest was in attendance. "Sexy" Steve Hotbody came to the ring and offered an open challenge to anyone who would be willing to walk down to the ring and face him one on one. The fit and muscular Hotbody looked like he would leave without a challenge when "Spanky" Richards reluctantly came down to the ring. Given his apprehension, you could tell that Spanky wasn't really out there by his own choice. That didn't matter to Hotbody though who immediately went to work and tore into Richards. Hotbody nailed him with a spinning neckbreaker, a tilt-a-whirl suplex and finished him off with his "Spining Cut" combination Airplane Spin/Diamond Cutter. Hotbody ended it in less then two minutes and walked away with his first CSWA win. After the match Hotbody went to an attractive lady at ringside and planted a kiss on her cheek. She fainted and he left laughing. Aaron Douglas vs. Crash McLarson BUCKLEY: Fans this was a see-saw matchup for the first ten minutes with both men putting on an impressive display. Crash surprised Douglas in the beginning, but Aaron was able to hang on and keep the pace with McLarson. McLarson however started to look winded and the superior conditioning of Aaron Douglas really began to show through. Douglas methodically took apart McLarson with a fantastic display of offense and ended the match with a Piledriver and a three count. Elite Domination vs. Degeneration Next BUCKLEY: Fans we had a tag-team match tonight that had a very special provision involved. Page Michaels and the wrestler formerly known as Da Crack Baby, collectively known as Elite Domination were signed to take on The Crippler and HBK2000 who are billed as Degeneration Next. The interesting aspect of this match, was that the loser will be forced at our next card and in humble form offer apologies and praise to the victors AND on top of it be forced to withstand five minutes of punishment from the entire stable of the winning tag-team. And as of yet, Hornet hasn't renamed the wrestler formerly known as Da Crack Baby, after winning that stipulation in their first round World Tournament matchup in Dallas. With that said, you can be certain that neither team wanted to lose this battle and they fought hard. All four men gave it their all tonight, with both teams looking fairly impressive. Early on it looked like Degeneration Next was going to walk away with the win, as they kept the wrestler formerly known as Da Crack Baby isolated in the wrong corner and worked him over with excellent teamwork. But the former DCB was able to tag his partner Michaels who was ready to roll and he went wild, taking down both the Crippler and HBK 2000 with repeated roundhouse rights and thunderous dropkicks. Both teams regrouped and fought on, but Degeneration Next was never able to overcome Page Michaels and Michaels walked away with the win for his team when he nailed The Crippler with a Diamond Cutter. HBK tried to make the save, but the wrestler formerly known as Da Crack Baby prevented him. Should be interesting to see what will happen to HBK2000 and The Crippler at our next card. 'Godfather' Vito Corleone vs. Eli Flair BUCKLEY: Eli led to the ring by Poison Ivy looked like a man possessed tonight. After an unexpected early defeat in the CSWA World tournament, Eli Flair looked tonight to get back on the winning track and The Godfather had the unfortunate duty of stepping into the ring with him. Eli showed flashes of pure power tonight in press slamming the Godfather and for a moment we thought he was going to take a page out of GUNS playbook when he considered throwing the Godfather clear into the audience. Eli continued to pound on him with an array of brawling and power moves, finally ending this after about six minutes with a Running Powerslam. Ivy stepped into the ring and raised Eli's hand and you can be sure that you will be seeing them in the spotlight again soon. Shawn Diamond vs. Mysterious BUCKLEY: We had a short intermission between the matches because the cage had to be properly assembled for our next match, but when it was you could tell that this was going to be a brutal encounter. Not much pure wrestling in this one as both men looked like they had bloodlust. The cage was a weapon right from the opening bell and it never really stopped being one. Both men were covered in blood, and both were equally up to the challenge. Diamond however looked to be taking the worst of things and for a while looked like he might not be able to continue from the severe bleeding and pure brutality of this match. But he fought on, only to be outdone by Mysterious in the end when Mysterious went to the top rope and drop kicked Shawn Diamond who was leaning face first on the steel cage at the time. Diamond had no fight left in him and Mysterious easily scored the three count. Unified Tag Team Titles on the line SWAT Team vs. Disco Express BUCKLEY: The highlight of the night however might have been in our tag-team event for the Unified World Tag-Team Championship. Former CSWA World Champion Troy Windham is scheduled to take on former CSWA Presidential Champ Billy Starr. "Marvelous" Mark Vizzack is going up against Deacon in a battle of the CSWA newcomer heavyweights. GUNS will take on Eliminator, in a bout where power is paramount. Michael Sparks and Julius Godreign will heat the ring up, along with Mark Windham vs. Preacher, and many others. Let's get down to it!!! CSWA WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP TOURNAMENT SECOND ROUND Hornet vs. Steve Radder The Iceman got by his tag team partner in the first round, beating "The Godfather," while Hornet took advantage of his win to re-name and train the wrestler now known as Nemesis. Many commented that maybe Hornet was on a downslide, and could go down in this one. Others seemed to be underestimating Steve Radder, giving him no shot in this one. This one started with Steve Radder on top, working on the back of Hornet. He attacked Hornet with a quick belly-to-belly, following with a sledgehammer blow to the back, and a snap suplex. A quick one-count averted the end of the match, as Hornet catapulted Radder off of him. With Hornet on his feet, Radder quickly went for a drop toe hold that missed, as Hornet went airborne with a dropkick. Hornet followed up with full nelson to a dragon suplex, bridging over for a one-count. After the kick-out, Radder and Hornet both got to their feet, circling each other, looking to go in for the kill. Once again, Radder took control, sending Hornet into the ropes and catching the former World Champion with a swinging DDT. With Hornet on the mat, Radder headed up top to go for the kill, but nobody was home as Radder came down hard on his knee. Hornet went in for the kill quickly, keeping Radder on the mat with a brainbuster, and then hooking in the Scorpion Deathlock in the middle of the ring for the submission victory. God's Protege vs. Pat Black The battle of the newcomers, both with big wins in the first round. Pat Black started out things early with Lady Death in the corner, nailing Protege with a clothesline that sent the youngster into a 360 to the mat. Protege got to his feet quickly, but got caught off guard after a spin wheel kick from Black. The man who beat Mike Randalls in the first round pushed Protege to the limit, firing away with a systematic pummelling of kicks, punches and forearms. Protege finally fought his way out of the corner, scooping the larger man with a body slam, and quickly following with a springboard splash and a figure four leg lock. After grabbing the ropes to break the hold, Black quickly rolled outside to get a morale booster from Lady Death. Protege caught his breath, and as Black entered, God's Protege send him sprawling back outside against the guardrail with a baseball slide dropkick. Protege followed Black outside, using the guardrail to his advantage as he worked to wear down the big man. Black countered, however, blocking his head from getting slammed to the rail, and instead scooping Protege and dropping him neck-first onto the steel. As things went on outside, Apocalypse started slowly walking down the aisle, watching the goings-on. He stopped at the end of the ramp as Black quickly rolled Protege into the ring, breaking the nearly-finished ten-count and went for the cover...only getting a two count on the worn-out Protege. Black followed with a knee drop to the head, but again, Protege kicked out at two, refusing to let his World Championship dreams die. Black pulled Protege to his feet, sending him into the ropes for a rolling DDT, but again, Protege refused to give, getting his right shoulder up at the count of two. Finally, Black took Protege down with a swinging neckbreaker, continuing to capitalize on the beaten body part, and getting the three-count. After the three-count, Apocalypse entered the ring and stood toe-to-toe with Pat Black for a moment, before helping Black send God's Protege through the ropes to the floor. The two men shook hands in the middle of the ring, before walking backstage together. Eddy Love vs. Kevin Powers It could be said that Eddy Love and Kevin Powers are twins born from different mothers. Nearly a clone of one other, Love and Powers had to decide which was the better between the two. Eddy has spared no time at all, in becoming a household name in the CSWA. Getting the better of legends like Mike Randalls. Kevin Powers is still a CSWA virgin. Proven in other federations but just beginning to set out on the long trek of making a name for himself in the CSWA. It's a shame the match took place in the 1st round, because it was a classic. For over 20 minutes, Love and Powers gave as good as they got. Powers wearing Eddy down moves like the Figure 4, and Double chicken wing. Love countered with a top rope dropkick, sleeper hold, and a devastating STF. The match set the tone for the rest of the evening. In the end, Love pinned him with a Top Rope Sunset Flip. In an interesting development to the match. Towards the end, members of the CORPORATION came out to ringside. Apparently scouting one or both men. Preacher vs. Mark Windham The Preacher looked to follow the lead of his fellow CSWA rookies by knocking off a legend. Mark Windham was on of the original 4 members of the CSWA. He's long removed from the days of his Unified World Heavyweight Championship, and 4 reigns as United States champion. Windham hasn't seen the site of gold, in quite a while. As the Preacher looked to usher in a new era, Mark hoped to right his ways. The match lasted less than ten minutes, but it was brutal. The Preacher, who had promised to punish Mark for his wrong deeds, opened the match doing just that. The Preacher took him to task with a powerbomb, chokeslam, and a vicious clothesline. Windham somehow managed to survive. Regaining his composure, Mark went to work on the Preacher..throwing him outside the ring. Once there, he cut him down to size with a chair, and just about anything else he could find. The Preacher though found a few things to his liking. They both beat each other senseless. Back in the ring, Windham nearly lost as a masked man-with E.L stamped on his tights-swung a chair for the Preacher but instead whacked Windham. Windham was able to regroup, and put away the Preacher. As the big man went to the top, Windham seized the moment. Superplexing him from the Top Rope, and then finishing the job with an implant DDT. Eliminator vs. GUNS BUCKLEY: Two big men, two very different backgrounds. The Eliminator a relative unknown here in the CSWA faced off against one of the biggest names in wrestling history, GUNS. These two men stood toe to toe for large portions of this match, as they quickly turned to brawling tactics. GUNS with his large muscular build was able to gain the upper hand with the brawling and was really tearing into the Eliminator. Eliminator showed some signs of brawling back into the match but he wasn't able to keep up with GUNS by going face to face with him. The match looked just about over when GUNS whipped the Eliminator into the corner and came barreling in with the Bionic Knee. But to the surprise of GUNS... NO ONE WAS HOME. Eliminator had played possum and was able to slide out of the way to safety. GUNS crashed hard into the ring post. Eliminator quickly followed it up with a diving clothesline and then a powerbomb. After a two count, the Eliminator again went to the offense, and the two battled back and forth till the Eliminator clotheslined GUNS right over the top rope and down to the arena floor. Both men hit the cement and looked to be somewhat injured. The Eliminator made it to his feet first and whipped GUNS through the ring steps... GUNS was able to get back to his feet and his charge at the Eliminator was met with a Back Body Drop that landed GUNS right back on the cement. The Eliminator nailed him once more into the ring post this time and then The Eliminator rolled in to escape the count. GUNS was not able to do the same and was counted out. PARSONS: The arena was so quiet when the referee counted to ten and called for the bell that it was almost frightful. No one could believe that GUNS wasn't going to advance. I think everyone, myself included had thought he'd be in the final four... if not in the final match. BUCKLEY: I totally agree Stan. GUNS, considered by most to be one of the best in the sport, if not already a legend, was thought to be the top man in that bracket, but he was unable to meet the expectations. One really has to wonder how good this Eliminator can be. MELTON: Well don't forget he wasn't able to really beat GUNS... a countout victory is at best weak. GUNS will probably still be a force and now he has a mission... REVENGE. If he's the same as he used to be, you can guarantee that he'll be back looking to even the score. Julius Godreign vs. Michael Sparks BUCKLEY: The old battled the new tonight as CSWA fresh face Michael Sparks did battle with long time veteran of the wrestling world, Julius Godreign. The two faced off in what was a very interesting and well fought scientific match for the first ten minutes. Both men showing impressive technical apptitude as they kept long portions of the match on the mat, using various headlocks, arm bars, chinlocks, submission holds, and the like. Godreign though, the crafty veteran took advantage of a mishap and began to look like he was going to dominate the match up.
