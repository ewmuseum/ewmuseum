Forums Featured Archives CSWA Roleplay Central CSWA Fan Discussion CSWA: Blue Moon Thread starter Chad Start date Jun 1, 2012 Chad The Godfather Jun 1, 2012 #1 Yes, it's (sad, but) true, the CSWA returns on July 8 with a card called CSWA: BLUE MOON. Other than being a reference to the CSWA's slow pace of the past, what is BLUE MOON? You'll find out more here soon. 4 matches to be announced. UNIFIED World Championship: "The Ego Buster" Dan Ryan vs. "The Epitome" Troy Windham The match that was supposed to bring the CSWA back to prominence was interrupted by the Hacker and never took place. The "new" CSWA returns with a nod to the old, restoring the UNIFIED Championship and pitting the last two men to hold it against each other. The last time they were supposed to meet at FISH FUND, there was animosity over the title and Troy's antics. Now, years later, the two have personal animosity over their failed partnership in the Windham Clan. With the UNIFIED Title hanging in the balance, how will the two best wrestlers in the world react? ​
