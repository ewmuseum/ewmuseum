The Bottom Line, January 2001

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Bottom Line Back to TRIBUNE From the desk of CSWA Co-Commissioner Chad Merritt.. "Yoo hoo, I'll make ya famous...." That's right, the Kid is back, and the Enforcer is still enforcing. For those of you who thought Thomas could rid of me once and for all....what were you smoking? Before Austin 3:16, before Sting had "Showtime!", even before Hornet was "Takin' Care of Business", one man was telling it the way it is... ..and that's the bottom line. PREVIOUS COLUMNS January 18, 2001 October 24, 2000 July 12, 2000 April 13, 2000 February 1, 2000 August 21, 1999 December 16, 1998 August 30, 1998 March 29, 1998 April 1996 March 24, 1996 September 29, 1995 September 14, 1995 January 1995 *includes some of the previous columns from "Enforcer Speaks" and "The Bottom Line" March 29, 2001 It never ceases to amaze me, but it's a true all the same -- there are just some people you can never please. First, on a completely different note, let me give my sincerest condolences to Ivy McGinnis on the recent loss of her father. Look at the CSWA as it starts its thirteenth year: Kevin Powers is furious at me because he is known for 'choking' when he's going after the World Title. Somehow it's all my fault. Then there's Jean Rabesque, who is just sure that I have some ulterior motive against him. And then we've got the "ClaimStakers." All three of them, but especially Hornet, feel like I'm to blame for all the 'wrongs' the wrestling world has perpetrated against them. You know, there's no doubt that I've done some things I'm not proud of in the business. I was busy playing 'evil promoter' long before Vince McMahon had any on-air role other than announcer. But the truth is, I've done more to help build careers then I can remember, both because it helped build my business, the CSWA fanbase, and because it was the right thing to do. Here's the question though? Whatever happened to being responsible for your own actions? Whatever happened to personal responsibility? Why is it alright to find the nearest scapegoat, the nearest person in authority and blame them for everything that's gone wrong in your life. And here in the wrestling world, it appears the favorite scapegoat isn't dear old Mom or Dad, it's me. Well, let me break it down for you guys. Powers...I'm sorry you haven't won the World Title. Don't you think I was pulling for it when you and Eli were bringing down the house with your feud? But you didn't get the job done. Rabesque...it's not my fault that you're not a national name...yet. Don't you think I want you to be a big draw? But that doesn't mean you automatically walk in and get everything you want. Eli...it's not my fault your wife died. It's not my fault you felt underappreciated by the fans. I've given you a paycheck for years, as well as every opening I can. Randalls...it's not my fault you're a sadomasochistic goon. That was beaten into your head by whoever long before you and I ever met. I've done everything I can to break it out of you before you kill somebody on my time. Hornet...it's not my fault that you're an egotistical son of a mama's boy. It's not my fault you think everyone in this world is out to get you. I've given you every break in this business...I helped you build your character from a cheap rip-off to one of the ultimate trademarks in the sport. So don't step up on your high horse and accuse me of taking your soul...because I don't think you had one intact to start with. I'm tried of being the scapegoat around here folks. I've been called corrupt for years...and maybe that was true for a while. But things are a little different now. ClaimStakers...if you want my company, then you're going to have to pry it from my dead hands. Because I'll fight with all I've got to help keep afloat this ship I've been sailing on for thirteen years. It doesn't just belong to me...it belongs to all the employees who have set foot on it...and to all the fans who have contributed as well. So if you want a mutiny boys...then prepare to get keelhauled. Kayfabe note: Don't miss previous "Bottom Line" articles to the left. And not only that, but we've gone back and dredged up ancient history in "Enforcer Speaks" from 1995 and 1996!
