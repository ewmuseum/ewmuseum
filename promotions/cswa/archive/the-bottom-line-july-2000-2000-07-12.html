The Bottom Line Back to TRIBUNE From the desk of CSWA Co-Commissioner Chad Merritt.. "Yoo hoo, I'll make ya famous...." That's right, the Kid is back, and the Enforcer is still enforcing. For those of you who thought Thomas could rid of me once and for all....what were you smoking? Before Austin 3:16, before Sting had "Showtime!", even before Hornet was "Takin' Care of Business", one man was telling it the way it is... ..and that's the bottom line. To those who haven't...well, you've got 24 hours to get one in before you're fired. So...there have been three major questions thrown at me in the last few weeks...I'm sure you can imagine what they are. First, how does it feel to be the sole owner of the CSWA? And second, how does it feel to have put on such a great show like ANNIVERSARY 2000. Well, the short answer is, it feels great. Stephen is on to bigger and brighter things like directly the upcoming movie featuring an animatronic Elvis, as well as planning stunts on MTV's "Jackass" show. I think we can all agree that he's found the perfect fit. As far as A2K goes...I never could have imagined it being a bigger show. Every wrestler on the show knew that this was the biggest show of their careers to date, except for a couple of notable exceptions. Which leads me to a roundabout answer of the third question. There are, and always have been, certain wrestlers in the business, and even in the CSWA, who thought they could do whatever they want, simply because they're wrestlers. There appears to be some genetic defect in the mind of some grapplers, or maybe it's beat into them, that makes them believe that because they have one God-given ability, that it makes them some sort of supreme being. Thus, they feel like the world is in the palm of their hand...and it doesn't matter who they step on or disrespect. Men like Cardigo Mysterian...better known as "Famine", who went so far as to kill a man in the ring as part of a feud. It put a black eye on the sport. As far as I'm concerned, Famine should be behind bars for the rest of his life. And then there are men like Hornet. Like Mike Randalls. Like Eli Flair. I apologize to you fans who had to see the biggest pity party on the face of the earth at A2K. I hadn't seen anything like it since Guns decided to ruin any shred of dignity or reputation that he had by walking around with a bottle of piss around his neck. Hornet, Randalls and Flair. Men who have made millions off this sport. But now it goes against their 'principles'. Now it's my fault for using them to make money. It's my fault because I'd allowed all 'this' to happen to them. Just what is it that I did that ruined their lives? I'm not the one that killed Alicia Flair. I didn't even use that tragedy to try and blame someone else...like Mike Randalls did. I'm not the one that ran into a burning building and got injured. I'm certainly not the one that decided to run off to Japan and the nuclear wasteland of Arizona in an attempt to 'find myself.' I didn't sleep with my best friend's wife and betray my girlfriend, causing her unenviable heartache. I didn't turn on my brother-in-law in favor of that aforementioned 'soulmate' either, like a certain woman we know did. I didn't force anyone to 'go extreme' and end up in the hospital for major surgery. What did I do? I suspended a man who put a stake through another man's leg. I took the title away from a man who plunged a branding iron into another man's back. I helped a gangbanger turned wrestling superstar make a name for himself and make enough money to do whatever he wanted in life. I find it hard to sympathize with those three men. They've all been World Champions of their own making. They've all been millionaires with my help. Well folks...I may not be able to fire them...but I can sure make life interesting, can't I? Hornet's right...there are certain things I can't do to him because of his contract. But in between the ropes...he's still subject to me....and he's gonna find that out.
