Robert W. Ryder Invitational Tag Team Tournament

July 1997

Robert W. Ryder Invitational Tag Team Tournament You've been patient....and now's it time. So stop holding your breath, and look below for the latest infomation on the now-underway ROBERT W. RYDER INVITATIONAL TAG TEAM TOURAMENT. 'Cause Bob may be in the hospital, but the show must go on! This is your page for the latest information on the Ryder Tournament from the first rounds to the finals. Be sure to check here and on the boards for special updates. The Robert W. Ryder Invitational Tag Team Tournament is a PRODIGY-based Wrestling Bulletin Boardwide Tournament, consisting of 32 generated tag teams from leagues across PRODIGY. The tournament is being held in the honor of Bob Ryder, wrestling's Internet Czar. This one's for you, Bubba! <G> RYDER First Round Pairings BRACKET ONE The Waveriders vs. "Lost Soul" Bryan Simek/Ace The Winners vs. Ares/Death Scar & Roadkill vs. Glory ARROGANCE vs. Destruction Crew BRACKET TWO The Dream Team vs. Masquerade Bruisers Killer Instinct vs. Dawn of a New Day Steve Fiennes/partner to-be-announced vs. Bonecrusher/Prophet Hells Assassins vs. Midwest Wrecking Crew BRACKET THREE Armed Forces vs. Mark Windham/MJ Dean The Mountain Lions vs. Butterball/Sgt. Riddick Blow Fantastics vs. Storm The Wylde Brothers vs. The Blood Brothers BRACKET FOUR Hornet/Randalls vs. Ancient Ones Light & Darkness vs. Og & Cthulhu Dirty Rotten Scoundrels vs. Steve Lewiston/Jean Rabesque Masters of Misery vs. Jonathon Nash/Rob Anthony Roleplay Deadline for First and Second Rounds: July 20 The first and second rounds will be posted the week of July 20th, following the roleplay deadline. Roleplay will be judged on quality, and matches will be decided by the Unified Championship Committee. The rounds of 32 and 16 will be posted concurrently, following which a roleplay deadline will be posted with the third round. Only roleplay done in Ryder Tournament subjects on the Wrestling BB will be accepted. Angles may be e-mailed in and will be considered in matches as well by the UCC.
