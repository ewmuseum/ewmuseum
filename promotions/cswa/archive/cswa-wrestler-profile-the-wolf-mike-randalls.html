CSWA Wrestler Profile: "The Wolf" Mike Randalls Name: "The Wolf" Mike Randalls Hometown: Mojave Desert, CA Height: 6'4 Weight: 243 Age: 29 Alignment: Who Knows? Music: "Oh Well" by Fleetwood Mac Biography/Profile: See Randalls' "Spotlight" Profile here Appearance: Long, straight brown hair, trimmed brown beard, green eyes Personality: Well, he's been in a crazy house more than once, but he's an intelligent man. Very calculated, it's just that not many people agree with his philosophies. The only side he's really ever on, is his own...and he's got a short fuse, so he really doesn't back down from challenges, unless he sees an opportunity for later. Characteristics: Comes down to the ring in a red cloak with the hood over his head; he walks very slowly and deliberately. On the back of the cloak is a yin/yang superimposed over the face of a wolf. He wears pants similar to the Great Muta w/ a snarling wolf on the left side, Chinese letters on the right side, and a yin/yang on his butt. He's barefoot when he wrestles. In the ring, he's obviously the extreme type. He'll try anything and everything to hurt his opponent. He's not afraid to hurt himself, matches usually are bloody, etc. History: Born in Raleigh, NC, later wrestled in college, after college he really had nothing. He started training under Eastern philosophies, but then tried out professional wrestling first coming to the CSWA. You know the rest...for the last two years, Randalls has been in the Orient experimenting in self-masochism, and new wrestling techniques...more painful ones. Previous Titles Held: Various World Titles Unified World Heavyweight Championship (twice) --defeated Hornet, post-Winter's Warriors VI (3/9/95-5/13/95) --won IRONMAN of CHAMPIONS (1997) Enterprise World Championship (twice) --defeated Flic Rair to become first EN Champion (11/12/94-c. 12/19/94) --defeated Timmy Windham (c. 12/23/94-3/9/95) Moves: 1. Implant DDT 2. Ringpost Figure-4 Leglock 3. Royal Octopus Hold 4. STF w/bridge 5. TR Flying Headbutt to Opp. Head 6. Belly to back suplex 7. Reverse knife edges 8. Scorpion deathlock 9. TR Superplex 10. Diamond Cutter 11. Full Nelson German suplex Finisher: The Devastator (leg lock combined with arching backbend and one-arm headlock)
