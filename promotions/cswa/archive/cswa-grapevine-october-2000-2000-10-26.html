CSWA Grapevine:  The Rumor Mill's Cookin! Back to the TRIBUNE For years, I've been accused of being a "Hornet mark." I am. So what? I'm a wrestling commentator...that doesn't mean I don't have opinions. And I believe that Hornet is one of the men that made this business. For years, he did it the 'right' way, a way that I respected. I was outraged when he was kidnapped ten years ago, when the Unified Title was taken away from him seven years ago, when he was hospitalized two years ago. I was outraged when he put a branding iron in Mark Vizzack's back. But 'outrage' isn't the word for what happened at ANNIVERSARY 2000. I guess I'm still in shock. Hornet and Eli, I can understand. I could even understand it if they wanted to 'be bad' as a team. But Mike Randalls? I guess we traditionally think of Randalls as a loner...but he's always been willing to team up to reach a goal. Whether it was with FULL FORCE, or even with GUNS against America's Team, Randalls knows how to play the odds. So who's the 'leader' of this 'pack,' to borrow Deacon's metaphor? Is it Randalls? Has he found some way to manipulate Hornet and Eli? Or is it Hornet? Did he somehow use his friendship with Eli and his history with Randalls to create this coaltion? Is it Eli or Ivy? Did Eli see the writing on the wall and find some way to forget common ground between the two enemies? I don't know. I do know that this isn't America's Team. The friendship that Hornet and Windham had helped keep them together for years...but even they had some problems. I think the egos in this group are too big. They all have the same ambition. And eventually, they're all going to have the same goal. It may be sooner rather than later, but the seams are going to be exposed quickly. The question is: will they stretch, or will they burst? UPDATED 10/26/00 Updated 10/26/00 I'm Rudy Seitzer, and welcome to CSWA Grapevine, where we track down the rumors running rampant here in the world of wrestling. As always, I have to start with a standard disclaimer: we cannot verify any of the rumors reported here. Some may be true, some may be outright false...but they've all been passed to me by various sources. So don't come crying to Rudy...got it? Thomas is gone...I guess that means I'll finally get my choice of cereal in the CS cafeteria. I've never seen a man that could eat more cereal in my life. So was the lawsuit all a gag? Appears that it was and it wasn't. Thomas and Merritt were actually in court to draw up the 'division of power' in CS Enterprises, as well as address concerns from the FTC. The court normally requires mediation in these types of decisions. So the Mike Randalls/Hornet/Eli Flair association has been in the planning stages for just under a year? And I didn't get a whiff of it? This may qualify as "best kept secret of the year"...and maybe longer. Speaking of the 'triumverate', Poison Ivy and Hornet have been relatively circumspect on where their 'after-hours' relationship stands. I've heard that things have been heating up, but no major announcements yet. Deacon may have been putting on a brave face. I've confirmed reports that Deacon has been having that right arm looked at by specialists. He entered the match at ANNIVERSARY with a tingling sensation in his arm. While his shoulder may not be at 100% following the match, doctors are trying to find out if the two are related. Commissioner Merritt has stated that he may not be able to fire or suspend Hornet, but that he can deal with him in the ring. I've heard rumors that he may actually start things *outside* the ring. The question is, who's on Merritt's side? Is a Powers of Love reunion in the works? One might think so after Love helped put Powers into the World Title match in Pensacola by laying down for him at ANNIVERSARY. But I don't think it's gonna happen. Sunshine's back...and she's bad. Did you get a load of the change from goth to hot? But where's Vizzack? He hasn't even been spotted backstage, which is odd for the rarely-separated two-some. Is he biding his time, or has Sunshine got a new managerial focus? After hearing his name invoked at ANNIVERSARY, could Ray S. Cornette be planning a restart of his CORPORATION? Cornette retired almost three years ago, and has been focusing his efforts on a small business in promoting entertainment events. Word has it that the old wrestling juices may be flowing. Does this mean that he might be able to coax Joey Melton out of the retirement home as well? Or even Wall? First JW Locke, then Erik Zieba, is another independent promotion exec about to show up in the CSWA? That's it for now, Fans... I'll keep you posted.
