(fade in to a wide shot of the empty FISH FUND PARK Arena, where CSWA crews are putting the finishing touches on the arena for FISH FUND XI. The shot cuts to Bill Buckley, at the commentators' table.) BB: Fans, I want to be the first to welcome you to the FISH FUND Park Arena here, where we're just hours away from one of the CSWA's biggest events of the year. Beginning today and continuing tomorrow, this arena will not only be filled to 103,000 capacity, but it'll hold two nights of the top wrestling action that you've ever seen. This card has been dubbed the "End Of An Era." Either tonight or tomorrow, CS Enterprises, Chad Merritt and Stephen Thomas, the current co-owners of the CSWA, will continue their 'quest' to find new management for the CSWA. Way back at BATTLE OF THE BELTS XIV, Merritt and Thomas dropped a bombshell, when they announced that they were bored with running the top federation in the wrestling business. Since then, they've 'defended' their ownership against all-comers in the ring. Tonight, they'll put a million dollars and that ownership on the line against Maxwell Diamond and Dr. Knight after the WAR GAMES Main Event. Should they survive that match, they'll face their final opponents in part of the Triple Main Event on Day Two. At PRIMETIME POOLJAM in Key West, PRODIGY Board Leader Bob Ryder announced his intention to accept the challenge leveled at him by CS Enterprises. But to Merritt and Thomas's dismay, he introduced his tag team partners as former CSWA Vice-President Jon "Cruncher" Katz and a mystery partner. We've been told that Merritt and Thomas will have current CSWA Commissioner AJ Honold as their tag team partner. But against former wrestling champion "Cruncher" Katz and whoever else Katz and Ryder have up their sleeve, I'm not sure if Thomas, Merritt, and AJ are up to the task. And while that's a major part of this two-day colossal event, folks, it's certainly not all of it. On both days, eight young superstars will face off in the first ever Double Elimination Tournament held in the CSWA. The prize on the line? The Greensboro Heavyweight Championship. These eight men, mostly newcomers to the CSWA, are looking to etch their names on the CSWA's roster of champions. And speaking of that Greensboro Championship, the man who formerly held it will have his hands full over these two days as well. Current Enterprise World Heavyweight Champion ANARKY will face two men who want him for different reasons on the first night. In a triangle match without the EN World Championship on the line, former EN World Champion and the man who lost the title to Anarky, "The Living Legend" Mark Windham, is looking to prove that the EN World Champion's win over him was a fluke. But Windham's still got a lot on his mind, especially the return of his younger Timmy Windham, who brought the news that their father, Alan, is dying of terminal cancer. In the third corner, "The Strongest Arms In The World" GUNS, will be looking for a repeat of his last CSWA triangle match, in which he defeated Mike Randalls and Mark Windham. GUNS and Anarky certainly have heat between them, considering that GUNS has been proclaiming Anarky as a 'glorified jobber' since Anarky broke into the CSWA months ago. Most recently, GUNS has been calling Anarky the "King of Glorified Jobbers," and his vicious attack on Anarky in Nashville at an FWC card made everything personal. Anarky is looking for respect, Windham is looking for revenge, and GUNS is looking for, well, who knows what he's looking for. It'll either be "The Living Legend," "The Strongest Arms In The World", or the current holder of three world championships who emerges victorious. And all three of those men will continue to be busy throughout FISH FUND. Anarky will put his EN World Championship on the line against Heartbreaker on the second night. However, things get a little stickier as another stipulation comes into play. The loser of the match will have to leave the CSWA for one month. Can Heartbreaker avenge his loss to Anarky for the Greensboro Championship from just one month ago? As for Mark Windham, he'll continue to be in action on the first night, when he steps into the most devastating match that the wrestling world has ever known. Along with his America's Team partner and best friend, Hornet, CS Express, and a mystery partner, Windham will face off against long-time rivals Tom Adler, the <> Cutters, as well as the Untouchables. The Main Event will boil over as ten men enter WAR GAMES: THE MATCH BEYOND. We'll have much more on that later. That won't be all for "The Living Legend" either. On the second night, if both he and "Mr. Magnificent" Tom Adler are still able, they'll face off against each other with the CSWA's United States/National Championship on the line. Tom Adler took the title from Scotty Michaels, and then marked the logo of the AAWC on the title. But that belt is one that Mark Windham has held on at least four separate occasions. Adler has added fuel to an already raging fire. And on the second night, one of these men will attempt to settle it, once and for all. And for GUNS, FISH FUND won't end with his first night triangle match. In part of Day Two's TRIPLE MAIN EVENT, "The Strongest Arms In The World" will face off one more time against the man who has been called the "Franchise" of the CSWA. When that match concludes and the smoke clears, either HORNET or GUNS will have to walk out of the CSWA once and for all. Hornet has made it very clear that he won't allow it to be him, while GUNS has made it obvious that he expects it to be him. We'll find out tomorrow night. And again, folks, we'll have much more on that match as FISH FUND XI gets underway. The CSWA's Intracontinental Championship is on the line on night one, as IC Champ Wild Asian looks to defend the belt against the man who you could say helped win it for him. Mark Windsor's vicious attack on Billy Starr may have enabled Wild Asian to take the belt in just seconds, but it was obvious that Asian didn't feel obligated to Windsor in the least. Windsor looks to take his first taste of CSWA gold, while Asian looks to hold on to a belt that will help him establish his name in the upper echelon once and for all. Steven Gage continues his CSWA return, taking on one of the CSWA's ranked competitors, the Heartbreaker. His friend, Aaron Reno, will look to continue his climb up the rankings, taking on a man who can only be called an extremist, Nathan Storm. Another triangle match will be in the works, as three of the Greensboro Tournament competitors looks to settle a score. Rocky Ford Meloneer, Wolf, and Clint "Critical Mass" Collins, look to establish dominance when they face off. Also, tag team partners in POWER UNLIMITED will go after singles glory, as TNT looks to take on #4-ranked Unified contender, Xavier Virile, and to avenge his tag team partner's loss to the same man. Meanwhile, the Grim Reaper will take on X in singles competition. And folks, that's just part of it. Also in tag competition, Unified Tag Team Champions Japanese Assault look to merge their belts with the EN World Tag Team Titles. The only problem? Generation X-Press has had a break-up, when Shawn Matthews took it upon himself to throw the belts into a trash can, and then turn on partner Troy Windham. Windham has announced that he'll defend the titles by himself if need-be. And like I said, that's just part of all the Day One action here at FISH FUND XI.
