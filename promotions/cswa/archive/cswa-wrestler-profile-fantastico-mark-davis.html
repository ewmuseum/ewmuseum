CSWA Wrestler Profile: "Fantastico" Mark Davis Name: "Fantastico" Mark Davis Hometown: Sacramento, CA Height: 6'2" Weight: 230 lbs Age: 22 Alignment: Face Entrance Music: "We Don't Give A..." by The Murderers Manager: Terra Lopez Biography/Profile: Appearance: Mark is a little guy with short brown hair, he's got a nice build for his size, and he's lightning quick. He has only one tattoo on his right bicep which is a barbwire thing, and in the middle of it it has "Fantastico" looking as if its covered in the barbwire. He has blue tights that say Mark Davis down each side and he wears white boots, his fists are taped up. For an interview he'll probablly wear his street clothes. History: Davis comes from a long time wrestling family. Davis is a high flyer extraordinaire. He got into wrestling when his father dreamed it would be great for him to become a wrestler. Mark didn't like the idea of his father planning out his future at first, but agreed after wrestling his first match in an independent promotion. Previous Titles Held: Moves: 1.
