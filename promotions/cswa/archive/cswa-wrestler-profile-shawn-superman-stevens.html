CSWA Wrestler Profile: Shawn Stevens Name: Shawn "Superman" Stevens Hometown: New York City, NY Height: 6'6" Weight: 268 lbs Age: 26 Alignment: Heel Entrance Music: "New Noise" by Refused Biography/Profile: Shawn Stevens grew up in New York City. His mother had died when he was very young. His parental guardian was his stepfather who was an abusive alcoholic who tore the life out of Shawn's childhood. Stevens moved on, as he grew older Stevens began to bulk up...He joined many different exercise programs, but it wasn't until he finally was accepted into Michigan University under a college scholarship that he was able to show his true talents. The pressure began too much for him to handle; trying to handle his destiny was tough...so he turned down a path he should have thought twice about. He started taking steroids to improve his muscle mass. He began to win more and more in college as no one could stop this machine or the danger he was causing, until he was forced to take a test. A test to prove if he was taking steroids or not... He was kicked out of Michigan University, it led to a life of crime, which led to his brutal instincts and emotions that would evolve more as his life went on. After a life a crime he found his way to a an independent federation in the outskirts of Pittsburgh Pennsylvania, where he met up with a man who would later become his Tag-Team …Alex Strider. After a long Title reign as champions, they were left in the dust as the federation went under. They found their way to TCW, where they became huge stars off the bat. Their friendship would soon crumble. Shawn Stevens began to grow more with anger and intensity, as Alex began to gain the respect and admiration he deserved.
