CSWA Wrestler Profile: Mark Vizzack Name: "Daredevil" Mark Vizzack Hometown: Height: 6'2" Weight: 228 Age: 25 Alignment: Face Music: "Smoke on the Water" - Deep Purple Manager/Valet: Sunshine Del Payne Biography/Profile: Appearance: Vizzy looks like Brandon Lee, sans the Crow makeup. Like, how he looked in the "flashbacks" in the movie, or in Rapid Fire. He typically has his hair pulled back into a ponytail. His ring attire is Shawn Michaels-esque tights, only they are a solid color, usually black or dark blue, with "DAREDEVIL" emblazoned on the side. For his ring entrance, add to this an Eric Bischoff-like leather jacket, only VERY worn and faded - like he's had it for YEARS and it's been through a lot of wear and tear. Ring Entrance: He and Sunshine come through the curtain. He looks around at everyone and gives off a HUGE shout, whilst Sunshine gives a slightly nervous smile. They smack hands with as many fans as they can, and do a lap around the ring before getting in. Vizzy slingshots himself over the top rope, then holds the ropes down for Sunshine to step through. During the match, Sunshine will typically not interfere, but as we've seen, exceptions can be made. History: Vizzy started wrestling in 1992 in a promotion in Washington called the HCW where he won the National Championship in his fourth match. He had told the promoters they were holding him down, so they put him in the ring against the marquee wrestler of the place, Tough Guy, in hopes that they would humble their upstart, eighteen year old rookie. After pleading guilty to charges of assault (He beat up Sunshine's abusive boyfriend), he was dismissed from the HCW, and signed with two promotions in his home town of Baltimore, the AWA and BWL. In the BWL, he met a man named Julius Kessler, and they immediately took a liking to each other, forming a tag team that, to this day, is technically the BWL World Tag Team Champions. The AWA beckoned with a World Title shot, and Vizzy stepped up and won it. A month later, the inevitable happened. The Nightmare Maker, with his manager, Silky Rose, won a Battle Royal, giving the giant of a man, the United States champion, a shot at his belt. But when the day came, Vizz was nowhere to be found. The belt, consequently, was awarded to The Nightmare Maker. Three days later, the rematch. 5/28/97. Nightmare was ready, Rose was ready... and Vizz brought with him a young girl who looked like death warmed over. He battled with Nightmare for twenty five minutes, before this girl pulled him to the outside, whispered something in his ear, and sent him back in, only to have him garner the pinfall in less then five minutes. The rest, as they say, is history. :) Vizzack entered the CSWA in early 1998 as one of its new talent for the 'reopening' at ANNIVERSARY 1998. He made waves during the World Championship Tournament; but quickly rode the crest of a title wave as he feuded with "Hurricane" Eddy Love, eventually taking the CSWA World Title at ELVIS LIVES XI in January 1999. His celebration was marred when friend and mentor Hornet attacked Vizzack just minutes after the title win. Hornet, with the help of Poison Ivy, attacked Vizzack with a branding iron, eventually branding "TCB" on Vizzack's lower back, standing for "Takin' Care of Business." Vizzack's title reign was marred by interference, as Hornet made sure that no one else would secure the title from him. Vizzack was fed up, at one point even dropping the belt in the ring and leaving. That set up their SHOWTIME matchup, where Hornet stacked the deck and took the title.
