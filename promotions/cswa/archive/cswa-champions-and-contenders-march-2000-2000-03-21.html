Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
CSWA Champions and Contenders The CSWA Championship Committee periodically issues new lists of contenders for the CSWA's titles. While these lists are generally referred to when booking title matches, challengers are not limited to top contenders. Title Histories In a special feature below, we'll be giving you some of the history behind the belts. While the entire 11-year history would take pages, we'll give you looks at recent history. CSWA World Heavyweight Championship (since ANNIVERSARY 1998) From its return after Merritt's 'unfortunate incarceration,' the CSWA immediately looked to crown a new World Champion. Unfortunately, every opportunity was met with interference. From the ANNIVERSARY 1998 match between Hornet and GUNS for the title; to the later four-way between Troy and Mark Windham, and Hornet and GUNS, no one could claim the title. CSWA officials finally announced a tournament, with the Final Four commencing at FISH FUND XII.
