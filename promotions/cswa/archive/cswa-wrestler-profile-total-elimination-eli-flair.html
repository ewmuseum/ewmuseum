CSWA Wrestler Profile: Eli Flair Name: "Total Elimination" Eli Flair Hometown: Bronx, NY Height: 6'9" Weight: 303 lbs Age: 28 Alignment: Face Entrance Music: "Wait and Bleed" by Slipknot Manager/Valet: Poison Ivy Biography/Profile: He has black hair, shoulder length, and usually unkempt and hanging. Black eyes. His arms and chest are covered with tattoos, as well as scars from his years as a wrestler. He has a very good mind for ring psychology, unparalleled by nearly anyone in the game today. However, if he gets angry, he stops thinking and wrestles through instinct and emotion. His years of wrestling have taken their toll - in addition to the scars that adorn his body, he also has two 'bionic' knees, the culmination of three different surgeries. His entire family is deceased, including his wife, Alicia. Currently managed by Alicia's sister, Ivy McGinnis, as well as involved with a woman named M that he met during the 1999 POOLJAM tour. Eli began in the CSWA in the mid-nineties as "The Eliminator."
