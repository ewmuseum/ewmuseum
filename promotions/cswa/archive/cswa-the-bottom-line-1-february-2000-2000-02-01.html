The Bottom Line, 1 February 2000

The Bottom Line Back to TRIBUNE From the desk of CSWA Co-Commissioner Chad Merritt.. "Yoo hoo, I'll make ya famous...." That's right, the Kid is back, and the Enforcer is still enforcing. For those of you who thought Thomas could rid of me once and for all....what were you smoking? Before Austin 3:16, before Sting had "Showtime!", even before Hornet was "Takin' Care of Business", one man was telling it the way it is... ..and that's the bottom line. Previous Articles August 21, 1999 April 23, 1999 December 16, 1998 August 30, 1998 March 29, 1998 Enforcer Speaks September 14, 1995 September 29, 1995 January 1996 March 24, 1996 April 1996 Updated 2/1/00 You know, I could go into the vast corruption you people saw under "Commish" Thomas... but frankly, we all know about it, and it wouldn't be productive to go into it here. All I'll say is.....aren't ya glad I'm back? And I am back, folks. For better or worse. No more power plays, no more ownership deals...Steve and I have come to an agreement following the Thanksgiving fiasco. And now...to the business at hand. As I discussed at TWS, the CSWA didn't have any major events scheduled for the holiday season. Due to lack of planning and foresight by a colleague who shall remain nameless, the CSWA found itself trying to scramble to book arenas for events. I decided rather than giving the fans shows that were less than CSWA-caliber...that we'd hold off and kick off the new millennium with a bang...and a purpose. WINTER'S WARRIORS is just over a month away....and it should be interesting to see how things shake out. Deacon is the World Champion, Eddy Love wants to return to the throne, Eli Flair is out with injuries, and Troy Windham has 'lost his smile', so to speak. And then there's the return of Hornet and Mark Vizzack, JT Tyler...and who knows who else. All former World Champs in their own right. And how can you count out some of the other CSWA talent that's converging on the top tier...men like Blade, Aaron Douglas, Steve Radder, Kevin Powers, Lawrence Stanley, Randy Harders, Wicked Sight...and so many others. The talent roster in the CSWA is close to an all-time high...and I intend to put it over the top. At the upcoming CSTV and PRIMETIME events, I'll be making some additional announcements concerning upcoming events, championships, as well as some changes in the front office. The CSWA has historically been known for surprises....as TWS continued to show. And we've only just begun.... Kayfabe note: Don't miss previous "Bottom Line" articles to the left. And not only that, but we've gone back and dredged up ancient history in "Enforcer Speaks" from 1995 and 1996!
