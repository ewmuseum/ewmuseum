CSWA Wrestler Profile: Simply Stunning Name: "Simply Stunning" "Simply Devine" Michael Hardy Hometown: Sunderland, England Height: 6'2" Weight: 280 Age: 20 "Stunning" Simon Wilcox Hometown: Sunderland, England Height: 6'0" Weight: 275 Age: 20 Alignment: Face Entrance music: "Let Me Entertain You." - Robbie Williams. Biography/Profile: Two young guys who like to entertain the fans. Their love of the arial style of wrestling thrilled their fans in England, now they are looking for a bigger stage to thrill people and thrill themselves. Ever since they can remember these two have been close friends. Brought up on American Wrestling as children their heroes were the Rockers. As they grew up their natural builds allowed them to follow in their heroes footsteps. They competed in England but the competition was not of the standard of that in America. Their families saved up knowing that "Simply Stunning" would be a huge success both with the fans and in the ring. Hardy and Wilcox both wear costumes like the Rockers as a sort of a tribute. The are carefree young men who like to have a good time. They are however tough competitors who despite their love of ariel wrestling can mix it with their fists in they have to. They dislike cheating and try to wrestle to the letter of the law when in the ring. Some say that their quest to entertain the fans will cost them matches as they like to go for high risk flashy moves too often. Previous Titles Held: Moves: 1. Dropkick 2. Flying Elbow 3.
