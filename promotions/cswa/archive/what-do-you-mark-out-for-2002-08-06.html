P.S. I agree, I'm a huge MoA mark too! :-) GreggG — 08-07-02, 01:23 PM The things I mark out for the most is the insane attention to detail that has become a CSWA trademark. (Actually, now that I think about it, it's probably more of a JN/[name withheld] trademark that I and a few others stole.)

The RP's of [name withheld], NJ, KS, TW, Chad and (sometimes) Steve (and, I hope, myself) are really of the old-school style. I recently wrote on the EW Forums about this style... where less is more. These guys write in a style where there is a lot of description without there being a lot of words used to describe said description. There'a a great usage of word economy and the like to get across points, which is really effective.

On top of that, other old-time RPers like [name withheld] and Steve also go about RPing in different ways a lot of times, but the same sense of not being too wordy and letting the story come about naturally comes across great.

And that sense has also been really picked up on by the guys who came on board after the move over to the web. Some guys are really old-school with how they write-- Eddy Love, Steve Radder, Kevin Powers, Lawrence Stanley come to mind. Other guys use the more "current" style that is popular in other leagues but have tweaked the way they write towards the CSWA-- Triple X comes to mind. And then others are somewhere in between, like Evan Aho and Wicked Sight.

But everyone has one thing in common in their writing styles-- there's an attention to detail and the little things that really make the CSWA what it is.
