LIVE! From Dayton, Ohio January, 11th, 2002 "Sinister Urge" by Rob Zombie plays and clips of the events that led up to the PPV are showed. It stops with a picture of Venom and Myrddin face to face. The screen goes black and opens up to a blue and green logo flashing across the screen. It says "PPV Prelude" and as the camera zooms out it shows Chad Madden smiling. As the music is still playing, Chad slides over to the desk and looks at the camera. Chad: Hello I'm Chad Madden and I will be hosting this weeks special edition of PPV Prelude. I'm sure you Jackass's are all dying to see what Santa Madden has in store. And now, I don't have to listen to the incessant whining of that baby Mike Boom! Now to my knowledge, which of course you know is a lot, the Tag Lottery will have seven teams, each drawn at random by President Paxton and the last one will be determined at the tag matches at the PPV. And the winner's will be crowned with the LTD Tag Championships! Madden looks around at takes a deep breath. He stares at the camera and prepares to speak.. Chad: And like I said before there will be one tag match to qualify into the tag tournament. The tag match between Kensuke Suzahara and Jin Akujin, boy is that a mouth full, and their opponents will be "The Loose Cannon" Willie Style and "Crimson Lord" Michael Djin. Chad: We also have another tag match between HaVoC and (He looks perplexed at the name) Humpy? What kind a a name is that?! And Baine? Were do these guys get there names at!? I mean come on, HaVoC is the most reasonable name in the universe. It's simple, original and describes what they look like!! HAHAHAH!! Oh you guys know I'm kidding . But theses two , well I don't know if you can call one of them but, theses two "teams" will be fighting it out in a tables match. HaVoC has a score to settle as Humpy and Baine interfered in their match a few weeks ago from the front row!. Role that clip!! As most of you Jackass's should know by now, Poltergeist and Black Wolf DON NOT like each other. Man when I saw that pilled lump called Myrddin, I almost wet my pants laughing so hard!! Madden: Ladies and Germs, we are here in the butthole of America known as Dayton, Ohio for the Winter’s Wrath PPV which is guaranteed to be a huge show. It’s a miracle that all the members of the fed are still alive. Ok, now let’s get down to Business! Dr. Paxton: If you will ladies, get the lottery machine ready. Dr. Paxton: Our first competitor in the Tag Title Tournament is…Inferno Kid! Paxton holds up the ball and smiles as the crowd boos. Dr. Paxton: OK, and his tag team partner will be…. The crowd expresses it’s disbelief and anger by booing again and starts a “fixed” chant. Dr. Paxton: Shut the hell up you fools. Ok, the next team consists of… Another ball is pulled out. Dr. Paxton: Brutakis!! And his partner will be…..Bones! Wow, HaVoC will wrestle together in the tournament! Isn’t that great? The girls nod their heads in approval but the fans continue to show their displeasure and things start to fly in the ring. Dr. Paxton: And now the next team will be………Scott Royal and…. Paxton reaches in, pulls out another ball, looks at it and smiles and then addresses the audience again. Dr. Paxton: The return of “The Icon” Jason Stevens!! The crowd realizes that the drawing is fixed as all the heel wrestlers on Paxton’s side have been chosen to be on the same teams and have all been chosen first. As Paxton argues with the crowd as the boos continue to echo throughout the arena, suddenly ”Big Shot” by Billy Joel hits the PA and out comes the original Big Shot Matt Henstock the former LTD President with Mike Boom, former LTD announcer at his side! The crowd erupts as they walk down to the ring. Paxton looks upset and Henstock shakes his finger at Paxton as they climb into the ring to confront Paxton. Henstock checks out the girls and then walks directly up to Paxton and snatches the mic right out of his hands. Henstock: Well, I don’t know what these girls are smoking to be up here with you or what you promised them but I couldn’t sit back there and watch this tirade any longer. Paxton shrugs his shoulders is disbelief as if he has done nothing wrong. Henstock: OH, I guess it’s just a coincidence that all the wrestlers who have been helping you out lately are picked first and on the same teams? Paxton looks angered but then says “Why not!” Henstock: I’ll even let you pick a team first. Henstock hands the mic to Paxton. Paxton: All right then my first team will be…Venom and Inferno Kid! Paxton: My next team will be HaVoC! Even more boos for the axe handle bandits are announced into the tourney again! Henstock: Let me guess, your third team will be Stevens and Royal? Ok , then I get to pick two more teams. How about Damien Diablo and Poltergeist and then Barabbas and Black Wolf! Paxton nods and gets the mic back from Henstock. Paxton: Well that leaves three spots and one of them will be filled after our first match tonight with Suzahara and Akujin going up against Djin and Style. Henstock: Well that makes it uneven, So I am gonna take your pick of HaVoC back out and make them earn a spot in the tournament tonight if they can win the Tag Tables Match against Humpy and Baine. So the Winner of that match will get the 8th spot in the tournament! Paxton seems angered as the crowd cheers Henstock’s decision and Paxton gets the mic back from Henstock again. Paxton: I don’t like it. This isn’t fair! I am the damn President around here so I say what goes! Yet another mic snatched out of Paxton’s hands. Henstock: You listen here you little dork! You and Me, TONIGHT in this ring, in a street fight, the winner is named the permanent President of LTD and the other has to leave the federation for good! Paxton: You got it and I will finally get rid of this Big Loser from my federation once and for all. Henstock signals for Mike Boom to take his position at ringside with Chad Madden and as Paxton yells at Boom while he is walking over to the announce table one of the ladies hands Henstock a steel chair and Matt nails Paxton over the head with it knocking him out completely and busting his head wide open. Henstock laughs as the girl stands beside him and then Matt spits down on Paxton. Henstock: See you later on fool. I guess now you need a real doctor? The moment they sit down, 'Bullet With Butterfly Wings' kicks in. It soon fades into Creed's 'My Sacrifice', as 'Crimson Lord' Michael Djin and 'The Loose Cannon' Willie Style appear at the top of the ramp. Pyro explodes, as Style just puts his hands in his sides. Djin on the other hand, flips off the crowd, drawing immediate boo's. They walk down to the ring, Style just focused on their opponents, while Djin's talking trash to the fans. Boom: Two more newcomers to LTD! Madden: Now these two seem like nice guys! Crimson Lord slides into the ring, and Style steps up the apron, while on the other side of the ring Akujin stays in the ring, and Suzahara steps out of it. Djin runs in Akujin's direction, as the bell rings for the official start of the match. He goes for a clothesline, but Jin ducks it, and hits Michael in the back with a dropkick. The force of the kick sends Crimson Lord stumbling into the ropes, and bouncing out again, still facing the same direction. He stumbles in Akujin's direction, who tries to hook Djin's arms, and set him up for a Tiger Suplex. He gets the left arm hooked, but Crimson Lord manages to hit Jin in the face with his right elbow. Again. And again. Akujin, finally dazed, releases the hold and stumbles back, while Michael Djin turns around. Boom: 'Crimson Lord' Michael Djin with the advantage here. Madden: I like it! Crimson Lord grabs Akujin's wrist, and Irish whips him into the opposite ropes. Style is back on his feet again, runs at the downed Akujin and hits him with a Running Senton Splash! The Loose Cannon brings Jin Akujin back to his feet, before he whips him into the ropes, and takes him down in a wicked Hurracanrana when he bounces out of them. Style's quick to jump back to his feet, and pulls Akujin back up. He whips him into one of the neutral corners, and runs at him. Akujin's thinking fast however. Boom: Akujin managed to defend himself, and tagged in Suzahara! Madden: He'll get his ass kicked too! Kensuke enters the ring, and immediately storms at Style, who's getting back to his feet.
