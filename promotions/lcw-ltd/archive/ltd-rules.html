Lethal LTD is a federation for improving, developing, and training roleplayers. That is our number one priority here in Lethal LTD, so the matches in this federation will be based 100% ON ROLEPLAYS. Please note that we also judge the roleplays with both quality and quantity. However, as, once again, this is a development federation, we take the finest care not to mis-match any talent when booking, and you will be booked against only people in your level of skill. 2. There is no roleplay minimum or maximum, however, we ask that you put your best effort into all roleplays. We would prefer not to see 10 word roleplays. We also don't want you busting a vain to produce huge roleplays, as there's nothing special about a 10 000 word roleplay. Their boring to read, and nobody wants to read them. As long as you can keep it entertaining, then all of the power to you! 3. As every federation goes, we absolutly hate it when people hound us about when the shows will be posted. So please, don't. 4. Despite what certain people say, Lethal LTD is not only a federation for rookies. We house many big name LCW talent who want to improve or get some roleplaying rust out of their system. We house experienced roleplayers who wants to develop their skills. And we also house new rookies as well, who want to learn the bases. More information about this system can be found on our FAQS page. 5. If you lose a match, please do not complain. One key point to being a good e-wrestler is to accept defeated, trust your staff, and move on. 6. No racial, sexual, national, ethnic, or religious insults are allowed. This rule has zero tolerance to it, as all of the other rules do. 7. Seeing as we hold one event per week, you must roleplay at least once per week. If you do not roleplay at least once per week without a good excuse, it is considered unacceptable, and you will be taken out of the program. 8. Absolutly no OOC wars! 9. ROLEPLAY JUDGING: (details to come soon) 10. TRAINING EXERCISES: Various training exercises will be given otu from time to time to help polish up roleplay skills in certain areas. ex: each member will be emailed interview questions to be answers and returned for an interview promo to see how each roleplayer handles that situation.
