From Uniontown, PA on Tuesday, March 19th, 2002 The camera flies over a set of large mountains and into a small valley and over a small town beneath and centers in on a medium sized venue on the outskirts of town. The sign out front reads, “Leaning Towards Death Wrestling – TONIGHT! LTD Progression’s last show!” Fans are filing in the building with LTD t-shirts and signs. Magman: Uniontown, a medium sized town near the Laurel Mountains in South Western Pennsylvania. This is the hometown of the LTD President Dr. Jacob Paxton and the rest of the now defunct Steel City Committee from the old LCW. Madden: Does that mean we will see the “OutKast” Blaze McCoy again tonight? Magman: Maybe we will but we will definitely see the President here tonight as this is the last Progression show. Madden: That’s right Mags, Progression will have a new name once we go Independent for good at the end of the month and what a show we have for tonight. We have big matches and surprises as always. Let’s get it all underway with another contract match. Somebody’s getting’ FIRED! LTD CONTRACT MATCH: Tommy Steele vs. Viper Magman: Well, we haven’t heard from either of these new LTD competitors in the past week, presumably because they have been too busy training. This should be a good, simple Good guy/Bad guy match up. Madden: Oh just shut up and lets get on with the damn match. The arena is lit, and the crowd are mumbling excitedly in anticipation of this first match of the night. ‘Raise Up’ by Petey Pablo hits, and most of the crowd cheer for Tommy Steele, one of the many newcomers to the federation. Tommy stands at the top of the ramp, and soaks up the audience’s appreciation before walking down the ramp, pulling off his white wife beater, and spinning it around his head. He throws it into the crowd, and climbs in the ring, waiting for his opponent. Redman’s ‘Lets get dirty’ starts, and the crowd know who is going to make his way down to the ring. It takes a few seconds before Viper walks out from the back, to loud boo’s. He shoves his middle finger up to the fans, and walks down to the ring, scowling heavily. He then looks up, and looks Steele straight in the eyes. He doesn’t take his eyes off his opponent until he steps into the ring, and looks round to the fans. They are still boo’ing and shouting obscenities, and he doesn’t look happy. He turns back to Steele, and they tie up. Steele gets Viper in a headlock, and holds it for a few seconds before Viper breaks free. They circle each other, and once again Steele places Viper into a headlock. Once again, he breaks free. The crowd is starting to get impatient now, and are boo’ing loudly. Steele turns around to try to calm the fans down. As soon as his back is turned, Viper lunges at Steele, knocking him down. Magman: That’s a dirty trick! Steele was merely calming the fans down, and Viper took advantage. Viper lifts Tommy Steele up, and throws him to the ropes. As Steele comes back, Viper hits the spinebuster, then bounces off the ropes himself and drops a big elbow. Steele is left doubled up in pain, and Viper shows off to the crowd. The crowd shouts and boos the wrestler, and Viper turns back to Steele. Tommy Steele then suddenly lunges at Viper, bringing him down with a spear. Madden: Call that a spear?! I’ve seen the tellytubbies do more damage. Madden: To be fair, Chad, that was a perfectly executed maneuver. Madden: Maneuver, my ass Madden: I tend to think of your ass as more of a lump than a maneuver, Chad. Viper is down, and Steele goes for the cover. 1…2... Barely a kickout! Steele flops back, totally exhausted. He looks up at the fans, who are cheering him on, and that seems to give him a new lease of life. He stands shakily on his feet, and signals to the fans. They start chanting “Death From The Icon, Death From The Icon…”. Steele slowly lifts Viper up. He drags him over to the turnbuckle, and somehow manages to lift him onto the top rope, so Viper is sitting facing the crowd. Steele then slowly climbs to the second rope, puts his arm round Viper’s neck, and jumps down, performing a reverse-type stunner. There is a sickening crack as Steele brings Viper down, neck first. Magman: Did you hear that?? Will someone get some medics out here! Steele then slowly rolls on top of Viper, and the referee delivers the 1…2…3 The crowd go crazy for Steele and he stands up and has his hand lifted by the referee. Medics rush out from the back to the sound of ‘Raise Up’ by Petey Pablo. WINNER: Tommy ‘the war train’ Steele Steele walks off victorious and Viper pushes away the medics and storms off up the ramp. Magman: Will that be the last of Viper in LTD? Madden: The last? I don’t remember seeing the first of him!
