He has a doctorate in sports medicine, physical therapy and pychology. Paxton is from the Steel City of Pitsburgh, PA. He was a member of the now defunct Steel City Committee alongside his childhood friend Blaze McCoy. Paxton joins the LTD staff as a coach and training supervisor and well as a booker. Paxton's creative training ideas will help all the members of LTD improve their skills in one way or another. Lethal LTD Announcer/Interviewer: Chad Madden Chad is the main commentator for LTD. He is a straight forward tell it like it is type of announcer. He is a bit overweight but don't tell him that. He's heard it too many times and it doesn't affect him. He loves to poke fun at people and try to embarass them or degrade them. He usually wears a hawaiian shirt and sunglasses. He very often sides with the heels of the federation and kisses the President's as, whoever it may be at the time. Lethal LTD Color Commentator/Interviewer: Magman: He was the long time manager of Bob Yobbo Junior. He discovered Bob fighting in bars and helped him progress to a world title. Magman has also managed The Sex Machine and Carlos Rodriguez. Magman has a number of schemes and money making ventures. He owns Magman Inc, an operation that provides a number of services to celebrities. Magman once co owned a federation with Bob Yobbo Junior and served as a Color Commentator at the time. He also had a short stint as a Color Commentator on MMWF 2K weekend programs. He is a man who wears colorful suits(Purple and Gold Suits being his favorites) and is is animated in his opinions and comments. SIDE NOTE:She is always"frisky' LOL i just thought I should put that in. Lethal LTD Interviewer: "Sly" Sammy Fox Status: Heelish-Neutral Biography: Sammy grew up in Perth Amboy, New Jersey. He's been in the "business" for years, originally as a small-time independent wrestler in the seventies and early eighties, a manager for a little while, and eventually turned into a commentator due to his impressive microphone skills. He's 43 years old, but looks like 35 due to his good conditioning. Announcer Info: He's not so much as a corporate "yes man" but more as a guy who values his situation. He'd mark out for Paxton/Venom not because they're the President and Commissioner, but more because they're evil S.O.B.'s. He's intelligent, not as likely to make a crude joke (which he'd be more than happy to do...) as he is to make some smart-ass comment about what the face could do better (which are usually fairly true) and dig on him for that. Intelligent, crafty (as his nickname suggests), and relentless in his marking and digging on certain wrestlers. I'd like to find one mid-level heel that Sammy could just totally mark out for...give him this little idiosyncracy.
