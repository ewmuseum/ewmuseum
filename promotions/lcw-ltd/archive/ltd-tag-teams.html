You hear the words “ Its all up to them. You just have to take it” The song “Take It” By ICP starts to play. A fog comes up from under the ramp way. some red spinning lights come on. You see HaVoC putting Baine through the table. Then you see them beating up Poltergeist. It then shows Cotton Getting in Baines face. Bones, Brutakis and Cotton are standing at the top of the ramp. Bones and Brutakis have black baggy pants on with black baggy hockey jerseys that say HaVoC on them. Their hair is in dreads. Bones are red and Brutakis are black. They both have axe handles. Cotton has black baggy pants but they ride low on her hips. You can see her red G string. Her shirt is a HaVoC LTD shirt. It is cut off just under her boobs. Cottons hair is about to the middle of her back and is colored blue. They slowly make their way to the ring stopping to have words with some of the fans. Bones slides under the ropes. Brutakis gets up on the apron and sets over the ropes. Cotton gets up on the apron and steps over the bottom rope and under the second rope. Bones and Brutakis go to two corners and pose for the crowd as Cotton does a little of the Cotton mouth in the ring. They then get ready for who ever their next victims may be!!!!!! Tag Moves: Double Drop kick Double Close line Double Suplex Double DDT Double team in corner Double super plex Brutakis pick them up and Bones does a Spring board Diamond Cutter Brutakis pick them up for the hatchet bomb as Bones does Jokers ride ( both moves are done together) Bones does a leg take down as Brutakis leg drops off the second rope Double Power Bomb from in side ring to out side ring Tag Finisher: " Super Moon Mist" The Moon Mist From the top of Brutakis Shoulder as he is standing on the 2nd turn buckle) Tag Team History: Grevious Bodily Harm Members: Humpy and Baine Entrance: "This is no dream…This is your worst nightmare." is shown on the LTD Big Screen. A picture of Humpy's face is shown on the Titantron. It gets morphed into Baine's face. Then morphed back again. This happens 3 times before 3 spotlights come on that are hidden underneath the grills of the lamp. They point directly upwards, and then start to move randomly.
