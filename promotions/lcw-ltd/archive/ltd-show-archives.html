From Halifax, Nova Scotia, on Saturday, November 17th, 2001! Special Edition: LTD FRIDAY NIGHT from the LTD Studios Mike Boom and Chad Madden are sitting behind the studio desk on the set of LTD Friday Night the warm up show for LTD Saturday and a recap show of events that lead up to the federations signature show. Mike Boom: Good evening ladies and gentlemen I’m Mike Boom and this is Chad Madden and welcome to this special edition of LTD Friday Night. I’m afraid we are here tonight to give everyone a little bit of bad news as well as some good news. Chad Madden: Allow me to give the bad news. It seems LTD is off to somewhat of a bad start as far as a corporation goes. Our President has been missing for over a week and now because of a horrendous buy rate and some legal problems within the upper ranks of LTD, the federation has lost it’s PPV rights and the New Blood Rising show could not be televised. Mike Boom: Some good news is that the LTD Saturday Night show still has its syndicated spot on Saturday Nights and the first show will be on December 1st and it will be a huge card. On the better end of the good news, we are here to give you a quick run down on exactly what happened at the first LTD show where we crowned our first LTD champion. Chad Madden: Let’s cut the bull and get down to Business please? Mike Boom: All right Chad. Halifax, Nova Scotia was the site for the first LTD show and despite the PPV televised rights falling out, the show was a sell out up there in Canada. Chad Madden: That’s right, a packed house and the first shocker came when LTD VP announced that the tournament would be all no DQ matches so anything could happen. We saw some awesome competition and some shocking events so let’s get to the annoying still shots and tell these people who won. Still shots of the matches are shown as the two explain exactly what happened at LTD’s first event. ROUND ONE Match 1: Myrddin vs. Mystery Opponent (Jamal “J” Taylor) Mike Boom: The established LCW veteran Myrddin walked confidently down to the ring and climbed in and looked around in amazement to see a packed house and smiled. J Taylor, upset that he was not initially put in the tournament stormed down and jumped into the ring and went right after Myrddin. Myrddin absorbed the initial blows and then got bent over as Taylor continued to hammer away. Chad Madden: Then the fun began; Suddenly Myrddin pulled his head up and grabbed Taylor by his neck and throws his arm over the shoulder and executed a devastating chokeslam sending Taylor hard onto the mat. Myrddin covered for the quick one, two, three and an impressive win by the Legion member destroying Taylor and sending a statement to the rest of the combatants in the tournament. Mike Boom: That’s right Chad, the first LTD match was short but sweet and Myrddin went on to face the winner of the second match up. WINNER: Myrddin Match 2: Venom vs. Damien Diablo Chad Madden: Venom and Diablo stared each other down in the center of the ring almost burning holes in their opponents before they finally locked up and got it going. Diablo was fierce and surprisingly had the upper hand for most of the match. The devil himself must have been present in that ring because Diablo was possessed and took it to Venom. Mike Boom: Venom did however come back and just when it looked like the tide was going to change once again Diablo went for his Death Move a bit too soon and ended up falling in an awkward position on the concrete outside the ring. Chad Madden: Hell yeah, the freak laid there motionless and when Venom went to attack him outside the ring, the referee stopped him, as Diablo didn’t budge a bit. The sucker was out cold and it didn’t look good. Mike Boom: Paramedics rushed to ringside and carted Diablo out of the arena and to the hospital. Later in the night we got word that Damien was in critical condition with a serious neck injury and that’s all the information we have right now. Chad Madden: Well, we hope he’s ok but I don’t think prayers will help this follower of darkness. Maybe he will need to sell his soul to the devil if he is ever gonna compete again. Mike Boom: Damn you Madden, have you no respect? Everyone here at LTD wishes Damien the best and look forward to his return to the ring. Chad Madden: Anyway, Venom advanced in the tournament to face Myrddin in the semi-finals. The next match was definitely the best one in the first round and the start of some weird happenings. WINNER: Venom Match 3: Bob Yobbo Jr. vs. Barabbas Mike Boom: The winner of our first match Myrddin accompanied his long time friend and tag team partner of the former LCW Tag Champion team Legion to the ring. Barabbas stands well above any competitor here in LTD and it was obvious here even though Yobbo is 6’6. Chad Madden: Legion got booed all the way down the ramp and into the ring. People were throwing stuff at the two of them and they didn’t like it and they continuously told the fans that they were number one, but with the wrong finger. Mike Boom: Bob Yobbo Jr. came out to an enormous pop from the crowd. The Man from Down Under is definitely a fan favorite here in LTD and the capacity crowd let him know that first hand. Many Yobbo signs and shirts were seen throughout the arena in support of the former LCW wrestler making his comeback. Chad Madden: Ah who cares about that Crocodile hunting fool? Barabbas took control of the match right off the bat and was crushing the down under blunder landing various power moves almost crippling Jr. Mike Boom: But Yobbo’s resiliency came through and with the crowd in his corner, fought back to even it all up. Chad Madden: Myrddin watched on in approval at ringside as Barabbas had Yobbo in a bear hug for about 5 minutes squeezing the life out of him. I thought his spine was gonna snap. Mike Boom: Yobbo finally got the strength to hit the big man over the head a few times and freed himself from the hold. It took a little while for Bob to get back at full strength but Barrabas held him off and threw Bob him into the ropes looking to go for a finish. But Yobbo reversed it and threw Barabbas into the ropes. Yobbo nailed the monster with a flying lariat and the crowd came to their feet in support of Bob. Yobbo picked Barabbas back up and tried to send him into the ropes again but Barabbas reversed it back on Yobbo and followed him as he sent Yobbo flying towards the nearest turnbuckle. Chad Madden: Myrddin saw that Yobbo had a chance to gain the upper hand and attempted to try and help his partner get back in control of the match. Myrrdin jumped up onto the apron with a chair awaiting Yobbo in the corner. Yobbo quickly ducked and slid right out of the ring and Myrddin ended up smashing Barabbas right in the cranium with the steel chair knocking him flat out in the middle of the ring. Big mistake. Mike Boom: Yobbo, outside of the ring, kicked Myrddin in the stomach and delivers a deadly DDT on the concrete. He crawled back in the ring and covers the unconscious Barabbas for the win. Chad Madden: Barabbas comes to and couldn’t believe what had just happened. Myrddin got in the ring to assure his partner that the chair shot was not meant for him but for his opponent. The two Legion mates argued for a second but then head up the ramp together. Barabbas seemed a bit upset with Myrddin as they walked behind the curtain giving him the stare of death. Myrddin, on the other hand looked optimistic and apologetic with his arm around the big man. WINNER: Bob Yobbo Jr. Match 4: “The Icon” Jason Stevens vs. Scott Royal Mike Boom: The next match turned out to be one of the sleepers of the night. Chad Madden: You mean we could’ve fallen asleep during it! Mike Boom: Not exactly, these two are battled toe to toe in a great match up. This match is what LTD is all about. Those two wrestlers were in there trying to polish their skills and prove to the world that they can make it in this tough sport. Chad Madden: Come on Boom, this match sucked. The Icon had Royal rolled up in a small package but Royal reversed it and grabbed a little tights but the referee didn’t see it and counted Stevens out of the match and Royal advanced in the tournament. Nothing special Mike. Don’t exaggerate. Mike Boom: Please Madden, these two competitors put on a show and both are destined for greatness here in LTD. Just because you don’t like them you don’t give them any credit? Let’s stick to the script man. Chad Madden: Well my script says the next match was the semi-final match between Myrddin and Venom. WINNER: Scott Royal SEMI-FINALS Match 5: Myrddin vs. Venom Mike Boom: Ok, all right. The Legion members were back on track and came down to the ring to the chorus of boos again but ready for battle. Chad Madden: Those two are great. The fans don’t mean crap to them but winning does and boy they can do that with the best of them. Mike Boom: Myrddin showed his high flying skill nailing several high risk maneuvers in this one. Chad Madden: That dude is incredible. He can make it big if he doesn’t injure himself trying some of those crazy stunts of his. He has no concern for his body at all. It was just about over when Myrddin hit the Sodimizer DDT off the second rope. Mike Boom: Myrddin celebrated as he had Venom out cold in the ring. It looked like Myrddin would easily advance to the finals. Myrddin signaled to Barabbas with a thumbs up and a big smile. Barabbas smiled back but when Myrddin turned back around to gloat to the crowd again Barabbas’ smile turned to a look of concern and shook his head as Myrddin caught the wrong end of the great equalizer. Chad Madden: BAM right in the nads! Myrddin was too hasty and thought he had it in the bag and there was nothing Barabbas could do about it. Myrddin may be singing a few notes higher from here on out. Mike Boom: Barabbas did jump up onto the apron to try and interfere as the two continued to give it there all in the ring. But from the back came Yobbo Jr. who grabbed Barabbas by the legs and sent him straight down face first onto the ring apron and keeping him from interfering in the match. Yobbo smiled and looked into the ring at Venom. Chad Madden: Yeah, Venom and Yobbo had been all buddy buddy so far here in LTD and it made me sick! Mike Boom: Well Yobbo evened the score and Venom and Myrddin had several close 2 counts back and forth until out of no where Myrddin sprayed venom with a blinding mist right in his eyes. Chad Madden: Hell yeah, the mysterious man from England caught him off guard just a bit and that would prove to be a big momentum shifter. Mike Boom: That’s right. Myrddin nailed Venom with another Sodimizer and then hit an amazing moonsault frog splash known as the Conjour to get the pin. Chad Madden: A big win for Myrddin moving him to the finals. Yobbo came in the ring to help his butt buddy out who was still blinded by the mist. Damn it was funny to see him stumble around the ring trying to hit something? He almost hit Yobbo a few times! Mike Boom: The Legion members seemed to be back on the same page again as they left ringside and went to the back. Yobbo helped Venom out of the ring but Scott Royal wasted no time and rushed to the ring and smashed Yobbo from behind with a clothesline and immediately hit a falling reverse neck breaker to start the match. WINNER: Myrddin Match 6: Bob Yobbo Jr. vs. Scott Royal Chad Madden: Yobbo never expected that one and Royal controlled the match for a long time just beating down on Yobbo. That’s what Junior gets for trying to help his old buddy Venom when a damn title tournament is going on. I got a new found respect for Royal. He saw the opening and took it right to him to get the advantage. Mike Boom: I can’t believe I’m saying this but you are absolutely right Chad. Royal used his head and had Yobbo in big trouble with several consecutive suplexes and then got to work on Yobbo’s knees. Yobbo was in big trouble but fought back and finally evened the score with a reversal of an attempted suplex and hitting a t-bone suplex of his own. Yobbo continued to recover but Royal went back to work on the right knee. Chad Madden: Yobbo’s knee had to have been close to falling completely off with the beating it took. Royal was relentless and continued to work on the knee and even applied a Boston crab attempting to rip his damn leg off! Mike Boom: Well, Yobbo managed to grab the bottom rope breaking the hold. Yobbo slowly but surely regained some strength and began to fight back. Chad Madden: Then out of the blue, Legion showed up on the top of the ramp. Of course the boos came flying out everywhere but they just waltzed down to ringside and watched the two men beating the pulp out of each other in the ring. Mike Boom: It was obvious that Legion was up to no good and was going to try and ruin this great match up. Yobbo spotted the two Legion members at the side of the ring and began to yell at Myrddin at ringside. Royal saw it as another opening and went right back to work on the bum knee again from behind and Myrddin just laughed as Royal worked on the knee. Madden: Myrddin and Barabbas seemed to be planning something at ringside. Royal had Yobbo down on the mat and looked over concerned at the presence of Legion and Myrddin gave Royal the thumbs up and Scott smiled back. Royal then went to his patented Asai Moonsault but Myrddin jumped in and pulled Yobbo out from underneath Royal and he missed the move. Myrddin continues to work over Yobbo’s knee and Barabbas comes in the ring and picks Royal up and nails him with the Nirvana Chokeslam as Myrddin continued to work over Yobbo’s knee. Mike Boom: Legion took advantage of the No DQ rule and tried to took out the healthy wrestler knowing that Yobbo’s hurt knee would make it easier for Myrddin in the final. Myrddin signaled for Barabbas to place Yobbo on top of Royal and got the three count. Chad Madden: A brilliant plan by Legion! They eliminated Royal so Myrddin will have an easy time beating up on the injured Yobbo in the final. Mike Boom: Myrddin celebrates in the ring bragging to the crowd that he is the man taking all the credit for Yobbo’s win and kicks Bob a few times in the midsection as he is still laying in the middle of the ring holding his right knee. Myrddin walks out of the ring and heads to the back and Barabbas reluctantly follows. WINNER: Bob Yobbo Jr. FINAL MATCH Match 7: Bob Yobbo Jr. vs. Myrddin Chad Madden: We had a guest at ringside for this one. Venom decided to bring it upon himself to join Boom and myself at the announce table. I didn’t want him there and I know you didn’t want him there either B. Mike Boom: Well, it was unexpected but nevertheless Venom was here to help us call the championship match to crown the first ever LTD champ. Chad Madden: That snake sat there as Yobbo and Myrddin went at it talking about how good of friends him Yobbo were and that he was there “just in case.” Mike Boom: Well, with Barabbas coming down to ringside with Myrddin, it seemed Venom was just looking out for his friend and making sure it was a fair match up. Yobbo and Myrddin showed the world why they were in the final match with an astounding performance in the ring. Chad Madden: After countless signature moves back and forth Yobbo gets the upperhand with the Hangover submission hold. Myrddin manages to reach the ropes to break out but he is exhausted and falls to the mat. Mike Boom: Barabbas stepped up on the apron with a chair in disapproval ready to hit Yobbo but Yobbo spots him up and nailed him with a drop kick for his troubles sending him outside the ring and he dropped the chair into the ring. Barabbas falls awkwardly and his head hits the corner of the steel steps and he was out cold. Chad Madden: Yobbo turned around and Myrddin had gotten back to his feet and grabbed Yobbo and landed the Awakening, a sweet reverse Russian leg sweep directly onto the chair. All righty then! Mike Boom: Instead of making the cover, Myrddin quickly left the ring to attend to Barabbas who was down at ringside. Myrddin who appeared to be yelling at him for not helping in the ring helped Barabbas up? Chad Madden: What the hell was up Myrddin’s ass? His partner gets pummeled and his opponent is out in the ring and he’s too busy complaining to Barabbas? What a jackass! Mike Boom: Well finally, after being ignored by Barabbas, Myrddin gets back in the ring and stood at the ropes scolding Barabbas even more. Myrddin called for Barabbas to come in the ring and he did. Yobbo was still down and regaining his composure just a bit and Myrddin called for Barabbas to put the finishing touches on Yobbo. Barabbas nodded his head in approval and then grabbed Yobbo by the throat. Chad Madden: Myrddin jumped around that ring like a little kid at Christmas anticipating the title around his waist. Just before slamming Yobbo into oblivion, Barabbas dropped Yobbo and turned around and grabbed Myrddin by the throat and nailed the Nirvana chokeslam on his own partner! Mike Boom: Unbelievable as it seems, Barabbas was finally fed up and turned on his long time partner. Chad Madden: Myrddin was taking the big man for granted and didn’t realize what he was doing! I guess Barabbas just got pissed of and had enough. Mike Boom: Barabbas then spit on the fallen Myrddin and helped Yobbo up and then left the ring and walked out. Yobbo jumped all over the opportunity and placed the dazed Myrddin in position for the deadly Yobbo drop. Chad Madden: Damn, that face first jack knife power bomb is devastating! Mike Boom: Yes indeed! So devastating that it crowned our first ever LTD. Yobbo celebrated in the ring and Venom finally left the announce booth to go to the side of his friend. He takes the LTD title and gives it to Yobbo and raises his hand in victory. Chad Madden: Then the “you know what” hit the fan. Just when you think these two were on the same side, the jealousy kicked in and Venom went after Yobbo. Yobbo was celebrating on the other side of the ring with the belt showing it to the crowd and Venom sized him up and hit his 'You Never Saw That Coming' - Running Yakuza Kick! Mike Boom: Venom continuously kicked Yobbo on the mat disgusted at the fact that he had won the title. Chad Madden: Venom thought he got cheated out of a fair shot and rightfully so. Mike Boom: Proof positive that anything can happen in these events and the envy just took over and Venom lost total self control. Chad Madden: Venom sought out to end the career of Yobbo by strategically placing the steel chair in the middle of the ring and then executing a tombstone piledriver landing Yobbo’s head right on the chair! Mike Boom: Unfortunately, Venom was not done. Chad Madden: That crazy bastard put the chair under the right knee that Yobbo had injured earlier in the night and then stomped away even more. Venom ripped the damn tape off from the injured knee outside his tights and blasted him with 'Deep Impact' – a full nelson into forward sit-out facebuster on the chair to finish him off. Mike Boom: Venom spat on the fallen Yobbo and walked up the ramp and out with an evil grin on his face. He’s a sick puppy. The fans had quickly turned on Venom throwing things at him and screaming obscenities and he left the ramp area. Chad Madden: Is spat really a word Boom? Mike Boom: Does it matter? Finally, we have an LTD Champion. WINNER: LTD CHAMPION – Bob Yobbo Jr. Chad Madden: With a serious limp! Mike Boom: Well, Yobbo doesn’t have too much time to recuperate because the next event is this Saturday and the card is out! Chad Madden: 5 new members get to test their skills in the first LTD Saturday Night show! What a dumb ass name for a show! Mike Boom: Funny you mention that. There is actually going to be a contest to name the Saturday show. The winning name will be announced this Saturday in Worchester, Massachusetts.! December 1st, 2001: LTD Saturday Night: Worchester, Mass. Specialty Match Show Chad Madden: Well there is a them for this historical show. All the matches will be specialty matches and stipulation will be in place for each one. Mike Boom: Two new members of LTD will square off in the first ever Initiation Match has Tony Mecca vs. Headbuster. They will not only be fighting for a win but fighting to not get punished after the match. Chad Madden: Oh yeah. VP Paxton will get to actually lay the board of education on the loser and paddle the hell out of the loser’s ass! Mike Boom: Two more newcomers will face off in a LTD Contract Ladder match . It will be Jin Akujin vs. John Black for a spot in the federation. Chad Madden: One will be in and one will be out. Talk about pressure! Mike Boom: The next match will be “The Icon” Jason Stevens vs. The Inferno Kid in a submission match. No pinfalls so countout, no DQ. Chad Madden: Gotta make the opponent tap out. One of the toughest matches in wrestling. Mike Boom: Then after an impressive showing in the Title tournament, Scott Royal will take on Jamal “J” Taylor in a double tables match ! Chad Madden: Not one but two tables you must put your opponent through to win the match up. That’s gonna hurt. Mike Boom: Then your first main event of the night will be a highly anticipated match up of two former teammates. After then tension reached a high point at the PPV, the members of Legion will be facing off against each other in a Lumberjack Strap Match . Chad Madden: The big Myrddin vs. Barabbas match will also be for the number one contendership for the LTD title and a match with the champ at the December PPV Winter’s Wrath in Dayton, Ohio on December 15th. Damn that’s gonna be a good one. Nothing better than seeing two old friends battle it out for a big prize. Mike Boom: That will definitely be a landmark match here in LTD. But the main event of the evening is our LTD Champion Yobbo vs. Venom ! Chad Madden: Oh yes. Another former friendship down the toilet at the PPV and the feud will come to a head very soon with the LTD title on the line. Mike Boom: Yobbo must first rehab that injured knee of his for this match up which will be in a steel cage with a top on it. Chad Madden: Pin to win this time and no can interfere. Anyway, the two friends who would’ve interfered are now enemies! What a show it will be, let the sh*t talking begin! Mike Boom: That’s it for now. Stay tuned for the latest information on the missing President, the pending PPV television contract for the federation. Chad Madden: See ya later, Jackass!
