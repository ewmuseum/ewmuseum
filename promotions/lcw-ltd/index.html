Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Lethal LTD opened on Hallowe'en 2001, announced on the newsdesk of its parent federation as “The Only Official Developmental Promotion for LCW” and glossed there as a mission: “There, we make Learning, Training, and Development LETHAL!” It was where applicants who had not made the cut were sent to sharpen up, and where established wrestlers came back to do the same. Its roster menu listed Grads apart from Wrestlers, which made the promise structural.

It ran shows of its own — Progression, Winter's Wrath, Steel City Carnage, a Saturday show, and a Genesis card by the spring — with an LTD Championship, an LTD Progressive Championship, and tag titles decided by a tournament whose eight teams were drawn at random. Its front page set roleplay deadlines against the card and signed off in capitals: “DON'T FORGET TO PROOF, PROOF, PROOF!”

Its staff page is the fullest thing it left. Five on-air personalities are written out at length — a president with a doctorate in sports medicine and an amateur wrestling background, a commentator who “very often sides with the heels of the federation,” a colour man in purple and gold suits who once co-owned a federation with the wrestler he used to manage — and behind them a booking staff and a bench of match writers.

By the spring it was leaving. Its own last Progression show opens on a camera flying over the Laurel Mountains into Uniontown, Pennsylvania, and a venue sign reading “Leaning Towards Death Wrestling – TONIGHT! LTD Progression’s last show!” The commentary says the rest of it plainly: “Progression will have a new name once we go Independent for good at the end of the month.” The following month its own Progression page carries that name in its title and a Hall of Fame in its navigation. The earlier gloss and the later name are both its own, and both are recorded here. What became of it after 19 April 2002 is not in the surviving record.
