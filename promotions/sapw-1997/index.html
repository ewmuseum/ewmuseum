Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Super Atlantic Pro Wrestling renamed itself from a predecessor called the ICA on 25 June 1997, the same move that brought it into the Global Wrestling Council, a self-described alliance of regional federations worldwide. It ran booked matches graded on roleplay, sanctioned five titles at launch, and covered, in its own words, “Atlantic Canada, and at times… the Atlantic coast of America,” with tour and pay-per-view deals in the works with fellow alliance members such as SJPW.

Its first broadcast, Control Centre, aired 27 June 1997 hosted by Ricky Waters, who explained its place inside that alliance directly: its own champion would be the All-Atlantic champion, never the world champion, since the world title belonged to one alliance-wide titleholder who toured defending it against each region's own champion in turn.

Waters laid out five weekly programmes: Face to Face on Tuesdays; its flagship, Prime Time Wrestling, on Thursdays, six to ten matches hosted by John Chambers and “Razor” Ron Stryker; a Saturday Hotline Report of rumors, hosted by Chambers and Waters together; an uncensored, gimmick-heavy Sunday Night Extreme where its own Extreme title would usually be defended, hosted by Jason Hate; and a Monday International Report on the rest of the alliance, hosted by Tommy Chang. A president was promised for the following week, tournaments for the Television and Extreme titles were announced, and the promotion asked the wrestling community directly for more tag teams before it would start that division.

No card of any of the five ever aired, and no closing statement of its own survives; the last trace of it, two days later, is a hostile reply posted elsewhere in the newsgroup.
