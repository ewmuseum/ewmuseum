SAPW — Roleplay

SAPW Control Centre

27 June 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
<Scene fades in on a news room type setting. The main desk is navy and it has two monitors located on it. On the wall behind the desk is the SAPW logo, which is in navy and outlined in gold. Sitting behind the desk is Ricky Waters, host of SAPW Control Centre.> Ricky: Welcome everyone to the very first Super Atlantic Pro Wrestling broadcast! I'm Ricky Waters, your host for the evening. As you all know, the ICA has changed its name to SAPW. The reason is very simple, we have joined the Global Wrestling Council, which is made up of many other 'regional' feds from across the globe. The SAPW title holder will be known as the All-Atlantic champion, but not the world champion. The World champion will be the main champion for all of the GWC, and he will tour around the globe defending his title against all the other regional fed champions. For instance, say that our champion was to meet the world champion, and he won. Then he would defend the title against the top challengers here, then go on tour with his belt. It's pretty simple! But before we can seriously talk about the world title, we have to talk about our own titles! Let's see what we have here...yes, here it is. Here is the schedule for the upcoming weeks... SAPW Schedule: Face To Face -Every Tuesday -Flashes and Interviews for PTW. Prime Time Wrestling -Every Thursday -Our 'big show.' -6-10 matches -Hosted by John Chambers and "Razor" Ron Stryker Hotline Report -Every Saturday -Rumours and gossip -Hosted by SAPW insiders, John Chambers and Ricky Waters. Extreme 'Face To Face' Live! -Every Saturday night -Uncensored Sunday Night Extreme -Every Sunday -Mostly extreme/gimmick matches. Extreme title is usually defended on this show -5-8 matches -Hosted by Jason Hate International Report -Every Monday -News from other GWC leagues -Hosted by Tommy Chang Ricky: Pretty impressive, eh! From what I have heard from the SAPW board of directors, a President will also be announced on next Thursday's Prime Time Wrestling. Tournament brackets will also be introduced for the SAPW Television and Extreme titles. The Heavyweight title tournament will not start until we have more wrestlers signed. The last piece of information I have is a word from a member of the board of directors, who is in charge of our tag team division... "SAPW has been filling up very nicely, but we do not have enough tag teams as of yet to start a competitive division, so we are asking the wrestling community to enter some tag teams to expand our division. Thanks." Ricky: Well that's all for now folks, I'll be seeing you in a couple of days with the announcement of matches for next Thursdays card. <Scene fades to black as Ricky Waters is reading through some papers.>
