SAPW - Needs New Wrestlers!

Super Atlantic Pro Wrestling **************************** Formerly known as the ICA, SAPW is a brand new organization that will soon be joining the GWC. We are booked and base our matches on how well our handlers role-play. We currently sanction five titles. We are also currently working on deals with other GWC members, such as SJPW, to share PPV's and to go on tours to their regions. We mostly cover Atlantic Canada, and at times, we will dip into the Atlantic coast of America. We will be starting very soon, so if you want to be apart of SAPW, just send me an email at [detail withheld] , and I'll send you a hand book and creation sheet right away, or you can check out our web page at: http://www.geocities.com/colosseum/5412 [name withheld] SAPW C.E.O. TAEWF Match Writer SJPW Advisor
