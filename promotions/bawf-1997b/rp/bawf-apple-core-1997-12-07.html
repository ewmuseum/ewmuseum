BAWF Apple Core 12/7/97

Remember, roleplaying leads to victories, and quality always counts over quantity! ||===============|| |__________________| ::Camera fades to a dimly lit locker room... A figure appears having a towel draped over his shoulder:: Chris Kill: Coming soon is the BAWF debut. The number one star is surely going to be me. Some may say I cannot handle top competition like Lyle Lorens and Shawn Arrows. Soon that will be nothing more, but just a lie. The hype that they give Lyle Lorens is just incredible. At the age of 17 he is pronounced a "Legend"...wow just give him the world. From what I am hearing, he is just another person with his head full of lies. If you want to challenge a real man? Just look for "The Ringmaster". And about your little thesis of how people think of nicknames. I didn't get this nickname. I earned it...I deserved it. I named myself that because I do rule the ring. Soon I think you may find that out the hard way. Now in the BAWF tournament I am facing some wrestler named Boris. Well one thing is, Boris, you are going to be crippled at the hands of "The Ringmaster". The tempo of my game is going to heat up. If the crowd feels so bad how I whip you around from bell to bell. Then those fools can cry about it too. It's not arrogance, it's greatness! ||===============|| |__________________| Give me the microphone! Now, as you all know, unless you are stupid, that the BAWF Championship Tournament is coming up. My number 1 goal in the fed is not to win the title, but to beat the living crap out of every person in the fed, although the title is good. Now, I would like you all to focus your attention on the Spellcaster. What has he done? Nothing!!!!!!!!!!!! I've held the EWF Championship, and he hasn't done crap!!!!!! And he expects to whin the title!!(laughing) You just better watch out, cuz if a rock nails you in the back of the head, it's your own fault. -The Killer Rock ||===============|| |__________________| Spellbinder: Well, well, well. The Rockster we meet up in the ring. This tournament will be my ladder to greatness. I will use this tourney to propel me to greatness. I am going to become the champion and none of you will stand in my way. I will run through the competetion like you aren't even there. I am also going to start a stable. If you would like to join my stable the BLOODCLAN just talk to me and I will get you hooked-up. Anyone who joins will have an automatic advantage over anyone else. With me backing you the opposition won't stand a chance. I have dominated in every federation I have been in and this one will be no different. Good Luck to everyone else in the tournament and you will need it with me in it. ||===============|| |__________________| PHAT Beef, whatever you are, you aren't as big as you think. I challenge you to a match on Bad to the Bone. If you accept, you will be feeling a huge.............................. BRAIN FREEZE!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! -Iceberg ||===============|| |__________________| 9-Ball> MIKEY! I don't know ya, and ya don't know me. First round match, I gotta face ya in the tourney. This may be our first match, but it'll be your last. The pressure, and the power with take you away, and then you fall pray to the Corner Pocket. It's all clear sailin' from there for me! ||===============|| |__________________| :::The scene opens with a shot of a trophy case filled with plaques honoring all different types of achievements. Suddenly, a voice is heard::: Okay, okay...enough with my accomplishments. Let's give them what they really want...ME. :::The camera moves over to a well dressed man standing at what must be a few good inches over 6 feet tall. His obviously long hair is hidden under a baseball cap turned backwards::: Hello, Big Apple Wrestling Federation runner-ups. I'd like to introduce all of you to the Main Event. As most of you probably know, I am "The Legend" Lyle Lorens. This entire state is my stomping ground, and I have made quite the name for myself in the process. :::Lorens walks over to the trophy case and turns his back to the camera. On the back of his shirt it says "The Legend"::: Look at this! I've done it all! Most of you go out and think as hard as you can until you develop headaches from the feat trying to come up with catchy nicknames to get noticed. Not me. You see, I was given this name, dubbed it by the media at age 17. Imagine that, a legend by age 17. Five years later and the legacy has only grown. Now, it's time to claim my first professional wrestling title. I see that our beloved commissioner has put some Vegabond in my way. :::Smiles, then laughs::: That's just great! I've made a career out of taking out the trash, and this will be no different. So don't get your hopes up. This is mine for the taking. The legacy continues. :::One last smile as the camera fades to black:::
