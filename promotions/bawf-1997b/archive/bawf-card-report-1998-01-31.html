BAWF Card Report, 31 January 1998

Jan 31, 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
======================================================= CARD REPORT ======================================================= BIG APPLE WRESTLING FEDERATION - THE 'CORE' OF THE NWC ======================================================= Card Update NEXT CARD: Bad to the Bone CONTRACTS DUE: N/A STATS DUE: Sun 7pm EST CARD DUE: Mon 7pm EST ___________________ JANUARY 31, 1998 ___________________ o Heya. As you know, Ray f'ed up big time. According to him, the results won't be out until later tonight, but somehow I seriously doubt that. Don't count on them in this lifetime ;) o Anyway, this was getting too ludicrous, so I have to just release the Blood & Guts results in bulleted format. The More Blood & Sore Guts card will be uploaded to the webpage shortly, so you can go check that one out. ======================================================= BAWF BLOOD & GUTS Thur Jan 29, 1998 ======================================================= DARK MATCHES: o Brian Steal over BadAss Blood via pinfall in 2:11 o Serpent over Minion via submission in 7:24 o "The Rattlesnake" Butch Mcrae over Al Skapone via pinfall in 5:05 TELEVISED MATCHES: x Hard Stuff -vs- Masters of Disaster Winners: MoD via pinfall. Time of match: 4:32 At the start of this match, Stephen Douglas came down alone, and announced that Creed is no longer in the BAWF. He then said that his partner for the night was none other than Shawn Arrows, and the two went on to defeat Hard Stuff. x Stonelight -vs- "Magnificent" Marcus Mann Winner: Marcus Mann via submission Time of match: 9:10 Mann gets the clean win with his Hyperextension armbar submission hold. x "The Legend" Lyle Lorens -vs- Leo Cartier Winner: Leo Cartier via countout Time of match: 5:32 During this match, Lorens went for a Legendary Leap, but landed on his knee outside the ring, and couldn't make the count. Lorens will be out of action for a while, and we wish him a speedy recovery. x Axe Dugan -vs- Animosity **Winner goes on to Battle Royal.** Winner: Animosity via pinfall Time of match: 7:23 Animoisty gets the win after a thrust kick to the jaw. Animosity advances to the battle royal. x FOUR CORNERS MATCH: Rules: Two men start in the ring. Anyone can tag anyone in the match. When you are pinned, submit, are DQed or are counted out, you head back to the showers. When two people are left, the match stops, and those two advance to More Blood & Sore Guts on Friday. #2) Spellbinder -vs- #3) Sephiroth -vs- #4) Shotgun Smith -vs- #5) The 911 Kid - Sephiroth over Shotgun Smith via pinfall in 7:23 - The 911 Kid over Spellbinder via pinfall in 15:36 **SEPHIROTH AND THE 911 KID ADVANCE** x MINI BATTLE ROYAL: Rules: Five men start in the ring. Anything goes. If you fall over the top and your feet touch the floor, you are eliminated. When two wrestlers are left, the match is stopped, and those two advance to More Blood & Sore Guts on Friday. #6) Masked Blood -vs- #6) Ward Enigma -vs- #8) Keith Schiano -vs- #8) Iceman -vs- #10) Animosity - Masked Blood eliminated by Triad in 5:13 - Ward Enigma eliminated by Triad in 8:53 - Keith Schiano eliminated by Animosity in 13:28 **TRIAD AND ANIMOSITY ADVANCE** o OK, I will put up the More Blood & Sore Guts results shortly, so you can actually read that card. Here is the house show, in bulleted format: ======================================================= HOUSE SHOW Bridgeport, Connecticut Sat Jan 31, 1998 ======================================================= o Spike Mortimer (local) over BadAss Blood via pinfall in 4:14 o The New Generation over Hard Stuff via pinfall in 3:12 o Rob Atlas over Brian Steal via countout in 9:19 o Minion over "The Warden" Norm Cash via pinfall in 4:45 o Alex Perfect and Serpent fight to a time limit draw. o GRUDGE TAG TEAM MATCH: Keith Schiano/Stonelight over The 911 Kid/Ward Enigma via DQ in 19:10 o 3-WAY DANCE: The Bloodbrothers -vs- The New Revolution -vs- MoD - The New Revolution over MoD (Arrows/TA) via pinfall in 5:24 - The Bloodbrothers over The New Revolution via pinfall in 7:41 The Bloodbrothers win the 3-Way Dance. o TELEVISION TITLE REMATCH: The 911 Kid over Triad via pinfall in 9:10 The 911 Kid retains the Television Title. o RELUCTANT PARTNERS JOIN FORCES Sephiroth/Jason Jinx over Shawn Arrows/Shotgun Smith via pinfall in 20:42 ======================================================= BAWF BAD TO THE BONE Mon Feb 2, 1998 Written by: Alex/Mike ======================================================= o Note that since the Blood & Guts card was never released, Bad to the Bone has been pushed up a day, so STRATS are due for this week only on Sunday at 7:00 PM EST, and the card itself should be out by that time on Monday. DARK MATCHES: o "The Animal" Stephen Douglas -vs- BadAss Blood o Al Skapone -vs- Leo Cartier o Serpent -vs- Doomstar TELEVISED MATCHES: Note: These matches will be split into two venues: half of them will take place in Shea Stadium and the other in Yankee Stadium. Alex will write one half and I will write the other. **The matches with "x" in front will take place in Shea Stadium, and the ones with "-" will take place in Yankee Stadium.** x Rob Atlas -vs- Masked Blood - The New Generation -vs- Red Lightning & Deston x The New Revolution -vs- Mann/Cartier - Minion -vs- "The Warden" Norm Cash x The Killer Rock -vs- Animosity - Keith Schiano -vs- Ward Enigma - Stonelight -vs- "The Rattlesnake" Butch Mcrae x TELEVISION TITLE MATCH: The 911 Kid (c) -vs- Sephiroth ======================================================= ALL CARDS SUBJECT TO CHANGE ======================================================= o As far as the rankings go, I am going to work on them now, so expect them to be up shortly along with the More Blood & Sore Guts card. o Reminder: Strats for Bad to the Bone are due on SUNDAY at 7pm EST. Mark "Magic Ref" Greene BAWF Commissioner
