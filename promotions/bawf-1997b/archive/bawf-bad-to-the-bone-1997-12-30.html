[NWC] (BAWF) Bad to the Bone!

30 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The following is the write-up of the Big Apple Wrestling Federation promotion's Sunday Afternoon card, 'Bad to the Bone!' Featuring a top contenders Battle Royal, this card was exceptionally exciting stuff. The BAWF is a regional promotion affiliated with the National Wrestling Council. Enjoy!) BAWF: http://base.bawf.ml.org/ NWC: http://members.tripod.com/~nwcouncil/nwc.html -------------------------------------------------- ::The camera opens as we see a sold-out St. Raymond's Catholic High School. You notice on your upper right hand corner of your screen banners saying "BAWF" and "BAWF Bad to the Bone". We also see millions of posters being held. One poster says "Alex Perfect is the TRUE TWW World Heavyweight Champion!" and another says "Sephiroth fears Cloud!". The camera zooms down to ringsdie and we notice Charlie Rongton and Mr. Quinby standing near a commentator's booth.:: Charlie Rongton: Welcome to the BAWF! Welcome to BAWF Bad to the BONE! Welcome to St. Raymond's Catholic High School in the South Bronx. I am Charlie Rongton and with me is my commentator partner for the evening. He is Mr. Quinby! Mr. Quinby: Thanks, Charlie. It gives me great pleasure for standing here and commentating with you right now. C.R: Same here, Quinby. But, right now let's go up to ringside with Scotty Walker. Scotty Walker: Ladies and gentlemen will you please rise for the singing of our national anthem. To sing the national anthem, here is Bob Skipper! ::You here the national anthem in the background.:: Scotty Walker: Thank you, Bob Skipper. DING DING DING!!! ------------------------------------------------------------------------ PERFECT BEEF & PHAT BEEF vs "MAGNIFICENT" MARCUS MANN & LEO CARTIER Scotty Walker: Ladies and gentlemen, the following contest is scheduled for one fall. Coming down the aisle, at a total combined weight of 560 lbs. Here are "Magnificent" Marcus Mann and his partner from Charlotte, North Carolina, Leo Cartier! Their opponents, both from Washington D.C, at a total combined weight of 485 lbs., here is the team of Perfect Beef and PHAT Beef! C.R: Those men are really going to have to some trouble with Mann and Cartier. Cartier is 321 plus pounds. He has a weight advantage over all of these wrestlers. DING DING DING! C.R: Cartier and Perfect will start. Cartier tries for a European Uppercut, but Perfect reverses it with a jawbreaker. Perfect sends Cartier against the ropes. BOOM! What a back body drop by PERFECT! Cartier was at least ten feet in the air! Perfect nails Cartier with a kick to the kidney area. Perfect tries to put Cartier in a sleeper, but Cartier rams his leg between Perfect's legs. He nailed him right in the bread basket area. Cartier is in pain. Perfect tags out to PHAT. PHAT sends Cartier into the turnbuckle, but Cartier won't move. He tries it again, but... M.Q: Wait! Mann is on the turnbuckle. BOOM! C.R: Mann just nailed PHAT with a flying clothesline. The ref I guess didn't see Mann. Mann quickly returns to the corner. Here is the pin... 1 2 KICKOUT!!! C.R: PHAT just barely kicked out. Cartier tries to tag Mann, but PHAT lands a boot to the bread basket area once more time. PHAT measures and lands an elbow onto Cartier's chest. PHAT is wasting some time here. PHAT sends Cartier against the ropes, but Cartier reverses it with a Cartier Classy Doom! The cover... 1 2 KICKOUT!!! C.R: How did PHAT kickout? Cartier tags Mann. Both send PHAT against the ropes. Double Swinging Neckbreaker by Cartier and Mann. Mann places PHAT on the turnbuckle. WHAM!!!!! Huracanrana! Mann tags back to Cartier. Cartier picks up PHAT and BOOM! The Cartier Classy Doom! The pin... 1 2 3!!!! DING DING DING!!! Scotty Walker: The winner of this match is the team of "Magnificent" Marcus Mann and Leo Cartier. "MAGNIFICENT" MARCUS MANN & LEO CARTIER over PERFECT BEEF & PHAT BEEF via PINFALL at 0:11:26 ------------------------------------------------------------------------ C.R: What a match! I never saw such tag team display better than Cartier and Mann. M.Q: I agree with you, Charlie. The Beefs were wasting time and that is what caused them the upset. Let's go back up to ringside with Scotty Walker. ------------------------------------------------------------------------ 10-MAN BATTLE ROYAL Scotty Walker: The following contest is a ten-men battle royal. The winner of this battle royal will go on to face the Big Appple Heavyweight Champion. ::The crowd starts to cheer wildly.:: Scotty Walker: Here are the participants in this battle royal. ::We can here Walker's voice as the camera zooms to the aisle.:: Scotty Walker: "The Legend" Lyle Lorens, Shawn "Bullseye" Arrows, Boris Ukraineno, "The Ringmaster" Chris Kill, The Sycho, "The Jiggster" Jimmy Jazz, "The Warden" Norm Cash, Breaker X and Minion. C.R: WOW! I never saw so much tension as there is right now in that ring. DING DING DING!!! C.R: As soon as the bell rings, nobody wastes any time. As Jazz attacks Sephiroth, Sephiroth ducks and lands a huge uppercut to the throat area. Kill is attacking Boris before Boris can even attack Lorens. Kill is wailing at Boris with those huge punches to the head. Cash hammers away on Arrows. Sephiroth has Cash up and comes down to the mat with a huge jawbreaker. Cash is eliminated! Scotty Walker: "The Warden" Norm Cash has been eliminated. M.Q: Boris reverses Kill's DDT with a Kiev Moonsault almost sending Kill over the top. Kill attacks Lorens with Arrows. Both men are hammering Lorens with kicks to the throat area. Minion and Breaker are double teaming on Sephiroth. Minion has Sephiroth up, but Sephiroth slides down and nails Arrows from behind. C.R: Arrows has no idea what happend! Arrows immediately attacks Sephiroth from behind. On the other side, Boris just nailed Jazz with an Odessa Suplex eliminating Jazz. Scotty Walker: "The Jiggster" Jimmy Jazz has been eliminated. C.R: From behind, Kill helps Sephiroth with Arrows. Arrows is helpless right now! Sephiroth has Arrows up and down! Sephiroth just choked slammed Arrows. Sephiroth continues to choke Arrows. We can see puss coming from Arrow's mouth. Sephiroth is still choking Arrows. Sephiroth picks up Arrows and Kill dropkicks Arrows and Arrows is eliminated. Scotty Walker: Shawn "Bullseye" Arrows has been eliminated. C.R: Let's say that Arrows won't forget this night! I am sure that Arrows is going to get Sephiroth back someday! Meanwhile, Kill just eliminated Breaker with a Release DDT! Breaker came from no where and Kill nailed him with a Release DDT! Scotty Walker: BreakerX has been eliminated. C.R.: Kill immediately attacks Boris from behind, but Boris nails Kill with a Danube River Piledriver. Kill gets up and immediately crawls to the nearest turnbuckle for safety! Lorens and Boris are double teaming on Sycho, but Boris instead of hitting Sycho, nails Lorens with a Soviet Sweep Clothesline which sends Lorens over the top rope. Scotty Walker: "The Legend" Lyle Lorens has been eliminated. C.R: Minion finds Kill hiding from the crowd of wrestlers and slams Kill to the mat. Minion rams Kill with a boot to the mid-section. Minion picks up Kill and POW! The Minion Bomb! Both men have been eliminated due to the Minion Bomb! WOW! Unbelieveable! Scotty Walker: Minion and "The Ringmaster" Chris Kill have been eliminated. M.Q: Three wrestlers are left in the ring. Boris immediately attacks Sycho from behind. Boris sends Sycho against the ropes and connects with a Kherson Suplex. Boris again sends Sycho against the ropes and nails him with a Soviet Release Suplex. Boris is going to the top! C.R: That might not be a smart move on his part! He is giving the signal for the Cossack Splash. He leaps and...BOOM! Sephiroth just nailed Boris with a jawbreaker and Boris goes flying out of the ring. Scotty Walker: Boris Ukraineno has been eliminated. C.R: Sycho takes this oppurtunity to attack Sephiroth. Sycho sends Sephiroth against the ropes and BOOM! Sycho nailed him with the Sycho Spike Bomb! Sephiroth is not moving. M.Q: I think as soon as Sephiroth hit that mat, he stopped breathing. Sycho has no idea what happened. Sycho picks up Sephiroth and throws him over the top ropes. DING DING DING!!! Scotty Walker: Here is your winner, The Sy.... ::As soon as Walker starts to say Sycho, a lightning bolt hits the side of the ring and Sephiroth disappears.:: THE SYCHO WINS THE BATTLE ROYAL AT 0:30:16 ------------------------------------------------------------------------ C.R: Did you see that? He disappered out of nowhere! Let's go back to ringside with Scotty Walker. ------------------------------------------------------------------------ Semi-Final Match - Big Apple Tournament SPELLBINDER vs LIVING BEEF Scotty Walker: The following contest is scheduled for one fall. It is a Semi-Final match in the Big Apple Tournament. Coming down the aisle and hailing from Atlanta, Georgia and weighing in at 285 lbs., here is Spellbinder! His opponent, hailing from Washington D.C and weighing in at 250 lbs., here is Living Beef! DING DING DING!!! C.R: Both men cirlce each other. Living rams his shoulder against Binder and hits the floor hard. Living nails Binder with a boot to the kidney area. Living sends Binder agaunst the ropes, but Binder nails Living with a crucifix. Living kicks out before the ref can count. Binder sends Living against the ropes and nails him with a Frankensteiner. The pin... 1 2 KICKOUT!!! C.R: Living just barely kicked out. Binder sends Living against the ropes, but out of no where PHAT nails Binder with a chair. The ref didn't see anything. Living sends Binder against the ropes and nails him with a DDT. The hook and the kickout by Binder. This man is strong! Living tries to scoop up Binder, but Binder reverses it with a bridging back suplex! 1 2 3!!! DING DING DING!!!!! Scotty Walker: Here is your winner and advancing to the finals, Spellbinder! SPELLBINDER over LIVING BEEF via PINFALL at 0:13:07 ------------------------------------------------------------------------ C.R: What a victory for Spellbinder. Let's go back to Scotty Walker for our last bout of the night! ------------------------------------------------------------------------ Semi-Final Match - Big Apple Tournament MIKE KUTSANATO vs DEATH Scotty Walker: The following contest is scheduled for one fall. It is a Semi-Final match for the Big Apple Title Tournament. Coming down the aisle and hailing from Tokyo, Japan, here is Mike Kutsanato! His opponent and hailing from Unknown, here is Death! DING DING DING!!! C.R: Both men cirle each other, but Death lands a forearm to the stoamch of Mike. Death sends Mike against the ropes and nails him with knee lift. Death sets up Mike and delivers a bridging back suplex! 1 2 3!!!!! DING DING DING!!! Scotty Walker: Here is your winner and advancing to the Finals, Death! DEATH over MIKE KUTSANATO via PINFALL at 0:02:38 C.R: That's all, folks! I'm Charlie Rongton for Mr. Quinby, Scotty Walker and the rest of the BAWF staff saying so long from St. Raymond's! -----------------------------------------------------------------
