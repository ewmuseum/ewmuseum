BAWF Friday Night Heat, 22 May 1998

22 May 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
http://members.tripod.com/~nwcouncil/ ::Camera opens up to inside the jam-packed Jacob Javits Center in midtown New York City, New York. "Don't You Stop" by Puff Daddy is blasting over the public announcement system. Fans throughout the venue are on their feet, expressing their First Amendment Rights. Among the most notible signs are "Free the BAWF!" "Floyd, Will You Marry Me?" "VO 4 Life!" and "BAWF and LSW - The Only NWC Regions That Mean a Damn.":: Lance Key: Hello everyone, and welcome to Friday Night Heat! I'm Lance Key, and joining me at this time are Franky Frig and Cult Palomino! Franky Frig: What a historic night we have for you tonight! In the main event, inside a steel cage, Jimmy "Pretty Boy" Floyd will once again challenge Sephi... Cult Palomino: Wait a minute, Franky! Who said Sephiroth is even gonna show up? He hasn't been seen all day! Let's face it. He WON'T show. Franky Frig: Well, Commissioner Greene has said that if Sephiroth doesn't show up to defend his title tonight, that title will be stripped and the new champion will be none other than Jimmy "Pretty Boy" Floyd! Lance Key: Speaking of Commissioner Greene, he has something he wants to say a little later on. Are the rumors true? Franky Frig: No one knows for sure what he has to say, but there has been speculation, and we'll leave it at that. You're definitely gonna wanna stay tuned for this, folks. Cult Palomino: I know what I'm here to see. I'm here to see the Dream Partners Tag Match! Lance Key: That'll be a big one to say the least. This match will feature BAWF Television Champion Troy Croft teaming with Lord Wrath to take on Manhattan Champion Shawn Arrows and his unlikely partner of "Offspring" Aaron Oakley! Now don't get us wrong, folks. These wrestlers did not choose their partners. Not at all. Franky Frig: At our last Dream Partners Match, which took place at Cloud 9, the wrestlers themselves got to choose their partners. This time, Commissioner Greene chose who was on who's team! This match is NOT sanctioned by the BAWF, meaning it will not go on any records, BUT there is a special stipulation involved... Lance Key: Notice how both teams have a singles champion on them. Well, the winning team will square off against each other next week right here on Heat for that title, whichever it may be! Cult Palomino: But guys, answer this. What would the motives of Arrows and Croft be to win this match!? Franky Frig: Greene has announced that should he feel one of the champions blew the match purposely, then it will be THAT champion who has to defend the belt next week, in the stead of the champion of the winning team! Cult Palomino: Wow! Lance Key: Plus, tonight on this telecast, the Big Apple Tag Team Titles will be on the line in a 3-Way Dance! Franky Frig: All of this plus so much more! And we're gonna start off in a big way, folks, as Dark Shadow locks horns with BAWF veteran "Ringmaster" Chris Kill! Lance Key: Just by taking a look at these two names you know we have another spectacular contest on hand. Dark Shadow is among the hottest rookie talents in the NWC today, when he became one of the first EVER graduates from the very tough Training Camp. Franky Frig: The Training Camp is going to pump out some great stars in this federation, this is just the beginning. But what a big time recruit this Dark Shadow was for the BAWF! Cult Palomino: I've seen him in limited action.. the guy's an exciting wrestler. Lance Key: On the other hand he's facing one of the veterans of our sport. Chris Kill is still a young guy, but when it comes to wrestling terms he is a veteran in the ring. He's known as the Master of the DDT. Cult Palomino: Because he only knows one move.. the DDT. Franky Frig: That's not true! Kill is a very technical wrestler.. his DDTs highlight his great ability. Lance Key: Kill has yet to find a real groove in the BAWF, but when he's on his game, he's one of the best. I can't wait to get this one underway! Franky Frig: Let's get to Scotty Walker for the ring introductions! Scotty Walker: Ladies and gentlemen, introducing first.. ::Dark Shadow walks out of the back, wearing a tee-shirt with "DS" on the front, and "MSE" on the back.:: Scotty Walker: Hailing from the great unknown.. and weighing in at.. ::Suddenly Chris Kill's music blares over the PA. Scotty Walker stops mid-sentence as Shadow turns around and looks for him. The music quiets down and he continues.:: Scotty Walker: Weighing in at... ::The music again blares. The crowd suddenly is in a rush of screams and boos as Shadow looks towards the back.:: Lance Key: What on earth!? OH NO!! CHRIS KILL IS COMING OUT OF THE STANDS!! Cult Palomino: Look at that grin on his face! He's ready for this one.. Shadow doesn't have the foggiest idea where he could be! Lance Key: OH NO!! HE HAMMERS AWAY! Franky Frig: He drives shot after shot into the back of the head of Shadow! Dark Shadow goes down! A kick to the jaw.. and elbow drop.. Chris Kill ambushed him! Lance Key: The crowd responds the only way they can.. by booing and throwing things at him! Cult Palomino: Kill is going to drag him up.. This match hasn't officially started.. ANYTHING goes right now! Lance Key: KILL SLAMS HIM INTO THE CADES! Franky Frig: OUCH! Chris Kill just flung him right into those steel ring barricades.. and Shadow is hurting early! Lance Key: Slowly he's going to drag him towards the ring.. running his face across that metal! He lifts him up.. CRASH!! Franky Frig: OH! He throws him into the steps! Cult Palomino: Gotta love that! Franky Frig: Why would you love that? Cult Palomino: Cuz he's bleedin! Lance Key: That he is! He's cut over the left eye and Kill lifts him into the ring! This match is finally going to begin officially! Franky Frig: Good, maybe we'll get a little order around here.. Cult Palomino: I don't want order I want blood! Lance Key: Kill lifts Dark Shadow up in the air.. Backbreaker! Straight onto that outstretched knee.. Franky Frig: This crowd.. who was jumpin when Dark Shadow came out, is a little stunned by the Kill's early attack. Lance Key: Chris again is going to slowly take him up to his feet.. This time he backs him towards the corner with a series of chops.. ut-oh... he's going for the 10 hit salute! The crowd counts along.. Crowd: 1..2..3..4..5..6..7..8..9.. Cult Palomino: NO! HAHA! Franky Frig: The crowd loves that.. Dark Shadow uses a burst of energy to hit Kill where the sun don't shine, and stun him! Cult Palomino: Yeah, but instead of attacking Shadow's going to use thistime to rest.. Franky Frig: Rest!? He's hurtin.. he's not wasting time, Cult. Lance Key: The crowd is mocking Kill, and he doesn't like it one bit! What in the world is this!?! Chris Kill is rolling outside of the ring.. OH NO!!! GET SECURITY!! Franky Frig: He's right up in the face of a fan.. Cult Palomino: Yeah! Some nappy looking older woman!! Lance Key: Cult!! Cult Palomino: I guess that's the type Lance is into.. if you know what I mean.. Lance Key: Oh come on! Franky Frig: Kill better worry about Shadow on the inside! He's back and standing.. he heads over, and climbs out onto the apron! Lance Key: When Kill turns around.. he's going to get an eye full! Cult Palomino: NO!! A FIST FULL! Shadow comes flying off and POUNDS him with a right hand! Lance Key: Wow.. Kill hits the ground hard! Franky Frig: This time it's Shadow that's going to roll him back into the ring! Lance Key: Man did he ever rock him with that right hand.. Kill doesn't know the current year right now! Cult Palomino: He didn't know it anyway.. Franky Frig: Shadow sets Kill in the ring.. Lance Key: He's going to lift him into a delayed vertical suplex! WHAM! Nicely executed! Franky Frig: Ut-oh.. he's going to take it up top! Cult Palomino: Kill's done for! I think Shadow used a foreign object.. He must of!! Lance Key: He has nothing in his hand!! Cult Palomino: Yeah, but we don't know where that hand has been! It's got to be foreign! Franky Frig: What are you talking about? Lance Key: I don't want to know.. Shadow leaps.. Missile Dropkick!! Shadow's on a roll! Franky Frig: It looks like he wants to end it all right here!? Lance Key: It would appear that way! He's going to grab ahold of Kill... and boost him up onto that top rope! Cult Palomino: This crowd is getting very rowdy.. Why? Lance Key: He's going to try to hit the Shadow Plex.. this move is incredible!! And he's got him high into the air! Here it comes.. off the top rope.. IF HE HITS.. IT'S OVER... Franky Frig: YES! Cult Palomino: WHAT A MOVE! 1.. 2... 2 1/2.. 3!! Lance Key: Turn the lights out on Chris Kill.. this one's over! What an upset!! Scotty Walker: Here is your winner, Dark Shadow!!!!! Franky Frig: A GREAT victory for the upcoming star! Wow! Lance Key: Next up folks is an exciting chance to see future BAWF superstars in action! Both Miguel Gudino and Sir Winston Cahill have impressed everyone with their young talents here at the BAWF.. Franky Frig: Yeah, these two have been going at ever since their induction into the BAWF, and this rivalry has just gotten hotter and hotter. The only thing they seem to agree on is that Americans are insupieror to them. Cult Palomino: Yeah.. but one thing I know, EVERYONE hates these guys. If you take a look at most of the BAWF wrestlers that the crowd boos, they have their loyal following to back them up. But when it comes to these two foreigners, all we hear is booing! Lance Key: We've been following an interesting twist that shocked a few viewers when they heard the news Cahill's manager Duke has left him. I wouldn't be shocked if he made an effort to get back at Winston.. but that's left to be seen. Franky Frig: The crowds getting a little bit rowdy here and that could only mean one thing! Lance Key: On his way to ringside now is the "Latin King" MIGUEL GUDINO!!!!! ::Miguel is rolled out on a golden throne by three female escorts. The crowd boos wildly.:: Cult Palomino: Now this is what I'm talking about! I wonder if he really speaks Latin. ::Both announcers role their eyes as Cult chuckles.:: Lance Key: Miguel's in the ring.. and the booing is growing louder and louder! Folks, here is Winston Cahill!! ::The booing continues, never slowing from Miguel's introduction. Cahill takes a few seconds to pause and spit in the crowd every couple of steps. He is responded with several beers to the face, flying paper and lots of cursing.:: Lance Key: Security better watch this crowd, they're liable to head out there and get involved in the match their selves! Franky Frig: These two are starring each other down, it's very obvious from what has gone down between these two that this is going to get very ugly, very fast! Cahill's going to grab the microphone.. this is a gutsy move! Cult Palomino: He's already got Miguel's blood boiling, I don't see what he's planing to do here. Winston Cahill: American filth.. listen to me! You say I can not win with out Duke.. HA! I laugh at you, I don't need him.. I NEVER NEEDED HIM!! Now let's do this, YOU [BEEEP!!!] ::FAST FORWARD:: Franky Frig: Gudino's going nuts on him! He's gouging at the eyes and swinging with lefts and rights! A kick to the stomach.. Lance Key: He runs off the ropes.. MOONSAULT BODY BLOCK!! WOW! Cult Palomino: This crowd is noisy in the early going.. they're cheering because Cahill got hit.. but they're booing Miguel as well, this is nuts! Franky Frig: That's an orthodox start.. High risk move early! Lance Key: But it's also high impact.. and he's back to his feet to continue the attack! He drags Cahill up and delivers a series of European uppercuts, driving him to the corner. Irish whip to the other end! He's going to follow it up.. Handspring Elbow Smash! Franky Frig: What a show Miguel's putting on! Cult Palomino: He's got a contrasting style.. right now he looks like a luchador, but we've seen other times where he's using all submission locks. Lance Key: Gudino's waiting on the middle turnbuckle.. Cahill's up, he spins around.. Miguel leaps! Franky Frig: No! Cahill rolls away, Miguel lands on his feet after the missed flying clothesline! Cahill comes on the way back.. a kick to the gut by Cahill.. Snap suplex!! Lance Key: Cahill stays on him.. a left.. a right, another left! Cult Palomino: I love it! Lance Key: The ref pulls him away.. But now Cahill's up kicking away at the rib area. Gudino's going to bail out of the ring! Cult Palomino: Good call there, he wasn't fairing to well and Cahill went after him with everything that he had. Franky Frig: But Winston's going to join him on the outside! It's gonna be wild! Lance Key: Miguel has no idea he's out there yet! Cahill's sneaking around.. looking for.. OH NO! He has the ring bell! He has the ring bell in his hand! Miguel doesn't see him.. OH NO!! Franky Frig: Cahill swings.. NO!! HE DID SEE HIM! Cult Palomino: Miguel was baiting him in! He quickly snatched up Cahill's own scepter and clubbed him with it! Cahill hit's the ground hard.. He's in serious pain! Lance Key: The crowd reacted with a hush there, they can't believe how hard he hit him! Franky Frig: Neither can I.. that echoed through out the entire arena! Lance Key: Miguel's going to roll back into the ring.. he finally has a chance to recover, while Winston is on the outside, and he may be in la-la land! Cult Palomino: He's got 10 seconds to figure out where he is and get back inside of that ring, or this one is all over! Lance Key: Gudino's waiting for him now.. The second he does roll into that ring he's going to be assaulted! Franky Frig: But if he doesn't, he loses anyway.. it's a lose-lose situation! Cult Palomino: Not for Miguel! 7... 8... Lance Key: Cahill slowly rolls in.. And he's greeted by kick after kick! Now he's scraping the toe of that boot across his back! Oh my! Cult Palomino: HA! We used to call that a kick start.. Franky Frig: Back in your wrestling days, huh Cult? Cult Palomino: Shut up! Lance Key: Cahill's slowly works his way to his feet.. but this time he's answered with a Jawbreaker! Franky Frig: This could be the begining of a set-up to work the neck over on Cahill for Miguel's V.I.P. Treatment! Cult Palomino: Ya think? Lance Key: Miguel picks Cahill up, and sends him off the ropes.. WOW! He nails him to the mat with a thrust kick right to the chest! And follows it up with an elbow right to the Adam's apple! Franky Frig: The mark of a good wrestler is to put one move in succession with another. That way you're always on the attack. Cult Palomino: It seems like we make a point of it every week to not waste time.. Lance Key: Apparently Miguel doesn't watch the show to often.. he's taunting Cahill! But Cahill answers with a quick sweep of the leg! He kips up.. a lariat! Franky Frig: He's mounting some quick offense! Lance Key: He drops a leg right across Miguel's chest.. but Miguel is quick to his feet anyway! Cult Palomino: That wouldn't be my move of choice to attack the chest.. Lance Key: Cahill heads one way off the ropes, Miguel heads the other.. Here they come towards the center.. Cahill goes low.. Miguel hurdles him! Franky Frig: On the way back.. Miguel ducks his head too soon! A Sunset Flip by Cahill!! 1... 2... 2 1/2... Lance Key: KICKOUT!! He barely got out of that! Cahill thinks he was robbed! He's complaining to the ref! Cult Palomino: He better watch himself! Miguel isn't too hurt from a Sunset Flip!! Lance Key: You're absolutely right! MIGUEL HAMMERS HIM TO THE MAT.. THERE IT IS!!! Cult Palomino: THE V.I.P TREATMENT!! Franky Frig: HE TAPS OUT! Immediately he taps out!! Scotty Walker: The winner of the match via submission, "Latin King" Miguel Gudino!!!! Lance Key: Miguel won't let it go! He won't let it go! He's your winner via submission, but he refuses to release the lock! Franky Frig: The ref is begging him.. and.. finally he lets the hold go! This one's over.. What a match!!! Lance Key: We'll be back with more action after this!
