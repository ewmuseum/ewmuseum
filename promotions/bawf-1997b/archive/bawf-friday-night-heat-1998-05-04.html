http://members.tripod.com/~nwcouncil/ ::As the show's transmission begins, a voice begins voicing over.:: The National Wrestling Council. An organization like no other. Through pride, cult followings, and propaganda, the tension between all six territories, or "regions," has dramatically escalated into what many would be hardpressed not call an interpromotional war. Two regions in particular are the basis of this war: the Texas region of LSW, and the New York region of BAWF. The BAWF and the LSW had originally planned to hold a joint supercard to take place on May 1, titled "Mayday Massacre." As the event approached, the tension between the two regions became so unbearable that the card was actually delayed. A major spark was the propaganda of Keith Schiano, who served as the common denominator between the respective "regional heads," BAWF Commissioner Mark Greene and LSW Commissioner David Cooper. Cooper translated Schiano's insults on the LSW region as a personal attack on him, dictated by Greene himself. The Monday before the Massacre was set to take place, Cooper announced publicly: ". . . .It's evident that the last thing LSW needs is a bush league holding us down. Therefore, until an apology is made from Mark Greene, personally. Lone Star Wrestling is no longer apart of Mayday Massacre. . . ." Cooper wasn't satisfied on simply pulling out of the event, however. He needed something else, something to extract revenge. Somehow, Cooper was able to manage stealing the NWC World Tag Team Champions, 911 and "Hot Stuff" Jason Jinx, from the BAWF roster to join his LSW. This is how a third region, the MWA, got involved. Since 911 and Jinx run the MWA themselves, they naturally sided with the LSW. Jason Jinx verified this in a recent interview when he said: ". . . .BAWF, get over it. . .You're losing the war with the entire NWC (LSW-MWA 1, BAWF 0). . . ." Mark Greene, now furious himself, responded to Cooper: ". . . .You demand a formal and personal apology, Mr. Cooper? I'm sorry. If this is what it takes, I'm sorry--I truly am. I'm sorry this whole mess came about. It didn't have to be this way, Cooper, it didn't have to be. But it is. . . ." Meanwhile, 911, Jinx's partner, threw in his two cents: ". . . .I have a feeling that he [Greene] will be a foe that cannot be dealt with by one blow. To me, the best way to deal with the BAWF is to focus on both their lack of talent and their lack of any true superstars. . . ." Cooper saw opportunity to prove his region superior, and finally gave in: ". . . .I am a man of my word. The fans want Mayday Massacre. Mayday Massacre is what they'll receive. . .But Mark, it's never been about business. It's always been personal. Since our days back in Oklahoma. It's always been personal. You know it, and so do Chris, Ken, Al, and the rest of them. Now you're dubbing Lone Star Wrestling an outcast? A second MWA of sorts? . .This is no longer about the MWA. It's LSW and BAWF, just how it should be. . . ." The battleground has been set. On Saturday night, May 9th, in Chicago, Illinois, the BAWF and the LSW will virtually be at war with one another. Divided the regions will stand, but will they fall United at the Center? Although we can only hope so, history dictates otherwise. It's Mayday Massacre, and it's the war... to settle the score. ::The camera now opens to inside the jam packed Jacob Javits Center in midtown, New York City. The fans are on their feet as usual.
