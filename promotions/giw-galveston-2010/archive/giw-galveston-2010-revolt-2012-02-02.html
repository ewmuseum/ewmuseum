Revolt, 2 February 2012

2 February 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: THURSDAY NIGHT REVOLT LIVE! From Stewart Beach – February 2, 2012 BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! The show starts with a bigger then normal pyro show on the Stewart Beach this week as the opening music for Revolt hits and the fans react with cheers for it, but quickly those cheers falter as they again realize that already standing in the ring is David Sinclair with a microphone they then quickly begin to boo. He lets them simmer down as he looks out at them with a smirk. DAVID SINCLAIR: “Let’s all calm ourselves, there is no reason to boo me. Because at the end of the day you are still going to tune in and I am still going to be in charge of this place. This company is in my hands and that means that it’s going to progress. I have seen Blake for too long make this company a staple of this small little island trying to make it seem like home. Truth be told this should have always just been a starting place so eventually we could get the hell out of here and go on to bigger things. That’s why in just a few weeks we will be in New Orleans for ‘Retaliation’ an event that should truly start our expansion out of this place.” Sinclair drops down his microphone as the expected boos come from the crowd as he looks out at them with a grin and then continues. DAVID SINCLAIR: “Quiet, quiet. I don’t care what you people say, you can’t stop it. We have all the ingredients of a global federation. Just last week we had our star Alioth Starre win the Survive and Conquer match meaning we will get even more publicity. So while you all love him, he just basically pushed us out of this city even more. Now that does not mean that we won’t still have shows here…Well at least until I can get solid bookings every where and then we will be gone for sure.” Sinclair has a good laugh at the crowd’s expense as they boo his sarcasm, this being one of the louder boos we have heard in quite some time. After all he was basically admitting to them his plans to leave. DAVID SINCLAIR: “Last week I announced the Main Event of ‘Retaliation’ when I stated that it will be ‘GIW Undisputed Champion’ Aspen Chaud defending her title against--.” Just like that "Wayward One" by Alter Bridge hits and the fans go wild as Alex Castellanos heads out from the back heading right towards the ring not seeming to be toying around at all with him as Sinclair looks over at him, as he steps through the ropes and into the ring with a mic of his own. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “You are going to come out here and insult these fans intelligence by saying you are going to leave them high and dry? This has been our home for how long now? These people are just as big of a part of GIW as you and I. They represent what we are all about; heck I saw some traveled all the way to Florida to see us. These fans are dedicated and you know what we figured eventually we might branch out, but we will never desert these fans. Especially not because of a man like yourself that ran over another human being and took a company that wasn’t yours because you couldn’t run one yourself.” The fans laugh at the joke some letting out loud “Ooo” chants as Sinclair glares back at him. DAVID SINCLAIR: “Alex I don’t know if you know what you are getting yourself into. You really think it’s wise to go ahead and play with fire like you are doing? That’s a bad…bad idea. You think because I gave you a title shot at ‘Retaliation’ that I respect you? No. I plan on getting rid of you the moment you lose. This will be a message to anyone that thinks they can stand up to authority around here. I am not going to be toyed with and they’ll think of the day that Alex Castellanos career ended, when I make sure you leave ‘Retaliation’ with nothing to fight for.” Sinclair seems to have been genuinely upset by Alex’s remarks but with the fans he is not going to win this battle. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “Not only am I going to win the belt, I am going to kick you out of power at the same time.” But as he finishes up his retaliation to David, "Did It On 'Em" by Nicki Minaj hits and the fans start to boo even louder as Aspen Chaud walks out from the back still done up in a white dress as she heads to the ring with her title belt wrapped around her shoulder as she heads up the steps and into the ring with Alex watching as she walks over to Sinclair taking the mic away from him. ASPEN CHAUD: “Isn’t it cute how you think you have a chance Alex? Don’t you realize you are fighting against a group you can not beat? It’s the simple truth of the matter, you can not beat us. Who is going to help you? NOBODY! This company is ours now. We call the shots. If I wanted to see you fight your mother in a match I could book it. You are nothing Alex, you never have been. So why don’t you go ahead and lay down that mic and admit defeat.” Aspen points at the mat as the fans do not let up on their boos the entire time she speaks as Sinclair stands next to her nodding at some of her statements. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “Unlike you Aspen, I don’t need anyone with me. But I think you’ll find that by the end of the night not only will I have people with me. I will have enough to combat that entire group of yours. Tonight, we start to tear this thing down before it can begin.” Alex grins and then drops his mic stepping forward to look Aspen in the eyes as she glares back at him as he looks from her over to Sinclair and then down at her title as we go to a commercial break. QUOTE After the eventful opening portion of the program we now return to the ring with a ring full of superstars already to go. In total there are seven stars all crowded into the ring in various spots in the ring, all looking ready to go. RILEY STEVENS: “Pretty packed Over the Top Rope Battle Royal this week, huh Oliver.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Rather odd bunch of characters.” There is two referees on the outside to watch the eliminations, one outside tells them to get ready then calls for the bell. DING DING DING! As the match starts all the stars look to the man in the middle, the biggest of them and a man who is screaming at them to come get some that man being Brody Harper. The other stars look around at one another and then collapse on him, throwing big punches on him. Brody tries to fight back but the six other man back him into a corner and then each grab a limb pushing him over the top rope and out of the ring. The fans boo as Brody is shoved over the top rope and flying out to the outside. Brody Harper is Eliminated! RILEY STEVENS: “That was quick.” From there the six men bounce back a bit and then right away they start to brawl. No real direction, each separate into three little groups and go at it. In one corner we see a battle between Henry Malkin and Joshua Acquin. In another corner we have Matt Matlock and Draven going head to head and finally Austin Reynolds and Daniel Starr both sort of squirming around in corners neither going after one another instead trying to stay out of it for a bit. The other two groups do not stop however as Malkin whips Acquin into the ropes delivering kick after kick as Matlock whips Draven off the ropes and catches him with a big punch on the rebound. Malkin jumps up at Acquin only to be caught with a big shoulder press that sends him over the ropes, barely landing on the edge on the outside of the ropes. Acquin heads off the ropes and rushes at him only for Henry to duck down holding the ropes sending Joshua harshly to the outside and eliminated. Joshua Acquin is Eliminated! RILEY STEVENS: “There goes Joshua.” Henry gets up looking at his handy work with a smile as now both Reynolds and Starr see an opportunity heading over after him. But not ready to go out, Henry punches Austin away and then does the same to Starr. On the opposite of the ring Draven has just delivered a big dropkick to Matlock, the fans getting behind him a bit on that side. Meanwhile back on the other side of the ring Henry jumps over the ropes and throws a Thrust Kick to Reynolds and then a Running Knee Strike to Daniel. The fans on this side of the ring are rooting for Malkin on the other side they are rooting on Draven. RILEY STEVENS: “Henry is taking on two men at once.” Matlock and Draven continue to go at it, seeming like there is some legitimate issues between the two. Malkin waits on Starr to get up but he keeps his back to Reynolds who clubs him in the back. Daniel gets up and this momentary truce between Austin and Daniel seems to be working as they grab Malkin and then spin around throwing him over the top rope. But Henry shows some amazing athleticism as he uses the ropes to hold himself up. Starr rushes to push him over but Henry grabs a hold of his head with his legs trying to send him over but Reynolds rushes forward and dropkicks Malkin through Starr’s legs to take Malkin out. Henry Malkin is Eliminated! RILEY STEVENS: “Took two men, but Henry is out.” Now on the other side of the ring, Matlock seems to have Draven in a bit of a predicament as he has him in the corner over the ropes just using his hands to stop it. Reynolds and Starr trying to speed up the process a bit rush over to help him out. Matlock doesn’t seem to want it and the two trying to get involved ends up getting Matt kicked away, stumbling back with Reynolds and Starr. He turns to them pushing them but while they do this Draven heads to the top rope. Starr, Reynolds, and Matlock don’t see it but Draven comes off with a Crossbody able to stretch himself out to take out all three of them as the crowd goes crazy. Matlock hops up first of the bunch and immediately Draven grabs him and tosses him over the ropes to a loud cheer from the crowd. Matt Matlock is Eliminated! RILEY STEVENS: “Draven just took him out.” But Matlock upset with it gets right back up, slapping the canvas and jumping up onto the turnbuckle only to jump down when Draven throws a wild right at him. But while he is distracted Austin Reynolds gets to his feet and rushes up picking him up by his feet and tossing Draven out. Draven is Eliminated! RILEY STEVENS: “Matlock’s distraction takes out Draven.” Matlock on the outside scoffs at him as he heads to the back with Draven getting up looking at the crowd in disappointment. Unfortunately for Reynolds he to waits a bit too long celebrating and its Starr that comes from behind clubbing him over the ropes. Reynolds tries to hold on but loses his grip and heads to the outside with three eliminations rapid fire to end this thing. Austin Reynolds is Eliminated! JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…Daniel Starr!” The fans boo as Daniel stumbles back holding up his arms in victory, though he didn’t do much in the match that proved to be a very sound strategy as Reynolds gets up on the outside kicking the ring barricade in anger. RILEY STEVENS: “Just picking his spots allowed Starr to get the win tonight.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I like this guy…” Starr continues to celebrate the win, as the fans have not at all gained any more respect for him tonight as we go backstage. WINNER: DANIEL STARR As we head backstage for the first time tonight we find ourselves looking at Altair and Scar a very freaky duo that always catch the eye as they prepare for their upcoming tag match. But soon they are interrupted by the heavy foot steps of Tony Miranda who wastes little time starting off the conversation. TONY MIRANDA: “Listen up! I am apparently not getting my title shot, so I decided that I would go to Sinclair and make my request to fight you Scar. I think we both want it and he gave it to me, you and I will fight at ‘Retaliation’ in a Taipei Death Match.” Miranda glares over at Scar who looks back at him ready to go right now if he could but Altair steps up with a smile on his face. ALTAIR “He accepts, but I hope you know that when he beats you he’ll knock you even farther into obscurity until you are just a fragment of GIW’s past.” Altair smirks as Scar seems to be doing his best to contain himself from a fight as Tony does the same both staring at each other before Tony heads on his way the two containing themselves from a fight for now… ==== Else where backstage we now find ourselves intruding what seems to be a very personal matter as a still sweaty Henry Malkin from his match just a few minutes ago seems to be listening in to Alex Castellanos. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “Listen man I know you are new here but I know that you have morals. You signed into a company currently with bad people running it and I want to know that you’ll have my back out there tonight.” Henry nods his head in agreement right away seeming like the type that’ll be there for a friend no matter what. HENRY MALKIN: “You know I will.” Alex looks around to make sure that nobody is around mainly anyone that would be able to bring it back to the Boss. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “So here’s the plan, I have been hearing rumors about something happening at the end of the night. Keep an eye out, be there.” Castellanos pats him on his back as Henry gives him a willing nod as Alex heads out on that having just gained at least one man to fight with him. QUOTE First to the ring was Momma’s Pride. For the second time in a row they would face the Brotherhood of Blood, but they also had new opponents, Dark Haven to contend with. They chose a corner; the far left, and stuck to it. Istanbul decided he would begin the match in the ring for his team. From there, Dark Haven arrived. Pride lead the duo toward the ring and they chose the far right corner. For them, Gabriel would start the match off. The four individuals, two tag teams waited for their last opponents to arrive and that’s when the lights blackened, only to raise into a blood red filter and a modified ‘Man That You Fear’ played over the sound system. RILEY STEVENS: “This is literally the last team I’d want to be faced with, these guys are truly scary.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “That’s for sure.” A light fog caressed over the entry way and bellowed out Altair and Scar. Altair lead the way down the aisle with Scar moving slowly behind him as they used to have entered before Scar reattached an attitude to his mouth, courtesy of his interactions with Tony Miranda. Scar climbed up from the rim of the ring as Altair ascended the short steps and slipped in. Scar walked the rim of the ring to the left corner of the ring, his back to the aisle. He let the black venetian mask remain on his face as Altair took to the ring to start the bout off for their team. DING DING DING! With each contestant in their corners, the bell rang. Istanbul charged Altair, as did Gabriel. With this initial assault, Altair kicked Istanbul in the lower abdomen and then ran Gabriel into the corner. He then took hold of Gabriel’s arm and irish whipped him into Istanbul to topple into a two pile mess at the off-center of the ring. Istanbul was the first to be picked up and thrown out of the ring where he’d have to remain until tagged in. With the initial bull rush over, it was just Gabriel and Altair in the ring. Altair knelt down and scooped up his opponent in a sleeperhold. “You will never be like me!” Altair yelled into his opponent’s ear and then slammed his head down onto the canvas. RILEY STEVENS: “Altair is demented.” Disgusted by the mockery that was Gabriel, Altair tossed him to his corner and forced a tag with Pride. Immediately Pride threw an eye rake Altair’s way and then a gut kick. Altair fought back with a rushed clothesline over the top rope. Though neither were the legal individuals in the ring, Momma’s Pride slid into the ring in unison and charged. This caused Scar to enter as well to aid his partner. Scar knocked Angel down with a throat thrust as Altair used a snap suplex and then the two of them both ran Momma’s Pride over the top rope. Neither Scar, nor Altair really wanted to deal with a tag team that had no reason being in the ring with them. Their focus was on the other team, the team that seemed to be a mock up of them. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “This is why we never have any black stars…” Dark Haven entered the ring and as Pride tried to fight his way against the two of them at first, Gabriel waiting in the wings, the numbers game dominated him. Scar hoisted Pride up with a double choking grip and tossed him over the rope and onto Momma’s Pride. This left Gabriel in the ring, whom instead seemed to cower back out. Altair went for a quick, successful cover. Being the only person to have actually fought back, they let Pride walk out of the ring, but none others were allowed back in. JAMAL JACKSON: “Here are your winners…Altair and Scar!” Momma’s Pride ran. Their pride tucked between their legs, they escaped the arena with what little skin they had left on their yellow bellies. Dark Haven collected themselves and slowly backed up the aisle. Pride threw insults, but they fell on deaf ears. In the ring Altair began to taunt the crowd. RILEY STEVENS: “These two have yet to be tested as a team, they have truly been dominant.” As he did this, Scar stood in the center of the ring and watched the teams flee through his black venetian mask and then raised an arm up to signal the dropping of the lights to a blood red hue as we head to the back. WINNERS: ALTAIR & SCAR Back to the back we go where Cassidy Hightower is quickly typing away on her phone perhaps on her Twitter as Alexis Terry strolls up to her with a grin on her face not looking someone about to defend her belt at all. ALEXIS TERRY: “Hi there. You know that you made a mistake right? You messed with the wrong person. And now you find yourself having to deal with the simple fact that you are going to have to face me sooner or later. And you know what? I am going to crush you; I am going to ruin you. I AM GOING TO END YOUR CAREER.” This all seems to catch Cassidy off guard as she looks across at Alexis struggling to come up with words as Beck Ramsey now enters the room walking over to the two stepping beside Alexis. BECK RAMSEY: “Shouldn’t you be off getting ready for your match? Rather then picking on Cassidy because I happen to like a girl that isn’t a trashy, manipulative, frankly easy loser like yourself. Don’t blame me for upgrading.” The rest of it she could take as just insults but that last bit was a little too much for Alexis as she let out a big gasp and then slapped him across the face. But before he could start a fight Trent Lost stepped into the screen looking at Cassidy. TRENT LOST: “Really? Really…You are going to side with this guy, just minutes prior to our match. After I fought for you last week when he NEARLY punched you in the face. That’s just fucking grand isn’t it? You don’t deserve my respect and you won’t get it out there either.” Lost clearly angry says this all right into the face of Cassidy as Beck steps in his way pushing him away as Trent quickly slaps his hands away. TRENT LOST: “Get your God damn hands off me.” With that Trent is sure to elbow passed him as Alexis now gets a bit of a grin on her face as she talks past Beck to Cassidy. ALEXIS TERRY: “Good luck out there.” Alexis waves as Cassidy quiet the entire time looks on shocked on what has happened with out her even saying a word the entire time as Beck looks back at her to see if she’s ok as the show continues. As she is waiting "Scream If You're Crazy" By D.R.U.G.S hits and the fans begin to boo as Paul Defias heads on out from the back heading towards the ring with that smug look. He was young and he was no doubt confident. RILEY STEVENS: “Last week Defias made his return and he ruined both Rollo and Laurel’s night with sneak attacks in there debut match. Now he has to pay for those mistakes as he faces both in the ring.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I expect this kid to show why he is always one to watch.” Defias heads into the ring looking across at Laurel with a grin, blowing her a kiss as she rolls her eyes and then his music cuts off and Greenback Boogie - Ima Robot hits as the fans cheer on Rollo Durand as he heads out looking completely focused tonight as he heads to the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Rollo was the one truly screwed by Paul last week, having got him eliminated.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Boo hoo.” Durand heads into the ring looking across at Paul never taking his eyes off him as the referee starts the match. DING DING DING! Wasting little time Defias rolls out of the ring going to the outside with a smile on his face as Rollo and Laurel watch him both looking upset at him as Defias heads over to the barricade talking with a woman at ringside. While Rollo seems to want to settle some of the things from last week Laurel throws a big elbow while he is looking the other way putting an end to that. Lane goes right after him, as he seems to be trying to keep an eye on Defias the entire time. Rollo spins and whips her off the ropes; on the rebound he nails her with a big T-Bone Suplex. But as he hops up he turns around to see Paul on the outside still conversating with that same female. The fans boo him but the girl seems into it as Rollo looks around and waits as Laurel gets up, kicking her in the gut and hitting a DDT. RILEY STEVENS: “Rollo is going to work, but Defias continues to watch from the outside.” One to keep it entertaining for the crowd Rollo heads to the top rope but as he is climbing up he finally takes his eyes off Paul and just like that Defias jumps up onto the side of the ring grabbing him and throwing him off the top rope to the outside. Rollo hits the ring barricade hard as Defias watches him for a second taunting him before jumping over the ropes into the ring walking over to Laurel who is just now getting up from the DDT from earlier. He pushes her head around just toying with her but as she gets to one knee he jumps up hitting her with an Enzuigiri that catches her clean and drops her down on to the mat. Rather then pinning her he chooses to run off the ropes and then do a flip then hit the Rolling Thunder at which point he finally decides to go for the pin, 1…2…Kick Out by Laurel. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Defias doing it big.” Defias gets up not seeming worried at all as Rollo gets up on the outside as he grabs up Laurel looking to want to finish it. Lane stumbles up and then he delivers the ‘Knockout Dropper’ a Running Jumping Knee that lands on her chin and sends her falling backwards seeming to be out cold. Defias takes a moment to throw up one arm then drops down for the cover just as Rollo slides into the ring, 1…2…Rollo jumps on top to break it up. The crowd goes wild as Rollo hoists him up and goes wild with punch after punch to Defias sending him stumbling backwards. Paul takes a gamble going for another ‘Knockout Dropper’ but Rollo sees it coming a mile away and ducks out of the way but the referee isn’t as lucky as he takes it on the jaw. RILEY STEVENS: “The referee is out.” While Defias looks down at the referee he turns around right into an ‘Up-n-Over’ an Uranage that lands on Paul. But the referee is out as Defias rolls to the side of the ring Rollo tries to get the referee up but it’s too late. Finally one comes rushing out from the back, as Durand sees this and grabs a hold of Laurel and starts to lock her in the Figure Four to get the win. He gets it locked in as the referee slides in, just as he does this both Paul and the other referee come to and Defias crawls over draping an arm on top of Lane as the referee counts 1…2…TAP-3-OUT! RILEY STEVENS: “What just happened?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Defias got the pin!” Both referees call for the bell to end it but when they see that they both slide out and head to Jamal to tell him what happened as Rollo gets up looking just as confused as everyone else as Defias rolls out of the ring stumbling back. Finally the referees seem to give up arguing and they tell Jamal what to announce. JAMAL JACKSON: “Here are your co-winners… Paul Defias and Rollo Durand?” The crowd boos as Rollo looks around with his hands on his head, not realizing how this happened. Sure Paul didn’t win but he was able to get half of it and judging by the smirk that was good enough for him. RILEY STEVENS: “Wow, the referees made it essentially a two way draw?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Defias had the pin first.” They show several replays and it seems like on every angle we get a different answer but for tonight both men were leaving with a win but one that they both would have to share. WINNERS: PAUL DEFIAS & ROLLO DURAND Nearly time for his match we can see Trent Lost steaming up in the back area breathing heavily as he sits on a bench thinking about what had just happened. Like a sly little fox in comes David Sinclair sitting next to him. DAVID SINCLAIR: “Alexis told me what happened, pretty rough. Teammate, friend, betraying you like that? Pretty harsh.” Lost looks over at him with his angry eyes as he looks back at the ground. DAVID SINCLAIR: “What if I were to tell you that I had a way for you to get back at her and take out Beck at the same time?” Trent looks over at him, but just like that Sinclair motions for the camera man to get out of here and he does quickly as we then take another commercial break. After all it wasn’t often he held this kind of muscular advantage on a person. Cassidy hopped up with a smirk on her face as she goes back to circling Seth trying to get something in. She ducks under a wild punch by Seth and then rolls him up 1…Seth kicks out and then rolls her over so he is on top 1…Then she does the same again trying her best to keep him down 1…Kick Out. Finally the two hop up to their feet, both looking at each other with respect. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Oh yay, happy fun times. Good lord.” The camera cuts over to Trent who stands on the edge of the ring his gaze not having moved since the start of the match. Cassidy and Seth go in for another luck up but this time she is able to move before he over powers her instead grabbing an arm and pulling it behind him. He switches it around and hoists her up but in air she is able to grab the back of his head and drop him down with a Wheelbarrow Bulldog to the mat. These fans seem to be just enjoying this one as does Cassidy who gets up having a chuckle at Beck who screams at her “Just wait until I get in.”. Seth stumbles up to his feet as he rushes at Cassidy who does a Matrix Evasion to dodge him. Ramsey stops and now it’s Cassidy coming at Seth with a Clothesline. He tries to do the same, while he dodges it he seems to throw out his back a bit as he gets up holding probably just joking around a bit. Beck falls to the mat as Cassidy goes to confront but he grabs her by her tights spinning and tossing her over the top rope sending her to the outside harshly hitting the mat. Beck turns around just in time to see it but not stop it and he rushes at him. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Thank you Trent!” Beck turns this into just a brawl the two going all out with punches and the like going back and forth. Seth on the outside jumps down heading over to check on Cassidy who is sitting but is holding onto her shoulder. Back in the ring Ramsey’s rage seems to take over as while Trent stops punching he does not, backing Trent into the corner punishing him with punches. Finally the referee breaks them up, Lost tries to use this to his advantage but Beck ducks his clothesline attempt then rushes forward and boom crushes him with ‘The Collision’ a Running Test Boot that takes him off his feet. Cassidy assures him she is ok as Beck checks on her when all the sudden all three are caught with a brutal attack as Alexis Terry, Austin Reynolds, and Joshua Acquin slam into them sending all three in different directions. Finally as he gets to his hand and knees trying to fight back Joshua rushes forward with a Running Punt to the head that takes him down. RILEY STEVENS: “So let me get this straight, Lost helps them…Then two seconds later they take him out?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “It’s called Sinclair flexing his power.” Just like that Terry, Acquin, and Reynolds roll out of the ring heading to the back. Lost lays sprawled out cold in the center of the ring while Beck, Cassidy, and Seth start to get up trying to recover as it seems Sinclair and this group has gotten the edge tonight. WINNERS: SIN CITY EXPRESS (Beck Ramsey & Seth Ramsey) Again we head backstage this time we come into a rather odd scene as we see Alex Castellanos standing in front of a big group of some of the newer stars here in GIW including the aforementioned Henry Malkin and a few others. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “Thank you all for coming, I wanted to get you all together to explain to you the serious issues that have come up because of the new regime as they call themselves. Aspen and Sinclair are in control but that does not mean we have to kneel down and take it. We can fight it and we can put a stop to it. I can’t do it alone, so I need the brave stars that are willing to do the right thing to put an end to this.” Some of the stars seem to be talking amongst themselves about it but David Sinclair walking into the pile quickly dissolves them. DAVID SINCLAIR: "You better choose your enemies wisely, because I have the power to get rid of you the same way I had the power to get rid of Trent Lost." He says this just loud enough for the wrestlers that dispersed can hear it as Alex gets up into his face not willing to back down. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “I think you may be the one that needs to closer choose who you toy with, because I am going to take you down and I am going to enjoy doing it.” Just like that Alex heads on his way and you have to wonder if any of the stars listened to him as David looks around scoffing a bit as he walks off. QUOTE When we went back to the ring Marcus Hister was the first to the scene heading to the ring up first to a loud ovation from the crowd. He slid himself into the ring and awaited his opponent, Alexis Terry. RILEY STEVENS: “Everyone is pulling for Hister tonight to pull the upset and take the belt.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “The rest of us are going with the sure thing. Alexis Terry.” She came soon after with Lady Gaga playing through the arena. She received a loud jeering response from the crowd to her entrance. She didn’t care. Alexis made her way to the ring and entered on her own time. When she was in the ring, she stood toward the center of the ring with Hister and the referee between them, but off to the side. She handed the title over to the ref who presented it for all to see as this thing got under way. DING DING DING! She snickered and shook her head before giving Marcus a harsh, audible slap across the face. In response, he stepped forward and pushed her to the canvas. He didn’t have an issue hitting a woman and she didn’t have issue kicking him in the knee with both feet. He collapsed and held tightly to his right knee cap. Alexis pulled herself up and began to stop on that knee and used it as a target for the bout. Marcus would know hell when she was done with him. She continued the stomping until she was forced off of him by the interrupting referee. RILEY STEVENS: “Right away she goes after his knee.” She returned to Marcus and raised his right leg, moving him onto his stomach, she bent his leg to expose the knee and slammed it into the canvas three times, ending with another little stomp before she finally backed off to admire her handiwork. Marcus writhed on the canvas as she snugged against the ropes and watched. Referee checked on her opponent, but Alexis would soon interfere him and pull Hister up from the canvas like he were a reluctant, child. She set him up with an Irish whip, but the injured knee buckled beneath him and he crumbled to the canvas. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Haha, he is done.” She took this as a sign to pin her opponent, but Hister kicked out at the count of two. Clearly it wasn’t enough. She pulled him back up from the canvas and smacked him with a European uppercut. Marcus hopped back and then retaliated with a wheel kick. Alexis immediately rolled out of the ring after smacking against the canvas. Hister stood back up, the ropes aiding him, and tended to the tendered knee, but his eyes remained on Alexis. She slid back in, knowing Hister wouldn’t be able to capitalize on the opportunity and then charged him with a clothesline. He countered with an elbow to the chin and then another wheel kick. RILEY STEVENS: “Hister hurt and all is still fighting back.” Marcus pinned Alexis, but she bolted out of it before the referee could fall to the canvas and count. Frustrated, he beat her head into the canvas and tried again, but the results were the same. If he tried again, he’d be bordering on the definition of insanity. Instead, Hister pulled back up and tried to ease that knee of his. He limped only slightly and seemed to get better as he continued to move. He pulled her up, but as he did, Alexis tripped Marcus using that knee and grabbed hold of it again. She began to stomp in on it and then dropped an elbow down on the knee. When she got back up, she never lost grip of Hister’s knee and eventually began to drag him toward the corner post where she exited to the outside and pulled him snug against the ring post. RILEY STEVENS: “…Just brutalizing that knee.” With the pole between his knees, she slammed the injured one into it, then again, and again for good measure. She wanted him down for the count. As Alexis slid back into the ring for a pin, Hister crawled to the rim of the ring for protection. He then pulled out and onto his good leg, the other limping and numb. He tried to massage it back to health, but Alexis was back out of the ring to continue the assault. With a hard right chop, she knocked Hister into the rim. He retaliated with a European uppercut and grabbed a fist full of Terry hair to slam her face down into the rim of the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Hister will not go down without a fight.” Marcus escaped back into the ring and tried to walk off the knee damage. Alexis followed him in soon after. It didn’t take much for her to perform the ‘Flawless Victory’ finisher that would end the match on a three-count. Hister had been weakened and though he tried to kick out, it was over before he knew it. Alexis Terry was the victor. JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner and STILL the GIW Legacy Champion…Alexis Terry!” The crowd boos as Alexis rolls off of him having won this thing by taking out Hister’s knee to guarantee her victory. She hops up as the referee fetches her belt and hands it over to her. RILEY STEVENS: “Just like that she retains her belt and continues her reign.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “That’s why I love her.” Alexis throws up her title into the air, in victory as the fans boo her. This was the last thing they wanted to see but tonight that’s exactly what they get. WINNER: ALEXIS TERRY [Still GIW Legacy Champion] Alioth stood with Sarah Sharp in the back. She held the microphone and began to ask the questions. SARAH SHARP: “Alioth Starre, you were the winner of the massive Survive and Conquer match over at APW, what are your thoughts on your enormous victory?” She asked him holding over the microphone to him. ALIOTH STARRE: “It was all right. A nice way to clear the mind, I suppose. I got the chance to focus on other things instead of what was going on inside. I won, and I knew that I would because no one else puts in the passion and drive that I do, but that can also be to a fault. This time it helped me.” He replied. SARAH SHARP: “Now, Mr. Sinclair has returned to Galveston Island Wrestling. What do you think about these turn of events?” ALIOTH STARRE: “Who? I’ve met him and I’ve heard of him only recently. I still don’t really know what he’s all about. Why is he knocking about like he’s something big? I don’t know him and thus he’s not important.” Alioth answered. SARAH SHARP: “Well, surely you’ve spoken with him? He was the one that put you in the match tonight against Alex Castellanos and Tony Miranda.” Sharp held the mic over to him for his response on a subject she had vested interest in. ALIOTH STARRE: “We’ve spoken, yeah. He tried to bring me to his side of thinking, but in all honesty, no one can sway Alioth Starre. I do what I want and when I want to. I’m not arrogant about it, I’m just an individual and I pride in knowing what I want and need in life. Those are the things I strive for and no one else can tell me otherwise. Not Sinclair, not Aspen, and not Castellanos.” Alioth answered, bringing the topic over toward one half of his opponents tonight. SARAH SHARP: “Speaking of Caste–” Alioth interrupted her and took the microphone. ALIOTH STARRE: “Alex, I’m pretty sure you saw the segment I had before tonight with the cards. You know, three red cards faced down. Two ripped and one, that I hold now in my hand.” He held it up, but dared not show the face of it. ALIOTH STARRE: “I think you know who the first two were. Those two are beneath me and don’t deserve respect, at least not mine considering how they treated me when they were in this company. No, Alex, I’m pretty damn sure you know who they are and who this one is. This last one. The one I think will bring me to my moment of glory. All things in due time, of course, right mate? Cheers.” He walked out of the interview, after returning the microphone to Sarah Sharp. SARAH SHARP: “Well, uh, there you have it. Ever the enigma, Alioth Starre always has strong words to say. We’ll see if he can back them up tonight in the main event for Thursday Night Revolt!” She ended the segment on that note as we take our final commercial of the night. QUOTE Alex Castellanos’ “Wayward One” by Alter Bridge played him and Tony Miranda to the ring. The crowd cheered on Castellanos with vigor. The two entered there own separate ways and reconvened at the center of the ring. They wouldn’t wait in a corner like cowards, but were ready to face their opponent’s front and center. Alex pandered to the crowd briefly as Tony seemed to remain in his own world, focused on the bout ahead of them against Aspen Chaud and Alioth Starre. Two opponents they knew would be difficult to overcome, but knew with effort that they would, without any doubt. RILEY STEVENS: “This team seems to be cohesive in their entrance but they might just be delaying the inevitable break up.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “That’s for sure.” Aspen Chaud was first of her peculiar team. She didn’t wait at the aisle for her partner, no, she was getting the full entry treatment down to the ring. She came face to face with her long time rival and former ally Castellanos. She pointed to the corner and demanded they wait there, that the ring space was hers. Her time. Her moment. Alex merely snickered to himself and in a sarcastically cordial manner, backed away from Aspen with Tony but only about a pace or two. Aspen wasn’t satisfied, but it didn’t matter at this point as the lights dimmed. RILEY STEVENS: “Here is our champion Aspen Chaud who will team with a familiar foe.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Alioth better not let her down.” A projection of the cosmos displayed over the ceiling of the arena and a modified ‘Grand Central’ by Edward Shearmur played over the audio system. After a minute in, Alioth arrived to the aisle to cheers, the opposite reaction of Aspen Chaud’s. He made a quicker entry to the ring to make time and climbed up to the top of the right turn post, facing the aisle side of the ring and pandered to the crowd for a moment. He looked back and down at Aspen, then hopped off to meet her at the center of the ring. The two would decide who was first in the bout. RILEY STEVENS: “The two thousand twelve Survive and Conquer winner, Alioth Starre.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Luck.” It was decided by Aspen that Alioth would be first. However, he insisted ladies first. Eventually Tony Miranda decided it would be Aspen Chaud as he came at her with a clothesline. Alioth threw his arms up and backed to the corner, amused with Tony’s subtly. With Alex and Alioth behind the ropes, the bell rang and the bout began! DING DING DING! Tony collected Chaud up from the canvas and started to strike her with light jabs to the face and upper chest. This went on until he had backed her into the opposite corner, away from Alioth, but on the same side of the ring. She tried to retaliate, but he through her back to the corner with multiple headbutts that the crowd began to count, but he had abruptly ended before they could finish. He took joy in taking that away from them for some reason. With an eye rake and setting Aspen up for a DDT, Tony seemed to have a handle of the situation. Aspen whipped out of his grip and pulled him down with a sit–out jawbreaker. This lunged Tony backward, but only stagger him. RILEY STEVENS: “Tony has wanted to get his hands on Aspen for awhile now.” Aspen followed up on the counter to gain ground over Tony Mirand. She threw a hard backhand chop to her opponent until he felt it and the red in his skin spread like a tidal wave across his pectorals and upper chest. After about ten hard chops, she figured he’d be softened enough for a snap DDT, but instead, Tony threw her off of him and she staggered, only to come back fighting. Aspen kicked his abdomen and tried for the snap DDT a second time. The second time was no charm for Aspen and Tony countered with a snap Suplex. RILEY STEVENS: “Miranda is starting to show that new fire.” He began to taunt her, laughing to himself about her failure to drop him. She had yet to plant him down onto the canvas, but she was on her back, as everyone thought she preferred. He fetched Aspen from the canvas and set her on her own two feet only to knock the female back down with a few head butts. She needed the tag and reached out for Alioth, but she was too far from him. Tony saw this and chortled. He taunted her as she extended her left arm to Alioth for a tag. He clamped her hand between the canvas and his right boot, and then squished down like he were putting out a lit cigarette. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Alioth needs to help out Aspen!” Aspen screamed out. Alioth extended his arm as well, though he knew it was futile unless she could get closer. Tony released her hand and dragged her up. Aspen knew she needed to break away immediately and bitch slapped Miranda hard across his right cheek. He rubbed at the red mark just as she lunged for her corner. She was pulled down by the leg and Tony then began to drag Aspen Chaud toward his corner and tagged Alex Castellanos in eagerly. RILEY STEVENS: “Aspen misses the tag and things start bad and continue to be bad as Alex comes in.” Aspen crawled as far as she could to get away before Alex could seize her, but he didn’t. He seemed to let her go, but only to reach the center of the ring. She felt the tag coming, but then felt Alex pull her up off the canvas for a northern lights Suplex that ended with a pin attempt. She kicked out after two, but Alex never let her go from his grasp. She managed to kick him square in the nose to break free and pull herself to the ropes, on the same side as her opponent, but still on the opposite end of the ring, this time her back to the commentary tables. RILEY STEVENS: “Can Aspen get the tag?” Alex charged her at the ropes, but it backfired when his opponent used the top rope as a rest to prop her up for a big boot. Alex snapped back and hit the canvas like a ton of bricks. Aspen fell to her knees and using the ropes, began to guide herself toward her corner. The crowd actually began to chant for her, and Alioth. They also chanted for Alex and Tony. The crowd seemed divided. Alex was getting back to his feet just as she was reaching her target! RILEY STEVENS: “Here comes the tag!” Finally, Aspen Chaud tagged in her partner! Alioth climbed in slowly, letting Alex take his time to get up.
