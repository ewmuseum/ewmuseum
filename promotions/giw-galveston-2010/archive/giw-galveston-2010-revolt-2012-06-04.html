GIW — Card

Revolt, 4 June 2012

4 June 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: MONDAY NIGHT REVOLT! Trans-Atlantic Cup Tournament Edition! Live from Galveston's North Beach! BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! The ION Television Cameras are LIVE for a special edition of GIW's Revolt for the FB18 Trans-Atlantic Cup Tournament. A crowd of around 6,000 screaming fans are piled around the ring, some standing, others sitting, most elevated in the many rows of stands. Inside the ring, all six competitors in Galveston bracket are standing in the ring, side by side, all of them in ring gear except for Eliza Gray and Ellis Smith, who had byes for the week. “The Rat” by Dead Confederate hit the speakers, and the crowd erupted when the interim Chairman of Galveston Island Wrestling, Matt Kluge, stepped onto the ramp apparatus and made his way down to the ring. He wasn't dressed in attire that would be normal for a Chairman, that wasn't his personality anyway, but rather dressed in a “I'm Aspen's Daddy” t-shirt and blue jeans. He slid in between the blue ropes and immediately made eye contact with Tony Miranda, his friend and former parter, as well as Eliza Gray, whom he despised greatly. He stood in the middle with the contestants divided three on each side – Eliza, Adrien, and Draconis to his left, Tony, Ellis, and Justin to his right. His music died down and he began to speak. MATT KLUGE: “So, last week when I took over this position, I wont lie, I had no idea that this tournament was happening. It must have been another one of David Sinclair's 'growing' plans, so even though I personally believe this is a waste of my time and yours, here we are, and I'm going to run this tournament better than he ever could. So, let's meet the participants in the Galveston bracket of the Trans-Atlantic Cup. First, Eliza Gray, representing... me? Wait, Eliza is who he picked to represent us? No, no, no, we're not going there. We're just going to skip Eliza for now, because thankfully Aspen Chaud isn't in the tournament, and Alexis has been shipped to New York - I'm going to treat this as a vacation and just not listen to you.” Eliza rolled her eyes and held her hand near Kluge's face, telling him to just get out. Kluge obliged easily, and moved on to Adrien Cochrane. MATT KLUGE: “Anyway, what's your name bud? Tell me about yourself.” Kluge handed Adrien the GIW microphone with a big grin, Adrien took it. ADRIEN COCHRANE: “Alright, well my name is Adrien Cochrane. I am twenty six years old, and I'm from New Orleans, representing Premium Championship Wrestling.” Kluge nodded, the crowd cheered for him, Cochrane smiled. Kluge moved the microphone over to William Draconis, who had a totally different deminor to himself. WILLIAM DRACONIS: “For those of you who don't actually know who I am – first, turn on your televisions. I represent Code Red Wrestling. I am William Draconis. Straight from the lion's den. Do you know what I posses more than anyone in the tournament, Kluge? Aggression, a wild side – these paws can do so much with one swipe. In the wild, we take what we want – with force. I'll win this tournament, and I'll take that cup back home to my promotion. Count on it.” Kluge took the microphone carefully from the hands of Draconis, who was practicly seething at the audience who was showering him with boos. Cochrane was trying to contain laughter. MATT KLUGE: “Well, I like Adrien better already.” Draconis chuckled, eyeing Kluge now. They stared down for a moment, then Kluge nodded his head up and down, handing the microphone to Tony Miranda – the GIW fan favorite, legend in the company, and hot wrestler just off a huge win at the last supershow. TONY MIRANDA: “Well... hi... I'm Tony Miranda. I'm a wrestler. For GIW.” The crowd isntantly goes off, Tony smiles, handing the microphone to Ellis with a shrug. There wasn't much more for him to say, the fan reaction said it all. Ellis tipped his cowboy hat to the auience with a slick grin on his face. ELLIS SMITH: “And I am Ellis Smith!” Ellis was obviously expecting a similar reaction to Tony's, but no such luck for him as you could practically hear crickets chirping in the crowd. He clenched his fists, his face turned red. ELLIS SMITH: “No love for Ellis tonight in Galveston, huh? It doesn't matter, you may be able to fool everyone else by putting this show on the beach, but I grew up in Dallas! I know this town is total shit. I can't wait to win this tournament over your beloved Tony Miranda, rub it in his face and yours, and scamper back to the TFWF with the Cup in my hand.” Ellis drops the microphone, sending a horrible noice through North Beach as he raises his hands high into the air, as obnoxiously as humanly possible. He tips his hat and bows, getting huge amounts of heat from the audience. Justin Freebourne, who was standing beside Ellis, looked around awkwardly, then picked up the microphone and jumped up to the top turnbuckle. JUSTIN FREEBOURNE: “HOW YA DOIN' TONIGHT GALVESTON!?” The crowd pops for him as he holds his hand by his ear with a big smile on his face. JUSTIN FREEBOURNE: “WHAT?! I ASKED YA HOW YOU WERE DOIN' TONIGHT GALVESTON!” The crowd responded louder than ever to the energetic eighteen year old, Ellis Smith rolled his eyes and folded his arms. JUSTIN FREEBOURNE: “Well, hey everybody. I'm doing good too. I'm Justin Freebourne, ya know? I'm from ELITE, and lemme' just say, I'm SO PUMPED to get to wrestle here tonight guys! Its going to be so GREAT and hopefully I can win and we can celebrate together!” The crowd cheers loudly again, the energy on North Beach is high as Kluge is given back the microphone. MATT KLUGE: “Alright, you wrestlers, head to the back and get ready for your matches – we have a commercial break up ahead, but we'll be back with the first match... Tony Miranda versus William Draconis of Code Red Wrestling.” Slowly, we fade to commercial break with the Revolt theme, “Over and Under” by Egypt Central, playing us all the way out. - - - - - - - - - - As we return from break, we see Eliza Gray standing backstage with the Revolt backstage interviewer, Zachary Pagonis. ZACHARY PAGONIS: “Eliza, what motivates you on a week like this, when you don't have a match?” A scowl takes over Eliza's face. ELIZA GRAY: “Zachary, I'll tell you what motivates me. Nothing! I signed up for this not to represent GIW, but to represent DAVID SINCLAIR! The rightful chairman of GIW, not Kluge.” Eliza sighs, obviously pissed, but Zachary isn't done with the interview yet. ZACHARY PAGONIS: “Well, Sinclair was stealing money from the company Eliza. That's why Kluge took over, because he's the one who caught him... you can't possibly condone -” Pagonis is stopped mid sentence when Eliza extends her hand, ready to slap the hell out of him, but she obviously smartly reconsiders. She pointed a finger right in his face. ELIZA GRAY: “You have three seconds to make this right, or I'm out.” Zachary looked down, thinking – so many thoughts running through his brain. He looked back up attempting to solve the problem. ZACHARY PAGONIS: “What about the possibilty of Eliza Gray vs. Tony Miranda next week?” Eliza smiles, chuckling at the question. ELIZA GRAY: “Ooh... Good question Zach. After what he did to my Ryan Cavallari – he's practically like my brother in law now – I'll beat him up so bad he won't know what island he's on. Maybe I've found my motivation. Thanks Zach honey.” She kisses him on the cheek, he turns beet red as she walks off camera. ZACHARY PAGONIS: “Yes... you're welcome Eliza...” He smiles, we cut down to the ring for the matches to begin. QUOTE That familiar feeling of a big time match is back in the air as the fans here on North Beach seem excited. "Natural Born Killer" by Avenged Sevenfold the theme of William Draconis hits as the fans begin to boo the entrance of a man they hate so much. RILEY STEVENS: “Ya know, William Draconis had a whole lot to say during the introduction period of the show tonight, do you think he can back it all up Ollie?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Of course he can. Tony is semi-retired, and I'm sure that Ryan Cavallari wore him down pretty good last week at Aggressive Expansion.” RILEY STEVENS: “But Ollie, that match was over in seconds – Tony hardly had to lift a finger. I don't see how he could be worn down from a match like that.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Shut up Riley.” Draconis heads toward the ring scowling at the fans as his music cuts off and “Thunderstruck” by AC/DC hits sending the fans into a frenzy as out comes Tony Miranda fresh off a win and looking ready to earn his shot at the cup here tonight. RILEY STEVENS: “Listen to these loyal Galveston Island Wrestling fans cheering on their man! Tony Miranda has a great shot at winning this whole thing in my opinion.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “No one likes a homer Riley. Do as I said, shut up.” Tony high fives a few fans but still looks focused all the way down to the ring, looking across at Draconis as the referee comes between the two and calls for the bell. DING DING DING! This thing is under way and simply put we all know who everyone is rooting for, it’s Tony Miranda. Not a fan in the building is on William’s side as the two circle and then go in for a lock up. But Tony chucks him to the side; just too perhaps send a message to Draconis. It only seems to anger him as he rushes at him going for a big punch but Tony ducks under it. Draconis spins around, angered by Tony’s action so he loses his game plan. When he turns around Miranda goes for an early Spinning Roundhouse Kick this time its Draconis that ducks under it. The fans boo him as William grabs him from behind and drops him with a falling facebreaker From here he hoists Tony back up smart enough to know that one move is not going to put away Tony. Putting him in position for a DDT but as he goes for it Miranda pulls out his head, but Draconis is still a step ahead as he heads forward decapitating him with a Clothesline any how. Now he drops down on top of him trying to put him away, 1…Kick Out. Still not even close to putting away Tony at this point. RILEY STEVENS: “William Draconis is looking good early on, but can he keep it up?” Perhaps always the problem for Draconis, his anger now seems to start to get the best of him as he hoists up Tony quickly. Trying for a Dragon Fury but again Tony breaks free and grabs the back of his head and hits a Muay Thai Knee. The fans start to come back alive after being shut down earlier. Not releasing his head he delivers another both seeming to make Draconis lose his footing and knowing that Tony grabs him and nails him with a quick Snap Suplex. The fans go wild as he hops up to his feet a rejuvenated man as he stalks him from behind, lining up for something big. Finally when he turns around boom, a Spinning Roundhouse Kick to the face that sends the fans into an eruption as he crawls on top and makes the cover 1…Kick Out! Still not close to putting away the Dragon as the fans stand shocked he kicked out at one of all things. Now hoisting him up knowing he can’t give Draconis any time to recuperate, but as he lifts him up Draconis rakes his eyes which is completely missed by the referee and as Miranda backs up holding his eyes Draconis runs across the ring hitting him with the Spear then the cover 1…2-Kick Out! RILEY STEVENS: “Cheap eye rake leads only to a two count in this one.” Tony gets up holding his gut, perhaps losing his breath with this one. Draconis walks to the corner starting to pull the padding off, he loosens it and the referee comes over to stop him. He pushes him away and then starts to tie it back up. All while this happening Draconis grabs Tony from behind wrapping up his arms and pulling him towards the side of the ring. He's setting him up for a rope assisted DDT, but Tony trips him up, sending Draconis neck first onto the blue wire ring ropes! Draconis gets up to his feet, wobbly feet as Tony grabs a hold of him and hoists him up and puts him away with the ‘Second Impact’ a Running Lygerbomb, the fans going berserk as he makes the cover they count along with him 1…2…3! DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner…Tony Miranda!” The fans go wild as Tony immediately rolls out of the ring throwing his arms into the air celebrating the victory backing up into the fans who all lean forward trying to get a piece of their guy. RILEY STEVENS: “I told you Tony Miranda would be able to fight off William Draconis! Next week he will advance to face Eliza Gray one on one to determine who will go to the final round of the Galveston bracket. Who do you pick to win it all Ollie?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Eliza is going to crush him. Retire already!!!” Back in the ring the referee is helping Draconis up to his feet, however he pushes him away and then slides out of the ring heading to the back on his own as we head backstage WINNER: TONY MIRANDA We cut backstage to see Justin Freebourne sitting on a crate in the hallway, wrapping his wrists with some neon green tape. Out from off screen somes Ellis Smith with a menacing look on his face. ELLIS SMITH: “What are you thinking, trying to outshine me out there?” Justin looks up to him, honestly confused. JUSTIN FREEBOURNE: “What are you talking about Ellis?” Ellis' nostrils flared as he took his black cowboy hat off, resting it on the crate beside Justin. ELLIS SMITH: “That's Mr. Smith to you, kid. And you know damn well what I'm talking about – getting the crowd all riled up. Next time, when I talk, you should just leave the ring.” Justin raised an eyebrow, ripping some tape off the roll and starting on the next wrist. JUSTIN FREEBOURNE: “Isn't that what I'm supposed to do – get the crowd into whatever is going on? I really enjoy wrestling – Mr. Smith – and I wansn't trying to -” Ellis grabbed him by his neck and pressed him against the brick wall – one hand wrapped firmly across his throat, the other pressed hard against the teen's bare chest. ELLIS SMITH: “I'm the best wrestler in the tournament – understood? I've got a reputation to uphold, and if I have little snot nosed brats like you undermining my work – well that doesn't look good does it? Apologize.” Justin said nothing. Ellis was getting tired of his games. ELLIS SMITH: “APOLOGIZE!” He tightened the grip, Justin was begging to turn blue. Then suddenly, the voice of Adrien Cochrane could be heard from down the hall, much to the relief of the fans. ADRIEN COCHRANE: “Hey! What are you doing Ellis?!” Ellis turns his head to Adrien, dropping Justin, who collapses to the floor, gasping for air. ELLIS SMITH: “Softening him up for you, Buck.” Ellis patted Adrien on the cheek, then took off down the hallway as we cut to commercial break, with Cochrane helping his soon to be opponent. QUOTE It is time for the Main Event of the night and the last of the round of six qualifiers. "Whyyawannabringmedown" by Kelly Clarkson hits and though he is relatively new the fans have already begun to like him as the cheers, while not quite yet at the adoring stage are still pretty loud for Justin Freebourne as he heads out from the back walking towards the ring looking pumped up, but holding his bruised neck tightly.. RILEY STEVENS: “Eliza, Ellis and Tony have punched their tickets could this rookie be the third person in?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Ellis roughed him up pretty good backstage, who knows how tough this little pretty boy is. My guess: not very, and Adrien Cochrane is going to clean house.” Freebourne jumps over the ropes and into the ring heading to the top rope and posing as his music trails off and “Last one to Die” by Rancid hits eliciting a loud response from the card who show exactly what they think about this guy known as Adrien Cochrane, who just stood up for his soon-to-be opponent. He was walking straight towards the ring accepting the cheers. RILEY STEVENS: “You’ve got to be a little in awe of this man after he helped out his opponent backstage from a brutal attack by the slick talking Ellis Smith. You dont see that a while lot these days Ollie.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Yup, and this guy calls himself the Dropkick King. He knows he has this match in the bag – I can see it behind that happy face of his, he's an arrogant guy.” Not seeming quite as focused, perhaps taking Cochrane lightly he enters the ring Freebourne falls back towards his corner and the referee then calls for the bell. DING DING DING! This main event is a big one both guys who are looking to advance in the T-ACT but both have that enigma about them that gets people excited about what they can do. Cochrane circles around him, the two shake, as they finally go in for a lock up. While Freebourne is the more lanky fighter Cochrane has just a few pounds on him but the result is a relatively even lock up, however just as Freebourne starts to get the advantage Cochrane pulls him right into a knee to the gut. The fans seem to have enjoyed Freebourne's intoduction here and are rooting for him, but Cochrane sure earned their respect too. Adrien keeps a hold of him and whips him off the ropes. Freebounre hits them hard but his highflying abilities come into play as on the rebound he slides underneath Adrien and ends up on the outside of the ropes when he gets up. Angered by the random move Cochrane rushes at him only to get an elbow to the face. Freebourne then goes Springboard right into a Crossbody Press with a pin 1…Kick Out. Though it was a quick kick out the fans gave him a big cheer as he hit the move as Freebounre got to his feet nodding his head at the fans as he thinks about his next move. RILEY STEVENS: “This a classic technician meets high flyer match up.” Justin waits for him to get to his feet once there he hoists him up going for a Suplex of some sort but Cochrane hooks his leg so he can’t get him up. Now he knees him again in the gut pushing him away at the same time. Getting Freebourne on his heels Adrien rushes forward and hits him with a perfect dropkick to the chin! The fans cheered him as he takes Justin out with that one. Finally he decides to go for a pin but as he reaches down Freebounre pulls him into a Roll Up, 1…2…Kick Out! The fans go wild thinking it was three but come down when the referee holds up a two. Both men get up quickly but it’s Cochrane who is up first rushing at him and hitting a Russian leg sweep that puts Freebourne back on his back. Adrien as he hoists him up calling for the end, what he doesn’t see coming is a Japanese Arm Drag that sends him flipping right through the ropes. RILEY STEVENS: “Can Freebourne pull off the upset?” The fans love it as he rushes immediately off the ropes as Adrien gets to his feet on the outside but doesn’t look back in the ring when he finally does it is to late as here comes Freebourne down on top of him with a Somersault Flip landing right on him! There may be nothing these fans love more then a high flyer and its clear Justin has some fans here tonight….1…2…The count now starts as Justin tries to pull him up so he can throw him back in…3…4…He goes to throw him in but Adrien reverses it and whips him right into the Steel Post on the outside. The fans boo it as Freebourne bounces back and then immediately gets thrown into the ring, from there. Adrien slides in and Freebourne seems to have lost all his wits as he has troubles just getting to his feet these troubles allow Adrien back into the ring as he grabs him and puts him right into the Adrien Cutter, it’s a beautiful move and it’s a winning move here 1…2…Kick Out! RILEY STEVENS: “Justin Freebourne kicked out!” This sends the fans into a frenzy as they start to believe that he can make the come back. Cochrane gets up shocked just as well as everyone else as he asks the referee if it was three. But instead of complaining too much he turns around hoisting up Freebourne to put him away officially. Adrien tries to lift him up for the ‘Dream Breaker’ but Justin is able to get out of it and ends up behind him for a Pure Imagination, a double knee facebreaker! The fans start to go wild as still a little stunned he heads up to the top rope, looking for his move. Everyone fan in the building is on their feet now as he goes for the ‘FREEBOURNE’ a 450 Splash. He stands on the turnbuckle – but is Ellis Smith! He pushed him off. With a thud, Freebourne lands in the center of the ring, head first. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Ellis Smith just ruined that 'awesome' move! What a hero!” Smith wastes no time unleashing blow after blow to Freebourne, relentlessly pounding his head. He takes off his suit jacket, undoes his tie, and rolls up his sleeves, bare knuckles to the forehead. Justin Freebourne is a bloody mess in the center of the ring, totally knocked out and limp, the bell is ringing, but there is nothing that can be done. But Cochrane is up... a LIGHTNING STRIKE! Right on the jaw of the Right Wing Warrior, Ellis Smith, sending him running out of the ring, and on his but with his back against the retaining wall. DARRYL CLINTON: “This match, by the T-ACT rules, will be rendered a no contest through interference.!” Cochrane doesn't seem to care though, as he is spening all of his time helping Freebourne. Several medics rush to aid him as well as Ellis Smith sits on the outside, examining his work. WINNER: NO CONTEST “The Rat” by Dead Confederate once more hits the speakers, and out comes Matt Kluge, not looking happy as he walks quickly down to the ring, microphone in hand. MATT KLUGE: “What the hell are you thinking you asshole?” Ellis Smith jumps over the barricade, away from Kluge, but still has a sick grin on his face as the fans boo him to hell, throwing trash and soda at his blood stained body. MATT KLUGE: “If there wasn't a waiver in this tournament, I'd whoop your ass myself Ellis.” The crowd cheers heavily now as Kluge and Ellis are face to face, with only a barrier separating them. MATT KLUGE: “One thing you may not know about me you Washington DC bag of horse shit – you don't mess with my shows or my wrestlers. This isn't my first fucking rodeo.” Ellis holds his arms out wide, just asking Kluge to attack him. MATT KLUGE: “So next Thursday, right here on another special Revolt, you'll get your tournament match all right, but it will be against both Adrien Cochrane and Justin Freebourne. A triple threat match.” Kluge throws the mic at Ellis, hitting him right in the lip, then takes off back up the ramp. Ellis is furious, he holds his lip and jumped over the guard rail, yelling at Kluge, then he turns... another LIGHTNING STRIKE! A SUPERKICK from Cochrane on the outside of the ring! Ellis is left out cold, Freebourne is being stretchered off, and the show fades away with Adrien Cochrane standing tall.
