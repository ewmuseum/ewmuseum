GIW — Card

Revolt, 10 June 2010

10 June 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: REVOLT! Live: From the Hilton Resort . This is Galveston Island Wrestling: Dare to Defy BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! Fireworks explode from the roofs of the Hilton Resort buildings and the crowds of seven hundred wrestling fans from the Galveston area and beyond are sent into frenzy. This week’s arena is jam packed and they can’t wait to get this edition for Revenge on the road! Inside the ring stands Paul Marshall, the resident play-by-play commentator and overall fan favorite. He is dressed in a nice suit and begins to speak in an excited tone. Paul Marshall: Ladies and gentleman, boys and girls, and children of all ages above eighteen, let me be the first to welcome you to this week’s edition of Thursday Night Revenge! The fans begin to yell out and scream, and just be loud. Paul seems pleased with all of the new excitement, as his face breaks out with a smile. Paul motions for the crowd to quiet down, and they do. Paul Marshall: Tonight is the start of the Galveston Island Championship tournament, the first four matches of the first round will be fought, and only four competitors can advance to the quarter-finals. Let’ send it over to the lovely General Manager Amanda Rowland for some more info… The televisions placed around and above the arena flash over to an image of Amanda Rowland who is sitting behind her desk in her office. She smiles when Paul tosses it over to her. Amanda Rowland: Thanks Paul, tonight is a revolutionary night for the company, tonight is the night that we begin our journey to crown our first official Galveston Island Champion. Because of the time constraints that we have, round one of the tourney had to be split into two parts, tier one and tier two. There are four matches and eight competitors in each tier. The next three rounds each will have a special match type. For instance, if El Ladder defeats Aspen; and Austin Acquin defeats Shayne Helms; the match between El Ladder and Austin Acquin in two weeks could be a ladder match, or a submission match, or maybe even a run around Galveston match… who knows, but what we do know is that participants in the matches are going to have improvise, adapt, and overcome to become the foremost Galveston Island Champion. Now, let’s go over to Michelle Perez, who is about to interview a new fan favorite, Stoner!” The televisions switch over to a view of Stoner, already in ring gear, and Michelle Perez standing in front by the door of the Hilton Resort. Michelle seems somewhat bored. Michelle Perez: Yeah. Thanks Amanda. I’m here outside the Hilton with the fans new favorite son, Stoner. So, ‘Stoner’, do you think you deserve the entire hubbub that is being brought towards you? You don’t look like much… Stoner looks as if he is trying to restrain himself from pummeling Michelle Perez, who knows how long he had been standing with her. Stoner: I can already tell that the fans in this federation are amazing, I can’t even begin to describe how grateful I am to be here, and I’ll do anything I can to make this company a success. Michelle Perez laughs at the answer before rolling her eyes and vigorously smacking her gum. She looks toward the camera, and then back behind it at a cameraman, our best guess is that they told her that she needed to continue with the interview, because she made a very fowl looking face directed towards something beyond the camera. Michelle Perez: OK! FINE! Uh, what do you think of your partner this week and your opponent next week, Paul Defias? He is pretty much universally despised here.” Stoner scratches his chin before he comes up with an answer; he begins to speak in an excited tone of voice. Stoner: Uh… he just seems out of place to me. How old is this guy, like fifteen? I don’t care; I don’t hate the dude… I am his partner this week so we will be partners. I am his opponent next week, so we will be opponents. That’s just how it is, nothing more nothing less. Michelle blows a huge bubble and rolls her eyes once Stoner stops talking. She pops the bubble and pulls the microphone back up to her lips. Michelle Perez: Thank God you’re done now… do you have any idea how long you have been talking? People could give a crap about you… whatever… interview over… Perez out! BATTLE ROYAL MATCH Apollo Calix defeats two local superstars in easy fashion. After the match, the television screen switches over to the scene in Blake Beckett’s office. Blake is standing behind his desk. In front of the desk stands the team of Ima Goner, El Ladder, and Izzie Dead; along with Paul Defias. The Grateful Dead are standing on one side, while Paul is standing off to the other. Blake Beckett: OK guys, I just want to make this clear… you all know what your job is tonight, right. Izzie throws his face into his palm and lets out a sigh. Ima Goner gives him a hug and a pat on the back while glaring at Blake Beckett. Ladder and Paul Defias stand there and observe the situation. Ima Goner: GRAH! Blake Beckett seems confused as Ima is now seething with rage. Paul Defias seems concerned, but a bit interest. Ladder is standing in the background stoically. Paul Defias: Blake, really? This is the best help you could find? These idiots are going to do is slow us down, we can’t afford to be slowed down! Ima shifts her attention from Blake to Defias; Izzie Dead lifts his head off of Ima’s shoulder and begins to yell out zombie like words to Paul who just turns back to Beckett and laughs, he must think that his point had been proven. Blake Beckett: We need extra manpower, or ladder power, or zombie power. I’m guessing that you all know what you’re supposed to do, so we meet at the rendezvous point later in the show. Don’t miss it. “The Climb” by Miley Cyrus echoes through the loud speakers as the fans bust into cheers as from the back comes El Ladder being carried to the ring by none other then his partner in crime Izzie Dead. Izzie sets him up in the corner of the ring and pats him on the back and heads to the back looking to be in a hurry a bit. El Ladder seems ready for this match that’s for sure. Paul Marshall: Here is one of our first combatants in the Galveston Island Tournament a man who last week had a huge showing against Austin Acquin with his team. Colt Cox: Now he faces one of Galveston’s best, there’s no way he can do this alone. Allison Beckett: Yeah right, El Ladder is the best! Colt can be heard sighing as the fans await Aspen’s music. However they are instead by Kenya’s National Anthem. The fans look around waiting to see what in the world is going on when their question is answered as Damu Akpu-nku heads out from the back and slides right into the ring. El Ladder goes on the attack but Damu catches him with a huge clothesline. The fans are in a frenzy realizing that they know who is behind this attack. Paul Marshall: This is a travesty, El Ladder is getting taken out by Damu who we all know is with Aspen Chaud! Colt Cox: We don’t know that Paul, as far as we know Damu is just a beast that has been let off his leash here tonight. Damu waits for El Ladder to get up and connects with a vicious move he calls Da Big Boot’ a Bicycle Kick with his monstrous legs. El Ladder falls down even more lifeless then usual when “Envy” by Nicki Minaj hits and out from the back in her ring attire comes Aspen Chaud with a big grin as she walks down the isle the fans jeering her the whole way. She steps up into the ring and gives a big kiss on the cheek to Damu who then exits the ring as Aspen commands the referee to ring the bell. Though he doesn’t want to the referee knows that he must. DING DING DING! The referee seems ashamed that he has had to do that as Aspen crawls on top of El Ladder for the pin the fans know it’s over already, 1…2…Kick Out! The fans are amazed by the fighting ways of El Ladder as he has just kicked out. Aspen stands up and screams at the referee to count faster as El Ladder gets back to his bottom rungs. Aspen runs at him and tries for a clothesline but instead Ladder no sells! Aspen tries again but only makes Ladder more angry. The fans are in shock as El Ladder seems to have it all going until Aspen connects with a shot to the lower rungs. El Ladder falls back into the corner and Aspen follows it up with Tush Push in the corner perhaps only hurting her own butt. But regardless as El Ladder dazed falls back into the ring Aspen heads top rope and hits a AirBarbie a Crossbody right on Ladder. Usually not a finishing move but after Damu’s attack and a valiant effort from Ladder, Aspen has to have it 1…2…3! She’s won the match after an attack from her associate Damu. Colt Cox: Here is your winner advancing in the tournament Aspen Chaud, see when you have the power she does you can do anything you want! Aspen stands over El Ladder the fans booing her as she holds up her arms in victory moving on here tonight in the tournament thanks in part to Damu who injured El Ladder prior to the match. After the match is over and the crowd settles down the televisions switch over to a new scene. Mitch Conner is standing on the beach with the surf rolling in behind him. He is standing with the REVOLT General Manager Amanda Rowland and seems pretty excited to be here. Mitch Conner: Hello ladies and gentlemen, I’m Mitch Conner here on the North Beach of Galveston Island, the Bolivar ferry is chugging along and the surf is rolling in, but thanks to Galveston Island Wrestling, the North Beach might be getting some more publicity. Here is Amanda Rowland with a huge announcement. Mitch hands the microphone to the seemingly excited Amanda Rowland. She smiles and thanks him for the introduction and continues with her announcement. Amanda Rowland: As many of you know, the Galveston Island Championship Tournament is in full swing, but what is going to happen to the losers of the top tier of the tourney? Next week, when REVOLT is live from North Beach, we will debut the Galveston Island North Beach Championship. It will be a six person match up, and the winner will become the new North Beach Champion. Mitch Conner takes the microphone back from Amanda Rowland and smiles towards the camera. Mitch Conner: Thanks Amanda, as always it’s great to have you for an interview, and hopefully we don’t have to wait until you make another huge announcement to talk again. Back to REVOLT! After another backstage segment we come back to Shayne Helms already in the ring yelling at the crowd to shut up his ego as usual outdoing him. In fact just last week Stoner the hometown boy dominated him in a match. Regardless he doesn’t seem to have any less confidence this week as he prepares in the ring. Colt Cox: This is another one of our tournament matches here tonight with Austin Acquin and Shayne Helms! This will be a match to advance on to face Aspen Chaud. Paul Marshall: Yup and both of these men our very confident and that bit them both in the back last week when they were both dominated, now one will be able to turn things around. Allison Beckett: They are both meanies! An instrumental version of “Papercut” by Linkin Park hits and out from the back with that always cheeky glare comes Austin Acquin heading straight towards the ring ignoring the fans boos. He seems focused as ever wanting to avenge himself after last week. He slides into the ring and points at Shayne and tells him “You’re nothing” before heading top rope and holding both arms high. He then jumps down and throws off his coat as the referee calls for the bell. DING DING DING! The fans hate both men and have no real favorite for this one. They both come together in the ring and both begin to yell different stuff at each other. Both men’s egos coming to a forefront here until Shayne Helms slaps Austin right across the face. Austin follows that up with a clobbering punch to Shayne knocking him right to the ground. Austin stands over him in dominance while Helms backs his way to the corner almost looking scared. Austin waits for him to crawl his way up and then runs at him in the corner. However Shayne jumps out of the way at the last second and connects with a big kick to the face. Shayne pounds his chest now getting to stand over Austin this time. Shayne lifts Austin up and throws him off the rope and on the return falls right into a High Knee Strike by Acquin! The fans aren’t really happy to see either go on the offense. Shayne holds his head in pain as Austin now seems to have snapped a bit as he grabs Helms and tosses him violently into the corner. Shayne hits his shoulder hard and starts to scream in pain rolling around holding it as the referee attends to him. Paul Marshall: It looks to me like Shayne may be very injured here, not sure he should continue. He may have popped his shoulder out of place! The fans actually seem a bit worried however Austin goes back on the offensive despite the referees pleas not to and starts to pound away at Helms on the ground until the referee forcefully pulls him off. Austin stops and grabs his arm and pulls it back into an Arm Bar! The fans watch as Shayne screams in a lot of pain as Austin continues to wretch at him. When it seems Shayne is about to tap Austin releases the hold throwing his arm down at the ground hard. The fans watch in a bit more fear as Austin seems to want to go ahead and finish this guy off right here and right now. He heads to the corner and begins stalking his opponent who is in clear pain on the ground. After a long wait Austin brushes his hair out of the way as Shayne uses his one workable arm to pull himself up in the corner. As he falls forward a bit Austin runs across the ring and clocks him with a huge IMPALER! The fans watch as now Shayne is completely out, for sure. Austin gets on top of him yelling out at the fans as the referee gets down and makes the count 1…2…3! Austin may have seriously injured Shayne all while advancing in the tournament. Colt Cox: That is how you make a statement in Galveston. By physically injuring an opponent all while getting the win, Austin Acquin has really impressed me tonight. Paul Marshall: He did look dominant but Shayne Helms will probably not be back here for a long time, but one has to wonder against his brother’s girlfriend in the next round will Austin let Aspen win, we’ll have to wait and see. Austin Acquin smirks leaning up against the ropes pounding his chest as the fans boo his action tonight. The referee continues to try and get Shayne back conscious while Austin heads to the back moving on here in this tournament. After the match is over and the crowd calms down the televisions above and around the ring begin to show a video stream of Apollo Calix backstage in the interview area in a black suit; he smiles at the camera when they tell him that he can start his segment. Apollo Calix: Hello ladies and gentlemen, my name is Apollo Calix; and as you have seen, I am Galveston Island Wrestling’s newest attraction, and as you’ve seen, I am no joke. I’ve been wrestling for five years prior in various federations, so it’s safe to say that I know my way around the ring. Next week, I make my official debut in an actual match, and it’s going to be the North Beach Championship match. I’m going to compete against five other men, women or maybe even a ladder, I don’t know. What I do know is that now that Apollo Calix is in Galveston Island Wrestling, a whole new era has begun. Be prepared, Apollo arrives June 17. The video feed dies out and the crowd is directed back to the ring, where the match is about to begin. Daniel Days is introduced, seemingly as a jobber before the match begins. "The Way I Am" by Fozzy illuminates the atmosphere, creating a decent reaction from the crowd. The crowd seems to remember that Kennedy defeated Davis last week in a very epic fight. Kennedy makes it to the ring and seems to look down on Daniel Days. The bell sounds, the match begins. Daniel Days attempts to lock in some sort of submission maneuver on the arm of Kennedy but it is quickly reversed into an armbar. Daniel Day's uses a very savvy technical skill to get out of this predicament and then proceeds to drop kick Kennedy right on the chest. Kennedy is now on the turnbuckle. Daniel Days rushes at Kennedy to hit some sort of flying forearm but he misses when Kennedy gets out of the way. Kennedy attempts a roll up but to no avail, Daniel Days kicks out relatively easily. Daniel Days rushes at Kennedy only to be hit by a clothesline. Kennedy picks up Daniel Days and attempts to hit a standing drop kick but he is caught and Daniel Days transforms it into some sort of pinning move. Kennedy, however, kicks out. Daniel Days follows up with a chokehold which is applied for at least 30 seconds. Kennedy struggles to get out of it and does so only after using a couple of elbows to the gut of Days. Kennedy bounces off the ropes but Days counters with a powerslam. Days goes for the cover. 1...2.. kick out! Daniel Days starts hitting Kennedy with a couple of closed fist punches. The referee breaks up contact between the two of them. Daniel Days perches himself on the top rope of one of the turnbuckles. Kennedy gets up. Daniel Days goes for a crossbody but Kennedy gets out of the way. Daniel Days is now dizzy, this is an opportune time! Kennedy hits a nice and crisp DDT and plants Days on the floor. Kennedy gets on top of the ropes and launches himself, hitting a diving leg drop from the top rope (Here comes the BOOM!) ! He hits it! Kennedy goes for the cover! 1...2...3! It's over, Kennedy is the winner! The crowd seems pleased with the outcome. Kennedy is announced to move onto the next round of the tournament. Kennedy seems quite pleased and looks at his opponent one last time before going back to the backstage area. As the match wraps up, the televisions above the wrestling ring and all around it light up with a video feed of Blake Beckett, Paul Defias, and the Grateful Dead out in the parking lot. They are each holding a weapon, Paul with a baseball bat, Izzie with a lead pipe, Ima with a sledgehammer, and El Ladder with a ladder. The group stops in front of a black Mercedes Benz car. Blake turns to his group. Blake Beckett: Are you sure this is the car Izzie? I could have sworn Aspen drove a Lexus… Izzie walks up the car, carefully sniffs the muffler, tire, and license plate before turning back toward Blake and offering an enthusiastic thumbs up. Paul Defias: Blake… I don’t know about this… Blake looks at the situation and re-evaluates. He seems like he doesn’t care though, he mutters the word ‘Go’ and Izzie, Ima, Ladder, and Paul begin demolishing the car. They continue this for a half minute or so, with El Ladder causing the most damage. After the deed is done, the car’s headlight flash and beep can be heard. The doors unlock. Blake and the rest of his crew turn around, and there stands Megathreat, Blake’s old business partner. The camera fades out as the Grateful Dead runs away. The camera heads back into the ring where this time another star is standing there with a big smile as Mike Danielson is holding up his arms with a big grin on his face as the fans give him a half hearted clap figuring that though he has no chance at all, at least he is on their side. The fans are clapping when suddenly Kenya’s National Anthem hits and out from the back comes Damu Akpu-nku comes out screaming at the top of the lungs. Paul Marshall: This may right here may just be the scariest man in the entire tournament known as the “The African Warrior” he’s not one I’d mess with. Colt Cox: And earlier tonight the rumors were true as he has sided with Aspen Chaud; Blake Beckett is surely shaking in his boots knowing this guy is on her side. The fans are all in awe as Damu stands above his opponent in the ring the two men are ready to face off as the size advantage comes into clear site. The referee calls for the bell and right away we can see who looks to be the winner in the faces of the two men. DING DING DING! The two men come man to man in the ring Danielson looking straight up at Damu who looks down with little emotion in his eyes. Danielson charges off the ropes and connects with a clothesline that does nothing to stir Damu. He tries it again but to little avail the fans getting a bit worried. He tries one more time but instead runs this time into a devastating Clothesline by Damu that flips him all the way around. Mike already seems to be dismantled as Damu goes back on the offensive taking him to his feet and whipping him off the ropes into Da Big Boot! The same move he hit on El’ Ladder earlier in the night. Mike is already gone knocked out but instead of finishing him off here the African Warrior lifts him to his feet and sets up for his Damu Drop, a Batista Bomb. The fans look on in fear as Damu lifts him up and slams him down crushingly to the mat. Mike seems to be folded over and for the second time tonight Damu has left an opponent down and out. He puts one foot on the lifeless body of Mike and flexes as the referee makes the count 1…2…3! The fans may not like it but thus far Damu has looked dominant. Colt Cox: This guy is a BEAST! He just killed Mike Danielson here tonight, we may not understand him but sometimes actions speak louder then words. Allison Beckett: He’s really big. Paul Marshall: One thing is for sure he’ll be advancing to the next round of the tournament and after this, can he be stopped? Damu grabs Mike’s lifeless body and disposes it out of the ring as he stands high in the ring yelling in his native tongue as the fans boo him, already wanting to see this guy meet someone that can truly challenge him. "Kings Will be Kings" by Helloween" begins to play. Stoner makes his way to the ring along side his partner Paul Defias. The two get a nice warm reception from the crowd. The two run to the ring and pose. "I Get Money- 50 cent" blasts through the speakers. The crowd gives little to no reaction, however, some of them boo the song. Out comes Chris Armstrong. He seems like quite the cocky individual. He goes inside the ring quickly. "Til Something Breaks - Sick Puppies" begins to play. Scott Rydell comes out to a couple of boos from the crowd. He smirks and laughs at some of the people in the crowd. He enters the ring and seems to want to cause problems with Chris Armstrong, telling him not to screw up. "Horsepower" slowly hums, exciting the crowd. Wake Walker comes from behind the curtain and does a peace sign which gets the crowd roaring. He circles the ring, slapping the hands of fans and finally enters it after about two minutes. Wake Walker's music continues to play as Izzie Dead comes out, limping towards the ring. Wake Walker looks at him and smiles. Once Izzie Dead makes it inside, he takes an empty corner which Wake later meets him by. "Zombie Prostitute by Voltaire" shocks the audience as it plays. Ima Goner comes out, also limping. She enters the ring. Finally, Acquin is announced but there is nothing but silence. Scott Rydell attacks Stoner which causes a major fight to ensue. Eventually, the referee takes care of it and gets everything under control. In the meanwhile, Ima Goner asks everyone where Acquin is but nobody seems to know. The match begins with Izzie Dead and Chris Armstrong. Chris Armstrong begins to go on the attack, revealing a mixture of technical moves. Chris Armstrong finally ends his assault on Izzie Dead by hitting a German suplex. Whilst Armstrong does these moves, Scott Rydell screams, "That's pathetic!" "Is that the best you can do?" "I've seen El Ladder do better than you!" and other such rude comments. Armstrong eventually has enough of it and hits Scott Rydell square on the mouth which gets the interest of the crowd. Technically, Rydell is now the legal man. He enters the ring and knocks down Armstrong with an uppercut. In the meantime, Izzie Dead is tagged out by Stoner. Stoner goes inside and watches the two argue and hit each other. Eventually, Rydell is pushed by Armstrong into the corner of Walker. Walker tags himself in and pushes Rydell out of the ring. Wake Walker attempts to clothesline Armstrong but is caught by the devastating clothesline. Armstrong leaves the ring and stands on the apron. Rydell gets back next to Armstrong. The two don't seem happy. Stoner locks in some chokeholds on Wake Walker and hits some clubbing blows. Stoner tags in Defias who continues the attack of Walker. Walker, however, easily gets the upperhand and spinebusters Defias. Wake Walker begins to pose for the crowd. Without thinking, Defias crawls to Ima Goner's corner (who is alone) and tags in Ima Goner. Ima Goner limps quickly towards Walker who hits another spinebuster, this time on Ima Goner. Rydell and Chris begin to argue as Wake Walker hits Ima Goner with the Trickster. Before Ima Goner is hit by the move, she can be heard saying, "I'm a goner". Chris elbows Rydell on the chin, knocking Rydell out. Chris continues the assault on Rydell on the outside. Wake goes for the cover! 1...2.. Stoner looks like he just recovered from the spinebuster, but it's too late! 3! Wake Walker's, "horsepower" begins to play. Izzie Dead looks around cluelessly. Wake Walker picks up Izzie Dead and begins to run around the ring celebrating the win. Rydell and Chris are broken up by referees. Defias and Stoner begin to argue themselves and Ima Goner begins to wake up after 15 seconds. She clutches her fist, as if angry that Acquin was not there. Wake Walker raises his arm way up high along with Izzie Dead's to end the show.
