GIW — Card

Revolt, 2 September 2010

2 September 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Galveston Island Wrestling Presents: Thursday Night Revolt! Live! From Tiki Island on September 2nd, 2010 BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! The scene opens as the cameras zoom around the GIW Ring, signaling that GIW Revolt has begun! The fans go wild and chant the names of their favorite superstars as the camera focuses in on Oliver Thibodaux and Riley Stevens at the Revolt announce stand these two have become the solid announce team for the show. RILEY STEVENS: “Welcome everyone to another edition of Revolt! Tonight is a pretty stacked care in my opinion. From top to bottom you got stars all over the place. Starting of course with Chad Munroe versus Mike Danielson. Chad’s been impressive to say the least and quite frankly, nobody has proven they can stop him yet.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “After that we have a very interesting tag match between Joshua Acquin teaming with ‘GIW Women’s Champion’ Kacie Church facing off with Ryan Daniels and the debuting Pandora. Which means at least it won’t just be divas in the ring the whole damn time.” RILEY STEVENS: “Since lord knows you can’t put up with that I know you can put up with watching Tony Miranda versus a former ‘ION Television Champion’ Jake Kennedy square off for the very same title in what should be a great bout.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “And then it’s time for EPW to put their money where there mouth is. Because Aspen Chaud has put out a open invitation to any of the guys from that show to come and wrestle her here on Revolt. One has to wonder if anyone will show up, I doubt it they got a punch of pansies anyways.” RILEY STEVENS: “Well our main event is not a bunch of pansies as we will see ‘GIW Legacy Champion’ Jamie Vaccaro versus Eric Sparrow who last week crashed the main event and though he failed to do any damage we all took the message loud and clear. There’s no better way to prove he deserves the shot then to defeat Jamie Vaccaro. What a show now let’s get ready for our first match.” We come to the ring where the fans already see one man in the ring which is Mike Danielson who is stretching out his legs. As we turn our attention to the stage where soon “Watercolour” by Pendulum echoes through the crowd as Chad Munroe heads to the ring looking more focused this week then he has in the past. Looking like he’s in an ass kicking mood. RILEY STEVENS: “Here is the undefeated Chad Munroe, heading down to face fan favorite Mike Danielson. What is that in his hands however?” The fans give a rather mixed reception for Chad as he steps into the ring staring over at Mike who is stretching out his legs. One thing we notice in his hands is a clip board that he sets down in the corner of the ring. The referee calls for the bell almost immediately to start this match. DING DING DING! Mike runs full steams right after Chad who runs forward crushing him with a clothesline that literally sends him spinning into a circle. The fans are a bit in awe of his strength and if there was any doubt about who was winning this before, well yeah that’s gone now. Danielson hops back up rather quickly but again runs into a Bicycle Kick from Munroe. The fans are stunned at how quick this is going as Munroe lifts him up and sets him up. This is a fitting move for what just happened as it was quick, but very painful. As Munroe hits ‘The Execution’ a Running Powerbomb. He bends down and makes the cover as the fans sit in awe as he hooks the leg 1…2…3! OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Well that was quick.” The referee immediately checks on Mike as Chad gets to his feet and calls for a microphone after having picked up his clipboard. He holds the microphone to his mouth and starts to talk. CHAD MUNROE: “LISTEN UP! I have an announcement to make. Since I have been here on Revolt I have been knocking people back one by one by one! And I am still undefeated yet I continue to get booked against these weak competitors? I’m sorry but it’s gotten a bit old. So I was offered a spot by David Sinclair on Evolve.” The fans boo the hell out of him realizing where this is going as he raises his clipboard and then pulls it open showing off the contract that he was given to come to that show as well. CHAD MUNROE: “And if I can’t get any respect on this show then you know damn well I can over there! So GIW, my time with you is OVER! And I am officially a member of EVOLVE!” The fans boo as Chad signs the contract right then and there tossing his mic out of the ring not caring about GIW property any more. Before he leaves however he looks back watching as Mike gets to his feet. He shakes his head and lifts Mike to his feet and crushes him with ‘The Decapitator’ a Modified Vertebreaker basically ending Mike Danielson. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Good Riddance to him we don’t need him!” RILEY STEVENS: “Another person stolen by EPW here tonight perhaps to fill the empty spot.” The fans are giving him all sorts of boos as he stands over Mike before heading out of the ring and towards the back having done what he came here to do as the segment comes to a close. WINNER: CHAD MUNROE We return to the backstage area where an EPW Member’ is walking the halls. This man is perhaps the biggest mystery on Evolve his name is Marcus Hister. As he is walking through the halls, when suddenly stepping in his path is Joshua Acquin staring him down. JOSHUA ACQUIN: “Just what do you think you’re doing back here! You think you can just waltz around our show?” Before Marcus can give him an answer Joshua gives him a big shove backwards not afraid to fight when suddenly Amanda Rowland intervenes jumping in between the two men. AMANDA ROWLAND: “Joshua back off in case you forgot Aspen has a open invitation to face anyone she’d like from EPW. So until we learn otherwise that’s just what he is here to do.” Amanda holds back Joshua with one arm while staring over at Marcus though there is a bit of perhaps lack of trust between the two as Marcus grins across the way at Joshua who is still breathing heavy wanting to fight. MARCUS HISTER: “You should keep your dogs on a tighter leash next time. You wouldn’t want him to get hurt.” Marcus brushes by the two of them with Joshua making one last advance but is pulled back by Amanda as the segment comes to a close with Marcus heading on his way. After the short break we come back to the ring where we are set up for Mixed Tag Action as Kacie Church and Joshua Acquin are chatting in there corner while Pandora and Ryan Daniels are doing the same in there’s. RILEY STEVENS: “Time for some Mixed Tag action between these two teams. Pandora the debuting diva with the Ryan Daniels a man trying to climb back after a fall from grace. Against Joshua Acquin a man who we just saw nearly fight with an EPW star backstage. And finally our ‘GIW Women’s Champion’ Kacie Church.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “What a jumbled mess this is.” Back in the ring it’s decided by both teams that it’ll be the men that start the match with both of them heading to the center of the ring. As the referee sees that the teams are ready and calls for the bell. DING DING DING! The two men circle around each other and they go right away into a test of strength in which Ryan seems to actually win. He backs up Joshua a few steps before Joshua catches him with a big knee to the mid-section. With him set up he grabs him and whips him fast off the ropes. However Ryan jumps up using them to propel him back towards Joshua with a big flying dropkick. The fans go into a frenzy after that stellar move as Ryan hops up and waits for him to get up. When Joshua gets to his feet he walks right into a Russian Legsweep from Daniels who has gotten out to a good start. He goes for a quick cover 1…Kick Out. Not yet weakened enough that’s for sure. He goes on the offensive again lifting Josh to his feet only to be hit with a big shoulder thrust from Acquin. With Daniels sent back Joshua whips Ryan into the corner. As he goes on the attack the referee pushes Joshua away allowing Kacie to pull at Ryan’s eyes while the referee is looking the other way. Ryan pulls away while Pandora begs the referee to watch what’s happening. Joshua grabs Ryan and pulls him out of the corner. He again whips Ryan off the ropes and on the return Daniels grabs him and hits a big Facebreaker Knee Smash that sends Josh to the mat. With him downed Ryan goes right into a Boston Crab! Locking it in. The fans cheer as he holds it in tight only for Kacie to step into the ring and kick him off. The fans boo as she walks back to her corner. As Ryan gets up a bit annoyed by that. With Joshua getting up slowly the fans cheer when he dropkicks him in the back sending him to the corner in a seated position. With him downed he runs across the ring and connects with a huge Knee to his face. The fans cheer as Ryan seems in complete control here tonight. He backs away smiling to the crowd as he waits for Joshua to get up saying that it’s over. Finally Ryan lifts him up and whips him into Kacie’s corner. He bounces forward but unseen by him Kacie makes the tag in. When Josh falls forward he walks right into ‘The Beefy’ a Scoop Lift Powerbomb that crushes the ring. Some fans go wild as he hooks the leg but the referee then informs him that there was tag telling Ryan that he must head out. RILEY STEVENS: “That was the match right there! But now its wide open as the women will come in.” Ryan Daniels seems a bit frustrated as Acquin rolls out of the ring but he heads to his corner and wastes little time cheering on Pandora who prepares to wrestle for the first time in GIW against the champion. Both girls come into the ring and things are ready to get interesting. They go for a lock up but its clear Kacie Church is stronger as she slams her to the mat with a huge toss. The fans boo as Kacie stands over Pandora taunting her, as Pandora gets back up not willing to give up. She hops back up and then runs at Kacie again only to be given a big Hip Toss. Pandora sits on the mat looking up at Church who seems to be enjoying tormenting the new girl. She gets back up and Kacie holds her head out for a free shot from her. As she does however Pandora instead goes to the mid-section hitting a big kick that catches Church off guard. With Church now holding her mid-section, she runs behind her and hits a big Running Bulldog. The fans cheer her on as she hops up and when Church gets back up, she takes her back down with a clothesline. Pandora seems to be a bit on fire now when suddenly a revived Joshua Acquin gets up from the ground and rises up onto the ring apron to distract Pandora. Ryan hops down to pull him down but Pandora is on fire right now and she instead hits a big dropkick that sends him backwards right onto Ryan Daniels on the outside. Pandora gets up realizing her mistake but when she turns around she walks right into ‘The Ice Breaker’ an X-Factor as the crowd boos. Church pulls up one of her legs as the referee makes the count 1…2…3! OLIVER THIBODAUX: “That’s why she is the champion!” The fans boo as Joshua’s distraction perhaps just led to a loss by Pandora here tonight. Kacie hops up and the referee hands her belt back to her which she holds high up not worried about her partner at all as she slides out of the ring. As she walks up the ramp out from the back comes Alexii Ravynova. This causes Kacie to stop in her tracks and stare back at her. RILEY STEVENS: “Last week in that title match Kacie won the belt but Alexii wasn’t involved in the finish and it’s clear these two girls who claim they are the best have some unfinished business.” The two continue a wicked stare down as in the ring Ryan Daniels has recovered and helps up Pandora in the ring after a tough loss tonight. Back on the ramp Alexii continues to glare down at her as Kacie responds by holding up the GIW Women’s Championship as we head to the back. WINNERS: KACIE CHURCH & JOSHUA ACQUIN After a break from the action we head to the parking lot in which Scott Rydell is currently sitting in. He seems to be thinking or just wasting time knowing that he isn’t booked for tonight’s show. Suddenly we hear a car brake in the background and from out of no where we see Jack Johnson and Bret Battery run around and go right after him. Both men grab him and toss him against the wall. Rydell powers out trying to battle away from both men, but the numbers is just to much. We can now see David Sinclair walk into the scene, watching as Bret uppercuts Scott into a big clothesline to the back from Johnson. Both men get down and start to pound him with left and rights. DAVID SINCLAIR: “Hurry up let’s go!” The two stand up and finally Bret puts the finishing touches on the attack by dropping a trash can onto Scott’s head before heading off with his Boss into the car before pulling away. We roll back to Scott Rydell who is down and out and other then those watching this, it seems to have gone completely unnoticed by those in the back; one of their own had just been attacked. We return to the ring after it’s done. Tony Miranda and Jake Kennedy begin in the ring. Jake Kennedy goes for a drop kick and misses. Tony Miranda stomps on Jake Kennedy a couple of times. Jake Kennedy tries to get the upper hand because of the support of the crowd and eventually does, hitting a bunch of clotheslines and managing to get Tony Miranda off his game. OLIVER THIBODAUX: "You know, sometimes I just think that life isn’t worth living for anymore." RILEY STEVENS: "What do you mean, Oliver?" OLIVER THIBODAUX: "I mean, with a name like this…once I’m fired from this job…nobody will hire me!" RILEY STEVENS: "I see. Well, how’d you get this job?" Eventually, Jake Kennedy has so much momentum behind him that one person is actually cheering! Jake Kennedy goes for his finisher rather quickly and commit’s a rookie mistake. Tony Miranda tries to roll up Jake Kennedy but Jake Kennedy kicks out at the count of two. Jake Kennedy quickly stand sup but Tony Miranda goes for another roll up. OLIVER THIBODAUX: "I begged Blake Beckett for two hours straight and cried." RILEY STEVENS: "Well I’m glad you got the job." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Me too. I still don’t have enough money to pay off my FAFSA loans though." Kick out by Jake Kennedy at the count of two. Jake Kennedy gets up and Tony Miranda goes for another roll up. Jake Kennedy kicks out. RILEY STEVENS: "You went to college?" OLIVER THIBODAUX: "UCLA! *Begins to cry*" RILEY STEVENS: "It’s okay, it’s okay Oliver! Everyone at UCLA is a failure... especially the ones who want to get into medical!" OLIVER THIBODAUX: "You’re right, I should’ve transferred to Rice, the best University in Texas~!. Thanks for making me feel better." Finally, Jake Kennedy stays on the floor and finds it useless to continue since Tony Miranda is spamming roll ups. Jake Kennedy tricks Tony Miranda into thinking that he has given up but in reality, Jake Kennedy intended to lock in an armbar! Tony Miranda looks like he is in extreme amounts of pain! RILEY STEVENS: "We should probably focus on the match that’s going on right now." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "What match?" RILEY STEVENS: "This match. It’s Tony Miranda versus Jake Kennedy." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Oh, I haven’t heard of any of them. Are they as good as chicken?" RILEY STEVENS: "No, but they ARE FIGHTING FOR A CHAMPIONSHIP!" Tony eventually manages to get out of the armbar and applies an armbar of his own to Jake Kennedy! Jake Kennedy easily gets out of it and changes this to a crossface! Tony counters it and changes this into a crossface of his own! Jake Kennedy gets out of the crossface and begins to lock in a Texas Cloverleaf! This match is amazing! But wait, Tony Miranda counters it yet again but this time simply gets up! Jake Kennedy is confused and also gets up but it’s all a trap! Tony Miranda hits his leg lariat and then pins Jake Kennedy. 1...2...3! The move seems to have put Kennedy out for long enough to be pinned! Tony Miranda is handed the ION Television title and begins to celebrate but Jake Kennedy comes from behind and attacks Tony! He picks up the ION title and picks it up and poses. He gets booed and then leaves, talking trash. WINNER: TONY MIRANDA (New ION Television Champion) Backstage, Wake Walker and Aspen appear to be talking in a locker room. Wake Walker looks like he has a small cast on his pinky, as if he had broken it. WAKE WALKER: "Oh, c’mon Aspen. You’re the toughest woman I’ve met, there’s no way that you’re actually intimidated by these failures, they’re GIW rejects! You could actually take them all on and single handedly defeat them all." ASPEN CHAUD: "Wake, stop it. I know… but just in case, you can never be too sure! It’s not like we’re both machines that can take on the entire roster!" WAKE WALKER: "Well…you’ve got other people to help you. You’ve got the rest of the Revolt crew. What about them?" ASPEN CHAUD: "I know that I can depend on them but just in case this situation gets a little bit sticky, I want to make sure that you’re on our side and not with Sinclair." WAKE WALKER: "Rest assured that I’m on your side, Aspen but… I’m going to… have to tell you the truth. See this right here?" Wake Walker lifts his pinky cast. WAKE WALKER: "As a result of Eric’s brutal beat down on me last week, I have broken my pinky. Because of said injury, I am unable to compete this week. Having said that, how much could I possibly help any of you? I have a broken pinky and I don’t want it to get any worse. Maybe when it gets better, I’ll be there for any situation." Wake Walker leaves and takes his belt with him. Aspen crosses both her arms and then shakes her head. We come to the ring when suddenly "Bullseye" by Aly and AJ hits and the fans explode into cheers for their sweetheart. Out from the back with a huge smile is Aspen Chaud holding up one arm as she waves to them before heading down towards the ring smiling the whole way. She stops to high five a few fans before jumping up the steps. RILEY STEVENS: “Here is Aspen Chaud herself, who tonight may have a huge challenge after calling out all of EPW to a match up. Normally one guy is a challenge but potentially six of them? Well that could spell trouble.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Let’s face it, she’s done for.” She steps into the ring with a few whistles from the crowd before walking to the middle ropes and leaning through them giving the camera a kiss before looking to stage awaiting her opponent, if any. Soon her question is answered as “The Man Who Sold The World” by Nirvana hits and the fans begin to boo as Marcus Hister walks out from the back representing EPW. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “One guy?” RILEY STEVENS: “I am a bit surprised there is only one of them but he is the show’s resident scary monster, so perhaps that’s all they feel they need.” Hister shows little real fear as he slowly heads up the steps, he enters the ring glaring at Aspen before screaming out “This company is nothing without me.” as Aspen nods her head ready for a fight as the referee calls for the bell. DING DING DING! Right away the two come face to face and though Marcus Hister isn’t a very tall man he still has a distinct size advantage in this one. He smirks slowly getting down onto his knees showing no respect at all for Aspen. Aspen looks at him shaking her head then looks from the crowd back to him before slapping him hard across the face bring a loud cheer from the crowd. Marcus gets to his feet slowly holding his cheek, suddenly becoming infuriated as he grabs her and screams something at her. She tries to tug away then throws a big punch that’s caught by Hister now having hold of both of her arms. The slap seems to have brought out a lot of anger in him as he throws her hard to the mat. With her on the ground he begins to stalk her taking so steps only for Aspen to kick up into his chest then hop to her feet and run at him. She however runs right into a huge clothesline from Marcus taking her head off. The fans are a bit surprised that an Evolve guy could be doing this right now. He lifts her up and throws her into the corner and starts to choke her out. The fans boo him as the referee counts 1…2…3…4…At the last second Marcus breaks the hold as Chaud holds her neck in a bit of pain. The referee pushes him back to let her get up but he walks around him and lifts her to her feet. She however crushes him with a big elbow to the face and when he falls back she goes to the top rope. However when she gets up there, he runs forward shoving her off the top to the outside where she lands hard. The fans are stunned at the beating she’s taking while Marcus taunts in the ring. The referee begins to count her out 1…2…3…4…At about four Marcus slides out of the ring breaking the count as he sees her getting up. He grabs her and throws her right into the ring pole, hard her shoulder getting crushed. The fans boo as Marcus grins wasting a bit of time to yell at a few fans before sliding back into the ring, telling the referee to count. 1…2…3…4…5…Aspen now is finally getting to her feet…6…7…8…She’s up…9…She slides into the ring narrowly getting there before the referee counts to ten. RILEY STEVENS: “That was a close call right I thought maybe he’d defeated one of our own.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Only a kid from Evolve would want to win on a Count Out.” The fans cheer as she gets back in struggling to her feet as Marcus seems frustrated not expecting her to have it in her to get back in. He waits in the corner for her to get to her feet and when she gets up he runs across the ring connecting with a big Wheel Kick! Right away he hooks her leg 1…2…Kick Out! The fans are witnessing a beat down but as of now Aspen is not out yet. Hister gets up and decides to end this thing right here and right now calling for a ‘Mystery's Darkness’ known in most circles as the RKO. Aspen gets to her feet and stumbles into it but is able to avoid the move by jumping back. Hister swings wildly caught off guard as Aspen runs forward and hits him with her move the ‘Rage Within’ a Thesz Press Followed by Punches! The fans cheer as she unloads before being shoved off by Marcus. Both of them get to their feet at the same time but Marcus out of no where connects with a big Spear. The fans boo as he again makes the cover 1…2…Kick Out! The fans cheer as Marcus angrily lifts her up and whips her into the corner. He runs at her only for her to dodge it at the last second. Now with him in the corner she runs forward and hits the ‘Tush Push’ a Turnbuckle Flying Butt Shot! The fans cheer as she heads up to the top as Marcus Hister falls out to the center of the ring where she follows up with the ‘AirBarbie’ a Flying Crossbody right into a pin 1…2…Kick Out! Aspen wastes little time hopping to her feet as Marcus slowly gets to his hands and knees. She runs off the ropes and connects with the ‘Dream Ride’ an Axe Kick! The fans cheer as she stares down at him realizing he’s not quiet done yet as he stirs a bit. She slaps her butt calling for her infamous move as she grabs his ankle and locks in the K.M.A.! The fans cheer in approval as Marcus squirms around trying to get out before tapping out. Aspen has defeated him. RILEY STEVENS: “Aspen wins! She defeats EPW Member, Marcus Hister!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “See look at that, we are better.” Aspen gets to her feet holding up her arms as the fans chant GIW in chorus over her. Marcus slides out of the ring in pain as the fans go insane. Marcus slides out limping to the back angry with himself wondering if there would be consequences for his loss as GIW has gotten the win over EPW here tonight. WINNER: ASPEN CHAUD [pic] We head to the backstage area where there is a meeting currently being held by Amanda Rowland and Blake Beckett we come in mid-conversation so we can hardly even really heart the two of them. But as soon as we get in there the door opens and Joshua Acquin walks into the office. JOSHUA ACQUIN: “You wanted to see me?” Amanda looks from his over to Blake who nods his head very calmly, realizing that Mrs. Rowland is asking him to do the honors as he speaks up to Joshua. BLAKE BECKETT: “Joshua we can’t have you sparking fights around here with the EPW guys. We don’t need you escalating anything further so we’ve decided to suspend you for one week without pay, you won’t be allowed in next week.” Blake seems to feel a little regret for this but knows he must set an example as Joshua seems a bit stunned by it, not thinking in a million years he’d be suspended for trying to stick up for his brand. JOSHUA ACQUIN: “Did you see what they did to Scott Rydell tonight? You’ll miss me next week when they attack again.” Joshua slams out of the room closing the door hard behind him not happy about this at all as the camera returns to Amanda and Blake who both seem a bit unhappy about Josh’s reaction to that. AMANDA ROWLAND: “He is right about Scott Rydell; you have any plans for what we can do about it?” Amanda looks over at Blake who rubs his chin for a bit thinking long and hard about ideas for it before seeming to come up with an idea before shooing the cameraman as the segment ends. "Hero" by Skillet begins to play. Jamie Vaccaro comes out, with title in hand then begins to try and get the crowd pumped up which actually works. He poses with his title belt once at the ramp and then enters the ring. He begins to speak with the ring announcer, however, we never know what the two of them actually discuss. Jamie Vaccaro lays his title on the floor which is then soon picked up by one of the time keepers on the outside. Sum 41's "Fatlip" blasts through the speakers. Eric Sparrow comes out to a mixture of cheers and boos, with the boos predominating after last week’s attack on Wake Walker. Eric enters the ring and begins to get Jamie in a frenzy by shouting at him. The match begins with the bell. Eric Sparrow and Jamie Vaccaro begin to lock, similarly to how Wake Walker and Jamie did last week only this time Eric Sparrow gets in a cheap shot on the gut and then hits a closed fist on Jamie. Eric Sparrow begins to kick Jamie several times whilst he is on the turnbuckle. Afterward, Eric does a pose which gets the entire room booing. Eric tries to pick up Jamie but he begins to fight back with a couple of elbows to the gut. Eric gets the upper hand however by hitting a clubbing blow to the back of Jamie. Eric goes for a cover but only gets a one count. Eric begins to lock in a headlock which Jamie actually can’t seem to get out of. Once Jamie looks like he’s out of it, he goes to the outside and picks up a steel chair. He enters the ring again. The referee tries to stop Eric Sparrow from hitting Jamie with the chair. RILEY STEVENS: "Wake Walker to the rescue!" Cheers can be heard! Wake Walker comes out, with his pink in it’s own little cast and takes down Jamie. He begins to hit a couple of hits on Eric before the match is thrown out. RILEY STEVENS: "Horray!" Wake Walker finishes it up by hitting The Trickster on Eric Sparrow. Jamie heads to the back, dazed. Looks like Wake received his retribution! As the show comes to a slow close. WINNER: ERIC SPARROW [After Disqualification]
