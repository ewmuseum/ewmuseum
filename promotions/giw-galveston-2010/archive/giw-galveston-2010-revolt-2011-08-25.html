GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: THURSDAY NIGHT REVOLT LIVE! From The Commodore Hotel – August 25, 2011 Rather than the normal pyrotechnics that come with each edition of Thursday Night Revolt, the camera pans around the empty arena of the Commodore Hotel. The fans haven’t packed the stadium seating like they normally would have, the TV’s in the audience were flashing blue screens. We cut over to Riley Stevens at the announce booth, noticeably separated from Oliver Thibodaux. Riley looks directly into the camera and speaks directly into the camera in an almost somber, but very serious tone. RILEY STEVENS: “Ladies and gentlemen, this is Revolt… and this is our two hour commercial free special edition. Unfortunately, some circumstances are causing the show to be delayed this evening. Earlier this afternoon we received a package, inside were statements that we felt gave us a just cause to call the police and wait for them to wrap up a thorough investigation of the premises before letting fans inside. Unfortunately, we do not have any delay packages available to you, and cutting to a commercial break is not an option. So, bare with us while this wraps up. Thank you.” The camera fades out on Riley, then cuts over to Amanda Rowland who is walking quickly backstage with a huge file in her hand. She stopped right in front of a police officer and shoves the file into his chest. AMANDA ROWLAND: “There is your file, this is just a prank. Let the fans inside NOW! We have a show to put on. This is all the information you need to know about him - but as far as I know he is in Bermuda.” The Officer looks at her with a twinkle in his eye. The younger man peeks through the file, then nods, then sucks on his cigarette. He stares at Rowland for a moment, then smiles. POLICE OFFER: “You know, I’ve been on this job for a while and I have never seen such a strong, determined woman like you. Funny, on TV you seemed pretty ditzy.” She bit her lip, she noticed the cameraman behind her, then the picture quickly cut over to Blake Beckett, Paul Marshall, Megathreat, and La’Sarah Audric, who were standing behind some barricades. Megathreat jumped over to assuage the fans anxiety by singing some autographs. Audric looked on dismay as Beckett and Marshall turned their backs to the crowd. PAUL MARSHALL: “On this night of all nights. If it really is him, he is going to pay. We can sue him if he doesn’t get sent to jail for this. I can’t believe it Blake. I really cant.” Blake leaned against the barricade and took a deep breath, his eyes were fixed on a single spot on the asphalt. Hundreds of fans were screaming behind the barricade, every shout hurt Beckett a little bit more. From the crowd, Apollo Calix and La Vienta come hurdling over the barricade. APOLLO CALIX: “Yeah, there is a good seven hundred and fifty tonight Blake. The police are trying to cram them all into the parking lot, but we need to get these people seats pronto.” La Vienta and Apollo Calix wait for a moment, there is no reaction from the chairman so they decide it would be best if they went back out to the crowd to keep things interesting. The camera cuts over to a new shot of Megathreat trudging through the crowd armed only with a Sharpie and a will to sign a hell of a lot of autographs. He signs posters and shirts among other things, welcoming the fans along the way. Suddenly, the group of fans begin to churn forward, the gate had been opened. Megathreat is nearly lost in the stampede. We fade into the Revolt pre-show video package featuring all of your favorite stars, then the BOOM! BOOM! BOOM that alerts the fans that they are on their way to a Revolt show. We cut over to the former Revolt announcer, Allison Beckett, talking to El Ladder. ALLISON BECKETT: “…and then they told me to leave my hotel room and clear out because of an arson threat. I mean, seriously, who arson-izes thing anymore? Like, yeah. I was pissed.” Finally we fade to a commercial. Although this was billed as a commercial free episode of Revolt, when it came down to it, this was the poison the producers decided to pick. QUOTE “Warrior by Disturbed” hits and John Rawlings comes walking out with a smug look on his face and some small boos are heard. "Gasoline” by Porcelain and the Tramps blasts and “The Pride of St. Petersburg” comes out with a louder more intense booing section. RILEY STEVENS: “These two both have small back stories…Rawlings is known as a member of Damien Dunn’s group…” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “While Katya drinks and dates Trent Lost.” DING DING DING! The match starts with a body slam by Rawlings he goes for a chinlock but Katya slides out and kicks him away. Both wrestlers are up and Katya drops Rawlings with a Jawbreaker. Rawlings gets up and doges a elbow, he stands behind Katya and hit’s a backbreaker. He applies an Indian Deathlock but Katya reaches the ropes. Katya whips Rawlings into the corner and uses a boot choke for five seconds. She runs in with a short forearm shot and fires off a small volley of strikes. Rawlings makes his way out and both wrestlers brawl, Katya using a more refined style while Rawlings swings more for the fences. Katya kicks Rawlings in the ribs and he responds with a sharp punch to the side of the face which brings Katya down. Rawlings comes over only to be suckered into a pin which gets a 1 count. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I’d let Katya pin me…” Katya tries for a Cloverleaf but Rawlings knocks her away. Both of them continue to batter each other Rawlings catches a kick and Katya turns it into a Dragon Whip. Katya rolls away from Rawlings but is met with a clothesline. He catches Katya before she goes down and tries to put her away with a Kati-Hajime, Katya was able to reach the ropes before hand. Rawlings pulls her away and hit’s the Jersey Drop for a count of 2. He puts her in a Double Underhook and lifts her up, Katya shifts her weight and comes down landing on her feet. She breaks free and goes under Rawlings and uses an Abdominal Stretch. Rawlings tries to flip her but it just cinches the move in even tighter. Finally he is able to power free and is met with a Swinging Neckbreaker. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “You think she is drunk right now?” Katya hit’s a standing moonsault and follows it with a pin for 2. She hooks Rawlings up for a From Russia With Love but takes to long and Rawlings is able to break free taking Katya down with a T-Bone Suplex. He goes for a Fall From Glory but Katya pushes him away and drops with a surprise Tiger Suplex. Both wrestlers are groggy now, Katya dodges a punch and pulls Rawlings over her shoulders. She bails on the Agent Provocateur instead using a quick fireman’s carry. Her sharpshooter attempt is met with a hand to the ropes. Rawlings get up and Katya DDTs him. Katya goes to the top rope but Rawlings racks her. He picks her up for a Double Arm DDT off the top of the turnbuckle. Panting Rawlings stands up holding onto the ropes for support. Katya manages to stand and is met with ‘The Fall From Glory’ for a 3 count. JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…JOHN RAWLINGS!” The fans seem unwilling to really cheer either so it’s met with boos as Rawlings gets to his feet taunting them as, this great contest comes to a close with Rawlings advancing. WINNER: JOHN RAWLINGS We cut backstage to Aspen Chaud and Alex Castellanos standing in front of a Blackout poster. We seem to have caught them in the middle of conversation. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “I have no idea what happened… this has been a really strange night.” ASPEN CHAUD: “If it really is him, that problem needs to be dealt with ASAP.” ALEX CASTELLANOS: “And what does he stand to gain?” ASPEN CHAUD: “He operates off of revenge - the guy is a maniac Alex, trust me.” As soon as the final words came out of her mouth, Sean Galen and Nick Starks appeared on screen. Alex stood up from leaning against the wall. The four turned and faced each other. SEAN GALEN: “We were thinking, about last week. That’s not how we like to work. We will beat the hell out of you, but it is much more fun if we do it in the ring. So, since we don’t have a match at Blackout… and neither do you two… why don’t we have us a little tag team match?” NICK STARKS: “And no funny business. Best team wins.” Alex and Aspen say nothing; they just stare at the duo. NICK STARKS: “I’ll take that as a yes.” The stare down for a moment, then Starks and Galen head off. We cut back to the ring. QUOTE Jack Waters was first to the scene. He stepped down the aisle with mixed reactions from the crowd around the arena. He climbed into the squared circle and prepped himself by the ropes until his opponent arrived, A.J. Fairchild. RILEY STEVENS: “The TV title tournament continues as two more men vie for a shot at earning a shot at gold.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “This sure is fun…” Upon arrival, A.J. burst through the entry way, but his reaction seemed only slightly bigger than that of Jack Waters. He was young and still had a lot to prove, but he would definitely do that in this match against Jack Waters on Thursday Night Revolt. He slid into the ring and as the bell signaled the match to begin. DING DING DING! Immediately he threw a gut kick toward his opponent and leapt into action with a jumping face buster. Waters turned onto his back and tried to recover quickly from the swift offensive maneuver, but Fairchild was already there to follow up on the assault. He pressed the point of his knee against his opponent’s back and held back against the chin with forceful pressure. Jack Waters was not amused and not about to surrender so early in the game, but Fairchild held tightly to the hold like a child to an item it claims is its. Soon, however, Waters began to show signs of release. Fairchild refused to break the hold, or allow Waters the chance to recuperate, so he simply lowered them both onto the canvas with the hold still intact, but let his knee free to simply grip the chin from behind. Waters scanned his surroundings to discover he was further than the ropes than he’d like. He tried to reach, inch, anything, but Fairchild kept him in that vice-like grip and held tightly to the reverse chin lock. The referee constantly chattering in his ear for a surrender, but Waters would always refuse the annoying referee. It was as if the only job a referee had was to annoy the man in pain, never the one causing it. RILEY STEVENS: “What a match we have here.” Waters began to tear down at the seams. His hand would raise slowly with exhaustion, at first to show no sign of quitting, but now it seemed he was ready. The chin lock had taken hold of him for far too long and he was done. Just as Waters’ hand came down to tap, Fairchild released him forcefully into the canvas. A.J. then recovered with a step backward and pulled his groggy opponent up after him. A.J. set Jack where he stood and then rushed to the ropes where he was propelled back and prepared to connect with a maneuver, but Jack snapped to and countered these plans with a super kick right to the chin. A.J. slapped the canvas like a dead fish and Waters covered for the win, but it was only to the count of one. He could have sword that the referee tapped at least twice, though, but no one acknowledged it. Conspiracies were abound. He forgot it and pulled his opponent up to follow up the assault. A.J. like a cheating cheetah grappled his opponent and used the Stroke of Genius almost out of nowhere to knock his opponent back down to the canvas and complete a full three count for the win. JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…A.J. FAIRCHILD!” RILEY STEVENS: “Great performance here by AJ Fairchild and he’s one step closer.” Crowd cheers him on as he jumps up to the top rope screaming out at them as they cheer him on jumping back down as we take a break. WINNER: A.J. FAIRCHILD Somewhere backstage, Mark Cross walked down the corridor of the arena and stopped abruptly. The camera turned to reveal two individuals blocking his path – Mark Turner and Alioth Starre. Cross smirked and folded his arms, then looked behind them as if to indicate he had somewhere to be. MARK TURNER: “Hey, Cross.” Turner initiated the conversation. “Turner, is it? Alioth.” Cross replied. MARK TURNER: “Goodluck in your match tonight.” Turner offered his hand to Alioth’s future opponent for Blackout. MARK CROSS: “Right. You too, then.” Cross cautiously accepted. MARK TURNER: “So, Cross, things have changed since last you fought Alioth.” MARK CROSS: “I’ve heard, but the only change I’m looking to see is myself with the championship title at Blackout, Turner.” MARK TURNER: “Well, I promise nothing from us will come to you. I would hope that the match will be a fair bout, as well. I know we’ll be professional.” MARK CROSS: “Fair and square? I’m all for that, so I can agree to that. Until the match, Alioth, you can trust me to keep my hands clean and I’ll go out on a limb and trust you as well.” MARK TURNER: “It’s a new world, Cross, but I assure that come Blackout there won’t be a new champion. We’ll just have to see on that one, however.” Turner said with a grin and then the camera returned to Mark Cross as the other two left these two sharing some words before their Co-Main Events. QUOTE “Country Heroes” played into the arena as Curtis Durrington made his entrance. He climbed into the ring and the moment his foot stepped upon the center, his opponent arrived to the aisle. RILEY STEVENS: “You know these two guys are the epitomy of what this tournament is all about. Guys making their own path in GIW.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “You read that off a prompter?” Jak Hendricks made his entrance to the theme of the crowd’s overbearing jeers. He had a different theme, but due to its content, the crowd opted to boo him twice as hard as they may have to a normal bad guy. He too made it into the ring and the referee took a moment to inspect them both before calling for the bell to ring out and begin the match. DING DING DING! It began with a lockup and a twist of the arm that soon transitioned into an Irish whip, which Jak gained the upper hand after touching with a lariat. Curtis wasn’t going to take it laying down, but after a few stomps to follow up, he had no choice, but to wait until Jak decided it was time for him to get up. Soon that came and as Hendricks pulled up his opponent for a maneuver, Curtis Durrington countered with a Northern Lights Suplex that reversed their positions. Jak arched his back in the pain, but managed to kick out after a quick pin attempt by ‘Big Country,’ Durrington. Curtis paused on his knees and considered what to do next. Jak rolled himself up as well and the two returned to their original lock up hold. Jak took control after an elbow to the temple and set his opponent up for the spine-buster, but Durrington returned an elbow to Jak’s head and shoulders until it was him with the upper hand. With the power his, Curtis slammed his opponent’s back onto the canvas with another suplex, this one being the Fisherman’s Suplex. He tried another pin attempt, but it was in vain as he kicked out. RILEY STEVENS: “Close match at the start…” Jak pulled himself free and up to his feet before Curtis could follow up with an attack. He began to wildly thrust his fists into the air, then kick like a disturbed and rabid animal. Some might call it a Jak attack, but a doctor would call it someone that will end up on their mental ward’s roster. Curtis watched Jak and blocked the only few strikes that came close to touching him and landed a big boot to drop his opponent. His third pin attempt failed before he could even cover his opponent as Jak rolled him up and nearly got the three count, but it only lasted a full two and a half. Curtis would not be pushing it any longer. RILEY STEVENS: “Nearly caught him there.” Jak punched at the fallen Curtis until reluctantly pulling off at the request of the referee. With both free, the returned to their lockup. This time Curtis had pulled into another Suplex and then set his opponent up for the ‘War Eagle’, his DDT finisher. With Jak firmly planted onto the canvas, he covered one final time for the full three count. Finally, Curtis picked up the victory and pulled off his opponent to celebrate. JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…CURTIS DURRINGTON!” RILEY STEVENS: “There goes your favorite with a loss Oliver, as Curtis advances.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “That was a quick count!” The crowd cheers for Durrington who got their respect in this match as he gets up holding up his arm in celebration as Jak crawls to the corner holding his head from the DDT still trying to argue the count as Curtis puts himself one step closer to GIW gold. WINNER: CURTIS DURRINGTON Kluge appeared in front of the camera after it panned toward him. He was facing the camera and spoke, cutting a promo. MATT KLUGE: “You know tonight I have to team with The Shadow my new Tag Team Champion. I could make a joke about how I’ve had more tag reigns then Lost has had losing streaks but this isn’t the time. You know my days in the business might be numbered you never know how much time is left…So tonight I’m going out there with the intent to put on a show for the fans and get the win. You know if I complained about every little thing that management did…my promos would be a lot longer. So tonight I’ll handle business...” Kluge grins and walks off this perhaps being his way of reassuring the fans of the tag situation as he walks off the scene. QUOTE We have reached the forth and final tournament bout of the night and the fans are ready to go to see who the last man will be to advance as we return we find Trayton Deluca smiling out to the crowd who give some cheers for his effort last week. “Thunder Struck” by AC/DC hits to a rather small reaction from the crowd whom are still unfamiliar with the man coming out from the back known as Christian Rivers. RILEY STEVENS: “You know these two men both want to make an impression unfortunately only one can.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “When is Full Throttle over?” Rivers heads up the steps and into the ring grinning out at the crowd whom are still trying to make their choices about him. He heads up into the ring and gets ready to go as the referee calls for the bell. DING DING DING! The crowd cheers as Deluca immediately runs across the ring at Rivers going for some sort of tackle, out of no where he wraps his head up and drops into a Guillotine Choke. This catches the crowd very off guard just as did Trayton who struggles to get free, still fresh so he’s able to slip out very quickly but as he does Christian transitions into a Triangle Choke! Wrapping his legs around his head and pulling down as Deluca struggles free but as he pops out he falls to his back and right away Rivers moves into an Arm Bar! The crowd seems almost in awe as Trayton struggle to fight free. Finally he rolls over so that the arm is not in pain. But from there Rivers is able to tug back on it a Kimura! The crowd is starting to get behind him as Deluca slips out and gets to his feet looking around holding his arm almost in disbelief. Taking a big swing with his other arm Rivers ducks it and then jumps into a Rear Naked Choke but as he locks it up Trayton dives back towards the ropes able to grab them. Rivers releases the hold getting to his feet, as Trayton does the same. Deluca almost seems at this point depleted of all heart as he stumbles up to his feet as Christian bounces off the ropes and then into a One Handed Bulldog! The fans cheer as he looks out at them grinning then drops down into the ‘Stay Dead’ a Figure Four Neck Lock…The move causes a very quick tap from Deluca who seemed to have lost all will to fight already. JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…CHRISTIAN RIVERS!” RILEY STEVENS: “Have you ever seen that many submissions in such a short time?” There is no answer but as we admire Rivers he gets to his feet as Deluca rolls out of the ring hurting…everywhere. Christian gets up holding up an arm in the air, knowing he’s got a long ways to go in this tournament but this was good for phase one as he makes quick work of Deluca making a huge statement. WINNER: CHRISTIAN RIVERS As we head to the back we find Katya Rose icing her neck from her match earlier as Trent Lost walks back and forth, pacing as he thinks about something. TRENT LOST: “You know I like being the ‘GIW North Beach Champion’ and all but that’s not enough. I want all the gold, I want everything!” Katya seems to be listening but seems in no shape to really respond as Lost walks around imaging it in his head. TRENT LOST: “Maybe I’ll get the job done and take out Damien Dunn? Or maybe after tonight we could go get those Tag Team titles. Or perhaps after I beat Cross I can get my rightful shot at the Undisputed title…” Trent grins as he nods his head… TRENT LOST: “That’s what I’ll do…I’ll make sure Cross doesn’t make it out of here tonight.” Lost seems to have some bad thoughts in his head as he turns and sits with Rose as we head to the ring. He walks out with a grin on his face stopping at the bottom of the ramp as "Upload Complete" by Last Chance to Reason hits and out from the back comes Nick Starks heading down the ramp with a big smirk on his face meeting up with Galen at the bottom looking at him and shaking his hand. RILEY STEVENS: “When you talk about making a quick impact you talk about what these two men did last week. Both with wins on Full Throttle then joining sides with Damien Dunn and quickly making enemies with Aspen Chaud and Alex Castellanos…” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “They are great, that’s all I have to say.” Galen and Starks together head up into the ring stepping in and taunting the crowd as "Suffer" By Shawn McPherson, with "The Shadow Knows" Radio Drama Introduction now starts up as out from the back wielding his half of the tag titles as the fans boo him, hating the way he got it. He heads up onto the apron then into the ring as he looks across at Galen and Starks who are plotting. “Dawn Of A Million Souls” by Ayreon now echoes out and finally a huge reaction comes out as here comes Matt Kluge with his tag title as well looking focused and none to pleased. RILEY STEVENS: “Galen and Starks will be tested with Shadow and Kluge but after all their wars in the past, can the two even work together?” Kluge heads up the steps into the ring, his fans cheering him on as he looks at Shadow handing over his tag belt to the referee as does Shadow, he hands both out then turning and calling for the bell. DING DING DING! As we get it going The Shadow angrily demands that Kluge get out of the ring which he does probably just so he doesn’t have to argue. Nick Starks steps into the ring for the other team being the ever so slightly bigger of the two. He starts to circle around The Shadow who stares back at him through his mask both going in for a lock up with Shadow basically throwing Starks back towards the corner. The fans are really only interested in Kluge winning this match as they seem sort of unwilling to react to the two men in the ring. Starks gets up angrily rushing back at him but this time Shadow pushes him back into the corner then showing his athleticism he runs at him crushing him in the corner right next to Kluge. Nick slumps down as Shadow looks at him Matt with a big grin on his face, taunting his own tag team partner. Grabbing Nick up to his feet he throws him off the ropes and waits for him to come back when he does he crushes him with a big clothesline. Quickly Starks bounces up however but does so right into the hand of Shadow who grabs him around the throat. The crowd boos him as he looks over at Kluge saying something to him then going for the Chokeslam. The delay allowed Nick to get his wits about him as he slaps his arm getting free then jumping up and Raking his Eyes through the mask! The fans boo these devious acts as he follows it up with a dropkick to his knee then bouncing off the ropes and catching him with a big clothesline! RILEY STEVENS: “The Shadow’s taunting of Kluge may have cost him the momentum.” The fans boo as Starks gets up throwing out both arms taunting them as he soaks in taking the big man down. Shadow starts to get up however as Nick stalks him from behind as he tries to wrap up the big man for a German Suplex. Immediately he realizes he can’t so he ducks the incoming Shadow elbow then jumping up and hitting a Double Knee Backbreaker! The fans boo as Starks goes for a cover 1…2…Kick Out! Getting up to his feet he looks over at Galen wanting a tag as he pulls Shadow up to his feet whipping him into the corner. He rushes over at him and hits him with a roaring elbow then starts to choke him with a leg the referee counts 1…2…3…4…Starks backs up the referee following to scold him, this leaves Galen open to take a cheap shot at Shadow while in the corner. Starks follows it up with a big shot at Shadow before making the tag into Galen. Sean steps into the ring and the two push him off the corner then lift him up able to do it together dropping him back with a big Double Suplex! The fans boo, wanting Kluge in the match. Sean walks around Shadow looking just as confident as he throws out his arms the fans quickly beginning to hate these two cocky individuals. Grabbing him he wraps up his legs into the Sharpshooter! The fans boo as Shadow crawls forward grabbing onto the ropes. Galen reaches up and tags in Starks as he heads in and both men grab him whipping him off the ropes, as he comes back out of no where Shadow crushes both men with Clothesline which brings a pop from the crowd whom want Kluge. RILEY STEVENS: “Shadow needs to tag out here!” The fans are going wild as Shadow realizes he needs to get out of here crawling towards his corner as Starks and Galen get to their feet as well but too late as they do Shadow tags Kluge! They go insane as he steps into the ring rushing in and clotheslining down Galen! Starks wants some to rushing him but going right into another clothesline. Sean the not legal man gets back up throwing a wild punch but Matt ducks it and wraps him up hitting a big German Suplex. Galen rolls out of the ring in pain as Starks gets up as well stumbling back right into Kluge who does his patented ‘The Matt Cooke’ a Running Knee to Opponent's Knee! The fans go wild as Starks falls to the mat favoring his knee. Matt screams out at them clearly pumped up right about now. Shadow has rolled out of the ring to recover on the outside. Kluge waits for Nick to get up, he does so very slowly. Turning around he continues to hold his knee which makes the ‘Hortonville Suplex Special 1’ a Suplex rolled into a Mounted Punching an easy move for Matt who pounds away at Starks. Nick pushes him off and gets to his feet; Kluge throws a big punch that lands flush with Starks face. Nick stumbles away holding his eye claiming it was a poke. The referee momentarily steps in Matt’s path, when Kluge pushes him out of the way Starks jumps forward dropkicking his knee! The fans boo as Matt falls to a knee as Nick makes the tag to Galen! Both men fight to their feet but it’s Galen with a blatant low blow followed up by a quick rotation into a Inverted Twist of Fate that he calls ‘The Equalizer’ Shadow bails out as the referee comes back making the cover 1…2…3! JAMAL JACKSON: “Here are your winners…NICK STARKS AND SEAN GALEN!” The fans let out furious boos mostly towards The Shadow who backs up the ramp looking none to worried about his partner as Starks and Galen get up yelling at the referee to raise their arms. WINNERS: NICK STARKS & SEAN GALEN Heading to the back we have Ace Baldwin looking angry in the backstage area as Zachary Pagnosis walks by him with a microphone. ZACHARY PAGONIS: “Maybe you should bring it up with management...” Ace chuckles to himself shaking his head quickly. ACE BALDWIN: “No thank you. Here’s what I want you to do…Since apparently they forgot about me…Go and round up some of the guys that lost earlier tonight…Tell them next week I want all of them to come down and meet me in the ring…So that I can show GIW why I should be the one Main Eventing…Blackout…Run along!” Ace pushes Pagnosis away clearly upset with how the night’s gone as Pagnosis runs off to do as he says as we continue along. QUOTE “Lift Me Up” by Moby played into the arena as Mark Cross made his way to the aisle with a considerable pop from the crowd around them for Thursday Night Revolt. He prepped by the ropes and awaited his opponent, but in the meantime his song continued to play and the crowd pandered before simmering when his opponent finally arrived. RILEY STEVENS: “This man is on his way to facing Alioth Starre at ‘Blackout II’ what better tune up then Trent Lost the ‘GIW North Beach Champion’.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “If Lost wins, can we please him a shot?” His opponent was Trent Lost. He stepped through the entry way and tried to pander to crowd, but even though his pop was loud, it wasn’t as much as the first entrant into the arena, Mark Cross. He entered the ring quickly. DING DING DING! The referee signaled for the bout to start and with the ring of the bell, Lost charged into a snapmare suplex, provided gladly by Mark Cross.
