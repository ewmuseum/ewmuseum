RILEY STEVENS: “She nearly tapped there.” Aspen looking battered now crawls to the ropes in pain as Kluge looks ready and willing to finish this one as she walks towards him trying to fight still as he lifts her up for a Suplex only for Aspen to end up reversing the move in mid-air falling down onto his back into what she calls the ‘Champion Lock’ a Standing Dragon Sleeper w/ Body Scissors as the fans go wild hoping to see Kluge go out as she tugs on the hold stretching out Kluge’s neck as her legs keep him from breaking the hold as she holds it in for a bit until he falls to one knee trying to break the hold still as she pulls her hardest on it, tugging it backwards until Matt breaks her legs apart then with no leverage is easily able to pull apart her arms as he she loses grip and falls backward to the mat, both unsuccessful in their champion locks. RILEY STEVENS: “Those two moves have to have drained a lot out of both of them.” Both of them now get to their feet a whole lot slower now as Aspen gambles going for a ‘Queen's Wrath II’ a Heel Kick but the kick misses as Matt ducks under it then grabs her with a Side Belly to Belly Suplex that tosses her across the ring as Matt heads up to the top rope in a very rare occurrence here as Chaud gets up slowly to her feet as Matt puts his body on the line to successfully hit her with a big Missile Dropkick sending her back down into the corner of the ring as the fans have begun to get to their feet waiting for the finish one way or another as Aspen gets up to her feet again seeming to walk right into ‘The Tiger's Roar Gold Edition’ a Whale Hunt but as he puts her on his back she slides down off the back landing on her feet and landing with a ‘Queen's Wrath I’ a Double Knee Backbreaker that has finished several people before as she hops on top for the cover 1…2…Kick Out! These fans react regardless, as Aspen looks up at the referee in anger thinking it’s his fault. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Lock in the K.M.A.” These fans are ready for it as well as Aspen immediately goes to it grabbing his Heel trying to get it locked in only for Kluge to kick up knocking her back and this time as she runs at him she runs right into ‘The Tiger's Roar Gold Edition’ a Whale Hunt that connects to Aspen but as he spins the referee is too close and he takes the shot with force knocking him down her legs catching him in the head as Matt looks up at him not stopping on his behalf as he mounts Aspen and begins to punch away at her delivering some very stiff looking shots as Aspen is so out of it she can’t defend herself just as expected Alexis Terry heads out from the back causing Kluge to drop her as we get a shot of her face, one eye already swelling up with her nose now beginning to bleed. RILEY STEVENS: “Of course here comes Alexis!” Wielding a steel chair in hand Alexis jumps up onto the ring apron with it as Kluge invites her into the ring as we hear ruffling on the announce table as into the ring unnoticed by Kluge is Jacob O’Connelly with a chair in hand and Matt doesn’t realize it instead taking a knockout blow to the back of the head as O’Connelly delivers a chair shot with a huge thud as Matt falls to the mat and O’Connelly exits the ring as Alexis rushes over to Aspen the chair shot hitting him hard enough to put his lights out. RILEY STEVENS: “What the hell was that!” Even these fans don’t seem to like it as many of them begin to boo as Alexis lays Aspen on top of Kluge, Chaud seeming to be oblivious as the referee comes to and just the right moment as he rolls over and makes the count as the crowd even begins to hope Kluge will regain consciousness in time 1……2…..3! But he doesn’t as O’Connelly grabs a mic immediately getting his revenge on Kluge as he starts to announce. JACOB O’CONNELLY: “Here is your winner and STILL GIW Undisputed Champion…ASPEN CHAUD!” These fans who went in mostly on Aspen’s side even seem unhappy with the ending as Aspen just lays there on top of Kluge, his punches seeming to have done serious damage to her as Alexis slides back into the ring as O’Connelly re-enters with the title as well handing it to Aspen only for her to drop it, unable to really do anything at this point. RILEY STEVENS: “I mean David Sinclair sends Jacob O’Connelly out here to be an announcer and they do that? It seemed like they had it planned the whole time to me, this to me was just another Aspen screw job.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “A win is a win, she is still champion.” Again being helped up by Alexis and Jacob we get a glimpse of her face and yet again she doesn’t look like a victor her eye now completely swollen shut and her nose bleeding pretty badly as this celebration is basically for O’Connelly as Kluge rolls to the corner of the ring coming to holding the back of his head, having a small cut as he is even getting a few claps from the crowd as he has to be wondering what exactly happened just a few moments ago. RILEY STEVENS: “You know we go from that classic match between Starre and Kluge to this type of ending tonight it makes me sick.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Shut up Riley.” RILEY STEVENS: “Well I guess that’s all the time we have for you folks here in London. Alexis Terry and Henry Malkin, both from England walked in as champions but lost their belts to Hayden Carter and James Dean respectively. Ryan Cavallari won the Controlled Chaos match, Alioth Starre and Alex Castellanos returned, Daniel Starr defeated Paul Defias, and sadly Aspen Chaud is still champion. See you next Monday as we start a tournament to decide the next contender, hopefully someone who can dethrone her.” While there is confetti pouring out from the rafters now in the colors of the United Kingdom flag Alexis and Jacob continue to help her out of the ring to get some medical attention, as Kluge gets to his feet in the corner looking around his head hurting as we get one last glimpse of Aspen with her belt held by Alexis as they take her to the back, our champion. WINNER: ASPEN CHAUD (Still GIW Undisputed Champion)
