Revolt, 28 May 2012

GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: MONDAY NIGHT REVOLT LIVE! From Cox Convention Center in Oklahoma City, OK – May 28, 2012 BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! Panning around the Cox Convention Center in Oklahoma City, the ION TV cameras catch a glimpse at GIW fans going wild for iconic three booms of Revolt! Smoke from the pyro fills the area, an aroma and sight that the fans have been waiting for for weeks and weeks since these sold out tickets went on sale. RILEY STEVENS: “Welcome to Galveston Island Wrestling folks – we are live from Oklahoma City, Oklahoma and we are one week away from what will be the biggest iPPV of the year, Aggressive Expansion. I'm Riley Stevens alongside my color commentators Oliver Thibodaux and Jacob O'Connelly; now Jacob, we'll start with you – to kick off the show tonight we have an open challenge from the Aggressive Expansion poster boy, Daniel Starr. We don't know exactly who will show to participate, but we can inquire about Starr's status with the company. Last week we saw him on Evolve complaining about a lack of respect being shown to him by the GIW Chairman, David Sinclair, as well as superstars in the back. Do you think there is any substance to this claim?” JACOB O’CONNELLY: “Riley, let me put it to you this way – Daniel Starr is a diamond in the rough. This guy is oil just waiting to be drilled – gold waiting to be mined! He is such an extraordinary talent, and obviously Sinclair knows it by putting him on the Aggressive Expansion poster – all this kid needs is time. Time and title shots.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Well, Jacob, if he is that weak to where he can't take having to deliver a promo on Evolve once in a while... fuck him! He can back his bags and get out. If he wants respect, he is going to have to earn it. He has to win high profile matches and make a name for himself to be successful around here.” RILEY STEVENS: “One man who is truly making a name for himself in this company is Lokie, who lost his match to Aspen and Alexis last week, but that is nothing to scoff at. He has been extremely impressive thus-far in GIW. Tonight, he will compete against Piotr Czarneki, a European import and winner of the Evolve battle royal next week over AJ Fairchild, who will compete in the other beat the clock match. Jacob, who will have the best overall time in these two matches – advancing to face Alexis Terry at the iPPV?” JACOB O’CONNELLY: “Piotr Czarneki is a solid choice for me, seeing as how I was the one who traveled to Poland and found him. I really am a great talent scout you know. Czarneki is the next big GIW star!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Not quite Irishman. Lokie is a hoss. The guy will steamroll right through that little twerp, and he will steamroll right through Alexis and take that title! Believe it!” “Have a Cigar” by Pink Floyd caught the fans off guard to start to the show, and out comes David Sinclair, black suit and all, waving to the crowd with a microphone in hand. He begins to speak to the audience as he makes his way down to the ring, climbing up the steps and bending in. DAVID SINCLAIR: “Ah, look at this building. Smack dab in the middle of Oklahoma – hundreds of miles from Galveston Island, and yet here we are. Jam packed like sardines up to the ceiling, fans going stark raving mad at the beginning of every show when the BOOM BOOM BOOM booms every time. Then the music goes on, sending chills up each and every one of your hairy little spines – and you what? I did it. I made all of this folks, love me or hate me, I did. I get a lot of traditionalists coming to me and saying, 'David, you ruined this company. I miss Blake. Blah.' Well, to them I say, look around you. When I hit Blake Beckett with a car, ran him over, I didn't just give myself a promotion. I didn't just make sure that I would be the man in charge, I single handedly freed this company from the iron grip of a benevolent dictator. He didn't care about you fans. He didn't care about the wrestlers in the back – all he cared about was maintaining his image so that when he left the company he twisted all the life out of, he would be able to move on to bigger and better things, and increase his pocket book. Blake is a business man. He doesn't like wrestling – I like wrestling. I love wrestling. And I know how hard it is to be a wrestler, that is why I have been building this company up and Aggressively Expanding to different regions throughout the southwest. You can catch us on ION from Galveston to New Orleans. From New Orleans to Nebraska. From Nebraska to Santa Fe. From Santa Fe to Cancun, and all the way back to Galveston. This is a southern phenomenon, yet you all hate me? You all hate for paying your favorite wrestlers more money? For paying designers more money so we can have better posters, t-shirts, and a look that doesn't look like backyard indy fed? Let me break it to you all – Beckett founded this company on the idea that indy is OK. That having Paul Defias vs. Joshua Acquin headlining the gym at the school was OK – but it isn't. It isn't and I honestly believe that. And folks like Alex Castellanos – guys that are constantly complaining about every move I make, every time I change anything – these ignorant, simple minded people like Alex Castellanos have the nerve to tell me that free enterprise is WRONG? That growing and developing is wrong? He spoke to me last week like I was ruining his career, like I was damaging his integrity? Well, the fact of the matter is I'm only helping him out by making sure his workplace will withstand a downfall in tourism for more than two weeks. So, I'd like to call Mr. Castellanos out here to personally apologize to me for hurting my feelings first, and second, for undermining my authority, and third for slandering the crystal clear reputation of this company.” “Did It On 'Em” by Nicki Minaj hit the speakers, and the fans were extremely disappointed to see Aspen walk to the ring in her gear. She was smiling though, enjoying it. ASPEN CHAUD: “I don't think he is here, David. Alex Castellanos needing time off, no showing a company event? What else is new, I'm sure you were expecting it. I know I was. But David, I just wanted to come on out here and say a few things about my opponent at ‘Aggressive Expansion’. I'm sure that's okay. Regardless, Alex, I just want to make something clear to you. I am better. I know. It is shocking to you, isn't it, oh mediocre one. Honestly, I've defeated you so many times, and it isn't luck Alex. I know that deep in your head you have convinced yourself that I have to cheat to beat you – and I do have to cheat to beat a lot of people, but not for you Alex. If I ever left this company, what would happen? It would die, yes, I know. It’s a horrible thing to imagine isn't it. I'm that important to this company. I am that vital to everything. Now, what if you left? The thing is, Alex, that is not a hypothetical question. You leave all the time! Oh, you need some time off to clear your head, want to go work things out with your girlfriend who hates you, need to recover from an injury, the excuses are endless. You leave these fans. You abandon them! YOU ABANDON THEM ALL! Look at them, little helpless people – all they need is their Alex to guide them, but you left. Numerous times you left them, and you didn't even say goodbye. Alex, at Aggressive Expansion I will be the messenger of the people. Aspen Chaud! The voice of the silent! The Queen of the peasants. That is the only reason I'll fight you again Alex, for the people. God knows I have nothing else to gain from beating you senseless again.” “Wayward One” by Alterbridge is barely audible over the screams of the fans who erupt when Castellanos makes his way to the ring, talking on the way down. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “I don't know who died and crowned you two as the center of attention, but these weekly meetings at the beginning of each and every show are really starting to piss me off. Aspen, you do cheat. You cheat each and every time you throw that belt over your shoulder because you know you don't deserve it. You act like you are so vital to this company – without you it'll be dead, you say, but I disagree. GIW doesn't need you. One person doesn't make or break a company; it’s the fans – the multitudes of people that keep it afloat. You say you carry the company when I leave, but that isn't true either. If you left too, there would be someone right behind you willing to work and take your place because that is what GIW is. It is a community above all else. An idea that people can behind – the idea that pure wrestling is ideal, that pure wrestling is still relevant today. Not bastardized bullshit like weddings and touring Oklahoma. Sinclair, you've slowly ruined it. GIW was an oasis in the wrestling world, then you toured to Oklahoma. Then you manipulated the system to get the belts on your three stooges, and now look at this? You have an upheaval on your hands. People are sick and tired of you slowly but surely ripping this company apart thread by fucking thread. Maybe I'm wasting my time even talking to you. I should talk to Aspen, seeing as she is the one who really controls you. She pulls the same game on every single person. Every man in the history of time has fallen for the same exact trap you are locked in to, but you don't realize it? You don't run this company, she does! But she doesn't want the credit, it looks bad on her, to run the company and have the belt – all her friends with belts, her enemies constantly being undermined. She uses you. She tells you want to do, and I'm sick of it. Nothing is ever going to get fixed unless I am the one to fix it. You two are to blame for all of this, so that is why I am going to defeat you at the SUPERSHOW, because with one pin fall, all of your credibility will be lost, and slowly you'll disappear.” The scene slowly begins to fade as Alex's music rolls with Aspen whispering into Sinclair's ear.
