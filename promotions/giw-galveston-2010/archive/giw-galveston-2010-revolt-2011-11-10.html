GIW — Card

Revolt, 10 November 2011

10 November 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: THURSDAY NIGHT REVOLT LIVE! From Mario’s Bar and Grill in Galveston – November 10, 2011 BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! The show starts and after a long time Revolt has finally returned back to where it belongs right here on Galveston Island. The show has just started and the fans here in Galveston seem to have missed it as they are going wild with cheers as the camera pans around them before finally ending up here on the announce team of Oliver and Riley. RILEY STEVENS: “Welcome back ladies and gentleman, I speak for everyone when I say it’s good to be back here in Galveston at Mario’s Bar and Grill familiar territory. We return with some big time action and the three headline bouts tonight are between ‘GIW Televison Champion’ Austin Acquin and Tony Miranda…Aspen Chaud versus Nero and last but not least Draven and Johnny Violence, go ahead and tell them what the prize is Oliver.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Simple, the winners all get to lose to Ace.” RILEY STEVENS: “Oliver the odds do not favor Ace when he’s in Fatal Four Way like that defending his belt. But with how impressive he’s looked he’s got just as good of a chance as anyone. Who will join him in that four way? We’ll see tonight.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “More importantly when those matches are finally done with, we’ll have a CELEBRATION! Tonight it’ll be Ace Baldwin’s official coronation as champion!” RILEY STEVENS: “…The fans will surely love it, regardless lets get on with tonight’s action after this quick commercial.” With that we take the first commercial of the night. QUOTE As we return from break the ring is already full with Analise Meyers and Justin Impact both stretching out when “Bodies” by Saliva hits and the fans look up at the top of the ramp as here comes Brody Harper heading towards the ring, one of the most menacing men in the GIW in terms of looks and power. RILEY STEVENS: “You know Brody was a late addition to the match and though he doesn’t have the best win percentage here in GIW, he may just be one of the biggest guys here.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “McDonald’s will do that to you.” Harper enters the ring, looking around at his opponents both staring back at him with a look of fear in their eyes as the referee stands in between them then calls for the bell. DING DING DING! With that the match starts and Impact tries to make the first move rushing at Brody only to be clotheslined to the mat hard. The fans here in GIW love Brody Harper for the way he always brings the fight. Justin hops back up this time he runs right into big shoulder block knocking him to the ground. Analise now timid the whole time in the corner rushes out throwing a punch at him. But Brody ducks it and then pulls her down onto his knee; the crowd goes wild as the reckless Harper looks around at them grinning before proceeding to spank her repeatedly getting a kick from the male audience. Finally Impact breaks it up, pulling him away but he gets a big DDT for his troubles. Now comes the long winded “Brodyyyyyyyyyyy” chants from the crowd, only further making him look like a bad dude. From there Harper stares down at him but Meyers gets up rushing off the ropes going for a Lou Thesz! But she gets caught mid-air as Brody hoists her up on his shoulders then drops her hard with a Powerbomb right on top of Impact. Covering both of their battered bodies at once Harper gets the cover. JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…Brody Harper!” The crowd is going wild for their fan favorite Brody Harper who just destroyed these two underachieving stars as he heads up to the top rope posing. RILEY STEVENS: “Got to give it Brody he always comes to fight and he might have just ended two GIW careers tonight.” Harper jumps down then heads to the other turnbuckle posing again as the crowd again lets out a big cheer as we head to the back. WINNER: BRODY HARPER From there we head to the back where Aspen Chaud is just arriving to the arena running a tad bit late but as soon as she enters her locker room, she is met by the waiting face of Ace Baldwin leaning against the wall with a paper in hand. ACE BALDWIN: “Was beginning to think you weren’t coming.” Ace gives that cocky chuckle as Aspen rolls her eyes angrily placing a hand on her hip as she responds. ASPEN CHAUD: “Not before I take that belt of yours.” Baldwin just laughs as he readjusts it on his shoulder, not because he needed to but just to show off. ACE BALDWIN: “Enough with the jokes, I came here for a reason. You see later tonight we are having a title celebration for my title victory at GIW. I went to the Board of Directors and they agreed that everyone on the roster would have to come and watch from ringside.” Ace shines in his own glory as he hands over the envelope over to her, she takes it with an annoyed look on her face. ACE BALDWIN: “But I wanted to give you your invite personally, after all I am sure you are going to want to be there. See you later…” Ace smiles and walks off, waving at her as he goes as she takes the envelope and throws it towards the trash as we continue on. ==== Else where backstage Austin is waiting to go on out to the ring holding his belt over his shoulder with Joshua Acquin standing a few paces behind him. JOSHUA ACQUIN: “Listen you can beat this guy, just don’t get into a striking match with him it won’t work. Keep him grounded and you can win this fight, don’t let him use those feet of his.” Like a Coach he lists off ideas finally Austin whips around just as Josh finishes up. AUSTIN ACQUIN: “I am fine! Ok bro; don’t need you coaching me through everything. Let’s go.” Austin heads out towards the ring as Joshua looks at him a bit shocked before following him up as take a break. QUOTE That familiar feeling of a big time match is back in the air as the fans here in the Bar and Grill seem excited. "Nightmare" by Avenged Sevenfold the old theme of Joshua Acquin and the new one for Austin hits as the fans begin to boo the entrance of a man they hate so much, as both Austin and Josh walk out Austin wielding his belt. RILEY STEVENS: “You know Austin has looked great against lesser competition but can he handle this huge step up?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Of course he can.” Austin heads toward the ring scowling at the fans as he heads up into the ring as Joshua walks around it heading over to his corner as his music cuts off and "World Warrior" by RedRapper hits sending the fans into a frenzy as out comes Tony Miranda fresh off a win and looking ready to earn his shot at the gold here tonight. RILEY STEVENS: “Fresh on the heels of a victory over Aspen, Tony who has never held the top belt here looks to be the first to qualify for the Four Way.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I hate sob stories like that.” Tony high fives a few fans but still looks focused all the way down to the ring, looking across at Austin who hands his belt out to his brother as the referee comes between the two and calls for the bell. DING DING DING! This thing is under way and simply put we all know who everyone is rooting for, it’s Tony Miranda. Not a fan in the building is on Austin’s side as the two circle and then go in for a lock up. But Tony chucks him to the side; just too perhaps send a message to Acquin. It only seems to anger him as he rushes at him going for a big punch but Tony ducks under it. Austin spins around, angered by Tony’s action so he loses his game plan. When he turns around Miranda goes for an early Spinning Roundhouse Kick this time its Austin that ducks under it. The fans boo him as Austin grabs him from behind and drops him with a Falling Neckbreaker. From here he hoists Tony back up smart enough to know that one move is not going to put away Tony. Putting him in position for a DDT but as he goes for it Miranda pulls out his head, but Austin is still a step ahead as he heads forward decapitating him with a Clothesline any how. Now he drops down on top of him trying to put him away, 1…Kick Out. Still not even close to putting away Tony at this point. RILEY STEVENS: “Austin is looking good early on, but can he keep it up?” Perhaps always the problem for Austin, his anger now seems to start to get the best of him as he hoists up Tony quickly. Trying for a Floating DDT but again Tony breaks free and grabs the back of his head and hits a Muay Thai Knee. The fans start to come back alive after being shut down earlier. Not releasing his head he delivers another both seeming to make Austin lose his footing and knowing that Tony grabs him and nails him with a quick Snap Suplex. The fans go wild as he hops up to his feet a rejuvenated man as he stalks him from behind, lining up for something big. Finally when he turns around boom, a Spinning Roundhouse Kick to the face that sends the fans into an eruption as he crawls on top and makes the cover 1…Kick Out! Still not close to putting away the Television Champion as the fans stand shocked he kicked out at one of all things. Now hoisting him up knowing he can’t give Austin any time to recuperate, but as he lifts him up Austin rakes his eyes which is completely missed by the referee and as Miranda backs up holding his eyes Acquin runs across the ring hitting him with the Spear then the cover 1…2-Kick Out! RILEY STEVENS: “Cheap eye rake leads only to a two count in this one.” Tony gets up holding his gut, perhaps losing his breath with this one. Austin walks to the corner starting to pull the padding off, he loosens it and the referee comes over to stop him. He pushes him away and then starts to tie it back up. All while this happening Austin grabs Tony from behind wrapping up his arms and pulling him towards the side of the ring where Josh with the Television belt now stands, when he gets him there Joshua swings it but at the last second Tony ducks it and he hits Austin! The fans go wild as Tony heads off the ropes bouncing off them and then with Joshua not having enough time to run he hits him with the ‘Third Strike’ a Running Leg Lariat that sends him flying off the ropes. The referee turns around just as Miranda kicks him off. Austin gets up to his feet, wobbly feet as Tony grabs a hold of him and hoists him up and puts him away with the ‘Second Impact’ a Running Lygerbomb, the fans going berserk as he makes the cover they count along with him 1…2…3! JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…Tony Miranda!” The fans go wild as Tony immediately rolls out of the ring throwing his arms into the air celebrating the victory backing up into the fans who all lean forward trying to get a piece of their guy. RILEY STEVENS: “There we have it, the Acquin’s plans falls apart and now we know that Tony Miranda will have a shot at the ‘GIW Undisputed Championship’ a well deserved one at that.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Ace is going to crush him.” Back in the ring Joshua returns helping Austin up to his feet, however he pushes him away and then slides out of the ring heading to the back on his own as we take a break. WINNER: TONY MIRANDA After the match we head backstage just when Tony Miranda is heading through the curtains where waiting for him clapping his hands is Johnny Violence. JOHNNY VIOLENCE: “Bravo…bravo. You’ve got your shot at the belt you wanted so badly. The only bad thing is you are going to share that ring with three other people.” Tony looks at him clearly agitated by him as Violence shakes his head grinning. JOHNNY VIOLENCE: “Must just get on your nerves that Aspen’s getting another shot doesn’t it? In a few minutes she has a match to get into that Four Way as well, and you already beat her…” Tony listens but he does so clearly restraining himself still tired from the match. JOHNNY VIOLENCE: “I mean what does that say about what GIW thinks of you? They don’t think that they can sell an Ace Baldwin versus Tony Miranda fight. So they instead basically give her a free ride into it and throw me in there as well. What lack of trust do they have in you that even after you beat Aspen, she is still getting another shot in your match? Must get annoying be in her shadow all the time.” Violence shrugs his shoulders clearing trying to get into the head of Tony, however his straight faced demeanor doesn’t really give us any clues as to what way he is leaning. JOHNNY VIOLENCE: “Let’s face it man…Nobody around here thinks you are champion material…Nobody.” With that Johnny pats him on the shoulder smirking as he walks on his way, as Tony does his best not to go ahead and kick his face off as he watches him go, still not knowing what his thoughts on it are. ==== Again in a different area backstage there is a difference scene as Austin is seen throwing a chair into the wall, Joshua heads into the scene trying to calm him down. JOSHUA ACQUIN: “Austin, calm down.” Josh grabs a hold of him trying to keep him contained but Austin throws him off angrily. AUSTIN ACQUIN: “Get the hell off of me you idiot. You cost me that match! Without you I would be a million times better. They always say I am in your shadow? The only reason I am is because YOU are holding me back!” Perhaps no time are you more honest then when your angry and it seems to strike home with Josh who seems taken back as Austin breathes heavily, but coming into the scene is the always spunky Amanda Rowland stepping between the two. AMANDA ROWLAND: “Guys, guys calm down. You are like, brothers you can’t be arguing like this.” Austin looks at her, and his words make her turn to towards him. AUSTIN ACQUIN: “Shut up you stupid bimbo, this isn’t your battle. So, before you make the rest of us any more stupid go ahead and leave.” The younger Acquin points the other way as Amanda stands there shocked before Austin eventually angrily walks off as Josh stands next to her. JOSHUA ACQUIN: “I’m sorry about him, he can be a jerk when he is angry…Let’s get a bite to eat and let him cool down.” Amanda nods her head as the two head down the hallway with one another, with the Acquin brother’s relationship seemingly rocky. QUOTE As soon as we are back in the ring "Bullseye" by Aly and AJ hits and the crowd goes wild with applause as Aspen Chaud heads out from the back looking as excited as usual as she walks towards the ring high fiving the fans along the way, all of them cheering her on. RILEY STEVENS: “You know Aspen lost on Halloween Night but many still feel she deserves another crack at the belt due to the controversy surrounding that match.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “What controversy? She got pinned.” Aspen heads into the ring, looking around and blowing the crowd a kiss as she turns to wait for her opponent. “Hurricane” by 30 Seconds to Mars hits and the fans cheer again this time for the masked man Nero as they wait for his arrival…That waits ends up being longer then expected. RILEY STEVENS: “Where in the world is he?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Maybe he lost his mask…Haha.” The fans here look around confused thinking maybe it’s a mix up as even Aspen looks at the referee wondering what is going on. RILEY STEVENS: “You know if he doesn’t show up the referee will have to start the match regardless.” Just as he says that the referee calls for the bell. DING DING DING! Aspen looks around confused asks the referee what is going on as he proceeds to start to count Nero out. 1…2…Aspen in the ring looks around just as confused as the people here in the building here tonight…3…4…5…6…Everyone has their eyes on the entrance area waiting for the inevitable Nero arrival but still nothing…7…8…9…One last big gasp as the referee waits a tad longer and then calls it…10! JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…Aspen Chaud?” In the ring everyone here seems confused as the referee raises Aspen’s hand in the air but it’s not a win she seems happy with. RILEY STEVENS: “I suppose that means she qualified for the Four Way?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Wow…That seems fair.” Aspen shakes her head no not exactly seeming willing to just happily take this marching her way to the back to get explanations for what just happened here in the ring. WINNER: ASPEN CHAUD After an essential no show in the last match we head backstage and once there we see Aspen Chaud roaming the halls looking for someone finally she sees Nero standing in the hall way looking around. ASPEN CHAUD: “Where the heck were you? We were supposed to have a match…You know like just a second ago?” Nero stares back at her but the mask gives no real sense of emotion as he continues to look around. ASPEN CHAUD: “Who the heck are you looking for?” Aspen waits for an answer but it does not come instead Nero continues to look around the halls. ASPEN CHAUD: “God, this place is so weird.” Aspen finally just gives up walking away as Nero looks around still behind the mask, leaving us to wonder what he could possibly be looking for. QUOTE It is time for the Main Event of the night and the last of the ‘Final Solution’ qualifiers. "Battle Flag" by Lo-Fidelity All Stars hits and though he is relatively new the fans have already begun to like him as the cheers, while not quite yet at the adoring stage are still pretty loud for Draven as he heads out from the back walking towards the ring looking pumped up. RILEY STEVENS: “Aspen and Tony have punched their tickets could this rookie Draven be the third person in?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Yeah right he is being fed to the fishes.” Draven jumps over the ropes and into the ring heading to the top rope and posing as his music trails off and “Bat Country” by Avenged Sevenfold hits eliciting a loud and angry response from the card who show exactly what they think about this guy known as Johnny Violence. Heading out from the back he doesn’t seem to care at all, walking straight towards the ring accepting the jeers. RILEY STEVENS: “You’ve got to be a little in awe of this man after his last performance one in which he demolished AJ Fairchild.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Yup and in about, two minutes he’ll do the same to Draven.” Not seeming quite as focused, perhaps taking Draven lightly he enters the ring grinning across at Draven as he poses for the crowd which only makes them boo, he falls back towards his corner and the referee then calls for the bell. DING DING DING! This main event is a big one both guys are relatively new here in GIW but both have that enigma about them that gets people excited about what they can do. Violence circles around him talking some early trash as Draven does not let it get to him as they finally go in for a lock up. While Johnny is the more lanky fighter Draven has just a few pounds on him but the result is a relatively even lock up, however just as Draven starts to get the advantage Violence pulls him right into a knee to the gut. The fans seem to have enjoyed Draven’s first match here and are rooting for him. Johnny keeps a hold of him and whips him off the ropes. Draven hits them hard but his highflying abilities come into play as on the rebound he slides underneath Johnny and ends up on the outside of the ropes when he gets up. Angered by the random move Violence rushes at him only to get an elbow to the face. Draven then goes Springboard right into a Crossbody Press with a pin 1…Kick Out. Though it was a quick kick out the fans gave him a big cheer as he hit the move as Draven got to his feet nodding his head at the fans as he thinks about his next move. RILEY STEVENS: “This a classic brawler meets high flyer match up.” Draven waits for him to get to his feet once there he hoists him up going for a Suplex of some sort but Violence hooks his leg so he can’t get him up. Now he knees him again in the gut pushing him away at the same time. Getting Draven on his heels Violence rushes forward and hits him with the ‘Walk’ a Clothesline from Hell! The fans boo him as he takes Draven out with that one, rather then pinning him right away he treats him like he did AJ with little respect, walking around taunting him while he is down. Finally he decides to go for a pin but as he reaches down Draven pulls him into a Roll Up, 1…2…Kick Out! The fans go wild thinking it was three but come down when the referee holds up a two. Both men get up quickly but it’s Johnny who is up first rushing at him and hitting ‘The Beast’ a Spear that puts Draven back on his back. The fans boo Violence as he angrily kicks the ropes, thinking about how close that roll up was. But perhaps all it did was start up that angry side of Johnny as he hoists him up calling for the end, what he doesn’t see coming is a Japanese Arm Drag that sends him flipping right through the ropes. RILEY STEVENS: “Can Draven pull off the upset?” The fans love it as he rushes immediately off the ropes as Johnny gets to his feet on the outside but doesn’t look back in the ring when he finally does it is to late as here comes Draven down on top of him with a Somersault Flip landing right on him! There may be nothing these fans love more then a high flyer and its clear Draven has some fans here tonight….1…2…The count now starts as Draven tries to pull him up so he can throw him back in…3…4…He goes to throw him in but Johnny reverses it and whips him right into the Steel Post on the outside. The fans boo it as Draven bounces back and then immediately gets thrown into the ring, from there. Violence slides in and Draven seems to have lost all his wits as he has troubles just getting to his feet these troubles allow Johnny back into the ring as he grabs him and puts him right into the ‘Paranoid’ a Ranhei, it’s a beautiful move and it’s a winning move here 1…2…Kick Out! RILEY STEVENS: “Draven kicked out!” This sends the fans into a frenzy as they start to believe that he can make the come back. Violence gets up shocked just as well as everyone else as he asks the referee if it was three when he says no, he angrily screams something at him. But instead of complaining too much he turns around hoisting up Draven to put him away officially. Johnny tries to lift him up for the ‘Glory Fades’ but Draven is able to get out of it and ends up behind him for a Reverse Twist of Fate! The fans start to go wild as still a little stunned he heads up to the top rope, looking for his move. Everyone fan in the building is on their feet now as he goes for the ‘Hollywood Suicide’ a Shooting Star Press into a Leg Drop, while he is in the air it looks beautiful but as he makes his descent everyone watches as Johnny rolls out of the way at the last second. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “He moved.” Boom! Draven hits the mat hard, his lower back crushing the mat. Right away the fans know it’s over as he tries to get up to keep fighting but the pain is too much and he walks right into a ‘Glory Fades’ a Tigerbomb, with Violence on top making the pin 1…2…3! JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…Johnny Violence!” Violence rolls him off of him, looking tired after nearly losing that match if he hadn’t rolled out of the way regardless he gets up holding up an arm as he punches his ticket to a title shot. RILEY STEVENS: “Great match up between these two guys, Draven did well but in the end it is Johnny Violence that finds himself as the forth man.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Just as I expected.” Violence rolls out of the ring with that smirk as he motions that belt around his waist as we take a break before the ‘Ace Baldwin Celebration’. WINNER: JOHNNY VIOLENCE After that break it is time for what we have all been waiting for as the wrestlers have started to pile in from the back all heading towards the ring. Everyone is in attendance; Tony Miranda, Aspen Chaud, Johnny Violence even random crew members, the referee’s, and Road Agents like Izzie Dead have joined the fray. None of them seem all that happy to be there but standing in the ring with a mic is Jamal Jackson reading right off a prepared card. JAMAL JACKSON: “Ladies and gentleman, please rise and honor the greatest man to ever grace the GIW ring…The man who ended the reign of Alioth Starre…the man who single handedly made an entire roster look like nothing…the man who rose to the belt quicker then any before…please welcome the greatest star in GIW and your NEW ‘GIW Undisputed Champion’ ACE BALDWIN!” Jamal throws the card down and quickly exits the ring as the music by Matador hits and out from the back comes Ace Baldwin. In the ring the mat has now been turned into a red carpet looking thing, with balloons all over the place as he walks out wearing an expensive suit and wearing that belt over his shoulder. RILEY STEVENS: “Has there ever been a celebration quite like this for a new champion?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Nope, but that’s because we have never had a champion this great before.” Ace heads towards the ring with a big grin on his face waving to the crowd who unanimously boos him. Heading up the steps into the ring we see a lot of angry stares from around the ring, as he is handed a mic as he looks around at the people gathered around. ACE BALDWIN: “Give it up everyone, give it up for your new champion!” Ace waits for the applause but only gets boos, some coming from the stars around the ring. ACE BALDWIN: “Tonight is truly a night to celebrate after all I put an end to the reign of Alioth Starre! And in ushers the Age of Ace here in the GIW! A champion, you can all enjoy watching.” Baldwin holds up the title with a grin on his face as he pulls out a huge list of paper from his suit pocket. ACE BALDWIN: “I’d like to take this time to thank all the people that helped me get to where I am today. First me of course for doing all the hard work to become better then everyone else here in GIW…My parents for providing me with superior genetics and of course my many…many sponsors….Also---” With that the fans have already had enough as has Tony Miranda at ringside as he has grabbed a mic and now walks up the steps behind him. TONY MIRANDA: “Just shut the hell up already! You know when I win that belt; at ‘Final Resolution’ there will be no self thrown title party. I won’t come out here just to show it off, I’ll do what a champion is supposed to and that’s fight with it on the line.” Ace is clearly angry that someone would interrupt him as he spins around staring at him angrily. ACE BALDWIN: “Funny that you the guy that has no idea what being a champion is all about is giving me lessons on it.” Baldwin laughs at his own little joke as Tony stares back at him not finding it too funny at all. TONY MIRANDA: “I know more about fight then you ever will and when I win that belt, I am going to make sure it’s a reign nobody will forget unlike yours as a transitional champion.” This brings out loud applause from the crowd all in agreement as before Ace can speak it’s now open season as Johnny Violence heads up the steps into the ring with a mic of his own. JOHNNY VIOLENCE: “Blah, blah, blah. You people talk way too much, you see I come out here every week and have to listen to this crap over and over again. You know what the most annoying part is? Despite Tony’s history of choking under pressure, Ace’s essentially one his wonder, and Aspen’s continued decline I am still treated as the forth wheel in this match up. When I should be treated for what I really am and that’s the FAVORITE in this match.” Before the champ or Tony can respond another voice comes into play as Aspen now heads into the ring with a mic of her own. ASPEN CHAUD: “The favorite huh? Johnny I think it’s fair to say, not a single person in here thinks you have a chance winning. Now I don’t want to get too into depth about ‘Final Solution’ yet but in a Fatal Four Way match you need heart and that’ll lead me to a victory, to regain that belt.” From there its open season as Ace is the first to raise his mic to response. ACE BALDWIN: “When is the last time your heart helped you win anything? Matter of fact had that masked guy shown up you wouldn’t even be in this match.” Now things get really crazy as all four start to bicker over the microphones when suddenly they are cut as standing on the stage looking like the God of GIW the way he just shut things down in the ring, his mic now only the live one. BLAKE BECKETT: “Enough, enough, enough. Clearly this was not my idea, but regardless the Four Way was my idea. And that’s why I have made a couple plans for Revolt next week. You see first of all we have a tag match it will be the team of newly signed Altair and Draven taking on the team of Johnny Violence and Tony Miranda.” This gets the crowd going as Johnny and Tony look at one another shocked by the news before Violence begins to argue it but Blake does not stop there. BLAKE BECKETT: “That’s not all. Aspen regardless of if it was your fault or not, you didn’t get a chance to prove yourself. But I am going to do just that so everyone that is around the ring, be ready next week. Because our main event will be a Lumberjack match pitting Aspen Chaud versus Ace Baldwin, one on one!” That news makes the crowd go wild as Aspen nods her head as Ace grins a bit as well not seeming all that worried about it but out of no where Ace whips around grabbing her and tossing her over the ropes onto one side of the ring. Aspen lands on a few of the wrestlers as Tony now does the same grabbing Ace and hurling him over the other side onto some of the other guys and last but not least Violence grabs a hold of Tony and tosses him over the final side landing on all that is left. RILEY STEVENS: “Two huge matches next week. Violence and Miranda will be forced to team and Ace and Aspen duel in a Lumberjack match. Could this scene in the ring be the one we see at ‘Final Solution’?” Just like that Violence was the last man standing watching as those around the ring rolled around to their feet as the show slowly came to a close with Johnny leaving his mark as the final person standing.
