GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: REVOLT LIVE FROM GALVESTON ISD GYM ON JANUARY 27th, 2010 = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! With all the controversy the fans are all sitting at the edge of their seats, waiting as the show starts. Right away we come into a scene, seeming almost like a parent scolding two kids as Blake Beckett ‘GIW Chairman’ stands between La’Sarah Audric and Garrett Holloway. All three are holding microphones, but it’s Blake Beckett that speaks first. BLAKE BECKETT: “As anyone that’s tuning in already knows, over the last few weeks Revolt and Affliction have been at odds due to a series of attacks. I do have to admit that both sides put on a pretty convincing show. And while everyone else may have been fooled, I of course knew from the beginning. However, I think it’s a bit foolish to let it go on any longer. So, rather then take all the glory myself…why don’t the two of you share just who you sent to attack the victims.” Blake takes a step back as Garrett and La’Sarah share a brief stare down, as the fans in the arena wait to hear just who it’ll be. It’s La’Sarah that decides to speak up first. LA’SARAH AUDRIC: “You want to know who attacked Megathreat? That’s all too simple. What group of guys could do such a great job? You see while Riley Stevens is back tonight, my guys put yours out for the foreseeable future! Let’s just say I made them promises, they couldn’t refuse including representing us at ‘Exile’ in the GIW Tag Team Championship Main Event! Our attackers were Perfect Combination.” The fans boo, not at all encouraging the success of this much hated duo. La’Sarah has a big smirk on her face, letting Garrett soak in the fact that she had not one but two attackers, thinking she has trumped him. The fans suddenly begin to go a little wild, as suddenly A.J. Lightning and Justin Volt slide into the ring surrounding La’Sarah Audric. GARRETT HOLLOWAY: “Meet…my attackers…High Voltage. You see I knew full well, that these tag titlse need to be on the ‘A’ Show. So I gave them this them opportunity with full confidence that they’ll bring back the titles.” La’Sarah looks back and forth between Justin and A.J. who seem to be willing to attack, but it’s Garrett that clarifies why they are here. GARRETT HOLLOWAY: “Rather then make an example out of you. We heard there was a match that was an Open Invitational. So why don’t you pull up a seat and watch the best tag team in GIW make an example out of one of your wrestlers.” La’Sarah at this point, figures that it’s in her best interest to agree as Blake seems to admire the war that he’s just brought on. Before the match starts we are taken to a break with La’Sarah exiting the ring and High Voltage preparing to wrestle. QUOTE After a short break we return to the ring where A.J. Lightning and Justin Volt are deciding who will start while Bret Battery is trying to complain to La’Sarah Audric who is sitting at the announce table, basically ignoring him. RILEY STEVENS: “Well here we go; it’s time for the opening contest of the night.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Great…to have you back here.” RILEY STEVENS: “It’s good to be back.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I’m talking to La’Sarah.” LA’SARAH AUDRIC: “Shush you two, we got to see what we are dealing with.” Bret continues to try to argue that this isn’t fair as Garrett watches the match from ringside as Justin decides to start. DING DING DING! With Bret not paying attention, Justin runs across the ring crushing him in the back of the head with a big punch. The fans are not fans of Bret but there basically lack of care towards his alignment leads them to boo High Voltage. Bret flails around until he’s in the corner, where Justin smirks. Jumping up he connects with a roundhouse kick. The fans boo as Volt lifts him to his feet and whips him over into the corner with A.J. At ringside we can hear Garrett shouting instructions at them, though it appears they are showing little care for him. Justin tags in A.J. who steps in. Both members kick Bret in the mid-section then nail him with a DDT. The fans boo as Willy Morgan the referee surveys the match, from afar. Rather then immediately taking him out, Justin exits the ring. Bret gets up but falls right back into the corner. A.J. begins to talk with the referee allowing Justin to hit a cheap shot behind his back. Bret turns trying to throw a punch at Justin, he misses then turns walking right into A.J. who grabs him and lifts him up into the air, crushing him down with a Powerbomb! The fans boo realizing this is the end as A.J. tags in Justin, who heads top rope. He takes a look over at La’Sarah at ring side then finishes their finisher the ‘Amp it Up’ with a Frog Splash right into a pinning combination 1…2…3! That’s it. LA’SARAH AUDRIC: “It’s one guy! And not even one of our top twenty five.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “They beat him down like they did you Riley.” RILEY STEVENS: “I really wasn’t expecting any sympathy from you Oliver…I really wasn’t.” Garrett steps right back into the ring as La’Sarah steps out of her chair, looking into the ring at High Voltage. Holloway gets in between both of them and raises their arms up into the air, while La’Sarah holds up one finger the whole time. Bret Battery is now using the ropes to get to his feet, when he turns back to the center of the ring High Voltage ends his consciousness with a big ‘The Good Conductor’ a Double Superkick! The fans boo as High Voltage and Garrett Holloway head out, exiting the building as we go to break. WINNERS: HIGH VOLTAGE Right as soon as the match is over we are taken to the backstage area where the fans see Tony Miranda watching the television with The Shadow no where to be seen. His focus is broken as Sarah Sharp barges in very much so rudely. SARAH SHARP: “What are your thoughts on finding out that High Voltage were Riley’s attackers and that you’ll be facing them at ‘Exile’ for the Tag Belts?” Tony seems to be calming himself from this rude entrance from the always impolite Sarah Sharp, none the less he turns the TV off and turns to her with a grin. TONY MIRANDA: “It’s like a copy cat murderer, nobody ever does it as good as the first man did it. These guys High Voltage have the appeal. You know they think that they can compete with us. I’ve even heard them state they were the best tag team in GIW. Every team that has said that in the past has ended up in the hospital in no time.” Tony smiles, looking deeper into the camera now as Sarah continues to hold the microphone up to his mouth. TONY MIRANDA: “We don’t fight for La’Sarah, in fact we only did this for a bit of fun and she made a promise to me I couldn’t turn down. We never lost those belts, and as far as I am concerned we are still the champions. We’ll regain the belts, as Perfect Combination just continues to demolish anything in our paths.” Tony lets out that very evil smirk of his as Sarah steps away, as we are taken from here to another area backstage. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = Back in the back we go where we are now thrown right into a big commotion between four stars. On one side is Jak Hendricks alongside his buddies Seth and Mitch, arguing with Seth Seeker their feud continuing. Not far behind them is another argument going on between Dallas Steele and Frankie Raptor. Their words are inaudible due to the amount of talking; suddenly La’Sarah Audric who is just getting backstage walks right into it. LA’SARAH AUDRIC: “Gentlemen…Gentlemen…GENTLEMEN!” The third time is the charm as she gets every single one of their attentions, turning quickly to look at her. LA’SARAH AUDRIC: “Save it for the match, I mean it is next.” La’Sarah prepare to walk off as the guys seem to be willing to let it go, but suddenly she stops an idea popping into her head. LA’SARAH AUDRIC: “Actually…if you guys can’t get along. I think the best way to fix that is to go ahead and switch the teams tonight. Seeker with Hendricks and Steele with Raptor. Buh bye.” La’Sarah waves them goodbye, either currently angry or just being a bitch because immediately we see the disliking of this decision from all four of them as she walks off and we take a break. QUOTE After that break we return to already see Dallas Steele and Frankie Raptor standing in the ring, arguing the entire time. “Forever” by Drake, Kanye West, Lil Wayne, and Eminem hits as now we see Jak Hendricks walking out from the back to a whole lot of boos, everyone having seen what he did last week. RILEY STEVENS: “I saw this man’s debut on a replay this week; I think we are all confused about his tactics in that Open Battle Royal last week. Now he teams with the man that he faced in Seth Seeker.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I mean it’s pretty simple he didn’t want to get hurt for nothing. Seeker is scarier in person.” Jak steps into the ring with a big smirk on his face, there’s something you have to like about him but the fans boos seem to go against that. “Nightmare” by Avenged Sevenfold now echoes through the arena bringing a huge amount of cheers from the crowd. Though his words can some time anger folks at home, his presence is one they’ll cheer for. His eyes are on his own partner the whole time. He steps into the ring, talking with Jak about starting the match. RILEY STEVENS: “Can these two teams truly get along here tonight? I have my doubts but the winners may just push themselves up the ladder.” Jak willingly lets Seth have the start as Dallas demands that he start on the other, Frankie finally letting him do so. DING DING DING! Dallas not having realized who he’s facing yet turns, then quickly turns back to make the tag but Frankie Raptor jumps off the turnbuckle. Dallas screams at him but this slight miscue turns into a big mistake as when Dallas turns back to fight he receives a big bear like paw from Seeker. The fans cheer in approval as he grabs Steele and whips him off the ropes. On the return, Steele tries to throw a big clothesline but to everyone’s surprise it does literally nothing to sway Seeker. Steele seems a bit discouraged so he backs up and runs back at him, trying it again. Yet again he receives the same treatment hurting his own arm more then he did Seeker. Willingly giving up he again calls for a tag, but Seeker shows no remorse grabbing him before he gets there. Wrapping his arms around his waist, he lifts him up nailing him with a German Suplex releasing him during the move sending him nearly out of the arena. The fans are in awe of his raw strength as he gets up letting out a primal scream, walking across the ring to grab Steele up. The fans cheer as he gets up but a tag from Jak Hendricks makes him and everyone else a little angry. Jak steps in and orders Seeker out of the ring, throwing caution to the wind. RILEY STEVENS: “It takes some real heart to command anything from a man like Seth Seeker.” The fans boo as Seeker does it, staring at Jak the whole time almost as if to say to him that his time will come later on. Jak now takes advantage of Seth’s work, lifting up the nearly out of it Dallas and throws him at the ropes. To everyone’s surprise Steele is able to tag in Raptor, diving at him and then rolling right out of the ring in pain and exhausted. Raptor now heads into the ring, realizing it’s his time. He comes into the ring, locking up with Jak. It seems as though Raptor is going to win the test of strength, but it’s Jak that out smarts him with a big knee to the stomach followed up with a Lariat that nearly takes Frankie’s head off. He stops turning and beginning to taunt the crowd then turning to Seeker and in another big show of disrespect spits his way, coming up short. With Frankie now starting to get up Jak lets him to his feet then immediately hammers him down with a Spinebuster right into a pin 1…2…Dallas Steele gets back in the ring and stops the pin from happening. Jak gets up, as a desperate Steele starts to hammer him with left and rights. Backing him up into his own corner, Seeker makes the tag before Steele tackles Jak to the outside. RILEY STEVENS: “Seeker makes the tag!” Seth steps into the ring watching his enemy in a fight on the outside. He pounds his chest looking down at Frankie Raptor who is trying to get back to his feet. The fans are cheering him on, as Raptor gets to his feet only just barely able to stand. He lifts up Frankie easier, showing off a bit with him on his shoulder. On the outside, Jak hits Dallas with a Yakuza Kick knowing him down and out. Inside the ring Seeker finishes his move the ‘Don't Fear The Reaper’ a Jackhammer! Right away he makes the pin but to everyone’s surprise it seems to be Jak trying to break up the pin 1…2…3! He’s too late. RILEY STEVENS: “Seth Seeker and Jak Hendricks win, though to me it seems that if he got in sooner Jak would have stopped that pin.” With his fist clenched to try and punch Seeker off, Seth gets to his feet turning Jak in quite a precarious position. The fans wait to see what is going to happen as Seth Seeker offers to let him hit him. Jak shakes his head and turns to leave but suddenly he turns catching Seeker with a big punch to the face. Suddenly out from the back come Jak’s lackeys Seth and Mitch, sliding into the ring as Seeker only seems angry from the punch. RILEY STEVENS: “Seeker does not seem happy.” All three men begin to beat down Seeker. After a few seconds of being hammered from all sides the fans start to cheer as he throws Jak’s Seth right out of the ring with one hand. Jak turns and bails out of the ring leaving his friend Mitch in the grasps of Seeker. The fans cheer as Mitch tries to worm his way out before being hit with a Clothesline that takes him out. Seeker walks across the ring, telling Jak to get back in the ring. For the second week in a row Jak leaves unscathed as he yells back “One on one, I’ll crush you.” While everyone else seems to doubt this Jak seems purely confident as this feud between the two opposites continues to heat up. WINNERS: SETH SEEKER & JAK HENDRIKS Back to the back we go again this time into the office of La’Sarah Audric who is on the phone, talking with someone hanging up right as we basically are able to hear anything. Right away Sarah Sharp comes into the screen, standing across from La’Sarah. SARAH SHARP: “I know a lot of thought must be going into the events earlier tonight. But a lot of people are wondering when you’ll announce a Galveston Island Championship Match.” Sarah walks around the table realizing it’ll be easier as she holds her microphone up to La’Sarah. LA’SARAH AUDRIC: “Well let’s just say, that I just signed a wrestler from a certain OTHER brand that may just be involved in that title picture.” Almost as soon as she finishes her sentence the door opens and in walks a very annoyed Chris Jericho. CHRIS JERICHO: “Why are you wasting your money on pathetic nobodies when you already have your champion standing right in front of you.” Jericho points to himself, slamming his hands down onto the table as Sarah Sharp stares at him, seemingly entranced by the wealthy and very famous Chris Jericho while La’Sarah replies. LA’SARAH AUDRIC: “I can’t just hand you the belt. And you did win last week, but last time I looked I really couldn’t tell if it was you or Rydell who was better.” La’Sarah’s words seems to get under Jericho’s skin, as he snaps back right away. CHRIS JERICHO: “Well then so be it, you’ll see just who is better tonight.” Jericho storms out of her office as Sarah snaps out of her little trance, with La’Sarah pointing her out as she gets back on the phone as the segment ends. QUOTE The fans in the arena are pumped up knowing that it’s time for the GIW Television Championship Scramble, this given away by the fact that the belt is sitting at ringside. “Monster” by Kanye West hits and out from the back comes Kydd Lightning and almost immediately the sense of respect out of the crowd has clearly increased, though they still boo him anyways. RILEY STEVENS: “This man made a big debut last week, striking first and eliminating Zack Starr from the chase.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Told everyone from the start he’d be good.” Kydd jumps up and over the ropes looking at the crowd and throwing his arms out, spinning a bit before walking over to the corner preparing for the match. “The Man Who Sold The World” by Nirvana nows hits bringing more boos from the crowd as he walks out with that very diabolical look on his face. The fans show him little respect but he doesn’t seem to care one bit, he gets into the ring looking over at Kydd both men know how important this is. Last but not least “Monster” by Skillet and the fans explode into cheers. RILEY STEVENS: “Here is the fan’s choice Joshua Acquin.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Also known as worst case scenario.” Joshua walks out with his soon to be wife Maria trailing not far behind him. The fans cheer them on as both give high fives before Joshua turns and gives her a kiss. Right after he slides into the ring, all three men glaring at one another. Finally the bell rings as the three are ready to fight. DING DING DING! The match starts and all three men realize that it’s time for them to fight it out to be in the finals. Right away Kydd and Marcus come into an agreement agreeing to go after Joshua. The fans boo as Acquin gets cornered but still tries to fight them off. He gives a left to Hister then a right to Lightning but they still manage to come right back and begin to crush him with blows. The fans boo as they begin to stump him in the corner, before the referee forces them back. Acquin gets to his feet wanting to end this double team. He goes for them but walks right into two shots. Both men grab him and crush him with a Double DDT working together greatly. The fans continue to boo, hating this. Acquin is flat on his back as Kydd, tells Hister what to do. He does so running off the ropes then dropping an elbow onto Joshua. Marcus now tells Kydd to do the same. Kydd runs off, however he runs forward dropkicking Hister then doing an amazing flip right on top of Joshua 1…2…Kick Out! The fans cheer his kick out but were very much so impressed with the move regardless. Kydd gets up smirking looking out at the crowd, as he goes right for the top rope. But as soon as he gets set up Hister is there to reign on his parade with a very pretty looking kick to the stomach of Lightning. With him doubled over, he grabs him and tosses him off the top rope back into the ring to the sound of a huge thud. Lightning rolls around holding his back, while Joshua now gets back to his feet. Hister runs at him only for Joshua to duck last second, then catch him with a Falling Neckbreaker. The fans cheer, getting behind there guy as Maria claps him on at ringside. With Hister now downed, it’s Kydd returning to his feet. Josh grabs the back of his head and attempts to throw him over but unknown to him Kydd is able to hold on, and begins to lift himself up. This show of athleticism is cut short as Josh turns around and nails him with a big dropkick sending him dangerously to the outside. RILEY STEVENS: “Ouch that fall may have eliminated him from the match; he landed awkwardly on his back.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “In this match it’s not such a bad thing, hide you’ll make it to the finals.” Acquin turns around right into a brutal spear from Hister. The fans boo realizing this may be it as Hister pins him 1…2…Foot on the Rope! The referee sees it and gives the news to Marcus. The fans cheer and a small Acquin chant begins as Hister is busy arguing with the referee. While he is, behind him Joshua Acquin is slowly but surely getting to his feet. The fans cheers clearly are carrying him up. Hister finally turns around walking right into the ‘Face Breaker’ a Kill Switch! The fans go wild until, they realize that Hister almost through some divine miracle was able to roll out of the ring right after the move. Joshua holds his head stunned. When he turns to look for Kydd it proves to be a mistake as Lightning explodes at him taking him on a trip with a Double Tilt-a-Whirl Headscissors! The fans boo, hoping that Joshua didn’t just lose his chance. Rather then capitalize right away Kydd decides to taunt the fans and then Maria at ring side. He turns back to grab Joshua but receives a big kick to the stomach for his troubles sending Kydd stumbling back. Acquin attempts to get back up from his knee but instead receives a ‘Shining Black’ a Shining Big Boot! Right away Kydd goes for a cover 1…2…Kick Out! The fans cheer, realizing it’s not over yet. RILEY STEVENS: “I thought that was it.” Kydd gets up; he himself thinking it was over as well. He tries to plead that it was three to the referee who is having none of it. He walks back over trying to get Acquin up to finish him up. He finally gets him up to his feet, calling for his finisher the ‘Electroshock Therapy’ some how Acquin counters out right into another ‘Face Breaker’ out of no where. Hister is up on the outside with plenty of time to get in. At first he starts to make progress to stop it, but then stops and jumps off the ring apron as Acquin makes the pin attempt 1…2…3! Acquin eliminates Lightning. RILEY STEVENS: “Talk about shocking, Joshua Acquin just beat the man that won last week. And for the second week in a row Marcus Hister wasn’t involved in the ending, in fact it looked like he may have not wanted to intervene.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “It’s called survival you idiot. The man’s in the finals.” The fans go wild as Joshua Acquin gets to his feet, holding up his arms in victory. Maria walks up the steel steps and into the ring, joining her man in celebration. On the outside Marcus Hister is too busy staring at the title to even notice. Though he may have ducked out of the ending this time, he did so with his mind on the ending as the field has been cut to two as Marcus Hister and Joshua Acquin will fight for the gold at ‘Exile’. WINNER: JOSHUA ACQUIN (Marcus Hister Also Advances) Back to the back we go again, now we are right in front of the curtain where The Killers are getting set for action. Before they go out, one of their opponents Chris Jericho pats both men on the back with a big smirk, putting his arms around both of them. CHRIS JERICHO: “Listen guys. Your young, you’ve got talent, and as far as I am concerned you’ve got a bright future around here. Making friends with a guy like me is not a bad idea. See tonight, we’ve got a match with one another. And I know it’s tempting to try to get a win over me to further your careers. But here’s something that can make you famous. Why don’t the two of you, take out Scott Rydell. Do you know the type of news you’d make if you two were the ones that ended his career?” Both Nelson and Brody listen to his offer, seeming to be a bit intrigued by it as Jericho shrugs a bit. CHRIS JERICHO: “So see you out there, when the match starts and comes to an end I trust you guys will make the right decision.” Jericho pats both of them on there backs and then pushes them towards the curtain where they head out, as we cut from there to the cameras around the ring. QUOTE By the time we get there however The Killers are nearly already to the ring. Both men intimidating as they get into the ring, a great combination of size and speed. The fans continue to boo them as they take there spot in the corner as “Motherfucker of the Year” by Motley Crue and the fans go absolutely insane. Out from the back is Scott Rydell with that big grin on his face as he walks down towards the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “A lot of people predict this man to be our next champion.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “And a lot of people are idiots to think Chris Jericho won’t win.” He steps up and into the ring, going through the ropes and walking over to the corner, going to the top rope and posing for a moment. “Break The Walls Down” soon follows turning the cheers into heavy boos as Chris Jericho walks out from the back down, with that big smirk. On his face, Jericho points down at Rydell and motions a belt around his waist. Jericho strolls down to the ring with his usual sense of confidence. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “This man is our champ, right here.” Jericho steps into the ring, as a argument ensues over who will start. Finally Rydell concedes the spot to Jericho as the bell rings. DING DING DING! Suspiciously Brody Harper jumps down off the ring apron, slipping behind Rydell whom does not notice it. In the ring Jericho, points to the mat and Nelson willing lays down allowing Jericho to get on top 1…2…3! That’s it, already. RILEY STEVENS: “What in the world was that?!” The fans begin to boo as Rydell looks around as shocked as everyone else. However when Jericho and Fielder begin to turn their attention to him, he jumps down trying to run. This is where Brody comes into play as almost immediately he grabs him and tosses him in the ring. The fans boo as Nelson and Jericho tackle him down starting a two on one beat down. Both men lay into him as Brody returns to the joining in on the attack. Jericho then demands a chair be given to him by Nelson who jumps out immediately. The fans continue to boo as Nelson hands the chair to Jericho, whom tells them to holds Rydell up. RILEY STEVENS: “No! Don’t do that.” Suddenly with no remorse he steps back then with a bit of a running star comes forward crushing Rydell with a chair shot to the head. The fans reign down boos as a late but still trying Leon Drake comes booking it down towards the ring. The fans cheer him on as he slides in. He punches Nelson and then Brody however right to the back he’s given a chair shot sending him right out of the ring. The fans boo as he folds over in pain, as the camera is able to catch a view of a very bloody Scott Rydell. RILEY STEVENS: “This is one of the worst things I’ve seen awhile. Yet again Chris Jericho takes it way too far.” Jericho now leans down grabbing the bloody head of Rydell, holding it up as he screams into the camera “This was the last man in my path, next week I want that belt handed to me!” He throws down Rydell’s head and then pats The Killers chests as all three men raise there hands in victory having taken out Scott Rydell tonight. WINNERS: CHRIS JERICHO & SCOTT RYDELL Suddenly a video begins to play hyping up Affliction. It show cases three matches starting with the match between Jake Avery and Nate Amazing that’ll be contested in First Blood Rules. Followed up by that is hype for Mike Bryant’s NOX title defense against the always amazing Eric Sparrow. And finally it hypes up the main event which is a match between Madison Seton and Mark Cross versus High Voltage as the video closes. As soon as the video ends we see a distraught Kydd Lightning pacing around. However Garrett Holloway, stands not far behind him apparently he hasn’t left the arena yet. GARRETT HOLLOWAY: “Feeling mistreated? You see if this place had any sort of respect they’d have given you the belt when you won last week. You know, I’ve heard through the grape vine that La’Sarah may have stolen one of my guys. Clearly they don’t know how to use you, so why don’t you sign on the dotted line and we’ll strike back.” Kydd at first seems to be resisting this, thinking whether it really is a good idea. However still angry and upset about the match tonight, he takes the pen and signs on a clip board that Garrett handily pulled out. Garrett throws his arm over him and the two walk off together, as Garrett has struck back taking a Revolt star just like Revolt took one of theirs. QUOTE It’s time for the MAIN EVENT! Already we see the opportunities in the arena. Weapons that could be used are all over the place, they just don’t stick out until a match like this comes into play. "The Human Stain" by Kamelot now hits and the fans begin to boo as Tony Miranda comes out from behind the curtains. Surprisingly he lived up to his word coming alone. The days of these fans loving Tony are long in the future. RILEY STEVENS: “This match has some huge stakes. Either Aspen will become Tony’s slave for lack of a better term or Aspen Chaud ends Perfect Combination before they can even get their title shot.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Wonder how these fans will react when Aspen is shining Tony’s boots?” Tony has that sadistic smirk, almost like he knows that tonight he’ll walk out with Aspen’s services. The fans will not let up, the fact they hate him coupled with the situation at hand makes them hate him like none other. Tony steps into the ring, looking around the arena perhaps looking for what he can use. "Bullseye" by Aly and AJ now hits sending the fans into a frenzy, everybody wanting to let her know they are firmly in her corner. RILEY STEVENS: “Aspen has been through a rough patch these past few weeks, she wants…well really has to turn it around tonight.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Doubt it.” Aspen high fives everyone that she can, before skipping up the steel steps and then stepping over the bottom rope and into the ring. She looks over at Tony, then with her hand calls for him to come at her causing the referee to call for the bell right then and there. DING DING DING! The two meet in the center of the ring for a test of strength. Both of them are locked up, but right away it’s Tony that is able to basically toss her back onto her butt. The fans seem a little nervous here, knowing what’s all on the line. Rather then keep up the attack Miranda steps back allowing her to get to her feet. All too used to this treatment Aspen gets to her feet and wastes little time running at him and catching him with a Flying Forearm! The fans go wild as Tony falls back into the corner of the ring. Aspen claps her hands and runs at him only to be caught with a big elbow. The girl known as Barbie stumbles backwards a bit, allowing Tony to walk forward hitting her with a big haymaker of a left hand. Aspen falls to the mat, but is quick to get up only to run right into a Discus Clothesline. The fans boo as Aspen has started slow in this one, with Tony seeming in total control of her. He waits behind her, having something up his sleeve as Chaud slowly starts to stumble to her feet. He goes for a another clothesline his specialty but she’s able to duck away from it, while tripping him sending him right through the middle rope and to the outside of the ring bringing a cheer for the first time from the crowd as Aspen gets her first bit of momentum. She slides right out of the ring with Tony, who was able to land pretty smoothly. He’s up quickly but eats a big elbow that’s followed up with a Backhand Chop from Aspen. The fans are getting a little more excited as Tony stumbles back into the railings around the ring. With him there he nails him with another Backhand Chop! The fans in that side of the ring are going nuts, watching this right up close. RILEY STEVENS: “Aspen is taking it to him.” Tony reels a bit, trying to walk away but his chest clearly hurting from the shots. She sets up for one more, but Tony is able to move.
