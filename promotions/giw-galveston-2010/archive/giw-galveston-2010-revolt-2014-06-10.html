GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: REVOLT Live from the Kauffman Arena – June 10th, 2014 QUOTE OPEN BATTLE ROYAL This weeks Open Battle Royal featured three different debuting stars in Cassandra Knight, Jessie James and the unique Mo Chronic. The fans knowing Cassandra a bit due to her association with Beck and Harlequin. The match up started more like a triple threat then an Over the Top Rope Battle Royal, with each start seeming to want to show their talents and really prove themselves in the ring. It was Jessie that started off the match hot, really giving it to both Mo and Cassandra but as she was looking to make the first elimination on Cassandra, it was Chronic who snuck up from behind her and treated her like the last bit of hash eliminating it quickly by tossing her over as he then went after Cassandra nearly putting her out to but he got burnt by Knight who countered him nearly tossing her over by wrapping him up and taking him over instead falling out as well but doing so just after he hit to get the victory in the Open Battle royal making her team proud despite great efforts from Jessie James and even the man you can’t judge by his appearance Mo Chronic. WINNER: CASSANDRA KNIGHT BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! The Kauffman Arena springs into life as the fireworks stream into the air and the music begins to pulse through the air. This is the last show before Cat 5, and the people know it. They get even louder when Ian Mendoza’s music hits and the man in charge makes his way down to the ring. IAN MENDOZA: “Ladies and gentlemen, I come here with a memo from Mr. B regarding the Sole Survivor match at Cat 5. As you all know, Madison Seton, Michael Lynch, and Alexis Terry have already qualified – which leaves one spot still available. At this time, please welcome to the ring… my friend… Wake Walker.” The lights go out and a spotlight shines on a disoriented Mendoza. Walker makes his way down to the ring with a microphone in hand. He slides in and stands face to face with his enemy as the lights fade up. WAKE WALKER: “Mendoza. Do you have something you need to tell me? I’ve been waiting to hear it all week.” Mendoza pulls a folded sheet of paper at his pocket and opens it. He hands it to Wake. IAN MENDOZA: “Mr. B has instructed me to give you a chance to qualify for Sole Survivor tonight when you fight Paul Lane. If you win the match, you’re in. If you lose, then you will be watching from home.” Wake is looking at the paper as he speaks, clearly displeased. WAKE WALKER: “You’re giving me a qualifying match? I’ve already beaten Alexis Terry and Madison Seton – don’t you think I’m more than qualified to be in this match already?” He looks up at Mendoza, who shrugs. IAN MENDOZA: “The ropes broke in the last match Walker. It was either a freak accident, or you rigged them to break. Either way, I’m not convinced that you deserve this opportunity, but Mr. B is in your corner. We are meeting in the middle.” Wake holds the paper in the air. WAKE WALKER: “I thought your only purpose was to read his memos and do what he says.” Mendoza nods. IAN MENDOZA: “In every other case, yes. But Wake, I have a bad feeling about you. Mr. B doesn’t see what you are, but I do. Besides, if you are so good, you shouldn’t have any problem with Paul Lane tonight.” Wake rips the paper up in anger, then points at Mendoza. WAKE WALKER: “I wont, unless you try to pull something –“ Mendoza rolls his eyes. IAN MENDOZA: “If I try to pull something? Are you kidding me Wake? The ropes, bodyguards – you don’t have a good track record.” “Cupid Carries a Gun” by Marilyn Manson hits and the crowd erupts. The music keeps Wake from hitting Mendoza because he turns around quickly are stares at Alioth Starre on the ramp. ALIOTH STARRE: “Can we please stop bickering?” The crowd cheers. Wake stares Alioth down from the ring. ALIOTH STARRE: “Wake – qualify tonight. Prove to everyone you can win the big one. Become champion… and then we can have our battle.” Alioth’s music hits again and we have a stare down as we fade to commercial.
