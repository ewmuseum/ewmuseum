GIW — Card

Revolt, 5 January 2012

5 January 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: THURSDAY NIGHT REVOLT LIVE! From Poretto Beach – January 5, 2012 BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! The blue fireworks stream into the night sky, signifying the beginning of another GIW show, and the fans go insane when they hear the the rocket blast off. The ION cameras pan around Porretto Beach, they show the Galveston skyline in the background, and whitecaps rushing in on the sandy beaches. “Demon Eyes” by The Answer hits the PA system, and the crowd instantly recognizes this music as the chairman’s, and they go insane. The music plays for a while as the cameras show us a throne in the ring, and a cloaked podium. As the music plays, we cut over to Riley Stevens and Oliver Thibodaux at the booth. RILEY STEVENS: “What an ovation for the Chairman Blake Beckett as we begin this edition of Thursday Night Revolt It looks like there is a coronation in store for us, Oliver!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Yuck. It is the crowing of Alex Castellanos, and it is evident that Beckett is on a power trip against Aspen. Not only did he fire her long time ally Joshua Acquin, but he is also going to make her place the crown on Alex’s head, that’s just cruel and unusual punishment.” RILEY STEVENS: “Aspen Chaud has had her way with this company for far to long, and Beckett is absolutely sick of it. I completely understand why he would try to take back what is rightfully his.” Blake Beckett appeared on the ramp with a huge reaction from the fans. He made his way into the ring with a huge smirk on his face and the crown in his hands on a pillow. He carefully climbed up the stairs and entered the ring, and took the microphone from Jamal Jackson before beginning to speak. BLAKE BECKETT: “Last week I made a deal with Aspen Chaud. I told her that if she could win the beat the clock matches; she would be made the number one contender for the Undisputed Championship at Malice. However, if she was to lose the contest, she would have to crown the winner. And so, without further a do, let’s welcome our winner into the ring, Alex Castellanos!” “Wayward One” by Alter Bridge hits the PA system, and Alex Castellanos enters onto the ramp in his new “Golden Age” t-shirt and jeans to a major reaction from the fans. He walked down the ring propelled by the cheers from the fans, and slid into a position opposite of Beckett. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Oh good God! They gave this man a t-shirt? Who the hell even likes this guy. He’s a joke. A fraud. Aspen said it best he was irrelevant the first time he was here, and he still is now!” RILEY STEVENS: “Hey, that’s the King you are talking to!” Blake motions for Castellanos to take his seat on the throne. Then Aspen Chaud’s music hits and the arena blows up with boos. Alex laughs as Aspen makes her way down to the ring, wearing a shirt that is exactly like his and a dunce hat, just like a servant would have had towards a real King. Beckett smirks as she enters the ring to the chuckles of people in the crowd. She stares down Beckett, obviously holding herself back from slapping his face off. She took a microphone, but before she could speak, Beckett cut her off. BLAKE BECKETT: “Oh, I see you found the wardrobe I selected for you this evening.” The crowd “oooh”s that as Beckett laughs and Aspen bites her lip. ASPEN CHAUD: “Now listen Blake. I’m doing this only because I’m such a good person, so true and loyal to my word. So give me the crown, and let’s get on with the show please.” Beckett does indeed give her the crown, but not without saying something else. BLAKE BECKETT: “Oh? You’re a good person now, huh? So, before I give you this, I want you to beg for my forgiveness. You didn’t do it last week, and I want you to do it now.” Aspen took in a lot of air and released it quickly. She shook her head. ASPEN CHAUD: “Just who the hell do you think you are. Do you think it is OK for you to unfairly single me out like this! I’ve sued you ONCE and I’m not afraid to do it again. So, why don’t you apologize to me? That’s an order, Beckett. But, unlike you, I can back it up.” Joshua Acquin, Assassin, and Alexis Terry show up on the ramp to a massive amount of boos from the crowd. Beckett shakes his head, and Alex gets in between him and Aspen. BLAKE BECKETT: “That’s your posse? Acquin, Terry, and Assassin? Those are the only ones in this company that still fear you enough to follow you. That’s pathetic, and it shows how sad you’ve become.” ASPEN CHAUD: “We will see how pathetic they are when you try to defend yourself, Blake. I dare you to try. But first, I want you to put me in the Undisputed Championship match at ‘Malice’, or I’ll have them do it for you.” Blake looks at Alex, who shrugs his shoulders. ASPEN CHAUD: “Beckett. It’s not a threat, it’s a promise.” BLAKE BECKETT: “You know what, that is a great idea Aspen. I’ll put you in the match against Alex Castellanos and Tony Miranda. No problem.” The crowd obviously doesn’t like Beckett’s decision, but Aspen turns over to her posse on the ramp and nods. BLAKE BECKETT: “But, you will have to do it in a No Escape Match.” Blake’s music hit the PA again, and Aspen’s eyes got extremely wide. Alex walked over and placed the crown on his head, then winked at Aspen, who was exasperated in the center of the ring. We fade to commercial as the fans go wild with cheers for the announcement. QUOTE To start the night as per usual we await the entrants of the Open Battle Royal but as "Start The Show" by Jet hits the fans look to the ramp both confused and interested as out from the back comes Beck Ramsey and Seth Ramsey heading towards the ring together, the two looking like complete opposites. RILEY STEVENS: “This match tonight is a tag match, with GIW trying to bring some focus back to tag team wrestling.” The two enter the ring, Beck posing on the side of the ring while Seth jumps up top rope throwing up his arms. The two jump down as their music trails off and “Bodies” by Saliva hits and the fans give them some cheers as Brody Harper enters with Nelson Fielder by his side for the first time in a long time. RILEY STEVENS: “A reunion of sorts here with Nelson and Brody.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Jesus, we hired him back?” The two head to the ring high fiving fans all the way to the ring, heading up and into the ring as the match gets ready to go with Nelson and Brody heading to the opposite corner. DING DING DING! The fans cheer as we get this thing under way, on one side we have Nelson starting the match the other we have Beck Ramsey. Fielder having just returned looks around at the crowd calling for some cheers but as he does that Ramsey rushes across the ring clubbing him in the back. Though the fans were only giving Nelson a half hearted cheer, they still go wild with boos on Beck who basically gives them a glimpse into what he is all about. He takes a second to hold up his arms to taunt the crowd then grabs Nelson spinning around and whipping him into the corner. Fielder bounces off and walks right into a European Uppercut from Beck. Surprisingly he doesn’t fall to the mat instead he spins around right into the grasps of a German Suplex from Ramsey. The fans continue to boo him as he gets up, wiping off his hands seeming none to worried about Nelson in this one as he starts to walk around Fielder kicking him while he is down. RILEY STEVENS: “This Beck Ramsey is making no fans here tonight.” Beck finally allows him to get to his feet as he smirks and grabs Fielder and whips him off the ropes. Nelson uses the ropes to stop himself and then dives to his corner making the tag to Brody. The big man comes right in, looking all sorts of pumped but Beck backs up heading over to his corner where he invites Seth to have a go in the ring. Seth makes the tag not seeming too scared as he heads in and immediately asks Brody to stop which he does. Harper looks at him confused as Seth takes the chance to play the bongos on Brody’s belly getting some laughs from the crowd as he ducks under a Brody right then bounces off the rope jumping up into a Hurricanrana taking big Brody down to the mat as he hops up and screams out to the crowd to get it going. The fans starting to like him, leaving them confused on where they lie with the tag team. RILEY STEVENS: “These two are working well together.” Brody hops up stumbling back into the corner where he makes the tag back to Nelson who goes Springboard. Seth rushes at him but Nelson jumps right over the top of him. But as he bounces off the ropes Beck takes a cheap shot kicking him in the back as the referee isn’t looking and then he walks right into a Seth Ramsey Flurry Kick. Seth heads over making the tag into Beck, but as he does Brody heads in trying to save his partner. But instead he walks right into a big eye poke from Beck that leaves him holding his eyes. Seth cuts him out from the back as Beck hits a Running Test Boot a move they together call the ‘Cross-Collision’. Having been taken out from both sides Brody rolls out of the ring and now Nelson gets up stumbling right into Beck who hoists him onto his shoulders while Seth goes top rope as Seth comes down with a Asai DDT in a movie they call ‘Super ^Q!’ that’s lays Nelson out as Beck covers 1…2…3! JAMAL JACKSON: “Here are your winners…The Sin City Express!” The fans are split some wanting to cheer Seth, others wanting to boo Beck so they get a mixed bag instead as they hop up and the referee raises both men’s hands into the air. RILEY STEVENS: “These two made an impressive debut here in GIW, and might just become the focus of the GIW tag division if they keep it up.” Beck and Seth head to the back together after posting an impressive win as we take it to commercial. WINNERS: THE SIN CITY EXPRESS We cut backstage to the GIW interview area, where Alexis Terry was ready to address the audience. ALEXIS TERRY: “Last week after Revolt, I talked to Blake Beckett. He told me he was impressed with my win over Tony Miranda, the Undisputed Champion, and he basically told me I was the best thing ever to happen in this company. That is word for word what he said. He put me in a match, and you’ll NEVER believe what it is. I, Alexis Terry, the rightful Queen of GIW, will face Faith Simpson at ‘Malice’ for the Legacy Championship.” Alexis smiles a huge, arrogant smile, then continues on. ALEXIS TERRY: “I’ve always liked the saying ‘taking candy from a baby’, but now I will get to change it up a tad. Taking this gold from Faith Simpson is going to be like taking gold from a teenager, so EASY! Hah! So, when you all see me with a new golden accessory, you can’t say I didn’t warn you.” We head to the ring, with Alexis smirking. QUOTE ‘Cruel Melody’ by Wes Borland’s band, Black Light Burns, played into the arena. Soon after the ascent of sound, Altair stepped out to the entry way and without anyone or anything, just himself. He knelt down onto his knees and gestured with his hand the reverse of the Christian cross prayer then pointed toward the ring and moved on his way toward it. His grin was missing; his cocky, smug grin that would show off the tips of his fangs. His cheeks even looked a little puffy, but one would only think he looked healthier, rather than the pale, sunken cheek, youth that he appeared to be. Altair rounded the corner of the ring and climbed up to the rim, his back to the ropes and his arms draped over them at each side. He looked out to the crowd the jeered him, and then leaned back as if he were aiming or using the ropes to propel forward. Altair sprayed a mist of blood red liquid into the air above, raining down on the crowd and matted floor. The substance was thicker than water and dripped, pooled, and stained along his lips, the floor, and any that it touched. Blood. RILEY STEVENS: “Altair’s record may now show that of a man whose been dominant but there is no person in this company that elicits fear like he does.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “He’s a weird dude all right.” As Altair entered the ring, Kevin Carino’s theme and presence hit the arena. Kevin was new, but even so, his crowd reaction was more welcoming than Altair’s. The new wrestler slid himself into the ring and charged for Altair. He got in several right and left jabs, forcing his opponent to the ropes just as the bell rang. Altair leaned over the top rope, pummeled by the quick strikes, but he saw his opening when Kevin backed a few paces, seeming to charge with a clothesline to knock the Blood Raven over the top rope. Altair ducked and tossed his opponent knee and face first onto the matted floor. He backed away himself to recover from the little bout and watched with amusement as Kevin found himself lightly covered in the bloody spray that had pooled on the floor. DING DING DING! Carino returned to the fray and the got into a rough lockup with Altair. The paler wrestler meant business now. He wanted to give Carino a rough start after Kevin’s little tantrum in the ring. Altair threw a head butt to stumble Carino back, but with him in grasp, he pulled his darker skinned opponent back and used a throat thrust to finish it off. Carino fell to the canvas and rolled himself back up, but Altair was there to follow up on the assault. Altair yanked his opponent’s head back and kicked him to his knees to apply a shoulder claw to Carino’s right shoulder. He applied pressure and dug deep into the flesh, but Carino refused to tap. Altair released the hold and gave a quick run to the ropes to come back at Carino and boot his opponent’s skull, knocking Carino off his knees. Kevin rolled himself out of the ring to recover, and Altair stayed in the ring, stretching his wrists out and biding his time. He had the upper hand in higher ground. Kevin would have to enter the ring, thus giving Altair the opening to attack. If Altair slid out, he’d open himself for attack. Eventually Carino did return to the ring. He had climbed up to the rim of the ring, his back to the aisle. Altair closed the distance between them and let Kevin get a jab in on him unprotected, but it only served to soften Carino’s guard. In a matter of moments, Altair ascended his opponent’s body, and using the ropes he wrapped his legs over and around Carino’s waist, his arm over his opponent’s neck and applied the guillotine choke with the ropes supporting his wait, but pulling down on Carino. RILEY STEVENS: “Come on now, just win the match.” The referee called for it to be broken, but Altair held on as long as he could before finally relaxing the grip. Carino fell slack against the rim of the ring, just barely holding on. Altair too used the ropes to hold himself up. The arena fell to darkness. A red filtered light filled the arena and from the entry way came a black wooden casket and Scar pushing it down from the back. He slowly moved the casket down toward the ring whilst Altair and Carino began another struggle of exchanging fists and locked up, either man trying to apply a suplex. Altair tried to suplex Carino into the ring, and Carino tried to send Altair outside of it. Scar set the casket lengthwise behind Carino and then opened it. Altair caught this image, positioned between Carino’s arm and then pulled out to jab his opponent’s skull to break their struggle. He punched a few more times and then ran to the other end of the ring, using the ropes to propel his charge and elbow Carino off of the rim of the ring like a cannon ball crashing into the casket. Scar closed the lid and began to drag Carino’s coffin toward the back all the while the referee began a count down to disqualification. At the count of ten, the end of the casket had gone through the curtain and the light returned to normal with Altair left standing in the ring. Victory. JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner by count out…Altair?” The crowd is left looking at one another in shock and confusion as Altair had just put Carino away by using that casket that he’s been talking about so much. RILEY STEVENS: “This man is just sick, hopefully someone helps that poor soul.” As the fans continue to ask each other what might happen to Carino, Altair stands in the ring heartless as he seems to enjoy what he’s just done but we cut away. WINNER: ALTAIR After a short commercial break we return to the ring where the fans are currently booing Altair who is twirling a mic around in his hands as he paces around the ring. ALTAIR: “Tonight you witnessed the end of a man’s career and it left you all scared and confused but truthfully…none of you cared. This man was not important, this man held no significant meaning, and to me he was just a symbol. A harbinger of things to come…Thus far I have eliminated some weaklings from GIW but I have my eyes set a little higher on a man that you all know as Johnny Violence.” Altair lowers the microphone as the fans react to the name; it’s become a mixed bag when it comes to Violence some cheering and some booing. ALTAIR: “That’s why tonight this symbol, was just the beginning. At ‘Malice’ it will be Johnny Violence versus myself testing his will against my will to break him. And that match will only be won when one of us puts the other in that very same Casket you saw here tonight. The winner will go home the loser will leave in that Casket…” Altair smirks as the fans seem to be intrigued yet a little creeped out by Altair as per usual as he exits the ring, heading to the back having made his point. ==== Standing in the back is Aspen Chaud, Joshua Acquin, Alexis Terry, and Assassin who seem to have started a ‘We hate Beckett’ group now as they sit in the catering area, starting too bad mouth him. ASSASSIN: "He had the nerve to tell me that there would be consequences if I lost tonight…Are you kidding me?" In a circle the four seem to all be discussing why they hate Blake, with Joshua going next. JOSHUA ACQUIN: “He made me a Road Agent? Literally I do nothing. They told me unless a fight breaks out, I basically do nothing. I was the best GM this place ever had!” Joshua screams it angrily as Assassin nods with him but Aspen seems to be distant the entire time as Alexis leans over to her. ALEXIS TERRY: “Aspen you ok?” Aspen looks over at her angrily, not seeming to be in the mood any more for talking. ASPEN CHAUD: “A NO ESCAPE MATCH?! I am locked in a cage with Tony and Alex? They are going to team up on me. BLAKE HAS DONE IT FOR THE LAST TIME!” But as she talks Alexis looks past her as Alex walks up, when she turns around he smiles and pulls the referee shirt off his shoulder. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “Just got my shirt, look good?” Castellanos smirks folding it out for her as Aspen screams, not really anything just an angry scream as Alex chuckles and walks off as Alexis tries to comfort her as the show continues. QUOTE “Nightmare” by Avenged Sevenfold played into the arena and, not alone, but with Jak Hendricks, Seth Seeker gathered at the entry way of the aisle. The two, performing as a tag tonight walked together down the aisle and entered the ring in their own respective ways. The two seemed to have chose the far corner of the ring as their tag section and waited for their opponents to arrive. RILEY STEVENS: “We welcome back Jak Hendricks and Seth Seeker two long time rivals turned tag team partners who look to shake things up here in GIW.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Jak was always my guy.” Unlike the two prior to him, Johnny Violence arrived to the aisle alone and with his theme playing. He got about half way down when Castellanos’ made his entrance. Alex’s theme and entrance carried the two toward the rest of the way into the ring. The two entered together and took to the only available corner of the ring to them, the left corner that faced the aisle way. It was decided amongst them that Johnny Violence would start off the bout against Jak Hendricks. RILEY STEVENS: “You have to wonder how Alex and Johnny will do as a tag team for the first time ever.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Against Jak and Seeker? Poorly.” DING DING DING! Violence and Hendricks met at the squared circle’s center, with Violence being only an inch taller, the two seemed evenly matched in appearance. J-Vi pushed off his opponent and then handed his gold rim aviators to Castellanos to dispose of, this sent Hendricks into a rage of disrespect. Jak grabbed Johnny by the shirt and yanked him back, tossing him to the canvas. Jak climbed onto his opponent with a knee bent one one side and the other stretched out to tower over him and get up if he needed without much effort. He held up his opponent’s head and began to punch away at him before being forced to break it off. He stepped to the right of J-Vi and gave him a few extra stomps to finish the message off. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “That’s my guy, go Jak!” J-Vi rolled onto his stomach and slowly pulled himself up to his feet and into Jak’s grasp. Jak hoisted his opponent up in a swift, devastating spinebuster and then covered him for the pin, but only reached to the count of two before a kick out broke it. He jabbed his opponent with another flurry of blows before pulling off in frustration. Jak swiped the referee’s arm away when being scolded on his aggressive assault. He was there to win, the referee wasn’t a wrestler and thus should mind his own business. He stood over his opponent for a moment in thought, then dragged him up by the skull and sent him across the ring with an Irish whip. Jak too went for the ropes to meet his opponent at the center of the ring, leaping toward him with arm extended and landed the lariat perfectly to drop Johnny down like a ton of bricks smack dab in the center of the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “I’ll go ahead and say it for you Oliver…Hendricks still has it.” The next time Jak approached and took hold of his opponent, Violence retaliated with a few punches of his own and broke free. He positioned away from the roped, Jak in his grasp and performed the German suplex, aptly named ‘The Harlot’ by himself. Jak was quickly on his feet with Johnny Violence and was catapulted into another Germal suplex. One more time, a third suplex and both men were strewn flat on the canvas with nothing but their heaving chests to show they were still breathing. Jak lay near his corner, and Johnny Violence near his. Slowly they turned and realized where they were and began to crawl toward their respective corners. The crowd was cheering and chanting and in an uproar as the suspense rose. Who would get the tag first? RILEY STEVENS: “Both men going for a tag!” Both men seemed to reach their partners at the same time, but Jak tried to climb the ropes first to each the tag, whereas Johnny lunged his body forward and connected in a hot tag with Alex Castellanos. Just as Alex hit the ring, Seeker got the tag and entered as well. Seth charged, Alex hopped up to the top turnbuckle and leapt downward onto his opponent with a diving crossbody. He rolled off and onto his feet to continue the hot assault. Seth was up fast as well and drove Alex’s momentum into the canvas with a countering clothesline. Seth yanked on Alex’s arm and tugged him up, then toward his corner to tag Jak back in for a double attack. The two exchanged hard chops across Alex’s chest, then stomped him into the bottom turnbuckle. Seth, before he returned to the rim of the ring, turned briefly, expecting the referee to mindlessly order him out of the ring, but instead it was Johnny Violence with a double arm DDT! Johnny slid out of the ring quick and returned to his tag corner whilst Seth slowly slumped out, hands covering his head where the skull connected with the canvas. RILEY STEVENS: “Johnny takes the big man down.” It was Alex Castellanos and Jak Hendriks in the ring now, Jak with the upper hand still. Jak set Alex up at the turn buckle, slack and clung to the ropes. He backed a few paces and charged, driving his boot with a Yakuza kick to Alex’s face. He followed up this attack with a pin attempt, but had only managed to reach to the count of 2 before Alex shoved his shoulders off the canvas. Jak pulled away and tagged his opponent back into the ring just as Seth had dragged himself up to the rim of the ring. Seth, still rubbing a hand against his temple, climbed into the ring and took hold of Alex before his opponent could reach his feet. He put his back to the turn post and Castellanos’ head between his knees. He hoisted him up for a powerbomb, but Alex shot a burst of energy downward in the form of a torrent of punches that freed him and gave him the momentum to whip Seth into the corner turn post, where J-Vi waited and recovered. RILEY STEVENS: “Seeker tries to recover.” Seth arched his back in pain for a moment and then walked out from the corner to get away from the danger zone he had placed Alex in. Unfortunate for him, Alex had charged and Seth walked right into a swinging neckbreaker. Alex covered, but the pin was broken by a stomp to the back from Jak. Jak retreated quickly before J-Vi could reach him. The damage was done. Both men were slow to their feet, each threw a stray punch to try to overcome the other, but the fight was match and they were exhausted. Something had to give, and someone had to give up. When they did reach their feet, Alex pushed Seth off a step and launched a standing dropkick and a kip up after to take the momentum in his favor. He slowly backed away to tag in his partner, only to see Seth quickly roll and tag in Jak Hendricks. Alex had no time for a tag, he and Jak met at the center of the ring with a lock up, a kick to the gut, and an elbow to the face. They broke off and Alex followed up with a snap Suplex. He jumped to his feet again and stepped back, against the ropes in wait. He held in time until Jak arose and with the face exposed, Castellanos drove the ‘Golden Arrow’ right to the chin of his opponent. This signaled J-Vi to drop from the ring and go for Seth whilst Alex pinned for a successful three count. JAMAL JACKSON: “Here are your winners…Johnny Violence and Alex Castellanos!” The fans go wild for the Alex win as he hops up stumbling backwards tired, this not an easy match at all as he barely squeaked it out. He rolls out of the ring patting Violence on the chest the two nodding heads seeming to have worked pretty well for their first time together. RILEY STEVENS: “Big win by Alex and Johnny tonight but Jak and Seth put up a good fight.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “You ever hear about luck? Well it just struck tonight.” Alex and Violence head towards the back as Jak and Seth get back up in the ring, watching them go angrily glaring at them as we take it to commercial. WINNER: ALEX CASTELLANOS & JOHNNY VIOLENCE After the tag match and the commercial we see Jak Hendricks and Seth Seeker heading to the back angrily barging through the halls as they head right towards both Seth and Beck Ramsey who are celebrating their victory from earlier tonight with NEXTWAVE~! Another tag team standing near by; Adrian and Rachel Tanner as Jak walks into them seemingly on purpose. JAK HENDRICKS: "Oh look if it isn’t the jobber tag teams they just signed this week. Real good job beating the Killers by the way, they are tough. Oh and you two, way to really take the initiative and get involved." Jak glares at both the other tag teams as Beck steps up for his tag team to go back and forth in the exchange. BECK RAMSEY: “That’s better then a loss right?” From there all hell breaks loose as the six all start to argue each finding something to talk about when Blake walks around the corner and into them with a smirk. BLAKE BECKETT: “Calm down, calm down.” This seems to stop everyone as they all turn their attention to the Boss who has a grin on his face. BLAKE BECKETT: “Been a long time since I have seen tag teams running around here like this. You three seem to want to prove who deserves the top spot? So…at ‘Malice’ it’ll be Jak Hendricks and Seth Seeker versus NextWave versus The Sin City Express…The winners will be GIW Tag Team Champions.” Blake grins and lets them go back to arguing as he walks off; Rachel and Adrian walk off leaving Jak, Seeker, and Beck to argue as we head to the ring. QUOTE The Plumber was first out to the ring. He made a quick entrance and slid in just as Marcus Hister’s theme cut his and played into the arena. He was slower than The Plumber, but didn’t take too long to enter the ring. He stuck to a corner and kept a cautious eye on his opponent and another occasionally on the aisle as the last opponent was still to enter. RILEY STEVENS: “Hister returned last week impressively and you’ve got to think this is his shot to really get up there in the ranks.” Their opponent, Assassin, was last to the ring. He entered via the steps and climbed into the three way match. The bell rang moments after with each men filling a corner with the exception of the back right corner post. RILEY STEVENS: “Last week Assassin had the victory well in hand but The Plumber stole it from him at the last second, he’ll want to rebound tonight.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “He’s a stud, he’ll get it done.” DING DING DING! The bout began with Assassin and Marcus giving one another a quick glance, a nod, and then the two ganged on The Plumber. The two drove their opponent into the turn buckles and shared chops across chest until they knew he had to be beet red in pain. Marcus drove a fist into the Plumber and Assassin stabbed his abdomen with the flat of his boot. The pulled away to let their opponent take a step forward and then crash into the canvas like a plane to Earth. The two had one more combined maneuver to pull off before they felt the Plumber was clear out of the picture. Assassin set up his opponent on his feet and used the ropes as momentum for a European uppercut. Marcus also used the ropes on the opposite end to then spear the Plumber after being forced around by the uppercut. Driven into the canvas, the Plumber was limp and beaten and then kicked out of the ring by both men so that they may now take one another out. RILEY STEVENS: “This has become a singles match with Plumber gone.” Marcus took another lunge for the ropes and as his opponent turned from kicking out Plumber, Marcus went for his finisher superkick, but Assassin saw it coming and ran his opponent over the top rope and into the barricade. Assassin then took himself out of the ring and drove a few punches to Marcus’ skull before throwing him back into the ring to continue the bout. Both men seemed to walk over the fallen Plumber as they moved about outside of the ring. Assassin had the upperhand and lead Marcus’ by the wrist toward one of the corners of the ring and pushed him, back first, into the buckles. He punched a few times and then bent him forward and hooked under both arms. He hoisted up his opponent and swung away from the buckles to tiger bomb Marcus into the canvas. He leaned forward with him still in grasp for a pin attempt, but Hister broke free. RILEY STEVENS: “These two are going at it.” Assassin drove a right into Hister’s skull and locked his grasp back in, this time hooking underneath his opponent’s hips and hoisted him up for a modified spinebuster. He went for a second pin attempt, but although Hister was slower to respond, he had still kicked out of the cover. Assassin had carried the match with Hister, but now he seemed to lose his temper and argued with the referee about the count. This incidentally gave Hister the chance to climb the ropes and pull up to his feet.
