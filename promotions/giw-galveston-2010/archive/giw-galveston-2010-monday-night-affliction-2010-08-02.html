GIW — Card

Monday Night Affliction, 2 August 2010

2 August 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Galveston Island Wrestling Presents: Monday Night Affliction! Live! From Stewart Beach on the Galveston Seawall! August 2, 2010 BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! Fireworks explode from the ring posts from the GIW arena on Stewart Beach as the waves come rolling in. The jam packed crowed of over two hundred people are gathered around the Affliction arena screaming. Signs that say, “Synn sucks!” and “Threat for President” are being held high by proud GIW fans. The camera pans around to show the madness on the beach, then finally settles on Carlos Castillo and Adam Calloway sitting in the commentary booth, they show is now live on My Network TV, the commentators begin to speak… running down tonight’s jam packed card. CARLOS CASTILLO: Hello there ladies and gents, welcome to one of the finest beaches here on Galveston Island, the privately owned… operated… and CLEAN beach, Stewart Beach! Right behind us you can see the world famous Sea Wall that keeps the waters of the Gulf from pouring over the island, but tonight they may need to keep the energy from the show from demolishing everything in its path. Tonight is JAM PACKED! We had one of the best Throttles to date, with Chicken Man winning the open battle royal, and Kevin Kiley and Aaron Snow demolishing each other, they may never compete here again in fact! You can view both of those matches on GIWrestling.com. But, lets stop with the chit chat and get to the main show… our opening contest today is a fatal four way match, with a special stipulation made by our GM, Amanda Rowland. The person who looses this match, or puts out a bad showing, will be to Throttle next week! ADAM CALLOWAY: That’s something none of these four competitors want. They all are trying to work up the ranks on the show, and a detour to Throttle would not be taken well by anyone involved. The match following that is a singles match featuring Chris Crawford and Jack Prowler, two guys who are looking for a victory over one another, and maybe push themselves higher up the card… something they both want. These are two of the most impressive young stars, and are certainly in the running for the North Beach title… who knows… we may be seeing the future king of North Beach in this match. CARLOS CASTILLO: That’s right, if you play your cards right and have a little bit of talent, you can go far here in Galveston Island Wrestling. In the next match we have one man who hasn’t exactly made the best choices since joining the company, Bret Battery… or as he goes by now, just Battery. After being accepted to Joshua Acquin’s stable a few weeks ago, Battery proceeded to quit, making an enemy out of everyone involved in the stable. A few days later, Battery wanted back in… so now he has to prove his worth, and maybe even pick up a win over the leaders brother, Austin Acquin. ADAM CALLOWAY: That sure would give him some credibility with the boss, Joshua Acquin. The match after that features two very impressive stars here in GIW. Our Hardcore Champion Jamie Vaccaro, and one of the most feared competitors here, Eric Sparrow. Eric Sparrow has been one of the most consistent members of GIW, but has been placed in awfully hard matches, contributing to his bad record. He is just as talent as Jamie Vaccaro, and now he gets the chance to prove that he is the man who should be the Hardcore Champion. It would really up his stock, a win over a champ is huge, regardless if it is a title match or not. CARLOS CASTILLO: That’s right Adam, and an instant credibility maker is a victory in the main event of the evening, something both Damon Synn and Ryan Daniels could use. Last week in a six man tag match, Ryan Daniels cost Synn the match… giving Synn his first loss since his debut in the Undisputed Wrestling Alliance many years ago. Damon Synn is not pleased, and asked Commissioner and Board of Directors member, Megathreat, for a match against Ryan Daniels, and it was granted. Both competitors will be thrown into the spotlight, who will falter and who will gleam? ADAM CALLOWAY: Enough with the talking though, Carlos. Its time to start the Fatal Four Way Match to determine who will be deported back to Throttle to work on their game. These competitors really don’t want that, so we should be seeing a great match here on Monday Night Affliction, lets start the show! The camera switches over to the ring, and the contestants begin to enter. We head to the ring where we have a full ring for the opening bout’ of the night. In the ring at this time on one side is Model C-22, with Jack Johnson are new Galveston Island North Beach Champion. The two are side by side and one can easily tell they’ll be working together. Damu Akpu-nku is standing in the corner looking poised and ready while Izzie Dead sits in the corner as we are getting ready for our match. Adam Calloway: What a way to kick off the show then to have four competitors do battle. Carlos Castillo: Maybe have four good competitors do battle? Adam Calloway: That was just cold. Jack Johnson is our North Beach Champion, for god’s sake. Though one has to wonder if his robot Model C-22 will be on his side. The two can be seen planning out for there match while Damu screams at the top of his lungs. Izzie looks out at Ima Goner at ringside then looks back at Damu as the referee starts the match. DING DING DING! Jack Johnson quickly slides out of the ring before even starting the match allowing his robot to start the battle. Damu screams at both the robot and then the zombie, running at Izzie Dead. Dead ducks the clothesline and then drop toe hold’s Damu straight into the turnbuckle. The fans are stunned as Izzie has just taken out the beast but Model C-22 comes from behind, grabbing him and tossing him off the ropes. On the return Izzie jumps up for a clothesline but Model C-22 proves to be stronger then you’d imagine by catching him and dumping him out of the ring. Jack Johnson returns to the ring commanding C-22, to go after Damu with him. They both start to stomp a hole in Damu’ as the fans boo there teaming. Damu starts to battle his way up causing Jack to bail out of the ring while Damu lifts Model C-22 into the air, and tosses it out of the ring at Jack. Johnson gets out of the way but Model C-22 falls to the ground outside hard. Back in the ring Damu seems poised to get back into this thing but doesn’t notice Izzie Dead sliding back into the ring. He runs across the ring going for a Shambling Lariat. But instead he only stuns Damu momentarily. He runs off and goes for another and this time he gets the exact same result. Finally he realizes what he must do and hits a Shambling Wheel Kick that drops him down to a knee. He runs off the ropes and hits a Running Dropkick that sends Damu flying out of the ring hard. Adam Calloway: Quite the impressive flurry from Izzie. The crowd is giving Dead there full support here tonight clearly he is the fan favorite around here. Izzie is looking out at Damu when the always sneaky Jack Johnson gets in behind him, and when Dead turns around towards him he is picked up into a Scoop Slam. Jack smirks as he feels that he’s getting the momentum, here. Izzie hops back up only to run right into a ‘Back-2-Backbreaker’ a Randy Orton style Backbreaker. Dead holds his back in pain as Jack realizes that he can end it right here and right now with a ‘Cashbomb’. He’s calling for it when Damu who has returned to the ring, spins him around. And instead of getting the advantage Jack hits a ‘Priceless Neckbreaker’ a Stunner that does just that to Damu. As he is stunned he locks in the ‘Cashmission’ a Half Nelson Choke and Leglock! The fans realize that this move has made others tap. But instead Izzie has gotten back to his feet and breaks the hold. Damu rolls out of the ring basically down for the count. Izzie takes a big swing at Jack but he ducks it and then locks his arms up. Model C-22 has returned to the ring and with Jack holding him, commands it to hit Dead. It goes for the ‘Tech Slap’ an extremely hard woman’s slap but Izzie ducks it and she hits Jack instead. The fans are a bit stunned as Jack falls back through the ropes and out of the ring. Damu has gotten up and slides back in still unaware where he is. Izzie spits ‘Red Mist’ at Model C-22 temporarily messing with its camera or vision whatever. It instead hits another ‘Tech Slap’ on Damu before being tossed out of the ring by Izzie onto Jack. With Damu stunned Izzie catches him with a ‘BOOM HEADDROP!’ a Tiger Driver 91! He’s down for the count but first Izzie adds insult to injury by hitting the ‘Zombie's Elbow’ a Feint Legdrop Elbowdrop! Dead gets down for the pin 1…2…3! Winner~! Adam Calloway: What a match by Izzie Dead tonight. Beat our ‘North Beach Champion’, Model C-22, and the big man Damu. You got to give it to him, he did great. Carlos Castillo: He’s a damn zombie for goodness sake. Izzie Dead has his arm raised by the referee as Ima Goner hops in and celebrates with him, after another win. Outside of the ring Jack Johnson is handed back his belt as he screams at Model C-22 unhappy with its shot at him and the fact that they lost, as he heads to the back. One set of weirdo’s happy, the other not so much. After the match is over, the crowd begins to settle down. The My Network TV Cameras switch over the makeshift locker rooms at the Sandpiper Motel, right across from Stewart Beach. Outside one of the locker rooms, Ryan Daniels is standing and looking nervous. His eyes dart back and forth, not wanting anyone to see what he is up to. He knocks on the door three times with a quick pace, and looks around him. The door slowly slides open, and standing in the doorway is Jamie Vaccaro, who greats Daniels with a handshake and a smile. He motions for Daniels to follow him into his room. The camera man tries to enter the room, but Jamie slams the door in his face. CARLOS CASTILLO: Vaccaro and Daniels? They cant be up to any good. Chris Armstrong and Jack Prowler are already in the ring and are both individually introduced by the announcer. They both stare each other down and see each other as an obstacle that need to go over in order to further their careers. The bell sounds and both men begin to circle the ring almost like lions waiting to pounce on their prey and to feed on them. Jack Prowler quickly hit’s a blow to the back of Chris Armstrong’s back. Chris Armstrong yells in pain. Jack Prowler hit’s a couple oh chops across the chest of Chris Armstrong. Adam Calloway: Looks like this match is fast paced and will end rather quick. Carlos Castillo: You're right about that. As the referee is distracted trying to get Jack Prowler to move back, Chris Armstrong hit’s a low blow on Jack Prowler. Chris goes for the pin. 1...2... Kick out! Chris picks up Jack and puts him in a chokehold. However, Jack Prowler counters it into a back suplex which makes Chris Armstrong yell loud due to the pain. Jack Prowler starts stomping at Chris and is again held back by the referee. Chris Armstrong reaches into his pocket and looks like he’s going to hit Jack Prowler with something. The referee jumps Armstrong and restrains him but it’s too late, Chris has taken out….MONEY? The referee apologizes, gets off Chris. Chris starts mocking Jack about how much richer he is. Jack shakes his head and rolls up Chris. 1...2...3! Jack Prowler laughs and then runs to the back as Chris Armstrong hit’s the floor, pretty angry after what happened. We come back from break and currently on his way to the ring already to the theme of "Nightmare" by A7X. The fans have come to hate this man, a lot. And give him boos especially as a Special Referee, a role we have come to realize he can’t be trusted in. As he enters the ring next up is his brother as "Re-Education" by Rise Against hits and out next actually set to wrestle is Austin Acquin. The fans give it to him just like they did his brother as he comes out with such a similar swagger to his brother. Carlos Castillo: Here are my guys, the Acquin’s. Taking this place over! Adam Calloway: Quite frankly these guys haven’t impressed me one bit. All talk no walk. They have yet to prove themselves in the ring, though with his brother as referee Austin should be sitting pretty. Austin joins his brother in the ring, walking over to him and shaking his head then turning there attention to the stage where the man who has been vying to impress them. He heads out to the ring with a big smirk on his face as he heads towards the ring. All three man have the same bit of arrogance to them. And one has to wonder if Joshua is trying to help his brother or actually see if Bret can get in. Joshua looks at both men with a glare then calls for the bell. DING DING DING! The fans are not even really sure what to do here. I mean they don’t like Bret Battery, they don’t like Austin Acquin, and they damn sure dislike Joshua Acquin. Other then hoping that someone comes in and beats them up, we have no idea what can happen here. Austin smirks over at his brother with a grin, before Battery runs across and tackles him to the mat. Battery begins to unload on Austin with rights and lefts. Joshua however quickly tears him off of his brother, not giving Bret a count at all. Battery gets up into Joshua’s face telling him off about what he just did. While he’s distracted Austin gets up and tries for a School Boy that Joshua gets down for and counts super fast 1..2..Kick Out! Even with a fast count it was just not enough yet. Austin groans and pulls Bret to his feet, whipping him off the ropes. On the return he connects with a High Knee Strike. The fans seem to be leaning towards Bret now, just because of the unfair situation he is in. Austin high fives his brother before turning his attention back to Bret who is on the mat. He bounces off the rope and connects with a Jumping Knee Drop, further assaulting Bret’s face. Austin tells his brother that he’s ready to pin, but Bret knows he has no time to waste and struggles not allowing his shoulders to touch. Finally Austin gets up angrily and starts to call for a ‘Facebreaker’ aka a Killswitch while waiting for him to get up. As he sets it up Bret rolls out of it and hits a big Spinebuster! Finally some offense from Bret. Adam Calloway: What a move by Bret, finally he gets some offense in. Battery looks over at Joshua not sure what he’ll do if he’ll even make the pin. Right now he is watching as his brother uses the ropes to get to his feet. Battery runs across the ring full steam and hits a big Clothesline. The fans are stunned as he goes for the pin and surprisingly Joshua gets down to make the count 1…2…Kick Out! Bret gets up eyeing Joshua surprised he’d even make the count. As Austin struggles to his knees Bret runs off the ropes and connects with a Knee Facebuster sending Austin up and down to the mat hard. Battery sets up for a Figure Four Leglock, hoping to make Acquin tap out for the world to impress Joshua. He locks it in and does so tightly as Austin begins to wince in pain. He screams at his brother for help but surprisingly again Joshua seems to want to see his younger brother fight out of it. Now realizing he doesn’t have his brother’s help, he crawls towards the ropes. Finally he reaches out and grabs a hold of them, as Joshua begins to count 1…2…3…and Bret releases the hold. Battery rolls up to his feet as Austin holds his legs in pain. Joshua gets down on his knee to check on Austin while Bret calls for him to get up. After a moment Austin gets to his feet and out of no where both Acquin’s go after Bret. Both get him to the mat and stump him before Joshua grabs his arms and tells Austin to finish him off. Austin sets up in the corner then runs off the ropes goes for an ‘Impaler’ a Spear. But Bret dodges it and Austin instead hits Joshua! With Joshua down and out Bret lifts Austin into the air and hits a F5! Almost as if they had it ready a referee comes down to the ring to fill in for Joshua. The referee makes the count for Bret 1…2…3! Bret won it, thanks to a replacement referee. Adam Calloway: I may not like Bret due to the way he acts but there’s no doubt he overcame the odds tonight, he had to have impressed Joshua tonight. Carlos Castillo: Joshua is the damn referee, can’t have some replacement make the call. I’m not sure Battery did himself any good, just embarrassed the Acquin’s out here. Adam Calloway: I think it should be Austin that Joshua is really mad at. I may not like the team but I think Bret just earned his way in. The fans boo them all not caring about this right now, hating all three. Joshua has returned to the ring holding his gut while Battery is heading up the ramp. Joshua glares up at him and then down at his fallen brother, his reaction at this time unreadable. What will happen now? "Fat Lip" by Sum 41 hit the speakers and the crowd showed some respect, albeit a very mixed reaction, as Eric Sparrow made his way out to the ring. Sparrow's music faded out and was replaced by the sounds of "All I Do is Win" by DJ Khaled. The mixed reaction IMMEDIATELY changed to a loud chorus of boos as Jamie "The ShowStealer" Vaccaro made his way out to the ring. With both competitors in the ring, the referee checked both men for hidden weapons. Adam Calloway: This should be a great match-up tonight. These men wrestled on Revolt a few weeks ago with Vaccaro coming out on top in that contest. You've gotta believe Sparrow is looking to make a statement here tonight. Carlos Castillo: He's definitely looking to make one, but whether he accomplishes that is the real question. The referee called for the bell and both men started to circle each other as the fans reached a fever pitch. Both men collided in the middle of the ring with a collar-and-elbow tie-up. Sparrow, being the smaller of the two, ducked underneath Jamie's right arm and went behind his back into a waistlock. Vaccaro threw a back elbow at Sparrow's head, which completely missed, as Eric moved to the front side of Vaccaro and caught him with a thunderous step-up Enziguiri as he turned around. Sparrow made a quick cover, but barely got a two count out of it. Sparrow wasted little time and quickly ran into the ropes as Vaccaro got back to a seated position. Sparrow came back and went for a missile dropkick, but Vaccaro laid flat out on his back and watched Eric sail over the top of him. As Sparrow came down, he landed on top of Jamie, who immediately locked his arms around the much smaller man. The ShowStealer rolled over, still holding onto Eric Sparrow, and then slowly but surely stood up, now carrying Sparrow around the ring with him. Adam Calloway: What a phenomenal display of brute power there by Jamie Vaccaro! Carlos Castillo: It's not really that impressive, Adam. That vato weighs maybe a buck-oh-five soaking wet after a big dinner. Jamie carried Eric around the ring for a few seconds before driving him down to the canvas with a devastating running powerslam. Vaccaro covered Sparrow, but Eric was able to kick out. Jamie pulled Eric back up to his feet and irish whipped him into the corner with authority, causing Sparrow to hit the turnbuckles chest-first! Sparrow stumbled backwards out of the corner and turned around just in time to be DEMOLISHED with the Ain't That a Kick in the Head (Running Yakuza Kick)! The crowd groaned loudly at what they had just seen as Vaccaro moved across the ring to the opposite corner. Jamie started slapping his right elbow as the crowd booed wildly. Sparrow struggled mightily to get back to his feet as Jamie loaded up the elbow. Eric finally got back to a vertical base and turned around just in time to DUCK THE KTFO!! Vaccaro couldn't believe it, but had no time to be shocked, as Sparrow started peppering him with vicious lefts and rights. Sparrow rocked Vaccaro all the way back into the corner and the crowd was going wild. Adam Calloway: Would you listen to this reaction for Eric Sparrow?! What a testament to his heart and determination! Carlos Castillo: I think it's actually more of a testament to the crowd's hatred for Vaccaro. Sparrow pulled Vaccaro out of the corner and irish-whipped him into the ropes. Sparrow went for a leaping clothesline, but completely missed as Vaccaro duck and ran under him. Sparrow turned around looking for Jamie and found him...more specifically his right elbow, as Jamie hit the KTFO (Rolling Elbow)!! Sparrow falls to the outside. Suddenly, Austin Acquin comes and starts attacking Sparrow, getting Jamie disqualified. Jamie , in a frustrated manner, gets a steel chair and hits Austin Acquin generating a huge reaction from the crowd! He hits Acquin several times until Acquin runs away to the back. Jamie raises the chair in the air. Sparrow is up now, dazed. Jamie hits Sparrow square on the head, causing him to bleed and leaves. Winner: Eric Sparrow via DQ. Suddenly, the camera becomes fixed on the locker room backstage. Jack Prowler, sweaty and seemingly tired is sitting on a bench removing his wristguards and drinking a bottle of water. Again, Brooke Ellis is by his side holding a microphone. This time however, Brooke actually has a pretzel in hand even though it is half eaten. After swallowing a bite of pretzel, she turns to the camera. It seems she was completely unaware of the camera recording her eating. After looking suprised, she tosses the pretzel off in the distance. Jack looks p from his bottle of water, watching the pretzel fly across the locker room. Brooke initiates the interview acting oblivious to the situation. Brook Ellis "At this time, will you please welcome my guest, Jack Prowler!" In the stadium, the crowd cheers unrestrained. Jack looks down to the ground to hide a smile. Brooke turns to her left, continuing her interview. She begins to twirl her hair. Brook Ellis "Now Jack, earlier tonight you defeated Chris Armstrong. Knowing the influence he has, do you think he'll try and buy the victory from management? And if so, will you try and stop him?" The Mongoose takes a breath mulling over the question. It appears he has been thnking about this quite a bit. Jack Prowler "You know, I've been thinking about this Brooke, and it seems likely that he might do that. And if he does.. I'll ask management for a rematch. And I'll beat him.. again. And if he buys that victory, then I'll have another rematch against him. But I have faith in Galvestons management that they won't take that bait." Prowler crosses his arms and looks at Brooke, who seems to be in a trance, twirling her hair endlessly. Jack sighs at Brooke, growing tired of her obsession. Brooke comes to suddenly and continues. Brook Ellis "Speaking of management, rumor has it that since you won your match,you have in fact gained favor with the big dogs. Where do you think you'll be going next Mr. Prowler? Willl you use this so-called 'favor' to cash in some upsetting matches?" Jack wipes his hand across his chin, then seats his hands at his hips. He gives a second of silence and then moves closer to the microphone to answer. Jack Prowler "Brooke, I don't see myself 'cashing' in any favors to cheat my way through the system; I'm not going to take anything I haven't earned. But that doesnt mean I won't use anything I have earned. If I have earned 'favor', I'd like to go to the next step. If Jack Johnson wants to cheat his way through, I will end his championship reign. But ultimately I want to get myself in somewhere for the NO.1 contender for the Affliction Championship." Brooke blinks a few times, almost stunned at The Mongoose's plan for the future. Brook Ellis "You mean you want to face Damon Synn for the Affliction Championship? Do you really think management will pull that out for you?! Do you even know who Damon Synn is!?" Jack seems to become irritated. “Wait and Bleed, by Slipknot” plays throughout the arena. Damon Synn comes through the curtain and looks like he’s about to murder someone. Synn cockily walks down the ring and goes inside. The referee starts checking him for weapons but Synn scares the referee away by lifting his arm. Synn starts jumping up and down which shakes the ring due to his massive body moving around. He hands the belt to the ring keeper who is reluctant to take it from him in the first place. Adam Calloway: Synn is such a scary individual. He looks like a serial killer. “Pursuit of Happiness by Kid Cudi” blasts through the speakers. Ryan Daniels looks pumped up. He has a steel chair in hand. He runs into the ring and then runs up to Synn, expecting him to be scared. The bell rings. Synn grabs Ryan by the head and literally throws him to the outside, dropping the steel chair somewhere in the audience in the process. Adam Calloway: Poor Ryan. He’s essentially a walking corpse. Compare him to Synn and see the size difference, no way in hell this match is going to even last two minutes. Carlos Castillo: Well I think you may be right but Ryan Daniels…he has to step up! Synn starts laughing at Ryan’s pathetic attempts to try and actually hurt him. Ryan enters the ring again and looks at Synn right in the eye. Ryan starts talking to Synn. Synn looks like he’s just there for the hell of it and doesn’t appear to care. Synn looks away but Ryan Daniels slaps Synn in the face, catching him by surprise. Synn wasn’t hurt but shocked at the guts of Ryan Daniels. Ryan Daniels continues to speak and eventually starts losing his mind, yelling at Synn. Synn swings a punch and Ryan Daniels slaps him again. Synn swings another punch and Ryan Daniels hit’s a drop kick sending Synn bouncing off the ropes! Synn bounces off and tries to hit a clothesline that could have possibly killed any normal man. Luckily, Ryan dodges it. Ryan hit’s a rolling kick that sends Synn to the outside. Synn quickly gets up. Ryan bounces off the ropes and hit’s a suicide dive. However, even though the suicide dive hit, Ryan did more damage to himself than Synn. Synn didn’t even fall down. Synn picks up Ryan and with one hand dumps him into the ring from the outside. Carlos Castillo: Ryan Daniels is fighting with all his might but Synn is absolutely too much, what a damn monster. Synn enters the ring. Ryan quickly hit’s a hard kick to the head of Synn! Synn falls! Synn falls! Synn has fallen and is now on his knees! Ryan Daniels goes for The Beefy which is essentially a power bomb! He somehow picks up Synn with all his might but fails to execute the maneuver due to Synn being too heavy. Synn gets up slowly and then tries to hit a big boot on Daniels. Daniels dodges it and pushes Synn with all his strength, having him bounce off the ropes. Ryan then bounces off the ropes himself and hit’s the Meaty, a low angled shoulder block! However, it’s as if Ryan has crashed with a brick wall. Synn remains intact. Synn picks up Ryan Daniels and then hit’s a one armed choke slam that nearly stops the heart of poor Ryan Daniels. Synn picks up Ryan and ridicules him for even trying. Synn tries to hit The Beefy, a power bomb which Ryan uses to stop his opponents but Ryan knows this move all too well and counters it into a hurricarana! Synn is down again! Synn is down again! Ryan bounces off the ropes and looks to hit a manuever of sorts but Synn lifts him like a toddler into the gorrila press then hit’s a tombstone pile driver as Ryan’s helpless body spinned in the air. Jamie Vacarro comes in with a steel chair. 1...2...3! WHAM! Jamie has hit Synn with a steel chair. Luckily for Synn, he got the pin. Jamie starts an assault on Synn. After two minutes, Ryan stands up hardly able to coordinate himself and starts attacking Synn as well. The show ends with Synn on the ground, not bleeding but bruised as Jamie lifts the hand of Ryan Daniels who gets a standing ovation for his effort. Carlos Castillo: Well, I can’t say that beat down was fair but Jamie has made his statement. He wants in on the world title and Ryan Daniels has proved to us that he has the heart of a champion and is not to be underestimated. I just hope he gets medical attention, he seems to be hurting. Winner - Synn via Pin.
