GIW — Pay-per-view

Exile 2012, 1 April 2012

1 April 2012

GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS EXILE!! LIVE on iPPV, from the New Orleans Arena in New Orleans, Louisiana – April 1, 2012! BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! The show starts up and the crowd is already in their seats ready to go as the camera pans around them all as the opening tune for ‘Exile’ plays through the arena as the fans that paid for this iPPV watch from home get ready to go as this 9,000 person capacity crowd seems almost completely full as the camera flies all around the place until some boo’s start to be heard as David Sinclair already stands in the ring with a microphone in hand and a smile on his face. DAVID SINCLAIR: “Calm down, calm down. Last month at ‘Retaliation’ I told you all that GIW was going to continue to grow bigger and stronger. And look at where we stand right now? The New Orleans Arena the home of the New Orleans Hornets, a place where such stars as Katy Perry, Britney Spears, Usher, and Journey have all held concerts. This crowd tonight you will be proud to hear is the biggest in GIW history.” Sinclair smiles as he lowers the mic as the fans though they hate him are forced to cheer realizing they are a part of history. DAVID SINCLAIR: “But soon we will get even bigger and this record will surely be broken. I know that you fans think of me as a bad guy because I have some different ways of doing business but look at what GIW has become? The company is at an all time high. We are growing faster then anyone ever figured we could. But we are not stopping there...Last year GIW took a show to Mexico for not only...Well we are going even farther this time, cute the video.” Sinclair lowers the microphone as all the fans turn their attention to the monitor as a video begins to play. The video comes to an end as the fans give a few claps for the huge announcement for GIW but not huge for the New Orleans area per say. Sinclair has a smirk on his face as he looks back out at the crowd. DAVID SINCLAIR: “London, England. Home of Alioth Starre and several of GIW’s finest. See you in a few weeks time for that but tonight we got an even bigger show, enjoy the night that I made for you.” Sinclair walks towards the exit of the ring as the fans boo him anyways, as he heads up the ramp we head over to the announce table with Riley and Oliver. RILEY STEVENS: “What a great night we have for you here in New Orleans. The GIW Undisputed Championship will not even be defended here tonight and we still have one of the biggest shows ever in store for you all. From top to bottom it is a night of great matches, starting with the Open Battle Royal as per usual and then transitioning to a great contest between Alex Castellanos and Ryan Cavallari. After that one we go into a triple threat match that will determine the next contender for the GIW Legacy Championship. Competing for that shot will be both members of DefiaStarr; Daniel Starr and Paul Defias who will have to fight each other if they want that shot. The third member of that contest will be Hayden Carter best known for her upset of Alioth Starre.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Her one and only win, luck.” RILEY STEVENS: “But things only get bigger from there as the GIW Television Champion, Henry Malkin will compete with number one contender Angel Vegnaduzzo in a match that many consider to be the match that gets that belt back on the right track. After that one thought you might want to take your kids out of the room for a couple minutes as bitter rivals Scar and Tony Miranda take the next step in their battle as they end their rivalry once and for all when they meet in a Last Man Standing match to determine who the better man really is.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “That match might last all night.” RILEY STEVENS: “Following that contest we will get a Tables Ladder and Chairs match between Alexis Terry and Beck Ramsey for the GIW Legacy Championship. These two wrestled to a draw last month but with tables…ladders…chairs all around the ring and only one belt hanging above the ring not only will we find our champion but these two will put an end to their long rivalry.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Alexis Terry will march into England with her belt.” RILEY STEVENS: “That match would usually be enough for everyone to call it a night, but that match will only be the second to last as immediately following that we will have Alioth Starre and Matt Kluge set to fight for a future shot at Aspen Chaud but the stakes are truly so much higher then that. These two truly do not like one another and they truly want to prove they are the better wrestler. Alioth Starre comes in as a man that has held the GIW Undisputed Championship longer then any other wrestler. The belt has eluded Matt Kluge but once could say that he has deserved a shot all along. So while it may not be for the title this match could truly determine who the best wrestler in GIW history really is,” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Matt Kluge.” RILEY STEVENS: “Let’s take it to the ring for the first match up.” With that the camera pans out to the ring where it is starting to fill up for the Open Battle Royal. QUOTE After a few hype videos for later matches we head back for the Open Battle Royal where we have four participants spread out in three corners of the ring for the Open Battle Royal. JAMAL JACKSON: “Please welcome your Open Battle Royal participants Maria Sortiris, Faith Simpson, Darius Drake, and Big J!” DING DING DING! The bell rings and the bout is started with a ceremonious stare down between the competitors in the Exile open battle royal. Maria Sortiris look deadly into the eyes of the fellow female competitor in the match, the returning Faith Simpson. The men also stare daggers into each other’s eyes. The Action begins with a joint lock up between the females, and the males. Simpson and Big J get the advantage over their respective competitors. Big J takes Dark to the ground with a rough hip toss; Simpson on the other hand has Sortiris locked in a tight side head lock. RILEY STEVENS: “This is a chance for all three competitors to prove something.” Big J goes to work on Drake, kicking him into the corner, while Drake still grounded is looking for an opening to slip through. Simpson has taken to throwing a few strikes at Maria’s exposed head , however Sortiris slips out of the headlock and throws a quick book to the back of Faith Simpson who is pushed forward into the back of an attacking Big J. Big J turns slowly around facing Faith with a unwelcoming scowl across his face. Deeming that Faith was trying to eliminate him, Big J slowly walks Faith to the other side of the ring, Faith backs away with her hand held innocently trying to explain the situation. Big J shoves Faith into the turnbuckle, putting both arms outwards, boxing her in the corner. RILEY STEVENS: “All four really look impressive tonight.” Sortiris barking instructions while also taking a breather, rapidly motions for Big J to strike Faith. Faith has her hands up cowering in the corner, Big J reads to throw a punch at the defenseless Simpson, until Big J was thrust forward from a dropkick to the back from the forgotten about Darius Drake. The fans cheer for Drake, however as Big J was pushed forward into the ring, he collided and crushed Faith into the turnbuckle. Faith grounded crawled her way from a grounded Big J, however only into the clutches of a fresh Maria Sortiris. Sortiris angrily kicks at Faith while taunting her at the same. Sortiris takes a moment to pose for the audience, garnering a heated reaction from the crowd. She then ran towards the other side of the ring, Maria rebounded from the ropes and began rushing at the grounded Faith Simpson, however she was caught in mid stride by Darius Drake, who tugged her hair back, sending Sortiris down to the canvas. RILEY STEVENS: “This Darius Drake has a bright future here.” The fans cheered for Drake as he slowly lifted Sortiris up from the mat, still clutching her hair. He walks Sortiris to the ropes who is trying to claw and slap at him. Faith who was recovering threw a couple of shots at Maria’s exposed side. Maria looked hurt, and Darius teased the crowd asking them if they wanted to send her over the top. However before getting the chance, Big J who was back up and well rested, lunged out at Darius. However Faith was able to push Drake away at the last second, leaving no one for Big J to collide with than Sortiris. Big J shoulder checked her, sending her head over heels toppling over the top rope and onto the hard floor. Maria Sortiris is Eliminated! The crowd cheered as one was down, with two more left to be eliminated. Drake and Faith gave a head nod to each other, and the both began kicking and punching at the larger opponent in Big J. The double teamed him, going for a double suplex to send him over the rope. However Big J fought his way out, and landed a DDT on both Faith and Darius… Big J stood the lone man standing at the moment, as he smiled at his handiwork, before pacing around the ring with his arms high in the air, as if he had been victorious. Big J wet to the opposite end of the ring, and began to stake Darius and Faith, goading them to get up. Big J seemed to be reading to send them both over the top rope in a double clothes line. Faith and Drake made it to their feet, still appearing to be wobbly. Big J then let out a loud yell, as he rushed at Faith and Drake. Faith and Drake ducked over the outstretched arms of Big J. Big J’s momentum sent his upper body hanging over the rope, the fans cheered in anticipation for an elimination as Faith and Drake both grabbed a leg and flipped Big J out of the ring, and out of the match as he landed to the mat below. Big J is Eliminated! With only two competitors left, the fans got on their feet and cheered. A tired and winded Faith and Darius exchanged. Hand shakes in mutual respect. However being the man to quickly try and win the match, Darius kept hold of Faith hand and attempted to Irish whip her over the ropes. However a smart and attentive Faith recognized this, and used her own body strength to reverse the Irish whip into one of her own. Darius was caught off guard which meant sending him over a lot easier. However Darius landed on the apron on the other side of the ring, still in the match. Faith rushed to finish him, by pushing him over, however Darius responded with a quick tie up. Darius looked to suplex faith to the cold hard ground below, however as he lifted Faith swung her legs in a swift but hard kick to the mid section of Darius. He was off balance and was easily beaten by Faith who, gave him a dropkick to the stomach to send him out. Darius Drake is Eliminated! JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…FAITH SIMPSON!” The fans in the building go wild as Faith hops up to her feet with a big smile on her face getting a big win here at ‘Exile’ tonight as Drake got up on the outside just barely losing tonight in a great opening contest, as he slid back into the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Never count Faith Simpson out, that’s for sure.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Great…She is back.” Drake slid back in seeming a lit but disgruntled but he reached out and shook her hand regardless as he raised her arm in victory both at least sharing a mutual respect. WINNER: FAITH SIMPSON After all is said and done with the Open Battle Royal “Did it On Em’” by Nicki Minaj hits as the fans begin to boo furiously as the champion walks out with a smile on her face wearing a red dress and for the first time in awhile no boot on her ankle as the crowd doesn’t let up with the boos as she stops at the top of the ramp with her Undisputed belt over her shoulder. ASPEN CHAUD: “I know, I know. You are a little mad about the fact that you all do not get to see yours truly defend my belt. I myself am pretty sad about it as well; I mean I hate missing a single week of wrestling. But you know what? As of…tomorrow I will be fully healed from my ankle injury and will be cleared once again to wrestle here in GIW.” Aspen smiles nodding her head as this only gets an angry reaction from the crowd who do not stop booing her the entire time. ASPEN CHAUD: “It’s ok I understand that you are all a little sad that you don’t get to see the best professional wrestler in the world do her thing in the ring. But fear not tonight you will get the next best thing, Alexis Terry defending her Legacy championship! Not to mention that great match between Matt Kluge and Alioth Starre in the main event where surely they will destroy each other…So badly that neither will ever be the same again.” Aspen chuckles as she strolls around the top of the ramp just letting the fans get all their boo’s out. ASPEN CHAUD: “So you know what? I will let you guys enjoy the show the best you can without me. But next Revolt I will be back! One time let’s hear it for me! On three, let’s say it GOOOOOO ASPEN! One…Two…Three!” Not a single person in the building does it as Aspen smiles dropping the microphone but the sound of it hitting the ground is drowned out by the boo’s of the crowd as she waves to them as she heads to the back. QUOTE As we return to the ring it is time for our second bout of the night a classic, win to impress match. The match was not for any contendership but you had to think a win would get them very close. “The House of the Rising Sun” by The Animals hits and the fans begin to boo as the ever so self loathing Alex Castellanos walks out from the back heading towards the ring. JAMAL JACKSON: “The following contest is scheduled for one fall! Introducing first weighing in at two hundred ten pounds, standing six foot one inches tall…from Cardiff, Wales…ALEX CASTELLANOS!” RILEY STEVENS: “You know we have seen a complete change in Alex Castellanos demeanor the past week and I don’t like it one bit.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “He realized that nice guys finish last.” Alex heads past the fans smirking as he heads up the steps into the ring as his music trails off and “Brand New Day” by Ryan Star hits and the fans pop as the recently debuting Ryan Cavallari heads out from the back with a big smile on his face he has quickly gone two and oh and also earned some love from these fans. JAMAL JACKSON: “And introducing his opponent weighing in at two hundred and twenty pounds, standing five foot eleven inches tall…from Los Angeles, California…RYAN CAVALLARI!” RILEY STEVENS: “This guy is a true prospect, he has a chance to put down a veteran and show us all how good he really is.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Hate him, Alex will destroy him.” Ryan high fives a few fans then slides into the ring as Castellanos watches him the entire time prepping up in the corner as Cavallari heads to his corner using the ropes to stretch out, turning around as the ref calls for the bell. DING DING DING! The two competitors meet in the center of the ring as the bell sounds. The crowd is firmly behind Ryan, which causes Alex to take notice. The two lock up and it’s Ryan with the upper hand. He sends Alex into the ropes, Alex comes back and attempts a clothesline but Ryan ducks. Alex turns around and gets a mouth full of Ryan’s feet thanks to a beautiful springboard high kick. Castellanos crashes to the mat. Ryan wastes no time, quickly picking Alex up and delivering a snap Suplex. RILEY STEVENS: “This Ryan kid is undefeated thus far into his GIW career.” He covers but only gets a two. Once again, Ryan picks up his fallen opponent off the mat, only this time Alex pushes Ryan backwards. Alex rushes Ryan and throws a wild forearm but Cavallari ducks once more just like earlier in the bout. Ryan hits dropkick to the knee of Alex, which causes him crumble in pain down to a kneeling position. Ryan is in position and connects with “A Heartless Moment” otherwise known as a devastating snap DDT. Ryan covers and picks up the win with the three count in about ninety seconds. JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…RYAN CAVALLARI!” The fans go wild as Ryan gets up with a smile on his face, getting yet another win here in GIW as the fans clap him on as Alex rolls out of the ring in pain. RILEY STEVENS: “Ryan Cavallari is for real he just destroyed Alex Castellanos a former contender, this guy has a bright future here in GIW.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “What is going on today? This best not keep up.” Cavallari heads to the top rope to pose as we are left to wonder how far this guy can go, and if he can keep this winning streak alive. WINNER: RYAN CAVALLARI Heading to the backstage area for the first time tonight we find ourselves looking at Jacob O’Connelly standing in a set up area where the GIW Tag Team Championships are set up right behind him. JACOB O’CONNELLY: “These Tag Team titles have had a long history around here from the glory days of High Voltage and Perfect Combination, through the Matt Kluge era and all the way up to the Sin City Express. But it’s time that we found champions that we could truly be proud of; these belts will be restored over the upcoming weeks on Revolt with a tournament to crown new champions. If you don’t have a partner yet, you should get one because soon we will have NEW Tag Team champions.” Jacob says this with a smile as Beck Ramsey walks by and as he sees him he looks the other way but Ramsey sees this so he walks on over to him patting him on the shoulder. BECK RAMSEY: “So…The belts are getting brought back eh? You know I don’t recall ever losing them.” O’Connelly seemed to fear this a bit as he nods his head. JACOB O’CONNELLY: “Don’t shoot the messenger.” Beck smiles as he walks over to the Tag belts grabbing one up then setting it back down. BECK RAMSEY: “You tell David Sinclair that I want a spot in that tourney, I deserve it.” Ramsey walks by patting him again as Jacob nods his head in agreement as the show goes on. QUOTE It is now time for the next contest here tonight this one for a shot at the GIW Legacy Championship. "Earthquake" by Labrynth featuring Tinie Tempah hits and the crowd immediately starts to boo as Daniel Starr heads out from the back with a big smirk on his face as he heads down the ramp. JAMAL JACKSON: “The following contest is a triple threat match for a future shot at the GIW Legacy Championship! Introducing first weighing in at two hundred thirty six pounds, standing five foot eleven inches tall…from Detroit, Michigan…DANIEL STARR!” RILEY STEVENS: “This match is an interesting one as fellow tag team partners Starr and Defias will be forced to meet in the ring tonight.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Or they can always just take out Hayden Carter.” Starr heads into the ring posing on the top rope as his music fades and "Scream If You're Crazy" by D.R.U.G.S hits and the boos only just continue as Paul Defias walks down from the top of the ramp with a big grin on his face as well. JAMAL JACKSON: “And introducing the second opponent weighing in at two hundred pounds, standing five foot eight inches tall…from Little Rock, Arkansas…PAUL DEFIAS!” RILEY STEVENS: “But Oliver only one man can get the pin tonight.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “They can decide that after taking her out.” Defias enters the ring heading over with Starr the two seeming on the same page as they set up in the corner almost like it’s a tag match as "I Want To Dance Without You" by Skylar Grey hits and finally the fans can cheer as Hayden Carter heads on out from the back with a big smile on her face as she high fives some fans on her way out to the ring. JAMAL JACKSON: “And introducing the final competitor weighing in at one hundred and ten pounds, standing five foot six inches tall…from Manhattan, New York…HAYDEN CARTER!” RILEY STEVENS: “I don’t envy Hayden in this one as it really is almost a Handicap match.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “That’s for sure, she’s screwed…It’s awesome.” Carter heads up the steps not looking deterred as she enters the ring stand across the ring from them as both members of DefiaStarr look back at her with a grin as the referee steps in between them and then calls for the bell. DING DING DING! Hayden looked toward her two opponents. She knew the moment would come when they’d finally get a chance to double team her and they did. Paul Defias and Daniel Starr charged their female opponent. Hayden gestured for them to come at her and raised her fists, readied for the fight. She preemptively dropped Paul Defias with a straight punch and then ducked under Daniel’s clothesline and keeled him over with a kick to the gut. She hoisted up to finish with a dropkick. RILEY STEVENS: “Hayden starts out battling them off.” Hayden pulled in Daniel for a quick cover, but Paul broke it. He threw a big boot to her skull that knocked her clear off of Starr. Paul backed himself against the ropes, he was going to charge and hit Daniel hard with an attack, as he ran when Starr slowly rose up, Hayden interrupted with a flying clothesline. Daniel followed up from her attack and pulled her up form the canvas. He positioned her for a suplex, but she refused to give in. It took an elbow to the temple to force Hayden into the suplex and drop her hard against the canvas. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Haha there we go Starr.” Paul slid himself back into the ring and together with Daniel, they stalked Hayden Carter a second time. She turned her gaze toward them and spat at them both. Paul reached out to grasp her, but Daniel grabbed him and ran him over the top rope. Paul had narrowly missed falling by landing on the rim, the top rope held tightly. Daniel wanted her for himself, but when he saw Paul up on the rim, he couldn’t resist. He maneuvered toward his partner and tried to position him for a Chokeslam, but he was pulled away by Hayden. RILEY STEVENS: “Starr and Defias are pulling no punches at once another.” She sent Daniel to the canvas with a DDT and then turned to Paul. A quick dropkick would have ensured he crash into the barricade, but Paul had the time to duck. Hayden collapsed on her side, but recovered quickly. Paul then ascended the top turn post, the left most one nearest him. Hayden rolled out of the way just in time for Paul’s attack to land on Daniel Starr. Finally he had connected with a physical struggle between he and his partner, but Daniel rolled him off. He refused to let Paul get the pin over him. He recovered to his feet and elbowed Hayden off of him. He also refused to let her continue to build momentum, but she responded by hoisting him into a sudden atomic drop. She then closed the gap between herself and Paul and wrapped him into a sleeperhold. RILEY STEVENS: “She’s got Paul in a Sleeper!” Paul writhed and tried to counter, but Hayden had the hold expertly applied. She was far from the ropes, but still at the off–center of the ring. A few paces back and she’d be at the turn post. It took a few moments, but soon Paul went slack. As Daniel had slowly recovered, Paul’s responses to the referee were lackluster. At the third raise of his arm, it dropped and the referee called for the match. Daniel still charged when the bell rang and Hayden threw his partner away from her so they would collide with one another. Daniel barreled through Paul with a clothesline and continued to bullrush toward Hayden, but she had cleared the ring by then. JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…HAYDEN CARTER!” Victorious over DefiaStarr, she raised an arm as she slowly ascended the stage as Defias got up to one knee as Starr angrily looked over the ropes at her, angry that he had lost this one. RILEY STEVENS: “Hayden Carter stamps her way to a future title shot at the person who leaves with the GIW Legacy title tonight.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Damnit.” Carter continues to smile as the fans cheer her on as Starr walks back to Defias helping him up, no love lost in this one as they together glare up at Carter who one upped them here tonight. WINNER: HAYDEN CARTER Backstage we go again this time we find ourselves with a still slightly sweaty Ryan Cavallari taking off his pads as he walks into Alexis Terry’s locker room with a smile on his face. RYAN CAVALLARI: “You ready to go?” Alexis looks up at him with a grin and nods her head as she stretches out her leg already in her ring gear. RYAN CAVALLARI: “Good luck then. But I was thinking that maybe after the show you and I could go out to dinner?” But before she can start to answer the door flies open as Aspen Chaud walks into the locker room stopping as she looks at Ryan with a raised eye brow. ASPEN CHAUD: “Bye now…” Aspen points to the door as Alexis goes to tell him to wait but he listens to Aspen not wanting to start any drama as she watches him go then looks over at Alexis. ASPEN CHAUD: “Really Alexis? I mean he is cute and all but don’t just toy with him like all the others.” Aspen chuckles as she closes the door behind him as Terry shrugs her shoulders as she couldn’t be more wrong. ASPEN CHAUD: “But did you see the announcement earlier? We are going to England.” Alexis gets up she was after all probably the biggest fan of the news as the two jumped around. ALEXIS TERRY: “Oh my God yes I heard. I am going to show you all the hot spots. Shopping sprees…The wine is great…you will love some of the night life. We are going to have some fun.” The two sit down now to talk all about it as the crowd is spared any more of it as we head back to the ring for the next match. QUOTE Back to the ring we go for one of two title matches here tonight this one being a match for the GIW Television Championship. The crowd is ready to go as “Look Sharp!” by Joe Jackson hits and these fans immediately respond with boo’s as Angel Vegnaduzzo walks out towards the ring with slicked back hair and a big smirk as he heads to the ring. JAMAL JACKSON: “The following contest is a singles match and it is for the GIW Television Championship! Introducing first, the challenger weighing in at one hundred and seventy pounds, standing five foot ten inches tall…from Buenos Aires, Argentina…ANGEL VEGNADUZZO!” RILEY STEVENS: “I know that you happen to be a fan of this guy and he has looked very impressive the past few weeks.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “The belt is his! BOOK IT.” Vegnaduzzo scoffs at the fans who reach out to high five him and then he heads up the steps into the ring where he waits as his music cuts off and “Busy Being Born” by Middle Class Rut hits to a loud ovation from the crowd as Henry Malkin walks out with his belt around his waist as he heads to the ring for his first title defense, not to mention perhaps his hardest opponent ever. JAMAL JACKSON: “And the champion, weighing in at one hundred eighty pounds, standing five foot eleven inches tall…from Norham, Northumberland, England…The GIW Television Champion…HENRY MALKIN!” RILEY STEVENS: “You know Malkin has been on such a impressive role the past few weeks, his matches really ending quickly.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Luck…Luck…Luck.” Malkin heads past the crowd high fiving them as Henry heads up the ramp into the ring looking across at Angel who waits on him as he heads to the top rope where holds up his belt into the air then jumps down handing it off to the referee who displays it over his head and then hands it out and calls for the bell. DING DING DING! As soon as the match starts Angel goes on the attack running right into a big Thrust Kick that doubles him right on over as Henry hits the ropes throwing a big knee to his face that sends him stumbling backwards as Malkin capitalizes on it as he hits a Bulldog. Looking out to the crowd and calling for them to get to their feet he hits the ropes and as Angel gets to a knee he crushes him with a ‘Euthanasia’ a Shining Wizard! The fans go wild as Angel falls to the mat looking around confused as Henry is not quite finished with him yet as he wraps him up into a ‘Queen's Vice’ a Crossface! The fans go wild as Angel struggles then taps out! JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner and still GIW Television Champion…HENRY MALKIN!” These fans go wild as yet again Henry finishes in under a minute as he hops up to his feet even he seems to be confused by how easy it has been as the referee slides out then bring the title back into him. RILEY STEVENS: “Another match and another quick win by Henry Malkin. What is this guy doing right?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “No idea…” Malkin takes his belt holding it into the air after his first successful title defense here in GIW as he rolls out of the ring celebrating with the fans as he heads up the ramp towards the back, making quick work of another man. WINNER: HENRY MALKIN (Still GIW Television Champion) As soon as Henry walks through the curtain to the back The Shadow comes storming out of it as he heads down the ramp towards the ring as the fans look around in shock, they were not at all expecting this as he heads into the ring as Angel Vegnaduzzo crawls to his feet trying to get away but Shadow grabs a hold of him tossing him into the ropes and crushing him with a clothesline. RILEY STEVENS: “We haven’t seen Shadow in months.” Shadow then grabs Angel up to his feet and Chokeslam’s him out of the ring as he asks for a microphone as the fans are murmuring about him, he gets the mic and walks to the center of the ring. THE SHADOW: “Get out here right now.” Confusion sets in as the fans wonder who he could be talking about as a figure walks out from the back heading down the ramp at The Shadow who waits in the ring. The new man heads up the steps as everyone looks around confused as to what is going on. RILEY STEVENS: “Who is this?” The new man enters the ring and is met by The Shadow who throws punch after punch at him only for the new man to walk through them unfazed as he grabs him by the throat and pushes him back off the ropes then crushes him with a Big Boot that dazed Shadow long enough for him to hit a Gutwrench Falling Powerbomb, amazingly on the much bigger Shadow. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “What are we witnessing?” This new man stalks him following him to the ground where he gets on top wrapping a hand around his throat and choking him away as The Shadow, a huge man at that is unable to even get away eventually passing out as this new man stands over him with a smile as a hype video for the next contest begins amongst the confusion. QUOTE It is now time for the next contest as this place just continues to get more amped as the night wears on as the lights go black and that means that Scar is here as “Scarred” by Nicholas Conners hits through the crowd and Scar makes his presence felt making his usual slow descent down towards the ring to a loud chorus of boo’s as usual. JAMAL JACKSON: “The following contest is a Last Man Standing Match! Introducing first weighing in at two hundred pounds, standing five foot ten inches tall…from San Francisco, California…SCAR!” RILEY STEVENS: “These two nearly killed one another last month, I expect only the same this time.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “One if not both these men are leaving in ambulance.” Scar enters the ring looking around the arena as he enters the ring here tonight, walking to a corner waiting for the man he wanted so badly to beat. As his music faded the lights slightly got lighter as "World Warrior" by RedRapper hits and the fans go crazy for the man who always has had the biggest heart here in GIW, Tony Miranda who heads out from the back eyes locked on Scar as he walks with purpose to the ring. JAMAL JACKSON: “And introducing his opponent weighing in at two hundred twenty pounds, standing six foot two inches tall…from Chicago, Illinois…TONY MIRANDA!” RILEY STEVENS: “This is going to be violent…” Tony and Scar’s eyes are locked all the way to the ring as Tony enters it with some anger in his step as he tells the referee to start the match as these two men glare back and forth at one another and then the match starts. DING DING DING! Scar and Tony watched one another from across the ring and slowly approached. They met at the center. RILEY STEVENS: “Alioth really saved himself there.” Starre goes for an early exclamation point with a Super Kick that is caught by Matt. Kluge spins him around then catching him with into a Bridging leg hook belly to back Suplex that sends Alioth crashing to the mat. Kluge gets up patting his chest for a quick moment having to feel good after hitting the first real significant move of the night. Respecting Alioth enough to know that he won’t go down that easily he steps away regrouping in the center of the ring as Alioth favors his back as he gets up turning around as he gets up as Kluge heads toward him. Saving himself he ducks under the Clothesline then with Kluge’s back to him he nails him with a Chop Block that drops Matt down to the…mat holding his knee. These fans are on the edge of their seats the entire time as Alioth immediately pulls him to his feet wrapping up his neck then planting him with a Snap DDT. RILEY STEVENS: “Alioth just spiked him into the mat.” These fans are going crazy now as Starre goes for a pin attempt 1…Kluge literally tosses him off with ease feeling a little disrespected by the early pin attempt. Both men get to their feet Starre trying to make the move on Kluge who seems cornered. But as he goes for him Matt ducks under using his shoulder to propel Alioth into the air landing face first on the turnbuckle. Immediately spinning around holding his face he walks right into a Northern Lights Suplex and instead of going for a pin he uses Alioth’s pain to set up the Cobra Clutch locking it in as the fans start to boo as he torks on it with everything he’s got but Alioth is able to reach out a leg to reach the rope 1…2…3…4…Kluge releases the hold after four to get the most he can out of it as he gets to his feet, waiting for Alioth who catches his breath then starts to get back up. Kluge thinking he’s got him dazed a bit walks slowly towards him and BAM! Gets a chin full of a Superkick from Alioth Starre out of no where. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “What a kick.” Any other man would have fallen to the mat, Kluge took the kick and simply seemed to see birdies for a second allowing Alioth to hoist him up into a Fisherman Suplex with a hook for the pin 1…Kick Out. Still not even close yet as Kluge crawls towards the ropes to use them to get up only for Alioth to drag him away keeping a hold of his ankle which he uses to lock in an Ankle Lock. These fans come unglued seeming to count out Matt way to easily as he uses his power to kick Alioth away then use the ropes to get to his feet. Alioth doesn’t give him a moment to rest as he rushes him only for Kluge to duck under him and use his momentum to toss him over the ropes, but Alioth is able to find his footing on the ring apron some how. Matt thinking he’s gone takes a second to break then turns around right into a hard elbow from Alioth that drops him back. Starre prepares to go springboard but Kluge recovers quickly and is able to punch him nearly sending Alioth out of the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “He some how held on.” With one arm keeping him on the ropes he regain his footing only long enough for Kluge to crush him with a big punch that sets him up for a Suplex over the ropes only for Matt to show his ring awareness as he drops him belly first on the ropes. Alioth falls forward into the ring as Kluge takes a moment to look out at the crowd with a bit of a grin on his face now as Alioth crawls into the corner where Matt rushes at him delivering a powerful boot in the corner that rocks Alioth. Neither man nice enough to let the other rest Matt reaches down hoisting him to his feet and dragging him to the center of the ring where he delivers the ‘Hortonville Suplex Special 1’ a Suplex rolled into Mounted Punching, and these punches seem to come with extra force this time. These fans are booing wildly, though a small contingent seem to be with Kluge in this battle as Alioth is able to push him off of him. Matt gets up first with Alioth’s speed slowed by the beating as he gets up right into a kick in the gut from Kluge. RILEY STEVENS: “Kluge is really starting to put a lot of offense together.” Matt then shoves his head away just now seeming to want to show off his advantage a little as Alioth backs away a bit trying to get his wits about him. But Matt makes a big mistake when he slaps him across the face as soon as it his Alioth goes after him with punch after punch that drops Kluge back into the ropes before he uses them to send Matt off. Kluge heads back tossing a clothesline that Alioth ducks under then as he bounces back he runs right into a Dropkick that floors him. This crowd gets wild as Alioth awaits Matt, who hops right back up only to head into a big European Uppercut then a Shin Breaker from Alioth that sends him to the mat really favoring his left shin as Starre goes on the offensive getting on top throwing some uncharacteristic wild punches from a mounted position as Kluge does his best to protect himself from some of them before using his power to toss Alioth away. RILEY STEVENS: “Have these men even started to slow down yet?” The answer was no as they got up just as fast as they did the first time in the match this time heading right into a big Powerslam from Kluge who stumbled back into the ropes almost doing the slam just to buy himself some time to breath as Alioth held his back but got up right away as Kluge bounced off the ropes tossing a punch that connected to the back of his head. Alioth stumbled into the ropes as Kluge ran at him going for a Clothesline not seeming to realize his own strength this time as the move sends both men over the top rope crashing to the outside right next to one another. These fans now hop to their feet so they can see what’s happening as the referee urges them to get back into the ring as both men recover taking some time to get up. By the time they are up it’s a count of three and its Starre that is up first as he takes Kluge by the back of the head slamming him down into the side of the ring. Matt stumbles away towards the announce table. RILEY STEVENS: “Oh God here they come…” Kluge saves himself more pain as he uses a kick to Alioth’s stomach to double him over and turn this thing around as he now slams Alioth face first down into the announce table before spinning him around and tossing him into the ring. Following him in he heads instead to the top rope, uncharacteristic but not unseen for Kluge as he jumps off just as Stare gets up and hits a Missile Dropkick that takes all the wind out of Alioth and sends him to the mat holding his stomach in pain. This crowd is amped up and have decided to go ahead and just stay on their feet until this one is decided as Matt gets up with that look in his eye as he seems poised and ready to try and end this thing once and for all, as he grabs him from behind and slams him to the mat with a German Suplex. But he does not release it instead using it to bring him back to his feet where he turns it into a Backbreaker! RILEY STEVENS: “They are using everything in their arsenal.” But that vicious combo does not even take Alioth off his feet as he maintains his balance holding his back as he stumbles into the corner. Kluge rushes at him and catches him with a Clothesline this time it sends Alioth stumbling forward then falling to the mat as Matt yet again heads to the top rope climbing up ready to go for another huge move as he jumps off for a Flying Legdrop but Alioth rolls out of the way! Matt’s leg takes the most of the fall as he gets up gingerly as he does so right into the awaiting arms of Alioth who locks him into a Abdominal Stretch! The fans go wild as he fully gets it locked in tugging hard on it then releasing one arm so that he can deliver elbows to Matt’s stomach as he does it. Matt seems to get back some energy after a few elbows and is able to break free spin around and go for a Suplex but as he hoists Alioth up he receives a knee to the face that causes him to drop Alioth, and for the second time tonight Alioth floors him with a Superkick! RILEY STEVENS: “What a kick!” Alioth walks right over Kluge’s fallen body to head to the top rope as everyone in the building gets up, all eyes on Alioth as he looks to head up the stars as he looks around at them then jumps off for a Shooting Star Press! While everyone’s eyes are up top at Alioth, Kluge’s eyes are to the ropes where he rolls out of the way underneath them. The thud of the unforgiving mat can be heard by everyone as Starre’s body bounces up and immediately he holds his ribs in pain. Kluge gets back up recovering from the kick as he stumbles into the corner as Alioth gets up but it proves costly as he gets up in time for ‘The Matt Cooke’ a Running Knee to Alioth’s Knee that sends him limping for a second before receiving a Exploder Suplex that puts him down as he crawls on top for a pin attempt 1…2-Kick Out! RILEY STEVENS: “These men have been wrestling for a long time now it may just end up who succumbs to the pain first?” Matt gets up looking down at Alioth as calculates his next move then screaming at the crowd that it is over as Alioth rolls over onto his stomach as Kluge folds his legs over and locks him into the devastating ‘Rat Trap’ a Leg Trapped Camel Clutch! These fans are in a frenzy now trying to will Alioth out as Matt pulls his neck backwards in extreme fashion. His only error might just be letting Alioth having a chance to get to the ropes as he is not too far. But the move is so painful, Alioth can be seen cringing as he reaches out the best he can to reach the ropes as the thought of tapping has to be crossing his mind. Kluge grits his teeth and tears back on it some more as Alioth crawls trying to reach out his arms, wishing they were an inch longer! It looks as though he might tap but he gives one last reach and he gets the ropes in his hands as the crowd goes insane! OLIVER THIBODAUX: “HE MADE IT?!” The crowd can be seen jumping around like it’s a House of Pain concert, as Kluge releases the hold showing signs of anger as he gets up to his feet calming himself down as Alioth uses the ropes to get up, so close to tapping as he turns around as Kluge goes for ‘The Tiger's Roar Gold Edition’ a Whale Hunt but as he goes to lift him up Alioth throws a knee that breaks up the move before it can begin. Matt stumbles back and Alioth goes forward dropping him to the mat and immediately turning it into the ‘Four Star Mary’ a Figure Four Leglock! The crowd goes insane, the move that Tony Miranda passed out from…The move Aspen Chaud, Mark Cross, and Trent Lost all tapped to…Kluge fights it then rolls over but Alioth uses Matt’s own momentum to send him right back onto his back! The fans now start to go wild as its Matt’s turn to dig down deep as he turns around pushing himself to the ropes! Can he get there? YES! RILEY STEVENS: “What is it going to take?” These fans let out a collective sigh as Matt gets to the ropes and even Alioth Starre has to be wondering what will it take? Both men limp to their feet looking at one another again, face to face just as the match started before they exchange punches. Both in so much pain but only wanting to win this match! Boom! Kluge rocks Alioth with a haymaker and the fans boo. Right away Alioth answers back with one of his own. BOOM! Matt throws another punch and the power in his can truly be seen as Alioth nearly loses his footing throwing a punch back that does not seem to faze Kluge who tosses one back only for Alioth to run at him throwing a punch but it turns right into Matt’s favor as he hoists him right into ‘The Tiger's Roar Gold Edition’ a Whale Hunt! The crowd goes crazy as Kluge falls on top it’s over 1…2…KICK OUT!!! RILEY STEVENS: “HE KICKED OUT! HE KICKED OUT!” Matt turns onto a seated position his jaw dropping as he looks back at Alioth who kicked out but immediately fell back in place. Kluge shakes his head as he wipes some sweat off his face as he gets up to his feet pulling Alioth up as well. Seeming dead as he lifts him up Starre surprises us all as he pushes Kluge away then BAM! SUPERKICK! The move stuns Kluge as he stands there, rocked as BOOM! A second Superkick, still not putting him off his feet until finally he gets crushed by one final Superkick that sends him to the mat hard. RILEY STEVENS: “Three Super Kick’s are you kidding me?” With that we now near the end as Alioth Starre heads to the top rope looking out at the crowd drained and battered, sweating hard as he gets up there slowly. Like a bullet Kluge shoots up to his feet going all out jumping up to the top rope where he hoists up Alioth out of no where, Starre as shocked as everyone as Matt recovers so quickly from the top rope jumping off with a ‘Tigers Roar Gold Edition’ from the top rope! The crowd goes wild as Alioth seems to be out and Kluge crawls on top 1…2…3!!! JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…MATT KLUGE!!!” The crowd gives a mixed reaction most of them however completely stunned not just due to the way it ended but because they have all just witnessed the greatest contest in GIW history, never before have we seen a match quite like this. RILEY STEVENS: “Matt Kluge is the victor! Alioth Starre has defeated every man ever put in front of him but tonight here at ‘Exile’ Matt Kluge brought it and after the longest match EVER…EVER in GIW history we have our winner Matt Kluge!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Wow…” These fans seem as shocked as everyone as Kluge falls backwards after getting the pin so tired, both men sweating profusely having just put on a legendary show here at ‘Exile’. RILEY STEVENS: “I can’t believe this, what a match…” Matt Kluge breaths can be seen as his belly goes up and down, unable to celebrate his own victory as Alioth Starre exits the ring looking back into the ring, as he holds his hands on his knees. RILEY STEVENS: “Name a star in GIW and Alioth Starre has beaten them at one point, but tonight after a legendary match it is Matt Kluge that wins, cementing himself as one of GIW’s elite.” These fans have nothing but respect for both men in the ring tonight as Kluge crawls to a seated position catching his breath as the fans pat Alioth on the back as he walks up the ramp slowly looking down at Kluge in the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “What a night, what a show! Exile will go down in history as the place where we saw Matt Kluge and Alioth Starre do BATTLE.” With that Kluge crawls to his feet turning to the stage where he looks at Alioth, they may have hated each other going into it but after this match it was hard to think they could not respect one another. Slowly the confetti comes down from the ceiling as these fans give both men standing ovation as the show fades with Matt Kluge standing in the ring, the victor. WINNER: MATT KLUGE
