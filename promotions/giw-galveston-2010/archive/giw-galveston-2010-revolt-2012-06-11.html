GIW — Card

Revolt, 11 June 2012

11 June 2012

GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: MONDAY NIGHT REVOLT! LIVE! From Galveston's North Beach – June 11th, 2012 BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! Those trademark fireworks stream up into the nigh sky above Galveson's North Beach as the crowd of seven hundred locals and tourists pile in the stands surrounding this famous venue. The camera pans around the crowd as “Over and Under” by Egypt Central, Revolt's theme, plays in the background. Signs such as “The Golden Age”, “Kluge = Savior”, “J-Bal For President”, and “Kiss My Aspen” are being waved with pride as the fans look down on the elevated GIW ring. We cut over to Riley Stevens and Oliver Thibodaux at ringside with the screaming fans behind them. RILEY STEVENS: “Ladies and gentlemen, at long last we are back on Galveston Island for Revolt! I'm Riley Stevens alongside my partner Oliver Thibodaux, and if you'll notice Jacob O'Connelly is gone tonight. Well, at Aggresive Expansion, the poles shifted in our world. David Sinclair was fired once again for embezling money from the company, thanks to the keen sleuthyness of Matt Kluge, our new interim Chairman until Black Beckett is physically ready to return to the company. Thankfully for us, Kluge fired O'Connelly with Sinclair, so its just you and me partner.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I never thought I'd say it Riley, but thank God! Sinclair's ego was getting out of hand, even more than mine was! And having to constantly deal with that little Euro prick beside me every night – its true what they say, they really do smell like prunes! Maybe he is off watching that Euro Cup thing on ESPN that keeps running over into SportsCenter.” RILEY STEVENS: “Well Kluge's first card as Chairman is a sure enough good one. As always we start out with an Open Battle Royal that will feature well known stars like Brody Harper, as well as wrestlers new to the company like Jace Gryphon, Brandon Moore, Adam Miller, and the beautiful Lluvia Luna. Kluge has always had a knack for finding the next biggest star in GIW, we saw it with Ross Rydell – Oliver, which one of these stars do you think will make it big in Galveston Island Wrestling?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Well, this is going to be one hell of a match Riley, one of the best Open's we've seen in a very long time. Harper is what he is, the fans love the kid, but he won't ever amount to anything here. Jace Gryphon seems like the cockiest little prick from the few seconds I saw of him backstage, yelling at the caterer for putting too much cheese on a cheese burger! Now, I wouldn't get mad about something like that, but he seems physically fit – I like him, he has strong feelings about anything. Brandon Moore is a strange cat, I feel like he's a druggie that Kluge found on a street corner. No chance he can keep up. Adam Miller reminds me of... oh whats his name... c'mon the Backyard Superstar! North Beach Title... Mike Danielson! Yeah, he reminds me of Mike Danielson, that goes way back. Miller seems solid, but I don't think he'll be super famous here – doesn't have the 'it' factor. Then we have the lovely Texas girl, Lluvia Luna... mmm... I tell you what if she loses I'll find a way to cheer her up... ANYWAY. Jace wins.” RILEY STEVENS: “Ollie, you're married. Our next match up is a singles match between to stars in GIW that have the potential to go very, very far. We'll have Naviel Sunderland going one on one with Jordan Westbrook. Naviel is 0-1 since joining the company, Jordan is 0-0... who is picking up win number one?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Westbrook is another one of those hood rats, I don't know what Kluge sees in them, and for that reason I have to go with Naviel Sunderland. She just lost a really close match to Jeremy Baltimore at Aggressive Expansion, and she'll be out to prove she can win here in GIW.” RILEY STEVENS: “Moving along, our next match is a VERY provacative one for sure. Its a five way match between five of GIW's most famous legends, all of them making combacks to the federation after long periods of time. You have Mark Cross, who returned a few weeks ago and has been dominant ever since, along with Damien Dunn, Megathreat, Madison Seton, and the former North Beach Champion, Trent Lost. Who do you have in this match – I'm sure you have plenty of conflicting emotions.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Well, I'm a huge Megathreat fan – everyone knows that. At the same time, it will be hard to root against Mark Cross, who's new attitude is something I really have respect for. Madison Seton is well known from other companies, but hasn't done too much in GIW, Damien Dunn is interesting too... then we have Trent Lost! Uh... I think I'm going to pick... Trent. Yeah. Trent Lost is going to be eager to get back into the spotlight in GIW, and this match is a great way to do it.” RILEY STEVENS: “That match certainly has potential to steal the show, but so does the match for the Television Title. Jeremy Baltimore will cash in his victory at Aggressive Expansion for a shot at Eliza Gray tonight, and she has to be on upset alert. Are you Ollie?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “No. I hate Jeremy Baltimore. That kid has no respect for people, and as far as I'm concerned, he can kiss my ass Riley. He wont win the belt, no chance in hell.” RILEY STEVENS: “I guess you're still bitter about the beating he gave you before Aggressive Expansion? But after that, we have Ace Baldwin and Alioth Starre is a match fit for the main event at Moment of Glory. Ace was all set to return triumphantly last week, then all of a sudden – here's Alioth Starre, crashing the party! Needless to say, Ace was PISSED, and now we have this match.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Well, of course Ace Baldwin is pissed. I'd be pissed too. Everyone in GIW knew he was coming back in that match – but he got complacent! Ace deserved to lose because he thought it was just going to be him! Well in GIW, thats not the attitude you need to have – Alioth was just keeping him on his toes, and you know what, thats fine. Alioth will win tonight too.” RILEY STEVENS: “Well, we know that those two men aren't going to give us a bad show – but the main event is just as captivating. We have Alex Castellanos and Lokie, the new champions, teaming up against Aspen Chaud and Alexis Terry, the former champions and bitter enemies themselves. This match has the potential to go up in flames – what do you think Ollie?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Well, poor Alexis had the week from hell last week – ruined wedding, best friend lost, title lost, but Aspen's wasn't much better losing the title to Castellanos. I know that inside these two still love each other, and we are going to see shades of the old International Glamour tonight – mark my words! And there will be nothing Lokie and Alex Castellanos can do -” “Wayward One” by Alterbridge hits the speakers, and the place absolutely erupts in cheers for the new GIW Undisputed Champion, Alex Castellanos. They only got louder as he made his way out from the curtain dressed in blue jeans and his white “Golden Age” t-shirt available in the GIW shop, the title tossed over his shoulder, his name newly engraved in it. He ascended the steel steps and climbed through the ropes, taking a microphone from Darryl Clinton. He stands in the center of the ring as his music slowly dies down, but the arena is filled with “CAST-ELL-AN-OS” chants. He steps back, letting them go, and the cheer for that too. Finally, he decides to speak. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “At Aggressive Expansion, I defeated Aspen Chaud for the Undisputed Championship.” There is a huge pop from the crowd. Alex smiles, nods, and looks down at his championship belt, finally holding it into the air as he stands on the middle rope of the ring. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “But thats not all that happened at Aggressive Expansion – you see, it was a trifecta of things that are going to make this company a much better place. Aspen lost her belt. Terry lost her belt. Sinclair was removed from his position of Chairman, and replaced by Matt Kluge who promptly cancelled a tour of Nevada and brought us back to where we belong – Galveston Island.” There are more heavy cheers from the audience who are obviously very happy to have their home town promotion back in their home town. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “Things are looking up in Galveston Island Wrestling now, because with me as the champion, you can actually be proud of the person representing your company. I don't have to cheat to win my matches, I don't have to whore myself around to the boss to get what I want – I just win.” “Kiss My Lips” by Dev hits the PA system, and the fans pop with hatred for Alexis Terry and the remnants of International Glamour – Eliza Gray and Ryan Cavallari. They stop on the ramp, and Ryan goes to speak but Alexis Terry snatches the microphone from him, shooting him a look. ALEXIS TERRY: “Alex, congratulations. After all this time, months and months of getting your ass kicked day in and day out, you finally managed to pull of a fluke win. Alex, I hate Aspen more than you can imagine, but even I can admit that she is a better wrestler than you.” Alex stays in the ring, but is leaning against the side ropes, holding his title high up into the air. The fans are mixed right now, some cheering Alex, others booing Alexis. ALEXIS TERRY: “And Alex, I'm a better wrestler than you too. I mean, look at what I've done. Aspen Chaud, the longest tenured GIW superstar was humbled, thanks to me. The highest rated segment in GIW history, my wedding, was thanks to me. Your Television Champion, Eliza Gray, is a product of my work. And Ryan Cavallari... well we are still working on him.” Alex cuts her off, the fans cheer for that too. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “Alex, do you remember that time I beat you before Retaliation? Clean victory, 1...2...3, if you are such a superior wrestler? How did that happen? And why do you think you are any better than Cavallari or Eliza behind you? I'd take either of them before I took you.” Rage covers Alexis' face, she turns towards Cavallari and motions for him to go towards the ring. He takes off his suit jacket, unbuttons his collar, and rolls up his sleeves, taking off. ALEXIS TERRY: “It was a fluke Alex, and you know it! I am next in line for your title, and I'll take it. Mark my words Alex, I wouldn't lie to you.” Ryan slides into the ring and goes for a clothesline, but Alex ducked it and slid out of the ring, taking off up the ramp towards Alexis. She realized she was in danger and scampered away behind the curtain. Alex was caught in the middle of the ramp now between Eliza Gray on the stage, and then Ryan Cavallari in the ring. Eliza looked at him for a moment, then dissapeared behind the curtian as we faded to commercial. WINNER: BRANDON MOORE As we return from commercial, "World Warrior" by RedRapper begins to play, sending the crowd into a frenzy of cheers and chants as out walks the GIW Legend Tony Miranda, fresh from his victory at both Agressive Expansion and the first round of the Trans Atlantic Tournament, defeating Ryan Cavallari and William Draconis respectively. Tony smiles as he looks around the arena, looking at the many fans who were cheering him. He slowly begins walking down towards the ring, slapping the hands of the fans as he walked down. Tony quickly hops into the ring, asking for a microphone from the ring announcer, Darryl Clinton. Tony walks towards the ropes, resting on them as he looks out at the crowd. As his theme dies down, it is replaced with a sea of "Get Hype" chants. Tony smiles and raises the microphone to speak, but before he can say a single word, he was interrupted. "Masquerade" by Versallies begins to play, a wave of boos fill the arena as Phoenix Matsuda walks out, he seems to be drowning out the boos as he locks eyes with "The New World Warrior", who stands looking at Matsuda in the middle of the ring. Matsuda slowly begins walking down the ring, not even paying attention to the crowd, just staring right at Tony. Matsuda quickly gets into the ring, neither Matsuda nor Tony taking their eyes off the other. Matsuda walks into the middle of the ring, standing nose to nose with Tony. As his music dies down, the crowd begins chanting for Tony immediately, however...neither man seems to pay attention to the crowd. The rest of the world to them, was silent. After what seemed to be an eternity's worth of waiting, Phoenix Matsuda raised his microphone up to speak. PHOENIX MATSUDA: "Ever since I got here to GIW, I've heard stories. Get Hype, KID." With the emphasis put on the word, Tony walks past, bumping Matsuda's shoulder. Tony turns around and walks up the stage, looking at Matsuda as he stands in the ring.
