GIW — Card

Revolt, 22 March 2012

22 March 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: THURSDAY NIGHT REVOLT LIVE! From the Baton Rouge River Center in Baton Rouge, LA – March 22, 2012 BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! The show starts out with a bang as we pan around this Baton Rouge crowd then heading around the ring where already standing at the ready is Beck Ramsey with a bundle of Tables, Ladders, and Chairs all around the ring with him as he holds a microphone and the fans attention. BECK RAMSEY: "We're nine days from Exile when Alexis Terry has to face-off with The Sin City Stallion with her Legacy title on the line. It isn't just any match. No, it isn't..." Beck Ramsey walks across the ring, examining the surrounding weapons. The steel chairs which are set-up, the tables which are also set-up, and the two ladders which are standing tall in the ring. BECK RAMSEY: "On April 1st, Alexis will find herself stuck in a Tables, Ladders, and Chairs match." The crowd roars in approval of the match as it is the highly anticipated main event of GIW's next Supershow, Exile. BECK RAMSEY: "Anyone of these weapons can be used in whatever creative way we see fit, and, Alexis, I can be very creative. At ‘Exile’, you and everyone watching will find out that I'm not just a little creative, but sadistically artistic. Everyone will learn that Beck Ramsey is the Virtuoso of Violence. It won't be senseless violence though, Alexis, it will be me teaching you a lesson. That lesson is: payback's a bitch." Naturally, that particular use of words gets the crowd into a frenzy. Beck waits for them to settle down then lifts the microphone to his lips again and continues speaking as he walks toward one of the steel chairs. BECK RAMSEY: "Alexis, there's no doubt that every single one of these weapons will come into a play at Exile. This chair for example. There are many things one can use a chair for: sitting down and resting for instance or one could use it the best way possible and crack another person upside the skull with it. The latter sounds good to me." The crowd gives a light cheer as Beck Ramsey winks into the camera before continuing. BECK RAMSEY: "Then we have the table..." Beck walks over to one of the tables and pounds his fist on the table a couple times. BECK RAMSEY: "I can't imagine crashing through this would feel too good. I'll let you tell me after our match at Exile, Alexis." He nodded then focused his attention on the ladder, walking over to it and looking up at the top of it. He lifted the microphone back to his lips again. BECK RAMSEY: "Then there's the ladder, Alexis. I hope you're not afraid of heights 'cause you're gonna have to climb one of these ladders in order to grab your title and win the match. If you think you have a chance of that happening though, Alexis, you're just setting yourself up for a bad April's Fool's Day joke. Remember, denial isn't a river in Egypt. Alexis, here's what's going to happen..." Beck reaches out to the nearest ladder rung and pulls himself up. BECK RAMSEY: "I'm gonna climb this ladder. Rung..." He continues to pull himself up toward the top of the ladder. BECK RAMSEY: "By rung..." Beck nears the top of the ladder with one more step. BECK RAMSEY: "By damn rung." The Sin City Stallion turns around on the ladder and looks around at the crowd as he continues. BECK RAMSEY: "At the end of the night, I will be the one celebrating, Alexis. I can't make GIW bring back my brother or Cassidy, but I can sure as hell make you pay. So, tonight, Alexis, I want you to pay close attention to my match 'cause when I kick Matt Kluge's ass. You'll have an idea what your fate looks like." Almost just taunting her Ramsey lets the mic drop from the top of the ladder falling to the mat where it breaks into two pieces as Beck raises up his arms and the crowd lets him hear it as they cheer him on as we take a quick commercial break. QUOTE We return to the ring where the Tables, Ladders, and Chairs have been removed and we see Darius Drake nearing the ring with Alex Castellanos already ready. RILEY STEVENS: “This was a last second addition to the card this week and we get to see Alex Castellanos back in action against the debuting Darius Drake.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Thought Alex was out?” "International" Darius Drake steps through the ropes, Alex Castellanos goes immediately on the offensive even before the referee calls for the beginning bell. Alex lands a running boot to Drake's chin causing the crowd to boo and groan with the impact. Drake nearly falls out of the ring from the impact, but is able to grab onto the bottom rope to prevent himself from falling off the ring apron. Alex immediately continues his assault by reaching over the ropes and pulling Drake to his feet by his hair as the referee goes ahead and calls for the bell. DING DING DING! Castellanos sets Drake up for a suplex back into the ring and lifts him up, but before Alex can drop back to slam Darius to the mat, Darius lands on his feet then quickly turns to his opponent who turns around just in time to eat a roundhouse kick to the side of the head causing Alex to drop to the mat like a sack of potatoes. With Castellanos out cold, Drake takes this moment to pull Alex a little away from the ring ropes then hook the leg for the pin attempt. Unfortunately, it's far too early in the match, and Alex kicks out after a two-count. Drake keeps on the assault though by picking Alex back up to his feet and Irish whipping him to the far turnbuckle. Drake immediately charges towards the turnbuckle that Castellanos is in, but is caught off-guard when Alex pushes himself out of the turnbuckle and dropkicks Darius to the knee, sending him face first into the middle turnbuckle. RILEY STEVENS: “Alex seems to be fully recovered but with a brand new demeanor.” As Darius is dazed on the canvas, Alex poses as implying that he's superior to Drake. The fans dislike the display and begin booing him which Castellanos just ignores. With that, Alex goes back to his attack, laying some swift boots to his downed opponent. Alex then grabs Drake by the ankles and drags him toward the middle of the ring. He seems to be setting up for a Mexican Surfboard Stretch. When he can't reach Darius' arms, he tries chopping Drake's sides to get him to loosen up long enough for him to grab his arms. When he isn't able to get his arms, he just jumps up into the air with his feet still entangled in Drake's legs and comes back down on them causing them to slam on to the canvas. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I like the new Alex.” With a proud grin, Alex gets off Drake's knees. While Alex does this, Drake clutches his knees which are clearly in a lot of pain from the maneuver. Alex, again, stomps at Drake, taking his time. Alex, with a grin plastered on his face, methodically walks away from his opponent then looks around at the fans and taps his finger against his head, implying how smart he is. Once he turns back and methodically walks back toward his opponent, he goes to grab Drake again only to be yanked by the tights causing him to run face-first into the near turnbuckle. RILEY STEVENS: “Drake is really showing Alex some stuff here tonight in his debut.” Alex turns around in the turnbuckle only to receive a flurry of kicks, punches, and elbows, ten of them to be exact. After the striking combination in the turnbuckle, Drake shoves Alex away from the turnbuckle, dazed. Darius ascends to the top rope rather quickly and waits for Alex to turn around, but when Alex does, having outstanding ring awareness, he runs toward the turnbuckle, but Drake leaps over him and lands on his feet. When Alex turns around, Darius immediately knees Alex in the gut and goes for his version of the Angle Slam which he calls Windfall, but Alex counters and lands low on his feet and immediately goes for a school boy roll-up on Drake only to get a two-count before Darius kicks out. RILEY STEVENS: “Impressive match with these two.” Alex seems a little frustrated as he gets back up after the pinfall attempt, but Drake is back up in his face. Darius sends another roundhouse kick flying toward Castellanos' head, but when he ducks, the referee gets leveled by the kick. Noticing this, Alex takes this opportunity to hit a low-blow on Drake then he backs up a few long steps and shuffles forward and puts Drake's lights out with his ‘Golden Arrow’, a Super Kick. With a smug look on his face, he yanks the referee close to his felled opponent, rousing him then hooks Drake's leg. The referee comes to in enough time to make the count and Castellanos gets the pinfall victory. JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…Alex Castellanos!” The fans boo Alex hard as he gets up rolling out of the ring holding his arms up high into the air as Darius rolls over trying to explain to the referee what had happened here. RILEY STEVENS: “Close one but Alex is able to steal it with a low blow into a Golden Arrow.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Seems like Kluge might have knocked some sense into him.” The referee tries to explain to Darius what happened from his end and Drake nods his head patting him on the back as he gets up glaring at Alex as we go backstage. WINNER: ALEX CASTELLANOS Backstage we go where Zachary Pagonis is standing by with Matt Kluge the two a fixture of the show the past few weeks. ZACHARY PAGONIS: “Matt Kluge the last few weeks we have seen you become a new man we could say and last week we found out you would be facing Alioth Starre what are your thoughts on that match and the fact that if you win you get a shot at Aspen Chaud’s belt?” Pagonis held the microphone over to him as Kluge shook his head as he answered the question. MATT KLUGE: “You think I give a crap about that belt? Not at all. What I care about is being known as the best something that I have been disrespected out of ever since I have been here in GIW. Now just think I will have beaten Tony Miranda, taken out Alex Castellanos, humiliated Alioth Starre, and ruined Aspen Chaud in just over a month’s time. Then I can leave this company with everyone knowing who the best wrestler really is.” Kluge goes to leave but Zachary tries to ask one more question before he goes. ZACHARY PAGONIS: “What about your match tonight with Beck Ramsey?” Matt was finished already as he headed on his way for the match that would be next. QUOTE After a short break for commercials we return to a announce table now consisting of Oliver, Riley, and Alexis Terry holding her ‘GIW Legacy Championship’ over her shoulder as she smiles into the camera. RILEY STEVENS: “We are being joined for this next match by Alexis Terry who I assume is here to watch Beck Ramsey?” ALEXIS TERRY: “Oh you know I just figured I would savor in his loss here tonight before I beat him come ‘Exile’.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “We are glad to have you.” “The Rat” by Dead Confederate now hits as the fans begin to boo heavily for this next man as Matt Kluge heads out from the back with that very familiar angry look upon his face that has followed him ever since his recent return here in GIW. Talking trash to a few fans on the way down to the ring as he heads into the ring for this highly anticipated match. RILEY STEVENS: “So I assume you are in the corner of Matt Kluge, Alexis?” ALEXIS TERRY: “Don’t get me wrong he is a gross, disgusting, prick but yeah tonight I hope he hurts Beck.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Which he will.” Alexis chuckles as Kluge enters the ring bouncing off the ropes then waits as "Two Sides of Me" by Hinder hits and the place explodes for one of the few men left that these fans can love as Beck Ramsey heads down the ramp, two men who have drawn comparisons in the past ready to meet as Beck heads down the ramp. RILEY STEVENS: “Last week Cassidy and Nathan were taken out of that match meaning Beck and yourself get a chance to settle the score at ‘Exile’.” ALEXIS TERRY: “I can finally put an end to his little hype.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I can’t wait.” Beck enters the ring now catching Alexis in his eyes as he looks over at her angrily as he heads over to say something to her only for Kluge to attack from behind crushing him with a blow from behind. Ramsey falls to a knee as Kluge grabs him spinning around and tossing him into the ring post. Matt backs away as the fans boo him loudly as Alexis can be heard laughing loudly on the mic. ALEXIS TERRY: “Typical Beck eyes off the prize.” Beck turns around in the corner holding his shoulder as the referee scolds Kluge then asks Beck if he can go and of course Ramsey nods his head as the referee turns and calls for the bell. DING DING DING! Kluge starts to talk some trash as he points to Alexis and then mouths “I am the one you need to worry up.” Before immediately going in for a lock up, which Beck can’t handle after the shot to the shoulder as Kluge uses it to his advantage tossing him to the mat just too sort of show him who is boss. Beck hops right back up but Matt is waiting for it as he grabs him and whips him off the ropes. Ramsey heads back on the rebound right into a Side Belly to Belly Suplex as these fans angrily boo Matt for the way he got the upper hand to start this one off as he gets up watching as Beck crawls away from him running at him and delivering a knee to his gut that sends Beck rolling under the ropes to the ring apron as Matt finishes the job running and dropkicking his back sending him harshly down to the ground on the outside right in front of Alexis. ALEXIS TERRY: “Wow this is a beating.” Alexis chuckles, enjoying this as Beck crawls on the ground getting back up to his feet as Kluge pushes past the referee to the outside hoisting him up and turning him going to throw him into the ring steps only for Beck to really just kick into survival mode as he is able to instead send Kluge shoulder first into them as the fans go wild. Ramsey stumbles back taking a few deep breaths as Matt cringes while holding his shoulder. Ignoring the count by the referee Beck grabs him and rolls him back into the ring following right behind him. Matt gets up but Ramsey is already prepared almost doing a 180 with the offense as he now hits a Belly to Belly Suplex right back at him as the fans continue to show off their support for Beck. RILEY STEVENS: “Beck is starting to get into it.” Waiting for Kluge to hop back up he immediately nails him with a European Uppercut that only seems to slightly affect Matt as he crushes him with another this time it allows him to set up a Northern Lights Suplex which he uses to set up a pin attempt 1…Kick Out. Kluge was not about to lose that easily as both men hopped to their feet and it was Beck that kicked him in the gut going for a DDT only for Matt to spin out of it and connect with the ‘Hortonville Suplex Special 1’ a Suplex rolled into Mounted Punching which he uses violently on Beck. Ramsey throws him off and both men hop up and rush at one another right into a Double Clothesline as they both connect harshly with it. RILEY STEVENS: “And both men are down.” Now it is a test of toughness as both men recover and start to get back to their feet, they do so both probably a little dizzy as they throw punches back and forth finally breaking it up as Matt whips Beck into the corner rushing at him with a big clothesline attempt only for Beck to duck out of the way. Kluge bounces off holding his stomach turning around right into a Reverse DDT dropping him down to the mat as Beck yet again makes a cover, 1…Kick Out. Beck bounces off rolling right under the ropes looking to go springboard until Alexis gets his attention at the announcing table yelling at him. ALEXIS TERRY: “Only a few more weeks until you are irrelevant!” Beck looks over at her saying something back but he takes too much time as Kluge is back up now and knees him in the gut. Beck holds on to the ropes but Matt helps him back into the ring with a Vertical Suplex over the rope and back into the ring sending Beck into the mat hard. Kluge claps his hands together and then jumps on top for a cover 1…Kick Out. The fans cheer as Ramsey rolls over to get back up as Kluge gets up wanting to go ahead and get this thing over with as he hoists him into a ‘Hortonville Suplex Special 2’ a Inverted Suplex followed with a Gedo Clutch but Beck quickly kicks out of it. ALEXIS TERRY: “Beck just doesn’t know when to give up.” Kluge gets up annoyed that Beck yet again kicked out as he looks back at him watching him use the ropes to get up as he rushes at him with ‘The Matt Cooke’ a Running Knee to Beck’s knee but Ramsey has him well scouted as he dodges out of the way at the last second to avoid it. Not expecting to miss Matt leaves his back open as Beck follows it up with a German Suplex! Right away with Matt on the mat he jumps up delivering a Knee Drop. The fans go wild as he hops up now ready to go ahead and try to put this one away as he lines up for ‘The Collision’ a Running Test Boot. ALEXIS TERRY: “Be right back.” Suddenly Alexis throws off her head phones jumping up onto the ring apron as Beck turns around throwing a punch at her only for her to immediately jump right back down. He looks at her and then turns around still going for ‘The Collision’ but with his focus lost he runs right into ‘The Tiger's Roar Gold Edition’ a Whale Hunt from Kluge that connects hard as the fans boo and Matt makes the cover 1…2…3! JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…Matt Kluge!” The fans respond as you’d expect as Alexis laughs as Kluge rolls out of the ring holding up his arms in victory as Terry walks around the ring heading up the ramp while taunting the fans with her belt the entire time. RILEY STEVENS: “And Alexis cost’s Beck the match here tonight, I sort of figured it was going to happen.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Beck couldn’t keep his eyes on the ball.” Beck gets up in the ring immediately looking for Alexis as he rolls out of the ring rushing up the ramp as Alexis now hurries backwards as well as he goes in pursuit as we take a break. WINNER: MATT KLUGE “Busy Being Born” by Middle Class Rut plays over the P.A. System and the fans go wild as emerging from the back clad in his ring gear and a ‘GIW Exile’ t-shirt. He high fives fans as he makes his way to the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Always a treat to see, the GIW Television Champion Henry Malkin makes his way to the ring, receiving a huge reception from the fans here at the Baton Rouge River Center.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Change the record, I’m getting tired of seeing this guy talk all about the weak link division he’s champion of. Someone get Kluge out here to deport this guy back to England too.” Henry has a microphone in hand, and his television title strapped across his waist as he climbs dead center into the ring. He walks a few steps, pacing as his music fades away. HENRY MALKIN: “Last week, we all saw the result when you underestimate the GIW Television Champion. And as sad as things panned out for Nathan Bailey last week, we have to move forward, and forward is my match at ‘Exile’. April first, New Orleans Arena, will play host to my first title defense, where I will take on Angel Vegnaduzzo. Last week, after my riveting fifty second bout against Bailey I ran across…” Henry was cut off by the growing familiar tune of “Look Sharp” by Joe Jackson. The theme of Angel Vegnaduzzo who interrupts Henry with a few words of his own; the fans jeer and boo, Angel making his way down to the ring with a microphone in hand, speaking as he is walking down the ramp. ANGEL VEGNADUZZO: “Henry, Henry, Henry… Let’s not bore the good people here with a replay of what these people already saw. You must think that they’re stupid or something… Well they are, but any way. You’re making too much hype around our match at ‘Exile’. Like I told you last week, the match won’t take long; I’ll beat you before the bell even finishes ringing to begin the match.” Angel is in the ring now as Henry has a rebuttal, the fans firmly in his corner in this bout. HENRY MALKIN: “You’re going to beat me so easily… Well I’m beginning to believe that you actually think that you have a chance in hell of getting the win next Sunday. You and I are not so far apart as time in GIW is concerned, however I’m not as green as you when it comes down to competing. See Angel, you’ve run rampant through your fellow Evolve wrestlers, and you made an impressionable showing on Revolt. However you haven’t even come close to facing a skilled competitor such as myself. Look at the facts Angel, in two weeks I’ve had two minutes of ring time. I get the job done, so if there’s going to be a match as quick as you say it is, that’s because I’m going to come out a easy winner.” Malkin lowers the mic as the fans cheer him on as Angel rolls his eyes and then rebuttals. ANGEL VEGNADUZZO: “Listen Henry, I didn’t come out here to talk… I came out here to tell you and all these other idiots what’s going down at ‘Exile’. I’m going to get you in that ring, and I’m going to personally scrape the little stick of letters that spell your name off of my belt. And become the true champion that the Television title division deserves… And if you have a problem with that… Then that’s too damn bad.” With that Angel thrust his microphone too the mat and Henry tosses his as well. The two men come closer together, getting eye to eye. A final facedown before their eventually collision at ‘Exile’. QUOTE After a commercial break before the last segment we find that Henry Malkin stayed in the ring and his opponent tonight Matt Matlock has already made his walk down to the ring for his match here tonight. RILEY STEVENS: “Malkin has dominated two wrestlers in a row the past few weeks and now he gets one more match before his title defense against Angel Vegnaduzzo.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “That streak ends tonight.” Malkin hands out his belt as Angel surely has returned to the back to watch this one as the referee spins and calls for the bell. DING DING DING! The bell rings at Matlock comes full force at Malkin not seeming to learn a thing from watching the tapes of Henry’s previous two matches as he runs right into a Thrust Kick. Doubling him over Malkin follows it up immediately with an impressive Running Knee Strike that dazes Matt letting him bounce off the ropes and finish the three move combo with a Bulldog. These fans are into it as Henry calls for him to get up already seeming ready to end this, as Matlock gets to one knee then receives the ‘Euthanasia’ a Shining Wizard to the skull that puts Matt down and out as Henry rolls over hooking the leg 1…2…3! JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…Henry Malkin!” The fans pop as Malkin gets up recording this win in under a minute yet again for the third straight week as the referee holds up his arm in victory. RILEY STEVENS: “Henry Malkin has been a scary man these past three weeks, for the third time in a row he finishes his opponent in under a minute.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Ends when he meets Angel.” Malkin holds up his belt tonight for the final time before getting his chance to cement his status as champion when he meets Angel Vegnaduzzo at ‘Exile’ as he looks into the camera and mouths “Top that” to Angel Vegnaduzzo before his match next. WINNER: HENRY MALKIN Backstage we go where Beck Ramsey is seen storming through the halls in a puff as suddenly a big group of security guards get in front of him. BECK RAMSEY: “Get the hell out of my way.” Ramsey clearly in search of Terry tries to push past them but stops as David Sinclair now steps into frame. DAVID SINCLAIR: “Listen Beck I am sorry but we have decided that the best course of action is to have you removed from the building tonight.” Ramsey glares at him as Alexis now pops up behind the security guards as well as Beck counts heads realizing he is out numbered by a lot here. BECK RAMSEY: “Alexis you have security now but Sunday you will be stuck in a ring with me…And a bunch of tables…ladders…and chairs.” Beck now willingly heads to the door as Alexis watches him go as Sinclair points to the door sending the security group to make sure he doesn’t come back. QUOTE It is now time for the next match up of the evening the second of our two Television title participants warm up matches for ‘Exile’. This one was easily the more hyped of the two as two undefeated prospects would meet as “Brand New Day” by Ryan Star hits and the fans start to cheer starting to like this new star Ryan Cavallari already. RILEY STEVENS: “What a debut this man had last week a impressive match with Faith Simpson where he got the win.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Hate him.” Cavallari walks down to the ring high fiving a few fans along the way jumping up the steps into the ring as he throws his arms up for the crowd as his music cuts off and “Look Sharp!” by Joe Jackson now hits and the fans tune change as well as they begin to boo the Number One Contender to the Television belt as Angel Vegnaduzzo heads out with his usual smugness. RILEY STEVENS: “You know I may not like the guy much but two wins in a row here in GIW you got to think he’s a real threat to Malkin’s belt.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “By that you mean, he is our next champion.” Vegnaduzzo enters the ring looking across at Cavallari with a grin then heading to the top rope where he poses for a moment before jumping down as the referee steps in between them then calls for the bell. DING DING DING! The two circle around each other right off the bat the fans in this building getting ready to go as they meet in the center of the ring with a lock up for a test of strength, which quickly goes in the favor of Ryan as he pushes him back all the way to the ropes. The referee tells him to break due to the ropes and he does but Angel shows no respect slapping him across the face. Cavallari holds his face for a second then swings at him out of anger only for Vegnaduzzo to duck out of the way and then push him in the back into the corner as he backs up with a smirk on his face having out smarted Ryan a bit with that as Cavallari turns around nodding his head as if to give him a little bit of respect for his antics getting him a little angry as he heads across the ring back at him, as he dodges a wild punch from Angel then pulls him into a Knee to the gut. Doubling him over Ryan gets his early revenge with a big Snap Suplex to the mat. Both men hop right back to their feet however only Angel runs right into a Snap Hurricanrana, sending him into the mat as Ryan hops right back up. RILEY STEVENS: “Cavallari turns things around.” Cavallari backs away letting Angel get up, he does so brushing himself off as he glares back at him as they go in for another lock up. This time Angel doesn’t even try to make it a battle instead using both arms to drag his right arm behind the back. Cavallari throws his other elbow backwards to break it but Angel ducks it and then rolls him up while he has him unexpecting 1…Cavallari immediately kicks out as they rush to their feet but Angel gets up first and Dropkicks him right in the knee sending him to the mat in agony. Vegnaduzzo backs up leaning over the ropes with a grin as he looks out at the crowd who boos him as Ryan hops up now favoring his knee as Angel now follows it up with a Chop Block to the back of his knee, the same knee he just kicked. RILEY STEVENS: “Angel may have just taken out that knee.” Vegnaduzzo gets up to his feet with a smirk on his face as he realizes he has just done some serious damage as he backs away waiting to see Ryan get up as he does so, falling over into the ropes as soon as he tries to put pressure on it. Vegnaduzzo walks across the ring toying with him stalking him as Ryan throws punches to try to protect himself. Angel goes for that knee again grabbing a hold of it but as he lifts it up Ryan throws whatever he can to get out of that being an Enzuigiri. He falls to the ground but the kick lands on Angel who stumbles back in pain as Ryan crawls to the corner using it to prop himself up. But Angel recovers quickly rushing at him and there’s nothing for Ryan to do but take a big clothesline in the corner. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Cavallari is a sitting duck.” Ryan falls to the mat as Angel slaps his chest as he immediately heads up to the top rope, looking out at the crowd with a grin as you got to question why he would do this in a situation like this as Ryan lays on his back and Vegnaduzzo goes for a Moonsault, it looks pretty but Cavallari has the sense to roll out of the way sending Angel into the mat with a loud thud. The fans cringe as Vegnaduzzo gets up holding his gut and Cavallari does the same his knee starting to come back to normal as he limps across the ring waiting for him to get up and when he does he gives him a Inverted Power Slam. RILEY STEVENS: “Can he make the come back?” Cavallari now goes for the cover 1…2-Kick Out. Angel is able to catch his breath after the hard fall and recover enough to kick out. Ryan gets back up, turning back into his legs to go for some sort of submission but as he tries Angel throws up a big kick into that knee again sending Ryan again bouncing backwards holding it as Angel gets up immediately clotheslining him to the mat as he takes a few deep breaths before heading outside the ropes and going Springboard with a Dropkick that sends Ryan back to the mat. But Vegnaduzzo goes immediately for the ‘Sharpener’ a Texas Cloverleaf but before he can get him in, Ryan spins out. RILEY STEVENS: “Ryan dodged a bullet there.” Both men hop up to their feet and Cavallari swings at him only for Angel to duck it and roll him up with a huge handful of tights 1…2…Ryan finds a way to roll through 1…2…3! That is it. JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…Ryan Cavallari!” The fans explode as Angel kicks out a moment to late as the referee calls for the bell and Cavallari gets up still favoring his knee but now getting his arm raised by the referee. RILEY STEVENS: “Ryan found a way to get the win here tonight and improves to two wins here in GIW but you got to wonder how much this will shake Angel’s confidence?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “This was luck he will get the title at ‘Exile’.” Angel rolls out of the ring slamming his hands on the mat as he does before heading backwards up the ramp with the fans jeering him the whole way as the next match he has the stakes will be even higher. WINNER: RYAN CAVALLARI Hayden Carter is now shown in the backstage area prepping up for her match next as Daniel Starr and Paul Defias walk by her stopping when they see her walking over to her with smirks. DANIEL STARR: “Oh looks its Ms. Fifteen Minutes of Fame. Enjoying living off your one big win?” PAUL DEFIAS: “And your last win, all together.” The two share in a laugh as Hayden looks up at them rolling her eyes. HAYDEN CARTER: “It’s funny cause didn’t you lose to Alioth just a few weeks prior Daniel? But you know what that’s not important because tonight I get to beat you two up.” Hayden smiles at the thought as Jacob O’Connelly the Evolve Commissioner and Sinclair buddy walks into the frame. JACOB O’CONNELLY: “Wow this is convenient, needed to tell you all something.
