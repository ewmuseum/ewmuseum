GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: AGGRESSIVE EXPANSION LIVE! From the San Luis Hotel – June 23, 2013 BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! The San Luis Hotel has never been so jam packed full of people! 532 screaming GIW fans are lined around the ring, some sitting, some standing, but all focused in on the magnificent display of purple fire works that stream up into the Galveston sky above the glittering Gulf of Mexico. Ever since GIW was reopened one month ago, the fans have supported the federation wholeheartedly, and now it is time for Aggressive Expansion, one of the biggest shows of the year, to re-solidify GIW's place amongst the best wrestling promotions in the United States. Two large spotlights way in the night sky as a projector throws the GIW logo down into the center of the ring. It was truly a marvelous sight as the feed began to stream live on YouTube for iPPV. Alexis stumbles out after he’s a safe distance away, as Alioth rears up for a second Superkick, this time Terry ducks it and smack, the shot goes right into the referee who has so often been a thorn in Starre’ side tonight. WINNER: ALEXIS TERRY (New GIW Undisputed Champion)
