Moment of Glory II, 7 August 2011

7 August 2011

GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: MOMENT OF GLORY II LIVE! From Moody Gardens – August 7th, 2011 The crowd is wild, but when the number 10 appears on the TV's placed throughout the plaza and the lights begin to dim, everyone quiets down. The number counts down to nine, then eight, then seven... several women scream pure emotion... a golden light envelops the ring, dust falls like a golden shower into the fabled 'squared circle'. Six...five...four... the wind picks up from the coast. The smell of the ocean fills the arena; an ominous feeling is felt from the crowd. Silence. Three...two... golden light fades to black... One... we are left in the dark. Pitch black. Only the flashes from the cameras illuminate the night. Zero. Sound fills the arena. In the sweeping story of life, we don’t remember days. Only moments. As a child you had a vision of the future; and you promised yourself you would struggle, fight and bleed for this chance. As a man, you awaken on the threshold of your own legend. So much work is waiting, but this moment is glorious. Today your soul is charged with the electricity of new beginnings. Today you can reflect on those powerful moments when you knew somebody loved you, somebody believed in you. Heroes of quality and character showed you the way, and now it is your moment to carry those kinds of expectations. Are you good enough to make other better? To be remembered? Are you good enough to be great? Today you will be judged, but no matter when your chosen, it is you who determines your worth. Today it’s not the effort, or the game. This is your life. Push away the fear and roll up your sleeve. Be in this moment. Let the new journey begin. This is your moment of glory. A new GIW opening package is shown, Trent Lost posing on the top rope, Aspen holding out her title, Kluge stomping a mud hole in some poor soul, Alex Castellanos hitting the Golden Arrow on an unknown wrestler, Alioth Starre getting the pinfall…the package fades away, then the words “One Year Ago” appear on the screen. The words fade away, and the black background fades into the San Luis Hotel and Resort, twenty or so people crowded around a wrestling ring, and Aspen Chaud holding the title in her hand on the top rope. Aspen fades into the background after we freeze on her joy, then the words “Thank you.” appear in golden letters. We fade out again, into silence from the fans. Several whistles can be heard, but everything is still. BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! The golden fireworks stream high into the night sky as “Red Dress” by TV on the Radio blasts over the speakers. The fans are on their feet, a crowd of over one thousand screaming fans are piled into Moody Gardens. The towering water slides of Shlitterbahn are illuminated in a wash of gold and the moon is shining bright. The ION TV logo appears in the bottom right of the screen and the LIVE! Banner appears on the top left. We pan around and look at the signs, “Matt Kluge is my baby daddy!”, “News Flash: Aspen is on her period all the time!”, and “SUPER STARRE!!!” are signs that stick out. The fans are on a steep incline, so it looks as if they are staring down at you. Gold and purple lights bounce around the arena, we cut to Riley Stevens and Oliver Thibodeaux, both dressed in suits, sitting at the announce booth. RILEY STEVENS: “Ladies and gentlemen, welcome to the show that has been in the making for one year. It is the most respected and anticipated show in Galveston Island Wrestling. It book ends our year. It creates legends. It forms stars. It never ceases to amaze. This is Moment of Glory II.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “We have waited OVER one full year to taste this, and I was about to go insane. There are titles at stake, there are careers at stake, there are lives at stake. These men and women in the back have donated their blood, sweat, and tears to this sport for this one chance. This one moment. This Moment of Glory.” RILEY STEVENS: “This is a dog eat dog profession we are in. Everyone who is here tonight, everyone who is on this card has displayed enough passion, enough vigor, enough love for this sport to earn a spot here. There weren’t any last minute additions. There wasn’t any settling for a lesser man. This is truly the best of the best that we have to offer, and they know that. Tonight is the night they give it their all.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “This is more than just a wrestling match to these competitors; this is their lives. Every day they wake up and train for this day, they dream of this day. Why? Because they know that with a win here - they thrust themselves into immortality. They will live forever in the hearts and minds of you fans with a win tonight, that’s all any wrestler wants. Regardless of their attitude. Regardless of what they say. Regardless of what they have done in the past - deep inside all a wrestler wants is to be remembered for what they are: warriors of the people.” RILEY STEVENS: “There are fives matches scheduled for tonight, but then again anything can happen. There are five chances for immortality and fourteen wrestlers that must compete for that honor. Tonight, we will start our show off with a battle for respect and the Television Championship with Nick Chaos and Seth Seeker. Nick Chaos disrespected Seeker and tried to cost him a match, and in her last order as the Revolt General Manager, La’Sarah Audric booked this match to settle their odds. Which man can leave Galveston as the Television Champion? Can Seth Seeker restore his dominant image? Can Nick Chaos prove he is more than your average little twit?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “After that, we have the staple match of Moment of Glory - the Seize the Moment Match. In this match, you must pin two of your opponents, then ascend a ladder and grab a briefcase that would guarantee you a title match of your choice any time in the next year. Competing in this match are a mix of GIW veterans and rising stars. Beginning the list is Ace Baldwin, the pretty boy from the Midwest Wrestling Alliance that signed here as well. He looks to prove that he is more than an outsider here in GIW. Next, we have David Graves - a man who has sort of fell through the cracks here thanks to missed opportunities, he looks to jump back on the winning wagon. Then we have the scruffy, grizzled veteran Hank Mardukas. When Hank signed with GIW, he lacked a spark necessary to be a winner here. Over the past few weeks, Hank found it. He is now a dominant force in the company and is looking a moment to break the glass ceiling. Also in the match is the stunning girlfriend of Trent Lost, the talent Katya Rose. Many people call her the next Aspen Chaud, and she wants to prove it tonight. Following her is one half of the GIW Tag Team Champions, Leo James. He made Kluge pass out on Revolt a few weeks ago, so this guy has plenty of talent to offer up, can he show it here? And finally, we have the favorite to win the match, Mark Cross. Over the past few weeks, Cross has told GIW Insiders that he wants to change Aspen back to the Aspen he used to know - a good girl. Winning this match would catapult him to the main event, a place he is very familiar with. If Aspen wins tonight, he will get a chance to fufill his goal, if not, something tells me he will be fine dismantling Alioth Starre as well.” RILEY STEVENS: “But that’s not even half of the card. Next, we have the North Beach Title Match - a Last Man Standing bout between Alex Castellanos and Trent Lost, they have been feuding back and forth for as long as a normal person can remember. Alex is one third of the super group, Absolution, along with The Shadow and Aspen Chaud, whom Trent used to team with. Alex Castellanos defeated Trent Lost for the title months ago with some help from Aspen Chaud, and now Trent wants to get even. These two men must beat each other down so much that the other cannot answer a ten count - there will be blood shed. Who will exit the ring with gold around their waist? The Golden Age or the Lost Cause Crusader?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Next up is Matt Kluge taking on The Shadow. Kluge was originally supposed to battle Wake Walker, but the Shadow ended that pretty quickly when he demolished Wake and sent him packing. These two were brought together by fate - this match will be decided somewhere in Moody Gardens. Matt Kluge wont ever give up, the man always has fight left inside, but will that fire finally be extinguished by the Shadow in a match that surely will be one for the ages?” RILEY STEVENS: “Then, we have the main event. Alioth Starre, champion. Aspen Chaud, self proclaimed champion. These two are undoubtedly the best wrestlers in GIW. Week in and week out, they put on an amazing show. They will fight until they can’t fight any more, but just in case Aspen tries to pull a fast one with Absolution, Blake Beckett has enlisted Joshua Acquin as the special enforcer.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Joshua Acquin, a wash up. I’m sorry Riley… I’ve been good all night reading this rehearsed stuff, but now it is time to be real. Joshua Acquin is a punk. He just needs the money, he has no qualifications to be a special enforcer because that idiot couldn’t win a match on his own when he was here. Aspen Chaud will win this match fair and square, and I wouldn’t doubt it if Alioth and Acquin where scheming up some sort of plan to keep the belt with him.” RILEY STEVENS: “I was beginning to think this show would be good since you were on your best behavior. Oh well, lets send it over to Colt Cox who is backstage with the man who will be competing for the TV Title in a few moments, Nick Chaos.” The camera quickly flashes over to the GIW Interview Lounge, which is now totally refurbished with a plasma TV, black carpet, and a black leather couch. However, Nick Chaos and Colt Cox are not sitting. They are standing in front of a massive GIW Logo Cutout that lines the back wall. Chaos looks extremely focused as we start the interview. COLT COX: “Nick, although you and Seeker arrived at around the same time in Galveston Island Wrestling, I am sure you are aware that he was here a while back - and was extremely impressive. How has that changed the way you have prepared for this match against him tonight?” Nick Chaos nods, still looking out in the direction opposite of Cox. Slowly he turns his head around and snickers. NICK CHAOS: “Colt, it doesn’t matter that Seth Seeker was here in the past. The past isn’t important. What is important is now, and the future. Right now, I am the best acquisition GIW has made as of late, and after just two matches - look where I am. I am on the biggest show of the year while all of the others that signed near the same time I did are stick back on Extreme TV, or Full Throttle, or whatever the hell it is now. The point is, Colt, in my few weeks here I have made an impact. Seeker is living off of what he has accomplished in the past. The fans are used to him, the management knows him. That is why he is here. He doesn’t deserve to be here, only I do. I shouldn’t have to even compete for this title. But, regardless of that fact, when I win tonight… when I pin his big, fat shoulders to the mat and I take my title and I stand over him and soak up the crowd’s ovation… you will see; THEY ALL will see why I am the future of this company. And if you don’t believe that after tonight… then you must not be a believing king of man, Colt.” Nick nods, then promptly exits to Colt’s side. We cut over to the ring, where their match is getting ready to begin. QUOTE As we return to the ring Nick Chaos theme hits as the fans begin to boo as out comes the very cocky star heading towards the ring with a big smirk on his face receiving nothing but “praise” from the crowd who’ve quickly begun to hate him. JAMAL JACKSON: “The following is a GIW Television Championship match! First entering the ring weighing in at two hundred and seventy five pounds hailing from St. Louis, Missouri…he is “The Chaotic Nightmare” NICK CHAOS!” RILEY STEVENS: “Not many people get an opportunity as fast as Nick Chaos has here in GIW. Winning his match last week led him right into this contest for the GIW Television Championship.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I like the kid he has the confidence to get the job done.” Chaos enters the ring looking at the title belt that is currently in the referee’s hands as “Nightmare” by Avenged Sevenfold hits as the fans begin to cheer, whether it be due to tenure or just the lesser of the two evils out comes Seth Seeker completely calm as he heads towards the ring. JAMAL JACKSON: “Next entering the ring weighing in at two hundred and sixty five pounds hailing from Calgary, Alberta, Canada…he is “The Grim Reaper” SETH SEEKER!” RILEY STEVENS: “Since his return this man has looked dominant, can he get the job done against this new star?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I doubt it.” Seeker heads up the steps and into the ring looking across at Chaos who grins back at him not seeming to fear the reaper as the referee looks at both men walking between them and holding up the belt in the air before walking it over to the side of the ring and handing it out as the bell is rung. DING DING DING! The crowd roars in approval for the first match on the big show as Chaos and Seeker begin to circle around one another as Chaos jumps in after him for a lock up only to be tossed by Seeker. The crowd cheers this as Nick gets to his feet angrily going back after him only again receiving the same treatment. This time when he gets up he goes for the knee as he does a dropkick to Seeker’s right knee as he stumbles backwards into the corner as Nick realizes that if he is to win he needs to take down the giant. Running at him in the corner Chaos lands a big clothesline as Seeker was trying to tend to his knee as Nick lets him fall to a seated position as he walks away slowly taunting the crowd before running forward and using his knee to crush the face of Seth Seeker. The fans boo not expecting this early domination from Nick as this time he slides out of the ring walking straight up to a fan and trash talking to him. Wasting valuable time Seth gets to his feet in the ring as Chaos returns going after him only to receive a big elbow from a recovered Seth Seeker. Rushing forward seeming fully healed he crushes him down with a Clothesline. The fans are starting to get going now for him as Nick hops up right into a Double Arm DDT! Seeker makes the cover 1…Kick Out! Not close to enough. Getting up he seems ready to crush him, calling for him to get up. Chaos does so only he does so right between Seeker’s legs where Seth grabs him and smirks to the crowd as he lifts him up for a Powerbomb! The crowd goes wild waiting for the end but Chaos manages to slip over him falling down behind him and quickly landing a big clothesline to Seeker’s right knee again bringing the big man down. RILEY STEVENS: “Nick continues to try and work over that knee to take down the big man.” Nick gets up with a smirk as Seth continues to favor the knee as he tries to get up but Chaos puts him right down with a DDT! The fans boo as he makes the cover 1…2…Kick Out! Still not enough as Seeker literally throws him off. This causes the fans to get some hope but before he can even get up Nick runs forward grabbing his leg lifting it up and throwing it down on the knee. The fans continue to boo his very one dimensional attack but as he heckles a heckler it doesn’t seem like Chaos cares as he waits in the corner for Seeker to get up as he lifts him up and throws him into the corner. Running at him he nails a big clothesline then immediately wraps up his head and runs forward for a Bulldog but at the last second Seeker bails himself out by throwing Chaos across the ring. The fans go wild for him as Nick hops up and runs at him but gets pulled right into a Belly to Belly Suplex! The crowd is going nuts for him but that dies down when he falls to one knee holding his hurt one. RILEY STEVENS: “The longer this goes the more it favors Chaos who may have really hurt that knee.” The match continues as Seeker forces himself up to his feet as a little dazed Chaos gets up to his feet throwing a wild punch that ends up with him receiving a big German Suplex! The crowd goes wild as Seeker goes for the cover 1…2…Kick Out! Still not enough to put the brash superstar away as Seth gets up looking rather focused knowing he needs to put him away soon looking to go for the ‘Don't Fear The Reaper’ a Jackhammer! The crowd goes wild as he lifts him up but right away Nick is able to fall back down to his own feet kicking Seth in the gut and jumping up for a Dropkick! Quickly crawling towards him for the cover he goes for it 1…2…Out of no where Seeker rolls over on top right into the ‘Graveyard’ an Arm Triangle Submission! The crowd goes insane for this turn of events as Nick tries his best to get out of it before verbally submitting to the referee as he turns and rings the bell the crowd goes insane. JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner and the NEW GIW Television Champion…“The Grim Reaper” SETH SEEKER!” The crowd goes wild for Seeker as Nick rolls out of the ring in pain as the referee grabs the belt and hands it in to Seth Seeker who grabs it and heads to the top rope with it, his first title here in GIW. RILEY STEVENS: “After a string of unimpressive champions I think we finally found someone that will represent the GIW Television Championship with the respect it deserves!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I say he got lucky.” As he is celebrating out of no where out comes Damien Dunn, Johnny Stylez, and Kevin Mero! The fans seem at first confused until they slide into the ring all three surrounding Seeker who looks at them all then goes after Stylez. However the three collapse on him all three starting to beat him down getting him down on the mat and beginning to stomp away at him to the boos of the crowd. RILEY STEVENS: “What the heck are these guys doing? Early tonight Damien Dunn told us we hadn’t seen the last of him is this what he meant? The other two men Kevin Mero and Johnny Stylez are only debuting tonight…And they chose to do it like this?” The fans continue to boo as Dunn grabs up the Television title as Mero and Stylez lift him up as Dunn spins around and crushes him across the face with it to a loud boo from the crowd as Seeker crumbles to the mat as Damien walks over asking for a microphone from ringside. DAMIEN DUNN: “I promised you a revolution did I not? And what you see before you is just that. We are the Syndication and we will take this god forsaken company over from the ground up. I told you I was sick and tired of Blake Beckett and his lackey's running GIW into the ground with his politics. I was tired of sitting in the back and watching this travesty happened. So, I took it upon myself to do something about it. And now I'm going to unleash this cancer upon GIW. I am the uncrowned GIW Undisputed champion. I beat Alioth single handedly in the middle of the ring and how was I rewarded? Was I ever given a title shot? No. Was I moved up the ranks in this piece of crap company? No. I was thrown into a dark match at Moment of Glory II? What does that show you? It shows me that we got complete idiots running this company and if I keep sitting back and allowing this to happen it's going to be run into the ground. I will beat every last so called superstar in GIW into the ground until my point has been proven. You watching Blake? This ain’t your fuckin' company anywhere. We're taken it over and there's not a god damn thing to can do about it but sit back and watch it happen. You made your bed....and now you can lie in it!” Dunn drops the microphone as he walks out alongside Stylez and Mero as Seeker struggles to get to his feet as we head to the back. WINNER: SETH SEEKER (New GIW Television Champion) After we finally cut away from Seth Seeker’s title victory, we see Mark Cross stretching backstage in front of the women’s bathroom. From the hallway off screen, Ace Baldwin comes into the shot, chuckling at Cross’s pre-game choices. ACE BALDWIN: “Oh look at you, scared you will pull a hammy?” Cross barely acknowledges him, he just keeps stretching. MARK CROSS: “The arena is the other direction, or do you find wandering the halls picking on people pleasurable, Ace?” Ace laughs, then crosses his arms and leans against the wall Cross is using to stretch against. He looks out towards the arena. ACE BALDWIN: “Yeah, it is kind of fun I guess. Out there Cross, out there is where all of my goals will be obtained. When I grab that case, I will be the number one contender for every title in the federation. I would like that kind of power, wouldn’t you Mark?” Cross doesn’t look at Ace, he keeps stretching, this time digging deeper than he has before, maybe letting out any anger from Ace being around. MARK CROSS: “Titles aren’t everything, Ace. You’ll learn that some day.” Ace chuckles, then looks at Cross with a smirk on his face, shaking his head. ACE BALDWIN: “You know, you would probably be the Undisputed Champion right now if you had some drive in you.” Now it is Cross who laughs, but he still won’t look at Ace. MARK CROSS: “I have plenty of drive. More drive than you, that’s for sure. I just don’t need a tacky waist ornament to prove I am the best. I like to let the fans decide that for me.” Ace slides down the wall and sits by Cross who still is so immersed in the stretching to bother looking at him. Ace continues anyway. ACE BALDWIN: “You can’t let the fans determine who the best is, you have to force it down their throats. Let’s be honest here, Mark. Between us two guys, is Aspen Chaud really the best wrestler here now? What about Alioth Starre? Those are the ones in the main event, and now we are stuck down here. In the low card. Working our way to the top. Do WE deserve that? Hell know we don’t. But you know why we don’t get the same treatment those two do? Because we don’t get on TV and blab every week. That’s why we are here. Graves, Rose, Mardukas, James… they are all capable of beating Aspen or Alioth, but they are stuck under the glass ceiling here.” Cross stops stretching, and then looks at Baldwin. ACE BALDWIN: “We beat each other down until we have to quit, then we are replaced like the little worker mice we are. Meanwhile Aspen beats down on Alioth, Alioth returns the favor, Alioth retires - replace with Kluge. Repeat. It’s an endless cycle Mark, and we are trapped in inside of it.” Ace shakes his head, we hear a flush of a toilet. The door opens, out comes Aspen Chaud. She stands behind the two men, chuckling. ASPEN CHAUD: “News flash, boys. When I win that title, I am going to show you why I am in that spot, not you. That is, if either of you can even the match.” Aspen exits down the hallway. Ace and Mark look at each other. ACE BALDWIN: “I’ve got to go get my elbow pads on. Good luck Cross, because I know I want that gold more than you do.” Ace exits, the camera stays on Cross for a moment. He shakes his head, then continues stretching. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = We cut to the GIW Interview Lounge where Zachary Pagonis is standing by with the GIW Undisputed Champion, Alioth Starre who has the title around his waist. ZACHARY PAGONIS: “With me now is Alioth Starre - man who has been nearly unstoppable since arriving here in Galveston Island Wrestling. Alioth, what is your secret?” Alioth nods and chuckles, he crosses his legs. ALIOTH STARRE: “Don’t consider it a secret, Zachary. I am very upfront with my wrestling abilities. What you see is what you get, actually, maybe my secret is my stability and predictability. Most people here are not stable in any meaning of the word, and most people here try to hard to be unpredictable. I may be a tad crazy, sure, but it works for me now and it always has in the past. I see no reason to change.” Zachary nods, then moves on. ZACHARY PAGONIS: “What weaknesses do you want to exploit in Aspen tonight?” Alioth searches for an answer, then offers his reply. ALIOTH STARRE: “Aspen is mentally weak. That is the tragic flaw in the epic hero, or heroine. She believes she has already won the match - in head I believe she honestly does. However, I’m a lot stronger in real life than in imagination. She, and everyone else, will realize that this evening.” Zachary nods, he begins to close the interview. ZACHARY PAGONIS: “Thank you, Mr. Starre.” Alioth nods and smiles, then we cut to the ring where the Seize the Moment match is getting ready to begin. QUOTE When we return to the ring already standing in the ring waiting are the likes of Katya Rose, Leo James, and Hank Mardukas looking ready to go as the ladders are shown around the ring and the briefcase with the paperwork for a title shot hangs above the ring. JAMAL JACKSON: “The following contest is the Seize the Moment Match! The rules are as follows you must first pin two of your opponents before you are eligible to climb the ladder and grab the briefcase. Standing in the ring are Katya Rose, Leo James, and Hank Mardukas.” Most of them get cheers as "Libera Me From Hell" by Tarantula hits to a rather confused reaction from the crowd as out from the back comes Jack Waters looking as confident as ever as he heads towards the ring. JAMAL JACKSON: “Heading to the ring he is a late replacement for Ace Baldwin; he is “The King of Darkness” JACK WATERS!” The fans give him a mixed reaction as they aren’t sure what to think of late replacements as he heads towards the ring confidently. RILEY STEVENS: “You know you have to give props to these guys for stepping in on short notice, this is not an easy match but the prize is still huge. Apparently Ace Baldwin is out with an undisclosed illness, so a huge opportunity for Jack Waters.” He heads up the steps and into the ring staring across at the other three competitors then up to the briefcase as “My Curse” by Killswitch Engage hits and the fans wait to see who it is before booing as Kevin Mero one of the attackers earlier tonight heads out looking focused. JAMAL JACKSON: “Heading to the ring he is a late replacement for David Graves; he is “The Soul Survivor” KEVIN MERO!” The fans don’t let up on him as the debuting star heads up the steps and into the ring the competitors taking note of him not knowing who he is. RILEY STEVENS: “This man was part of that attack on Damien Dunn and he is filling in for the recently released David Graves, what a story it would be if he won.” The five stand in the ring as “Lift Me Up” by Moby hits and this changes the whole reflection of the match as out comes Mark Cross to easily the loudest cheers of the night as he heads towards the ring looking ready to go. JAMAL JACKSON: “And the final participant he is “The Dragon” MARK CROSS!” The crowd continues to go wild for him as he enters the ring all eyes on him as he heads to the top rope. RILEY STEVENS: “He’s the best wrestler in the right now but how will he fair when the ladders come into play?” All six competitors walk to the center of the ring each of them staring up at the briefcase hanging above them as suddenly the bell is rung to get this thing started. DING DING DING! Right away the match gets underway as the competitors all begin to brawl around the ring as Cross corners Mardukas…Waters goes to work on Rose…while Mero goes for James. The fans are going nuts as fists fly and go all over the place as finally Cross spins and tosses Mardukas out of the ring following him out there as in the ring Mero throws James through the ropes and then walks across the ring crushing Waters in the back with a punch. Agreeing to work together with Rose the two run at Jack for a double clothesline but he ducks it and then stops on a dime rushing at them and hitting a clothesline on both of them instead! The crowd is busy watching Cross on the outside taking it to Mardukas with big punch after big punch. Inside of the ring a recovered Leo slides into the ring running at Waters only to be lifted up right into a Double A Spinebuster! Going for the pin right away 1…2…Rose kicks him off. While this is not a one fall match up, the more you can stall others from getting points the smarter. Waters gets up stalking her as she backs up a bit; however it’s Mero that comes from behind crushing him down to the mat. Rose and Mero start to stomp him down before Rose goes for the cover herself, however Mero quickly pulls her off shaking his head no as she tries to tell him about a new plan before she receives a ‘Head Trauma’ down to the mat as he makes the cover 1…2…3! JAMAL JACKSON: “Kevin Mero has one fall.” On the outside Cross has just landed an Exploder Suplex on Hank and hearing the announcement quickly rushes back into the ring. The fans cheer him on as he slides in jumps up and quickly nails a Running Forearm to Mero! Then when Leo is up he gives him one as well. Finally he hits one on Waters and then one to Rose as the crowd goes insane for him. Rose falls out of the ring to get a breather while Waters sits in the corner in pain. Mero sits along the edge of the ring however Cross rushes and dropkicks him down off the ring apron to the outside. This leaves just Leo who gets up slowly right into Mark’s always perfect Go 2 Sleep! However to everyone’s surprise just after he hits it Waters gets up pushing him out of the ring then making the cover himself, 1…2…3! JAMAL JACKSON: “Jack Waters has one fall.” The fans boo his actions as he gets up giving them a little shrug as if to say, so what as James rolls out of the ring and coming back into the ring is Hank Mardukas looking to get a pin fall. Rushing at Waters he goes but as he does he runs right into a Spear! The crowd boos him as Cross tries to get back into the ring but receives a big boot from the currently dominate Jack Waters. Quickly turning around he grabs up Hank, kicking him in the gut then rushing off the ropes and hitting a Clothesline from Hell! Going down for a second cover 1…2…3! He is eligible. JAMAL JACKSON: “Jack Waters has two falls.” The fans boo him as he gets up taunting everyone having now become the first man to qualify. However with Mero and James fighting on the outside the only man left in the ring is Cross who waits for him to turn around still angry about losing his pin fall Waters turns right into Mark who lifts him onto his shoulders with a smile on his face before ending him with the Go 2 Sleep, before making the cover to finally get his first pin fall, 1…2…3! JAMAL JACKSON: “Mark Cross has one fall.” Waters is able to roll out of the ring while Katya slides back into the ring looking to get an advantage as she catches Mark from behind. On the outside Mero is able to land a Big Boot to Leo on the outside. Heading back into the ring is Kevin who again joins arms with Rose to take on Cross. The two grab him and toss him into the corner, Rose runs at him first only for Cross to kick up sending her to the mat. But Mero comes so quickly Mark doesn’t have time to avoid his big clothesline in the corner. Cross falls to the mat as Rose gets up to her feet choking out Cross in the corner. However as she is distracted Mero stalks her from behind with a grin on his face as she turns around he kicks her in the gut and then puts her up and slamming her down with a Powerbomb holding her shoulders down 1…2…3! As she walks out the camera cuts over to Joshua Acquin sitting at ringside next to the announce table for his role as Special Guest Enforcer. RILEY STEVENS: “It’s been a long time since we’ve seen Aspen Chaud win anything by herself…With Acquin at ringside she’ll have to do just that here tonight in the main event.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “You people act like she is incapable of doing so.” She heads up the steps and into the ring looking across the ring to the outside at Acquin scowling at him as she heads up to the top rope posing as “Subterraneans” by David Bowie cuts her entrance off as the fans react with more of a mixed reaction. Most of them still boo but when it comes down to it, they respect Alioth just a bit more for at least doing it on his own. Though when he walks out he doesn’t seem to care about it all, the belt shining around his waist as he heads towards the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “You know you can hear the crowd seems in favor Alioth but at the end of the day if they had a choice these fans wouldn’t want either of them walking out with the belt.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Thankfully it’s not up to them.” Starre heads up the steps and into the ring holding up his belt high into the air as if to taunt Aspen before heading up to the top rope posing with it again. Hopping down he hands the belt to the referee as Jamal prepares for introductions. JAMAL JACKSON: “The following contest is scheduled for one fall and it is for the GIW Undisputed Championship! Introducing first the challenger she weighs in at one hundred and twenty pounds and hails from Hollywood, California…“The Queen of Galveston” ASPEN CHAUD!” The crowd boos her as she raises one arm into the air as Jackson turns to Alioth now. JAMAL JACKSON: “And the champion he weighs in at one hundred and sixty pounds and hails from Brixton, London, England…the ‘GIW Undisputed Champion’ ALIOTH STARRE!” Again mostly boos overshadow the few cheers as Starre stares across at Aspen, as the referee raises the title in between them. Aspen staring up at it as the referee turns and hands it out and then calls for the bell. DING DING DING! This is the main event of the biggest show of the year and the fans are having to choose between two people whom they hate. Aspen begins the trash talk across the ring as the two start to circle one another. Aspen decides to test his strength as the two lock up, right away and though Starre isn’t a physically imposing person he’s still stronger then her as he pushes her back into the corner, as the referee calls for him to pull off. He pretends to do so but then slaps her across the face! The fans cheer this a bit, as Aspen angrily runs at him right into a Hip Toss. She hops up still angry from the slap throwing a wild clothesline but he ducks it and then rolls her up 1…2…Kick Out! Chaud hops up angry at him as he grins a bit having done just what he wanted. She throws another big elbow at his grinning face but this time it lands as she hits another and another then whipping him off the ropes. She throws a clothesline but he ducks it and runs off the ropes but this time on the rebound she jumps up into the air into a Thesz Press followed with Punches on the ground. Starre throws her off as the two head right back to their feet but she is up first and rolls him up 1…Kick Out! She lifts him up in the air but he slaps away her hands and whips her into the corner. He runs full steam at her, but she jumps up wrapping her legs around his head and then pushing off into an unexpected Hurricanrana! But Starre waste no time getting up as the two run at one another and go for dual clotheslines but instead they both simply take one another both going down. RILEY STEVENS: “Some fast paced action to start this match neither star getting the advantage.” Both of them start to get up at the same time as they go in for another lock up this time, she grabs him and whips him into the corner. Once there she runs at him hitting a flying forearm, which drops him into a seated position. Immediately using her feet she begins to choke him out using her feet as the referee counts it 1…2…3…4…At the last second she pulls away as she listens to the referee walking away but rushing around him and landing a running knee as he’s in the corner. Quickly dragging Starre out away from the ropes she goes for the pin 1…Kick Out. Not even any doubt in anyone’s head he’d kick out from that one. Both of them now get up to their feet, both of them looking in decent shape she goes for the ‘Queen's Wrath II’ a Heel Kick but at the last moment he ducks it. Her leg goes flying over her his head as he gets behind her and whips her into the corner. Going full blast he nails her with a huge Running European Uppercut that sends her falling forward onto her back. Heading off the ropes he brings down a big Fist Drop onto her then makes the cover 1…Kick Out! Rather then wasting any time he transitions it right into a Body Scissors. Which gets the fans on the edge of the seats, but unfortunately she is too close to the ropes and gets one foot draped over it. RILEY STEVENS: “Alioth is starting to get it going.” Alioth breaks the hold at a three count rolling off as Aspen attempts to get up. Alioth bounces off the ropes going for a dropkick that would send her out of the ring. But impressively Aspen’s able to roll up and out of the way. Starre gets up on the other side of the ropes, she runs at him to try to knock him off but he shoulders her away. He grabs the ropes preparing to go springboard but as he does so she runs forward swiping his feet causing him to fall down onto the ropes, immediately holding his groin. The male fans in attendance let out groans as Aspen grabs the ropes shaking him up and down as the referee counts. As she breaks it Alioth falls off landing right on the ring apron. Now it’s her turn as she runs and dropkicks him out of the ring landing hard on the outside. Rolling out with him she taunts the crowd at ringside. Knowing that she can’t win with a count out she grabs Alioth and attempts to toss him into the ring but at the last second he reverses and tosses her right into the side of the ring. Hitting her back hard Alioth grabs her and shoulders her into it again. With the referee at a count of six he grabs her and throws her into the ring, following her quickly in. However taking the time to taunt ends up costing him as he heads into the ring she catches him with a big low blow! That the referee seems to not have noticed as he falls down into the ring as she covers him 1…2…Kick Out! RILEY STEVENS: “A blatant low blow there but it doesn’t pay off.” Aspen gets up angrily staring at the referee telling him to count quicker. She goes to grab his legs but he kicks her up and she stumbles right back into the referee. Starre hops up and then goes for a Superkick! But at the last possible moment Aspen ducks it and the shot lands right into the face of the referee. He goes out cold into the corner as Aspen rakes Alioth’s eyes then dropkicks him! The fans booing this as she looks around calling for someone. All the sudden over the barricade comes Damien Dunn! The fans are shocked as he grabs the belt and gets up onto the apron to give it to her. But before she can Acquin pulls his legs out from under him sending him falling right into the side of the ring head first! The fans go wild for Acquin as he grabs Dunn and throws him over the barricade, as Chaud screams at him that it’s not fair. But as she turns around she does so right into a Fisherman Suplex Pin! The crowd goes wild as the referee crawls over still dazed 1…2…KICK OUT! Aspen gets her shoulders up at the last minute. RILEY STEVENS: “Acquin stops the potential interference and Aspen just barely kicks out.” The fans are now on Alioth’s side after Aspen’s attempted interference and all of them are standing now sensing that this is almost the end. And as Starre gets up you can tell he does as well. He looks at her legs and starts to try for the Figure Four but before he can she kicks him off! The fans sigh as Aspen hops up to her feet and whips him right away into the ropes. Rushing into him she hits the ‘Tush Push’ a Turnbuckle Flying Butt Shot! The crowd boos as Starre stumbles out and she heads to the top rope following it up with the ‘AirBarbie’ a Flying Crossbody right into a pin 1…2..Kick Out! Close but no cigar! Both get to their feet but Alioth has his back to Aspen and in a very desperate state she grabs him and nails him with the ‘Queen's Wrath I’ a Double Knee Backbreaker! The crowd almost has nightmares of her title celebration as the referee counts 1…2…Kick Out! RILEY STEVENS: “He just kicked out of all of her arsenal!” The fans are going wild at this point waiting to see who wins the match, as she hops to her feet with doubt in her eyes as she tries to plead with the referee that it was three. But when he shrugs her off she turns as anger fills her eyes as she goes to grab his heel for the ‘K.M.A.’ but as she does Alioth out of no where rolls her over going for the Figure Four again. But sensing danger Aspen grabs the ropes to avoid it. Alioth gets up walking across the ring as Aspen also gets up, running at him but almost immediately having suckered her into the middle of the ring Starre drops her down right into the Figure Four Leg Lock! The crowd goes insane, as Aspen tries to break free hoping to get to the ropes but Alioth has her right in the middle of the ring…The crowd is on their feet as they almost all wait for the inevitable as the pain gets to her and she taps out! JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner and STILL GIW UNDISPUTED CHAMPION…ALIOTH STARRE!” RILEY STEVENS: “Starre made her tap, he made her tap! He is still the champion!” The crowd basically all jumped on his bandwagon when Aspen’s interference came go wild for him having picked the lesser of the two evils as the referee hands him his title belt which will still have his name as well as the word Undisputed as he falls to his knees holding the belt to his chest. RILEY STEVENS: “What a night this was…two new champions as Trent Lost and Seth Seeker bring home gold. Mark Cross is your Seize the Moment winner…Matt Kluge survives…and Alioth Starre is still the Undisputed Champion of GIW!” Here comes the confetti falling down into the ring as an angry Aspen Chaud is helped to the back and inside the ring Starre is up and holding the title into the air showing it off to all of the doubters as the Age of Starre continues as he jumps off that turnbuckle and heads to the other, as we are left to wonder is this man stoppable? WINNER: ALIOTH STARRE (Still GIW Undisputed Champion)
