GIW — Card

Revolt, 14 July 2011

14 July 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: THURSDAY NIGHT REVOLT LIVE! From Bolivar Lighthouse – July 14th, 2011 BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! As the show gets going we are taken straight to the backstage area where Blake Beckett is seen getting call on his phone, he answers it very seriously as the person on the other line speaks. BLAKE BECKETT: “Ok mom, I will fly out right now…No it’s fine La’Sarah can handle the show…Yeah I’ll be right there.” Blake hangs up the phone, then quickly looks around for someone as he walks over to Amanda Rowland the only one near him. BLAKE BECKETT: “I have a family issue I need to get to, can you make sure that La’Sarah knows that the show is in her hands tonight?” Amanda nods her heads happily, as Beckett seems to double take whether she is someone he can even trust to do that as he quickly heads out the door in a hurry as we cut back to the ring. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = Right into the ring we go as the opening pyro goes off and the crowd goes wild all of them having just seen what we saw backstage, as we cut around the crowd all the way to the announce table. RILEY STEVENS: “This should certainly be interesting it seems as though something is wrong with Blake’s family so the show is in the hands of La’Sarah Audric who hasn’t exactly been very focused these past few weeks.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Hopefully Aspen gets wind of this.” RILEY STEVENS: “That would be awful…because tonight we have a great show for you all tonight. Two matches to start the night will surely show who are the future in this company as Bam Isaac Glazer, David Graves, and Logan Abbott meet in what should be a hotly contested Triple Threat Match. Then after their confrontation last week we will have the fan favorite Hank Mardukas taking on the new DeShawn Banks for a spot in the huge Seize the Moment match at ‘Moment of Glory II’. After that we’ll have the match that perhaps we should have had last week when the new ‘GIW Television Champion’ Unforgettable Jones led by La’Sarah Audric will take on Johnny Scar a man many people thought was within three seconds of becoming the champion last week. For Jones it’s a chance to prove that he is the rightful champion…” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Which he is…” RILEY STEVENS: “And for Scar it’s a chance to put himself right back in the hunt for gold with a big win. Then in what many are calling a Dream Match it will be ‘GIW Undisputed Champion’ Alioth Starre one on one with Mark Cross in a match made by Aspen to apparently wear down Alioth. Then in the main event it’ll be Absolution members Aspen Chaud and The Shadow going head to head with the ‘GIW Tag Team Champions’ American Gold; Matt Kluge and Leo James. What a night! We will be right back with the first match.” Riley grins as Oliver rolls his eyes, as the camera cuts to some happy members in the crowd before the commercial break. QUOTE As we return already standing in the ring ready to go are Bam Isaac Glazer and Logan Abbott both in separate corners as “Become The Catalyst” by All That Remains hits as the crowd goes wild as a man who has become a fan favorite for his wrestling style heads out from the back as David Graves heads confidently to the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “This man last week was the first person to qualify for the Seize the Moment match at ‘Moment of Glory II’ and while the match may not be a cake walk if you win it’s a huge opportunity. Last week he made quick work of his opponent; this week should be a bit harder.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “He’ll lose.” Graves heads into the ring, heading to the top rope as he jumps up the crowd goes wild for him as he jumps down as the referee calls for the bell. DING DING DING! Logan walks straight into the middle of the ring pointing at both men talking some trash as he screams out some words. Graves shakes his head rears back and yet again catches his opponent right off the bat with a Superkick just like last week that sends the fans into roars of approval. Now he turns to Bam who nods his head and then agrees to go for a lock up. Both men do so, fighting back and forth before Graves rolls off and locks his arms around him nailing him with a slick German Suplex! The crowd cheers as Abbott who has returned to his feet runs at him trying to take a cheap shot only for David to duck it, then grab him whipping him into the corner. Using his superior speed by the time Logan hits the turnbuckle he’s already following him up with a big Stinger Splash! The crowd cheers as he pops out to the outside going for a Springboard Bulldog that he hits with beauty. The crowd is going wild for him as he gets up, screaming for them to get up which they do. Turning around it’s now Glazer that’s coming at him, this time he blocks it with one hand then following it up with a Northern Lights Suplex that he holds on for the pin 1…2…Logan kicks him off. The fans boo as Abbott drags him, thinking he might be in trouble they seem worried however he pushes him away then runs at him and delivers a Hurricanrana! RILEY STEVENS: “He is rolling!” Calling for Logan to get up, Glazer gets up thinking he might have something but instead he turns around at the last possible moment and Bam runs right into a ‘Heat Stroke’ a Jumping Corkscrew Roundhouse Kick! But before he can make the pin, Logan gets to his feet as well while Bam rolls out of the ring. Looking out at the crowd Graves grins then spins around and as soon as Abbott is up he to receives a picture perfect ‘Heat Stroke’ that sends the fans into some more cheers as he makes the cover 1…2…3! He wins again. RILEY STEVENS: “How can you not be impressed with this guy? The fans love him. And I think his arsenal will suit him well in that Seize the Moment match.” Graves gets up letting the referee raise his hand as the fans give him a big round of applause clearly if he keeps this up he’ll find himself in title contention sooner rather then later as we go to a break. WINNER: DAVID GRAVES Heading backstage we find ourselves watching as Amanda Rowland searching through the halls perhaps still for La’Sarah Audric, with worry on her face unfortunately waiting around the corner is Aspen Chaud who looks at her raising an eyebrow. ASPEN CHAUD: “What’s wrong Amanda?” Aspen raises her eye brow; it was odd for her too actually pretend to care so clearly she must have assumed something was up. AMANDA ROWLAND: “I’m not-a suppose to say.” Aspen shakes her head patting her on the back, rubbing her back trying to calm her down. ASPEN CHAUD: “Come on we are friends…you can tell me.” Continuing to rub her back, Amanda takes some deep breaths as she begins to speak struggling to get through it. AMANDA ROWLAND: “Well…ok…Blake has some sort of family issue and he just left.” As soon as she is finished Aspen smirks and pushes her away as she walks off scheming as Amanda seems angry but isn’t really all that mean looking as we all realize that this doesn’t spell good things for GIW. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = Else where we find Alioth Starre sitting alongside his protégé Logan Abbott who has just returned to the back after a very embarrassing loss, he’s icing his head as the two stand in the main lobby. ALIOTH STARRE: “You know Logan, these fans they are fickle one day they’ll love you the next they won’t. All that matters is what you do in that ring…” As he finishes his sentence his opponent for the night Mark Cross walks up behind him, having heard this. MARK CROSS: "For a guy that says that you sure do a lot of talking and show boating. But I agree with you, all that matters is what happens in the ring. So tonight, may the best man win." Cross smiles, not usually getting involved with stuff like this backstage but this seems to be an exception. He walks off as Starre glares at him, shaking his head. QUOTE We return to the ring with the fans already booing the entrance of DeShawn Banks who stands in the ring having made an enemy out of himself last week. As his music dies out “The Mob Goes Wild” by Clutch hits as the fans go wild for a man who has become quite the fan favorite of late as Hank heads out from the back staring down in the ring and the kid whom he stopped last week from assaulting Damien Dunn. RILEY STEVENS: “Seeing as this is the second Seize the Moment Qualifier it only seems right that we have David Graves down here fresh off his win earlier tonight.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Oh joy.” As Hank makes his way to the ring we cut to the announce table where David Graves is sitting getting ready to go. DAVID GRAVES: “My match earlier was easier then I expected so I decided I’d come out here and see who will be in that match with me.” The camera cuts back to the ring and in the ring the trash talking has already begun as the referee calls for the bell. DING DING DING! Rather then showing some respect DeShawn Banks instead chooses to start the match screaming across the ring at Hank telling him that he’s an old man and needs to go. Hank gives him a second to stop but instead Banks keeps it up. Out of no where he shows him that his age is no issue showing his speed as he runs across the ring with a Flying Shoulder Block! The crowd goes wild as DeShawn caught off guard hops right up into a Spinebuster! Looking down at him he shakes his head as if to say that he’s not getting off that easily as he goes for what he calls the ‘Chest Slam’ but luckily for Banks he slips out and runs his way to the other side of the ring. The fans boo him as he shakes his head as if to say it’s fine but when he turns around his head gets nearly popped off as Hank hits the appropriately named ‘Pit Bull Attack’ a Charging Shoulder Block that sends him to the mat in pain, as Hank makes the cover having done his damage 1…2…3! RILEY STEVENS: “Well there we have it the other man in that match will be Hank Mardukas with a quick defeat.” DAVID GRAVES: “Very impressive, going to go in there and shake his hand.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “How cute…” David steps off commentary heading up the steps as he enters the ring, not trusting him Hank looks at him suspiciously as David claps for him extending a hand to him as Hank takes a second then accepts it to a loud cheer from the crowd. RILEY STEVENS: “These two men are the first two people in the Seize the Moment match but for now it seems they are still respectful of one another…When it comes to ‘Moment of Glory II’ I have a feeling that’ll change.” Graves slips out of the ring giving Hank a chance to celebrate, as the fans show some love for this show of respect as we head to break. WINNER: HANK MARDUKAS (Qualifies for Seize the Moment) Returning from break we are backstage where we find Trent Lost in the parking lot area talking with some members of staff not allowed to be near the ring as he’s there yet again Aspen Chaud comes walking up to him with a smirk on her face. ASPEN CHAUD: “Oh if it isn’t Mr. Anger Management himself what are you doing here?” Lost looks at her pretending to laugh at her joke before answering. TRENT LOST: “You haven’t seen me angry yet. And in case you didn’t realize it, I am allowed to be in the hotel public property I just can’t compete…” Aspen chuckles to herself, as she rubs her chin seeming to be pondering up an idea. ASPEN CHAUD: “You know…I am sort of in charge right now. Soooooooo….I have decided you’re your going to be reinstated next week.” Lost looks at her raising an eye brow wondering why she would be so nice. TRENT LOST: “What’s the catch?” Chaud stands there making him wait a second as she basks in her own power before grinning and answering. ASPEN CHAUD: “You remember how you broke Shadow’s arm? Well…he’s been dying for another match. So next week it’ll be The Shadow versus Trent Lost…” Aspen begins to walk as Trent takes the news looking both a little stunned but also confident as she walks back to him. ASPEN CHAUD: “Oh yeah…and it’ll be a Tables Match.” Aspen lets out a loud laugh as she walks off as this news even seems to make Trent even more serious as the show goes on. QUOTE Back to the ring we head where already standing in the ring are new ‘GIW Television Champion’ Unforgettable Jones along side La’Sarah Audric who currently holds a microphone looking out at the crowd who is still booing her. LA’SARAH AUDRIC: “We are the Black Flag City! We are the most dominant group of people this company. Together I am going to make sure that Unforgettable Jones will be just that Unforgettable. This TV title is only just the beginning for him, we are going to continue to run through each and every opponent until he becomes the top dog here in…” But as she says this she is cut off by “Passive” by A Perfect Circle hits as the crowd pops for the much smaller fan favorite Johnny Scar who runs down towards the ring sliding in. Jones glares over at him as La’Sarah looks over at him. LA’SARAH AUDRIC: “People like Johnny Scar…already have witnessed just how powerful Jones is…” La’Sarah slides out of the ring, looking out at the crowd walking up to the ones in the front row talking at them. LA’SARAH AUDRIC: “You know how powerful he is!” But as this is going on and as Jones is watching La’Sarah the referee too many people’s surprise calls for the bell. DING DING DING! Jones turns around confused but when he does he receives a huge Heart Punch at the hands of Scar that catches him off guard. While he is stunned he jumps up and catches him flush across the head with an Enzuigiri! The crowd goes wild as La’Sarah who is still looking out at them smiles thinking they are responding to her. LA’SARAH AUDRIC: “You know how dominant he is!” Back in the ring Jones is starting to falter a bit, teetering on end as Johnny grins and then jumps up onto the top rope realizing this might take some extreme measures. With Jones holding his head perhaps a little confused where he is Scar jumps off with a Flying Dropkick that sends him to the mat! LA’SARAH AUDRIC: “And nobody will ever beat him!” Just as she says this you can hear the loud thud that is Jones falling backwards into the mat. Turning around she screams for him to get up but at this point it is far too late as Johnny jumps up onto the ropes and does a Springboard Moonsault! As he flies through the air, photographs can be seen being taken as he lands on top of Jones, covering him and the fans count with the referee 1…2…3! RILEY STEVENS: “What a huge win for Johnny Scar!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “That’s called cheating where I am from.” The crowd pops as Scar slips out of the ring stepping backwards into the fans, letting them engulf him as La’Sarah slides into the ring checking on Jones as she glares up at Scar who this night got the one up on Jones and at least for now has put himself in the TV title hunt. WINNER: JOHNNY SCAR Backstage we go again and this time we find Leo James prepping for his match here in the Main Event with his Tag Team title sitting next to him, soon however in the trend of the night Aspen walks right up to him rather seductively leaning on the wall next to him. ASPEN CHAUD: “You know Leo…you seem talented…you seem like the type of person that would benefit from being with someone like me. Maybe just maybe you scratch my back tonight and I’ll scratch yours later on.” Aspen grins looking down at him, as he grabs his belt putting it over his shoulder. LEO JAMES: “You mean I could be your new partner? And get to do whatever you tell me to do? Be one of your little Knights?” James pretends to be really excited about it as Aspen nods thinking she might have lured him in. LEO JAMES: “Yeahhhh…No. I am with Kluge in American Gold. We are the GIW Tag Team Champions and tonight we are going to show the world that Absolution isn’t as perfect as you guys make yourselves seem.” Aspen glares at him coming off the wall and getting right into his face. ASPEN CHAUD: “You just picked the wrong side to be on! You are going to regret this!” Aspen blasts away from him as James shakes his head grinning not seeming to be too worried about it all. QUOTE Returning to the ring it’s time for one of the most anticipated matches here on Revolt this week as “Lift Me Up” by Moby hits causing the fans to go wild for the man that was all business as usual Mark Cross walking out from the back looking very calm as he walks down towards the ring heading up the steps and into the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Cross has been impressive since his return but no match is bigger then this one where he will go one on one with Alioth Starre.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “He’ll choke.” The crowd continues to cheer him on as he puts up one hand in the ring as “Subterraneans” by David Bowie hits as those cheers transform into boos as out from the back comes the ‘GIW Undisputed Champion’ Alioth Starre heading towards the ring looking out at the crowd, not seeming phased. RILEY STEVENS: “Many people are calling this a Dream Match but others are calling it Aspen’s way to wear down Starre on his way to their match.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Oh come on Aspen is just trying to make this show enjoyable for the fans, she does a great job.” Starre steps up into the ring, looking down at his belt then up at Cross almost showing it off as he heads to the top rope displaying his belt as he bounces off handing the belt over to the referee as he hands it out then quickly calls for the bell. DING DING DING! There is an excitement in the air for these two men. Cross whom many consider to be the Number 2 Contender at this point and Alioth the long time champion. Right away they go for a lock up, but right away Starre spins around him and rolls him up 1…Cross is able to kick him off into a roll up of his own 1…Then Starre is able to do the same again this time with a much better pin attempt 1…2…Cross kicks out and both men hop up to their feet. Cross nods his head seeming to realize that Starre is every bit as technical as he is. The two go back into another lock up this time Mark is able to show his superior strength pushing back Alioth against the ropes whipping him right off. Cross goes for a Clothesline but Starre ducks it and bounces off the ropes again. This time Mark ducks his clothesline attempt, but grabs him flipping him around into a Belly-to-Back Hammerlock Suplex that sends the fans into a loud applause for the man whom they are firmly standing behind in this one. Waiting for Starre he wastes no time when he gets up connecting with an Exploder Suplex! The fans cheer him on as he makes the cover 1…2…Kick Out! The fans let out a collective gasp thinking he had it already however as Alioth gets up, it’s clear that these two are not close to being done. RILEY STEVENS: “Thus far this match is living up to the hype.” Knowing he can’t waste any time with a competitor like Starre, Cross gets him to his feet attempting to go up with a Northern Lights Suplex but Starre is able to hook his leg and then showing off his very secretive power with a Suplex of his own. The fans boo this turn of the tides as Alioth gets to his feet, taking a second to breathe as Mark stumbles up into the corner, as Starre runs full steam at him and hits a Running European Uppercut! The fans boo as Mark stumbles forward then falls face first into the mat. Then immediately eats a Fist Drop from Alioth as the fans continue to boo, this turn for the worse. Alioth looks out at them smirking then decides to make a pin 1…Kick Out! Wasting too much time Alioth shakes his head and pulls Cross up, going straight for a Super Kick but Cross catches it. Shaking his head tripping up the other leg but as he does Starre nearly turns it into a Figure Four Leg Lock but Cross is able to avoid it and step away. Alioth gets to his feet, seeming a bit angry that it didn’t work taking a very rare wild shot that misses and right away Mark lifts him up onto his shoulders for the Go 2 Sleep! The crowd goes wild but at the last possible moment Alioth slides off and immediately slides out of the ring to take a second to regroup. RILEY STEVENS: “Looks like Alioth realizes how close that really was.” Leaning against the announce table taking a second to regroup. In the ring the fans watch as Cross walks to the other side of the ring then out of no where runs full steam. This causes Alioth to spin around as Cross goes flying through the ropes sending himself like a Missile right into Alioth sending both men into the announce table, Starre’s back hitting hard while Cross seems to hit it with his neck at a very odd angle. The fans can be seen putting there hands on there heads in a very worried manner as the referee begins to count both men out. 1…2…3…4… RILEY STEVENS: “I think these men are out cold!” The crowd cheers on Mark to get up but it seems to go with out any response as it hits 5…6…7…8…The crowd cheers when they see that Cross is up to his feet however he doesn’t seem at all knowing of the ten count…9…10! That’s it! RILEY STEVENS: “Double count out!” The fans boo the result; this causes the bell to go unheard as Starre gets up to his feet as well. Both men looking at one another before Starre throws a wild punch as Cross throws one back nearly man knowing at all that this match has been stopped. The crowd goes wild as the two begin to wildly throw punches back and forth before from the back comes some backstage staff and referee’s pulling these men apart. RILEY STEVENS: “I can’t help but think that Aspen is enjoying this, these two men took a huge fall on the outside and I think they may just be realizing what the result of this match is. This may not be the last time we see these two face off.” The crowd boos as the referee explains to both what happened as Cross considerably gets calmer realizing seeming actually disappointed that he got caught up in the moment and tried that move. As Starre turns around heading towards the back. The fans still cheer on Cross, patting him on the back as for now this scene comes to a close. DOUBLE COUNTOUT It’s nearly time for the main event and right behind the curtain we find Zachary Pagnosis standing by with a mic. ZACHARY PAGONIS: “Hello GIW fans I’m here with one half of the GIW Tag Team Champions, Matt Kluge.” As he says this the camera pans out and we see Matt Kluge standing with his belt. MATT KLUGE: “Yeah, it’s great to be here Zach…” It’s not exactly convincing but at least he pretended to be honest with that. ZACHARY PAGONIS: “So the GIW fans are wondering what are your thoughts on the main event and Absolution.” Zachary holds the microphone over to Matt. MATT KLUGE: “Wow pull no punches interviewing, well my thoughts on this match are simple. I’m not a fan. Listen we have one real functioning tag team in all of GIW, one. See when Ross Rydell went down it was a huge blow, but Leo James? I see a lot of upside in him, great talent. Then on the other side we got Aspen Chaud and The Shadow. One is a inbred lummox with an obsession for D&D, the other is a harlot and co general manager of Revolt. Ok why the hell doesn’t Blake find people qualified for this job? I mean both GMs are complete bitches who couldn’t run a wrestling program if it was loaded in EWR. I see this match as a cake walk, and that brings us right over to Absolution. See you guys have no respect at all. Respect is earned in that ring, and you are looking at two guys respected as all hell. It’s simple really Zach, Absolution is a disease. Now it’s true I’m no doctor, but I know how to treat a couple diseases. Aspen and Shadow, I suggest you go sit down, maybe drink a Sprite and eat some chicken noodle soup, take some medicine, and try not to throw up on the floor, because this disease is done before it gets the chance to spread.” Finishing up he heads on his way towards the ring as the fans cheer his words as Zach stands by with a smile as we go to our last break. QUOTE As soon as the break ends “Dawn Of A Million Souls” by Ayreon hits as the fans go into what is easily the loudest cheer of the night going wild as out from the back comes GIW Tag Team Champions, Matt Kluge and Leo James both of which with their belts heading down towards the ring. James high fives some fans while Kluge looks all business. RILEY STEVENS: “You know as you just heard it seems as though this duo known as American Gold is willing to take on this Absolution problem.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “And they’ll fail accordingly.” As they head into the ring both going to opposite corners and posing, the fans continue to cheer. But it ends abruptly as "Suffer" By Shawn McPherson, with "The Shadow Knows" Radio Drama Introduction hits as the fans boo as out they come together Aspen Chaud and The Shadow looking like a bad sit-com couple walking towards the ring looking as usual very confident. RILEY STEVENS: “Last week Absolution defeated Team GIW and Team MWA. But I have a feeling that American Gold will be on the same page unlike last week’s teams.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Yeah but they’ll still lose.” Not wasting any time Aspen takes her place on the apron as The Shadow enters the ring as the referee calls for the bell. DING DING DING! On the other side there is absolutely no fighting it is decided right away that it’ll be Matt Kluge starting the match. Walking to the center of the ring Matt Kluge looks up at The Shadow. Perhaps one of the few men in this company that would not back away from Shadow. Wasting little time its Kluge throwing the first bomb with a huge punch that lands flush but Shadow seems to shake it off. Matt screams out, “Sell you idiot” then throws another punch but again The Shadow shows no signs of caring. This time when Kluge throws a punch Shadow catches his hand and then releases it long enough for him to send him to the mat with a Clothesline. The fans boo as The Shadow pulls him up whipping him into the corner. Running at him Shadow shows that he isn’t all big with some agile movement as he crushes him with another Clothesline in the corner. Matt falls down in the corner, as the fans seem to be a little worried about this match up as Kluge gets up walking forward up into Shadow’s grasps as he tosses him into Absolution’s corner. Quickly tagging in Aspen who shows no fear knowing that Kluge’s taken a bit of a beating. Shadow lifts him up and puts him down with a Body Slam as Aspen stands over him, talking some trash as the fans try to will Kluge on. Aspen then takes the chance to make a cover 1…Kick Out! The fans cheer as Kluge gets to his feet as Aspen nails him with a Bitch Slap. Matt seemingly used to this, shrugs it off but Aspen runs at him with a flying forearm that sends him stumbling into the corner. RILEY STEVENS: “Kluge desperately needs a tag here.” Once there she runs at him going for the ‘Tush Push’ a Turnbuckle Flying Butt Shot but Kluge puts up a knee and she sends her butt right into it. The fans cheer as Aspen bounces away holding her rear as Matt runs forward spinning her then putting her down with a Belly to Belly Suplex the fans firmly back on their sides as they cheer him on as Kluge who is struggling to make it, starts to crawl towards the corner. Aspen does the same already finding herself on her way out. The fans go wild for Kluge as he jumps up and tags in James just as Aspen does the same. The crowd goes wild for Leo James as he jumps in and runs at Shadow ducking his Big Boot attempt then as Shadow turns around nails him with a Flying Knee! The crowd goes wild as the knee causes Shadow to teeter a bit as James bounces off the ropes and nails him with a Big Boot of his own that he had to extend so much on that he stumbles himself. Quickly he goes for a cover 1…2…Kick Out! This is not enough to put away The Shadow as the fans are on their feet as Leo gets up urging them to get loud as he grabs Shadow as he gets up. Perhaps just in the moment, James tries to lift Shadow up for what he calls the ‘Goldie's Lock’ a Torture Rack but it’s clear it won’t work and Shadow pushes him into the corner. The Shadow walks forward choking him in the corner as the referee counts 1…2…3….4…The referee pulls away The Shadow but as he does Aspen rakes Leo’s eyes. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “That is why Absolution will not lose, teamwork.” The fans boo as this has all but stopped James momentum as he walks forward rubbing his eyes clearly a little stunned as Shadow rams him back into the corner and tags in Aspen. Quickly she steps in running and off the ropes then nailing him with the ‘Tush Push’ she missed earlier. He bounces out of the corner still seeming to be feeling the effects of the eye rake as she goes for him with a Crossbody that lands right into a pin 1…2…James kicks out. The fans cheer as Aspen gets up arguing with the referee that this couldn’t have happened. As she does Leo starts to try to get to the corner but Aspen jumps on top of him. James gets up with Aspen basically in back pack position as she jumps off and catches him in the back of the head with a ‘Queen's Wrath II’ a Heel Kick! The fans boo as Leo goes down and Aspen goes for a cover 1…2…Kick Out! Still not enough, as Aspen gets up even angrier with the referee this time. James gets up trying to make a tag but he goes to the wrong corner as Aspen goes for him but he nails her with a Headbutt! The crowd goes wild as she stumbles back then he follows it up with a Big Boot! This sends the fans into an uproar as they again realize a hot tag is in order as both crawl towards their corners. RILEY STEVENS: “Here we go again!” Aspen jumps up and makes the tag first but James does right after as Shadow tries to catch him on the apron but Kluge ducks and nails him with a Shoulder Block through the ropes then jumps into the ring running at him and nailing him with ‘The Matt Cooke’ a Running Knee to Opponent's Knee! The fans go wild as he prepares for ‘The Tiger's Roar Gold Edition’ but at the last second Aspen breaks it up coming into the ring and nailing him with a Flying Forearm! Kluge stumbles back and right into James who still seems tired but tags himself any way. Leo jumps in grabbing Aspen and tossing her out of the ring! The fans go wild as Kluge walks forward lifting him up and some how hitting ‘The Tiger's Roar Gold Edition’ as the fans go insane and James jumps on top 1…2…3! The Tag Champions win! RILEY STEVENS: “American Gold has defeated Absolution!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “That’s not fair Kluge wasn’t the legal man.” The fans go insane as James and Kluge celebrate in the ring while The Shadow rolls out of the ring out to Aspen who is also struggling to get up holding her neck in pain as the referee hands them their belts as they head to the top rope having just shown the world how very mortal Absolution is. WINNERS: AMERICAN GOLD [Leo James & Matt Kluge] After a few moments for celebration there is a loud screaming from the outside as the camera cuts to Aspen on the outside who is sitting down but has a microphone. ASPEN CHAUD: “Cut their music! Cut their music!” The music stops and instead we hear boos from the crowd as Kluge and James walk over to the ropes looking out at her. ASPEN CHAUD: “You know Blake is gone! WHICH PUTS ME IN CHARGE! So, next week! You two are going to be in a match. So you know just who the top dogs are around here. It will be Matt Kluge versus Leo James…in a Submission Match!” The crowd seems a bit caught off guard as James and Kluge look at one another also seeming to be a little surprised by this as the show comes to a close with American Gold standing tall while Absolution is forced to regroup.
