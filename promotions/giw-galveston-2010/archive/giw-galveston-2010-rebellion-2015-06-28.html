GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS SUNDAY NIGHT REBELLION 6/28/15 FROM KAUFFMAN ARENA IN GALVESTON, TEXAS This match featured a debuting Geneva Lost and Madison Murdock. They had a good back and forth match, but Geneva was clearly the more powerful of the two. It became difficult for Madison to gain momentum after she lost control of the match early on. But to the surprise of everyone in the audience, Murdock got a second win towards the end. Unfortunately, Geneva picked up the win with a rollup and a rope assist. The referee didn't catch it, but this will not be last time these two meet up in the ring. WINNER: GENEVA LOST Landshark was next out to host his karaoke contest. Alioth Starre was the one to answer the challenge. Landshark sang and danced to "Bad Blood" by Taylor Swift. Alioth did this: While the crowd was confused and afraid, Brody Harper and his mother free fell from the rafters to land on Landshark. Alioth got the hell out of there. Botantist's Experiment made the save and the duo danced to "Bad Blood" Botanist's Experiment wanted us to note that he did not have blood, but rather chlorophyll. WINNER: ALIOTH STARRE (LOL) This match was closer than many expected, but then again, Kazan is quite great and quite powerful. Towards the middle of the match, Altair gained control after countering a back suplex attempt. He connected with the "Downward Spiral" and that was the end of the match. WINNER: ALTAIR
