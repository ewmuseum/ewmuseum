GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: REVOLT! Live from the Kauffman Arena – September 28th, 2014 The GIW Logo comes on the screen as it's time for the best damn Indy show in the south. "Bartholomew" by Silent Comedy plays and a video package for Revolt airs. Showing dozens of new shots the production team pick up from last week's Revolt. Somewhat of a recap of sorts. From Michael Lynch hitting Artemis Kaiser with his new finisher, to The Gentle Sir tipping his fedora after his meeting with Auva Skye. A quick clip of M. Tech Michaud training is displayed. Astro going for his HDMI neck breaker but being countered by CDE's Uppercut From Hell is shown. A clip that has made rounds all over social media is also apart of this package, Jack Waters jumping off the top of the steel cage and hitting Tony Miranda with a moonsault. The lasting image of The Harlequin holding the Undisputed Championship above her head after the confrontation between her and Lynch and Erns is shown before the Revolt banner comes on the screen. Pyro goes off in the Kaufmann Arena and the fans go insane for this much anticipated Revolt. The camera swims through the sea of signs in the crowd, this week's signs include "GIW conquers UWL", "Michaud = Overrated", and "Marry ME Mel!".
