GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: WEDNESDAY NIGHT REVOLT LIVE! At the San Luis Hotel – September 5, 2012 on Showtime BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! The cameras fade in on the parking lot outside the GIW arena where Blake Beckett is walking, oh so similar to the way he was when he was brutally ran over by Aspen Chaud and David Sinclair a few months back. The differences between the two scenes are nearly nonexistent. Sure enough, from the edge of the camera’s view, we see a white sedan speed through the back of the parking lot, and towards Beckett. The squeal of the tires prompt him to turn around quickly, and just as the car was barreling close to him, he ducked and military rolled out of the way. The car stopped. The door opened, and out stepped Koji Nakamura in a white button down shirt, short black pants, and a fanny pack with a Kodak camera sticking out. KOJI NAKAMORA: “Oh sari missure Beckett. I didn’t meansh to phrun yew ova.” Koji jumped back into the car as Beckett stood up, brushing off his suit. BLAKE BECKETT: “The one time I hire an Asian…” The car peels out and awkwardly left the scene, Beckett continued walking. He scoffed at Jake Kennedy and Liviana Valentine making out in the corner half naked, shaking his head. From the side of the view, Chris Jericho walked up and extended his hand to Blake. CHRIS JERICHO: “WAZZZZZZZZZZUP BLAKEY? ROCK ON MAN!” Chris was suddenly upended by a hard microphone shot by Mark Cross, who then stood over his fallen enemy sipping a warm glass of Earl Gray. Blake continued on his way, he didn’t want to stop and talk to Mark because he was so fucking boring. He watched as Trent Lost revved up his Harley and peeled away, Katya Rose desperately chasing him while trying to keep her precious vodka in the bottle. As he continued through the parking lot, Violet Vanity was whoring on the corner. VIOLET VANITY: “Oh hey there big boy, wanna good fuck? I ain’t cheap but a I ain’t expensive either.” He scoffed at her, and as he walked off we saw Vega in the background approach her. VEGA: “Nice haircut.” Suddenly the camera stopped and froze, Beckett was looking to his right. Slowly Wake Walker floated across the screen, releasing doves as he came. BLAKE BECKETT: “The man is a God.” Blake kept walking, then he encountered an ethnically diverse group of Baltimore’s, two of them white, one of them half, and the other full black. WHITE BALT 1: “Yeah being white has its benefits.” WHITE BALT 2: “It is so hard to be motivated while white though.” BLACK BALT: “I just have too many girlfriends to keep this up.” MIXED BALT: “I can do a plancha!” He shook his head and walked further, this time into a much more… eccentric meeting. ROBERT ACRES: “I LOVE these new tights doll.” ALIOTH STARRE: “They are quite exquisite.” ROBERT ACRES: “I’m so glad I met you.” ALIOTH STARRE: “Your eyes sparkle like the summer stars.” ROBERT ACRES: “Oh you.” ALIOTH STARRE: “It reminds me of Kluge’s. Mm… what a hunk.” Suddenly a ruckus breaks the intensely homosexual moment. The voice of an angry black man is heard off screen. It is Unforgettable Jones. UNFORGETTABLE JONES: “Get back hurr Chicken! I want some food in dis hizzy.” The former TV title holder, Chicken, scampered by, terrified. Blake kept on. Suddenly we cut away to a dark room. Jacoby Jackson was there - his eyes were wide and bloodshot. JACOBY JACKSON: “9/11/11.” We quickly cut away to Blake, who was breaking up a fight between Jamie Vaccaro and Joshua Acquin for the TV title. JAME VACCARO: “No. I didn’t lose it, that was an awful storyline and its MINE!” JOSHUA ACQUIN: “I AM ABOVE THE LAW.” JAME VACCARO: “HOW?” Joshua pulled him real close, and got real serious. JOSHUA ACQUIN: “I’m in the Air Force.” Suddenly some stupid bitch on RoughKut called out. STUPID BITCH: “Hey, don’t mention the military! I know people there!” Blake left the two people alone. Why they would fight over that worthless belt, he would never know, but then he ran into Pandora and La’Sarah Audric comparing their new born babies. PANDORA: “Oh he looks so good in white La’Sarah.” LA’SARAH AUDRIC: “Why? Cuz he’s black?” A catfight ensues. Unforgettable is rooting for Audric from behind, both babies are dead more then likely. Blake continues to walk, frazzled. He runs into CDE, Chris Armstrong, Jack Prowler, and Travis Tate. TRAVIS TATE: “Hey, where is the On Hallowed Ground arena Blake?” CDE: “We’ve been looking forever.” Blake rolled his eyes. BLAKE BECKETT: “That show was two years ago.” CHRIS ARMSTRONG: “FUCKING PROWLER.” Jack Prowler shrugged. JACK PROWLER: “At least Wake found it.” Suddenly Walker floated through them, knocking them over like bowling pins. A screaming could be heard from far away. Blake took off to run, and what he saw was a full WEW show in place. Chance Rugani was fighting Alexandra Rossi, and he was probably winning because he is the greatest wrestler alive and speaks from the heart for sure. SAYGE JEMSEN: “YES! YES! OH YES! WEW IS SO MUCH BETTER THAN GIW IT MAKES ME SICK HOW MUCH THEY FUCKING SUCK UGH.” Blake tapped her on the shoulder. BLAKE BECKETT: “Hey, you can’t do that here. This is our parking lot.” She wasn’t pleased. SAYGE JEMSEN: “I’m a FUCKING SOCIALITE RAPPER-WRITER-WRESTLER-PROMOTER-MODEL. Who the FUCK are you? I’LL KILL YOU. I GOT THAT GUCCI AND THAT FENDI AND I LOVVVEEE ALL MY PRADA.” Blake smiled. BLAKE BECKETT: “Oh you sing that?” She nodded. BLAKE BECKETT: “Sign this?” She pulled a pen out of her bra. SAYGE JEMSEN: “Wait. No. RAHSHHHFHGWV GET OUT OF MY FACE AND GET MY FED OUT OF YOUR MOUTH RGREHHEH UGH I HATE YOUFEJGREEBR I JUST LOCKED MY TWITTER.” Blake slowly stepped away, in the background we see Nathan Bailey IV in a three way with Robert and Alioth - turns out he cheats on Alexandra with men too. We see Cassidy Hightower pleading with Roughkut. CASSIDY HIGHTOWER: “Black list them. OMG.” KUT: “…” CASSIDY HIGHTOWER: “But they are so mean!!!” KUT: “…” CASSIDY HIGHTOWER: “They make me SO SAD.” KUT: “…” CASSIDY HIGHTOWER: “Ugh. Did Aspen fuck you too?” The camera zooms out, we see Aspen on her knees in front of Kut. You imagine the rest. CASSIDY HIGHTOWER: “OMG. UGH. TWITTER TIME.” Blake continued walking, we saw Eliza Gray peak out of a trashcan, snapping secret pictures of the proceedings. Beckett turned around, waved her away like he had done so many times before, then he turned back around. BLAKE BECKETT: “Fuck this place.” We faded into the traditional package. Followed by: BOOM BOOM BOOM. And the matches were ready to begin. Attack Attack is one of the best new guys in the company, but I’m confident in my skills if I do what I need to do. Maybe we can take out the trash tonight.” Kluge nodded. Lots of nodding here. MATT KLUGE: “And we’ll work on your insults tomorrow.” They all chuckled together as we faded away.
