GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: REVOLT Live from the Kauffman Arena – May 9th, 2014 QUOTE OPEN BATTLE ROYAL In the Open Battle Royal this evening we had two debuting stars in the match, making it a singles contest. The two were Giovanni Merced and The Harlequin, each pretty unique characters. The match was a very solid match up here tonight, before the opening of the show. In the match the much bigger Giovanni started the match wearing Harlequin down, however after a good start from Merced it was Harlequin that started to come back with some high flying arsenal stealing the show and really getting the fans into it. The fans themselves were split in this one, deciding whom they were going to root for. But the ending of the match left much to be desired as the match was called off at the twelve minute mark declaring it a time limit draw. Both wrestlers clearly made a good debut but were not able to finish one another before the time ran out. None the less they both gave very good showings and one can expect both to get big matches here on Revolt next week. DRAW BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! The pyro blasts off in the recently finished Kauffman Arena, the place that will be home to Revolt on ION Television. The crowd goes wild as the camera pans around the arena and we see a wide variety of signs and props that show just how excited everyone is to be here. Their screams turn into cheers as “In The City” hits and Chris Callum steps onto the stage. RILEY STEVENS: “Here he is – the man who will challenge the new Deep South Champion, Michael Lynch, for the belt at Category 5!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I’m sure he is excited, but he should be scared! Lynch is a dangerous man!” RILEY STEVENS: “Chris has been working hard to get back to the main stage, and now he can finally say he made it.” Chris began to make his way down the ramp, slapping hands with some of the fans and being cheerful. Lynch pulls him up by his hair and trunks and tosses him into the barricade. Callum is screaming in pain as Lynch unfastens the belt from his waist and holds it up into the air. He looks at the belt like it is his child, something extremely personal to him, and as soon as Callum stands up, Lynch slammed the belt across his head.
