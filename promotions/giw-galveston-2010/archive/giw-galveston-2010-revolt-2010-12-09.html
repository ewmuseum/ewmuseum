GIW — Card

Revolt, 9 December 2010

9 December 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: Thursday Night Revolt! LIVE DECEMBER 9th, FROM UTMB CAMPUS PLAZA IN GALVESTON, TEXAS BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! We come into the arena with the fans in the UTMB Campus Plaza pumped to see some GIW action here tonight. But their excitement is doubled as right away “Bullseye" by Aly and AJ hits and the fans go nuts. Out from the back she comes wearing her new title over her shoulder as she walks out with a black dress on and heels, with a huge smile on her face. She begins to walk down to the ring high fiving a few fans before walking up the steps into the ring, being handed a microphone as the fans continue to cheer with her soaking it in. ASPEN CHAUD: “Can you believe this? FINALLY! The belt is mine. I’ve been waiting to come out with this thing since the very first night when I won it, without deserving it. I wouldn’t be here without any of you, so for that I thank you. Surprisingly I thank Blake Beckett for stripping me that first night; this night feels a million times better. And lastly I want to say this to Wake Walker. I know you said you were retiring but honestly, I know that none of us want that. You were hurt and honestly, I don’t know if you are here, maybe you’re nursing your ankle. But Wake we want you back, because I know that everyone here wants to see us fight for real this time. Regardless Wake, we want you to come back, nobody wants you gone, am I right?” Aspen holds out the microphone to the crowd who cheer Wake back however “Fatlip” by Sum 41 hits and their cheers turn to boos as out from the back strolls Eric Sparrow he himself holding his ‘GIW Evolve Championship’ with a mic in hand as Aspen places her hand on her waist as he walks up the steps and then into the ring, with a big smirk on his face. ERIC SPARROW: “Now, now, now just because you talk for these people does not mean you talk for me. And I for one am happy that Wake’s gone because that’s just one less person to delay my rise to the top. I find nothing funnier then you standing in here with your Prada dress on, with MY BELT. Honestly, this belt on my shoulder has more prestige then yours does. At the end of the day we all know that you couldn’t even put up a fight with me! So go ahead fans cheer for her but at the end of the day, she’ll be gone just like Wake and it’ll be me standing alone at the top.” Aspen looks at the fans shaking her head as Sparrow lowers the microphone however it’s “Turn Up The Trouble” by Airbourne that echoes through the speakers as the fans look almost in disbelief as out from the back this time comes Chris Armstrong, who hasn’t been on the show in a long while. The fans seem shocked as do both Sparrow and Chaud as he walks down towards the ramp, with a big smirk on his face seeming proud of his appearance as he now heads into the ring, stepping up between the two as the three look back and forth at one another. CHRIS ARMSTRONG: “Not expecting me huh? You know I’ve been out for awhile now. And I watched as my belt was taken away without me being able to put up a fight. But Eric I’m not out here to address you, I’m here to talk about this new champion we have. I think you all know the history I have with her. And though you beat me once, it was simply a fluke. I was the face of Evolve and I’m going to be the face of Revolt. That starts by taking you out!” Armstrong tosses the microphone down and attacks the vulnerable Aspen with Sparrow standing by watching. The fans boo as he slams her down to the mat when suddenly Jack Prowler runs down to the ring. Sparrow quickly bails as Prowler pulls Chris off and tosses him out of the ring. Armstrong looks up at Prowler shocked as he helps up Aspen who seems equally as shocked as it seems Jack has turned over a new leaf, as he turns his attention to Sparrow who has a small grin on his face as we take a break. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = We return to the ring to the presence of two men in the ring, one seemingly a small local talent the other a physically built man with a big smirk across his face this man is Gavin Masterson. The fans are dead as they meet up in the center of the ring as eventually bell is rung with even Riley and Oliver seeming to sit this one out. DING DING DING! BOOM! Gavin Masterson wastes absolutely no time crushing his opponent with a clothesline. He picks him up off his feet then nails him with a follow up, ‘Market Crash’ a Barry White Driver! The fans sit in amazement with that move as he smirks out at them, simply putting a foot on top of the local talent 1…2…3! RILEY STEVENS: “Talk about making quick work out of him. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I like this guy, a lot.” Gavin slides out of the ring wasting no time in walking over to the nearest camera which he yells into, screaming “Next stop GOLD! The fans seem to boo him shocked by his dominance but already sensing his extreme arrogance as we take a break after this huge statement. WINNER: GAVIN MASTERSON = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = Back to the back we go, where Amanda Rowland is pacing back and forth before Blake Beckett walks around the corner, she immediately steps in his path. AMANDA ROWLAND: “So what’s the deal, you ever get anyone? You ever decide on anything. What you doing to get this show a boost in ratings, after all your job depends on it. Amanda grins as Blake smiles realizing how dependant she is on him right now, as he holds up a finger and continues to walk by her. BLAKE BECKETT: “Let’s just say, you won’t be let down. Blake smirks back at her walking off as she shakes her head wanting to know what’s going down as the segment ends. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = We flash back into the ring where the fans are booing Ryan Blake who is in the ring already, with a big smirk on his face. "Re-Education" by Rise Against hits and the fans begin to cheer as Austin Acquin comes out from the back with his brother Joshua Acquin right behind him. RILEY STEVENS: “As we all know if Ryan Blake defeats Joshua’s brother, he’ll get a title shot against Joshua Acquin, so big pressure here tonight.” The fans cheer him on as he makes his slow descent towards the ring, we all know what’s at stake here tonight a title shot for Ryan Blake. He steps in the ring looking over at Blake as his brother takes his spot on the outside with the referee calling for the bell. DING DING DING! These two men begin to circle each other, going round and round before hooking up in a lock up. It’s Austin who gets the early advantage until Ryan cheaply knees him in the lower regions then tosses him to the ground. The fans boo as Blake smirks walking across the ring over to Joshua pointing at the belt on his shoulder and talking some trash. The fans are booing until they realize that Austin is getting up behind him slowly. Slowly Ryan backs up, still talking when he walks right into the ‘Crusher’ a Full Nelson Facebuster! The fans go nuts as the referee jumps down with Austin flipping him over 1…2…3! RILEY STEVENS: “And there goes his title shot!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Never really liked him anyways.” The fans go nuts for Austin who holds up his arms, however unnoticed by him Jake Kennedy has come down the ramp crushing Joshua from behind with a chair! The fans boo as Jake lands another big shot to the mid-section! RILEY STEVENS: “ANOTHER, bitter Jake Kennedy attack it’s getting out of hand.” Austin finally realizes what has happened as he slides out of the ring chasing after, Jake who gets out of dodge while Austin goes to check on his brother as the fans are all holding their breaths, with Joshua seemingly hurt again as we take a much needed break. WINNER: AUSTIN ACQUIN = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = Back from break we are backstage where Jake Kennedy has just arrived backstage with a big smirk on his face, having dropped the chair as he has seemingly taken out Acquin again. His smirk is wiped off as he’s met by an already annoyed Amanda Rowland. AMANDA ROWLAND: “Just what do you think you’re DOING? You think what you did was funny? Better yet do you think you’ve proved anything with these attacks? All you’ve proved is that you are a sore loser. And honestly, I’ve gotten word you put Josh on ANOTHER hiatus. Which is bad for business, just like you. You’ve been here awhile but, your time here is done, you’re fired. Get out of here, right now.” Amanda points to the exit with Jake Kennedy seemingly ready to do something to her; however he’s stopped by Austin Acquin stepping in his path. Realizing defeat, Jake turns and leaves as we all watch the last appearance by Jake Kennedy here in GIW. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = Here we head back to the ring for the next contest which is a tag team match where continuing the trend of the night Los Firemen are already in the ring this time to a happy applause from the crowd in the arena as Jos C stands on the outside. “Taste The Blood” by Shawn McPherson hits and the fans look to the stage where soon Tony Miranda enters followed not far behind by The Shadow. The fans aren’t quite sure how to react as Tony leads the beast to the ring, both members hopping up into the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Big change for Tony Miranda a great singles competitor heading into the tag division with his faithful partner The Shadow.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Perfect Combination as a name sets a pretty high expectation level.” They stand across from the ring from one another with Jose deciding to start the match out while Tony starts it out for the other team; the fans wait to see how the teams do as they fight to cement themselves as contenders. DING DING DING! The fans cheer as Jose and Tony begin to circle each other ready to fight, Jose offers his hand for a shake however to the surprise of the fans Tony slaps it away instead, grabbing him and heaving him off the ropes. On the rebound Jose ducks a clothesline but as he runs off the ropes he runs right back into a big time big boot. The fans now have turned on Tony booing him as he looks out at them with a complete lack of care as he lifts up the much smaller Jose. Grabbing him he pulls him towards his corner, and tosses him into it. With him there he kicks him in the gut and then tags in the beast of a man The Shadow. Shadow steps into the ring with that mean looking stare as Jose walks forward into his big hand around his neck. The fans watch in fear as The Shadow lifts him up and while doing so tags in Tony. Boom! He comes down with a big Chokeslam. From the top rope Tony jumps off hitting an amazing Top Rope Front Flip Leg Drop! The fans are stunned at this early dominance as Tony goes for the cover 1…2…Kick Out! RILEY STEVENS: “Truth be told I thought that was the end of it, however it seems that these guys ‘The Perfect Combination’ mean business.” The fans watch as Tony gets to his feet smirking as he tags in, The Shadow. Almost sensing that his friends about to be crushed Jos B jumps the ropes and runs to save him but runs right into a ‘Cross Bomber’ a Double Axe Bomber Clotheline! The fans watch as Jos B seems to go unconscious from that blow with Jos C pulling him out of the ring leaving the now standing up Jose to walk right into a ‘Vicious Beatdown’ as Tony locks in the Pallo Special, and as Jose taps out, The Shadow hits him with a vicious Yakuza Kick! RILEY STEVENS: “That’s what we call domination.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Finally we see the real side of Tony Miranda tonight and I love it.” The fans give them all their boos as they have just won their first match in GIW as a tag team in convincing fashion putting EVERYONE on notice. As they stand over their fallen enemies as we head to the backstage area yet again. WINNERS: THE PERFECT COMBINATION = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = This time we head to the backstage area we are met with the face of Aspen Chaud who is standing by with Jack Prowler talking with him, after he saved her early in the night. However it appears we are just too late to hear anything as right then Prowler heads off. Right as Aspen goes to leave however it’s Eric Sparrow putting a hand over her cornering her against the wall. ERIC SPARROW: “You think that guy is going to do anything to help you? You should count your lucky stars that it was Armstrong and not me that attacked you; otherwise you wouldn’t be standing right now.” Aspen slowly moves out of the way with little resistance from Sparrow who just smirks as she stands there responding to him not seeming all that scared, more so just creeped out. ASPEN CHAUD: “You’re just jealous it was him I was talking to and not you. I sort of wish it was you that tried something because I would have snapped your ankle. But you know the sad part is? Now not only can you not only look at me and not touch, you can’t touch this either.” Aspen smirks and grabs her title tossing it over her shoulder as she begins to walk away however it’s Sparrow that doesn’t stop. ERIC SPARROW: “Keep telling yourself that. I want you to make sure to watch what happens to pretty boy tonight, because the same things coming for you.” Sparrow finishes up now heading towards the ring for his match as we take a short break with tension high tonight. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = It appears while all that was happening backstage Jack Prowler has already made his entrance for his match tonight, standing inside of the ring with the fans behind him for the first time. “Fatlip” by Sum 41 hits and ends any little bit of cheers that were left, as the most hated man in all of GIW heads out from the back walking with the usual amount of swagger. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Here comes the best fighter in ALL of GIW! My man Eric Sparrow.” RILEY STEVENS: “Honestly I have a new found respect for Jack Prowler tonight, saving a vulnerable Aspen Chaud helped make up for all his wrongs in my book.” The fans are continuing boo one fan even going as far as to throw a plastic bottle at Sparrow which he slaps away, he jumps over the ropes and then without any hesitation crushes Prowler with the Evolve title square across the face! RILEY STEVENS: “WHAT WAS THAT!” The fans boo as Sparrow glares down at his fallen opponent as he turns to the referee and commands him to start the match since they are both in the ring, he tries to say no however Sparrow gets in his face and fearing for his own safety he calls for the bell. DING DING DING! The fans watch as Prowler attempts to get to his feet though it’s a sad attempt at that. He is slow and unable to do so; however Sparrow instead slowly kicks him back down while he’s trying to get back up. When he tries to do it again he receives the exact same treatment. Finally the last time Jack pushes Sparrow away and gets to his feet rather quickly yet dazed but he walks right into the awaiting arms of Sparrow who grabs him by the head and then locks him into the ‘Purple Haze’ a Guillotine Choke. Prowler’s legs quickly give out as Sparrow brings the hold to the mat as the fans boo him. Jack finally taps out as the referee calls for the bell, but Eric does not stop. RILEY STEVENS: “This guy is sick.” He continues to tug on the hold as the fans go wild as Aspen explodes from the back but it’s a little to late as Jack has now lost consciousness. Aspen slides in and calls for a fight which Sparrow stops to think about. Finally he rolls out of the ring with a big smirk on his face as the fans boo him as he walks to the back as Aspen checks on Prowler, who seems out, perhaps like his career. WINNER: ERIC SPARROW = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = Back in the back we come with Mark Cross suiting up for his match pulling on a pair of knee pads as the fans cheer him on he’s quickly become a fan favorite around here. It’s the presence of one Blake Beckett that turns those cheers into boos as he stands behind him. BLAKE BECKETT: “Good match at ‘Requiem’, you made a mockery out of me. You see I’ve been given some powers around here and let’s just say it’s better to be on my good side then to be on my bad side.” Blake smirks seeming to enjoy the fact that he’s gotten his power back around here as Mark pulls up his other knee pad then looks at him to respond. MARK CROSS: “Honestly I got sucked into that whole thing with Alexii and Amanda at ‘Requiem’ I’m not looking to do that again. I get your sent on some sort of mission for ratings and I’m sorry for nearly getting you fired, but I’ve got an important match my first main event here. Think I should make it a good one…” Mark attempts to walk by him but he’s stopped by Blake taking a small scoot to his side in front of him. BLAKE BECKETT: “That’s not what I was looking for. You see I thought maybe Falls Count Anywhere would be enough for people to be excited. But turns out it wasn’t so…to up the ante a bit…the loser gets fired, and since you’re a heavy favorite and thought it was great beating me in forty seconds at ‘Requiem’, if you don’t win in forty seconds you lose and your fired as well. Now good luck.” Mark stops thinking about how much he’s gotten into already without seeming to have done anything to get this treatment as Blake now turns to walk away, happy to be the one walking away with confidence. BLAKE BECKETT: “Oh and one more thing, watch your back tonight. Never know who can be waiting behind these walls.” Blake smirks and walks off leaving Mark to soak in what just happened, as he is forced to shrug it off as he heads towards the ring for the main event. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = Finally it’s time for the main event which just took a big leap in meaning with the addition of a loser is fired ruling with it. The fans watch as Assassin watched the entire little debacle backstage with them as he claps his hands realizing for him this will be easy. “Lit Me Up” by Moby hits and the fans go nuts seemingly having a new hero in form of Mark Cross as he walks out from the back. RILEY STEVENS: “This is disgusting, this guy Mark Cross has done nothing wrong yet just like old times its Mark Cross being picked on by Blake Beckett who some how got his power back around here.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “It’s called getting the best out of your wrestlers, Blake always did that well.” Cross seems to not let this whole thing get in his head walking with confidence to the ring, stepping up into it as he watches his opponent Assassin here tonight. The fans are chanting his name as the referee calls for the bell. DING DING DING! Right away Assassin bails from the ring as Mark’s worst fears are realized as his opponent knows about the stipulation and knows he only has to last forty seconds. Cross quickly follows him out of the ring as Assassin forces some staff out of the way and grabs a steel chair. He takes a lofty swing at Cross who dodges it, forcing Assassin to slam it against the ground ringing through his hands. Cross grabs him and tosses him towards the ring. 15 Seconds In! The fans boo as Assassin immediately begins to crawl across the ring hoping to get out of their, but he’s grabbed and pulled up at the last second by Mark who is scrambling here. Assassin turns and attempts a big haymaker, but Cross dodges it and rolls him up hoping to take this one, 1…2…Kick Out! 25 Seconds In! Assassin kicks out as Cross with no time to ask for the time quickly lifts him up and turns him around kicking him in the gut, setting him up for the ‘Ketteiteki Desaki’ a Tiger Driver '91! Assassin struggles but eventually he nails it and goes for the cover, 1…2…3! Right at 38 Seconds! RILEY STEVENS: “YES HE DID IT!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Ugh.” The fans explode into cheers as Cross can’t help but to give some strong emotions, as he goes to the top rope holding up his arms. Suddenly the lights flicker out in the arena as it goes dead silent. The lights come back on and in the center of the ring is Chris Jericho with the ‘Walls of Jericho’ locked in tight on an unexpecting Mark Cross. The fans are going nuts with many just stunned that THE Y2J is here in GIW! Some fans still in favor of Cross have begun to boo as Jericho continues to wretch at it. RILEY STEVENS: “Is this Blake’s surprise, its CHRIS JERICHO here in GIW!” The fans are now actually booing him as he continues to pull back on the Walls as the show slowly fades with CHRIS JERICHO IN Galveston Island Wrestling! WINNER: MARK CROSS
