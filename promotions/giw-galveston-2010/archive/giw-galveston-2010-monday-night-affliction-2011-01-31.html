GIW — Card

Monday Night Affliction, 31 January 2011

31 January 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Galveston Island Wrestling Presents… MONDAY NIGHT AFFLICTION! BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! Outside of the Jesuit High School Gym in New Orleans Louisiana, fireworks that are blue and silver are pouring into the sky. The crowd of 600 people is jam packed into the gym, waiting patiently for their favorite Affliction stars to come out from backstage. The camera heads over to the announcing booth where Emmanuel Russo and Colin McKendry are joined by the GIW Chairman, Blake Beckett, on commentary. EMMANUEL RUSSO: “Ladies and gentlemen, welcome to tonight’s edition of Monday Night Affliction! We are two weeks away from GIW Exile, and you can feel the anticipation of every single person here floating in the air. We are in for a treat tonight folks as the GIW Chairman himself, Blake Beckett, is joining us on commentary for the entire night! It is going to be a good show and everyone wants to watch it… but what brings you here tonight with us Mr. Beckett?” BLAKE BECKETT: “Well Emmanuel, I am here to kind of keep an eye on things. I don’t want anyone to be attacked tonight… so maybe my presence will… deter the undesirables.” COLIN MCKENDRY: “I don’t think it is working Mr. Beckett, Emmanuel Russo is still here tonight.” BLAKE BECKETT: “Well, I’m not perfect.” EMMAUNEL RUSSO: “Thanks guys, I appreciate that. Tonight is going to be a great night, and lets go ahead and start the University Credit Union Affliction Preview! The first match of the night will be Assassin, the member of The AKA, taking on Scott Reave in singles match. Things haven’t been going well for Reave as of late, he will try to turn things around with a win tonight.” COLIN MCKENDRY: “Reave didn’t look right last week, but I think he will have no trouble dealing with Assassin tonight in this match. We might need to get some EMT’s to stand at ringside.” EMMANUEL RUSSO: “Let’s also remember that Scott Reave’s new trainee, Jake Avery, will be at ringside tonight. He might have an impact on the match, he is the X Factor.” COLIN MCKENDRY: “Yeah, he really is. All it takes is one little trip or an eye poke to swing the match in Reave’s favor; and a competitor like Scott Reave will capitalize on every opportunity.” EMMANUEL RUSSO: “And after that match, Jake Avery gets a chance to be in the spotlight. He takes on the electrifying Nate Amazing in a first blood match that he asked for last week. There WILL be EMT’s at ringside for this match to carry off the loser.” COLIN MCKENDRY: “Jake Avery is making his debut in this match… we don’t know anything about this guy. However, if Scott Reave liked him enough to mentor him… he can’t be a slouch.” EMMANUEL RUSSO: “At the same time, Nate Amazing has proven himself to be a solid competitor. Nate Amazing has the talent, but he just can’t seem to catch a break to thrust him up the card… maybe this is it?” COLIN MCKENDRY: “I just can’t see Amazing going anywhere in GIW.” EMMANUEL RUSSO: “Not everyone has it in them. The next match features Eric Sparrow and Mike Bryant in a match for the New Orleans X-Treme Championship. Eric Sparrow has tried so hard to make the main event scene, but he just can’t reach it. Mike Bryant has gold around his waist, and he is a target. Will Sparrow get a bulls-eye?” COLIN MCKENDRY: “This is the year of the Sparrow! He can’t be stopped. Mike Bryant will leave New Orleans tonight with his waist cold; Eric Sparrow will be shining bright!” EMMANUEL RUSSO: “And then we have the main event… Madison Seton and Mark Cross take on High Voltage. All four of these competitors can expect to be high up on the PPV card… but which team will come out on top tonight?” COLIN MCKENDRY: “I can’t choose.” EMMANUEL RUSSO: “It really is a close match… I don’t blame you.” COLIN MCKENDRY: “If it was Seton or Cross in a singles match against Volt or Lightning, I would take Cross or Seton… but as a team… High Voltage is very experience. It will be a great match.” EMMANUEL RUSSO: “And that will conclude the UCU Affliction Preview… let’s send it backstage where Malissa Wiggins has a special guest for us tonight.” The cameras cut backstage where Malissa Wiggins is standing with Megathreat in the interview lounge. Megathreat is dressed in a red plaid shirt with a cowboy hat and jeans with boots on. He has a blank expression on his face; almost like he doesn’t really want to be here right now. Malissa Wiggins is smiling bright in her new, Royal Blue, Monday Night Affliction shirt and worn out jeans. She accepts the handoff. MALISSA WIGGINS: “Thanks guys! I am joined tonight by the General Manager of Affliction… Megathreat! This is your first full week back as general manager… is there anything you have to say?” Megathreat turns toward Wiggins very slowly and takes the microphone away from her. He uses his hand to tell her to go away. She stands still for a moment, obviously offended, but then she realizes it is in her best interest to leave. Megathreat turns to the camera and stares deep into it and begins to speak. MEGATHREAT: “I am a broken man, ever since my attack two weeks ago. I didn’t know who did it, they got me from behind… but now I do. Tony Miranda, Shadow… La’Sarah Audric… you have some guts to come on my show and try to take me out. It, in a way, makes me respect you, but the way you did it was cowardly. Garrett Holloway said it last week, and I will say it again: There will be actions taken to get you back. Unlike Garrett Holloway, I have no issue in using force to get even, and don’t think I won’t. It may not be this Thursday… it may not be next Thursday… it may to be a Thursday until next year dammit… but I will get you back.” Megathreat drops the microphone and we cut back to the announce booth. Emmanuel Russo and Blake Beckett have shocked looks on their faces but Colin McKendry seems to like the threat of an impending brand war. Russo turns to Beckett. EMMANUEL RUSSO: “What do you think of what just transpired? Megathreat has vowed to get redemption on the Revolt attackers…” BLAKE BECKETT: “I’ve known Megathreat for a long time Emmanuel. I have been in this business for two years, and it was Megathreat who gave me a job in UWA as the Warfare General Manger. I learned everything I know from him… and I think it is safe to say that I know him better than anyone else in this company. As much as I like La’Sarah Audric, I kind of fear for her. Megathreat is the type of person that holds grudges for a long time. He doesn’t forgive and forget. He is very tricky… he doesn’t make mistakes; he is a very methodical person. When this attack happens… I don’t know if even I can stop it.” COLIN MCKENDRY: “Why would you try to stop it? It is natural selection Blake! The stronger predators pick off the weaker ones to get stronger! Affliction will overtake Revolt soon anyway… especially with Megathreat back at the helm, why would you want to stop it?” BLAKE BECKETT: “As much as I agree with you Colin, I have to do what is best for my company and the folks that work here. Right now, we need two shows and right now, we need all of our wrestlers. I can’t have a bunch of vigilantes running around and picking off the weak ones. In the ecosystem that is GIW… everyone is important for it to be stable. That’s why I am here tonight, to promote stability.” EMMANUEL RUSSO: “And, speaking of stability… what are you going to do with Alexii Ravynova now that Megathreat is back on the job?” BLAKE BECKETT: “I think she has out done herself the two weeks she has been here, so she might actually stay on the job.” The fans are booing now in the background. BLAKE BECKETT: “The fans may not like her, but she is the total package. She can wrestle, she can manage, she can book, she can file. She is an elite administrator.” EMMANUEL RUSSO: “I agree… she may not be the nicest person in the world… but she gets the job done as well as anyone I have ever seen. Enough yapping though… it is time to get our first match of the evening started. Scott Reave will take on Assassin of AKA next up here on Monday Night Affliction!” The TV begins to show a hype video for the super show, Exile, which takes place February 13th, 2011. The main event will be Affliction’s High Voltage vs. Revolt’s The Perfect Combination for the GIW Tag Team Championships! = = = = = = = = = = = After the hype video is finished showing, the camera shows the GIW Arena from above. The crowd is buzzing, but not loud. Suddenly, “Fucking in the Bushes” by Oasis begins to play and the boos echo throughout the arena. Scott Reave and Jake Avery walk out and soak it all in for a moment before walking slowly down the ring… torturing the fans by dragging their entrance on forever. JACOB POTTER: “Now entering the ring… accompanied by Jake Avery… weighing in at 225 pounds… from The Bowery… Scott Reave!” By the time the announcer is done introducing him, he enters the ring and climbs onto one of the turnbuckles and mimics a title around his waist. JACOB POTTER: “Now entering the ring… accompanied by Slade Craven… weighing in at 255 pounds… from Dallas, Texas… The Assassin!” He enters the ring, Craven stays on the outside. Jake Avery slides out of the ring. The referee beings to instruct Assassin and Reave on how the match will occur, but then Jake Avery tackles Craven outside the ring! Totally blindsided by the attack, Craven just tries to cover up… but Avery has landed a few good blows. Assassin rushes to the ring ropes to try to help his partner… but Reave hooks him for a roll up! DING! DING! DING! The match begins, and the ref goes for the count…1…2… KICKOUT! Reave retreats back to the corner. The ref gets the attention of both Avery and Craven and throws them both out! EMMANUEL RUSSO: “The match has just begun, and it already is chaotic! The ref is doing all he can to restore order now… but things aren’t looking good for The AKA!” A swarm of guards run out from behind the curtain and begin to haul Avery and Craven, who are still kicking and throwing punches, away from each other. The referee redirects attention to Assassin and Reave who are talking trash to each other from opposite sides of the ring. They lock up in the center, and then Assassin whips Reave into the corner. He charges, but is met by Scott Reave elbow. Reave hops up to the top rope and tries to hit a cross body on the staggering Assassin, but he is caught midair by the much larger opponent. A power slam! Assassin holds one hand up in the air, showing the fans he is going for a choke slam… he locks in the grip and picks Reave up high above the mat… but Reave counters it into a rollup…. NO! IT’S THE VOODOO LOCK! He has it locked in on the tree like arm of Assassin! Assassin is screaming in agony! COLIN MCKENDRY: “Scott Reave has Assassin right in the palm of his hand… just like I said he would before this match even started!” Assassin begins use his power to inch towards the rope. He lifts one arm up as if to tap out… but Assassin powers through it. He is inches away from the rope… but Reave pulls him away! There are screams from Assassin now. He raises his arm again… but now he twists and PUSHES REAVE OFF! Reave is thrown across the ring and right into the referee, who may be knocked out! EMMANUEL RUSSO: “A stunning display of strength by Assassin there Colin!” Reave is stunned. He pulls himself up using the ring ropes and looks over at the referee. He quickly slides out and pulls and a steel chair from under the ring. He holds it up so that all the fans can see it and then he slides back in. He waits as Assassin stands back up… then slams the steel chair across the injured arm! He stomps away on his head before slamming the chair against the elbow now! He locks in a hammer lock, then uses the rope to twist the arm some more. He hangs Assassins arm on the rope, and then hangs on it outside the ring. EMMANUEL RUSSO “What a sick strategy by Scott Reave!” The ref is up now, and is counting on Reave who is outside the ring. When the count reaches four, Reave drops the hold and slides back inside. He does an arm wrench than sends him in more pain. He twists and twists… then he goes for a quick cover…1… KICK OUT! Reave quickly locks in another arm bar. He drops elbows and locks in arm wrenches… anything to make Assassin tap. ASSASSIN POWERS OUT! Reave hops up quickly and rushes at Assassin… but is clotheslined! He tries again and is met with a power slam! Assassin is in control now! He whips Reave against the rope now and connects with a huge BIG BOOT! Assassin pulls Reave up and sets him up on the ropes. He charges, going for a big boot again, but Reave ducks it and Assassin has one foot on one side of the rope, one on the other. Reave sees an opening and he connects with a German suplex! He goes for a pin… 1…2… KICKOUT! Reave is furious. He exits the ring and pulls out a chair! He slides back in and lifts it up, going for a headshot… but Assassin catches it midair and snatches it from his hands. He twists and bends up the chair and then tosses it outside. Reave is shocked and is in the corner begging for mercy. He throws a punch that hits Assassin right in the face… but it doesn’t faze him… he tries again… Assassin stares him down some more. Assassin kicks Reave in the gut, and sets him up for the Ricola Bomb he calls the Assassin 3.0, and he connects hard! Assassin pulls him up and throws him into the corner. He throws a paw around Reave’s neck and lifts him up to where Reave is standing on the top turnbuckle. COLIN MCKENDRY: “THIS IS GOING TO GET VIOLENT!” Assassin tightens the grip, Scott is squirming for his life… but in one fluid motion… Assassin picks him up and tosses him into the ring. Reave lands limp on the mat… he isn’t moving. Assassin goes for the pinfall… 1…2… 3! JACOB POTTER: “Here is your winner… Assassin!” Assassin stands up and, without any celebration, exits the ring and begins to walk up the ramp. The referee begins to work on Scott Reave who is coughing now. Jake Avery runs down the map and begins to tend to his mentor. The camera goes to the top of the ramp where Assassin is standing. Assassin raises one of his gigantic arms and smiles as we cut backstage. = = = = = = = = = = = Backstage we see Madison Seton in her locker room, putting some tape on her wrists. There is a knock on her door and she turns around. MADISON SETON: “Mark? Come on in!” The door cracks open and Mark Cross slowly enters with a bunch of roses in his hand. He walks over to her and holds them out. MARK CROSS: “I got you some, congratulatory roses… as if the title wasn’t enough.” She smiles and takes them. She takes a huge whiff of the rose aroma and sets them on the table where all the rolls of tape are. MADISON SETON: “Well, I really appreciate it. I also really appreciate that you are willing to team with me one week after I won the belt.” Cross seems crushed, but Seton doesn’t seem to think it was an insult. MARK CROSS: “I’ll have my time with it.” Seton pinches off the last piece of tape. MADISON SETON: “Tonight, no matter what we may have to fight over in the future, we need to work as a team and take out High Voltage! We can do this.” They high five each other and laugh. MARK CROSS: “Don’t worry; I don’t think any of us will be pulling any stunts tonight.” There is a knock on the door. They look at each other; it is obvious neither of them knows who it could be. MADISON SETON: “Come in…?” The door opens, it is Alexii Ravynova. ALEXII RAVYNOVA: “Excuse me for interrupting. Mark, could you leave please… Madison and I need to have some girl talk.” Mark seems annoyed, but he complies. Alexii takes his seat. ALEXII RAVYNOVA: “Before I surprise you, I just want you to know that you are my main target right now. I don’t like you. I don’t like your game. I will do everything in my power to make sure you fail. See you around… girly.” Ravynova exits the scene, Seton doesn’t really understand what just happened, and we cut back to the announce booth. EMMANUEL RUSSO: “Alexii Ravynova just let Seton know how things were going to be, I like that in a general manager… a no nonsense type of girl.” COLIN MCKENDRY: “Did Scott Reave really just loose his match?” EMMANUEL RUSSO: “Yeah, they had to help him out during the last segment.” COLIN MCKENDRY: “…WHAT IS THE WORLD COMING TOO?” BLAKE BECKETT: “That was one hell of a match by both men though. It was a tossup all through it.” COLIN MCKENDRY: “Scott Reave lost… AGAIN?” EMMANUEL RUSSO: “Yes, he really did Colin. And now Jake Avery is in the ring with a microphone in his hand… he looks like his is about to speak.” We cut to the ring where Jake Avery is pacing around and pulling at his hair. He begins to mumble into his microphone. JAKE AVERY: “This can’t happen… it can’t happen. Scott Reave is good, Scott Reave is great… Scott Reave is my mentor… he is my boss… uh….” Jake Avery suddenly regains his composure. JAKE AVERY: “What you just saw was an anomaly. It won’t happen again for a hundred years! A HUNDRED YEARS! Scott Reave is the best damn wrestler in this company… everyone else is a JOKE! A BIG FUNNY JOKE! He will be back later… he will be back…” Suddenly, “Sing for the Moment” by Eminem bursts through the speakers and Nate Amazing makes his way to the ring with a microphone in hand. NATE AMAZING: “Jake… calm down… this is really overreacting at its finest.” Amazing enters the ring and gets close to Avery. NATE AMAZING: “I know how it can feel to be alone in this company. I begged to be a part of High Voltage… but you know what… I was denied. I sulked over it… I acted like it was the end of the world… but you know what… it wasn’t. The man who has fed the fact that he is unbeatable has been beaten, and now you have no idea what to do with yourself. How about DROP HIM! Leave him; go be your own man. It isn’t hard. You need to make a name for yourself… not leach off of his fame.” Avery and Amazing stare at each other for a moment. JAKE AVERY: “Scott Reave is why I am here today… you wouldn’t understand that. He brought me here, he got me this match I am about to fight. I am going to show him that I am not mistake. I am going to make you bleed Nate.” Jake drops the microphone and retreats into the corner. Nate pulls off his shirt and goes into his corner as well. The stare each other down for a moment, and then the ring announcer shows up and being the stare down. = = = = = = = = = = = JACOB POTTER: “The following contest is a first blood match! In this corner… weighing in at 215 pounds… from Georgetown, Texas… Nate Amazing!” The crowd goes wild. Amazing lifts an arm up to them. JACOB POTTER: “And in this corner… weighing in at 200 pounds… from Springfield, Missouri… Jake Avery!” The crowd boos him, but Avery doesn’t care. DING! DING! DING! The match begins and both Jake Avery and Nate Amazing are given kendo sticks. Amazing swings at Avery’s head, but Avery ducks and hits him square in the back. Avery continues to swing at the legs. Avery tosses Nate into the corner and pulls a screwdriver out of his boot! He heaves straight for the head, but Nate ducks it and the screwdriver gets stuck right in the turnbuckle! EMMANUEL RUSSO: “That thing is stuck deep inside that cushion… think if that was Nate’s eye!” The match continues. Avery grabs a chair and swings at Amazing’s head, but he looks like he was going to block it, so he settled with a knee shot instead. Then he hits a shot to the back after Nate Amazing ducks another head shot. Avery fires again, but this time the chair hits the ring rope and bounces back and nearly hits him in the face. Amazing sees and opening and launches himself off of the ropes to hit a running STO that sends Avery to the ground. Nate starts throwing punches to Avery on the ground. After five hits, he gives up and goes to grab the chair. He folds out the chair in the center of the ring and slams Jake’s head against the seat. He does it once more, and then unfolds the chair and lays it flat on the ground. Since Avery is actually smaller than Amazing, he goes for a choke slam onto the chair… but Avery gets out of it with an elbow to the back of Nate’s head. Nate is dazed. He hobbles around the ring a bit before taking a shot at Avery again, but Avery ducks it and connects with an STO of his own onto the chair. Avery checks Nate to see if he is bleeding, and he isn’t. Avery paces around the ring until he sees the kendo stick, which he breaks in half. COLIN MCKENDRY: “Those splinters can cause some damage for sure!” Avery walks over to Amazing with the jagged edge of the kendo stick pointing towards his face, but Amazing holds it back, they struggle for a bit before Avery kicks Amazing in the gut. Avery goes for the cut again… they struggle for a bit… but Amazing hits a low blow on Avery out of desperation. Amazing gets the kendo stick now and comes up from behind Avery with the kendo stick and tries to dig the jagged end into his skull, but Avery pushes him off. Avery digs into his boot once again and pulls out a pair of brass knuckles. He slides them on and when Amazing comes back for more, he just turns around and goes for a punch, but NATE HITS A SUPERKICK TO THE FACE! Avery is out cold flat on the mat, but there is no blood. Nate peels Jake up and whips him into the turnbuckle and attempts a stinger splash… but JAKE steps out of the way and swings for a punch, but misses high when Nate ducks. Nate pushes Avery back first into the turnbuckle, and Nate gets into his corner as well. DING! DING! DING! EMMANUEL RUSSO: “What just happened?” The camera cuts over to close up, who is bleeding ever so slightly out of his nose. BLAKE BECKETT: “He got a nose bleed in a first blood match!” Avery notices he has a nosebleed and pleads to the referee that it isn’t his fault, that he has gotten them since he was a kid. JACOB POTTER: “Here is your winner… Nate Amazing!” Nate Amazing raises one arm into the air as he watches Avery yell at the referee. Nate slowly exits the ring as we cut to commercial. = = = = = = = = = = = After the commercial break is over, we cut backstage were High Voltage are talking in front of Garrett Holloway’s trailer. JUSTIN VOLT: “I haven’t seen him all night.” AJ LIGHTNING: “You probably scared him last week; you were about to tell everybody.” JUSTIN VOLT: “Well, everybody has the right to know.” Just then, the door to the trailer opened and Garrett Holloway walked out with a briefcase in his hand and a coffee in the other. You need to pay attention to your match tonight… it is rather important, don’t you think?” AJ LIGHTNING: “Maybe, but I think we can handle it.” GARRETT HOLLOWAY: “Well, good luck then.” Holloway exits the scene and High Voltage stands there confused. We cut to the announce booth where Blake, Colin, and Emmanuel are sitting. EMMANUEL RUSSO: “Welcome back to Affliction, I’m Emmanuel Russo alongside Colin McKendry and the GIW Chairman Blake Beckett… it is almost time for the NOX Title match. Sparrow is in control as the stops continue. He is now choking Bryant on the ring rope…1…2… KICK OUT! BOTH MEN ARE DOWN! Sparrow goes for a stinger splash but Bryant moves out of the way. Both men are down. Sparrow charges again, but Bryant counters with a flap jack! Both men are down again. The winner of the match will be the GM for the night… I can’t wait to see who shows up!” Garrett Holloway exits the ring and begins to walk up the ramp. He spies Blake, Colin, and Emmanuel and begins to walk over towards them. GARRETT HOLLOWAY: “I think I’ll join you guys tonight!” COLIN MCKENDRY: “Welcome Mr. Holloway!” BLAKE BECKETT: “Pleasured to have you join us!” EMMANUEL RUSSO: “Well there you have it; the winner of the open battle royal next week on Affliction will be the GM the week after Exile! What a stipulation!” GARRETT HOLLOWAY: “We like to make an impact here on this brand.” EMMANUEL RUSSO: “We sure do! When we get back from this promo video, we will be all set to begin the main event of the evening. High Voltage vs. Madison Seton and Mark Cross… next on Affliction!” = = = = = = = = = = = “To Be Loved” by Papa Roach hits the speakers and High Voltage pops through the curtain. Cross slams Volt into the barricade and Seton is landing chops on AJ. The crowd is going insane! Cross and Seton throw Justin and AJ into the crowd! The crowd begins to toss them back, and when they hit the mat Seton and Mark are quick to begin the stomping. Seton lands some more chops, but Volt pokes Cross in the eye and DDTs him on the concrete floor! AJ gets the upper hand on Seton and slams her into the steps. Volt gets Cross into the ring, and Seton slides in as well. Cross stomps a few times on AJ, then goes and tags in Seton. They double team AJ in the corner. Mark is tagged back in. Mark whips Lightning into the corner and then hits him right in the neck with a clothesline. COLIN MCKENDRY: “I guess the match is under control now.” Cross locks in a hammer lock on AJ. Tag in to Seton. Seton rams Lightning’s head into the corner and then connects with a few chops. She whips him into the opposite corner, then goes for a shoulder to the gut… but AJ dodges it, sending Seton into the steel post. Volt wants in, and he gets in! Volt goes right after Madi, landing rights and lefts. He whips her into the corner and charges, but is met by a boot. Chop! Seton does snap mare and then stomp to Volt’s face. She punts him in the ribs, and then hits a soccer kick to head! Tag to Cross. Seton holds Volt still while Cross hits a right hand that sends Volt spinning, followed by another! He goes and drops a knee right on his face! Cross uses the ring to his advantage. Seton distracts the ref while he drapes Volt out of the ring and hits a good elbow to the face from the outside! He does it once more, and then he enters the ring. Volt is still draped over, and Seton executes a leg drop that sets his head VOLTING backwards. BLAKE BECKETT: “That is great use of the ring as a weapon!” Cross pulls Volt out…1…2…KICK OUT! Cross pulls Volt up, undeterred. He pulls him into the corner and tags in Seton. She hits a chop then locks in a choke hold, then she lets go. Volt is bleeding. Two right hands from Seton, then she tags in Mark Cross who hits two of his own! Volt is on his knees. Cross cups Volt’s head against his stomach and then let’s loose a flurry of right hands to open up the wound. Cross pulls him up and then whips him into the corner… NO! Volt reverses it and sends Cross into Lightning’s knee! The cheap shot didn’t do much damage though; Cross turns around and throws AJ into the ring! RIGHT! LEFT! LIGHTNING IS BACKED UP INTO THE CORNER! Volt gets up and spins Cross around, going for a few blows of his own, but Cross blocks it and punches him right in the face! They are heading towards the opposite corner now… but Lightning gets behind Cross and chop blocks him to his knees. Volt tags in AJ. AJ continues to work on his knee… with punches and stomps to the knee. He kicks in in the knee again, and then works on torqueing the leg until he screams! He slams the leg into the steel post! Volt kicks him in the knee again, and then plays to the crowd. He goes to lock in the figure four that would end it all, but Cross kicks him off and right into the steel post! Cross is crawling towards Seton! TAG! CHOP! CHOP! CHOP! WHIP AGAINST THE ROPES… IT’S COUNTERED! Seton is ready though, a flying shoulder block! Atomic Drop, then a straight right hand! Seton goes and knocks Volt off of the ring! With all her might, she hits a scoop slam on AJ! She climbs to the top rope… SHE DROPS THE ELBOW! Justin Volt is back in the ring, and when she turns around she is brought down with his clothesline! The crowd is silent. COLIN MCKENDRY: “That is the way to take care of a woman… right guys?” Cross comes in for the save with a right hand to Volt. He catapults him out of the ring! The ref sends Cross out of the ring. AJ charges Seton, going for spear, but Seton moves out of the way and AJ goes through the ropes, colliding with Justin Volt! Seton gets momentum and leaps over, landing on them both! Seton is up, and she tosses AJ into the ring. When she gets on the apron, Volt grabs her leg, but he is kicked off. The ref didn’t see the hit. The ref forces Cross away, and AJ is still inside the ring. Seton pulls herself up to the apron, where AJ gets a hold of her. Volt pulls Seton into their corner and then tags in AJ. AJ gets to work, stomping on Seton once again. More punches to the face! They are making her bleed! She is in the corner, and the assault continues. AJ pulls her up and then hit a backbreaker, followed by a pinfall. 1…2… KICK OUT! AJ tags Volt. He clubs her I the back, then hits a DDT. He puts a foot on her chest and taunts to the crowd, but when he realizes she is stirring, goes back to work… clubbing her again. She is pushing him back, and then he rakes the eyes again, sending her down. Tag to Volt. They pull Seton by her hair and connect with a double STO! 1…2… CROSS BREAKS IT UP! VOLT SPINS HER AROUND, THEN IS HIT WITH A DDT! THE CROWD WANTS THIS TAG! CROSS WANTS THIS TAG! EMMANUEL RUSSO: “GO!” They are INCHES AWAY! THEY BOTH REACH FOR IT… VOLT HITS A REVERSE DDT! HE TAGS IN AJ! AJ tells Seton he setting up for his signature powerbomb… Seton gets up… kicks her in the gut… SHE TRIPS HIM UP! THEY ARE CRAWLING FOR THE TAG! TAG TO VOLT! TAG TO CROSS! Cross comes in and demolishes Volt, HIGH KNEE! PUNCHES! FACEBUSTER! DOUBLE A SPINE BUSTER! Cross has momentum! The ref is looking at the High Voltage girls on the outside… VOLT hits a low blow on Cross! He gets ready for a spear! HE GOES FOR IT! CROSS DODGES! HIS FACE HITS THE TURNBUCKLE! Mark Cross connects with the GO 2 SLEEP! 1…2… AJ BREAKS IT UP! Seton suicide dives AJ out of the ring. They are slugging it out. Cross is back up, she is setting Volt up for another go to sleep, but Volt pushes him into the turnbuckle! Volt is up, puts a dazed Cross in the center of the ring, setting up for the frog splash when he just happens to look over at the ramp. EMMANUEL RUSSO: “IT’S TONY MIRANDA AND THE SHADOW! Cross turns him around and hits him with the Ketteiteki Desaki… a Tiger Driver…1….2…..THREE! DING! DING! DING! GARRETT HOLLOWAY: “SON OF A BITCH!” JACOB POTTER: “Here are your winners… Madison Seton and Mark Cross!” = = = = = = = = = = = Volt and AJ are ambushed by Miranda and The Shadow.
