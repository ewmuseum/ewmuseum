RILEY STEVENS: “Alex won this belt from Aspen now he faces a woman that helped cost him the belt several times before and in Alexis he faces a real challenge in his first defense.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “She’s got this.” Alex finishes the home stretch down towards the ring walking up the steps and through the ropes into the ring, looking across the ring at Alexis handing the belt to the referee as he takes it from displaying it to each side of the ring. As he finishes he walks to the side of the ring handing it out as the referee calls for the bell. DING DING DING! This here is our main event tonight where the winner will meet Madison Seton at ‘Moment of Glory III’ in the Main Event as these two go ahead and circle one another as this crowd shows who they support as an “Alex! Alex! Alex!” chant erupts as Alex stops for a second to let it soak in as Alexis rolls her eyes, and then the two go ahead and start things off with a lock up as Alex quickly shoves her off of him, pushing her back easily into the ropes as Terry shakes it off the two again circling before going into a second lock up this time Alexis uses it to tug his arm behind his back, wrenching on it but not wanting to really waste any time Castellanos ducks down and uses his strength to drag her with his arm over him. Both hop right back up and its Alexis again initiating throwing a Spinning Wheel Kick that Alex catches with a big smirk on his face as she bounces around on one leg as he seems to be having some fun with it finally tossing her foot to the side spinning her around which he uses to roll her up 1…Kick Out. RILEY STEVENS: “Crafty move early from Alex.” Castellanos hop up to his feet as does Alexis but with some momentum Alex immediately allows her to run right into his awaiting arms hoisting her over his shoulders, dropping her down with a Powerslam again taking the pin 1…Kick Out. Still not enough but it’s got these fans going as Alex gets up to his feet with a grin on his face as he waits for Alexis to get up, she does so a lot slower now as this time he grabs her going for a ‘Starlight Suplex’ a Fisherman Suplex but as he lifts her Alexis knees him in the face causing him to drop her down to the mat and with Alex momentarily stunned she successfully hits the Spinning Wheel Kick this time, setting her up for a quick DDT. These fans boo her as she doesn’t bother with a cover instead standing up to her feet calling for him to get up as she takes a few steps backwards as he gets up to one knee but that’s just what she wanted as she rushes forward smacking him with a big Shining Wizard to the face that drops him as she now makes the cover 1…Kick Out. RILEY STEVENS: “These Main Event’s never disappoint.” Alex shakes it off a bit as he rolls away to the ropes using them to get up as Alexis runs at him as soon as he gets to a vertical base only for him to shoulder her up and over the top rope flipping her over only for her to catch her balance on the apron. These fans boo the fact she landed, as Alex turns around and catches a big elbow from her over the top rope as he stumbles back and she now tries to crawl back in but before she can get more then one foot through Alex rushes her hitting her side with a knee, crushing her rib as she falls through the ropes in obvious pain as she lands hard on the outside of the ring a loud applause from the crowd as the referee walks over to the side of the ring preparing to count her out, 1…2…Alex realizes it and never wanting a count out win quickly slides out of the ring following her up grabbing her off the ground and hoisting her up trying to slide her in but he instead eats a elbow as she throws one backwards, he stumbles back into the crowd and Alexis rushes him tackling him into the barricade around the ring slamming his back into it. RILEY STEVENS: “These fans are getting this up close and personal.” Castellanos grimaces in pain as the referee now starts to count both of them out 1…2…3…Alexis quickly follows up with a knee to his stomach just to add more pain finally grabbing him and pulling him forward for the trifecta slamming his head down onto the ring apron, then realizing she can’t win the belt via count out she rolls him back in. Following him on inside Alexis quickly covers him but Alex kicks out before the referee can even get down. Annoyed now Alexis gets up to her feet, raising her arms and taunting a bit only getting more boo’s from the crowd as Alex crawls up in the corner of the ring, hurting all over after her assault outside the ring as she rushes at him going to kick his head off with a Big Boot in the corner but he ducks out of the way sending her leg stretched over the top rope as Alex finishes the job placing the other up there trapping her there with both legs tied up in the Tree of Woe. RILEY STEVENS: “Alexis is all tied up now…” With a head of steam Alex runs forward with a baseball slide kicking Alexis’s jaw in as this basically immediately releases her legs as she drops forward awkwardly on her head as these fans cheer Alex on as he takes a few steps away from her now looking to capitalize on what he has done thus far by hoisting her up to her feet then delivering the ‘Starlight Suplex’ a Fisherman Suplex that lands hard but respecting Alexis enough he doesn’t go for the pin instead he decides to go extremely high risk right about now looking towards the top rope walking over to it to a loud ovation from the crowd as he steps up to the top as Alexis gets up slowly in the middle of the ring and as soon as she looks to turn around he jumps for a Diving Dropkick that surprisingly works, the force sending Alexis flying backwards across the ring as this crowd goes wild, the fall still seeming to hurt Alex just as well. RILEY STEVENS: “What a move!” Getting up to his feet none the less Alex looks out at the crowd nodding his head holding his back, taking some damage in the match as he walks over to get Alexis but smartly she uses the ropes to pull herself out of the ring willingly letting herself take the fall to the outside to delay any further offense from Castellanos. Not wanting her to get any sort of breather Alex goes ahead and follows her out of the ring, rolling out hoisting her up with ease using the back of the trunks to throw her into the ring sliding in himself but Alexis seems to recover quickly and is able to pound him with boots from the top, over and over again but Alex fights his way through them getting to his feet regardless grabbing Alexis and whipping her off the ropes throwing a big clothesline out there for the rebound but she ducks it instead throwing a knee into his gut then dropping him with a Bulldog to end his little run once and for all, going for a pin 1…Kick Out. Still nothing close. RILEY STEVENS: “These two are going at it right now.” Getting up to her feet now Alexis seems to be ready to get rid of Alex now, wanting that gold and wanting it bad and as soon as he gets up she nails him with a Tornado DDT as soon as he gets up, to a loud boo from the crowd as she doesn’t pin him instead she sets up in front of him finally going for a Standing Moonsault which lands looking beautiful, as she makes the cover 1…2-Kick Out! And just like that things get more interesting as Alexis gets to her feet, screaming at him to get up wanting to end this once and for all as he gets up and she grabs him nailing him with the ‘Flawless Victory’ a Inverted Stomp Facebreaker! The crowd even lets out a gasp as the move lands and Alex falls to the mat with Terry quickly jumping on top 1…2-Kick Out. Again he kicks out to a loud response from the crowd, Alex still battling here despite losing all momentum here fast. RILEY STEVENS: “Huge move only gets a two count by Alexis.” Terry gets up now looking more annoyed then ever about him kicking out there as she looks around the ring at the fans all of them wanting her to lose as she walks to the corner perhaps getting a little full of herself as she jumps up from one rope to the next jumping backwards from the top for the ‘MGME’ (Most Glamorous Moonsault Ever) but rolling towards the ropes Alex is able to avoid it sending Alexis chest first into the ropes, the crowd going wild just for that reason as Alex gets up to his feet rather quickly now seeming to realize that his opportunity has now been given to him as she gets up holding her chest in pain but caring less Alex grabs her whipping her off the ropes and on the rebound he hoists her up into the ‘Gold Standard’ a Sitout Spinebuster! This crowd goes wild as he falls down on top covering her 1…2-Kick Out, her will to win still strong. RILEY STEVENS: “What a match so far, both not giving an inch.” Having been in this type of match before Alex gets up knowing he can’t give her any chance to try to get out of it and rather then mess around he bends down locking in the ‘24 K’ a Texas Cloverleaf! This crowd goes ape…crazy as the hold gets fully in! However Alexis does have the benefit of being close to the ropes already as she immediately goes for them reaching out as Alex tightens it on her, screaming out just wanting to make her tap out here as the referee steps in getting a close look as Alexis seems about ready to tap out but she makes one last pull and gets to the ropes! This crowd groans as Alex even seems a bit let down as he breaks the hold taking a few steps away now readying up for the finale wanting to end this one as he preps for the ‘Golden Arrow’ as this crowd gets up sensing the end. RILEY STEVENS: “Could this be it?” Alexis gets up stumbling forward as Alex goes for the Superkick! But she ducks under it, hitting him with a Yakuza Kick that she uses to set up the ‘Glam-Go-Round’ a Ranhei! This crowd watches as the move hits perfectly with her on top 1…2…KICK OUT! This crowd goes absolutely bonkers as Alex kicks out as Alexis sits up looking around confused as she puts her hands on her head in shock as Alex just won’t go away, as she gets up looking at the referee yelling at him seeming to be telling him to “Just get it over with!” As Alex gets up behind her and when she turns around SMACK! A ‘Golden Arrow’ as the crowd goes wild and Alex jumps on top 1……..2……Alexis gets her foot on the ropes after a very slow count as this crowd boos as the referee breaks it up. RILEY STEVENS: “That was nearly it!” Alex gets up even he seems to take a glance at the referee as if to ask what exactly happened with that one as he shakes his head knowing he can’t waste any time as he walks over to Alexis who is still on the ground. As he does she kicks up nailing him right where the sun don’t shine as these fans groan as Alex stumbles backwards in the ring turning his back to her as the referee reaches into his pockets and hands Alexis something, as she puts them on her hands they are revealed to be Brass Knuckles! Confusion sets in as Alex turns around and she smacks him with them, maybe not the strongest punch but the brass knuckles do all the work connecting on his jaw as he falls to the mat out as she falls down on top, seeming to just be able to get that last punch off as the referee slides down counting, 1..2..3! After the referee slipped Alexis the brass knuckles she wins! REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner and NEW GIW Undisputed Champion…Alexis Terry?!” This crowd boos in confusion as Alexis Terry holds up her arms in victory falling to one knee holding her rib that seems to have taken some damage in the match, as this crowd and everyone in the building boos as the referee rolls out of the ring grabbing the belt and bringing it inside handing it over to Alexis who hugs it tight as Alex lays still out. RILEY STEVENS: “That referee just gave her brass knuckles! WHAT THE HELL IS GOING ON? Did she pay him off, these referee’s are supposed to be fair and impartial and now we have a new champion because of this guy!” The crowd’s boos make Riley have to raise his voice to even be heard though he is yelling anyways as Terry continues to hug the belt as the referee starts to rip at his own skin for some reason finally that skin comes off in the form of an extremely well done mask and make up, as he rips it off to reveal himself as David Sinclair bringing even more boos from the crowd. RILEY STEVENS: “David Sinclair! DAMNIT! NO!” This crowd is at this point deafening as Alex who rolled to the corner holding his jaw has now come to and is looking up with his head laid against the bottom turnbuckle to see Sinclair standing behind a kneeling Alexis who is holding the belt so tightly not wanting to let it go. RILEY STEVENS: “This is wrong! After an amazing event, Sinclair does this! DAMNIT, I can’t even talk. Tune in next week! Hopefully we get some punishment laid down.” The boo’s continue to rain down on them as Sinclair helps her to her feet putting her arm around him to prop her up as she uses the other arm to raise her belt into the air as the show slowly fades to this site. WINNER: ALEXIS TERRY (New GIW Undisputed Champion)
