Thursday Night Fusion, 4 July 2013

4 July 2013

GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: THURSDAY NIGHT FUSION LIVE! From Porretto Beach – July 4, 2013 QUOTE In the open battle royal tonight we have the debuting Trent King versus local wrestler Mark Manning. Having made little friends on his way out Trent already has the fans turning on him as he prepares for this singles match. RILEY STEVENS: “Two guys looking to make a name for themselves, often times that makes for the best matches.” King preps in the ring as Manning does the same before the referee steps in and starts the match up here on Fusion. DING DING DING! Match Summary Though they were both new stars it was clear Trent King was one with a future while Mark Manning was one who just wasn’t quite big enough. Using the home town boy, like a rag doll taunting the crowd the entire time he did it…Made them hate him even more then they already started to early on. Taunting Manning through out the match, there was never any real doubt who was going to win, it was more so how Trent King was going to win. After toying with him for a bit, he decided to end it, doing so with a big time move he dubs ‘The Killing’ a Tiger Clutch Piledriver to end it and get him the pin. REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner by way of pinfall…Trent King!” King gets up with the fans disapproval already, standing over Mark Manning in the ring as these fans hope but still wonder how he’ll do next week when he faces someone on the main roster. WINNER: TRENT KING QUOTE It’s now time for our second match here on Fusion and this time, we have Harley Havoc waiting in the ring as “Let Me Be Myself” by 3 Doors Down hits and these fans react with mixed reactions for the return of Nathan Bailey IV as he heads down towards the ring, high fiving a few fans along the way before entering the ring, these fans showing him a lot more love then they did the first time perhaps having some weight off his shoulders as a single man. RILEY STEVENS: “Nathan Bailey IV was heading towards the top here in GIW before his departure, perhaps this time he’ll be able to reach the top.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “This whole being a nice guy thing is a joke, he’s going to choke!” Bailey enters the ring across from Harley, nodding his head as the referee calls for the bell. DING DING DING! Match Summary If you were ever curious about Nathan Bailey’s moveset, this match would have been a good time to get an update on it as the fans were witness to an absolute clinic in Nathan Bailey IV’s return to the GIW ring. Having had success in GIW before, this wasn’t a huge surprise but despite being else where, he didn’t seem to show any signs of let up as he dominated Harley Havoc from the start of the match, all the way to the finish. Showing a bit of a change of heart the fans were actually getting behind Bailey, due in part to his aerial arsenal that wowed them. In the end, it was the ‘Pearl Necklace’ a Flipping Neckbreaker that ended the night for Harley and secured Bailey the win. REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner by way of pinfall…Nathan Bailey IV!” These fans cheer as Nathan gets to his feet with a smile, having successfully won his return match, though it’s clear he’s more wanting to get a bigger name and back on the main card next week. WINNER: NATHAN BAILEY IV QUOTE As we do every week here on Fusion we end the show with one of the most anticipated matches of the week, every week. After winning the belt at ‘Aggressive Expansion’ and defending it last week we have "Frontline" by Pillar echoing through the speakers as we return to the ring Daniel Starr heading down to the ring the Deep South belt around his waist as in the ring waiting are challengers HardKore Comley and Nathan Starr. RILEY STEVENS: “This week Starr looks to keep his reign going against Comley and Nathan Starr, in this triple threat match.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “This should be a good one.” Starr heads into the ring looking out at the crowd with a smirk as he yet again holds up his belt, looking to continue his reign by defending it for the second time, a feat thus far unaccomplished as he hands it over to the referee who displays it then hands it out before calling for the bell. DING DING DING! The four corners of the ring would have been covered, had the referee been a competitor, but instead it was the champion and two challengers. Nathan and Comley wanted the Deep South championship and this was the only way they knew they’d be able to sky rocket themselves to that entitlement. However, despite their desires being the title on Daniel Starr’s shoulders, it didn’t have to be Daniel getting pinned to become the victor and subsequent champion. In the long run, the odds were always against the defending champion, and Daniel would soon learn that message the hardest way possible. Comley and Starr descended upon the champion together, as if a silent alliance was formed between the two and they secretly agreed to take out the champion first before having to deal with one another. Daniel Starr was not a fan of this idea, nor this alliance. He made Comley his dodge target and swept under the right hook, but Nathan caught him with a standing dropkick. This knocked Starr into the corner turn post where Comley began his barrage of physical assault. Daniel Starr had been taken under the control of the unruly alliance, but Nathan took the reigns. AMANDA ROWLAND: “Wow!” He turned Comley away from Daniel to get himself into the corner with his foe. He threw a few hard punches that bounced the fading body of his opponent into the post like sludge, despite Daniel’s efforts to counter the attacks. It only took one good swing to miss before Nathan found himself on the receiving end of Daniel’s vengeance. Comley shoved Nathan out of the way as Daniel struck him and slammed the champion back into the turn post with a running clothesline. Daniel Starr, dazed by the physical explosion of hard hitting mass against his chest, pooled down from the turnbuckle to the rim of the ring and fell to the outside. RILEY STEVENS: “That’ll hurt.” This now left Comley with Nathan Starr to wrap things up. That was assuming Nathan was going to go out silently and willingly. He charged forth, in hopes to get a quick drop on the larger Comley. A big boot silenced any dissent from the opposition and Comley dropped to his knees to cover Nathan Starr for the pin, but little did he knew that his opponent refused to be down and out. A shoulder raised and a count broken later, Starr rolled himself away and up to his feet, ready to continue the assault. He managed to pull in some punches to the gut and even maneuvered Comley into position for a bridging fisherman’s suplex, but Comley was too big for Nathan. The blonde tried harder, but Comley wouldn’t budge. Eventually he gave up on the move, but as Comley reared his forearm to strike down on the smaller man like a violent giant exploding in engorged rage, Nathan, whipped back a few steps, propelled himself against the ropes and connected with a swinging neckbreaker. The thud was loud for all to hear, but Comley was down. RILEY STEVENS: “The bigger the man, the bigger the thud.” Nathan went for the pin, but it was intercepted by the champion, Daniel Starr. Daniel punted his opponent’s skull to break the pin and as Nathan rolled onto his back, his hands immediately cradling the swell on his cranium, Daniel rushed to the turn post opposite of the one he had been held captive at and performed a swift, rushed swanton bomb to seal the deal with his vengeance toward his nemesis – Nathan Starr. Nathan was seemingly down and out, but Comley was just coming to when Daniel tried for a pin. He realized hope would be lost and pulled away from the attempt at victory to work on his next opponent. The monster of a man wrapped his banana hands around the neck of his opponent and hoisted him up, but Daniel was spry enough to writhe out of the hold and throw a low gut kick to catch Comley off guard. A leaping bulldog later and Comley was down again, but by a second man’s attack. Daniel went for the pin as quickly as he could, but realized Comley wasn’t out for the count just yet. The pin was broken, but Daniel’s spirits certainly were not. RILEY STEVENS: “Can the champion get it done?” He guided his opponent up off the canvas and struck him dazed to prep his next attack. He would pull out all the stops to get his title back around his waist, where he believed it belonged. He readied Comley for the Starrstruck and just as he charged forth to lunge the flat of his boot against the raw flesh of Comley’s body, Nathan, out of no where, bodied his opponent with a sudden running neck snap. With Daniel out of commission until further notice, and even left to the side of the ring like the next day’s carcass, it meant Comley, still dazed, was all set up for Nathan and ripe for the kicking. A strike to the lower tendons brought Comley to his knees and that’s where it all ended. Nathan Starr performed an early onset alzheimer upon Comley, with a super kick so charged, the smack could be heard throughout the arena. He floored his opponent and immediately dove in for the cover. The victory was soon his and so was the title. He was quick to raise up in victory as the new Deep South Champion. REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner and NEW GIW Deep South Champion…Nathan Starr!” These fans don’t seem to happy but there was really no way they were going to be too happy with the winner in this one as Nathan Starr rolls out of the ring with a big smirk on his face as the referee runs over grabbing his belt then bringing it over to him as Daniel gets up shaking his head. RILEY STEVENS: “In this battle of Starr’s, it’s Nathan that steals the belt by defeating Comley and accomplishes his goal of winning gold here in GIW.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Been telling you for weeks, this kids good.” Daniel stares out at Nathan shaking his head as Nathan holds up his belt, having won gold already tonight having a chance later on to continue that trend by advancing in the tag team tournament. WINNER: NATHAN STARR (New GIW Deep South Champion)
