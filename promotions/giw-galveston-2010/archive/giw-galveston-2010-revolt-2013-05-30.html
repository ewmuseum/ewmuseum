GIW — Card

Revolt, 30 May 2013

30 May 2013
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: THURSDAY NIGHT REVOLT LIVE! At San Luis Hotel & Resort – May 30, 2013 BOOM ! BOOM! BOOM! Rather than the normal sounds and flair of the opening of GIW Revolt, “Did It On 'Em” began to play and the crowd's usual cheers were turned into boos as Aspen Chaud made her way down to the ring. She was already in her ring gear as her match would be opening the show, but the microphone in her hand warned the fans that she was going to be giving a speech beforehand. RILEY STEVENS: “Ladies and gentlemen, welcome to Thursday Night Revolt! I'm Riley Stevens alongside my partner Oliver Thibodaux, and new to the table this week... Ms. Amanda Rowland!” AMANDA ROWLAND: “Oh my gosh, this is the best moment of my life.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Really? That great? Probably because Aspen is here right now.” RILEY STEVENS: “The most dominant woman in GIW history is making her way down to ring, I wonder if she is going to have anything to say to Alexis Terry one week after a crippling loss.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Two queens and only one crown.” AMANDA ROWLAND: “Wait... what?” Aspen climbed into the ring as her music began to stop. She stood right in the center with her microphone and smiled. ASPEN CHAUD: “Alright, lets get down to business here. I dont plan to stay long because, frankly, being in front of you people makes me sick to my stomach, but I have something I need to address. Alex Castellanos is running a charity case right now, and he has been launching a twitter drive to BEG people to come to Galveston Island Wrestling. Probably because no one wants to work for him. Ha! So, he created this challenge and the first GIW wrestler to re-tweet the account would be given an opportunity to pick his or her opponent next week. I was the winner! As always.” The crowd boos and jeers Aspen. ASPEN CHAUD: Now I thought about this for a very, very long time you see. There are so many people on the GIW roster that I would love to make tap out to my KMA, but I found it very hard to settle on just one.” “Alex”, “Alexis”, “Alioth”, and “Tony” chants poured from the audience. ASPEN CHAUD: “Oh don't waste your breath. I already made my decision.” The crowd cheered in anticipation. Aspen was milking it for all it was worth. ASPEN CHAUD: “I choose... NOBODY!” The crowd is confused, they don't even know if she can do that... but Aspen continues on. ASPEN CHAUD: “And you better soak up what you can from me, because this week will be the LAST TIME you ever see me wrestle in a GIW ring.” The crowd goes nuts. Aspen rolls her eyes. ASPEN CHAUD: “Thats because I am going to become a full time manager! And over the next few weeks, I will be scouting the entire roster to find the one person who is on my level.” The crowd boos her arrogance. She laughs. ASPEN CHAUD: “But whoever I choose. They will be working for ME, I wont be working for them.” Her music fades back on now as the crowd boos some more. We cut back to the announce table. AMANDA ROWLAND: “Wow. That's a big announcement.” RILEY STEVENS: “Yes indeed it is. Aspen Chaud will become a manager.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “For shame! A three time Undisputed Champion calling it quits!” RILEY STEVENS: “I have a feeling we haven't seen the last of Aspen in the ring. This may be a ploy to get herself back into the main event.” AMANDA ROWLAND: “Yeah.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Riley, she is past the main event. Aspen IS GIW.” AMANDA ROWLAND: “Yeah Riley.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Aspen doesn't need to manipulate her way to the top anymore. She's already there.” AMANDA ROWLAND: “Yeah. Fo shizz.” RILEY STEVENS: “Well, I suppose we will find out. Her match is NEXT, right here on Thursday Night Revolt!” QUOTE As we reach the time for the opener these fans are excited and ready to go as “New God Flow” hits and the fans give a very bad reaction as out from the back comes Ryan Cavallari heading towards the ring to stand opposite of Aspen. RILEY STEVENS: "This match is sure to be one for the ages." The two lock up in the center of the ring, Aspen rakes the eye and pushes Ryan away from her. She smirks, then slides out of the ring like a snake in retreat. The ref begins the count. RILEY STEVENS: "Why is Aspen leaving?" OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Maybe she decided to leave early tonight." Cavallari stands in the ring shaking his head, as Aspen once again has ruined a match that had potential to be great. He doesn't let it bother him though, as a win is a win. He flips off Aspen from the ring, but she just smiles and laughs. The crowd starts the "hey hey hey, good bye" chants, but then boo Cavallari as he stands on the ropes shouting that he is still the best in the world. REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner by way of countout…Ryan Cavallari!” RILEY STEVENS: "Cowardly way out!" OLIVER THIBODAUX: "She can't risk being hurt for her new clients!" WINNER: RYAN CAVALLARI We cut backstage where Chris Callum is warming up for his match. His title belt is place in the background. Sara Hollister sits in a steel chair and watches him. CHRIS CALLUM: “You better get moving. Your match is right after mine.” SARA HOLLISTER: “I know. I just... I feel weird all of a sudden.” Chris stops jumping around for a moment and goes to Sara. CHRIS CALLUM: “Nervous?” SARA HOLLISTER: “No. Just... I just don't know if I am supposed to be here yet.” CHRIS CALLUM: “What do you mean?” SARA HOLLISTER: “Like... I'm not ready yet.” Chris gets down on his knees, appealing to Sara. CHRIS CALLUM: “Yeah, it is daunting here. Everyone is so good.” SARA HOLLISTER: “I just know I can be better.” CHRIS CALLUM: “Just go into your match confident, and you'll be alright.” SARA HOLLISTER: “I think I want to go back home and train some more. Take a bit of time off from GIW.” CHRIS CALLUM: “You don't have to do that..” SARA HOLLISTER: “I don't want to function at a lower level than I know I can someday. I just have to be patient. Hit the gym. Study the sport.” CHRIS CALLUM: “Lets just see how tonight goes, okay?” Sara nods, reluctantly. SARA HOLLISTER: “Alright.” They stand. CHRIS CALLUM: “Lets go show everyone what rookies can do.” And together, they exit towards the ring. Fade to commercial. QUOTE As everything settled down the camera cut to Chris Callum getting ready for his second match up of the night so far. Joshua Acquin obviously was not in the best of moods. Last week on Revolt he had been disrespected by the new GIW star in Chris Callum. Joshua had stated that he was out to teach the 'kid' some respect. Acquin had done many things in GIW. He was three times a TV champion, and a tag team champion. He felt an intense anger when someone as new as Callum came through like he owned the place. RILEY STEVENS: “These two had a little scuffle backstage last week and what better way to solve it, then let them battle it out in the ring?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Duh Riley!” Right out side the ring the camera cut to Sara Hollister who was tapping on the canvas trying to give Chris Callum some motivation to best Joshua Acquin. As she cheered the bell was rung to get things started, these fans going wild. DING DING DING! The moment the bell to start the match sounded off the disrespected GIW veteran was on Chris' tail. Callum felt the weight of Joshua's arm come across his chest. It seemed like all of the oxygen inside of the new GIW star's lungs had just escaped his body. It was not a good feeling at all. Callum hit the canvas below him hard back first. Joshia taunted him, the fans started to boo the former TV Champion. RILEY STEVENS: “Callum better watch it. He got himself into this mess.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I think he has the talent to back it up.” Joshua Acquin decided that he wanted to keep this match down on the ground. He dropped to his knees. Then attempted to arm an arm around the neck of Chris Callum, some how the newer wrestler was able to reverse it and twist the veteran's arm around into a submission hold. For a moment it seemed like Acquin thought of tapping out. He turned Chris Callum's submission hold around and brought him down with an arm drag. Callum landed on his side. It wasn't enough impact to keep him down for the count. Chris ran at Joshua again, Acquin placed his forearm under Callum's under arm. Attempting to flip him over one more time. Chris flipped it around this time hitting Acquin with an arm drag of his own. RILEY STEVENS: “What is this? An arm drag war?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Doubt it. But the kid's holding his own here.” Chris goes for the first cover of the match hooking Joshua's inner leg ONE! TWO! Acquin kicked out. Both men were able to rise up rather quickly. Adrenaline pumping through their bodies due to their hate for one another. Acquin charged forward for a clothesline. Chris ducked, the referee was far behind Chris but Acquin still kept running until he pushed the referee back stunning him for a little while. A dirty tactic. Joshua pulled a pair of brass knuckles out of his tights apparently ready to use the weapon against his new opponent. RILEY STEVENS: “Acquin don't stoop that low...” Sara spotted it, she jumped up on the ropes distracting Joshua Acquin. He turned to face Callum's friend. The referee was now aware of what was happening. Joshua pushed the brass knuckles back into his tights then attempted a running clothesline. Callum caught Acquin's arm and turned it into a backslide pin ONE! TWO! THREE! RILEY STEVENS: “No way....” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Chris Callum just beat a former champion here tonight! I'd be looking at him as a future North Beach Title holder if I were Alex Castellanos.” REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner by way of pinfall…Chris Callum!” Chris Callum exited the ring, walking up the ramp with Sara Hollister as Acquin in the ring angrily slapped the canvas yelling at the referee for what he believes to be a fast count as for the second time tonight Callum has picked up a huge victory! WINNER: CHRIS CALLUM A small promotional package is shown between the two matches hyping GIW's first iPPV, Aggressive Expansion, which will be held June 23rd, 2013. The package features Alioth Starre defending his belt during his first title reign, which lasted 140 days – the longest in GIW history. The tagline of the show is “Can he break his own record?” Only time will tell.. QUOTE “We Own It” by 2 Chainz hits as these fans cheer on a girl whose quickly come out as a fan favorite in Sara Hollister alongside the previously victorious Chris Callum riding high tonight carrying the Deep South belt on his shoulder, but here now to support Hollister as she heads down the ramp high fiving a few fans along the way before heading up the steps into the ring as Callum take’s a spot on the outside. RILEY STEVENS: “Hollister had a good showing last week but couldn’t come out with a win and she takes a real step up tonight against the strong starting Eliza Gray.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “This girl’s in another world now, she isn’t ready for it!” As her theme dies down “Bad Romance” by Lady Gaga hits to mixed reactions from the crowd as Eliza Gray heads out from the back with a smile on her face; these people not really sure about Gray yet, staying reserved while they are a hundred percent for Hollister in this one, something Gray doesn’t seem to let effect her at all as she heads down towards the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “You know I’d be lying if I told you Gray hasn’t looked good of late, she’s doing well.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Yeah I really like her new makeover…” Gray enters the ring looking across at Sara the two sharing a little stare down before the match as Callum gets the fans up on the outside and the referee calls for the bell. DING DING DING! The bell echoes and the crowd is ready as Eliza Gray and Sara Hollister stand toe to toe and lock up in the center of the ring. With ease, the GIW veteran Eliza Gray is able to hook Sara’s head in a side headlock and apply the pressure, hoping for the easy victory tonight. Sara however is a fighter and she will not go down this easy. Sara is able to power out of the move and send Eliza into the ropes. Eliza Gray is a smart wrestler and is able to use the move against her, flying off the ropes and hitting Sara with a shoulder block that sends Sara to the mat. Eliza drops down for the pin but is only able to get a one count on the new GIW upstart Sara Hollister. Eliza continues her early assault, now locking in a camel clutch. Sara cringes in pain as the referee asks if she wants him to call the match but Sara screams “No!” which the crowd can clearly hear. The fans begin to stomp their feet and pound on the guardrail allowing Sara to get to one knee, then the other knee. Now Sara is back to her feet and Eliza is wrapped around her neck like a monkey. Sara falls to her back and squishes Eliza underneath her. RILEY STEVENS: “Not sure who won that move…” Both competitors are back to their feet and Eliza, groggy, rushes Sara who nails a hard clothesline. Eliza smacks the mat but quickly is back to her feet. Sara is able to land a few right hands and then a kick to the gut. Dragon Suplex! Sara hits the move perfectly. She covers but is only able to land a two count. Smartly, Sara continues on the offensive. Sara bounces off the ropes closest to the downed Eliza and hits a beautiful standing cannonball legdrop. Once again, she covers but only receives another two count for her trouble. Sara mounts Eliza and begins to smash her face with right hand after right hand. The ref starts his count..1…2….3….4..and finally Sara breaks the move and screams loudly as the fans applaud her. Sara’s manager, Chris Callum is happy on the outside as he claps for the intensity Sara is showing. Sara picks Eliza up by her hair but Eliza counters and hits a beautiful dropkick. Both Sara Hollister and Eliza Gray are on the mat as the ref begins to count them both down. RILEY STEVENS: “Can one of these two competitors get up?” 1….2….3….4….5….6….7….8…Sara is to her feet first but not far behind is Eliza. The two exchange rights and lefts in the right. Sara whips Eliza into the turnbuckle and follows it up by a huge Yakuza Kick! Eliza stumbles out of the corner and right into a huge inverted neckbreaker. Chris is screaming for Sara to cover! ONE…TWO…TH NO! Eliza smartly gets her foot on the bottom rope. Sara looks frustrated but keeps her cool. She knows this would be a tough challenge. Sara grabs Eliza by the arm and begins to drag her away from the ropes in an effort to cover her again. ELIZA COUNTERS! She rolls Sara up in the center of the ring. ONE…TWO..KICKOUT! Eliza and Sara return to their feet with Sara knowing she was only a second away from defeat. Eliza and Sara meet again in the center of the ring and this time they once again exchange rights and lefts. Sara swings wildly and this allows Eliza to sneak around her and connect with a huge German Suplex. Eliza bridges it. 1…2…No! Another kickout from Sara who is showing she has a ton of heart. RILEY STEVENS: “This girl will not go down without a big fight.” Eliza picks Sara up by her hair this time but Sara slaps her hands away. A kick to the gut from Hollister. Legalized Suplex! Wait..no..Eliza is able to escape. She locks in the Princess’ Crown! Sara has nowhere to go! She taps..Eliza is able to pick up the win. REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner by way of tapout…Eliza Gray!” Gray gets up as Sara rolls away in pain and Gray gets up slowly holding up her arms, coming narrowly close to defeat before turning things around to keep her hot start going here in GIW. RILEY STEVENS: “What a match by this two and after a great showing by Sara its still Eliza who has come looking extremely good that picks up the win.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “She’s the future Riley, book it.” Callum rolls into the ring checking on Sara as the referee raises Eliza’s hand but she pulls away and exits the ring, celebrating on her own as she picks up a win to continue her ascension here in GIW. WINNER: ELIZA GRAY Cut to the interview area. ZACHARY PAGONIS: “I'm standing here live with Alexis Terry... Alexis...” She grabbed the microphone from him. ALEXIS TERRY: “Piss off.” He rolls his eyes, as his first interview back with the company has went the same way so many of the ones did last time. ALEXIS TERRY: “I don't need him to ask me questions to get my point across. Last week, I defeated Aspen Chaud, one of the most famous wrestlers in this place. This week, I will be one of the few to ever defeat Alioth Starre in singles competition – yet my name doesn't seem to always come up when someone mentions the title hunt. I defeated Alex Castellanos and held the Undisputed title before – and if there is one thing I want in this world more than anything, it is to become champion again..” She was cut off. ELIZA GRAY: “Hey, Alexis..” ALEXIS TERRY: “What do you want.” ELIZA GRAY: “I want you to get out of my way. I've already won my match tonight.” She shoves past Alexis. ALEXIS TERRY: “Oh no... no...no...no..” Alexis pulls Eliza by the hair and throws her on the ground. ALEXIS TERRY: “You think you're going to be the top bitch here?” Eliza gets to her feet and slaps Alexis across the face. ELIZA GRAY: “You're the one who taught me to always be on top.” Alexis swings, but Eliza blocks it and pushes Alexis backwards. ELIZA GRAY: “You're going to regret teaching me that.” Fade to commercial. “Sadeness” by Enigma hits and these fans cheer on the recently debuting GIW Star, Caballero Blanco along with his mask as he heads out from the back as usual full of energy, these fans unable to see his face but really starting to like him none the less as he heads towards the ring, high fiving a few fans before jumping in and rolling under the ropes into the ring impressively. RILEY STEVENS: “Blanco turned some heads last week in his debut, but he gets a big step up against a man like Tony Miranda.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Lot of people come from different countries here to GIW and most of them, end up sucking!” As Blanco reaches the ring "The Edge of Paradise" by Kamelot hits and these fans go crazy with cheers as even though they may like Blanco, he still doesn’t have the fans admiration and love quite like Tony does, as he heads to the ring looking ready for battle as he looks up at Blanco and heads up the steps. RILEY STEVENS: “These two really ended up battling in that Battle Royal last week and it was Blanco that ended up eliminating Tony, Tony’s going to be looking for some revenge here.” Miranda gets into the ring, these fans loving him win, lose, or draw for his fight first attitude as he enters the ring and the referee gets them squared up to start. DING DING DING! The match began with a tentative lock up. Blanco wanted speed, and Tony was more calculated. The masked man threw a kick, Tony caught it. Blanco whipped and leapt to contort his body for an enziguri kick, but Tony ducked with the man’s foot still in his grasp. He let Blanco’s knee slam into the canvas and propped him up for a rear naked choke. The referee closed in on the two of them as Blanco tried wildly to escape the hold, but Tony was the stronger of the two and held it in tight like a fist. The submission hold had slowly begun to do its job and exhaust its victim. Like a boa, Tony would constrict as Blanco tried to free himself, or even breath. With every second under Tony’s hold, Blanco’s chances fared worse and worse. The referee noted Blanco’s arms tire and wilt like a dead plant. He lifted the right arm to check for signs of life, but there was no one home. He tried a second time and a little bit of blood began to pump back into Blanco’s skull, his arm holding still, but still wilting. Tony didn’t wait for a third check, he let loose the hold and slammed his opponent’s skull back against the canvas. RILEY STEVENS: “These two are two very evenly matched competitots.” Tony took a calculated risk with the ropes. He propelled himself for attack to continue the assault, but it missed. Blanco rolled quickly out of the way and took that chance to perform a high risk springboard moonsault that connected with his opponent. He went for the pin, but Tony pulled out of it with a shoulder. Blanco propped up, hopped back and waited for Tony to rise to the occasion, by performing a diving crossbody from the nearest turn post. It connected and he rolled off of his opponent to pull himself back up and ready to continue. Tony was slower to get back to one knee than the last time, but as he did, he blocked a kick from his opponent, only to be drawn into a springboard arm drag and left in tattered remains by the center of the ring. Blanco quickly covered him for the pin, but it was a no go. Tony Miranda would not drop so quickly as Blanco had hoped by his swift and needle precision attacks. He’d have to be more calculated and wear down his opponent instead. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “The Mexican is pretty decent.” Tony was up when Blanco was off like a bullet again toward the ropes for another sudden burst of speed. He ended it like a train to a cliff wall using a hard hitting discuss clothesline. He watched Blanco crumble like the mexican government to the canvas floor. He dropped down to his knees and reapplied the rear naked choke. It had drawn out what little energy Blanco had to get out of it the first, to all of his failures. The more Tony applied the pressure and slowed this hidden dragon, the more the crouching tiger could calculate where and when his victim would fall. So, as soon as the opportunity presented itself, the tiger struck! Tony slammed his opponent back down to the mat and pinned him, he didn’t care if Blanco refused to be quelled by the hold, he wanted victory and was going to get it. Now. RILEY STEVENS: “Can Tony get him here?” The referee went for the one, then the two, and as he his hand came crashing down for the third count, Blanco’s legs were whipped into a frenzy. He jerked out from under his opponent and broke the pin. Tony couldn’t understand it, but it didn’t enrage him. He had a bit of respect for this unbreakable bullet. He figured one last go would be the end of Blanco. The two rose together, Blanco under Tony’s grasp, but Blanco broke free! He leapt up with a huricanrana and then performed a final springboard moonsault to catch his opponent clear off guard and clearly beaten with a three count. REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner by way of pinfall…Caballero Blanco!” Barely victorious, he rose up with an arm raised and his eyes cast upon the crowd. He defeated Tony Miranda, the man with thirty-two victories under his belt, but Caballero Blanco refused to be number thirty-three. RILEY STEVENS: “What a huge win here for Blanco in a hard fought match, but he beats former Undisputed Champion; Tony Miranda here tonight.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Back to Home Depot he goes.” Blanco stands up and gives a respectful nod to Tony, who nods back at him for the second week in a row these two having really gone at it two weeks in a row now but in this match it’s Blanco getting his second GIW win, moving him up the ladder. WINNER: CABALLERO BLANCO We return from commercial to Alex Castellanos' office, where he stands behind his desk in his suit – Chris Callum, Tony Miranda, and Caballero Blanco all still in their ring attire standing on the other side. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “Gentlemen, you've impressed me since GIW has returned. I want this place to be filled with talent that makes other federations green with envy – and because of you, I believe we are succeeding thus far. Next week, I promised we would crown a North Beach Champion, and I want it to be one of you three men. The match will be a No Escape match, meaning there will be cage surrounding you. It will be brutal. It will not be easy, but at least I can rest knowing that the best man is champion.” The three men turned to one another. TONY MIRANDA: “I don't lose to the same man twice, Blanco.” Tony knew this match was right up his alley. CHRIS CALLUM: “Tony, you're a legend here, but I took Acquin out tonight, and I'll take you out next week.” Alex hurried around his desk to break up any tension. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “Well, I suppose we will find out next week.” Fade to the ring. QUOTE The main event was ready to begin as Alioth Starre and Alexis Terry made their entrances to opposite reactions. OLIVER THIBODAUX: "This crowd has it all wrong. Alexis is the best! The Queen!" As the bell rang both Alexis and Alioth started by feeling each other out, there was a tie up in the middle of the ring as Alioth swiftly gained the advantage by ducking behind Alexis with a rear waist lock. Alexis wriggled through the lock and was able to trip Alioth’s leg rather quickly with a drop toehold like maneuver as Alioth was flat on the back Alexis clearly tried to take advantage. With a headlock Alexis wrenched away at Alioth’s neck cutting off the circulation. Alioth wasn’t about to give Alexis the advantage, so he began fighting out of the headlock quickly. As Alioth got back to standing he went to backdrop Alexis, but Alexis was able to get to her feet as she flipped backwards and pushed Alioth towards the ropes. Alioth shot off at the ropes as Alexis ducked underneath him, and as Alioth came back he was met with a Yakuza Kick from Alexis sending Alioth sputtering backwards, Alexis thought it was safe and was met with a Kitchen Sink that doubled her over. Alioth saw her drop and went in for the pin 1…2! KICKOUT! RILEY STEVENS: "So close!" Alexis kicked out as Alioth looked towards the referee somewhat disgusted. Alioth turned back grabbing Alexis by the wrist dragging her to the corner, Alexis was trying to fight off the wrist lock but Alioth was a primed and ready technical wrestler. Pushing Alexis into the corner Alioth buried a knee into her midsection before shooting her off into the opposite ropes. Ducking down low Alioth nailed a front Chop Block that would make anybody cringe. Alexis popped up sprawling back first into the mat, she rolled around as Alioth brushed his hair out of his face somewhat annoyed. Alioth continued the pressure stomping down on her leg and dropped down was able to apply a Knee Bar. Alexis fought out best she could as she grabbed at the bottom ropes breaking Alioth’s submission, the Referee ended up counting 1…2…3..4…Alioth let her out of the submission. The Referee made sure Alexis wasn’t hurt. RILEY STEVENS: "This match is getting brutal!" Alexis stood up on her wobbling knees and was ready to go back on the offensive, then from the corner of her eye she saw Eliza Running towards the ring. OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Oh lawd! In the classing wrestling distraction technique, Eliza got on the apron and began to cuss out Alexis, who bit the hook and went to confront her. Alioth swept up behind and picked up the rollup victory. 1....2....3! Eliza stepped of the apron and joined Alioth on the outside. The crowd was going crazy. It seemed like the two had almost planned it, though no one knew if they did or not. REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner by way of pinfall…Alioth Starre” RILEY STEVENS: "This could lead to some very interesting things.." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "I might want to retract my endorsement." AMANDA ROWLAND: "Who?" WINNER: ALIOTH STARRE
