GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: WEDNESDAY NIGHT REVOLT LIVE! At Texas A&M Galveston – August 1, 2012 on Showtime BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! This show gets kicked off like all the rest with a wave of excitement as these fans are on their feet ready for some action but just as they are mid-cheer the lights go out just like that as the fans know exactly who is coming as an ‘A’ appears in lighting in the ring, for a split moment before disappearing when a moment later it seems the lights come back on and standing in the ring is none other then Alioth Starre with a smile on his face twirling around a microphone in his hand. ALIOTH STARRE: “It was only a year ago that we were in the middle of my reign as Undisputed Champion, a reign that lasted longer then any being ever here in GIW and at ‘Moment of Glory II’ I proved I was the best by defeating none other then Aspen Chaud…It was a night that I showed the world that I was the best ever and yet…one year into the future people have begun looking past me…I am still the best ever and I will prove it-” But before his sentence can be finished it is interrupted by none other then “Notes From A Sunday Morning” by Unjust as the crowd continues to boo as Ryan Cavallari walks out from the back with that same usual emotionless looking face as he looks down at Starre from the top of the ramp with a microphone of his own. RYAN CAVALLARI: “The best ever? Yet you couldn’t even beat Aspen a week ago. You know Alioth everyone else around here has this thought of you as some great wrestler but me personally I don’t see it. You came to me last week saying you didn’t think I was a future champion, yet…I am. And more so is the fact that a man whose once great career has dwindled into being nothing more then a name in the history books. I deserved a shot at the Undisputed title shot a long time ago and deserve it more then Seton, Castellanos, or even the current champion. But apparently they don’t think I am ready yet so I will prove it by defeating you one on one at ‘Moment of Glory III’.” Uncharacteristic of Ryan to say the least a long little speech but seeming to get his point across either way Cavallari lowers his mic his challenge laid down as Starre looks back to him shaking his head. ALIOTH STARRE: “You don’t challenge me Ryan, I challenge whoever I want. This is a new Alioth, the same one that defeated every person on the roster in a matter of two months not long ago. But you want a match but under these terms that GIW and you must agree to…No Aspen at ringside and the winner gets a shot at the Undisputed title…” With the challenge laid down now by Alioth as well Ryan gives just a simple nod to let him know it’s on as he heads to the back another epic match added to ‘Moment of Glory III’ in the form of Alioth versus Ryan for a shot at the Undisputed title. QUOTE After a small teaser for the Main Event of ‘Moment of Glory III’ between Madison Seton, Alexis Terry, and Alex Castellanos, "Party in the USA" by Miley Cyrus hits and these fans go wild as usual as out from the back comes Madison Seton with that big bright infectious smile high fiving some of the fans along the way as she heads towards the ring sliding on in with a grin on her face. RILEY STEVENS: “This is the woman who Sole Survivor set for a big match tonight with a woman who seems to have it out for her.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Is there a faker person then Madison Seton?” Nobody seems to share Oliver’s sentiments as she jumps to the top rope and poses some more jumping down and getting prepped up for her match but as she turns to the ramp no music hits instead, there is a long drawn out silence as confusion hits for these fans. RILEY STEVENS: “Perhaps Zerotine is late?” Everyone continues to wait for the music hit until the referee looks down at his watch and starts to head to ring the ball, forced to start the match after so long. Seton tries to tell him to wait wanting a match but he has to do it as he rings the bell. DING DING DING! These fans sit in confusion waiting for Zerotine to come walking out but as the referee starts the count they realize that may not be reality 1…2…3…4…5…Seton looks around confused as well, hand on her hips seeming a little angry with the whole situation…6…7…8…9…10! As the referee shrugs and calls for the bell. REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner due to count out…Madison Seton?” Seton and these fans are not left with much to celebrate but Madison raises her arms in victory for just a moment drawing some laughs from the crowd as she rolls out of the ring to sign some autographs as the camera shits to the announce table. RILEY STEVENS: “Well actually we have some answers as I have just been relayed a message that Zerotine Valentino appears to have had a relapse and was unable to make it tonight, the news only just coming to us now backstage. We wish her well in her recovery…” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Pathetic druggy.” A bit of a lack luster way to start the show to say the least but Seton does the best to remedy the situation signing some autographs around the ring as we head to the backstage area. WINNER: MADISON SETON We cut backstage to the GM Office, now occupied by both Matt Kluge and David Sinclair. They sit on the leather three seated couch on opposite sides. Kluge controls the remote control and smirks as he switches it off, turning to Sinclair who is staring blankly at the TV. MATT KLUGE: “Oh, would you look at that domination David? Did you see it? That will be Alexis Terry at Moment of Glory, and then we will have a new GIW champion, and you will be gone for good. Isn’t it sitting well in that pot belly of yours?” DAVID SINCLAIR: “Madison Seton is a great wrestler, but I have faith in Alexis Terry. She is clutch and isn’t addicted to drugs, Seton is just good on Revolt. I’m not worried in the slightest. You will be the one without a job.” MATT KLUGE: “Only clutch on Revolt? She won the Sole Survivor match at Category 5, don’t you remember. Beat Eliza, Alioth, and Ace... how did Alexis do at Cat 5? Didn’t she have to use brass knucks to get past Castellanos to win the belt?” DAVID SINCLAIR: “Alex... what a joke.” MATT KLUGE: “Hmm... it is a shame he got put in that match now that I really think about it. He already lost, regardless of how close of a match it was. Plus, if he wins, then Beckett is in control of Revolt booking again, and we know how awful that can be.” DAVID SINCLAIR: “He is the one who booked the Taipei Deathmatch if I recall.” MATT KLUGE: “And the Seize the Moment Ladder Match...” DAVID SINCLAIR: “And the EPW vs. GIW match...” MATT KLUGE: “On Hallowed Ground I just in general was mostly him...” DAVID SINCLAIR: “I mean, I tried to have my guys save that card, but it was too much to handle.” MATT KLUGE: “Look, I think you’re a slime ball...” DAVID SINCLAIR: “And you’re the biggest prick I’ve ever met in my life...” MATT KLUGE: “But at least I can book an entertaining show...” DAVID SINCLAIR: “Or keep a company afloat without changing the TV deal or sponsors every two weeks.” MATT KLUGE: “Yeah, he can’t be allowed to win that match.” DAVID SINCLAIR: “Hmm...” MATT KLUGE: “You can do something about it. You’re a bad guy. I have a reputation to keep.” DAVID SINCLAIR: “This is true.” Just then, Blake Beckett pranced through the cracked doorway to the GM’s office in a gray suit and two women on his elbows. He nodded his head to Kluge and Sinclair, who watched on in disgust. BLAKE BECKETT: “Sorry I’m late gentlemen, I hope I’m not missing the party!!!” They continued the stare down. BLAKE BECKETT: “Well, that’s sad. I figured you two would be celebrating considering you both are going to be out of a job in two weeks or so.” They both rose at the same time and brushed past Blake and his whores, he just shook his head and just the door, winking at them as we faded into a promotional package for MOG III. QUOTE “Forever” by Drake, Kanye West, Lil Wayne, and Eminem hits as the crowd begins to boo the man who has so recently returned in Jak Hendricks and a man whose return has thus far been a bit lack luster but judging by his smirk and trash talk to the fans you could never really tell that he had lost a match, especially last week a Tables Match. RILEY STEVENS: “I am sure Jak’s happy to get away from Storming Raven and prove perhaps that Raven may have just been a bad match up.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Hendricks is a notorious slow starter, he’ll get it going and fly right towards the gold around here.” Standing in the ring already is Rylie James as Hendricks enters the ring with a smirk on his face looking at her and intentionally walking to the corner she is by to climb up to the top rope taking his chance to pose as the crowd boos him before he jumps down as the referee separates them and calls for the bell. DING DING DING! Hendricks holds out his arms as soon as the bell is rung walking towards James who seems a little bit untrusting but he takes her hand in his, pulling it up to his lips and giving it a gentle kiss before dropping it as she seems a bit confused but quickly he shows his true colors as he points to the mat yelling at her “Lay down!” she refuses naturally still having some sort of pride as Jak shakes his head and whispers something under his breath before pulling her right into a Lariat spinning her around before hitting the mat as these fans boo as Hendricks shakes his head, pulling her up even though she’s dead weight before hoisting her up with ease into the air then dropping her head first onto the Turnbuckle with a BRAINBUSTAAAAAAAAAAHHHH! a Brainbuster on the Turnbuckle. Like a sack of potatoes she falls into the ring, completely out. Hendricks jumps down on top of her with the pin and even though she is completely unconscious he puts his feet on the ropes almost on instinct, 1…2…3! REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner…Jak Hendricks” Hendricks pulls away with a smirk hopping to his feet as James is checked on by the referee but wasting little time he asks for a microphone. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I told you, he’s about to get it going.” These fans try to drown him out with boos but as he stands there looking more then willing to wait, they end up dying down and giving him his chance to talk. JAK HENDRICKS: “I am Jak Hendricks, former and future Undisputed Contender!” Just like that he tosses the mic to the ground raising his arms into the air as these fans seem to shrug off his claims as Hendricks rolls out of the ring with his arms still raised as he heads to the back. WINNER: JAK HENDRICKS We cut backstage to the catering table where we see Daniel Starr, his North Beach title slung over his shoulder, screaming at the woman in charge. Starr turned to see a man at the other side of the catering table eating. He sighed and rolled his eyes. DANIEL STARR: “You’re supposed to ask my permission before you come and eat at the CHAMPION’S catering table. The table for the normal folk is over there, so go.” He turned around, it was Ace Baldwin. ACE BALDWIN: “Excuse me?” DANIEL STARR: “Champion’s don’t repeat themselves.” ACE BALDWIN: “But I’m hungry.” DANIEL STARR: “Well go starve somewhere else leach.” ACE BALDWIN: “Who’re you calling a leach pal?” DANIEL STARR: “You, for eating all my cream cheese danishes. Professionally. Like the men we are.” DANIEL STARR: “Or you could just leave.” ACE BALDWIN: “No, it isn’t that easy dudebro. Ok, how about this. We have a match next week...” DANIEL STARR: “No.” ACE BALDWIN: “And if I win...” DANIEL STARR: “No.” ACE BALDWIN: “I get a title shot.” DANIEL STARR: “No.” ACE BALDWIN: “Hmm... well... how about this...” DANIEL STARR: “NO!!! SHUT UP AND GET AWAY FROM MY FOOD!” ACE BALDWIN: “If I win, I gain permanent access to your catering supply.” Through the past exchange, Matt Kluge had been carefully sneaking behind Daniel Starr, reaching for the cheese blocks and ham that he loves so much. ACE BALDWIN: “Can you make that happen Kluge?” Starr turned around, and Kluge looked at him like a deer in headlights. He withdrew his hand and looked at Starr with a smile now, nodding. MATT KLUGE: “Yes on the catering bet... no on the title shot bet. I just gave that match to Vega at MOG.” Daniel Starr was in a rage right now, a vein was popping from his head. DANIEL STARR: “VEGA??? VEGA??? You SERIOUSLY let Vega have that title shot?” Kluge slowly walked off, Starr turned back to Ace. ACE BALDWIN: “You have a match up next.” Starr stormed off, leaving the camera on Ace as he smiled, eating some cupcakes. Alex Castellanos needs to be worrying about Alexis Terry and Moment Of Glory, not settling some score with Alioth Starre. I'm predicting the upset. Alex lost the Undisputed Championship fair and square. He has no business in the title picture." RILEY STEVENS: "She cheated..." Castellanos, the always highly competitive star, is all focus tonight. He readies himself in the ring waiting for his longtime rival Alioth Starre in a match that could headline any supershow in GIW. These two can’t stand each other and the fans are on there feet waiting for this highly anticipated matchup to take place. Alex’s music begins to fade away and the fans await Alioth. 'Grand Central' by Edward Shearmur cues up and the fans turn their attention to the entrance ramp. Nothing. Alex looks confused wondering where he could be. A large commotion ensues on the commentary tableside of the ring. Suddenly, Alioth slides into the ring from behind Alex and he has a crowbar in hand! He aims low and delivers a shot right to the ankle of Alex Castellanos. The crowd is pissed and they erupt into one large “Boo”, Alioth follows it up by stomping away on the ankle of a defenseless Castellanos. RILEY STEVENS: "What is the meaning of this?! Stop this ref!" OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Alex should have been paying attention.." The referee has yet to ring the bell and Alioth continues his assault with the referee doing anything he can to get Alioth to stop, even tugging on his arm. Alioth refuses to be stopped. He quickly locks in the Four Star Marry and Alex screams in pain. From the back strolls a casual Alexis Terry, the Undisputed Champion and an opponent of Alex’s at Moment of Glory clapping at the work being done by her “friend” Alioth Starre. She is still in her ring gear from earlier. The Undisputed Championship is around her waist perfectly as she walks around the ring looking at Alex still wrenching in pain. She grabs a steel chair from ringside and slides into the ring. Alioth lets go of the hold but the duo are not through just yet. Alexis quickly places the same ankle of Alex’s in-between the chair and a giant evil grin emerges on her face. She slowly climbs the turnbuckle as she looks down at the man she beat for the Undisputed Champion some weeks ago. RILEY STEVENS: "NO! DON'T DO IT ALEXIS! DON'T DO IT! NOT THIS WAY! The crowd lets out a loud “Ohhhh” as Alex flails around the mat in pain. Alexis’ bounces off the ropes and delivers a shining wizard for good measure but the damage was already done. She points at Alioth and demands he pins him. “RING THE BELL REF!” He doesn’t listen and refuses to ring the bell. ‘RING IT DAMNIT! I’M THE CHAMP!” Reluctantly, he does. She slides into the ring but Alexis and Alioth hightail it out of the ring and through the crowd. Madison quickly attends to Alex who is screaming “It’s Broken! It’s Broken!” as EMTs and other medical personale attend to him as Revolt slowly fades to black.
