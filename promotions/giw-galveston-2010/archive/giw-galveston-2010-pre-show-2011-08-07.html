Pre-show, 7 August 2011

7 August 2011

GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: MOG PRE-SHOW LIVE! From Moody Gardens – August 7th, 2011 As the Pre-Show gets underway the fans are starting to pile in and are ready for a great show. Already in the ring standing with a microphone is none other then Paul Marshall the man who last week made some big promises. PAUL MARSHALL: “Alright, so we are just a few minutes away from the biggest show of the year. The show that will electrify GIW fans of all ages: Moment of Glory. But last week on Extreme TV… I told everyone there was a new pre-show on the horizon. It will be called… Full Throttle!” Marshall points over to the ramp, a huge banner that says ‘Full Throttle’ to an instant ovation by the crowd. PAUL MARSHALL: “This is different than anything we have ever tried before. You see, you will sign up to compete on this show and we will schedule an independent worker from the area to go against you. You have five minutes to defeat them. If you cannot do it, you have to try again the week after. Everyone must compete here to earn a spot on Revolt! To add another twist, the person who performs the worst on Revolt will have to earn their way back up there. This way, we learn who can truly fight, we can scout young talent, and people on Revolt will always have to perform at their best. We debut next week, see you there!” There is an instant ovation from the crowd; we fade into a promo hyping Aspen Chaud vs. Alioth Starre later in the night. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = Heading to the back we see Richard Youngblood standing with Crysis who is prepping for his match up. RICHARD YOUNGBLOOD: “This guy thinks he can insult us! He thinks he can insult you? Come on now tonight you and I will show the world why he is nothing more then an overrated loser.” Richard pats him on the back as Crysis continues to limber up before the match as he looks at Youngblood and nods. RICHARD YOUNGBLOOD: “Let’s go.” Youngblood motions towards the door as the two head towards the ring as we take a break. [/color] QUOTE [font=cambria]With the Open Battle Royal participants pulled to the main show this fight between Crysis and John Matthews was the opener and it did not disappoint. With Richard Youngblood standing at ringside to watch the two do battle it would be a good ol’ fashion brawl to start the match. Both men wanting to prove themselves and both men have a little feud going on it led to some very stiff shots in this match. Crysis used his superior size advantage to over power Matthews through out the match but it was John who impressed the crowd with some of his maneuvers. To end the match it was Matthews who seemed in control until Matthews got momentarily distracted by Youngblood at ringside which led to a Chokeslam from Crysis that ended the match just that quickly. The fans were split on their reaction; most were cheering Crysis up until that point. Crysis and Youngblood went to the back happy while Matthews tried to plead his case to the referee as the first match came to a close. WINNER: CRYSIS When we head to the back this time we see Ninja Strike Force standing getting ready to go when in walks Blake Beckett making his first appearance tonight patting both on the back. BLAKE BECKETT: “I know that you two are leaving after the match, I just want to say we enjoyed having you.” Both men reach in giving him hand shakes as they accept his congratulations however the entrance of the Faces of Death ends this happy little moment. DEMON NIGHT: “You know this is a happy little moment for you two I am sure. Frankly we plan on your last night ending with you in the hospital. And then you Blake will have no choice but to give us our deserved shot!” Night and Azriel glare at Blake as they turn and head towards the door heading on out of here as the segment ends. QUOTE Many people in the crowd feared this would be a bad ending for Ninja Strike Force considering the Faces of Death had looked impressive thus far. However in their final match the Ninja Strike Force showed why they were fan favorites ever since their debut in the company. Combining an aerial assault with some comedy spots they were never not in control of this one. Finally at the end the two nailed Azriel off the ropes with a ‘Shell Shock’ a Double Superkick before hitting Night with the ‘Higher Heights’ a Double Moonsault from both as they picked up the win. After the match the Faces of Death went to the back angrily while the Ninja Strike Force were given a standing ovation for their contributions here in GIW and to the fans. WINNERS: NINJA STRIKE FORCE [Colby Hunter & Jamal Mitchell] This time heading to the interview area backstage we see that Damien Dunn has found a way to take the microphone to make his words felt. DAMIEN DUNN: “I find it disgusting that I am here wrestling on the Pre-Show against some no name…When I defeated the man in the Main Event! But you know what…I want this to be a message to all of you here tonight…” Dunn lowers the microphone for a second as he grins for a second, something on his mind. DAMIEN DUNN: “I assure you…you’ll be seeing a lot more of me tonight.” Dunn drops the microphone as a staff member quickly enters to pick it up as he heads to the ring. QUOTE While this was still the Pre-Show the final match had a big show type of feel as the fans were strongly against Damien Dunn and strongly for Trayton Deluca as the two entered the ring for their Pre-Show Main Event match. Right away one thing was clear in the strength department it was in Dunn’s favor by a long shot as he started the match completely dominant, treating Trayton like a rag doll. However to everyone’s surprise it was Trayton who battled back using some big offense seeming to be back in it. But in the end it was Damien’s power that was all the difference as he nailed him with a ‘Complete Devastation’ his version of the Pedigree to end the match as it was Dunn who got to celebrate before the intermission before the Main Card. WINNER: DAMIEN DUNN
