GIW — Card

Revolt, 7 March 2014

7 March 2014
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: REVOLT Live from Porretto Beach - March 7th, 2014 Two belts with prestigious histories, two belts that have of provided us with some of the greatest rivalries, confrontations, and matches of all time. Two belts that will finally come together to see us crown a singular champion, truly proving that the Undisputed Champion…Truly is Undisputed. A prospect whose rise could only be stopped by unfortunate circumstance returns to put the finishing touch on his rise to the top. A champion and legend, looking to build on her already illustrious career adding yet another major milestone to her career. The GIW North Beach Champion…The GIW Undisputed Champion…The Return. Two enter, one leaves as the Undisputed Champion of GIW. BOOM BOOM BOOM! The familiar start of a Revolt echoes through the crowd as what is a decent sized pyro goes off at the top of the ramp for the crowd in attendance tonight alerting them that the show is about to get underway as the camera comes into the familiar sites of Porretto Beach, the location the same but the set up a little different tonight the colors and set up updated a bit for the new year and the return of GIW. The camera pans around the crowd who have filed into their seats and are cheering at the top of their lungs, the sound of their applause not just a normal chorus of claps…Rather what seems like months of anticipation raining down on the ring tonight as the fans have brought their signs, have there expectations and now have their attention on the man in the ring none other then Ian Mendoza whom we know as the spokesman for Mr. B the man who made all this happen. IAN MENDOZA: “Welcome everyone to Revolt, streaming to you live on GalvestonWrestling.com. Tonight is a very special night, as thanks to Mr. B we are all here to celebrate the return of Galveston Island Wrestling!” Ian says this with excitement as the fans give him his cheap pop going crazy, many starting to chant “GIW” over and over again, as Ian steps back a bit lowering the microphone letting them have their moment before stepping back up to continue. IAN MENDOZA: “And it’s a special night tonight. Mr. B has booked some huge matches including Tony Miranda and Christopher Darren Erns in what should be a great kickoff to the show momentarily. The return of Madison Seton here tonight as well as the debut of several new stars here in GIW as well. But it all leads up to our huge Main Event, between Alexis Terry the GIW Undisputed Champion and the GIW North Beach Champion, Michael Lynch! To crown a unified Undisputed Champion. And I now have a statement from Mr. B that he’d like me to read…” Ian reaches into his pocket pulling out a sheet of paper but before he can uncrumple it, "Glory And Gore" by Lorde hits the fans momentarily waiting to see who it is but when they see it is the Undisputed Champion with new music they react the way they will every time it hits from here on out with a huge roar of cheers as the Undisputed Champion, Alexis Terry walks out from the back still in her regular attire for the night. RILEY STEVENS: “What a night, we have here Oliver. GIW is back and I couldn’t be happier and what a way to kick off the show with Alexis Terry the girl who when GIW last closed, ended up on top it only makes sense we see her out here first tonight and Oliver, I got to say I kind of missed you…” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Can’t say I am too happy to hear your voice again, I’d gotten used to the peace and quiet but I miss all these amazing matches so I’ll put up with you and, our champion…For now.” Her match won’t take place for a bit but she seems to have something on her mind as she heads towards the ring, highfiving a few fans along the way as in the ring Ian has his arms behind his back letting her come, as she walks around the ring to high five a few fans and also grab a microphone along the way as she heads into the ring now looking out at the fans with a smile, Ian waiting on her patiently but she isn’t in too big of a hurry as she walks past him over to the ropes standing up on top of them holding up her belt drawing another wave of cheers as she jumps down and heads across the ring stepping up onto the other turnbuckle and doing it yet again before stepping back down and letting out a big sigh of relief to be back in this ring as she shakes her head and holds the mic to her mouth. ALEXIS TERRY: “You can’t begin to know how amazing it feels to do all that again for you fans…” Alexis lowers the microphone for a second, walking around the ring looking out at them seeming too caught up in the moment to even think about Ian at this point as he continues to wait as the fans die down a bit allowing her to raise her microphone back to her mouth. ALEXIS TERRY: “As for you Ian, it’s nice to meet you. Welcome to GIW. But before we get to this little message from Mr. B, let me go ahead and get the elephant out of the room and ask you why exactly his identity is supposed to be a mystery? I mean…Mr…B? I’ve had my fair share of bad Bosses in my day but there is one that was selfish, egotistical, and didn’t care about my well being very much. And that Boss, was also one who would probably enjoy hiding under a fake name so that nobody can blame him for anything that goes wrong in GIW. Oh and…You know that Boss’s last name happened to start with a B as well.” Terry shrugs now as Ian stands by seeming to have prepared himself for all of this as he listens to what she has to say as Alexis looks to pry him for answers but only gets a simple nod. IAN MENDOZA: “I’m sure a lot of you have questions about the identity of Mr. B but I can assure you that it is of little importance because Mr. B is going to do what is best for these fans and this company and will be fair to all of his employees.” Alexis grins a bit, seeming to have him pegged as someone in particular but willing to play along as these fans seem to be none to pleased by the fact that Mr. B will remain a mystery as Terry looks to them shrugging and turning back to Ian raising the microphone. ALEXIS TERRY: “You know I my doubts--” Before Alexis can get through her statement, "Satori, Pt. 1" by Flower Travellin' Band hits as the other half of the Main Event doesn’t get quite as warm a reception as the fans start to boo as both Ian and Alexis look to the entrance ramp as it’s now the North Beach Champion, Michael Lynch that is entering down the ring though as he walks with his belt around his waist and a mic already equipped in hand he doesn’t bother waiting to talk, instead talking on his way to the ring. MICHAEL LYNCH: “Alexis, Alexis, Alexis. I really hope you are not starting to doubt this match tonight, because it doesn’t matter which Boss was in charge this was inevitable. I was going to get a shot at that belt eventually and it didn’t matter if it was you or someone else, they were just going to be the one that I beat to get it. So if I were you, I’d worry less about Mr. B and more about our match tonight.” By the time Lynch finishes he is now stepping through the ropes, forced to lower the mic though finished anyways as Ian does his best to not show any signs of being a bit upset about his start of the show being hijacked as Lynch gets into the ring while Terry raises up her microphone. ALEXIS TERRY: “Trust me Michael, I knew this match was coming and I’m not upset about getting the chance to beat you at all tonight. In fact whether this Mr. B is who I think it is, or it’s anyone else. They won’t stop me from going out to the ring and proving that I am still the top of the food chain around here. Some may have called me a gold digger in the past, and maybe they are right because I am going to take that gold of yours tonight.” The fans cheer her on as Michael starts to get a little closer, Terry doing the same the two coming face to face now in the ring as Ian can be seen motioning to the back for someone as Lynch raises his mic now up to his face, the two nearly face to face in the ring. MICHAEL LYNCH: “You don’t stand a chance.” Ian’s hand motions now seem to come to light as from the back Jamal Jackson as well as Damien Dunn head to the ring as Lynch turns to leave the ring but out of no where he unstraps his belt and uses it to take a swing at Terry, trying to get the upper hand before the match but having to unstrap it gives the quick on her feet Alexis time to duck under it and then rush forward with a big running elbow to his face, then another as finally he pushes her away as Dunn and Jackson the Security team here on Revolt slide into the ring stepping in between the two but by then Michael has already slipped out of the ring backing up the ramp as he stares in at Alexis. IAN MENDOZA: “Just because Mr. B is not here in person, does not mean that there will not be order. Anyone else that tries anything like that will be punished. Save it for the Main Event and lets get on with the show and remember he’s always watching…” Mendoza sets down the microphone seeming a little bit annoyed about the start of the show not going as planned as by now Lynch has already reached the back as Alexis exits the ring and high fives a few of the fans around the outside, before heading up the ramp as just then "Firestarter" by Prodigy hits as these fans wait and then go wild yet again as this time it’s Tony Miranda a legend and the second ever Grand Slam Champion as he makes his way out from the back. As he does though Alexis is just heading to the back, as the two stop for a moment Terry with a smile and Tony with his eyes on the belt on her shoulder. RILEY STEVENS: “An interesting kick off to the show but as Tony Miranda heads out for the first match, a friendly reminder from him to Alexis that no matter if it’s her or Lynch that wins the Main Event tonight, they will have a lot of people targeting them.” The two give almost a respectful eye to eye gaze though their history probably preventing much more as Terry heads through the ropes and Tony starts to make his way to the ring. QUOTE These fans are giving him a very excited reaction as he heads to the ring, throughout the history of GIW you’d be hard pressed to find someone more beloved by the fans for as long as he has been as he heads up the steps and into the ring stepping through the ropes as he heads across the ring to the top rope posing for a moment as his music fades and "Running Under" by King Black Acid is the next song to play as these fans react with mixed results, most cheering but perhaps the fact that he’s up against Tony Miranda, the reaction is a little lessened. None the less Christopher Darren Erns is out next, with his eyes on Tony these fans ready for the kick off of Revolt with this amazing match. RILEY STEVENS: “You got to be excited for this match here, if there was one way that you could start Revolt and guarantee fireworks it would be putting these two wrestlers into a match. It may not be the most technical match but they will brawl.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I love a good fight and I got to admit that this one is going to be a war.” The anticipation in the air, already there just for the show itself but as Erns heads to the ring even these fans seem to realize that they may be about to witness something special as CDE walks up the steps, his eyes on Tony for most the walk to the ring as Miranda’s do not stray as well as Erns steps through the ropes and doesn’t bother with anything else, he came for a fight and he’s ready for it as the referee steps between them and calls for the bell. DING DING DING! The bell alone gets it’s very own pop these fans just excited to see them go again as Erns and Miranda get ready to go, both seeming to respect each other enough not to jump right in instead sizing each other up for a moment before the bell is rung. The two start to circle around one another, knowing how much damage the other is capable of at a moment’s notice as they finally go in for a lock up, but quickly things go as they were planned as Tony even with their hands clinched throws a leg kick, CDE not to be out down throws one back as they exchange for a moment, each waiting to see who will break first. Finally the two release their hands but continue going back and forth with kicks, now hitting other various parts of each other’s bodies using their legs like most would their fists in this exchange until finally Tony seems to be getting the better of him, not letting him respond instead throwing one kick after another backing Erns with them, one after another. With Erns against the ropes Tony turns and bounces off the ropes then turns to go back at him but Christopher catches him coming throwing a running shoulder block his way, knocking Tony to his butt as these fans are cheering both of them on at this point as Miranda hops right back up. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “You knew this was going to be a fight.” Tony gets up acknowledging Erns good counter with a friendly nod as the two yet again get set up this time Tony throws up a big Spinning Roundhouse Kick trying to catch him on the jaw but Christopher ducks under it then leans in for a European Uppercut but Tony pushes his arm spinning him around as Tony grabs him around the waist for a German Suplex but Erns uses his strength to pull apart Miranda’s hands, then throwing a back elbow that sends Tony stumbling backwards as Erns rushes him for a big clothesline but Miranda ducks under it and shoulders him up and then brings him down right neck first onto the ropes. Erns falls to the ground, holding his neck as Tony now starts to get the advantage as CDE starts to get up in the corner as Tony rushes at him with a big Running Dropkick sending him back into the corner post, with a thud then the momentum sending him down to the mat as Tony gets up himself first yelling out at the fans to get up as Erns gets back up to his feet in the corner as Miranda goes full steam only for Erns to save himself spinning out of the corner sending Tony into the corner padding hard, holding his chest and spinning he walks right into CDE’s big Belly to Belly Suplex that sends him across the ring to a loud applause from the crowd. RILEY STEVENS: “You got to agree with whoever booked this match for the opener, already great.” Riley speaks what most have to be thinking as Tony gets back up to his feet seeming to shrug off the move for the first part as Erns lands a big European Uppercut as soon as he gets to his feet and Tony seems to be dazed a bit from the massive shot, losing his footing for a second as CDE takes the second to kick him in the stomach kneeling him over as CDE shows off his power grabbing him and holding him up into the air with a Stalling Vertical Suplex, holding him into the air drawing some applause from the crowd but the move proves to be costly as before he can go back with him Tony shifts the weight back so he drops back to his feet still in position for a Suplex but this time it’s his turn as he throws him over with a quick Snap Suplex that gets the people going as he gets up and again yells at them to get up as he motions for a European Uppercut recalling the one that rocked him and looking to pay it forward as CDE bounces to his feet holding his back a bit and that leaves him wide open for a big European Uppercut from Tony, one that rocks him but almost just naturally in his blood Christopher throws a European Uppercut right back that sends Tony backwards. RILEY STEVENS: “These two are exchanging big uppercuts now.” Neither wanting to be the one to lose the exchange, they each continue to go back and forth each shot seeming to slow them down even more the next time as they continue to punish each other’s chin as this time it’s Erns who seems to rock him yet again as he grabs him with a Muay Thai Clinch and throws a few big knees that send Tony stumbling into the corner as Erns now circles around and begins to rush him in the corner but Miranda always one to have a few tricks up his sleeves hits the ‘Action Star Kick’ bouncing up off the turnbuckles and landing a Spinning Roundhouse Kick that drops Erns, his momentum only making it worse as for the first time in the match Tony hops on top for a cover 1…2-Kick Out. Just a two despite the size of that kick as Miranda shakes his head a little unhappy that wasn’t enough but getting ready for the next move anyways as he yells at Erns to get up which he does slowly as Tony throws another big shot but Erns ducks it, and jumps up grabbing his neck and bringing him right into a Rear Naked Choke falling to the mat with him sinking it in. RILEY STEVENS: “He’s got that moved locked in, can he make Tony tap?” The thought alone seems foolish as right away Tony shows off his ground game, breaking it and rolling over so he is on top quickly transitioning into a submission of his own the Anaconda Vice as it’s now Erns turn to fight out of it but Christopher rolls over and out of it but does so right into a North South Choke that gets these fans going, loving the two battling it out with submissions as they start to clap not even seeming to think there is a chance Tony could tap out as this time Miranda breaks the hold and rolls over to his knees but Erns is quick to his feet catching Tony on his knees with a Running Knee! Tony falls to the mat out for a second as Erns makes the cover 1…2-Kick Out. Still not enough as Miranda kicks out and Erns gets back to his feet yelling at him to get up as Tony starts to do shaking off the cob-webs a bit as he turns right into a Russian Legsweep sending him right back down to the mat but followed up immediately with a Kip Up by CDE who is starting to get it going as he throws up his arms getting these fans into it, though they are already rocking as Tony gets up and CDE sets him right back down with a Spinebuster, then a cover 1…2-Kick Out. RILEY STEVENS: “What a move, Erns seems to be putting together a rally here can he finish it?” Calling for the end of it now Erns waits on Tony to get halfway up but grabs him and sets up for ‘The Package Piledriver’ getting it set up but Miranda shows his fighting spirit breaking the grip and spinning right into the ‘Lightning Buster’ a Crash Thunder Buster out of no where! The fans familiar with the move as Tony make the cover for it 1…2…Kick Out! Not quite as these fans are now standing on their feet, admiring the match and admiring the return of GIW. Tony gets up holding his back still from the Spinebuster he took as Tony waits on him to get up walking backwards and looking to go for the Running Leg Lariat a move that will certainly mean the end for Erns but as he is about to prep Erns gets up and rushes at him before he can get his leg up instead pushing him back against the ropes. Off the rebound he throws him into the air and hits ‘The Uppercut from Hell’ out of no where as Tony falls limp to the mat as the referee jumps down to call for the end of the match 1…2…Kick Out! These fans are in awe and CDE himself seems a bit shocked that wasn’t it as he rolls over and shakes his head trying not to think about what it’ll take to beat Tony. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “These are the type of guys you can’t hold down for three.” Knowing he can’t let Tony recuperate he goes to pick him up but jumping up with a big headbutt is Tony completely catching Erns off guard rocking him as he lands another, then another sending him backwards finally as he gets to his feet grabbing a hold of him and sending him for a throw with the Release Belly to Belly Suplex! Sending him flying across the ring to a huge roar from the crowd again as Tony gets up and sprints at Erns who makes the mistake of getting right back up as Miranda runs and hits a Running Leg Lariat! These fans go wild but much to Tony’s dismay, his momentum caused him to send Erns out of the ring to the outside out cold. These fans wonder what will happen next as Tony immediately slides out and grabs the deadweight that is Erns pushing him back into the ring, struggling cause he is dead weight. When he finally gets him in Tony steps up to get back and just as he gets back into the ring Erns grabs him and pulls him into a roll up pin 1…2…Tony now takes his turn rolling through on top 1…2…Erns rolls back on top and seems to really have him 1…2…3! That’s it, Tony kicks milliseconds too slow! REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner by way of pinfall…Christopher Darren Erns!” Miranda kicks out but the referee says it was too late as he calls for the bell as Tony gets up looking at him confused at first as Erns gets up seeming just as shocked that he got him as Miranda not one to overly complain nods his head seeming to understand as Erns gets up to his feet and helps Tony to his feet. RILEY STEVENS: “What a great back and forth battle, each landed some big shots but in the end Erns caught him out of no where.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “You knew neither would give up or go down, so it had to end like this.” Tony seems a bit upset with how it went down but he nods his head as Erns holds out his hand for a hand shake, both breathing heavy and bruised up from the several kicks to the legs but none the less Tony extends his hand shaking it as he walks past him heading to the back as Erns throws one hand up for the crowd then heads out as well looking to get some ice on his chin after a war to start the show. WINNER: CHRISTOPHER DARREN ERNS Taking us to the back now after the very first match the crowd still buzzing we get a shot of Amanda Rowland with her big smile as she stands in front of the desk of Ian Mendoza the two seeming to be caught mid-conversation. AMANDA ROWLAND: “I am just so thrilled to be back here in GIW I was so happy to hear that my contract got renewed further. I was a little shocked frankly, I mean Blake always said that I am not that smart but like I’ve gotten the best of him many times. That’s not to say I don’t like Blake or anything he’s a great guy and all…” Amanda seems to have fallen prey to a similar thought process as many, back tracking quickly on the Blake talk as Ian has a slight chuckle at her quick turn around as he shakes his head and continues. IAN MENDOZA: “You know these fans love you and I’m glad to have your help as Assistant GM. Mr. B picked you personally, he seems to like you.” She smiles but as she’s about to give her peppy response the sound of three men catch both their attention as Wake Walker followed by Butch and Michael storm into the room, the two flanking him with Wake not seeming exactly pleased about something, Ian seems to prepare himself. IAN MENDOZA: “How may I help yo--” Before he can even finish, his night of being interrupted continues as Walker doesn’t seem to have time nor the desire to listen to him at all. WAKE WALKER: “You know I don’t think you can. I came here to talk to the Boss and all I get is some guy who reads texts, papers, from the real man in charge and I don’t like not knowing who is controlling my job. So if you don’t mind, can you show me to where I’ll find Mr. B…Or Beckett whatever he calls himself now days.” Walker doesn’t say it without any hesitance in his voice, seeming to have it all figured out as Ian tries to keep calm about it all having to get used to this now as Butch and Michael stand behind him menacingly, scaring Amanda a bit. IAN MENDOZA: “As I said out in the ring, Mr. B prefers to keep his name and identity private. It’s not information that you’ll get from me and considering you have a match with Ryan Cavallari my focus would be on that if I was you.” Ian doesn’t back down or concede at all as Wake looks back at Michael and Butch then momentarily at Amanda with a grin then back at Ian. WAKE WALKER: “Fine then, just send him a message then. When I get rid of Ryan tonight, I’m next for the Undisputed title.” Wake takes liberties with touching a few things at Ian’s desk, something that seems to annoy Mendoza but with all three there not something he’s really going to argue with as Wake reaches to the last object on the end of the desk, a glass owl. He twists it in his hand for a moment a few thoughts rolling in his head as he sets it down and walks out of the room calling his two men with him as they leave the area as Amanda shakes her head. AMANDA ROWLAND: “I never liked him…” Rowland shakes her head incapable of seeming angry as Ian doesn’t really disagree as the two go back to talking as we now head back to the ring for the next contest of the night one between the new stars here in GIW. QUOTE Back from Ian’s desk we return to the ringside area where “Killing In The Name Of” by Rage Against The Machine is playing and Tommie Gunn is just finishing his entrance to the ring, getting a solid reaction from the fans upon his arrival they seem to like him from the start as already standing in the ring is both Melancholy and Selah all three of them making their debuts here tonight in the second match of the night riding the momentum of the Tony and CDE match, though with much to follow. RILEY STEVENS: “Here we go with a triple threat between three debuting stars; each of them with one goal to make a statement in this match tonight. What an opportunity they have.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “The male is going to win, let’s face it.” Gunn gets set up in the ring as Selah stands in her corner the fans seeming to have gained some resentment towards Melancholy as she continues to taunt them even as she is in the ring as the referee here tonight gets the match going by ringing the bell. DING DING DING! The three stars in the ring couldn’t be anymore different if they tried all three on opposite ends of the spectrum but that’s what is great about wrestling there are all sorts of characters. As we get it going, right off the bat Melancholy backs up holding her hands up as if to say they can go at it and rather then going after her the two do decide to go ahead and get things started. Gunn seems timid at first, against a girl like Selah who seems to have gained the crowd’s favor with her looks and nice demeanor. But as soon as she throws a big punch the lights go back on for Gunn, as he remembers what he’s there to do as he hoists her up over his shoulders with ease walking around with her before dropping her down into a gutbuster as she rolls out of the way as Tommie gets up and looks at Melancholy almost as if to say it’s your turn but Melancholy is having none of it as she rushes him throwing a big kick to the stomach that doubles him over as she wraps his arms going for a DDT but he spins out and lands a Enziguri that catches her clean, dropping her to a knee as he bounces off the ropes and finishes the job with a Running Leg Lariat that lands clean, dropping her to the mat as she now rolls out of the ring and Selah returns to her feet. RILEY STEVENS: “Tommie is making an early impression with a very good start.” With a lot of momentum already Tommie doesn’t plan to let up, waiting on Selah to go for the attack and when she does he hits a Spinning Wheel Kick that stops her in her tracks and with her momentarily stopped he grabs her up and hoists her into the air, her size allowing him to pick her up with ease and slam her down hard with a Brainbuster but as he does this move Melancholy is now up onto the top rope and when he turns to find her she jumps off with a Diving Crossbody that takes him to the mat for the first time as she goes for a pin 1…Kick Out. He pushes her off as she rushes to her feet, beating him up and allowing her to drop him back to a knee with a kick then a follow up DDT that plants him as it’s now Gunn’s turn to roll away as Selah now takes her chance rushing across the ring trying to catch Melancholy before she gets all the way to her feet but she ends up getting planted with a Spinebuster hard instead, taking a beating so far in this match as Melancholy goes for a cover 1…2…Gunn breaks up the cover at the last second to keep his hopes alive. Melancholy gets up first though, momentum on her side now as she throws kicks to both Selah and Gunn as they are trying to get up to keep them down. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “This girl is crazy, I can dig crazy.” As he says that she further confirms it by letting out a loud scream as she lands one last kick that puts Gunn down as she turns her attention over to Selah now, telling her it’s over as Tommie recovers on the other side of the ring. Selah having taken a beating is slow to get up, and makes the mistake of doing so using Melancholy’s legs and right away she kicks her down and then sets up the ‘Sinful Death’ a Double Underhook Facebuster but right before she can complete the move Gunn is back to his feet and rushes at her landing a big Running Enzuigiri over the Selah that lands flush on Melancholy’s chin, nearly sending her all the way out of the ring and stopping a move that would have surely led her to victory. RILEY STEVENS: “Gunn may have just turned the odds in his favor!” Now ready to get the finish himself, Gunn gets a hold of Selah and throws her with a Belly to Belly Suplex and as soon as she hits, he’s back on her hoisting her up and deciding it’s time for him to go for the kill as he lines up for the move he calls the ‘Gunn Downed’ a Vertebreaker. The move hits as Selah is out cold, down and out for the count as Gunn goes for the cover 1…2…Melancholy is up and pushes him off the pin to the outside landing hard, as she makes the cover now instead 1…2…3! Gunn hopped up quickly and was nearly back into the ring but was just a second too late as Melancholy literally steals a pin. REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner by way of pinfall…Melancholy!” The fans who have just gotten to know all three of these stars boo heavily as Tommie Gunn landed the big shot but Melancholy snuck out the pin to win the match, she hops up knowing that a win is a win but Tommie doesn’t seem pleased at all knowing he nearly had it but it slipped through his fingers. RILEY STEVENS: “Both Gunn and Melancholy showed great talent here tonight and it looked like it was Gunn’s match to win but Melancholy slipped in and got the victory.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “You only get one debut match, don’t matter if you got to cheat you got to win.” Melancholy yells at the referee to raise her hand which he does, though with little pleasure as Gunn rubs his head knowing he had it but still having a good showing in his debut and one he can surely build off while Melancholy will look to continue after her good start as we are greeted backstage by a man wearing a Megathreat shirt. WINNER: MELANCHOLY After that match a video begins to play highlighting the historic career of Megathreat in GIW as it shows him winning the Television Championship then retiring moments later. There is a short pause as if to wait for me but the video appears to end there as we head to the back to man walking towards the ring for a match wearing a Megathreat shirt when suddenly that man is blasted across the screen, and by the time the camera pans out we get a view of Daniel Starr mounted on top of a man that appears to be Megathreat, at least the grey hair is there as Starr pounds on him with punch after punch screaming at him. DANIEL STARR: “I’m not facing some washed up old man! I DESERVE BETTER!” Starr throws a few more big punches as he finally stops himself the man underneath him clearly done for as he angrily lays one more kick to his stomach before walking off in a fit as the camera zooms into the man laying on the ground revealing that it’s a much more elderly man, even older then Megathreat that Starr seems to have just attacked. Bleeding from the mouth and not looking too good, worse then that nobody seems t be around. Regardless we head back to the announce table with Oliver and Riley out in the ring both looking shocked. RILEY STEVENS: “Starr seems to have been a little upset with the match he got here tonight and took it out on a man whom he thought was Megathreat, but looking at him I’m not even sure if that was him.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I’m not a hundred percent sure but I think that Daniel may have just attacked the last person who bought a Megathreat shirt or the man himself, I really can’t tell…” RILEY STEVENS: “Regardless from what I have just been relayed that match has been cancelled as Starr seems to have made an exit and the backstage staff is scrambling to help Threat’ or whoever that man was…As we head towards our next match.” From there Riley looks to the camera as we first fade to a teaser for an upcoming match up as we get a short video hyping up the contest between Ryan Cavallari and Wake Walker that is about to happen shortly as we head back to the ring. The night has been full of twists and turns but we have all been leading up to this as the lights come back on and "Satori, Pt. 1" by Flower Travellin' Band hits as these fans voices are heard as everyone in the place starts to boo Michael Lynch as he walks out from the back and the first belt and first champion are brought into the equation, the GIW North Beach Champion. Walking to the ring full to the brim with confidence one can tell that despite the stakes, Michael has no doubt he’ll take this one. RILEY STEVENS: “I know whose side you are going to be on in this one Riley but I just want to take a moment to just soak in this moment, GIW is back and right off the bat and the two most prestigious belts are head to head in a Unification match in our Main Event, amazing.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “If you think I am picking against Terry, you’d be wrong. She’s going to-Haha I’m kidding Michael is going to tear her apart the gold is leaving with him tonight.” Lynch enters the ring and walks over to each side of it, displaying his belt one that has long been considered the second tier the belt that leads people to the Undisputed belt. Tonight he proves that despite which belt they hold, it’s about who is more talented. But as he lowers the belt "Glory And Gore" by Lorde hits and now with her theme in their heads these fans go crazy right off the bat, supporting the champion fully as the girl whose come so far in GIW now heads out from the back for just another huge match in her career. Alexis Terry makes her way out to the ring, taking the time to high five a few fans but the size of the match makes her rush it a bit to the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Alexis has overcome so much in her career; winning the title twice, defending the belt in Sole Survivor. If she wins this one it would just add another huge feat to her already long lists.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “But what about Lynch? The guy has a claim to never having been defeated, and if he wins again tonight? You have to wonder if anyone can stop him.” Terry takes a few steps up the steps as the stare down has now begun, she takes her eyes off Lynch only for a moment as she comes into the ring and Lynch wastes little time stepping forward and holding up the belt high into the air to sort of get into Terry’s head but she responds in kind holding up her belt as well with one hand, each of them saying a few words before Lynch lowers his title and shoves it into the referee’s hand as Terry does the same only much more careful as the referee now displays the belts to each side of the ring as finally we are ready to go for the Main Event as he puts both belts out of the ring and rings the bell. DING DING DING! The anticipation for this one fills the air as Lynch and Terry look at one another and start to circle around each other, both waiting for their spot as these fans are still cheering since the referee held up both belts, knowing how big of a deal this match really is. After a few moments it is Terry who makes the first move, moving in on him quickly only for Lynch to spin her around with a hold of her around her waist as he looks to go for a German Suplex but Alexis quickly throws an elbow that connects, then breaks off his hold by tearing one arm away then spinning around him going for a German Suplex, but when she tries to flip him over Michael overpowers her chuckling in the process as even though he isn’t by any means the biggest guy in the wrestling world he still has the size advantage easily over Terry. Lynch ducks under and hoists her up onto his shoulders, not seeming to really have much in mind rather just wanting to show her what he could do with her as he walks over to the corner apron, and dumping her over his shoulders right onto the ropes dangerously close to the corner pole as these fans groan a bit as she is draped over the top rope as Lynch walks around the ring with one arm in the air, bringing even more boos as he turns to head back to the corner as Terry has adjusted herself a bit and as he reaches for her she throws out a big elbow, then a follow up kick that sends him stumbling back a bit. RILEY STEVENS: “Dangerous spot early in the match!” Terry now jumps off the top rope, not something she is known for unless it is the MGME but in a match like this you throw caution to the wind and she does just that throwing a diving dropkick from the top rope sending Michael across the ring but still sending her to the mat hard as these fans go crazy for the move, cheering Terry all the way in this one as she gets up rather quickly. That move one you usually hit late in a match but with the stakes this high, she’s going to the big guns early as Alexis gets up and screams for the crowd to get up as Michael struggles up to his feet in the opposite corner doubled over a bit. Rushing him now Terry throws a running elbow to him in the corner, that sends him reeling out of the corner but still on his feet as Alexis bounces off the opposite ropes looking to send him out of the ring but as Terry goes for a clothesline Michael ducks down pulling the ropes down with him as Alexis is unable to stop her momentum in time and she goes over, ungracefully hitting the ground as these fans feeling pretty stressed with each move that doesn’t go her way, go silent for a moment as she struggles to her hands and knees on the outside as Lynch wipes a little blood from his lip that Alexis seems to have busted open with a stiff elbow. RILEY STEVENS: “In a match that will surely test both’s cardio they are really taking some big falls early on.” Alexis gets up using the barricade as the fans pat her back, Michael wastes little time sliding out of the ring grabbing her while she is trying to recover and slamming her head down against the barricade with her fans right there, forced to cringe as she hits. Lynch screams something at a fan member that is talking up then grabs Alexis by her tights and spins throwing her back into the ring seeming done with being out here by the crowd. He quickly slides back into the ring as Alexis is back to her feet by the time he gets in there, as she lands a kick to his leg then one to his stomach but Michael walks through it knowing that Terry is trying to buy some time, as she throws one more kick this time being caught by Michael against his body. With a smirk on his face Alexis backs up one leg bouncing around showing off her flexibility that most the roster would already be able to attest to anyways. Throwing the leg around her, he forces her to spin to keep her balance and when she does he grabs her around the waist and connects with a Bridging German Suplex, for the first pin of the match 1…Kick Out. Terry is a fighter; it’ll take a lot more then that. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “How’s Terry doing now Riley?” Knowing that Terry has taken a lot worse these fans weren’t even partially worried about that pin but they do seem a bit worried by the amount of momentum Lynch is starting to pick up as he gets to his feet as Alexis does the same, just much slower allowing Michael to hit the ropes and by the time she is fully up she is slammed back down by a Running Bulldog from behind that plants her. Michael really starting to feel it now as he gets up and tells the fans he’s going to finish her, already. Terry struggles back to her feet as he approaches her and hoists her up onto his shoulders for the ‘Test Of Faith’ a Burning Hammer but as he tries to throw her over Alexis adjusts and ends up landing on her feet much to the happiness of the crowd. Lynch himself seems caught off guard allowing her the chance to kick him in the gut and follow up right away with a DDT to buy herself some time to recover. She gets up hurting all over at this point, but finally stopping his momentum as he made the mistake of going for the finish a bit too early. Something Alexis has probably experienced far too much in her life as she gets up and waits for him to get up. RILEY STEVENS: “Can Terry start to turn things around now?” Michael struggles up to one knee, now holding his head in the process but as he goes to get up it’s a big too late as Terry goes for it and lands a Shining Wizard that does some more damage to Michael’s head as he falls to the mat and Alexis makes a cover 1…Kick Out. Still not close, as Terry gets up seeming to just be gauging how hurt he is, the one count all the answer she needs as she gets up and gets the fans going by calling for them to get clapping as they do as Lynch again struggles up to his feet and this time when he gets up she lands one big European Uppercut, that rocks him for a moment allowing her to hit the ropes behind him and then drop him with a Bulldog over her own. Getting up she screams out at the crowd getting them fired up as the place is rocking now as she stands facing the opposite direction of Michael as he lays on the mat, showing off her athleticism as she hits a Standing Moonsault. These fans cheer her as she makes another cover 1…2-Kick Out. The move looked pretty but wasn’t probably the Moonsault that she could put him away with as she gets up and continues to let out some energy by getting the crowd riled up as Michael again gets up as the momentum has certainly swung Terry’s way. RILEY STEVENS: “With all the momentum can she land a move ender?” Judging by the look in her eyes she seems to have exactly that planned as she looks out at the crowd then back at Michael as she waits for him to get up then rushes at him and lands a Superwoman Punch as soon as he gets up, but Lynch stays on his feet a testament to his toughness but he may regret not just going down as she lands a follow up Yakuza Kick that again rocks him as she runs off the ropes and after all of that, Michael explodes out and catches her with the nastiest of Lariat’s you’ll ever see sending Terry spinning before she hits as the fans go from roaring to dead silent in just a second as Lynch angrily pumps his arm one time angry at himself for taking such a beating for a bit there as Alexis takes a second to get up but does so right into a waiting Lynch who props her up and hits a Tornado DDT that plants her on the top of the head as she lays flat on the mat, looking out of it but Michael showing some respect knows he’ll need to do more then that as he gets up and heads to the top rope as these fans get up to their feet, waiting to see what is next as Alexis starts to stir as he gets ready and jumps off with a Senton Bomb, landing hard on Terry then rolling over for the cover 1…2…Kick Out! Not quite! RILEY STEVENS: “I thought that was going to be it!” Alexis rolls over holding her stomach as the fans are all a little confused as from the top of the ramp comes Madison Seton starting her approach to the ring. Seton stands at the half way point for a moment, perhaps just coming down for a closer look as Lynch sees her and shakes his head then grabs Terry up to her feet and looks into her eyes slapping her once just to be disrespectful before going for another slap but she catches his hand this time and drops right into the ‘Flawless Victory’ a Inverted Stomp Facebreaker! These fans go crazy as she catches him out of no where, as she struggles to her feet falling over to the corner which is convenient because that’s where her next match will be coming from as she gets prepped when Seton jumps up onto the side of the ring, right beside Alexis as Terry stops and sees her for the first time looking at her confused yelling at her “What are you doing out here?!” Seton doesn’t answer instead she steps down, off the ropes out of no where. RILEY STEVENS: “What in the world is Madison doing out here?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Don’t you get it, she’s cashing in on Lynch’s offer!” Terry seems confused but she none the less shrugs it off knowing she has no more time to waist as she jumps up for the ‘Most Glamorous Moonsault Ever’ but the time she took with Madison is just the time Lynch needed as he rolls out of the way at the last second. The move lands so hard it literally bounces Alexis off the mat right back to her feet with Lynch who spins and connects with a vicious move he calls ‘The Ram's Horn’ a Roaring Elbow that absolutely decimates Alexis as these fans hold their heads in shock as we get a shot from Seton who is expressionless as Lynch goes for a cover 1…2…KICK OUT! The fans go nuts, pushing each other and cheering as Terry managed to kick out of that huge shot as Michael gets up pretty slowly seeming to be starting to wonder if she is beatable as Seton is shown raising an eyebrow from the outside as Lynch gets up rubbing his head as Alexis some how finds a way to push herself off the mat her nose now bleeding as well. RILEY STEVENS: “What will it take to put her down?” Terry gets up her legs looking like spaghetti as Michael grabs her by the hair and screams at her to stop fighting as he spins and goes for a second ‘Ram’s Horn’ but this time Terry ducks it and sets up for the ‘Glam-Go-Round’ but she doesn’t have it in her to get him over as Lynch pushes her away and then this time actually does connect with a ‘Ram’s Horn’ that doesn’t appear to connect clean but rocks her, but she’s out on her feet as she starts to fall forward but Lynch hoists her up onto his shoulders and plants her with the ‘Test Of Faith’ a Burning Hammer and it’s over, the fans are standing but have gone silent as Michael makes the cover 1….2…3! That is it. REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner by way of pinfall and the new GIW Undisputed Champion…Michael Lynch!” The place is silent as Lynch gets to his knees holding up his hands in victory as the referee calls for the belts. Terry is out of it at this point, laying their like a sack of potatoes as Michael sits their breathing heavily as we get another shot of Madison on the outside as the referee now hands Lynch both the Undisputed and North Beach Championships, as he holds them both to his chest knowing that he just fought his heart out for them. RILEY STEVENS: “What a match, what a fight worthy of the high praise it received but one has to wonder why Seton was even out here. I think she was next in line, either way she didn’t need to help Lynch to do it.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Terry took her mind off the task at hand and that is what she gets for it, tough luck.” Alexis is still out on the mat as Lynch now gets up to his feet to celebrate his title win as he holds up both belts now on top of the GIW world as the referee helps Terry out of the ring, wrapping her arm over his shoulder as he looks to get her to the back. RILEY STEVENS: “But regardless Michael Lynch is our new champion; you have to respect him for that.” Lynch is again shown celebrating as the referee gets half way around the ring as another referee comes out to help him as a out of it Terry comes face to face with Seton who is still standing where she was before, when suddenly she reaches out grabbing Terry and spinning tossing her viciously into the steel steps! RILEY STEVENS: “WHAT THE HELL!?” These fans are in shock now as Seton stands over her as Michael has caught wind of it all, still holding his belts as something we thought we’d never see as Madison has just attacked a prone Alexis Terry as the referee’s again check on her as Seton rolls into the ring as Lynch takes a step back. RILEY STEVENS: “Madison Seton, the girl these fans love…Just attacked Terry and I know she want a shot at that belt but this is just sick.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “It’s called doing what it takes, you got to love it!” Lynch with his two belts on his shoulder, holds out his hand telling her “Don’t worry, you are next.” as Seton eyes the belts and nods her head looking to turn around but spins and hits the ‘Round-A-Bout’ a Roundhouse Kick again out of no where as these fans are stunned into silence as Seton has just laid out Lynch as well, both the belts he just won laying on the mat next to him as both the champions heading into the night are laid out to end it. RILEY STEVENS: “Nobody saw this coming…But on a night where GIW returned, and a new champion was crowned in Michael Lynch…It’s Madison Seton standing tall to end the night. Tune into the After Show for more…On this and next week’s show.” Riley can be heard throwing off his headset, seeming to be taking Seton’s actions pretty hard as do most of the fans in attendance as she finishes the show as we get a shot of Alexis out of it then Lynch done in the ring as well then the show ends with Madison Seton as we fade to black. WINNER: MICHAEL LYNCH (New GIW Undisputed Champion)
