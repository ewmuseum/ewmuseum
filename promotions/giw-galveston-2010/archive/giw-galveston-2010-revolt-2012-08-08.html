GIW — Card

Revolt, 8 August 2012

8 August 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: Wednesday Night Revolt LIVE FROM BENNO’S ON THE BEACH - AUGUST 8, 2012 BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! “No Feeling” by the Sex Pistols hits the PA to kick off the final edition of Revolt before the biggest show of the year, Moment of Glory; and the fans who were expecting a positive start, a celebration of sorts, were extremely disappointed to see “Enlightenment”, comprised of Alioth Starre and Alexis Terry start the show by walking down the ramp, signature smug looks on your faces. The drained voice of Riley Stevens came to life over the picture. RILEY STEVENS: “I’m surprised these two even have the audacity to come out here tonight after what they did last week. It has been confirmed folks, Alex Castellanos has a broken leg, so we can only assume he won’t be able to compete in the title match at Moment of Glory.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “How many times has Alexis broken Alex? First it was a power of the punch to the jaw, then a broken leg? I think Alex needs to hit up the calcium.” Alioth held the ropes open for Alexis, who climbed in very slowly. As he climbed in the ring behind her Alioth began to speak as his music lowered to a trickle. ALIOTH STARRE: “Well, good evening everyone. I hope our presence here tonight isn’t a downer for your big opening block, but I assure you there is no one in the back more deserving or capable than the champion and myself. “ The fans boo now, but in the meantime Alexis had obtained a microphone from the ring keeper. ALEXIS TERRY: “AS CHAMPION, of Galveston Island Wrestling, and member of the Enlightment, I feel as if it is my obligation to begin the show tonight and explain the actions I committed last week, because for whatever strange reason you all find them to be ‘gruesome’ and ‘unfair’. “You Screwed Alex” chants boom down from the stands. ALEXIS TERRY: “Oh please. I didn’t screw Alex Castellanos. I helped him and I helped you all just as much. Alex shouldn’t have been placed in that match in the first place, and can you imagine if he won? When he was champ for the month he was, it was the dark ages here. And can you imagine if he was champion, AND Blake Beckett was booking shows? You can’t be serious. Every single dud GIW has ever experienced came at the hands of Blake Beckett, even Kluge is a better choice. Now I won’t lose that match at Moment of Glory. I will be champion until the end of time, but I have to make it a point to protect this company that I LOVE oh so very much...” “Demon Eyes” the music of Blake Beckett hit the speakers, and Alioth and Alexis were startled to see him, even though everyone knew he would show up. The fans were exploding at the sight of the chairman who stood on the stage with a GIW microphone close. BLAKE BECKETT: “I’m the worst thing for GIW, Alexis? How about I enlighten you and your little friend on something right here and now, I don’t tolerate being undermined. I don’t tolerate people ruining my plans, and that is just what you did last week when you broke my guy’s leg right there on live television, and in front of everyone in the audience. No matter how much you try to mask it by saying it was for the best, or it was the only way to make sure everything stayed alright, you still screwed a man out of his title shot, and that he not only wanted, but deserved. Alex won’t be competing in that match, which means that I am left without representation in the contest which will determine the new general manager of Revolt. I don’t think there is a suitable replacement on that roster, so thanks to you, I’ll have to deal with either Kluge or Sinclair for a very, very long time.” He took a breath, the crowd was booing Alexis, but she and her partner didn’t seem to mind, they got what the wanted after all.” BLAKE BECKETT: “Now, don’t think that is the end of it. I have the upmost confidence that you will not defeat Madison Seton at Moment of Glory, and that will be the ultimate punishment for you, Alexis. And as for Alioth, I have the upmost confidence that you will lose to Ryan Cavallari, thus continuing your downward spiral to mediocrity ever since you lost to Kluge. So, I’ll punish you with this: your match with Cavallari at Moment of Glory III will be a number one contenders match for the Undisputed Championship. So when you lose, you’ll know you were just... that... close. Enjoy your night off Alexis.” His music started up again, Alexis and Alioth were actually very pleased at the “punishments” handed down by Blake Beckett, and the chairman exited the stage as we faded into a MOG promotional package with a smug look. It would be feast or famine or Enlightenment at Moment of Glory III, which would it be? QUOTE With the Human Disaster already in the ring, “Forever” began to play as Jak Hendricks made his way slowly down to the ring. He slid inside and found a corner opposite of his opponents, when Baltimore’s music began to play, he rolled his eyes, but the fans went nuts. The energetic young star made his way to the ring with a burst of speed sliding in and immediately offering his hand to Hendricks who ignored it and climbed outside the ropes. Baltimore shrugged it off, and the match began. DING! DING! DING! Jak immediately jumped down to the floor, Baltimore turned around and was frustrated at the lack of compliance from his partner, but there was really nothing he could do. Jody was right behind him, and hit a german suplex that shook the ring. Jak joined Riley and Oliver at the desk. RILEY STEVENS: “What... you have a match???” JAK HENDRICKS: “Well, yes, but no.” Baltimore was up and countered a suplex from Jody, he hit a German of his own, this time popping him back and hitting him quickly with “The Thriller Driller”, a BRAINBUSTER! 1...2....3! Baltimore won the match in seconds, literally! RILEY STEVENS: “That was one of the fastest wins in GIW history!” The ref raised his hand. He turned to Jak at the booth. WINNERS: JAK HENRDICKS & JEREMY BALTIMORE Jak Hendricks rose from the announcer’s booth clapping for Baltimore, which even though he was doing it in jest, it was still an impressive performance. He took a microphone and began to speak. JAK HENDRICKS: “That was a very good show Baltimore. Very impressive. I’m sorry I couldn’t be out there tonight with you. I, well, I could lie and say I’m sick or I’m hurt or something, but honestly I just didn’t want to tag team with you. You’re gross, you’re below me, and honestly I find it horrifying that GIW management thought it would be a good idea to team ME, with someone like you.” A ring keeper handed Baltimore a microphone. JEREMY BALTIMORE: “Yeah, you’re real cool man. I didn’t need you anyway, but if you think you’re so much better than me, what about we settle that at Moment of Glory?” Jak shook his head, the fans boo’ed. JAK HENDRICKS: “No, you don’t get the point obviously. I want nothing to do with you Jeremy. I don’t want my name to be associated with yours. You’re dead weight, and I am rocketing to the top.” “The Rat” by Dead Confederate hit the PA. The fans welcomed Matt Kluge to the ring with plenty of cheers. Jak sunk back into his chair. MATT KLUGE: “Ok, be quiet. You two need to get off television pronto, so I’ll make this quick. You two have a match at Moment of Glory. Woo. Kill each other.” His music picked back up, Jak was very disappointed but Baltimore was ready to go right now. We faded backstage to the playground behind Benno’s on the Beach. On the top of the slide on his belly was Cobrody Harper, in full snake mode, ready to slide down. Waiting for him at the bottom was Ace Baldwin, cheering for him. ACE BALDIWN: “C’mon Cobrody, you can do it. You can slide it isn’t that far.” The cobra writhed and slid a few feet, before being trapped in the small slide by his girth. Ace went to help him down. COBRODY HARPER: “HSSSSSSS!?!” Eventually he was let loose, and he slithered away into the darkness towards the trees. Coming into the shot was Tony Miranda with a paper in his hand, approaching the laughing Baldwin. TONY MIRANDA: “You’ve been having too much fun lately Ace.” Ace calms down and smiles. ACE BALDWIN: “A little bit of fun never hurt anyone.” Tony shrugged, examining the paper. TONY MIRANDA: “Well no, but when I’m looking for you, I expect you to be getting ready, not playing with Brody on the slide. Sign this.” Ace took the paper and pen, reading over it. ACE BALDWIN: “He lacked a father figure growing up. But yeah... I’ll sign it. Contract for the match.” Tony nodded. TONY MIRANDA: “You know, I’ve been here for a very long time. I’ve faced Chaud, Castellanos, Starre, Terry, Sparrow, Kluge, Scar and everyone else basically... but you were my best challenge. My favorite challenge. I can’t say I’m done forever after our match, if GIW ever needed me, I’d come back in heartbeat, but I need to rest.” They shook hands. ACE BALDWIN: “It’ll be one hell of a match.” Tony began to exit as Ace handed him back the contract. We cut to the ring. QUOTE Alioth Starre and Lokie Lindsay stand opposite each other in the center of the ring, as the ramous crowd is ready for this big time match up. Lokie, the current Legacy Champion, is ready as the bell sounds and he quickly makes the first move and locks up with Alioth, the GIW veteran. It’s not a surprise that the much stronger Lokie is able to back Alioth into the far corner. The referee begins the count and at four and a half Lokie breaks the tie up. Lokie backs up but Alioth, the snarling and ever arrogant performer, slaps the taste right out of the mouth of Lokie. The much larger champion looks shocked at the antics and doesn’t take to kindly to them. Alioth throws a right hook but Lokie catches it and reverses it into a military press slam! Quick pin by Lokie but only a two count is given in his favor. RILEY STEVENS: “The Legacy Champion was so close to victory there!” Lokie picks Alioth up and attempts a huge powerbomb but the quick-witted Alioth slips out of it. Lokie turns around and is met with a huge chop block that brings him down to a knee. Alioth uses the ropes for momentum and is able to connect with a running dropkick to the face of Lokie. Alioth quickly covers but Lokie is able with his long legs to get his foot on the bottom rope. Alioth quickly gets up and drops a knee to the face of Lokie. Using all his strength he pulls the larger man to the center of the ring and once again covers. 1….2…No! Lokie with the shoulder up and Alioth looks a little flustered. Alioth goes to lock the leg and attempt an ankle lock but Lokie gets him directly in the face from the mat. From out of nowhere, SIDEWALK SLAM! Lokie covers but another near fall and the match continues. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “He can’t keep Alioth down. This is exactly what is going to happen when he faces Aspen at Moment of Glory.” RILEY STEVENS: “Oh, we will see about that Oliver.” The duo is back to there feet and they are exchanging rights and lefts in the ring. Alioth ducks a huge clothesline attempt from Lokie and just as Lokie turns he is met with a huge superkick from Alioth. Lokie crashes to the mat. Alioth climbs the turnbuckles. He wastes no time as the smart veteran and comes off with a huge shooting star press! But wait, Lokie rolls out of the way! Alioth crashes to the mat like a bag of bricks. The referee starts a ten count at the feet of both men. 1…2…3…4…5…6…Lokie is up to one knee. He sees his fallen opponent and goes to cover. 1…2…Wait, Alioth rolls through and now has the tights of Lokie…1..2…3..Alioth wins! RILEY STEVENS: “Cheap win! Why can’t anyone just wrestle these days?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “A win is a win is a win is a win!” RILEY STEVENS: “Lokie dominated this whole match, but Alioth grabbed the tights and that was it. God this place is going to shit!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Oh quit complaining Riley. Get over it!” Alioth slipped out of the ring with a smug grin as Lokie pounded the mat, obviously upset. WINNER: ALIOTH STARRE We quickly cut to the backstage area, Aspen is standing with Ryan Cavallari. ASPEN CHAUD: “Go! Go get him this is you’re best chance. Just take out his legs for me.” Ryan hung his head, obviously frustrated. ASPEN CHAUD: “Lokie deserves it. You know he does Ryan.” Ryan continued to keep his head down. ASPEN CHAUD: “Do it for me.” She said the last line as pathetic as possible. She hung on Ryan like a monkey, and he embraced her back. RYAN CAVALLARI: “No. What if I were to get hurt? I have a number one contenders match at Moment of Glory. I don’t want to be sore going into that one Aspen. This is important to me.” She peeked her head up, he looked down to her. ASPEN CHAUD: “More important than... me?” He chuckled. She chuckled back. Everyone in the audience was on the verge of vomiting. RYAN CAVALLARI: “Well, I have priorities. Right now, being at the top of the company is number one. I have an opportunity to get there at Moment of Glory, and I’m not going to waste it.” He took off down the hallway for his match. Aspen stood there very still with an awful look on her face. We cut to an Alioth vs. Cavallari promo package. QUOTE Vega was already in the ring when “Notes From A Sunday Morning” by Unjust hit the speakers, and Ryan Cavallari made his way out from the back, focused on the ring and his opponent for tonight. REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “No entering the ring, from Los Angeles California... “The King of Everything... RYAN CAVALLARI!” The crowd booed, but it didn’t phase the focused wrestler. He slid into the ring and went to his corner, Vega was still warming up. RILEY STEVENS: “Both competitors will be looking to go into Moment of Glory with a win streak.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “For Vega... well... yeah that wont be happening.” Out from the crowd, before the bell has even been rang, came Daniel Starr, with his belt in his hand and the crowd going crazy. With one stiff blow, he slammed the belt into her back, sending her to the ground. RILEY STEVENS: “Its an AMBUSH!” The ref canceled the match and tried to push Starr off, but was promptly thrown from the ring. Daniel Starr continued the vicious assault on Vega, rights and lefts, Cavallari stood still in the corner of the ring, ignoring the fans that were begging him to step in and help. Starr went to the corner of the ring, and BOOM! A brogue kick that sent Vega down to the mat in a heap. WINNER: NO CONTEST OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Daniel Starr has eradicated the enemy!” RILEY STEVENS: “What a cowardly act!” Starr waved his belt in the air, and looked Cavallari out of the ring as he celebrated his triumph. The the crowd exploded, but it wasn’t for Daniel Starr. Zerotine Valentino was making her way to the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Its Zero! She’s back!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Get her to rehab!” RILEY STEVENS: “She is coming to protect her friend Vega! Thank God someone actually stepped up.” She slid in the ring and traded blows with the champion, before landing a hard kick to the crotch that sent him rolling out of the ring in pain and sending the crowd into a frenzy. She immediately went to help Vega, but the damage was done. Several medics ran down as Starr slinked to the back, in lots of pain but proud of what he did. We fade to a promo package for Starr. QUOTE Daniel Starr was already in the ring after his little incident, he seemed to still be kind of sore but was basically recovered. “Who Dat” by J-Cole hit the PA and the fans erupted in cheers and ovation for Ace Baldwin who made his way to the ring slapping hands, and even kissing a baby that a woman graciously held out in the first row. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “He isn’t focused. You can’t beat Starr without focus.” The competitive level of this match was higher than usual and brought the fans to their feet since both the North Beach Champion and the man who almost won Sole Survivor stood across from one another. The two started out the match by staring at one another, often times more than not psychology played a bigger part as the two circled the ring Ace and Daniel were still talking trash as the bell rang signifying the start of the contest. Daniel seemed to be telling Ace he wasn’t on his level nor was he ever going to become a champion pandering to this fans and its what sparked Ace to throw the first punch, showing the first signs of aggression early on. Daniel rocked back only to respond, the fans cheering when Ace hit Daniel and booing when Daniel hit Ace. This prompted for a high Missile Dropkick that sent Daniel cascading over the top rope without much effort or ingenuity into sending him sailing out of the ring, Ace was on fire as he jumped up screaming at the fans. “C’mon!” Ace sure was feeling himself so much so he looked as if he were going to dive out of the ring but Daniel classically waved him off and walked out of the way taking his time getting back into the ring. Daniel leaned into the corner the referee asking him if he was okay and he was barking at the referee that Ace just cheated the face of the company in the opening seconds. Ace was reluctantly backed up and that’s when Daniel shot off hitting the ropes, and with as much momentum and ingenuity as Ace had shown with the Dropkick he hit him with a Clothesline that sent Ace reeling. Quickly Daniel turned his attention to the ailing Ace as he was taunting him, luring him into a false sense that he was safe the moment he got back up and then darted once more into his general direction. Daniel went for his patient Running Bicycle Kick also known as STARRSTRUCK! But Ace showing athleticism ducked pulling the back of his tights unbeknownst to the referee for a pinfall…1…2! KICKOUT! Ace was thrown away from Daniel’s body as he got up complaining to the referee. The North Beach Champion sure was doing a lot of complaining for someone who had a championship match not even a week away, Daniel was wasn’t going to be caught off guard again and turned his attention grabbing the arm of Baldwin laying it flat and stomping upon it repeating said pattern over and over. When it looked like he was done, Starr would continue beating down Baldwin’s arm until he was forced to roll out of the ring himself, it was the only saving grace in this matchup for him. RILEY STEVENS: “This match is far too close to call. What a slobber knocker!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Oh now you’re the parrot announcer?” RILEY STEVENS: “BAH GAWD call the match!” Starr jumped through the ropes directly behind Ace who was crawling away smashing him over the back whilst running with some type of Axe-handle style club at the center of his shoulder blades, and the champion was in control for the moment. Ace tried fighting back but on instinct he swung wide right, it seemingly gave Starr the opportunity to duck with ease. Starr lowered his center of gravity as he tucked himself shoulder first into Baldwin’s midsection as he rammed him into the apron aside of the steel steps in the upper left corner of the ring trying to isolate him from his offense, didn’t take long for Starr to want to follow it up he pulled him from his stunned heap as he flung him directly at the barricade with an overhead Belly-to-Belly Suplex again Baldwin’s back was taking damage it wasn’t safe how Baldwin landed on top of the barricade in such a tree-of-woe like fashion as his legs stuck out at fans they didn’t want to help but where heckling Starr into not doing whatever he had planned. “Shut Up” Starr ordered pushing them aside before sending a barrage of at least ten to fifteen stomps to Ace’s midsection, stomping a mud hole and walking it dry apparently. Starr screamed aloud out of frustration even if he wasn’t losing this match, he just wasn’t going to lose to Ace Baldwin any time soon. His Manager looking on as she was just surveying the damage done, Ace crumpled against the mat and the floor on the outside as Ace wasn’t looking so hot. Even if his mind was on other things it would appear that Starr had this match under control. The Referee urged Starr to get Ace back into the ring, Starr only complied because he knew he didn’t want to win by count out or lose by disqualification. He was out to make a statement. Rolling Ace’s slumped body back into the ring he drug him into the center of it where he would hit two Scoop Slams back-to-back and head in for the lateral press…1…2!...KICKOUT! Almost three, Ace was able to get the shoulder up so quickly even though it didn’t appear that he had much strength left. Ace was trying to get back to a knee and Starr kicked him directly in the face. “I’m the face of GIW…not you!” Starr exclaimed in a very loud tone. Ariana Duroni clapped trying to get the fans behind Starr, but they weren’t buying his new found attitude. Ace slumped back on the mat but found offense by elbowing Starr in the gut as it seemed to have rocked him slightly. RILEY STEVENS: “The fans really aren’t buying that jezebel’s tactics.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “But LOOK at those puppies.” Ace shot off at the ropes aiming for a Shoulder block knowing if the first was successful, the second was sure to land and he was right Starr was whirled from his vertical base onto his ass but he didn’t pin instead he waited for Starr to scramble back to standing where he’d continue his surge in momentum. Starr wasn’t having that as he clubbed him over and over into the stomach backing him up into the corner, Ace was fired at with Shoulder Blocks to his mid section and by the time the second came Ace was able to catch himself as he was able to maneuver that into a Second Rope Tornado DDT. Blocked, Starr was able to catch himself before Baldwin completed the revolution and turned it into an almost Brainbuster-like Suplex that made people’s hair stand on end. Starr was flustered slightly running on adrenaline alone as he scrambled clutching the ropes for life, unsure of how damaged Ace might’ve been. Ariana came alive seeing the quick fox-like thinking Starr was able to get away with right there. Starr went up to the rope rope, but from out from nowhere fans and everybody saw Zero Valentino come from behind the curtains as Daniel Starr looked at the curtains slightly pissed off as she motioned towards his North Beach Championship. Zero slightly taunted Starr with a stripper-esque like move as he hopped down just staring at her “What are you doing here, aren’t you supposed to be in rehab or something!” Starr wasn’t happy but it gave Baldwin the opportunity he needed as he bounced Starr chest first off the ropes in a very unique pin that nobody saw coming…1…2…3! Baldwin had pinned the North Beach Champion which left Zero smirking at her handy work as she would depart back through the curtains. Ariana would climb in the ring trying to cool Starr off but he wasn’t having that seeing as he pushed her back slightly frustrated that Zero would ruin him putting Baldwin away riding the high wave of momentum he had amassed since winning the North Beach Championship. WINNER: ACE BALDWIN We cut backstage, Aspen is waiting in front of Lokie’s locker room. When he turns the corner and sees her, he exhales, tired, Aspen is the last person he wants to see. When he gets close enough to her, she slapped him hard across the face. He chuckled. ASPEN CHAUD: “I really hope Alioth humbled you out there.” Lokie nodded. LOKIE LINDSAY: “Like I told you last week, Aspen, nothing is going to stop me from retaining this title. I’m going to be the longest reigning champion in GIW history. I don’t care how hurt I am, or how tired I am, I’m not going to lose that match.” She tried to kiss him again, throwing herself at him, but he pushed her away, sending Aspen to the floor. From behind him came Ryan Cavallari, still in his ring gear from his match. He helped Aspen up and turned the Lokie, making eye contact with him, then walked away, taking Aspen with him. Lokie quietly followed them with his eyes, and then retreated back into his locker room. QUOTE First to light, Aspen Chaud. Though an introduction was given via announcer, none was needed for such a woman. She stepped to the ring, entering quickly. As she waited for her opponent, Aspen yawned, then checked the watch she found off a crew member. She wasn’t waiting for batman, just someone to come and get beaten. It seemed like hours, but mere minutes passed and Madison Seton was on her way. Cheered for and regarded as a favorite in the bout, she entered the ring with a cautious step and smile. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Her beauty... like a Goddess... ahh...” RILEY STEVENS: “Oh so you like Madison now?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I’m talking about Aspen.” Just as the ring bell tolled, Alioth and Alexis Terry walked out to the stage. The Undisputed Champion held her title high, Madison saw. They began a descent toward the ring, but Aspen lurched forward to her opponent. Madison was caught off guard, but she countered Aspen quickly. A DDT later and she was on the offensive with Aspen. Chaud tried to fight the flurry of blows, but Madison had her ground, and her opponent well covered. It was like a rabbit defending against a direwolf, granted, a very well armed rabbit. RILEY STEVENS: “Seton in control!” Madison tore her opponent from the canvas and followed the assault with a Russian legsweep. Aspen bolted to her feet and sent a kick Madison’s way. Seton dodged the bullet and joined Aspen at the ropes. Aspen sent her opponent across the ring with an Irish whip, Seton stopped at the center of the ring and ducked. Normally the maneuver someone in Aspen’s position would do. Aspen moved to kick her up, but Madison’s trap sprung loose. She grabbed Aspen’s foot, curved her leg around it and performed an enziguri by definition. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Her face! Her beautiful face!” Madison set herself up for a final kick to Aspen Chaud, a bout ender. She waited for Aspen to rise up from the canvas and when she had, Madison Seton drove her foot to the woman’s skull as hard as she could with a swift roundhouse kick. The shot drove Aspen to the canvas surface as flat as cloth. With a clean victory, Madison rose up to celebrate. RILEY STEVENS: “What a big victory for Seton!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Fluke, fluke, FLUKE!” RILEY STEVENS: “Aspen has been defeated!” This is about when Alioth and Alexis entered the ring at opposite sides so that Madison would be cornered. Aspen Chaud rolled herself out of the ring and rubbed at the back of her head. Alexis closed in first, to which Madison replied against this assault with a forearm to Terry’s nose and a kick to Alioth’s gut. Starre backed off cautiously, but Terry clenched her fists around the hair of the girl she would face at Moment of Glory. She pulled back and drove an elbow down into Madison’s chest. Madison, burned by the maneuver took a few hard stomps before she rolled out of the way. She managed to drag herself out of the ring, where Aspen was, and knocked her opponent to the matted floor. Alexis and Alioth followed in their pursuit of her, but as they ran the ropes and leapt over to tackle her with an airborne assault, Madison pulled tightly to the rim of the ring, dodging it entirely. RILEY STEVENS: “She just survived the ambush!” Starre and Terry crashed and rolled like burning cars to a wreck. Seton’s music starts up, and we fade to black with her climbing on the top turnbuckle, rising above her opponents. WINNER: MADISON SETON
