GIW — Card

Extreme TV, 28 July 2011

28 July 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: ETV LIVE! From Courville Stadium – July 28th 2011 Fans are filing into Courville Stadium by the dozen for the pre-Revolt festivities. The fans are gathered around on the grass in the center of the football field watching the sun go down and sucking in the smell of sulfur from the power plants that can mysteriously only be smelled at this spot. We cut over the Zachary Pagonis and Colt Cox who are sitting at a foldout wooden table at ringside. ZACHARY PAGONIS: “Welcome all to GIW Extreme TV! We are only one week away from the biggest show of the year, Moment of Glory II, and event that signifies one full year of Galveston Island Wrestling! Tonight on Revolt, you will see matches finalized and blood boil, it is going to be a great show!” COLT COX: “I agree Zach, but first we have a special announcement from Paul Marshall who was once a commentator on Revolt and is currently the head of the television and marketing department for GIW. Lets see what the GIW Legend has to say.” The camera cuts over to Paul Marshall in the ring with the fans chanting his names - they haven’t forgotten his contributions to the company early on. PAUL MARSHALL: “Ladies and gents here watching us in the sulfur pit, and the thousands watching LIVE on GIW.com… I am here with news that will shake the very foundation of GIW, especially that of Extreme TV. You see, Revolt has been picked up to broadcast live on ION affiliates in Dallas, Texas - San Antonio, Texas - New Orleans, Louisiana - Cedar Rapids, Iowa - Fort Pierce, Florida - San Francisco, California - Santa Barbara, California - and last but not least… Bangor, Maine! And ETV will follow Revolt and remain the pre-show! ION Television has been experiencing some financial difficulties as of late, but so has GIW. They are having trouble paying for all of the syndicated shows they run, and we are having trouble paying for elevated production costs and wrestler contracts when our main source of income is through our television deal and sponsors. Since GIW already gets better ratings than most of the other shows, and it costs less to produce, we are moving on up to states all over the US!” The crowd begins to roar, but Marshall quickly quiets them down. PAUL MARSHALL: “But, some of the more conservative markets we are expanding to feel that the name “Extreme TV” might draw away viewers. I told them that ETV was a staple of the GIW brand… but money rules this world. So I am saddened to say that the last ETV will take place before Moment of Glory II. But it will replaced with a totally new concept that will change GIW! But enough of me talking… enjoy the show!” QUOTE This match featured three local Galveston Wrestlers: TJ Frost, Mike Optical, and Danny Matthews - but the real headline was the return of Damien Dunn who left a month ago due to an injury. Dunn dominated the match, eliminating Matthews in a matter of seconds. Matthews was dissapointed that he didn't last longer, but he probably should get used to it. Optical and Frost worked together to try to take down Dunn, but experience proved key, Dunn kicked Optical off the top rope and speared Frost off of the apron. Dunn recieved a massive amount of boo's post match. WINNER: DAMIEN DUNN We return from commercial break to see Richard Youngblood in the ring with his four “Young Picks” and an instant ovation from the crowd. He begins to speak. RICHARD YOUNGBLOOD: “Ladies and gentlemen, I hope you are enjoying the show so far. Coming up next we have a continuation of my ‘Young Picks’ series - a match between Shock Collar and Antonio Nappa! These two men could easily be the future GIW Undisputed Champion, so without further a due…” Youngblood is cut off by "Cult of Personality" by Living Colour, John Matthew’s theme song. He enters onto the ramp to a massive amount of boos - they don’t seem to bother him at all. He begins to speak while walking down the isle. JOHN MATTHEWS: “In case you didn't know me, Richard, my name is John Matthews. I am Satans Gift to Galveston Island Wrestling. I was backstage but all I could hear was your voice ripping at my ears; you skipped me Richard. You said I was to demented. You didn't think I had what it takes. I could defeat any of your little shrimp army with a snap of my fingers, but you didn't want me.” Youngblood laughs and pats Nappa on the shoulder to calm him down before getting into John’s face. RICHARD YOUNGBLOOD: “Look, no one knows who you are. I pulled these guys off the street and they already have a more marketable name than you. I’ll tell you what, if you honestly think you are better than any of these four men, accept the challenge I am about to offer you. Since you aren’t booked for Moment of Glory, why don’t we headline ETV beforehand? One of my guys that I will choose later on in the night, versus you. One on one.” Matthews nods his head, then turns his attention to the four men standing behind Youngblood. We walks over to each of them, examining them. JOHN MATTHEWS: “You know I was trained by UCW, right? You know that I am world class wrestler, right? You know that I am the perfect combination of speed, strength, and skill… right? These guys are straight out of the backyard leagues and you want to feed them to me… no problem… I accept.” Out of nowhere, Crysis nails Matthews with a bicycle kick that he calls the “Downgrade”. Nappa pulls him up and hits him with his version of the dominator - the “Seeing Stars”. After that one, Matthews rolls out of the ring and leans against the retaining wall as we fade to commercial. QUOTE Nappa and Collar started off very even, but Nappa quickly took the upperhand. Nappa used his superior size to keep Collar grounded the entire time before finishing him with a "Seeing Stars". Youngblood raised Nappa's hand after the match, only to have John Matthews do a run and and witness him take Nappa out. Matthews looked on at the decimation with a smile. WINNER: ANTONIO NAPPA After the ‘Young Picks’ match has wrapped up, the camera cuts backstage to the GIW interview lounge where Nick Chaos is standing alone. NICK CHAOS: “Ladies and gentlemen, feast your eyes on one of the newest GIW superstars, and the best looking too. My name is Nick Chaos, I’m here all the way from St. Louis, Missouri to prove a point- I am the best wrestler in the world. After I am done here my name will be known throughout the world. In Latvia, in Greece, in Singapore, in Brazil… I will be a legend. Tonight, I headline ETV and even though that isn’t much of an accomplishment, I will feast on any opportunity that I get. I am like a scavenger, I don’t care what anyone else does I am going to top you and do everything you do better. This is my company now…” A slow clap can be head off camera, Nick turns to see who it is… when he finally finds the sound he clenches his jaws and fists. Seth Seeker enters the shot. SETH SEEKER: “You talk a big game kid. That’s fine. I like to let people do whatever the hell they want to do, as long as it doesn’t bother me. But this does bother me. Your eating my interview time. So scram you little prick.” Chaos stands his ground, clenching his jaw too. He turns away, as if to break the tension, but then tries a right hook on Seeker, but he sees it coming. Seeker catches the arm and tosses him against the wall, he presses him against the concrete wall by applying pressure to the jaw. SETH SEEKER: “I understand you are new here, but there is a certain pecking order it would be best for you not to mess with. You’re lucky I’m in a good mood tonight, or else you wouldn’t be able to fight in a few minutes.” Seeker lets Chaos loose, then walks off camera. We stay on Chaos for a minute holding his jaw before we cut to commercial break. QUOTE This match went bad from the start for David Rackham who was doubleteamed by DeLuca and Chaos from the get go. DeLuca used an intertesting technique to keep Chaos off balance the entire match, until Chaos landed a hard left hook that had DeLuca reeling on the outside. Rackham and Chaos went back and forth for a while before fighting off DeLuca together for a final time. The match ended quickly when Chaos landed the Gutwrench Powerbomb he calls the Chaotic Bomb on Rackham for the pinfall victory. WINNER: NICK CHAOS
