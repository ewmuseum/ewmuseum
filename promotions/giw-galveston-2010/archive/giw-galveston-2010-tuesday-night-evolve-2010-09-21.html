GIW — Card

Tuesday Night Evolve, 21 September 2010

21 September 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Evolution Pro Wrestling Presents: Tuesday Night EVOLVE! Live! From Carlos and Charlie’s in Austin, Texas! BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! It’s the third edition of Evolve and the fans are pumped up for another edition of the show as we come into the arena and head straight to the announce table as the crowd roars. AUSTIN ST. AUDE: “WELCOME everybody to Evolve! Tonight we have a show for you, that’s for sure. It marks a turning point for Evolve as we continue the battle with that other lesser company.” COLT COX: “Your right about them being the weaker show. I mean there guys are over here trying to exposure.” AUSTIN ST. AUDE: “Exactly. I mean tonight we start with a Mentor versus Trainee Six Pack Challenge. It’ll be Trainee’s; Jack Prowler, Jack Johnson, and Paul Defias with two of their Mentors, Cody X and Mike Pace in the match alongside Matt Kluge. If a trainee found a way to win that match they’d surely deserve it.” COLT COX: “Then we have three men; Marcus Hister, Bret Battery, and Chad Munroe going head to head in a match to become the Number One Contender for the Evolve title. Who will be the next in line?” AUSTIN ST. AUDE: “Then THE main event, a match up between our ‘Evolve Champion’ Chris Armstrong versus Joshua Acquin! We can finally show that it is us who is the dominant brand. Now it appears Joshua Acquin is showing up, and we have the camera on him.” The camera cuts to the backstage area where Joshua Acquin walks through the arenas doors with a big smile on his face as he stares around. Suddenly out of no where comes Bret Battery! He runs at him going to club him but Acquin ducks it and throws him hard into a big set of chairs. Next out of the woodworks if Marcus Hister who grabs at him from behind only for Josh to elbow him away before tossing him into the catering table. Last but not least Chad Munroe attacks only to receive a huge Knee before turning it right into a ‘Face Breaker’. Munroe is out cold as Bret gets back up only to be turned into another ‘Face Breaker’. And then it’s the last guy can this happen? Boom a third ‘Face Breaker’ to Hister. The fans don’t know what to think as Joshua stares down at the three before grabbing his bag up and walking away as the segment ends. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = It’s time for the first contest of the night and already packed into the ring is six men. Right away we come into it where Paul Defias standing next to Cody X both in ring gear as they talk over the match. Then we head to the next star Jack Johnson standing next to Mike Pace who are not communicating at all, they’ve had troubles together in the past. Then Matt Kluge in his corner alone for whatever reason his trainee isn’t here. And then last Jack Prowler in his corner with his mentor Celina standing over the ropes talking with him in her heels and black dress not ready for a match. COLT COX: “Looks like the six men are in order. We have all of them set up and it seems we may have a few factions forming with the mentors and there students working together perhaps?” AUSTIN ST. AUDE: “Celina is out here, shut up.” COLT COX: “Thank God you’re here. Regardless we have two teams of them together then Prowler has his mentor Celina at ringside and lastly Matt Kluge whose student Chad Munroe I can only assume is licking his wounds backstage.” Celina steps down to the outside of the ring while Cody X breaks apart from Paul Defias clearly the two won’t work together, they’ll fight alone. Pace and Johnson however with little communication seem to be willing to work together. The referee steps in between them all, and then calls for the bell. DING DING DING! The match gets underway and the six men all stare each other down. Cody X motions for Defias to go after Prowler which he does which sparks the rest of the fight. Johnson and Pace working together run across the ring and corner Matt Kluge. Cody X watching his trainee go to work sees this and runs over to make the save. The crowd is pumped as so much can go on in this one. Prowler has gotten down Defias and is working him over in the corner much to the cheers of Celina on the outside. Prowler is forced away by the referee to allow Defias to get to his feet. Before Paul can get fully up Jack runs forward and dropkicks his legs. Cody X on the other side has pulled away Pace then crushes him with a big punch. The fans are a bit behind a few people here at this point. Johnson has no idea that his Mentor is getting attacked and continue to connect with big boots to Kluge near the corner. Jack Prowler now getting confident having taken Paul Defias down calls for him to get up. When he does he Defias walks right into a big Enziguri from Prowler. Right away he goes for the pin 1…2…Cody X pushes Pace back right into Prowler that breaks up the pin attempt. Defias slowly rolls out of the ring in pain. Both men get to there feet and Cody X runs at him jumping up for a big Falcon Punch but Jack ducks it and Mike Pace gets the grunt of the blow. Pace falls out to the outside and as Cody rolls back Prowler rolls him up 1…2…Kick Out! The fans cheer the kick out as Celina seems a bit angry with the count. On the other side Kluge has fought out of the corner kicking Johnson away before running at him. Johnson ducks and throws Kluge right out of the ring. Finally it’s Jack versus Jack as the two start to fight it out exchanging punches. Suddenly both of them not expecting it are caught with a dropkick with each one of Cody’s legs sending them out of the ring. Cody X is the last man in the ring as he gets to his feet after the dropkick and looks to the outside. He grins and heads to the top rope as the other five get to there feet. Nearly ready to jump off Celina pulls Prowler out of the way as Matt Kluge also slyly jumps out of the way as Cody X comes down right on Jack Johnson, Paul Defias, and Mike Pace! The crowd goes nuts for what was a pretty insane move. COLT COX: “What a move by Cody X, he may have taken all three of those men out of it.” Celina checks on Prowler to see if he’s ok but soon it’s Matt Kluge who walks over catching Prowler off guard with a big dropkick. The fans clap as Kluge gets to his feet backing Celina up, with a big smirk on his face as he stares at her being his usual pervy self. Jack however gets up little to Kluge’s realization. He grabs him from behind and whips him right into the pole crushing Kluge’s shoulder hard into it. Prowler looks over at Celina who pats his back, happy for the help as she tells him to get back in the ring. As he does almost exactly at the same time Cody X returns to the ring as well. Only Cody gets in first and he runs across the ring and dropkicks Prowler right out of the ring. Celina runs over to quickly check on him as both Pace and Johnson return to the ring at the same time. Cody runs at them but he’s caught and whipped off the ropes. On the return he receives a big time double clothesline from both of them. Pace quickly commands Johnson to get him to his feet. When he does he calls for the end of the match but Cody X reverses it and crushes him with a Spike DDT. When he hops up however he walks right into a ‘Cashbomb’ from Jack Johnson laying him out cold. Johnson hooks the leg 1…2…Defias just kicks him off. Johnson gets up to his feet angry that he didn’t get it there instead calling for a ‘Cashbomb’ on Defias. He’s about got it when Kluge returns to the ring and tosses him out of the ring. Out of no where Prowler returns to the ring and hits a Running Bulldog on Kluge from behind. Defias gets to his feet and Prowler kicks him in the stomach and then sets him up on the Top Rope for an ‘On The Prowl’ a Top Rope DDT! He falls on top with the cover as Johnson has to go around Celina to try to get back in the ring but is to late 1…2…3! AUSTIN ST. AUDE: “Prowler with a huge win!” COLT COX: “After going on a losing streak he said it himself the past is the past and perhaps he’s on the path to success. One can’t say Jack Johnson didn’t do well here tonight however and if anyone is in trouble it’s Paul Defias he looked awful.” Celina slides into the ring and jumps up hugging Prowler with the win, as he holds her up then letting her down as she raises his arm. The fans give a mixed reaction as Jack Johnson slams his hands against the turnbuckle. Before leaving his mentor high and dry not helping him up. Cody X however comes over and holds Defias up, he’s out cold. Kluge gets up shrugging as we return to Prowler who got a huge win tonight. WINNER: JACK PROWLER = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = The camera cuts back to the backstage area where Jack Johnson, Jack Prowler, Chad Munroe, Bret Battery, Paul Defias, and Marcus Hister are lined up some in pain while others seem fine. Soon however Sinclair appears in the screen in front of them all like a drill sergeant. DAVID SINCLAIR: “Some of you need to count your blessings. I’m not firing you right here and right now. But let me start by saying this Jack Johnson and Jack Prowler go ahead and leave you two are still in my good graces.” The two smirk and head on there way out of the scene as the rest of the men seem worried as David’s wrath is about to be felt. DAVID SINCLAIR: “Now Paul Defias I expected more from you after last week, but at the end of the day you are still the same guy. Bret Battery it’s clear you’re a one hit wonder and you are clearly our weakest link. Chad Munroe perhaps I made a mistake letting you come to my show. And Marcus Hister you lose to a girl and now this? Let’s put it this way your contenders match is GONE!” The three man all seem furious Battery breathing heavily out his nose while Munroe throws out his arms and Hister glares at Sinclair before he continues. DAVID SINCLAIR: “Oh and one more thing, none of you are representing us at ‘Unscripted’ unless you impress the hell out of me next week. But otherwise you can all expect the worst because this is about future talent, not embarrassing David Sinclair. So all of you get out of this arena before I change my mind on keeping you.” All four men stare at him before walking out having been put in there place by there leader as we now know that none of these three will be representing EPW in its ‘Unscripted’ matches unless they win next week. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = "Nightmare" by A7X begins to play. Joshua Acquin begins to walk down the ramp with a rather cocky smile on his face. He makes it down the ramp and enters the ring. He gets on top of one of the turnbuckles and begins to pose. However, he is quickly interrupted by the song, “Turn Up The Trouble” - Airbourne. Chris Armstrong quickly walks down the ramp and then looks at Joshua Acquin in the eye. Both men stare down before the bell sounds. AUSTIN ST. AUDE: “Chris has a lot to prove here as champion.” COLT COX: “ I think he‘s already proven that he is the best man on Evolve.” AUSTIN ST. AUDE: “Well some may say that Prowler is stronger but we’ll have to see. If Chris is truly the champion, he’ll be able to defeat Acquin. Joshua Acquin tries to get into a test of strength but he tricks Chris Armstrong and kicks him in the gut and follows it up with a punch to the face. Joshua Acquin slowly and methodically throws Chris to the ropes and when he bounces off, he hit’s a power slam and then an elbow. Joshua goes for the cover. 1...2... Kick out! Jsohau Acquin starts hitting some elbows on the chest of Armstrong and then follows it up with a triangle choke with his legs. Chris Armstrong counters the move by turning it into a pin where Joshua Acquin’s shoulders are both on the mat. 1...2.. Kick out! Joshua Acquin keeps the hold but Chris Armstrong begins to fight out of it. Once he gets out, he bounces off the ropes and tries to hit a clothesline but Joshua Acquin quickly counters with a DDT. Cover. 1...2... Kick out! COLT COX: “Joshua Acquin is clearly dominating this match and is truly holding a clinic.” AUSTIN ST. AUDE: “You‘re almost right. Chris will bounce back, I‘ll bet on it.” Joshua Acquin starts stomping on Chris Armstrong and starts yelling. “This is your champion?” Joshua begins to yell. He looks around and is booed by the crowd. Chris Armstrong takes advantage of the fact that Joshua is distracted and kicks him in the gut then in the ankle. Joshua Acquin stumbles back. Chris Armstrong gets up and starts hitting some punches but Joshua Acquin quickly grabs Chris and hit’s a backbreaker. Acquin for the cover again. 1...2... Kick out! Joshua Acquin then gets a really scary look in his eye and then starts stalking Chris. Chris gets up. Joshua goes for a high impact move but Chris pushes Joshua away. Joshua runs at Chris only to get hit by a big boot. Chris quickly gets to work and quickly picks up Joshua Acquin and then hit’s a German Suplex. Chris quickly picks up Joshua again and hit’s a roundhouse kick! Joshua is KO’d and is standing somehow. Chris picks up Joshua and hit’s a back body drop. Chris keeps up with the high impact moves until finally, he hits Money Maker from the top rope after setting up Joshua. Chris looks like he’s stepped up his game and has made easy work of Acquin! 1...2...3! It’s over. COLT COX: “Wow, very impressive! It‘s as if something woke up Chris Armstrong half way through the march and then suddenly, he made short work of Joshua Acquin! This kid is really a big deal. AUSTIN ST. AUDE: “Lance Hollywood’s in the back and probably isn‘t impressed. Is he ever?” Chris Armstrong grabs his title and begins to pick up his title belt in the air. The crowd generally approves of him. Suddenly, however… Lance Hollywood comes from the back and hits Chris with a steel pole ! Hollywood then picks up Chris Armstrong and hit’s a crisp spine buster, laying out Chris Armstrong on the floor. Lance Hollywood looks like he’s in some crazed state. He starts shouting, “You think you’re the best? YOU’RE NOTHING!” and then leaves. The segment ends with Chris on the floor, holding his head and look at Lance as Lance exit’s the arena. WINNER: CHRIS ARMSTRONG = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = The crowd thinks the show is over as Joshua Acquin has recovered from the loss and with the loss heads up the ramp. He shakes his head knowing he lost as the rest of the wrestlers have gone. Soon however David Sinclair walks onto the stage with a microphone of his own. DAVID SINCLAIR: “Now, now, now. I guess EPW isn’t so bad after all, right? Well Joshua Acquin as you may have seen earlier not all of my stars are in my good graces. But you can book Jack Johnson, Jack Prowler, for the Six Man Tag Match. And to my two men if you win, you’ll both be given a title shot. And now you mind telling this to that short little girl, Aspen.” Joshua is breathing heavy after the loss barely able to stand as Sinclair talks down at him as he continues. DAVID SINCLAIR: “At ‘Unscripted’ she wanted to face our best. Thus it’ll be her versus Chris Armstrong, NO DISQUALIFICATIONS. And though some of my men let me down I know that these three will not. Because at ‘Unscripted’ we prove once and for all that we are the dominant COMPANY!” Sinclair smirks dropping down the microphone to the ground as we cut to Joshua Acquin barely able to stand, will this be the site at ‘Unscripted’ with Sinclair standing as GIW crumbles?
