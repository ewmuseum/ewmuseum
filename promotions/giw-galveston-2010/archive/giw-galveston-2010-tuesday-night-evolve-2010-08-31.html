GIW — Card

Tuesday Night Evolve, 31 August 2010

31 August 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Evolution Pro Wrestling Presents: Tuesday Night EVOLVE! Live! From Castaways Bar and Grill in Port Aransas, Texas! BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! The debut edition of Tuesday Night Evolve is here, and the crowd of over one hundred people in Port Aransas, Texas is letting the world know it. Everyone is piled outside of Castaways Bar and Grill, and all of them are ready to witness the newest wrestling promotion in Texas show themselves. The camera flicks over to the announce desk. AUSTIN ST. AUDE: “Welcome all to Evolution Pro Wrestling! Welcome all to Tuesday Night Evolve! Welcome all to the best wrestling program in the world! This is Evolution Pro Wrestling, an Evolution in the wrestling business. By the time we are done with the show tonight, you will stick with us forever.” COLT COX: “That is a true statement my friend. All of the stars who weren’t thought much of in… other companies, will be given the shot they deserve to shine here in EPW. We are different from the rest of the promotions in the world because we respect those who work with us, and know that if we give them the proper time to grow and prosper, they will end up on top.” AUSTIN ST. AUDE: “Right you are, partner. But before we can start with the wrestling aspect of our show, we have the pleasure to hear from our creator… the man behind it all! Mr. David Sinclair!” The arena goes silent as David Sinclair’s music hits and he enters the ring with tOWLette following close behind him. David cracks a smirk when his music stops, and he begins to speak. DAVID SINCLAIR: “Welcome to Evolve.” The crowd goes insane after Sinclair mutters those simple words. He lets it all soak in, and then begins to speak. His pet, tOWLette, is perched on the far left ring post. DAVID SINCLAIR: “Before we begin this evening, I would like to address the matters that describe why we are he tonight. For those of you who don’t know… I was recently in a management position at Galveston Island Wrestling on the Revolt brand. I was accused by a meddling little whore of corruption, and then the idiot of an owner fired me. They FIRED ME! I was approached by seven men before my departure from the arena that Thursday night on Revolt. They said that I supplied the company, they would jump ship… and that is exactly what I have done. I have gathered seven of the best and brightest stars and brought them with me. Together, Galveston Island Wrestling will be history. Now, I am no fool. I know that seven people can’t take down a company… especially seven guys just starting their career. So, I improvised. I have hired seven of the best professional wrestlers in history to come here and mentor each and every person on the EPW roster. Let’s introduce the roster and the mentors now! First, we have Battery and his mentor, Laura Seton! “ Bret and Laura enter the arena. They walk down the runway to the ring and enter the ring and shake David Sinclair’s hand. Laura raises Bret’s hand in approval. DAVID SINCLAIR: “Next, we have Paul Defias and his mentor, Cody X!” Cody X makes his way down to the ring; Paul Defias enters through the crowd. Snickers can be heard coming from the announce table. They enter, Cody and Paul shrug off David Sinclair. DAVID SINCLAIR: “The next pairing is Jack Johnson and Mike Pace!” Jack Johnson seems annoyed that he is being announced as a rookie, with Mike Pace as the mentor. He walks down the ring with a scowl on his face, while Pace signs autographs. DAVID SINCLAIR: “Now we have the always entertaining Jack Prowler, and Celina!” Celina and Prowler walk down the ring ramp, Celina cracks her neck and knuckles and stares down Prowler once they enter the ring. Prowler laughs and shakes his head, then jumps up on the turnbuckle, pandering to the crowd. Celina, who is obviously annoyed, pulls him off. DAVID SINCLAIR: “Next up is Don Hairston; and his mentor… the Shinigami; MATT KLUGE!” Kluge and Hairston enter the ring and stare down the other mentors and rookies. They obviously want to make an impact. Sinclair steps out of harm’s way as Hairston begins to exchange words with Battery. DAVID SINCLAIR: “Next up is Marcus Hister and his mentor, Bryson Chandler!” Hister and Chandler make their way to the ring, bickering back and forth with each other. Bryson finally convinces Hister to shut up, and the roll call continues. DAVID SINCLAIR: “Finally we have the North Beach Champion… Chris Armstrong! And his mentor; Lance Hollywood!” Armstrong enters the ring, with the title around his waist. Lance Hollywood follows closely behind him, clapping and talking smack to the crowd. When they get to the ring, Sinclair throws his arm around him. DAVID SINCLAIR: “Ladies and gentlemen, this is the future of wrestling. These are the men your children will be watching. These are the future world champions. Tonight, we gather here for one purpose… to TAKE DOWN GIW!" Cody X steps up from the massive amount of people. CODY X: “David, before you get so caught up with yourself… let’s make something clear. We aren’t here because we like you. We aren’t here for EPW. We aren’t here to make these rookies better. We are here because we all want GIW to be burned to the ground. Don’t think that we care about anything you say or do, because we don’t. Sinclair laughs. DAVID SINCLAIR: “Cody, the fact is that without me… you all would still be unemployed. You wouldn’t be able to take down GIW because you wouldn’t work for anyone. I am the one who is providing you a chance. Don’t disappoint me." The screen fades to commercial. Triple H’s least awesome theme song plays over the PA system and out walks “Peerless” Bret Battery to a respectable pop. Whatever Gets You Through The Day by The Radio blasts and Laura Seton comes running out with a loud cheer from the crowd. The Theme song for WWE Superstars plays and out comes Jack Johnson doing the whole shooting Monopoly money out of a cannon thing while being a Heel shtick that leaves most of the audience confused. Then King Of My World by Saliva starts up and Mike Pace comes walking out acting like he’d rather be anywhere else. As the match is about to begin tOWLette comes flying to the announcer table. COLT COX: And according to this note we have a guest color commentator…… you have got to be kidding me. "The Climb" by Miley Cyrus starts to play and a Stagehand brings El Ladder to the announcer table along with his unofficial rookie El Hijo Del Stepladder. Jack Johnson and Bret Battery start off this match with a myriad array of arm wringers and headlocks which results in the crowd chanting boring. Jack Johnson applies a hammerlock and Battery stops it and hit’s a side headlock takeover into a Japanese Armbar. AUSTIN ST. AUDE: "Ah yes a Japanese Armbar so named because of Samurais using that hold to break the arms of attacking Ninjas." EL LADDER: "………………" COLT COX: "I’m pretty sure that isn’t why it’s called that. AUSTIN ST. AUDE: "Oh my bad, hey look a girl!..." EL LADDER: "…………………" Despite being a brutal MMA hold known for causing people to tap instantly Johnson is able to maneuver himself to the ropes. Battery whips him into the ropes for a Big Boot but since Johnson is taller it ends up being a Medium Shoe. Battery climbs up for a Moonsault but this match starts to encroach on being good so Johnson crotches him on the ropes like a boss. After doing the whole bounce Battery on the ropes because nut shots are funny spot Johnson tags in Mike Pace and Battery tags in Laura Seton. Mike Pace jolts up realizing he has to actually do something now and runs right into a clothesline by Laura. Mike gets up and attempts a Copy And Pace-st but Laura doesn’t bother with computers and hits an Enzugiri. Now that Laura Seton officially did something she tags in Battery. COLT COX: "And here the team of Laura Seton and Bret Battery are using the quick tags to wear down the opposing team… Austin are you listening?" AUSTIN ST. AUDE: "…………" EL LADDER: "……………" Mike Pace shoots up and catches Battery in an anticlimactic armbar. He tags in Johnson but instead of doing the whole hit Battery in the arm with a axe handle move Johnson just gets into the ring and a stern talking too from Pace. Battery rolls up Johnson but gets a one count. Johnson goes for a Million Dollar Dream that makes Ted Dibiase die so he can roll over in his grave then become reincarnated and go back to preaching about Jesus. Battery doesn’t tap or anything mind you, he just becomes mildly annoyed and elbows Johnson in the gut. Battery then head butts Johnson…. in the fist, if you know what I’m saying and then a Mike Sanders inspired Back-2- Backbreaker. He then goes for Silverback but Battery knows next to nothing of geometry so he doesn’t get into the right angle for the Cutter portion. EL LADDER: "………….." AUSTIN ST. AUDE: "I agree." COLT COX: "Why did I join this federation again?" Battery gets dropped with a Scoop Slam followed with a stomp. He makes his way to Laura Seton for the hot tag but Johnson grabs him! He plants him with a Priceless Stunner Neckbreaker that has the same effect on Terry Funk as his Million Dollar Dream had on Ted! He hoists Battery up for a Gorilla Press Slam but Battery slips off does a roll and dives to Laura Seton making the tag! Laura comes running in, admonishes Battery for making a Hot Tag because only Faces do that and tags Battery back in. Jack Johnson sets Bret Battery up for The Temple Of Love The Cashbomb! Mike Pace barrels in to defend Johnson’s pin from Laura Seton but she’s busy applying make up to care about this match. Thankfully it’s over. Mike Pace, one of the Evolve mentors, looks quite pumped and is jumping jump rope. After he’s finished jumping it for about ten seconds, he quickly drops it on the floor and then begins to throw punches in the air and even kicks here and there. Once he’s done, he picks up the jump rope again and gets back to working on his cardio. Finally, he stops and then drops the jump rope and begins to laugh. MIKE PACE: "(Wheeze) You do that … a couple of… (Wheeze) times a day… you’ll be fitter, faster and (Wheeze) stronger than you were ever before! Think of all the benefits, Jack! (Wheeze)" The camera pans to Jack Johnson, sitting on a chair that appears to be encrusted in gold. He doesn’t look too amused. Jack Johnson crosses his leg and then looks around, annoyed. MIKE PACE: "Alright, you don’t want to do any work?! Wait till I tell Sinclair, yeah! That’ll do it! He’ll take care of a punk like you!" Jack Johnson looks slightly alarmed and then gets up and stops Mike Pace from leaving. JACK JOHNSON: "Wait a second. No. You’re not doing that. There’s no way you’re telling Sinclair that I’m not doing any work." MIKE PACE: "So WILL you work?" JACK JOHNSON: "No.. (Mike Pace begins to leave again) WAIT A SECOND. I’ve got a deal for you… Well, I’ve got a very big bank account and well… if you don’t tell Sinclair, I might just write you a check." Mike Pace gets really mad and then pushes Jack Johnson back to his chair. MIKE PACE: "MIGHT? I want to see you write it now! I WANT MY DAMN CHECK RIGHT NOW!" JACK JOHNSON: "Whoa, easy there. Give me a second to write it." Jack Johnson quickly writes the check and hands it to Mike Pace. Mike Pace grabs the check and looks at it. He doesn’t look pleased. MIKE PACE: "Maybe if you add two more zeroes to the end of that number, we MIGHT have a deal." Jack Johnson quickly writes the extra two zeroes in and hands the check to Mike Pace. Mike Pace looks at the check and nods his head in approval. He begins to exit through the door. Jack Johnson looks a little scared. JACK JOHNSON: "Where are you headed now?" MIKE PACE: "To Sinclair’s office. Someone’s in trouble!" Mike Pace quickly runs away. Jack Johnson looks mad. Scene fades. Living Colour by Cult Of Personality hit’s the PA System and out comes Don Hairston and his mentor Matt Kluge to a chorus of boos. The Man Who Sold The World by Nirvana cuts the boos and Marcus Hister walks out with Bryson Chandler. Sing For The Moment by Eminiem starts playing and Paul Defias and Cody X come out and get pelted with garbage. Paul Defias and Marcus Hister lock up and Defias overpowers Hister with a Knuckle Lock. Don Hairston comes in and locks a Cravate onto Paul Defias. COLT COX: "Ah a signature move of Matt Kluge right there. I wonder how much influence he’s had on Don." AUSTIN ST. AUDE: "WOOOOH LADIES!" Marcus Russian Legsweeps Paul taking Don with him as well. Don gets up and gets blasted with a Wheel Kick. Paul gets decked with a spear. Marcus tries to Superkick Don but he catches the leg into a Dragonscrew. Don applies a El Christo leglock but Marcus reaches the ropes. Paul comes from behind with a Wrestling Takedown taking Don to the mat. Paul launches some Soccer punts into Don’s skulls and Marcus makes his way back up. They dump Don over the ropes and into the floor. Matt Kluge makes his way over to Don and immediately starts screaming over the supine body of Don when Marcus hit’s a Baseball Slide into Matt’s head. COLT COX: "Hey now! Matt Kluge did nothing wrong! He’s helping his student!" AUSTIN ST. AUDE: "I don’t think that was helping." Paul pulls in Marcus and applies an Ankle Lock grapevining it in the middle of the ring. Don gets up and charges in but Paul lets go and trips up Don. Marcus limps up and ducks a Roundhouse Kick. Both stars break into a kick battle and Paul jumps in with an Enzugiri. Don gets up and delivers a straight hook to Marcus’s face taking him down. Paul jumps up and chokes Don. Don backs into the corner and Marcus spears both of them in the corner. Marcus whips Don to the ropes and Paul and Marcus Back Body Drops him over the ropes and into the barricades. Marcus turns around and gets hit with Knockout Dropper and then gets locked in The Ender. Bryson Chandler distracts the ref while Marcus taps out. Matt rolls in Don and Paul lets go. Don struggles up as Marcus takes a breather and hit’s the Mystery Darkness. Paul throws Marcus into the corner and pins Don 1.23 Cody X comes in and congratulates Paul Bryson Chandler rushes in and tackles Cody. Matt Kluge comes in and delivers two quick forearm shots to Bryson’s head. Cody comes too and both attack Matt Kluge seeing him as the biggest threat. All three war around the ring while refs try to stop the mayhem. Cody brings in a chair but Matt ducks and hit’s a European Uppercut to the back of Cody’s head. Bryson comes from behind and puts Matt in a Full Nelson. Cody drops Matt with a Steel Chair shot. Finally the ref pushes away Bryson and Cody. Matt Kluge gets back to his feet with help from Don Hairston. Matt offers his hand to Don in a good job gesture, he breaks the shake and stiffs Don with a Shinigami’s Blade! Matt turns him around and locks in a Rat Trap, he even squats up further wrenching the hold. Finally he drops Don spits on the mat and walks off alone. As the fans watch as Don Hairston finally gets to his feet after a long time of being unable to do so. Defias and Hister the two stronger competitors have just gotten to the back when yet again David Sinclair's theme hits and the fans look up to the ramp where Davin storms out from the back heading towards the ring wasting little time as Don is still trying to get up. As he gets into the ring he demands a mic and then stands over Don, in a very menacing way. DAVID SINCLAIR: “Well, well, well. You know when I made this place I made it because I wanted future stars, guys that showed promise. And Don Hairston I thought that you were one of them. Well at the end of the day you proved me wrong. See you made me realize that your the weak one around here! And I'm not wasting my time on weaklings.” David seems to have no remorse as Don is at his feet grabbing onto the bottom of his pants trying to beg for him to change his mind, pleading that he didn't have a chance to prove himself yet. David takes a step back, shaking him off his foot. DAVID SINCLAIR: “You know it's best I make this clear from the start. What you did tonight is not acceptable and you'll be used as an example. You won't have a chance back at GIW you are already a traitor and now your chance to Evolve has ended. Let this be a lesson to all the other six around here, these types of actions are not tolerated around here. Good luck finding another job Hairston, Security come get this man.” Sinclair drops the microphone onto Don's back then heads out of the ring as a few security members walk by him to the ring lifting him up as he begs for a second chance as we head to the back. Chris Armstrong and Lance Hollywood are backstage, seemingly discussing something. Lance Hollywood looks very cocky and doesn’t appear to take Chris Armstrong seriously at all whilst Chris Armstrong is growing redder by the second and becoming frustrated with Lance Hollywood. CHRIS ARMSTRONG: "What do you mean, “I’m not good enough” ? I’ll have you know that I’m Chris Armstrong, the damn future! The one who is bringing you the future era! Everybody on Revolt will soon know that." LANCE HOLLYWOOD: "Ha…Ha…Ha. Good one, Chris. Now…let’s get realistic. You’re seventeen years old and your matches are as predictable as a coin-toss. You lose half your matches. I’d make it positive and say, “You win half your matches” but this isn’t about making you into an even bigger baby. What you have to realize is that you’re absolutely nothing. You’re a nobody. I don’t care if you’re North Beach champion, that title belt is meaningless! If anything, it deserves to be burned because you just carrying it around like means you want to be a part of Revolt and that you can’t wait to get back. Why don’t you EVOLVE and get rid of that little toy?" Chris Armstrong appears to disagree with every Lance Hollywood says but then he takes a good long look at his title. CHRIS ARMSTRONG: "… We’ll see what happens." Chris Armstrong begins to leave but Lance Hollywood stops him. LANCE HOLLYWOOD: "Hey, hey…hey! Where exactly do you think you’re going, little man? Do you think that by saying something that sounds cool to you and then leaving MAKES you any more credible? I see it done all the time and it only makes you stupid and besides, you were doing it wrong. It’s done like this." Lance Hollywood pushes Chris Armstrong and walks away. Chris Armstrong is left scratching and shaking his head in frustration. AUSTIN ST. AUDE: "Up next is our main event featuring what are expected to be two of EPW's top performers; “The Future” Chris Armstrong and “The Lone Wolf” Jack Prowler. If you remember, at Blackout just over a week ago, Armstrong won the North Beach Championship in a four corners match which included Jack Prowler." COLT COX: "Needless to say, the two aren't best friends and having been sharing their non-sentiments for each other over the past week. Tonight is different as not only do they have their EPW mentors at their side, but it's a tag match." Austin looks towards the ring, amongst which all four competitors are standing in. Unsurprisingly his focus is towards Celina and he falls silent. COLT COX: "Um...Austin? AUSTIN!" Austin snaps out of his daze. AUSTIN ST. AUDE: "Yes, they do. I must add that Celina is looking fantastic tonight." COLT COX: "You're really enjoying this, aren't you? First Laura Seton and now Celina." AUSTIN ST. AUDE: "Doesn't get much better than that!" The teams set themselves up, as the mentors Lance Hollywood and Celina start things off. AUSTIN ST. AUDE: "Looks like the vets are going to give Armstrong and Prowler a look at what they're made of first." COLT COX: "Egotistical I call it. Besides, Armstrong could probably take Prowler and Celina by himself. I'm sure Lance Hollywood wouldn't mind taking a payoff to get the night off." Hollywood and Celina approach each and Hollywood takes the early advantage, giving Celina a quick kick to the gut. He quickly takes hold of her right arm and begins thrusting his right shoulder into hers. After three, he continues his hold of her hand and throws her to the ropes. Celina rebounds off the ropes and Hollywood hits a clothesline sending her to the canvas! He quickly follows up by getting behind her and locking in a headlock. COLT COX: "Lance Hollywood. It just doesn't get better than him.". AUSTIN ST. AUDE: "But you just said before this match started that Armstrong didn't need him!" COLT COX: "Well, if there ever was a mentor that could lead Chris Armstrong to a new level, it's him." Feeling he's weakened Celina enough, Hollywood relinquishes the headlock. Instead of going for another move, he stands over her and chuckles to himself as he watches her squirm, trying to regain her strength. As she gets up though, she goes for a legsweep. Though a simple move, it proves effective, causing Hollywood to drop to a knee. Seeing an opportunity, Celina gives a knee to Hollywood's head, knocking him face-first to the canvas. Celina quickly plants herself atop him at a 90-degree angle and locks in her Fujiwara armbar. Hollywood gives a wince of pain that grows with the passing seconds. The ref slides in front of Hollywood to check on him, however Hollywood has no intention of tapping. Celina realizes this after a number of seconds and breaks the hold. She attempts to change her positioning to stay atop Hollywood, but this time to sit on his back, but Hollywood squirms away at the last minute. He gets to his corner and tags in Chris Armstrong. Celina smirks as the “The Priceless Prince” enters and she wastes no time in going for him. AUSTIN ST. AUDE: "Looks like Celina is looking to show Armstrong why she indeed is worthy of being a mentor." Celina takes hold of Armstrong and quickly locks in an ab stretch. Armstrong manages to keep his composure and gets a rush of adrenaline and backs up enough so Celina is sandwiched between him and the ropes. She lets go of the hold and manages a quick kick to Armstrong's head before ducking out of the way towards the middle of the ring. Armstrong comes towards her and the two tie-up before Armstrong locks in a front chauncery—but Celina powers out of it. COLT COX: "Armstrong is obviously trying to let Celina build false confidence. No way she can legitimately get out of that." AUSTIN ST. AUDE: "You can think what you want, but she's wasting no time here! A DDT! Armstrong's head was planted and we have our first pin attempt!" 1... 2... NO! Not quite two as Armstrong gets a shoulder up. Celina backs up to her corner. AUSTIN ST. AUDE: "Jack Prowler now in the ring and so the two's feud picks up further." COLT COX: "There's a reason Armstrong is North Beach Champion and Prowler isn't—and I guarantee you we're about to find out why!" Prowler gives Armstrong no time to completely regain his senses, as he rushes towards him and hits a running bulldog! Prowler gives a couple stomps at Armstrong's head and then tries for a cover himself... 1... Armstrong quickly powers out of this cover as well. Though Prowler is first to his feet, Armstrong is the first to attack—hitting a running reverse elbow which he quickly follows up with a snap mare and into a side headlock. Prowler manages to get to his feet after a bit, using his strength as Armstrong continues with the hold. Prowler then falls backwards, slamming Armstrong to the canvas, much to the fans' delight. Prowler runs to the near ropes, hops on the middle set and leaps backwards with a springboard moonsault, but Armstrong lifts his knees at the last moment! Prowler rolls to the side holding his torso in pain from the countered manuever. Armstrong gets up and tags in Lance Hollywood. Hollywood gets in and heads to Prowler and lifts him up. After a bit of trash talk to Prowler, Hollywood hits a standing clothesline sending Prowler back down—but Prowler has his own adrenaline rush and pops back up! He quickly rushes off the far ropes and strikes with a high knee! AUSTIN ST. AUDE: "Hollywood is taken off-guard there! Prowler looks to be sizing him up...OOOHHH!! What an enzuigiri kick! Hollywood looks like he got hit by a truck there! Prowler tries for the cover—what an upset this would be!!" COLT COX: "Come on Lance! You can't possibly let this guy pin you!" 1... 2... THR—NO! Hollywood shoulders out at the last minute and Prowler can't believe it! As Prowler tries convincing the ref it was indeed a three count, Hollywood manages to come to and picks up Prowler and nails a huge powerslam. He makes his own cover... 1... 2... AUSTIN ST. AUDE: "Jack Prowler kicks out!" COLT COX: "Lance Hollywood not looking to happy and I can't blame him. PUT THIS GUY AWAY!" As if hearing Colt, Hollywood gets picks up Prowler and hits his spinebuster—but before Hollywood can make a cover, Celina rushes in. She begins firing away with various kicks towards Hollywood, knocking him down. She locks in an inverted Boston crab but the ref is quick to rush to her and begins making a five count towards disqualification. She lets go of it at “three” and gives a quick word to her protogee before climbing back out of the ring. Prowler looks to follow up with a second enzuigiri but Hollywood ducks out of the way! As Prowler gets up, Hollywood tags Armstrong back in. Prowler begins making his way to his corner to tag Celina back in but just before he gets there, Armstrong hustles to him wraps his arms around Prowler's waist and leans back, hitting a German suplex! Prowler takes a couple seconds rolling on the mat before getting to his feet—seeing Armstrong between him and his corner. Prowler rushes forward and as Armstrong looks for a clothesline, Prowler ducks down and hits a legsweep. AUSTIN ST. AUDE: "Prowler wastes no time as he quickly fires a set of kicks at his rival's body." COLT COX: "I'll give the guy credit, he's hanging in there—but now look at him! He's used so much to counter there that he's out of breath—and just out of reach of Celina!" Celina begins yelling at Prowler to get him enough inspiration to reach the corner. He crawls his way there but just before he makes the tag, Armstrong manages to pull a leg of his and he falls way out of reach. AUSTIN ST. AUDE: "Once again Jack Prowler unlucky to get to his corner and you have to wonder just when his fatigue will catch up with him." COLT COX: "If you ask me—I think he's plum out of luck.". AUSTIN ST. AUDE: "What the heck is Armstrong doing now? It looks like he's trying to send a message to Lance." COLT COX: "That's the makings of a great team, Austin. Being able to communicate in a match." AUSTIN ST. AUDE: "And what is Lance doing!?" Hollywood quickly heads back in the ring, but instead of going after Prowler, he has his sights set on Celina. Celina realizes the situation and climbs in the ring herself. She manages to grab Hollywood before he can do anything to her, but instead of being able to make a move on him, Hollywood keeps her in a tie-up. The two fall to the mat and begin rolling around and wind up rolling under the bottom ropes and to the floor. Smiling—Armstrong takes advantage. AUSTIN ST. AUDE: "Both legal competitors up...Armstrong quickly spins Prowler around. Oh boy..." COLT COX: "This is it! Say good night, Jack!" AUSTIN ST. AUDE: "Chris hooking Jack's arms and ducks beneath him—set up for his Loaded vertebreaker! And there it is!" 1... 2... 3! YOSHIRO HAMADA: "The winners of this match by pinfall—Team...Hollywood!" Lance Hollywood quickly gets up on the outside, giving Celina one last stomp and rolls into the ring so as to let the ref be able to hold his arm in victory as well. AUSTIN ST. AUDE: "Chris Armstrong apparently knew he had this in the bag and that communication moments ago was to make sure Celina was distracted enough so as not to be able to interfere." COLT COX: "Great teamwork there. Not only that beautiful bit there at the end but also managing to keep Jack Prowler isolated from Celina once he entered the ring. Chris Armstrong had said he should be the face of EPW and tonight he backs up his words." All of the EPW superstars come out to the music of Evolve. All of them appear to have some sort of plan formulated in their minds. Their cocky grins tell the entire story. All of them have microphones in hand. One is missing, however and that’s the North Beach champion, Chris Armstrong. Bret Battery gets in front of all of the other members, with microphone in hand and then begins to speak. BRET BATTERY: "Well, there’s a reason why we’re all out here. As you can see, Chris Armstrong is not with us because we’ve got a surprise for him that we’ve arranged for him. So without further delay, Chris Armstrong get out here!" Bret Battery appears rather angry after having given those words. Chris Armstrong comes out to the music of Evolve with his title over his shoulder. He seems a little wary of the rest but he still enters the ring without really thinking twice about it. He is given a microphone by Jack Johnson. BRET BATTERY: "Chris, I know Jack just gave you that microphone but after what we’re done doing what we’re going to do to you…you won’t even want to speak for the time being. Oh…have we got something in store…" Suddenly, all of the superstars quickly take the belt of Chris and then begin to pour a fluid all over it. Battery, who appears to be the front man of all of them gets out a match and gives it to Paul Defias who drops it straight on the belt. The belt catches fire. Chris Armstrong looks at all of them with disbelief. Meanwhile, a valet comes out holding another championship. She enters the ring. Bret speaks again. BRET BATTERY: "We’ve burned your belt because it’s tainted. It’s property of Galveston Island Wrestling! Now, though…you’re not the silly North Beach champion…you’re the EVOLVE champion!" All of the superstars pick up Chris and begin to celebrate. Chris holds the title way up high as the crowd cheers and boos at the same time.
