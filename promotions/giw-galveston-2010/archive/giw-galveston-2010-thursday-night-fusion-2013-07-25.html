GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: THURSDAY NIGHT FUSION LIVE! From Porretto Beach – July 25, 2013 QUOTE To start the night off as usual we have the Open Battle Royal, this week we are greeted in the ring by two very different looking men as we have Bradley Queen a physical specimen taking on the much smaller Spencer Allen, the two quickly have made fans and enemies out of these people already in the seats tonight as the little guy gets himself some fans while they all quickly turn on Queen. RILEY STEVENS: “Two interesting prospects here in this match as these two are very different body type’s.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “One’s a man, ones a boy!” DING DING DING! With a jumping half kick, Spencer began the bout swift and hard. Queen arose shortly after, still fresh for the match. He blocked the next maneuver and positioned Spencer into a powerbomb that dropped the man like a ton of bricks into the canvas below. It wasn’t long before he had turned the tides of battle for the Open Battle Royal. Considering they were the only contestants, it was now a singles competition. Spencer Allen versus Bradley Queen. Started quick, hard, and still going by the looks of a stiff german suplex performed by Queen on to Spencer Allen, roughly. A dodged superkick and Queen took hold of control once again. Body language suggested Queen was going to make a breakthrough with Spencer, as he stalked toward his opponent to continue the assault. He positioned him for a powerslam, but Spencer ducked and maneuvered out with as much speed as he could muster. Queen charged headlong against Spencer and flattened him with the flick of the wrist, a heavyset clothesline that downed the escapee. There wasn’t any chance he would let Spencer play hero, and instead dropped a human body on him like an atom bomb on a high school. RILEY STEVENS: “Great match so far here.” It seemed Spencer’s fate was in the lap of the gods, when suddenly he managed to get another counter in. Like the invisible man, he swooped around and ticked Queen into a crucifix pin using speed and guile. It, however, only lasted for a two count before his opponent powered out of the attempt.Spencer was quick to pull out and come over Bradley, not letting up any chance to let him counter again. He was like a man on the prowl, mad the swine, and refused to have a lost opportunity with Queen. However, after a quick misfire attempt at another superkick, something he hoped to smack his boot into the skull of his opponent, Queen took to chance and performed his grand entrance, the burning hammer. RILEY STEVENS: “Queen could take it here.” Like a procession, Spencer was buried beneath the mass of Queen, but that too ended only in a two count. Disappointed, Queen knelt up and began to argue with the referee. He knew it would take moments for Spencer to recover, but he didn’t realize the man had tapped into all of his reserves of energy to jump to his feet and smack his opponent square in the face with a hard hit boot. After that, he set himself up along the top turn post, nearest to the body of Queen and shot out like a cannon to execute the shooting star press, what he called: Death Star. With a quick pin rolled up and ready to go, he was cleared for victory over Queen by the referee. A three count under the belt and he was off like a rocket. No longer under pressure, Spencer shot his arm up in sweet victory, looming over the fallen mass of Queen. He was the champion, in the end. Fighting until he would win. REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner by way of pinfall…Spencer Allen!” These fans go crazy as Bradley kicks out but does so a second too late as these fans go wild as Spencer smartly slides out of the ring to celebrate with the crowd but more importantly get away from Bradley who pushes the referee in anger, stating that he kicked out as these fans go wild patting Allen on the back as he pulled it out of no where as we fade for the night on Fusion with no Deep South title match this week. WINNER: SPENCER ALLEN
