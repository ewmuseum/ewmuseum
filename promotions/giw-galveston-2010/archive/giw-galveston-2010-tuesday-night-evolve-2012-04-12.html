Tuesday Night Evolve, 12 April 2012

12 April 2012

GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: EVOLVE LIVE! From the Monroe Civic Center in Monroe, LA – April 12, 2012 QUOTE It is now time for the Evolve match which is just a singles match tonight as we have Matt Matlock and Sean Mendez standing in the ring getting ready to go. RILEY STEVENS: “Evolve match here tonight Matlock returns to face the debuting Sean Mendez.” Both men line up in their corners, ready to go. DING DING DING! The bell sounds and the Evolve preshow competition is underway as Sean Mendez and Matt Matlock are ready to duel in the center of the ring. Matlock smirks and the fans boo him loudly, thanks a lot to his arrogant swagger around the ring, almost as if he is blowing off the match with Sean as not much of a challenge. The two finally lock up and Matt, the much bigger and stronger competitor has control as he takes down Mendez with a headlock. The crowd continues to let Matt how they feel as they boo him again, which he answers by disrespecting Mendez by rubbing his forearm in Sean’s face followed by using his foot to push Sean down on the canvas. Matt then taunts Sean to come at him, almost begging him to hit him. RILEY STEVENS: “Matlock’s record may not say great things about him but he is a fighter.” Sean Mendez has had enough and the new superstar runs at Matlock and connects with a flying forearm. Matt crashes to the canvas but bounces right back up, only to get drilled with a hard knife-edged chops followed up by various kick combos, including one final one that connects to the temple. Matt crumbles to one knee and Sean uses the ropes for momentum and nails a huge drop kick right to Matt’s face much to the delight of the fans. Sean covers but only gets a two count. RILEY STEVENS: “Sean looking good in his debut.” Both men are back up and Sean catches his breath quickly. He attempts a forearm but Matt catches it and delivers a huge kick to Sean’s gut. Snap suplex by Matlock and he covers but Sean is able to kick out at two. Matlock smacks the mat in frustration and asks the referee how that wasn’t three. He assures Matt that it was only two. Matt turns around and is rocked by a Krush Kombo, one of Sean’s signature combinations, two kicks to the chest followed by a huge roundhouse kick. Matt is gorgy and this is the chance Sean was looking for. Sean Mendez hits The Gringo Killa, a back-to-back underhook piledriver. The fan’s let out a thunderous applause for the marvelous move. Sean hooks the leg and this one is over. JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…Sean Mendez!” The fans applaud for the debuting star giving him his respect for an impressive performance tonight as he hops up to his feet holding up his arms in victory. RILEY STEVENS: “Sean Mendez gets the win tonight in a solid debut.” Matlock rolls out of the ring in pain and angry as Mendez heads up to the top rope as this one match Evolve comes to a close. WINNER: SEAN MENDEZ
