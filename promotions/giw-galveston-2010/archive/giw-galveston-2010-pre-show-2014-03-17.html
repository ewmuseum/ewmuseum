Pre-show, 17 March 2014

GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: REVOLT PRE-SHOW Live from Porretto Beach - March 17th, 2014 QUOTE As we get started on the GIW Revolt Pre-Show we are out in the ring ready for the Open Battle Royal the only match to be featured here tonight and we start with the old face of Colt Cox who tries his best to force a smile as he sits out at the announce table. COLT COX: “Welcome to the GIW.com Revolt Pre-Show, its Colt Cox here and I am here with…I’m here with my commentary partner…Assistant General Manager…Amanda Rowland.” The camera pans out to show a happy Amanda Rowland smiling and leaning in for a hug with him one that Colt doesn’t offer much back for. AMANDA ROWLAND: “You know I have a lot of duties as Assistant General Manager, but I thought I’d come down and we can do this like old times! You better do well though, I can fire you. Just kidding.” The grim reality that Amanda still has much more power then Colt hits him as he sighs as we head to the ring where we get a glimpse of the competitors in the ring as Dennis Driver, Jack Waters, The Sir, and the two members of Sinful Sexuality are ready to go for an Open Battle Royal, where the last person in the ring will be the winner. DING DING DING! Things start out quickly as Driver the speedster heads at Waters in the corner the two starting to exchange as across the ring The Sir has to face both the members of Sinful Sexuality as they approach him. Both Barbie and Rose, have him cornered as he holds out his hands to stop them, taking a second to pose something that draws laughter from the crowd as both Rose and Barbie look at one another seeming impressed as he rubs his hands through his beard as the two girls get closer and it looks like an unlikely three way kiss is about to occur as the two start to kiss Sir, who seems to have fully expected to have this effect on them until they break the kiss and drop him with a Double DDT to the mat. Sir bounces up and shakes his head, as he repeats “That’s not fair” as the two girls continue the double team in the corner. As across the ring Driver has made the dangerous decision to head to the top rope, where he is about to jump off when Waters rushes him and hits out one leg dropping him on the corner post. If he had any plans of trying anything with Rose and Barbie the plans are out the window now, as he holds his lower regions as Waters tries to push him over as on the other side The Sir has fought his way out of the corner, hitting Rose with a Big Boot then dropping Barbie with a Running Knee as he starts to hulk up a bit, or rather Sir Up as he gets ready to try to take them both over the top rope as they get up against the ropes. COLT COX: “Looks like we got some real talent here.” Sir rushes them but the two girls, duck down and pull the ropes down with them as The Sir nearly goes over the top, but catches himself on the other side. Holding on by a thread the two girls rush him again, but this time he manages to punch one of then the other as they stumble then he rams the two of them into each other. Forcing them to fall to the mat as he turns to the crowd “Nobody can beat The Sir!” as he says this Waters on the other end of the ring attempts to throw Driver over the top rope. But the small and athletic Dennis grabs a hold of the rope and pulls himself back up as Jack sees that Sir is a little bit caught up and rushes to the other side of the ring landing a Clothesline from Hell over the top rope sending The Sir, to the outside of the ring with a big thud. The Sir has been eliminated! Sir shakes it off and gets up looking shocked that he just got eliminated as Waters waves at him over the top rope then turns around and walks across the ring and he ducks when he sees Dennis flying through the air after having gone springboard, while Jack moves Barbie gets up and eats the Missile Dropkick dropping to the mat as Driver gets up and yells out something to the crowd that they don’t seem to like as they quickly start to boo as The Sir is arguing with the fans all the way up the ramp. Driver now turns his attention to Rose across the ring, hitting her with an Uppercut that drops her back into the corner as he follows up with a big punch then reaches for her leg trying to throw her over the top. But Waters pulls him off of her across the ring, landing an Uppercut of his own rocking the much smaller competitor as Jack grabs him and whips him into the corner. He’s about to rush him but just as he does Barbie is back in smashing him with a kick to the gut, that kneels him over as at the same time Rose is in the corner in front of Driver grinding on him something he isn’t sure about. COLT COX: “Not sure if that is a wrestling move…” Dennis doesn’t seem quite sure how to handle this, not really threatened by it but the whole thing is just a distraction as Rose moves just in time for Barbie to hit him with a clothesline in the corner that drops him down to a seated position as Sinful Sexuality now turns and hits him with a duel Stink Face in the corner as Driver struggles but he’s not sure why as the two wait a bit too long as right in the middle Waters clotheslines both in the corner sending them down with Driver seated in the corner as Jack takes a step back and runs a knee into all three of them in the corner as these fans are getting a treat in this Open Battle Royal as Waters grabs Rose and hoists her up throwing her over the ropes, to the other side. Rose grabs a hold of the ropes but barely has a hold, considering the knee rocked her good. Jack reaches down going for Barbie as well now but she delivers a nasty low blow out of no where that drops him to a knee as Rose now holds the ropes down for Barbie who grabs Waters and does the best she can throwing him over but he lands on the ring apron as well. Rose on the outside with him now as Rose and Barbie try to take him out with punches and kicks, as Jack fights back but they are in a dangerous position and a recovered Dennis sees it and rushes at Barbie kicking her in the back sending her into both Rose and Waters sending them out of the ring and out of the match. Rose Gasm & Jack Waters have been eliminated! With just one person left, Barbie looks over the ropes trying to apologize to Rose as she turns right into a mean slap from Driver that takes her off her feet as he taunts her while she is down reaching down and grabbing her as she throws a big counter punch dropping him back as he responds with a Mortal Kombat uppercut that sends her reeling to the ropes as Driver yells out that it’s over as he rushes Barbie and clotheslines her over the ropes and out of the ring to win the match. REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner…Dennis Driver!” These fans aren’t happy as at least the men in the crowd were pulling for Rose and Barbie to walk out victorious but instead Rose is on the outside helping her up as an angry Waters walks to the back cursing at a few audience members as he walks to the back and COLT COX: “Dennis Driver ends up the victor here tonight and you got to give it to him, he looked great. But the rest of the wrestlers in the match really showed some promise.” AMANDA ROWLAND: “That’s for sure! Any how I better get to Ian Mendoza he may need my help, enjoy the rest the night off Colt.” She smiles happily patting him on the back as Colt lets out a sigh taking off his microphone and throwing it to the side as Amanda heads to the back as in the ring Dennis Driver celebrates really showboating as these fans boo him but after a win like that you have to think his future is bright in GIW as the Pre-Show fades just as we are about ten minutes out from the Main Card. WINNER: DENNIS DRIVER
