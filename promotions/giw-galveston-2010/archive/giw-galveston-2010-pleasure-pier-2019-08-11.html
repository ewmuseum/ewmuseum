GIW — Card

Pleasure Pier, 11 August 2019

11 August 2019
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Live! From the Pleasure Pier! Sunday August 11th, 2019 - Galveston, TX [pic] The GIW logo flashes across the screen, and within moments we are shown a high shot panning over the Pleasure Pier on Galveston Island. Extending over the ocean, the Pier plays host to a wrestling ring and an unorganized swarm of fans gathered around it. We see the stage for the first time, a small raised structure with large screens on either side of the entrance portal. As music plays in the background, the voices of the commentary team can be heard. Riley Stevens: Ladies and gentlemen, for the first time in a long time, Galveston Island Wrestling is LIVE from the Pleasure Pier! My name is Riley Stevens and I am joined by my longtime broadcast partner, Oliver Thibodaux. I know it is my job to be descriptive, but Ollie, I can’t even begin to describe the atmosphere in this place. The camera cuts to reveal Riley Stevens and Oliver Thibodaux behind a table. Oliver Thibodaux: It feels right, doesn’t it? To be back here in Galveston? Riley Stevens: Without a doubt. Oliver Thibodaux: Man, it really takes me back to all of those great memories. This place has been part of my life, and the lives of so many others, for nearly a decade now. It hasn’t always been amazing times, I’ll be the first to admit that, but there’s a passion here… a love for this place that not many companies can say they have. Riley Stevens: I gotta say, I wasn’t expecting sentimentality from you today. Oliver Thibodaux: I’m getting older. Getting softer I guess. But you’re right, enough of that, let’s get down to business. We’re live on MTV! They wanna see some violence. Riley Stevens: Violence is certainly something we can provide! This afternoon, and every time we head to the Pleasure Pier, we will be putting the GIW North Beach Championship on the line. Five wrestlers have announced that they will be entering the match: Carlos Cruz, Nick Garnet, Joey Rappaport, Riley Savell, and Kimitsu Zombie. If you don’t know those names yet, you certainly will by the end of the show. Oliver Thibodaux: One of those names is going to be added to a long list of some of the best to ever do it that have held the North Beach Championship. Wrestlers like Madison Seton, Alioth Starre, Trent Lost, Alex Castellanos, Michael Lynch, Matt Kluge, and Tony Miranda. Of the twelve different men and women to have held the North Beach Championship, half of them have also held the Undisputed Championship at some point during their career. Riley Stevens: That’s the kind of analysis we pay you for. And I’m sure that each and every one of those wrestlers entering this match is hoping their career has the same trajectory. Oliver Thibodaux: It takes a lot to even put yourself out there for this match. It’s extra work on top of an already intense workload. But the reward is worth it. “What You Know” by TI hits the speakers on the Pier. Fans immediately erupt in cheers as the entrance video of Bronx Valescence is shown on the screens. The man himself steps onto the stage and puts on his sunglasses. Riley Stevens: What a way to kick off a new era of Galveston Island Wrestling - it’s Bronx Valescence, and listen to this crowd! Oliver Thibodaux: Yeah Riley… but this isn’t the same Bronx Valescence that we knew five years ago. The man is a star now. Riley Stevens: You know it’s true based on this reaction - but he was always a star, Ollie. Oliver Thibodaux: Not like this. I’d say he’s the most popular professional wrestler on the planet. “Bronx-y” chants pick up in the crowd, mixing with “Welcome Back” as Valescence steps into a GIW for the first time in a long time. With a mic in hand, he savors the crowd reaction for a while before deciding to speak. Bronx Valescence: I thought a little while about what my first words would be when I stepped back into this ring. But I think it is safe to say that you guys did all the talking for me. It’s great to be back in a GIW ring. With a smile, Bronx continued on. Bronx Valescence: Aspen asked me to come out here and talk to all of you today and I was happy to do it, but I was pretty surprised because the last time I had words with that woman… well it wasn’t so pretty. She never thought I was good enough. I was always the red-headed step child of Galveston Island Wrestling and I was damn proud of it! The crowd popped, Bronx pressed on. Bronx Valescence: So is this the beginning of corporate Bronx? No… Of course it isn’t. But at the end of the day it’s nice to know that Aspen Chaud finally realizes what all of you have known forever: I live and breathe this company. I love this company. And I’m going to do anything in my power to make sure that this company succeeds. I’m going to put the company on one shoulder and the GIW Undisputed Championship on the other and carry them both straight to the top! As the crowd’s hype reaches another fever pitch, “Control” by Halsey cuts them off. Riley Savell steps onto the ramp with a microphone and the look on her face is enough for the crowd to start booing. Riley Savell: Cut the music. Bronx, that’s enough… really. Bronx opens the ropes for her condescendingly as she makes her way to the ring. Bronx Valescence: The crown jewel of the Savell wrestling family folks… and the star of the prestigious dead wrestling promotion Empire Pro Wrestling. Let’s give Riley Savell a big Galveston welcome. The crowd responds with boos, prompting a laugh from Bronx. Riley sidesteps his offer to help her into the ring and climbs in on the other side, eventually meeting Bronx in the middle. Riley Savell: If there’s one thing I’m going to do in this company, its make them forget about the ‘old guard’ like yourself, Bronx. More boos. Bronx raises an eyebrow. Riley Savell: I understand that dragging you out here for a nostalgia pop is good for the first show, but that’s all you are Bronx. Nostalgia. You’ve lived on that for so long now. But the future is here… standing right in front you. What are you going to do about it? She squares up with Bronx. For a moment he seems like he is going to do something, but he steps away. Bronx Valescence: I’m gonna let you make your own path. You wanna reach up the card and put a target on my back, that’s fine. But you have a few rungs to climb before you get to me because here, your name doesn’t mean shit. No one is gonna hand you anything. You’ve gotta grab it. With a smile, Bronx begins to make his way out of the ring as Savell holds position in the center. Bronx Valescence: And, hey - you’re going to have a chance to do all of that today. You cut me off before I could get to the point of all this talking. The North Beach Championship match you’re about to enter is going to be a LADDER MATCH! Savell stares down Bronx as he exits onto the ramp. Bronx Valescence: Good luck kid, maybe I’ll see you on the other side. Bronx’s music kicks up again. Savell can be seen extending her arms and mouthing “coward” at Bronx as he disappears behind the curtain. The voice of Riley Stevens can be heard over the scene. Riley Stevens: You hear that Ollie, a ladder match to crown the new North Beach Champion! Oliver Thibodaux: Looks like Riley Savell is going to have to put her money where her mouth is if she wants to walk away as champion… Riley Stevens: We will find out if she has what it takes on the other side of this commercial break when we bring you the entire North Beach Championship match uninterrupted! Stay tuned, you won’t want to miss this. We fade to commercial with a lasting shot of the North Beach Championship in the hands of a crew member, waiting to be hung over the ring. GIW North Beach Championship Match Carlos Cruz vs. Joey Rappaport vs. Kimitsu Zombie vs. Nick Garnet vs. Riley Savell Back from the commercial break, “How Soon Is Now” by The Smiths begins to play over the speakers. We get a short shot of Riley Savell finding her corner and then move to Joey Rappaport making his way to the ring. Riley Stevens: Joey Rappaport finds his way to us from Anchorage, Alaska - he has a lot of experience under his belt, but translating all of that to a new place can be difficult. Oliver Thibodaux: They breed them tough up there in the great white North, I’m sure he will be fine. Besides, from what I can find about this guy, he’s not afraid of heights. He’ll be able to use the ladder to his advantage in this one. As Rappaport joins Savell in the ring, “Jesusfreak” by CJAY picks up, and Carlos Cruz steps out onto the stage with Agustin Gates following closely behind. Boos pick up throughout the crowd. Riley Stevens: Well it looks like half of the Celestial Warriors weren’t allowed to come out tonight, which I think is appropriate. Even still Carlos Cruz is a veteran with some backup in Agustin Gates today… that’s gotta be a powerful combination. Oliver Thibodaux: Absolutely. Even just having one man in your corner can really change the match in your favor. I’m sure Cruz is planning to return the favor when Gates is fighting for the Undisputed Championship next Sunday. Gates begins jawjacking with Rappaport as Savel and Cruz come face to face before the referee pulls them apart. Shortly after “You Will Be Remembered” by Alterbridge begins to play and Nick Garnet steps onto the stage. Riley Stevens: Now this is an interesting story. If you are a longtime Texas wrestling fan you’ve heard of Nick Garnet, but his career didn’t really come together as he planned. He’s looking to revitalize it here in GIW. Oliver Thibodaux: Look - sometimes it just doesn’t work out for you. This guy is thirty-seven years old and he’s still looking for a big break. Expectations for him are low but that’s the kind of man I used to fear the most in the ring - the one with nothing to lose. He’s the biggest man in this match by a mile and maybe he can use that to his advantage. Finally, the music of Kimitsu Zombie begins to play. One of the bigger names in this match, the crowd rises to their feet to see her. Riley Stevens: An x-factor. I feel like Kimitsu Zombie is going to play that role in all of her matches. Oliver Thibodaux: She’s pissed… look out! Kimitsu Zombie rushes toward the ring and slips under the bottom rope, immediately going after Carlos Cruz as the bell sounds. She tackles him to the ground and begins pummeling him with punches, getting a big pop from the crowd. On the other side of the ring Joey Rappaport and Riley Savell are trading shots. Rappaport picks her up and then slams her down with a big body slam that eventually turns into a dropped elbow across the throat. Garnet stands in his corner and watches the brawl, not quite sure which way to go, but that decision is made for him when Kimitsu charges him with a running double knee that squishes him into the corner. Kimitsu tosses Garnet to the outside under the bottom rope and then climbs to the top. When the moment is just right, she nails him with a diving crossbody and both wrestlers are down on the floor. Oliver Thibodaux: Kimitsu Zombie has certainly set the tone for this match! With Cruz leaning in the corner, and Zombie and Garnet on the outside, the middle of the ring is clear for Savell and Rappaport. Savell nails him with a spinning elbow that knocks him silly, but Rappaport is able to quickly counter with a jumping enziguri to the side of the head. Cruz rushes in with a double clothesline that lays the both of them out! He poses for the camera as Gates cheers him on from ringside! Riley Stevens: Smart tactics, to the credit of Carlos Cruz. He hoofs Savell out of the ring and slaps the head of Rappaport, getting him back on his knees before bouncing off of the ropes and then connecting with a big double stomp to the back, flattening Rappaport. Gates is quick to grab a ladder from under the ring and slip it under the bottom rope for Cruz. Cruz takes it and drives it right into the chest of Rappaport, sending the Alaskan rolling under the bottom rope to the outside looking for cover. Kimitsu Zombie manages to enter the ring without attracting the attention of Cruz, who is ascending the ladder and ready to go for the title. She tugs him on the foot and pulls him down before levelling him with a forearm strike right to the chin. Cruz quickly makes it to his knees, but Zombie hits the ropes and connects with a beautifully executed Rising Banzai God Kick that lands right on the top of his head. Cruz falls limp to the mat and Zombie tosses him to the outside. Oliver Thibodaux: That kick just brought Carlos Cruz closer to heaven. Zombie sees Riley Savell entering the fray once again, and decides to cut her off. Zombie avoids a flying knee strike and turns it into a cravate suplex, but Savell rolls to her feet quickly, albeit dazed, and ends up in the corner. Zombie moves in, lands one stiff punch right to the jaw of Savell, and then goes to the top looking for a hurricanrana. She’s in the perfect spot, but Nick Garnet shows up on the apron and pushes her off, and Zombie crashes hard to the outside! Garnet enters the ring and Savell realizes what is going on. He manages to toss her across the ring using brute force, and then looks to the ladder. He doesn’t know if this is the perfect time, but decides to shoot his shot and begins climbing. As it turns out, it was not the perfect time. Joey Rappaport is on the top rope in the corner and of the ring and he nails Garnet with a missile dropkick that sends the big man down to the mat with a big thud. Rappaport spots Savell and rushes her, looking for a swinging neckbreaker, but she counters it and grips his head before drive a knee hard into his gut. From there she bounces off of the ropes and looks for the Savell Stomp, but Rappaport dodges it at the last second. He nails her with The Cataclysm, a jumping cutter, and then sends her to the outside under the bottom rope. Riley Stevens: Rappaport narrowly avoided disaster there, and managed to turn it into a big win. That’s the mark of a true veteran. He goes to work trying to send Garnet out of the ring to get a clear shot at the North Beach Championship, but getting Garnet over the top is harder than he thought. Garnet manages to fight back with some well placed elbows. With Rappaport staggered and out of the mix for the moment, Garnet rears back and absolutely levels him with the running big boot that he calls “ReBOOT”. As Rappaport rolls to safety, Garnet’s attention is drawn to Gates at ringside who jumps onto the apron and begins screaming at him. Garnet shrugs it off, knowing better, and turns around straight into a superkick from Carlos Cruz. Gates hypes up his man some more as Cruz begins to climb the ladder, the title nearly in his grasp. Oliver Thibodaux: Agustine Gates is a genius! What a tactical mastermind! Cruz, however, did not see Kimitsu Zombie slip into the ring with a steel chair in hand a mean look in her eye. Gates tried to warn Cruz, but Cruz wasn’t listening, so he slid into the ring and charged Zombie! But she slapped him right in the gut with a big shot from the steel chair and then followed it up with a shot across the back. Gates quickly rolled out of the ring looking for safety, but Zombie turned to Cruz looking to finish the job. However, Cruz had caught on and manages to jump off the ladder onto her with a big Solemn Truth, a double foot stomp, that catches her on the shoulder. Zombie is in a lot of pain as she rolls out of the ring. Cruz collects himself and… wouldn’t you know it… turns around just in time to eat a spear from Nick Garnet! Riley Stevens: What a car crash this match is! The crowd is getting behind Garnet now, and the big smile on his face shows us that he loves every second of it. He looks up the ladder and crowd pops even more, so he decides to begin climbing to get up there. It’s a slower pace than normal, but after a few moments he’s at the top. Oliver Thibodaux: Look out! Behind you! It’s Riley Savell - she climbs up a few rungs and then connects with a low blow that takes the breath out of Nick Garnet. She then speeds up the process by pulling him off the ladder and sending him down to the ring hard. Savell flashes a smile for the cameras as she climbs the ladder and grabs the title from the hook, officially becoming the first North Beach Champion of the new GIW era. WINNER: RILEY SAVELL (NEW GIW NORTH BEACH CHAMPION) Riley Stevens: I’ll say it, I forgot that Riley Savell was on the outside. But she picked the absolute perfect moment to swoop in and steal the belt. Oliver Thibodaux: There was no theft involved. She picked her spot, just like you said. Anyone could have done it, but she was the only one who was capable. Riley Stevens: And now she’s champion, just like she said she would be. Oliver Thibodaux: I’ll say this - I’m impressed. No need to go out there and get all the big moves and all of the gifs, all that matters is holding up that title in the end. No one else in the world can claim to be a GIW champion right now, except for Riley Savell. The camera shows Riley standing on the top rope as she holds her title up high, observing the carnage around her. We get a shot of Nick Garnet looking totally dejected. Zombie is still holding her shoulder. Rappaport is leaning against the ring post. Gates and Cruz are beginning to gather themselves. Riley is the only one standing. Riley Stevens: That just about does it for us here at the Pleasure Pier. Next Sunday night we have the first Revolt back, and it is a win and you’re in battle royal! There will be a new Undisputed Champion crowned, and could you imagine if it happens to be Riley Savell? Oliver Thibodaux: I wouldn’t put it past her. What an exceptional young talent. Riley Stevens: We’ll see you next time on Galveston Island Wrestling! One final shot of Riley Savell holding the title is shown before we fade to black
