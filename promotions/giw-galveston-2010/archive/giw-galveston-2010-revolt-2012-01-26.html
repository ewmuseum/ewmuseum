GIW — Card

Revolt, 26 January 2012

26 January 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: THURSDAY NIGHT REVOLT LIVE! From Moody Gardens – January 26, 2012 The show does begin with its usual loud music and pyro instead we head somberly into a dimly lit Moody Gardens where standing in a ring currently filled with a table and Zachary Pagonis. The camera zoomed in on him as he looked out at the crowd who were extremely quiet as Pagonis welcomed to the show. ZACHARY PAGONIS: “Welcome everyone to Thursday Night Revolt…Last week we all witnessed a very sad tragedy when Blake Beckett was hit by a car at the end of Revolt. While we don’t want to get into the guessing game now, we did want to let you all in on his situation. He is breathing and coherent however, there has been serious damages to his ribs, arms, and a punctured lung as well as serious brain and nerve damage. The Doctors say that he won’t be out for quite some time and will not be able to resume his duties here for awhile, so for now the search for a replacement has begun.” These fans listen intently to it all, as Pagonis always professional looks around nodding his head. ZACHARY PAGONIS: “But the show must go on and that’s why tonight we are going to start the show with a contract signing. Next week we will host an extended Revolt Supershow that will feature a GIW Legacy Championship match between champion, Alexis Terry and Marcus Hister. Can we get both out here?” “Paradise Awaits” by Killer Tracks hits and the fans start to cheer for Marcus Hister whom they have begun to cheer the past few weeks due to both his mother’s passing and the fact he has the chance to take the belt away from Alexis Terry. He heads down to the ring joining Pagonis in the ring, shaking his hand. RILEY STEVENS: “Marcus Hister has long desired a title here in GIW and this is a huge opportunity for him, can he get over the hump?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Against Alexis? Don’t joke around.” Hister takes a seat on his side of the table and then "Paparazzi" By Lady Gaga (Alexis Terry Remix) hits and the cheers turn to boos as Alexis Terry walks out with the title strapped around her waist walking down to the ring in a dress, having no match here tonight. The fans jeer her as she heads into the ring walking to the table and taking a seat on her side immediately picking up one of the mic’s laid out for the two. ALEXIS TERRY: “Zachary I really like this idea you have here. I mean picking a random fan to fight for the title? Pretty creative stuff you got there.” The fans boo her snarky little remark as Hister shakes it off with a grin as Pagonis tries to get it started. ZACHARY PAGONIS: “We are here today to make this title match official…” But as he is talking he gets cut off by Alexis. ALEXIS TERRY: “Are you at least going to tell the crowd his name?” This only draws more heat from the crowd as Alexis chuckles to herself and Hister only lets it drop off his chest some more, seeming a bit annoyed. ALEXIS TERRY: “Oh no, did I make him sad. What’s he going to do, go cry to his mommy?” At that point it was too much he grabbed the mic standing up and snapping back at her. MARCUS HISTER: "Why don’t you just shut your mouth right now. I didn’t come out here to get taunted by Aspen’s newest trainee so let’s sign the damn contract so I can get that belt and end your pathetic reign as champion." This gets the crowd riled up as Alexis looks up at him stunned and offended looking at Zachary for saving as Hister grabs the clipboard off the table flipping it open not even pretending to read it as he signs it immediately. ZACHARY PAGONIS: “Hister has signed, now the champion.” Hister tosses it onto the table in front of her as Alexis looks up at him and shakes her head as he rolls out of the ring not waiting around to watch her sign it. Alexis flips it open and starts to read it through, holding the pen in one hand finally signing it. ZACHARY PAGONIS: “This match is official tune in next week to see these two battle one on one for the Legacy title.” Pagonis goes to head out of the ring but Alexis grabs the mic and turns around as Hister heads up the ramp. ALEXIS TERRY: “Listen you loser! Next week you are going to be made example of that I am the real deal and you’ll be recognized as the first person to fall at my feet for this Legacy title.” With that Alexis dropped the microphone holding up her belt as Hister turned around and back pedaled up the ramp nodding his head both seeming ready. RILEY STEVENS: “That’s going to be a great match next week, and I got to admit this thing went over well all things considered. We’ll see how the rest of the show goes without Blake.” As Hister heads to the back, done talking Alexis now heads on out as well showing off her belt the entire time. QUOTE As we return and are now prepared for the Open Battle Royal as usual we find ourselves met with three different superstars in the ring. Cassidy Hightower, Laurel Lane, and Rollo Durand. The fans seem to be intrigued by all of them as they all are in different corners ready to go. RILEY STEVENS: “Three new stars tonight, let’s see what they got. The rules will be Over the Top Rope, this week.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “More women? Good lord. That one doesn’t even look legal either.” The referee steps in between the three ready to go, as he turns to them all then calls for the bell. DING DING DING! Right off the bat the two girls in the match Cassidy and Laurel head to the middle of the ring seeming to be exchanging some words. Rollo sits back in the corner as both look over at him, as if to see if he wants in but he holds out his hand telling them to go ahead. With that the two girls go straight into a lock up, the two having very different frames. While Cassidy is lanky, Laurel has a better vertical base and the strength advantage is shown as Laurel starts to push her back into the ropes. The fans are into it as Lane pulls away seeming to have sent her message loud and clear. But as she takes a few steps back Cassidy shows why you never let your guard down as she heads forward hitting a punch that catches Lane way off guard. Laurel responds with a bit of a wild left hook that sets her right up for a Hightower, Snapmare Driver that plants Laurel. But Lane doesn’t hesitate as she gets up, stumbling into the corner as Rollo moves slightly to the side. RILEY STEVENS: “Cassidy is getting it going right off the bat.” Now Hightower being the show off that she can be at times sets up a Handspring Back Elbow. She finishes the Handspring but as she lands on her feet Rollo steps in with a smile on his face locking her into a Full Nelson and then hoisting her up and slamming her down. The fans boo him at first but as he gives them a “I had to do it.” look, they ease up on him. But as he goes to reach down for Cassidy, Laurel heads up behind him hitting him with an Elbow Smash to his back. Durand stumbles forward, spinning around as she goes after him only to walk right into a T-Bone Suplex. The fans are into it now, some picking sides most of them just enjoying the match. RILEY STEVENS: “Honestly, all three seem like they could last around here.” Rollo gets up just as Cassidy is doing the same as he bounces off the ropes and heads for her but the noise alerts her and she turns around and does a Matrix Evasion to dodge his clothesline attempt. He stops on a dime but as he turns around she has already used her speed to bounce off the ropes and meet him with a Spear her momentum taking her through him. These fans are on there feet now as Cassidy gets up, pulling Rollo with him now trying to throw him over. She gets one of his legs over, but he comes to his senses in time and pushes her away just in time. Cassidy quickly reacts running back at him only to get nailed with a kick to the stomach that sets up a DDT. But suddenly there is some commotion as a man jumps out of the crowd, sliding into the ring and as we get a glimpse Riley figures out who it is. RILEY STEVENS: “That’s Paul Defias!” These fans, many who have been with them from the start recognize him as he slides in and as Rollo has his back turned to him he slides in and grabs him from behind and hurls him over the ropes Durand not expecting it at all as he goes to the outside his night over with. Rollo Durand is Eliminated! RILEY STEVENS: “Now we see some signs of Blake’s absence.” Rollo gets up mad as he looks up into the ring at Defias who simply smirks and then slides out of the ring heading for the barricade. Durand just misses him and he runs off. Back in the ring Cassidy is watching this all and it allows Laurel to crushes her in the back with a big Forearm. Hightower falls to one knee and then is lifted into a Powerslam, Lane having an easy time throwing Cassidy around. On the outside of the ring Rollo has started to head to the back, upset with what happened but realizing he couldn’t chase Defias forever. RILEY STEVENS: “These two are going to battle.” Laurel took a second to taunt her a bit, seeming to have a lot of self-confidence. She hoists up Cassidy with a bit too much confidence when out of no where Cassidy hits a ‘Canada’s Calling’ a Ranhei that makes the crowd go crazy, loving the move as Hightower gets up stumbling away hitting the move out of no where. She goes to grab Laurel but she is out, so she tries her hardest to hoist her up essentially picking up dead weight. RILEY STEVENS: “Can she get her over the top rope?” Cassidy gets her up, with a lot of effort and then starts to head towards the ropes but as she pulls her over there Lane snaps to her senses just as Cassidy tries to throw her over and she reverses it around out of no where throwing her over the top rope to the outside out of no where. JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…Laurel Lane!” The fans give a mixed back, some clearly behind Cassidy in this one as Laurel stumbles back and holds up her hands in victory when for the second time tonight Defias jumps the barricade sliding into the ring and hitting her with a Running Jumping Knee that catches her right on the chin. RILEY STEVENS: “What the hell is Defias doing out here!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Making a statement.” Rollo runs out from the back heading to the ring ending Defias celebration early as Paul slides out and jumps the barricade yet again but as this is happening Riley gets to his feet with a microphone. RILEY STEVENS: “Listen up, listen up.” Rollo turns as Laurel in the ring gets to her feet, holding her chin. Cassidy now long gone by now. RILEY STEVENS: “Some of us guys have been given a little bit of pull with matches, being a former wrestler myself I have dealt with situations like this all the time. Next week on the Revolt Supershow it’ll be Laurel Lane, Rollo Durand, and Paul Defias in a Triple Threat Match.” The fans go wild as Rollo looks over at Defias with a smile as Paul just continues to run his mouth as he back pedals out as Lane gets to her feet her first win spoiled by Defias but now with a chance for revenge. WINNER: LAUREL LANE The aftermath of the Open Battle Royal has given us a winner and as they complete their celebrations they make their way backstage, leaving the camera and our eyes to concentrate on the GIW commentary team. RILEY STEVENS: “You have to admit that was a great opening contest. And we're not stopping there folks, tonight you're going to see some amazing matches between some of the best that GIW has to offer. The boss may be absent but our talent is definitely here and raring to go.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Good match or not, you're hyping it up as if it was the most amazing thing you've ever seen. Holy shit, how biased can you get?” RILEY STEVENS: “Is it a problem for you that I actually really like my job?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Maybe.” RILEY STEVENS: “You're detracting from the fact that even without the management, we've still got some of the best wrestling going on and I for one... Hold on. It appears as if there is some sort of commotion going on in the crowd, someone appears to be trying to gain entry to the ring area.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Well damn, let's get a camera on it!” Immediately we switch views to a shot of the GIW crowd and just as reported, there is a disturbance within the crowd itself. An individual is making their way to the front row before arguing with one of the security team before brushing him aside. The individual leaps the barrier and makes their way to the ring before removing their hooded top and revealing themselves to be a man. The man calls out for a microphone to be handed to him and as he waits for it to happen he paces the ring in an aggressive manner. RILEY STEVENS: “That's certainly not one of our guys.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Of course not, our security team aren't dumb enough to mistake this thing for a GIW wrestler.” The man in the middle of the ring has now received his microphone and puts it close to his mouth. Some of the fans in the crowd recognize this individual and begin cheering, others are merely awaiting what the man has to say. ???: “For those of you who don't know me, my name is Keaton Saint and I am an APW wrestler. Don't get your hopes up though, I'm not here to start an invasion. I am here for an important reason though so if you'll bear with me, I have something to say about GIW.” The crowd have a somewhat mixed reaction to this. KEATON SAINT:: “A couple of weeks back, I made my official debut as an APW wrestler. Now when this happened I was given a match against one of the standout wrestlers in our sport. When I entered the match and when I made my way to the ring it was under the impression that I would have a fair and emphatic contest. For some time during that match that was the case. I was there in full knowledge that the match I was having was going to be something the fans of APW, the fans of wrestling would talk about for a long time to come. But then there was the interruption...” Saint pauses, taking in the reaction from the crowd. The majority seem intent on listening instead of displaying an immediate reaction. KEATON SAINT: “What I'm talking about was a couple of the esteemed, I use that term loosely, wrestlers from GIW. Like I said, I was having one of the better matches of my career at that point so once those two harpies made their presence known they helped corrupt the purity of the match. Aspen Chaud and Alexis Terry wanted, no, needed to make an impact on APW television so they picked the moment where the true fans of wrestling would all be watching. By doing so they invoked the hatred and spite of those very same fans. They took away something real, something tangible. They took away a chance to have a true, complete match for the ages and I let it slide.” Saint shakes his head at his last sentence, some of the crowd booed the mention of Aspen and Alexis but once again the majority are letting this continue on. KEATON SAINT: “As I said, I let the whole thing slide. You see, APW has a big match coming up which I'm sure you're all aware of. This big match has been responsible for some of the toughest tests of wrestling prowess since the very beginning of our sport. A match like this, a BIG occasion like this one is always bound to throw up a few people who want or need to make themselves heard. I let it slide because I wanted to believe that Aspen and Alexis were entitled to make a mistake. That was my mistake. The week after, I took on another prime wrestler in APW and had another chance to prove my worth within APW. Aspen and Alexis made their presence felt once again, this time by screwing me out of a legitimate finish to my match. I've spent the last four years trying to make a career out of being someone who is a definitive wrestler but to be faced with something as ugly as a win via DQ, it sickens me. It sickens me that they needed to make themselves noticed at the expense of another, at MY expense. That mistake is one that shall not go unpunished!” Saint clenches his fist and paces the length of the ring. Some of the crowd are supporting Saint's statements more vigorously now however the majority continue to wait out the speech. KEATON SAINT: “That is why I am here in this ring, only a few days away from APW Survive and Conquer. We're days away from the match where a bunch of your fabled GIW stars making their way to the APW ring. There they will compete in one of the most grueling matches ever devised for the chance to hold half a million US dollars in their hands. That is the moment where Aspen, Alexis and all of your GIW stars will face the punishment that I have to bring. The mistake was made when I was targeted and now with both of those harpies trying to assault what I create in the ring, I now have my chance to destroy ANY chance they might have had at holding all that money in their hands. I'm not here for an invasion, I'm here to prove a point and I am here to prove that just like the security team that couldn't hold me back, the ring crew that GAVE an outsider the time and chance to say whatever the hell he wanted and the fans who settle for this second-rate excuse for a promotion. You're all responsible for this and you're all going to bear witness to the retribution at Survive and Conquer, LIVE on PPV!” Saint lowers his microphone and maintains a stern face in spite of the adverse reaction of the crowd. At the derogatory mention of the fans, the reaction was immense and now the crowd in full are booing Saint with animosity unlike anything else. KEATON SAINT: “Fans of GIW, you have a chance to take on board what I say and recognize the truth of it. The longer you settle for mediocrity is the longer you settle for bland, unappetizing wrestling. Why settle for a burger when you can have steak? This Sunday on PPV, you get to witness the truth about GIW and the whole truth about Aspen and Alexis. For all that they've accomplished here and for all the statements they tried to make in APW it all comes down to one thing. They are now MY targets. I want you all to watch Survive and Conquer and I want you to witness the time counting down until my entry, it wouldn't matter if I was first, last or anything in-between but my entry is the death knell for any and all GIW wrestlers. That is if they even survive that long. But that is Sunday, this is Thursday. And what I'm going to do is take a cue from your management and do the one thing you should all be doing. I'm getting the hell out of here because where I wrestle things aren't second-rate, they're ACTION PACKED!” Saint throws down the microphone and exits the ring to a chorus of boos. In spite of the leanings of Aspen Chaud and Alexis Terry, the fans recognize the need for a united front and they unanimously boo the outsider from APW. As he leaps over the barrier again the crowd greet him with a chant of "GIW, GIW, GIW!" Saint ignores this and makes his way to the nearest exit. RILEY STEVENS: “Strong, strong words from APW wrestler Keaton Saint there.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Whose idea was it to give him a microphone anyhow?” RILEY STEVENS: “If I knew I'd tell you, but what he mentioned was important. We've got a contingent of wrestlers in the upcoming Survive and Conquer match and they all have a chance at taking home the victory and the prize fund. APW will be airing this live on pay per view this Sunday but if you're in the area you can show your support for GIW at the event or at a live screening. Half a million dollars on the line and GIW have some prime athletes in position to take home the victory. This Sunday, you can cheer on our contingent of wrestlers in that match.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Or you can just boo whoever that guy was.” ==== After that segment ends we head to the backstage area where the fans are greeted with the sad looking face of Cassidy Hightower as the camera pans out we see Trent Lost come into the picture. CASSIDY HIGHTOWER: “Can’t believe I lost.” Cassidy has a frown on her face, but Trent pats her on the back trying to console her. TRENT LOST: “Listen it’s your first match and you got caught. Happens to the best of us, Over the Top Rope matches are always tricky. Besides I just got word that next week you and I are teaming up…” Trent grins as she looks over at him a little intrigued at the prospects of the match. CASSIDY HIGHTOWER: "Who we facing?" Cassidy smiles and just like that Trent’s expression changes to a sour one as walking up to them is the current GIW Tag Team Champions, Beck Ramsey and Seth Ramsey both wearing smirks. BECK RAMSEY: “That…would be us.” Beck grins as he hoists up his title, always liking to show it off as Cassidy looks over at him, history already formed between the three. TRENT LOST: “Yup…So at least we get a gimme’ right?” Cassidy seemed not to want to get involved it at all, as she stayed quiet as Beck replied. BECK RAMSEY: “After I beat you tonight, I think you’d be lucky to make it to next week.” Seth stood there quietly feeling just as awkward as Cassidy did as Trent and Beck did as they got into each other’s faces. TRENT LOST: “This time the headlines won’t be Beck Ramsey Joins Alexis and Aspen it’ll be Beck Ramsey Runs Scared Out of GIW.. See you out there…” With that Trent and Beck walked off in separate directions both too angry and pumped to stay leaving Seth and Cassidy alone. CASSIDY HIGHTOWER: "Hi?" Cassidy waves at him as the two stand there awkwardly together. SETH RAMSEY: “Hey…So…I have a match.” Seth waves and heads on his way as Cassidy looks around going from surrounded to alone in a matter of seconds. QUOTE After the break we have Konvict already waiting in the ring when “Start The Show” by Jet hits and the fans go wild. They may not be a fan of Beck, but they sure like them some Seth Ramsey as he heads out, heading to the ring high fiving his fans all along the way his title strapped around his waist. RILEY STEVENS: “Seth Ramsey is fighting by himself here tonight, his brother fighting later tonight in the Main Event.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “…Such a shame Beck teams with this guy.” Seth heads into the ring with a smile on his face posing with his belt then handing it over to the referee who hands it out and then starts the match. DING DING DING! These fans are ready to go as Konvict starts to run at Seth but Ramsey holds up his hands telling him to stop calling for a timeout. Konvict stops asking “What?!” as Beck pretends to hold his gut then kicks him in the gut doubling him over setting him right for the ‘^Q!’ [Carrot-Q-Exclamation-Point] an Asai DDT that lands flush. Konvict seems out of it as Seth heads over to the corner, shrugging his shoulders as the crowd cheers him on. Heading up the ropes the fans are on there feet as he does one jump then another jumping backwards with ‘The Broski Backflip’ his version of the Best Moonsault Ever landing right on Konvict, hooking his legs 1…2…3! JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…Seth Ramsey!” The fans jump to there feet as Seth gets up as well hoisting up an arm just adding a big finish to his highlight reel. RILEY STEVENS: “Seth wasted little time proving himself here tonight with a quick finish.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Ugh, Beck’s still better.” Seth takes his belt and slides out of the ring heading over to the fans to celebrate with them as we take a commercial break. WINNER: SETH RAMSEY “Grand Central” began to play over the sound system and soon Alioth Starre walked out to the entry way, a microphone in his right hand. He gazed out to the crowd that cheered his entrance into the arena despite knowing he was not there for a match. Alioth seemed content to speak at the entry way, but the crowd’s cheer coaxed him to enter the ring where he would have more control over the situation and be able to see all around him as he spoke. ALIOTH STARRE: “I don’t know why I’m still surprised by the reaction from all of you when I enter this ring. Perhaps it’s the ever humble individual inside of me. I’m still so young, and still have a career ahead of me that could trump all I’ve done in Galveston Island Wrestling so far, but yet I’m treated like legend as it is. I’m no legend though, at least not yet.” He spoke. Then added, after giving the crowd some time to simmer. ALIOTH STARRE: “With violent words and empty threats there are those that wish to take this back into dust and graveled voices, but the star risen up. Of course, I talk about Altair and the newly resurrected monster within Johnny Scar, which I always knew was there, even when he fought for all of you in his first tenure within this Galveston ring. It’s not just them whom threaten the integrity of this ring, and the beloved entertainment you all enjoy. As long as Starre holds this ring they’ll never see it breaking, but the question isn’t of them, but the future in which we reside. What is the future of Galveston Island Wrestling. Of Alioth Starre?” It was a rhetorical question that he had readied an answer for the crowds and thus fed it to them a beat, or two after. ALIOTH STARRE: “I can’t say much for the future of Galveston Island Wrestling. I know it will prosper further and grow as long as all of you still hold interest in being here and participating in each Thursday Night Revolt show, as well as our Super shows. It’s all of you that are why we’re here and all of you as to why we’ve entered the Survive and Conquer competition within APW. I expect to win, but that isn’t the arrogance in me talking, I consider any GIW wrestler winning that match to be a personal victory, because of the pride in this company. If I am the one to literally win the match, then I’ll be glad for it, but a GIW individual would be better overall.” The tables then turned on the topic at hand and Alioth wanted to get one thing off of his chest before he departed for the back. ALIOTH STARRE: “Trent Lost.” Just uttering this name made the crowd react with jeers and other mixed shouts, but overall they knew of the history that stood between the two. “We had a single tag match last week and it went pretty well. I noticed you seemed to have a hard time actually caving in to tag me into the match. I don’t know exactly what it is you hold over me, because I don’t care about you anymore. Remember, Trent, I’ve beaten you more times than you can count and I agreed to do something different with you, but instead I come to the back to find you running your mouth about how you’d rather defeat me than work with me. I also heard you had other plans in mind, yet none of them seem to be happening, well, isn’t that a surprise folks? Trent Lost shifts focus from one thing to another, to another, to something that turns out to bite him in back in the arse. That’s your problem, Trent. You lack focus. I wished you well in your return, and still do, considering I know you have a main event match tonight, but I don’t see you as a threat, nor competition. Any words I hear from you are simply going to roll off my chest. I’m working from the bottom of the totem pole again, but so are you, and yet everyone here knows that if you and I were to face again, it would be nothing more than a squash match of Alioth Starre versus a jobber. You’ve got bigger fish to fry, Trent, so I’d consider focusing on that and not on me. Good luck, or else.” With that said, Alioth exited the ring and traveled up the aisle. ==== Heading else where to the back we see Tony Miranda walking through the halls with a purpose as he storms through the halls when he comes upon Joshua Acquin standing by a wall texting. TONY MIRANDA: “Where the hell is Aspen?” Joshua shrugs his shoulders but Tony grabs him by the collar of his shirt pushing him against the wall. TONY MIRANDA: “I said where is she!” Acquin answers sternly while pushing his arm away. JOSHUA ACQUIN: “She’s not here tonight, Jesus.” Tony looks around confused as to why she wouldn’t be but as Joshua walks off to avoid him Miranda hears a voice behind him. SCAR: “You feel your relevance, slipping away.” But as Tony spins around to confront Scar he is too late as he’s already gone as Tony looks around. So much anger yet nobody to take it out on. QUOTE The area lights darkened and then raised back with a crimson hue. This, doubled with a modified ‘Man That You Fear’ that played through the sound system signaled the entrance of Scar and Altair. Scar, adorned in his black, horn rimmed mask lead the way toward the ring where Momma’s Pride had already entered prior to them. RILEY STEVENS: “With the tag team division’s recent return, the division is in need of contenders the winner of this match might find themselves next in line.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Vampires and black people?” Scar took a hold of the top rope and pulled himself up, then stepped through and into the ring. Altair ascended the steps and walked along the rim to where their tag corner would be and waited there. He played little role in their entrance aside from Scar handing the mask to him to be placed by ringside. DING DING DING! As the bell rang, Scar knocked Istanbul down with a large right hook to the cheek and then pulled him back up by the hair and performed a throat thrust. As Istanbul staggered back, turned away from Scar, his opponent wrapped his arms around his head and neck and pulled him into a sleeper hold. One hand maneuvered to grasp at Istanbul’s neck like a choke that transitioned from the submission maneuver into a hard choke slam into the canvas. Scar had a sinister smirk that scratched itself across his face and he turned toward Angel, then back down at Istanbul. He reached down to drag his opponent by the fro toward his corner and then threw Istanbul’s back against the buckles and tagged Altair into the match. Altair tucked under the top rope and into the ring to throw a methodical row of kicks toward Istanbul’s abdomen. Altair’s own abdomen seemed pained as he struck, five holes marking where he had hosted larvae of the botfly. The creatures seemed removed, but that didn’t seem relevant as he continued his assault on Istanbul, only to tag Scar back in after just a moment or two of in ring time. RILEY STEVENS: “Back comes Scar.” It didn’t take long for Scar to maneuver Istanbul into the arm trap neckbreaker, his finisher, and then pin his opponent for the full count. Altair crept back into his white, ruffled dress shirt and stepped toward the center of the aisle before he realized Scar was not with him. JAMAL JACKSON: “Here are your winners…Altair and Scar!” He turned to see his partner in the ring looking down on Angel overseeing her fallen partner Istanbul. Scar moved toward the ropes only to get a microphone and then returned, standing over the two. JOHNNY SCAR: “A one Tony Miranda claims that a monster never fights.” He said and then threw the microphone to his side and picked up Angel by the throat; both hands grasped at her neck and then tossed her outside of the ring onto the matted floor. He then set his sights on the referee, but the ref had fled the scene quickly after witnessing the fall of Angel Watkins. RILEY STEVENS: “These guys are sick and a real threat to the tag belts as well.” He then stepped out of the ring and just as it seemed he would join Altair down the aisle, he looked toward Istanbul, whom had started to pull himself up and then Scar slid into the ring and waited for the man to rise up to his feet. As Istanbul stood, Scar grabbed him in a choke hold and performed the same toss toward the outside of the ring as he did with Angel Watkins. With that settled, the team of Scar and Altair finally retreated from ringside leaving a message behind for their detractors, especially a one Tony Miranda. WINNERS: ALTAIR & SCAR As we return backstage we are now greeted with the face of Alexis Terry with the ‘GIW Legacy Championship’ on her shoulder joined yet again by Zachary Pagonis. ZACHARY PAGONIS: “I am here with ‘GIW Legacy Champion’ Alexis Terry. Earlier tonight you signed the contract to face Hister next week. What were your thoughts on the signing?” Terry rolls her eyes, it had only been less then a couple hours and she was already answering questions about it. ALEXIS TERRY: “Listen, I don’t care about this guy. He is basically being fed to the wolves so that GIW can get some ratings. I don’t know why he snapped on me out there, I was just asking for his name.” Pagonis looks at her looking almost bewildered by her lack of compassion. ZACHARY PAGONIS: “Well if you didn’t know his mother passed away and you did say something about that.” Alexis looks at him and then has a chuckle, completely unhuman. ALEXIS TERRY: “Boo hoo! Life goes on. Not like I knew anyways.” Pagonis takes a deep breath trying to stay professional here. ZACHARY PAGONIS: “No sign of Aspen, where’s she at?” Alexis grins and then quickly responds. ALEXIS TERRY: “She took the night off, that’s all.” With that Terry heads on her way, as Pagonis looks to the camera trying his best to smile as we go else where. ==== Now we head to the locker room areas where we find ourselves with a familiar scene as Alex Castellanos sits in a suit with Sarah Sharp in a dress both looking dressed up for the night. SARAH SHARP: “Listen babe, I just want you to be calm about it all. You can’t let her control your emotions. If you stay calm, you can take her down. But if you get too angry you’ll make mistakes.” Alex seems a bit hard pressed to not be completely angry about the way he lost last week and the week prior but his trust in Sarah seemed to help him. SARAH SHARP: “The show’s almost over, you can drive me home afterwards.” Sarah smiles as does Alex who nods his head and then we take a commercial break before the last match of the night. QUOTE Trent Lost was first to the scene. With his theme playing over his entrance, he made his way to the ring and slid in with ease. The fans were clearly behind him in this one, having embraced him ever since his return. RILEY STEVENS: “Trent Lost has returned and thus far has done so very impressively. Is this a new age for Trent?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Seems as though, perhaps he’s got the motivation. But tonight he’ll face one half of the GIW Tag Team Champions in Beck Ramsey who has been on the tip of everyone’s tongue this week.” It was only a moment’s wait before his opponent, one half of the tag team champions, Beck Ramsey made his entrance to the ring. Alone, Beck would have to compete without Seth by the corner, nor even at ringside. The bell rang and the two squared off. DING DING DING! It started with a classic circling of competitors and the two began to narrow the gap between one another until a small lock up was formed, then broken. Another occurred and Beck tugged his opponent closer to him and then threw Trent off in a chesty display that only served to get him slapped across the face by Trent Lost. After the slap, Beck stopped and his cocky grin fade away as if he never had it. He pushed Trent and slapped him across the face as well to wipe the smirk off of his opponent’s lips. The two then got into a more serious lockup and Beck overcame Trent with a knee to the gut and transitioned into a headlock that Trent had transitioned into a whip toward the ropes. Beck bounced back and like a brick, smacked his shoulder to Trent’s to knock him flat onto the canvas. Trent rolled over to his stomach as Beck took to the ropes a second time, letting his opponent leap over him the first time so he could get up and as they met a second time, Trent ducked to hopefully toss Beck over his back, but Ramsey saw it coming and stopped inches before Trent. Trent pulled up and found himself slapped a second time across the cheek. RILEY STEVENS: “Beck is disrespecting Trent here.” This angered Trent enough to try and end the match quikc with his Midnight Call maneuver, but Beck shoved him off with a hip toss and then a DDT to seal the deal. With Trent on the canvas, Beck maneuvered him into a Fujiwara arm bar and held on tight. Trent underneath could barely move and with the ropes close, but not enough to reach, he was under the thrall of the submission hold until Beck decided he had enough and pulled off. Beck threw a sharp kick to Trent’s skull after the arm bar was broken, just for good measure. Trent rolled himself to his feet and held to the ropes. He leaned toward the center of the ring, his back against the top rope and found Beck charging with a clothesline, but instead of Trent toppling over the ropes, he had used a counter to hip toss his opponent over and onto the matted floor. Trent then took to the turn buckles near him and ascended to the top buckle and watched as Beck tried to collect himself outside of the ring. When Beck rolled onto his back, Trent leapt with a top rope leg drop, but Beck rolled out of the way, hugging the barricade as Trent crashed into the matted floor. RILEY STEVENS: “Crash and burn by Trent.” Beck rolled over with a punch to Trent’s skull and then pulled himself up the rim of the ring to reenter as Trent writhed in pain that coursed through his lower back and tailbone. The referee reached to the count of seven before Trent was back in the ring. Beck followed up the failure with an assault. He stomped away at his opponent and pulled Trent up against the ropes for an Irish whip. Beck went for his big boot finisher, but Trent dodged and ran toward the ropes again. He returned with a running backstabber. It laid both men flat on the canvas and the referee began a count. It wasn’t until five that both men started to stir and it would be Beck Ramsey to reach his knees, then his feet first as Trent was still pulling himself up. Beck pulled close his opponent and set him up for a backbreaker that laid Trent out and kept him down for Beck’s diving knee drop from the top turn buckle, coming down from Trent’s top right. Beck covered his opponent, but Trent broke it with a lifted arm. Beck shoved his opponent’s head into the canvas in frustration, but pulled off of him all the same. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Finish him off Beck!” Beck pulled Trent up to his feet and fed him a few punches and backhand chops before satisfied enough with the end result. With Trent groggy and in Beck’s grasp, Ramsey sent him toward the ropes for one final Irish whip that would serve to set up ‘The Collision’. A move that had previously been countered, and as Trent returned, he dodged again! He maneuvered behind Beck and applied his own finisher, ‘Midnight Call’ to end the match on a full three count! With Beck pinned and the match over, Trent rolled off his opponent and took in a few needed breaths of air before he rose up as the victor of Thursday Night Revolt’s main event of the night. JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…Trent Lost!” The fans go wild with cheers as Trent hops to his feet holding up his arms in victory having taken this hard fought victory here tonight in what was a truly worthy Main Event. But Ramsey was quick to get up and was not about to let Trent have his moment as he ran forward and nailed him to the ground from behind. RILEY STEVENS: “You had to figure this would happen.” Ramsey starts to beat him down as Cassidy Hightower heads out from the back alongside Seth Ramsey both heading to the ring as Trent now rolls on top the two now just throwing punches with intent to hurt one another. Seth and Cassidy slide into the ring both heading for each man to hold them apart. Cassidy pushes Trent away as does Seth, but Beck pushes away his brother and throws a punch at Lost. But his natural reaction is to duck out of the way and the punch nearly hits Cassidy but Beck is able to pull back on it at the last second. RILEY STEVENS: “He nearly punched Cassidy there!” Cassidy looks stunned which allows Trent to tackle Beck to the mat and throw some more punches finally Seth pulling Beck away as he now has some blood down his nose and Trent has some blood coming from his lip. RILEY STEVENS: “This show has been pandemonium to say the least, we are not done yet we will be right back.” With that Trent rolls out of the ring with Cassidy who tries to keep him from sliding back in while Seth does the same with Beck as these two hatred seems to have turned up to the next level as we take a break. WINNER: TRENT LOST The night seemed to be coming to a close as we come back from break when suddenly the lights dim and we hear the sounds of an Owl as out from the back comes David Sinclair with his signature owl on his shoulder adorning a black suit as he heads towards the ring to perhaps the loudest boos we’ve ever heard here in GIW. No music, no nothing just the echoing of these boos. RILEY STEVENS: “What in the world…” Sinclair heads confidently towards the ring heading up the steps and into the ring as the fans continue to boo. He asks for a mic as Jamal at ringside looks around confused then hands it in to him just as confused as everyone. The boos are deafening as Sinclair walks to the center of the ring with no expression on his face, now wielding a mic. He waits for the boos to die down, to begin. DAVID SINCLAIR: “Hello…Everyone.” Just that is enough to piss the crowd off again as they let out even more boos as Sinclair now lets out a grin. DAVID SINCLAIR: “By now you are all probably starting to put some things together. Who hit Beckett last week? Well that was unfortunately my Driver who made a fatal error. But regardless I come back here tonight, looking at a show in need of serious leadership. That’s not to say that Blake was any good at his job anyways. However I understand that they are looking for a replacement. I am putting that to an end right now.” Sinclair grins a bit as he pulls out a piece of paper from his front pocket flipping it open. DAVID SINCLAIR: “You know Blake was always good at providing some forms of excitement. But what he was never good at was the business side of things. TV deals, expansion, and most importantly paper work. When you create a company you have a back up plan in case something happens to the Boss. And you name a…Replacement. You see this here is GIW’s back up document something that hasn’t been updated in a long time here in GIW. Let me read it for you In the case that Blake Beckett is to be injured and unable to do his duties this company will be handed over…to David Sinclair.” With that the loudest boos ever can be heard as the fans don’t want to believe it as Sinclair drops the paper to his side as he has a big grin on his face. DAVID SINCLAIR: “Things are GOING TO CHANGE around here. This company is going to get bigger and we are going to leave Galveston. We are going to become global; we are going to be bigger then ever. Blake’s gone and he’s not coming back for a long time. So consider this the start of a new GIW…run by David Sinclair! And you see none of this would have been possible without…the person on the inside. Aspen Chaud.” The boos only double as now Aspen Chaud emerges from the back heading to the ring with the biggest smile on her face ever holding the Undisputed title over her shoulder as she walks down the ramp and up into the ring wearing a red dress as she steps into the ring and immediately walks over to David embracing in a hug that these fans boo even more. As they break apart Aspen now has the mic as she looks out at the crowd with the biggest smile. ASPEN CHAUD: “WE DID IT! WE DID IT! Blake is gone! Blake is gone! YES! YES! YES!” This only aggravates the fans more as Aspen jumps up and down in the center of the ring as David nods his head and takes back the microphone. DAVID SINCLAIR: “Things are going to change around here.” Seeming to want that to be the end Sinclair instead is cut off as Alex Castellanos now enters from the back with Sarah Sharp behind him already holding a live mic as he heads to the ring and starts to talk over the fans ruckus cheers for him. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “Really? Now I may not have liked everything Blake was doing. But I understand that he still cares more about this company then you ever will Sinclair. I may not have been here when you were, but I have heard plenty of the horror stories. And I am not going to sit around and let the two of you run this place into ground with your biased anarchy.” Alex heads into the ring but before he can continue he now is cut off by another mic this one being Tony Miranda’s who now heads out from the back as well as Aspen tucks her tail and heads back into the corner. TONY MIRANDA: “Night off? I knew that was too good to be true. Sinclair, I WAS HERE when you tried to take this place over and if you recall it was me that put the nail in the coffin to your crappy federation. Listen I saw what you do first hand and I could care less about it all right now.” Tony now enters the ring with anger in his eyes as he points past Sinclair to Aspen in the corner. TONY MIRANDA: “I want her in a match for that belt. That’s all I want, and you can do whatever you want with the place.” Aspen shakes her head no as Alex looks over at Tony walking towards him trying to bring some unity together. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “Listen we can’t let gold get in the way of what the real issue here is and that’s you Sinclair. If you think I am just going to play this game you are wrong, you are way wrong.” Suddenly it seems Sinclair has an idea as he looks at them both and then starts up. DAVID SINCLAIR: “Cut their mics, right now.” Now with documents to prove his power the workers are much more quick to the trigger, flicking off their mics as both men try to talk into it until they realize they are dead as Tony tosses his out of the ring. DAVID SINCLAIR: “You both want a shot at her right? Well to prove I care what the fans think we’ll let them decide who gets the next shot.” Both men seem confused as Sinclair points to Alex and then talks. DAVID SINCLAIR: “If you want Alex Castellanos to get the shot, scream.” Sinclair waits but Alex waves his hands no to stop the crowd not wanting the subject changed here tonight and it significantly lowers his cheers as they try to listen to him. DAVID SINCLAIR: “Now if you want Tony Miranda to get the shot, scream.” So badly wanting his shot Tony calls for the crowd and they go wild for him clearly beating out Alex as Castellanos sighs not wanting them to get sucked into his game. DAVID SINCLAIR: “Next week it’ll be Alioth Starre teaming with Aspen Chaud versus Tony Miranda and Alex Castellanos in the Main Event.” The fans seem confused at the quick subject change as Sinclair heads out of the ring with Aspen following him as Tony and Alex look at one another but David stops as he gets to the top of the ramp. DAVID SINCLAIR: “Oh yeah and the title shot goes to Alex.” These fans seem confused, while Tony seems more mad then anything as Sinclair and Aspen head to the back as Alex lowers his head confused but now Tony turns his head over to Castellanos placing the blame firmly on his shoulders suddenly screaming out “That’s my SHOT!” as the show slowly fades with Tony angrily screaming into his ear as Alex looks over at him with not much to say just like most people after the return of David Sinclair.
