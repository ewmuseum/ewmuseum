GIW — Card

Extreme TV, 7 July 2011

7 July 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: ETV LIVE! From Stewart Beach – July 7th, 2011 ETV starts off with five men in ring with one of them being Richard Youngblood on the microphone. RICHARD YOUNGBLOOD: “Ladies and gentleman, I am glad we now have all of your attention here at ETV. I want you all to know about a little something going on here. As you all know, Anthony Brenske has left us for the IFL which leaves a spot open here. I have been trying to find the right talent to replace him, but why should I have all the fun. Why don't we have the public watch some great wrestlers and decide who they want to see. I brought this idea up to Blake Beckett and he absolutely loved it. One of the four men you see in this ring right now will be under a brand new contract here at Galveston Island Wrestling.” The crowd cheers loudly about the idea of their voices being heard as Youngblood passes the microphone to the man with a large black mohawk and wearing a baggy Ghostbusters t-shirt. He whispers something in his ear for a second before stepping a side. SHOCK COLLAR: “My name is Andrew “Shock Collar” Gibson. Some of you may know me from a little punk rock band called Popcorn Fairy from Southern California. To make things clear, I am not here for my music career. I am here because I love professional wrestling and I wanted to be one ever since I was a little kid and this is an amazing opportunity for me to join one of the biggest up and coming companies in the United States today. Woooooooo!” Shock Collar puts up two devil horns up from both of his arms as he walks back to the line up while he passes the microphone to the nearly seven foot tall man in the black and red suit. The man breathes heavily into the microphone. CRYSIS: “I want everyone to know one thing clear. All of these men are great athlettes, but there is one man that stands above more than any of us. This person is me, Crysis. I am 23 years old and I have the look of of an athlete. I was an all-conference basketball superstar at Florida International. I won a championship at every wrestling organization I have been in so far and I haven't even reached my prime. None of these men can take that away from me. I am a diamond chiseled out of gold.” The large man smirks with confidence as he passes the microphone off to the smallest man out of all four of them. He looked like he was ready to wrestle with all of his ring gear on. He hold on to the microphone and drops it for a second before picking it up. He looks very nervous as he stutters over his words. TESORO “Les voy a contar la historia de un niño que luchó durante 16 años antes de América decidieron darle un tiro. Quiero que todos conocemos la historia de tesoro. Esta es una historia que seguirá aquí en Texas y vivirá. Puede que sea la más pequeña y más antigua uno entre nosotros, pero tengo por mucho el corazón de la mayoría, la experiencia y la pasión por el anillo.” The crowd gives mixed reaction as he smiles brightly. The man wear the designer sunglasses and big black hoodie didn't seem as impressed as he walks up to the masked wrestler and takes away his microphone. ANTONIO NAPPA “No body understood a damn thing you said there hombre. So, why don't you take your little cholo ass back over there and let the winner of this competition speak on this. This competition is a joke, you are a joke, and ,Richard, you are an idiot if you don't see the potential in me right now to be the champion here.” Tesoro didn't seem to enjoy those words to much as he tried to get in the face of Nappa. TESORO “Know this, just because I speak the language of my people doesn't mean I do not know what you are saying.” ANTONIO NAPPA “Are you trying to make something of it jumping bean?” The two are staring at each other down and tensions begin to raise. Richard comes in and takes the microphone away from the men. RICHARD YOUNGBLOOD: “Whoa there kids, lets not get carried away here. I know you guys want to prove yourselves worthy, but we need to do things according to the plan. Next week the plan says that all four of you men will be in action. Crysis and Antonio Nappa will face Shock Collar and Tesoro in tag team action.” Crysis and Shock Collar claps there hands at the idea, but Nappa and Tesoro don't take their eyes off of each other. QUOTE Opener In the Opener we have as usual the ETV Open Battle Royal! This week we saw three stars competing. Amber Stone, DeShawn Banks, and Logan Abbott were all ready to go. Though it was an Over the Top Rope Battle Royal, the match started more like a triple threat. The fans were solidly behind Amber Stone the lone female in the bunch and she didn’t disappoint fighting with a strong will against the boys. At the end of the match with Banks momentarily out of it Stone and Abbott found themselves fighting on the ring apron as easy pickings. Both trying to balance while throwing punches, Logan had Amber reeling when off the ropes came Banks using both feet to knock them off while they were already teetering getting himself a huge first win here on ETV and surely sending him up to Revolt. WINNER: DESHAWN BANKS QUOTE Mid-Card Match The next tag match was one added late in the week between The Darkness and Followers of the Lost. It was Waters and Hister known by any one that followed Marcus Hister’s career that were the fan favorites in this one. The match was a clinic with Waters dominating before making tags to Angela for a rest. Slowly but surely they demoralized with solid tags, the Followers rarely getting any sort of offense in the match. Finally the domination ended when Angela was able to dropkick Dream off the ropes while Waters nailed Omega with a ‘Kings Spear’ to end the match leading to a celebration from two of the four members of The Darkness. WINNERS: THE DARKNESS [Angela Hister & Jack Waters] QUOTE Main Event Before the main event it was announced that Hank Mardukas would fight alone when Wallace Colton did not show up for the event. With the fans behind him Hank seemed determined to do it alone. To many peoples surprise he did just that, showing why many consider him to have one of the biggest hearts in GIW. Using tags The Killers tried to take him out, but to no avail. Finally at the end of the match upset by the way things had gone The Killers jumped into the ring to try and double team Hank. However with both lined up he only decided to kill two birds with one stone crushing both with a ‘Pitbull Attack’ a Charging Clothesline that took em’ both down. Quickly he covered Nelson, and got the one two three in an improbable Handicap Match win. WINNER: HANK MARDUKAS
