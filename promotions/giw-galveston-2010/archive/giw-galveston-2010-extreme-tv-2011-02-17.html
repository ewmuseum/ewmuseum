Extreme TV, 17 February 2011

GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: EXTREME TV! LIVE FROM THE GALVESTON ISD GYM QUOTE Opener The first match of the ETV Broadcast was the Open Battle Royal. The wrestlers who ended up showing up were Reyna Silva a small seventeen year old whom got a big pop from the crowd. Trent Arianna was her opponent for the night, and he got mass amounts of boos mostly due to the fact the fans had decided early they would be firmly behind Reyna Silva. On paper this match was not going to be close. However once the fight started it was Reyna who brought the fight to Trent, surprising him and everyone in attendance. Early on a Hurricanrana from the top rope by Silva captivated the fans. But not long after it was Trent who began to over power her. Finally the match became a bit of a beating, before finishing her with the ‘Trent Express Mail’ a Running Leg Lariat that sent Silva over the top rope for the ring, securing a win for Trent. WINNER: TRENT ARIANNA QUOTE Mid-Card Before the second match started Colt Cox entered the ring to announce that the originally scheduled six man tag match would now be changed from that to a Six Pack Challenge for a Match on Revolt the following week. Right away it was Omen who was the stand out, impressing the fans with an all around assault on everybody. Bala Devil and Christina Marie were fighting the entire time as Omen completely dominated the other three guys. In the end he took Aries out of the ring with a Hurricanrana. Then followed it up with an Enzuigiri to Fury whom was on the top rope. And last but not least he finished off Danger with ‘The Blessing In Disguise’ a Flip Double Arm DDT that got a pop from the crowd. He hooked the leg and got the pin fall, assuring that next week he would get a shot in the big leagues while the rest of the stars would have to go through the Open Battle Royal to earn their spot. WINNER: OMEN QUOTE Main Event The main event of the night featured the much hated Killers going against the newly formed NXTgen. The match was easily the most anticipated of the night and ended up a tightly contested match up with Marcus Hister seeming like a man among boys. However every time he tagged out it seemed as though The Killers started to gain some momentum. Though The Killers had Harley beaten nearly a few times Hister was always there to bail him out. However it was Harley who made the tag that allowed Hister into the match. He caught Fielder and tossed him over the ropes before giving Brody’s chin a Superkick that sent him to sleep as he got on top for the one, two, three. After the match however Marcus shocked the fans by giving his partner a Superkick, ending NXTgen before it started as he stood tall to end ETV on perhaps the last time we’d see him there for awhile. WINNER: MARCUS HISTER & HANDSOME HARLEY
