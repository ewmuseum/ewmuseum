GIW — Card

Pleasure Pier, 25 August 2019

25 August 2019
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Live! From the Pleasure Pier! Sunday August 25th, 2019 - Galveston, TX [pic] We are back at the Pleasure Pier for another North Beach Championship defense. After the opening video package, we pan around to view a surprisingly bright blue Galveston shoreline and then zoom in on the Pier which is packed with GIW fans. Music plays in the background as we hear the announce team speak over more panning shots and still images, promoting the show. Riley Stevens: Ladies and gentlemen, with Revolt in an off week, we are here Live from the Pleasure Pier to see Riley Savell defend her North Beach Championship! We are hearing that three other wrestlers will challenge her tonight… and that match will be a tables match! Oliver Thibodaux: You never know what exactly you’re going to get when you tune in to this show Riley, and the prospect of the tables match is one that really gets me excited. Tonight we are going to see Aaron Pace, Johnny Violence, Agustin Gates, and, of course, Riley Savell step into that ring, and only one of them will walk out as the North Beach Champion. Riley Stevens: Of course we are still reeling from the end of Revolt: Revival. A long night of professional wrestling was net to be culminated with Moxie James and Bronx Valescence battling it out to call themselves Undisputed Champion, but it was Aaron Pace who left a stain on the main event by tossing both of them out AFTER he was eliminated, with Bronx managing to hold on just a few seconds longer than Moxie. Oliver Thibodaux: I’m sure the owner of GIW, Aspen Chaud, is going to have a lot more to say about this one by next week. But at the end of the day Bronx Valescence is the Undisputed Champion. Tonight we are going to play interviews that both he and Moxie James conducted after last week’s Revolt. Riley Stevens: I’m excited to see what they have to say. I’m sure both are frustrated for very different reasons. The other competitors in this match had interesting nights as well, so let’s start with Agustin Gates. Oliver Thibodaux: He had a smart strategy for sure. He and his brothers jumped David Scott backstage right as the show started. He got an earful from Aspen Chaud, ran afoul of Xiangdao Del Toro, and then got his brothers banned from ringside. Without the help of his team, David Scott was able to make him tap out in the preliminary matches. And after pissing off Casey Holliday later in the night, it’s safe to say Gates has a huge target on his back. Riley Stevens: We also found out that Johnny Violence is wrestling on a week to week contract in GIW. Next week he faces off against Alexis Terry for a shot at a full time gig here in GIW. Oliver Thibodaux: He may be able to make that match a moot point if he picks up the win here tonight. I can’t imagine keeping a wrestler on a week to week contract when they hold a title that is a main point of the brand moving forward. And I’m sure Aspen Chaud has thought of that as well. Expect JVi to come out here tonight and really push as hard as he can. He’s hungry for the glory. Riley Stevens: Finally we have Riley Savell. She burst onto the scene two weeks ago by winning the North Beach Championship, but then fell to Moxie James in a close match at Revolt: Revival. Oliver Thibodaux: Riley will be just fine. She got caught by Moxie with a rollup, and Moxie was in a perfect state of mind to win… as we found out later. Riley, however, is at a distinct disadvantage in this match. Does she have the size and strength to put someone like Aaron Pace through a table…? I guess we will find out. Riley Stevens: Now, ladies and gentlemen… we are going to show you some excerpts from the interviews that Zachary Pagonis conducted with Bronx Valescence and Moxie James after the show last week. Take a look: The shot shifts to a pre-tape. We see Bronx Valescence sitting across from Pagonis, his title in his lap, barely seen. Bronx Valescence: No. Of course this isn’t what I expected. But this is what happens when you try to get the title on someone in one night. I was willing to fight for weeks to prove I was the right man for the Undisputed Championship. Instead, we got a battle royal. And when have those ever gone as planned? A moment of silence passed. Zachary Pagonis: Do you have any words for Aaron Pace? Bronx shook his head. Bronx Valescence: Aaron Pace lost the match. There’s nothing he can do or say to change that - it’s a fact. No temper tantrum… no screwjob… nothing’s gonna change it. As far as I’m concerned, Aaron Pace is at the bottom of the ladder. Even behind Alexis Terry. He has no right to be mentioned in the same breath as me and Moxie. We cut quickly over to Moxie. Moxie James: I want to kill him. Is that what you want to hear? The camera switched over Pagonis. He shrugged slightly. Moxie laughed. Moxie James: Aaron Pace took that opportunity from me. Bronx was already champion, even if none us want to admit that. He was preordained. If he lost he’d still be around the top. Me? I have no history here - nothing to fall back on. I could have gone down in history as the one who squashed the fairytale fantasy of Bronx’s return and inevitable victory. And he took that from me. He will pay for that. And when I’m done with him, I’m going to finish the job for the Undisputed Championship. We fade back to the announce table. Riley Stevens: It seems like Bronx is ready to move on. Moxie… not as much. Oliver Thibodaux: Bronx has the championship. Moxie doesn’t. “Tonight” by Sunset Neon cuts off the thought as Aaron Pace’s music hits and the crowd transitions into boos. The man that all GIW is talking about steps onto the ramp and begins making his way down to the ring. Riley Stevens: Well ladies and gentlemen, it’s time for the North Beach Championship. Out first is none other than Aaron Pace... Oliver Thibodaux: Oh come on, don’t let your voice droop like that… this man has made a name for himself. That’s exactly what we ask of our wrestlers. Riley Stevens: It’s just… what he took away from Bronx and Moxie… it makes me sick Ollie… It makes me real sick! Oliver Thibodaux: You’ve gone soft over the years… LOOK OUT! The crowd pops and the camera quickly cuts back to Pace. Moxie James has just slammed him in the back with a baseball bat! She pulls him up to his feet and then dropkicks him into the steel railing, sending Pace spilling into the fans. Riley Stevens: Moxie James is here to put Pace in his place! Moxie picks up the bat and hops over the railing, but Pace is already on his feet running into the group. Like the Red Sea, the crowd parts for them as security guards scamble to keep everyone out of the way. Riley Stevens: You can’t run from this one, Pace! You can’t run from what you deserve! Moxie nails him one more time from behind with the bat before using it to choke him. Security guards are pulling her away. Pace screams out as he tries to get her to release the hold. Oliver Thibodaux: Moxie is taking control of the narrative! The guards finally manage to pull Moxie off of Pace, and in that instant Pace scrambles to his feet and disappears into the crowd. The guards release Moxie as she holds the bat up for the crowd. Big cheers follow. Oliver Thibodaux: We’re going to try to get some control here on the Pleasure Pier. In fact… I’m not so sure Pace has many places to run that don’t involve going for a swim. We’ll be back after the break. “MOX-IE” chants break out as fade into the break. GIW North Beach Championship Match Aaron Pacevs. Agustin Gates vs. Johnny Violence vs. Riley Savell © After the attack on Aaron Pace at the hands of Moxie James - the dust has settled and Agustin Gates, Johnny Violence, and the champion Riley Savell have made their way to the ring. Aaron Pace, as it seems is out of the match entirely. Gates is out in the ring alone this week - it appears as if his brothers are still prohibited from being at ringside.The bell sounds, and we are off. Riley Stevens: Remember - Riley Savell can lose her title without being involved in the decision! Johnny Violence and Agustin Gates start the match up by going right after each other, leaving Savell in her corner. Violence pins Gates against the ring ropes and manages to land some hard shots to the gut before Gates ducks through to give himself some room. Violence rears back and nails Gates with a knee that sends him splattering to the outside, and Violence immediately gives chase. At the same time, Savell spots an opportunity and goes to the outside on the other side of the ring. Riley Stevens: It didn’t take long for the action to spill to the outside in this one! Oliver Thibodaux: And it doesn’t matter. If you get put through a table, you lose - inside or outside of the ring. Violence picks Gates up on his shoulder with the intention to slam him into the ring post, but Gates is able to squirm free. He lands on his feet behind Violence and pushes the GIW original face first into the post with a lot of power. Violence collapses to the floor in pain as Gates smiles and admires his dirty work. He turns around and runs right into a running knee from the Champion, Riley Savell! Oliver Thibodaux: Savell has mastered the art of trash talk and knee strikes. Riley is not slow to follow up. She tosses Gates into the ring and begins pulling out an unwieldy table from underneath the apron. She slides it into the ring, but Gates is on his feet waiting for her, though his knees are still pretty wobbly. He reaches over the top rope and grabs her by the hair, pulling her roughly onto the apron and shouting something at her, but she necks him on the top rope and sends him back into the ring. As Riley catches her breath, she spots Violence rising to his feet out of the corner of her eye and moves over there to keep him down so that she can focus on Gates. But Violence is ready for her and meets her with a knee to the gut, followed by a hard suplex onto the wooden pier floor. He tosses Savell into the ring before joining her and Gates inside. Riley Stevens: Is this the moment when Johnny Violence claims the North Beach Championship? Oliver Thibodaux: That would be a great accomplishment for him. But as Aspen said, Johnny has a knack for turning a good opportunity into a wasted one. Hate to the person that has to say that… but its true. Violence sets up the table in the middle of the ring and proclaims to the crowd that this is his moment and his championship, and from the looks of it he may be right. He lifts Savell onto his shoulders, thinking, and probably rightly so, that Savell will be easier to put through the table than Gates. But Savell grabs his eyes and his nose and claws for dear life. She definitely does not want her title to be taken from her this way. Violence drops her and Savell transitions into a headlock, where she originally looks for a suplex, but then settles for a DDT hard onto the mat. Riley Stevens: Not quite what Savell was looking for there, but it did some damage to Violence. Oliver Thibodaux: Every little bit helps. Gates cuts her off with a low uppercut. He mounts Riley and brags to the crowd for a bit before landing some closed fists strikes right to her pretty face. He pulls her up by the hair again and sets her down on the table before making the decision to climb to the top rope. Gates wants to win this title in style, and he thinks he can do it. He takes a moment to steady himself on the top rope, but before he can make the dive, Johnny Violence is there to cut him off. Violence makes his way to the top rope and it looks like his plan is to suplex Gates through the table, but that is going to be a big challenge because Gates is fighting like mad. Riley Stevens: That’s not the place you want to be in a tables match! In the meantime Savell slips off of the table and makes her way over to the corner where Gates and Violence are battling. She pulls Violence down and nails him with a hard elbow, bringing him to his knees. Gates jumps off of the top with an axehandle that nails her right on the head, and she quickly rolls out of the ring to get some composure. But Gates acts swiftly, he pulls Johnny up but Johnny fights back with a right hand. Tired of it all, Gates nails Johnny with a low blow! The crowd was getting behind Johnny, maybe just because he was a familiar face, and is deflated when Gates makes the move. Gates picks Violence up and hits a spinebuster through the table, defeating Johnny Violence and winning the North Beach Championship! Riley Stevens: And just like that, Agustin Gates is the North Beach Champion… I don’t think we will ever hear the end of this one! Oliver Thibodaux: More people are going to be after this kid than ever before. Let’s see how he deals with the pressure! Riley Stevens: Thank you for tuning in folks. We will see you next week on Revolt! We get one more shot of Gates lifting the title into the air. His brothers have joined him in the ring to celebrate. Boos send us home. WINNER: AGUSTIN GATES (NEW GIW NORTH BEACH CHAMPION)
