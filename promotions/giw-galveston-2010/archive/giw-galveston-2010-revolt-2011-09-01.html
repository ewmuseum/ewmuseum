GIW — Card

Revolt, 1 September 2011

1 September 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: THURSDAY NIGHT REVOLT LIVE! From Stewart Beach – September 1, 2011 BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! Our crowd of around four hundred fans are packed around the ring on the sands of Stewart Beach waiting for the action to begin. There are still murmurs in the crowd about what could have caused the fire last week, but most of the fans have pushed that to the back of their mind. The show package plays, and then we begin to broadcast live around the nation on ION Television with Blake Beckett kicking off the show in the center of the ring. He has a stern look on his face, and waits for the crowd to quiet down before he begins to speak to them. BLAKE BECKETT: “Last week’s edition of Revolt began in confusion, and ended in confusion. I am out here to - and I’m sure that you have already done this - to forgive us for this anomaly. We want to make it very clear to all of you that Galveston Island Wrestling is a safe place to enjoy yourself. We take pride in providing a risk-free entertainment for you. We are looking into what caused the fire last week at the Commodore, but I can say without a shadow of a doubt right now that no member of the GIW roster or backstage staff started it. So with this said, let’s get this show on the road, and pretend that nothing happened last week…” “Big Shit Poppin’” by TI hit’s the PA system and the crowd erupts in boos. ‘Amazing’ Ace Baldwin walks through the curtain and pauses on the ramp. He gets a microphone from a crew member on the edge, then begins to walk as he makes his way down to meet Blake Beckett. ACE BALDWIN: “This is some tight ship you are running here Blake… you know, ever since I made my debut here in Galveston Island Wrestling as a part of team MWA, I’ve been SHOCKED by the lax backstage management of the talent, among other things. Also, I find it appalling that Revolt, or any other past incarnation of a GIW show, can’t keep a general manager for more than a month and a half.” Ace slides into the ring with a furious Blake Beckett, and the crowd is booing harder than ever before. Ace knows he has struck a nerve, but continues regardless. ACE BALDWIN: “The Midwest Wrestling Alliance was a cluster from the very beginning, but even an upstart federation with poor management still was able to treat me better than a near national company like this one. Blake, you have had the General Managers, law suits, bets, and now an ARSON investigation? Face it, you are a horrible boss. And I don’t think that I am off base at all by asking… for your resignation.” The crowd is obviously against this proposition, but Blake just nods his hand and raises his microphone, ready for a reply. BLAKE BECKETT: “Was this your plan? Do you think that you asking for me to resign is going to make me do it right here, in the middle of a show, in the middle of the ring, without any lawyer present? Obviously, Ace, I have no intentions on stepping down from my position of chairman of Galveston Island Wrestling. If you have a problem with me, you know where the door is Ace.” ACE BALDWIN: “Whoa, whoa, whoa Blake. I have nothing against you or this company… it is a great company and I am sure you are a very nice man… but surely there is someone more competent than Joshua Acquin to be the General Manager, and someone more competent than you to run the company.” BLAKE BECKETT: “You are entitled to your opinion, however it might not be smart for you to share it. Especially in a time like this. Plus, what ground do you have to stand on in this company? It seems like every time we put you in a pressure situation you loose. You lost with team MWA, and you had a ‘freak illness’ during the Seize the Moment match. You may call me incompetent, but at least I am a winner.” The crowd lets loose an ‘ooooooooooo’ that infuriates Baldwin, but amuses Beckett to a point where he laughs. ACE BALDWIN: “You don’t even give me a chance to win Beckett! This little Television title tournament, yeah, I WASN’T EVEN IN IT! You stick me in a five-pack challenge with four other mediocre wrestlers that don’t have any other place to go. I am a talent that you are wasting Beckett. You know it, I know it, and every single fan out there deep down inside knows it too. I‘m tired of not being the focus of the show Beckett. I DESERVE it. I would be in Mark Cross‘ place had I not gotten mysteriously ill before Moment of Glory. Actually, Blake, I have an idea. Since I was royally screwed out of the Seize the Moment match, why don’t we have a second one at Blackout?” The crowd cheers in approval, but Beckett doesn’t seem to appreciate that idea. BLAKE BECKETT: “No. I can tell you no right now. But, I’m sure I can think of something by the time your match is over. Why don’t I take a seat right over there, and watch you, since apparently I have been missing something. Afterward, I’ll let you know my decision.” Blake’s music hits, and he stares at Baldwin in the ring for a moment, before joining Thibodaux and Stevens in the announce booth. The match was set to begin. QUOTE After the introductions of the men in the ring all five stand in the ring looking like a band of misfit toys. Trayton Deluca in one corner looking lost. Ace Baldwin in another looking handsome and cocky as usual. Koji tatted up from head to toe, an intimidating presence. The rather focused looking Jack Waters set up as Jak Hendricks rounds it out looking rather ready. RILEY STEVENS: “You know last week Ace Baldwin called these men out, so they shouldn’t be too happy with him.” BLAKE BECKETT: “Can you blame them? The guy thinks he’s bad because he won a title in what I considered a developmental company.” As the five men stand around the ring, Blake Beckett at ringside on commentary only upping the ante a bit as the referee stands in between them then rings the bell. DING DING DING! Right away the fans single out Ace as the man whom they hate most at this point. Walking to the center of the ring he looks at the other guys, trying to calm them down as they are in every corner glaring back at him seeming none to pleased about his actions. Ace grins and then points at Deluca the weakling in the match, pitching the idea for them all to pick on him. Instead the four go in for the kill like a pack of wolves pounding away at him before Waters lands a big Wheel Kick with Ace staying on his feet. Hendricks follows it up with a Yakuza Kick and yet again Baldwin stands on his feet looking dazed and confused. Finally Koji jumps up and ends him with a Dropkick sending Baldwin through the ropes right to the outside in front of Blake Beckett. BLAKE BECKETT: “I call that, getting what’s coming to you.” With Ace taken care of the four other men go to war with Koji and Waters taking shots at one another while Hendricks corners Deluca crushing him with shot after shot. The fans are into it as Ace struggles on the outside getting to one foot then falling over clearly his head is still spinning. In the ring Deluca pushes Hendricks out of the way then ran at him jumping up right into the awaiting arms of Jak who brings him up and puts him down with a Spinebuster! Trayton rolls out of the ring and Hendricks follows looking to eliminate him. Back in the ring the fans are all watching as Koji throws a stiff jab to the face of Waters. Jack throws another punch back into his face. Suddenly this turns into a boxing match as Waters and Nakamora exchange back and forth. Showing no signs of slowing down the fans actually start to give them a standing ovation as they deliver blow after blow. However out of no where comes Ace sliding in and slamming Koji down from behind ending this intense battle. The fans boo as Waters throws a big punch then another sending Ace into the corner. Baldwin goes to cover his face but to his surprise he gets a ‘BreathTaker’ a Chop in the Chest that echoes through the place as Ace quickly bails out of the ring in pain. RILEY STEVENS: “That sounded like it hurt a little.” The fans are into it as on the outside Hendricks spins around throwing Deluca back into the ring as Waters picks up Koji. In unison Jak whips Deluca off the ropes just as Waters does the same to Koji. They bounce off but the results differ big time as Jak hits a huge Lariat to Trayton! However Koji ducks under Waters arm but Jak sensing a chance to distract him screams something at him distracting Jack long enough for Koji to follow it up with a big Bulldog! Koji hops up turning to Jak and taking a swing but Hendricks ducks under it and then goes wild in a move he calls the ‘Jak Attack’ throwing punches, leg kicks wildly landing with most of them. With Koji dazed he lands a kick to the gut then a Half and Half Suplex right into a cover 1…2…Ace returns and kicks him off. The fans boo him coming back again as he pushes Jak away trying to steal the pin 1…Jak quickly breaks it up. RILEY STEVENS: “Ace is trying to steal this one.” The fans cheer as Ace gets up as Jak throws a punch that misses and leads right into him hitting the Chop Block! The fans boo as Jak holds the back of his leg in pain but before he can cover him a returning Waters gets up and runs at him going for a Spear! But yet again Ace dodges a bullet throwing him into the ring post. He bounces back in obvious pain. On the other side of the ring Koji and Deluca are back up and the referee is watching them. As he’s watching them Ace rakes the eyes of Waters then throws him out of the ring. Jak gets to his feet from the previous move and with the referee distracted still Baldwin crushes his lower regions with a huge Low Blow! The fans boos are loud as Hendricks falls to the mat rolling out holding them. As all this is happening Koji hits Deluca with the ‘Nakamora Death Drop’ a Vertebreaker! The fans cheer as he goes for a pin but Ace runs up behind him pushing him out of the ring and then covering Trayton 1…2…3! JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…“Amazing” ACE BALDWIN!” The fans are irate with this as Ace gets up rolling out of the ring just as Jack, Koji, and Jak return all three furiously staring at him Baldwin quickly heads towards the announce table. Blake throws down his headset standing up as in the ring an angry Waters sets up as Deluca gets up and then nails him with the ‘Kings Spear’ knocking him down hard. As soon as he falls to the mat Koji picks him up wanting not to be out done hitting the Burning Hammer! The fans are booing this now Deluca really the one fan favorite in this match. Last but not least Jak locks in the Sharpshooter basically ending Deluca who seems to pass out, blood dripping down his face. RILEY STEVENS: “These men just got screwed I can’t blame them for being a bit upset but this is too far.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “A wins a win.” On the outside Ace watches all this then is caught off guard as Blake walks up behind him with a mic putting a hand on his shoulder. BLAKE BECKETT: “You know I really didn’t think you could piss these guys off any more. But raking their eyes, low blowing them, then stealing their pin well that’ll do it. So since you want a match at ‘Blackout II’ you will be facing the two men who lose in the TV title tourney matches as well as the three men left standing in the ring.” The fans pop as the three men in the ring all with different expressions but the clear consensus is that they are all pleased to be given another shot at Ace. BLAKE BECKETT: “And seeing that there’s been some blood spilled tonight let me add this small little stipulation…I’m sure your all familiar with the match First Blood…Well at ‘Blackout II’ we are going to take that to another level. NO RULES! NO DISQUALIFACTIONS! Simply put, if you bleed your eliminated. And the man who loses no blood will be the winner!” The crowd goes insane for this announcement as Ace seems a little flustered by it staring into he ring at the men whom he doubly pissed off tonight as they glare back at him all wanting a piece as we head to break. WINNER: ACE BALDWIN We cut backstage to see Matt Kluge talking to a random backstage employee. From the left side of the shot we see Trent Lost, North Beach Championship around his waist, approach Kluge from behind. Kluge notices him and turns around, which prompts the employee to scurry off into the darkness. MATT KLUGE: “How’s the biker thing working for you?” Trent nods, then pats his title belt. TRENT LOST: “Obviously pretty well. But Kluge, let’s not make this an argument. I heard from Beckett today that our match is a three way match - me you and Shadow, at Blackout, for all three of our belts. They all get morphed into the North Beach title! I just came to tell you that, I have no intentions of loosing this gold. And, seeing as how everyone thinks this will be your last match with the company, you wont want to try your hardest right? I mean, what if you do win? It happened to Megathreat. Remember when he won the Television title then retired the same night? The belt got vacated. This isn‘t some little fairytale world where you can leave the company yet remain the champion and take the belt to - uh - Cubs games or whatever, this is Galveston Island Wrestling. You don’t want to force Beckett and the board into another tournament if you win, do you?” He is cut off quickly by Kluge. MATT KLUGE: “Hold up, lets make something real clear. Yeah, Blackout may damn well be my final night with the company, and I am not the kind of guy that has to go out in a blaze of glory to be proud of what I have accomplished in this business. If it was anyone else, I might take a bow. But Trent, I don’t have an ounce of respect from you anywhere in my body. The feeling for Shadow is mutual. I am going to treat my match at Blackout like I have something to prove - like it is my first match not my last. And if I have to drop that belt as soon as I win it, so be it, as long as it isn’t around your skinny little waist.” TRENT LOST: “Oh. Wow. Harsh words Mr. Kluge. You practically have me shaking in my sneakers. Look, you have always been a decent wrestler, over hyped and appreciated. You have had short little title reigns, but nothing that ever amounted for anything. Face it, in a few months… everyone will have totally forgotten about you Kluge. Hell, sometimes I forget about you and I have to see you nearly every day. A win at Blackout isn’t going to make you memorable… but you know what will? Whenever people buy my DVD’s, read my book - they will see your name, your face, as the guy I had to step on to make my way to the top. That’s your fame Kluge.” Trent exits to the right hand side of the shot as we fade to our first commercial break of the evening. QUOTE AC/DC sonically invaded the arena and Christian Rivers flood through the entry way and down the aisle, then into the ring. The fans were giving him mixed reactions, some growing to like him others still fair-weather. RILEY STEVENS: “You know it’s time for the first TV title Qualifier. The winners of these matches here tonight will take on Damien Dunn at ‘Blackout II’ for the GIW Television Championship. Right now we have Curtis Durrington versus Christian Rivers.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Yay!” Not far after him did Curtis Durrington make an entrance to the ring as well. It seemed, though, that Durrington pushed his entry until last minute, but once he entered the ring, the bell rang. DING DING DING! They started with a general lockup. Curtis transitioned a snapmare with an armbar and held tightly to his opponent. Gun-shaped and writhing with bullets of resolve, they both fought for power in the struggle, but an armbar wasn’t going to quell the queller. Curtis released it to follow up with another maneuver. Christian rolled up to his feet and shook his arm awake and followed suit into another lock up with his opponent. He pushed out and pulled himself toward the ropes for the right forward momentum to pin Durrington on the clothesline from hell, but Curtis ducked under and upon Christian’s return, Curtis used his variation of the belly-to-belly suplex and downed his opponent. He kicked Christian’s legs together as Rivers held his arms out and looked up into the light above his head, Curtis pinned him. It only went as far as two, but this did not deter Durrington. RILEY STEVENS: “Great start to this match.” Christian managed to beckon forth any strength he had after the pin attempt to bring Curtis down into his own armbar. Holding tight onto the submission hold and the two writhing like a serpentine form, Christian clung for as long and as tight as he could before Curtis began to lift himself over his opponent for a makeshift pin that forced Christian to release to release himself from the pin. Curtis nursed his arm briefly and resumed his forward momentum. Christian too revolved to his feet and backed himself against the corner turn post where Curtis used his to knee his gut, punch the temple, and Irish whip him toward the other turn post diagonally across them. Christian turned his back on the post and charged as Curtis did. They both collided with clotheslines that knocked one another flat onto the canvas like blank and white picket signs laid out in protest. The referee approached and over looked the bodies below him and threw up his arms to begin a count out. By seven, it was Curtis to pull himself to his knees as Christian climbed toward the ropes nearby and help himself up. Once both men reached their feet by the count of nine, the referee broke it. The match would resume and Christian felt a running bulldog would be the best maneuver to resume it with, but using his momentum, Curtis caught his opponent and swung around to slam his opponent into the canvas. RILEY STEVENS: “Big slam, could this be it?” Curtis sat up his opponent and locked in the ‘Daphne Deathlock’ with each arm holding tight to his opponent. Christian tried to struggle, but eventually the persecution of Curtis’ superior submission hold relinquished him from coming a second time in the bout. He called his quitting toward the referee and Curtis released him and hopped to his feet. JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…CURTIS DURRINGTON!” The fans cheer this announcement as Curtis gets to his feet throwing up his hands in victory knowing he now has a guaranteed shot at the gold. RILEY STEVENS: “Big win for Curtis here and he’ll get a chance at Damien at ‘Blackout II’.” Rivers rolls out of the ring disappointed at how close he came as Curtis heads to the top rope celebrating with his fans. WINNER: CURTIS DURRINGTON We cut backstage after the TV Tournament match to see Aspen Chaud and Alex Castellanos in a locker room. Alex is sitting on a chair in the center of the room while Aspen is stretching behind him. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “I just think it is cute, personally; that these two guys would pick us out of everyone on the roster to fight. Maybe it is because we look so nice and approachable. You should get some tribal tattoos… it worked for that INK guy.” Aspen chuckled in the back, then there was a knock on the door. Alex hopped up out of the chair and opened it, he was surprised to see Joshua Acquin standing there, with a contract attached to a clipboard in his hand. JOSHUA ACQUIN: “I am just here to get you two to sign off on the contract for your match versus Sean Galen and Nick Starks at Blackout. And to wish Aspen luck in her match tonight. You surely will need it.” Alex takes the contract and steps back, realizing this isn’t his fight. Aspen steps away from the wall and towards Joshua Acquin. ASPEN CHAUD: “Why do I have the feeling that you are up to something Joshua? I have known you for a very long time, and I know what that little smirk means.” Joshua laughs, then leans against the door, toying with Aspen. JOSHUA ACQUIN: “Me? Manipulating my power? Oh come on Aspen is that something I would do? You know all I want is a good wrestling match, all of the time.” Alex signs the contract, then hands it over to Joshua, who nods back at him, then exit’s the scene. Aspen shuts the door, furious. ASPEN CHAUD: “He is up to something, I can feel it.” We fade to commercial. QUOTE First to the ring, as announced to the crowd was a one, John Rawlings. The crowd barracked and jeered at him as he entered, but he wasn’t having any of it. This was his time; his match. They’d just have to deal with it, just as his opponent would have to soon enough. RILEY STEVENS: “Rawlings a member of Syndication hasn’t really been involved with many of there issues due to the fact he’s been dealing with this TV title shot. Have to wonder how he’d react to having to face Dunn in the Finals? But first he’s got to beat A.J. Fairchild.” Described at the height of 5’10”, which oddly enough was the same height as Rawlings, came A.J. Fairchild. The crowd were behind him enough to show they were not about to favor a man such as John, but A.J. played with it as he entered the ring as the bell rang. DING DING DING! Immediately, Rawlings snapped his opponent with a jab and double underhook powerbomb and a sudden pin. The referee counted to three immediately against A.J. Fairchild! Had it not been for the fact Fairchild’s foot still lingered on the rope just as the referee pulled up from his pin, the match would have been done and over with quicker than anyone could distinguish the clear difference between the two competitors. This threw Rawlings out of his loop for a few moments and he argued with the referee, only to find himself in a rollup pin underneath his opponent. This count, however, only managed to reach two before Rawlings freed himself. Fast paced, the two broke off and collided again, but it was a thrust kick that collided with Rawlings’ body and then Rawlings recovered and charged forward with superkick he titled, ‘Exit 9.’ A.J. was flatted against the canvas, so his opponent maneuvered him into the Texas Cloverleaf submission hold to slow the match down and bring control back into his favor. It was going well enough until A.J. crawled slowly, inch by inch, toward the ropes and hung tightly for dear release. It was reluctantly granted to him and with that freedom, Fairchild rolled outside of the ring for a moment to catch his breath and sense of self. RILEY STEVENS: “Both stars could use a breather after this fast paced action.” Rawlings did not approve of this. He followed Fairchild outside of the ring and hammered his forearm across the competitor’s back. It brought Fairchild down on one knee, then Rawlings booted his opponent’s skull to force him the rest of the way down. With his opponent down, Rawlings locked the Texas Cloverleaf onto Fairchild again outside of the ring, just in front of the aisle. A.J. began to tap, but the maneuver was not in the right location to be counted as a victory and as soon as the referee reached the count of seven, Rawlings slid himself back into the ring and then right back out to continue dishing out as much pain as he could on Fairchild. He dragged his opponent back into the ring with him and covered him for the pin, but the count only reached two. A.J. rolled himself back out of the ring. Vexation overcame Rawlings and he followed after his opponent, only to find A.J. strategically slide in and ascend the ropes. Fairchild lunged out of the ring from the top rope and hit his opponent with a Springboard Forearm Smash. The crowd popped for the maneuver and Fairchild himself popped right back into the ring. He backed toward the other side of the ring and awaited the other competitor to rise up. When John stood at the rim of the ring to reenter, A.J. rushed forth and used a baseball slide kick to propel his opponent backward and into the outer mats, without himself being shoved outward. When John did manage to get into the ring, it was at the end of the ten count and A.J. once again retreated, but victorious. JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner via count out…AJ FAIRCHILD!” The crowd gives mixed reaction to this ending as AJ rolls out of the ring seeming subtle with it as Rawlings gets into the referee’s face claiming that he got in at nine. RILEY STEVENS: “Well, AJ gets into the TV Title final but not by much.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “He was in, he was in the ring!” Rawlings screams at the referee then shoving him aside heading to the back as we take a break. WINNER: A.J. FAIRCHILD We cut backstage after the commercial break to see Curtis Durrington and AJ Fairchild talking in the Best Western parking lot, which is directly across the street from Stewart Beach. CURTIS DURRINGTON: “Our match at Blackout is going to steal the show. How long have we been here, two or three weeks? Talk about proving yourself…” AJ FAIRCHILD: “Hard work man… hard work.” CURTIS DURRINGTON: “Well, congrats on that win and good luck Sunday. Hopefully it is a great match.” AJ FAIRCHILD: “I wouldn’t expect anything less.” Curtis nods at AJ, then begins to walk away towards the hotel, which is off-screen. He stops, then walks backward a bit. The camera turns to reveal Damien Dunn standing there, his belt over his shoulder. DAMIEN DUNN: “This is what the tournament yielded? How the hell are you two planning to compete with me? I am the most dominant star in GIW today… how many people can say they have defeated Alioth Starre? How many can say they did it twice? You two are just minor detours.” AJ FAIRCHILD: “How about you save the talk, and we fight right here right now? I’m sure everyone would rather see me and Curtis without you in the middle of it.” DAMIEN DUNN: “I highly doubt that. You two are about as irrelevant as Brody Harper. Keep thinking you have a chance, it might make for a better match Sunday.” With that, Dunn exits towards the arena and we fade to a commercial. QUOTE As we return from the break this time we see Trent Lost the ‘GIW North Beach Champion’ standing in the ring alongside his girlfriend Katya Rose both with big smirks ignoring the fans boo as they await their opponents. RILEY STEVENS: “Has there ever been a girl Lost wouldn’t date?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Come on she’s way hot, it’s just a shame she never seems to show up.” As soon as "Wayward One" by Alter Bridge hits the fans go wild as out from the back comes Alex Castellanos heading out from the top of the ramp with a big smile on his face as the fans cheer him on, one of the loudest pops of the night. He walks down to the bottom of the ramp then waits as "Bullseye" by Aly and AJ hits and the fans cheers continue as out comes Aspen Chaud heading down with him at his side as the two walk up the steps and into the ring standing in their corner. RILEY STEVENS: “You know it’s still hard to completely cheer for Aspen. But frankly these fans seem to have reopened their hearts for her and beating up these two here tonight would certainly help that.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Yeah, she’s already lost as far as I am concerned.” The fans go wild as they set up in their corner as they wait as Trent urges Katya into the ring as this decides theirs as Aspen heads on in. DING DING DING! There is not a doubt in anyone’s mind who the fans are rooting for, it’s completely for the AC team. Aspen and Katya start to circle one another as Rose runs at her only for Aspen to step out of the way and trip her with one foot. The fans cheer at Katya’s expense as she gets up angrily screaming something at Aspen who simply grins not taking her too seriously as she runs at her only to get side stepped and slapped on the butt by Aspen. The fans again cheer at Rose’s expense as she angrily runs full blast at Aspen who jumps into the air bringing her down with a Lou Thesz Press followed by punches. The crowd is going wild for her as Katya pushes her off and both hop to their feet Aspen first as she whips her off into the ropes. Rushing at her she hits the ‘Tush Push a Turnbuckle Flying Butt Shot! The fans go wild as Alex tags in stepping into the ring as Katya stumbles around he picks her up onto his shoulders as Aspen goes to the top rope! The fans are on their feet as Aspen jumps off with the ‘AirBarbie’ a Flying Crossbody! This sends the fans into a frenzy with the tag move as Alex goes for a cover 1…2…Lost kicks him off. RILEY STEVENS: “Alex and Aspen are clicking on all cylinders here, Trent may have just saved the match.” Castellanos gets her to her feet not looking completely willing to hit her instead throwing her over into Trent’s corner, urging him to tag her. Katya reaches up for him trying to make the tag but Lost instead backs away not wanting into this thing, jumping off the turnbuckle. Alex seems shocked even for Trent that’s low as he instead whips her back into their corner tagging in Aspen. Chaud jumps in as Alex holds her throwing her right into Aspen’s raised foot. Looking out at Aspen she seems to realize that Katya is alone. Still not feeling to sorry for her she looks around at the crowd, with a grin as Rose tries to crawl to her corner dropping down right into the ‘K.M.A.’ a Heel Lock! The fans go wild as she locks it in as Katya looks for help but when none comes she taps out quickly. JAMAL JACKSON: “Here are your winners the team of ALEX CASTELLANOS and ASPEN CHAUD!” The fans go wild for them as Aspen jumps up heading out of the ring joined by Alex as the two hug in celebration then head to the fans to celebrate with them. Sliding back into the ring is Trent who now suddenly wants to be there for Rose. RILEY STEVENS: “How can you defend a man that just left her out to dry like that Oliver?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “He has a match at ‘Blackout II’, she took one for the team.” The crowd is cheering still for them as Alex quietely re-enters the ring the fans cheering louder as Trent happens to get up and right away eats a ‘Golden Arrow’ a Superkick to the jaw! This sends the fans into more cheers as Lost falls backwards in pain. Alex stands over him mouthing “I owed you one!”. RILEY STEVENS: “Can’t say Trent didn’t have that coming.” The fans cheer as Alex rolls out of the ring back with Aspen the two heading up the ramp as we head to a break. WINNERS: ALEX CASTELLANOS & ASPEN CHAUD After the match is over, we immediately cut over to the door of Alioth Starre’s locker room. Outside of it, we see Mark Turner whispering some things to Alioth, then Mark Cross enters the shot to an audible response from the crowd. MARK TURNER: “Well hello Cross, prepared for the match this evening?” MARK CROSS: “Always.” Turner smiles, then turns to Cross. MARK TURNER: “Good, you need to be on your game. We don’t want anything bad to happen tonight that will put Blackout in jeopardy.” MARK CROSS: “That’s actually why I am here. I just want to make it clear to both of you - even though we have been cordial since Moment of Glory… I don’t plan to be cordial at Blackout.” MARK TURNER: “Of course not! We wouldn’t expect that from a competitor like yourself.” MARK CROSS: “I just don’t want you to get the wrong idea.” It is then that Ace Baldwin walks into the shot, a huge grin on his face. ACE BALDWIN: “Enjoying your time in the spotlight Cross, might as well seeing as you wont be in it for much longer.” MARK CROSS: “We will see about that.” ACE BALDWIN: “No, actually we wont. When I win my First Blood elimination match at Blackout, I will SURELY be the next in line for the Undisputed Belt… whoever holds it after Blackout will be on borrowed time.” MARK TURNER: “Who is this guy again?” Ace clenches his teeth. ACE BALDWIN: “You’ll know my name.” Ace quickly exits, as we fade to commercial. QUOTE As we return to the it is time for the main event and the sound of “Cult of Personality” hits as the fans begin to boo as out from the back comes Syndication.
