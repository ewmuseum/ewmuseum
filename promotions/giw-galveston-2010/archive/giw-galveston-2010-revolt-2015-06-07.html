GIW — Card

Revolt, 7 June 2015

7 June 2015
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS SUNDAY NIGHT REVOLT 6/7/15 FROM KAUFFMAN ARENA IN GALVESTON, TEXAS BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! The camera pans around the audience as fireworks stream into the night sky above Kauffman Arena, signaling to the entire island that GIW Revolt is getting ready to begin. As “Over and Under” by Egypt Central plays, the camera focuses in on several signs in the audience, notably “Callum’s Number One Fan”, “Seton Will Survive”, and “Journeyfandom”. We cut down to the announcer’s booth. SARAH SHARP: "Good evening everyone! I’m Sarah Sharp and welcome to another edition of GIW Sunday Night Revolt. We are just one week away from Category 5 live on iPPV, and we have a jam packed show for you tonight, especially our main event. Beside me, as usual, is my partner Oliver Thibodaux. Oliver, are you ready for this clash of the titans?" OLIVER THIBODAUX: "You better believe I’m ready Sarah! Eight of the best GIW has to offer will be in the same ring tonight. All four qualifiers for Sole Survivor, the Undisputed Champion and the challenger, and the Deep South Champion and the challenger." SARAH SHARP: "We all know the importance of Momentum as we head into such a big show, and both teams will be looking to get that one last win tonight. It should be an instant classic for sure." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Smart money has to be on Team Alexis tonight. With Bronx Valescence, Kestrel, and Alioth Starre at her side, I can’t imagine anything other than a victory for the Queen of Galveston Island tonight." SARAH SHARP: "You say that, but the opposing team is stacked as well. Chris Callum is flanked by the number one contender to the GIW Deep South Championship, Gavin Achilles, as well as two qualifying participants in Sole Survivor – Madison Seton and Journeyman Phillips." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "I can’t argue with you that the other side is stacked too, but Alexis Terry knows how to motivate her troops, and she knows how to win. I don’t think she is going to let this one slip away tonight Sarah, not with so much on the line." “Gunboat” by Vixtrola hits as the crowd erupts in cheers. Madison Seton steps onto the ramp as her nameplate flashes across the screen. The voices of the commentators can he heard as she makes her way down to the ring, microphone in hand. SARAH SHARP: "Well here comes Madison Seton. Tonight she teams with Chris Callum and the others one week before she attempts to become a three time winner of the Sole Survivor match, but after what Callum had to say when the show kicked off last week, I’m she has been itching to reply." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Chris shocked the world last week when he came out with a mask and demanded that the fans hold Madison Seton to the same high standard that they held him to. And honestly, he has a point. It wasn’t so long ago that these fans would boo Madison every time she made her way down to the ring, and listen to them now. Hypocrites." SARAH SHARP: "They aren’t hypocrites. She asked for their forgiveness, and they gave it to her. This business can change people for better and for worse, no one is immune to that. I agree that Callum has a good point, but singling out Madison Seton is unfair. She hasn’t done anything to warrant boos since she returned two weeks ago at Extreme TV." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Two weeks isn’t enough time to judge a person’s character, Sarah. Callum knows it, and so do these fans. The only difference is, these fans don’t care. Seton doesn’t deserve this kind of reaction, and I’m glad Chris decided to call everyone out on it." Seton had, in the time period elapsed, made her way into the ring. Her music died down and she looked directly into the hard camera as she spoke. MADISON SETON: "Everyone knows why I am out here tonight, so let’s cut to the chase, shall we? Last week, Chris Callum came out here and had a lot to say about me and several others in this company. I know what he is thinking right now – he’s been here since the company reopened, and in that time he has managed to become the top guy here. You all love him, and you should. He’s a great wrestler and an even better man. There is no ill will from me to Chris Callum. I respect him. I said that the first time I stepped into the ring to address you all three weeks ago in this very ring." She paused, allowing the fans to cheer. MADISON SETON: "The problem is, I don’t know if Chris Callum respects me. He is dwelling on the way I acted a year ago. I’m willing to be accountable for my actions. They were wrong, and I should pay the price. When he came out here and asked all of you to hold me to a higher standard, I didn’t have a problem with the suggestion. You all should hold me to a high standard, and I am willing to rise up and meet it. The problem is that when he made the suggestion, he wanted you all to turn on me. It was a loaded suggestion. He called me a wolf in sheep’s clothing, but I’m not the one that comes out here and tries to guilt trip you all into turning on another person." Another pause with more cheers from the crowd. A humble smile comes across Seton’s face. MADISON SETON: "Three weeks ago, I didn’t come out here expecting to get a reaction like I did. Almost all of you cheered me, and the feedback I have gotten since I’ve returned to the company has been extremely positive. Most of you are happy to see me back, and I’m happy to be back. I say, if you’ve forgiven me, then you’ve forgiven me. You all are smart enough to make your own decisions, so don’t let Chris Callum make them for you." She moves closer to the hard camera. MADISON SETON: "I came back because I want to compete against the best that GIW has to offer, and Chris Callum is one of the best. If he doubts me, that’s fine, but I hope his newfound success doesn’t go to his head. I will win Sole Survivor for the third time this coming Sunday, and I will be the one to go toe to toe with Chris Callum at Moment of Glory 5… if he can make it past Bronx Valescence." A strange voice pierces through the cheers, turning them into boos. Alioth Starre makes his way onto the stage, but he doesn’t stop. He is coming to the ring. ALIOTH STARRE: "Madison… Madison…" More boos as Alioth begins to ascend the stairs. ALIOTH STARRE: "You seem very confident that you will be the Sole Survivor. Coming back to face the best of the best, that’s admirable, but you’re focusing too much on Chris Callum. Don’t turn a blind eye to a GIW Hall of Famer, and the longest reigning GIW Undisputed Champion in history – me." Alioth climbs into the ring, coming face to face with one of his opponents this Sunday. ALIOTH STARRE: "I’ve accomplished many things in GIW, but I have never won Sole Survivor. I came back to finish what I started, and that includes finally attaining many of the accolades that were just out of my reach the last time I was here. I know I have an uphill battle ahead of me – these fans and all of the staff in the back fawn over you. They say Madison Seton is the best ever, and Chris Callum is the only man to come close to her dominance. The difference is, Madison, that Chris Callum is a true talent. You’re just lucky. You’ve always been lucky. And the fact that you of all people as a claim to ‘most dominant in GIW history’ frankly makes me sick. Taking you down at Category 5 will be an honor, and then two true talents can face off at Moment of Glory. Alioth Starre versus Chris Callum… the way it should be." Seton smiles at Alioth as the crowd continues to boo him. He doesn’t seem to mind. MADISON SETON: "You’re great Alioth. I said it, and I have no shame in saying you. You aren’t good, you’re great. But every person that calls me the most dominant wrestler in GIW history does so because they’ve seen what I’ve done, and they arrived to that conclusion on their own. I don’t force it down their throats like you do. Maybe you would get a little bit more respect around here if you showed some humility." Before the boos could rain down, Alioth fired back with a response. ALIOTH STARRE: "You’re telling me to be humble, Madison? You can’t tell another person to be humble, because that means that you consider yourself humble. If you consider yourself humble, then you clearly aren’t. Chris Callum is absolutely right about you. You’re still as egotistical and narcissistic as you ever were. I’m not afraid to admit that I deserve more than I have been given, but at least I embrace it. That’s the difference between you and me, well, one of them anyway. You’re a fraud." Alioth was clearly beginning to get under Madison’s skin. MADISON SETON: "How am I a fraud, Alioth? I admitted my mistakes. It was the first thing I did when I came back to Galveston Island Wrestling. And I know I’m good. I know I’m one of the best. I’m not ashamed to admit that. The difference that you fail to see is that I don’t walk around touting my accomplishments and expecting handouts. That’s why the fans respect me." As her words become more intense, Alioth takes a step forward, not intimidated. ALIOTH STARRE: "Your accomplishments? Let’s weigh yours to mine. I’m a triple crown champion, can you say the same? I’m a GIW Hall of Famer, can you say the same? Oh, and those two Undisputed title reigns you tout so often… I’m a two time champion as well, and my combined reign trumps yours by many days. I came back to moderate fanfare. What did I do? I immediately destroyed The Sir, a man that everyone in the back thinks is one of the best in the company. You walked straight into a battle royal you had no reason to be in, and then you qualified for Sole Survivor. Since then, all I have heard was hype about an inevitable matchup between Chris Callum and Madison Seton in the main event of the biggest show of the year. The marketing team is practically handing you the victory in Sole Survivor, but I’m going to make them reprint all of their posters and rewrite all of their feature stories. You won’t make it past the first round next Sunday, I’ll see to it." Seton rolls her shoulders, perhaps getting ready for a fight. MADISON SETON: "If you want to belittle me right now, that’s fine. If you want to target me in Sole Survivor, that’s fine. I’m ready for whatever futile offense you throw at me, Alioth. I’m not here to be a flash in the pan. I’m here to stay. But I’m tired of all of this talking – if you want to take me out so bad, to relegate me to the past, then fight me right here, right now. I’m not gonna back away.” Alioth raises an eyebrow, looking Seton up and down. Any blows between the two are silenced by Journeyman Phillips as his voice echoes through Kauffman Arena, drawing a positive response. JOURNEYMAN PHILLIPS: "Seton and Starre. Madison and Alioth. Two legends. However you want to phrase it, I’m tired of hearing about it. I get that you two are amongst the best to ever walk through that curtain and step into that ring, but both of you need to remember that the Sole Survivor match has four wrestlers competing for the prize, not two." Alioth took his mic and walked towards the ropes, screaming out to Journeyman Phillips as he stopped in the middle of the ramp. ALIOTH STARRE: "Why don’t you go back to catering and let the legends talk?" A massive chorus of boos rain down on Alioth as Journeyman chuckles. JOURNEYMAN PHILLIPS: "Two legends. Absolutely. You both are legends and I recognize that. But legends are legends because their time has passed. Both of you have had multiple reigns at the top, and now that time is over. GIW is led by Bronx Valescence and Chris Callum now, whether you like it or not. I know this may sound ridiculous coming from a man like me, but I believe my time is now. No one, and I mean no one, is expecting me to make it out of the first round next Sunday – except me. I came out here because I don’t want you two to soften each other up for the match, because whenever I win, I don’t want my win to be tainted. There can be no doubt in anyone’s mind that I fight the hardest, and I lasted the longest." Another voice echoes from the stage as Journeyman turns around to see Kestrel making her way down. KESTREL: "Since we are having a group meeting… there is something I need to say…" Kestrel smiles as the crowd greets her with a mixed reaction. KESTREL: "All this talk about certainty… Alioth will CERTAINLY be the winner. Madison will CERTAINLY be the winner. Journeyman will CERTAINLY be the winner. Well, it’s all a joke. One big joke. Because if there is one think I know better than any of you, it is that nothing in life is certain. Anything can happen at any given time. Madison, you beat me last week. Congratulations. But you made a mistake. You threw out your entire arsenal, and you barely managed to beat me. I know you now. I understand how you wrestle. And if the rest of you are smart, when we all meet up again later tonight in the main event, you’ll hold back your best stuff. I want to be surprised come Cat 5." Alioth points down the ramp, passed Journeyman, and directly to Kestrel. ALIOTH STARRE: "Oh Kestrel, one day you’ll realize that some things are eternal." With a smile, “Over and Under” begins to play as the camera cuts back and forth between the Sole Survivor participants mouthing back and forth to each other. We fade to commercial break. QUOTE “Remember Everything” by Five Finger Death Punch welcomes us back from the commercial break as we see Zuri Chastain warming up inside the ring. After a moment, her music is replaced by “More Than Meets the Eye” by Testament, as George Starks makes his way down to the ring as the crowd boos him. He makes sure to flex as much as he can as he takes his time. RUBY REYNOLDS: "Now entering the ring, weighing in at two hundred and sixty five founds, from St. Johns, Nova Scotia, he is ‘Canada’s Strongest Man’, George Stark!" George makes his way to the ring as the commentators chirp away. SARAH SHARP: "George is certainly the favorite in this match, given his size, but where is Melissa Stark? She is normally always by his side when he competes." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "What a shame. I love it when Melissa comes out here! It’s a treat every single time, isn’t it Sarah?" George enters the ring as the referee gets set to ring the bell. DING! DING! DING! Zuri began to circle George Stark, ready to compete in the opening bout of Revolt. She leaned against the ropes and did a few stretches to warm herself up as he watched her, and waited. When she closed the gap between them, Stark threw a road block up in the form of a big boot to the chest that knocked Zuri into the right corner turn post. He closed the space between them and grabbed her by the jawline. He pressed her skull and held her head back to apply more strain on her, more pain for her to endure. His look was vicious and when the referee forced him off of her after a count of four, he glared back at him as a warning that the ref could be next. In response the referee back away and so did Stark, just a few paces and enough to give him a running start for a powerfully struck gut kick that planted Zuri into the turn post before she collapsed, or perhaps withered before him like a dying weed. SARAH SHARP: "George Stark is dominating this poor girl. This isn’t even a fair fight." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Zuri was confident enough to sign on to this match, but I don’t think she realized she was signing her own death certificate." SARAH SHARP: "It has been a long time since we have seen George Stark this dominant, but we all knew what he was capable of." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "You’re damn right we did. I think we need to expect this kind of performance every week by Canada’s Stongest man." He grimaced at the crowd reaction and then turned back toward his opponent. Zuri managed to crawl toward the other side of the ring, but Stark was on her fast and whipped her back into the corner she was planted in earlier. She fell before him and Stark signaled that it was over. After an elevated powerbomb, what he calls 'Stark Contrast' it was all lights out for Zuri Chastain. RUBY REYNOLDS: “Here is your winner… “Canada’s Strongest Man”, George Stark! SARAH SHARP: "Goodnight, Zuri. Thanks for coming. That was about as one sided of a match you can get." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "George Stark continues to look strong as he heads into Category 5. I would hate to be whoever has to stand in the ring with this man." We slowly fade out as George climbs to the top rope to flex, drawing major heat from the fans. WINNER: GEORGE STARK We cut to the back were Aspen Chaud is standing with Zachary Pagonis in the GIW Interview Lounge. ZACHARY PAGONIS: "Please welcome my guest at this time, the Commissioner of Revolt, Aspen Chaud." Aspen smiles as Zachary turns to her. ZACHARY PAGONIS: "Aspen, you know why you’re here tonight. At your weekly press conference yesterday, allegations of an unprofessional relationship between you and Chris Callum surfaced. There have been murmurings of this for a while now, and you have been seen on television attempting to grab his hand. I understand that you are ready to make a statement now, so I’ll leave you to it." Aspen is handed the microphone and speaks directly into the camera. ASPEN CHAUD: "Thank you Zachary. I was blindsided by the reporter’s question at my press conference. Of course I’ve heard the rumors swirling for some time now, but there have been nasty rumors following me my entire life. When I touched Chris Callum’s hand a month ago on Revolt, it was a friendly gesture, not a romantic one. I care about my employees, so when they are stressed or injured, I feel the need to console them. That is also why I took it upon myself to take care of him after his broken jaw. It happened under my watch, and therefore I felt responsible. I understand that it could be taken the wrong way, and given my past, I don’t blame anyone for wondering if something was going on between us. But I can say without any second thoughts that I do not have romantic feelings for Chris Callum. I never have. I take this job seriously, and I wouldn’t put it in jeopardy." Zachary manages to get word in while Aspen pauses. ZACHARY PAGONIS: "You may not have feelings to Chris, but does he have feelings for you?" Aspen turns off camera. ASPEN CHAUD: "Why don’t you ask him, Zach?" Chris Callum enters, clad in his black mask. He glances at Aspen before speaking to Zachary. CHRIS CALLUM: "This is a waste of time. Aspen and I aren’t even friends; merely colleagues. I didn’t ask for her to take care of me after Exile, she did it on her own – probably because she feels the need to cleanse herself of her past transgressions. I appreciated the altruism, but that’s all it was. It wasn’t favoritism. I’ve earned every shot I’ve gotten in this company, and if there anyone out there who wants to question my ability, I wish they would just come out and say so rather than trying to tear me down through these rumors." He turns to Aspen now, locking eyes with her. CHRIS CALLUM: "But, Aspen, I do have issues with you. I don’t think your job should be spared just because these rumors aren’t true. They often say where there is smoke, there is fire, and that is true in this case. What happened to Alexis Terry when she broke my jaw? Nothing. What happened to Alexis Terry when she took out Duff Briggs? Nothing. Your inaction isn’t just towards Alexis Terry either – what happened to Gary Ford when he was bullying Gavin Achilles? Nothing. Matt Kluge when he was ruining Alioth Starre’s chances at title shots? Nothing. You need to face the music. You’ve been an awful Commissioner and you need to step down. You’re reputation is inexcusable, and if you weren’t known as a whore, none of this would be happening to me. Let someone more credible take your place." She didn’t smile, but instead stared back him blankly. ASPEN CHAUD: "I’m not stepping down. I haven’t handled every situation perfectly, but neither have you. The difference between me and you, Chris, is that I own up to my mistakes. I don’t hide behind a mask. But if you want me to come clean about who I really am, then you should consider doing the same thing. And I’ll leave it at that." She pressed the mic against his shoulders and left quickly, leaving Chris and Zachary looking on. We cut to another shot. The scene opens with Melissa Stark inside a lingerie store somewhere on Galveston Island. She is looking through various items, but doesn’t seem pleased with the selection. MELISSA STARK: "He's not gonna like any of these." A store attendant catches her off guard. Melissa turns to her quickly. ATTENDANT: "Can I help you find something?” Melissa smiles as she turns back to the rack. MELISSA STARK: "Yes my special someone's birthday is and I want something very special to wear for him." The Attendant gestures to Melissa. ATTENDANT: "Follow me. I know exactly what you’re looking for.” Together, they move around the isle and arrive at a new rack. ATTENDANT: "How about this? I think it suits you well.” Melissa smiles a big wide smile, taking the selection from the woman. MELISSA STARK: "This is perfect. I’ll take it." Melissa admires the getup as we fade to commercial break. QUOTE As we come back from the commercial break, “I Coulda Been a Contender” by Gaslight Anthem greets us as Jackson Reid leans against the ropes with his Television title over his shoulder. He is eagerly awaiting his opponent for the evening. SARAH SHARP: "This should be a good one. The GIW Television Champion takes on one of the two men who will compete for a shot at the Deep South title." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Johnny Violence is so much more than that! He should be Undisputed Champion right now, and he would be if it wasn’t for that stupid first blood stipulation." SARAH SHARP: "The stipulation that the fans voted for, you mean." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Just because the fans vote for something doesn’t make it good, Sarah! Gah!" “Bat Country” by Avenged Sevenfold begins to play as Johnny Violence makes his way down the ramp, egging on the boos from the fans. He takes them with pride. RUBY REYNOLDS: "Now entering the ring, from Calgary, Alberta, Canada… weighing in at one hundred and eight nine pounds, “The King of Anarchy”… Johnny Violence!" Johnny climbs into the ring with a smirk on his face as he looks at his opponent. SARAH SHARP: "Well, Johnny certainly seems confident this week. I wonder if he will be so confident when he takes on The Sir at Category 5?" OLIVER THIBODAUX: "He should be! This guy is great!" DING! DING! DING! The match began with a standoff between Johnny and Jackson. Johnny held open his arms and shook his head in mockery that Jackson Reid would even bother to show up to fight him. He mouthed off toward his opponent, even poke the man. When Jackson tried to smack Violence's hand away, he was knocked off his rocker with a strong clothesline in retaliation. Jackson's body rolled, more stumbled on the canvas toward the ropes, which Johnny held to and used to help lift with his hard stomps toward his opponent's exposed head and shoulder. SARAH SHARP: "Johnny Violence may have the most direct and brutal wrestling style in the company. Jackson Reid didn’t know what hit him tonight." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Didn’t? Johnny is still hitting him! No need for past tense!" Violence climbed over Jackson, a hand tucked under the man's head to pull him up for a rigid right punch, then another, and another until he felt Jackson had received enough punishment, or when the referee forced him off. Violence pulled away, Jackson rolled onto his side, holding his bruised cranium. This prone position allowed Violence to come in and kick the spine of his opponent, then back again to tuck that boot with a jerking kick at Jackson's ribs. He pulled Jackson onto his knees and leaned his head over the middle rope. Jackson's arms tucked over as well and Violence kneeled on that rope and on Jackson to choke him, fingers at the man's mouth to stretch it apart to inflict more pain and punishment. He pushed off the rope and then pulled Jackson up to finish the match for all Glory Fades and that's just what he performed to end Jackson Reid. Pinned. RUBY REYNOLDS: "Here is your winner… Johnny Violence!" Johnny steps over his fallen opponent to climb the turnbuckle in celebration. SARAH SHARP: "Well Johnny certainly has momentum heading into his match at Category 5. We will find out later in the night if The Sir can do the same." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "It doesn’t matter how much momentum The Sir has! Johnny Violence is going to win the match at Category 5 because he is an uncrowned Champion! Look at Jackson Reid! That kid just got crushed tonight! Let’s go Violence!" Our parting image is of Johnny Violence motioning to a belt around his waist, pointing at the camera, and mouthing something to The Sir. WINNER: JOHNNY VIOLENCE After the match, we fade in to Alexis Terry’s locker room. She sits on the same black leather couch that we saw last week, and she is drinking straight from the bottle. Bronx Valescence enters the locker room slowly, a bit deterred by the state of the leader of the Main Event Monarchy. BRONX VALESCENCE: "Alexis… are you alright?" She takes another gulp of the whiskey and shrugs Bronx off. She is clearly drunk. ALEXIS TERRY: "What do you mean? Of course I’m alright." Bronx takes a seat beside Alexis, clearly showing some concern. BRONX VALESCENCE: "You’ve been drinking more than usual." Alexis goes to take another drink, but stops. She turns to Bronx. ALEXIS TERRY: "I’m fine. Don’t worry about me. I can handle my booze." He looks down, a bit ashamed. BRONX VALESCENCE: "If you say so… Hey, I need some advice." Setting the bottle on the side table, Alexis mutters out a few words. ALEXIS TERRY: "I can give you some." After a deep breath, Bronx speaks. BRONX VALESCENCE: "How do I beat Chris Callum? So far, you’re the only one that has really done it." Alexis laughs a bit too loudly to be considered normal. ALEXIS TERRY: "Don’t tell me you’re getting scared. He’s just a little man." Bronx shakes his head. BRONX VALESCENCE: "I’m not scared. I just don’t want to fail again. How many times have I tried for a title in this company and come up short?" She places an arm on Bronx’s shoulder. ALEXIS TERRY: "Bronx, beating Chris Callum is easy." Though Alexis seems confident, Bronx does not. He frowns. BRONX VALESCENCE: "Please explain." She exhales before reaching down into a bag beside the couch. She pulls out a small black pouch and tosses them to Bronx. He managed to catch them before examining the bag. ALEXIS TERRY: "Here. These are the secret." He opens it and pulls out a shiny pink object. His jaw drops as he turns to Alexis. BRONX VALESCENCE: "Brass knuckles." She nods, glancing at the bottle. ALEXIS TERRY: "Use them like I did, not like Johnny Violence did. And don’t get caught for Christ’s sake." Bronx begins to slide his fingers through the holes, showing a bit of apprehension. BRONX VALESCENCE: "I don’t know…" Alexis grabs his hand and forces his fingers through the holes as she speaks. ALEXIS TERRY: "Bronx. Chris Callum is a broken man. I broke him, don’t you remember? You may not even need these, but if times get tough, don’t be afraid to use them. Just accidently knock the ref out, and then they are free to play with." Bronx lifts his head to make eye contact with Alexis. BRONX VALESCENCE: "Will you be at ringside?" She scoffs at the idea. ALEXIS TERRY: "Please. Am I ever at ringside?" Again, Bronx lowers his head. Alexis isn’t exactly the type to engage in positive reinforcement. BRONX VALESCENCE: "No. Never." She sighs as she grabs the bottle. ALEXIS TERRY: "Don’t worry though. Just because the Monarchy doesn’t play under the spotlights, that doesn’t mean we don’t play in the shadows. We’re always watching, Bronx." Bronx doesn’t seem convinced. BRONX VALESCENCE: "But who is left?" She nears the bottle to her mouth. ALEXIS TERRY: "I have a plan." She takes a sip. Bronx grimaces at the Deep South Champion. BRONX VALESCENCE: "I just can’t waste this chance." Alexis smiles at him, in a sort of motherly way. ALEXIS TERRY: "This is your time now Bronx. I’m letting you become Undisputed Champion. You’re the only one in this damn company that deserves it. I’m on your side." Bronx nods. He seems a bit more reassured, but this is quickly erased by more dread. BRONX VALESCENCE: "Are you ready for your match with Gavin? I haven’t seen you watching tapes… and when is the last time you worked out?" Another scoff by Alexis. ALEXIS TERRY: "To beat Gavin Achilles, I’ll get as drunk as I possibly can, close my eyes, and swing until I know him out." Bronx is thinking back to Exile… BRONX VALESCENCE: "That didn’t work for Gary Ford." A third scoff by Alexis. Bronx is getting tired of this. ALEXIS TERRY: "Am I Gary Ford?" He pauses. BRONX VALESCENCE: "No." Alexis stands and begins to gather her things. Her match is coming up soon, and she needs to get dressed. ALEXIS TERRY: "Then don’t worry about me. I have a plan." Bronx narrows his eyes, finding some courage. BRONX VALESCENCE: "How come I never get to hear about your plans before they happen? I think I would be more useful to you if I knew what was going on." Alexis begins to rummage through her bag, pulling out her ring books. ALEXIS TERRY: "Hmm…" Bronx stands, speaking with newfound intensity. BRONX VALESCENCE: "I’m ready to take the next step Alexis. I have learned so much from you, but there is still a lot more I can handle. Let me in." She turns to stare him directly into the eye. Is Bronx ready for some sort of real authority? ALEXIS TERRY: "Alright Bronx. I’ll let you in." She pats him on the chest as we fade to black. When we fade back up, we find Paul Marshall and Gavin Achilles in the hallway.
