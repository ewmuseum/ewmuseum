And I am not going to confront him, after just fighting a match.” Jak now in the parking lot, quickly gets into his car but before he can close it Colt says this. COLT COX: “You’re not scared are you?” But he closes the door before we get the answer. Just as his car pulls off, we hear loud foot steps as the camera watches as Seth Seeker approaches. SETH SEEKER: “Was that Jak?” Colt nods his head looking at the car as it turns out of the parking lot as Seeker grins. SETH SEEKER: “He’ll be all mine soon.” Seeker pats Colt and walks off seeming to have been given new life as Colt shrugs and the action is taken back inside the ring. QUOTE When we return to ring side we find the ring set up with tables all around it, ladders every where, and chairs laid out every where in between. “Taste The Blood” by Shawn McPherson hits as the fans begin to naturally boo, though there is a small contingent of cheers in the complex as well. RILEY STEVENS: “These fans don’t seem to be sure if they can trust Tony and The Shadow yet, instead they seem more in favor of the weapons winning in this one.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I myself, have lost a lot of respect for Tony. Doesn’t he realize these fans are just a burden?” Tony Miranda walks out towards the ring with The Shadow following not far behind. Tony walks beside one of the tables, patting it as he jumps up and then steps into the ring. The Shadow stops at the table, gazing at it before walking up the steps and into the ring. Inside the ring the hook can be seen readying for the titles as "To Be Loved" by Papa Roach now hits as the fans, all of them boo. RILEY STEVENS: “Here comes High Voltage, Aspen Chaud, Trent Lost, and Marcus Hister. Remember that Justin Volt is the real champion, but Trent is filling in. And something tells me that Hister might just get involved as well.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “They are a team, why wouldn’t he?” Just as they said Aspen Chaud walks out with both titles on her shoulders, holding one for her boyfriend as Trent Lost walks out beside her with a big smirk on his face. Following behind them is Marcus Hister who is looking right at The Shadow in the ring, Aspen walks into the ring herself as Hister and Lost join her, and she hands the belts to the referee who hooks them up. RILEY STEVENS: “First team to retrieve the belts wins.” As soon as they begin to raise, Aspen looks from Trent to Marcus then backs up as they go on the attack Marcus attacking Shadow while Trent goes after Tony. The fans boo their advantage as Aspen slides out of the ring as the referee frantically calls for the bell. DING DING DING! Four men are in the ring fighting it out, as Aspen looks at one of the ladders on the outside. She picks it up; right away we realize that she’s even having trouble just lifting the steel ladder. Pushing it up into the ring she slides it and almost right as she does Shadow tosses Hister right onto it. The fans cheer the first usage of the ladder as Aspen gasps, not intending for that to happen. Hister rolls around holding his back as it’s now Trent that finds himself in trouble as Tony whips him off the ropes, and as he comes back Shadow crushes him with a Clothesline. Aspen grabs a chair and slides in with it, stopping as she looks at Tony and The Shadow who look at her both with grins. The fans cheer as they begin to surround her in the corner. Tony pats The Shadow on his chest, as Aspen tries to bail out of the ring only to be pulled back in by Shadow who lifts her up into the air. Aspen begins to stomp away at him as Trent and Marcus lift up the ladder, preparing to use it on The Shadow. RILEY STEVENS: “That’s one way to use it.” Trent and Marcus now begin setting up the ladder in the ring, as Aspen on the outside is caught by a kick from Tony before he basically tosses her into the barricade. Trent begins to climb the ladder as Hister, decides to watch. Tony slides back in, where he’s met by Hister. RILEY STEVENS: “Perhaps this is Tony’s chance to retrieve the titles.” Using the ladder that was used earlier he sets it back up in the center of the ring taking time to set it up where he wants it.
