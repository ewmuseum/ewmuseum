GIW — Card

Tuesday Night Evolve, 5 October 2010

5 October 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
EVOLUTION PRO WRESTLING PRESENTS: EVOLVE LIVE FROM THE GALLERIA IN HOUSTON, TEXAS = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = Bryan Battery is the only start to show up for the Battle Royal Declared Winner Automatically. WINNER: BRYAN BATTERY = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! The crowd goes insane as the opening pyro goes off and the whole arena is shaking for what might be the biggest Evolve to date with the sudden influx of new talent being poured in. However instead of heading to the announce table we head to the backstage area where it's David Sinclair with a smile on the screen. DAVID SINCLAIR: "Welcome ladies and gentleman to another episode of Evolve. But as many of you already know tonight is no normal episode. Tonight is no, simple little show. You see we were already heading to the top and now we have some new additions. Because the man that fired me, realized his mistake. And he's here now working with me, in EPW. So without further hesitation, let me go ahead and let Mr. Beckett speak.” The camera pans out and it now shows the face of Blake Beckett, with a simalir grin. Never did we imagine that these two would get back together after what had happened but here it is the two most egotistical Bosses in the world...together. BLAKE BECKETT: "You got that right and I can already feel the difference, I can already tell that the times are changing. And with that comes EPW's continued rise to the top. You see I didn't come along I brought my best stars with me. And tonight they'll be showcased as Wake defends the Galveston Island Championship in the main event. While THAT OTHER show, dies we will be having our biggest show to date. So let this be a message to GIW, you are officially...DEAD.” Blake lets out a big laugh as he turns to David and shakes his hand, in what is a scary faction for the world and mostly GIW. And with the two together you have to think that regardless of their past history, their smarts will certainly help them rise to prominence as we head to the ring for the first contest. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = First match of the night is on it’s way as "Eruption" by Van Halen erupts out of the PA System as the returning Bret Battery is ready for his first match back. He comes out with his own customized shirt and tags the fans hands with an awesome reaction. "Re-Education" by Rise Against then all of a sudden plays and Austin Acquin comes out to very little fan-fare the crowd basically silent. He joins his partner in the ring and right away the two don’t seem to have much chemistry after their past. AUSTIN ST. AUDE: “This if the first team and some may recall these two men were at one time bitter rivals oh how times have changed.” "Pretty Handsome Awkard" by The Used interrupts as Jack Prowler gets a mixed crowd reaction as he heads towards the ring with a big grin on his face. "How You Like Me Now" by The Heavy hits and the crowd is happy to see Travis Tate out from the back. AUSTIN ST. AUDE: “Both teams are made up of new and old EPW guys this one is a team of guys on the rise for sure.” DING! DING! DING! The match starts off with Travis Tate and Austin Acquin. Travis dominates already and shows the EPW roster that he truly is The Big Ticket. He connects with a few stomps and punches. He then goes for the Million Dollar Punch but Acquin narrowly avoids it. He goes for the tag to Prowler as Bret waits for his tag impatiently. Prowler gets technical right here while he locks in a crucifix hold to the arm of Austin Acquin. COLT COX: “Man, Austin Acquin just sucks! He should just go back to GIW! What was Beckett thinking.” Acquin counters by slamming Jack on the ground. Austin then attempts a Sharpshooter but Travis makes the save by hitting a K.O punch to him. Austin is out cold. Prowler makes the pin! 1...2...PIN INTERRUPTED BY BRET BATTERY! Jack Prowler is mad at Bret so Jack goes for a punch but Battery catches it, backs up and hits a Superkick! He does it again to Tate as the crowd is going wild! The Canadian Destroyer lays Austin on Jack and the referee counts to three! 1...2...PIN INTERRUPTED BY BRET BATTERY! Bret turns on his partner out of no where. Battery steps out of the ring to avoid getting disqualified and comes back in! He hits the GTS to Austin Acquin knocking out his own partner before stepping out of the ring and heading to the back showing little care for his partner here tonight. Back in the ring Prowler stares down at Austin who is just now getting up and instead of showing any sort of sorrow for him, he finishes him off by pulling him to the corner setting him up on the top rope and hitting a ‘On the Prowl’ a Top Rope DDT. The crowd isn’t sure how to react as Prowler make the cover 1…2…3! AUSTIN ST. AUDE: “Prowler and Tate get the win though mostly in part to Battery’s betrayal, I guess the bad blood between Acquin and Battery isn’t over.” COLT COX: “Or Battery just got sick of his partner sucking.” Back in the ring Prowler holds up his arm in victory as the always friendly Tate pats him on the back for the win. Prowler however blows him off and exits the ring as the crowd seems to realize that these two were never on the same page after all. Tate shakes his head unhappy about what just happened, as these two rising stars may just be meeting up later on. WINNERS: JACK PROWLER & TRAVIS TATE = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = We head to the backstage area where Chris Armstrong is getting ready for his match in his lockeroom, he picks up his belt and turns however he walks right into Jack Prowler who has a smirk on his face still sweaty from his match. CHRIS ARMSTRONG: "What do you want?" We know the history between these two some would say that Prowler has been the one man that has competed with Chris's spot at the top of the company for awhile now. JACK PROWLER: "I just thought I'd run by and tell you good luck before your match. Because honestly I hope you win, I don't want to see these new guys take over. And not to mention I just came back from Sinclair's Office and he assured me that I was next in line for a shot at that belt. So if you win tonight, you best watch your back because I'll be on the Prowl." Prowler smirks and pats Armstrong on the back before heading out of his locker room as Chris simply shakes his head not to pleased to hear that as we continue. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = We head to the ring where Marcus Hister is lined up across from Pandora for their match. The crowd doesn't seem all that excited and right before the bell can ring the lights dim. The entire crowd and some of the children begin to cheer and yell. On the giant screen the date, “10-31-2010” can be seen. Soon afterwards, the song: Dragon begins to play. Some of the fans know it’s Inchiro Hueda. The song continues to play for a couple of seconds. Until finally, the lights go back on. In the ring laid out is Marcus Hister with Pandora no where to be seen. The crowd begin to talk about the inevitable debut of some new superstar or giant event happening on 10-31-2010, the day of 'On Hallowed Ground', The Pay Per View. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = It’s for one of the duel main events of the evening this one being the one that seemingly could go either way. “Bring It On” by Nathaniel Dawkins echoes through the crowd and the fans begin to cheer the newcomer. Christopher Darren Erns walks out from the back having come over from GIW, in fact just having defeated them in the six man tag match. He heads towards the ring looking completely focused high fiving a couple of fans on his way into the ring. AUSTIN ST. AUDE: “Well this man is getting his shot early, for those of us that don’t watch that OTHER show this is Christopher Darren Erns who is undefeated. Some of you may recall him as one of the guys that beat our team at ‘Unscripted’ which I am not sure is a great thing.” COLT COX: “That just means we stole one of there best up and comers you idiot. Though I have heard rumblings that some of the Originals are not happy about his title shot so early.” CDE gets into the ring and stretches out getting ready for his match having his mouth guard in as “Turn Up The Trouble” by Airbourne hits and the crowd give a mixed reaction some giving respect for the champion while most boo the young champion. Regardless that doesn’t falter his confidence at all as Chris Armstrong has a big smirk on his face. COLT COX: “This is our guy! This guy represents EPW! And that’s why he is our champion right there. Let’s go Chris!” Chris Armstrong has his Evolve Championship over his shoulders as he blows off the fans. He steps up into the ring looking over at his opponent shaking his head as the crowd boos him. He hands the referee the title who hands it out of the ring and then calls for the bell to get this title match under way! DING DING DING! The two men begin to circle each other and in a somewhat crazy moment we realize just what this is, this is the New EPW versus the Old EPW. And you can almost feel the tension between the two as though Beckett and Sinclair may be on the same page; these two men’s faces show that of complete disliking for one another. They go in for a lock up and CDE displaying his in ring smarts whips him over right into a Sleeper Hold. The crowd realizes just how much is on the line here as Armstrong elbows out of the maneuver and he almost seems upset to even be in this match in the first place. The two again go for another lock up but this time Chris is able to hit a knee up into Erns lower regions before hitting a big time kick to Christopher’s jaw. The fans boo as CDE falls to the mat as Armstrong begins to walk around cheering himself on in this match, never one to shy away from being the center of attention. However knowing the importance of the match he grabs Erns as he is getting up and whips him off the ropes. As he bounces back he catches him with a Running Superkick to the jaw that basically sends the crowd into silence finding his early arsenal pretty impressive. Yet again he doesn’t let up to much as he grabs Christopher as he is standing up into the corner. Armstrong taunts the crowd before running full steam at him only for CDE to jump out of the way Armstrong’s force bounces him off the turnbuckle but CDE nails him with a big time Side Kick that takes Chris off his feet. CDE gets down for the first pin attempt of the match 1…2…Kick Out! The fans boo his kick out as Erns gets to his feet nodding his head not really expecting that to be enough to end it. He calls for Armstrong to get to his feet but when he doesn’t he heads up to lift himself only for Armstrong to be playing possum hitting an unseen eye poke before pulling CDE’s face right into the turnbuckle padding. Christopher holds his face in pain as Armstrong gets to his feet and looks out at the crowd repeatedly yelling out “This is my show!” Showing how he feels about the new additions to the roster namely at this point Christopher Darren Erns. COLT COX: “Chris Armstrong doesn’t like this new guy and rightfully so, this is his show.” The fans cheer CDE on in the corner as he begins to get to his feet. However Armstrong is ready and waiting and when he gets to his feet he turns around right into a Snap Suplex from Chris. However he’s not finished there as he lifts him up and nails him with a second Snap Suplex. We all know Snap Suplex’s come in threes as he prepares for one final one but CDE locks his foot around him and is able to maneuver around him to hit a big time German Suplex! Erns is showing some life as both of them lie on the mat in pain both starting to get to their feet at the same time. Christopher goes for a big time Superkick but he uses his last bit of energy on a move that Armstrong avoids at the last second. And then it’s time for the ‘Loaded’ a Vertebreaker! The crowd lets out a collective angry boo as Armstrong lets out a big smirk as he makes the cover this has to be it 1…2…The Referee Notices that CDE has got his foot on the ropes and stops the count. The crowd cheers the fact that Erns has survived a close scare as Chris gets up yelling at the referee that this is his show and that this should be over. He angrily goes back on the attack lifting up Christopher and throwing his seemingly out of it body to the corner. Armstrong pounds his chest and runs across the ring hitting a flawless clothesline. However he doesn’t stop there as he holds up CDE’s body as he gets up to the top rope behind him the crowd realizes that he is setting up to end this thing. He pulls Christopher into position and begins to lift him only for Erns to grab the ropes and throw him over his shoulders off the top rope. The crowd is going nuts as CDE heads to the top rope going for the 450’ Splash! The fans are cheering him on as he gingerly gets to the top rope. Finally when he’s up there he jumps off seeming to be about to hit it when Armstrong hops out of the way at the last second! The crowd lets out a loud gasp as Erns hit hard on the mat both men are basically out of it. COLT COX: “Come on Chris capitalize.” Armstrong looks up at Erns who may have successfully set himself out of this one as he begins to crawl on top of him to end this thing 1…2…KICK OUT! The crowd begins to cheer and chant CDE repeatedly as Armstrong looks up at the referee almost like he’s run out of options. However things suddenly change when his anger kicks in and he screams out that he’s going to end this thing. He lifts up CDE who has nothing left in him and sets him up for a second ‘Loaded’ of the match. The crowd boos as he seems about to hit it and end it when CDE is able to slide off into a Pinning Combination 1…2…3! Out of No Where! AUSTIN ST. AUDE: “NEW CHAMP, NEW CHAMP!” COLT COX: “No….” Erns seems to have just been able to pull it off as Armstrong immediately hops up and tries to plead his case with the referee who walks by him and hands CDE the title. Erns slides out of the ring and walks over to the fans falling back into them exhausted as Armstrong continues to scream at the referee before finally pushing him over. Erns slides back into the ring as Armstrong heads to the back, Erns checks on the referee to see if he is ok. Before the referee gets up and in act of valiance holds up CDE’s hand in the air as we have just crowned a new Evolve Champion in this new era of Evolve! WINNER: CHRISTOPHER DARREN ERNS (New Evolve Champion) = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = Backstage, Blake Beckett can be seen in his office. There’s lots of money on his table and he begins to play with it as he talks on what appears to be a golden cellphone. He laughs and talks about how much money he has made ever since he managed to weaken Revolt from the inside and take the entire quantity that is six superstars. Blake tells whoever he’s talking with on the phone that he’ll see them after the show. It looks like it might be some kind of lover, a woman perhaps. Then again, Blake is a little…. Anyway. A knock can be heard on the door. Blake Beckett suddenly turns serious and then looks towards the door. He quickly rushes to it and opens it revealing Wake Walker. The entire crowd begins to cheer. The two powerful men stare at each other for a few seconds with Blake Beckett managing to pull the trigger first. BLAKE BECKETT: "So I see that you’ve made it, Wake Walker. Looks like you finally came to your sense and accepted that Evolve is what’s going to make you, Evolve is what’s going to be better for your future. Evolve…is…No wait, Wake Walker….is…Evolve! What do you think about that?" WAKE WALKER: "I think you’re a delusional little man but setting that aside, your golden case was essentially fraud. I expected something like this to happen but you still have to compensate me for that case. And no, Wake Walker is not Evolve. Don’t even compare the two. Wake Walker is above your tiny little brand that is Evolve. Face it, I’m the biggest superstar you’re ever going to have." BLAKE BECKETT: "That may be true, Walker….that may be true. Say, why don’t you fetch me some coffee? Your Evolve management superior orders you to do it." Wake Walker laughs and then pushes Blake Beckett to the ground and leaves. Obviously, he’s not getting him the coffee. Blake Beckett at first seems mad but then begins to smile wickedly. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = It’s been a special night thus far and it’s now time for the main event match in which it’ll be for the GI-Championship. We’ve already seen one title change hands tonight so will the supposed new face of Evolve, Wake Walker be able to defend his? “Pursuit of Happiness” by Kid Cudi hits and the crowd begins to give cheers to a man that many have gravitated towards since his start over in GIW. He runs out and high fives a few fans on the way to the ring. AUSTIN ST. AUDE: “This is another former champion though he never did get his belt, which is a shame because it would have been a nice addition to our collection.” COLT COX: “I’m just wondering why this title match is the main event? I mean come on.” Daniels hops over the ropes and jumps up to the top rope as the fans cheer him on. Next up Wake Walker’s theme hits and the crowd gives out a very mixed reaction. Not everyone is excited to have him on Evolve, having been one of GIW’s biggest guys. However there is a strong contingency that wants to see him as well. Regardless the feelings are very spread out as he makes his entrance from the back, frankly a site we though we’d never see. COLT COX: “This is just disgraceful; get this guy out of here.” AUSTIN ST. AUDE: “He’s probably the biggest star in all of Galveston. I’d get used to him if I were you, he’s our ticket to the top.” COLT COX: “Yeah cause he seemed so willing to be here.” Walker doesn’t seem to be too unhappy however he is instead quite focused on the match at hand. He heads down towards the ring not even taking his eyes off of Ryan Daniels who is stretching out in the ring. Walker enters the ring and hands over his title to referee who then shows it off the crowd before setting it outside of the ring starting the match. DING DING DING! This match is underway as the crowd cheers and lets out a flurry of cheers for the main event this evening. The two men are ready to fight with Walker looking much more physically dominant something Ryan is of course used to. The two come to the middle and are ready to go at it. Daniels catches him with a big fist to the face and then lands another one to Walker who shows little reaction to them. Daniels shakes his head and runs off the ropes running forward and hitting a big forearm that only sets back Walker a bit, Wake seems to be trying to assert early fear into his opponent. Daniels shakes his head and yells at Wake to do something however he still doesn’t move at all, just trying to get into Ryan’s head and it seems to be working until Daniels runs off the ropes and connects with a Dropkick to Wake’s knee that brings him down to one knee. He runs off the ropes again but Walker gets up this time and catches him in his grasps and knees him in the gut and nails him with a Suplex! The fans are actually getting behind Daniels a bit which is a bit new for Walker who has been known as a big face over on Revolt. Daniels gets to his feet as Walker stands behind him waiting for him to turn around. However Wake’s failure to go on the attack hurts him as Daniels nails him with a Facebreaker Knee Smash! The crowd cheers as Wake stumbles back holding his face as Daniels nails him with a Russian Legsweep as a follow up as the crowd is getting behind him as Daniels starts a slow clap as he stalks Walker from behind. AUSTIN ST. AUDE: “Could Ryan possibly win this? What an upset it would be.” The crowd continues to clap for him as he actually seems to be going for ‘The Beefy’ early. Walker gets up and is set up for it before rolling off his back and as soon as Ryan turns around he is grabbed and literally tosses across the ring by Walker. The crowd is stunned by that act of power by Walker. Daniels however quickly hops to his feet always on the move as he runs at Walker and gets flipped around by a monster clothesline from Walker. The tide is certainly turning here as Walker seems completely focused on his opponent staring down at Daniels who for the first time in the match seems to be showing some pain and its massive pain for sure. Daniels tries to get to his feet almost looking to be crawling to get out of the ring for a breather before getting hit with a Drive by Kick that seems to basically rip off Daniels head off. The crowd is in shock as Wake has suddenly made this one a laugher in an instant as Daniels doesn’t seem to be moving. Walker however doesn’t even make the cover instead stares down at Daniels as he begins to stir a bit. Daniels uses Wake’s legs to get to his feet then grabbing him by the tights and trying to get up but Walker shows little in terms of mercy as he lifts him up and hits a Sit-Down Powerbomb that ends it right then and there as Walker makes the pin 1…2...3! That’s it. AUSTIN ST. AUDE: “For a second I thought Ryan Daniels was in this match.” COLT COX: “You know I might like this guy after all.” Walker is handed the belt in the ring as the crowd gives him another set of mixed reactions. He stands dominantly over Ryan Daniels fallen body as he holds up his belt as the show begins to fade out on GI-Champion and perhaps the new face of Evolve.
