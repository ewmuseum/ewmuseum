GIW — Card

Revolt, 9 September 2010

9 September 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Galveston Island Wrestling Presents: Thursday Night Revolt! Live! From Carlos and Charlies! on September 9th, 2010 BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! The scene opens as the cameras zoom around the GIW Ring, signaling that GIW Revolt has begun! The fans go wild and chant the names of their favorite superstars as the camera focuses in on Oliver Thibodaux and Riley Stevens at the Revolt announce stand these two known as the voices of the show. RILEY STEVENS: “Welcome to another action filled show of Revolt, where tonight the card is looking pretty stacked. I’m going to get this part out of the way before Oliver butchers it. Tonight we’ll see our ‘GIW Women’s Champion’ Kacie Church and her rival Alexii Ravynova in action. Kacie last week pinned Pandora in a tag team match after a distraction and now many wonder if she can do it by herself. And also we’ll see Alexii Ravynova against Ashleigh 3 Tears to try to cement herself as the Number One Contender, pretty packed night for the women tonight.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Thankfully that’s not the whole show. We’ll also see Ryan Daniels versus J.J. Evans two men that will be trying to rise up in the ranks around here, the winner may be in line for one of the mid card belts.” RILEY STEVENS: “Then in what many believe could end up a classic we have the former ‘Galveston Island Champion’ Scott Rydell versus the current ‘GIW Legacy Champion’ Jamie Vaccaro. These two are known as some of the best around here; both have been on a bit of a bad stretch can one of them get out of it?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “And the main event is one in which we have no idea how it’ll go down because we have two teams of enemies paired up. On one side you have Jake Kennedy and the ‘ION Television Champion’ Tony Miranda whose eventual match at Unscripted has already been booked. And on the other side we have recent rivals Eric Sparrow and ‘Galveston Island Champion’ Wake Walker. Can these two teams get along long enough to have a match?” RILEY STEVENS: “It’s going to be interesting for sure, but currently in the ring we have Aspen Chaud who has the night off and will probably have something to say about EPW tonight.” The camera cuts to the ring where Aspen Chaud is standing wearing a pink dress and heels and of course a big smile on her face the fans gave her a huge ovation when she came out earlier but give her a smaller cheer when it appears she is about to speak her mind. ASPEN CHAUD: “On Evolve this week my call out was answered by David Sinclair and he suddenly accepted. This comes a week after only one of his stars showed up for a match, thus only one of them earned my respect. So now he says I’ll be facing their best which makes me wonder just who that is…." Aspen taps her chin a few times with a bit of a dumb look on her face seeming to be thinking long and hard about this only then coming out of it shaking her head and laughing. ASPEN CHAUD: “I think we all realize that Sinclair is behind his little boy Chris Armstrong. So you know what I say to them. I say bring it on. I don’t care if it’s Chris, Bret Battery, or anyone else. At the end of the day no matter who I face I am going to beat them! Because at the end of the day your best is GIW’s worst…and that’s a fact. So bring who you want regardless of who they are…they’ll all end up tapping out to my K.M.A." Aspen smirks planting one hand on her waist as she speaks with so much confidence as she addresses EPW, never having shown any fear of them. ASPEN CHAUD: “Last week you snuck by an attack on us, this week you won’t be so lucky. EPW we are going to put you in your place soon enough, because at the end of the day we are the better company. And if you don’t think so, well you can KISS MY…" Aspen lowers her microphone as the crowd finishes her sentence before she heads out of the ring and having said her piece we have a short intermission. JAMAL JACKSON: Ladies and Gentlemen, the following contest is is scheduled for one fall!, Making their way to the ring first, From Hollywood, California... This is Ashleigh Three Tears! The lights go out, And "Getting Away With Murder" by Jeffree Star hits the P.A system. As the music picks up a few white spotlights hit the stage, And Ashleigh is standing there looking out in the crowd with souless expression on her face. She then begins to march down the ramp almost in rhythm with the song. Once she approaches the ring she grabs onto the middle rope, and then pulls herself up to the apron. She then walks in between the middle and bottom rope and into the ring. Ashleigh walks to the middle of the ring, and puts her hands on her hips as she waits for her opponent. After the infamous loud drum beats from Amatory's "Butterfly Effect" The fans in the arena start to share their feelings towards Ms. Ravynova. After a few seconds, Alexii Ravynova walks out from behind the curtain as a mixture of red and black lights sling around the stage. Alexii stops before the ramp starts, and takes a deep breath, and looks around the arena while "Butterfly" By "Amatory" blares on the public announcement system. JAMAL JACKSON: And her opponent, She hails from Marieville-Quebec, Canada, weighing in tonight at one hundred and forty pounds... Alexii Ravynova! Alexii begins to slowly make her way to the ring, as the fans continue the constitutive Boos. She finally reaches the end of the ramp, and slides in the ring, and locks eye-to-eye with her opponent .. "Ding!,Ding!,Ding!" As soon as the bell rings out, the fight is on! A3T runs right at Ravynova, and is throwing anything she can! Fists, feet, knees! She's giving Ravynova her all, as Ravynova is in the corner getting kneed repeatedly by A3T! The ref begins the count"1!.......2!......3!.....Come on! get off of her!" A3T gets off for a second, She then points at the ref, and tells him to shut up. A3T goes to grab Ravynova who's leaning up against the corner, but as soon as she does Ravynova quickly breaks out of the grapple, and throws one hell of a punch to the heart! A3T stumbles back trying to get her balance back, as Ravynova looks to continue to capitalize on it! So she throws yet another hard blow; a Hooligan Kick, - this sends A3T crashing to her knees... as A3T slowly gets up, Ravynova goes for yet another Hooligan Kick, this connects with a loud pop! She knocks A3T all the way into the far corner. She walks over to there, and attempts to lift her up for a possible superplex. But as soon as Ravynova picks her up, and sets her on the turnbuckle... A3T sends a hard kick right into the skull of Alexii Ravynova! Ravynova was on the middle rope setting up for the 'plex, but after that perfectly placed kick, She was sent tumbling off the middle rope cashing against the canvas! Ravynova slowly retains her composure... But as soon as she straightens up, she's met with Asleigh Three Tears' amazing CROSSBODY from the top rope! She lands on a standing Ravynova, and catches the leg while the ref drops for the count! "1!...................2!..............No! kick out!" Alexii is without a doubt a veteran of the ring, and she's not that easy to put away. A3T gets up, and picks up Ravynova by her head, But as she tries to make something happen, Ravynova quickly punches her in the gut, and grapples A3T by the head. She starts to throw some forearms into the back of the head of Ashleigh, just taking her down a notch with each hit! We all know because of A3T's past... When going against her you have to use your mind as your most dangerous weapon, But then again of course Alexii knew this, and that's why she was picking her apart so well. After the four forearm shots, Ravynova leaps in the air, and hits a beautiful dropkick! A3T smacks the mat, and then gets up only to get met with another one! Ashleigh fly's back again, and quickly gets up, but gets hit with yet another huge dropkick! Ravynova's on fire!!! As soon as A3T gets back up, she stumbles into the side corner, and Ravynova wastes no time to take off running, and to leap in the air spring boarding on the middle rope on the left side of the turn buckle, before rocketing at a 360 angle, and he ends up on the right side of the turnbuckle, and he connects with a out of this world kick to the head sending Ashleigh flying to the ring canvas! My god! Orton is barely moving after that kick! Ravynova walks around with her head held high, and her arms on her waist. After her little 'mini party' Alexii grabs Asheligh by her head, and pulls her to her feet. Having a hold under her chin, she pulls it back; arching the back of A3T before throwing her arm around her neck, and swinging side way hitting a devastating swinging neckbreaker. Ashleigh's head bounces off the mat, and knocks her basically out of consciousness. Ravynova smiles with delight as she once again picks up the lifeless corpse that is Ashleigh Three Tears. With a grin on her face, Ravynova stands behind A3T and bends her forward. She pulls one of Ashleighs's arms back between her legs and holds it, while she hooks the other arm. Then Alexii Ravynova lifts Ashleigh Three Tears up over her shoulder, and delivers a amazing move that she calls, 'IGNORANCE'! Ravynova then covers the knocked out body that is A3T! "1!..........................2!.....................3! Ring the bell!" "Ding!,Ding!,Ding!" JAMAL JACKSON: And the winner of the match as a result of pinfall... Alexii Ravynova!! WINNER: ALEXII RAVYNOVA After the women’s match we head back to the back where our champion Wake Walker has his belt sitting in front of him as he seems to be in a completely relaxed state before the door flings open and its Eric Sparrow that walks into the room with a big grin on his face. ERIC SPARROW: “Hey there pal’, you know these past few weeks have been a bit fun between us right? I mean the back and forth it’s been a good time. But tonight I hear that they are teaming us up putting the two of us on the same side? Apparently they don’t realize what I really want is on the table there, but they’ll realize that soon enough. Regardless I think it’s best the two of us work together, wouldn’t be good to see the best and the second best in GIW lose to those guys right?” Eric smiles down at Wake who sits up not seeming that amused especially with the way Sparrow implied he was the second best in GIW. He lifts his belt up over his shoulder to basically taunt Sparrow. WAKE WALKER: “You know I want to beat them as well, so yes let’s team. But I hope you’re not thinking about trying any thing out there. After all how has that worked out for you the last two times?” Sparrow nods his head knowing that this is true as the tension between these two is fairly evident from the awkward silence. ERIC SPARROW: “Perhaps the third time is the charm.” Sparrow pats Walker on the shoulder as he turns and leaves, leaving Walker with even more question marks and if people thought they weren’t going to co-exist before this may have just given them more doubts. Pursuit of Happiness by Kid Cudi hit’s the P.A System and out bops GIW’s plucky and loveable Ryan “RD” Daniels to a modest pop. The beats of B.M.F by Rick Ross cuts the sounds and out comes J.J. “Bob” Evans doing typical Heel like shenanigans. Ryan Daniels lives up to his RD nickname by starting the match off with some “Risky Damage” in the form of a Missile Dropkick right out of the gate before locking a Boston Crab near the ropes in. J.J gets out without tapping but obviously flabbergasted by the turn of events. Ryan Daniels bum rushes in, like a rushing bum, for clothesline but J.J gives him a bum cheek and dodges. Ryan comes in and brawls with J.J for a little bit gaining a small upper hand and oh damn, a overused cliché, I mean Right Hook sending Daniels back across the ring, J.J Evans showcases why the Road Agent of this match likes helping in Alexxi Ravynova matches by hitting a big running Medium Shoe on Ryan Daniels RILEY STEVENS: Wow hard boot by J.J there, and in an instant the tide has shifted. OLIVER THIBODAUX: Daniels is starting to look like my mom after a hard night of my dad drinking. J.J goes for a Single Leg Boston Crab but Ryan Daniels shows his disdain for seafood by flipping J.J over. He locks a Cobra Clutch on J.J but he has disdain for poisonous snakes and breaks free. He gets behind Daniels and signals for a German Suplex but Daniels counters into a Russian Legsweep cementing both wrestlers dependence on alcohol which is no laughing matter at all. Despite being much smaller Daniels is able to hurk and jerk with all his might slamming J.J back into the mat with a Suplex. He goes back to working the leg with a Boston Crab until J.J. breaks it. J.J hit’s a Back Body Drop and climbs the turnbuckle. He makes like Jay Jay the Jet Plane and goes for a Moonsault. Ryan Daniels proves why the original is better and pulls a Thomas The Tank Engine moving out of the way in Shining Time (Station.) They couldn’t work a Blue’s Clues reference in the match but a Latino woman with a backpack was kicked out of the arena for not having her papers. RILEY STEVENS: And now Ryan Daniels can take control. And with that Ryan Daniels does indeed take control stomping J.J’s legs further wreaking them. J.J tries to make it back up but Ryan Daniels dropkicks his legs from underneath him. Writhing around in pain Ryan Daniels locks an Indian Native American Deathlock on J.J. J.J makes it to the ropes. He stumbles up and is met with a Kneebreaker Face Smash meaning the only action J.J will get tonight is a Nose Job. He finishes it with a Jawbreaker. Ryan Daniels signals for The Beefy, which is much weirder then you would think looking back on it. But J.J is too Beefy! Not wanting to risk a hernia Ryan Daniels let’s go. He signals for The Meaty which is even more vulgar and J.J. signals for The British Express another move that could serve as a euphemism in fact Cloverleaf Leg Lock could be considered the least likely to be confused for sexual based things in this entire match. Anyway J.J grabs Ryan’s arms for his double arm DDT but Ryan smashes his twigs and berries. He whips J.J into the ropes and hit’s the Meaty for the one two three. WINNER: RYAN DANIELS We head to the backstage area where a sweaty Ryan Daniels has just reached the back after a solid win out in the ring. He is walking the halls when he comes upon Jamie Vaccaro with his GIW Legacy Championship. He stops and looks over at him with a smile. RYAN DANIELS: “Hey what’s good bro, heard that your match was cancelled tonight. Sucks what happened to Rydell.” Jamie looks up from the catering area over at Ryan Daniels really no bad blood between the two here tonight as there really isn’t any tension in the air. JAMIE VACCARO: “Yeah man but its fine good job in your match though. Looks like you might be my next contender for this belt right here.” The two smile and nod at each other as Ryan holds out a hand and the two high five then pull each other in for one of those hip hugs. Daniels walks off the other way but when Vaccaro turns around he’s crushed out of no where. The camera pans to a show masked figure standing over Jamie as he grabs him and tosses him into the food at catering. The man holds his belt in his hands staring at it, before tossing it down on Jamie. The masked man leaves as Ryan Daniels rushes back into the scene having heard the ruckus. RYAN DANIELS: “Someone get help!” Ryan screams out for some help as he leans down and checks on Jamie as a few people crowd around and the segment ends. Match was cut due to rain, the full match can be read on GIW.com. Kacie Church came out on top. WINNER: KACIE CHURCH We return to the backstage area where this time it’s the site of Jake Kennedy with a big smirk on the screen as he sits watching TV in his lockeroom, perhaps the show going on tonight. Soon the door slams open and in walks Tony Miranda holding his ‘ION Television Championship’. TONY MIRANDA: “Listen! You better count your blessings I am not slamming your head through that TV set right now. Because sadly later I got to carry you through a tag match. So you better do your damn best so we can win, because maybe then I’ll take it easy on you after the match!” Miranda is fuming and it’s reasonable after what happened last week after their title match but Jake Kennedy doesn’t seem overly scared. JAKE KENNEDY: “You’ll be lucky if I even show up.” Jake stands up to him both men staring each other down we’ll see this match in not to long but tonight they’ll have to be partners. TONY MIRANDA: “I’d be better off two on one anyways. If I were you after the match I would get out of harms way because if you don’t you are going to end up hurt. Because if you thought what you did to me last week was anything, it’s going to be a million times worse tonight. I’m going to destroy you.” Miranda heads out of the room leaving Jake Kennedy to think about his plan of action later tonight and if you thought Walker and Sparrow had issues these two may have even more of them. From Jake Kennedy’s locker to the office of Blake Beckett we go as again sitting in their seats is Blake Beckett with his feet up while Amanda Rowland sits in her chair sitting at the edge of it. AMANDA ROWLAND: “We have this whole Aspen thing where they are scheduling a match of her versus their best. Then on their show Joshua Acquin goes over and gets himself put in a match on their show this week plus scheduled a match with three on three. Last week Scott Rydell is attacked and is unable to compete this week. And now Jamie Vaccaro gets attacked and I can only assume it’s one of them that did it. This whole thing has gotten out of hand.” Amanda lays back in her chair unable to cope with all the issues that have come about the past week as Blake Beckett puts his feet down and sits forward about to talk when the door opens and in walks Don Hairston. DON HAIRSTON: “Thank again for giving me another chance Boss. However you won’t regret it when I end up rising straight to the top around here.” Hairston quickly heads back out of the room closing the door as Amanda looks from him over to Blake throwing up her hands confused. BLAKE BECKETT: “I’m giving him a second chance. He impresses me then we keep him, if not he’s gone right away. Besides it can’t hurt to have a few people around here that hate EPW as much as we do. But about the whole situation, you wanted my decision.” Amanda seems tired of it and nods her head quickly wanting to hear what Blake’s decision is on the whole debacle. BLAKE BECKETT: “At Unscripted we are going to let them come! And that’s not all the Aspen Chaud match? Well it’s going to happen they can choose whoever they want to face her. And the three on three match, EPW versus GIW. That’s happening to. Three of their guys versus three of ours. Maybe then on fair ground they’ll realize who the better brand is and who is that?” Blake grins and points to Amanda to finish his sentence but she instead rolls her eyes and heads out of the room as Blake whispers under his breath ‘GIW’ as the segment ends. "Valhalla" by Blind Guardian scares everybody as it suddenly plays. Tony Miranda starts jumping around and yelling, getting himself pumped up. The crowd cheers and roots on for Tony Miranda, giving him a nice applause. Tony Miranda quickly enters the ring and poses on one of the turnbuckles. As he’s doing so, "I Will Not Bow" by Breaking Benjamin appears to disrupt the entire celebration. Jake Kennedy comes out receiving a mixture of boos and cheers but the cheers appear to be predominantly dominating. Jake Kennedy slowly walks to the ring with a cocky grin on his face. Once he’s in, Jake Kennedy and Tony Miranda begin to have a talk with each other but it looks like it’ll probably get aggressive if it lasts any longer. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Lots of tension between these two. Look at that belt on Tony Miranda, doesn‘t it look great!” RILEY STEVENS: “Speaking of which, here comes Eric Sparrow!” “Sum 41 Fatlip” gets everybody’s attention. Before Eric Sparrow can even come out, the crowd gives a really hostile reaction to Eric Sparrow. There are cheers out there but after having interrupted a great match, the crowd doesn’t appear to have forgiven Sparrow. Sparrow tries to ignore the fans, enters the ring and then starts telling his opponents that they’re nothing. “Dazed and Confused” begins to play. For forty seconds, absolutely nothing happens. Afterwards, Wake Walker comes out. The lights go out and the song continues to play. Eventually, Wake Walker begins to walk down the ramp and enters the ring slowly, not taking his eyes off Eric Sparrow. Once the music stops and the lights are back on, Eric Sparrow tells Wake Walker not the screw up. Wake Walker calmly nods. The match begins with Eric Sparrow and Tony Miranda. The bell sounds. RILEY STEVENS: “Tony Miranda looks extremely pumped up. It‘s kind of strange, actually.” Eric Sparrow rushes at Tony Miranda who manages to lift his leg and hit a big boot. Eric Sparrow looks like he’s in trouble already. Tony Miranda clotheslines Eric Sparrow once, Eric gets up… another clothesline. Tony Miranda bounces off the ropes and tries another clothesline. Eric Sparrow dodges it, Tony Miranda bounces off the other side of the ring and hit’s a MASSIVE discuss clothesline! Eric Sparrow looks hurt already. Tony Miranda quickly picks up Eric Sparrow and hit’s a suplex. Then, he picks him up again and hit’s a DDT. Jake Kennedy tags himself in. Tony Miranda looks back at Jake with a , “What the hell, man?” type of face. Tony Miranda exit’s the ring and goes on the apron. Jake Kennedy begins stomping on Eric Sparrow several times - maybe even too many times. Eric Sparrow manages to grab the leg of Jake Kennedy and then applies a heel hook! Jake Kennedy struggles for survival but manages to hold onto the ropes. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Looks like Jake Kennedy is off his game this match! Any longer and he won’t survive. RILEY STEVENS: “Maybe Jake Kennedy isn‘t ready to hang around with the likes of all the other three? OLIVER THIBODAUX: “That may just be true. It‘s a damn shame too, Jake Kennedy is almost there, we agree on that.” Jake Kennedy gets up whilst favoring one leg. Eric Sparrow looks like he’s going to fly, AIR SPARROW! Jake Kennedy is hit! Eric Sparrow gets a cheap shot on Tony Miranda and goes for the cover. 1...2.. Tony Miranda somehow made it to help Kennedy out. Eric Sparrow slaps Tony Miranda. Kennedy is on his knees about now. Tony Miranda looks like he’s infuriated by that. He tries to go after Eric Sparrow but Sparroq quickly backs off and falls to the floor. Wake Walker tags himself in and quickly hit’s the Trickster on Jake Kennedy! Tony Miranda begins to run after Eric Sparrow only for Eric to jump the barricade and as soon as Tony Miranda tries the same, Eric hit’s a drop kick that devastates Tony Miranda who falls the wrong way. As Wake Waker is celebrating, Eric Sparrow quickly enters the ring and clubs Wake Walker on the back of his head. Eric Sparrow grabs Wake Walker and locks in the Purple Haze. Wake Walker hits Eric several times in the gut but he slowly fades away until Eric hit’s the devastating DDT. Eric Sparrow laughs and begins to walk up the ramp slowly. Tony Miranda stands up, holding his head and looks at Eric Sparrow. Meanwhile, Jake Kennedy looks very confused and looks around. WINNERS: ERIC SPARROW & WAKE WALKER
