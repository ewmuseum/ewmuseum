GIW — Card

Revolt, 11 November 2010

11 November 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: Thursday Night Revolt! LIVE NOVEMBER 11th, FROM GALVESTON’S NORTH BEACH IN GALVESTON, TEXAS BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! What a night after nearly a three month feud with EPW David Sinclair and Blake Beckett have been sent packing as GIW has stood tall eliminating all the Evolve stars very handily with this show set up as the show after, a week off left everyone waiting as we return to Galveston’s North Beach for this show a fitting return for the night as we head to the announce table. RILEY STEVENS: “Well Oliver I never thought I’d say this but you have no idea how happy I am to be here tonight commentating with you and most importantly it’s great to be back here on Revolt! For a bit there I thought we were gone.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I never doubted.” RILEY STEVENS: “We come back with a statement starting the show with a match up between Santa Claus who we got from EPW against our former ‘ION Television Champion’ Chicken.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “...Yay…we are back.” RILEY STEVENS: “Then we have a match up between Jake Kennedy and Paul Defias where the winner will be named the Number One Contender to the ION Television Championship. Our champion Joshua Acquin is currently hurt so we are not sure, who will be his new contender?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Then a match I personally hope Sinclair watches as four of our GIW guys fight for an Evolve title shot. Haha that’s awesome, who will it be Cory Chevelle, Mark Devine, Thor Vegard, and Tony Miranda.” RILEY STEVENS: “Then we have a rather intriguing match when Aspen Chaud teams with returning ‘Galveston Island Champion’ Wake Walker against Eric Sparrow and Inchiro Hueda. After all we’ve seen between Aspen and Wake how could they possibly ever team? Plus with Eric’s volatile ways how will he team with Hueda? We will have to see regardless its Revolt and we are here to stay!” Riley throws up a fist in a show of passion however Oliver slams his face into his palms embarrassed as "Bullseye" by Aly and AJ plays. The crowd goes into a standing ovation. They all clap and cheer for their beloved hero, Aspen Chaud. Aspen can’t help but to be extremely excited and begins to jump up and down and says hi to some fans. She slowly goes down the ramp and slaps the fans of hands and even has somewhat long conversations with them. Meanwhile, the commentators are talking all about how Aspen Chaud and her team managed to defeat EPW in a rather easy match to save the entire brand of Revolt. Aspen smiles at the crowd, enters the ring and then begins to pose on the turnbuckle out of sheer happiness. She actually pretty much has the crowd in the palm of her hand. After she’s done pandering to them, she grabs a microphone and allows for the cheering to be quieter. Once it’s silent enough for Aspen to speak, she does. Actually, she can’t. She appears to be so happy about the entire situation that it actually takes her a while to get enough composure to say what she wants to say. ASPEN CHAUD: “Thank you! Thank you so much! This is the most amazing reception that I have ever received! Thanks, all of you! Last Sunday was possibly one of the greatest moments of my life! Galveston Island Wrestling managed to defeat the evil Evolve! It was a heated battle but thankfully; in the end, we prevailed! We sure did… however, there’s something that’s bothering me. Although we’re all happy that Evolve is finally gone, I don’t feel entirely complete. You see… at On Hallowed Ground, a man revealed his true colors. This man showed the entire world that he truly isn’t indifferent to Galveston Island Wrestling. This man displayed just what type of hatred he has for this company and that man is none other than Wake Walker. On Sunday, when it was only down to him… Wake chose to fight until the end and even managed to eliminate Daredevil from our team. I can understand his reasoning to some degree… maybe it was all pride. You know, Wake Walker is the type of guy who has far too much pride for his own good. I could see how simply lying down would have been embarrassing to do in front of everyone… but did anyone see just exactly what happened out there after Revolt won the match? Walker pushed me out of the blue but I know why…I know that he did that out of sheer hatred. He was… all this time, in favor of Evolve. With that said, some of us started dubbing that push he gave me as, “The Great Betrayal”. Indeed it was. I thought you were different, Wake. Even when you said you didn’t care, I respected that… but to care more for Evolve? Why, Wake…why? I’ll tell you why… it’s because you are a coward. You can talk in metaphors and be a nonconformist without a purpose all you want but when it comes down to the action, making real decisions and handling stress, you cower in the face of danger. Face it Walker, you’re nothing but a no good…” Suddenly, “Dazed And Confused” by Wake Walker begins to play. The song plays for about ten seconds before Wake Walker comes out with a microphone in hand and his Galveston Island title on his shoulder. He looks around at the crowd who appears to be booing him a lot. He walks down the ramp, walks up the steel steps and enters the ring. He gets in the face of Aspen Chaud for as few moments. Aspen Chaud looks like she wants to attack Wake Walker. Wake Walker backs off and then begins to speak. WAKE WALKER: “Enough of the lies, Aspen. Me pushing you was no “Great Betrayal” if anything, this is a great misunderstanding. If anything, this is a great uprising.” Walker smirks lowering his microphone as he allows Aspen to give her response. ASPEN CHAUD: “Stop talking about, “what’s to come” and start talking about what transpired on Sunday! We want answers Walker, not your silly little codes!” Aspen grins seeming ready to hear his story but Wake shakes his head chuckling under his breath as he responds. WAKE WALKER: “You never WERE too smart, Aspen….or strong for that matter. No wonder you only held the Galveston Island title for only one day.” Walker smiles as the crowd boos, many of them remembering her first reign as the champion admittedly cut short by Beckett. ASPEN CHAUD: “Don’t you dare even go there! Oh yeah, Aspen…the big idiot, right? Yeah, well this idiot was the one who led GIW to victory and eradicated Evolve! Oh yeah, and not so strong? You mean after I defeated you and laid you flat on your ass at the Pay-Per-View?” Crowd begins to cheer for Aspen, laughing at her insult as she now gets a chuckle at Wake’s expense. WAKE WALKER: “Oh, Aspen? You defeated me? What did you say just a few moments ago…? That I should’ve lied down and take the pin? Is that what you said?” Aspen nods her head and leans forward expecting him to finish finally asking the question. ASPEN CHAUD: “What’s your point?” WAKE WALKER: “My point, Aspen, is that you’re Barbie Makeover that led Revolt to, “victory” may have hurt but… you know me.” Aspen begins to look somewhat confused as Wake gives off a bit of a smug look with his words. WAKE WALKER: “It takes more than just one of those to take me out. Do you honestly think I was actually knocked out to the point where I couldn’t kick out?” Aspen begins to shake her head immediately doubting what he’d said. ASPEN CHAUD: “What are you trying to say? That you allowed yourself to lose?! That you took this one for the team?!” WAKE WALKER: “That’s precisely what I’m saying.” Aspen shakes her head even more so, not even for a second agreeing with what Wake said. ASPEN CHAUD: “That’s absurd! You’re so used to winning that I think all of it has gotten to your head! Just admit that you lost! No wait, I’ll do it for you since you’re probably too scared to do it! C’mon everybody! Walker lost! Walker lost! Walker lost!” Crowd responds and begins to cheer/ chant “Walker Lost” back as she lowers her mic smiling knowing that it has to be annoying Wake. WAKE WALKER: “Nice pandering to the crowd, Aspen.” ASPEN CHAUD: “Either way, Wake… you know as much as I know that deep down inside, I have the potential to beat you and I showed that on Sunday.” Aspen grins as the fans give out a little bit of a murmur realizing that those were indeed fighting words. WAKE WALKER: “Speaking of deep down inside, that’s about as far as I went with you in terms of our relationship and I’m glad it went no further than that.” Aspen looks angered and then gives Wake Walker a look like she’s about to lose it before screaming into the mic. ASPEN CHAUD: “…That is a lie!” WAKE WALKER: “Regardless of whether it is or isn’t a lie, the fact of the matter is that when it comes to this crowd, you can say whatever you want and if they agree with it, they’ll cheer you. People like you keep pandering to them, making sure that you have them at the palm of your hand to do your bidding when you’re too weak to do it by yourself. These people are like puppets, they can be manipulated by pulling a couple of strings by saying things you really don’t mean but as soon as you do or say something that they don’t like, all of a sudden you’re the bad guy.” Crowd boos him almost seeming to be reading off a script as Wake looks out, listening just expecting it. WAKE WALKER: “See what I mean? They refuse to acknowledge the truth.” ASPEN CHAUD: “This crowd technically never liked you, Walker. You always barged into the ring thinking you’re the greatest thing since sliced bread. You’re not, Walker. You may be good, but you’re not THAT good!” WAKE WALKER: “Really? That isn’t what you said a couple of weeks ago.” Crowd begins to get on the side of Walker laughing at another one of his jokes as Aspen rolls her eyes. ASPEN CHAUD: “Enough of these immature jokes! You have yet to explain your actions. Had enough time to formulate a lie yet, Wake?” WAKE WALKER: “No, but I’ve had enough time to compile the truth in such a way that even a fool like yourself can understand. I did it because…” Aspen sticks out her head waiting for his reasoning as Wake takes a second seeming to be thinking about his words. WAKE WALKER: “…Because…” ASPEN CHAUD: “Out with it!” Wake Walker suddenly looks angry very annoyed by Aspen’s badgering as she and the fans wait for his reasoning. ASPEN CHAUD: “C’mon, go ahead! Admit to all your ex-fans that you did this for Evolve!” Out of no where Wake Walker slaps Aspen Chaud as the fans boo his actions a bit some just taken off guard. WAKE WALKER: “Don’t you dare accuse me of that again!” Aspen Chaud tries to hit a clothesline in a fit of rage but Walker dodges it and quickly exit’s the ring. Wake Walker begins to walk to the back. He mouths, “I’ll tell you Aspen…I’ll tell you soon. Sooner than you think.” Suddenly, Amanda Rowland comes out with a microphone in hand. Wake Walker turns around and is surprised to see her. She begins to speak. AMANDA ROWLAND: “You know what, Walker? Aspen got me thinking. What if they brain washed you down there in Evolve and you are like an infection waiting to spread and take over once again after being forced to be dormant? I won’t allow this so to see just what your intentions truly are… you will be placed in a tag match against two superstars of my choosing. In fact, I’ll say them now. It’ll be Eric Sparrow and the newcomer Inchiro Hueda versus Wake Walker and … his partner, Aspen Chaud! Good luck.” Aspen Chaud talks to Wake from afar and tells him, “I’ll have my eye on you!” Wake Walker smiles and then takes himself and his title backstage. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = Back in the ring the fans have a look of disappointment as they are handed out a ‘Paul Defias Greatest Hits’ DVD literally the cheapest thing you could buy. The man handing them out is Santa Claus as he returns to the ring one brave fan throwing the DVD back in the ring as suddenly “Chain Hang Low” by Jibbs hits and the fans absolutely pop, as out from the back comes Chicken. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I literally refuse to commentate this.” RILEY STEVENS: “Sadly Chicken’s greatest hits DVD would be a whole lot longer then Paul Defias’s.” Chicken heads down to the ring sliding into the ring and looking at disgust at the DVD on the mat, though you can’t see it since he’s in a costume. However Santa shakes his head and begins to dig through his bag as the referee calls for the bell. DING DING DING! Chicken patiently waits as Santa digs through his stuff again and then pulls out a wrapped present. Santa hands it to Chicken who nods his head and begins to open it up, looking happy about it. It’s a box and he opens it up and it’s a T-Shirt. He pulls it apart and to everyone’s dismay it is a Jack Johnson t-shirt a collector’s item only because of the few that were even sold, Chicken throws the shirt away in disgust then out of no where gives Santa an ‘Egg Barrage’ better known as throwing a lot of eggs at Saint Nick. The fans cheer as Santa falls around in pain before falling to the mat out cold with Chicken going for the pin 1…2…3! RILEY STEVENS: “He’s back, on his way to a title.” Chicken hops up and throws up his arms as the fans cheer him on, however he stops when “Fatlip” by Sum 41 hits and out from the back comes Eric Sparrow to a lot of boos from the crowd. Chicken lets him into the ring figuring he saw the disgusting act that Santa committed by handing out Defias and Johnson apparel. Sparrow heads immediately to the top rope crushing Santa with a ‘Spinal Tap’ an amazing looking Spiral Tap. Chicken calls for a high five however Eric suddenly hits him with a Snap DDT right into a ‘Purple Haze’ a Guillotine Choke! OLIVER THIBODAUX: “You have no idea how happy this makes me.” The fans boo as Eric continues to tug until you can visibly see Chicken’s body go limp and unconscious as the referee attempts to pull him off, finally getting him to release the hold as Sparrow wastes little time heading to the back, seemingly doing this for no other reason then to do it as the referee takes a look at Chicken, who seems out of it as we cut to a break due to the violent nature. WINNER: CHICKEN = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = We head to the backstage area where the fans pop when they see Amanda Rowland the now basically Head Woman in Charge of Operations around here sitting at her disk looking ever so confident. Suddenly the door opens and the camera pans over the fans stunned to see Blake Beckett standing before her. BLAKE BECKETT: “I’m not going to say anything other then that I deserve this show back! This is my show that I built and I DEMAND you give it all back to me.” Blake seems confident that this is going to work as he glares at Amanda but seems taken back a bit when she looks up at him nonchalantly, she responds seeming completely at ease with his presence. AMANDA ROWLAND: “When I heard the rumors about you being unable to get a new job as a promoter, I thought that they may have been just that rumors. But judging by the fact you’re here it leads me to believe the job hunt didn’t go so well, take it your having a hard time paying the bills without a job now?” Amanda grins as Blake stares down at her unamused with her response as he seems about to go off on her, when she holds up her finger which makes him stop seemingly the first time she was truly in charge around here. AMANDA ROWLAND: “But I’ve been thinking Blake Beckett does deserve a spot here in GIW.” Blake nods his head and mouths “My Spot” as Amanda rubs her chin pretending to be thinking about this when she finishes her thought. AMANDA ROWLAND: “That’s why I have you BOOKED in a match next week, yeah your debut. So unless you have something better planned you best show up next week now leave before you’re forced out.” Blake looks at her like she’s crazy as she waves him away and he turns and heads out the door looking a bit worried as Amanda chuckles to herself then gets back to her paperwork as we head to the ring. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = Back in the ring we come this time it’s Paul Defias inside the ring looking pumped up as he bounces around the ring ready for a fight, the fans giving him no reaction they’d stopped caring. "I Will Not Bow" by Breaking Benjamin hits and the fans immediately boo as out from the back is Jake Kennedy to the surprise of many he is followed not far behind by Wake Walker holding his belt. RILEY STEVENS: “What in the world is Wake Walker doing with Jake Kennedy here tonight?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Seems to me like he’s his protégé, not a bad guy to learn from.” Jake Kennedy walks into the ring looking over at Wake who nods his head as he goes into the ring the fans still booing him, though some are looking around in confusion over Wake’s involvement. Jake is ready to go as the referee calls for the bell. DING DING DING! Kennedy wastes absolutely no time blowing forward and catching Defias with a big flurry of punches pushing him back towards the corner. Walker can be heard shouting stuff at him from the outside as the referee pulls Jake away. Defias runs at him trying to get a cheap shot but Jake seems very much prepared as he lands a big time Dropkick! The fans seem to still be pondering why Wake’s out here with one of their least favorite guys Jake. Kennedy waits in the ring waiting for Paul to get up as Wake can be heard screaming, “Bulldog!” from the outside. Seemingly hearing this Jake runs off the ropes and as Paul gets up nails him with a big One Handed Bulldog! Jake gets up screaming in a bit of passion as the fans boo him still, he turns around looking at Wake who points to the top rope. Jake heads up there in a hurry with Defias still downed. Jake jumps off hitting a big ‘Here Comes The Boom!’ a Diving Leg Drop! Jake seems about to pin but Wake yells at him to give him something else. Jake instead lifts the dead weight named Paul Defias to his feet before nailing him with a ‘Breakdown’ a Full Nelson Facebuster that will finish it as Kennedy hooks the leg 1…2…3! RILEY STEVENS: “Just like that Jake Kennedy is back in the title picture. Having not been pinned at ‘On Hallowed Ground’ you know he’ll be hungry to become a three time champion.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “And having Walker behind you may not be a bad thing.” Wake Walker slides into the ring holding up Jake’s arm into the air as the fans still boo him as Joshua Acquin has been given his challenger and it’ll be a Wake Walker lead Jake Kennedy, after a dominating win tonight. WINNER: JAKE KENNEDY (New ION Television #1 Contender) = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = Instead of wasting any time we return to the ring where all four members in the Evolve contender’s match are in the ring. On one side we have Thor Vegard the daunting big man then the next the equally as big and bad Cory Chevelle as we continue to get smaller we head to Tony Miranda and then lastly Mark Devine. All four men are different in every single way. The referee is about to ring the bell when Amatory - Ýôôåêò Áàáî÷êè/Butterfly Effect plays and out from the back is Alexii Ravynova all four wrestlers looking up at her the fans booing her. ALEXII RAVYNOVA: “I’ve got a few announcements to make about some titles around here. I know some of you were expecting Chris Armstrong however I’ve got some bad news about him, he has actually refused to show up. So we’ve decided to make two title changes. First is the change I came up with because I have decided that the GIW Women’s Championship, will be RETIRED once and for all, the belt hadn’t been that great since I held it anyway. The other one Amanda and I decided that tonight’s match up is one of three qualifying matches for an Evolve title shot at ‘Requiem’ our next PPV. The winner tonight will have his spot while the others may be left on the outside looking in, good luck.” The fans soak in the announcements while Alexii heads to the back as the referee turns around looks at all the man then calls for the bell. DING DING DING! The four men standing in their corners begin to survey each other, all of them calculating how to win this match here tonight. Thor Vegard however lunges forward at Tony Miranda while Mark Devine attempts to cheap shot Cory starting out this thing with a bang. This is the first qualifier and the men realize just how important it is. It’s a case of big and small on both sides. Cory countered Mark’s cheap shot and has just now thrown him into the corner. The fans are definitely on both Cory and Tony’s sides here tonight. Thor has Tony cornered and seems ready to nail him with a big headbutt however he ducks it at the last second and gives him a big kick to the chest seemingly doing no damage at all. Cory however is taking simple advantage of Mark, as he lifts him into the air and literally tosses him out of the ring no give on the mat for Devine. Cory turns his attention to Thor as both he and Tony look at one another seemingly in agreement they need to take this big dude down. They both go at him however Thor grabs them by the head and pushes them back, however both men run right back up at him grabbing him by the neck seeming ready for a Double Suplex! Right before they can do it Thor pushes Tony away but to the surprise of some Cory instead lifts Thor alone seemingly with ease nailing him with a Suplex! RILEY STEVENS: “Cory Chevelle is a freak of a human being! An absolute beast.” The fans are going wild after that as Cory looks over at Tony and both men seem to be in agreement that they shall now go at it. They begin to circle around when Mark Devine slides into the ring slamming into Cory and cornering him. Miranda watches this then realizes Thor Vegard is now getting to his feet. Rather then seem scared Tony yells out and the fans cheer him as he waits for the big man to get to his feet. He connects with a big Yakuza Kick that stuns Thor and then follows it up with a big knee to the chest then runs off the ropes going for a Discus Clothesline it hits but again only momentarily stuns Vegard. On the other side of the ring Chevelle has just nailed Devine with a Powerbomb and is pounding his chest waiting for him to get up. Tony follows his last move up with a Spinning Roundhouse Kick that seems to nearly bring Thor down. Mark is now to his feet taking a second to turn into the waiting arms of Cory. Tony screams and then runs off the ropes nailing a gigantic Running Leg Lariat that brings Vegard down. Right as he does this Cory lifts Mark up for the ‘Gorilla Warfare’ an F-5 but Mark grabs the ropes. Tony however gets the referee’s attention and he makes the pin count 1…Cory nails the move crushing Devine…2…Cory sees the pin attempt but is too late…3! OLIVER THIBODAUX: “That’s a tough way to lose a match right there.” RILEY STEVENS: “Your telling me Cory did nothing wrong but was just a bit distracted allowing Tony to get the pin, take nothing away from Tony who stopped the seemingly unstoppable Thor Vegard.” Tony slides out of the ring holding up his arms as Cory realizes that he cost himself this by not totally paying enough attention his hands on his head as he realizes how close he was to this one. RILEY STEVENS: “Regardless Tony Miranda will be one the three men competing for the Evolve title at ‘Requiem’.” Cory has his hands on his head as Tony heads to the back getting one step closer to holding gold once again around here, though Chevelle will almost have to be in the hunt having looked great tonight. WINNER: TONY MIRANDA (Qualifies for GIW Evolve Championship Match at ‘Requiem’) = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = Amanda Rowland can be seen backstage with her finger on her chin. She appears to be talking on her cellphone and punching in some numbers into a calculator. When suddenly, Wake Walker barges into her office. Amanda Rowland quickly gets up and takes off her glasses. She looks pretty angry with Wake Walker. WAKE WALKER: “You summoned me here?” AMANDA ROWLAND: “Why … yes, Walker… yes I did! As the one who makes the big decisions around here, I need to make sure that after this entire Evolve fiasco that everyone is on the same page! You were on Evolve and I simply can not trust you. I can’t have my top superstar walking around with my big title belt without knowing whether or not you’re going to be loyal to us. Speaking of loyalty, the other Evolve members came to my office earlier and apologized for their actions. That’s more than enough for me to trust them again. They all came together and made it clear that their hearts was with GIW. Apparently, though… you didn’t want to take a part in it! So today… you’ve been booked in a title match as you know. But… just to make sure we get rid of the possible two-faced individuals, if you lose this match or cost Aspen this match in any way, shape or form… you will be forced to defend your title belt against her.” WAKE WALKER: “Good.” AMANDA ROWLAND: “Oh and let’s not forget the most important thing! There is a stipulation! If you lose to Aspen Chaud at Requiem, our upcoming Pay Per View, you will be fired on the spot!” Wake Walker looks rather annoyed but then begins to smile. WAKE WALKER: “Are you sure you want to do that, Amanda?” AMANDA ROWLAND: “It’s Ms. Rowland to you and yes, I am quite sure of it!” WAKE WALKER: “Don’t be surprised if I come back knocking on your door after I crush Aspen Chaud. Who knows… since you’re giving out so many surprises tonight, maybe I’ll give you one.” AMANDA ROWLAND: “Is that a threat?” WAKE WALKER: “Yes.” Wake Walker leaves. Amanda Rowland looks like she fears for her life for a few seconds but then realizes just what type of power she has. She stares at Walker as he shuts the door with a lot of force. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = “Sum 41” by Fatlip begins to play. Eric Sparrow comes out. The crowd begins to cheer but still, Eric Sparrow manages to get a somewhat mixed reaction. Eric Sparrow waits on the ramp for his tag partner, Inchiro Hueda. “Dragon” begins to play. A huge mechanical dragon begins to come out from the tron and begins to throw out fire through his nostrils. Inchiro Hueda stands next to Sparrow. Eric Sparrow, despite not being such a tall person seems to tower over Inchiro Hueda. The two walk to the ring together. Inchiro looks like he’s not too happy being here. Followed up by this, “Bullsyee” begins to play. The crowd gives off a very loud cheer and out comes Aspen Chaud. She goes into the ring. She has a brief staredown with her two opponents but then redirects her attention to the ramp. “Dazed and Confused” begins to play. Wake Walker comes out with his title belt in hand. He appears to have this new vibe to him. He walks down the ramp. Once all four wrestlers are set, Aspen Chaud and Eric Sparrow agree to start off the match. The bell sounds. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “This will determine whether or not Walker is against Galveston Island Wrestling or not. What will transpire?!...” RILEY STEVENS: “I don‘t know but I think I‘m getting Goosebumps!” Eric Sparrow and Aspen lock horns in the middle of the ring. Eric Sparrow quickly does a sambo takedown which surprises Aspen. Eric Sparrow goes for a quick cover. 1...2.. Kick out! Eric Sparrow runs to the turnbuckle and stays there, looking at Aspen’s movements. Aspen looks towards Walker without confidence. She shakes her head. Sparrow takes advantage and hit’s a spinning running kick to the face of Aspen. Aspen falls to the floor but gets up. Aspen pushes Sparrow away. Sparrow bounces off the ropes and punches Aspen, dazing her. Sparrow begins to work on the legs of Aspen by kicking them repeatedly. Aspen quickly bounces one of the kicks and turns it into a backbreaker. She goes for a cover but Sparrow counters and tries to roll up Aspen! 1...2.. Kick out! Aspen appears to be taken off guard and goes to the turnbuckle. Sparrow runs at Aspen and tries to hit a body slam but Aspen gets out of the way. She picks up Sparrow and puts him on the top turnbuckle. The two begins to struggle there for about thirty seconds, punching each other to try and dislodge the other. Eventually, Sparrow gets the best and hit’s a superplex from the top rope, hurting both adversaries! The time for a tag has come. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Moment of truth?! Walker doesn‘t look like he wants to take a tag! This is foreshadowing!...” RILEY STEVENS: “Inchiro looks so psyched to get in!” Sparrow begins to drag himself towards Inchiro as Aspen drags herself towards Walker. Inchiro looks very excited to get in. Walker looks like he has no intention. Sparrow drags himself closer to Inchiro. He jumps for the tag…and Inchiro leaves! Inchiro begins to walk away. Aspen stands up, wobbly… and looks at Inchiro walking away. She looks shocked. Walker tags himself in! He runs towards Sparrow! Sparrow had this predicted and tries to hit an Air Sparrow on Walker! Walker counters and turns it into The Temple Of Love! Aspen begins to jump up and down in excitement. Inchiro is being booed by the crowd as he slowly walks up the ramp. Walker looks at Sparrow and then at Inchiro. Walker exit’s the ring and begins to chase after Inchiro! Eventually, he brings Inchiro to the ring without being counted out and begins to beat the living daylights out of him. Meanwhile, Aspen enters the ring in hopes of trying to get some offence on Sparrow. Sparrow hits Air Sparrow on Aspen! Aspen is out of the equation! Wake Walker continues to destroy Inchiro until he’s bleeding. The referee tells Walker continuously to stop but he doesn’t and so, the referee calls the entire match off. Walker stands up and begins to argue to the referee. However, Eric Sparrow turns Walker around, kicks him in the gut and hits Purple Haze! OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I think we can‘t for sure say whether or not Walker was on the side of GIW tonight but in my opinion, he was! He seemed to let out some steam though!!...” RILEY STEVENS: “Inchiro will probably get fired over this!” Eric Sparrow celebrates and leaves as Walker and Aspen lie on the canvas, side by side…both taken out. WINNERS: NO CONTEST
