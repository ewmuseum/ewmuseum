GIW — Pay-per-view

Exile 2011, 13 February 2011

13 February 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS EXILE BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! The scene around the Moody Gardens Plaza is electric. We pan around and watch the crowd of over 500 going wild, waiting for the show to start. We cut over to the announce booth where Riley Stevens and Oliver Thibodaux are ready to start. RILEY STEVENS: “Welcome to Moody Gardens… one of the best theme parks in the United States, and home to GIW Exile! We haven’t seen a show that carries this much weight since Blackout back in August, and this show might actually rival it.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Oh, come on Riley. Now, this show will be good an all, but I am pretty sure Blackout was our debut show... no one can rival that.” RILEY STEVENS: “One the pre-show earlier in the night, we saw Aspen Chaud and Ash Aiken pick up victories; but let’s focus on this show. The opening contest is the culmination of a feud that has been brewing since Genesis. Jak Hendricks will take on Seth “The Reaper” Seeker.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Two dudes who just flat out hate each other… you have to love a good old fashioned rivalry like this!” RILEY STEVENS: “Right, these guys are looking to settle the score once and for all. After that, we have Joshua Acquin vs. Marcus Hister for the GIW Television Title.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Marcus Hister is crazy man, Josh has his hands full. Watch out, because Hister is quick and effective… but Acquin is a grizzled veteran of the game.” RILEY STEVENS: “Indeed he is, and then we have Revolt’s main event; Mark Cross vs. Chris Jericho, round dux, for the GIW Championship.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Chris Jericho has been rather unimpressive so far in GIW, can he finally prove that Beckett didn’t waste his money? I think not.” RILEY STEVENS: “From there, we go to the Affliction main event. Five men, one woman…” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Sounds like a porno Riley.” RILEY STEVENS: “… Notably Madison Seton, Eric Sparrow, and Wake Walker. The first pin gets the Deep South Title, the second gets the NOX Title.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “That’s what is wrong with today’s world… we reward 2nd place. Come on!” RILEY STEVENS: “And then we have the main event. A match that has been being built up to since Genesis… High Voltage against the Perfect Combination for the GIW Tag Team Championships.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Well, this one is going to be a one for the ages. These two teams flat out hate each other… kind of a Muslim and Jew scenario right?” RILEY STEVENS: “It’s time to start the show… let’s send it down to ringside! QUOTE We come back to the ring where the fans are ready for the opening match of the night. “Nightmare” by Avenged Sevenfold rings out causing some cheers from the crowd popping for the big man as he walks out from the back. His demeanor would scare anyone and as he makes his descent down towards the ring RILEY STEVENS: “This man is one scary dude, he’s defeated nearly everyone in his path and tonight he’ll get his final revenge on Jak Hendricks.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Oh, attacking the guys two friends wasn’t enough? The guy has taken it too far.” Suddenly there is some commotion as out from the back comes Jak Hendricks, grabbing the big man by the back of the head and running him forward right into the steel steps! The fans begin to boo as Seeker’s knee takes the brunt of the damage. The referee jumps out there to stop him, as Jak grabs him and then tosses him inside the ring. The fans boo as Jak gets up, only to be held back by the referee. The ref walks over to Seth and asks him if he can go and still limping he nods his head. DING DING DING! The fans boo as Jak runs forward dropkicking his hurt knee right off the bat. He falls over in pain, holding the knee as Jak watches on admiring his handy work. Seeker attempts to get up as Jak taunts him but he’s barely able to without falling back into the ropes. Seth comes forward limping trying to fight but walks right into a perfect executed Yakuza Kick! However it doesn’t take Seeker down instead it just stuns him momentarily! The fans are booing now as Jak watches him and then runs off the rope running forward nailing him with a Lariat! Normally it wouldn’t take the big man down but with him unable to put any weight down on one leg, he falls to the mat. Hendricks immediately makes the cover trying to put this one away 1…2…Kick Out! Seeker is hurt but he is not yet down and out. RILEY STEVENS: “Seeker can still do this.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Stop it, Riley. Jak Hendricks is about to show the world why he is the superior wrestler.” Jak gets up, looking at the referee but knowing that he’s doing far to well to even complain. Seeker now tries desperately to get up the fans realize that unless he hits something right here and right now this thing is over. Seeker stumbles to his feet, taking a big swing at Hendricks! But he misses. And when he does Hendricks goes crazy with the ‘Jak Attack’ a variety of randomly assorted punches and kicks that stuns Seth. The last shot is a full blown kick to the knee, that sends him falling to the ground in pain. Instead of finishing him off there, he chooses to increase the pain a bit. Realizing he has the opportunity he starts to spin the big man over until he has it locked in, the Sharpshooter! The fans boo knowing this is the end and as Seeker grunts to try to escape, it’s clear he is trapped in the middle of the ring. Jak pulls back harder with a big smirk on his face as Seth finally just admits defeat, tapping out! RILEY STEVENS: “This is a damn shame; I thought this match could be a classic.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Instead, Jak Hendricks dominates. This guy is going to make waves around here.” Jak gets to his feet, pushing Seeker out of the way almost as to say I’m done with you. The referee raises his arm reluctantly in victory as the fans shower him with boos. While it may not have been pretty, he got the win and he’s on the way up around here. WINNER: JAK HENDRICKS After the match is over, we see a promo video for the next super-show, Revelation. When we return, we see Handsome Harley in the ring with his own interview set. There is a couch and two chairs, and Harley is perched on a stool which is separated from it all. The ring is coated in bright purple carpet. The fans boo when Harley begins to talk. HANDSOME HARLEY: “Welcome to GIW’s newest interview segment… BIG TIME! With me, Handsome Harley! Tonight, we have a wonderful guest planned. He is the man that will be the next GIW Television Champion… Marcus Hister!” Hister’s music hits and the arena is filled with boos. Marcus takes his time getting into the ring, and when he does he shakes Harley’s hand and sits down on the couch. MARCUS HISTER: “You know Harley; it is an honor to be the guest for the first ever televised episode of Big Time. I really am… ELATED!” Harley laughs smugly, and pats himself on the back. HANDSOME HARLEY: “Well, they said I needed a big time guest. So I got one! You are in a match with Joshua Acquin tonight; up next in fact; and I just wanted to give you something to ponder over while you are kicking his ass tonight?” Hister leans forward… interested. MARCUS HISTER: “Go on…” Harley gets up and takes a seat on the couch beside Marcus. HANDSOME HARLEY: “You see Marcus… I have had an idea in my head for a long time. It is a tag team, called nXtgen… and all I needed was a young guy, such as you; for it to work. Let’s face it, I’m a bit past my prime… but I can still go. And with you on my side Marcus… we could be champions in no time.” Hister looks Harley in the eyes, thinking of the pros and cons of the decision. Harley stands up and offers his hand to Hister. After a few moments, Hister shakes the hand. Harley raises Hister’s hand as we fade to darkness. A video hyping the main event for tonight is shown. QUOTE When we return we see Marcus Hister still in the ring from the last segment looking up the ramp with a smirk, waiting for his next challenge to come down. "Monster" by Skillet now hits and the fans go absolutely insane as out from the back, is Joshua Acquin wearing a very calm look on his face as he heads down to the ring looking inside it at his enemy for the night. RILEY STEVENS: “These two were the last remaining in the Scramble and now they’ll do battle for the gold here tonight. Acquin never lost the belt…” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “But this loss will count as that tonight.” The fans continue to cheer as he high fives a few fans on the way down to the ring. He now jumps up and into the ring, looking across the way at Hister, both men very focused for the task at hand. The referee holds up the belt and both men take a second to stare at it, before the bell is rung to get this thing started. DING DING DING! As soon as the match starts they come in for an immediate lock up. At first we can’t tell who is stronger as both men don’t seem to budge. Both of them push back and forth, until Marcus breaks it up by kneeing him in the chest and whipping him off the ropes. As he comes back he ducks under Marcus’s clothesline attempt and comes back with one of his own. The fans are going wild for him as he calls for Marcus to get up and he does so right back into another clothesline. Its clear Josh wants his belt back as he waits for Hister to get back up. This time he attempts to hit a Falling Neckbreaker, but Hister is able to reverse out of it and then roll him up 1…2…Acquin rolls over on top 1…2…Hister now flops over and has Acquin down 1…2…Kick Out! Both men hop back up their feet after a nice little back and forth. While the fans seem impressed the men are just focused on getting the advantage as they start to circle around one another. It’s Acquin that comes forward only to run right into a Wheel Kick! The fans boo him as he goes for another cover 1…2…Kick Out! Not even close to enough to put Josh away. They both start to get to their feet, Hister up first and calling for something. He tries for a very premature Superkick that doesn’t land, instead Acquin is able to jump out of the way and then nail him with a Facebreaker Knee Smash. With Hister momentarily stunned Acquin, now tries to do the same thing that he did. Trying to put him into a ‘Face Breaker’ the Kill Switch! The fans go wild until Hister is able to spin out of it, and hit an unseen Low Blow! The referee blocked by Josh’s back. With Acquin stumbling back Hister comes forward nailing him with a Spear but to his disliking Acquin falls right through the ropes and outside the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “The referee didn’t see the low blow!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Go cry about it.” Hister slams the mat angry that Josh fell out thinking he may have had it. He backs up then realizes he can win on a count out! He pulls on the referee’s arm telling him to start the count. 1…2…3…The fans are cheering Josh on from the outside, but to this point he still has yet to move…4…5…6…Finally we see some movement from Josh, as he tries to climb up the side…7…8...Boom! He slides in at the 9 count and the match is still going. The fans are excited but realize he is still in some trouble. This time Hister wants to end things looking for his ‘Mystery’s Darkness’ as he stalks Joshua from behind. When he goes for it Joshua is able to shove him off, sending him to the mat hard. Wasting little time the suddenly rejuvenated Acquin jumps up and nails him with a Jumping Knee Strike! The fans go wild as Hister starts to get up holding his face. Acquin takes a swing at him only for Hister to duck it, sending Acquin right into punching the referee! The fans boo as Acquin checks on the referee hoping he’s ok. When he turns around Hister tries to take a swing at him but Josh throws him right into the ring post! The fans go wild as he avoids it. But the start to boo when they see Handsome Harley entering the ring, able to attack with the referee down. Hister is back up, trying to walk forward. Harley goes for a big boot but Josh rolls out of the way and Harley nails Marcus! The fans cheer as Acquin low blows Harley and tosses him through the ropes! With him out Acquin now lifts up Hister who is dead weight and hits him with the ‘Face Breaker’ a Kill Switch! The fans cheer as a second referee comes out from the back sliding into the ring 1…2…3! That’s it! RILEY STEVENS: “That’s it! Joshua Acquin is the champion again!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “NO! That’s not fair. Acquin should have been DQed he punched the referee!” RILEY STEVENS: “That was accidental! Handsome Harley comes in here to help Marcus Hister and your fine with that?” The fans are going absolutely insane for Joshua who gets up and is handed his belt. He holds it tight, becoming the champion again after never having lost the belt in the first place. He rolls out of the ring as both Hister and Harley start to get up both bitter about the loss as Acquin holds up the belt celebrating with the fans as he heads towards the backstage area. WINNER: JOSHUA ACQUIN (New GIW Television Champion) Backstage, we see Madison Seton in the hallway, bouncing around and getting loose for her match later on in the night. We hear a scream from far off in the distance, then another scream. Seton stops moving and begins to look down the hallway. Nothing is there. She goes back to working out, then the scream returns. She looks down the hallway again, and this time she sees a lump in the middle of the floor. She runs over to it, and turns it over… it is Jake Avery. His head is bashed open by a blunt object and blood is pouring down his face. MADISON SETON: “Uh… get some help over here! Hurry!” A few moments pass and then a herd of paramedics arrive and begin to tend to Jake. Seton pulls a small piece of paper off of Jake’s back. Written in orange ink, the words say: Things may be going bad now… but you just wait. At Breakdown… GIW will learn its fate. Seton seems dumbfounded by the paper, and we cut back to the announce booth. RILEY STEVENS: “Another attack by some mystery assailant.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “This is insane… I thought the attackers were the General Manager’s sending out assassins the other shows… but maybe Beckett was wrong?” RILEY STEVENS: “Maybe not… Beckett said he had video proof that High Voltage and the Perfect Combination were the attackers.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Something is fishy around here… and now we have one less competitor at the main event. What now?” RILEY STEVENS: “I don’t know Ollie, at least it was just Jake Avery.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Nice one Riley… you seem like you are getting a bit more spunky as of late.” RILEY STEVENS: “The mystery attacker thing is a touchy subject for me.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “All it took was a sledge to the head to set you straight.” RILEY STEVENS: “It’s time for the next match… Jericho against Cross.” QUOTE “Lift Me Up” by Moby hits and the crowd goes wild. He walks down the ramp and mimics a title around his waist making his slow descent towards the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “This man is the definition of class right here. I don’t think there is anyone that dislikes the guy, other then Chris Jericho.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Yeah right the guy is fake, no way he can be that respectful. Everyone has a cold heart on the inside.” He gets in the ring, and when Jericho’s “Break the Walls Down” hits, the place erupts in boos. When he walks out the fans give it him, showing him little respect at all as he heads towards the ring. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “This man’s a legend and I am the only one that gives him respect around here.” He enters the ring, the two faces off and the bell rings. They waste no time slugging it out, but Jericho hits an eye poke that backs Mark into the turnbuckle. Jericho is laying kick after kick; the ref has to pull him off. Cross hits a football style tackle on Jericho, but Jericho gets him back into the corner. Cross whips Jericho into the corner then Jericho bounces back with a perfect dropkick to the jaw. Two clotheslines and another dropkick send Cross out of the ring. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Jericho is rolling early on in this match!” Both men are outside now, and Jericho is really laying into Mark. Finally, Mark drops Jericho with a stiff punch to the head. He rolls Jericho back into the ring and goes for a pinfall. Cross is slowing down the pace of this match by locking in various submission moves to Jericho. Jericho wiggles out of it, then is slammed back to mat with a back suplex that shakes the ring. Cross is stomping away on Jericho. Mark Cross bounces off the ropes and goes for a splash on Jericho, but he rolls out of the way. Jericho looks to lock in the Walls of Jericho on Cross, but Cross twists his hips and gets out of it. The two go back and forth for a few minutes; then Jericho attempts a Code Breaker, but falls on his back. RILEY STEVENS: “An essential counter by Mark Cross; if Chris would have hit that one, it would be over!” Mark takes control of the match again, hitting several left and right hooks on Jericho and backing him up into the corner. He puts Jericho up top and goes for a super plex, but Jericho knocks him off. Jericho waits until Cross is up to his feet… then attempts a cross body! 1..2…CROSS FLIPS IT OVER! THE PIN IS ON JERICHO! 1…2…KICK OUT! OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Good counter by Cross… Jericho is getting momentum though.” The two trade blows, but then Cross hits one good punch to the jaw, sending Jericho down. He sets Jericho up for the powerbomb, but Jericho counters… THE WALLS ARE LOCKED IN! Mark is close to the rope… but JERICHO PULLS HIM AWAY! CROSS IS STRUGLING!!! CROSS TAPS! Jericho breaks the hold and slides out of the ring almost immediately! OLIVER THIBODAUX: “What a night! YES! Chris Jericho is our new Galveston Island Champion!” RILEY STEVENS: “The legend has finally won the big one here in GIW.” He grabs the belt from the ring keeper and begins to run up the ramp with his GIW Championship. He holds it high, Cross stares him down from the center of the ring, holding his back. WINNER: CHRIS JERICHO (New Galveston Island Champion) We head backstage where High Voltage is sitting in their locker room. AJ is getting tape on his wrists while Volt is sitting and watching television. AJ LIGHTNING: “You know, you should be getting ready for the match.” Justin ignores AJ, he takes a huge gulp of soda and slams it down to the table. AJ LIGHTNING: “All I ask is that you put a little bit more effort into this… that’s all.” Justin speaks, but doesn’t turn his head. JUSTIN VOLT: “I have it covered.” AJ seems annoyed. He throws the tape back on the table. Justin approaches him from behind. JUSTIN VOLT: “You just focus on not getting pinned… ok?” Justin pat’s AJ on his back, and then leaves the room. The camera continues to follow Justin Volt out of the room, and down the hallway. After a few seconds, Aspen Chaud appears in the shot. They hug, and the camera cuts to ringside. RILEY STEVENS: “Whoa… this could get interesting.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Oh, Aspen is just doing what she does best. Getting into the mind of the opponent… Tony just won this match.” RILEY STEVENS: “Only time will tell I guess… but now it is time for the, now, four way match for the Deep South and NOX Championships.” QUOTE When we get into the ring we already find Assassin and Mike Bryant ready for action inside of the ring. We now know that Jake Avery is out of this match and it’s time for the entrances. “Circles” by Incubus alerts the fans of the next mans presence as out from the back comes Eric Sparrow holding his ‘GIW NOX Championship’ over his shoulder as he smirks to the fans who boo him so harshly. RILEY STEVENS: “You can feel the tension in the air tonight.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I’m just excited that finally Eric Sparrow will get the belt he so truthfully deserved.” Sparrow strolls down towards the looking calm as he jumps up the steel steps and then over the top rope and into the ring. This match may have been thinned by one person but he knows that the chances he retains are very slim. "Party in the USA" by Miley Cyrus now hits and the fans go insane. Sparrow looks back angrily at Madison who walks out with a big smile with her belt strapped around her waist. It’s clear whose side these fans are on as she walks out towards the ring, happily. RILEY STEVENS: “Our great champion Madison Seton is here tonight and it looks to me like she is the favorite.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “To you and these stupid fans maybe. I personally have had it up to here with her.” RILEY STEVENS: “Regardless the rules are simple. The first pin will be for the ‘GIW Deep South Championship’ and the second pin will be for the ‘GIW NOX Championship’.” Seton walks by her fans, high fiving them then slides into the ring handing her belt to the referee rather politely while Sparrow shoves it into his chest. The fans seem suddenly realizing that Walker is not here, when suddenly they hear his voice as he walks out onto the stage with a microphone. WAKE WALKER: “Why would I even think about fighting in this match here tonight? You see I won on Monday and that means I can book myself against any of you that I want. So as for tonight, I will not be making my return. Instead the winner of this match should know that I will be coming for them.” Walker immediately walks backwards exiting the area as the four people in the ring look at one another a little confused but as the referee holds up both belts they realize what exactly is at stake. The referee hands them out as the four stand in separate corners. DING DING DING! As soon as the bell rings Sparrow runs full steam at Mike Bryant these two becoming some what rivals over the past few months due to Sparrow’s belt. Sparrow holds him in the corner where he beats on him a bit as Seton and Assassin by default now start to brawl it out on the other side. It’s clear that things are going to be exciting as all of them are going at it. When Sparrow finally levels Bryant down into that corner, Seton rolls up Assassin on the other side of the ring 1…2…Sparrow now pulls her over and has her pinned 1...2…Assassin is back up and rolls through for the pin 1…2…Finally Bryant disrupts the flow by kicking him off. All four stars are up to their feet realizing any one of those pins could have crowned the Deep South Champion! The fans watch as they again go at it this time Bryant tackles Seton, rolling over one another until they fall out of the ring. Those two are going at it all while Sparrow and Assassin exchange blows inside of the ring. With Bryant and Seton getting up on the outside, Eric runs trying to throw Assassin over onto them. Instead Assassin lands on the other side of the ropes and punches Sparrow away. Bryant and Seton oblivious as to what is going on, continue to fight. Eric however runs forward dropkicking Assassin sending him flying back right onto Seton and Bryant on the outside! The fans are a bit impressed by Sparrow but most still boo. As the three on the outside start to get up, Sparrow clearly is not yet done with them. He holds onto the ropes waiting for them to get up. When they finally do he jumps off going Springboard. As he’s in the air both Bryant and Seton bail ending up with Assassin getting crushed with an ‘Air Sparrow’ a Springboard Clothesline! The fans have a little buzz going on from the move. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “That’s why he’ll be our champion!” Almost as soon as Oliver finishes Bryant grabs him and tosses him into the railings on the outside. Back into the ring he goes just at the same time as Seton. Both meet up in the center of the ring and it’s Seton who is able to catch Bryant with a kick to the gut that sets up an immediate DDT! The fans cheer as she goes for a pin 1…2…Kick Out! It was close but it’s clear that he is not done quite yet. She gets up not upset at all; calmly ready to inflict some more pain to finish him off. With Sparrow and Assassin on the outside this would be the perfect time to get the win. When he gets up she starts to deliver a vast array of kicks coming from all angles. Finally with him stunned she goes for the finisher, the ‘Round-A-Bout’ a Roundhouse Kick! Bryant is able to duck it and Seton is able to catch herself. But Bryant quickly grabs her and tosses her from the ring, coincidentally just as Sparrow is returning to the ring. Bryant turns but is given a big European Uppercut that sends him stumbling back into the corner. He backs up running full steam and nailing him with the ‘Pineapple Express’ a Corner Dropkick! Bryant holds his chest in pain as he stumbles forward then falls to the mat. He looks back and forth smirking as he seems ready to finish this thing. Seton is now getting up as Assassin jumps up onto the side of the ring trying to stop Sparrow. Instead he receives a kick to the face and Eric then sends him tumbling back where he falls right onto Seton! The fans boo realizing this may be the end as Bryant still has yet to move. He jumps off with the ‘Spinal Tap’ a Spiral Tap! The fans are basically silenced as he hooks the leg 1…2…3! NEW CHAMP. RILEY STEVENS: “No way…” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “YES! YES! YES! Eric Sparrow is the GIW Deep South Champion! YES!!!” The fans are basically silenced as Sparrow gets up jumping up and down the moment a little bit surreal. Seton now returns to the ring where she finds out what has just happened. She looks at Sparrow, then down at Bryant. Sparrow grins shoving it in her face, pretending to put the belt around his waist. She looks at the crowd stunned a bit but suddenly comes forward with a ‘Round-A-Bout’ a Roundhouse Kick to Sparrow that sends him stumbling out of the ring. The fans go wild for her as Assassin slides back into the ring where he receives a big Russian Legsweep! Back to his feet is Bryant, and almost blaming him for the loss Seton comes forward giving him a ‘Round-A-Bout’ as well taking him down. She now turns her attention back to Assassin whom she is able to set up perfectly into the ‘Freezing Rain’ a Gogoplata. Though it’s not the belt she wanted, Assassin taps and we have ANOTHER new champion! RILEY STEVENS: “This is insane. Seton just traded down and Sparrow traded up.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Yeah baby!” On the outside Sparrow has just gotten to his from her kick, realizing he is still the champion. He tears the Deep South belt away from the ref and then walks around the ring hugging the belt tightly to his chest! The fans are booing as Seton is now handed her belt, which for many would be an exciting time for Madison its bittersweet. She watches as Sparrow celebrates with the belt up the ramp the fans still booing as the last match before the main event comes to a close. WINNER(S): 1. ERIC SPARROW (New GIW Deep South Champion) 2. MADISON SETON (New GIW NOX Champion) We cut backstage where Sarah Sharp is standing by with Chris Jericho, who has his new GIW Championship over his shoulder. He smirks at the camera and rubs his title, knowing it is killing all of the fans to see his success. SARAH SHARP: “Well Chris, you did it. You defeated Mark Cross, fair and square might I add, and now you are the GIW Champion… what do you have to say to everyone who has ever doubted you? The ones that have said you were a fat, washed up legend.” Jericho snatches her microphone. CHRIS JERICHO: “I think I have more than proven them wrong. I don’t understand why people wouldn’t worship me anyway..? I am Chris Jericho; EVERYONE knows my name, EVERYONE knows my accomplishments. And now, I can add one more… a GIW Championship.” Sharp looks at Jericho and nods her head. SARAH SHARP: “Chris, I believe you are the best thing to happen in GIW since… well… ever. You really are a role model for so many kids out there, and I just wanted to say… thank you Chris Jericho.” Jericho smiles and nods; but then the moment is shattered when Eric Sparrow enters the scene… breathing heavily with his Deep South title around his waist. ERIC SPARROW: “Role model? You call this guy a role model?” Jericho wastes no time getting into Eric’s face. CHRIS JERICHO: “You aren’t even in my league. Just get out of my segment.” Eric laughs. ERIC SPARROW: “Not in your league, eh? It seems as if we both have equal titles… but then again… it isn’t all about titles is it? It is also about talent… but hey, I’m more talented than you are too. So, explain to me again how you are in league of your own?” CHRIS JERICHO: “I don’t need to explain myself to people like you… I have better things to do.” ERIC SPARROW: “We will fight eventually Jericho, and you will find out the hard way that just because you think you are elite, doesn’t mean you are.” Jericho pulls away, leaving Sparrow shaking his head in the interview lounge. We cut back to the ring where the main event is set to begin. QUOTE “Taste the Blood” by Shawn McPhearson begins to play, and the Perfect Combination stroll out from behind the curtain with Aspen dragging behind them. The boos in the arena are enormous, and the Perfect Combination egg it on by taking their time getting to the ring. COLT COX: “Now entering the ring… the tag team of Tony Miranda and the Shadow… The Perfect Combination!” They enter the ring, and Shadow and Miranda go to opposite ring posts and stand on them. Aspen stands in the center of the ring, twiddling her thumbs. “To Be Loved” by Papa Roach begins to play, and High Voltage walks out to a mix of cheers and boos. COLT COX: “And their opponents… accompanied by Amanda Henry and Elizabeth Reeve…. Justin Volt and AJ Lightning… HIGH VOLTAGE!” They enter the ring, and the bell sounds three times. The match is set to begin. Tony Miranda exits the ring and gets on the apron, but High Voltage decide to start the match early and begins wailing on the Shadow. It takes Miranda a second to realize what is going on, but eventually he gets back into the ring and clotheslines the hell out of Volt, they both fly out of the ring. Shadow is using his superior size over AJ to get the upper hand. Shadow hits a powerful one armed sidewalk slam on AJ. He hits a clothesline, and then goes for another; but on the second go around AJ ducks it. Before he can hit anything though, Shadow punches him right in the gut and tosses him headfirst into the corner. He hits two clubbing blows to AJ’s head, then shoves a boot in his face. He pulls AJ up again for a another punch, but AJ ducks it and lands a few of his own. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Those punches don’t mean as much coming from AJ.” He climbs up on top of the turnbuckle and lands a few more punches. Shadow tosses him off! Volt is running towards Shadow on the apron, but Shadow kicks him off as well. Shadow picks AJ up over his shoulder and slams him to the ground. Volt is on the apron now, Shadow tosses him in as well! When Volt stands up, he is hit by a huge big boot to the head! Shadow goes to get momentum off of the ropes, but when he goes back, Elizabeth Reeve clubs him in the back. Shadow isn’t hurt by it though and turns around and grabs her by the throat. AJ runs towards the unsuspecting Shadow, but he gets tossed over the ropes onto the floor! RILEY STEVENS: “How much power does his guy have?” Tony Miranda is outside now, and he is landing blows on AJ, who is the legal man. Shadow has Volt set up for a choke slam in the center of the ring, but IN COMES AMANDA HENRY! She hits a low blow on Shadow! Meanwhile, Miranda power bombs AJ onto the announce table, but it doesn’t break. Volt is stomping away on Shadow in the center of the ring now. He begins stomping and working on Shadow’s arm. Shadow powers out. He goes for a tag, but Volt cuts him off. More hits to the arm! Arm wrench! Shadow hits several punches to Volts head… but then Volt hits a DDT out of nowhere. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I don’t guess the referee cares Volt isn’t the legal man?” Volt goes for a cover, but Miranda breaks it up. Tag into AJ. More stomps and punches to Shadow’s arm. It is obvious that is their focus point. Shadow powers out of an armbar by lifting AJ up and hitting a back suplex! Both men are rolling around. Both a crawling towards partners. TAGS! Wait…. The ref didn’t see Shadow tag in Miranda! Voltage is double teaming Shadow. More stomps. 1…2… KICK OUT! Shadow and Volt are trading blows. Volt tries to whip Shadow into the corner, but he reversed it and Volt ends up in the corner! Back suplex… he tries to tag Tony in, but AJ is running in to stop him… Shadow hits a clothesline on him too! HE TAGS TONY! Tony cleans house! Back body drop! Spinebuster! He has taken both of them out! Volt is tossed out! Miranda picks AJ up for a spinebuster…. BUT ASPEN CHAUD HITS HIM IN THE BACK WITH A STEEL CHAIR! RILEY STEVENS: “OH MY GOD! WHAT DID ASPEN JUST DO?” Volt and AJ are double teaming Tony now. The Voltage girls are double teaming Shadow outside by stomping on Shadow with heels on. AJ lets Volt pick Tony up for the brainbuster he calls the Justin Concussion… but when he does… AJ LIGHTNING HITS A LOW BLOW ON JUSTIN VOLT! OLIVER THIBODAUX: “HOW MANY DOUBLE CROSSES ARE THERE IN THIS MATCH?” AJ is pounding away on Volt, and the referee had no idea how to regain order in the match, so he lets it all just happen. Aspen crawls back into the ring and hits a chop block on Lightning and pushes him out of the ring. The ref was yelling at Henry outside the ring and didn’t see it, Aspen throws Volt on Tony. The ref finally turns around...1…2…3! RILEY STEVENS: “Wow! What else could have happened in this match?” After the match, the other members of High Voltage who tried to rebel walk up the ramp in shame. Aspen takes a mic and stands over Tony Miranda. ASPEN CHAUD: “Ladies and gentlemen… the NEW High Voltage!” WINNERS: HIGH VOLTAGE (New GIW Tag Team Champions) The fans are all in a state of shock as Aspen and Volt stand in the ring as the new GIW Tag Team Champions! Suddenly both La’Sarah Audric and Blake Beckett come out from the back together. ASPEN CHAUD: “Blake! La’Sarah! Justin is coming with me to Revolt! We are dumping that dump Affliction once and for all!” The fans are not sure what to think about all this as Blake whom has a mic looks over at La’Sarah smiling. BLAKE BECKETT: “Thanks for that, perfectly timed in fact. You see I have already made the decision. With all of the fighting going on between Affliction and Revolt and all of the trouble it has caused. I already fired Affliction’s staff. And this week I realized that the show would benefit from seeing all the stars on one show. So here’s the deal about next week. Affliction is GONE! And this upcoming Revolt we are going to find out who are real champions are.” Aspen and Justin take that as a shot to them, both of them holding onto their belts tightly as Blake continues. BLAKE BECKETT: “So come next Thursday, it’ll be Joshua Acquin versus Madison Seton to merge their belts back into the ‘GIW North Beach Championship’. And that’s not all it’s time we have an Undisputed Champion! So in our Main Event it’ll be Eric Sparrow versus Chris Jericho for the ‘GIW Undisputed Championship’. Yet again, things shake up. And if you don’t like it well frankly I don’t care. See everybody next Thursday!” Yet again the show ends with Blake essentially reshaping the GIW World! Aspen and Justin seem unsure about the switch as La’Sarah claps on Blake as the show comes to a close.
