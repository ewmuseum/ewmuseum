GIW — Pay-per-view

On Hallowed Ground 2010, 31 October 2010

31 October 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: OHG LIVE OCTOBER 31ST, FROM HOTEL GALVEZ IN GALVESTON, TEXAS = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! The show starts off with the Hotel suddenly going dark as the fans go completely quiet. Suddenly a green tint lights up the entrance area of the arena as suddenly from the back walks a man in a Frankenstein costume walking towards the ring with mixed reactions from the crowd. RILEY STEVENS: “What a weird way to start out the show huh?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Sadly this hardly seems strange to me any more.” RILEY STEVENS: “Regardless we have Colt Cox here in the booth with us, to have unbiased commentary tonight.” COLT COX: “Also, because you know I’m talented.” Frankenstein stands in the ring as the fans continue to pop for him, some fans blowing him off thinking it’s just a lame start up. "Another" by Peroxwhy?Gen hits and out from the back is Mark Devine a new wrestler from EPW. He walks down to the ring, hopping into it and immediately getting into Frankenstein’s face who begs him that he doesn’t want a fight. COLT COX: “What a show.” Devine however calls for a referee, telling him to get in as the fans seem a bit confused. Frankenstein tries to bail as the referee slides in as it becomes clear this is the start of the GIW Over the Top Rope Battle Royal as the referee calls for the bell. DING DING DING! The fans seem confused as Frankenstein tries to bail out of the ring only to be grabbed by Mark Devine and spun around into a big clothesline. Frankenstein gets back up and then turns right into a big Twist of Fate! The fans boo as Devine glares out at them not caring at all about the man, just wanting to make a statement. He heads up to the top rope with Frankenstein reeling in pain as he jumps off with a Swanton Bomb! It’s over now as the referee tries to make him stop before he grabs him and throws him over the top rope to win the match. RILEY STEVENS: “I’m not sure that’s why Frankenstein came out here, but looks like Mark Devine made a statement out here tonight.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Well, that’s how EPW stars make a statement beat up helpless Frankenstein’s.” COLT COX: “That’s better then having a Chicken as a champion.” RILEY STEVENS: “Before this gets out of hand we are going to kick it to Sarah Sharp in the back who is standing by with Amanda Rowland.” WINNER: MARK DEVINE = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = We head to the backstage area where as Riley stated Sarah is standing by unwillingly next to Amanda Rowland. SARAH SHARP: “Tonight the main event is a match that will decide which company lives or dies. Would you willingly take in the EPW Superstars after all they have done to your show?” Sarah holds over the microphone to Amanda who has a smile on her face realizing Sarah doesn’t want to be here as she answers. AMANDA ROWLAND: “You see I am very willing to let them come to my show, because at the end of the day they were tricked into going to that show, and I’m willing to give them a second chance but you know who isn’t going to get that same second chance?” Amanda looks to Sarah who is looking away bored as Amanda then tugs the mic away and answers it herself. AMANDA ROWLAND: “Blake Beckett and David Sinclair, because when we beat them, they’ll be FIRED. God it feels good to say that, can’t wait until it really comes true.” Amanda happily walks off dropping the mic as Sarah bends down and picks it up with a mean glare as the segment ends and we head back to the ring. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = We return to the ring where suddenly “Sing for the Moment” by Eminem hits and out from the back comes Paul Defias to a whole lot of boos from the crowd. The support seems clearly in favor of GIW now as he walks out with a big smirk on his face heading towards the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “This is what many are calling the preliminary fight before the main event.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “The win before the win for GIW?” COLT COX: “Oh come on Defias is one of our originals, a man that represents EPW excellently. You’d have to be a fool to not bet on this guy, come on.” Defias jumps into the ring and then heads top rope where he holds up his arms as “Seek and Destroy” by Metallica hits and the crowd goes nuts as out from the back comes the new beast on the block in Cory Chevelle. He walks out from the back heading towards the ring, glaring down at Paul. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “You’d have to be a complete idiot not to think this guy has the advantage.” Cory steps up into the ring as the crowd continues to cheer him a small murmur starting his size having begun to gather him quite the reputation as he steps into the ring across from Defias as the referee calls for the bell. DING DING DING! The match up starts and immediately the two begin to circle one another as they then go in for a lock up. However things become clear who is the stronger man when Cory basically one arm throws him down to the mat. The fans cheer as Cory glares down at Paul who quickly crawls backwards and then hops to his feet. The two go right back into the fight with Defias slipping behind him and catching him with some punches to the back before running off the ropes however running right into a big shoulder block from Cory. Defias slowly gets to his feet this time, Cory’s size clearly being the advantage thus far into the match. The fans cheer as Paul gets to his face and Cory lifts him up onto his shoulders wanting to end this one early. However Defias catches him with a big eye rake that allows him to roll off and nail him with a big Jumping Knee to the Face! The fans boo as Cory stumbles back into the ropes. Defias with a full head of steam runs off the ropes and nails him with a dropkick to the chest that sends Cory through the ropes and to the outside hitting with a thud. The fans boo as the referee begins to count him out 1…2…3…4...However with Cory getting up Defias runs off the ropes and then jumps through it with a Suicide Dive nailing Cory down to the ground again. The fans get a little pop loving the move as Defias gets to his feet and claps one time getting pumped up as he rolls back into the ring as the referee again begins to count out 1…2…3…4…Cory is up to his feet as inside the ring Defias is already celebrating a win…5…6…7…Chevelle climbs into the ring at the exact eight mark. COLT COX: “Your guy is out of it.” Defias turns realizing Cory is back in as he runs forward catching him with a kick however Chevelle continues to get up. Paul continues to hammer him with blows but again Chevelle continues to fight his way up as the fans suddenly begin to get louder and louder for him. As he finally gets to his feet and shoves Defias away across the ring. The fans cheer but are caught off guard when ‘Knockout Dropper’ a Running Jumping Knee! The fans are a bit stunned as Cory falls to the mat and Defias gets on top kicking his legs thinking this is over 1…2…Kick Out! The crowd screams thinking that was it as Defias sits up mouth wide open. He gets to his feet beginning to beg the referee, that it was over. However he turns around getting angry and attempts to lock in ‘The Ender’ a Gogoplata! The fans seem stunned as he almost has it locked in when Cory begins to get up. Defias doesn’t release the hold instead grabs him around the head trying to choke him out. Suddenly in a great show of strength he whips Paul onto his shoulders and hits the ‘Gorilla Warfare’ an F-5! The crowd goes nut as Cory gets on top of him, for the pin 1…2…Kick Out! The fans seem again stunned as this time it’s Defias that impresses the world by kicking out of a huge move. Chevelle gets up he himself seeming a bit distraught about it. But he doesn’t waste much time as he gets to his corner getting down ready to hit the ‘Bloodlust’ a Spear! The fans cheer waiting for it as Defias gets up holding his back. Cory runs across the ring and goes for it but at the last second Defias jumps out of the way sending Chevelle into the corner. Cory falls back right into a roll up by Defias 1…2…Kick Out! Both men hop right up and it’s Paul who runs right into Chevelle who out of no where unleashes ‘The Silverback Brainbuster’ to Paul! The crowd knows it’s over as they begin to cheer as he goes for the pin 1…2…3! OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Take that Colt!” RILEY STEVENS: “I have to admit though this was the most impressive I’ve ever seen Paul Defias, he almost had it here tonight.” The fans cheer as Cory gets to his feet as the referee holds up his arm and even the look on his face shows that of a man who may have underestimated his opponent tonight looking at Paul with seemingly a bit more respect for him. Regardless it was a win for GIW to start the night, with Cory Chevelle continuing to show his talent to the world. WINNER: CORY CHEVELLE = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = We again head to the backstage area where its Aspen Chaud stretching out her legs before the match holding it up on a table as she reaches down to it. The crowd can be heard cheering when suddenly they begin to boo as Blake Beckett enters the scene, his footsteps catching Aspen off guard. BLAKE BECKETT: “Well, well, well. You know this whole thing between us has been going on for so long you know. It’s such a shame that it has to end like this for you. But I want you to know something, I’ve been talking with David a bit and he thinks that hiring you as an Interviewer wouldn’t be a bad idea.” Blake smiles and chuckles at his own joke as Aspen pulls down her leg not seeming to enthused by the joke as she waits very little time to respond to her. ASPEN CHAUD: “You know I’ve been thinking the same thing, when and I say WHEN we beat you tonight. Maybe I’ll talk to Amanda and she’ll let you come be our Janitor. After all you have a lot of history working with trash over there on EPW.” Aspen now gives him a big sarcastic smirk back as the crowd can be heard laughing with her in support as Blake seems to snap, though the words weren’t that harsh it seems that the pressure of the night has gotten to him. BLAKE BECKETT: “Listen up, Aspen. Don’t come begging to me for a job when you lose tonight. Because I don’t plan on hiring any of you losers from GIW. Tonight I am going to kill what I created once and for all.” Blake turns and heads out the door as the camera then returns to Aspen who shakes her head, looking ready to go for the match later on in the night as we return to the ring. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = Bret Battery is already in the ring after a couple of promo packages hype up some of the later matches. In his hand, Bret holds the letter that he received - the invitational from Inchiro Hueda. RILEY STEVENS: “This doesn’t seem good for Bret.” Slowly, the lights begin to dim and it’s as if some supernatural force were about to enter itself onto the stage that is On Hallowed Ground. Naturally, the crowd makes creepy ghost noises but they stop when they are frightened by sudden pyrotechnics. A familiar song, “Dragon” begins to play. Coming from the ramp is Inchiro Hueda, wearing a mask and an unusual outfit similar to that of a luchador. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “This guy, really? Haha.” Bret Battery begins to laugh and point at Inchiro Hueda, clearly laughing at him due to his small stature. Inchiro Hueda races to the center of the ring and stares down Bret Battery. Bret becomes serious for a few seconds. The two continue to stare at each other until finally; Inchiro Hueda slaps Bret Battery right across the face. Bret takes the hit and then looks at Inchiro Hueda in a surprised manner. RILEY STEVENS: “What was that for?” Inchiro Hueda says something inaudible but some people are able to lip read it. “You’re not ready. Nobody is.” Inchiro Hueda then proceeded to walk away. We take a break after what was a rather unknown circumstance. WINNER: NO CONTEST = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = Again it’s time to head to the back only this time we come back into a gathering between Team EPW in the back. With them is David Sinclair with Erns and Tate talking over stuff while Armstrong and Prowler seem to be discussing with Sinclair what will be happening tonight. Suddenly Sinclair excuses himself from that and walks across the way over to Wake Walker who is sitting down against the wall. DAVID SINCLAIR: “Listen up Wake, I can make you a very happy man after tonight. You see all we need to do is win this one match. And no longer do we share the spot light. We will corner the market and will be heading to the top! And either you are with us, or I can you’re your life a living hell.” Wake just now looks up at him acknowledging the fact that he is there as Sinclair continues to talk down to him, knowing full well that he’ll need him tonight though they seem to be going about it all the wrong way. DAVID SINCLAIR: “Let me just say that we don’t need you, never did. It was Blake that wanted you, not me. So tonight you just better do your best, because either you’re losing your job or you’re keeping it and going through hell on Evolve. Regardless, things don’t look good for you Wake unless you are the one that leads us to victory.” Sinclair slams his finger into Wake’s chest which clearly angers Wake as he then walks away back to his team as the hype for the main event continues. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = Some clucking noises begin to be heard. Everyone looks around waiting for the inevitable - the entrance of Chicken. There happens to be a big egg right in the middle of the ring. It hatches and out comes Chicken frolicking around with his TV title belt. Some of the crowd looks embarrassed to simply look at this. COLT COX: “Good lord.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “There’s not a lot to be said...” The egg shells are taken away and out comes Jake Kennedy to the song, “I Will Not Bow”. Jake Kennedy gets a decent reaction from the crowd. However, as soon as “Nightmare” begins to play, the crowd stands up for Joshua Acquin who looks like he’s ready to just get the fight over with. The match starts. DING DING DING! Jake Kennedy quickly runs at Joshua Acquin and tries to hit a clothesline but is lifted up the top rope and sent o the outside. Chicken begins to dance and starts making fun of the referee for no apparent reason. Right as Joshua Acquin is about to destroy Chicken and punch him, Jake Kennedy quickly enters the ring and tries to roll up Joshua Acquin! 1...2... Kick out! Chicken made absolutely no attempt in breaking that pin up. Chicken goes to the top turnbuckle and starts dancing again. Jake Kennedy looks at Chicken in anger and tries to go for him but Joshua Acquin clotheslines Kennedy from the back. Joshua Acquin tries to get to work on the leg of Jake Kennedy but Jake Kennedy quickly gets out of the way. Chicken keeps fooling around. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Maybe we should lose.” The match continues like this until finally, Kennedy is thrown out and Acquin hit’s the ‘Face Breaker’ on Chicken then ‘The Last Call’ and Acquin goes for the cover. 1...2…3! To win the title! OLIVER THIBODAUX: “YES, YES NEW CHAMP.” The crowd cheers as Joshua Acquin is handed his belt holding it tightly to his chest. The fans continue to cheer as he holds it up in the air, when suddenly it’s Kennedy that slides into the ring catching him with a big chop block to the knee. The fans boo as he begins to stomp away at the knee while Joshua groans in pain. RILEY STEVENS: “Who didn’t see this coming?” The crowd boos as Jake slides out of the ring grabbing a chair and walking back into the ring with it. Chicken is up and tries to stop him, but he instead gets crushed with the chair by Jake. With him downed he raps the chair around Joshua’s knee then heads top rope. He jumps off hitting a huge ‘Here Comes The Boom’ a Diving Leg Drop right onto the chair. The fans seem worried as Jake slides out of the ring heading to the back angry while Joshua screams in pain inside the ring. WINNER: JOSHUA ACQUIN (New ION Television Champion) = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = It’s again back to the backstage area we go where this time the camera is centered on Aspen Chaud and then it begins to pan out to show Eric Sparrow and Tony Miranda and then DareDevil and Thor Vegard, Team GIW has surprisingly got together right before the match here tonight. ASPEN CHAUD: “Won’t lie wasn’t expecting you all to show up...had a pep talk in mind but hadn’t really thought it out.” There is an awkward silence as Aspen gives a rather unsure shrug having called everyone together yet forgot what she had planned to say. Sparrow seems restless about being here; DareDevil seems bored, while Tony seems to at least realize it was a good idea. THOR VEGARD: “I haven’t been here long, but I’ve been here long enough to know that EPW needs to fall. And tonight, I am going to torture EACH and EVERY one of them in that ring.” Thor storms out of the lockeroom with DareDevil following not far behind him. Sparrow seems angry to have been brought here and heads out as well as Aspen shrugs and nods to Tony as they walk out together as the segment ends. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = The excitement is certainly in the air tonight as the fans begin to move to the edges of their seats waiting for what has been the match that they have all been waiting for. There is almost a silence around it when suddenly "Seasons" by The Veer Union hits and the crowd begins to boo knowing this is Team EPW’s theme song. Out from the back comes David Sinclair and Blake Beckett leading their team of guys ‘Evolve Champion’ Chris Armstrong, Jack Prowler, Christopher Darren Erns, Travis Tate and following a bit behind them ‘Galveston Island Champion’ Wake Walker. COLT COX: “Must suck to see them team that’s going to make you lose your job.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Does it?” The five men head into their corner all of them piling into their side as the fans boo them, some of the fans still showing their EPW pride. When suddenly “Powertrip” by Monster Magnet hits and the crowd begins to show their support as out from the back comes Eric Sparrow followed not far behind by Tony Miranda, Aspen Chaud, and DareDevil and finally taking up the rear is Thor Vegard. RILEY STEVENS: “Here is Team GIW, while EPW has Beckett and Sinclair with them it appears Amanda is not coming out here tonight.” The five all get into the ring, across from the other five as the tension has picked up as they begin to discuss who will be starting on each side of the ring. For EPW they have decided on Travis Tate while GIW lets the big man Thor Vegard get unleashed to start it off with as the referee calls for the bell. DING DING DING! The match starts out and the crowd goes quiet knowing just how much pressure is on both sides here tonight. Right away Tate goes on the attack taking on Thor crushing him with some left and rights. The fans are mixed not real fans of Thor, but most of them rooting for GIW as well. However Thor changes everything with a sudden headbutt that sends Travis to the mat. He hops back up and then goes right back at him running right into another headbutt as Team EPW and Sinclair and Beckett shout out instructions to him. However when he gets to his feet Thor whips him off of the ropes and right into a Big Boot! The crowd cheers realizing that he is setting up for one of his signature moves as he follows it up with a Remi Double Foot Stomp to the face a move that in total is called a ‘The Norwegian Terror’ he keeps his legs on him for the pin and nobody comes in to help him 1…2…3! Travis Tate is Eliminated COLT COX: “Already?” The fans cheer as next in is Jack Prowler wasting little time in going on the attack. However he runs right into Thor who whips him into the corner. Thor corners him and begins to crush him with Headbutt’s over and over again in the corner basically giving him some trapping headbutt’s. The fans are on Thor’s side now as Prowler falls to the mat in pain, as Vegard backs up a bit looking out at the crowd. Jack gets to his feet and runs at him, trying for a desperation move but runs right into the grasps of Thor Vegard who spins him around right into a ‘Afterlife’ a Piledriver! The fans cheer as he makes the cover looking up at Team EPW who doesn’t do much to stop him 1…2…3! Jack Prowler is Eliminated OLIVER THIBODAUX: “One by one, let’s go Thor!” Next in is Christopher Darren Erns who surprisingly begins to pummel him with blows however his kick to the stomach sends Vegard tumbling over to his corner and it’s Eric Sparrow that makes the tag as Thor falls to the mat before jumping up onto the ropes and then jumping at Erns only for him to duck under it, with Sparrow landing over him on both feet then running off the ropes and running at Christopher again but this time it’s Erns ducking underneath the clothesline but Sparrow stops on a dime and nails Erns with a big Superkick taking him to the mat as he goes for the pin 1…2…Kick Out! Sparrow not to mad about it knowing they have the advantage gets up, walking over to the outside stepping through the ropes and preparing to go springboard. As Erns gets up its Sinclair grabbing his foot that stops his lift off for only a second. This momentary set back allows Erns to grab him and Suplex him into the ring. COLT COX: “There is our Genius Boss.” However it’s Aspen stepping down off the ropes and walking around to the outside as she prepares to confront Sinclair who eggs her on. As she walks towards him Chris Armstrong jumps down right behind Aspen seemingly ready to crush her from behind. But right before he can its Wake Walker jumping in his way before he can do anything. Blake screams at Wake to get out of the way as back in the ring Sparrow has just landed a ‘Pineapple Express’ a Corner Dropkick that has Erns set up perfectly inside of the ring. He goes to the top rope and then hits a ‘Spinal Tap’ a Spiral Tap right onto Christopher and hooks the leg. At the last second Armstrong realizes it and attempts to stop it 1…2…3! Christopher Darren Erns is Eliminated COLT COX: “That’s not fair, she distracted our guys!” RILEY STEVENS: “But Sinclair grabbing Sparrow’s boots is ok?” Armstrong is in a second to late but regardless he tackles Sparrow to the mat and begins to beat up on him. The fans are still pumped from the last elimination as Sparrow tries to fight out finally kicking Chris away and jumping up and tagging in Tony Miranda which brings the fans to huge cheers as he jumps in. Sparrow seems to be going for a clothesline on Chris but he ducks however with Aspen and Wake still on the outside having a short stare down it’s Sparrow who jumps through the ropes hitting a Suicide Dive right onto Wake! Back in the ring with Armstrong ducking Sparrow’s shot he’s right in position for a Spinning Round House Kick from Tony. The fans cheer him as he begins to beat up on the Evolve Champion, waiting for him to get to his feet. On the outside Wake and Sparrow continue to fight with Aspen pulling the two apart from fighting. Sparrow blows her off heading back to her corner while Wake does the same as well. Back in the ring however it’s Armstrong who is in trouble as he gets to his feet slowly walking backwards right into a ‘Lightning Buster’ a Crash Thunder Buster! The crowd cheers as they realize another one is gone 1…2…3! Chris Armstrong is Eliminated COLT COX: “This can’t be happening right now.” The fans cheer as Armstrong rolls out of the ring and Tony seems ready to fight Wake as he jumps in but it’s DareDevil who pleads to get in as Tony ends up walking over to him and making the tag. DareDevil hops in looking at Wake, who stares out at the crowd hearing the GIW fans chants. From the outside it’s Sinclair and Beckett screaming at him to fight. However it’s DareDevil that is telling him to just lay down and not fight. The fans watch in anticipation as DareDevil gets real close pointing at the mat however he’s suddenly kicked in the stomach and hit with a ‘Temple Of Love’ a Sit-Down Powerbomb! The fans are shocked as Walker makes the cover 1…2…3! DareDevil is Eliminated COLT COX: “There we go! Come on Wake it’s still possible.” The fans actually begin to feel a bit eerie thinking, can he really do this right now. However those questions are seemingly answered when it’s Thor Vegard who enters the ring across from him. Thor comes at him but Wake begins to lay into him with big punches left and rights over and over. Vegard is basically just barely set back as he continues to advance, onto Wake crushing him with a big Headbutt that sends him to the mat. With him downed Thor sets up in the Three Point Stance for a big clothesline. When Wake is up he goes for it but Wake ducks it and Thor falls into the corner being tagged by Sparrow who jumps up onto the ropes and hits a ‘Air Sparrow’ a Springboard Clothesline! The fans cheer as he sets up for the ‘Purple Haze’ a Guillotine Choke. The fans begin to go into a frenzy when he locks it in as they wonder if Wake will tap out. He continues to try to get out of it with seemingly no hope. RILEY STEVENS: “There’s no way he can continue to fight, this move has to be taking all of his stamina.” He’s in the hold for about two minutes when he suddenly breaks the hold as both men get up and he crushes Sparrow with a kick but yet again he makes the mistake of kicking him into the direction of Team GIW and its Tony who makes the tag. Miranda hops in and just as Wake gets to his feet, he’s taken off of them with a Running Leg Lariat! The fans cheer as instead of making the pin Tony tags in Aspen who jumps in. Wake Walker is all but out of it as he gets to one knee struggling just to do that. Suddenly it’s Blake Beckett getting up onto the ring apron. She stops and suddenly runs at him hitting him with a big clothesline off the mat. She turns back getting behind Wake as he gets up, in her eyes you can almost sense remorse as she hits ‘The Barbie Makeover’ a Straight Jacket Sitout Sleeper Slam! The fans cheer as she gets on top of him for the three count 1…2…3! Wake Walker is Eliminated RILEY STEVENS: “WE DID IT, WE DID IT.” COLT COX: “I lost my job because of this place again?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Yeah get out of here Colt!” The crowd is going wild as the four remaining superstars get into the ring Thor Vegard showing basically no emotion at all, instead standing by while Aspen hugged Tony the emotions taking over her after the win. Eric Sparrow however stands over top of Wake Walker, yet again talking trash to him as out from the back comes Amanda Rowland! The fans in the arena continue to cheer GIW’s victory as David Sinclair walks by Blake Beckett the two getting into each other’s faces before David shoulders him and heads to the back leaving Blake to watch as Amanda steps into the ring being greeted by her stars, all of which embracing in victory. RILEY STEVENS: “GIW lives! Galveston Island Wrestling is alive and well folks, EPW is GONE ladies and gentleman it’s gone!” Amanda Rowland is held up by Tony Miranda as a few other GIW Superstars begin to fill the ring celebrating as well as Blake finally realizes defeat watching as Amanda celebrates as heads to the exit. But suddenly the crowd begins to die down as Wake Walker gets to his feet in the middle of the ring; Aspen Chaud has a hold of his title as she stares down at it. Wake gets up looking around before Aspen hands him the belt and offers to pat him on the back. However out of no where Wake Walker shoves her down to the mat. RILEY STEVENS: “What in the world was that!” Sparrow goes after him but Walker slides out of the ring, heading up the ramp as Tony begins to check on her. The fans begin to boo the moment of joy suddenly turning into anger as Aspen falls to the mat, as Walker walks out the door showing no mercy at all. Back in the ring Aspen is helped up by Tony as they all stare at him before shaking it off, and beginning to celebrate the fact that Galveston Island Wrestling LIVES ON! WINNERS: TEAM GIW
