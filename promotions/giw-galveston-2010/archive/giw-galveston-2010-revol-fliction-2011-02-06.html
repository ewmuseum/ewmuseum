Revol-fliction, 6 February 2011

6 February 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: REVOL-FLICTION LIVE FROM NORTH BEACH ON FEBRUARY 6th, 2011 = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! This is a huge night where GIW’s Revolt and Affliction will come together for a big night of action. The fans that got exclusive tickets for this event at North Beach all seem pumped as we come to the table where Riley Stevens Colin McKendry, and Colt Cox are sitting at ringside. RILEY STEVENS: “Welcome everyone to a joint show here Revo-Fliction as dubbed by many. As you know this is just an hour long special featuring one GIGANTIC Open Battle Royal that will decide who books Affliction after ‘Exile’, big stakes right Colin?” COLIN MCKENDRY: “God your just as bad as Russo. I just hope that it’s an Affliction star that gets the win.” COLT COX: “You know as the only real unbiased commentator here tonight, I am going to reserve my pick until we see who all shows up.” RILEY STEVENS: “Before we say anything else, we should send it into Blake Beckett who is in the ring ready to make an announcement.” The camera heads into the ring where the fans are greeted by the smiling face of Blake Beckett who is inside the ring with a microphone, his presence only becoming bigger as the GIW Chairman. BLAKE BECKETT: “You know I had my worries about making this show here tonight. With all of what has happened between the brands it seemed a bit dangerous…but at the same time. You fans love dangerous. So regardless I made it anyways. The stakes are big tonight, so I have already made one decision. You see while La’Sarah is a good friend of mine, I have no choice but to kick her out tonight.” The fans go wild, not wanting to see Audric at all here tonight, though it seems suspicious he’d let Affliction staff stay at first. BLAKE BECKETT: “As for Affliction I have some news for them. You see I have decided that Alexii Ravynova is FIRED! We can’t have staff putting hits on people. Megathreat is also FIRED! He’s been a mess for weeks, he needs to go. And lastly Garrett Holloway is FIRED! Because it’s clear he can’t handle the job. So consider me in charge of Affliction now. This is my show from here on out.” Beckett smirks as we already take an early commercial break, as the stage has been set for here tonight. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = When we come back from the short break we head to the backstage area where Aspen Chaud is slowly getting ready for Battle Royal tonight. She has just tightened up her boots when her locker room door opens and in walks Tony Miranda. TONY MIRANDA: “What in the world are you doing?” Aspen looks up at him a little confused by his statement, as she gets to her feet. ASPEN CHAUD: “You told me I needed to show up?” Aspen looks around a little confused as Tony chuckles a bit, shaking his head quickly. TONY MIRANDA: “No, no, no. I asked you here so you could be my valet. You’re going to make sure I win. It’s time to go.” Tony turns walking out leaving Aspen a little shocked but not really expecting anything different from Tony. As she turns and grabs her arm thing she heads out, shocked by who she sees. When the camera pans over, it shows none other then Wake Walker standing right in front of her. ASPEN CHAUD: “Wake?” She seems like she’s seen a ghost as he walks right by her not saying a word as the show takes another commercial break, hyping up ‘Exile’ as we go to break. QUOTE When we return we head to the ring where the fans are all in the same murmur. Standing in the ring are all of Affliction’s stars all around the ring. Right away we see everyone staring at Wake Walker whom stands in the corner. The fans themselves are stunned to see him again and he looks better then ever. RILEY STEVENS: “Can you believe this! WAKE WALKER is here tonight. Former Galveston Island Champion!” COLIN MCKENDRY: “This really caught everyone off guard.” COLT COX: “Walker has always had a flare for the dramatic.” Eric Sparrow looks the most angry about it while Aspen Chaud who is at ringside still seems to be soaking the fact that Wake is back. Some of the stars who were not around when he was here seem very unfazed as the bell rings! DING DING DING! Right away all hell breaks loose as everyone begins to battle it out in the center of the ring. On one corner we see Eric Sparrow run right at his rival Walker throwing punches as he gets him into the corner. Otherwise not a lot can be seen as the ring is so full. Suddenly we realize one group that could do well as on one side we see all of High Voltage standing in a corner with one another fighting off competitors. Suddenly we see big Seth Seeker clotheslining the hell out of Ashten Dawkins over the top rope! Ashten Dawkins is Eliminated (Seth Seeker) The fans go wild seeing the first elimination as on the other side we see the two females in High Voltage working together to take out Christina Marie. Christina Marie is Eliminated (High Voltage) The fans continue to go wild the ring still full of stars but the field is beginning to thin out. We see a mistake on the other side of the ring as Ashley Taylor tries to slap Sparrow only for him to duck her and toss her out of the ring. Ashley Taylor is Eliminated (Eric Sparrow) Things are progressing quickly as we can see Seeker try to throw Sparrow over only for him to hold onto the rope and slither back in. Walker is starting to look good as he battles with Jacoby Jackson, in the corner. Tony Miranda can be seen being double teamed by High Voltage’s males only for the AKA to save him. Suddenly camera cuts real quickly back to Walker as we see Jackson flying over the ropes to the outside. Jacoby Jackson is Eliminated (Wake Walker) Walker is back for sure as he turns around and begins to brawl with Mike Bryant now the two going at it. It’s Seth Seeker that is looking impressive now as he holds Kydd Lightning helplessly in the air before literally tossing him out of the ring, to a big thud on the outside! Kydd Lightning is Eliminated (Seth Seeker) Brandy Danielle has now picked a fight with both Elizabeth Reave and Amanda Henry bouncing back and forth giving them shots. The fans go wild for her, cheering her on as her friend Roxie Nash now comes to help her with the beating. We cut to Phat Fury who has a ‘Pass Out’ a Dragon Sleeper on Nate Amazing! The fans watch as Nate goes limp and Fury throws his limp body over the ropes. Nate Amazing is Eliminated (Phat Fury) Almost immediately we cut to see Scott Reave trying to hold onto the ropes. He can’t for long though as he gets caught with a vicious Clothesline by Tony Miranda sending him out of the ring and to the mat eliminating the former contender. Scott Reave is Eliminated (Tony Miranda) The eliminations are coming quickly in this one as the numbers have dwindled here in this one. The fans watch as Acquin lifts Bryant into the air, in a Powerbomb position bringing him to the ropes and tossing him harshly out of the ring where he lands hard. Mike Bryant is Eliminated (Joshua Acquin) The fans are on nobody’s side in particular but they are booing as A.J. and Justin get Craven against the ropes jumping up and dropkicking him right over the ropes. Slade Craven is Eliminated (High Voltage) The fans watch as Assassin tries to catch them off guard with a Clothesline only for them to duck and give him a big back body drop over the ropes right onto his teammate Craven, High Voltage continuing its reign of terror in this match. Assassin is Eliminated (High Voltage) Things have thinned out to the point where we can see more of the ring now. Everyone is now fighting it out, with nobody seeming to have the advantage. Seeker though proves his strength as Avery gives him a few kicks, before heading top rope. He dives off for a cross body but Seeker catches him and dumps him over the top rope. Jake Avery is Eliminated (Seth Seeker) Now the camera focuses in on the other side where we see Roxie Nash storm at Reave and Henry. They however both duck down, grabbing the rope and sending her right over them, falling to the mat and to her elimination. Roxie Nash is Eliminated (High Voltage) The fans boo at High Voltage’s team tactics. They cheer as Reave and Henry attempt to lift Brandy up, only for her to give a big punch to Reave and then one to Henry. She rushes forward giving both a Clothesline out of the ring! The fans go absolutely wild for her. Elizabeth Reave & Amanda Henry are Eliminated (Brandy Danielle) Suddenly everything comes to a calm. Everyone in the ring begins to look around at one another. We have A.J. Lightning, Justin Volt, Wake Walker, Tony Miranda, Seth Seeker, Phat Fury, Joshua Acquin, Brandy Danielle, and Eric Sparrow all in the ring! The fans go insane as suddenly they all run at one another. AJ and Justin surprisingly attack Seeker while Acquin and Fury come to fight and naturally Sparrow and Walker fight again. The odd balls out are Tony Miranda and Brandy Danielle. At first Tony shakes his head telling her to leave, but she gives him a big slap to the face! The fans go insane as she bounces off the ropes and goes after him! She nails him with an elbow then turns to run off the ropes again, only to everyone’s surprise Aspen Chaud jumps up and pulls down the ropes causing Brandy to tumble backwards and be eliminated! Brandy Danielle is Eliminated (Tony Miranda) After a moment Brandy gets up upset, pushing Aspen who tries to tell her that Tony made her do it but to no avail. Brandy suddenly calls her out then turns and heads to the back, as Tony looks over at what is happening with a smile on his face. He hides in a corner, allowing the others to fight. We see Walker and Sparrow exchanging punches with ruthless intent for one another. Seeker is trying to battle off both AJ and Justin but he’s having troubles. When they get him down in the corner they suddenly turn seeing Tony and immediately chasing after him. The fans now watch in the corner where Fury catches Acquin with a big elbow, with him stunned he grabs him by the back of the head and gives him a big toss over the ropes. Joshua Acquin is Eliminated (Nate Fury) Almost right away when he turns around meeting a big Seeker punch that sends him reeling to the ropes. Tony however is still trying to fight AJ and Justin but their numbers are clearly getting to him. Perhaps the best fight is Eric Sparrow and Wake Walker whom can’t seem to take one another out, though they have been trying hard to do so. Suddenly the crowd gasps as Seeker tosses Phat Fury over. They are shocked as he is able to land on the ropes. He turns and gives a big punch to Seeker then tries to go Springboard only for Seeker to land a big punch that instead sends him tumbling backwards out of the ring. Phat Fury is Eliminated (Seth Seeker) Sparrow out of no where hits a Superkick on Walker! The fans go silent not expecting that. He tries to lift him up, but is unable to lift the dead weight. Seeker now turns his attention to them. Sparrow points at Wake commanding Seeker to throw him over. Seth looks down at Eric giving him a second to reconsider commanding him, but when he does it again Seeker grabs him by the throat and lifts him over the ropes. He’s able to reach forward and rake his eyes, dropping onto the side of the ring barely able to hold on! The fans realize things are tense, as Seeker holds his eyes then comes forward swinging at Sparrow. Eric ducks under it and then jumps up wrapping his legs around Seeker’s head trying to pull him over! Suddenly the fans watch as a recovered Wake Walker sits up, and then gets right up seeming fine. Sparrow is trying with all his heart to do it, but Seeker with his body hanging over is able to keep his feet down. Suddenly Walker grabs him by the legs and tosses him over the ropes when he goes over so does Eric Sparrow eliminating both men. Eric Sparrow is Eliminated (Wake Walker) Seth Seeker is Eliminated (Wake Walker) Finally we are down to four men. Tony Miranda has just been pulled over the ropes where now A.J. and Justin are on both sides of him. They both start to try to take his grasp of the ropes off, when suddenly to crowd is shocked as Aspen Chaud comes around pulling A.J. by the leg and out of the ring eliminating him. A.J. Lightning is Eliminated (Tony Miranda) Aspen now tries to do the same to Justin only for A.J. to grab her and toss her into the ring post taking her out of the equation. Walker has now turned around watching the two fight, suddenly A.J. grabs Tony’s legs trying to pull him away. Right as he does Walker comes across crushing Justin with a kick just as he grabs onto Tony sending all of them falling on top of one another to end the match. Tony Miranda is Eliminated (Wake Walker) Justin Volt is Eliminated (Wake Walker) Everyone in the arena cheers as the return of Wake Walker has been met with success. He raises his arm in the air in celebration as the match is over. RILEY STEVENS: “Wake Walker has won, here! It appears High Voltage attempts to take out Tony Miranda caught them in the end when Walker was able to capitalize.” COLIN MCKENDRY: “Wake Walker is in charge next week, scary thought right there.” COLT COX: “But we can’t think too far ahead, so everyone tune in to ‘Exile’ for what will be a huge show.” Wake Walker is in the ring, with a big smirk. Tony Miranda has gotten up and has started to bail out of there, realizing he’s outnumbered with Aspen out cold. High Voltage gets to their feet screaming at him, their feud continuing as we cut back to Walker in the ring, the man that will get to book Affliction on his return as the show fades. WINNER: WAKE WALKER
