GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: REVOLT: UNSCRIPTED Live From Stewart Beach - April 21st, 2014 BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! The show begins in spectacular fashion as fireworks stream into the air. The music plays and the camera pans around as we cut to the ring relatively quickly tonight where we see Amanda Rowland in the ring with Dennis Driver and Michael Lynch. Dennis stands on one side of the ring, Lynch the other, with Rowland in the middle. AMANDA ROWLAND: “Ladies and gentlemen, please welcome my guests at this time… the Deep South Champion… Dennis Driver!” Dennis holds the title belt above his plans to massive amounts of cheers. AMANDA ROWLAND: “And the number one contender… Michael Lynch!” Lynch refuses to pander to the fans who boo him loudly. AMANDA ROWLAND: “At Aggressive Expansion, these two men will square off with the Deep South title on the line. But, tonight, they will have a very rare opportunity – to pick their opponent’s poison.” The crowd screams, Rowland pauses a moment. AMANDA ROWLAND: “These men have been thinking all week about what stipulation to choose for their opponent’s match that will give them the maximum edge for their match at the iPPV. So, Dennis, as the champion, why don’t you go first and tell us what you chose.” She turns towards Driver who smiles. DENNIS DRIVER: “Well, Amanda, I thought long and hard about this. I thought maybe I could put Lynch in a cage match, I thought I could put him in a strap match… hell… I considered a barbed wire match – but I decided to pick the one match he has proven he can’t win…” Dennis stares down Lynch as the crowd cheers loudly. DENNIS DRIVER: “A tables match.” “WE-WANT-TA-BLES” chant. Lynch chuckles. DENNIS DRIVER: “Michael, you showed us just how bad you are at title matches when you lost your belt to Madison Seton in one just a few weeks ago… I have no doubt that you will get put through a table again tonight.” One fan loudly screams out “LYNCH FEARS THE TABLE”. Driver is amused. MICHAEL LYNCH: “That is really funny Dennis. I can tell you thought long and hard about that. But I thought harder.” Lynch takes a step closer to his opponent. MICHAEL LYNCH: “You see, I understand that If you want something done right, you have to do it yourself. AMANDA ROWLAND: “I’m sorry Lynch, but that won’t work. We all know you’ll just refuse to count pins for Driver, and you’ll probably jump him. AMANDA ROWLAND: “Get ready for your match Lynch – it is next!” Lynch starts to stretch as Rowland climbs out of the ring. Madison gets up looking at him as Erns sends a early message to the champion. Getting back up Seton angrily charges CDE as he ducks under her clothesline attempt and then spins her around landing a kick to her knee then her left leg then her chest going crazy with kicks as these fans go crazy. Not even close to getting Erns down in this big of a opportunity for him. Seeming angry that he even kicked out Seton kicks him in the gut before he can get up then steps down looking at the Camel Clutch trying to get it locked in but CDE fights it off doing his best to keep out of it but after a big shot with a elbow she gets the Camel Clutch locked in. These fans cringe knowing how devastating the move is as she cranks on it really pulling it back on Erns as he tries to reach for the ropes but she does her best to tug him away from them. Seeming far away Erns seems to kick it into another gear crawling and fighting to the ropes reaching out and grabbing a hold of it with his fingertips leading to a huge roar from the crowd as the ref makes the count 1..2..3...4...Seton breaks at the last moment getting up and angrily stepping back as Erns rolls under the ropes. These fans go wild as Madison falls to a seated position holding her shoulder as the referee hits a five count. Erns follows her back into the ring now as Seton continues to favor her shoulder as Erns catches her as she gets up grabbing her and landing with a Russian Legsweep of his own followed quickly by a kip up that gets these fans going, as he calls on them to get his momentum as he bounces off the ropes and times Seton getting up perfectly with a Running Shoulder Block. ITS OVER!” People can’t help but think what Oliver is thinking but Erns starts to churn his legs a bit trying to get to the ropes as his hand is prepped for a tap and his face looks ready to pass out as he reaches out his leg missing the ropes then finally he kicks out in his last ditch effort and he manages to hook the leg! RILEY STEVENS: “What a match, these two just went down together who will get up first.” Erns and Seton lay on the mat getting a slight breather but CDE scoots himself closer looking perhaps to be going for a pin but suddenly locking in ‘The Twister’ his own submission move here now as he cranks on her neck now using everything he has left in his gas tank to lock in the move as Seton tries her best to get out but Erns really has it tight and is really cranking her head to the side as it’s her head that is now turning read as CDE cranks that Twister as hard as he can but Seton refuses to tap despite being near the middle of the ring and finally after pulling on his arms and slipping her head out, she finally pulls apart his arms long enough to slip out of the move as she rolls away but Erns moves up grabbing her from behind and rolling her up 1…Kick Out!
