GIW — Card

Revolt, 12 April 2012

12 April 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: THURSDAY NIGHT REVOLT LIVE! From the Monroe Civic Center in Monroe, LA – April 12, 2012 BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! As the show starts we already hear the crowd booing as the ring has balloons at every corner of the ring with a huge table in the center of the ring with a cake reading ‘23’ and then of course Alexis Terry sporting a dress and her GIW Legacy Championship over her shoulder with a microphone in hand as she lets the fans boos die down so that she can talk. ALEXIS TERRY: “As if Aspen and I didn’t have enough to celebrate you know with having both retained our titles at ‘Exile’ and soon to be heading to my home country England in a few weeks, it just so happens that tonight is a very special day…For Aspen Chaud. Now as her best friend I came out here so that we could give her the birthday she deserves. So without further delay the birthday girl Aspen Chaud!” Naturally the fans don’t give her any more love considering it’s her birthday instead just booing louder as her music hits and she heads out from the back with a smile on her face heading to the ring pretending not to have expected this as she walks up the steps into the ring walking across and hugging Alexis as she grabs a mic placed for her off the table. ASPEN CHAUD: “You are such a sweet heart Alexis, thank you for this and you fans for attending.” As if these fans had a choice as Alexis reaches over grabbing her a huge bag of things handing it to Aspen who sets it down. ASPEN CHAUD: “You shouldn’t have.” Alexis urges her to look inside as she reaches in pulling out a jewelry box, Aspen covering her mouth as she opens it up to reveal a necklace. Smiling she puts it around her neck allowing Alexis to click it on for her. RILEY STEVENS: “Make it stop.” Aspen again reaches back into the bag this time pulling out a bikini, as she shows it off for the crowd a few whistles can be heard as Alexis smiles stepping back. ALEXIS TERRY: “Should she try it on?” For perhaps the first time at least the males in the crowd give her a better reaction as Alexis and Aspen look around as Aspen shrugs and pretends to put it on only to put it back in the bag. ASPEN CHAUD: “They don’t deserve it.” The fans now boo them as Aspen reaches back into the bag pulling out two shirts one she hands to Alexis and then one she takes for herself as they both put it on over what they are wearing. Alexis’s shirt reads “Terry > Carter” and Aspen’s reads “Chaud > Kluge” as the fans begin to boo these pink shirts. ASPEN CHAUD: “Oh my God I love it all Alexis.” ALEXIS TERRY: “There is one more gift but that will be ready come at ‘International X’.” Aspen smiles and the two embrace in a hug as “The Rat” by Dead Confederate hits and the fans begin to actually cheer more then boo just because they knew at least Kluge would end this as he walked out from the back with a mic in hand. MATT KLUGE: “Just shut the hell up. You know when I say I want to change this place, starting the show off with a birthday party every week is one thing I want to get rid of. But most importantly I want to get rid of the two useless things in the ring right now. I know you guys think that you got the belts, you got Sinclair wrapped around your fingers, that you run this place. But here’s what you fail to realize that those belts are what gives you power. I won’t pretend to think Hayden will beat you Alexis but Aspen your days as champion are numbered. I’ve beaten Alex Castellanos, Tony Miranda, and Alioth Starre and that means that I have one last superstar to beat and that is you Aspen. And then there is no one else left and it’ll be me that runs this company.” Aspen and Alexis angrily glare up the stage at him as he finishes up, the fans still not liking him at all as Aspen raises her mic to answer him. ASPEN CHAUD: “You want to know what Tony, Alioth, and I have in common that you don’t? We have all been champion. It’s funny you keep calling yourself the best ever yet…Never once have you been the top star. You got rid of Alioth for me and that means when I beat you, I will have cleared this company of every challenge I have and then it will be Alexis and I that lead this company into a future where entertainment is what matters, because after all these pretty faces sell a whole lot better then your ugly mug Kluge.” Kluge doesn’t really even pretend to care as Aspen and Alexis high five in the ring thinking that she got him good as he walks back and forth answering her. MATT KLUGE: “Aspen…I am going to hurt you, I am going to disfigure that so called pretty face of yours and then with out your…looks…your belt and your power around here you will become irrelevant. Two weeks Aspen and there will be no arguing that I am the best ever. Happy…Birthday.” Kluge drops the microphone heading towards the back as Aspen and Alexis bicker in the ring as we head to a commercial break. QUOTE It is time for the first match of the night as Mercy and Omen already are ready to go in the center of the ring as their music ends and "Frontline" by Pillar hits and the fans begin to boo as out from the back comes Daniel Starr and Paul Defias together walking towards the ring both with that big grin on their face. RILEY STEVENS: “These two guys have been a great tag team, working together so well.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “That’s for sure they lost to Hayden at ‘Exile’ but they belong as a team.” Starr and Defias head into the ring where they go to their corner looking across at Mercy and Omen as the referee calls for the bell to start this thing off. DING DING DING! Like a ball of fire Mercy starts this match as DefiaStarr elects Starr. Mercy rushes at him only to be given a Hip Toss over him by Daniel. Hopping up with all his speed Mercy heads back at him only to walk into a boot from Starr then a follow up Belly to Belly Suplex that sends him flying across the ring where he tags in Omen. Omen jumps the ropes and rushes at Starr swinging at him and missing as he hits the ropes and then runs into Starr’s awaiting hand which he uses to Chokeslam him. With Omen down and holding his back Mercy tries to save the match re-entering the ring only to receive a ‘Starrstruck’ his version of the Brogue Kick! Mercy rolls out of the ring as Daniel heads to the top rope jumping off with a Shooting Starr Press on Omen, beautiful. As soon as he connects he hooks the leg 1…2…3! JAMAL JACKSON: “Here are your winners…DefiaStarr!” Starr gets up holding up his arms in victory as Defias enters the ring hugging him as he then gets the referee to hold up his arm as well, though he never really got into the match. RILEY STEVENS: “Starr did it all by himself tonight and picks up the win for his team in dominant fashion.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “These guys are good.” As the two are celebrating Paul heads up to the turnbuckle as Starr does the same on the other. Starr steps down first as Defias jumps down of his heading towards Daniel for Starr to rear up and connect with the ‘Starrstruck’ a second Brogue Kick to Defias?! RILEY STEVENS: “What the…?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Starr what are you doing?” Laying waste to his own tag team partner now Daniel slips through the ropes with Mercy, Omen, and Defias all laid to waste as he heads towards the back with the fans all looking around just as confused as we are as Starr has just given Defias the ‘Starrstruck’, but why? WINNERS: DANIEL STARR & PAUL DEFIAS Backstage we go after the apparent break up of DefiaStarr where we see Faith Simpson warming up in the back as James Dean walks up to her with a big smirk on his face patting her on the back. JAMES DEAN: "So you are the little fifteen year old girl I have been hearing so much about?" Faith looks up at him a little confused as he grins patting her on the back. JAMES DEAN: "Don’t worry, don’t worry. I am not hear to hurt you, I am just hear to simply warn you. You see the brackets line up that if the two of us win, we will meet next week. So let me put it this way, I have no problem beating a girl your age to get to the title. All right?" Dean grins as Faith shrugs her shoulders and responds confidently. FAITH SIMPSON: “A lot of people have said that before you.” Faith now looks to get away as she heads away from him but James smirks and talks as she goes. JAMES DEAN: "But they weren’t me." With that the tables were set for next week, if these two could find a way to win and get to the next round for a match to decide who faces Henry Malkin. QUOTE After a small ‘International X’ teaser we return to the ring where Big J already stands in wait as his music ends and "Darker Side of Me" by The Veer Union hits and the fans cheer the returning Joshua Acquin who heads out from the back down towards the ring with a smile on his face. RILEY STEVENS: “This match is the first of two Television title contendership semi-final matches. Acquin returns tonight looking to get back to the belt which he has held several times already.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Yeah he ditched those that helped him, he is a fool.” Acquin high fives some fans then walks around the ring heading up the steps on the other side going into the ring as his music now fades and “Bawitdaba” by Kid Rock hits and the fans give a mixed reaction not yet knowing much about the debuting James Dean. RILEY STEVENS: “This guy got bumped up from Evolve to this match and is looking to capitalize on it.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I think he looks rather good.” Dean walks past the crowd seeming all business as he looks into the ring at Big J and Acquin then steps through the ropes; the three getting set up as the match is started. DING DING DING! James Dean and Joshua Acquin began the bout with a lock up. Big J waited at the ropes, an opportunist. Acquin broke the hold with a high knee strike. Instead of a second lockup, the two turned toward their waiting opponent. Together, Dean and Acquin slammed Big J with a clothesline over the top rope. With Big J temporarily out of the picture, Joshua Acquin pulled James Dead into a flowing DDT. Dean rolled out of the ring after the attack and struck Big J before he could follow up on Acquin’s assault. Acquin stood and backed away a few paces, awaiting his enduring opponent’s return. James Dean slid back in and squared off with Acquin, both scrutinizing the other and waiting for their in on the opponent. Dean charged first. Acquin went for a clothesline, but ducked under and continued his sprint across the ring. A bounce of the ropes and he was at Acquin again, but Joshua countered with a falling neckbreaker and retaining the upper hand. He covered Dean for the pin, but it was only for the two count. Acquin pulled away and then his opponent up with him. Acquin hooked an arm over Dean’s neck for another DDT, but Dean pushed off and performed a standing dropkick to counter. RILEY STEVENS: “Dean and Acquin are really going at it.” He climbed over Acquin and performed a few raw forearm smashes before the referee called him off. He backed off and kept his opponent down with a few hard right stomps. Dean was about to apply an ankle lock when he noticed Big J return to the scene. He had enough time to quickly run up the turnpost and leap back off like a gliding raptor and swoop down onto Big J with a diving cross body. He pinned him for a two count, broken by Acquin’s hard punt to the skull. The impact was enough to convince Acquin to then cover Dean, but it had only reached a two count as well. Dean backed himself into the corner to recover when Big J came in for Acquin. Acquin countered a strike with his own and then toppled Big J with a chop block. He leapt in place and came down concisely with a jumping knee drop to Big J’s right knee. This became a theme with Acquin and Big J, strikes to the knee. He continued to weaken it as much as possible, even pulling his opponent into a sharp shooter. RILEY STEVENS: “Can Acquin draw the tap?” Big J raised his hand out, ready to tap, when out of almost no where James Dean dove from the ropes with a missile dropkick that knocked Acquin clear into yesterday. His back laid flat on the canvas next to Big J. Dean was down stood tall over the two and had his choice of whom he would content against, so he chose Big J first. He used a european uppercut and a few hooks to soften up his groggy, weakened opponent, then with an LD 50, he dropped Big J into the canvas with a thud and splat. The splat from dropping him on top of Acquin. RILEY STEVENS: “Two birds one stone.” Acquin recovered slowly, but quickly enough to stop the cover attempt Dean had made over Big J. He rolled over his opponent and quickly took hold of his legs and began to fold him into the figure four leglock, but Dean wouldn’t allow it. The two struggled for a moment with who might get the upper hand, but eventually Acquin managed to put the hold in tight and dropped down to help apply pressure on his opponent. Dean’s back lay flat as he suffered. The referee would begin a count and Dean would have to raise up his shoulders. The suffering continued for him until he slowly found a way to roll his opponent over onto his stomach and reverse the momentum of the hold. Acquin eventually crawled to the ropes and clung for safety. The referee forced James Dean off, just in time for him to counter a move from Big J with an Olympic slam. RILEY STEVENS: “Who can get this win?” James Dean looked to Acquin, who was huddled at the ropes trying to catch his breath and held onto his lower back. Dean then locked that weakened leg of Big J into an ankle lock. It didn’t take long for Big J to start tapping out with eager to escape. Acquin slid himself out of the ring in disappointment as Dean pulled away from his opponent the victor. JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…James Dean!” Dean holds up his arms stamping his spot in the finals next week as Acquin just couldn’t get there before Big J tapped out, both men really having strong performances regardless. RILEY STEVENS: “And James Dean capitalizes on his big opportunity in this match and gets the victory.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “This kid looks talented.” The fans pat Acquin on the back as he walks up the ramp as Dean celebrates in the ring, needing one more win to get to that title shot. WINNER: JAMES DEAN Backstage we go again this time we head to Alexis Terry getting geared up for the Main Event match tonight when the door opens up as we see first a bundle of flowers then the man holding them Ryan Cavallari as Alexis looks up at them smiling. ALEXIS TERRY: “Oh Ryan...” Terry grins as he walks across the room presenting them to her as she takes them taking a sniff of them. RYAN CAVALLARI: “You know I just wanted to come back and apologize for what happened at church on Sunday, I messed up.” Busy looking at the flowers she sets them to the side then finally looks back at him shaking her head. ALEXIS TERRY: “It’s ok. I forgive you…” Ryan smiles then is pulled into a kiss by Alexis, the two engaging in a lick lock for a few moments before Alexis pulls away as the two chuckle and we go back to the ring. QUOTE Back to the ring we go for the second of the two Television title tournament bouts this one Faith Simpson and Maria Sotiris, the ladder who has already found her way down to the ring standing in the corner as "Power On Me" by Katy B hits and the fans go wild as Faith Simpson heads out from the back with a smile on her face as she bounces down towards the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “This girl right here is a former Legacy Champion and many have proclaimed her the favorite in this thing. But as you see James Dean is down here at ringside to watch the match.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “God she better not win.” Faith heads up the steps and jumps over the top rope where she is most comfortable as she stretches out in her corner as the ref calls for the bell as we get a shot of James Dean at ringside. DING DING DING! As soon as the match starts Maria Sotiris heads to the middle of the ring staring down at Faith, seeming to be enjoying taunting her a bit. Maria was after all an extremely tall woman, standing at six feet tall. Faith looking up at her didn’t seem too worried as Maria shoved her backwards into the ropes. Simpson smiled and did just what she was taught, exploit that weakness as she rushes forward and dropkicks her tree like legs, as Maria stumbles back holding her knee. Faith wastes little time following it up with a Running Crossbody that knocks Maria to the mat. Simpson grinned as she stepped through the ropes grabbing a hold of them going to give these fans a show as she jumps up going Springboard for a Springboard DDT just as soon as Maria gets up. RILEY STEVENS: “Faith takes flight!” Simpson steps back with a smile on her face as the fans as usual cheer her on as she waits for Maria to get back to her feet and when she does she looks to finish it as she grabs her and hits the ‘Losing Faith’ a Twist of Fate that plants Maria near the corner of the week. And while Mark wouldn’t condone doing more then you have to Simpson wanted to give these fans something to remember as she climbed up to the top rope, Maria out of it and not getting up. Just like that Faith went airborne with a ‘Have A Little Faith’ a 630 Senton as she smashes down on Maria then hooks the leg 1…2…3! JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…Faith Simpson!” These fans go wild for her as she stamps her ticket to the finals of the TV Title contendership tournament as she heads to the top rope. RILEY STEVENS: “James Dean and Faith Simpson in the finale, a very worth while conclusion to this thing.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Let’s go James.” Sliding into the ring Dean walks up behind her as she turns into him as the fans boo him expecting him to attack instead the two simply share a little stare, as by next week they will meet in a match for a shot at the Television belt. WINNER: FAITH SIMPSON To David Sinclair’s office we go only instead of Sinclair we see Beck Ramsey again taking a seat in his office having found a little niche messing with Sinclair but as he sits there looking at paper work he probably shouldn’t be Sinclair walks into the room immediately turning red. DAVID SINCLAIR: “What the hell are you doing in my office again?” Ramsey chuckles taking a drink out of a cup that was probably originally David’s setting it down no coaster on David’s desk. BECK RAMSEY: “Oh just looking at a few things you know, why not right?” Always relaxed Beck grins up at David looking down at some paper as Sinclair walks over ripping them out of his hands. DAVID SINCLAIR: “Listen Beck you realize that the only realize I ever signed you to that big contract was because we needed you to finish out that match with Alexis. Now that you LOST to her I can fire you whenever you want.” Sinclair glares down at him as Beck pulls out another file slipping out his contract as he over looks it. BECK RAMSEY: “That’s funny, says here you can’t fire me for the next year.” Ramsey points it out as Sinclair realizes that he is right then takes the contract out of his hand and smirks. DAVID SINCLAIR: “Fine then you are suspended indefinitely! I can do that.” Beck seeming to realize that he is now serious gets up looking at him. BECK RAMSEY: “You are going to suspend me? For what fooling around?” Sinclair pushes him aside taking the cup off the wood and moving it to a coaster as he looks back at Beck angrily. DAVID SINCLAIR: “That’s right! You can wrestle tonight but after that you are suspended. And by the time this suspension ends nobody will remember your name.” David tries to organize his things as Beck looks at him seeming to be thinking. BECK RAMSEY: “You know sooner or later someone is going to take your place and they are going to run this place a lot better then you.” Just as a final insult Beck shoves over his pencil holder sending them all over the floor as he heads out of the room with Sinclair glaring at him all the way out. ==== In the locker rooms things can be scary at times and as we head back there we find an angry Matt Matlock slamming his fist into a locker room still upset from his Evolve loss earlier tonight. MATT MATLOCK: “That was a fast count! I had that match in the bag.” As he slams his locker shut now Sean Mendez the man whom he lost to earlier tonight. SEAN MENDEZ: “Nice match out there man, was close…Give or take.” Matlock seems to zone in on just the first few words, walking towards him angrily. MATT MATLOCK: “Give or take? What is that supposed to mean? You think you are better then me? You got lucky. I’ll prove it, me and you next week in a rematch.” Matlock storms by him exiting the building as Mendez turns watching him go shrugging his shoulder as we head to commercial. QUOTE After a short break we return to the ring with The Shadow standing in the ring having just debuted back at ‘Exile’ impressively taking out the former Shadow. As he stands in the ring ready to go his music trails off and “Busy Being Born” by Middle Class Rut hits and the fans erupt into cheers as the Television Champion enters from the back with a smile on his face; Henry Malkin heading towards the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Shadow made an impressive debut can he end Henry’s streak of under a minute wins?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I am guessing yes.” Malkin does not seem the least bit scared of the man in the ring as he heads up the step undoing his belt and holding it in the air then handing it out of the ring heading to his corner as the referee steps in between them and calls for the bell. DING DING DING! Never blink in a Malkin match, Henry rushes forward perhaps making a huge mistake as he runs right into the awaiting hand of Shadow who grabs him around the throat as the fans get up thinking there may be a change of guard but Malkin knees him in the gut then dropkicks his leg out from underneath him as the fans go wild as he gets back up to one knee and Henry hits the ropes and runs at him with the ‘Euthanasia’ a Shining Wizard! The fans go wild at first until they realize it didn’t knock Shadow down only seeming to daze him. If it don’t work the first time? Try it again. That’s exactly what Malkin did as he got back up hitting the ropes and bam hit the ‘Euthanasia’ a Shining Wizard again this time it knocks Shadow out as Malkin hooks the leg 1…2…3! JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…Henry Malkin!” These fans go wild as yet again he did it in under a minute as Malkin gets up to his feet heading up to the top rope where he holds up his arms in yet another quick victory. RILEY STEVENS: “Wow he did it again! This is getting to be crazy, Malkin gets the win.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “This is insane.” Shadow rolls out of the ring not really building off his impressive debut instead falling victim to the Malkin “Minute Challenge”. Henry hops down out of the ring heading over to celebrate with the fans yet again, a quick win. WINNER: HENRY MALKIN As we return from commercial "World Warrior" by RedRapper began to play. The crowd exploded in cheers as out walked "The New World Warrior" Tony Miranda, a man who stood victorious after the brutal Last Man Standing Match against the psychotic Johnny Scar. RILEY STEVENS: “What a win this man had at ‘Exile’ defeating Scar in that Last Man Standing match.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “That psycho tried to kill him!” He looked at the fans with a smile on his face as he walked down towards the ring, holding a microphone in his hands. Tony slides into the ring and hops up. He walks towards the ring ropes and rests on them, looking out at the crowd. As his theme dies down he listens to the crowd as they chant "Last Man Standing" which puts a smile on Tony's face. He slowly raises the microphone to his lips, prepared to speak to the crowd. TONY MIRANDA: "Wow. I swear, even after a year and a half here in GIW, I still get a rush from you guys. This is why I love this place. Galveston Island Wrestling has without a doubt, the best fans in the world." The crowd cheers. TONY MIRANDA: "Last week at Exile I fought against one of my most dangerous opponents, Johnny Scar. For weeks I was his target, I was the man that he wanted to destroy. But every time he tried, every time he tried to end me, I refused to die. I stood up from even his brutality during the Taipei Death Match. It was then he knew, I could not be taken down by normal means. He knew that he needed to kill me to keep me down. At the same time I knew that I had to do the same against him. And so, the day came. We didn't care about Alexis Vs Beck. We didn't care about Kluge vs Starre. The only thing that mattered to us were the fight against each other. I told Johnny Scar many times, that his story would end with mine. I told him that the moment I wrote my epilogue, his story would meet its eventual, tragic end. And I was right. I stood victorious against him." The crowd once again cheers for his victory. TONY MIRANDA: "However, I was not expecting to see this day. Last week at Exile...I was prepared to die. I was prepared to die against Johnny Scar. I expected him to kill me, and he indeed tried to. But, if it weren't for Hayden Carter...I probably would not be standing here right now. People have told me that investing in Hayden Carter was a waste. They all told me that I shouldn't have befriended her. If I didn't do that, if I didn't decide to befriend Hayden Carter, I would be dead. She saved my life, and a million thank you's would never be enough for that. I survived against Johnny Scar. And it has led to this moment. This is my epilogue, ladies and gentlemen. This is where I end my story. As of this moment...I announce my retirement from Professional Wrestling." Shock engulfs the entire crowd, they all boo at his decision, the camera showing the faces of a few shocked fans in disbelief. TONY MIRANDA: "I told Johnny Scar that I only had one good fight left in me. He was that last fight. I was prepared to die against him, I was prepared for him to kill me, but Hayden Carter saved me. Even though I stand here alive and well. I stand here with my life and my future while his has ended. While he may still breathe he is behind a cage, perhaps tied up in a straightjacket, slowly waiting for the Grim Reaper to come and claim his life and drag him to Hell for all of his sins. However...the fire that brought me here. The fire that made me who I am, has burned away and died. Every good fighter knows when it's time to call it a day and hang up the gloves. That time has come for me. I may not have ended my career the way I wanted it to, but I could have never asked for a better wrestling promotion to end my career in." The crowd has a mixed reaction to what they were witnessing. Some of them even began to chant "Please don't go". Tony shook his head, showing a sorrowful smile. TONY MIRANDA: "Believe you me, I don't want to leave. But it’s no longer the same anymore. After fifteen years, fighting stops becoming fun. It starts becoming more like work, and when that happens you slowly lose your passion and your fire begins to die. That has happened to me. I know that sooner or later I'll be hearing from fans all across the world to have that "One More Match"...but it won't come. This is where it ends. The final chapter has concluded, all that is left is the epilogue." Tony exits the ring, and slowly begins walking up the stage. TONY MIRANDA: "The Warrior solemnly exited the ring, not looking back for he knew once he did his emotions would become uncontrollable. He only looked forward, listening to the sounds of the people, some silent, while others begged him to stay. But he could not grant their requests. He was tired; he no longer wished to fight. All he wanted now was to live the rest of his days in peace. As the warrior walked away he began to think of his life as a fighter. Starting off as a nobody he fought for pocket change against Chickens and village idiots. But as time went on he moved up through the ranks, fighting tougher and tougher opponents, some he won, some he lost." Tony reaches the halfway point of the stage, he continues to narrarate. TONY MIRANDA: "With each week, his name grew in popularity. And in time he fought against those that many wouldn't even dare think of challenging. He fought against Kings, Beasts, Champions, Queens, and even the Gods. He fought men like Eric Sparrow, Wake Walker, Alioth Starre, Aspen Chaud, and even the man many considered to be the strongest in the world, Ace Baldwin. The Warrior fought them all, and even defeated the great Ace Baldwin. And then came his greatest opponent, the man named Scar. A ruthless savage who knew only of killing and bloodshed. Tony eventually slayed him, with the help of a girl named Hayden." Tony reached the top of the stage, he turned around, looking out at the GIW Arena one final time. TONY MIRANDA: "From a nobody, he became a legend. He became a hero. Now his fight was over and now he had the rest of his life to look forward to. He wondered what his future would hold for him...and as he thought, only two words came to his mind. Two words to describe his thoughts..." Tony looked up to the heavens, and prepared to scream those two words. TONY MIRANDA: "GET HYYYYPE!!!" Tony smiled, unable to hold back his emotions. He lowered his head, still smiling. TONY MIRANDA: "...The End." Tony lowers the mic, and hears the crowd giving him a standing ovation as "World Warrior" begins to play once more. This would be the absolute last time they would hear this song. Tony turns around and walks backstage. Putting an end to his career in not just Galveston Island Wrestling...but professional wrestling altogether. QUOTE After a short break we return to a now much more somber arena filled with the sounds of Ryan Cavallari’s music as the announcers are too busy to talk about him instead focusing on Tony Miranda. RILEY STEVENS: “Just moments ago Tony Miranda retired from pro wrestling and I have to say this now Tony Miranda will always be remembered as one of the best GIW has to offer and without a doubt the man with the most heart. He will really be missed around here.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “See you later!” Ryan Cavallari stands in the ring ready to go as his music fades and "Two Sides of Me" by Hinder hits to a huge response from the crowd as an angry Beck Ramsey heads out from the back seeming a bit upset about the treatment by Sinclair as he heads to the ring for what might be his last match here in GIW for awhile. RILEY STEVENS: “Earlier tonight Sinclair suspended Ramsey indefinitely and he seems to be ready to take that anger out on Ryan.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Beck deserves it, for too long he had been taunting the boss.” Beck stomps up the steps looking across at Ryan who he had expressed respect for earlier but now his anger seems to be fueling him as Cavallari gets ready as the bell is called to start this one. DING DING DING! Cavallari always the quick one tries to get the upper hand quickly but as he storms at Beck he rushes right into a European Uppercut which Beck follows up with another and then another launching Ryan backwards with each as he hits him into the ropes and then grabs him tossing him into the corner where he rushes at him with a Stinger Splash. Cavallari stumbles forward and Beck steps through the ropes in a rarity as Ryan turns around right into a Springboard Clothesline. These fans like Ryan but tonight Beck is the only one getting their love as he pulls away screaming at them to get on their feet and just like that they listen. RILEY STEVENS: “Something tells me these fans will be vocal about Beck’s suspension.” Ramsey turns back towards Ryan who seems really to have just met Beck on a bad day here tonight as he stumbles to his feet trying to get his wits about him but instead of getting them back he loses them in the rafters as Beck runs forward nailing him with ‘The Collision’ a Running Test Boot!
