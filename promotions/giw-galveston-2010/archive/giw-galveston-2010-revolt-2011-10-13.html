GIW — Card

Revolt, 13 October 2011

13 October 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: THURSDAY NIGHT REVOLT LIVE! From Nassau in The Bahamas – October 13, 2011 BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! And here comes the show here in the Bahamas where the fans have taken to GIW just like all of the other stops along this journey. They are going wild as the show gets underway and we head over to the announce table with Riley and Oliver. RILEY STEVENS: “Hello ladies and gentleman we are here in the Bahamas for another great night. We start off with the new ‘GIW Television Champion’ Austin Acquin taking on Koji Nakamora in singles action. Then we see Aspen Chaud and Tony Miranda team up to take on the team of Kevin Mero and Johnny Violence. Then the Ace Baldwin and Alioth Starre competition continues as they each are in singles action as they meet AJ Fairchild and Alex Castellanos respectably. Should be a great--” Before he can finish Ace Baldwin’s Matador made music hits and the fans begin to boo as he walks out wearing that always familiar smirk as he strolls down to the ring to the boos from the fans here in The Bahamas who apparently already know all they need to know about him. He enters the ring with his mic already in hand as he looks out at the crowd with a grin. ACE BALDWIN: “You know I don’t know about any of you but I am getting a little restless waiting for ‘On Hallowed Ground II’. I know for a fact I am going to win the belt but delaying the inevitable is getting annoying. Each week I come out here and embarrass another GIW star…And it’s got me wondering…Who is left? Other then Alioth Starre the answer is very small. Tonight I’ll take care of Alex and I am demanding that I have next week off to rest for my title match.” Ace lowers the mic looking over at the entrance way waiting for Blake but instead it’s "Blood & Fire (Out of the Ashes Mix)" by Type O Negative that hits and the fans immediately pep up as out from the back now is Alioth Starre holding his belt as he shakes his head as he heads towards the ring up the steps and into the ring joining Ace who rolls his eyes annoyed. ALIOTH STARRE: “Next week we are in Key West, Florida. And I think we owe it to them to have a match…I’m just as sick of waiting to expose you so why not move it up a week…Me versus you next week?” But before he can finish to both men’s surprise "Nightmare" by Avenged Sevenfold hits and out from the back comes Austin Acquin wearing his GIW Television Championship over his shoulder as he strolls towards the ring with a grin on his face as he heads up into the ring adding another voice to the mix, both Alioth and Ace stare at him confused. AUSTIN ACQUIN: “Listen, the two of you going back and forth frankly it’s gotten stale. The fans are interested in a new champion…me! The new GIW Television Champion! So if you would politely go to the back and let me start the show…That would be appreciated.” Austin points to the back ready to go tonight as both Ace and Alioth stare at him like he is crazy as Joshua’s younger brother taunts them before "Bullseye" by Aly and AJ hits and the fans go wild with cheers as now coming on out half way through wrapping up her hands for the night is Aspen Chaud but it’s clear she couldn’t wait any more as she heads out towards the ring with a microphone of her own heading into the ring as Ace, Acquin, and Alioth now turn their attention to her. ASPEN CHAUD: “Really guys? I think I speak for everyone in attendance when I say…Shut up! They don’t want to hear you two talk they want to see you fight and Austin don’t get me started about how you were gifted that belt. I’m ready to wrestle for these fans and then earn my shot at the winner of the title match whenever it is.” Aspen stares across at them as Ace laughs at her while Austin seems offended by her remarks but the chaos does not stop as "World Warrior" by RedRapper hits now as the fans again go wild as the recently returned Tony Miranda walks out onto the stage, shrugging as he heads towards the ring with yet another mic…Who knew we had so many. He enters the ring, and it’s now getting rather crowded. TONY MIRANDA: “Can’t help but agree that we are sick of the two of you going back and forth. And Austin, I’m not going to say a word to you. But Aspen we already spoke about this and we are in agreement for a match to determine contendership. So I say lets get on with the show without any further delay.” However the most surprising part yet is coming as “Bat Country” by Avenged Sevenfold hits and the fans look up to the ramp confused as the man who showed up last week Johnny Violence enters from the back with a smirk on his face as he enters the ring and looks around. JOHNNY VIOLENCE: “Is this how every Revolt starts? You all come out here and whine and complain about I want this…I want that. Frankly I am here to win gold so count me in on any contendership match. Cause I will beat the guy with the General Manager as his brother…I’ll beat the short little dude you call a champion...I’ll beat this so called Amazing filler…The lost little girl…And even the martial arts drop out and anyone else in the back. I may be new here…but shut up.” Violence makes a statement but the fact he’s both new and saying it doesn’t seem to make him any friends as the whole ring erupts into a bickering match until there is a loud voice at the top of the ramp, the camera heads to him to reveal it’s Blake Beckett. BLAKE BECKETT: “All of you calm down! Tonight’s matches will go as planned and Alioth…Ace your match will still happen on Halloween Night. But over the past few weeks I keep getting complaints about how this show isn’t unpredictable enough. I keep getting told that we don’t have enough hardcore action. So it got me thinking about how we should head in Revolt next week at Key West. So I came to a conclusion…The six people in the ring all in one match…It’ll be three versus three in a six man tag match only here’s the thing…Falls Count Anywhere…Tornado Tag…Elimination rules will apply! The last person or persons will be declared the winners.” Even though it’s not happening here the fans love the idea as the people in the ring all seem to have differing opinions as Blake continues. BLAKE BECKETT: “Oh and I’m sure your wondering what the teams will be…Right? Well here’s what I have in mind. We go grade school with this one…Ace Baldwin and Alioth Starre as the Captains. Ace gets the first pick.” The fans look around nodding their heads a little shocked as Alioth and Ace head to two separate corners as Ace looks over them and then stops with a huge smirk. ACE BALDWIN: “Behind every great man…there’s a woman to ride his coattails. I pick Aspen.” Ace grins loving this as Aspen walks towards reluctantly as we head now to Alioth who looks at the remaining three. ALIOTH STARRE: “A formidable foe will make a good partner…Tony Miranda.” Tony seems pleased with this as he heads over to Alioth’s side as Ace now looks over the last two before nodding his head. ACE BALDWIN: “Austin Acquin.” Austin heads over to his side with a smirk on his face as Aspen looks at the two disgusted with the two men she finds herself with as Johnny Violence now joins his team. ALIOTH STARRE: “Good luck Ace you’ll need it.” The two sides share a final glance before heading their own ways as we head to the announce table. RILEY STEVENS: “Blockbuster match for next week. Let me get this straight Six Man Tag…Falls Count Anywhere…Elimination Match. That’s a mouthful and it’ll be Team Alioth consisting of Alioth Starre, Tony Miranda, and Johnny Violence taking on Team Ace with Ace Baldwin, Aspen Chaud, and Austin Acquin…What a huge match!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “TEAMMMMMMMMMM ACE!” With that we head to break as the fans are already pumped and ready to go as we take a short break. QUOTE After the commercial break we return and already set up in the ring is Austin Acquin with his GIW Television Championship not having left the ring from the earlier segment. Now joining him in the ring is Koji Nakamora ready to go as well. RILEY STEVENS: “You know last week Austin was awarded the GIW Television Championship by his brother Joshua…” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Could you hate any more then you do? Curtis failed as champion. So did the other twenty champions we’ve had since the belt was reinstated…You want to know the last good champion? Joshua Acquin. So shut your mouth.” Austin hands out his belt and look across the ring grinning at Koji as the referee turns and calls for the bell. DING DING DING! Like a bomb out of a cannon Koji runs recklessly at Austin right as the match starts. But almost knowing its coming Acquin crushes him with a big Clothesline. Nakamora hops right back up and eats another clothesline. Acquin takes a second to throw up his arms as Koji gets back up. Nakamora tries to use his educated feet for a kick but he misses and ends up putting himself right in position for the Flowing DDT! Acquin doesn’t pin him instead he gets up, grinning then running off the ropes and Jumping up and hitting a Knee Drop. Koji gets up holding his face as Austin bounces off the ropes again this time hitting the Spear! He makes the cover 1…2…Kick Out! Koji shows some resiliency but it might be a bad idea as he gets up to his feet struggling to catch his breath as his kick out only seems to anger Austin. Running up on him he grabs him and then sets up the ‘Crusher’ a Full nelson Facebuster! The fans boo as Koji goes out. But rather then pinning him Austin angrily pulls him up even though he is dead weight and finishes him off with the same move the ‘Crusher’ a second time, sending a message even if it wasn’t truly needed as he makes the cover 1…2…3! JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…the ‘GIW Television Champion’…Austin Acquin!” The fans boo him as he gets up kicking Koji out of the ring as he turns and grabs away his belt from the referee holding it up into the air with pride. RILEY STEVENS: “You know too many times I’ve said that this man could be the one to bring back prestige to a belt but I actually think this one has a chance.” Austin heads to the ropes and demands a microphone which he receives walking back to the center of the ring hardly breaking a sweat. AUSTIN ACQUIN: “If that doesn’t tell you that I am serious when I say that I mean business nothing will. I am your GIW Television Champion and you should treat me with respect. No more looking down upon this belt it’s time that I restore its prestige.” With that he throws the microphone down to the mat as the fans boo him as he rolls out of the ring and heads to the back having sent a message with this win. WINNER: AUSTIN ACQUIN After the match we head to the back where Johnny Violence is on his way to the ring when he is stopped by Zachary Pagonis in the hallway who is wielding a microphone. ZACHARY PAGONIS: “Johnny. Can we get your thoughts on being the last pick out there?” Pagonis says this always having to ask the tough question as Violence glares at him and then talks into the mic. JOHNNY VIOLENCE: “The issue is that none of these guys realize what I am about. There is no rules I can use anything against them I want? They should know that is my kind of environment. So if you expect some sob story because I was the last pick. That’s not what you’ll get…What you WILL get will be the blood of each and everyone of them by the end of next week. Just because you people don’t know who I am doesn’t mean that at the end of the day I am still going to take this place over!” Violence shoves the microphone away and then heads onwards to his match as Zachary smiles and we take a break before the next contest. QUOTE Its now time for the tag team part of the night already in the ring waiting is Kevin Mero ready to go. “Bat Country” by Avenged Sevenfold now hits and the fans more familiar with who it is now start to boo it’s clear he hasn’t endeared himself to the fans here tonight. Regardless he walks out into the ring talking some crap to the fans along the way. RILEY STEVENS: “You know while this kid might not be the nicest guy in the world you have to respect that during that opening sequence he had the bravery to walk out with all the veterans and say his piece.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I hate tattoos.” Violence enters the ring and right away what we feared might happen is as Violence and Mero start to bicker as soon as he enters the ring. While they are doing that "Bullseye" by Aly and AJ hits and the fans start to go wild as Aspen Chaud jogs out from the back stopping at the top of the ramp and blowing a kiss to her fans. Heading down towards the ring Aspen high fives some fans then heads up the steps and through the bottom two ropes. RILEY STEVENS: “Aspen and Tony seem to have some sort of an agreement to face off at ‘On Hallowed Ground II’ in some sort of a contenders match your thoughts?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Hopefully they do some sort of stipulation so neither walks out.” Aspen takes her place in the corner stretching out her legs as she watches Kevin and Johnny argue on the other side as "World Warrior" by RedRapper hits and the fans go wild for the man now entering the building in Tony Miranda the New World Warrior. RILEY STEVENS: “You’ve got to think that no matter who wins at ‘On Hallowed Ground II’ Aspen or Tony would make a good next in line.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “If you say so.” Tony Miranda heads up the steps and into the ring next to Aspen, as she immediately calls starting first which Tony seems subtle with on the other side the two argue until Johnny gives Mero a big shove and he gets out. DING DING DING! With Mero exiting the ring Johnny now circles around Aspen with big eyes almost sensing how big of a size advantage he’s got here. Chaud goes in as usual for a lock up that she already knows she won’t win but she tries anyways. Violence pushes her back and then off the ropes. Aspen hits the other ropes and bounces back right into a move Johnny calls the ‘Turn The Other Way’ a Superkick. Fortunately for her she does just as the name implies ducking the kick and going under his legs. Quickly he spins around as Aspen jumps up going for the Lou Thesz but Violence is able to push her to the mat and quickly go for the submission move he calls ‘Scream’ but Aspen again avoids the punishment by kicking him away. Both scramble back to their feet and as Aspen goes after him she runs right into a big kick that stuns her. As she takes a second he rushes off the ropes and then shows off his strength with a ‘Walk’ a Clothesline from Hell! The fans boo as Aspen literally goes airborne 1…2…Kick Out. Aspen is able to fight her way out of it. As Violence gets up soaking in the boos that dominating her brings as early on he’s looked very impressive. RILEY STEVENS: “Thus far it’s all Johnny Violence and maybe he isn’t just all bark and no bite he’s got some talent.” Aspen gets up a little dazed from that huge shot as Johnny attempts to go for the kill here lifting her up onto his shoulders. From there he proceeds to unleash ‘Trashed’ a Torture Rack that bends Aspen in very terrible ways. The fans seem worried as Aspen tries her best to fight through it. Most guys would have tapped but Aspen’s a little more flexible then most and getting angry he drops her off his shoulders harshly to the mat. Chaud starts to get up quickly but has to use the ropes in the corner. Johnny sets it up and runs at her in the corner but at the last second she rolls out of the way. The fans go wild as he hits the corner hard. Aspen immediately bounces off the ropes and with Violence set up in the corner she nails him with the ‘Tush Push’ a Turnbuckle Flying Butt Shot! The fans go wild as Johnny stumbles forward as Aspen finally gets some offense going. From the top rope she follows it up as always with the ‘AirBarbie’ a Flying Crossbody! The crowd goes wild as Aspen starts to crawl towards her corner that’s all she could do at this point after taking a beating. RILEY STEVENS: “Can she get the tag?” Aspen jumps up into the air and makes the tag to Tony! The fans go wild as on the other side Johnny gets up to his feet but stumbles into his corner. A confident Mero tags himself in and it doesn’t sit well with Violence who didn’t want out yet. Tony with the head start on him has the momentum as he nails him with a big clothesline. Both hop up and Tony grabs him around the head and nails him with a Muay Thai Knee Strike. The fans cheer as this stuns Mero as he follows it up with the always great looking Spinning Roundhouse Kick. The fans go wild as Tony gets them on their feet as he bounces off the ropes and does a move we haven’t seen him hit often as he hits the Standing Shooting Star Press with a pin 1…2…Kick Out! The fans go wild as Tony gets up not seeming daunted at all as he walks to his corner getting set up for the ‘Third Strike’ a Running Leg Lariat. The fans are going wild when out of no where Aspen reaches over the ropes and makes the tag. RILEY STEVENS: “Aspen makes the tag?” Tony looks over at her confused as the two start to share a few words as they do in comes Violence. However he doesn’t go after them instead he grabs Mero just as he’s getting up and he in turn hits the ‘Paranoid’ a Ranhei right in the center of the ring. Right away he rolls out of the ring as the referee was distracted with Aspen and Tony who were also distracted with the confusion over the tag. Regardless the referee gets Tony out as Aspen gets into the ring looking to finish this not knowing what just happened. Getting down on his heel she locks in the ‘K.M.A.’ a Heel Lock. Showing his true colors Mero wastes no time trying to fight and just taps out. JAMAL JACKSON: “Here are your winners the team of…Aspen Chaud and Tony Miranda!” Tony returns to the ring and looks at Aspen who gets up with a big grin on her face. He nods his head seeming to be willing to admit that she sort of one upped him in this match but as the referee raises their hands a voice echoes through the place as we find Violence on the outside with a microphone. JOHNNY VIOLENCE: “I am not going to try when I am handcuffed to a joke of a wrestler like the one laying in that ring. So congratulation you two on beating a nobody. You know next week Tony we may be on a team. But I have been hearing through the grapevines about some sort of arrangement between the two of you over some match for contendership. So I am not asking I am telling you that you can have that match but I’ll be included! Because I won’t wait around for my shot, I’m taking it.” Violence drops the microphone and then heads towards the back to the boos of the crowd as Aspen and Tony stare up at him as we head to the back. WINNERS: ASPEN CHAUD & TONY MIRANDA Again it’s to the back where this time we are joined by Austin Acquin who is walking into his locker-room after his win earlier tonight. As soon as he walks in with a red shirt over one shoulder and the belt on the other his brother is waiting. JOSHUA ACQUIN: “Good match out there…Made quick work of him. What is that?” Joshua points to the t-shirt on his shoulder as Austin pulls it off showing it off for Joshua it says “Team Amazing” on the front. AUSTIN ACQUIN: “Ace had them made for next weeks show…No idea how he did it so quickly.” Joshua grabs it and shakes his head, before putting it down and then looking up at his brother. JOSHUA ACQUIN: “Whatever. Next week you have to perform well in that match, do whatever it takes to win the match and make sure you’re the only one standing. Beckett continues to disrespect me so if you can get some wins he’ll have no choice but to realize that I know what I am doing…Then the power will be ours.” Joshua says this with a determined look on his face as Austin pats him on the shoulder. AUSTIN ACQUIN: “I’ll handle it brother.” Josh gives him a reassuring smirk as he throws his arm around him as from there we head back to the ring for the next match. QUOTE It’s now time for the Ace part of your broadcast as already standing in the ring getting a warm welcome from the fans as Alex Castellanos stands in the ring ready to go until Matador’s music hits and out comes Ace Baldwin with that always bright smirk, full of confidence. RILEY STEVENS: “Here comes Ace Baldwin towards the ring. Last week he defeated Tony Miranda but now he faces Castellanos in a singles match.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Another star, another win for Ace Baldwin.” Oliver’s the only one present that seems to be on his side as he heads up the steps and into the ring, jumping up onto the top rope and screaming loud enough for everyone to hear “I’m your next champion!” this only angers the fans even more he jumps down and looks over Alex with a smirk scoffing as the referee calls for the bell. DING DING DING! Baldwin begins to walk around as Alex does the same both men sizing one another up to start the match. However never one for respect Ace pats his head reminding everyone that Alex is returning from a concussion. They finally go for it as they lock up in the center of the ring. But before they can even get into any sort of battle Baldwin pulls him into a knee. Alex doubles over as Baldwin gets the hooks in and then hits a Double Underhook Backbreaker. Baldwin goes for a pin right away 1…Kick Out. Too soon in this one for that as Ace gets back up to his feet making sure to take a few seconds to show off to the crowd. But just to taunt them more as Alex gets to his feet Ace immediately puts him back down with a big Dropkick. Baldwin walks around throwing out his arms and taking in the boos as he walks over bending over and talking trash to Alex. But out of no where he reaches up and goes for the pin! The crowd goes wild 1…Kick Out. Not even close as Ace kicks out and immediately gets up hitting a Chop Block that puts Alex back down. RILEY STEVENS: “Perhaps Alex came back too soon.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Or perhaps Ace is the best damn wrestler in GIW.” Ace gets up angry about his little attempt there as Alex bounces around on one leg in pain but with no remorse Baldwin grabs him and lifts him up for a Suplex. It lands and he keeps Alex wrapped up as he twists and gets back to his feet. With his leg hurt Alex can’t do anything to try to reverse the move instead he eats a second Suplex but yet again Ace holds on. Finally he gets Alex back up and this time for the third move he hits a Fisherman’s Suplex with the pin 1…2…Kick Out. Just barely kicks out as Ace gets up, getting back all of his confidence. Knowing he’s got Alex right where he wants him as he watches him, stalking his prey. Castellanos struggles to try to get to his feet unable to do so finally getting up but as he does Ace lifts him up onto his shoulders looking for the ‘T.F.C.’ but before he can hit it Alex rolls off his back and rushes off the ropes trying to turn things around but he runs right into a Spinning Spinebuster! The fans seem to lose all hope with that one as Ace drops down wrapping his hands up around Alex and locking in the ‘Smokin' Aces’ a Omoplata Crossface! Though Alex still wants to fight it he’s in the middle of the ring with no where to go so he’s forced to tap out. JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…“Amazing” Ace Baldwin!” Though we expected this the fans are still let down as the Ace streak continues as he gets up, looking down and laughing at Alex as he has the referee raise his hand. RILEY STEVENS: “You know I really want to doubt this guy but every week he comes out here and dominates. He might just have the best chance yet against Alioth.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “He WILL beat Alioth its not really a question.” Baldwin looks at the camera and then mouths “Who you got left Blake?” then he answers his own question as he backs up displaying the belt around his waist as we take a break. WINNER: ACE BALDWIN For the final time tonight we head to the back where Aspen Chaud is getting ready to leave. She comes outside of her locker room waiting with her bags, to get going but as she does back comes Ace Baldwin with a smirk on his face and a shirt in hand. ACE BALDWIN: “So that’s the guy you said I was a poor man’s version of? Judging by the way I embarrassed him out there…I think you might have misjudged.” Ace has a chuckle more then likely wanting to get back at her for that statement for awhile as she rolls her eyes. ASPEN CHAUD: “Good job you beat a guy that was coming off a concussion injury. Not the same as Alioth Starre now is it?” Aspen chuckles now as this time it’s Ace angrily staring back at her. ACE BALDWIN: “Whatever. Regardless next week you are on my team so I took the liberty to get you a shirt. But it wasn’t really your size so I made some of my own previsions.” Ace chuckles and hands it to her as she unfolds it to reveal what used to be a shirt but is now essentially a head band masquerading as a shirt. ASPEN CHAUD: “Oh that’s wonderful…” Ace nods his head and then stops her before she heads on her way. ACE BALDWIN: “Just so you know I didn’t draft you because I thought you could help. I picked you because I figured you’d make a good cheerleader…So bring your pom poms.” Baldwin grins and laughs at his own joke as he walks on his way as Aspen already shows her distain for the man that’ll be her Captain next week as we take the final break before the Main Event. QUOTE Main Event time! The fans are ready to go as this highly anticipated main event. "Blood & Fire (Out of the Ashes Mix)" by Type O Negative hits as AJ Fairchild is already in the ring. Out now comes Alioth Starre to a loud applause from the crowd loving the man that has for the past few months put down one challenge after another. RILEY STEVENS: “Ace had an impressive showing earlier but now Alioth gets the main event slot to prove himself.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “We all know that Ace was the main event, this is essentially just misplaced.” Alioth enters the ring looking across at AJ whom last week he called upon to impress him. Starre displays his belt for all to see and then hands it out as the referee looks around and calls for the bell. DING DING DING! The lock up was slow. It had taken forever for officials to force A.J. Fairchild into the arena, and then some time to the ring. It had taken Alioth to get out of the ring and physically shove A.J. Fairchild into the ring to allow the bell to signal their match to begin. When they locked up, Alioth threw A.J. back with little resistance. He took a step back and opened his arms to yell at Fairchild, wondering why the man refused to put in any effort into the match. Fairchild rambled things off, but Alioth shoved the air as if to throw it out and kneed his opponent in the gut. He took a fist full of Fairchild’s head and threw him into the canvas below. Alioth then took out several angry stomps on Fairchild’s chest and shoulders then forced him back up to push him toward the turn post where he set both arms under the ropes on each side and lay a heavy hand across Fairchild’s chest. Again he did this until the count of ten and his opponent’s chest began to show red. RILEY STEVENS: “This is why he’s the champion.” A.J. Fairchild continued to show little resistance and little interest in the match. Starre stared, annoyed and flabbergasted. He turned to the ropes to complain toward a crew member, but there wasn’t much that could be done once the match was in. He turned and returned his attention toward his opponent. He pulled A.J. close and sent him toward the ropes with a bitter Irish whip. Instead of a common physical attack, or maneuver that a running opponent would be transitioned into, Alioth grabbed Fairchild’s leg and pulled him down to apply the ‘Four Star Mary’, his Figure-Four Leglock. With the submission hold locked in and no resistance to it, Alioth kept it applied until his opponent tapped. “You’re a brick in the wall, Fairchild!” Starre yelled to him. JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…Alioth Starre!” Alioth turned over the maneuver and transitioned the figure-four leglock into the sharpshooter, the maneuver of the best there is, best there was, and best there ever will be. With that idea in mind as a message to send to any watching, he kept the submission hold locked in until finally his opponent had tapped out. Alioth threw himself off and slid out of the ring without acknowledging his victory, his opponent, or the crowd. Visibly upset, he left the arena as Fairchild was pulled up to his feet and helped to the back as well, until Alioth returned and forced the two referees holding Fairchild back toward the ring, chair in hand. He held the chair to point at Fairchild, yelling something inaudible to them and then slowly backed away, leaving the match on that note, but finally, and to his surprise, Fairchild charged forth. Smack! RILEY STEVENS: “Uncharacteristic actions from Alioth Starre in this one.” With a single chair shot to the face, Fairchild was down and Alioth let the folding chair drop to the aisle and left. He looked to the crowds and shrugged as they cheered the hit. RILEY STEVENS: “I think A.J. might not have impressed Alioth and this is what he gets. Regardless it stands as a message to the man whom he’ll face in two weeks in Ace Baldwin.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “If Ace had done that, it would have been a crime.” RILEY STEVENS: “We might just see that as next week it’ll be a HUGE…Falls Count Anywhere, Elimination, Tornado Six Man Tag Match! Team Starre vs. Team Baldwin…Tune in.” With AJ laid out and Alioth having sent the message loud and clear it seems as though Starre might have sent the louder message this week after being topped last week…The question is who will do so next week on the final show before ‘On Hallowed Ground II’. WINNER: ALIOTH STARRE
