GIW — Card

Revolt, 22 September 2011

22 September 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: THURSDAY NIGHT REVOLT LIVE! From The Grand Cayman Islands, Georgetown – September 22, 2011 BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! We are liver her on The Grand Cayman Islands in Georgetown where the ring is surrounded by fans whom have come to see the show here tonight. We head over to the side where Oliver and Riley are in the waits to get the first stop on the tour underway. RILEY STEVENS: “Welcome ladies and gentleman watching wherever you may be here tonight and what a show we have for you ladies and gentleman. As we continue the Alioth Starre era here in GIW we get some great action from top to bottom. First we start with Jack Waters taking on someone I think we are all happy to see back Tony Miranda whom will look to pave his way back to the top. Also on the card here tonight we have Koji Nakamora who has looked nothing but impressive of late taking on Alex Castellanos in singles action. You know back at ‘Blackout II’ Matt Kluge retired both the GIW Tag Team titles as well as the North Beach with him. So our new ‘GIW Television Champion’ Curtis Durrington will have much more pressure on him to raise that titles prestige he’ll look to do so against the debuting Doombringer whose name really does say it all about him…Finally in the main event…” Just as he says that he’s cut off as "Big Sh#t Poppin'" instrumental by T.I. hits and the crowd quickly unleashes boos as out from the back comes Ace Baldwin looking just as pleased as always strolling down towards the ring soaking in the fans boos. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “That ladies and gentleman is your new Number One Contender!” The fans seem not to share Oliver’s happy sentiment as he heads up into the ring looking out at them with a big smirk as he reaches out for a microphone walking back to the middle of the ring shaking his head. ACE BALDWIN: “Thank you! Thank you! Thank you! You know these past few weeks I have been ridiculed, I have been doubted, I have been insulted by people who claim me to be only a champion of another company you deemed unworthy…Well I have taken the liberty to prepare something for all of you, so let me pull it out now.” Ace reaches into his pockets pulling out a little sheet of paper which he unfolds several times, holding it out in front of him and coughing a few times before continuing. ACE BALDWIN: “This is a list of everyone who I have beaten here in GIW… Jack Waters…twice. Jak Hendricks…twice. Koji Nakamora…twice. Trayton Deluca. John Rawlings. Christian Rivers. Curtis Durrington. Nick Starks. And of course…Aspen Chaud.” By about the third name the fans are booing him relentlessly forcing him to raise his voice to talk over them as he finishes up throwing the page away with a grin. ACE BALDWIN: “All that in a span of just a few weeks. You know Blake tried keeping me away from the belt he really did but at the end of the day you have to listen to the fans and the fans wanted me as their Number One Contender. So now on Halloween Night I will face Alioth Starre for the title…And I will become your new GIW Undisputed Champion!” Ace only just barely finishes his sentence when "Blood & Fire (Out of the Ashes Mix)" by Type O Negative hits through the crowd and after a few minutes it’s Alioth Starre walking out with his title around his waist as he makes a b-line for the ring as Ace stands in seeming none to pleased by the interruption. Alioth steps in the ring already equipped with a microphone as he stands across from Ace. ALIOTH STARRE: “You know it’s funny you came out here with a little list like that. Forgetting that while you’ve won just a few matches here in GIW. I’ve tallied up more wins then anybody else has in the time I’ve been here. And while you make false claims about winning this belt, I actually have it…and I’ve had it for over a hundred days…” Alioth lowers the microphone the fans cheering his little jab at Ace who angrily stares back at him firing back. ACE BALDWIN: “Aren’t you funny little man? You know why you’ve been champion so long? It’s because you have been beating loser after loser. And the only reason you’ve held that belt so long is because you haven’t faced me!” Alioth nods his head agreeing with the statement however not seeming to phased by it as Ace soaks in the boos that his words inevitably bring. ALIOTH STARRE: “You know while I am not a belieber that you can win this match. I do have an idea for you, run down to the store and get yourself a little replica belt…and then on Halloween Night when I beat you…at least you can dress up like me…a real champion.” This sends the fans into a bunch of cheers loving that big slam as Baldwin angrily throws down the microphone walking towards him as a fight seems ready to break out but “Demon Eyes” by The Answer hits and the two stare up at the ramp as Blake Beckett walks out in a suit and a smile. BLAKE BECKETT: “You know next week we head to Kingston, Jamaica and I am here to announce that on that card we will have both of you in action. It will be Ace Baldwin going one on one with Aspen Chaud. Then you the champion Alioth Starre will go one on one with Nick Starks. But you fans could care less about what happens next week, so as for tonight we have a great main event as the two of you are on opposite sides in a tag match. And to make sure that things go according to plan I am adding a little special bonus for the winner. You see because the winner gets to choose their opponents match stipulation next week. Now can you both exit the ring so that we can get this show on the road.” Alioth seems subtle with the announcement rolling out of the ring as Ace glares up at Blake as well as Alioth showing his distain for the two men as they head to the back and we take a quick break. QUOTE "World Warrior" by RedRapper hits and Tony Miranda was on his way to the ring for the first match of the night. The fans were elated to see him back, after seemingly wrestler his last match earlier in the year. Though he was focused he couldn’t help but break a smile, after his long awaited return. RILEY STEVENS: “You know I think we all missed this man, Tony Miranda one of the biggest legends in GIW history has returned to the ring.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Woopty doo!” When we turn over to the ring already waiting for him is Jack Waters waiting with his three girls surrounding him in the ring, trash talking Tony as he makes his entrance. The referee instructed the girls to leave the ring just as Tony entered and then the bell was rung. DING DING DING! The match began with Jack trying a maneuver on his opponent, but it Tony countered effortlessly. Jack charged his opponent and because caught in a roundhouse kick. Tony Miranda hoisted his opponent onto his shoulders, back to shoulder and then slammed him down into the canvas. Jack turned over in pain after the inverted Samoan Drop looking to be in a world of hurts in this match as he gets to his feet taking a huge swing at Tony looking for some sort of miracle however looking agile and totally healed Tony ducks the shot then catches him with a big kick to the gut that doubles Waters over. Miranda heads off the ropes and on the rebound crushes him with a huge ‘Third Strike’ his almost patented Running Leg Lariat that nearly pulls his head off. The fans go wild as Tony gets to his feet, the win probably his already but perhaps wanting to prove something to himself he waits as we cut to Jack’s girls on the outside whom are screaming at him to get up. Sadly for him he does just that still dazed from the shot and he walks right into the ‘Second Impact’ a Running Lygerbomb that spells the end as Tony makes the cover 1…2…3! JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…TONY MIRANDA!” The fans erupt into cheers as Tony gets to his feet looking fresh and really not showing much signs of rust as his arm is lifted into the air for the victory. RILEY STEVENS: “Well Tony Miranda is back and perhaps better then ever. You know he’s been a constant top tier guy who’s always been on the cusp of winning that Undisputed title....perhaps it will be sooner rather then later.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I really doubt that.” Tony slides out of the ring high fiving a few fans on the outside as Angela, Angelica, and Angelina jump into the ring around Waters trying to help him up after a poor week on his part. WINNER: TONY MIRANDA As we head to the backstage area we find Aspen Chaud and Alex Castellanos in their own little makeshift locker room area with Alex just finishing tying up his boots. ASPEN CHAUD: “You know I swear wherever I go any more I feel like he is right there watching me. He’s probably around here right now just staring at me. I know we messed him up with those kicks but injuries happen all the time how can he blame it on me? Besides you were just as much to blame.” Alex chuckles at the statement, nodding his head as he finishes tying one boot’s shoe laces then starting the other. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “Something tells me he enjoys staring at you more then he does me. But don’t worry about it; I got us the room with a working lock. The trade off is we don’t get a television but at least you’ll know your little lover won’t be watching you.” Castellanos says this in a joking tone as Aspen reaches over punching him on the arm as he stretches out his arms. ASPEN CHAUD: “It’s not funny it is actually pretty creepy. But go out there and win for me, I’ll be back here waiting for you to tell me how you did.” Aspen smiles as he opens up the door to their locker room to head on out. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “I’ll be back in say…a minute.” Alex winks and heads on out closing the door behind him as Aspen gets up looking around then locking the door behind her as clearly Starks is starting to get to her as we take a break. QUOTE When we go back to the ring for the second time tonight we have Alex Castellanos already in the ring ready to go as “Voice of the Damned” by Terror hits through the place and out from the back comes Koji Nakamora getting a rather positive response from the crowd here tonight. RILEY STEVENS: “You know Koji lost two times in a row but both times you could argue that Ace Baldwin stole the matches from him. Frankly I have been nothing but impressed with him.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “He lost! That’s all that matters.” Koji enters the ring and looks across at Alex, nodding his head as the referee stands in between the two then calls for the ball getting this thing started quickly. DING DING DING! These fans are excited for this one as Koji Nakamora and Alex Castellanos head to the middle shaking hands as they circle around the ring together. Finally the two go in for a lock up and it’s basically a stalemate as both men sort of go back and forth. However it’s Koji that ducks under and ends up behind him with his arm wrapped back. Koji uses this to try to whip him off the ropes but he reverses it and throws Nakamora instead. Koji hits the ropes and rather then slow down he picks up steam. He ducks the clothesline and having gained enough speed runs fast enough that he ends up hitting a Bulldog on Alex before he can spin around. Knowing better then to go for a pin, he waits a second then bounces off the ropes and as Alex starts to get up he puts him down a Running Shining Wizard now he takes a chance with a pin, 1…Kick Out! He hops up as does Alex, Castellanos rushes at him unfortunately he does so right into a Tornado DDT! The fans are going wild for Koji as Alex hops up to his feet but suddenly seems completely out of it as Koji takes a step back as Castellanos seems in his own world his head hitting hard. RILEY STEVENS: “I’ve seen someone with a concussion and that’s what they look like.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Get on him Koji!” Nakamora looks at the referee almost for him to stop it but when it’s clear he won’t and still wanting the win at the end of the day Koji rolls him up into a pin attempt which Alex is in no shape to kick out of 1…2…3! JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…KOJI NAKAMORA!” The fans seem legitimately worried as Koji calls to the back for help as out comes a few medics along with Dr. House to check on Alex. House pats Koji on the back and tells him he can go as House starts to take care of him, we take a short break from here. WINNER: KOJI NAKAMORA As we return still in the ring is Alex Castellanos getting attended to by Dr. House who shines a light in his eyes. Suddenly the crowd starts to go insane as out from the back comes an angry Nick Starks heading straight towards the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “What in the world is Nick Starks doing out here!” Heading up into the ring one of the medics gets up asking him to stop and Starks responds with a big right hook that knocks him out of the ring. Next comes the other one trying to stop him but he gets a huge kick to the stomach then tossed from the ring like a rag doll. Dr. House gets up to his feet, holding out his arms for him to stop but Nick throws him to the side lifting up Alex and screaming out “You will learn your lesson!” loud enough for everyone to hear as he kicks him in the face as Alex is unable to defend himself. RILEY STEVENS: “This is just wrong…” The fans boo as he glares down at him starting to head towards the ropes to leave but just as he goes to step out he turns around heading back into the ring pulling Alex up to his feet and just as security heads on out he does the unthinkable nailing him with a ‘Seeing Starks’ a Reverse STO, driving Alex face first into the middle turnbuckle! RILEY STEVENS: “…” Out comes Security just as he already heads out of the ring grabbing him and pulling him towards the back as the fans boo him the entire time as in the ring lord knows what has happened to Alex as Dr. House returns to his side trying to see the damage that has been done. ==== Back to the back we go as Curtis Durrington is getting ready for his match with his title belt sitting on a table next to him while he stretches out as a rather beat up Jack Waters walks up to him with his girls behind him staring at him. JACK WATERS: “Listen man to my knowledge you don’t have a challenger for that title of yours and frankly I want nothing more then to take it from you. So why don’t you and I ask somebody and we’ll make it happen.” Curtis pulls himself to his feet looking across at Waters then back at his girls smiling a bit. CURTIS DURRINGTON: "Not to be disrespectful or anything but after your performance earlier tonight against Tony I am not sure you’d be first in line for the title. But I am willing to defend against all comers…" Before he can finish just hitting the back from his match is Koji Nakamora not even breaking a sweat he stops between the two looking over at them then at the belt. KOJI NAKAMORA: “Anybody huh? You know I wouldn’t mind getting a shot of my own. And you know I just heard that the winners of the three singles matches this week are fighting next week so maybe we’ll see each other sooner rather then later.” Koji stares down at the belt and Curtis nods his head hoisting up the belt onto his shoulder as Koji walks off leaving Waters to glare at him then back at Curtis before angrily storming off as the segment ends. QUOTE Spawning by the entrance was Doombringer as “So Cold” by Breaking Benjamin echoes through out the ringside area. He stepped down toward the ring in his debut bout for Galveston Island Wrestling. Into the ring with him, he may have looked like he wore a law suit well, but he knew he had to work hard for his first bout against Curtis Durrington. He waited like a cracked actor, but as he entered already in the ring was Curtis holding his GIW Television Championship. RILEY STEVENS: “Now that right there is an intimidating man…” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “You got that right.” Doombringer enters the ring staring across at Durrington who is either not afraid or at least hiding it well. He enters the ring and looks across, no emotion seen due to the mask as the bell is rung. DING DING DING! Doombringer scooped his opponent with an atomic drop, situated into a reverse DDT and a failed pin that only reached until the count of two. He climbed down to his opponent’s level and pulled him into a physical sleeper hold. The referee moved to bother Curtis with all of his senseless questions about when Curtis would give up, or tap out, or other inane questions the referees would usually pester the worker’s with when they were in such painful or mentally demanding predicaments as this. Doombringer wasn’t going to let Curtis tap to such a maneuver, no, he spun his opponent around and then up for the ‘Tainted Soul’ a Elevated Powerbomb and dropped him down hard onto the canvas for a full three count and taking the first victory in his first match within Galveston Island Wrestling. JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…DOOMBRINGER!” Doombringer looks down at his opponent almost as if to let it soak in for the fans whom look at one another shocked at how dominating he just looked against the Television Champion. RILEY STEVENS: “You know sometimes people that look the way he does, are putting on a little show. I think he might be able to back it up in the ring as he just crushed our Television Champion.” Having sent his message loud and clear he exits the ring with the fans still in a bit of shock due to it all. RILEY STEVENS: “You know our Television Champion did not look as he had in past few weeks…But as we learned earlier it will be the three match winners in a triple threat next week…So that means it’ll be Tony Miranda, Koji Nakamora, and Doombringer in action next week…that one should be huge.” Heading to the back we all now know the name of Doombringer as his message has been sent loud and clear, he’s a force to be reckoned with here in GIW. WINNER: DOOMBRINGER The final match is nearly ready and we head back to the back again where Aspen Chaud is just heading out of her room having been locked in there awhile just now exiting in her ring gear walking over to a staff member. ASPEN CHAUD: “Have you seen Alex?” Just then we hear some loud commotion as Aspen goes right towards the source of the noises as we hear the sounds of Alex letting out groans of agony as he lays in the stretcher he was in earlier. ASPEN CHAUD: “Alex!?” She heads over towards him but is pushed away by the paramedics; she ends up by Dr. House who she turns to in fear. ASPEN CHAUD: “What happened to him?” Aspen looks over at him; he’s in no shape to respond. DR. HOUSE: “Starks attacked him after his match…He has a severe concussion hopefully nothing more serious then that but I wouldn’t doubt it. They didn’t have an ambulance ready so they just now got here.” House says this honestly but it only stands to freak her out even more as we take a commercial break with the Main Event now seeming at stake. QUOTE It is now time for the main event of the evening as the fans here in Georgetown on The Grand Cayman Islands are ready to go for this big one. We start with "Upload Complete" by Last Chance to Reason echoing through the place as the fans begin to boo as out from the back comes Nick Starks wearing his mask as he heads towards the ring, with a rather determined look to him. RILEY STEVENS: “You know this man is basically obsessed with getting revenge on Aspen Chaud and earlier tonight he took out some frustrations on Alex Castellanos and I think he should have been fired for his actions.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Boo hoo, it’s called sending a message…Hopefully Aspen heard it and doesn’t come tonight.” As he enters the ring and heads towards his corner, "Big Sh#t Poppin'" instrumental by T.I. now hits and the fans just extend their boos longer as out comes the pretty boy himself and the Number One Contender, Ace Baldwin. Heading out he looks confident as always, not seeming phased at all for this match. RILEY STEVENS: “You know I think we are all wondering if this man can be stopped after how dominant he’s looked of late.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Let me answer that for you…no he can’t.” Ace heads into the ring walking across the ring where Starks is waiting both men look at one another, seeming to be on the same page if only because of their opponents. After a dull moment, "Bullseye" by Aly and AJ hits and the crowd turn their frowns upside down as out comes Aspen Chaud looking angry and focused heading down towards the ring stopping at the end of the ramp waiting. RILEY STEVENS: “Aspen can’t be happy after what she just found out about Alex, knowing it was Starks that took him out.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “How can anyone take her seriously when she’s mad? Haha.” She waits as "Blood & Fire (Out of the Ashes Mix)" by Type O Negative hits and the crowd cheers only continue as Alioth Starre walks out heading down towards the ring taking his spot next to Aspen with the title, they are not the most intimidating team just be looks as they both head into the ring together as Ace and Starks glare back. RILEY STEVENS: “Alioth has been looking to get a piece of Ace for awhile, here’s his chance.” They all are in the ring and the referee is ready to put these fans wait to an end as he rings the bell. DING DING DING! Unfortunately we don’t get to see either match up we were hoping for as an angry Aspen Chaud enters the ring, willingly by Alioth as Ace jumps in on the other side…only angering Starks who wants to get his hands on her. The two start to circle one another as Aspen runs in for a lock up and Baldwin throws her to the mat, as she rolls to her feet as Ace lets out a loud chuckle at her attempt to try to over power him. Rushing at him again Baldwin continues to toy with her, throwing her to the side with ease. The fans boo him as he holds out his hands telling her to stop, as he drops down to his knees egging her on. She walks over to him standing in front of him with an angry look on her face already as he offers her a free shot. Aspen pretends to amp up her first for a shot then jumps up and Dropkicks his chest! The fans go wild as she hops on top of him 1…Kick Out! The fans angrily let out a big sigh of anger as Ace hops up holding his gut a bit breathing in heavily as he yells something at her as she gets back into her fighting stance. RILEY STEVENS: “Can’t say he didn’t have that coming.” Ace now charges at Aspen tackling her back and into the corner. He takes a step back taking a big swing at her, but she luckily ducks it and jumps up kicking him away. Angered by what she did earlier he rushes back at her but she jumps out of the way. Already getting fired up she kicks him in the back now sending him back into the corner. Rushing off the ropes Baldwin turns around but it is a bit too late as Aspen jumps into her ‘Tush Push’ a Turnbuckle Flying Butt Shot! The fans go wild knowing what this leads to as she heads to the top rope, looking over at Starks to make sure he doesn’t try anything as she jumps off for the ‘AirBarbie’ a Flying Crossbody it lands and she ends up on top for a pin 1…2…Kick Out! Both competitors get up to their feet, Ace stunned as Aspen whips him into her corner running and tagging in Alioth. RILEY STEVENS: “Here’s a little a preview for the title match.” In comes Alioth looking focused as Aspen holds Ace in the corner long enough for Starre to come up kicking him in the gut and pulling him back to the center of the ring where he nails him with a Snap DDT. Turning around instead of going for the pin he looks to be setting up for an Ankle Lock but at the last second Baldwin kicks away and crawls towards his corner jumping up and tagging in Starks. Ace rolls out of the ring in pain as Nick enters looking across at Alioth the two slowly circling one another. Finally they lock up and to one’s surprise Nick has the advantage. But quickly he transitions it into a ‘Rip Cord Roaring Elbow’ as he uses his Wrist–Lock transitioned into a Discus Elbow that stun Alioth, with him reeling he heads off the ropes and by the time Alioth is able to get his wits about him he’s already taking a huge Running Mafia Kick! The fans are shocked a bit as he gets the advantage and takes the pin 1…Kick Out. Still not enough to put away the champion. RILEY STEVENS: “Impressive by Starks thus far.” Nick gets up staring over at Aspen with anger in his eyes as he lifts Alioth to his feet but to his surprise Alioth out of no where hits a Fisherman Suplex with a pin, 1…2…Kick Out. Shocked by the move both men hop up and Alioth throws a Superkick that misses as Nick ducks out of the way. Using Alioth being off balance he jumps up going for a Springboard Thrust Kick but yet again its Starre this time that ducks it. Both men turn around however Starks true colors show through as he rakes the eyes of Alioth setting up a big ‘Seeing Starks’ a Reverse STO, driving Alioth into the turnbuckle! The fans angrily boo as Nick pulls him to the middle of the ring 1…Kick Out. Still not enough. Rather clearly Ace Baldwin can be heard screaming “Hurry up loser just finish him off.” as Nick gets up staring over at him angrily as Ace has gotten up to the ring apron and is now trashing his own partner. Starks walks over to his corner, getting in his face as Ace continues to talk. RILEY STEVENS: “Uh oh, we all thought these two’s egos might clash.” The two exchanges words back and forth before Ace reaches over and tags himself in. Nick stands there angrily as Baldwin enters the ring, pushing him towards the ropes. Aspen enters the ring, thinking that she might need to be there to even the odds. Starks shakes his head and rolls out of the ring as Ace screams out “This is how a star gets the job done!” but as he turns around he walks right into a ‘Queen's Wrath II’ a Heel Kick from Aspen that stuns him followed right away by a ‘Nervous System's Down’ a Mat Backbreaker from Alioth that puts him down for good as Aspen watches to make sure Starks isn’t coming back as he walks up the ramp not even looking back 1…2…3-Kick Out! Ace kicks out but it’s late as the referee calls for the bell. JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winners…ASPEN CHAUD and ALIOTH STARRE!” The fans go wild as Aspen turns around high fiving Alioth in the ring as Starks is shown not even looking back as he heads backstage as Aspen and Alioth roll out of the ring together. RILEY STEVENS: “You know Nick Starks was looking impressive in this one but really you can’t expect Ace Baldwin to be much of a team player and for that he just got pinned by the man he claimed he could beat.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “His partner ditched him, Aspen and Alioth teamed up on him, and then the referee didn’t see he kicked out…It was a screw job!” Aspen high fives the fans at ring side, as in the ring Ace sits up looking at the referee trying to make a plea that he got his shoulder up before the count. RILEY STEVENS: “Frankly Ace had this coming tonight. And now having been pinned by the man he claims to be better then, can he bounce back? We’ll see next week as we get Ace Baldwin versus Aspen Chaud and Nick Starks versus Alioth Starre…And Alioth gets to pick Ace’s match stipulation. Tune in next week as we go to Jamaica.” Riley finishes off the show as we end with Alioth Starre and Aspen Chaud celebrating the win as Ace Baldwin is forced to swallow his pride as the show closes. WINNERS: ALIOTH STARRE & ASPEN CHAUD
