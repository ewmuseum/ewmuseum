GIW — Card

Revolt, 14 October 2010

14 October 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: Thursday Night Revolt! LIVE OCTOBER 14th, FROM HARBOR VIEW INN IN GALVESTON, TEXAS BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! It’s time for another highly anticipated edition of the new Thursday Night Revolt the show that has been slowly taking over Thursday nights around here and as usual the place is packed with the same loyal fans as usual as we head to the announce table with Oliver and Riley the voices of Revolt. RILEY STEVENS: “Well we are back here in the lovely Harbor View Inn for a big night for Revolt. We start the night as usual with the Open Battle Royal followed up by a match featuring Joshua Acquin coming off a tough loss against Thor Vegard the man who killed several guys in the Open Battle Royal last week, plus some other guy.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I don’t even want this one but Jake Kennedy our ION Television Champion defends the belt against Chicken. Please lord, don’t let this happen...” RILEY STEVENS: “While you may hate that one everyone is going to love this HUGE main event when we see the two victors of last week’s match and the two that made us proud in their attack of Wake Walker; Kacie Church and Eric Sparrow go one on one. Stay tuned for this huge night as we continue on to our next PPV ‘On Hallowed Ground’.” The two have big smiles that Oliver can’t hold for long before scowling as we head to a quick break between action. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = As we return to Revolt from a commercial break for a local pizza shop, we see Amanda Rowland standing in the middle of the ring with a microphone in her left hand; she looks to the man that is standing to the right of her in the ring. This man is hell ugly wearing a straw hat, roughly scruffy beard, missing many teeth and has one overall strap strung poorly over his red and blue plaid shirt. AMANDA ROWLAND: “As you all know, it is time for our Open Battle Royale to anyone in the back. Well this week I have decided to give Tommy Toothless a shot here in Revolt, but I decided I’d change things up a little bit. Instead of a Battle Royale style match, let’s throw him in the ring with newcomer DareDevil and see how he does! I’ll be watching this match closely, so let’s get it on! The crowd lets out a cheer as Psycho by System of a Down hits, those cheers slowly die down as DareDevil comes out of the entrance curtain. This newcomer looks set to go as he has a cocky smirk on his face as he slides under the ring ropes as his music cuts out. He looks up and down Tommy Toothless and begins to laugh to himself while Tommy is taking this match very serious. The referee makes sure both men are ready to go as he calls for the ring bell. DING! DING! DING! DareDevil walks to the center of the ring and sticks out his hand, showing some respect for Tommy Toothless, and Tommy not being too smart steps to the center and reaches out and shakes DareDevils hand. DareDevil twists the hand of Tommy quickly jumps up and hits him with a perfect Dropkick to the jaw. DareDevil jumps on top and begins to hit Tommy with many Mounted Punches. The referee finally gets DareDevil off of Toothless but the beating just doesn’t stop there. DareDevil picks up Tommy and hits him with a quick Snap DDT, hurting the neck of Tommy severely. Dare picks up Tommy, drives his head into his armpit, jumps into the air and hits Tommy with a textbook 720 DDT! DareDevil then hooks the leg as the referee begins to count 1....2.... DareDevil, not quite done with Tommy gets off him as the referee was just about to hit the canvas for a three count. With Tommy laying lifeless in the middle of the ring, DareDevil points to the top turnbuckle, runs over and climbs it. He then laughs and points at poor Tommy Toothless as he jumps into the air and before landing on Tommy with a thud executes his other finishing move a Corkscrew Senton which clearly ends the night for Tommy Toothless. Again, DareDevil goes for a cover. 1....2....3! RILEY STEVENS: “Sweet Jesus, DareDevil just took Tommy Toothless by a storm!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Well, what did you think was going to happen? We had some local hick take on a trained professional, I’m not impressed.” RILEY STEVENS:: “Of course you’re not! Don’t go anywhere folks we have a Triple Threat Match coming up between Justinn Sane, Joshua Acquin and Thor Vegard! Stayed tuned, Revolt returns shortly! We fade into a commercial break as Amanda Rowland is raising the hand of DareDevil. WINNER: DAREDEVIL = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = We head to the backstage area where Kacie Church is standing by looking around for something in the backstage area when Eric Sparrow makes his presence felt by walking right up to her. ERIC SPARROW: “You know it was kind of cool that we tied because though I didn’t need it. It was nice to have a little valet with me when I attacked him.” Kacie seems about to angrily respond but he cuts her off right before she can say anything. ERIC SPARROW: “Don’t bother. You see because you people have it all around here. You think I give a crap about this company. You really think that I’m on your side when in reality the only thing I want is what Wake Walker has. And none of you are going to keep me from getting it. Later tonight, when I beat you I’ll be on my way to forcing Walker to defend against me, otherwise what happened to him on Tuesday will be a lot worse.” Sparrow smirks and then looks her over and then walks away from the scene as Kacie watches him go rolling her eyes as the segment ends. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = Instead of getting any huge entrances we head right into the ring where our second match of the night is about to be set up. In the ring currently on one side is Thor Vegard and on the other is Justinn Sane. The crowd is a bit confused and out announces begin to explain why there is that confusion. RILEY STEVENS: “Well it seems as though Joshua Acquin, isn’t here tonight so I suppose he forfeits the match. Where do you think he is?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “He lost to Chicken…I’d assume hiding.” Back in the ring Justinn Sane looks up at the gigantic Thor Vegard with fear in his eyes as the referee calls for the bell. DING DING DING! The crowd watches as Justinn Sane looks up at Thor with huge eyes as he runs off the ropes and attempts a clothesline…nothing. The fans realize that he’s in trouble as he begins to give him a few kicks and punches to the legs hoping to take down the tall man. However all he does is annoy Thor who instills fear in all of his opponents. Finally Sane gets desperate and heads to the top rope and jumps off at Thor only to receive a huge Headbutt! The crowd continues to watch realizing that this Sane kid needs to just lay down so he doesn’t get hurt. However when he gets to his feet stumbling he walks right into an ‘Afterlife’ a Piledriver from Thor that officially ends him. He take the Piledriver right into a pin attempt 1…2…3. Over already. RILEY STEVENS: “Thanks for coming.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Grown men check their closets for Thor Vegard.” Thor gets up as the referee raises his hand in the air as the crowd continues to not boo him or cheer him, just watch in both fear and awe. However as he’s about to leave he suddenly has second thoughts and then turns around walks back over to him and with him already downed decides to nearly administer the Remi-Double Foot Stomp portion of his finisher however the lights go off and on and when they come back laying on Justinn’s chest is a letter. Thor reaches down grabbing it off of him quickly. RILEY STEVENS: “What is that?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Looks like a letter with the date “10-31-2010” on it. That’s the date of ‘On Hallowed Ground’.” RILEY STEVENS: “I sincerely hope that’s a love letter and not hate mail, because I’d rather be on this man’s good side.” Thor distracted by the letters walks out of the ring holding it in his hands as we cut back to Justinn Sane who the referee who can now get to him comes over to check on, he’s out cold. WINNER: THOR VEGARD = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = Again it’s time to head to the back where Amanda Rowland is sitting in her desk looking over some paperwork on her desk when the door opens and she looks up taking off her glasses as Alexii Ravynova walks over to her desk with a big smile. ALEXII RAVYNOVA: "Hey there partner." Almost immediately we can sense the bad blood between the two as Alexii stands in front of her desk as Amanda glares up at her not sharing the same fake enthusiasm. AMANDA ROWLAND: “What was that last week? You made a match so that our wrestlers could go and attack them? That’s not our way. If they want to fight they can come to us.” Alexii shakes her head not agreeing with Amanda at all, these girls couldn’t be more different if they tried. ALEXII RAVYNOVA: "You don’t win a war by standing back and waiting. We caught them off guard; I think it was a success." Alexii smirks showing little worries for what Amanda thinks as Rowland slowly sets down her pen. AMANDA ROWLAND: “I get that you have power around here now, but this is still my company. And we need to get on the same page sooner or later. However since you seem so willing to get involved with these fights, can you go make sure that Security is tight so none of them retaliate after last week’s events.” Alexii grins and nods though for her it’s very suspicious she’d do so without a fight as she walks out of the office as we cut back to Amanda who seems just as doubtful as the rest of us. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = “I Will Not Bow” by Breaking Benjamin begins to play. Out comes Jake Kennedy with his title belt around his waist. The crowd appears to approve of him. Regardless, he still kind of gives a really cocky strut as he walks down the ramp. Once he’s in the ring, he poses for the crowd and lifts the championship up in the air. He then gets on one corner, hands the referee the title belt and then waits for his opponent. A generic song with lots of clucking can be heard. The crowd turns towards the ramp and kind of just watch in agony of Chicken. Chicken skips down the ramp, with a basket in his hand and then begins to hand out a lot of eggs to some of the younger members of the crowd. Every time he hands an egg to someone, the egg hatches into a baby chicken. Chicken enters the ring and playfully puts his back to Kennedy and then turns around rather menacingly. Jake Kennedy shakes his head in embarrassment and the fight begins as soon as the bell sounds. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I know usually I’m the impartial one here but I sure as hell hope Chicken doesn’t win.” RILEY STEVENS: “What if he did win?.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “If he wins, then that just proves anything can happen. It even means Ralph Nader actually stands a chance of being elected president in 2012 .” Jake Kennedy runs at Chicken and tries to hit a clothesline. Chicken dodges it by ducking. Jake Kennedy bounces off the ropes and tries to hit another clothesline. Chicken, however, bounces off the ropes in the same direction as Jake Kennedy, causing both of them to be running in the same direction. Jake Kennedy confusingly turns around to wonder where Chicken could have went. Chicken hits a clothesline of his own and then runs to the outside of the ring. Chicken begins to dance outside. Jake Kennedy gets angry and exits the ring. Chicken enters the ring. Chicken does the same playful back turn and looks at Kennedy. Kennedy is offended by it and tries to enter the ring. Once he does, he runs after Chicken around the ring. Chicken jumps on top of one of the turnbuckles. Jake Kennedy tries to push him off but Chicken does a back flip and lands back into the center of the ring. Jake Kennedy tries to hit a running leg lariat but Chicken does the splits, dodging it. Jake Kennedy starts becoming angry. He runs after Chicken again. Chicken goes to the outside. Jake Kennedy at first, almost pulls his hair out but then a big smirk comes across his face which slightly confuses Chicken. The referee begins to count out Chicken. RILEY STEVENS: “See this? Jake Kennedy is smart. If Chicken wants to go ahead and be a chicken, he will be counted out.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Quite manipulative of him.” Jake Kennedy waits for Chicken to get back in and when he does, he begins his beat down. He kicks and punches Chicken many times, actually causing the crowd to cheer him to keep on going. He picks up Chicken and tries to hit a suplex but Chicken turns it into a small package! 1…2.. kick out! Chicken runs at Kennedy but only falls victim to a powerslam. Kennedy goes for a quick cover. 1…2…. Kick out! Kennedy tries to quickly lock in a chokehold but Chicken slips out of it and hits Kennedy with his foot. Kennedy backs away into a corner. Chicken runs at Kennedy. Jake dodges a running elbow by Chicken and then rolls him up! 1…2… Chicken counters it into a small package! 1…2… Kennedy counters it! 1…2… Chicken quickly does a jackknife cover! 1…2… Kennedy kicks out and then quickly places his body on top of Chicken. 1… kick out! Both men stand up and look at each other as the crowd claps and cheers the efforts of the two to win. Suddenly, Acquin’s music begins to play. Acquin starts running towards the ring. Chicken realizes that his chance is now or never. So he quickly rolls up jake Kennedy. 1…2…3! RILEY STEVENS: “Bret Hart just turned in his grave.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “He’s not dead.” RILEY STEVENS: “Exactly.” Chicken begins to jump up and down and celebrates. Acquin enters the ring and hits a back body drop. Acquin starts punching Chicken right on the face and even tries to rip off the costume … if it IS a costume… Eventually, Kennedy gets up. Acquin clotheslines him to the outside. Acquin sets his sights back on Chicken. However, Chicken quickly throws an egg at Acquin – blinding him. Chicken runs to the ring announcer, grabs his title and runs away to the back. Acquin can be seen with his fists clenched. He doesn’t seem too happy. Jake Kennedy just realizes what has transpired. He starts asking the referee, “The chicken won?! The damn chicken won?!” The referee shrugs, saying that Chicken won fair and square. Jake continues to argue the call not agreeing with it at all. RILEY STEVENS: “I'd never thought I'd see the day when Chicken actually put on a good match, and against Jake Kennedy of all people!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “You don't do much thinking then, do you?” [color=#8B008B]RILEY STEVENS: “Oh, shut u—“ Riley stops talking when he notices a gigantic figure walking towards the ring, with his eyes set on Jake Kennedy. RILEY STEVENS: “Who the heck is that?!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “That's The Shadow, One of GIW's newest talent. Just what we need, a big dumb goth giant.” The Shadow climbs up onto the apron and steps over the ring ropes, his eyes continuing to look only at Jake Kennedy. He watches him as Jake finally gets air into his lungs, and manages to stand up straight. He turns around, only to get completely wrecked by The Shadow with a violent Yakuza Kick, which sends Jake Kennedy to the floor in record time. RILEY STEVENS: “Oh god! What a kick! He just caved in Jake Kennedy's face!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I uh...I think I'm going to take back that "Big Dumb Goth Giant" comment.” The Shadow stands silently as he simply looks down at Jake Kennedy who lies motionless on the floor. He turns his head towards the stage to see "The New World Warrior" Tony Miranda walking down towards the ring as well. He then turns back towards Kennedy to continue his mindless attack. He lifts up Jake to his feet, then slams his fist deep into his gut with a powerful punch, then with his other hand, continues the attack with a violent punch to his jaw. The Shadow then begins to pound away at Kennedy with a number of punches, before grabbing his arm and irish whipping him towards the ropes. As he comes back, The Shadow lifts him up into the air and viciously slams him down with a Spinning Spinebuster. As The Shadow rises again, Tony slides into the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “And now The New World Warrior is here? Do you think he's here to stop The Shadow?” Tony looks at The Shadow, who simply nods, he lifts up Jake Kennedy, and whips him towards Tony. Without missing a beat, he jumps into the air and hits Kennedy with the Leg Lariat. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Nope.” Tony looks down at Jake Kennedy, who is clutching his head from the Leg Lariat. Rage quickly fills the eyes of The New World Warrior, and he quickly drops down and begins pounding away at Jake's face with punch after punch, and from his intense match with Chicken, the violent attacks of The Shadow, and Tony's Leg Lariat, Jake can barely defend himself. RILEY STEVENS: “I know these two don't like each other, but this is a bit too much.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Oh please, you've heard what Tony's said. He doesn't care about anything else except for ending Jake Kennedy's career! And this just proves that he will do anything to achieve that.” After a few extra punches Tony finally stops punching away at Jake and stands up. He looks at The Shadow once again. Tony does a cutthroat taunt, to which The Shadow nods. Shadow grabs Kennedy and drags him to the middle of the ring. He picks him up and stands Jake on his feet, who barely manages to stand. RILEY STEVENS: “What the hell are they going to do now?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “All I know is that whatever it is, its not going to be good for Jake.” Their questions are answered as Tony and Shadow run off the ropes, and as they come back, they hit Kennedy with their signature tag finisher, a Double Axe Bomber Clothesline...The Cross Bomber. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “HOLY SH**!!” RILEY STEVENS: “My god! I'm surprised that they didn't tear off Jake Kennedy's head off with that move!” Tony and Shadow back away from Jake as he collapses to the floor, twitching in extreme pain. Tony looks down at Kennedy with a look of twisted joy on his face. He lets out a small laugh while looking at Jake. The Shadow begins walking out of the ring and tells Tony to follow him. The New World Warrior looks at the damage he and Shadow did one last time before finally exiting the ring. WINNER: CHICKEN (New ION Television Champion) = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = The camera heads to the backstage area where the crowd sees the new face DareDevil walking through the backstage area. It doesn’t seem to hold much matter until; suddenly the lights flicker off for a few seconds and then come back on. DareDevil stares around him a bit confused but not thinking much of it. He is about to walk when he looks down at the ground then reaches down and picks something off the floor. He at first holds it up on the wrong side it’s a letter of some sort and when he holds it up we see the date “10-31-2010” as he then flips it around and reads it. He curiously opens it up and then holds open the letter reading it. He lowers it down seeming a bit intrigued while also, still very confused. Regardless he tucks it away and then walks away as the segment ends. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = Sum 41’s “Fatlip” begins to play. Eric Sparrow comes out to a mixed reaction but mostly a predominant cheering by the crowd. As a joke, he pretends to flap his wings. He smiles and walks down the ring, smiling. It looks like he’s happy with what he did on Evolve to Wake Walker alongside Kacie Church. Eric Sparrow gets on top of one of the turnbuckles and does a pose for the crowd. It seems like he’s starting to get on top of his game recently and perhaps is ready to step it up even further. "Impossible" By Anberlin blasts through the speakers. Kacie Church, with her belt on her shoulder makes her entrance. She puts her belt near one of the corners. The bell sounds for the match to begin. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “This should be a really good match.” RILEY STEVENS: “No joke. This is the type of match you’d see at a Pay Per View! Enjoy this while you can, folks!” Eric Sparrow quickly runs at Kacie Church and drop kicks her. Kacie falls to one of the corner of the turnbuckles. Eric Sparrow runs at his a running body slam on Kacie. Kacie gets up. Eric Sparrow hits Kacie across the face with a punch and Irish Whips her to the opposing turnbuckle. Kacie lifts her boot in an attempt to try and stop the early momentum of Eric Sparrow but Eric Sparrow stops just in time to not get hit by the big boot. He places Kacieh Church’s boot on the ground and he clotheslines her from a short distance. Kacie Church looks rather dizzy and begins to stumble around. Eric Sparrow hits a roundhouse kick to Kacie. Kacie falls to the floor. Eric Sparrow starts saying that this is over. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Sparrow is doing excelent tonight!” RILEY STEVENS: “What'd you expect? It's Eric Sparrow!” The crowd begins to cheer. Some are in awe and silence and how Kacie is being dominated. Eric Sparrow goes to the top rope. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Wow. Strangely, this is becoming a one sided match.” RILEY STEVENS: “Why is it even a surprise? Eric Sparrow has been on fire as of late and he wants to redeem himself for that loss against Wake Walker. Eric Sparrow means business now.” AIR SPARROW! Somehow, Eric Sparrow went from the turnbuckle to the second rope and then shifted his direction in Kacie’s way, hitting her with a vicious clothesline. Eric Sparrow goes for another one. Kacie Church gets out of the way but Sparrow rolls through, not hurting himself too much. Eric Sparrow hits a calf kick to Kacie, sending her right on the floor and setting her up possibly for some high risk maneuver. Eric Sparrow goes for a standing moonsault and hits it! He goes for the cover. 1…2… no dice! Eric Sparrow gets up and begins to choke out Kacie. Kacie fights out of it by punching Sparrow in the gut several times. She then bounces off the ropes but Sparrow catches her with a backbreaker. Sparrow follows it up with a brainbuster and finally, he goes for the chokehold and hits the snap DDT (Purple Haze). Eric Sparrow goes for the cover. 1…2…3! An impressive and almost one sided victory here! RILEY STEVENS: “And with that win, we know exactly who will lead Revolt to victory.” WINNER: ERIC SPARROW = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = Suddenly the crowd begins to murmur and the almost expected presence of EPW is out of no where Jack Prowler, Bret Battery, Jack Johnson, and Chris Armstrong run into the ring tackling Sparrow to the mat while one of the others attacks the downed Kacie Church. The crowd continues to boo until yet again the backstage area begins to empty as out first is Aspen Chaud with Tony Miranda who however brings The Shadow with him out towards the ring. The crowd goes insane as they slide in the ring as Aspen kicks Battery in the back sending him out of the ring while Armstrong and Prowler then bail as well. However Johnson doesn’t get the memo at all. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Kill that guy!” The crowd cheers as Jack Johnson turns and walks right towards Aspen when he tries to turn around he realizes the predicament he’s in. He turns back to Aspen and then tries to escape only to be punched in the face and bounced into a ‘Lightning Buster’ a Crash Thunder Buster from Tony! We cut to Armstrong, Prowler, and Battery backing up the ramp with Prowler mouthing “He sucks anyways” to his partners as out from the back with a mic, is Amanda Rowland opposite side of the EPW guys. AMANDA ROWLAND: “Weren’t expecting such unity were you? You see that’s what GIW is all about. You think you hurt us by taking our guys Blake? You made us stronger. And since this fighting non-sense has to end, this is what’s going to happen. You want to kill us? I know you and David both want to. How about this then. At ‘On Hallowed Ground’ our Halloween Day Supershow, we have a match. If we win, EPW will rejoin with GIW! And if we lose, then GIW will…disband.” The crowd seems a bit uneasy wondering just if they can win at the PPV as Amanda then looks at her wrestlers in the ring who seem equally stunned about her offer. AMANDA ROWLAND: “And what I’m thinking is this time we go big. And we have a match where whoever has the last member or members remaining wins! So that’s why if you accept it’ll be four of my stars versus your four best. And if you’re wondering who my team will be. They are all in the ring Eric Sparrow, Kacie Church, Aspen Chaud, and Tony Miranda!” The crowd goes insane as Aspen looks around at her partners Miranda who nods his head ready to go while Church as well seems to be pretty happy with it. However despite saving him Eric Sparrow still seems to be the rotten egg of the bunch, glaring at them unhappily as he has gotten to his feet. The show ends with the four standing in the ring looking at one another unsure what to think of what they’ve just been tossed into.
