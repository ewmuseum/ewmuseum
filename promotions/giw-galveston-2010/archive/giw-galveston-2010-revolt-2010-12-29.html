GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: REVOLT LIVE FROM GALVESTON’S NORTH BEACH ON DECEMBER 29th, 2010 = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! The GIW fans are PACKED all around the GIW ring set up on the North Beach Pier, placed over the icy December Gulf waters. Christmas is in the air, the traditional fireworks light up the sky crimson and green. THE GIW CHRISTMAS SPECIAL IS HERE! OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Merry fucking Christmas to you all!” RILEY STEVENS: “Welcome to the GIW Christmas Spectacle, brought to you by Mario’s Bar and Grill and also the University of Texas Medical Branch at Galveston: what you do today will shape your tomorrow!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Merry fucking Christmas to our sponsors!” RILEY STEVENS: “Tonight is a big one for GIW! We are on the home stretch to Genesis, and the tension can be felt all the way in Houston! Tonight, The Perfect Combination try to steal the tag champion’s title belts in a match that just might steal the show! Can Damon Ramon and Ombio maintain at the top of the tag division?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “If they do then I bet they will have a merry fucking Christmas.” RILEY STEVENS: “Indeed they would Ollie, but The Perfect Combo is on a hot streak, and I don’t know if the tag champs can keep up with their speed and power. They truly might be the Perfect Combination! In the match after that, you have to be a Perfect Combination to win the match! It’s a nine man Christmas Scramble match, where everyone will be paired up with an opponent. We could have enemies paired together; we could have legends paired together. ANYTHING can and will happen in this match." OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I’d just like to take this time now to wish the beautiful Aspen Chaud, who is in the match this wonderful evening, a merry fucking Christmas.” RILEY STEVENS: “And the main event for tonight pits two former allies against each other, with their common enemy as the special guest referee. Last week right here on Revolt, Aspen Chaud had Scott Reave beat before that evil snake Chris Armstrong screwed it all up. Now, Reave and Armstrong square off for a shot at Aspen in the main event of Genesis. Which one of them do you think will pick up the victory Oliver?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Merry fucking Christmas Riley.” RILEY STEVENS: “… and a merry Christmas to you as well partner!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Say it.” RILEY STEVENS: “Say… what?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Fuck.” RILEY STEVENS: “Well, I don’t want to offend anyone… why?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Because if you don’t, I’ll fucking kill you.” RILEY STEVENS: “Uh…” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Remember, if you don’t, I’ll fucking kill you.” RILEY STEVENS: “Uh… this is highly irrational Oliver…” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “F-U-C-K-I-N-G KILL YOU!” RILEY STEVENS: “Merry fucking Christmas.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “It’s time for the match to start.” The room suddenly went black, the same noises that were heard in Requiem were heard again, the same screams, the same laugh. Then when the lights were turned on again there was a man in the middle of the ring. It had black hair, glasses and was wearing a lab coat. THE PSYCHIATRIST: “Hi, you don't know who I am, and to be honest you don't even need to know, what you should know, however, is that I'm a psychiatrist and I'm here in name of a colleague and friend of mine, that you also don't need to know who he is right now. He asked me to come here and tell you that in ‘Genesis’ he will be there. There to analyze your mind, to uncover who you really are, and in the end, to defeat you. Let me tell you something, I'm fairly sane, but he isn't... He was put in the serial killers area, and it seems that the patients are affecting him. Not that he was a calm person when he first entered that room, but he became an even scarier person over the time. Well, that's all... for now; I'll be around in case you want to ask anything... RILEY STEVENS: “I guess we are going to get a new star on the show...” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “And it seems is going to be another fucking freak!” We take a quick commercial break after that indeed random appearance by the haunting individual. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = As we return from break the opening match has already begun and the match begins with A.J. Lightning and Jacoby Jackson locking up in the center of the ring. Since only two people entered this match, it will be fought like a traditional singles match. Jacoby is overpowered by Lightning from the get-go; he is slammed into the ring post with brute force shoulder first. He wails in agony as the rest of the members of High Voltage laugh at him outside the ring. A.J. picks Jacoby up and sets him up for a power bomb early on in the match, but Jacoby squirms out under his legs and then executes a leg sweep to tear A.J. down. RILEY STEVENS: “When you are the smaller guy, you have to use moves like that to get on top.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “That’s the story of Riley’s sex life.” Jacoby Jackson is now on offense. He locks in a tight headlock that A.J. quickly maneuvers out of. A.J. picks Jacoby up and slams to the ground with a sidewalk slam! The members of High Voltage outside are screaming for A.J. to go for the pin fall, but he ignores them. A.J. Irish whips Jacoby against the ring ropes, setting him up for the double A spine buster he calls the “Perfect Shock”, but just before he arrives at A.J. to get slammed to the mat, Jacoby connects with a tornado DDT out of nowhere! A.J. quickly gets up only to be hit by one of Jacoby’s favorite moves, the one handed bulldog! A.J. jumps quickly up from that as well. RILEY STEVENS: “A.J. doesn’t like to be on the ground. Can Jacoby maintain control?” Jacoby waits for the perfect time and then closes in for the Jacoby Jolt, but he is reversed into a power-slam pin combo! The ref closes in for the count. 1…2…KICKOUT BY JACOBY! OLIVER THIBODAUX: “This kid has got moxie, I dig it.” A.J. seems frustrated with the count, as do the other members of High Voltage waiting by the ring. Justin Volt gets up on the ring and begins to argue with the referee while the girls sneak around the other side and slip something into A.J.’s hand. A.J. hides the object in the tape on his wrists. A.J. goes back on offense, grabbing Jacoby up by his tights and slinging him into the corner. A.J. lays down punch after punch to Jacoby’s head and body. He turns around to gloat to the fans while Jacoby falls down in a heap in the center of the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “The little guy always goes down first.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Hehe…” A.J. slowly walks over to Jacoby and begins to stomp directly on his head. A.J. puts Jacoby in position for a suplex and then points to Justin Volt outside the ring. Volt jumps up on the ring, calling for the referee. The referee goes over to Volt threatening to kick him out. A.J. relinquishes the headlock and lets Jacoby fall back down to the mat. From his wrist tape he pulls out a razor blade. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Oh shit, this could get messy.” A.J. goes down to a knee and holds Jacoby’s head in one hand and the razor blade in another. He mimes the cutting to the crowd, and just when he goes in for the swipe, the referee turns around and tackles him, stealing the blade at the same time. RILEY STEVENS: “How on Earth does he not get disqualified by trying to cut someone with a blade?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “It happens all the time in wrestling.” A.J. goes to argue with the referee, Jacoby is stirring. The other members of HV are yelling at the referee too, no one seems to notice Jacoby Jackson as he gains momentum by bouncing off the ropes. Jacoby throws himself over A.J. Lightning, the referee, and the ropes only to land on the members of High Voltage in one swoop. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Whoa.” A.J. and the referee stand there, amazed at what just happened. Jacoby slowly gets back up and slides back in the ring to stare down A.J. Lightning. A.J. goes for a quick left hook but Jacoby dodges it, he runs and gets momentum off the ropes yet again and connects with a low dropkick to the knee of A.J. Lightning, sending him down to the mat on his knees. Jacoby swings his knee to the temple of Lightning, hitting with brute force. RILEY STEVENS: “What a knee to the head!” Quickly, Jacoby reacts with a double knee face breaker! A.J. is bleeding from his forehead now. Jacoby looks over outside the ring to make sure High Voltage is still incapacitated, and then he climbs up to the top rope. He waits for A.J. Lightning to stand up and then he jumps off for a diving axe handle, but A.J. catches him in mid-air and connects with the “Perfect Shock”! OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Game.” The referee slowly walks over to count the pin fall… 1…. 2…. KICKOUT AGAIN! RILEY STEVENS: “HOW DID HE PULL THAT OFF?” A.J. is livid with the referee; he grabs him by his shirt and slings him into the corner much like he has done to Jacoby all match long. The referee warns him once more and sends him back to the match, when he gets there, Jacoby is waiting for him and he connects with the JACOBY JOLT! The ref counts the pin fall…. 1… 2… 3! RILEY STEVENS: “That was one hell of a match, you almost can’t expect anything less from these open battle royals, they truly do showcase the best young talent in GIW.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Yeah, the best part about it is that we get to see who sucks before we push them to the top. GIW doesn’t make mistakes; if you suck we fire you.” The fans cheer as Jacoby celebrates in the ring after the finish of a very competitive and great match to start the show. WINNER: JACOBY JACKSON = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = After that short break we return where standing inside of the ring is Amanda Rowland wearing an all to small Santa’s Helper outfit as she holds a microphone, preparing to talk. AMANDA ROWLAND: “I’m out here to address what happened last week regarding Eric Sparrow, you see I’m not sure what he did, or how he did it. But some how he got Dr. House to get him pulled from his match. So I want him to come out so we can see how’s he recovering…” Amanda smiles thinking highly of her little sarcastic statement as she turns to the ramp, it’s hard to take her seriously in that outfit. “Fatlip” by Sum 41 hits and right away the fans begin to shower Sparrow with boos as he walks out from the back, slowly heading towards the ring still wrapped up in that sling, with a big smirk on his face as he heads into the ring, stepping up across from Amanda mouthing “What?” as she shakes her head smiling. AMANDA ROWLAND: “It’s a shame to see that your still hurt, but it’s also a shame that while last week you were able to trick Dr. House on short notice, we have your results back and turns out that there is nothing wrong at all with that arm. Now I’m not going to assume that you were lying, because that would be wrong of me. However, that does not mean I’m going to be so lenient, when it comes to you defending your belt. So, while you have the night off tonight, you’ll fight at ‘Genesis’ against this man.” The crowd looks around waiting to see who it is as even Eric Sparrow looks around confused. Suddenly there is a loud pop from one side as Christopher Darren Erns jumps the barricade into the ring, Sparrow realizes this at the last moment but he’s grabbed from behind as he tries to bail from the ring, the fans are going nuts for CDE as he rips off the sling, tossing it out to the crowd as he grabs Sparrow and turns him around. However to nobody’s surprise Sparrow pushes away with his supposedly broken arm and bails out of the ring dropping his belt on the way. The crowd boos him as he escapes but begins to cheer when Erns reaches down grabbing the Evolve title off the mat, staring at the title that he once held before setting it down on the mat staring down his oppononent for ‘Genesis’. RILEY STEVENS: “Wow! Christopher Darren Erns is BACK in GIW and he’ll look to regain his belt at ‘Genesis’ when he meets champion Eric Sparrow now let’s send you backstage where Sarah Sharp is standing with ‘The Perfect Combination’; Tony Miranda and The Shadow.” We head backstage where Sarah Sharp, dressed in a sleek pink dress, is standing in front of plasma TV and a sea green curtain. Miranda and Shadow are in full ring gear to her left. SARAH SHARP: “Thanks Riley. So, I’m standing here with ‘The Perfect Combination’ who in just a few moments will go head to head with Technical Mechanica for the GIW Tag Team Titles. How have you guys been preparing?” TONY MIRANDA: “This is a huge moment for us Sarah. The Perfect Combination will not leave North Beach tonight without those belts around our-“ Miranda and Shadow are suddenly forced to the floor by Damon Ramon and Ombio! They are being demolished by a chair and a chain. The onslaught continues for thirty or so seconds before Tech Mechanica decides they have done enough damage. They leave the interview site with Tony and Shadow a bloody mess in the center as we cut to commercial. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = We return to the ring where the fans are in shock over what just happened, we slowly pan around until we meet up with Riley Stevens and Oliver Thibodaux at the announce table Riley looking concerned while Oliver seems to not be too worried about what just transpired. RILEY STEVENS: “Unfortunately as you all just saw Tony Miranda and The Shadow will be unable to compete tonight. Which means unfortunately that our planned GIW Tag Team Championship match won’t happen, however we do have word from Amanda Rowland herself that at ‘Genesis’ if Perfect Combination is able to compete, they will meet Technical Mechanica in a No DQ’s Match!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Hate to be a buzz kill but don’t places usually have these days off? Seems unfair we are forced to be here. Can’t we just take a big break like Joshua Acquin?” RILEY STEVENS: “Quit your complaining, though that match may not happen we have two huge matches still on the card; the Christmas Scramble and then the huge Number One Contender’s match in the main event. With that we send you backstage…” = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = We cut backstage where Bobby “The Bullet” Bower and Aspen Chaud are getting ready for their match which is coming up next. Aspen is stretching and Bobby is sitting in a chair just watching her. ASPEN CHAUD: “When you cramp up in the middle of the match, I have every intentions of pinning you.” Bobby smirks and messes with his hair nonchalantly. BOBBY BOWER: "Hey, you have your ways and I have mine. I got this.” Aspen stops stretching and sits in a chair adjacent to Bobby’s. ASPEN CHAUD: “Look kid; you may think you know what is going on here… but you really don’t. You are getting a head start by me bothering to mentor you over the past few days, but you can’t handle this match at all.” BOBBY BOWER: “How do you know?” ASPEN CHAUD: “Because I have been in your situation before…” BOBBY BOWER: “How sweet…” ASPEN CHAUD: “When I first started wrestling, I thought I was invincible. I wasn’t. I got beat up pretty bad. I just don’t want that to happen to you like it did to me.” BOBBY BOWER: “I can do this. Good luck Aspen…” Bobby leaves the room. ASPEN CHAUD: “Yeah… same to you…” We fade to commercial. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = After that commercial break we come back and are in the center of the ring, where the fans are anxiously awaiting the next match. It’s time for the Christmas Scramble, as we are taken to the announce table. RILEY STEVENS Well ladies and gentleman before this match starts lets reveal who is paired with who for the Scramble tonight! We are taken to the backstage area where we pan out from a Christmas hat that as we pan out is revealed to be on the head of Amanda Rowland who is wearing a little Santa’s Helper outfit, how professional. As we pan out we are taken to see the nine competitors, where just then Chris Jericho walks into the room as the fans boo him as Amanda prepares to talk. AMANDA ROWLAND: “As you all know, you’re here to get your teams. So who wants to go first?” Amanda holds out eight different envelopes, the stars look around and it’s Gavin Masterson that steps forward with that big smirk of his as he tears an envelope out of her hands, pulling it open as he looks back at the people behind him. He rips it open and looks at it, then gazes over at his partner as Amanda reads over his shoulder. AMANDA ROWLAND: “Our first team is Gavin Masterson and Landon Ryan.” The two come together and though they both have massive egos, they seem rather pleased with who they got as they both head out, as Amanda pulls away the envelope of Gavin’s and Landon’s with six left. Out pops Bobby Bower as he walks up confidently to the front, grabbing out an envelope as he pops it open and then as soon as he reads it, a little smile goes on his face as he looks up showing it to his teammate, who as it reads is ‘Aspen Chaud’. ASPEN CHAUD: “Figures.” The two meet up and walk out as its Kaos Jones turn, as Aspen and Bobby walk out talking to one another. Kaos grabs it with just three choices left, and then pulls it apart and as soon as he reads it, a simple nod comes with it as he’s been paired with Los Firemen. AMANDA ROWLAND: “Kaos Jones is paired with BOTH members of Los Firemen.” Both Jose and Jos B crowd him walking off with him both talking to him in Spanish as he realizes just what he’s gotten into. However as we pan back we realize just what has happened as Chris Jericho and Mark Cross are now face to face. CHRIS JERICHO: “I’m stuck with this NOBODY? Come on Amanda, I’d rather alone then team with this guy.” Amanda shrugs her shoulders, as Jericho turns back to Cross who seems to actually be a little happy with this choice. MARK CROSS: “Let me put it this way, I wouldn’t turn your back on me. Because the moment you do, you’ll be lucky if you can ever wrestle again.” Cross storms off not at all wanting to talk about this match any more as he heads out of the room with Jericho glaring at him as we return to ringside for the match. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = We come back from a ‘Genesis’ hype video to see that all of the contestants of the ‘Christmas Scramble’ match were already in the ring. The camera pans over to Oliver Thibodaux and Riley Stevens at the announce table. RILEY STEVENS: “Ladies and gentlemen, it is now time for the ‘Christmas Scramble’ match, and here is how it will work. There are four teams of two, with the exception of Los Firemen who count as one wrestler. There will be two active teams at a time, with one of their members representing them in the ring. You can tag any member of any team, and then their team will be the active team. When elimination occurs via pin fall, submission, or disqualification, the person who lost will be eliminated along with their team mate. The last team standing is the winner of the match, and will have heavy momentum heading into Genesis.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Yep, that, ditto.” Gavin Masterson and Chris Jericho are the two that are set to begin the match. As the bell rings, Jericho gets an early advantage, hitting an impromptu enziguri on Masterson out of nowhere. Jericho stays on offense, trying to lock in the Walls of Jericho, but Masterson easily twists his hips which send Jericho flying across the ring. Angry now, Jericho makes a tag to the rookie Bobby Bower who flies into the ring, only to be met by a still clothesline by Gavin Masterson. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “That’s a good way to deal with the homeless population.” Masterson wastes no time going for a pin, but Bower kicks out… bloody lip and all. Gavin whips Bower around into the corner and clotheslines him there too. He does a hip toss to get Bower to the ground and stomps his chest until Bower is coughing heavily and squirming under his boot. Gavin makes the tag to Kaos Jones. Jones wastes no time heading to the top rope for the Kaos Theory, but Aspen Chaud pushes him back into the ring, saving her partner from sure defeat. RILEY STEVENS: “Slick move there by Chaud.” Kaos runs over to confront Aspen, and Bower goes for a roll-up! 1…2… KICK OUT! Kaos Jones jumps to his feet and begins to pummel Bower with closed fists to every exposed part of the body. Kaos picks Bower up and goes for his modified pedigree finisher, but Bobby reaches his leg out and tags Aspen in. Aspen clotheslines the back of Kaos’ head, sending him down to the mad hard. She locks in her patented finisher, the KMA and Kaos Jones taps out quickly. RILEY STEVENS: “There goes Kaos Jones, what a poor showing!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Wait… that means Los Firemen are eliminated too… DAMN YOU KAOS JONES!” Los Firemen tackle Kaos Jones by ringside to let out their aggression. Landon Ryan enters the match. Aspen is taken down by a running knee to the gut, and Landon sledges her back until she lays flat on her belly. Landon tries to lock in Aspen’s finisher, the KMA, but Aspen flips out of it, and tags in Mark Cross. Mark Cross enters the match fired up, knocking Ryan down with a myriad of different clotheslines. He throws Ryan into the corner and waits for him to bounce back, and then connects with an exploder suplex! He goes for a pin, but Landon Ryan kicks out before even a 1 count. Cross picks Ryan up again and slams him to mat with a body slam. He stares at Jericho and offers his hand for a tag. Reluctantly, Jericho slaps his hand, and now he is the legal man. Jericho gets momentum off the ropes and hits a picture perfect LIONSAULT on Landon Ryan. He goes for the pin… 1….2…. KICK OUT. Mark Cross is laughing now, and Gavin Masterson is trying his hardest to make a tag to his partner. Jericho is frustrated and pulls Landon up by his tights and throws him against the ring ropes, when Landon is tossed forward by his own momentum, Jericho connects with a code breaker… to the air! Gavin Masterson pulled Landon Ryan under the rope before Jericho could hit his finisher. Gavin is the legal man now, and slides into the ring. He stomps a few times on Jericho’s face before making a tag to Bobby Bower. Bower climbs up on the rope, and hits a powerful moonsault onto Jericho. He goes for a pin…1…2…CROSS BREAKS UP THE PIN! ASPEN CHAUD RUNS ACROSS THE RING AND SPEARS CROSS OUTSIDE. Jericho makes the tag to Gavin Masterson who is met by a myriad of kicks from the pumped up Bobby Bower. Bobby tries for a super kick but Masterson ducks under it. Bower rebounds with an enziguri that knocks Gavin out cold. Bower climbs to the top rope for a shooting star press, but just before he jumps, Jericho tags himself in. He waits for Bower to connect with the move, then pushes him off and takes the pin for himself. 1…2…3! Landon Ryan and Gavin Masterson are eliminated. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I’ll admit, they lasted longer than I figured.” Bobby Bower surprised Jericho from behind with a dropkick to his back, sending him the opposite turnbuckle. Bower gains speed again and connects with double knees to Jericho’s back. Bower climbs the top ropes again, he jumps up going for the flying stomp, but Jericho rolls out of the way. Bower lands on his feet, but falls over wincing in pain and holding his ankle. Sensing a weakness, Jericho begins to stomp on the ankle of Bower. Bower yelps every time Jericho drops his foot. Bower is reaching towards Aspen, but Jericho stomps on his elbow too, sending his arm back into the safe zone. Jericho picks Bower up and connects with a German suplex. He tags in Mark Cross who immediately goes for a pin on Bower… 1…2… KICKOUT! Cross pulls Bower up again and whips him into the corner adjacent to Aspen. He lands a few punches to the head and then tosses Bower to the ground again. Cross stomps away on the ankle of Bower. RILEY STEVENS: “Picking out the weak point of the opponent, cruel yet effective.” Cross tags in Jericho, the two seem to be working well together… both want to win this match. Jericho locks in the Walls of Jericho to Bobby Bower and he squeals in pain. Bower pulls himself within rope grasping distance, but his free elbow is still hurt from the merciless stomp by Jericho moments ago. Fighting through the pain, he grabs the rope and the ref forces Jericho to break the hold. Jericho pulls Bower back into his corner and tags in Mark Cross. They begin to stomp Bower in the corner. Eventually, Cross pulls Bobby back out into the center of the ring. He sets Bower up for what we presume to be a powerbomb and then pulls him up, only to be slammed back down to mat by a hurracanrana by Bobby! Still hurting badly, Bobby tries with all his might to get to Aspen. Just before he gets there, Jericho grabs him by his bad ankle, but Bower flips over and kicks Jericho right in the nose, sending him down to the matt. Bobby completes his trek across the ring and tags in Aspen Chaud! OLIVER THIBODAUX: “SHIT IS ABOUT TO GO DOWN!” The GIW Champion comes in and rocks Cross with clotheslines and rocks Jericho with fists to the head. Aspen whips Jericho out of the ring and sets Cross up for The Barbie Makeover, but he powers out of it and lands an elbow to the jaw of Aspen, sending her backwards. Cross connects with a dropkick sending Aspen into the corner. Cross charges her, but at the last moment she ducks out of the way. When Cross turns back around he is met by a spin kick from Aspen. Bobby Bower has climbed back on the top rope and he hits a spilt legged moonsault on him. Aspen flips him over and locks in the KMA. After a short struggle, Cross taps out. RILEY STEVENS: “What a match!” After the match, Aspen and Bobby are celebrating with the crowd. Mark Cross is back on his feet, trying to hobble out of the ring when Jericho’s voice can be heard off camera. CHRIS JERICHO: “Whoa! Where IN the HELL do you think you are going HONCHO?!” Cross stops in his tracks and turns back toward Jericho. Chris slithers into the ring with a GIW microphone in his hand. CHRIS JERICHO: “YOU cost me this match! YOU tapped out to a GIRL! If you tap out to her, what the hell do you think you will do to ME at Genesis?” Mark Cross grinds his teeth and snatches the microphone from Chris. MARK CROSS: “This.” As quick as he muttered the word, the microphone was being bashed against Jericho’s skull over and over again. He continues beating Jericho with the object until security can pull him off. We cut to commercial. WINNERS: BOBBY BOWER & ASPEN CHAUD = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = We cut away to a blank screen. We can hear voices in the background. The only time this has happened before is when the cameraman was hiding to obtain secret information; that’s probably what is happening now. AMANDA ROWLAND: “Mr. Morgan, I don’t know what you want me to do.” MR. MORGAN: “Amanda, GIW has pulled amazing ratings for ION Television thus far, and we have no doubts that it will continue to do so… but the content is no longer what we are looking for.” AMANDA ROWLAND: “…how so?” MR. MORGAN: “Your color commentator swore seventeen times during the opening segment, there was a brutal surprise attack… I am afraid that ION Television can’t have this on this… family station.” AMANDA ROWLAND: “It’s just the nature of the sport sir…” MR. MORGAN: “Regardless… we have decided to finish airing this episode of Revolt, but then we are dropping the program. I’m sorry.” AMANDA ROWLAND: “What! What about Genesis?” MR. MORGAN: “We can’t risk airing it.” AMANDA ROWLAND: “But…” MR. MORGAN: “Good luck finding a new television station.” BLAKE BECKETT: “Is this really what you want to do?” MR. MORGAN: “Yes.” BLAKE BECKETT: “Our ratings for this show are projected to be 1.02 and Genesis will only raise the ratings… are you sure you want to drop your number one show on your network because of a risk issue?” MR. MORGAN: “Mr. Beckett, we have considered the repercussions.” BLAKE BECKETT: “People don’t watch ION Television for the re-runs of ‘3-2-1 Penguins’, Mr. Morgan, they watch this channel for Galveston Island Wrestling.” MR. MORGAN: “That’s a fair statement, but our network is suitable for all family members, not just adults.” BLAKE BECKETT: “Adults pay your bills Mr. Morgan, not children. GIW doesn’t cater to children because they don’t matter. I hate to have to tell you this, but without GIW… ION is a worthless channel.” MR. MORGAN: “ION offers many other options.” AMANDA ROWLAND: “They all suck.” MR. MORGAN: “Why don’t we just drop the streaming of Revolt right now?” BLAKE BECKETT: “Why don’t you? I’m sure all the people watching wouldn’t mind if you didn’t show the main event.” MR. MORGAN: “ION cares about their viewers; that is why we don’t drop the stream now. That is also the same reason why after this week, GIW will not air on ION television.” BLAKE BECKETT: “Mr. Morgan, if you care about the viewers, you will at least air Genesis.” AMANDA ROWLAND: “Yeah!” MR. MORGAN: “Why?” BLAKE BECKETT: “That is where everything finishes up. It is like the end of the book.” AMANDA ROWLAND: “You can’t just not finish a good book Mr. Morgan.” MR. MORGAN: “Very well. Genesis is the stoppage point then.” BLAKE BECKETT: “Thank you Mr. Morgan.” We head to commercial. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = It’s reached that time of the night after a Tag Team titles match that never happened and then a Christmas Scramble match that stole the show and it’s now time for the MAIN EVENT of the evening. As we come into the ring standing there already prepared to go to war for the Number One Contender’s spot is the former face of Evolve, Christ Armstrong! The fans are booing the hell out of him, as Riley and Oliver pick it up. RILEY STEVENS: “Well we are here at the Main Event of this evening which if it ends up like I think it will, is going to be a huge match. First off we have Christ Armstrong who I understand is one of your favorites?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Are you an idiot Riley? Armstrong was a star two months ago. The times have changed and you want to know whose side I’m on now?” Oliver points to the stage where suddenly “Fuckin’ in the Bushes” by Oasis the oh so naughty titled song hits as out from the back comes Scott Reave to an even louder amount of boos. He’d become hated rather quickly and the smirk he wore to the ring did not halt that at all. As he looked out at the fans who hated him so much he couldn’t help it. He walked up the steps into the ring, looking over at his opponent who only in the papers was even close to his level. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “This kid is the goods! Scott Reave beat Aspen Chaud already once and once he disposes of this guy, he’ll be a step away from the title. Finally a champion we can be proud of.” RILEY STEVENS: “While a title win would certainly make for one of the most impressive starts to a career ever, I don’t think these fans agree with you cause they love our champion.” "Bullseye" by Aly and AJ finally hits and the fans burst into an explosion of cheers as Reave who wasn’t quite finished with his entrance looks to the ramp with a sick glare. As out from the back Aspen Chaud walks with her black and white referee shirt on as she looks towards the ring, where whether she had to change or she was set up to come out last, she stares into the eyes of two men who want nothing more then her title belt and to make a statement off of her. RILEY STEVENS: “Here’s our champion, who walks into enemy territory as she will be in charge of who becomes her Number One Contender.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I just hope she doesn’t cheat like usual.” Aspen walks down to the steps then walks up them looking in at her contenders and then stepping over the bottom rope and into the ring. There is an awkward moment as both Reave and Armstrong glare at her, almost like she was in the match as well. However to end that she quickly calls for the bell to start the match. DING DING DING! Rather then right away seeing action it’s Chris Armstrong that approaches Aspen first screaming into her face. The fans boo him as she tries to insist that he wrestles the match. He continues to lay it into her, while Reave sits back seeming to allow it. However after a few moments of listening to Armstrong insist the belt is his, he walks up from behind grabbing him and twisting him around landing a huge uppercut that stuns Armstrong as he falls back and Aspen jumps out of the way. As he’s stunned Scott grabs him and tosses him into the corner as he backs up then runs forward connecting with a big high knee, but right away grabs Chris’s head then brings him forward for a big Bulldog! The fans are a little impressed as we can tell from their lack of boos. But Reave quickly gets them back on his bad side as he bad mouths them before turning around staring at Chris as he tries to get up. Seemingly not worried about him at all he looks at Aspen staring at her, as if to send a message with this one. Chris still a bit rocky from the last move stumbles up getting his footing but walks right into Reave who lifts him up for the ‘R.C.D.’ (Reave Crash Dynamite) an Emerald Frosion right into a pin attempt as Aspen slides down for the count 1…2…Kick Out! The fans seem a bit surprised Chris even did that, as Aspen hops up with Reave doing the same glaring at her the whole time. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “That was a slow count!” RILEY STEVENS: “Oh come on Oliver that was a perfectly fine count.” Reave has yet to take his gaze away from her while she pretends not to notice as she looks at Chris who is starting to just barely get up. Rather then let him get to his feet it’s Reave who chooses to make a statement, slowly kicking him down to the mat like he’s not even a former two time Evolve Champion. The fans boo his actions but it seems very clear that they are the least of his worries as he turns and nails him with a huge kick to the chest as he’s on his hands and knees. The fans boo as Chris flops over holding his chest as Scott looks now to Aspen, and begins to motion for the belt which brings even more boos from the crowd. As Chris now gets up it’s him that is bleeding from the mouth, after that brutal shot. Reave screams at him to get up, as the fans have actually begun to feel sorry for Armstrong as Scott backs up to the corner. This time Chris is able to get up only to his knees, but that’s all Reave was looking for as he runs forward and crushes him with a big ‘Street Spirit’ a Shining Wizard! The fans are crushed seeing it as Reave goes for the pin attempt 1…2…He pulls Chris up himself. Aspen tries to insist that he just pins him but he shakes his head again, as he wipes Chris’s blood off his foot. Aspen seems a little worried about Chris’s well being at this point, though to her surprise he is able to get up only using the ropes to do so however. The fans actually give him a half hearted cheer for that but as he turns around he falls to the mat unable to stand on his own two feet, landing on one knee. This is right where Reave wants him as he runs off the ropes and jumps off his shoulder right into a ‘Voodoo Lock II’ the fans begin to boo as he tugs back on the arm! Aspen checks on him when suddenly we either hear a tap or a snap but regardless Aspen pushes Scott off calling for the match. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “New Number One Contender!” RILEY STEVENS: “In brutal fashion as well. I’m not sure from my angle but I think I may have heard a snap. None the less it’s Scott Reave who will face Aspen come ‘Genesis’.” The fans boo him as he hops up basking in their boos as he realizes that Aspen’s title is right outside of the ring as he slides out. In the ring we see a bloody Chris Armstrong biting his lip in pain holding his arm as to everyone’s surprise it would be Aspen Chaud checking on him as she calls out for some EMT’s. They don’t come right away and as she gets up to make it more evident she turns right into Scott Reave staring down at her belt. She flinches a bit almost expecting that he would hit her with it, but he shakes his head “I’ll crush you when the light’s are brightest” Scott smirks and hands her the belt, as he heads from the ring the EMT’s enter the ring as Reave has made his statement loud and clear tonight. He’s here and he’s here to stay, as the two share one last stare down as the show seemingly comes to a close. WINNER: SCOTT REAVE = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = To our surprise after a short break the show is instead not over as standing in the ring with a microphone is Amanda Rowland, the fans are cheering her on with a small little clap. AMANDA ROWLAND: “As some of you may know, earlier today the people at ION Television decided that our product was no longer something they wanted on their channel. While I did a lot and eventually got them to reconsider and have ‘Genesis’ broadcast. So I’m sorry but I’ve done all that I can do, it appears as though this company is going to fold after ‘Genesis’.” The fans boo as Amanda Rowland nods her head as it seems that she’s given up hope on this one. “Demon Eyes” by The Answer hits and the fans begin to boo the presence of Blake who they almost surely think is here to spit on the grave of GIW. As he walks out he looks down at Amanda and without wasting any time, he begins to speak into the mic he already has as he heads towards the ring. BLAKE BECKETT: “That’s not true at all. You see Amanda the problem with you, is that you give up to easy. While I never give up. I’ve been sitting back and watching you pretend like you know what you’re doing. When in reality you’ve only continued to embarrass my company. I helped bring in Chris Jericho, I helped make this company. So seeing it end on account of a channel being unwilling to pick us up was a laugher to me. So I went and told ION Television that we are OVER WITH.” Amanda tries to cut him off as he paces back and forth on the ramp before she can say anything about wanting to air ‘Genesis’ he chooses to speak over her. BLAKE BECKETT: “You see it’s funny how quickly the Board of Directors willingly begun to hand things back to me in these times. I see ideas and I make them work. And that’s why this show will NOT STOP. And that’s why this show will continue to grow. I’ve decided that Amanda you’re no longer cutting it as General Manager so my first change is demoting you! And that’s not all that’s going to happen because I worked out a new deal with Fox Southwest! To air not one but two shows a week.” The fans are seeming to be soaking this all in and don’t even bother to truly notice the distraught look on Amanda’s face. For the fans they are all realizing that Blake has effectively just saved the place. BLAKE BECKETT: “So starting at ‘Genesis’ we will be live on Fox Southwest! But after that things are going to expand. You see while Revolt will still be live here every Thursday night we are going to expand by putting Monday Night Affliction back on air as they work the New Orleans area. I will be the all seeing eye, the puppet master for everything. And starting at ‘Genesis’ my creation only gets bigger!” The fans are actually giving him a bit of an applause as they cheer him as their savior, as the camera now focuses in on him during his ego trip as he says the final thing to tip the scale. BLAKE BECKETT: “I’ve always run this place, I’ve always been in charge. And just like it used to be I am back as GIW’s GOD!” The fans are in shock still not only that he’d say that but also about all the news they were just given as he glares out at Amanda and to the crowd as the show slowly begins to fade on the face of the man that’s back in charge here in GIW. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = =
