Extreme TV, 12 May 2011

12 May 2011

GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: E X T R E M E T V! QUOTE Opener As usual ETV started with an Open Battle Royal, this time it was one filled with several stars. You had a few locals; Jack “And Coke” Daniels and Mr. White as well as GIW Stars; Abram Green a debuting star, Mr. Black a man who would also wrestle in the Main Event of ETV, and lastly Pandora who was returning to action here in GIW. Right away it was clear a majority of the fans were behind Green to win, some siding with Daniels. As the match started it was chaotic people were fighting all around the ring. Though it was expected the smaller Pandora would be eliminated first, she shockingly fought and eliminated Mr. White. The fans got behind her, as she started to brawl with Mr. Black, doing better then most would think. In the battle of the fan favorites Abram finished Daniels off with a clothesline over the top. Green delivered a crushing Spear to Mr. Black that put him out. He then grabbed Pandora trying to toss her over, however she wrapped her legs around him and started to attempt to pull him over. She didn’t have the strength, but she was going to try. However it was a recovered Mr. Black that capitalized and got up, flipping up Green’s legs and sending them both over. The fans were not happy with it, but Mr. Black knew he was half way to a big night here on ETV. WINNER: MR. BLACK QUOTE Mid-Card Match Though this fell in the middle of the show and was considered the “Mid-Card Match” it was anything but a Mid-Card match. Instead it was a Classic; to date it may have been the best ETV Match to date. It was an all out war that no man seemed to want to lose. For every punch England landed on Hister he received one back and for every move Hister landed on Chris he was given another back. Calling it a match doesn’t do it justice, it was a war. In the end it came down to both men bloodied and it was up to who got up first. Hister was up first but unfortunately when he went for his Superkick, England dropped down and delivered a brutal shot to his lower regions. He then finished it with ‘The Cross of St. George’ a Border Toss. England left with the victory, by doing whatever he had to do including cheat to win. WINNER: CHRIS ENGLAND QUOTE Main Event The winner of the Open Battle Royal returned to the ring for his second match of the night, this time he’d be facing Techno Viking a man who came in with big expectations. However what proceeded was anything but a good showing as Mr. Black, completely dominated this match from the beginning. Showing off his complete arsenal, he ended the match up only perhaps three minutes into the match with ‘The Blackout’ a Psycho Driver! The fans seemed impressed by his performances here on ETV and at least for the time being it would probably be the last time we see him here. WINNER: MR. BLACK
