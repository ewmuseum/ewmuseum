Blackout II, 11 September 2011

GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: BLACKOUT II LIVE! From The Bolivar Lighthouse – September 11, 2011 BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! The show opens with the traditional flares skyrocketing into the air, this time they are bright white and cast a shadow onto the fans below. The crowd is piled into and around the Bolivar Lighthouse waiting for this evening show, the second edition of Blackout,to begin. As the footage begins to be picked up by GIW's ION affiliates all around the United States, we cut to Riley Stevens and Oliver Thibodaux at the announce booth. RILEY STEVENS: “Ladies and gentlemen, this is an electric atmosphere here tonight in Port Bolivar, Texas where over SIX HUNDRED screaming GIW are here ready to see some of the best action wrestling has to offer. Tonight grudges will be settled, blood will be shed, new stars will prove their worth, gold will be consolidated, and we see if our champion can retain,or if Mark Cross is to strong for the Undisputed one.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Oh yes Riley, this is wrestling at its finest. Anyone who loves this sport will surely love the show that we are going to put on for you tonight – undoubtedly headlined by a match that features two amazing, interesting, and strange wrestlers – Mark Cross vs Alioth Starre for the GIW Undisputed championship.” RILEY STEVENS: “These two men are extremely even competitors. Usually I can pick a winner just by looking at the match, but in this case either man could come out on top.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Personally, I think Starre's time as a champion is numbered. He is the longest reigning champion we have ever had in GIW, and he has to loose that belt some time. Mark Cross may not act like it, but he has his sights set on the crown jewel of the company just like everyone else does. And you know what, if he didn't, he wouldn't stand a chance tonight against a guy like Starre.” RILEY STEVENS: “Well, I think that we have sufficiently prolonged the inevitable; coming up next is a match that is as unique as the people competing in it: a first blood elimination match. But first, let’s send it over to Blake Beckett who has promised a very special announcement for tonight.” The camera quickly cuts to Blake Beckett in his office, we see him sitting behind the desk; waiting for his cue to begin. BLAKE BECKETT: “Ladies and gentlemen, it seems like once a month, everyone we do a Supershow, I always have a special announcement to make. For a show as big as Blackout, I couldn't make an exception. As you all know, GIW has grown exponentially since our successful journey to Mexico for Category 5. We now have a considerable Mexican audience thanks to live simulcasts to other countries on GIW.com. Following this, we expanded to nearly the entire state of Texas, major coastal markets in the west, bits and pieces of New England, and in select places on the Gulf Coast – namely New Orleans and Jackson. It is time to go further. Galveston Island Wrestling has signed a contract lasting until Halloween Night, 2011, to work exclusively off of the Carnival Cruse Liner on a trip across the Caribbean, and onto Mexico. Our stars will board immediately after tonight show and perform a Revolt at Sea this Thursday. On the 22nd, we will do a show in Georgetown on the Grand Cayman Islands. Following this, GIW will go on the 29th to Kingston, Jamaica. On October 6th, we invade San Juan, Puerto Rico. Then, on the 13th we hit Nassau in The Bahamas. The 20th we will be in Key West, Florida. Following that, a special Monday Night Revolt on the 24th, we end our journey where it began in Cancun. And then, Halloween Night, GIW returns to Galveston for On Hallowed Ground II at the legendary Hotel Galvez. We will broadcast right here on ION TV, and we hope you enjoy the ride just as we will.” QUOTE The time has come for the opening match here at ‘Blackout II’ and right away we realize things are going to be intense as filling the ring to its brim is six men. From top to bottom we have Jack Waters, John Rawlings, Koji Nakamora, Jak Hendricks, Christian Rivers, Koji Nakamora, and Ace Baldwin. The crowd is pumped up and ready to go knowing just the rules behind this match as like sharks they wait for blood. RILEY STEVENS: “Are you ready to go with this one Oliver? DING DING DING! Right away like a war things go wild as men start to fly towards the middle. Waters and Rawlings go at it back and forth all while Rivers and Hendricks do the same. However Koji having serious beef with Ace runs at him going for him as Ace quickly slides out of the ring. Koji follows him out going for him and chasing him around the ring. Baldwin watches from not far away enjoying it as he watches as Rivers walks over grabbing a steel chair, preparing to use it on Koji as he gets to his feet. Taking a big swing at him Koji avoids it sending it into the ground. Almost immediately he jumps up kicking it right into the face of Rivers! The fans go wild as he falls to the ground, holding the chair in his hands right over his face. Baldwin is shown and his smile has turned into a look of shock as Nakamora jumps up and delivers a Double Foot Stomp to the face of the fallen Rivers!
