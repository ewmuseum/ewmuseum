Galveston Island Wrestling Presents: Thursday Night Revolt! Live! From the Harbor View Inn! July 22nd, 2010! BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! Fireworks rain down from the roof of the Harbor View Inn, and the fans go wild because they know that it is time for the flagship show in Galveston Island Wrestling to begin. The swarming crowd of over two hundred people are gathered around the ring clamoring to get a glimpse of their favorite Revolt star. The cameras from ION Television pan around the ring, showing the insanity before focusing in on the commentary team of Riley Edwards and Oliver Thibodaux. RILEY EDWARDS: Ladies and gentlemen, thank you for tuning in to this week’s Thursday Night Revolt. Tonight we are coming to you live from the Harbor View Inn, and this show is jam packed with the apex talent in the organization. Tonight, Jaden Daniels seeks revenge against our former Hardcore Champion, Billy Spizatoni, in a match that is sure to have high-flying theatrics along with tough technical wrestling, it will be a match you will remember for a long time. OLIVER THIBODAUX: Then after that, we have a four person slugfest to determine who will be become the number one contender for the Lightweight Championship. Later tonight, of course, David Sinclair will announce the new name for the championship… but the fact still remains the same. Kaiju, Danielson, Sparrow, or Vaccaro are really gonna have a step up from where they are this week. RILEY EDWARDS: They sure will Oliver, and the person who they will be chasing after will be determined in the next match, which is for the Lightweight Championship. Jake Kennedy takes on the champion, Nitro, who won the title by surprise after defeating Mike Danielson last week on Revolt. Jake Kennedy, if you remember, made it all the way to the final round of the Galveston Island Championship tournament, but lost to Scott Rydell, who is our current Galveston Island Champion. Jake is really a tough cookie Oliver, wouldn’t you agree? OLIVER THIBODAUX: I don’t know about that Riley, he kind of had to have the help of the chairman, Blake Beckett, to defeat Aspen Chaud in the semi-final round of the tourney, if I remember correctly. Any who, Aspen Chaud hasn’t done so well since then either. This week, because of the brilliant Blake Beckett, she has to go up against our Divas champion, Violet Vanity; our resident lackey, Catalina Mendoza; and the woman who has made a HUGE splash since signing here, Liviana Valentine. No one knows how this match will transpire, but there will fists flying and hair being pulled here! RILEY EDWARDS: You got that right Oliver, those women flat out hate each other, and Aspen will be looking to prove a point to Blake Beckett. But, there is still another match left on the card. A main event match featuring Wake Walker, and our Galveston Island Champion, Scott Rydell. Last week before his tag team match against Violet Vanity and Joshua Acquin, Wake was brutally beaten by Scott Rydell, and no one really knows why. Tonight, not only will Scott Rydell tell us why he did what he did, but he also has to fight him later in the show. OLIVER THIBODAUX: That match is going to awesome! Maybe Fake Falker will make an appearance too? Maybe not… Also, just a programming note from Fusion an hour or so ago. Alex Athanasios won the open battle royal match. He was then moved to Affliction, but was then moved back to Revolt due to a trade. Gotta love the politics of wrestling right? Fusion also featured a fatal four way match with Sherilynne Martinez, Justin Sane, Candice Rose, and Eva booked to slug it out. Candice Rose flat out decimated everyone, and she pinned Sherilynne Martinez after hitting her patented finishing move “Baby Down”. We are looking forward to seeing more of this diva in the future. RILEY EDWARDS: So there you have it, that’s the rundown of tonight’s show brought to you by Riley Edwards and Oliver Thibodaux. Before we get to the opening contest, lets send it over to the always amazing Sarah Sharp in the back with a very special guest tonight, General Manager David Sinclair, and tOWLette! The camera switches over to Sarah Sharp standing in the backstage GIW Interview Lounge. There is a purple curtain hanging behind them, and a flat screen television hanging behind them, with the GIW Logo frozen on the screen. Sarah Sharp, wearing a black dress, is standing in the middle of the screen with a GIW microphone in her hand. Standing beside her is General Manager David Sinclair in his black suit, with the infamous tOWLette perched on his shoulder, on high alert as always. Sarah Sharp begins to speak, in a lethargic tone. RILEY EDWARDS: This is sure to be a very close match! Both Rydell and Wake are some of the top dogs of GIW! Whoever comes out on top is definitely the better man. Wake follows up with a clothesline. The match continues with Wake Walker having a clear advantage over Scott Rydell. Rydell eventually gets the upper hand half way through the match. It's a never ending cycle of back and forth back and forth. The match actually lasts longer than any of the other matches until eventually... OLIVER THIBODAUX: Wait, what the hell is going on?! Johnny Stylez comes in with a steel chair! He enters the ring and to the disdain of both Wake and Rydell, he hits the ref. Wake and Rydell both don't know what to do. Stylez swings at Rydell and hits him. Stylez slowly walks towards Wake. He swings! Wake dodges the chair shot only to be hit by it the second time! Stylez continues his attack on both Wake and Rydell and leaves them both to be a bloody mess to end the show on a sad note. The crowd is booing Stylez heavily. WINNER: No Contest
