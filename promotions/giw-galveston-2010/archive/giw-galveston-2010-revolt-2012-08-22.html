GIW — Card

Revolt, 22 August 2012

22 August 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: WEDNESDAY NIGHT REVOLT LIVE! FROM COURVILLE STADIUM AUGUST 22ND, 2012 BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! The show sprang to life with “Over and Under” by Egypt Central, the theme song of Wednesday Night Revolt was blasting through the speakers. Revolt was live from Courville Stadium, right smack dab in the center of Galveston Island, and the crowd was spilled onto the grounds of the field, all three hundred and fifty screaming fans. Signs that read “Seton Is Champ!”, “I Read Sayge's Book”, and “Aspen Can Read My Bible” were being waved around as we panned around, getting sight of the massive crowd. We cut over to the announce booth. RILEY STEVENS: “Good evening, I'm Riley Stevens alongside my partner Oliver Thibodaux, and we are live here on Showtime tonight, and we have a blockbuster tag team main event for you guys.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “That's right, Ryan Cavallari will team up with Attack Attack to sqaure off against the team of Daredevil, and of course, the NEW GIW Undisputed Champion, as much as it pains me to say it, Madison Seton.” RILEY STEVENS: “It was an amazing match at Moment of Glory, but Madison Seton was able to pull off a victory against Alexis Terry, who has since retired from the federation. However, Seton has bigger things to worry about, because since Ryan Cavallari was able to defeat Alioth Starre at Moment of Glory, we will see him go one on one with the champion with the title on the line at Blackout 3, which will be September 19th from the Bolivar Lighthouse, on iPPV.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I find it hard to believe that anyone would really be picking Seton to outlast Ryan Cavallari in that match. She barely was able to get by Alexis Terry, who was ready to retire anyway! The only reason she lost that belt was because she wanted to!” RILEY STEVENS: “Regardless, Madison Seton is the champion, and therefore she is the one everyone will be gunning for over the next month. How she handles the pressure of tonight and GIW100 will be signs of how reign will be over the coming days. Will it be dominant, or will it be short lived?” “New God Flow” by Kanye West and Pusha T hit the speakers, and the fans immediately began to boo as Ryan Cavallari stepped out from behind the curtain and made his way down to the GIW ring. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “See, even he knows that Seton is boring! He has to come out just to make this segment better. Way to go Riley.” RILEY STEVENS: “Well, indeed here is the number one contender Ryan Cavallari, and he looks like he means business.” He took the mic and held it close, waiting for the music to die out, and then spoke. RYAN CAVALLARI: “1, 2, 3, and 1. Do you know what those numbers are, ladies and gentlemen? GIW fans, do you know what those numbers mean to me? I've earned those numbers, week in and week out here in the ring, and those numbers reflect just how dominant I have been in this company. I've defeated opponents twelve times, drawed only once, and been defeated a mere three times. That winning percentage is one of the best in this company, and that is because I am one of the best in this company. For months and months I have sat back and been held down, fed the middle of the road talent, and been considered 'good, but not good enough.' I've waited in the wings for an extremely long time, and now, ladies and gentlemen, GIW fans from all around the United States and the world, I'm taking the spotlight.” The fans booed, but Ryan carried on. RYAN CAVALLARI: “The big idea they hype for Moment of Glory is to seize the moment – do something for yourself, and that is just what I did. I had plenty of doubters going into my match with Alioth, but the fact is he couldn't even keep up with me. No one in this company can keep up with me, and over the next month I'm going to prove it to anyone who is still ignorant enough not to believe it.” More boos now, heavier than the last go round. RYAN CAVALLARI: “So let me forewarn any of you little teenagers who idolize Madison Seton. I'm want you to listen to me carefully, and closely. Tonight I am going to beat her. I'm going to beat her clean, and I am going to show everyone here that it is merely the politics of having to wait to challenge, but that I am the true champion of GIW. I may not have a title... I'm still uncrowned, but there will be no shadow of a ...” “Demon Eyes” by The Answer hit the PA, and the fans jolted to life as the Chairman Blake Beckett stepped onto the stage. Cavallari seemed surprised that he had shown up. BLAKE BECKETT: “Hi Ryan, its nice to see you're making yourself at home in the middle of the ring.” Cavallari grinned real wide. RYAN CAVALLARI: “Blake, lets face it, I own this ring. I can do anything I want in it.” Blake nodded as the fans booed some more. BLAKE BECKETT: “Well that is all fine and good, but I just wanted to come out here and make something very clear to you Ryan. I don't have much respect for you. Your obvious wrestling skills are nice, but as person, you're complete shit. I watched from my hospital bed as you and your pupeteer Aspen Chaud turned on Alexis Terry – and I think its just a horrible waste of talent that you feel the need to associate yourself with the likes of that lying, conniving, whore. So I wanted to warn you, just like I did Aspen, because you're one in the same with her. You're life wont be easy in this company as long as I have any authority here.” His music started up again as he locked eyes with Cavallari for a while, then disappeared with a smug grin behind the curtain. RILEY STEVENS: “The Chairman just put Cavallari in his place!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “These next few weeks should be very interesting for Cavallari as he tries to capture his first title with the company...” We faded into a recap package of Moment of Glory III with Cavallari nodding, as if he accepted the challenge. QUOTE Darkness fell and the grey, muted light arose. As ‘Scarred’ played, Scar himself walked down the aisle. The flicker of lit lighter fluid illuminated the audience in tiny, star-like dots. He himself was a white, ghostly presence that moved toward the ring and then entered. As he parted the ropes, he stepped forth with his right foot first and the trench coat that fell from shoulder to boots swayed with his motions. Scar abandoned it as the lights rose up to a normal hue. The mask he wore, the signature venetian with a red stain on the corner, the only color besides white that marked it, was removed and set in the corner of the ring to await its master’s victory. He tugged on his white gloves and awaited his opponent’s arrival to the arena. RILEY STEVENS: “Scar is strange to say the least.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Yeah…To say the least.” Hers was quicker, but there was something far more interesting about Zerotine Valentino’s arrival that caught the ire of her opponent, and the attention of others. Her left shoulder was raw, her fingers digging at it like a squirrel looking for its fruit for winter. Her eyes jittered across like swift darts seeking out its target, but aimed loosely and unfaithful to its thrower. She was forced along, and even helped into the ring. The crew around the ring seemed a little anxious as their eyes befell Scar. RILEY STEVENS: “Something seems off about Zerotine tonight.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “As if you don’t know what’s wrong with her…” His head cocked to the left and he gave her a long scrutinizing stare. He didn’t recognize it at first, but after the bell rang, he realized what was going on. What it was that made this girl’s entrance seem so much odder than even his. She was higher than all of the kites that any of the children in the arena could fly at once. Cocaine, he figured by the redness of her eyes, as well as how wide they were. She seemed fleet-footed, if only to be clumsy in her step work. The match was forced onward, however. Scar, had he been anyone else may have stopped the match and called for the bell. How dare Zerotine fight under the influence, but considering the sadistic nature of who he was, he opted to work with it. As penance for her ways, and even as acceptance for that even he has his dark vices, Scar went on with the match. He grabbed Zerotine by the wrist and tugged her into a wrist lock, but she pulled out quickly to tend to a sudden nosebleed that had been spurred on by the high she was dangerously encased in. The warm fluid enticed Scar closer, but only to grab that wrist again and this time shove her to the ropes. RILEY STEVENS: “Is this perhaps the weirdest thing we have ever seen?” When she returned, he flipped her onto the canvas, but her landing had been botched, thus toppling over him. They lay like a bundle of sticks strewn about a stable until he shoved her off. She was first to her feet, despite his more sober condition. She took a swing which hit him square in the chest, but she had aimed for his face. The second one struck gold and he snapped back with a short-arm clothesline. After the strike, Zero scratched at her opponent like a wild animal. She pulled herself to her feet, shaking off the attack and managed to knock down her opponent with a a hard reverse elbow, then launched herself with the standing moonsault, only to land flat on her stomach. Scar had already moved by then and the landing would have certainly injured someone, especially in her condition. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Haha she missed him.” It was around that time he had decided to end the match. With her incapable of performing as well as she should have under a normal setting, Scar wasn’t going to threaten his own wellbeing, just hers. A quick Kesagiri chop silenced her anxious movements and then he hoisted her up with both gloved hands. She crashed down into the canvas with a loud bang that echoed throughout the arena even despite her small frame. The force behind it was enough to realize he meant business now. Scar didn’t want to play games with a high opponent. It was disrespectful to him, and even the crowd that she would have done this. He peeled her off the canvas, but Zerotine leapt to her feet after she tore herself from his grasp. He stood their in annoyance as she raised up with a dropkick that knocked him back to the canvas. She had actually manipulated the maneuver correctly, something she even grinned at for doing right in her high state. She went for the pin, but Scar shoved her off before the referee could even start a count. RILEY STEVENS: “Quick kick out by Scar!” Though he was not known for it, Scar used a submission hold to silence his opponent. A hand clutched across her face that sealed the openings that allowed her to breathe. She struggled and writhed under his clutch, but he held his white gloved hand firmly to suffocate her. He was snuffing the very life out of her, but she was too high on substance to quite grasp the concept. The dove-white of his right hand’s glove began to darken with red and moisten with her fear. Sated to the point that the referee forced him off of her after a five count, Scar shoved her face into the canvas, a now bloody wreck as she gasped for that very life she was reaching a high for. RILEY STEVENS: “This condition really seems to have hurt her chances in this match.” She held her mouth and nose, gasping for each breath. Scar stayed seated on his knees, one hand held to his hip and the other was out in front of him as he watched a strand of blood course its way down his thumb and drip to the canvas below. His eyes then gazed over toward the referee, whom looked back at him with uncertainty, especially after seeing his wry glare. It didn’t take long for him to decide to cover Zerotine Valentino then and when the match was over, he pulled himself off of her and back onto his knees where he watched the blood, the crowd, and then ignored his opponent. He was not amused. REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner…Scar!” Scar gets up to his feet shaking his head as he looks down upon Valentino as he exits the ring walking to the back. WINNER: SCAR The crowd was still in silent shock at the awful scene when “Demon Eyes” by the Answer hit, and Beckett came out to little or no ovation at all. He stood on the stage as Zero began to pick herself up slowly, using the ropes. He was shaking his head. BLAKE BECKETT: “What a fantastic performance Zero. You really are someone we can look up to. Did you really think that bullshit drugtest was going to convince us that you were clean? Well, when Kluge told me you passed, I didn't buy it, so I found the most brutal guy in the company to take you out, and teach you a lesson. So actually learn for once in your life, get clean, or I won't worry about it anymore. Next week, if you don't put out an amazing performance and win your match in less than five minutes, I'll gladly inform you that you have been fired from the company. I don't have to waste my time on pieces of shit like you.” The crowd was in absolute shock as his music faded back up. Zero was getting bandaged. RILEY STEVENS: “Blake... oh my God.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I don't like Zero, but there comes a point in time where you just need to stop.” RILEY STEVENS: “Why fire her? Help her!” The cameras zoomed in on Zero some more, then we eventually faded out into a recap of Attack Attack vs. Daredevil on the pre-show, for those who missed it. QUOTE After we come back from the back we have Jak Hendricks prepping up in the ring stretching out as “Attitude” by Chris Goulstone hits as the fans begin to cheer on Jeremy Baltimore for this match here tonight, where he gets perhaps his biggest opportunity to date against one of GIW’s all time greats. High fiving some fans he jumps up onto the ring apron and steps through the ropes. RILEY STEVENS: “Baltimore and Hendricks tonight have a huge opportunity to beat one of GIW’s bests and really jump up the ladder around here.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “That’s for sure.” Baltimore looks over at Jak who has a smirk on his face as he heads into a different corner as the lights go down as an A appears on the screen as that then fades and the lights return as these fans cheer loudly for Alioth Starre as he strolls down to the ring with a grin on his face. 'No Feeling' by Sex Pistols echoes through out the crowd as Jak and Baltimore stare at Starre, he is clearly the target in this one. RILEY STEVENS: “How will Starre respond after his loss to Ryan Cavallari that set him back a bit?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Hopefully poorly, lets go JAK!” Starre enters the ring with both men staring at him in the corner as he looks from one to the other; it’s no secret that he is the veteran of the group. The referee steps in to get this thing started and then calls for the bell. DING DING DING! This match starts up and immediately the three men are waiting to see who they should go for first but finally that decision is made as Alioth steps back telling them to have at it, and both knowing that the real prize is Starre…They look to get the other out of the way as Jak throws a punch that Baltimore blocks then follows up with a big shot and then another finally getting him up against the ropes where he whips him off and on the rebound he catches him and hits a Belly to Belly Suplex tossing Jak into the corner where he rolls under the ropes as Baltimore turns his attention to Alioth who now starts to walk around him, the two ready to go at it as they go in for a lock up! The two start to battle it out and with ease Baltimore shows his immense size advantage as he pushes him back into the ropes and tosses him into it following it up with a elbow, and then another finally hitting the ropes and rushing at Starre looking to take his head off but as he throws the clothesline Starre ducks it and as his chest hits the corner post he bounces back and Starre rolls him up 1…Baltimore uses his size to kick out with ease sending Starre flying a bit. RILEY STEVENS: “Starre nearly snuck one in there.” They both hop to their feet as Jeremy wildly tosses another clothesline at Starre only for him to duck it again this time hitting him from behind with a Chop Block taking the six foot four man down his knee now having to be killing him as he holds it in pain rolling around, as Alioth has successfully taken away a bit of that strength advantage. Alioth goes down looking to lock in the ‘Four Star Mary’ early knowing he has Baltimore weakened but before he can get it locked in Jeremy uses his other leg to kick him away and as Alioth stumbles back a recovered Jak now pulls the ropes down sending Alioth falling through them to the outside. Hendricks now looks at Baltimore with a smirk as Jeremy struggles to get to his feet using the ropes to do so. Jak thinking he has this in the bag calls for the end as Baltimore limps off the ropes as Hendricks grabs him but Jeremy out of no where hoists him up over his shoulders and drops him down with a Scoop Powerslam as these fans go crazy as he stumbles back on his knee into the ropes however the damage being done to Hendricks. RILEY STEVENS: “Even with a bum knee Baltimore successfully hoists him up, amazing!” Trying to put weight down on it to recover; Baltimore continues to shake off the cob-webs as he walks forward grabbing up Hendricks seeming to throw caution to the wind as he waits for Jak to get up. But from behind he hears Alioth getting to up onto the apron so he turns around and tosses a big clothesline that knocks Alioth back down to the outside but as he turns around Hendricks delivers a kick to the hurt knee sending Baltimore back into the ropes as Hendricks throws every punch in his arsenal at Jeremy as he’s up against the ropes finally Baltimore tosses him away and as Hendricks gets back up he walks right into a Big Boot using his good leg to plant while the other does the damage, seeming to hurt himself a bit Jak gets right back up woozy stumbling right into Baltimore who amazingly despite the hurt knee hoists him up into the ‘Sudden Impact’ a Sitout Powerbomb! The crowd goes crazy 1…Alioth rolls back into the ring grabbing him and tossing him over the top rope as he jumps right to the top rope in a hurry jumping off with the Shooting Star Press, as he lands on the already hurt Jak 1…2…3! Baltimore tries to get back in but gets in too late. REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner…Alioth Starre!” These fans actually seem to feel a bit for Baltimore even though they are excited to see Starre win they are a little disappointed in the fashion he did it in but as Starre rolls out of the ring he seems to really just realize winning is all that really matters sometimes. RILEY STEVENS: “You got to admire what Baltimore did here tonight on a hurt knee he still managed to take down Jak before Starre came in and took it away from him.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “That’s what being a big game player is all about, winning.” Baltimore slams his hand down against the mat in anger clearly upset with how things went down as he seems to eye up Jak getting up as he goes to grab him with bad thoughts the anger with how he lost coming out as he goes to hoist him up but finally he tosses him down shaking his head as he turns his attention back to Starre at the top of the ramp clearly getting his anger under control as Starre grins as he heads to the back as the show goes on. WINNER: ALIOTH STARRE Cutting to the back, we see Daredevil talking to Matt Kluge in his office. DAREDEVIL: “Thanks for giving me a main event match tonight. I'm not going to let you down.” Kluge nodded and fiddled with a pencil. MATT KLUGE: “You deserve it, and let me tell you something...” At this point we zoom out to see Attack Attack listening through the cracked door. MATT KLUGE: “I got you a one on one match with Madison Seton next week in the main event. You're going to be a star here Adam, I know it.” Attack walked through the doorway now, obviously pissed off. ATTACK ATTACK: “Really, just because you two know each other, you're giving him an advantage? Why not me Kluge? I beat that guy in the open battle royal, and I didn't get anything.” Kluge rolled his eyes and crossed out of his desk to Attack. MATT KLUGE: “Look, it isn't anything against you. My job is to make sure I put together good matches week after week, and I know Devil here can I really wrestle a match. I don't know you well enough. However, it does tell me a lot about your personality that you think you're being screwed here.” Attack laughed and shook his head. ATTACK ATTACK: “I don't think. I know it when I see it. If this is how it is going to be, I'll just have to do something about it.” He exited, slamming the door, as we faded to Blackout hype package. QUOTE As the last segment ends we return to the ring with Sam Cantrell standing in the ring stretching out as his music fades and "Frontline" by Pillar hits to a loud amount of boo’s from the crowd as he walks out from the back with a big smirk on his face as Ariana Duroni walks out beside him the North Beach title still over his shoulder as the duo walks down towards the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Well I have to admit Daniel Starr looked great at ‘Moment of Glory III’ as the only champion that left with his gold. And now he looks to continue his amazing stretch the past few months.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Are you not going to introduce our lovely Legacy Champion, Aspen Chaud joining us at ringside?” As Starr and Duroni reach the half way point of the ramp the camera pans to the announce table where Aspen is seated in a black dress ready to announce her Legacy belt strapped over her shoulder. ASPEN CHAUD: “Thank you Oliver, you know I was supposed to be in this match but the other guy got himself taken out of the match so I thought these fans at home would enjoy it if I came down and watched the lesser champion, wrestle.” These fans in attendance spared listening to Aspen seem the lucky few however as of right now they continue to boo Daniel Starr as Duroni walks around to the side of the ring while Starr enters the ring walking across to the top rope hoisting up his North Beach title high in the air as he looks over at Aspen at ringside, tapping his own belt as he jumps down and hands the belt out to Duroni on the outside as the referee calls for the bell. DING DING DING! As the match starts Cantrell and Starr start to circle each other as they go in for a lock up but Daniel pushes him away telling him to wait with Sam confused Daniel slaps him across the face! These fans boo as Cantrell wildly throws a punch trying to get him back but Daniel ends up behind him giving him a big Hip Toss! These fans are clearly on the new guy Sam’s side but things do not seem to be starting well for him as he gets up as Daniel hits the ropes and then grabs his head and brings him down with a Leaping Bulldog from behind as he hops up, pointing at his face “The Face of GIW!” which as usual gets some boos from the crowd as Daniel looks over at Duroni with his belt and then calls for it to be over as the young rookie gets up clearly a little caught off guard with Starr’s quick offense. Backing up as Sam gets up he rushes at him and smacks him with the ‘Starrstruck’ a Brogue Kick! Boom Cantrell falls to the mat out of it as he decides to make things even bigger heading to the top rope looking over at Aspen as he does it and then jumps off with the Shooting Starr Press landing and making it look amazing in the process as he doesn’t even bother hooking the leg 1…2…3! REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner…Daniel Starr!” These fans boo as Daniel Starr gets up as Duroni walks up the steps into the ring with his belt handing it to him as she raises his arm in victory, yet another for him. ASPEN CHAUD: “Yay he beat a nobody.” RILEY STEVENS: “I don’t know, not sure anyone could beat Starr right now he has a lot of momentum going.” As Starr celebrates Aspen gets off the commentary booth grabbing a mic as she walks up the steps Starr and Duroni not realizing it as she steps into the ring fixing her dress as she gets in as Starr looks over at her the celebration stopping. ASPEN CHAUD: “I really hope you don’t feel great about beating that guy, whatever his name is. Because I just want to remind you that the order around here is that my belt, the Legacy belt is a bigger deal then that little title of yours. So if you don’t mind leaving the ring so that I can celebrate winning a belt that matters around here.” Aspen points him to the back but Starr shakes his head saying something to her back about his belt being the bigger deal as the three in the ring start to exchange words when the sound of Blake Beckett’s voice now fills the air as the three turn towards him at the top of the ramp. BLAKE BECKETT: “Now I am not going to get into a debate about whose belt means more, that all depends on what you two do with it. But Aspen I told you I was going to make your life hell and I wasn’t lying, and I found someone that wants to take you on pretty badly.” Aspen screams something at him dropping the mic down as Starr steps to the side as Duroni quickly rolls out of the ring with the belt as "Soul Wars" by Awolnation hits as Beck Ramsey comes out from the back as these fans go wild! Heading right towards the ring Aspen takes a moment to go ahead and bail out of the ring just as Beck gets into the ring as Chaud heads around the ring as Beck screams at her to get into the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Beck is here to take out Aspen!” Ramsey continues to yell at her to get back in here but with his back turned Starr looking to make a statement rushes forward crushing him from behind dropping him as he knees him in the gut and then the head over and over again finally pulling away as he screams at Duroni to toss him his title but as she throws it in Beck charges forward tackling him to the mat as the belt flops down beside them. Starr rolls over on top as the two exchange finally Beck kicks him off as Daniel now steps through the ropes. RILEY STEVENS: “Beck Ramsey has both champions running!” Duroni retrieves the belt walking around joining Starr who holds the back of his head as he walks up the ramp with her as not far higher up the ramp Aspen has her own belt looking from Beck in the ring to Blake who has a smirk on his face as Beck in the ring seems pumped to be back as he calls for either of them to come back, ready to fight them both. WINNER: DANIEL STARR Backstage we go before the huge Main Event later tonight as Madison Seton is seen making some last minute preparations for her match but as she looks up she sees Aspen Chaud who is still looking over her shoulder having just made it to the backstage area after the Beck incident. MADISON SETON: "That a old friend of yours?" Seton joked as she smiled up at her as Aspen glared at her angrily pretending to laugh. ASPEN CHAUD: “Funny. You won’t be laughing when Ryan takes that little belt off of you and you become just another one of those one month wonders with Alex and Alexis.” Aspen smirks feeling as though she stuck it to her as Seton hoists up her title just to rub it in her face a bit. MADISON SETON: "We’ll see about that." With that Seton heads to her match with Aspen scowling as we head to the ring. QUOTE As we reach the time for the Main Event these fans are excited and ready to go as “New God Flow” hits and the fans give a very bad reaction as out from the back comes Ryan Cavallari heading towards the ring with a very unwilling Attack Attack following him from behind. The two seeming to have zero love lost for one another as they head to the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “An odd pairing here” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “But they are dominant!.” Cavallari enters the ring quickly heading towards his corner as Attack heads up the steps following him in the two making no efforts to get on the same page as Ryan’s music trails off and "Party in the USA" by Miley Cyrus hits and the fans as is only natural begin to cheer as Madison Seton walks out with her Undisputed Championship at the top of the ramp as Daredevil walks out next to her. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Ugh. I just hate Seton so much.” The two head past their adoring fans as they head up the steps slipping into the ring with another heading up to a corner together both heading up side by side and presenting their belts as the fans continue to boo them as they to head to their corner. DING DING DING! As soon as the match starts it is already very clear who is going to be able to work together better as Seton and Devil high five as she heads into the ring looking across the ring at the other two who are having a bit harder time deciding, standing in the corner bickering a bit as Seton creeps towards them but Attack is oblivious to it, and though Ryan's supposed to be on her team he doesn’t seem to care much as he tells him to calm down as he steps out of the ring so as she turns around he eats a big Forearm shot from Seton sending him backwards as she follows it up with another one as Attack falls back into the ropes as Seton grabs her arm whipping her off the ropes. On the rebound Attack ducks under her clothesline attempt then hits the ropes this time as he bounces off she runs right into Seton who jumps up dropping down on him with a Thesz Press followed by Punches. The fans boo as Attack pushes her off but before she can get back up to her feet Seton kicks her back down. RILEY STEVENS: “Seton starting out strong.” With Attack holding his gut Seton takes the chance to play to the crowd a bit as Attack uses it to get back up to his feet. As he gets up Madison walks towards him but she throws a big punch to her stomach then another as he stands up dropping the champ back a bit. Attack now hops up to his feet rushing at her with a big dropkick that sends seton back into the corner holding onto the ropes to keep her footing. Attack backs up with a bit of a grin on his face as he rushes at Seton in the corner crushing her with a Clothesline. Seton drops down to a seated position as Attack backs up a bit looking over at Cavallari with a grin on his face as he turns around rushing at Seton in the corner where he nails her with a boot to the face in the corner. Seton seems dazed and confused as Attack pulls her to her feet, looking for a DDT only for the champ to spin out of it and use Attack’s hair to viciously slam him to the mat then stumbling back a bit a little shaken from attack’s offense. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Blatant hair pull there.” Seton walks back over to Attack who gets up holding his hair as Seton whips hum into her corner where she runs at him hitting a big Forearm to the face before tagging out to Devil. Devil steps in as Seton holds Attack in the corner before flipping him over to a seated position as Devil bounces off the ropes and hits a Shining Wizard. Seton exits the ring as Devil walks around him taunting the man he hates so much as the fans continue to cheer him, before going for a pin 1…Kick Out. OLIVER THIBODAUX: "No problem at all!" Devil pulled attack up and whipped him into the ropes, but Attack slid between his legs and connected with a dropout kick that sent them both flying. he climbed over and made the tag just as Seton was tagged in as well. Now it was Seton and Cavallari. They locked up with the entire fed watching them, Seton pushed him away and Cavallari bounced off the ropes with Thesz press of his own with a few punches. The ref pushed him off and seton tried a quick rollup...1.....2....KICKOUT. Cavallari was really about be finished there. Meanwhile, on the outside, Attack and Daredevil were trading blows back and forth. Seton kicked Cavallari in the gut and sent him rolling, then turned to the two battling on the outside. She was going to try a springboard something, and when she jumped on the ropes, Cavallari pulled her down, and very hard. He connected with his finisher, the Spike DDT, and 1...2....3.... she never saw it coming. REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here are your winners…Attack Attack and Ryan Cavallari!” The camera faded in on Seton, who nodding and clapping, seemingly impressed by Cavallari's quick move there. The show faded out on Attack and Cavallari celebrating. Those two were ready to meet one on one. WINNER: ATTACK ATTACK & RYAN CAVALLARI
