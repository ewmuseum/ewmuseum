GIW — Card

Revolt, 21 October 2010

21 October 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: Thursday Night Revolt! LIVE OCTOBER 21st, FROM SEAGULL AUDITORIUM IN GALVESTON, TEXAS BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! This is the final stop before the huge ‘On Hallowed Ground’ show that the world will be watching we pan around the fans who are screaming their heads off as we come back to the familiar faces of Riley and Oliver. RILEY STEVENS: “Well, welcome to the final Revolt before ‘On Hallowed Ground’ on the heels of David Sinclair accepting Amanda’s offer, we come in to tonight with many questions. Some being with their team coming in at five people and ours having four, who will Amanda recruit?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Not to mention three of our wrestlers will be fighting in the main event and we don’t even know yet if they can co-exist.” RILEY STEVENS: “Wait…something’s going on in the parking lot.” We cut away from Riley and Oliver to the parking lot where on the ground in pain is Kacie Church. In the background a car can be seen driving off as EMT’s coming running up to check on Kacie! She seems to be in terrible pain, as we head to a commercial break. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = We come back to the ring where already standing inside the ring to very little fanfare is Brock Charnel taunting to the crowd, who aren’t giving him much of a response. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Those losers from EPW came and attack Kacie!” RILEY STEVENS: “I don’t usually like to point fingers, but who else could it be? Regardless we have in ring action to worry about including the debut of this man…” “Seek and Destroy” by Metallica hits and the crowd looks excited to the stage where walking out is a beast of a man Cory Chevelle. The crowd all look on a bit taken back by the size of this man, he’s gigantic. He walks down to the ring nodding at the fans who seem a little enamored by him. RILEY STEVENS: “That’s one big dude…” Cory steps into the ring looking over at Brock and then heading to the top rope holding up his arms before jumping down stretching out his arms ready to get this fight started as the referee calls for the bell. DING DING DING! Brock walks across the ring taunting to the fans, then over to Cory Chevelle yelling something at him. Looking at Chevelle you wouldn’t see why anyone would want to mess with such a man. However Brock keeps it up getting into his face and talking some more trash. He turns back walking over to the other side of the ring, still seeming pretty confident. Chevelle looks to the crowd with a smile and then as Charnel turns he charges and crushes him with a ‘Bloodlust’ a GIGANTIC Spear! The crowd goes nuts as he hops to his feet and pounds his chest as Charnel rolls around on the mat in clear pain. The crowd cheers him on as Charnel stumbles to his feet doubled over holding his chest in pain, however he walks right into the grasps of the giant Cory Chevelle who brings him up and crushes him with a ‘The Silverback Brainbuster’ another huge move a Brainbuster that more then likely knocks Charnel out. The fans cheer as Cory hooks the leg and gets the pin 1…2…3! That’s how you make a debut. RILEY STEVENS: “The Silverback has arrived!” The crowd cheers him on as he gets to his feet with the referee holding his arm up. He turns around and looks out at the crowd, with his arms up. Suddenly there is a bit of a loud rise in the crowd as a figure jumps over from the crowd and into the ring with a steel chair. The man is none other then Paul Defias a wrestler who was signed to EPW awhile back and seems to have made his return. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Who is that?” He crushes Cory in the back with a steel chair bringing Chevelle to a knee. He crushes him with another shot to the back. The fans boo him as he then rolls out of the ring and jumps out into the crowd. However the fans roar as Cory Chevelle gets back to his feet just slightly showing effects of the attack, as he looks out his attacker glaring at him as we wonder why Defias chose the rookie. And then we begin to realize that, it’s becoming increasingly clear that EPW is here. WINNER: CORY CHEVELLE = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = We head to the backstage area where a stressed Amanda Rowland is pacing back and forth in her office watching the TV as the door opens up and in walks Alexii Ravynova. Amanda immediately glares at her and doesn’t even give her time to close the door before getting angry at her. AMANDA ROWLAND: “What happened last week? You were suppose to make sure nobody got in. And now I found out that Kacie Church is injured which means we are down two members. Then did you just see what happened? One of EPW guys just attacked our newest acquisition.” Alexii has a bit of a smile on her face as she lets Amanda pour it all out, with a smirk beginning to respond to her. ALEXII RAVYNOVA: "Calm down, last week was a fluke. I took my eyes off the ball for just a second and they got by me, that won’t happen again. You see they let one guy slip by; I’ve got things under control. In fact I doubled the amount of security from last week. You can calm your little self, because I’ve got things under control." Alexii smiles and then pats her on the back, before heading on out. Amanda shakes her head biting her nails a bit still clearly not at ease with the situation. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = We come back to the ring where the fans have been watching as Chicken has been slowly setting up the ring with four eggs in the corners they’re all huge and actually seem pretty lifelike. Chicken smiles as he sets them up until suddenly "Nightmare" by A7X hits and the crowd begins to boo as out walks Joshua Acquin angrily stomping his way towards the ring, jumping on in as he stares over at Chicken and then hops up into the ring glaring at the ever pestering Chicken. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “This Chicken thing is so awful...” RILEY STEVENS: “I’m just wondering what is in the eggs.” Joshua stares over at the Chicken as he inspects the eggs until "I Will Not Bow" by Breaking Benjamin hits and the crowd again boos as this time its Jake Kennedy who walks out he walks to the ring just as angrily as Joshua did. The fans boo him as he enters the ring he to angry at the Chicken. RILEY STEVENS: “What are the rules of this match?” Chicken begins to explain the match up to both of them, both men seeming not to even be listening as Chicken finishes and calls for the bell. DING DING DING! Rather then starting to fight its Jake Kennedy and Joshua Acquin who begin to corner Chicken, their anger for the pest coming out. Chicken backs himself into the corner as the fans boo them. However Jake suddenly grabs Joshua and tosses him right into the egg cracking it and spilling…actual yoke all over him! The fans cheer not really happy about Jake doing it but just loving seeing Joshua covered in yoke. Suddenly Chicken pulls Jake away and declares him the winner by raising his hand. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Maybe we deserve to let our company die…” The fans boo Jake Kennedy’s victory however they begin to boo even more when Kennedy slaps Chicken’s hand away and then grabs him and throws him into another egg on the other side, he gets yoked as well. The fans boo as Jake Kennedy grabs the ION Television title and holds it proudly as he heads into the title match with all the momentum as we head to a break. WINNER: JAKE KENNEDY = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = Whether or not we are supposed to see this remains unknown however we suddenly cut to the parking lot where David Sinclair is currently sitting in a big SUV. Paul Defias the man that attacked Cory earlier tonight walks out near him. DAVID SINCLAIR: “What in the world do you think your doing? I’m pretty sure the plan was clear from the start and you attacking someone was not in the plan. Get back with everyone else, before I make this worse for you.” Defias shakes his head and heads back; not figuring arguing is worth it. However we spot Bret Battery a man who was absolutely destroyed on Evolve wearing some bandages. He is standing near his brother, when suddenly a reaches down and picks up a letter. He lifts it up and begins to tear it open we see a “10-31-10” on one side. As he begins to read it out loud. BRET BATTERY: "I’m chosen? For a match at ‘On Hallowed Ground’?” Battery looks over at his younger brother Bryan, who shrugs as Bret then looks over at Sinclair who basically kicked him off Team EPW. BRET BATTERY: "He’s on!" Battery throws the letter away as it falls to the ground as the cameraman zooms in on the date on the back as we take a short break. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = We come back after the break with Liviana Valentine standing in the ring with a big smile on her face as most of the fans have seemingly forgotten who she is, or still don’t care. “Psycho” by System of a Down hits as the fans begin to boo as DareDevil walks out from the back, with a big smile on his face showing off them pearly whites. He shows himself off a bit as he walks down towards the ring, the fans still jeering him the entire time. RILEY STEVENS: “This man made his debut made his debut last week and impressed a lot of people.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “This guy is the future right here, he’s got it all!” DareDevil steps into the ring walking over by Liviana who seems to be unable to take her eyes off of him, he smirks at her and then heads to the top rope. As he’s up there “Havenless” by Enslaved hits and the fans go quiet still a few booing him. Out from the back walks the ever intimidating Thor Vegard. He wastes little time walking down to the ring, as even his teammates seem a bit in awe of him. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “What a great idea, lets book the meanest guy in the company against the people that are set to fight for the company.” Thor walks over between his teammates who both stare up at him, as he readies himself to fight quite an odd team there for sure. “Fatlip” by Sum 41 hits and out from the back comes not only Eric Sparrow but Tony Miranda and Aspen Chaud. Sparrow leads the charge with his big smirk as Tony shakes his head not seeming to want to be doing this at all. RILEY STEVENS: “Coming out as a team, I like it. Though I assume Sparrow only agreed if it was him leading with his music.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Sounds like a corny Aspen idea to me.” Aspen begins high fiving all the fans at the ring side as Sparrow heads up the steps. The fans are mostly cheering them, after initially assuming to boo due to the music. Miranda then heads up the step bending down and pulling up the second rope as Aspen goes underneath it and then Miranda follows her in, both teams preparing to fight Sparrow demanding that he starts it off as the bell rings. DING DING DING! Aspen and Tony step to the outside and then Sparrow turns around however it’s soon agreed upon on the other side that Thor Vegard will start. Sparrow looks up to him and then backs up and tags Tony on the chest then hops out. Miranda looks at him shaking his head and then steps into the ring ready to fight with this gigantic man. The two go in to a lock up that Miranda at first seems to be winning. He begins to back him up, before suddenly getting thrown across the ring by Vegard. Tony immediately hops up nodding his head as the fans begin to cheer him on as the two meet up in the center of the ring however Thor is caught off guard with a big punch from Tony. The fans cheer as Miranda delivers another one, before finally hitting a big Dropkick! That sends Thor to the ropes with a little weakness shown for the first time. Tony gets to his feet and screams out to the crowd then runs off the ropes, and goes for a clothesline however Thor ducks down and throws him over him with. The fans cheer however as Tony lands on the ring apron and crushes Vegard with a punch! The fans cheer as Tony gets back into the ring and runs across the ring going for a big clothesline however he runs right into the longer arm of Thor! The fans boo as Tony falls to the mat, reeling a bit. Vegard looks back to him pulling him up to his feet, however catches him with a Yakuza Kick out of no where. The fans cheer as Thor falls to one knee as Tony then connects with a Spinning Roundhouse Kick! That lands directly on Thor’s face sending him to the mat. The fans cheer as Tony goes for the cover 1…He’s thrown off with ease. Tony begins a slow clap with the fans as he waits for Thor to get to his feet, seeming ready for something big. Vegard gets to his feet however when Tony tries to go in for a move the big men pushes him away and then stumbles bag into a tag from DareDevil who jumps up over the ropes and then connects with a big Dropkick to Miranda as he’s charging. The fans boo him as he taunts them a bit as he waits for Tony to get to his feet. When he’s up he receives a Snap DDT, weakening Miranda a bit more. As the fans continue to cheer him on as Tony makes the pin 1…Kick Out! Not close yet, Tony won’t give up. RILEY STEVENS: “He still needs to make the tag.” The fans boo as DareDevil lifts him up to his feet, however in an act of desperation Tony connects with a Dropkick! The fans cheer as Aspen begins to call for a tag on the outside wanting in this thing. Tony begins to crawl towards his corner, as DareDevil gets up. Liviana yells at him to stop the tag as he runs over trying to stop Tony however he reaches out and makes the tag however it’s Sparrow that gets the tag first. He jumps up onto the ropes and when DareDevil realizes it it’s a bit too late as Sparrow jumps off with a Springboard kick to the chest that sends him to the mat. Sparrow in perhaps an act of just momentum runs across the ring dropkicking Thor’s knees sending him falling to the outside in pain. Sparrow runs back to DareDevil and attempts to whip him off the ropes but DareDevil counters and whips him instead. However amazingly Sparrow jumps up over the ropes and to the ring apron. Out of no where DareDevil distracts the referee as Thor Vegard pulls Sparrow down before he can try a big move. Sparrow however with the referee not being able to see kicks up and nails Thor with a big low blow! The fans are actually a bit behind him. As he stands over Thor triumphantly however he doesn’t realize that in the ring DareDevil has just run off the ropes and then jumps through them with a Suicide Dive that takes Sparrow out! The fans are a bit impressed finding all of this to be pretty exciting. Liviana hops down coming over to aid her partner DareDevil. The fans boo as she helps him back into the ring, seems like Liviana and DareDevil have made a good partnership. Aspen comes around the ring trying to help Sparrow up now. However unknown to her is Thor getting up, angry after the low blow. He grabs her and begins to turn her around however it’s Tony that comes to her aid, spearing him to the ground bringing more cheers from the crowd. Aspen seems very thankful as she then finishes the job and helps Sparrow into the ring. Sparrow gets up and goes after DareDevil only to run into a vicious Jawbreaker. The fans seem a bit disappointed as he falls to the mat seeming out of it after the last two moves. Both Thor and Tony are still fighting a bit on the outside as DareDevil heads to the top rope. The fans wait in anticipation as he jumps off for a Corkscrew Senton at the last second Liviana tags in, however Aspen yells out something to Sparrow and he rolls out of the way. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “That’s going to leave a mark!” DareDevil flops around holding his butt as he rolls out of the ring in pain as Liviana hops into the ring, all while Sparrow makes the tag to Aspen. Both girls jump into the ring however it’s Aspen that connects with a Thesz Press Followed with Punches! The fans roar as Liviana pushes her off and both girls get to there feet in a hurry. However it’s Aspen that tosses her into the corner then follows it up with a ‘Tush Push’ a Turnbuckle Flying Butt Shot! The fans cheer as Liviana stumbles out and she heads to the top rope. On the outside we see a downed Tony after a big boot from Thor. With Liviana stunned she goes for an ‘AirBarbie’ a Flying Crossbody that connects right into a pin 1…2…Kick Out! The fans are loving this as Aspen waits for her to get up looking for her finisher. However Liviana lands a stray elbow and then jumps forward tagging in Thor Vegard! The fans boo as he steps into the ring, now facing the much smaller Aspen Chaud. He walks over to her staring down at her as she looks up at him, realizing there’s no where to run. She begins slugging away at him doing little damage. She runs off the ropes and then nails him with a Dropkick; she falls to the mat hard he simply stands tall. When she gets to her feet he has a big hold of her, hitting her with an almost mega Hip Toss. The fans boo as Aspen holds her back in pain she’s trapped. However it’s Eric Sparrow to the rescue as he heads up to the top rope, trying to come to her aid. He jumps off at him however he just receives an in air Headbutt! The fans boo realizing Thor has all the advantage here as he again turns his attention to Aspen, as Eric has rolled out of the ring. Aspen is cornered in the corner however the fans cheer as its Tony now that steps into the ring. The referee tries to get him out however Thor stands tall challenging him to do his best move. Tony runs across the ring leveling him with a clothesline however it’s Aspen on her hands and knees behind him that sends Thor falling to the mat hard, Tony has taken the big man off his feet. RILEY STEVENS: “What teamwork!” The fans cheer as Tony mounts him and then begins to blow him up with punches before being shoved off by Thor. Liviana jumps up into the ring and walks right into a trip from Aspen as she locks in the K.M.A.! Lastly it’s DareDevil and Sparrow who decide why not and slide into the ring and start to exchange punches. The referee has lost control as all hell is going on in the ring as he instead decides to call for the bell. RILEY STEVENS: “No Contest?!” The fans boo wanting to let them go however there is suddenly a silence as the fans see at the top of the ramp a large amount of security walking out being lead by both Blake Beckett and David Sinclair. Inside the ring Aspen sees it and let’s go of Liviana who rolls out of the ring in pain. Aspen runs across to DareDevil and Sparrow and pull them apart and then does the same with Thor and Tony. From the crowd comes the Evolve roster as nearly everyone expected. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “They’re stranded!” The fans boo as the stars in the ring realize the trouble they are in. Bret Battery and Bryan Battery are the first one in as they corner Sparrow. Santa Claus…even Santa Claus is here sliding into the ring helping out Evolve. Next in is Travis Tate, Chris Armstrong, Jack Prowler, and Christopher Darren Erns the team for EPW! The fans boo as they begin to massacre those in the ring. Both Battery’s get Sparrow to the mat and begin to deliver punches to him. RILEY STEVENS: “Someone’s got to help them.” Aspen is the victim of a clothesline from behind by Paul Defias. Erns and Tate begin to double up on the big Thor Vegard. Prowler connects with a Running Bulldog on DareDevil as Aspen’s tossed into a ‘Money Maker’ a Top Rope Powerbomb from Chris Armstrong! The crowd continues the boos watching as their stars are massacred. Alabaster catches an off guard Tony Miranda with a ‘Murderous Intent’ a Brain Buster! The fans suddenly begin to cheer as the huge debuting Cory Chevelle slams through the security that Blake and David have set up to stop anyone from helping. He runs down towards the ring and when Defias turns he receives a gut crushing ‘Bloodlust’ a Spear! RILEY STEVENS: “Here comes the cavalry!” Out from the back comes The Shadow, Chicken, Jake Kennedy, and Joshua Acquin! Enemies trying to help their team. Shadow bulldozes through the security while the other three have a bit harder time. Back down at the bottom the Battery brothers have just throw him into the steel ring post taking him down. Back in the ring Thor Vegard continues to fight until he’s taken to the mat by Jack Prowler, Chris Armstrong, Christopher Darren Erns, and Travis Tate who have done a number on him. Suddenly we cut to Wake Walker who is standing by in the crowd watching as all of this happens the fans pleading with him to get in and fight for GIW. RILEY STEVENS: “Wake Walker needs to come fight for the company!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “He’s a traitor it’s simple Riley.” When Acquin, Kennedy, and Chicken get down to the ring they are caught with similar consequences being brought down almost immediately by the much more thought out Evolve Roster. The fans boo as Beckett and Sinclair walk down to the ring, looking on at the carnage the roster has left. They slide into the ring and then slide in between their guys raising their teams hands the four that will represent them at the PPV as the rest of the Evolve roster sticks out of the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “They just crushed us, is this the end of Revolt as we know it? Or will our guys find a way to win?” We cut back to Wake Walker looking over his shoulder as he looks on at the four men he’ll be teamed with alongside his bosses in the ring as the entire Revolt roster has been laid to waste here tonight as the show comes to a slow close with EPW standing tall. WINNER: NO CONTEST
