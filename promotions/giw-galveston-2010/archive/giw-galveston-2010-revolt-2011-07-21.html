GIW — Card

Revolt, 21 July 2011

21 July 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: THURSDAY NIGHT REVOLT LIVE! From North Beach – July 21st, 2011 BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! As the show starts the crowd goes wild with cheers as we head around the North Beach tent where the fans are going insane for the show here tonight, the opening pyro finishes as we head to the top of the ramp as “Now I'm That Bitch” by Livvi Franc hits as the crowd begins to boo out from the back comes Aspen Chaud the crowd booing her though she walks with a whole lot of confidence. The crowd is as per usual absolutely giving it to her with boos as she walks up the steps and into the ring wearing a black dress naturally having the night off, she yells at Jamal to get her a mic and he does handing it in to her as she grabs it with a smirk on her face. ASPEN CHAUD: “Welcome everyone to Aspen Island Wrestling! You see I am in charge here tonight and that’s why you’ve been given such a treat for the show. Unfortunately you will not get to see the technical gifts of yours truly. But tonight features tag team champions…Leo James and Matt Kluge fighting each other in a Submission Match. If that wasn’t enough you’ll also get to see Trent Lost in a Tables Match with The Shadow in what should surely continue his losing streak. Then in the main event it’ll be Mark Cross and Alioth Starre in a Singles Match with ME as the Special Referee.” Aspen grins raising her hand as though this should please the fans as they instead boo her, upset that the rematch was slightly ruined by her presence. ASPEN CHAUD: “I don’t know what is wrong with Blake and his family, but who really cares? I AM IN CHARGE! And I will continue to run this company the best that I can. Enjoy your night…” Aspen’s music hits as the fans boo her as she leaves, heading towards the back waving to the crowd as though they love her. RILEY STEVENS: “If that wasn’t an ego trip I don’t know what one is. Clearly she just wanted to remind us who was in charge this week.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Oh come on Riley she was just telling the fans what was on deck for tonight. A great night of matches I might add.” RILEY STEVENS: “Yeah for Aspen they certainly should be good. We’ll be right back after these commercial break with the first match.” The camera cuts to Aspen turning at the top of the ramp waving to the crowd as she heads to the back as the fans continue to boo her and we go to break. QUOTE When we return to the ring the crowd immediately erupts when "The Mob Goes Wild" by Clutch hits as out from the back comes the team of Hank Mardukas, David Graves, and Johnny Scar a threesome of guys that had certainly gained favor with the crowd. RILEY STEVENS: “These three guys have all been hot of late; Scar last week defeated Jones easily and Mardukas and Graves have faired well the last few weeks heading towards their Seize the Moment confrontation.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Yeah I am not buying their little respect thing they got going on.” As the three enter the ring heading towards their corner the fans cheering them on, “Nightmare” by Avenged Sevenfold now hits as the crowd gives a mixed reaction as out from the back comes Logan Abbott and Seth Seeker heading towards the ring both seeming focused. RILEY STEVENS: “These men enter with a one man disadvantage due to Unforgettable Jones being released from GIW mid-week due to a confrontation with La’Sarah Audric.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I heard the guy was hoping to run the fed…never will we see a black man in charge.” Riley can be heard sighing as the two head to their corner not showing any reason to believe they fear their odds as the referee calls for the bell. DING DING DING! The crowd is ready to go as our the men as Seth Seeker steps into the ring for his team while Johnny Scar does the same for his team. The two begin to circle one another and Scar whom is no stranger to big men, goes right after him only to be met with a big blow from Seeker. Grabbing him Seeker whips him off the ropes, Scar bounces off and ducks under Seeker’s clothesline attempt then runs off the ropes this time jumping up for a Hurricanrana! The crowd cheers however he is caught by Seth in a very precarious position as Seeker puts him down with a huge Powerbomb that shakes the ring 1…2…Kick Out! The fans cheer as Seeker seems a bit angered by the kick out thinking such a move would end it. Smartly Scar starts to crawl towards his corner only for Seeker to grab his foot. Scar gets up balancing on one foot then jumps up with a big Enzuigiri! The crowd goes wild as Seeker stumbles back into the ropes as Johnny jumps up and tags in Hank Mardukas! The crowd goes wild as he runs in showing off his strength with a huge Clothesline to Seeker sending him over the ropes and out of the ring. Logan sensing his teammate in trouble runs the apron to try to help only for Hank to catch him with a punch that knocks him back down with Seeker. Hank turns and directs traffic telling Graves to get in, as he does setting up by the ropes. The fans go wild as he waits for Seeker and Abbott to get up. Finally when they do he runs off the ropes and flies through the middle ropes and right into both men the crowd going wild as the referee tries to get control. However they are not done as soon as Graves gets up Scar heads to the top rope. Seeker and Abbott stare up at him as he jumps up with a Crossbody to both men as the crowd goes insane for all this high flying action. RILEY STEVENS: “Look at these three all on the same page, great high flying action.” The crowd is slamming on the barricades in excitement as the referee realizes he’s lost all control as Scar and Graves together lift up Seeker and toss him back into the ring heading to their corner perhaps having taken the big man out. Hank sets up in the corner looking for the finish as he runs at him going for the ‘Pit Bull Attack’ a Charging Shoulder Block but at the last moment Seth is able to avoid it. This sends Hank into the turnbuckle bouncing right off into a German Suplex from Seeker! The crowd is stunned that after all that outside stuff, Seeker is still able to go. He bounces up and tags in Logan who jumps into the ring with confidence. Not letting Hank get to his corner, he whips him into a neutral corner running at him and delivering a big Corner Clothesline! The fans boo Logan who proceeds to taunt them before putting Mardukas down with a Big Boot then a pin attempt 1…2…Kick Out! Getting up Logan walks to the referee arguing with him, angrily going back at Hank however and delivering a DDT this time making a pin with a hooked leg 1…2…Scar kicks him out. The fans cheer for this as Logan gets up and instead of worrying about Hank he goes after Scar throwing him into the corner and going for some punches as the referee is watching him Seth enters the ring pulling Hank back into their corner. RILEY STEVENS: “That distraction allowed Seeker to pull him to the corner.” Abbott pulls off as he goes back over towards Hank taunting him as the referee gets Scar out of there. But as he taunts him Hank catches him with a punch, Seth uses his long arms to make the tag into Abbott as Mardukas delivers a Spinebuster to him. Trying for a pin Hank is caught off guard when the referee tells him that he’s not legal as Seeker catches him with a Spear! The crowd boos as Seth goes for the pin 1…2…Kick Out! The fans seem shocked as does Seth as he gets up pulling Hank up and with his never give up attitude Hank delivers a mean Powerslam out of no where! The crowd goes insane as Hank stumbles to his corner and on his last gas prepares for another ‘Pit Bull Attack’. But as he is prepping Graves makes a surprise tag in. The crowd seems a bit shocked as does Mardukas who stops him to talk about it. The two however waste too much time as Seeker runs at them nailing both with a Double Clothesline! The crowd boos as Mardukas stumbles out of the ring. Abbott takes the chance to run in, running across the ring and nailing Scar off the turnbuckle. Graves who took less of the brunt of the Clothesline gets up, stumbling towards Seeker and walking right into Seth’s ‘Don't Fear The Reaper’ a Jackhammer, that puts him out 1…2…3! That is it. RILEY STEVENS: “Some miscommunication from Graves and Mardukas leads to a win from the undermanned team.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Surprise, surprise.” The crowd doesn’t seem happy however Abbott does telling the referee to hold up his arm in victory all while Seeker exits the ring not wanting to share his victory with another man as we head to a break. WINNER: LOGAN ABBOTT & SETH SEEKER As soon as we return just reaching the area behind the curtain we see David Graves walking back into Hank Mardukas who doesn’t look too happy. HANK MARDUKAS: “What the hell was that?” Hank points out to the ring for an explanation as Graves breathing deeply and holding his neck replies. DAVID GRAVES: “It was a mistake, I thought I could finish him off. I didn’t think you’d stop me…” HANK MARDUKAS: “I was about to finish him off and win for us, what part of that don’t you get?” Graves shakes his head seeming genuinely just as upset about this as Hank however seeming to snap a bit. DAVID GRAVES: “Who made you the Captain of our team? I thought you were gassed and I was trying to get in and end it. That’s all.” Graves and Mardukas seem to have a little stare down however Hank holds out his hand. HANK MARDUKAS: “You know what we both made a mistake; let’s not let this come between us.” Graves takes a few deep breaths extending his hand and shaking Hank’s hand both men heading their own way seeming to have put an end to this little argument. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = Heading to the hotel area we have Matt Kluge and Leo James both with their tag titles laying near by stretching out and getting ready for their match as Kluge stands up looking over at him. MATT KLUGE: “Listen man when I make you tap, just know that it doesn’t mean anything…other then that I am better then you.” James lets out a laugh in reply realizing his sarcasm as he looks over at him and replies. LEO JAMES: “When I put you in that Goldie Lock, you’ll be wishing you’d teamed with someone else.” James and Kluge share a laugh however it ends when walking in staring at both men is Demon Night a member of Faces of Death. DEMON NIGHT: “Good luck tonight…Know that I’ll be watching. Because I don’t know if you watched our match earlier but the Faces of Death, will be getting those belts sooner rather then later.” Night heads on his way as Kluge and James look at one another seeming a bit befuddled by the random intruder putting them on alert. QUOTE Returning from break the crowd cheers as “Pain” by Four-Star Mary hits as out from the back comes Amber Stone with a big smile on her face running down the ramp high fiving people all along the way. RILEY STEVENS: “This will be the third Seize the Moment Qualifier with the winner joining Hank Mardukas and David Graves in the contest. First entering is Amber Stone whom the fans love.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Yay, a ditzy blonde!” Amber slides into the ring heading to the top rope and posing for the crowd as her music cuts off in favor of "Gasoline" Porcelain and the Tramps which turns the fans around into boos as Katya Rose walks out from the back with a scowl rather then the cheeky grin of Amber. RILEY STEVENS: “Last week these two met in ETV action and many have claimed Katya stole the victory from Amber, this should settle the debate.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “This is my type of girl right here.” Rose heads up the steps and into the ring glaring out at the crowd then in at Stone as she steps through the ropes heading to the corner of the ring demanding the match be started. DING DING DING! The male fans in attendance can be heard whistling as Amber walks to the middle of the ring holding out a hand for a shake. Rose walks forward looking at it, then up at Amber having a little laugh at her expense. Stone seems very adamant that she shakes however a big slap from Katya puts an end to that as she follows it up by throwing Stone off the ropes. On the rebound she runs right into a big Hip Toss from Katya who takes a second to taunt her before running off the ropes. The posing allows Amber to roll under Rose who runs off the ropes and on the rebound runs right into a feisty Stone who catches her with a big punch. The crowd cheers her on, loving the spunky Stone as Katya runs at her only to be met with a Arm Drag right into a Arm Bar sending the fans into a loud cheer thinking she may end this. However the move landed Katya close enough to the ropes for her to grab one with her opposite hand 1…Stone quickly releases the hold. The fans aid her with cheers as she gets up screaming for Rose to get up so she can go back on the offensive. Katya shakes her hand trying to shake out the pain as she turns and walks into a boot from Amber then right into a Scoop Slam and pin cover 1…2…Kick Out! The fans all let out a sigh, as Rose gets back up not nearly done falling into the corner. Holding her arm she asks Amber to wait claiming to be hurt. Being too nice Amber falls for it and as she stops, Rose bounces forward with a big kick to the stomach that she transitions into an Implant DDT! The fans booing her actions as she makes the cover 1…2…Kick Out! RILEY STEVENS: “Rose lures her in and it turns the tides.” Amber crawls to the corner trying to get out of harms way however Katya follows her squishing her head against the bottom turnbuckle with her foot as the referee counts, 1…2…3…4…She releases it at the last second as Amber gets to her feet rushing at her trying for a last second Tackle attempt but she walks into a kick from Rose that sets her right up for a Jawbreaker! The fans boo as she then transitions this right into a Standing Moonsault which the fans admittedly seem impressed by as she hooks the leg 1…2…Kick Out. This time Katya gets up angry with the referee for not counting at a reasonable pace. Shaking her head she turns and lifts up Stone going for a Northern Lights Suplex but Stone is able to push her away and hit a Roundhouse Kick out of no where! Rose falls to the mat as does Amber both tired. Katya starts to use the ropes to get to her feet as Stone gets up seemingly with only one last move in her. She reaches for Katya’s back going for it as she tries a Double Knee Backbreaker but Rose holds onto the ropes. Amber slams back, into the mat. Slowly getting up holding her head a little dazed she walks right into Rose who lifts her up and catches here with a picture perfect ‘Agent Provocateur’ her version of the Go 2 Sleep that puts Stone out as she makes the quick cover 1…2…3! She qualifies for Seize the Moment. RILEY STEVENS: “Well it appears we have found who really was the best, but no shame for Stone who fought well.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Yeah but she’s not qualifying for Seize the Moment. Maybe it’s just her looks getting to me, but I am pulling for Katya.” Rose stands over Stone for a moment shoving the win in her face as she slaps her hand and has the referee raise it. Looking down at Stone she purposely walks over her and heads towards the back adding her list to competitors in the Seize the Moment match. WINNER: KATYA ROSE (Qualifies for Seize the Moment) After the match we see Katya Rose heading towards the back as Leo James is heading towards the ring with his belt walking right towards her. LEO JAMES: “Good job out there.” Letting out a huge sigh Rose stops staring at him. KATYA ROSE: “I suppose you want me to sign your little replica belt?” James lets out a big chuckle at the confusion leaving Rose both annoyed and confused. LEO JAMES: “Actually I am one half of the GIW Tag Team Champions. I just learned I’m a participant in the Seize the Moment match as well. So good luck…” James seems sincere with this however all Rose can do is chuckle a bit. KATYA ROSE: “Luck? Really? If you’re all the challenge I have I think that I’m as good as a shoe in.” Rose waves and heads on her way as Leo shakes his head realizing that he’s now 0-2 when it comes to confrontations with women backstage as he heads to the ring. QUOTE “El Dorado” by Iron Maiden blasts over the Public Address system and out comes Leo James. The Golden Lion comes walking out to a nice pop, he slaps hands with the fans as me makes his way into the ring. He has a smirk knowing the reasoning behind this match. RILEY STEVENS: “Leo does not seem too worried about this match that Aspen conjured up to try and tear American Gold apart.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Lets wait until the match starts to decide on that.” “Battle Metal” by Turnisas cuts everything off as out steps Matt Kluge. The rebellious American Tiger gets a loud cheer from the crowd. He climbs into the ring and offers his hand to Leo James. They both shake hands and as soon as the bell rings both men start hammering each other with forearms and elbows! RILEY STEVENS: “There goes any thoughts that they wouldn’t fight.” Matt is the smaller but more feisty competitor, for every stronger strike Matt hits two. They go into a collar and elbow tie up and start punching, Leo rears back and head butts Matt. Dazed Matt backs away and Leo tries to hit a Belly to Belly Suplex but Matt switches around. Matt hits him with a mat slam and then goes into a spiral ride, spiraling to Leo’s head Matt drops knees to his skull. Leo catches hold of one leg and drives foreword grabbing Matt‘s other leg. Putting Matt into a Alabama Slam position. Matt pushes off Leo’s calves rolling into a Sunset flip, he stands up and goes into a Superman punch. Leo catches Matt with a body scissors and rolls over putting himself into his own mount. Leo punches Matt a few times before Matt catches one punch and turns it into a triangle choke. Leo simply picks Matt up and Powerbomb’s him. RILEY STEVENS: “What a war!” Leo rolls Matt over and stomps his back. He picks Matt up only for Matt to catch him in a Cravate. Matt holds Leo down and knees his head a few times before pulling him back and dropping him across his knee. Matt climbs the turnbuckle. Matt leaps for a Flying Crossbody but Leo catches him. He flips him over his Shoulder for a Goldie Lock but Matt drops him with a Crucifix Driver. He gets up and shouts that he meant to do that. Leo makes his way up and Irish whips Matt. Leo throws up a Big Boot but Matt sidesteps and rams him with the Matt Cooke! Leo goes down hard, but Matt stumbles to his own knees. Matt hooks uses a Bow and Arrow lock to weaken Leo’s back but he reaches the ropes. Matt tries to Suplex him but Leo doesn’t budge, Leo picks Matt up and throws him from the ring, but Matt flips out and onto the apron. Leo turns around and is met with a Springboard Shoulder block. RILEY STEVENS: “What happens when you let two friends go at it? This.” Matt scrapes his boot across Leo’s face. The ref calls for Matt to stop and Leo makes his way up. Matt puts him in a Double Underhook and tries to hit a Rude Awakening but Leo breaks it and flips Matt over with a German Suplex. He lifts him up again, but Matt elbows him repeatedly. Finally Matt tries to Pele Kick Leo but Leo catches him and rolls it into a Grapevine Ankle Lock! Matt gets on his hands and good knee, he somersaults throwing Leo onto his stomach. Matt locks in a Rat Trap but Leo breaks it. Both men get up and trade some blows again. Matt kicks Leo hard and hit’s a DDT. Leo Rolls Matt into a Boston Crab but Matt kicks him away. Matt tries to throw him with an Belly To Belly but Leo has the leverage and throw him over. Leo stomps Matt and drops an elbow on him. He climbs the turnbuckle hitting Matt with a Double Axe Handle as he stands up. Matt uses the ropes to get up and Leo tries for a German Suplex. Matt holds on to the ropes for dear life. Finally he springs on the middle rope bouncing onto Leo’s shoulders. He rolls Leo out and over the ropes. RILEY STEVENS: “Here we go!” Matt goes back into the ring and charges off the far ropes running off with a Plancha. Matt makes his way up and picks Leo up for a Powerslam. He runs into the turnbuckle before slamming Leo down. He hits some rabbit punches to Leo as he makes his way up. Matt climbs the ring but Leo clotheslines Matt’s back. Leo climbs the apron. Both wrestlers fight each other on the sides. Leo elbows Matt; Matt kicks Leo in the side. Leo picks up Matt and Urnages him into the apron! Leo gets into the ring as Matt struggles to get up. Matt makes his way into the ring but Leo Hot Shots him. Leo runs off into a Golden Claw but Matt pulls the rope down hanging Leo on it. Matt grabs the hung arm and bends it across the ring ropes. The ref breaks the hold. Matt tries for a Tiger Roar Gold Edition but Leo rolls out of it. He lifts him for a Goldie Lock but Matt escapes as well. They both go into a Test of Might. Leo pushes Matt down but with a surge Matt Leo and drops him with an Exploder. He climbs the turnbuckle for a Leg Drop and hits it. He tries for another Rat Trap. He has it locked but Leo reaches the ropes. Matt tries for another Suplex but Leo hit’s a High Knee causing Matt to bleed from the nose. Matt responds by hitting Leo hard in the face with a elbow, causing Leo to bleed from the nose. Matt then fishhooks pulling Leo to the ground. Leo stand up and hit’s a Electric Chair Drop. Leo lifts Matt up and Deadlifts him into a Slam. Leo goes for a Big Swing, he let’s Matt go, sending him careening into the turnbuckle! Matt starts coughing hard. RILEY STEVENS: “That looks like it did some serious damage.” Leo runs in and kicks Matt in the head. Leo backs away and Matt stumbles out. He looks rather dizzy; he falls over into a coughing fit. Matt tries to get up and Leo brings him into another Goldie Lock! Matt fights it for a while but the ref calls for the bell without Matt tapping out! RILEY STEVENS: “I didn’t see him tap did you?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Yes, he did!” Leo James brings Matt down, and gets into the refs face. Still feeling the effects of what was going on; Matt makes his way up and also tries to argue. The ref is simply holding his hands up pleading that he is not at fault for stopping the match. RILEY STEVENS: “Was this a set up?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “No! The referee was just protecting Kluge.” The crowd begins to boo realizing what has happened here as James and Kluge both unhappy with the result head to the back both men exchanging words as the fans pat them on their backs as we head to a break. WINNER: LEO JAMES Heading to the back almost revealing who did it we have Aspen Chaud giggling with The Shadow watching the ending of the last match as she turns to him with a smile on her face. ASPEN CHAUD: “Now that we successfully made those two tear each other apart it’s time for you to go and put an end to Trent Lost and make Alex’s defense at ‘Moment of Glory II’ a whole lot easier.” The Shadow nods his head seeming excited about the opportunity as she pats him on the back as he exits the area heading off for his match just as he leaves in walks Mark Cross. MARK CROSS: "There goes another one of your helpers. Seems like you have a new one each week…" Aspen glares at him, always annoyed when Cross enters to talk about old Aspen as he would say. ASPEN CHAUD: “What do you want?” Aspen rolls her eyes waiting for his answer as Cross looks at her and smiles. MARK CROSS: "I just came by to make sure that tonight your going to call this match right down the middle." Aspen patted him on the back walking to the door, pushing him out. ASPEN CHAUD: “You have nothing to worry about.” Aspen grins and then closes the door on a very confused Cross as we are sent back into the ring for the next part of the show. QUOTE When we return to the ring we have Tables around the ring set up for what is the set up for the Main Event of the evening. “Falling Away” by Curse Icon hits and the crowd goes wild as Trent Lost comes out fresh off his one week suspension looking almost deprived of action as he runs toward the ring past the tables and into the ring stopping to hold out his arms basking in his return in front of the fans. RILEY STEVENS: “Trent looks deprived of the ring does he not Oliver? I’ll be honest I miss him.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Considering he hasn’t won since Walker still mattered I don’t see why he is so happy.” "Suffer" by Shawn McPherson next hits as the cheers turn to boos as walking out seemingly completely in his element is The Shadow glaring out at the crowd as he stands by a table running his hands along it as he chuckles and heads up the steps and into the ring across from Trent. RILEY STEVENS: “If we recall these two men in the now infamous TLC Match and it was Lost that put Shadow through a table nearly ending his career.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “He’s going to wish he’d ended it after this match.” Shadow stands across from Lost as there is a very vivid smell in the air, that of expected injuries as the fans seem pumped for this one as the referee calls for the bell. DING DING DING! The fans go wild as the two men begin to circle around one another with big grins on their faces Lost seeming none the less confident after his losses while Shadow fresh off a defeat looks also to get back on the winning path. Finally they go in for a lock up a foolish attempt by Lost as he is hurled away, falling back into the corner. The fans cheer him on as he gets back up to his feet seeming still confident though knowing that a test of strength was not the smartest of approaches. Rushing at Shadow he ducks a big punch, wrapping his arms around him trying to give him a German however the attempt also goes rather badly as Shadow throws a big elbow that releases himself then throws him into the corner. Shadow shows his much hidden athleticism when he runs at him however Lost still is able to avoid it at the last moment. The crowd cheers him on as he rushes back at Shadow in the corner only to be crushed to the mat with a Vicious Clothesline. This angers the fans as Shadow glares out at them then slides out of the ring fixing up the ladder so he can slide it in. However when he tries to get it into the ring Lost is waiting for it as he runs off the ropes and dropkicks the table back into Shadow. This causes him to drop it as Trent throws caution to the wind throwing himself over the ropes down on top of Shadow to a roar from the crowd. RILEY STEVENS: “Knowing he can’t over power Shadow he’ll have to be creative getting him through the table.” Quickly getting up Trent throws out his arms posing for the crowd then turning and grabbing the table sliding it into the ring and then grabbing Shadow who was on one knee and throwing him in as well. Grabbing the table he goes to set it up in the corner but it takes too long as Shadow clubs him from behind just as he finishes setting it up. Lost spins around only to be lifted high into the air in Powerbomb position! The crowd can be heard gasping as Lost just barely avoids it by pushing himself over his head and to the other side. Trent then catches him from behind with a punch to the back of the head then going for a Powerslam but it fails and Shadow elbows him in the back then grabs him by the throat again he seems in trouble but he is able to break free of the choke and catches him with a Enzuigiri! The fans cheer as Shadow falls to a knee and then heading around him he hits a Reverse Snapmare Driver that puts Shadow down. Getting up to his feet Lost looks around, seeming to be thinking of a way to put him through a table. Lost nods his head seeming to have thought of a way as he calls for Shadow to get to his feet. The crowd is on their feet sensing the end as Lost runs off the ropes and goes for a Running Dropkick however it only sends Shadow teetering and not falling through the table. Trent gets up seemingly shocked that it didn’t work as Shadow throws him into the corner running at him and hitting a Clothesline. RILEY STEVENS: “They are dangerously close to that table.” Shadow lifts him up onto the top rope with bad intentions on his mind as he follows him up looking for something awful as Lost seems dazed and out of it. However as Shadow looks back at the table judging how he’ll Suplex him Trent in desperation mode catches him with a Headbutt! The crowd goes wild as this catches Shadow off guard as Trent catches him with another. Finally with Shadow starting to teeter, he nails him with one more then uses all his might to push him back and it works as Shadow falls back right through the table to a loud thud from the crowd! The crowd boos as Shadow holds his back in pain and Lost jumps down holding up his arms. RILEY STEVENS: “Lost wins, he puts him through the table.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “If that wasn’t lucky I don’t know what is.” The crowd goes wild as Lost stumbles over Shadow holding up his arms in victory. The fans go wild however due to their cheers a man jumps over the barricade unnoticed. Sliding in behind him we see a title in hand as the man crushes Trent over the back of the head with the North Beach title! The crowd boos as Lost falls down to the mat out cold as Alex Castellanos stands over him with a smirk on his face. RILEY STEVENS: “Alex Castellanos he was suppose to still be suspended!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Your forgetting who is charge twit.” The fans don’t let up giving it to Alex as he helps The Shadow to his feet, brushing off his back as Castellanos slides out of the ring grabbing another table as security starts to come out. Alex slides the table in and puts it in the middle of the ring, as the security tries to get into the ring Castellanos gives the first to try it a ‘Golden Arrow’ then looking back at Shadow who lifts Trent into Powerbomb position. RILEY STEVENS: “No don’t do that!” Seeming still out of it the Security now enters but they are too late as Shadow hurls him through the table! Together Alex and Shadow head out of the ring leaving Lost a broken mess. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Does this still count as a win for him?” The fans jeer Alex and Shadow as they head to the back all while Security checks on Trent, this scene broken up as we head backstage. WINNER: TRENT LOST It’s nearly time for the main event and as we head to the back we are met with Logan Abbott still seeming excited about his win earlier as Starre ties up his boots for his match. ALIOTH STARRE: “That’s win number one in a long list upcoming for you. However we turn to my own match tonight where I face Mark Cross in Round Two. You ever heard of lightning striking the same place twice? Well it won’t happen tonight and I’ll show everyone why I am the champion.” Alioth grins finishing up tying up as he takes his belt throwing it over his shoulder and heading to the ring as we take our last break. QUOTE “Now I'm That Bitch” by Livvi Franc hits as the fans begin to boo as out from the back comes Aspen Chaud wearing a black and white striped t-shirt with black spandex heading to the ring with a big grin on her face happy with the way the show has gone as the fans don’t give the referee any love. RILEY STEVENS: “You know most times I am happy when a rematch is given to two men who draw but not when it’s in order for Aspen to wear them down.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “You know she just wants to be there to make sure that we have a winner this time.” As she enters the ring heading to the corner with a grin “Lift Me Up” by Moby hits as the fans go insane as Mark Cross heads out from the back making a b-line for the ring high fiving a few fans but quickly heading up the steps and into the ring eager to get this started. RILEY STEVENS: “You know Cross laid it all on the line last week and that’s why many people consider him to be real Number One Contender.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I’m sorry but…he’s not.” Cross enters the ring eyeing up Aspen with untrusting eyes as he gets prepped in his corner as “Subterraneans” by David Bowie hits this time the fans giving Alioth Starre a very bad reaction still not letting up on the man whom turned on them. RILEY STEVENS: “You know I have to admit I am not sure if Aspen is just not trying to count a pin fall or what her motives are. She hates both men…” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “That’s why she is the one with the master plan.” Starre heads down the ramp with the belt that all three desire heading up the steps and into the ring holding the belt and then posing with it in the middle of the ring before handing it over to the referee Aspen who takes a second to stare at it before handing it out of the ring then calling for the bell. DING DING DING! The energy in the crowd makes this feel as though it is a big time event as Starre and Cross begin to circle around one another the memories of the previous week surely in their head. The two start off with a lock out just like last week only this time it’s Cross that one ups him rolling around for a Roll Up, 1…Aspen surprisingly counts before Alioth kicks out and he himself does what Cross did last week as he kicks him off and does a roll up of his own 1…Kick Out! Then right back up to their feet, the two look at one another yet again stumped then looking at Aspen both seemingly surprised by her willingness to count. But this moment is quickly ruined as Mark throws a big haymaker that Starre responds back with one of his own. Like a tennis match Cross crushes him with a big forearm to a loud cheer from the crowd. Alioth paddles it back with a huge European Uppercut of his own. Realizing that this benefits nobody Mark runs at him throwing a big clothesline that knocks Alioth down if only for a moment as he hops back up running right into a Exploder Suplex from Cross into another pin attempt 1…Kick Out! The fans boo, only really caring for Cross at this point as he gets to his feet looking at Aspen nodding as if to show respect for her thus far respectful showing. RILEY STEVENS: “I think Cross is as surprised as anyone with Aspen’s thus far good officiating.” Mark calls for Starre to get to his feet, looking for a Belly-to-Back Hammerlock Suplex however this fails as Alioth breaks free pushing Cross into the ropes then using this to roll him up 1…Kick Out! Thus far it’s not Aspen’s counts causing these one counts, it’s both men’s desire for victory. Both men hop up and it’s Starre on the attack going for a Running European Uppercut but Cross rolls through and then off the ropes running at Alioth only for him to duck out of the way and use Mark’s own momentum to send him through the ropes and out of the ring! He ends up right by the announce table as the crowd seems to reflect to last week as Alioth looks out at him and Aspen seems almost giddy by the idea of doing this again as Starre runs off the ropes but as he heads toward Cross at the last moment he instead just slides under the bottom rope walking over to Cross slamming his head against the announce table! Angry by Starre’s lack of bravery Aspen starts the count 1…2…The fans boo this wanting to see another dive as Alioth grabs him and throws him into the corner as Mark holds his shoulder in pain…3…4…5…6…Angrily Starre throws Cross back into the ring following not far behind him. RILEY STEVENS: “Thank goodness another count out was avoided.” Seeming to have given himself the advantage Cross follows it up with a big Japanese Arm Drag on Mark as soon as he gets up. Looking out at the crowd with a grin as he bounces off the ropes and then goes for a Fist Drop. At the last moment Cross rolls out of the way, crawling up to his feet as Starre throws his all with a Super Kick that Mark ducks his head away from. Sending him off the ropes both men go for a huge Clothesline but instead just both give each other one sending both down to the mat with one another! The fans seem stunned, these two seemingly at a stale mate as Chaud finally seems to snap seeming to be prepping for something as she stares down at Starre showing her real motives. RILEY STEVENS: “Oh no, she’s about to go for Starre.” However to her surprise it’s Cross that is up first as she goes to grab Starre’s Heel only for Mark to push her away. Aspen tries to claim innocence as Mark explains that this was to be a fair match. As the two are arguing Starre rises to his feet behind Cross. While the two are arguing Alioth runs up crushing Mark with a big Punt Low Blow then pushing him forward sending Aspen and him into colliding heads. Chaud falls out of the ring as Mark stumbles back right into a Superkick from Alioth. Looking around with no referee Starre calls to the back as a referee comes sprinting out from the back. Cross gets up holding his lower regions and as the referee slides in he hits a Fisherman Suplex with a Pin, 1…2…3! RILEY STEVENS: “What a travesty for this match to end this way.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Yeah Cross had no right questioning her.” Starre rolls out of the ring grabbing his belt up from ring side walking around the ring towards the back having thwarted Aspen’s plan as he heads towards the back with a big smile on his face as he heads behind the curtain as back in the ring Aspen gets up to her feet as Cross does the same getting to one knee. All the sudden there is some commotion as sprinting down towards the ring is a hooded man. RILEY STEVENS: “Now what?” The man slides into the ring and the hood comes off revealing it to be Ace Baldwin the man from MWA who appeared a few weeks back. Aspen covers up assuming he is after her but he throws her out of the way then kicks Cross in the gut and nails him with the ‘Thanks for Coming’ a Death Valley Driver, the crowd booing him for attacking one of their favorites. RILEY STEVENS: “That is Ace Baldwin but why is he after Cross?” The crowd seems to be asking the same thing as Mark Cross lays limp and Ace Baldwin looks out at the crowd taking a second to pose as Aspen who is sitting down looks up at him confused as the show comes to a close. WINNER: ALIOTH STARRE
