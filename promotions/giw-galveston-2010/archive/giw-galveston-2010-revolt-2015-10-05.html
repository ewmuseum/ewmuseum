GIW — Card

Revolt, 5 October 2015

5 October 2015
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS MONDAY NIGHT REVOLT 10/5/15 FROM FERRELL CENTER IN WACO, TEXAS BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! Fireworks stream into the air over Waco, Texas as Revolt fades onto the screen. One hundred screaming fans are live in attendance as the aired portion of this show begins after a night of exhibition matches, and the crowd is ready to get everything started. RILEY STEVENS: “Ladies and gentlemen, welcome to a very special edition of Revolt this week, the first of its kind. We’ve been going here in Waco for a long time, the kind of action you can only see if you attend a Revolt live! But now it is time for the live portion of the show, and we have quite a bit planned for you in addition to the Television title defense later in the night.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: "That’s right Ollie, we have two confrontations tonight between some people that really hate each other! Thunder in Paradise is only one week away, and on that show we will see Alexis Terry and Katie Keller, as well as The Sir and Geneva Lost, bring their rivalries to a head." RILEY STEVENS: “All of that action and more is coming up later tonight, but first, we want to take you to a recorded interview with Chris Callum and Aspen Chaud.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Thunder in Paradise will be a huge night for our ruling faction as Chris Callum looks to take out Jason Spirit before he can become a member of Katie Keller’s Revolution, and Aspen Chaud gets ready for a live job evaluation from the owner, Blake Beckett." RILEY STEVENS: “Blake did offer a short reply to Chris and Aspen’s statements, and we’ll share that with you as well.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I think its pretty clear from these tapes that both sides already know exactly what they are planning to say and do at Thunder in Paradise." RILEY STEVENS: “Let’s take you to the tape -” The screen fades out as Chris and Aspen appear on the screen, the Mississippi River behind them. A small set of words appears in the bottom corner of the screen – “Pre-Recorded, from New Orleans, Louisiana”. Chris sits with his arm around Aspen and her head on his shoulder while Zachary Pagonis sits across from the two of them, turning up to the screen. ZACHARY PAGONIS: "Chris, Aspen – thank you for joining me on such short notice." Aspen rolled her eyes as she pulled her head up. ASPEN CHAUD: "Thank you for ruining our holiday, Zachary. If I didn’t have to talk to you, I wouldn’t." Pagonis pursed his lips as he looked down at his notes. ZACHARY PAGONIS: "Well, my apologies as always Ms. Chaud. But you brought it up, so… why are you and Chris in New Orleans tonight while Revolt is in Waco?" Chris leaned forward, pressing his hands together. CHRIS CALLUM: "In one week, Zach… in one week Blake Beckett is going to try to break apart what we built. He’s going to try to fire Aspen, and he’s going to replace her with one his stooges – someone who he can trust to follow his orders exactly. Look, we’ve spent a lot of time making this company great, and we aren’t just going to watch and let that happen. Every time this company has been under the thumb of Blake Beckett, it has failed. Now it is thriving, and make no mistake, that is because of Aspen Chaud, and the esteemed Mr. Beckett wants a piece of the pie." Aspen raised her finger to grab Pagonis’ attention. He allowed her to speak. ASPEN CHAUD: "Chris is right, as usual. Under me, this company has risen to new heights – TRULY the best wrestling promotion of any size in this nation. I brought you stars like Johnny Violence, Bronx Valescence, Katie Keller, Luke Jones… and Chris Callum. If they were here before, I brought them back. If they are new to the company, I found them. That was me -" Pagonis interrupted her. ZACHARY PAGONIS: "A lot of people would say that the wide array of talent in this company was signed as a result of Paul Marshall, and you fired Paul Marshall. Now we see you barely having any say on what goes on during the show – most of the day to day operations are handled by David Sinclair. What you have done, however, is take the company on the road, crippling its finances. Is that your legacy as Commissioner?" She leaned back in the chair, exhausted. Chris turned to her, and then back to Pagonis. CHRIS CALLUM: "Aspen does more for this company than you will ever realize, Zach. You’re fully aware of that, and what you’re also aware of is that any tour schedule needed to be signed by Blake Beckett. If indeed the company is losing money, and we doubt that it is, Blake Beckett is just as responsible. He’s using this as a way to force out Aspen because he hates her and he always has. You’re fully aware, Zach, but you don’t care. You’ll stick your nose in Blake’s ass any time he wants. I’m surprised you haven’t been made Commissioner." Pagonis turns very pale as he turns back towards Aspen. ZACHARY PAGONIS: "Aspen, what will you do if Blake Beckett fires you? What will Chris do?" She smiles, rubbing Chris’ arm. ASPEN CHAUD: "Zach, you should hope that you never have to find out." We linger on the scene in New Orleans for a moment before we fade back to Waco where Riley Stevens and Oliver Thibodaux are ready to pick up where they left off. RILEY STEVENS: “Some eerie words for Aspen Chaud as the interview came to a close. We got a statement from Blake Beckett earlier this afternoon. Do you want to read it, Ollie?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Sure – it says, “Aspen Chaud is in no position to make threats, considering her performance evaluation is this coming Monday. Everything will be considered, and I will be fair and just with my decision. I assure you, if she is indeed fired, we will have a replacement available immediately." RILEY STEVENS: “So, Blake doesn’t seem concerned at all?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Not by the sound of it. I think this could get uglier than he estimates, though. Aspen doesn’t usually like it when things get taken away from her." RILEY STEVENS: “I think that’s inevitable, Ollie. There is no way Aspen is still around after this show. I think the bigger question is, what happens to Chris and who will be the new commish?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Like Aspen said, I hope we never have to find out." RILEY STEVENS: “Well, it’s time to pivot. Over the past few weeks, we have seen the complete collapse of what seemed to be a happy relationship, all because Aspen Chaud decided to share some personal information about Katie Keller -” OLIVER THIBODAUX: "No, no. Don’t even play it like that. Katie Keller made the choice to cheat on Alexis Terry, and now she is paying for that choice." RILEY STEVENS: “Aspen shared the information to break them apart.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: "So what? Keller broke them apart the second she decided to be unfaithful." RILEY STEVENS: “Alexis was heartbroken, and in her rage she cost Katie Keller the Undisputed Championship at Unscripted. At Thunder in Paradise, the two will meet in singles action for the first time.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: "And tonight… they confront each other face to face." RILEY STEVENS: “Wait… wait… I’m getting word that it is happening.. now!” The cameras follow Alexis Terry walking backstage toward the gorilla position with the Television title over her shoulder. She stops short, a smirk crossing her face as the cameras pan over to the right and reveal Katie Keller standing there in her street clothes. ALEXIS TERRY: “Huh, didn’t expect to see you here without your new pet.” Katie rolls her eyes, crossing her arms over her chest. KATIE KELLER: “Must be a shame for you to be all dressed up with no chance to screw me over tonight.” ALEXIS TERRY: “If you were woman enough to talk to me, maybe it wouldn’t have come down to that. I did what I had to do.” KATIE KELLER: “What you ‘had’ to do? That’s pathetic, Alexis.” ALEXIS TERRY: “And yet here I am, with a championship over my shoulder and you… without one.” KATIE KELLER: “I’d rather be without a championship than still engaged to someone who doesn’t trust me.” The crowd at ringside makes an audible ‘ooooh’ and Alexis turns her head away and nods before she snaps back and tackles Katie into a collection of shipping boxes for Thunder in Paradise. The TV title crashes to the ground and Katie shrieks as boxes fall everywhere, Alexis trying to throw lefts and rights through the debris. Alexis kicks away a box and grabs Katie by the hair, but Katie clubs Alexis in the stomach and sends her stumbling backward. Katie swipes her nose with her thumb and kicks Alexis in the gut, doubling her over and Katie grabs a handful of Alexis hair. Alexis fights Katie off, raking her eyes and Katie steps back blinded. Alexis sizes up Katie and JUST as she’s about to hit her #Superkick, Katie is yanked out of harms way and held back by Jason Spirit as backstage personnel rush to break up the fight between the two former lovers. ALEXIS TERRY: “Jason can’t save you at Thunder in Paradise.” She picks up the TV title from the ground and the cameras focus on Katie’s face before they fade to commercial break. QUOTE The camera fades back in from commercial on a shot of the ring. Melissa Stark, Adam Spencer, Brittney Filmore, and some local talents are already in the ring. RILEY STEVENS: "Welcome back to the new and improved Revolt format. As you can see the ring is already filling up with competitors. A lot a familiar faces and a few new ones. Who are these people, Ollie?" OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Well we’re all familiar with Stark, Fillmore, and Spencer at this point but those two new guys are Juan South a luchadore out of Kentucky, and Mick Connors the self described hardcore legend. " RILEY STEVENS: "Should make for an interesting match." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Indeed!" The arena lights dim slightly and then Alexis Terry’s entrance music starts to play. The crowd cheers loudly drowning out the sound of the music. RUBY REYNOLDS: "This match is set for one fall. Introducing the reigning and defending television champion, from Newcastle England, Alexis Terry!" Alexis makes her way to the ring with the title over her shoulder. As she makes her way down the entrance ramp she slaps hands with some of the excited fans. Her entrance ends with a shot of Alexis on the second turnbuckle with the title held above her head. RILEY STEVENS: "Alexis is on the brink of becoming the longest reigning TV champion of the modern era." OLIVER THIBODAUX: “If she wins tonight the record in hers!" DING! DING! The bell sounds and the ring comes to life. Fillmore shoots in on Alexis but the champ easily sides steps it. Fillmore bounces off the ropes and right into the waiting clutches of Juan South - ENZIGURI! With Fillmore temporarily disposed of, Stark is the next to make a move. She ties up with Mick Connors and forces the older man to the ropes. A stiff clothesline from Melissa sends Connors to the outside where he lands awkwardly on his hip. RILEY STEVENS: "Bad landing for the new guy I’d say he’s going to be out for a few weeks after that." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Just look at him, Riley. No one that old has any business in a ring in the first place." Back in the Adam Spencer has finally made a move by tying up with Alexis. The two are jockeying for position. Spencer uses his strength advantage to power Alexis into a headlock but to little avail. Juan South comes out of nowhere and nails Spencer with a Shining Wizard. Spencer goes down hard and South shoots in for a pin. 1 2 NO! Alexis breaks up the pin attempt with a stomp to the back of South’s head. The mask man rolls away clutching the back of his skull. Alexis lays into the downed Spencer with a series of mounted punches. That’s followed up by a Snap Suplex, and a Drop Kick. During the assault on Spencer both Fillmore and South were able to get back to their feet. Fillmore and South attempt to lock up Melissa Stark comes off the top rope and nails them both with a Missile Dropkick! RILEY STEVENS: "What a devastating maneuver!" OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Them Starks are strong, Riley - #StarkStrong!" Spencer manages to reverse one of Alexis moves and swing momentum back in his favor but it doesn't last long. He manages to get in a Suplex and Snapmare before Stark hits him with a Big Boot! Spencer, Terry, Fillmore, and South are all down. Melissa can see it’s now or never she picks up Juan South and delivers her finisher, The Stark Truth a variation of a Gory Neckbreaker. RILEY STEVENS: "This is it, Ollie! Both Starks are gonna be golden!" OLIVER THIBODAUX: "NOPE!" Outta nowhere Alexis kicks up to her feet and hits Stark with a jaw shattering #SuperKickParty. Stark spins on her heels three time before collapsing on her stomach. Alexis Terry goes for a pin on Juan South making sure to hook both legs. 1 2 3 DING! DING! RUBY REYNOLDS: "Your winner and still GIW Television Champion, Alexis Terry!" The referee brings Alexis her title and helps her back to her feet before raising her hand in victory. The other combatants roll out of the ring and start for the back. Except for Mick COnnors who gets carried out on a stretcher. RILEY STEVENS: "It’s official Alexis Terry is the longest reigning TV Champion of the modern era! ’" OLIVER THIBODAUX: "No one deserves it more in my opinion." RILEY STEVENS: "Stay tuned folks we’ll be back with more GIW right after this!" OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Things are just getting going here don’t miss it!" The scene fades to commercial with Alexis holding the title above her head in victory. WINNER: ALEXIS TERRY (STILL GIW TELEVISION CHAMPION) Back from commercial break, we find Riley Stevens and Oliver Thibodaux at the announce desk. RILEY STEVENS: “Ladies and gentlemen, welcome back from the commercial break. We still have a lot planned for you tonight, including the contract signing for the main event of Thunder in Paradise – Geneva Lost versus The Sir.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: "But first, let’s take it to Marissa Mathis with George Stark and Dustin James Holt, two men who will be adversaries next week with the Hardcore title on the line." The camera fades in on a tight shot of Marissa Mathis. As we zoom out we can see Melissa Stark drenched in sweat standing and standing next to Marissa. MARISSA MATHIS: "Please welcome my guest at this time, Melissa Stark." Some cheering can be heard from the fans watching on the other side of the curtain. MARISSA MATHIS: "You nearly took home your first title in GIW tonight Melissa, how do you feel?" Melissa places her hands on her hips and takes a deep breath. Each word is accented by a heavy breath. MELISSA STARK: "I feel bad Marissa. That title should be mine." MARISSA MATHIS: "Are you saying Alexis Terry cheated?" Melissa shakes her head and lets out a long sigh, Her breathing is finally returned to normal. MELISSA STARK: "No, Terry didn’t cheat but she might as well have. I had that joker Juan South down for the count before Terry blindsided me." MARISSA MATHIS: “There you have it ladies and gentlema..." DUSTIN JAMES HOLT: "Hold up toots!" Marissa is cut off by DJH as he walks in from off screen. There's an odd swagger in his step almost like he's drunk or listening to music the rest of us can't hear. DUSTIN JAMES HOLT: “I’m tired of these Stark losers blaming everyone else for their shortcomings. Every Time one of them loses they blame the other guy, pathetic!." Both Marissa and Melissa are visibly annoyed by the arrival of DJH. MARISSA MATHIS: “I don’t appreciate you interrupting me, Dustin. Now if you don't mind I’m trying to conduct an interview." Marissa’s tone is sharp and takes Melissa and DJH by surprise. They’re not used to an interviewer with a back bone but the shock quickly passes. DUSTIN JAMES HOLT: “Calm yourself sugar tits. I’m not here for you. I just want Melissa to pass a message on to her dear ol’ brother for me, okay?" Marissa crosses her arms and starts to tap her foot. MARISSA MATHIS: “And what’s the message, Dustin?" DHJ smiles. DUSTIN JAMES HOLT: “This!" Out of nowhere DJH hauls off and slaps Melissa Stark as hard as he can right across her face. The blow is enough to knock Melissa down to one knee. DJH swaggers off laughing under his breath while Marissa Screams at him and Melissa rubs her swollen cheek. We fade to commercial break. --- We come back from commercial to see a table sitting in the middle of the ring with black lining and two clipboards set on top. Sinclair is standing in the center of the ring with a microphone as he waits with a serious look on his face as the fans as drowning him out in boos and jeers. RILEY STEVENS: “Looks like there’s no love out there for Sinclair.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “It’s just jealously. I bet all those plebs out tonight wish they could be assistant commissioner for Revolt." Inside the ring as well is Ruby Reynolds. She gives a small smile as she announces Sinclair her voice quieting down the fans a bit as she speaks. RUBY REYNOLDS: “Ladies and gentlemen… Your Revolt Assistant Commissioner… David Sinclair!" The fans drown him out again as he starts to speak through his microphone. A big “Sinclair Sucks.” chant takes over the entire venue as the cameras pan around showing all the loud and proud GIW fans in attendance tonight. The camera cuts back to Sinclair as a throbbing vein appears in his forehead. He’s visibly agitated now, and puts the microphone up to speak. DAVID SINCLAIR: “Well… If I could be allowed the opportunity to speak… This up coming super show for GIW is Trouble in Paradise! It’s where we take our show on the high seas, and wow and amazing you even off dry land!" The crowd cheers at this all of them in anticipation for the coming show. DAVID SINCLAIR: “We’ll have on display a wide assortment of bouts and grudge matches for your entertainment. But the big match at Trouble In Paradise is our main event… The Sir going one-on-one with the Deep South Champion Geneva Lost! You already know how much these two want to kill each other!" The fans pop at this once again, and Sinclair grows a small smile as he waits for them all to settle down a bit as he continues to speak. DAVID SINCLAIR: “But… but. These two can’t face off in a simple singles match. No-no-no sir. There’s been far to much thats happened between these two, and a simple one fall match couldn’t possibly do this match justice. So I’ve decided to spice this match up a bit… " Sinclair reaches over the table and picks up one of the clipboards. He smiles wide as he holds it high in the air while bringing back up the other arm with the microphone. DAVID SINCLAIR: “In my hand is a contract for Trouble In Paradise. On each of these clipboards is the match for Geneva and The Sir… However, it’s not for a simple singles match. On these clipboards is the waiver for a No Disqualification match!" RILEY STEVENS: “Can you believe it Ollie? A big announcement for our Trouble in Paradise main event!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “More kerosene on an already flammable match between Geneva and the Sir! It’s gonna be a helluva fight!" The fans go crazy with ruckus applause as Sinclair places the contract back on the table. DAVID SINCLAIR: “So without further ado, let’s bring out both of our guest of honor and get this match contract signed!" “I Have Seen Where It Grows” by Demon Hunter plays over the PA. The fans take up a mixed round of applause and boos as The Sir makes his way from the back. RILEY STEVENS: “And here he is your number one contender to the GIW Undisputed Championship...” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Cept at T.I.P. it won’t be about the Undisputed Championship. It won’t be about championship gold at all. It’s gonna come down too two people who absolutely hate each other." The Sir makes his way down and enters the ring, as Sinclair gives him a wide berth. The Sir walks around the table all eyes on the entrance ramp before he takes a seat down at the table. DAVID SINCLAIR: “Ladies and Gents… The SIR!" The fans give him a lukewarm reception once again as he leans back in his chair with an expressionless face. He placed his hand on top of one of the clipboards as Sinclair passes a microphone and a pen too him. “Murda Bizzness” by Iggy Azalea hits the PA next as the fans take up another mixed reaction. Geneva Lost emerges from the back with her Deep South championship over her shoulders. She stares daggers down at the ring with her ire directed at The Sir. RILEY STEVENS: “And The Sir’s dancing partner for Trouble In Paradise Geneva Lost.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Queen G baby! She looks pissed, so the’s not good for The Sir." RILEY STEVENS: “Well he’s not smiling either Ollie.” Geneva hangs on the outside of the ring not getting in as she stares at The Sir from the outside. She then calls over Ruby Reynolds. As Reynolds approaches Geneva takes her belt off of her shoulders and floors Reynolds with it to a explosive chorus of boos. RILEY STEVENS: “And Geneva just took out our ring announcer!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “You might wanna try to be more incognito Riley… You know before she looks this way." Geneva picks up the microphone seething a bit as she places it to her lips. GENEVA LOST: “It’s funny. It’s like God hates you… Do you believe in God Sir?" Geneva rolls under the bottom rope and slowly approaches the table. GENEVA LOST: “You should… because you’re starting at her right now. Months ago I left you as bad memory, and now you come crawling back on your knees to fight another day? What are you fighting for huh? Glory? Respect? Revenge? Whatever it is… you won’t find it. You’ll only find the back of my ass as I walk away from you once again… victorious." Geneva holds her free hand out towards Sinclair who promptly places a pin in it. She reaches down and flips through the contract to sign her name as the crowd roars. The Sir remains mum, and calmly flips through his contract and signs. DAVID SINCLAIR: “The match is official!" Geneva tosses the microphone and smiles as she stares down at the Sir. She throws a few more verbal jabs at him before she reaches down and slaps him across the face with a big smack. The crowd goes crazy as The Sir quickly snatches her up by the throat. RILEY STEVENS: “Looks like we don’t have to wait for T.I.P. after all!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “These contact signings never end well! Ever." The Sir picks her up and slams her on top of the table. He starts to lay into her with punches, and Geneva starts punching from her vulnerable position as well. The crowd is going crazy, but their cheers quickly turn into boos as GIW security pour into the ring to separate the two. The horde of black shirts are able to pull the two apart for a second, but the two break free and meet up once again with fist for each other. They are pulled apart again, but the Sir’s anger boils over as he simply pike up on of the security guards and slams him through the table. The fans erupt in a loud round of cheers. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Its anarchy! Pandemonium and anarchy!." More guard pour out from the back and swarm the ring. They begin to pile on The Sir while Geneva has been forced by a few security guards to cool off in the corner. Geneva feigns her calm, but she quickly kicks one of the unsuspecting guards in the nether-region and starts to scale the top rope. She throws up the bird to the cameras and leaps off into the mass of security guards trying to get at the Sir! She breaks through the pile and starts punching wildly and blindly as the GIW show graphic appears on the screen. RILEY STEVENS: “World War Three is upon us! But we’re outta time folks.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Geneva versus the Sir at Trouble In Paradise! You all won’t wanna miss this… Goodnight everybody!."
