GIW — Card

Revolt, 24 March 2011

24 March 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: THURSDAY NIGHT REVOLT! LIVE FROM THE SEAWALL BOULEVARD ENTERTAIMENT COMPLEX = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! The show starts up panning around the fans piled into the complex, the place is full again tonight, even though the show is shortened this week. But before things can even begin "To Be Loved" by Papa Roach hits and the fans are sent into a frenzy of boos, as out from the back walking with two titles over her shoulders is Aspen Chaud a few moments later walking on her left and right are Trent Lost and Marcus Hister all three heading towards the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Great, I didn’t think we’d get through the night with out these guys coming out and gloating.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “They retained the belts, why wouldn’t they?” RILEY STEVENS: “Perhaps because it took three of them to do it. The Shadow is out for an unknown period of time, they stole the belts.” As they continue to walk down Aspen walks up the steps first as Trent holds the bottom rope down for her as Hister taunts the fans a bit bringing more boos from them. Aspen steps in and then Trent follows as Hister now steps in as well as Aspen walks over and is handed a microphone as Hister and Lost stand behind her looking around at the crowd. ASPEN CHAUD: “As you can see. We are STILL the tag team champions. At ‘Revelation’ you people all witnessed just how dominant we are. And here’s the kicker we did it all without my boyfriend Justin Volt. I hope your used to this because from here on out, High Voltage runs this show!” Aspen lowers the microphone as the fans boo them as Trent claps nodding his head while Marcus turns throwing out his arms as if to goat them into more boos as Aspen goes to continue. ASPEN CHAUD: “Tonight our dominance continues. While I may not be wrestling here tonight, the two men behind me will be. And you should all know that I will be right there to cheer my boys on, because this will take us one step closer to taking your beloved Eric Sparrow’s title!” They again boo as Aspen turns back to look at her teammates nodding at both them in a show of confidence, turning back to the fans to continue. ASPEN CHAUD: “From here on out this is Thursday Night HIGH VOLTAGE!” The crowd boos for a bit showing their distain for the group as suddenly “Demon Eyes” by The Answer echoes through the speakers and the fans actually cheer Blake hoping that he’ll put an end to this. He walks out wearing a suit and wielding a microphone, looking confident as he makes his way down to the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “I wonder what the Boss thinks about all of this.” Blake walks up the steps and then through the ropes and into the ring, holding a microphone of his own as Aspen backs up a bit not looking pleased to see him, but proudly showing off the belts they retained in the match he designed. BLAKE BECKETT: “I could go on and on about how the three of you managed to keep those belts in the un-fairest of ways. But frankly I have much bigger things to attend to. Over the last month I have been trying my hardest to figure out who attacked Wake Walker and put him in the retirement home. Who attacked Jake Avery. Basically who has been behind these last few months’ attacks? And frankly the search led me right...to…you! Every one has said that they saw you there Aspen and they saw your boyfriend to. Lord knows Hister would do something like that and Trent seems willing to do anything as well. So that’s why I am blaming you, for the attacks.” Blake points a strong finger right at Aspen, as the fans react with some shocked reactions as Aspen shakes her head looking back at Marcus and Trent and then back to Blake raising the mic. ASPEN CHAUD: “Really Blake? REALLY? Are you that paranoid about things like that? You know for a fact that if I had taken out Wake Walker, I’d have taken credit. So would any one else in my group. Plus, what benefit would I have gotten from attacking Jake Avery? The guy was a nobody. Sorry to say, Mr. Beckett. But your little search is going to have to continue, because frankly none of us did it.” Aspen smirks, lowering the microphone as Blake seems ready to respond when suddenly mariachi music begins to play as everyone looks around as confused as ever, as out from the back comes a well groomed Latin man, wearing a suit heading towards the ring with a microphone of his own, he walks down to the ring and heads up the steps stepping in with them as even Blake seems confused about who he is. EDUARDO SANTIAGO: “Greetings every one, you may not know me but I know all of you people. You see I have been watching you from afar in my home country of Mexico! And being a big fan of wrestling I couldn’t help but admire what you had going. You see I have had plenty, of big Lucha Libre’ promotions. If your watching, masked wrestlers on any television Network it’s probably run by me! That’s why I have come to GIW! And today I am proud to announce that I am now the majority share holder of Galveston Island Wrestling.” The fans seem a bit confused as Trent can even be seen mouthing “We have shares?” as Blake is looking at him seeming just as caught off guard as anyone. EDUARDO SANTIAGO: “So from here on out, if I want something to happen. I have the ability to make it happen. That’s why tonight in the Main Event, nobody not in The Shootout can interfere in the match.” The fans cheer the announcement; with Eduardo seemingly have already gotten the fans to like him as he smiles out at them. EDUARDO SANTIAGO: “Oh and one more thing, the next Super-Show will take place in Cancun, Mexico and it will be called ‘International X’ and we will blend a little Mexican flavor with your American company, adios.” The fans seem pretty happy with the new guy as he walks by patting Blake on the back as he leaves, as Blake seems to be thinking this is some sort of joke as we take a commercial break. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = From there we are taken to the office of Blake Beckett who is now slouching back in his chair seeming to be thinking about what just happened out there earlier tonight as La’Sarah Audric sits across from him. BLAKE BECKETT: “So…who in the world was that?” Blake sits up seeming to be angry with the new guy stepping in and taking a bit of his power with out him knowing about it. LA’SARAH AUDRIC: “His name is Eduardo Santiago. As he said he’s a big time Lucha Libre mogul over in Mexico. Apparently he came in today and bought the stocks.” Blake leans forward rubbing his hands through his head shaking his head. BLAKE BECKETT: “How exactly did I not know about this? You’d think the people at Fox Southwest would tell me about this.” La’Sarah nods her head trying to be the voice of reason here as Blake seems ready to snap. LA’SARAH AUDRIC: “I guess the deal just finalized. Apparently it has something to do with Fox Southwest thinking we need to expand our viewership.” La’Sarah shrugs she too clearly caught off guard by all that has just happened here tonight. BLAKE BECKETT: “But Mexico? Really? That’s a long ways away. And he’s in America now, what makes him think he knows how to run a company?” Beckett continues to look upset about the whole situation, especially thinking he should be in the know. LA’SARAH AUDRIC: “Seems like the fans liked him…” Audric says this, but Blake giving her a glare causes her to shake her head angrily. LA’SARAH AUDRIC: “But yeah you’ve done nothing wrong this place has only gone up with you at the helm. I’ll make some calls and see what we can do about this.” Blake nods his head agreeing with as he goes back to slouching back in his chair as Audric walks off to handle some business as the scene changes again to a different location backstage. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = This time we find ourselves in the middle of the catering area where Seth Seeker is plowing through people, stopping as he walks into a staff member. SETH SEEKER: “Do you know where Jak is?” The staff member quickly shakes his head as Seeker turns and grabs a staff member walking by his collar. SETH SEEKER: “Have you seen Jak Hendricks?” Again the answer is the same as Seeker quickly lets go looking around then walking right into Amanda Rowland. SETH SEEKER: “Have you seen Jak Hendricks?” Amanda takes a second to rub her chin thinking long and hard about this. AMANDA ROWLAND: “Is he the one with the really big…” Before she can finish Seeker cuts her off angrily. SETH SEEKER: “No that’s not him.” Amanda shakes her head realizing her mistake then suddenly having a big realization. AMANDA ROWLAND: “Wait that’s Ross Rydell. But Jak no, I haven’t seen him.” Seeker stares at her and then walks off to angry to care about what was just said as he turns to the cameraman. SETH SEEKER: “Jak? Where are you?!” Seeker pushes the cameraman away and storms off in anger as the segment ends and we are taken else where backstage. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = We head to the backstage area where Tony Miranda is stretching out getting ready for tonight’s Shootout Match. The fans are reacting positively to him, as he reaches down into his bag when suddenly there’s a loud crash as the door flies open. Before he can even look up its Marcus Hister and Trent Lost with Aspen Chaud a few steps behind them yelling out at them. ASPEN CHAUD: “Get him, get him!” They pound away as he struggles on the ground trying to fight back. For a moment he gets up punching Trent away and then doing the same with Marcus but they collapse back on him. Suddenly Hister grabs him and tosses him right into the locker causing it to smash in. ASPEN CHAUD: “How do you like that! How do you like that Tony!? Kiss your title dreams…GOODBYE! Aspen smirks as she gives him one big kick to the chest before turning and walking away calling for Trent and Marcus, Trent follows her out as Hister turns around grinning down at him before walking out as we take a break before the match. QUOTE When the camera’s begin rolling again we come back into the ring to find the place packed. Standing in the ring is not only six superstars, but also one referee while two others stand outside of the ring all of them ready to call what is sure to be an exciting fight. RILEY STEVENS: “It is time for the GIW Shootout, all six men in the ring will be fighting for a shot at the GIW Undisputed Championship next week at ‘Night of Champions’. The six men in the ring have decided to participate. Each man will get a point for every pin fall or submission and will lose a point every time they are submitted or pinned…” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “We have a twenty minute time limit as well.” RILEY STEVENS: “I was getting there…” Early on we find a bit of a separation in the ring, as Trent Lost and Marcus Hister find themselves in one corner. Other wise there seems to be no other alliances brewing, as the referee in the ring calls for the bell to start the carnage as well as the clock. DING DING DING! The crowd is excited as almost right away all hell breaks loose. Alioth Starre and Noah Namith go after Trent Lost and Marcus Hister while Floyd Mason and The Fallen go after one another as well. Right away things are clearly going to be a bit crazy. Lost and Hister are able to take out Starre as they then Double Suplex Noah near the side of the ring. They get up high fiving one another when suddenly the fans roar as Starre comes full steam clotheslining both men out of the ring tumbling over with them. On the other side it’s Floyd that out of no where rolls up, The Shadow holding tight 1…2…3! +1 Floyd Mason, -1 The Fallen As soon as Floyd gets the pin the Fallen flips him back over, getting him in a pinning combination of his own as the referee jumps back down to count this one, 1…2…3! +1 The Fallen, -1 Floyd Mason Just like that both men are back to where they started as Noah now recovered is waiting for them as they get up, catching Floyd with a big punch then following it up with one on The Fallen as well. The fans are cheering the new guy, as he lifts up Floyd and delivers a perfect Body Slam to the mat. He makes the cover hoping to get some points 1…2…The Fallen kicks him off making sure he doesn’t pull away. Floyd rolls out of the way as Noah gets to his feet as well, getting caught by The Fallen who whips him into the corner then runs over landing a big elbow to him. Flashing to the outside of the ring we find that Starre has recovered but so to has Hister and Lost and it appears they are willing to dish out some punishment. They both grab him tossing him right into the surrounding barricade, following it up with some gruesome looking stomps all over his body. Back to the ring we go where we find that The Fallen is seemingly ready to Suplex Noah. However suddenly he lifts him high up into the air as he flips him over the top rope with a big back body drop! The fans cheer as Noah, looks out at him. Running off the ropes he seems prepared to land something big when Mason hops up jumping up and landing a perfectly executed Head Kick! The fans cheer the maneuver as Floyd hops up, slapping his chest. Unknown to him in the ring Hister and Lost have returned. Grabbing Floyd they toss him over the ropes right into The Fallen who is only just getting up. Hister heads up to the top rope, looking dangerously out at them where he jumps off landing right on top of both men as they are getting up. They don’t like him but they realize he’s always a dare devil, inside of the ring Trent is stalking Noah, as he gets up he turns around right into a ‘Strung Out’ a Hangman’s DDT! The fans boo as Trent hops on top with a cover 1…2…3! +1 Trent Lost, -1 Noah Namith Lost gets to his feet, starting to celebrate. However the decision is a little premature as Alioth Starre has now joined him in the ring and when he turns around he stumbles into a big punch from Alioth that brings some cheers from the crowd. Trent stumbles his way back to the corner, where Starre follows him looking to do more damage. He starts to throw bombs, trapping Trent in the corner and unleashing some damage. But sliding back into the ring is Marcus, who comes from behind catching Starre with a punch to his back. Starre turns around walking right into a Wheel Kick that takes him down. Hister points to the top as Trent scales the ropes, looking out at them. As Alioth gets to his feet, Hister stands there behind him grinning. Just as the name says Trent takes the ‘Leap of Faith’ a Flying Crossbody but Alioth rolls under it and instead Trent flies right into Hister! The fans go wild as Lost falls to the mat as Hister is hurt, Starre quickly runs up making the cover on Hister 1…2…3! +1 Alioth Starre, -1 Marcus Hister The fans are jazzed up after that one as Starre gets to his feet, looking pumped up with a new sense of rejuvenation. When Trent gets up he runs forward using his trunks to dispense him out of the ring. But it’s The Fallen whom returns to the ring, spinning him around. To his surprise mostly, Starre drops him down right into a Figure Four Leg Lock! The fans go wild as The Fallen struggles to get free, before tapping out! +1 Alioth Starre, -1 The Fallen The Fallen rolls out of the way as Starre stumbles back to his feet after gaining another point this time it’s Floyd who has come back into the ring. He tries to lift him into a Powerbomb but instead Alioth drops him down right into another Figure Four Leg Lock! The fans go wild again; as they start to get behind him a bit as yet again he makes a person tap. +1 Alioth Starre, -1 Floyd Mason After impressive win and taking a big lead with three Alioth gets up to his feet and this time he’s not as lucky as before. With Floyd rolling out of the ring holding his legs and The Fallen doing just the same, it leaves Marcus Hister left. With an evil look on his face Hister waits as Starre turns around crushing him with a gigantic Superkick! Starre falls to the mat, downed as Hister makes the cover 1…2…3! +1 Marcus Hister, -1 Alioth Starre Hister gets up screaming down at Alioth taunting him as he raises up his arms to the sky, looking as cocky as ever. But it’s to his detriment as returning to the ring is little known indy star Noah Namith, who catches him with a School Boy pin 1…2…Kick Out! Hister jumps up, as both men quickly hurry to their feet. Noah does so a little bit faster and quickly lifts him up into a ‘Natural Selection’ a Scoop Brainbuster with a floatover, Noah falls to the mat then hooks the leg excitedly as he gets the count 1…2…3! +1 Noah Namith, -1 Marcus Hister The scoring is now Alioth in the lead with two, followed by Trent Lost with one, as Hister and Namith strike even at zero followed by two negatives in The Fallen and Floyd Mason. The fans in the arena are pumped as we have now hit the two minute mark and it seems all systems go for Alioth. Returning to his feet, he comes to the center of the ring meeting up with Noah as the two enter into an exchange. On the outside we see Trent Lost tossing Floyd right into The Fallen taking both men out. Hister has now returned to his feet as Noah whips Alioth off the ropes. On the rebound Noah throws a big clothesline but Alioth ducks under and dropkicks Hister on the run sending him out of the ring. Bouncing up he walks right into Noah, who tries to lift him up for a Body Slam, however he flips off nailing him with a big DDT! The fans cheer as the one minute mark now hits. Alioth waits for him to get up as suddenly Trent Lost returns to the ring low blowing him! With him bent over in pain he propels him out of the ring! The fans boo as Trent gets up waiting for Noah. Thirty seconds left as Trent goes for his finisher, the ‘Lost and Found’! Noah escapes as the fans go wild but to their surprise Trent rakes his eyes leaving him lost as he now stumbles around offering up his back as Trent lifts him up right into the ‘Lost And Found’ a Lifting Full Nelson Twisted into a Reverse STO! The fans boo as Trent makes the pin attempt with nearly ten seconds left, Starre tries to get up hopping up on the apron but to his surprise Hister jumps up holding his leg 1…2…3! +1 Trent Lost, -1 Noah Namith Trent rolls off as Alioth kicks Hister down. He jumps in trying to desperately get another pin catching Lost off guard with a School Boy 1…2…Kick Out! Just as time expires. The referee waves it off as the fans look up at the score realizing it ended up a draw. RILEY STEVENS: “So it’s a draw? Trent Lost and Alioth Starre with two points, while the rest of the wrestlers ended up with negative one point. I have to admit everyone looked impressive tonight, but will there be over time or what?” Alioth and Trent get up both arguing over who will be going to the title match as Riley Stevens has entered the ring. On the outside Noah, Floyd, and The Fallen have all left the ringside area. Inside of the ring it’s Oliver that gets up holding a microphone too many people’s surprise. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “You see, the Boss thought this might happen. That’s why he chose me to reveal his choice for what will happen. Next week it’ll be Eric Sparrow defending his belt against BOTH OF YOU!” The fans seem half way pleased, cheering on Alioth who doesn’t totally seem pleased with the decision. RILEY STEVENS: “Wow what a match that will be. Alioth Starre will defend his GIW North Beach Championship and then try to win the GIW Undisputed Championship in the Main Event! Trent Lost will look to bring the gold home to High Voltage.” The tension is in the air as suddenly the crowd boos as sliding into the ring is Marcus Hister, attacking Alioth from behind. Both Marcus and Trent start to pound away at him as out from the back comes Aspen Chaud running down and casually walking up the steps into the ring, cheering on from afar. Suddenly the crowd goes into an ear crushing scream as Eric Sparrow comes flying out from the back. RILEY STEVENS: “Here comes the champ! Here comes the champ!” He slides in dropkicking Marcus then clotheslining Trent. Aspen tries to jump out, but she’s caught from behind by Sparrow. He shakes his head no as he proceeds to crush her with a clothesline! The fans continue to cheer as Trent and Marcus get up tackling Sparrow down. To his aid is Alioth who pulls Hister away catching him with a big punch to the skull. RILEY STEVENS: “Starre and Sparrow may be fighting next week but tonight they are fighting together side by side.” The fans are going insane as Alioth and Sparrow seem to be handling the numbers ok. The camera oddly cuts to the crowd as jumping over the barricade is Justin Volt! The fans almost seem stunned to see him back already as he slides in grabbing Sparrow and hitting him with ‘The Volt’ a Reverse RKO! The fans boo as Alioth gets pushed back right into another one! RILEY STEVENS: “Justin Volt is back ladies and gentleman, he’s back!” Justin stands over both men glaring at each of them, turning around right into a big hug from Aspen. She spins out from the hug and raises his arm as Hister and Volt follow suit as the four of them raise each other’s arms in unison. RILEY STEVENS: “We thought it was bad when there was three of them, now there numbers have grown larger. Be sure to tune in next week for ‘Revolt Night of Champions’ where High Voltage defends the Tag Team Championship against Brotherhood of Steel, Alioth Starre starts the night by defending his GIW North Beach Championship against Seth Seeker, before ending the night in a GIW Undisputed Championship match with Eric Sparrow and Trent Lost, all this plus more next week!” They took out Tony Miranda and now Sparrow and Starre as the fans end the show in basically silence as the four members lower their arms, looking as strong as ever as the show ends. WINNERS: ALIOTH STARRE & TRENT LOST
