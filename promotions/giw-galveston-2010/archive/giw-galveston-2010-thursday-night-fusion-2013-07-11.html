Thursday Night Fusion, 11 July 2013

11 July 2013

GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: THURSDAY NIGHT FUSION LIVE! From Porretto Beach – July 11, 2013 QUOTE It’s now time for the Open Battle Royal, where we have two debuting stars facing off in the ring right now with Seth Cohen and TJ Williams looking eye to eye, knowing that the other man is there key to getting a big win here in GIW. RILEY STEVENS: “Two new stars enter; one will get his first win here in GIW.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “And one goes home unhappy!” DING DING DING! Match Summary In the opening match up we had a pair of stars making their debut in the open battle royal, that would be a singles match this week as TJ Williams and Seth Cohen did battle. Knowing how big first impressions are both men really went at it in their respective matches, battling back and forth as they went one on one. The crowd seemed impressed by both as neither man seemed out of place in a GIW ring, as they really got the crowd into it with a very even match up. The finish came when both men went for their finisher’s but were reversed out of, but it was Cohen that got the roll up out of no where taking the victory! REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner by way of pinfall…Seth Cohen!” These fans are in shock at the suddenness of the victory as Seth quickly rolls out of the ring throwing up his arm in victory as TJ sits up in the ring, shaking his head realizing he just got caught as we head to a short opening video of last week’s tag team tourney matches. WINNER: SETH COHEN QUOTE "Dirty Little Thing" by Adelitas Way hits and these fans start to boo as Nathan Starr walks out from the back with the Deep South Championship around his waist, looking into the ring at his challengers this week almost a more bar’s in more places type of look as we go from Alioth Starre to the slightly taller Michael Harris to the big man HardKore Comley standing in the ring looking down at Starr as he heads to the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Starr picked up the win last week over Daniel Starr and now faces the challenge of defending the belt.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Not easy when you got three guys like that waiting for you in the ring.” DING DING DING! There was only one thing in common with Comley, Harris, and Starre. That was the desire for the Deep South Championship which was currently held by defending champion, Nathan Starr. This wasn’t Comley’s first attempt at Deep South gold, but he was hoping it to be the last. It was, however, the first time for Alioth, twice former Undisputed Champion. Unlike Comley and Harris, he had gone in under the desire to also hurt Nathan Starr. Whether that threat would come through was to be determined, but Nathan Starr stood confident, even against the mounting odds of three men in the ring with him. RILEY STEVENS: “This match is going to be good.” Starre moved first, toward Starr, but Harris lunged in the way of him. A hard hit running big boot knocked the former Undisputed Champion silly three kinds of sideways. No way was he going to get the first hit, but ultimately, neither did Harris. Starr was amused that they had already broke down and begun to compete with one another before even reaching him, but it was he that would get the final say and first strike. Nathan dropped Comley first with a standing dropkick, rolled himself up and met Harris with a swinging neck breaker. RILEY STEVENS: “A swinging neck breaker with authority!” All of this in the nick of time to catch Starre off guard to continue the assault and spree of hot violence he was on. He aimed for a back breaker, but Starre used the ropes to avoid the assault. He couldn’t condone Starr to touch him first, he needed to inflict pain on him. He tucked under the ropes and back into the ring to follow-up on that chain of maneuvers Nathan had performed, and dropped into action with a knee drop, but as the weight of his body fell upon his knee, hoping to strike the collar bone of Starr, he rolled out of the way. Starr rolled himself out of the ring and collected his wits just as Alioth rolled and grabbed at his knee in frustration. Harris followed up and dropped down upon Starre with a quickly, and expertly applied Crossface, but Comley was there to save the day. He wasn’t there to save his opponent, just his own chances at winning. Afterward, however, he targeted Starr, whom had tried to secretly slip back into the match, only to find himself in Comley’s hard grasp. The two quarreled briefly before Harris joined the fray, bitterly. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Harris isn't done yet!” So far, Starre had yet to even lay a hand on Starr, and the other two worked together to weaken their foe. It seemed that all hope was lost and this dangerous alliance had struck gold until Harris was torn away as if a violent animal had ripped him from the kill, ribs first and performed a stiff shin breaker to knock him out of commission for a few minutes. He then targeted Comley, but Starr had found a way to fight back. Comley was a bigger guy, but Starr had more endurance and will to fight than anyone may have taken him for. Starr knocked Comley around and out of the fray, finally leaving an opening for himself and Starre to compete against one another for the first time since the their exchange on social media outlets. Nathan went for the first blow, but Starre blocked it, so Starr pulled him close for a lock up, but Starre tweaked his wrist and shot out of the hold, locking his opponent in a wrist hold, weak, but effective in gaining the upper hand quickly. It didn’t last long as Starr sweved under, curving Starre’s arm in an awkward angle to get out of the meek hold and then set his opponent up for a russian legsweep, but Starre refused to partake in any of those shenanigans. He swiveled around his opponent and quickly applied a sleeper hold upon Nathan Starr. Starr shook him off and locked him back up head to head, readying another maneuver when the final explosion in this egomaniacal struggle came when Michael Harris tore the two apart, used a short arm clothesline to drop Starre, and then a flurry of sharp right hand punches to ready the champion. Once groggy, Starr fell to a release german suplex. RILEY STEVENS: “Starr could be seriously hurt here.” He hand landed somewhat awkwardly, on his right knee. His body had turned in mid-air. He tried to ignore it as he recovered from the attack, but it would take time before even that was shaken off. Instead, he let Comley take advantage of the situation with Harris and pulled himself to the ropes as the two competitors used an opposed grapple to determine whom had the better strength check of the two. Clearly it was Harris after a few hard rights and a boot to the knee. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Hard shots from Michael there!” As the two battled it out, Starre rolled himself back into the ring after a quick breather outside and spotted Starr huddled against the ropes, favoring his right knee. He took the offense and charged. He hoped Starr would lean up and he could possibly knock his rival out of the ring with a clothesline, but instead Starr ducked under the lowest rope, to which Alioth replied with an improvised baseball slide to send the champion flying into the barricade. His knee was banged even harder against the barricade and when forced back into the ring by Starr, he found himself barely able to support that leg. Starre didn’t care. RILEY STEVENS: “That leg looks like its broken.” The former undisputed champion, finally able to lay his hands on the current Deep South champion used that injured knee to set him up with a figure four leglock. He made sure to watch out for Comley and Harris, but neither paid attention to them. At least, not until it was too late. By the time the two had come to break the submission hold, Starr was already crying out and tapping for sweet release. Alioth gave it to him, but only so he would not be stomped out by the two challengers still left in the ring. He had won, and completed his task of taking out Nathan Starr personally. REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner and NEW GIW Deep South Champion…Alioth Starre!” These fans go wild as Alioth Starre has added another title to the trophy case this time the Deep South belt as he stands up on his feet holding up the belt high in the air as the fans cheer him on while Harris and Comley are shown both in different area’s disappointed with the loss while Starr gets up favoring his knee, unable to stand all the way up as the Fusion Pre-Show ends with Alioth Starre celebrating. WINNER: ALIOTH STARRE (New GIW Deep South Champion)
