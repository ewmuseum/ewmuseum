GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: THURSDAY NIGHT REVOLT LIVE! From Porretto Beach – July 11, 2013 As Revolt goes live from Porretto Beach on the 11th of July, the show does not begin with the typical BOOM, BOOM, BOOM! Instead, standing in the ring, pacing back and forth, is the new GIW Deep South Champion Alioth Starre, a title that he had won just moments before the show went live. He is still tired and in his ring gear, and the crowd is still chanting “AL-I-OTH, AL-I-OTH” after his impressive victory. His music blares in the background as the commentators struggle to speak over it. RILEY STEVENS: “Ladies and gentlemen, welcome to Revolt! We have a huge show for you guys tonight, but right now in the ring is the brand new Deep South Champion, Alioth Starre.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I'm sure whatever he has to say could wait. This guy is a has-been.” RILEY STEVENS: “Well this has-been has now held every single title currently active in GIW. He was an Undisputed Champion, a North Beach Champion, and now he is the Deep South Champion.” AMANDA ROWLAND: “And this week he gets to fight the North Beach Champion, Michael Lynch. If he wins that match he may very well be able to say that he deserves a title shot. Could we have a double champion on our hands?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I sincerely hope not.” Alioth's music begins to die down and he holds the mic closer to his lips. The crowd cheers. ALIOTH STARRE: “I've been in GIW for a very, very long time. I've accomplished a lot, but I've also had my low moments. In my time here, I've come across a lot of cowards. Men who run at the mere sight of a challenge. But eventually, as time passes, I get into the ring with those cowards. They all after a while man up and face me – all except for one man. Wake Walker.” A mixed reaction of boos and cheers come from the crowd now. Alioth smiles. ALIOTH STARRE: “Dream matches have happened in GIW many times in the past. Alex vs. Aspen, Alexis vs. Aspen, Alioth vs. Aspen, Alioth vs. Kluge, but one match always seems to fall apart as soon as it is planned. Alioth vs. Walker. Now I get it Wake, I get that you have a great reputation here as one of the most dominant figures of your period. I get that you enjoy people inflating your ego when they call you great, but the fact that you won't put that all on the line, era vs. era, Alioth vs. Wake, it shows that you're the biggest coward of them all, because even time hasn't hardened you up.” A couple of “wow”s from the commentator booth are heard. ALIOTH STARRE: “But now you're back in GIW, and although we have only spoken briefly, I thought it would be fair for me to come out and cost you your match a few weeks ago on Revolt. Ripping that match away from you just like you have ripped our match away from me countless times. I regret nothing Wake. I regret nothing because if there is one thing I love in this world more than winning, it is seeing you lose.” Alioth looks into the camera and smiles before readjusting the belt on his shoulder. ALIOTH STARRE: “That's one thing it seems you've done a lot since you got back in GIW – lose. You are even more scared to face me because you are realizing now that this is a different time, that the competition is tougher. Maybe you are still rusty from your extended leaves from the company. Maybe you were never even that good in the first place. But, I'd rather not speculate Wake. I want to know. I want you in the ring, one on one. Now come out here and accept my challenge.” “Master of Ceremonies” hits the PA system, and the peculiar music draws out a peculiar reaction from the fans. Slowly from the back, Wake Walker make his way onto the ramp. He has a microphone himself, and looks down at Alioth from his elevated position. WAKE WALKER: “Soon, Alioth. As soon as the stars align.” “Master of Ceremonies” hits again, and Walker stares down Alioth for a moment, then makes his way to the back. Alioth seems both confused and frustrated as we fade to commercial break. QUOTE It’s time for the first match of the night and yet again just as it did last week this time to the tune of “Hi Haters” by Maino as these fans give a mixed bag of responses as Miss Jade and Justin Harmony victors last week in the first tag team match in this tournament, now face off with a new team looking to really blow through this tournament and really put themselves on the map here in GIW. The two seeming a little more comfortable with one another after having overcome there lack of chemistry to get a win, though the challenge will be much bigger this week. RILEY STEVENS: “Harmony and Jade last week defeated Harper and Jacob Jett, to get here tonight and one step closer to GIW gold.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “That girl is colorful…” Jade heads up the steps first followed by Harmony as she heads into the ring as does he the two walking calmly to there corner as “Chick Habit” by April March hits now and another duo who worked pretty well together enters as Joshua Acquin and Veronica Taylor enter from the back, side by side showing a bit of unison as Acquin pretty much willing to work with anyone if it means gold here in GIW. The two head down the ramp together, still obviously not a team but seeming more in sync then most have thus far in this tourney as Acquin ever the gentlemen heads up the ramp and holds down the ropes for Veronica. RILEY STEVENS: “Got to admit that these two were two stand out’s last week, though they did face a team with two combustible members, they still looked good as a team.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Acquin’s a tag team specialist, what did I tell you?” Veronica enters into the ring as Acquin soon follows her in as the two teams now meet eye to eye ready for action, as one team will move on and one will find themselves out of GIW, as the referee starts the match. DING DING DING! Acquin began with a few quick strikes to lure Harmony to drop his defenses in anger. It seemed to work when Harmony went for a strike back, only for Acquin to maneuver him into an abdominal stretch. Though simplistic, Harmony quickly succumb to the aches and pains of the hold. He was strung like an octopus, and stretched from his belly. Acquin made sure to apply the hold front and center, to eliminate any risk that his opponent might be able to reach for the ropes, or obtain some outside interference from Miss Jade, his waiting ally at the ropes. RILEY STEVENS: “Nice teamwork!” The move was slow and the struggle hard. Harmony tried to escape, but he could here Jade yell to him from his corner, as Taylor only cheered on Acquin. The crowd seemed to side with no one until they began stomp, but in the heat of the moment, no one know who for. No matter the surroundings, Acquin kept the hold applied and refused to unleash Harmony upon the canvas, even when he begged for it. Still, with Acquin’s clutches stretching him out and the referee shouting in his ear to give up or fight back, he resisted all temptations. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Harmony needs a tag. Bad.” Soon enough, Acquin pushed Harmony to the ring mat and tagged in Taylor with a smooth exchange between the two. She took off where he left Harmony in the ring. It was awkward trying to apply a bigger figure into the abdominal stretch, but she, unlike Acquin, kept Harmony closer to the ropes so that she could occasionally use them for support when referee was too focused on the man applied in the hold. She noticed Jade getting bitter in the corner, not able to enter the match, and then Acquin in the corner supporting her. This was clearly theirs. RILEY STEVENS: “Harmony and Jade need to get on the same page and fast.” In the end, the referee had caught Taylor at the ropes and forced her to release Harmony or else face disqualification. The wildcard match didn’t mean she had to play wild with the rules of the game. She released her male opponent and swiftly tagged Joshua Acquin back into the scene. Before Acquin could get ahold of Harmony, he had rushed to make a hot tag to Miss Jade, who came barging into the ring and into Acquin’s running clothesline. He took full advantage of Jade on the canvas and Harmony outside of the ring, recovering. Acquin applied the sharpshooter. RILEY STEVENS: “Acquin has it locked in. Jade's time is ticking unless Harmony can help her.” The hold, barely seconds in was enough to get Miss Jade to tap. She had given it all up for her team, with no hope of success or any resergence of vengeance to exact upon either of her opponents. For them, it seemed as though the match had lost before it was even started, as though destiny had chosen their numbers raw and quick. She scurried from the ring as Acquin and Taylor celebrated their quick victory. Harmony did try to lunge back in, but he hadn’t fully recovered and before he could get his legs onto the rim, Acquin and Taylor were there to shut him down. This was theirs. REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here are your winners by way of submission…Joshua Acquin and Veronica Taylor!” WINNERS: JOSHUA ACQUIN & VERONICA TAYLOR We cut to the back where Jamie Vaccaro is busy lacing up his boots. Michael Lynch enters the scene, Jamie turns around to greet him. JAMIE VACCARO: “Champ. What can I do for you?” Lynch chuckles as he stands over the seated Vaccaro. MICHAEL LYNCH: “No, Jamie, I'm here because I can do something to help you.” Jamie is clearly surprised, but he bites. JAMIE VACCARO: “Really? What is that?” Lynch crouches down to Vaccaro's level. MICHAEL LYNCH: “Just some advice. You interested in taking it?” Vaccaro shrugs. JAMIE VACCARO: “Sure.” MICHAEL LYNCH: “I want you to tell Sarah Sharp tonight that you want to revoke your number one contendership for my North Beach title. I want you to tell her that you see that I'm a dangerous man, and that you don't want to put your career at risk. JAMIE VACCARO: “Let's get one thing clear Lynch, after I get done with this Wildcard Tag Team Tournament, I'm going to make the North Beach title my main priority. But, for now, I want to become a tag team champion. When we finally do step into the ring Lynch, I'm going to give it my all. I know you're a tough competitor, I know you have made quite an impact since you showed up in GIW, but so have I. You aren't better than me in any way Lynch.” Lynch chuckles. MICHAEL LYNCH: “I gave you the chance, but your pride kept you from realizing its value. You will be a bloody mess when I'm done with you Jamie. You'll have broken bones, you'll be bruised all over. Just watch what I'm going to do to Alioth tonight. Then your future will become clear.” Jamie nods and grabs one of his bags full of equipment. JAMIE VACCARO: “I'll be watching it. But I can't stay here and listen to you go on and on about something that isn't going to happen. I'll see you in the ring a few weeks from now. It will be champion vs. champion. Tag Team vs. North Beach.” Jamie exits, Daniel Starr enters from behind Lynch. DANIEL STARR: “Excuse me Michael, but I couldn't help but overhear. You seem to be pretty confident that you can beat my friend Alioth this week on Revolt don't you?” The two men meet chest to chest. MICHAEL LYNCH: “Why wouldn't I be? He's a little man who wants to play with the big boys. Kinda like you.” Starr laughs. DANIEL STARR: “I've been called little so many times before it starts to wear on me. But you may or may not know that Alioth and I have an informal alliance called Star Power. If you mess with one of us, you mess with both of us, understand?” MICHAEL LYNCH: “Typical that the two weakest of the herd band together.” Daniel takes a step back. DANIEL STARR: “I'm challenging you to a match this week. Let's see if you are as good as you say you are.” With that, Starr leaves, leaving Lynch alone with his title belt in the locker room. We fade to a commercial break.
