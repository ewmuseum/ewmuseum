Rebellion, 31 May 2015

31 May 2015
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS SUNDAY NIGHT REBELLION 5/31/15 FROM KAUFFMAN ARENA IN GALVESTON, TEXAS Brody Harper vs. The Great and Powerful Kazan This match was about as one sided as you would expect. Brody Harper was out of it for the entire match, allowing Kazan to get the upper hand quickly using his superior strength. Brody got his moment to shine with he caught Kazan with a low blow while the referee was not paying attention, but Kazan no sold the move, using that opportunity to crush Brody Harper with a gut wrench powerbomb, one of Kazan’s favorite moves. After that, what little hope was left for Brody Harper was extinguished when Kazan connected with his finishing move, The Face Turn, for a three count. Overall, this match was entertaining, and Kazan came out of it looking like a worthy challenger for Alexander. After the match was over, Land Shark came down to ringside to dance for Brody, growling at him, presumably telling him to “Bo-Lieve!” Brody was having none of it, but the security team kept him and Land Shark from brawling. WINNER: THE GREAT AND POWERFUL KAZAN Land Shark vs. The Great and Powerful Alexander Alexander felt the need to show up, Kazan, proving that he was greater and more powerful. But Land Shark was on his game tonight, so the match was much less of a squash. At one point, Alexander tossed Land Shark into the crowd, but Land Shark managed to surf his way back to ring, picking up a few bites of popcorn for good measure. One back in the ring, Land Shark gained momentum with several well placed fin shots right to Alexander’s head. But Alexander was not to be outdone tonight, slamming Land Shark to the ground and locking in The Submission Alexander Saw on Youtube Once! While this move may be enough to cripple any man, Land Shark has no bones, only cartilage. He emerged from the hold okay and ready to lurk his way to victory. However, as the match drew to a close, Brody Harper entered the picture and grabbed one of Land Shark’s evolved legs, allowing Alexander to hit the Alexander Buster for the victory. The two on one assault continued until Kazan made the save. Land Shark and Kazan stood tall at the end of the match while Brody and Alexander were left fleeing to the back. WINNER: THE GREAT AND POWERFUL ALEXANDER Ryan Smith vs. Terry McKenna This match featured two technical specialists facing off as they try to get back to winning ways in GIW. Ryan Smith mysteriously picked up four wins since we had seen him last. House shows. Don’t ask any questions. Anyway, this was two weeks since his last great match so the crowd was expecting him to give it everything he had. However, this match was incredibly one sided. Terry McKenna showed flashes of the brilliance we all expected from him when he signed with GIW. The chain wrestling in this match was suberb, but McKenna ended up gaining control once and for all as the match drew to a close. The spike hurracanrana had Ryan Smith dazed long enough for McKenna to connect with an adopted Regal-Plex for the finish. Afterwards, McKenna got on the mic to tell the fans that he would be entering the Television Championship match later in the show, so this would not be the last we saw of him tonight. Overall, this was a great match where the crowd was firmly behind McKenna from the start. A good win for the young wrestler still trying to make a name for himself in GIW. WINNER: TERRY MCKENNA QUOTE Achilles/Reid/Lucia vs. Starks/Zuri Rosa Lucia begged her teammates to let her start this match against the much larger and more powerful George Stark. This turned out to be a mistake, as soon as the bell rang it was all George Stark. He quickly overpowered Rosa and nearly finished her off several times if it wasn’t for Gavin and Jackson jumping in to make the save. It appeared as though George managed to injure Rosa when he connected with his finishing move, The Stark Contrast, as Rosa was a non-factor for the rest of the matchup. She did managed to get a hot tag to Gavin Achilles as George made the tag to Zuri Chastain. These two went back and forth for a while with Gavin getting the upperhand towards the end of their interaction. He locked in his patented guillotine choke, and chaos descended. However, because Rosa Lucia was out, the Starks and Zuri managed to gain control of the match. Zuri attempted to finish Gavin off with her finishing move, the Overnight Drive, but Gavin managed to kick out at the last second. Zuri made the tag to Melissa Stark, who continued to work over Gavin until he landed an enziguri that gave him enough time to make the hot tag to Jackson Reid. Reid came in guns blazing, knocking Zuri and George off of the apron before turning his attention to Melissa. Melissa managed to briefly gain control with an eye rake, but it wasn’t long before Reid connected with “The Fall” for a hard fought victory. WINNER: GAVIN ACHILLES, JACKSON REID, AND ROSA LUCIA
