Rebellion, 7 September 2015

7 September 2015
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS MONDAY NIGHT REBELLION 9/7/15 FROM THE LOS MORALES BALLROOM IN LAREDO, TEXAS We open up with a ton of new faces being in the ring with the show being in Laredo. Cuchillo stands in the far corner, a face that GIW fans have picked out in a potential new signing. As the bell sounds the crowd who are still filing in watches Cuchillo immediately flatten a couple of local talents with vicious short arm lariats before he spins one wrestler around and bends him up with a rolling release suplex. The fans begin to turn on Cuchillo as he mouths off to them, he easily disposes of the wrestlers in the ring one by one until there’s only Cuchillo and a local favorite in the ring. Cuchillo goes for another clothesline but the local talent pulls the top rope down. Cuchillo pops over the top but lands on his feet easily. The local wrestler rushes at him but Cuchillo ducks and pulls the local talent over the top with him. The two battle for a moment before Cuchillo rakes the eyes of the local talent and then easily throws him to the floor as the bell sounds. WINNER: JAMIE CUCHILLO Altair and Dragasha lock up in the middle of the ring only for Dragasha to get the advantage early, the two exchange a couple of blows early but it’s Altair who takes over first, he hits a couple of signature moves before Dragasha reverses a belly to back suplex into a small package. Altair rolls away and then uses a couple of submission holds to ground the mysterious Dragasha. Dragasha is able to get out and comes off the top rope with a double axe handle but Altair takes Omega out of the air with the spear. Dragasha gets a shoulder up at two but Altair continues to ground Omega. Finally Omega is able to take back the offensive with a flurry of offense including a springboard back elbow. Omega covers but only gets another two count. Altair kicks back up and reverses a couple of attacks from Omega before he finally hits the Ruiner on Omega for the 3 count. WINNER: ALTAIR It’s rare to see Alexis Terry on the preshow, but the fans still give her the same welcome as they always do against Kelsi Ross. The match begins back and forth with Alexis Terry taking over early, she goes to throw a superkick but Ross grabs the top rope and slides to the outside. Alexis poses a bit and motions for the fans to get loud, she goes to dive through the ropes but Kelsi hits her with a forearm to the face. Alexis stumbles back and Kelsi tries to get a quick pin but Alexis rolls out. Kelsi remains on the offensive for quite some time before she finally is fought off by Alexis who nails her with a superkick right on the chin. She goes to cover Kelsi but Kelsi rolls out of the ring. Alexis is flustered. The two battle on the outside before Alexis rolls Kelsi into the ring. Alexis flips over into the ring herself and before she can get to Ross again, this time Ross slides out of the ring and motions that she is done with the match. Alexis holds up her hands and points to Ross and then the referee who merely shrugs. He begins to count Ross out until he reaches 10. Ross is given a microphone where Ross says she is above all of this and will not wrestle on the preshow ever again. Alexis is declared the winner by count out. WINNER: ALEXIS TERRY The most back and forth match on the preshow belonged to Cross and Spirit. Spirit an ECWF native wasn’t taking anything for granted, despite being on the preshow the two absolutely brought everything they had. Spirit got the upper hand first but Cross quickly took the match back over. They battle to the outside and into the first row of fans, the referee, instead of counting them out tries to get them back into the ring, and they only do so when Spirit throws Cross over the barricade and back into the ringside area. He peels the mat back and hits a suplex and then throws Cross into the ring. Cross is quickly to roll back to his feet and he hits a drop kick on Spirit. The two keep throwing bombs at one another until Spirit hits the For Celeste for the win as Cross is out. Jason slides outside of the ring holding his hand in the air as the crowd cheers, he doesn’t see Cuchillo coming from behind after the battle royal win, he nails Jason in the back with a forearm and then tosses him into the ring post as he dusts off his hands with a smirk on his face. WINNER: JASON SPIRIT
