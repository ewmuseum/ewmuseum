GIW — Card

Pre-show, 22 August 2012

22 August 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The pre-show kicked off with the open battle royal that feauted Calvin Chambers and Chase Brugani. The match began with Calvin taking a heavy advantage on the much weaker opponent, and the crowd was generally very impressed with both the size, speed, and technical wrestling ability of the newly signed GIW wrestler. The striker from Mississippi had the overmatched Brugani on the ropes extremely quickly, every punch placed perfectly to cause the most damage, and his slow, methodical method of wrestling left the fans without a doubt that he would win this match easily. However, Brugani showed signs of life late in the match, connecting with a few blows that sent Chambers bouncing off of the ropes, only for him to bounce right back and connect with a clothesline that shook the ring and sent his opponent down to the mat in a heap. He waited for Brugani to get back up to his feet, at which point he bounced off of the ropes and and connected with his finisher, the “Black Hawk Down”, when was accomplished by jumping and driving his opponent's face into the mat after pushing him down with his knees. The cover was quick and unopposed, and as “Highly Evolved” by The Vines hit the PA, the fans knew they might have a star on their hands. REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner…Calvin Chambers!” WINNER: CALVIN CHAMBERS
