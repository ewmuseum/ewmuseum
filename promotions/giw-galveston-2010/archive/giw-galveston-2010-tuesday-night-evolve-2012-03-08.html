Tuesday Night Evolve, 8 March 2012

8 March 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: EVOLVE LIVE! From the Lake Charles Civic Center in Lake Charles, LA – March 8, 2012 QUOTE Here on Evolve the show started the same every week, with the stars that are ready to fight coming down to the ring for an Open Battle Royal that only changed when there was only two, as was the case tonight. RILEY STEVENS: “Here is the first match of the night, since we have only two participants it has become a singles contest.” In the ring we have Angel Vegnaduzzo and Michael Lobo ready to go as the referee turns and calls for the bell. DING DING DING! The opening contest for Evolve is ready to get underway as the bell sounds and Michael “Xtreme” Lobo and Angel Vegnaduzzo stare at each other for a moment and tie up in the center of the ring. Lobo sends Angel into the ropes and connects with a standing dropkick. Quick cover by Michael Lobo but Angel Vegnaduzzo kicks out at two. Lobo stays focused and delivers discus punch after discus punch to the face of Angel who does his best to get his arms up to block the attacks. Eventually, Angel sees his chance to strike and connects with a chop block. Michael Lobo crumbles to one leg in pain and Angel stays on the attack, connecting with a huge vertical suplex into a quick pin, but Angel Vegnaduzzo only gets a two count for his troubles. RILEY STEVENS: “Both men coming out guns blazing.” Angel continues the attack, stomping away at every part of Michael’s body. Angel backs up and kicks Michael in the jaw, almost like he was kicking a soccer ball. Michael looks to be out of it and once again, Angel covers but only gets a two count. Vegnaduzzo looks flustered, grabbing at his hair waiting to know what it’s going to take to put Lobo away. Angel checks with the referee to make sure it was a two count and after taking a moment to catch his breathe, he scales the turnbuckle. Lobo is back up but groggy on his feet. Lobo turns to face Angel and Angle flies off the top rope attempting a moonsault but Michael is able to get out of the way in the nick of time. Angel crashes to the mat and this could be it. RILEY STEVENS: “Lobo now has a shot for the win.” Michael Lobo hurries to the top turnbuckle and the fans our on their feet wondering what the man is going to do. Michael signals for the end. He delivers the X-Bomb, a shooting star press with a frog splash ending, but Angel Vegnaduzzo is able to get his knees up at the last minute! Angle rolls Michael up; he has the tights, and is able to score the one..two..three for the win! JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner… Angel Vegnaduzzo!” The fans seem a bit let down they wanted to see that land as Angel rolls out of the ring as Michael hops up kicking out half a second too late. RILEY STEVENS: “Impressive debuts for both men but it is Angel who picks up the big win.” Angel heads backwards up the ramp posing as Michael watches him, realizing he just caught as we go to some hype videos. WINNER: ANGEL VEGNADUZZO Four enter as budding stars…Four leave with blood and scars. Alexis Terry defends her Legacy Championship live at ‘Exile’ versus Beck Ramsey, Cassidy Hightower, and Nathan Bailey IV. Four will enter only one will climb the ladder in this TLC match, who will leave with the Legacy Championship? QUOTE After the break and that video we return to the ring already facing the huge size discrepancy in the ring as Big J and Mya Denton stare across the ring at one another. RILEY STEVENS: “This is a rather odd match up, size wise it’s not even close.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Big J is going to kill that poor girl.” While everyone in the arena seems scared for her, Mya seems to be the only one with confidence as she looks across the ring at him as the bell rings. DING DING DING! The bell sounds and the much smaller Mya Denton walks right up to Big J, who is smirking, obviously happy with his opponent this week. Denton looks up at Big J and hits a hard forearm shot to the side of his face. Mya keeps it up, hitting a few more forearm shots, letting Big J know she will not be intimidated by the obvious size difference. J stumbles backwards, a little caught off guard by the fire Mya is bringing to the match. Mya connects with a MMA style kick to the leg of Big J, She goes for the kick again but this time, J is able to catch the kick. He forcefully pushes Mya hard to the mat, which garners a few boos from the crowd. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “She’s just too small.” Mya is right back up, not wanting to prove that the size difference doesn’t matter. She rushes Big J and connects with a flying forearm, which sends Big J into the ropes and out to the floor. Mya is able to catch her breather for a moment, as Big J looks a bit shocked out on the floor. He pounds the mat in anger and quickly slides inside. He is welcomed by a spinning wheel roundhouse kick from Mya that sends him to the mat. She quickly covers but only gets a two as Big J powers out. Mya drops a few axe handles to the back of Big J but Big J is able to get back to his feet, shrug the attacks off, and deliver a huge chokeslam to Mya. The crowd counts along, sure this one is over, but everyone including Big J is surprised when Mya once again has the heart to kick out at two. RILEY STEVENS: “You’ve got to give her props for heart, she’s a warrior.” Big J can’t believe it. His face is red with anger. He was sure that was it and looks down at the beaten and exhausted girl and shakes his head in disbelief. He uses her hair to pick her up. He looks her in the eye and then smirks. He connects with a devastating Samoan Drop and covers. This one is over as Big J gets the three. JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…Big J!” The fans boo his victory, they were clearly on Mya’s side in this one as he gets up raising his hand in victory he after all needed this one to get ahead here in GIW and that’s exactly what he just did. RILEY STEVENS: “First win here in GIW for Big J but you have to give props to Denton who would not give up.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “She took more of a beating for it though…” Big J exits the ring as Mya gets up to a loud applause from the crowd who appreciate her efforts as she slides out of the ring heading to the back as well. WINNER: BIG J After a short break we return to the backstage area where Jacob O’Connelly the Commissioner for Evolve stands around with the four stars from tonight’s previous matches crowded around him seeming confused. JACOB O’CONNELLY: “I have some good news for all four of you, passed down from David Sinclair.” Jacob looks up at them with a grin on his face; he seemed to care about the stars. JACOB O’CONNELLY: “All four of you will be in a GIW Television Championship contenders match next week for a shot at Henry Malkin, good luck all of you.” O’Connelly walks past them as all four share a awkward moment staring at one another before heading in their separate ways. QUOTE After the backstage segment we had back to the ring to see Frost getting ready in the ring as "Big Spender" by Adrienne Bailon hits and the fans immediately start to boo as Alexandra walks out from the back towards the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “I would hate to be anyone facing Alexandra, after what happened this week.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “That’s for sure.” Alexandra Rossi looks on with a purpose tonight, obviously with the thoughts of the last few days running through her mind. Frost looks on with grin, thinking the match is all ready in the bag, Why shouldn’t he? Alexandra is zero and two and isn’t really known as being the greatest wrestler in the world. The bell sounds and Frost mocks Alexandra Rossi, a little bit which only causes the scorned woman to become even angrier. Rossi walks right up to Frost and slaps him right across the side of the face. Frost holds his face in pain and can’t believe he was embarrassed like that. He uses his right arm to throw a punch but Alexandra hooks it and connects with a hiptoss, followed by a split across the neck, formally known as her Dethroning finishing move. Just like that, she picks up the one two three and her first victory in Galveston Island Wrestling. JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…Alexandra Rossi!” Rossi gets to her feet as the referee tries to raise her hand she pulls it away heading right out of the ring she came here to feast on poor Frost essentially, taking out her anger. RILEY STEVENS: “If I said I didn’t see this coming, I’d be lying.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “That is a mad, mad woman.” Rossi heads up the ramp towards the back as Evolve slowly fades away giving the fans ten minutes to get ready for Revolt! WINNER: ALEXANDRA ROSSI
