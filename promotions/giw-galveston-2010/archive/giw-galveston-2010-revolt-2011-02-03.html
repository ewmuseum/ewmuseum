Revolt, 3 February 2011

GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: REVOLT LIVE FROM MAR DE GALVEZ ON FEBRUARY 3rd, 2011 = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! We are brought right into the Mar De Galvez the last stop for Revolt until we head into our weekly stadium the week after ‘Exile’. In attendance are the usual die hard GIW fans all of which sporting their favorite stars gear, with the amount of people showing up seemingly growing by the week. Right away we are sent over to the announce table with Oliver and Riley. RILEY STEVENS: “Hello everyone and welcome to another edition of Thursday Night Revolt the last show before the huge show ‘Exile’. Before anyone even begins to wonder High Voltage has been barred from the show here tonight. And this card is a treat, but for the most part it’s all about momentum heading into that Super-Show.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Momentum? Isn’t the best method to lay back and not exert any energy so you’re are the freshest?” RILEY STEVENS: “Only you Riley, only you. Tonight as usual we open the show with an Open Battle Royal followed by a tag team contest featuring The Killers tagging up against rivals Frankie Raptor and Dallas Steele. They are put in a sticky situation because if they lose they’ll be fired. After The Killers actions last week, I hope that’s not the case.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Oh come on, they were just putting their names on the map. And looks to me, like it worked.” RILEY STEVENS: “It may have worked but it may have cost Scott Rydell his shot at gold in the process. A man you have seemingly turned on, all because of your love affair with Chris Jericho who fights Bret Battery here tonight.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Rydell is a GIW Legend, but Jericho is a World Wide Legend. I mean thus far he’s proven he’s the best man for the title. I don’t get why La’Sarah just hasn’t handed him the belt yet.” RILEY STEVENS: “We’ll see about that Oliver, but our main event tonight is huge. It’ll be a Six Person Elimination Tag Match. On one side you’ll have Tony Miranda, Jak Hendricks, and Marcus Hister against their rivals Aspen Chaud, Seth Seeker, and Joshua Acquin. Talk about a chance to get into your opponents head?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Who are we kidding? Aspen is going to help Tony. These fans will hate it, but I will love it.” RILEY STEVENS: “I really hope Tony shows that he’s a better man then that.” The two go silent for just a second allowing “Break the Walls Down” to hit, bringing a flow of boos from the crowd who have grown tired of Chris Jericho’s constant talk. He walks out from the back sporting a very fine looking suit, marching his way towards the ring already equipped with a microphone in hand. He steps into the ring looking out at the crowd with an arrogant smugness to him, as he starts to talk. CHRIS JERICHO: “Each and every week you hypocrites constantly doubt me. You boo me, you jeer me, and you treat me with little to no respect. You all don’t realize that I didn’t have to come and save this small company from mediocrity. I didn’t have to bring ratings, but here I stand. You all laughed at me when I lost to Mark Cross, not knowing that I actually went into that match hurt. Now I have come out here demanding the title and you choose to yet again disrespect me. I expect that from your parasites in the crowd. But La’Sarah I really expected better from you. However I took out Scott Rydell, so I expect that title brought out and presented to me right here and right now.” Jericho lowers the microphone as the fans begin to boo, in very little time La’Sarah Audric finds her way out from the back bringing with her a microphone as she stops at the top of the ramp looking down at Jericho in the ring. LA’SARAH AUDRIC: “You know Chris, I really wanted to give you the belt. Truth be told I know that you’d bring some big ratings as champ, knowing you’d be a great one. Unfortunately these fans are too simple minded to realize that. And while I would love to give you the belt, I have been constantly met with angry e-mails demanding I do not. I don’t usually care what the people think, because they’ll like what I put on either way. But this time I made an exception because this past week on Affliction I struck them in the heart. You see I signed someone away from them, without even him knowing what was happening at first. You know who I am talking about Chris. He won’t be here tonight because he and I are working out a contract, but I came out here to announce this. At ‘Exile’ it’ll be Mark Cross one on one with Chris Jericho for the Galveston Island Championship!” The fans burst out into cheers, knowing that one of their favorites Mark Cross is now in line for a shot. La’Sarah mouths “I’m sorry” while shrugging and heads to the back as Jericho brings his mic to his mouth in a fit of anger. CHRIS JERICHO: “That’s not fair! That belt should be mine! Not some guys who couldn’t cut it on Affliction! You know what; you people don’t think I can beat him do you? You people don’t think I can do it? You know what. I am going to embarrass him at ‘Exile’. I am going to make him look like a chump. Cross I hope your watching tonight, because what I do to Bret Battery will be exactly what I do to you WHEN I become the Galveston Island Champion!” Jericho screams this into the camera as Cross wherever he is, will certainly get the message loud and clear. The stakes have certainly been raised from there first match, this time it’s about the title. RILEY STEVENS: “That’s huge! Chris Jericho versus Mark Cross Two at ‘Exile’ this time it’s about the championship.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “This is a travesty! That belt should belong to Y2J!” Jericho slams the mic down and exits the ring as the segment cuts away and we head to a break. QUOTE Standing in the ring already ready to go are two men, both differing completely in looks. On one hand you have Ash Aiken a man that has simply put caught all the ladies eyes in the Hotel tonight. On the other side you have average Joe, whom literally bares the looks and name of the pun. The fans know what time it is, it’s time for the Open Battle Royal a match where new fighters can make a name for themselves. RILEY STEVENS: “Two guys looking to make a name for themselves. “Average” Joe on one side against Ash Aiken a man whom has garnered a lot of hype before his debut.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Maybe it’s because he was in those movies…what were they called.” RILEY STEVENS: “It’s a matter we shouldn’t discuss knowing we have young audiences as well.” DING DING DING! The match is started suddenly as the fans have begun to cheer “Average” Joe as he is a local wrestler whom is known to be a very nice family man. The fans cheer him on as the two come to the center of the ring where they meet up for a test of strength. When Joe is able to trip him up the fans go wild. Joe waves to his son and wife at ringside, whom marvel in their father’s success. Ash however is able to kip up right back up in impressive fashion bringing a few claps from the crowd. Joe runs at him, clearly not trained well as he runs right into a big kick to the mid-section. BAM! The crowd goes silent as he nails Joe out of no where with a ‘Sweet Cheapshot™’ a Superkick that nearly knocks him out cold. Rather then simply pinning him he decides to gloat, walking around his fallen opponent realizing that he may have just ended it there. The camera shows his son, looking up at his mom scared as Ash, screams at Joe to get up. With too much pride to just stay down, Joe tries to do just that using the ropes to get to his feet though judging by his feet he’s not all there. When he finally gets up he attempts to turn around only walking right into a move that Ash calls the ‘Nigerian Donkey Punch’ a Styles Clash only differed by spiking his opponents head into the mat. The fans go completely silent as Joe falls out of consciousness to the mat. Knowing that he must throw him over, Ash picks him up off the mat. His body is dead weight as he grabs it and tosses him over, falling harshly to the mat in front of his wife and son, the match over. RILEY STEVENS: “…Is that man ok?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Who cares? This guy looks great! Having a heart is not needed in this business.” Almost immediately the referee slides out checking on Joe who is out cold. The son is trying to see if his son is ok. Shamefully Ash slides out of the ring, blowing a kiss to Joe’s wife whom almost appears to not mind it. Ash heads to the back to the fans silence as the referee continues to survey the damage as we head backstage. WINNER: ASH AIKEN We head to the backstage area this time in Aspen Chaud’s locker-room where she currently sits looking down at the ground, sighing a bit as she thinks about the match tonight. There is a knock at the door, she looks up to answer. ASPEN CHAUD: “Come in.” The door opens and in walks Joshua Acquin, wearing a shirt and jeans as he looks over at Aspen who looks up at him still looking demoralized. JOSHUA ACQUIN: “I’m sure your not feeling that great right now, but I wanted to swing by and see whose side you really are on tonight. I don’t want to go out there and get betrayed.” Aspen looks up at him, realizing that one of the people she used to consider one of her best friends is now just as untrusting as anyone else. ASPEN CHAUD: “Honestly, I don’t know. He hasn’t even come to me about it yet. I don’t want to screw you or Seth over tonight.” Aspen says this seeming to realize that it’s a very likely chance it happens. Joshua doesn’t seem totally all that sympathetic. JOSHUA ACQUIN: “Then don’t.” Joshua turns and walks out the door, leaving Aspen a little taken back by what his thoughts were on the situation as she held her head again, realizing that things were just going to get worse as we take a commercial break. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = From there we are taken to another area backstage where Jak Hendricks is walking around, looking for something. He comes into the catering area, where a little crowd has gathered around someone. He walks over spreading apart the people to move in between them. JAK HENDRICKS: “What’s going on?” As he spreads the people apart he’s shocked to see that it’s one of his friends sprawled out on the floor, out cold this one being Seth. Everyone looks at him realizing that he’s his friend. JAK HENDRICKS: “SEEKER!” Jak angrily walks off, heading back towards the locker-room areas, leaving Seth to be checked out by Dr. House whom can be seen coming onto the scene before we head to the ring. QUOTE This time when we come into the ring we see Dallas Steele and Frankie Raptor arguing away inside the ring. After last week’s defeat you’d think they may have tried to get on the same page, but it’s clear they have not. While they bicker, "Bodies by Saliva hits causing the fans to boo as The Killers, Brody Harper and Nelson Fielder make their entrance from the back. Before last week they were unknown now they have made a name for themselves off of the Rydell attack. RILEY STEVENS: “These two teams differ a lot. The Killers seem very much together while Steele and Raptor are bitter rivals.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Thus why Steele and Raptor will find themselves fired when they lose.” Already arguing over who will start the match when Brody gets into the ring, he watches on waiting to see just who will start on the other side. Finally they settle on Raptor who pushes Steele out then turns around ready to fight. DING DING DING! Almost right away Brody comes forward demolishing Frankie with a clothesline. The fans boo; though they don’t give a damn about Steele or Raptor they have come to hate The Killers. With him down, he sets up back in the corner waiting for him to return to his feet. When Raptor does he receives a second clothesline that nearly spins him into a three sixty. The fans are not pleased already seeing Brody demolish him early on. Steele realizing their jobs are on the line begs to get in, but its clear Raptor is in no condition to do so. Trying to crawl away proves to be to no avail as Brody grabs him by the tights and pulls him to his feet, spinning him around and then setting him up into a Powerbomb! The fans boo as Brody delivers it with perfection before turning to Nelson and yelling “You want some” in very redneck fashion. He walks over tagging in Nelson as Steele has become frantic in reaching out for a tag not wanting to lose his job. Fielder, heads to the top rope where he jumps off hitting a perfect Leg Drop! With Raptor very much so out, Nelson makes the cover 1…Steele tries to get in…2…Brody jumps in stopping him in his track…3! That’s it The Killers send them packing. RILEY STEVENS: “And just like that there careers are over.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “The Killers baby, the rise continues!” The fans boo as Steele immediately stands over the fallen Raptor screaming at him that it’s his fault. However Brody ends up behind him, while Nelson stands across from him. He turns shaking his head and pleading for a hand shake, instead he’s kicked in the gut and lifted into the air by Brody first as Nelson grabs hold and together they hit ‘The Killer Move’ a Flapjack DDT! With that they take their leave having ended two careers, while adding injuries to insult as we take a break. WINNERS: THE KILLERS (Brody Harper & Nelson Fielder) Before returning to the show we are shown a hype video for the upcoming Affliction show on Monday which will be a short, yet huge Open Battle Royal featuring all the Affliction Stars as well as many of Revolt’s stars as well. It’ll surely be a huge show and the last stop before ‘Exile’. Back to the back we go where Jak Hendricks is still looking through the locker-room areas. He stops at his own locker-room where he opens up the door, looking around. JAK HENDRICKS: “Mitch! He took out Seth; we got to go get him.” Jak waits for a second only to hear no response; he angrily knocks on the door to the bathroom then opens it up only to find no Mitch at all. Suddenly he walks out shocked to see Mitch laid out behind the couch. He runs over, jumping down beside him. JAK HENDRICKS: “Mitch!” Jak checks on him, he too has been left out cold only he’s left with a big lip and a bloody nose as well. The fans in the arena are cheering as Jak finds a note on top of him; he opens it up and then crumples it up throwing it away. Jak storms out of the locker-room as the cameraman reaches down picking it up, and opening it up in front of the camera as the notes reads Now Who Will Help You?. As we take a break. QUOTE Coming back from commercials we come to see Bret Battery being jeered by the crowd. It’s clear that after having come and gone so many times, he’d become nothing more then a filler to all of them. Though he tried to insist he was still a threat, none of them took him seriously. When “Break the Walls Down” hit however the fans began to boo, turning their hatred onto the man that truly mattered as out from the back Chris Jericho came walking with his usual confidence yet still looking angry about what happened earlier tonight. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Here’s the man that should be Galveston Island Champion everyone.” RILEY STEVENS: “Why? Because he took out Scott Rydell in a three on one attack? Hardly seems means to make a man champion.” Jericho steps into the ring, looking across at Bret Battery who seems to actually be marveling at him from across the ring. Jericho scoffs a bit at him, as the referee checks to see if both men are ready to go before calling for the bell. DING DING DING! The two men begin to circle each other and very clearly Bret Battery wants no part of Chris Jericho. The fans begin to boo him as Jericho shoots in only for Bret to back away, quickly. Jericho points to the mat offering Bret a way out, telling him that it would be easier to just lay down. At first it appears Bret does not want to do it, but after only a few moments he begins to get to one knee willingly doing it. But as he is half way there Jericho rears back a leg and then brings it forward with a massive kick to Battery’s chest! The fans actually give him a small cheer for Bret’s pain only to immediately turn on Jericho when he screams out “I’m the champion!”. Bret rolls around holding his chest, giving Jericho to ask for a microphone from the outside, the staff member giving it to him seems unsure if he should be as Jericho looks down on Bret whom continues to clutch his chest. CHRIS JERICHO: “Mark Cross! You got lucky once but it won’t happen again. I told you to watch what I did tonight and I hope you are. You see Bret Battery is a symbol of what you and every other fan and wrestler is to me, beneath me!” Jericho sets the microphone down, as Bret has begun to get to his feet. Running forward he nails him with a vicious ‘Codebreaker’, that sends him right back to the mat this time holding his jaw which may have been dislocated during the move. Wasting little time Jericho walks back to the mic lifting it up, holding it to his mouth again. Though the match should be over he uses this time to send out his message even louder. CHRIS JERICHO: “I am the star of GIW! Rydell learned that the hard way and so will you Mark. This title is mine. When I get my hands on it, nobody will be able to take it away from me. NOBODY! I am the best at what I do, and at ‘Exile’. You fans will all call me, GIW’s Savior!” This time he tosses the mic out of the ring and turns back, getting Bret in position he turns him and then gets him right into the ‘Walls of Jericho’! The fans boo him the entire time as almost immediately Bret taps out. However rather immediately releasing the hold, he holds it in a few more seconds before letting it go as the referee basically has to pry him off. RILEY STEVENS: “Tonight has been hard to watch. We’ve seen a local crushed in the first match, two men fired and beaten in the second match, and now we see Bret Battery embarrassed all so Chris Jericho can send a message.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “It’s called wrestling, where only the strong survive! Chris Jericho is our Savior and the Next Champion!” In the ring, it appears Jericho may have heard Oliver’s words as he turns to the camera flashing a title around his waist as he points into the camera, namely at Mark Cross whom he will face come ‘Exile’, as we head to the back. WINNER: CHRIS JERICHO The camera heads to the back where to the surprise of nearly everyone we are met with the presence of Leon Drake with Scott Rydell following not far behind him. Running up on them quickly is Sarah Sharp with a microphone. SARAH SHARP: “Scott! Everyone thought you were out of action, what are you doing here?” Rydell stops not looking completely pleased, his partner Leon stopping with him standing right behind Scott as he answers. SCOTT RYDELL: “You know if I were anyone else that question would seem almost fitting, but I am not anyone else. I am Scott Rydell; though I did suffer a minor concussion the Doctor cleared me for ‘Exile’.” Basically cutting him off she brings the microphone back to her lips, immediately asking another question. SARAH SHARP: “Then what are your thoughts on La’Sarah booking Mark Cross versus Chris Jericho for the belt at ‘Exile’ earlier tonight.” Rydell looks down at her a little angry that she would cut him off like she did, but as she holds the microphone back up to him he finishes anyways. SCOTT RYDELL: “Of course I’m fine with it, I told her she could do it. You see I am not going to compete for the belt with this concussion. At ‘Exile’ I’ll win the Open Battle Royal and then be on my way back to the title. I have a hard time seeing Jericho winning, but if he does he should know I’ll be waiting.” Before Rydell can walk away, he basically walks right into Ash Aiken who has a big smirk on his face. ASH AIKEN: “You really think you have a chance in that Open Battle Royal? You see while I personally think I should be in the Main Event already. If I am relegated to this match I’ll make the most of it. I just didn’t realize that a former champion would be in this match. Will make my win seem only greater.” Ash smiles walking by Scott purposely nudging him with his shoulder as Sarah can be seen checking him out as he walks by as Rydell looks over at Drake shaking his head as we go to the ring for the main event. QUOTE We have arrived yet again at the main event of Revolt, where the stars truly do shine. The fans are ready for the night’s big main event, which will feature six of Revolt’s biggest stars. “The Man Who Sold the World” by Nirvana hits as the fans hear the familiar music and immediately begin to boo. Out from the back comes Marcus Hister walking with that usual scary look on his face as he walks up the steps and into the ring, receiving the boos with pride. RILEY STEVENS: “Marcus Hister as we all know, will face Joshua Acquin in the Finals of the GIW Television Championship Scramble at ‘Exile’. Though after having basically stayed out of the finishes, do you think he deserves to?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Are you dumb? Marcus Hister is one of our most intelligent wrestlers. He knew that if he stayed back, he’d make it safely. And look he did it.” Hister goes to his corner, waiting as “Forever” by Drake now echoes through the crowd and an angry Jak Hendricks walks out from the back. We’ve seen him through out the night, watching as his little buddies Seth and Mitch were taken out and it’s clear he’s in a bad mood wasting little time storming to the ring, walking across with Hister. RILEY STEVENS: “Someone is not in a good mood.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Would you be if some beast decided to take out two of your friends for no reason?” Finally "The Human Stain" by Kamelot hits and too many the leader of the team Tony Miranda makes his entrance from the back. He has a big smirk on his face; soaking in the very heavy boos he is receiving here in the Mar De Galvez. His smirk shows that of a man that does not give a damn about it at all, as he walks out slowly towards the ring looking extremely confident and when a member of the other team is under your command why wouldn’t you be. RILEY STEVENS: “This man is the definition of arrogance. If I hear him one more time say this is Perfect Combination’s year, I’ll go mad.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Let’s see, they won the tag belts back at ‘Genesis’. Then Tony defeated Aspen last week. And at ‘Exile’ they’ll regain the belts. I mean has it not been there year?” Judging by Riley’s quiet he realizes it is probably the truth. "Monster" by Skillet now hits bringing a very loud pop from the crowd in attendance here in the hotel. The fans know that the former champion Joshua Acquin is in attendance after a huge win last week. He walks down towards the ring staring into the ring where Tony Miranda has gathered Jak and Marcus and is talking over the match, all of them still staring at Josh who stops before getting into the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Here’s our former GIW Television Champion, think he will regain the belt?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Nope. Marcus Hister will find a way.” "Bullseye" by Aly and AJ is the next music to hit bringing a gigantic cheer from the crowd as she walks back from the back, rocking black and pink as she waves to the crowd high fiving them as she heads down to the ring. She takes her spot by Acquin, who looks at her seemingly very untrusting of her presence. RILEY STEVENS: “How can you trust her? Tony’s got her controlled like a puppet.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “And I love it.” The whole team in the ring is looking out at them, Tony giving Aspen a big smirk as lastly “Nightmare” by Avenged Sevenfold hits sparking another loud cheer from the crowd as the big man walks out from the back. Seeker is by far the biggest man in the match and as he walks up behind Acquin and Chaud it seems to have become very even. RILEY STEVENS: “And then there’s Grim Reaper whom has been in a bit of action today having taken out Mitch and Seth earlier on.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “They didn’t need to be involved.” All three look at each other and then run all at once into the ring none of them trusting the other team enough to go in by themselves. They gather in their corner and it’s Aspen that’s chosen from her team to fight against Jak Hendricks here to start the match. DING DING DING! The fans cheer as the two begin to circle around each other, when suddenly Jack stops chuckling to himself turning to Tony and pointing at her screaming “Tell her to lay down”, with a big smirk on his face. However he’s a bit shocked when Tony shakes his head no, when he turns back Aspen comes forward with a ‘Rage Within’ a Thesz Press Followed by Punches! The fans go wild as she lets loose apparently Tony has let her fight like he said he would. Jak is able to use his strength to push her off, scrambling to his feet. Aspen catches him with a big Backhand Chop! The fans cheer as she land another one. She sets him up against the ropes and then whips him off. She swings at him but he ducks under it and cheaply grabs her by the hair and slams her down to the mat. The referee gives him a warning about the hair as Aspen holds her head clearly having been hurt a bit by that move. She slowly starts to get up with Jak stalking her from behind, waiting for something big. However to his surprise she nails him with a forearm and then bounces back jumping over and tagging in Joshua Acquin! The crowd pops as he comes in like a ball of fire. He crushes him with a clothesline and then another, as Aspen and Seeker cheer him on in the corner. Jak is able to push him back into his own corner but doesn’t relent, going after him trying to choke him out. The referee pulls him off and gives him another verbal warning but as he does Tony and Marcus land some shots in the corner. Aspen and Seeker try to point it out, but when the referee finally turns it’s too late as he walks forward right into a big Yakuza Kick! The fans boo the actions as Jak makes the cover 1…2…Kick Out! The fans cheer as Jak angrily gets up lifting, Joshua to his feet. As he does Josh breaks free and lands a big punch! The fans boo as Jak bounces back complaining of being poked in the eye. As the referee checks on him, Tony catches him in the back of the head with a punch. Aspen attempts to come in to stop him, but Tony yells at the referee to stop her allowing Marcus Hister into the ring where he lands a Superkick right onto Acquin! The referee turns and Hister just barely gets out before he does, allowing Jak to make the cover on Acquin 1…2…3! Joshua Acquin is Eliminated RILEY STEVENS: “How cheap was that?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I call that team work.” Wasting little time, Seeker steps into the ring screaming at Jak to come fight him. Looking at him, he shakes his head complaining about his eye injury tagging in Marcus Hister who seems none to pleased to be tossed in against Seeker. As he gets in, he tries to insist Jak should fight him but this momentary break allows Seeker to catch him with one of his gigantic fists! The fans cheer as Marcus bounces around back into the corner. Seeker goes full blast at him only for Hister to move out of the way at the last second. With Seth bouncing back holding his chest he goes to the top rope, where he tries to jump off at Seeker only to land right into the big mans arms. Hister flails around trying to get free, and he’s just able to by giving Seeker an elbow to the head. The fans boo as he falls down to the mat, however he is met with a big kick to the chest that Seeker follows up right into the ‘Don't Fear The Reaper’ a Jackhammer! The fans go nuts knowing Hister is down for the count as he makes the cover 1…2…3! Marcus Hister is Eliminated RILEY STEVENS: “Just like that Seeker evens up the score.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Luck.” The fans are still going wild when without hesitation, Tony steps into the ring fearlessly looking up at the big man. The two go in for a test of strength and despite Tony’s initial confidence it’s still clear that he won’t over power Tony. Right away he begins getting backed up, into the corner. The fans are suddenly caught off guard when a suddenly enraged Jak Hendricks is able to basically tackle Seth running full blast across the ring tackling him out of the ring! The fans begin to boo as the two start to battle outside of the ring. The referee immediately Disqualifies Jak Hendricks but it’s clear what his goal is, it’s to punish Seeker. Having taken quite a nasty fall out of the ring, Seeker finds himself on his back in pain as Jak pounds away at Seeker from on top. The referee has reached a count of seven, as Aspen has now jumped down trying to break them apart. It’s too late though as the referee, has counted out Seth Seeker! The fans immediately begin to boo thinking this is a sham. Jak Hendricks and Seth Seeker are Eliminated RILEY STEVENS: “Jak just basically eliminated himself as well as Seth Seeker.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Can you blame him? I mean the guy had to find two of his friends attacked.” It’s clear that the two are not done at all, as Jak takes his eyes off the ball for a second as when he waits for the announcement of their eliminations Seeker is able to basically toss him off. The fans cheer as he bounces to his feet, coming at him as Jak tries to back up. The referee now has called for Aspen to get into the ring where Tony is waiting for her. The fans are half way watching the battle on the ramp and the other half watching inside the ring. As the two wrestle around, trying to gain an advantage referee’s storm out from the back separating them. They are barely able to, as they settle that. Finally they are able to drag them both separate ways, as the camera returns to the ring where Aspen and Tony are still standing across the ring from another. Aspen is set up in fighting stance, but Tony shakes his head bringing a lot of boos from the crowd. Chaud, lowers her hands a bit as Tony points to the mat. Aspen at first shakes her, seeming reluctant. Miranda shows no letting up, pointing again to the mat. Finally she starts to get down onto the mat, slowly bringing a lot of boos from the crowd who want to see her pop him. Instead the unthinkable happen as Tony hooks the leg 1…2…3! Getting the cheap win. RILEY STEVENS: “Could these guys have cheated any more?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “You know how long I have wanted to see Aspen put in her place? This is so great.” RILEY STEVENS: “It’s a travesty is what this is. Everyone be sure to tune in for Affliction this week where a Huge Battle Royal will be the last stop before the HUGE ‘Exile’.” The fans have not let up, feeling that this match has ended in a sham all the way through. Tony Miranda gets to his feet smirking. With Jericho and Cross booked in a title match, Acquin and Hister ready to fight for the GIW Television title, and Jak Hendricks and Seth Seeker ready to finish their rivalry all set up for ‘Exile’, things are all set. The last piece is Tony Miranda who commands Aspen, to raise his arm. She shakes her head, embarrassed to look out at the friends as she raises his arm in victory. Perfect Combination has continued it’s run towards the Tag Titles as the show comes to an end, on what may become a weekly thing Aspen Chaud raising Tony Miranda’s arm in victory as the show fades. WINNERS: TONY MIRANDA, JAK HENDRICKS, & MARCUS HISTER
