GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS SUNDAY NIGHT REVOLT 5/10/15 FROM KAUFFMAN ARENA IN GALVESTON, TEXAS BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! “Over and Under” by Egypt Central hits the PA system as the blue and white fireworks stream into the air outside of Kauffman Arena, signaling to the fans both in and out of the arena that it is time for Revolt. Just one week removed from GIW’s first pay-per-view since signing a television deal and two weeks away from Extreme TV, the fans know they are going to be in for a big night tonight. The camera pans around to show signs being waved around by the fans: “One More Match CDE”, “Alexis Terry Two Week Reign” and “Justice for Bronx” are the notable ones. The music dies down as “Bat Country” by Avenged Sevenfold takes over, and the fans give a mixed reaction to Johnny Violence as he steps out from behind the curtain. RUBY REYNOLDS: “Ladies and gentlemen, please welcome the number one contender for the GIW Undisputed Championship… Johnny Violence!” His nameplate flashes across the screen as the voices of the commentators can be heard. Once the fans are reminded that Johnny Violence will be the man to take on Chris Callum next, they begin to boo loudly. He shakes his head as he begins to make his way to the ring. SARAH SHARP: "As we return to Kauffman Arena for Revolt, it seems that Johnny Violence has something he needs to say." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Well after that absolutely disgusting attack Chris Callum at the hands of Alexis Terry last week at Exile, I’m sure Johnny is coming out here to call his shot. Chris isn’t here this week, and from what we are hearing, he won’t be here next week either. There is no way he is going to be ready for a match at Extreme TV, let alone a hardcore match." SARAH SHARP: "Aspen isn’t here tonight either. She is with Chris right now helping him recover. But based on what she has been leaking over the past few days, she seems confident Chris will be able to compete. I’m sure this a rough time for her right now. She takes responsibility for making Alexis Terry the embodiment of evil, and we all know that she cares pretty deeply for Chris. I don’t think she would rush him out to the ring unless she was sure he was ready." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "The thing is, you’re never ready for a hardcore match. No one ever can be. But as talented as Chris is, Johnny Violence is going to be at full strength. That, and the fact that he doesn’t mind going hardcore – if you’re picking against Johnny at this point, you’re nuts." Johnny climbed up the steel steps to enter the ring, microphone already in hand. His music began to die as he took his place in front of the camera. JOHNNY VIOLENCE: "You all have no idea what to make of me, do ya?" Johnny tossed the microphone between his hands as he chuckled. JOHNNY VIOLENCE: "You don’t know if I am the man who took out Christopher Darren Erns, or the man who turned on Alexis Terry, do ya?" He receives a loud mixed reaction from the fans as he paused. JOHNNY VIOLENCE: "Well since Aspen Chaud is away tending to her fallen Superman, I thought I would take this time to clear the air. I thought it would be good to explain to you who I am, and what you should expect at Extreme TV when I get my Undisputed title shot against Chris Callum." Johnny moves closer to the edge of the ring, and therefore the camera as well. JOHNNY VIOLENCE: "Ever since I made my debut here in GIW, the management has seen me as a threat. A guy that could knock off their top stars and take all of the title belts for himself. Well, when the management sees you as a threat, you become a problem; and for years now muscles have been flexed, and I have been left out of the title picture." Boos begin to overtake the cheers as the fans recognize Johnny’s tone. JOHNNY VIOLENCE: "When I get my title matches, I always have to compete against two, three, or even four other guys in the ring. That way the bosses know that someone else will screw up, someone else will get pinned, and someone else will win the belt." Johnny leans back on his heels as he examines the crowd. JOHNNY VIOLENCE: "I came back to Galveston because I know that I deserve to be Undisputed Champion. I took out Christopher Darren Erns on my first night back – and what did I get for a reward? Nothing. Not a damn thing. Meanwhile, CDE and Lynch are main eventing on pay per view." More boos from the crowd. They clearly respect and recognize the work CDE and Michael Lynch went through to make that match one to remember, and now Johnny was trashing it. JOHNNY VIOLENCE: "But Aspen Chaud made a mistake. A big mistake. She gave me a the title shot I have always wanted if I turned my back on Alexis Terry. The thing was, and I thought I made this very clear from the beginning, was that I never had any allegiance to Alexis Terry. She was just a stepping stone that would be get me into the main event – and guess what everyone – it worked." A big smile comes across Johnny’s face. JOHNNY VIOLENCE: "So in two weeks, I get to go one on one with Chris Callum and the rulebook will be out of the picture. Chris Callum is injured and we don’t even know if he will be able to go at Extreme TV. Aspen Chaud is at home feeding him soup and fluffing his pillow all because she knows that what I do to Chris at ETV is going to be her fault. She gave me this opportunity, and I’m not going to waste it." More boos from the crowd at the mention of Chris Callum. JOHNNY VIOLENCE: "I saw what Alexis did to Chris. I saw his blood and spit make the first few rows a splash zone. It was hilarious because everyone on commentary and everyone on Stewart Beach one night ago thought that was the most brutal thing they had ever seen. Well let me assure you, if you thought Alexis Terry was deadly with brass knucks, wait until you but a man’s strength behind them." Johnny holds up a fist and examines it closely, imagining the knucks. JOHNNY VIOLENCE: "Maybe I will bring some knucks, maybe I’ll bring some chairs or a sledgehammer. It doesn’t matter, because whatever weapon I choose to beat Chris Callum with, I’m not going to miss. Aspen, there won’t be a Chris Callum for you to tend to. There won’t be a Chris Callum for the paramedics to hoist away. All that will be left of Chris Callum when I am done with him will be a blood stain and his title belt. If Chris is really the reincarnation of Jesus Christ, he won’t die for my sins, he’ll die for my title belt." With a big laugh, Johnny soaks in all of the boos. JOHNNY VIOLENCE: "This match won’t be fair, but in the weeks leading up to it, I want to make sure Chris and I get the same advantages. I already have to fight Gaylord Prime tonight while Chris is at home lounging on his couch, but I’m not angry. Gaylord Prime will be a good tuneup for me this week, so I appreciate Aspen doing me that favor. But I’m not going to let you make me fight some gauntlet next week just so your little boyfriend has the advantage heading into ETV. No, next week, I’ll be taking the week off." His tone rises to more of joking manner. JOHNNY VIOLENCE: "Who knows where I’ll go. Maybe Miami, Las Vegas, or New York City… those all sound nice. Anywhere but this shithole that is Galveston, Texas." He stares directly into the camera. JOHNNY VIOLENCE: "And I’ll be able to sleep easy because this will be the last time I walk out of Galveston without championship gold around my waist. Chris Callum, Aspen Chaud – consider yourselves warned. If you thought Exile was rough, just wait for Extreme TV." “Bat Country” hits again as Johnny begins to make his way out of the ring. Once he throws a foot through the ropes, the sound of an old gravely voice comes over the speakers. PAUL MARSHALL: “Wait a minute. Wait just a minute. Cut that damn music.” Paul Marshall, a longtime GIW road agent, steps through the curtain as the music abruptly stops. Johnny steps back into the ring with a confused look on his face as Paul stands on the stage. PAUL MARSHALL: “Johnny, with Aspen away for the next few weeks, and Matt deciding to join the roster again as a wrestler, that puts me in charge of the show.” Cheers from the crowd. Paul had always been a fan favorite, and although the fans still respect Aspen, they are more than willing to allow Paul to run the show. Johnny begins to chuckle in the ring. PAUL MARSHALL: “Now I ain’t gonna lie. I’m a bit old fashioned, and I have my way of doing things, but I know how to run a show. And I know that if you want time in the ring to speak, you gotta ask for it. And I know that if you want time off, you gotta ask for it.” Johnny raises an eyebrow, but Paul is completely serious. He speaks slowly and methodically, clearly exhausted by the new duties, but willing to put his foot down. PAUL MARSHALL: “I’m just here temporarily, but I’ll be damned if I’m gonna let you all walk over me. It’s too late to take away your air time, but it isn’t too late to take away your vacation.” Johnny was quick to jump on the veteran’s words. JOHNNY VIOLENCE: "I am the number one contender for the most prestigious title belt in this company, I deserve to call my own shots." Paul shook his head. PAUL MARSHALL: “That just ain’t true kid. It just ain’t true. But I’m gonna cut you a deal.” Johnny was laughing hysterically in the ring now. JOHNNY VIOLENCE: "Oh, you’re gonna cut me a deal? Who the hell are you?" Paul raised his voice for the first time as Johnny watched on in amusement. PAUL MARSHALL: “I’m the acting commissioner of Revolt, and if you don’t show me some respect, I’m gonna take this deal off the table – so listen up, because if you wanna be the man on all the banners, you gotta play the game my way for the next few weeks.” Cheers from the fans as Paul pauses. PAUL MARSHALL: “If you want a week off, I’ll give you a week off. That’s fair in my book, and I’m a fair man. But because, and ONLY because you came out here acting like an entitled little shit, you’re gonna have to work for that week off.” Johnny waves a hand towards the stage. JOHNNY VIOLENCE: "This is EXACTLY what I’m talking about…" Paul shakes his head and switches hands with the microphone. PAUL MARSHALL: “Aspen seems to think hardcore wrasslin is what these people wanna see. I don’t agree, but I’m not gonna turn the company on its head. So tonight, I’m gonna give them a taste of what they will see in two weeks. You match with Gaylord Prime is gonna be a hardcore match, and if you win that, I’ll give you the week off next week. Now is that fair?” Johnny leans over the top rope as he responds. JOHNNY VIOLENCE: "I will NOT be Galveston next week, no matter what you tell me to do old man. I’ve already made up my mind, and that’s what is going to happen…" Paul points directly to Violence. PAUL MARSHALL: “You were given a title shot for no damn reason. If you want a week off, you’re gonna earn it. That’s how I do business, and I’m gonna make you play by my rules.” “Over and Under” begins to play as Johnny steps back into the ring, a frustrated, but still amused look on his face. Paul glares at him for a moment before moving back behind the curtain. SARAH SHARP: "Paul Marshall just laid down the law! I think he is going to be a welcome replacement here in GIW, even if it is only temporary." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "You see that look in Johnny’s eyes? Paul just made an enemy." SARAH SHARP: "We’re gonna take a quick commercial break, but when we come back we will return to our regularly scheduled programming, starting off with The Sir defending his Television Championship." We fade to commercial. QUOTE “Not I” by Demon Hunter was the first music to play as we got ready for the Television Championship match. SARAH SHARP: "Defending the TV is no easy feat, and we will see tonight if The Sir is up for the challenge." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "He’s been on the rise as of late, so I think he can pull this off if I’m honest. But then again, we have no idea who is going to enter." The Sir settled into his corner as he handed the title belt to the referee. She smiled and blew kisses on the ramp as she made her way down. SARAH SHARP: "Now this is an interesting development. Beauty and Grace shocked the world last week at Exile when they were one of the most dominant teams in the match. That said, no member of the group has managed to pick up a win so far in GIW, but they have proven themselves to be tough competitors." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "These girls show a lot of moxie, and they are getting better every week. Don’t underestimate Wanda in this match." RUBY REYNOLDS: “Now entering the ring, representing Beauty and Grace… Wanda Baez!” Wanda stepped into the ring and immediately offered her hand to The Sir, which he accepted. There was a great deal of respect between these two competitors, but they both know that in GIW, becoming a champion means everything. The Sir knew Wanda would be a worthy challenger tonight. SARAH SHARP: "Who is going to come out next?" As soon as Sarah finished speaking, “Off With Her Head” by Icon for Hire began to play and received almost instantaneous boos from the audience. Alexis Terry and Bronx Valescence stepped through the curtain with big smiles. The Deep South title belt looked disgusting around the shoulder of Alexis Terry. Bronx Valescence had a microphone in his hand. RUBY REYNOLDS: “Ladies and gentlemen…” Bronx screeched at the top of his lungs. BRONX VALESCENCE: "SHUT UP RUBY." The music died down as the boos were the loudest thing in the arena. BRONX VALESCENCE: "LADIES AND GENTLEMEN… NOW ENTERING YOUR PRESENCE AND THE TV TITLE OPEN MATCH… PLEASE WELCOME, WEIGHING IN A SEXY 205 AND ONE HALF POUNDS OF AWESOMENESS… SIR SUPLEX, THE ANSWER, THE BEST KEPT SECRET IN PROFESSIONAL WRESTLING… ME.. BRONXY V – AND THE DEEP SOUTH CHAMPION OF TEXAS… THE QUEEN… THE MOST GLAMOUROUS EXPORT… AND THE NEXT GIW GRAND SLAM CHAMPION… ALEXIS TERRY!" Terry put on a sinister smile as she and Bronx made their way to the ring to a chorus of boos. SARAH SHARP: "Wait, so Bronx AND Alexis are in this match? And if he introduced her as the next grand slam champion, does that mean they are going to be working together?" OLIVER THIBODAUX: "It sure seems that way Sharpie. Alexis is out for blood." The Sir and Wanda Baez ended up standing together on one side of the ring as they began to go over their game plan. They were competitors, but it seemed like they were going to work together in this match to ensure fairness. Bronx and Alexis slid into the ring laughing and joking. DING! DING! DING! SARAH SHARP: "Well, here we go. Let’s see how this turns out." The match began with Sir charging Alexis and Wanda charging Bronx, but Bronx quickly intercepted The Sir, totally dismissing Wanda, as Alexis watched. Alexis used this as a time to hit a dropkick on the confused Wanda. She pulled Wanda up by the hair and immediately tried to toss her over the top rope, but Wanda managed to catch herself on the apron and then snap Alexis’ head across the ropes. From the apron, she climbed the turnbuckle and much to the amusement of the fans, the risk taker of Beauty and Grace threw herself off the top and connected with a big crossbody/pin combo. 1…2… Terry managed to kick out. Across the ring, The Sir had Bronx in the corner landing shot after shot. While the ref was focusing on the pin attempt, no one was there to stop The Sir from absolutely laying into Bronx. But eventually, Bronx managed to get out of the assault with an eye rake. He took time to brag about it to the fans as The Sir staggered backwards, but then rushed forward and connected with a big boot to Bronx in the corner. OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Bronx knows better than to brag like that." SARAH SHARP: "He can’t help it Oliver." Wanda was still on Alexis Terry. She connected with a standing Moonsault for another big pop from the fans, but also a quick one count. She pulled Alexis up and tossed whipped her into the ropes, connecting with a bicycle kick as the Deep South Champion was thrown back. Another pin attempt..1….2 KICKOUT. By this point the sound of the referee making the count was concerning The Sir, who knew that he could lose the match without being pinned. The Sir charged Wanda, but the much quicker wrestler was able to avoid the attempt a spear. The Sir’s head was sent straight into the bottom turnbuckle as he was off his feet. With all three competitors down, Wanda looked to the fans for support before she climbed the turnbuckle. SARAH SHARP: "Wanda is going high risk! I don’t know what she is looking for, but The Sir is going to find out really quick." Just before Wanda made the leap, Bronx Valescence appeared from out of nowhere to push her straight off the turnbuckle. Wanda landed hard, hitting her head on the steel barrier on the outside. Even Bronx seemed amazing by the brutality of the move, but that didn’t stop him from holding his arms out as he stood over The Sir and gloated. SARAH SHARP: "Someone needs to check on Wanda. She may be seriously injured, and look at Bronx acting like he just won the championship! That could have taken years off this girl’s career." Alexis Terry, still holding her gut from the standing Moonsault, rolled out of the ring just as The Sir grabbed Bronx’s foot. Bronx acted like he had just seen a ghost, but The Sir didn’t mind. Bronx was wavering now, only being held up by The Sir at this point. Bronx screams in panic as The Sir is carrying him around like a wheelbarrow. The Sir tosses him across the ring, then connects with a big boot as Bronx quickly rises again. The Sir signals that it is time for this match to end. SARAH SHARP: "He has Bronx right where he wants him! This could be The Regicide." Indeed, The Sir picks up Bronx and tosses him over, setting up for the Regicide. The Gory Bomb connects, and Bronx falls to the floor unconscious. The Sir goes to pin Bronx, but then stands up and decides otherwise. He looks around the ring as the ref bends down to attend to Bronx. OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Where did Alexis go?" The crowd explodes in boos as Alexis pops out from under the ring across from where The Sir is and charges her opponent, the champion, dumping him over the top rope with a thud. She smiles as she looks down at the fallen Bronx, his leg kicking as he lays face first on the mat. SARAH SHARP: "A man as big as The Sir is going to stay down when he gets dumped over the top like that." Alexis smiles and rolls Bronx over to boos from the fans. 1…2…3. Alexis Terry has become a grand slam champion. But until next time… this is just one more thing for me to cherish." Her music picked back up as Bronx sat up, still dazed. We cut to commercial break.
