GIW — Card

Revolt, 20 December 2011

20 December 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: TUESDAY NIGHT REVOLT LIVE! From Saltwalter Grill – December 20, 2011 The show starts off with a bang as the opening introduction plays loudly as the fans here in attendance roar with approval for the beginning of the show. As soon as the show starts though the boo birds come out as "Did It On 'Em" by Nicki Minaj hits. RILEY STEVENS: “Oh great.” And out from the back with that always big frown comes Aspen Chaud heading towards the ring looking around at the fans with a glare still wearing a dress as she heads past the fans and through the ropes into the ring, wasting little time screaming for a mic which she is handed. ASPEN CHAUD: “…Quiet down you losers. Tonight I am forced with the task of putting myself into a match where the odds of me not being put through a table are…one to four. So I found myself as usual screwed by Blake Beckett. But you know what I look at this opportunity as one that will be of great service to my cause. That cause being taking out Tony Miranda once and for all so that when it comes time for him to face Ace, he’ll fail miserably…” "Wayward One" by Alter Bridge bellows out from the speakers mid-sentence as Aspen turns towards the ramp as Alex Castellanos heads out from the back to cheers from the crowd stopping at the top of the ramp with a mic of his own. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “Aspen, long time no see. How are things going, well I presume? Normally words like those would be a cause for alarm, but I'm used to your complaining. Did you miss me?” Aspen glares back at him not seeming amused as Alex grins to the crowd and her, as they cheer him on in this exchange. ASPEN CHAUD: “Really Alex? What are you doing out here? Do you think I really care that you are back? I am out here talking about much bigger things then you. So if you’ll excuse me, I’d like to get back to what I was saying.” Chaud shoos him but clearly Alex didn’t come out here for just no reason, though shutting her up seems to be something the fans enjoy. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “It doesn't get much bigger than me. Oh, no, no, no... that was a bit to far. But, I want you to talk to me. The people don't want you to be out here much longer complaining. So let me ask you this, do you know why I decided to come back to GIW?” Aspen doesn’t seem to want to put up with him much longer as she shakes her head no with a very uncaring look in her eyes. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “I decided to come back, because I felt like I had unfinished business with you. Last time I left, you had just screwed me out of a North Beach Championship. Do you remember that?” Aspen nods her head then snaps back with a quick comment. ASPEN CHAUD: “Yeah and I don’t regret it, now get to your point.” She looks back at him sternly waiting for an answer. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “Fine. But you should know that I call myself the Golden Age for good reason. I came back to overthrow you, to rid this company of your reign. I won't waste any time beginning the rebellion.” Alex starts to head towards the ring lowering the mic as Aspen slowly backs away towards the ropes. ASPEN CHAUD: “You think you stand a chance against me? Heck you won’t even make it out of tonight when you face Ace Baldwin. You were always my sidekick and that’s the biggest thing you’ll ever do around here.” Alex continues towards the ring, as Aspen backs all the way up to the ropes. He jumps up onto the ring apron then into the ring but just as he does Aspen drops the microphone and bails out of the ring. She scurries as fast as she can in her heels around the ring and up the ramp as Alex hoists up his mic. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “You can’t run forever, Aspen.” With that he drops the microphone as Aspen turns around looking both scared and angry at what he just did as she heads to the back as we take a commercial break. QUOTE We return from break for the next match and right away as "Paparazzi" By Lady Gaga (Alexis Terry Remix) hits and the fans wait as Alexis Terry enters from the back, some who recognize boo her others just watch and wait as some guys just rudely cat call her as she walks down to the ring ignoring all the fans on her way. RILEY STEVENS: “This Alexis Terry is someone to look out for, I’ve heard nothing but good things about her at least in the ring.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “…I like her already.” Alexis enters the ring looking around at the crowd, seeming almost disgusted by the lack of support she’s getting as the fans here really only know much about GIW, not other places. Her music trails off and hit “Bodies” by Saliva as the fans go wild for Brody Harper as we get a glimpse of Alexis disgusted they’d cheer this guy. RILEY STEVENS: “Fan favorite Brody Harper, here ready to go.” Harper heads into the ring as Alexis looks at him with a disgusted look on her face; he’s not exactly a beautiful site, the referee steps in between them and calls for the bell. DING DING DING! The crowd is behind Harper the veteran as he walks forward looking her over which only seems to disgust her more. As he proposes a kiss on the cheek, she rushes at him and he bows out of the way pulling her into him and going for a kiss but she pushes away and then pokes him in the eyes. The fans boo him as she angrily hits a Spinning Wheel Kick that knocks Harper into the ropes. Clearly disgusted and annoyed by the fact she is in a match of this magnitude, Alexis looks to make a statement as Brody turns around and she nails the big man with a big kick to his chest that sends him spinning around so she follows it up swiftly with a Bulldog, right into a pin 1…2…Kick Out. Alexis gets up brushing herself off, disgusted with the man she is facing as he gets to his knees, she wastes little time bouncing off the ropes and putting him back down with a Shining Wizard. Looking down at him realizing she already has him out she heads up to the top rope, where she poses for a second before hitting the ‘MGME’ aka the ‘Most Glamorous Moonsault Ever’ right down on top of Brody as she pushes herself away from him while pinning him 1…2…3! JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…Alexis Terry!” The fans boo her as she gets up brushing her hands against the referee’s shirt to get the Brody off her as she then demands he raise her arm which he does. RILEY STEVENS: “You know as if we needed another conceited girl on the roster here comes Alexis Terry who seems like she can back up her strong demeanor as she did tonight with a win.” Alexis pulls away her arm then takes a bow for the crowd, who respond with boos regardless. WINNER: ALEXIS TERRY From that match we head to the back where Aspen Chaud has just cowered her way into the locker room areas this week where she finds Ace Baldwin taping up getting ready. ASPEN CHAUD: “Ace, did you see that out there? Alex was like after me! You need to take him out tonight. HURT HIM BADLY!” Aspen says the last part with almost too much giddiness in her voice as Ace groans and answers. ACE BALDWIN: “Listen I have a headache I really can’t handle your voice right now…” Ace holds his head in pain as Aspen’s jaw drops; her voice was after all one that could cause headaches. ASPEN CHAUD: “Really ACE? There are more important things then your headaches. Now you better get the job done and take out Alex, ok?” Baldwin gets up to his feet as he walks towards the exit, right beside her. ACE BALDWIN: “I know I can handle him, think you can maybe do a little damage to Tony this week?” Baldwin heads on by her as she glares back at him not believing what he just had the nerve to say as we take a break. QUOTE Again after the break we find ourselves ready to go in the ring only this time we open it up with Analise Meyers, Katya Rosa, and Aaron Young already sitting in the ring ready to go as the referee stands up in between them. RILEY STEVENS: “This contest is three stars fighting for a shot at Faith Simpson next week.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “…Really these three? That’s the best we could do.” All three seem ready to go, as the referee steps in and rings the bell for them to get things started. DING DING DING! Right away it’s a train wreck as Analise rushes at Katya throwing a punch that misses. Rose goes down anyway, tripping on her own feet as Young looks on at the two from far away. Suddenly there is some commotion as walking down towards the ring shaking her head is Alexis Terry. The fans look at her in confusion as she heads up into the ring where the referee, confronts her. RILEY STEVENS: “What is Alexis doing back out here?” The question is soon answered as she pushes aside the referee then drops Young with a big ‘Flawless Victory’ an Inverted Stomp Facebreaker that catches the entire crowd off guard as Young flops out of the ring. Rose soon rushes at her but she side steps her sending Katya flying out of the ring finally it’s Analise’s turn as she waits until she is right up on her before dropping down the ropes sending her right on over. RILEY STEVENS: “She just ruined this match.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “It was ruined to begin with.” The referee quickly waves off the match, to Jamal who gets up and announces the result. JAMAL JACKSON: “This match is a No Contest!” Back in the ring Alexis demands a microphone which she is handed quickly by a staff member at ringside, she turns and heads back to the center of the ring hearing the boos. ALEXIS TERRY: “So from what I hear this match was for a shot at some little girls title. Now I know just debuted here only a few minutes ago, but I feel as though I am entitled to skipping the introduction process. Clearly these three out here were not ready for a shot. So let me be the one to send a message to this little girl…You are about to know what a real Queen looks like.” Terry smirks and drops the microphone heading out of the ring, as the fans boo her. She steps literally on top of Young on her way up the ramp as we head to yet another commercial. NO CONTEST We head to the back where we find ourselves full screened on the GIW Undisputed Championship, sitting on the ground as we pan out we see Tony Miranda stretching out getting ready for the match as Zachary Pagonis enters the room. ZACHARY PAGONIS: “Hey Tony, can I get a few words?” Tony looks up at him nodding his head as he pulls out of the stretch, joining Zachary sitting next to him. ZACHARY PAGONIS: “Later tonight you are set to battle in a four way tables match. You know with Aspen involved you have to think that Ace might get involved your thoughts?” He holds over his microphone to Tony who hoists up his belt into his lap. TONY MIRANDA: “This belt right here means I no longer can worry about that. These two are going to be trying to screw me out of this belt at every turn. It’s my job to make sure that I make all their plans failures. I did it once at ‘Final Solution’ and I’ll do it again whenever I need to…to keep this belt.” Tony pats his Undisputed title as Zachary takes back the microphone and continues. ZACHARY PAGONIS: “A match like this doesn’t really favor anyone, the odds are stacked against all four competitors. How do you plan to survive a cruel match such as this one?” Pagonis again brings it over to Tony who does not seem at all worried about the match tonight. TONY MIRANDA: “You know the odds have been stacked against me awhile now. It was a four way at ‘Final Solution’ and I won that. This time I get to put people through tables and trust me I am going to enjoy doing just that to Aspen tonight.” Zachary gets up and pats him on the back, then extending his hand which Tony takes and shakes. ZACHARY PAGONIS: “Good luck tonight champion.” Tony gives him an accepting nod as Pagonis exits and Tony looks down at his belt in between his legs as we head to the ring. QUOTE It’s time for the next match this one hoping to have an ending and as we enter the ring we see Ace Baldwin already in the ring already getting booed and already holding a mic in his mouth. ACE BALDWIN: “Tonight I am booked in a match against a joke of an opponent! He’s the same man that two months ago I put out of action with a concussion. Now he returns thinking he can take me out? Haha. Referee I demand you call this match off, I am not wasting my time.” Ace looks over at the referee who shakes his head no as he walks over to him grabbing him by the collar. Then pushing him away as "Wayward One" by Alter Bridge hits and the fans go wild. Baldwin pushes away the referee into the corner then turns his attention to Alex who walks out with a microphone in his hand. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “Ace you are almost as annoying as Aspen herself. You want to dodge me? Trust me when I say my head is in a lot better place then it was the last time we met. And when I am done with you it’ll be you crawling back to Aspen telling her that…she is next.” Castellanos walks up the steps and into the ring as Ace brings his microphone back to his mouth. ACE BALDWIN: “Listen KID! I don’t have time to waste beating guys I already know I can beat. I am going for my title so…lets save some time and you just hit the back now.” Baldwin points him to the back as Alex looks over at the referee and then Ace with a grin on his face. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “You know last week when my opponent trash talked me…he ended up getting a…” Alex drops the microphone and the referee getting revenge on Ace calls for the bell. DING DING DING! BOOM! Sounding like a forth ring of the bell Ace’s jaw gets jacked with a ‘Golden Arrow’ Superkick that sends the fans into a frenzy as Ace’s knees buckle and he falls to the mat as Alex gets on top and the referee proudly counts it 1…2…3! JAMAL JACKSON: “Here are your winner…Alex Castellanos!” Alex grins and rolls out of the ring grabbing the mic with him as the referee proudly taunts Ace a bit before rolling out of the ring as he starts to get his senses back as Castellanos walks backwards up the ramp using the mic again. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “Truth be told, I don’t have time for you.” Alex drops the mic as the fans cheer him on as he backs up the ramp high fiving a few of them as Ace holds his jaw kicking the ropes next to him as we go to the back. WINNER: ALEX CASTELLANOS Right before the next match we have Zachary Pagonis in the backstage area waiting as after a few moment Faith Simpson walks by holding her Legacy title. ZACHARY PAGONIS: “Faith can we get a few questions?” She nods stopping right there stepping beside him with a big smile on her face, seeming excited for her match. ZACHARY PAGONIS: “Let me start by wishing you luck tonight, you’ve started your career here in GIW in phenomenal form going undefeated thus far in your career. I think you’ve caught a lot of people off guard and I’d like to be one of the many to tell you what a great job you’ve done.” Faith smiles almost to the point of blushing as she replies. FAITH SIMPSON: “Thanks, that means a lot.” But Pagonis quickly changes his tone to a much more serious one as he asks the next question. ZACHARY PAGONIS: “However I am sure earlier you saw what Alexis Terry did during the contenders match, your thoughts?” Pagonis asks the question as Faith seems to get more serious as well. FAITH SIMPSON: “Well I knew that eventually I’d get people hot in pursuit of me, to take this belt. I was excited to see who I’d be facing but now I have no idea. But if Alexis wants a match, I’d be glad to give her one.” Faith smiles as Pagonis replies. ZACHARY PAGONIS: “Thank you.” From there she heads to the ring for her match as we take a quick break before her match. QUOTE Back to the ring we go where Austin Acquin is already sitting ready to go when "Power On Me" by Katy B hits and the fans go wild for one of the biggest fan favorites here in GIW in Faith Simpson the ‘GIW Legacy Champion’. She has a big smile on her face as she heads to the ring high fiving fans along the way. RILEY STEVENS: “This Faith Simpson is truly something special, undefeated to start here career you’ve got to be impressed.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “She is a fifteen year old girl who beat the one man who was a bigger joke then her to win the belt.” Faith heads into the ring with her belt posting with it for a second before handing it over to the referee who hands it out and then calls for the bell. DING DING DING! As soon as he rings the bell Austin rushes at her trying to catch her off guard only for Faith to duck under it swiftly. He turns and rushes back at her, but heads right into an Arm Drag. Austin hops up letting his temper get to him as he hops back to his feet and then rushes back at her only this time he gets a kick to the stomach that sets her up for a quick bounce off the ropes into a Running Crossbody. The fans go wild as she hops up calling for a hand clap but as she does this Austin rolls out of the ring holding his chest seeming to want a quick time out. However Faith is not one to slow down so while he shouts something to the crowd she rushes off the ropes and then just as he turns around comes through the ropes with a Suicide Dive that really gets the crowd into it. RILEY STEVENS: “This girl is phenomenal, high flying excitement.” She high fives a fan that extends her hand and then whips Austin towards the ring and he crawls himself into it seeming to have been taken out of things by that last move. But Faith is not done yet, not even close. She crawls up onto the side of the ring, jumping up going springboard as the fans go wild as she finishes it off with a Springboard DDT! It’s a beautiful move that she leads into a pin attempt 1…2…Kick Out. The fans let out a sigh of relief, strangely. Mainly because it’s the ‘Have A Little Faith’ they want to see. She reaches down to hoist up Austin but he pushes her away back into the corner. Austin then follows it up with a Clothesline finally hitting a move. This gets the fans on edge as he walks back over to her in the corner pulling her up to the top rope where he follows her up, but as soon as he sets up for the Superplex she kicks him away and then one big kick to the chest sends him flying backwards to the mat with a bang. She looks around at the crowd who all get to their feet at once as she jumps off with a ‘Have A Little Faith’ a 630 Senton that sends the crow into a frenzy as she hooks the leg 1…2…3! JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…Faith Simpson!” Faith gets up holding her chest slightly, only hurting from her own move as she holds up her arms in the air in victory with the fans cheering her on. RILEY STEVENS: “Thus far this girl looks to be unstoppable.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “With Alexis Terry on her tail, that might soon be put to the test.” The referee hands her back her title which she holds up proudly in the air as yet again Faith ends a match with her always exciting move leading many to wonder who or what can derail this girl who is surely on cloud nine. WINNER: FAITH SIMPSON On our last stop we go backstage where our fans are greeted with the face of Aspen Chaud who is looking around backstage now in her ring gear. ASPEN CHAUD: “Altair! Has anyone seen Altair?” Chaud paces around the area screaming this out to anyone in general, looking around for him as Blake walks up shaking his head. BLAKE BECKETT: “So let me get this straight, you are going to ask Altair for help? Let me run through my old Aspen files to replay this one really quickly. Let’s see here you are going to first try to seduce him, which with him I doubt will work then you’ll fall back on trying to turn him against Tony and Johnny with some sort of made up non-sense. Just so that you have his help in the Main Event?” Blake says this although as Aspen scowls back at him, he already knows the answer but he does not let up there. BLAKE BECKETT: “You know there was a time there when you were capable of doing things by yourself. Then what happened? Suddenly you became an after thought. Whenever you weren’t leading a personal army you were just nothing but a whiner. Good to have you back to your usual spot, having someone else lead you to victory.” Aspen looks back at him clearly getting angrier nearly the point of fuming as Blake smiles. BLAKE BECKETT: “Good luck out there tonight. Maybe Ace will help you out?” Beckett heads on his way clearly in her head now as she continues to angrily watch him go as we take one last break before a commercial-less main event. QUOTE Back for the main event we go where Johnny Violence stands in wait for the next match to start as we see Tables set up all over the place tonight as Violence looks around at them with a grin on his face until "Did It On 'Em" by Nicki Minaj hits and the fans begin to boo relentlessly as Aspen Chaud walks out from the back, angrily staring out at the crowd tonight not looking pleased at all. RILEY STEVENS: “There are tables all over the place tonight, and three of these stars will be put through them by the end of the night.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Hopefully our champion is.” Aspen heads into the ring with Violence staring him down as “Cruel Melody” by Black Light Burns hits and the fans for the most part boo the Crimson Reaper as Altair the very strange star walks down to the ring, not taking his eyes off Violence the entire time. Aspen sets up in a corner as Johnny invites him into the ring. Altair heads up the steps and into the ring, then out of no where rushes at Johnny with a big clothesline that gets the fans going as these two begin to brawl, the referee panics and just starts the match. DING DING DING! RILEY STEVENS: “We knew these two couldn’t contain themselves.” Altair gets him down to the mat laying down some elbows on him while he is grounded as the fans go crazy as out from the back dropping his ‘GIW Undisputed Championship’ on his way out is Tony Miranda who rushes down to the ring throwing off his shirt as he slides into the ring, turning right towards Aspen who quickly bails out of the ring. Tony wastes little time going after her sliding right back after her as she runs around the ring. In the ring Violence throws Altair off him and gets back to his feet throwing a big haymaker punch that Altair barely avoids, and then drops him into a STF that at this point is just to weaken him. On the outside Tony starts to gain on Aspen so she tries to bail over the barricade but Miranda catches her by the legs smirking to the crowd as he pulls her backwards sending her down into the mat. RILEY STEVENS: “Can’t say I didn’t expect this match to be a little crazy.” Aspen crawls away as Tony stalks her; seeming to be taking his time as in the ring Altair has released the hold getting up with a smirk on his face as he exits the ring looking around for a suitable table which he grabs and then slides into the ring. Altair sets it up then turns his attention back to Violence who he goes to pick up. Back on the outside Tony chases down after Aspen grabbing a foot but she kicks his knee and then crawls underneath the ring. The fans boo her antics as she hides under there as Tony searches around for her going around the ring looking underneath for her. But importantly back in the ring Altair hoists up Johnny only to be pushed away back towards the table. The fans go crazy as Violence jumps up rushing at him to tackle him through but at the last second Altair spits blood right into his eyes. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “What the hell?” The fans boo as Violence tries to wipe it from his eyes as Altair takes the chance to double him over with a big kick to the stomach leading right into the ‘Ruiner’ a Lifting Inverted DDT, only this time it sends Violence crashing through the table much to the fans dismay as Johnny cracks through it and seems to go out a bit. JAMAL JACKSON: “Johnny Violence is eliminated!” Tony on the outside continues to look for Aspen who remains hidden, as Altair looks down upon Johnny with a grin on his face hoisting him up despite the referee’s objections and deciding to do more damage as he hits his other big time move in ‘The Downard Spiral’ a Corkscrew Neckbreaker that this time seems to put Violence on dream street as his unconscious dead weight body falls out of the ring as Altair watches with no emotion. However he quickly rolls out of the ring and as Tony lifts up the little ring apron to check for her Altair catches him from behind with a big punch to the back of the head causing the fans to boo. RILEY STEVENS: “It seems Altair has turned his attention to Tony with Aspen currently hiding out.” Altair throws Tony into the ring then follows it up by throwing not one but two tables into the ring seeming to have some big intentions. The punch caught Tony in a bad spot and seems to have caused him to lose a bit of it upstairs as he tries to get his wits back to him. Altair crawls in and sets up one table up against the corner then the other upright in one of the other corners, making the ring a whole lot smaller. On the outside we see Violence getting helped up as Aspen finally crawls out from under the ring looking around then when she sees the coast is clear she slowly crawls on out. In the ring Altair does not seem to care as he gets Tony in the corner and delivers punches and kicks before dropping him down with a Side Slam, the fans starting to lose a bit of hope as the almost unhuman Altair inflicts damage. RILEY STEVENS: “While Aspen watches these two in the ring do battle.” Aspen intently watches hoping for an Altair win or at least weakening him up long enough for Tony to end up being slim pickings. It seems that’ll be the case as Tony get’s hit with a Snap Suplex, luckily away from the tables by Altair who gets up looking around at the crowd not caring about their objections. He holds him up and Tony who seems a bit drained by the attack, tries to punch his way out of it but he instead gets ‘The Downard Spiral’ a Corkscrew Neckbreaker out of no where, this seems to finally put Miranda out as Altair kicks him while he is down like a sack of potatoes. He struggles for a second to get him up but he then goes for a similar ending as he prepares for another ‘Ruiner’ but never one to give up Tony elbows his way out then pushes Altair off the ropes. Altair jumps up going for a standing Guillotine Choke but Tony hoists up his legs thus breaking his arms off his neck then right in position as he spins around and runs across the ring with a ‘Second Impact’ a Running Lygerbomb that puts Altair through the table and makes the fans go crazy! JAMAL JACKSON: “Altair is eliminated!” Tony stumbles back to his butt clearly gassed a bit, but just as he does that Aspen crawls back into the ring looking at him with a big smirk on her face setting up behind him waiting for him to get up which he does using the ropes. He turns around stumbling off the ropes to see Aspen grinning thinking she has it won, but he throws a punch out of no where that sends her backwards. The fans go wild as like a zombie he walks forward only Aspen knows that there are no rules as she rushes forward kicking him where the sun don’t shine as Miranda falls to one knee, in huge amounts of pain as she follows it up with a ‘Queen's Wrath II’ a Heel Kick and Tony’s body falls limp to the mat as she looks around excited. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Put him through the table!” The fans boo but as she goes to pick him up, she’s unable to. His body at this point dead weight, she can’t even get him up at all. Looking around she waves to the ramp calling for someone and then when Ace Baldwin heads on out we find out just who it is as he rushes to the ring sliding in with her as she points at Tony, the two hoisting him up together with just the table in the corner left. The two go throw him but at the last possible moment Tony falls to the ground out of their hands, to the mat his body still limp. RILEY STEVENS: “Tony is clean out.” Ace screams at Aspen to pick him up as Aspen tells him to do it the two bickering back and forth as the fans go wild when they see Tony open up his eyes, pushing himself up ever so slowly. Aspen and Ace see this as they go back to stomping away at him finally hoisting him up and going to try it again but this time Miranda pushes away Ace and then kicks off Aspen as well. The crowd’s back into it in a big way cheering as he hits a Spinning Roundhouse Kick to Aspen that sends her into the table, leaning against it. Baldwin gets to his feet lost as well as Tony rushes at him going for the ‘Third Strike’ a Running Leg Lariat, but he ducks it and instead he hits Aspen in the corner sending her through the table! JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…Tony Miranda!” The fans go wild as the table crumbles over Aspen who falls down into a sitting position in the corner. WINNER: TONY MIRANDA But before Tony can even celebrate Ace slams him down from him behind going after him, pounding him with punches. Baldwin angrily gets up telling Tony to get up. The champion gets up slowly, tired from the long match as Ace goes for a big punch but he ducks it and Tony hits a Rolling Wheel Kick. Aspen starts to get up in the corner realizing he should get out of there the champion heads on out as the fans cheer him on. RILEY STEVENS: “Tony battled out of it!” The fans cheer him as he backs up the ramp as Ace gets to one knee in the ring as Aspen gets up behind him. However she is not looking at Tony rather she is glaring at Ace from behind as she breathes in heavily then out of no where she catches him with a ‘Queen's Wrath I’ a Double Knee Backbreaker! The fans gasp as Ace falls to the mat holding his back in pain. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “What the hell is this?” Aspen rolls out of the ring not seeming done at all as she grabs a chair from ringside sliding back into the ring and as Ace gets up she slams the chair hard into his back yet again. At the top of the ramp Tony watches in confusion as Aspen sets the chair around his ankle, first giving it a boot then getting down locking in the ‘K.M.A.’ a Heel Lock with the chair only increasing the pressure as Ace taps repeatedly only for Aspen to apply more pressure. RILEY STEVENS: “Aspen has snapped.” Chaud holds the lock in until some referee’s run down pulling her away as Ace groans in agony holding his ankle as Aspen gets up falling back into the ropes pulling at her hair until literally she pulls some of it out, Tony continuing to stare from the top of the ramp. She stands there with her hands full of her own hair as Ace groans as EMT’s rush out to help him for what seems like a broken ankle as Aspen slowly turns his gaze from Ace up to Tony the two staring back and forth as the show fades. user posted image King/Queen of the New Year! For the first time ever we watch as six superstars compete for the crown of the King/Queen of the New Year. Three matches…All beat the clock matches…Quickest win…Gets the crowd. Aspen Chaud vs. Faith Simpson Alex Castellanos vs. Altair Alexis Terry vs. Tony Miranda Tune in NEXT WEEK!
