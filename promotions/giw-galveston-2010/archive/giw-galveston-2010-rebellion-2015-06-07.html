Rebellion, 7 June 2015

7 June 2015

GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS SUNDAY NIGHT REBELLION 6/7/15 FROM KAUFFMAN ARENA IN GALVESTON, TEXAS Open Battle Royal Rebellion kicks off with the typical “Open Battle Royal” that has been the staple of GIW for years. Entering this week is Brody Lee Prince as well as some local talent wanting to make a name for themselves in Chase Queen, “Kentucky Born” Jon Johnson, and Cody Cornette. As the bell sounds, Brody Lee Prince drops to the mat and rolls underneath the bottom rope, walks to his manager Max Von Grant, and points to the ring. Max looks a bit disheveled but reluctantly enters the match as Starla and Max look on from the outside. Max tries to attack Jon but is met with a big boot that sends him to the mat. The three local talents continue to fight until Chase is able to eliminate Jon. Cody sneaks over and eliminates Chase but Max is back to his feet and dumps Cody over right after Chase’s elimination. Max is beaten but throws his hands up in the air in victory, that is until Brody renters the match and dumps his manager over and earning the cheap victory. WINNER: BRODY LEE PRINCE Austin Averell vs. Land Shark The Land Shark enters the ring to a huge ovation which also seems to make Austin Averell more upset. Austin however was no match for The Land Shark who destroyed his prey. Austin looked good in the beginning hitting a few dropkicks and was even able to hit a clothesline that took Shark down to the mat. But The Shark recovered and hit a few hard “Fin Butts” that sent Austin hard into the corner. A Big Splash from The Land Shark crushed Austin and Shark finished him off with a “Sharknado DDT” and picked up the 1 2 3 which gave way to a loud “Streak! Streak! Streak!” chant. WINNER: LAND SHARK Beauty and Grace vs. Alexander & Kazan Beauty and Grace members Felicia Gloss and Britney Fillmore decided who would start and it’s Felicia. Alexander and Kazan decided on Kazan starting for their team, though reluctantly as two still share bad blood over “The Great and Powerful” moniker. It was all Kazan tonight who put on a clinic as he hit every big move in his arsenal including a Samoan Drop, Diving Crossbody, and then hit Face Turn. Britney tried to do a run in but Alexander stopped her with a huge spear that turned her inside out. Kazan covered, the team won, and we get a stare down from the two to end Rebellion on a high note. WINNERS: ALEXANDER & KAZAN
