GIW — Card

Revolt, 12 May 2011

12 May 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: T H U R S D A Y N I G H T R E V O L T Who's gonna teach you how to dance? Who's gonna show you how to fly? Who's gonna call you on the lame-dope-smoking, Slackin' little sucker you are? BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! The crowd erupts as the show begins as the opening pyro ignites them into excitement. They are ready for the show as we again come to you Live on ESPN2. Everyone prepares for the show when suddenly “Down” by Curse Icon hits quickly flip flopping the fans, as they begin to boo as out from the back comes Trent Lost wielding his GIW North Beach Championship on his shoulder. The fans are hurling boos his way as he walks out confidently looking out at them. Walking into the ring, he has a mic already in hand as he pulls off his shades and begins to speak. TRENT LOST: “Last week, this show was utterly embarrassed by the actions of two individuals. Those two individuals were a little flirty couple, known as Alex Castellanos and Sarah Sharp. Now I don’t know about all of you. But if we want our ratings to bounce up, I think we need to start by taking out the trash. Sometimes the best move is not addition it is subtraction. We don’t need some little, girl running around with a microphone pretending to be an Interviewer. And the sad part is, she does a better job at her job then Alex Castellanos who has had issues with me since he came here. So La’Sarah come out here and fire them publically.” Lost lowers the microphone but instead of La’Sarah we hear "Ugly" by The Exies, it hits as the fans burst back into cheers. Heading out from the back, shaking his head is Alex Castellanos. He stops at the top of the ramp, looking down at his rival as he holds a mic of his own up to his lips. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “Can I politely ask you to shut up? I don’t know if you’re reading of a prompter or what. But apparently Aspen let you off your leash long enough to come out here. Since you believe that I am not worthy of being in this company let alone in the ring with you. How about we prove it…You…versus….me tonight for that belt!” The crowd goes wild as Trent begins to scoff off the suggestion. Raising the mic to his lips for his quick reply. TRENT LOST: “…No. If we are going to fight, it will be on my terms. So here’s my offer…to you. You versus me, at ‘Breakdown’. If you win you get this belt…but if I win…Sarah Sharp is fired and never allowed on this island again!” The crowd boos the ladder half of the offer, as Lost drops his microphone with a smirk. As Alex is thinking about it, behind him comes Sarah Sharp grabbing the mic away from him. SARAH SHARP: “He accepts!” The crowd seems iffy on the idea, as Alex shakes his head as the two argue back and forth while a confident Trent Lost grins. TRENT LOST: “Sarah…I hope you enjoyed your time here. Watch later tonight, because I am going to give you a teaser of your boyfriend’s chances at ‘Breakdown’.” Lost drops the microphone with a thud as the crowd boos him, as Alex and Sarah continue to argue. RILEY STEVENS: “Huge news with that match to start the show. These two men meet on opposite sides in a tag match later tonight. Also two title matches; the Tag belts will be defended as well as the crowning of a new GIW Undisputed Champion…Stay tuned!” The scene fades out to a commercial break before the start of the first match. QUOTE The first match is ready to go and the fans seem pumped and ready to go. Out first is Jak Hendricks and Seth Seeker, the duo almost seeming surreal as they walk to the ring. Jak is heading out first while Seeker follows behind him not far behind. Mostly boos can be heard however for the most part, it’s more so still a sense of confusion as to how this duo will work. RILEY STEVENS: “Still a lot of confusion regarding whether these two can work together, their debut was less then impressive.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “It was their first match, they’ll redeem themselves tonight.” Jak and Seth both enter the ring heading to their corner as “Sleeping Giant” by 10 Fold hits as the fans pop as out from the back comes Ninja Strike Force a team that has had a lot of success and have garnered some love from the crowd. They both run down to the ring and slide down heading to their corner as well. RILEY STEVENS: “These two have looked impressive as a team thus far; it’ll be interesting to see how they do on the big show.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “…I can’t stand these guys.” They high five as Jamal decides to start meanwhile on the other side its Seth that opts to start for his team as the bell is rung. DING DING DING! Laughing with Colby, Jamal turns around to see big bad Seth Seeker staring at him. Realizing how big he is, he quickly turns and tags in Colby sliding out of the ring to some laughs from the crowd. Hunter shakes his head and enters the ring starting to circle around the big man. He runs forward with a big kick to the side, as he bounces away from him. This time he runs forward and dropkicks Seth on the knee, dropping him down onto one. Looking around as if he is even surprised Colby runs off the ropes and then catches him with a Bulldog to the back! The fans cheer for him as he makes the cover 1…2…But Seth tosses him off. Colby gets up, seeming to gather some confidence about him as Seeker struggles to get to his feet. Grabbing him he surprisingly tosses him into the corner. Colby runs full steam, giving him a gigantic elbow to the chest. Hunter then tags in Jamal as he holds Seth there. Jamal runs off the ropes and then jumps up giving him a big jumping kick to the face! The fans cheer as Seth stumbles forward clearly out of it, before being sent to the mat with a Dropkick, quickly Jamal pins him 1…2…Kick Out! The fans continue to cheer them on as they simply continue to dismantle, Seeker. RILEY STEVENS: “Seeker is not doing well and the tag team of Ninja Strike Force are using great tag teaming to beat him down.” The camera cuts to a shot of Jak on the turnbuckle, angrily screaming at Seeker to do something. Getting up Jamal tags in Colby as together they walk over, grabbing him only for him to use his brute strength to push them both away. But quickly they bounce off the ropes and before he can make an attempt to tag out, they give him a Double Spear! The fans go wild as Jamal rolls out of the way and Colby pins him 1…2…Kick Out! An angry Jak can be seen, stomping the ground trying to Seth into it. Hunter gets up preparing for something as Seeker gets up. He runs at him only for Seth to finally push him away right into a tag from Jamal. Seth stumbles and is able to get far enough so that Jak can tag himself in. A tired Seeker rolls out of the ring as Jak steps in; angry as he gives Jamal a huge Clothesline as he runs at him. Colby then goes for him only to crush him with a Spinebuster! The fans boo as Hunter rolls out of the ring, Jamal turns and walks right into a Sharpshooter. Everyone angrily boos as Jamal is forced to tap out to end this one. RILEY STEVENS: “Jak Hendricks came in and took care of business but not until after Ninja Strike Force gave a beating to a very weak looking Seth Seeker.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Told you they’d win.” RILEY STEVENS: “I can’t say they did much as a team, but yes Jak did win here tonight.” The fans boo as Jak rolls out of the ring not waiting for his partner at all as he heads to the back clearly angry with how the first two weeks have gone being partnered with Seeker as we go to the back. WINNERS: JAK HENDRICKS & SETH SEEKER Heading to the back we find ourselves greeted with Nelson Fielder lacing up his boots for his match later tonight, soon big bad Brody Harper walks into the room causing Nelson to look up. BRODY HARPER: “What the hell was that last week? What are you an idiot!? You threw it right in my eyes.” Nelson shakes his head trying to plead his case as an angry Brody seems ready to just crush him. NELSON FIELDER: “Man, I am sorry you know I didn’t mean to do that.” Brody shakes his head, not believing him any more things had gotten bad between them. BRODY HARPER: “You know what, you better lay down tonight. Otherwise…we are through.” Brody turns heading out of the locker room area slamming the door behind him as the scene fades. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = Transitioning to a different location backstage we find a very tired looking Seth Seeker walking through the hallway, being met soon by a very angry Jak Hendricks who immediately gets in his face. JAK HENDRICKS: “I don’t know what’s wrong with you! But until you can fix your personal problems or whatever is causing you to completely suck out there. I am riding solo!” Jak brushes past him, purposely shouldering him as he does as Seeker rubs his head, something not seeming right with him as we head for a break. QUOTE After the break in the action returning to the ring we go as we see Trayton Deluca standing inside of the ring stretching out. Soon “Silly Boy Blue” by David Bowie hits as the fans go wild for the man whom walks out from the back Alioth Starre. Seeming ready to go he strolls his way towards the ring, rubbing his hands together the entire time. RILEY STEVENS: “Some would say this man deserved to be in the Main Event tonight fighting for that belt.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “But that’s why we don’t pay ‘some’ much mind.” Walking up the steps Starre enters the ring, looking across at Trayton who seems a little nervous about being thrown in there with a big dog. The referee doesn’t waste a whole lot of time before calling for the bell. DING DING DING! Trayton quickly rushes in right into an Arm Drag from the always smart, Alioth Starre. Twisting his arm, Deluca quickly gets to his feet with Starre tugging on it. He tries to spin around but can’t so he backs Alioth up and then uses them to spring off. Bouncing off the ropes he goes back at Alioth only this time he is met with a big, European Uppercut that sends him stumbling backward. Starre very calmly walks towards him grabbing him and whipping him into the corner. Trayton holds his back as he walks forward trying yet again to throw a flashy kick only for Alioth to counter with a less flashy yet effective kick to his planted foot that drops him to the mat. Quickly he runs off the ropes, dropping down with a Fist Drop that connects hard with the face of Trayton. Not wasting any time at all, as Deluca bounces up with the Fist Drop, Starre locks in a Body Scissors. The fans don’t at all seem surprised by the complete domination by Starre as he clenches his legs together, putting a lot of pressure on Deluca’s stomach. However he is a bit too close to the ropes as he reaches out and is able to grab hold of the ropes. Starre breaks the hold immediately getting up to his feet, his very plain emotions show that of a man who knows what he needs to get done. Deluca gets up using the ropes holding his chest, when he turns around he meets even bigger problems as Alioth drops him back down and locks in the Figure Four Leg Lock. This time he’s right in the middle of the ring, with a little fight he tries to escape but quickly taps out. RILEY STEVENS: “Not often do we see a complete technical blow out like we just did, Alioth didn’t make a single mistake.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I’d love to insult the guy, but with that match I just can’t…Actually wait, as great as that match was at least I know what it’s like to be tall enough to ride a roller coaster.” Riley doesn’t even pretend to laugh as the joke falls flat. Instead we watch as Alioth gets up, waiting as the referee raises his hand. Quickly he turns and heads out of the ring, either not happy about not being in the main event or knowing wins like this are going to get him there. WINNER: ALIOTH STARRE We head to the familiar location of Blake Beckett’s office where currently he is on the phone, talking with somebody. BLAKE BECKETT: “Yes, yes sir I understand. I realize that they have been slipping but--” Blake is cut off mid-sentence by the man on the other end as he then continues. BLAKE BECKETT: “Don’t worry the main event will draw. New champion should be great. These two are huge rivals, trust me I wouldn’t lie to you…Ok, good bye.” Beckett shakes his head, as he hangs up the phone lying back in his chair. Seeming as worried as ever, about the state of the company in regards to its ESPN2 deal. QUOTE Back in the ring while the Beckett segment was going we find that the GIW Tag Team Champions, ‘Golden Duo’ Austin and Joshua Acquin are already in the ring along side Brotherhood of Steel members, Sergio Moura and Pedro Cabrita. Realizing one team is missing the camera takes us over to the announce table. RILEY STEVENS: “As some of you may notice; Ross Rydell and Matt Kluge were not able to make it. Unfortunately they were involved in a minor car crash on their way to the show. While nobody is hurt, they were unable to make it here in time and have been deemed ineligible for this match.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Good riddance.” In the ring the referee shows off the belts to all of the fans, going to each side to show them. He hands them out to Jamal Jackson then heads back to the middle where he calls for the bell. DING DING DING! The crowd is ready to go as Joshua will start for the Acquin’s and Pedro the former tag team champion will start for his team. The two start to circle around one another when Joshua holds up a hand and then pulls out a mic. The fans boo as he holds up the microphone, as Pedro stands back waiting to listen. JOSHUA ACQUIN: “Before we start…I have someone I want to shout out. Antonio Martins, I you’re you’re watching in a hospital bed. I wanted to say…I hope you get well so--” He is about to finish but before he can Pedro slams him down with a clothesline. The fans go wild as Cabrita quickly knocks him down with another as soon as he gets up. Anger running through him, he runs across the ring giving a big running big boot to Austin sending him falling off the ropes. The fans go wild as Joshua stumbles up from the clotheslines, right to the ropes where Pedro using all his strength clotheslines him a third time sending him over right by his brother! The fans go wild as Cabrita backs up screaming something, clearly the anger about Antonio’s injury fueling him in this one. Sergio steps into the ring, being the big man he is, he walks over standing by the ropes. Austin and Joshua get up, both men stunned by the fast start. Pedro runs forward as he jumps up off Sergio’s outstretched arms as Moura basically pushes him up over top of him giving him extra lift. Pedro flies out, landing flush with a huge kick to both men’s chests! The fans go berserk for the move as Sergio looks out as Pedro may have fallen just as hard as Joshua. RILEY STEVENS: “What a move! That was one of the most impressive things I’ve ever seen in this ring. And you have to think Josh’s words may have just helped the Brotherhood.” The referee begins to count them out 1…2…Sergio realizing they can’t win if these guys are truly out heads to the outside lifting Pedro up and helping him into the ring. With Austin getting up first he gives him a big boot to the ground then turns and tosses Joshua with relative ease back into the ring. The fans cheer as Pedro holding his back, landing on it hard begins to start a slow clap with the entire crowd all of them getting it going. Josh gets up, holding his stomach from the kick earlier. He turns right into Pedro who grabs him for a Suplex, but Josh pushes him away. Sergio makes the tag and enters the ring as they go for a ‘Steel Wings’ a Double RKO, but Josh slips out of it. He kicks Sergio in the gut but out of no where Pedro delivers a Superkick perhaps in ode to Antonio! The fans go nuts as Josh falls to the mat, out of it. Sergio gets on top for the cover as Cabrita makes sure that Austin can’t stop it 1…2…3! It’s over! RILEY STEVENS: “What a win by the Brotherhood of Steel! Your new champions are Pedro Cabrita and Sergio Moura as they bring the gold back to the Brotherhood of Steel.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Please no, not again.” The fans go wild for them as Pedro jumps up hugging Sergio as they won this one for Antonio. The referee brings in the belts as he hands Pedro his, this being his second reign now as the other is handed off to Sergio the big man. Out from the back comes Goncalo the third member to celebrate, with his partners as the happy moment continues into a commercial break. WINNERS: BROTHERHOOD OF STEEL [Pedro Cabrita & Sergio Moura] (New GIW Tag Team Champions) When we head backstage the crowd pops at the site of Tony Miranda, preparing backstage for his title match. Looking to be in the hallway he is stretching out his leg, soon things get interesting when Alioth Starre walks into the scene looking at Tony, who stops stretching. ALIOTH STARRE: “…Good luck. Hope that you can slay the witch tonight.” Tony nods his head and then offers a hand shake, the two men who have their eyes on the same prize coming together with a hand shake. Tony goes to pull away but Starre does not release his hand. ALIOTH STARRE: “Just know…I’m coming after the winner whether that’s you or her.” Alioth now is the one to break the hold as he walks the way he was heading in the first place. Tony watches him and then turns around, nodding his head as the match is quickly approaching. QUOTE With the increase of commercials here on ESPN2, we come back and yet again find ourselves with both teams already in the ring. This match having perhaps the most tension of any tonight. Across the ring is not only Brody Harper and Nelson Fielder but also Trent Lost and Alex Castellanos. RILEY STEVENS: “You can smell the bad blood from here. It’s clear these two teams are not exactly fans of one another. The Killers have fallen apart and as we heard earlier Alex and Trent will be dueling at ‘Breakdown’.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Thanks for the recap Riley…Anyone ever tell you that’s a girls name?” In the ring things start to get heated as Brody and Nelson begin to argue back and forth. The referee separates them and an angry Brody steps out of the ring, Alex steps in instead to start it out. DING DING DING! The fans are behind Alex as he goes right in for a lock up with Nelson. Gaining the upper hand in the strength department, he backs up into the ropes. Grabbing him he chooses to use the ropes to propel Nelson off. The speedy Fielder runs off the ropes hopping to come back with something big but he runs into the awaiting, leg of Alex who doubles him over. Quickly grabbing him he delivers a fast Snap Suplex, which sends the fans into applause. Getting up, he wastes little time grabbing him and bringing him to his feet. Looking over at Trent as if to send a message he grabs him and lifts him up and drops him down with a Northern Lights Suplex with a Pin, 1…2…Trent kicks him off. The crowd boos as Alex gets up, angrily looking at Lost who backs up to his corner with a smirk hoping to get into Alex’s head. Castellanos knowing he can’t worry about him turns around lifting up Nelson to his feet, his small stature making this a lot easier. Grabbing him he whips him into the corner, where he runs forward and delivers a clothesline in the corner. Quickly lifting him up, he follows him up and then starts setting up for a Superplex! The fans are ready for it, but Lost runs along the ropes trying to distract him. Unfortunately it works as Alex tries to kick him away, and Nelson catches him with a big sucker punch. Fielder climbs down from the top as Alex stumbles and falls upside down, his leg getting caught. Lost chuckles as Nelson runs forward going for a dropkick. To everyone’s surprise, Alex pulls up at the last second sending Nelson’s attempt into the ropes. Fielder begins to crawl towards Lost, as Alex tries to untangle himself. Finally he does, but by the time he does Lost has tagged himself. Castellanos has just hit the ground when Lost comes in and starts delivering boot after boot to him while he’s down. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Get him Lost!” The fans boo as an opportunistic Lost takes this chance to start to beat down his rival. Castellanos is able to grab the rope, and the referee pulls Trent away from him. Alex gets up in the corner, walking forward as Lost feints a dropkick then pokes him in the eye! The fans hate it as the referee scolds him for giving him a warning. Alex stumbles back into the corner and Trent runs forward with a big kick that doubles him over in the corner. Using this he props him up and then crushes him with the ‘Strung Out’ a Hangman DDT! The fans boo as he makes the cover 1…2…Kick Out! The fans quickly turn to cheers for Alex’s kick out. Lost gets up yelling at the referee that he needs to hurry up his count. Shaking his head, the time elapsed while he was complaining allows Alex to push him away as he tags in Brody. The big man steps in, going after him only for Trent to land a head butt followed by another one. Trent brings him up and Brody pushes him away, he turns around looking at Nelson but his momentary lapse of judgment leads him right into a ‘Lost And Found’ a Lifting Full Nelson Twisted into a Reverse STO! The fans boo as Lost makes the cover with Alex just barely getting into the ring 1…2…3, he is too late. RILEY STEVENS: “Another impressive performance here by Trent Lost getting the win for his team.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Of course he did he’s the champion.” Quickly rolling out of the ring he demands his title. Pointing in at Alex who has his hands on his head, realizing he didn’t get in quick enough to break it up. Trent shakes his head mouthing “This is only the beginning” to Alex inside of the ring. As Trent heads to the back, Alex slides out heading to the back now as inside of the ring Brody is just now getting up. Nelson comes in checking on him. Brody gets up angrily giving him a shove, then exiting to the back as we take a break. WINNERS: NELSON FIELDER & TRENT LOST Heading to the back we find Aspen Chaud putting on her entrance jacket, standing in her locker room where Marcus Hister also happens to be in the room with him. ASPEN CHAUD: “When Trent gets back here, tell him what I told you. I want this to be perfect, we can’t afford to mess up.” Hister nods his head as Aspen turns and buttons up the top button of her jacket before heading out the door as the short yet revealing segment comes to a close. QUOTE *Sorry for this being cut down because if there was any match that deserved to be written fully this week it would be this one but, I banked on having today to do it and I came home not feeling well at all from school. So here is the ending of the match at least.* The crowd is into it, thus far what has been a great match is continuing and it’s Tony Miranda who is the one standing tall at this point, with Aspen in the corner struggling to get to her feet. The fans are cheering for Tony to get it done as he stalks her in the corner. She gets up, slowly using each turnbuckle to boost herself up. Suddenly the crowd begins to boo as running out from the backstage area is Marcus Hister heading towards the ring, in a hurry. Jumping up on the turnbuckle he screams something at Tony to get his attention. But whatever the plan was, it backfires rather quickly as Miranda runs at him and hits a Running Leg Lariat through the ropes right at Marcus, sending the fans into a roar of cheers. RILEY STEVENS: “That plan backfired.” Tony looks down at him, saying some words before turning around. However he turns right into a Low Blow attempt from Aspen, but some how his reflexes are quick enough to stop it. Aspen tries to pull away but Tony simply laughs at her, shaking his head knowing he has her in his grasps. Lifting her up onto his shoulders, he starts to walk around carrying her with relative ease. She kicks her legs trying to get free, but Tony has her tight. Suddenly jumping over the barricade unseen to us Trent Lost as well, with Hister out cold the next partner of hers is out to the rescue. He jumps up onto the ring apron and Tony spots him immediately. Running at him he tosses Aspen off his shoulders and sends her right at Lost, sending both of them out of the ring to another loud cheer from the crowd. RILEY STEVENS: “Think it’s safe to say, she’s run out of help.” Both Aspen and Lost roll around on the outside, in pain. As Tony quickly rolls out of the ring, lifting Lost up he grabs him and tosses him over the barricade before turning around and walking over to grab Aspen. The crowd starts to go insane when he starts brushing off the announce table, seeming to be throwing caution to the wind. He tosses her on top as Riley and Oliver back up, he sets her up on one end looking to be ready to do the unthinkable hitting his new move ‘The Endgame’ a Running Lygerbomb! The fans are going wild, but perhaps due to the noise Tony can’t hear that the referee is already up to a seven count. He lifts her up, holding her there before running forward and nailing her with ‘The EndGame’ sending the crowd into one of the loudest cheers ever. Aspen is out cold and the table is smashed under her. Tony gets up, but as he does he looks into the ring to see the referee calling for the bell. RILEY STEVENS: “Oh God, it appears Tony as well as Aspen have been counted out…Who is champion?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Serves him right.” Jamal announces what has happened declaring it a Double Countout, as quickly the mood in the arena turn to boos. Never before seen in GIW the fans begin to toss trash into the ring, a pile of water bottles, pop cans, and everything else begin to fly from all over the place. Tony has his hands on his head, seeming distraught as Beckett comes running out from the back with a mic heading towards the trash filled ring. BLAKE BECKETT: “Please everyone calm down…” A look of fear, crosses the face of Beckett…this was not the way he’d impress ESPN2 and he knew it. A few fans still were throwing trash as he prepared his statement. BLAKE BECKETT: “Since the title was not won here tonight, at ‘Breakdown’ we will have another match to declare one. It will be; Tony Miranda…versus…Aspen Chaud…versus Alioth Starre! In a triple threat match where a champion must be crowned!” This gets a small pop from the crowd but for the most part they are upset about the ending. Beckett exits the ring, as Tony looks around he may have destroyed Lost, Hister, and Aspen but he didn’t win the belt…the show slowly fades on what might be the last time we ever seen GIW on ESPN2. DOUBLE COUNTOUT
