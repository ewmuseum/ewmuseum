GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS SUNDAY NIGHT REVOLT 8/23/15 FROM HOFHEINZ PAVILION IN HOUSTON, TEXAS After the opening intro package for Revolt plays, we find ourselves in the locker room area with “Earlier Today” pinned to the corner of the screen. Katie Keller stands in the middle of the locker room, flanked by Bronx Valescence and Alexis Terry. Luke Jones and Duff Briggs are in the back. Johnny Violence is lacing his boots. McKenzie Wacha, George and Melissa, Austin Averell, Brody Harper, and the rest of the roster are gathered around Keller.
