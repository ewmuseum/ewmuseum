GIW — Card

Revolt, 16 June 2011

16 June 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: T H U R S D A Y N I G H T R E V O L T BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! Yet again the pyro goes off this time on the ramp way here on the Poretto Beach, where the show is currently being prepared. The fans are shown screaming heads off, ready to go as the last stop before ‘Category 5’. "Now I'm That Bitch" by Livvi Franc hits as the fans begin to boo the hell out of the girl known as the Queen of Galveston. Out from the back comes Mrs. Chaud herself, with a big grin on her face wearing a blue dress heading towards the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Last week, Aspen Chaud dropped the bombshell when she tried to buy the company off of Blake Beckett. Suppose she has something bigger in store this week?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Hopefully he sold her it.” Aspen walks up the steps, heading along the ring apron before stepping over the bottom rope and across the ring demanding a microphone. Walking back to the middle, she waits as the crowd dies down before starting. ASPEN CHAUD: “Listen up! I don’t plan on asking Blake again to sell me this company. Eventually, he’ll agree to it. See, I came out here because as of right now there is no Main Event for ‘Category 5’. So I am here to announce that it will be yours truly versus Alioth Starre for my Undisputed Championship!” Aspen has a big grin on her face as the crowd boos her little match idea. Suddenly, “Down” by Curse Icon hits as the crowd goes wild for Aspen’s own personal rival Trent Lost. Out from the back he comes with his sunglasses on and his ring gear ready to go. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Who invited this tool?” Shaking his head, chuckling to himself listening to her as Aspen continues to glare at him. Walking down towards the ring, he high fives a few fans. Heading up the steps and into the Trent asks for a mic which the staff member gives to him. Turning to Aspen, he begins to address her. TRENT LOST: “You know Aspen that might sound good in your head. But when was the last time you won a match that didn’t involve someone else helping you? These people they don’t want to see you fight for the belt…They don’t even want you here.” The crowd cheers showing their approval, as she shakes her head mouthing “Who cares about these people?” TRENT LOST: “If anyone deserves a shot at the belt…it’s the guy that beat Alioth Starre. And I beat him with out the help of you.” ASPEN CHAUD: “YOU! YOU! PLEASE! You have to be kidding me. Trent Lost as the Number One Contender? You do realize that this isn’t a joke right. This is for a shot at the belt and just because you got lucky one time doesn’t mean a thing…So if you don’t mind I am going to wait until it’s officially announced that I am the Number One Contender.” The crowd boos her as both of them lower their mics exchanging in a verbal spat that’s ended with the sound of “Demon Eyes” by The Answer through the speakers. Out from the back comes Blake Beckett, with a grin on his face looking snazzy in his suit. BLAKE BECKETT: “So I have a choice don’t I? Who do I give the shot to? Should I give it to…Aspen Chaud?” The crowd boos as he rubs his chin, pretending to think of himself doing that as Aspen screams something at him, her voice so high you can’t really understand it. BLAKE BECKETT: “Or her old friend Trent Lost? You know…this is a really hard decision. So…at ‘Category 5’ it will be Alioth Starre defending his belt against…Trent Lost!” The crowd goes insane as Aspen’s jaw drops, Trent grins and rolls out of the ring. He begins to high five a few fans, heading up the ramp happy with the decision. But in the ring Aspen begins to pace around, Blake just turning to go but stopped by her words. ASPEN CHAUD: “You see Blake that is why your company is dying! Because you make decisions like this. You have been trying to hold me back since the first show! You stripped my belts! You’ve done nothing but…HARASS ME! You will not get away with this…even if I have to take it to court and try you for harassment!” Clearly angry Aspen brushes the hair that fell into her face out of the way as Blake turns around not seeming too worried about it. BLAKE BECKETT: “Well you can do that…For now I am going to give you a shot at trying to get your belt back…But it won’t be an easy one. You see Alex Castellanos and Damien Dunn already qualified for the Sole Survivor match. You will be the third entrant…Good luck.” Blake smirks heading to the back, leaving Aspen to angrily glare at him as she breaths in heavy not seeming to see much opportunity in the Sole Survivor match. As she begins to head out of the ring we head to the Announce table. RILEY STEVENS: “Frankly I think he made the right call. As of right now Trent is the rightful Number One Contender.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Yeah…let’s ignore the fact he lost the past two weeks.” RILEY STEVENS: “Yeah because both matches were both very fair weren’t they? Regardless stay tuned the show is going to be a good one. When we return we’ll have the exciting Controlled Chaos match to determine GIW Interim Television contenders, then we have another Sole Survivor Qualifier. Undisputed Champion, Alioth Starre will compete against ‘Sole Survivor Competitor’ Damien Dunn. Then Wake Walker and Matt Kluge will try their best to team up against Ninja Strike Force. All of this capped off by a huge eight man tag match. Stay tuned!” Riley’s excitement is evident while Oliver started to look away about half way through as we head towards a break. QUOTE The match begins with all of the men involved in the Controlled Chaos match already in the ring, ready to go. Jamal Jackson reminds the audience of the rules: you can be eliminated by pinfall, submission, or being removed in any way from the ring. The bell rings and everyone in the match gets on their toes; this is the match of a lifetime for some of these guys. RILEY STEVENS: “Here we go, the last three wrestlers left in the ring will go on to compete for the Interim Television Title while Pandora is rehabilitating an injury given to her at the hands of Alioth Starre. Who will emerge on top?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Hmm… I bet Harper wins this match.” Within seconds of the match beginning, Brody Harper tosses his partner Nelson Fielder over the top rope. He laughs and claps, but Nelson seems shocked, he must have thought the two would work together. Herman Bludge tries to capitalize by clothes lining Brody out, but Brody ducks the clothesline and hit’s a very bad dropkick on Bludge. Bludge doesn’t go out of the ring, but David Graves fixes that by flipping him over the rope easily. Meanwhile, Adam Syndal is in the corner taking punches from Drakken Sangre at an alarming rate. Sangre hits one more big punch that sends Syndal down to his butt, then he goes to taunt to the crowd. Johnny Scar sees an opportunity and hit’s a rolling heel kick that lands right on Sangre’s throat. Scar pulls him up and tries to throw him out of the ring, but Sangre doesn’t want to go over the ropes. From behind the two of them, Leonard Jackson hit’s a German Suplex on Rejinaldo, then peels him up and tries to toss him out. Graves and Harper are battling it out in the other corner. Syndal is up now, and in one large burst charges across the right and hit’s a big boot on Rejinaldo, sending him over the top. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Rejinaldo just ate a leather boot… the poor guy didn’t even get some Ketchup… and those things are awfully dry you know?” Unfortunately, the boot has places Syndal in a bad spot; his ankle is perched on the top rope. Jackson tries to toss him over, and gets him to the apron but Syndal rocks him with a shoulder to the gut. He jumps over the top rope for a sunset flip and rolls Leonard into a pin…1.…2.….3! Leonard Jackson is eliminated! Scar and Sangre are still battling on the north side of the ring. Scar eventually gets the upper hand a drops Sangre with a dropkick. Scar gets momentum from the top ropes for his finisher, but Syndal pushes him off the ropes. Scar almost falls off, but by the skin of his teeth he holds on to the ropes. Syndal runs toward Sangre and hits his finisher, “In Syndication” 1...2.….3! Sangre is eliminated! RILEY STEVENS: “Adam Syndal is for sure the star of tonight’s match. That’s three eliminations by him so far! But can he save himself?” Scar bounces off the ropes and hits Syndal with a springboard dropkick like maneuver. Graves has Harper on the outside of the ring and is pushing with all of his might. Harper claps Graves’ ears which gives him enough time to get back into the ring and hit his finisher, a POWERBOMB on Graves! 1.…2.………. KICK OUT! Harper seems shocked and goes to yell at the referee. Scar comes out from nowhere with a rollup on Harper! 1...2.…HARPER ROLLS OUT! Harper is the first up and hit’s a shoulder block on Scar followed by some more bar fight like punches to Scar, who is trying to cover up. Syndal is up and is waiting to hit his finisher on Brody, as soon as Harper stands up Syndal goes in for the kill. Harper COUNTERS with a sidewalk slam out of nowhere. 1...2.…KICK OUT! Harper pulls Syndal up and lifts him in power bomb position. Harper moves to the edge of the rope and tries to throw Syndal down, but it is a HURRACANRANA COUNTER! Harper lands on the apron though, on his knees. Syndal climbs back into the ring. He is focused on Harper…. He doesn’t realize Graves was waiting on him with his finisher “Grave Danger”, a spinning fisherman’s suplex! 1.…2.………Brody pushes Graves off! Graves and the entire crowd is shocked at the move. RILEY STEVENS: “Why would he do that? There are four left and the final three advance! Syndal was done for sure, but Brody broke up the pin.” Graves watches as Harper pulls up Syndal and sets him up for a power bomb. Syndal drops to his knees and hit’s a low blow on Harper! From behind, Johnny Scar inches up on Harper; he connects with the Arm-Trap Neckbreaker! 1.…2.….3! The match is over. Brody rolls out of the ring, defeated, but all three of the winners meet in the middle of the ring. JAMAL JACKSON: “Here are your winners… Adam Syndal, Johnny Scar, and David Graves!” The three men look at each other and size each other up. Graves points to Syndal and tells him “I had you.”, More words are exchanged between the three as the scene fades to commercial. WINNERS: ADAM SYNDAL, DAVID GRAVES, JOHNNY SCAR Heading to the back we find Aspen Chaud pacing in her locker room with her team in attendance. Marcus Hister is in the background, seeming to be listening in as Damien Dunn sits on the couch as she begins to vent. ASPEN CHAUD: “Can you believe this? Trent Lost is getting the shot and not me? This guy used to be my lackey! I should be fighting Alioth Starre not him. This isn’t fair!” Angrily flailing her arms around she flops down into the couch next to Damien. Who puts his arm around her. DAMIEN DUNN: “Listen…I will take out Alioth tonight. Then in the main event you take out Trent Lost. We’ll make sure that this little match…doesn’t happen.” Aspen looks up grinning; she seems to be intrigued by the idea. Damien gets up to his feet. DAMIEN DUNN: “I need to go get ready.” Aspen nods her head, seeming to be thinking of something. Damien opens the door and heads out; right as the door closes Aspen speaks up. ASPEN CHAUD: “Wait! Are you going to help me win the Sole Survivor?” Aspen says this but apparently it ended up to late as Damien does not come back through the door. She looks back at Marcus then gets up going to talk with him as we head else where backstage. ==== In the backstage area Trent Lost, is seen stretching out a bit preparing for his big main event match tonight. As he bends over, we watch as Alioth Starre comes around the corner walking over to him with a grin on his face. ALIOTH STARRE: “Lightning…it strikes all over the place…but does it ever strike the same place twice? Not likely. You see Trent…ever since you beat me I have been putting down one competitor after another. I’m happy…that you’ll be the first challenger and victim in the reign of Starre.” Grinning as Trent comes back up, stretching out his arms now as Trent seems to be ignoring him until he talks. TRENT LOST: “Listen I may be the underdog. But I know when we get out there the fans; they’ll fuel me to a win over you. And that belt…will be around my waist.” Lost stares long and hard at the belt over Alioth’s shoulder, Starre slowly readjusts it and grins. ALIOTH STARRE: “I’m sorry Trent, these fans are going to lead you right to the slaughter house. Good luck tonight…I’ll be watching.” Alioth walks past him, heading on his own way as Trent watches him go realizing just what exactly that means as we head to the ring. QUOTE When Anthony Brenske’s music hit’s the PA system, the crowd lets out moderate books. Richard Youngblood, his manager, ushers Brenske down to the ring. Youngblood makes fun of the audience members as he makes his way down to the ring, calling several of them fat and telling one that her kid looked like a rabbit. When he gets into the ring, he grabs a microphone from Jamal Jackson and shoo’s him out of the ring. He begins to speak. RICHARD YOUNGBLOOD: “Ladies and gentlemen… this the man who will revolutionize the wrestling industry. No matter what you people say to him, it wont matter. This man is Anthony Brenske, and God’s gift to him was to take away his hearing. Do not pity the man, it is a blessing that he doesn’t have to hear your disgusting voices…I…” Youngblood is interrupted by the music of Bam Isaac Glazer who actually receives some cheers from the GIW audience for shutting up Youngblood. Bam has a microphone and begins to speak to the two men from the end of the isle. BAM ISAAC GLAZER: “Richard Youngblood, you may talk a big game… but if it wasn’t for me being here, ain’t nobody wouldda came. You see you can stand there in the center of the ring and talk all you like, but if you think that mic can save you… SIKE!” BIG runs down to the ring to take out Youngblood, but when he slides into the ring, Brenske keeps him on the mat by stomping him several times. Richard Youngblood is furious now and slides out of the ring. Brenske pulls up Glazer quickly and pulls him up for a belly to belly suplex that drives Glazer into the mat. Brenske grabs Glazer’s arm and starts working on his shoulder before locking in an armbar. Glazer gets his foot on the rope and the referee makes Brenske break the hold. Brenske moves back and argues with the referee while Youngblood takes advantage of Glazer by banging Glazer’s arm against the apron. Brenske moves away from the referee and concentrates on Glazer again. He tries to lock in an armbar, but Glazer rolls our of it before he really can get a grip. Glazer heads back to a corner to recover. Brenske charges, but Glazer manages to get a boot to the head. Brenske heads straight to the ground, right under the turnbuckle. He looks out right into one of the camera and yells “this is for you”, then he climbs to the top turnbuckle. The 450 SPLASH CONNECTS! 1...2.……..3!!! JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner, “BIG” Bam Isaac Glazer!” RILEY STEVENS: “BIG has qualified for the Sole Survivor Match at Category Five… I wonder what he meant by “this is for you” though..” B.I.G. rolls out of the ring holding his chest the move hurting himself a bit but as he gets up he heads top rope showing off. Brenske rolls out to his manager, holding his gut. The crowd were not going to be happy either way with this one but either way Bam is heading to the Sole Survivor Match. WINNER: BAM ISAAC GLAZER We cut backstage to the GIW Interview Lounge. Sarah Sharp is sitting with Alex Castellanos on the couch; Alex gives her a kiss before she starts to speak. SARAH SHARP: “You need to go Alex… I have to do an interview in a few minutes…” Alex laughs and shakes his head. He grabs her hand and tilts his head sideways in a curious way. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “With who? Surely they aren’t as important as me…” Sarah shrugs, he goes in for another kiss but she pushes him away and grabs her microphone. Mr. Black walks up from behind them and just stares at Castellanos. MR. BLACK: “Excuse me, I hate to interrupt this professional behavior, but I think I have an interview scheduled for… oh… five minutes ago.” Alex looks at Black and laughs to himself. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “Oh, well I sincerely apologize. I’d hate to deprive the GIW viewers of your interview, since you are so important and all. I mean, how could I let them miss a nut with two personalities bitch and moan about how he can never beat a washed up footballer?” Alex chuckles to himself and turns back to Sarah and professionally shakes her hand. He turns back to Black and pats him on the shoulder. ALEX CASTELLANOS: "Good luck tonight Mr. Black but don’t worry I’ll pick up your slack." Alex chuckles at his rhyme then walks off past Black. Sharp moves in for the interview, but Black just storms off in the opposite direction as we cut to commercial. QUOTE Back in the ring we go and when we return "OV" by Three 6 Mafia hits as the fans begin to boo as out from the back comes Damien Dunn and only making them angrier is the fact that Aspen Chaud is walking with him. Together they walk down towards the ring, as Aspen pats him on the back and continues to walk around the ring heading towards the announce table. RILEY STEVENS: “Oh joy looks like we are going to be joined here at ringside by Aspen Chaud.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “What do you mean Riley? That’s great news.” Aspen sits down on a third chair, that just so happened to be there. In the ring Dunn gets ready to go as “Subterraneans” by David Bowie hits as the fans yet again begin to boo, even louder then before. Out from the back comes an arrogant champion Alioth Starre holding his belt over his shoulder as he walks towards the ring. ASPEN CHAUD: “Can you believe our champion comes out to David Bowie? I mean really. Not to mention the fact that he looks like a ten year old.” RILEY STEVENS: “Do I hint some jealousy? Or are you still bitter about Trent Lost getting the shot?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “You disrespectful asshole. We all know that she deserves that shot. Trent is a nobody. Heck, he shouldn’t even be in the Television title hunt. The guy is NOTHING! Aspen do what I do and ignore him.” Heading up the steps and into the ring comes Alioth, walking over to the corner where Aspen is closest to and almost shoving it in her face he jumps up, showing it off. ASPEN CHAUD: “Enjoy it while it lasts!” Alioth jumps down and hands the referee the belt who then hands it out as Alioth now looks over at Damien, with a grin on his face as the referee calls for the bell. DING DING DING! Showing little respect Starre jumps in going for a lock up but Damien shows off his power with a big shoulder block. Getting up right away Starre bounces off the ropes and goes after him again but yet again receives a shoulder block. Dunn, now goes on the attack as soon as Starre gets up he grabs him and whips him into the corner. Running full blast at him Dunn goes for a Clothesline. Thankfully for him, Starre ducks it and sends Damien into the turnbuckle hard. Spinning around Dunn walks forward walking right into a big European Uppercut! The crowd is not really picking sides, mostly just an angry vibe from them. Dunn bounces into the corner again and Alioth runs at him and throws a big running Lariat that sends Dunn falling to a seated position in the corner. Rushing off the ropes, Starre comes forward nailing him with a Running Knee! Falling to the mat, Starre drags out Dunn to the best of his ability and covers him 1…2…Kick Out! Big move that stunned him but not enough to put Dunn away. Alioth gets up; shaking his head as he surprisingly goes to the middle rope. But as he’s prepping for a Fist Drop, Dunn gets up making him switch his game plan. Trying to get down, he’s not quick enough instead he’s grabbed by the clutches of Dunn who wraps him up and then hits a Belly to Belly Suplex! The crowd cheer’s Starre’s down fall as Damien goes for a cover 1…2…Kick Out! ASPEN CHAUD: “That was a slow count.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Yeah!” Back in the ring Dunn gets up, seeming confident for a new guy being fed to the Undisputed Champion. Usually known for his aerial attacks, Dunn has instead relied on his big size advantage. Starre holds his back but gets up rather quickly but he walks right into an Implant DDT! Rather then going for the pin Dunn, instead decides to take a gamble as he heads to the top rope. The crowd gets a little antsy seeing what he’s got planned from way up there. Jumping off he goes for a Flying Elbow Drop! But the always ring savvy Alioth rolls out of the way sending Damien into the ring his elbow hitting hard. The crowd boos, as Starre gets up smirking and rushing off the ropes nailing him with a Fist Drop! With the cover 1…2…Kick Out! Still not enough Alioth gets up. He came into this expecting a fight as he, yells out “This is Over!”, turning and waiting for Dunn to get up. Finally the big man does so and Alioth grabs him and locks in a Reverse Chin Lock! The crowd begins to boo thinking this will be it. Suddenly Damien begins to show life, attempting to rip off Starre’s hands! Realizing that he’s losing his grasp Alioth releases it and lands a very crafty low blow that drops Dunn and goes unseen by the referee. ASPEN CHAUD: “What was that!” The crowd boos his actions as Starre grins and goes to run off the ropes but as he does Aspen takes off the head phones and throws them to the side hopping up and walking up to the ropes. Starre stops and the two begin to exchange words, grinning he says “Watch this!” Alioth turns around but when he does so he walks right into a Clothesline from Dunn! Starre seeming shaken up struggles to get to his feet, before being kicked in the gut and then taking the ‘Complete Devastation’ Damien’s version of the Pedigree! The crowd is stunned as Dunn jumps on top 1…2…3! JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner, “Devastating” Damien Dunn!” RILEY STEVENS: “Wow, Damien Dunn picks up the win against our Undisputed Champion! Though partly in part because of Aspen’s slight interference.” Dunn immediately rolls out of the ring joining with Aspen. Chaud hugs him and throws up her arms as a dazed and confused Starre gets to his feet, holding his head in pain as he seems to realize what happened as we take a commercial break on this HUGE upset. WINNER: DAMIEN DUNN Heading to the back yet again we see Matt Kluge having a look at the card for ‘Category 5’ where he’s just learned he’ll compete in a Six Man Tag Match right next it is also the announcement made regarding the Tag Titles. Walking up behind him, putting a hand on his shoulder is Wake Walker. WAKE WALKER: “Let’s face it, you were better without him.” Kluge takes a second to realize who it is and turns slapping the hand off of him, Wake takes a step back. MATT KLUGE: “You have a lot of nerve coming around here Walker.” Grinning, not at all worried knowing that the two are about to have their match. WAKE WALKER: “I just came by to make sure you were ready tonight. You’ll finally know what it’s like having a tag team partner with some talent.” Walker again pokes the bear this time Kluge comes at him but hearing the scuffle some security comes over stepping in between them. WAKE WALKER: “See you out there.” Walker turns and heads towards the ring as Kluge breathes in heavily as he watches him go as we take a break before these two are forced to try and work together. QUOTE “Sleeping Giant” by 10 Fold blares over the Public Address system and out comes Ninja Strike Force. The two high energy tacticians come down slapping hands with the fans. The team unity is apparent with The Gruesome Twosome planning everything out as their opponents haven’t come out. RILEY STEVENS: “Alright, The Ninja Strike is force is an upcoming young tag team. What they lack in experience they make up with…” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Yawn who cares Stevens, these guys won’t go anywhere if these two guys have anything to say about it.” Battle Metal by Turisas cuts everything off. Matt Kluge comes walking out in bright white shorts with red crosses on the sides. He kept the black ones away due to being outside and the seamstress realized that he doesn’t wear orange. He marches down the ring staring at The Ninja Strike Force. He climbs up the turnbuckle but Dazed and Confused by Led Zeppelin cuts off his entrance. Wake Walker comes out in all black ignoring the heat. He stands on the apron and motions for Matt to start the match. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Well I know one dumbass that will get heat stroke.” The bell rings and Colby makes his way foreword. Matt immediately tags in Wake Walker and snickers at pulling a fast one on him. Wake Walker simply stares Matt down but climbs into the ring. Colby hits an Enzugiri kick right away that leaves Wake standing. Colby gets up and Wake simply chokes Colby, the ref starts counting but Wake refuses to let go. Jamal rushes in and Wake catches him with the other hand. He continues to hold them until the ref starts counting to 5. Matt actually comes into the ring and shoves Wake to make sure he breaks the chokehold. Matt turns around to get onto the apron and Wake shoves him off hitting the crowd barricades. RILEY STEVENS “Matt Kluge and Wake Walker show no signs of being a tag team tonight.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Which I for one, am glad.” Wake turns around and is met with a Double Dropkick. Jamal stands on Wake’s hands to keep him down as Colby stomps Wake. The ref has Jamal leave the ring. Colby climbs up for a Top Rope Knee Drop but Matt drags Wake out of the ring. And then Irish whips him into the barricades. He starts screaming at Wake when Colby Suicide Dives through the ropes hitting both men. Colby is the first one up, but Wake is up soon after. He pushes Colby into the apron and starts delivering strikes to his chest. The ref’s count reaches 8 when Wake comes back into the ring. He tells Matt to continue the assault on Colby, but Matt rolls Colby into the ring. Wake Walker snarls and runs at Colby for a clothesline, Wake runs straight through Colby and Matt punches Wake right in the jaw! Since Matt was holding the tag rope it makes him the legal man. Colby struggles up and Matt picks him up into a Powerslam position, he walks over toward Jamal’s corner and places Colby done letting him make the tag. Jamal comes in slowly, both men circle each other. Matt comes in Jamal tries a Super Kick that sends Matt dodging. Matt launches a left hook that Jamal ducks. They both grapple up delivering blow after blow. Matt is the bigger wrestler pushing Jamal into the ropes but Jamal hits more strikes. Matt switches going for a German Suplex but Jamal backflips out surprising Matt. Jamal shoots for a takedown but Matt shoves him down and hits some punches on Jamal as he’s on his hands and knees. Matt picks him up and hit’s a suplex! Jamal works his way up with the ropes and Matt locks a Cravate on him. He drags him into the middle of the ring and Jamal starts elbowing Matt in the chest. He breaks free and delivers a few forearms. Matt gets rocked and backs up. He makes his way to Wake and punches him in the face. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Those guys are going to finish each other off.” Wake uppercuts Matt, the strike causes some minor blood flow. He gets into the ring looks at Jamal and laughs. Jamal tries another forearm strike but Wake catches it and Irish whips him. He lifts Jamal up for the Spinebuster but Jamal elbow him in the head and escapes. Wake picks up Jamal and power slams him. He kicks Jamal while he’s down. He lifts Jamal up and knees him hard in the chest. Jamal doubles over and Wake lifts his up but Jamal hit’s a Hurricanrana. He makes the hot tag to Colby! RILEY STEVENS: “And now The Ninja Strike Force can take the offense.” Colby rushes in but is met with a crushing clothesline. Ruining the hot tag, Wake takes delight in picking Colby apart with grounded strikes. Colby takes a page out of Wake’s book and rakes his eyes. Wake let’s go and Colby puts a Hammerlock in. When Wake starts to break free, Colby let’s go and tries to apply the Kryptonite Crippler but can’t lock it in time. Wake turns around and is met with a Bicycle kick. Colby lifts Wake up for a Suplex and Matt rushes in destroying Colby’s knee with his Matt Cooke. RILEY STEVENS: “Ooh that has to hurt.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “You think he realized by taking out Colby’s knee Wake still kissed the mat?” RILEY STEVENS: “To be honest I really don’t think Matt Kluge cared.” Matt drags Wake into their corner and does a manual tag. Matt holds Colby’s leg and drops a elbow into his knee. He then wraps the leg around his waist and wrenches harder on Colby’s leg. Jamal comes in and the ref orders him out. Matt continues with the damage on the knee by smashing it into the mat a few times. Colby kicks his leg out and somersaults to Jamal tagging him in. Jamal knocks Kluge to the mat, Wake comes in and he wheel kicks him taking Wake down! Jamal looks down and starts signaling for Death Row. He grabs Matt into the Piledriver position but Matt back body drops him. Jamal rolls over to get up and Matt puts Jamal into a Wheelbarrow position and hit’s a Deadlift German Suplex for a 2 count. Jamal kips up and Matt suplexes him again this time with a float over. Jamal kicks out again. After Jamal gets up Matt hit’s a Northern Lights Suplex getting another two count. He lifts Jamal up and a surprise roll up by Jamal! Matt kicks out at 2. Both come up and Matt throws him into his corner. He runs in and does a Stinger Splash. Jamal stumbles out and locks a Standing Dragon Sleeper in. He tags Wake, but Wake doesn’t move. Matt tags him again and Wake still won’t move. Matt simply shrugs and hit’s a twisting belly to belly suplex. He goes back and pulls Wake into the ring and shoves him into the corner. He looks at the crowd and runs at Wake.
