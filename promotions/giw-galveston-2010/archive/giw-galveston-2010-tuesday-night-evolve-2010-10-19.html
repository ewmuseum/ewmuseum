GIW — Card

Tuesday Night Evolve, 19 October 2010

19 October 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
EVOLUTION PRO WRESTLING PRESENTS: EVOLVE LIVE FROM THE MANOR DOWNS IN MANOR, TEXAS BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! The camera pans into the arena where the Evolve fans are going nuts for tonight’s show. Coming off the heels of a huge challenge tossed out by Amanda Rowland on Revolt, we come into Evolve where the fans are ready to see what will happen here on Evolve. As per usual we head over to the announce table where St. Aude and Cox are ready to call the show. AUSTIN ST. AUDE: “WHAT A NIGHT! We have in store for you all at home. You see this place is packed because they know that we have not only one…BUT TWO! Title matches. Plus we have to wait and see if Sinclair will accept the challenge laid out by Amanda Rowland.” COLT COX: “OF COURSE! He will. We’ll kill, Revolt once and for all. And then it’s just us on the way to the top of the wrestling WORLD!” AUSTIN ST. AUDE: “But as for tonight the show starts with Joseph Erns the rookie who impressed a few people last week with his win taking on a veteran Travis Tate who due to his loss last week was taken out of the title match, big way for him to come back here.” COLT COX: “Then it’s OUR title on the line. The title that truly matters on this show, the Evolve Championship! The champion Christopher Darren Erns defends against the two ORIGINALS, the guys that made this show Jack Prowler and my guy Chris Armstrong.” AUSTIN ST. AUDE: “Then after dismantling Bryan Battery to defend the title last week Wake Walker defends his title against the other brother Bret Battery tonight. He’s been defending that belt awhile now, so we’ll see if he can continue. But the big deal, is the challenge made by Revolt, will Sinclair accept it? Well we will find out tonight I am sure.” = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = We come back to the ring where the fans are greeted by the new face of Joseph Erns who is in the ring with a big smirk looking out at the crowd throwing out his arms getting into it. "How You Like Me Now" by The Heavy hits and the fans begin to cheer on Travis Tate one of their favorites as he hops out from the back and then heads down towards the ring as the crowd continues to clap. AUSTIN ST. AUDE: “Looks like we are ready for the opening contest our only non title match of the night.” COLT COX: “Not sure what these fans like about this Tate guy. I’m more into Joseph Erns, hell I like him better then his brother.” Joseph Erns then slides out of the ring before Tate steps in and runs over talking some trash to a fan at ringside. Joseph gives the fan a shove and then slides back into the ring where Travis Tate is waiting, as the referee calls for the bell. DING DING DING! Travis walks over pointing out to the fan that Joseph shoved wanting an explanation of what he did to deserve it. Erns turns and begins to spout off some sort of answer before giving a shove back to Travis Tate. The fans boo him as Tate looks out at them, with a bit of a smug look and when Joseph goes for a punch Tate ducks under it and then turns it right into a ‘Big Ticket Prices’ a Bubba Bomb! The crowd goes insane as Joseph flops around holding his lower back area as Tate hops up. And points out to the fan who Erns shoved and yells “This one’s for you!” and then turns back to Joseph who has gotten up still holding his back and walks right into a ‘Tossing The Stub’ a Rock Bottom! The Big Ticket has this one in the bag as he immediately goes for the cover 1…2…3! Win! COLT COX: “Really? That quick.” AUSTIN ST. AUDE: “That’s what she said…However Joseph’s win last week may have gone to his head as Travis Tate seems to be back!” Travis Tate gets to his feet holding up his arms with a grin as he points out to the fan who is giving him a big thumbs up. Suddenly the crowd is interrupted as walking out onto the stage is David Sinclair with a microphone sort of raining on Tate’s moment. DAVID SINCLAIR: “Amanda Rowland you think I am going to back down from you? Evolve is the best damn wrestling show in the world! And if you think we are going to let you take us down, think again. You see I have decided to ACCEPT your offer. And tonight I am putting together a team of my own. And Travis Tate, you’re on it.” Sinclair walks to the back not waiting for any real response from Tate as the fans cheer the fact Travis will be representing them as he nods his head in the ring accepting the call as we take a short break. WINNER: TRAVIS TATE = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = After that short match we head to the backstage area where the fans are greeted with the site of Blake Beckett and David Sinclair discussing things together, perhaps the Challenge. Suddenly there’s a loud ruckus as its Jack Prowler, Chris Armstrong, a Bret Battery walking over to them angrily. JACK PROWLER: "If you’re putting together a team to face GIW, you better pick US!" Prowler stands triumphantly in front of them as Armstrong pushes him aside and steps in front of Battery and Prowler both. CHRIS ARMSTRONG: "You need me and us three are the Originals around here. It’s only right we be the one’s who are fighting to kill GIW." The three stand tall waiting for an answer from Sinclair who turns looking at Blake Beckett, then back to the three stars that came with him first. DAVID SINCLAIR: “You both are in.” All three nod and turn to walk away Prowler and Armstrong immediately heading different ways not wanting to see each other however Sinclair’s hand goes onto Bret’s back and spins him around. DAVID SINCLAIR: “Didn’t say anything about you…you see Christopher Darren Erns is on our team as well. So let me put it this way if you can beat Wake Walker…then your in.” Sinclair walks off with Blake smirking down at Bret who looks stunned into fear as Beckett pats him on the back and then walks away leaving Bret fearful about his chances tonight as the segment ends. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = The lights dim slightly. Then a red light surrounds the entrance on stage as “Turn Up The Trouble” by Airbourne hits the P.A system. A moment later Chris Armstrong walks quickly out onto stage to a roaring crowd of boos. He is dressed in his wrestling bottoms and boots and a black EPW t-shirt. He stands on stage for a moment with a huge smile on his face as he looks over the crowd and taking in the boos cockily. He then beats his chest with both hands and yells. COLT COX: “There is our real champion Chris Armstrong!” AUSTIN ST. AUDE: “Well, CDE is actually the champion.” He begins walking down the ramp; he then climbs the stairs and enters the ring going between the ropes. He goes to a corner and climbs to the second turnbuckle and yells and beats his chest again with both hands. He then jumps down and goes to the middle of the right and looks around at his opponents Jack Prowler and champion Christopher Darren Erns who are already in the ring. COLT COX: “Lets go CHRIS!” The referee calls for the bell and the match is underway. The match starts off with Erns and Tate locking up while Chris Armstrong leans against the ropes watching these two duke it out. Finally they realize that Armstrong is doing nothing so Prowler sends Christopher Darren Erns with an Irish Whip towards Chris Armstrong but Armstrong sends him over the top rope as he hits the outside of the ring with a sickening thud! Chris Armstrong then jumps over the ropes and hits the startled champion with a Moonsault, a move not in his favorites, but executes it nicely! He then gets up and slides under the ring rope to be greeted with stomps from Jack but have no effect to Chris Armstrong who gets up and send Prowler off the ropes and as he comes back hits him with a vicious Tornado DDT. And before we know it Armstrong has Jack in the Powerbomb position off the top rope. He then lifts him up onto his shoulders and showing his strength jumps off and hits The Money Maker! He then hooks the leg of Prowler. 1..2... And as CDE is sliding under the ring rope ... 3! AUSTIN ST. AUDE: “Christ! Chris Armstrong came out here tonight not messing around and he is now the new Evolve Champion!” COLT COX: “I TOLD YOU. Yes that’s the face of this company right there. The title is back where it belongs!” The referee then hands Chris Armstrong the Evolve Championship as he begins to celebrate with it. Jumping to the top rope he holds his belt tightly to his chest as he smirks to the crowd who are a bit shocked at how quickly this one ended as well as the fact that Armstrong is once again the Evolve Champion! WINNER: CHRIS ARMSTRONG (New Evolve Champion) = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = We again head to the backstage area where there is basically tranquil quiet as Wake Walker has his title laid out in front of him in a zen state until suddenly a loud celebration can be heard as walking around the corner with his belt held high and a woman on his arm is Chris Armstrong! Walker stares up at him as Armstrong walks around to him and then stops. CHRIS ARMSTRONG: "You know it feels pretty good to know that I am walking out with MY BELT! You on the other hand I’m not so sure about. After all rather then just coming around to joining us, you’ve decided to ride solo. Well let me tell you something Wake if we wanted to, we could have made you lose that belt a long time ago. But you see, they are making a team. And we are going to kill the place you formerly called home. So either you get on board and help us kill GIW or…we’ll kill you." Armstrong smirks and then turns kissing the girl on the cheek then taking a big drink of his Champagne before turning back to Wake one last time. CHRIS ARMSTRONG: "Oh and I think that you’ll be making your decision tonight." Armstrong chuckles and then drags the girl off with him as they go to celebrate the title coming back to him as Wake Walker stares up at him, emotionless as the segment ends. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = Bret Battery is already in the ring. “Dazed And Confused” begins to play. Wake Walker comes out with a microphone is his hand. He talks about how coming out to his full entrance isn’t worth it for Bret Battery because he’ll probably beat him in 20 seconds anyway. Wake Walker keeps talking about how pathetic Bret is and the entire crowd begins to boo. They don’t seem to appreciate Walker’s cocky attitude. Walker poses with the belt, hands it to the ref and then lets the match start. COLT COX: “Should I bother cheering for Bret Battery, when by the time I’m done he’ll have already lost?” AUSTIN ST. AUDE: “I wouldn’t bother.” Bret Battery rushes at Wake Walker. Wake Walker hit’s a spine buster and sets up Bret Battery for The Temple of Love. He hits it. Wake Walker goes for the pin. 1...2...3. It’s over in a total of 8 seconds. Wake Walker raises his hand with the ref but then begins to stalk Bret Battery. He picks him up and then hits another Temple Of Love. Then, ANOTHER ONE! AND ANOTHER ONE! AND ANOTHER ONE! Finally, after six Temple of Love’s, Bret looks dead. There are referees on the outside pleading Walker to stop. Wake Walker hit’s a seventh one. Suddenly, the Evolve theme song begins to play. Wake Walker turns towards the ramp. It’s Sinclair. He’s holding a mic. COLT COX: “Goodness could have come out here quicker to save his wrestler, goodness.” DAVID SINCLAIR: “You know what Walker? I don’t appreciate the fact that you refuse to join Evolve in the war against Revolt… and I don’t appreciate the fact that you’re destroying one of my superstars even though the referees have told you to stop already. So...” Wake Walker quickly picks up Bret Battery and hit’s an eight Temple of Love. The referees at this point don’t care and fear the worst for his health. Sinclair looks angry. DAVID SINCLAIR: “Let’s just say that you’ll regret doing that.” Suddenly, Joseph Erns comes from out of nowhere. However, Wake Walker quickly exit’s the ring and grabs a steel chair. Joseph looks scared now. He tries to run but Wake Walker slams him in the back with a chair. Joseph falls and is beaten by Walker over and over with the chair. Sinclair looks intimidated by the aggression displayed by Wake Walker. As the EMT’s help Bret to the back, Wake Walker quickly runs up to them and hits Bret with the chair. He falls off his stretcher. Wake Walker runs back and corners Joseph Erns. However, from the back…the entire Evolve roster comes out! Wake hits Erns three more times, leaving him a completely bloody mess. The EMT’s have to help him to the back soon as well. The entire Evolve roster runs towards Wake Walker and begin to attack him. Wake tries to fend them off with his steel chair but is unable to do so. After a great beating, every single star hits their finisher on Wake Walker and leave him in the ring. Sinclair walks down the ramp and goes up to Wake Walker with a microphone. He begins to speak. DAVID SINCLAIR: “Now, Wake Walker…I’m sure you can’t hear me right now because you’re unconscious but when you watch the tape again to see what happened to you… you will know that you never mess with Sinclair, EVER. You are on our team whether you like it or not and that’s final.” Sinclair ends the show by standing over the body of Wake Walker, laughing. The team is set for EPW now; Chris Armstrong, Jack Prowler, Travis Tate, Christopher Darren Erns, and Wake Walker whether he knows it yet or not. WINNER: WAKE WALKER (Still Galveston Island Champion)
