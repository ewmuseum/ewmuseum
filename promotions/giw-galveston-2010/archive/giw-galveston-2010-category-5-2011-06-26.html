GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: CATEGORY 5 LIVE! From Porretto Beach on Galveston Island BOOM BOOM BOOM! The pyro goes off and the crowd goes nuts as we come into the Poretto Beach for what should be a HUGE show here tonight on the last stop before ‘Moment of Glory II’. In the ring already waiting is none another then Jamal Jackson with a microphone as he looks out at the crowd and waits until they die down to speak. RILEY STEVENS: “Welcome ladies and gentleman to ‘Category 5’ what a show we have for you here tonight. Let’s get this thing underway with the introductions of tonight’s Sole Survivor particpants.” JAMAL JACKSON: “Please join me in welcoming the four participants in tonight’s Sole Survivor tournament. Starting first hailing from Cardiff, Wales weighing in tonight at two hundred and ten pounds…he is the ‘GIW North Beach Champion’…ALEX CASTELLANOS! ” The crowd goes wild as out from the back comes Alex Castellanos alongside Sarah Sharp heading to the ring in his ring gear as no music plays instead Jamal goes right into the next introduction. JAMAL JACKSON: “Introducing participant number two; hailing from Brooklyn, New York weighing in at two hundred and twenty pounds he is…“Devastating” DAMIEN DUNN!” Next out from the back is Damien Dunn heading down the ramp towards the ring where Sarah Sharp and Alex Castellanos currently seem to be talking things over. Looking confident he to heads up the steps as Jamal continues. JAMAL JACKSON: “Introducing participant number; hailing from Hollywood, California she weighed in at one hundred and twenty pounds…”The Queen” and ‘New Revolt General Manager’ ASPEN CHAUD!” The loudest boo yet emanates from the crowd as out from the back walking to the ring is Aspen Chaud however the boo’s only get louder when it’s revealed she has a microphone. ASPEN CHAUD: “Listen up! I have something important to say…” This naturally only makes them boo louder as she enters the ring, patting Damien on the back as she walks by him and over to the corner where Alex and Sarah are. ASPEN CHAUD: “I’ve waited long enough for you to make your decision. Frankly, I am not waiting any longer! So…either you join me…join me in a group that will take this company OVER! Or you choose this pathetic little girl…and be doomed to a career full of me making your life miserable. So what’s it going to be?” Showing a lot of anger Aspen naturally talks a big game when her partner or at least someone she trusted, stood not too far behind. Sarah walks forward talking back to her as Alex pulls her back while he’s handed a microphone. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “You know Aspen I have been thinking a lot about this. Mulling it over in my head and I think…that I have made up my mind. So…watch this and tell me whose side I’m on.” Alex flips around Sarah and plants a huge kiss on her that sends the crowd into an uproar of cheers while Aspen’s eyes seem to be popping out of her head. The two break apart after the long smooch as Sarah turns walking and getting into Aspen’s face. Suddenly she turns to walk back to Castellanos but he winds up and absolutely clocks her with ‘The Golden Arrow’ a Superkick that lands flush on Sarah’s jaw…The crowd goes quiet as Sarah falls to the mat completely motionless. Everyone in the building is standing still…in complete silence. RILEY STEVENS: “Damn it! He’s on her side…he’s sided with the devil!” Suddenly Aspen realizes it as she starts to jump up and down as Alex stands over Sarah muttering something as he looks up and receives a big hug from Aspen, as the crowd begins to boo the formation of this new unit. RILEY STEVENS: “We are going to get some help out here for Sarah Sharp…we will be right back after this.” QUOTE Back in the ring we go where Sarah Sharp is currently being taken to the back on a stretcher with a neck brace. In the ring Aspen is still very giddy about it, jumping around and talking to both Damien and Alex as they wait finally for the final man. JAMAL JACKSON: “Out final participant hails from Miami, Florida and he weighed in tonight at one hundred and seventy four pounds…“Big” BAM ISAAC GLAZER!” The crowd goes wild as out from the back all alone comes Bam making a b-line towards the ring. The crowd is behind him though odds are not looking good. RILEY STEVENS: “Come in Bam get in there and overcome the odds!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Are you stupid? He has a hard enough time one on one.” As soon as he slides into the ring the referee turns and calls for the bell to start the match knowing this won’t take long to get underway. DING DING DING! And here we go! Big slides into the ring and tackles Alex down to the mat sending the fans into a uproar as the man they are behind has become very much so evident right off the bat. Hopping up Dunn runs at him next only to receive a stiff Chest Chop followed by a Short Clothesline! The crowd is firmly behind him as he turns around and starts to head to Aspen Chaud who was watching all of this happen in the corner. Holding up her hands asking him to stop, but he shakes his head with a big grin. But from behind comes a recovered Castellanos who takes him down from behind. The crowd boos as together the two begin to hammer away at him with big stomps all over his body. This of course angers the fans who realize that it’s not going to be a very fun night with Aspen, Alex, and Damien working together like this. Aspen tells Damien to get him up and he does, tossing him into the corner. She runs at him, going for her signature ‘Tush Push’ a Turnbuckle Flying Butt Shot! But Bam catches her in mid air and tosses her over the ropes. The crowd goes wild as he bounces out from the ropes and Clothesline’s Dunn, and then does the same to Alex! Showing signs of over coming the odds, he grins and turns to the crowd waving his arms for them to get up as he turns and kicks Dunn in the gut then nails him with a DDT! The crowd cheers him on as he makes the cover 1…2…Alex kicks him off. Though this is every man for himself Bam gets up realizing that he’ll be fighting all of them as Alex grabs him whipping him off the ropes and on the rebound lifts him up and puts him down with the Double A Spinebuster! Grinning to the crowd who are booing him, Castellanos makes the cover 1…2…Kick Out! RILEY STEVENS: “I don’t get how Bam Isaac Glazer can possibly win this facing three people, it’s a damn shame” Alex angrily yells at the referee for the slow count as he calls for Damien’s help as the two go to give him a Double Suplex. But Bam slips out and then runs forward nailing them both with a Running Clothesline, making the cover on Alex 1…Kick Out! He bounces over to Damien 1….2…Kick Out! Getting up he realizes that it’s going to be a lot harder then that as he calls for Damien to get up. On the outside we still see Aspen sitting on her butt, either hurt or just sitting this one out. Bam makes sure he knows where she is as Alex gets to his feet, then turns right into an attempted Suplex from Big. But Castellanos catches himself and turns it right into a Snap Suplex of his own quickly making the cover on Bam 1…2…Kick Out! Getting up he pounds the mat clearly thinking that Bam isn’t much competition. Dunn is now back up as well, looking to Alex for leader ship. Castellanos lifts him up and then tosses him to Damien who goes to lift him up only for Bam to slide off and nail him with a Dropkick to the back. Aspen has gotten up on the outside, fearing something. Alex goes after him but Big does a huge big back body drop sending Castellanos over the top right onto Aspen with a huge ovation from the crowd. Showing off his unexpected power Big waits for Dunn to turn around then nails him with the ‘BNB’ a Double Underhook into a Piledriver! This sends the crowd into a frenzy as he makes the cover looking to the outside where Alex and Aspen are in no way able to stop it 1…2…3! JAMAL JACKSON: “Your survivors are Alex Castellanos, Aspen Chaud, and BAM ISAAC GLAZER!” The crowd goes wild as he hops to his feet holding up his arm in victory as Aspen and Alex get up on the outside, brushing themselves off looking into the ring at Bam who holds up his arms taunting them. RILEY STEVENS: “Well that’s the first round of Sole Survivor and right now Bam Isaac Glazer is looking to over come the odds and become the Sole Survivor!” Clearly the crowd believes in him as Aspen and Alex seem to be experiencing some doubt as Bam’s grin is shown as we head to the back. SURVIVORS: ALEX CASTELLANOS, ASPEN CHAUD, & BAM ISAAC GLAZER We cut backstage where Mr. Black is taping his wrists alongside Richard Youngblood and Anthony Brenske. Youngblood signs something to Brenske, who then chuckles. Black seems annoyed by the whole thing, but he shrugs it off. Youngblood does it again, but this time Black rips the tape off and decides to say something. MR. BLACK: “Would you mind not being so obnoxious? Why are you even here anyway, it isn’t like Brenske is going to be a factor in this match.” RICHARD YOUNGBLOOD: “Wake hand picked us to be on the team.” MR. BLACK: “No. He picked Brenske, which was a stupid decision. You are just here because you follow him around. All you are is a leach.” RICHARD YOUNGBLOOD: “Are you kidding me? What makes you think you deserve to be here more than we do? Last time I checked, you weren’t so impressive last week.” MR. BLACK: “That’s irrelevant. I’m here because I am the best pick. Wake knows I can help him win this match, he just wanted you here so Blake wouldn’t get angry that it was a handicap match, although it still basically is.” RICHARD YOUNGBLOOD: “Ha. Why don’t we just make it a handicap right now, when Anthony breaks your nose right here and right now.” From the back, Mark Cross and Tony Miranda walk through the door and in between the two men; Miranda grabs some tape and smiles while Cross starts to speak. MARK CROSS: “The fact is, we don’t want it to be a handicap match. We are here so we can beat both of you out there tonight, so get on the same page… for us.” TONY MIRANDA: “You two just kiss and make up, ok?” Miranda and Cross exit with all seven rolls of tape, Black shakes his head while Youngblood leads Brenske to the other side of the room. JAMAL JACKSON: “And the opponent, from Boston, Massachusetts… David Graves!” Syndal slides into the ring and taunts to the fans a bit. JAMAL JACKSON: “And the opponent, from San Francisco, California… Johnny Scar!” Scar makes his way down to the ring to his music. He slides inside and the bell sounds, signifying the start of the match up. Syndal quickly slides out of the ring, leaving David Graves and Johnny Scar to go after each other. Scar rolls out of the ring and onto the floor, Syndal see’s a spot and begins to stop Scar out on the floor. Syndal pulls graves up and suplexes him onto the floor on the outside. Graves, meanwhile, gains momentum and heaves himself over the ropes, but Syndal sidesteps it, sending graves into the sand. Syndal throws Graves back into the ring and hit’s a knee drop right on Grave’s stomach, knocking the wind out of him. Scar is on the apron now, Syndal charges him, but Scar ducks it with a big shoulder to the sternum. Syndal flutters backwards into a roll up by Graves…1.….2.….SCAR breaks the pin. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “That was almost a quick victory for David Graves there… if it wasn’t for the meddling Johnny Scar!” Scar whips Graves into the corner, then opts to work some more on Syndal who is flat on his back. Graves wastes no time, he puts Scar into a surfboard hold. Syndal is up, he dropkicks Scar in the face during the surfboard. Scar rolls to the outside while Syndal and Graves trade blows. Syndal argues with the ref for a few seconds, then decides to take David’s foot off. He goes to the outside and uses the rope for leverage, twisting the foot on top of it. Syndal is in shock, he argues with the referee some more, Scar slides into the ring, he traps Syndal’s arm…. ARM TRAP NECKBREAKER! 1.….2.………………………….. The referee gets pulled out the ring by someone in a black sweat suit?! The person slides into the ring and lands a punch on Scar… BRASS KNUCKLES! Scar was robbed!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I think I know who this person is…” Syndal gets his arms raised by the referee in charge, then he turns to the hidden person. Yeah it’s not happening. And at Revolt, I choose you to try to take my title.” Syndal nods, but almost as soon as she finishes, La’Sarah Audric’s theme hit’s the PA system, followed by tons of boo’s. Right beside her is her new body guard, Unforgettable Jones. She grabs begins to speak. LA’SARAH AUDRIC: “No, that’s not how it works little girl. You see, you have no authority around here. I, on the other hand, do. So, I think I am going to use it for a minute here. At the July 7th edition of Revolt, it won’t be just yourself and Adam Syndal in the ring for the TV Title; it will be Adam Syndal vs. Pandora vs. Johnny Scar vs. MY MAN… Unforgettable Jones!” Audric laughs on the ramp as all of those involved look at one another. Pandora to Syndal, Scar to Audric as we cut to backstage. WINNER: ADAM SYNDAL We head backstage where Alioth Starre, the GIW Undisputed Champion, is standing by with Zachary Pagonis, who is filling in for Sarah Sharp who is no longer at the event, due to very obvious reasons.
