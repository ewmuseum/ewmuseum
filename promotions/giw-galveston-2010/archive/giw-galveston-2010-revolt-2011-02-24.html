Revolt, 24 February 2011

GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: THURSDAY NIGHT REVOLT! LIVE FROM THE SEAWALL BOULEVARD ENTERTAIMENT COMPLEX = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! As soon as the opening pyro ends, “Circles” by Incubus hits to a loud ovation from the crowd. Not a person in the building is sitting down as out from the back comes Eric Sparrow with a big grin on his face as he strolls out from the backstage area, holding the recently christened ‘GIW Undisputed Championship’ around his waist. The fans are cheering him on as he’s dressed up in his ring gear. RILEY STEVENS: “Here he is, our NEW GIW Undisputed Champion.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “You have no idea how bittersweet this moment is. I’ve been asking for it forever, but I didn’t expect fake Sparrow to win.” RILEY STEVENS: “If you ask me, I think this is the Sparrow that has been waiting to come out for months now.” Sparrow high fives a few fans, as he steps up into the ring and then jumps over the ropes. As the camera pans to the crowd many of which are clapping, others fist pumping for him. All in all it’s a joyous occasion as we return to the ring, where Sparrow is standing with a microphone. ERIC SPARROW: “This moment, this day, has been a long time coming. I’ve been in GIW for a long time and I’ve watched as Ross Rydell rose to the top, I was beaten by Wake Walker during his reign, and then I witnessed Aspen Chaud shock the world with her title reign. But the one thing none of them can claim to be, is the first ever GIW Undisputed Champion. You see last week, I won this belt. I defeated a legend all while earning all of your backing. This title means the world to me, so from here on out…here on Revolt...I’m going to prove each and every night…why I am the ‘GIW Undisputed Champion’!” The crowd roars with approval as he unstraps his belt and then raises his title high in the air, before lowering it down onto his shoulder. ERIC SPARROW: “The Eric Sparrow era stars now!” The crowd cheers as he hands the referee his title as we take a quick commercial break before he has his match here tonight, as we fade out.[/font] QUOTE As the next match starts Sparrow is already in the ring from the last segment stretching out his arms as “Down” by Curse Icon plays causing the fans to boo. They are impressed that he’s out here yet again but none the less the fans are not fans of Trent Arianna. RILEY STEVENS: “You’ve got to give it to Trent, pulling double duty here tonight. But at the same time, doesn’t seem smart when you’re facing our champion, Eric Sparrow.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I like the guy, seems like he’s got ‘it’. Who knows, he wins tonight he might just find himself as the new contender.” Arianna walks down towards the ring, ignoring the fans who jeer him as he steps into the ring with the GIW Undisputed Champion. Though he doesn’t seem discouraged at all, rather he has a confident grin across his face as the referee calls for the bell. DING DING DING! The two come together quickly, meeting up for a lock up. The fans are behind Sparrow but unfortunately for him the strength advantage is not. Trent slowly backs him up towards the corner and as the referee tells him to get off, he cheaply rakes Eric across the eyes. The fans boo the act as he backs up, shrugging and running forward with a big clothesline to Eric’s chest. Thus far things are looking good for him, as he backs away calling for Eric to get to his feet. As Sparrow gets up Trent comes forward with a flurry of head butts which he calls ‘The Ram’, the final one sends Eric to the mat. Right away Trent bounces up to the top rope, looking down at his opponent. With a smirk on his face he waits for him to get up. Finally he takes a gamble with a ‘Leap of Faith’ a Flying Crossbody! At the last possible moment Sparrow rolls out of the way sending Trent to the mat hard. The fans cheer as Eric gets up, pounding his chest as Trent gets up holding his chest. Eric runs full steam forward, with a Wheel Kick that sends Trent falling back into the corner. With him there, the fans roar as Sparrow goes his fastest nailing him with a ‘Pineapple Express’ a Corner Dropkick! The fans go wild as almost immediately he steps through the ropes, waiting patiently to go springboard. Just as Arianna gets up and turns around Eric times it just right and lands an ‘Air Sparrow’ a Springboard Clothesline! Right away he hooks the leg 1…2…Kick Out! The fans don’t seem too worried as Eric gets up nodding his head, calling for them to start a slow clap. As Trent stumbles to his feet Sparrow wraps him up for the ‘Purple Haze’ but Trent pushes him away and feints a dropkick then gives him a eye poke, a move he appropriately calls ‘Nobody Home’ which he follows up right away with a real dropkick, as he quickly hooks the leg 1…2…Kick Out! RILEY STEVENS: “Trent’s sticking with the champion, but thus far most of his offense has been illegal moves.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “They are working Riley, who cares. The man’s just doing what it takes.” Trent gets up to his feet arguing that this should be over as he shakes the referee off, walking over and grabbing Sparrow and lifting him up onto his shoulders. He seems to be ready to end this but Eric rolls off his back and rolls him up 1…Kick Out right away and both men are right up to there feet. Trent quickly whips him into the corner and then goes full steam at him. Eric narrowly dodges the clothesline attempt and then jumps up top rope. He jumps off for a Hurricanrana! But is thrown right into a Powerbomb from Trent, 1…2…Kick Out! The fans breathe a sigh of relief as Sparrow kicks out. Arianna gets up angrily, and then lifts up Sparrow. BOOM! Superkick to the jaw. Trent falls to his knee holding his jaw, as Sparrow backs up and knocks him to the mat with a ‘Chow Moda’ a swift kick to a kneeling opponent, that puts him down. Right then Sparrow goes top rope, realizing his chance. RILEY STEVENS: “Here it comes!” He jumps off and crushes Trent with the beautiful ‘Spinal Tap’ a Corkscrew Somersault! The fans go wild as he hooks the leg 1…2…3! That’s it. RILEY STEVENS: “There we have it; the champion continues his path of dominance.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Trent still looked solid.” RILEY STEVENS: “I’ll give him that, for competing twice here tonight he did well but neither was a victory you have to ask if he put too much pressure on himself.” Suddenly there is a bit of a buzz as Sparrow goes to the top rope celebrating. The fans cheers turn to frantic pleas with Eric. When he turns around, he gets popped with a big kick. Then quickly he’s spun around on the turnbuckle by Jak Hendricks. Almost right away he nails him with the ‘BRAINBUSTAAAAAAAAAAHHHH!’ a Turnbuckle Brainbuster, that takes Sparrow out. RILEY STEVENS: “What in the world was that for!?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “It’s called putting your name in the pot. Jak Hendricks realized there was no clear cut contender and he just made himself one.” The fans boo as Jak looks down at Sparrow and then over at his title belt, as he nods his head and slides out of the ring the world hearing his statement loud and clear as we head to the back. WINNER: ERIC SPARROW As we head to the back we come into a scene with Joshua Acquin standing by with Sarah Sharp, whom has the microphone. SARAH SHARP: “Joshua, you called us here today to make a very big announcement, mind if you tell us?” Joshua nods his head as Sarah holds the microphone over to his mouth, as he responds. JOSHUA ACQUIN: “Yes and unfortunately it’s not good news either. You see, during my training this week I tore my ACL. This is no simple recovery and it means that I will have to sit out of GIW, for quite some time. I don’t want to say goodbye, because I will fight through this. And when I get back, I’ll be better then ever...” Just as he finishes some footsteps can be heard as both Sarah and Joshua watch as Justin Volt with Aspen Chaud walk up towards him edging Sarah out of the way. JUSTIN VOLT: “Really man? You think anybody cares. Your old news, ‘High Voltage’ is all the rage now. You see, while you’ve been the leader of the ‘Hate the New Aspen’ brigade, she’s happier then ever. Heck, she upgraded from you way back in the day, all the way to me. I mean, talk about an upgrade. But since, you can’t do much wrestling why don’t you and everyone else tune in and watch us, show the world why we are the ‘GIW Tag Team Champions’.” Justin smirks and shoulders him as he walks by as Aspen follows, with Joshua shaking his head slowly at her as we take a small commercial break. QUOTE As we return to Revolt we see the smiling faces of Jacoby Jackson and Reyna Silva in the ring, seeming pumped up for their match tonight. "To Be Loved" by Papa Roach now plays bringing a loud mass of boos from the crowd as Aspen Chaud walks out with Justin Volt following not far behind him. They both have their belts on their shoulders as they slowly head towards the ring, with big grins on their faces. The fans as per usual give them mixed reactions. RILEY STEVENS: “It still seems like nobody is quite sure what to think of the new Aspen. I will admit I’m just as baffled.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Oh come on, she’s a champion now. She’s with a man she loves. Heck, she’s a lot better now then ever before.” The duo reaches the steps as Justin walks across first stopping and holding down the bottom rope as Aspen steps under the middle rope, she then sits down on the middle rope as Justin steps in. The fans continue with their mixed bag as Aspen walks across the ring leaning through the ropes and blowing the camera a kiss as Justin holds his belt up on the top rope. Both jump down and hand their belts to the ref who hands them out and calls for the bell. DING DING DING! It’s decided right away that it’ll be Jacoby Jackson and Justin Volt starting the contest. Jacoby comes forward running right into a huge European Uppercut! The fans boo as Justin follows it up with a big whip off the ropes. Jacoby is able to dodge his first punch attempt, but the second time he runs right into a big arm drag. Jackson slowly fights to a vertical base but is quickly sent flying over into the corner where Justin comes forward with a big clothesline. As Jacoby stumbles forward he wraps his arm around him and then slams him down with a Reverse DDT right into a cover 1…Kick Out! It’s not enough yet as Jackson will not go down that easily. Both men get to their feet, ready to continue. Justin calls for him to get up and then as he does runs forward jumping up into a Head Scissor Sleeper Hold! The fans are a bit impressed as Jackson falls to the mat trying to break free. After a few moments of tearing at it Jackson seems to be fading only for Silva to get in and break it up. The fans cheer for her as she gets up to walks back to her corner but Justin quickly gets up, stalking her into the corner as she falls down in fear. He smirks and turns around ducking a big a kick from a recovered Jackson. Volt tries to grab his legs but he kicks him back and then jumps up and tags in Silva and as Volt stumbles back Aspen tags in. RILEY STEVENS: “Here comes the ladies!” Both girls come forward but it’s Aspen who takes her right into a ‘Rage Within’ a Thesz Press Followed by Punches! The fans aren’t sure who to be behind so there’s an awkward split in the crowd. Both girls hop back up, but it’s Aspen that crushes her with a kick that sends her flying to her corner. Jackson retags himself in and uses his strength to throw Aspen across the ring. With the referee checking on Reyna, Volt angrily jumps in crushing him from behind with ‘The Volt’ a Reverse RKO! The fans boo as he exits the ring as the referee turns around. Quickly a recovered Silva tries to stop them, only to be caught with a ‘Dream Ride’ a Running Axe Kick from Aspen. The fans boo as Silva rolls out of the ring in pain as Aspen crawls onto the mat covering Jackson, still out from Justin’s move 1…2…3! The tag champs win. RILEY STEVENS: “I can’t say I am impressed with the way they won it.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “What? Did they function too well as a team for you? Goodness Riley you’re hopeless.” Justin hops in smiling and holding up Aspen’s arm as she reaches over and hugs him happy with the win. The celebration continues as they are handed their belts, as suddenly Justin spots the fact that Jacoby is getting up. Shaking his head he taps Aspen and points at him. At first she shakes her head and then just as he gets up she agrees as they crush him with ‘The Good Conductor’ a Double Superkick that lays him out for good. RILEY STEVENS: “I guess they felt the need to put an exclamation point on this one…” The fans are booing as Justin and Aspen slide out of the ring together heading towards the back, as the camera now takes us there as well. WINNER: HIGH VOLTAGE As the camera heads to the back we are greeted by the rather odd grouping of Amanda Rowland standing by with Jak Hendricks, whom seems to be talking her ear off. JAK HENDRICKS: “…And then I nailed him with the BRAINBUSTAH! The fans booed me, but frankly I am the new contender. How does this sound, Jak Hendricks ‘GIW Undisputed Champion’? Good right.” Amanda who seems to be his willing ears, smiles and nods her head quickly with a grin. AMANDA ROWLAND: “Yeah like totally! And it’s sort of cool, just as you do that, Seth Seeker your best buddy returns.” Jak goes from a big smirk to an almost angry glare at her as he shakes his head, never sure with a ditz like Amanda. JAK HENDRICKS: “You mean…Seth Seeker? He’s back.” Amanda quickly nods her head, as she responds. AMANDA ROWLAND: “Yeah I overheard La’Sarah; she said it was supposed to be a big secret. I figured you’d already know, since you two are like best friends.” Amanda giggles not knowing just how wrong she is as Jak gets up to his feet quickly calling someone with his cell. JAK HENDRICKS: “I want Security standing by during my match…Why? Because I said so!” Jak angrily slams closed his cell and walks off leaving Amanda with a big smile on her face as she shrugs her shoulders and we go to commercial. QUOTE As we come back to the ring, we see Omen already standing in the ring prepping for his match as he waits for his opponent. “Forever” by Drake, Kanye West, Lil Wayne, and Eminem hits as the fans immediately starts to boo. They boo even more as Jak Hendricks walks out with a team of five security guards surrounding him at all of his sides, as he walks with them towards the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Seems like somebody is a bit scared. I haven’t heard anything about Seth Seeker being here tonight.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Who cares? We all know Seeker’s a lunatic. Smarter for Jak to just be safe in case, Seeker’s the guy that attacked both of his friends. Thankfully Jak put him in his place at ‘Exile’.” The fans reign down their boos as Jak Hendricks as he slides into the ring with Security surrounding all sides of the ring, two on the entrance side. Omen seems to be a bit confused none the less the referee rings the bell. DING DING DING! Omen comes running forward trying to make an impact but runs right into a Yakuza Kick! The fans boo realizing just how much impact the move has as Hendricks gets up smirking. Regardless the whole time he can be seen looking out at the crowd searching for Seeker as Omen gets to his feet. Angry about the situation Hendricks unleashes with a move he calls the ‘Jak Attack’ a flurry of punches and kicks from every where. Finally he whips Omen off the ropes and right back into a huge Spinebuster! The fans are booing as he gets up, searching the area as he turns his attention back to Omen. He shakes his head, clearing his mind of Seth as he locks in the Sharpshooter! Right away Omen groans in pain before tapping out to the hold, ending this one before it really got started. RILEY STEVENS: “You have to be impressed, with his mind not totally on the match he got the job done.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “That’s why he’ll be our next champion.” He quickly exits the ring, positioning his guys around him as he starts slowly walking up the ramp way. As he’s about half way up the ramp, “Nightmare” by Avenged Sevenfold hits and the fans go absolutely insane! Jak brings his guys close to him as he waits for him to come out. Suddenly the crowd goes wild as the big man comes up from behind him! At the last second Jak realizes it and he jumps out of the way. Jak quickly bails to the back as the Security guards try stopping him but are met with big punches. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “This guy is insane!” The Security puts up just enough fight to hold him off, allowing Jak to get away but when he finally finishes all five of them off he turns to the crowd and flexes as the crowd cheers the return of Seth Seeker. WINNER: JAK HENDRICKS As we come to the backstage area we see Tony Miranda standing with The Shadow, whom is sitting down in front of a table observing it as Tony paces behind him. TONY MIRANDA: “Marcus Hister chose to interfere with our business! People that do that often times end up, either dead or severely injured. Tonight I don’t mind what you do with Marcus just as long as he is exposed of. Because at the end of the day we need to send a strong message, messing with us…is not a good idea. Tonight you will show them why…” Tony pats him on his shoulder as Shadow angrily flips the table and then stands up heading towards the ring as we cut to a different area backstage. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = As we come to Marcus Hister’s locker room we see him prepping for his match just as well, taping up his fists for a big match. Standing not far from him, is Aspen Chaud still in her ring gear. ASPEN CHAUD: “I just came to let you know, that we are very appreciate for what you did last week. Now we don’t need you to fight our battles, but I’ll admit you saved me from having to pick out a bunch of splitters last week. Just know that we’ll be rooting you on from back here and if we need to, we’ll be back out there in a second. But yeah, thanks a lot.” Marcus finishes his wrapping and throws the tape away looking up at Aspen having listened to the whole thing. MARCUS HISTER: “Anything for a friend.” Marcus grins and then walks by her heading towards the ring just as well as the Tables Match is next, after these commercial interruptions. QUOTE We return with "The Shadow Knows" Radio Drama Introduction followed by "Suffer" by Shawn McPherson, as the crowd immediately begins to boo as The Shadow makes his daunting entrance from the back. As he walks down to the ring the camera pans out showing several tables surrounding the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “As you can see the following match is a Tables Match. Which means, only one thing…carnage.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Not to mention the two biggest psychos are going at it.” The Shadow walks up the steps and then steps over the top rope showing his daunting size as “Under the Knife” by Rise Against now hits as the fans give Marcus a bit warmer welcome. It’s clear a few of the fans have opted to choose him, being that he’s seemingly the underdog. RILEY STEVENS: “It takes a crazy man, to not back down from The Shadow. It takes an even crazier man to challenge him to a fight, Marcus Hister did just that.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “We shall see how smart that really was.” Suddenly Marcus breaks into a full on sprint sliding into the ring and jumping up and hammering The Shadow with a few blows as the referee quickly calls for the bell. DING DING DING! The crowd goes wild as the much smaller Hister begins to deliver shot after shot to The Shadow not backing down for a moment. The fans in the arena are seemingly a bit excited by this and have come to his side in the fight. Continuing to throw blow after blow, Hister seems to be inflicting some pain. But almost all too easily The Shadow gives him a huge toss, throwing him back across the ring. Hister is quick to get up as now both begin to circle one another, ready to go at it but being a bit smarter about it. Marcus comes forward again only this time he walks right into the clutches of The Shadow. Lifting him up into the air Shadow starts to slowly bring him towards the ropes, threatening to throw him out of the ring into one of the tables on the outside. At the last second Hister rolls off, quickly kicking away at his knees trying to ground the beast. Finally he gets him to one knee and as he runs off the ropes and comes at him, only for Shadow to crazily heave him up over his shoulders sending him flying to the mat with a harsh thud. His strength is clearly his advantage as Marcus holds his back, the fall ending up being farther then one would imagine. Hister gets up slowly as Shadow is already up. He runs forward, going after him with a big boot only for Marcus to duck down as Shadow’s leg gets caught up over the ropes. Realizing his advantage Hister starts to unleash a flurry of haymakers from all sides. Finally Shadow falls off, as Hister heads to the top rope, jumping off and connecting with a Diving Elbow! Bringing a small pop from the crowd. RILEY STEVENS: “I’ll admit Marcus Hister has impressed me here tonight. But it’s all about putting your opponent through a table.” Realizing what his goal is Hister exits the ring, grabbing one of the tables. He kicks in the legs and then slides it in as The Shadow is still down and out from the elbow drop. Marcus sets it up in the ring, clicking in both sides as he walks across the ring to lift The Shadow up. He slowly gets him to his feet, but either Shadow was playing possum or he just made a recovery as Shadow elbows him in the face and then viciously clotheslines him to the mat. As Marcus crawls to his feet, he does so right into The Shadow’s trap as he’s set up right where The Shadow wants him. The Shadow lifts him up right into the ‘Instant Death a Tiger Driver '91, its name seemingly fitting. The fans seem to be calling it over as The Shadow, slowly lifts the seemingly dead weight that is Marcus Hister. He grabs him by the throat and is about to lift him up, but Marcus slides out and with a flurry delivers a few punches. Then BAM! A Superkick to the jaw, slowly makes The Shadow start to stumble seemingly about to fall into the table. Marcus jumps off the ropes going for a Spear but The Shadow catches him by the throat on his way. Just like that The Shadow ends his momentum by lifting him up and then tossing him through the Table with a One Handed Chokeslam! As the referee calls for the bell. RILEY STEVENS: “The Shadow wins here tonight, but one can’t help but be impressed by the fight in Marcus Hister.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “He lost, who cares.” The fans boo as The Shadow stands tall over Marcus Hister. Almost as expected High Voltage comes running down towards the rings. The Shadow seems to be ready but what he’s not ready for is the fact that they are coming with a Ladder! The fans are caught off guard a bit as The Shadow, waits for them in the ring. Holding both ends they run forward slamming The Shadow down with their ladder. RILEY STEVENS: “High Voltage is here and they brought a ladder!” The fans are going insane as The Shadow seems out of it, as he gets up he receives ‘The Good Conductor’ a Double Superkick from Aspen and Justin! The fans are going wild wondering where Tony is as Justin sets up the ladder and starts to scale it, looking down at The Shadow with evil intentions. RILEY STEVENS: “Oh my! What’s he got planned here!” The fans are going wild and as Aspen watches him she doesn’t realize that Tony has just entered the ring. Justin is about to jump but Tony pushes over the ladder sending Volt flying to the outside right through a table! The crowd pops as Aspen slides out right away checking on Justin. In the ring Tony does the same with The Shadow as Aspen and Tony look up at one another as it seems both have lost one half of their teams as we go to a commercial break with the crowd reeling with excitement. WINNER: THE SHADOW This time as we go to the backstage area the face of Ross Rydell is the focal point of the screen, as the camera pans out and he begins talking. ROSS RYDELL: “Oh come on, the people already know I am going to beat Chris Jericho. So why don’t you put something on the line, to make it a bit more entertaining for em’.” The camera pans out as it’s revealed we are in La’Sarah Audric’s Office, as she’s sitting behind her desk listening to Ross. LA’SARAH AUDRIC: “Now I wouldn’t be that confident, if I were you. But I can’t help but think both of you are deserving of a shot. So tonight’s match will earn the winner a shot at the vacant ‘GIW North Beach Championship’ at ‘Revelation’. How’s that?” Ross rubs his chin a bit, nodding his head slowly. ROSS RYDELL: “That’ll work, that’ll work.” Ross heads out of the office and just as he leaves to go to his match the door slams open and in walks Jak Hendricks, clearly angry. JAK HENDRICKS: “What the hell was that!? You’d think you would tell the guy whose biggest enemy is returning, that he’s returning! Instead I have to hear about it from that ditz. I demand that he be fired, he’s crazy!” Jak seems adamant about this breathing a bit heavy from running away as La’Sarah shakes her head. LA’SARAH AUDRIC: “Well the fans love him, so I can’t do that. But what I can do is this, he’s forbidden to lay a hand on you until after ‘Revelation’.” Jak thinks it over and doesn’t seem to totally understand why that would be the deadline. JAK HENDRICKS: “And just why ‘Revelation’?” Jak raises his eye brow curiously as she smiles at him. LA’SARAH AUDRIC: “Because at ‘Revelation’ you’ll be facing Eric Sparrow one on one for the ‘GIW Undisputed Championship’.” This brings a big smirk to his face as he nods his head. JAK HENDRICKS: “I’m glad you and Blake have come common sense. Sparrow’s reign will end before it even began.” Jak walks out of her office as La’Sarah sits back breathing a few times realizing that the show is nearly over as we take one last commercial break. QUOTE The crowd is ready for the main event of the evening, as “Break the Walls Down” sends the fans into the biggest boos perhaps of the night. Out from the back comes Chris Jericho, the crowd showing him no respect as he walks out with a confident smirk. He slowly struts his way to the ring, ignoring all of the fans angry remarks. RILEY STEVENS: “As if these two’s rivalry was not enough on the line La’Sarah just announced a shot at the North Beach Championship is on the line as well.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “If Jericho can’t be the ‘Undisputed’ Champion at least he can hold that belt.” As he enters the ring, he bounces up to the top rope where he pounds his chest ignoring the fans boos. “Motherfucker of the Year” by Motley Crue perhaps one of the most recognizable themes in GIW hits as the fans pop for Ross Rydell. Out he comes with a big grin, heading straight for the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “But we all know the story with these two. Originally it was suppose to be the two of them competing for the Galveston Island Championship back at ‘Exile’. Instead Jericho ended up injuring Rydell and costing him his shot. Revenge is surely in his eyes.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Yeah right, Jericho will just crush him again.” Ross Rydell clearly has bad intentions as he quickly goes up the steps and into the ring, walking across shouting some words at Jericho as the crowd is on their feet for this one. The referee separates them just long enough to call for the bell. DING DING DING! There is clearly no amount of respect as the two go right into a battle, meeting with fists right from the start. Both men are throwing wild punches, none of which seem to be doing much more then make the other person angry. Finally, Rydell catches him with a big elbow that sends Jericho stumbling back. He comes forward with a big punch, BOO! Rydell answers back with one of his own, YEAH! Jericho comes with a Knife Edge Chop this time, BOO! Rydell ends this back and forth with a nasty European Uppercut. He runs off the ropes jumping at Chris with a huge Roaring Elbow! The crowd goes wild as he takes Jericho off his feet, getting up and pounding his chest clearly throwing it all on the line against Y2J. He waits for him to get up and connects with a big ‘New York City Speed’ a Savate Kick that sends Jericho to the mat hard. Ross goes for a cover 1…2…Kick Out. Not enough yet to put away Mr. Jericho. Rydell goes to pick him up only to end up being caught with a big rake to the eyes, causing him to stumble back a bit. With the referee clueless to what happened Jericho runs forward nailing him with a One Handed Bulldog! The fans are booing as he goes for a cover 1…2…Kick Out! The fans pop for Ross, hoping he’ll soon end this. As he gets to his feet, Jericho waits seeming to be ready to crush him with a move. Instead Ross is able to push him away and then whip him into the corner. As he comes at him Jericho gets up a foot at the last second. Quickly he bounces up to the top rope and jumps off nailing Rydell with a huge Missile Dropkick! Quickly he goes for a cover again 1…2…Kick Out! This time he gets up angry, blaming the referee as he demands that he count faster. As he does this Rydell is getting up behind him, slowly. RILEY STEVENS: “Jericho best his eyes on his opponent.” When he finally turns around Ross is up and he nails him with a kick to the groin then hooks the arm which sets up the ‘Kill the Noise’ a Tiger Bomb that is right into a cover 1…2…Kick Out! The fans thought that was it as they seem nervous about worse case scenario here. Ross gets up however, not seeming worried at all as he waits for Y2J to get to his feet. Right as he gets up Rydell goes for a Running Knee but Jericho dodges it. As Ross turns around having missed he walks right into a ‘Codebreaker’! Sending the crowd into a booing frenzy as Jericho makes the cover 1…2…Kick Out! The fans go wild as Chris sits up seemingly shocked by what just happened here. He slowly gets up, seeming to have seen the unthinkable happen. He shakes it off and realizes what he needs to do, as he prepares for a ‘Walls of Jericho’. But he’s too late as when he goes for it, Ross quickly kicks out of it. The fans go wild as Ross jumps up to his feet, and then kicks Jericho in the groin going for a ‘New York Minute’. Jericho is able to spin out of it as he pushes Rydell away. He goes for a clothesline and right as he does Rydell School Boys him out of no where 1…2…3! Whoa, out of the blue! RILEY STEVENS: “That’s it, that’s it.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Fast count!” The crowd goes insane as Ross hops up holding his arms high. Jericho gets up, staring at the referee in anger. As Rydell celebrates Chris grabs the referee by his shirt and screams at him. Finally he throws the referee down just as Ross turns around. Shaking his head, he grabs him and just like that tosses him out of the ring. The fans are going wild as Rydell slides out of the ring right after him, grabbing him by the back of the head and slamming him head first into the announce table. RILEY STEVENS: “Looks like Ross isn’t finished!” Just as Jericho seems out of it, he throws off everything from the announce table and then tosses Jericho onto it. The fans are starting to go crazy realizing what might be coming. Ross hops up onto it with him waiting as Y2J, slowly pulls himself up. Just as he does he kicks him in the gut and then sets up. Rather then going through the announce table it’s clear Rydell has other intentions. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Somebody stop this man!” With a big grin on his face Rydell, slowly waits looking out at the fans as they cheer him on. Just like that he does it, a ‘New York Minute’ a Cross Arm Pedigree right off the Announce Table to the ground as the crowd goes absolutely wild! Everyone is on their feet in the Complex. RILEY STEVENS: “Ross Rydell has just got his revenge here on Chris Jericho!” We are shown several angles of the move as the crowd is going wild. Ross poses above Jericho with lots of emotion as the show slowly comes to a close with a HUGE finish that we’ll not soon forget. WINNER: ROSS RYDELL
