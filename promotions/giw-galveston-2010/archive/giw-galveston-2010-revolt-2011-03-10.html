GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: THURSDAY NIGHT REVOLT! LIVE FROM THE SEAWALL BOULEVARD ENTERTAIMENT COMPLEX = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! The crowd goes wild as the opening pyro goes off, we come into Revolt’s new home and pan around what is a packed house here tonight. Everyone’s on their feet cheering as the camera flies by all of them, many wearing GIW merchandise. Finally we end up on Riley and Oliver both of which are wearing smiles. RILEY STEVENS: “What a night we have in store for you, full of exciting action! The card is falling into place for ‘Revelation’ and tonight we find out the answers to a few more questions when the final matches are put into place. The show starts with a Triple Threat match featuring Seth Seeker, Omen, and the debuting Michael Rosenberg.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I really hope somebody stops that lunatic Seth Seeker! I’m just glad he can’t lay a hand on Jak Hendricks.” RILEY STEVENS: “That’s very true. Following that will be a tag match featuring Ross Rydell and Matt Kluge going against The Killers. This will serve as a tune up for Ross’s match at ‘Revelation’ where he’ll attempt to win the ‘GIW North Beach Championship’. And immediately following that is two matches to join him in that match as Marcus Hister and Alioth Starre as well as Trent Arianna versus The Fallen. All four are worthy, who will walk away the victors?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “God forbid I get a word in.” RILEY STEVENS: “Sorry, it’s my job. And in the main event it’ll be a Strange Bedfellows Match as Tony Miranda and Aspen Chaud team up against ‘GIW Undisputed Champion’ Eric Sparrow and his Contender, Jak Hendricks. What a show...” Just as he finishes the crowd is caught off guard by something new, blaring out of the speakers. Hi My Name Is…You Won’t Remember, Wait Til’ December. Cause You Thought That I Was Pure as Snow. Guess You Didn’t Know. “Now I'm That Bitch” by Livvi Franc hits as the fans look up towards the stage as to their surprise its Aspen Chaud walking out to this rather explicit theme. Wasting little time she heads towards the ring, rather then high fiving the fans as per usual she instead walks straight by them and walks up the steps. She bends down and steps over the bottom rope as, she picks up the mic sitting at ringside. Stopping on a dime she stands in the ring looking out at the crowd. ASPEN CHAUD: “You know over the past month, I’ve walked out to split reactions. I’ve come out to you fans not giving me the respect I have earned from all of you. And if you all think you can turn on me. If you all are going to turn on me, then I suppose it’s time I cut the cord with all of you. You see I have tried endlessly for you fans yet the moment I do something for myself, I get booed. I get disrespected. So you know what? I am sick of fighting your battles. I am sick of fighting your wars. And most importantly I am sick of you fans!” Aspen lowers the microphone seeming as angry as ever as the fans have now fully turned on her, everyone letting their boos be heard as she glares out at them with little remorse. ASPEN CHAUD: “Justin’s been telling me for awhile now that I should drop you guys. And frankly I can already feel a weight off my shoulders. No more trying to please you. No more trying to be the good girl. It hasn’t been working out. So I’ve been waiting to say this for a long time now. I have been waiting to say this for a long time now…THE BITCH IS BACK! Tonight marks the start of a long reign around here! I’ve been ridiculed for months for what I’ve done. And it seems you fans gave me the ultimatum…Justin or You? And that was your biggest mistake, because I choose Justin. At ‘Revelation’ we’ll retain our belts and from there we will start taking them all, one…by…one! You made this happen and now you all have to deal with it…THE QUEEN IS HERE!” Aspen tosses down the microphone the message being received loud and clear. Both announcers seem to be left speechless as Aspen glares out at the fans, not one of them left on her side as we take a break.[/font] QUOTE After the break we return to a ring already full with three stars. Seth Seeker being the one who clearly stands out, basically a giant compared to the other two men in the ring. Mike Rosenberg however does not seem unconfident instead he seems ready to go. RILEY STEVENS: “I don’t envy anyone that has to face Seth Seeker right now.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Why? Because the man’s lost his mind. I’m hoping Omen or Mike Rosenberg can punish him.” With all three men ready to go the referee calls for the bell and right away the match gets started. DING DING DING! Foolishly it’s Omen that goes full board at Seeker trying to take advantage of him. Instead he runs right into a big shoulder block as Seth basically swats him out of the air. Rosenberg comes in as Seeker is a bit distracted and it allows Mike to get him down to a knee. Mike is clearly trying to take advantage of him while he’s down, landing a few kicks that seem to be hurting the big man Seeker. The fans are clearly on Seeker’s side in this one, as he fights up to his feet giving Mike a shove that basically sends him flying back across the ring. Omen is now back to his feet and he again tries to get Seeker, this time he choose to jump on his back looking like a midget trying to strangle a bear. The fans laugh as Seeker, basically reaches over his back grabbing Omen and tossing him over his shoulder right down onto the mat hard. Out of no where it’ Mike Rosenberg that catches Seeker with a dropkick that sends him stumbling through the ropes and out of the ring. He takes a second to admire his work as turns his attention to trying to finish off the already weakened Omen. Lifting him up he smirks takes a step back and then crushes him with a Superkick! Rather then going for a cover he goes top rope, bringing a murmur from the fans. He waits for Omen to get up but before he can, Seeker is now up on the ropes beside him, grabbing him with one hand he basically tosses him off sending him into the barricade. Right away he steps right back into the ring where Omen stumbles right into a move Seeker calls the ‘Don't Fear The Reaper’ a Jackhammer! The fans go wild as he makes the cover 1…2…3! RILEY STEVENS: “Seeker returns and he does so with a win.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Damnit.” Seeker gets up pointing at the camera and screaming “Jak Your Mine!” as the referee raises his arm. Suddenly there’s some commotion as on the outside Mike has a microphone. MIKE ROSENBERG: “You know what Seeker! I heard what you had to say about me. I heard how you said you thought I didn’t belong here. So you know what, you don’t have a match at ‘Revelation’. And you didn’t beat me tonight. So why don’t you face me one on one!” Mike hands the microphone back as he walks towards the back as Seeker nods his head accepting the offer as these two will have another go at one another come ‘Revelation’ this time one will walk out with the clear win. WINNER: SETH SEEKER As we return to the backstage area, we find Seth Seeker just making his way to the back where he’s patted on the back by a staff member, however as he takes a few more steps he walks right into Jak Hendricks who is smirking and standing right in front of him. JAK HENDRICKS: “Well, well, well. It’s really nice to have you back Seth. If you haven’t noticed, the Openers have been rather weak without you. Thanks for warming the crowd up for me though; after all I wrestle in the Main Event.” Jak smirks spreading out his teeth as Seth, clinches his fists at his sides seemingly ready to strike at any time. JAK HENDRICKS: “Don’t forget, if you lay a hand on me…YOU ARE FIRED.” Jak has a big grin on his face clearly enjoying the power he has over Seth, who shakes his head easing up. SETH SEEKER: “Listen! You have your fun while it lasts. And honestly I hope you win the belt. Because I am going to enjoy; ending your career and taking your belt all in the same night. Just know that this little safety net you have going for you, won’t last forever. And when it’s up, I’ll be right there waiting.” Seeker walks by patting Jak on the back as he heads to his locker room as Hendricks glares back at Seeker, shaking his head as we head to a commercial break. QUOTE As we come back from break we hear the sounds of “Bodies” by Saliva as The Killers have just entered the ring, Nelson and Brody nodding their heads ready for there match up here tonight. RILEY STEVENS: “The Killers have been a consistent bunch, but tonight they have their work cut out for them when they face Matt Kluge and Ross Rydell.” “Phoniex” by Stratovarius now echoes through the crowd as the fans pop loud as out from the backstage area comes Ross Rydell and Matt Kluge, both looking pumped up for their match here tonight. Both nod and then slide into the ring after high fiving the fans. RILEY STEVENS: “It’s not often we see a team as on the same page as these two are.” They both get to their feet staring over at The Killers before striking a pose in the middle of the ring. Together they head to their corner where it’s decided Matt Kluge will start against Nelson Fielder. DING DING DING! The two immediately go in for a lock up where it’s clear that Kluge is the stronger of the competitors here. He slowly backs him up into one of the unoccupied corners where he almost immediately whips him right into the other one. Going full steam he nails Nelson with a Clothesline in the corner! The fans cheer as he goes up to the top rope as Fielder bounces out into the center of the ring. He jumps off going for a Missile Dropkick but at the last possible moment Nelson rolls out of the way sending Kluge into the mat hard! The fans are a little disappointed but are sent into even more when Fielder runs forward dropping down with a big dropkick to Matt as he’s trying to go up. He goes for a cover 1…Not even close to enough to putt Mr. Kluge away. Slowly Fielder lifts him up to his feet, where he plots his next move, before he can do a thing however Kluge flips out and ends up behind him where he gives him a big time Backbreaker! The fans are clearly on the Strong Styles Soldiers side as Matt pulls up Nelson and whips him over into Ross’s corner. Running forward he delivers an elbow then tags in Ross who jumps into the ring. Nelson slowly stumbles back into Matt’s grasps as he lifts him up and the two combine for a ‘Strong Style Shootout’ as Matt hits a German Suplex and Ross hits a Leg Lariat! Ross goes for a cover after a great tag move 1…2…Kick Out. To a lot of people’s surprise Nelson is able to kick out. Rydell calmly lifts him to his feet but out of no where Nelson delivers a drop kick that sends Ross tumbling to the mat. RILEY STEVENS: “Here’s a chance for a tag and a fresh partner.” Fielder bounces back and tags in Brody just as Ross is recovering. Brody comes in like a man on a mission, swinging with a huge clothesline. However he swings and misses as Brody instead runs right into the ropes. On the rebound he walks right into a Roaring Elbow from Rydell that sends the fans into a frenzy as he hops up slapping his chest, kicking Brody in the chest and setting up for the finish. It appears as though we are just a ‘New York Minute’ away from the end but a desperate Nelson jumps into the ring and crushes Ross in the back breaking up the move. Rydell holds his back in pain as Brody sets him up for his own move now, what looks to be a big time DDT! Just at the last moment, Rydell is able to get a jolt of power as he basically tackles the big Brody into his corner where Matt Kluge tags in. Instead of going for the man we think he will, he runs across the ring crushing Fielder with a Elbow as Rydell holds Harper into the middle of the ring. Together they lift him up and finish him off with the ‘Dojo Breaker’ a Two Man Muscle Buster! The fans go wild as Kluge makes the cover as Rydell goes to make sure Nelson won’t break it up 1…2…3! Just like that it’s over. RILEY STEVENS: “Not a question about it; Rydell and Kluge win. This is good for momentum and with the tear Rydell has been on of late. Last week he injured Chris Jericho and put him on a long leave, now he’ll have a shot at the ‘GIW North Beach Championship’, we’ll see who will be joining him in just a little bit.” The two stand in the ring, holding each other’s arms up as Kluge leans over pretending to put a title around Rydell’s waist as the crowd cheers them all the way to the next segment where we head to the back. WINNER: STRONG STYLE SOLDIERS [Ross Rydell & Matt Kluge] This time we are taken to the office of Blake Beckett himself as he sits behind his desk flipping through paper work, the door opens and in walks La’Sarah Audric. LA’SARAH AUDRIC: “If it isn’t my Boss, Blake Beckett. It’s been awhile since you’ve been here in person, was starting to think you didn’t like us.” Blake looks up at her, giving her a clearly fake laugh though his mind seems to be on a completely different thing. BLAKE BECKETT: “I’ve been trying to piece some things together. I mean we’ve been ignoring some attacks, many of which were never accounted for. For starters Jake Avery back at ‘Exile’ and then Wake Walker just a few weeks back. The funny thing is, suspiciously the same days Security tapes are all gone. You and Garrett didn’t do any follow up attacks did you?” La’Sarah shakes her head no, while Blake rubs his chin seeming to be very interested in all of this. LA’SARAH AUDRIC: “I mean it was more then likely one of the guys in the back. Everybody can make a name for themselves by attacking Walker. I’ll go ask around…” La’Sarah seems to be heading towards the door but Blake makes sure to stop her. BLAKE BECKETT: “Stop. That still doesn’t explain the lack of security tapes. I don’t want this getting out ok? I’ll figure it out.” Audric nods her head back at Blake, always willingly listening to the top dog as she heads out for other business as Blake flips through some papers as we head to the ring. QUOTE “Silly Boy Blue” by David Bowie immediately echoes through the arena as the unfamiliar yet cheered Alioth Starre walks out from the back. Scrawny in stature this man made his debut in impressive fashion on ETV in a match that caught the eyes of several including apparently management as he high fives the fans and then jumps up into the ring, rolling through the ropes and into the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “This man debuted last week and already he has a big opportunity here in this match, where the winner will join Ross Rydell in a three way for the ‘GIW North Beach Championship’.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “The guy’s a tooth pick.” The fans finish cheering him as “Under the Knife” by Rise Against now hits as the fans now turn their reaction to mostly boos though it’s clear the fans have garnered a new respect for the man as he walks out from the back. He walks out not seeming worried about them, instead seeming focused on the task at hand. RILEY STEVENS: “This man was put through a table last week, but not with out putting on a show. He’s a maniac that is for sure.” Hister walks up the steps and then into the ring, staring across the ring at Alioth both stars completely focused as the referee calls for the bell starting this Qualifier. DING DING DING! These two seem evenly matched though Hister has a slight size advantage at least in the weight department. But instead of going for a lock up, the two surprise everyone when they engage in what can only be considered chaos as they exchange punches! The fans are going wild loving it as both men, are giving it to each other. Finally it’s Marcus that grabs him using his strength to end this punching war as he whips him into the corner. Hister goes at him, crushing him with a Spear into the turnbuckle. The fans cheer as Alioth falls down in the corner. Backing up it appears Hister has something in mind as he runs forward nailing him with a big boot to the face that nearly rips Starre’s face off. Unable to pin him, he pulls him up to his feet and away from the ropes where he now decides to whip him off the ropes. As he comes back Alioth ducks under the incoming clothesline then runs off the ropes coming back and catching him with a big dropkick to the chest. The fans cheer as Starre hops up getting pumped up after starting this match slow. Hister gets to his feet slowly where he’s caught with a big kick to the knee, which Starre follows up with a Spinning Neckbreaker! The fans are going wild as Alioth has turned this thing around completely. Marcus now seems to be completely shook up as he gets up, having to use the ropes just to do so. Alioth runs at him, full steam but this ends up a huge mistake as Hister recovers at the last possible moment and then gives him a push over the ropes sending Alioth airborne and landing on the outside, right on his back and hard. RILEY STEVENS: “Alioth may be seriously hurt here!” Alioth cringes down on the ground, kicking around while holding his back in obvious pain. In the ring Hister seems to get an idea, as he walks over to the corner jumping up onto the top rope. The fans watch as he sets up for it, as Alioth remains down and out inside the ring. Suddenly Marcus goes for the Moonsault! The fans go wild as time seems to almost slow down as Hister comes down but sensing danger Starre bails out of the way and it’s Marcus that comes down this time landing extremely hard! RILEY STEVENS: “HOLY SHIT!” Riley’s words are almost synonymous with how the rest of the crowd feels like as Marcus holds his chest, very clearly struggling for air. Realizing that winning is the only option Starre grabs him and tosses him back into the ring. He slides into the ring with him as Hister, gets to his feet still holding his chest seeming to be trying to salvage any air he can. Starre won’t give him any time as he throws a big haymaker of a punch! The fans are into this one fully as Marcus ducks the punch. He goes for a Superkick, but Alioth is able to catch it. He throws the leg away and then rolls him up 1…2…Kick Out! Hister throws him off and both men scramble to their feet. Forward they come into a lock up as Hister flips him around and does a German Suplex, with a Bridge 1…2…Kick Out! Both men again right back up as Hister throws a big Bicycle Kick trying to come out on top but he misses him and Alioth rolls him up 1…2…3! That’s it! RILEY STEVENS: “What a match!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Travesty, Hister had this one.” The fans go wild for Alioth who immediately slides out of the ring, seeming genuinely surprised he just won there to. Hister gets up immediately grabbing the referee by his collar screaming at him as Starre backs up the ramp. RILEY STEVENS: “This was a close one for sure, and just like that Alioth Starre has qualified himself for a title shot.” Starre holds up one arm, with a very big smile on his face. Hister has just given the referee a push as he heads to the back and we take a commercial break after a great match. WINNER: ALIOTH STARRE (Qualifies for GIW North Beach Title Shot) As we head to the backstage area we come right into the celebration of Ross Rydell and Matt Kluge both of which having some sort of liquid as Sarah Sharp comes onto the scene. SARAH SHARP: “Hi Ross, everyone is wondering just why you did what you did to Jericho last week? It was a very mean thing to do.” Sarah doesn’t seem happy always having been a big fan of Jericho as Ross chuckles to Kluge and answers. ROSS RYDELL: “You mean…you…were wondering? Cause as far as I have heard not a person in here gives a damn about Jericho. In fact I have gotten rave reviews for what I did to him last week, perhaps it’s time you start rooting for someone…you know talented.” Sarah seems like she wants to snap back at him but instead she actually does a good job and asks the question. SARAH SHARP: “Just moments ago Alioth Starre qualified for a North Beach title shot, plus Trent Arianna and The Fallen will fight next to qualify as well. Any thoughts on the match?” Ross nods his head, actually caring about this part. ROSS RYDELL: “Let’s face it I would rather be fighting for the Undisputed belt. But it looks as though I’ll be facing two new guys at ‘Revelation’ and unfortunately for them, that belts coming home with me. Now get.” Ross shoos her off as she angrily walks off as Ross returns to his little celebration as the show continues. QUOTE “Down” by Curse Icon now plays as the fans begin to give a mixed bag of reactions for the man coming out to the ring. Wearing a pair of sunglasses and a smirk it’s clear Trent Arianna has no shortage of confidence. Walking up the steps he looks out at the fans, with a grin as he steps into the ring slowly pulling off his shades and handing them to the outside. RILEY STEVENS: “Trent has made some waves around here. Though he lost twice last week he was praised for fighting twice and even more so praised for putting up a fight against Eric Sparrow.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I like the kid; he’s got ‘it’.” As he turns around “Ain't No Grave” by Johnny Cash now hits as the fans look to the ramp, not totally sure how to feel about The Fallen either. As he walks out from the back he does so to a very luminous song. Walking slowly it’s a rather uneventful entrance, but yet so telling just as well. RILEY STEVENS: “The Fallen debuted as well last year, losing. But he did put on quite a show in defeat. One of these two men will go on to face Ross Rydell and Alioth Starre for the belt.” The two men are squared off and it’s clear there is no love lost between these two as the referee starts the bell. DING DING DING! Trent comes forward pushing the pressure, locking up with The Fallen but quickly backing him up into the ropes. He has the strength advantage as he throws him off of them and towards the ropes. The momentum takes The Fallen off of them and then right back at him, the fans watch as Trent fakes a drop kick which causes Fallen to put on the breaks. But they boo as Arianna uses the chance to land a big eye poke. Fallen struggles around holding his eyes as Trent smirks while the referee scolds him for the move. Rather then seeming to be feeling any remorse, he grabs the currently blinded Fallen and then lifts him right up into a Snap Suplex! The fans don’t seem to have a preference but the eye poke seems to have sent them in Fallen’s favor. Regardless Trent is in total control as he hooks the leg 1…Kick Out! Trent argues with the referee to speed it up as he pulls The Fallen up by his head and whips him into the corner, where Trent slowly stalks him into the corner. Having him cornered he shows his head strength as he delivers a few Headbutt’s to The Fallen in the corner, something he fittingly calls ‘The Ram’. The fans in the arena are starting to think this one’s going to be over before it even started as Trent lifts The Fallen up from his seated position and then drags him to the center of the ring where he seems to be calling to end it. Lifting him up he takes him right into the ‘Strung Out’ a Hangman’s DDT! That is it, it has to be. Trent makes the cover and counts with him 1…2…Kick Out! The fans and Trent are both equally shocked as he gets up screaming at the referee telling him to do his job. He turns his attention back to The Fallen whom he lifts up trying something else, instead The Fallen counters with an Enzuigiri out of no where! The fans having taken his side burst into cheers. RILEY STEVENS: “The first life from The Fallen in this one, can he make a come back?” The Fallen gets to his feet, seeming to have rejuvenated himself out of seemingly no where. The fans in the arena are willing him on as Trent comes back to his feet seeming more surprised then hurt. Trent takes a big time swing at him only to be caught with a Hip Toss instead! The Fallen takes a few steps backwards stepping through the ropes as he prepares to go Springboard! Off he jumps with a big time Dropkick that catches Trent just as he’s back to his feet for maximum effectiveness. He goes for a cover 1…2…Kick Out! Trent gets to his feet, holding his stomach. The Fallen, does a Flip going for his ‘Crestfallen’ finish but Trent is able to bail out of the way as he’s doing the flip. Instead The Fallen lands and catches a big kick to the face by Trent who recovers from a bit of a beating. Both men seem drained as Trent stumbles back as The Fallen gets up slowly. RILEY STEVENS: “Both men have taken a beating here tonight, both seem drained.” Running on fumes both sides come forward throwing bombs. Trent throws his all into a punch that connects; The Fallen stumbles back then throws one of his own. Trent comes back for one of his own but The Fallen out of no where gets a surge of energy as he hits just Neckbreaker portion of the ‘Crestfallen’ and rather then really pinning him, he just ends up on top 1…2…3! The Fallen qualifies. RILEY STEVENS: “These qualifiers have been insane, two great contests in a row!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “There were so many slow counts by the referee it’s not even funny. Trent and Marcus got screwed here tonight!” The fans clap both men’s performance as The Fallen gets up quietly heads to the back, happy with his defeat but knowing this was just one half of the goal. RILEY STEVENS: “So the match is set; Ross Rydell, Alioth Starre, and The Fallen for the ‘GIW North Beach Championship’. Crazy.” In the ring Trent has come to, slamming his hands down onto the mat in anger. The Fallen heads to the back as we are taken to another commercial break. WINNER: THE FALLEN (Qualifies for GIW North Beach Title Shot) After the break we are taken to the back where Aspen Chaud is standing there along side Marcus Hister who is still holding his stomach from earlier in the night. They find Trent Arianna who has just made it to the back as she begins to talk. ASPEN CHAUD: “Are you guys ok? I heard that The Shadow’s in the building tonight. I need you guys, please, please.” Aspen seems worried as Marcus and Trent both answer her. MARCUS HISTER: “I’ll be there.” TRENT ARIANNA: “Yeah don’t worry, I’ll be there.” Aspen’s smile widens as she realizes she’ll have her friends with her tonight. As she gives both kisses on the cheek before walking through them and heading towards the ring for her match. QUOTE “Now I'm That Bitch” by Livvi Franc hits as the fans begin to boo as out from the back comes Aspen Chaud with a big smirk on as the fans show no remorse booing the hell out of her. As she walks towards the ring she does a small princess wave as she walks up the steps, heading across the apron before stepping over the bottom rope and into the ring, heading towards her corner. RILEY STEVENS: “It’s been awhile since we’ve heard Aspen booed like that.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Fickle fans, that’s all they are.” "The Human Stain" by Kamelot now hits as Aspen finishes as she glares up at the ramp as the fans boo the entrance of Tony Miranda whom stares down at Aspen, the tension in the air as he walks down towards the ring storming up the steps and into the ring where he walks right across talking a bit of trash to Aspen as things are heating up. RILEY STEVENS: “How in the world can these two co-exist?” As they continue to bicker “Forever” by Drake, Kanye West, Lil Wayne, and Eminem now hits as the fans continue to boo, just almost a continuous stream of boos as Jak Hendricks walks out from the back, strolling towards the ring looking full of confidence as he walks up the steps scoffing at Aspen and Tony as they argue taking his place in the corner. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “There’s our next Undisputed Champion, Jak Hendricks!” Finally “Circles” by Incubus hits causing the fans to go wild with cheers as they cheer on the entrance of the champ! Out he comes holding his ‘Undisputed’ belt over his shoulder as he high fives the fans before jumping up the steps and then over the ropes into the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Sparrow has been on a tear, can he be stopped?” Sparrow flashes his belt, lowering it slowly and handing it to the referee who hands it out. Aspen agrees to let Tony start while Jak goes to the outside allowing Sparrow to start. DING DING DING! The two men begin to circle around one another all of the fans looking to the ring watching as two of GIW’s finest prepare for action. They go in for a hold but Tony out smarts him immediately taking his arm and spinning it behind his back. Tony tugs on it a bit, but amazingly Sparrow reverses and ends up spinning around behind Tony. However he chooses to dropkick him in the back sending him toppling into the ropes. The fans cheer as Sparrow runs off the ropes and then dropkicks him again sending him into the ropes. Innovative as usual is Eric as he hops up, calling for the fans to cheer as he lifts Miranda to his feet slowly tossing him off into his owner. Sparrow comes forward with a big elbow and then lifts him up onto the ropes, but just as he does Hendricks tags his back. Eric argues with him but the referee forces him out as Jak goes top rope, lifting Tony up and nailing him with a Superplex from the top rope! The crowd boos his actions as he gets up and rather then pinning, he walks across the ring to Sparrow grinning and throwing out his arms which brings more boos from the crowd. RILEY STEVENS: “How will this team work together when one half is trying to show off?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Did you just totally miss that Superplex? Biased.” Jak turns around turning his attention back to Tony walking over to him slowly grabbing him and lifting him up only for Tony to catch him with a big European Uppercut that he followed up with a Discus Clothesline! The fans are not sure who to root for at this point as Tony looks back at Aspen who is calling for a tag but he shakes his head not willing at all to tag in her. Hendricks slowly gets up perhaps regretting the trash talk he just decided to do. When he’s finally up he stumbles forward into Tony’s grasp, Tony prepares for a Suplex but Jak is able to roll over at the last second landing behind Tony as he pushes him up against the ropes then rolls him back into a cover 1...Kick Out! Jak’s momentum sends him forward right into Sparrow who makes the tag as Tony does just the same tagging in Aspen. Both of them come in full steam but it’s Sparrow that catches her off guard with a big kick to the stomach as he then grabs her and whips her into the corner. Running forward he lands a big ‘Pineapple Express’ a Corner Dropkick! That sends the fans into a frenzy of cheers. Sparrow hops up to his feet, pumped up. Aspen starts to stumble forward as Sparrow immediately steps out of the ring. Readying himself he goes for the ‘Air Sparrow’ a Springboard Clothesline. Aspen is able to duck down because of her height, but Sparrow lands on his feet. Rather then deal with this any more, Aspen dives and tags in an un-expecting Tony. Aspen rolls out of the ring as Tony gets in, staring at her. RILEY STEVENS: “Sparrow’s going nuts tonight.” Tony jumps in going after Eric, but he ends up running right into a big Calf Kick that takes Miranda to one knee, followed immediately by a ‘Chow Moda’ a Swift Kick to a Kneeling Opponent. Tony falls down as Eric makes a cover 1…2…Tony kicks out. The fans boo wanting Sparrow to get the win. None the less he gets to his feet, suddenly there is a loud murmur as from behind we see Jak Hendricks coming up behind Eric. Suddenly he slaps his back and kicks him in the gut setting up for a DDT. But instead Sparrow pushes forward tackling him out of the ring. As he turns around Tony tries to capitalize with a big kick but instead Eric ducks it. Actually trying to help Aspen jumps into the ring. Running at Sparrow she throws a punch but he again ducks it and this time Aspen’s fist hits Tony. Immediately they start to argue, as Sparrow backs up running forward and dropkicking both of them. Tony falls down and then Aspen falls down right on top of him. Right away Sparrow looks out at the crowd then jumps up to the top rope, looking out at them with a grin as he jumps off nailing both with a ‘Spinal Tap’ a Spiral Tap! The crowd goes wild as Aspen rolls out of the way and Sparrow covers Tony 1…2…3! RILEY STEVENS: “What a showing here tonight by Eric Sparrow took out all three men.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “That sucked.” The fans are going wild as he hops up to his feet, holding up his arms. BOOM! His lights go out as Jak Hendricks crushes him from behind with the ‘Undisputed’ title. The crowd goes silent as Jak stands over him with a big smirk. He stares down at them, glaring with a grin as he holds the belt up perhaps foreshadowing as we take a break. WINNERS: ERIC SPARROW & JAK HENDRICKS As the show comes back we find that Jak has left the ring and a few referee’s are helping Sparrow to the back. In the ring just recovering is both Tony Miranda and Aspen Chaud. Suddenly the crowd boos as out from the back comes Marcus Hister and Trent Arianna sliding into the ring. Immediately they hammer Tony down as the three start to go after him. RILEY STEVENS: “Here we have Aspen and her new allies Trent and Marcus beating down Tony.” Suddenly there are more murmurs as out from the back comes big ol’ Shadow heading towards the ring. He slides into the ring as all three of them slide out of the ring. Aspen ends up with a microphone as she backs up the ramp. ASPEN CHAUD: “I have some bad news for you all! Earlier tonight Justin Volt gave me word that his Doctor, didn’t clear him for our match at ‘Revelation’ so I am declaring that match cancelled!” The crowd boos, though they don’t really like any of them they still were looking forward to the TLC match. Tony and The Shadow listen from in the ring as suddenly standing behind Aspen, Marcus, and Trent is Blake Beckett. BLAKE BECKETT: “Oh come on Aspen, did you really think I was going to let you out of that match? You see I heard the news earlier. And originally I had different plans but if Trent Arianna wants to get involved then it’ll be Perfect Combination versus Aspen Chaud and Trent Arianna…And the belts are still on the line!” The crowd pops with that announcement as Aspen seems stunned by the news. Tony and Shadow nod their heads in the ring as the final Revolt before ‘Revelation’ comes to an end with the two new teams fighting for the belts revealed.
