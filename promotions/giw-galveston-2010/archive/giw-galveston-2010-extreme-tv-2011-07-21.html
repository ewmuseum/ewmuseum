GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: ETV LIVE! From North Beach – July 21st, 2011 QUOTE Opener In the opener it was the debuting Jack Rackham going head to head with local wrestler Mark “The Bulldozer” Bridges. It was a very hyped up match right away the fans turned on Bridges in favor of Jack. And when the match started, their choice was rewarded when Rackham showed Mark all of his talents. Using most of his arsenal in this singles match the match mostly featured Bridges trying to get out of harms way. Unfortunately for him the end came when Jack finished him with ‘The Rack’ a Cutthroat Argentine Backbreaker Rack that made Mark tap out right away and left the fans very impressed with the man. WINNER: JACK RACKHAM QUOTE Mid-Card Match Shades of last week came in this match as size began to overcome Tesoro in the early going again. He kept focusing on the back of Tesoro with some big power moves like Spinebuster’s and back body drops. Crysis pulled Tesoro up from the hair and gripped his neck with one hand as he signaled the end. He lifted the man up into the air, but Tesoro reversed it into a quick arm drag that sent Crysis flying outside the ring. Crysis tries to shake off the cobwebs as Tesoro comes running in with a diving body press. Crysis catches him in midair and runs him into the steel post. He backs up and does it again before dropping him to the ground. Crysis rolls back into the ring as the referee continues the count from five. Tesoro begins to stir at the count of eight and moves up the apron to get back in the ring, but he makes it back at the count of eleven making him the loser in this match. WINNER: CRYSIS QUOTE Main Event The main event was something the fans were very excited about. Mostly because The Killers and Ninja Strike Force two teams that had found their place in ETV were set to compete. However this time they were joined by Azriel and Demon Night a duo better known as Faces of Death. The fans were as usual behind Ninja Strike Force however when the match started it was not ever close. Though The Killers and Ninja Strike Force were the GIW Veterans it was clear the Faces of Death had teamed before. In a very impressive ending together they nailed Jamal with a ‘Forced Dreams’ a Hip toss into a Neckbreaker that put him down. Night proceeded to throw Nelson out onto Brody then they put Colby right into a ‘Nightmare’ a Double Falling Dream to end the match, and put them firmly on the mat. WINNERS: FACES OF DEATH [Azriel & Demon Night]
