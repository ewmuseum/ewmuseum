GIW — Card

Monday Night Affliction, 17 January 2011

17 January 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: MONDAY NIGHT AFFLICTION! LIVE FROM THE RIVERSIDE CAFÉ IN NEW ORLEANS, LOUISIANA! BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! The GIW logo flashes across the screen, and then we are quickly taken backstage where we see the view of a camera filming the ground and feet running in and out of the picture. EMMANUEL RUSSO: “Welcome to Affliction everyone, we have no idea what is going on. Just bear with us for a few moments while we sort this out.” A police siren is heard in the distance. The camera man, audibly wheezing, pulls his camera up; showing the viewers the scene. There are police cars, an ambulance, and Affliction Co-GM Garrett Holloway running around in a frenzy asking questions to all of the EMTs. GARRETT HOLLOWAY: “Who did this? Why? Where is he now? WHAT’S GOING ON!?!” The camera man moves around behind the walls set up behind the police and begins to film inside a circle of people who are staring in one place. He places the camera inside, and we see the bloody body of Megathreat. Several EMT’s are working on applying the neck brace and getting him on a stretcher; blood is pouring from three scratch marks to his head and a huge laceration on his jaw. EMT: “Get out of here! Can’t you see this is a crime scene?” The camera slowly pans out and then quickly turns away to see Garrett Holloway who is staring down at the ground. The cameraman rushes over there to see what is going on. The camera pans down to see a group of white feathers lying on the ground, spelling out “You’re Next Holloway”. Garrett calls a cop over and, his face white as a ghost’s, points down to the ground. We flash back to Russo and McKendry at the booth. EMMANUEL RUSSO: “Well, ladies and gentlemen, we are sorry you had to see that. I wonder what happened.” COLIN MCKENDRY: “I guess this means Holloway is in charge? God, I hope Threat recovers. He was looking forward to taking Affliction back to the top.” EMMANUEL RUSSO: “Well, we all were, but now we are without our fearless leader.” COLIN MCKENDRY: “So, what now…? Threat was supposed to open the show…” EMMANUEL RUSSO: “Let’s cut to a quick commercial break while we regroup here in New Orleans. When we come back, we will have the invitational battle royal and some answers, hopefully.” = = = = = = = = = = = = A COMMERCIAL PROMOTING GIW EXILE IS SHOWN. EXILE WILL BE A CROSS BRAND EVENT ON FEBRUARY 13TH, WITH THE GIW TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIPS BEING THE HEADLING BOUT. = = = = = = = = = = = = When we return from the commercial break, we are backstage in the Affliction interview lounge where Malissa Wiggins is standing with man in a black suit and dark sunglasses. MALISSA WIGGINS: “Welcome back to Affliction. I’m Malissa Wiggins standing by with Martin Garcia, a spokesperson for the New Orleans Police Department; and Martin, what can you tell us about what just transpired.” MARTIN GARCIA: “Malissa, at this moment all I can divulge is that Megathreat, also known as Adam Kennedy, was attacked by an assailant approximately twenty minutes before Affliction went on air this evening. He was found by an intern who will remain unnamed for the time being. We will let you know more when I am told it is OK to talk to you; I plan on having an update before the end of the program.” MALISSA WIGGINS: “What about the feathers, what is their purpose?” MARTIN GARCIA: “Oh yes, well, all we know about them is that they come from some type of Owl. Look, I have to run; I’ll get back with you later, alright?” MALISSA WIGGINS: “Thanks for stopping by.” The camera flashes backstage where Bobby Bower and CDE are watching a TV in the locker room area. Bobby shakes his head and begins to speak. BOBBY BOWER: “It’s a shame that someone would do that to another human.” CDE: “It really is. Kind of puts a damper on the night doesn’t it? It’s supposed to be special, the reemergence of Monday Night Affliction, now it is tainted with blood.” Suddenly, Scott Reave comes into view. He walks right in between Bower and CDE and smirks at both of them, before directing his attention to the TV. Without moving his eyes, he begins to speak. SCOTT REAVE: “Ingenious, isn’t it?” Bower and Erns seem perplexed. CDE: “How do you mean?” Scott Reave laughs again and pats both of them on the back. SCOTT REAVE: “It’s some goon from Revolt; is it really that hard to figure out? They are trying to take us out, bring us down, and make us fail. Best way to do that is take out the man in charge. That’s just what they did. Oh, and good luck tonight Erns, you are going to need it.” With that, Reave exits the camera view, leaving Bower and CDE shaking their heads again. The camera cuts to ringside, where the New Orleans Invitational Battle Royal is set to begin. = = = = = = = = = = = = “My Hero” by The Foo Fighters plays over the GIW PA System, and the fans let loose a mix of cheers and boo as Mike Bryant enters the ring. COLIN MCKENDRY: “I’ll tell you what Emmanuel… this guy looks legit.” After the crowd dies a little bit, “Get Ready to Fly” by GRITS begins to play, and Nate Amazing rushes down to the ring. EMMANUEL RUSSO: “It is a risky move by Nate to go ahead and enter this match; I wonder how Dolo feels about this!” DING! DING! DING! Nate Amazing wastes no time going on offense, he goes for a legs weep that brings Bryant down to the ground. Nate runs to get momentum off of the ropes, but when he gets back to Mike, he is met by a quick elbow to the head. Bryant peels Amazing up and connects with a textbook sidewalk slam. He goes for a pin quickly, but Amazing has no trouble kicking out. Bryant pulls Nate up again and tries to apply a bear hug, but Nate uses his amazing flexibility and speed to counter it. Nate whips Bryant down to the mat using some sort of ninja looking move, then drops his leg right across Bryant’s neck. 1…2… KICKOUT BY BRYANT! Amazing slugs a few more fists down to Bryant’s face before hopping up to the top rope. Nate Amazing taunts the crowd… he leans back… HE GOES FOR THE CORKSCREW 450 SPLASH! HE LANDS ON HIS BACK! MIKE BRYANT ROLLED AWAY! Bryant slowly gets up and leans back in the corner. He is taunting Nate now, just begging for him to get up. Nate is motionless, the ref checks on him. Nate slowly begins to rise up. He turns around only to find Bryant running towards him, HE IS GOING FOR THE CARPE DIEM! A RUNNING LARIAT! NATE QUICKLY DUCKS! Mike’s momentum sends him into the corner, but he catches himself. Nate quickly turns back around and begins running towards Mike Bryant. Bryant doesn’t have much time to react, but he manages to hit… THE AURORA BOREALIS! A SUPERKICK THAT HITS NATE TIGHT ON THE JAW! EMMANUEL RUSSO: “Wicked move there by Mike Bryant!” Bryant laughs and falls on top of Nate Amazing, who is now bleeding from his mouth. 1…2…3! MIKE BRYANT HAS WON THE MATCH, AND WILL FACE BOBBY BOWER FOR THE NOX TITLE LATER ON! WINNER: MIKE BRYANT = = = = = = = = = = = = Once more we cut backstage where Madison Seton, Mark Cross, and a random intern are watching the television screen. MARK CROSS: “At least the perp will be punished, that’s always good to know.” MADISON SETON: “I just don’t understand why Megathreat of all people would be attacked. It doesn’t really make sense. Everyone I know likes that guy.” MARK CROSS: “Well, whoever it is wasn’t just in it to hurt Megathreat. I think they wanted to hurt Affliction more. You remember all of those feathers? Holloway is next.” MADISON SETON: “Wait. The feathers… Feathers… oh my god! Could it be…?” Mark Cross and the intern look up at Seton. MADISON SETON: “Eric Sparrow?” Cross’ and the intern’s eyes widen, but after a few moments, Cross rolls his eyes. MARK CROSS: “No, it was a bunch of owl feathers.” MADISON SETON: “I doubt that the NOPD actually has a feather analyst on site. I’d like to get them tested.” INTERN: “Good luck with that one Madison. Ha!” The intern and Cross exit, leaving Seton alone in the hallway. Suddenly, Eric Sparrow enters the shot. He gets up in Madison’s face; she lets out a muffled scream. ERIC SPARROW: “You think it is me, huh? Well, just to let you know little lady, I was much too busy examining you to attack any old men in parking lots. With that, Madison, I depart.” Sparrow lets her go and retreats back into the shadows, leaving Seton trembling. We cut back to ringside for the triple tag match. = = = = = = = = = = = = [font=cambria] Los Firemen, Dolo and Amazing, and High Voltage are already in the ring. AJ Lightning and Jose are targeting Dolo in the corner. AJ is landing left and rights while Jose has his foot lodged in Dolo’s neck. The ref orders both men off, and they both back off. Lightning seizes the moment though, and connects with a clothesline to the back of Jose’s head. AJ tags in Justin Volt, who goes straight for Dolo. He locks in a grapevine sleeper hold, Dolo is fading fast! Nate Amazing, who’s lip is still swollen from earlier in the evening, forces Volt off of his partner. The referee sends Nate back to the outside, and while Justin Volt is distracted Jose connects with a dropkick that sends him flying out of the ring! COLIN MCKENDRY: “Killer move there by Jose, perfect placement!” Jose whips Dolo into the corner, and connects with a stinger splash! He doesn’t waste time and goes for his finisher on Dolo, but Dolo counters it and sends Jose down to the mat with a DDT. Dolo tags in Amazing. Amazing heads straight towards Jose, but from the top rope he is hit with a crossbody from Justin Volt! EMMANUEL RUSSO: “Volt came from nowhere!” Volt pulls Jose up from the mat and connects with his finisher… THE JUSTIN CONCUSSION! He goes for a pin…1…2…DOLO BREAKS IT UP! Jose slides to the outside, holding his neck; Jos B goes to check on his partner. Dolo and Nate start stomping on Justin Volt, the ref regains control and sends Dolo back out. Nate goes back to work on Volt. Nate sets Volt up for a reverse DDT, HE SPIKES HIM RIGHT ON HIS HEAD! EMMANUEL RUSSO: “BRUTAL! I think he is out cold!” AJ smiles and tags in Dolo. Justin Volt isn’t moving. Dolo slowly pulls Volt up by his trunks and connects with a delayed vertical suplex that sends chills up the spines of the audience! He pulls Justin up by his trunks once more… this time with even more brutal intentions. He grabs his neck with one arm and supports his limp body with the other. A CHOKESLAM! EMMANUEL RUSSO: “Oh come on ref, he can’t compete!” Dolo goes for the pin, but before the ref can even get on the ground AJ Lightning had broken it up. AJ gets a few shots in on Dolo before retreating back to his corner. Dolo goes to talk more smack to AJ… BUT JUSTIN WAS PLAYING OPOSSUM! HE GOES FOR A QUICK ROLLUP…1…2….3!!! WINNERS: HIGH VOLTAGE = = = = = = = = = = = = We cut backstage where Jacoby Jackson is standing with Malissa Wiggins in the GIW Affliction in the interview lounge. MALISSA WIGGINS: “Welcome back to the interview lounge, I’m here with Jacoby Jackson, a man that is in the running for the Deep South Heavyweight Championship later on in the night. Tell me, Jacoby, what is going through your mind tonight before the match?” JACOBY JACKSON: “You know, I’m really looking forward to this match. Now, I am not on a hot streak like I was heading into Genesis, where I ended up losing in match that never really went my way. Tonight, if I get to a good start, I don’t think there is anyone stopping me.” Eric Sparrow once again enters the camera’s view from the shadows. ERIC SPARROW: “Look, it is very nice to see kids like you with moxie… but you have to be rational. You are stepping into the ring with me; you’re a lost cause. Maybe tonight, I’ll just end you?” Jacoby smirks back at Sparrow and jokingly nods his head. JACOBY JACKSON: “Look man, I’m not going to lie to myself and say that I don’t think you can compete with me. You can… but the X factor is that you keep prowling around the arena trying to play mind games. They may work on a teenage girl, but I am a little bit more emotionally stable. Try as you might, but you can’t shake me.” Sparrow turns around and tries to LAND A PUNCH ON JACKSON, but Jackson catches him in mid swing; just holding his fist in the air. JACOBY JACKSON: “You can’t shake me.” Jacoby lets Sparrow’s fist drop as the scene fades to commercial. = = = = = = = = = = = = A COMMERCIAL HYPING THIS WEEKS EPISODE OF REVOLT IS SHOWN; HYPING LA’SARAH AUDRIC AS THE NEW GENERAL MANAGER OF REVOLT. WHAT WILL SHE HAVE IN STORE FOR REVOLT THIS THURSDAY? TUNE IN TO FIND OUT! = = = = = = = = = = = = We return from commercial break to show Garrett Holloway observing the crime scene. He is looking at the feathers again. Malissa Wiggins rushes onto the scene with a microphone in her hand. MALISSA WIGGINS: “Mr. Holloway, what do you make of these threats?” Garrett continues staring at the mound of feathers. He chuckles. GARRETT HOLLOWAY: “I think they are shallow. I’d like to see them try to harm me.” Garrett kicks the feathers away. We cut over to Emanuel Russo and Colin McKendry at ringside. EMMANUEL RUSSO: “Ladies and gentlemen, we have a report on Megathreat from the hospital. He is said to be in stable condition, but likely has a concussion and will need quite a few stiches.” COLIN MCKENDRY: “Sounds to me like he came out ok, Megathreat will be here on Affliction next week, and it is then when he will announce who the assailants were, and maybe what their purpose was.” EMMANUEL RUSSO: “But now it is time for the NO-X championship match! It should be a good one.” = = = = = = = = = = = = Bobby Bower and Mike Bryant are locked up in the ring, Bower spins Bryant around and hits a perfect German Suplex, sending Bryant across the ring. Bower climbs the top rope and goes for some kind of flip, but when Bryant rolls out of the way ends up on his back. Bryant wastes no time, he locks in a full nelson and Bower is squirming around in his arms. The connects with a full Nelson slam on bower that shakes the ring. He goes for a pin…1…2….KICKOUT! Bower is pulled up by Bryant and hit with a double A spine buster. Bryant puts Bower in a chin lock before pulling him up by his neck. He whips Bobby into the corner and hits him with a stiff clothesline. Bryant goes back into the other corner, setting up for his lariat. He charges toward Bower, but it is ducked! Much like last time, Bryant tries to hit the superkick, but Bower ducks this too and dropkicks Bryant out of the ring. EMMANUEL RUSSO: “Sweet move there by Bobby Bower!” Bower climbs the top rope, and he connects with a double stomp to Bryant’s chest outside of the ring! The fans are going wild! Bower pushes Bryant back into the ring and goes for a pinfall…1…2… KICKOUT BY BRYANT! Bower pulls Bryant up and tosses him into the corner. Bobby gets momentum to go for his patented corner dropkick… but as soon as he arrives within range… BRYANT CONNECTS WITH THE SUPERKICK! 1…2…3! COLIN MCKENDRY: “Out of nowhere! Out of nowhere! Twice tonight it has came out of nowhere and we now have an NOX Champion!” WINNER: MIKE BRYANT (NEW CHAMPION) = = = = = = = = = = = = We cut backstage where, in their locker room, High Voltage is celebrating a victory. Justin Volt and Elizabeth Reeve are getting very comfortable together on the couch and AJ Lightning is sharing a bottle of champagne with Amanda Henry. There is a knock on the door, and Henry goes to answer it. To their surprise, it is Nate Amazing standing in the doorway. He looks at them with tears in his eyes as he holds an icepack on his shoulder with one hand and holds his stomach with another. NATE AMAZING: “Hello…” Amanda Henry giggles to herself as she observes a beaten Nate Amazing. AMANDA HENRY: “How can I help you, little man?” Henry, Volt, Lightning, and Reeve all laugh together. Nate doesn’t blink. NATE AMAZING: “I want to join you. I want to become a member of High Voltage.” High Voltage goes silent. Henry raises an eyebrow. AMANDA HENRY: “I think we have it all taken care of, just the four of us. No thanks.” Henry goes to shut the door, but Nate grabs it just before it closes. NATE AMAZING: “Give me a shot… please.” Amanda rolls her eyes. AMANDA HENRY: “Fine; next week it will be Nate Amazing vs. Our Choice. If you win, you are in.” Nate smiles and begins to leave. NATE AMAZING: “I won’t let you down.” We cut back to Garrett Holloway’s office, where he is sitting behind his desk, staring directly at the camera man. We can hear a producer count down before Holloway begins to speak. GARRETT HOLLOWAY: “Ladies and gentlemen, after the events that transpired at the start of the broadcast this evening, I’d like to formally announce that I am stepping up into the role of the main management position on Affliction. I feel that having a sense of stability is best at this point for the show. I am leading an investigation along with the NOPD to find the culprit of this vile attack. I assure you that the attacker or attackers will not be left unpunished. That’s all for now, enjoy the rest of the show.” We head back to the ring. = = = = = = = = = = = = The match begins and both Cross and Jackson set their sights on Scott Reeve who just laughs and slinks into a corner. Mark Cross acts fast and charges Reave, but CDE grabs him by his bad arm and Cross falls to the mat in agony. Reave is laughing as the ref checks on Cross, who is moaning on the matt. Jackson sees a spot and takes it, he brings Reave to the ground with a hiptoss and keeps him there with a chinlock. CDE tries to lock in a dragon sleeper, but Reave overpowers him and hits him with a closes fist to the face. Reave is up, and is setting up for the shining wizard, but Cross uses his good arm to trip him up and bring him down. Cross rises from the mat and uses a quick judo kick to the head to knock CDE back down. Cross attempts to pull Reave up, but Reave simply knocks his good arm away. Reave is on offense now, putting his bad arm in an armbar that sends Cross into a frenzy. CDE breaks it up with a dropkick to Reave’s back, but Cross lands once more on that arm! CDE sees an opportunity, he locks in a sitting hammer lock on Cross who is wailing in pain. Using his lower body, Cross powers up to a verticle position and hip tosses CDE down to the mat. He goes a for a no hook pin, CDE has no issue kicking out. Reave is up now and connects with a lariat that brings down CDE. He attempts to lariat Cross, but Cross is much to sturdy and strong. Cross uses his good arm to land punches to the jaw of Scott Reave. Reave is backed up into a corner now. Cross lifts Reave up onto the top rope and then pulls him off a bit, HE IS GOING FOR A DDT! EMMANUEL RUSSO: “This guy is still elite even with one arm!” HE CONNECTS! HE GOES FOR THE PINFALL! 1…2…. CDE BREAKS UP THE PIN! CDE starts stomping away on Cross’s arm. He locks in an armbar again… but Cross reaches the rope. CDE breaks the hold turns his attention to Scott Reave. He walks over towards Reave… AND FINDS HIMSELF IN A ROLLUP! 1…2…KICK OUT IN TIME! COLIN MCKENDRY: “Scott Reave is quick, watch out.” Reave pops up quickly and locks in his finishing move, the VOODO LOCK ON CDE! Cross is there to break it up quickly! Cross stomps a few times on Reave’s chest and then, uncharacteristically, climbs up the top rope. Cross stalls for a moment, and CDE is there to kick him off outside of the ring! Reave is in the corner… waiting on CDE to turn around. When CDE does… Reave charges and LOCKS IN THE VOODO LOCK! CDE IS STRUGGLING! HE TAPS! WINNER: SCOTT REAVE = = = = = = = = = = = = After the match is over and the crowd dies a bit, Scott Reave exits the ring and looks at Mark Cross, who is leaning against the ring and holding his arm while staring Reave down. Reave begins to mock Mark using the title belt taunt when a familiar face’s ring music begins to play. It is Alexii Ravynova. She enters the arena and stands at the base of the ramp clapping her hands. ALEXII RAVYNOVA: “Very good Scott! Very good indeed. You put on a great show tonight, but I fear I have an announcement that you won’t like very much.” Reave looks up at Alexii confused. ALEXII RAVYNOVA: “Now, a super show ago… at On Hallowed Ground… Mark Cross helped me defeat that whore Amanda Rowland and Blake Beckett. I told him I owed him a favor, and then I was forced to tend to my ailing cousin, I was unable to be here… and unable to grant him a favor. But now, with the attack of Megathreat at hand and Affliction without a co-GM… I got the call to return to Galveston Island Wrestling. I will be the new co-General Manager of Affliction!” The crowd boos, Reave still doesn’t understand what is happening. ALEXII RAVYNOVA: “Now is as good a time as ever to help Mark Cross out, right? Next week, it will be Scott Reave vs. the winner of the next match… but there is a twist! Mark Cross will be added into the hunt!” Cross leans his head back and laughs, the crowd cheers, and Reave is having a tantrum as we cut to commercial. A COMMERCIAL HYPING THE GIW RECAP SHOW LIVE: FROM NORTH BEACH IS SHOWN. GARRETT HOLLOWAY AND LA’SARAH AUDRIC WILL JOIN COLT COX AND AMANDA ROWLAND! = = = = = = = = = = = = DING DING DING! What a match this is going to be as we prepare for the three way main event. But right away Sparrow comes to the forefront looking back and forth between the two at both of his sides He begins to bad mouth each of them telling both guys he’ll be walking out with the title. Finally Seton walks forward slamming him with a fist he bounces back into Jackson who gives him a fist of his own. Boom he walks back into another one from Seton then another from Jackson that knocks him to the mat. He gets up and for the moment these two are on the same side. Jackson and Seton line him up and whip him off the ropes. On the return both men hit a double clothesline. As he hops back up both grab him and prepare for a Double Suplex. But as they are about to lift him up Jackson rolls off and when Seton gets him in the air he kicks Seton in the gut sending both to the mat hard. The fans boo as Jackson pulled a fast one on Seton there. Starr is down and out right now while Seton gets to her feet slowly with the wind knocked out of her. As she is getting to his feet Jackson runs up grabbing the back of her head and slamming to his knee. Momentarily stunned Jackson finished the job with a big haymaker. Turning around and realizing Sparrow is to his hands and knees Jackson runs across the ring hitting a big knee that sends Sparrow reeling. Both men are down as Jackson is rolling in the match thus far. With both men holding there heads he walks to the center of the ring beginning to taunt the crowd who naturally boo him. While he is doing so both Sparrow and Seton begin to get to there feet. Jackson opts to go after Sparrow calling for him to get up. As he does so he stumbles right into Jackson who locks him up and hits the a Tiger Bomb and the referee makes the call 1…2…Seton kicks him off. Sparrow rolls out of the way as Seton waits for Jackson to get up calling for a move behind him. Wake goes for a big clothesline but Jackson ducks it and hits the dropkick that knocks Seton clean out of the ring. Jackson is literally rolling tonight having not really even been touched yet. EMMANUEL RUSSO: That’s a champion right there! Jackson smirks looking out at Seton whom is out of the ring holding her nose after the kick. Behind him is Eric Sparrow getting up after the last Tiger Bomb. Looking a bit stunned Jacoby does not see him. Eric comes from behind and connects with a Low Blow. The fans boo as Jacoby folds over to the ring and Sparrow goes for the cover 1…Kick Out. Barely a one count as he kicks out. Jacoby gets up angry after taking a beating. Getting to his feet he realizes Seton is getting up onto the ring apron. Already angry he runs off the ropes and dives for a Spear. But Seton narrowly dodges it sending Eric to out side with very little helping his fall. Seton looks back at the fallen Sparrow who is slowly getting up. Jackson however gets up quickly running at Seton and clotheslining her off the Ring Apron down to the mat next to Eric. Not wasting any time he slides out of the ring to get to them. Angrily he starts to clear off the announce table as Russo and McKendry back away still calling the match regardless. With it cleared off Jackson grabs up Sparrow and tosses him onto the announce table. Now looking at Seton he goes to do the same but as he gets him to his feet Seton knees him in the gut and hits a big suplex. Realizing what Jackson had planned for him she realizes it may not be such a bad idea. As she lifts Jackson and sets him onto the announce table. Looking at them then into the ring the fans all get to there feet realizing they are about to see some crazy stuff. Seton slowly gets into the ring looking out at the two on the table as he heads to the top rope. Both men are not moving at all, as Seton gets to the top rope. Not one generally to fly she sets up as the fans are dead quiet as she leaps off of the top rope at the men on the table. At the last possible second Sparrow rolls out of the way but Jackson isn’t so lucky as Seton hits a double leg drop that crushes him through the table. The crowd starts to chant ‘GIW’ over and over again as Jackson is literally out cold, Sparrow was only able to get out of the way narrowly, and Seton may have just hurt herself just as much as Jackson. Sparrow picks Seton up and throws her into the ring. He goes for a pin...1...2...KICKOUT! Sparrow pushes the referee into the corner and begins yelling at him. Seton is rising behind him. Sparrow turns around and finds himself locked in the FREEZING RAIN! HE TAPS! WINNER: MADISON SETON = = = = = = = = = = = = GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING: DARE TO DEFY!
