GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: THURSDAY NIGHT REVOLT LIVE! From Porretto Beach – July 4, 2013 BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! The fireworks stream into the night sky as a crowd of over 800 people are lines around the Revolt ring for this special holiday edition of Revolt: Red, White, and Bruised. All of the tourists that were visiting the beach for their holiday vacation had made their way to the GIW ring, and they were a raucous crowd to be sure. RILEY STEVENS: “Welcome to Red, White, and Bruised ladies and gentlemen, and happy Independence day! Tonight we kick off the Wildcard tag team tournament.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “There are some very interesting pairings tonight here Riley, but there is one looming question: how will they all be able to work together.” RILEY STEVENS: “The team that can get on the same page the fastest is going to be the team that comes out on top. Six teams are going to advance to the tournament this week. Then there will be three advancing after next week, and it will all culminate with a three team ladder match on the 18th to crown a new tag team champion. This comes straight from the office of the new GM Sarah Sharp, who has been promoted now that Alex is back on the active roster.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Well, thats a big match for her first one as GM!” RILEY STEVENS: “Let's waste no time tonight. This edition of Revolt is going to be jam packed with all of your favorite GIW stars. Lets head to the ring!” QUOTE Returning from break after the opening segment of Revolt we return to the ring, where Brody Harper and Jacob Jett are already standing in the ring waiting for there opponents to arrive “Alive” by Krewella hits and the fans give mixed reactions as Justin Harmony and Miss Jade enter from the back with big grins on there faces as they head to the ring, a random team of two relatively new GIW stars heading to the ring. They may not know each other too well but they are both smart enough to realize that this tag team tournament gives them a chance to really make waves here in GIW. RILEY STEVENS: “We have two new stars here in Harmony and Jade teaming up to try to win the Tag Belts and more importantly make names for themselves in this one.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “But they are facing Harper, a tag team specialist!” The duo reaches the bottom and Harmony allows Jade to head up first, the two trying hard to make this work now as Jade enters the ring with her usual peppiness as Harmony sits on the outside, letting her start this one off as the referee gets in the middle and starts the match. These fans are shocked as Harper rushes over at Harmony on the side of the ring, clotheslining him off as well.
