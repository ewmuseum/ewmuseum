GIW — Card

Revolt, 23 June 2011

23 June 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: THURSDAY NIGHT REVOLT BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! As we start the show the opening pyro goes off as we pan around the crowd here in the Tremont Hill. The number of people here are naturally lower however the amount of people watching at home is just as high. This show is known as ‘The Challengers Show’ made for people not on the ‘Category 5’ card so that they could impress here in GIW. As we head to the ring we see Drakken Sangre, Herman Bludge, and Leonard Jackson standing ready to go as we cut over to the announce table with Riley and Oliver. RILEY STEVENS: “It sure should be a terrific night of matches here tonight with four contests all of which were made so that those in the matches can impress. Starting with this triple threat match we then move on to a Six Pack Challenge, then Ninja Strike Force versus The Killers then the huge main event between two HUGE prospects Nick Riot and Unforgettable Jones. We are certainly going to get a look at who might be the future here in GIW huh Oliver?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “If by future you mean whose heading towards the unemployment line you’d be right.” Heading into the ring we see the match nearly ready to get underway when suddenly "Now I'm That Bitch" by Livvi Franc hits as the crowd begins to go wild with boos. Walking out from the back wearing a little black dress and glasses looking rather professional is Aspen Chaud following not far behind her is The Transcendence members Damien Dunn, Adam Syndal, and Marcus Hister. RILEY STEVENS: “What is this woman doing out here? She has no business.” They come wearing their wrestling gear as Aspen begins to lead them towards the ring. Aspen, heads up into the ring with her guys following her. The three men in the ring seem a bit confused, as Aspen looks around then nods her head and suddenly each man picks a target and starts to go after them. Hister wastes absolutely no time ripping Sangre’s head off with a monstrous Superkick that literally puts Sangre down. Dunn corners Bludge while Syndal kicks Jackson in the gut runs off the ropes and then hits an ‘In Syndication’ a Running Inverted Spinning Neckbreaker! Leonard flops around in pain as finally Dunn kicks Bludge in the gut and puts him down with a ‘Complete Devastation’ his version of the Pedigree! RILEY STEVENS: “This is just wrong.” Slowly Dunn kicks Bludge out of the ring as Aspen walks to the side of the ring asking for a microphone. A few referee’s run down to get the attacked stars all while Aspen grabs her mic and walks to the center of the ring with it. ASPEN CHAUD: “Your welcome for saving you all from five minutes of pure embarrassment. I came here with The Transcendence because I have an announcement to make. Earlier today, I filed my lawsuit against Blake Beckett and this company for harassment! Which means that sooner rather then later this company is going to fold because my lawyers are the best this world has seen…Thanks daddy. So, I want Blake to come out here right now and let’s see what his thoughts are about selling me the company.” Quickly the crowd boos her but soon begin to cheer as “Demon Eyes” by The Answer goes through the speakers. Out from the back comes Blake Beckett, with a rather serious look on his face as he heads down towards the ring the fans cheering him on, hoping for him to handle the Aspen situation. Walking down towards the ring he doesn’t look near as confident as he steps into the ring, looking around at The Transcendence who currently fill the ring with a mic of his own he begins to speak. BLAKE BECKETT: “Listen Aspen you can’t just go around filing a lawsuit whenever you feel like it. You do realize that if this place goes under you don’t have a job, right? Just like you a lot of people in the back won’t have jobs either. So for once think about someone else before you rush and make a decision.” Aspen looks around at her team, all of which are standing there very seriously making this even worse for Blake as she begins to bust up laughing showing little signs of any heart for those people. ASPEN CHAUD: “REALLY? You think I give a damn about anybody back there in the back? You must be delusional. And in fact you sound a little scared Blake…You must have realized that the hot shot lawyers you have working for you here in GIW didn’t even go to law school…Right? So, before things get any worse hand me over this company and I’ll drop the suit against you and you can make a few dollars on your way out.” Aspen grins lowering the microphone seeming rather confident knowing she’s flanked with three guys here tonight the crowd boos the idea as Beckett seems to mull it over. BLAKE BECKETT: “Aspen there is no way in hell I am ever going to sell this company to you.” The crowd goes wild as Blake lowers the mic however he somberly raises it back up bearing bad news. BLAKE BECKETT: “However…I am willing to give you something so that you’ll drop the lawsuit.” Seeming to realize that the suit will end up bad for him he lowers the microphone the crowd looking around at one another shocked as Aspen looks around at her team with a big smirk. ASPEN CHAUD: “I want you to name me the new General Manager of Revolt!” Aspen seems giddy with a big grin on her face as the crowd in attendance boos her outlandish demands but Blake raises the mic and nods his head. BLAKE BECKETT: “It’s done.” The crowd can be seen muttering “What? to one another in the crowd as even Aspen’s jaw drops realizing that all of this worked as she looks at her team with a big smile on her, biting down on her lip as we cut to the announce table. RILEY STEVENS: “This can’t be happening...” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “YES! Haha! Finally someone intelligent in charge.” RILEY STEVENS: “Tonight was supposed to be a night where we looked at new talent but I think the thing most will take from this show is the fact that we have a new Co-General Manager of Revolt. Wow…stay tuned up next.” Riley seems bewildered as Oliver seems just as excited as Aspen in the ring who we slowly fade to black on as we head to a commercial. QUOTE Returning to the ring we find ourselves looking at six superstars all of which are crowding the ring; some standing out more then others. RILEY STEVENS: “Going to be hard calling the rest of the show knowing Aspen’s in charge but in the ring we have six men all vying to impress.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Trying to impress Aspen? Haha this is wonderful.” Standing in the center of them all catching the crowd’s eye if Paul Montuori who stares out at them as the bell begins to the ring. DING DING DING! The five other stars begin to look at him, wondering what he is doing standing in the center of the ring. Quickly it’s Young that walks up to him, seeming to be a bit confused but out of no where Paul kicks him in the gut then lands a quick ‘Eye Opener’ Implant DDT! The crowd seems shocked as anyone as Young rolls over to the side as Paul’s attacked from behind by Jacoby Jackson. Haydyn rolls out of the ring in pain as we focus on Paul while Wayans and Galen fight off in the other corner and Heavens hides off in her own corner. Jumping up Paul lands with an Enzuigiri to the face of Jackson that drops him to the mat. Heading straight to the top rope the fans seem to be impressed by him as he jumps off landing with a Frog Splash! The crowd actually begins to cheer him not knowing much about him yet but starting to like his arsenal. He covers Jacoby 1…2…Wayans runs over and kicks him off. Wayans who had won the fight with Galen waits for him to get up then throws a wild right that misses. Montuori ducks under and then lands a T-Bone Suplex! That sends the crowd into more cheers for the new guy. Paul gets up kicking Wayans out of the ring as last but not least, Galen steps up from the corner running full steam at Paul. Montuori dodges it at the last moment sending him face first into the turnbuckle. He falls down to the mat, as Paul shows little respect as he drops a big knee to his face. Looking out at the crowd he grins as heads up to the top rope, where he jumps off and lands a beautiful ‘Up In Smoke’ a Shooting Star Press! The crowd goes wild as he covers him, 1…2…Surprisingly it’s Heavens who’d been in the corner the whole time gingerly kicking him off. Paul gets up to his feet, slowly stalking her as she backs up into the corner. Stopping he grins then taps his lips proposing a kiss will make it all go away. Suddenly she slaps him across the face; he holds his cheek then crushes her in the gut and delivers another ‘Eye Opener’ an Implant DDT! Getting on top of her, he makes the cover 1…2…3! That is it in convincing fashion. Paul gets up to his feet, the referee raises his hand but he pulls it away quickly as he walks over to Heavens looking around at the crowd before spitting in her face. The crowd begins to boo as Paul slips out of the ring. JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner… Paul Montuori!” Walking over to the fans he begins to badmouth them as suddenly walking out from the back comes La’Sarah Audric on the top of the stage. She motions to the back as out comes Unforgettable Jones! The crowd seems a little shocked as Paul, looks up at him seeming ready to fight. However as Jones walks out from the back he first meets Young with a big ‘Unforgettable Suplex’ a Belly to Belly Suplex that he literally sends Haydyn airborne with. After disposing of him he heads on to Jackson who is met with an ‘Unforgettable Attack’ a Clothesline from Hell! Again Jacoby is out cold as Jones then meets up with Jordan Wayans who drops to his knees begging for mercy, only for Jones to grab him and put him down making it look easy with a ‘Unforgettable Crush’ a Dragon Suplex! The crowd looks on in amazement for many of them this is the first time they’ve really seen the man. Unfortunately for Galen yet again he’s the last man up to bat which means he’s the exclamation point as he lifts him up and puts him down with a ‘Unforgettable Bomb’ his version of the Awesome Bomb right outside of the ring on the barely cushioned area. Looking around we head up to see La’Sarah clapping him on, looking over at Paul…Jones begins to head back up the stage leaving many confused. RILEY STEVENS: “Wow what an ugly scene that was. Those men had just gotten through being embarrassed by Paul Montuori and to add insult to injury…or rather injury on top of injury Unforgettable Jones led by La’Sarah Audric came out and man handled these men. We have to take a break…” Jones heads to the back with La’Sarah trailing him as Montuori looks around confused as we head to a break. WINNER: PAUL MONTUORI Heading to the back we see Colby Hunter and Jamal Mitchell standing together facing one another almost seeming to be in a battle. COLBY HUNTER: “Oh ok here I got one! Aspen’s butt is so big…small objects orbit around it!” Jamal begins to crack up laughing as does Colby, as Jamal shakes his head and then stops and says one of his own. JAMAL MITCHELL: “Aspen’s butt is so big…I told her to haul ass and she had to make two trips! Haha!” The two again begin to crack up as Colby nods his head and then struggles through the laughter as Jamal goes still. COLBY HUNTER: “Aspen’s butt is so big…” Jamal points behind him as Aspen stands there, staring at them both looking none to pleased with them at all. Colby slowly turns his eyes wide. ASPEN CHAUD: “You two want to be comedians huh? Well you know what I like to stay serious. So, since I am in charge now if you lose your match tonight You are Fired!” Aspen shakes her head, unable to believe what she just walked in on as she heads off on her way. JAMAL MITCHELL: “It looks a lot smaller in person!” Jamal says this as the two then start to head towards the ring as Colby grins and nudges him. COLBY HUNTER: “Anyway her butt is so big…she sat on a Nintendo GameCube and it became a GameBoy!” Hunter and Mitchell let out a big laugh not seeming overly worried about losing their match as we take another commercial break, during which we hype up the feud between Wake Walker and Matt Kluge and their match at ‘Category 5’. QUOTE Heading back to the ring we are again met with the familiar scene of seeing Ninja Strike Force across the ring from The Killers both of which seem ready to go, though it still appears as though Jamal and Colby are way too relaxed for this. RILEY STEVENS: “With the tag belts currently needing challengers these two teams will be vying for a shot.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Fairly certain Matt Kluge could beat either of these teams…by himself.” DING DING DING! The match is underway and immediately we find that Brody Harper will start for his team while Jamal Mitchell will start for his. The two begin to spin around the ring finally going for a lock up. As soon as their hands are grasped Harper tosses Mitchell back showing off his brute strength. Mitchell gets up nodding his head the fans certainly behind him as he runs forward dropping Brody with a big kick to the knee. Bouncing off the ropes he goes but runs right into a huge clothesline from Brody Harper! The crowd boos as he gets up grinning then dragging him over to the corner setting him up. Grinning he looks around then runs off the ropes and nails him with a Running Boot! Jamal seems to be out of it a bit as he gets up, walking into a big punch from Brody. Harper drags him over to his corner then tags in Nelson who jumps through the ropes and drops him with a kick. Stopping to show boat Nelson holds up his arms, as he then goes for a DDT. But Jamal reverses out of it and lands a big kick to the stomach followed up by a big Enzuigiri that drops him. Wanting to make a tag Jamal, heads towards his corner. Brody wants a tag but Nelson decides to try to stop Jamal but he’s too late. Colby jumps over the ropes and lands a Springboard Clothesline to Nelson! The fans go wild as he gets up and lets out a scream for them to get up. They oblige cheering him on but to everyone’s disliking Brody steps into the ring to save his partner but instead Jamal jumps in and they hit a Double Dropkick! The crowd goes wild as Brody rolls out then they back up and deliver a ‘Shell Shock’ a Double Superkick to Nelson. Jamal rolls out of the ring as Colby covers 1…2…3! JAMAL JACKSON: “Here are your winners the team of Colby Hunter and Jamal Mitchell, NINJA STRIKE FORCE!” Colby gets up, holding up his arms as Jamal returns to the ring holding up his own as well as the two get to keep their jobs. RILEY STEVENS: “Ninja Strike Force keep their jobs and impress here on Revolt.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Damn.” The two continue to celebrate as we head from this to the backstage area. WINNERS: NINJA STRIKE FORCE [Colby Hunter & Jamal Mitchell] As we hit the backstage area we find La’Sarah Audric sitting in her office looking through stuff as the door opens and Blake Beckett walks in. BLAKE BECKETT: “What was that earlier?” La’Sarah chuckles to herself a little, she expected a little bit of confusion from most people. LA’SARAH AUDRIC: “Oh just helping one of our new talents is all.” Blake looks at her confused but shakes his head anyways and then sits down in her chair rather casually. BLAKE BECKETT: “I need to talk with someone about this whole Aspen situation.” The door opens and then in walks big bad Unforgettable Jones. La’Sarah gets to her feet, walking towards him. LA’SARAH AUDRIC: “We’ll talk about it later Blake.” Beckett shakes his head as La’Sarah heads off with Jones as Blake shakes his head seeming to be unable to live with the fact she now has some power here in GIW. QUOTE It’s now time for the main event and while the fans may not have been sold on the match earlier, what we saw from Unforgettable Jones earlier made this match a lot more intriguing. “Undead” by Hollywood Undead hits through the speakers as out from the back comes Nick Riot. Most of the fans are still new to him as he walks out, immediately we begin to see his cocky nature which turns the fans against him. RILEY STEVENS: “Nick Riot this young man seems promising that’s for sure. Faces a big challenge here tonight, a win should put him in the lime light.” Heading up the steps he shows little reason to think he’s scared as he enters the ring and throws up a taunt as “Many Men” by 50 Cent hits as the crowd begins to boo as the camera is sure to get a look at him from the bottom to the top as the big man Unforgettable Jones walks out onto the stage followed not far behind by La’Sarah Audric. RILEY STEVENS: “This is one big man…Still not completely sure what La’Sarah Audric is doing out here with him.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Isn’t it obvious, she’s just helping the man out.” Jones heads down towards the ring with Audric on his tail clapping him on. Wearing shades he looks menacing, heading up the steps he goes then stepping through the ropes as La’Sarah stands at ringside looking up at him. Riot does not seem worried at all, instead just stands across from him with confidence. DING DING DING! Jones is an intimidating presence in the ring but really Riot shows no signs of backing down from him. Running at him, he runs right into a big shoulder from Jones. Wasting no time Nick hops up to his feet then tries to knock him off his feet with a big Clothesline but it doesn’t do a whole lot more then stagger him. Nodding his head as Jones grins he runs off the ropes and expecting him to go up high Jones, waits only to get a kick to the knee dropping him down to one knee. The fans are not sure who to root for in this. Some just rooting for Jones due to his size others rooting for Riot for being faced with this big obstacle. With him downed to one knee, Riot grabs him only for Jones to show his strength pushing him away with one arm rather easily. Unforgettable gets right back up going to grab him but Riot being the quicker man ducks out of the way. Going up high Nick jumps up for a Dropkick that sends Jones into the ropes which catch him. Running at him he nails him with a Clothesline but it doesn’t do a whole lot. Clearly a little bit out of it Jones stands there still and Nick steps back then runs going for another Clothesline this time using so much force that both men flip over the ropes and to the outside both men landing fairly hard. The crowd goes wild for the move not thinking that Nick had it in him. Getting up he returns to the ring, taunting the crowd which quickly gets them back off his bandwagon. RILEY STEVENS: “Thus far Nick Riot having a great showing here.” La’Sarah runs over to Jones, urging him to get back up. He gets up and she yells at him to go get him as Jones gets up walking up the steps at the count of five, as heads into the ring. Not even letting him get a foot into the ring Riot begins to throw hay makers on Jones. Angrily Unforgettable tosses him away, so that he can get fully into the ring. But to everyone’s surprise Riot rears back then delivers a Superkick! That sends Jones to the mat 1…2…Kick Out, Jones literally throws him off. Getting up holding his chin, feeling the effects of that kick Unforgettable is slow to get up. Riot runs at him only for Jones to grab him and toss him into the corner. Being so big Jones traps him and begins to go wild with the ‘Unforgettable Hammer’ a series of hooks and punches from all angles that clearly stun Nick. The referee backs him up, because Nick is in the ropes. Riot comes out seeming a bit off from the attack heads right into an ‘Unforgettable Suplex’ a Belly to Belly Suplex! Getting on top of him he makes the cover 1…2…Kick Out! This surprises many including Jones who pulls him to his feet. Feeling the effects of the move, Riot stands still long enough for Jones to head off the ropes then nail him with the ‘Unforgettable Attack’ a Clothesline from Hell! The camera cuts to La’Sarah who is heading up the steps to celebrate as Jones covers him 1…2…Kick Out! RILEY STEVENS: “Wow I really thought that may have been it.” La’Sarah slowly heads back down the steps, realizing this isn’t over. Jones now angry that he isn’t going away picks him up and throws him into the corner. Running at him he is caught off guard with a Double Knee Facebreaker from Riot! The crowd starts to get on their feet thinking this could be it. Going to the top rope, Nick is setting up for his move the ‘The Prediction’ a Diving Somersault Leg Drop! But before he can do it, La’Sarah hops up onto the ring apron next to him. This distracts Nick just long enough for Jones to get to his feet and catch Riot with a big haymaker. Riot slumps down and Jones picks him up off the top rope, setting up perfectly into the ‘Unforgettable Bomb’ his version of the Awesome Bomb into the center of the ring 1…2…3! JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner, Unforgettable Jones!” The crowd boos the ending of the match as La’Sarah heads up the steps and into the ring joining Jones, by raising up his hand in victory. RILEY STEVENS: “You know it’s a shame this match had to end in that fashion. These two men were having a very close back and forth battle, that was ruined by interference by La’Sarah Audric.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Stop being a baby.” In the ring the two continue to celebrate, before heading out of the ring and towards the back with one another as we head to the announce table. RILEY STEVENS: “That’s all the time we have for you this week on Revolt be sure to tune in this Sunday for ‘Category 5’ where Matt Kluge teaming with Tony Miranda and Mark Cross face off against Wake Walker and his team of Anthony Brenske and Mr. Black. Then we have the GIW Undisputed Championship match between Alioth Starre and challenger Trent Lost to determine once and for all who the better man really is. And through out the night we will be ready to determine the Sole Survivor and who will challenge for the Undisputed title at ‘Category 5’…WHO WILLL SURVIVE?” We take one more moment to watch as Jones and Audric celebrate before cutting to a hype video for ‘Category 5’ as the show slowly comes to a close. WINNER: UNFORGETTABLE JONES
