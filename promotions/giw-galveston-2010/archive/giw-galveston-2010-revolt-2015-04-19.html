Revolt, 19 April 2015

GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS SUNDAY NIGHT REVOLT LIVE! From Kauffman Arena in Galveston, TX – April 19th, 2015 BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! Three sets of fireworks stream into the night sky outside of the Kauffman Arena as this episode of Revolt is set to begin. The crowd of screaming GIW fans are on their feet as “Raised By Wolves”, Revolt’s official theme song, blares through the speakers in the arena. Signs such as “End of the Monarchy”, “CDE’s Nightmare”, and “Prime-Time” catch the viewer’s eye as the camera pans around the arena. The voices of our announce team for the evening, Sarah Sharp and Oliver Thibodaux, suddenly appear. SARAH SHARP: "Ladies and gentlemen, it is time for another edition of GIW’s flagship program, Sunday Night Revolt! We are coming to you live from the Kauffman Arena right here in Galveston, Texas, just two weeks away from our iPPV event, Exile!" OLIVER THIBODAUX: "That’s right Sarah, everything is beginning to fall into place as we prepare for what looks to be one hell of a night on May 3rd." SARAH SHARP: "We know that Exile will be held at Stewart Beach to accommodate what we expect to be a crowd too large for Kauffman Arena, and it will mark the first time we see GIW return to the beach since we signed our new television deal. It should be an awesome atmosphere for the action, don’t you think Ollie?" OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Oh man, I love it when we go to the beach. The crowd is hot, the babes are hot, and oh… what are you planning on wearing to the beach Sarah? Are you gonna call the matches in a bikini?" SARAH SHARP: "Don’t hold your breath. I’ll dress up, and you should dress up too, Ollie. It’s going to be a big night. We already know we will see Chris Callum defend his Deep South Championship against the member of the Main Event Monarchy that picks up the win in the five way match in the main event later tonight." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "So I should dress for a funeral? We all know the other members of the Main Event Monarchy are going to lay down for Alexis… that’s their job! And then finally I’m going to get to see Chris Callum meet his kryptonite – the GIW Hall of Famer, Alexis Terry." SARAH SHARP: "Whether or not the Monarchy intend to work together to ensure Terry’s title match remains to be seen Ollie. The Sir and Johnny Violence have outwardly stated that they don’t want this title chance to go to waste. Blade Vega hasn’t exactly been treated very well from Alexis Terry as of late, so he certainly has motivation to turn on his boss. And Bronx Valescence, though he seems loyal, has entered the Television Championship match every week has been eligible, so he certainly has gold on his mind. I wouldn’t be so sure this match doesn’t turn into a free for all." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "You just have no faith in Alexis and David’s motivational strategies!" SARAH SHARP: "Motivational strategies? You mean intimidation?" OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Shut up Sarah! It works!" “Did it on ‘em” hits the PA system as the fans begin to cheer for the Commissioner of Revolt, Aspen Chaud. She makes her way onto the ramp as the voices of the commentators return. SARAH SHARP: "Here comes Aspen Chaud, we were told before the show that she had some announcements to make regarding Exile, and the future of GIW after Exile. What do you think she has to say?" OLIVER THIBODAUX: "I think she is going to quit and hand the company over to Alexis Terry. That’s what she should do, anyway." As Aspen climbs into the ring, she waits little time after her music dies down to begin speaking. ASPEN CHAUD: "Good evening Galveston, Texas!" Massive cheers for the cheap pop. Aspen seems pleased with herself. ASPEN CHAUD: "Now that we have that out of the way, I will get to what I need to get to. I have several announcements that I need to make, and I think you will like what you hear. In two weeks, you all will head out to Stewart Beach as we prepare for Exile. Several matches have already been discussed, and now it is time to make them official." More cheers from the crowd. She pivots to play to another side of the arena. ASPEN CHAUD: "Tonight, we will see all five members of the Main Event Monarchy be forced to fight it out for a shot at Chris Callum’s Deep South Championship. The winner will face him at Exile, but you all knew that. What you didn’t know is that next week to open the show, Chris Callum and the number one contender will meet face to face to sign the contract for their match. As you know, contract signings frequently go wrong, so I will make sure that the other members of the Main Event Monarchy are banned from ringside." Cheers – the fans can rest easy knowing that there will be no gang attack before Exile. ASPEN CHAUD: "But the Deep South Championship will not be the main event of Exile. Instead, we will see the culmination of the long war between Michael Lynch and Christopher Darren Erns. These two GIW legends and future Hall of Famers will meet for the final time at Exile in a two out of three falls match. The loser will be gone from GIW for good, so that the winner can continue here in peace." The crowd seems split on who to root for. With so much on the line, one fan favorite is going to be gone from the company at the end of the match. No one seems comfortable on how to feel. ASPEN CHAUD: "We have also announced that at Exile, the GIW Tag Team Championships will be returning as an open title belt. Meaning wrestlers from any promotion around the world may enter the gauntlet at Exile and take the titles back home with them if they are victorious. Teams from GIW will be entered into the match – Mo’ Love, Team Relativity, The Starks, Bro and Sawdust, and The THUNDERMASHERS… but the rest of the entrants are still very much up in the air. We may see GIW legends return in the match, we may see new faces… but either way, a new set GIW Tag Team Champions will leave Exile with gold around their waists." “GIW” chants ring through the arena as the fans anticipate wrestlers from other promotions making an appearance. ASPEN CHAUD: "Also – we will see Gavin Achilles get his rematch with Gary Ford at Exile. You all were here or watching at home when Gary Ford showed Gavin Achilles why he has managed to stay in the business for so long, but since then Gavin has continued to improve. Gary Ford said he could beat Gavin Achilles with his arm tied behind his back, and we are going to put that to the test at Exile." The camera cuts away for a moment, only to zoom in on a “Hashtag Gunslinger” sign in the audience. ASPEN CHAUD: "I am also proud to announce that on May 24th, Galveston Island Wrestling will go on the road to San Antonio, Texas for a Revolt Supershow that will broadcast live right here on Fox Sports Southwest. San Antonio has been one of our most loyal markets since the company was founded in 2010, and fans will file into the Freeman Coliseum to watch Revolt go Extreme for one night only. Extreme TV returns to GIW, and we will see Chris Callum go one on with a contender of my choosing with the Undisputed title on the line in a hardcore match." The fans cheer loudly, though they do not know who the contender will be. ASPEN CHAUD: "I have a man in mind, but it is on a conditional level only. The man in mind has defeated a former Undisputed Champion in Christopher Darren Erns, was moments away from winning Seize the Moment, and has made a huge impact since he re-signed with the company. That man is Johnny Violence – a man who is not afraid to go hardcore when he needs to. But like I said, this is a conditional booking, so Johnny, come on down to the ring so I can lay it all out on the table for you." Aspen turns to the ramp as silence and a few boos fill the arena. “Bat Country” hits the PA system and Johnny Violence begins to make his way down to the ring to a chorus of boos. He slides in through the bottom rope and grabs a microphone from the ring announcer, a look of confusion on his face. JOHNNY VIOLENCE: "Aspen, let me make something very clear to you right from the start. I want to be Undisputed Champion. I deserve to be Undisputed Champion. I would be Undisputed Champion if Christopher Darren Erns didn’t screw me out of winning Seize the Moment. I’ll gladly accept a title shot, especially against a little kid like Chris Callum in a hardcore match, but I refuse to be indebted to you. Whatever your offer is, it better not require me to beg, because I’ll take that title whether you see it coming or not. I don’t need your approval." Aspen chuckles, leaning back on her foot. ASPEN CHAUD: "If you want the match, Johnny, the match is yours. I’m not going to ask you to beg, I’m just going to ask that you take your job, as a wrestler, seriously. Tonight, Alexis Terry is expecting all of the members of the Main Event Monarchy to lay down for her. You are too good for that, Johnny. You are too close to the top to be selling yourself to Terry. Now I get why you think that joining the Monarchy will help you become Undisputed Champion, I get that Alexis Terry makes a lot of promises. But I can tell that you have your reservations..." Johnny Violence interrupts the commissioner – JOHNNY VIOLENCE: "Everyone in this company knows I have reservations. Alexis knows I have reservations. She and everyone else in the Monarchy calls me the wildcard, and I guess I am. I don’t like being told what to do. I work by myself, for myself. I have made it clear that I won’t stay with the Monarchy for long, but look at what has happened since I joined. I have a chance to become the number one contender for the Deep South Championship. I would say I made a good decision." Boos from the crowd. Aspen pauses a moment. ASPEN CHAUD: "I’m offering you a chance at the Undisputed Championship in a match that is uniquely suited for your skillset. I will put you on all of the posters, promote you like you already are the champion. This could be your breakout moment at Extreme TV, all you have to do is agree to my condition." Johnny clearly likes what he is hearing. He tilts his head. JOHNNY VIOLENCE: "And what, exactly, is that condition, Aspen?" Aspen smiles, moving the microphone to her other hand. ASPEN CHAUD: "I don’t want you to lay down for Alexis Terry tonight. I want you to fight her, and I want you to hurt her. I want you to show Alexis and everyone else in the Monarchy that I am the one they should be mindlessly following; that I control this company. I want you, win or lose, to show her that she does not control you. That’s it. You may have already decided that you don’t want to lay down for Terry, and if you have, that makes it all the easier to accept my condition. Or, maybe you planned to lay down. Now, I hope you give that idea a second thought." Violence looks around the arena as the fans chant “RISE ABOVE TERRY”, a term coined by Chris Callum. It would take a lot for the fans to be cheering on Johnny Violence, but Alexis Terry is just that hated. Violence turns back towards Aspen. JOHNNY VIOLENCE: "By the end of the night, you’ll know where I stand." He drops the mic as “Bat Country” resumes over the speakers. As he exits the ring, we see Aspen Chaud with a slick grin on her face, thinking that she has finally ruined the Main Event Monarchy. Violence stops at the top of the ramp to look at the crowd once again as we fade to commercial break. Ashley O'Ryan steps through the curtains with a smile across his face. The two stare each other down, Ashley realizing that Gaylord is tougher than he seems. SARAH SHARP: “Battling to make an impression here tonight.” Bronxy V is back into the ring but by unorthodox measures,he springboards from the apron diving on both men with a double clothesline! He covers Ashley but Gaylord is there to break it up. Bronx is on the attack, knocking Prime back down and pounding away at the back of Ashley as he tries to get up again. Quick snap DDT by Bronx and he follows it up by a quick jumping elbow drop. He covers Ashley but Ashley is able to get his foot on the bottom rope at two. Bronx drags Ashley to the middle of the ring and tries to a sharpshooter. He isn’t known for his submission skills but it’s obvious he is attempting everything tonight. Only after a few seconds, Ashley is able to escape it quickly, rolling up Bronx into a pinning predicament! 1.... 2.... Bronx kicks out at two and a half! SARAH SHARP: “He almost stole one!” Ashley. Gaylord. and Bronx are all up and even though they have a weird hatred for each other since last weeks television title open, they team up on Prime to hit a double snap suplex. Ashley shrugs it off and scales the turnbuckle. The crowd builds . He dives off with a beautiful diving senton but Bronx moves out of the way and O'Ryan crashes into the steel guardrail back first! The crowd erupts into a “Holy Shit” chant! SARAH SHARP: “Oh my god, that didn't look good.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: "That's the price you pay for going off the top rope." Bronx rolls slowly back into the ring while Ashley O'Ryan is barely moving on the outside. Bronx looks outside and sees a couple of ring side medical practitioners checking on O'Ryan, Bronx smiles, and turns but runs into a deafening chop by Gaylord Prime! The hit sends Bronx back into the ropes and as he comes back Gaylord hooks him into a school boy pin! 1... 2... 3!!! OLIVER THIBODAUX: "No way!" SARAH SHARP: "He does it two weeks in a row!" Both men are up with both of their jaws wide up open. Bronx is more surprised than anyone, Gaylord is more excited as he is handed his Television Title. Prime successfully defends this week! Bronx angrily slams the mat and rolls out of the ring. Gaylord heads to the turnbuckle to pose with the belt. Ashley O'Ryan is shown outside of the ring still being attended to by more medical personnel, not moving, we can see the worry in the eyes of the fans as we hear them calling for more help just before we fade into the back. Chuck drops one of Alexis Terry’s special brownies when he sees George. CHUCK LOVEMAN: "Whoa. Mo. He’s like… bigger than I thought. He’s like a… superhero." " Chuck shrugs. CHUCK LOVEMAN: "Well, sorry that we are the favorites. I guess we will stop trying to win as much." Mo offers out his hand. MO CHRONIC: "Hahaha. That’s a funny joke. Melissa points a finger at the duo. MELISSA STARK: "It doesn’t matter who decided to come to GIW from other companies to fight for the belts, George and I will stand tall at the end of the night, and pick up our first championships too." From far into cater, Brotree laughs as he indulges on the chicken. BROTREE HARPER: "Lol pls." From off screen, Gary ford enters the shot. GARY FORD: "Well I hate to interrupt this circus, but I need to talk to you George. Come with me." He leads George to the side, but Melissa, with an angry look on her face, stops him. MELISSA STARK: "I’m coming too." Ford shrugs. GARY FORD: "Whatever." The trio move around the corner as the camera follows them. We see the heads of Mo’ Love peeking around the corner. GEORGE STARK: "What do you want, Gary?" Gary speaks quietly. GARY FORD: "Call me Gunslinger. But anyway, I just saw that you have a match tonight with that one armed freak tonight. GARY FORD: "I’m not asking for a hit. I’m asking for you to put a rookie in his place. I shouldn’t have to ask." George turns to Melissa, then nods. GEORGE STARK: "I’ll rough him up. You realize if you screw over Duff and Ashley that they will never respect you, right?" Aspen smiles wide, followed by a quick laugh. ASPEN CHAUD: "I don’t need respect, I have authority."
