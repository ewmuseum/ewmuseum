GIW — Card

Revolt, 13 December 2011

13 December 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: TUESDAY NIGHT REVOLT LIVE! From Seawolf Park – December 13, 2011 The show starts up and this place has an electric atmosphere to it on this rare Tuesday Night broadcast of Revolt as the fans here know that things have changed, we’ve gone from it being Ace Baldwin’s show, to Tony Miranda’s the new ‘GIW Undisputed Champion’ of the world. "World Warrior" by RedRapper hits and the fans go wild not a single person not jumping out of their seats immediately as our new champion’s music hits. RILEY STEVENS: “What a night we have here, the official first day of the reign of Tony Miranda. I for one, love it as do these fans. As I am sure you do as well Oliver…” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “…” Tony walks out from the back the belt strapped over his shoulders as he looks around at the fans with a big grin on his face as he heads down towards the ring, the fans reaching out to touch the champion one fan showing off her “Tony Miranda, Our Champion” sign as he walks past it, with pride as he heads up the steps and into the ring where he is tossed a microphone. He walks to the center of the ring with it, looking out at the crowd as he goes to talk but then lowers the microphone seeming to almost be at a loss for words as they don’t let up with their cheers. RILEY STEVENS: “What a atmosphere we have here tonight.” Tony stares out at them nodding appreciatively as a Tony! Tony! Tony! chant breaks out in the crowd which only makes him smile bigger as the fans show their appreciating for the longtime GIW star. As he raises the mic they show their respect by quieting down as the chants trail off. TONY MIRANDA: “You know that really means a lot…I’ve been here for a long time. I’ve fought some of the best wrestlers this company has ever had. From the cheaters to the greats, all of which I fought my heart out against. For a long time I was labeled as the guy that would never win the big one, the guy that would always come up short. But now I stand before you, you’re new ‘GIW Undisputed Champion’!” The fans roar in approval as he pulls the title off his shoulder raising it proudly in the air as the fans again start up the chant before they are trailed off as "Did It On 'Em" by Nicki Minaj hits as the fans turn their attention to the top of the ramp booing as out walks Aspen Chaud wearing a red dress, fitting for her recent switch. She stops at the top of the ramp, not entering the ring with Tony. ASPEN CHAUD: “Gag! Can you say BORING? Haven’t we seen this story a million times before? I can turn on the TV and see a million underdog stories. But look what we have here; we have a real life Rocky in the flesh. The champion that came in there and defeated the champion Ace Baldwin…Oh wait…No you didn’t. Matter of fact myself and Ace were out of the ring while you pinned Johnny Violence. Right?” Aspen grins as she lowers the microphone the question clearly sarcastic as she awaits his answers the fans booing the heck out of her. TONY MIRANDA: “You know why you were out of the ring? Because I kicked you out of the ring you’re your new bestie was thrown out alongside you. So, it’s not my fault that the two of you were…out of it. By the way, I think you need a little more make up on your eye.” Aspen reaches up, feeling around her eye that we can still see the little outline of the black eye that she wasn’t able to cover up completely. ASPEN CHAUD: “Real funny Tony! You can make jokes, all you want. But the fact remains you didn’t beat Ace Baldwin…You didn’t even come close. All you did was pin the weak link in the match and stole the belt. So lets all give a round of applause to the ‘GIW Paper Champion’ Tony Miranda. Give it up people.” She starts to clap for him as the fans quickly boo her little snide remark as Tony paces around the ring shaking his head. TONY MIRANDA: “So let me put a few things together. At ‘Final Solution’ you jump in front of the kick thus saving Ace Baldwin then you ruin my moment after the match by attacking me with him. Now you are out here defending him…I think I’ve seen this show before, this is the one where Aspen Chaud hooks her wagon to a star because she can no longer do anything on her own. What is this, the eighth episode?” Tony grins as the fans have a good laugh alongside him as Aspen glares down at him shaking her head. ASPEN CHAUD: “You know Tony you can have your fun now. Go ahead and continue to call yourself a champion but at ‘Malice’ Ace Baldwin gets a rematch and this time you won’t have anyone to pin, anyone to fall back on. And we’ll find out who really is the best and trust me when I say it’ll be Ace Baldwin.” Aspen waves down at Tony as she turns to head to the back but before she goes Tony gets one more word in. TONY MIRANDA: “Hey deliver this message to your master…Tonight I am going to do what I should of at ‘Final Solution’ and that’s take him out!” Tony drops the mic as the fans go wild and Aspen storms backstage as we take a quick commercial break. QUOTE When we return to the ring we have two superstars already in the ring ready to go, the two Alex’s…Alex Young and Alexander Simpson who entered to a whole lot of nothing. "Push Push" by Kat Deluna now hits as the fans turn their attention up into the entrance way as Sherilynne Martinez enters as some fans who perhaps remember her clap others with hold judgment. RILEY STEVENS: “This Sherilynne Martinez is making her return to GIW after a long absence. Some might remember her for a lackluster early stint here in GIW, but she’s back to really show these fans the real her.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Jeez, another girl? Is catering really full?” Martinez heads down to the ring looking focused and determined to make a good impression as she enters the ring looking around at the fans nodding as she looks across at the two Alex’s who scoff at her. DING DING DING! As per usual the guys in the ring show little respect to the returning star as they mock her a bit, mostly her size. She calls for a lock up right off the bat and both Alex’s start to laugh as Simpson points to Young to take it. He moves forward with his arms up his as he locks up with her starting to move back but not expecting her to jump up and use his arms to propel her into a dropkick. Young falls back on his butt in the corner as Martinez gets to his feet as Simpson looks over at Young laughing at his effort as he now proposes the same as he now takes his turn locking up with her. This time she pulls off with one of his arms twisting it around as he pats it in pain, as she pulls it around his back using it only to jump up onto his back wrapping up his neck with a Rear Naked Choke as the fans seem to be getting on board, a little impressed with her. RILEY STEVENS: “Can she draw the early tap?” Sherilynne has it locked up tight as he seems to start fading, fighting it only causing him to seem to go out quicker. But before she can get the tap Young heads on over pulling her off from behind. He drops her to the mat releasing her trying to throw a clothesline but she smartly ducks under it then jumps up with a Pele Kick to the face! The fans go wild as this sends Young falling back into the corner holding his jaw as the little Latina turns her attention back to Simpson who gets up stumbling up recovering from the choke. But a quickly recovered Young sets up from the corner rushing at her going for a big boot but she rolls under it and he ends up crushing Simpson with it. Young turns right into a ‘Latina Pride’ a Roundhouse Kick that connects sending Alex through the ropes to the outside. Now even more so dazed then before Alexander gets up looking around confused as she finishes the job with a ‘Hot Fire’ a Sit-Out Facebuster, as the fans start to now cheer for her as she gets on top of him for the pin 1…2…3! JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…Sherilynne Martinez!” The fans cheer for her but they still seem a bit skeptical wondering if she can maintain this as she steps up the ladder. RILEY STEVENS: “Impressive return from Sherilynne, but how will she handle a big step up in competition next week here on Revolt.” The referee raises her hand as she exits the ring happy with her performance as she gives some fans high fives on the way out after putting herself on the map. WINNER: SHERILYNNE MARTINEZ When we head to the backstage area we find ourselves following Faith Simpson around as she walks toward a little area marked off as “Aspen Chaud’s Locker Room”. She knocks and then hears a voice telling her to come right in. She walks in and when she opens the door, she sees Ace Baldwin sitting in the area. FAITH SIMPSON: “Oh sorry, thought Aspen was here…” Faith turns to leave when Aspen enters in behind her, walking behind her with a grin heading into the locker room. ASPEN CHAUD: “Oh look if it isn’t Mr. Boring’s little trainee, the fifteen year old girl wonder.” Aspen smiles as she walks over to Ace handing him a water, as she opens up the one she got for herself. FAITH SIMPSON: “…Ummmm…I am sorry I should be going.” Faith now clearly uncomfortable turns to head out but is stopped by Aspen’s voice. ASPEN CHAUD: “What is the rush? Clearly you came by to tell me something.” Aspen takes a sip of the drink as Ace starts to open his as she turns back to them, nervous. FAITH SIMPSON: “I was coming by to thank you for what you did at ‘Final Solution’…” Aspen chuckles now understanding why she got so nervous as the situation now seems a bit interesting. ASPEN CHAUD: “Oh, that…Funny now isn’t it? You see I wasn’t trying to help you I just wanted to make sure Ace was prepared. Now if you’ll run along, we have to get ready for the Main Event…Good luck in your match though…Your starting to be just like Cross…A great mid-carder.” Aspen smirks as Ace chuckles a bit at the joke as Faith knows that this isn’t the time to get into a battle as she heads on out as Aspen and Ace laugh between each other as we head to the ring for the next match. QUOTE As we return to the ring we have Jason Star in the ring, none of his prior title reigns mattering at all to the fans who give him no reaction at all. His entrance theme cuts off as "Wayward One" by Alter Bridge hits and the fans go wild for the return of the former ‘GIW North Beach Champion’ Alex Castellanos who heads out from the back, with focus in his eyes as he strolls towards the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Isn’t it a great site to see the return of Alex Castellanos here tonight.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “People still care about this guy?” Castellanos heads up into the ring as Star proceeds to scream his accomplishments at him from across the ring as Alex looks at him ignoring the fact that Jason won a belt in Provision Wrestling Championships as the referee calls for the bell. DING DING DING! Star continues to run his mouth about that one time he won a belt before the company died out as Alex looks around at the crowd shaking his head as he continues to go on and on. Finally he backs up and then goes forward crushing him with a ‘Golden Arrow’ a Superkick that shuts his mouth and turns off the lights upstairs as Star falls backwards out cold as Alex walks forward shrugging as he makes the cover 1…2…3! JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…Alex Castellanos!” The fans cheer him for shutting up Star who the referee goes to check on as Alex immediately gets up and calls for a microphone from ringside as he’s handed it as he walks back to the center of the ring looking down at Star before talking into it. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “Wow, where do they find these guys? Regardless I am not here to fight guys like this whose hype clearly beats out there talent. I am here because yet again we find that the true Aspen Chaud is not the one I was friends with just a few months ago. You see for awhile there I bought into the little act she was putting on…For a second there I even considered her a friend…” Alex seems sick at just the thought as the fans boo the statement; clearly Aspen is not the most popular person here tonight. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “But we all realize her true colors now as she yet again has found someone willing to put up with her, for her to leach off of. I was that guy once now it appears its Ace’s turn. You see it was Ace that put me out these past few months with a concussion and now my so called friend went from promising to take him out to joining him…So now that I am healed up and ready to go…I am putting my sites on taking Aspen Chaud out once and for all!” Alex drops the microphone his message sent after the match as opposed to in it as he rolls out of the ring and heads towards the back as we take a commercial break with the fans cheering on his new goal. WINNER: ALEX CASTELLANOS As we return “Bat Country” by Avenged Sevenfold hits right away and the fans begin to boo the entrance of none other then Johnny Violence who heads out from the back towards the ring not stopping at all clearly having something on his mind as he heads up the steps. RILEY STEVENS: “This man came just that close to winning the belt at ‘Final Solution’ but I don’t think he lost any momentum after the loss.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “No way, he’s still one of the best.” Violence steps into the ring having picked up the mic that was laid out for him on his way in as the fans boos die down, as he looks out at them. JOHNNY VIOLENCE: “I’m not the type to come out here and bitch about my loss because I leave that to people like Ace and Aspen. No I am the type of guy that comes out here and admits like a man that Tony Miranda beat me at ‘Final Solution’. But I am also the type of guy that comes out here and tells you that its not going to happen again!” The fans seem to respect that but still their hatred for him causes them to boo him any how as he continues on. JOHNNY VIOLENCE: “Tonight I am going to team with that very same man, to fight the two cowards of GIW…Aspen and Ace in the Main Event. People continue to assume the two of us are going to have issues but let me be the first to tell you that when two people hate the other two people across the ring as much as he and I do…enemies become great teams quickly. So tonight the two of us are going to take out the trash.” This actually gets some cheers from the crowd, almost throwing the world out of tilt completely. JOHNNY VIOLENCE: “But after that I understand that Ace has the next shot with his rematch. Which means I am going to have to bide some time until I get my next shot. So what I plan on doing is beating up everyone until I am officially named…Number One Contender.” The fans actually don’t seem to completely hate the idea but soon “Cruel Melody” by Black Light Burns hits and the fans look up confused at the ramp as Altair walks out from the back heading to the ring with that same sinister look we’ve seen in him the past few weeks, Violence looks at him confused as he heads up the steps. RILEY STEVENS: “Altair a man who single handedly ended Trent Lost’s career at ‘Final Solution’ now enters the ring, for what reason?” The devilish man steps across from Violence in the ring with a microphone in his hand but before he can talk Violence cuts him off. JOHNNY VIOLENCE: “Let me guess, you are out here to tell me that you are going to be the Number One Contender?” Violence makes his prediction as Altair shakes his head quickly raising the mic up to his mouth. ALTAIR: “Quite the contrary. You peg me as someone that cares about the meaningless titles that currently are held in this company. No…no…no. As you may have seen what I did to Trent Lost this past week I am here to rid this place of stars that I don’t see fit. And as of now I have my eyes on one.” Altair stares across at Violence who stares back at him, some intensity forming in the air as Violence replies. JOHNNY VIOLENCE: “You want to put me to the test? You think you are just going to do what you did to Trent to me? Let me start by telling you that you don’t scare me, heck you’re about ten pounds soaking wet. But if you want to try to make me your next target, trust me when I say that I’ll be ready.” Violence drops the mic heading out of the ring as Altair remains ready for his match which is up next as we take a commercial break before the match. QUOTE With Altair already in the ring from the previous segment "Power On Me" by Katy B hits and the fans begin to cheer for the young ‘GIW Legacy Champion’ who walks out from the back with a big smile on her face, starting to get used to atmosphere as she walks out with the belt wrapped around her waist as she heads to the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Back a few weeks ago, Faith’s first big match she won due to the fact that Altair essentially left Trent Lost as slim pickings. Now with Trent, could Altair set his sites on Faith?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I hope so, she doesn’t belong here.” Faith heads up the steps and through the ropes into the ring as Altair grins from across the ring not seeming all that worried as Faith hands out her title before stretching out her arms as the referee steps between them and calls for the bell. DING DING DING! When it comes down to it, the one male in GIW that Faith wouldn’t give up much size to it would be Altair. Right away the two go in for a lock up and at first Faith actually starts to push him back but he straightens up and starts to push her back before spinning and whipping her off the ropes. Altair throws a big clothesline that Faith ducks under bouncing off the ropes and coming back at him but with ease Altair throws back a kick to her stomach. She doubles over and Altair shows off his smooth wrestling ability as he spins behind her and wraps her arms around into a Straight Jacket lifting her up then dropping her down face first on the mat, spinning her over into a cover 1…Kick Out. The feisty young champion will not go down that easy as she turns around starting to get up as Altair does the same with his very familiar expressionless face, as he stalks her from behind ready for his next move as he can’t wait walking forward and hoisting her to her feet and then spinning her around up into his arms then down to the mat with a Side Slam this time not even bothering with the pin attempt as he stalks around her. RILEY STEVENS: “Deep down, I think we all feared this kind of beating.” Faith crawls toward the ropes as Altair walks around her in the ring as he reaches down going for a STF but she apparently recovered quickly as she pulls him down into a pin, 1…2…Kick Out! Altair kicks out and angrily gets to his feet going for a kick while she is on her knees but she ducks it and then rolls him up yet again 1…2…Kick Out! Even angrier Altair bounces up to his feet not happy at all with this but now it’s Faith that gets up first rushing forward as he just gets to his feet and slamming him back down face first with a Bulldog. The fans are back into it now as Faith starts to get things going as she turns around and as soon as Altair gets to his feet she rushes at him with a Headscissors Takedown that puts him back down this time she wastes little time going to her favorite place on the outside of the ring holding onto the top rope. She jumps up into the air as soon as Altair stumbles to his feet and nails him with a Springboard DDT that sends the fans into a frenzy sensing a come back in this one. RILEY STEVENS: “Here she comes!” Faith crawls on top after the big DDT as the fans count with her 1…Kick Out! The fans count two just assuming it would be a three as they stop shocked at the early kick out as Faith gets up; even she seems a bit worried that Altair kicked out that quickly. But not wanting to waste any time, she rushes forward with a Running Crossbody right into another pin 1…Kick Out. This time he pushes her off and she rolls again to the outside, where she hops up yet again grabbing the ropes where she jumps up going for a Springboard move but as soon as she propels herself she regrets it as Altair rolls out of the way. Luckily for her she is able to land on her feet, unlucky for her Altair spins her around and connects with ‘The Downard Spiral’ a Corkscrew Neckbreaker that puts her down and perhaps out, but rather then making the cover Altair backs up with sick intentions in his eyes. RILEY STEVENS: “What does this sick individual have in mind here?” He backs away from her as Faith gets up to her feet, completely dazed only seeming to be getting up out of instinct. Altair has bloody intentions as he walks forward grabbing her and heading for a bite on the shoulder but Simpson out of no where turns it right into the ‘Losing Faith’ a Twist of Fate that catches Altair off guard. She backs up and jumps up onto the ropes realizing she just dodged a bite but with Altair down and out long enough for her to get up to the top and jump off with the ‘Have A Little Faith’ a 630 Senton that connects right on top of Altair. 1…2…3! JAMAL JACKSON: “Here are your winner… Faith Simpson!” The fans go wild as Faith rolls out of the ring holding up her arms as the referee rushes around to hand her the GIW Legacy Championship which she hugs proudly. RILEY STEVENS: “This girl just continues to win, overcoming all the odds.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Jesus; Tony Miranda and Faith Simpson is too much for me.” Faith heads towards the back excited as in the ring Altair stands up not at all seeming to show effects of the match as he rubs some blood that was opened up on his lip, licking it up as he shakes his head as we take a break. WINNER: FAITH SIMPSON As we come back from commercial we see Faith Simpson entering the back with a big smile on her face holding her belt as she walks into Joshua Acquin who pats her on the back. JOSHUA ACQUIN: “Congratulations on the win.” Faith smiles up at the current General Manager of Revolt as he gives her a reassuring nod then stops her before she goes. JOSHUA ACQUIN: “Just wanted to say that the Boss has been impressed with you. In two weeks on the special year end broadcast you will defend your belt against the winner of a triple threat match next week, good luck.” Faith seems to take it in, nodding her head seeming ready for it but before the two can separate. AUSTIN ACQUIN: “Whoa, whoa, whoa! Let me get this straight. You retired my belt, in favor of this one held by this little girl? Seriously Josh. I should be a champion, heck maybe I can bring some prestige to that belt.” Austin glares over at Faith who angrily looks back at him as Joshua smiles clearly on Faith’s side with this one. JOSHUA ACQUIN: “Fine. Next week you’ll go one on one with Faith and if you win you can be added to that title match.” Joshua says this and then heads out as Austin smirks down at her staring at her belt as she pulls it closer to her and then walks off as we head to the ring. QUOTE The theme by Matador hits as the fans begin to boo as they turn their attention to the top of the ramp where Aspen Chaud and Ace Baldwin walk out from the back, their entrance really putting an end to all rumors that the two have not formed some sort of alliance as together they head down to the ring ignoring the boos. RILEY STEVENS: “We finally see Aspen’s true colors don’t we Oliver, walking to the ring with Ace Baldwin of all people.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “It’s called being smart, together these two are a unstoppable duo.” Aspen heads up the ramp followed by Ace, she holds down the rope for him then he does the same back as the two head to their corner as the fans continue to jeer them until “Bat Country” by Avenged Sevenfold and for perhaps one night only the fans actually give Johnny Violence a rather mixed reaction better then his usual full on hate as he heads out from the back still looking as confident as ever. RILEY STEVENS: “You know Johnny Violence might be stating a fact when he says he’s the next number one contender right now there is nobody else worthy.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “That’s for sure but Ace Baldwin is still first in line.” Violence shows little fear heading into the ring across from the two bad guys as his music cuts off and before his music even hits the fans go insane for the champion. "World Warrior" by RedRapper hits and this only further raises the bar as the fans go crazy for the new top dog here in GIW, Tony Miranda who walks out with that belt for the first time as champion. RILEY STEVENS: “It’s been a long time coming for us to see Tony Miranda enter the ring with that belt.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Enjoy it while it lasts.” Tony heads down to the ring high fiving the fans as he jumps up into the ring and then through the ropes joining Johnny in the corner as the two discuss things before the match as the referee calls for the bell. DING DING DING! For Aspen and Ace; Aspen quickly talks Ace into letting her start and he takes a step to the outside as Johnny gets to go first as Tony Miranda takes his spot on the ropes as the match is ready to go. Aspen waves her hands for Violence to stop as she walks over to the side of the ring where she demands a microphone which she is handed as Violence and Miranda try to urge the referee to stop her but when Aspen has a mic, that’s not the easiest thing to do. ASPEN CHAUD: “Before we get this thing started, I want to send a message to you Johnny. You know you and I are not so different really. You a reckless do whatever it takes to get ahead type of guy, that’s what I do as well. Do you realize what is happening right now? Blake is booking you as the second wheel, you are his Robin. Listen Johnny you are a man that likes to make an impact. Right? Well there is no bigger impact that can be made, then you helping Ace and I take Tony out once and for all.” Aspen looks over at Tony as she lowers the microphone as Violence looks at her then back at Tony as the fans begin to boo thinking he just might do it. ASPEN CHAUD: “Don’t play second fiddle! Show the boss why you deserve the shot…DO IT! DO IT!” Chaud points past him at Miranda urging Johnny to do it and yet again he peeks back at his tag team partner before turning back to Aspen and slapping her right across the face sending the mic out of her hands and Aspen reeling into the ropes, with the crowd going wild. Tony claps in the corner as angry Aspen wildly runs at him throwing a forearm that Violence ducks under as he grabs her around the waist throwing her over his shoulder with a German Suplex. Get this; the fans are going crazy for Violence at this point. Chaud gets up holding her back trying to get away, stumbling towards Ace for the tag but Johnny stops her pulling her by her trunks back into his arms then over his shoulder into the ‘Trashed’ a Torture Rack that really stretches out Aspen. She seems to be in some extreme pain until Ace rushes into the ring kicking him in the gut, which actually causes him to drop her probably hurting her more. Violence stumbles back as the referee tells Ace to get out; all this allows Aspen to roll out of the ring to safety. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Come on Ace, come on Aspen!” Aspen clicks the reset button as Violence heads back to his corner saying something to Tony. Chaud slides back into the ring and tags in Ace quickly as she takes her spot on the outside as Ace steps into the ring looking across at Violence who circles him ready to go. Baldwin throws a wild punch that missed and sends him right into a Suplex! The fans cheer as Violence spins over and back up hitting a second Suplex. Finally he gets back up and finishes the ‘Critical Acclaim’ on Ace, jumping down on him for the three count 1…2…Kick Out. Ace will not go down that easy as Violence gets up to his feet with a big smirk on his face, seeming to like how this match has gone so far. Johnny waits for Ace to get up, but Aspen screams something at him which catches his attention long enough for Ace to rush forward slamming him back into the corner and retagging in Aspen. RILEY STEVENS: “Tony has yet to get into the match.” Ace and Aspen together lay punches into Johnny in the corner as Ace holds him there long enough for Aspen to come forward hitting a ‘Tush Push’ a Turnbuckle Flying Butt Shot that lands flush. The fans boo as Johnny stumbles out into the center of the ring and Aspen jumps off with an ‘AirBarbie’ a Flying Crossbody right into a pin 1…2…Kick Out. This gets the fans going as Violence gets back up but Aspen puts him right back down to the with a big Sitout Jawbreaker. The fans boo her as she yet again rolls him over for a cover 1…Kick Out. She loses ground with that pin attempt as she gets a little angry with the referee demanding he hurry it up. She reaches back down to grab him but he pushes her away, then gets up jumping towards the corner and tagging in Tony Miranda to a huge ovation from the crowd. RILEY STEVENS: “Here comes the champion!” Aspen now starts to back pedal towards her corner as Tony goes in for a big clothesline then another to Ace who jumps into the ring trying to get involved. Then he hands out a pair of Spinning Roundhouse Kicks first to Aspen and then to Ace as the fans start to go wild as the referee tries to maintain control. Tony turns his attention to Aspen whipping her into the corner where he rushes at her throwing a big kick that misses when Chaud ducks out of the way at the last possible second. Though he just got out there Aspen does not seem to care as she tags in Ace regardless, who seems caught off guard by it. On the other side Tony gets up slowly and this causes Johnny to reach over and make a tag to him. RILEY STEVENS: “Violence and Baldwin are the legal men.” Ace gets in with Aspen standing in front of him the two trying to regroup but Tony who still thinks he’s legal rushes across the ring going for the ‘Third Strike’ a Running Leg Lariat on Aspen but she ducks away from it sending it right into Ace who takes the big foot to the face. Aspen bails out of the ring as Ace stumbles right up seeing birds as Violence who just got back into the ring signals for the end then lifts him up into a ‘Glory Fades’ a Tigerbomb that sends the fans into a frenzy as he gets the pin as Tony watches Aspen who sits on her butt on the outside having just bailed out of the ring 1…2…3! JAMAL JACKSON: “Here are your winners… Johnny Violence and Tony Miranda!” The fans react with happiness as Tony and Johnny turn to one another looking at each other nodding their heads at one another seeming to enjoy that they got the job done. WINNERS: JOHNNY VIOLENCE & TONY MIRANDA Both men roll out of the ring together as they stop at the bottom of the ramp, to exchange one more glance this time as Tony is handed his belt Violence has his eyes set firmly on it. Aspen returns to the ring crawling over to check on Ace here. RILEY STEVENS: “So much for unstoppable right Oliver?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Whatever.” The fans pat them on their backs the entire time as Aspen looks from Ace up to Tony and Johnny angrily as the two back pedal up the ramp. All the sudden CRACK! We hear the loud thud of metal to flesh as the camera transitions back to Johnny and Tony as Altair has just returned with a Steel Chair and has slammed it into Johnny’s back then spinning around and nailing Tony as well. RILEY STEVENS: “What the hell is this?” Altair lets Tony fall over to the side not seeming worried at all about him as he heads towards Violence who stayed on his feet, holding his back as yet again Altair cracks the chair over his back this time dropping him to the ground. RILEY STEVENS: “Altair, who had words with Johnny Violence earlier is out here to assault him!” The fans boo him as Altair delivers the final blow a chair shot to Johnny as he is on his knees that sends him face first to the ground as Altair simply drops the chair heading towards the exit as in the ring Aspen looks just as surprised as everyone else. RILEY STEVENS: “What in the hell is going on? Tune in next week, as we see what happens next.” We cut to a recovering Tony holding his back in confusion then a sprawled out Violence on the floor as the show fades.
