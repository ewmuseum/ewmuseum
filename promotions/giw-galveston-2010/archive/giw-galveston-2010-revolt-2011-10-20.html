GIW — Card

Revolt, 20 October 2011

20 October 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: THURSDAY NIGHT REVOLT LIVE! From Key West in Florida – October 20, 2011 BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! We are nearly back to Galveston now as our final stop off here in Key West, Florida is our go home show until Halloween night when things will surely get crazy. As for tonight the fans have gathered here in Key West and they are ready for the night’s action. As we enter the ring we see Zachary Pagonis already standing with a big carpet laid out in the ring with two opposing sides, one being red the other being blue. The fans are anxious to see what this is all about as Pagonis starts to speak up. ZACHARY PAGONIS: “Welcome everyone to Revolt! You know tonight is like an All Star Game before our Super Bowl. We are just a few weeks away from ‘On Hallowed Ground II’ which will be headlined by ‘GIW Undisputed Champion’ Alioth Starre defending his belt against “Amazing” Ace Baldwin! And tonight the two will be on opposite sides of the ring in a huge Falls Count Anywhere Elimination Six Man Tag Match!” This news brings the fans to their feet in excitement. If there was one way to make a Six Man Tag Match really interesting this is it. As they calm down Pagonis continues. ZACHARY PAGONIS: “So tonight the two captains Ace Baldwin and Alioth Starre will go to battle. But before that happens we’d like to have both men come out here and talk about the match tonight. First the challengers team...Team Ace!” The fans boo mostly at the mention of Ace Baldwin as his music created by Matador hits and the fans respond accordingly with loud boos. Out he comes wearing a red shirt with the name ‘Team Ace’ on it, holding onto the MWA Championship as he heads towards the ring followed not far behind by Austin Acquin holding his GIW Television Championship. Farther back is Aspen Chaud the only one that gets cheered as they make their entrance down towards the ring. ZACHARY PAGONIS: “Their members are Ace Baldwin…” Before he can get any further Ace Baldwin pulls his mic away from him and then shoves him out of the way turning around to face the crowd as Aspen heads to the corner with a upset look on her face as Austin proudly stands in the ring ready to go, all three have their own Team Ace shirts even Aspen who did so unwillingly. ACE BALDWIN: “Let me get this straight…You are booing the man that is going to end the boring reign of Alioth Starre? Lets face it nobody is excited about our champion anymore. He’s become more of a detriment to this company then a help. So when the reign ends you will have me to thank…As for tonight I enter the ring with my team knowing full well that I’ll be the only one left when the night is over.” This upsets Aspen who steps up from the corner trying to calm herself down as even Austin realizes it’s a slight to him as he stares at Ace with a glare. ACE BALDWIN: “From there I’ll go on to become the GIW Undisputed Champion! Now let’s hear it for ACE BALDWIN!” The fans immediately boo as Ace shakes his head and lowers his mic glaring out at the crowd as the welcomed sound of "Blood & Fire (Out of the Ashes Mix)" by Type O Negative hits and the fans go wild. Out comes Alioth Starre holding that belt he just mentioned as Tony Miranda trails him not far behind and Johnny Violence heads out way behind them both. ACE BALDWIN: “Ladies and gentleman if you haven’t already located the bathrooms you should do so now…Alioth is about to speak.” Ace says this with a big sigh as in comes Alioth’s team standing appropriately on the blue side of the ring only they are not as color coordinated with a team shirt. Alioth does in fact have a microphone as he stares across at Ace. ALIOTH STARRE: “I am sorry you people had to listen to him. Possible side effects of Ace Baldwin promos include drowsiness, upset stomach, and a bad case of regret. I see that you brought that little replica belt with you tonight. The belt holds about as much value as the company you held it in. I just hope you realize that while my belt still matters yours…yours is just a memories best forgotten.” Ace has a good laugh at this as he repositions his MWA title over his shoulder as the fans cheer on Alioth’s statements. ALIOTH STARRE: “As for your team tonight I hope you know that you have a girl I already defeated and a man whose belt is about as prestigious as the one you have around your shoulder there on your team.” This seems to get Aspen and Austin going as both now seem to be paying attention as they walk up behind Ace, Austin yelling out some trash talk at Alioth. ACE BALDWIN: “That’s funny because your team is a man I beat and…and…and…that guy!” Baldwin points out Violence as Johnny seems to be sick of the disrespect he’s getting from the other stars as he to gets into his face now. Now it’s Austin trashing Alioth while Violence and Ace exchange verbal jabs as Aspen pulls the mic away from Ace’s hands. ASPEN CHAUD: “As fun as hearing you two talk is, I think I speak for most when I say they’d want this show to get going so that we can get on with the match.” Aspen hands him back the mic and then rolls out of the ring as the fans like that idea. She heads towards the back as soon Tony follows suit heading out. Next out is Violence and then finally Acquin leaving just Alioth and Ace in the ring for a stare down as Starre holds up the belt as we take a commercial break. QUOTE After the break a long one indeed the ring is already fixed up and standing in the ring is Analise Meyers is standing in the ring alongside a huge man. One of those guys you’d see at a terrible hardcore show named Terry Inferno. The type of guy that would make a ‘My six pack is hidden in my gut’ type reference, wearing a Def Leppard shirt and cut up jeans. RILEY STEVENS: “You know Terry Inferno is huge but Analise Meyers is said to be very talented.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I love these random battle royals only here can you see a huge redneck squash a woman and it be totally legal…Haha!” The redneck looks around at the crowd laughing as he points out Analise as the referee steps in between them and calls for the bell for this seemingly huge underdog match up. DING DING DING! The crowd seems genuinely worried for Analise as the big man Inferno walks to the center of the ring chuckling his butt off as he points down at her laughing. Meyers looks up at him looking focused as he proposes a kiss on the cheek, patting his big red grizzly cheek. Analise posters up like she’s going to before throwing a huge right then actually makes him stumble back a little shocked. Now he seems a bit angry as he runs at her only he’s not exactly Mr. Swift. She kicks him in the knee, and then runs off the ropes throwing a big punch at him. This only sort of effects him as she does it again, landing it again. Finally she goes for it one last time but this time gets caught by the big man. With anger in his eyes from the embarrassment she is causing him he lifts her up into the air in probably one of the few moves he knows a Powerbomb position. But at the last possible second she pushes off landing in front of him and nailing him with a big Standing Dropkick! The fans go wild as Inferno starts to teeter, if one thing is good about his weight is that he’s got a good vertical base. RILEY STEVENS: “Can she get him down?” The fans are on her side now loving the underdog story as she rushes off the ropes and with Inferno barely able to stand she nails him with a big Spear that sends him down. The fans are shocked just as everyone else is but their shock turns into amazement as right away she jumps up into a Standing Moonsault right on top of him! The fans go wild 1…2…Kick Out! Inferno throws her off; trying to maintain his last bit of dignity and respect his friends may have for him. She gets up with a smirk growing on her face as the big man gets up stumbling trying to get his feet under him. Rather then waste any more time she stalks him from behind before bam hitting the ‘Icy Throne’ a Roundhouse Kick! Right away the big mans lights seem to go out as he falls down to the ground and she jumps on top 1…2…3! Just like that it is over. JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…Analise Meyers!” The fans go wild for her as she gets to her feet, standing over him with a big smile on her face as she turns and asks for a mic. RILEY STEVENS: “You know sometimes talent really does trump size as Analise just crushed this redneck from start to end.” Back in the ring Analise has received her mic and is now staring out at the crowd as the referee checks on the redneck. ANALISE MEYERS: “First of all shut up! You people want to cheer me because I just trumped a sexist redneck that didn’t think I stood a chance against him? Well you should probably get used to it. It starts with one guy and then one by one they’ll all fall until I stand at the very top. I don’t need your cheers, I don’t need your encouragement, and I don’t need you people. I will get to the top all by myself.” Meyers drops the mic and after a match like that it’s hard to argue as the fans realize that they had her wrong as she slides out of the ring blowing off extended hands as she heads towards the back. WINNER: ANALISE MEYERS Heading to the back we see Austin Acquin and Joshua Acquin enjoying a chat in the backstage area. Walking up to them is Blake Beckett as both men turn and glare at him their hatred well documented. BLAKE BECKETT: “Hey Josh I have been meaning to talk to you about ‘On Hallowed Ground II’. Your brother here needs a match…What are your thoughts?” Blake says this with a smile as Joshua rubs his chin and then looks over at Austin with a grin. JOSHUA ACQUIN: “You know I was thinking…Nobody has really earned a title shot these past few weeks. So I am thinking that Austin should probably have the week off.” Joshua pats Austin on the back but Blake shakes his head and then responds. BLAKE BECKETT: “You know that’s not possible. Since I do sort of agree with you the match will be non title but you make a choice on his opponent.” Right as he says this Analise Meyers walks back from the ring, having broken just a small sweat Austin sees her and very obviously checks her out. AUSTIN ACQUIN: “How about her?” Austin says this with a sly smile not knowing a thing about her as Blake stops Analise as she turns into them confused. BLAKE BECKETT: “That’s a great idea. Josh I don’t know if you heard yet but this is Analise Meyers our newest signee. She just won a match out there, so I’ll go book the match.” Blake heads on his way as Josh and Austin look at one another then back at Analise who smirks and nods her head seeming happy with the signed match as she turns and heads on her way as the show continues with another match booked. ==== When we return to the ring for the next match suddenly the lights go out, the screen starts to flicker and the words "He’s Coming" show up. Flickering, then fading into black before the lights return. RILEY STEVENS: “Well Oliver that didn’t give us many clues, but it looks like someone is planning on arriving here in GIW.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “It could be anyone from Paul Defias to Doombringer? I’ll hold judgment until we see who it is.” RILEY STEVENS: “I suppose we should keep our eyes peeled then.” With that things return to normal and we head to our next match. QUOTE Commercials finally end and we return ready for the next match as already in the ring is Brody Harper and Koji Nakamora ready to go as “Sirius” by The Alan Parsons Project hits and the fans go wild as AJ Fairchild walks out, energetic as per usual. RILEY STEVENS: “Let me start by saying that the reason Alex Castellanos is not in the ring is that he was not medically cleared by the Doctor. However the three men in the match tonight all really need a win to recover.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Speaking of recovering wonder how AJ’s face is feeling?” Fairchild however doesn’t seem to be showing too much signs of injury from the chair shot he took last week. He high fives the fans and then jumps over the ropes and into the ring, stepping in between them the referee calls for the bell. DING DING DING! Fairchild bounces around with a big grin on his face as Harper runs at him as usual throwing caution to the wind as Fairchild shows off his athleticism jumping right over him. The fans seem impressed as Harper stops and angrily charges again only this time AJ sidesteps him. Now its Koji’s turn as he tries his hand at it throwing a big left at Fairchild but he ducks it and then pushes him right into Harper. The two bounce off one another as AJ jumps up dropkicking him right in the back right into Harper. The big man Brody falls to the ground but Koji stumbles back right into a big Thrust Kick that doubles him over. From there AJ grabs him and goes for a Sliced Bread #2 jumping up off the ropes then bringing him down! The fans go wild as Fairchild is pulled up immediately by Brody and thrown over the top rope. AJ athletically lands on the outside using the ropes to hold himself up. The fans are on his side as Brody rushes at him only for AJ to jump up and land a big kick. This sends Harper stumbling backwards as Fairchild uses the ropes to go Springboard and lands a big Forearm Smash! The fans go into another loud cheer as AJ is putting on his own little highlight reel in this one. RILEY STEVENS: “So far it is all AJ in this one, looking a lot more motivated this week. There is nobody more athletic in GIW then this guy.” Before he can even think about going for a pin Koji has recovered and pulls him away trying to prevent the end of this one but as Nakamora goes to use his feet AJ catches his leg and lands a Dragon Leg Screw. Both men hop right back up but Koji swings wildly as AJ ducks it grabs a hold of him and hits a German Suplex. Now he goes for his first pin 1…2…Brody kicks him off. The fans boo as Harper exhausted already pulls AJ up and throws him towards the corner. Brody rushes at him only to be up kicked then followed up with a Jumping Facebuster that sends him to the mat. AJ turns around and jumps to the top rope as the fans go wild as AJ looking to put on a show looks out at them and then jumps off with a Diving Senton! The fans go nuts as Fairchild hops up holding the back of his neck as Brody rolls out of the ring flopping onto the floor, done. Koji now throws one last desperation leg kick but when it misses he gives up his back and from there AJ grabs him around the neck and drops to the ground wrapping him up into the ‘Holy Mutilation’ a Rear Naked Choke! The fans are going nuts as it’s either tap our or pass out and Koji chooses option A as he taps out to end this match. JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…AJ Fairchild!” The fans go wild for the winner as Fairchild hops up, sweating but only because of the moves he himself did. The referee raises his hand as AJ gives one big fist pump for the crowd who love the way he wrestles. RILEY STEVENS: “You know it seems there are two types of AJ Fairchild’s the motivated one and the unmotivated one. If the one that showed up tonight continues to do so…The sky is truly the limit.” Fairchild rolls out of the ring and starts to make his rounds high fiving the fans here in Key West all the way around the ring as with this we go to the back. WINNER: AJ FAIRCHILD As we head to the back we are greeted with a happy AJ Fairchild just now walking through to the back with a grin on his face as he stumbles right into Johnny Violence as he’s looking over at a Staff Member. JOHNNY VIOLENCE: “Watch where your going!” AJ takes a few steps back holding out his hands as he brushes himself off. AJ FAIRCHILD: "My bad, my bad. No need to get…violent." AJ has a good laugh at his little pun as Johnny glares at him not amused in the slightest with that one. AJ FAIRCHILD: "Jeez what is your issue?" Violence shakes his head and points to a bulletin board behind him which shows the card for ‘On Hallowed Ground II’ which now has Tony Miranda versus Aspen Chaud lined up to fight. JOHNNY VIOLENCE: “They snubbed me from the Tony Miranda and Aspen Chaud match…I was suppose to wreck those two.” Fairchild looks at it and shrugs his shoulders then takes a second to think to himself before looking back at Johnny. AJ FAIRCHILD: "You know…I don’t have a match scheduled and neither do you. I think our agenda is the same, getting a shot at Alioth Starre. So let’s do it me versus you…" Violence at first looks at him like he is crazy for even thinking they were on the same level. JOHNNY VIOLENCE: “You versus me? I want to fight the top guys, not bottom of the barrel nobodies…Actually you know what why not? I have nothing better going for you. And when I leave you stretchered out they’ll have no choice but to make me the next contender.” Johnny shoulders past AJ who seems to at least be happy that he’s got the shot to fight as he turns and looks at the card staring at the tip top…Alioth Starre. ==== Else where in the backstage area Tony Miranda is doing his final adjustments in his own little set up locker room area when Aspen Chaud strolls on up with a focused look. ASPEN CHAUD: “Don’t worry I am not here to hurt you before the match tonight or make any little teams. Just wanted to hear if you’d heard that we got our little match booked for ‘On Hallowed Ground II’.” Tony takes a second then nods his head as he finishes wrapping up his left hand as Aspen grins. ASPEN CHAUD: “I think this is at least the fortieth time we’ve met in the ring. But let’s leave the stakes simple this time, cause while it’s no official contendership match I think we both know it should be…So no owning each other…No potentially company ending stakes…Especially no more MMA matches…Just one versus one.” Aspen extends her hand and Tony does the same their two hands very different in size but both in agreement with this one. TONY MIRANDA: “Winning that title is the biggest stake there is…” Aspen nods her head in agreement as the two share one final rather awkward yet telling moment before breaking hands as Aspen goes on her way as Tony watches with focus in his eyes. QUOTE It is now time for the Main Event of the evening. The fans are ready to go and things are underway right away as Ace Baldwin’s music by Matador hits and it’s now time for Team Ace. Out from the back lead by Ace Baldwin wearing the MWA title over his shoulder followed by Austin Acquin with the GIW Television Championship and finally Aspen Chaud. Ace and Austin walk confidently into the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “You know Aspen will come to fight but can she work well with these two?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “She has the best captain in the world she better.” Aspen follows them into the ring heading to their side of the ring; the rules are Tornado so they don’t bother leaving. Already out there is three referees so they can watch all the potential action that goes on. Their music cuts off and now "Blood & Fire (Out of the Ashes Mix)" by Type O Negative hits and here comes Team Starre. Leading the way is Tony Miranda walking far ahead of his partners as Alioth Starre follows him and lastly Johnny Violence. RILEY STEVENS: “This team I am not sure about, Violence is probably the X-Factor.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “That’s true.” Tony gets to the ring far before anyone else and right as he enters the ring BOOM! The crowd gasps as Ace Baldwin clobbers him with the MWA title. This doesn’t put a pep into Violence’s step at all but Starre runs full steam towards the ring sliding in. But Austin hops in front starting a brawl as the referee quickly calls for the bell. DING DING DING! Aspen seems shocked at what just happened here in this one to Tony. Alioth tries to get past Acquin but he is enough of a distraction as he tackles him back into the corner to keep Starre held up. In the ring Tony with stars in his eyes stumbles to his feet and right into Ace’s awaiting arms as he lifts him up into the air with a T.F.C. ‘Thanks For Coming’ a Standing Death Valley Driver with the Cradle for the Pin…1…2…3! Tony Miranda is Eliminated! Despite Alioth’s best attempt to get out of the corner to stop it the pin happens and Ace turns and goes right after him. Starre jumps up kicking Acquin out of the way and then throws a big punch at Baldwin knowing now that the numbers are not on their side. Aspen who was watching from afar disagreeing with how they got the first pin is suddenly pulled to the ground and then pulled from the ring by Johnny Violence. From there he throws her at one of the barricades, hitting her back hard. Back in the ring Alioth out of no where crushes Austin with a Super Kick! Acquin folds back to the ground but the double team comes into play as Ace rams him back into the corner and then hits him with a big elbow. Alioth pushes him away then runs at him only to be lifted up into a Spinning Spine Buster that plants him. Baldwin goes for the early elimination 1…2…Kick Out! The fans cheer as they are on Alioth’s side. Outside of the ring we go the camera having trouble keeping up with the action as Violence again lifts Aspen into the same move he hit her with last week the ‘Trashed’ a Torture Rack. But while it hurts her, she isn’t quite as un-flexible as most. Yet again he gives up on it just dropping her from his back. RILEY STEVENS: “Violence and Starre need an elimination to really even this thing back up.” In the ring Starre is back up and he’s being grappled by both Acquin and Baldwin who whip him off the ropes. On the rebound Starre jumps up clotheslining both men down! The fans go wild as he gets up and kicks Austin in the gut then hits a Fisherman Suplex with a Pin 1…Ace breaks it up. Unfortunately for Alioth he’s going to have to struggle to find a way to get both men out of it. Ace lifts him up and then doubles him over with a knee to the mid-section. The fans boo him as he runs off the ropes and hits a Shoulder Block. Alioth hops right back up but this time as Ace goes for another one Alioth ducks it and throws him through the ropes. The fans go wild as Austin rushes at him only to get dropped on his butt and then locked into the ‘Four Star Mary’ a Figure Four Leglock! The fans go wild as Austin quickly taps out. Austin Acquin is Eliminated! RILEY STEVENS: “Teams are even…” However just as this is happening on the outside Johnny looks to try to finish Aspen when out of no where she drops down spinning him over right into the K.M.A. Kiss My Aspen a Heel Lock! The fans actually cheer for her move as well as she holds it in tight as Violence tries to get some where to get some leverage but he’s stuck in the middle of the surrounding area and he succumbs to the pain and taps out. Johnny Violence is Eliminated! RILEY STEVENS: “And now it’s back to two on one.” The fans cheer Aspen as she pulls off and then she looks into the ring where Starre is waiting looking back and forth from Ace to Aspen. Chaud is the first one up as she hops into the ring and the two start to circle one another. They’ve been here before and here they go again. They go in for a lock up which Starre quickly wins. He backs her up against the ropes and then throws her off. On the rebound she ducks under his clothesline attempt then runs off the ropes again. This time Alioth jumps right over her as she hits the ropes again and this time the two run at each other and hit simultaneous clotheslines that knock them both down. On the outside of the ring we can see Ace getting up to his knees, then crawling over so he’s hidden beside the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Look at your Captain out there hiding Oliver.” In the ring Aspen and Alioth get back to their feet as Alioth grabs her recovering first and then hitting her with a Snap DDT! The fans seem split here on who to cheer as Alioth covers 1…2…Kick Out! Aspen is not done yet that’s for sure. Starre now looks to finish as he starts to get ready for another ‘Four Star Mary’ but Aspen is able to kick him away as he’s about to get it locked in. But as she rushes to her feet Alioth always calculating crushes her with a Superkick that knocks her down. Before he can make the cover Ace hops up and while we couldn’t see him he went and grabbed Alioth’s belt. Jumping up onto the ring apron he holds it up in the air as Starre says some words as does Baldwin they exchange. RILEY STEVENS: “Ace has gotten under Alioth’s skin a bit by touching his belt.” But while the two exchange words Aspen gets to her feet not really even noticing what’s going on before she hops up and hits a ‘Queen's Wrath I’ a Double Knee Backbreaker! The fans again are split but before she can make the cover Ace jumps into the ring and throws her away. The fans boo his actions as he reaches down and picks up Alioth’s belt and stalks him as he gets back to his feet. Rushing forward he again clobbers Alioth with the belt to the face, putting Starre on dream street as he falls to the mat. Ace quickly makes the cover, 1…2…3! And Ace has pinned Starre. JAMAL JACKSON: “Your winning team is Team Ace! The Survivors are Aspen Chaud and Ace Baldwin!” The fans boo the end as Ace hops to his feet with a huge smirk on his face as he stands over Alioth with his Undisputed belt in hand. Aspen got up in the corner watching in anger as Ace just took what could have been her fast track back to another shot. Ace hugs the belt tight and then holds it up into the air bringing the boos. RILEY STEVENS: “Alioth pinned Ace a few weeks back and now he has returned the favor in this one with some help from Aspen. Now he stands in the ring with Alioth’s Undisputed Championship.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “It won’t be his for long.” RILEY STEVENS: “This concludes are cruise and the next time you see us we’ll be back in Galveston at the Hotel Galvez on Halloween Night. We have a great card of fights but none as big as this one. Can Alioth defend his belt against his biggest challenge to date or will this be the site as Halloween comes to a close?” Back in the ring Ace continues to pose over Alioth with his own belt as perhaps we have found the man that can put an end to the Alioth Starre reign as the show comes to a close. WINNERS: TEAM ACE (Ace Baldwin, Aspen Chaud, & Austin Acquin)
