Pre-show, 15 August 2012

GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS MOMENT OF GLORY III – PRE SHOW LIVE! FROM THE MOODY GARDEN’S PLAZA - AUGUST 15, 2012 QUOTE This show was ready to get going and after some hype for the Main Card we returned to the ring looking ready to go for the Open Battle Royal where we had two stars ready to go. DING DING DING! The jam-packed crowd who have come from near and far are hungry for the first bit of action of the evening. It’s time for one of the oldest traditions in Galveston Island Wrestling history, the open battle royal. Only two competitors had the guts to step up and compete in this match up. Standing on one side of the ring was the newest addition to the GIW roster, Sam Cantrell. On the other side was Sage Gemsen who before the match was carrying on about how she was the “baddest bitch on the planet” much to no reaction from the crowd. She wasn’t over at all. The bell rang and quickly Sage strutted around the ring a slutty manner doing anything to get a reaction from the crowd. Sam simply laughed in the corner before hitting a huge swinging neckbreaker. He followed it up by stomping away at Sage who was screaming in pain that she wasn’t worthy of being on the stage of GIW. The crowd almost felt bad. Quickly Sam picker her up and nailed the SC15, an overdrive, and put Sage out of her misery. He pinned her and picked up the win, an impressive debut for the young superstar. REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner…Sam Cantrell!” These fans cheered him on, they were on his side through out as he got to his feet celebrating the win knowing that this was just the beginning however and that next week his rise really would start. WINNER: SAM CANTRELL QUOTE “The Show Goes On” by Lupe Fiasco plays on the PA system and the fans begin to cheer loudly as Daredevil comes out of the curtain fairly quickly and immediately walks down the ramp slapping the hands of the fans and getting into the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “What a night this is going to be for Daredevil. The opportunity to reign victorious at the biggest show of the whole year.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Well that’s a shame, Riley, it’s going to be Attack Attack who takes that opportunity tonight!” “Diamonds Are Forever” by Bring Me The Horizon hits the PA system and the fans begin to boo. The very confident and arrogant Attack Attack comes out of the curtain and starts walking down the ring patting his chest and mocking the fans before getting into the ring. DING! DING! DING! Daredevil in the blue tights and Attack in the black jeans square up to each other and begin circling around the ring. Attack makes the first move and grabs Daredevil into a headlock. Daredevil lifts him up and drops him down with an atomic drop causing Attack to stumble a little. Daredevil grabs him by the head and then rams it into the turnbuckle before pinning him into the corner. Daredevil climbs up and starts ramming his fist into Attack’s face before Attack delivers a punch to the sternum causing him to fall back to the canvas on his feet. Attack quickly stands up on the second turnbuckle and jumps down delivering a flying European uppercut. He crawls onto Daredevil quickly and hooks the leg. 1… 2… but right after the two, Daredevil gets the shoulder up. Attack rolls him onto his back again and stomps on his chest. He runs off the ropes and drops down with an elbow but Daredevil quickly moves out of the way. Daredevil grabs Attack quickly and drops him down in the centre of the ring for the DDT. Daredevil tries to rally the crowd and get them excited so he runs off the ropes leaps over Attack bouncing off the opposite ropes and hitting a lionsault to the delight of the fans. Attack gets up to his feet and bends over holding his stomach. Daredevil bounces off the ropes and jumps to try and connect with the scissor kick but Attack flips his legs over causing Daredevil to fall awkwardly. He brings Daredevil to his feet and throws him into the steel post in the corner. RILEY STEVENS: “Attack using the steel post as a weapon!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “It’s legal isn’t it!? Attack is neutralizing Daredevil’s fast highflying ability.” Attack takes a few steps back before running and jumping to deliver a high knee to Daredevil who was resting against the corner. Attack runs out and delivers a jumping bulldog, as Daredevil went face first into the canvas. Attack rolls him over and hooks the leg, 1… 2… Daredevil escapes the pin by locking his legs around Attack’s neck and shoulder area in some sort of triangle choke. Attack uses some of his strength to stand up lifting Daredevil in this position but then the high flier flipped over Attack Attack and rolled him up into a pin, 1… 2… Attack rolls out and then kicks Daredevil square in the face as he sat on the floor. Attack taunted to the fans as they showed their lack of liking towards him by booing him but he clearly didn’t care. He climbed through the ropes and mounted the top turnbuckle with his back to the opponent. Attack leaped downwards, flipping his body to try and hit a moonsault but to no avail. Daredevil rolled out of the way but somehow Attack used his athleticism and reflexes to land on his feet. Daredevil reacted the quickest jumping up and hitting the head scissors throwing Attack onto the middle rope. He ran off the opposite ropes and hit the tiger feint kick sending Attack out of the ropes and very groggy in the middle of the ring. Daredevil balanced himself on the apron for a moment before spring-boarding over and hitting a diving crossbody onto his opponent. Daredevil hooked both legs, 1… 2… but Attack pushed Daredevil off of his body to stop the pinfall attempt. RILEY STEVENS: “This match has been so back and forth so far.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Yep, but I have a feeling it’s going to be going in Attack’s favor very soon.” Attack slowly got up, beating Daredevil to a vertical base. He approached the corner and glared at Daredevil who began to get up. Attack was ready to finish off his opponent as he twitched his leg looked to hit his patented super kick. Daredevil got up to his feet and turned around Attack Attack struck him with ‘Eyes Set to Sleep’. Daredevil fell backwards onto the canvas and Attack quickly covered him by hooking the leg, 1… 2… but Daredevil gets the shoulder up once more and Attack begins to wonder what he has to do to win here. Attack brings Daredevil to his feet but he quickly counters and hits a roundhouse kick to Attack knocking him to the canvas. Daredevil points to the turnbuckle and the fans begin to cheer. He climbs up onto the top rope and points downwards at Attack Attack before hitting ‘Dare to Fly’, a sensational 630 corkscrew Senton. Daredevil holds his stomach before hooking the leg of Attack Attack, 1… 2… but another kick out this time from Attack and now everybody is wondering how somebody is going to win this match. Both men slowly get to their feet but again Attack acts first ramming Daredevil into the corner. He lifts him up onto the top rope and then climbs up onto the top rope himself as the crowd looked on, very excitedly. Attack throws Daredevil’s arm over his shoulder, holds onto his tights and then hits a Superplex into the centre of the ring. Both men lay flat out in the centre of the ring. It seems like Attack Attack took as much damage as Daredevil did as both men are motionless. “Demon Eyes” by The Answer hit the PA system, and the fans were absolutely shocked to see Blake Beckett come out onto the ramp, microphone in hand. BLAKE BECKETT: “Attack, Devil... this has never really happened before and I'm not sure how to put this, but we are going to have to call the match right here and now.” The fans booed the hell out of Beckett, who shrugged on the ramp as Daredevil and Attack Attack were both using ropes on opposite sides of the ring to pull themselves up. BLAKE BECKETT: “This was a great match, and I know it is unfair to you guys, but we can't let the pre-show bleed into Moment of Glory. I'm sorry. It’s all business.” His music picked up again as Attack and Devil looked at each other, completely stunned and pissed off about what just happened. Beckett disappeared behind the curtain as the GIW faded up, and the pre-show concluded in a veil of confusion. DRAW After a few moments for the recently deceased Damu Akpu-Nku as a video begins to play on the tron. “Before there were stars like Ace Baldwin, Daniel Starr, there was a man named Damu Akpu-nku a Nigerian wrecking machine.” As the voice over plays we see shots of Damu in his matches early on here in GIW, showing off his athleticism and explosiveness as Aspen Chaud comes on the screen with a tear in her eyes “Damu was one of the best, everyone in the back loved him.” As the video continues to play shots of Aspen and Damu working together, Blake Beckett comes on the screen now as well. “Damu was a guy I wasn’t sure about when I signed but he really ended up a star around here.” As the video shows a few more shots of him in ring as the voice over guy again takes over. “There was no man that Damu could not over power, however this Saturday Damu was shot at his residence and died on the way to the hospital. A true GIW Pioneer, you will be missed Damu.” The video showing one last shot of Damu smiling as the Pre-Show fades to black.
