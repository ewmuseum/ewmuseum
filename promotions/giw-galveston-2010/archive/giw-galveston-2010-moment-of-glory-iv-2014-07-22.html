As a child you had a vision of the future; and you promised yourself you would struggle, fight and bleed for this chance. As a man, you awaken on the threshold of your own legend. So much work is waiting, but this moment is glorious. Today your soul is charged with the electricity of new beginnings. Today you can reflect on those powerful moments when you knew somebody loved you, somebody believed in you. Heroes of quality and character showed you the way, and now it is your moment to carry those kinds of expectations. Are you good enough to make other better? To be remembered? Are you good enough to be great? Today you will be judged, but no matter when your chosen, it is you who determines your worth. Today it’s not the effort, or the game. This is your life. Alexis thought she had him there and she lays back down on the mat holding her face in shock. “Lets Go Ramsey!” chants emerge and at the 14:00 minute mark of the match Alexis is back to her feet and calls for the end. She drags Beck into the center of the ring and scales up top. MOST GLAMOROUS MOONSAULT EVER! Alexis leaps off but Beck counters with his knees. Alexis rolls around the ring holding her stomach in pain. Beck is back up. The Money Shot! He hits The Money Shot and he covers… ONE…. TWO….. No! Charity drags the ref out of the ring before three! Omar is arguing with Charity now threatening to disqualify Alexis. Charity clocks him with a right hand and Senior Official Omar Gonzalez is out cold. Charity runs around the ring and grabs a chair from the timekeeper area. She slides into the ring with it and the fans are booing now. She looks ready to hit Beck with it when suddenly….Aspen Chaud has jumped the guardrail and is in the ring! Charity goes to hit Aspen but Aspen tackles Charity to the mat! RILEY STEVENS: “Everything is breaking loose here!” Aspen and Charity are brawling all over and it spills up the ramp and to the back. It’s just Alexis and Beck now and Beck looks tired as does Alexis. Beck checks on Omar and rolls the referee back into the ring, he is awake now. Alexis hits Glam Go Round from out of nowhere! ONE….TWO….NO! Alexis can’t believe it nor can the fans. Alexis grabs the chair and is ready to end this. Alexis has the chair up but Beck with The Horror Show! He has a brutal triangle choke and Alexis cant escape! She taps! Alexis taps out and we have a brand new Undisputed Champion! REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner by way of submission and NEWWWWW GIW Undisputed Champion…Beck…RAMSEY!!!” Beck Ramsey is handed the Undisputed Championship and he clenches it close to his chest. He is beaten and bruised but he has finally climbed the mountain in Galveston Island Wrestling after nearly two years of being with the small island promotion. Tears stream down his face as “Vicarious Devil” plays over the speakers in Kauffman Arena. The fans are all on their feet and he is covered in streamers in appreciation for all of his hard work. RILEY STEVENS: “What a moment here tonight for Beck Ramsey who ends Alexis Terry’s in ring career all while getting some long awaited revenge on his longtime rival. And most importantly he etches his name in GIW history as the new GIW Undisputed Champion of the world.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Interim champion! We still have Seton you know.” RILEY STEVENS: “Oh she’s hurt right now and when she’s back that will without a doubt be a great match but this moment is all about Beck Ramsey who came into this needing that signature win in his career. And a win over Alexis Terry at Moment of Glory, it doesn’t get any bigger then that.” Running down the ramp and sliding into the ring is The Harlequin who hugs Beck tightly, emotion pouring over her as well. The two having to soon face the realization that has come with them both winning tonight, but regardless they leave as victor. Beck Ramsey has conquered The System on the grandest stage of them all in GIW, Moment of Glory. The fans continue to clap and cheer for him as we get a final shot of Beck holding the championship belt, of GIW high with the UWL belt in his other hand, high into the air as Moment of Glory IV closes with Beck Ramsey the new Undisputed Champion celebrating his MOMENT. WINNER: BECK RAMSEY (New GIW Undisputed Champion)
