GIW — Card

Revolt, 8 October 2014

8 October 2014
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: REVOLT! Live from the Kauffman Arena – October 8th, 2014 HERE WE GO! "Bartholomew" by Silent Comedy reigns through the televisions of GIW fans across the southern states of America, and with a quick glimpse of our logo; Revolt is under way! Fading into the weekly recap intro we have become accustomed to, our opening image is of GIW newcomer Johnny Hashke successfully winning the open battle royal last week by lastly eliminating Christina May. Quick clips of The Sir's and Mo Chronic's match is played, following up with an arena shot of the fans jumping out their seats when Chuck Loveman returned to GIW! Glimpses of the dark figure attacking backstage personnel is shown for a brief moment - easy missed at the blink of an eye. Several shots from the tag team match from last week is shown, Jack Waters hitting his finisher to both Lynch and Erns is repeated several times before the image of Waters staring at the tombstone that had his own name engraved into it which was left by Jane Doe; Tony Miranda's visionary. A few back and forth clips are shown from Michaud's and Harlequin's main event last week, with the last shot ultimately being the two sitting in steel chairs as the duo of Michael Lynch and Christopher Darren Erns walk back up the ramp. We fade into a live shot of the Galveston faithful here tonight! Growing more and more each and every week, the sea of colorful signs has become a staple for GIW. "We Want Beck!"; "Puppa Show Me Your Puppies""; "Mo Cena 4 Lyfe"; and "I'm MLP's & SIR'S Fanclub Admin" are just a few of the creative signs the GIW fans brought here tonight. They are riled up as all hell for what would be the opening battle royal match, but since only two men stand in the ring, the announcer builds this match as a single - one fall to a finish match featuring a new competitor we have yet to see here in GIW. QUOTE Standing in the ring are two men, both's sizes are severely different. One a scrawny, pale, long-haired young man standing at one corner of the ring. A close up shot of his face shows the sheer nervousness that is obviously building in this kid's body. His eyes are constantly blinking, he seems afraid of his opponent. Standing across from him in the other corner is a nearly seven-foot tall beast of a man whose menacing face is covered by an equally menacing black mask. The tall man is announced as El Muerte, which to all of the Spanish speaking GIW fans means "The Death." The bell sounds, and quickly the scrawny man runs at his opponent looking to gain some quick momentum at the start of this match, but El Muerte swiftly lifts his seemingly size fourteen shoe up and nails the poor guy in the jaw with a big boot! The kick in itself folds the nameless man in half, the fans let out an "Ooo!" as the referee also cringes. Muerte slowly stalks his victim, standing over him, he stares a hole deeply into the back of the kid's head. Suddenly, he reaches down and grabs the bleach blonde hair of the guy, slowly pulling his lifeless body up. Turning him around, Muerte reaches under his arms and applies the most cruel looking full-nelson submission hold we in GIW have ever seen! Bending, twisting, and cranking the neck; El Muerte looks to leave with the kid's head by the end of the night. The referee seems to almost have seen enough, reaching his hand in the air to call off the match already, but Muerte catches a look at him. Wanting to cause more pain, he surprises everyone by lifting the dead weight in the air and throwing him down with a full nelson slam! The man lands right on the back of his neck, easily possibly could have been a paralyzing move. The fans in the front row shield their eyes, even in an indy show, everyone has their limits. Muerte once again slowly walks to his victim, again lifting him up by his hair. This time tossing him comfortable on his shoulders. Looking around the audience with his soulless eyes through the holes in his mask, he picks up speed - leaps in the air - and delivers a thunderous Death Valley Driver that shakes the building! A finishing maneuver we will later know as "Adios" is the exclamation mark in El Muerte's destructive showing as he lays on top his opponent. The referee quickly counts. 1.... 2... 3! To nobody's surprise, El Muerte walks out of this match as the victor. "Wait and Bleed" by Slipknot hits the PA system as the man simply known as death leaves the ring. There's a new force here in Galveston Island Wrestling, everyone should take notice. The camera fades into a graphics saying "Auva Skye to speak! Next!" Normally that would cause some ruckus from the announcers, or at least from the crowd. But everyone seems stunned by El Muerte's dominance in this opening match. We fade to commercial. WINNER via PINFALL: EL MUERTE! Coming back from a quick commercial break we see Auva Skye centered in the ring. She waited for the crowd, and as they softened their voices, she spoke into the microphone in her hand. AUVA SKYE: “This is your administrator speaking! You know who I am and may know of the individuals I have represented in the past. You know about my failed ventures in California, and South Florida, but there is something about me you don't know.” The crowd simmered again, but she let them get it out before continuing. AUVA SKYE: “I've never done good things, and I've never done bad things. I've always tried to maintain a middle ground in everything I've done. I let Dakota destroy my past venture, PCW, because he was in over his head and it was the only way he was going to learn how a business operated, so, when it crumbled before him, I pulled out of that organization and moved to Florida. When my own organization became as stale as bread, I came here to be more than a leader, but a follower of the fans.” The crowd cheered, despite the divulging of her point not yet reached. AUVA SKYE: “I plan on announcing the matches for Hallowed Ground tonight, because that's we do here at GIW. We don't need any surprises here, because that would upset the roster. It would be unfair to the people in the back if we had surprise entrants to matches - unfairness is not something I ever want to describe my leadership! I am, and forever will be a fair and honest leader of this wonderful company!" Half of the crowd cheered as others questioned her honesty, yet she continued. AUVA SKYE: “Here's the point, I know a lot of you have been questioning my methods and my absences. I am a more hands-off leader, because I believe that it is the roster and the fans that make the show believable and worth tuning into! I want this to be oriented to the men and women in the back, and to all of you. I have ideas, but I'd rather let them share theirs first. Lately I've been on a bit of a hiatus, and am still so, but I make my appearances as expected of me - by me. This will continue until the end of November, when I will become more hands-on. Yes, once you have all had your chance to build the company the way you wanted it, I'm going to show just the kind of leader I really am when I finally take a step forward! It begins, of course, with a cleansing...” The lights blackened then flickered into a crimson red, the shade of blood that splattered across the arena. The fans quickly murmur among each other in confusion as Auva trails on this time with a darker tone in her voice. AUVA SKYE: “There is a phantom menace stalking the past employees of GIW. Who better to cleanse the past with someone from the past? I have let you run this company, Christopher Darren Erns, Michael Lynch, Astro, Jack Waters, Harlequin and her hubby, etcetera, all of you whom are excelling excellently in the ring and on screen. I've let you all run amok, because it hasn't been you of whom I've herded into the darkness. It has been the reverent, a man who has no place in my company, then Ms. Rowland; a woman who has no place working on my website; and several more are to follow, and it all began with Alexis Terry.” The crowd was silent for once. Uncertain of the words being spoken, they listened intently, though some made calls, they were quickly silenced by the lights reverting to normal. AUVA SKYE: “Say what you will of my methods; bringing back a past terror to remove the scourge of unnecessary employees. I'm cleansing this company for the better and who I bring in after them will make those before them seem like pond scum in comparison! Note, none of the roster is going to be involved, with the exception of Madison Seton and Alexis Terry, as they have crossed me. Until he is revealed, I will depart for a deeper hiatus. I won't be a push over like past administrators! Cross me, and there will be hell to pay... May the inmates run the asylum on this Hallowed Ground.” Auva Skye lowers the microphone and takes in the cheers and boos from the crowd. Not taken back by the mixed reaction from the crowd, she confidently leaves the ring as we fade into the back. ------------------------------------------------- We come to the back following Jack Waters and his manager, Puppa who are walking around the corridors in the backstage area of the Kauffman Arena. The camera follows the two down the hallway until they abruptly stop in front of a brown door. They take a moment to read the nameplate on the center of the door, "ASTRO". Waters smiles and loudly bangs his fist on the door. However, Puppa doesn't give anybody time to reply, grabbing the door knob instantly. JACK WATERS: "What if he's naked?" Puppa snaps her head at Jack and answers nonchalantly. PUPPA "Don't care, he's not my type." Jack lets out a small snicker. JACK WATERS: "Nobody is." Puppa gives him a quick wink before swinging open the door. PUPPA" "Touché. Hey, Astro! How's life?" Both Waters and Puppa stumble into the room where a startled Kevin Astro tightly clutches his towel which is the only thing covering him. Puppa smiles, but Astro isn't amused as he angrily yells at the two. ASTRO: "Are you serious right now? Seriously? You just barge into my locker room ... let me guess. I've done something yet again to be told I'm an asshole right? Humor me." Waters and Puppa both look at each other and smile as Astro waits for a response. JACK WATERS: "You really don't need to do anything for us to call you an asshole, it's just what we do. No, I came here to ask you if you're ready and all that, today's match is not going to be easy, and after holding me down so hard last week, I wonder if you have what it takes." Astro raises an eyebrow, snapping back. ASTRO: "What?! Hold you down? When did I hold you down? Have I mentioned that my matches are better than yours? Have I mentioned that I've put on star studded performances every time I stop through those ropes? You know what - fuck that. Hold on. Allow me to introduce myself, my name is Kevin Astro, the King of the Undercard. Astro reaches out his hand, but before Waters could think of even shaking it, Astro brought it back to his waist. JACK WATERS: "Yeah, yeah, I know. I saw your name on the under card dozens of times. Your matches are better than mine? Look, I don't care if your ability to take a beating is amazing, I'm not here to please the people or have the best match possible. I'm here to win." Astro inches his way over to Waters, with fire in his eyes. ASTRO "We all are, but understand we won that tag match last week because of my and I repeat, MY HDMI and cover, not yours!" Jack too, gets up in Kevin Astro's face. JACK WATERS: "So, we're going to ignore my two finishers and your miss of a Superkick? Sorry kiddo, but without me you'd be on the losing side." Both men stare at each other nose to nose before Puppa stands between them. With her hand on Jack's chest, she begins to lead him out the door. ASTRO" "You're missing the point, but maybe, just maybe, I'll be able to show you that tonight... so with all do respect, Jack Waters, I'll just see you in the ring." Puppa leads an angry Waters out of the lockeroom just as Astro slams the door behind them. We fade into the arena for our next match. ------------------------------------------------- Backstage, we are treated to the always lovely sight of Mo Chronic and Chuck Loveman who are standing around talking about the mysterious wonders that life has in store for them. However, their conversation is cut short as Steve Realman comes out of nowhere to hit Mo Chronic in the back of the head with his protein shake bottle. For a moment, everything in the world slows down as Mo collapses to the floor. Realman runs off with the protein shake as Chuck watches; his mouth wide open in shock as Mo hits his head on the corner of one of the storage containers. Nothing. Several minutes pass before Mo finally starts to open his eyes CHUCK LOVEMAN “Whoa, man, you okay, man? You took a pretty rough bump, brah.” Mo looks at his hands and sees the neon colored wristbands on his wrists. The veins in his arms are rippling through the flesh; pulsing with every beat of his heart like a steel drum in Jamaica, mon. Mo grabs hold of Chuck’s dreads and gently pulls him closer MO CHRONIC “Hustle…loyalty…bruh.” The scene cuts to commercial. QUOTE Just the chorus of “She Hates Me” by Puddle of Mudd plays throughout the arena as a chorus of boos rain out on the sunny day. The Sir walks out with his signature fedora tipped ever so slightly with a Highschool of the Dead themed Hawaiian shirt on; various semi-naked anime women painted in provocative poses with a Fluttershy t-shirt underneath. Steve Realman isn’t far behind as he looks at his client with a disapproving look the entire time, shouting many hurtful names that nearly causes The Sir to shed a tear not once, but twice. The Sir isn’t bothered by it too much as his enlightened state of mind has elevated him to new heights that won’t hurt him; picturing all of the waifus that will be waiting for him after his victory. "Bright Side of Life" by Rebelution plays much to the enjoyment of the fans as Chuck Loveman makes his entrance with a giant smile on his face that would make any April 20th celebrator jealous of how chill he is. Mo steps out behind his partner and stares into the camera; a new attire on. Chronic speaks to the camera as Chuck makes his way down to the ramp, but as he looks back, Mo is still jabbering to the camera and salutes the crowd at home. Confused, Loveman just keeps on his cheerful way before getting bumped out of the way by Mo Chronic running and sliding into the ring. He takes his shirt off and tosses it into the crowd along with his snapback hat as if he was ready to fight, but calmly exits the ring and joins Oliver and Riley at the commentary booth while Chuck makes his entrance into the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Welcome to the table, Mo! I’m surprised to see you out here after that nasty bump you took earlier in the night.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Wha…what are you talking about, Riley?! This isn’t Mo Chronic!” MO CHRONIC: “Well, Riley, it wasn’t easy to overcome the odds of a concussion, but I sure as hell did it to support my dude, Chuck Loveman.” The match begins with a simple lock up, but The Sir’s weight allows him to toss Loveman around like a ragdoll. And that’s just what The Sir does as he throws Chuck Loveman into each corner of the ring. The fourth time, The Sir charges like a giant bull and hits Chuck with a body splash that nearly crushes the poor hippie. Steve Realman stands close to the ring; not really giving The Sir any helpful tips, but calling him names still. This only causes the neckbeard anger in The Sir to rise as he takes hold of Chuck and hits him with a quick spinebuster that sends a jolt of pain throughout Loveman’s body RILEY STEVENS: “Oh, what a move from The Sir!” MO CHRONIC: “I guess he isn’t being too…GENTLE tonight, huh guys?” RILEY STEVENS: “Right you are, Mo!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “No…seriously…are you telling me Mo put on a hundred pounds of muscle?!” Chuck gets up slowly as The Sir gets distracted by his harsh manager. Tip-toeing on his wrestling boots, Chuck sneaks up on The Sir and gently taps his shoulder. At first, The Sir ignores it, but the second time he turns around and gets a high five to the face! Temporally blinded by Loveman, The Sir stumbles around the ring trying to get focused back on the match. This time away allows Loveman to leap up to the turnbuckle, turns to the camera, and says “Hey mom! Hope you’re making lasagna tonight!”, then jumps off with a diving crossbody that takes both men down! As the two men are sprawled out on the floor, Chuck goes for a quick pin 1… 2… KICK OUT! RILEY STEVENS: “Oh, man! The Sir isn’t going to give up so easily tonight.” MO CHRONIC: “I agree with you there, the guy never gives up, but I have a feeling that people are gonna know that it’s Chuck Loveman’s time now!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “What does that even mean?! Look at this guy! I didn’t even know you could have that many veins in your arms! You look like you’re forty now!” The action in the ring really picks up as The Sir rushes Chuck into the corner and rams his shoulder into Loveman’s stomach over and over again. Tired and worn out, Chuck nearly falls down, but is brought back up to the top turnbuckle as The Sir puts him there. For a moment, Chuck looks like he’s going to recover as he hits The Sir a couple of times with some open palm strikes and looks to take flight, but while the ref is looking away; Steve Realman holds onto the leg of Chuck Loveman allowing The Sir to hit his Baja Buster! Steve smiles wickedly as The Sir goes for the cover MO CHRONIC: “That goes against all of my morals! I didn’t want to say this, but that really rustled my Fruity Pebbles.” RILEY STEVENS: “I agree with you there, Mo. This is just sickening to see such an act from Steve Realman!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Riley, that’s not Mo Chronic!” 1… 2… THRE- NO! KICK OUT! The crowd cheers louder than ever as Chuck manages to break out at the absolute last second much to the amazement of The Sir and Realman. At this point, Realman is shouting insults at Loveman, calling him a dirty hippie among other names and Mo Chronic has had enough! The shirtless man power walks over to Steve Realman and takes him down with a quick forearm shot. The Sir is stunned to see Mo take out his manager and starts to slowly walk over; looking to enact some revenge. As he does this, Mo quickly looks under the ring for something. But what is it? A steel chair? A table? A kendo stick? After a few seconds, Mo finally pulls out a…a…a PIE!...a pie? The Sir puts his head between the ropes to try and grab hold of Mo, but only gets a face full of whipped cream! OLIVER THIBODAUX: “What the hell is this?!” RILEY STEVENS: “It may not be legal, but you can’t say The Sir didn’t have it coming!” Instantly, The Sir starts screaming as Steve Realman gets up to watch his client; a look of complete horror on his face while Chuck Loveman goes for a quick roll up 1… 2… 3! Chuck rolls out of the ring to celebrate his victory with his partner while The Sir is still rolling around in the ring grabbing at his face. Steve Realman rushes into the ring and quickly throws up an “X”, signaling for the training staff and doctors to run down to the ring. Realman takes another trip outside of the ring to grab a fire extinguisher and hurries back in to spray The Sir with it. The training staff takes their time to get down to the ring as they look at one another; confused as to what the actual injury is. WINNER via PINFALL: CHUCK LOVEMAN! Once Steve Realman has finally managed to stabilize his client, he begins screaming for a microphone. Mo and Chuck just watch as a mic is tossed into the ring and caught by Realman, getting ready to address Mo Love STEVE REALMAN: “Are you proud of what you’ve done?! ARE YOU?! This worthless sack of meat was just about to complete his transformation into looking like me, but NO! YOU JUST HAD TO COME IN HERE AND RUIN IT! So you want some pie? Do you?! Next week on Revolt, I want that white wannabe gangsta in this ring against The Sir. I want it!” Mo and Chuck look at one another before shrugging and Mo takes a mic of his own MO CHRONIC: “If you want some…COME…GET SOME!” The crowd erupts in excitement as Steve offers a smile of his own, something devious underneath his words STEVE REALMAN: “You just agreed…but I never said what kind of match. Next week. Mo Chronic. The Sir. Pie. Eating. Contest.” Mo’s opinion of the match hasn’t changed at all, but The Sir is smiling to the side, naming various types of pie as we go to a commercial break ------------------------------------------------- After commercial we find ourselves in a very "blinged out" lockeroom for lack of a better term. The walls are hot pink, while the lockers are a lighter shade of pink as well. The camera pans over to the end of the room where we see GIW new comer Christina May standing in front of a mirror fixing her lipstick. Her continues pouting of the lips is cut short by a few knocks coming from the door. CHRISTINA MAY: "Yeah, who is it?" Without warning, the doorknob turns and the door slightly opens. Out pops Johnny Hashke's head, with his signature goofy smile. Startled, she jumps back and drops her lipstick on the ground. She quickly tries to grab it, but Johnny rushes to grab it for her. Such a gentleman. However Christina doesn't see it that way as Johnny's hand meets on top of hers, he smiles and looks into her eyes. She scoffs and pulls her hand away in disgust. CHRISTINA MAY: "Ew!! Don't touch me! Just because we're partners tonight doesn't give you the right to barge in here!" Johnny points to the door. JOHNNY HASHKE: "But... uh... I knocked?" Christina puts her index finger in front of Johnny's face. CHRISTINA MAY: Whatever, it doesn't matter. What the hell do you want? Johnny's face brightens, he fixes his hair before speaking. JOHNNY HASHKE: Haha, well uhm, about last week... I totally get why you sacked me! No worries, it's a competitive sport, I understand. I just want you to know that there's no hard feelings heading into our tag match tonight! Johnny extends his arm, reaching out for a hand shake from his partner tonight. Christina May however doesn't seem too inclined to take it, she looks at his hand then back up to Hashke with a disgusted look on her face. CHRISTINA MAY: "Oh honey there is no way in hell that I'm touching your hand. I'm in it to win it, and if teaming with your dorky ass this week is somehow going to catapult me to super stardom - then I guess I'll have to live with it for a bit." Hashke nods his head, although he didn't get a handshake; that was enough. He smiles and goes to leave, but before leaving through the door he stops for a brief moment. JOHNNY HASHKE: "Hey, uhh, Christina?" Christina, who already started applying another layer of lipstick onto her lips, snaps at Johnny. CHRISTINA MAY: "What?!" Johnny takes a big gulp of air, finding the courage to speak up. JOHNNY HASHKE: "I was thinking maybe... after our match... you and I, we could y'know, head to the movies together. I heard Guardians of the Ga-" Christina angrily pushes Johnny out of her lockeroom and slams the door behind him. CHRISTINA MAY: "NO!" Christina locks the door so nobody else can interrupt. Turning herself back to facing the mirror, she notices a giant red smear across her cheek from the lipstick. We last see Christina May's fuming expression before cutting into the arena. QUOTE “Bad Girl’ by Pussycat Dolls hits and the boos are as in full force tonight as Christina May walks out from the back seemingly angry and ready to go in this one after her debut loss from last week. RILEY STEVENS: “We saw Johnny Hashke try to smooth things out with Christina tonight, didn't seem to have worked that well.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Let’s hope Hashke doesn't cost his team the victory, that idiot!” Walking up the steps and through the bottom two ropes May looks around at the crowd with a glare seeming annoyed to even be here as "Electric Worry” by Clutch hits and oh how quickly the fan’s tune changes bursting into cheers as the energetic Johnny Hashke runs onto the stage. The new comer walks down towards the ring looking at Christina May, he high fives the fans on the ramp as we get a glimpse of May glaring back at him seeming none to pleased. RILEY STEVENS: “This should be fun.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Poor Christina, she deserves better than this.” Hashke walks up into the ring with a smile perched on his face as he enters through the ropes and heads over to the corner where Christina currently stands walking past her and hoisting his arms high in the air getting more cheers as he looks back at May just to rub it in as his music fades. “I Move It” by Millionaires now hits to another loud boo from the crowd as this time it’s Barbie Sexton and Rose Gasm walking out from the back trying to show some team unity as they head to the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “In theory these two should be the better team but after last weeks battle royal, there is going to be some serious heat with Sinful Sexuality, Hashke, and May in the same ring again.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Yeah things may get ugly - uglier than Hashke's smile.” RILEY STEVENS: "What's your beef with him, Oliver?" OLIVER THIBODAUX: "I have a thing against people like him." RILEY STEVENS: "What?! How can you say that?" OLIVER THIBODAUX: "What? I hate happy people." Right away we had our first test of team unity as the two sides had to make a choice for who to start but Christina made it simple for Johnny, stepping out on her own allowing him to start on the other side they agreed it would be Sexton that started things off as these former opponents come head to head as the two circle around one another, finally going in for a lock up that seems to come to a stand still both attempt to over power each other, pushing one another back and forth before Hashke pushes Barbie back into the ropes bouncing her forward off of them, as Barbie hits off and comes back towards him, Hashke throws a Lariat that Barbie ducks under again hitting the ropes as this time Barbie Sexton gives Johnny no chance to think about it rushing him and clobbering him with a running elbow to the face dropping Johnny down to the mat. Gasm grins pulling her back to her feet as the the team of Sinful Sexuality seem to want to take advantage of her in the corner as she tags back in Barbie Sexton. Kick Out. Johnny runs across the ring, over towards Rose who is standing on the ring apron. RILEY STEVENS: “That wasn’t a smart idea at all!” Johnny slowly stands but an enraged Barbie Sexton now follows him to the outside. Johnny pulls Sexton back up, pulling her over to his corner tagging in Christina May who still seems pretty pissed at him as she reluctantly walks through the ropes and into the ring. Taking a break from getting a pin fall victory, Harlequin surprises the GIW audience with a sharpshooter that she locks in perfectly. The aggravated knee looks like it’s coming back to haunt CDE as Harlequin doesn’t let up the hold. Several seconds pass by as it looks like Erns is coming close to tapping out, but he manages to use his upper body strength to carry him over to grabbing onto the bottom ring rope. Not wanting to give him a break, Harley keeps the sharpshooter locked in until the referee reaches a four count. Harlequin tries for another sharpshooter, but Christopher is quick enough to kick her hands away. Going to lift up CDE proves to be a mistake for Harlequin as the veteran is able to get her on the mat with a double leg takedown; making eye contact with M. Tech and shouting, “This is how you do it!” Instead of going for some mounted punches, Erns lifts his opponent up and takes Harlequin down with a spine buster. Unfortunately for Harlequin, the Deep South Champion is far from finished as he lifts her up again, and holds on in a vertical suplex position. Showing off his brute strength; CDE parades around the ring with the Undisputed Champion above him as all of the blood begins to rush to her head. Several seconds pass by before Erns drops the champ to the mat and hooks her leg 1… 2… KICK OUT! OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Can we test her for steroids too?!" Erns rolls his eyes as he looks at his opponent; almost as if he’s offended that she kicked out. Christopher stands up and steps back to stalk his wounded prey. Again, Harlequin finds herself on her knees in prime position for a CDE boot, but before the Deep South Champ can start off again, he retrains himself as he watches Harlequin make the leap to tag in M. Tech. Still feeling the effects of hitting the turnbuckle, but he’s not going to let that hold him back from saving his teammate. M. Tech calls for Erns to meet him in the middle of the ring and surprisingly, Christopher agrees much to the dismay of his leader. In the center of the ring, the two men lock up in an even clinch formation. Both men trade body blows back and forth until M. Tech transitions from a single collar tie to a Muay Thai clinch. In control now, Christopher tries to break free, but to no luck as Michaud hits Erns with a skull shattering Muay Thai knee that rocks the Deep South Champion. A second knee sends Erns flying back into his corner as Lynch watches intently and slyly taps the back of his partner. Michaud runs toward the corner, but to his surprise; Christopher is ready with a thunderous European uppercut that nearly knocks him out of his ankle wraps. RILEY STEVENS "Did...did Lynch make a tag?!" OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Oh, hell yes he did! He made a tag! The referee signaled a clean tag!" Harlequin begins slapping the top of her turnbuckle, calling for Michaud to make the quick tag. And Christopher taking the time to taunt the crowd gives him the small slot to make the hot tag. Priming herself on the apron; waiting for CDE to turn around. As his taunting continues, Michael Lynch steps into the ring just as Erns turns around to continue his assault on M. Tech Michaud. Much to his surprise, he’s only met with Harlequin launching off the top ring rope with the Joke’s On You! A 720 DDT sends the crowd to their feet in excitement as Harlequin gets up to make the pin, but suddenly Michael Lynch comes behind her with The Ram's Horn! The ripcord roaring elbow crashes into the back of Harlequin’s skull and drops her to the mat. The air in the building is sucked out as Michael Lynch falls on top of the Undisputed Champion RILEY STEVENS "No! This isn't fair!" OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Wasn't it you who said that all was fair in love and war?!" 1… 2… 3! RILEY STEVENS "No! Not like this!" The crowd voices their opinion for the finish quickly as Michael Lynch just stands over the fallen body of Harlequin. The referee begins to take some heat over the end result, but he informs everyone that there was a clean tag made. Lynch turns around to help his disciple up to his feet; taking a moment to embrace in a proud hug while the entire arena vocalizes their hate for these two. WINNERS via PINFALL: MICHAEL LYNCH & CHRISTOPHER DARREN ERNS! The hectic match has finally come to an end as Michael Lynch stands over the fallen body of the Undisputed Champion, Harlequin. Elsewhere, Christopher Darren Erns carries in the body of M. Tech Michaud and drops him to the mat. Michael steps out of the ring and grabs onto both championships and heads back into the ring. Holding up the Deep South Championship; the title Michael Lynch once claimed as his own, but instead of giving it over to the rightful owner, Lynch holds back. Instead, Michael Lynch hands Christopher Darren Erns the Undisputed Championship and raises his hand. MICHAEL LYNCH: “THIS IS WHAT YOU WANT!” Michael shouts at the hard camera while he lets Christopher Darren Erns stand over the fallen bodies. THE MESSIAH HAS SPOKEN!” The last shot of the show is Michael Lynch applauding his follower while Christopher Darren Erns poses with both titles over M. Tech Michaud and The Harlequin.
