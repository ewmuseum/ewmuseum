Pre-show, 19 April 2015

19 April 2015
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This match featured two of GIW’s newest attractions, Journeyman Phillips and hush. Because there were only two entrants this week, the open battle royal took the form a singles match. Journeyman got the quick upperhand on hush, despite some mind games being played at the beginning of the match by the mysterious wrestler. Journeyman was far more experienced, however, and was able to see through the games and keep hush grounded. Towards the middle of the match, hush began to build momentum as the fans booed him loudly. That all changed when hush went for a diving crossbody and was caught in mid air by the stronger Journeyman, and then slammed to the floor. Journeyman looked to the top rope going for his finishing move, The Fall From Grace, a diving headbutt… but hush rolled out of the ring and climbed straight underneath it. Journeyman was perplexed, but soon realized where his opponent was when hush leveled him with a hard blow to the back with a steel chair. WHOMP! WHOMP! WHOMP! Three more slaps of the chair, then hush slammed it to the floor. The referee had long since called for the DQ, but hush made his statement. WINNER: JOURNEYMAN PHILLIPS QUOTE Brotree Harper was in peak form tonight as he took on the veteran Ryan “Kick” Smith. Galveston’s favorite son, which was totally Brody’s nickname and not Sammy Glasgo’s cruised through the match without breaking a sweat due to Ryan Smith. He did still sweat quite a bit just because he wasn’t used to moving so much. About halfway through the match, Brody passed out and landed right in the center of the ring, but the referee decided this was not a submission and allowed Brody to continue to wrestle despite the obvious medical issues at hand. The referee was fired, for the record. Brody won the match when he locked Ryan Smith into an expertly applied hammerlock, to which Ryan Smith had no choice but to tap out. It was an almost flawless victory for our resident flora as spring fades to summer. WINNER: BROTREE HARPER
