Revolt, 5 July 2015

GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS SUNDAY NIGHT REVOLT 7/5/15 FROM KAUFFMAN ARENA IN GALVESTON, TEXAS BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! Two weeks away from Moment of Glory V and one night removed from Independence Day, fireworks stream into the night sky above Kauffman Arena. The fans know that the show is about to begin when they hear the bangs from a distance and begin to wave around their signs – “Duff’s Revenge”, “We Want Bronxy”, and “Down With Alexum”. After a while of panning through the crowd, the camera settles in on the announce table. SARAH SHARP: "Ladies and gentlemen, welcome to another episode of Sunday Night Revolt! I’m Sarah Sharp joined alongside Oliver Thibodaux, and we are set to call the action just two weeks away from the night when stars are made – Moment of Glory." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "You can feel the intensity in the air, Sharpie! We have one hell of a show planned for you guys tonight – three epic collisions are set to take place tonight. We have Alexis Terry and Alioth Starre in one on one action, before that we have the Undisputed Champion Chris Callum facing a returning Wake Walker, and then to top it all off, the finals of the Deep South Championship Number One Contender tournament will take place in the main event. Jessica Tendonin and Terry McKenna have their chance to shine!" SARAH SHARP: "The winner of that match goes on to face The Sir at Moment of Glory with the title on the line, so this match is huge for both of these competitors, each of whom are still looking to pick up their first title belt here in GIW. The Sir will be in action tonight against two of the men who failed to qualify for the finals – Johnny Violence and Duff Briggs. They are both going to want to put their names back in the tile picture going forward." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Moment of Glory is beginning to take shape, Sarah! There are still quite a few question marks left to resolve, and I think this week will go a long way in resolving them." ”Starre-Crossed” by NC begins to play as the fans welcome Alioth Starre onto the ramp with a mixed reaction. He will be out to address the crowd at the beginning of this show. SARAH SHARP: "Here comes the number one contender for the Undisputed Championship – the Hall of Famer, Sole Survivor, and one half of the main event picture of Moment of Glory." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "I’m sure he has quite a bit to say, Sarah. Last week he had one hell of a match with Madison Seton, and it was Chris Callum who, inexplicably, helped Alioth win the match. I bet the number one contender wants to get to the bottom of that right here, right now." Alioth slid through the ropes carefully, microphone in hand. When his music began to die down, he took his place in the middle of the ring, ready to speak. He watched the crowd as they cheered and jeered him. The camera panned the arena for their reactions and then back on him in the ring. When the sound simmered down some, he lifted the microphone and opened Revolt with a monologue. ALIOTH STARRE: “Galveston Island, Texas. We are in the arena with a misspelled name. Welcome one, and all; ladies and gentlewomen, I hope you all have come to enjoy another monotonous episode of Revolt!” He introduced. “My name, as you know, and for those just tuning in, as well as those joining us through the open battle royal contest, my name is Alioth Starre. I became the longest reigning Undisputed Champion for a record one-hundred and fifty-five days. Split, of course, between two title victories. That doesn’t matter though, not at all! What matters is who I’ve lost that title to.” He let himself pause for a moment when the crowd began to chant mixed responses. Some shouted for Ace Baldwin, others for Alexis Terry. Both were right, but only one was what he wanted to talk about. He filled them in on which part of the crowd got it right. ALIOTH STARRE: “Alexis Terry took my last title run with the Undisputed Championship after, quite literally, a month of holding it for the second time. Back then I applauded her efforts. At times we were the best of friends; and now we are the worst of friends. There is something we do for our friends, and that is not admitting their worst qualities to their faces. We don’t want to betray them and hurt their emotions.” He continued, “for the few times Alexis Terry and I met in the ring as we will tonight, I’ve usually stayed my hand for these fists weren’t meant to hurt the ones I love, but tonight, that’s all going to change.” Alioth stopped the second the crowd seemed to cheer at his promise to withhold no punches from Alexis Terry. He held out a hand to stop them and deterred their chants. ALIOTH STARRE: “Please, enough with the cacophony of stupidity. I am not your champion; never again will Alioth Starre be a champion of the people. At one point I used to say that we are here because of all of you,” he said as the camera panned around the crowds. “However, I’ve learned that if you weren’t here, I’d still be in a ring somewhere holding people down being cheered; jeered; loved; and hated. You are all just leeches latching on to whatever we want you to. You chant when we tell you to, and boo when we want you to. You guys are completely automated and absolutely irrelevant!” This killed the momentum of their cheering, and a chorus of boos followed. He returned them a wry smile. ALIOTH STARRE: “Alexis Terry, the thing I never told you, because I used to think that we were such good friends is that you are the most boring; bland, and pathetic person in this industry. Sure, you’re pretty damn successful in everything you do, but usually that’s because someone else is making sure of that. Aspen Chaud here, or whoever is the current power. You are a shadow. You are just like all of these insects in the crowd latching on to whatever the spotlight’s on today just to get a foot in on it! You are nothing against me, someone that has worked his arse off from the moment I stepped foot of the plane from London to this very moment, because if you notice, Terry, it is Alioth Starre versus Chris Callum at Moment of Glory. In fact, Terry, it’s typically Alioth Starre in the main event at Moment of Glory, not you. I rise to every occasion, you just hide behind the occasional champion.” He stepped off of the ropes and moved toward the center of the ring as he spoke. ALIOTH STARRE: “You are a drunken whore that has to be carried every step of the way, and pulled through every match by your opponent. You only win when that person is tired of having to deal with your political garbage, or too tired carrying the dead weight of your useless body. Tonight, I’m going to treat you like who you really are; a speed bump. You are a halter of people who deserve more than you and have done better than you! Tonight, I’m going to dust my hands of our friendship, and pick the botfly of your parasitic desires and flick you into the trash where you belong. You are just a distraction from Chris Callum.” ALIOTH STARRE: “That leads me now to my next topic; The Trio of Power in Galveston Island Wrestling. Aspen Chaud, Alexis Terry, and Chris Callum. Two of them are not like one and one of the two is just a shadow of the first. I’ve said my piece about that dumb, monotonous Dodo; but what really pissed me off is that she and Aspen had to latch on to Chris Callum!” The crowd began to boo heavily at Callum’s name, but Alioth interrupted them. ALIOTH STARRE: “Shut up! You liked him before! We told you to cheer and then you got bored of it and only then did you start to turn on him! Chris Callum never turned on the crowd, you turned on him!” He added, “Callum represents true competition. I remember who beat me, because I was nearly unstoppable. You’ll remember who beat him, because he is still your Superman. I know I will take him out at Moment of Glory, but there is still that shred of doubt in my mind that is certain he has just as much a chance against me. Alioth and Callum represents true competitiveness in this sport. Neither of us abused the powers that be to get where we are today. I didn’t use Aspen Chaud, Blake Beckett, Sara Sharp; no one! They used us! It’s because Aspen Chaud and Alexis Terry don’t know how to keep your attention any better than Callum personality, that they leech onto him! Because what they do isn’t cutting it anymore, but yet you still tune in to see if Callum gets beat on Revolt! You still tune in to see me slowly creep forth with victory after victory until Moment of Glory and this growing suspense between who will win,keeping your eyes glued to the screen, needs to be cut! Needs its final resolution! That’s why I came back, because Callum is the true champion I’ve always searched for all my time in this company to face! You don’t fabricate what he and I have and who we are; that’s why Alexis has to hide in the spotlight and Aspen has to steal it from the champion. They aren’t important anymore, and they aren’t interesting. Tonight, I’m going to prove that to you by destroying Alexis Terry’s chance of hindering Callum any further. Tonight, I’m going to force Aspen Chaud to tap the mat when I pin her little mini-me, or call the bell when Terry taps out. I’m going to break the machine by throwing the proverbial wrench in it so when Moment of Glory comes, you people get to see what we are really about and get the show you really came here for: Alioth Starre versus Chris Callum. No one else. No stipulations involving anyone. Just us and the Undisputed Championship Title. I came to bring legitimacy back to Galveston side by side with my greatest opponent and rival.” “Starre-Crossed” picked up again as Alioth Starre began to make his exit. We fade to the back now where Aspen Chaud, Chris Callum, and Alexis Terry were standing around a monitor in Aspen’s office. A grim look was washed over Chris’ face while Aspen and Alexis exchanged a glance. ASPEN CHAUD: "Bless him. He has no idea what is coming to him tonight." Alexis snickered before she spoke. ALEXIS TERRY: "I think tonight is as good of a night as any to break out the trusty brass knuckles." She crossed her arms, following Alioth with her eyes as he disappeared from the screen. ALEXIS TERRY: "That would wipe the smug look off of his face. You know all about that, don’t you, Chris?" Alexis nudged Callum with her elbow, but his eyes were transfixed towards the screen. Aspen spoke, if only to break the tension between the two. ASPEN CHAUD: "He’s too sure of himself. I don’t’ think he will ever be able to understand the storm that tracks down those that stand in our way. He’ll walk out of Kauffman tonight, but not in one piece." She turned to Chris who’s eyes were still locked on the screen as if they could spot Alioth’s specter somewhere in the distance. ASPEN CHAUD: "Chris, talk to me." He did, quietly, gripping the title belt over his shoulder. CHRIS CALLUM: "He doubts me. He should." Quickly, he turned towards Aspen and Alexis. CHRIS CALLUM: "I ruled this company for months. I was the man to beat. I was the face of this brand. The past three weeks, I haven’t won a match without some kind of interference." He exhaled loudly, only making Aspen’s anxiety worse. He ran his hand over his eyes. CHRIS CALLUM: "That ends tonight. Aspen, I’m going to crush Wake Walker. Don’t worry. But I’m going to do it on my own." With that, he exited, leaving Aspen’s arm outstretched towards him. ASPEN CHAUD: "Chris…" Alexis rolled her eyes, speaking to Aspen’s back. ALEXIS TERRY: "Well, I’m going to let him show me up. I’ll crush Alioth tonight too, on my own." Aspen swiveled around quickly. ASPEN CHAUD: "This is not the plan we agreed to." Terry shrugged. ALEXIS TERRY: "Who cares? In fact, I’m going to go become a two time TV Champion. Watch this…" Alexis exited quickly, leaving the camera on Aspen Chaud with a concerned look on her face as we cut to commercial break. QUOTE Alexis Terry, Johnny VIolence, and Kevin Marshall are already in the ring. Violence is running the ropes, Terry is crouched in the far right corner with her head hung low, and K-Mart is sitting on top of the turnbuckle closest to the ring steps. Marshall’s eyes are fixed on Alexis, he never takes his eyes off of her. RUBY REYNOLDS: “The following match is set for one fall and is for the GIW Television Championship. From Dallas-Fort Worth, Texas, the reigning and defending Television Champion, Brody Lee Prince!” Brody and his two managers emerge from the back. The Von Grants flank their client as he makes his way lazily to the ring. The gold of the TV Title glimmers under the bright stage lights. In the ring Marshall finally takes his eyes off Alexis as all three challengers move to opposite corners. Brody climbs up the apron while his managers move to ringside. SARAH SHARP: “This is the best turnout we’ve had in a TV title match in weeks. I think it’s obvious that Alexis is the safe choice to win.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Look, I love Alexis but BLP is going to retain. He has both of the Von Grants at his disposal. No way he leaves without the belt tonight.” SARAH SHARP: “Well if that’s the case we might as well end the match right now. Why bother having a contest at iff if BLP’s managers are just going to interfere?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Now you’re getting it Sarah. I’m with you, lets call this whole thing off and go for pizza.” SARAH SHARP: ... The referee signals for the bell and K-Mart is the first to strike. Marshall explodes out of his corner and goes after Alexis. Terry sees him coming though and manages to sidestep him. A clubbing blow to the back of the neck followed by a Snap Suplex removes K-Mart from the equation. Meanwhile Brody Lee has rolled out of the ring and is consulting with his managers. Johnny Violence catapults himself over the top rope and takes out all three. The Von Grants, Brody Lee, and Johnny Violence are all down and out at ringside. SARAH SHARP: “Alexis is the only person left standing in the ring. I knew she was the favorite to win but I didn’t expect her to pull ahead so early.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “This match isn’t over yet.” Alexis Terry takes disingenuous bow over K-Mart’s body. However, while she is bent over, Marshall grabs her by the head and neck and rolls her up. Terry manages to kick out at two but is visibly shaken by almost losing the match. Marshal holds up two fingers a few millimeters apart and mouths the words, “this close”. Further enraged, Terry charges at Marshall and the two lock up. Marshall slips to the from and hits a Snapmare. He follows that up with a running Dropkick to the back of the head. Terry is down. SARAH SHARP: “Marshall is bringing it here tonight. He want’s that gold almost as much as he hates Alexis.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “She did ruin his life. Convinced him to turn his back on the Marshall family, and for what? Nothing! I’d be pissed off too.” Marshall goes for a pin just as Johnny VIolence rolls back into the ring. J-Vi dives into the K-Mart to break up the pin. Violence gets Terry to her feet and hits his finisher, “Glory Fades” connects. K-Mart blindsides Johnny with a Superkick. Johnny stumbles back and falls out of the ring. Brody Lee slides in and comes at Marshall with a “Tooth Loosener”. Marshall ducks, Brody hits the ropes and runs right into a Superkick! Marshall goes for the cover! 1 2 3 DING! DING! DING! RUBY REYNOLDS: Your winner and new GIW Television Champion, Kevin Marshall! K-Mart grabs the title from the referee and hoists it above his head. The crowd isn’t sure if they should cheer or boo. Marshall rolls out of the ring with his title still in hand and parades it around ringside. Alexis and J-Vi limp to the back while Brody Lee and his managers plot for the next match. Eventually, Kevin Marshall collapses against the ring steps, hugging and kissing his title belt. SARAH SHARP: “What an upset! No one was expecting Kevin Marshall to walk out a champion tonight. Just goes to show that anything can happen on GIW Revolt! We’ll be back with more wrestling action after a short word from our sponsors.” WINNER: KEVIN MARSHALL (NEW GIW TELEVISION CHAMPION) After the break, we cut to the GIW interview lounge. ZACHARY PAGONIS: "Ladies and gentlemen, please welcome my guest at this time, Johnny Violence." The camera zoomed out to reveal The Anarchist himself. ZACHARY PAGONIS: "Johnny, you just tried and failed once again to recapture your GIW Television Championship. That is three championship opportunities gone in two weeks. What is going through your head right now?" Johnny was fuming, clearly. He growled an answer back towards Pagonis. JOHNNY VIOLENCE: "What’s going through my head? Jesus, Zach. What a stupid question. I’m pissed. I’m really pissed. But that’s fine. I’m not going to dwell on, once again, losing a match without getting pinned. Instead, I’m going to focus all of my energy on two things before this night is over – beating The Sir and Duff Briggs, and then making sure Jessica Tendonin walks out as the number one contender for the Deep South Championship." Timidly, Pagonis continues the interview. ZACHARY PAGONIS: "How do you think Terry McKenna feels about that?" Violence is dumbfounded. JOHNNY VIOLENCE: "I don’t care?! I laid his ass out last week, and his old man friend too. Terry McKenna isn’t going to get close to that title shot. Jessica Tendonin is the next Deep South Champion." The distant pitter patter of crutches can be heard from a distance as Journeyman Phillips enters the shot. JOURNEYMAN PHILLIPS: "Don’t you think that should be sorted out in the ring? I know I think so. I know Terry thinks so." Violence is attempting to hold back his rage as he addresses the veteran. JOHNNY VIOLENCE: "Listen man, if you interrupt me one more time, I’ll make sure you never walk again." Journeyman shook his head. JOURNEYMAN PHILLIPS: "Well I’m not here to chat about the weather with you Johnny. I don’t need to explain that if anything funny goes down tonight in the main event, I’ll be there to… clear things up." Johnny looked down at the crutches and chuckled. JOHNNY VIOLENCE: "What are you going to do? Limp down there? What a joke." JOURNEYMAN PHILLIPS: "I’m quicker than I look. But you’ll find that out soon enough." JOHNNY VIOLENCE: "What?" Journeyman smiled wide. JOURNEYMAN PHILLIPS: "I just got out of Aspen’s office, and everything is in place. You and I are going to have a match at Moment of Glory. And I don’t know about you, but I’m looking forward to it." Johnny shook his head. JOHNNY VIOLENCE: "You’re on crutches." JOURNEYMAN PHILLIPS: "Worry about yourself for once." Journeyman turned to leave, but span back around on his crutch. JOURNEYMAN PHILLIPS: "And hey, you better lay off the weed at the biggest show of the year. Hardly professional, Johnny." We fade down to the ring with Violence staring at Journeyman as he exits. --- Backstage, Paul Marshall is seen texting on his phone. A few seconds later, Wake Walker walks behind him and pats him on the pat a couple of times. Marshall looks at Walker. Before he can tell Walker not to touch him, Walker speaks. WAKE WALKER: “Ah, what a wonderful night it is. Everything has gone back to normal. Ever since Aspen made that match with me and Callum … I just have this feeling that I’ll make a great main-eventer, just like I used to.” PAUL MARSHALL: “That’s great to hear you’re optimistic, Walker…. But shouldn’t you be getting ready for your match right now?” WAKE WALKER: “Ah, Marshall, I’m a very calm man … I always try to be very lax about everything, I mean … that’s what people know me for, right? And I mean … considering I’m the main event tonight, I think it’s a little early to start getting ready for that match, don’t you think?” PAUL MARSHALL: “What? Just because Callum is the champion doesn’t mean that every single one of his matches is going to be a main-event! In fact, Walker, you’re dead center in the middle of the card! Normally, Callum would main-event but considering he’s facing YOU tonight, they had to move him down a few notches.” WAKE WALKER: “Haha, don’t be silly Marshall. Walker versus Callum? I mean, really? You’d have to be a dumb son of a bitch to think that’s a midcard match. … Marshall? You were just joking about this whole thing, right?” PAUL MARSHALL: “No. I can tell you with hundred percent certainty that you will not be main-eventing tonight. So, you better get ready … your match is coming up really soon.” WAKE WALKER: “ARE YOU KIDDING ME RIGHT NOW?! ME NOT IN THE MAIN-EVENT? I HAVE NEVER BEEN SO INSULTED IN MY LIFE. WHO DOES ASPEN THINK SHE IS?! HUH?! WHO THE HELL DOES SHE THINK SHE IS?! I’M GOING RIGHT NOW TO HER OFFICE AND I’M GOING TO SHOW HER A PIECE OF MY MIND.” PAUL MARSHALL: “No, Walker. If you do that, I’ll be the first to make sure you get fired.” Wake looks at Paul for a few seconds, clenches his fists and walks away very angrily. In the distance, we can hear Walker screaming. We cut down to the ring. QUOTE RUBY REYNOLDS: ”Ladies and gentlemen, the following match is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first, from Dallas-Fort Worth, Texas. The one and only Mr. Texas, Brooooody Leeeeee Prinnnnnnce!” Brody is already in the ring when his music begins to play over the sound system. He never bothered going to the back after the television title match ended. Starla and Max Von Grant are at ring side coaching their client while he is hanging over the turnbuckle. Max takes a glass jar out of his jacket and hands it to Brody. Smiling, Brody Lee Prince parades around the ring with the glass bottle held high above his head. SARAH SHARP: ”I don't get it, what's so special about a jar?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: ”It's not the jar that's special, Sarah. It's what's inside the jar that Brody is proud of. That right there, is Kevin Marshall's tooth.” SARAH SHARP: ”Well then, it looks like BLP is trying to psych Marshal out before the match even starts.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: ”I don't think Brody is smart enough to think up something like that. He's just being an asshole, and I love it!” Brody's celebration with Kevin Marshall's tooth is cut short when “Fat Bottom Girls” by Queen replaces Brody's own music. Brody screws up his face in dissatisfaction and tosses the jar back to Max haphazardly. RUBY REYNOLDS: ”And now, weighing in at 219 pounds, from Corpus Christi, and the new GIW Television Champion, Keeeevin Marrrrrshallllll!” Kevin Marshall is still waiting, crouched beside the ring stair. When his music hits, he runs back up the ramp with the TV title around his waist. Marshall tries to recreate his previous entrance as best he can. He runs back to the ring at breakneck speed. He passes by the throngs of fans along the ramp like a man possessed. Marshall slides into the ring and immediately shoots in on Brody, taking the man out at the legs. The two scuffle on the ground and exchange a few punches before the referee is able to separate them. SARAH SHARP: ”Kevin Marshall already took the TV Title off Brody and now he's looking to get his tooth back too. I have a feeling this could be a hellacious match.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: ”What else can you expect? MArshall is tired of being looked down upon. He's a champion now. Brody Lee knock out Marshall's tooth and has been carrying it around in a jar. That's disrespectful, and you don't disrespect a champion. I'd be looking for pay back if I were Marshall.” SARAH SHARP: ”I think it's more than that. Kevin brought shame to the Marshall name when he Superkicked Gavin Achilles a few weeks back. I think he might see this as a way to redeem himself.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: ”What ever Kevin Marshall's motivation is, it can only mean bad news for Brody Lee Prince.” DING! DING! DING! The referee signals for the bell and both men leap into action. Marshall lunges for Prince but misses. BLP hold his hands to his head so they look like antlers and sticks out his tongue. If Max lays his hands on Kevin Marshall this whole thing is over.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: ”Hey now, all I said was we've seen the strategy before. I never said it was a good strategy.” SARAH SHARP: ”Touche.” Max is trying to get in Kevin Marshall's face but the referee isn't having any of it. The veteran official keeps his body between the two opponents and forces them apart. The ref continues to force Max back against the ropes and then orders him out of the ring. However, while he is distracted with one Von Grant, the other is busy at work. Starla climbs up on the apron and slaps K-Mart as hard as she can across his face. Kevin goes down while Starla drops off the apron and pretends like nothing happened. OLIVER THIBODAUX: ”But that on the other hand, that was a brilliant strategy. I swear it's like Brody Lee Prince is some sort of idiot savant. He's like one of those kids that eat paste but's also real good at math. Except he's really good at cheating, ya know what I mean?” SARAH SHARP: ”I think you're giving Brody too much credit. I'm not entirely convinced he can tie his own shoes without one of the Von Grants there to help him.” While Kevin is still down, Brody Lee Prince rolls back into the ring. Brody starts to put the boots to the downed Marshall with ruthless ferocity. The Von Grants cheer from ringside while their client stomps the vigor out of his opponent. Brody goes to the well one too many times as Kevin Marshall manages to pick a leg. Brody goes tits up and Marshall capitalizes by locking in a Single-leg Boston Crab. The referee slides into action and checks on Brody Lee. Meanwhile at ringside, The Von Grants are freaking out, pulling their own hair, and screaming at one another. SARAH SHARP: ”Looks like Brody's team is coming apart at the seams.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: ”Don't count the boy out yet, Sarah. I'm sure he has another trick or two left up his sleeve.” SARAH SHARP: ”No, it's over, and besides, Brody doesn't even have sleeves." OLIVER THIBODAUX: ”Fine, He has a trick up his tights. Either way, this ain't over yet.” Once again it's Starla Von Grant that climbs up the apron to distract the referee. It works like a charm. The official has no choice but to turn his attention to Starla. Meanwhile, Brody's resolve has worn thin and he is tapping out like a mad man, but the ref is too busy with Starla to make the call. Stealthily, Max Von Grant slides the jar containing, K-Mart's tooth into the ring. Brody manages to get his hand around the jar. BLP contorts his body so that he can smash K-Mart across the back of the head with the jar. Kevin Marshall goes limp and slinks forward. Brody uses his boot to quickly kick the pieces of broken glass out of the ring before Starla drops off the apron. OLIVER THIBODAUX: ”I told you, Sarah! I told you, Brody was still in this!” SARAH SHARP: ”And I told you he would be up a creek without the Von Grants. Looks like we're both right.” Brody falls on K-Mart with malice intent. The would be cowboy starts to rain down punches on the near comatose Kevin Marshall. Brody Lee follows up the mounted punches with a Side Slam and a Big Boot that leaves K-Mart twisted up in the ropes. Then Brody Lee charges, looking to send Kevin Marshall to the outside with a Clothesline. But no! Kevin Marshall drops and pulls the ropes down with him. Brody goes spilling over the top rope and crashes onto the broken glass at ringside. Both Von Grants rush to Brody's aid. SARAH SHARP: ”And just like that, Kevin Marshall is back in this thing.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: ”Poor Brody. His back is going to be shredded to ribbons.” SARAH SHARP: ”Poor Brody? Are you serious? He broke a jar over Marshall's head?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “He had no choice. K-Mart was going to break his leg off. You're blaming the victim Sarah and I'm not going to stand for it.” Brody manages to get back to his feet as the ref's count hits eight. The Von Grants help roll their client back into the ring. Kevin Marshall immediately goes to work. First he hits a Shinning Wizard that lays Brody out in the center of the ring. Then Marshall stalks his victim and waits for Brody to get back to his feet. Discuss Forearm connects! That's followed up by a series of stiff stomps. K-Mart works Brody over a little more before Irish-whipping him to the corner. A Bronco Buster adds insult to injury as Kevin Marshall's pelvis grinds into Brody's face. Dazed and confused, Brody Lee stumbles out of the corner where a waiting Kevin Marshall nails him with a Superkick! Marshall shoots in and hooks both legs. SARAH SHARP: ”This is it folks, it's all over!” 1 2 … No! Both Starla and Max Von Grant are up on the apron trying to distract the ref. Utter chaos ensues. The ref turns his attention to the Von Grants, while Brody Lee Prince pokes Kevin Marshall in the eye, and then just when you think things can't get any more out of hand… “More Than Meets The Eye” begins to play through the arena speakers. George Stark rushes the ring and nails both Von Grants with a clothesline when they jump down off the ring apron. Back in the ring, Kevin Marshall is staggering around blind and about to walk right into a “Tooth Loosener”. But No! This time it's George on the apron with a distraction.
