Extreme TV, 9 June 2011

9 June 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: E X T R E M E T V QUOTE Opener The opening match as per usual was the Open Battle Royal which this week featured quite a few stars. The most notable ones were Anthony Brenske a deaf star, Damien Dunn a member of The Transcendence, and Bam Isaac Glazer an Indy rap star a rather big cast of characters. Also involved were Jack Johnson returning after an unsuccessful stint at everything else he did in his life as well as local talent Jim. In total five stars, the match started quickly and it was Jim who was tossed out first at the hands of Brenske showing off his strength. Next up was Jack Johnson, showing yet again why he was such a failure when Bam Isaac Glazer took him over with a clothesline. The last three had quite a war, but it was Bam Isaac that took a risk going top rope. Brenske pushed him over the top and out of the ring, but a sneaky Dunn tossed him over the top to win the match. After the match it was announced that with this win Damien Dunn had Qualified for the Sole Survivor Match at ‘Category 5’. A huge reward for a new guy and very good match to start the night. WINNER: DAMIEN DUNN QUOTE Mid-Card Match To say that the Ninja Strike Force were the most loved in this place would be an understatement. The fans here loved them and it almost seemed like they were there to see them specifically. Tonight they were met with the challenge of taking on The Board. The fans pretty much didn’t give a damn about them. In the match it was all Ninja Strike Force. From the beginning to the end the fans were on their feet cheering them on. In the end it was a ‘Shell Shock’ Double Superkick that took Nathan out of the match. They then followed it up with a ‘Higher Heights’ Double Moonsaults to both men, pinning Scott as the match came to a close. Just another win for the Ninja Strike Force whom continued their success. WINNERS: NINJA STRIKE FORCE [Colby Hunter & Jamal Mitchell] QUOTE Main Event The final match witnessed the return of Chris Armstrong, teaming with Taryn McGrath. It was Chris that had the people talking. But it was The Killers that shut them all up. Rather then a triumphant return we witnessed a beating. The Killers crushed him, and held nothing back against McGrath. Seeming to be completely back on the same page the two worked together perfectly. In the end Brody crushed Taryn over the top with a Clothesline then turned around lifting up Chris with ‘The Killer Move’ a Flapjack DDT! The crowd were in silence, the hype train had been derailed as Brody covered him for the 1, 2, 3. Picking up another win for the duo here on ETV. WINNERS: THE KILLERS [Brody Harper & Nelson Fielder]
