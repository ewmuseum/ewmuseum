GIW — Card

Revolt, 2 June 2011

2 June 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: T H U R S D A Y N I G H T R E V O L T Who's gonna teach you how to dance? Who's gonna show you how to fly? Who's gonna call you on the lame-dope-smoking, Slackin' little sucker you are? BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! As the pyro goes off around the Seawall Boulevard Entertainment Complex the fans are in an uproar. It is as usual full to the brim, though this week is a little different because it is full due to the fact that admission was free this week. Simply because on a short week, it meant a short show. The camera pans around the crowd, all loving being shown on the camera as finally we wind up in the ring where Blake Beckett is standing at the ready with a microphone in hand. RILEY STEVENS: “Let’s see what the Boss has to say?” As the crowd dies down so that he can address them, he raises the microphone up to his mouth. BLAKE BECKETT: “As many of you know at ‘Breakdown’, Aspen tried her best to try and sneak back in after stating right here in this ring that she was leaving. So…being the guy I am I kept her out. Told her that to get her job back…she’d have to do something here tonight. What I told her she’d have to do…was apologize. Not to me…not to the guys in the back…to you people.” Blake has a big grin on his face, the two had never gotten along and knowing that she would hate doing this would only make it better for him. BLAKE BECKETT: “So Aspen…come on out.” The crowd starts to boo even before her music hits, with "Now I'm That Bitch" by Livvi Franc echoing through the complex as the fans throw every possible insult at her as out she comes wearing a gold dress and big scowl. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “This is just wrong, she doesn’t owe anyone an apology.” Walking towards the ring, she is shaking her head as if what he said has just put her into a terrible mood. Walking up the steps, she steps over the bottom rope and into the ring walking across being handed a microphone by someone at ringside quickly turning and talking at Blake. ASPEN CHAUD: “REALLY? You think I am going to apologize to these…fat…ugly…ungrateful fans? You must be delusional. When did I ever give you a reason to believe that would be the case? So…no thank you.” The fans boo her the moment she began to speak as Blake chuckles to himself rubbing his chin with a grin. BLAKE BECKETT: “See that’s the thing this isn’t an option…This is me telling you to do something that if you don’t do…your not getting your job back.” The crowd cheers, that being the ideal situation to them as she stares at him beginning to get angrier. ASPEN CHAUD: “I apologize!” The crowd boos, her less then sincere apology as Blake turns to the crowd for their thoughts as they begin a “We can’t hear you!” chant as Beckett turns back to Aspen pointing out at them. ASPEN CHAUD: “I SAID I APOLOGIZE!” Angrily she lowers the microphone as the chant continues; finally Blake begins to shush them as he raises the mic back up. BLAKE BECKETT: “You know…if you really want to prove that you’re sorry. You’ll buy all these people a free drink right?” This gets a cheap pop from the crowd who seem eager to get their hands on a free pop. ASPEN CHAUD: “Fine…” She says this very quickly, angrily glaring at Blake who has a smile on his face stepping out of the ring and walking over to a fan at ringside. BLAKE BECKETT: “Do you want anything else?” He leans over so that the man can whisper him something, he is a rather large fan wearing as you’d expect a Wake Walker t-shirt cause he’s an original fan. BLAKE BECKETT: “He’d like…a kiss.” Aspen’s jaw drops as the fans cheer, as Blake grins. She shakes her head no, but Beckett doesn’t let up standing there waiting. Finally she walks over to the ropes, walking down the steps and over to where Blake and the fans are standing. BLAKE BECKETT: “Lay it on him.” Aspen seems prepared to throw up, as the fan puckers up. Looking around Blake tells her to go ahead. Quickly she leans in and gives him a peck on his cheek, turning and quickly bending over prepared to puke. BLAKE BECKETT: “You know what…I think you’ve done enough to get your spot back on the roster. We’d actually never released you but this was just as fun.” Aspen gets up, brushing some hair out of her face as she turns quickly running towards the back to perhaps brush her teeth as Blake shakes his head grinning. BLAKE BECKETT: “All right everybody, go get your free drinks then come back because we’ve got some action later tonight.” Blake hands the microphone to someone at ringside as he heads towards the back as the fans file out to get their drinks. RILEY STEVENS: “That was…awesome Oliver. Sometimes you get what you deserve don’t you?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “That was just wrong…This poor girl didn’t deserve this? She might have caught some disease from that guy for goodness sake.” RILEY STEVENS: “Regardless stay tuned coming up we have an Open Battle Royal for what Blake described as a Special Prize.” Riley and Oliver grin as the camera pans out and we head to the back. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = As we head to the back we find ourselves with a very happy scene between Sarah Sharp and Alex Castellanos who is suited up and ready to go. He’s clearly prepared to wrestle here tonight for the first time since he was mysteriously attacked. As the two are talking Trent Lost walks around the corner holding his belt as he bumps into them, quickly the two have a stare down. TRENT LOST: “Didn’t know you’d be here. I told Sarah already but I want to tell you as well. What happened between us, that’s in the past all right?” Lost holds out a hand to Alex who seems to be a bit unwilling to trust him after all they’d been through however Sarah urges him to so he does. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “All right man.” The two release as Trent heads on his way as Alex turns to Sarah perhaps talking that over as we take our final break before the match. QUOTE Heading back to the ring, we find ourselves readying for an Open Battle Royal. Soon, "The Mob Goes Wild" by Clutch hits as the fans still unfamiliar with the new man watch as Hank Mardukas walks out from the back. Heading towards the ring, some of the fans who perhaps saw the Open Battle Royal are cheering him while the others are withholding judgment. Heading up the steps, he walks along the apron and into the ring. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Who in the world is this?” RILEY STEVENS: “This man showed up for the Open Battle Royal at ‘Breakdown’ and had an impressive performance however in defeat. Showing up to an event like this as a new guy, looks VERY good in the eyes of Blake Beckett and I fully expect him to be rewarded win or loss.” Stepping over into a corner Hank seems ready as "Ugly" by The Exies hits and the fans react with huge happiness for the man whose career started impressively but fell off. Walking alongside him is Sarah Sharp, happily with her guy. He heads up the steps, as Sarah takes her place alongside of the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Welcome back Alex Castellanos, a talented prospect who started his career with a rivalry with Trent Lost. Randomly he was attacked putting him on the shelf just a week before his title shot. Regardless he is back, ready to go it seems.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Ugh, who cares?” Alex stretches out in his corner, as “Down” by Curse Icon hits and the fans go wild as out from the back comes Trent Lost wearing that ‘GIW North Beach Championship’ proudly as he heads down towards the ring. The fans are clearly on his side in this one as he heads up the steps and into the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “This is the final entrant here tonight I am told and he’s got to be the favorite. The current North Beach Champion and a guy whose break away from Aspen and The Transcendence has been going pretty well I’d say.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “One win? A lucky one at that? Get out of here. And seriously three entrants? I guess some guys just don’t want to move up the ladder do they?” Trent hands out his belt as the referee looks around at all three men, explaining that this match will be an Over the Top Rope Battle Royal then calling for the bell. DING DING DING! The three men look at one another while it doesn’t appear that hate is fueling them it’s clear they all want to get this special prize that awaits the winner. Quickly it’s Alex going after Trent; he pushes him back into the corner then delivers some big shots. Trent pushes him away, right into the awaiting arms of Hank who lifts him up and hits an Overhead Belly to Belly Suplex! The fans clap on the move as Lost runs at him as well only he runs right into a Spinebuster! The fans cheer as Mardukas has just laid both out, as he picks up Alex and slowly whips him off the ropes. On the rebound Alex drops him with a flying kick to the mid-section then as Hank is doubled over he heads off the ropes getting up some steam then puts him down with a Spinning Neckbreaker. The fans cheer him on, as Lost sneaks up behind Alex who is getting up grabbing him by the head and trunks running towards the ropes and trying to throw him over. Castellanos quickly grabs the ropes, flipping over and landing his feet on the apron. The crowd cheers as Trent runs at him to knock him off but Alex ducks down and shoulders him in the gut. Lost backs up, holding his gut as Alex jumps and goes Springboard with a Diving Crossbody that sends the fans in an uproar. Castellanos gets up, with a bit of a fist pump in the moment but as he turns around Hank is waiting, lifting him up and hitting a Body Slam right on top of Trent making sure to hurt both men. RILEY STEVENS: “Thus far it’s been some very close action everyone seems to be getting in some moves. Still nobody eliminated.” Hank turns his attention to Lost who is getting up first grabbing him and attempting to throw him over the ropes. At the last possible moment Trent counters and pushes him into a nearby corner. Quickly he turns; landing some big head butt’s to him as he’s cornered. Lastly he backs up and runs hitting a Clothesline, before heading up to the top. Before he can jump off Castellanos hops up and quickly lands an amazing Superplex that yet again gets the fans going. Alex gets up, holding his own back after the move. The fans are into it, that’s for sure. Walking over to Hank holding his back, he waits for him to get up then lands a beautifully executed Dropkick! Looking around he realizes Trent is getting up and he throws him off the ropes, coming back he nails him with a Double A Spinebuster! Trent rolls out of the way and into the corner as Alex goes right after Hank, grabbing him and throwing him over the ropes! The fans don’t expect it and neither does Alex but Hank is able to hold onto the ropes and balance himself along the apron. Turning around shocked Alex goes after him but Mardukas punches him away! The fans are ready to see what will happen. But out of no where Trent sprints across the ring and lands a ‘Trent Kick’ Running Leg Lariat! This knocks off Hank to the mat. Hank Mardukas Has Been Eliminated RILEY STEVENS: “Good effort by Hank, still he’s been eliminated.” The fans give him a solid round of applause for his effort though he gets up angry at himself for getting caught like that as he heads to the back. Trent and Alex now turn around meeting up in the center of the ring the tension growing higher as these two former rivals are face to face. Finally Alex breaks the moment up by throwing a big right that sends Trent back. Lost responds with a punch of his own then grabs Alex and hits him with a European Uppercut that sets him right up for Trent following it up with a Suplex! He prepares to hit it, but Alex counters it and lands back on his feet hitting Trent instead with a Snap Suplex. Hopping up, he looks out at Sarah who is cheering him on for support. Looking back at Trent he lifts him up then tosses him towards the ropes. On the rebound Alex drops down and Trent jumps over him. Castellanos hops up but Lost is too fast and returns with a ‘Trent Kick’ a Running Leg Lariat! The fans in favor of Trent cheer as he pats the mat, getting pumped up. Suddenly there is a loud murmur from the crowd, as Lost takes a second for a breather. RILEY STEVENS: “Oh God, there is Aspen what is she doing out here!” Just as he says that Aspen stops at the side of the ring, with a big smirk on her face. Trent turns and begins saying something to her, leaning over the ropes yelling something at her. Aspen says some words back and then backs up the ramp with a grin on her face. Lost holds up his arms confused as to why she just did that but as he turns around he walks right into Alex who has no idea any of that just went on as he’s lifted up and receives a Samoan Drop! Sarah who seemed prepared to confront Aspen now instead is back to cheering on her man. Castellanos waits as Trent gets to his feet. Grabbing him he quickly tosses him over the ropes but Trent is able to land on his feet with ease. He pushes Alex away and then just as he begins to propel for some kind of Springboard move Castellanos kicks up hitting a stunning ‘Golden Arrow’ a Superkick that is strong enough to send Trent backwards losing his grip of the ropes and to the outside! Trent Lost Has Been Eliminated RILEY STEVENS: “Alex wins, Alex wins.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Hopefully for our sake his prize is walking papers.” WINNER: ALEX CASTELLANOS The crowd goes wild for Alex and mostly the move that won him the match. Sarah steps into the ring hugging him as the referee raises his hands in victory. Not long after Blake Beckett’s music hits as all eyes go to the top of the ramp where Blake walks out onto with a microphone clapping him on before talking to him. BLAKE BECKETT: “Congratulations on the win Alex. Let me first start by saying that you’re going to receive two…prizes here tonight. Starting with an obvious one, right here on Revolt next week it will be Trent Lost defending his North Beach title against Marcus Hister as well as you Alex Castellanos.” The crowd pops for the announcement as Sarah shakes Alex in excitement as Castellanos waits for the second part. On the outside Trent has received his belt; he nods his head upon the announcement. BLAKE BECKETT: “Also…at ‘Category 5’ coming up on June 26th you will be in a match…Well a HUGE ONE. It is an innovative new contest simply called the ‘Sole Survivor Match’. The rules are simple. The night will start with a fatal four way match. Alex you and four other people will start the night. In that match whoever is pinned or submitted will be eliminated. Then later on in the night there will be a triple threat match. Yet again the person who is pinned or submitted will be eliminated. Then in the main event it’ll be the final two fighting for a spot to main event ‘Moment of Glory II’ for the GIW Undisputed Championship!” The crowd goes wild for the announcement as Sarah begins jumping up and down, as Alex nods his head grinning as Blake says one final thing. BLAKE BECKETT: “Good luck.” Blake heads to the back as Trent enters the ring, shaking hands with Alex and holding the belt into the air. RILEY STEVENS: “Wow that is HUGE! What a match, what an innovative way to determine who is truly deserving of a spot in the ‘Moment of Glory II’ Main Event. Tune in next week where we might find out who the other three participants. And as announced by Blake Beckett; Lost, Hister, and Castellanos for the North Beach title. Tune in next week!” Trent now rolls out of the ring, even though some would say he was screwed here tonight by Aspen he lets Alex have his moment as Sarah raises his arm as this show comes to a close.
