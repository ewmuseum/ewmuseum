Thursday Night Fusion, 6 June 2013

6 June 2013

GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: THURSDAY NIGHT FUSION LIVE! From North Beach – June 6, 2013 QUOTE As usual this Fusion broadcast begins with a ring full of wrestlers this time six of them total, all making there debuts as we have. Aiden Reid, Clay Krueger, Darian, The Great, Fantabulous, Hijo De Sombra, and Michael Lynch. A really varied cast of characters, all however sharing the same goal tonight. Make an impact in there first match, by winning the Open Battle Royal. RILEY STEVENS: “Here we go again; the Open Battle Royal!” AMANDA ROWLAND: “My favorite opening mach in GIW history!” The wrestlers in the ring are prepared for action as the referee gets ready and he calls for the match up to begin. DING DING DING! It’s that time of the week again, the open battle royal. The weekly showing of future GIW stars has kicked off the careers of many GIW current stars and legends. This week crop is full of talent and future prospects. Aiden Reid, Darian The Great, Fantabulous, Michael Lynch, Clay Krueger, and Hijo De Sombra are all in the ring ready to fight it out. The bell sounds off and all six men find a partner to go to war with. Darian, The Great squares off with Fantabulous as the two exchange some rights and lefts. Aiden Reid and Clay Krueger lock up with Aiden backing Clay into the corner looking for an early elimination. Sombra and Lynch are on the opposite side of the ring going to war, Sombra scoring with chops to the chest. Fantabulous is in trouble but scores with an eye poke and low blow saving himself from elimination. He begins to celebrate, doing a little dance and clapping his hands together with excitement but is quickly eliminated by Aiden Reid who clotheslines him out of the ring seemingly out of nowhere. Fantabulous is Eliminated As Aiden looks down at the fallen Fantabulous he is almost dumped over the ropes by Clay Krueger but holds on. Sombra and Lynch continue their battle until Sombra runs and attempts a clothesline. Lunch steps out of the way and Sombra is eliminated, falling to the floor hard. Hijo De Sombra is Eliminated Its now down to Aiden Reid, Clay Krueger, Darrian, The Great and Michael Lynch. Krueger and Reid square off, as do Michael Lynch and Darian. Reid hits a DDT on Krueger and notices that Lynch now had Darian up and almost over the ropes. He rushes over to help but Darian kicks away both of them Reid goes for a clothesline but his own momentum and a push by Darian sends him over and to the floor. Clay is back up and goes for Michael Lynch who backdrops him hard to the floor. Aiden Reid is Eliminated Clay Krueger is Eliminated We are down to two. Darian, The Great and Michael Lynch exchange rights and lefts in the ring with nether going for an elimination. Finally Lynch throws Darian over but Darian saves himself by landing on the apron. Lynch comes back to finish the job but Darian pulls him over and now the both of them are on the apron in danger of being eliminated. Darian hits a right. Michael hits a left. Finally, Darian uses his foot to sweep Lynch from under his feet. He falls to the floor but has the leg of Darian. They both hit the ground at the same time! Who is the winner? The ref from the outside signals that booth feet hit the floor at the same time, thus we have a tie! REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “This match has been declared a draw!” These fans are shocked by the ending as Darian, The Great and Michael Lynch have gone over at the same time, and with the referee unable to see the winner, it has been declared a draw! Most are upset with this lack of a clear finish as the referee gives his side to both wrestlers as they argue wit him. RILEY STEVENS: “Neither man gets a sole victory but both share the win in this one, and one has to hope these two get to settle this down the line.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “How’d the referee miss it?” Both Lynch and Darian don’t seem happy with the referee’s response, Lynch heading to the back first with Darian following not far behind him as though these two may have tied, everyone in the match had good showings and would have big shots here next week. WINNERS: DARIAN, THE GREAT & MICHAEL LYNCH We cut to the back as we see THE Brandonator and "The Great And Powerful" Alexander, having hijacked a camera from some poor cameraman who was now crying in the corner with his chosen religious artifact as the two new wrestlers stood in front of the camera, looks of superiority across their faces. ALEXANDER: "LISTEN UP, PEONS OF GIW! You may not know who we are but you're about to learn very, very quickly. My name is "The Great And Powerful" Alexander, and this man right next to me is the wielder of the Spiritface, The one and only THE Brandonator! We came over from that boring ass Brownwood promotion, we forget the name of it because it sucked! I came here for one thing...to take this man out! To reclaim my Starscream Transformer and then prove to the world that I am the greatest, the powerfulest, and the awesomest wrestler that has ever wrestled!" Alexander then smirked. ALEXANDER: "But tonight, I learned something. I don't get many opportunity to learn these valuable lessons. You see "The Great And Powerful" Alexander learned that THE Brandonator and I, we make one hell of a team. Not just any one hell of a team, but THE BEST ONE HELL OF A TEAM! And that's when we decided, with the combination of his Spiritface, and my muscles...we would make the best team of all time!" THE BRANDONATOR: "Now "The Great and Powerful" Alexander and I will begin an uprising in GIW against all those foolish enough to gawk at us or disrespect us, or more importantly, the Spiritface or Muscles." ALEXANDER: "We are going to prove to GIW, nay...to THE WORLD that we are the greatest, the bestest, the most tag teamiest tag team to ever step foot in a wrestling ring. We've combined the spirit, we've combined the muscles, and we've combined all the talent! So get ready, peons...for we are..." "TEAM SPIRIT MUSCLES!!!" From behind the two, fireworks and fanfare exploded seemingly out of nowhere. The cameraman hunched in the corner was now holding at least ten religious items as the scene fades to commercial. QUOTE After there promo and a short break we return to the ring with the two tag teams already in the ring as the longest active tag team in GIW history, The Killers take on Team Spirit Muscles in a…big…well…interesting tag team match up here in GIW, the two in each corner right now deciding who will start the match for there team. RILEY STEVENS: “Amanda you got anything for this one? I am out.” AMANDA ROWLAND: “OH MY GOD! So pumped for this one, got to love these two amazing tag teams.” As the two sides stare each other down from both sides of the ring, the referee gets us ready for this featured tag team match here on Fusion. DING DING DING! This match gets underway and right away it’s the two big men in the ring right away as Brody Harper and Alexander meet up in the center of the ring, and start to exchange blows like two bar hopping drunkards, reckless abandon and no defense being used as the punches land over and over again, these fans seeming into it as the two continue this for a minute before cardio becomes a factor for both men, as they each land one big punch and fall over to there knee’s, each of them breathing heavily as cardio has really become an issue here, as the two are left with no choice but to tag but the ring must feel like a vast desert for these two big men, having to get across the ring to there much skinnier halves; as they both get up and these fans are on there feet, both men crawling to there corners reaching for a tag then falling over down and out, neither able to make it all the way. These fans are shocked as the referee checks on both men, and at the same time they have a second win crawling a few extra feet to the corner tagging in there skinnier halves. RILEY STEVENS: “And here we go!” In comes the Brandonator and Fielder, rushing at each other engaging in a technical battle as Nelson rolls him into a cradle, 1…Brandonator then rolls through as well 1…Then Fielder does it again 1…Brandonator goes through again, 1…The referee looking now just as tired as the two big men as he jumps up then right back down as the two exchange a few more pin attempts never getting past one as finally they hop up to there feet, and look around at the crowd who give a lack luster cheer, the two tag teams both tired and beaten…in the early stages of this one. Brandonator grabs a hold of Nelson and tosses him into the corner, as he rushes at him and clotheslines him before tagging in Alexander who gets in. Alexander rushes across the ring and hits Brody off the ropes as Brandonator hits the ropes as Alexander heads towards, Fielder…Will he have the energy? He does as while Alexander crushes him with the Mother of all Clothesline’s, which name may imply nurturing, though it’s anything but, however as he hits the clothesline Brandonator hits a Spear as Brandonator rolls out and Alexander hooks the leg 1…2…3! REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here are your winners by pinfall…Alexander and THE Brandonator….Team Spirit Muscles!” These fans seem to have no idea what to think in this one; surprisingly pretty split on both sides of this match some die hard Killers fans, others liking the new duo but tonight it’s the debuting team that upset the GIW Veterans. RILEY STEVENS: “…Really moves them up the tag team ladder/” AMANDA ROWLAND: “So true Riley!” Team Spirit Muscles continues to celebrate there victory as the Killers regroup on the ramp, glaring in at the opposing tag team in anger. WINNERS: TEAM SPIRIT MUSCLES QUOTE After a short video displaying the history of the Deep South Championship; in it’s newest form we catch reigning champion Chris Callum entering the ring with the belt to his ring full of challengers in Devlynn Smith, Hijo De Sombra, Jack Johnson, and Nathan Starr. RILEY STEVENS: “So far we have had two new champions, since this belt began. Can Callum defend it here tonight?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “He stole it last week and this week, it’s as good as gone.” Callum enters the ring and hands the belt over to the referee who displays it for the referee and the crowd, as he hands it out of the ring and calls for the bell. DING DING DING! Surprising a few we here see the return of Jack Johnson a former GIW Alumni, whose had his fair share of “moments” here in GIW as the heel that sprayed money over all the fans as he faces Devlynn Smith and Nathan Starr as well as the Deep South Champion, Chris Callum. These fans start to boo right away as Devlynn, Nathan, and Jack come to a conclusion that they are going to eliminate the champion as the three of them corner Callum, a fan favorite as they collapse in on him as he throws a couple punches before the numbers game catches up to him as the three of them lay boots to him in the corner; Nathan slowly sneaks out of the pile and rolls up Johnson from behind trying to steal this one and catch Jack at the same time 1…Hijo who was out of the previous beat down is able to stop the count, stepping in now to do some damage. As Starr rolls off the pin and Hijo keeps on him as Callum gets the chance to rest a bit after the beat down as Hijo gets Starr up and back into the corner while Johnson and Devlynn start to go at it as well; Johnson a former champion here in GIW, Smith looking to be a future one. These fans try there best to keep up with all the action and roar as Hijo goes springboard and lands a Springboard Tornado DDT to a huge ovation from the crowd. RILEY STEVENS: “Some good early action in this match up.” Starr rolls under the ropes to recover as Callum gets back to his feet and grabs a hold of Sombra from behind whipping him off the ropes only for Hijo to impress yet again, this time with a Springboard Arm Drag to the champion, his aerial assault impressing the fans. Meanwhile it’s Devlynn getting the better of Johnson in the corner, showing off his varied moveset with a Push Kick followed up by a Axe Kick that gets these fans going. Hijo rushes at him but runs right into a Legtrip, sending him into the ropes. He turns around and gets smacked with a big Standing Dropkick, which he hops up from right away, taunting the crowd a bit as they boo him. His taunts almost lead him to trouble as Callum gets back up and rushes him but he ducks the move then hits a ‘S.Y.N’ (Snap Yo Neck) a Reverse Hurricanrana Driver! These fans watch as Callum rolls out of the ring, looking done for in this one as we see why it’s so hard to defend the belt now as Jack takes Nathan out with a clothesline from behind as he goes for his finishing maneuver the ‘Cashbomb’ but before he can get Nathan up, Starr rakes his eyes then spins and dropkicks him right into a ‘ToxiCity’ a Spinning Backfist from Smith that floors Johnson, but as Smith and Starr go for the pin, they end up in a stalemate and end up on the mat flipping each other to land punches from the top. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “This is a brawl!” The two stars rush to there feet and end up face to face, throwing punches only to be caught off guard as Hijo flies through the air and connects to Nathan’s back sending him into Smith, the two clashing heads, stumbling away. As Hijo regains his footing however he turns right into an unexpected ‘Cashbomb’ a Sitout Powerbomb from Jack Johnson that floors him, but before he can make the pin, Hijo smartly rolls out of the ring and Jack gets up and walks right into a ‘Falling Starr’ a Hurricarana transitioned into a Sunset Flip Piledriver that takes him out, by Nathan Starr but out of no where Devlynn recovers and grabs Starr tossing him through the ropes down onto Hijo taking him out as he turns as this time it’s Jack that rolls out of the ring but Callum heads back in and as he watches Johnson roll out he walks right into Smith’s boot, as he hoists him up and hits the ‘Toxic Evolution’ a Reverse GTS to take out the champion as he makes the pin, 1…2…Starr tries to get back in but he is too late…3! REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner by way of pinfall and the new GIW Deep South Champion…Devlynn Smith!” After a hotly contested bout we have our winner and that winner is the debuting Devlynn Smith who becomes champion Number Three having taken the belt from Callum as the referee hands it to him in the ring, and he holds it high. AMANDA ROWLAND: “Now the hard part, defending it!” RILEY STEVENS: “Word for word what I said last week Amanda, but regardless Devlynn wins the belt and now will have to do his best to keep a hold of it.” With our final shot of Devlynn Smith with the Deep South belt we end our third straight Fusion with a new Deep South Champion, as we all get ready for another crazy Revolt. WINNER: DEVLYNN SMITH (New GIW Deep South Champion)
