Revolt, 20 January 2011

20 January 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: REVOLT LIVE FROM CLINE BEACH ON JANUARY 20th, 2010 = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! The show starts and almost immediately we are taken into the center of the ring where La’Sarah Audric the New Revolt General Manager is standing in the ring wearing a rather wacky outfit. Regardless she has a rather serious look on her face, as the fans are quiet standing at attention as she glares out at them with a grin. LA’SARAH AUDRIC: “Welcome everyone to Revolt, where I stand before you with some very good news. First of all the good news, which is that I am your new Revolt General Manager. I’ll give you all a moment to take that in, because let’s just say I have big plans for this place. Just so none of you are worried about me not having the tools to run this place, I’ll let you know that I learned everything I know from Mr. Blake Beckett.” La’Sarah smiles as the fans boo her for saying that, it’s rather clear that none of them find that to be good news at all, not a single one of them showing Beckett any sort of respect at all. LA’SARAH AUDRIC: “You see this place is under my grasp now. And under me the ratings will continue to go up and under me, this place will be ruled with an iron fist. But unfortunately the night got started in a VERY bad way. You see just before the show, this happened!” La’Sarah points up at the monitors which shows a bloody Riley Stevens being attended to, as the fans gasp some of them booing, Riley had become the beloved voice of Revolt. The camera flips through a few different photos before coming back to La’Sarah in the ring. LA’SARAH AUDRIC: “Now I don’t know who did it, but let me assure you of one thing. If this is some sort of response to a supposed attack on Megathreat by any of my stars, Affliction is going to have to deal with me. I have personal guarantees from all of my stars that not a single one of them laid a hand on Megathreat. So if this is in some way a retaliation, they are in for a rude awakening. I have given my roster a warning that if any of them are caught as the person who attacked or while attacking anyone on Affliction they’ll be fired; I hope that Affliction does the same.” La’Sarah seems completely serious with this statement as she glares into the camera, lowering the microphone. She goes to raise it up to say something else but suddenly "Break the Walls Down" hits and the fans immediately begin to boo. Out from the back comes Chris Jericho, wearing a very usual angry look. He walks towards the ring, looking unhappy as he walks up into the ring demanding a mic before stepping into the ring as La’Sarah has moved back making room for him. CHRIS JERICHO: “Do you know how ridiculous my stint here in GIW has been? It has been constant disrespect from every single person! First I am not treated to the things a star should be treated. I should be the Main Event! I should be the Champion! Yet you all think of me as just another wrestler. I am the star, not anyone here! I am the one who single handedly boosted the ratings. So I really hope you’re not as dumb as the last girl La’Sarah because I want the Galveston Island title and I want it handed to me…right now.” La’Sarah seems a bit taken aback by his demands as Jericho stands over her glaring down at her, seeming mad at her and the rest of the world as the fans boo him and his demands. LA’SARAH AUDRIC: “I am a smart business woman that’s what got me here. I know how to work the politics of this business. And I know for a fact that making Chris Jericho the Galveston Island Champion would bring ratings like we’ve never had…” The fact that La’Sarah seems to be considering this brings a lot of boos from the crowd, as Jericho nods his head liking where this is going when suddenly “Motherfucker of the Year” by Motley Crue hits and the fans cheer this welcome interruption as out from the back this time walks Scott Rydell with his massive partner Leon Drake standing not very far behind him, though it’s Rydell with the microphone. SCOTT RYDELL: “As fun as this little meeting of egos is for everyone here tonight, I came out here to put an end to it. At first I was sitting here ignoring you, because I figured that whatever you both had to say wasn’t important. However through the sounds of my music I believe I heard you Jericho asking for the belt to be handed over and I didn’t hear you say no. So I came out here to make sure that, the belt I once held isn’t made a joke out of by a man out of his prime that tapped out to a guy that he couldn’t even beat despite cheap shotting him before the match.” Rydell lowers the microphone to applause from the crowd who are in complete agreement of his statements on this one. As Jericho raises the microphone and leans over the ropes talking to Scott who is on the ramp way. CHRIS JERICHO: “You SHUT THE HELL UP! Your nothing to me! You and everyone else on this pathetic island shouldn’t even call yourselves stars! Especially you, the most overrated star I’ve met yet.” Scott seems a little taken aback by Jericho’s statements as the tensions are rising here, as Jericho backs up a bit as Rydell looks over at Leon and then to the crowd. SCOTT RYDELL: “Overrated? Overrated...” Rydell shakes his head and then starts to walk down towards the ring; however it’s La’Sarah who steps in between the two stars keeping them from doing anything. LA’SARAH AUDRIC: “Guys, Guys! You are both in the main event tonight, save it for the match. The winner might just get himself a shot at the Galveston Island title.” La’Sarah looks back and forth between the two as Jericho and Rydell now bad mouth one another as we head to a commercial break. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = As we come back from the break we are taken to the backstage area where the fans begin to boo the site of Tony Miranda who is apparently picking on a backstage employee. We see him pushing him, and then point him away as the staff member runs off. The boos turn to cheers when Aspen Chaud approaches Tony from the side. ASPEN CHAUD: “Wow, you’ve taken to bullying backstage employees now huh? I guess Tony Miranda is really moving up in the world. What ending two people’s careers weren’t enough for you?” Aspen is referring to former champions Technical Mechanica as she raises an eyebrow, legitimately asking the question as Tony looks at her smiling. TONY MIRANDA: “Not even close…consider them lucky that was all we did to them.” Tony smiles as he takes a step closer to Aspen, who doesn’t seem overly intimidated by him at this point. ASPEN CHAUD: “Needless to say, going around ending peoples careers and crushing them, isn’t the best idea when a week after we have Police running around these halls wondering who attacked Megathreat and who attacked Riley…” Tony seems a bit stunned that Aspen would accuse him of such a thing, but his nearly eerie smile makes him all the more suspicious. TONY MIRANDA: “Oh come on, feathers? Who else could it be? Even I know you’re not that dumb. Think we all need to take a step back and think of the one man that would do that. However that still doesn’t mean I like being accused either, in fact…we hate it.” Tony looks back as The Shadow appears from behind him, having arrived just now at the perfect time as Aspen looks from Tony up to him. ASPEN CHAUD: “You know this isn’t even about that. It’s about me ending this little massacre you guys think you’re going to commit. So how about this, I want to end your little team thing you’ve got going. Since I’ve got nobody to back me up. You and me Tony, if I win then you two are over as a team and can no longer assault another person, so that we can keep this place safe…Deal?” Aspen has laid down the challenge as Tony looks up seeming to still be very confident about this all. TONY MIRANDA: “See that’s not fair at all. Why would we put our team on the line for nothing? So how about this then, we meet One on One…in a Hardcore Match next week. If you win, we’ll disband but if I win…then you have to be under my command for the next two months…” Aspen’s eyes widen a bit as Tony now flashes his hands out there, to seal the deal. At first Aspen seems hesitant, however she then leans forward and the two shake hands. TONY MIRANDA: “Good. Now I’ll see you tonight, this match happens even if you don’t come out of tonight unharmed.” Tony smirks and then turns around heading down the hall leaving Aspen to look up at The Shadow before he to follows suit as we are taken away from the backstage area and to the ring. To the ring we go where the whole thing is already full. On one side we see a man that was listed as Jak Hendricks during the backstage segment. Beside him are two other men one only known as Seth and the other known as Mitch. And lastly we are taken to the other side to see the face of a rather intimidating and scary looking man, whom has stunned the fans with his presence his name being Seth Seeker. AMANDA ROWLAND: “Well, as you may know Riley is out tonight so I’ll be filling in for this broadcast.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “They really…had nobody else? Nobody?” Regardless back into the ring we go as the three men on the one side are all talking about the man on the other side. The bell rings as the match starts, two referees on the outside to call eliminations. DING DING DING! As the match starts the three men, Jak as well as the two no namers Seth and Mitch get together and Jak basically commands them to attack. The fans boo this tactic as Seth and Mitch run at him only for Seth to explode out with a big left for Seth and then a big right to Mitch. The fans cheer him on liking him thus far as Seth and Mitch back up a bit holding their jaws seemingly trying to think of a new plan. For this they return to Jak who demands that they attack again. This time they are a bit more lucky as Jak joins them as the three of them start to go to work on Seeker. The fans boo as Jak leads the charge attacking them; however Seeker begins to battle out. Boom he tosses Seth to the side and then boom throws Mitch to the other. The fans are roaring as he gets up, looking down at Jak who steps back a bit then to the surprise of everyone connects with a brutal Yakuza Kick to the skull of Seeker. The fans are a little impressed as Jak backs up and Seth and Mitch run forward attacking Seeker in the corner. Jak looks back at the crowd beginning to taunt them a bit. Suddenly he hears a thud and that’s the brutal sound of Seth being slammed to the mat by a huge Powerbomb! The fans are all going wild as he turns and then boom connects with a Clothesline to Mitch. Both of them get up and try to double team him however he tosses Mitch over the rope nearly sending him into the crowd. Seth returns to Jak’s side pleading with him to come help. Jak in a fit of rage however grabs Seth and tosses him over the top and out of the ring leaving just Seth and Jak now. The two of them begin to circle one another when suddenly Jak reverses and pushes the ropes down, before jumping out of the ring eliminating himself. AMANDA ROWLAND: “Why would he do that?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “It’s called knowing when to turn back. He was not prepared to fight a man like this…this…thing Seth Seeker.” The fans boo him as he backs up the ramp, screaming up at Seth that “Next week, I’m making you tap” a threat that seems a little mistimed however Seeker leans over the ropes, clenching his fists mad that he didn’t get to put Jak away tonight as these two’s issues seem like they may have just started. WINNER: SETH SEEKER The camera cuts backstage where we see a firm set of abs, right in the middle of the screen. As we pan out this man is revealed to be Frankie Raptor showing off his abs to a female staff member backstage. FRANKIE RAPTOR: “Get a long hard look at these things, it might be the last time you see me with out some gold around my waist. Might be new around here, but from the looks of things I am going to make some waves around here. Might just be better to be along for the ride.” Frankie grins, throwing his hand up against the wall letting his shirt fall as the girl seems to think about it for a second before being grabbed and pulled out of the way by another man and another new star Dallas Steele. DALLAS STEELE: “I really hope that I did not just hear you say, that you were going to be a champion any time soon. See, in my country Canada I am considered a huge star, just like I will be here.” Dallas smirks as he looks up at the much taller Frankie Raptor, these two coming from nearly completely opposite backgrounds. FRANKIE RAPTOR: “Ok eh’, you know what we call you here though? A chucky!” Frankie smirks and tosses up an ‘L’ before walking away from Dallas, who seems confused over what he just said as the fans that actually get it laugh it up at Dallas whom they have already begun to dislike. We come into the ring where we are just making it in time to hear the end of Austin Acquin’s "Re-Education" by Rise Against as he stands in the ring, looking serious and ready to go. “Bodies” by Saliva hits and the fans boo as out from the back with big smirks comes the team of Nelson Fielder and Brody Harper, Nelson the man who is wrestling tonight leading the way. AMANDA ROWLAND: “Here are The Killers a team that back at ‘Genesis’ actually eliminated The Acquin Brothers in the Tag Team Rumble match.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I’m really trying to like them…but it’s hard.” Nelson jumps up onto the ring apron and then over the ropes and into the ring, as the fans continue to boo these two. Brody stands on the outside as Nelson looks at Austin with a big smirk. DING DING DING! The two begin to circle each other, before going in for a lock up. The fans cheer as Austin begins to back him into the corner. He lands a few shots before the referee hits the three count and he backs off. To his surprise Nelson catches him with a big kick to the chest, followed up with a big time dropkick. The fans boo him as Austin falls to the mat, reeling a bit already as Nelson calls for him to get up. When he does Nelson goes for a Spinning Heel Kick however Austin ducks it at the last second. The fans boo as almost immediately Brody gets up onto the ring apron, making both Austin and the referee turn their attention to him. Austin decides to focus on the match at hand, but with the referee’s back turned he walks right into a massive low blow from the recovered Nelson. Fielder hops up to his feet as Brody jumps down, as Fielder jumps over the ropes and then sets up on the ring apron. After a few seconds of Austin holding his lower regions in pain, he attempts to get to his feet but it’s a failed attempt when Nelson hits a ‘Kill Shot’ a Springboard Somersault Neckbreaker which even impresses the fans. Right away he makes the cover 1…2…3! Winner. AMANDA ROWLAND: “That was really quick; this doesn’t bode well for Austin’s attempts to claim he isn’t just a product of his brother.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “That’s because he is just that, he’s nothing without his brother...These Killers guys might not be so bad after all.” Right away Brody slides into the ring, holding up his partners arm into the air. The fans boo the both of them as Brody then looks over at Austin who is beginning to get to his feet. Nelson smacks him on the chest and tells him to set him up. Brody pulls him up and prepares to set him up when suddenly Joshua Acquin comes full steam to the ring. AMANDA ROWLAND: “Here comes Joshua!” Both Nelson and Brody bail right away, as Joshua helps out his brother. The fans are still cheering on Joshua as he checks on his brother, while The Killers soak in the carnage they have created as we take a break. WINNER: NELSON FIELDER We return to the backstage area where an angry Austin Acquin has just returned to the backstage area after nearly being beaten to death by The Killers, saved only be Joshua Acquin who is standing by with Maria. JOSHUA ACQUIN: “Listen man, you did your best tonight ok? Quick loss I know but for the next few weeks I want you to sit back and I’ll help you out ok?” Austin nods his head while holding the back of his neck when suddenly the fans begin to boo as Bret Battery walks up behind him with that always big smirk, bringing back memories of these two’s old feud. BRET BATTERY: “Tough break out there tonight. You see it’s a shame that you’re constantly in your brother’s shadow. And the one chance you have to prove your not, you blow it.” Austin as usual seems to want to explode and hurt Bret however it’s Joshua and Maria that calm him down as Bret backs up a bit loving to annoy his past rival. BRET BATTERY: “Next week, I’m having an Open Invitational. If your big brother will let you off your leash, come on out.” Bret turns and walks away as Austin turns to Joshua wanting to discuss this but he’s got to go as he heads out for his match leaving this segment with no resolution. The fans in the arena are excited for this next one as already standing in the ring, given little fan fare is Zack Starr. The fans all have a unanimous who cares look, when he’s announced. However soon "Nightmare" by A7X hits which turns their lack of care into cheers as the former champion Joshua Acquin walks out with Maria. He seems focused as she walks behind him, both of them high five a few fans as Maria walks around the ring to the other side of the ring while Joshua gets into the ring, looking at the referee and then to the fans in attendance. AMANDA ROWLAND: “Here is our former champion himself Joshua Acquin. To those who don’t understand the rules of this match it’s simple. The man that is pinned tonight will be eliminated leading to a three way next week between the other three competitors, before we end up at just a singles match to decide the champion.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Seems like a really drawn out process.” “The Man Who Sold The World” by Nirvana hits as the fans look up at the ramp booing as ‘The Psychiatrist’ Marcus Hister walks out from the back with that always creepy looking smile of his, ignoring all of the fans as he stares in at Joshua Acquin, before walking up the steps and into the ring. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “This guy is my pick, right here.” “Monster” by Kanye West hits last as the fans look to the ramp, rather with a very confused look. Out from the back comes Kydd Lightning a small looking figure. Almost immediately the fans laugh him off as nothing more then a filler due to his very small stature. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Look at this runt.” AMANDA ROWLAND: “From what I’ve heard, he’s got a bad case of Napoleon syndrome.” Kydd walks up the ramp and into the ring; he is by far the smallest man in the ring here tonight. All four fighters take a look outside of the ring where the GIW Television Championship is sitting on its own little pedestal, as all four stand in their corners as the bell rings. DING DING DING! The bell rings and right away we have some action as Joshua Acquin and Kydd Lightning look up, with Joshua winning that battle for sure. On the other side of the ring we are also treated by an exchange by Marcus Hister with Zack Starr. Right away its clear Hister has the advantage in that department as the tension can almost be felt right away in this one. The fans sort of laugh off an attempt by Kydd to try to lift off Joshua, though it appears that if Acquin didn’t punch him off he probably could have done it. Joshua has him in the corner and is delivering some big blows. Just in the opposite corner we see Hister, trying to basically suffocate Starr with a chokehold in the corner. At the last second the referee catches him and orders him off, which surprisingly he adheres to. However seemingly having the same idea, both Hister and Acquin grab Kydd and Starr and suddenly whip him them into the center of the ring. The fans are shocked as the two go right towards each other, however impressively Kydd jumps up delivering a beautiful kick to the face, spinning and landing right on his feet. Hister runs at him, only for Kydd to duck under before tossing him up and over the ropes! The fans cheer as Hister falls to the outside. Now Acquin does the same, his arm is caught by Lightning who spins it. However it’s Joshua that reverses the move and then suddenly he himself ends up, behind Kydd. Hister gets up onto the apron. Acquin pushes Kydd forward into Hister at the last second however Kydd amazingly kicks up dropkicking Marcus back down onto the outside however his momentum sends him flying over Joshua’s shoulders as well. Acquin turns around as Kydd tries to jump up and deliver a kick. Acquin catches it sending Lightning harshly into the mat and then tries to turn it into some sort of leg lock. However it’s a recovered Starr who runs at him full steam, tackling him to the outside, right beside Hister. AMANDA ROWLAND: “Things have spilled out of the ring.” On the outside Joshua and Starr roll around, trying to gain the advantage. However it’s a sudden Clothesline that gives Josh the advantage over Starr. But as he gets rid of him it’s Hister back up, as the two begin to exchange some punches. Neither man is getting any sort of advantage instead they are just delivering huge punches back and forth. In the ring it’s Kydd Lightning that’s back to his feet. He stops for a second realizing that both men on the outside are distracted. He runs off the ropes and then jumps over them as both Josh and Marcus realize it as he comes down hitting a big Plancha to both who had no time to react. The fans are going wild; they have really gotten a lot of love for the new guy. However they turn on him when a fan that held out his hand for a handshake, receives a big push from Kydd sending him to the ground. Back in the ring, unnoticed by many is Starr. Lightning returns to the ring, realizing he may have just made this a one on one match. Starr tries to catch him off guard with a big punch however he receives a big, Vertical Suplex Stunner instead. The fans are now unsure, about Kydd many of them now going against him. Kydd makes the cover 1…2…Kick Out! The fans have now begun to cheer on Acquin who is getting up on the outside. In the ring Kydd screams that it’s time to end this as he sets up waiting for Starr to get up. Acquin tries to get into the ring; however it’s Marcus Hister that grabs his leg not knowing anything about a current pin just making sure he doesn’t let Josh get away. Back in the ring Kydd delivers a kick to Starr setting up for his finisher. On the outside Joshua has gotten his leg free and now tries to dispose of Hister. He swings at him only for the attempt to be ducked, then Hister connects with a Superkick! In the ring however Kydd has just finished off an ‘Electroshock Therapy’ a Hangman's Spiked DDT Driver that seems to have just hurt Starr, which leads to a pin 1…2…3! Kydd picks up the win. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “YES, I love him!” AMANDA ROWLAND: “I have to admit he did look pretty impressive, as he eliminated Zack Starr from the title hunt and perhaps GIW altogether.” The fans boo Kydd Lightning as he gets to his feet, holding up his arm in victory. Though he hasn’t won the belt yet, he turns looking at it with big eyes on it. On the outside an angry Hister, kicks the barricade upset that he didn’t get the pin. Joshua on the ground recovers, as the fans continue to boo. We flash back to Zack Starr who was eliminated tonight and as the referee’s look at him, seems to have something broken as well. For Kydd, it leaves him with huge momentum going into next week as Acquin may just realize getting back his title may be harder then he thought, while Hister seems prepared for the numbers to continue to thin as the segment ends on a view of Kydd eyeing the title. WINNER: KYDD LIGHTNING (Joshua Acquin & Marcus Hister Also Advance) A rewind is shown of everything that happened on Monday on Affliction. We run through the footage of Megathreat after the attack and then a few clips of superstars backstage discussing the clips. Then the voiceover guy asks “What will happen in the fall out of Megathreat’s attack as Garrett Halloway takes over Affliction. Plus who will reign supreme as the new GIW Deep South Champion, Affliction Live Monday Night” Finishing with a cut to all three of the competitors Madison Seton, Mark Cross, and Scott Reave as the hype video ends. Send it back to the back where we see La’Sarah Audric talking on the phone with someone. The name of said person is unrevealed however we listen in on as she paces back and forth. LA’SARAH AUDRIC: “No I assure you that we did nothing of the sort. You really think I was worried about them doing that good with ratings? Come on I am not worried about them at all.” She stops as the person on the other line takes the time to respond, which she listens to and then after a few seconds she gets right back into it stopping her pacing as she responds. LA’SARAH AUDRIC: “Yes, I know. It’s an issue. But truth be told I think that they were the one’s that attacked Riley. I know I wouldn’t put it beyond any of their stars…Yes Sir. Keep me posted on whatever is going on Blake.” The reveal is some what expected as La’Sarah hangs up the phone, sitting down into her desk as now both shows have been hampered by the effects of the attacks on Megathreat and then Riley Stevens earlier tonight. It’s finally time for the MAIN EVENT of the Evening, as right away the fans begin to boo as just the first little beat of “Break the Walls Down” as the biggest name in GIW Chris Jericho begins to walk out from the back. Still seeming upset about his loss at ‘Genesis’ and his lack of a title around his wait, he still walks down towards the ring walking slowly ignoring the fans on his way towards the ring. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Here is Mr. Ratings himself the Legend, Chris Jericho.” Up the steps he goes, stopping to look out at the fans who boo him perhaps more then any other man here tonight. Still he steps into the ring with complete confidence, as he turns his attention to the ramp as “Taste the Blood” by Shawn McPherson now hits, and instead of stopping the fans just continue to boo as Tony Miranda walks out with The Shadow following not far behind him. A generic “Worst Combination” sign is shown in the crowd as the two men head up into the ring. There doesn’t seem any real friendship between Jericho and the Perfect Combination just mutual respect as they join each other in the ring. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “And then we have the best tag team in the business today hands down.” All the negativity is blown up when "Bullseye" by Aly and AJ hits as the fans boos turn right into a massive amount of cheers. She walks out from the back, blowing the fans a few kisses as she walks down towards the ring. Jericho, Tony, and Shadow all glare at her, as she high fives the fans and then stops at the end of the ramp seeming unwilling to go up there alone. AMANDA ROWLAND: “Not a bad idea for Aspen to wait for help.” That help comes in the form of “Motherfucker of the Year” as both Scott Rydell and his own partner Leon Drake now come from the back. The fans have still not let up either, though Rydell returned at ‘Genesis’ this is truly his in ring debut. He walks down towards the ring, looking at Aspen on the outside. When suddenly the two nod their heads and all three of them slide into the ring, starting an all out brawl! The fans are going nuts as Aspen is able to use her momentum and Tony’s lack of footing to take him right out of the ring! The fans are going crazy as Drake and Shadow now exchange as well, both of them falling through the ropes as well. The referee having lost all control, realizes its Rydell and Jericho alone in the ring so he calls for the bell. DING DING DING! Rydell starts the match with Jericho in the corner, beating down on him. The fans are cheering him wanting to see Y2J shut up once and for all. The referee pulls Scott away, at a four count. He however goes back in for an attack, but when he hits Jericho, Y2J immediately begins to complain to the referee. The fans boo as Jericho claims that Rydell poked him in the eye. Scott can’t believe the acquisitions being made against him. As this is settled we return to the outside where Tony has reversed the fight, pushing Aspen up against the barricade swinging for the fences stopped by her sudden jump out of the way. Two referees have come down now and first they separate both of the big men, surprisingly they are able to get them to separate and go back to the corner. On the other side it’s a lot of the same, as Aspen starts to head to her corner as Tony talks some trash. In the ring the referee has issued a verbal warning to Rydell. In a bit of an annoyed state now Rydell tries to land a big haymaker, but a now recovered Jericho dodges it and immediately rolls him up 1…Kick Out! Doesn’t last as both men hop right up to their feet. Jericho however gets up a split second earlier which allows him to quickly get off a big Backhand Chop. The fans feel Rydell’s pain, as he holds his chest backing into the ropes. Jericho grabs him and then knees him in the gut before whipping him off the ropes. Rydell gains some speed then ducks under the clothesline attempt then on the next return connects with a Roaring Elbow! The fans cheer as Rydell gets up and then tags in his big man Leon Drake, a new face here in GIW. AMANDA ROWLAND: “Let’s see how the big man does.” Leon waits for Jericho to get up and then grabs him, lifting him up and power slamming him to the mat. The fans cheer on the newcomer as he makes the cover 1…Kick Out! Not even close to enough to put down Jericho. He now gets up and holds Leon in place, as he brings him over to the corner making the tag into Aspen now. Aspen jumps in and with Leon holding Jericho in place she connects with a big Backhand Chop! The fans cheer as she grabs him and whips him into the corner. She goes for a ‘Tush Push’ but at the last second Jericho is able to avoid it. Aspen takes a second to recover which is just long enough for Chris to get to his corner and make the tag into The Shadow. The fans sort of gasp and are in a little bit of shock as Tony screams at him to get her, as Aspen turns around staring up at him. Though she wasn’t scared of him much backstage, in the ring it’s a whole other thing. The fans cheer a bit when she lands a big punch though she’s only just able to reach his basically stomach. Nearly two foot taller then her, Shadow doesn’t seem all that hurt by what she’s done so far. Finally determined to prove that she can take him down, she runs off the ropes connecting with a dropkick that only barely moves The Shadow. Rydell throws out his hand, realizing that he needs to get in to make this some what even. She tries to get out but it’s Shadow that grabs her, lifting her into the air before dropping her down with a Single Handed Chokeslam! Rather then attempting to pin her, it’s made evident that Tony and Shadow want to hurt her. Shadow lifts her up, putting her on his shoulders as he carries her to his corner with ease. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “This is what you call man handling some one.” Shadow now sets her up, as Tony prepares to try something big from the top rope. The fans boo as he looks at them with a smirk. He goes for the move however at the last second it’s Aspen who is able to get out of the way. The fans go wild as Tony is basically saved by Shadow who catches him as Aspen runs across the ring, tagging in Leon Drake.
