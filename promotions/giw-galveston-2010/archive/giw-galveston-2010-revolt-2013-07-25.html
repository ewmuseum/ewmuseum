GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: THURSDAY NIGHT REVOLT LIVE! From Porretto Beach – July 25, 2013 BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! The show begins not in the GIW ring, but in the back inside Blake Beckett's office. He has just opened the door for Alioth Starre. Blake smiles and greets him as he goes back behind his desk and takes a seat. BLAKE BECKETT: “Please, Alioth, take a seat.” Alioth shuts the door behind him. ALIOTH STARRE: “I'd rather not. I don't plan on staying long.” BLAKE BECKETT: “Alright, then what can I help you with?” ALIOTH STARRE: “I'm here to talk about Ace Baldwin in the Sole Survivor match.” Blake sinks his head. He smiles then throws his hands up in the air. BLAKE BECKETT: “Alioth, there is nothing I can do. He signed a contract with Alex, and I can't void it. Trust me, I've looked into it.” ALIOTH STARRE: “Just think about the precedent that it sets for your company. That some guy who had success in the past can just walk on in and get placed in the biggest match of the year? It shows that we play favorites Blake -” BLAKE BECKETT: “No, it shows that Alex plays favorites.” ALIOTH STARRE: “You're wrong. If you let this happen you're just as responsible as he is.” BLAKE BECKETT: “Alioth. There isn't anything I can do. Ace Baldwin signed the contract already. He is in the match. I can't take him out, even if I wanted to.” Alioth turns away from Blake and pulls at his hair, obviously frustrated. ALIOTH STARRE: “Well what if I were to injure him before Sole Survivor. Then he wouldn't be medically cleared to compete in the match.” BLAKE BECKETT: “That doesn't work either, because then I wouldn't have time to name his replacement. You have to look at this from my point of view – marketing and all that business.” ALIOTH STARRE: “Fine. Then I'll do it before the night is over.” BLAKE BECKETT: “I don't think you're supposed to tell me that.” ALIOTH STARRE: “Well I did. And I promise I will deliver on my word.” BLAKE BECKETT: “The so be it.” Alioth nods to Blake and exits. The camera stays on Blake for a bit. He smiles then pulls out his cell phone. We cut back to ringside. RILEY STEVENS: “Well, Revolt has started off with Alioth Starre pledging to injure Ace Baldwin!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Did you see the intensity in Alioth's eyes? He means business.” RILEY STEVENS: “Tonight is going to be one hell of a night! Blood will be spilled.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “One week before Category 5, everyone is going to be trying to get the momentum Riley, we are going to see guys do things this week that we may never see again.” RILEY STEVENS: “It's time for our first match of the night, a grudge match between Seth Cohen and TJ Williams!” We cut up to the ramp for the first match. QUOTE In the next match up we see a rivalry come to a head here on Revolt as "Note To Self" by From First to Last hits and these fans start to boo angrily as Seth Cohen, a man whose quickly made himself the enemy here in Revolt heads out from the back smirking and chuckling at the hatred he receives as he spins around showing off then heads toward the ring with a smirk on his face the entire time as he prepares for match number two with his opponent here in the ring tonight after having tucked his tail the week prior. RILEY STEVENS: “These two faced off just a few weeks ago in the Open Battle Royal and put on a show but it was Cohen that stole the victory and the two have had issues ever since.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “TJ’s just jealous, he can’t beat him!” Cohen steps up into the ring seeming to agree with Oliver’s sentiments in this one as he walks over to his corner and his music fades as the crowd pops even before "Blues Saraceno (Justice Mix)" by Evil Ways hits and the man they’ll be sticking behind in this one now enters in TJ Williams the New Talent here in GIW. Williams has his eyes locked on Cohen as he makes his entrance a week after beating him in a tag match that Seth bailed on, but now getting to even the score with a victory this time on the big show in a featured contest here on GIW. Williams walks by the fans here high fiving a few of them before heading up to the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Williams looked great in that tag match last week, but Cohen didn’t let him get his hands on him. This week it’s just one on one, time to settle things in the ring.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “How’s he going to bounce back after two losses?” Williams steps through the ropes but as soon as he does Cohen walks across the ring getting right into his face the two sharing words, most of which we can’t quite hear as they start to push each other before the referee steps in and pushes them away just long enough to call for the bell to start this one. DING DING DING! The referee barely gets them separated long enough, but does just enough as the two return to the center of the ring going right into a lock up in the center of the ring. The two men looking to make a statement right away by proving whom the stronger man is, and at first there is a stalemate but quickly Williams with the slight size advantage starts to force Cohen back much to the happiness of the crowd as he backs him all the way to the corner, as he pushes away and grins at Cohen just trying to get in his head. Seth shakes his head and throws a cheap shot but Williams ducks under it and rolls him up trying to steal an early win 1…Kick Out. Not close as both men scramble to there feet and TJ crushes him with a European Uppercut that sends Cohen stumbling back into the corner as Williams clearly looking to make a statement early on rushes him and hips him with a big clothesline, yelling something out to the crowd as he jumps over the ropes and climbs up to the top rope as these fans roar in approval as Seth stumbles up but sees him out of the corner of his eye, and bails from the ring in a hurry. RILEY STEVENS: “Cohen dodges a bullet there.” Cohen leans against the announce table catching his breath thinking there is no way TJ tries something from that high up and he appears to be right as Williams steps down but instead he grabs the ropes and launches himself backwards just as Seth turns around dropping him with a Springboard Clothesline drawing a huge roar from the crowd as Williams even gets a little into it now holding up his arm as he reaches down for Seth, hoisting him up but having a bit of issues as Seth is mostly dead weight here. Needing him back in the ring he pushes him up onto the ring apron then rolls into the ring after him going to pick him up but as he does Cohen sneaks in a desperation eye poke that blinds TJ as he stumbles back against the ropes, the referee having missed it as Seth pushes himself back to the ropes getting back to his feet as TJ stumbles away from the ropes and walks right into a Double Arm Clothesline from Cohen that drops him as Seth looks to the crowd with a smirk, still showing some signs of injury from TJ’s early assault but seeming to slowly recover having halted his momentum a bit. RILEY STEVENS: “No surprise a eye poke gets him back in the match…” Williams gets up pretty quickly but still seems to be having a little bit of a hard time seeing and Cohen gives him a little shimmy, then pops him with a punch to that hurt eye. TJ trying his best to block it realizes he’s out of his element right now as Seth smirks and tosses another punch catching him as Williams continues to hold that side of his face. Finally Cohen feints a punch, and then jumps up with a Dropkick that sends TJ back first into the corner which holds him up long enough for Cohen to at first taunt him then nail him with a big Spinning Chop that clearly hurts Williams a bit as Cohen, pulls him out of the ring and tosses him back into the corner simply to do a bit more damage and to prove he can as he rushes forward stops on a dime and leg trips TJ with a big kick to the leg, sending him to the mat. These fans are getting a little worried now as Cohen looks down at him and then motions that it’s over now as he walks over to him grabbing him and pulling him a little bit away from the ropes as he locks in the ‘Jewish Cloverleaf’ a Torture Texas Cloverleaf, these fans realize how big of a lock it is and many can be heard gasping. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Two and oh, it’s over!” TJ groans in agony as he reaches out for the ropes trying his best to get there as these fans sense the end is near as he crawls his way to the ropes, reaching for them as Seth cranks on it wanting nothing more then to embarrass him and make him tap out. He’s nearly ready to tap out when he reaches out and gets a hold of the ropes as these fans roar in approval as Cohen holds onto the submission 1…2…3…4…He finally breaks up the submission hold as he gets up seeming a little angry that it didn’t work out with that one but not realizing that the move did it’s damage as TJ gets up using the ropes, unable to do it on his own will as Seth smirks and again says it’s over as Williams gets up and turns right into the ‘Dollhouse Driver’ a Small Package Drive, but as he goes for it TJ lowers a knee into his head and then drops him to the mat with a Swinging Neckbreaker. Getting up a little gingerly he risks it all going to the top with a Double Jump Moonsault but it lands as the crowd goes wild 1…2…Kick Out! These fan’s can’t believe it as Seth was able to kick out at the last second in this one. RILEY STEVENS: “How impressive, he kicked out!” These fans are even a little bit impressed as TJ gets up to his feet, but very slowly still hurting from that submission. But as he gets up he and the rest the crowd are shocked to see Miss Jade heading towards the ring, booing her immediately knowing exactly who she’s out here to see. Williams turns to her and shakes his head, telling her to go to the back now but as he turns around, he walks right into a Low Blow. Surely a DQ will follow but instead the referee is focused on Jade telling her to head to the back, Williams folds over a bit but Cohen catches him in position hoisting him up again for the ‘Dollhouse Driver’ this time landing as these fans boo furiously as Cohen holds the pin 1…2…3! It’s over just like that. REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner by way of pinfall…Seth Cohen!” These fans don’t let up continuing to boo as Miss Jade rolls into the ring heading across over to Cohen to raise his hand in victory as Seth smirks and lets her do so as Williams rolls over to the corner his whole body hurting after that finish as Jade hoists up Cohen’s arms. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Told you he’d win.” Cohen still looking tired holds up his arm with a big smirk as he looks over at Williams when out of no where Jade drops down and gives him a low blow of her own, dropping Cohen to his knees as he looks up at her in pain as she simply kicks him down to the mat. RILEY STEVENS: “What the hell? Jade just got him the win and now, she just took him out?!” The fans seem just as shocked as now both men in the ring are holding their jewels as Jade stands in the center of the ring with a little smirk forming having just sent the message to the rest of the roster that she’s been trying to do for the past few weeks. WINNER: SETH COHEN “Heaven's a Lie” by Lacuna Coil hits the PA system as we return from commercial break as Alex Castellanos makes his way down to the ring, slowly, sporting a noticeable limp. RILEY STEVENS: “Uh oh, it looks like the number one contender has a limp tonight.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Well after he tapped out to Alioth's figure four last week, I'd assume he would be tapping.” RILEY STEVENS: “Maybe he tried to fight the hold for too long. Hopefully he's okay.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “If he was a real number one contender he'd be wrestling tonight.” RILEY STEVENS: “The champ has the night off too! And the past few weeks have been really awful for Alex.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I wonder what he drug himself out here tonight to say.” By this point Alex has made his way up the stairs and into the ring. He has a microphone in his hand. His music slowly dies down and the crowd cheers him loudly. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “Now I'm sure you all have noticed by now, but I have a slightly injured knee thanks to Alioth Starre last week. But don't worry, because at Category 5 no injury is going to be able to hold me back from my ultimate goal in this company: the Undisputed Championship.” The fans cheer loudly at the idea of Alex bring champion. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “Over the past few weeks it seems like the odds have been stacked against me. But I'm not afraid. I'm not in the least bit worried that I won't be able to achieve my goal.” Blake Beckett's theme music hits and the arena erupts in boos. Blake steps onto the ramp with Colt Cox standing beside him dressed in scrubs and carrying a doctors bag. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “What the hell is Colt Cox wearing..?” Blake begins to speak. BLAKE BECKETT: “Alex, I'm sorry to hear that you are banged up this week. That really is a shame.” The crowd keeps booing. Beckett smiles. BLAKE BECKETT: “I brought Colt Cox out here because it is time to do your pre match physical. Nothing too major, I just figure the fans would like to see how it goes down.” Colt slowly makes his way to the ring, a defeated look on his face. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “This is bullshit Blake. Colt Cox isn't a doctor.” BLAKE BECKETT: “Sure he is! Just look at him, he has the scrubs and the bag and everything. He is as legitimate as they come Alex. Don't worry.” The crowd boos loudly. RILEY STEVENS: “Something is up..” Colt slides into the ring and sets up a chair for Alex to sit in. He walks around the number one contender and lifts up both of his arms and examines his pupils with a flashlight. BLAKE BECKETT: “You're doing good Alex. You've never failed one of these before; you shouldn't be so worried right now.” Colt lifts up Alex's right leg, then when he lifts up the left leg Alex winces. BLAKE BECKETT: “What was that Alex?” Alex pushes Colt away now and hobbles up from the chair. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “You're not going to do this Blake! Everyone can see that you're screwing me over right now.” BLAKE BECKETT: “Just remember that you need medical clearance to compete in any GIW match.” ALEX CASTELLANOS: “Blake! You f*cking prick!” BLAKE BECKETT: “Hey, hey Alex! Don't use that kind of language or I'm going to have to pull you from television!” Colt Cox scribbles something down on a clipboard then exits the ring, making his way back towards Blake Beckett. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “If you do this Blake I swear you will pay for it. I swear to God you will pay for it.” Cox hands Blake the piece of paper then, without looking at the Chairman, slinks to the backstage area out of sight. Blake holds the paper up and begins to read from it as he pulls some glasses out of his coat pocket. BLAKE BECKETT: “Galveston Island Wrestling Medical Evaluation Report: Alex Castellanos. Conducted by: Colt Cox, PhD. Well... he's a doctor right? Notes: Alex is very injured after his match with Alioth Starre.” The crowd boos. Alex is seething in the ring. BLAKE BECKETT: “The consensus is that Mr. Castellanos is not able to compete for the Undisputed Championship at Category 5.” Blake folds the paper up and slides it into his pocket. BLAKE BECKETT: “I'm sorry Alex.” ALEX CASTELLANOS: “No you aren't Blake! I swear I'll never let you forget what you -” BLAKE BECKETT: “Cut his mic.” Alex's mic dies. He throws it as far as he can up the ramp but it misses Beckett. BLAKE BECKETT: “Enjoy the night off Alex. Judging by your temperament, you need to go home and relax.” Blake's music hits and we fade to commercial. RILEY STEVENS: “Jamie has turned heads since his return here in GIW and will soon get his chance at GIW gold when he challengers for the North Beach belt at ‘Category 5’.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Lynch is unstoppable, he don’t got a chance.” Vaccaro holds down the ropes allowing Jade to step in as these two head to their corner together to talk strategy as soon “Sick or Sane” by Senses Fall hits and these fans start to boo relentlessly as Clay Krueger enters now from the back alongside Krishna Ivs who few know too much about yet here in GIW but she’s guilty by just walking out with Clay who many have turned on, but few can argue what a statement he made last week in his matches. DING DING DING! Alioth Starre and Veronica Taylor are ready to go. The bell sounds and this anticipated match is now officially underway. Veronica, who has been rather impressive thus far in her Galveston career, looks more focused than ever. Alioth is one of the smartest men in the business today though and quickly reverses the hold by attempting a back suplex. Veronica screams like a tribal member, the sounds echoing. The crowd boos her loudly. Alioth begins to stir now, trying to get back to his feet. Running double leg dropkick to the face of Alioth! Veronica covers again! 1….2…no! Alioth gets a foot on the bottom rope. Veronica is mad now, yelling at the referee that she had a three. Veronica shakes her head in disbelief.
