Rebellion, 19 July 2015

19 July 2015
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS SUNDAY NIGHT REBELLION 7/19/15 FROM MOODY GARDENS PLAZA IN GALVESTON, TEXAS RUBY REYNOLDS: “The following bout is a Five Minute Ironman Match. Introducing first weighing in at 1500lbs, from Parts Unknown, The Land Sharrrrrrrrrrrk!” Teenage Dream by Katy Perry hits and the crowd turns electric. The Land Shark walks out on stage and it bends under his massive frame. All 20 feet and 1500LBS of Sharky goodness lurks toward the ring. Following in tow are hundreds of members of The Land Shark army. Land Sharks of all shapes and sizes trail behind their leader as he bumps fins with all the children in attendance. The Land Shark climbs into the ring and his army surrounds it. SARAH SHARP: “I guess The Land Shark’s army is going to be acting as unofficial lumberjacks in his match.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Land Shark is going to need all the friends he can get if he wan’t to take out Harper. Did you know Brody almost went into the Hall of Fame this year? He was a shoo in until he got grounded and his mom told the Boss he wasn’t allowed in.” SARAH SHARP: “Well there’s always next year, right Oliver?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “But it should’ve been this year, Sharpie. I’m starting a protest. Lets get #Brody2015 trending world wide.” The Land Shark’s music cuts out abruptly and is replaced by the docile sounds of “Bodies” by the band Saliva. RUBY REYNOLDS: “And from Round Rock Texas, weighing in at 300Lbs, The Monster, Brooooooooody Harrrrrrrrrrrperrrrrrrrr!” Brody Harper descends from the rafters on a zipline. He is wearing a long black leather coat that he may have pilfered from Bronx’s locker room. He has a baseball bat in one hand and his face is painted ghostly white accented with black eyes and lips. Brody points up to the rafters with his bat, directing landsharks gaze. Brody’s mom looks down on them dressed in a blue mask and feathered cape. Land Shark looks back to Brody and nods in understanding, he isn’t the only one that brought back up. SARAH SHARP: “SO what should be the game plan going into a match like this?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “This isn’t a match, it’s a marathon. The key is to pace yourself or you’ll never last the whole five minutes.” SARAH SHARP: “What about The Land Shark Army and Brody’s Mom, do you think they’ll play a deciding factor in the outcome?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I don’t know, Sharpie but I hope so.” DING! DING! DING! After the bell rings Brody starts in on Land Shark with his bat. He brings down hammering blow after hammering blow but it’s absorbed by Land Shark's foam costume. Eventually The Land Shark manages to grab the bat and rip it out of Brody’s hands. He tosses it into to ringside where it is immediately set upon by his army. When the feeding frenzy is over not even splinters are left. Shark proceeds to Fin Smack the taste out of Brody’s mouth. Brody fights back with his own punches but the power and size advantage of Land Shark win out. One massive right fin sends Brody to the canvas and shark tries for a pin. Brody wriggles free at two. SARAH SHARP: “That was close. Brody will have to be more careful than that. He can’t let shark get on top of him or it’ll be pin, after pin, after pin.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “1500LBS is a lot of weight. I don’t even think George Stark could kick out of from under that. Brody is back to his feet. He ties up with Land Shark and the two start to exchange power move after power move. Brody hits a Brain Buster, Face Buster, and then a Powerslam. Land Shark springs back up and hits a Big Boot. He follows up with a Big Leg Drop but Brody manages to roll away. Land Shark lands hard on his backside and a jolt of pain shoots up his spine. Brody rushes in to capitalize but the arena goes dark. When the light come on a few seconds later a coffin is in the center of the ring. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “What the hell?” The lid of the coffin swings open and the shambling corpse of Nelson Fielder slowly emerges. Fielder locks up with Brody while Land Shark nurses his injured posterior. Fielder is no match for Brody Harper. A few well placed punches and a Canadian Destroy leaves Fielder comatose in the middle of the ring. Brody isn’t finished yet, oh no not at all. He lifts Fielder over his head and Gorilla Press to the outside. The Land Shark Army tear into Fielder and devour him alive. SARAH SHARP: “Oh god I can’t watch that!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Tell me I did not just see that.” Shark springs back into action while Brody’s back is turned and tries to force him over the ropes and into the waiting jaws of his frenzied army. Brody fights back and a few elbows creates the separation he needs. Brody gives the thumbs up and his mother leaps from the rafters. Octuple Shooting Star Press connects! Brody rolls in for the pin... but no! The LandShark army rush into the ring and break it up. SARAH SHARP: “Madness this match is pure madness.” Things only get worse as former GIW superstars rush the ring. Mo Love, Awesome Xombi, and Wake Walker rush the ring. Mo Love are quickly thrown back over the rope and head to the back but Xombi and Wake Walker are devoured hole, swallowed by unknown members of The Land Shark Army. The Referee has had enough and tries to call fro the bell but the ringside official wont allow it. SARAH SHARP: “Just getting this info from ringside officials. The time limit has passed without a single pin fall and we’re now moving to sudden death! First man or beast to get a pin or submission will be your winner.!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “This is literally the craziest thing I have ever seen.” It takes some time but the referee gets all the stray Land Sharks, Mo Chronic, and Brody’s Mom out of the ring and sends them to the back. It’s just Brody and The Land Shark now… but not for long! Brody’s long estranged father rolls out from under the ring and gets into the fray. Both Harpers rush at Land Shark. Not knowing what else to do, Land Shark looks to the rafters and calls out… KAZAN! SARAH SHARP: “I don’t believe it!” A bolt of blue lightning shoots down from somewhere unseen and strikes The Land Shark. Brody and his father are sent flying backward. Brody lands in a corner but his father isn’t so luck. Brody’s Dad sails over the tope rope and crashes through the spanish announce table. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I didn’t even know we had a spanish announce team. Where the hell did they come from?” Back in the ring the intense heat from the lightning bolt has burned away Land Sharks foam costume. The camera cuts to a shot of Brody’s face as his eyes go wide with rage. “STUP BLACK SKIN NIG”, Brody screams. The camera quickly cuts back to The Land Shark and we get the first ever glimpse of the man beneath the foam. It’s mother fucking R-Truth. SARAH SHARP: “That’s Ron Killings!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Who?” SARAH SHARP: “Ron Killings! Better known as WWE Superstar R-Truth!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “You mean The Land Shark has been R-Truth all along?” SARAH SHARP: “It makes perfect sense now. That’s why he’s such a good singer and dancer.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Nothing about this make any sense whatsoever.” Brody rushes at R-Truth and nails a devastating Big Boot. Truth goes down and the remaining remnants of his costume fall away. Brody hits his finisher Canadian Destroy and then hooks both legs. 1 2 3 DING! DING! DING! Truth rolls out of the ring and picks up an armful of cinged foam before heading to the back. He is passed on the ramp by Brody’s Mom. She helps Brody’s Dad to his feet and the roll into the ring. Together the proud parents raise their son’s hand in victory RUBY REYNOLDS: You’re winner, Brooooooooooooooooooooooooooooody Harrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrperrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrr! SARAH SHARP: “What a match, Oliver. It’s going to down in history as one of the all time greats. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Wait, is this the end of The Land Shark?” SARAH SHARP: “Only time will tell” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “We’ll be right back folks after we take a pause for the cause and tell you about some of the other great shows we’ll be offering on iPPV later this year.” WINNER: BRODY HARPER QUOTE We head to the ring, these fans are already greeted with the arrival of Kelsi Ross and Karl Parris alongside Geneva Lost. All three of them are in different corners getting prepped up for the night; these fans taking there picks right about now Karl Parris perhaps the fan favorite considering he’s been the underdog since his debut here before but Lost and Ross both have their own fans in the crowd . SARAH SHARP: “And here we go into triple threat action before the main card tonight. We have three competitors all looking and hoping to make a name for themselves early into their career.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Both Geneva and Karl have been trained by former GIW Champions, so its interesting to see how much they bring to the table her on the pre-show before the biggest show of the year.” Lost glares at both of the other competitors, bad mouthing them both a bit. Kelsi Ross looks away, while Karl shakes his head shrugging him off as the referee gets this match started. DING DING DING! The match begins with the three competitors each in their own corner, sizing each other up as the roar of the crowd began to shake the ring. Geneva stayed put as Kelsi charged Karl, throwing lefts and rights at him, hoping that one would land squarely on the much larger competitor. But Parris used his size and brute strength to peel Kelsi off of him and away by pulling her hair, despite the referee's warnings. Geneva Lost emerged from her corner and quickly pushed Parris away from Ross, and the two stared each other down for moment. Parris chuckled and retreated into his corner arrogantly, his hands held high, claiming innocence. Lost charged him and connected with an enzuigiri that sends him back into his corner, dazed. Kelsi Ross charged Lost who ducked, sending Kelsi to the edge of the ring. SARAH SHARP: “Well timed by Geneva there.” She moved along the rim of the ring, her hand upon the apron until she had reached the opposite corner, on the same side she had rolled out from. She watched Lost and Parris quarrel briefly as her breath was regained, but when Karl Parris turned to her, she knew action was the only thing she could do. She rolled back into the ring and curled up by the ropes as Parris charged. As he was seconds from clotheslining her over the top rope, Ross dropped the act and her body to use a drop toehold and smack her opponent’s throat into the middle rope. She went for a quick pin, but even though he had been deceived, Parris would not give up. He held up a shoulder and then reached for the bottom rope to ensure the pinfall was broken. OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Karl Parris better watch out or he's going to get embarrassed.” Parris stood up and backed away towards Geneva. She was ready and waiting. He turned to find himself decked right in the chin by a perfectly placed kick. Lost then moved toward him. Parris rolled under the rope and stood upon the rim as Lost headed toward him. He lunged with a right hook, but Lost retaliated with one of her own. She then ducked under the top rope and thrust her shoulder into her opponent’s rib cage. Lost keeled over, but she would not let Parris get a hold of her. She countered a grapple and clotheslined Parris to the outside of the ring. She dared not follow whilst she had, who she believed to be the weaker opponent flat in the ring behind her. She turned to find a swift dropkick landing on her chest. OLIVER THIBODAUX: ”Kelsi Ross is looking to get into the swing of things in this match." Lost is holding herself up by the help of the ropes, she gets bombarded with forearm shots from Kelsi, each one more vicious than the next. She irish whips Lost to the other side of the ring, she too uses the ropes to gain some momentum. As the two meet in the center of the ring, Ross smacks Lost down with a back elbow. Lost grabs her jaw, looking like she was caught off guard. Kelsi walks around her fallen opponent with a grin swiped across her face. From the outside comes Karl Parris who springs over the top rope and come crashing down onto Kelsi with a springboard crossbody! He holds on for the pin. 1... 2..... Geneva Lost is quick to break up the pin attempt. She relentlessly stomps and kicks at Karl Parris who rolls over onto his back. Lost isn't looking to play around tonight, as she pulls him up by his hair and keels him over with a sharp knee to the ribs. She locks his head up in a cravate and goes for one of her deadly signature moves. SARAH SHARP: "Broken Promise!!" She jumps and falls backwards, smashing her knees into Karl's face. Karl springs back and falls limp on the mat. Geneva looks around and sees Kelsi Ross getting to her feet, Lost is quick to act on that as she runs at here and drop kicks her, sending Ross through the middle rope and out of the ring. She hustles back to Parris who has yet to move, she covers him and digs her forearm right in his face as she does. 1... 2... 3! RUBY REYNOLDS:: “Here is your winner by way of pinfall... Geneva Lost!!” Geneva Lost showed her dominance throughout the match. Easily taking out both of her opponents tonight. Kelsis Ross is shown on the outside holding the back of her head after getting dropkicked out of the ring and hitting the mat outside hard. Parris is slowly beginning to regain consciousness after having his head dropped on Geneva's knees. SARAH SHARP: “All three competitors looked good tonight but Geneva Lost shines bright tonight.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “After this performance, you can expect to see her name on the radar of any champion. I bet you she's not going to wait long before wanting to add some gold to that beautiful waist of hers.” As Kelsi Ross heads to the back, Geneva Lost celebrates her monumental win here on the Moment of Glory pre-show after a hotly contested match as we head to the back for a break. WINNER: GENEVA LOST
