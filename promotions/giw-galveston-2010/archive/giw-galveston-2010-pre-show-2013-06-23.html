GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: AGGRESSIVE EXPANSION PRE-SHOW LIVE! From the San Luis Hotel – June 23, 2013 QUOTE The time has arrived for the Open Battle Royal here tonight where as usual stars will compete in either there return or first match here in GIW to get themselves back started on the road the top, tonight we have Michael Harris and Veronica Taylor involved and after determining it will just be those two it’s quickly decided that the match will be a Singles Match instead. RILEY STEVENS: “Interesting duo here, with the returning Michael Harris. He did do pretty well his first go around here in GIW but, seemed to be pulled away before he could reach the top.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “He was one of my guy’s, he’s a stud this is his match to lose.” In the ring Taylor and Harris are prepared to go as the referee steps in and declares it time to go, as they prepare to toss one another from the ring. DING DING DING! Match Summary The opening match for the pre show was a two person Open Battle Royal, which after being determined there was only going to be two participants was switched into a singles match up between the returning Michael Harris and the debuting Veronica Taylor. In the match the two would go back and forth, though it was very clearly Harris with the strength advantage and it was him that really wore Taylor down with big shot after big shot. Showing some real talent and heart Taylor really did put up a good fight but in the end it was Harris that was able to get the victory when he defeated her with ‘The Harris Effect’ a Tombstone Omega Piledriver, that put her down for the count. The fans seemed to be impressed by his performance but also came away with some respect for Veronica who made a solid debut. REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner by way of pinfall…Michael Harris!” After the match the fans do seem a bit upset by the Harris victory, not a man many are going to like with his strong opinions of himself as he heads out of the ring with his arm held high in this one, the victor tonight. WINNER: MICHAEL HARRIS QUOTE It’s now time for the second match of the night here on the GIW Prelims; Harley Havoc, Miss Jade and Sarah Frost line the ring looking ready and willing to go for a shot to move themselves up the ladder here in GIW. RILEY STEVENS: “Three prospects here in the ring tonight with a chance to prove that they are here for the long haul and really can stick with GIW’s tough competition.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Winner gets a chance to becoming a champion, loser becomes a stay at home mom.” The three girls in the ring, all eyeing each other up trying to get the mental edge before the match as the referee gets into the ring looking at them, then starting the match. DING DING DING! Match Summary This match up between three female wrestlers really allowed Miss Jade’s talents to be displayed as she really made quick work of her two opponents in the ring. Showing off some of her high flying moves she got the fans attention, while also getting Frost and Havoc’s with several big time moves. Limited offense was hit by Frost and Havoc, the majority of there game being trying and mostly failing to defend Jade’s arsenal, one that she eventually used to put both ladies down with hitting Havoc with ‘The Kiss Goodnight’ a Roundouse Kick that sent her out of the ring before hitting ‘The Peacemaker’ a Inverted leg drop bulldog into a pin on Frost, that got her the three count. A impressive debut, for Miss Jade. REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner by way of pinfall…Miss Jade!” The fans can’t help but be impressed as she holds up her arms in victory and jumps up the top rope as we get a few replays of her handy work before we head to some more Main Card hype videos. WINNER: MISS JADE QUOTE After a short break hyping up the Alioth Starre and Alexis Terry match later in the night we return to in ring action; standing in the ring we have Jacob Jett and Justin Harmony ready for singles action as they stand in the ring, looking right at one another. RILEY STEVENS: “Two more newer stars here in the middle match of the opening prelims, so to speak who you got Oliver?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I got the one that wins.” With that statement, the referee steps up into the ring checking on both guys then quickly calling for the bell. DING DING DING! Match Summary This was a solid match up between two stars looking to make a name for themselves. Harmony got off to a slow start with Jett perhaps more accustomed to the GIW ring having had a match before. But after an early hot start, Harmony started to turn things around about mid-match. These fans were definitely on Jett’s side in this one, but were forced to watch Justin put together several moves to turn the momentum around to lead him to being able to set up the victory. Jett was able to counter out of the ‘Devastation’ a Swinging Neckbreaker but the second time he wasn’t so lucky as Harmony was able to hit the move after ducking Jett’s kick attempt, to put him down allowing him to get the pin and the victory here in his debut. REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner by way of pinfall…Justin Harmony!” Another impressive debut here on the pre-show as Justin Harmony picks up a hard fought victory over Jett much to the crowd’s dismay. Though he had a few illegal maneuvers in the match, it was him that got the victory after ducking a Jett kick, as the next step will be moving up to the big dogs. WINNER: JUSTIN HARMONY QUOTE Another break leads us into the next match; Jamie Vaccaro the former GIW Champion is in the ring standing across from Michael Neeley, who will really have to take a quick step up against a guy whom has had a lot of success in GIW in not the biggest amount of time. RILEY STEVENS: “Neeley really is in deep waters now as Jamie Vaccaro looks to get himself back into the title hunt, and back on top.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “We’ll see about that…” Vaccaro seems to be confident while Neeley is showing some signs of real fear, shaking a bit as the referee calls for the bell. DING DING DING! Match Summary To put it bluntly this match wasn’t even close, Vaccaro started the match by hitting rushing Neeley wih a Spinebuster and from then on the match was pretty much over as Vaccaro was able to land several suplex’s, a move he dubs the ‘Suplex Machine’ and he stopped Neeley from ever even getting a bit of offense in this one. It did however allow Vaccaro to show off his entire impessive arsenal however it was Sharpshooter that he locked in, that eventually drew the pain screams of Neeley as well as a tap, to get a win for Jamie something these fans were very pleased with as the former champion gets back on track. REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner by way of submission…Jamie Vaccaro!” After the mach up Vaccaro stands over a fallen Neeley with a smile on his face having just shown why he is a former GIW Champion, and Neeley may just be heading to the unemployment line. RILEY STEVENS: “That was quick…” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Jamie’s a monster!” Vaccaro heads out of the ring, happy with his win but seeming to realize that Neeley is not the one he wants to be beating, he’s ready for a lot bigger opponents as he heads to the back, ready to watch the rest the show and see who he’ll be after next. WINNER: JAMIE VACCARO QUOTE We have reached the top of the prelims now, with the whole ring already lined with different wrestlers now as we have Eddie Madox and Gator Grimm making up Surrender Corp in one corner then in the other corner we have Streetcar Rendezvous; Tyler Haskell and Ben Keeton in there corner with there manager Bruce Armstrong standing on the outside then finally we have guys whom have not yet lost in Spirit Muscles; The Brandonator and Alexander ready to take the step up from The Killers in this one. RILEY STEVENS: “Not usually known for tag teams, GIW is looking to broaden that horizon with this match as three real tag teams, do battle in our Pre-Show Main Event.” The wrestlers are already in the ring deciding whom to get things started Streetcar with the aid of there manager as the ref steps in and gets things started. DING DING DING! Match Summary As would be expected these three teams provided a lot of excitement for the crowd, as the amount of wrestlers kept them all fresh and running at a high level, allowing for few dull moments in the match. Though Alexander did do well it was Streetcar Rendezvous that looked both like a well oiled machine as well as the winners from pretty much the start. Though Alexander tried his best, he could only carry his partner so long. Surrender Corp’s were never able to get out of the starting gate either, and at the end of the match after all hell broke loose. Alexander tackled both members of Surrender Corp out of the ring allowing Streetcar to hit ‘The Rendezvous’ a move in which Haskell catapulted The Brandonator into a Keeton lariat to put them down. REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here are your winners by way of pinfall…Streetcar Rendezvous!” These fans seem to enjoy the young fresh new tag team as Bruce Armstrong joins them in the ring holding up his tag team members hands into the eye as the pre-show comes to an end and we are reminded that coming up next is…AGGRESSIVE EXPANSION! WINNERS: STREETCAR RENDEZVOUS
