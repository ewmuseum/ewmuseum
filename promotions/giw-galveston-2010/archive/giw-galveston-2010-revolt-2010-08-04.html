GIW — Card

Revolt, 4 August 2010

4 August 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Galveston Island Wrestling Presents: Thursday Night Revolt! Live! From Porretto Beach on August 4th, 2010 BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! “Your Going Down” by The Sick Puppies blasts through the speakers situated on Porretto beach, and the crowd of 400 plus screaming GIW fans erupt in cheers. After the opening video is done showing on the televisions around the ring, the music of Revolt General Manager David Sinclair begins to play, the fans give him a mixed reaction as he walks down to the ring with his pet tOWLette perched on his shoulder. Sinclair is wearing a white suit, slicked back hair, newly polished shoes, and a wild smirk on his face. He uses the steps to enter the ring, tOWLette elected to fly over to the down right ring post. RILEY STEVENS: “Ladies and gentlemen, welcome to this weeks edition of Thursday Night Revolt. As usual, we have a packed show set up for you today, but first our General Manager David Sinclair has an announcement.” Sinclair grabs a microphone from the bell keeper, and waits for the crowd to die down a bit before finally speaking to the audience. DAVID SINCLAIR: “In case you people have forgotten, which wouldn’t surprise me mind you, next week’s edition of Revolt is the last one before our second ever Supershow, Blackout, that will take place in the city of Port Bolivar, more specifically the Bolivar Lighthouse.” The crowd goes insane at the mere mention of Blackout; Sinclair seems annoyed that he is unable to speak over the crowd, and waits until they quiet down a bit more before putting forth the effort to speak. DAVID SINCLAIR: “With that said, I have decided to use my general managerial powers, and I have made a few matches that will announced right here on Revolt… right now!” The crowd cheers loudly again, several drunk people go even more insane and have to be removed from the crowd. Sinclair seems proud of himself. DAVID SINCLAIR: “Yes, I know I am an amazing general manager… it just comes naturally to me. Anywho, the first match I am scheduling for Blackout is because of a feud that needs to end now. We will Aspen Chaud facing off against our Women’s Champion Liviana Valentine WITH the title on the line.” The crowd begins to chant “ASPEN! ASPEN! ASPEN!”, Sinclair laughs at them, obviously thinking that they are just being ignorant… and begins to speak again. DAVID SINCLAIR: “Oh yes… but there is one more match. Wake Walker has officially been added to the Galveston Island Championship match… in the MAIN EVENT! That’s all, we have to get to a match.” Strangely, a bunch of horses with Japanese hate symbols appear again. He remain there for the rest of the show. Sinclair and tOWLette promptly leave the arena. The arena is ready for the first contest of the night as we head to the ring. "Godzilla" by Blue Oyster Cult hits the speakers and the fans give a nice favorable reaction as Kaiju of the Battel makes his way out from behind the curtain. Kaiju walks down the ramp and climbs up to the ring apron, then up and over the top rope. Riley Stevens: Here's a guy in a dragon robot costume. He's also a guy that's been very successful on Revolt. Oliver Thibodaux: Indeed. He's now 3 and 1, and he's going to be a tough test for Mr. Munroe tonight. Almost like clockwork, Kaiju's music fades as “Watercolour” by Pendulum hits. Chad Munroe makes his way out to the ring and the fans give a decent reaction to him. Chad makes a beeline down to the ring and the crowd is buzzing in anticipation of the first match of the night. Riley Stevens: Both of these men are looking to retain the momentum that they started last week. Which one will come out on top? Oliver Thibodaux: After winning his match with basically one arm last week, my money is on Munroe. Chad and Kaiju stare each other down from across the ring as the referee calls for the bell. DING DING DING! Both men come out of the corner and collide with a THUNDEROUS collar and elbow tie-up in the center of the ring. They struggle for just a few seconds until Kaiju uses his overwhelming size advantage to push Chad back into the corner. The referee admonishes Kaiju, asking for a clean break. The big dragon allows the clean break and backs up with his hands in the air in innocence. Chad seizes the opportunity, shooting forward and hitting the bigger man with a double-leg takedown. Munroe peppered Kaiju with some serious rights and lefts from his guard as the referee warned him about closed fists. Munroe got up to his feet and pulled the giant dragon back up to a vertical base. Chad attempted an irish-whip, but Kaiju reversed it!! Chad came off the ropes and Kaiju got NAILED with a brilliant Bicycle Kick. Chad covered, but only got a two count, as Kaiju still had a lot left in him. Riley Stevens: We've seen some amazing action so far in this contest. Both men definitely want to pick up the win here! Oliver Thibodaux: Yeah, yeah, yeah...the overgrown Asian is getting whooped by the aggressive Scotty. Kick his f@#$ing ass, Chad! Riley Stevens: WHOA!! Watch the language, Olly. What's wrong with you? Oliver Thibodaux: I told you my money was on Chad, genius! Chad pulled Kaiju back to his feet once more and hooked the side of his head. Chad spun underneath, looking to execute a neckbreaker. Kaiju reversed it, spinning around Chad once more, then dropping with a VICIOUS DDT!! Kaiju tries to take advantage of the situation, but he's still stinging from the bicycle kick. The dragon finally covers Chad, but only gets a two count out of the deal. Kaiju looks at the referee in frustration and lets out a MIGHTY DRAGON ROAR! The crowd cheers as Kaiju gets back to his feet and scrapes Chad Munroe off the canvas. As Kaiju pulls Chad up, Munroe starts throwing rights and lefts into the midsection of the big man, trying to rally. Chad starts throwing bombs to Kaiju's face and midsection, and finally gets some separation!! Chad runs into the nearest ropes and comes back, only to be BLASTED with a Big Boot from Kaiju. Riley Stevens: Kaiju of the Battel looks to be in firm control of this contest now. Oliver Thibodaux: COME ON, CHAD!!! YOU WON WITH ONE ARM LAST WEEK!! FINISH THIS OVERGROWN JAP!! Kaiju dropped down to his knees and covered Munroe and got a VERY VERY CLOSE 2 count, as the referee's hand was about an inch from the canvas. Kaiju saw his opportunity and pulled Chad up. The big dragon irish-whipped Chad HARD into the turnbuckles and ran into the ropes. Chad stumbled backwards and turned as Kaiju threw the Clothesline from the Depths of Hell, but CHAD DUCKED!!! Kaiju stumbled forward into the ropes and turned around just in time to see Chad boot him in the midsection!! Chad hooked Kaiju up and then struggled, but finally stood with him in Vertebreaker position. Chad Munroe jumped up in the air and came crashing down, delivering the DECAPITATOR to Kaiju!! Chad rolled the lifeless form of Kaiju over and made the cover. 1...2...3!! Oliver Thibodaux: YES, YES, YES!!! YOU BEAUTIFUL SCOTTISH BASTARD!! Riley Stevens: You've gotta hand it to Chad Munroe here tonight. He fought a much bigger opponent and still pulled it off. What a match to have for our opening contest!! After the referee raised Chad's hand, he simply exited the ring and walked back to the back without a word. Inside the ring, the referee tended to Kaiju, who was clutching at his neck. The camera switches backstage where David Sinclair is talking on a cell phone while tOWLette muches on Apple Jacks in the back. DAVID SINCLAIR: "I'll make it up you... I'll make sure you are big... I'll make sure you are a success." We are ready for the first of two divas matches tonight as the sound of "Push Push" by Kat Deluna alerts the fans of Sherilynne Martinez. The fans however are not sure how to react when she comes out, not knowing a whole lot about her. They instead give her no reaction reserving judgment until they know who the gal is. Riley Stevens: Great to see new divas here in the company, here’s the newest Sherilynne Martinez. Oliver Thibodaux: I was hoping this division would die by now. Sherilynne heads into the ring walking up to the turnbuckle and holding up her arms. "Bullseye" by Aly and AJ hits and the fans blow up literally out of there chairs as out from the back comes Aspen Chaud with a big smile on her face. Aspen walks out nodding her head as she heads down towards the ring. Riley Stevens: And here she is the lovable Aspen Chaud, these fans are electric. Oliver Thibodaux: What is Blake doing just toying around before firing her? She stops to high fives the fans before skipping up the steps and walking along the ring apron. She looks back at the crowd then steps into the ring. Finally she walks across the ring with a big grin still planted across her face as she leans through the ropes and kisses the camera as per usual. The fans have begun to die down now, as Aspen steps back looking at Sherilynne as the referee calls for the bell. DING DING DING! The crowd gets ready for some big time divas action as the two circle around each other before going in for a lock up. Aspen seems to have the upper hand until Sherilynne rolls around her pulling her arm tightly behind her back. Aspen tries to break free but Martinez has a firm grip on her arm. Finally Aspen gets to the corner jumping up and flipping over now turning the move on Sherilynne, her arm squeezed behind her back. Aspen tugs on it but Martinez elbows her head able to break the hold then run off the ropes. As Aspen is holding her head Martinez connects with a big Spear. The fans are a bit impressed as Sherilynne makes the quick cover 1…Kick Out! Aspen kicks out at nearly two, as Sherilynne angrily lifts her to her feet and whips her into the corner. She runs full steam into the corner but Aspen is able to narrowly avoid the clothesline. Martinez bounces back as Chaud hits a big time Backhand Chop! The fans seem to be into it, as Aspen lines up for another one and connects again nearly knocking Martinez off her feet. Aspen screams ”One More” before connecting with one more Chop that sends Sherilynne to the mat. The fans cheer as Aspen smiles out at them and then calls for Martinez to get to her feet. When she does she goes for a Jumping Dropkick but Martinez hops out of the way at the last second. Aspen hits the mat hard as Sherilynne waits for her to get to her feet and sets up a STO Takedown that does just that Aspen. She has her against the mat again and makes the pin 1…Kick Out! Martinez wastes no time pulling her up into a set up for a DDT that she hits perfectly. She again makes the cover 1…Kick Out! The fans are cheering as Martinez gets up and corners the referee telling him to count faster. Riley Stevens: Sherilynne has looked good but she needs to stay focused. As she continues to argue the fans cheers get Aspen to her feet as she pulls herself up to her feet. Shaking herself off she realizes that Sherilynne has noticed her and is walking towards her but she runs across the ring and jumps up into a Lou Thesz Press then unleashes her punches a move she nicknamed the ‘Rage Within’. Sherilynne after a few punches pushes her off and runs at her but Aspen ducks it. But Aspen is some how caught with a Reverse Neckbreaker! Sherilynne has it now she has Aspen down and holding her neck. Taking some deep breaths Martinez waits for Aspen to get to her feet. As she does she calls for it and connects with the ‘Latina Pride’ a Roundhouse Kick. The fans are stunned in the arena as she may have just knocked off Aspen Chaud! She goes for the cover quickly 1…2…Kick Out! The fans sigh being relieved she kicked out while Sherilynne immediately starts to claim that it was three count. Going back on the attack Aspen catches her with a Sitout Jawbreaker that catches Sherilynne completely off guard holding her jaw. Aspen whips her into the corner then runs off and hits the ‘Tush Push’ a Turnbuckle Flying Butt Shot! The fans cheer as she immediately goes top rope going with a slow clap. As always she follows it up with an ‘AirBarbie’ a Flying Crossbody! The fans are going wild now knowing that this is it. She gets up holding her stomach as she then turns back and prepares to finish her off. Waiting to get her from behind for ‘The Barbie Makeover’. Sherilynne gets up dazed and then realizes that Aspen is behind her and goes for a Pele Kick! But Aspen catches it and turns it into the K.M.A.! The fans go nuts as she locks it in the center of the ring. Sherilynne wastes little time tapping out, after a great counter and a solid match. Riley Stevens: That was a great match from both girls and as good as Aspen did, you have to think Martinez impressed a lot of people around here. Aspen Chaud pulls off and rolls up to her feet as the fans cheer and she slides out of the ring to give them some high fives. However she rolls back into the ring when she sees Sherilynne is getting up. She offers her a hand shake but Sherilynne instead slides out of the ring, showing her no respect. Aspen shakes her head not pleased at what just happened, but she can’t worry to long she has a match to referee later. We head to the ring where currently standing there still hurt after the beating he took last week Billy Spizatoni! The fans don’t give a damn about him at this point, as suddenly "The Final Countdown" by Europe hits and the fans clap as out from the back comes Mike Danielson with a smile on his face. Riley Stevens: These two guys really need a win; both have been on the downs the last month. Oliver Thibodaux: That’s for sure last week Mike was told to Evolve, whatever that means. Mike runs down the ramp high fiving the fans along the way as he jumps up the steps and over the ropes into the ring. The fans like him even though he’s become irrelevant since ‘Moment of Glory’. This is both men’s chance to get back on there feet. They line up across from each other and the referee calls for the bell. DING DING DING! Billy gets up in his face right away screaming into his face yelling at him that he’s nothing. Mike looks out at the crowd half to look at them for insurance and to avoid Billy’s spit. Mike turns and crushes Billy with a big punch sending him to the mat. Billy gets back up by walks right into a Swinging Neckbreaker! Mike has the advantage right from the get go. Mike runs to the corner yelling at Billy to get to his feet which he does after a few seconds, slowly getting to his feet. As Billy gets up a little dazed after this quick flurry. He runs across the ring full steam and hits the ‘Paintball’ a Big Boot! The fans cheer as Billy rolls down to the mat in a bit of a heap. Mike goes for the quick cover 1…2…Kick Out! The fans seem a bit disappointed but realize that he’s still very much in the clear. Billy tries to claw his way out of the ring, to get some rest. But Mike drags him by his feet to the center of the ring. He motions that it’s over and then locks in the ‘Command and Conquer’ a Crossface! Billy tries to get away but can’t do a damn thing. The fans are stunned as he seems to fade quickly. His arms fall limp as Mike pulls back hard on it. The referee finally calls for the bell pulling Mike off of him, after a dominating win. Riley Stevens: Looks like Mike Danielson got the message here tonight. Mike gets up looking down at his fallen opponent as the fans give him a small clap, only half way giving a damn about him. Mike leans into the camera and yells out “How is that for evolving?” The referee checks on the fallen Billy as Mike heads to the back after a dominating win. The scene begins after the match and the camera has moved backstage. Jake Kennedy is backstage sitting in Dr. John House’s medical room getting his wrists taped when Timothy Hunt walks through the door. TIMOTHY HUNT: “Well, look who it s. If it isn’t the ION Television Champion himself… Mr. Jake Kennedy. Ya’ know, I think me and you could have a pretty damn good match. What do you say for next week, champ?" Kennedy looks at Hunt and laughs. He stands up from the medical bench and gets in Hunt’s face. JAKE KENNEDY: “Sorry, “dude”. I have better things to do next week.” Jake begins to walk away, but Hunt grabs him by the elbow. TIMOTHY HUNT: “I said, I want to face you next week.” Kennedy laughs and pats Hunt on the shoulder. JAKE KENNEDY: “I’ll talk to Beckett for ya… we will work something out.” Kennedy walks out, the camera zooms in on Hunt smiling and nodding his head in approval. We head back to the ring where standing in there with a grin on her face is Liviana Valentine, the Galveston Island Divas Champion. The fans are booing her heavily giving her no sort of respect at all. Soon “Butterfly Effect” by Amatory hits and the fans begin to boo heavily as out from the back comes Alexii Ravynova with a big smirk on her face. We get a glimpse of Liviana and it actually appears that she is very much so scared of Alexii. Oliver Thibodaux: Oh my, Liviana is scared. Haha! That’s too funny. Riley Stevens: The way Alexii has been tearing people apart, I don’t blame her. She walks down the ring glaring at the fans before stepping up into the ring, stepping in while the fans continue to boo her it’s now evident they hate her a lot more then Valentine. But soon “Bullseye” by Aly and AJ hits and the fans burst into cheers for the next girl out. Aspen walks out with a smile wearing a black and white top over her ring attire. She heads down to the ring and into the ring. Oliver Thibodaux: Talk about an unfair referee. Riley Stevens: Aspen’s one of our most trustworthy people in my eyes. The fans are still cheering as she steps into the ring in between the two girls who show her little respect, staring a hole into her. She is handed the belt from Livi with force, she holds it up in the air then hands it out and calls for the bell. DING DING DING! Right away Liviana shows her distain for the girl that is the referee tonight, yelling into Aspen’s face probably complaining that she’s there in the first place. Aspen backs off wanting to stay subjective but when Valentine turns around she is caught with a ‘Hooligan Kick’ an Arched Big Boot that nearly rips Liviana’s face off! The fans are stunned as Alexii sadistically smiles up at Aspen, dragging Liviana to the center of the ring and saying “Watch this” before hitting a ‘Curbstomp’ a Standing Indian Deathlock followed by a Head Stomp. The fans are a bit surprised by this early flurry as Alexii takes her focus off of Liviana walking over to Aspen and saying something to her. Valentine is up to her feet holding her face the subject of Ravynova’s first two moves. Ravynova walks over slowly not really treating Liviana like much of a threat then taking her up into the ‘Cradle Shot’ a Cross-Legged Samoan Driver. The fans are completely stunned by the complete dominance here tonight. Aspen tells her something along the lines of, “Just pin her already” but Alexii looks up at her and shakes her head no. She reaches down and locks in the ‘Atlas Shrugged’ a Crossface however Liviana has no way to tap out. Aspen looks at her as Liviana begs for help screaming for it. But Alexii releases it before Liviana can tap out. The fans are booing her actions as she pulls Valentine’s weightless body to her feet. This match was over when it started but she hits the finishing touch with an ‘Ignorance’ a Pumphandle Piledriver to the mat. Alexii commands Aspen to count it and Aspen shakes her head and gets down to count 1…2…3! What a beating. Riley Stevens: Liviana one of our most established wrestlers was just eaten up out here. Oliver Thibodaux: I love this girl; so mean and so ruthless and now she’s the champion, YES! The fans boo as the staff hand in the title and Aspen hands it over to Alexii who rips out of her hand as Aspen gets down on a knee to check on Valentine. She calls to the back for help but from behind we can see Alexii Ravynova lining up with her belt for a crushing blow. Riley Stevens: Oh God, watch out Aspen! Oliver Thibodaux: Finish her off. As Aspen turns around Alexii takes a running stride to crush her but Aspen ducks it and slides out of the ring in a hurry as she shakes her head, not believing what Alexii just tried on her. The camera cuts to Liviana bleeding from the mouth and then back to Alexii and Aspen staring each other down as we head to commercial. After the commercial we are shown replays of what Alexii did to Liviana and then what she tried to do to Aspen. We head backstage where Sarah Sharp walks up to Aspen, who has just gotten backstage there. Aspen rips the mic away and then apologizes to Sarah, then shakes her head realizing she owed none. She turns to the camera to send a message to Alexii. ASPEN CHAUD: "Alexii, I didn’t see you as enemy before but you are one now. You’re in this for the completely wrong reasons. I hope you enjoy your moment tonight because since we all realize Liviana won’t be able to compete at ‘Blackout’ I’m calling out. You don’t have to put your title on the line all you have to do is face me one on one. Thus far you’ve dominated people the last few weeks you’ve dominated the girls en route to the belt, it’s time I show you who the real best lady is around here." Aspen begins to walk away but then stops and walks back still with the microphone pointing at the camera awkwardly lowering her hand and then saying what else she has to say. ASPEN CHAUD: "I just thought I’d say that something is fishy around here. David Sinclair calling the shots, since when does that even happen? Whatever something is weird around here and I am going to get to the bottom of it." Aspen walks away then a few moments later reappears in the screen to hand Sarah her microphone as the segment ends we head to the main event. Motherfucker Of The Year By Motley Crue begins to play. Scott Rydell comes out alongside with Jake Kennedy. The crowd has a mixed reaction for both of these two individuals. The two don’t really want to seem next to each other. However, they walk down the ramp and both enter the ring, awaiting their opponents. Scott Rydell comes out to no music and enters the ring. He appears somewhat different but something that has not changed is his case. He still holds it. He puts it down and one of the ring keepers puts it away. “Horsepower” begins to play. The entire crowd goes insane. Wake runs past Stylez and enters the ring. Stylez follows afterwards. Riley Stevens: Doesn’t Stylez look a little off to you? Oliver Thibodaux: He always does. Wake Walker looks to start off the match against Jake Kennedy. Wake Walker kicks Jake in the gut and then hit’s a stalling suplex. He goes for the cover. 1...2..kick out. Jake Kennedy stands up and hits some elbows to the gut of Wake and begins to fight out of it. Kennedy hit’s a drop kick which makes Wake bounce off the ropes. However, Wake quickly delivers a clothesline. Wake Walker begins to stomp on Jake Kennedy a couple of times and even precedes to hit a splash. He goes for the cover.. 1...2.…kick out! Jake Kennedy tries to fight out of Wake’s chokehold which he immediately applies after this move. Kennedy begins to get support from the crowd but not as much as he usually would. Eventually, he fights out and sends Wake flying to the corner. Oliver Thibodaux: Where’d that power come from? Wake tries to tag in Stylez but Stylez doesn’t look like he even wants to get into the match. He tags himself in however. Jake Kennedy tries to tag in Scott Rydell but Rydell tells Kennedy that it’s too early. Stylez runs at Kennedy only to be hit by several clotheslines. Then, he hit’s a spine buster on Stylez. Stylez appears dazed pretty quick. He stands up. Jake Kennedy hit’s the Breakdown (full-nelson face buster) and then goes for the cover! 1...2.…3! Even Rydell seems surprised as he expected Stylez to kick out. Wake just looks on and shakes his head. Stylez gets on his knee. Wake hit’s the Trickster (drive-by kick) on Stylez. Jake Kennedy and Wake both get Stylez and throw him out! Wake gets the hand of Rydell and Kennedy and lifts them to honor them for their win. However, Stylez kicks Wake in the gut and sends him to the outside. The lights go out. Kennedy is on the floor now bleeding. Rydell quickly leaves the ring and appears scared. Riley Stevens: Wow! Jake Kennedy and Rydell pull off a great win but Kennedy is again on the floor, beaten and bruised by his attacker. Stylez is the only one left standing. Suddenly, a man comes rushing past Rydell and Wake and enters the ring. It’s Alex Starr but none of the superstars know who he is! He quickly grabs Stylez’s case and begins to run to the back. He stops at the ramp and stays there. Stylez gets up, dazed, and tries to leave before realizing that his case is gone. He looks at the ramp and sees Alex with his case. He gets mad and gets a microphone but before he can even speak, Sinclair comes out. DAVID SINCLAIR: Don’t like this Stylez? Neither do I, after that poor performance I’ve decided to give Alex the case. Actually, he’s going to have to face you and beat you for it but it’s essentially giving him the case since we all know you’ll lose. Stylez is now fuming and angry. Alex enters the ring. A bell is rung out of nowhere and Alex takes down Stylex with a tackle. He then picks up Stylez and hit’s a nasty power bomb and goes for the pin. 1.…2...3 it’s over for Stylez. Alex celebrates and grabs his case, showing it off to the crowd as he walks to the back and the show comes to a close. Riley Stevens: Stylez was screwed! Oliver Thibodaux: Oh well. See you all next week!
