GIW — Card

Revolt, 19 December 2012

19 December 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: WEDNESDAY NIGHT REVOLT LIVE! At Poretto Beach – December 19, 2012 on GIW.com Heading to the back we have Ethan Wrathmore standing with his valet Rayne with a big smirk on his face as a camera man is not too far away taking photos of the two of them with a white background behind the two of them. The fans in the building can be heard booing the heck out of them as Aspen Chaud comes into the picture not far away with a smile on her face as she looks on at perhaps the new face of her company as Rayne and Wrathmore have a few more pictures taken of them before Aspen speaks up. ASPEN CHAUD: “Let me have a few shots with the man that very well might be our next cha-” As she speaks she slowly pushes Rayne out of the way but before she can finish her sentence Wake Walker approaches the two of them shaking his head, looking at Aspen as she tries to get in the frame with Wrathmore who doesn’t seem to be too happy about it. WAKE WALKER: “Now now, Aspen. Don‘t make the mistake of counting your chickens before they hatch. Wrathmore and Allioth have as much of a chance as Baldwin and I do. In fact, it‘d be fair to say that the outcome of this match is completely unpredictable considering we have four men who are on the same caliber.” Walker looks over at Aspen who doesn’t seem too pleased to see him here ruining her moment but before she can respond Wrathmore steps in front of him, with a smirk of his own. ETHAN WRATHMORE: “You really think you have a chance Walker? You and everyone else here should know by now that it’s a new age here in GIW. Your days as champion are in the past so is Ace’s, so is Alioth’s, so is everybody else’s.” Wrathmore looks at Walker then even gives a glance over at Aspen as well something she doesn’t notice as she looks on at Wake at that point. ETHAN WRATHMORE: “It’s my time now and tonight, I’ll make sure that I’m the one that’s one step closer to the gold.” Wrathmore grins widely as he looks back at Walker; as Aspen missing his slight to her seems to be on his side in the argument as Walker continues. WAKE WALKER: “You pretend as if I’m a thing of the past, Wrathmore. Soon, you’ll find our personally that this is not true. My previous championship reign was only a prelude to my career. I’m just getting started.” Walker does not back down from this new challenger the two having a long glare at one another as finally Wrathmore shakes his head walking off with Rayne as Aspen and Wake share a glance before she too heads off in a direction leaving just Wake as we head to the ring for the first match of the night. QUOTE For the first match of the night we head to the ring already accompanied by Alexis Terry who stills looks like a train wreck her knee in a heavy brace and clearly not at a hundred percent or even half way to that mark despite her best efforts to mask that having been put through hell the past few weeks, something that will continue tonight as ‘My Sacrifice’ by Creed hits through the building as the fans again get a glimpse at the giant of a man known as The Dark Angel as he approaches the ring from the backstage area, easily the biggest member of the GIW roster. RILEY STEVENS: “We pick up Revolt tonight with another one of Aspen’s almost grueling matches for Alexis as she gets to test this recently signed monster of a man The Dark Angel to see if he really is the real deal.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “This is what you get for messing with the boss.” The Dark Angel makes his slow entrance into the ring not seeming to be phased at all by the woman standing across from him and her accomplishments in GIW as he steps over the ropes into the ring towering over Alexis even from across the ring as the referee steps in, even he doesn’t seem to want to ring the bell but he has to as he calls for it. DING DING DING! This match is now underway despite many in attendance and in the back fearing the worst as an injured Alexis Terry already down and out from the past few weeks now has to face the new challenge that is the big man The Dark Angel. Terry always a fighter rushes at Dark Angel trying to do something but she rushes right into his awaiting arms as he grabs her and hoists her up with ease over her shoulder walking around with her over her shoulder if only to show that he can as the crowd boos him heavily as he finally drops her face first into the corner hitting the ropes then bouncing back with a Big Boot that sends Alexis down to the mat with a thud in a move he calls the ‘Texas Two-Step’. These fans already seem to have lost hope as the big man grabs Terry by an arm only as she throws a desperate elbow that doesn’t phase him, as he hoists her up yet again this time he spins her around and hits a ‘Deadhorse Deadlevel’ a Full Nelson ‘Bubba’ Bomb that sends Terry flying through the air then back down to the mat yet again. RILEY STEVENS: “Can’t say this wasn’t what I feared it would be…” Dark Angel walks around the ring looking down at Terry crawling across the mat looking for some sort of refuge as he seems to not mind putting her through this damage as he grabs her by the throat on the ground and hoists her up from the ground to her feet with just a hand around her throat as he then hoists her up into the ‘South Texas Deathride’ a Chokeslam From Hell that puts Terry on dream street, sprawled out in the ring. It’s now time to finish it…However rather then going for the pin Dark Angel seems to have one more sick thought going through his head as he hoists up the dead weight that is Terry putting her between his legs then pulling her over his shoulder, putting her down with a ‘Blood on a Southern Cross’ a Elevated Powerbomb. Even the referee begs for him to be done now as Dark Angel puts a put foot on her stomach as the referee makes a quick count 1..2..3! REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner by way of pinfall…THE DARK ANGEL!” These fans are no longer in awe of the big man as they were last week they now have decided to go against him for wrecking one of their own as Terry lays out on the mat a mess as the referee immediately calls for help from the back as Dark Angel quickly rolls out of the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “I guess we know now that he is the real deal but we also know he’s a ruthless man as well.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “That’s what it takes in this business Riley.” Dark Angel heads up the ramp as two people on the medical staff rush down beside him as he calmly heads to the backstage area as the medical crew attend to Terry as we go to the interview area backstage. WINNER: THE DARK ANGEL ZACHARY PAGONIS: “Ladies and Gentlemen, Allow me to introduce my guest at this time, he is the newest acquisition to the Wednesday Night Revolt Roster. Please Welcome Austin Rhodes.” Austin enters the scene with his signature smile and gives a handshake to the man that introduced him. Rhodes walks over to a seat which stood inches from where Pagonis was sitting. Rhodes raise his two fingers in a peace sign formation before responding to what Todd has asked. AUSTIN RHODES: “Thanks for that Introduction Zachary. So GIW how are you all today? Surprised? Amazed? Whatever it is i am sure you all of you must be excited with Austin Rhodes coming to GIW. It was quite a surprise to myself as well, getting the message on my phone to even be apart of Revolt was just astounding. You can say I’m skeptical but who wouldn't be? Can you picture it? Thousands of scream fans in attendance yelling my name, yelling at me to hit the Beautiful Disaster. Hell you can even boo me, just the fact i get to wrestle in a company like this is breath taking. So Zachary, i came here for a interview, are there anymore questions you’re going to ask me... or are you just going to sit there?” Austin cracked a smile as Pagonis began to rise the microphone back to his face. Pagonis nodded his head signaling he was ready for the interview to actually condone. Pagonis now seemed more serious as the Camera zoomed in close on him. ZACHARY PAGONIS: “Actually I do Austin. You've already answered how you felt, but what do you plan on accomplishing here in GIW? I'm sure you have lots to say to that.” The Camera now motions on Austin who seemed a little surprised by what he had said. Shelley sat there for a moment looking as if he was thinking of something to say. The silence stood for a good 30 seconds before then Rhodes broke the silence unexpectedly AUSTIN RHODES: “So what does Austin Rhodes want to accomplish? Could it be the GIW Undisputed Championship or could it just be walking out every week to thousands of cheers from these amazing fans? Zachary I don't care whether I win or lose? I care if the fans in attendance are happy. Without a doubt I’d love to go win that GIW Undisputed Championship, but Aspen Chaud doesn't look like he wants to get rid of that anytime soon. So I guess for the moment it's entertaining these GIW fans.” Zachary nods his head as the camera focus on him for one more time. Zachary again went from his casual look to a more serious look asking another question. ZACHARY PAGONIS: “That sounds good Austin. I am really looking forward to what you can do in this ring, but one thing I bring up is the size advantage. What are you going to do for example if you paired in a match-up with the likes of Joshua Acquin, a man a lot bigger and a lot more talented than you?” The camera now back on Austin shows him biting his lip, a good question coming from Zachary but Austin seemed ready as he raised the microphone to his lips, taking a swallow before beginning once more. AUSTIN RHODES: “You know ever since I started wrestling I've been the underdog. Just cause I’m six foot two doesn't mean I can stick with the big guys. You can compare to Kyle Karnage. We've both been downsized for years just cause of our size and stature. Zachary, you bring up a good point with that question but what about the big guys, the majority of them are so slow a turtle could beat them. They may have power but people of my size and stature have something we like to call Speed. Each wrestler in the back has a different ability that sets them from the rest. Every GIW superstar back in that locker room is unique Zachary, each one different in their eyes but yes your question was a good one and i rest with that answer Zachary.” Pagonis looked shocked at what erupted from Austin Rhodes, he nodded as the camera switched back to him. Todd adjusted himself looking at the camera then looking back at the ground. Zachary then asked another question which seemed like it was the last ZACHARY PAGONIS: “It seems like you mean business Austin. Best of luck and hopefully you get your first match soon. But before we go I think we have time for one more question. Is there anything you would want to leave with? Leave us with a note, a guarantee? Condone the interview with a bang as some would put it.” The camera switches to Austin one last time as stands out of his chair now revealing his entire body, which moments earlier was just a still portrait of his face. Austin begins walking around the room before stopping abruptly. He walks to the camera looking straight in the face of it and begins talking. AUSTIN RHODES: “Thanks for that show of luck Zachary, but it means nothing as I need no luck. So you want me to leave with a not huh? Well write it down December 19th, 2012 the day Austin gets debuts on Revolt. A guarantee? Let's see..... oh here's one I guarantee Austin Rhodes will be electric if signed. My name is Austin Rhodes and what you just witnessed was a Rhodes presentation, and if anyone around the world is watching this, blog it, tell your friends, record this do whatever because I am 100% Sure Austin Rhodes will be on a television screen near you.” The scene fades out as the GIW.com exclusive comes to a close with Austin Rhodes getting up from his chair and leaving the GIW production stage. Now after that interview, we all know Austin wants to be here on Revolt. But surprising enough, Austin Rhodes wasn't finished for the night. He had his debut match tonight and he was ready to show everyone that he was there to rock. QUOTE For the next contest Kyle Karnage is already in the ring as many are left to worry if by chance his middle name starts with a K as well, however he seems focused none the less as his music fades and “Verse” by New Fury hits through the arena and these fans give some various responses to the woman who thus far has turned some heads in GIW despite a bit of a losing streak at this point. RILEY STEVENS: “Has there ever been a more impressive star in GIW that has started with a few losses in a row like this? Despite the fact she hasn’t won, you could argue she nearly won the belt last week and almost knocked off Wake Walker in her debut.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Life is about winning and losing Riley and this girl is apparently…a loser.” Kayne takes a few steps then heads up the steps into the ring looking out at the crowd then in at Kyle the two having a bit of a score to settle as a little battle left them out of the ring for the finish in the North Beach title match a week prior and the referee gets in between them ready to let them settle that score as the bell keeper rings the bell. DING DING DING! To start this thing off Kayne is again the smaller wrestler as Karnage lines up ready for the match as the two stroll around one another finally going in for a lock up but Kayne smart enough to know she won’t win that battle, spins around him right into a roll up 1…2…Karnage kicks out but Kassandra rolls right into a different pinning combination 1…2…Kyle panics and rolls through but she does again as well again ending up on top 1…2…Kick Out. Karnage jumps to his feet trying to get off his back now but as soon as he gets up Kassandra is waiting, hitting him with a big time Discus Elbow Smash transitioned right into a DDT as these fans are getting behind her a bit as they have done the last few weeks after seeing her very technical wrestling abilities. Kyle gets up to his feet looking angry about how things have gone so far and Kayne feeds off his mistake as he takes a wild swing that she ducks under and hits a shot of her own as he angrily grabs her and tosses her into the corner. He rushes at her full steam but she rears back and then smacks him in the chin with a huge Superkick that gets these fans on their feet. RILEY STEVENS: “A loser eh Oliver?” Karnage slowly falls down to his back as Kassandra heads through the ropes looking out at the crowd who are waiting to see just what she will do as she jumps off the top rope landing a beautiful Springboard Moonsault as the referee jumps on top 1…2…Kick Out. Karnage barely gets out of this one if only on instinct alone as Kassandra gets up looking for her first win, as Kyle starts to stir a bit as these fans know that this thing might be over as Kassandra doesn’t let him get to his feet before grabbing a hold of his wrist as she connects with a ‘Ripcord Roaring Lariat’ a Wrist-lock transitioning into a Discus Lariat that takes him off his feet to the mat as Kayne takes her time getting down on top and hooking the leg 1…2…3! REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner by way of pinfall…KASSANDRA KAYNE!” These fans seem a little jacked up with the victory as Kassandra for the first time despite several great performances can finally call herself the victory holding her arm in the air with help from the referee. RILEY STEVENS: “And there we have it her first win and frankly I could see her going very far after this win.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “It’s one match, one match is all.” Kayne steps over Karnage having shown who really was the better star after last week’s in ring battle as she heads to the backstage area as do we the viewers. WINNER: KASSANDRA KAYNE In the backstage area we have Zachary Pagonis in the unenviable position of trying to interview the gigantic man known as The Dark Angel a man he finds in the backstage area seeming in thought after his earlier victory. Pagonis taps on his shoulder as The Dark Angel turns towering over Zachary who shows his very obvious fear as he looks up at him. ZACHARY PAGONIS: “Hey, I was just hoping to perhaps get a little more info on your previous statements in our earlier interview. Did not wanting to wrestle lead you to that quick and…ruthless victory?” Pagonis holds over the microphone to the big man but The Dark Angel seems to actually be trying to get out of here quickly as he nods his head while continuing to take off some extra wrist tape. ZACHARY PAGONIS: “Well then a lot of people seem to be really, a little scared of you around here. You might even say, everyone is a little worried what you are capable of.” Just as he says this Jack Redd is walking by him catching wind of what he has just said as his eyebrow raises as he stops The Dark Angel turns towards him looking down at him as Redd looks up at him unafraid, the two sharing a long tense stare down before Redd shakes his head over at Zachary as he walks by him towards the ring as we go from there back into the ring for that man’s first title defense. QUOTE For the next contest a brief ‘GIW North Beach Championship’ logo covers the screen before it disappears and we have Jeremy Baltimore again standing in the ring ready to go and set for action for another title shot, but as he stands there looking to not choke like he has done so often before his music ends and “South Texas Deathride” by The Union Underground hits and these fans hop to their feet cheering for the one champion here in GIW at this point as Jack Redd walks out from the back with that championship belt tossed over his shoulder laying on his broad shoulders as he heads to the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Last week Jack Redd won that North Beach Championship in impressive fashion and you have to think he may have come out of no where and put himself in contention for the Undisputed Championship as well.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “He best worry about this championship right now Riley, it’s on the line.” Redd enters the ring looking over at Jeremy not seeming the least bit worried in this one about losing the belt as he takes it off holding it over his head with one hand in front of Jeremy a taunt that this crowd even gets behind cheering him on as Redd hands it over to the referee who displays it for the crowd before handing it out of the ring then calling for the bell. DING DING DING! This match is for the belt and these fans are excited to see what Baltimore has in store for Jack Redd the fast rising star. Baltimore jumps out the gates like a ball of fury running at Redd full steam only for him to hoist him up and brutally slam him down with a ‘Iron Slam’ a Spinebuster! These fans go wild as Redd gets up, every move he hits coming with a little extra power behind it. Showing as usual no remorse Redd backs up waiting for Baltimore to get up as he starts to go towards him and when Jeremy turns around the last thing that goes through his head literally and visually is the boot of Jack as he nails him with a Running Big Boot! Knowing that he can finish this one, Redd waits for him to get up as Joshua Acquin is shown in the crowd along with his brother clapping on the match as Redd hoists him up with no resistance at all from Baltimore, as he smacks him with a ‘South Texas Deathride’ a Running Throwing Crucifix Powerbomb! Baltimore lays out cold on the mat as Redd steps on top not really putting much pressure on the pin knowing full well it’s over 1…2…3! REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner by way of pinfall and STILL GIW North Beach Champion…JACK REDD!” These fans cheer on Jack Redd but on the outside Acquin jumps over the barricade walking around to where Baltimore has just rolled out of the ring, standing over him clapping on his performance these two still clearly sharing a little bit of hatred for one another as Redd receives his belt in the ring looking out at Acquin who heads back into the crowd having sent his message to Baltimore. RILEY STEVENS: “Well not sure what that was about but Jack Redd is still undefeated and still champion.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Is Baltimore even alive?” These fans clap him on as Redd turns around to see The Dark Angel standing at the top of the ramp glaring down at him, these two’s backstage run in seeming to have sparked something as they share a stare down Redd hoisting up his belt with one arm as we go backstage. WINNER: JACK REDD (Still GIW North Beach Champion) It is now nearly time for the Main Event of the evening, with Wake Walker getting prepped in the backstage area for his main event match that is up next. His usual pre-match meditation going smoothly until Ace Baldwin enters the room, as Wake looks up at him the two clearly not the best of buddies but don’t really seem to have too much tension past knowing that they are in this Race to Five together. ACE BALDWIN: “Are you ready for this?” Walker gets up to his feet to look Ace in the eyes the men sharing a similar stature. WAKE WALKER: “Of course I am, Ace. Any differences we have amongst ourselves should be put aside for tonight. Remember, although it’s possible for one of us to win and the other to not get his win counted towards the race-to-five, it’s better for us to ensure the other stays alive in the match.” The two of them extend hands and shake hands in a manly manner, just testing each other’s resolve before the match doing what it takes to get on the same page. ACE BALDWIN: “You’re right, Walker. You know, I had some doubts about you as my tag partner but we might just be alright after all.” Baldwin pats him on the back as Walker gives him a agreeing nod the two seeming to be on the same page now as Walker responds as he sits down. WAKE WALKER: “I’ll see you out there.” Baldwin heads on his way out as Walker gets some very last minute meditation in as we head to the ring for the main event! QUOTE It’s now time for the main event of this Revolt as a video as usual begins to play on the tron to get this thing all set up. As the video begins we get a little bit of a rundown as Alioth Starre is shown in the ring with the words ‘2 Wins’ under him as quickly a few shots of Ace Baldwin are shown as yet again those same words appear on the screen. As those two shots fade out we are then shown some impressive in ring moves of Ethan Wrathmore before the words ‘3 Wins’ appear on a shot of him celebrating as the video moves along to Wake Walker in the ring as it to shows him victorious after a match as the words ‘3 Wins’ appear on the screen. “Four men enter, two teams, elimination rules where all that matters is being a survivor at the end of the match. Four will enter looking for wins, who or whom will leave with yet another win?” As we head to the ring it’s time for the main event of the evening as the lights go out and an ‘A’ appears on the floor of the ramp as it disappears moments later and Alioth Starre is already half way down the ramp with little emotion in his face as the fans relentlessly boo the man who over the past few weeks has found himself moving up in the Race to Five having lost a week to the others, with a chance to make up for that lost time with a win tonight as his music fades and “Hit the Lights” by Metallica hits just as Alioth enters the ring as with Rayne, Ethan Wrathmore heads down towards the ring with a big wide smirk looking at his team mate in the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “You know with these two’s egos, I have a hard time thinking they will work all that well together Oliver.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “They will be fine Riley, they want to win and that’s what they do, win!” Rayne takes her spot in their corner behind Alioth as Wrathmore heads across the ring to get alongside Starre in the corner for the match as "Who Dat" by J-Cole hits to a ruckus response as usual for the fans beloved Ace Baldwin as he heads out with a smirk that they have come to love the past few weeks as Baldwin heads towards the ring looking in at two men who he has no love lost for to say the least as he steps up onto the ring apron standing outside the ropes knowing Alioth’s unpredictability and not trusting Ethan either as “Master of Ceremonies” by KPM Music hits to another loud response, the cheers never dying down as Wake Walker heads out from the back as well high fiving a few fans along the way, fans he has made a lot of the past few weeks with his new ways. RILEY STEVENS: “This is my team Oliver, as you saw backstage these two are going to work together knowing that for them a win regardless of how is for the better.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “These guys have egos too Riley, maybe even bigger then the men on the other team for God’s sake.” Oliver does preach some very real statements as Walker gets up beside Baldwin on the ring apron both looking across the ring at Alioth and Ethan as they enter the ring together, all four big stars in the ring ready to go as the referee gets this humongous match started off. DING DING DING! The stage is set for another one of Revolt’s huge main events as so many variables lie in the making here tonight, the match starts off as so many before it has as Alioth Starre opts to start for his team while Ace Baldwin does the same on the other side, these two long time rivals slowly circle one another in the ring. These two’s history is a long one, and they go in for a lock up. As usual Ace starts to back the smaller Starre back into the ropes but Alioth always so crafty hits him with a big knee to the gut. Doubling him over now Alioth tosses him into one of the corners without their partners hitting him with a Clothesline followed up quickly by a Chop Block as Baldwin walks out from the corner. The fans boo him as Baldwin holds the back of his knee. Ace gets up none the less a little gingerly on the knee as Alioth grabs him and tosses him into his corner as we now have a chance to test just how well these two can work together. Starre tags into Ethan as these fans boo him as soon as he enters the ring, with Starre holding Ace in place Wrathmore decks him with a Dropkick that sends him down into the corner again. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “What were you saying about them working together Riley?” With that Wrathmore hoist up a man that has become something of a rival the past few weeks in Ace, as he tosses him off the ropes and on the rebound Ethan drops him with a vicious Spear dropping him down to the mat as these fans groan hoping for Ace to hulk up, needing that tag. Before he makes a cover Wrathmore taunts Walker then jumps on top 1…Kick Out. The hesitation made it even easier for Baldwin to kick out as Ethan shrugs it off not seeming too worried about it as he hoists him up to his feet again this time hitting him with a Spinning Heel Kick that seems to stun him as he heads across the ring pushing Walker off the ropes then turning around and running into a Leg Lariat on Baldwin, again making the cover on him 1…2-Kick out. Still not enough to put Baldwin down as the crowd gets behind him, wanting him to make it to the corner and it seems like he may have a shot as he pushes Wrathmore away only for Ethan to catch a hold of his leg before he can make it to the ropes. RILEY STEVENS: “Alioth and Wrathmore are trying to take out Ace in this one.” Ace with his foot caught tries to make a dive for Walker’s hand but just barely comes up short as Ethan pulls him back to the center of the ring finally back over to his corner where he tags in Alioth. Hitting him with a Arm Drag as soon as he makes the tag Ethan jumps up for a Standing Moonsault quickly exiting the ring afterwards as Starre now waits for him to get up knowing that he can end this one right here and now and get the win in this another battle between Ace and Alioth as Walker desperately wants into the match, but as soon as Ace gets up Alioth hits the ‘Stardust’ a Facebreaker Knee Smash! With all the damage already done to him Ace doesn’t have it in him to kick out as Starre hooks the leg 1…2…3! Ace Baldwin is Eliminated! With that these fans are a little distraught but go wild as Walker jumps into the ring finally able to get his hands on Alioth but only wanting to toy with him Alioth tags in Wrathmore and quickly exits the ring. Wrathmore caught off guard and Walker angry, leads to him hoisting Ethan over the ropes slamming him down in the ring as he looks to release a bit of pent up aggression on Wrathmore if he can’t get a hold of Starre, or at least get rid of Ethan so he can. Walker lets him get up and delivers shot after shot to Wrathmore’s chest taking away all the air out of him, finally capping it off with a Reckless Uppercut that drops the man with four wins. Walker gets a glimpse over at Starre on the ropes, wanting to get rid of Ethan so that they can do battle in the ring as Ethan gets up and Walker wraps his arms around him hoisting him over his shoulder into a German Suplex! These fans go wild as Walker is getting it rolling for his team, these fans all on their feet believing in him overcoming the odds. RILEY STEVENS: “Can Walker do this?” Not yet finished with Ethan he waits for him to get up, as Ethan does so groggily only to hoist him up over his shoulders dropping him down to the ground with a big Powerslam. Wrathmore in some trouble decides to try to explode to his feet only to end up with Walker putting him back down with a Spinebuster that shakes the ring. Finally knowing that he can finish this one to get to Starre; Walker hoists up Ethan over his shoulder going for the ‘Lethal Injection’ but with him in the air Ethan rakes at his eyes to blind him as the move causes Wake to release him dropping him to the mat as Wake falls into the corner holding his eyes in pain as Ethan heads over to the corner tagging in Starre! These fans boo as Alioth can now pick up the scraps in this one. RILEY STEVENS: “Oh no, Alioth is going to face a blinded Walker!” Alioth steps through the ropes and takes only a few steps across the ring before he is tapped on the shoulder by Ethan Wrathmore and as he turns around Ethan stabs him in the back with a ‘Hit the Lights’ a Pele Kick! These fans are in shock as Starre stumbles across the ring from the move right into a still blinded Walker barely able to see more then just the outline of a man backing into him as he rolls him up on instincts alone 1…2…3! Walker gets the blind pin on Starre after Ethan betrays him. Alioth Starre is Eliminated! These fans are still in shock over the move but before Walker can even get up, probably not really even knowing who he just eliminated he is hit with a huge blatant low blow by Wrathmore! Surely a DQ but the referee is too focused on rolling Starre out of the ring and when he turns around all he sees is ‘The Cut Above the Rest’ a Scoop Legged Stunner! These fans boo angrily as Wrathmore jumps on top with a big smirk having effectively screwed every other man in the match, his partner and Walker as the referee makes the count 1…2…3! REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is the sole survivor…ETHAN WRATHMORE!” These fans are in a frenzy as Ethan Wrathmore just used an eye rake and a low blow to put away Walker as well as screwing his own team mate to ensure he’d be the only man leaving with a win as Rayne steps into the ring to join him in the celebration as the referee raises his arm not realizing the two calls in this match he just blew. RILEY STEVENS: “I can’t believe this Oliver, Ethan Wrathmore just screwed over his partner and Walker to pick up the win and move himself within one of the title.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “We’re looking at a champion right there Riley, a bonafide champion.” The fans in the building do not seem to agree with Oliver judging by their harsh boo’s as Starre gets up to a seated position on the outside probably just now realizing what happened angrily holding his neck while Walker is still down on the mat, his sight probably just now returning as Wrathmore and Rayne hold up four fingers in the center of the ring knowing that they are one away from the belt. RILEY STEVENS: “Well next week we are off due to the holiday’s but a week after that GIW will be returning and Ethan Wrathmore will have a shot at becoming the GIW Undisputed Champion. For better or worse…” Walker now starts to get his wits about him in the corner looking to be in a bit of pain as the show fades with Wrathmore standing in the ring finding a way to get to four, one way or another. WINNER: ETHAN WRATHMORE
