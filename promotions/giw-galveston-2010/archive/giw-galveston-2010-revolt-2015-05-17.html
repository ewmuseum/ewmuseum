GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS SUNDAY NIGHT REVOLT 5/17/15 FROM KAUFFMAN ARENA IN GALVESTON, TEXAS BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! “Over and Under” by Egypt Central begins to blast through Kauffman Arena as fireworks pop into the sky outside. The fans are going nuts as we pan around to show signs such as “I’d Book It”, “Get Well Soon Chris”, and “I’m the Sole Survivor” waving around. We cut to the commentators at ringside. SARAH SHARP: "Ladies and gentlemen, welcome to Sunday Night Revolt live on Fox Sports Southwest. We are one week away from Extreme TV, and we have one hell of a show planned for you tonight. I’m Sarah Sharp joined alongside by broadcast partner for the evening, the lovely Oliver Thibodaux." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Oh Sharpie, I don’t think I’m the lovely one at this booth. But you do have one thing right, this show is going to be off the hook. With Extreme TV right around the corner, everyone on the roster will be looking to show what they’ve got." SARAH SHARP: "Tonight our main event pits The Sir against Alioth Starre in what I fully expect to be a match to remember. Last week, The Sir managed to pick up a win over Matt Kluge, who was also returning to the company. He really shocked the entire promotion by pulling that off last week, and he has the chance to do it again this week." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "He definitely proved he belongs in the main event scene with that win. I wouldn’t be surprised if he pulled off another upset. The question is, how many times can he do this before we stop calling it an upset? If anyone has stepped up while Callum has been off the show, it has been The Sir." “Not I” by Demon Hunter begins to play as The Sir steps onto the ramp to an extremely positive reaction from the fans. RUBY REYNOLDS: "Please welcome… from Halifax, Nova Scotia… The Sir!" The fedora clad star made his way down to the ring slapping hands with a microphone in his possession. SARAH SHARP: "Well here he is, kicking off the show tonight." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "With a match like he has later on in the night, who could be surprised. And these fans are pumped to see him." He slid into the ring as his music died down. He stroked his beard as the fans began to chant his name. THE SIR: "Never in a million years did I think I would get a reaction like that from you all. But it is here, and I am proud to get it." More cheers from the fans as The Sir smiles. THE SIR: "The past few weeks, GIW has been lucky enough to have legends like Matt Kluge and Alioth Starre return to the company. When guys like that come back, people begin to wonder, and rightfully so, how the current talent stacks up to the talent from the past. Last week, I proved that I could go toe to toe with Matt Kluge. I made him tap out for what, as far as I can tell, was the first time in his GIW career." Cheers from the fans at the thought of Matt Kluge tapping out. THE SIR: "This week, I plan to do the same for Alioth Starre." More cheers. The Sir nods with a stone face. THE SIR: "This business is all about paying your dues. I’ve been with Galveston Island Wrestling for a while now, and I think I have more than paid mine. Now, there is no one in the back that can deny my credibility as a main eventer. Because of that, I am coming out here tonight to make one thing perfectly clear - " The Sir moves closer to the hard camera. THE SIR: "I don’t care who walks out of Extreme TV with the Undisputed Championship. Whether it is Chris Callum, the man none of you think can be defeated, or Johnny Violence… it doesn’t matter. I know that I have proven myself as a worthy number one contender." This draws a favorable reaction from the fans as well. THE SIR: "While everyone else has to fight through Sole Survivor to get their title shot, at Category 5, it should be me getting mine. Because more than anything else -" “Off With Her Head” by Icon for Hire hits the PA system, cutting Sir off. The fans begin to boo as Alexis Terry, David Sinclair, Blade Vega, Bronx Valescence, and Zuri Chastain step onto the ramp. Together, they are the Main Event Monarchy. ALEXIS TERRY: "Sir… Sir… Sir. Look at you. You get one big victory and all of a sudden you think you’re a big fish in this pond? Well let me make one thing perfectly clear – without me, you would be nothing. You would still be fending off Chuck Loveman in curtain jerking matches. I made you." Bronx claps several times as Alexis points down the ramp to The Sir. ALEXIS TERRY: "But, of course, you decided to bite the hand that fed you." The Sir shook his head as Terry continued on. ALEXIS TERRY: "I don’t know if you thought I was through with you yet. If you did, you’re even more stupid than I imagined. Because when someone bites me, I bite back. I’m going to make it my new mission to never let you hold the Undisputed Championship in this company." She laughed loudly as she pressed on. ALEXIS TERRY: "I took your beloved Television title, and now I’m going to keep your Undisputed title dreams in my clutches as well. Just know that YOU made this happen." More boos from the crowd. Sarah Sharp can be heard on commentary scoffing at that claim. ALEXIS TERRY: "Because if you would have stuck with me, I could have given you the world. All I asked was that you help me achieve my goal of becoming Deep South Champion first, but you couldn’t even wait your turn. Of course, I managed to become a triple crown champion… and a grand slam champion, without your help, but that just goes to show how useless you really are, Sir." She adjusts the title belts on her shoulders as she turns to Zuri. ALEXIS TERRY: "If you want to see someone who isn’t useless… take one look at the beautiful woman standing to my right. Sir, this is Zuri Chastain, the newest member of my Main Event Monarchy, and she made the decision to follow me because that is what is best for her career. She is smarter than you ever will be." Alexis tosses her arm around the woman as continues to speak. ALEXIS TERRY: "But you’re right. You beat Matt Kluge last week, fair and square. You may beat Alioth this week too, but either way you’re next in line for a title shot. That’s fair. But people don’t just get title shots without earning them. I had to beat four other men to earn my shot at the Deep South Championship, and I think you should have to earn your way as well." The camera cuts over to The Sir who seems interested. ALEXIS TERRY: "I’ve already gotten it approved by Paul Marshall. At Extreme TV, you’re going to face the winner of Bronx Valescence and Blade Vega tonight on Revolt in a one on one match with a shot at the Undisputed Championship at Category 5 on the line. But of course, this is EXTREME TV. There are no rules. So while this is a one on one match, you should expect the full force of the Main Event Monarchy to be at ringside to ensure the result goes as planned. Bronx Valescence and Blade Vega have proven that they have what it takes to finish what I started with Callum, and they also have some unfinished business with Johnny Violence as well. So no matter who comes out on top later tonight, your next champion will be… royalty." Alexis finally drops the mic, and The Sir is quick to respond. THE SIR: "If you think that’s what you have to do Alexis, then fine. I don’t know how many times I have to run over your stooges before you decide to step into the ring with me yourself." Alexis rolled her eyes as the fans cheered. ALEXIS TERRY: "You won’t last long enough in GIW to step into the ring with me." “Off With Her Head” picks back up as we cut to the commentary booth. SARAH SHARP: "Alexis Terry is making her next goal perfectly clear – stop The Sir from becoming Undisputed Champion." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "As if it wasn’t enough of an uphill climb for The Sir already, now he has to deal with the Monarchy. I pity that man. The odds are stacked against him." SARAH SHARP: "Ladies and gentlemen, we are going to take a quick commercial break, but when we come back, you will see Alexis Terry in action with the TV Championship on the line." We fade out. Kluge calls out to Alioth to get his attention. They lock eyes and Matt slowly draws thumb across his throat in a threatening manner. Alioth’s eyes burn with fury. Sir rolls out of the ring and chases Bronx Valesence up the ramp screaming and cursing the whole way. Meanwhile back in the ring Alioth continues to stare down Matt Kluge. Their eye’s fixed in an epic battle of will. Neither man blinks. Instead Kluge just smiles his evil, dastardly smile and slips off the apron. He mouths “Next week” as climbs down and backs away from the ring. Alioth nods, raising an arm above his head in victory but he never takes his eyes off of Kluge. WINNER: ALIOTH STARRE
