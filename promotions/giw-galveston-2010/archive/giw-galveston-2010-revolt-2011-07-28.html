GIW — Card

Revolt, 28 July 2011

28 July 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: THURSDAY NIGHT REVOLT LIVE! From Courville Stadium – July 28th, 2011 BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! The show begins at Courville stadium in front of a massive crowd of over five hundred screaming wrestling fans. In the ring, Riley Stevens is standing by with the six participants of the Seize the Moment Ladder Match. As the fireworks fizzle, he quiets down the fans and begins to speak. RILEY STEVENS: “Ladies and gentlemen, welcome to Thursday Night Revolt! We have one hell of a show planned for you as we are only one week away from Moment of Glory II, the biggest show of the year! Inside the ring with me right now are six of the brightest stars in the GIW universe - they will compete in the second annual Seize the Moment Ladder Match. This isn’t just any normal tag team match, you must first pin two of your opponents to be eligible to ascend the ladder and grab the briefcase that contains the contract for a GIW Undisputed Title Match at any GIW event for the next year. Last year, the winner was Johnny Stylez - and although he was able to Seize the Moment, he could ever win the title. That is a result no one here could possibly want to mimic, so, how are you all going to make sure that when you walk out of Moment of Glory II, you wont have wasted your efforts?” Riley Stevens walks over to Ace Baldwin, who seems annoyed at the question. Ace takes the microphone and begins to speak. ACE BALDWIN: “Look, I have been here for two matches, and I am already within spitting distance of the GIW Undisputed Championship. I’m not a sub-par wrestling fraud like everyone else in this ring, when I have a chance like this… Ace Baldwin will always Seize the Moment.” Mardukas and Cross share a laugh in the corner of the ring. Riley takes the microphone and hands it to Katya Rose who is dying to speak. KATYA ROSE: “Ace you come from the slums of the wrestling world. Weren’t you a part of that MWA dream team that we squashed? I am a woman, a strong woman. You MEN think you have the upper hand, but I am quicker and smarter than all of you oafs. Mardukas, do you even know what a contract is? Or a breifcase? You will probably try to pull down a light you idiot. The fact is that I am the next GIW Undisputed Champion, why? Because I am better than everyone else in this company and I deserve it!” Leo James begins to speak on a second microphone that was handed to him by Jamal Jackson at ringside. LEO JAMES: “God will you please quit screaming? You all are forgetting that I made a GIW Legend pass out last week, and I am already a champion. So, we all can sit back here and say we are going to win… but the fact of the matter is that I am the only true choice to win this match.” David Graves takes Katya’s microphone and begins to speak. DAVID GRAVES: “I was the first one to qualify for this match. Blake Beckett knew it was my turn for a title shot, and so he knew exactly where to put me. I am destined to win this match… and even though there are plenty of great competitors in this match, I know I have the heart to carry me to victory.” “Now I’m That Bitch” by Livvi Franc hit’s the PA System, the infamous music of Aspen Chaud. Courville Stadium erupts in boos as she walks out to the ramp, Riley Stevens quickly exits back to the announce desk. ASPEN CHAUD: “Listen to you all. Little puppies barking at each other for one bone. Well, I’m the big dog, and that is my bone. You all can keep arguing over who will get a chance at MY title, but the fact of the matter is that none of you will be able to win it. You all will follow in the footsteps of Johnny Stylez. Look at what we have in the ring. Ace, I love you sweetie but you don’t have what it takes yet. Sorry. The truth hurts. Katya, no matter how much you want to be like me, it wont ever happen. You will always be the ‘other’ woman in the company. And David Graves… you couldn’t even win the Television title. Mark Cross and Hank Mardukas - you guys can try to be the likeable guys that focus on the ‘wrestling’… but nice guys finish last. Is there anyone I forgot? Oh yeah. Leo James. Great win over Kluge last week, thanks for weakening him up for the Shadow squirt. But I’m not here just to belittle you all, I have an announcement - and it doesn’t concern any of you, so just go ahead and leave the ring. Goodbye. See ya around.” Aspen slowly walks down the ramp, patting the shoulders of everyone who is leaving the ring. She slowly climbs up the steps and slides through the ropes to massive amount of boo’s from the crowd. ASPEN CHAUD: “Alright, now that the rubbish is gone we can let the star of the show make the announcement! I am still the Revolt Co-GM, but since La’Sarah Audric is in a crying mess… I am promoting myself the FULL Revolt General Manager! But that’s not why I am here either! I have another announcement! I am making a new rule - I cannot be touched by anyone who isn’t in Absolution! No one, not ever! Not a hand shake! Not a kiss! Definitely NOT in a wrestling match! And anyone who defies this rule… WILL BE FIRED! HAHAHA!” Boo’s shake the ring, Aspen stands in the center of the ring soaking it all in as we cut to commercial break. QUOTE DING! DING! DING! JAMAL JACKSON: “The following contest is a singles match scheduled for one fall. Now entering the ring, weighing in at 231 pounds; from Boston, Massachusetts… David Graves!” “Become The Catalyst” by All That Remains plays over the intercom to a positive crowd. David Graves enters the ring and begins to warm up as Jamal continues the introductions. JAMAL JACKSON: “And the opponent, weighing in at 265 pounds; from Calgary, Alberta, Canada… “The Grim Reaper”, Seth Seeker!” “Nightmare” by Avenged Sevenfold hit’s the PA to a mixed response from the crowd. Seeker stands on the ramp, a menacing looking man and stares down Graves before entering the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Here we go, two men of very different sizes and skills going at it one on one in the opening match of Revolt! This should be a good one!” Seeker and Graves lock up, but Graves never stood a chance - Seeker throws him to the ground and immediately attempts a rear naked choke, but Graves manages to grab the rope on the right side of the screen. Seeker backs off long enough to let Graves stand. Graves knows the match just about ended right there. They lock up again, then time Graves drops to his knees and pulls Seeker’s legs out from under him before pummeling him with bar room style punches. Graves whips Seeker into the corner, then ties him to the tree of woe. Graves shoves his foot right on Seeker’s neck, the ref counts to four, then the hold is broken, but Seeker is still tied. Graves goes to the opposite corner, then charges with a baseball slide that hits Seeker right in the jaw! He goes for a quick cover, but gets a two count on the big man. Graves goes to the top rope and dives for a cross body, but Seeker catches him in mid air! Powerslam from Seeker to Graves! Two count; Seeker keeps Graves on the mat. He tries for the choke again, but Graves always seems to be able to find a rope. Seeker pulls Graves up and whips him against the ropes; Graves hit’s a flying forearm! Graves hit’s another! And another! He is building momentum! The crowd is behind Graves! Graves connects with a superkick! RILEY STEVENS: “David Graves is moments away from winning this match!” He climbs to the top rope; the fans know his finisher is coming up. He goes for the 450 splash, but Seeker picks up his knees. Seeker props himself up on the turnbuckle to rest while Graves may have cracked ribs. Seeker smiles, then pulls himself up. As soon as Graves stands he charges for a spear; countered! Seeker runs into the post! Graves tries for a rollup… two count only! OLIVER THIBODAUX: “God, Aspen is a genius. Only she would think to put these people in hard matches so they wont be able to cash the case in on her when she wins the belt. So smart.” Seeker counters the suplex into a reverse DDT that shakes the ring. He locks in his finisher as quickly as possible… the “Graveyard” triangle arm-bar. Suddenly, boo’s can heard louder than ever… its Nick Chaos on the apron! Seeker and the ref go over to yell at Chaos, Graves is stirring. RILEY STEVENS: “These two men had an altercation earlier on Extreme TV, this must be Nick Chaos coming back for payback! Look behind you Seeker!” Graves spins Seth around, only to receive a knee to the gut and a jackhammer slam that is called the “Don’t Fear the Reaper”! Graves is motionless, but Reaper stands up and stares at Chaos who is far up the ramp now. Reaper puts a boot on Graves’ chest and points out to the ramp. 1...2.…3~! RILEY STEVENS: “That’s the match! And I think Seth “The Reaper” Seeker proved a point to Nick Chaos too. What a showing!” Seeker looks at the decimation in the ring, then realizes what could have happened. He exits the ring and runs backstage to find Chaos. WINNER: SETH SEEKER We cut backstage after a commercial break to see Seth Seeker walking backstage with a furious look on his face. He sees La’Sarah Audric’s office and barges in the door. She is plopped in her chair with food splattered everywhere and ice cream dripping on the floor. Seeker begins to walk out, but Audric mutters a ‘stop’ and Seeker turns around. LA’SARAH AUDRIC: “Don’t you leave me like he did! You stay here and tell me what you want or I swear I will scream and scream until the windows break. I warn you!” Seeker seems intimidated by this insane woman. SETH SEEKER: “Uhm, did you see what Nick Chaos just tried to pull out there. If he wants to interfere in my matches, that little twerp needs to be squashed. Give me a match with him a Moment of Glory II.” Audric swallows some cookies, then begins to speak. LA’SARAH AUDRIC: “Tonight is my last night you know. I asked Blake to let me run the new merchandise department. I’ve always liked to design clothes. I will need new clothes. I’m pregnant. It’s his baby, and he just left me. He QUIT ON ME! He ran away, at least he didn’t get to keep his title. You want a match? Ok. I’ll give you a match. A match for his title. You will fight Nick for the Television Title… is that alright?” Seth nods, he is obviously uncomfortable around this woman. She shovels some more Oreo’s into her mouth and wipes hair out of her face. LA’SARAH AUDRIC: “Good then. Good. Good. Good. Sarah did good. Great. I did great. I always do great. Good. No. Good. Yes. Jones. ASSHOLE!” She throws a stapler that flies through the air and carves a hole through the drywall. Seeker ducks out of the door as we cut to commercial break. QUOTE JAMAL JACKSON: “The following contest is scheduled for one fall. Now entering the ring; from Memphis, Tennessee; weighing in at 280 pounds, Hank Mardukas!” “The Mob Goes Wild” by Clutch hit’s the PA system to an instant ovation from the fans. From the back walks Hank Mardukas down to the ring. He sits in his corner, waiting for his opponent. JAMAL JACKSON: “And his opponent, weighing in at 234 pounds; from Hollywood, California; Logan Abbott!” “Burn In My Light” by Mercy Drive hits, the fans boo the hell out of Logan as he makes his way down to the ring. He slides into the ring so that referee Daniel Fredrickson can do his pre-match check. RILEY STEVENS: “Logan Abbott has been studying under the wing of the GIW Undisputed Champion, Alioth Starre, over the past few weeks. Tonight, he will test his new skills against a revitalized Hank Mardukas. Something has got to give tonight Ollie!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “In all seriousness Riley, both of these guys are jokes. Hank Mardukas is old enough to be the father to some of the members of our roster, but at least he doesn’t have to be a lackey to someone else. Did you know Logan Abbott is actually a year older than Hank? Gross.” DING! DING! DING! Hank Mardukas and Logan Abbott lock up with Abbott gaining the upper hand quickly. The two trade grapples back and forth for a bit before Mardukas eventually backs Logan into a corner and begins to land blow after blow. The ref backs Mardukas off, and Logan takes advantage of the opening with a poke to the eye, followed by a dropkick. Logan climbs up to the top rope and panders to the crowd. While he is totally immersed in the moment, Mardukas pulls him back down and hits him with his finisher! The chest slam! 1...2.….3! OLIVER THIBODAUX: “HAHAHAHA! That was the most amusing thing I have seen in a long time!” RILEY STEVENS: “Logan Abbott decided to play to the crowd rather than wrestle the match, Mardukas just capitalized on a poor decision!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Go back to your basement Logan!” Logan rolls out of the ring holding his face like he has no idea what just happened. Mardukas plays to the crowd in the ring while we cut to commercial. WINNER: HANK MARDUKAS After the match we cut backstage to see Blake Beckett talking to a member of the production team, an ovation from the audience can be heard. When American Gold walks up from behind the camera, the crowd explodes. MATT KLUGE: “Well, well, well. Look what the cat drug in. Where is Walker, Blake? I‘m assuming he is waiting for you back in your office?” Blake shoos the production member away and faces Kluge. BLAKE BECKETT: “Look Kluge. I don’t know what the hell I did you way back in the past in UWA… or if my family screwed your family over way back in the past but I am sick and tired of you constantly trashing me every time you do an interview. Free speech is fine, but you push your boundaries. I am the boss. I employ you. I suggest you choose your words wisely, tonight especially. I just came back from a pretty intense family situation only to come back and see GIW in ruins. So get off your high horse and prance on over to your match before you miss it.” Kluge chuckles, then exits behind Beckett who shakes it off and continues in to his office. He opens it up only to see Trent Lost and Katya Rose making out on the desk. They both look at Blake, embarrassed. Blake slams the door and keeps walking down the hallway. He finds Audric’s office and opens the door, a stapler flies through the doorway. Blake slams the door yet again. Furious, he continues down the hallway and out the EXIT door, pulling his phone out at the same time. QUOTE “Fight Song” by Marilyn Manson hits as the fans immediately begin to boo the team they know as the Faces of Death. Walking out from the back they look confident and ready to go. JAMAL JACKSON: “First entering the ring weighing in at a combined weight of four hundred and forty five pounds hailing from The Conscience of Wrestling…the Faces of Death!” The crowd shows little remorse with their boos as they head to their corner trying to ignore them as "Suffer" By Shawn McPherson hits and the crowd’s boos just continue only perhaps getting louder as next up is the team of The Shadow entering behind Katya Rose who doesn’t seem pleased by her entrance theme not being used though she’s the one on the tron. JAMAL JACKSON: “Next entering the weighing in at a combined weight of four hundred and ninety pounds the team of The Shadow and Katya Rose!” Rose enters the ring first heading to her corner wearing a smirk as The Shadow follows not far behind into the corner with her. As last but not least “Dawn Of A Million Souls” by Ayreon hits and the crowd goes insane for perhaps the most loved duo in GIW history as out comes the tag champions from the back with huge smiles. JAMAL JACKSON: “And last entering the ring weighing a combined weight of five hundred and twelve pounds they are YOUR GIW TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS! American Gold, the team of Matt Kluge and Leo James!” This ignites the fans further as they head down the ramp high fiving fans left and right, as they slide into the ring jumping up and holding up their belts in the air which the Faces of Death look at enviously. RILEY STEVENS: “Three teams, one member of each team in the ring at the same time. First pin wins…Huge match up as Faces of Death looks to become Number One Contenders and Katya Rose and The Shadow look to get a little parting shot at their Moment of Glory opponents.” They all seem ready as the referee takes the belt and hands them out as the three teams take their start. DING DING DING! It is decided right away by Kluge and James that Kluge will start while Azriel gives permission for Demon Night to start as The Shadow enters the ring letting Katya stand in the corner as the three begin to circle one another. Though there are no teams in this match Night and Shadow both begin to stalking Kluge into the corner seeming to have an understanding that he’s the man they are after. Matt takes some steps back before hitting the corner and realizing that his only option was to attack. Jumping forward he punches Shadow then spins around and punches Night trying to find out of the empty corner. However as he throws another right at Shadow its Night that tackles him into the corner. The crowd boos this double team action as both men proceed to stomping away at a defenseless Kluge in the corner. Stepping back Shadow waits as Night pulls him out and nails him with a DDT! The fans boo as Demon takes the chance to pin 1....The Shadow pulls him up off of him. The two stare eye to eye, as finally what we all expected to happen their little team breaks down as they begin to exchange lefts and rights. Neither man letting down these two monsters show no signs of letting up until Kluge gets to his feet. At the same time both men stop staring at him, as he sighs and runs at them catching them off guard with a huge Double Clothesline that sends the fans into cheers. Night rolls out of the way and Shadow gets up walking right into a Belly to Belly Suplex! The fans go wild as The Shadow rolls out of the way now as Demon Night gets up and receives a Snap Suplex as Kluge hops up to his feet, making the tag into James! Together they walk over to Shadow picking him up and whipping him off the ropes before catching him with a Clothesline then duel Elbow Drops as Kluge heads out of the ring turning it over to his partner. RILEY STEVENS: “American Gold showing why they are the GIW Tag Team Champions.” James gets to his feet stalking Night from behind, rushing at him and hitting a High Knee that sends him falling back into the corner. Grinning Leo spins around then runs at him nailing him with a Big Boot as he stumbles out of the corner! The fans go wild as Leo goes for the cover 1…2…Kick Out! The fans all seem confident in the champions as James gets to his feet noticing some stirring from Shadow going to pick up he is caught off guard as Shadow hops up clutching him by the throat. Still seeming a bit battered from earlier he still throws him up with a Single Handed Chokeslam! The crowd gasps as Leo hits hard and Shadow falls back into his corner where Rose tags in. The fans boo her entry as she walks over to James standing over him taunting him with a big loser sign. Before she can go to pin him, the surprise entrant Azriel who made a unseen tag to Night jumps in throwing her to the side looking only to go after James mounting him and unleashing some brutal punches. Feeling a bit insulted by this Rose now shoves him off the two now sharing a little eye to eye confrontation. When boom, a huge slap connects with the face of Azriel. All this time James is recovering. Azriel spins around going for Katya but as he does James does the same only for Katya to duck and send James into a tackle of Azriel! Rose takes this chance to school boy James 1…2…Kick Out! The fans again breathe a collective sigh of relief as Leo kicks out and all three get to their feet. This time Katya goes for a Pele Kick on Azriel but it’s him that ducks causing the kick to land on James, Azriel takes this chance to throw Katya into his corner rushing at her hitting her with a clothesline, as Night tags himself back in entering the ring as Katya stumbles out of the corner right into a Double Suplex from the Faces of Death! RILEY STEVENS: “Perhaps the dark horses Faces of Death will come through with the win.” As he makes the cover on Katya 1…2…In comes James to kick him off though Katya also kicked out. The fans cheer it any way as James who seems a bit drained from the kick, grabs him and whips him off the ropes only for Night to crush him with a Clothesline! Again he makes the quick tag in to Azriel who re-enters the ring and again they nail him with a ‘Forced Dreams’ a Hip Toss into Neckbreaker! The crowd boos thinking this may be the end 1…2…This time Rose pushes them off saving the match for her team. Night rolls out of the ring looking over at Kluge angrily as Matt calls for a tag. Azriel gets up and walks right into a Hip Toss from Katya followed by a superb Lionsaut with a cover 1…2…Kick Out! On the outside Night now pulls down Kluge from his corner sending the two into a brawl as Night’s anger seems to have got the best of him. James gets up behind Katya who angrily is asking the referee to count faster. When she turns around she does so right into an impressive ‘Lion Strike’ a Piledriver! The crowd goes insane thinking it’s the end but the move sends Katya rolling towards her corner while Leo tries to make the tag only for Kluge not to be there as Azriel tries to do the same but realizing Night is gone as well. Both men turn to one another however Azriel being the more drained man he isn’t able to avoid running into a Big Boot from James! Outside of the ring the two’s brawl continues Kluge and Night seeming unable to break up. However in the ring James takes a second for a breather as The Shadow enters the ring after a tag going after him only for James to duck his Clothesline and hit a Release German Suplex! The crowd goes wild as he looks out at Kluge realizing he’ll have to try to win this alone. RILEY STEVENS: “James and Azriel need a tag but Kluge and Demon Night are going at it.” James shakes his head as Azriel gets up seeming confused by what is going on as he heads right into a ‘Goldie's Lock’ a Torture Rack from Leo James! The crowd goes nuts thinking this is the end but they realize that the referee is now distracted by the fight outside of the ring. During this distraction Katya returns to the ring nailing James with a Low Blow! Dropping Azriel, Katya finishes off the job with Shadow together throwing Leo out of the ring onto both Kluge and Night on the outside! The fans boo as Azriel returns to his feet as the referee turns around Rose just getting out of the ring as The Shadow crushes him with an ‘Instant Death’ a Tiger Driver '91! This shuts the crowd up as Shadow makes the cover 1…2…3! RILEY STEVENS: “It looked as though Leo James may have had it, but that out of the ring brawl between Matt Kluge and Demon Night resulted in the distraction that allowed Katya to low blow him setting up the Instant Death from The Shadow!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “We all watched it you idiot.” The fans boo as The Shadow stands over Azriel whom fell victim to his partners anger as Katya Rose demands the referee raise her hand as on the outside Kluge and James begin to recover forced to watch their rivals celebrate on the final week before ‘Moment of Glory’ as we head to the back. WINNERS: KATYA ROSE & THE SHADOW After the commercial break, we cut to the ring where Zachary Pagonis is standing at the edge of a table with Trent Lost and Alex Castellanos sitting on either side. At the edge of the table sit’s the GIW North Beach Championship with Alex Castellanos’ name engraved in the gold. The two men look at the belt, then back up at Zachary when he begins to speak. ZACHARY PAGONIS: “Alex, Trent. This is the official contract signing for the Last Man Standing Match at Moment of Glory II for the GIW North Beach Championship. Your individual contracts are in front of you, is there anything you would like said before the contract is signed.” Alex motions for Pagonis to hand him the microphone. Pagonis agrees, Alex stares at Trent Lost for a moment before speaking. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “Trent, listen to me closely. Before you sign that contract, think of all the pain you feel during that match. Think of the risks you will have to take. You can’t beat me Trent, you don’t have it in you. But I do care about you, don’t let anyone tell you otherwise. Wrestler to wrestler; man to man… is this what you want? To chase some dream of yours? To try to prove to yourself you are as good as me? You don’t have to do any of that Trent. You’re a…great… wrestler, you don’t have to prove it to anyone.” Trent Lost picks up his microphone and chuckles before speaking to Alex. TRENT LOST: “Alex, let me make something clear to you right now. I can beat you. I have beat you. I deserve that championship; the only reason you have it is because of Aspen’s little shenanigans. This isn’t a match where Absolution is going to run down and take me out, it is you and me. One on one. To win a last man standing match you have to have heart. You have to have passion. You have to care about what you are doing and love it with all you got. You cant bring that to the table. That’s my x-factor Alex. I know that I am a better wrestler than you, you know I’m a better wrestler than you, all of these people know it too. At Moment of Glory, it will be historical fact.” Alex laughs out loud, Pagonis exits. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “You can sit there and bitch and moan about how that title is rightfully yours, but the fact of the matter is that the North Beach Title is mine, and only mine. I beat you Trent, that’s the historical fact.” Trent wastes no time retaliating. He signs the contract, Alex follows. TRENT LOST: “We have gone round and round like this for months. At the end of Moment of Glory, there will only be one rightful champion, and it wont be you.” As soon as Trent stops talking, Alex heaves his microphone right into Trent’s face, then proceeds to do a beat down. Alex lands punch after punch on Trent, but eventually Trent lands one of his own as well. Trent continues to throw punch after punch, then he hit’s a “Lost Cause” on Alex, sending him face first through the table! Trent stands over Alex and poses with the belt before counting to ten as we fade to much needed commercial break. QUOTE Heading back to the ring already inside of the ring both waiting for their opponent is Jack Waters and Trent Lost both ready to go as Johnny Scar now heads towards the ring with the fans cheers behind him as he high fives some fans along the way. JAMAL JACKSON: “And the final opponent weighing in at one hundred and seventy pounds hailing from San Francisco, California…Johnny Scar!” The fans go wild as he jumps up onto the apron and through the ropes heading into the ring as the fans cheer and he looks at his opponents with a smile on his face. DING DING DING! Right away the action starts as Lost goes after Scar only to receive a huge Enziguri that drops him to the mat in a hurry! The fans seem split between Scar and Lost on who to cheer for. Next up is Waters who runs right into a Kesagiri Chop! The fans cheer as Jack falls back into the corner, holding his chest. Rushing at him he hits him with a Jumping Clothesline that sends him to the mat. Trent comes back at him trying for a big time move but he gets hit with a Heart Punch that sets up an Arm Trap Neckbreaker! The fans are stunned at his domination as he covers Trent 1…2…Waters kicks him off. Right away the fans boo him for doing that as he tries to go for a Suplex but the little man does the unthinkable hitting him with a Fisherman Suplex! The fans go wild as instead of going for the pin he heads to the apron jumping off with a Spingboard Moonsault, with the cover 1…2…3! Lost tries to stop it but is too late. Already over. RILEY STEVENS: “Every time we count out Johnny Scar he wins in convincing fashion.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Luck.” The fans cheer him on as he gets to his feet asking for a microphone. Lost gets up heading to the corner seeming impressed himself with Scar while a hurt Waters gets out holding his stomach as he heads towards the back angry with himself for his effort as we return to the ring where Scar now waits with a mic. JOHNNY SCAR: “You know I’ve had some fun here in GIW but unfortunately….I have to leave.” This shocks the fans as he’s only just begun as he drops the microphone as Lost offers his hand for a shake showing his respect. The fans then start to go into a frenzy as into the ring comes Alex Castellanos again with the belt this time he goes for Lost but it’s Scar who takes the bullet due to the way they were angled. This sends Scar into Lost knocking both down, with Johnny Scar seemingly out cold. RILEY STEVENS: “Damn it! Scar is leaving there is no reason for this.” Thinking he’s gotten both men Alex stands over Scar chuckling and waving goodbye to the fans boos. However behind him slowly getting up is Trent Lost. Alex turns around and swings the belt but he’s able to duck out of the way then starts to lay into him with punches. RILEY STEVENS: “Here comes Trent Lost!” The fans go wild as he starts to crush him taking a second to pander to the crowd as he seems to be hulking up but as he does Castellanos bails reaching in and pulling out the North Beach title as he heads back up the ramp his plan thwarted. RILEY STEVENS: “That’s what he gets for that cheap attack from behind! It’ll be Trent Lost and Alex Castellanos their feud finally coming to an end at ‘Moment of Glory II’ as they battle for that belt in a Last Man Standing Match!” These fans seem excited for Lost who immediately checks on the fallen Scar as Alex holds his neck heading towards the back as these two men look at each other for the final time before their face off. WINNER: JOHNNY SCAR We cut backstage to the GIW interview area where Victoria Rio is standing by with the GIW Undisputed Champion, Alioth Starre. VICTORIA RIO: “Thanks for watching GIW; my guest tonight needs no introduction, but he deserves one anyway. He is the GIW Undisputed Champion Alioth Starre!” Alioth takes the microphone from her, then proceeds to take over the interview. ALIOTH STARRE: “With all due respect Victoria, I will be using this time to address the GIW roster and fans. I still exist, and no I am not joking about that. It seems as if over the past weeks I have been placed on the back burner by the powers that be. Everywhere I look it is Absolution this, Trent Lost that… and yet I am still the Undisputed Champion. Earlier in the evening Aspen told the Seize the Moment competitors that they had no chance at obtaining her title. There is one problem with that. She doesn’t have a title. All that Aspen Chaud does is walk around and make rules to benefit her, but she could not win a match if her life depended on it. I enjoy talking just as much as she does, but I never fail to deliver on my word.” We cut to a random area in the hallway backstage where Zachary Pagonis is standing with Mark Cross, who is already in ring gear and ready for a match. ZACHARY PAGONIS: “Quickly Mark, how are you going to go about winning this match?” Mark takes the microphone, but doesn’t stop warming up. MARK CROSS: “Alioth, I don’t know you that well… but tonight we are going to have to work together. I’m willing to put all of my ambitions aside for tonight, and I hope you will too. As much as I want your belt, I want to beat Aspen and Ace more. See, wrestling isn’t about titles for me… its about finding out who the better man - or woman - is. I’ll see you in the ring. No funny business, right?” Cross leaves the scene, we fade to commercial. QUOTE We cut back from the commercial break to see Aspen Chaud in the ring dressed in a black dress, obviously not wrestling attire. “Now I’m That Bitch” by Livvi Franc is playing in the background, she has a microphone in her hand ready to speak. ASPEN CHAUD: “So, since my no touching rule is still in full effect obviously I will not be participating in this match. Instead, I will be a special guest ring announcer and commentator!” Courville Stadium erupts in boos as Aspen stands in the ring laughing. The bell sounds three times, and Ace Baldwin’s music hit’s the PA. ASPEN CHAUD: “Now entering the ring, Ace Baldwin! I don’t really know where he is from, but I think its from somewhere in Nebraska… he gets to fight in a handicap match! Yay!” Ace enters the ring and sits in the corner, staring a hole through Aspen. She offers him a crooked smile, then she continues her introductions when “Life Me Up” by Moby begins to play. ASPEN CHAUD: “And the other team. He loves to fight because he has the IQ of a barbarian and no other marketable skills… the biggest wash-up in the federation… Mark Cross!” Cross enters from the back chuckling at Aspen’s introduction. He slaps the fans hands as he enters the ring, then takes his pace on the apron in his corner. ASPEN CHAUD: “And finally, my personal favorite, the man who I will have the pleasure of beating next Sunday at the second Moment of Glory, Alioth Starre.” Alioth enters the ring slowly then slides in to face Aspen. He mocks her by extending a hand, when she rejects he laughs and heads over to Cross stone faced. Instead of joining the announce booth, Aspen stands on the ring apron in her corner with a microphone. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “It’s because you smell gross Riley.” The match begins with Mark Cross and a very reluctant Ace Baldwin. The two lock up, Ace gets the upper hand with a thumb to the eye followed by a back body drop. Cross crawls back up, only to be sent back down with a textbook dropkick. Ace goes for a suplex, but Cross counters it and pushes him into Alioth’s corner. Tag is made, followed by some double stomping. ASPEN CHAUD: “Don’t worry, I’ll help you Ace! Not!” Alioth goes right to work on Ace by locking in a sleeper hold. Several moments pass with Ace fading, but several well placed elbows to the gut and then a few chops sent Alioth into a vacant corner. Ace lands a few knees to the gut but Alioth with an eye rake to get out of a jam. Followed by a DDT! There is a two count, Alioth goes right back to work. ASPEN CHAUD: “Let’s go Ace! You can do it!” Alioth pulls Ace into the corner and locks in a leg bar before tagging in Cross. Cross pulls Ace up and hit’s a suplex. Ace starts to crawl over to Aspen’s corner, begging for a tag, but she just hops off the apron, wagging her finger. ASPEN CHAUD: “You can’t touch me!” Ace becomes furious. He turns towards Cross and just shakes his head. He rolls out of the ring and promptly exit’s the arena. The referee counts to 10, Ace has been counted out! Cross throws his arms up in the air and chases after Ace. Alioth rolls out of the ring to grab his title and Aspen re enters the ring. Then, Blake’s music hits. WINNERS: MARK CROSS & ALIOTH STARRE After the match is over, Blake Beckett’s music hits and the crowd goes wild, he enters onto the top of the ramp and stares down Aspen for a moment. BLAKE BECKETT: “I go away for two weeks Aspen, two weeks and this entire federation is a joke! You have made matches that make you look good, made insane rules, La’Sarah Audric needs medical help, my office is used a ------- sex dungeon for crying out loud. You have left a title vacant, which is one of the stupidest things you can do, and you have the AUDACITY to try to take my job. I can’t fire you Aspen, you are to valuable of a commodity. Since you and La’Sarah Audric obviously couldn’t handle an ounce of power, I’ve decided I needed to hire a babysitter for you. This person will be the special guest ring enforcer in your match at Moment of Glory too. Please welcome, your former lover, Mr. Joshua Acquin!” “Monster” by Skillet hit’s the PA system as Joshua Acquin walks out beside Blake. The two shake hands and Blake disappears backstage. Aspen is livid in the ring, shaking the ropes, Alioth has wide eyes as he looks up at Acquin. Josh soaks in the cheers. JOSHUA ACQUIN: “It’s great to be back here in Galveston Island Wrestling. I knew this day would come again. I sat at home week after week watching Aspen whore her way to the top. I kept thinking, I remember when she was innocent.” Aspen is screaming at Joshua now from the ring. JOSHUA ACQUIN: “And I remember when I took that innocence away.” Joshua cracks a smug smile and watches Aspen throw a temper tantrum in the ring. JOSHUA ACQUIN: “They call me a nightmare, and for reason Aspen, I can make your life a living hell - and I intend to. This Sunday at Moment of Glory I have been made the special guest enforcer for your match, so if any of your lackeys try to pull a stunt - you will loose match and your title shot. Every GM decision you make from now on, has to go through me. So consider your no touching clause ended.” Aspen leans over the ropes screaming at him. Alioth slithers into the ring. He spins her around, kicks her in the gut and hit’s the fisherman’s suplex! Aspen is motionless. JOSHUA ACQUIN: “Goodnight, Aspen. Sweet dreams.” “Monster” hit’s the PA again as we fade to black.
