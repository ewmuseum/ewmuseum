GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: MONDAY NIGHT REVOLT! Live! From Stewart Beach on Galveston Island – June 18th, 2012 BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! The show begins with the fireworks streaming high into the air as the fans on Stewart Beach go crazy for the ION Television cameras broadcasting LIVE all through the United States. As we pan around the arena, we hear the voices of Riley Stevens and Oliver Thibodaux. RILEY STEVENS: “Welcome all to Monday Night Revolt! Live from Stewart Beach in front of a packed crowd of three thousand wrestling fans. I'm Riley Stevens alongside my partner Oliver Thibodaux, and tonight we have some GREAT action planned for you.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “You are right Riley, for once! Tonight, in the main event, we have Alex Castellanos, the Undisputed Champion, going one on one with an rival, Trent Lost!” RILEY STEVENS: “Also, don't forget... Tonight Ryan Cavallari will publically apologize for his involvement with Aspen Chaud! Oh man, its going to be crazy tonight here on Revolt!” “The Rat” by Dead Confederate hit the speakers and the fans EXPLODED in applause for the interim chairman of GIW, Matt Kluge. He stepped out on to the ramp and made his way to the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Well, it looks like Matt Kluge has something to say to start off the show.” He climbed the steps and slid in the ring, ready to speak. His music died down with the applause. MATT KLUGE: “Everyone knows my love for the sport of wrestling, and thus, I believe a wrestling company should focus on wrestling. As Interim Chairman of GIW I feel like I have already improved the wrestling in the company, but I can do so much more. Trust me.” The crowd cheers loudly at that thought, waiting to see what Kluge says. MATT KLUGE: “I was thinking – we have a Television Championship. Over the years it has been a farce, until Eliza Gray obtained it, now it has value. It has a true champion – but why is it called the 'Television' title? The Television Title is supposed to be reason the fans turn on the television and watch Revolt – so we will be going in a new direction with the belt. It will be defended each and every week on REVOLT!” The crowd cheers loudly at the announcement. MATT KLUGE: “I'm certain Eliza Gray can handle the heat -” “Falling Away” by Curse Icon hits the PA, and the fans pop with boos as Kluge turns around in the ring, staring down Trent Lost as he makes his way to the ring, talking as he walks. TRENT LOST: “Kluge, Kluge, Kluge. Why don't you save the opening segment of the show, for the stars, eh?” Boos, Kluge rolls his in the center of the ring as Lost climbs in to join him. TRENT LOST: “No, I shouldn't be too mean to you old man, because you did me a favor. You knew that I deserved to be Undisputed Champion, so you gave me a match with Castellanos, and I win, I get to be in the Sole Survivor Match.” The crowd boos, “CAST-ELL-AN-OS” chants pick up. TRENT LOST: “But I have things to say, and you are blocking my spotlight... so if you would kindly...” “Warning Shots” hits the PA system and Lokie Lindsay comes out, on the ramp to cheers. He polishes the title belt his his palm then gently lays it on the ground before popping his knuckles. Then, out of the blue, he charged the ring at full speed, and Lost was bracing for impact. SPEAR by Lokie, and the two are rolling around the ring throwing punches and elbows at each other. Evetually Lokie gets up and whips Lost out of the ring and onto the floor. RILEY STEVENS: “Oh man! They are out by ringside!” Lost lands a nice jaw shot that sends Lokie stammering backwards, but Lokie counters with a big boot that bloodies Lost's lip and sends him to the ground. Lokie pulled him up and threw him over hus shoulder. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “This is Lokie when he is really determined to do some damage! And look at Kluge just watching from the ring! Help out your wrestlers BOSS!” KING SLAYER!!! A running powerslam driving Trent Lost through the table. Lokie slid back into the ring next to Kluge and took the microphone. LOKIE LINDSAY: “Do you see that, Kluge? That punk got what he deserved, and I'm going to make sure I get the chance to do it to him again at Category 5.” The crowd goes insane... Kluge nods and claps along with the audience. MATT KLUGE: “That was... incredibly impressive Lokie.” Lokie nods, Trent isn't moving in the remnants of the table. MATT KLUGE: “So impressive... that if Lost is unable to win a spot into the Sole Survivor match, the Legacy title match between you two will be a TABLES MATCH!” The crowd pops as “The Rat” begins to play, and we slowly fade out into a commercial, zoomed in on a fallen Trent Lost. QUOTE The open battle royal was a very close match throughout, with two competitors making their debuts with the company. Shiloh Cormier was one half of the new tag team "The Oxford Dropouts" and Blanche and Val were at ringside to see him lock up with "Jackpot" Riley Thomas. Shiloh took over at the start of the match and powered Riley around for a bit, but it all changed hands when Riley connected with a textbook dropkick as Shiloh came flying down from the turnbuckle, aiming for a sledgehammer to the shoulder. Riley tried to toss Shiloh over a few times, but he just wouldn't budge. Shiloh started to mount a comeback on Jackpot, connecting with a Spinebuster and a few leg drops, but a drop toehold took Shiloh off his feet for a few seconds, just long enough for Riley to pick him up and toss him over the rope. WINNER: RILEY "JACKPOT" THOMAS We cut backstage where we see Adam Miller backstage with Zachary Pagonis in the GIW interview area. ZACHARY PAGONIS: “We are here with Adam Miller, an up and coming new superstar in GIW, now last week Adam we saw you bump into Naviel Sunderland hand have a rather odd exchange... can you explain that any further?” Adam nods and takes the microphone. ADAM MILLER: “Well... Zachary, it is really hard to explain. I just feel something weird about her...” Zach jumps at the opportunity for another question. ZACHARY PAGONIS: “Describe it.” Miller pauses, unsure of what he is going to say. ADAM MILLER: “I know she needs help. I can see it in her eyes, Zach, and I feel like, for some reason, I'm the only one who can.” Pagonis nods... obviously enjoying his first real interview in a while. ZACHARY PAGONIS: “Is it an... emotional attraction?” Another pause. ADAM MILLER: “I don't know...” Miller walks out of the lounge, leaving Pagonis alone as we fade to commercial. QUOTE First to the ring was Lokie. Secondly, was his opponent Naviel. As soon as the bell rang, Naviel smacked Lokie across the chest with fierce intent. She did this again, and again until the red was bared. Eventually, however, Lokie had enough and pulled her into a side slam. He went for a quick pin, but she refused to let up. She rolled over to her side to break the pin. Lokie wasn’t bothered, yet. He pulled himself and his opponent up to their feet and then threw her like a rag doll. Physically inferior, she had more to do to break him, but she wasn’t going to let a man manhandle her. Naviel forced Lokie to release her and dragged him down into a snap DDT. She then rolled over on top of him to perform a few precise strikes to the temple. Naviel then released herself from him and backed into the ropes, watched him briefly as he lay there holding his forehead, then used the ropes to ascend into the air with a springboard lionsault. Lokie tried to dodge, but she had caught him clean at the center of the ring. She lifted off from the springing impact, but then tried for a pinfall attempt. He too refused to go down without a fight and had pushed Naviel quickly off of him. The two were both soon up on their feet and circled briefly for a few steps. Neither certain which would strike first, but one would. It was Lokie. He lunged and pulled his female opponent into a lockup. He shoved her off and grinned at the display, but she would just throw a hard kick to his gut and lock up with him in response. She then threw all of her might into a shove that sent Lokie to the canvas. She charged soon after to connect a low dropkick to his reddened chest. Naviel then pulled at his hair to yank him back to his feet, but Lokie pushed off. One clothesline later and it seemed he had gained the upper hand. Lokie stood over her and stomped once, then again, then pulled her up with force like a rag doll. He hoisted her up over his shoulders, readying her for a press slam, but she dodged. Sliding off and onto her feet, she turned him toward her and used a wheel kick to complete the counter. Lokie ducked underneath the path of striking, and hooked a hand under her thigh, then the other around her waist and fell backward to slam her into the canvas below. He rolled her up for a pin, but the ropes were too close and she had gained freedom, even if it was just for a moment. She made have struck out of the pin, but she was still under his control. He struck her hard with a right and left, then dragged her to her feet by the hair. He wasn’t done with her just yet. No. OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Get this manic out of the ring...!" Lokie thrust her back down and into an arm bar submission hold. If she were too rough and quick to beat down, he would wear her down. He locked the hold tightly and she had begun to wince and gripe, but hadn’t tapped out, not just yet. The referee annoyed her with his inane hounding. He was always a nuisance and loved it. He may not have been a wrestler, but he loved bugging them until their last nerve bursted. He would bug her every second, demanding her to quit and to tell him so he could soak up the moment like a sudden blast of pleasure than only a truly striped villain would enjoy. She refused to give him that pleasure. This pissed the referee off, and so he insisted even further, hoping that Lokie would lock the hold in for longer. It seemed all the world was against her, and it was. He did his best and she began to grunt and yell, but it was in frustration. She reached for ropes she couldn’t touch, and scraped for inches she couldn’t travel, but she refused to tap out. Never, never would she tap out to a man. Lokie didn’t care what goals were set in her mind, he wanted this victory over her, anyone. He was going to get it. No matter what it took. So he applied further pressure on the arm bar submission and pressed down upon her with his weight. Hoping she’d soon tap out. OLIVER THIBODAUX: "He can't do that to her! This guy needs to be stopped now!" Finally, it all came to a head when Naviel found her out. In the worst way possible, Naviel bit Lokie to free herself. It may have been a dirty tactic, but it got her free and gave her a second chance. Besides, the only thing dirty about it was Lokie. He shook his wrist and pulled away from the vicious jaws of Naviel. He raised to his feet as she did, knowing he’d have to do something to keep the upper hand, and so he rushed toward her with a clothesline that she had quickly ducked under and used a sudden impact spear to down him. This gave her enough time to race toward the ring post, ascend and just as she leapt for her finisher, ‘Burning Spirit’, Lokie ducked out of the way so that she would land face and chest first into the white canvas below. She crashed like a train and collapsed backward onto her spine. He toppled over her immediately after and pulled a victory over her failure, but it wasn’t a failed attempt at success, just a near miss. One second later and it could have been him succumbing to the pinfall. After the match, Lokie rolled himself out of the ring to have the bite marks looked at by a physician. Adam Miller, whom was on commentary during the match rushed into the ring to make the save for Naviel, but was too late. He assisted her anyway and watched as Lokie raised an arm in victory. WINNER: LOKIE LINDSAY We cut to the backstage where Megathreat is standing with his partner for the night, Jordan Westbrook. I'm sure this wont be the last time I'll see you two jokes.” As he leaves, the scene fades out.
