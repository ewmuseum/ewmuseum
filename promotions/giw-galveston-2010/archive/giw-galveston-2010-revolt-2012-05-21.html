Revolt, 21 May 2012

GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: MONDAY NIGHT REVOLT LIVE! From Oklahoma State Fair Arena in Oklahoma City, OK – May 21, 2012 BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! “Over and Under” by Egypt Central blasts at the Oklahoma State Fair Arena, formally welcoming all of the fans watching live at home on ION TV as well as the six hundred that have gathered in Oklahoma City to watch Revolt transpire right before their very eyes. In an opening scene similar to the one on last week’s edition, the ring is crowded with talent: David Sinclair stands center stage with Aspen Chaud directly to his left, and Eliza Gray directly to his right. To his far left stands Alex Castellanos and to his far right stands Phoenix Matsuda, the finalists of the tournament, both dressed in street clothes. Aspen wears the belt over her left shoulder, showing off its newly polished glisten and gleam; Sinclair begins the show. DAVID SINCLAIR: “And then there were two. Ladies and gentlemen, I’d like to welcome you to Monday Night Revolt, and tonight we will finally decide which one of these two men will be fortunate enough to have the opportunity to face Aspen Chaud for her Undisputed Championship at Reckless Expansion. Now, last week was a little unstructured, and it got a little bit heated, so tonight, I will allow both Alex and Phoenix to speak, as well as the champion herself. You have thirty seconds, or until I stop you, to get anything off your chest that you want off your chest. Alex, you may begin.” The crowd cheers heavily causing Aspen to roll her eyes as Alex picks up the microphone. Throughout his speech Sinclair, Matsuda, and Gray all give him their attention, but she refuses to look up. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “There is a four headed beast in Galveston Island Wrestling right now, and it consists of David Sinclair, Eliza Gray, Alexis Terry, and Aspen Chaud. When I was on the road to my title match with Aspen at Retaliation, I severed Alexis Terry from the group and nearly crushed Aspen and Sinclair at the same time, but alas, they have regenerated. But so have I. After Retaliation, there was a state of decay in my abilities: I was not focused, I was not prepared, and therefore I was not successful. Things are different now, you see, but my goal remains the same. I will stop at nothing to make sure that the strangle grip you four have on this company is rendered obsolete: and to do that I must take the Undisputed Championship from you, Aspen. I’ll win this tournament, and I will win your title.” The crowd goes wild, finally Aspen looks up and stares a hole through Alex, Eliza offers up a sarcastic clap. Alex returns the favor with a smile, Phoenix begins to speak. PHOENIX MATSUDA: “You didn’t even mention my name. Do you know who I am, Alex? Aspen? David? I am Phoenix Matsuda, and over the past few weeks I have proven every single fan, every single critic, every single smark WRONG when I pinned both Alioth Starre - the longest reigning champion in GIW history, mind you - and Daniel Starr - the poster boy for your fucking supershow! I don’t even get a mention in your little fucking speech Alex? I am your OPPONENT, and I am GOOD. I’m better than good, I’m great, and you, sir, are overrated. You’re twenty something years old, but you are washed up. Clinging to the last little bit of animosity you have with that whore to keep you going. Well, it isn’t enough. Wrestling isn’t a sprint, it is a marathon, and I have so much more left in the tank than you do. So, while you go on your self-righteous crusade to rid GIW of the four headed, bitchy dragon, I’ll pin you and I’ll take her title too. Don’t bother trying to stop it.” There is a mixed reaction from the crowd - some welcoming a change in scenery, others offended at Phoenix’s cockiness. Aspen begins her follow up speech. ASPEN CHAUD: “I’ve grown really tired of all this, I really have. David, you know I respect you as a Chairman, much more than I ever did Blake Beckett, and I understand why this tournament is necessary, but why do we even bother? I think it would be more effective pulling a Native American off of a reservation here in Oklahoma to face me at Aggressive Expansion. Maybe the title would motivate them, but these two options are jokes. I’ve already beaten Alex more times than I can count, and Phoenix Matsuda is an Asian. He’s cute, isn’t he Eliza? I wonder if he can balance my checkbook for me, I’ve been spending far too much money on that wedding. Wait, what was I talking about? Oh yeah, you two. I don’t have much more to say. Alex, if you could just lay down for me like you did at Category 5 last year, that would be swell. And Phoenix, this little fat boy I sponsor back in Galveston has always wanted to learn karate; maybe you can make his wish come true? Thanks love.” “Did It On ‘Em” by Nicki Minaj, Aspen’s theme, hits the PA and she blows a kiss to Castellanos as she and Eliza file out of the ring. Sinclair nods to the both of them and files out behind them, leaving Phoenix and Alex alone in the ring together. We slowly fade out into a commercial. QUOTE After that opening sequence we return to the ring where we are joined by three very focused looking men looking around the ring at one another. Daniel Starr, Jamie Hayden, and Ryan Cavallari all getting prepped up for a good match here tonight. RILEY STEVENS: “This has match of the night written all over it.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Somebody can become a…Starr.” These three men all continue to stare around at each other until the referee breaks the tension by calling for the bell. DING DING DING! The bell rang signifying the start of the opening contest. Already there were fireworks as Jamie Hayden and Daniel Starr actually stared one another down before it was interrupted by Ryan pushing Hayden away and getting into Starr’s face the two exchanged words about Starr’s comments last week as Hayden walked back up to the two of them again he was pushed away until Hayden was ticked enough that he charged at both. There was a resounding thud of two well placed Savate Kicks to the stomach of Jamie Hayden as he would double over easily as a team both Starr and Cavallari would send Hayden over the ropes. The team would dissolve as quickly as it appeared as the two men remaining in the ring opened up by exchanging punches with one another. It was an even exchange until Ryan started to get the better of Starr before Starr countered by Irish whipping Cavallari into the ropes. Thinking a few steps ahead Ryan was able to duck a well timed Clothesline that sent Starr swinging violently missing it led him to only be hit with a running variation of the Missile Dropkick, and it dropped Starr to the canvas quickly. It allowed time for Ryan to survey Starr’s misfortune. From behind came an angry Hayden who would club Ryan over the back with a Double Axe Handle, it showed what a tactical brawler he could be. Ryan was winching as Hayden turned him around against the ropes burying a flurry of punches right into his ribcage. RILEY STEVENS: “A battle to start out here on Revolt.” That prompted Starr to roll out of the ring to live to fight another day as Hayden easily took control of the match up using his size. Hayden decided to go for a tasteful old school style Neckbreaker, but once Ryan felt Hayden starting to turn he began peppering him with kicks that only annoyed Hayden. Hayden again used his size to his advantage lifting all 220 lbs. of Ryan’s frame clashing into the turnbuckle hammering him with Shoulder Thrust after shoulder thrust. As if he was psychic Hayden seen the Missile Dropkick attempt from Starr coming as he batted away his legs causing a moment of disbelief form Starr. It lead to Hayden Irish whipping Starr into the opposite corner from Cavallari, there was a smirk that crept across his Hayden’s face for a moment as he started at Starr then turned around full steam ahead into Cavallari with a Corner Splash. Hayden tried to round his momentum out to hit Starr but Daniel was just too clever, ducking out of the way at the last second was pivotal Jaime crashed into the turnbuckle shoulder first. RILEY STEVENS: “That’s going to hurt in the morning.” Wasting little time Daniel went onto attacking Ryan with stomps to his back each all were well placed as he would go for a clean and crisp Suplex, only to pop his hips well enough to allow him to go from the Suplex into the Hip Toss right into a float over style cover but Hayden had other plans as he pulled Starr up one leg first and slammed that very knee of his flat into the canvas. Ryan found himself crawling to the ropes for relief as Starr and Hayden seemed to be at odds at the moment. Hayden would apply a very nifty Ankle Lock that forced Starr to scream out, seemingly wanting to do damage to that body part he started working on a second ago. Hayden released it once Starr was at the ropes, keeping track of Ryan who was in the corner trying to shake the cobwebs out still. Hayden turned his attention to Ryan who was at the ropes there was a Forearm Smash exuded by Ryan only for Hayden to be rolled up by Starr 1…2…NO! JACOB O’CONNELLY: “Starr nearly stole it.” Hayden kicked out and Starr went to the ropes there was another Forearm Smash by Ryan as he watched Starr turned around as Ryan spring boarded off the ropes only to be clubbed in the stomach quickly. Starr interlinked his arms executing a very prime Double Underhook DDT that drilled Ryan into the canvas, Starr wasn’t done there he wrapped Ryan’s legs up setting up for a Curb Stomp but only pulled Ryan’s arms back wrenching before he tied them up handcuff style behind Ryan’s back he pushed his head to the canvas with his foot and pulled back on his arms with this unique variation of a Curb Stomp style Submission. Hayden went to saving the match up hitting a Diving Lariat on Starr taking him out as Ryan rolled out of the ring trying to save himself the trouble. RILEY STEVENS: “This type of match is all about heart.” Starr and Hayden battled back and forth exchanging blows until Hayden went to throw Starr over the ropes, at the last second Starr used his weight shifting it to throw Hayden over the ropes he ducked between the ropes smashing his shoulder into Hayden’s midsection. Hayden flew off from the turnbuckle exasperated as Starr stepped between the ropes onto the apron looking for something. Hayden was stirring as Starr took off but as he jumped Ryan came from out of nowhere placing a well placed sliding Roundhouse Kick to the knees of Starr with overtook his momentum and caused him to front flip and propel back first against the mat on the outside. Ryan sat on the apron as he used his knees to slide across the apron in the first place before stepping off the apron and climbing the barricade having something in mind, he went to jump at Starr who was now leaning against the barricade until Hayden caught him mid move turned around and hit a very nasty Oklahoma Stampede on the steel steps. Ryan looked to be finished Starr came too but was met with Hayden grasping him by the neck and being thrown into the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Can one of these men get another down for three?” Inside the ring, both Hayden and Starr exchanged punches until Hayden bullied Starr up against the ropes. A Belly to Belly Suplex is what Starr was hit with only for him to pop back up with such ferocity for a second helping. Hayden quickly found himself going for the cover. 1…2…Kickout! Starr was able to get the shoulder up just a mere second before the hand came down for three. Hayden would finally hit that Neckbreaker he was trying to hit Ryan with for the cover 1…2…Kickout! Starr seemed to be using his second wind to his advantage. Starr got back up to standing and Hayden seemed frustrated only for Starr to slide his leg out for the Drop Toe Hold that lead to him applying a vicious STF that wasn’t really suppose to tap out Hayden but slow him down, he released the hold when he felt that Hayden was out and began ascending to the top rope. Quickly as Daniel ascended the turnbuckle Ryan began pulling at his leg it forced him to try to fight him off turning him around. Ryan stepped between the ropes only to land a top rope Inverted Powerslam somehow Ryan rolled him over using his shoulders and neck into the cover 1…2…NO! Starr got his shoulder up once more. The crowd could tell they were pouring their hearts out. Ryan got up at the same moment Starr did and the two began swinging wildly in an exchange for some reason Starr went for STARSTRUCK but Ryan ducked and went for YOU GIVE LOVE A BAD NAME that also was ducked by Starr both moves were the Brogue Kick and Flying Knee respectively. RILEY STEVENS: “Fast action!” The two ended up in the middle of a stare down before Hayden returned with vengeance as he nailed the Big Boot on Ryan and took him clean out somewhere near the ropes. Starr went and hit STARSTRUCK but it only stumbled Hayden for how close he was to him to begin with, Starr would shoot off to the ropes to get a full head of momentum but Hayden like Starr had done before sidestepped at the very last second only for Ryan to hit him with YOU GIVE LOVE A BAD NAME the flying knee straight to the face and he was out like a light. Ryan wouldn’t stop there because Ryan quickly ducked some sort counter attempt from Hayden as he rolled Hayden up from a vertical base, pulling the back of his singlet for even more leverage as he would make the cover…1…2…3! REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner…Ryan Cavallari!” Quickly after the match was over Alexis came into the ring kissing Ryan, displaying their affection for one another for the first time. Starr looked irate at the fact that Cavallari pinned him after their “friendly” exchange. Ryan escorted Alexis out of the ring, Ryan looked back at Starr with a smirk walking with his girl up the ramp. RILEY STEVENS: “That was a classic match right there all three men brought it but in the end Ryan got the win.” JACOB O’CONNELLY: “Just a week before his wedding, he deserved it.” These fans seem disappointed with how Ryan won the match but none the less he continues to back pedal with Alexis looking down at an angry Starr and a upset Hayden as we take a commercial break. WINNER: RYAN CAVALLARI We cut backstage to the combined dressing rooms of Aspen Chaud, Eliza Gray, Alexis Terry, and Ryan Cavallari. Aspen is sitting on one of the benches tying Eliza Gray’s boot for her. ASPEN CHAUD: “There you are! Are you ready to become the third golden member of International Glamour? The Television Title will look so much nicer around your waist than James Dean’s.” Eliza smiled and laughed, sliding on her other ring boot. Aspen proceeds to tie this one too. ELIZA GRAY: “I was literally born to wear that belt Aspen Chaud, it compliments my eyes too, did you notice? Now our uniform will consist of sexy clothes, sexy hair, sexy shoes, sexy makeup, and sexy gold belts.” Aspen finished up tying the other boot, and Eliza stands up and begins to stretch. Aspen remains seated, crossing her legs and pulling out a nail file and some lip gloss. ASPEN CHAUD: “Gold belts went out of style in the 1400’s, but I have a feeling that when we wear them, they will be cool again. What’s great is that these nasty people in Oklahoma can only buy cheap plastic knockoffs. Seriously, everyone is so poor and inbred here...” The door to their locker room opens as Alexis Terry enters with huge sampling of flowers in her hand. She turns and looks at Eliza and gives her a huge hug, then gives her the flowers. ALEXIS TERRY: “Sorry I couldn’t be there to hear you talk about the final in the tournament Aspen, I was off choosing the flowers for the wedding next week! And while I was there, I saw these and I just had to get them for our FUTURE TELEVISION CHAMPION! Are you excited!?” Eliza and Alexis perform some horrible handshake that involves kissing each other on the cheek and locking pinkies. Aspen applies some gloss. ELIZA GRAY: “Very, are you going to come watch?” Alexis grabs Eliza by the elbow and begins to walk towards the door. ALEXIS TERRY: “I wouldn’t dare miss it! Let’s go. I’ll be back in time to get ready for our tag match, it’s not like I’ll need to do much preparation for Jason Slave and Loco. Kisses!” They exit, leaving Aspen alone with her filing for a moment. After around ten seconds or so, the door opens once more, and it is Ryan Cavallari, sweaty from his match just moments ago. Aspen is startled at first, then looks at Ryan and smiled a devilish smile - almost as if she likes what she sees. She unbuttoned a button on her shirt and continues to file her nails. Ryan doesn’t seem to do notice. She speaks without moving. ASPEN CHAUD: “Hey.” Ryan was fumbling through his locker, grabbing a towel and some clothes. He didn’t bother looking at her to reply to her half hearted, seemingly disinterest comment. RYAN CAVALLARI: “Hi.” He promptly grabs his things and heads for the showers. Aspen watches him go, raising an eyebrow. The scene slowly fades to commercial with the sound of Aspen’s nail file grinding down her nails. "Bad Romance" by Lady Gaga hits and the fans begin to rain down boos upon Eliza Gray as she walks out from the back heading towards the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Lord, can you imagine if all three of them had titles?” JACOB O’CONNELLY: “It would be great, they earned it.” Eliza enters the ring looking across at Dean with a smirk as she heads to the top rope to pose for the fans, who all seem to be thinking the same thing…Please not all three of them. DING DING DING! The bell sounds and James Dean and Eliza quickly lock up. James connected quickly with a hip toss take down and Eliza rolls into the corner shocked. James begs for her to bring it but Eliza looks to want no part of it. Alexis hops onto the apron and yells for the ref to give Eliza a “time out” but the ref simply argues back with Alexis, leaving James looking upset that Alexis is getting involved. Alexis points and screams for the ref to count. ONE…TWO…THREE..and we have a new Television Champion! REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner and new GIW Television Champion…Eliza Gray!” These fans all soak back into their chairs in misery as the referee gets up to his feet as does Eliza who is jumping up and down in the ring as she is then handed her belt. RILEY STEVENS: “And she did it…” JACOB O’CONNELLY: “Congratulations, a worthy champion!” Eliza continues to jump up and around with her belt in her hand as the fans continue to boo, as she points at the camera telling Alexis and Aspen that they have all the gold now as we watch her celebrate a little longer before going to commercial break. DAVID SINCLAIR: “Alex, your attitude is holding this company back. I don’t know what your personal vendetta against me is, but it needs to stop, or I’ll unfortunately be forced to fire you.” Alex laughs at the thought. He rises, as does Sinclair. ALEX CASTELLANOS:: “You don’t understand why I hate you. David, you are Aspen’s little puppet Chairman. She literally tried to kill Blake Beckett to get you in control so that, in reality, she could be in control. You are everything I hate in a man: weak, malleable, spineless - yet cocky. GALVESTON ISLAND Wrestling has been on a tour of Arkansas and Oklahoma, and you call it expansion. Fans have to pay to watch our Supershows and decide if they want to watch the global companies on Monday Nights or Revolt, and you call it profit. On behalf of everyone in the back, the fans, and anyone with any self respect - fuck you, David Sinclair.” The ‘oooooooo’s from the crowd are heard even in the back. Phoenix tries to keep Alex's shoulders down ONE....TWO....THR- Alex forces himself to kick out, near fall. RILEY STEVENS: “Nobody wants to be second place.” Phoenix tries to keep Alex grounded by applying an armbar. He places his palm on Alex's left elbow and pulls back on his hand. Alex yells out in pain pounding the canvas once. The referee asks him if he wanted to give the match up but Castellanos shakes his head no. Fans could be heard all over "LET'S GO ALEX!" followed by claps. Alex begins to get up trying to get back on his feet but Phoenix pounds Alex on the top of the head with the point of his right elbow. He's back on the pressure with the armbar. He had no intention of letting go. Phoenix begins to talk trash "I'm the NEXT Undisputed Champion, not YOU!" to the former North Beach Champion, Alex. JACOB O’CONNELLY: “Matsuda is three seconds away from facing Aspen.” Suddenly all of the fans stand out of their chairs to clap for Alex. He finds the strength to power his way back to his feet and place his right arm under Phoenix's left arm lifting him up and throwing him with a hip toss. Phoenix landed right on his butt. He was sitting up. Alex cocks his leg back and kicks Matsuda right in the temple with a right low kick. Alex drops down and covers Matsuda's chest with his own. ONE....TWO....THR- This time Phoenix kicks out, yet another near fall. RILEY STEVENS: “Back and forth action here in the main event.” Alex gets up and bends to grab Phoenix by the top of his hand and chin forcing him to stand. He then grabs Phoenix's left forearm with his right palm wrapping his fingers with it and launching him across the ring with an Irish whip. Phoenix falls out of the ring through the second rope. Alex exits the ring and goes after Phoenix. Alex lifts Phoenix up and throws him back first into the four foot gated barricade. Phoenix kicks at Alex shin causing Alex to step back and hold his leg up in pain. Phoenix wastes no time in clotheslining Alex down to the hard outside floor with high impact. Phoenix guides Alex back to his feet and grabs his injured left arm and throws it right down onto the four foot barricade hard causing Alex to yell out in pain "ARGHH" And dropping down to his knees holding his arm. RILEY STEVENS: “Alex’s arm may be done.” Phoenix pounds down on the back of Alex's neck and lifts him up. He then looks back at the barricade. He Irish whips Alex toward the barricade with everything he had after he turns around to lift his arms up saying "Golden age will never start around here." But Alex quickly drops on his stomach turns his head sideways. He slide under the open space under the barricade. Phoenix turns around and Alex is already balanced on top of the barricade. He jumps up and hits a flying fist right to Phoenix's mouth knocking him back. Phoenix fell and hit the back of his head on the ring edge. RILEY STEVENS: “It all comes down to this.” Alex walks over to Phoenix; the fans are clapping because of the match the two were putting on. Alex throws Phoenix into the ring and slides in himself. Phoenix gets up not wanting to stay down he drops a knee on Alex's injured left arm. Alex doesn't get up he stays down to clutch his arm. Phoenix exits the ring but doesn't drop down to the outside floor he springboards up balancing on the top rope. Alex kicks up right onto his feet once Phoenix jumped off to attempt something he flies right into a 'Golden Arrow' in mid air on his chin. He falls flat on his back; as the crowd goes wild and Alex is able only to crawl over tossing his arm over him…1…2…3! REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner…Alex Castellanos!” These fans go wild as Alex rolls over holding his arm in pain as he has just won the tournament and a shot at Aspen Chaud at ‘Aggressive Expansion’ in a few weeks time. RILEY STEVENS: “What a win! Alex Castellanos wins!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Damn it.” The crowd is in a frenzy for Alex as Matsuda rolls out of the ring having just frankly been caught with that kick out of no where as Castellanos gets to a seated position holding up his arm in victory. RILEY STEVENS: “Alex will get another crack at Aspen.” JACOB O’CONNELLY: “But more importantly tune in next week as Alexis Terry and Ryan Cavallari get married!” With that we continue to watch Alex hold up his arm getting to his feet as the referee raises his good arm as the show cuts off a tad early to show the wedding invite of Alexis and Ryan as the show goes off air. WINNER: ALEX CASTELLANOS
