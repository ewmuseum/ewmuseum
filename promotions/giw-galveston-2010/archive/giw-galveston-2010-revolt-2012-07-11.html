GIW — Card

Revolt, 11 July 2012

11 July 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: WEDNESDAY NIGHT REVOLT Live! On Porretto Beach July 11th, 2012 on Showtime BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! The crowd here on Porretto Beach is going wild with cheers as we pan around them airing here for the first time on a new day on a new channel! These fans are showing their love for GIW until "Tear The World Down" by We Are The Fallen hits as the fans begin to boo as Alexis Terry standing by with David Sinclair walk out from the back with a big smile on her face and the Undisputed Championship around her waist as Sinclair walks beside her clapping all along the way. RILEY STEVENS: “Well here we go, starting on a new channel in Showtime, a new day, and a much longer show here tonight and unfortunately we start the show off with our new champion…Alexis Terry, oh and the man that helped her get that belt Sinclair as well.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Hey Riley? Fuck you. I can say it now.” Alexis followed Sinclair up the steps as he held the ropes for her as she slipped into the ring walking around the ring unstrapping her belt and hoisting it up into the air as the boos only get louder as David claps from behind her as she walks around the displaying the belt to each and every person as David walks across the ring asking for two microphones walking across the ring handing one to Alexis keeping the other for himself. ALEXIS TERRY: “Well, look what happened ladies and gentlemen, boys and girls, and of course all my royal subjects, take a hard long look! LOOK AT YOUR BRAND NEW UNDISPUTED CHAMPION! IT’S ME! Me! I DID IT! I BLOODY DID IT! All of you doubted me! All of you did! But look now doubters look what your stupid little ranting and raving got you. It got you nothing! Now, I demand you all sit there and listen to the words I have to say. Unlike you all, this man in the ring with me – he believed! He believed in what I could do and he knew deep down the woman I could become. This Championship over my shoulder is everything I’ve ever wanted, it’s everything I knew I was destined for and David Sinclair believed! HE BELIEVED!” Alexis let the title slip of her shoulder and into her hand. She then raised the championship high into the air as the crowd booed intently. She then turned her attention to Sinclair and smiled. ALEXIS TERRY: “Sinclair, I want to dedicate my match tonight to you. I want to thank you Sinclair for being the honest, caring, and most of all loyal man I know you are. Never once did you ever give up on me and now, tonight, together we reign over Galveston Island Wrestling as thy king and thy queen! Now, bow at our feet! BOW YOU PATHETIC BLOODY SCUM! LISTEN TO YOUR CHAMPION! I’M YOUR QUEEN DAMNIT!” Alexis lowered the microphone and walked around the ring screaming at the fans to bow which only caused the packed crowd to once again let her have it, booing louder and louder every time her loud English accent escaped her mouth. Sinclair walked over and placed a hand on her shoulder, doing his best to calm her down but the boos turn to cheers as "Party in the USA" by Miley Cyrus hits and the fans go wild as now out from the back comes Matt Kluge walking alongside Madison Seton, both of whom get the crowd on their feet as Alexis and Sinclair turn towards them, Kluge already equipped with a mic. MATT KLUGE: “Shut…UP! You know when I took over I told everyone that I would change this place so that wrestling actually mattered yet here we go again on Sunday with you coming out of no where to ruin my main event and screw over our former champion. Now thankfully you aren’t the one in charge anymore and I am happy to announce that Madison Seton the winner of the Sole Survivor match will take on our...champion at ‘Moment of Glory III’ and I will make sure that, you will have nothing to do with it.” Seton stood behind him seeming only to come out here due to Kluge’s request and the fans cheer her on anyways wanting her to win the match so badly as Alexis rolls her eyes as Sinclair steps forward. DAVID SINCLAIR: “Kluge? You were never that good of a wrestler anyways. I brought this company to heights it had never reached before, we were in stadiums! We were heading on our way to becoming a global company yet now we are back again in this piss poor little area on this awful looking beach. You are not even half the General Manager I am, and that is a fact!” The fans are clearly on Kluge’s side on this one as the two sides step forward bickering when “Demon Eyes” by The Answer hits as the fans come unglued as they all look up at the ramp, as Sinclair, Terry, are shown with shocked looks on there faces as they look up the ramp as soon we realize it isn’t a ploy as Blake Beckett walks out from the back for the first time in months! Wearing a confident grin Beckett heads down to the ring with a mic in his hand as everyone but Kluge seems surprised right now as he strolls up the steps entering the ring, looking around at the crowd as he lowers the mic as he looks out at the crowd as they cheer him on. BLAKE BECKETT: “You know it feels good to be back…in MY ring.” Beckett chuckles a bit as he now turns back around to the people in the ring Sinclair who is yelling something at him as Kluge stares at him seeming to be the only one who doesn’t give a crap in the building. BLAKE BECKETT: “Stop with all the bickering and fighting, you don’t need to fight over being the top guy anymore because I am here to claim the company that has always been mine. However with La’Sarah pumping out babies, Amanda…doing whatever she is doing, and seemingly every other prospect at this point out of the loop I do need a General Manager. Let’s face the facts, Sinclair you are a douchebag and Kluge…you are the biggest prick in the business.” The fans laugh as Kluge nods his head knowing this full well as Sinclair and Terry continue to bicker amongst each other about Blake’s return. BLAKE BECKETT: “So you know what? That match at ‘Moment of Glory III’ will happen and if Alexis wins Sinclair your GM…Madison you win, Kluge you’re GM…Now get out of my ring.” Seton shrugs her shoulders and rolls out of the ring right off the bat as Alexis and Sinclair head out of the ring as Kluge glares across at Blake nodding his head as he now takes his leave as well as we head to commercial break with the almost unbelievable view of Blake back in a GIW ring. QUOTE As we return from break standing in the ring is Caden Charters stretching out in the corner as "Frontline" by Pillar hits to a rousing amount of boos from the crowd who are clearly not too fond of “The Face of GIW” as Daniel Starr walks out with that always smug look on his face as he strolls down towards the ring looking out at the crowd showing himself off as he strolls into the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “This is the first of three championship qualifiers for a yet to be named title that will be introduced later tonight apparently by Blake Beckett the man who just made his return…Crazy right Oliver?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Daniel Starr is entering the ring, who gives a damn about Blake Beckett?” Starr walks along the ring apron shouting something at the crowd before entering the ring heading to the top rope getting more poses as he poses on the top rope, jumping down and looking over at Caden as the bell rings. DING DING DING! Charters rushes at him but Starr stops him with both hands out, Charters stops on a dime as Daniel looks around at the crowd then asks “Who the hell are you?” before Caden can answer Starr slaps him across the face. A angered Charters tosses a huge right hand but Daniel ducks under it and rolls him up 1…2…But surprisingly it’s Starr that pulls off of him, jumping backwards and walking to the camera pointing at his face shouting “The Face of GIW!” which gets some boos from the crowd as an embarrassed Charters hops up rushing at him but with eyes in the back of his head Starr ducks it and as Charters bounces off the ropes he crushes him with the ‘Starrstruck’ a Brogue Kick that nearly sends Caden’s head into the rafters as he falls to the ground out cold, but always looking to make a statement Starr takes his time jumping up onto the top rope motioning a belt around his waist before jumping up with a Shooting Starr Press that lands perfectly on the already defeated Charters as Starr doesn’t bother hooking the leg 1…2…3! REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner…Daniel Starr!” Starr gets up to his feet with a smile wiping off his hands like it was too easy as he walks around the ring with his arms raised in the air as the crowd boos him. RILEY STEVENS: “Well Starr will be in the title match next week, the question is who will join him?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Does it matter? He’s the favorite.” Daniel now heads to the top rope to once again pose with his invisible belt as we head to the backstage area. WINNER: DANIEL STARR As we head to the backstage area we find Matt Kluge standing in Blake Beckett’s office which he has apparently found his way back into as he fixes some things up. MATT KLUGE: “You know I was doing just fine with out you, you know that right? I mean I nearly got Joshua to show up tonight.” Kluge shakes his head as Blake fixes some things in his desk as Beckett grins up at him. BLAKE BECKETT: “Yeah things were fine but I didn’t exactly love the idea of someone with your history running things and oh I made a slight change to the card tonight…” Kluge raises an eyebrow to ask him what exactly as Blake grinned just seeming to be enjoying this whole return. BLAKE BECKETT: “That tag match later tonight; switching the teams Vega’s with Genocide and Katya’s with Jace…Oh and it’ll be Tornado Tag.” MATT KLUGE: “Why?” BLAKE BECKETT: “Because I can.” Beckett smirks at Kluge who rolls his eyes as we head to a commercial break. QUOTE After the break we head back into the ring where Eliza Gray stands getting some jeers about Kluge throwing her title away as she tries her best to ignore it as the lights go out as “Grand Central” by Edward Shearmur hits and as the lights flicker back on Alioth Starre makes his slow entrance down towards the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Both these stars had very poor performances in Sole Survivor to say the least but one has a chance to get right back on track here tonight.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Only one..” Into the ring Starre heads seeming to be a hundred percent business tonight as he stands across the ring from Eliza as the bell is rung to get this underway. DING DING DING! The bell sounds and Eliza rushes Alioth but is promptly dropped with a huge heart block that sends Eliza to the ground. Alioth comes back with a kneedrop to the chest of Eliza. Alioth quickly locks in the Four Star Mary and within seconds Eliza taps out. In a matter of seconds the match starts and is already over. REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner…Alioth Starre!” Alioth Starre’s hand is raised and Eliza quickly rolls out of the ring and drops to a knee looking at Alioth victorious in the ring. She demands a microphone and quickly raises it to her mouth. She wastes little time getting to the point. ELIZA GRAY: “I quit!” These fans booed her for the way she seemed to have just given up here in GIW, the former Television Champions career ending on a very shallow note as in the ring Alioth continues to celebrate seeming not to care about her departure in the slightest. WINNER: ALIOTH STARRE Eliza just gets through to the back as Alioth begins to roll out of the ring when walking out to the top of the ramp together getting booed out of the building are Aspen Chaud and Ryan Cavallari standing side by side, Alioth stops as he exits the ring as Aspen has a mic in hand. ASPEN CHAUD: “Calm down people! You know later tonight Ryan and I face four of the top stars in GIW in the Main Event in a three way tag team match. The last few months you have all enjoyed watching me spiral down here in GIW but at ‘Category 5’ I joined with a man that together I would rule over GIW with! This man is our future GIW Undisputed Champion, Ryan Cavallari!” Aspen turns to Ryan laying a kiss upon him which only generates more boo’s as Alioth watches from afar wondering why exactly they picked now to come out here and send a message. ASPEN CHAUD: “A year ago heading into ‘Moment of Glory II’ I was the winner of Sole Survivor and I was preparing to face Alioth Starre in the Main Event and now I find myself on the outside as Alexis and Seton have their…match. But if there is one thing I continue to have comfort in is the fact that I have not fallen as far as you Alioth. I mean I saw your performance at ‘Category 5’ and I couldn’t help but wonder what happened to the man that used to run through the GIW roster one person after another? It seems to me like that man is gone and now replaced by someone that blames everyone else for his failures. Ryan soon will become Undisputed Champion and not only will he beat my record as the longest reigning champion but he’ll beat yours as well.” Smirking down at him as Ryan just stands beside her, letting her do the talking right now as Alioth continues to just stare up at them taking it in but not seeming to really be letting it get to him. ASPEN CHAUD: “But you know what Alioth a little birdie told me that next week you and I have a match, a rematch of the one at ‘Moment of Glory’ and this time it’s not me that’s going to be tapping out, it’s going to be you!” With the blockbuster match announced Aspen walks with Ryan to the back as Alioth shakes his head and continues up the ramp as we take a break. QUOTE The next match up had huge stakes going into the night but now the four people involved found themselves in a sticky situation as the teams were flipped by Beckett earlier in the night as Katya Rose and Jace Gryphon stood in the ring with one another, heading into the night thinking they would be against each other only to be now teaming as “D-7” By Nirvana hit as the fans responded with a mixed reaction as the duo of Vega and Genocide walked out from the back together seeming just as insecure as their opponents. RILEY STEVENS: “These four were prepared to have to rely on one another but now find themselves with new partners, going to be tough to really get on the same page especially now knowing it’ll be a Tornado Tag match!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Well they better a title shot goes to the winners.” Vega and Genocide head up the steps going different ways Vega entering the ring first as Genocide stops to pose then enters as well looking across at his opponents here tonight as the bell rings. DING DING DING! These new tag team partners take a moment to get things settled as all four stand in the ring together, knowing they are supposed to be partners but knowing in Tornado Tag Rules it was always going to be sort of a free for all. Finally getting things started its Gryphon and Rose rushing at Vega and Genocide only for Genocide to jump up landing a dropkick on Jace as Vega kicks Katya in the gut and then runs at her taking her down with a Hurricanrana. Taking a moment to make eye contact Vega and Genocide wait for their opponents to get up as Rose is up first and Vega jumps up connecting with a Enzuigiri that sends Katya down to the mat quickly rolling under the ropes to safety as she turns to face Genocide who waists for Jace to get up, as he grabs him and connects with a Fisherman Suplex! Gryphon rolls around holding his back as Genocide steps up looking at Vega the two seemingly playing a bit of one up-manship at this point. RILEY STEVENS: “Perhaps some mind games being played for next week?” Realizing it’s now her turn Vega waits for him to get up rushing at Jace and grabbing his neck and slamming it down with a Running Tornado DDT that sends him under the ropes as well as Rose still dazed from the kick re-enters the ring walking right into a Superkick from Genocide, Vega and Genocide barely taking their eyes off of one another at this point dominating with ease. Realizing that this need not go on any longer Genocide walks over to Jace dragging him to his feet, though he is barely more then just dead weight at this point. Rose slowly but surely gets to her feet as Genocide sets up and hits the ‘No Remorse’ a Canadian Destroyer, just as Rose gets up and Vega smashes her down with a Code Breaker! The two quickly jump on top for the cover looking up at one another as the referee makes two counts with both hands 1….2….3! REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here are your winners…Genocide and Vega!” Both Genocide and Vega get to there feet not taking their eyes off the person they would now be facing next week as the referee raised their arms in victory. RILEY STEVENS: “Well it’s clear these two realize they will be opponents next week and they join Daniel Starr in the match.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Is she really bald?” A graphic pops up on the screen showing Daniel Starr, Vega, and Genocide in the new title four way next week with a spot open for the final match taking place later tonight as Genocide and Vega exited the ring heading their separate ways for the night. WINNERS: GENOCIDE & VEGA Backstage to the interviewing area we go as we see Zachary Pagonis standing next to Alex Castellanos who seems a bit depressed right now. ZACHARY PAGONIS: “Alex at ‘Category 5’ David Sinclair took the place of the referee and cost you the match as he obviously slipped Alexis brass knuckles, surely you must be pretty let down?” Holding the microphone over to Alex, who struggles to really make eye contact with the camera nodding his head as he looks anywhere but. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “Yup…” Pagonis waited a bit before realizing he wasn’t going to say anything more as he pulls the mic back to his mouth and continues. ZACHARY PAGONIS: “Do you think it’s a little unfair that you are not being given a rematch considering the way it ended?” As Zachary holds the mic over to him before Alex can answer Beckett walks over stepping in between the two, looking off Zachary. BLAKE BECKETT: “Come to my office, we’ll talk…” With that Blake leads him down the hall Alex following him not far behind making us all wonder what was in store for them as we went to a ‘Moment of Glory III’ teaser before the commercials. QUOTE Taking our time to have another title qualifier now we head to the ring as Jeremy Baltimore with all his hidden rage stands in the ring as "World Warrior" by RedRapper hits and these fans go wild as the Triple Crown Champion, Tony Miranda heads out from the back fresh off his win at ‘Category 5’ high fiving some of the fans along the way. RILEY STEVENS: “Tony Miranda has made it clear he wants to become a Grand Slam Champion here in GIW, he’d be the first ever Oliver.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Wasn’t he just in that MMA thing? The hell was that.” The crowd is completely behind Tony who is coming off a huge return to the ring at Category 5 in the epic MMA match, he doesn’t seem to be showing too much beating right now instead seeming completely focused on the task at hand as he enters the ring looking across at Baltimore. DING DING DING! The bell sounds ands Jeremy Baltimore is oozing with confidence, almost to the point where he isn’t taking the match as serious as he should be. Jeremy in an arrogant manner steps up to Tony in the center of the ring and begins to run his mouth, spewing off with the usual blabber about being better than Tony and Tony being washed up. Tony shakes his head and laughs it off knowing what is coming to Jeremy. Jeremy pushes Tony and the crowd lets out a loud “Ohhhhh” in which Jeremy motions for Tony to bring it. Jeremy even goes as far as to blow Tony a kiss. This rubs Tony the wrong way and finally in the first move of the match hits a huge step up Enzuigiri that lands square on the side of the head and Jeremy falls to the mat like a sack of potatoes. RILEY STEVENS: “What was Jeremy thinking?” Tony is on fire now hitting a huge swinging neckbreaker. He wastes little time picking Jeremy right back up and nailing a nice snap suplex. Tony is putting on a wrestling clinic now. Multiple Judo throws send Jeremy around the ring like a rag doll. The crowd loves it, eating it up like candy. Armbar is locked in now and Jeremy is screaming in pain! Somehow though Jeremy is able to get to the ropes. Tony is crotched in the corner waiting for Jeremy to get back to his feet. Groggily Jeremy is on his feet but Tony destroys him with a Running Leg Lariat!~ Tony pins Jeremy and this one is over! REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner…Tony Miranda” Just like that the brash Baltimore is sent to sleep, out cold on the mat as Tony gets up holding an arm in the air the final entrant in the upcoming title match now headed to Vega, Genocide, and Daniel Starr. RILEY STEVENS: “Tony Miranda will be the biggest veteran in the match as he meets Starr, Vega, and Genocide for the new title next week, what title will it be and who will get the win?” In the ring Tony lowers his hand knowing that there is still much to do before he celebrates too much as the field was now completely set as we headed to the back. WINNER: TONY MIRANDA “Down” by Curse Icon plays over the P.A. System in the Porretto Beach venue. Fans immediately boo as the hated Trent Lost emerges from the backstage area. Trent has some medical taping from the rough table match he had at Category Five. Trent has a microphone in hand and has his head hung as he limps his way into the ring. His music dies away as he makes his way to the center of the ring. TRENTON ARIANNA: “I’m sure you all know what transpired last Sunday. I went to war against Lokie Lindsay. And from the bottom of my heart I can say that Lokie is the toughest son of a botch in all of GIW. Lokie last Sunday when you sent me through that table, man you’ve not only earned my respect. I’m pretty damn sure you’ve earned the respect of everyone here. However speaking of respect, that’s something I’ve been lacking around here as of late. You see a few months ago when Joshua Acquin sidelined me after punting me in the skull, I expected a hero’s welcome when I regained the strength to step back into the ring. But that was the same night I took on three GIW icons in Madison Seton, Megathreat, and Mark Cross. I win the match, but I felt that you all didn’t care that I was back. You all seemed more excited to see the other three than I myself. It set off something in me that I couldn’t control. I thought that I was bigger than GIW… But those thoughts were crushed along with the table I was sent through Sunday. I was even barred from competing tonight because of a few choice tweets, and general insubordination… However I’ve had time to clear my head. And I want you all to know that I’m being genuine when I say that I would like the chance of having a fresh slate with you all. I’m tired of playing this part that was successful a year ago… I just want to be myself again… My name isn’t Trent Lost… My name is Trenton Lain Arianna, and for the first time in a long time, I’m proud of that.” RILEY STEVENS: “Looks like Trent… I mean Trenton is turning a new leaf.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I don’t believe a word of it. Remember the Trent Lost we all know is very deceptive…” TRENTON ARIANNA: “Now moving on, the next stop we have is Moment of Glory Three, an event that is sure to send GIW into higher heights. That coincides the fact that only a few weeks ago, Alex Castellanos was bailed out in our last epic match that we had. I lost my opportunity to became a participant in the Sole Survivor match last Sunday. I’ve done it all in GIW, except that one title that has eluded my grasp forever has been the GIW Undisputed Championship. When I came back I promised to become champion! And that’s a promise I plan on keeping. So Kluge I know you’re watching this. Send out anyone and I mean anyone and I will beat the challenge, and try to solidify my spot on the card, because this is a new day for both myself and GIW… And it’s about damn time that I Seized the Moment! With that Trenton drops the mic as the fans seem to be willing to give him the benefit of the doubt cheering him on for his change of attitude as we head to a commercial break. QUOTE “Victimized” by Linkin Park plays over the Porretto Beach P.A. System, and the fans take up a mixed reaction of jeers and cheers as the number one contender to the GIW Legacy Championship; Zerotine Valentino mad her way from the backstage area down the ramp. REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “This scheduled bout is one fall! For the GIW Legacy Championship. From Rio Rancho, New Mexico, weighing one-hundred and twenty-eight pounds…ZEROTINE VALENTINO!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Our Legacy title contender Zerotine Valentino is out first. I like this girl, she’s got spunk. But I think that Lokie keeps the belt tonight, what do you think Stevens?” RILEY STEVENS: “Both Zerotine and Lokie are very talented, so I can’t really decide if we will crown a new Legacy Champion, or will we see Lokie add another notch to his growing list of failed challengers.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Just pick a side man…” Zerotine is in the ring as “Radioactive” by Imagine Dragons plays over the P.A. System. The GIW Legacy Champion Lokie Lindsay makes his way out of the back hoisting up the Legacy Championship belt. The fans are electric and give Lokie a warm cheer as he heads on down the ramp and into the ring. REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “And from Los Angeles, California… Weighing in at two-hundred and forty-five pounds your GIW Legacy Champion! LOKIE LINDSAY!” Lokie makes his way into the ring still showing off the belt to the crowd. Zerotine is crouched in the opposite corner turnbuckle waiting. Lokie hands the belt over to referee Scott Richards. Richards holds the title in the air in the center of the ring before giving it to Darryl Clinton. Both competitors were staring holes into one another from opposite ends of the turnbuckle. Richards ran by each one of them checking their gear and informing them of the rules. Ding! Ding! Ding! Zerotine rushed out of the corner like a house on fire only to eat a quick standing dropkick by the champion Lindsay. Lokie waste no time trying t mount the grounded Valentino trying to maneuver his way into locking in his finishing submission, the camel clutch. Zerotine is able to fight and squirm her way to the bottom rope before Lokie could get anything going. The referee orders Lokie to get off of Zerotine, as she clung to the bottom rope. As Lokie was getting up, Zerotine kicked out and struck Lokie on his knee. Zerotine gets to her feet quickly and kicks at Lokie’s knee once more, dropping him to one knee. Seeing an opening, Zerotine exits to the apron. She waits for Lokie to get his footing before spring boarding herself over the ropes in an attempt at a springboard roundhouse kick. Lokie ducks under the kick and Zerotine lands on her feet but stumbles due to her momentum. Lokie turns and charges Zerotine trying to clothesline her. Zerotine turns just in time and ducks under Lokie’s extended arm, and rolls away to the opposite end of the ring. Zerotine turns and stares down Lokie once more. RILEY STEVENS: “Pretty even match so far, with neither competitor with an advantage.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Yeah but I think the pace favors Zero so far.” Zerotine motions for a test of strength. Lokie nearly laughs knowing that he’s stronger but obliges. As they meet in the middle of the ring Zerotine deceptively side steps Lokie, and throws a couple of kicks at Lokie’s legs. One kick after another Lokie is gingerly hobbling on one leg. Zerotine goes for another flurry of calf kicks but Lokie extends out his arm and shoves Zerotine away. Zerotine falls back, but hops immediately back up to her feet and rushes at Like again. Zerotine is spot on with a dropkick that landed right on Lokie’s exposed calf. Lokie is sent to one knee. Zerotine gives the champion no time to breath as she immediately runs in showering clubbing shots to Lokie’s head and back. Lokie suffers through the onslaught, however in a quick moment he sizes Zerotine by her throat. The fans start to murmur as it seemed that Lokie was about to turn the tables on Zerotine. However Zerotine is able to chop away Lokie’s grasp on her neck. And follow it up by scoring with a dropkick to Lokie’s upper body that sends the big man on to the mat. Zerotine takes advantage of Lokie’s grounded state, by getting read to land a standing moonsault upon Lokie. The moonsault connects cleanly and Zerotine hooks Lokie’s leg for the pin attempt.1..2..Lokie is able to kick out, and Zerotine looks zeroed in as she immediately gets up for another standing moonsault. This moonsault lands flawlessly again, and Zerotine once more goes in for the cover. 1..2..Lokie is able to power out and push Zerotine off of his frame. RILEY STEVENS: “Two quick pin attempts here by Zerotine, she seems to be in control of the match so far.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “That’s good, she needs to keep the pressure on the champ.” Zerotine seems to be up and at Lokie’s legs trying to position herself to lock in a Boston Crab submission hold. Lokie is able to kick Zerotine away, and with enough force to send Zerotine out of the ring and tumbling hard to the outside. Lokie gets up to his feet and walks over to the ring apron. However Zerotine is up and climb back onto the ring apron from the outside. Lokie looks to toss Zerotine back into the ring, but she swiftly avoids his grasp and throws a quick thumb to the eye. Lokie staggers back cupping his hands over his eye, as Zerotine readies her self. She springboards herself over and lands her springboard roundhouse kick to Lokie, sending him hard to the ground again. Zerotine takes a few moments to catch her breath. She them walks toward the set of ropes nearest Lokie’s grounded body. Running at the ropes with full speed Zerotine jumps and lands on the top rope and backflips in midair in an attempt to cave Lokie’s chest in with and Arabian double foot stomp. However jutting his arms forward, Lokie his able to catch and stop Zerotine with an inch of space separating Zerotine’s boots from Lokie’s chest. The fans are going crazy with this display of strength as Lokie keeps both of Zerotine’s feet while slowly making it to one knee. Lokie gets to one knee, and eventually to both of his feet while still holding possession of Zerotine. Zerotine desperately tries to rain down some punches on Lokie’s head, but an energized Lokie powers through the punches. Lokie then lets Zerotine fall forward to Lokie’s other arm. He then lifts her high in the air with both of his arms holding her horizontally in the air. Lokie then parades around the ring with a pleading Zerotine before planting her with a press slam. RILEY STEVENS: “The physical specimen Lokie displays his brute strength in planting Zerotine unceremoniously to the mat below.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Watch your tongue Stevens, this may be Showtime, but this is still a family show!” RILEY STEVENS: “What did I say?” Lokie gets ready, as he waits for Zerotine to get up to her feet. However Zerotine sensing danger quickly slides out of the ring after she gets to her feet. The fans boo her as she holds her sides trying to catch her breath. Lokie not wanting to waste any time walks over to the ropes and grasp an unaware Zerotine by the hair. He lifts Zerotine by her hair to the ring apron.
