GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS SUNDAY NIGHT REVOLT 5/31/15 FROM KAUFFMAN ARENA IN GALVESTON, TEXAS BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! PYRO~! goes off along the ramp inside Kauffman Arena, a novel sight for the fans that week in and week out pack GIW’s arena along Seawall Boulevard in Galveston, Texas. The camera pans around the crowd while “Over and Under” by Egypt Central plays. Signs such as “BRONY V TINDER MVP”, “MARSHALL INVASION”, and “UPRISING’S NEXT STAR” can be seen waving through the crowd as the voices of the commentary team can be heard. SARAH SHARP: "Welcome to another edition of Sunday Night Revolt, right here on Fox Sports Southwest!" OLIVER THIBODAUX: "And not only Southwest! Don’t forget about Fox Sports New Orleans and Fox Sports Oklahoma! GIW is spreading throughout the south!" SARAH SHARP: "We certainly are. One week removed from Extreme TV and two weeks away from one of the biggest shows of the year, Category 5, it is a great time for new fans to be jumping into the world of Galveston Island Wrestling. Tonight’s show will be a good one." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "A good one? A great one! The main event tonight, as advertised, will be Chris Callum, our Undisputed Champion, going one on one with one of the most popular wrestlers in the company, The Sir!" SARAH SHARP: "The fans are certainly going to be torn on who to root for in that match. But I’m willing to bet that people will favoring The Sir. That guy is a true underdog." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Callum eats underdogs for breakfast, Sharpie! If the fans want to be on the winning side of things tonight, they’ll be Team Callum!" SARAH SHARP: "More than anything though, I’m expecting both of these guys to get a lot of respect because they are tremendous competitors. Win or lose, these two are what GIW is all about." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "The Sir is out of his league!" “When Angels Fall” by Breaking Benjamin begins to play as the crowd erupts in cheers when they hear the music of the Undisputed Champion, Chris Callum. SARAH SHARP: "Listen to that reaction! I wonder how many people came out here tonight just to see Chris Callum?" OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Judging by what we just heard, I’d wager a lot." The music continues to play for a while with no sight of Callum. When he finally does step onto the ramp, the crowd hushes down quickly. SARAH SHARP: "What… is that?" OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Is Callum wearing a mask?" SARAH SHARP: "That is him, right?" OLIVER THIBODAUX: "I mean… he has the title belt… and the same tights. That’s gotta be Callum." Chris was indeed wearing a mask. It was black as the night and covered his entire head. There were small slits around his eyes, and an opening for the mouth. Callum slowly walked to the ring, not slapping the hands of the fans as he usually would during his entrance. SARAH SHARP: "We saw him enter with a protective mask at Extreme TV, but everyone expected that. This one doesn’t have any padding, as far as I can see." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "No. That’s just a normal wrestling mask." Callum took his place in the center of the ring with a microphone in his hand. He held the GIW Undisputed Championship belt over his shoulder. CHRIS CALLUM: "Don’t be alarmed, don’t be afraid. It’s still me. I promise." His hands slowly drift over his mask. CHRIS CALLUM: "I know it may seem weird, but when you are in the spotlight every week, sometimes all you want to do is hide yourself. I think it is important than when every GIW fan turns on the show and sees me out here, defending my belt, fighting like a champion, that they can relate to me. When I wrestle without a mask, I am an individual. But now, I am faceless. I invite each and everyone one of you to project your image onto my mask, because that is why I’m here after all. To fight for you. To fight with you. Because you all deserve something good to root for in this world." With a smile, clearly seen through the opening in the mask, he presses on. CHRIS CALLUM: "Now that the mask is out of the way, we can get down to business. I asked for this time because I understand that there is controversy swirling around right now about how I defended my title last week. Some are calling it a fluke. I don’t see it that way. While it may be true that Johnny Violence was able to continue fighting, that wasn’t the stipulation that you, the fans, voted on. You wanted a first blood match, and you got one. Johnny Violence tried to take me out with a set of brass knuckles, and his own ego cost him the opportunity to become a champion. It wasn’t a fluke, it was fate." Cheers from the crowd. CHRIS CALLUM: "And fate is why The Sir and I will cross paths later tonight, why Bronx Valescence and I will cross paths at Category 5, and why, in time, I will get my hands on Alexis Terry once again. Good will triumph over evil, and I will be the catalyst. One by one, they will fall, but I will always stand tall because I have the trust and the wills of each every one of you pressing me on when I step into this ring." There is a slight murmur through the crowd, which Chris Callum immediately notices. CHRIS CALLUM: "You’re probably thinking right about now why I decided to put The Sir into the same group as Johnny Violence, Bronx Valescence, and Alexis Terry. After all, he is changed right? He fights for all of you, right? Wrong. The Sir only turned on Alexis Terry because he was given the opportunity to earn a shot at a title. He fights for titles, and therefore, he fights for himself. Nothing has changed. He is still the egotistical man that you have been booing for a year now. Just because he asks for forgiveness doesn’t mean he truly wants it or deserves it." The crowd doesn’t really know what to think. They sit, perplexed. CHRIS CALLUM: "Tonight is important. I have the opportunity to knock off The Sir and expose him for what he truly is. He is no friend of yours. And while that pill may be hard to swallow, it is true. The roaring ovation you all gave him at Extreme TV was evidence enough that he has pulled the wool over your eyes. He is deceiving all of you. If put my title on the line tonight, I’m sure he would revert back to his old ways. Because this title is all he wants." A few boos begin to trickle down from the audience but the majority of them continue to sit quietly. CHRIS CALLUM: "You’ve trusted me all this time. You’ve cheered me. You’ve accepted me as a hero. I appreciate that. But sometimes, a hero has to say the hard things for the greater good. That is what I’m doing tonight. Don’t think of this as anything more than a warning. I still fight for you, but now I must beg you all to open your eyes and see The Sir for what he is." More boos. CHRIS CALLUM: "And for that matter… see Madison Seton for what she is! It was only a few months ago that she would walk down to this ring and lambast all of you. She was egocentric. She was despicable. But now she has a baby, and I get that being a mother is incredibly admirable, but even snakes are mothers, ladies and gentlemen. Madison Seton has returned to Galveston with a smile on her face, but if you look into her eyes… you can see it. You can see that’s lusting for gold. My gold. OUR gold." More boos. Callum is mortified. He composes himself, and then continues. CHRIS CALLUM: "I’m not asking you to hate these people. I’m not asking you to boo them. All I’m asking is that you make them earn your forgiveness. Don’t allow them to show up instantly be forgiven. You made me earn your love. I came out here and won, won, and won some more. Only then did you respect me. Hold them to the same standard, because I would be willing to wager that they will buckle under the pressure while I continue to stand tall." With that, cheers return to the mix, largely draining out the boos. Callum stares directly into the camera. CHRIS CALLUM: "But I love to be proven wrong." He drops the mic as the music picks up to mostly cheers. He moves to exit up the ramp, slapping the hands of fans this time as he goes. SARAH SHARP: "Everything about that was weird." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "You can’t deny that Chris makes some pretty good points though. Why do fans cheer Seton and The Sir the same way they cheer Chris? Seton and Sir have a pretty bad track record here. But Callum? That dude is spotless, Sarah." SARAH SHARP: "But doesn’t he believe in forgiveness? Redemption?" Maybe he’s just… stressed?" SARAH SHARP: "Maybe we’ll find out later tonight in the main event… but for now… we are going to jump to a quick commercial break. We’ll back after these messages."
