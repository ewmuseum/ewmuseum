GIW — Card

Pre-show, 14 September 2015

14 September 2015
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
In this Rebellion match, we find a returning Britney Filmore in the ring with a local talent, Layla Hollen. The crowd is excited to see Britney back in a GIW ring, and by the looks of it, she is happy to be back. The match begins with Layla getting a quick upperhand, but it doesn't last long as Britney is able to power her way back into the match and get her offense rolling again. Once she started, she never stopped, and it just took one "Stunning Makeover", a double knee jawbreaker, to get the job done. Filmore celebrated her victory with the fans. WINNER: BRITNEY FILMORE The next Rebellion match of the night featured Dustin James Holt making his debut in the company against Adam Kennedy, a local San Antonio talent. DJH started the match off quickly, showing the kind of explosive offense that would remind diehard GIW fans of Christopher Darren Erns. A few stiff punches and kicks were all it took for DJH to get the best of Kennedy, and when he hit his finishing move, "The Jawbone Cracker", a seated three quarter facelock jawbreaker, it was all over. An impressive win for a man who may make a huge impact here in GIW. WINNER: DUSTIN JAMES HOLT The final Rebellion match of the night featured Nick Black, a man who's attitude didn't take long to piss off the fans. Nick was up against local talent Darnell Hooks, a local icon, or so we gathered. Nick's high impact offense was impressive as he landed several variations of the DDT on his opponent quickly in the match, taking time in between each one to brag about how good he is. Finally, the match came to an end when he locked in "Reflects of Futility" for a quick tapout. After the match, he continued to gloat. The fans were ready to get him out of the ring. WINNER: NICK BLACK
