Revolt, 5 April 2015

5 April 2015

GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS SUNDAY NIGHT REVOLT LIVE! From Kauffman Arena in Galveston, TX – April 5th, 2015 Raised By Wolves by Family Force 5 on Grooveshark BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! The camera pans around Kauffman Arena as the fireworks spew into the night sky one after another. The sound of the booming lights in the distance signal to the fans that it is now time for the show to begin, and in response they all rise to their feet and cheer loudly. We move through the crowd slowly, seeing signs such as “CALLUM IS SUPERMAN”, “CDE IS GONNA KILL YOU”, and “NO TRIPLE CROWN” are waving proudly. While this is happening, the announcers begin to speak. RILEY STEVENS: "Tonight is the night ladies and gentlemen! Welcome to GIW Sunday Night Revolt right here on Fox Sports Southwest!" OLIVER THIBODAUX: "We are one week removed from Seize the Moment, and we have a new GIW Undisputed Champion. Tonight will be the beginning of the long road to Exile which will be aired right here on May 3rd." RILEY STEVENS: "That is right. Last Sunday we saw Chris Callum become a double champion when he was able to pick up a pinfall and then ascend the ladder to grab the ultimate prize." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Yeah, yeah… superman unbeatable Jesus right." RILEY STEVENS: "Chris Callum would be the first to tell you that his work here is not done Ollie. He isn’t the type to rest on his laurels with two title belts." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Well he better be ready, because Alexis Terry is coming after him, and everyone else on the roster too! He has two targets on his back, and now we are gonna find out if the California Kid will sink or swim." RILEY STEVENS: "It will certainly be an uphill battle for the new champion, but if anyone can do it, it will be him." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "He is a talented wrestler, but I have my doubts." “Dignity” by New Politics hits the PA system as every fan in the arena, big and small, jumps to their feet. Chris Callum steps onto the ramp with his two title belts thrown over his shoulder. He waits on the stage for a moment, soaking it all in which a big grin on his face before he makes his way down to the ring. RILEY STEVENS: "Well here he is. You can tell how excited he is, can’t you Ollie?" OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Yeah. And it won’t be long until someone wipes that big goofy grin off his face, and then takes both of his belts." RILEY STEVENS: "There is no reason for that kind of negativity. This man is the Undisputed Champion, and he is also the Deep South Champion. He may have just earned both of those belts, but dammit, to hold two belts at once is a big accomplishment." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Gonna make it even better when someone makes him bleed." RILEY STEVENS: "He’s already proven himself. He fought a match against a maniacal Devlynn Smith where he showed he could tolerate a lot of punishment. Then he outwrestled one of the best wrestlers in the nation, Carla Owens, proving he can hang against a technically superior opponent. Then he won Sole Survivor, outlasting the likes of Alexis Terry, Johnny Violence, and Gary Ford. Who else does he have to beat to earn your respect?" OLIVER THIBODAUX: "He won’t ever have my respect." Callum has since arrived in the ring. As his music fades out, the intensity of the positive reaction from the fans is made even more clear. He holds the mic down and throws both of his title belts up into the air as the reaction only gets louder. With a big smile, he stops, and then begins to speak. CHRIS CALLUM: "Alright, alright. I get it. This wasn’t just a big week for me, this was a big week for all of you because you can finally say you have a champion who appreciates everything you do for this company." There were roars from the crowd once again. CHRIS CALLUM: "It wasn’t an easy road to get here. I struggled, fought, and bled for the chance… and then I was the one to Seize the Moment." “CALLUM. CALLUM. CALUM” chants can be heard. CHRIS CALLUM: "But now that I hold two titles. I know that there are two targets on my back, and the entire roster is aiming for what I have. I am ready to take on any challenge they can throw at me, but I will not be duped into defending my title against an unworthy opponent. Which brings me to Alexis Terry…" There mentioning of her name elicits loud boos from the crowd. CHRIS CALLUM: "I know all of you have been watching the promos posted on gogalveston.com, and so you know that Alexis Terry wants me to defend my Deep South title in our match tonight. She threatened to hurt me if I didn’t, and she definitely has the capability to do that. With guys like Blade Vega and David Sinclair at her side, the odds against me aren’t very inviting. I’ve received a lot of feedback from you guys and most of you are telling me it is a fight worth fighting. But some of you think I should just give her what she wants so I can live to fight another day." There is a mixed response from the fans. CHRIS CALLUM: "So the question is, did I go to Aspen Chaud earlier in the night to tell her that I will defend my title? And do I plan on laying down for Alexis Terry?" The majority of the fans don’t like this option. CHRIS CALLUM: "The answer is..." Callum smiles wide. CHRIS CALLUM: "No!" His announcement is just what the fans wanted to hear. CHRIS CALLUM: "Alexis Terry – if you want to become a triple crown champion, or if you even want to become a grand slam champion, you’re going to have to earn your shot just like everyone else in the back has to. I am a fighting champion, and I will not run for you, but I am not going to put something I EARNED on the line just because you think you’re entitled to it. You haven’t won a match two weeks in a row, and in my book, that doesn’t make you a worthy champion." He turns towards the ramp now. CHRIS CALLUM: "If you have a problem with anything I just said, then you can go and whine to Aspen, or you can come out here right now by yourself and we can get the main event going right here at the beginning of the show!" Chris is leaning against the top rope now. He rips his shirt off and throws it to a fan in the audience. He is ready for a fight as “Off With Her Head” by Icon for Hire hits and the fans know this can only be Alexis Terry. Off With Her Head by Icon for Hire on Grooveshark Indeed, the GIW Hall of Famer and runner up of the Seize the Moment Ladder Match last week steps onto the ramp, David Sinclair, the brains of the operation, to the left, and Blade Vega, the muscle, to her right. The trio stand on the ramp for a moment while the boos shower in. Alexis smirks, darting her eyes to both sides of the Kauffman Arena before she speaks. ALEXIS TERRY: "Well Chris, that is an interesting decision. But before I tell you how stupid you are, let me build you up first. You won the match at Seize the Moment. Congratulations. You’re the Champion of Champions and you should be proud of it. And I’m glad you realize that everyone in the back is gunning for you, but I don’t think you realize how isolated you really are. I could march down there with my army and you would be helpless. So hold on to your gold while you can, because you won’t have it for long." More boos from the fans. Callum is itching to get at Alexis, but she doesn’t move from the stage area. CHRIS CALLUM: "Right – of course. You could come down here and kill me, right? Well if you can do it, come on and do it." Alexis turns to Sinclair and the two share a laugh. ALEXIS TERRY: "I’m not in the business of slaughtering helpless little animals. So I will give you one last chance. Put your title belt on the line, tonight. I’m not even asking that you give it up easy. You can give everything you have in the match, but I am confident that I will leave Kauffman tonight with the Deep South title around my waist. You’re alone Chris. Don’t fight the inevitable. Live to fight another day." Chris turns to the crowd who are booing the hell out of Alexis and her gang, then he turns back to Terry. He adjusts both title bets over his shoulder and then holds up the mic. CHRIS CALLUM: "I don’t need any other wrestlers. I have one thousand screaming fans to back me up. Come get me, Alexis." Terry looks at Blade, who is rearing to go. ALEXIS TERRY: "Alright. You made your choice, and I like my odds." Terry drops the mic, sending a loud thud through the arena. She, Sinclair, and Vega made their way down to the ring. Sinclair rolls up his sleeves and Vega flexes his biceps, but Terry never breaks her gaze from the ultimate goal – the Deep South title to complete her triple crown. Terry stops at the apron while Sinclair, a larger man than Callum in his own right, slides with Vega under the ring. The assault begins. Callum begins stomping away on Blade Vega while Sinclair sneaks behind and clubs him in the back. This prompts Callum to turn around and lay a big forearm shot to Sinclair which sends him to the mat. Vega is up now, and he begins to trade blows with Callum. Back and forth they go, but Blade ultimately is the stronger man and wins the battle. He grabs Callum by the neck as Terry climbs into the ring. “PICK HIM UP BLADE”, she screams. Blade does so – lifting Chris into his finisher, the overhead press slam, but Chris wiggles out and lands on his feet. Hitting a cheap shot on Terry and then dropkicking Blade out of the ring. RILEY STEVENS: "Callum is in control of the situation now! Will he be able to overcome these odds?!" Callum begins slamming Terry with right hook after right hook. He turns to the crowd and signals that it is time for one of his signature moves, the spinout powerbomb. The erupt in cheers as he sets Terry up, when out of nowhere Sinclair lands a huge low blow that sends Callum folding over to his knees. Terry and Sinclair stand on either side of Callum while Blade slides back into the ring. They begin to size up their prey. RILEY STEVENS: "Is there anyone from the back that is gonna help this guy?" OLIVER THIBODAUX: "No! Of course not! This is competition!" RILEY STEVENS: "This is inhumane, Ollie!" OLIVER THIBODAUX: "How nice of Callum to reenact the Passion for us on Easter Sunday." The boos in the arena are thunderous at this point. Terry begins to pull Callum up, and then sends him back down with a slap to the face that echoes through the arena. The crowd is nearly riotous as Callum looks like he has no shot at recovery. Then, suddenly, two cups and a pile of popcorn are thrown into the ring, landing on Terry’s heel. She doesn’t seem to notice, but the others in the audience certainly did. Within the span of few seconds, every fan in the audience was throwing their merchandise at Terry. Popcorn, soda, hot dogs, shoes, t-shirts – all of it was bombarding her and her faction. RILEY STEVENS: "Oh my God! It’s a revolution! Look at this sight!" OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Someone get Terry out of there before they start throwing chairs!" Blade didn’t hear Oliver, but he knew what to do. He scooped up Terry over his shoulder and carried her out of the ring, running to the back with Sinclair following closely behind. “F*CK YOU TERRY” chants are the dominant ones in the arena as the trash throwing stops. Callum uses the ropes to pull himself up, but though he is hurt, he is smiling. He picks up his title belts, tossing them both over his shoulder, then points to all of the fans before he points back to the title belts. “Dignity” begins to play again. RILEY STEVENS: "The fans saved Chris Callum from a beatdown tonight! What a moment!" OLIVER THIBODAUX: "This is despicable! Now someone is going to have to clean all of this up before we start the show. But I guess these fans just want to stand there and watch Callum all night! What a stupid group of people!" Callum was leading “GIW” chants now. RILEY STEVENS: "Ladies and gentlemen, we are going to have to take a short break while we restore order here in the Kauffman Arena. We will be right back with the show." Fade into a commercial with Callum standing on the top turnbuckle, holding both title belts into the air. QUOTE As we come back from commercial, it seems that the action has already gotten started in the ring. Gaylord Prime has Yuri in an airplane spin that is going round and round and round. Brody Harper is laid out in the corner. RILEY STEVENS: "Sorry folks! We told these guys to wait up but they were dying to go after each other." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "This is embarrassing.!" Gaylord sets Yuri down while taking a quick breather, then he signals to the crowd that it is time for the dreaded heart punch. He goes for it, leaning wayyyy back to get all the extra heart stopping power, but Yuri manages to back away just in time before landing a beautiful dropkick that sends the old time to the ground. Still a bit dizzy from the spin, Yuri takes a moment to lean on the ropes, but Gaylord Prime doesn’t want to give him a chance to catch his breath. He pulls himself up then hits a big chop block that sends Yuri to his knees. Gaylord is on a roll now, and he doesn’t want to stop. He holds out two fingers and tries to place them in Yuri’s mouth for the dreaded claw submission, but Yuri rolls under the bottom rope and pulls Prime’s feet out from under him, sending him to his butt. OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Oh, his pelvis!" Gaylord screams, holding his lower back. Yuri slides back under the rope and lands a few stiff kicks to the fallen veteran. He then pulls Prime up and tosses him into the corner before landing a huge stinger splash. Prime takes a few steps forward, then, dazed, plops to the mat in a heap. Yuri pulls Prime to the center of the ring and flips him over, before signaling to the fans that he wants to hit a lionsault. He CONNECTS. 1…..2…..KICKOUT. How did Prime manage to get out of that one? OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Prime is dead. He’s just twitching like a snake." Yuri spends little time being worried about the near fall. He pulls Gaylord towards the corner and climbs to the top. He goes for the Phoenix Splash, but Brody Harper catches him in mid air with a huge shoulder tackle. RILEY STEVENS: "Brody was playin’ possum." With Yuri down and out, Brody sets his eyes on the fallen Prime. He takes step forward, moving ever closer. He picks Prime up and sets him up for a powerbomb. Once Prime is lifted, HE ESCAPES. He jumps down behind Brody and rolls him up… 1…. 2….. 3!!! RILEY STEVENS: "GERIATIC GENOCIDE – THE THUNDERMASHERS HAVE A WIN." Yuri rolls out of the ring, clutching the back of his head from the brutal shoulder tackle. Gaylord is wheezing as the referee raises his hand high into the air. WINNER: GAYLORD PRIME We cut to the GIW Interview Lounge where we see Sarah Sharp standing with a microphone. SARAH SHARP: "Ladies and gentlemen, please welcome my guest at this time, Gavin Achilles." The camera zooms out as Gavin steps into the scene. He smiles at Sarah. GAVIN ACHILLES: "Thank you for giving me a chance to talk tonight, Sarah." She nods, turning towards him. SARAH SHARP: "No problem at all. Last week at Seize the Moment, you picked up your first win in GIW by defeating a veteran in Ryan Smith. Go ahead and explain what that win means to you." Gavin nods, running his hand across the back of his neck. Clear interviews are not something he is comfortable with. GAVIN ACHILLES: "Yeah, it was a big win, no doubt. I have been struggling so far in GIW, but a win like that last week at a big event is just what I needed to get my feet under me. Ryan Smith is one hell of a competitor, and he took me to limit for sure, but I fought through the pain. I did what I had to do to win the match, and I’m proud right now." He smiles wide. SARAH SHARP: "What is next for you moving forward?" Now he turns a bit more serious. GAVIN ACHILLES: "You know, I have a lot of doubters. It isn’t hard to find people in the back and in the audience that don’t think I have what it takes to compete here because of my disability. But that’s why I chose the name Achilles to represent me in the ring. I have an Achilles heel, and it’s my disability. The only difference is, I can’t hide it. Everyone who steps into the ring with me has been thinking about ways to exploit me, and that’s fine. I don’t want to be like Achilles. I don’t want to have a weakness that can be exploited. My name reminds me each and every week that I need to fighter harder than the rest to make up the difference." Sarah was floored that he had put so much thought into his ring name. SARAH SHARP: "That’s certainly an interesting choice. What have you been doing to get ready for your match tonight with Sammy Glasgo, a man who was trained and brought to GIW by former Undisputed Champion Alex Castellanos?" At the mention of Castellanos, Gavin clenched his teeth. GAVIN ACHILLES: "I don’t know much about Sammy Glasgo other than that he was trained by Alex Castellanos. Sammy is new to this, just like I am. The only difference is, while I can’t watch a lot of tape on him, he doesn’t even need to study me. All he has to do is look at a picture, and he can formulate his game plan. But I’m not worried. We have similar experience, and if I can beat him tonight I will show everyone in the audience and in the back that I am progressing faster than everyone thinks." Sharp nodded as Gavin exhaled. SARAH SHARP: "And how are you going about achieving that week by week progression?" He raised an eyebrow. GAVIN ACHILLES: "Well every match I fight here in GIW, I learn a little bit more about myself. When I was fighting Ryan Smith last week, I learned that I can take a lot of punishment, and I was proud to know that. It wasn’t that long ago, only two weeks actually, when everyone thought I would run away from GIW after Gary Ford pummeled me. But I don’t run. I’m here to stay." Sharp offered her hand to Gavin, which he shook. SARAH SHARP: "Thanks for coming tonight Gavin, we really appreciate it, and we hope you have a good match up next. Do you have any final words?” Gavin turned towards the camera. GAVIN ACHILLES: "Yeah, I do actually. I want to thank Paul Marshall. He is a guy that the fans may not know about, but he does a lot to help everyone in the back get acclimated to the fed. Last week, came to me after my match with Ryan and told me that I was facing Sammy this week. I felt like it was a cheap shot. I had just won my first match, and I barely had time to celebrate before I was forced to do it all again. But that’s the nature of this business, I guess. He came up to me and told me that ‘fear makes the wolves seem bigger’, because he could see that I was worried. Most people wouldn’t have said anything, but he decided he needed to share that little bit of wisdom with me, and I appreciate that. It’s nice to know that there are people looking out for you." A voice is heard off screen. The voice of Gary Ford. GARY FORD: "Ah, but Gavin… sometimes the wolves are bigger than you could even imagine." He enters the scene wearing a Cowboy’s shirt, with a toothpick in his mouth. GARY FORD: "Did you think I was done with you, kid? After I drug your face across all four sides of the ring, did you think I felt like I was finished? No. I got sidetracked with that stupid gimmick match in the main event, but I haven’t forgotten you. Fear may make the wolves seem bigger, but confidence makes them seem less deadly than they really are. You have one win over your belt, then you got some philosophy from an old timer like me and all of a sudden you’re superman? That isn’t how this works. You’re still the same little shit you were two weeks ago when everyone was laughing at you in the ring." Gavin moved face to face with Gary. GAVIN ACHILLES: "What is your problem? Why do you feel like you need to target me every chance you get? I’m trying to do the same thing you are – make a name for myself…" Gary chuckled, tossing the toothpick to the ground. GARY FORD: "That’s the difference, Stub. I’ve already made a name for myself, I’m here because this is all I know how to do, and I’ve been doing it for a long time. You’re just a gimmick, better suited in a carnival." Just after the final word came out of his mouth, Gavin reared back and slammed Ford with huge punch that send the veteran to the ground, face down. GAVIN ACHILLES: "Do I punch like a gimmick?" As Gary clutched his face, the camera moved back up to Gavin, who didn’t seem proud of what he did, nor did he seem regretful. For him this was something that needed to be done. He exhaled, and then exited as we fade to commercial. QUOTE When we cut back from commercial, Gavin Achilles and Sammy Glasgo are already in the center of the ring ready to square off. DING! DING! DING! RILEY STEVENS: "Alright, Sammy Glasgo, the proclaimed new golden age by Alex Castellanos, is all set to square off against the one armed wrestler Gavin Achilles." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "This one should be fun." Sammy goes straight for Gavin’s legs, just like Alex taught him. But he placed himself in a vulnerable position sinking straight to the ground. Gavin sank down to a knee and began nailing Sammy with several strong blows to the back. Sammy is quickly overcome by Gavin and ends up covering himself from blow after blow. Sammy quickly slides out of the ring to regain his composure, but Gavin tracks him down. A few big elbows later and Gavin has tossed Sammy back into the ring, RILEY STEVENS: "It looks like going for the leg might not have been such a good idea after all. Especially when your opponent only has one arm." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Yeah, Sammy needs to realize he can’t just replicate Alex step by step. He has to become his own wrestler." Gavin keeps the ground and pound going, but Sammy manages to nail a thumb to the eye which sends Gavin back for a split second, but long enough for Sammy to try a rollup. 1….2…. he couldn’t catch Gavin this early. Gavin and Sammy rise at about the same time, but Sammy quickly sinks to his knees again to try to take Gavin down from the legs. Gavin simply repeats what he did last time. Several punches to the back later and Glasgo was going for the outside of the ring once again, but this time Gavin was ready. He snatched Glasgo and locked him into the guillotine choke. RILEY STEVENS: "I guarantee you that Alex Castellanos wouldn’t try that kind of takedown twice... even if he wanted to target the legs. That’s so stupid." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "We all try to emulate our trainers, but wow. That’s just sad." Sammy is locked in tight, and he is miles away from the ropes. He has no choice but to tap out, and quickly. Gavin releases the hold and rises to his feet as Sammy slinks out of the ring holding his neck. RILEY STEVENS: "See… Gavin learned from his mistakes. Sammy did not!" OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Hopefully he gets it all together soon or we may just see Alex Castellanos come back sooner than we thought – just to kick his ass." We fade to commercial as Gavin gets his hand raised. WINNER: GAVIN ACHILLES We cut to the back where we see Ryan Smith doing pushups on the ground to prepare for his match. Austin Averell, dressed in street clothes, enters the scene. AUSTIN AVERELL: "Ryan! Kick Smith! Hey man, are you ready for your match tonight?" Smith spins around and stands opposite of Averell. RYAN SMITH: "I’m getting there. Did you need something?" Austin scratches his head. AUSTIN AVERELL: "No, I just wanted to wish you good luck tonight. I really wanted to beat George Stark last week, but I couldn’t get it done. I’m not through with him though. I just would like to see you kick his head off tonight." Smith chuckles, then nods. RYAN SMITH: "That’s my plan, and I’m glad you’re not down on yourself after the loss. I came up short at Seize the Moment too, but all we can do is keep looking towards the future. George Stark is a guy who needs to be taken down a peg, and if I’m the man who has to do it, then I will gladly take up the challenge." Austin didn’t seem to be listening. Instead, he was dreaming of what might have been. AUSTIN AVERELL: "I’m going to be the one to take down George Stark. I know I’m better than he is, I just got caught last week. That’s all it was. But now I’m not even booked in a match. It’s like the management doesn’t trust me anymore. I need to make an impact." Smith wasn’t interested, but he decided to entertain Averell. RYAN SMITH: "And how do you plan to do that?" Austin screams his next quote. AUSTIN AVERELL: "I’m gonna kick his ass after the match tonight!" Ryan Smith simply stares at Averell. George Stark enters with his sister, Melissa, as we hear the crowd boo inside the main part of the arena. GEORGE STARK: "You really are stupid, aren’t you Austin. Just give up. If you think the best way to prepare for a sneak attack is to scream your plans throughout the arena, you really aren’t worth my time. And besides, even if you were smart, I have eyes in the back of my head." Melissa smiles, placing her arm on George’s shoulder. MELISSA STARK: "I’m always at ringside for George’s matches. If you think you can slip by me, I’d like to see you try." Smith turns to George and Melissa, holding out his hands. RYAN SMITH: "Nothing is happening after the match. I’ll make sure of that." Austin pauses for a moment as anger consumes him. He takes a step back. AUSTIN AVERELL: "You all are just going to take turns underestimating me, huh? Condescending me, huh? That’s fine. I’ll remember this. I expected it out of George and his sister wife, but not you Ryan!" Smith turns his attention back to Averell. RYAN SMITH: "Matches are matches. Matches are fights. When I beat George Stark, I deserve the attention. I deserve the praise. Do you disagree, Austin?" George took offense to this statement. He and Melissa moved closer to Smith. GEORGE STARK: "When you beat me? That’s a lot of confidence from a little guy like you when he is going against the strongest man in the world. You’ll probably be wanting Austin to come out so people will forget how pathetic you looked when you were stuck to the mat." Melissa laughed loudly. MELISSA STARK: "And we Austin tries to run out and land a cheap shot or two, but he will fail. He always fails. In the end, it will be the same as it always is – Starks standing tall." Austin, feeling left out, decided to interject. AUSTIN AVERELL: "Yeah, well, the Starks really suck on Game of Thrones!" George rolled his eyes. GEORGE STARK: "Keep living in your fantasy world. In this kingdom, the Starks stand tall. See you in the ring, Kick." Melissa poked Ryan Smith in the chest. MELISSA STARK: "If you decide to show, that is!" The two smiled smugly as they exited. RYAN SMITH: "I’m serious. Stay out of the match tonight. You’ll get your chance, but tonight is my time." Austin was full of rage now. AUSTIN AVERELL: "You can’t tell me what I can and can’t do! No one can!" Smith leaned back against the wall. RYAN SMITH: "Fine. You’re right. But you’ve been warned. I don’t want you to be my enemy." Ryan Smith pulled up his elbow pad, and then exited the scene, leaving Austin Averell alone in anger. QUOTE As we come back from commercial, we see Ryan Smith in the ring warming up, swinging his arms back and forth and generally just stretching out. “More Than Meets the Eye” begins to play as George Stark steps onto the ramp with his sister, Melissa by his side. RILEY STEVENS: "George Stark has been pretty damn impressive since he made his debut in GIW, but he will have a tough test tonight in Ryan “Kick” Smith." George and Melissa take their sweet time making it to the ring, soaking in every single boo they possibly can before they get near the ring. Melissa stays outside while George climbs in. He comes face to face with Ryan Smith as the ref implores them to stay apart until the match starts. DING! DING! DING! George is quick out of the gate with a huge clothesline that shakes the ring and Ryan Smith is sent hard down into the mat. Melissa is cheering as George is quickly in control. He hoists Smith up and tosses him across the ring, landing with a huge thud. He then proceeds to do it again. The crowd is booing the hell out of George by now, but his one big shot looks like it may have knocked Ryan Smith out cold. RILEY STEVENS: "That quick attack is going to make the difference here. Ryan Smith isn’t moving." George has had enough fun. He screams out to the fans ‘I’M THE STRONGEST MAN IN THE WORLD’, much to the amusement of his sister who is snickering on the outside. George hoists Smith up and nails the stark contrast. 1. 2….. 3……! George rolls off of Ryan Smith and raises his arms high into the air. He looks to the ramp, waiting for Austin Averell, but Averell never comes. Stark laughs and screams “HE IS AFRAID OF ME” as the fans continue to boo. WINNER: GEORGE STARK We cut to the backstage catering area where Blanca Nieves is sitting off to the side at a table in the corner. She is eating one of Chef Baldwin’s menu items for this week, a buffalo slider. She looks up when Sammy Glasgo approaches her. SAMMY GLASGO: “Let’s fight. LET’S FIGHT." She swallowed her food. BLANCA NIEVES: "For what? You lost your match tonight, is that kid with one harm in your head?" Sammy laughed, running his fingers through his hair. SAMMY GLASGO: “Yeah, he is IS. There are people out there who think some freak with one arm is a better wrestler than me, and I can’t have it. Fight me little lucha bitch – RIGHT NOW." Blanca wiped her mouth with a napkin as she thought. BLANCA NIEVES: "If you want to fight me, we can do it in the ring. I won’t tell you no, but it won’t happen here." ALEXIS TERRY: "You played your card. Next week, we won’t be outdone." She drops the mic as Revolt fades to black, staredown still ensuing.
