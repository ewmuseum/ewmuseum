GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: BLACKOUT! Live from the Seawall Wrestling Exhibit – September 7th, 2014 The arena was black like the void of space. Little flickers of lighters, and flashes of cameras gasped for life within the empty darkness the Seawall Wrestling Exhibit was swallowed in. Suddenly and without warning a single, luminescent burst of light exploded from the stage that nearly lit the entire stadium in a muted blue hue that has been known to be the color of Galveston Island Wrestling. It shaped like the Lone Star of Texas, and burst as bright. Once the last sparks trickled down from its dying vapors over the stage, the lights came back to a roaring crowd of thousands. Chants, signs, and selfie-taking times were had and displayed throughout the crowd as the show was amped and ready to go. Then, again without warning, the lights dimmed, but this time to a deep pink hue and the lights, a brighter version of the same color affixed themselves to the stage as two converging into one. A blonde female, about 5-foot-4 stepped out from behind the wide crimson and black curtains of the decorated stage for the iPPV theme. The crowd were uncertain of who this individual was, very few marks may have recognized her, but not the majority. Still, she made her way to the ring with a microphone in hand, and an envelope tucked between the pipe bomb and her fingers. When in the ring, she waited for the crowd to simmer, then she spoke. AUVA SKYE: Greetings, everyone, this is your administrator speaking." Once she outed herself, the chants began. Some in favor, some already against her, most of which involved some cheating of Tony Miranda and Jack Waters. Others against and for Alexis Terry made their voices known, but soon she raised her hand chest level and waited for them to simmer down. AUVA SKYE: My name is Auva Skye, formerly of PCW, SFWF, and a longtime, well, business associate of Alioth Starre and the enigmatic Altair. I also–" ALEXIS TERRY: "And I'm Alexis Terry! Maybe you've heard of me? I am, after all, the actual bitch in charge here between the two of us! So, Auva, excuse me, but I'm taking back Galveston Island Wrestling." Alexis interrupted the new administrator of GIW. The crowds stood in uproar. Two powers, a crowd divided, and they knew a war was about to happen. Thought Alexis Terry was stripped of her powers, she relinquished them unwillingly. Now, the new COO was here, and she was ready to tear up. Auva set down the microphone so that Alexis may speak her piece, knowing it would do her nothing to return power in her favor. ALEXIS TERRY: "Though you could build a monument with my sins, I'm the ruler GIW wants me to be, so why don't you get off your high heels and leave, bitch. I'm Alexis Terry, the British revolution and glamorous solution to whatever problems have faced me. I have thwarted all of them time and time again. You are no different. So, I've decided to bring an ar–" AUVA SKYE: Not so fast! The board told me to stick your ego up your ass. I'm the owner they need to see, not some brit with a set of bad teeth. So take your little allies and turn those revolting fat cheeks. That rhymes, actually, but it's all true. Now that I'm in control of this company, I will never give it back to you." ALEXIS TERRY: "I can take you on in a match tonight, Auva. Between us with the winner taking GIW." At this point, Auva gave a light snickering at the attempts Alexis made to stifle some sort of reaction out of her. She took the envelope and parted the seal, revealing the paper inside was actually the last page of Alexis Terry's contract. The sight of it surprised the former COO. She did not like seeing her last string that kept her attached to GIW in the hands of her enemy. AUVA SKYE: This is your contract, Alexis Terry. You know it, I know it, and as the camera zooms in to this piece of paper, the crowd will know it. I rip this, and you can never come back." ALEXIS TERRY: "You can't do that!" AUVA SKYE: I am the power now, Alexis. I can and will if you push me to. I can be a fair and just administrator here, and you have fought in matches that will last throughout the ages, but if you push me, I will end you. You may return when you're ready, but only as a performer. What do you choose?" Uncertain of what to do, and too flustered to respond, Alexis Terry looked to the crowd. The tension couldn't even be cut with a knife, but a decision had to be made. The closed in on Alexis Terry with a mid-shot. Her hands were tight around the microphone and her eyes piercing through the woman in the ring. She knew she could take her own in the squared circle, but her power was gone. As she dropped the microphone, Alexis Terry had accepted defeat. The show quickly cut to a shot of the back. Artemis Kaiser was walking the corridors of the arena halls, decorated with past legends of wrestling as themed by the Seawall Wrestling Exhibit. RILEY STEVENS: “We see the return of Artemis Kaiser tonight, and in action against the deranged Michael Lynch." OLIVER THIBODAUX: “She was after the Deep South Championship, but now she'll have to get through Michael Lynch. Since her departure, Kevin Astro has grown stronger and bolder and has taken on the challenge." RILEY STEVENS: “Well, Artemis Kaiser versus Michael Lynch, that match is next!" The camera followed her moments later before cutting back to the ring. QUOTE As ‘Heart-Shaped Box’ played into the arena, Artemis Kaiser made her entrance down the aisle. He then jumped up and continued a running motion. He ran up the turn post and pivoted around with momentum to perform a diving head butt, however his landing was cushioned by the canvas. Kaiser had moved aside at the last second. As the two rest recovering, the referee began a count to force them to their feet. It wasn’t until the count of six that Lynch was first to his feet, Kaiser quickly after him. She made a great show of keeping up with the masked competitor, but he struck her down with ease after an overhead kick. He rolled her over onto her back and covered her, but something struck him funny. His lips curled into a wry smile and he pulled off her to break the count. He wanted to do more to this girl. Lynch used a quick strike to keep her in her place as he dragged her up and spun like the ram to drive the horn into her skull. His signature elbow strike ‘The Ram’s Horn’ knocked her out cold. Lynch dropped to his knees, crossed her arms, and gazed out to the crowd as the referee made the full three count. If she were going to get to the contender spot, he made sure she’d have to work from the ground up. Lynch pulled away and wiped the sweat from his air and beard onto her and then the lights dimmed for his music to signify his victory over Artemis Kaiser. WINNER via Pinfall: MICHAEL LYNCH! The show cut to a close up of Tony Miranda training for his match. Slowly, the camera panned back, his hands wrapped, his body covered in sweat and his intent locked on his match. The punching bag had no chance against him, but soon something broke his concentration. The camera zoomed toward the door, opened when it should have been closed. Tony crossed the room to shut the door when something peaked his interest. The camera cut to a tight close e shot on the note stuck to the door. It changed to a medium shot in front of him, showing him take and slowly open the note. As he looked at the content of the note, his face seemed expression seemed to darken. He was locked in a pensive state until he ordered the cameras out of the room so he may prepare for the match alone. He crumpled and tossed the paper at the door, but the camera did not cut. As Tony returned to the equipment, the camera panned down toward the note to show it's only message was a single red stain in the center. Then, finally, the show cut to the ring. CDE peeled Astro from the mats, turned him toward the ring when the referee threw his arms up, fingers stretched to the sky and the count of ten making the bell ring loud and clear. Astro pushed off of CDE and quickly pulled off an super kick that sent him into Lynch. After that, he fell to a knee and grinned. There was no winner, but at least he didn’t lose. There was still a chance for him yet. NO WINNER via DRAW The show cut from the disturbing ending of the previous match toward a slowly zooming close up of the Undisputed Championship title. It sat in the office of the administrator, but was quickly taken by the referee of the main event. A cut to a wide shot facing toward the stage showed the referee making his entrance to the ring with the belt in one hand, held at his side. The plating of it was masked against his pants, but everyone knew what it was he was holding and what it meant. A winner was going to be crowned tonight. One match saw a draw, but this one had to see a clear victor. He received cheers when he entered the ring and showed the crowd the title, the new and improved design of the Undisputed Championship title, that is. No more clone titles held by two individuals. No, this was one belt, for one champion and that person was going to fight for it tonight. The camera than panned over toward the stage as the lights lit up to the music of the first entrant of the main event. QUOTE OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Harlequin is first to the ring, of course. She is the challenger for the title, having won the chance to seize the moment. Tonight may very well be her moment, on a grand stage in front of thousands. Good luck to them all.” RILEY STEVENS: “The match could belong to anyone, Oliver. She has one-third of a chance, but I’m betting that it is a good possibility of seeing her as the new champion tonight if she applies herself well enough. I know she and Beck might strain against one another, but if she can pin Madison, then she’s got it.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Speaking of, now we have Seton coming to the ring. Note that neither of the champions will be entering with their versions of the Undisputed title. No, because the real one is in the ring and held high by the referee. Tonight, we’re going to blackout that there was ever two championship title holders and crown a single leader amongst the roster.” RILEY STEVENS: “Exactly, Oliver. That is the sole reason for the theme of this event being called ‘Blackout’. We are blacking out the past and stepping into the unknown. A new journey, though we may have some old faces, we’re going to be seeing a lot of new paths open up and that starts with the most important one of them all–” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “The Undisputed Champion.” RILEY STEVENS: “Absolutely. Now we have Beck coming to the ring, and both he and Madison are firm in their beliefs that they are the cemented champion, but tonight it doesn’t matter about who believes what. Tonight is about ending the confusion of old feuds and past mysteries and moving forward with a clear path ahead. Maybe Beck Ramsey will make it out the champion, or maybe it will be Madison Seton. Time will tell, no, not time; tonight.” The referee dropped his arm from the sky and displayed the title to all three competitors. He then set it aside to a man outside of the ring. This man then retreated toward the side tables where the bell ringer sat and placed the title on a small folding table specifically placed for it to be displayed throughout the match. Tonight was the night. After the ring, the three converged upon one another. First it started with a double-team on Madison Seton. Harlequin and Beck both threw kicks to the gut of their shared opponent and performed a double DDT. Beck then rolled Madison out of the ring, intending to follow her out, much akin to how CDE and Astro had fought, but before he could, noting that he would try to utilize the brutality of outside environs, Harlequin pulled him away from the ropes. He turned toward her and stopped. He wasn’t about to strike her just yet, not so soon in the match, but then she shrugged it off and performed a spinning wheel kick that landed Beck outside of the ring where he desired to be. She quickly exited and threw him back in, she did not want a repeat of the last match. Soon, he was drawn into a chin-lock. He tried to escape and could even feel the ropes with his fingertips, but it wasn’t enough to hold on to. He could not extend further to grab that lower rope and because of it, she tortured him. The hold was tight and he knew she would let go only when she wanted to, or when he tapped. She finally did push off of him, but only to transition into the next maneuver. She held to a theme here, and applied another hold, this time the sharpshooter. He resisted at first, but as the crowds recognized the maneuver they cheered and he was drawn into it like all of the power in his body just drained and his strength to resist withered beneath her. Letting go, she did not stop the assault, she merely wanted to move from a submission hold to a double foot stomp. Then, of course, she resumed the sharpshooter only to torture Beck Ramsey further. She knew applying it harder, and longer would only weaken him more. As the hold lingered, he tried his best to crawl and reach the ropes. The crowd got behind him with stomps and cheer, but just as his hands were about to grasp the ring’s barrier, she would pull him back in. This happened twice before he nearly flipped out and finally flipped himself over. The mere act forced Harlequin to stagger forward and stop herself at the ropes. He used a kip-up to pull himself onto his feet and charged at her for a clothesline. He knew she’d counter, so he jolted to the side and caught her off guard. She leaned back on the ropes, hoping that his charge was let her use his momentum to send him toppling to the matted floor, but his sudden side step confused her. He used this to pull off a quick belly-to-belly suplex and then a sudden cover. She refused to let him win, so she pulled a shoulder up off the white canvas and the referee broke the pin. He made the call which agitated Ramsey, but there was nothing he could do but to continue. Harlequin tried to recover, but Beck rolled her toward him, onto her back again and folded her up into a single crab submission hold. If she could do it to him, he will to her. It was around this time, as the clock ticked with seconds passing like hours, that Madison Seton finally returned to the ring. She had bided her time outside and let the two wear themselves down a bit. As she slid in from behind, Beck hadn’t even noticed her, but unfortunately Harlequin did. Like magic, she pushed out of the submission hold, which forced Beck to turn and catch Madison’s charge and counter it with a bridging German suplex. The pin lasted all of zero seconds before Harlequin lunged down and struck Beck’s stomach to break the cover. He recoiled and held his stomach just as Harlequin pulled Madison to her feet, performed her finisher ‘Clowning Around’, which was a variation of the jumping implant DDT and then stole the pin from Beck. At the count of two he turned, reached for a leg and tried to jerk one of them free, but as the referee’s palm came down to strike three, Madison’s shoulders pulled up by Beck’s forceful tug. It was so subtle that the referee nearly missed it and a victor was almost crowned right then and there. This upset Harlequin. She turned with piercing daggers for eyes against Beck and kicked him away, a cold boot to the face to punish him. She rolled out of the ring, not worried about a fast one being pulled by Ramsey, because she knew he would pull away from another cover attempt the moment her favorite weapons were pulled from a plant in the audience. Two black pistols, one in each hand, and her fingers on the triggers. Beck, the referee, and Madison scattered as the shots were fired. Harlequin giggled with maniacal glee as they bought the faux-firing. Beck was first to catch on and launched himself at her from the turn post, running first to gain momentum. She aimed up at him hoping he’d be deterred, but when he came crashing down on her with a flying lariat, she knew that failed. He was quick to throw her back in the ring and try another cover on Madison after a DDT planted the woman. Harlequin bounced back quicker than expected and pulled Beck from their shared opponent. She pulled herself and Beck to their feet, ran him over the ropes and then turned on Madison. This happened as sudden as a gunshot when Harlequin descended upon her victim, performed her 720 DDT variation and, like a blink of the eye, picked up the win. WINNER via Pinfall: HARLEQUIN! As the bell rings, signifying the end of the match. The realization of what has happened has already hit Beck as Harlequin celebrates with GIW Undisputed Championship in the ring. Instead of heading directly back into the ring, Ramsey heads around the outside of the ring while Harlequin hugs the title belt to her chest. In a slightly uncharacteristic manner, she bursts into tears as she holds the gold plate close to her. Meanwhile, Ramsey picks up his coat and pulls out an object small enough for him to conceal in his hand. He then returns to the ring apron and climbs in. While Harlequin savors her epic win, Beck walks over to her. When she notices his approach, she stands up and faces him while holding the title. Unexpectedly, Beck pulls her into a hug, holding her tightly. He says something to her that we can only be left to assume is “congratulations” before giving her a kiss on the head. Both of them then turn toward the audience and Beck raises Harlequin’s hand to a massive pop from the crowd as Harlequin holds the GIW Undisputed title up with her free hand. After a few long moments, Beck releases Harlequin’s hand then walks over to the ropes and gets a microphone from the ring announcer. He takes his time walking back toward the center of the ring while Harlequin is watching him. BECK RAMSEY: "Mel, congratulations on your win. There’s no doubt about it to me or anyone else here that you deserve that title.” The crowd cheers for the good sportsmanship that is being displayed by Beck Ramsey, but he continues through it causing the crowd to silence once more. BECK RAMSEY: "You and I have known one another for quite some time now, and no matter what I’ve done. Whether it has been bein’ the good guy, bein’ the bad guy, or whatever, there has always been one person by my side…” Suddenly, Beck Ramsey drops to one knee while he looks up at Harlequin, who seems at a loss for words as if she can’t believe what she’s seeing. Before she can say anything though, Beck continues speaking as he holds out the object that he had been hiding, a beautiful engagement ring with a rather large diamond set atop. BECK RAMSEY: "There is only one person I want by my side… for the rest of my life. Melissa Lynn Reed, would you do me the honor of being my wife?” The whole crowd seems to be hushed as they await Harlequin’s answer. Beck’s blue gaze doesn’t leave Harlequin’s green eyes as she seems very hesitant to answer. After moments of silence, tears begin to well up in her eyes as she removes the purple glove on her left hand and holds her hand towards him. The Harlequin is nodding with a humbled smile on her face as Beck slides the gorgeous engagement ring onto her finger. The crowd erupts as Ramsey smiles and returns to his feet before pulling Harlequin close and kissing her deeply. The program cut to the GIW! Logo, then slowly faded to black.
