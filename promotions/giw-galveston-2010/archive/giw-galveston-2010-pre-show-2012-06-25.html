Pre-show, 25 June 2012

25 June 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Open Battle Royal did not dissapoint tonight as three stars showed up to compete - Vega, Zerotine Valentino, and a local wrestler by the name of Steve Henry. Vega quickly jumped on Henry, seeing him as the weaker link, and showed the intensity she possesses by throwing wild and left and rights at him, stunning long enough for Zerotine to swoop in from behind and take him down with a reverse DDT that shook the ring. The two women pulled him and tossed him over without much disruption, then they locked up. Vega was stronger and quicker, so Zerotine struggled out of the test of strength and rakes the eyes before spearing Vega to the ground. The crowd was behind Vega now after the cowardly move. Zerotine pulled her up and attempted a DDT, but Vega hit a Northern Lights suplex on her opponent to get the upper hand. Zerotine tried a small package, but pins don’t count. Vega grabbed Zerotine by the hair and went for her finisher, the Code Breaker, but missed, landing right on her spine. Zerotine got momentum and dropped her leg right across Vega’s throat. The entire crowd thinks this match is over and Zerotine tries tossing Vega out, but she landed on the apron but by a few inches. Zerotine charged, but Vega went under the second rope and drove her shoulder into her gut. She picked Zerotine up, trying to suplex her out, but Zerotine was coordinated enough to land on the apron as well. They traded shots, they were both teetering, but then Vega got pay back and scraped the eyes herself. With Zerotine disoriented, Vega climbed the turnbuckle, and connected with an enziguri like kick, landing safely inside the ring, but sending Zerotine in a heap down to the floor. WINNER: VEGA
