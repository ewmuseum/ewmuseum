Tuesday Night Evolve, 21 May 2012

21 May 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: EVOLVE LIVE! From Oklahoma State Fair Arena in Oklahoma City, OK – May 21, 2012 GIW Evolve starts off with a bang as “Break” by Three Days Grace, the familiar entrance music of Daniel Starr, hits the PA system, and this raucous crowd emits boos that shake the Oklahoma State Fair Arena. From the entrance ramp that GIW has constructed on the north side of the structure, Daniel Starr begins his trek down to the ring in his street clothes, a black shirt and blue jeans, and makes sure to take his time, milking every possible boo from the fans. Sliding into the ring, he grabs a microphone from ringkeeper’s table and stands front and center as, slowly, his music begins to fade. Cameras pan around to show the masses in the stands as he begins. DANIEL STARR: “You all showed up this early, to watch Evolve? The pre-show? You all could be working out right now, burning the fat rolls and sweating out the ding dongs you all pile down your gullets here in Oklahoma, the bastard son of Kansas and the returned adopted orphan of Texas - but you aren’t, you are here, half drunk and ready to watch the wrestlers GIW refuses to allow on the main television show. But I understand, working out and having a body like this is something that not every Oklahoman can achieve, so your method of achieving happiness is to drown your sorrows in beer and shitty whisky. It is a real respectable way to live, stay classy Oklahoma.” As you would expect, the crowd wasn’t exactly pleased with what Starr had to say, and they let him know it by trying to boo him out of the arena, but it didn’t seem to have an effect on him. He pressed on. DANIEL STARR: “Now, putting the obligatory obesity comments aside - wait, do you folks down here yonder know what that means? Obligatory? Oh well, putting the intelligence - or lack of intelligence - jokes aside, I am sure that you are all ecstatic that I, a truly captivating performer, am here to make your night more interesting. I am sure you are thinking that I am here, on Evolve, the pre-show for the undeveloped rookies and slobs who are unable to cut it with the folks like me on the pre-show, to entertain you. However, this is not the case, I didn’t come here out of pity. I was sent here; in what is surely the single most offensive moment in my career, David Sinclair moved my time in the ring to the first segment of Evolve, because there wouldn’t be enough time on Revolt. So here I am, in the ‘Oklahoma’ of GIW shows, wasting all of my time explaining why I am here.” The crowd interrupts him with ‘GET OFF OUR SHOW’ chants, and he is taken aback by this, honestly offended and upset that the fans would have the audacity to boo him. DANIEL STARR: “I just wanted to say that I deserve more respect in this company. I ridded Galveston Island Wrestling of that pitiful runt, Paul Defias - and I am certainly the future of the company, although in actuality I should be the present. So tonight, on the REAL show, the one that matters, REVOLT, you are going to watch me steamroll the ‘undefeatable’, but defeated, Ryan Cavallari, and ‘blue chipper’ Jamie Hayden. As for this gross little abomination that GIW calls a show, I hope you enjoy it, because once I leave, it all will go downhill from there.” “Break” hits the speakers again, and as we fade into a promotional package about the impending wedding ceremony of Ryan Cavallari and Alexis Terry, Daniel Starr is ushered to the back and the ring is beginning to get set for the open battle royal match, the staple of Evolve. QUOTE Back into the ring we go with a ring filled with three different superstars; the returning Brody Harper, AJ Fairchild, and debuting Piotr Czarneki. RILEY STEVENS: “Three interesting stars in the ring tonight, as usual the Open Battle Royal always seems to bring out diversity.” JACOB O’CONNELLY: “That’s because I am the best talent scout in the business today.” The open battle royal match begins with a bang as Piotr Czarneki locks up with Brody Harper, right in the center of the ring. Czarneki is overwhelmed by the power of Harper, who promptly shoves him to the ground. AJ Fairchild, also a smaller competitor, is shoved to the ground by Brody Harper, who is starting this match out looking like the competitor to beat. Czarneki and Fairchild were confined to corners diagonal to each other with Harper splitting them in the middle; they exchanged a charge and simultaneously charged the massive Harper, Czarneki going low with a barrel roll and Fairchild going high with a spectacular drop kick that hits Harper right on the jaw! The big man falls to the floor and Czarneki and Fairchild team up to pull him up and lean him against the ropes, but Harper isn’t going to go out without a fight. He bashes his two opponent’s heads together sending them both rolling to the ground. He goes after Czarneki first, setting him up for a powerbomb that looks like it will dump Czarneki to the outside. He lifts him up, but Czarneki counters out of it, landing behind Harper. He pushed the big guy chest first into the ropes and lifts him up and over! Brody Harper is Eliminated! Czarneki takes a moment to reflect on his work, but it is a moment too long as Fairchild attempts to toss him over, but the nimble Pole lands on the apron and jabs AJ right in the temple with several elbows. With Fairchild dazed, he makes his way to the top rope by way of the apron and launches himself at his opponent with a perfectly executed flying cross body. Czarneki is up first followed quickly by AJ, armdrag! And another! AJ is disoriented now, and Czarneki sees his chance, The Red Tide Rising! He connects with his finishing move, a variation of the Rock Bottom! He picks up his opponent and tosses him out! AJ Fairchild is Eliminated! REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner…Piotr Czarneki!” These fans give a bit of mixed reactions some rooting for Brody others for AJ and then some for Piotr but there is no denying that he picked up the win tonight as he raises his arms in victory. JACOB O’CONNELLY: “Can I pick them or can I pick them?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Whatever.” Piotr walks around the ring holding up his arms in victory as AJ looks in from the outside nodding his head, holding his back in pain as we go backstage here on Evolve. WINNER: PIOTR CZARNEKI Back from a commercial break, we journey to the office of Jacob O’Connelly, the commissioner of Evolve, as well as talent scouting and development. He stands in front of his desk with the lumbering, muscular giant that he had just recently signed, Lokie, and was speaking timidly, trying to mask his fears of the man. JACOB O’CONNELLY: “Well, Mr. Lokie, uh, first of all I’d like to congratulate you on your victory in the open battle royal last week. You’re in good company, some of GIW’s greatest wrestlers have come out of that same exact match you know: [COLOR=#B8860B]ALEX CASTELLANOS:, Matt Kluge, Alioth Starre, Tony Miranda...” Without displaying hardly any emotion, Lokie cuts the man off. LOKIE: “Thank you.” There is an awkward pause as O’Connelly struggles to jump back into his pattern of speech. He struggles for words, all the while cautious of Lokie’s presence. JACOB O’CONNELLY: “Well, I suppose I will be frank with you Mr. Lokie, there is an immense amount of pressure being placed on me and my programs by David Sinclair. Each and every week, he expects new GIW legends to be pumped out of Evolve, and unfortunately we are on a bit of a cold streak, you see? Now, I have a proposition for you, Mr. Lokie, if you stick around GIW and become successful, not only will I make sure that you stay in the good graces of Mr. Sinclair but, Mr. Lokie, I could make it well worth your while.” O’Connelly picks up a briefcase that was sitting near his feet and opens it, revealing a large amount of money. Lokie looks down at the money, then back up at O’Connelly. LOKIE: “So you’re bribing me.” O’Connelly chokes on his breath, Lokie cracking his knuckles only hurts the situation. He fumbles around with the briefcase, finally closing it. JACOB O’CONNELLY: “No, no, Mr. Lokie, I am offering you an incentive to perform. They taught me at business school that employees perform best when offered... incentives.” It seems as if Lokie stands seven feet above O’Connelly’s head, based on his reactions anyway. Lokie is obviously holding himself back from lighting up O’Connelly. LOKIE: “Let me tell you something about me, Jacob, and this business. I am not an employee. I don’t file papers. I don’t pull out old fucking staples. I am a wrestler. You pay me to beat the hell out of people. I enjoy it. That is why I do this, and don’t work in an office. I don’t need an incentive. Now, if you just want a big guy to be your bodyguard, I’ll be damned if you are going to find one in me. I actually have potential, and I’m not going to waste in on a little weasel like you. I’m out of your jurisdiction now. Goodbye.” With O’Connelly trembling in his boots, and Lokie exiting the office, Evolve fades to black.
