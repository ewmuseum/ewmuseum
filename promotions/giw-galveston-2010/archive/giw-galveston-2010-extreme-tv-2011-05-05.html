GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: E X T R E M E T V! QUOTE Opener The recent success of Ninja Strike Force here on ETV continued as the duo featuring Jamal Mitchell and Colby Hunter looked great in a tag team against the less then impressive Justin Daniels and Drew Galloway. The duo that has garnered a big fan following on the Pre-Show, literally never allowed their opponents any offense. Instead the match was finished with a ‘Shell Shock’ a Double Superkick to Daniels for the pin. The fans gave them a big ovation and from Colt’s mouth he had announced that while Daniels and Galloway had been released, Ninja Strike Force would be moved up to the Main Card next week. WINNERS: NINJA STRIKE FORCE (Colby Hunter & Jamal Mitchell) QUOTE Main Event While the main event was listed as a Triple Threat the name fans would remember would be Marcus Hister. In the end he proved why he has garnered such descriptions as crazy, insane, and sadistic as he tore his opponents Mart Skies and Masked Warrior to pieces. It was their chance to overcome the big GIW name however they failed to even prove they were even fighters. With performances that would generally leave them fired, Hister instead disposed of them himself leaving both men laying in agony when the match was finished, as he covered Skies after a ‘Mystery's Darkness’ a RKO. He left the fans fearing for the other men in the end as he picked up another win to further cement his status here in GIW. WINNER: MARCUS HISTER
