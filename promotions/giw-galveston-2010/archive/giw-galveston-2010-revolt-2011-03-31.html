GIW — Card

Revolt, 31 March 2011

31 March 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: THURSDAY NIGHT REVOLT! LIVE FROM THE SEAWALL BOULEVARD ENTERTAIMENT COMPLEX = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! Coming into the place nicknamed simply ‘The Complex’ we find ourselves in the middle of perhaps the most packed house for a Revolt show ever. The place is filled to the brim with fans all here to see the night that has been dubbed the ‘Night of Champions’! A night where every belt will be on the line as we pan around the crowd wondering how they fit them all in, we finally end up in front of Riley Stevens and Oliver Thibodaux’s commentary table. RILEY STEVENS: “We are here again preparing for what is sure to be one of the biggest GIW shows in recent memory. As you may know tonight is ‘Night of Champions’ a night where every belt will be on the line. I’m not sure who set it up whether it was Blake Beckett or perhaps Eduardo Santiago, but regardless we have a big night in store for you all.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Better not have been that damn Mexican…” RILEY STEVENS: “OLIVER! Anyways, the show will start with Alioth Starre a man who’s risen to prominence here in GIW rather quickly. After becoming the GIW North Beach Champion at ‘Revelation’ he’ll make his first defense right away against Seth Seeker a man on a mission. Though it may not be Jak Hendricks, the thought of getting gold has to be intriguing for the big man. This is a guaranteed war, to start the show. The winner will be the champion.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Hopefully neither of them walks out on their own two feet.” RILEY STEVENS: “Thanks Oliver. While we are anchored by three title bouts, we will also have some big non title matches. One of them being a tag match featuring some new talent in GIW. Trying to make a name for themselves Floyd Mason teams with Noah Namith to face Mike Rosenberg and The Fallen. But that’s not all; the following contest is a match that will not be over shadowed. Former Number One Contender, Jak Hendricks will face Tony Miranda a man who has his eyes on the gold now as well.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Hopefully Seeker doesn’t think he can attack Jak here tonight.” RILEY STEVENS: “That’s certainly on everyone’s minds. In another title match the GIW Tag Team Champions, Aspen Chaud and the recently returned Justin Volt will compete against Brotherhood of Steel’s, Antonio Martins and Pedro Cabrita who earned this shot at ‘Revelation’. Will Justin be fully healed from his injury and could we possibly see Brotherhood of Steel walk out as champions, if it were up to these fans that’s what they’d choose.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Thankfully they don’t choose, otherwise it would all be rainbows and hand shakes, goodness.” RILEY STEVENS: “Ignoring that the main event is HUGE! Alioth Starre…yes you heard that right will fight for a second time in the Main Event fighting for the GIW Undisputed Championship alongside Trent Lost whom he tied with in last weeks GIW Shootout Match as our great champion Eric Sparrow defends his belt. This has to be the most danger his reign has been in since it started.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Hopefully it ends, can’t stand the guy.” RILEY STEVENS: “However I was told earlier today that we would start the show with a special message from one of our old friends, let’s send it to the titan-tron.” The tron lights up as the entire crowd look up to see who it is, to their surprise the face is that of Chris Jericho whom has a big smirk on his face as well as a suit. CHRIS JERICHO: “Hello, ladies and gentleman. I know that many of you parasites thought that back not too long ago when Ross Rydell attacked me, I would be out forever! Many of you thought I would leave your pathetic island and never come back.” The fans cheer the idea presented to them by Jericho, whom takes a breath in the video perhaps expecting this reaction. CHRIS JERICHO: “But…I’m afraid that’s not going to happen. You see my Doctor says I will be cleared to wrestle…SOON…very soon! And soon I will take back that company. I will become the GIW Undisputed Championship. But most importantly I’ll get revenge on the man who put me out, Ross Rydell. Because this injury will not hold me back, I AM STILL THE BEST IN THE WORLD AT WHAT I DO! And soon, I’ll be back to prove it.” Jericho finishes it as the tron slowly fades and the fans answer with boos, having perhaps forgotten about him as the camera heads back to the announce table. RILEY STEVENS: “Wow big news, Chris Jericho is close to a return!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Finally, we need that man back.” RILEY STEVENS: “Let’s send you to the back.” Riley happily smiles as Oliver seems genuinely excited about the Jericho news as we are taken backstage. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = When we get there we find ourselves zoomed up tight to the Owner’s face as Blake Beckett seems to have something on his mind as the door opens and the camera pans out to show La’Sarah Audric walking in. BLAKE BECKETT: “So? Is it for real?” Blake waits for the answer judging by La’Sarah’s less then enthusiastic look it’s not good news. LA’SARAH AUDRIC: “Unfortunately it apparently is. Apparently Fox Southwest thinks it’s a great idea and are fully on board with this Santiago guy.” Blake angrily kicks the chair beside him as La’Sarah seems to have expected such a reaction. BLAKE BECKETT: “I’m the damn Owner of this company not them. They think giving some guy who knows nothing about our audience the chance to do whatever he wants is a good idea? Come on!” Blake continues to pace around his Office as La’Sarah nods her head agreeing with mentor. LA’SARAH AUDRIC: “Apparently they are only keeping him around until ‘International X’ unless it’s a big success. Perhaps we should try and chat with him; maybe he’s not as bad a guy as we think.” Blake looks over at her angrily not even thinking for a second that she could be right about Eduardo. BLAKE BECKETT: “Where is he?” LA’SARAH AUDRIC: “Apparently he hasn’t arrived yet.” BLAKE BECKETT: “How professional!” Blake angrily shakes his head, sitting down in his chair as the segment comes to a close and we go to break. QUOTE As we head to the ring for the first match “Nightmare” by Avenged Sevenfold hits sending the fans into a ruckus as they cheer on “The Reaper” Seth Seeker as he makes his entrance grinning as he walks towards the ring looking as menacing as ever. Heading towards the ring with a grin he heads up the steps, slowly stepping into the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Seth Seeker earned this opportunity by defeating Mike Rosenberg at ‘Revelation’ we will see what he does with the chance here tonight.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “He’s a lunatic; he doesn’t deserve a damn thing.” Seeker steps into the ring walking to his corner as “Silly Boy Blue” by David Bowie now blares out of the speakers and the fans turn their attention to the stage as out from the back walks Alioth Starre holding his GIW North Beach Championship over his shoulder, strolling towards the ring to the fans cheers. RILEY STEVENS: “Here comes a man that will be very busy here tonight. Starting with this title defense before fighting in the Main Event for the GIW Undisputed Championship, could you imagine if he walked out with both titles?” Riley’s given little attention as Oliver gives no response as Alioth heads up the steps and then into the ring, walking to the corner of the ring holding up his belt then returning to hand it to the referee. While there doesn’t seem to be any huge beefs between the two, the title can bring out a lot as they both stare at it as the referee holds it up as he turns and hands it out and then leans over the ropes calling for the bell. DING DING DING! Not to date has there been a man that stacked up well against Seeker and as he comes forward at Alioth it’s clear that this is no different. Seeker goes to grab him but Alioth slips behind him delivering a few shots to his back before backing up to rethink his plans as Seth turns to him seeming unfazed by the punches. Alioth offers up for a lock up, seeming to have something up his sleeve. Seeker comes forward grabbing him but Alioth’s plan seems to back fire when Seeker easily pushes him to the mat using his brute strength. The fans are split on this one, some for Seeker and some for Starre and it’s hard to even notice the difference between boos and cheers as they have become fused together. Starre gets to his feet as Seth has a bit of a grin, as if to show Alioth that there’s no planning for a man his size. However he throws caution to the wind coming forward landing every kind of punch known to man, one after another. Seeker seeming to actually be feeling these pushes him away and explodes forward, he may be big but he’s still fast. Swinging for a Clothesline but Starre ducks it and gives him a leg kick to the back of the knee trying to chop the big man down. Seth turns around, using his speed to grab hold of Alioth. Starre is trapped between his gorilla like arms as he throws him for a ride hitting a Belly to Belly Suplex! The fans cheer the big move as Alioth lands hard. RILEY STEVENS: “There is nobody stronger then Seeker in Galveston.” Seth doesn’t waste his time going on the attack, grabbing Alioth by the ankle for an Ankle Lock. Nearly having it locked up, Starre spins to his back and kicks Seeker in the chest with his free leg. When that doesn’t break it, he kicks him in the face twice causing Seeker to stumble back a bit. Starre hops up quickly, giving him a big jumping punch. Seeker stumbles back into the ropes. Alioth takes a few steps back then comes at him, but Seeker ducks down and gives him a big back body drop over the ropes. Starre is able to land on the apron. When Seth turns to give him a punch Alioth ducks down and then hits a big up kick that causes Seeker to fall to the mat. Trying to hurry as fast as he can Alioth heads top rope, looking out at the crowd. But Seth is too quick, able to recover and jump up to meet him on the top rope. Not looking to be in the best position Alioth tries to elbow his way out. Seeker has a hold of him and seems ready to deliver something when Alioth uses all the strength in his legs to kick him off the top rope sending him to the mat hard as the fans cheer. Climbing to his feet Alioth goes for it, putting it all on the line for a Leg Drop. Seeker is just barely able to roll out of the way at the last second as Starre lands hard on his butt, flopping around to the other side holding his back as both men are down. RILEY STEVENS: “Both men are down, it may just be who gets up first that wins this one.” The referee checks on Alioth to see if he’s ok. He is up first but is using the ropes to aid him in his attempt. Seeker gets to his feet just as Starre turns around. He goes full board at him diving for a Spear that connects. Starre and Seeker both go back falling right into the referee! The referee is out cold, from the weight of both men. There is suddenly a murmur as sliding into the ring is Jak Hendricks wielding a Steel Chair. Trying to lift up the referee Seeker is distracted as Jak swings at his legs connecting hard to the back of his knee! The fans boo as Seth lets out a big groan of pain as he falls to the mat. Hendricks slides out of the ring bringing the chair with him. The ref, Alioth, and Seeker all start to get up. The referee shakes it off as Seeker limps a bit. Seth limps forward as Alioth could only think he tweaked it on the Spear. Seth throws a Clothesline that misses, Alioth waits until he turns around to dump him on the ground right into a Figure Four Leg Lock on the leg he hadn’t known Seth had just been hit in. Trying not to Seth is forced to tap out. RILEY STEVENS: “That’s a travesty! This was a great fight and Jak Hendricks just ruined it with that chair attack while the referee was out.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Classic!” Oliver has a good laugh as Alioth gets to his feet. The referee hands him his belt, as he holds it in the air proudly displaying just one of the belts he wants to walk out of here with tonight. RILEY STEVENS: “None the less Alioth Starre starts the night with a win, he’ll have just a bit of time to rest up before the huge main event here tonight.” The fans give mixed reactions as the referee helps Seeker to his feet, angrily he breaks free trying to walk off by himself but his leg lets out underneath him. From there we are sent to the backstage area. WINNER: ALIOTH STARRE (Still GIW North Beach Champion) We go to the backstage area where Ross Rydell is walking with a few lady friends on his arms, not in action tonight but looking to be having fun anyways. The girls are all over him as Sarah Sharp walks up to him with her microphone. SARAH SHARP: “Ross, may I have a moment?” Lowering his shades he has a smile on his face looking at both of his girls and then nodding his head. ROSS RYDELL: “Yeah just make it quick…Oh and trust me girls, that won’t happen tonight.” Ross chuckles as his friends laugh it off as Sarah who clearly is not fond of Ross angrily asks her question. SARAH SHARP: “How do you feel knowing Chris Jericho the man you wrongly took out is coming back soon?” Ross seems unfazed by the news as Sarah holds up the microphone to him. ROSS RYDELL: “Wrongly? You mean the guy that cost me a shot at becoming champion…Him? Yeah I say it was an eye for an eye. If he wants to come back after me, then so be it. Just means his return will only last…a New York…Minute.” Ross winks to the camera and walks off as Sarah shakes her head as we take a break. QUOTE As we return we find ourselves catching just the end of Noah Namith’s entrance as he steps in, slapping five with Mason before exiting the ring as we cut to the other side where Rosenberg and Fallen are together. RILEY STEVENS: “This match features four of our newer stars, all of which will be trying to catch Blake’s eye or perhaps even Eduardo’s.” Both sides are ready as The Fallen decides to start on his side and Noah opts to start for his as the bell rings and the match up starts. DING DING DING! Noah and Fallen start to circle around one another getting this started. Fallen goes in on the attack but Namith is able to duck under his attack pulling him right a Roll Up, 1…2…Kick Out! Fallen hops up angrily taking a swing as Noah ducks it and does the same 1…2…Kick Out! Noah now gets up backing away as The Fallen angrily slams his feet against the ground as he gets up to his feet. Showing a bit too much aggression, Fallen throws a reckless punch that misses and sets him right up for a Sleeper hold! The fans cheer as Noah tugs on it. Into the ring comes Mike kicking Noah in the gut. Floyd tries to get in but the referee stops him. Rosenberg looks for a cheap shot, but instead Noah lifts him up and Body Slams him. The referee turns around as Rosenberg rolls out of the ring holding his back. The distraction seems to have paid off as Fallen drops him with an Axe Handle from behind. The fans boo as Fallen goes to grab a leg, Noah kicks him back. He stumbles into his own corner where a just recovered Rosenberg tags in. He jumps the ropes and runs at Namith. To his surprise Noah gives him a big knee to the stomach then whips him into the corner. Running forward he hits a Corner Clothesline, dropping him to a sitting position. Noah tags into Floyd who jumps over the ropes and then delivers a Head Kick to the seated Rosenberg. A pumped up Mason, takes a few steps back getting the fans into it as Mike gets to his feet wondering where he is. RILEY STEVENS: “Mason and Namith have worked well as a team.” Rosenberg stumbles right into a punch from Floyd who then whips him off the ropes. On the rebound he lifts him right up for a Spinebuster! The fans are going wild as Fallen jumps in to try to take a cheap shot. He’s met half way by Noah who grabs him and hits the ‘Natural Selection’! The fans go wild as Fallen slides out of the ring out of it just as Mike gets up walking right into the ‘One Hitter Quitter’! The fans go wild as he makes the cover 1…2…3! That’s it already. RILEY STEVENS: “A short dominant performance by Noah Namith and Floyd Mason here tonight who have to have put their names firmly in the mix here in GIW.” Noah steps into the ring as the referee raises both men’s hands. Both men branch off heading to their own turnbuckle to celebrate, both getting their first win here in GIW as we go backstage. WINNERS: FLOYD MASON & NOAH NAMITH As we head to the back we find Seth Seeker getting iced up in the back as Dr. House stands by evaluating his knee from a few feet away. SETH SEEKER: “Come on, Doc you got to let me go. I need to get revenge on that little punk!” Dr. House has a look at it, getting down to look behind his knee as Seeker tries to move it. DR. HOUSE: “Not a chance, you need to rest it. I have to run a few scans so just calm yourself and I’ll get you out of here as soon as I can.” Dr. House takes his clip board and heads out of the room as Seeker turns his attention to the cameraman. SETH SEEKER: “Get the hell out of here.” Seeker pushes the camera out of the room as he realizes revenge will not be had here tonight as we go to break. QUOTE We head back to the ring for the highly anticipated contest between Jak Hendricks and Tony Miranda. “Forever” by Drake, Kanye West, Lil Wayne, and Eminem hits as the fans unleash their boos. Out from the back comes Jak Hendricks the former Number One Contender, seeming to have not lost much confidence after the loss at ‘Revelation’ as he strolls out. RILEY STEVENS: “I’d like to say this is the first time we’ve seen Jak today, but I’d be lying. After his despicable actions earlier tonight I can’t help but root for karma in this one.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Shut your mouth, Seeker had it coming.” Jak strolls by the fans with a big smirk on his face as they boo him the entire time. Walking up the steps he walks along the side of the ring, stopping to look out at them before popping into the ring as “The Human Stain" by Kamelot hits sending the fans back into cheers as Tony Miranda heads straight for the ring looking all business. RILEY STEVENS: “One has to wonder if Tony is a hundred percent after the attack by High Voltage on him last week.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Already making excuses for him, figures.” Tony doesn’t seem overly hurt, or at least he’s hiding it. He slides into the ring wanting to fight as the referee stands between them before he calls for a quick bell to start this one. DING DING DING! The fans know whose side they are on as the two meet up in the center of the ring. To everyone’s surprise Jak holds out a hand for a shake. Tony seems a little hesitant as he offers his hand out. To nobodies surprise Jak uses that same hand to slap Miranda right across the face. The fans boo as Jak throws out his arms, showing off a bit. An angry Tony throws a big haymaker but in his fury Jak is able to duck it. When Tony spins around he receives a second slap, that sounded even bigger then the first. The crowd almost seems to realize what a mistake it was as Tony this time comes forward with a big Clothesline. Jak quickly bounces up only to receive a second one. This time Hendricks is a bit slower to his feet and he receives a big Spinning Roundhouse Kick that floors him! The fans cheer as Tony makes the cover 1…2…Kick Out! It’s not enough to put away the former Number One Contender as both men get to their feet. Tony is up first feeling fresh as he grabs Jak and whips him into the corner. Going full blast he attempts a big boot in the corner but nobody is home as Jak stumbles out of the way. Miranda holds his groin, his foot really getting up there as he walks back into a roll up, 1…Kick Out! Not even close as Tony hops up to his feet. Hendricks meets him there with a knee to the stomach as he brings him to the ropes. Tossing him off the ropes he comes back right into a huge Spinebuster, the fans seem to be a bit caught off guard with this offense from Jak as he makes another cover 1…2…Kick Out! Jak pounds the mat as he gets up a little annoyed that didn’t finish him as Miranda starts to get to his feet. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “How’s this for karma Riley?” The fans boo as Jak gets behind him stalking him as Tony uses the ropes to get up favoring his back. When he turns around Hendricks unleashes a ‘Jak Attack’ a flurry of punches and kicks, from all over the place. But to his surprise and to even the fans, Tony only seems to power up. Jak stops thinking he’s done enough but Tony seems fine as flies forward with a Discus Clothesline! The fans go insane, realizing this might just be the end. Running off the ropes he times Jak getting up perfectly as he connects with a Flying Side Kick! Instead of going for the cover Tony has other plans. Walking to the outside of the ring, he steps out onto the apron preparing to go spring board. Suddenly out from the back comes Aspen Chaud and Marcus Hister. Tony looks out at them and suddenly jumps that way double clotheslining both of them! The fans go wild as he grins looking down at them and saying a few words that can’t be heard. Marcus and Aspen roll around apparently not expecting that as then turns around to head back into the ring. The referee is looking to the outside and as he’s getting in Jak low blows him and immediately rolls him up, and as if that wasn’t enough he holds his legs up onto the ropes 1…2…3! RILEY STEVENS: “Another match ended in controversy here tonight!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “It’s Tony’s fault, he took his eyes off the match.” RILEY STEVENS: “It’s not like Aspen and Marcus were coming to just watch his match.” Jak immediately rolls out of the ring not wanting to stay around to long. He walks by Aspen and Marcus taunting the fans as Aspen and Marcus get up, both realizing that it worked in the end as they back up the ramp, with Aspen laughing a bit. In the ring Tony gets up holding his lower regions as he tries to tell the referee what happened, before patting him on the back not wanting to blame him as he turns to the real culprits Aspen and Marcus as they head to the back and we go to a commercial. WINNER: JAK HENDRICKS After the commercial break we head to the backstage area where Tony Miranda has just gotten backstage, as he walks back to the back he meets Eric Sparrow who is holding his belt over his shoulder preparing for his match. ERIC SPARROW: “Hey man, just saw what happened. If I’d have seen it in time I’d have tried to stop it. But see I think we need to do something about this High Voltage problem. I know you want this belt and all; it would be a great match between us. But let’s face it none of us are going to beat four on one odds, perhaps it’s in our best interest to have each other’s backs.” Sparrow presents the offer with sincerity as a still angry Tony seems to think about the offer for a bit. TONY MIRANDA: “I’ll have your back.” Sparrow nods his head as the two shake hands as Tony walks by heading to his locker room as Eric returns to stretching as the segment ends. QUOTE Heading to the ring again we find ourselves getting prepared for the second title match of the night one that will be for the GIW Tag Team Championships. “Walla Walla” by The Offspring hits as the fans go wild for two fourths of the team. Out come Antonio Martins and Pedro Cabrita seeming confident despite many thinking they are underdogs. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Who in the world are these guys?” RILEY STEVENS: “Oh come on Oliver, you know just who they are. Two members of ‘Brotherhood of Steel’ and the two members who have really come in strong here in GIW.” Pedro and Antonio high five fans all the way down towards the ring before sliding into it and heading to the top rope, they are cut off mid-celebration as "To Be Loved" by Papa Roach hits sending the fans into perhaps the loudest boos of the night. Walking out from the back is Aspen Chaud with her belt around her waist as Justin Volt follows not far behind her with his. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Here are the great champions! Justin Volt and Aspen Chaud!” Justin and Aspen walk down towards the ring, Aspen stopping to taunt a few members in the crowd as she heads up the steps with Justin behind her, she holds the ropes for him as he steps in as he holds the middle rope up for her as well. They step into the holding up their belts, before handing them off seeming supremely confident as the referee shows them, then hands them out of the ring and calls for the bell. DING DING DING! For both sides it is decided that Justin Volt will start for High Voltage as Antonio has decided to start for Brotherhood of Steel. The two men start to circle one another as they go in for a lock up. The fans cheer as Antonio starts to back him up into the corner. Hitting him with a big kick to the mid-section, as the referee tells him to back it out of the corner. Listening to him he backs away as Justin talks a bit of trash as he walks out, receiving a big Dropkick! The fans cheer as Tony goes for a cover 1…Kick Out! Aspen cheers Justin on as he gets to his feet. But to everyone’s surprise he gets up receive a big Enzuigiri! The fans continue to cheer, all for the underdogs here tonight. Aspen screams out “He’s fine!” though judging by Justin’s slower stand up perhaps he is not. When he gets up he’s sent flying into the corner, where Tony runs forward giving him a big kick to the gut in the corner. Tagging in Pedro, who goes top rope. Volt stumbles forward, where he receives a Spinning Neckbreaker from Pedro off the top rope! The fans go wild as Pedro goes for a cover 1…2…Aspen kicks him off. The fans boo as Aspen, quickly heads to her corner when Antonio threatens to go after her. Suddenly there is a Aspen Chaud Sucks started in the crowd which causes Aspen to turn around pretending to eat it up. Unknown to her behind her Pedro has just tagged in Antonio as Justin is getting up, together they jump up and hit the ‘Steel Wings’ a Double RKO! The fans are going wild, which Aspen thinks is for her as she bows and Antonio makes the cover 1…2…3! RILEY STEVENS: “Oh My God! New Champs, New Champs!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “…” The fans break out into a frenzy as the referee hands them the belts as Antonio and Pedro immediately roll out of the ring with them, as the fans embrace them at the side of the ring where both men lean in, holding their belts. RILEY STEVENS: “You remember you asked who they were? They are the new GIW Tag Team Champions!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “...” Aspen Chaud who has just realized what happened jumps into the ring to check on Justin in the center of the ring, seeming to be as shocked as everyone. Antonio and Pedro walk up the ramp, proudly displaying their belts as the fans continue to go insane as we head to break. WINNERS: BROTHERHOOD OF STEEL [Antonio Martins & Pedro Cabrita] (New GIW Tag Team Champions) When we return from the break we find ourselves in the locker room of High Voltage where Aspen Chaud is nearly in tears seeming to be devastated while Justin is icing the back of his head and Trent Lost and Marcus Hister are standing by. ASPEN CHAUD: “How could this day be any worse?! We lost the belts and Tony hit me!” Justin cranks his neck looking at her like she is crazy quickly snapping back at her. JUSTIN VOLT: “He hit you? I JUST GOT DOUBLE TEAMED! Where were you?” Justin gets to his feet looking down at her as she tries to come up with an answer. ASPEN CHAUD: “I’m…I’m sorry I got distracted.” JUSTIN VOLT: “Distracted? During a title match? I’m leaving, see you at the Hotel.” Justin heads out the door, as all three of them watch them go as Marcus speaks up. MARCUS HISTER: “I told you guys not to underestimate them.” His words only seem to build the tension in the room as Trent Lost sits down next to Aspen. ASPEN CHAUD: “The titles mean power…We need them.” Trent pats her on the back, trying to comfort The Queen. TRENT LOST: “Don’t worry; I’ll make sure we don’t go home with out any gold tonight.” Trent’s confidence seems to make Aspen a little happier as she nods her head as he gets up and heads to the ring as we take a small break before the main event. QUOTE After a long night of twists and turns here on ‘Night of Champions’ the fans know that it’s time for the Main Event which means that the GIW Undisputed Championship is on the line. “Silly Boy Blue” by David Bowie hits for a second time sending the fans into some loud cheers as Alioth Starre walks out from the back still sporting his GIW North Beach Championship over his shoulder. RILEY STEVENS: “Alioth Starre has exploded onto the scene here in GIW, with a big undefeated streak going. He defeated The Fallen and Ross Rydell at ‘Revelation’ to become the GIW North Beach Championship and defended that belt earlier tonight against Seth Seeker, one has to wonder if he might be tired or if a warm up might just help him.” If he’s tired Starre isn’t willing to show it as he slides into the ring patting his belt and showing where he’d put the other one if he got it as “Down” by Curse Icon hits and the fans begin to boo as walking out from the back is Trent Lost with a big smirk, hoping to turn High Voltage’s bad night into a good one with a win here. RILEY STEVENS: “Trent Lost apparently has confidence he’ll walk out the champion here, as he’s just told Aspen Chaud he would walk out with the gold. He’s been impressive in his GIW career but has been known to bring it one week and then be lack luster the next how will he fair in his title match.” Walking to the ring you wouldn’t know he wasn’t confident as he steps into the ring with a big smirk on his face, looking across at Alioth who glares at him as finally “Circles” by Incubus hits and the fans go insane for the champion Eric Sparrow who walks out with his gold around his waist as he heads to the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Finally we have the champion, a man who has looked supremely dominant in his reign thus far, but I think we all realize this is the biggest jeopardy his belt has ever been in.” Sparrow high fives a few fans, just a few of the many cheering him on as he hops up the steps then through the ropes looking at his challengers as he hands his belt over. The referee raises the belt high into the air for all to see, before handing it out as the referee calls for the bell. DING DING DING! The three men are all in their own little corner as Trent Lost steps up between them with a big smirk on his face. Sparrow looks to Starre as Starre looks back to Sparrow both of them nodding as they collapse on Trent in the center of the ring and start to beat him down. The fans go wild as they deliver a few punches before he bounces out to Alioth who nails him with a punch. Stumbling the other way he walks right into a punch from Sparrow again he goes the other way as he’s hit by Starre again almost like a human ping pong. Finally he walks back to Sparrow who gives him a Superkick that sends him stumbling back to Starre who takes him and tosses him out of the ring, the fans cheering one last time as he hits the outside hard. Alioth turns around to Eric and the two realize it’s time to get going. Similar in appearance they come together for a lock up but it’s Eric Sparrow who carries some more meat on his bones as he starts to back Starre up to the ropes which he uses to propel Starre off of. On the rebound Sparrow goes for a big punch but Starre ducks it. He uses the ropes now to shoot off now at Sparrow but as he swings for a Clothesline, Eric ducks under it. Finally they end up back at square one as Alioth puts on the breaks and turns around as they receive some claps from the crowd for their effort. Regardless using his strength Sparrow goes back in for the kill, locking up with Starre again only this time he’s turned right into an impressive Wrist Lock that Alioth holds tight, a move that would make the past legends proud. Holding it tightly until Sparrow gets to the ropes, as Starre quickly breaks the hold. RILEY STEVENS: “Thus far, I can’t really tell who is getting the advantage is it Sparrow or Starre?” Coming back together, Alioth goes for an attack but walks right into a big kick to the stomach by Sparrow. Quickly he whips him into the corner where he blasts forward hitting his always exciting ‘Pineapple Express’ the Corner Dropkick! The fans cheer as Sparrow slips from there to the outside apron as Alioth gets up holding his gut after having the wind knocked out of him. Not noticing Sparrow who jumps up to go springboard only to be grabbed by Trent and dropped right down head first on the ring apron. The fans boo as Eric falls to the mat and heading back into the ring is Trent, looking to capitalize on a winded Alioth Starre. Running forward he lands a ‘Trent Express Mail’ a Running Leg Lariat that sends Starre to the mat hard. He makes the cover hoping to finish it, 1…2…Kick Out! The fans let out a sigh of relief as Starre gets to his feet. Trent is in the wait as he just prepares for him to get to his feet. Taking a big swing at him, he misses as Alioth seems to have recovered enough to fight back as he lands a few rapid fire punches before catching him with one big kick that stuns him. He spins around falling right into Alioth’s trap, as he locks in Reverse Chin Lock, bringing Goosebumps to the fans who know this might be it. RILEY STEVENS: “Could this be it, Alioth has it locked it!” Starre seems to have it in tight when Sparrow returns to the ring, wasting little time before dropkicking Alioth which breaks the holds. Trent rolls out of the way out of it as Sparrow seems to realize what a predicament that was. Alioth gets to his feet and the two trade shots, back and forth. Out of no where, Alioth drops him looking for the Figure Four Leg Lock! The crowd goes nuts as he tries to twist it around only for Sparrow to slip out his leg and rush to his feet. Now Starre rushes in right into a ‘Purple Haze’ Guillotine Choke but before he can get it all the way locked, Starre manages to pop his head free as both men get to their feet. Trent has now regained his composure as he attempts to throw Sparrow over the top rope, only for Eric to land on the outside using the ropes to skin the cat. When Trent turns around he’s dropped right into a Figure Four Leg Lock by Alioth as the fans go wild! Starre spins and falls to the mat with it locked in as Trent struggles not to tap. But as the camera pans out we see Sparrow going top rope, jumping off with the ‘Spinal Tap’ a Spiral Tap right on top of Alioth Starre! The fans go insane as the force causes Sparrow to bounce off; Starre releases the holds and rolls out of the way. Trent starts to get up, with a bit of a limp due to the figure four. Sparrow runs forward and dropkicks Starre out of the ring, then just as Trent is getting up he catches him with a Snap DDT that sets him right up into the ‘Purple Haze’! On the outside Alioth gets up, trying to return to the ring but it’s too late as Trent taps out. RILEY STEVENS: “Eric Sparrow is still the champion, he just can’t be stopped. But one has to wonder if the Figure Four may not have softened Trent up for Eric.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Of course it did, this was a two on one handicap match for Trent, not fair at all!” WINNER: ERIC SPARROW (Still GIW Undisputed Champion) The fans cheer as Eric Sparrow pulls off the submission getting to his feet as he’s handed his belt. Alioth is in the ring just standing up, rubbing his hands through his hair as he seems to realize that the title just slipped through his grasp. None the less Sparrow holds out a hand to shake as Alioth still angry seems to be about to do so, when suddenly they are blindsided by an attack. Into the ring are both Aspen Chaud and Marcus Hister taking both men down to the ground as the fans boo loudly for the most hated group in GIW. RILEY STEVENS: “Figures this would happen, High Voltage is looking to take their bad night out on others.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “They are just showing Alioth and Eric what numbers feels like, since that’s what they just did to Trent.” Sparrow and Starre start to fight back but a recovered Trent forces the numbers their way again. Suddenly the crowd roars as flying down to the ring is Tony Miranda who slides into the ring like a man possessed. RILEY STEVENS: “The cavalry has arrived!” When Tony arrives it causes three one on one fights between Aspen Chaud and Eric Sparrow, Tony Miranda and Trent Lost, as well as Marcus Hister and Alioth Starre. Finally Aspen, Lost, and Hister tuck their tails and head for the hills sliding out of the ring. All three of them back up the ramp as the fans cheer on Sparrow, Starre, and Miranda in the ring as suddenly a loud voice booms over the crowd. EDUARDO SANTIAGO: “Hello everyone, it is I Eduardo Santiago! This fighting between all of you has gone far enough; in my country we settle things like this in the ring. So, next week on Revolt it’ll be; Alioth Starre versus Marcus Hister, Tony Miranda versus Trent Lost, and Aspen Chaud versus Eric Sparrow in three separate one on one matches!” The fans go wild as the three men in the ring all seem happy with that idea as Aspen, Trent, and Marcus scream up at Eduardo who has only seemingly caused the fans to like him more. EDUARDO SANTIAGO: “Adios!” Eduardo heads to the back with a grin as Aspen Chaud starts to throw a tantrum as Starre, Sparrow, and Miranda talk some trash as the show slowly fades away.
