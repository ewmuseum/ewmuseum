GIW — Card

Revolt, 1 March 2012

1 March 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: THURSDAY NIGHT REVOLT LIVE! From Lakefront Arena in New Orleans, LA – March 1, 2012 BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! Cue the cameras and Revolt is live from the Lakefront Arena in New Orleans. There is no fancy opening video package or pyrotechnics; the camera simply pans the arena of a couple thousand fans that are on their feet ready for some Galveston Island Wrestling action. The camera finally focuses on the ring, which is covered with a red carpet and in the middle is a metal trashcan. “Kiss My Lips” By Dev abruptly kicks in and the hot crowd erupts in a thunderous boo. Alexis steps out onto the stage in a classy looking red dress with the GIW Legacy Championship around her waist. Suddenly. As Alexis makes her way down to the ring giving an arrogant “Princess” like wave, confetti falls from the ceiling which only angers the crowd more. The GIW fans are not very thrilled with the over-the-top celebration and let Alexis hear it, as her theme music is hardly audible with the amount of heat the fans give her. She enters the ring via the bottom rope and is quickly handed a microphone. Alexis paces around the ring with a smirk on her face, taking in the scene as she swings the microphone around in her hand. Her music finally fades and is quickly replaced with a “You Suck” chant that echoes throughout the arena. ALEXIS TERRY: “Why doesn’t this surprise me? Alexis Terry, the QUEEN OF GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING, not only steals the show at Retaliation but also walks out still your Legacy Champion, yet you bloody ingrates still boo me. Have you no respect? Have you no decency? I’m the new face of this organization and you fools will respect me! Now bow to your queen and beg me to forgive you!” The fans rebutted with more boos which only angered the self-proclaimed, “queen”. Alexis continued pacing only this time she shook her head in disgust. Her face was a deep red and the camera zoomed closely to garner the full effect. ALEXIS TERRY: “You people will rue the day you crossed Alexis Terry just like Beck Ramsey, Marcus Hister, Faith Simpson, Seth Ramsey, and that tramp Cassidy Hightower do. As much as you fans might think you actually matter, this isn’t about you tonight. No, you see, this is about me; it’s always about me. I’ve said it many times in the past but it never gets old saying it. Alexis Terry is the greatest professional wrestler in the world. “The Queen” and “England’s most glamorous export” aren’t just nicknames, I earned them by beating people like Beck Ramsey, Faith Simpson, and Marcus Hister. I’m unstoppable. I just can’t be beaten. It’s not me being arrogant people, its just fact.” Alexis lowers the microphone and stops directly behind the garbage can that is centered in the ring. Alexis slowly begins to unhook the Legacy Championship and then holds it in front of her as she continues to speak. ALEXIS TERRY: “Just like in the past, tonight I’m going to make history again. Ever since I’ve won the Legacy Championship, I’ve made this little belt relevant. Actually, I’ve made it more than relevant; I’ve made it the most important Championship in all of Galveston Island Wrestling. People pay good money to see if anybody can beat me for this precious Championship, sadly, your all to stupid to understand that it’s never going to happen. I just have one little problem. This thing is SOOO ugly! I mean, really hideous. I can’t take being seen with this piece of trash anymore. What’s that saying you Americans use? Out with the old, in with the new?” Alexis opens the small metal trashcan and without warning drops the Legacy Championship inside. The crowd boos loudly at the disrespect Alexis shows towards the Championship. Suddenly, the lights dim and a spotlight highlights Alexis. She looks up and smirks. Descending slowly from the ceiling is a small black covering in the form of what looks like a wrestling championship belt. The item finally holts in front of Alexis and she yanks the covering off. ALEXIS TERRY: “LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, THE BRAND NEW GLAMOROUS LEGACY CHAMPIONSHIP!” Hanging on the hook is a gold plated, white leather, and diamond-covered belt. A red royal crown is also visible near the top of the belt, symbolizing Alexis’ “queen” moniker. Alexis placed the new Championship over her shoulder and smiles from ear to ear as the crowd boos once again. The crowd is really hating on Alexis tonight, which Alexis does her best to ignore. ALEXIS TERRY: “This is what a real Championship looks like! One that is full of class and truly glamorous, one that only is suited for a royalty! You people may hate my bloody guts but you better get used to seeing me. I will hold this title for years to come. I will continue to not only be the face of this company, but the Queen is needs! There isn’t a person in the back who can take this from me. I will continue to win, and continue to make history. It’s only a matter of time until, I love you Aspen and don’t take this the wrong way babe, I become the first ever duel Legacy and Undisputed Champion of the world!” The fans boo the thought of the idea as Alexis stands there with her belt for a few moments longer before "Two Sides of Me" by Hinder hits and the fans tune quickly changes as Beck Ramsey heads out from the back as Alexis looks up the ramp at him with a glare as he heads to the ring in a hurry. RILEY STEVENS: “There has been a lot of talk over the past few weeks over these two stars draw at ‘Retaliation’, and whether or not Beck will get his rematch.” Beck heads up into the ring looking across the ring at Alexis and her new belt looking unenthusiastic to see her as she seems to be about the same as he wields a microphone himself. BECK RAMSEY: “Let me save you fans from having to hear another minute of this crap. You are really going to come out here with confetti…a new belt…after a Draw? You didn’t beat me Alexis we simply didn’t finish the match so I don’t know why you are putting me in a list of people that you beat when we all know you never did. But you know what? It’ll only make it sweeter beating you when I take your newly designed belt.” Beck points to it on her shoulder as she looks down at it and then snaps back with her witty little remark as per usual. ALEXIS TERRY: “Beck you had your chance you blew it! I am the champion and now you are heading to the back of the line…I mean isn’t your contract up this week? You won’t even be here next week.” The fans look on confused about this as Alexis smirks feeling as though she just one upped him however a grin forms on Beck’s face that seems to indicate he isn’t too worried about it. BECK RAMSEY: “It’s funny you would bring that up actually. You know I wasn’t exactly on the greatest terms with Sinclair but he is a man of his word when he says he cares about making the biggest matches for GIW and that’s why when I went to him he made me some big offers to keep me around. Starting with putting me on the poster for the upcoming ‘Exile’ Supershow…Upping my wages…And guaranteeing me a rematch with you!” Beck says this with a huge amount of eagerness in his voice as the crowd goes crazy but before Alexis can respond “Bitches” by Hollywood Undead hits and the crowd begins to boo as now it’s Nathan Bailey IV walking out confidently as both Alexis and Beck look up at him as he heads down to the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Making this more interesting by the minute Nathan is the man that won a contenders match for the Legacy belt at ‘Retaliation’!” Riley states what is on everybody’s mind as Bailey now heads up the steps and into the ring with a microphone of his own talking before anyone else can say anything. NATHAN BAILEY IV: “Listen, this whole thing can be settled whenever. I don’t mind who I face because at the end of the day I’ll beat either of you one on one. But I don’t like having to wait to find out who I am beating so why don’t you two get it over with so you can fight over the honor of losing your belt to me…” Bailey smugly grins as he lowers his microphone and yet again before Alexis and Beck can make a rebuttal "Midnight Romance" by A Kiss Could Be Deadly hits and the fans yet again go wild as Cassidy Hightower heads out from the back only seeming to be slightly more serious then usual as she to heads down to the ring. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Who invited her?” Cassidy heads up the ramp and Alexis mood has gone to full on anger now as she looks back at Cassidy who enters the ring as Bailey heads across the ring with Cassidy standing next to Beck as she looks around the ring. CASSIDY HIGHTOWER: "Frankly Alexis this whole celebration is ridiculous if there is anything that I have learned from this business is that a champion is someone that can defend their belt and doesn’t celebrate a draw like you are. Frankly I am fed up with you and Aspen and I want to get rid of you and the funny thing is that Beck and I have that chance tonight." Cassidy lowers the microphone and now things start to get heated as Cassidy and Alexis start to bicker without the mic as Beck steps in between them with Bailey watching from afar rolling his eyes until the crowd hears the sound of someone clearing out their throat on the stage as they look up to see David Sinclair standing at the top of the ramp with a mic of his own. DAVID SINCLAIR: “Calm down, save it for the match tonight. I have often said I make the biggest matches possible that’s what made ‘Retaliation’ the best event in GIW history. However ‘Exile’ will have the largest attendance for a GIW show ever and that’s why I am planning on making it huge and that new belt of yours Alexis…Will be on the line.” The crowd cheers before they even know who she is facing as Alexis looks up at him screaming something at him but he does not stop. DAVID SINCLAIR: “You see your belt is going to hang above the ring again because at ‘Exile’ you will defend the belt against…Beck Ramsey…” The crowd goes wild as Beck grins at her in the ring as he looks over at Alexis as she glares back. DAVID SINCLAIR: “Nathan Bailey...” It’s now Bailey’s turn to gloat as he smirks as well as he looks across the ring at Alexis and Beck who eye him now as well. DAVID SINCLAIR: “And Cassidy Hightower…” Cassidy seems to be the most surprised of the bunch as Alexis’s jaw drops; she was after all the only one she didn’t see coming as well. DAVID SINCLAIR: “In a Tables Ladders and Chairs match!” The crowd goes insane now as all four stars in the ring react in separate ways Beck with a smile, Alexis with a loud scream, Cassidy with intrigue, and Bailey with a confident calmness as Sinclair heads to the back and Bailey is the first to exit the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “What a HUGE match! Four stars will enter but only one will take home the belt, who will it be?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “This is a travesty, Alexis deserves better!” It’s now Beck and Cassidy who head out of the ring as Alexis is left in the ring with a scowl across at her face with her new belt over her shoulder as the crowd continues to react all the way to commercial. QUOTE As we return from break we hear the tune of "Realist Alive" by Clams Casino as the fans give a mixed reaction for a guy whom they have not made their mind up about in Evan Clarke who strolls out from the back heading towards the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Evan Clarke won the Open Battle Royal at ‘Retaliation’ and now looks to show his worth with a win over Alex Castellanos.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Looks like one of them emo queers…” Riley can only sigh as Evan heads up the steps into the ring looking out at the crowd before stepping through the ropes, he had made his name known at “Retaliation’ but he was in for a whole new world tonight. “The House of the Rising Sun” by The Animals hits and the fans cheer on Alex Castellanos who heads out from the back looking completely focused after his loss. RILEY STEVENS: “Alex had the win at ‘Retaliation’ but the referee didn’t see Aspen tap so now he is trying to battle his way back up through the rankings.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “He’s done! Aspen finished him.” Alex heads down right past the crowd seeming to just want to get this match started as he enters the ring and the referee calls for the bell. DING DING DING! The fans are ready to go behind Alex tonight as he and Evan start to circle around one another as they prepare for the match finally going in for a lock up. It’s Alex the bigger man that backs up Evan against the ropes before throwing him off of them, on the rebound Alex shoulder blocks him to the mat taking advantage of his size advantage as he goes to bounce off the ropes Evan twists over onto his belly sending Alex jumping over him and then hitting the ropes again this time off the rebound Clarke gets the better of him with a big shoulder toss to the center of the ring. Castellanos hops right back up only to be hit with a Right Roundhouse Kick that knocks him off balance setting him up for a quick Running Bulldog that puts him down early on as the fans boo, they don’t seem to hate Clarke but they are firmly behind Alex as Clarke gets to his feet respecting Alex enough to know that a pin now wouldn’t do him much good. RILEY STEVENS: “Clarke looking good early on.” Clarke waits for him to get up and when he does he immediately goes for an Enzuigiri but it proves to be ill fated as Alex ducks under it sending Clarke spinning to the mat. He gets up quickly however Alex grabs him and spins him around kicking him straight in the gut. Clarke doubles over and Castellanos takes the chance to hook him up and nail him with an impressive Snap Suplex but he as well does not go for the pin instead getting right up to his feet, stalking Evan from behind waiting to inflict some more damage as Clarke gets up favoring his back however Alex known for his Suplex’s simply grabs him from behind hitting him with a Delayed Back Suplex to his already hurt back, jumping on top of him for the pin 1…2-Kick Out. Clarke kicks out however Alex has picked a target, that being Evan’s back as Alex gets up not seeming to be too worried as Clarke takes more time to get up now showing some signs of pain. RILEY STEVENS: “Alex has really turned things around here.” Alex the whole night just seeming focused on the match as Clarke gets all the way up to his feet and turns around right into another boot to the gut by Alex who looks to make it three different kind of Suplex’s in a row but before he can hoist him up Evan hooks the leg and then uses a barrage of short elbows to his face knocking him back as he then jumps up into Alex locking him up in a Guillotine Choke with Alex still standing as the fans seem just as shocked as everyone as he seems to have it locked in well as Castellanos tries to fight his way out, instead he realizes he is close enough to the ropes to walk towards them and grab them. Quickly Evan drops his feet down and releases the hold as he lands a huge Running Knee that drops Alex to a knee as he jumps back up to the top rope ready to jump off as Alex gets to his feet and boom Clarke comes off with a Missile Dropkick that lands perfectly. RILEY STEVENS: “Huge move there by Clarke!” The fans even seem to be impressed by him as Evan crawls over to Alex and hooks his leg going for the pin 1…2-Kick Out! These fans now go back to cheering Castellanos as Evan gets up keeping himself composed as he gets up plotting his next move as Alex gets up as well both men starting to show a little fatigue as Evan takes a breather while Alex gets up and then he goes back on the attack hitting him with a big punch that gives him enough time to go for a Snap Suplex however Alex brings a knee up into him and ends whipping him off the ropes bouncing right back into a huge Double A Spinebuster as the fans go wild with cheers, as Alex goes for another pin 1…2-Kick Out. They groan then seem confused when Matt Kluge walks out onto the ramp heading towards the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Matt Kluge? His match isn’t until next.” Alex gets up breathing heavily as he looks outside the ring to see Kluge a little confused by his arrival as he raises his arm for a reason but this distraction gives Evan the time to get up to his feet, when Kluge gives him no response Alex turns around right into a Exploder Suplex! The fans boo as Alex drops to the mat in pain and immediately Clarke heads to the top rope, where the fans get up knowing hit or miss this match was over as Evan took ‘The Leap’ a Diving Headbutt that connects as the fans know it’s over as he hooks the leg 1…2…3! JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…Evan Clarke!” The fans boo the result wanting to see Evan win as he gets up to his feet with the referee raising his hand as Clarke gets yet another win here in GIW this time against the former number one contender as he quickly exits the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Impressive showing by Evan Clarke who picks up the win…Wait Matt Kluge is getting in the ring?” Just as he says Kluge slides his way into the ring as Alex gets to one knee, holding his head as Matt walks up to him looking out at the crowd and then just like that rushing at him with ‘The Matt Cooke’ a Running Knee to Alex’s knee that sends Castellanos limping he tries to fight back only ends up walking right into ‘The Tiger's Roar Gold Edition’ his version of the Whale Hunt the crowd going crazy with boos. RILEY STEVENS: “What the hell is this all about?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Finally! He’s being the guy I knew he really was.” Alex falls to the mat out of it as Kluge looks down at him, walking over him and out of the ring heading to the back being booed the entire time. WINNER: EVAN CLARKE After the first match we see Alexis Terry storming her way through the halls fresh off the announcement of her match at ‘Exile’ which seems to not be sitting well at all with her as she stops in front of a worker who is walking the halls. ALEXIS TERRY: “Have you seen Aspen?” In a bad mood she seems to be forcing it out but the staff member shakes his head no as she continues down the hall until she gets to Aspen’s locker room barging right on in only to find Aspen and Nathan Bailey locking lips. ALEXIS TERRY: “…” Alexis turns and heads on her way out shocked as Bailey and Chaud stop for a moment as she goes to catch up with but by then Alexis is already gone as the plot thickens. ==== From there we head to the interview area backstage where Pagonis is standing by with a focused Kluge. ZACHARY PAGONIS: “I’m here with Matt Kluge; can you explain your reasoning for the vicious attack on Alex Castellanos?” Pagonis holds the microphone over to Kluge as he looks at him grinning as talks into the mic. MATT KLUGE: “My reasoning? You really need to ask me for my reasoning? Isn’t it obvious Zach? How long ago did that little St. David’s Day celebrating bitch break up with the only decent interviewer here? Yet he still brings it up every chance he gets. What else oh remember Absolution? Of course you don’t, long story short he joins up with Aspen and then tries to blame her for his problems? Listen here Alex, I beat the shit out you tonight because I’m sick of listening to you, I’m sick of seeing you, and I’m sick of your pathetic Welsh ass existing in GIW. Alex Castellanos pack your little immigrant bags because I’m taking you out.” Kluge pushes the mic away from him as he heads out to the ring for his own match now as we head to break. QUOTE When we return back to the ring it’s time for the next match one these fans were really looking forward to as “Gluttony Fang” hits and the fans go wild with cheers as Tony Miranda heads out from the backstage area towards the ring showing off the many scars and bruises still ailing him over a week or so since the brutal Taipei Death Match. RILEY STEVENS: “The fact that he has a match here this week is amazing in itself.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “He’s an idiot for trying.” The fans clap him on as he walks past them and heads up into the ring as his music cuts off and then it’s “The Rat” by Dead Confederate that hits and all the sudden the boos come out in full force for a man who could be considered one of GIW’s most beloved stars in Matt Kluge who has shown a complete different side of himself tonight and has quickly made himself an enemy. RILEY STEVENS: “You know I really thought better of Matt Kluge, that attack earlier was uncalled for and his explanation? Just doesn’t seem like the man I know.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Kluge is a PRICK! I am a PRICK! He’s always just been masking it.” Kluge walks by the fans who for the first time take the chance to insult him rather then high five him as he heads up the steps and into the ring, ignoring the jeers to meet up with Miranda as the ref calls for the bell. DING DING DING! This match starts out with a big as the two men head to the center of the ring and though we are in a wrestling ring a boxing match breaks out as the two hurl punches back and forth like it’s their jobs as the two men known for their toughness go toe to toe in the center of the ring, answering punches back and forth with fans planted behind Tony firmly. When Tony throws a punch they cheer and when Kluge does they boo, but regardless both men’s punches start to slow down a bit and finally it’s Matt that bails out of the punching to step forward and nail him with a knee to the body to put a quick exclamation point on the start of the mat as Tony holds his stomach, sore just about every where even before the match started. Tony holds his gut in pain as Matt grabs him and throws him back into the corner following him into it. RILEY STEVENS: “Hope the EMT’s are ready in case one of Tony’s cuts rip open…” Miranda favors his back just long enough for Kluge to follow up with a big Clothesline in the corner, as the fans boo him he simply delivers punch after punch to Tony as the referee pulls him away to stop him, however as soon as the referee steps away from him he walks forward with some more as the referee starts the five count 1…2…3…4…Kluge backs away throwing up his hands as if to show his innocence as Tony now walks forward like a zombie as Matt grabs him going for a Northern Lights Suplex however it’s Miranda that is able to push away then nail him with a European Uppercut that sends Kluge reeling back to the ropes as Tony rushes forward clotheslining him on over them to the outside. RILEY STEVENS: “To the outside we go.” Matt gets up on the outside trying to recover as Miranda bounces off the ropes and jumps through the ropes sending himself right into Matt on the outside to a huge ovation from the crowd for the unexpected move. Tony gets to his feet seeming to get himself pumped up with that move as the crowd pats him on the back as Kluge gets up stumbling back into the barricade where Tony hoists him up and drops him right on it as things have gotten a bit dangerous as the referee starts to count them out 1…2…3…Matt gets up holding his gut as Tony attempts to throw him into the ring steps but as he pulls him Matt spins it around and ends up throwing Tony into them…4…5…6…The fans boo as Tony hits them hard as Kluge slides back into the ring seeming willing to take a win like that if need be…7…8…Tony gets up to his feet…9…and slides in right before the referee gets to ten which brings an angry sigh from Matt. RILEY STEVENS: “Tony barely gets back into the ring.” Matt doesn’t let up however as he walks right over to him as Tony gets up against the ropes grabbing him and pulling him away landing the ‘Hortonville Suplex Special 1’ a Suplex rolled into Mounted Punching as Kluge lays into him only for Tony to throw him off as both men got up at the same time, only surprisingly it’s Tony that’s first and he nails him with a Rising Side Kick as the fans get behind him, as Matt stumbles back a bit and Tony taunts him before slamming him down with a DDT. These fans are into it as he gets up heading towards the corner for the move that has become famous due to Tony’s usage the Running Leg Lariat as Matt starts to get up but before he can the familiar red tint of Scar’s entrance hits and Tony turns to the ramp in preparation, wanting a fight. RILEY STEVENS: “Is Scar really here?” Tony’s desire for Scar ends up getting the best of him as Kluge rushes at him as he has his eyes off him and nails him with ‘The Matt Cooke’ a Running Knee to his knee as the fans boo and almost identically to his attack on Alex earlier Tony limps forward right into ‘The Tiger's Roar Gold Edition’ his version of the Whale Hunt and plants Tony to the mat as he hops on top for the cover 1…2…3! JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…Matt Kluge!” These fans let out all the booing they can as Matt Kluge hops up to his feet getting his hand raised tonight having taken out Alex and Tony back to back with the very same move. RILEY STEVENS: “While I may not like his new attitude, he’s started out well. Don’t know how the red lighting happen but Tony’s desire for a fight with Scar cost him.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Kluge and I need to go out for some drinks tonight.” Kluge slides out of the ring heading up the ramp with a grin on his face; it had been a very good night for him as he heads to the backstage area with Tony struggling back to his feet angrily hitting the mat as we go to another commercial. WINNER: MATT KLUGE Heading to the backstage area before the next tag team match we find ourselves watching as Henry Malkin heads to the ring with his Television Championship over his shoulder but as he walks he walks right into a fresh off a win Evan Clarke. HENRY MALKIN: “Congratulations on the win.” Malkin gives him a nod but before he can walk by its Clarke responding that gets his attention to stop for a second. EVAN CLARKE: “Yup good luck in your match as well.” Clarke says this while looking at the belt on his shoulder, seeming to be eyeing it up. Malkin simply shifts it on his shoulder before walking onward towards the ring. ==== Else where we see Daniel Starr and Paul Defias getting prepped up in the backstage area at least Daniel is with Defias seeming to be flying right now after his win earlier in the night. PAUL DEFIAS: “I got the job done earlier, now it’s your turn. DefiaStarr two and o on our first night, I can already see it now.” Defias smirks as Daniel seems a bit too focused to really be all that happy with him. DANIEL STARR: “To be fair you weren’t facing Alioth Starre.” Defias shrugs his shoulders, he was right after all but these two stars were looking ready to make a name for themselves. PAUL DEFIAS: “Get the win, put yourself on the map and make DefiaStarr the talk of GIW.” Defias pats him on the back as Daniel now hops up to his feet, nodding his head heading towards the front area to get ready for his match. QUOTE It’s time for the third match of the night and as we return we see Joshua Acquin already stepping into the ring getting ready for the next match as the fans boo until his music trails off and “Busy Being Born” by Middle Class Rut hits and the fans go wild with cheers as Henry Malkin heads out from the back proudly wearing his GIW Television Championship around his waist. RILEY STEVENS: “This team is quite humorous as we have the new Television Champion, Henry Malkin with the former champion Joshua Acquin. Apparently Joshua tried to get out of this match but it was Sinclair that wouldn’t allow it.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Granted Henry didn’t pin Acquin…” Henry enters the ring proudly with his belt around his waist as he looks across at Acquin who stands in the corner angrily glaring back at him as his music cuts off and "Remedy" by Seether hits to a mixed response from the crowd for a man they are only starting to get to know in Michael Harris a man whom debuted back at ‘Retaliation’. RILEY STEVENS: “You know we really never how good a wrestler is until we see him wrestle in a normal match, rather then the Open Battle Royal and I have heard some good things about this guy.” Harris heads down to the ring as Acquin has commenced trash talking with Malkin who tries his best to ignore him as Michael heads up the steps and then into the ring looking across at his opponents with a smirk as they continue to argue as his music ends and “Man That You Fear” by Marilyn Manson hits as the fans begin to boo the entrance of Scar who enters with a new mask. RILEY STEVENS: “Scar was the victor back at ‘Retaliation’ topping Tony in a war, however it seems to be far from over.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “God, he’s a weird dude.” Scar enters the ring next to Harris both not seeming to be too worried about one another however despite saying nothing they were on better terms then Acquin and Malkin who were arguing a bit in their corner as the referee calls for the bell. DING DING DING! Quickly Scar willingly exits the ring for Harris to let him start however on the other side it’s Acquin that pushes Henry out trying to be the one to show him who is boss. Malkin exits only trying his best to maintain some form of a team despite early bickering as Joshua turns to him and can be heard saying “I am the champ!” But as he turns around he walks right into a big Dropkick from Michael who gets up with a big smirk having just gotten the first strike in this one as Joshua quickly recovers to his feet throwing a big right hand that misses Michael as Harris now wraps his arms Acquin only for Joshua to back him up against the ropes before Harris uses the ropes to propel Joshua off of them. On the rebound Acquin ducks under a big punch by Harris but as he hits the ropes again he ends up making it worse as he runs straight into a Double A Spinebuster, as he gets up not bothering yet with a pin instead deciding to pose a bit to boos from the crowd. RILEY STEVENS: “Impressive start here by Harris.” Michael walks back towards him going to hoist him up but Joshua quickly pushes him away and in some pain rushes to his corner slapping Henry on the chest to let him in as he rolls out of the ring to recover a bit. Henry gets in now and faces off with Harris, the two slowly circling around the ring ready to go as they go in for a lock up and it’s Henry that shows off his technical prowess by spinning around him turning it into a headlock, Harris spins around but Malkin transitions it right into a Snap DDT that gets the crowd into it, he’s really the only one they seem to like out here right now as he gets up to his feet quickly as Michael does the same shaking off the DDT only to get hit with a big Thrust Kick that doubles over Harris as Henry hits the ropes and follows it through with a Bulldog down to the mat as he goes for a pin 1…2-Kick Out. Not quite over yet as Malkin gets to his feet with Acquin returning to his corner now. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Let Acquin back in.” Malkin sadly has to keep his eye on his own tag team partner as he looks back at him to make sure he won’t try anything as he stalks Harris from behind. Michael gets to his feet only for Henry to go for a Half Nelson Suplex but before he can get a grip Harris throws a big elbow to knock him off balance and then immediately hits a Enzuigiri that sends Henry to the mat as Harris gets to his feet, shaking off the earlier onslaught by Malkin as he waits for him to get back up, however as he goes to pick him up Henry throws both feet up into Michael’s chest sending him stumbling back into the ropes where Scar makes the tag.
