Requiem 2010, 28 November 2010

GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: Requiem LIVE NOVEMBER 28th, FROM STEWART BEACH IN GALVESTON, TEXAS = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! We come to the ring set up smack dab in the middle of Stewart Beach in Galveston, Texas and the fans are all excited knowing that the show has officially started. We pan around them as per usual ‘Requiem’ standing for dead does not imply to these fans who are all out of their seats cheering as the next blockbuster show has started as we pan around the camera until we are at the announce table with Riley and Oliver the good and the bad voices of GIW. RILEY STEVENS: “Here we are you can feel the excitement in the air as these fans many of which having been here for awhile now are sitting at attention for what should be one of the biggest shows in GIW history! A night in which every title new and old will be contested; you’ve got to be excited with this one Oliver?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “No EPW, no Chicken, talk about a show! Just to stick it to David Sinclair we have the Evolve title in GIW now and we will crown a new champion as Eric Sparrow, Tony Miranda, and Ryan Blake compete for the belt and we will crown a new contender as well.” RILEY STEVENS: “And in a feud that has been brewing for a long while now we have Jake Kennedy taking on our returning ‘ION Television Champion’ Joshua Acquin in what should be a fantastic contest between two guys that always bring the excitement to GIW and two guys who really to me represent the division.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “And then the man who I am pulling for to overcome the odds Blake Beckett with Amanda Rowland in his corner will take on newcomer Mark Cross with Alexii Ravynova in his corner. It’s a shame our staff can’t get along and have to use two wrestlers to get themselves over.” RILEY STEVENS: “In one of the most anticipated matches of the night we’ll have two tag teams in Los Firemen and Technical Mechanica in a two out of three falls match for the new GIW Tag Team Championships. Then the headliner the match that everyone is split fifty fifty on, as Aspen Chaud and Wake Walker fight for Walker’s prestigious Galveston Island Championship, all this tonight. Can you handle it Oliver?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “God you’re corny.” The two smile as we take a break as the fans continue to stand in excitement waiting for the first match on tonight’s huge show. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = “Nothingwrong” by Jimmy Eat World hits and the fans look around a little confused knowing the song but confused as to who it could be as out from the back comes Jack Prowler the former EPW Star and one of the men that terrorized this place for awhile, the fans boo him as he looks out at them with those movie star good looks as he bounces up into the ring looking out at the crowd with a grin on his face as he’s handed a microphone and begins to talk. JACK PROWLER: "What did you all think that I was gone for good? You see I came over in the merger but I’ve decided to sit back and wait for them to rightfully bring back. Yet tonight the title that I should have held a long time ago is being fought for, yet I’m not even in the match. Now tell me what exactly makes them worthy of a shot but not me? So I’ve decided to come out here for this Evolve title shot match and get what I deserve." Prowler lowers the microphone looking up the ramp as he waits for any sort of indicator someone is coming. However after about thirty seconds a smirk comes onto his face as he raises the microphone back to his lips. JACK PROWLER: "See, that’s what I figured. Let this message to everyone I’m officially back and consider this my first step towards the Evolve Championship. This time nobody is going to stop me." Prowler drops the microphone and exits the ring to a few boos from the crowd as he shakes it off heading to the back as the segment ends and we take another short break. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = Back to the ring we come where "I Will Not Bow" by Breaking Benjamin hits and the fans begin to boo as out from the back comes Jake Kennedy walking down towards the ring with his new mentor Wake Walker following not far behind holding his title over his shoulder. Though the fans do like Wake they have no love lost for Jake and continue to boo him. He steps up into the ring as Wake heads to his corner. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “This man is fine without Wake, but with him he should only be going up. New champion here tonight I’m calling it.” RILEY STEVENS: “Well, quite frankly I’m not sure he deserves it. The way he put Joshua out of action after the last show, I hope Acquin beats the hell out of him.” Jake gets to his corner as "Nightmare" by A7X hits and the fans begin to cheer out from the back comes Joshua Acquin with the ‘ION Television Championship’ over his shoulder, to the surprise of many following him out is Austin Acquin who has been absent from GIW for awhile now. Both men head towards the ring to the fans cheers. RILEY STEVENS: “Looks like Austin will be joining his brother at ringside for this match; perhaps Josh thinks Wake may try something?” Joshua now heads up to the ring handing his belt over to the referee as Austin goes to his side of the ring. The referee turns and holds the belt up before handing it out of the ring then turning and calling for the bell. DING DING DING! These two men have been rivals for awhile now and for Josh it’s his night back after a long absence from the ring. Both men begin to circle each other; however Jake tries to shoot on Joshua’s knee the same one he hurt before but at the last second Acquin backs up. The fans boo Jake’s actions but when Jake turns to see what Wake has to say Acquin runs at him tackling him to the ground. The fans cheer as Joshua begins to release his anger onto Jake crushing him with blows before being pushed off of him. Both men hop up to their feet but it’s Joshua who goes for a kick only for his leg to be caught then hammered with a big thrown punch, sending Acquin folding over in pain as the fans begin to boo. Jake smirks as he holds out his arms showing off a bit as he begins to slam down with boots onto Josh’s chest. Acquin begins to get to his feet with the fans cheers as Kennedy backs up and sets up for a Dropkick! But Joshua grabs his feet and slams him down attempting to turn it into the Sharpshooter. However Jake is able to spin out of the move and both men get back to their feet and we are right back where we started with these men glaring at each other from across the ring, as they go in for a lock up. It’s Jake that gets the advantage slowly pushing Joshua back to the corner, before slamming his knee into Josh’s, which hurts Acquin a lot more then it does him. Acquin holds his knee in pain allowing Kennedy to back up and then run forward nailing him with a Flying Forearm Smash in the corner! The fans boo as Joshua falls to the mat in pain as Wake yells at him to pin him, as he drags him back into the ring and makes the cover 1…2…Kick Out! RILEY STEVENS: “You may be right after all Oliver, Kennedy has looked a lot better.” We are shown Austin on the outside who continues to cheer on his brother as Jake pulls Acquin to his feet, realizing he’ll have to do some more damage first. Rather then just doing it Jake grins to the crowd and then slaps Joshua straight across the face. The fans boo him however it seems to have ignited Joshua who catches Kennedy with a big punch sending him backwards. The fans cheer as Kennedy runs forward right into another one from Acquin who is hulking up. Jake gets up and seems to bait Acquin into trying another one before dropkicking Josh’s knee. The fans boo as Acquin falls to a knee, as Kennedy runs off the ropes and connects with a One Handed Bulldog! The fans boo as Jake wastes no time making the pin 1…2…Kick Out! Yet again he kicks out to the fans delight as it now seems Jake is getting a bit angry which Wake attempts to stop from the outside. However going against Wake’s wishes Jake heads to the top rope setting up for the ‘Here Comes The Boom’ a Diving Leg Drop! But his attempt goes in vain as Josh rolls out of the way at the last second! The fans cheer as he gets up to his feet as Kennedy rolls around in pain. Acquin calls for him to get up and when he does he attempts his finisher the ‘Facebreaker’ an Inverted Double Underhook Facebuster. But Kennedy spins it around attempting his own finisher a ‘Breakdown’ Full Nelson Facebuster. His attempt is blocked and Acquin turns it back into the ‘Facebreaker’ but this time it lands and the fans go nuts as Wake holds his head with Acquin making the cover 1…2…3! Still Champ. RILEY STEVENS: “What a win! Acquin comes back from injury to defend his belt.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “He was faking it.” Austin brings in the belt to his brother handing it to him as Wake rolls Jake out to the outside, checking on him. Austin holds up his brother’s arm as the two celebrate him keeping the belt. On the outside Wake is trying to say something but Jake pushes him away and heads to the back, leaving Wake seemingly very angry watching his protégé yet again snap after a loss. Back in the ring Josh holds up his title one last time as the fans continue to cheer him on as we head to the back. WINNER: JOSHUA ACQUIN (Still ION Television Champion) = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = We now head to the back where the fans begin to boo the immediate presence of Alexii Ravynova on the screen, she is looking at something and as we pan out we find it to be Mark Cross who is stretching out and getting ready for his upcoming match though Alexii seems to be the one that’s more worried. ALEXII RAVYNOVA: “You can’t lose tonight! I will look like a fool. Amanda has to lose! You have to realize that she’s the power hungry one. She treats this place like she’s the Dictator. I should be the one with the power I’m the smarter one.” Mark looks at her raising an eyebrow at her slight contradiction as Alexii continues to pace back and forth, seeming a million times more worried then the debuting Mark Cross is. MARK CROSS: “I’m not doing this for some little cat fight between you and her. I’m doing this because I love wrestling, so don’t worry about me winning I’ll be fine, ok?” Cross looks up at her nodding his head as he stretches out his back as she seems a little irritated by the fact that he didn’t just fall in line like she’d sort of expected as she quickly responds. ALEXII RAVYNOVA: “A win will get you on my good side; let’s just say that I have the ability to give you countless title opportunities if you get me the win.” Alexii gives a big smirk nodding her head her scheming head thinking this’ll surely motivate him however a chuckle turns her smirk into a much more angry tone. MARK CROSS: “I plan on earning my spot around here, so I think I’ll pass. Now I’m ready let’s go.” Mark smiles and walks by her out the door as Alexii watches him, she glares at him and then follows him out of the room as we turn are attention to the ring. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = The arena fades to black. Suddenly the titan-tron starts producing some weird sounds, like it was broken. Then some red letters, that looked like blood, appeared. "Soon" "Darkness" "Madness" "Decimation" They were appearing and disappearing really fast, the sound changed to someone crying, another one screaming and yet another one begging for help. Then everything went black, the sound stopped, it staid like that for a minute, and then a maniac laugh started, non-stopping. After two minutes of that laugh, everything went back to normal, like nothing had happened. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “What the hell was that? That scared me for real!” RILEY STEVENS: “I don't know, but it may be something good.” = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = ”Lift Me Up” by Moby hits and the fans begin to give a big applause as the debuting Mark Cross comes out from the back, while they dislike Alexii he’s earned their respect just moments before backstage. He walks out nodding his head as he high fives the fans, on his way out to the ring not far behind him is Alexii Ravynova. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “As much as I want to root for Alexii, who is this guy? She picked a NOBODY! I mean come on.” RILEY STEVENS: “This guy is a veteran, and quite frankly will be looking to make a statement here tonight, I happen to like her pick though I don’t feel I’m in any position to choose between Alexii and Amanda I think both should settle the quarrel.” Cross jumps into the ring and goes to the top rope holding up an arm as the fans cheer him as Alexii stand behind him now looking completely irked by her pick after he back talked her. “Demon Eyes” by The Answer now hits and a literally crazy amount of boos comes from the crowd as Blake Beckett comes out with Amanda Rowland at his side, she seems reluctant as well as Blake looks at her seeming to love having her forced to be at his side. RILEY STEVENS: “Never did I imagine this right here happening. It’s literally hard to even fathom at all, two huge rivals forced together here tonight.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Couldn’t they have just put Blake with Alexii and Cross with Amanda to make my choice easier? I can’t choose.” Beckett walks up the ramp and then bends over getting the ropes for Amanda with a big grin on his face as she shakes her head and goes ahead and heads in with Beckett following. It’s not Beckett or Cross fighting instead its Alexii and Amanda talking smack before the referee forces them out of the ring. He turns to the bell keeper and calls for the bell. DING DING DING! Right away Alexii yells out commands to Cross that are very clearly ignored as he walks to the center of the ring for a hand shake but is instead ignored by Beckett who is looking over at Amanda mocking her a bit. The fans are booing him as Cross willingly waits a bit as Blake continues saying something to her however suddenly Mark whips Beckett around and lifts him up onto his shoulders then catches him with a huge ‘Go 2 Sleep’ that catches everyone in the arena off guard. Blake falls to the mat out of it as Cross makes the cover 1…2…3! That quickly! RILEY STEVENS: “Whoa! Out of no where this thing is over, Blake was too busy with Amanda and its over.” Alexii heads up the steps into the ring holding up her hands like she just won the match as she basically pushes Cross to the background as the fans cheers turns to boos as she takes the center stage. Beckett slowly rolls out of the ring, catching himself on the outside looking clearly out of it. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “That was unfair! He wasn’t ready.” Mark watches Alexii seeming to be a bit annoyed by her actions but stays calm none the less as Beckett starts to head to the back with Amanda following not far behind him as we cut back to Alexii with that big smile then finally to Mark Cross who had a quick yet impressive win here tonight yet has been completely overshadowed by his manager and the storyline that he walked into as we take a break. WINNER: MARK CROSS = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = After the break we come back right behind the curtain as Blake is holding his head in pain with Amanda Rowland not far behind him, he seems in mood to talk but she taps on his shoulder and does so anyways. AMANDA ROWLAND: “What in the world was that? Ratings are already low enough as is and you’re going to have a performance like THAT? I’m sorry I gave you your chance and you know what, your fir--” She’s cut off mid-sentence by a very angry yet also worried Beckett not wanting to lose his job at all. BLAKE BECKETT: “You can’t do that! I’ll figure something out. I’ll double your ratings; I have a friend that owes me one and would really help the ratings around here. I swear.” Amanda seems to be just as fearful about the company as Blake is about his job as she takes a second to think while Blake winces his head still in clear pain after that one. AMANDA ROWLAND: “Ok…but you triple them and your deadline is the next supershow ‘Genesis’, otherwise your gone.” Amanda walks off as Blake sighs not sure what to say clearly worried about his job hoping his idea will work out as the segment comes to a close. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = It’s time for the Tag titles match up as we start off with Stereo Palma’ “El Mariachi” alerting the fans of the entrance of Technical Mechanica whom they immediately begin to boo. Both members come out rather slowly as they head to the ring, talking trash to the fans at ringside. They jump up onto the ring apron then over the ropes and into the ring making their in ring debuts here in GIW. RILEY STEVENS: “Here is Technical Mechanica who I have heard some good things about, but for the most part we know they are hated by the fans.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Was our Spanish demographic suffering or something?” They stand in their corner readying themselves for the match as “Fireproof” by Piller hits and the fans begin to cheer as like a shot fired out of a cannon both members of the Los Firemen pop out running down and high fiving the fans on their way to the ring, running the whole time until they reach the ring and they slide in. RILEY STEVENS: “I heard that Los Firemen picked up some tips from the Grateful Dead this week.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I want to make a witty joke about them but it’s sad when they were our best team ever, rather depressing in fact.” Los Firemen go to both of their corner’s and hold up their arms as the fans cheer them on, the referee cracking out the new GIW Tag Team titles and holding them up as the teams decide who will start. DING DING DING! Los Firemen begin to play rock paper scissors for it however Technical Mechanica waste little time running across the ring and taking a cheap shot from behind on Jose who bumps Jos B out of the ring. The fans boo as the referee pulls Ombio away while Damon Ramon begins to deliver some big kicks to the downed Jose. The fans boo as Ramon completely ignores them as he lifts Jose up and throws him into his corner. Damon runs across the ring and connects with a big clothesline then tags in his partner Ombio who steps into the ring, allowing both men to start hammering him with their boots. He tries to cover up as the referee tells Damon to get out, which he does. Damon however points to Jos B who has returned distracting the referee allowing Ombio to catch Jose with a big eye poke in the corner. He quickly tags in Ramon who heads into the ring with both of them taking advantage of him beginning to dance around him catching him with punches as Jos B watches helplessly from the corner. The referee again demands that Ombio head out of the ring but when he does Damon lands a huge Low Blow! The fans boo as the referee turns around and Damon is on top, and Jos B respects the rules knowing he can’t break up the pin 1…2…3! First Fall: Technical Mechanica RILEY STEVENS: “First fall goes to Technical Mechanica! One more fall away from becoming the champions and if they keep these cheap antics up they may do it sooner rather then later.” Instead of wasting any time Damon makes the cover again 1…2…Kick Out! The fans cheer this slight sense of hope as Ramon demands the referee to make another count 1…2…Kick Out! The fans cheer as he’s done it again as Damon gets up pulling Jose with him whipping him into the corner yet again. He runs at him going for a big dropkick but Jose dives out of the way. Ombio tags in and goes after him however it’s too late as Jose tags in a fresh Jos B! The fans cheer as Jos B catches a punch attempt from Ombio then jumps over the ropes bringing him down with a Hurricarana! The fans are going nuts at this point as Ombio hops back up running right into a DDT as Los Firemen have bounced back. Jos B calls for him to get back and Ombio does just that looking every bit stunned as he walks right into the Los Firemen’s move as Jos B makes the tag to Jose and then lifts him up onto his shoulders as they hit the ‘Call 911’ a Doomsday set up into Jose’s Blockbuster! The fans cheer as they now make the cover with Jose making sure Damon does not come in 1…2…3! Second Fall: Los Firemen The fans cheer as Jose now looks over at Damon as he lifts up Ombio and tries to whip him off the ropes. Ombio catches himself and dives over tagging Ramon into the match. Damon comes into the ring looking across the ring as he and Jose begin to circle one another. They go in for a lock up but it’s Ramon that catches him with a big knee to the gut then pushes him away so that he can attempt a big lariat only for Jose to duck it and kick him into his corner, he makes the tag quickly to Jos B who goes Springboard and they hit ‘Hotsauce’ a Springboard Elbow and a Russian Legsweep from Jose underneath. The fans cheer as Jos B pulls him to the center of the ring trying to make the cover 1…2…Kick Out! It’s not over yet as Jos B backs up starting a slow clap as the fans join in. As he calls for Ramon to get to his feet. Jos B lifts him up onto his shoulders going for a Fireman’s Carry Slam but Damon is able to slide off and pushes Jos right into the referee! The fans boo as Jos tries to see if he’s ok while Ombio wastes little time jumping into the ring running across the ring catching Jose with a Superkick! The fans boo as Jose falls ungraciously to the outside as Ombio sets up on the outside as Damon grabs up the off guard Jos B onto his shoulders as they deliver the ‘La Arolladora’ the Doomsday Device! The fans are giving it to them as the referee just now begins to get to his senses turning and starting the count as Jose tries to get up 1…2…3! RILEY STEVENS: “This is a travesty.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “It’s called good game planning you idiot, not their faults they do what it takes to win.” Damon and Ombio slide out of the ring demanding their belts as they head to the backstage area celebrating with the belt the entire time to the fans boos. In the ring Jose now joins Jos B in the ring, helping him out as they watch Technical Mechanica celebrate after doing every cheap trick they had in their arsenal to win this one, as we take a break. WINNERS: TECHNICAL MECHANICA (New GIW Tag Team Champions) = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = Back to the back we go where yet again it’s Amanda Rowland pacing back and forth in her office when the door slams open and its Sarah Sharp that’s standing at the door with a microphone, Amanda looks at her angry at her for entering so loudly as Sarah asks the question on everyone’s mind. SARAH SHARP: “Earlier in the night you spared Blake’s job because he made you a promise to boost ratings in fact you demanded he triple them. That’s no easy feat but has he told you anything about it?” Amanda begins to shake her head no as Sarah holds the microphone up to her mouth while Amanda decides what information to tell. AMANDA ROWLAND: “He hasn’t told me anything at all other then that next week’s Revolt’s main event will be Mark Cross taking on new star Assassin in a singles match.” Rowland nods her head not having anything else to say as Sarah moves the mic back to her lips to add something to it. SARAH SHARP: “Cool it’s sort of like Blake’s back in charge.” Sharp smiles widely until she turns back to Amanda who seems none to happy with her assessment of the situation as Sharp quickly turns and heads out of the room as we again end with Amanda looking a bit weary of the situation. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = Back into the ring we head and it’s time for one of the most anticipated matches of the night. "The Edge Of Paradise" by Kamelot hits and the fans immediately explode into cheers as out from the back comes Tony Miranda. He heads to the ring looking to be in serious mode as he heads up the steps the fans continuing to cheer one of their favorites. RILEY STEVENS: “It’s now time for the Evolve Championship match, a match that all three of these guys qualified for on Revolt.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Its due time this title gets some prestige.” As soon as Tony steps through the ropes “Kick in the Teeth” by Papa Roach hits and the fans turn their attention to booing the new and young star as he walks out holding his arms arrogantly strolling his way down towards the ring. Walking by a few teenage girls that are seemingly in love with him as he heads up the steps. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “This kids a star right here.” RILEY STEVENS: “Think it’s a little premature to call him a star. His debut was impressive but to me he seems awfully arrogant.” Blake steps through the ropes staring over at Tony as he heads up to the top rope as “Fatlip” by Sum 41 hits and the fans even throw out more boos as out from the back is Eric Sparrow whom many have called the villain of GIW. He walks out with that always similar swagger to him as he heads towards the ring soaking in all of the boos. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Can’t believe Sparrow isn’t wrestling in the main event where he belongs!” RILEY STEVENS: “Yeah, yeah he was screwed blah, blah, blah you two need to get over yourselves.” Sparrow bounces up the steps and then over the rope as the three men stand in different corners of the ring as the referee now brings out the currently vacant Evolve title holding it up into the air as the three men stare it before he hands it out of the ring and then calls for the bell. DING DING DING! Right away the men start to circle each other when suddenly Sparrow looks over at Ryan and begins to point at Tony wanting a double team attack. Blake at first seems to think about it but then turns back and quickly nods his head. As both stars run forward and begin to go on the attack. Tony crushes Blake with a shot and then gives one to Sparrow but he only knocks them back for a second as they come back crushing him into the corner and starting to lay into him. They get him down with boots as he falls down into the corner holding up his arms to try to protect himself, however a brutal kick from Sparrow lands that seems to momentarily stun him. Ryan now sets him up in the corner locking up his legs as he backs up then runs forward catching Tony with a big boot to the face, no way to protect himself at all. Tony wiggles his hands free now as Sparrow sets up, he runs forward and Tony attempts to protect his face but Eric instead brutally dropkicks Tony’s right leg, sending him falling out of the corner. Ryan Blake quickly slides out of the ring with Tony grunting in pain. Grabbing his legs he now sends him hard into the ring post as the fans, are beginning to boo this two on one onslaught. Ryan backs up smirking as Eric now slides out with him, grabbing Tony’s leg a second time and sending it into the ring post. But just as he does Ryan grabs him from behind and throws him hard into the surrounding barricade. The fans are shocked almost expecting Sparrow to betray him first but Blake quickly slides into the ring and has a hard time pulling Tony into the center of the ring to pin him but eventually he does 1…2…Sparrow got in at the last possible second to break up the pin attempt. RILEY STEVENS: “Ryan Blake nearly took the victory there but he didn’t quite take Eric out enough.” Sparrow pulls Eric to his feet and clobbers him with a big European Uppercut sending him falling back to the corner. With him set up he goes for the ‘Pineapple Express’ a Corner Dropkick but at the last possible second Blake moves sending Eric’s legs into the corner. He hops up rather quickly with a small limp right into the awaiting arms of Ryan who grabs him and gives him a HUGE German Suplex. But he doesn’t release the hold as he sets up for another one and delivers it. Time for the three peat but Sparrow is able to spin out of it and he delivers a flawless Superkick to Ryan sending him to the mat hard. Sparrow climbs on top as the fans boo him 1…2...Ryan Blake kicks out. In the corner Tony is starting to stir a bit but neither Sparrow or Ryan notice as Eric instead lifts Ryan back to his feet hoping to inflict some more damage to him. Eric takes his eyes off of Blake for a second realizing Tony’s up but as he does he’s caught with a brutal Buzzsaw Kick to the face. With Sparrow falling underneath the ropes Blake now notices Miranda’s back up as he runs at him only for Tony to some how connect with a Clothesline though he’s putting all his pressure on one leg. Ryan hops back up quickly as Tony gives him another one. The fans are cheering as Tony calls for him to get back up, as he seems to be setting up for the ‘Lightning Buster’ a Crash Thunder Buster! But as he prepares to lift him, his legs give out from underneath him. Sending him to the mat setting up nicely for Ryan. He looks to the crowd with a smirk as Tony now on his knees wincing in pain favors his leg and doesn’t notice that Ryan’s setting up for something when he finally does it’s to late as Ryan delivers ‘The Causality’ a Running Shinning Wizard! That’s it it’s officially over. The fans are stunned as Ryan gets to his feet arrogantly showing off as he stands over Tony. RILEY STEVENS: “This kid needs to make the cover.” Finally he seems to turn his attention back to Tony but as he does Sparrow has recovered and is now going Springboard and Blake notices at the last second as he’s caught with a ‘Air Sparrow’ a Springboard Clothesline that sends Ryan to the mat. When he pops up he’s caught with a kick to the jaw and is tossed from the ring ungraciously by Eric. Back in the ring the fans boo as rather then just finishing Tony off Eric now heads to the top rope and looks out at them before delivering a picture perfect ‘Spinal Tap’ a Spiral Tap that lands to perfection. He makes the cover immediately hooking the leg 1…Ryan gets to his feet on the outside...2…He starts to slide into the ring…3! He’s to late as Sparrow picks up the win and the title. RILEY STEVENS: “Eric Sparrow is the new champion!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Simply because he’s the best in this company.” Sparrow quickly rolls out of the ring as Ryan Blake watches him angrily kicking the ropes that he was just a tad to slow to break up the pin. Sparrow now is handed the belt which he holds to his chest, striking his first gold here in GIW. RILEY STEVENS: “Though his showboating may have got in his way, Ryan Blake showed the world that he’s certainly capable of being a star. He’s certainly impressed me and he’ll have a big future around here if he can keep this up.” Sparrow holds up his belt into the air yet again loving the boos that come with it as he throws the belt over his shoulder backing up towards the back while Ryan again slams the mat, with his hands showing his anger as Sparrow walks away the new Evolve Champion. WINNER: ERIC SPARROW (New GIW Evolve Champion) = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = Back to the backstage area we come where Ryan Blake is standing in a corner fuming over the loss knowing that he was not even involved in the ending but has his first loss regardless. From the side comes Joshua Acquin still ‘ION Television Champion’ with his brother Austin Acquin. JOSHUA ACQUIN: “Tough break kid, you did really well though.” Joshua looks over at Austin who nods his head in agreement however Ryan seems none to pleased his face looking bitter right before he snaps back. RYAN BLAKE: “Shut up! That title should have been mine and you know it. I KNOW for a fact I could beat you or anyone around here. In fact why don’t you and I fight, for that belt of yours?” Blake taps the title on Acquin’s shoulder as Joshua seems to have basically expected such a reaction as he responds. JOSHUA ACQUIN: “Around here we earn what we get, so how about this? If you beat my brother Austin on Revolt you can have your shot.” Ryan nods his head in agreement looking over at Austin who stares back at him, before Josh pulls Austin away as we head to the ring for the huge main event. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = We head to the backstage area where Wake is getting prepared for his match as he stretches out his arms before turning and grabbing up the title off the table and turning looking into the camera. WAKE WALKER: “For me, being the best is the only option. Tonight I face my biggest challenge I’ve had here, against another person who wants to be the best just like I do. But for me being the best is the only option, so if I lose. It’s the last time you’ll see Wake Walker around here.” Walker says this seeming completely sincere as he adjusts his title belt and then heads out the door going towards the ring as the fans in the arena become even more anxious for this war in the main event. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = The arena at this point is in a small little frenzy having just heard Wake’s announcement further making them wonder just who will win this clash of the titans. "Bullseye" by Aly and AJ and the noise is deafening as out from the back comes Aspen Chaud soaking in the cheers as she heads down towards the ring high fiving her fans as she heads towards the ring for her biggest match yet in GIW. The fans are all excited as she steps into the ring, the fans not dying down yet. RILEY STEVENS: “It’s time for the biggest match of the night this huge main event between Aspen Chaud and our long time champion Wake Walker.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “…Should be Sparrow.” Aspen now goes through the ropes inactivating a camera with her lip gloss by giving it a kiss as the fans cheer her on as everyone turns their attention to the entrance area as “Dazed and Confused” by Led Zeppelin hits as the lights dim and out from the back comes Wake Walker to just as loud a response as Aspen as he holds his belt, over his shoulder walking to the ring the fans all patting him on the back as he heads to the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Who you got winning this match Oliver?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Unlike these fans I’m not excited for this one at all.” Up the ramp and into the ring, handing off the belt to the referee is Wake as he stares across the ring at Aspen and this colossal match up between these two now seems real as the referee holds up the most prestigious belt in Galveston, before calling for the bell. DING DING DING! Both fighters begin to stare back at each other knowing just how much this match truly means to them as they come to the center of the ring shaking hands before coming in for a tie up. The fans are completely quiet just watching this in awe, having waited for this for so long. They are locked up for a second with Wake getting the clear advantage pushing her up against the ropes then whipping her off of them, she comes back and gets caught with a Knee to the mid-section followed right into a Suplex! The fans of Wake cheer as he backs up allowing Aspen now to get to her feet, as she shakes it off and the two come back into battle. They come back into a lock up but this time its Aspen breaking free and going behind him going for a roll up 1…Wake rolls through with a pin attempt of his own 1…Aspen bounces back into her own pin attempt 1…Finally Wake is able to bounce free sending Aspen off of him as they again get to their feet, Aspen goes on the attack but Wake pushes her back easily then goes for a Dropkick but it proves to be a HUGE mistake as she catches him leg then brings him down on his chest as she locks in the ‘K.M.A.’ a Heel Lock! The fans are absolutely stunned that Wake just made that mistake as she pulls it with all of her force with Wake trying to battle to the ropes, but unfortunately for him he’s right in the middle of the ring. The fans are stunned as Wake seems to be near out as Aspen continues to tug at it. The hold is on for nearly two minutes when Wake uses all his strength to throw her off of him. Aspen falls onto her butt as Wake gets up but as soon as he puts weight on that ankle he falls over onto the mat. Aspen seems genuinely worried as Wake tries to get up again but again can’t do it. The referee steps in checking on him asking him if he can continue. RILEY STEVENS: “Wake may have broken his ankle with that move, I mean if he did he can’t continue and this match would win in favor of Aspen.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “And all you who thought this would be great would be laughed at.” Wake continues to bounce on his ankle not wanting to put any weight on it however he continues to insist that the match continues. The fans cheer him on for it, as Aspen tries to talk him out of it as well not wanting to fight a hurt Wake. However the referee lets him go as he tells Aspen to come at him. Chaud doesn’t want to at all however a big clothesline will probably change her mind. The fans cheer his resilience as he battles through the injury with Aspen getting back to her feet, watching him as she runs at him throwing a big forearm that sends him back into the corner. With him there she sets it up seeming to be willing to fight now as she hits him with the ‘Tush Push’ a Turnbuckle Flying Butt Shot that sends him forward limping as she goes to the top. However as she’s there she again becomes resilient to hit the move. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Just do it, finish him.” The fans are split on what she should do however the choice is not hers any more as it’s Wake that makes the choice for her by grabbing her off the top rope and throwing her off of it, favoring his ankle. Aspen gets up still struggling to breath having taken a big fall there however Wake amazingly is able to hit ‘The Trickster’ a Drive-by Kick! The fans go nuts until he falls to the mat holding his ankle, forcing himself to the cover 1…2…Kick Out! The fans cheer her kick out while more have gotten on Wake’s side due to the injury. He lets out a big sigh thinking he may have had it there but the ever resilient Aspen was able to kick out. He now lifts her to her feet she’s out of it seeming like dead weight as he sets her up for ‘Temple Of Love’ a Sit-Down Powerbomb! But at the last possible second Aspen is able to roll off and then tie him up for the ‘The Barbie Makeover’ a Straight Jacket Sitout Sleeper Slam! The fans basically think it’s the end as she makes the cover with the same move that Wake laid down for at ‘On Hallowed Ground’ and it’s time to see if he truly did 1…2…Kick Out! The fans go nuts as Wake kicks out and Aspen looks at him stunned just as well. She gets to her feet still seeming out of it from The Trickster as Wake gets up surprisingly, catching her with a single punch. Before she gets behind him and hits another ‘The Barbie Makeover’! 1…2…Kick Out! The fans are stunned as he gets up once again and Aspen responds with a look of pain for herself not wanting to do much else to him but she hopes that the third time will be the charm, as she hits it one last time. The fans realize it just as well as she does this is over as she makes the cover 1…2…3! RILEY STEVENS: “New champion, but I think even for her it’s a bittersweet moment.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Who cares about Wake? Quite frankly good riddance.” RILEY STEVENS: “You’re literally the only one here who thinks that.” Aspen gets to her feet as some of the fans cheer for her while others seem to be realizing what this means for Wake. Many will disagree with it knowing that he more then likely has either a broken ankle or a severely hurt one but Wake is not a man to go back on his word. Aspen’s handed the belt but she simply tosses it to the mat instead helping up Wake from the mat. The fans clap as she pulls him to his feet; he continues to favor his ankle. RILEY STEVENS: “Quite an emotional moment here.” The fans cheer for both fighters as Wake shoves by her limping over to the title and hands it over to Aspen. Instead of celebrating she asks for a shake these competitors showing a moment of respect. Wake raises her hand instead but she let’s go and then does the same for him. Finally she does what we all want to do as she asks him to stay, even pointing out his ankle but he shakes his head no. The fans cheer them both as both competitors now embrace in a hug the show coming to a close with the start of Aspen’s reign as Walker’s comes to a close. WINNER: ASPEN CHAUD (New Galveston Island Champion)
