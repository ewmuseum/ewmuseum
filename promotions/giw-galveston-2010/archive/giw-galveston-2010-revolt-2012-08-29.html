QUOTE We head to start this match up that is tonight’s main event between two of GIW’s most beloved stars as the video teaser for the fun contest ends and “The Show Goes On” by Lupe Fiasco hits to a ruckus response from the crowd for DareDevil who walks out from the back with that grin on his face looking out at the crowd who cheer him on so loudly as he heads down to the ring high fiving a few fans along the way. RILEY STEVENS: “DareDevil is a fast rising star here in GIW and tonight is a huge opportunity for him against the champion.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “People say he’s so great, why isn’t he winning then?” Devil heads over to the corner jumping up and posing for the crowd who clap him on as he steps down and looks up to the ramp awaiting his opponent as the crowd already starts to buzz before the music even hits as "Party in the USA" by Miley Cyrus hits as the Undisputed Champion herself walks out from the back showing them pearly whites as she high fives both sides as she heads down the ramp looking into the ring at her opponent tonight. RILEY STEVENS: “As champion you never get days off tonight is no different for her as she faces a tough challenge.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Doesn’t matter she’s a month away from losing her belt regardless.” Seton enters the ring heading across it to the opposite corner of Devil jumping up raising her belt into the air as usual eliciting an even louder response from the crowd as she jumps down ready to get things started here in this one handing out her belt as the referee calls for the bell. DING DING DING! It’s time here tonight for the Main Event as these two begin to circle around each other both clearing sharing mutual respect as they go in for a lock up, many having to be shocked by the fact that Seton is taller then DareDevil in this one. Madison however hops right back up taking a swing at him with a clothesline only for DareDevil to duck it and jump up off the ropes flying back into a Crossbody onto Seton taking her down into a pin attempt 1…Kick Out! RILEY STEVENS: “DareDevil is looking great here tonight.” Madison gets up a little slower this time holding her neck as she does as DareDevil grabs her going for a Suplex only for Madison to hook his leg so he can’t get her up. Madison ends up working it back in her favor instead hoisting him up and over with a Suplex of her own as these fans slap on their champions, these fans trying to pick sides in this one. Getting up trying to shake off the earlier assault Seton waits for Devil to get back up kicking him in the gut and then to the chest finally with a shot to the head that causes DareDevil to stumble back into the corner as she rushes him with a clothesline knocking him down off his feet into a seated position in the corner as she hits the ropes heading towards him with a knee to the face that looks brutal. RILEY STEVENS: “This guy is crazy!” Seton holds her back in clear pain as DareDevil grabs her not wanting to waste any time as the referee gets to three on his count as he tosses her back into the ring as she rolls into it on her back still as he hops up onto the ring apron grabbing a hold of the ropes as Seton, gets up slowly as he flies off going for a Springboard Dropkick but Madison rolls under it and Devil is left with nothing to do but brace for the fall landing hard with his miss. Bouncing up holding his arm that took the brunt of the damage he stumbles right into a Russian Legsweep.
