GIW — Card

Revolt, 14 August 2014

14 August 2014
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: REVOLT Live from the Kaufman Arena – August 14th, 2014 A wide shot of the parking lot pans until it stops toward a section of vacant spaces. Seconds after the camera stops, its focus blankly on the lot, a black limousine pulls up to one of the vacant parking spots. Quickly, the camera man closes in on the right side of the limousine toward the passenger door. The vehicle turns off, and the door opens. Before a the passenger can exit, the camera cuts to the arena, the ring in center focus. A mid-shot brings us closer to the individual inside the ring. Alexis Terry. She is holding a microphone and a bad attitude. ALEXIS TERRY: “Unfortunately, yesterday, and without my knowing, a friend and great ally to The System was unceremoniously released from Galveston Island Wrestling! I am the one in control of GIW and I am the one who decides whether someone is fired, or not. I believe that CDE has something to do with this, and I am going to find him and whoever let this happen and make sure that this person has been eradicated! No one screws with Alexis Terry's regime.” She continued, the crowd silent. ALEXIS TERRY: “So, to prove that I am in charge and that no one is going to undermine me, I am going to get Charity Ryan officially reinstated!” Alexis stopped when the jeers filled the arena. The camera cut to a short break. QUOTE “I am the Roman Destroyer” played over the sound system within the arena before the Smashing Pumpkins brought the crowd to their feet. An instrumental of the song ‘Zero’ played the newcomer to the ring. He looked rough, strong, and the epitome of masculinity as he stepped down with boots like lead and a gaze that could break stone. RILEY STEVENS: “Is this man going to be a force to be reckoned with here in GIW?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “He looks like he could break a thousand men in half with one punch.” RILEY STEVENS: “We’ve seen big and buff before, though, and we’ve seen them dominated by people as small as Aspen Chaud and Alexis Terry.”  OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Well, he has the looks of a dominant male.” Antonio Zeroni stepped into the ring and turned toward the aisle as his opponent for the opening match was announced. “And his opponent, coming to the ring, weighing approximately one-hundred and thirty pounds; Zitty McGee!” The announcer called out as Kid Rock’s ‘Bawitdaba’ rocked the ears of the crowd. Zitty McGee was a small, slender little man. His red face and oily hair certainly made him more recognizable than some of the wrestlers in the back, but his physique left a lot to be desired. He tagged the hands of the grounds, though they were mostly unwilling as he was announced as a local wrestler. Certainly this had to be a squash match to introduce the Roman Destroyer, Antonio Zeroni. Before he entered the ring, Zitty beckoned for a microphone so he could speak to his opponent. He wanted to psych out Zeroni first before entering the ring. His laugh was like a motorboat drowning in oil and he pointed toward the bigger man with his scrawny, bony hand. “I’m going to show you that I’m tough enough to beat you, and everyone else on this roster. I’m way tough enough! More than tough enough! I’m Zitty McGee, Super model and super wrestler extraordinaire!” His words did not make anyone shake in their boots, least of all Zeroni. The moment he slipped into the ring, Zeroni was on him. All it took was a powerful and modified powerbomb to drop Zitty flat onto the canvas. Zeroni didn’t even need vindication from a three count. With Zitty out cold, he had left the ring, declared victor by knock out. Riley Stevens tried to say something to comment on what he had just seen, but all he could do is let out a grunt, reeling from having seen Antonio Zeroni completely and utterly destroy his opponent in a matter of seconds. Quickly thereafter, the cameras cut to a break. WINNER via Knockout: ANTONIO ZERONI! QUOTE Mo’ Love made their way to the ring during the break and were already set for the match when the show returned from break. They were anticipating the mystery ahead of them of who their opponent would bring to the ring in the tag match against The Captain. He appeared at the stage, without his music but vocal on the microphone. He was going to introduce his opponent and bring down the house, starting with Mo’ Love. The Captain stood tall, proud and began the introduction. THE CAPTAIN:  “I’m tired of looking at your ridiculous, goofy faces, Mo’ Love. Tonight, I’ve got a special surprise for you, and it’s my mystery partner come to take you down to what ever drop of something funny you’ve been taking. This is it, you’re demise! Ladies and gentlemen, my partner tonight is–” Voice: “This is your new administrator speaking. I’m sorry, Captain. Unfortunately, and this is part of the ineffectiveness that Alexis Terry brings to the table when dealing with talents, but your partner was never booked for the match. This means you’ll have to face Mo’ Love in a handicap match. Good luck.” Not liking this option one bit, but not wanting to back out, Captain made his way down to the ring to face the two alone. Chronic started the bout with Captain, but it was soon discovered that this wasn’t so much a tag version of a handicap, but both of them allowed in the ring at the same time against him. Captain knew it was futile, but he still fought. He sent Chuck reeling with a few right hooks, but soon Mo was on him. Together, they dominated Captain. He fought valiantly, but it wasn’t enough to hold off two men when he expected help. The two worked together to drop him, pin him, and then party afterwards. WINNER via Pinfall: Mo' Love! Cut to a close shot of the door to the office of Alexis Terry. A small silver plated sign of her name is on the door, front and center, until suddenly a hand reaches out and removes it. The camera cuts out to a mid-two shot. CDE is the individual that removed the sign with Alexis Terry, her arms folded, and eyes glaring through him. ALEXIS TERRY: “So, it would appear you're the criminal trying to mask himself with power to undermine my authority. CDE, you can consider yourself completely, and utterly fi-” CDE: “It brings me delight, and a tingling sensation to inform you that, no, Alexis Terry, I'm not, but I am working with the person who will take over after you are fired!” Alexis grabbed him by the shirt and pushed him into the door. Her anger flared and his lips cracked a wry smile. ALEXIS TERRY: “You have no power over me! I will book you in a match next week against three people of my choosing! I will have my revenge against you, and I will have control over GIW. This is my company now!” He shoved her hands away and brushed his chest off of her touch. CDE: “Get your paws off me, you damn dirty ape!” Cut to the ring, where Madison Seton stands tall, her title over shoulder, and microphone in hand. MADISON SETON: "Sorry for the sudden violent content last week, Harlequin, but you can understand that this is modern warfare. You and Beck Ramsey in league with one another makes me both sick with contempt and laughter." She continued, the crowd mostly silent as she spoke. Her eyes turned toward the big screen at the start of the entry way. MADISON SETON: "I would like this alliance to come to an end, Harlequin. I find it stupid, and silly, that you would even consider aligning with Beck Ramsey to eliminate a common enemy within myself. Remember, dearie, that he is your enemy as well. The old saying of 'the enemy of my enemy is my friend' holds no ground between the three of us. I am the title holder and you two are contending to obtain it, but not only that, you have the upper edge being the actual number one contender for my title. Ramsey is trying to shove you out of that opportunity. The war I have with him, you are involved as well, but know that no one is your ally, but yourself." RILEY STEVENS: "It sounds like Madison Seton is trying to break up this unsteady alliance between Harlequin and Beck Ramsey that she thinks exists." OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Thinks? Harlequin knows she's next in line, but Madison Seton and Beck Ramsey need to settle this war to unify the titles. Until then, she's just going to have to wait and see how things turn out. I am sure both Beck and Harlequin know that once a true champion has been crowned, she's gunning for that person, no matter who it is. Perhaps in this case, however, she's helped Beck only because Madison is the worse of two evils.” The camera cut to a short break. Upon return, Beck Ramsey has joined Madison in the ring and a table has been set up with a black clipboard containing the contract for their match at Blackout to unify the Undisputed Championship Title. The camera cut to a wide shot of the two across from one another with the table separating them. Two chairs for them both on each side and a pen per individual. BECK RAMSEY: "I don't know what it is you think you're doing, Madison. I am the true champion of GIW, and you're a cast off that refuses to accept this. Why don't you go the way of Aspen Chaud, Alioth Starre, and Alex Castellanos and leave, before it's too late." MADISON SETON: "Alioth may have had the longest reign, and Aspen the most title wins, but I am currently the greatest champion this company has ever seen. Very few have ever beaten me, and I continue to display my dominance." BECK RAMSEY: "Great, so you're a dominatrix now? Listen, bitch, I don't give a damn who you are, where your jigglies are on your body, because all I care about is the title that's around my waist. This is what makes me the leader amongst all of you. I am Beck Ramsey, and I am the true Undisputed Champion!" Madison Seton quickly signed the contract and turned it over to Beck Ramsey. MADISON SETON: "Then sign the dotted line, Beck. If you think you are the best there is, then prove it. I know I can beat you, but you are uncertain if you can beat me. How else would you be manipulating Harlequin?." BECK RAMSEY: "I'm under no guise that when this is over between us, Seton, that she'll be the first person I defend this title against. Her, Seton, not you." A close up shot showed Beck Ramsey signing the contract as well and just as he lifted his pen, the lights went black. The camera pulled to a wide shot when it returned, finding Seton on the canvas, her chair on its side and Harlequin standing over her with the briefcase in an offensive stance. BECK RAMSEY: "Harlequin earned the chance to take on the Undisputed Champion, Seton. You lost this title and have earned nothing to take it back. You aren't part of the big picture and it's time I eliminate you from it." The camera cut to a short break. QUOTE Upon return, the triple threat match had already gone underway. It didn’t seem fair how one-sided it was as Astro had started the match dominating his two opponents. It was almost as if their minds were elsewhere, and with all of his connected strikes, soon it would be. Diego and Sam Haim, two individuals seemingly capable of working against one another, let alone Astro, found themselves victim to his physical prowess. Astro took Sam by the shoulder and ran him out of the ring, then turned to Diego, whom had tried to capitalize on that opportunity, but failed. It took an expertly performed tiger suplex pin to end the bout with Diego submitting via pinfall to Astro. WINNER via Pinfall: KEVIN ASTRO! After the bout the camera cut to the staging where Alexis Terry walked with quick, concise strides toward the ring. The ring was quickly cleared for her arrival and a microphone brought and handed to her when she stepped between the ropes. As she parted her lips to speak, CDE's music cut her off. The camera panned to the stage again to show him with a wry grin and a paper in one hand, microphone in the other. Once the music was cut, his voice echoed throughout the arena like tournament halls. CDE: "I have in my hand a speech made ready by someone of great power in GIW. Your treatment of me, and other contenders in this company has been cause for great concern amongst this System you think you've constructed. Unfortunately for you, Terry, a system has been set in place well before you even took the helm. Now you and all of your conspirators like Charity Ryan and Reverend What's-his-voice and several others will be eradicated for a newer and better, wrestler oriented Galveston Island Wrestling. Luckily, being the forefront of this change, it benefits me greatly and hopefully a title shot will come of it, but for now, I'm going to let the new administrator of this organization speak their words through the letter they have asked me to recite." He unfolded the paper and took care to speak clearly the words written on it. A shot to Alexis showed that she was not amused by these developments to undermine her authority. She was about to interrupt him until she realized that her own microphone has been turned off. CDE: "Dear Alexis Terry, the board appreciates all that you've tried to do for Galveston Island Wrestling, but sadly the appreciation only goes so far. Your actions are clearly biased and you act as though you are still a competitor here, when you have stepped down from that position and stepped up to become the chief operating officer of this business. Consider this notice your resignation from administrative duties and reactivation onto the roster. Of course, understand that you'll be forced to take an extended break, a forced one, that is. When you choose to return, you'll be on active duty as a competitor. All decisions you've made tonight will be undone and further action against you, if you decide to retaliate, will come swiftly and without remorse. Lastly, if you are wondering who it is that has taken your job out from under you, I guess you'll just have to wait until Blackout." With the message sent, the camera cut to a closeup of Alexis Terry, a horrific expression stretched across her face with glow of anger to intensify her reaction to the grim news. The show then cut to the back, Tony Miranda's locker room where he sat at the wooden bench wrapping his wrists with white wrist tape. He was ready for the first of five matches that would determine the best of the best between he and Jack Waters. Once prepared, he got up from the bench and to the door of the room. Something stopped him, however, a note on the door. TONY MIRANDA: "What's this?" QUOTE As the main event, both men had their entrances and was extended as long as they needed to reach the ring. Tony removed his hood and entry attire after passing between the ropes. Soon it was just the two of them and the referee to mediate between them in this saga involving Tony Miranda versus Jack Waters in a best of five series. Tony was the first with the offensive. A few European uppercuts staggered opponent into the ropes where Tony used the advantage to send Jack running to the opposite end of the ring with an Irish whip. Upon return, Tony sealed the deal with a hard hit German suplex that seemed to echo throughout the arena when Jack impacted the white canvas. Tony proved to start fresh and swift as he got up and made a run for the ropes himself. Jack was quick to recoil from the attack, but as he turned to face his opponent, a running dropkick met him and sent him over the edge of the top rope. Jack didn’t fall to the matted floor, but instead found himself holding tight to the ropes to steady himself along the rim of the ring. Tony took his boots to Jack’s bared shoulder and tried to stomp him off to peel him away from the canvas, but Jack was resilient. He swiped at Tony’s heel to trip him, but only staggered his opponent. It was enough for him to get back onto his feet, steel himself, and then slip back in. Tony tried the quick-on-his-feet strategy, but Jack Waters countered with swinging neckbreaker put an end to Tony’s momentum. He went for the pin, but the referee could only get a two-count out of Tony Miranda. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “These two wanted this. They wanted to have these long, grueling series of matches.” RILEY STEVENS: "I don’t believe each match will be long, but the series of matches will be grueling on these two men. It’s going to be something they’ll use to learn one another’s movesets and find each other’s weaknesses. I don’t believe that the winner tonight will be the winner of the next week, or maybe even the victor of the war between these two in their best of five matches.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “It’s only telling how great these competitors are that this special series of events are being main evented and gifted to them; to the fans.” Back to the ring, Jack Waters had Tony at the turn post with his chest reddened by a flurry of hard backhand chops. The crowd was counting down with him and it eventually stopped at the count of ten. Tony’s chest quickly reddened like an apple. He winced, but retaliated with vigor. He caught Jack in a snap suplex, twisting it around to land away from the turn post and more inward toward the canvas. He went for the cover, but Jack refused and kicked out at the count of two. Tony prepped his opponent. He positioned him upon his knees and struck him with some high volume kicks, and hard hitting punches. Jack was soon dazed. Tony then returned to his swift strategy of using speed against Jack Waters by backing into the ropes to use as propulsion force to get him running toward his opponent. Upon impact, Tony smacked Jack in the head with his running leg lariat. He went for the cover, and the crowd were quiet. The referee called for one, and no reaction from Jack had come. The second number called and the crowd were on their feet. They expected him to kick out and carry on the match, but as the referee struck for three, Jack finally pushed his shoulder free of the canvas, but a second too late. He rolled over onto his side to break the pin, but Tony Miranda stole it. He was just lucky enough to strike the right nerve in Jack to get him down for the count, but he knew, by the very nature of this war he had gotten himself in, that Jack wasn’t about to let him celebrate completely. He had four more matches, and Jack’s reaction to the loss, pulling out of the ring in disbelief and eyes like daggers toward Tony, meant that he was ready for the next one. Until then, Tony held his hand high and the crowd celebrated with him. He had their respect, even Jack’s, but the war wasn’t over. With that in mind, he at least won the battle, and that, for the week, was enough. WINNERvia Pinfall: TONY MIRANDA! Immediately, the camera cut to the big screen, interrupting Tony's celebration. It showed a shadowy, albeit dimly lit room until Harlequin's face bled onto the screen like a nightmare. She cracked a smile with her painted lips and chuckled maniacally. The camera quickly panned out to show her holding the briefcase chest high and then she spoke. THE HARLEQUIN: “Sorry to interrupt your victory, Tony. Unfortunately for you, I've got something far more important than you playing with matches. Ha, see, all night long other people have been speaking for me, on my behalf!" She had quickly caught the attention of the audience and they were silent for her speech. THE HARLEQUIN: “Beck Ramsey wants me to be his little puppet, and Madison Seton wants me out of the way. Both of them underestimate me and both of them have been speaking for me all night long. Little do they know that I have a voice as well, and it's time to use it! I've spoken with the new administrator of operations in Galveston Island Wrestling and she's agreed that it's time to cash in! That's right, you heard me! This little game you two are playing is wearing my patience thin. You could have your little war against one another, or I could just come up and take that title away from you, and that's exactly what I'm going to do! At Blackout, it's going to be a triple threat match and I'm going to seize the moment, take my chances, and beat both of you. The only one that speaks for me is me, and I say it's time to collect, and that is undisputed!" The show faded to an image of the GIW logo, then to black, thus capping another week of GIW's REVOLT.
