GIW — Card

Revolt, 14 May 2012

14 May 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: MONDAY NIGHT REVOLT LIVE! From Jack Stephens Center in Little Rock, AR – May 14, 2012 BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! The crowd here in the Jack Stephens Center are on there feet and ready for a great night here on Monday Night Revolt as these fans ignore the fact that David Sinclair and Aspen Chaud are standing in the ring to excitedly cheer on the fact that the show is beginning as the new theme plays in the background but as it slowly fades these fans come back to the reality that standing in the ring is David Sinclair with a microphone and Aspen Chaud not to far away wearing a white dress with the title belt over her shoulder. DAVID SINCLAIR: “Welcome everyone to another edition of Monday Night Revolt! Last week we kicked off a tournament to declare a new contender for Aspen’s GIW Undisputed Championship and though it provided us with several exciting new stars getting wins over the likes of Matt Kluge, Alioth Starre, Faith Simpson, and Eliza Gray. But we look forward to the future where the winner will face off with Aspen Chaud at ‘Aggressive Expansion’ for the GIW Undisputed Championship. So let me introduce the participants in tonight’s matches Alex Castellanos, Daniel Starr, Phoenix Matsuda, and Ryan Cavallari!” These fans give a mixed bag to the announcing of the participants in the Semi’s tonight as the Revolt theme plays as out they come Daniel Starr leading the way with a big smirk on his face showing off in his ring gear already as Alex Castellanos follows behind him looking calm and collected, Phoenix Matsuda is then behind him with that same sly grin on his face and following everyone up is Ryan Cavallari with a smile on his face as all four step into the ring heading to separate corners. DAVID SINCLAIR: “These are the final four men in the tournament here tonight and all four are worthy of a shot at the belt but only one will be the next contender...” Sinclair is about mid-way through his next word when Starr reaches over for the second week taking the mic off of Sinclair as he walks away from him with a grin. DANIEL STARR: “Excuse me Boss let’s skip the formalities for a bit so that the new face of GIW can say a few words about this tournament. Last week while you all struggled to get victory I ran through Faith Simpson in quick fashion. I am on the poster for ‘Aggressive Expansion’ I have the most momentum and Aspen you are looking at the man who is next in line for a shot at your title.” Daniel throws out his arm to pose giving him some time to pose as he turns and then heads over to Ryan with a grin on his face. DANIEL STARR: “Good luck with that bitch of a wife by the way…” Cavallari looks to go on the attack but Daniel drops the mic rolling out of the week quickly throwing his arms up in innocence as Cavallari picks up the mic looking over the ropes at him. RYAN CAVALLARI: “Get back in the ring, let’s go.” Ryan offers for him to come back in and fight but Daniel stands on the outside shaking his head as Sinclair takes back the microphone from him as Aspen stands watching it all. DAVID SINCLAIR: “Calm down, calm down, calm down everybody. Starr, Cavallari win your matches and you can handle this little fight next week.” Ryan looks back at him then out at Starr nodding his head wanting just that as Alex is now the one asking for a mic from the outside which receives. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “I know I wasn’t expected to win this thing, but Aspen just know that I am only two more wins away from getting another crack at you. You know as well as I do that the last time we met it took Acquin, Terry, a title shot, and nearly breaking my ankle to beat me. This time I’ll make sure that I finish the job to end this reign of yours.” Alex lowers the mic staring right across the ring at Aspen who looks away not to make eye contact as its Phoenix that reaches out grabbing the mic from David. PHOENIX MATSUDA: “Excuse me…Poster boy, unlucky broom, guy with a vendetta, lets all shut up. Need I remind you that I am the man that defeated Alioth Starre last week? The supposed favorite to win this thing, the longest reigning champion ever? Listen up. Keep looking at yourself on the poster Daniel, get ready for your wedding Ryan, and go back into hiding Alex because when it’s all said and done I’ll be taking this bitch’s title.” These fans get a kick out of the last part as Aspen’s jaw drops as she screams something at Phoenix, then Alex, then Ryan, and then Starr the high pitched voice making it inaudible as we are taken from that to our first commercial of the night. QUOTE After the commercial break the two men who just sent messages at the start of the show remain in the ring now with Daniel Starr and Phoenix Matsuda on separate sides of the ring as the camera pans over to show Aspen Chaud sitting down at the commentating table. JACOB O’CONNELLY: “We are being joined here by Aspen Chaud for the first match of the night.” ASPEN CHAUD: “Hi Jacob glad to be here, scouting out my opponents.” They head back into the ring where these two men share a glance over at Aspen for a moment as the referee then turns and calls for the bell. DING DING DING! The two circle around one another to start the match but Matsuda starts things off with a Feint Roundhouse Kick followed by an eye poke as Starr tries to duck under it. Daniel backs up holding his eyes as the referee scolds Phoenix who uses it to grab Starr whipping him off the ropes where on the rebound Matsuda rushes at him nailing him with a Running Knee Strike to the jaw that Starr couldn’t see coming as he stumbles back into the corner. As Phoenix rushes at him Starr throws up his legs kicking him backwards then quickly heading to the second rope where he jumps off only to receive a Spear in mid-air that plants Daniel into the mat as Phoenix hops on top going for the cover 1…2…Kick Out. These fans seem reluctant to cheer either man instead just booing every move that’s landed by either man. RILEY STEVENS: “Phoenix starting out the match well.” As Starr gets up Matsuda goes for a Double Leg Takedown propelling him into some big punches repeatedly on the mat as Daniel takes a few then forces him off trying to get to his feet only for Phoenix to go for the Crane Kick out of no where but Starr ducks under it and rolls him up 1…Kick Out. That seems like the one chance Starr may have had as the momentum seems all on Phoenix’s side as the attack slows Daniel as he gets up allowing Phoenix to nail him with a Roundhouse Kick that stuns Starr long enough for him to jump up dragging Daniel to the ground with him using it to get right into a Gogoplata out of no where as Starr is stuck in the middle of the ring. Quickly Daniel starts to roll around trying to get free but it is locked in and Starr is forced to tap out! REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner…Phoenix Matsuda!” These fans seem impressed but through their boo’s you wouldn’t know it as Phoenix hops up to his feet as Starr holds his throat as he rolls out of the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Well there’s the winner Aspen, Phoenix Matsuda.” Aspen to busy looking at him in the ring to respond stares at him as Matsuda walks over to the ropes pointing out at Aspen displaying the belt around his waist as he backs up into the ring to celebrate the win as we go to break. WINNER: PHOENIX MATSUDA QUOTE After the wedding invites we return to the ring where Trayton Deluca is already waiting in the ring as “Above The Law” by Bad Meets Evil hits and the fans give a very mixed reaction still not to familiar with Jason Dave as he enters from the back heading to the ring seeming focused on the task at hand. RILEY STEVENS: “Last week these three men were in the Open Battle Royal together and now they meet in a Triple Threat match here on Revolt.” JACOB O’CONNELLY: “Some argue that the Open Battle Royal doesn’t truly show us who the better man is so this may help us determine that.” Dave jumps up the steps and heads into the ring and as his music fades “Addicted” by P.O.D hits the crowd still unfamiliar with Jamie Hayden as well but there is still a strong contingent that watched last week and have come to like new star who heads out towards the ring an impressive physical specimen. RILEY STEVENS: “This man Jamie Hayden was the victor last week so we’ll see if he can follow it up.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Not a fan…” Hayden high fives a few fans along the way then heads up the steps into the ring looking across at his two opponents the same as last week this time the stakes ten times higher as the referee steps in and calls for the bell. DING DING DING! The bell sounds and the three men from last weeks open battle royal are have taken center stage this week on Revolt. Deluca quickly wastes little time, wanting to make up for last week tries to catch Jason Dave off guard but gets met with a kick to the gut and The Reality Check II, a super kick that groggies Trayton followed by a springboard kick to the temple. Trayton is already out and Jason covers but only gets a one as Jamie was waiting in the corner. Jamie delivers a few hard rights to the side of Jason’s head. Jamie picks up Jason and delivers a nice looking Gutwrench Powerbomb and goes for the cover but only gets a two as Jason gets his shoulder up. Jamie quickly tries an ankle lock submission move but Jason kicks and wiggles his way out of it. Jason is quickly back to his feet and the two stare each other down, Jason realizing it was almost over there. RILEY STEVENS: “Battling to make an impression here tonight.” Trayton is back into the ring and this time it’s Jamie Hayden who makes quick work of him. Jamie hits the London’s Calling, a devastating Shouten move of Japanese origin. He covers but Jason is there to break it up. Jason is on the attack, pounding away at the back of Jamie. Quick snap DDT by Jason and he follows it up by a quick moonsault off the top rope. He covers Jamie but Jamie is able to get his foot on the bottom rope at 2. Jason drags Jamie to the middle of the ring and tries to a sharpshooter. Jason isn’t known for his submission skills but it’s obvious he is attempting everything tonight. Jamie is able to escape it quickly, rolling up Jason in the process into a pinning predicament. Jason kicks out at two and a half. RILEY STEVENS: “Jamie and Jason are really battling out there.” Once more, Trayton staggers to his feet. Jamie and Jason are also up and even though they have been at each other’s throats the entire match manage to hit a double snap suplex. Jason then turns his attention back to Jamie and attempts a twist of fate. Jamie crashes to the mat but smartly rolls out of the ring so Jason is unable to pin him. Jason scales the turnbuckle. The crowd boos but Jason just shrugs it off, not really caring what the fans think. He dives off with a beautiful Diving Corkscrew Senton but Jamie moves out of the way and Jason crashes into the steel guardrail in a sick looking fashion. The crowd erupts into a “Holy Shit” chant. JACOB O’CONNELLY: “That’s going to leave a mark.” Jamie rolls slowly back into the ring. Trayton is back up and swings wildly at Jamie. Jamie ducks. Jamie grabs Trayton’s leg and shoves him to the mat. Jamie applies an ankle lock and Trayton quickly taps out from the pain. Jamie Hayden is victorious two weeks in a row here in GIW. REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner…Jamie Hayden!” These fans cheer it was clear as the match went along it was Hayden that they wanted to win and for the second straight week he gets the win despite some fight from Dave as the referee raises his arm. RILEY STEVENS: “Well it looks like Hayden proved last week was no fluke, huge win for him tonight.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Whatever…” Hayden walks around the ring with a smile on his face having picked up his second win here in GIW this time putting the company on notice. WINNER: JAMIE HAYDEN To the back we go where Matt Kluge is seen stepping out of his car walking out with a bag of probably wrestling gear heading towards the back entrance however as he heads toward it; Jamal Jackson and another member of the Security team stop him as he goes to enter the door. MATT KLUGE: “Jamal big guy what’s going on?” Jamal looks around shaking his head as he answers him, looking a bit sad. JAMAL JACKSON: “Sinclair told us to keep you out, says you have the night off.” Kluge chuckles a bit knowing why he is but having a laugh any how. MATT KLUGE: “Night off huh? Well I’ll be here next week then Jamal, could use a quick break. Just tell O’Connelly to be ready, have a good night.” Matt gives him a pat on the shoulder as he turns heading back towards his car seeming to be planning something as he goes. QUOTE Back after a commercial break we are again shown a video of Alexis’s invites as we enter the ring with Alioth Starre, ‘GIW Television Champion’ James Dean, and Sean Mendez in their corner as "Bad Romance" by Lady Gaga hits as the fans begin to boo and it only escalates when they watch as Aspen Chaud, Alexis Terry, and Eliza Gray walk out side by side heading towards the ring the three of them together brings out perhaps the loudest of boo’s yet. RILEY STEVENS: “This is too much…” JACOB O’CONNELLY: “They are like a super team, unstoppable.” The three head up the steps posing on the side for a moment as they walk around the corner Alexis and Aspen holding up their belts along the side as Eliza enters the ring as on the other side Dean decides to start as the referee calls for the bell. DING DING DING! James Dean and Eliza Gray circle around one another until they go in for a lock up and of course Dean starts to back her up slowly but surely into the corner where he tosses her and goes to get on the attack but Gray crushes him with a slap that throws him off his game. Angered by the slap he throws a big right hand that missed as Eliza ducks out of the way grabbing his Wrist and tearing it behind his back with the ‘Deep in Debt’ a Wrist Lock that tears away at Dean’s wrist as these fans boo her as on the side of the ring we can see Auva Skye in the crowd motioning towards Alioth who jumps down off of the ring apron then jumps the ring barricade following her out of the building the crowd booing him as Dean watches him go as Eliza continues to tear away at Dean’s wrist in the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Alioth just seems to have bailed on the match?” That’s how it seems as Eliza uses the hold of the wrist to drag Dean towards the corner where she reaches out tagging in Alexis who steps into the ring crushing Dean with a kick to the stomach doubling him over as she follows it up with a Tornado DDT that plants Mendez into the mat. Terry gets up with a smirk on her face taking the chance to pose for the crowd and her team mates in the corner who cheer her on as James gets back to his knees as she bounces off the ropes and puts him right back down with a Shining Wizard that nearly takes his head off as he falls to the mat and Alexis takes a moment to hold up her arm and pin him 1…2…Kick Out. Dean just barely gets up his shoulder as Alexis gets up to her feet rolling her eyes at the fact he didn’t just take the pin right then and there as she hoists him up only for him to desperately push Alexis away and jump tagging in Mendez. RILEY STEVENS: “Desperate tag there.” Sean enters the ring rushing at Alexis only for her to give him a drop toe hold in the corner sending him face first into the corner. Terry starts to deliver a few boots to him in the corner as the referee drags her away as Aspen and Eliza use the distraction to deliver kicks through the ropes as well to the downed Sean stopping as the referee turns around as Alexis walks over tagging in Aspen this time as she steps in and tosses Sean into the corner as Terry steps to the side as Chaud runs at him hitting him with a ‘Tush Push’ a Turnbuckle Flying Butt Shot and as he stumbles out of the corner Alexis rushes forward giving him a Bulldog! These fans are not happy with how dominant they are looking as Alexis steps out of the ring and Aspen kicks him while he’s down. JACOB O’CONNELLY: “We all knew they would look this good.” Sean gets to his hands and knees starting to get up but Aspen rushes over wrapping her arm around his neck then her legs around his stomach from behind as he starts to get up to his feet with her on his back in what she calls the ‘Champion Lock’ a Standing Dragon Sleeper w/ Body Scissors! These fans are hoping for a kick out and sensing danger Dean jumps back into the ring rushing to stop it but Eliza and Alexis jump in clotheslining him before he gets there. Sean falls back to his knees as James gets back up to his feet and Eliza goes low with a Russian Leg Sweep and Alexis hits a Roundhouse to take out Dean as Sean finally gives in and taps out. REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here are your winners…Alexis Terry, Aspen Chaud, and Eliza Gray!” These fans boo though it may have been expected considering how good of friends these three are that they’d make a good team as Aspen pushes off of Sean and joins Eliza and Alexis hugging one another. RILEY STEVENS: “You know I want to say something bad but I can’t, they make a good team.” JACOB O’CONNELLY: “That’s for sure, looked amazing tonight.” Dean and Sean roll out of the ring as Alexis, Aspen, and Eliza raise each other’s hands high into the air International Glamour getting the win here tonight. WINNERS: ALEXIS TERRY, ASPEN CHAUD, & ELIZA GRAY Backstage we see Daniel Starr we see him tossing around some bags and such in the catering area not seeming happy at all with the loss tonight as Jamie Hayden walks in talking to a staff member as he enters the area. JAMIE HAYDEN: “Thanks though was a good win tonight.” The staff members walk off as Starr turns around looking at him laughing a bit. DANIEL STARR: “What you think your good now cause you got a couple of wins in GIW? Doesn’t mean a thing until you stepped up against top competition, like myself.” Hayden holds out his hands not meaning to offend him but Starr a little testy seems to just be wanting to argue with somebody. JAMIE HAYDEN: “Never said that, I look forward to stepping up against some top talent in the future.” Jamie doesn’t have any real anger in his voice at all but Daniel doesn’t seem to be willing to let him off to easy. DANIEL STARR: “Good cause you may get it sooner then you expected.” Starr walks off shoving down a water bottle as he goes as Hayden watches him go wondering how that just happened. ==== Else where backstage we find Aspen Chaud, Alexis Terry, and Eliza Gray finding their way into the back all happy with their win huge smiles across their face. ALEXIS TERRY: “That was to easy! Did you see the way Alioth ran?” The three share a laugh, sending some into flash backs of the preppy girls in High School as they all continue to speak up. ELIZA GRAY: "We so embarrassed them it was easy! Dean so had that coming." These three girls have to be heard by everyone in the back all so happy with their win. ASPEN CHAUD: “Get the win and the wedding invites were sent, what a night. Let’s go celebrate…” But before they can get going Zachary Pagonis walks over to them all three of them stopping and glaring at him as in unison they all ask “What do you want?” ZACHARY PAGONIS: “I was told to come by and inform Eliza that due to this win her title shot has been moved up you’ll be facing James Dean next week for the Television title.” Pagonis says this though he clearly doesn’t want to as he walks off as the girls look back at Eliza and the three jump around screaming as we head back to the ring. QUOTE It’s time for the Main Event of the evening and these fans are ready to go as they are first met with the sounds of "Did It On 'Em" by Nicki Minaj hits as the fans begin to boo as the champion again heads from the back fresh off a mini-celebration with Eliza and Alexis as she walks around the ring heading to the announce table, as we then get a glimpse of Phoenix Matsuda who has also come down to ring side only he is in a chair far away from the announce table. JACOB O’CONNELLY: “Wow we are blessed with Aspen’s presence not once but twice tonight, welcome back Aspen.” ASPEN CHAUD: “Hi again guys just thought that if I watched the first one may as well watch this one as well.” Aspen gets her mic situated as her music ends and “Wayward One” by Alterbridge hits and these fans go wild with cheers as Alex Castellanos walks out from the back after his recent return and win last week walking down towards the ring all business as usual as he heads up the steps into the ring. ASPEN CHAUD: “Can you imagine if he got another title shot? I already beat him what else is there to prove?” RILEY STEVENS: “This tournament is set up to find the best contender so if he wins, he’s the most worthy.” Aspen can be heard scoffing as Alex heads to the top rope to pose as his music fades and “Brand New Day” by Ryan Star hits to a loud applause from this crowd as well. While the Starr and Matsuda match left the fans with nobody to root for this one has caused a bit of a split amongst the crowd as Ryan Cavallari heads out from the back looking down at Alex in the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “What would your thoughts be about facing your best friend’s fiancé in the final?” ASPEN CHAUD: “It’s not ideal but I’ll root for him here tonight.” Ryan now heads into the ring going to the top rope as seemingly the other half of the crowd cheer him on as Castellanos stretches out in his corner as the referee sets up and calls for the bell. DING DING DING! Alex Castellanos and Ryan Cavallari are standing in the ring. Both are pretty sore from the harsh matches they had to go through last week. Ryan extends his hand to shake Alex's hand to show sportsmanship. This was a rematch happening. Alex invites Ryan over into a grapple. Ryan goes over and Alex quickly changes plans and drops to both his knees pulling the back of Ryan's left leg and taking him down to the canvas. Alex shifts his body over to where his chest is pressing down against Ryan's. He made sure both of Ryan's shoulders were down on the matt. The referee counts ONE....- Ryan gets his left shoulder up at one. He wanted Alex to know he'd have to go through hell to win against him. RILEY STEVENS: “This is a match everyone has been looking forward to.” Alex kept his right forearm pressed down on Ryan's nose. Ryan moves his body and tried to turn on his stomach quickly. It worked. Ryan was able to push Alex on his back and quickly pull both of Alex's legs up for a jackknife pin. The referee counts again ONE! But Alex kicks out of that tightly secured pin attempt by Ryan. Alex rolls a few times so he is out of Cavallari's hit range. Ryan charges Alex and Ryan's right shoulder connects with Alex's stomach. But Ryan's momentum causes both men to go through the second rope and right down to the outside ground. Fans were screaming "ALEX COME ON ALEX!!! WE LOVE YOU!!!!!" And others were going "RYAN!!! RYANN!!! HE'S! OUR! MAN IF HE CAN'T DO IT NO ONE CAN!!!" The referee counts 1...2....3...4....5....6.... Both men were still down. RILEY STEVENS: “These fans are split down the middle for these two men.” Alex and Ryan get up and they exchange punches. Ryan hits Alex in the chin and Alex hit right dead in the nose. Ryan gets back into the ring at eight and Alex BARELY makes it in at nine. Ryan tries to stomp on Alex while he was on his knees but Alex rolls to the side. Ryan runs at him once more, Alex hits him with the ‘Golden Arrow’ sending him down right on his back. But before Alex could cover Ryan, Ryan rolls out of the ring. It was instinct. People could see that Alex wasn't too happy about that. Ryan finally starts to get up. His mouth was bleeding. RILEY STEVENS: “Alex hit him with the Golden Arrow but Ryan was able to smartly roll out of the ring.” Alex grabs the top rope with both of his hands. He was ready to go springboard. The fans get to their feet. Ryan is up and Alex pulls back on the rope before leaping up straight and landing both his feet on the top rope. He jumps off and dives straight down wrapping both his legs around Ryan's neck and doing a head scissors takedown. Ryan is sent flying. He holds his neck in pain. Alex is in control. Alex walks over to Ryan and lifts him up by his chin and the top of his head. He throws Ryan back into the ring. Alex goes for yet another springboard. He leaps up and tries to hit an elbow to the top of Ryan's head. But Ryan jumps up and grabs Alex's head placing his knees right in Alex's face as they both go down. Once Ryan's back hit the canvas his knees smashed into Alex's face sending him flying on his back. After hitting the double knee facebreaker he covers Alex one....two...thre- Alex gets his shoulders up right before the three and Ryan is surprised. RILEY STEVENS: “What a battle between these two!” Ryan crawls behind Alex and lock him in a tight chin lock with his knee pressed against Alex's back. One Alex's blonde fans could be seen crying because of what Alex had to go through. Alex fights back to his feet not wanting to give up. Cavallari pushes Alex forwards and goes up to grab him again. Alex snaps his right foot back hitting yet another ‘Golden Arrow’ Superkick. RILEY STEVENS: “Another Golden Arrow!” Ryan falls right down to the canvas. Alex waves to the crowd as they are behind him. He drops down and hooks one of Ryan's legs one......two.......Ryan actually kicks out of the golden arrow. Alex places both of his hands on his head wondering how that could have happened. He gets up and locks Ryan in a single leg Boston crab. Ryan fights it and turns it around to lock Alex in a small package pin. One....two.....Alex pulls it back to pin Ryan one....two....Ryan pulls it back again to pin Alex...one....two....Alex kicks out and both men get up. Ryan tries a high impact clothesline. Alex ducks and wraps both his arms around the front of Ryan's neck and brings him down for a falling Neckbreaker. Alex goes to the corner and climbs to the top rope. ASPEN CHAUD: “This is a mistake.” Alex dives off and once again Ryan counters by jumping and extending both his legs to bash into Alex's abs sending him back. Alex fights to get back up. Both men are tired. They charge each other and Ryan tackles Alex right into the referee. The referee is down and out as Alex gets up to his feet where he is hit by ‘You Give Love a Bad Name’ a Flying Knee Smash! These fans go wild as Alex hits the ground but the ref is still out. Cavallari tries to pull him up but the ref is groggy so he goes back on the attack but Alex on his knee pushes him away as Aspen jumps up off the announce table jumping onto the ring apron with her title using it to hit Alex but Ryan charges and Castellanos ducks under his punch and the belt smacks Ryan in the face the momentum sending Aspen to the ground as well. RILEY STEVENS: “Aspen just missed and hit Ryan!” Still groggy from the knee Alex doesn’t even have a clue Aspen just tried that as Ryan stumbles back setting himself up as Castellanos hits him with a third ‘Golden Arrow’ this time with the title shot leading up to it as Alex gets down on top of him as the referee crawls over 1…2…3! REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner…Alex Castellanos!” These fans begin to boo the ending leaving Alex even more confused about it all not knowing why they’d be booing it as the referee raises his arm in victory as Aspen gets up walking around the ring heading over to the other side as Ryan rolls out of the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “I think Aspen was trying to use the title belt on Alex but he ducked under and Ryan took it instead, Alex still doesn’t look like he knows what happened.” JACOB O’CONNELLY: “Total accident!” Ryan rolls out of the ring holding his head in pain as he stumbles backwards up the ramp as Aspen tries to explain to him what happened but he doesn’t seem to be hearing it as he tells her to leave as back in the ring Phoenix Matsuda has entered the ring standing behind Alex who turns around ready to fight but Phoenix simply stares him down. RILEY STEVENS: “Regardless that is out final Alex Castellanos versus Phoenix Matsuda for a shot at Aspen’s title.” We get a close up between the two staring each other down as the camera angle then switches back to Aspen looking in the ring at the two as we look over her shoulder as we have reached the finals of this tournament. WINNER: ALEX CASTELLANOS
