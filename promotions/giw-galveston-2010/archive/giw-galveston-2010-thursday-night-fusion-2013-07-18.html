Thursday Night Fusion, 18 July 2013

18 July 2013

GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: THURSDAY NIGHT FUSION LIVE! From Porretto Beach – July 18, 2013 QUOTE It’s now time for the opening match up here on Revolt and for Robbie Hart it’s a chance to make a big debut against a former GIW star as Pandora the former Television Champion, is in the ring looking to get a few bucks for her baby back in home as she gets ready in the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Robbie Hart making his debut tonight, think he’ll put on a show?” AMANDA ROWLAND: “He’s cute.” DING DING DING! Match Summary In this match it was clear that Pandora wasn’t really into it anymore just looking for some money while Hart didn’t show any mercy from the get-go as he abused the former champion for coming in out of shape in this opening match up. It was never really close, as despite her extra out of shape weight, Hart tossed her around with ease as these fans were reserved in making their judgment about Robbie. But in the end they couldn’t be too harsh as he was flawless in the ring as the end came when he landed a Sidewalk Slam and followed it up with ‘The Solution’ a Corkscrew 450 landing Knees first on Pandora, a move that dropped the jaws of most the crowd before he made the cover. REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner by way of pinfall…Robbie Hart!” After the match Hart showed a bit of enthusiasm for the win but didn’t seem too eager to celebrate this win, knowing this was just the beginning as he’d get a much bigger leap up the ladder in the next few weeks. WINNER: ROBBIE HART QUOTE After a short break here on Fusion we return to the ring for the weekly staple match for the Deep South Championship. “New Killer Star” by David Bowie hits meaning the former two time Undisputed Champion and current Deep South Champion, Alioth Starre was heading to the ring with his Deep South belt over his shoulder as he looked into the ring at Angelle Laree and Krishna Ivs who are ready for a chance to make the debut of a lifetime. RILEY STEVENS: “Standing in the ring we have Angelle and Krishna looking back at one of GIW’s all time greats, as they prepare to take his belt in their debut’s.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Oh please, please make Alioth look like a loser.” Alioth doesn’t seem willing to give these girls more then a glance while they don’t take their eyes off him as he hands over his belt as he realizes how hard it has been for anyone to defend this belt thus far. As he poses with it for a moment, just to show the two who’s champion before the referee starts the match. DING DING DING! Only two came to take up the challenge for the Deep South Championship title, currently held by former Undisputed Champion Alioth Starre. Rhiannon and Angelle Laree, two women that were eager to jump in with both feet and rise up against all for the title. Though it was listed as two females against a single male, it didn’t suggest that there were any allies based on gender, or alignment. The match was triple threat by means of population. Three entered and only one could leave with the championship, or defend it. It was up to them as the bell rang. Rhiannon and Laree started together against Starre, as a sort of uneasy unit to take on the champion and weaken him before they could capture the title for either one of them. If the greatest threat was annihilated, then the odds were in their favor. Alioth was certainly the better of two evils for either female in the bout. With that headed into the match, Rhiannon took Alioth with a whip across the ring, which set up Laree for a follow-up attack, but it was reversed when she bent down, hoping to toss her opponent. Not shy about the equality of gender roles in the company, Alioth stopped mid run and struck Laree in the chest with his boot. She snapped back up and he followed the assault with a clothesline. A knee drop later and he rolled out of the way to let Rhiannon break the truce with an elbow drop on Laree. RILEY STEVENS: “There goes that.” There was nothing between her and Starre, for he grasped at her head and sent her diving between the ropes. With her out of the ring, it allowed him to focus on commencing the punishment upon Laree. Nothing too drastic, of course, just a strained knee bar, transitioned into an ankle lock, then hoisted her up and applied more pain to the area with a shin breaker. He worked her legs, but never climbed up her smooth, timid thighs. He valued the region from knee to ankle and made sure to stomp, bar, or apply a hold to that space as much as he could before Rhiannon found the ire to jolt back into the ring. It didn’t take long for her to lunge forth to strike them both, Alioth tried to dodge, but was struck oddly despite. He favored his wrist, which had gotten struck by Rhiannon, but he shook it off. Laree, still on the ground kicked out of a quick pin attempt from Rhiannon, but that didn’t mean she’d be given the chance to breath and recuperate. That it was what the case seemed, until Starre dragged Rhiannon between his knees and used her body as an object for his piledriver. The move left her flattened and holding her neck, as if to have nicked it in the fall. She was fine, however, fine enough to kick out of a pinfall attempt. RILEY STEVENS: “Nice kick out there.” With neither female conceding so far, Starre opted to pull out the best he could on both, assuming they didn’t rise up against him like they had tried at the start of the match. He conceived the notion of a backfire, but opted to go ahead and apply his signature Figure-Four leglock submission hold on Laree. It was her knee and ankle he had started to wear down and so he figured she would be the best bet to have submit. She didn’t, at least not at first. With the referee screaming wildly in her ear like some deranged maniac intent on knowing if she was going to tap, she held out her hand, screamed out no, and the punishment continued. Rhiannon finally turned to see what was happening and motioned to her knees and crawled toward the mess entangled within the center of the ring, where Alioth had dragged Laree. The confrontation seemed unstoppable. The conceived notion of a concise strike Rhiannon could make landed and knocked the hold ajar. Starre kicked away to save himself from further attack as Rhiannon to a pin attempt over Laree, but she managed to escape yet again. Laree then rolled to her knees, struck Rhiannon at the abdomen and continued to until she was able to work herself up to her feet. She eyed Alioth and sent Rhiannon toward him with a quick running whip. RILEY STEVENS: “And here it comes…” He struck her hard with a facebreaker knee smash that downed the woman like a flattened pair of pancakes. Laree didn’t dive in, as Alioth expected, to pin her, instead she lunged for him to contend against him for the title. He was caught surprised by her attack and found himself upturned outside of the ring. This was Laree’s moment to shine and she took it. She rushed back to Rhiannon, banged the woman’s head against the mat and took to another pin attempt that would hopefully signal her victory and championship status as the Deep South Champion, but Rhiannon kicked out after the second call. RILEY STEVENS: “That was nearly it.” He commenced his return and converged into the center of the ring where Laree met the contentious Alioth. He conspired to drop her upon her knee with a second shin breaker, and then finally reapplied the Figure-Four Leglock. It was almost too late for Laree when Rhiannon intervened. The three converged into one mess, but Rhiannon pulled away first. Alioth second, but performed a quick facebreaker knee smash to end all of Rhiannon’s whims. She landed flat on her back and had even rolled to the rim of the ring just as Starre turned to pin Laree for the last time. A solid victory was all he needed to defend the title and that’s exactly what he had achieved. The match was his, and the title still to be placed around his waist. Rhiannon and Laree had put up what they could in the fight, but ultimately it was the determination to hold onto the Deep South Championship that let Alioth take hold and begin his new title reign. REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner and STILL GIW Deep South Champion…Alioth Starre!” These fans go wild as despite two great showings from the debuting Angelle and Krishna, it was just too much too soon as Alioth Starre retains the Deep South Championship for the first time, still no easy feat as he is handed his belt back again, one of two he’s got his eyes on as he poses with the belt mostly for the fans as we take a small break before the Main Card. WINNER: ALIOTH STARRE (Still GIW Deep South Champion)
