GIW — Card

Revolt, 7 July 2011

7 July 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: THURSDAY NIGHT REVOLT LIVE! From Stewart Beach – July 7th, 2011 BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! The opening fireworks go off with the crowd in attendance roaring with cheers, as they are ready to go on this monumental night here in GIW. Ready to start the show with a big, but soon “Subterraneans” by David Bowie hits sending the crowd into boos as they hate on the man that at ‘Category 5’ retained his GIW Undisputed Championship in a war with Trent Lost. Walking out with the belt, he casually walks down to the ring with a grin on his face stepping through the ropes and heading up the steps and into the ring letting the fans boos die down. ALIOTH STARRE: “Thank you for that warm reception…you see I understand why your angry I defeated another one of your little heroes. This time it was Trent Lost erasing one of my few defeats here in GIW. Now I look on to bigger and better things as I will head into ‘Moment of Glory II’ with this belt around my waist, where I will meet Aspen Chaud one on one finally, now before I continue…” Before he can further talk the fans ears off, “Falling Away” by Curse Icon hits as the crowd roars in approval for Trent Lost still supporting him even after his loss. Walking out onto the stage he heads down towards the ring, staring up at Alioth as he heads up the steps and through the ropes with a mic of his own. TRENT LOST: “You know I wanted to come out here and congratulate on the win, you beat me this time. But last I looked that put us at one and one. So while I will wait for my next opportunity I want to make sure you’re focused on tonight’s match because I know that you hate Aspen just as much as I do which means you hate her new group Absolution just as much.” The crowd boos loudly at the mention of Absolution as Lost looks out at them nodding as Alioth steps forward with a cocky grin. ALIOTH STARRE: “Please never bring in to question my effort level again. I am more worried about you not with holding your end of the bargain tonight.” While these two get into each others faces clearly unable to work together "Wayward One" by Alter Bridge hits as out from the back to the crowd’s boos is Aspen Chaud with a microphone as well as Alex Castellanos with one of his own and The Shadow trailing them looking menacing. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “You know it’s sort of sad isn’t it Aspen. That when you take away the three of us the two men in the ring are the best two available when it comes to Team GIW. Alioth is an overrated poem speaker while Trent hasn’t won since the date read BC. And these two are suppose to beat us? Come on.” Alex lets out a small chuckle as Aspen steps beside him putting her hand up onto his shoulder and speaking. ASPEN CHAUD: “You know it’ll be enjoyable when Team GIW, aka the team Blake Beckett chose himself falls at our feet! The reason we don’t represent GIW any more is because…We are not a part of GIW any more! And thus, I hope you two are ready to try and make friendly tonight long enough so that it won’t be too easy for the three of us to beat you…But I doubt it. We just wanted to come out here and wish you both good luck…you’ll need it more then we do.” Aspen laughs and then turns and heads the other way with her partners in crime while Alioth and Trent turn to one another talking back and forth seemingly unable to agree on things. RILEY STEVENS: “Well ladies and gentleman, Alioth Starre and Trent Lost will team with Bam Isaac Glazer later tonight against Team MWA and Absolution and I can’t help but think Absolution is on the same page unlike the rest of them…Also tonight we have a Fatal Four Way for the GIW Television Championship and so much more…Stay tuned.” Before we go to break a video clip begins to play showing fighters from last years Seize the Moment match trying to climb the ladder to win the match. Suddenly a voice over begins to say “Six Men Enter…One Man Will Climb the Ladder to Win a Shot at the Undisputed Title…Whenever He Wants It.” The video ends with Johnny Stylez grabbing the Briefcase as, the lights go out and we go to break. QUOTE After the commercial break we return with Anthony Brenske already in the ring alone as “Become The Catalyst” by All That Remains hits as some cheers emanate from the crowd as David Graves walks out from the back heading towards the ring looking as focused as ever as he high fives some fans before heading up the steps. RILEY STEVENS: “We have just gotten word that this match will indeed be for a spot in this year’s Seize the Moment match up. This is a huge opportunity for both men to earn their way into a match where really…they can become a threat to the Undisputed Champion at any time.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I thought there were no short cuts in wrestling…” Graves enters the ring looking across at Brenske who stares at him with a grin on his face, as the referee calls for the bell. DING DING DING! Brenske walks across the ring taunting him a bit, by offering him a free shot. At first Graves pretends to shy away from it but then he takes a step then POW, Superkick to the chin that knocks Brenske down. The crowd cheers as David calls for Brenske to get up, which obviously he can’t hear. Anthony hops up and spins around right into a big Uppercut that sends him stumbling backwards. Running at him he lands a Diving Clothesline that takes him to the mat. Both men hop up; Graves first then he delivers a beautiful looking follow up Hurricanrana! The crowd cheering him on as he hops up and screams out at them, before turning around and starting a slow clap as they wait for Anthony to find his senses. Finally he does so, as Graves goes for a big clothesline. Brenske is able to duck it at the last moment seeming relieved as he turns around however when he does Graves is waiting with a huge ‘Heat Stroke’ a Jumping Corkscrew Roundhouse Kick! That sends the crowd into a frenzy as he makes the cover 1…2…3! That’s it already. RILEY STEVENS: “Wow what a match, already finished here tonight. David Graves made quick work, of Anthony Brenske and punched his ticket to Moment of Glory II.” Graves hops to his feet, letting the referee raise his hand as he seems to realize just how big of a moment this is for him as the fans cheer him on, taking a liking to his wrestling style. WINNER: DAVID GRAVES (Qualifies for Seize the Moment) Heading to the back we go again this time we head to see Aspen Chaud having gone back to the hotel across the street to change getting out of her bag, in her hotel room when there is a knock at the door. ASPEN CHAUD: “Come in.” Not bothering to look up, she continues to dig through her bag as walking in is Ace Baldwin wielding the MWA Championship he won just last night. ACE BALDWIN: “So…I know you already know and all, but I just wanted to remind you that I am fine tonight and that I’ll be ready for our match later.” Aspen rolls her eyes, she and Ace had sort of had a little rivalry going and she knew as soon as he won last night he’d be here to rub it in. ASPEN CHAUD: “Oh that’s cute, it’s a real belt. I thought with your companies lack of money it would be a little replica belt. Hope they didn’t take that out of your minimum wage salary.” Aspen chuckles at her joke before continuing. ASPEN CHAUD: “Now if you don’t mind, I need to change.” Ace instead does not move. ACE BALDWIN: “Do I really got to leave?” Aspen looks up as The Shadow walks into the room, standing behind him perhaps having heard as Ace turns around and looks up at him. ASPEN CHAUD: “I think it’s best you do.” Baldwin turns around, readjusting his belt as he looks at the big man before turning and walking out of the room realizing the numbers as The Shadow angrily watches him go as we continue on with the show. QUOTE Again after the break we return to the ring where already in the waits is Damien Dunn and Paul Montuori both of which look ready to go. Soon “Lift Me Up” by Moby hits as the crowd goes wild as out from the back coming together is Matt Kluge and Mark Cross two of the fans favorites. RILEY STEVENS: “These two have teamed up a few times now and it appears they are set for action for action again together.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I heard Cross wants to make a tag title run!” The crowd continues to cheer as the two men enter the ring heading to their corner as Dunn and Montuori argue over in there’s the referee calls for the bell, regardless. DING DING DING! As the two are arguing, in their corner over who will start Kluge and Cross make the decision quickly as Kluge enters the ring. He looks across the ring at his opponents waiting for them to make a decision. Finally after a bit of wait; Kluge runs between them and lands a slap to Paul then turns blocking a punch from Dunn and then slapping him as well. Angrily Paul throws a haymaker but Kluge ducks it and he instead hits Damien. Dunn stumbles out into the ring, as Kluge jumps up and hits a Dropkick that sends Paul through the ropes and out of the ring. The crowd cheers him for getting rid of one of them as he turns and walks over grabbing Dunn and whipping him off the ropes. Damien stops on a dime in front of Kluge, but he does so right into a ‘Hortonville Suplex Special 1’ a Suplex Rolled into Mounted Punching that sends the crowd into a frenzy. Dunn pushes him off as Kluge backs off, waiting for a big punch that comes that he ducks then giving him a German Suplex! While he’s down he makes the tag out to Cross who steps in, only for an angry Paul Montuori to slide into the ring trying to disrupt the beating. Instead he walks right into an Exploder Suplex from Mark Cross. As soon as Dunn gets up he receives an Exploder Suplex of his own, as he calls for the fans to get up onto their feet which Kluge urges as well. RILEY STEVENS: “These two are rolling!” Picking between the two Cross goes for Paul who is getting up first as he grabs him by the back of his trunks spinning then throwing him through the middle ropes to get the illegal man out. Spinning around he grabs Dunn just as he’s getting up and whips him into his corner where he tags in Kluge again. Cross holds him in place as Kluge goes to the top, with the fans on their feet he jumps off and lands a Missile Dropkick! The crowd goes wild as he makes the cover 1…2…Kick Out! Just not quite enough, he gets to his feet turning around and waiting for Dunn to get back up. Making the tag to Cross just before he lifts up Dunn then puts him down a Northern Lights Suplex. Cross steps in and looks ready to go, waiting for him to get up. However unwilling to follow the rules Paul Montuori again heads into the ring, attacking Cross just as he gets him up. Matt Kluge steps in, spinning him around, kicking him in the gut right into the set up for ‘The Tiger's Roar’ a Double Underhook Piledriver, that puts Paul out. Just as he does this Cross has improvised hitting a ‘Ketteiteki Desaki’ a Tiger Driver '91 on Dunn instead before making the cover 1…2…3! Game over. RILEY STEVENS: “Can’t say I didn’t expect this Matt Kluge and Mark Cross make a very good team. Making quick work of these two guys here tonight.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “When it Walker back?” The two men wait as the referee raises their arms into the air as they turn and shake one another’s hands before exiting the ring and heading towards the back. Almost as soon as they head to the back, some commotion can be heard as DeShawn Banks the man who debuted earlier on ETV heads down to the ring, sliding in and looking down at Dunn with a grin. RILEY STEVENS: “What in the world is he doing out here?” Banks looks out at the crowd as he walks to the corner bounces up then hits the ‘Credibility Statement’ a Split-Legged Corkscrew Moonsault that both awes the fans and makes them boo for the dirty time he chose to do it. He stands up grinning and calling for a mic when the crowd goes wild as out from the back comes Hank Mardukas heading down into the ring sending Banks on his way out fleeing as Hank just gets into the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Here comes Hank to stop this kid from doing any more damage.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “The kid was just trying to make a statement.” Hank looks down at Dunn and while he more then likely doesn’t care for him, he clearly respects when and where you make a statement as he stares up at Banks who backs his way out of there heading to the back as we more then likely have not seen the last of these two together. WINNERS: MARK CROSS & MATT KLUGE Heading behind the entrance area the camera catches Matt Kluge just as he gets back there. Asking for a water from a staffer he receives one when Leo James a relatively new star walks over to him with a smile on his face. LEO JAMES: “Listen man, I’ve seen you the last few weeks just wanted to say that I want to be the other half of the tag team champions with you. Call me crazy, I know I am pretty new here but frankly you need a partner and I think the two of us would make a pretty good team.” Kluge looks at him a little surprised about a lot of what he said, as he took a swig of the water then set it down. MATT KLUGE: “Took some balls to come and ask that. Frankly I do need a tag team partner…but I have not yet seen what you’re capable of. So before I go handing out belts to some brave guy I’ve never met…You win your match tonight and you can have the other half of these belts.” James nods his head, realizing this is a fair and huge opportunity as Leo heads towards the ring for his match as Kluge takes another swig of water as he watches him go holding onto one half of the GIW Tag Team Championships. QUOTE When we return to the ring following the trend of the night, Nick Riot is already in the ring in waits as “El Dorado” by Iron Maiden hits as the crowd responds with some cheers still relatively new to Leo James who walks out with a focused look as he heads towards the ring, high fiving a few fans along the way. RILEY STEVENS: “Interesting that we now know if Leo James is to win, he will become half of the GIW Tag Team Champions.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Jeez’, first Seize the Moment now this are we handing out opportunities.” James steps into the ring looking across the ring at his opponent as the referee turns and calls for the bell. DING DING DING! Realizing what is at stake, James waits as Riot explodes out of the corner trying to get an early advantage only to run straight into a Big Boot from Leo. The crowd is clearly on his side, they’ve grown a hatred towards Riot over the last few weeks. James lifts Riot up manually and spins and whips him into the corner, running at him he catches him with a Clothesline in the corner, rather then pulling away he locks his arms around him and then spins holding on and then doing a Belly to Belly Suplex that tosses Nick across the ring, getting up he calls for the fans to get up as he gets behind Nick as he gets up with some German’s on his mind, as he wraps his arms around him and then lifts him up and puts him down with a German Suplex…rather then pull apart he keeps the hold intact and then lands another this time releasing him as he pulls him over. Going for the cover quickly 1…2…Kick Out! James gets up confidently as there is a bit of a commotion in the crowd as out from the back comes Matt Kluge walking with both GIW Tag Team Championships walking around the ring, stopping and pulling out a chair from the side. RILEY STEVENS: “Looks like Kluge came out to see James first hand.” During this James takes his mind off the ball for a second as Riot catches him with a stiff punch that sends him faltering back a few steps. Riot with James a little off guard goes for a Dragon Sleeper however; right away James begins to battle out nailing him with elbows clearly wanting to be one of the Tag Champions. The crowd cheers him on as Kluge watches from ringside. Nick breaks the hold thinking he’ll take a wild swing, but he does so lifted right into a ‘Goldie's Lock’ a Torture Rack, that looks extremely painful. The crowd cheers as Kluge stands up at ringside, as Nick finally taps out giving Leo James the huge win. RILEY STEVENS: “There we have it; Leo James is the other half of the GIW Tag Team Champions.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Is this against the rules?” Kluge walks up the steps then into the ring looking down at the belt that used to be Rydell’s, before handing it over to Leo James before raising his arm seeming to have officially found his new GIW Tag Team Champion partner as we go to a break. WINNER: LEO JAMES (Becomes One Half of GIW Tag Team Champions) Heading to the back we find ourselves around a table where Jeff Ricketts the MWA Owner and of course Blake Beckett the GIW Owner seeming to be having a little argument over who will win tonight. JEFF RICKETTS: “I don’t care if my guys just had a ladder match last night; Ace Baldwin and Solo will g get the job done.” Blake lets out a big condescending laugh as he shakes his head. BLAKE BECKETT: “Exactly only two of your guys are here, Team GIW has ‘GIW Undisputed Champion’ Alioth Starre, Trent Lost, and Bam Isaac Glazer we are set. Trust me…when the nights done you’ll owe me all of that thousand dollars.” However both men look up when entering the area is Aspen Chaud already in her ring gear walks up to the edge of the table. ASPEN CHAUD: “You mind if I get in on this bet? You see I have no doubt in my mind that Absolution will defeat both of your pathetic teams. I just don’t want to see either of you crying to me later when we destroy your teams.” Aspen waves and heads on her way, nearly time for her match as Jeff looks over at Blake. JEFF RICKETTS: “How do you put up with that bitch?” The two actually share a laugh as the scene comes to a close and yet again we head else where backstage. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = Heading to the back we have Blake Beckett searching the hotel for someone when he finds just who he is looking for when he finds La’Sarah Audric standing outside of a hotel room. BLAKE BECKETT: “Listen I really need to talk…” Just as he says that the door opens and walking out is the huge Unforgettable Jones, standing beside La’Sarah, she looks up at him with a grin. LA’SARAH AUDRIC: “Sorry Blake I am sick of being the one you go to with all your problems, this here is Black Flag City and what we represent is going against people like you!” La’Sarah grins as Jones puts his arm around her; Blake seems stunned by the transformation Audric has taken the last few weeks. La’Sarah leads Jones out towards the ring as Jones bumps Blake out of the way. UNFORGETTABLE JONES: “Get out of the way.” Beckett angrily glares at him for just saying that as the two head to the ring as we take a break. QUOTE This time when we return a GIW Television Championship graphic flashes over the screen as the ring is already full. We have the current champion Pandora holding her belt in one corner. Johnny Scar standing in a corner of his own looking at the competition as Adam Syndal looks across the ring at big bad Unforgettable Jones who has La’Sarah Audric at ring side talking to him. RILEY STEVENS: “What a match this should be, a lot of bad blood between Scar, Pandora, and Syndal after what happened during ‘Category 5’ and Unforgettable Jones seems to be against just about everyone here in GIW other then La’Sarah Audric.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Angry black man, how typical.” Pandora gives up her belt out to the referee as the four look at one another, he displays it to each sides of the ring then hands it out then backs up and calls for the bell. DING DING DING! Immediately it’s Johnny Scar running at Adam Syndal whom he lost to at ‘Category 5’ cornering him he begins to deliver shots to him, using his speed to land several of them. However as we head to the other side of the ring we have Unforgettable Jones walking towards Pandora in the corner as the crowd seems almost in fear of what he could possibly do to her. Jones glares at her, as La’Sarah gives him the final word as she screams at him to get her and he does just that running at her and landing a big corner slam that sends the crowd into some big gasps wondering if Pandora will make it out. When he backs up Pandora has fallen to a seated position already seeming out of it, as on the other side Scar has just been tossed over the apron by Syndal only to catch himself on the ring apron. Landing a big punch on Syndal he stuns him long enough to go Springboard and do a perfectly executed Arm Drag that sends Syndal to the mat. Jones now has pulled Pandora up to her feet, looking around he grins then begins to throw hooks over and over again sending her whipping back and forth something he calls the ‘Unforgettable Hammer’ and it may not be something Pandora soon forgets as she falls down to the mat looking basically out cold, rather then leaving her be he again lifts her up and then hoists her into the air and disposes her like a trash bag over the top rope, her small frame hitting the outside with a loud thud as the crowd seems to actually feel a little sorry for Pandora due to the always menacing Jones. RILEY STEVENS: “This match has just gotten underway and already the champion seems to be gone.” Back in the ring Scar is working over Syndal’s arm with an arm lock as Jones watches from a few feet away before finally landing a kick to Scar’s stomach as he turns and throws him over the top rope, at least he thinks so. Turning around he picks up the scraps grabbing Syndal basically engulfing him as he hits the ‘Unforgettable Suplex’ a Belly to Belly Suplex that sends Adam into the corner. However little to Jones knowledge Scar skinned the cat and has returned to the ring, running at him and nailing him with an Enzuigiri to the back of his head. This does not; take him down however it seems to be enough to stun him. Though a million times smaller, Scar does not back down instead he delivers kicks to the legs of the clearly a little lost Unforgettable Jones. Realizing he needs to try something else Johnny heads to the top rope, with the fans backing him firmly as he jumps off with a big Axe Handle to Jones that again only seems to set him off a little. But he jumps up scaling his back to lock in a Sleeper Hold that sends the fans into a frenzy with Syndal and Pandora out it seems like he’s got his chance. RILEY STEVENS: “Can he put the big man to sleep?” The crowd is behind him but all at once they are shut down when Jones almost easily reaches up and throws Scar over his shoulder and to the mat. Back to her feet and seeming to have realized where and who she is Pandora enters the ring running at Jones to try to save her reign but she runs right into a ‘Unforgettable Attack’ a Clothesline from Hell that very literally sends her spinning in the air before landing with a thud on the mat, out cold. Jones covers her to get the belt, 1…2…Scar is there to break it up! The crowd is stunned, they thought it was over. The referee helps Pandora as she rolls out of the ring, with Jones just getting up Scar nails him with a Running Dropkick to his knee! Jones falls to one knee and then speeding off the ropes using all of his power Scar crushes him with another Dropkick! The crowd going wild as Jones falls back and to the mat. A recovered Syndal goes to steal this one throwing a wild punch, but he gets a huge Enzuigiri for his troubles! The crowd goes wild as Scar heads out of the ring, going Springboard, then hitting with a Springboard Moonsault that gets the crowd screaming 1…2…Jones tears him off and tosses him out of the ring! Lifting Syndal up he nails him with the ‘Unforgettable Bomb’, covering him now as the crowd goes insane with boos 1…2…3! That is it. RILEY STEVENS: “Damn it! That is two weeks in a row Scar got his win stolen away from him.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “That was Jones that hit the Unforgettable Bomb you idiot, he won the belt fair and square.” La’Sarah jumps up into the ring, tearing the belt away from the referee along the way handing it over to Jones who holds it up in the air the fans boos not phasing him one bit as he looks out at Johnny Scar who is getting up holding his head realizing just how close he was on this one, the camera then showing Syndal and Pandora’s broken bodies but finally we get one more shot of our new GIW Television Champion, Unforgettable Jones! WINNER: UNFORGETTABLE JONES (New GIW Television Champion) It’s now nearly time for the Main Event but as we head to the backstage area we see Alioth Starre with his belt standing by with Logan Abbott with a big grin. ALIOTH STARRE: “You know Logan I think you are a lot like me in a lot of ways, that’s why I want to help you. I understand you lost on ETV tonight and that’s why I want to take you under my wing…I’ll make you a star really quickly.” Logan seems to accept this though he’s the older of the two; he realizes he can learn a lot from Alioth. ALIOTH STARRE: “Now watch what I can do out there, we’ll pick this up next week.” Alioth heads on his way to the ring for the next match up as we head to a break before the HUGE main event. QUOTE When we return from break we hear the sounds of “Subterraneans” by David Bowie hits as out from the back comes Team GIW. Alioth Starre leads them with Trent Lost and Bam Isaac Glazer at his wings, with Blake Beckett following them up as well. Alioth has a big grin, as Trent seems a bit reluctant to consider Alioth the Team Leader like he’s surely expecting. RILEY STEVENS: “Here is our team, Team GIW! Lets hope they can work together long enough to win.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Doubt it.” They enter the ring heading to the top right corner where it’s decided that Bam will start the match as “Big Shit Poppin” by T.I. hits as the crowd gives mixed reactions to the invading Team MWA, as to a few people’s surprise the only people that come out are Solo led by the new MWA Heavyweight Champion, Ace Baldwin as well followed by Jeff Ricketts as they confidently walk down towards the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “It’s admirable that these two showed up, after last night’s show. Baldwin as you can see won the MWA Heavyweight title in a grueling Ladder Match.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Who cares about these guys?” They head to their corner where Ricketts joins them at ringside arguing with Blake Beckett as finally "Wayward One" by Alter Bridge hits as the crowd gives the loudest boos of the night as out come Alex Castellanos and Aspen Chaud followed by The Shadow as they head down towards the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “And here is Absolution, whom consider themselves not a part of GIW.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “They are better then GIW!” Riley sighs at Oliver realizing he was going to be on there side as Aspen heads up the steps, holding the rope for Shadow and Alex before Alex does the same for her as they head to their corner, all three sides looking at one another angrily as the referee calls for the bell. DING DING DING! The crowd goes wild ready for this to get under way as it’s decided that it’ll be Bam for Team GIW, Aspen for Absolution, and Baldwin for MWA. All three head to the center of the ring, looking at one another as to many people’s surprise Ace and Aspen work together, by grabbing Bam and tossing him off the ropes. On the rebound he runs right into a big double clothesline from Aspen and Ace. Together they both drop down and hit a elbow drop, on him as Aspen sneaks in trying to get a pin as Ace stands up, 1…Ace kicks her off. She gets to her feet, backing up as Baldwin whom was working together with her shakes his head to tell her that isn’t about to happen. But quickly Aspen reels back and tags in The Shadow, who steps into the ring with a big grin on his face. Ace takes a few steps back, only to gather himself as Aspen holds up a big L on her forehead as Ace jumps up and lands a Dropkick that sends Shadow falling back into the corner nearly knocking off Aspen and Alex. He runs at him only for Shadow to raise a boot right into his good looking face. Baldwin flops down to the ground while Bam Isaac Glazer back to his feet, runs at Shadow only to receive a Vicious Clothesline that sends him back to the mat. Turning around he gets to Ace too late as he jumps up and tags in Solo, who jumps into the ring running at Shadow only to get a clothesline of his own. Both Solo and Bam, get up trying to knock the big man down but Shadow instead punches Solo to the mat while Bam ducks under and bails with a tag to Alioth Starre! RILEY STEVENS: “Here comes the champion.” Entering the ring Alioth shows little fear of the big man Shadow as he takes him on with a Super Kick that sends him stumbling back into the corner as he runs at him and nails him with a Jumping Punch in the corner. Heading to the second rope, he jumps off throwing an Axe Handle to his face that doesn’t do a whole lot. Still with Shadow stunned, he runs at him and nails him with a Running European Uppercut! Stumbling back into the ropes the crowd is actually behind The Shadow to win this one, as most are rooting for Team GIW in this one. Solo attacks Alioth from behind, which causes him to turn his focus to him. During this The Shadow makes the tag out to Castellanos when he realizes he’s in trouble as Alex jumps in and walks up behind Alioth, setting up for the finish. He rears back and throws all his might into a huge ‘Golden Arrow’ a Superkick but Alioth ducks it and instead it’s Solo that eats it, sending him right into his corner where Ace makes the unseen tag. Alioth runs at Alex only for Castellanos to duck, as Lost makes the tag without Alioth knowing about it. Lost jumps in wanting a piece of Alex badly, as he runs at him only for Baldwin to cut him off with a Clothesline! The crowd boos as Ace, and then tosses Alex out of the ring. To many people’s surprise, Alioth Starre gets off the ring apron heading towards the back despite Blake’s pleas for him not to go. RILEY STEVENS: “It looks like Trent’s eagerness to get in has angered Alioth and he’s leaving the team!” Heading to the back he goes leaving Ace to wait for Lost to get up. Realizing that Alex is out of it Aspen calls for Shadow as the two run around to the other corner, pulling Bam off the ring apron while Baldwin calls for Lost to get to his feet. With Alex still getting up and Solo out of it, Ace and Lost are the last one’s left in the ring. Aspen and Shadow pull down Bam and then immediately Aspen slaps him as he spins around he turns right into a ‘Instant Death’ a Tiger Driver '91 that causes the crowd to cringe as in the ring, Ace spins Trent around and hits a ‘Thanks for Coming’ a Death Valley Driver with a Cradle 1…2…3! Team GIW is Eliminated RILEY STEVENS: “There goes that…” The crowd boos as Ace gets to his feet, taking a bow for the fans here in GIW having just pinned the former Number One Contender. But as he celebrates Alex returns to the ring clubbing him down to the mat from behind. Aspen and Shadow return to their corner as some referee’s come out to help both Trent and Bam back to the back. Castellanos lifts him up, as Aspen cheers him on in the corner. Alex whips him off the ropes and on the rebound nails him with a Double A Spinebuster! Getting up to his feet, he grins and taunts the crowd rather then going for the pin. Aspen claps him on encouraging the taunting even more as he turns around going after Ace only for Baldwin to jump up and nail him with a Side Effect! The crowd actually seems to be behind the MWA guys now not wanting to see Absolution lose as both Alex and Ace start to crawl to their respective corners for the tags. RILEY STEVENS: “Both men need to tag!” Alex makes the tag first to Aspen as Ace then makes the tag to Solo as well, as Aspen jumps in and calls for him to come at her. At the last second she ducks it and rolls him up 1…2…Kick Out! The crowd cheers, as he kicks out and she whips him into the corner looking for her patented ‘Tush Push’ a Turnbuckle Flying Butt Shot! But at the last second Solo ducks out of the way sending her butt first into the corner. The crowd cheers her misfortune as she walks out right into a Fisherman Suplex 1…2…Kick Out! Aspen is just able to get out of it. Both of them get up to their feet, as Solo throws a wild punch that clearly misses but Aspen backs up holding her eye complaining to the referee that she was poked in the eye. With this Shadow comes along the ring apron crushing Solo with a big punch to the back of the head. Quickly Ace jumps into the ring and runs along jumping through with a Spear like tackle to Shadow that sends him through the ropes, but causes Ace to fly out of the ring with him. With the referee currently having his back turned Alex jumps into the ring and nails Solo with another ‘Golden Arrow’ before exiting the ring, as Solo still stunned stumbles right into a ‘Queen's Wrath I’ a Double Knee Backbreaker! That sends the crowd into boos as she makes the cover 1…2…3! Team MWA is Eliminated! RILEY STEVENS: “…After all that cheating they win.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “It’s only cheating if you get caught Riley!” Castellanos hops down and helps Shadow to his feet as the two head into the ring where they celebrate with Aspen, all three raising one another’s arms in victory as the crowd boos the hell out of them. RILEY STEVENS: “Well props to Team MWA for a great showing but it is Absolution that picks up the win here tonight.” Blake is stunned at ringside while Ricketts helps Ace to his feet still arguing with the referee for messing it up at the end as Absolution stands tall in the ring as we take a oddly timed commercial break with time still left on the show. WINNERS: ABSOLUTION [Alex Castellanos, Aspen Chaud, & The Shadow] After a long commercial break we head to the hotel area where Alex Castellanos has just waved good bye to Aspen who heads into her room to get changed out of her ring gear, but out of no where Trent Lost tackles him! The crowd have already left while only the fans watching Live on Television are seeing this. Lost grabs him and tosses him into a room, where it turns out nobody is in. Seeming extremely disgruntled Trent spears him into the TV causing it to fall off and smash beside them! Grabbing one of the chairs Alex fights back by tossing it at Trent! But this is only a distraction so that Alex can tackle him out of the room and back into the hallway where some members of GIW staff come to try to break them up. But they are not able to hold back these two men as the two begin to throw punches at one another, during the commotion some of the paintings on the wall fall down their frames smashing, finally some of the wrestlers come out pulling the two apart as Blake who has just arrived to see the last bit of it, stands between them. BLAKE BECKETT: “You are both suspended indefinitely.” Angrily both men try to break free from the wrestler’s grasps as the show slowly comes to a close.
