GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS SUNDAY NIGHT REVOLT 7/26/15 FROM KAUFFMAN ARENA IN GALVESTON, TEXAS After the opening promo package for Revolt plays, we cut immediately to the ring where “Angels Fall” is blaring through the PA system in Kauffman Arena. The Undisputed Champion, Chris Callum, stands with his belt over his shoulder in the middle of the ring with a microphone in his hand. We hear the voices of the commentary team over the music. SARAH SHARP: "Ladies and gentlemen, the first Revolt after Moment of Glory is LIVE, and we are kicking the show off where we ended Moment of Glory – with a triumphant Chris Callum in the ring. It appears the Undisputed Champion has something he wants to say." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "So shut up and listen, Sarah! This man just took down one of the biggest names in GIW history in the biggest match on the biggest show. Let him speak in peace! He’s earned it!" The music begins to die down now as Callum stares directly into the hard camera. He begins to quietly speak. CHRIS CALLUM: "How many of you out there doubted me? Hmm? Never mind the fact that since March, I have been the top dog in this company. Undisputed. Many have tried to take this title away from me, but all of them have failed. That’s the legacy I forged in this company. That’s the legacy I will leave behind, and that is also the legacy that I kept alive when I defeated Alioth Starre last Sunday Night at Moment of Glory." Boos from the crowd, but they didn’t bother Chris a bit. He pressed on, looking into the camera. CHRIS CALLUM: "He took my Television Championship, and he is rightfully proud of that accomplishment. But the war between the two of us was never over the Television Championship, it was always over this belt right here, the belt over my shoulder – the Undisputed Championship. And I put the nail into Alioth Starre’s coffin once and for all when I pinned him right in the center of the ring." Chris dropped a finger towards the middle of the ring. CHRIS CALLUM: "It wasn’t easy. I can’t even pretend it was easy. He fought harder than anyone else has fought before, but at the end of the day, I overcame his onslaught. And in doing so, I beat the man who could claim he was the best ever. I proved that I was the best ever." He smiles now as the boos came in louder than ever. CHRIS CALLUM: "And the best thing about it all is that I won that match for myself. I wanted to prove to myself that I was as good as I knew I was. I didn’t do it for any of you, I didn’t do it for any of the people watching at home. None of you can take credit for my success because a long time ago you decided that I wasn’t worth your cheers. Now you boo me to try to rationalize that decision because you know you made the wrong choice. You cheered for The Sir, and he lost his title last night. You cheered for Bronx Valescence, and he has already failed to take this title than me. So desperately you wanted to cheer Alioth Starre because he was your last hope to take me down. You resurrected the biggest star in this company because you knew it would take someone special to beat me. But he too failed." Callum turned around to the other side of the crowd as the camera followed him. CHRIS CALLUM: "Here I stand before you, in spite of you, the Undisputed Champion. This title makes me the face of this company by default. So I’ll give you all one last chance to change your mind about me. The train is leaving soon, so jump on while you still can." “Morning Glory” by Oasis interrupted the Undisputed Champion as Terry McKenna arrived on the ramp to a thunderous ovation from the fans. Callum moved towards the ropes, leaving McKenna alone on the ramp as he spoke. OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Now what is this guy doing out here interrupting the champ?" SARAH SHARP: "Terry McKenna is the Deep South Champion now, Oliver – fresh off a victory over The Sir and Katie Keller at Moment of Glory... I would say he has every right to be out here tonight." Terry’s music began to die down, but the cheers didn’t. He pulled the mic away for a moment of offer a wide smile, but after absorbing it all, he finally got down to business. TERRY MCKENNA: "Chris, see I have an issue with what you’re coming out here and saying tonight. You claim that being Undisputed Champion makes you the best in this company, but what you don’t realize is that the title doesn’t make the man, the man makes the title." McKenna twisted on his heel as Callum looked on from the ring. TERRY MCKENNA: "Now, I didn’t come out here to claim that I am the best. I came out here to tell you that the reason you get no respect from these fans is because you don’t show them any. They turned on you because you don’t connect with them, mate. You look into the camera every week and talk to them like they are a bunch of children. They know a phony when they see one, and they saw through you before most of the guys in the back did." Callum spoke with laughter in his voice. CHRIS CALLUM: "They saw through me? No. I gave them the man they asked for -" McKenna interrupted him, much to the disgust of Callum. TERRY MCKENNA: "Hold on just a second. I wasn’t finished." The Undisputed Champion was irate. CHRIS CALLUM: "You don’t get to finish when you’re in the ring with me. You listen to what I have to say. You do not interrupt the Undisputed Champion if you know what is good for you." Terry nodded, stroking his chin. TERRY MCKENNA: "I know what is good for me, Chris. I know that I struggled, fought, and bled for the chance at Moment of Glory – just like all the video packages said a true champion would. I came up short more times than I figured I would, but I didn’t give up. I didn’t lose sight of what was important, and I was rewarded for my persistence at Moment of Glory." Shaking his head, Callum replied quickly. CHRIS CALLUM: "You aren’t a true champion, Terry. When I held that belt, it meant something. It was around the waist of the best wrestler in this company, and then it was prematurely stolen from me. As far as I’m concerned, the Deep South Championship is mine still, and you’re just a paper champion." McKenna spoke with passion towards the Champ. TERRY MCKENNA: "Ah… back in the days when you wanted to hold every single title belt at one time. You did it, Chris. Congratulations. You were the walking triple crown, but it only lasted for one week. I bet that really makes you sick when you are reminded of it, doesn’t it? You lost to Alexis Terry. You lost to The Sir…" Callum’s face turned beet red as he yelled back up the ramp. CHRIS CALLUM: "Both of those losses were formalities, Terry. I accomplished more in one week than you’ve accomplished in your entire wrestling career, and I bet that makes you feel sick, doesn’t it? Or maybe it just makes you feel worthless, and it should. Because a loser like you holding a belt that used to mean so much is a tragedy. You and The Sir and Alexis Terry ruined that belt." After a pause, McKenna spoke around a cheeky smile. TERRY MCKENNA: "Chris, tonight we are in a match against each other. A champions only tag team match, they are calling it. I’m excited to step into the ring with you, because I think you need to be reminded that you’re just a man, just like all the rest of us. You’ve been my harshest critic, and now you’ll eat those words." Callum stepped back from the ropes, amused. CHRIS CALLUM: "Good luck, Terry. But when you lose tonight, I don’t want to hear any excuses. Your whole career has been one excuse after another. If you want to lose your first match after becoming a champion, then fine. I like having easy targets." TERRY MCKENNA: "Good luck tonight, Chris. I sure hope you can back up those words." “Morning Glory” picked back up again as McKenna lifted his Deep South title high into the air. The voices of the commentary team became audible. SARAH SHARP: "Things are already heating up for the main event tonight when we see Terry McKenna team with Alioth Starre to take on Madison Seton and Chris Callum!" OLIVER THIBODAUX: "A champions only tag match, but there is only one man in this company that can claim to be the best." SARAH SHARP: "We are going to take a quick commercial break, but when we get back the action will get rolling when the TV Championship is defended. See you on the other side!" And with that, we fade into a commercial break as Callum and McKenna continue to mouth off at each other from opposite sides of the arena. Alioth Starre walks through the curtains with the Television Championship strapped around his waist. He slowly walks to the middle of the stage and looks around at the crowd in attendance. RUBY REYNOLDS: “Ladies and Gentleman the following match is scheduled for one fall and is for the GIW Television Championship! Making his way to the ring first from Brixton, London, England. After a monumental main event match at Moment of Glory, the fans give Alioth a standing ovation out of respect, regardless of how they feel about him personally. OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Although Starre didn't walk out of Moment of Glory with the Undisputed Championship, he did walk out with the Television Championship." SARAH SHARP: "I can only imagine how disappointed he feels being so close to winning the Undisputed title and dethroning Chris Callum. I wonder if that major loss will play with his ability to defend his TV title this evening." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Of course it'll be a factor, but Alioth Starre isn't a new dog in the game, Sarah. He's been around the block, he's been through huge defeats before, and he always comes back stronger. I'm predicting a long and dominant reign as Television Champion." SARAH SHARP: "We haven't seen a single Television Champion hold the title for more than two weeks, if anyone can do it, it would be Alioth Starre. Tonight will be his first test." Starre enters the ring and unstraps his Television Championship, holding it above is head. Soon he makes his way to his corner and awaits his challenger(s). Without skipping a beat, Starre Crossed fades out and is replaced by 'The Day is My Enemy' by The Prodigy. The fans erupt as Journeyman Phillips pushes his way through the curtains and walks onto the stage. RUBY REYNOLDS: "And his opponent... from Exmouth, England. Here is Jouuuurrrrnnneeyyyymaaaaan Phillips!!" Journeyman trucks his way down the ramp, a stern face locked onto Alioth Starre as he natural reaches out to slap a few fans' hands. He climbs the steel steps and walks between the ropes to enter the ring. SARAH SHARP: "Well this is interesting. The two finalists from Sole Survivor a couple months back." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Being locked in Alioth's Figure Four submission was what aggravated Journeyman's previous knee injury. He's partially responsible for Journeyman missing nearly a months worth of action in the ring." SARAH SHARP: "We'll see what kind of emotion these two bring in this match after Moment of Glory. Journeyman Phillips looks ready for a brawl, while Alioth Starres looks... different." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Its the calm before the storm. He knows how to beat Journeyman Phillips. He knows what he's doing." Both men stand in the ring, the referee joins them and as he rings the bell it is apparent that for the first time ever we'll be having a one on one Television Championship match! DING DING DING The two men circle each other, they're both veterans in this business, so they're aware running into the battle would be a death sentence. Journeyman is the first one to make a move, reaching out looking for a test of strength. Alioth reaches up, and as Phillips looks at his head, Alioth quickly kicks him in the gut kneeling him over. OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Phillips is the bigger man, of course Alioth wouldn't want to get into a battle of strength with him." Starre is quick to add onto his momentum. Clubbing Phillips in the back with a few forearm shots and running to the ropes and coming back with a dropkick to the knees. Phillips flips over onto the ground and lands hard. He winces in pain, but he doesn't want to show any weakness so early into the match. He picks himself up, but Starre is right back again, running at him from behind with a quick chop block to the back of the knee. OLIVER THIBODAUX: "He's targeting those knees early on." Alioth Starre stalks him, grabbing the leg that he has been working on since the beginning and rolls him over, looking like he is setting up for a knee bar submission. But before he could lock it in fully, Phillips kicks him off; sending him into the rope. Phillips quickly sits up, but Starre is back again but this time with a running stiff kick to the side of the head! Journeyman falls back onto the mat and Starre is quick to cover. 1.... KICKOUT! Phillips easily shoves Starre off of him and rolls to his side. Starre tries to stay on top of him, spinning around him locking him in a chickenwing pin! 1... 2... Journeyman rolls out of it. Starre is up, grabs Phillips by the hair and picks him up. Phillips springs out of it and smashes Alioth with a european uppercut that sends him back. Starre tries doing what he did earlier on and runs at him, Phillips ducks under his attempted lariat and grabs his waist, hitting him with a big belly-to-back suplex that gets the crowd amped! SARAH SHARP: "He's showing some life!" Starre is up again and walks right into another european uppercut. As he shakes the cob webs out and runs at Phillips, he is met with a big boot to the gut and is hit with a snap suplex. As both men hit the mat, they both show signs of pain on their face. Phillips' knee is slowly starting to come back to bite him, nonetheless he buys some time by going for a quick cover. 1... 2... KICKOUT! Phillips smacks the mat in frustration. He wants the title so bad but is unable to pull off any big offence. He pulls Alioth up and drops him back down with a implant double arm DDT. He drags the moitonless Television Champion to the center of the ring, and looks off at the turnbuckle. The fans erupt, knowing what is going through his head. He slowly walks to the corner, and pulls himself onto the top rope. OLIVER THIBODAUX: "We know what's coming..." Phillips sits parched on the turnbuckle like a grand bald eagle waiting to strike. Alioth isn't moving. There seems to be some hesitation on Phillips' face, but he shakes it off. He wants a championship, and he's going to take the Television Title. He stands on the turnbuckle, looking like he's going through a ton of pain trying to balance on the bad knee. SARAH SHARP: "FALL FROM GRACE!" Phillips leaps off the turnbuckle and comes flying down with a headbutt. Starre sees this at the very last second and rolls out of the way! Phillips crashes onto the mat head first causing the whole ring to shake. It doesn't knock him out however, he clutches his forehead and makes his way onto his knees. But Alioth is waiting, he leaps over him and rolls him up in a pin attempt! 1... 2... 3!! DING DING DING RUBY REYNOLDS: "Here is your winner... and STILL Television Champion, ALIOTH STARRRRE!" Starre stands a victorious man, the referee hands him his title as he walks to the edge of the ring and raises it high, facing the camera. Phillips is left still in the center of the ring, in disbelief. We fade to the next set of commercials. WINNER: ALIOTH STARRE (STILL GIW TELEVISION CHAMPION) The Sunday night Revolt! broadcast fades back in from commercial break and we see Frank Leary standing backstage with a microphone in hand. There is a makeshift interview area setup behind him consisting of two chair and curtain that hides the brick facade of the arena. Cory Chevelle is sitting in one of the chairs behind where Frank Leary is standing. FRANK LEARY: “I’ve been doing this a long time folks and I’ve seen a million white meat babyfaces get fed through this business like it was a meatgrinder. I’ve seen the most dastardly and underhanded individuals rise to the top not because they were the best wrestlers but because they knew how to take advantage of others weaknesses. I’ve seen it all but never have I seen someone like Cory Chevelle.” Frank steps aside and makes a sweeping motion with his arm in Cory’s direction. The mountainous man makes the chair look like a children's toy. Chevelle gets up from his seat and joins his manager without speaking. Cory’s bulging muscles shine under the lights from the camera crew. FRANK LEARY: “Six foot five and nearly three hundred pounds of pure muscle. This man, this monster has no weaknesses to exploit. He is a genetic freak and and skilled combatant that will walk through the roster of Galveston Island Wrestling like a hot knife through butter.” Chevelle drags his thumb across his throat in a threatening manner and then flexes the muscles in his neck. It looks like his muscles have their own muscles. FRANK LEARY: “My client showed the world this week what a hillbilly gorilla is capable of. He came out looking unstoppable in his debut match against... KEEGAN RYAN: “I’m gonna have to stop you right there pops.” Keegan Ryan enters the scene cutting of Frank Leary mid-sentence. KEEGAN RYAN: “Your client might have won his match this week but he didn’t do it by pinning me. In fact if it had just the two of us in the ring earlier tonight I’d be the one standing there running my mouth not you.” Chevelle sweeps Frank out of the way and gets in Ryan’s face. Even though Chevelle is grinding his nose into Ryan’s forehead, Ryan doesn’t back down. KEEGAN RYAN: “Is this supposed to scare me? I’m about as afraid of you as I am my own shadow, Chevelle. If you want to throw down just say so.” Keegan Ryan tosses his back over his shoulder and off camera. He starts to push his forehead hard against Chevelle’s nose. It looks like the two might come to blows right here, right now, but Frank Leary edges his shoulder between the two and separates them. FRANK LEARY: “Calm down now boys. No sense in giving it away for free. If you want a one on one shot against my client you can have it, but it’s gonna have to wait until next week. My client went out there and did his job, and you couldn’t stop him. Sorry about your luck, but I’m sure we could arrange something to tie up loose ends.” Keegan Ryan nods his head up and down and starts to walk away backward. KEEGAN RYAN: “You're on!” Keegan continues to nod and walk backward out of frame. Chevelle nods back with a twisted smirk on his face. The scene fades out. RUBY REYNOLDS: “This match is set for one fall and will have a fifteen minute time limit. Introducing first from Newark, Delaware, The Paragon, Karlllll Parrrrrrrrrrisssss!” “Centuries” by Fallout Boy pumps out of the speakers as Karl Parris comes out from the back. He is flanked on either side by one of his managers. The group makes its way to the ring to some cheers and a few boos but there is no grand reaction. The GIW universe is simply undecided on Karl Parris. After the uncomfortable walk to the Karl Parris climbs up the apron while his two managers continue to their spot at ringside. Karl climbs the turnbuckle and makes a triangle shape with his hands that gets the crowd a little more hyped. SARAH SHARP: “Karl Parris is coming off a pretty serious loss at Moment of Glory. DO you think that will affect his performance here tonight, Oliver?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I think it might. The first loss is always hard to come back from and that goes double if your first loss is at the biggest show of the year. That said, Parris has Daniel Starr in his corner to keep him focused.” SARAH SHARP: “So what you’re really saying is you don’t know?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Exactly.” “More Than Meets The Eye” by the band Testament replaces Karl Parris’ entrance theme, announcing the coming of The Starks. Now the fans come to life. A lot are booing but not nearly as feverishly as the majority that are chanting “Stark Strong”. She springs to her feet and the crowd cheers again. Melissa takes her place in the corner opposite Parris and waits for the bell. SARAH SHARP: “Well we can be sure of one thing, Oliver. The people of Galveston have fallen in love with The Starks.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I liked them better when they went around destroying people. Remember when George took out Austin Averell? Melissa slips out and runs the ropes. She comes back with a Chop Block that takes Parris down and follows up with another when Parris quickly gets up. After a brief pause, “Gunboat” by Vixtrola hit the PA system and Madison Seton stepped onto the ramp to a thunderous ovation, her sister Laura at her side tonight. RUBY REYNOLDS: "And the opponent, accompanied by her sister Laura Seton, from Oshkosh, Wisconsin, she is one half of the GIW Tag Team Champions – Madison Seton!" Madison and Laura held their titles high into the air before they made their way to the ring. Laura crossed around to the side while Madison stood on the stairs, waiting for her partner tonight. SARAH SHARP: "Madison and Laura Seton managed to overcome their differences last week at Moment of Glory to take down the reigning GIW Tag Team Champions, The Perfect Combination. That is no small feat, and it just goes to show how effective of a tag team wrestler that Madison Seton can be – even with a partner that she doesn’t always see eye to eye with. " OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Yeah but they are still sisters – they still have that connection Sarah. It wasn’t so long ago that Chris Callum told Madison that she should have had a miscarriage. That isn’t a disagreement, that isn’t not seeing eye to eye… that is pure hatred." “Angels Fall” by Breaking Benjamin begins to play now as Madison turns her head to the ramp, a small smile on her face as the fans relentlessly boo the man who steps out. Chris Callum, with his Undisputed title belt over his shoulder, slowly makes his way onto the ramp. RUBY REYNOLDS: "And her partner, from Oakland, California and weighing in at one hundred and one hundred and eighty pounds, he is the reigning GIW Undisputed Champion… Chris Callum!" He found himself ignoring Seton, climbing the steps before climbing the turnbuckle, raising his title towards the audience, and then down to Seton’s face with a grin. SARAH SHARP: "And there you see it clearly – Chris Callum is going out of his way to disrespect Madison Seton tonight. It’s not necessary, and it isn’t respectable." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "He’s the champion. He can do whatever the hell he wants." The music dies down as the referee makes sure the two sides stay apart for the beginning of the match. McKenna will start for his side, Callum for the other side. Seton didn’t even bother arguing her case, talking to Callum, she figured, wasn’t worth it. DING DING DING SARAH SHARP: "Here we go! The main event of Revolt starts now!" Callum and McKenna locked up in the center of the ring. Despite his larger size, McKenna failed to get the upper hand because of Callum’s quickness. Swiveling behind the Deep South Champion, Callum locked his arms about McKenna’s waist, dropping him down to the mat before moving into a headlock that McKenna quickly broke out of. With Callum’s arm wrenched, McKenna managed to land a few stiff kicks to the biceps, but that strategy was thwarted when Callum landed a kick to the gut, followed by a slap to the face. Never a man to tolerate disrespect, even from the top guy in the company, McKenna exploded with several hard shots to Callum’s face, sending him into the corner. He managed to get off a few knees to the gut before Callum raked the eye and then pushed McKenna away. Callum definitively gained Momentum when he connected with a chop block and a dropkick to the side of the head. OLIVER THIBODAUX: "McKenna should get out of there. This is how things spiral out of control in matches with Callum." With McKenna down, Callum landed a few more slaps to the back of McKenna’s head before slamming his boot straight into his back. He turned and looked over at Madison Seton who had her arms folded on the apron, not amused by these tactics. Callum asked Seton if she could handle it from here, which only elicited an eye roll from one half of the tag champions. Callum made the tag. Seton stood in front of McKenna, allowing him to rise. Once he did, the two locked eyes in a showing of mutual respect, but that didn’t last long, McKenna was the first to strike – slamming Seton with a powerful punch that landed right on the jaw. Seton countered with one of her own, but McKenna was determined to regain control in this match. He landed a European uppercut, and then another to Seton, before locking in an abdominal stretch and pulling her into his corner before he made the tag to Alioth Starre. Alioth landed a shot to the gut and Seton dropped to the ground. SARAH SHARP: "Alioth and Seton are the legal partners. This one could get interesting." Alioth whipped Seton against the ropes, going for a back body drop, but Seton saw it coming and connected with a boot to the side of the head, sending Alioth bouncing off the ropes. On the rebound, she caught him with a northern lights suplex with a pinning bridge. 1…2…KICKOUT. Alioth tossed Seton off from the top and managed to get back to a vertical base. He tried to drop an elbow, but missed Seton as she was rising. Seton used this opportunity to try to lock in a Boston Crab, but Alioth grabbed the ropes, leading Seton to break to the hold. Frustrated, clearly, she pulled Alioth back towards the middle of the ring as Callum leaned his head on his fist, frowning. Seton saw this and turned towards him, asking him if he could do better. Callum laughed and said of course, and that only made Seton more angry. She crossed over towards him and held out her hand, pointing towards Alioth. Callum made the tag, and was now the legal man. He moved straight towards Alioth, but got caught in a rollup! 1…2….KICKOUT. SARAH SHARP: "That would have been so embarrassing for Callum." Instead of allowing Callum to stand once again, Alioth kept the head locked, doing a gator roll until he could get back onto his belly, and then rose. Knee after knee made contact with Callum’s head, leaving the Undisputed Champion dazed as Alioth bounced off the ropes and connected with a spinning heel kick straight to the jaw. He went for the cover again… 1… 2… KICKOUT. OLIVER THIBODAUX: "It’s going to take a lot more than that, Alioth." Callum was screaming in pain as he drug himself to the ropes. When it became clear that he was going to break the hold, McKenna intelligently rolled out of the submission and drug Callum back into the center of the ring, but this led straight into a rollup by Callum! 1…2… KICKOUT. The referee was back in position to make the pin as Seton watched with a frown from the apron. 1…2…3! SARAH SHARP: "Callum stole the win with a blatant low blow. This our champion, ladies and gentlemen." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "What the hell is Madison Seton frowning for! She was just given a win by Chris Callum." Seton entered the ring to help McKenna rise to his feet while Alioth glared at Callum from a distance. RUBY REYNOLDS: "Here are your winners – Chris Callum and Madison Seton!" SARAH SHARP: "This match was supposed to be a celebration of our Champions. Instead, Callum managed to ruin the whole thing – as usual." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Stop crying." Revolt faded to black as Callum held his arm close to his body while using the other to raise the Undisputed title high into the air. WINNERS: CHRIS CALLUM AND MADISON SETON
