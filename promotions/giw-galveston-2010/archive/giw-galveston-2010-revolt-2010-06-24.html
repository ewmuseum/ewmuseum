GIW — Card

Revolt, 24 June 2010

24 June 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Galveston Island Wrestling: Thursday Night REVOLT! LIVE! From Poretto Beach, Galveston Island Texas BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! The fireworks explode in the sky and the crowd goes insane for this weeks installment of Galveston Island Wrestling! The crowd that has gathered on Poretto Beach brought signs, beach balls, vuvuzelas, and anything else they could use to cause trouble and make noise. They quiet down when they hear El Ladder’s music play through the speakers… he is being helped down to the ring. El Ladder: … The crowd goes wild as the match begins. Eye Of The Tiger by Survivor blares over the P.A. system and out walks UWA Job boy Mikey Optical he gets halfway down the ramp when The Immigrant Song by Led Zeppelin hits and walks TJ Frost the other Job Boy. All of a sudden The Climb by Miley Cyrus cuts the noise. Fans are off their seat, one group of big fat people even has their arms around each other’s shoulders and bop along to the song. El Ladder is carried to the ring by a stage hand and placed in the ring. Mikey Optical and TJ Frost start with a double team on El Ladder, they pick him up and toss him halfway across the ring. El Ladder hits a rope and shoots back with a rebound top step. Both wrestlers go flying and get blood everywhere. Mikey says that it was a dumb idea, but Frost would have none of that and throws El Ladder into the ropes. He rebounds again and smacks Optical. Mikey pleads with Frost to stop but Frost says the third time is the charm. Same result though and Mikey goes down hard. Mikey gets up and shoves Frost, and then Frost shoves Mikey! They shove each other! Mikey climbs the turnbuckle and hits Frost with an Optical Illusion! But Frost no sells it because he’s colorblind and couldn’t see it. Frost applies the Frost Bite but they’re in Texas so nothing happens. Mikey punches Frost into the ropes and goes for a clothesline over the top ropes and ends up doing the whole skin the cat thing but the ref accidently tripped El Ladder and he rungbutts Mikey hands making him fall. After the match is over, the cameras switch over to see a cameraman running over to a large group of people huddled around something. The cameraman arrives, obviously tired, and sticks the camera into the herd of people. The camera shows a bloody Damu Akpu-nku laying on the ground in a heap. The camera switches over to an EMT standing by. EMT: We don’t know what happened… janitor just found him here. I’m not thinking he is going to compete tonight, if that is what you’re thinking. The camera goes back to the image of Damu, still not moving. We are now prepared for our first real match of the night this being of course Aspen Chaud and Austin Acquin in the second round of the title tournament. This was a match that many believe Austin would give up in a ways back but now one has to believe after recent events things will end up exciting. Paul Marshall: Here we go the first tournament match of the night… Colt Cox: Which means Aspen’s one step closer to the belt. Paul Marshall: Knowing that there is no interference allowed you sure are confident. The fans boo immediately as “Envy” by Nicki Minaj hits and out from the back heads Aspen Chaud the fans giving her usual warm reaction aka one that only a bitch of her magnitude could warrant. She walked towards the ring with a big smirk on her face as she enters the ring first demanding someone get the ropes before she slides in and then leans through the ropes and kisses the camera as usual momentarily ruining that lens with her lip gloss. Soon however “Papercut” by Linkin Park hits and after a bit of a pause out from the back a bloody mess is Austin Acquin. Paul Marshall: What in the world Austin’s bleeding before the match. Allison Beckett: I hate nose bleeds. Paul Marshall: Allison that’s no nose bleed he’s had his head beaten in, Aspen has to be involved with this. The fans give him a faint cheer hating Aspen so much they’d cheer Austin on. They also realized the blood and seem a bit confused but know full well what happened. Austin enters the ring barely able to stand as the referee checks on him asking if he can go and Austin nods yes the referee shakes his head then calls for the bell. DING DING DING! Aspen runs over and checks on Austin right away the fans immediately booing the whole charade. She tries to see if he is ok and when he shoves her away she seems to insist that he lay down so that he doesn’t get hurt. Austin shakes his head no but Aspen pats him on the back and tries to tell him it’s ok. When he again shakes her head she angrily slaps him across the face. She tosses him into the corner and Austin can barely even stand up. Aspen runs across the ring and hits her Tush Push followed immediately by her going up top with her combo move. Austin falls out into the ring barely standing on one knee as Aspen jumps off for an AirBarbie, Crossbody. However to the fans surprise Austin catches her mid air, but before he can do anything his legs give out from underneath him and he falls down with Aspen on top, 1…2…Kick Out! Aspen gets to her feet in anger yelling at the referee as she then goes back on the attack not waiting for Austin to get up before setting up for the Barbie Makeover. She attempts to hit up but Austin pushes her away but again falls to the mat in pain. Aspen smirks and this time sets up the Barbie Makeover and hits it hard, 1…2…Instead of pinning him Aspen pulls his shoulder up and stand to her feet. She looks down at him with devious eyes then slaps her butt and locks in her move the K.M.A. the Kiss My Aspen, the fans boo as Austin starts to struggle in pain. He tries to stay in it but eventually the pain gets to him and he passes out the referee calling for the bell immediately. Aspen has won it due to the mysterious attack before the match. Paul Marshall: This is a damn shame… Colt Cox: We have no idea it was her Paul, for all we know it could have been anyone. Paul Marshall: Colt your full of it we all know it was her and Damu’, she’s out done herself this time. The fans boo as Aspen stands over Austin a bit bloody from his blood naturally. She brushes it off of her hands by wiping it all over his tights as she stands up and demands the referee holds her arm up as she advances farther into the tourney. The match is over and Aspen is once again victorious. She is celebrating in the ring when Joshua Acquin’s music begins to play. Joshua makes his way down to the ring and Aspen seems elated. Joshua enters the ring and holds Aspen’s hand up in the air, and mutters one word into the microphone. Joshua Acquin: Congratulations. Joshua slams the microphone across Aspen’s forehead which immediately brings her to the mat. He stands over her with a slick grin on his face as the screen fades out and an intermission begins. Here we go on to the next match of the night this being of course the highly anticipated match up between Damu Akpu-nku and Jake Kennedy. You could say these two have become rivals over the past few weeks, however tonight early in the show their was a mystery attacker that left Damu down and out. Now we are not even sure that Damu will be able to compete. The fans are waiting when “The Way I Am” by Fozzy hits and they explode in cheers as Jake Kennedy heads out into the ring, he high fives a few fans along the way before hopping into the ring and getting ready. Paul Marshall: Now the moment of truth will Damu be able to compete? The fans look to the stage waiting to see what will happen and they wait for a bit but soon Marshall gets into the ring with a microphone much to the surprise of the fans. Paul Marshall: I know you were all very excited about this match here tonight but unfortunately due to the random attack of Damu Akpu-nku early in the evening Damu has been forced to forfeit this match up! Therefore your winner advancing in the tourney is Jake Kennedy! The fans have no choice but to cheer always happy to see Jake win, Paul exits the ring while Jake almost seems a bit disappointed that he didn’t get to fight in this one. But as he exits the ring he high fives a few fans and seems to feed off their energy as he hits the back. After the match is over the televisions around the arena light up with a video feed coming from back stage. Aspen Chaud, still in ring attire holding an abnormally large ice pack laid across her head. She is walking down the hallway with an angry look on her face, pushing through staff members and other wrestlers alike. She comes to Blake Beckett’s door and beats on it. Aspen Chaud: Blake! Blake, get your ass out here! You ruined it! You ruined my life! You ruined it all! BLAKE!!! The scene fades out… Aspen is still beating on the door, almost in tears. Next up in the tournament is Stoner and Ima Goner a match up that might just put a stop to this crazy little zombie run once and for all. After having one in the first round against Defias easily Stoner now heads on to face Ima Goner, who many thought wouldn’t get past the first round. Now regardless he we are ready to battle it out as the camera first shows Ima Goner in the ring in a very revealing dress as the camera then heads to the entrance area where “Kings Will be Kings" by Helloween hits and the fans cheer immediately for the home state boy. He runs out from the back with a big grin on his face. Paul Marshall: Here is Stoner, what a statement he made last week by taking out Defias EASILY. Colt Cox: Long as he gets this girl out, I’m happy. Make her tap! Stoner heads into the ring and looks over at Ima almost a little worried about doing anything to her. But he heads top rope gets a few more cheers then hops down and seems ready to get this underway. The referee calls for the bell as the fans wait for this Submission match. DING DING DING! Right away Ima Goner starts to give him a strip tease, a few fans whistling from the crowd. Ima starts to slowly take off her dress as Stoner has a face that screams “what the hell is going on right now”, but regardless he waits for this little thing to end. She tosses her dress out of the ring that starts grinding up on Stoner which only surprises him even more. Stoner looks at the crowd who are cheering him on as Ima now seems ready to take off her bra, but Stoner instead stops her by locking her in a Sleeper Hold. The fans laugh due to the circumstances while Ima tries to fight her way out of it, but eventually she is quickly forced to tap out. Stoner wins this one easily as some may have expected. Colt Cox: Thank God. The fans watch as Stoner lets her go and waits to see if she is ok. She gets up and offers again to dance, but Stoner instead invites her to dance for the fans as he slides out of the ring and heads to the back, satisfied with just getting by while we can see Goner in the background as we head to the back. FLASHBACK BLAKE BECKETT: “Ladies and gentlemen, let me be the first to welcome you to the third installation of Thursday Night Revolt, our flagship show. Tonight, we have a spectacular show planed for you; tournament matches, championship matches, debuts, and so much more. The possibilities are endless tonight. Today will go down as a significant day in GIW history, it will go down as the day that GIW signed its first television deal.” The crowd seems very excited, they murmur back and forth to each other, and Blake remains silent until they quiet down, then begins to speak. BLAKE BECKETT: “About five minutes ago, I signed a deal that will bring REVOLT to the local channel KPXB-TV, which is affiliated with the ION Channel. GIW Revolt will be placed in the 10-11 o’clock time zone, right after the replay of the hit television show NCIS and right before the midnight hour of 3-2-1 Penguins. The show will repeat again on Saturday Night and Tuesday Night in the 3-4 AM time zone in its entirety. Next week will be our last week of un-aired Revolt. This deal lasts for a trial period of eight episodes. On our second week of broadcasting, that’s three weeks from now, we hold our “Super Show”, if you will, and it will be named Moment of Glory. That is the night we will crown our first ever, well, first official Galveston Island Champion. It will be a two hour broadcast live from the Moody Gardens Conference Center, with a capacity of 2,500 wrestling fans. This is truly a huge moment for GIW, and we express our gratitude to you, the fans, for giving us the power to do this. That is all.” The crowd goes INSANE at the news of a GIW Super Show. Mutherfucker Of The Year by Motley Crue hits the P.A. System like a Tornado hitting Kansas. Out walks “The King Of The Modern World” Scott Rydell walks out to a chorus of boos. He puts his sunglasses on a young fan only to get it whipped right back at him. Dead And Gone by T.I featuring Justin Timberlake explodes like a movie being directed by Michael Bay and out walks Izzie Dead. Fans cheer on the Shambling Supernova as he makes his way to the ring. The ref explains the rules about how a San Diego Street Fight works. No touching of the face or hair (of course) and how each wrestler has been allowed to bring one weapon. Scott Rydell brings out the lead pipe he hid in his ring jacket. Izzie Dead brings out a grenade from his pocket. Izzie starts by throwing the grenade at Scott, Scott pulls the pin out and throws it back at Izzie. Izzie makes an explosion noise and proceeds to do a backflip. Izzie then no sells death and locks up with Scott, after pushing around Scott breaks the grapple and shoots a takedown. Izzie gets back up and the camera picks him up slipping something on his wrist. Izzie lifts his hand up and tries to lock up. Scott places his hand on Izzie and goes into electric shock. Izzie flips him over for a pin and gets a 1 count. Scott gets up and smashes Izzie’s joybuzzer with his lead pipe. Izzie starts crying and Scott kicks Izzie in the gut. He then hits him in the back and locks in The One Wild Night. Izzie makes it to the ropes but it’s no DQ so Scott doesn’t let go. Izzie keeps pulling and the bottom rope snaps giving Izzie some wicked rope burn. Izzie gets up and gets a Savate Kick in the ribs and then a Cobra Clutch Backbreaker. Scott signals for a New York Minute. He waits for Izzie to get up and cross the arms, the ref reminds him he can’t touch Izzie’s face, he turns it into a double underhook backbreaker. He picks up Izzie’s legs and Izzie kicks him in the junk. Being a Zombie Izzie clamps down on Scott’s skull. The ref signals for a DQ. Just for kicks Scott hits the New York Minute. After so far an exciting evening we now prepare for a match up with three official GIW Debuts. Nathan Black last week won the Open Battle Royal where as Extreme Enigma as well as The Shadow did not yet debut. But here now in a match up to impress the crowd we prepare for a big one. We head into the ring where Extreme Enigma as well as Nathan Black are already in the ring, both staring each other down. But soon “Miseria Cantare” by AFI echoes through the speakers and out from the back comes The Shadow. Allison Beckett: I’m scared… Paul Marshall: Your right this guy known as The Shadow sure is intimidating. The two men in the ring seem to share their sentiment as both men stare at The Shadow as he walks slowly to the ring. Both men’s eyes are wide open as Shadow steps into the ring and through the ropes the fans in awe of him. Black and Enigma nod and then run right after him as the referee calls for the bell. DING DING DING! Both men start to pound at him but Shadow tosses the pretty big Black down onto the mat while pushing Enigma away, as he runs back Shadow lifts his leg and Big Boot’s the hell out of him. Enigma hits the mat hard while Black gets to his feet and tries to clothesline Shadow to know avail. He runs off the ropes and tries it again but again nothing. He gives it one more go but runs directly into a Sidewalk Slam! The fans seem stunned as Black holds his back in obvious pain. Enigma gets to his feet in the corner and Shadow realizes it. Enigma crawls top rope scared and jumps off at Shadow! The fan seem stunned as Shadow catches him up on his shoulder. Enigma tries to escape his clutches desperately but Shadow drops him with a huge Slow Play a Powerbomb that nearly breaks Enigma in half. Shadow waits for Black to get up and soon he does get up stumbling towards Shadow in a daze but right into his grasp. Shadow grabs him by the throat and looks around at the crowd before hitting the Shadowized a Chokeslam on very big man in Black. Shadow looks at the carnage in the ring the fans not sure Black or Enigma even have a pulse. He gets down on top of Enigma as the referee gets down and makes the count 1…2…3! Colt Cox: This guy is going to make some waves in this company. The Shadow gets up looking down at the two men, hidden behind his mask but you’d have to expect that he’s happy with the destruction he’s caused. He stands their over the two unconscious bodies as the match comes to a close. We head back to the ring where as usual the always mysterious Daniel Days heads towards the ring with a grin on his face though after last week coming so close to the North Beach title he’ll be looking for revenge on Apollo Calix that’s for sure. He heads into the ring and throws out his arms grinning under his mask, the fans continuing to boo him. Next out to the theme of “The Final Countdown” by Europe is Mike Danielson still taped up the fans give him a warm welcome as they have the whole time he’s been here regardless of the fact he’s been a loser both teams being crushed by Damu and then losing valiantly to Nathan Black last week, one of the fan favorites for sure. Then we have Apollo Calix the final entrant into the contest walking along with his new North Beach title the fans booing him though with a bit of respect since he’s a champion. Paul Marshall: What a main event we have in store for you here tonight, three men going at it. Daniel Days who last week came so close to the belt who will want to take it out on Apollo and Mike Danielson the underdog fighter whose ribs still aren’t fully healed. Both wanting to make a statement by beating the champion… Colt Cox: And more importantly our GIW North Beach Champion, Apollo Calix. I love this guy. Apollo steps into the ring and pats his belt to the other competitors before handing his belt to the referee as the three men stare each other down before the bell is rung. DING DING DING! The fans cheer as the three men start to circle each other but Apollo steps up in between them and motions where his belt will be before saying “I’m the champion”. Apollo smirks as the fans boo before Days and Danielson team up and go right after him knocking him down and sending him to the corner where he desperately tries to fend the men off. The fans cheer Apollo’s whooping as both men wait until he comes forward to hit a Double Suplex! Days turns and goes for a quick pin 1…Danielson quickly tosses him off and shakes his fingers no, it ain’t happening like that. Daniel hops up and shoves Mike in the ribs, then pretending that he’s crying like a baby about it. The fans cheer when Mike connects with a big kick to Days chest before hitting a Swinging Neckbreaker! The fans cheer him on as now Apollo returns to the ring and tries to take a swing at him only for Mike to duck it and roll him up 1…2…Kick Out! The fans cheer the near fall as Apollo hops up realizing how close he was to defeat. Apollo tries to kick Mike’s ribs but Mike catches his foot and proceeds to toss it down and hit a big dropkick. The fans cheer him on as now Days returns to his feet hits him with a big punch then whips him into the corner but as he does Mike jumps up and quickly off the ropes into a Missile Dropkick! The fans go nuts as Days rolls around in pain however Mike seems to again have hurten himself with that maneuver. Paul Marshall: Mike has been doing very well in this match but that injury may well slow him down. All three men are down but all at once they start to get up. Days and Apollo look at Mike who is holding his ribs and agree to team up on this one. They go after him like wolves getting him down and starting to punish his ribs. They stop and lift him to his feet and together whip him off of the ropes. But he jumps off of the rope and grabs both of their heads and hits them both with DDT’s the fans themselves are a bit shocked as Mike goes for a pin on Days 1…2…Kick Out! He quickly crawls on top of Apollo now as well 1…2…Kick Out! The fans are a bit disappointed as is Mike who falls to the mat spent here, unsure if he can even continue. Finally he uses the ropes to get to his feet as Days gets to his feet as well. Mike runs at him and gets him to the mat and tries to apply the Command and Conquer a Crossface! The fans roar as he almost locks it in when Apollo crushes him in the back with a leg drop to his ribs. He pushes Mike away and tries to steal the pin 1…2…But Mike gets up last second and knocks him off. Apollo stands up not believing that Mike continues to fight. He waits for him to get up motioning for the Golden Arrow, but as Mike turns around he ducks the kick and hits a Galvenizer Suplex, the fans go nuts as Days now gets to his feet trying to catch Mike off guard as well but he to misses a punch and gets hit with a Galvenizer Suplex, as well. The fans cheer as Mike falls on top of not one but both men in the center of the ring 1…2…3! He’s done it. Paul Marshall: What a win by Mike Danielson he just beat the North Beach Champion and perhaps jumped Days as the Number One Contender! Colt Cox: I can’t believe this… The fans cheer Mike Danielson’s victory as he holds up one arm in victory the other firmly holding his ribs in pain. The fans give him a standing ovation for overcoming the odds and winning the big one here tonight and perhaps making himself a contender for sure.
