GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS UNSCRIPTED! 9/21/15 FROM THE UNION BALLROOM IN AUSTIN, TEXAS BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! Orange fireworks stream into the night sky over Austin, Texas as UNSCRIPTED is set to begin live from the Union Ballroom on the University of Texas Campus in just a few moments. The promo videos end and the lights go down as the camera pans around the ballroom to show all of the screaming fans. When the ballyhoo is hover, we cut down to the announce desk. SARAH SHARP: "Ladies and gentlemen, we are here LIVE in AUSTIN, TEXAS for Unscripted and it is a packed house! We have a great show for you tonight, but first, let me introduce my broadcast colleague, the incomparable Oliver Thibodaux." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Thanks for the warm welcome Sarah. With the newly reinstated Hardcore title on the line, we will see George Stark and Keegan Ryan square off the match the belt is known for – a hardcore match." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Luke Jones… Johnny Violence… Alexis Terry… all of these people will be looking to earn a shot at one of GIW’s big four pay per view events with the title on the line. George turned to his managed and looked at them sternly before imploring that they head to the back. After quite a bit of debate, Melissa and Robyn move back through the curtain. George moves towards the ring. SARAH SHARP: "It looks like George is going to send his posse to the back. After moving to the back of the ring, he signaled for the bell – DING! DING! DING! The match begins when Keegan drops the bag to the floor and meets George in the center of the ring. The two start with a test of strength – something that would normally give Stark a decisive advantage, but Keegan is no small man in his own right and he is quite tenacious. George does, however, managed to get the better of Keegan at the beginning, resulting in a loud YAY chant from the fans, but Keegan turns the tables, shifting all of his weight into the test of strength. They go back forth as the fans BOO every time Keegan gains some momentum, but ultimately it ends with Keegan sweeping his leg under George’s to send the big man to the mat with a thud. George doesn’t let go, still having a good grip on Keegan’s wrist, but his shoulders are on the mat. 1…2…George rolls one shoulder up just in time, powering back as the fans are firmly behind him. Once he returns to a vertical base, he snaps Keegan’s arms around Keegan’s back and slams him to the mat with a belly to back suplex. Keegan pops up immediately, running into a stiff clothesline from George, and then another. George has all the momentum in the world until Keegan rakes the eye and connects with a dropkick to finally win the initial battle in what will surely be a long war. OLIVER THIBODAUX: "And that is the reason Keegan is the favorite in this match! He’s so crafty in the ring, especially when the rulebook is thrown out the window." Keegan is now on the offensive, locking in a headlock on George Stark, but he is quickly pushed into the ropes. He immediately went for the hockey stick, but George had followed him out of the ring and wasn’t going to give him the opportunity to do any damage with it. Keegan swiped at George, but George ducked it and slammed Keegan back first into the steel barricade right in front of the audience. But he wasn’t done, one more slam into the steps just for good measure left Keegan holding his back in pain as he leaned against the steps. George grabbed the shopping cart and turned to the crowd for approval. When Keegan located him, he was met with a seated right hook that left him staggering backwards. SARAH SHARP: "Look at that impact! And suddenly George is back in control." George tossed it into the ring and slid in after it. Once they were both in the ring, George picked it up and slammed it against Keegan’s back several times, crunching the metal into a twisted shard. George tossed the busted trashcan out of the ring and slid out to grab the second trashcan that he had brought with him. He placed it right in the center of the war and the picked Keegan up for a Stark Contrast onto the can, but Keegan got out of it. OUT LIKE THAT! The cutter connects right onto the trashcan. SARAH SHARP: "That’s it! George is out cold!" OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Go for the pin Keegan!" 1…2…KICKOUT. George managed to get his shoulder up just in time as Keegan placed his hands over his head in total shock. He argued with the referee for a while, but when he noticed George starting to stir, he rolled out of the ring to pick up the baseball bat that was in the shopping card. He lifted the bat up, ready to bring it down onto George’s injured back, but missed at the last second when George rolled out of the way. Keegan picked the bat up again and this time managed to connect right on George’s arm, causing the big man to roll out of the ring in a lot of pain with his arm tucked closely to his chest. SARAH SHARP: "If the arm is out of the picture, how is George ever going to hit his finishing move?" OLIVER THIBODAUX: "That’s the strategy, Sarah!" Keegan slipped out of the ring to chase George down, which he did easily. Stark was moving slowly, which gave Keegan enough time to slam him against the steps, arm first, for good measure. Keegan moved over to the cart again, but decided there wasn’t anything worth his time in there. He dug under the ring until he found a steel chair and then moved towards George with a sinister smile on his face. He placed George’s arm under his foot and slammed the chair down onto it. SARAH SHARP: "Oh my God. That arm has got to be broken." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Just wait until Keegan gets to his face!" The HOCKEY STICK! Keegan immediately got to his feet holding his face right above his eye. He staggered around the ring until he knew he was safe and then pulled his hands away.
