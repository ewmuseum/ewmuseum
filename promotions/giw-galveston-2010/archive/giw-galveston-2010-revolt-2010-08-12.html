GIW — Card

Revolt, 12 August 2010

12 August 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Galveston Island Wrestling Presents: Thursday Night Revolt! Live! From Galveston’s North Beach on August 12th, 2010 BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! This match begins with Michael Mayhem and Justin Bull already in the ring. Bull and Mayhem lock up in the middle of the ring, and Justin Bull quickly gets the upper hand. Mayhem switches the momentum with a quick elbow to the head, stalling Bull. Mayhem connects with a fall away back suplex that slams Bull down to the ground hard. Mayhem doesn’t waste time, he locks in a headlock on the fallen Bull. Bull quickly squirms out of it and gets upright. He hit’s a few clubbing blows to the back of mayhem and then connects with a couple of stomps for good measure. Riley Stevens: Playing it safe there, it’s always better to beat your opponent down before trying to throw him over the top rope. Mayhem stands up and he trades blows with Bull. Bull Irish Whips Mayhem into the turnbuckle and connects with a clothesline that sends him to the ground. He pulls Mayhem up and tries to throw him over the top rope. Mayhem counters it and uses leverage to throw Justin Bull over! Mayhem celebrates his victory in the center of the ring. “Your Going Down” by The Sick Puppies blasts through the speakers situated on Porretto beach, and the crowd of 400 plus screaming GIW fans erupt in cheers. After the opening video is done showing on the televisions around the ring, the music of Revolt General Manager David Sinclair begins to play, the fans give him a awful reaction after the week he's had, not a person in here likes him, He walks down to the ring with his pet tOWLette perched on his shoulder as per usual. Sinclair is wearing a black suit, slicked back hair, newly polished shoes, and a wild smirk on his face. He uses the steps to enter the ring, tOWLette elected to fly over to the down right ring post. He walks across the ring with that smirk of his, before being handed a microphone. DAVID SINCLAIR: “Hello ladies and gentleman it is I David Sinclair as usual here to restore order here in this company. Though I have garnered a few haters, per say I still continue to try and make this show the best it can be. That's why tonight I am going to make a few change--” But as he's about to say something the sound of Amanda Rowland's theme hits and the fans begin to cheer Sinclair's Affliction opposite. She walks down towards the ring wearing her business attire with heels, marching proudly down to the ring with something in her hand again. She walks up the steps and then into the ring while Sinclair simply grins in the ring. DAVID SINCLAIR: “I hope you brought my sandwich I am quite hungry...” Sinclair smirks at his own joke as Amanda now is handed a mic of her own as she stands proudly in front of David Sinclair, still holding the papers down by her side. AMANDA ROWLAND: “David last week on Affliction I threatened to show the Boss papers of all the things you have been doing behind his and the rest of the Board's, back. Instead your creepy little owl, swooped in and stole them. However what you some how failed to think about is that I might just make a copy. And that's what I have right here...” Amanda holds up the papers into the air proudly as ever as the fans cheer her winning, but just as it happened last week that damn tOWLette swoops in and steals it. The fans boo as the bird brings it back to David who laughs at how easy it was. But soon he begins flipping through the papers then tosses them on the ground and they are revealed to be just blanks. AMANDA ROWLAND: “Oops, I must have misplaced the real ones. Perhaps into Blake Beckett's hands...” The fans cheer while David seems to become furious when he realizes that he's been outdone soon Blake Beckett's theme hits and the fans cheer there Boss a little different from the norm. Out he comes from the back with a smirk as he now has the real papers as he stops at the top of the entrance area. BLAKE BECKETT: “David I really thought better of you, see if there's one thing you never do it's cross Blake Beckett. So since Amanda provided all the info I needed, I guess there's really only three words that need to be said...” The fans begin to buzz a few of them yelling out the two words he has in mind, but while Blake lowers his mic Amanda raises her own with a big smile on her face. AMANDA ROWLAND: “You...are...FIRED!” Amanda says this with a lot of happiness in her voice having gotten rid of the troublemaker. He turns around getting into Amanda's face but soon Security comes out from behind Blake heading to the ring. Interesting tid bit one of them is Damu Akpu-nku who has found different work apparently. BLAKE BECKETT: “Get him out of here!” The security guards namely Damu grab a hold of David while tOWLette flies off and begin to pull him out of the ring, forcing him up the ramp. He pulls away and tries to get away but soon they catch him and start to drag him up the ramp. As he passes Blake he screams out "You are going to pay for this, your whole company will!" Blake pays him little care as now Amanda walks up to Blake and the two head back to their areas as we head to commercial. . DING! DING! DING! The match begins rather frantically as Tate attacks Munroe before the match had even begun, kicking him in the back of the skull in line with the ring bell being struck. Danielson takes a shot to Barett’s chest, however is overpowered by his brute strength as the Manchester cities hits a European Uppercut, sending Munroe down to the canvas. Being the only one standing, Tate makes a cheeky cover on Danielson; ONE.. TWO.. KICKOUT!. Surprisingly close by Tate, he then follows it up with a pin fall attempt on Munroe; ONE.. TW-SHOULDER UP!. Unleashing a strike to Munroe’s skull, Tate pushes himself up to a standing position, lifting Danielson off the ring mat... before sending him straight back down with an impressive backbreaker. Chad Munroe wasn’t as affected by Tate’s attack as Danielson was, approaching him from behind as he hit him with a vicious head butt, following it up with a strong clothesline to send him down to the canvas. Taking a pin fall situation; ONE.. TWO.. DANIELSON BREAKS IT UP!. Munroe immediately fended off the threat of Mike, swinging him into the opposing set of ring ropes and pulling his leg, Danielson epically counters with a Leg Lariat. He considers a pin fall however doesn’t want to waste time, instead drop kick Travis Tate(still on the canvas), and then attempts a minor pin fall; ONE.. TWO.. KICKOUT!. He then takes a huge risk, heading up to the top of the turnbuckle, he peers down on his opponents before launching himself towards Munroe, connecting with a Diving Spear. Refusing to try and cover him, Mike Danielson is then attacked by Travis Tate, who found his way back into the match by kicking Danielson in the groin, lifting him up before storming him to the canvas with a devastating Power Bomb. Oliver Thibodaux: OWYUH NYOH! No babies for Mike tonight! Munroe sees this as a perfect opportunity to try his luck, sneakily covering Danielson, however is eventually spotted by Travis Tate who throws him head first out of the ring and onto the concrete floor. With an aim of winning the entire match, Tate takes a this time more reasonable pin fall; ONE.. TWO.. TH-NO! HOW DID HE KICK OUT OF THAT!?. He decides that only a finishing move is going to secure Tate the victory, lifting Daneilson up to his feet, digging deep into his groin at the turnbuckle area before slamming him down to the ring mat with The Spine Buster. The fans take it as a foregone conclusion that Tate is set for victory, he hooks Danielson’s leg, ever-so-confident of a win as the referee begins to count; ONE.. TWO.. WAIT!. The camera shows Chad Munroe diving into the ring, drop kicking Tate from behind, sending HIM out of the ring this time. With a perfect opportunity for victory, Munroe drags Danielson up, hitting his finishing move The DECAPITATOR. He hooks Mike's leg; ONE.. TWO.. THREE! CHAD MUNROE IS THE WINNER!. Celebrating, the referee raises his arm in victory as Chad Munroe won the match, with Tate looking on, whilst still on the floor outside the ring win humiliation. We come in on Madison backstage speaking on the phone, a preoccupied look on her face. She nods her head and speaks into the receiver. MADISON VALENTINE "Well...just...just get some rest okay. Okay. Okay I love you too. Bye." Alexii enters right and Madison stares apprehensively as she nears. ALEXII RAVYNOVA "Hello, Madison. How's your sister? Still in the hospital? " Alexii backs Madison up against the wall, ALEXII RAVYNOVA "You know why I was able to do what I did to Liviana last Thursday night? It was because I could. Because it was easy. Because your sister was weak." Alexii presses her finger into Madison's temple and pushes her head back against the wall. ALEXII RAVYNOVA "Just. Like. You." Alexii lifts her hand, displaying the Divas title. ALEXII RAVYNOVA "You know what this means to me? To me, this is just a strap of leather and gold. It's a worthless championship for a worthless division. But it proves what I've always said. That there's only one Objective in GIW. There's only one Fountainhead. And as long as I hold the title, as long as I destroy any and all contender that stands in my way, everyone else will recognize just how much better I am." Madison swallows hard as Alexii inches closer. ALEXII RAVYNOVA "You know, I really owe a lot to your sister. If she wasn't so mediocre and unnecessary, I wouldn't have ever gotten a chance at this belt and people would never understand what I've been telling them all this time." Alexii leans in. Madison shrinks away, closing her eyes as Alexii whispers in her ear. ALEXII RAVYNOVA "Thank you." Alexii exits right, leaving a terrified Madison alone backstage. Fade in on Madison, still shaking from the ordeal. From one diva to the next as we head back to the ring to see Sherilynne Martinez stretching in the ring getting ready to go. The fans have already grown to not caring about her as soon "Impossible" by Anberlin hits and the fans look to the stage where soon out comes Kacie Church. Some of the fans that already know her begin to boo, while others reserve judgment. Riley Stevens: Here comes Kacie Church a girl who last week, won the Open Battle Royal over two guys, talk about impressive. Oliver Thibodaux: You say impressive I say luck, though I do sort of like her. She slides into the ring scoffing at her opponent before heading to the top rope where she poses for the camera then hops down getting ready to make her debut on the big show. The referee calls for the ball so we can get this on the road. DING DING DING! The crowd is ready to see these two go at it, many believing Martinez to be the favorite after her strong performance last week. The two come face to face in the center of the ring, both girls starting each other down. However Kacie gives her a shove telling her to come get some. Sherilynne takes a swing at her but Church ducks it and hits a big time Hip Toss. Martinez hops back up and attacks again only to run into the very same move. Martinez gets up holding her back now already feeling the pain of what is a very strong attack from Church. Not giving her any time to really recuperate she sets up for a Suplex only for Martinez to block it. Rather then really worry about trying to do it, Kacie rolls around her and drops her into a Rear Naked Choke! The fans are stunned as Sherilynne begins to roll around in pain, but she’s close enough to the ropes to force a break in the action as the referee counts 1…2…3…4…Kacie releases the hold at the last possible second. Martinez holds onto the rope clearly in pain while Church begins to taunt her telling her to get up. The fans are releasing just what kind of girl Kacie is and are beginning to boo her, as Sherilynne gingerly gets to her feet already having suffered quite a bit of pain. Church doesn’t seem to have a care in the world. But as she uses the ropes to get up Martinez throws up an elbow into Kacie’s face. Church stumbles back after the shot she just received not expecting it. Sherilynne who has gotten up runs across the ring for a clothesline but is caught by the Submission Specialist into an Armbar Takedown right into a Cross Face! The fans are stunned as yet again Martinez begins to suffer in pain but before she can tap out or doing anything of the sort, Church lets go and gets to her feet. Martinez now tries to crawl her way out of the ring. But Church drags her back to the center of the ring, shaking her head down at the girl who’s basically thrown in the towel. Sherilynne kicks her back and then tries one last time to get her only she runs right into the ‘Ice Breaker’ a Sitout Facebuster. The fans realize it’s over as Sherilynne falls to the mat helpless as Kacie gets on top for the pin 1…2…3! An impressive debut. Riley Stevens: Wow she dominated here tonight, no better way to debut then that. Oliver Thibodaux: See all of those submissions; I may just like this girl after all. The referee begins to check on Sherilynne who is barely coherent at this point. Kacie begins to head to the ropes and is about half way out when she turns around and walks back pushing the referee out of the way. Riley Stevens: Oh come on now, she’s had enough! She contorts Sherilynne into a move we all know as The Sharp Shooter as Martinez begins to scream in agony, she begins to tap out right away. But Kacie doesn’t stop instead she just pulls harder until Martinez completely passes out. The fans boo her actions as she now releases the hold and this time heads to the back for good while the referee returns to Martinez then calls for help as we head to commercial. The segment heads to the backstage area where Blake Beckett is sitting in his office, his feet up on the desk as he holds some sort of paperwork in his hand. Soon the door flies open as the camera slides over to show Aspen Chaud walking into the room. She has on an eccentric purple dress and heels, having the night off. The fans go wild at the site of her as Blake grins with that arrogant demeanor he always maintains, saying his words with an almost demeaning tone. BLAKE BECKETT: “Well, well if it isn’t my secret admirer Aspen Chaud.” Blake grins confidently while Aspen fake laughs sarcastically back at him, but it’s clear there’s something on her mind as she shakes her head and goes on trying to ignore Blake’s rude ways. ASPEN CHAUD: "Very funny. Unlike some of the other little girly’s who are begging to get in here with you, I’m not one of them. In fact no offense but look at what siding with you has gotten Liviana, Violet, and Catalina…I guess you’re not exactly the best luck charm. But here’s why I am really here. I’m here to question why last week David Sinclair, made all the calls. I thought you were the man in charge around here." Aspen chuckles at Blake being a little bit demeaning right back at him as Blake smirks letting his feet down and leaning forward on his desk, cupping his hands as he replies. BLAKE BECKETT: “I honestly had nothing to do with it. I had no clue what he was doing. However while we are on the topic of power. Since Liviana Valentine had such a poor performance last week, I’ve decided that at ‘Blackout’ you’ll be facing Alexii Ravynova instead for the title.” Blake grins itching at his cheek flashing that flamboyant smile as the fans give a minor cheer for her getting a shot, while Aspen seems a bit flabbergasted by this as she walks forward to the edge of his desk. ASPEN CHAUD: "You giving me a title shot huh? That doesn’t seem right. I take it you have something up your sleeve?" Aspen showing little trust, for obvious reasons smiles raising an eyebrow while Blake leans back in his chair, shaking his head. BLAKE BECKETT: “No…simply put I don’t think you can beat her.” Blake leans forward tapping her hand as Aspen pulls it back taking a few steps back smiling as she stands in front of the door. ASPEN CHAUD: "I’m going to beat her. And either you’re going to have to step in the ring with me or admit defeat, buh-bye." Aspen waves her hand as Blake condescendingly blows her kiss as Aspen heads out the door. The camera zooms in on Blake who shakes his head and rolls his eyes as we head to commercial. ----------------- We return to the ring where both Andrew Bartlett and Timothy Hunt are in the ring ready for action. Soon "Motherfucker Of The Year" by Motley Crue storms around Galveston’s North Beach meaning that their Galveston Island Champion Scott Rydell is gracing them with his presence here tonight. He glides down the ring ever present smirk as he taunts the crowd with his belt. The fans are confused as to why he is here but soon when he enters the ring, it becomes clear as he lays out Andrew with his title. Then holds out his hand for a shake from Timothy, only to set him up for the 'New York Minute' laying waste to both men scheduled to fight. He calls for a mic as the referee checks on Timothy, soon Rydell walks by slowly kicking him out of the ring. Needless to say he didn't care about this crappy mid card match. SCOTT RYDELL: If you think I’m going to be losing my Title at Blackout, you must have used your illusion. I mean really have you seen the way to the this match that the other guys have used? I mean we have nepotism up the asshole, ironically the leading factor also to how Wake Walker got here right Mr. Beckett? Rydell begins to pace around the ring stopping to look down at Andrew Bartlett, shaking his head with a smirk. SCOTT RYDELL: Let’s just think things through which must be pretty hard for you longhorn loving inbreeds. What has Wake Walker done to get to Blackout? No not who, what. He interferes in my matches! He hits me with disco balls, he talks loads of shit, he fucks the owners, hell he could walk away with the title based solely on those merits if Blake had his way. The fans have begun to boo him as the referee calls to the back for help with Hunt, whom the 'New York Minute' has apparently done serious damage to. SCOTT RYDELL: And then we have Alex “Starr” now I know you’re all thinking who the hell is Alex Starr well. Hell I don’t even know. Apparently he’s David Sinclair’s little golden boy now. Whatever bestiality isn’t enough for him then alright. Listen Alex Starr, I know you fancy yourself being a new Wake Walker ok? The whole looking like a dirty hobo and going through the backdoor of those in power I understand that I really do. But look around, look at this belt. You think you can win this from me? Rydell slowly steps over Bartlett showing little respect his fallen workmate, as he continues on telling his fans what's up. SCOTT RYDELL: Alex Starr, Wake Walker, hell David Sinclair, Blake Beckett, and anyone sitting in the back during Blackout yeah even that big oaf Damon Synn. You’re always welcome to try and take this title from me, but you won’t I’ll beat you in one New York Minute just like I always do. Then maybe hit the clubs, fall a sleep in some ring rat’s bed, or maybe crash at Aspen’s again I don’t know her cooking is sub par to be quite honest. Maybe just maybe, you guys sitting in the back will start to realize I am The Motherfucker Of The Year! The fans boo as Rydell smirks and tosses the microphone out to a staff member as we take a short commercial break. We return after a short break to see our Lumberjacks already surrounding the ring. The camera pans around them first on one side is Alexii Ravynova, then Scott Rydell then lastly Wake Walker and Aspen Chaud on one side currently to allow entrances. The camera then returns to the ring where Johnny Stylez is pacing back and forth complaining to himself about how he lost the Briefcase. Oliver Thibodaux: Talk about a baby, he has nothing to complain about it’s his own fault. Riley Stevens: I think many would disagree with you on that, Oliver. To many he was screwed last week. Soon Alex Starr’s theme hits and the fans begin to give a very mixed reaction some of them liking him while others hate what he did last week regardless of the situation. He comes out from the back holding the Seize the Moment Briefcase high in the air, as Johnny in the ring shakes his head. Riley Stevens: Here’s the man who got the Case and will now fight for the belt at ‘Blackout’ he’d make things a lot easier for himself if he won this match and kept Stylez out of the match. He runs down the ring sliding in and handing the case to the referee as he then tosses off his shirt into the crowd. As Wake Walker walks around the side of the ring over to the other side as the have all four sides covered as the referee calls for the bell. DING DING DING! In the ring the match is ready to go as Johnny Stylez walks across the ring never one to close his mouth as he begins to mouth off to Alex about how that Briefcase is his and that he deserves it. He leans in real close spewing spit and such all over as he screams into Alex’s face, before he crushes him with a Kick to the Stomach. With Stylez doubled over Starr steps back and kicks up right into his shoulder, Stylez now stands straight holding his shoulder as Alex finishes off the combo move with a big kick straight to Stylez huge mouth. With Stylez momentarily stunned Starr runs off the ropes and connects with a mini Spear! The fans cheer Stylez misfortune as Starr makes the pin 1…2…Kick Out! Starr gets to his feet nodding figuring he might kick out, but he then grabs Stylez by the trunks and back of the head and throws him right out in front of Alexii. Many figuring she won’t do much will be surprised as instead Alexii just wanting to inflict pain runs forward and punts Stylez in the chest. The fans give her a minor cheer as she lifts him up and tosses him back into the ring, where Alex Starr is waiting with a smile. Stylez gets up and holds out his hands telling Starr to back off for now. Starr walks towards him shaking his head but perhaps not really think Johnny had a lot left in him, he calmly stalks him but Styles reaches up and connects with an eye poke. The fans boo as Stylez gets up smirking and nodding then grabbing Starr and tossing him this time right out to Scott Rydell. Rydell grins as Starr looks up at him, from the ground. Rydell instead of doing his job holds up his title showing it off, rubbing it in the face of the man at his feet currently. Starr gets up looking Rydell in the eyes before sliding back in the ring. Stylez runs off the ropes trying to set up a clothesline but Starr ducks it and connects with a brutal kick that takes the breath out of Stylez. With him momentarily out of it, Starr runs off the ropes only for his feet to be grabbed by Rydell from the outside. Riley Stevens: Damnit that is not Rydell’s job out here. Oliver Thibodaux: He’s our champion he could do what he damn well pleases. Rydell backs up holding out his hands as if he did nothing. Starr looks down at him which brings Rydell to motion for him to get back to the match. As Stylez has recovered he runs across the ring catching Starr off guard 1…Kick Out! Starr hops up kicking Stylez in the leg and then to the chest, as he’s used his kicks to a tee tonight. With Stylez holding his ribs Starr runs off the ropes and goes for a Clothesline but Stylez ducks it and instead Starr jumps over the ropes onto the caught off guard Scott Rydell on the outside. The fans cheer the champion’s unfortunate placement as Starr hops up to his feet with a smile. Wake Walker who has walked around the ring, to where they are located. Tells Starr to get back into the ring which he does. Starr slides in and Stylez mounts him trying to get some punches in but Starr is able to push him off. Both men get up at the same time and run at each other but it’s Starr who connects with a Flying Clothesline that takes Stylez down. Starr walks around grinning as he realizes that he’s got the former contender reeling. On the outside Rydell has gotten to his feet and is about to get into the ring, but is stopped by Walker. Rydell pushes him away which Walker takes little liking to as he runs at him and tackles him to the ground. In the ring Stylez is slowly getting to his feet using the ropes. He stumbles forward and boom gets hit with a Knockout Punch! Sets right up into the ‘Paralyzer’ an Anaconda Vise! The fans realize this may be it as after only a few second Stylez taps out! Riley Stevens: Alex Starr with the win but it looks like there is some havoc on the outside. Walker has tackled Rydell up against the ring. Rydell pushes him away then sliding in the ring to avoid any further fighting. But instead he walks right into Alex Starr on the inside of the ring. Starr is handed his Seize the Moment Briefcase which he taps to Rydell. Scott looks away then slaps Starr straight across the face. This time it’s Starr who tackles him to the ground the two fighting all over the place. Aspen Chaud and Wake Walker now return to the ring trying to pull them apart but its Alexii Ravynova who shoves her then proudly shows off her Divas title. Aspen looks back at the crowd for confirmation then runs at Alexii and hits a Lou Thesz Press taking her down! Oliver Thibodaux: It’s anarchy, I LOVE IT! Aspen and Alexii roll around and then fall right out of the ring, continuing to wrestle around for position. Inside the ring the three have been separated by the referees. All three in separate corners of the ring, as Security that was brought just for tonight runs out from the back. Three go to the girls while the rest slide in for the guys. They separate them from fighting, but Scott Rydell sparks things again when he shows off his belt to his two contenders. Finally Alex Starr snaps and goes after him only to be grabbed by Security allowing Rydell to crush him with the title. Starr folds over out while Walker breaks free of the Security and tackles Rydell down. The Security tugs his arms back leaving him open to a Low Blow for the ages. Walker seems out of it from the shot, as the Security has only helped Rydell. With Walker stunned Rydell pushes the Security away and delivers a ‘New York Minute’ to Walker right on his title. Oliver Thibodaux: That’s our champion right there, YES! On the outside it’s a little of the same as the Security contains the two for only a moment before Alexii Ravynova punches her two security members away then runs and hits a ‘Jawbreaker Lariat’ right to Aspen knocking her out cold. The security is back up and they pull her away but the damage has been done. The camera shows Alexii grinning as she is contained, as the camera cuts back to Scott Rydell who has been cornered by the Security. Riley Stevens: Our champions have stood strong tonight; will that be the same result come ‘Blackout’? The camera quickly shows Aspen Chaud being attended to by Security while Alexii is forced to the back by Security. Back in the ring Alex Starr and Wake Walker’s broken bodies are laid to waste while Scott Rydell slides out of the ring and walks to the back, being directed by the Security. He holds up his title proudly as the show comes to a close after pure chaos ensued.
