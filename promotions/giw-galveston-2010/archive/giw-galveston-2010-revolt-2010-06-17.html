GIW — Card

Revolt, 17 June 2010

17 June 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Galveston Island Wrestling Presents: Thursday Night REVOLT! Live! From Galveston’s North Beach (472) BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! Fireworks stream out from the sides of the arena and burst in the air, signifying the start of another edition of GIW REVOLT! The jam packed crowd of 472 wrestling fans are crowded around the ring placed on the beautiful sands of North Beach. There are televisions set up on tables in the crowd areas, and they all light up to show a picture of Blake Beckett sitting behind his desk. BLAKE BECKETT: “Ladies and gentlemen, let me be the first to welcome you to the third installation of Thursday Night Revolt, our flagship show. Tonight, we have a spectacular show planed for you; tournament matches, championship matches, debuts, and so much more. The possibilities are endless tonight. Today will go down as a significant day in GIW history, it will go down as the day that GIW signed its first television deal.” The crowd seems very excited, they murmur back and forth to each other, and Blake remains silent until they quiet down, then begins to speak. BLAKE BECKETT: “About five minutes ago, I signed a deal that will bring REVOLT to the local channel KPXB-TV, which is affiliated with the ION Channel. GIW Revolt will be placed in the 10-11 o’clock time zone, right after the replay of the hit television show NCIS and right before the midnight hour of 3-2-1 Penguins. The show will repeat again on Saturday Night and Tuesday Night in the 3-4 AM time zone in its entirety. Next week will be our last week of un-aired Revolt. This deal lasts for a trial period of eight episodes. On our second week of broadcasting, that’s three weeks from now, we hold our “Super Show”, if you will, and it will be named Moment of Glory. That is the night we will crown our first ever, well, first official Galveston Island Champion. It will be a two hour broadcast live from the Moody Gardens Conference Center, with a capacity of 2,500 wrestling fans. This is truly a huge moment for GIW, and we express our gratitude to you, the fans, for giving us the power to do this. That is all.” The crowd goes INSANE at the news of a GIW Super Show. They go even more insane when Paul Marshall announces that the Battle Royal Match is ready to begin. The camera cuts to the ring where the fans are getting ready for the match that always prepares them for the rest of the show it’s the open battle royal for all those not involved with the show tonight. The camera cuts to the ring where the combatants are ready tonight only two have arrived this time one being Nathan Black making his Galveston debut while the other Mike Danielson has made a triumphant return from an injury at the hands of Damu Akpu-nku last week, all taped up due to that. Paul Marshall: We got us a good first bout of the night today, with Nathan Black making his debut here tonight. Not a lot is known about him but that’s what these matches are for, so they can show us who they are. Colt Cox: He’s up against Mike Danielson who last week was absolutely crushed by Damu’, I’m surprised to see this kid even come back. Paul Marshall: It sure shows a lot of heart, he won’t go down without a fight that’s for sure. These fans have taken a liking to him as well; let’s get this thing under way. The fans are showing support for the injure Mike while a small murmur can be heard about the man across from him tonight his mysteriousness giving them all some reason to talk as the referee calls for the bell. DING DING DING! The fans cheer right away as the two go in for a lock up but almost immediately Nathan starts to back him towards the corner, he’s clearly got the strength advantage. He fakes a punch up top which Mike goes to cover up for however Black instead starts to pound away at Mike’s injured ribs. Mike falls down in pain as the referee tells Black to back away which he does right away, as the referee asks Mike if he is ok. Mike gets to his feet and tells the referee he can go and goes back on the attack. However this time his punch misses and Black grabs him and hits a Suplex hard to the mat. The fans can be heard trying to cheer Mike on in this one hoping for his safety all the while. Black backs up to the corner waiting for Mike to get back up. Black goes for a big running clothesline however Mike ducks it and connects with a dropkick to Black’s back sending him into the corner. Black bounces off the turnbuckle falling back a bit, as Mike heads top rope. The fans cheer his courage but it proves to cost him as he jumps off for a Missile Dropkick but the still healthy Black dodges his attempt. The fans watch in fear as Mike hits the mat extremely hard. Black quickly takes advantage hoisting Danielson to his feet. He goes for a Belly to Belly but Mike some how reverses it and tries to lock in the Command and Conquer a Crossface, the fans are cheering until Black’s strength allows him to get his way out. Both men get to their feet and Mike in a last ditch ever goes for a Paintball, big boot. Mike catches his foot and reverses it into his move the Fade to Black! He hits it perfectly and Mike is out cold, Nathan makes the cover 1…2…3! Black wins in his debut. Paul Marshall: Mike gave it a fight but tonight, Nathan Black came out victorious. Quite frankly this guy looks like he’s ready for business, if I were the roster I’d be scared. Colt Cox: Nathan Black is my kind of guy; I can see him getting to the top very fast around here. The fans have mixed reactions for Nathan Black as he holds his arm traveling along the beach towards the tent and the back, starting his career right. In the ring Mike Danielson uses the ropes to get to his feet, his injuries adding up now the fans give him a big standing ovation for his attempts as he to exits the ring. Paul Defias is standing out by North Beach, when he spots a young lady sunbathing, facedown. Paul being the smooth man that he is walks over to the lady and asks if she needs some suntan lotion. He really can't hear the muffled noises but sounded like Yeah. He couldn't help but notice that she was very pale. I don't usually do this, but...I felt compelled to tell you something. You have... an absolutely breathtaking... buxom. I mean, that thing is good. I want to be friends with it. The lady turns around revealing herself to be Ima Goner. Paul throws up into a bucket that happened to be sitting by. He notices that Izzie Dead and El Ladder are walking towards him in their swim suits. El Ladder has a shovel duct taped to him. Izzie notices the puke in their bucket. GRAH BRAH!!!! MRAHHAAAA!!!! BRAINS!!!!! Izzie chases Paul through out the beach. After doing his promo in the ring, Jake walked down the beach. He looked at the water before someone jumped in front of him. Jake went down with an "Ahhhh." He looked up to see the owner Blake Beckett. Jake sighed and got up. He brushed off the sand from his legs and butt. Then brushed his hands off. Blake smirked. Blake Beckett: “Sorry. Heard your promo. It was good.” Mr. Jake Kennedy: “Thanks. I just want to win this match. First Main Event. I also want the Island Championship, which I know isn't in this match, but just saying.” Blake Beckett: “I know. A lot of the wrestlers want it, even Aspen. You are working your way up. You will get there.” Jake nodded. He looked around. Blake looked at the sky. Jake turned his head back to Blake. Mr. Jake Kennedy: “I would be a great champion. I can't wait to get a title shot. I hope soon.” Blake looked at Jake and nodded. Blake Beckett: “You will have a title shot soon. Got to talk to Amanda first.” Jake nodded again. He kicked the sand with his boots and sighed. He looked back at the ring as some other wrestler was in the ring now. Blake looked at the ring, too. Jake checked his tape around his wrists. Mr. Jake Kennedy: “I see myself as the Island Champion. I'll be the second wrestler to hold the title. Aspen can fight me anytime for it.” Jake smiled and looked at Blake, who looked at him with a raised eyebrow. Jake began to laugh. Blake Beckett: “Maybe you will fight her, hell, you are tonight. Main Event. Try your best to win.” After Jake got settle down, he nodded and smirked. Mr. Jake Kennedy: “I will make sure to win. I'll make the stipulations next week.” Blake Beckett: “Yes. Good luck. Well, I have to go and do some other things. Chill out here before your match.” Mr. Jake Kennedy: “Sure. See ya later.” Blake nodded then left. Jake watched him leave then looked at the water. It was getting dark. So he sat down and looked at the ring, waiting for his turn. A PROMO PACKAGE IS SHOWN FOR MOMENT OF GLORY It’s then announced that the tourney matches between Ima Goner/Joshua Acquin, Scott Rydell/Chris Armstrong, and Izzie Dead/Wake Walker has been combined. Next up is the match that was just changed into a six man tag match to decide who’ll go further in the tournament. Whether it was due to time constraints or just a piss poor staff, the match was changed. Regardless it just meant that people would have to work together to get to the next round. First out to the tune of “Til Something Breaks” by Sick Puppies, Rydell’s theme comes the first team led by just that man Scott Rydell heading towards the ring while Izzie Dead and Ima Goner walk not far behind him. Rydell heads into the ring and immediately this team decides he’ll start. Colt Cox: Great the Zombies, I just loveeeee seeing them. Allison Beckett: I like them to O-M-G! Colt sighs as out next is the other team they come out to “Horsepower” Wake Walker’s theme. The fans cheer for him however as soon as they come out Wake Walker is with his partners Chris Armstrong and Joshua Acquin, all three of which are arguing right off the bat. They step into the ring and continue to argue but Joshua pushes the other two and says he’ll start. He gets into the ring and the two start to get ready, as the referee calls for the bell. DING DING DING! Joshua cockily tells his teammates he’s got this and walks into the center of the ring right into a Savate Kick from Rydell. The fans start to laugh as Joshua holding his head rolls back and tags in Chris. Chris heads in and tells him that he’ll have to handle this. He gets into Rydell’s face and tells him that he’s nothing; Rydell backs up joking and then catches him with a Savate Kick of his own. Chris falls back to the center of the ring and to his corner and tags in Wake himself. Rydell shrugs it off as the fans cheer him on. Wake Walker heads in and the two immediately tie up in the center of the ring. They bounce back into Wake’s team’s corner. The referee tells Wake to back off but as he does Joshua and Chris start to pound away at Rydell while the referee can’t see. Rydell gets out of harms way as Wake tells his partners to stop that. Joshua tags his chest hard and enters the ring as the referee by force makes Wake exits the ring much to the crowd’s dismay however they begin cheering when Rydell who was downed is able to reverse Josh’s suplex attempt into a Tigerbomb. He goes for the three count 1…2…Chris breaks up the pin attempt. The fans boo him as he’s forced back to the corner while Rydell gets to his feet only for Joshua to rake him in the eyes and jump for a tag. However both Chris and Wake think they were tagged. They both enter the ring and argue over who was tagged while the referee tries to tell them, no tag was actually made. As they argue Izzie Dead heads top rope and when they notice him flying through the air it’s too late as he crushes them with a Double Crossbody, both men as well as Izzie roll out of the ring. Joshua gets to his feet thinking he’s able to leave however Rydell turns him around and hits a deadly New York Minute! The fans cheer as the referee gets down and makes the count 1…2…3! Rydell’s team advances. Colt Cox: Oh lord they advanced… Paul Marshall: They earned it Colt they looked dominant tonight! The fans cheer in approval as Rydell holds up his arm and soon Ima Goner and Izzie Dead join him all celebrating going to the next round. On the outside Wake and Armstrong continue to bicker with Walker eventually walking away. In the ring to the surprise of the crowd Rydell pulls in Izzie and gives him a New York Minute, perhaps getting a one up on his opponent next week. The fans boo him as he heads to the back while Ima checks on Izzie worried. FLASHBACK TO LAST WEEK After the match is over and the crowd settles down the televisions switch over to a new scene. Mitch Conner is standing on the beach with the surf rolling in behind him. He is standing with the REVOLT General Manager Amanda Rowland and seems pretty excited to be here. MITCH CONNER: “Hello ladies and gentlemen, I’m Mitch Conner here on the North Beach of Galveston Island, the Bolivar ferry is chugging along and the surf is rolling in, but thanks to Galveston Island Wrestling, the North Beach might be getting some more publicity. Here is Amanda Rowland with a huge announcement.” Mitch hands the microphone to the seemingly excited Amanda Rowland. She smiles and thanks him for the introduction and continues with her announcement. AMANDA ROWLAND: “As many of you know, the Galveston Island Championship Tournament is in full swing, but what is going to happen to the losers of the top tier of the tourney? Next week, when REVOLT is live from North Beach, we will debut the Galveston Island North Beach Championship. It will be a six person match up, and the winner will become the new North Beach Champion.” Mitch Conner takes the microphone back from Amanda Rowland and smiles towards the camera. MITCH CONNER: “Thanks Amanda, as always it’s great to have you for an interview, and hopefully we don’t have to wait until you make another huge announcement to talk again. Back to REVOLT! ------------------ After the last segment is over and the crowd settles down, the televisions switch over to Amanda Rowland’s General Manager’s Office. Inside with her is Daniel Days, in full ring gear, yelling at her with the top of his lungs and flailing his arms in the air. DANIEL DAYS: “I WILL NOT STAND FOR THIS! WHAT KIND OF PLACE ARE YOU RUNNING HERE? GOING AROUND AND DEMOLISHING PEOPLES CARS? IS THIS SOME KIND OF JOKE? I EXPECT FULL PAYBACK FROM YOU AND BECKETT. I WILL NOT STAND FOR THIS!” Amanda takes a deep breath and looks Daniel in the eye. AMANDA ROWLAND: “There is nothing I can do, Daniel. Unfortunately for you, you aren’t contracted here… we only pay you if we book you, so you don’t have any of the perks that our signed roster gets, like all different kinds of insurance. Daniel seems frustrated. DANIEL DAYS: “They can’t just go and wreck people’s cars like that. It’s against the law.” Amanda smiles and speaks matter-of-factly. AMANDA ROWLAND: “Then you need to take it up with the police. Not me.” Daniel looks at the clock above Amanda’s desk and determines he has to leave. He walks out of the office staring with angry eyes at Amanda. We come back from a short break after the Daniel Days and Amanda Rowland segment where that same man has now taken his place in the ring. The camera pans around the ring where all six competitors have taken their place in the ring. On one side we see Apollo Calix with a cocky grin and then an angry Daniel Days whose time in the company hadn’t been great yet. However things could change for the better for all six men as the referee holds up the prize the North Beach title. The fans cheer in approval as they realize a new champion will be crowned here tonight. Paul Marshall: This is an exciting night, we’ll get to see our first ever North Beach champion crowned. Six men, one belt. Allison Beckett: Let’s go Ladder! Colt Cox: Please Lord don’t let Ladder win. The six men all eye the prize as the referee hands it out of the ring and then calls for the bell, the ring crowded with men. DING DING DING! The fans cheer as all six men start to go at it. Bull and Calix exchanging punches, Ladder no selling RAGE’s punches, and Days giving a beating to Way as well. The fans watch in excitement as all at once Days, Calix, and Ladder are able to clothesline their opponent’s right out onto the sand. The fans roar as the three men start to celebrate but soon the always smart Ladder heads off the ropes going for a double clothesline. Instead he is caught by both Calix and Days. They head towards the ropes where the men on the outside are starting to get up and toss Ladder right out on top of them sending everyone crashing to the outside. The fans roar as this match has started just as exciting as they could have hoped. Days and Calix start to circle around ready to meet one on one. However at the same time they turn and see that the men on the outside appear to be getting to their feet. Both head off the ropes and Days goes through the ropes while Calix goes over it, both landing right on all four men on the outside. The fans start to chant North Beach as both Days and Calix get to their feet while holding their backs. Paul Marshall: The action has started right away, what a match we are having. The fans cheer as Calix and Days get back into the ring and start to exchange punches back and forth. Days grabs Apollo and whips him off the ropes only for Calix to catch him with a huge Running Knee on the way back. Days falls and rolls out of the ring, holding his head. DeAndre Way has gotten to his feet and jumps into the ring running at Calix only to be caught with The Golden Arrow a Superkick out of no where! The fans have to be impressed by Calix as Way rolls around holding his face. Next in is Bull who jumps off the top rope at Calix only to be caught with the same move The Golden Arrow! He to holds his head and rolls out of the ring. Next in is El Ladder who heads in after Calix. He gives him one to, but wait Ladder chooses to not sell and instead gives him a big Topstep sending Calix to the mat hard holding his head. RAGE enters the ring and steps up to Ladder. This is a battle of beasts here as both men are huge; RAGE throws a huge punch but Ladder goes …..! Ladder follows it up with an Irish Whip into a High Rung into a Rungdrop 1…2…Days kicks him off. The fans are a bit disappointed as Days now tosses Ladder out of the ring, he lands in the sand and actually gets stuck due to the sand. Days wants to steal this one and covers RAGE, 1…2…Way and Bull kick him off. Both men whip Days off the ropes. He instead connects with a big double clothesline sending both of his opponents nearly into a flip. The fans have to be impressed with Days as well tonight he’s been dominant. Colt Cox: This guy is releasing his anger out on the men in the ring tonight. Way and Bull both get to their feet though a bit out of it and walk into Days who grabs both and attempts a double suplex. But Way and Bull keep him down and instead try to Suplex him. Soon however Calix renters the ring and joins Days by instead suplexing both Way and Bull, a momentary teaming by these two. The fans have no real favorite in this one other then Ladder who is on the outside stuck in sand. The fans watch as Days and Calix meet in the center of the ring; these two have been the best in the match thus far. They begin to tell each other that the title is there’s each motioning where the belt would be around their waist. Both men are about to go at it when RAGE comes into the ring and tells them he’s going to get the belt. Calix then gives him a Golden Arrow sending him towards the ropes where Days clotheslines him right out of the ring. However as he’s looking the other way Calix connects with a Golden Arrow to Days sending him out of the ring to. Way heads back into the ring attempting to get him from behind however Calix kicks him in the gut backs up and gives Way one last Golden Arrow! The fans watch as Calix makes the cover 1…2…3! He’s done it; he wins the North Beach belt! Colt Cox: I knew he’d win from the very start! Paul Marshall: I may not like him but I have to admit he earned his belt; he gave that Golden Arrow to everyone in the match tonight. Apollo is handed the belt and he hugs it tight to his chest as he has that big smirk on his face. We see Days on the outside angry after doing so much to walk away empty handed while the referee helps Ladder out of the sand. The fans cheer what had been a fantastic contest. A PROMO VIDEO FOR THE NEWEST SUPERSTAR, THE SHADOW, IS SHOWN. Here we are ready for the main event of the evening the fans turn to where the wrestlers were going to make their entrance. Already standing in the ring is Austin Acquin given a jobber entrance during the last hype video. Soon however “The Way I Am” by Fozzy hits and the fans start to cheer right away as they recognize that one of their favorites ‘Mr.’ Jake Kennedy is here. He walks out from the back with a big grin on his face as he high fives a few fans on the way down while looking at the tables set up around the ring. Paul Marshall: This is a big match tonight because the winner gets to decide the stipulations for next week’s match. Colt Cox: Those tables look like they are going to hurt being put through; whoever puts someone through a table first will win. He heads into the ring with a big smile on his face as he looks over at Austin shaking his head at him while grinning. Kennedy turns as he hears the familiar sound of the Kenya National Anthem being played, out from the back comes big ol’ Damu Akpu-nku to a bad reaction from the fans. Kennedy stares him down these two will face each other next week in the tournament. Damu heads towards the ring stepping up and going over the top rope as he stares at the much smaller Kennedy. Both men seem ready to fight. Paul Marshall: These two have some issues that I think they may settle before next week. But soon “Envy” by Nicki Minaj hits and the fans go into a frenzy of boos because out next is the biggest bitch in the industry and the girl they hate more then anyone else. She heads out to the ring with a bit of a swagger in her step as Kennedy turns his attention to her while Damu smiles up at her. He holds the rope down for her as she steps through the ropes, before she gives him a kiss on the cheek and whispers something into his ear as the referee calls for the bell. DING DING DING! Right away Damu goes straight for Kennedy and the two start to exchange shots while Aspen walks over to Austin and they instead start to talk arguing over stuff. Damu whips Kennedy off the ropes and goes for a clothesline but Kennedy ducks it and hits a Dropkick! However Damu simply shrugs it off as Kennedy realizes he’ll have to do a lot more to tame this beast. He runs off the ropes only to run right into a Spear from the man beast Damu! The fans boo as Kennedy rolls out of the ring to the beach to recover. Aspen on the other side of the ring is talking trash to Austin who doesn’t seem to want to hit her. However she slaps him right across the face and he realizes he has to but as he goes to punch her Damu grabs his fist and then grabs him and pulls him into a humongous clothesline! The fans are booing these two’s partnership. Back into the ring is Kennedy who unknown to Damu and Aspen has gone top rope behind them. As Damu and Aspen seem to talk they turn around right into a big Crossbody which catches both Damu and Aspen! The fans roar as Kennedy hops up and lets out a scream as he’s starting to get it going. He watches as Damu gets to his feet and Kennedy hits him with a Chop Block. As Damu is on the ground Kennedy tries to tire him by locking in a Bodyscissors on Damu’. Aspen starts to get up on the other side of the ring but doesn’t notice Damu’s predicament right away. However Damu uses his strength to get to his feet while Kennedy tries to keep the lock on. Damu falls back to the ground as Aspen realizes it and runs across the ring and starts to kick Jake off. Kennedy gets to his feet and starts to back Aspen into the corner. He threatens to hit her but is caught off guard as she connects with a shot to his lower regions, he holds them as he turns around into a Powerbomb from Damu who had gotten to his feet. Paul Marshall: Seems like every time Kennedy has something going the numbers game catches up to him. Aspen pounds Damu’s chest and tells him to go get a ladder so that they can put Kennedy through it. He heads out of the ring slides one into the ring. The fans are on their feet excited to see the table used regardless. Aspen sets it up near the corner of the ring and tells Damu to put Kennedy down on it. He starts to try and do it but instead Kennedy was playing a bit of possum and knees Damu in the gut and then dropkicks him straight out of the ring. The fans roar as Kennedy tries to go after Aspen only for her to run and get out of dodge. Back in the ring behind Kennedy however is Austin Acquin who goes for a big clothesline only for Kennedy to turn around and throw him over the top right onto Aspen. The fans cheer as Kennedy holds up his arms having taken care of everyone in the ring. He turns his attention to Damu who is getting up. Jake stops and motions for him to get in the ring. Damu gets up on the ring apron as Kennedy runs off the ropes only to run right into Damu’s arm. He sets up for a Chokeslam, but as Damu goes for it Kennedy is able to get his footing and back away. Damu angrily gets into the ring and goes after Jake but instead is caught with a Dropkick to the Knee! The fans cheer as Kennedy crushes him with a DDT as he’s down. Next Kennedy heads top rope where he calls for his move Here Comes the Boom! The fans cheer as he jumps off and hits a devastating leg drop to Damu. From behind Austin runs at him but Kennedy tosses him into the corner. Austin climbs up top to hit a desperation move but Jake instead connects with a big punch to his stomach. He heads up and starts to set up for a Superplex right towards the table! Colt Cox: This is it! But as he sets it up Austin gives a little fight not willing to go. This momentary stall allows Aspen to return to the ring. Instead of stopping Kennedy she gets in beside him and together they lift up Austin high into the air and Double Superplex him through a table! Both of them send him through the table as the fans are extremely confused. Aspen starts to jump around thinking that she has won the match while Kennedy seems a bit angry about what just happened yet still clearly confused. Colt Cox: Aspen Won! Allison Beckett: No Jake Did! Paul Marshall: I’m getting word that the referee has announced that both of them have won… The fans start to boo mostly towards Aspen who wrongly got into the Superplex and some would say stole it. Kennedy seems angry that he’s just been screwed, still winning but having to share the victory. There is a moment of confusion as the fans wait for a legitimate answer and soon Blake Beckett’s theme hits and out from the back with his group he comes with Izzie Dead, Ima Goner, and El Ladder under Izzie’s arm with Blake leading the charge. Aspen out of fear exits the ring as she helps Damu to her feet for her own safety; he seems to be hurt a bit. Blake has a mic and seems ready to talk. Blake Beckett: “Well it looks to me you have snuck out another unfair win. And since you both won I think the tie goes to me. So here’s the deal about that. Next week in your match Aspen, it’ll be a No Interference Match. Nobody is allowed to be near the ring, or I will personally fire them. As for Damu and Kennedy I’m going to make that a No DQ’s match so anything goes, don’t bother helping Damu because my men will be out there to stop you if you do. Next we have Ima Goner and Stoner which will be a Submission Match. And for Izzie Dead and Scott Rydell why not make it a Street Fight. So good luck to everyone involved.” Blake smiles as he lays down the law as the fans cheer him on while Aspen screams at the top of her lungs in anger while Damu and Kennedy stare each other down. Behind them all Izzie Dead can be seen slamming Austin Acquin through a table for funzies. They then head back to Aspen and Blake who continue their stare down as the show comes to a close.
