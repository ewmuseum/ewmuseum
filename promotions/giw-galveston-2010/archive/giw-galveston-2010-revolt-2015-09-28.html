Revolt, 28 September 2015

28 September 2015
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS MONDAY NIGHT REVOLT 9/28/15 FROM MARCHESA HALL IN AUSTIN, TEXAS The scene opens up from the Marchesa Hall in Austin, Texas. Fans are gathered around three sides of the ring in silence as they all look up towards the ramp for the first entrance of the night. They have read the cryptic tweets about change on the way, but they are not sure exactly what is going to happen. The voices of the commentary team come over the scene. RILEY STEVENS: “Ladies and gentlemen, welcome to Monday Night Revolt… LIVE from Austin, Texas!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Riley, is it you!? I can’t believe my eyes and ears!" RILEY STEVENS: “We are just one week removed from what was a game changing show indeed – Unscripted! I’m here because Sarah Sharp was so disgusted by the events in the main event that she walked out on the spot. So, it’s Riley and Ollie once again.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: "She would have gotten fired soon anyway. She wasn’t committed to this business! No one stands in the way of Aspen Chaud." RILEY STEVENS: “Katie Keller certainly learned that, didn’t she. She was getting back into the match after a long beatdown when Alexis Terry made her way into the ring and hit the Most Glamorous Moonsault ever while the referee was down and out. Afterwards, Terry gave Callum a low blow, I suppose to signify that she didn’t do what she did to help him, but Aspen Chaud made her way down to the ring, made sure Callum made the pin, and then counted it herself.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: "She was just making the best out of an awful situation. Just terrible! Poor Alexis! Poor Katie! They deserve so much better! The Revolution was going so well!" “Demon Eyes” by The Answer blares over the speakers, signaling the arrival of the Owner of Galveston Island Wrestling – Blake Beckett. The crowd jumps to their feet as he walks through the curtain with a smile on his face. RILEY STEVENS: “Well, that’s the owner right there. He called me and said he needed the void to be filled on commentary, so I knew that he would have something up his sleeve tonight.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: "It’s Blake! This should be interesting." RILEY STEVENS: “You’ve seen him recently conducting informal interviews with staff and talent about the performance of Aspen Chaud. Maybe he is out here today to share what he found?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: "I’m sure it was nothing but good things! Aspen has done a great job as Commissioner." Beckett made his way into the ring and then directly in the center, taking a microphone from Ruby Reynolds in the meantime. His music died down as he began to speak. BLAKE BECKETT: "Well, well, well… back in Austin, Texas." The crowd exploded at the mentioning of the city. Blake stepped back for the moment to let them have their fun, but went right back to business. BLAKE BECKETT: "I’ll admit, it is great to see Galveston Island Wrestling branching out once again. In just two weeks, we will find ourselves back on the Carnival Ecstasy cruise ship for Thunder in Paradise. We’ve already been to Laredo, Corpus Christi, San Antonio, and Houston. But… while all of this nice… there is a reason we stay in Galveston. We invested a great deal of money into a very nice complex right on the Seawall and now… and I don’t mean to be offensive… we are wrestling in ballrooms, dance halls, and cruise ships." The audience in Marchesa Hall lets out a small boo, but they know this is true. Beckett rolls his eyes until they are finished. BLAKE BECKETT: "The company has always been ran on a shoestring budget, and I intend to keep it that way. It keeps the focus on the wrestling side of things, which is what you all pay for. Now, I understand that touring improves our image, but I’ve received calls from Fox Sports Southwest about the dingy taping conditions over the past few weeks. This tour has been a mistake for us, and it could very well be fatal. When we tour, we have to pay for lodging, pay for venues, haul equipment around the state. I know that Aspen Chaud, the person who made this decision using the power I bestowed upon her, comes from a family that has seemingly endless supplies of money… but that is a luxury we do not have." There is an eerie silence in the crowd as Beckett pivots. BLAKE BECKETT: "It is no secret that I have been speaking to the talent that Aspen has disenfranchised. There is a Revolution in my company, and I hate to see that happen. I’m beginning to wonder if I made a mistake by choosing Aspen. I knew that she wasn’t the most trustworthy, but she is a household name here, and I thought it would establish some credibility when we reopened. Her collaboration with Chris Callum, Geneva Lost, and David Sinclair, however, is not a uniting force, but a dividing one." Cheers of agreement echoed through the crowd. BLAKE BECKETT: "So I took things into my own hands, and I will continue to do so. Our tour around Texas will be finished next week in Wichita Falls, and then we will go on the Cruise Ship for Thunder in Paradise. These venues are booked, and we will put on an amazing show for those who chose to buy tickets. We owe you that much. But starting next week, there will be a change – for budget concerns. Revolt as you know it will never be the same, ladies and gentlemen." The silence returned. BLAKE BECKETT: "Starting immediately, Revolt will be coming to you live in full force every other week. On the week when a full Revolt is not planned, we will feature a Television title defense and interviews with the talent to fulfill our television obligations. We are not slowing down, we are restructuring. But it won’t end there. At Thunder in Paradise, I will be present to provide a public evaluation of Aspen Chaud’s performance as Commissioner, and, if I deem it necessary, I will name a replacement." The crowd erupts in cheers, but Beckett is not finished. BLAKE BECKETT: "I’m sorry for starting on such a somber note, but please, enjoy the show." “Demon Eyes” picks up again as Beckett makes his exit and the commentary team comes over the audio. RILEY STEVENS: “So Revolt will stay a weekly show, but will be a limited card every other week. I’ve got to hand it to Blake, he always seems to have an answer for everything. Oh, and Aspen may be out of the picture once and for all.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Riley, you have been gone for a long time, but you need to remember that Aspen Chaud is a woman to be feared, so I would advise that you keep your mouth shut!" We fade to commercial break with the much anticipated Handicap Match coming up next. QUOTE RUBY REYNOLDS: “Ladies and gentlemen, this match is scheduled for one fall! Fist from Portland, Texas... Your GIW Deep South Champion! Geneva Lost!" “Murda Bizness” hits the PA as the fans starts to boo. Geneva emerges from the back with Duff Briggs and OJ3 following right behind her. Geneva stops at the top of the ramp and holds her Deep South Championship up high to even louder jeers. RILEY STEVENS: “And always a crowd pleaser, her she is the GIW Deep South Champion Geneva Lost.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Team Geneva is in full effect tonight. Duff Briggs looks like he’s ready to turn The Sir into Sirloin." RUBY REYNOLDS: “And coming Stright Outta Compton! Her partner Duff Briggs!" She hands the belt over to OJ3 before she steps forward leading the way for her team down the ring. Geneva pulls on the strings of her hoodie as she approaches the ring. Duff pounds his chest before he rolls into the ring himself. Geneva leans in the corner turnbuckle as Duff paces around the ring. Her music dies down and "I Have Seen Where It Grows" - Demon Hunter hits the PA next. The lights die out and a mixed reaction comes from the fans as The Sir reveals himself on the top of the ramp. His arms are extended and he shoots a deathly glare into the ring at Geneva. RUBY REYNOLDS: “And thier opponent, from Halifax, Nova Scotia, Canada... THE SIRRRRR!" RILEY STEVENS: “The Sir is here folks. He looks ready for a fight too.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “His mind is still fresh on the brutal beat down my girl Queen G gave him a few months ago. If he plays his cards right, he’ll get another one tonight!" The Sir slowly defends down the ramp, and the crowd getting to roar. A “Sir’s Gonna Kill You” chant breaks out across the arena as the light return. The magnitude of such an explosive fight builds to a breaking point as the fans are ready to see some action! The Sir approaches the steel steps an- RILEY STEVENS: “Oh my God what is Geneva doing!” The camera cut back to the ring as Geneva is rebounding from the ropes. With a whirl, building up momentum Geneva’s elbow collides against the back of Duff’s head as he was turned yelling at the Sir. Duff falls forward into the rope and bounces back as Geneva hits him again with her loaded elbow pad. The Sir’s music cuts and the fans erupt in boos. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Geneva is taking out her own partner!" The Sir looks on as Geneva mounts the laid out Duff and starts to smash his face with the loaded elbow pad some more. OJ3 steps through the ropes shocked and confused. Geneva gets off of Duff and approaches OJ3 with an evil glare. She yells at him to get a chair, and OJ3 hurriedly does so. OJ3 returns to the ring with a steel chair in hand. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Oh man… Geneva’s snapped, she’s gonna kill Duff!" Geneva snatches the chair from OJ3’s grasp. She looks over at the Sir standing outside the ring and points the chair at him. She then quickly turns and rams the chair into OJ3’s gut doubling him over. She then smack him across the head with the chair sending the man rolling outside the ring holding his head. She then turns her attention back on Duff and starts smashing the chair over his head repeatedly with the fans counting along. 1… 2… 3… RILEY STEVENS: “This is getting hard too watch.” 13… After finishing with a bloodied and battered Duff Briggs Geneva pulls back her hoodie and stares back at The Sir. Without a word she tosses the chair to the outside. The bent and dented chair lands next too The unflinching Sir. The crowd’s jeers pick back up again as The Sir starts stepping up the steps and heading into the ring. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Ah man looks like this is gonna be one-on-one now!" Geneva smiles and ducks under the ropes and out of the ring to a chorus of boos. She scoops up her belt and starts retreating. The Sir begins to seethe as the referee calls for the bell. The fans cheated out of their match boo louder as Geneva draws her hood over her head again and hops the barricade. RILEY STEVENS: “Looks like The Sir won’t have a chance to get his hands on Geneva tonight and these fans are letting her have it.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Smart move by Geneva, she’ll make the Sir fight on her terms! Man! She’s such a genius." The Sir merely points at Geneva as she backs away into the aisle. RUBY REYNOLDS: “Ladies and gentlemen, this match has been officially ruled as a no contest!" The fan’s jeers turn into cheers as The Sir picks up the chair and vents his frustrations into Duff with more chair shots to the head. RILEY STEVENS: “Ah man we need to get Duff out of there!” The Sir finishes his work with the remains of Duff Briggs. The fans are stunned as The Sir exits the ring and makes his way to the back. An emergency EMT team rushes out to get Duff as we fade away to the commercial break WINNER: NO CONTEST Back from the commercial break, we find Marissa Mathis behind the curtain. MARISSA MATHIS: "Ladies and gentlemen, this is Marissa Mathis here behind the curtain. Please welcome my guest at this time, the GIW Television Champion, Alexis Terry." The camera pans out to reveal Alexis Terry with the title belt around her waist. MARISSA MATHIS: "Alexis, thank you for joining me. Let’s start with something positive – you are just a few moments away from another TV title defense. How are you feeling?" Alexis turns a frown into a fake smile. ALEXIS TERRY: "I’ve been better, honestly. But it’s time to go to work." After a pause, Marissa presses on with the interview. MARISSA MATHIS: "Well, we should talk about what happened last week at Unscripted. You entered the ring and hit the Most Glamorous Moonsault Ever on Katie Keller, and then you low blowed Chris Callum. Even after that, he managed to pick up the win, and a lot of people say that only happened because of you. Care to comment?" Alexis let out a long sigh before she spoke. ALEXIS TERRY: "I’m not proud of how it turned out, but it is done. Chris Callum got the win because of me, but that wasn’t my intention. I needed to get even with Katie." She held back a tear. ALEXIS TERRY: "I loved her, and she lied to me. After everything I did for her, after I changed for her… because I loved her… she lied to me. That video was proof." Alexis ran her hand over the title belt. ALEXIS TERRY: "I don’t have any room for people like her in my life. And I got even. Now, I’m just ready to move on. A fresh start begins tonight. Step one, I guess." Marissa nodded. MARISSA MATHIS: "Thank you for your time, Alexis." We fade to commercial. QUOTE The camera fades in from black on a wide sweeping shot of the crowd. “#SuperKickParty” and Two Time, Two Time TV Champ” signs litter the audience. The ring is already full of competitors. Keegan Ryan, Jason Spirit and Katie Keller are in their respective corners waiting for the champion to arrive. Keegan is seated lazily against the ropes still looking a bit beaten up from his Hardcore Title Match the week before. Meanwhile Jason Spirit and Katie Keller are both playing to the fans from their corners. RUBY REYNOLDS: "Ladies and gentleman this match is scheduled for one fall and is for the GIW Television Championship. Already in the ring are your challengers Keegan Ryan, Jason Spirit, and Katie Keller!" All three challengers raises their arms when their names are announced. The crowd boos Ryan but cheers for Spirit and Keller. RILEY STEVENS: "This is going to be a hell of a match, Oliver. Keegan Ryan looked ruthless in his match last week. If he can bring that kind of ferocity to this match he’ll be walking out champion." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "I agree. Keegan Ryan is a certifiable lunatic and this is his match to lose." RILEY STEVENS: "Then again, Spirit and Keller have history in ECWF. If they can work together there’s a chance one of them could walk out victorious." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "I doubt it. My boy Keegan Ryan will just smash ‘em with a chair." The lights of the arena go dim while “Heaven's A Place On Earth” plays over the P.A. RUBY REYNOLDS: "And finally, from New Castle, England. She is your reigning and defending GIW Television Champion, England's most glamorous export, Alexis Terry!" Alexis explodes out of the back accompanied by her music and a chorus of cheering fans. running down the ramp, Alexis high-fives the fans standing along the barricades. Once she reaches the bottom of the ramp, Alexis looks between the ropes and locks eyes with Katie Keller. The two start shouting at one another. Alexis hands the title to the referee from the outside and then cautiously climbs up the ring apron never taking her eyes off of Keller. She ducks between the ropes and takes her place in her corner. RILEY STEVENS: "I’ve been keeping up with this feud while I was away and let me tell you something Ollie, those two women are going to destroy each other tonight." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Ever since Aspen out Keller as a cheater a few weeks ago, Keller and Terry have been at each other’s throats." RILEY STEVENS: "Can you blame them? Keller took advantage of Terry plain and simple. Alexis has every right to be angry." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Damn right! Let's go Terry!" DING! DING! The bell sounds and all four combatants go to work immediately. Katie Keller and Alexis Terry shoot for one another but Keller is caught off guard by Keegan Ryan. Keegan barrels into Keller at full speed knocking her down. Jason Spirit lays out Ryan with a stiff kick that lands flat on the jaw. Jason Spirit and Alexis Terry tie-up and jockey for position. Spirit gains the upper hand on the champion by powering her into a headlock. Judo Throw! Alexis lands flat on her back and quickly rolls through. She comes back at Spirit looking to end things early with a #SuperKickParty but Spirit ducks it and hits a Shoot Kick! This time Alexis goes down and doesn’t get up. Spirit tried for a pin but it gets broken up by Keegan Ryan. RILEY STEVENS: "These guys have been all over each other so far." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "I think we’re going to see a new champion tonight!" Keegan Ryan starts to hammer on the back of Spirit’s neck. Katie Keller tries to break up the lynching but Keegan scouts it. An Arm Drag sends Keller rolling to the outside. Keegan Ryan goes back to pummeling Spirit. On the outside Keller quickly gets back to her feet only to be taken you by Alexis Terry and a Suicide Dive! Both women crash to the hard concrete floor. Back in the ring Jason Spirit has started fighting back. Ryan and Spirit exchange hay-maker blows as they battle back to their feet. A stiff right swings the momentum in Spirit’s favor. He follows up with a series of quick kicks to the the thigh that if you blinked, you’d miss them. Keegan goes down to one knee. Enziguri! Spirit puts Keegan down hard and goes for the pin again… 1 2 NO! RILEY STEVENS: "Keegan Ryan gets the shoulder up at two. Spirit is going to have to do more than that if he wants to win." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Keegan Ryan isn’t going down without a fight. Hell, last week the referee had to stop the match so Keegan wouldn’t end up in a wheelchair. Keegan doesn’t quit!" On the outside of the ring Keller and Terry have both gotten back to their feet. Like Keegan and Spirit before them, the two women start to exchange blows. Keller goes for a hay-maker but Alexis ducks it. Back Drop to the ring apron! Keller lands back on the concrete floor holding her back. Alexis Rolls into the ring and goes to work on Spirit. She blindsides him with a Shining Wizard to to the back of the head. Alexis follows up with a series of three Snap Suplexes. Keegan Ryan gets back up in the meantime but Alexis is ready for him. #SuperKickParty! Keegan stumbles backward and over the top rope. He crashes to the outside just as Keller rolls back in. RILEY STEVENS: "This is it Keller versus Alexis!" OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Cat fight!" Before the two women have a chance to lock up Jason Spirit slips his hand between Alexis’ legs and goes for a roll up. Keller was going for a spear but overshoots due to the roll-up and crashes shoulder first into one of the turnbuckles. Alexis manages to kick out of the pin at two. Spirit gets back to his feet first and whips Alexis into the ropes. Springboard Elbow connects! Spirit stumbles backward and lands against the ropes. Keegan Ryan climbs onto the ring apron and hits and Inverted Suplex on Spirit sending the martial arts master to the outside. RILEY STEVENS: "It’s pandemonium out there tonight!" OLIVER THIBODAUX: "No one is winning this match, Riley. They’ll be luck if they survive it." Keller is just starting to stir in the corner when Ryan grabs Alexis by the hair and starts to hammer her with punches. Alexis tries to fight back but Ryan’s size and strength is just too much. Keegan hits a DDT followed by a pin. 1 2 NO! Katie Keller dives in breaking up the pin. Keegan Ryan gets to his feet screaming and rushes in on Keller. He tries for a Clothesline but Keller ducks it. Ryan bounce off the ropes and right into Keller’s own patented DDT, the #SubTweet! RILEY STEVENS: "It’s all over folks!" OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Katie Keller is gonna be golden!" RILEY STEVENS: "Oh my god! Look at Alexis." #SUPERKICKPARTY CONNECTS! Alexis blindsides her former bride to be with a devastating Super Kick. Keller spins on her heels and then collapses with her head hanging over the ropes. Alexis dives on top of Keegan and hooks both legs. 1 2 3 DING! DING! RUBY REYNOLDS: "Your winner and still GIW Television Champion, Alexis Terry!" The referee hands Alexis her title and raises her hand in victory. Alexis quickly rolls out of the ring and makes her way to the back with the title held above her head. Spirit rolls into the ring and helps Keller recover. The two ECWF stars watch Alexis walk up the ring while shaking their heads. RILEY STEVENS: "Alexis did it. SHe managed to retain her title despite the best efforts of her former lover." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "I would have liked to see Alexis really sink her teeth into Keller. I mean these two must be dying to fight each other." RILEY STEVENS: "True but that will have to wait for now just like our viewers will have to wait for more live wrestling action." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "We’ll be right back after a word from our sponsors. Stay tuned!" Fade out to commercial. WINNER: ALEXIS TERRY (STILL GIW TELEVISION CHAMPION) Back from commercial break, we see Katie Keller walking through the halls, a bit distraught over what just happened in the TV title match. Jason Spirit, one of her adversaries just a few moments ago, approaches her from behind. He reaches for her shoulder. JASON SPIRIT: "Katie, are you alright?" Keller’s head sinks as she balls her fists. KATIE KELLER: "Yes.. Jason.. I’m fine. Really." Spirit doesn’t seem to buy the statement. He removes his hand from her shoulder. JASON SPIRIT: "Look, you and I go back a long way. I know when your mind isn’t in it, and now is one of those times. Did you get my note?" She nodded as the gears in her mind began to spin, bringing her back to reality. KATIE KELLER: "Yes. The note. Yes." There was a pause between the two as composure returns to Katie Keller. JASON SPIRIT: "I’m willing to do anything for you. Just let me know." She speaks quietly, but with clear intent. KATIE KELLER: "Well, I’m not done here. There is a lot left to do, and it looks like Blake is about to drop the guillotine on Aspen Chaud. I don’t want to leave before this Revolution is finished." Spirit offers her a small smile. JASON SPIRIT: "Well, you can count on me." Keller nods slowly. KATIE KELLER: "I appreciate that." He planted his feet as he spoke to her. JASON SPIRIT: "For anything. I know we just traded some blows in that match, but it’s all business…" Keller cut him off mid sentence. KATIE KELLER: "I don’t expect anyone in this Revolution to be subordinate. You have every right to try to win a title over me. This is about equal opportunity, not the Callum show." Another pause. JASON SPIRIT: "Then we’re on the same page." She nodded with a tentative smile. KATIE KELLER: "Yes." JASON SPIRIT: "Okay." Keller motioned to the direction she was heading at the beginning of the scene. KATIE KELLER: "I should go. I have a match to get ready for. So do you." Spirit motioned the opposite way. JASON SPIRIT: "Up next for me, actually." She nodded before she left him. KATIE KELLER: "Good luck." The two went their separate ways as we faded into a promotional package for Thunder in Paradise. QUOTE “Sweet Daze” By Pete hits as we get a nice camera view of the crowd standing to their feet as the now familiar music fills the arena. Jason Spirit is out first to a nice ovation from the crowd. He looks a bit beat up already from the Television Championship but he is ready to go. RUBY REYNOLDS: "The following contest is set for one fall. Making his way to the ring, Jason Spirit!" He makes his way to the ring as his music fades and is replaced by “The Nomad” By Iron Maiden. Jaime Cuchillo makes his way to the ring. He looks at Jason in the ring and smiles, knowing Jason is already a bit battered and should be “easy pickings”. He slides into the ring and waits his introduction. RUBY REYNOLDS: "And his opponent, Jamie Cuchillo!" Jaime Cuchillo and Jason Spirit are both ready to go in this match that has been building for the last few weeks spawning from a match where Jaime left Jason high and dry as the man walked out on his partner. Jason hears the bell sound and strikes for with a few stiff kicks to the kidney but Jaime fights them off early. Jason tries for some unorthodox “wrestling” as he again goes for some kicks but Jaime is able to shuck and jive away from them before backing up into the corner and holding onto the ropes. The referee is forced to move Jason back and away from Jaime who can take the time to regroup. Jaime notices Jason talking with the referee and uses the opening to strike hard with a forearm shot. He follows it up with multiple European style uppercuts before dropping Jason to the canvas with a big standing dropkick. He hooks the leg quickly but Jason gets the shoulder up at one. Jaime picks up Jason and quickly drops him back to the mat but this time by connecting with a quick snap suplex. Instead of going right for the pin Jaime hits a double foot stomp and then drops back, hooks the leg, and this time gets a two count. RILEY STEVENS: “Jamie taking advantage of the fact Jason already wrestled once tonight.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: "You might not have been here but this goes back a few weeks. Jamie is going to show that ECWF reject that he doesn't belong in GIW – Jamie is above this guy in every way and he's showing it right here and right now." Jason fights back to a standing position but there is Jaime to whip him into the corner. Jaime tries for a flying clothesline but Jason gets both feet up. Here comes Jason with some life left in him. Bicycle kick connects from Jason and he follows it up with a flying lariat! Jason is back to his feet but Jaime misses with a clothesline and eats a big judo throw. Jason tries for a quick cover but gets a two count. Jason has Jaime up and tries for a suplex but it's blocked. Jaime gets a few stiff jabs into the midsection before Jaime drops Jason with a hurricanrana and holds the leg. 1...2..No! Jason is able to fight out right before the three count. Jaime can't believe it but wastes too much time fighting with the referee. Jaime turns and is met with a big spinning roundhouse kick that echoes throughout the arena. Jaime drops to the mat and Jason covers but the foot of Jamie is on the bottom rope at the last second right before the referee is about to count the three count. RILEY STEVENS: “Jamie was saved by the ropes right there! Jason almost had him.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Almost only counts in horseshoes and hand grenades." Jason was so close to securing the victory there. Unusually for Jason, he climbs the turnbuckle and waits for Jaime to get to his feet. He comes off with a huge flying cross-body but Jaime is able to somehow catch him! Rolling Release Suplex! Jaime hits the move beautiful and quickly hooks the leg...1....2....NO! So close! Jaime is now pounding on the mat and the fans are on their feet screaming for Jason. Jaime measures Jason and tries for a big clothesline but Jason ducks and hits a big spinning kick to the chest. Spirit Driver~! 1... 2.... 3...! RUBY REYNOLDS: "Here is your winner Jason Spirit!" The referee raises the hand of Spirit in victory as he looks a bit exhausted after a back and forth contest. We fade to a couple replays of the match and when we fade back Jason is by the entrance and once more throws his arms up in victory as Jaime holds his head in pain in the ring with a disgusted look on his face. WINNER: JASON SPIRIT After the match, we fade into Aspen’s office. She and Chris Callum are lounging on a leather couch. Aspen is leaned back onto Chris as he sits with his legs extended. CHRIS CALLUM: "I can’t tell you enough how great you are." She giggles like a school girl – a nasally laugh that evokes a large smile from Chris. ASPEN CHAUD: "Oh Chris, stop." He chuckles as he squeezes her tight. CHRIS CALLUM: "No." She shifts onto her elbow inches away from his face. ASPEN CHAUD: "We’re supposed to be scared. Shaking in our boots. Didn’t you know?" Chris’ eyes were locked onto hers as he spoke. CHRIS CALLUM: "Blake Beckett is a loser. We’re winners." Aspen smiled as she ran her fingers along his chin. ASPEN CHAUD: "We’re eternal in the way Alioth Starre always wanted to be." Chris spoke to her quietly, amused by the idea of Beckett dropping the guillotine, as Keller put it earlier in the show. CHRIS CALLUM: "Blake can’t fire you. You’ll tear his company to the ground." She smile, moving in close. ASPEN CHAUD: "And I’ll tell him that." Aspen closed her eyes. CHRIS CALLUM: "Like you should." ASPEN CHAUD: "Mmm…" The door to the office flew open as David Sinclair entered quickly before he stopped in his tracks. Aspen pulled away to smile at him. DAVID SINCLAIR: "Oh, am I interrupting something?" She giggled before she spoke. ASPEN CHAUD: "Of course not. What’s the word?" Sinclair turned to Callum, who’s eyes were still fixated on Aspen. DAVID SINCLAIR: "Uhm… excuse me… Chris I thought… you were?" He turned to Sinclair slowly. CHRIS CALLUM: "Gay?" Tentatively, Sinclair nodded. DAVID SINCLAIR: "Right." Chris rolled his eyes and turned back to Aspen, running his fingers through her hair. CHRIS CALLUM: "Well, they say when you meet the perfect person…" She finished his sentence - ASPEN CHAUD: "… things just fall into place." They giggled together as Sinclair awkwardly adjusted his feet. DAVID SINCLAIR: "Oh. Right. Well, uh, I assume you saw what Beckett had to say." Aspen sighed as she sat upright, away from her toy. ASPEN CHAUD: "He’s fishing for excuses. We can’t stay on that dingy island forever. We need to see the world." Sinclair sighed. DAVID SINCLAIR: "Well, he has a point. The company is hemorrhaging money." Aspen shook her head. ASPEN CHAUD: "Excuses… excuses… as long as we are here, Chris and me, the company will be fine." Sinclair cleared his throat. DAVID SINCLAIR: "I just wanted to be honest with you." Callum spoke to him sternly. CHRIS CALLUM: "And Aspen said not to worry about it. So don’t worry about it." His head sank. DAVID SINCLAIR: "My apologies." There was a pause while Callum stared him down. CHRIS CALLUM: "You’re forgiven." Sinclair held his lips tight. DAVID SINCLAIR: "…thank you.
