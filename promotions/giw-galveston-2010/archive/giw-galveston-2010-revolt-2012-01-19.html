Revolt, 19 January 2012

GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: THURSDAY NIGHT REVOLT LIVE! From North Beach – January 19, 2012 BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! The show starts up and immediately we notice the ring done up really extravagantly with red velvet on the mats and a big table in the middle with cake and other goodies as well as a bunch of balloons scattered around to the ring posts tied up to the ring posts. "Paparazzi" By Lady Gaga hits and the boos in this place are deafening. As if watching them win the belts wasn’t bad enough now they had to hear them gloat about it as Alexis Terry came walking out in a black dress smiling from ear to ear with her Legacy title belt flung over her shoulder. RILEY STEVENS: “If I told you that this was not how I expected the show to start I would be lying. Here is Alexis Terry the new ‘GIW Legacy Champion’ apparently out here to celebrate.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “She’s got the belt, she’s got the title and her best friend is champion to they have a lot to celebrate about.” Alexis heads past the crowd taunting them with her belt as she heads up the steps and into the ring with a smile on her face grabbing a mic that was laying on top of the table in the center of the ring the fans not letting up with the boos. ALEXIS TERRY: “Let’s be quiet everyone, your champion is in the ring.” Naturally the booing only escalated as she rolled her eyes and then raised the mic back up continuing any how. ALEXIS TERRY: “Anyways! I am out here because at ‘Malice’ we witnessed the greatest night in GIW history. In the duel main events the two greatest superstars in GIW overcame the odds and both became champions. Firstly I started the night by defeating Faith Simpson; with ease I might add to become ‘GIW Legacy Champion’. If that was not enough for you fans Aspen Chaud despite facing two other men in essentially a Handicap match won the ‘GIW Undisputed Championship’ to become the champion. And we are out here today to celebrate that so join me in welcoming your new champion…ASPEN CHAUD!” "Did It On 'Em" by Nicki Minaj hits and the boos from earlier are doubled now as not only do we get one of these two we get them both at the same time as Aspen Chaud walks out wearing a white dress and her ‘GIW Undisputed Championship’ around her waist as she heads up the steps and into the ring walking over to Alexis and leaning in for a hug as she looks at the cake, which is a picture of her with the belt. Then she turns and grabs the mic and brings it to her mouth. ASPEN CHAUD: “You are far too kind Alexis, but this night is just as much about you as it is me. I mean we both won the belts at ‘Malice’ which means that any thoughts about anyone else being in control here can be put to rest. WE ARE IN CONTROL! You see with these belts comes the power we need to finally get rid of the problem that is Blake Beckett. This company has for too long been faltering, this company for too long has suffered at the hands of this terrible leader. And with Alexis I stand here today ready to make a change, ready to put an end to his rule. Because HE CAN NOT STOP ME!” On queue “Demon Eyes” by The Answer hits and the fans start to cheer, anything to get them out of listening to these two go on for hours. Out from the back is Blake Beckett wearing his suit as usual heading to the ring as Aspen flips out on the mic. ASPEN CHAUD: “THIS IS MY NIGHT! YOU ARE NOT GOING TO RUIN IT! GO AWAY!” Her voice screeching into the mic as Blake heads up the steps already equipped with a mic of his own as Aspen takes a few steps back and lowers the mic now just screaming at Blake without it as he joins her in the ring. BLAKE BECKETT: “Shut…Up. You know if I was there at ‘Malice’ none of this would have happened. Alexis you wouldn’t have been there to help her out. Joshua wouldn’t have been there give you hand cuffs and we’d have all seen you got your proper ass kicking you so truly deserved. But just like tonight I was not there. Tonight I have a meeting with the Execs from a local venue in New Orleans where we will be holding ‘Retaliation’. But that does not mean that I am not going to be watching and knowing exactly what you are doing here. You see I originally planned to come out here tonight and strip both of you of your title belts.” Aspen almost immediately tenses up seeming ready to throw a punch but contains herself because of the ‘originally planned’ part. The fans however love the idea letting out a big pop for it. BLAKE BECKETT: “But I wanted to give you both a chance to reconsider. Alexis you’ve been following in her foot steps since day one and if you want to you can again and I’ll have no problem stripping you of your belt. Aspen I have stripped you three times already and you know that I am not afraid to do it again. So my ultimatum is this, in that Survive and Conquer match tonight…You two are going to play by the rules. I even barred Joshua Acquin from the area. But just so you know I am going to be back by the end of the night and if you two pull anything…You’ll both be stripped of your belts.” Blake drops his mic and gives them both a smile as he turns and heads out of the ring as Alexis turns to Aspen trying to get her opinion about it all but Chaud simply watches him go, not taking his eyes off him as she breathes in heavily in and out as we take a break. QUOTE “South Texas Death Ride” played over the audio system and Assassin made his entrance toward the ring. He entered and awaited the opponents of the opening battle royal that would begin Thursday Night Revolt. RILEY STEVENS: “After gaining some steam before ‘Malice’ Assassin was derailed by Marcus Hister quickly at ” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “He’ll bounce back don’t worry about it,” His opponent was soon announced and came to the entry way of the aisle. Criss Marquoe, a slender 210 pound wrestler with the height of 5–foot and 7-inches. He seemed fearful to get into the wring with Assassin, as if his entire career rode on his winning, but he hadn’t the ability to overcome it. He slid into the ring and the bell rang to signal the start of the match. DING DING DING! The opponent tried to start with an Enzuigiri after Assassin had caught his side kick, but Assassin pushed his opponent onto the canvas and snickered it off. He then pulled his opponent up from the canvas and performed a hard Spinebuster that connected his opponent’s spine with the flat canvas. With the toying done, Assassin dragged Criss up from the pancake like substance he had become on the canvas and then wrapped up his back and then dropped him down into ‘The Career Killer’ a Rear Naked Choke that is locked in tight. Criss the local star tries to fight his way out doing anything and everything he can to do so but eventually he falls to sleep right in his arms, out cold as the referee jumps in to break the hold as Assassin releases him and he drops like a bag of potatoes. JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…Assassin!” The fans boo him as Assassin gets up throwing his hand into the air in victory as the referee tries to get some help for his unconscious opponent. RILEY STEVENS: “Well he bounces back with a quick victory here tonight and he’s got to be back in the mix here in GIW.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “That’s for sure.” Assassin pulls the camera into him as he starts to mouth a message to the rest of the locker room “One by one, I am coming to the top!” and with that he pushes the camera away and heads to the back. WINNER: ASSASSIN The scene cut to the locker room where Beck Ramsey was finishing lacing up his boots. Him and his brother were about to go out to the ring for their match against Konvict and Outlaw. Seth was already ready in his ring attire as he stood beside his brother who was seated on a steel folding chair. SETH RAMSEY: "Beck, I'm gonna go get a soda. Want one?" BECK RAMSEY: "Nah, get me a grape Gatorade." SETH RAMSEY: "Okay, I'll be back in a minute." With that, Seth walked out of the locker room door and headed off to the left toward the vending machines, leaving Beck to finish off his laces. Once he was finished, he stood up and began pushing his hair back with his hands. Upon one wrist was a hairband which he put around the ponytail that he bunched up in his hands. Walking into camera view was none other than Alexis Terry, who had her Legacy Championship firmly planted over her shoulder. Alexis began to tap her foot on the ground as she dawned a smirk and shook her head. ALEXIS TERRY: "Well, well, well..Mr. Ramsey. You know..I waited up almost all night for that call. All I want is an answer Beck, a lousy answer." BECK RAMSEY: "Oh, my bad, girly. Completely forgot. Got a little drunk at the Malice After-Party." Beck smirked at Alexis as he reached into his locker and pulled out the GIW Tag Team Championship belt and draped it over his shoulder. He glanced down at it then back at Alexis, who he walked over to. They stood very close together so close that Alexis would be able to feel the warmth emanating from Beck's body. BECK RAMSEY: "I promise tonight you'll get your answer. First, I gotta go out there and kick a couple of jokers' asses." Alexis smirked. She leaned in and planted a light kiss on his cheek. That smirk on Beck's face didn't leave through out the whole thing. If anything, it was something that made this asshole even more arrogant. ALEXIS TERRY: "Well..good luck Mr. Ramsey. I know you won't disappoint me." BECK RAMSEY: "Luck is for losers." Beck walked past Alexis and walked out of the locker room door. She watched as he walked out with that smirk still on his face. Perhaps ideas of treason against her impending ally or perhaps Beck had her right where he wanted her. Only time could tell… QUOTE Konvict and Outlaw were first to the scene. The two made there way toward the ring under the sounds of jeers as well as Konvicts theme that played out on the sound system into the arena. Konvict was first in, approaching from the steps and then Outlaw. The two were secured within the ring and had chosen the far right corner as their tag position, where they would meet to continue the brutal battle they had planned for the coming match. It wasn’t long for them to wait before their opponents were present. RILEY STEVENS: “This is a bit of an odd ball team with Konvict and Outlaw. Konvict a proven talent while Outlaw is a bit of a wild card.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Sound like a true tag team though don’t they.” The tag champions, as they had become, made their way to the entry to the aisle. Beck and Seth Ramsey both joined at the aisle and made their hasty way down with the titles on full display for the crowd to see and cheer. RILEY STEVENS: “Here is Seth Ramsey and Beck Ramsey collectively known as Sin City Express. They won the GIW Tag Team titles at ‘Malice’ and are now will carry this division.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I like to call them Beck and the other guy, these fans naturally cheer the doofus.” At least Seth had been cheered. Both men slid into the ring and pandered to the crowd with the titles briefly before moving to their designated corner and it was decided between them that Seth Ramsey would start the match against Konvict. The two stood nearly face to face with one another at the center of the ring just as the bell rang and the bout began. DING DING DING! Konvict started strong with Powerbomb he had transitioned into a Facebuster. He lifted off his opponent and applied the German Suplex when Seth returned to his feet. Konvict got down to knee and foot, looming over Seth and held his head to barrel his fists down with punch after punch before the referee called him off. Seth turned onto his side and grabbed his had with both hands, this opened his back to a hard kick from Konvict to finish out the assault. Konvict wasn’t finished, not in the slightest. Seth had rolled himself out of the ring, away from his corner and in hot territory, but Outlaw made no gesture for him. After a second to recover, he found Konvict hot on his heels and used a drop toe hold to counter his charging opponent face first onto the matted floor. Seth then urgently climbed the rim of the ring and waited for his opponent to get back up, which Konvict did rather quickly. Seth then leapt from the rim and smacked his opponent into the guard barricade with a Hurricanrana. Seth then rushed back into the ring, then back out to break the count. RILEY STEVENS: “Crafty moves here by Seth.” He propped Konvict back up and then positioned him for a Powerbomb outside of the ring. With the maneuver connecting back to matted floor, Seth was satisfied enough that Konvict wouldn’t harass him in time to finish his recovering. That, and Seth went into the ring to tag in his partner Beck. Instead of entering the ring, Beck dropped down from the corner of the ring and met his opponent about half the distance he was going to travel. Konvict struck with a right, and Beck performed the DDT to stun his opponent on the matted floor. RILEY STEVENS: “Beck’s a real stud.” Beck didn’t want to keep the fight outside and soon enough pulled the two of them back into the ring. He went for a cover, but it was countered with a kick out. Konvict pulled himself up with aid of the ropes and jabbed his opponent’s ribs with the flat of his boot, and his knuckles. He then dropped Beck with a big boot to the chin and stumbled back a bit getting ready to finish Ramsey off with his finisher but as he stepped back Outlaw reached over and tagged himself in. He jumped in; heading towards Beck but Konvict grabbed him and pulled him back screaming something at him. When Outlaw shoved him to walk away Konvict grabbed him and hoisted him up right into a Powerbomb throwing him down into the center of the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Konvict just took out his own partner.” Outlaw was out of it as Konvict rolled out of the ring heading to the back as Beck now lined up in the corner. As soon as Outlaw got to his feet he was hit with ‘The Collision’ and the big boot finisher was enough to drop Outlaw as Beck got on top for the three count. JAMAL JACKSON: “Here are your winners…the ‘GIW Tag Team Champions’…SIN CITY EXPRESS!” The fans gave them another mixed reaction as Seth walked over grabbing their belts before re-entering the ring and handing Beck’s to him as the two started to celebrate the victory. RILEY STEVENS: “Another win here by these two and they continue to cement their status as the top tag team here, who will be the ones to come provide these two with a true challenge?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “We shall see.” Konvict never even looked back simply heading to the back as Beck and Seth took some time to celebrate before we went to the back. WINNERS: SIN CITY EXPRESS [Beck Ramsey & Seth Ramsey] After that match we head to the back where Trent Lost is getting ready for his match which is up next and storming through the back not looking pleased at all is Konvict. Who at first seems to head straight by Trent, but it’s Lost who eggs him on with a little joke. TRENT LOST: “Two straight eh?” With that Konvict stops quickly turning and getting right up in Trent’s face breathing in heavily in and out. KONVICT: “If I were you I would shut my mouth. You know as well as everyone else I had a crappy partner and you know even further that I was kicking your ass before you got lucky and snuck out a win. I’d beat you nine times out of ten.” But as he goes to leave Trent sneaks in yet another verbal jab at him just poking the bear. TRENT LOST: “That’s funny cause I’ve beaten you a hundred percent of the time’s we’ve met.” Trent grins as Konvict seems ready to throw a punch but its Zachary Pagonis that intervenes before the fight ensues. ZACHARY PAGONIS: “Guys, guys calm down. I am sort of running errands while Blake is gone tonight, and he wanted me to tell you both something. Next week all of the Survive and Conquer participants from GIW are being given the night off and since neither of you are involved in that you are both going to have matches but not against one another.” This doesn’t seem to sit well with either man but Pagonis hurries into his next sentence to not let them think too hard about it. ZACHARY PAGONIS: “However if you two have something to prove to another, here is what is planned. Konvict you will face one half of the ‘GIW Tag Team Champions’ Seth Ramsey one on one. Likewise you Trent will face the other half of the champions Beck Ramsey. Good luck guys.” With Pagonis heads on his way, figuring if they fought now he wasn’t going to be able to stop them anyways. Konvict heads on his way now as the two were now given a chance against the Tag Team Champions to prove themselves yet again plus one up the other man. ==== Else where in the locker room we see Faith Simpson getting ready for her match as despite previous thoughts to the contrary she would in fact be wrestling in the warm up match here tonight. But as she was getting her shoes off to switch into her wrestling gear the ‘GIW Undisputed Champion’ walks into the room with a smirk and a grin. ASPEN CHAUD: “Oh…You are here? I really didn’t expect to see you. You know since your daddy and mommy wanted their little girl to be safe and all. I hurt my nose at ‘Malice’ to but I guess continuing to fight is what a champion does…Something you now realize you only were because you faced bottom tier fighters.” Faith looks up at her, gritting her teeth really not interested in getting into any drama here tonight but now forced to do so with Aspen. ASPEN CHAUD: “But when you are fighting for some other company that daddy or Mark tells you is supposedly better and you are beating nobodies I want you to see me as champion and remember that you were NEVER GOOD ENOUGH for GIW…” Aspen readjusts her title pulling no shots on the young girl who just like Cross held back her words. ASPEN CHAUD: “You know how they say the only way to go from here is up? That’s not true. You peaked Faith, you peaked. Now Mark Cross, your daddy, and your mommy are going to bring you to some other company where sure you’ll win but you’ll never really feel like you accomplished anything. Your career…WILL BE A JOKE!” Faith continues to look down at Faith who she had always actually been taller then. ASPEN CHAUD: “I am glad that you chose to fight tonight because I am going to make sure your last night here, is a sad one. Because just like your career, I am going to send you falling. Tell Mark I said hi, I hope he’s enjoying beating up Japanese losers.” Aspen goes to head out but Faith grabs her arm before she can go turning her back towards her. FAITH SIMPSON: “You are an awful person…And I am going to make sure that my last night here, will end with me celebrating while you watch.” One of the more serious moments from Faith as Aspen blows it off and heads on out as Faith angrily looks at her sitting down and continuing to get ready. QUOTE The first out to the entry way was the tag team of Momma’s Pride. Incurring the jeers of the crowd, they slid into the ring and awaited their opponents. RILEY STEVENS: “Here we go with the debuting tag team of Momma’s Pride and before you say a word I am going to have them mute your mic Oliver.”] The second to the aisle was Trent Lost. The returning Trent stopped about half way at the aisle, his theme cut off as a small trickling of ambient noise, Alioth’s new theme, delved into Edward Shearmur’s “Grand Central”. The crowd cheered this odd couple team and the two met at the middle of the pathway, finishing the route into the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “These two teaming together? It hardly even makes sense.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Black people are-” RILEY STEVENS: “You had your shot and you blew it.” DING DING DING! It was decided that Trent Lost would start the match for their team, and I.B. Watkins for the other side. Trent maneuvered to the center of the ring with his opponent and locked hands, fingers clenched. The other arm raised as well, but instead of another lock up, Trent belted I.B. in the abdomen with the flat of his right boot. He twisted the hand in his possession into a wrist lock and then pushed him off. Trent motioned to his opponent for a second lock up, but this time it was a proper grapple. I.B. began to hold his weight over Trent, even pushing him down into the canvas, which Trent quickly recovered from. They locked up for a third time as Trent was adamant about correcting that wrong. I.B. began the power struggle again and loomed over Trent, their bodies arched and locked in the conflict until Trent performed multiple head butts to his opponent’s skull. The final cranial smash knocked I.B. backward, which he too recovered quickly from, but not as well as Trent had. RILEY STEVENS: “Trent and Alioth have faced each other so many times, they have to have built some sense of chemistry through that.” Angel moved behind I.B. at the ropes and urged him on. She wasn’t ready to enter the ring just yet, but she cheered I.B. to continue, until Trent came a little too close for her comfort and she returned to the corner. Trent shooed her off and pulled I.B. up against the ropes for a few loud, hard chops across the chest, then an Irish whip. Trent quickly set his opponent up for the argentine Facebuster and covered him for a pin, but his opponent had kicked out at the count of two. The crowd began to build from a slow rouse. They wanted Alioth in the ring, and Angel could tell a hot tag was what the crowd needed. She kept to her corner and let I.B. take control of the match as he had begun to after the botched pin. After a DDT and the set up for a big boot, the crowd simmered back down. RILEY STEVENS: “Trent needs to get Alioth in there.” I.B. yelled back at the fans as he lifted Trent’s leg, then stomped on his abdomen. Trent curved his body to a fetal position from the attack, but managed to counter a second stomp with his boot driven into I.B.’s knee. I.B. limbed backward, more of a stumble, really. He began to move toward his corner when Trent had gotten to his feet and charged, spread arm, and clotheslined his opponent over the top rope. I.B. crashed like a spiraling plane into the matted floor, his back shot up to smack into the barricade. The crowd cheered and some even stood from their seat in chant. Trent looked across the ring toward his long time rival, the man’s hand extended for a tag, but Trent instead stepped outside of the ring and onto the floor to gather I.B. up like the mess he had become and throw him back into the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Trent and Alioth working well together thus far.” Once back inside, Trent pulled I.B. up by the large head of afro’d hair and performed another set of multiple head butts that knocked him toward his corner. Angel took the liberty of tagging herself in and charged into Trent’s boot when she had tried to spear him. He covered her for the pin, but the count was broken just after one. The crowd was still demanding a tag from him, and Trent seemed intent on weakening Angel first, but he would glance back at his former rival, his current tag partner. Trent mopped Angel with his boot back toward her corner and forced I.B. to tag back in. She was too small to handle either him or Alioth, but Trent had no qualms about wrecking I.B. further. I.B. came in and immediately drove his head into Trent’s abdomen, then a fist, then a kick. He smacked Trent with a few fists to the face and chest, ultimately aiming him toward the center of the ring. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I am so confused who to root for…” I.B. set Trent up with an irish whip, then backed into the other ropes to propel himself as well with enough momentum to meet his opponent at center again. Both men threw up their boots and knocked the other flat on the canvas. This, the crowd saw, was the time to chant for a tag and demand one be made. The heat began to build upward from the center of the ring as a count-out began and the two men slowly moved from their positions. I.B. seemed like he would be the first one to make the tag, he clambered up the ropes, instead of arrowing for Angel in the corner, whereas Trent had continued to crawl toward his own tag corner and leapt up to tag in the returning former Undisputed Champion, Alioth Starre. RILEY STEVENS: “Here comes Starre!” As I.B. pulled himself up the ropes and turned, leaning his back against it, he was met with another clothesline to the outside. Angel would have none of this and she pulled through into the ring, only to find herself clotheslined to the matted floor. The crowd cheered and chanted en masse. It took a few moments for I.B. to collect himself and Angel, then clamber back into the ring, but when he did, thus breaking the referee’s count, he was rounded up by Alioth and pulled closer to him by the arm. Alioth twisted under that arm in his grasp and yanked down on it. The canvas reverberated the banging and then a second time for good measure when Alioth did it again to keep I.B. powerless to counter him. With arm held up, Alioth drove a heart punch into I.B.’s upper chest and staggered him back. Alioth then hoisted him up by the knee and waist, then dropped his opponent down on his with a shin breaker. I.B. was still on his feet and staggering, though one leg was now limp. RILEY STEVENS: “I.B. is not in good shape.” Alioth saw this as the perfect opportunity to seal the deal with a kick. He moved back, pressing into the ropes for momentum and came back at his opponent with a hard hitting super kick. He covered I.B., but his opponent seemed to just barely break the count. Intrigued, Alioth had a different plan for his opponent. He grabbed the limp knee of I.B. Watkins and other after that. He molded them with his and dropped down on the flat of his back to finalize the application of his figure-four leg lock finisher, the ‘Four Star Mary’ as he called it. After moments of trying to escape, and grating his teeth for release, I.B. tapped out to flee the hold, and the match. JAMAL JACKSON: “Here are your winners…Alioth Starre and Trent Lost!” The fans cheer for the duo as Alioth Starre pulls away from the hold as I.B. rolled out of the ring to the outside falling to the mat holding his legs as Angel ran over to check on him. RILEY STEVENS: “These two made their return at ‘Malice’ and you have to be impressed. Whether or not this team lasts or not, they got their first win here on Revolt in a long, long time.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Whatever.” The two look at one another and give a respecting nod as Alioth leaves the ring and heads to the back with Trent exiting the ring soon after both happy but moving on to bigger and better things. WINNER: ALIOTH STARRE & TRENT LOST It’s right before the last match of the night that being the one everyone came to see. And backstage as usual doing his pre-fight stretches is Tony Miranda trying to block out everything before his match but like a fly that just won’t go away his tranquility is broken as Alexis Terry walks over with a grin on her face and her ‘GIW Legacy Championship’ over her shoulder. ALEXIS TERRY: “Hey there champ—OH WAIT!” Alexis has herself a good laugh as Tony gets up looking at her, looking like a man who has had just about enough of all this stuff. TONY MIRANDA: “I am guessing that telling you to go away won’t work?” Tony stares at her as she shakes her head giddily seeming to be enjoying poking fun at him. ALEXIS TERRY: “Remember before the Main Event this Sunday at ‘Malice’ how you said that it would be me laying face down on the mat…Well…Look what happened.” Terry has a big laugh again at his expense as he looks at her with a very straight face suddenly snapping back at her with a very angry tone. TONY MIRANDA: “I’ve come to the realization that if I am going to get the fair match I desire with Aspen I am going to take each and every one of her little helpers out. So if that means putting you on the shelf…I am going to do just that.” Tony brushes by her, his words really echoing into Alexis who looks at him a little shocked as she watches him head to the ring finally repositioning her belt and then blowing him off as she to heads out to the ring. ==== Else where Alex Castellanos is seen tying up his boots as sitting next to him done for the day with her job Sarah Sharp sits not to far away from him looking at him a little worried. SARAH SHARP: “Are you sure you don’t want me out there tonight?” Alex nods his head not wanting her to be involved in anything that might potentially get her hurt as he finishes one boot’s shoe laces. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “Not tonight, I’ve got to get the win tonight…Get my number one contender’s shot.” Alex finishes off the other one now as he is talking as he stands up and she does so with him. SARAH SHARP: “That’s the thing, Aspen made that offer this week. I really hope that you didn’t listen, right?” Castellanos looks at her like she is crazy then shakes his head no as he stretches out his arm. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “Of course not. I just want to get that win and make sure that I have the opportunity to get her back for everything she’s done to me…” Alex prepares to head out but Sarah stops him before she goes. SARAH SHARP: “Blake wouldn’t want you to flip out; I don’t care what she does or says…I want you to walk away…Be the bigger person until you get your match.” The two leaned in and shared in a hug before Alex turned and headed on out for his match as we take a break before the Main Event. QUOTE It’s now Main Event time and as we come back to the ring we find the ring full of superstars already. Faith Simpson, Tony Miranda, Alex Castellanos, and Marcus Hister all lined up watching Alexis Terry and Aspen Chaud make their entrance together despite the fact it’s a all for yourself battle royal. RILEY STEVENS: “All six of these stars will represent GIW in the upcoming Survive and Conquer event a annual match featuring the best wrestlers in the world. They’ll be a huge amount of superstars from all over the world in that match and it’ll be the last four in the ring to advance through the Battle Royal stage. Tonight they’ll have a chance to work on that, against their fellow GIW superstars. But right now it looks like though Alexis and Aspen are joining into the ring together they are entering the ring in a hostile environment with several stars who hate em’.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Whatever, they’ll still overcome the odds.” Aspen and Alexis hand out there title belts to the man at ringside as they then head into the ring heading to a corner together as Aspen and Alexis talk amongst each other. As they do this however the rest of the people in the ring them eye them like the last food on an island as the referees on the outside two of them to be exact start the match. DING DING DING! Right away Aspen starts scheming as she looks around at the four, then focusing in on Hister. The others seem to be waiting to see what she does as she points to him and waves him over to them as she starts to try to call his name, the camera catching her mouthing it. “Hey…hey…hey…you.” Like the awful person she is she is unable to think of his name despite their long time friendship and this makes him angry as he’s the first to rush at the duo heading at them in the corner going wild with punches. The crowd goes wild for Hister hoping he’ll be the one to take them out but now the others Faith, Tony, and Alex head on over the four all wanting a piece of them. Faith pulls Alexis away and throws her into the opposite corner now going after her as Tony, Marcus, and Alex are going after Aspen. She balls up in the corner in the fetal position trying to save herself but it’s now Marcus that pushes Alex away telling him that she is his. Alex now pushes him back and then Alex also pushes Tony all wanting to take out Aspen themselves. RILEY STEVENS: “These men’s hatred for Aspen is so strong that they are willing to fight one another for her.” They all exchange some words as Aspen crawls under the ropes to protect herself but it’s Alex that randomly snaps off a ‘Golden Arrow’ to the jaw of Hister that sends him flying into Tony who grabs him and hurls him over the ropes sending him to be the first man eliminated. Marcus Hister is Eliminated! With that we are now down to just five as Alex and Tony reach down pulling out Aspen from the corner yet again willing to work together for the sake of it tonight. They whip her across the ring and Alex rushes at her nailing her with a Clothesline as she stumbles out right into a European Uppercut. On the other side of the ring Alexis is nearly over the top rope as Faith tries to pry her grip on the ropes. But Terry is able to fall back into the ring to avoid elimination. This does not stop Faith from continuing to drop boots on her, her last night but wanting some revenge here tonight on the girl that beat her on Sunday. Faith stands her up and then Arm Drags her to the mat. Back to the other side of the ring Alex and Tony attempt to throw Aspen over the ropes but showing her true fighting spirits as they try to hoist her up she just falls to the mat as dead weight to avoid it. RILEY STEVENS: “That is our champion ladies and gentleman.” Alex and Tony both go to lift her up at the same time but it’s now time for their team work to end as the two stare at one another and they push back and forth then starting a big brawl with punches going back and forth finally with Alex kneeing him in the gut and throwing him into the ring post. All the while Faith and Alexis continue to go at it, Alexis seeming to get the advantage now trying to focus on the face area that she busted open at ‘Malice’. Back with Tony and Alex fans boo their inability to team up and then start to boo even more when Alex walks over and extends a hand to Aspen pulling her up to her feet. He nods his head seeming to say something which makes her happy. RILEY STEVENS: “No…Way…Is Alex joining her again?” But as she goes to pull away from him he keeps a hold of her hand, suddenly shaking his head no as he looks at her. Alex Castellanos is Eliminated! The fans boo Tony’s actions a bit for the first time but they seem to be ok with it when he starts to hammer away at Aspen who is yet again hiding against the ropes. On the other side of the ring Alexis whips Faith into the corner and rushes at her only for her to dodge it and side step out of the way. Simpson immediately heads to the top rope going where the fans love to see her as she jumps off landing a Top Rope Hurricanrana that gets the fans going. On the outside we see Alex heading to the backstage area trying to keep his composure as Sarah asked. RILEY STEVENS: “Wouldn’t it be amazing for Faith to win tonight, maybe she’d stay?” Alexis falls down into the corner struggling as Faith rushes at her following it up with a Dropkick to her chest as Terry sits in the corner. These two are treating this like a ‘Malice’ rematch having not taken focus off one another since the match started. On the other side Aspen now starts to get up as Tony pulls her up constantly taunting her, which gets the fans back on his side as she holds onto the corner as he grabs her and Eye Rakes her, a completely new Tony we are seeing out here tonight. RILEY STEVENS: “What is Beck Ramsey out here for?” Ramsey slides into the ring using his title belt first on Tony who has his back to him and nails him with the title belt to the back of the head. Tony falls to the mat not looking too hot as he seems to not be moving as Faith rushes at Beck ducking under his title and then Dropkicking him in the back sending him to the mat trying to fight back. RILEY STEVENS: “These referees can’t do a thing, this is legal.” Aspen points over at Tony who is getting up but is still foggy as Alexis and Beck now grab him up and hoist him up as he seems to be done for. They pull him towards the ropes and then start to push him over. They get him over but at the last second he breaks away and gets his footing on the outside of the ropes. Ramsey heads out of the ring getting booed by the crowd as Faith gets up to her feet, looking at her predicament as she realizes she is in trouble. Aspen and Alexis join arms and rush at her but Faith ducks under and it and bounces off the ropes trying to use her speed as she often has before. Rushing off the ropes Aspen and Alexis reversed directions and then Faith jumped up using both legs to take them out with double dropkick! RILEY STEVENS: “Can she do it!?” These fans are getting behind her as she gets to her feet and looks around at them using their energy as she kicks away Aspen as she gets up and then does the same to Alexis. Left out to dry Faith raises a leg into her face sending her falling back into the ring.
