GIW — Card

Revolt, 18 November 2010

18 November 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: Thursday Night Revolt! LIVE NOVEMBER 18th, FROM SEAGULL STADIUM IN GALVESTON, TEXAS BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! Rather then the usual start we head right into a match as "The Edge Of Paradise" by Kamelot begins to blast throughout the speakers. Tony Miranda begins to walk down the ramp, very cockily about his upcoming match up. He goes up the steel steps and enters the ring. Quickly, he poses as though he were in some sort of rush. He gets off the top turnbuckle (where he posed, of course) and then begins yelling some instructions at the referee. The two get in an argument rather quickly. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Combustible elements here, folks.” RILEY STEVENS: “This should be a good match. Both Jake Kennedy and Tony Miranda have impressed over the past couple of weeks, especially Kennedy. Whoever wins this one won’t win it because they are the better but because they got lucky. These two are virtually the same in terms of caliber.” "I Will Not Bow" by Breaking Benjamin begins to play. Jake Kennedy stops on the ramp and begins to waste the time of a lot of people by simply standing there. Once he’s done looking around, from behind him a shadowy figure begins to tower. Jake Kennedy looks to his left, where the shadowy figure stands. It’s Wake Walker with a really wide grin. Jake Kennedy walks into the ring. Wake Walker stands on the outside, keeping this terrible grin. The match begins. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Wake Walker is sure to play a big factor into this match.” RILEY STEVENS: “No kidding.” Wake Walker begins to tell some special instructions to Jake Kennedy. Jake Kennedy smiles and nods. Tony Miranda goes to the center of the ring and then invites Jake Kennedy to get closer. However, Jake Kennedy doesn’t really budge from his spot and just watches Tony Miranda for a few seconds. Tony Miranda immediately thinks this is the work for Wake Walker and then changes his attention to Wake. Usually, Jake would take advantage but he doesn’t this time. He waits. Tony Miranda tries to throw a shot at Jake but Jake blocks the hit and then lands a right fist of his own, sending Miranda to the floor. Miranda quickly cowers to his corner and then begins to look around in a lot of shock. He stands back up and then begins to try and surround Jake Kennedy. He tries to hit a roundhouse kick but Jake Kennedy catches the leg of Tony Miranda. Tony Miranda manages to keep his balance for a few seconds. He tries to go for an enziguri but fails after Jake Kennedy dodges it. Jake Kennedy suddenly kicks the crotch of Tony Miranda blatantly. Jake Kennedy smiles. Wake Walker slowly claps. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “What the hell just happened?” RILEY STEVENS: “….” Jake Kennedy begins to entice a major beat down on Tony Miranda. Tony Miranda begins to gain some steam but Jake Kennedy quickly runs away. Wake Walker points at Tony and says, “Nothing personal, buddy!” RILEY STEVENS: “The referee has disqualified Jake, but you have to wonder why Wake would have Jake do this? Perhaps this is all the momentum he thought Jake needed?” The two leave in disapproval of the crowd with Tony watching on getting the Disqualification victory, but these two’s beef having to be settled some other time as it appears Walker didn’t want Kennedy to waste any energy before his title match. WINNER: TONY MIRANDA (By Disqualification) = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = We now head to the backstage area where the fans are greeted by the seemingly upset face of Amanda Rowland seeming to be rolling through the halls, when suddenly Alexii Ravynova talking to a staff member appears and she makes a B-Line towards her, tapping her shoulder and wasting little time addressing her. AMANDA ROWLAND: “What was that last week? I mean you making the calls on two title belts? Without asking me about either of it. You do remember that I am in charge around here right? You are the Head of Talent Relations, which means you ask me before making those decisions.” Alexii seems to be a bit unworried as usual as she looks over at the staff member and waves him off before returning to respond to Amanda. ALEXII RAVYNOVA: “Is this you arguing with my decisions or is this you arguing with the fact I made them?” Alexii seems all too coy as she responds however it seems to strike a nerve with Amanda, who perhaps is letting the power go to her head. AMANDA ROWLAND: “Whatever, the fact remains that I don’t want you hear. I just have to abide by the contract Blake gave you before he left. Besides Head of Talent Relations, seems hardly fitting when you couldn’t see talent if it were staring you in the face. Just then Mark Cross GIW’s newest star walks across the backstage area and Alexii stops him with her hands. ALEXII RAVYNOVA: “How about this then? The guy I just signed Mark Cross against a star of your choosing at ‘Requiem’. So we can see who really knows talent?” Mark nods his head willingly taking on the challenge as Alexii walks away leaving Amanda to basically have to accept it as she seems to think of who she’ll pick. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = Back in the ring we come where Nick Ridicule and Rex Armstrong are in the ring arguing as they prepare to tag up here tonight. They look to the stage as “Ring of Fire” by Johnny Cash and the fans look excitedly to the stage as suddenly out pops Jose and Jos B. making up Los Firemen! The fans seem pretty into them right from the start as they run out high fiving them as they head to the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Here’s our newest tag team Los Firemen!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “This looks promising…” Together they slide into the ring the crowd clapping for them now as they head to the top rope, holding up their arms then jumping down with Jos starting and Rex on the other side starting as the referee calls for the bell. DING DING DING! Perhaps not expecting them to be so quick Jos catches Rex off guard as he runs at him nailing him with a big dropkick right to start off the match. The fans cheer as he hops and Rex does the same walking right into an Arm Drag. Jos B waits until he’s up again before going in for a lock up, that Rex avoids by trying to throw him backwards. Instead Jos falls back into the ropes then jumps over them and quickly right back onto them going Springboard on Rex as he tries to catch his breath, nailing him with a Springboard Elbow Smash! The fans cheer as Jos gets them FIRED UP, as he goes to lift Rex up but is given a big kick to the knee, out of desperation from Rex. Awesome jumps up and tags into Nick who hops up and attacks. Jos instead whips him into the corner then tags into Jose who jumps up onto the Top Rope and with Jos holding him for just a second hitting a Diving Armdrag to Nick. However he wastes little time in setting up his infamous ‘Jaws Of Life’ a Mandible Claw on Nick. But Rex hops in and he instead gets hit with it! The fans cheer as Rex suffers in pain as Jose tortures him with the move. Behind him Nick is getting to his feet and seems to be about to hit a move on Jose, when Jos turns him around having gotten into the ring and picks him up and hits him with the ‘Firetruck Driver’ a Fire Thunder Driver! The fans cheer as Jos steps out of the ring and Rex bails out of the ring in pain as Jose turns around and makes the cover 1…2…3! Great debut. RILEY STEVENS: “Talk about a debut the fans love them!” The fans cheer loud as both men head to the top rope holding up their arms in pride as they happily celebrate their victory. However the fans seem a bit confused when two more masked men come running down towards the ring, they slide into the ring and immediately attack Los Firemen from behind. This team is called Technical Mechanica as they begin to assault Los Firemen who finally fight back forcing Damon Ramon and Ombio to bail out of the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Looks like we got some enemies here.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Looks more like the Spanish Channel to me.” Los Firemen call for Technical Mechanica to get back in the ring but they instead head backwards up the ramp waiting to continue this fight another time. WINNERS: LOS FIREMEN = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = Again it’s time to head to the backstage area where there is quite the commotion as we come into a scene where three divas are bickering amongst each other about something. It’s Alexii Ravynova that separates the three of them, they are the new divas known as Syd, Skye, and Ivy. All of them seemingly unable to get along at all. ALEXII RAVYNOVA: “Ladies, calm it down. I have a feeling that you are bickering about whose prettier, who’s more talented, and who would win in the ring? However as some of you may know after last week, the Women’s title has been retired. But if you know anything about GIW coming in, you know that Aspen Chaud is one of our best competitors and she faces men just as well. So to settle this little quarrel I want all of you to show up for the Open Battle Royal at ‘Requiem’ because the winner will be getting an Evolve title shot, bye ladies.” Alexii waves and heads on her way as this has only seeming caused more of a stir between them but the camera instead pans away from that scene to across the hall where Aspen Chaud is leaning against a wall seeming deeply into thought, she’s suddenly caught off guard when Wake Walker walks into view speaking up. WAKE WALKER: “Have a feeling you’re thinking about me?” Walker seems to have a little innuendo in his words as she seems to snap back to reality looking at him, still with that same level of distrust as she’s had the past few weeks. ASPEN CHAUD: “I’m thinking about whether I can trust you as my partner tonight. Certainly not about the way you look, there’s certainly a lot of guys better looking then you around here, like him.” Aspen smiles and points to Dr. House the resident Doctor around GIW who waves to the both of them as he walks by Aspen giving Wake a little grin. WAKE WALKER: “Apparently I wasn’t too bad looking considering you used to date me.” Wake seems to realize that his joke was the perfect counter as she seems unsure what to say next to that, then answering back. ASPEN CHAUD: “And just like I was the Boss of that relationship, I expect to be in our tag match tonight. Just know that I still don’t trust you, so if you try anything I will be waiting. As far as I’m concerned, you’re still an enemy.” There jokes seem to have been turned real quickly as Aspen walks away from the scene these two still clearly not on the same page as we take a quick break. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = We suddenly come back into the ring where we are already in the middle of a match with Ryan Blake absolutely hammering away at Cory Chevelle on the mat of the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Welcome back everybody! To our surprise this fight was started during the break and almost immediately Ryan Blake began his career by absolutely dominating Cory Chevelle! In what might be the shocker of the year in what many thought was a shoe in for Cory Chevelle to qualify for the Evolve title shot.” Back in the ring we watch as the ever strong Cory begins to get to his feet despite Ryan’s attacks, the fans cheering him on wanting him to beat Ryan who they clearly dislike from the start. As he’s about to his feet he again shocks the world when he nails Cory with a Double Knee Facebreaker! The fans are actually in full shock having been caught off guard by Ryan Blake who stands over the beast of a man, seemingly abusing him as he backs up watching as Cory attempts to get to his feet though clearly in pain. When he’s up to his knees still seeming out of it Ryan runs across the ring and crushes him with ‘The Causality’ a Running Shinning Wizard! The fans are stunned as he makes the cover 1…2…3! RILEY STEVENS: “This kid Ryan Blake just stopped the seemingly unstoppable Cory Chevelle and just two matches into his career will have a shot at the GIW Evolve Championship!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I’m already liking this guy.” The fans seem shocked into amazement as Ryan Blake holds up his arm in the air as he looks at the man that he just took out before motioning to the crowd that soon the title will be his as we go to another quick break. WINNER: RYAN BLAKE (Qualifies for GIW Evolve Championship Match at ‘Requiem’) = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = Rather then heading to the back we remain inside of the ring and with the announcers as we cut back to GIW’s biggest jobber Paul Defias standing in the ring jumping up and down as he prepares for one of the most anticipated matches in a long time as “Demon Eyes” by The Answer hits and the fans begin to immediately boo as out from the back in ring gear this time Blake Beckett, walking towards the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Never thought I’d see the day when the founder of GIW would wrestle here on Revolt.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I for one want him to do well, let’s go Blake.” Beckett doesn’t seem scared but the fans seem to think of him as more of just a joke then a real wrestler as he steps into the ring. The referee that he employed looks at him with a bit of a chuckle as he calls for the bell, with Beckett starting the match off. DING DING DING! Everyone in the arena seems to be waiting to see what stupid stuff he starts off with first; including Defias who can’t help but think that this match will be a cake walk. Defias goes in for a lock up but to the surprise of everyone Blake spins him right into a Sleeper Hold! The fans are stunned as he seems to know just what he’s doing as Paul seems to start to fade right away. But Paul battles out of it just before he passes out walking forward, looking back at Blake with a surprised expression that’s shared by the rest of the fans in the arena. Defias seeming to be a bit embarrassed runs at him but Blake ducks his clothesline attempt and Paul decides to continue on off the ropes running right into a Big Boot! The fans again seem shocked as Blake looks out at them giving them a slick grin as he sets up for the move as Paul gets up before he runs off the ropes and nails him with a Spear! He immediately pulls up his leg 1…2…3! Blake Beckett, The Boss with the win. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “He’s a beast!” RILEY STEVENS: “As hard of a time I’m having taking this in it appears that Blake does know a bit about wrestling after that win. Though wins over Defias are meaningless.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Just wait, he’s going to show the world what he can do.” The fans still boo him though they all have to agree that it was quite the showing here from Blake Beckett who demands the referee to raise his hand as it appears Amanda’s means of punishing Blake has instead turned into him finding a new career as he heads to the back. WINNER: BLAKE BECKETT = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = Almost immediately we cut to the back where the two teams from earlier Technical Mechanica and Los Firemen seem to be arguing in Spanish with one another, with Amanda Rowland looking on in between them, she coughs to get their attention as they all look at her. AMANDA ROWLAND: “Look guys I have an idea. I’ve had an idea for tag team belts for awhile now, so you know what? At ‘Requiem’ we are going to debut the tag team belts and the rules for the belts will be simple. You have to win three tag team matches together to become contenders. And seeing as it only seems right the match for the belts at ‘Requiem’ will be two out of three falls.” The wrestlers all seem to be appeased as they storm off in separate ways with Amanda shaking her head and walking forward as to the back has come Blake Beckett, walking right towards her. BLAKE BECKETT: “How was that for you? You thought you had me beat? But I could beat anyone you put up against me. Now where’s my money?” Amanda holds up her hand indicating for him to wait as she seems to conjure up the ability to say what she has planned. AMANDA ROWLAND: “Actually, that’s what I came to you about. You see I need a wrestler to face Alexii’s at ‘Requiem’ and well…” Amanda seems to be unable to continue as she thinks about it with Blake stopping her with a big, big grin now. BLAKE BECKETT: “And you’ve run out of people to ask? Funny. I’ll do it, but if I win you owe me!” Beckett walks off with a big smirk on his face realizing just how much this is going to hurt Amanda as she stands there, doing just that regretting having done this already. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = Back to the ring we go as seemingly the same trend continues as Techno Viking a new star stands inside of the ring, looking up at the ramp. “Fatlip” by Sum 41 hits and the fans begin to boo as the villain as of late Eric Sparrow walks out from the back only this time he wields a microphone in his hand talking as he heads to the ring. ERIC SPARROW: “So Amanda Rowland think it’s ok that I be pushed aside when the world knows I deserve the Galveston Island title shot. If I recall right I was this close to winning it, and by some sort of luck Walker beat me. Yet now Aspen Chaud an undeserving woman, get’s the shot instead of me. While I am relegated to facing the likes of this guy…” Sparrow points into the ring at Viking who is now pacing angry with Sparrow’s mocking ways as Eric steps into the ring still continuing to talk on the microphone. ERIC SPARROW: “And rather then just being handed the Evolve title, they choose to make me qualify for it. Yet this place wonders why I ended two careers last week and took out Aspen and Wake? They don’t treat me well enough. And unlucky for them I’ve reached my breaking point so from here on out people are going to pay for their actions.” Sparrow suddenly crushes Viking with the microphone completely taking him out. He however quickly points out to the referee that they are both here and that he must start the match, though unwillingly he does just that. DING DING DING! With Viking out cold Sparrow wastes little time in scaling to the top rope where he looks down upon his victim Techno Viking. Rather then just do it he chooses to taunt him and the crowd as he looks out at them soaking in their boo’s as he continues to look down on his prey, a completely knocked out Techno Viking. Finally he jumps off nailing him with the always spectacular ‘Spinal Tap’ better known as the Spiral Tap, to finish him off. He instead just simply lays a foot on him, as the referee reluctantly makes the count 1…2…….3! RILEY STEVENS: “What a travesty! This is the worst way to qualify for a match.” Sparrow now gets to his knees beside Viking holding up his fallen foe who is clearly out cold as he wraps his hand around his head then grabs the broken yet functioning mic as the blood pours down Viking’s face. ERIC SPARROW: “The blood is on your hands GIW.” Despite having qualified Sparrow shows little care instead choosing to send a message to GIW as he lets Viking fall to the mat standing up with a big grin on his face as we take another break. WINNER: ERIC SPARROW (Qualifies for GIW Evolve Championship Match at ‘Requiem’) = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = We come back to the ring where Will Fane and Se7en are both already standing in he ring, talking. Next out is Aspen Chaud coming to huge cheers from the crowd, clearly on her side as she gets into the ring waiting for her partner. When Wake Walker came out, people cheered but some were unsure as to whether to continue supporting. It is to be noted that Jake Kennedy was on the side of Wake Walker, seemingly returning the favor from earlier. RILEY STEVENS: “You can feel the tension between Aspen and Wake even against these lesser opponents.” The match begins. Aspen Chaud begins to speak to Wake as though he was some sort of stranger. She volunteers to start the match. Se7en runs at Aspen and tries to hit a clothesline but Aspen easily dodges it and hits a regular suplex. She then picks up Se7en and then hits a back body drop. She quickly goes for the cover. 1…kick out! Aspen Chaud begins to lock in a chokehold. She looks at Wake Walker. For some reason, the two appear to be hypnotized. Aspen Chaud loses focus and is hit in the face by a couple of elbows that Se7en managed to hit. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “These two, can’t work together I swear.” Se7en tags in Fane and now a two on one assault begins to take place. Wake Walker watches as the referee forces Se7en to exit. The next two minutes of the match involve Aspen Chaud trying to get back into the groove of things. She finally manages to hit an enziguri to Fane. Fane and Aspen are at the center of the ring. The two begin to drag themselves towards their partners. Aspen Chaud jumps in the air from the mat, with all her trust and tags in Walker! Wake goes in and begins to wreak havoc, absolutely dominating both the rookies. RILEY STEVENS: “Here comes the champ!” Wake Walker eventually sets up Se7en for The Trickster and hits it! Square in the center of the ring, Wake Walker begins to go for the cover but before he can fully get the pin count, Will Fane quickly enters the ring. Aspen Chaud notices that he wants to break up the count so Aspen hits a big AirBarbie on Fane of the top rope! The referee begins to count! 1…2… 3! Aspen Chaud and Wake Walker begin to jump up and down, celebrating their victory and realizing that they were able to be a cohesive unit after all. Suddenly, they realize what they are doing and stop the hugs. There is a brief stare down between the two. Wake Walker retrieves his title belt and begins to leave. Aspen Chaud asks Wake to stop. He does. Aspen grabs a microphone. ASPEN CHAUD: “I just can’t….why would you…. You were honest? You’re really honest about all this! We won the match! You…me… we…. You’re not a … I just want to say that…I’m surprised, Wake. I am surprised.” Aspen seems to be letting out all her emotions seeming to now trust Wake. Wake Walker quickly grabs the microphone off the hands of Aspen Chaud. WAKE WALKER: “No need for acting so surprised, Aspen. I was always on the side of Galveston Island Wrestling. However… that’s the past and the present is now. Don’t you think for one second that I’m not going to try my best to defeat you at ‘Requiem’. This title belt is mine… it always will be mine and you’ll never have it again.” The fans give out a little anticipation wondering if him bringing up their match would set in the reality of the situation. Wake Walker hands the microphone back to Aspen who brings it quickly to her lips seemingly slightly giddy. ASPEN CHAUD: “We’ll see about that!” “Bullseye” begins to play as Aspen drops the microphone. Wake Walker stares at Aspen longer and holds his belt high up. The crowd cheers for the two seeming to realize that these two’s match will be about respect after all. Aspen looks at the title belt as though she were looking at the brass ring. Wake Walker exits the ring and tells Jake Kennedy to leave with him. RILEY STEVENS: “After all the turmoil between the two it seems that their personal beefs are gone and this match at ‘Requiem’ will be all about the gold, be sure to tune in for this HUGE show coming up on Sunday.” Jake Kennedy looks back at Aspen. Aspen gets out of her happy state and then becomes more serious, realizing that the challenge is real and that it waits for her. The show ends with Wake heading out with Jake at his heels as Aspen stares out at the ground nodding as Revolt ends and we are on our way to ‘Requiem’. WINNERS: WAKE WALKER & ASPEN CHAUD
