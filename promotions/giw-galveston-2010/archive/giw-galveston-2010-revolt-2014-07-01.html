GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: REVOLT Live from the Kauffman Arena – July 1st, 2014 QUOTE OPEN BATTLE ROYAL In this week’s Open Battle Royal we saw three stars in action in the form of the debuting Astro and Awesome Xombi as well as a retuning Jeremy Baltimore as the three all looked to start things off with a bang in their GIW careers. The three started and really stole the show from the get go, really pulling all the stops in the match with each having some moments that left the fans in ah but at the end of the day the point was to throw your opponents over the top rope, and after some action it was Baltimore and Astro who formed a momentary partnership to try to throw Xombi out. They did so seemingly eliminating him but Xombi held on but before he was even out, Astro turned on Baltimore spinning and tossing him up and over the ring with Baltimore nearly holding on before Astro smacked his arms to drop him from the ring. With Baltimore eliminated Astro turned back to Xombi who returned to the ring as he attempted to toss him out but a bitter Baltimore jumped back onto the ropes distracting Astro just long enough for Xombi to catch Astro off guard tossing him over leading to Awesome Xombi getting the victory! After the match tensions were high with Baltimore and Astro as the two seemed ready to fight when suddenly it appeared the two seemed to have some what of a realization that the two might work better together as the two seemingly went to the back, together. WINNER: AWESOME XOMBI BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! Revolt starts in an odd manner, as we don’t usually get the pyro to kick off the night or the normal panning view of the crowd. Instead “Dangerous” By Within Temptations cues and the fans remain silent, not recognizing the song as one of the GIW’s wrestlers themes. Charity Ryan walks out from the back to boos from the crowd. She is wearing a black business suit. She moves to the left side of the stage. The song continues to play and the fans wonder what this whole thing is about. Ian Mendoza is next out, to a mixed reaction from the crowd. He is wearing his usual business attire as well. He moves to the right side of the stage. David Sinclair emerges from the back to a huge reaction from the crowd, mostly negative but a loud reaction for the surprised return. The fans wonder if he is what this whole nonsense is about. He stops in the center of the stage. Finally, Alexis Terry in a grey business suit enters the stage and is met with loud boos from the fans. She smirks and greets all three of the fellow people around her. She makes her way to the ring as the other three follow behind. She enters and is handed a microphone quickly. The music dies all the four of them settle in the ring as the crowd continues to give them a hard time. RILEY STEVENS: "We are live tonight but an unusual start to the show as Alexis Terry, Charity Ryan, Ian Mendoza, and surprisingly David Sinclair have taken the ring" OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Sinclair looks great. You guys bought into Mr. B like I wanted you to because I wanted to remain anonymous. I wanted my privacy and I didn’t want the critics saying that Alexis Terry was only Undisputed Champion because she was the boss. I wanted to earn my title with honor but you people made that hard for me. I always had your best interest in mind but you; none of you had my best interest in mind did you? Nope. You are all bloody backstabbers. You held that knife for four months and finally; you stuck it right in the spine like I am common dirt, a streetwalker in the night that you mugged. You took and took and took, and finally I have had enough of you taking from me. Five months ago Galveston Island Wrestling was dead in the water. Men and women were out of work. The Galveston economy was sinking and sinking. Nobody had a bloody reason to step foot on this island. I loved GIW and I THOUGHT you all loved me back so like the hero that I am, I poured every ounce my money I had into this place and brought new life to it. I bought Kauffman arena. I hired state of the art production people. I spent hours editing and shipping footage to TV outlets. Finally, the hard work paid off and GIW was back in business. I went out and listened to you people and against my better judgment I gave you want you wanted. You wanted Madison Seton back? Fine – I hired her. You wanted Christopher Darren Erns back? Fine, I hired him. I even went against everything I knew and stood for and I gave Wake Walker a contract because you people wanted him.” Alexis stops speaking for a moment to take a breather and calm down from the frustration. The fans boo. ALEXIS TERRY: “But, like I said I knew if I revealed myself as the one behind the Galveston reboot I would have the naysayers and the newsletters and dirtsheets saying that I was only Champion because I was the one running the show. So, I created Mr. B, the anonymous investor who was CEO of GIW. Together, I sought after the expert team that would run things. The man standing over there is Ian Mendoza, who much to the surprise to all of you is an accomplished pro wrestler in his own right. He got me booked in the United States long before I came to GIW. Ian Mendoza is head of talent relations for Galveston Island Wrestling and has helped me from day one in this venture. Over there is David Sinclair, who you all know very well. David Sinclair has always supported me and like it or not, GIW was most successful when Sinclair was running things. He is my financial advisor and has been here much like Mendoza since day one. And finally, Ms. Charity Ryan, the newly promoted GIW Image Consultant and long time Alexis Terry supporter.” Charity blows a kiss into the camera and smiles much to the annoyance of the fans. Alexis quickly continues. ALEXIS TERRY: “Together – TOGETHER this team before you works together to form a System, a System that is created to work and HAS WORKED. We are The System. For months The System worked that was until people, people like Beck Ramsey, The Harlequin, and Cassandra Knight or collective known as Cirque du Péché - together, they started to push so to speak against The System. A system only works when you work within it and Beck and his hooligans did everything to not work within the system. They were so hell bent on finding out who Mr. B was that they started to become a problem. So, I ended it. I am sure you all saw and read the news the last few days; I released Cassandra Knight from her contract here in Galveston Island Wrestling. You can call it flexing my muscles, I believe that was what the headline said, or you can call it an abuse of power but I did what was best for GIW. If you are not a product of The System you do not belong here.” The fans chant "Ramsey! Ramsey! Ramsey!" but Alexis ignores the collective voices of the crowd and continues. ALEXIS TERRY: “Beck and Harlequin need to learn that quickly or they will face similar consequences. Because, they’re little parade on Galveston is over. This is my world and my company. Play by my rules, by my system or get the bloody hell out! Cassandra was the first but she will not be the last, not even the last tonight. Things are going to change and things are going to change tonight! Now, as far my match goes with Madison Seton and the events that took place at Cat 5, I made a point with my actions. Wake Walker was taken care of. Ian Mendoza was hired to deal with the Wake Walker problem and he did it and finally, Wake is gone from the main event scene. Now, my focus turns to Madison Seton and her malicious reign as Undisputed Champion. I could have easily taken that title from Ms. Seton at Sole Survivor but the moment of that title coming back to where it belongs, back within The System, is a moment of glory, so it’s only fitting that it happens at Moment of Glory. But, let us not get ahead of ourselves. I promise to leave Moment of Glory as Undisputed Champion but I never said it would be against Madison Seton. Madison has been a thorn in my side for far to long and Ms. Seton should have eyes in the back of her head because she will be lucky to even make it to Moment of Glory to defend that title. Madison, tonight I have two options for you, Option A you can hand that title over and walk away with no harm or foul or Option B The System takes it from you. You have until the end of the night to decide.” "Dangerous" By Within Temptations plays again as the crowd boos the abusive of power by Terry and "The System". Alexis gathers the others in the center of the ring as they raise their fists in the air as we cut to ringside.
