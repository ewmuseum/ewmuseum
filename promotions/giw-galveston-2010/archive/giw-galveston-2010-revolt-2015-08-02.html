GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS SUNDAY NIGHT REVOLT 8/2/15 FROM KAUFFMAN ARENA IN GALVESTON, TEXAS BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! The welcoming sound of the fireworks streaming into the air outside of Kauffman Arena can be heard from miles away as the fans continue to find their seats for the last Revolt before the next Supershow, Malice. “Over and Under” by Egypt Central plays us in as the Fox Sports Southwest cameras pan through the audience, revealing a raucous group of loyals waving their signs and cheering for their favorite wrestlers. Over the ballyhoo, the voices of the announce team can be heard, officially welcoming us to the show. SARAH SHARP: "Ladies and gentlemen, welcome to another episode of GIW Sunday Night Revolt! I’m Sarah Sharp joined tonight by my broadcast partner Oliver Thibodaux. We are live from Kauffman Arena here in Galveston just one week away from Malice, where many rivalries will come to a head. You can catch that show for free right here on Fox Sports Southwest, but we have a great show for you tonight as well. Tonight we start off by putting the GIW Television Championship on the line." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Alioth Starre is the current GIW Television Champion, just another title on his already full resume. But that resume has the chance to get a lot bigger tonight because, if he wins one of his two matches on this show, he will pass the current Commissioner of Revolt, Aspen Chaud’s, all time wins record. Both of these legendary competitors find themselves at fifty one wins, and Alioth Starre has not just one, but two chances to eclipse Aspen tonight." SARAH SHARP: "A betting man will certainly have his money on Alioth Starre tonight. In addition to the TV title defense, Alioth finds himself as one of six wrestlers that will enter the ring later tonight for a blockbuster six man tag team match that pits Alexis Terry, Bronx Valescence, and Katie Keller against Alioth Starre, Geneva Lost, and Johnny Violence. That match should blow the roof off of this place." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "It’s certainly a match fit for a pay per view, and you’re getting it tonight free on Revolt. There are so many combustible elements in this match – for instance, Bronx Valescence and Alioth Starre are currently fighting over the right to challenge for the Undisputed title at Blackout. Meanwhile, Geneva Lost has aligned herself with the boss, and after ridding GIW of The Sir last week, sets her sights on the duo of Katie Keller and Alexis Terry. Also in this match is Johnny Violence, who with Jessica Tendonin has made a commitment to uproot this company until he is given the opportunities he thinks he deserves." SARAH SHARP: "And you know what makes this match even better? It isn’t even the main event. We have so much more for you tonight, culminating in a clash of champions to end the show. The GIW Deep South Champion, Terry McKenna, called out the Undisputed Champion, Chris Callum, last week, and was given a low blow for his troubles. Tonight, they face off one on one to settle the dispute. Should the Undisputed Champion be on upset alert tonight, Ollie?" OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Absolutely not! Chris Callum is the most dominant force this company has ever seen. Terry McKenna should be content with his Deep South Championship, but of course he isn’t! Chris Callum might lose this match tonight, but only if he decides to kick McKenna right in the family jewels again. That kid better be careful or his family will go extinct when he dies, if you catch my drift." SARAH SHARP: "Well I certainly hope that our Undisputed Champion shows a bit more class than to get himself disqualified from what would be an amazing match between two very talented athletes, but I won’t get my hopes up. Chris Callum has been on an extraordinary ego trip as of late, and I for one am happy that Terry McKenna decided to call him out on it." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Terry McKenna has been here a while, but he still has a lot to learn. For instance, he should learn not to call out the man that will break the record for longest Undisputed Championship reign of all time when he defends his belt at Blackout. McKenna is good, but he isn’t that good. The sooner he realizes that simple fact, the better." SARAH SHARP: "Well, we will find out what happens later tonight – and despite Oliver’s certainty that Chris Callum will emerge victorious, I wouldn’t be surprised if McKenna catches him tonight. But speculating is useless, and we will see who is right later in the evening. For now, let’s toss it down to the ring to the ring announcer, Ruby Reynolds, as we get set to witness history in tonight’s TV Championship open match." We fade in on Ruby Reynolds, wearing a blue dress, in the middle of the ring. She welcomes us with a smile as she moves the microphone up towards her mouth, ready to announce the brave few who decided to enter this match.
