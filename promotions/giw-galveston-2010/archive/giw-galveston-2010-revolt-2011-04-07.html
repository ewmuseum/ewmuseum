GIW — Card

Revolt, 7 April 2011

7 April 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: THURSDAY NIGHT REVOLT! LIVE FROM THE SEAWALL BOULEVARD ENTERTAIMENT COMPLEX = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! As soon as the show starts we hear the sounds of “Nightmare” by Avenged Sevenfold echo through the arena as the fans cheer for the big man as he walks out from the backstage area. Looking focused and angry he makes a b-line for the ring, not high fiving anyone as he heads up the steps and into the ring, picking up a mic on his way in. Once in he looks out at the crowd we all know who is on his mind. SETH SEEKER: “You know last week Jak Hendricks cost me my shot at the North Beach title when I took on Alioth Starre. Usually I would be angry about that but frankly he dug his own grave. Now I’m not worried about a title, I am not worried about Starre…I am completely focused on CRUSHING YOU, Jak Hendricks!” The crowd pops it is no secret whose side they are on in this feud, as he angrily paces back and forth in the ring. SETH SEEKER: “If you think you hurt me, not even close. You just hurt yourself, because I am coming for you! Tonight I know we are supposed to team up, but I say to hell with that come out here right now and face me like a man!” The crowd cheers as Seeker turns to the ramp seeing if Jak will have the courage to come out and confront him, his answer comes in the form of “Forever” by Drake, Kanye West, Lil Wayne, and Eminem as out comes Jak Hendricks to boos from the crowd, holding a mic he walks half way down the ramp then stops as Seeker calls for him to get into the ring. JAK HENDRICKS: “Really? You really think that I am going to get into the ring with you? I’m a star! You have to work your way up! I don’t want to team with you any more then you do me. But the fact of the matter is, I already beat you…I already made you tap…What makes you think I want to beat you…AGAIN?” Jak seems pleased with himself as he lowers his microphone, before Seeker can angrily respond Mariachi music hits as the fans cheer as Eduardo Santiago walks out from the back wearing that big grin of his. EDUARDO SANTIAGO: “You know this tag team match was my idea tonight. In my country we call this a Parejas Incredibles Match, when two rivals team up. I have decided that the first match I am going to announce for the ‘International X’ show in MEXICO, will be…JAK HENDRICKS VERSUS SETH SEEKER…” The crowd goes wild as Seeker has a huge grin on his face as Jak seems a bit shocked by the news, but Eduardo is not finished. EDUARDO SANTIAGO: “You see the next two weeks the two of you are banned from any contact with one another unless it be tagging…because I want you to save up all that aggression…Because that match at ‘International X’ will be…a FIRST BLOOD MATCH!” The crowd goes wild again as Seeker forgets all about the no contact ruling for the next two weeks and while his excitement is clear Jak keeps his wits as well walking towards the back, as Riley and Oliver pick it up. RILEY STEVENS: “There’s a block buster! Jak Hendricks versus Seth Seeker in a First Blood match at ‘International X’ finally we will see the end of their feud and what better way then the first match to draw blood winning? I can’t think of anything.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “What does Eduardo have against Jak?” RILEY STEVENS: “Oh come on it was bound to happen sooner or later, these fans love him. As well as seeing them teaming here tonight; we will see a contest between Jacoby Jackson and Floyd Mason as well as three singles matches between Tony Miranda, Eric Sparrow, and Alioth Starre and their High Voltage counter parts, who will take control here tonight? The first match starts next.” As he poses the question we head towards a commercial, QUOTE “Under The Knife” by Rise Against hits as the fans immediately start to boo in one of three matches that we will truly know whose side, they were on. Coming out he looked as scary and unstable as usual as he looked out disgusted at the crowd, making his presence felt as he walked towards the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “This man is not normal, Head Case are two words that truly fit the man because simply put he’s not normal. We will see how he does here tonight when he fights the North Beach Champion.” As soon as Hister enters the ring “Silly Boy Blue” by David Bowie hits sending the fans into a ruckus for the man they call Alioth Starre. Thus far he’s lived up to his name, having rolled his way to the belt he now carries proudly over his shoulder. It’s clear he is focused tonight as he stops looking in at Hister then heads up the steps. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Can you say overrated?” RILEY STEVENS: “Overrated? The man has yet to even look bad in a single match. He was close to becoming a duel champion last week and wasn’t even involved in the finish; he’s had one of the most impressive starts to a career thus far in GIW.” Starre steps into the ring handing his belt over to the referee who hands it out of the ring, Hister can be seen eyeing it up as the referee hands it out, then calls for the bell to start this one. DING DING DING! Though coming in with a slight height advantage, Starre does not match Hister in the physical department. They come together for a lock up and right away Hister shows off his strength advantage backing Alioth into the ropes. Marcus whips him off the ropes, as he comes back Starre ducks under the punch and rolls him up 1…2…Marcus pops out and rolls him up himself 1…2…Starre bounces up and ends up behind him, tossing him forward he goes forward with a European Uppercut that catches Hister’s jaw clean. The fans cheer as he follows it up with another then runs the ropes and runs right into a Wheel Kick by Hister. The fans boo as he falls down and covers him 1…Kick Out! Not even close as he gets up, lifting Starre with him before whipping him into the corner. Marcus goes full steam at him but Starre lifts a foot kicking him away. He steps up the top rope, looking for something. But a quickly recovered Hister jumps up to the top rope with him. The two exchange punches back and forth teetering a bit. It’s Starre who seems to be getting the advantage hitting him with some big haymakers; finally Marcus seems to be teetering towards the outside. When he’s about to go he gives Alioth a huge shove that sends both men flying to the outside. Starre ungraciously hits the side of the ring then tumbles out while Hister falls with a loud thud, bringing a big gasp from the crowd. RILEY STEVENS: “Ouch both men took a big fall there, we’ll see if either man can get up after that one.” Both men at first don’t show any signs of moving as the referee starts the count 1…2…3…4…Finally at the four count we see some signs of stirring by both men. Hister grabs the steps which he landed very close to using them to get to his knees. Alioth has turned onto his back, breathing heavily clearing his mind…5….6…7…Alioth gets up quickly as Marcus seems to be doing so as well…8…Starre slides into the ring and right after Hister follows him. The count is broken and Alioth tries to come after him on attack, but Marcus catches him with an eye rake as Starre’s back blocks the referee. He falls away holding his eyes as Hister comes forward with a huge Spear! The fans boo as Hister makes the cover 1…2…Kick Out! It’s not enough. Though Alioth still holds his stomach, on the mat Hister gets up giving the referee a small shove in anger which the referee scolds him for, he heads to the top rope where he looks down at the still hurting Starre. Jumping off he nails him with a big Diving Elbow! The fans again seem discouraged as the referee makes the count 1…2…Kick Out! The fans take a big switch as they cheer thinking there is nothing that he can do. Marcus gets up again blaming it on the referee for what has happened. Out of no where he connects with a big Superkick to the jaw of the referee! RILEY STEVENS: “Whoa! Completely uncalled for kick by Hister, the referee is out cold!” Marcus stands over him saying something under his breath as Alioth is getting to his feet behind him. Hister turns around and throws a punch but Starre shakes it off as he drops him down to the mat! Suddenly out from the back is another referee. And seemingly rather then just DQing Hister he instead is going to call the match, but as it goes Starre has dropped him right into a Figure Four Leg Lock! The fans go wild as he has it synched in tight before he taps out. RILEY STEVENS: “Hister’s out burst may have just cost him the match. Surprisingly they didn’t disqualify him; instead Starre finishes it with his impressive Figure Four Leg Lock. None the less Alioth Starre starts the night out on a high note for his side.” Alioth Starre gets up, sliding out of the ring and going to grab his own belt. Inside the ring the new referee checks on the one who was Super kicked. To a lot of people’s surprise Alioth heads towards the backstage area with out any High Voltage attack as we head from him, backstage. WINNER: ALIOTH STARRE As we head to the backstage area we are greeted by the face of Sarah Sharp smiling with a microphone as she starts up. SARAH SHARP: “I am here with Tony Miranda…” The camera pans out slowly to show Tony Miranda standing next to her, with a smile as the fans show how much they love him with some cheers. SARAH SHARP: “Later tonight you will face Trent Lost in a one on one match. A few weeks ago Aspen and you agreed to a deal that said if you were to defeat Trent, you’d get your match with Aspen Chaud you even said you’d be willing to put your career on the line. Well I just talked with Eduardo, and he said he was willing to go through with that idea and that the match against Aspen would happen live next week. So how motivated are you to face Trent here tonight?” Tony seems a bit shocked she’d even question it, but as she held the microphone to him he answered anyways. TONY MIRANDA: “That’s just more motivation for me to get the job done. I’ve been on a bit of a downward spiral of late, mostly due to High Voltage. Tonight it’ll be Trent next week it’ll be Aspen Chaud. I’m going to make sure that this…problem is dealt with once and for all.” Tony walks off going to get ready for his contest, as Sarah stands there happily as the segment ends and we head to a break. QUOTE As we come back to the ring we find ourselves watching as Floyd Mason heads towards the ring, high fiving the fans. Suddenly a loud murmur comes as Jacoby Jackson his opponent tonight grabs him and then tosses him hard right into the steel steps! RILEY STEVENS: “Jacoby attacks his opponent from behind before the match can even start!” The fans boo as Floyd groans in pain, wasting no time Jacoby lifts him up and tosses him into the ring, sliding right in with him. As Floyd holds his shoulder in pain Jacoby walks over to the referee demanding he start the match, he says no and then walks over to Mason who is attempting to get up. He asks if he wants to fight and a prideful Mason says yes, as the referee calls for the bell reluctantly. DING DING DING! Just as you’d expect Jacoby flashes in tugging on Floyd’s arm, pulling on the shoulder he just hurt. Mason winces in pain as he tries to break the hold, as Jacoby smirks showing little remorse. Rather disrespectfully he breaks the hold pushing Mason away and then runs forward nailing him with a One Handed Bulldog! The fans are booing the entire time upset with how this one began. Not seeming one bit daunted by the boos Jacoby follows up on him, waiting for him to get up before nailing him with a Double Knee Facebreaker a rather brutal move. Making the cover it has to be over 1…2…Jacoby pulls up his shoulder, wrenching on it again as he drags him to his feet. When he gets him to a vertical base he screams out “It’s over!” as he hits the ‘Jacoby Jolt’ a Swinging Reverse STO! Getting down he makes the arrogant pin attempt, being less then cautious about it as the referee makes a slow count 1…..2……3! That’s it. RILEY STEVENS: “What a cheap victory by Jacoby Jackson here tonight…” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Floyd didn’t have to agree to the match did he?” RILEY STEVENS: “God forbid he have a little pride?” Jacoby pulls the referee away from Floyd and demands he raise his arm, the referee does so just so he can go back to Mason. Jackson asks for a microphone which he is handed by a staff member at ringside, walking back looking down at Floyd just as the boos reside he starts. JACOBY JACKSON “I finished this one quickly so I can take some time to address the NEW ME! My first run here was less then stellar. Now two weeks into my rebirth I have dismantled two men in a row…And if you think I am going to just sit around here and wait for anything think again…You see you will all realize just how serious I am…And you’ll realize that…VERY SOON.” Jackson grins and drops the microphone as the fans go back to booing him as heads to the backstage area as do we. WINNER: JACOBY JACKSON Heading to the backstage area we find ourselves in the hallways where Eduardo Santiago is seen catching up with Brotherhood of Steel talking with them about a few things. EDUARDO SANTIAGO: “Well guys congratulations on the tag team titles. I know you may be from Portugal but I think you guys are going to love Mexico, you’re going to be defending those belts for sure, I have to be going.” The Brotherhood of Steel seem happy with it as he waves them good bye then turns heading right into an office, where we finally get what we have been waiting for as he ends up right in front of Blake Beckett. EDUARDO SANTIAGO: “Hello my friend I got a little caught up outside. Regardless I understand that you wanted to have a little chat with me, what about?” Blake looks up at him having a hard time not showing his real emotions here as he glares at Eduardo with his big friendly smile. BLAKE BECKETT: “What do you think you are doing…” As he’s about mid-way through his sentence Eduardo’s phone rings, as he reaches in and then looks at Blake. EDUARDO SANTIAGO: “This is going to have wait, one of my other shows is getting a big new deal we’ll talk another time.” Eduardo answers the phone and begins to head out as Blake seems stunned by the way he was just disrespected, as the tension has only seemed to grow as we head to a quick commercial break. QUOTE It was time for another match one that was perhaps the most intense of the night; with so much at stake this was guaranteed fight of the night from the start. “Down” by Curse Icon hits as the fans pop as out from the back comes Trent Lost with that big smirk on his face wearing those shades as usual. RILEY STEVENS: “Here is Trent Lost a member of High Voltage and while Aspen may be the brains behind the group, this man is the talent. If he wasn’t so arrogant he may be able to make something with it.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “He was screwed last week it’s like I said; it was a double team attack!” Riley can be heard scoffing as Trent steps into the ring and heads to the top rope where he poses for the camera. "The Human Stain" by Kamelot hits as the fans go wild perhaps the loudest cheer of the night for the veteran. Out he comes with a look of determination we have perhaps never even seen reached as runs down into the ring and as soon as he slides in the ref calls for the bell. DING DING DING! The two go head to head meeting in the center of the ring and exchanging back and forth with solid punches. Neither man seems to be getting any sort of advantage instead they are both just doing whatever it takes to try and get this win. Finally Tony lands a punch that sends Trent stumbling back. But it’s Lost that answers back with one of his own sending Tony back. The fans are into it as the electricity can be felt in the air as finally Miranda takes him down as he runs forwards and hits him with a huge Flying Side Kick that sends him to the mat. Though he’s down it’s not long before he gets up as he walks right into a follow up kick to the stomach by Miranda. He whips him off into the corner as the fans go wild, Miranda looking just plain faster then usual goes full blast but runs right into a big boot from Trent that stuns him. Taking a few steps back Trent goes at him with a clothesline that drops him, Tony pops up he drops him again. When he gets up he goes for the move he coins the ‘Nobody Home’ a feint drop kick but as soon as he goes for the eye poke, Tony grabs his arm then spins around into a Spinning Roundhouse Kick. He goes for a pin attempt 1…Kick Out! It’s clear as day that this match is not going to end this early. Tony gets to his feet, stepping back and stalking Trent as he gets up. Suddenly he has a realization as he turns and walks to the ropes, stepping through them. The crowd stands excited as he quickly jumps up going for a Springboard Dragonrana but Trent is able to roll out of the way and Tony falls to the mat hard. RILEY STEVENS: “There is a lot on the line in this one and these guys are showing how much they want it.” Tony rolls over to the corner and as he gets up Trent walks over kicking him in the gut and lifting him onto the top rope with his legs. Holding him around his neck he drops him with a Hangman’s DDT he calls the ‘Strung Out’. The fans boo as he pulls him away from the ropes and makes the cover 1…2…Kick Out! The fans cheer as Tony kicks out, clapping him on as Lost argues for a second with the referee. Tony gets up stumbling to the corner where Trent comes back at him wanting to finish him off. To the fans chagrin he punches him away and then with a desperation move he Dropkicks him with all his might and both men end up on the ground. Both men slowly start to get to their feet at the same time on different sides of the ring. As they get up they start to stir then explode up at one another, Trent going for his ‘Trent Express Mail’ a Running Leg Lariat as Tony does the same with his Running Leg Lariat, it ends up an awkward exchange with both men ending up on the mat, landing awkwardly. RILEY STEVENS: “Both men went for the same move; whoever gets up first may just win this thing.” By his logic it should be Trent who is up and stumbling his way over to Tony lifting him up. Right into a ‘Lost and Found’ attempt he goes but Tony is able to reverse out of it, going for his own finisher the ‘Lightning Buster’ a Crash Thunder Buster! The fans go wild as Lost now rolls off; able to end up behind him as he rolls him up 1…2…Tony now pops up and both men are up and Tony hops up onto his shoulders catching him off guard as he drops elbows a move he calls the ‘Storm Elbow’, Trent isn’t sure what to do as he takes the elbows he stumbles forward and Tony slides into a Victory Roll Pin 1…2…3! JUST LIKE THAT! RILEY STEVENS: “Whoa he sneaks one out of no where!” Trent kicks off as Tony slides out of the ring quickly holding up his arm in triumph. In the ring Trent argues that it was only two with the referee, as Miranda backs up the ramp slowly as we head to a commercial break. WINNER: TONY MIRANDA The camera cuts to the backstage area right at the ring area entrance as Matt Kluge and Ross Rydell are set to walk out, the fans cheer as they see them. Suddenly Sarah Sharp walks into frame holding her mic at her side. SARAH SHARP: “Hey Ross, I just got a tip off that Jericho is supposed to be arriving right now.” Rydell looks over at Kluge who seems to be indicating for him not to go but eventually he does just that. ROSS RYDELL: “I will be right back man don’t worry.” Rydell walks off as Kluge shakes his head and continues stretching. The camera follows him all the way out to the parking lot, where he is looking around for him. ROSS RYDELL: “Where is he?” Suddenly there’s a loud boom as Jacoby Jackson slams him down from behind. The fans boo as Jackson grabs him and tosses him right into a few foldable chairs. Sarah runs for help as Jackson starts to stomp away on him, bending down next to him he screams at him. JACOBY JACKSON “Consider yourself...My stepping stone!” Jackson tosses his head down and walks off as the fans boo the entire time as we go to a break after these events. QUOTE As we return we find ourselves in the middle of action already in the ring we see Jak Hendricks hammering away at Matt Kluge in the corner as the fans give him no remorse, showing him how much they hate him. RILEY STEVENS: “Welcome back to Revolt as some of you may have just seen Ross Rydell was attacked by Jacoby Jackson in the parking lot and Matt Kluge came out here to fight in this tag match alone, we have no idea how bad the injuries are at this time.” In the corner the fans boo as Jak slowly chokes Kluge with his boot in the corner, the referee gets to a four count and he breaks the hold. Getting to his feet Matt holds his neck as Hendricks turns to Seeker taunting a bit as Matt walks right into a Spinebuster! Hendricks goes for the cover as the partner less Matt Kluge is down, 1…2…Kick Out! The fans cheer for Kluge as Hendricks gets up, smirking still not seeming too worried that he kicked out. Matt struggles to his feet, this would be a good time for a tag if he could but unfortunately the option is not available. Jak starts to toy with him, kicking him once sort of calmly walking around him. Out of no where Kluge jumps up Clotheslining him to the mat. Hendricks pops right up and then walks right another, the fans going wild. Jak gets right back up stumbling around, to his corner where Seeker tags his shoulder to get in. RILEY STEVENS: “Seeker tags in.” The fans seem happy with him getting in but are clearly still on the side of the underdog in this one. But Seeker doesn’t look to be showing any sort of remorse as he clobbers him to the ground. Grabbing him with a Belly to Belly Suplex that sends him flying. Getting up he pounds his chest waiting for Matt to get to his feet. Finally he goes full steam and crushes him with a Spear, nearly splitting him in half! Getting up right away he walks across the ring over to Hendricks who he yells “This is what will happen to you.” as he turns around, slowly stalking Matt as he tries to get to his feet. With the wind knocked out of him, Seeker grabs him and lifts him up and as he does Jak reaches over the ropes and tags him. RILEY STEVENS: “Jak has made the tag, but I don’t think Seeker noticed.” Seth finishes with the ‘Don't Fear the Reaper’ a Jackhammer that he brings Matt all the way to the center of the ring with. He goes for a cover, but the referee tells him there’s been a tag. Hendricks goes around him as Seeker angrily exits, making the cover himself 1…2…3! Jak has just stolen away from the pin. RILEY STEVENS: “Usually who pins the man doesn’t really matter for a tag team, but these guys are not a team at all.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Jak was just making sure that nothing went wrong.” The fans boo as Hendricks gets to his feet, holding up both arms as Seeker gets into the ring. Hendricks wastes little time in reminding him about the No Contact ruling as Seth seems to be restraining himself. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I hope Seeker is ready to lose, again.” RILEY STEVENS: “One thing is for sure, Seeker is going to have a lot of pent up anger for this First Blood match.” The two continue to exchange words the tension in the air, with that we go to a commercial break. WINNERS: JAK HENDRICKS & SETH SEEKER As the Doritos’s Commercial finishes we return to Revolt as we head to the backstage area where Trent Lost is standing angrily in the locker room while Marcus Hister is plopped down in the chair, not pleased either. Aspen walks into the scene not looking pleased. ASPEN CHAUD: “Really guys? REALLY? You both lost? I mean how will anyone take us seriously if these guys keep beating us. And Trent, now Tony gets to face me? I mean guys come on.” Lost seems to angry to even respond while Hister is off in his own world, as she seems to be trying to keep her cool. ASPEN CHAUD: “Now I am going to go out there and make sure we end the night on a high note! Be watching.” Aspen heads out of the locker room area as the toll of losing seems to have kicked in for High Voltage here tonight. QUOTE *For now this will only be the ending, I don’t have a lot of time. If Tony submits the match later we will use his for now, this is just the finish.* Aspen begins to stalk Sparrow from behind waiting for him to get up after a big Crossbody. The fans are booing her thus far she got to this point through a bit of back handed tricks. Suddenly the crowd cheers as down to the ring comes Tony Miranda! Standing outside the ring his presence catches Aspen’s eye who turns around talking to him. She screams at him angrily as suddenly Sparrow rolls her up 1…2…Kick Out! Aspen hops up as does Sparrow as she angrily tries to dive at him but he drops her right into the ‘Purple Haze’ a Guillotine Choke! The fans go wild as Aspen struggles only for a bit before tapping out! OLIVER THIBODAUX: “That was unfair! That was unfair!” RILEY STEVENS: “Aspen finally got what she had coming to her.” Sparrow hops up holding his arms up in celebration as the crowd goes wild for him. Tony stays on the outside watching him smiling. WINNER: ERIC SPARROW Suddenly as to be expected yet again Trent Lost and Marcus Hister come flying out of the back stage area. Together they grab Tony and toss him into the Steel Steps on the outside. Into the ring they go as Sparrow stands there waiting. A hurt Aspen tells them to after him and they do cornering him and clobbering him with punches and kicks. RILEY STEVENS: “Not again!” The fans boo as Sparrow tries fighting back but it goes to little avail. Tony is hurting on the outside as suddenly Alioth Starre comes flying out from the back. He hammers Trent then Marcus as they too corner him starting to beat him down. A recovered Sparrow comes to his aid pulling Trent off and tossing him over the ropes then grabbing Marcus and doing the same, but as he does Aspen low blows him from behind then pushes him out of the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Two left!” Aspen backs away slowly, trying to get away from him as Starre stalks her. Suddenly a crowd member comes hopping over the fence and into the ring grabbing a steel chair; he slides into the ring stepping in Alioth’s path, glaring down at Aspen! Starre seems confused calling for security to remove the man, when suddenly the crowd member whose face is covered with a ski mask turns and crushes Alioth with the chair. RILEY STEVENS: “What the hell?” The man stands over the fallen Starre, tossing the chair to the outside as everyone Aspen Chaud included seems confused as the show fades.
