Revolt, 16 August 2015

16 August 2015
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS SUNDAY NIGHT REVOLT 8/16/15 FROM KAUFFMAN ARENA IN GALVESTON, TEXAS After the Galveston Island Wrestling logo flashes across the screen, we fade into a scene unlike anything we have ever seen before in the company. The entire roster – from Chris Callum to Alioth Starre, to Johnny Violence and Melissa Stark, all find themselves on the stage as “Did It On ‘Em” by Nicki Minaj welcomes us in rather than the usual fireworks. Aspen Chaud is in the ring, flanked by Geneva Lost to her left, as she looks into the hard camera on the south side of Kauffman Arena. The familiar voices of the announcers greet us as the music continues to play. SARAH SHARP: "Ladies and gentlemen, welcome back to another edition of Sunday Night Revolt right here on Fox Sports Southwest. We are just seven days removed from our latest supershow, Malice, where the main event ended in a time limit draw. We do not have a number one contender for the GIW Undisputed Championship, our Television Champion is injured, our Tag Team Champions are nowhere to be seen after a tough loss, and Blake Beckett has been roaming the halls for the past few weeks. Because of that, Aspen Chaud has called a company meeting with the entire roster present on the stage, as you can see. Please welcome my broadcast partner tonight, Oliver Thibodaux, as we look to sort all of this out." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Oh man Sarah, do you see that look in Aspen’s eye? The company is in a bad way right now, and it looks like heads are about to roll. Who knows, this may be her last night as Commissioner considering that Blake Beckett can fire her at any time, and I’d be willing to bet that she is going to try to rein everything in tonight with a iron fist. After all, that’s what she does best." SARAH SHARP: "The music is dying down now, and look at Geneva Lost with that smug grin on her face! She is so happy to be out there in the ring away from the rest of the roster. She doesn’t have any friends here other than Aspen Chaud, and she is probably counting her lucky stars that she doesn’t have to rub shoulders with all of those people who would like to squash her. I hope Aspen gets fired. This is disgusting." The music, as Sarah mentioned, is indeed dying down. Aspen held the mic up to her lips as the boos began to flow in, but this was nothing she isn’t used to. She pressed on, perfectly content – ASPEN CHAUD: "Alright, alright. Get your fill in right now, because when I’m ready to speak, I expect your complete attention. I am the Commissioner of Revolt – the most dominant wrestler in GIW History, and a Hall of Fame talent. I am not asking for your respect… I’m demanding it!" Through the boos, she somehow pressed on. A true expert, she is. ASPEN CHAUD: "It seems that over the past few weeks, the entire landscape of the company has changed. New stars are emerging, old stars are gaining traction, our Undisputed Champion has proven himself to be incompetent in the ring… and best of all, Blake Beckett has been moving through the hallways trying to collect dirt on me. I haven’t commented on it before, I didn’t think I needed to, but over the past few nights, I’ve collected some information. Now is my time to address the state of the company… MY company… and the situation with Blake Beckett. So I called the entire roster out here tonight… MY roster… the roster that is supposed to bow to ME… because we all need to be on the same page with Blackout IV only two weeks away. Listen up, all of you – fans and wrestlers – because I do not intend to repeat myself." The fan’s heckling was beginning to get extreme, so Geneva Lost decided to step in. GENEVA LOST: "You don’t think Aspen can’t remove your sorry asses from the arena? Keep booing and see what happens next!" This only made the boos louder, but regardless Aspen gave Geneva a pat on the back. ASPEN CHAUD: "Thank you Geneva – I appreciate your support though this… tumultuous time. I like having allies in this world, kind of like Journeyman Phillips and Terry McKenna enjoyed their alliance while it lasted. You know, I thought it would be a great idea to turn those two against each other at Blackout with the Deep South Championship on the line. Terry McKenna is one of the fastest rising stars in this company, and Journeyman… well… Journeyman was performing on a level that none of us, including me, ever expected. I wasn’t surprised to learn, however, when the news came to me that Journeyman’s body had finally given out. I was amused to learn that it was Terry McKenna, his best friend and ally, who delivered the nail in the coffin. Indeed, after that collision on the apron last week at Malice, Journeyman Phillips has decided to take time off from wrestling. Of course, he decides this right after he becomes Television Champion – and you all thought the curse was lifted! So with Journeyman gone, not only is our Television Championship vacated, but our number one contendership for the Deep South Championship has also been vacated. Congratulations, Terry – you lost an ally in this place, and you also lost a paycheck for Blackout. I am pleased to announce that there will be no Terry McKenna and no Deep South Championship at the next iPPV." The fans booed as Aspen placed her finger on her chin, tapping in thought. ASPEN CHAUD: "Unless… unless there is someone in this company that has proven themselves to be loyal to me. Someone who is rising through the ranks even faster than Terry McKenna! Someone… like…" Geneva exclaimed after a few moments of waiting - GENEVA LOST: "Geneva Lost!" This elicited a loud hiss from the audience. Geneva laughed. ASPEN CHAUD: "Wow, Geneva. What a wonderful idea! I think you would make the perfect number one contender, especially considering I can’t trust a dangerous wrestler like Terry McKenna with any kind of responsibility in this company. Who knows who he will injure next? Geneva, you’ll make a great Deep South Champion, and I can’t wait to see you with the belt around your waist at Blackout." Aspen pivoted on her foot as she switched to a new topic. ASPEN CHAUD: "And then there is the case of our Tag Team Championships, currently held by the Seton Sisters. What wonderful story, two sisters coming together despite their differences to hold one more title in GIW before their time is gone. But after losing at Blackout to McKenna and Journeyman, Madison decided to tell me later in the week that she needed to spend time at home for the next few weeks. She gave me some bullshit excuse about her little daughter being ill, and that’s perfectly fine. Anyone who does not want to be here, should not be here. Why Laura Seton needed to go too, I will never know, but that is beside the point. If Madison needs time off, she can have it, but she can’t expect to hold on to her title. That is why, as of now, I have stripped and vacated the GIW Tag Team Championships. Consider them dead, like tag team wrestling. This company was built on singles competition, and we won’t waste any more time fostering a division of losers that can’t get the job done on her own. Seton, honey, we’ll have you back when you’re ready – but consider this a stain on your career that will never, ever go away." She then turned to the ramp, making eye contact with Chris Callum towards the edge of the mass. ASPEN CHAUD: "Ah… but the championship woes continue. With our SUPERMAN falling to Terry McKenna two weeks ago on Revolt and our Undisputed Championship prestige damaged for as long as he holds it, and then the number one contenders match at Blackout ending in a time limit draw for the first time in GIW history, the title picture is cloudy at best. I think we need some legitimacy brought back to this belt, and the only way to do it, that I can see, is to take it from Chris Callum. Up until this point, Chris, my friend, you’ve only defended the belt against one opponent at a time. That changes at Blackout. Chris Callum will defend his title against two opponents – the two men who took each other to the limit at Malice and beyond. Alioth Starre will be the first competitor, giving him the opportunity to defend the record he worked so… hard to earn so many years ago. And the second opponent will be Bronx Valescence, a man who, despite his odd personality, has become a fan favorite here." Chris wasn’t going to stick around to be berated. He turned to exit, but Aspen saw this coming. ASPEN CHAUD: "No. Chris, I’m not done with you yet. Come down to the ring, now. Geneva, get him a microphone." Chris made his way down to the ring, reluctantly, as Geneva fetched a microphone from the time keeper. When he entered the ring, Geneva handed it over to him. ASPEN CHAUD: "Chris, you and I go way back. It wasn’t so long ago that we were the best of friends… maybe even more than that. I protected you for months. I helped you defeat Bronx Valescence at Category 5. Then you decided to go against my orders and come out before Moment of Glory, casting me aside in the process. Now listen up, my little gay champion, I understand that the past month has been a hard one for you. I understand your personal life has changed quite a bit, and trust me, I am willing to forgive you for casting me aside. I can’t help but say I told you so, but I told you so." Aspen moved towards Chris, straightening the collar of his shirt like a mother would. ASPEN CHAUD: "But enough gloating. Since you came out, you’ve been weak. You lose matches you should be winning. You went from being hated to being forgotten. And now, imagine my surprise when I find out you are in cahoots with Blake Beckett?" The fans were surprised, and judging from his facial reactions, Chris was surprised that she knew. He had met with Alex Castellanos, but that was in secret. ASPEN CHAUD: "Oh, you didn’t think I would find out? I have eyes and ears all over this little island, Chris. If a leaf falls from an oak tree that I care about, you better believe I will know it. What makes all of this even sweeter is that Blake wants to replace me with none other than… Alex freaking Castellanos? What a joke. What an elaborate joke." Aspen pushed him away. ASPEN CHAUD: "You had a meeting with Alex Castellanos earlier this week. He asked you for information about me, and you refused to give it to him. That was a good choice, but if I know Alex, he won’t stop hounding you. He has always wanted to be me, and that isn’t changing any time soon. He isn’t going to stop until Blake has just cause to fire me. I want to make sure that doesn’t happen, so I recognize why you are important to this situation. I recognize that you and I need to be on the same page here – so let’s get on the same page." She spoke to him clearly and directly. ASPEN CHAUD: "Chris – I can ruin you. I made you, and I can ruin you. If I want to, your title reign will be history come Blackout. Alioth and I don’t see eye to eye, but we respect each other. Bronx Valescence may not be an ideal champion, but he will work just fine until Geneva Lost calls her shot, right Geneva?" Geneva nodded in agreement. Chris held the microphone at his side. ASPEN CHAUD: "So here is what I’m going to do – Chris. Next week, I am going to make a match. It will be a match that GIW has never seen before! A truly innovative idea from the mind of Aspen Chaud. Chris, you will team with Geneva Lost to take on Alioth Starre, Terry McKenna, and Bronx Valescence in a three on two handicap match!" He frowned, confused. If she was trying to earn his support, a handicap match against three of GIW’s best probably wasn’t the best way to get it. ASPEN CHAUD: "Oh wipe that dumb look off of your face, Chris. You don’t think I would hang Geneva out to dry like that, would you? Of course not. I’m smarter than that. To even the odds, I’m going to allow you and Geneva to bring weapons to the ring. The other team will not get the same privilege. This is your opportunity, Chris, to prove to me that you’re willing to work with me. Take out Alioth. Take out Bronx. I know Geneva will take out McKenna! If you get a few good shots in, they will be softened up enough for you to easily defend your title at Blackout. And even if they aren’t, I’m prepared to stack the deck for you, just like I did at Category 5, to make sure you walk out as champion. I know I’m generous. All I need is your absolute loyalty Chris. Not to Alex, not to Blake… just to me. Remember how dominant we were together? When you became a star? You can have all of that back, and then some. What do you say?" Chris paused as the fans chanted NO loudly at him. He, once upon a time, would have listened to them, but it seemed like they were tuned out. Finally, he quietly replied. CHRIS CALLUM: "I don’t have an answer for you right now." Needless to say, this was not the answer Aspen was anticipating. ASPEN CHAUD: "You… what?" Geneva was equally as shocked. GENEVA LOST: "Are you joking?" But the cogs were turning in Chris’ head. CHRIS CALLUM: "It’s something I need to think about." After a sigh, Aspen continued on with her diatribe. ASPEN CHAUD: "Well. I guess you have that right, even if you really shouldn’t have much thinking to do. Fine. I’ll hold off my dogs for now, and you… think about it. Until then though, Chris, keep away from Alex and Blake, if you know what is good for you. If you had made your decision right now, I’d let you stay in the ring… but since you want to play hard to get, why don’t you just get out?" She sarcastically smiled at him. Chris was more than happy to oblige. CHRIS CALLUM: "My pleasure." As he left the ring, the roster members on the ramp were beginning to stir towards the back as well. But Aspen didn’t want this to happen, and so it didn’t happen. ASPEN CHAUD: "Hey hey… whoa now. I have one last thing to do here, so you all need to stay put. See there are a few other turncoats that need to be addressed. So Paul Marshall and Sarah Sharp, why don’t you two come down to the ring right now." Paul emerged from the back of the pack, pushed by Johnny Violence, and was bit confused at the situation. The commentary team was also caught off guard. OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Oh God, Sarah… What did you do?" SARAH SHARP: "I didn’t do anything!" OLIVER THIBODAUX: "You did something! And Aspen, I just want you to know that I had nothing to do with it! I am loyal to you until the day I die, I swear it!" Sarah removed her headset and made her way to the ring. The ominous thump when the mic hit the table was enough to jar the viewer’s hears. Paul was slowly moving towards the ring. ASPEN CHAUD: "Yeah, Paul. Waddle your way down here a little bit quicker, we don’t have all night. For those of you who don’t know, Paul is a close personal friend of Blake Beckett, and one of his first targets to frame me. And then there is this pretty little girl, Sarah Sharp, a long lasting girlfriend of Alex Castellanos. She will surely have loyalty to him." Once they both arrived in the ring, Aspen pointed a finger at the mat. ASPEN CHAUD: "Get on your knees. The two of you. Now." Paul and Sarah looked at each other. Sarah obeyed, Paul did not. ASPEN CHAUD: "Paul. Get on your knees." Though he didn’t have a mic, we could see Paul asking Aspen what this was all about. He wasn’t willing to comply. ASPEN CHAUD: "Geneva, get him." Geneva crossed the ring and threw her entire weight into a kick to Paul’s knee. This brought the old man down quickly. ASPEN CHAUD: "Now I want you two to listen to me very carefully. I run this show. I own you. If you want to keep your jobs, kiss my feet and promise to be good little dogs." Sarah took the microphone from Geneva and pleaded with Aspen. SARAH SHARP: "Aspen… really… I haven’t talked to Alex in months. He’s a psychopath. I don’t know anything about anything." Aspen smiled before kicking off her heel. ASPEN CHAUD: "That’s great. If you like your job, kiss my feet." Aspen wiggled her toes towards Sarah as she and Geneva shared a hearty laugh. Sarah dropped the microphone as some members of the roster were beginning to get restless. Finally, Sarah dropped her head and… kissed Aspen’s feet. ASPEN CHAUD: "Ah… you’re a good kisser. I see why Alex kept you around for so long. You’re not out of the doghouse yet, little girl, but for now, go back to your little chair. Call the matches. Pretend like everything is perfect. I’ll be watching you." Sarah rolled under the bottom rope, holding her lips as if they were bleeding. Aspen turned towards Paul. ASPEN CHAUD: "Paul. It’s your turn. Kiss my feet." Retrieving the mic dropped by Sharp, Paul responded from his behind. PAUL MARSHALL: "No." Aspen sighed, dropping down to Paul’s level as he held his knee in pain. ASPEN CHAUD: "Don’t make this harder than it needs to be, Paul. A retirement check is nowhere near as good as a check from me. Kiss my feet." She stood and held her toes in front of him again. PAUL MARSHALL: "No. I ain’t kissin your damn feet. You’re crazy." He pushed her leg away hard as Aspen span around and raised an eyebrow, amused. ASPEN CHAUD: "I’ll give you one more chance, Paul. Just one more. I’ll ask you nicely. Please, kiss my feet." It was then that the first member of the roster – Terry McKenna – found a mic and spoke up. TERRY MCKENNA: "That’s enough! There’s no need to embarrass the man. Just fire him if you’re gonna do it." Duff Briggs and Luke Jones were on his flanks as they moved towards the ring. DUFF BRIGGS: "If you treated us with some respect, maybe you wouldn’t have to overcompensate like this!" LUKE JONES: "Paul is the only one that has been working with me since I got here. I can’t say that about you, Aspen. Without him, who is going to be the one WE trust?" Aspen screamed out at them from the ring. ASPEN CHAUD: "You don’t get to trust anyone! You fight at my discretion, not his! Geneva, go." Geneva kicked Marshall square in the head with her boot, stomping away at him over and over again. She was targeting the body, the legs, the hands, the arms, the head – anywhere she could stomp, she stomped. Jones, McKenna, and Briggs rushed the ring. ASPEN CHAUD: "One more step and your jobs are gone." They stopped midway down the ramp, maybe against their morals. But this job was important to each of them for many reasons. Clearly they were all disgusted. Aspen bent down over Paul and bellowed her next words - ASPEN CHAUD: "PAUL MARSHALL – YOU’RE FIRED!" She then turned towards the rest of the roster with fire in her eyes. ASPEN CHAUD: "As for the rest of you. GET IN LINE OR GET OUT OF MY BUILDING. YOU KNOW THE PHRASE. YOU’RE EITHER WITH ME, OR YOU CAN KISS MY…" GENEVA LOST: "ASPEN!" “Did It On Em” picked up once again as the camera cut to Aspen standing over Paul Marshall from the east side of the arena, revealing McKenna, Briggs, and Jones midway through the ramp watching in horror. The rest of the roster was silent on the ramp, many of them holding their hands over their mouths in shock. We faded to commercial break. QUOTE The scene pans from the gleaming Television Title sitting at ringside to the ring beginning to feel with wrestlers from the back. Johnny Violence stands on the outside, sizing up the title. He nudges Duff Briggs who stands beside him as the two joke around. In the ring Katie Keller steps between the ropes with a very good reaction as he waves to the crowd. Already standing in the ring is Geneva Lost with both hands on her knees, eyeing Katie Keller as she comes in after their beef from last week. Luke Jones sits on the top turnbuckle and watches the action unfold as Keller bounces from side to side to keep her blood pumping. RUBY REYNOLDS: “The following match is for the vacant Television Championship! Already at ringside, Johnny Violence! In the ring, Luke Jones! Geneva Lost! Duff Briggs! And Katie Keller!” The bell sounds, Duff and Johnny are still on the outside. Luke Jones doesn’t bother to hop down. It’s Geneva Lost who steps into the middle of the ring, mouthing off to Keller after her win last week. Keller shrugs and moves to lock up with Lost as they jockey for position. Jones looks down to Duff and Johnny who are just taking in the action. SARAH SHARP: “Of course, we learned that Journeyman Phillips surrendered the TV title at the beginning of the show tonight. So there has to be a new champion here tonight. Johnny Violence being the only one in the ring who is a former champion.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Luke Jones had an impressive showing last week, he’s going to look to capitalize on that. There’s a new found friendship with Duff and Johnny Violence. I’m sure they’re going to look to use the numbers game to make sure one of them leaves as champion. Lost is my pick to win though, she has all the momentum in the world heading into this one. And a win over Keller!” SARAH SHARP: “Of course there’s also the fact that Duff and Violence will take on Keller and Alexis here tonight, if Duff wasn’t such a good guy, could be a good time to get the one-up on one half of that tag team” Lost and Keller lock up. Finally it’s Lost who wins the lock up and shoves Keller down and against the ropes. Keller flips her hair from her eyes and looks up to Lost with a scowl who wants again brags. Keller then sprints from the corner and spears Lost out of her boots, the two trade rights and lefts as Luke Jones finally walks over and pulls Lost off of Keller, he pulls Lost from off of Keller into a german suplex, bridging for the pin. 1! 2! Geneva gets a shoulder up, as Luke rolls to his feet, Johnny Violence and Duff Briggs both nod and decide it’s their time to get involved. They grab hands and take Luke Jones out with a double-clothesline. Luke goes down. Duff turns around to see Keller, he throws himself into the turnbuckle and rushes across the ring, racing by Violence and hits Keller with a drop kick which sends her stumbling out of the ring. Johnny Violence hits a clothesline and covers Keller. 1! 2! Keller shoves away from Violence. Duff Briggs is locked up with Luke Jones now as Duff hits a couple of knife edge chops. He whips Luke across the ring and then chases him, but Jones flips up on the turnbuckle and rolls around sunset flipping Duff. 1! 2! Geneva Lost soars in with a shot gun drop kick to his chest which sends Jones rolling off to the side. Duff gets to his feet as Johnny Violence gives Keller a forearm and then whips her into the ropes. Keller reverses it and then rushes at Johnny, giving him a swift super kick right to the chin which sends him out of the ring. Luke rolls out the opposite side, holding his chest from the drop kick as Duff goes to throw Lost out to JVi, but Lost reverses and throws Duff between the ropes, he hits into Violence and the two butt heads and fly back into the barricade. SARAH SHARP: “Meeting of the minds between Duff and Johnny. I know they wanted to get a bit closer here tonight but that certainly isn’t what they had in mind.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “This isn’t the time to make jokes! We need to make sure they’re alright, they have a big match coming up later with Alexis and Keller!” Violence and Duff try to get to their feet but Geneva Lost looks at both of them as they lie on the outside and slowly get up. She nods and then bounces off of the ropes and rolls over the top of them and lands on top of Duff and Violence sending them all into the barricade and Lost over the top into the first row in the crowd. Luke Jones slides back into the ring and realizes Keller has her back turned, catching her breath. Luke dashes forward and rolls Keller up! 1! 2! Keller gets a shoulder up at the last possible second, she continues her momentum forward and swings a kick at Jones, he ducks it and pops back up, he lunges at Keller but she catches him with his head underneath her arm, which forces his legs around her waist, she wrenches him a couple of times before tossing him backwards with the Royal Butterfly! Keller makes her way over and makes the pin, leaning her back against Luke and pulling his leg up. 1! 2! 3! The crowd pops as Johnny Violence and Duff were slow to get up and Lost claws back over the barricade to realize she is too late. Luke Jones rolls away as Keller is awarded the TV title, she smiles wide and walks over to the turnbuckle to hold up her title. RUBY REYNOLDS: “The winner of this match and NEWWW Television champion…KATIEEEE KELLLERR!” Keller pumps her fist and puts the title over her shoulder and slaps the front a couple of times as the referee points to her. SARAH SHARP: “Katie Keller with a defining win early in her GIW career! We all knew this moment was coming, we just didn’t know when. This week, Keller captures the TV title!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Big time win for Katie Keller. We will see if luck, yes luck, Sharpie. We’ll see if it carries over when Alexis and Keller take on the Taco Bros or whatever.” We fade into the backstage area with a big smile on Keller’s face. WINNER: KATIE KELLER (NEW GIW TELEVISION CHAMPION) We fade to Alexis’ locker room in the back where she is skimming through a bridal magazine. Bronx Valescence sits cross legged on the floor swiping through twitter, chuckling occasionally. The door opens, capturing their attention. Through the door steps Chris Callum, his collar still nicely folded, the title belt over his shoulder. CHRIS CALLUM: "I need advice." His voice was grave as he turned his eyes to Bronx. CHRIS CALLUM: "That doesn’t mean you, Bronx." He frowns, grabbing his phone and standing chest to chest with Callum. BRONX VALESCENCE: "Whatever bruh. I’m excited to lose to you again at Blackout." He pats Callum twice on the shoulder, and then exits. Terry raises an eyebrow. ALEXIS TERRY: "Yes?" Callum lets out a long breath. CHRIS CALLUM: "I know we don’t always see eye to eye. But if there is one thing we can agree on, it’s that Aspen isn’t a person we need to associate with." Alexis nodded with a slight smile. ALEXIS TERRY: "This is true." Chris looked down at his belt. CHRIS CALLUM: "I like this title, Alexis. It’s the one thing I can count on having, week in and week out. I want to keep it. Aspen can help me keep it." Alexis chuckled before going back to her magazine. ALEXIS TERRY: "You don’t deserve the belt if you can’t defend it on your own." Chris stammered over his words. CHRIS CALLUM: "I… can defend it on my own. I… have defended it on my own." Alexis spoke to him without looking up. ALEXIS TERRY: "Have you? It’s always been you and Aspen against the field. I was just a bit part, and thank God I didn’t do more. Why don’t you try to be your own person for once, Chris?" Suddenly, Katie Keller brushed past Callum with a ton of momentum, jumping straight on top of Alexis on the couch. KATIE KELLER: "I’m golden!!" She held her title out for Alexis to see. They embraced. ALEXIS TERRY: "Congratulations baby! I watched it and it was amazing. I’m so happy for you." Between laughing, Keller noticed the stoic Callum across the room. KATIE KELLER: "What’s this?" Callum grit his teeth as his eyes bored through Keller. CHRIS CALLUM: "How can you tell me to be my own person when you have Katie Keller running around this arena as your proxy, Alexis?" Keller moved to a more reasonable position on the couch before she spoke. KATIE KELLER: "Excuse me? I won this title on my own, for myself. I don’t know what else I have to do around here to be anything more than Alexis Terry’s girlfriend." Callum shook his head, leaving the room. CHRIS CALLUM: "You’re both idiots. Thanks for wasting my time." Keller scowled as the door slammed closed. Alexis grabbed her by the chin and turned her head away from the door. ALEXIS TERRY: "Don’t let him get to you." They embraced in another long hug with some laughter as we cut to a different scene. ---- Bronx found potato chips. He was snacking on them as he wandered the halls. BRONX VALESCENCE: "Alioth! My man! My partner! My five star buddy!" He called out to Alioth before rushing towards him eagerly. Starre was less excited. ALIOTH STARRE: "What do you want?" Bronx shrugged before grabbing Alioth by the shoulder. BRONX VALESCENCE: "Just saying what’s up. All the guys on Wreddit loved out match man. We’re like, legends now." Alioth scoffed at Bronx’s comment. ALIOTH STARRE: "I was a legend before you signed here." Bronx nodded, hesitantly. BRONX VALESCENCE: "Okay then! Have you seen these pictures of my cat? Luke Jones says it is ugly but I think he is ugly. Biggie loves him. Here look. Swipe left, not right." He held his phone in Alioth’s face for a while before the legend pushed it away. ALIOTH STARRE: "Bronx, can I ask you a question? I promise it will be simple." Bronx pulled the phone back, adoring his cat. BRONX VALESCENCE: "Sure buddy!" Alioth spoke quietly to Bronx. ALIOTH STARRE: "You and I are set to be in a match that will define our careers. For me, it is my record title reign. For you, it is legitimacy. Why would you want to be my friend? Why do you walk around this place pretending that an opossum is a cat? Are you really so delusional as to not see the stakes here?" Bronx scratched his head. BRONX VALESCENCE: "Well, I mean, do you want me to brood?" He held his shoulders up high and frowned – brooding. BRONX VALESCENCE: "You want me to walk around in the shadows and flick my lizard tongue?" He flicked his tongue and held his wrists to his chest. BRONX VALESCENCE: "You want me to ask for promo time and use it to talk about how good I am?" Bronx smiled. BRONX VALESCENCE: "I think we have enough of that between you and Chris." Alioth paused, thinking about the response. He nodded. ALIOTH STARRE: "Fair enough. You might want to get the cat checked for rabies." Bronx again slapped Alioth on the shoulder. BRONX VALESCENCE: "LIVE DANGEROUSLY ALIOTH." And he left, leaving Alioth alone backstage. We faded to the ring. QUOTE “Blood Red Sandman” welcomes us back to the ring after the commercial break as the fans stand to boo Keegan Ryan. One of newest acquisitions, Keegan has proven that he can hold his own in the ring with some of GIW’s finest, but hasn’t done that without drawing the ire of the fans. McKenna yells out in pain as Callum leaps flat footed from the mat and drop kicks McKenna which flips him backwards and onto the mat. He goes and makes the cover. 1! 2! McKenna gets a shoulder up. Callum picks McKenna up and shoots him into the ropes, he hits him with a shoulder block and then bounces off the ropes, McKenna rolls to his feet and Callum leaps back with a springboard back elbow, but McKenna leaps into the air and drop kicks Callum in the back. Callum lands flat backed and looks like he is out of breath. McKenna scampers over to make the pin. 1! 2! Callum gets a shoulder up easily. McKenna rolls away and tries to grab Callum to wretch him back down to a seated position but Callum tosses McKenna over his shoulder. McKenna quickly gets to his feet as does Callum. They go to lock back up but Callum goes behind, he lifts Terry up for the Chris Cross but McKenna tucks his head and rolls between the legs of Callum and sends him into the ropes. As McKenna turns around he’s nailed with a forearm to the jaw which rocks him against the ropes but he fires back with one of his own. As McKenna comes back off the ropes he goes to forearm what seems to be a stunned Callum but he quickly comes to life, he hits two quick arm drags on McKenna and then a quick third. As McKenna gets back up he goes for a desperation kick but Callum catches his foot and spins him around. He pulls McKenna up into the Chris-Cross! In the middle of the ring, Callum rolls over and hooks both legs of McKenna as he nods along with each pound of the mat with a smirk on his face. 1! 2! 3! Without a second glance to Terry, Callum stands up, looking at his undisputed title as the referee hands him his Undisputed title belt. Callum holds it up as the referee raises his hand. RUBY REYNOLDS: “The winner of this match…CHRIS…CALLUM!” McKenna rolls out of the ring with a confused look on his face as he holds his midsection, not quite sure what happened or what went wrong. Callum lowers his hand. SARAH SHARP: “What a display here for Chris Callum, if anyone had any doubt what-so-ever of the Undisputed champion, it has to be gone now.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “As impressive as Bronx Valescence has been lately, as good as Alioth Starre had looked. Chris Callum just proved to the world and the GIW faithful that he is still the man to beat.” Callum holds up his belt as the screen fades to black. WINNER: CHRIS CALLUM
