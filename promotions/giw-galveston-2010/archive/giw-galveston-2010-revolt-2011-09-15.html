Revolt, 15 September 2011

15 September 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: THURSDAY NIGHT REVOLT LIVE! From The Ecstasy Cruise Liner – September 15, 2011 BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! The crowd here seems rather interested here on the Ecstasy Cruise Liner. Whether they knew of GIW or were just intrigued to find out what they were, the fans had come flocking to the show here tonight. The ring was set up in a central part of the huge boat and had a pretty large crowd around it, barricaded off but the area from the ring to the barricade was a lot smaller. Any how the fans were alerted that the show had started and all of them reacted accordingly. Soon “Demon Eyes” by The Answer hits and the fans look to where the entrance area is. Just a door from the cabin area of the ship as out comes Blake Beckett wearing a suit and a smile heading towards the ring to the fans cheers, some recognizing him others hearing he’s the boss through word of mouth heading up the steps and into the ring he looks out at the crowd with a grin. BLAKE BECKETT: “Welcome ladies and gentleman to Revolt! I know some of you may not have heard of us, hopefully a lot of you have. As you may know we are the biggest entertainment you can find on Galveston Island. But you know our fan base is growing and that’s why we are starting on this trip to expand the GIW’s boarders. So tonight we have a special show for you, featuring an Open Battle Royal with all the stars of GIW invited! The rules are simple be the last person not pinned or submitted and you win…So enjoy the night everyo--” Just like that he is caught off mid-sentence as "Big Sh#t Poppin'" instrumental by T.I. hits through the speakers as the fans look around and Blake lets out a big sigh as he turns his head and watches as Ace Baldwin walks down towards the ring with a big grin on his face scoffing the entire way down the ring, heading up into the ring with a microphone of his own. ACE BALDWIN: “You see Blake you just keep making the same mistake OVER and OVER again. You come out here and not even one mention of the fact that your best wrestler…the main attraction “Amazing” Ace Baldwin will be in this match tonight! And you know you say that everyone will be involved? But that’s a lie. We all know that Alioth Starre won’t show up tonight. In fact I bet you told him not to…You guys are trying to protect him from me! You know it, I know it…EVERYONE knows it. So, why don’t you restart and do that little intro over again.” Ace lowers the mic staring over at Blake as the fans quickly catch on that Baldwin isn’t the nicest of guys and begin to boo him as Beckett raises his mic up. BLAKE BECKETT: “You know full well these people don’t care who you are. When you came out I could practically hear them making dinner way back in the kitchen. You really think your hot stuff don’t you Ace? You know I never tried protecting Alioth Starre and I can’t deny you have looked impressive these past few weeks. But before I grant you any type of title match I am going to need to know why, you were talking with David Sinclair at ‘Blackout II’…because yes I do watch my show.” Beckett emphasizes the my for Ace as Baldwin grins having perhaps expected this since the beginning. ACE BALDWIN: “You know Blake lets just say he and I agreed on many things about this company. But that’s not the subject at hand right now. The issue at hand is the fact that you have not yet announced me as your new number one contender. I want my title shot, so give it to me.” Baldwin does not seem to be budging as the fans boo his thoughts, catching on real quick to the story here in GIW as Blake shakes his head. BLAKE BECKETT: “I’m afraid I just can’t go around handing out title shots. You see everyone that gets a shot EARNS it. Aspen Chaud won the Sole Survivor back at ‘Category 5’ and Mark Cross won the Seize the Moment match that your mommy called you out of. So, if you want that shot you have to earn it. You’ve won the past two weeks, so win tonight and you can have your shot…lose and you can kiss it goodbye. Have a good day…” Blake pats him on the back stepping through the ropes and heading towards the back as the fans cheer on his choice as Ace pulls up the mic his ego demanding he have the last word. ACE BALDWIN: “You get your paper champion ready then! I will soon be your Undisputed Champion!” He says this then throws down the microphone in anger as the camera cuts to Blake shaking his head heading to the back as Baldwin struts around the ring as we take a break. ==== When we head to the back, this time its right outside of a cabin door. Reaching down to one of her legs is Aspen Chaud stretching out getting ready for the Open Battle Royal. Looking up back into the room where Alex Castellanos is laying on the bed. ASPEN CHAUD: “I can’t believe your not wrestling tonight, what your stomach hurts? Alex suffers from sea sickness?” Aspen chuckles at his expense as Alex groans in the room, clearly in the just starting to get sick stage. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “It was probably all the food I’ve been eating, maybe it was poisoned. Besides, I’d want you to have a chance.” The camera has an interesting view as we are looking over Aspen now and watching her from afar is a man in a mask that looks to be Nick Starks. She comes up looking that way and almost just as she does the figure disappears. ASPEN CHAUD: “You know, I’ve felt like I have been being watched…” Alex sits up behind her as she returns to the room, while stretching out her arms. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “Suddenly your worried about guys looking at you? The same girl that used her looks to trick several guys into helping her is suddenly worried about guys looking at her?” Aspen reaches down and socks him on the arm for making fun of her as she looks back towards the window. ASPEN CHAUD: “Whatever, you were one of those guys lest we forget. But this just feels different…I suppose I am just paranoid.” Castellanos nods his head as Aspen sits her wrestling boots up on the bed and ties them up. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “Good luck.” Aspen nods her head and then heads towards the door for the match that is next as we go to break. QUOTE As we return to the ring it has already hit the time for the Main Event aka a huge Open Battle Royal. "Big Sh#t Poppin'" instrumental by T.I. hits and immediately the crowd begins to boo as out comes the man they already know too much about as Ace Baldwin returns to the entrance area heading towards the ring mouthing something at the camera. RILEY STEVENS: “Well we are only here for one match and let me just start by saying that Oliver apparently didn’t think he had to be here because last we saw of him he was at the bar…So I’m hear alone and ready to watch Ace Baldwin here tonight fighting for a title shot.” Ace heads up into the ring, heading top rope to pose for the crowd to boo him as his music cuts off and then "Upload Complete" by Last Chance to Reason hits and out from the back comes Nick Starks walking with a mask on his face, expressionless but his walk is that of an angry man making a straight line towards the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Some of you may not recognize this man but that is Nick Starks a man whom lost to Aspen Chaud and Alex Castellanos and apparently had some facial damages that put him into this match. Word is he is none to pleased about it…and I fear about what he might do here tonight.” Starks enters the ring heading to a corner as "Bullseye" by Aly and AJ hits and as Aspen Chaud comes out we get a very positive from the crowd. She high fives new and old fans on her way to the ring heading up the steps heading into the ring right away and blowing a kiss to the camera turning into Nick Starks who starts to advance towards her but is stopped by the referee. RILEY STEVENS: “You know I’ve heard he’s been almost obsessed with revenge on Aspen and I don’t know if her coming out here was the wisest of ideas.” Aspen heads to a corner seeming a little spooked by the whole thing as Ace is seen smirking over the incident as "Country Heroes" by Hank Williams III hits through the speakers and out comes the ‘GIW Television Champion’ Curtis Durrington a man whom has impressed everyone of late carrying his Television belt with him one that he’s already given prestige to. RILEY STEVENS: “This man right here has endeared himself to everyone in the back due to his no nonsense approach and frankly as the TV champion I see him bringing that belt to new heights.” Durrington enters the ring, handing the belt out of the ring politely to Jamal Jackson as he heads back to a corner all four in different corners ready to go as we wait to see anyone else, nobody comes so the referee steps to the middle of the ring and calls for the bell. DING DING DING! Right away the four in the ring stand at their corners and immediately its Starks walking out of the corner not taking his eyes off of Aspen the entire time. However it’s Ace bumping him that stops him as he points at Aspen suggesting that they go after her together. Wanting nothing more then to hurt her Nick nods as they advance but it’s Durrington that walks over besides her evening up the odds. The fans cheer for him as Aspen nods over at him and the two go to bat. It’s Aspen throwing a big forearm at Nick and his mask while Curtis and Ace exchange blows. Chaud attempts to whip Starks to the corner but he reverses it and throws her into it instead. He rushes at her but at the last moment she raises up her legs kicking him in the face. Stumbling back Aspen rushes at him with a Lou Thesz Press following it up with mounted punches. On the other side of the ring Durrington whips Ace off the ropes then puts him down a Clothesline. The fans cheer him on as Ace stumbles to his feet and he grabs him around his waist and hits a Release German Suplex to a loud cheer from the crowd. Baldwin immediately rolls out of the ring holding his back in pain as on the other side as Starks gets to his feet Aspen rushes and dropkicks him through the ropes and out of the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Durrington and Chaud are rolling much to the delight of these fans.” Aspen gets to her feet then turns around looking right at Curtis both of them setting up in wrestling position, reaching out and shaking hands showing their mutual respect as they go for a lock up which we all know never favors Aspen. However this time as he starts to back her up she releases and drops under him rolling him up 1…2…Durrington reverses it pulling himself on top 1…2…Aspen now rolls through and gets on top with a bridge 1…2…Durrington pulls himself up then spins out as both end up on a vertical base. The crowd gives them both standing ovations as they share a nod going in again this time Curtis ducks a wild swing from Aspen, then grabs her and nails her with a Belly to Belly Suplex! The fans are behind him at this point as Starks hops up onto the ring apron and Curtis rushes at him nailing him with a big jumping knee that sends Starks right back to the outside. Ace is seen taking a breather happy to be out of the action for a second as the distraction allows Aspen to get up. She takes a wild swing with the ‘Queen's Wrath II’ a Heel Kick. But Durrington with eyes in the back of his head ducks it and kicks her in the gut then crushes her with the ‘War Eagle’ a Scissored DDT! The fans are in shock as Curtis gets on top hooking her leg 1…2…3! Aspen Chaud is Eliminated RILEY STEVENS: “What a move, and perhaps to some people’s surprise Curtis Durrington just eliminated Curtis.” Ace gets up onto the ring apron as Aspen is rolled out of the ring by the referee. Curtis begs him to get into the ring with him, wanting to take him out next. Starks on the outside is up to his feet and he looks over at Aspen whom has gotten up and is starting to stumble her way towards the back with the fans cheering her on. This seems to make Nick angry having wanted to be the one to take her out. He rolls back into the ring, breathing heavily as he stalks Curtis from behind who is still asking Ace to get in. Then boom from behind Starks smashes him from behind, picking him up and in angry fit spins around and throws Curtis through the ropes into the corner ring post. Looking out at the crowd taking out all of his anger on Curtis he doesn’t even allow Durrington to fall to the mat before he hits the ‘Seeing Starks’ a Reverse STO, driving Curtis face first into the middle turnbuckle! The crowd boos as he makes the cover pulling him away from the ropes first 1…2…Kick Out! They breathe a sigh of relief as still standing on the outside of the ring is Ace Baldwin having taken a long breather. RILEY STEVENS: “Ace is letting Starks take out of all his anger on Curtis before trying anything.” Curtis gets up to his feet but it’s a whole lot slower as he favors his shoulder that hit the ring post. Jumping up and going Springboard he goes for a Thrust Kick but he hits all air as Durrington ducks it. He throws his good hand a few times punching him back as the fans start to get behind him again hoping he can overcome the odds. Trying to use all of his strength he goes for a Fisherman Suplex but as he goes for some lift his shoulder won’t let him and he drops down holding it. This basically sets up right away for Starks to hit the ‘Starkpocalypse’ a Swinging, Inverted Diamond Cutter this time without the Hammerlock instead going right into a pin 1…2…3! Curtis Durrington is Eliminated RILEY STEVENS: “I think Starks is obsessed with payback on Aspen and he just took out his anger on Curtis for taking her out before he could…” Starks gets up looking down at him as he screams out “I am the one that takes her out! NOT YOU!” still seeming angry about the fact that he took him out. As he starts to seem to lose his anger. Sliding back into the ring is the opportunistic Ace Baldwin, crawling in behind him grinning as he looks out at the crowd. While the referee helps Curtis out of the ring Ace crushes Nick with a low blow, even though it couldn’t have happened to a worst guy the fans still boo after all that’s never nice. Hopping on top confidently he makes the cover 1…2…Kick Out! RILEY STEVENS: “Not quite yet, Ace is guaranteed a shot if he wins and you’d have to think Starks might have the same opportunity.” Ace gets to his feet grabbing the referee by the shirt screaming at him “Is this a set up! COUNT THE PIN!” Baldwin releases and turns around going to put him away but to his surprise Starks hops up at the last moment punching him away. Right into a Wrist Lock then transitioning into a Discus Elbow a move he calls the ‘Rip Cord Roaring Elbow’ that knocks him down. The fans at this point are not really interested in either man winning just interested to see who it will be. Nick tries to lock in the ‘Simply Outclassed” but at the last second Baldwin kicks him away. Both men hop up to their feet and right as they do Ace rakes the eyes. The referee scolds him but unfortunately it’s enough as Nick blinded walks right into the ‘T.F.C’ aka the ‘Thanks For Coming’ a Running Death Valley Driver with cradle for the pin, 1…2…3! JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…“Amazing” ACE BALDWIN!” The fans are not happy at all booing as Ace gets to his feet with a smirk on his face demanding the referee raise his arm which he does. These fans new and old already hate him and as he parades around the ring they only hate him more. RILEY STEVENS: “There we have it…Ace is your new number one contender.” The boos continue as people start to head on their way back to whatever they were doing as Ace grabs the camera and pulls it towards him mouthing “Your scrawny champion is next!” he pushes it away so that he can continue to gloat as the short week’s show comes to a close. WINNER: ACE BALDWIN
