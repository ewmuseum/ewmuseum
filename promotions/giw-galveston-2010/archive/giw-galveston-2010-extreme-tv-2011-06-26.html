GIW — Card

Extreme TV, 26 June 2011

26 June 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: ETV LIVE! From Benno’s On The Beach - June 26, 2011 BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! “Hurricane” by 30 Seconds to Mars is playing over the speakers, the crowd of over one hundred people are gathered in and around Benno’s on the Beach getting ready for Extreme TV, the prelude to Category 5. The ION Television cameras cut over to Colt Cox, Amanda Rowland, and a new commentator over at the announce table. COLT COX: “Ladies and gentlemen, welcome to Galveston Island Wrestling’s Extreme TV. I’m Colt Cox alongside my broadcast partner, Amanda Rowland, and GIW’s newest commentator… Zachary Pagonis. Welcome to the announce booth.” ZACHARY PAGONIS: “I’m happy to be here Colt. This may just be the pre-show to Cat 5, but these fans are treating it like it is the real deal. Extreme TV is loaded tonight with several matches that will catch your attention. It is sure to be one hell of a show Colt.” COLT COX: “That’s a true statement, just being on ETV should be considered an honor, and tonight will be great. We are going to have an open battle royal match as always on ETV, but this one is a bit different, the winner of the match can pick anyone on the GIW roster to challenge for the GIW Tag Team Championships on an upcoming episode of Revolt! You have to be able to choose wisely, because teamwork is key.” ZACHARY PAGONIS: “Tonight at Category 5, Matt Kluge has another shot at redemption when his team of Tony Miranda and Mark Cross takes on Wake’s team, the winning captain will get to pick the stipulation for their match at Category 5.” AMANDA ROWLAND: “Bra and panties…!?” ZACHARY PAGONIS: “For some reason I don’t think that will happen.” COLT COX: “There is a lot on the line in that match, and those two men legitimately hate each other. When you have that kind of hate being given off by two men, it never disappoints.” ZACHARY PAGONIS: “Then of course you have the staple match of Category 5, the Sole Survivor match. Four competitors enter and in a grueling four match gauntlet, we will crown the number one contender for the GIW Undisputed Championship at the biggest show of the year, Moment of Glory! There is some speculation about Aspen Chaud though.” COLT COX: “She has Damien Dunn on her side, and personally I think Alex Castellanos is working for her as well.” ZACHARY PAGONIS: “The new Revolt GM promises to have a special announcement about a new faction of hers tonight, could Alex be the newest member?” AMANDA ROWLAND: “When does the match start?” COLT COX: “Sole survivor isn’t until Cat 5, Amanda…” AMANDA ROWLAND: “No. God. ANY match, I don’t want to listen to you two all night.” “Hard” by Rihanna hit’s the speakers, replacing the Category 5 theme music. La’Sarah Audric walks out from the backstage area with Unforgettable Jones by her side. The crowd boo’s Audric as she walks down to the ring and takes a microphone from Paul Marshall, who is acting as the Extreme TV ring announcer for the evening. LA’SARAH AUDRIC: “Last week on Revolt, you all witnessed the ruthless aggression that can be dished out by Unforgettable Jones, but it raised some questions from the fans. I came out here tonight, and I would like to formally announce that Unforgettable Jones has been hired by ME, to protect me from you bottom dwellers as well as the rest of the cretins that call Galveston Island Wrestling their employer. You see, I feel as if over the past few weeks I have fallen through the cracks on Revolt. Now, I’m not even totally in charge of Revolt any more. If Aspen can have a group, so can I. So welcome, Unforgettable Jones… to Team Audric!” Audric raises Jones hand, they laugh together, then Audric hands the microphone over to Unforgettable, who begins to address the crowd through the boos. UNFORGETTABLE JONES: I deserve more than I get here in GIW, so when the beautiful La’Sarah Audric offered me a spot on Team Audric, I couldn’t refuse.” The crowd ruthlessly boos Jones, but he just pushes through them. UNFORGETTABLE JONES: “I don’t care what you people say, at the end of the day you don’t sign my checks. This woman does. At the end of the day, you guys can’t get me championships, this woman can. And by the time I’m done in GIW, I will be truly… Unforgettable.” Unforgettable drops the mic and Audric exit’s the ring. He goes into his corner and waits for the open battle royal to begin. QUOTE As we return from a short break centered mostly around hype for ‘Category 5’ later tonight we find ourselves with a ring full of stars. Three locals named Jim, Ryan, and Carl as well as recently signed Leo James and then of course Unforgettable Jones. Looking at them it’s clear that Jones is the clear favorite in this one as the referee stars the match. DING DING DING! Standing on the outside La’Sarah cheers them on as Jones grins in the corner waiting for them to come at him. All four men go after him, looking like they are being fed to a beast. Right away Jones kicks one away then grabs up Ryan and tosses him across the ring. Finally grabbing Carl he hammers him with a punch that sends him reeling. However to the surprise of the crowd little known Leo James begins to hammer away at Jones in the corner showing absolutely no signs of backing down from the big man. However a big kick to the stomach sends him stumbling back away from the big man. Grabbing up Ryan, he lifts him into the air with an ‘Unforgettable Crush’ a Dragon Suplex! Being so close to the ropes he sends him right over the top to the outside, Ryan hits with a thud and seems seriously injured. Next up is Jim who tries to attack him only for Jones to grab him over his head and throw him right to the outside on top of the already hurt Ryan. The crowd is booing him as again Leo James runs at him catching him with a big punch to the face but being so big Jones tosses him away with relative ease. Foolish Carl runs at him trying to do something about the big man, but he receives an ‘Unforgettable Attack’ a Clothesline from Hell that literally sends Carl flipping into the air then landing on the mat out cold. Grabbing him up, despite knowing he is out cold Jones turns and tosses him over. When he turns around he receives a huge Running Knee from James that catches everybody off guard! Jones stumbles against the ropes and seems to be out of it. James tries to lift him over but to no avail…As Jones when finally recovered pushes him away. Shaking his head he grabs him and tells him he shouldn’t have done that. La’Sarah jumps up onto the apron pulling down the ropes for Jones. Turning he goes to throw him over but at the last possible moment Leo James slides out of his grasps and uses Jones own momentum to push him over the ropes Audric pulled down! Out goes Jones and Leo James wins! ZACHARY PAGONIS: “He pulled it off, the huge upset!” The crowd goes wild as Leo holds up his arms in victory. Showing little signs of being hurt Unforgettable slides back into the ring and goes after him only for James to slide out of the ring heading to the back. Angrily Jones punches the turnbuckle in the corner as La’Sarah tries to calm him down, as Leo heads to the back as this may not be the last time we see these two fight. WINNER: LEO JAMES We cut over to Poretto Beach backstage at the Category 5 arena set up. Blake Beckett is backstage with the MWA Chairman, Jeff Ricketts and MWA superstar, Ryan Hart. Ricketts and Hart are dressed up in suits for the occasion while Beckett is in more relaxed attire, as it is still a while before the show. Beckett is typing on a computer backstage in the plaza of the Hotel Galvez, which serves as the locker room area for the show, seeing as it is right across the Sea Wall from Poretto. RYAN HART: “What he did wasn’t excusable. You need to terminate his contract now.” BLAKE BECKETT: “I’m really sorry guys, I sent Alex to Lincoln to be cordial with the MWA, not insult it. All I can say is that he doesn’t represent GIW and it wont happen again.” JEFF RICKETTS: “It really wasn’t that big of a deal. My fans are smart enough to know that he is just a stain on this good promotion, I don’t think we lost any in attendance. As far as I’m concerned it never happened.” RYAN HART: “Are you kidding me, this is just not excusable Jeff. Blake, look, that guy is your GIW North Beach Champion, you hand selected him to come on to our show and badmouth us. This hasn’t fallen on deaf ears Blake. There will be retaliation.” JEFF RICKETTS: “No there wont.” RYAN HART: “Yes, there will be. Blake, I will lead an army into GIW and demolish every single star that you have, starting with Castellanos.” JEFF RICKETTS: “Ryan… stop.” RYAN HART: “No, it’s official… I’ll do it next week…” JEFF RICKETTS: “We don’t want to stoop to their level.” Beckett slowly turns around to Ricketts and Hart. Ricketts hangs his head, knowing what he said wasn’t the best choice in words, Hart has a big smile now. BLAKE BECKETT: “Stoop to our level?” Ricketts opens his mouth to try to talk his way out of it, but he just ends up biting his tongue. BLAKE BECKETT: “Fine, Hart… if you want to see who has the best talent, why don’t we have a match. July 7th Revolt. Three people from GIW versus three of MWA’s finest competitors. Deal.” Hart quickly shakes Beckett’s hand, the match was made. Beckett walked off into a locker room while Hart turns to Ricketts and just nods his head. We cut back to Zachary, Colt, and Amanda at the announce booth. AMANDA ROWLAND: “It is time for some good old fashioned ass kicking, GIW style.” ZACHARY PAGONIS: “An interfederation match up announced, and this is only the prelude to Category 5. It is six forty five right now everyone, Category 5 starts at eight. Be sure to keep it on ION television until then. We are going to take a quick commercial break, then we will have the main event of the evening for you. Marcus Hister versus Hank Mardukas.” = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = After the commercial break, we cut backstage where Leo James is backstage, talking to GIW interviewer Sarah Sharp. They are just chatting backstage before the show, until Nick Riot walks up from behind Sarah. Leo stops talking, Sarah turns around to look at what has walked up behind her. NICK RIOT: “Get lost.” Sarah stares at him for a moment, then walks down the hallway away from the two men. Leo is very annoyed at Riot. LEO JAMES: “Is there something I can help you with, Nick?” NICK RIOT: “I want to challenge you to match. Next Thursday on Revolt. You see, last week I didn’t do so well. This week, I am going to make up for it. If I win, you have to choose me as your partner for the tag team titles. If not, the decision is up to you." Leo mulls over this for a moment, then nods. LEO JAMES: “I accept. I hope you are prepared.” Leo walks down the hallway, Riot watches him leave. We cut back to the announce booth where the main event is ready to begin. QUOTE Heading back to the ring it is time for the featured contest here on ETV, as we head to the ring we are waiting as “The Mob Goes Wild” by Clutch hits and the fans go wild for the man whom had become a big fan favorite Hank Mardukas walks out onto the stage. ZACHARY PAGONIS: “Here is Hank Mardukas a man many have taken a liking to because of his always come to fight attitude.” COLT COX: “Yeah, look where that’s gotten him.” Walking towards the ring he high fives a few fans then heads up the steps and into the ring just as “Under the Knife” by Rise Against hits and the crowd again begins to boo as out comes Marcus Hister looking just as focused as usual only this time we sense some anger. ZACHARY PAGONIS: “We’ve heard a lot about Marcus Hister being unhappy with his spot here on ETV, a win here would certainly move him up in the ranks.” Heading up the steps he stares into the ring at Mardukas as he angrily steps through the ropes. But before anything can even happen, he runs across the ring and Spear’s him! The crowd begins to boo as Hank holds his gut and the referee pulls Marcus off pushing him away. ZACHARY PAGONIS: “There is his anger being released there.” The referee lets Hank get to his feet as he asks him if he can still go or whether he’d rather forfeit the contest. Mardukas shakes it off and tells him to start the damn match. DING DING DING! Wasting little time Marcus walks forward cornering him again and beginning to lay into him repeatedly with big punches in the corner. The crowd is booing the cheap shot that has led to him getting this early advantage. Pushing him away Hank tries to get out of the corner but Marcus lands a big haymaker that sends Hank stumbling back. Grinning he backs up and goes for a running clothesline but at the last possible moment Hank side steps it and gets out of the way. Hister hit’s the turnbuckle then turns and walks right into an Overhead Belly to Belly Suplex! The crowd has begun to cheer as Hister lands on his back but quickly gets to his feet, stumbling towards Hank who grabs him and whips him into the corner. Taking some time to remember where he is Marcus looks around only to be met with a huge Corner Splash from big bad Hank Mardukas! Marcus walks forward then quickly falls to the mat as Hank walks over making a cover 1…2…Kick Out! Not near enough to put away the always hard to beat Marcus Hister. Pulling up Marcus with him Hank whips him over to the ropes. Taking a running start himself he lands a Flying Shoulder Block! Marcus gets right back up then receives another. The third time Marcus gets up he is much slower and walks right into a Spinebuster! The crowd cheers as Hank, takes a step back and prepares to end this thing. ZACHARY PAGONIS: “Hank is going to try to end this!” In the corner of the ring he’s down to one knee preparing for the ‘Pitbull Attack’ as Marcus stumbles to his feet. Looking out at the crowd Marcus looks at his area where a few of his fans are and then when he turns around he is able to counter Hank’s finish right into a Tornado DDT! The crowd boos as Marcus seems to realize he dodged a bullet there as he makes the cover, 1…2…Kick Out! Hank shows his fighting spirit as Marcus gets up knowing what he has to do to win this thing. As Hank gets to his hands and knees, Hister runs forward and lands a punt kick to his stomach flipping him onto his back. Once there Marcus looks around and heads to the top, looking again at those people with him today he jumps off and lands a picture perfect Moonsault that wows the crowd! He makes the cover again 1…2…Kick Out! This time Marcus gets up angry that Hank kicked out of that move. Taking a few steps back he seems to snap, angrily turning and glaring at Mardukas as he gets up. Finally as he turns around Marcus goes for the Superkick but Hank ducks it and then grabs him by the neck, preparing for his ‘Chest Slam’ but Marcus now spins around and then right into the ‘Mystery’s Darkness’ his version of the RKO that shuts the crowd up as he makes the cover 1…2…3! That’s it. ZACHARY PAGONIS: “What a match from these two always resilient fighters, it’s a shame Marcus took a cheap shot but he got the win anyway.” COLT COX: “That’s all that matters.” Marcus gets up to his feet as the referee raises his arm, disgruntled for being on this show he rips away his arm and then exits the ring as we head to the announce table. WINNER: MARCUS HISTER ZACHARY PAGONIS: “That was one hell of a match to wrap up Extreme TV. Category 5 is just one hour away everyone. The sun is going down and the waves are rolling in. You can feel the aura on this island right now, can’t you Colt?” COLT COX: “It’s like the calm before the storm Zach, what you have just seen was the showers, now we are about to enter the eye of the hurricane.” AMANDA ROWLAND: “Hurricane’s have eyes?” ZACHARY PAGONIS: “That’s it for ETV everyone, stay tuned for GIW’s final super show before Moment of Glory II, Category 5. Remember, this is GIW… Dare to Defy!” The GIW logo flashes across the faded screen and we cut to commercial.
