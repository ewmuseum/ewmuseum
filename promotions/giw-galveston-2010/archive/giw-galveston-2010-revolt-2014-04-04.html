GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: REVOLT Live from Courville Stadium - April 4th, 2014 BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! The fireworks can be seen streaming high into the sky over Courville Stadium in this cloudy spring night in Galveston. Rather than panning around the arena we cut directly to the ring where Madison Seton stands, belt over her shoulder, mic in hand. MADISON SETON: “We don’t have time for all the production packages tonight. This is my time, my show.” The crowd showers her with boos. Seton rolls her eyes. MADISON SETON: “A few weeks ago, I came out here and exposed your hypocrisy, and none of you liked it! You called me crazy, bitchy, hormonal, overrated – I literally heard it all, either in person or online, or through gossip. But now, there is only one thing you can call me – champ.” She pulls the belt off of her shoulder and holds it high into the air, rubbing her hand against the newly engraved nameplate. MADISON SETON: “I know none of you expected it. Oh, how could I defeat the mighty Michael Lynch… who won his belt only two weeks ago when he defeated the mighty Alexis Terry. Well, guess what. Not only have I beaten Terry, but I’ve also beaten Lynch. And the best part is that I did it clean. Fair and square. No cheating required, even though you expect that from me.” Some small “Terry” chants break out in the audience. Seton presses on. MADISON SETON: “But I continue to defy expectations. And so do all of you. The only differences is that I exceed mine, and you always fall short of yours. I EXPECT you all to be able to recognize a top level wrestler when you see one, but week after week, boo after boo, you all prove me wrong. Frankly, I’m about to give up on all of you.” “SE-TON SUCKS” chants pour in from the crowd. MADISON SETON: “You fans are like the kid that sits in the back of the class who cracks jokes all time and never does his work, but the teacher tries really hard to relate to. Let me just say, I don’t think I’ll ever be able to relate to you, so I’m just going to stop trying. You say you want wrestling, well I’m the best wrestler in this company. You say you want charisma; Mendoza gives me the opening segment. You say you want sex appeal, I’m the best looking in this company. If I wasn’t the total package before, the Undisputed Championship really puts me over the edge.” Seton looks over at her belt, smirking. MADISON SETON: “I am having the security guards hand out pens and note cards to each and every one of you out there. Now, assuming you can read and write, I want you to PERSONALLY apologize to me for booing me, slandering me, and doubting me.” The camera cuts over the guards who are indeed following her orders. The fans are extremely confused, and one man even rips up the card and tosses the pen at Seton. Several others do this too. MADISON SETON: “And before you ask – YES I will read them. Because, as the people’s champ, I have decided that I will forgive the ones who seek forgiveness. I’ll have my people pick them up after the show.” More boos from the audience. MADISON SETON: “As for tonight, my match with Ruby Reynolds – you people in the audience may really enjoy March Madness, but Ruby Reynolds is not a bracket buster. I am the number one seed, and somehow this little girl made it into my tournament. Tonight, you’ll be lucky if you make it out intact.” Seton turns her attention the ramp where she awaits Ruby.
