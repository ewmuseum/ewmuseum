GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: THURSDAY NIGHT REVOLT LIVE! From Porretto Beach – July 18, 2013 BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! The show begins with fireworks streaming into the air and the camera panning around the ring revealing the raucous crowd that has gathered around North Beach. “Demon Eyes” by the Answer soon replaces the music playing in the background the fans erupt in boos as Blake Beckett makes his way out to the ring. The commentators are heard over the music. RILEY STEVENS: “Look! It's the chairman making his way down to the ring to kick off Revolt.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “This is the man who is behind the plot to retire Alex Castellanos once and for all.” RILEY STEVENS: “Only if he loses to Alexis Terry at Category 5, but that may not happen!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “We'll see Riley, we'll see.” Blake steps into the ring and raises up the mic. The crowd hushes except for a few hecklers who keeps booing him. BLAKE BECKETT: “Calm down, calm down. I'm not here to further distance your favorite Alex Castellanos from Galveston Island Wrestling, oh no, I'm here to make an announcement to right a wrong. An announcement regarding Sole Survivor.” Blake takes a moment, then continues. BLAKE BECKETT: “You see Sole Survivor is one of the biggest matches of the year. It will dictate who challenges for the Undisputed Championship at Moment of Glory. Anyone who enters this match has the chance to become champion in less than a month. This is why we have to position ourselves for success by stacking this match with only the best. That is why I am proud to announce the first entrant into the Sole Survivor match, a man who should still be champion, Alioth Starre!” Alioth's music hits to a mixed reaction from the fans. It is clear that they are divided between him and Wake Walker. Alioth steps up onto the ramp and raises his hand to a big reaction. ALIOTH STARRE: “Thank you Blake. You know... I talk a lot, but I've always been able to back...” Just then, completely out of the blue, “Who Dat” by J Cole hit and the fans erupted Ace Baldwin, after a long time away from the company, stepped onto the ramp with a big smile on his face. ACE BALDWIN: “Alioth... Alioth... I'mma let you finish, but I have a bigger announcement to make tonight in Galveston.” The crowd cheers. Blake seems confused as he looks up at Alioth and Ace sharing the stage. ACE BALDWIN: “Before you booted Alex out, he signed me back to GIW. We both sensed something was coming, so he made a special addition to my contract. Ace Baldwin is in the Sole Survivor match, and Blake, there is no way you can get out of it.” Blake raises and eyebrow. Alioth seems very angry. BLAKE BECKETT: “That's fine Ace. I just think that you need to know that there are other guys in the back that actually deserve the spot. Guys who don't just show up and play buddy buddy with the guys in charge.” ACE BALDWIN: “Don't worry Blake. I'll earn my place. I'll be the next champ.” “Who Dat” hits up again and the crowd goes nuts as we fade to break. QUOTE After that backstage segment we return to the ring, one that is full to the brim now with six different stars spread out on two different sides, due to the big teams making their entrance during the last segment to save time. On one side we have Justin Harmony on the other, we have Miss Jade the two having a falling out after there failed attempt at teaming up in the tournament last week. Alongside them we have TJ Williams and Seth Cohen who did battle in the Open Battle Royal last week and ended up holding some resentment for the finish. And finally Nathan Bailey IV and Trent King who were the stand outs in last week’s Six Man Scramble that Bailey IV left the victor of, despite King being close to taking it. RILEY STEVENS: “This match is six different stars, all having a little bit of issues from last week split into two teams to do battle. Seems appropriate to get these fresh stars, a chance to stand out in a crowd.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “There are a few I already like here.” Each side seems to be trying to get on the same page in their corner while also discussing who is going to start the match, as the two sides are ready to go to war and the referee starts the match. DING DING DING! These fans are started off a little spit on who exactly to root for, all these stars relatively new and the teams a mixture of good guys and bad guys. None the less they try to pick out there favorites to root for as this match gets underway and starting things off for the first team will be Seth Cohen while on the other side Trent King elects to get things going for his team. Cohen doesn’t seem focused on King at all, instead choosing to look past him at Jade and Williams as he walks around as Trent angered by his lack of respect rushes at him only for Cohen to side step him and kick him in the back smirking as he does so. The kick not really meant to hurt King, just take him off his game a bit which it does as he throws a wild haymaker that Seth ducks, then hits a Spinning Chop again more for show then any real damage as these fans have decided they hate Cohen now, as he smirks as Trent shakes his head trying to regroup as he runs and Seth doubles him over with a Gut Punch then hits a Chop Block to his knee, getting up and walking towards Jade and Williams smirking as he looks back at Trent who holds his knee in pain. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I like this kid…” Cohen turns back over to Trent as he gets up and kicks his knee as soon as he gets to one leg, dropping him back to the mat as King tries to push him away but Seth angrily throws a knee to the stomach, then pushes him to the mat going for a cover 1…2-Kick Out. Still not enough to put him away as Seth gets up walking over to TJ and yelling at him, which only seems to anger him some more. Cohen takes a bit as he blows a kiss over to Jade, playfully as she tries to stay focused as he turns back to King who is now up to his feet as he throws a big punch but King ducks it as he lips towards Williams and Jade reaching out for Miss Jade, but she doesn’t try too hard to reach back out as Williams tries his best, but Cohen grabs a hold of him and only seems to let King think he’s got a way out before pulling him back and kicking out his knee from behind again. This time TJ starts having a talk with Jade, realizing that she didn’t want to have to get in there with Seth as the two bicker for a bit on the ropes, this catches Seth’s eyes and he walks over to confront TJ about talking to his girl but it was all a ruse as Williams right away throws a punch landing on Seth before he backs up, and King now up jumps up and tags in Williams! RILEY STEVENS: “TJ is in!” Williams jumps over the ropes in anticipation of getting his hands on Seth but calmly Cohen walked back to his corner as TJ entered. He ran full steam at him but at the last second Seth tags out and slides out of the ring as Williams hits the corner and bounces back. In comes Bailey now taking a swing at Williams who ducks under it and then catches Nathan coming in with a huge European Uppercut then follow up Dropkick. Turning around he spins and takes Harmony off the ropes with a big punch, before climbing up to the top rope staring down at Seth who watches him from a few feet away as he catches Bailey standing up with a Diving Crossbody to the mat that the fans seem to enjoy. On the outside Cohen seems down with this match and watching Williams as he heads backwards up the ramp still watching, waving to Jade as TJ determined to make a statement to him while he’s still out there probably rushes into the ‘Emerald City’ a Double Underhook Flip Piledriver but Bailey spins out elbowing him before jumping up and tagging in Harmony just as Cohen reaches the back. RILEY STEVENS: “Cohen just bailed on his opponents here.” Harmony comes running into the ring at full steam, towards TJ who ducks under it and gives Justin a shove to the back to keep his momentum going sending him into there corner. King is still favoring his knee on the outside as Jade reaches out, TJ gives her the tag before pulling him back into the ring and connecting with a Suplex leading right into a Elbow Drop from Jade, as she goes for a cover 1…2-Kick Out. She gets back up, determined to make a statement and take out the man she’s had a bit of an issue with the past few weeks as she nails him with a Spinning Side Kick as he gets to his feet that doubles him over allowing her to hit a quick Russian Legsweep to take him down as Harmony gets up looking like he’s there for the taking as Jade can definitely tell as she goes for ‘The Kiss Goodnight’ a Roundouse Kick that he ducks but with a back up plan in mind she connects right away with ‘The Peacemaker’ a Inverted leg drop bulldog into a pin as these fans count along 1…2…3! REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here are your winners by way of pinfall… Miss Jade, TJ Williams, and Trent King!” Having pretty much turned on Cohen mid-match these fans seem thrilled to see this team win as King steps down off the ropes holding his knee checking on it as Williams joins Jade in the ring, putting his hatred for the man she’s with to the side to celebrate the two’s victory. RILEY STEVENS: “This Williams and Cohen rivalry is heating up here, I think Cohen is scared but that’s just me.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “He’s getting into his head Riley, just look how mad TJ is. He’s got him all heated and besides he’d have made Jade mad if he didn’t let her do her thing.” TJ looks over at Jade not with much response, shaking his head as he heads out of the ring while Jade celebrates her big win here on Revolt as we head to the announce table. WINNERS: MISS JADE, TJ WILLIAMS, & TRENT KING As we stay at ringside we have Riley, Oliver, and Amanda who stares blankly off into the crowd as Riley begins to talk, not the usual mid-match segment yet as we are about to figure out why. Not finished with what happened last week, it seems as Clay takes HardKore and catches him with a few big shots to the jaw, then a huge European Uppercut that drops him to the mat. Smith who was scheduled to face HardKore comes over to try to stop the attack but gets caught with a High Knee that puts his lights out as he falls to the ground. Not caring about where the two are Clay shows off his strength by hitting the ‘Goodnight Irene’ a Powerbomb on the Knees to HardKore, putting him out as he stood over him muttering something to him, before walking off as we head back to the ringside area. AMANDA ROWLAND: “Wow that was like, so mean!” RILEY STEVENS: “…Indeed, you could say that while the two had a draw last week, Clay sent a message loud and clear that he is not to be messed with…” Riley wraps it up as before the next mach we get another short break to hold us over, these fans waiting in anticipation as we fade. But rather then letting her off easy he lets her hold her chest in pain, then grabs her under her waist like a keg and hoists her up walking over to the corner tossing her through the ropes to the outside, she stops her fall a bit by holding the ropes but ends up falling to the outside none the less. As Clay gets up onto the ring apron as she tries to get back through the ropes but is hung up to dry for a Krueger clothesline taking her down to the mat.
