GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: FULL THROTTLE LIVE! From The Commodore Hotel – August 25, 2011 QUOTE “Amazing” Ace Baldwin vs. Mercutio Prince The match starts off quickly with Ace dominating the silly Shakespeare actor using gimmick wrestler. He crushes Mercutio with a clothesline that almost takes Prince out of the match all together. He Piledrives Mercutio into the mat before hitting him with a Thanks For Coming Death Valley Driver with a Cradle. The whole ordeal takes 2 minutes. QUOTE James Shark vs. L.A.P.D It’s poetic really… L.A.P.D goes out and tries to bounce the Muscleshark’s face off the mat a couple times. He throws Shark outside of the ring and whips him into the turnbuckle. He drops him with Sleeper neck breaker outside. L.A.P.D gets back into the ring. When Shark manages to get back into the ring L.A.P.D throws him right into the turnbuckle shoulder first and then pulls him into the Riot Control Backbreaker Rack holding Shark down until the time limit and then to further humiliate Shark, he taps out. QUOTE Koji Nakamora vs. “The Governor” Othello Lancaster In an East vs. slightly less east then moved to Texas showdown Koji uses his Japanese based wrestling while Othello uses a very old school European style. Both men trade stiff strikes and submissions. Koji turns the tides with a Powebomb and then a Shining Wizard. Othello works his way up to be met with a Nakamora Death Drop at the 4 minute mark. QUOTE El Ladder vs. The Pillow We Have a Theme Song by The Pillows start playing and The Pillow gets taken out by a stagehand. Everyone is pretty meh about it, someone is holding a sign that condemns The Pillow as a cheap soft knockoff, which is weird being a Pillow and all. Anyway The Climb by Miley Cyrus hits and the entire building starts shaking, no really, go look down at Revolt, the pop for El Ladder was louder then any other one, and I will never know why. Izzie Dead and Ima Goner come out holding El Ladder and milk the crowd for some more cheers. They set El Ladder up in the ring and the match is on! Why that exclamation point was needed I have no idea. With both objects stuck in place, Ima Goner starts dancing around ringside for the fan’s enjoyment. It even distracts the ref and Izzie Dead rolls into the ring. Even though it’s a pillow Izzie Dead unloads on it with stomps and elbows. When the ref turns around Izzie pulls out a blanket that matches the color of the mat and lies down underneath it. Trying to figure out if that lump has always been there the ref kicks it a few times. He shrugs and goes back to officiating, Izzie Dead crawls out of the ring. After some more standing around Izzie pulls El Ladder out of the ring. He pushes El Ladder underneath the apron and rolls under himself. Eventually Izzie Dead comes crawling out wearing a Ladder with a lucha libre mask duct taped to it. He comes into the ring, wearing the disguise. When the ref steps in questioning him Izzie responds with “…” the ref shrugs and allows it. Izzie hit’s the Pillow with a High Rung and then does a rung drop! “El Ladder” pins The Pillow! The ref counts to 3 and this match is over. As The Grateful Dead walk away a Wake Walker cons player thumbs up in agreement over the match.
