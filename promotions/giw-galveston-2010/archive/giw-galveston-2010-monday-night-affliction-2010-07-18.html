Monday Night Affliction, 18 July 2010

18 July 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GIW PRESENTS: Monday Night Affliction! Live! From Courville Stadium! July 18th, 2010! BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! Fireworks explode from the local gem on Galveston Island, and the crowd of around one-hundred and eighty fans go insane at the sight of Affliction Commissioner, Megathreat, standing in the center of the ring with a microphone in his hand. Megathreat: Ladies… calm down! Gentlemen… stand up! This is Monday Night Affliction; and you are about to view what is surely going to become the staple of this promotion. Tonight, we had the joy of seeing Chris Crawford winning a dark match battle royal. Without further a due, lets get on with the show! The members of each team make their way down to the ring as Megathreat walks up it. The bell rings and the match is now on the way. Adam Calloway: Looks like ladder is setting up in the ring first! Carlos Castillo: But how will he fair against Commander D? The bell rings and Commander D looks at the ladder confused what to do. He walks towards the ladder but it suddenly falls over and on top of Commander D. 1 2 Kickout! Commander rolls out of the way and sets the ladder up. He then throws it into the corner and begins to run towards it. Izzie Dead makes a tag to ladder and then Izzie pushes the ladder towards Commander D as he tries to stop knocking him on his back. Izzie helps ladder to the outside before starting to go to work on Commander D. He picks D up and looks to go for a DDT but Commander D flips him onto his back. He falls to the ground near his corner looking to make a tag. He reaches up and tags in Bret Battery. Bret picks up Izzie by the neck and pushes Izzie outside the ring. He starts to taunt to the crowd waiting for him to get back in. Izzie tags in Ima Goner trips over the bottom rope making her way in and slams her head into the mat. Bret drags her to the center of the ring and tags in the Women Droid. She goes to the center of the ring and makes the pin 1 2 3 The camera rolls over to the announce table to show Carlos Castillo and Adam Calloway. Adam shuffles his papers as Carlos Castillo is on the small lab top on the announce table. Adam Calloway || Netherlands Native Whatta great match that was, ey Carlos? Carlos, closed the screen of the labtop and looked into the camera. He had a scrappy beard and wore a suit. Carlos Castillo || Hispanic Heritage It wasn't a 'great' match, but it was enjoyable. They could have done so much better, and if they want to get far in this business, they better improve on some of their in-ring techniques. Adam rolled his eyes. Adam Calloway || Netherlands Native Well, in my opinion and everyone else in the arena, it was a great match! Last week, we saw a debut of a new wrestler. No, he isn't an Affliction superstar, but a Revolt superstar! Let's meet...Billy Spizatoni! As Adam announced it was a Revolt superstar, Carlos snickered as in his opinion Affliction was the superior brand. As both men are looking at the camera, it fades to the opening match last week; Billy Spizatoni vs Jaden Daniels. It then fades out to show Billy Spizatoni, dressed in a suit and shown in a chair. Billy Spizatoni || The Embassador I'm Billy Spizatoni... It then shows a part of the match, where Billy Spizatoni hits one of his more famous moves, a flying forearm. It then fades back to Billy. Billy Spizatoni || The Embassador ...and I'm your next Galveston Island Champion. The camera then shows Spizatoni jumping, and stomping on his, lifeless at the time, opponent, Jaden Daniels. Billy Spizatoni || The Embassador I've wrestled around the world and in countries such as Japan, Canada and England. The one thing that I get described as, no matter where I am is the guy who's too small to make it big. The camera then fades back to the match where Billy is executing his finisher "Face My Innocence" on Jaden Daniels; then back to Billy. Billy Spizatoni || The Embassador I came to GIW for three reasons; One, make money; Two, win championship belts; and three, no matter how cliche it sounds, but prove it doesn't matter about the size of the dog in the fight; but the size of the fight in the dog. The camera then goes back to the match, where Billy is getting the pin and getting his arm raised. You can tell in his eyes that Spizatoni wasn't playing around. Then, back to Billy for his final statements. Billy Spizatoni || The Embassador I'm Billy Spizatoni; you're NEXT Galveston Island Champion. Spizatoni looks straight into the camera, showing the 'I'm not playing around' eyes. The camera then fades to black, as Carlos Castillo and Adam Calloway are now seen again. Adam Calloway || Netherlands Native Man, that kid is gonna be huge! Castillo once again snickers. Carlos Castillo || Hispanic Heritage Well, you and I both know this, as he is twenty-seven years old, he will never be 'huge.' Carlos lets out a laugh, as Adam looks at him in an awkward way. Adam Calloway || Netherlands Native I think last week, he proved to be getting to the level he wants to be at here in GIW. Now, let's get back to the superior show...Revolt! We head back to the ring where three men are already in there Stoner the fan favorite smiling to the crowd, Rich Carwin stretching out his legs, and Sideshow Banks talks trash to the fans. However soon the camera cuts to the entrance where "Invincible" by Adelitas Way echoes out and from the back comes Jack Johnson with C-22 who has a briefcase, he has a big smirk on his face. Adam Calloway: Here comes the last competitor of the bunch, Jack Johnson! Carlos Castillo: This guy is the real deal; money, talent, and MORE MONEY! Adam Calloway: This match is for a North Beach title shot, so you know it’ll be good. At this time he throws up his arms and then tosses them down as two cannons shoot money into the crowd bringing some cheers from the fans. He heads into the ring and the four men all stare at each other as the referee calls for the bell. DING DING DING! Right away Jack steps up in front of the three throwing up his arms as they all stop before starting the match. Johnson asks for the briefcase and props it open, money as you’d expect. Jack smiles and pulls out a few rubber band bits of cash holding it out to Carwin. However Richard slaps it away out of the ring not wanting to fall prey to this. Jack shakes his head then turns to Sideshow and gives him the same offer telling him that they’ll take out Carwin, Banks smirks and nods shaking Jack’s hand. The fans boo as Johnson now turns to Stoner pulling out another few handfuls to Stoner. The fans wait as Stoner grabs them and then jumps out of the ring, picking up the previously tossed out money. He immediately bails to the back as the fans laugh it off. Jack tosses the money out of the ring to C-22 and Sideshow and Jack now corner Richard. Richard punches Jack and then Banks trying to battle out but the two take him down. Johnson backs away and lets Sideshow start to kick him. He delivers a beating to him, pounding away at him. Jack tells him to get him to his feet. Banks tosses him into Johnson who turns it right into a Cashbomb! The fans boo as Carwin lays lifeless on the mat as Banks turns to Johnson with a smile. Jack holds out his hand for a shake but as Banks goes for it Johnson turns it right into a Cashmission, A Half Nelson Choke and Leglock! The fans are a bit stunned as Banks rolls around in pain before submitting to Johnson, the fans boo immediately. WINNER: Jack Johnson Carlos Castillo: Might not be technical, but it’s a win none the less. Jack leans over Carwin and spits in his face, ultimate sign of disrespect. Jack picks out a hundred dollar bill from the money he was supposed to be giving Banks but instead he blows his nose into it and then sets it on top of Banks head. Jack motions to the back and C-22 follows, Johnson having won the shot in convincing fashion. Mother Fucker Of The Year by Motley Crue hit’s the P.A. System and out walks the Galveston Island Champion and member of Revolt Scott Rydell. He lifts the belt high in the air and walks down laughing and smirking at the people in the audience. He takes a microphone from the ring announcer. It’s ok I can handle it from here. Now before I begin, may I ask why each and everyone of you people here wasted your money coming to an Affliction show? I mean wouldn’t you rather spend your hard earned cowboy money on seeing something worth your while? Something you can tell your children and their children? I mean sure we got people like Jake Kennedy running around or Billy Spitzandswallows. And I suppose it’s hard to justify watching Johnny Stylez with the way the economy is running these days. Yet you come here to Affliction, where the top title doesn’t mean jack shit. Where people like Acquin run around, hell The Grateful Dead is all on this brand! Oh and don’t even get me started on the big guns like Damon Synn ohhhh! Listen closely you dumb hicks, watch Revolt it has guys like me on it. Champions you can be proud of, it has I don’t know I kind of covered it… wait! It has Aspen Chaud that’s right maybe you can catch some side boob action every Thursday night only on GIW Revolt! So go a head spend your time watching zombies, ladders, and robots oh my! While everyone else knows that Revolt is where the stars are. Revolt is where the big dogs of GIW roam free. Hell Revolt has me on it right there is evidence enough that you need to come to that show before you do this Affliction shit. So go right a head, stand up leave this place wait until Thursday, come to the Harbor Inn, give a ticket to Hank at the counter. And watch Wake Walker and myself throw down. Besides I mean it’s not like you hillbillies are all that far from home anyway that it burn to much gas. Go a head leave it’ll only take one New York Minute. The match starts off with all three men in the ring. Immediately, both Austin Acquin and Marcus Fisk begin to attack Paul Defias. It is unexplained why they would even pick on him to begin with but they do so anyway. They absolutely murder Defias for a few minutes until Defias is out cold. Austin Acquin grabs Paul Defias and grounds him and agrees with Marcus Fisk to do a special move. Marcus Fisk climbs the ropes and stomps on Defias' head. Defias struggles and screams as blood gushes from his mouth. Acquin tells Marcus Fisk to do it again. Defias begins to throw up massive quantities of blood. Marcus Fisk does it again. Defias' body goes completely frozen. The E.M.T's are called for help. It is now between Acquin and Fisk! 2:38 has gone by. The two battle it out for a few minutes until it's 6:03. Austin Acquin eventually rolls Fisk up when he is dazed. 1...2.. kick out! Fisk ets up quickly and tries to battle back but Austin begins a clinic here! He does all sorts of series of pins, some that we have not even seen before until finally, Acquin gets the pin at 6:47. Winner - Acquin Door opens. Its know other than Affliction superstar Ryan Daniels. Ryan is wearing a hoodie with a green DC hat and shirt. Ryan has a huge smile on his face and walks over to the camera man. //The DC king//Ryan Daniels Listen, since I signed my contract here I’ve been being a nice guy to everyone, and I got no respect. No one respects Ryan Daniels. And tonight I’m in a Triple Threat Match against Damu Akpu-nku and. Damon Synn and the winner will move one step closer to the Affliction title, Now I don’t know too much about these guys. Damu the only thing I like about you is your name. And Damon I don’t know about you. Ill see both of them in the ring and I am the underdog. But you know what. I like it. I like being the person people don’t know about. Tonight everyone will see the true DC King. Tonight I will gain my respect from all of the fans. And all of the superstars on the roster. I don’t play games. I am Ryan Daniels, I don’t lay down for no body, I will leave here tonight a winner and one step closer on become the Affliction champion. Im the man with the greatest talent in the world, Im the DC king. I am the future of this company and tonight everyone will find out I am the next big thing. I am Ryan Daniels, Ryan looks into the camera as it fades away. For a lot of people the best part of the show is here right now as in the ring we have five of Affliction’s lovely divas. On one side we have Trishta Michelle and Demonia Darc talking, both talking a bit of trash. Trishelle Jordan is smiling out to the crowd happily while Alexii Raynova pounds her first and T'ai Monique looks pretty in the corner. All five are ready to go already. Carlos Castillo: Can we go ahead and get this crap over with? Adam Calloway: Come on these girls are lovely, enjoy it. All five girls start to bicker a bit though Darc and Michelle seem to have made a bond in the corner. We are prepared to start the match as the referee calls for the bell, five girls but only one can win. DING DING DING! Right away we are set up and Darc and Trishta agree to go after Alexii in the corner while Trishelle tries to make friends out of T’ai on the other side. Alexii seems to be the superior diva though as she starts to battle out of the corner first punching Trishta then getting Darc as well. The fans are a bit impressed as she starts to go off. First with an elbow to Trishta then a kick to Darc. There plan to double team her has seemed to have failed as she grabs the currently bent over Darc and tosses her right into Michelle’s mid-section. Both girls fall into a bit of a heap as Alexii stands over them triumphantly, before pulling Darc off and lifting Trishta to her feet and crushing her with a big haymaker. On the other side of the ring Trishelle has seemingly made buddy with T’ai. She proposes a double high five but T’ai instead slaps her across the face and tosses her right out of the ring! The fans boo her meaness as she follows Trishelle out of the ring staying on the attack. In the ring Alexii seems to continue her beating as she lifts up Darc but is caught with a big kick up that sends her right out of the ring. Darc gets to her feet and holds her face from the beating she took earlier. Trishta gets to her feet now as well seeing Alexii on the outside recovering she smiles and pats Darc on the back. However there teaming proves to be just a bit of a ploy by Darc as she proposes they hug. Demonia however starts to bite Trishta! The fans are a bit stunned as Trishta pries herself away, looking at the bite stunned. Demonia spins around and connects with a brutal Axe Handle to the off guard Trishta, she goes for the cover 1…2…Kick Out thanks to Alexii. Adam Calloway: Crazy Demonia Darc nearly got the win there. Darc gets up but runs right into a clothesline from Alexii. Next up is Trishta who seems stunned by the events of what just happened. She tries to plead with the referee but when she turns she walks right into a Jawbreaker Lariat a Pendulum Short ranged Lariat from Alexii! Trishta is down and is certainly out after that one. Darc is now getting to her feet on the other side while T’ai has thrown Trishelle back into the ring. As Darc gets up Alexii goes for a Hooligan Kick an Arched Big Boot! But Darc ducks it and Alexii instead crushes the referee! The fans are a bit stunned as Darc kicks Alexii from behind then runs off the ropes for another move but she runs right into a move Alexii calls Ignorance a Pumphandle Piledriver! The fans are stunned as Darc falls out of the ring and Alexii crawls to cover Trishta. On the other side of the ring however T’ai has just hit a Monique's Moment a Springboard Running Facebuster! Wow both girls have the win here, as T’ai covers Trishelle and Alexii covers Trishta! The first referee has recovered and sees T’ai’s cover while the referee just ran out! 1…2…3! Wait, who just won this one?! WINNER(S): Alexii Ravynova & T'ai Monique Both referee’s get up and hold there victors hands up. One referee holds up T’ai’s hand up and then other holds up Alexii’s. The fans are just as confused as anyone as the referee’s begin to bicker on who just won. Adam Calloway: Well both won, I mean the replay shows it was the same time. I guess we will have to wait who is the dominant diva on the brand until next week. As the referee’s argue at the back of the ring T’ai and Alexii meet up in the center of the ring, staring at each other before Alexii shoves her and heads out of the ring. The fans boo the result they wanted to see a winner just like us. The crowd can be seen waiting for something to happen .Suddenly, a wind can be heard blowing. A man can be heard speaking in a foreign voice: 死のための時間は、内の多くの死のための時間に到着した(GIW)来る。私は最高に値するので、私は、最高のピンポイントしています。私は正直な人がいます私は私の国で最も偉大な戦闘機です場合と同様、この愚か者をさせてください。カウントダウンが始まる... INCHIRO HUEDA のみましょう。 All of the fans end up looking confused as they hear this mysterious foreign man speaking once again. Then a symbol appears for a few seconds [pic] Each and every participant come out to their very own theme song. Damon Synn is recognized by many of the fans and is given a pretty hefty cheer. They know him well so they seem to want to boo him however he cockily heads to the ring, not giving a damn about them however. Adam Calloway: I may not know much about Damon Synn, but from what I hear he is very talented. Carlos Castillo: This guy is great trust me Adam, the guy is amazing! Damu is out next and he is booed heavily by the audience. Immediately as soon as he steps into the arena, they begin their usual, "USA! USA! USA!" chants disrespecting his home country. He walks by them with little care as out next is the unknown Ryan Daniels who is given almost no reaction whatsoever. The match begins. Carlos Castillo: This could be to crown our new champion; it’s going to be crazy. Immediately, Synn mauls Ryan and Damu down with his entire body. Damu appears to be surprised and scared. Synn goes after him. Damu quickly exits the ring and runs away from Synn. Synn turns towards Ryan Daniels. Ryan Daniels punches Synn in the face. Synn shakes his head at Ryan and is about to attack before Damu comes from behind and hits Synn with a clubbing blow. Synn turns around. Damu and Ryan both try to attack Synn but it's like punching a brick wall; it's absolutely useless! Synn takes hits like nothing and walks to the turnbuckle as he's being hit. Damu kicks Synn in the gut and forces Synn's body to take the position of a DDT. Damu hits it! Adam Calloway: What a move there by Damu, could he be our new champion. Carlos Castillo: Not if Synn has anything to do with it. However, it only takes Synn 1 second to be back on his feet again. Synn towers over both men, even Damu! Damu exits the ring again and Ryan Daniels is grabbed by the head. Ryan begs for mercy but Synn has none of it. Synn picks up Daniels and hits his Synn Spin which is essentially a spinning tombstone piled driver with a gorilla press before hand. Ryan looks like he died. Synn pins Ryan as Damu looks on in horror and shock of this man's strength. 1...2..3. Forget about it. The clock wasn't even a factor in this match. The match lasted 1:05. Carlos Castillo: Yes he did it, Damon Synn is our new Affliction Champion! Adam Calloway: We may not all like him, but the fact is he is our new champion, wow. Synn raises his arms up high and attacks the referee soon after. Damu has sympathy and carries Ryan to the back. Finally Synn gets a hold of his belt and holds it up to his chest, grinning down at as we are ushered into a new era here in Galveston, Damon Synn now runs Affliction. The scene opens up to Brooke Ellis standing by. She smiles at the camera and continues to munch on her pretzels. She quickly wipes some crumbs off of her face and raises the microphone to her mouth in a nonchalant manor. INTERVIEWER | BROOKE ELLIS “Well hello Affliction fans, I would like you all to welcome my guest at this time, the two-ton Demonia Darc.” The camera pans out, showing a very pale Demonia looking strangely at the plus-sized interviewer. INTERVIEWER | BROOKE ELLIS “Okay... So, I’ve been asked by our GM, Amanda Rowland, to conduct an interview as soon as you were found... Could you tell us why you were not there at your match, tonight?” COME TO THE DARC SIDE | DEMONIA “Well, Brooke, I had better things to be doing... I could not, I repeat not, have gone out there without having a feast beforehand. I was weak and would’ve been thrown around like my voodoo doll.” Brooke raises an eyebrow and quickly shoots a glance at the remainder of her pretzels. She tries to ignore them. INTERVIEWER | BROOKE ELLIS “So, what exactly did you do? Did you eat? By the looks of it, you did...” COME TO THE DARC SIDE | DEMONIA “I did not eat, so-to-speak... I drank. I am on a new diet that only allows me to drink the blood of four legged animals... Sheep and cows are just examples... You see, Brooke, being a blood-thirsty member of the night is not as great as everyone makes it out to be... I mean; there’s the no sleeping, constant desire to rip the throat of the people around you and suck their blood, and the list goes on. Like I said, I would’ve been demolished if I didn’t stock up on- AH!” The camera moves, revealing Brooke reflecting a mirror on Demonia. She hisses as the light reflects onto her skin. Demonia quickly knocks the mirror out of Brooke’s hand and yells at her in an unknown language. The two begin to grapple, hitting off of tables and pulling each-others hair. A few moments pass and several security guards arrive, pulling the two very different ladies away from each-other. Demonia growls as Brooke picks up her mirror and storms off. The scene fades to black Scene goes backstage as Megathreat is taping his fingers before his upcoming match with the North Beach Champion Apollo Calix. As he finishes up the last few fingers a loud knock is heard at the door. Megathreat looks to the door and wonders who it is. He then speaks. Megathreat: I got a match coming up. I'm busy. There is a short pause at the door before the man at the door begins to speak again. Jack Johnson: I think you'd be very interested in what I have to say to you Megathreat. Your a smart man and so am I. I think I have something we can work out. Megathreat walks over to the door and opens it up. Johnson is holding a two sacks in his hand he walks in the room and the scene switches to the ring. Backstage where Apollo is beginning to walk towards the ring with the title on his shoulder. You can start to see Megathreat and Johnson walking behind him. Apollo turns around as he feels he is being watched. As he turns Megathreat and Jack Johnson stop in their steps. Apollo shrugs his arms and gives a weird look as he wonders whats going on. Megathreat: Now Apollo Calix, I know that you are North Beach Champion, but we have a offer for you. We can skip this weeks match and we'll never have to face off. You won't have to wrestle this week and you'll still get paid. You'll also get a bonus sum of money that I have in this sack here. Take a look. Megathreat slides the sack across the ground to Calix. He opens the bag and his eyes widen by the number of bills in the bag. He brings a smile to his face but then looks to Jack standing next to Threat. Apollo Calix: What is Johnson here for? What does he have to do with this? Megathreat: Well as you can see the sum of the cash in that bag is far more than what you'd make in a few years worth. You could take off and live the high life then come back if you wanted with that kind of money. Johnson wants one thing, your North Beach Title. Apollo Calix: So the cash for the title? Jack Johnson: Yes. Apollo Calix: You have a deal. The scene fades to black as the fans begin to boo Apollo and the other two men.
