GIW — Card

Revolt, 5 December 2012

5 December 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: WEDNESDAY NIGHT REVOLT LIVE! At the Courville Stadium – December 5, 2012 on GIW.com Back here at the Courville Stadium we go with the fans going crazy as usual for the show at hand, last week was big but it’s always going to get bigger from here as the Race to Five continues. We head to the backstage area right off the bat as the show starts with Ace Baldwin standing by with Zachary Pagonis, who currently holds a microphone. ZACHARY PAGONIS: “I’m standing by with Ace Baldwin, who in the return Revolt beat two stars to win Revolt’s first main event. Things now go forward as you face Jeremy Baltimore, with the winner heading to two wins. Your thoughts?” Pagonis holds up the microphone to Baldwin, who has a grin on his face ready to answer the question quickly. ACE BALDWIN: “You know it feels good to get the first one out of the way, and tonight against Baltimore I’ll make it two. I know that five isn’t going to be easy, but I’ll get there and I’ll become the new--” But before he can finish his sentence Baldwin is cut off by the unexpected appearance of Ethan Wrathmore entering into the side of the screen, grabbing Zachary and tossing him out of the screen speaking up as his manager Rayne stands behind him. ETHAN WRATHMORE: “No…You won’t. I know I am new here and nobody knows much about me. But you are the past Ace, you had your chance. Now it’s mine, last week I showed everybody how dominant I could be. This week I will do the same. Everybody keeps looking at you as the favorite but you aren’t, I am. So to show everyone who the real favorite is, I’m calling you out whether you win or lose against Baltimore…Me and you next week.” Baldwin has a look over at Rayne for a moment then back at Wrathmore, shaking his head at the new guy’s brashness. ACE BALDWIN: “Fine, you are on.” Baldwin holds out his hand to Wrathmore for a hands shake but he shakes his head and walks off as Ace offers it up to Rayne instead as she rolls her eyes and walks off with Ethan, as Ace watches them go as we head from there back to ringside. We get a view of the ring inside the stadium as the camera slowly pans around the fans on the barricade finally reaching the always familiar faces of Riley and Oliver down at ringside. RILEY STEVENS: “Well there we have it, before the show even gets going we already have a match for next week booked. Talk about a way to kick off the show, let’s get you recapped on what happened last week with the standings.” As he says this the viewers at home our shown a graphic on the screen displaying the wins from last week. RILEY STEVENS: “Ace Baldwin, Alexis Terry, Ethan Wrathmore, Jeremy Baltimore, and Wake Walker all have won and all will be in action tonight looking to pull ahead as four of them will be in competition against one another, ensuring that at least two will leave with two wins tonight. Big news Oliver.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Yup…” RILEY STEVENS: “Let’s get this show on the road ladies and gentleman…” QUOTE As he says this the camera slowly pans over to the ring where we are shown a jam packed ring full of several stars, as we go around them they are Alexandria Espoir, Attack Attack, Brody Harper, Cash Everett, Daniel Starr, Eliza Terry, Izzy Lancaster, Jack Redd, and Kyle Karnage. This ring is full of people ready to go. RILEY STEVENS: “This ring is full tonight Oliver, any favorites?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “The one with the belt.” As he says this we see Daniel Starr in the middle of the pile holding up his title belt high in the air for all the others to see, some glaring at him others at the belt as he lowers it down and hands it out of the ring as the referee calls for the bell. DING DING DING! Right off the bat all hell breaks loose as Jack Redd goes to battle with Attack Attack and Cash Everett and Kyle Karnage meet up in a different corner as well, while the champion himself Daniel Starr finds himself a corner to hide in to start this one. As usual the ladies flock to Brody Harper as he is attacked by Alexandria, Eliza, and Izzy. All three try to lay into them only for Brody to head butt each of them individually one after the other. Channeling his buddy Nelson Fielder he throws Izzy over his shoulder out of the ring first, then soon sends Alexandria out with her as Eliza woozy from the Headbutt stumbles into him as well met by the snakelike kiss of Brody before getting tossed out on her ass as well, three quick eliminations by Brody Harper. Alexandria Espoir, Eliza Terry, & Izzy Lancaster are Eliminated These fans go wild for Brody as he send all three women packing as he picks his next target going after Starr in the corner meeting him with a clothesline that rocks him. In the ring the battles between Jack and Attack as well as Kyle and Cash continue finally ending with Kyle hitting a Big Boot on Cash sending him back into the ropes where he is sent over crashing to the mat hard by a gigantic clothesline from Karnage, something he quickly gloats to the crowd about. Cash Everett is Eliminated Kyle breaks up the taunting to go after Brody attacking him from behind while Harper attempts to toss Starr over the top, saving the champion in the process. Starr slides his way back into the other corner as Kyle punches on Brody while Redd grabs a hold of Attack around the throat and hoists him up for a Double Handed Chokebomb sending him out of the ring to the ground to the sound of a loud thud. Attack Attack is Eliminated With only four men remaining things have thinned out pretty quickly as Brody fights his way out of the corner with shots to Kyle finally clotheslining him away only for Redd to drop him with a Running Big Boot that floors Harper quickly. All of Jack’s moves seeming to come with a little extra power then most people’s as Starr gets up in the corner of the ring trying to plea with Redd to go after one of the other’s as Jack realizes what eliminating the champion would do for him. He is about to go on the attack when Karnage slips behind Starr grabbing him and spinning him around tossing him over the ropes in the process eliminating him, himself. Daniel Starr is Eliminated Redd angry at him for taking the eliminations heads after Karnage but as he goes for a hugeeee Clothesline on Kyle, he ducks it and the momentum carries through to Harper on the ropes sending him flying over the top to the outside. Brody Harper is Eliminated Hoping to catch Redd off guard Karnage now goes for the same move only jumps up to clothesline him only to be caught set back on the ground then tossed over the ropes by Redd to the outside getting him the victory. REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner…JACK REDD!” These fans go wild seemingly impressed by the new star as Karnage gets up immediately slamming his hands on the outside angry about the loss while on the other side Starr as usual has found something to complain to the referee about something. RILEY STEVENS: “Impressive win here by Jack Redd tonight, gets his career started in the right direction and even gets himself four wins away from a title.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Karnage had that…” Redd looks around the ring as Karnage seems to think the same thing beside himself that he won, as we head to the back. WINNER: JACK REDD Backstage we go with Aspen Chaud at her desk again with her unneeded just for show glasses on looking down at paper work in her very business attire at her desk with Ryan Cavallari pacing around in front of the desk. Finally he seems to snap off at her. RYAN CAVALLARI: “I’m sick of this, you haven’t said a word to me the last few weeks. You’re always busy with all this stuff, half the time you aren’t even working. Yet you treat me like a piece of dirt, maybe you can’t handle being in a relationship and doing this job at the same time.” Ryan says it, finally unable to hold back his thoughts on the situation as Aspen looks up at him looking a little shocked that he would say something like that as she gets up to her feet walking around the desk over to him getting close to him, nearly lip to lip before pulling away. ASPEN CHAUD: “That’s probably true, so instead of calling me your girlfriend you can call me Boss and you should probably get ready for your match.” Aspen points to the door as Ryan glares down at her, his emotions hidden as usual as perhaps the stress of the job has gotten to Aspen as she has just broken it off with him, Ryan shakes his head at her and walks out the doors the door closing harshly as she pushes up her glasses a bit as we head to the ring. QUOTE RILEY STEVENS: “We all thought that Ryan Cavallari was going to get special treatment as Aspen’s boyfriend but apparently the new role tore them apart, Ryspen is no more and he’s got no time to think because his match is next.” As he says that the lights go dark as an ‘A’ appears on the floor as the fans mostly respond with excitement, whether they hate him or love him Alioth Starre always elicits a response. The lights return just light enough to show Alioth Starre making his approach to the ring to the sound of “I'm Afraid of Americans” by David Bowie as the fans now start to boo him on his way out as he heads up the steps walking along the ropes then stepping through them into the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “When it comes to GIW greats; Alioth Starre is definitely in the discussion for best ever but last week that message to Wake Walker? What do you think that was about Oliver?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “He probably doesn’t like him Riley and I can’t blame him, I don’t like this new Walker either.” As he steps into the ring he takes his place in the corner as Ryan Cavallari’s music hits only for a long drawn out pause as the fans wait to see what’s happening. His music kicks up again but yet again nothing as the referee looks over at Alioth, then back at the entrance ramp finally after the third reboot the music fades and the referee has no choice but to start the match. DING DING DING! The referee looks up at the ramp with some of the other members of the crowd hoping Ryan will come running out but he’s forced to start the count 1…2…3…4…As it hit’s four the fans begin to come to grips with the fact that Cavallari has chosen to leave after what transpired earlier as Alioth stands in the ring not seeming to show any care about the fact he’s gone…5…6…7…8…9…One last hope?...Nope…10! REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner by count out…ALIOTH STARRE!” Starre stands in the ring as the referee goes to grab his arm to raise it but he pushes him away heading to the ropes sliding his way out not seeming really happy or mad about the win, just looking like it’s another day. RILEY STEVENS: “It’s clear Ryan has left while Alioth may have the easiest win in the Race to Five yet with this one. But I think I speak for everyone when I say this only builds anticipation for his next match.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “That’s one way to keep fresh.” Starre heads to the back the fans booing the way he went about getting his first win the entire time, though for him it may be the best thing to happen to him. WINNER: ALIOTH STARRE To the back we go with Daniel Starr fuming with his belt over his shoulder screaming at the referee who was on the outside of the ring for the Open Battle Royal match earlier. DANIEL STARR: “You saw it yourself! I didn’t ever have my other foot down, I WASN’T ELIMINATED! I demand you get that match’s decision overturned.” Starr continues to poke at him as Zachary Pagonis enters into the screen having dusted himself off from the earlier scuffle again wielding his mic and again ready to deliver bad news to someone. ZACHARY PAGONIS: “Hi Daniel, I’ve been sent here to tell you that live here on Revolt next week you will be defending your belt against four other superstars in a five pack challenge! What are your thoughts on this huge match?” Starr seems caught off guard by the news nearly for the first time seeming at a loss for words but he manages to find them quickly enough to scream at Pagonis. DANIEL STARR: “FOUR PEOPLE? You have got to be kidding me, I deserve better then this! I AM THE CHAMPION!” Starr walks off in a huff as Pagonis shakes his head, probably relieved that was all he had to hear as we again return to the ring for the next contest here on Revolt. QUOTE It’s time for the next match and already standing inside of the ring is none other then Joshua Acquin with his brother Austin Acquin at ringside as “Headstrong” by Trapt hits and the fans begin to cheer as the Ninja Warrior himself Jeffrey “Colt” Douglas heads out from the back with a grin on his face high fiving a few of the fans along the way. RILEY STEVENS: “Both men fell short for wins last week but this week have the chance to really make a statement and get on the board.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Is this Douglas guy for real…like really?” Douglas heads around the ring high fiving near everyone as he heads up into the ring looking across at Acquin nodding his head ready to go as the referee square them off then starts the match. DING DING DING! Acquin as usual wastes no time at all rushing across the ring at Douglas only for him to roll under the clothesline attempt. Joshua looks back at him as usual getting a little heated about the miss heading back in again this time being evaded to the left by Jeffrey who uses a cartwheel to get out of the way. Finally Josh thinks he has him down pretending to go after him stopping to see which way he goes but rather then ducking away Douglas nails him with a Dropkick then hits a Kip-Up right into a karate stance as these fans can’t help but both have a laugh and clap as Acquin gets up to his feet with Austin cheering him on, on the outside. Josh gets up tossing a punch that Colt ducks then spins him over with a Hip Toss to the mat. Giving him only time to get up again Jeffrey hits a Hurricanrana flipping Acquin over to the ropes, where he rolls out trying to recover as Austin game plans with him as Jeffrey grins in the ring waiting for him. RILEY STEVENS: “This guy is putting on a show.” Acquin tries to catch him off guard rolling back into the ring jumping up for something only to be pushed off the ropes. On the rebound Douglas leap frogs him and as soon as Acquin spins around he pops him with a Superkick dropping him down so that he can head up top looking out at the crowd who scream for him as he hits the Diving 450 Seated Senton, looking spot on and landing hard as he hooks the legs 1…2…3! REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner by way of pinfall…JEFFREY DOUGLAS!” These fans are impressed tonight as Douglas came to play for sure, having some fun while getting his first win here in GIW. RILEY STEVENS: “What a performance by Jeffrey Douglas tonight.” Douglas gets his hand raised by the referee to the applause of the crowd as Acquin rolls out of the ring helped out by his brother as suddenly Kyle Karnage rolls into the ring going for a clothesline on Colt only for Jeffrey to duck under it again. Kyle stops himself from hitting the ropes and ducks his way out, as Douglas calls for him to get back in. RILEY STEVENS: “Whoa this new star Kyle Karnage looked to try to make a statement here on Colt from behind but Douglas saw it coming and managed to avoid it, but something tells me these two haven’t seen the last of each other.” Karnage is booed by the crowd as he walks around the ring yelling at them that he doesn’t care pointing at Douglas and saying he got lucky this time as Colt invites him back in but to no avail as we continue the show backstage. WINNER: JEFFREY DOUGLAS To the back we go where we find Alexis Terry seeming way too happy as she leans up against the wall right next to the door that reads ‘Aspen Chaud’. Seeming more then willing to wait, the door finally opens up as Aspen Chaud emerges from it only to find Terry standing there her stressed look quickly turning even angrier as she stares back at Alexis. ASPEN CHAUD: “What do you want?” Chaud never seeming happy to see Terry glares at her in anger, as Alexis pulls her arms out from behind her presenting a box of tissues with a grin. ALEXIS TERRY: “Just wanted to bring you some tissues, sure you are pretty sad after the break up earlier. You know if you need someone to talk to, I am here. It’s always nice to have a friend you can come to…” Terry digs it in at her as Aspen glares back at her waiting for her to be done, as Alexis goes through with the joke holding out the box of tissues waiting for Aspen to take them when finally she angrily smacks them out of her hand, sending them flying. ASPEN CHAUD: “Real funny! And as a matter of fact, I am just fine. I never have and never will need anyone by my side. In fact, generally all they do is ride my coat tails. And you should probably keep those tissues because when you finish the night tapping out you will need them.” Aspen retorts back the tension always in the air when these two are around as Alexis looks over at the tissues laughing. ALEXIS TERRY: “Maybe you can keep them for when you have to put that belt around my waist.” Terry starts to walk off but she is stopped by Aspen placing her hand on her shoulder as Alexis turns back around. ASPEN CHAUD: “You know what I have an idea. If you think you are getting an easy match after the big one you’re having this week, you are wrong. Alioth really didn’t get a challenge this week so you can be his opponent next week. Hope knowing that doesn’t hurt you before your match.” Aspen winks at her behind her fake glasses as Alexis shrugs her hand off her shoulder and walks off to get ready for said match as we get a teaser for the Baltimore and Baldwin match later tonight. QUOTE To the top of the ramp we go right as “New Fury” by Verse hits and Kassandra Kayne comes out from the back, the fans all knowing her very well now as she enters the building having really given them a reason to respect her with her performance last week, even in defeat as she slowly heads down the ramp. RILEY STEVENS: “You got to admire Kassandra after last week, what a performance hanging with a legend like Walker even in her first match, she didn’t really lose but Walker did get the win.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “She still lost, get over it. Don’t care if she didn’t get pinned, you don’t get to five by being left out.” It seems as though Kayne agrees with Oliver on that looking all business as she reaches the ring heading to the corner as “Hit the Lights” by Metallica hits and the fans begin to mostly boo having started to dislike Ethan Wrathmore after he got into a verbal spat with one of there guys Ace Baldwin. However as he walks out with Rayne he doesn’t seem to mind heading down the ramp with her by his side. RILEY STEVENS: “While Wrathmore didn’t have the same high profile competition as Kayne he did get a win and did so in very impressive fashion, even getting him pretty deep into the title talk picture.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “He’s the guy that I am starting to lean towards Riley.” Letting Rayne go around the ring Wrathmore jumps up onto the ring apron then over the ropes into the ring not releasing eye contact with Kayne the entire time as she stares back as the referee has no choice but to start the match before they start it themselves. DING DING DING! This match is underway now with Rayne cheering at ringside for Wrathmore as he and Kayne in the ring start to circle around each other the size heavily in favor of Ethan who is nearly a foot taller. The two of them go in for a lock up; Kayne showing her fearlessness but Wrathmore makes her pay tossing her back down to the mat with a thud. Kassandra gets right back up as Ethan eggs her into another one, wanting to again do the same thing and Kayne fakes like she’s going to go for that tie up but instead ducks under it and goes around him quickly rolling him up 1…Kick Out. Ethan uses his size to force up off her, as the two hop right back up with Kayne up first as she delivers a Discus Elbow Smash to the jaw of Wrathmore as soon as he gets up, rocking him a bit as she pulls him right into another one finally finishing it off by connecting with a Snap DDT down to the mat the fans getting behind her a bit more then Ethan, though neither is really seemingly a fan favorite at this point. RILEY STEVENS: “Solid start by Kayne.” Rather then taking her attempt at the pin, she waits for him to get up knowing full well that she hasn’t done enough for a pin yet. As soon as he gets up she rears up going for a Superkick but with some quick reaction time, Wrathmore catches the kick as its coming holding up her foot in the air. He forces her back as she jumps on one foot to keep her balance, as he shakes his head at her finally tossing her leg out her momentum spinning her around as he grabs a hold of her and smashes her down with a Reverse DDT! These fans just seem into the match more so then picking sides, as Ethan shakes off her previous shots as he doesn’t wait for her to get up as he instead decides to put her down early, grabbing her up then tossing her over into the ropes. He rushes at her going for a Spear into the ropes but she manages to duck out of the way and it sends him flying shoulder first into the Ring Post. The sound can be heard as his shoulder crashes into it as he stumbles out of the corner, and she manages to hit that Superkick after all flooring him to the mat. RILEY STEVENS: “Kayne may have this!” Walking over to the ropes she lines it up looking back at Wrathmore as she grabs a hold of the ropes then jumps up onto them flying backwards for a Springboard Moonsault but as she does this Ethan’s survival instincts kick in and he rolls out of the way quickly sending Kayne falling to the mat hard in the center of the ring, the force sending her back to her feet though the wind seems to be completely taken out of her as she holds her gut trying to get her breath again and when she finally does BAM, Wrathmore hits her with a huge Spear that sends her vertical then down to the mat with a lot of force taking it right back out of her as he hooks the leg 1…2-Kick Out. Even out of breath Kayne manages to kick out as Ethan shakes his head as he gets up shaking off his shoulder, as he pulls her to her feet then slams her back down with a Arm Drag which he follows up immediately with a Standing Moonsault and a pin 1…2-Kick Out. Again he gets two and though the move looked nice, it wasn’t quite enough. RILEY STEVENS: “Nearly had her there.” Wrathmore gets up ready to continue as he calls for her to get up to her feet. She does just that stumbling up to continue fighting as he grabs her looking for ‘The Cut Above the Rest’ a Scoop Legged Stunner but before he can hit it, Kayne elbows her way out and quickly hits the ropes then on the rebound connects with a Sliding Lariat out of no where as the fans are impressed thinking this one’s over 1…2…Kick Out. Ethan is not going to be put away that easily as he gets back up to his feet looking like a sitting duck as she grabs the Wrist Lock only for him to hit her with repeated elbows to break free from her being able to hit her finisher as he spins around grabbing her for a roll up out of no where 1…He gets his hands on the ropes…2…The referee doesn’t see it…3! REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner by way of pinfall …ETHAN WRATHMORE!” These fans immediately start to boo as Wrathmore slides out of the ring right over by Rayne who heads in for a hug as he raises his arm in victory, as Kayne gets back up immediately looking at the referee then pointing at the ropes but he claims he didn’t see it. RILEY STEVENS: “I think Kayne was heading towards a victory but Wrathmore managed to steal this one.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “It doesn’t matter how you win Riley, just that you win. He’s got two, deal with it.” Rayne on the outside raises up Ethan’s hand into the air as the fans toss insults his way for the way he got the victory as Kayne yet again was nearing victory before getting it stolen away from her this week, as she stands in the ring shaking her head and Ethan heads to the back with a grin on his face having just put himself in the lead her in the Race to Five at least for now. WINNER: ETHAN WRATHMORE A video begins to play showing Wake Walker with Zachary Pagonis the GIW Revolt Interviewer hanging out with some children as the screen displays which one it is, as Zachary appears on the screen with a smile. ZACHARY PAGONIS: “You know I had the opportunity with Wake Walker to participate in a charity fundraiser for a orphanage program the last week and we managed to raise a lot of money for these kids.” Pagonis smiles as we see more of him talking with kids while it then shows the orphans alongside Walker surrounding him asking him questions as he answers as its then him on the screen. WAKE WALKER: “Ah, yes, it's been splendid working together with the Galveston Island Orphanage and Pagonis. These children are fighters, the same way we both are, but these children have been through so much it'd be a crime to not consider them little brave warriors. All of the proceeds will go towards the foundation and we've also invited every orphan in this great foundation to our current event on December fifth, 2012.” With that the video shows Zachary high fiving a young kid then the two of them Zachary and Walker standing with the kids as they wave to the camera as the video fades away. As we head back to the backstage area. To the back we go where some wrestlers are gathered around talking as Jeremy Baltimore is standing by with Wake Walker a seemingly unlikely pair to get along. WAKE WALKER: “Good match last week by the way and good luck tonight.” Walker says to him seeming to only be prepared to say that before he walks by but Baltimore answers back stopping him. JEREMY BALTIMORE: “Thanks you to, the way things are going maybe we might be facing off next week. But aren’t you a little worried about Alioth?” Walker has a bit of a chuckle at that statement. WAKE WALKER: “Not at all.” Baltimore shakes his head and then walks by him heading to the ring as Walker stares into the camera as he says, he doesn’t seem worried at all. QUOTE "I Can't Keep Still" by KPM Music hits as Jeremy Baltimore walks out from the back to the applause from the crowd as a graphic shows us how these two stand, both able to get there second win here tonight as Baltimore walks past the fans one in particular holding up a ‘J-Balt’ sign as Baltimore enters the ring looking for win number two as he heads to the top rope posing for the crowd in the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Baltimore skinned the cat last week winning by count out but got the win none the less, a win over Ace would put him at two and not to mention would really up his stalk.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Like ten fold.” Baltimore jumps down and even though the fans like him, they go wild even before the next man’s music hits as "Who Dat" by J-Cole hits and these fans are rocking as Ace Baldwin heads out from the back fresh and ready after his last match where he overcame the odds to get the win, in what was a truly amazing performance. He approaches the ring with a grin high fiving a few fans along the way. RILEY STEVENS: “Ace last week looked so great in his victory over Attack Attack and Ryan Cavallari and that ending may be one of the best ever in GIW.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Yeah, yeah it was one match.” Baldwin walks along the side of the ring then jumps up then through the ropes tipping his head to Baltimore as he heads up to the same rope Baltimore was just on and raising his hand getting a much bigger response, there is no secret who the bigger star so far in GIW is as the referee gets them set and ready as we start. DING DING DING! These two are in the ring ready to go knowing that at least one of them will put himself only three wins away from the title with a victory as the two circle around each other, the crowd mostly for Ace though a small contingency is going with Baltimore as the two lock up to start this thing off. The two battle back and forth, both really imposing physical specimens. Baltimore seems to have him beat in the tie up starting to force him back a bit until Ace shows his always amazing ring savvy-ness spinning around him into a Wrist Lock from there he turns and tosses him off the ropes. On the rebound Baltimore picks up steam ducking under Ace’s clothesline attempt and rebounding into a Clothesline attempt of his own. Once down he waits for Ace to bounce back up and hoists him up onto his shoulders then back down into a Samoan Drop! These fans are getting into it now, surprised by Balt’s early momentum as he calls for Ace to get up, all business tonight it seems for Jeremy. RILEY STEVENS: “Baltimore looking to emerge as a real contender in this one.” Baldwin hops up to his feet again as Jeremy grabs a hold of him looking for a Suplex but Ace hooks the leg and manages to lift up Baltimore himself dropping him back with a Suplex. Not letting go he hoists himself back up with Jeremy then delivers the second Suplex! These fans are waiting for it, counting them off as he hits them as they wait as he brings him back up and as soon as he switches positions and sends him over for a third this time a Fisherman’s Suplex they scream out three as the referee counts 1…2-Kick Out. Though he kicked out Jeremy’s back has to be killing him as he gets back up to his feet, Ace standing in wait doubling him over with a boot to the gut followed quickly as he jumps off the ropes going for a Springboard Heel Kick but just as soon as Baltimore raises his head he ducks the kick just as quickly. Ace does manage to land on his feet, but with Jeremy behind him as he wraps his arms around him turning around to give himself room then landing a German Suplex! RILEY STEVENS: “Ace’s momentum is killed, just like that.” Baldwin gets up holding his back now as Jeremy has returned the favor but as soon as he gets up he gets hit by a ‘Tiger Whip’ a Spinning Heel Kick that rocks Ace and you can tell he might be seeing stars as he seems to be shaken up a bit. While he’s rocked and still standing Baltimore grabs him and tosses him into the ropes rushing at him nailing him with a clothesline. With Ace in position, Baltimore goes in for the kill hoisting him up onto the top rope as he follows him up. Baldwin tries to fight out but a head butt from Jeremy puts him back out as Jeremy grabs him and then jumps back with a Superplex that shakes the ring and sends the fans into a frenzy as both men roll around clutching at their backs. The two are down for a bit now, both having taken a lot of damage to the back but using the ropes it’s Jeremy that is able to get up first. Stumbling over to Ace he grabs him and then props him in between his legs, looking out at the crowd as he prepares for the ‘X-Factor’ a Sit-Out Powerbomb! The fans are on there feet waiting when Ace manages to slip out and roll it up trying to steal it 1…2-Baltimore kicks out and hops right back up, as they both do. But when they get up Baltimore rushes at him with a Spear! That’s what you get for trying to steal it Ace, a Spear! RILEY STEVENS: “Baltimore may just have this one in the bag!” Ace is down and is groggily getting up to his feet now as Baltimore again goes in for the kill but even groggy Baldwin still knows where he is at and even though he turns into Baltimore he still manages to lift him up and spin him with ‘The Last Blast’ a Spinning Spine Buster that shakes the ring! These fans go wild as Ace gets up stumbling to his feet as he does looking to end this one right here and right now as Baltimore who has really been in the driver’s seat the entire match now looks to try to recover getting up near the ropes. Ace again goes for it this time connecting cleanly with the Springboard Heel Kick. Baltimore manages to stay on his feet but is dazed while doing it as Ace hoists him up regardless hitting him with the ‘Thanks for Coming’ the T.F.C. a Running Death Valley Driver with a Cradle! These fans count alongside with the referee 1…2…3! It’s over. REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner by way of pinfall…ACE BALDWIN!” The fans go wild in the building as Baldwin picks up another win here tonight, he slowly gets up to his feet after the victory Baltimore holding his back in pain rolling under the ropes as the referee raises Ace’s hand up into the air in victory. RILEY STEVENS: “What a victory here tonight ladies and gentleman! It was a close match that Baltimore really did shine in but in the end Ace just finds a way to win.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “That won’t be happening next week Riley.” Baldwin looks over at Jeremy as he exits the ring giving him a nod acknowledging how well he did though Jeremy does seem a bit upset that he let that one slip through his hands, as he heads to the back as Ace holds up two fingers in the ring then motions for the belt around his waist a site these fans seem to think would be great as we get ready for the Main Event. WINNER: ACE BALDWIN After a short teaser for the night’s main event we are shown a split screen, of the two main event combatants warming up. Alexis stretching out one leg having it up against a railing while Walker meditates. Out of no where there is a loud bang heard as on the Terry side of the screen, a attacker unseen for more then a second is seen cracking a big red pipe on the knee and leg that was extended up on the railing for Terry. Someone can be heard screaming for help as Ace Baldwin rushes over to Terry’s side as we are quickly taken to a break featuring some commercials hyping up the Race to Five match and the matches already booked for next week such as Ethan Wrathmore and Ace Baldwin for ‘Three Wins’ as well as the announcement of Jack Redd, Jeffrey Douglas, Kassandra Kayne, and Kyle Karnage as the stars that will challenge Daniel Starr for the North Beach Championship. QUOTE After that trailer is shown with the matches for next week shown off we return to ringside area where a worried Riley Stevens and a calm Oliver Thibodaux sits at ringside. RILEY STEVENS: “We have no idea what just happened backstage, but apparently someone with a pipe has just attacked Alexis Terry…Where that leaves the main event we do not know…” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Screw it, count her out.” RILEY STEVENS: “Ryan left the arena Oliver, Alexis was just attacked from behind the situations are totally—wait I am being told that Alexis is making her way to the ring now and ids going to try to wrestle…We have no idea how hurt she is…” “Dazed and Confused” by Led Zeppelin hits as these fans react with cheers, though it isn’t quite as deafening as when he made a surprise return last week you can tell these fans are still stoked to see the returning legend. Wake Walker walks out from the back making his usual calm and collected walk to the ring, the crowd patting him on the back still not taking him out of his zen-like state as he strolls up the steps and into the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “I don’t want to speculate but there is no secret Aspen hates Alexis, she could have hired nearly anyone to do that.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “You are going to call out our Boss like that Riley? Are you…Stupid?” Walker heads over to the other side of the ring as "Clarity" By Zedd hits and the fans here go wild again as Alexis Terry heads out from the back as well however she does so with a heavy limp in her step as she heads towards the ring. Her effort only bringing more applause from the crowd in this one. RILEY STEVENS: “I’m not sure this is smart but Alexis is trying to make her way down here. And the worst part is, this is a Submission match.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Good, let her get beaten.” Terry heads up the steps then through the ropes really having to rely on them to do so, as she looks across at Walker favoring her knee hard as even Wake doesn’t seem to really want to have to do this as the referee comes to ask her if she can go but before he can finish, Alexis tells him to start the match which he does reluctantly. DING DING DING! Alexis limps to the middle of the ring as Walker tries to talk her into doing this next week, saying that it would be better but Alexis points to the crowd who came to see them to ensure him that it’s the right thing to do but a reluctant Wake showing that he really has turned over a new leaf, at least enough to not want to prey on an injured foe. However one big slap from Terry followed by a big punch is enough to get him going as he grabs her and whips her off the ropes. To everyone’s surprise she’s able to throw a clothesline but Walker ducks it and wraps up around her waist and slams her back with a German Suplex. Wake grabs her around the neck with a chokehold, telling her to tap out as he locks it in but as she starts to get up not tapping he tries to lock it in harder. Alexis manages to pull herself up and get her hands on the ropes forcing a break as Walker steps back, as Terry hits the ropes trying to unleash something only to trip on her bad knee right into position between Walker’s legs as he almost instinctively goes to hoist her up for the ‘Lethal Injection’ but showing some restraint he spins her around grabbing her knee and dropping into a knee lock, twisting it causing her to tap right away as he releases it moments later. REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner by way of submission…WAKE WALKER!” These fans don’t like the way it turned out but applaud Walker for showing some respect while also cheering Terry’s heart. Walker gets his second win regardless, as he grabs her hand and helps her to her feet. RILEY STEVENS: “Walker gets a second win, but you have to admire Terry for trying to put on a show. We really have to get to the bottom of who attacked her---” Almost on cue the crowd collectively boos as sliding into the ring behind them is Alioth Starre wielding that same red pipe from earlier smashing it across the back of the head of Walker sending him out of the ring in the process as without Wake propping her up; Terry falls back into the mat. Stalking her downed Alexis tries to back away but can’t as Starre stomps her knee. RILEY STEVENS: “It was Alioth!” As Terry holds her knee Alioth quickly wraps up into the ‘Four Star Mary’ a Figure Four Leglock tearing on that already hurt knee as these fans boo relentlessly as Alioth tugs at it until he finally releases the hold as Walker heads back into the ring; Alioth quickly slides out the other side Walker just moments to late. RILEY STEVENS: “What in the world is this, Alioth just used Alexis to send a message to Wake Walker and that message had to be received loud and clear.” The boo’s rain down hard on Alioth as he stands on the ramp looking into the ring as Terry in huge pain grasping at her knee as the referee tries to check on her as Walker stares up at Starre as it appears these two have some real issues that are waiting to be solved as tonight Alioth leaves making the biggest impact of all. WINNER: WAKE WALKER
