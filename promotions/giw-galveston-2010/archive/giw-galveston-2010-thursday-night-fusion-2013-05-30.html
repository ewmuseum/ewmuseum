Thursday Night Fusion, 30 May 2013

30 May 2013

GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: THURSDAY NIGHT FUSION LIVE! At San Luis Hotel & Resort – May 30, 2013 QUOTE The open battle royal. It was a neat little way new GIW stars and old stars that wanted to get rid of ring rust came in and showed everyone what they had. Currently in the ring stood Saito Taguchi, Nathan Starr, Gabriel Lauren, and Airica Linn. Right off the bat Airica and Nathan were plotting against the other two contestants in the match up. DING DING DING! To Airica and Nathan they had the idea to eliminate both Gabriel and Saito then fight it out for a winner. On the other side of the ring Gabriel Lauren and Saito were conversing. Letting one another know that Airica and Nathan were a couple. That they should get rid of those two then fight it out at the end. Both teams had the same game plan. Airica and Nathan attempted to gang up on Gabriel Lauren before anything. Shoving him into a corner. The couple planted multiple boots on to the chest and face of the downed Gabriel Lauren. When the boots connected with his chest he felt wind being knocked out of him. When they hit his face he saw a white light flash. A massive pain in his nose came soon after. In Gabriel's mind he had to find a way out of that corner before things took a turn for the worst. Both Airica and Nathan thought they had it in the bag. But they forgot one small part of the equation. RILEY STEVENS: “Fast start by these new comers here.” Saito grabbed Nathan Starr by the hair, Saito was offended. He thought that no one thought he was worth coming after. The Japanese wrestler pulled back a hard Russian Leg Sweep on Nathan planting the blonde down on the canvas. Saito jumped on top of Nathan. Airica saw what was going on. She thought that she needed Nathan Starr in the match with her to get rid of the other two quicker. She instantly stopped everything she was doing. Left the corner and made her way to the middle of the ring to break up that pin, dropping the point of her right elbow right on top of Saito's spine. Pain shot through his system, his first instinct was to get off Nathan and away from Airica. Nathan was only down for a two count. Saito was not happy about it. He was in a lot of pain. He held his back. RILEY STEVENS: “This is good.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Agreed, new wrestlers. Like a breath of fresh air. I like what Airica and Nathan are trying to do.” Airica motivated Nathan to get up. Once he did she told him to lift Saito up, Nathan lifted him up. Saito's back was on top of Nathan's left shoulder. Airica wrapped her arm around Saito's neck. The couple executed a perfect back breaker/neck breaker combination. Saito was out like a light, no thoughts, just out cold. Nathan took the pin quickly ONE! TWO! THREE! The couple celebrated Saito getting eliminated. Officials helped Saito to the back. RILEY STEVENS: “There goes Saito. He was impressive though.” Both Nathan and Airica turned around to see that Gabriel was on the top turnbuckle. Gabriel thought, jump off and take them both out. Airica and Nathan were in a panic. Gabriel jumped off extending his body. Airica rolled out of the way, leaving Nathan to be hit by the diving crossbody. The impact caused Gabriel to take some damage too, and fall off Nathan. Airica like an opportunist rushed over to Gabriel and pushed his legs upward. Pressing his shoulders down to the canvas ONE! TWO! THREE! Gabriel had been eliminated! Gabriel was out of the ring now. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “This is really fun.” RILEY STEVENS: “Gabriel Lauren attempted a diving crossbody but that's the risk that comes with going high risk.” Airica looked for Nathan to celebrate this one with her as well but she noticed that he was still on the canvas seemingly knocked out. She looked around. Then bent down to take the win. Nathan grabbed on to Airica's hair and pulled her into a small package pin ONE! TWO! THREE! OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Wow! Nathan stole one from his girlfriend!” REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner…Nathan Starr!” Nathan got up and shrugged, Airica was clearly upset about what had happened. Starr rolled out of the ring having out smarted her as Airica angrily glares at him, as he heads to the back and we head to some teaser’s for later tonight. WINNER: NATHAN STARR QUOTE As we returned from a little break that included a few hype videos for Revolt tonight we are greeted for the second time by the Deep South belt as Ryan Cavallari hands it over to the referee in the ring, looking around at those in the ring with him sizing up his first defense. As the bell rings. DING DING DING! The ring full at all four corners and one at the center. Cavallari, Hollister, Valentino, and Callum at the corners with Eliza Gray front and center. At the ring of the bell, Valentino and Callum descended upon Ryan’s corner simultaneous. Hollister had gone for Callum, but with the two men upon the other, she was left to meet Gray at center. A dropkick held Hollister at bay for Gray to start fresh and on top whilst the men wore one another out in the corner. She moved toward the ropes and ascended the turn post to perform a diving legdrop upon her opponent, clearing several feet of distance further than expected in order to reach her. Rolling with the moment, she helped her opponent to her feet and back down with a german suplex, chaining three together for as big an impact, and show as possible. On the third, she bridged it with a pin, but Hollister managed to pull herself out at the count of two. Still the men had yet to notice the women that took control of the rest of the ring. In a world of dominance and submission like a game of cat and mouse, whilst the women played a more calculated risk of wearing one another out through strategic techniques, such as Gray’s camel clutch, used hopefully to hinder Hollister’s chances at fighting back. RILEY STEVENS: “Gray might be trying to weaken Sara a bit before there match later tonight.” In the corner, as Ryan was descended upon, he struck Callum away with a hard right jab, but Valentino managed to crack in a slap to stir the current Deep South Champion crazy. Ryan’s sights were now set on the disrespect from Valentino’s slap and tried to retaliate, but was pushed to the corner by Valentino and smudged like mud with a kick into the corner buckles by Callum. The two, Callum and Zerotine both worked to round the edges of the champion whilst in the squared circle’s corner post. Ryan managed to get a fist, or kick in edgewise, but with it as two on one; he was unable to break free of their bondages. RILEY STEVENS: “They got the champion taken out here.” He wasn’t going to let anyone beat him into who and what they wanted him to be, and with a forearm smash to Callum, and then swift European uppercut, or two, or three, and another for good measure for the toying to Zerotine, he freed himself. About that time, he noticed the other two ladies at the off-center of the ring, but before he could take control of his match, and his ring, he’d need to show the two dogs where they belonged. One a cur, the other a bitch. Back in the center, Gray had worn down Sara well enough to exhaustion. She felt confident that she had the match sealed. It was hers for the taking, but suddenly Callum had noticed them. Ryan focused on Zerotine and the two left the third wheel out of the ride. Gray, hoping to take the initiative met Callum along the side of the ring, where Callum eagerly used her momentum to toss her between the ropes and out onto the mats below. Eliza Gray crashed against the barricade like a glamorous wreckage. RILEY STEVENS: “Might this be someone’s chance?” Inside, Callum took advantage of the damage done to Hollister and performed his signature variation of the gutwrench powerslam. Cyrano Drop. He covered the woman for the pin and just as Ryan had finally broke free of his chains, the referee marked the ring with the three count and out the window went Ryan’s title. REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner by way of pinfall…Chris Callum!” At least he was the first, but it wasn’t enough for him. It was gone. A better fan favorite than the cross Cavallari, Callum was cheered and accepted as the champion, the title handed to him with ease. Ryan dropped down to the rim of the ring and rolled out. He’d bide his time for now, but that wasn’t going to get let off easily. RILEY STEVENS: “What a huge victory for Callum who unseats the champion and becomes the second Deep South Champion, but now as Ryan found out tonight. It’s time for the hard part, defending it!” The women too managed to scurry like flies from sticky tape as the champion was tall and cheered in the ring. Chris Callum was the new Deep South Champion. WINNER: CHRIS CALLUM (New GIW Deep South Champion)
