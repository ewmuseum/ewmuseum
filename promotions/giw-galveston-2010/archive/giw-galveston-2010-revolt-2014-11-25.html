GIW — Card

Revolt, 25 November 2014

25 November 2014
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: REVOLT! Live from Barrie, Ontario, Canada – November 25th, 2014 Backstage, Auva Skye opened the show with a smile toward the camera. She stood with arms folded and wasted no time in addressing the issue at hand. AUVA SKYE: “Over the week we have learned that a former competitor in Galveston Island Wrestling desires to return for selfish reasons. Alex Castellanos will not be welcomed back, nor will Alexis Terry, or even Aspen Chaud.” She continued. AUVA SKYE: “I'm sorry, but the way they handled this company has brought it to a stand still and also crippled and closed down this organization on several occasions. Let us not forget that they were on the roster when this company took to the seas on its worst idea yet. Really, an independent wrestling organization on a cruise ship? Terrible.” Auva added more to it. AUVA SKYE: “Finally, Scar may have detached from the cause, but that is just as well. May he do his own thing, because I still hold control over Galveston Island Wrestling. I am doing my best to give to the current roster, and our current fans. Without you, I am nothing, and that is something the previous management couldn't understand. It is something that idiots like Alexis Terry couldn't understand. I will continue to make mistakes and learn from them. Ladies and gentlemen, I will be the administrator you both want, and need. Enjoy tonight's International X rendition of Revolt!” the camera quickly cut to another backstage segment as Scar began to make his move through the crimson darkness of the corridors. The lights suddenly raised and before him was none other than Alioth Starre. ALIOTH STARRE: “Still playing the same song, mate?” SCAR: "You–" ALIOTH STARRE: “Keep your 'lectric eyes on me, mate. You're fading glow of the past. And you're wretched hives are dried, so good luck getting back your fallen glory.” SCAR: "I still have a moment of glory to seize, unlike you." With those words parted, Scar moved past him. Starre turned toward the camera as the crowd could be heard cheering and chanting. ALIOTH STARRE: “Some people never learn when to take their guitar and go home.” He turned his black acoustic from over his back and struck a few chords from the song 'Ziggy Stardust' by David Bowie as the segment ended. QUOTE Jack Waters waited in the ring as the arena dimmed to a crimson hue. No need for music, Scar seemed to be ringside before anyone could tell he had arrived. He ascended the rim of the ring and entered behind Waters. Waters turned and caught himself in Scar's grasp as the vile villain took his opponent with a choke. The lights returned to normal and Scar pushed the combatant toward the center of the ring, despite Waters' attempts to fight strength versus strength. Scar then hoisted his opponent up as high as he could lift him and slammed Waters down flat on his back, like a pancake on the white canvas. He lowered to a knee over Waters for an immature pin, but the referee only managed to count by two before Waters rolled his shoulder over to break it. This phased Waters' opponent little. Scar pulled off and aided Waters to his feet, if only to keep a hold of him. He looped Waters' arm over his shoulder and took a tight grasp of the pants of his opponent and prepared a duplex, but Waters hooked his leg with Scar's. This forced the two to perform a stalemate. Scar tried again, but the same results followed. He was not insane and would not try it again, so he pushed Waters away from him only to receive a hard right jab to the ribs, then the face. Scar staggered back a few steps when the hits kept coming. Waters wouldn't stop the assault on the man that targeted him without reason. He forced Scar against the ropes, took his arm, and performed an Irish whip across the ring. Waters didn't wait, he ran to meet his opponent and sent him flying with a clothesline over the top rope. Scar collapsed into the outer matting as the crowds cheered Waters on. Waters climbed out of the ring and just as his feet touched the floor, the referee began a ten count. Scar climbed the rim of the ring for support, but Waters was already on him. A strike to the head, then guiding his opponent up to Irish whip him into the steel steps was all that was needed to keep the menacing figure from getting back on his own two feet. Waters had complete control of his opponent. He stalked him, impressed even by the strength of himself when the steps dislodged ever Scar was sent with ramming speed into them. The echo of the hollow metal rang out throughout the arena and Scar's face bleed. The count was now at five and the two had no reason to enter the ring again. Waters had Scar by the hair and slammed his bleeding face into the lowered metal step. Not a foul, because it was part of the ring. Lest a suplex be grounds for suspension as well. No, he enjoyed the punishment he was inflicting on this formerly dark robed mystery man. He had ransacked the organization of many backstage personalities that had lost their jobs because of it, now Waters was making sure he couldn't get to him as well. He had just accomplished a five series string of punishing matches against Tony Miranda, and won. He would now defeat Tony's haunted past. Waters went for an overhead strike, but Scar met him at the gut. He doubled over when Scar pulled himself up and tucked Waters' head between his knees and performed a piledriver outside of the ring. The count was now at eight and Scar started to take notice of this. To answer the referee, the foul figure rolled into the ring and then back out in order to refresh the ten count. The referee was annoyed by this, but by the laws of the ring, he had to perform his count again. Scar was now on borrowed time, but time he took from the referee. The hourglass tipped over, he used all of it to his advantage. Every count was a strike, and every number he used to dwindle the energy and strength away from Waters like a vampire. He used a throat thrust to smack Waters backward against the hard rim of the ring. He then raked the man's face against the cornered edge until they met the metallic post. Waters staggered near to the barricade where the crowd tried to tap him and cheer him to continue, but Scar charged him like a Romanian bull and the barricade gave way beneath the two of them. Seconds away from ten with the clock ticking against them, it was oddly Waters that was first to his feet. Scar had brushed his shoulder against a a corner matting that gave way overtop of him, hindering his movement. Waters didn't care, he pulled it off and dragged Scar to the ring post to smash the man's face into it. He slammed his head for every scar he could count on Scar's face, earned for every moment he spent in the ring with Tony Miranda. He rolled himself into the ring and Scar before him. With the dark figure out of sorts, Waters waited. Like a tiger in a bamboo forest, hunting the greatest dragon in all of China, he waited. The crowd was silent when they could, otherwise stomping their feet for every breath they took as Scar slowly rose up. When the demon turned, Waters charged and performed a hard hitting, body smashing clothesline from Hell. Scar flipped in the air as he was hit and landed nearly on his head and sides. The impact echoed almost as much as his head on the metal steps. Waters went for the pin, but the lights dimmed a second time. This time, they were black. The song of dust began to play, like a time machine in decay. 'Cruel Melody' by Black Light Burns signaled to everyone that a long lost competitor had just returned from the dead. As Scar turned, unable to collect himself off the ring, he, in blurred vision, saw a phantom figure appear at the stage and descend down to meet Waters in combat. The referee was beside himself. Any hit and the match was over! Altair ducked under a second clothesline from Hell and turned to strike Waters, but Scar was now up and next to him. Both men took Waters under and arm, looped the other, and performed a double suplex. Altair rolled out of the ring to let Scar take control before the referee even knew what happened. When the lights returned to normal, Waters rolled out of a hard stomp aimed at him and kicked back at Scar's gut. He refused to lose to his ruse. He rolled back onto his knees and came up with an uppercut, but missed Scar by inches. Scar took Waters' hand and twisted it into a wrist lock, but Waters turned quickly, out of the the lock, around Scar, and jerked him forward to face one another. In that moment, Waters smacked Scar hard across the chest with his Breath Taker, prompting Altair back in the ring to receive a clothesline from Hell. It was as though the referee had lost control when Waters ran Altair out of the ring, taking Scar with him. Waters climbed down and grabbed Scar, who fought for his freedom and whipped Waters into the turn post. Altair stood back and waited for his moment to strike, the phantom of the past close to haunting Waters with his devastating signature maneuver. The count was at eight when anyone realized that the referee was counting and the match was still on. Waters tried to fight to get into the ring, but it was too late. Altair finally intervened on Ten as the bell rang and Scar threw his opponent into his partner in crime. The Crimson Reaper took out Waters as quick as lightning when Scar offered him up as a sacrifice. The two took a glance toward one another, then to the crowd. The Crimson Order, as appeared on their shared exit video was revived from the dead. As Waters pulled up from the attack, phased, but not laid out, he watched as the two made their way around the ring and up the aisle. Waters grabbed a microphone from the temporary ring announcer and called out the two blood brothers. JACK WATERS: "This isn't over, Scar! You were ran out of this company by Tony Miranda, but I took him out and buried him alive! I defeated the man you could never dream of destroying. Not only that, you fiend, but you had to bring back a phantom from your past just to beat me down, but I'm still standing, and I'm challenging both of you for a main event of the next Revolt! I don't need a partner to fight you, but if you're going to bring one, then so will I!" WINNER via DOUBLE COUNTOUT: DRAW! Backstage in the parking lot, the camera revealed a large black limousine pulling up. A set of feminine legs stepped out, and behind her, a suited gentlemen. As the camera panned upward, someone knocked the lens down to the pavement to conceal their identities. The show then cut to the next segment. Auva Skye stood in the ring with a collection of Galveston Island's International, and National past. Previous champions and other alumni that have stepped in the ring at some point or another. She stood before them as they were lined behind her and spoke to the audience with a wide grin on her face. AUVA SKYE: “This is your administrator speaking. Unfortunately Astro and M. Tech were not able to properly cross the border into this beautiful country of Canada, but in place of that, what would have been a terrific match between two excellent competitors, is an equally great surprise!” She turned to the group, then to the crowd as four referees took to each side of the ring. AUVA SKYE: “I know you remember all of these wonderful talents! Some of them were former Undisputed Champions and many of them home heroes from Canada, such as Nelson Fielder; Seth Seeker; and the beautiful Artemis Kaiser! Representing Portugal is Marcus Hister, then we have our mother country ambassadors: Alioth Starre, and Mark Cross. Finally, representing the United States will be Trent Lost, Wake Walker, Ryan Cavallari, Ross Rydell; and finally Brody Harper!” The eleven individual then parted across the ring, leaning against the posts and ropes, keeping arms length or more from one another. AUVA SKYE: “The best part? Because two individuals could not make it tonight, you now get an international battle royal surprise for tonight's International X edition of Revolt! Ring the-” ??? "No! There won't be a surprise without me, the greatest wrestler in Galveston Island Wrestling before the former administrators of this wretched place kicked me out for being, as they said, a CHEATER!" At the top of the stage stood none other than Eric Sparrow. He made his way down to the ring as he spoke. Eric Sparrow "If I wasn't barred from that ring nearly five years ago, I would still be the Undisputed Champion and there would never have been an Alioth, a Wake Walker; Matt Kluge; or even Aspen Chaud! I am the foundation that built this company." AUVA SKYE: “Your reign, Sparrow, was built on lies and deceit, but you're right. You were a pivotal part of early GIW, and if you want in on this historic battle royal, then so be it! Ring the bell!” Just as it rang, Eric slid into the ring and the match was underway. QUOTE Across the ring members of GIW's past engaged in combat, but at the very center stood face to face Wake Walker and Alioth Starre. They had a troubled meeting at Moment of Glory, but now the crowds cheered them on to fight again and to make it count. The two eyed one another and then stood back several paces until they hit the ropes of the ring and exited, eliminating themselves at the same time. RILEY STEVENS: "What!? How will we know which one of them would have won against the other? Why did they do this?" OLIVER THIBODAUX: "I don't know if we'll ever see these two legends at each other's throats again, but that one moment was tense enough to hold me over." RILEY STEVENS: "Maybe one day, but for now we still have a plethora of GIW alumni, including the one and only Eric Sparrow in the ring! My money, with Wake Walker gone, is now on Sparrow!" OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Nonsense, Stevens. It's Brody Harper time, all the way!" Harper was hammered left and right by Seth Seeker and Trent Lost in the corner of the ring. It wasn't giving Oliver much hope, but the match wasn't over until one person was left standing in the ring. Ross and Fielder competed against one another since the beginning. Ross ducked under a swing and caught Fielder to perform a sidewalk slam. Across from them, Artemis Kaiser and Mark Cross were at one another's throats. She was thrust into the turn post as Brody had, but managed to charge out and strike Cross with a spinning backfist. Sparrow found himself contending against both Marcus Hister and a man that was once considered the future of GIW, but now trapped in the past: Ryan Cavallari. Ryan exchanged back and forth hits with Eric, but then the known cheat feigned and pulled Hister in front of him. Ryan pulled back, not caring if he hit Hister, but wanting greatly to punish Eric Sparrow. The crowd cheered on their home country Canadians. Artemis was dueling best against Cross, despite taking a few hits. Nelson pushed off Ross, whom found Trent to fight, and then turned over to Seth to tag with him against Brody Harper. It didn't take long before both men were struggling to turn Brody Harper's heavy body over the top rope to eliminate him. Seeing this, and then using a quick distraction to disengage, Eric Sparrow pushed Cavallari into Hister and ran to Brody's aid, or so some thought. Quickly, with both Seeker and Fielder busy trying to eliminate the heavy weight, Sparrow hoisted Fielder up by the foot and used that momentum to send him over the top rope. Unfortunately for him, Sparrow was nearly tossed over with him when Fielder's feet knocked into him and dragged him over. Seth spotted this, Brody and Sparrow both on the edge of the ring, on the opposite ends of the ropes, but before he could act, it was Brody that knocked Sparrow off. Sparrow landed on top of Nelson Fielder, though his feet touched, he held them up as the referee came to inspect them. Lying and cheating his way to the steel steps, hopping like an idiot, he slithered back into the ring, but the crowd knew the truth. How could anyone expect him to change his ways? Eric Sparrow had eliminated a Canadian and now the crowd's hatred poured down on him like a heatwave. He ignored it and started on Brody Harper for trying to eliminate him. Artemis was away from Cross, now taken up by Ryan Cavallari. Most of the contenders had switched competitors when they couldn't eliminate them. She now faced against Trent Lost, who swung at her and tried to hit, but she was quick like a hawk. Far from them, Ross and Hister engaged in combat. Hister tried for a dropkick, but Ross sidestepped it. He pulled Hister to his feet, leaned him against the ropes, and performed his savate kick to send Marcus Hister over the ropes and out of the ring. The next elimination was Mark Cross. After Artemis disengaged with him, Ryan Cavallari took little time to perform his signature Snap DDT. After that, all it took was a kick and Cross was against the turn post. A few slaps later and Trent Lost found himself joining the fray. He helped Ryan hoist Cross across the ropes and together sent a hard double kick to the man's back that sent him over the turn post and tumbling down the steel steps. Seven were left, six officially if you didn't count Eric Sparrow. He and Brody were hard at work against one another, but Sparrow was overpowering him. Soon, Brody was in a compromising position again and stood on the opposite end of the ropes. Eric charged him, but Brody ducked. Before Sparrow could even realize it, Artemis and Seth Seeker both speared him to the canvas with enough force to knock him senseless. Brody took in a breath of relief and reentered the ring. The entire match stopped. They all stood around Sparrow after a replay of his cheating move was on the big screen. Now they knew he shouldn't be in the ring and all at once they descended upon him like wolves. Each individual, by cheer of the crowd, performed a signature or two on Sparrow before it was Brody Harper for a second time that got to eliminate Eric Sparrow from the International Battle Royal. With that said and done, the group broke alliances and fought as a chaotic melee again. OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Sparrow falls again! Goodbye and good riddance!" RILEY STEVENS: "Nothing good can come from cheating the system, let alone everyone you depend upon to keep your job. You piss off the management and the roster and you're out of luck, buddy!" OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Oh, my! Trent is out of the match! He's just been eliminated by Ross Rydell! Brody's still got this, Stevens! I'm telling you he's going to win, and that's a spoiler alert!" Brody turned away from the ropes after taunting Sparrow, who begrudgingly left ringside, only to find Ryan Cavallari cross an arm over him and send Brody finally up and over the top rope. Brody Harper was finally eliminated. RILEY STEVENS: "Sounds more like a rumor mill to me, Oliver." The four remaining alumni stood at each corner of the ring. Ross Rydell, Ryan Cavallari; both from the United States. On the other posts were Seth Seeker and Artemis Kaiser, both from Canada, the country International X was hosted in. After a moment's breath, they were all at it again. Artemis took on Rydell, and Seeker with Cavallari. As Artemis charged Rydell, ready to perform a roundhouse kick when they met, Ross countered and with a New York Minute, slammed down Kaiser to the white canvas below. He picked her up and easily ran her right out of the ring. The United States now outnumbered Canada. The crowd wasn't too happy with this, but the match wasn't over yet. Seth turned toward Ross and Ryan and charged to clothesline both of them. The crowd roared with success. He grabbed both men and pulled them up, tucking them for a double DDT, which also garnered a loud roar from the crowd as the two Americans smacked into the canvas had first. Before they even knew it, Ross and Ryan were being handled toward the ropes and Ross was the first one out, but Ryan fought back. Being the last two, or so they thought, they quarreled hard against one another until a sound stopped everything. A voice in the background. A familiar voice. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “What would a ground breaking, history making match in Galveston Island Wrestling's history be without the man that's on the cover of that history book? I am back!” Alex raced down to the ring, the crowd behind him whole heartedly until he was quick to eliminate Seth Seeker. Not Ryan Cavallari and Alex Castellanos stood face to face. Not for long, as he pulsed back and shot a Golden Arrow to the chin of his opponent and Ryan was sent staggering back and over the top rope. WINNER via Elimination!: ALEX CASTELLANOS! Immediately, Auva Skye took to the stage again, her smile gone and her eyes ablaze with rage. AUVA SKYE: “You are not welcome here, Alex! I distinctly mentioned in a press release, and here tonight, that I would never open the doors to the likes of you again! You had your chance in Galveston Island Wrestling and you chose to abandon us! You chose the same path Alexis Terry and Aspen Chaud did! The same path as Blake Beckett and Alioth Starre! You have no right to be here! I demand to know who let you in this building!” From behind the administrator stood another. Alexis Terry. ALEXIS TERRY: “I did!” Alexis knocked Auva on her ass with the hardest, fastest slap heard throughout the entire arena. Before anything else could happen, security were on them like bees to separate the two. The crowd chanted 'This Is Awesome!" as Alex stood in the ring, and Alexis Terry on stage, towering over Auva Skye. QUOTE After a quick break, Lorde played into the arena to signal the arrival of The Harlequin, and the main event of the International X edition of GIW Revolt! She toyed with herself and the crowd as she made her way to the ring and slid in effortlessly. Second after her was Michael Lynch. More sinister, he took his time getting to the ring and kept his eyes on his opponent. On the prize. Once inside and forced to face off with the referee between them, the two parted and broke off to circle one another. Their first encounter was a headlock around Harlequin, but she was quick and smaller than Lynch, fleeing his grasp easily. He turned and swung at her, but she ducked and snickered. He a standing lariat, but she sidestepped him and came down with a chuckle and an elbow. Qucik, like a rabbit, she was off and he pulled himself up. As fast as their actions she came back and leapt up at him with a crossbody attack. He used that momentum to roll her over him and then on top of her. Smaller than he, she was pinned under Lynch and he punched away at her until the referee could let him no longer. Lynch reluctantly threw himself off of her and calculated his next attack. He had to give her a few seconds before the referee would part from her, but once he moved, Lynch was at it again. He knelt before her, held her green hair in a fist and threw down his other at her white pasty face. The referee broke up the altercation a second time and gave him a warning. Instead of heading it, he struck her one last time and locked her into a cross armbar. She refused to tap and found some way to weasel out of the submission hold. Harlequin then rolled to her feet and used a double foot stop, nearly merging that into a dropkick to keep Lynch down. Once he was on his back, she folded him up like origami to perform a Boston Crab. It didn’t hold, however, and his strength sent her staggering forward into the ropes. If she were in the previous match, she’d have been nearly eliminated. She rested for a second and turned back toward him as Lynch stalked his prey. He grabbed her hair, she punched his gut, it phased him not. Lynch retaliated by tucking her between the knees for a piledriver, but as he held her upside down and as straight as a board, she craned her neck and bit him! Yes, bit Lynch in the thigh to surprise him. A sneaky little tactic to free herself that worked. Harlequin followed up with spinning wheel kick and standing moonsault for good measure. Quickly, she pulled him up to his feet and performed her signature 720 DDT that planted Michael Lynch into the canvas like a bad joke. She went for the cover and found herself awarded the three count over him. She rose up in celebration and the crowd went wild with her. The show closed on confetti spiraling down around her as she ended the International edition of Revolt. WINNER via Pinfall!: THE HARLEQUIN!
