GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: CATEGORY 5 LIVE! From Galveston’s Pleasure Pier – August 4, 2013 “A belt with the most storied of history’s, born into controversy.” As the voice over begins we rewind all the way to the very first GIW show where we have Aspen Chaud getting stripped of the belt by Blake Beckett as that video fades and again the voice over continues. “A belt brought to glory by a champion whom stood above the rest…” From there we are shown glimpses of Wake Walker defending the belt on several occasions as the amount of days flashes across the screen as he holds the belt tight, ’98 Days’, as that part of the video fades. “A belt held by the greatest in the world…” We get a glimpse of every single champion in the GIW, a total of eleven all of them holding the belt with the same pride as the one prior. “But one man reigned supreme longer then them all…” After flipping through the other ten the video stops on Alioth Starre as we see glimpses of his several title defenses as well as his part of the video ends with ‘155 Days’, as we again fade to black. “A belt held by a champion determined to prove she’s worthy of the term, Undisputed…” As we see a glimpse of Terry’s first Undisputed title victory before it crashes down having lost it the next month, but finishing with a second glimpse of her in her second reign with the belt. “A belt that every star from the storied GIW Legends, to the rising stars dream for…” A few shots of some of the fast rising stars in GIW are shown before ending with Clay Krueger staring at the Undisputed belt with wide eyes, as the video fades to black. “But on this night, four stars from completely different backgrounds compete for that storied belt, looking to add themselves to another chapter.” Alioth Starre “The longest reigning Undisputed Champion, of all time. A man whose held the belt on two occasions one being for a total of one hundred fifty five days. Having risen to the top of GIW quickly, whether he be loved or hated Alioth Starre always had the fans attention. One of if not the greatest in ring competitors in GIW history, Alioth Starre has defeated seemingly every wrestler that stood across from him, having essentially done all there is to do in GIW, except for overcome the daunting prospects of the Sole Survivor match. Already having won the belt twice, Alioth looks for a third reign tonight and looks to further cement himself atop the list of GIW stars. Clay Krueger “The wild card as many have called him this week, the biggest wrestler in the match and not to mention the most imposing Clay Krueger made his name in GIW to start with for his rather blunt opinions on female’s in the GIW ring. But has quickly gone from a man with a strong opinion to a man with a strong chance at becoming the newest GIW Undisputed Champion. Some might consider his lack of experience in the big one a disadvantage as he’s the one non-former Undisputed Champion of the match but one might also argue that Clay looking to become champion for the first time, will simply want it more then the others. He has a chance to become champion but he also has a chance to overcome three GIW legends at the same time.” Wake Walker “When it comes to Wake Walker one is reminded of former dominance during the early days of GIW, where Walker truly stood out as a champion amongst men. There was little debate over whether he was the best but since then, time has passed him by. And as GIW went into its true prime, Wake was no longer there instead replaced by a new wave of GIW stars and legends. Often criticized for ducking a return, and namely ducking long time rival Alioth Starre, one can finally say that’s all put to rest as Wake has made his return and done so picking up right where he left off. But not one for settling, he won’t feel accomplished with just that as he looks to prove that he can once again become champion.” Alexis Terry “Never doubted as one of the greatest wrestlers in GIW history; Alexis Terry has the history and the gold to prove who her rank amongst the greatest of all time but has one thing left to accomplish and that’s to defend the belt that she holds currently and held once before. Having lost that belt as quickly as she got it, Terry’s Undisputed champion pedigree could be called into question but having won the belt back again Alexis looks to yet again put that behind her by doing what nobody has ever done, defend her belt in Sole Survivor and not only defend it but walk in as the champion and walk out the same way.” Four Will Enter One Will Win One Champion One SOLE SURVIVOR QUOTE REV.
