GIW — Card

Revolt, 7 October 2010

7 October 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: Thursday Night Revolt! LIVE OCTOBER 7th, FROM MARIO’S BAR AND GRILL IN GALVESTON, TEXAS BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! It’s time for another thrilling edition of the depleted Thursday Night Revolt where no longer is the show headed by Blake Beckett rather Amanda Rowland with Alexii Ravynova holding some new power as well. And the show that was before dominated by Wake Walker now is home to several up and coming stars. The fans are cheering hoping for another big edition of the show. RILEY STEVENS: “Last week was Blake Beckett’s last night here and this time we are without him and the future is looking bright!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “If you say so.” RILEY STEVENS: “Tonight we have three matches starting with the weekly Over the Top Rope Battle Royal between any stars that want to show up. This match has sparked a ton of new talent in the past; you think we could have a new guy coming out of the wood works?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Maybe.” RILEY STEVENS: “Then we follow that up with a Triple Threat match between Joshua Acquin, Chicken, and Brock Charnel.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Sweet Chicken’s in Contenders Matches now...Goodness our roster is depleted.” RILEY STEVENS: “And lastly we have a GIGANTIC main event tonight as it’ll be Eric Sparrow, Kacie Church, Tony Miranda, and Aspen Chaud competing for the right every star on this roster wants, the right to invade EPW!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Because we can’t just go attack, like they do us.” RILEY STEVENS: “As we speak the ring is filling up with stars for the Battle Royal lets go there now.” = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = We head to the ring where already standing in the ring is a full ring of stars in total there are five of them. On one side we have Zen, Big Papa, Gabriel Heavenly, and Jacob Moore while on the other side it’s Thor Vegard. The crowd has a slight murmur looking on at the very intimidating man. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Good lord, who is this guy.” RILEY STEVENS: “Thor Vegard, I’ve heard some nasty things about this one.” It seems as though these four are all a bit scared of Thor as the referee’s are on the outside to watch over the elimination as the referee then calls for the bell to start this thing up. DING DING DING! The match gets underway and it’s the four of them staring across the ring at Thor Vegard deciding what to do as he stares back at them with literally no emotion. Finally Gabriel Heavenly decides to be the first one to attack him but walks into a big time Headbutt that literally seems to knock him out, but before he falls down Thor grabs his arm and with one arm whips Gabriel out of the ring with force. Gabriel Heavenly is Eliminated The crowd is a bit in awe as the other three seem to realize what they are in for as they all go in for the attack at the same time starting to pummel Thor from all sides. However after a few seconds he begins to battle his way out, with a big fist to Moore that knocks him off his feet and then a big Headbutt to Zen and then finally it’s just Papa and Thor. Big Papa is a huge man and stares back at Thor and then begins to throw some punches at him seeming to be getting some offense going. However Thor throws him to the mat with relative ease. Papa gets to his feet near the ropes and Thor goes to the Three Point Stance before hitting a Clothesline to him sending him over the top rope and to the outside. Big Papa is Eliminated! Thor turns around and just now getting to his feet is Jacob Moore. He is walking rather gingerly however he walks right into the chest of Vegard who grabs him and twists him up and into what he calls the ‘Afterlife’ a Piledriver! The crowd is still in silence watching the carnage in the ring as Thor picks up the dead weight that is Moore and then proceeds to lift him up onto his shoulders and then shoulder toss him out of the ring. Jacob Moore is Eliminated! Thor turns to find the last man who is Zen who received a Headbutt earlier and is trying to crawl out of the ring. However rather then letting him escaping he lifts up Zen from the mat staring him into the eyes. Zen however decides to try to fight out as he punches Thor and then runs off the mat however he walks right into a Big Boot which triggers the start of the ‘Norwegian Torture’ as he follows it up by Remi Double Foot Stop to the face which draws blood from Zen. The crowd actually seems to fear for Zen’s life as Thor drags him over to the corner and then lifts him up and flips him out of the ring. Zen is Eliminated! As he wins his debut! OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Did we just witness a homicide?” RILEY STEVENS: “I’m not sure but I think Thor Vegard just made his presence felt in a big way here on Revolt.” The referee slides into the ring to raise his arm but when Thor instead glares down at him, he thinks better of it as Thor Vegard stares out at the crowd having done what he came here to do tonight. WINNER: THOR VEGARD = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = Backstage, the cameraman appears to be walking aimlessly until finally… he comes across a locker room door that gets everyone’s attention. He stares at it for a few seconds. It has a paper on it. It reads, “Reserved for 10-31-2010”. Right at the bottom left corner of the locker room, it has a Japanese letter. The cameraman shrugs and continues to walk where he comes upon Jake Kennedy still seeming to be injured. He is eating at the cafeteria when out of the bottom of the screen comes Tony Miranda. TONY MIRANDA: “How’s your injury bud?” Miranda has an unconvinced grin on his face as he looks down at Kennedy’s supposedly injured ankle, as Kennedy sets down his plate and replies. JAKE KENNEDY: “You know I am starting to feel a lot better.” Kennedy grins and then just to rub it in his face he lifts up the ION Television Championship and tosses it over his shoulder. TONY MIRANDA: “You know when I had that belt; I didn’t have to fake having an injury to keep it.” Miranda stares at him as the tension in the air is getting pretty strong as these long time rivals seem nearly ready to fight once again. JAKE KENNEDY: “Exactly, WHEN you were the champion. Last time I checked I’m the champion now and if I’m not a hundred percent I’m not going to wrestle. The good part is these fans don’t have to worry about you being the champion again, considering my contender is about to be named tonight, and it won’t be you.” Almost with perfect timing walking in between the two is Joshua Acquin the man who last week made this thing a three way fight as he looks from Tony the man he attacked to the one who he was trying to send the message to. JOSHUA ACQUIN: “That however will be me.” Joshua powers through the two of them, then heads towards the ring for his match as Tony and Jake both stare at him as the segment ends. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = “Nightmare" by A7X begins to play. Joshua Acquin comes out to his music and begins to walk down the ring. He plays the usual role of being the least liked individual in the back. He enters the ring slowly to try and absorb all of the hate that he is currently getting. Once he’s in the ring, he poses for the crowd (which of course, they don’t like). The announcer announces that the match is for number one contender ship of the Television title. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Just wake me up when this is over.” “Chain Hang Low” by Jibbs begins to play. Joshua Acquin begins to shake his head in embarrassment. Chicken skips his way to the ring with a basket, handing everyone eggs. As soon as people touch the eggs, little chicks begin to be born. Chicken grabs the entire basket and then throws it into the audience. Let’s hope those little chicks are okay. Chicken and Joshua Acquin wait for their opponent but it’s announced that Brock Charnel called in sick. The match begins. RILEY STEVENS: “I guess Charnel forfeited his way out of a title shot..” Chicken starts doing some poses. Joshua Acquin gets mad and swings a clothesline at Chicken. Chicken dodges it by accidentally tripping and falling to the floor. Joshua Acquin looks back at Chicken and then tries to lock in a headlock. He does so, but Chicken slips out of it easily! Maybe it’s because he’s a really too greasy! Joshua Acquin begins to get frustrated and picks up Chicken and tries to hit a suplex! Before he can actually hit it, Chicken lays an egg on top of the head of Joshua Acquin! Joshua Acquin lets go of Chicken and then tends to the egg. He puts it down and then is about to go ahead and continue the match when suddenly the egg hatches. It begins to call Joshua, “mama”. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “...” Joshua Acquin tries to convince the small chick that he is not its mama. Chicken, in the meantime, begins to lay a bunch of eggs. He lays about ten and then holds them all in his arm. When Joshua finally convinces the small chick that he is not its parent, he is hit by an onslaught of eggs by Chicken! Everytime he’s hit, the shell of the eggs cracks and chicks come out! Joshua Acquin looks like he’s out of it on his feet. Chicken rolls him up and goes for a pinfall! 1…2…what in the world?! 3?! Chicken has won! Chicken gets up and celebrates with all the chicks! OLIVER THIBODAUX: “And we wonder why Blake left?” Jake Kennedy comes out and then begins to chuckle on the ramp, shaking his head as Joshua Acquin may have just blown his chance at a title shot in the upcoming weeks while Chicken will instead take his place. WINNER: CHICKEN (New #1 Contender to ION Television Championship) = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = We return again to the backstage area this time in the new office of Alexii Ravynova where she is currently instructing one of the workers where to put one of her paintings. However the door opens soon and in walks Aspen Chaud, causing Alexii to turn to her almost immediately. ASPEN CHAUD: “Last week the whole roster was in unison, with everyone fighting off the EPW guys yet tonight I hear that you decide to make us fight for the ability to go invade EPW? Doesn’t that seem like we are going in the wrong direction?” Aspen seems a bit unhappy with the whole situation while Alexii seems to be completely loving her new state of power as she shakes her head simply. ALEXII RAVYNOVA: "Well every great team needs a leader and since I’m now in a more backstage role, we need to declare a leader. And since Amanda isn’t here tonight, what I say pretty much goes." Alexii smirks in a sense saying that the winner would be the leader of the GIW Resistance against the EPW Stars. ASPEN CHAUD: “You’re forgetting that last week we did just fine as a team, don’t you think it would make more sense to just be a collective group rather then putting all the power in one person’s hands?” Alexii seems about to answer that with probably the answer ‘no’ however entering the room is Eric Sparrow who like a perv seems to scope down Aspen, before saying anything to her. ERIC SPARROW: “Personally, I think it’s a great idea. I mean who better to lead the fight then, Eric Sparrow? Thought behind every great leader is a great woman, so don’t think you wouldn’t be involved. But quite frankly I want the opportunity to go over to EPW, so I can get what I rightfully deserve which is my Galveston Island Championship. And trust me, your not going to stop me.” Sparrow grins at Aspen who seems a bit disgusted by some of his remarks as he walks backwards staring at her as he then turns the corner as Aspen turns to Alexii who says one last thing. ALEXII RAVYNOVA: "Regardless of what you think, it’s happening. So why don’t you go ahead and head out to the ring, I think I may come down and watch." Aspen glares at her and then turns and heads out of the room not thinking this was the time nor the place for such a fight as she head out of the office as Alexii Ravynova continues to sport a scheming smile as the segment ends. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = As usual the crowd is on the edge of their seats for another one of Revolt’s big time main event matches in which four of the biggest stars will compete for a chance to be able to invade EPW. Coming to the ring first to the tune "The Edge of Paradise" by Kamelot is Tony Miranda walking with focus to the ring as he gives a single fan a high five before heading up the steps and into the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “So who do you agree with Oliver, do you think we should have a Leader or not?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I’d step up, but I don’t want to take anyone’s shine.” Miranda heads to the ropes and hops up onto them throwing up his arms as "Impossible" by Anberlin hits and the crowd begins to boo as the GIW Women’s Champion walks out from the back heading towards the ring glaring out at the crowd. She has become a very willing member of the crusade against EPW; however it doesn’t appear that she is not going to try tonight as she then heads up the steps herself, coming in across from Miranda before holding up her belt to the crowd. RILEY STEVENS: “Come on, do you agree with Aspen or Alexii?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I think they are power hungry, just like the rest of you fools around here.” As Kacie lowers her belt and hands it to the outside “Fatlip” by Sum 41 hits and the crowd begins to boo again for the former number one contender to the GI Title, Eric Sparrow. He blows them off a bit as he heads into the ring, jumping over the ropes as he smirks at Kacie then to Miranda seeming ready to go tonight. RILEY STEVENS: “Who you think has this one tonight?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Eric Sparrow, clearly.” The ring is getting packed as "Bullseye" by Aly and AJ hits and the crowd begins to let out a big cheer as Aspen jumps out from the back and runs down high fiving all of the fans however as she’s half way down to the ring, Alexii Ravynova’s theme hits as she walks out from the back heading down around the ring as Aspen watches her the whole time, not feeling to comfortable by this. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Looks like we are being joined by Alexii Ravynova don’t have to carry you through commentating any more.” Alexii Ravynova has walked around the ring and grabs an extra set of headphones for herself as she sits down and gets set up as Aspen Chaud, continues to start at her. RILEY STEVENS: “Welcome Alexii, to the Announce Table.” ALEXII RAVYNOVA: "Hi Mike and pleasure to see you again Oliver. I’m out here to watch the Leader of our team over here on GIW." Back in the ring everyone but Aspen seems ready to go as the referee steps in between them and calls for the bell. DING DING DING! Kacie wastes little time in running across the ring and decking Aspen with a big punch knocking her to the mat as on the other side of the ring Sparrow and Miranda begin to lock up. Right away we have basically two matches in one as these four are ready to go at it. Church doesn’t let up instead mounting Aspen and starting to pound away at her, as the crowd is all at the end of their seats, knowing the weight this match holds. Miranda wins the test of strength quite easily forcing Sparrow into the corner however when he goes for a punch Sparrow avoids it and gives him a Drop Toe Hold into the ropes. While he is down Sparrow runs off the ropes and when Miranda gets to his feet and turns around he’s nailed with a ‘Pineapple Express’ a Corner Dropkick! The crowd boos him as Sparrow grins to the crowd, having just taken Miranda out for a little bit at least. On the other side of the ring Aspen is back up but she’s in the corner and she is being assaulted at this point and in a bit of irony when Aspen pushes her back and walks forward she is nailed with a Dropkick from Kacie, however Aspen is sent flying out of the ring. On the other side of the ring Sparrow has pulled Miranda out from under the ropes and goes for the cover 1…2…Kacie kicks him off. Sparrow gets up angrily staring at Kacie and beginning to berate her. However he suddenly seems to start checking her out, looking her up and down before getting to a knee and in a rather sexist act grabs her hand and kisses it. Kacie looks down at him as Sparrow, looks up at her smirking before he commands she hit the back. Church gets up and seems to pretend she’s going before turning and pulling him by his arm down to the mat and right into an Arm Trap Crossface! And in this case the fans seem to be showing more liking to Sparrow as if he didn’t know before Kacie is not a girl to be joked with. However with him having no where else to go it’s Tony Miranda who kicks Kacie off breaking the hold as Sparrow rolls out of the way. Miranda doesn’t seem completely willing to fight a girl as the two get up at the same time but his unwillingness hurts him as she now uses an Armbar Takedown into another Arm Trap Crossface! This could be it. RILEY STEVENS: “Looks like we may have our new leader right here.” It’s now Miranda who is seconds away from tapping when this time its Aspen making the last second save by basically tackling Kacie off of him before rolling with her out of the ring hard to the floor on the outside. Sparrow is back on his feet in the ring as he gets the kinks out of his neck and then sees that Miranda is easy pickings as he’s just now starting to stir. Sparrow waits for him to get up then does a big jumping dropkick that sends Miranda right out beside the fighting Kacie and Aspen. Miranda gets up as Aspen and Church continue to fight as Sparrow walks over to the ropes and grabs them, unknown to the three on the outside. He jumps up and then nails a big ‘Air Sparrow’ a Springboard Clothesline onto all three on the outside taking everybody out! The crowd is impressed regardless of who it is as these four competitors are putting on quite the show here tonight. The first people up are Aspen Chaud and Eric Sparrow both sliding into the ring nearly simultaneously. Sparrow runs across the ring and grabs her tossing her off the ropes. However she ducks his clothesline attempt and stops on a dime. But it turns out for the worst as instead she receives a big Superkick to the jaw. Eric seems poised to make the pin when Miranda now catches him from behind with a big axe handle slam to his back. Tony waits for him to get to his feet wanting to end this thing right here and right now. However when Sparrow gets to his feet he’s caught with a back kick to the jaw as Sparrow goes on to attack Aspen, with Miranda downed. He backs her up in the corner and in an act of desperation she kicks up and sends him to the mat as she then gets to the top rope. ALEXII RAVYNOVA: "One moment." Before Aspen can jump off Alexii hops up which catches Aspen’s eyes long enough for Sparrow to have gotten up and to catch her with a big kick to the face as she falls from the top rope while he heads to the top rope. On the other side it’s Kacie who is waiting for Miranda to get up and when he does she nails him with ‘The Ice Breaker’ an X-Factor! However on the other side of the ring Sparrow catches Aspen with a huge Spiral Tap! Both of them make the cover at the same time as the referee is forced to use both hands to make the count expecting perhaps one person to kick out 1…2…3! RILEY STEVENS: “Wait who just won?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “First Chicken winning now this, what is going on tonight.” The whole crowd is unsure about which of them just won and even Sparrow and Church are both celebrating, when they both realize what has happened and begin their pleas to the referee’s. Suddenly it’s Alexii Ravynova stepping between them and pushing the referee away as she raises both Kacie and Sparrow’s hand signifying a duel win. The crowd gives mixed reactions as Sparrow seems satisfied with his ability to now go to EPW to get Walker while Church seems hesitant of trusting Alexii while not happy to be sharing a win as we cut to the crowd who all seem a bit confused as we come back to Alexii raising there arms to end the show. WINNERS: ERIC SPARROW & KACIE CHURCH (Alexii's Decision)
