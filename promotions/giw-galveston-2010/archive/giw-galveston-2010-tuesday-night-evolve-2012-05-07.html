Tuesday Night Evolve, 7 May 2012

7 May 2012

GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: EVOLVE LIVE! From Barnhill Arena in Fayetteville, AR – May 7, 2012 QUOTE Trayton Deluca, Jason Dave, and Jamie Hayden are in the ring as the typical Evolve Battle Royal was ready to begin, the same match that the preshow ran every week. RILEY STEVENS: “We are here with the Evolve Commisioner and new Commentator, Jacob O’Connelly what do we have here tonight?” JACOB O’CONNELLY: “Three new talented stars, sure to make a name for themselves.” So many stars have emerged from the open battle royal and the three in the ring were looking to make their mark in GIW by winning the contest. DING DING DING! The bell sound and we are underway. The fans are split as all three men are fairly well received by the fans. Trayton wastes little time as Jason is on defense early, doing his best to exchange blows with the quick-on-his-feet Trayton Deluca. Jamie Hayden rushes in and hits a huge German suplex on Trayton that sends him half way across the ring, crashing to the mat on his stomach. Jason is now on the attack, hitting a standing front dropkick to the face of Jamie. The action is fast and furious in the beginning of the match and the fans are eating it up. RILEY STEVENS: “Quick pace here.” Jason walks over and picks Trayton up by the hair and attempts to toss him over the ropes but Trayton kicks back and struggles out of it. Jamie is back up and hits a clothesline to the back of Jason’s head. Trayton throws a right hook but Jamie catches it. Hip toss from Jamie Hayden and Trayton falls over the ropes to the floor, thus being eliminated. Trayton Deluca is Eliminated! Jamie turns around after having eliminated Trayton only to be met with a beautiful Whisper in the Wind by Jason Dave. The crowd erupts loudly for the move, which brings a smirk to the face of Jason. Jason begins to stomp away at Jamie and the crowd that was once behind him is no booing him out of the building. Jason shrugs it off and does his best to pick the much larger Jamie Hayden. Jamie is to his feet and is groggy. Jason attempts a Twist of Fate maneuver but Jason reverse it and hits a Belly to Back Suplex that sends Jason right over the ropes. Jamie Hayden is the victor here tonight in a very close encounter on Evolve! REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner…Jamie Hayden!” Hayden hops up to his feet, the match was quick but it was Hayden that left the victor as he allowed the referee to raise his arm in victory. RILEY STEVENS: “Big win for Jamie Hayden. But all three looked good tonight.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Well--” JACOB O’CONNELLY: “I always find good talent.” Oliver can be heard growling a bit as Hayden celebrates as we have one last commercial hyping up the matches here tonight. WINNER: JAMIE HAYDEN
