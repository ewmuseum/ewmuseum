GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: REVOLT Live from The Waterfront Event Center - March 27th, 2014 BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! Fireworks stream into the night sky above the Waterfront Event Center and the fans go wild. The third Revolt back from the hiatus is set to be one of the biggest ones yet and the fans are clearly ecstatic. GIW cameras pan around the audience, the ring, and the announce table, but something unusual is happening. A long line of security personnel are entering the area through the ramp and surrounding the ring. The voice of Riley Stevens can be heard now as the camera stays on the men. RILEY STEVENS: “Welcome to Revolt ladies and gentlemen… something really weird is going on.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I don’t know who these guys are or what they are doing here. This isn’t on the show rundown folks.” The crowd doesn’t really know what to think at this moment, all they can do is murmur and speculate as to what is happening. Just then Ian Mendoza’s music hit and their heads snapped towards the ramp. RILEY STEVENS: “Well, if there is one man who is going to know what this is all about, its Ian Mendoza.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Maybe Riley. Mendoza doesn’t seem to have too much control of the company right now.” RILEY STEVENS: “I think he is more in control than you think Oliver. After all, he is the physical embodiment of the man in charge – Mr. B! He put Wake Walker in his place last week.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “And what did Wake do later in the show? He put Alexis Terry through a table. We probably won’t ever see her again – and as far as I’m concerned Wake did that to put Mendoza, Seton, and Lynch in their place. Alexis Terry’s career is dead because of them, not because of Wake!” RILEY STEVENS: “You have to hold Wake Walker accountable. He made the choice to hurt Alexis Terry. He is to blame. I personally hope these security guards are here to keep Wake Walker in check.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “That would be a very foolish mistake Riley – but Mendoza is a fool so you may be right.” RILEY STEVENS: “I bet you wont say that louder Ollie. You’re scared of Mendoza just like Walker is.” Ian Mendoza has made his way into the ring and grabbed a microphone. The security guards allowed him to pass through without incident, so most of the audience has come to the conclusion that these men were hired by Mendoza himself. The music stops and the crowd becomes silent. IAN MENDOZA: “My father used to tell me as a kid that rules were meant to be followed. He would tell me that someone wiser than me, someone superior to me, made them with the intentions of keeping me safe. I respect rules and rule makers, but evidently some people in this company never were taught that valuable lesson. So, today, I am going to take a step back from being a general manager to become a teacher. This show will run smoothly, the rules will be followed, and the fans will get to see what they actually paid for – good wrestling!” The crowd applauds Mendoza, but some of them still seem skeptical. IAN MENDOZA: “I hired these men to stand at ringside tonight to make sure that the number one contenders match between Wake Walker and Christopher Darren Erns is a fair and clean bout. Wake Walker’s hired body guards have been banned from the event tonight, and I have coordinated with the Galveston Police Department to ensure that they do not enter the premises. As for the men outside the ring, they are the last resort to keep people out – but also the first responders to keep people inside the ring. CDE and Wake Walker are amazing athletes; I know this to be true. Tonight, we will see a wrestling match – not a circus event.” The crowd cheers louder as Mendoza nods, proud of himself. IAN MENDOZA: “I will be at ringside tonight on commentary with Oliver and Riley so that I can watch this match unfold completely live. I’m excited to see what real wrestling is all about. So, without any more interruptions, let’s get this show on the road!” The crowd cheers as Mendoza climbs out of the ring and goes through the line of security guards, patting one of them on the shoulder with a smile as he takes his seat at the announce table. The crowd anxiously awaits the music to hit. QUOTE "The Waiting Makes Me Curious" by Fear Before is the music that hits turning these fans cheers into boos in a hurry as Wake Walker a man who has really done everything he can do to get these fans against him, walks out towards the ring this time without the Bodyguard’s that have aided him so much in his hunt for a shot at the Undisputed Championship. And without them? He looks a lot less confident heading into the match that could get him that shot as he walks through the group of Security on his way towards the ring these fans booing the hell out of him as he walks up the steps not looking his usual over confident self as he gets into the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Wake seems a little nervous tonight Oliver, funny right? Take away Butch and Michael and suddenly he’s a whole lot less scary isn’t he?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “You people act like he needs them, he’s a former champion for a reason and he’ll prove it tonight.” IAN MENDOZA: “We’ll see won’t we Oliver?” Walker walks around the ring, looking out at the security team around the ring as "Running Under" by King Black Acid hits and these fans explode for the dark horse that many thought wouldn’t make it out of the first round and has proven himself a worthy title contender in Christopher Darren Erns. As he walks out he is followed by Tony Miranda, who promised to be at ringside in case anything happened. These fans are fully behind him in this thing wanting nothing more then for him to enter the fray with Lynch, Seton, and Walker and be the one that walks out of it all with the belt. He high fives a few fans but isn’t quite as fan friendly as usual something they don’t seem to mind knowing the task at hand as he makes his walk up the ramp looking out at the security with a smirk. RILEY STEVENS: “This has to favor CDE, Walker has no help and is going to have to fight here with CDE. Something that really doesn’t favor many.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “He’s got Tony a Security team, and Ian here. Seems a little unfair to Wake…” IAN MENDOZA: “Give me a break’.” Tony takes his spot among the Security team, knowing that even though Walker’s men are gone he’s always got something up his sleeve. CDE steps into the ring knowing he’s facing a former champion tonight but knowing that it’s who he has to beat to get the next title shot. These fans have started a bit of a CDE chant now as he stands across from Wake, still looking nervous as the match gets started. DING DING DING! The chant dies down as the bell rings, these fans just ready to see how these two stack up in a singles match with no distractions. As Walker walks around the ring his hand on the ropes looking out at the Security team, Tony Miranda, and Ian Mendoza all around the ring in some way or another as he turns back to Erns who grins “No where to run.” Wake laughs it off then tries to catch him off guard looking to get a hold of him but Erns catches him on his way, the two ending up locking up instead with a tie up as Wake does his best to try to push CDE back but Christopher has to be one of the stronger competitors in the ring, as he pushes him back all the way to the ropes then using those same ropes to springboard him off, as Walker bounces off the ropes and heads back at CDE who telegraphs the European Uppercut a bit too much and Wake ducks down under it stopping and kicking CDE in the knee dropping him down to one of his own as Wake bounces back hitting the ropes and landing a Big Boot to Erns while he was checking on his knee. Christopher falls to the mat, as Wake now regains his swagger standing up and smirking yet again as he walks around the ring pointing at Tony and Ian and saying something about them not stopping him, as he walks back over to CDE who is already back up as he Clothesline’s him down to the mat, with one then as Erns make the mistake of bouncing right back up he eats a second clothesline yet again sending him to the mat as a smirking Wake is starting to get control now as he walks around Erns as he doesn’t bounce up right away this time. These fans getting a little discouraged at first as Wake wipes his hands and walks over to him, mounting him and throwing a few punches to his feet while he has him downed as Erns tries his best to defend himself but Wake really has it going now as CDE is able to push him off the two rushing to their feet only for Wake to get their first and land a quick Big Boot that drops him right back down as Wake looks down and grabs a hold of Erns and gets set up and to the dismay of the crowd he gets the Boston Crab locked in, in the middle of the ring as Erns really is taking a beating here as these fans are in shock at first but they don’t give up, as Christopher doesn’t either. Wake again jumps right back up and for the second time straight gets another Shoulder Block. Still not there as after a big knee, he knows he’ll need some more to get the job done as Walker on a little wobbly legs makes his way to his feet yet again as it’s Erns turn to have all the momentum now as he calls for him to get up, thinking big but at the last second Wake slides under the ropes. IAN MENDOZA: “Bad call Wake…” Thinking he can get a breather in, he is rudely awakened as several of the security members surround him and push him right back into the ring as these fans cheer them on as CDE waits for him to get up and he then turns right into a the Muay Thai Clinch where Erns lands a few knees and elbows before pushing Walker off the ropes and then right into ‘The Uppercut from Hell’ launching him up then catching him with a vicious European Uppercut as these fans get to their feet sensing the end, as he jumps down on top of Walker 1…2…Kick Out! At the last second Walker shows why despite his antics he is still one of the best, kicking out there as Erns gets up a huge adrenaline dump after thinking he had it there as he gets to his feet shaking it off knowing he can do more as he calls for Wake to get up as Tony and the crowd are all waiting to see the end, as Wake slowly makes his way to his feet. Walker who has recovered holds his cheek and slides over to the corner hiding as the remaining five Security members turn their attention to Tony, but he is fresh and he holds up the chair to buy some time. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner by way of disqualification and therefore the new GIW Undisputed Championship Number One Contender…Wake Walker!” These fans are in shock as is Erns and Miranda both of whom quickly approach the referee trying to explain what happened but still rocked from that brutal clothesline from Erns he isn’t having it, countering that Tony did land the move on Wake as these fans boos couldn’t be louder as this new scheme by RILEY STEVENS: “Wow, Wake found a way to get it done, apparently…maybe buying off the Security team?
