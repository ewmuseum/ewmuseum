Rydell comes form behind and hit’s a backbreaker on Wake. Rydell hasn’t noticed Wake yet and goes for another cover which Stylez obviously kicks out of. By that time, Wake has enters the ring. Rydell gets up and is hit right on the face with the glass disco ball, causing him to bleed and scatter pieces of glass all over the place. The match ends. Stylez has been disqualified. Wake then proceeds to hit Stylez across the head with the last disco ball. Wake stands over both men and begins to celebrate, jumping up and down and mockingly dancing. The crowd approves. He gets on one of the turnbuckles and starts yelling at the crowd, “You never interfere in MY matches, baby!” as the show comes to a close.
