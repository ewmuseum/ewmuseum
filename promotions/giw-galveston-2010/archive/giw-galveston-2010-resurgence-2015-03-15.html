GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS SUNDAY NIGHT REVOLT: RESURGENCE LIVE! From Kauffman Arena in Galveston, TX -- March 15th, 2015 BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! Blue and white fireworks shoot into the Galveston sky outside of Kauffman Arena and the fans are ecstatic to be back on the island. The capacity crowd of 1,000 screaming GIW fans are on their feet as “Raised by Wolves” by Family Force 5 blasts over the PA system. The camera continues to pan around the crowd, focusing in on signs such as "#BECKFOLLOWBACK" and "RESURGENCE", as well as intense reactions, while the familiar voices of Riley Stevens and Oliver Thibodaux can be heard. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Welcome back to Galveston Island Wrestling! Coming to you live on Fox Sports Southwest!” RILEY STEVENS: “We are back on the air after a four month hiatus and, Ollie, it is going to be one hell of a night! We have a lot of talent we are going to throw at you tonight, some of them you recognize, others you will be seeing for the very first time. There are a lot of things changing in our world right now, but one thing will always stay the same – Galveston Island Wrestling will DARE TO DEFY!” “Did it on ‘em” by Nicki Minaj begins playing and the crowd explodes in a mix of cheers and boos. This is the theme music of Aspen Chaud, a woman who throughout her career has only been seen in Galveston Island Wrestling. The music continues to play with no sign of Aspen as the commentators begin speculating. RILEY STEVENS: “Well we all know this music; Aspen Chaud is in the building. But where is she?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Stupid question Riley. If her music is going to play, she is going to be here. The real question is WHAT is Aspen doing here at the Kauffman Arena?” RILEY STEVENS: “Well, by the sound of it the fans are clearly wondering the same thing. Throughout her time in GIW Aspen Chaud has been both a hero and villain, but like her or not no one can say she isn’t an icon.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “She was inducted into the GIW Hall of Fame with the first class! That’s guys like Tony Miranda and Alioth Starre – and don’t forget about Alexis Terry!” As soon as Oliver finished, Aspen Chaud stepped out from behind the curtain. At this point, the boos were completely overpowered by the cheers. She walked onto the ramp wearing a blue dressed that matched the blue ring mat and the blue ropes for Revolt. But the really interesting part was what was over her shoulder. RILEY STEVENS: “What the hell? Aspen has the GIW Undisputed Championship?!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “If anyone deserves to be the champion, it is Aspen. Let’s just be honest with ourselves Riley. She is the best this company has ever seen.” RILEY STEVENS: “Well that is a debatable claim, but even if she is the most deserving, Christopher Darren Erns was champion when we went off the air and I know he has resigned with the company. As far as I’m concerned, it is still his belt.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Erns is a chump. But as far as you know, Aspen is just bringing the belt back to hand it to him right there in the ring. You know, a symbolic gesture.” RILEY STEVENS: “Well forgive me for not always assuming Aspen Chaud has the best intentions. She has ruined so many lives.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Ah well, you are only as good as the number of people you have ruined.” Aspen stepped into the ring as her music drew to a close. She smiled as the crowd changed “GAL-VES-TON” at her. The ring announcer handed her a microphone and she began to speak. ASPEN CHAUD: “It is great to be home in GALVESTON, TEXAS!” The crowd loves the cheap heat. Aspen smiles and nods some more. ASPEN CHAUD: “Nearly five years ago I stepped foot in this ring for the first time and I became the GIW Champion. The belt looked a little bit different, the crowd was much smaller, and my legacy was only just beginning. Since then I have established myself as one of the best to ever compete in this promotion: a grand slam champion and a GIW Hall of Famer. So now that Fox Sports has a majority stake in the company and they are broadcasting our show on Fox Sports Southwest every Sunday night, they wanted a big star to run the program. That is right – I am the new Commissioner of Revolt.” “King of the World” by Porcelain and the Tramps hits the PA system and the fans erupt in boos while Alex Castellanos steps out onto the ramp. For a moment, he looks around confused, but then begins to make his way down to the ring. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Listen to these ungrateful fans! The UWL World Heavyweight Champion is gracing them with his presence, and yet they boo him.” RILEY STEVENS: “Well, they have been watching Alex as he tours with the UWL and with ACE. I highly doubt there is a single person left on this planet that wouldn’t boo him.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I am still here Alex! I wouldn’t ever boo you!” By this point Alex has made his way into the ring. His music begins to cut out as he approaches Aspen with a smile. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “How great that you are back. You know, I really thought that you had gone –“ Aspen holds a finger into the air. ASPEN CHAUD: “Alex, we need to make something very clear right from the beginning. You and I have a lot of history, but that does not give you the right to interrupt me as soon as I start speaking.” The crowd lets out an audible “ooo” as Alex chuckles. ASPEN CHAUD: “I suggest that you step out of the ring and learn to respect your superiors.” Alex holds the mic up to respond. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “My superiors? Aspen, what right do you have to stand here and call yourself my superior? If the network is looking for someone to manage the show week to week, there are many men and women from GIW’s history who are more qualified to that than you. I see no reason why I should place my career in your hands after you tried to end it so many times in the past.” The two GIW legends adjust the belts over their shoulders. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “And I really take issue with the fact that you have the GIW Undisputed Championship thrown over your shoulder right now. Unless you plan to hand that belt to me, you should put it back in the case. But I am the most qualified to come back to GIW as our inaugural champion. If you doubt that, take a look at this belt over my shoulder right now. To earn it, I beat one of the best wrestlers in the world, and I have no doubts I can beat anyone that GIW tries to throw at me.” Aspen holds the belt out to Alex. He pauses for a moment, as if he doesn't really take her offer seriously. The fans begin to chant “CDE, CDE, CDE” as Alex reaches for the belt, but quickly Aspen pulls it away from him. “Hit Me Like a Man” hits the PA and Alexis Terry begins to make her way to the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Things are heating up now. Former GIW Champion, and GIW Hall of Famer, Alexis Terry makes her way down the ramp!” Aspen rolled her eyes as Terry came into the ring and offered to shake her hand, then pulled it away like Aspen had done just before. Terry crosses the ring and shakes hands with Alex. ALEXIS TERRY: “Man, it is sure good to be back inside Kauffman Arena… the House that Terry built!” The fans aren’t buying that claim. Terry is amused. ALEXIS TERRY: “I was watching from the back and I saw the belt and heard some discussion… and I just felt like I was being invited down to the ring. And Alex, you and I have become very close since GIW last went off the air. We have toured the states as the Master Race, and I was right there when you won the UWL title… you would be a very deserving champion.” The crowd doesn’t want to hear that. They erupt in boos. ALEXIS TERRY: “But Alex, with all due respect, your title reign ended because of me. Remember that? So you’re certainly more deserving than Aspen, but I’m more deserving than you.” A cold look comes over Alex’s face. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “You did not do that on your own. You didn’t pin me because you were better than me. You had David Sinclair and some brass knuckles. You can’t call yourself a champion.” "They Are Lost" by Last Remaining Pinnacle hits the PA now and the fans begin to cheer loudly. Christopher Darren Erns steps out onto the ramp wringing his hands. Slowly emerging from behind him is Michael Lynch. The last time we saw these two was the show after On Hallowed Ground where the duo celebrated Christopher Darren Erns' Undisputed title victory. The two make their way down the ramp, despite their personas - the GIW faithful give them a big pop for interrupting the self-proclaimed 'Master Race'. Christopher grabs a mic and taps it a few times to make sure it is live. Michael Lynch looks on over his shoulder. CHRISTOPHER DARREN ERNS: “Terry, Alex –“ Alex is quick to interrupt. If anyone has a right to call it their own, it is me.” Alexis goes to speak into her microphone but Erns continues - cutting her off. He stares at both her and Alex. CHRISTOPHER DARREN ERNS: “Both of you left this company the last time around and went else where. You guys tried finding success in other federations because of the stench this company had when it was under Auva Skye's control. Yet somehow, you two thought you could adopt the GIW Master Race moniker? That's bullshit.” The crowd cheers, agreeing with Erns. CHRISTOPHER DARREN ERNS: “Now you come back and demand to be put into title contention... No, I won't allow that. I am a GIW loyalist, I've stuck by this company through everything, and I'll damned if I let you two come in here and start acting like you're owed something!” Aspen stepped between the three stars now. ASPEN CHAUD: “Look. No one is getting this belt. It is not mine, and it is not yours, or yours, or yours. If I am going to be in charge of the show from now on, I am going to make one very bold statement. Former GIW Undisputed Champions will NOT be in contention for the belt again until we crown someone new. The same faces over and over again is not good business, and I am going to avoid that for as long as possible. But since you are all in the ring, we might as well start the opening match now.” Aspen lets out a smile as a referee slides into the ring. The four unwillingly head to their corners. The new commissioner of Galveston Island Wrestling begins to leave the ring, however not before giving one last glare to the silent Michael Lynch, who has remained completely static throughout this whole confrontation.
