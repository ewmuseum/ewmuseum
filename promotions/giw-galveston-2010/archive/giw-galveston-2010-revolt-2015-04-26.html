GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS SUNDAY NIGHT REVOLT LIVE! From Kauffman Arena in Galveston, TX – April 26th, 2015 This week, Revolt begins with a cold open. “Did it on em” is playing as Aspen Chaud stands behind a simple wooden table with a microphone in hand. ASPEN CHAUD: "Ladies and gentlemen, I announced last week that we would be seeing a contract signing for the GIW Deep South Championship match that will take place next week at Exile. Two of GIW’s finest will square off in just seven days. Without further ado, please welcome… the number one contender… Alexis Terry!" “Off With Her Head” hits the PA system as Alexis Terry steps onto the ramp to a chorus of boos from the fans. Following closely behind her are Bronx Valescence and Blade Vega with David Sinclair standing at her side. Terry is used to the boos, so she doesn’t pay them much attention. Bronx Valescence is living it up however, as Sinclair and Vega march forward with absolute focus. As the faction nears the ring, Blade Vega holds the ropes open for Alexis and Sinclair goes to climb the ropes. Terry calls out to her manager/legal consultant. ALEXIS TERRY: "No, David. You stay outside the ring." Terry finishes climbing in as David slowly moves back down the stairs. He is confused and disheartened. Terry moves towards Aspen. ASPEN CHAUD: "And the champion… Chris Callum!" “Dignity” by New Politics begins to play as Chris Callum steps onto the ramp wearing a gray GIW t-shirt over his ring gear. The Deep South title is tossed over one shoulder, the Undisputed title over the other. He makes his way down to the ring as the fans chant his name. He exchanges a glance to Bronx and Vega before brushing past Sinclair to climb into the ring. ASPEN CHAUD: "Chris, if you don’t mind placing the Deep South Championship on the table…" Chris takes a moment to process – he had been staring at Aspen. The two have a quite convoluted relationship after all. Chris pulled the title belt off of his shoulder and raised it high in the air towards the face of an annoyed Alexis Terry as the fans cheered. He then placed it on the table. ALEXIS TERRY: "Act like you’ve been here before." Callum offers a small laugh as he grabs a microphone from the table. CHRIS CALLUM: "Someone as cocky as you getting upset over a little bit of gloating? This is not the Alexis we know so well." Aspen moves towards the contract on the table as Callum laughs at Terry’s expense. ALEXIS TERRY: "You laugh now, but good luck being able to do anything after this Sunday." Chris nodded as Aspen handed a pen to the champion. CHRIS CALLUM: "You and I have gone round and round for the past few weeks, and it will end at Exile. I’m looking forward to putting you in my rear view." Terry smiled as Callum signed the paper. ALEXIS TERRY: "Look at you. You didn’t even read it. You don’t have a care in the world, do you? You honestly think you’re superman. Well Chris, I hate to break it to you, but, and pardon the cliché. I am your kryptonite. It doesn’t matter if you are Aspen Chaud or Alioth Starre, I always find a way to win." Terry grabs the contract and hands it to Sinclair. CHRIS CALLUM: "It is a basic contract. We have read these every week since GIW started." Terry turns back towards the champion. ALEXIS TERRY: "I want to make sure you and Aspen aren’t manipulating any of the rules." Aspen laughs, she goes to speak, but Callum cuts her off. CHRIS CALLUM: "You really think Aspen Chaud is going to help me out?" Aspen quickly turns to Chris with a shocked look on her face. CHRIS CALLUM: "My gut instinct says she is working with you. First, she belittled the men who came to my aid when you attacked me. Second, she gave Johnny Violence his dream match against me at ETV. Third, she gave you this title shot. If Aspen is working for me, then she is going a horrible job." Aspen moves towards Chris. ASPEN CHAUD: "I have done my best to protect you. Because that is what every wrestler on this roster deserves: protection. When people like Alexis decide to go and form factions, I need to do something. And what have I accomplished? I got The Sir and Johnny Violence away from her. I’ve managed to appease everyone. I think I have done a fantastic job. Have I been perfect, no. Of course not. Several times I let my emotions get the better of me – but what did you tell me three weeks ago backstage? You’re not perfect but you’re not your mistakes. I hope you can look past my faults." Chris narrows his eyes as Alexis begins to laugh. ALEXIS TERRY: "Look at you two. This romantic tension is so obvious. Chris, you wouldn’t be standing here today if it wasn’t for Aspen shoving you down all of our collective throats – you should be on your knees thanking her right now." Aspen pivots towards Alexis. ASPEN CHAUD: "I haven’t forced anyone to the top here in GIW. I’m trying to make this a place where people can achieve everything they are capable of. People like you make that difficult." Alexis offers a wide grin. ALEXIS TERRY: "I’m weeding out the weak. And Chris Callum is weak." The fans boo. CHRIS CALLUM: "We will find out who is weak at Exile, if you ever sign the contract." Sinclair sticks the contract through the ropes to Alexis Terry with a nod and a thumbs up. Terry snatches it and signs it. ALEXIS TERRY: "It is done. Khalessi is coming to Westeros, Chris." With a smile, Chris picks up the Deep South Championship and slings it over his shoulder once again. CHRIS CALLUM: "On that note, I have a surprise for you, Alexis. Since all of this trying to kill me has been about becoming a triple crown champion, I decided I would try to beat you to it. I have the Undisputed title, as well as the Deep South title. I want to go for the TV title, right here, next. I’m gonna take what you want right out from under your nose." Terry begins a slow clap as the fans cheer. She turns to Bronx on the outside. ALEXIS TERRY: "Kill him, Bronx." Bronx Valescence, who enters the TV title match every week, hops onto the apron. Callum’s music begins to play as we fade into commercial with the TV title match up next. QUOTE The first match of tonight's Revolt is for the GIW Television Championship. The current champion Gaylord Prime stands in the corner as he looks over to his belt at ringside. This week he'll be defending his championship against current Deep South and Undisputed Champion Chris Callum looking to make a clean sweep along with consistent contender for the title in Bronx Valescence. These three men are set to square off as the referee calls for the match to begin SARAH SHARP: "The action is about to get underway as Gaylord Prime is primed to defend his Television Championship against Bronx Valescence and Chris Callum this week. I'd argue that this is his toughest challenge yet in Galveston." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Are you seriously calling pretty boy Callum a tough challenge? That's a joke, Sarah, and you know it!" SARAH SHARP: "And you have to give Bronx respect. Week after week he puts himself in this match with one goal in mind. Winning the Television Championship. You may not like him, his methods, or the people he associates with, but you have to respect his passion for this belt." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "How can you not like the people he associates with? A former three Undisputed Champion, one of the sharpest minds in the business today, and one of the biggest guys on this planet! Add Bronx's energy and you've got yourself a formula for success." DING DING DING! With the match underway, Callum and Bronx are the first to lock up leaving Gaylord to sit back and pace himself in the match. There's definitely some beef from these two athletes as they forget the lock up and start to trade forearm strikes. Bronx seems to be the better striker of the two as he starts to back Callum up to the ropes with the forearms. Once there, Bronx whips Callum off the ropes and Chris surprises him on the rebound with an arm drag. Bronx gets up and is tossed with a second one, but he's smart enough to slide out of the ring for the oncoming third one. Gaylord steps up to the plate and looks to get into a collar-and-elbow tie with Callum, but Chris tosses the Television Champion for the third arm drag! SARAH SHARP: "Vintage Callum right there." SARAH SHARP: "You don't need to be a wrestler to know those chops hurt." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "And it's even better when they're unleashed on Callum!" SARAH SHARP: "Oh, come on, Oliver." With Callum trying to get back up, Valescence uses his chance to pin Prime OLIVER THIBODAUX: "We've got ourselves a new champion in three seconds!" 1... 2... NO! Chris is able to pull Valescence off of the champion after the two count. Grabbing Bronx by the hair, Callum hits a short-arm clothesline on Valescence to send him back down on the mat. Callum grimaces in pain as he is slowly picked up by Prime and sent head first with a snap DDT. Bronx's luck is no different because as soon as he starts to get up, Prime drops him with a snap DDT as well. The Television Champion is in control for the first time during the match and is beaming with energy. SARAH SHARP: "Prime is on fire right now!" Bronx is yelling in pain as he tries to reach out for a ring rope, but unfortunately for him, he's inches away from grabbing one. Callum uses his ring awareness to look at the situation and get back on his feet using the ropes. However, he doesn't stop there as he heads up to the top turnbuckle and without hesitation, he leaps off with a frog splash across Gaylord Prime! OLIVER THIBODAUX: "At least Bronx didn't tap...not that he would!" Bronx's hold on Chris stays as he locks his arms around Chris' waist and sends him flying across the ring with a high angled German suplex. The suplex rocks Chris, but he still manages to get back on his feet. Valescence charges and hits a running dropkick, but Chris managed to roll out of the way at the last second. As Chris dodges the attack, the referee isn't as lucky as he's blasted in the face with Bronx's dropkick. Valescence stands over the fallen referee as Callum looks at the damage caused. SARAH SHARP: "The referee is out and...what's this?" OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Oh yes!" Perhaps this was part of a plan as Blade Vega and David Sinclair make their way down the ramp! Gaylord Prime and Chris Callum stands next to one another as they watch Vega and Sinclair climb into the ring with Valescence. It seems like a war is about to erupt as Gaylord and Chris charge Blade and Bronx. Gaylord surprises Vega with a series of chops across the big man's chest, but they don't seem to do any real damage. Sinclair manages to get behind Prime and hits him with a low blow! Prime is clutching the family jewels after the strike and it doesn't seem like he'll have a lot of time to recover once Vega destroys him with a huge spear! Sinclair slides out of the ring after the damage he caused, leaving Blade in the ring to finish off Callum. OLIVER THIBODAUX: "I love it, Sarah! This is the happiest day of my life! Callum is next!" On the other side of the ring, a European uppercut dazes Callum and it looks like Bronx is going to repeat what happened with Prime. Valescence holds Chris in the proper position and allows Vega to set up for another spear. It looks like it will be executed flawlessly until Chris manages to hit Bronx with a forearm and send him into Vega's spear! SARAH SHARP: "Blade Vega just destroyed Bronx Valescence!" OLIVER THIBODAUX: "NO!" Vega stands with a look of surprise as he sees the fallen Valescence underneath him and doesn't get much time to reflect as he gets sent over the top rope with a high angled dropkick from Chris. With Prime out of the match, it appears like it will be a regular match. Blade tries to get back in the ring, but Sinclair holds him back as the referee starts to get back into the match. Callum seizes the opportunity in front of him and hooks Bronx's legs for the pin as the referee groggily gets over to make the count OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Not like this!" 1... 2... 3! SARAH SHARP: "And just like that, we open up Revolt with a brand new champion." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "This is the worst day of my life, Sarah. I can't believe this. Callum has to CHEAT to win here in GIW. It sickens me. And listen to these fans!" The Main Event Monarchy's plan has failed as Chris Callum stands up as the victor of this week's Television Championship match. Blade and Sinclair take Bronx out of the ring as Callum is handed his third championship in less than two months from the referee. Despite the interference, Chris was able to overcome the odds and become victorious. He walks over to Prime and pats him on the chest; thanking him for the match before he heads over to the ropes and watches the Monarchy sulk back up the ramp. WINNER: CHRIS CALLUM (NEW GIW TELEVISION CHAMPION) As we return from commercial, we see Alexis Terry fuming. She is pacing back and forth as the other members of the Main Event Monarchy look on. ALEXIS TERRY: "Are you all completely incompetent!? This is ridiculous!" Sinclair, peering back and forth, decides to say something… DAVID SINCLAIR: "Alexis…" Terry explodes in his face. ALEXIS TERRY: "No! David. I’m not gonna have it any more! Chris Callum just won a triple crown before I did. BEFORE I DID, David! That was the entire point of this stable!" Sinclair offers a hand towards Bronx. DAVID SINCLAIR: "Well if Bronx hadn’t gotten pinned…" Bronx, who had been looking down up to this point, suddenly pops up, removing his glasses as he does. BRONX VALESCENCE: "Hey… hey… WHOA. You guys were supposed to have my back tonight." Vega shakes his head. BLADE VEGA: "We did have your back, and you couldn’t get it done." Sinclair nods in agreement. DAVID SINCLAIR: "I agree with Blade completely. This falls on Bronx." Terry points a finger in Sinclair’s face. ALEXIS TERRY: "THIS FALLS ON ALL OF YOU." After a moment Bronx chimes in. BRONX VALESCENCE: "I had the match in the bag. I knocked both of those guys out cold. I was dominating the entire thing." Vega stares at Bronx with contempt. BLADE VEGA: "You’re kidding me." Terry is barely listening. She is too angry right now. ALEXIS TERRY: "It’s simple. This isn’t hard. Our plan is NOT complicated." Sinclair attempts to calm down Alexis - DAVID SINCLAIR: "Just tell us what you want us to do." Terry laughs and brushes her hair back. ALEXIS TERRY: "Tonight – if Johnny Violence and The Sir walk out of Kauffman with a win, there will be heads rolling. Got that? They DO NOT win their matches tonight." Vega nods. BLADE VEGA: "I can handle that Alexis." She clearly doesn’t trust Vega’s judgment. ALEXIS TERRY: "You ALL will handle it." There is a pause for a moment. DAVID SINCLAIR: "What about Callum?" Terry shrugs. ALEXIS TERRY: "Just leave Callum to me. Tonight, I’ll hurt that guy. The rest – Sir and Violence… you all better deal with." She storms off. Sinclair follows her. Bronx is next, but he notices Blade glaring at him. BRONX VALESCENCE: "What are you looking at?" Blade moves towards Bronx, who flinches and runs away towards Sinclair and Terry off screen. We fade in on Blade before we cut to the ring. QUOTE "The 11th Hour” by Rancid plays on the public announce system as we return from the commercial break. QUOTE As we come back from commercial break, our next match is set to begin. Gaylord Prime and Ashley O'Ryan are set to compete in a Beat The Clock match.
