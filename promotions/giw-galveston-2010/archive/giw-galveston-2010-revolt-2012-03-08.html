GIW — Card

Revolt, 8 March 2012

8 March 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: THURSDAY NIGHT REVOLT LIVE! From Lake Charles Civic Center in Lake Charles, LA – March 8, 2012 BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! The pyro goes off here in the Lake Charles Civic Center where the place is nearly completely full only a few areas taped off but the seats that are full already with fans coming back from the break before Evolve as they go crazy in their seats with the opening of the show, that is until "Did It On 'Em" by Nicki Minaj hits and those cheers quickly turn to boos as Aspen Chaud walks out from the back with that bright smirk wearing a white dress with matching heels, not yet in ring gear as she heads toward the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Here is our Undisputed Champion, Aspen Chaud who later teams with Nathan Bailey against Beck Ramsey and Cassidy Hightower in the Main Event. But we understand she has something to say here tonight.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “And she has that right, she’s the champion.” She walks past the fans who throw insults her way, letting it bounce off her shiny Undisputed Championship as she heads up the steps reaching down and grabbing a mic on the side of the ring as she steps through the two bottom ropes walking into the ring and walking around it as she lets the fans eventually run out of steam when she kicks it off. ASPEN CHAUD: “I am not out here to talk about Alexandra Rossi’s skinny untalented ass, though I just mentioned her tonight more then she ever will get on Revolt. And I am not out here to talk about Alexis Terry and I’s issues or how pathetic Cassidy and Beck are. I am out here for a different reason, that reason being you fans and your disrespectful ways. I am the greatest champion to ever hold this belt; I have brought this company to new heights. I made this company into the fledging thing it is today, yet you know what I hear every week? You are not the true champion…” A big scowl crawls over her face as she looks out at the crowd who cheer her last words, agreeing with her after all as she repositions the belt on her shoulder. ASPEN CHAUD: “I defeated Tony Miranda…I defeated Alex Castellanos…Yet they continue to bring up the name of one man…Alioth Starre.” Aspen again lowers the mic with that same angry glare out at that crowd as they go crazy for their star, pun intended. ASPEN CHAUD: “Really? Alioth Starre lost the belt and left as far as I am concerned and now he returns having done absolutely nothing and he gets the next shot at my belt? It is unfair. He doesn’t deserve the shot, I am the true CHAMPION and I am the star of this show--” Suddenly a voice plays over the speakers that gets hers and everyone else’s attention. ALIOTH STARRE: “Aspen, Aspen, Aspen.” Alioth emerged from behind the curtain and stepped to the edge of the stage, a microphone in hand. He held a hand to his temple and shook his head, as if exhausted. ALIOTH STARRE: “This again? Really? I understand you’re the third time Undisputed Champion here, but I’m also the longest reigning one. I’m not going to pander to your previous insults that I don’t do anything here, nor anything else to that effect. It doesn’t affect me. I will, however, state that you are not the show. There are individuals in the audience and those that enjoy the product elsewhere that are the show. Without them, you wouldn’t have the shoes, or hairstyle that you have. Without them, you’d be nowhere. Secondly, we are the show. Everyone of us that go out into that ring and perform to the best, or worst of our abilities. Without us, you wouldn’t have anyone to work off of and win that title. I may not have done anything to deserve the title shot yet, with the exception of my own name being that ‘it’ factor of it all, but you haven’t done anything to deserve the status you have with the exception of longevity.” Alioth looks down at her in the ring as the fans clap him on as Aspen now turns her body to face him angrily glaring up at him as she responds to him, Alioth walking further down towards the ring as he talks. ASPEN CHAUD: “Really Alioth? You seem to be mis-informed. Without ME there would not be any of you around here. I made this company what it is, I am the one that carries it every single week; I am the one that made this place a success. You think I care about your title reign Alioth? I think I and all these fans are thankful that it is finally over. I mean sure you were dominant, but this company didn’t go anywhere with you as the champion. This place needs me as the champion, I am the one that will carry it into the next era and you will be forgotten along the way. Alioth your days are numbered soon I am going to expose you as being beatable and when I do, you are going to crumble. This show is about Aspen Chaud not Alioth Starre.” Alioth folded his arms, trying to figure out the situation between he and Aspen, he walked through the ropes and then replied. ALIOTH STARRE: “Well, Ms. Chaud, you certainly do carry your weight around the backstage locker room as well as out here in the ring. I'll give you that, and I don't just mean the obvious insults, but the unwavering compliments that go with it. However, you've never really defeated me in a one-on-one bout. Sure, tag, but we can both agree I've never been good with teams. The last tag team I put myself in collapsed before it even hit the road. That is beside the point, however. The point of the matter is this: You are the most dominant female in this company, and I'm the most dominant male. You've yet to defeat me, and I've been itching for another title reign. I think you get where we're going with this. You can complain about me being here, or me getting a shot, or this, and that; etc, but you thrive on the hunt and deep down inside, you're scared of losing that title to me, because you know when I do get it, it's mine for good!” The crowd goes wild as he lowers the mic; they were after all in favor of that idea as Aspen looked around at them annoyed with their response as she answers back as Alioth now stood across from her. ASPEN CHAUD: “You think I am scared of you? I have gotten ten times better then I was when we met in the ring back at ‘Moment of Glory II’ and frankly you…you haven’t improved a bit. While I was beating Alex…Tony…and all the others you were sitting on a couch watching me. This is not just some rematch for me…Alex and Tony were the start but Alioth when I defeat you there will be no more questions only one simple truth, I AM BETTER THEN YOU. And I am the greatest wrestler in GIW HISTORY! So Alioth this rematch won’t be anything like the first one because when it’s all said and done I am going to make you do something you have NEVER done…TAP OUT!” Alioth does not waver at all, though she screams it out he does not show any fear as he replies back with some happiness. ALIOTH STARRE: “If I recall in our last meeting it was you who tapped.” Aspen angrily looks at him as he had pulled the trump card but with Alioth now just feet away from her she throws the mic away and then slaps him across the face hard before bailing out of the ring dropping her belt along the way. RILEY STEVENS: “Cheap shot by Aspen, typical.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “She has every right, he was being a prick.” Aspen jumps to the outside as Alioth turns around holding his cheek as he eyes the belt that Aspen dropped on her way out of the ring, quickly she grabs it hugging it tightly to her body walking backwards up the ramp with the fans booing her as Aspen and Alioth, now seemed to be growing more and more real as we went to commercial. QUOTE As we return from the opening sequence we are shown a reply of the slap then are taken to the ramp where “Oops!...I did it again" by Britney Spears hits as the fans boo not only the song but the girl coming out to it as Eliza Gray, at least to the hardcore’s was now known as Alexis’s best friend which was not the way to endear yourself to the fans. RILEY STEVENS: “These fans have no love lost for Eliza, who might be making her debut but anyone who is a friend of Alexis is not a friend of these fans.” As she heads to the ring ignoring the fans all the way into the ring she heads up to her corner as now it was time for her tag team partner for the night as "Remedy" by Seether hits and the fans give out mostly cheers for Michael Harris as walks out from the back, he’d been making a name for himself the past few weeks by just getting the job done in the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Can’t help but be impressed by Harris as he was the stand out in his match last week.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Happen to be a fan of the team he is facing tonight.” Harris enters the ring walking over to his corner as well where he meets up with Eliza, they don’t seem to be arguing but it’s clear that they are not all that great of friends as "Frontline" by Pillar is the final song and the tune for DefiaStarr who head to the ring side by side also garnering boos from the crowd who have Harris to root for in this one and that’s about it. RILEY STEVENS: “This is a new team here in GIW and you have to think that there ability to co-exist might help them tonight.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Absolutely these two kids are going to be good.” Both members of DefiaStarr head up the steps and into the ring heading to their corner both seeming supremely confident. DING DING DING! The two men talk about it for a moment before it is decided that Starr will start while on he other side Gray willingly exits to let Harris go ahead and start it off. The two head to the center of the ring where they start to circle around each other before going in for the inevitable lock up which gets them right into the battle as Harris and Starr duke it out. Harris is the taller man but they weigh about the same and that leads to a true test of strength with neither man getting any advantage until Daniel bails on it dropping one arm and twisting Michael’s behind his back as he looks over at Defias with a smirk, the two clearly on the same page as Harris now spins around kicking him in the gut then sending him off the ropes. But on the rebound Michael throws a big clothesline that Daniel ducks under, so quick he is that he bounces off the ropes and leaps up into a Bulldog that causes the fans to boo him in anger. RILEY STEVENS: “DefiaStarr lacks no confidence.” Starr hops up and throws up his arms as though he just won the match as Defias claps him on in the corner as Daniel now follows it up helping Harris up to his feet before sending him into his corner where he gives him a knee to the gut then tags onto Defias. Holding Michael there Defias enters and the two pull him out from the ropes, hoisting him up into a Suplex together, both men hopping up to their feet Starr exiting while Paul drops a boot down on him, then another as DefiaStarr gets their tag team careers started off with a bang as Harris starts to get back up only for Paul to put him right back down with a Jumping Knee to the Face! The fans boo him as Harris heads right back down, again however Defias chooses to taunt the crowd instead of going into his next move finally going for a Rolling Thunder, he hits the roll but at the last second Michael rolls out of the way. Defias lands on his back hard as Michael crawls towards his corner. RILEY STEVENS: “Harris needs a tag.” He gets just that as he reaches out for Gray who enters the ring while Defias gets up against the ropes rolling him up from behind 1…Kick Out. Defias hops right back up but Gray is up first and she Drop Kick’s him back down to the mat as she bounces off the ropes quickly before dropping a knee onto his face. Trying his best to distract her Starr yells out something at her but while she looks over at him for a moment she is able to notice Paul before he can do anything simply whipping him into a neutral corner before rushing at him with a big clothesline that sends him slumping down into the corner, where he then receives a Stink Face from Gray. RILEY STEVENS: “Now it’s Defias that needs a tag.” Gray pulls away now calling for Paul to get up as he does so only a little bit slowly walking forward, more so stumbling right into Gray’s ‘The Legit Legsweep’ a Russian Leg Sweep that takes him down as she drops down for a pin 1…2-Starr breaks it up with a big kick to her back. Daniel backs up at the referee’s command then rushes forward with a Belly to Belly Suplex to Gray, as the referee screams at him to get back to his corner which now he does as Paul tries to get up to get to his partner, Starr thinking he has made time for Paul to crawl. Eliza however is up first crawling towards Michael then jumping up and making the tag. RILEY STEVENS: “And Harris gets into it.” Paul however is not as lucky as Harris gets up as Paul tries to make a tag only for Michael to get his arms up around him and nail him with a German Suplex sending him backwards. Starr now desperate heads into the ring rushing across the ring taking a swing at Michael which he ducks under. Its Eliza trying a slap that distracts him as Starr grabs her arm and tosses it away turning around just as Paul gets up and boom Michael nails both with ‘The Brooklyn Knockout’ his version of the Gore. Both men go down as Harris hooks Paul’s leg, for the victory 1…2…3! JAMAL JACKSON: “Here are your winners…Eliza Gray and Michael Harris!” Gray enters the ring with Harris both of them getting their arms raised high into the air as DefiaStarr holds their stomachs in the corner both trying to recover. RILEY STEVENS: “Impressive performance by Harris and Gray tonight, as they start DefiaStarr’s tag team career on a low note.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “They are just working out the kinks.” Gray and Harris break away from the referee to celebrate by themselves as Defias and Starr get up to their feet, angry about the loss as we head to the back. WINNERS: ELIZA GRAY & MICHAEL HARRIS Heading to the back we have Aspen Chaud walking into an office angrily as she talks before even entering the ring. ASPEN CHAUD: “What did you need David?” Aspen heads in surprised to see Alexis and Beck Ramsey in the room as well, Alexis looking angry while Beck has a huge smile on his face. ASPEN CHAUD: “What is this all about?” Beck has a smile as she looks around at them and Ramsey more then eagerly responds. BECK RAMSEY: “Remember that whole resigning meeting I had with Sinclair? Well I had a clause in there, a special one. Where I would get to pick two star’s matches for a week, those two people are you two.” Alexis and Aspen angrily start to blabber on and on with Sinclair who tries to calm them down but Beck is not finished yet, enjoying the moment. BECK RAMSEY: “Oh I am sure you are both curious about who your facing right? Well Aspen I picked out someone special for you; it’ll be you versus Nathan Bailey IV!” Aspen looks at Sinclair as if to see if it’s legit and he shrugs his shoulders as Beck grins and then turns his attention to Alexis. BECK RAMSEY: “As for you…Well I have something really interesting, I can’t say I totally picked this one more like she picked you. Alexis you ready to suffer the consequences for your actions he past few weeks? Cause you’ve got Cassidy.” Beck is clearly loving this as Alexis is now the one looking for answers as Beck walks between them heading for the door. BECK RAMSEY: “Have fun ladies.” With that he exits through the door where luckily these fans are sparred hearing them complain as we head else where. ==== Else where we go as we find Alex Castellanos walking towards the ring for his next match when out of no where he is crushed from the side by none other then Matt Kluge. Alex turns to fight back but only ends up getting ‘The Matt Cooke’ a shot to the knee that sends Alex to the ground, from there Kluge grabs him spinning him around and tossing him into the wall laying him out even more so, finally kicking him once more while he is down before heading off. QUOTE It was now time for the second match of the night which would be Alex Castellanos versus Henry Malkin two former friends, as “Busy Being Born” by Middle Class Rut hits to a loud cheer from the crowd Henry had gained a lot of fans by just simply being against the whole “game” so to speak not leaching on to Sinclair like the others. RILEY STEVENS: “Malkin is an impressive wrestler, his one win was for the Television title but every match he seems to be the star.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I hate him.” Malkin heads down towards the ring with his Television title heading up the steps and into the ring as his music fades and “Those House of the Rising Sun” by The Animals hits to some surprise as after a few moments Alex Castellanos actually comes limping down to the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Wow even after that attack Alex is still coming out here.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Kluge should have finished the job.” Castellanos heads up the ramp limping the entire time as Malkin tries to explain it to the referee not seeming to want to fight an injured Alex, the referee asks him and Castellanos nods his head to say he is ready. DING DING DING! Malkin however does not shoot out of the gates instead he walks over to him trying to see if he’s ok, having not seen the attack as he was getting ready for his entrance. Alex pushes him away, causing Malkin to walk back at him confused saying he won’t fight him like this, but this time Castellanos slaps him across the face, hard as Malkin stumbles back. RILEY STEVENS: “Malkin is just trying to be nice.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Alex doesn’t need it.” Malkin holds his cheek as Alex yells at him that he doesn’t care about him and that they were never friends, as he walks forward towards Henry to try to do something but Malkin gets fed up with his words finally nailing him with a big punch to the face that sends him backwards. Alex comes forward again, seeming to not realize how hurt he is and though Malkin doesn’t want to do it he runs at him with a Knee Strike taking him down to the mat. RILEY STEVENS: “Alex got himself into this.” Castellanos gets up shaking off the knee as he continues to trash talk Henry who finally has enough with it as Alex gets to one knee he bounces off the ropes and slays him with a ‘Euthanasia’ a Shining Wizard that puts him down, pun intended as Alex goes out and Henry makes the cover 1…2…3! JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…Henry Malkin!” Malkin gets up rolling out of the ring seeming disappointed with his former friend’s behavior as he backs up the ramp watching him as he struggles to get to his feet with Malkin heading through to the back. RILEY STEVENS: “That was ugly no idea why Alex even came out here, then bad mouthed a man who was trying to take it easy on him.” But just as soon as Henry heads through the back here comes Matt Kluge yet again walking towards the ring in no hurry as Alex tries to crawl to get back up suffering from the kick by Malkin and the beat down by Kluge, as he rolls into the ring stalking Alex from behind. RILEY STEVENS: “Come on Kluge, he’s done enough.” But he said he would take him out and he was a man of his word grabbing him by the legs and then setting him up into ‘The Rat Trap’ a Leg Trapped Camel Clutch that he tugs on amazingly hard as Alex doesn’t put up much resistance instead just passing out as the referees head on out to stop him. RILEY STEVENS: “Kluge might have just taken out Alex.” The referee’s enter trying to pull Matt off but it’s more so just him pulling away as he spits down at Alex before shoving the ref’s off of him and heading to the back perhaps just ending Alex for the foreseeable future as we go to a break. WINNER: HENRY MALKIN Backstage we go where we see a angry Alexandra Rossi storming through the backstage area where Jacob O’Connelly the Evolve Commissioner is trying to calm her down. JACOB O’CONNELLY: “I am sorry Alexandra it’s just that David doesn’t think it’s in the best interested of anyone if you wrestle, he doesn’t think it’s safe.” Jacob tries to keep her calm while hoping she won’t shoot the messenger as she glares back at him. ALEXANDRA ROSSI: “If he wants to see unsafe, just wait until I get my hands on Aspen…” Alexandra has a big smile; she clearly wants a piece of her and has some sort of plan to do it tonight but as she says this two burly security guys walk up behind her. JACOB O’CONNELLY: “That’s why David is having you removed from the arena for the rest of the show for the safety of his wrestlers.” Rossi seems confused until the security guys grab her arms and start to take her towards an exit as she squirms around her words not being able to be made out as Jacob walks away. ==== Else where in the backstage locker room we see Zachary Pagonis in the back with a microphone looking straight at the camera and seeming a bit worried. ZACHARY PAGONIS: “Just a quick update on the status of Alex Castellanos it appears as though he suffered a--” Before he can finish however Matt Kluge walks up beside him stripping away the mic from him and holding it up to his own mouth. MATT KLUGE: “He suffered that’s for sure, just like he made us all suffer with his constant bitching. I gave him something real to bitch about this time.” Kluge lowers the mic not seeming even the slight bit remorseful as Pagonis sighs; he was quickly growing a strong hatred for Kluge. MATT KLUGE: “I told you all I would take him out and deport his ass, tonight I did just that.” Kluge slams the mic into Zachary’s chest as he holds it in pain as Matt walks off with a grin on his face as we head to another commercial break. QUOTE After that break we return right away with "World Warrior" by RedRapper hitting as the fans go wild for Tony Miranda who heads out from the back, with Hayden Carter following not too far behind him both looking ready to go here tonight. RILEY STEVENS: “This is an odd ball team but the two have joined together to try to take out Scar and Altair two wrestlers who seem to have gotten on their nerves.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Good luck with that, these freak shows seem unstoppable.” The two head up into the ring heading to their corner as their song fades and then “Man That You Fear” by Marilyn Manson now echoes through the speakers as Scar and Altair walk out from the back with that same intimidating presence as usual. RILEY STEVENS: “The rivalry between Scar and Miranda has truly been a war.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I think until one of them dies, it will not end.” The first to start was Hayden Carter versus Altair. The two circled for a moment, Hayden eyed Altair and then Scar, then back to Altair. The weight of the situation rested upon her shoulders to begin the match. Self proclaimed as a tough New York girl, she roughed Altair into a lock up and then pushed him off in an assertive display. Altair snickered and pulled her close to him as well. As he threw her off, Hayden fell to the canvas with a loud thud, but pulled herself back up quickly after. She charged Altair and grabbed on tight. The two struggled for power, but it seemed as though Altair was merely toying with her. He hoisted her over in a vertical suplex. She pulled up after the impact in time for Altair to pull her into another slamming maneuver. Side slam into the canvas and then a failed pin–fall attempt. Altair rolled off his opponent and burst backed his way toward the corner where his partner waited. Hayden crawled and glared back as Altair tagged in Scar. As Scar stepped into the ring, Hayden had reached her partner and tagged him in. Scar wasn’t after her anyway. RILEY STEVENS: “Here we go!” The pace picked up between the two legal men in the ring. Scar came in with a throat thrust that knocked Tony down, but he stood right back up. Tony retaliated with a double–handed palm strike and then hoisted Scar over his shoulders and slammed him down with a Samoan drop. He pulled himself up and stomped once, taunting his opponent as Scar tried to pull up. He’d stomp a few more times before he was forced by the referee to back away. Scar grabbed hold of his opponent and pulled him into a short-arm clothesline and then a leg drop. RILEY STEVENS: “When these two get together, it’s always fireworks.” Tony stood back up again after he threw Scar off of him. He can do it on his own. He stabbed Scar in the abdomen with a kick and then raced to the ropes for momentum that ran him into Scar with a Hurricanrana that wiped the canvas with Scar’s body. Scar stood up immediately after and the two collided at the center of the ring for a second time. This time, Tony struck first with a sleeper hold he transitioned into the suplex variant of the maneuver. Scar then head butted his opponent to free himself. They gathered themselves at the side ropes. Tony went for an Irish whip, but Scar countered with a clothesline over the top rope. Toppled near the commentary table, Tony pulled himself together as Scar joined him on the outside and the count began. Altair dropped from the rim of the ring as well, which signaled Hayden Carter to do the same. She raced around and caught Altair before her pale opponent could reach Tony Miranda. The two began to have a light skirmish to the off side of the matted area as Scar and Tony battled. Scar whipped Tony toward the barricade. RILEY STEVENS: “Carter and Altair fighting on the outside.” Tony collided back first into the barrier and ducked as Scar charged after him, causing his masked opponent to crash up and over the barricade, into the audience. On the other side, Altair grasped Hayden’s wrist and tugged down, then toward him, catching her in his clutch. He bit into her flesh and then kicked her away as he then released a red mist of her own blood. Back over to Tony, he climbed the barricade and was met with a hard throat thrust as the two began to brawl in the audience. Scar would whack Tony across the skull with a Kesagiri chop, and Tony would reply with a jumping uppercut. Scar staggered up the lane with Tony at his heels. The match had ended in a ten count disqualification. A draw. JAMAL JACKSON: “This match is a Double Countout!” This encouraged Tony to then pick up a chair from one of the audience members and stalk his opponent with it. Scar turned. Tony swung to drive it into Scar’s masked skull, but Scar threw his arms up and caught the chair. They struggled to gain control of the steel weapon. Scar had won, but only because Tony had turned away to send Altair back over the barricade with a spinning roundhouse kick. As he turned, Scar jabbed the edge of the chair into Tony’s ribs and then struck him with a big boot, toppling him over several fans and empty chairs. RILEY STEVENS: “This match is over, but these two men just want to fight!” Scar made his escape out of the arena and left Altair behind. Tony noticed this and reached him just as Hayden had. She wiped the last remains of her own blood from her eyes and held Altair’s arms behind him for Tony to take a free jab. This released him from her grasp and allowed Tony to set Altair up for the running leg lariat with the barricade as lift off, boom he lands Altair going down. RILEY STEVENS: “Scar just left Altair to take a beating.” Tony turns up towards the ramp as Hayden taunts Altair as Scar and Miranda have a stare down before Scar disappears into the back. DOUBLE COUNTOUT Again we go backstage where we see Nathan Bailey IV walking around backstage but as he turns a corner he stumbles right into Henry Malkin, still in his ring gear from earlier in the night. NATHAN BAILEY IV: “Watch where you’re going!” Bailey angrily brushes himself as Henry looks up at him a little annoyed himself with Bailey’s reaction. HENRY MALKIN: “Calm down. What were you in a rush to go kiss Aspen’s butt or is it Sinclair’s this time. Anything to get ahead right?” This seems to strike a nerve with Bailey, who sizes him up a bit as he replies. NATHAN BAILEY IV: “Who exactly are you? Oh wait don’t tell me you are holder of the oh-so prestigious TV title. Who’d you beat for that, my grandma?” Bailey chuckles at his own joke as Malkin shrugs it off, it was up to him to make that belt gain some prestige and he would do just that. HENRY MALKIN: “This belt will mean something soon enough, which is more then I can say about you when you get your finger tips on that title belt at ‘Exile’ then fail, nobody is going to help you when you are in TLC.” With that Malkin slaps him on the back walking down the hallway as Bailey glares at him as we continue. QUOTE “The Rat” by Dead Confederate played into the arena and the fans replied with a chorus of jeers as Matt Kluge stepped out onto the stage. He shrugged it off and continued down the pathway toward the ring. His opponent, Evan Clarke, had already made his entrance prior and had now just been waiting by the ropes and referee. RILEY STEVENS: “Since Matt Kluge’s change of heart last week we have seen him attack Alex twice this time very seriously and win a match.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “He’s going to be great Riley, trust me.” Upon entry into the ring, Kluge adjusted his gloves and stood for a moment as both men prepared, just absorbing the negative responses from the crowd to his attire, and him in general. Soon enough the bell was called and the match began. DING DING DING! Evan Clarke held out a hand, so did Kluge. The two got closer, the fingers even more so. Upon contact, Kluge twisted Evan’s arm and threw a hard gut kick to his opponent. Kluge then rushed behind him, propelled from the ropes back toward Evan Clarke, and then kneed Clarke’s knee from behind, a move he called ‘The Matt Cooke’. Evan dropped to one knee, but Kluge pushed him down the rest of the way into the canvas. Kluge dropped down, a leg on each side and held up Evan’s face, a good punching target for him to work on. RILEY STEVENS: “That Matt Cooke maneuver is one of the most vicious in all of GIW.” The heat poured down on Kluge from the crowd around him. He stopped the mounted punching and then dragged the limp remains of Evan Clarke toward the corner turn post in an almost seated position. Kluge backed slowly, until he reached the opposite, adjacent turn post and then charged. He struck hard with a face wash to the cheek that wiped Evan Clarke right out of the ring, like a dead octopus. Evan pooled on the matted floor, trying to collect himself after the kick, but Kluge was on him quicker than he hoped. Kluge dropped his opponent’s forehead into the rim of the ring, then the steel pole, then slid him back. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “He’s letting out his inner prick.” With himself in the ring as well, the brief count was interrupted and he could attempt a pin on his opponent, but he stopped. As the referee slid into position, Kluge raised up from his opponent. He kicked Evan’s side to turn him over onto his stomach. A better position for Kluge to then come around from the feet, scoop them backward and apply the Rat Trap. With it locked in, it was now a matter of whether Evan Clarke could handle the submission hold, the finisher that Kluge had expertly locked in. RILEY STEVENS: “Can Kluge finish it?” A few seconds was all it took for Evan to decide he had had enough. The moment the referee dropped toward Evan, the man yielded the victory to Matt Kluge via submission. Kluge let the hold on his opponent stay a few seconds longer before he pulled away. He had dominated over Evan Clarke completely, and the crowds jeers only intensified with his victory. JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…Matt Kluge!” Kluge hops up to his feet with a very dominant victory here tonight as the fans continue to boo him, the referee raised his arm as he looked out at the crowd with a big smirk on his face. RILEY STEVENS: “I’d love to say something negative about his change but since he’s become this new man he’s been doing big things.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “He’s my favorite now.” Kluge pulls away his arms heading up to the top rope to pose as the fans groan watching him on the top rope as we go to the back.
