GIW — Card

Pre-show, 26 April 2015

26 April 2015
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Wanda Baez, Steven Hunt, Terra Nova, Terry McKenna, and Ryan Smith find themselves in the open battle royal this week on the Revolt pre-show. Hunt is one half of the tag team Derpanomycs which will be entering the tag team gauntlet at Exile. Baez is one quarter of the group Beauty and Grace which will also find themselves thrown in the gauntlet. The bell rings and the participants go to work on one another with Terra Nova working on Baez and McKenna working on Smith. Hunt is forced to choose who to go after, and he decided to team up with McKenna. McKenna and Hunt are quickly getting the better of Ryan Smith before the veteran unloads on the two of them with a double dropkick. Meanwhile, Terra Nova and Baez are back and forth but that ends with Terra Nova tossing Baez over the rope, but the star manages to land on the apron. Hunt grabs Smith after a struggle and manages to toss him over the top, only before being tossed over by the man he was working with earlier in the match, Terry McKenna. Terra Nova is getting close to knocking Baez off the apron, but a big shoulder block sends her back. Unfortunately for Baez, McKenna is now free and dropkicks her off and onto the floor. The final two in this match are Nova and McKenna. They begin to trade blows but McKenna ends up with the advantage. The two chain wrestle back and forth to the amazement of the crowd before McKenna once again gains control. McKenna connects with one of his finishing moves, the Regal Plex, which stuns Terra Nova long enough to be tossed over the top. After what was a very evenly matched open battle royal, Terry McKenna manages to come out on top – but can he keep his momentum rolling? WINNER: TERRY MCKENNA In a match that was moved to the pre-show due to time constraints, The Thundermashers, Team Relativity, and Bro & Sawdust found themselves ready to go for this match earlier than they had expected. Before the match started, Brotree Harper got on the mic and said that it was a sham that he had to compete on the pre-show two weeks in a row because he was a big star in GIW. He and Botanist’s Experiment both stood on the apron as Larry Sangestino and Marcelo Speed went to work on each other. The unorthodox Sangestino managed to get the upper hand with a fireman’s carry followed by a bit of chain wrestling. He made the tag to his partner Yuri who completely overwhelmed Speed with his agility. Several dropkicks later and Speed was making his way to tag in Sang Whittaker. The big man came into the match and managed to grab Yuri and place him in an airplane spin and a bearhug. Yuri managed to claw away seconds before tapping, but could only tag in Brotree Harper. Harper charged Whittaker but a big shoulder block took down the younger star. Marcelo Speed is tagged in. He picks up the pinfall victory over Brody as Sang takes out Team Relativity. The Thundermashers have placed themselves firmly at the top of the GIW tag team favorites heading into the gauntlet. WINNERS: THE THUNDERMASHERS
