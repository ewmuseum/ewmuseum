GIW — Card

Revolt, 19 April 2012

19 April 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: THURSDAY NIGHT REVOLT LIVE! From Rapides Parish Coliseum in Alexandria, LA – April 19, 2012 BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! As the show begins this crowd is electric and it seems basically to be full already the fans all seeming ready to go for the night as we pan around them all of them seeming happy until they are reminded that standing in the ring was none other then David Sinclair dressed in a suit and a smirk as he looked out at the crowd with the red carpet laid out in the ring with a table in the middle, this is what we all knew as a contract signing. Standing next to him was Jacob O’Connelly standing just over his shoulder. DAVID SINCLAIR: “Welcome GIW fans to Revolt here tonight where we are proud to hold the Contract Signing for the match between Aspen Chaud and Matt Kluge for the GIW Undisputed Championship at ‘International X’ in London, England. Let’s kick it off with the challenger, Matt Kluge!” Sinclair points to the ramp as Kluge is given a very mixed reaction as “The Rat” by Dead Confederate hits and out from the back wearing his wrestling tights and ring gear Matt Kluge heads out not really looking formal at all for the occasion as he heads down to the ramp heading into the ring as Sinclair holds out a hand for him to shake which he looks at and then chuckles sitting at the chair. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Disrespecting the Boss, typical Kluge.” David looks down at him glaring as Kluge looks over the contract that is already laid down in front of him, O’Connelly watching on from behind him shaking his head whenever his Boss does. DAVID SINCLAIR: “And now introducing the GIW Undisputed Champion, Aspen Chaud!” The boos are now much louder as "Did It On 'Em" by Nicki Minaj hits as Kluge picks up the mic laid out on the table as Aspen Chaud walks out in a very business like attire heading down to the ring with a smile on her face and her belt on her shoulder as Kluge breaks up the formalities using the mic. MATT KLUGE: “While we wait for her let me get this long drawn out and very generic contract signing part done on my behalf, there…signed..” While Aspen is only half way down the ramp Kluge signs it off and tosses the pen to the side as Sinclair again stares at him as Aspen heads into the ring shaking David’s hand then taking a seat across from Kluge raising up her seat so they are at eye level. MATT KLUGE: “Go ahead Aspen, if you can write your name go ahead. Feel free to go ahead and print it out if need be.” Aspen reaches out grabbing up the microphone next to her as she rolls her eyes at Kluge, setting her belt in front of him just to taunt him. ASPEN CHAUD: “Let me just go ahead and set my title down while I sign it. You know that thing can get really heavy carrying it around all the time, you couldn’t imagine…Literally.” Aspen chuckles taking up the pen in her hand looking up at Kluge who responds with a smile. MATT KLUGE: “I think I’ll know in just over a week and a half when that belt finally gets someone worthy of it.” Aspen now raises the mic again, delaying her side of the contract signing so far to get the last bits of trash talk in. ASPEN CHAUD: “Aren’t you just so funny? But truth be told this belt will never be around your waist because even though you like to call yourself the best wrestler ever in GIW the simple truth is in the two years we have been open, there has not been even one week where you were the top guy. And that won’t change when we leave England you’ll go right back to being irrelevant.” These fans may not like either but they can respect the animosity between the two as Sinclair and O’Connelly watch on from just a few feet away. MATT KLUGE: “The only reason you’ve ever been champion is because you have some easily influenced guy at your feet or a border line Lesbian friend as an ally. When you beat Tony? You had help. When you beat Alex? You had help. And while I would ask you to leave them in the back at ‘International X’ I know you won’t but it won’t matter. I’ll take out Alexis…I will take out anyone you want. Now sign the contract so I know that I am getting to be in the ring with you in London.” Kluge urges her to sign it as she grabs up her belt throwing it back over her shoulder just to taunt him as she uses the pen to sign her name on the contract pushing it over to Sinclair. ASPEN CHAUD: “Tony Miranda…Alex Castellanos…Matt Kluge. Look where the other two are now? Retired. They’ll be waiting for you…” Aspen gets up to walk away as Sinclair and O’Connelly prepare to do the same but Kluge speaking stops them. MATT KLUGE: “You know you guys have all the sway and power now but when I beat Aspen you are going to have to deal with me as a champion, and trust me things are going to change around here.” O’Connelly holds the ropes for both Sinclair and Aspen who exit but if only to let off some steam Kluge grabs him pulling him into the ring and just like that grabbing his arm and then lifting him into a Cravate Suplex right through the table, O’Connelly going through a table sends the fans into a frenzy as Aspen and Sinclair start to head back to the ring but Kluge stands waiting, with a smile on his face. RILEY STEVENS: “There goes O’Connelly through a table, just because really.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “This guy better hope he wins in London because if he doesn’t Sinclair is going to fire him for his mis-treatment of the staff around here.” Realizing that she doesn’t want to fight right now Aspen backs up the ramp, Sinclair soon doing the same as Kluge watches them go as we take it to break. QUOTE The opening contest was just a few short moments form beginning and the fans were on their feet in anticipation for the first taste of action of the evening. Two new faces were in the ring, ready to prove themselves. Guy Virgo and Phoenix Matsuda were both highly talked about new signees and the both were capable of getting it down in the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Here we go three stars in the ring ready to make a name for themselves.” The two locked eyes with each other from across corners and the tension of proving themselves to the fans could be cut with a knife. On the opposite side of the ring was none other than the returning Eliza Gray. Eliza would be best remembered as Alexis Terry’s sidekick or “partner in crime”. She was back and according to her, “better than ever”. The three circled each other and finally the bell rang and we were underway with Thursday Night Revolt. DING DING DING! To start tings off, the two men tie up and Eliza takes the chance to simply back up and watch the two men battle strength, Guy coming out the victor by applying a side headlock. Phoenix sends Guy into the ropes but Guy is right back at it, sending Phoenix to the mat with a shoulder block. Guy this time uses the ropes for momentum and bounces off only this time he is drilled with a hip toss from Phoenix. Eliza finally takes this as her chance to enter herself into the contest. She rushes Phoenix like a mad woman and connects with forearm shot after forearm shot. She then hits beautiful DDT and goes for the cover, only getting a two count for her troubles though. RILEY STEVENS: “Gray as we all know is Terry’s good friend.” Eliza is back to her feet only to be hit with a European uppercut by Guy Virgo. He then connects with a devastating Leg Hook Saito Suplex. He hooks the leg but Phoenix makes the save and the match continues. Phoenix throws a punch but Guy catches it. Guy applies a wristlock and Phoenix does his best to fight out of it but Guy applies more pressure. Eliza is up again and from out of nowhere, jumps on Guy’s back and applies a sleeperhold. This causes Guy to break the wristlock. Phoenix now being free sends both Eliza and Guy crashing to the mat thanks to a thunderous spear. RILEY STEVENS: “Huge move by Phoenix.” Phoenix first covers Eliza but Eliza is able to get a foot on the bottom rope at two. Wasting little time, he quickly crawls over to Guy and covers. Guy is able to kick out at two and a half. Phoenix begs to the referee to hear him out, as he was sure that was a three. The crowd is on there feet, enjoying the back and forth contests. Phoenix walks over picks Eliza by her hair and without hesitation nails a grueling Belly-to-Belly slam that leaves Eliza motionless. Before Phoenix can pin, Guy is back up and from out of nowhere hits a bridging German Suplex. One..two..No! Almost! RILEY STEVENS: “Virgo with the near fall.” Phoenix rolls under the ropes as Eliza re-enters the ring rushing at Guy who is standing near the ropes and she tackles him sending both falling through the ropes landing hard on the outside. These fans cringe at the site as Phoenix gets up on the ring apron and as Eliza and Guy get up he jumps off clotheslining both down to the ground. These fans admire these three’s performance as already they have put on a show as Matsuda walks away with a smirk on his face as both Eliza and Virgo start to get to their feet. The referee urges them to get back into the ring, but it seems like these three have gotten past the point of a wrestling match their desire to be noticed really taking over as Eliza gets up jumping forward tackling him into the barricade. RILEY STEVENS: “The referee needs to get some control out here.” Trying to scream at them to get back into the ring these fans seem to be eating it up as Virgo waits for Eliza to turn around and walks right into a big punch that knocks her down. Finally the referee hops out of the ring trying to get some control only to end up getting punched by a stray punch that Guy threw at Eliza that she ducked away from. The referee stumbled back holding his head as he is then squished behind Virgo as Phoenix hops up slamming him into the ring post. The referee falls down out of it as Guy falls to a knee and then Eliza slaps Phoenix. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “This has become a fight.” After a few moments the referee gets up crawling to the bell guy telling him to wave off the match as these three have fought to a no contest just engaging in a war for some reason. JAMAL JACKSON: “This match has been waved off!” This seems to only keep the fighting going as these three new stars all trying to get noticed continue to brawl on the outside until a few of the referee’s rush out from the back jumping in between them as Eliza ducks out of the way seeming to only now realize what had happened with the match. RILEY STEVENS: “I am not sure what exactly happened with these three tonight but they waged war on the outside and the referee unable to get control waved it off, we’ll have to see what Sinclair does to fix this situation.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I say let them go…” With several referee’s now out there they get some control as we head to a commercial while order is restored. NO CONTEST Back we go to the backstage area for the first time tonight where Faith Simpson is walking through the hall way with a straight face until James Dean walks around the corner with a grin on his as well. JAMES DEAN: "Calm down, calm down not here to talk trash like you are used to. In fact last week I sort of expected you to win and I thought it made for the best finale tonight. Now I don’t like facing girls but to get to that belt means I have to face you. So while I plan on winning, no hard feelings right?" Simpson seemed still to be reserved after all she had been in GIW awhile and knew that trusting anyone was generally a mistake. FAITH SIMPSON: “You to.” Dean grinned and then patted her on the back, still having an air of confidence to him while still being respectful. JAMES DEAN: "Let’s just make it a classic." With that he walked on, the two were not really enemies but it was clear tonight we were in for a treat in the Television tourney final. QUOTE It’s now time for the second contest of the night where we join the crew with The Shadow and Matt Matlock already up in the ring getting prepared and ready to go as the music of Sean Mendez “Here Me Now” by Hollywood Undead hits as the fans give mostly a negative reaction as Sean walks out from the back not giving a damn as he heads down towards the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Three men will enter and one will leave with a big impression on these fans.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “This kid Sean is crazy but seems pretty talented.” Mendez heads into the ring looking across at his opponents; if it were a intimidating contest it would be very close. DING DING DING! This match gets underway and the fans are ready to go as Matlock jumps out of the gates rushing across at Mendez thinking he is the easier challenge of the two but Mendez uses a Matrix Evasion to duck under his clothesline and as Matt hits the ropes he waits until he bounces off into a Knife Edge Chop. Matlock stumbles back holding his chest as The Shadow stands in the corner, letting these two men have their little battle. Sean follows him up spinning him over into a seated position as he delivers one huge kick to the chest, then another finally wrapping it up with a Reverse Roundhouse a combo he calls the ‘Krush Kombo’. Matlock rolls around holding his chest as Shadow now steps to the middle of the ring ready to fight. RILEY STEVENS: “Shadow’s first match was a downer but something tells me this will be his time.” The huge Shadow looks down upon Sean basically willingly allowing him to throw something at him. Suddenly unleashing a flurry of kicks to the leg, to the stomach, everywhere he can Shadow seeming to take them all without any damage until Sean jumps up connecting with a shot to the head that seems to daze him a bit. This gives him the chance to head to the ropes jumping over them and going springboard where he hits a ‘Tidal Wave’ a Springboard Roundhouse that drops Shadow to the mat! RILEY STEVENS: “Holy cow.” These fans even have to be impressed with what he has done so far as Matlock returns to the ring throwing another failed punch this time getting kicked in the gut then spun around into ‘The Gringo Killa’ a Back to Back Double Underhook Piledriver right on top of Shadow. Both men end up right next to one another as Sean points to the to top rope, jumping up top taking a second to pose having all the time in the world. Jumping off and landing ‘The Ghetto Stomp’ a Diving Double Foot Stomp right onto The Shadow as the fans even give him a few cheers for the move as he hops on top 1…2…3! JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…Sean Mendez!” Mendez gets to his feet laying waste to both his opponents tonight looking down upon them as the referee raises his arm in victory. RILEY STEVENS: “What a impressive first match on Revolt for Sean Mendez tonight.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “This guy looks good.” Sean rips his arm away and then heads to the top rope where he poses, these fans now knowing a lot more about him after this one. WINNER: SEAN MENDEZ Right behind the curtain we go now as Hayden Carter is now ready for her entrance as she was about to come out for her match but before her music hit, with Acquin’s playing in the background it was Alexis Terry walking over to her with a smirk on her face. ALEXIS TERRY: “Weren’t you the one that was oh so confident that you could beat me a week or so ago? Now all the sudden you may not even be in the match. Trust me, I don’t care regardless if you win or lose. I am walking out of London with my belt regardless but good luck, I mean no pressure right?” Terry chuckles knowing that was not the case at all as Hayden looked over at her with a grin. HAYDEN CARTER: “Facing Acquin-So basically the same as if I was facing you.” Carter now had her laugh at her own response as she walked towards the curtain as her music hit, panning to the annoyed face of Terry as we head to the ring. QUOTE From that backstage segment we head to the ramp to greet Hayden Carter who heads down to the ring to her "I Want To Dance Without You" by Skylar Grey theme as Joshua Acquin awaits her in the ring, the fans giving her a loud response. RILEY STEVENS: “If you ask me it’s unfair she has to even defend her spot in the match.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “If she is deserving she will just get the win.” Acquin seems confident as the fans will cheer for whoever they prefer of the two, liking them both as Carter heads into the ring not seeming nervous at all despite the big stakes at hand. DING DING DING! This match starts and the two go ahead and circle one another, Hayden the much smaller competitor. They head in for a lock up and Acquin naturally gets the advantage slowly pushing her back until out of no where she slips around him applying a Sleeper Hold! The fans are impressed and cheer as she wrenches on it as Acquin heads towards the ropes, using his strength to get to them basically carrying Carter on his back. Acquin gets to the ropes as Hayden breaks off stepping backwards as Joshua turns around heading towards her only to get a Drop Toe Hold to the mat allowing her to go right into an Ankle Lock. RILEY STEVENS: “Hayden showing off her submission skills tonight.” Acquin flips over kicking her back away getting to his feet trying to find a way to get back into this one but as soon as he stands up he receives a Dropkick that puts him right back down on the mat where this time she looks to lock up the Boston Crab, getting it all the way in this time seeming to have him in trouble. However she breaks it off by herself, seeming to have bigger plans for him this time as he gets up he does so much more gingerly stumbling into her as she tries to lift him into a Piledriver only for her back to be unable to cope with it, as Acquin pushes her away and walks forward right into a Inverted Stomp Facebreaker, a move we all know Alexis Terry’s finisher as Carter uses it to taunt her as she gets on top hooking the leg 1…2…3! JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…Hayden Carter!” The fans go wild as Carter retains her chance at Terry at ‘International X’ as Acquin rolls out of the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “What a huge win and she uses Terry’s finisher to win it, sending a message perhaps?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “It’s disrespectful is what that is.” Carter walks over to the ropes where the camera man is videoing her as she mouths “Top that” to her opponent in a few weeks as she heads back into the middle of the ring to celebrate as she was now a week away from a Legacy title match. WINNER: HAYDEN CARTER David Sinclair’s office was now where we came after a commercial break with Jacob O’Connelly sitting in a chair with an ice pack on his back with Aspen sitting in the chair next to him with Sinclair behind his desk. JACOB O’CONNELLY: “You need to suspend Kluge for what he did out there! You suspended Ramsey he should get one to; he has no care for us as staff.” O’Connelly cringes just trying to talk as Aspen nods her head in agreement speaking up. ASPEN CHAUD: “He is right, he should be at least suspended for like the next show or something…” Sinclair shakes his head no holding up the clipboard in his hand, then dropping it down on his desk. DAVID SINCLAIR: “Match is already signed and ready to go, besides we can’t risk another Main Event. Beat him Aspen and we can punish him then.” Aspen pouts a bit that she couldn’t get him suspended but then nods her head. ASPEN CHAUD: “If I beat him, I want him suspended…better yet fired!” O’Connelly nods his head in agreement with her as we leave this little bickering scene to go back to the ring for the next match. QUOTE "Power On Me" by Katy B hits as we come back to a huge cheer from the crowd as Faith Simpson walks out from the back with a smile on her face with a chance to punch her ticket for a title shot with a win here tonight. RILEY STEVENS: “These two competitors won last week and truly have been very impressive in this tournament, we will surely get a worthy contender.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Just as long as it’s not Faith.” Faith jumps up over the ropes into the ring heading to the top rope to pose as her entrance music fades and “Bawitdaba” by Kid Rock now hits and these fans give another cheer this one not quite as loud as James Dean walks out from the back having given the fans a show last week. RILEY STEVENS: “Faith is a former champion here in GIW while this is Dean’s second match will experience be a factor?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “She is fifteen!” Dean heads down towards the ring heading up the steps and into the ring with Faith this all about the shot at the belt as the music ends and we are ready to start the match, as the bell rings. DING DING DING! Faith stood across from James Dean. The two locked up briefly, but James pushed her off. He dropped her quickly with a dropkick and then pulled himself up to continue the assault. He stomped at her shoulder, then again. She jerked away from him to gather up to her feet, but James Dean was on her quicker than she’d like. He used a forearm smash to the shoulder blade and knocked her down to her knee. Pulling her close, he dropped Faith Simpson to the canvas with double-arm DDT that planted her firmly. He went for a quick cover, but failed. RILEY STEVENS: “The stakes are high in this one.” Faith pulled away from Dean and got to her feet quick enough to pull off a moonsault off the second rope. With a couple arm drags to annoy him, she took quick control of the match with ease and now it was James Dean on the defensive. The two strugged within her sleeper hold, the awkward height difference gave him only a slight advantage, but it was his muscle mass greater than hers that allowed him to thrust her overtop of him and slam her down into the canvas, onto her back. He stomped at her for good measure and then applied his own submission hold. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Just beat this little girl already.” James held an ankle lock tightly to his opponent. She whinged, but it was for nothing as he would refuse to release her. She struggled to claw at the canvas for an escape, the ropes so close. She had only just barely reached the ropes when James had finally release her. He had control again and pulled her up by the hair, a move the referee called against. He set her up for an Olympic slam, but she dodged the first attempt. She was incapable of dodging the second and was slammed down again. RILEY STEVENS: “Faith is only fifteen but she is tough.” The constant wear and tear of being dropped to the canvas on her back began to break her down. She was tired easier, and finding it harder to fight back. Instead, she tried to wait it out. Wait for him to tire and a moment to open up for her. It came when he failed a diving cross body attacked. She had collapsed to her knees at the right moment and then reapplied her sleeper hold in laying position. It was hard to writhe and gather the right maneuvering to lock the submission hold, but she managed to fit it in snug and dangerously. RILEY STEVENS: “Can she do it?” James Dean was wild with trying to escape. He thrashed and had even stood up with her on his back like a blonde monkey and dropped her down onto the canvas, but she would just reapply the hold as quick as she could like a child squared of a jeep dropping over a ledge and on top of her. She held tighter, and he slowly began to wear down. His arms would drop like limp rubber tassels. He dropped to his knees, and she held on further, but even she began to tire from applying this vicegrip that had such a hold over James Dean. RILEY STEVENS: “Can she draw the tap?” Eventually Faith Simpson had peeled off like a wet sticker and collapsed behind her opponent, whereas Dean fell to the front. Both of them limp and the only signs of life were their chests showing signs of breathing. The referee had begun a count, but neither responded. As he reached ten, only Faith Simpson was capable of moving and even then it wasn’t enough movement to cancel out the disqualification. The match had come to a draw between the two competitors. JAMAL JACKSON: “This match has been ruled a Draw!” These fans seem to be a bit let down as both competitors exhausted and tired from the move were not able to answer the ten count leaving the victor in this one inconclusive as some referee’s from the back head out to help the two to their feet. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “No title shot for her, come on.” RILEY STEVENS: “Wait I am getting a text message from David.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “He actually has your number?” Wanting an answer these fans watch as both stars get to their feet on their own now able to get there feet back underneath them as Riley gets to his feet with a microphone. RILEY STEVENS: “One moment, one moment please! I have a text message from Sinclair right now and he is telling me to ask you fans who you believe should move on to face Henry Malkin. Should it be Faith Simpson?” The fans react with cheers for their beloved teenage phenom as Riley then points at Dean. RILEY STEVENS: “Or should it be James Dean?” Again we get a lot of cheers these fans impressed with both star’s performances the last few weeks as Riley shakes his head. RILEY STEVENS: “The only fair way to do it after this great match tonight is at ‘International X’ it will be Henry Malkin defending against both Faith Simpson and James Dean in a Triple Threat Match! Good luck.” Dean and Simpson look at one another giving a small head nod, this match didn’t give us a winner but for the belt there would surely be a victor as we head to another ‘International X’ Commercial. DRAW To the interview station we go with Zachary Pagonis standing by with a very focused Paul Defias standing by. ZACHARY PAGONIS: “Just a few moments ago it was announced that it would be you versus Daniel Starr at ‘International X’ in a singles match, what are your thoughts on that match?” Defias seems to be completely ready as Pagonis holds the microphone over to him as he answers him. PAUL DEFIAS: “Daniel was my friend and an ally but he tore that away from me. I am left with no friends, no partner, and nothing here in GIW. Daniel thinks by leaving me he is going to go onto become some big name star. But in that match when we go one on one, I’ll prove I am the better half and I will crush any thoughts of him ever being a star around here.” As he talks it’s Daniel Starr that walks into frame chuckling to himself speaking loudly to get Paul’s attention. DANIEL STARR: “The better half? Are you kidding me, I always got the pins, you always took the falls. Everyone knows who the better man is but you and in London, you get to be the man I use to send a message to the rest of the roster. Daniel Starr has arrived and he is going to take control.” Starr smirks as Paul walks toward him the two getting into one another’s faces as Pagonis brings the mic over so that we can hear. PAUL DEFIAS: “You are going to regret what you did last week!” DANIEL STARR: “I don’t need you, I never did.” The two now go head to head with one another, emotions over coming them until a few of the Road Agents head on over to break them up both heading separate ways this former tag team, now bitter enemies. QUOTE It is now time for our Co-Main Event of the night which will feature both champions as Henry Malkin the GIW Television Champion waits in the ring until "Kiss My Lips" by Dev featuring Fabolous hits and the fans unleash a wave of boo’s on Alexis Terry as she walks out with Ryan Cavallari her beau at her side, he now getting the unfamiliar feeling of being booed. RILEY STEVENS: “What a match we have for you tonight as Alexis Terry and Henry Malkin meet in a Champion versus Champion match.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Alexis will take it.” Alexis heads up the steps leaning down and giving Ryan a kiss before heading into the ring where she goes to the top rope to pose with her belt but as she does "I Want To Dance Without You" by Skylar Grey hits and the fans go wild as Hayden Carter enters from the back. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “What is she doing out here?” RILEY STEVENS: “Scouting perhaps.” Hayden walks down to the ring as Alexis jumps down angrily screaming something at her as the referee takes Alexis belt out of the ring and then starts the match. DING DING DING! Alexis points at Hayden who is standing by the ring, Hayden just simply watching as Alexis angrily yells at her to leave. The referee trying to make sure nothing happens walks over to warn her about not interfering but as he does Henry extends a hand even though he knows it will probably be rejected, only for Terry to deliver a kick down below to Malkin dropping him to his knees. The referee turns around but to late to see what has happened as Carter tries to tell him but it is far to late as Malkin being the fighter he is tries to get back to his feet only to receive a ‘Flawless Victory’ a Inverted Stomp Facebreaker, the second one tonight this time by Alexis. Henry falls to the mat out cold as Ryan tells Terry to pin him only for Terry to head to the corner where Hayden is standing and setting up pointing at her and then jumping off with the ‘Most Glamorous Moonsault Ever’ right on top of Henry as she hooks both legs 1…2…3! JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…Alexis Terry!” Hayden didn’t mean to have any effect on the match but unfortunately she gave the advantage to Terry who was able to finish it really quickly with the low blow as Ryan entered the ring handing her the belt. RILEY STEVENS: “With Henry’s recent streak we were not expecting this but Alexis makes quick work of him as Hayden and Alexis seem to be ready for a collision.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Carter doesn’t know what she has coming to her.” Alexis hugs Ryan in victory as Hayden watches from outside the ring, these two both got wins tonight but in just a few short weeks only one could leave the victor. WINNER: ALEXIS TERRY It is now nearly time for the Main Event but we stop off one more time backstage where we find ourselves staring at several stars gathered around in the backstage area with one another. Eliza Gray, Guy Virgo, Phoenix Matsuda, Ryan Cavallari, and Sean Mendez all eyeing one another angrily until David Sinclair heads up between them. DAVID SINCLAIR: “I know all of you have been wondering how you will be involved in the show in London. Many of you are new around and trying to make a name. Well over the past few weeks we have lost Alioth Starre…Beck Ramsey was suspended…Tony Miranda retired…Leaving some gaps here in GIW. That’s why I have made a match featuring all five of you.” Sinclair looks around at them as all five look around at one another seeming to be a little untrusting knowing these are now their opponents. DAVID SINCLAIR: “Phoenix, Guy, Eliza you three all had a big fight earlier tonight and you couldn’t stay in the ring. So at ‘International X’ we are going to have a Controlled Chaos match where are the rules are simple, the last person standing wins. If you are thrown or leave the ring for any reason you are eliminated and if you are pinned or submitted you are also eliminated.” The five all nod their heads understanding the rules, still awkwardly gazing at one another. DAVID SINCLAIR: “GIW needs big stars, someone go and take that spot.” Sinclair gives them all a nod turning and heading the other way as we got one last glimpse at the five all looking from one to another as we go to the ring for the Main Event. QUOTE As we head to the ring for our Main Event “The Rat” by Dead Confederate hits as the first duo tonight Matt Kluge and Daniel Starr enter from the back. Out first is Kluge heading towards the ring following behind him is Daniel Starr accompanied by Ariana Duroni all three heading down towards the ring the two not seeming like best friends but willing to work together. RILEY STEVENS: “What a match we have tonight with two rivalries coming together to make one great match as the lead in to ‘International X’.” Starr gives off Ariana to go ahead and head to the side of the ring as he climbs up into the ring and top rope to pose as Matt heads to the corner waiting as his music ends and "Did It On 'Em" by Nicki Minaj now hits and we again get a load of boo’s, these fans really just with nobody to love in this one as it’s now Aspen Chaud walking out slowly to the ring as Paul Defias starts behind her but walks right beside her walking towards the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “You have to question the ability to team with all of these huge ego’s in the ring.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “They all have tag team history they’ll be fine.” Defias heads into the ring stretching out in his corner looking right across the ring at Starr as Aspen heads to the ring apron as the ref starts the match. DING DING DING! As this match starts the team of DefiaStarr now start on opposite corners and they get their partners Kluge and Chaud to agree to let them start the match. The two walk around the ring circling around one another, both talking a bit of trash as Starr yells out at him “You are the weak link.” as Defias jumps in both not even thinking about going for a lock up instead going into a battle as they exchange punches back and forth. These two have a lot of pent up aggression and they are going to release it all right here and right now as they start a battle, the punches flying back and forth neither showing any signs of backing off. Until finally it’s Daniel that breaks it up pulling him into a knee to the stomach then shoving him by the head away from him, throwing out his arms to pose which the fans waste little time booing him for. RILEY STEVENS: “These two are going to tear each other apart before they can even get to their match in London.” Defias gets up as Daniel urges him to come at him as Paul rushes at him throwing a clothesline that Starr ducks under. But Daniel chooses to chuckle at Defias instead of follow it up and Paul uses his quickness to catch him from behind with a clothesline that knocks him down. These fans may never have liked Defias before but realizing he is the victim in all of this they have begun to cheer him a bit more as he stares down at Daniel then hits the ropes but Starr recovers rolling over as Defias jumps over him and then Starr jumps to his feet going for a Hip Toss but knowing his partner so well Paul shoves him away and goes for a Enzuigiri but Daniel knowing him just as well he ducks under it as well. Starr waits as Defias hops up grabbing him by the throat only for Defias to again use their tag team history to battle out of the move with a huge elbow to his head. RILEY STEVENS: “Defias and Starr know each other so well they are having issues getting moves off.” With that Defias now finally gets a move off hitting him with a big jumping knee to the head that dazes Starr as Paul hits the ropes rushing at him only for Daniel to duck under the clothesline attempt and then hit the ropes again this time Starr catches him by hitting a Hip Toss. Having battled for a bit down Starr grabs him by the head pulling him to his corner where he slaps Kluge’s hand who hops into the ring as Daniel whips him off the ropes and looking like a well oiled machine Kluge connects with ‘The Matt Cooke’ a Running Knee to Opponent's Knee on the rebound, sending Defias to the ground clutching his knee as Kluge walks around him dropping a few boots to him then hoisting him up to his feet and connecting with a Snap Suplex! RILEY STEVENS: “Defias desperately needs a tag.” Stranded in their corner Defias starts to make an effort to crawl towards his corner and rather then stop him Matt lets him so that Aspen can get into the ring with him a little earlier then their match in London. These fans cheer that on as Paul gets to the corner only for Aspen to back off from it taking a few steps back onto the steel steps to avoid the tag. Realizing she isn’t going to tag in Kluge walks forward towards Defias but as he gets up in the corner, Matt gave him time to recover and Kluge walks right into a big Enzuigiri out of no where. Suddenly Aspen is willing as Kluge stumbles backwards turning around as Chaud tags Defias and jumps into the ring trying to roll him up using the ropes as leverage 1…2…Kluge kicks out anyways. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “That was three.” Just as soon as she got in she gets back out tagging Paul who just got to the other side of the ropes as Defias is forced back into the ring as Kluge tries to crawl to his feet only for Defias to jump down trying to lock in an Ankle Lock. These fans get a bit excited about the upset but Matt will not let it happen turning to his back and kicking him away bursting up to his feet and as soon as Defias can attempt to throw a punch he is kicked in the gut then hit with the ‘Hortonville Suplex Special 1’ a Suplex rolled into a Mounted Punching as he throws punch after punch until Defias tosses him off and he gets up seeming jacked until Daniel reaches over the ropes to tag himself in. RILEY STEVENS: “That may have been a mistake.” Starr enters the ring but while they were working together at first Matt stops him before he can get back on Paul as he begins to explain that he had him right where he wanted where as Daniel argues that he should get the shot at him. While they bicker Defias heads over to the other side of the ring jumping up and tagging in Aspen who rushes at Starr and Kluge. Matt always aware of everything in the ring, he steps out allowing Daniel to eat a forearm to the back. Starr is caught off guard and thus takes another and another until Aspen has him in the ropes where she Dropkicks him back into the ropes. As he recovers she bounces off the ropes going for the ‘Tush Push’ a Turnbuckle Flying Butt Shot that connects as Starr stumbles out and Aspen heads to the top rope jumping off with a follow up ‘AirBarbie’ a Flying Crossbody across his chest landing into a pin 1…Kick Out. RILEY STEVENS: “Aspen capitalizing on Starr and Kluge’s miscue.” Aspen hops up to her feet calling for him to get up but as he does Aspen bounces off the ropes and then rushes at him only for the two to meet each other with clotheslines at the same time as the fans react as both go down to the mat both starting to crawl towards their corner’s for a tag. Aspen get’s there first and tags in Defias who jumps in as Kluge then gets the tag but Defias has the momentum and clotheslines him down once and then does it again as soon as he gets back up finally finishing the job with a Dropkick that sets Kluge right up for the Rolling Thunder. These fans are on the edge of their seats now as Paul hits the ropes but as he is turned Matt jumps up to his feet meeting him in the center of the ring where he hits a Side Belly to Belly Suplex that stops Defias momentum. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “That was a buzz kill.” Defias stumbles around as Kluge gets to his feet not noticing Starr reaching over and lightly slapping his arm as Defias turns around right into ‘The Tiger's Roar Gold Edition’ a Whale Hunt! These fans know it’s all over now but the referee pushes Kluge away as its Starr now that heads to the top rope while Matt is told about the tag as Starr comes off the top with a Shooting Starr Press that lands as he hooks the leg as Aspen doesn’t even think about re-entering the ring just jumping down off the ring apron as Starr gets the pin 1…2…3! JAMAL JACKSON: “Here are your winners…Daniel Starr and Matt Kluge!” Starr gets up onto his feet holding up his arm in victory as Aspen backs up the ramp realizing that it was better to save it for another day as Daniel stands over Defias taking the pin away from Kluge tonight. RILEY STEVENS: “Starr gets the pin over Defias tonight with some help from Kluge, will he do the same at ‘International X’.” Starr continues to trash talk him as Kluge now re-enters the ring walking up behind Daniel as he turns around to go ahead and celebrate only to walk right into ‘The Tiger's Roar Gold Edition’ a Whale Hunt from Kluge, clearly angry from how this thing ended as Starr now lays next to Defias in the ring. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Had to see that coming…” RILEY STEVENS: “All right ladies and gentleman that’s all we have for tonight but tune in for ‘International X’ where Daniel Starr and Paul Defias go one on one, Hayden Carter meets the home town champion Alexis Terry for the GIW Legacy Championship and then Matt Kluge challenges Aspen Chaud for the Undisputed title, tune in!” With that the show ends similar to how it started with Kluge in the ring looking up at Aspen who back pedals up the ramp as the show slowly comes to a close. WINNERS: DANIEL STARR & MATT KLUGE
