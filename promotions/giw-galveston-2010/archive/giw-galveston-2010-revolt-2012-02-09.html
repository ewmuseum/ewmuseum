GIW — Card

Revolt, 9 February 2012

9 February 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: THURSDAY NIGHT REVOLT LIVE! From North Beach – February 9, 2012 BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! The show begins and the crowd is already booing louder then ever as we enter the North Beach area to see David Sinclair standing in the center of the familiar ring with a microphone in hand and a smirk on his face as the crowd makes sure he hears them until finally they quiet down giving it up. DAVID SINCLAIR: “Thank you. As you all know in a week and a half’s time we will be in New Orleans for the ‘Retaliation’ Supershow. We already have two matches announced in the form of Scar versus Tony Miranda in a Taipei Death Match. Then we have the Main Event between Alex Castellanos and Aspen Chaud for her GIW Undisputed Championship. Most of you probably can’t afford the trip, so I hope you tune in to watch it live here in Galveston.” Sinclair smirks as he lowers the microphone as the fans respond accordingly with boos before jumping to their feet as "Wayward One" by Alter Bridge hits. RILEY STEVENS: “Thank God, a sane man to come out and put an end to this.” The fans get to their feet as Alex Castellanos heads out from the back making his way to the ring in a hurry with a mic in hand not looking at all excited to hear David’s intro as he steps through the ropes and walks across the ring over to David. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “You really think I am going to let you come out here and insult these fans that have been so loyal to this company? But…You know what it’s not about that this week. This week it’s about Aspen Chaud and I. About how next Sunday I will get the chance to take away her belt and put the power back in my hands. These people will he a champion they can be proud of and slowly but surely this new regime whatever you want to call it will lose power as more and more people realize what you are Sinclair a money hungry backstabber that is being helped by a manipulative bitch….” This gets the crowd going as Sinclair angrily raises the microphone to his mouth. DAVID SINCLAIR: “Listen up Alex, we don’t have to be enemies. This company is too big for this city anymore. You…Are too big of a star for this city, let’s face it. You want it just like I do, you want to be known world wide. Tell me I am wrong...” Sinclair gets closer to him, as he looks past him over his shoulder as Joshua Acquin, Aspen Chaud, and Alexis Terry head out from the back. Acquin rushing out first as the fans start to boo, trying to get Alex’s attention. Joshua slides in and Alex spins around popping him with a punch that sets him back falling out through the ropes as Sinclair scurries out of the ring. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “Do you really think I am stupid? Listen up. Joshua you pathetic slave, I am not worried about you in the least. But Alexis I know that you’ll be there to try to ruin my night at ‘Retaliation’ so let tonight be a lesson to you…And maybe just maybe when I kick you in the jaw you will realize that you don’t need Aspen she needs you.” Alex drops the mic as Aspen immediately redirects the two, as they head around the rings to get into it. Castellanos is ready to fight as they jump in from all angles but just like that the crowd pops as Henry Malkin, Marcus Hister, and Rollo Durand head out from the back right to the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Here comes the cavalry.” They slide into the ring, joining in on the little skirmish with numbers. Then the fans seem confused as Paul Defias heads down to the ring as well sliding into it. There is a momentary hesitation to see whose side he is on but when he clotheslines Durand to the mat it’s clear. RILEY STEVENS: “Defias is with Aspen!” The fans boo him as the two teams end up separating. Aspen, Alexis, Joshua, and Defias backs to the entrance way and Alex, Rollo, Malkin, and Hister ready to go. However Aspen shakes her head not wanting anything to do with it for now. RILEY STEVENS: “Typical they tuck their tails when the numbers are even. They can’t run later tonight when Aspen, Defias, and Acquin face Durand, Malkin, and Hister. Plus our Main Event featuring Alexis Terry taking on Alex Castellanos. What a night…” From there Aspen, Alexis, Josh, and Paul back up the ramp all shrugging off the fight as Alex looks at his guys with a smirk on his face as things are starting to truly heat up here in GIW. QUOTE The night starts and as is custom the night gets underway with a ring full of stars only this time we only find ourselves looking at three in the form of Nathan Bailey IV, Alexandra Rossi, and local star Mike McMichaelson. RILEY STEVENS: “Get this Oliver, the female in the ring Alexandra and Nathan Bailey are married. And apparently have had some issues so they are game to fight.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “That’s pretty exciting.” In the ring the three stand ready to go as Bailey and Alexandra head to the center of the ring for a little stare down as the referee starts it. DING DING DING! Rossi and Bailey start to exchange some words seeming to be bringing their little arguments into the ring. Mike the local boy comes closer in between the two listening into them seeming confused as both ignore him instead continuing to argue. Finally Mike yells out “Lets fight bros!” And at the same time Nathan and Alexandra look at each other and then to him at the same time nailing him with a punch that knocks him down. Mike hops back up right into the two of them as they grab him and together whip him off the ropes. Looking like a well oiled machine Bailey rushes forward catching him with a knee to the gut that doubles him over allowing Rossi to hit a Spinning Wheel Kick that puts McMichaelson down. RILEY STEVENS: “They went from arguing to teaming up like that?” Mike rolls away into the corner as just like that Nathan and Alexandra are back to bickering between each other when finally she slaps him across the face. He holds his cheek for a second then goes to grab her but she ducks under his arms and grabs him around the waist. Not really sure where she is going with it, Bailey grabs her arms and ducks under flipping her over him into a pinning situation 1…She kicks him away and hops right back up to her feet. Bailey looks at her and mouths “You want a lesson in how to wrestle?” Jumping forward and grabbing her and backing her into the ropes then hoisting her up into a Snap Suplex. RILEY STEVENS: “These two were at some point in love?” Bailey IV hops up standing over her just to taunt her but from behind foolish ol’ Mike rushes forward punching him in the back. As soon as Nathan turns around he seems to regret it as this was not what Mike signed up for. As he backs up trying to plea for him to take it easy but one swift Spinning Roundhouse Kick dazes him and then Nathan follows it up with a Snap DDT planting him. But before he can do anything else Rossi is back up and she rolls him through and literally spanks him…Catching everyone off guard as the ref makes the count along with it 1…Kick Out. Bailey hops back up as Rossi does as well, smirking at what she has just done. RILEY STEVENS: “Something tells me that might throw him off his game.” Bailey gets up raising his eyebrow at her for just a second before going to grab her only for her to duck under and then smack him with a Knife Edge Chop to the chest. Bailey heads to the corner as she follows him hitting another one then another but Nathan seems only to urge her on. Finally as the referee turns to look over at Mike she pulls him forward right into a knee in the lower regions. Bailey falls to a sitting position holding his jewels as Alexandra smirks and heads over to Mike who is up but is dazed. RILEY STEVENS: “Now Rossi is in charge.” Rossi heads over to Mike who seems only there enough to stumble forward and she hoists him over her with a Hip Toss then immediately goes for a Split Across his neck a move she calls the ‘Dethroning’ for the pin 1…2…Bailey heads over pushing her off to break the pin. Alexandra tries to get to her feet but he grabs her by the tights and spins tossing her under the bottom rope like a bowling ball. He scoffs at her as he heads over to the Top Rope where he jumps off with a ‘Cash Flow’ a Corkscrew Shooting Star Press that lands 1…2…3! JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner… Nathan Bailey IV!” Bailey hops up laughing to himself as Rossi gets up on the outside and slides in to confront him but he immediately slides out shrugging as she screams at him to get back in there as Bailey backs up the ramp. RILEY STEVENS: “Both were hugely impressive but it’s Bailey that takes out Rossi, some might call it cheating.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Funny that’s what he is good at.” Oliver has a chuckle as Rossi looks up the ramp at Bailey angry that he one upped her here tonight as it appears we might just have two new stars here in GIW. WINNER: NATHAN BAILEY IV Heading backstage after a very solid match up we find Alexis Terry walking through the halls after the earlier fight not seeming in such a good mood but when she walks towards Beck Ramsey who is leaning up against the wall she tries to turn around but it is too late as Beck ends up walking over to her. BECK RAMSEY: “Hey hold up…You must think you really got the one up on me last week? Using Josh and Austin to take me out. Real funny…” Terry looks back at him with a smile, seeming to enjoy hearing it again. ALEXIS TERRY: “Yeah it was, I especially liked the part where you, Seth, and Cassidy were lying in the ring in pain.” Alexis has a small chuckle as Beck seems to give her props for it with a shrug. BECK RAMSEY: “Yup. Now tonight I get to prove how much more of a man I am then Austin…But you could have already told me that couldn’t you?” Beck winks and walks off as Alexis angrily screams at him. ALEXIS TERRY: “That’s not true! You---” She seems to have a whole thing planned out but when Beck walks around the corner it’s no use as she lets out an angry sigh as she continues on where she was heading. ==== Else where we find ourselves with Alex Castellanos in front of Henry Malkin, Marcus Hister, and Rollo Durand addressing them. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “Thanks for having my back out there you guys. Really glad you’ve all realized that Aspen, Alexis, and all that group needs to be taken down. That being said the three of you face Aspen, Defias, and Acquin later.” Malkin nods his head confidently while Marcus seems more then ready for the match as well. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “You guys may seem like the underdogs, but if you can take out just one of them you can do it. Acquin, Defias, they are good but you are better. And if you get rid of her team Aspen will fall apart. Trust me, you guys can do this.” He seems to be preaching to a choir that already understands, as Hister and Malkin seem ready to go. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “And just on a personal note, make sure to dish out some punishment to Aspen for me.” Alex nods to them as Malkin nods while Marcus grins seeming to have some cruel thoughts himself as we take a commercial break. QUOTE Draven had already made his entry to the ring when Matlock’s theme played over the sound system. Announced as he walked to the ring, Matlock made as quick an entry inside as possible. He eyed his opponent, but only briefly as the next contender came to the forefront. RILEY STEVENS: “Matlock debuted last week and fought Draven the entire time in the Open Battle Royal. How will he do in this triple threat?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Draven is just a glorified stunt man, Matlock and Starr are the favorites here.” Daniel Starr burst to the scene and took the concise journey down the aisle to the squared circle. A glare from each and all before he entered, but when he did, everything became a little more serious. It was going to be a rough triple–threat match and only one could walk out the victor. DING DING DING! Draven was first to take the flight into battle. Daniel Starr knocked the charging enigma with a right fist. Draven quickly rose up and turned toward Matlock. Matt booted his opponent to the canvas and the combination effectively ripped Draven from his sudden burst of energy. Draven rolled himself out of the ring to take a moment of rest as Daniel and Matt took to one another like Torosaurs locking horns. Daniel forearmed his opponent’s chest and hooked a leg over the other, and titled Matlock’s body, one arm hooking over his opponent’s. Locked into the abdominal stretch, Matlock refused to give up so early in the game. Daniel applied more pressure on the submission move. RILEY STEVENS: “Starr with an early submission.” This was the moment Draven was looking for. He rushed into the ring and charged the two, but just as he reached them. Matlock countered the abdominal stretch with a hip toss that inadvertently struck Draven with Starr’s boots to his the side of his face, knocking him backward and through the ropes. Draven toppled like a wet egg noodle to the outside floor and Matlock descended upon Daniel to follow up his counter with an attack of his own. RILEY STEVENS: “You were right Oliver, Starr and Matlock have looked good.” With a side leg lock tucked neatly into form, Matlock put the heat under his opponent. Close enough to the ropes, Starr began to reach for them, but couldn’t touch freedom. With only inches away, he tried to turn the maneuver over, or break free. As each second turned, Matt locked in the submission hold tighter and wore down his opponent. Daniel knew he needed to escape this hold or it would be the end for him. He found Draven slowly ascending the rim of the ring from the floor and that was his out. Daniel struggled to get a grasp of Draven’s wrists and used his opponent to pull himself out of the hold and to the outside of the ring. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Take that loser out.” Outside, Starr quickly recovered to his feet and ran his opponent into the guard rail. Matlock followed Daniel out and struck him with a quick right, which the two begun to exchange. It was Matlock that managed to beat his opponent’s head into the canvas rim of the ring and slide him back in. He slid in after and covered Daniel Starr for a two count. Frustrated, he pulled up slowly to his feet and turned toward the referee to argue. RILEY STEVENS: “Matlock is arguing with the call.” While Matlock argued the two count Starr struggled up to his feet. Just like that he grabs Matlock and tosses him from the ring. Draven jumps back in the ring but Daniel and when he turned to him, Starr performed a brutal big boot, which he called the ‘Starr Struck’ and covered his opponent for a three count, 1…2…Matlock tries to get back into the ring…3! The fans booing him as Starr throws up an arm in victory. JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…Daniel Starr!” Matlock was just a second to late to stop the count as he slams his fists against the mat in anger as Daniel rolls out of the ring with a smirk seeming to have a knack for finding ways to win. RILEY STEVENS: “Daniel Starr wins again this time taking Matlock out of the ring then pinning Draven.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “This kid’s off to a good start.” Daniel makes eye contact with Matlock smirking at him as he exits the ring as Matlock glares back at him angry as we take a break. WINNER: DANIEL STARR We head to the backstage area where Daniel Starr walks through the ropes throwing out his arms smirking as he screams out in victory. DANIEL STARR: “I AM THE BEST!” Starr happily grins as he walks right into a battle as Alexander Rossi and Nathan Bailey turn to him for a second before arguing again. ALEXANDER ROSSI: “You got lucky you know it!” NATHAN BAILEY IV: “One…Two…Three. Nothing lucky about it.” The two continue to banter as Starr looks on at them disappointed they are ignoring his attempts to show off but soon Matt Matlock storms through to the back as well heading right over to Starr. MATT MATLOCK: “You stole that win from me, I had it!” Matlock angrily gets up into Starr’s face as the two now argue side by side as Marcus Hister can be seen standing not far away from them looking around confused. Finally as the words between the two arguments seem to filter into one, David Sinclair walks into it coughing to get their attention. DAVID SINCLAIR: “So I understand that the four of you seem to think you want another shot at one another so I have an idea. We need contenders and that’s why at ‘Retaliation’ the four of you and you Hister will wrestle each other.” Sinclair looks at the four and then over at Marcus who looks around confused at his mention. DAVID SINCLAIR: “You will fight in a GIW Shootout match. Rules are simple; pin or submit someone you get a point. Get pinned or submitted and you lose a point. Twelve minute time limit, three minute overtime in the case of a tie. Winner get a shot at the Legacy belt. Have a good day…” This seems to be ok with everyone involved as the four go in separate directions leaving Hister alone as we head back to the ring. QUOTE First to the arena was Seth Ramsey. Alone and without the aid of Beck. Seth walked the aisle in a different manner than he thought he would. He looked like a singles competitor walking into the lion’s den. He did have a partner however, but she had not arrived until after Seth had entered the ring. Seth’s partner for the night was Cassidy Hightower. She arrived quickly after Seth passed under the ropes to enter the ring. She traversed the aisle with glee and slid in. RILEY STEVENS: “Seth Ramsey and Cassidy Hightower team up tonight to face these two in a strange match where Tony and Scar, bitter rivals team up.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “What an odd couple.” Next was the dysfunctional team of Scar and Tony Miranda with Tony making the arrival to the ring first. Tony descended the aisle and ascended into the ring on his own. It wasn’t expected that either individual, whom seemed to hate one another, would ever be seen on good terms, let alone walking the aisle together. RILEY STEVENS: “Tony Miranda these past few weeks has been a changed man and a Taipei Death Match with Scar might just really show us how demented he has gotten.” Tony prepped and moved toward the tag corner he and Scar would occupy during the match. It seemed that between the two, Cassidy Hightower would be the first to compete in the bout, but it was still undecided who of Scar and Tony would go first. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Speaking of demented. This is the last guy I’d want to be in some kind of Death Match with.” That is when the lights blackened. A few of the lights raised to a filtered red hue and Scar’s modified theme began to play into the arena. He emerged from fog and darkness, Altair no where else in sight. Scar made his own entrances. It also seemed that he was starting to grow out his hair to about mid-length, enveloping the sides of the venetian mask. He stepped slowly to the ring and moved around toward his team’s corner before he climbed up to the rim of the ring by grabbing hold of the second rope and pulling his body up. He stepped toward the corner and glared with criminal intent at his partner already in the ring. DING DING DING! The lights returned to normal and the music abruptly ended. Tony Miranda would begin the match with Cassidy Hightower. The two locked up, but it became obvious that Tony was the stronger of the two when he thrust her to the canvas with a push. She glared back up at Tony and pulled up to her feet. He tied up with Cassidy a second time and he went for a push, but she countered with a hard right slap across the cheek. She insulted him for pushing a woman so he pushed her right back down to the canvas and snickered. Tony moved over toward her as she crawled backward on her elbows to retreat. He stomped at her, but not on her, just enough to make her flinch as he stalked forth. He snickered each time she would squinch. RILEY STEVENS: “Cassidy has proven that she is talented these last few weeks but still has no wins.” This ended abruptly when she took a chance and threw her boot up into Tony’s mid–section. She knicked the inner thigh, just barely missing the most tender of places, but Tony got the point. He backed off and let her up to her feet. He did this, only to traverse behind her and perform a chop block to knock her on the canvas, on her back where he felt she belonged. He snickered again, enjoying toying with his feminine opponent. Scar and Seth watched on, both seemed somewhat irritated by Tony’s slow advances. Unlike Seth, however, Scar did not hold out his hand for a tag, but stood still and calm, just waiting. Tony finally was forced to back off from Cassidy by the referee when she slid under the bottom rope during his follow up attack. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Get her Tony!” Tony turned toward Seth, then to Scar. He had this and didn’t need his partner. He let Cassidy to her feet and then pulled her from the ropes in a DDT into the ring. He pinned his opponent, but the count failed, only reaching a two. He reeled back as she crawled hastily toward her corner to tag in Seth Ramsey. Seth leapt in, eager and ready. He hoisted himself up onto his opponent’s shoulders with a Hurricanrana and slammed him into the center of the ring. Ramsey then turned to attack Scar, but realized the silent partner of Tony didn’t bother coming in to stop the hot tag. RILEY STEVENS: “You had to assume Scar and Tony would not be on the same page.” He continued with momentum unscathed. Seth performed a Powerbomb, and just as his energy started to retreat to normal, he configured in a sharpshooter to settle into the long haul of the match. Tony, however, had no plans to remain in such a hold and managed to turn over onto his back, thus applying a figure–four leg lock onto his opponent, but that too was broken quickly. The two pulled up from the canvas and it was Seth that took the lead with a few right hits. RILEY STEVENS: “Seth and Cassidy thus far have co-existed well.” Seth set Tony with an Irish whip toward the ropes whilst he took to the opposite end. Just as Tony turned and hit his back to the ropes, Scar took a tag for himself. He didn’t stop Tony, though, and his partner took the brunt of a springboard Moonsault from Seth Ramsey. When Tony was forced out of the ring by the referee, discovering what had happened, Scar entered. He finally removed the black Venetian mask and set it at the corner of the ring, on the canvas by the post. This gave Ramsey the incentive to sneak attack. He readied for a maneuver, probably his Mysterio Bulldog, but Scar had turned in the nick of time and countered with a throat thrust. RILEY STEVENS: “Here comes Scar.” He grabbed a hold of Seth’s head and hair and pulled him into a tighter grasp. Scar then performed a knee lift to his opponent’s gut, then chin. As soon as Tony ascended the rim of the ring, he smacked his partner with a tag and quickly entered the ring. Scar turned with a sharp glare in Tony’s direction. He took to the rim and just glared as Tony took hold of Seth Ramsey after the tag. RILEY STEVENS: “Tony tags himself back in.” Seth seemed out of it of sorts. Tony knew this. He set Seth up with an Irish whip across the ropes just as Seth had done to him. Tony ran toward his end as well, but just as he hit the ropes, Scar tugged on the top rope and sent his partner crashing to the matted floor and even hitting against the base of the commentary table. Scar stood back up before anyone could even second guess it. He looked down at Tony as Seth took that opportunity to take to the ropes and leapt over with a diving somersault Senton to the outside of the ring and onto his opponent Tony Miranda. RILEY STEVENS: “Big move by Seth after Scar betrays Tony.” Seth hastily through Tony back into the ring as Scar dropped down from the rim of the ring and collected his black Venetian mask. He retreated slowly as Cassidy jumped onto Tony for the pin 1…2…3! JAMAL JACKSON: “Here are your winners…Cassidy Hightower and Seth Ramsey!” Miranda kicks out just a second to late. But as he gets up, he does not at all seem tempted to argue it as instead he watches as Scar heads to the back. Rolling out of the ring and heading after him while Cassidy and Seth celebrate in the ring. Suddenly as expected BOOM Alexis Terry and Austin Reynolds slide into the ring. Alexis slams down Cassidy while Austin hammers Seth. RILEY STEVENS: “You knew they wouldn’t let them celebrate.” Alexis spins around Cassidy and tosses her from the ring. However already Austin hoists up Seth and nails him with the ‘Beautiful Disaster’ a Go 2 Sleep that puts Seth on dream street. Austin hoists up Seth and throws him over to Alexis who nails him with a ‘Flawless Victory’ a Inverted Stomp Facebreaker that puts him back down completely out as the crowd pops as here comes Beck Ramsey. Alexis and Austin immediately slide out of the ring just as Beck gets in. RILEY STEVENS: “Here comes Beck but he is too late the damage has been done.” Cassidy gets up on the outside and slides back into the ring with Beck going to check on Seth. Beck gets him awake trying to help him up but Seth seems to be really out of it as he struggles to even get his feet planted instead losing his footing as with that we go to commercial. WINNERS: CASSIDY HIGHTOWER & SETH RAMSEY Backstage we head this time in a little area that looks like a high school clique again with Aspen Chaud, Paul Defias, and Joshua Acquin all around one another talking up a storm as Henry Malkin walks straight up into the group seeming confident. HENRY MALKIN: “I know that tonight we are supposed to go out there and get killed by you guys. But whether my team comes to fight or not I am going to make sure that tonight I am the one these fans remember not any of you guys.” Defias is the one to step out of the group as Aspen doesn’t budge at all. PAUL DEFIAS: “You have some balls coming around here. The reason I joined this side…Well one of them was so I could take out pathetic losers like you.” Defias grins as Sinclair walks up seeming to be a bit caught off guard by the two fighting as they all turn their attention to him. DAVID SINCLAIR: “Listen Joshua I reconsidered your offer and I am going to reinstate that Television title of yours. You are the champion again....” Joshua smirks as he shakes his hand but Sinclair looks over at Paul and Henry and seems to get a bit of an idea. DAVID SINCLAIR: “You know what I have an idea…At ‘Retaliation’ you will defend that belt against Henry Malkin and Paul Defias in a triple threat match.” Sinclair pats Acquin on the back as he doesn’t seem pleased about being told that already as Defias and Malkin share a stare down before Malkin takes his leave as now Paul turns around over to Joshua looking at him a bit as Aspen steps up between the two. ASPEN CHAUD: “Let’s get ready.” Aspen puts her arms around the two leading them else where as we now head back to the ring for Beck’s next match. QUOTE After the break we return to the ring and immediately “The Red” by Chevelle hits and the fans begin to boo as out comes Austin Reynolds with a smirk on his face with Alexis Terry at his side coaching him up it seems all the way down the ramp to the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “This one right here seems very personal. Beck, Alexis’s former beau and her current one fighting it out to see who can get the job done.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I hate Austin, how did he get my girl?” Reynolds heads up into the ring as Alexis heads around the ring as his music fades and "Two Sides of Me" by Hinder hits and the fans go wild with cheers. How quickly they had turn to his side of this thing, now loving him as he heads to the ring seeming absolutely furious about what happened earlier. RILEY STEVENS: “Beck is a bad, bad mood after Austin and Alexis attacked his brother earlier leaving him in very bad condition.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Oh cry me a river.” Beck heads up the steps and into the ring not wasting any time rushing across the ring and tackling Austin to the mat as the referee tries to get some control. Finally he gives up and just calls for the bell. DING DING DING! Alexis screams at Austin from the outside as Beck on top throws punch after punch punishing him for what he did to Seth earlier. Finally Reynolds rolls him over and ends up on top throwing punches himself, but Beck kicks him away and both men hop to their feet. Austin backs up into the corner hoping to just get out of harms way long enough for Beck’s anger to run out of steam. Ramsey won’t have it though as he stocks him into the corner swinging at him with a clothesline. Austin ducks under and tackles Beck back into the corner, these two seeming to just be brawling rather then actually having a wrestling match. Beck tries to come forward but he receives a European Uppercut that seems to sort of kill his momentum instead knocking him into the ropes. RILEY STEVENS: “Beck is furious he is out here to hurt Austin.” Austin smirks as he takes a step back then throws one last punch that knocks Beck down into a sitting position as he backs up taunting the crowd a bit as Alexis claps him on from the outside. Beck hops up to his feet coming forward a bit as Reynolds waits for him nailing him with a big Hip Toss to the mat seeming to have killed Beck’s early energy a bit. Reynolds calls for him to get up and when he does he Dropkick’s him right back to the mat just being a jerk at this point. Austin hops up to his feet with a big old smirk on his face as he walks across the ring over to Alexis screaming “How is this for taking him out?” as Beck struggles on the mat. RILEY STEVENS: “Seems like Austin is doing just what Alexis wanted, tearing up Beck.” Reynolds walks back across the ring over to him now helping Beck to his feet this time seeming to have something in mind as he puts him on his shoulders going for a Powerslam. But Ramsey rolls down his back and out of his grasps. Austin spins around only to eat a Belly-to-Belly Suplex that sends him across the ring. Alexis is shown putting her hands on her head worried as Reynolds stumbles up into the corner as Beck heads across the ring jumping up and connecting with a Stinger Splash. The fans go wild as it lands and Austin stumbles forward right into a Reverse DDT to the mat by Beck as he makes the cover with Terry screaming at Austin to kick out 1…Kick Out. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “If I were with Alexis I would make sure to take out Beck…” Reynolds tries to roll away but Beck grabs his arm using it to pull him up and allow him to nail him with a Brainbuster that makes the ring rumble as Beck hops up to his feet, all the energy back in him as he lets out a big scream for the crowd who cheers him on as Alexis is showing angrily telling Austin to get up. Reynolds struggles to crawl over to the ropes hoping to get out of dodge. But Beck again grabs him this time by the leg going to pull him into the center of the ring but Reynolds flips over and uses both legs to kick him away and then roll out of the ring for a breather as Alexis walks over to him to check on him. RILEY STEVENS: “Apparently he wants a time out?” Beck gets up looking out at them angrily sliding out of the ring to go after him only for Austin to head around the ring as Beck chases him until Ramsey ends up walking right into Alexis while Austin slid back into the ring. Beck says something to Alexis and the two start to argue and this allows Reynolds to nail him with a Baseball Slide to the face. Alexis moves out of the way as Reynolds rushes out of the ring grabbing him and tossing him back into the ring immediately following him as he screams out “It’s over.” RILEY STEVENS: “Can Austin get the win?” He hoists Beck up onto his shoulders but before he can hit the ‘Beautiful Disaster’ a Go 2 Sleep the same move he used on Seth earlier on the night Beck knees his way out of it and then falls down beside him. Beck catches him with a German Suplex from behind but is distracted as Alexis hops up onto the ring ropes to distract him. It seems to work as Beck walks over to her telling her to get down. The distraction allows Austin to get to his feet though it takes him a second to realize where Beck is. RILEY STEVENS: “Alexis is going to cost him the match!” Austin rushes at him but Beck seeing it all coming side steps him and he slams right into Alexis knocking her off to the ground. Reynolds holds his head caught off guard as Alexis lands hard and when he turns around he walks right into a ‘Event Horizon’ his version of the Wave of the Future that connects as he rolls him over with a cover 1…2…3! JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…Beck Ramsey!” The fans go wild for Beck who hops up to his feet not letting the referee hold up his hand instead just looking from Austin and Alexis to basically show them what he’d just done as he rolls out of the ring with his Tag belt. RILEY STEVENS: “Huge win here tonight by Beck now he can go check on his brother.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “If I were Austin, I would have stopped and caught Alexis…” RILEY STEVENS: “Let it go Oliver.” Beck storms to the back not even looking back as Alexis rolls into the ring helping Austin to a vertical base as she angrily glares up the ramp at Beck as we take another commercial break. WINNER: BECK RAMSEY After a commercial break we find ourselves in David Sinclair’s office area for the night where he is looking down at some ‘Retaliation’ paper-work. Suddenly the door flies open nearly coming off its hinges as a sweaty Beck Ramsey enters the room. BECK RAMSEY: “What the hell was that! You let my brother get beat down earlier, lord knows how badly he is hurt then Austin and Alexis nearly screw me in my match. You better get some control around here before I do it myself.” Clearly upset about how the night’s gone despite the win, David looks up at him with a smile. DAVID SINCLAIR: “You know I am just looking over your brother’s medical sheet now, seems pretty bad. Doctors think he might have suffered some serious brain damage with all the shots to the face, for sure a concussion. Sounds serious...” Sinclair says this so casually as Beck takes a second to let it soak in, having come right here after his match. At a bit of loss for words over the news. DAVID SINCLAIR: “Listen kid, he was always the second fiddle to you any how. I am thinking you turn in those tag titles of yours because we are scrapping the division. Oh and your brother’s contract ends next week, so we have no plans to resign him.” Sinclair says it all, so heartless and cold. Beck finally snaps out of the daze firing back at David immediately. BECK RAMSEY: “Wait…You are going to let Austin and Alexis injure my brother then take our belts completely all in the same day? You realize I am about two seconds away from kicking your--” Sinclair stops him mid-sentence as he says his own piece. DAVID SINCLAIR: “Calm down Beck, you don’t want to do that. While your brother is out, I see a bright future for you as a singles wrestler. So I am going to book you against Alexis Terry in a GIW Legacy Championship match at ‘Retaliation’.” Though still angry, he seems to let it all soak in and nods his head wanting badly to get some revenge on Alexis as he heads out to go check on his brother as we go to the Co-Main Event. QUOTE When we return to the ring for the next contest we are greeted by “Busy Being Born” by Middle Class Rut the theme of Henry Malkin as he walks out with his team of Rollo Durand and Marcus Hister flanking him as they head to the ring together. RILEY STEVENS: “These men are the guys that seem to agree with Alex in his stance that Aspen and Sinclair run a corrupt company and they were ballsy enough to stand up to them.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “They’ll live to regret it.” The three head to their corner to discuss who will start things as their music ends and "Did It On 'Em" by Nicki Minaj hits and the fans let out a mass amount of boos comes as Aspen Chaud walks out with her GIW Undisputed Championship with Joshua Acquin and Paul Defias in front of her both seeming focused and ready to go as this group now heads to the ring, Defias showing off as he heads to the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Defias signed a deal with the devil earlier tonight joining up with Aspen, Alexis, and Sinclair.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “You mean he signed on for money, fame, and success? Sounds about right.” Defias heads up into the ring first then followed by Acquin and lastly by Aspen who steps into the ring with a grin on her face asking her guys if she can start and they willingly agree as the referee hands her belt out and the match starts up. DING DING DING! While Aspen starts on her side the other team elects Malkin to go ahead and start things off as Aspen and Henry start to circle around one another. Chaud talking to him the entire time, seeming to think he is nothing more then a bum. She goes in for a lock up and immediately he spins around her then ends up behind her holding onto an arm. She spins and swings at him only for him to duck under him and hit her with a Fireman’s Carry. Immediately he goes to lock in a Grounded Chin Lock but before he can Aspen pushes him away and crawls over to her corner tagging out immediately to Joshua. RILEY STEVENS: “Did Aspen already bail out of the ring?” Joshua steps into the ring not seeming at all deterred as the fans taunt Aspen as she takes her spot in the corner. Back in the ring Joshua and Henry now lock up and it’s Acquin that throws him across the ring taking a second to pose after all. He was clearly the bigger man as Henry bounced back up to his feet and rushes at him only for him to duck under it and then go for a Chop Block to the back of his leg. Malkin falls over holding his knee as Acquin gets up with a smirk on his face seeming to be enjoying this as he taunts him while he is down. Thinking he can win this for his team early he grabs one leg trying to twist him into a Sharpshooter but Malkin won’t have it as he kicks him away then tags out to Marcus Hister. RILEY STEVENS: “Hister steps in.” Marcus comes in as he and Joshua stare at one another; they were no strangers after all. The two lock up and share a little battle before Acquin whips him into his corner where he tags out to Defias. Acquin holds onto Hister from the back and Defias comes in delivering an unblockable kick to the stomach. Josh steps out and Paul now takes Hister down with a Jumping Knee to the Face. That fans boo as Hister falls to his back and then Defias heads off the ropes coming back and hitting a Rolling Thunder and immediately pinning him 1…2…Kick Out. Defias gets up, too confident to even worry about the two count as he calls for Marcus to get up. RILEY STEVENS: “Thus far it’s been dominance from Aspen’s team.” Hister gets up and pushes Paul away and then they run forward into a double clothesline that knocks both men down as they start to head to their corners for a tag. Aspen has her hand out as does Rollo and both get the tag. Aspen rushes across the ring and nails him with a Flying Forearm then an immediate Sitout Jawbreaker as the fans boo her the entire time. Aspen whips him into her corner and then takes the referee away as Defias and Acquin deliver shots while the referee is distracted. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Team work!” Defias and Acquin push him away as Aspen heads back after him and this time it’s Defias that distracts the referee allowing Aspen to nail him with just basically a full board kick to the groin. This is too much as Hister heads into the ring only to be met half way by Acquin who exchanges punches with him until it’s Joshua clotheslining him over the top rope. Next in is Malkin who rushes over tossing Joshua over with him as last but not least it’s Defias who gets so much steam behind him that he takes both himself and Malkin to the outside. RILEY STEVENS: “Everyone is out now.” Last in the ring is Aspen and Rollo, but not for long as she locks in the ‘K.M.A.’ [Kiss My Aspen] a Heel Lock that is in tight as the fans boo. Acquin tosses Hister over the announce table and then both Defias and Acquin stop Malkin as Rollo tries to fight it but eventually taps out. JAMAL JACKSON: “Here are your winners…Aspen Chaud, Joshua Acquin, and Paul Defias!” The fans boo as Defias and Acquin shove him backwards then slide into the ring where they are met by hugs from Aspen, one for each as the referee goes to retrieve her belt. RILEY STEVENS: “Malkin put up a good effort but it ended up three against one and it was too much.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Knew it would.” The three join hands as they raise them up high, taunting them with the win as go backstage one last time before the big one. WINNERS: ASPEN CHAUD, JOSHUA ACQUIN, & PAUL DEFIAS Alex finishes watching the end of the last match seeing Aspen tap out Rollo as he gets up disappointed as Sarah tries to calm him down a bit. SARAH SHARP: “Listen I know earlier you said it wasn’t a good idea but I think I should be down there with you for the match. I don’t trust Aspen…” Alex looks over at her not seeming to want that to happen at all. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “Malkin, Durand, or Hister will come if they need to. I don’t want you out there…” Castellanos knowing Aspen all to well knows that Sarah would end up getting herself into more trouble coming out there. SARAH SHARP: “Please…” Alex shakes his head again and then continues. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “Don’t need it. Tonight I take out the puppet then I take the belt from Aspen’s grimy hands.” Just like that a focused Alex heads out to the ring as we have the final commercial break before the huge main event. Heck I am surprised she isn’t out here right now.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “She’s honorable.” Castellanos heads to the ring even he seems surprised Aspen isn’t there as he high fives a few fans then heads up into the ring looking across at Alexis with a very focused face as the referee sets things up then calls for the bell.
