Pre-show, 4 April 2014

GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: REVOLT PRE-SHOW Live from Courville Stadium - April 4th, 2014 QUOTE Starting out here on the Pre-Show we have a ring with much less stars then we have seen the past few weeks but unlike past weeks the two in the ring are two men with GIW tenure, though neither had wrestled for a long time here in GIW each has made some noise and won some matches here as Assassin and Robbie Hart are two men in the ring, for this week’s Open Battle Royal. COLT COX: “We do only have two wrestlers in the ring this week, which does mean it’ll be a singles match now. But both men are former GIW stars, which means that the first show jitters should be out of the way.” AMANDA ROWLAND: “That’s like so cool they are back, right Colt?” These fans have chosen quickly as they have taken sides with Assassin in this one, Hart having turned them on him quickly reminding them why they hated him back in his first run. DING DING DING! Both men seem a bit different then the last time we saw them, nor sure why but none the less as the two start to circle each other it’s clear that Hart is the quicker man but Assassin is the much bigger man. Not thinking it through or perhaps having that big of an ego Hart decides to lock up with Assassin and with the huge size advantage he starts pushing Hart back to the ropes, before using them to launch him into the corner where he rushes him only for Robbie to use that speed to slip out of the way. Sending Assassin bouncing off the ropes and right into a big punch, then another from Robbie but while he lands multiple shots Assassin lands one big one that sends him stumbling back as he again charges him and again Robbie ducks under the clothesline attempt then bouncing off the ropes himself rushing back at Assassin and this time Assassin finally lands letting Robbie come to him as he connects with a big European Uppercut catching Robbie coming in as Hart stumbles back still on his feet but his jaw has to be hurting. Following it up Assassin grabs a hold of him and sends him into the corner this time charging as soon as he lets go, so as soon as Robbie turns into the corner Assassin connects with a corner clothesline rocking Hart again then pulling him out to the center of the ring dropping him with a bulldog starting to get some major momentum as he rolls over on top 1…Kick Out. COLT COX: “Assassin seems ready to go here tonight.” These fans are getting going with him, cheering him on as he gets up and looks down at Robbie enjoying putting a hurting on him. Hart stumbles to his feet, his head having to be spinning at this point as he gets back up and Assassin throws another big punch then another as he grabs Robbie and spins him going for a Russian Legsweep but Hart throws a bit of a desperate elbow that lands pretty had, releasing Assassin’s grip then flipping it around into a Side Russian Legsweep of his own as he stumbles away rubbing his jaw shaking off the beating his face took as he catches Assassin getting up with a boot to the face lowering him back to the mat. As Hart now seems to have shaken off the shots as he goes back to taunting the crowd as he pulls Assassin to his feet and shows off some strength with a Snap Suplex. Not seeming like he could lift Assassin long but able to get him enough to connect with the Suplex as he rolls over on top 1…Kick Out. Still not enough, These fans cheer Assassin to his feet, but Robbie shuts them down landing a Neckbreaker putting him right back down but he transitions right into a Boston Crab turning Assassin over, as he wrenches onto the hold as these fans start to cheer him on hoping he can get to the ropes as he starts to pull himself that way and Robbie does all he can to stop him. AMANDA ROWLAND: “Ouch that looks like it might hurt…” With one last grasp he reaches the ropes as the referee starts to count 1…2…3…4-Robbie breaks the hold and pulls away as Assassin now starts to get up slowly against the ropes as Hart walks back over to him going to spin him around but Assassin throws out a big elbow that catches him off guard. Hart stumbles back and Assassin grabs him around the waist landing with a German Suplex sending him near the ropes as well as Assassin gets back up to another cheer from the crowd as Hart slips out of the ring holding his back stumbling away to catch his breath as these fans see Assassin getting set up for something in the ring. These fans wonder what he can be doing as he sprints at the ropes then jumps through them with a Suicide Drive receiving a huge pop from the crowd, not expecting the big man to pull off a move like that as he pushes Hart to the ground on the outside as he gets back to his feet. Not as graceful as some with the move, as he gets up and calls for the crowd as the referee starts the count 1…2…Hart stumbles back as Assassin goes after him but he kicks out his knee and drops him into the barricade wall face first now…3…4…5…Hart gets up to his feet now and lines up forcing Assassin back with a shoulder thrust into the barricade…6…7…8…Hart turns to head back towards the ring but Assassin grabs him by the back and turns him around the two exchanging punhes…9…10! They are fighting all the way to the bell, as the match is ruled a double count out. REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “This match has been ruled a double count-out!” These fans are disappointed in the result but are still into it as the two continue to fight, exchanging blows as the referee slides out of the ring and steps in between the two as Assassin keeps coming forward as Hart backs up starting to head around the ring as Assassin gets held back by the referee. COLT COX: “Things heated up between these two tonight and winning their return matches meant so much to each they got caught up fighting and it ends a Double Count Out!” AMANDA ROWLAND: “That means the referee counted all the way to TEN! Whoa.” Hart backs around the ring telling him he got lucky as Assassin has pulled off on his attempt to go after him now as the two lock eyes as you have to believe these two have not seen the last of each other as the Pre-Show fades and we get ready for the Main Card. DOUBLE COUNT-OUT
