Revolt, 18 September 2014

18 September 2014
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: REVOLT! Live from the Kauffman Arena – September 18th, 2014 The GIW Logo comes on the screen letting the fans at home know that their favourite indy wrestling show is about to begin. "Bartholomew" by Silent Comedy plays and a new intro video package for Revolt airs. Showing dozens of new shots the production team has worked for weeks to create. From Michael Lynch hitting Alexis Terry with the Ram's Horn, to one of the many times we got the chance to see the beauty that is Jack Waters' Moonsault from the top rope. Other shots of Christopher Darren Erns hitting Beck Ramsey with his Malice Acrimony punt, and Artemis Kaiser putting Charity Ryan through a table are also shown. An odd image of Chuck Loveman and Mo Chronic high fiving is displayed for a split second before the lasting image of The Harlequin holding the Undisputed Championship is shown before the Revolt banner comes on the screen. Pyro goes off in the Kaufmann Arena and the fans go berserk for the new opening. A sea of signs run through the crowd is shown, some signs include "Hunt Me Tony!", "Astro Got Screwed", and "Harley > Maddie". The camera cuts sharply to a wide shot of the ring, the crowd deprived of their explosive beginning as Reverend Darryl Clinton stood in the ring with a microphone in his hands. REV. DARRYL CLINTON: "I am here tonight because I will not be silenced! The lord has given me the gift of speech, and the second amendment the right to use it!" The camera cut and panned along the crowds as he spoke, most silent, some jeering because of their detest of heavy religious folk. It then cut back to him, closer up to a mid shot. REV. DARRYL CLINTON: "If you haven't noticed, things are changing around here. It started with the firing of Charity Ryan, then Alexis Terry, and now the whole structure of the GIW staff has been sent into hell itself! I myself have been removed from my duty as an announcer and fired without even so much as a 'Goodbye Reverend Clinton!'" The crowds began to chant the very thing he wanted, but he ignored it as his face reddened and he turned to them as if preaching to the choir. REV. DARRYL CLINTON: "I will not stand idly as a man of God and let them strike us down. I will not stand idly by while God's children suffer the shackles of this new regime! Now, I do not entertain the vain viewpoint or the futile notion of the absurd idea that I am going to answer the question once and for all time on how to fight back against this evil, but I will have a final word. We people are experiencing trials of cruel mockings and scourging, yea, moreover bonds and imprisonment: we were stoned and sawn asunder! We will fight back! We will raise an army and they will be defeated by the sword! They will be the people slain by the sword and we will slay by the sword and we will do it with faith!" The camera panned out to a wide short from the corner of the ring so that the stage was in view as the lights blackened before he could continue his ranting. The camera cuts to a closer view of the stage, the entrance and and a building fog as the dim crimson hue bleed into the area through the lighting. A black figure appeared from behind the curtain and slowly began to step down the aisle. RILEY STEVENS: "What's going on here?" OLIVER THIBODAUX: "There is something treacherous going on here. Who is this guy? Why was the Reverend fired?" Attired in black leather and a black mask that covered his face and head, shaped sleek and almost inhuman, the man made his slow descent to the ring. He climbed into the ring and stepped through the ropes to meet the Reverend face to face, nearly towering over him. REV. DARRYL CLINTON: "Behold, he cometh with clouds; and every eye shall see him, and they also which pierced him: and all kindreds of the Earth shall wail because of him. Even so, Amen." The camera cut a close up of the two, the taller, masked man reached out like lightning and grasped Reverend Clinton by the throat, then took the microphone from his quivering hands. ???: "I am Alpha and Omega, the beginning and the ending, saith the Lord, which is, and which was, and which is to come, the Almighty. Your time has come." The lights blackened again and just as they did the arena lit back up. The camera searched, but there was nothing in the ring. RILEY STEVENS: "This is the oddest start to Revolt in a while." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Maybe, but I have a feeling we've casted out the devil we know for one we clearly don't.." With that, the camera cut and the show went to a break. QUOTE Cutting back to the ring, our first entrant in the next match hits the stage with his new theme, “Black Mass” by Electric Wizard playing behind Michael Lynch. Instantly, he’s met with the usual chorus of boos and hisses from the loyal GIW fans. Michael never acknowledges the crowd as he slides into the ring and heads to a ring post immediately, awaiting the arrival of his opponent. RILEY STEVENS: “Who’s your favorite to win this match tonight, Oliver?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Well, obviously the safe answer is Lynch! Looking to repeat his actions from last week, but I’ll admit…that extra side of Sam Hain may prove to be a tricky one.” A few seconds pass before “Heart Shaped Box” by Dead Sara hits and GIW’s own Newfie, Artemis Kaiser steps out from the curtains, instantly locking eyes with her opponent for the night. The crowd shows their love for Artemis as she makes her way down the ramp and eventually climbs into the ring. Kaiser might have fallen short a week before, but she’s ready for her rematch and she definitely shows it as she never breaks eye contact with Lynch. RILEY STEVENS: “Just look at this! This match might kick off before Hain even gets a chance to step in!” “Electric Sheep” by sHEAVY hits and it isn’t long before the returning Sam Hain makes his way out from the curtains, receiving a heavy reception of boos from the fans in attendance. It’s clear that Sam doesn’t look too thrilled to deal with these fans, but he snaps back into focus as he stares down Artemis and Lynch before entering the ring The crowd shows their complete hatred for Lynch as the self-proclaimed Messiah just smirks, not allowing the crowd to get to him. Double checking his wrist tape, Hain just rolls his neck in anticipation for the match as the crowd hasn’t stopped booing Again, the crowd cheers for Kaiser as she steps up and yells out, getting hyped for the match; never turning her back on Lynch. As soon as the Reverend clears the ring, the two competitors begin to circle the ring, waiting for the bell to ring and it isn’t too much longer before their wish is granted. The two quickly lock up and Lynch overpowers Artemis; shoving her down to the mat. The Archangel starts the trash talk early, but he’s shut up by a swift kick to his temple; sending him out of the ring in a hurry. With Lynch out of the picture, Sam Hain quickly takes advantage of a taunting Artemis with a sudden roll-up, but it doesn’t even make it to a one count. Already arguing with the official, Hain makes the dangerous mistake of turning his back on Artemis Kaiser and gets a swift kick to the temple as a result. Caught off guard, Michael takes a few seconds to collect himself outside of the ring, but he’s not safe there either as Kaiser takes a note out of the Toronto Blue Jays’ playbook and slides right into her opponent. Artemis doesn’t hesitate to throw Lynch back into the ring and follows him in. Before any more offense can start; Michael pulls himself up with the aid of the ropes as Kaiser takes a quick breather with a smirk on her own. It’s evident that the Messiah is a bit embarrassed at getting taken down so early into the match as he kicks the bottom ring rope, shouting in some incoherent babble. RILEY STEVENS: “Just what did he say?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Probably something derogatory about your mother.” The match continues as Lynch meets up with Artemis again in the center of the ring and the two waste no time trading blows. Michael’s arsenal of Japanese influenced stiff forearms and strikes are countered with professionally trained Kaiser kicks focusing on Lynch’s legs and body. The height advantage plays into Lynch’s favor early on in the exchange, but a strong series of kicks to Michael’s right knee causes it to buckle underneath him. On one knee, the Archangel finds himself in a very compromising position as Artemis rears up a powerful kick aimed directly at Lynch’s temple, but the devious masked villain reacts fast and grabs hold of Artemis’ leg. Using her leg as a support, Michael gets back up and levels Kaiser with a devastating lariat that takes her down. A sudden pin fall attempt is next, but Artemis breaks out before a one count. Early on, Artemis is already starting to frustrate her opponent with her resilience and sheer determination. Finally shaking the cobwebs free from that kick, Sam Hain re-enters the match after taking a breather and catches Michael Lynch with a DDT out of nowhere. A bit of adrenaline seems to be coursing through Sam’s body as he lifts up Kaiser and tosses her over to the corner like a ragdoll. Charging at full speed, Hain connects with a clothesline, but that isn’t enough to bring Artemis down as she somehow manages to stay up. Not wanting to be embarrassed like Lynch, Sam quickly brings a second clothesline, but Artemis still stays up! Out for blood, Sam goes in for a third one, but the luck of the Irish isn’t on his side as he gets a Falcon Kick straight to the jaw, a beautifully executed Yakuza kick that might have taken the Wisconsin native out of commission. RILEY STEVENS: “Oh my! OH my! That’s enough to break a man’s jaw! Or dislocate it at the very least!” Watching from his own corner, Lynch finally props himself back up to his feet to take on Artemis in the center of the ring. However, he’s prepared himself for a kick from Artemis, but is surprised as the smaller opponent grabs hold of Lynch! Artemis uses her training to grapple her way behind the Messiah and in an impressive feat of strength, lifts him up for a beautiful German suplex. But she doesn’t just stop at one, lifting Lynch up yet again and transitioning into a Dragon suplex and with her hands still around Michael, locks in an anaconda choke. With the ropes luckily nearby, Michael puts his foot on one to save himself from tapping. But that doesn’t stop Artemis from making her hold even tighter as the referee begins the usual five count that just happens to stop after an exaggerated four count and pleads her case to the ref that she just couldn’t hear him. Not paying attention to her opponent; Michael quickly rushes Artemis into a corner and repeatedly drives his knee into her side. Another four count from the referee forces Lynch to back off the wounded prey. But that doesn’t mean he won’t yell at Artemis; verbally assaulting her in the corner. Trash talking during a match almost never seems to go as planned as a standing dropkick from Sam Hain sends Lynch right into the corner and right into Artemis’ arms. Sam sees the opportunity ahead of him and steps into a corner before launching off for a body splash into his opponents! The proclaimed Messiah feels the pain early and falls over on the mat while Sam takes a brief moment to boast about his tactics. However, his moment of glory comes to a halt as Kaiser’s body has fallen right on top of Lynch 1…2… And Kaiser’s body is pulled away quickly by Hain; looking a bit red in the face after that slip up. Kicking the ropes in frustration, Sam looks to set up the finish for the match, picking Artemis up with a sinister smile on his twisted face. Looking for a bit of payback, Hain goes off the ropes with a huge big boot that completely destroys Artemis Kaiser! He goes for the pin! 1…2…KICK OUT! RILEY STEVENS: “What a fight we’re seeing from Artemis Kaiser tonight!” By the grace of some higher being, Artemis Kaiser managed to kick out! Hain rushes the referee into a corner to get some words in with him as Michael Lynch begins to make it back up to his feet; stalking his prey in the corner. Lynch slowly walks up to Sam as the crowd hushes down in anticipation for what’s about to come next. Grabbing ahold of Hain’s shoulder and whipping him around, a Ram’s Horn connects right to the back of his head! The roaring elbow sends the crowd into a frenzy as Hain just collapses, but their joy is quickly silenced as the last man standing turns his attention to his female opponent. Michael picks up Kaiser, meeting Lynch with a few quick jabs to the stomach, but that doesn’t work as Lynch just slaps the taste out of Artemis. Getting tired of her antics, Lynch quickly puts Artemis to rest with a Tiger Driver that he calls the Son of Lynch. Boos are heard all around the arena as the Archangel puts his boot onto Artemis’ chest as the official makes the count. RILEY STEVENS: “Oh, that’s just disgraceful!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “What do you mean? Lynch is just using his size to make sure Artemis stays down! No disrespect about it!” 1…2...3! WINNER via PINFALL: MICHAEL LYNCH! As Revolt comes back from our sponsor's commercials, we find ourselves in the middle of an office. Bookcases and well kept file cabinets surround the room's walls. The camera pans around the room in efforts to capture every inch of the cleanliness of the office, until we see a wooden desk directly across from the wooden door. Covered in loose-leaf papers, full binders stacked on top of one another, it seems like this desk has been the epicenter for importance. The person sitting firmly on the opposite side of the table is one the GIW fans have come to known ever since Blackout. Auva Skye, the new head of Galveston Island Wrestling, is fiercely typing out paragraph after paragraph on her laptop before her concentration is cut short due to a few knocks that come from the door. AUVA SKYE: “Come in!” Just as her demand comes to an end, the door slowly creaks open and from behind the doorway comes forth a man donning a bright orange Hawaiian button-up over a pink 'My Little Pony' shirt. The man steps into the office and tips his fedora that rests above his stringy, straggly dark black hair. ???: "Hello my fair lady, I have returned!" As Auva looks up at the husky man, she raises an eyebrow in confusion. AUVA SKYE: “I'm sorry... who are you?” The man cracks a smile and makes his way to the edge of her desk. THE SIR: "They used to call me The Sir. I received the invitation you sent out to all past superstars and I gladly accept." Auva seems to become even more puzzled as she closes her laptop, obviously more intrigued than before. AUVA SKYE: “My apologies... The Sir... I don't recall sending out any invitation to anyone. However if you're so inclined on returning to the company, next week I can feature you in the pre-show open bat-” The Sir quickly shoots out his arm and points his index finger near her lips. Shocked, and becoming increasingly perplexed as each hand of the clock ticks by - Auva quickly pushes his hand away. THE SIR: "Hush, hush. No need to fret my dearest, sweet crème brûlée, I will take on any folk who try to challenge me. There isn't any rhyme or reason for me to waste my select talents against those puny toothpicks that enter the battle royal. Feed me whoever you got, I'll gobble them up like a plate of rib eye steak - chicago styled, medium rare." Still at a loss for words, Auva sits there still trying to collect her thoughts. The Sir places his elbows on the desk and rests his head in his palms. With his eyes in full puppy-dog mode, the man who was once a sex symbol in GIW, tries to convince her once more. THE SIR: "You know you want me here. You can't fight the urge Ms. Skye, everyone needs a taste of The Candyman again." Like the gentleman that he is, The Sir reaches for Auve Skye's hand. She reluctantly lets him take it gently by his palm. He smiles, and leans in to kiss it. As she sees him lick his cracked lips, she quickly pulls back her hand in disgust. AUVA SKYE: “Okay! Okay! Okay! I'll give you a match next week Sir, just please leave already!” Although he was turned down, The Sir smiles and gentle tips his fedora towards Auva. He turns around and makes his way towards the door. Before leaving he looks over his shoulder at Auva Skye, who hasn't taken her eyes off of him as of yet. THE SIR: "The name's 'The Gentle Sir' now, lambchop pie." And with a quick and sudden wink, The Gentle Sir leaves the room leaving us with a lasting shot of a stunned and mortified Auva Skye before heading back into the arena for our next match. QUOTE Following that, the series that everyone has been waiting for is finally up as “The Hunter” by Mastodon hits and the fans explode for the Hall of Famer’s appearance. Hood up, head down, Tony Miranda steps down the ramp ignoring all of the fans cheering for him. He’s in the zone and being anywhere else for this match could cost him the series. Tony steps into the ring, taking a brief moment to look up at the cage hanging above him, and he just smirks underneath his hood. RILEY STEVENS: “Miranda is ready tonight! And trust me, I know when Tony is ready to throw down!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Yeah, yeah, Tony this and Tony that, but let's focus on the real deal tonight, Riley. JACK WATERS, BABY!" Silence as Miranda’s eyes are locked on the curtain. “People Are Strange” by Johnny Hollow wails out of the speakers in the always eerie fashion as Jack Waters steps out from the curtain with Puppa trailing behind him. Of course, these two are met with thunderous hate from the crowd, but that’s definitely not bothering Jack tonight; still riding with high confidence heading into this match, even if Puppa will have a hard time interfering. Waters slides into the ring and sits down in the corner, listening to the advice Puppa is giving him before the cage makes its way down. The cage begins to slowly descend as a collective deep breath is taken by nearly everyone in the building. Miranda looking as calm as always while he takes his hoodie off and tosses it to the outside ring hand. Puppa steps back, making sure to blow a kiss toward Miranda’s direction, as she begins rooting for her client. The cage finally settles around the ring and the two men each take a moment to place a hand on the cage; pure steel. They know what tonight means. A chance to retire a legend early. A chance to keep fighting for another day. The Hunter. The Monster. Before the bell even has a chance to ring, the two men rush one another and meet in the center of the ring, exchanging a series of stiff blows. Tony gets the upper hand with an uppercut that sends Jack back into the ropes, but he quickly rebounds with a steady lariat that takes Miranda down. Jack pops back up with an arrogant grin painted on his face while Tony just keeps his silent glare act going; the crowd slamming their palms onto the barriers in excitement. Locking up, Miranda pulls Waters into a clinch and delivers a beating with a mixture of knees and elbows to the head. Dazed, but not totally out of it, Waters is able to push his opponent back; forcing him to release the grip, and now Tony has a grin of his own; telling Waters to “bring it on”. Miranda quickly locks in a headlock on Waters and takes him down with a Judo-esque toss, taking another chance to deliver some punches straight to Jack’s dome. Puppa yells at the referee to do something, but he’s powerless. It isn’t until Jack manages to free an arm and poke Miranda right in the eyes is when he’s freed from the hold. Trying to regain his vision, Miranda falls victim to an enziguri that takes him down to the mat. Not wanting to risk taking Miranda to a ground game, Jack stands his ground and rivals the punches of his opponent with just straight up stomping Tony’s face and upper chest. The dirty tactics definitely don’t win the fans over as they let their displeasure for Jack’s methods known, but being up 2-1, he doesn’t care what it takes to win. Jack lifts Miranda up and takes a moment to look at the cage wall; a sickening grin developing as a small buzz begins to generate amongst the fans. Set to launch Tony fast first into the cage, Jack takes a leading step, but comes to a halt as Miranda manages to stop his momentum; switching the roles around and tossing Waters face first into the cage! Jack’s flesh meets the steel in an ugly harmony and Waters comes crashing down; his head bouncing off the ring with a sickening thud. Puppa begins screaming at Jack who looks like he’s completely out of it. Concussion or not, Miranda has a match to win as he takes hold of Jack’s head and brings him right back down with a snap DDT. Lifting him up again, Miranda hits Waters with a European uppercut followed by another quick DDT. Dragging him around, Tony grabs a hold of Waters’ legs and catapults him right into the cage for the second time. Taking advantage of the stunned Jack Waters, Miranda pounds on his chest with his fist, thirsty for blood. Stepping into a corner and waiting for his prey to awaken, Tony taps on his right knee as the crowd gets behind him. Insisting that he stay down, Puppa bangs on the cage to tell Waters to just stay down, but he’s not listening to any of it. Whether it be pride or the possible brain damage, he gets up on his own, but just falls victim to The Knee RILEY STEVENS: “A flying knee strike! That’s now the fifth attack focusing on Waters’ head!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Just stop this match already and award it to Jack Waters! Tony is acting like a bully!” Taking a look at the wounded animal, Miranda goes for the pin 1… 2… KICK OUT! The crowd boos and Miranda shares their frustration as Waters kicks out at the last minute. Miranda taps his knee again and gets ready to launch it into Waters’ head yet again, but the hurt rookie manages to pull himself together to trip up Tony as he was heading back to the corner. Jack quickly jumps onto Miranda and starts to unleash a barrage of punches to Miranda. Tony shifts his weight and throws Waters over before any more damage can be done; both men looking to end each other fast. Jumping back up at the same time; Jack makes the first move by rushing Miranda against the cage wall, his head tucked under Miranda’s chin. Taking a page out of Muhammad Ali’s book, Jack starts to deal out some kidney punches that send a sharp pain coursing through Miranda’s body. A few strong forearm clubs to the back slow Jack’s onslaught, but not by much as the younger man keeps the punches coming. Finally breaking free by just using all of his strength to shove Waters over, Miranda hangs on to the ropes, clutching his sides. But he sees a chance to strike as he goes for The Knee again! But Waters had scouted this one out, rolling out of the water, and countering with a Clothesline from Hell that nearly causes Tony to flip! Tony recovers quickly from the clothesline, but eats a boot, a Memory Check dropping the veteran yet again. On his knees, Waters slams his fists on the mat, screaming “IT’S OVER!” A sudden surge of energy hits Waters as he begins to hype himself up. Jack steps over Miranda’s body looking for the pin, but stops, taking a moment to look at his opponent and he just shakes his head. RILEY STEVENS: “What’s he doing?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “He’s ending Tony Miranda’s career, that’s what he’s doing, genius!” Jack looks up at the cage wall and takes ahold of it, slowly climbing up it. Heights, the natural fear of many, didn’t bother Waters for the moment as he makes his way to the stop of the cell; looking for an old school escape, but he doesn’t have any intention to climb back down. Miranda manages to wake back up, but it’s a bit too late as Jack has gone airborne! The MAME connects and both men come crashing down onto the mat; the crowd goes absolutely nuts! Puppa can’t believe it, the fans can’t believe it, and the commentators are speechless! With the reserves of energy he has left over, Jack puts his arm across Miranda’s body 1… 2… 2.9! The building erupts! His jaw wide open, Waters just looks at the ref in disbelief; begging for the victory. Pulling himself up with the ropes, Jack pleads his case toward the official. He just hammers his fist into the canvas yet again in anger and pulls himself up to a standing position. Leaning on the turnbuckle, Jack begins screaming at Tony to get back up. Listening to his request, Miranda does just that. A second Memory Check superkick sends Miranda down to the mat. This kick busts Tony open at the bridge of his nose as the camera gets a good shot of it trickling down his face. Realizing his mistake, Waters quickly goes for a cover, but Miranda breaks out before the pin even starts. The taste of his own blood fuels Miranda up as he hits one snap suplex, then another, then another! The Hunter begins to take control and Jack up for one more suplex; holding him in place. Letting all of the blood rush to his opponent’s head; Miranda slams Jack back down onto the mat. Waters leaps up in pain, but gets held back down by Tony looking for the pin 1… 2… KICK OUT! Tony Miranda just bites his lip; even with all of their energy used up, the fight of a warrior lives on. Miranda picks his opponent up and grabs a hold of his head; driving his own right into it! Headbutt after headbutt only forces more blood out of Miranda’s nose, but he doesn’t care! After about six headbutts, Miranda just tosses Waters to the side. Wiping the blood beginning to form at Miranda’s forehead and slide down into his eyes; Jack uses this chance to hit Miranda with a low blow that brings the Hall of Famer down to his knees! Miranda clutches over, holding on tightly to the family jewels as Waters slowly gets to his feet. Feeling some confidence in him, Jack slaps the back of Tony’s head before hitting him with a low angled enziguri that knocks Tony over. Puppa screams for Waters to finish the match and he nods in agreement. Miranda is lifted up from behind and finds himself caught for the set up to Jack Waters’ finisher. Jack sticks his tongue out at the audience and oh-so arrogantly runs his hand through his hair, looking to drop Miranda, but the Hall of Famer holds his ground! OLIVER THIBODAUX: “NO! NO!” Miranda breaks free and decks Waters with a stiff hook to the temple. Tony Miranda is ready to end this as he lifts Waters up and sends him roaring down onto the mat with a huge Uranage! Miranda smells the blood and quickly flips over, locking in an Americana! Locked tightly in the center of the ring, Miranda wrenches Waters’ arm to the point of breaking it before Waters has to reluctantly tap out! The series is now tied! WINNER via SUBMISSION: TONY MIRANDA! After the grueling Steel Cage match that we were just witness to, the camera gives us a close up of the announcers, who are attempting to do their job after recovering from seeing such a brutal contest. RILEY STEVENS: "And now, ladies and gentlemen, after that amazing battle between two of GIW's best... we now change gears as we will take a very exclusive look at GIW’s “secret weapon”… A man who has trained for this moment for the last 6 years… A man who has been signed with GIW since the start but has refused to debut until he felt he was ready. But now… now he’s ready. We present to you … The International Sensation… M. Tech Michaud." A hype video plays exclusively featuring M. Tech Michaud showcasing his lightning fast strikes and kicks. In the background, very intense drums play in the background making this presentation seem even more legendary than it already is. When the video is done, Oliver Thibodaux is seen staring at the camera and shaking his head in amazement. OLIVER THIBODAUX: "You know, Riley, over the years there have been plenty of things we’ve had disagreements with but this man… M. Tech Michaud… he is unanimously regarded within the offices of GIW as the secret weapon - a man who was built to be the greatest of all time – a man with enormous discipline unmatched by any other superstar. Riley, it’s safe to say – that the next big player in GIW has finally arrived." RILEY STEVENS: "You’re right, Oliver, M. Tech Michaud is the most impressive talent we’ve seen… with over 50 titles won in several different countries, at the age of 30, M. Tech Michaud is an expert at high-impact striking and submission maneuvers. Although he will be debuting soon, this won’t be his first rodeo. You see, M. Tech Michaud is the most experienced superstar GIW has to offer. It’s safe to say that he will be successful. In the back, we have a special interviewer as well so that we can get a first look at the man GIW has been saving for the longest time." We go to a very fancy office where there are two wooden chairs. A man comes up to one of the chairs, holding a microphone and paper in hand. The man stares at the camera. It’s everyone's favourite villain, Wake Walker. The entire audience goes crazy for a few seconds, not believing Wake Walker has returned. Wake begins speaking. WAKE WALKER: "Ladies and gentlemen, Wake Walker is indeed here but as we all know, my career has ended. However, with one legend having ended – another begins. Please welcome… the greatest superstar of all time … M. Tech Michaud!" A tall and very muscularly imposing Asian man in a tuxedo sits down on one of the other wooden chairs and smirks very confidently. He shakes the hand of Wake Walker. WAKE WALKER: "M. Tech Michaud… you have been specifically trained by me and others within the GIW-sphere to take on and DESTROY competitors the likes of Aspen Chaud, Madison Seton and even myself … having said that, with over 50 title belts on your resume … what type of impact do you think you will make in GIW right now?" M. Tech Michaud smirks and then looks at Wake Walker with a fiery intensity in his eyes. Finally, he speaks. M. TECH MICHAUD: "Instead of telling the people, why don’t we just show them?" Wake Walker begins looking a little nervous, as if he feared M. Tech Michaud. WAKE WALKER: "Mister Michaud, on that note, there have been several rumors on tabloids mentioning that you will never debut in GIW … is this true? Do you still not feel ready?" M. TECH MICHAUD: "Don’t trust these phony news outlets. They are, after all, tabloids." WAKE WALKER: "Michaud… I mean, Mister Michaud… you said you had a huge announcement to make." M. TECH MICHAUD: "That’s right, Walker. I will debut next week on Revolt once and for all and not only will I prove that I belong in GIW, I will establish myself as the best of all time." The crowd cheers as M. Tech Michaud smirks. The screen fades with Walker looking shocked. We catch the reaction of both announcers. They are both looking at each other with their mouths open, looking absolutely bewildered. We cut to a commercial break. QUOTE Revolt comes back from commercial, "Awake My Soul" by Mumford and Sons is playing when we return with a shot of Kevin Astro already in the ring testing the ropes readying himself for his rematch against the Deep South Champion. Soon enough, "Hello Zepp" by Charlie Clouser plays on the P.A system and the lights dim. The fans begin to boo as the man that once was their idol who has now aligned himself with the devil, Christopher Darren Erns walks onto the ramp. With a smirk across his face, he taps the Deep South Championship which is draped across his shoulder and starts making his way down the ramp. RILEY STEVENS: These two had a super close battle last weekend at Blackout, unfortunately for them and all of us that match resulted in a count-out draw. Thankfully Auva Skye has given us this rematch here tonight, hopefully these two can keep it inside the ring this time around. OLIVER THIBODAUX: If only she made this match a Falls Count Anywhere match then we could have seen these two battle throughout the arena. Its obvious these two absolutely despise one another, I can't wait to see them slug it out again! Erns makes his way to the ring and walks his way up the steel steps and through the middle rope.
