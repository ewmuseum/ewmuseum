GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: EXTREME TV LIVE FROM THE GALVESTON ISD GYM QUOTE Opener No Participants Showed WINNER: N/A QUOTE Mid-Card The first action for ETV featured a returning Jacoby Jackson taking on a debuting Jamie Styles. In the end it was all Jacoby who showed a different side then the fans were used to. Showing little respect he dismantled Styles, easily able to end it a lot quicker but instead dragging it on a lot longer then it needed to be. In the end the Gym was filled with Silence as the fans didn’t even know what to do. When it was all said and done the massacre ended with a ‘Jacoby Jolt’ a Swinging Reverse STO that put a exclamation point on a strong return for Jacoby Jackson, whose performance surely earned him a spot on Revolt. WINNER: JACOBY JACKSON QUOTE Main Event Simply put this was not one of your women’s power types of matches; instead it was the complete opposite. The Killers showed little remorse in dismantling the much smaller and weak Reyna Silva and Brandy Danielle. This was not for the feint of heart because it featured a whole lot of terrible moves that seemed nearly like they would snap the girls in half. The fans were just as silent as they were in the Jacoby fight but with girls involved it only made them even more sick. The finale came with ‘The Killer Move’ a Flapjack DDT to Brandy Danielle that ended the contest. In what was perhaps the darkest point in ETV’s history The Killers hit the same move on Reyna after the match, to end what was nothing less then a brutal day of ETV all around. WINNER: THE KILLERS (Brody Harper & Nelson Fielder)
