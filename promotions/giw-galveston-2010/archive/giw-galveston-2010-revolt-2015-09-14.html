GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS SUNDAY NIGHT REVOLT 9/14/15 FROM THE GRANADA BALLROOM IN SAN ANTONIO, TEXAS After the GIW logo flashes across the screen, we cut to a beautiful scene – the River Walk in San Antonio. A GIW cameraman is stationed on the other side of the river, attempting to focus his camera in on a man and woman having a conversation. When he finally gets it clear, we see that it is David Sinclair and Aspen Chaud. Their voices boom across the screen. ASPEN CHAUD: "This is the proof I asked for?" Sinclair handed her a small manila envelope with a smile that we could barely make out over the distorted scene. Aspen opened it and began to comb through the contents. DAVID SINCLAIR: "Absolutely. I had to call in a few favors, but this is the proof. The best thing is, the video isn’t even doctored. There is no way she can deny this." She pulled out a DVD. It caught the light of one of the street lamps overhead, shining a beam directly into the camera’s lens. ASPEN CHAUD: "I’ll let you do the honors of making this public then. Nice work." He shook his head and held out his hands. DAVID SINCLAIR: "I don’t want the glory. I serve you, Aspen." Aspen dropped the DVD back inside the envelope. ASPEN CHAUD: "Well, I’ll be more than happy to drop the axe." Sinclair snickered. DAVID SINCLAIR: "I figured. Keller’s been a thorn in your side for a while." Aspen let out a sigh, and then a small laugh. ASPEN CHAUD: "She was a thorn in my side." Sinclair mimicked her closely. DAVID SINCLAIR: "Was." A long pause came between the two of them. Aspen reached out her hand. Sinclair shook it. ASPEN CHAUD: "Thank you David. How can I repay you?" He pulled his hand away slowly as he glanced cautiously to the left and to the right. DAVID SINCLAIR: "No need. It’s my job." Aspen placed her hand on his shoulder. ASPEN CHAUD: "Well you go above and beyond the call of duty. Have a good night." He nodded. DAVID SINCLAIR: "You also, Aspen." The camera faded out. Next, we find ourselves in the Granada Ballroom where the commentary team is looking down at their monitors in a state of shock. Sharp and Thibodaux raise their heads. SARAH SHARP: "Oliver, do you know anything about this?" OLIVER THIBODAUX: "No… I don’t. But last week, when Katie Keller paraded around Aspen’s ring with dirt on our Undisputed Champion, you had to figure Aspen wouldn’t let that slide." SARAH SHARP: "Well… whatever is in that envelope is apparently pretty damning. We’ve said over and over again that Katie Keller needed to watch what she was dealing with… it doesn’t look like she did." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "There isn’t a person on this planet that is good enough for Sinclair not to find dirt on. It wouldn’t have mattered anyway." SARAH SHARP: "I’m sure Aspen will come out here tonight and end all of the speculation, but now it is time to get the show on the road." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Roll the intro, Chuck!" The Revolt intro plays followed by the traditional BOOM BOOM BOOM! And just like that, it was time for the TV Championship match. QUOTE The camera fades in from commercial on wide shot of the ring. Luke Jones and Kelsi Ross are already in the ring while Dr. Kolby Ross is present at ringside. Jones is resting with his back to one of the turnbuckles. His arms are resting on the ropes and he’s staring toward the entrance ramp. Ross on the other hand is bent over the top rope discussing strategy with her wife and manager. We cut to a close up of Ruby Reynolds standing in the center of the ring, mic in hand. The lights of the arena are dimmed slightly and the background music is phased out. RUBY REYNOLDS: "Ladies and gentlemen this opening contest is scheduled for one fall and is for the GIW Television Championship. Already in the ring are then challengers, Luke Jones and Kelsi Ross!" There are some cheers and a few boos and the mention of the challengers’ names. Luke Jones postures up in the corner and the fans cheer again. Ross tries the same but is met with booing. SARAH SHARP: "Bit of a mixed reaction there, Ollie. The fans are clearly behind Luke Jones but no so much for Kelsi Ross." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "It’s a shame really. Ross is the far prettier of the two." Pink lights start to shine along the lip of the entrance stage. The fans are cheering again, they know what’s coming. RUBY REYNOLDS: "And lastly, from Newcastle, England. She is a GIW Hall of Famer, the reigning and defending GIW Television Champion, Alexis Terry!" “Heaven's A Place On Earth” by Belinda Carlisle plays out of the Granada Ballroom’s soundsystem. Seconds later Alexis Terry walks out from the back with her title fastened around her waist. The gold face-plate of the Television Title glitters under the pink lights of Alexis’ entrance. The crowd is hyped for Alexis. Signs with her name and likeness on them scatter the crowd. There’s even a “two time, two time TV champion” chant. Alexis makes her way down the ramp quickly making sure to touch hands with as many fans along the entrance way as she can. SARAH SHARP: "The crowd is hot for Alexis tonight. What a way to open the show!" OLIVER THIBODAUX: "I might have liked Alexis better when he was running with Aspen but it’s always nice to see her with gold around her waist. Reminds me of the good old days." Alexis undoes the belt around her waist and holds it above her head. She climbs to the second rope in nearest corner with her belt still hoisted high. The fans are still chanting and clapping for her when she jumps down and hands the belt to the official. Apprentice referee, Jadore Dior-Ranezeb holds the belt up for the crowd to see and the hands it off to someone at ringside before calling for the bell. DING! DING! DING! The match gets off to a slow start with all three combatants circling each other in the ring. Kelsi Ross is the first to make a move, shooting in on Luke Jones. Jones manages to sidestep Ross’s charge. Ross bounces off the ropes and tries for a second Big Boot but again, Luke Jones dodges, this time with a leapfrog. Terry makes herself known by catching Ross with an Enziguri when she comes off the second rebound. Ross goes down hard but is back on her feet quickly. Luke Jones and Alexis briefly lock eyes and nod. Together they run the ropes and hit Ross with a Double Clothesline that lays her out flat. SARAH SHARP: "Nice teamwork by Jones and Terry in the opening minutes of this match." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Like I’ve always said, teamwork is a good strategy early on, but ultimately it’s every man for themselves out there." SARAH SHARP: "True, but these three have been in this match so many times that they should already know that." Terry and Jones continue to work together to take out Kelsi Ross. They hit a Double Suplex and a Double DDT! Both moves look fierce and connect with sickening cracks. Ross finally has enough and rolls out of the ring to safety. Dr. Kolby Ross attends to her client on the outside while Jones and Terry turn their attention to one another. The two lock up and jockey for position. Alexis Terry tries to cinch in a headlock but Jones powers out. He counters with a wrist lock, twisting Terry’s arm to the breaking point. Terry flips forward to her back to relieve the pressure and the does a kip-up to get back to her feet. SARAH SHARP: "Impressive! Terry showing everyone that she’s still got it." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Forty is the new twenty, Sharpie. Alexis is just getting into her prime." Even Luke Jones is impressed with Alexis’ display. He offers her and hand and she slaps it cheerfully. The two lock up again and this time it’s Jones that goes for a headlock. He wrenches the neck a few times before Terry struggles free and pushes his away. Jones bounces off the ropes and right into Terry’s waiting clutches. Shining Wizard! The move connects with the sweet spot and Jones’ lights go out. Terry goes for a pin… 1 2 NO! Kelsi Ross slide back into the ring and breaks up the pin at two. Ross goes to work on Terry while Jones is out. Wide rights and lefts connect with Terry’s unprotected head. SARAH SHARP: "Ross outta nowhere!" OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Yes! this is you chance Kelsi, make yourself famous!" Alexis Terry eventually gets her fists up to defend herself but it might be too late, the damage has been done. Ross dismounts Terry and pulls the veteran to her feet by the hair. Three European Uppercuts followed by a Belly to Belly Suplex. Terry crashes to the mat taking the brunt of the impact on her neck. The crowd makes an “Oooohhhhhh” sound in unison and even the announcers cringe. SARAH SHARP: "That move was wicked. It looked like it could be a career ender to me." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "I know I wouldn't be feeling too good after a move like that" Ross sees an opening and goes for it. She dives in, hooking both of Terry’s legs. 1 2 … NO! Terry gets the shoulder up just before the referee’s hand comes down for three. Kelsi and Kolby are livid, they can’t believe Alexis is still kicking. Kelsi Ross gets in the ref's face demanding that she ends the match while Kolby shouts the same thing from ringside. They both should have been paying more attention because neither of them see Luke Jones get back to his feet. Ross turns back to Terry only to be hit with an Annihilation, Luke Jones patented take on a Clothesline from Hell. The move sends Ross flipping through the air, ass over teakettle. SARAH SHARP: "It’s over folks, it's all over Luke Jones is going to be the new Television Champion!" Jones moves in for the pin but gets blindsided by a #SuperKickParty! Like Jones flops over the top rope and out of the ring. Holding the back of her head, Alexis Terry slowly climbs up one of the turnbuckles closest to the comatose Ross. Most Glamourous Moonsault Ever connects! Terry hooks both legs… 1 2 3 DING! DING! DING! RUBY REYNOLDS: "Ladies and gentlemen, your winner and still GIW Television Champion, Alexis Terry!" Jones gets to his feet just a second too late to save the match. With his head down and with a defeated look on his face, Luke Jones makes his way to the back. Kelsi Ross rolls out of the ring and does the same with the help of her wife, Dr. Kolby Ross. Meanwhile back in the ring, the referee hands Alexis back her title. The champion climbs up one of the turnbuckles and holds the title above her head, victorious. SARAH SHARP: "What a match! Everyone gave it their all but It was Terry that wanted it more in the end." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "I don’t know if she’ll be as luck later on against Calum and his crew later on tonight but at least this is a consolation prize." SARAH SHARP: "Have some faith, Ollie. Alexis is one of the all time greats and I know she’ll be the one to land the final blow later on tonight." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "And if you want to see that happen stay tuned. We’ll be back with more GIW action right after this." The camera fades out to commercial on a shot of Alexis walking up the ramp with her title draped over one shoulder. Knee to the midsection followed by a crisp Power Slam. Callum continues to man handle McKenna for what seems like ages hitting a series of moves including a Fisherman Suplex, Double A Spinebuster, and a Falcon Arrow. McKenna looks like he’s done for. Callum moves in for a pin… No! McKenna gets the roll up! 1 2 3 NO! Callum manages to break free at the last possible second. SARAH SHARP: "McKenna almost had the champ there." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Pssh, Callum was just building suspense for the fans." SARAH SHARP: "By almost loosing?" OLIVER THIBODAUX: "This is Chris Callum we’re talking about here. He was never in any real danger of loosing." Both man make huge dives to their corners looking for a tag. Callum manages to touch hands with the fresh Duff Briggs who explodes into the ring. The Duffalo runs right past McKenna and knocks Alexis off the apron. McKenna has no choice but to tag in the already weathered, Katie Keller. McKenna rolls out of the ring while Keller enters. Briggs falls on Keller like a wild animal. Clothesline, Clothesline, Clothesline, three in a row leave Keller in even worse shape than she was before. Briggs follows up with a series of Suplexes that would leave even the most veteran wrestler begging for mercy. Hook of the leg… 1 2 NO! Alexis Terry slides into the ring and breaks up the pin. Her and Briggs move around the ring exchanging blows. Callum, Lost, and McKenna also swarm the ring. It’s a free for all! SARAH SHARP: "It’s pandamonium right now. There isn’t even a semblance of law and order in that ring." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "That’s why I love these matches, anything can happen." Callum takes out McKenna with a Criss Cross and both of them roll out of the ring. Lost runs at Katie Keller looking for a G-Spot but Keller ducks it, Royal Butterfly! Geneva Lost sails halfway across the ring and then lands on her neck. Keller is back on her feet and tries to break up Alexis Terry and Duff Briggs. She inserts herself between the two of them and tries to force them apart. It’s not use, Duff is too big and Alexis is too pissed. Duff pushes Keller away with one hand and she lands seated against the turnbuckle. Next he boxes Alexis’ ears sending her stumbling back. That’s followed by a rack of the eyes. Now Alexis is staggering around the ring blind and deaf. Duff motions he’s going for his finisher, Da-Duff Bomb. No! Keller attacks from behind pushing him aside! #SUPERKICKPARTY! Blinded, Alexis hits her bride to be with a jaw shattering kick. Keller is out cold and Alexis thinks she’s done Duff in. Duff capitalise on the confusion pushing Alexis out of the ring and going for the pin. 1 2 3 DING! DING! DING! RUBY REYNOLDS: "Your winners, Duff Briggs, Chris Callum, and Geneva Lost!" SARAH SHARP: "No! Not like this." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Looks like the fun is just getting started too." Callum is the first back into the ring but Lost is quickly behind him. Together the three begin to assault Katie Keller with kicks and punches. Luckily for Keller, McKenna is finally coming around and dives back into the ring. He tackles Callum and Lost. Alexis Terry rolls back into the ring and goes for Duff. All five competitors are in the ring trading blows. Keller eventually comes back around and joins the mix by mounting Callum and feeding him punches. SARAH SHARP: "Someone get in there and stop this madness. They’re going to kill each other." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "All this and it’s only Revolt! Just imagine what's going to happen at Unscripted." SARAH SHARP: "It’s going to be a hell of a show." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "It certainly is… Wait! Who cut the lights." The lights in the arena cut out completely. The ring, crowd, the whole building is pitch black. Suddenly the titan-tron flickers to life. There on the screen displayed in dark red letters are the words, “I’m Coming”. The screen only stays on for a short time before flickering back out. The lights come back on soon after. No one in the ring is quite sure what just happened but Duff Briggs seems especially shaken. He rolls out of the ring hollering about ghosts and goblins. Callum and Lost join him on the outside and walk backward up the ramp while staring at their rivals in the ring. SARAH SHARP: "What the hell was that Oliver, I’m scared?" OLIVER THIBODAUX: "I have no idea, Sharpie, but I can’t wait to find out." SARAH SHARP: "Whatever it was, it certainly seems to have shaken Duff Briggs." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Speaking of the Duffalo, he managed to pick up the win for his team tonight, That will give him momentum going into the number one contenders battle royal at Unscripted. I think he might win it all." SARAH SHARP: "And on that bombshell it’s time to sign off. Goodnight ladies and gentleman and thank you for watching." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "We’ll see you all next week, LIVE! for GIW Unscripted!" Fade out to black. WINNERS: CHRIS CALLUM, DUFF BRIGGS, AND GENEVA LOST
