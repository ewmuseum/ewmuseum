GIW — Card

Revolt, 27 June 2013

27 June 2013
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: THURSDAY NIGHT REVOLT LIVE! From Porretto Beach – June 27, 2013 BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! The fans were absolutely electric in the stands surrounding the ring on Porretto Beach, ready for the short edition of Thursday Night Revolt to begin. One week removed from Aggressive Expansion, the crowd was eager to see the fallout from the amazing show. The Fox Sports Southwest cameras began to pan around the ring as the fireworks shot into the air. Their cheers soon turn to boos as “Killin' It” by Krewella hits the PA system, and out from the back comes the brand new GIW Undisputed Champion – Alexis Terry. She makes her way to the ring, and stops right in the center with a microphone in her hand. ALEXIS TERRY: “Last night at Aggressive Expansion, you all witnessed history!” She looks down at her title. The fans boo. ALEXIS TERRY: “Alioth Starre thought that his reign would last forever, but it came to an abrupt end when I, the most dominate woman in GIW history, defeated him.” The fans knew what really happened. They began to shout down at the new champion that she would not have won her match had it not been for Ryan Cavallari. ALEXIS TERRY: “But Ryan Cavallari is not the man I have to thank for giving me the opportunity. I want to thank Alex Castellanos! The man who accepted my bribe and gave me the title match I deserved!” The voices of Riley and Oliver are heard to the TV viewers. RILEY STEVENS: “Alex did what he had to do to keep the company afloat.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Yeah, and now Alexis is champ! Best thing Alex has ever done.” RILEY STEVENS: “He tried to stop Cavallari! He tried to make it right!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “But look who has the gold.” Alexis began to speak again. ALEXIS TERRY: “This will be the most GLAMOROUS title reign ever.” “Killin' It' hit the PA again. Alexis began to make her way out of the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Alexis doesn't deserve that belt!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “She took advantage of her situation. You mean to tell me that you wouldn't do the same?” RILEY STEVENS: “No! I have some self respect!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Well maybe that is why they are trying to replace you with Rowland.” AMANDA ROWLAND: “What?” The picture cuts to the hotel room of Alex Castellanos and Sarah Sharp. Alex watches the TV with an awful look on his face. Sarah stands behind him, concerned. SARAH SHARP: “Don't worry about it. She is trying to get in your head.” ALEX CASTELLANOS: “Well it is working.” SARAH SHARP: “Alex, no one blames you for this.” ALEX CASTELLANOS: “I took her money. I gave her the match. I knew Alioth would beat her, then Ryan Cavallari came down and changed the game. I couldn't stop him.” SARAH SHARP: “He was determined Alex, don't worry about it.” ALEX CASTELLANOS: “If I wouldn't have let Ryan blow past me Alioth would still be champ.” SARAH SHARP: “You don't know that.” ALEX CASTELLANOS: “I do.” Sarah turns off the television. She grabs the tag team belts and hands one to Alex. SARAH SHARP: “Let's go make this announcement. Alright?” ALEX CASTELLANOS: “Alright.” We slowly fade to commercial. QUOTE There is one match that makes legends. There is one match that kills dreams. There is only one match a year that can change the game. Sole Survivor. Four wrestlers. Three matches. Each match, one, and only one, gets eliminated. The winner goes on to challenge for the GIW Undisputed Championship at Moment of Glory IV. Who will survive? As we return to the Revolt broadcast, Sarah Sharp stands in the ring with Alex Castellanos – in between them is a bingo style ball mixer. Around the ring is every single member of the GIW roster. The tag team belts are around the shoulders of Alex Castellanos. SARAH SHARP: “At Aggressive Expansion, I announced that on the July 4th Edition of Revolt – Red, White, and Bruised, there will be a wild card tag team tournament that begins. I did not tell a lie. In this mixer are the names of every single member of the GIW roster. If you wrestle, your name is here. I will randomly draw out two names, and those two wrestlers will be partners. Red, White, and Bruised will be the qualifying round. The tag teams that win will participate in the tournament.” The crowd cheers loudly as Sarah begins to spin the handle. SARAH SHARP: “Seeing as we only have one official tag team, Streetcar Rendezvous will be allowed to team together for the tournament. Their names are withheld. But that note aside... let us begin!” AMANDA ROWLAND: “I wonder if my name is in there?” RILEY STEVENS: “Only wrestlers.” Sarah has the first two names in her hand. SARAH SHARP: “The first team will be... the “New World Warrior” and... the “New North Beach Champion” Michael Lynch!” The crowd lets out an audible “oooooo”. RILEY STEVENS: “What an interesting pair. Lynch just took Tony's title away, and now they have to team together!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “This may work. Lynch probably wants two titles” The two men were on opposite sides of the ring. They stare through the ropes at each other. Lynch rubs his belt. Tony smirks. Sarah begins to spin the wheel again. SARAH SHARP: “The next team will be... former tag team hero Joshua Acquin... and.... “First Class” Veronica Taylor!” They look at each other as Sarah begins to spin the wheel. Veronica and Joshua seem pleased. They make their way into a the corner and begin to speak – presumably about strategy. RILEY STEVENS: “Joshua knows how to work a tag team, but Veronica seems pretty self absorbed. This may end badly.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Shh! She's announcing the next one!” SARAH SHARP: “The next team is... Jacob Jett and Brody Harper!” They high five each other outside the ring. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Oh lord.” RILEY STEVENS: “Hey, the Killers have been number one contenders for the tag titles before. Jacob Jett is in good hands.” SARAH SHARP: “Ms. Johnson and Nathan Starr will be teaming!” Airica Linn audibly yells “WHAT?” Ms. Johnson begins to laugh. AMANDA ROWLAND: “These two have been fighting on Twitter.” RILEY STEVENS: “And Nathan Starr has had some issues with infidelity in the past. I bet Airica won't like him spending so much time with another girl.” Ms. Johnson makes kissy faces to Nathan as Sarah announces the next duo. SARAH SHARP: “The next team is... Silas Romero... and Michael Harris!” They were standing near each other anyway and seem pleased by the random draw. Alexis Terry taps them both on the back. RILEY STEVENS: “These guys are already on the same page. They may make a formidable tag team.” SARAH SHARP: “And after them will be... the team of... wow... a throwback to olds chool GIW... Wake Walker and Jamie Vaccaro!” The crowd ERUPTS. Jamie and Wake seem to have mixed feelings. RILEY STEVENS: “These two guys are GIW legends. Now they team?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Its all about what you've done lately Stevens. These guys need to earn their spot again.” Sarah draws out two more names. SARAH SHARP: “Clay 'The Chainsaw' Krueger will team with... 'The Wild Party Girl', Danielle!” Clay is outraged as the fans begin to cheer for Daniel. Kruger grabs a microphone. CLAY KRUEGER: “No, no, no. See I'm TIRED of having to deal with all of these women. I see men everywhere in this locker room. Those are the ones I should be fighting. Not women! They are NOT on my level.” Sarah smiles. SARAH SHARP: “Deal with it.” Out of nowhere, Danielle runs around the ring and plants Krueger with the roaring elbow that she calls 'Fire Born'. Krueger's nose is bloody but he manages to stand and trade shots with Danielle. She goes for a low blow but he sidesteps it and pushes her into the announce table. RILEY STEVENS: “Well this isn't going well!” He charges her but she kicks him in the face, sending him backwards. By this time the roster is trying to split them up, but the damage has been done. Krueger is lunching for her as she laughs and the fans roar. SARAH SHARP: “Well, you two are just going to have to manage because there will be no switching.” She draws two more names. SARAH SHARP: “HardKore Comley and 'The Great and Powerful' Alexander!” RILEY STEVENS: “These two don't like each other either!” AMANDA ROWLAND: “But look! They are the biggest team in this!” RILEY STEVENS: “I guess we will see if size matters.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “It does.” AMANDA ROWLAND: “It does.” SARAH SHARP: “Next up is Airica Linn... and her partner... the former Undisputed Champion Alioth Starre!” AMANDA ROWLAND: “WOW!” Both are clearly not pleased. RILEY STEVENS: “Airica actually has a pinfall victory over Alioth.” AMANDA ROWLAND: “Alioth will be MOTIVATED to win some gold!” Sarah begins to twist the wheel. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Alexis and Ryan Cavallari still do not have partners.” AMANDA ROWLAND: “Or Daniel Starr!” SARAH SHARP: “The next team is... Justin Harmony and Miss Jade!” The two smile and nod their head. RILEY STEVENS: “Both of them are promising stars! They both were victorious on the AE pre-show!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “But its a long... long road ahead.” SARAH SHARP: “Next will be Clayden Arkane and Ruby Reynolds!” The crowd cheers loudly for them both as they shake hands and raise up each other's arms. RILEY STEVENS: “Now this could be a cute couple.” AMANDA ROWLAND: “ohmuhgawsh.” SARAH SHARP: “If you have a tag team partner, will you take a step back please?” The roster does so, leaving only Alexis Terry, Ryan Cavallari, and Daniel Starr. SARAH SHARP: “Will you three stand on the apron please?” AMANDA ROWLAND: “Wait, there are three of them... and four balls.” RILEY STEVENS: “Either someone is missing, or someone messed up. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I don't notice anyone gone...” Sarah rolls the wheel. The three stars with so much history look back and forth at each other. Sarah pulls the names out of the wheel. SARAH SHARP: “The next team is...” The crowd is on edge, so are the three wrestlers. SARAH SHARP: “Ryan Cavallari! And...” Cavallari looks at his options, hating both. SARAH SHARP: “Daniel Starr!” RILEY STEVENS: “The Deep South Champ!” AMANDA ROWLAND: “This could get interesting.” Alexis charges into the ring with a microphone. ALEXIS TERRY: “Wait, wait, wait! Everyone is out here. Why do I not have a partner.” SARAH SHARP: “Well there are two names left. One is yours. One is your partner's.” ALEXIS TERRY: “But the WHOLE ROSTER is out here and I'm the only one left!” Sarah takes the names out. SARAH SHARP: “Alexis Terry will team with...” Sarah opens the second ball. She turns white and looks at Alex. SARAH SHARP: “This must be a mistake.” Alex shakes his head. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “It is no mistake.” ALEXIS TERRY: “What!? Who IS IT?” Sarah hands Alexis the ball. She becomes enraged. ALEXIS TERRY: “ALEX CASTELLANOS?????” The crowd pops! Alex plants Alexis with a Golden Arrow! Right in the center of the ring. The arena is coming unglued. RILEY STEVENS: “ALEX IS GOING TO WRESTLE AGAIN?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “He just kicked an employee! Arrest that man!” Alex takes one of the tag belts and throws it on top of Alexis. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “As of now, I am no longer the general manager of Revolt. I am a member of the active roster.” Just then on the outside all hell began to break loose. Danielle and Krueger began swinging again. Alioth went straight for Cavallari. Walker intervened. Alex and Sarah slipped out of the ring and ran up the ramp together as the show faded to commercial. RILEY STEVENS: “We're going to try to get some order here!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “What a mess!” The show fades to commercial. The Deep South match is next. QUOTE As we return to the ring it’s time now for the only match on this short episode of Revolt, but one that’s still for the Deep South Championship none the less. Already standing in the ring as we return from break are a couple of new prospects in the man whom was victorious at ‘Aggressive Expansion’ Justin Harmony as well as newcomers Ms. Johnson and Ruby Reynolds, both looking to make huge entrances into GIW with a victory tonight, but as they stand in the ring already half way down the ramp as we come back with “Master of Ceremonies” by KPM Music playing through the speakers as Wake Walker high fives a few fans down the ramp as Aspen Chaud follows behind him wearing a sleek purple dress, her first night with her Client Wake Walker, the fans confused whether to cheer for Walker or boo him as he’s with Aspen. RILEY STEVENS: “This is Wake’s first match back here in GIW, and tonight he’s going for that Deep South belt and would really make a statement with a win.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “He’s with Aspen now; she’s going to keep him in line and heading to the top here. He’s already good, this just makes him unstoppable.” Walker enters the ring as Aspen claps him on as she walks to the side of the ring, standing outside it yelling something at a fan who taunts her as Wake’s music fades and "Frontline" by Pillar hits and these fans start to boo but soon they are greeted with the familiar smirking face of Daniel Starr, one that’s even smirkier then usual as he looks out at the fans then at the belt around his waist as he does a little spin with it on then starts to head to the back with a big grin on his face as in the ring the new stars are all eyeing him and that belt knowing that’s what they are after, taking that home with them and really making a statement on what for most of them will be there first big Revolt match, while for Walker’ it’s a chance to get back in the spot light. RILEY STEVENS: “Starr won this belt at ‘Aggressive Expansion’ in absolutely dominant fashion and while you may think wrestling four days later for him may be a disadvantage, he absolutely demolished Smith in that match. He didn’t take much damage.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “It’s still unfair.” Starr walks up the steps into the ring holding the belt high in the air as these fans boo him as he walks around the ring showing it off to each and every wrestler in the ring, all of them shaking there heads at his cockiness as he walks over and shoves the belt into the referee and then stands in the middle of the ring, the referee calls for the bell to get the match underway. DING DING DING! With the ring full of five stars it ends up being Walker, Harmony, Reynolds, and Johnson in each corner with Starr right in the middle. Realizing suddenly where he’s at Starr looks around at each foe, realizing they are all looking back at him with big eyes as they all head towards him at once, he lowers his head expecting the worse and they all collapse on him but to his surprise they all end up more so hitting each other then Starr and eventually after a few seconds the two brawls fall off a bit with Walker and Harmony going at it while Johnson and Reynolds fall to the mat and start to roll around trying to get the best of each other throwing big punches and elbows to each other’s head all while Daniel counts his lucky stars and walks backwards towards the corner, watching as the others do battle in the ring with a wide smirk on his face the entire time. Walker seems a bit rusty and it leads to the impressive Harmony backing him into the corner, laying into him with some knees to the stomach and a few big punches to the head before running at him and dropping him with a Running Dropkick, that slowly drops Walker in the corner falling to the mat laying in a sitting position. RILEY STEVENS: “Harmony off to a good start here.” On the other side of the ring the two girls are back to there feet, exchanging punches back and forth until Johnson grabs a hold of her and whips her off the ropes, on the rebound she hits him with a big Arm Drag, holding onto the arm tugging on it when Starr tries to pick his shot rushing towards her going for a Roll Up, 1…Johnson rolls through and jumps right back up and before Starr can get all the way up she nails him with a Running Big Boot. Starr gets up to his hands and knees as Ms. Johnson hits the ropes and comes at him with a Running Bulldog, Daniel falls to the mat and heads right towards the ropes, sitting in a seated position in the corner. Johnson lines it up and starts to back it up towards Starr for a ‘Bootylicious’ a Stinkface, though Daniel seems more then capable to move out of the way he takes the move anyways not seeming to worried about it. As she turns her face around she walks right into a Flying Knee from Reynolds out of no where, that sends Johnson falling backwards out of the ring as Ruby heads back to Starr, getting down on top of him laying in punches as on the other side Walker now whips Justin off the ropes and on the rebound, Harmony catches him with a big Hurricarana out of no where. Walker falls out of the ring under the ropes, as Aspen runs around the ring to check on him. RILEY STEVENS: “These new stars are really holding there own out here tonight.” Aspen leans over as Walker gets up quickly but she tells him to relax as Johnson slips back into the ring rushing at Harmony only to be hoisted up and put down with a Spinebuster, planting him. These fans clap him on as he goes for a cover 1…Ruby sees it coming and leaves Starr to rush over to them breaking up the hold at 2. Justin gets up and Reynolds ducks his big right hook and catches him from behind in a Sleepher hold, locking it in tight as these fans seem to be all for one of these new stars taking the belt away from Starr. Daniel sits in the corner recovering as Reynolds tugs on Harmony’s neck, recovered Walker slides back into the ring behind Ruby grabbing her and flipping her over with a German Suplex while Justin holds onto the ropes to keep from going over, luckily getting free of the Sleeper. Harmony tries to catch Wake off guard but with the ring rust now seemingly gone, he plants him with a Spinebuster of his own, going for a cover 1…2-Starr jumps on top of them both breaking the hold to keep his belt, much to the distain of the crowd. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “That a boy Starr!” Starr quickly grabs Walker and tosses him out of the ring going for a cover of his own on Harmony but Justin kicks out at just one. Starr goes to get up but a recovered Johnson rolls him up from behind out of no where, but Daniel with his power forces her up onto his shoulders for a Gutwrench Powerbomb nearly folding her in half as she rolls under the ropes in pain as Ruby now hits him with a Standing Dropkick from behind sending him through the ropes, getting tied up in them but to most surprise it’s Johnson who is up first now stumbling backwards right into a ‘Ruby Red Driver’ a Double Underhook Piledriver that connects but before she can go for the cover, it’s Harmony that spins her around and hits a Belly to Belly Suplex tossing her across the ring, Johnson still out of it but before he can pin her Walker slides back in and takes him down laying into him with punches before he kicks him off as he bounces back right into Starr, knocking him off the ring apron to the outside. Wake walks forward going for a ‘Collapse of Starre Mountain’ his version of the Rock Bottom but Justin elbows his way out of it and does his best to toss Walker over the ropes but Wake is able to hold on, before falling out. However Alioth Starre heads down to the ring, out of no where the fans a little baffled by his presence. RILEY STEVENS: “What is Starre doing out here?” Walker gets his balance on the outside now as he prepares to step back in the ring just as Ruby and Justin who were doing battle in the ring end up locked up in a battle that sends them tackling one another through the ropes falling to the outside hard. Johnson gets back up but is still showing the effects of the two big moves she took prior and is a sitting duck but before Walker can get back in Alioth rips him off the turnbuckle all while Starr slides back into the ring the referee focused on him now as he stalks Johnson as she turns over to him and he goes for the ‘Starrstruck’ a Brogue Kick! But she ducks it, seeming to do so just on instinct alone. But thinking on his feet he quickly slaps her on the butt, something she’s immediately offended by but as she turns to him to let him know it he’s already hit the ropes and this time he hit’s the ‘Starrstruck’ clean, as he goes for the pin. Alioth and Walker are having a bit of a brawl on the outside, while Ruby and Justin hear the count and try to get back in but are too late 1…2…3! It’s all over. REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner by way of pinfall and still GIW Deep South Champion…DANIEL STARR!” These fans groan over the ending as Starr slides out of the ring with that big smirk grabbing his belt quickly tossing it over his shoulder now as he walks around the ring, right past Ruby and Justin who realize that they got caught up in worrying about each other and it took them out of the match and watching Starr taunt them with the belt isn’t helping any as he heads to the back, they soon follow but the brawl between Starre and Walker now takes them into the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Starr walks out of here with the belt but it seems this old hatred between Starre and Walker is boiling over into the ring tonight, sometimes old feuds die hard!” These two go at it for a second when the fans start to boo as Ryan Cavallari heads out from the back rushing towards the ring as these two are fighting it out as Ryan slips in and tears Alioth away punching him a few times before the three are separated by a few referee’s though, more so by themselves as they all realize that fighting it out here tonight will not do anyone any good. RILEY STEVENS: “Alioth Starre, Ryan Cavallari, and Wake Walker in the ring at the same time sounds pretty amazing and from the looks of things, these three wouldn’t mind doing battle!” Starre, Cavallari, and Walker each in there own corner looking back and forth amongst each other; the referee’s looking relieved they don’t have to really stop anything now as the show slowly fades. These men of equal power end in a stalemate. WINNER: DANIEL STARR (Still GIW Deep South Champion)
