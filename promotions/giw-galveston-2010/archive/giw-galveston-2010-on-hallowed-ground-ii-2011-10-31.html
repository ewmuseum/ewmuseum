GIW — Pay-per-view

On Hallowed Ground II, 31 October 2011

31 October 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS ON HALLOWED GROUND II! LIVE from Hotel Galvez – October 31, 2011! BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! It is Halloween Night and things here in the Hotel Galvez are even more spooky then usual as the place is decked out with GIW logos covered in cob-webs and several other spooky Halloween decorations. As we pan around the crowd we see many of them are dressed up in there Halloween get up and have taken some time away from trick or treating to spend some time watching this GIW action as the camera pans around we end up stopping at the announce table with Oliver and Riley, one of which smiling we’ll let you guess which. RILEY STEVENS: “It’s Halloween night everybody and we have a show for you. One of the lowest points in GIW history has to be the first ‘On Hallowed Ground’ and we guarantee that there will not be a repeat of such an event this time because boy do we have a card for you here tonight.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I still have nightmares about the last show.” RILEY STEVENS: “I think that is true for most of us. Any how tonight we star of course with the Open Battle Royal as usual. However after that we head in three very important match ups as we’ll see ‘GIW Television Champion’ Austin Acquin go head to head with Analise Meyers the impressive debutant of last week. Then we head into another heated contest as AJ Fairchild goes one on one with Johnny Violence in what is sure to be a good one. Then the one a lot of people are looking forward to; Aspen Chaud and Tony Miranda meet yet again in singles action with title implications heavily involved.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Lets get on to the main event, where are star is.” RILEY STEVENS: “Yes of course Oliver, this time our Main Event…is HUGE! It will be Ace Baldwin the Number One Contender going head to head with Alioth Starre the longest reigning GIW Undisputed Champion in the history of GIW. This could be one of the biggest matches of all time and when the dust settles the best here in GIW will be determined!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “ACE BALDWIN!” RILEY STEVENS: “We’ll see…Lets get the show started.” With that the camera pans out and we get ready for the first match here tonight. QUOTE As we pan out suddenly “Dead And Gone” by T.I and Justin Timberlake hits and the fans go berserk as out from the back for the first time in a long time is Izzie Dead! RILEY STEVENS: “This is a pleasant surprise…” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Just as we finished saying this wasn’t going to be as bad as last year…” Izzie heads down to the ring looking focused and out for blood. Dead heads up the steps and into the ring, fitting in quite well here tonight. In the ring he waits as the fans continue to cheer as "Battle Flag" by Lo-Fidelity All Stars hits and the fans again with hold judgment on this new star not willing yet to invest a hundred percent in him. RILEY STEVENS: “This is another new star here in GIW his name is Draven. Said to be a high flyer according to our sources here.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Just the guys I LOVE…” Regardless Draven heads up the steps and into the ring looking across at the oddity that is Izzie Dead and shrugging his shoulders as he jumps up to the top rope. Finally “Hurricane” by 30 Seconds to Mars hits and the last entrant a man known as Nero heads on out wearing a mask. RILEY STEVENS: “Last but not least we have Nero, a masked wrestler who we really know very little about.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Who cares? He’s wearing a mask.” Nero gets to the ring stepping through the ropes as he looks around at the others in the ring, the referee steps between them as they all look back and forth realizing how weird a group they were as the referee called for the bell. DING DING DING! As the three men circle around each other; from a masked man to a zombie to Draven the fans seem crazily in favor of Dead as he’s the one they know the most. The fans get behind him as the three circle before Nero throws a punch at Draven and then rushes at Dead but he ducks under as Nero ends up in the corner. Izzie throws a big kick at him in the corner stunning him as he rushes off the ropes but Draven jumps in his way. Trying to throw him into the air but instead he just elevates him right into a big clothesline on Nero in the corner. The fans cheer as Dead turns right into a Modified DDT. Trying to get rid of Dead early he goes for the pin 1…Kick Out. Not close to putting the fan favorite away as Draven gets up. Now getting up in the corner is Nero who runs forward Clotheslining a caught off guard Draven to the ground. Quickly he turns his attention to Izzie lifting him up and throwing him off the ropes. On the rebound Dead ducks under and then hits the ropes on his own doing as he rushes at him jumping for a Crossbody only to be caught in the air. Before Nero can do anything with him, Draven is back up and he jumps up Dropkicking Dead which consequently sends Nero down with him with Dead jumping into a cover 1…Draven quickly pulls him off. RILEY STEVENS: “Fast paced action from these three stars.” Draven turns and tries to throw Izzie over but at the last second Dead reverses and throws him over instead. Izzie goes at him but Draven throws a last second elbow instead. This sends Izzie stumbling back as Draven jumps up going Springboard going for something but it never lands as out of no where Nero gets to his feet and in mid-air Spears Draven to the mat! The crowd goes wild for the move, impressed as Draven rolls out of the ring holding his chest. Behind him suddenly Izzie seems to be hulking up a bit as Nero turns and starts to bite him! The fans go wild as Nero pushes him away clearly freaked out. He takes a wild swing that Izzie ducks and then lifts him up into a Brainbuster! The fans go wild as he does the Thriller Dance then hits a Feint Legdrop Elbowdrop. Rather then going for a pin he looks to the top rope then heads on up. The crowd is on there feet as he sets it up getting ready for the ‘Dawn Of The Dead’ a Imploding 450 Splash! But just before he hits Nero rolls out of the way. Izzie hits the mat and gets right up and is caught with a Cobra Clutch Slam to the mat that puts him away, 1…2…3! Izzie Dead is Eliminated! RILEY STEVENS: “Nero avoided that huge move and then took Dead out.” The fans seem let down a bit as they quickly are silenced until they see Draven heading up on the apron setting up as he goes Springboard yet again this time with a Crossbody Press into a Pin 1…2…Nero kicks out. Both man scramble to their feet, with Nero getting up first and putting him right down with a Dropkick. The fans seem split now, mostly leaning towards Draven due to the fact that Nero took out their guy Izzie. Nero takes a second to catch his breath as he looks down at him heading towards the top rope, slowly though. But just as he gets there Draven hops up to his feet, and jumps up to the top rope Arm Dragging Nero off the top rope to the mat! The crowd goes nuts as Nero hits hard, but gets up surprisingly fast. That doesn’t last long as Draven puts him right back down with a Reverse Twist of Fate to the mat. With that he stumbles over to the tope this fast paced action taking a toll on both men. Knowing it has to end now Draven looks to end it going for his move the ‘Hollywood Suicide’ a Shooting Star Press into a Leg Drop, if it lands he wins and if it doesn’t he loses…Fortunately for him it does and the fans go wild for it as he rolls on top for the cover 1…2…3! JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…DRAVEN!” As the match finishes the fans seem appreciative for what was a great Opener for the show, from these two new guys. As Draven gets to his feet holding up his arms in victory with this one. RILEY STEVENS: “Got to give it to all three guys they put on a great show to start this show off. Draven may have gotten the win but Nero put himself on the map as well we’ll be looking forward to seeing how these two grow.” With Draven celebrating and Nero rolling out of the ring holding his neck in pain his emotions hidden under the mask; the fans give both claps for a great starting match. WINNER: DRAVEN With the first match in the books we head to the back with Joshua Acquin and Austin Acquin talking and discussing things in the hallway where the locker rooms are located. JOSHUA ACQUIN: “Trust me, I’ll make sure that you keep that belt a long time…Blake thinks he is in control but I am going to make sure things go smoothly.” As he says the last part Blake walks into the screen, standing around the corner as he pops in between the two putting his hands on both men’s shoulders. BLAKE BECKETT: “Hey there guys, hoping your not planning on doing anything suspicious tonight. Because I have been thinking, that belt it means a lot. Joshua you made that belt mean something and lately it’s fallen on hard times.” Josh nods his head as Austin adjusts it on his shoulder as Blake rubs his chin for a second. BLAKE BECKETT: “It would be a shame if it weren’t on the line tonight wouldn’t it? So I am here to tell you that tonight in your match…that belt will be on the line.” Blake smirks and walks between them as Austin and Joshua stare at each other, both angrily glaring at him as he walks off. QUOTE When we return to the ring already standing in the ring ready to go is Analise Meyers stretching out and getting ready to go as Jamal starts the intros. JAMAL JACKSON: “The following contest is for the GIW Television Championship! Introducing in the ring weighing in at one hundred forty one pounds, standing five foot seven inches tall…from Green Bay, Wisconsin…Analise Meyers.” The fans don’t give much of a reaction as her music cuts off and then "Nightmare" by Avenged Sevenfold hits and the fans unleash the boos as out from the back comes Austin Acquin and Joshua Acquin heading towards the ring together with Austin holding his belt over his shoulder. JAMAL JACKSON: “And the champion, accompanied to the ring by Joshua Acquin, weighing in at two hundred and fifty pounds standing in at six foot five inches tall, from Las Vegas, Nevada…the ‘GIW Television Champion’…Austin Acquin!” RILEY STEVENS: “This is our champion and I am glad with all the scheming Austin and Josh had been doing he’s defending his belt.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Oh right, this is unfair he wasn’t prepared to defend his belt tonight.” Austin heads up into the ring as Joshua walks around the ring to the other side as Austin poses with his belt and then hands it over to the referee who shows it around then hands it out and calls for the bell. DING DING DING! As soon as the match starts the two start to circle around with Austin sporting a confident smirk as suddenly a voice echoes through the crowd. JOSHUA ACQUIN: “Stop…Wait…I have information about a change to this match…” Analise looks over at him as the referee seems confused as he looks at him and then right as he does Austin flips Analise around and hits her with a big Flowing DDT. The fans boo as Joshua drops the mic to the ground, the whole thing a ploy as Austin waits as a caught off guard and stunned Analise gets to her feet as he smirks and with ease wraps her up into a ‘Crusher’ a Full Nelson Facebuster! The fans boo as the referee still a bit confused jumps down to make the count to the out Analise 1…2…3! Still champion. JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner and STILL GIW Television Champion…AUSTIN ACQUIN!” Yet again these fans unleash a boat load of boos at Austin and Joshua who used a well set up distraction to end this one in record time. Josh rolls in with the belt and hands it over to Austin, who takes it and holds it over his head. RILEY STEVENS: “How long can Austin and Joshua go on screwing people to keep this belt on Austin?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “As long as they want.” As of right now Oliver seems right as Austin and Joshua raise each others arms in the air celebrating all while the fans boo them. WINNER: AUSTIN ACQUIN (Still GIW Television Champion) In the back we see Aspen Chaud standing in front of a television fully in her ring gear watching the match that just happened with a staff member next to her. ASPEN CHAUD: “I can’t believe those two, somebody needs to give those guys a beating.” Aspen shakes her head when suddenly the staff member is pulled away and now standing in his place is Johnny Violence with a smirk on his face. JOHNNY VIOLENCE: “You know it’s funny just a week or so ago, I was getting ready to be in that match with yourself and Tony thinking that it would be me versus the two of you in a triple threat. Suddenly the referee says I tapped when cameras show I didn’t and then poof I am here fighting…AJ Fairchild of all people. Now I don’t like to be the one that makes assumptions but frankly I’d say you’re being given preferential treatment because you’re the golden girl around here.” Aspen looks over at him; sighing a bit as this relatively new guy had been on her tails for the past few weeks looking to pick a fight. ASPEN CHAUD: “I know you are new here and all but if you even took a second to take a look at our history me and the boss Blake Beckett we aren’t exactly the best of friends. So as for preferential treatment, I really doubt it.” Aspen grins and rolls her eyes looking at the TV hoping that he’ll just go away. JOHNNY VIOLENCE: “Say what you will, I am not buying it. We all know I should be in your match, and we all know that I should have next dibs on the title. So, since your already here watching…Park it for a little while longer and make sure to watch me, do what I do best.” Violence grins and heads on his way as his match is next as Aspen watches him go then turns to the camera trying her best to ignore him. QUOTE Again back from break we head into the ring where AJ Fairchild is bouncing off the ropes getting ready and prepped up for the next match up here tonight. JAMAL JACKSON: “The following contest is scheduled for one fall. Introducing in the ring weighing in at one hundred ninety eight pounds, standing five foot ten inches tall…from Minneapolis, Minnesota…AJ Fairchild.” The fans give him a fair ovation as it seems even they have grown tired of his show one week and then blow it the next type of attitude as “Bat Country” by Avenged Sevenfold hits and here comes Johnny Violence. This brings strong hate from the crowd as he walks down towards the ring with a smirk on his face JAMAL JACKSON: “And his opponent weighing in at one hundred and eighty nine pounds standing in at six foot three inches tall, from Calgary, Alberta, Canada…“The King of Anarchy” Johnny Violence!” RILEY STEVENS: “This kid is gaining no friends around here, with his ruthless attitude and his lack of desire to earn his spot.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “He’s looked impressive as hell since his arrival, it’s people like you that give him a bad name.” These fans hate Johnny and they’ve grown a strong hate in a very short amount of time. He walks down towards the ring scoffing at them as he jumps up the steps and then into the ring staring across the ring at AJ with a smirk as the referee steps in between the two then calls for the bell. DING DING DING! Though the fans don’t like AJ as much as they once did, he runs forward and they still cheer him only before his head gets nearly taken off his shoulders with a vicious Clothesline by Johnny. Violence grabs him by the head lifting him up and with AJ already dazed throws him off the ropes and with ease crushes him using AJ’s own momentum to hit the ‘Turn The Other Way’ a Superkick that floors him. Not even thinking about a pin attempt yet Violence listens to the silence that his dominance has brought to the hotel. Waiting for Fairchild to get up he stares around at the fans before wrapping him up around the waist and hits ‘The Harlot’ a German Suplex, that crushes AJ’s back. Yet again no even thoughts of a pin enter his mind as he watches Fairchild roll around in pain. Looking around again the fans now start to boo him just as he wants them to as he soaks it in. This time Fairchild gets up using the ropes, pushing off them lasting long enough on his feet for Violence to hit a Double Arm DDT. This thing is getting ugly and it’s getting ugly fast as Johnny gets on top of him, throwing punch after punch before finally pulling off. RILEY STEVENS: “This is hard to watch…” Violence gets up and when Fairchild rolls over we realize that he is now busted open, bleeding out of a cut on the top of his head. This only seems to make it more enjoyable for Johnny who clearly didn’t come out here just to win, but rather to send a message loud and clear to the roster. Getting down on his knee beside AJ who crawls, looking for a way out perhaps as Violence says loud enough for those at home to hear “You picked the wrong guy.” and with that he pulls AJ to his feet. Though he stays up on his feet, the blood starting to rush down his chest as Violence heads off the ropes and then BOOM, sending AJ flipping as Johnny hits the ‘Walk’ a Clothesline from Hell. But still even though Fairchild looks to be out he does not yet pin him as the fans have gone quiet and even the referee is just telling him to get it over with as Violence hoists him up, dead weight. The damage already done, Violence decides to twist the knife as he hits the ‘Glory Fades’ a Tigerbomb in the center of the ring, before making the pin on AJ 1…2…3! JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…JOHNNY VIOLENCE!” Immediately the referee calls for medical attention as Violence rolls out of the ring not wasting any time looking at the scraps and not seeming to at all feel bad. RILEY STEVENS: “If that didn’t make everyone forget about his controversial tapping to Aspen Chaud a few weeks ago, I’d be surprised. This was a beating.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Hopefully now people will realize what a threat he really is.” The camera gets a glimpse of AJ who is unconscious then heads back to Johnny who pulls the camera in tight and screams “I AM NEXT IN LINE!” his message coming loud and clear as he pushes the camera and heads to the back. WINNER: JOHNNY VIOLENCE As we head to the back we have Tony Miranda standing next to Zachary Pagonis who has the microphone in the designated interview area for the night. ZACHARY PAGONIS: “Tony in just a few minutes you will be fighting Aspen Chaud in a highly anticipated match that would surely have title implications. How are you feeling and what you’re your thoughts on facing her yet again?” Pagonis holds the microphone over to him as he begins to answer the question at hand. TONY MIRANDA: “You know I feel great and I am ready to go, it’s going to be a great match and…” Before he can finish Ace Baldwin strolls into the camera with a big smirk on his face interrupting him. ACE BALDWIN: “I couldn’t help but notice you were talking about title implications…Now I don’t want to talk about this has been getting a shot. Oh wait, your not a has been you’re a never were.” Ace has a chuckle at his little joke as Tony glares over at him controlling himself knowing he fights next. ACE BALDWIN: “You know what good luck. I want to beat Aspen to but you’ll do just as well. Just so long as I am beating one of GIW’s so called veterans its fine with me.” Ace says this and then heads on his way, getting ready for his own match as Tony watches him go then heads on his way ending the interview as his match is next. QUOTE And now things start to get very interesting. After two one sided fights things are now heading to the Co-Main Event of the evening. "World Warrior" by RedRapper hits and the crowd blows up as it means this thing is ready to start. JAMAL JACKSON: “It’s time for the Co-Main Event of the Evening…The following contest is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first weighing in at two hundred and twenty pounds, standing six foot two inches tall…from Chicago, Illinois… “The New World Warrior” Tony Miranda!” RILEY STEVENS: “You know the one thing Tony hasn’t accomplished here in GIW is winning the Undisputed title a win here could put him next in line can he do it?” These fans go wild for Tony as he walks out from the back with his usual focus as he heads towards the ring, heading by the fans and up the steps through the ropes. He walks to the ropes and raises a arm and the fans give him another cheer as his music trails off and "Bullseye" by Aly and AJ hits and the cheering only continues. JAMAL JACKSON: “And his opponent weighing in at one hundred and twenty pounds standing in at five foot one inches tall, from Hollywood, California…Aspen Chaud!” RILEY STEVENS: “Also looking for another shot is Aspen Chaud, looking to get a shot at the winner of this main event tonight.” It’s Aspen turn to come on out as she walks out wearing a light blue attire as she heads towards the ring high fiving the fans as she looks down at the ring at the all to familiar site of Tony Miranda in the ring. She heads up the steps and then steps through the bottom two ropes and into the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “This is going to be a big one.” Aspen blows her signature kiss to the crowd then turns and stares at Tony nodding her head as the referee steps in between the two as the people here are standing as they wait for this one to start as the referee calls for the bell. DING DING DING! Here we go this one is underway and the fans are excited for it as the two circle around one another to start the match, all too familiar with one another. They go in for a lock up and Aspen immediately rolls around him holding onto his arm tugging it behind him. Tony spins around quickly and pushes her away. Right away he goes for the Spinning Roundhouse Kick but Aspen ducks it. She answers back with a kick of her own the ‘Queen's Wrath II’ a Heel Kick but he also ducks under it. When he ducks it he uses his strength advantage to shoulder her into the corner, he proceeds to strike at her in the corner as the referee counts 1…2…3…Tony throws one more but Aspen ducks and pushes him into the corner as she now reverses it and goes for a Corner Foot Choke as the referee counts 1…2…3…4…Aspen pulls away as the magnitude of this match is shown by the willingness of both to bend the rules early on. Chaud wastes little time going back after him with a Clothesline only for Tony with one Arm to lift her up and throw her harshly down to the mat. The fans are now starting to choose favorites as the split starts to show in the cheers here after each move. Tony pulls her away from the ropes to try to pin her 1…Kick Out. Not even close yet as Aspen gets her shoulder up. RILEY STEVENS: “Anyone expecting this to be a quick one, will be disappointed.” Tony hoists her to her feet and puts her right back with a Snap Suplex. The fans split still give their opinions whether it’s silence or cheering. Aspen holds her back as Tony lifts her back up looking to go for another but as he hoists her up she knees him in the face causing him to drop her. Swiftly landing on her feet she runs off the ropes and then jumps up into a Lou Thesz Press followed by Punches. The crowd in favor of her swings into cheers as she gets some offense going. Both hop to their feet and Aspen immediately whips him into the corner. From there she runs at him going for her ‘Tush Push’ a Turnbuckle Flying Butt Shot but again Tony shows their past history as he holds the ropes and jumps up kneeing her in the butt. Aspen lands on her feet and stumbles forward holding her butt as he runs forward and lands a Hurricanrana that sends Aspen stumbling up and into the corner. Rushing at her with full force he goes for a Clothesline but she ducks it and as he bounces off she hits a Sitout Jawbreaker and goes for the cover 1…Kick Out. This thing will continue as Aspen gets up, not seeming deterred knowing this is what she planned for. RILEY STEVENS: “Thus far both stars are giving it there all but they know each other so well neither can string much together.” Aspen gets up looking at the crowd for inspiration as she waits for Tony to get up and when she does she hits him with a Forearm then another before rushing off the ropes and landing one that sends him into the corner. Yet again she goes for the ‘Tush Push’ only this time it lands. Miranda stumbles his way out of the corner as she crawls to the top screaming out to the fans as she goes for the ‘AirBarbie’ a Flying Crossbody that gets the crowd up on their feet as she immediately covers 1…Kick Out. The crowd seems stunned as does Aspen, not by the fact he kicked out but how quickly he did. Both get up as Aspen lines up yet again going for the ‘Queen's Wrath I’ a Double Knee Backbreaker! But as she does he grabs the ropes and it causes her to fall to the ground. Without wasting a second he takes a running start and hits the Standing Shooting Star Press 1…Kick Out. Yet again the fans give them props as Aspen gets to her feet and Tony lines up going for a Spinning Roundhouse Kick but when she ducks it the kick heads right into the referee. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “That stupid ref got to close.” Aspen didn’t immediately notice it so she gave him a Dropkick that sent him to the ground. When she realized it she stopped for a second when suddenly a loud growing murmur from the crowd, shocked everyone as Johnny Violence ran out from the back wielding a steel chair. Aspen unaware of the situation tries to help the referee but when she finally gets up, WHACK she takes a Steel Chair to the face that sends her to the ground. The crowd boos as Aspen rolls around in pain but Johnny isn’t done as he turns to Tony who had just gotten up and crushes him in the back. When he falls to his knees in pain he crushes him again in the back, before rolling out of the ring and heading up the ramp. RILEY STEVENS: “Johnny just came to sabotage this match.” Aspen and Tony are down both holding the points of their injuries as the referee gets up looking at both out, as he throws up a one and starts to count…2…3…4…From the top of the ramp we see Johnny with a smirk enjoying his handy work…5…6…7…In the ring both Aspen and Tony start to stir…8…Right as the referee counts to nine they get up to their feet, right away going back to battle as Tony throws a big punch that nearly takes her off her feet. Aspen responds back with much less power as Tony stands still and delivers another bomb. Finally losing this battle Aspen out of no where hits the ‘Queen's Wrath II’ a Heel Kick that sends the fans in favor of her into cheers as she makes the cover 1…2…Kick Out! The fans for Tony go wild as Aspen gets up trying to grab his heel but he kicks her away and on her butt. Both hop up but it’s Tony who is up first as he lands a ‘Third Strike’ a Running Leg Lariat the move that’s taken tons of people out before her 1…2…Kick Out! Yet again the fans go wild. RILEY STEVENS: “Who can put the other away?” From the top of the ramp we can see an angry Johnny who seems to have been trying to make it a Double Countout. Aspen gets up struggling just to do so, dazed and confused as Tony hoists her up for the move that will end it the ‘Second Impact’ a Running Lygerbomb turning and making the cover 1…2…Kick Out! The crowd goes crazy as Aspen kicks out again, hoping to turn this thing around. But when she tries to get to her feet it’s a lost cause as Tony just puts her up and almost following the name gives her another ‘Second Impact’ this time her body goes limp as he crawls on top 1…2…3! JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…TONY MIRANDA!” The crowd in favor of him goes wild as Tony stumbles to his feet, the match having took a toll out of him. Aspen rolls over onto her chest holding her neck as Johnny stands at the top of the ramp glaring down at them. RILEY STEVENS: “You know after a great match, Johnny Violence looked to try and get involved and he did so but when they got up before the ten count it was Tony Miranda that finished Aspen off with not one but two Second Impact’s and he’s the one who gets the big win.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “The big winner is Johnny Violence who came out of this, being the most talked about man.” RILEY STEVENS: “One has to think these three will be seeing more of each other as we go forward.” The referee raises Tony’s hand in the air as he stands tall the victor in this one on one contest as he turns and looks up at Johnny Violence who stares back at him as the two share a long stare down before the show cuts to a promo. WINNER: TONY MIRANDA For every question there is an answer… For every fight there is a victor… For every problem…there is a Solution… It’s time for the Final Solution! The video proceeds to show several GIW stars in action, video after video of exciting action as finally the logo flashes up with ‘Final Solution’, December 4, 2011…Benno’s on the Beach. As the video ends we take one last look in the champion and challengers locker rooms where both are getting ready for the big main event as we head to the ring for the match. QUOTE Suddenly the lights go out and a video starts to play showing Alioth Starre… Tony Miranda… Trent Lost… Aspen Chaud… Mark Cross… One by one the list shows his opponents tapping out or being pinned however he finished them as it flashes through him celebrating at the end of the show every single time. Riley Stevens over the video can be heard “Nobody can beat this man…NOBODY!” It trails off at the end with Alioth celebrating when boom it cuts to Ace Baldwin crushing opponent after opponent here in GIW. As he does this his voice plays over the video “Alioth I’m the one that’s ends your reign.” as it continues to cut through the video and then finally it ends on the final Revolt with Ace pinning Alioth…Then posing with his belt as the video cuts in half and these finals words are said by the voice over guy “Two men enter…One leaves the Undisputed best in GIW” As the video closes with a split screen of both men holding the belt. Now the air in the building has changed, sure everything before this was entertaining. But now the lights dim and everyone in the building has goosebumps as it’s time for the main event we have been waiting for. Ace’s music by Matador hits and suddenly the place turns into warzone as boos reign everywhere as Ace Baldwin walks out onto the stage. JAMAL JACKSON: “This is your MAIN EVENT of the evening. The following contest is for the GIW Undisputed Championship! Introducing the challenger weighing in at two hundred forty five pounds, standing six foot four inches tall…from Hollywood, California …“Amazing” Ace Baldwin!” RILEY STEVENS: “You know every time we say Alioth has met his match he wins…” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “But that ends here tonight.” Ace heads towards the ring, taking his time as usual looking out at the crowd as the place is dark and he heads up the steps and into the ring, walking around it before heading up to the top rope…posing for the fans who are oh so accepting of him. With that over with "Blood & Fire (Out of the Ashes Mix)" by Type O Negative hits and here comes Alioth Starre, he being the one with the shiny gold belt. JAMAL JACKSON: “And the champion, weighing in at one hundred and sixty five pounds standing in at five foot ten inches tall, from Brixton, London, England…the longest reigning ‘GIW Undisputed Champion’…Alioth Starre!” RILEY STEVENS: “You know for the past month these two have been one upping each other, each and every week it’s hard to really know who has the advantage.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Not really it’s Ace.” Alioth saunters his way to the ring as well soaking in the vibe, this is a place he has been so many times before, as the man making his entrance last. He heads down the ramp heading up the steps and through the ropes as the fans in this place go absolutely wild for him. Unless Ace invited someone, it’s fairly clear that everyone is on the side of the champion tonight as the referee steps in between them. RILEY STEVENS: “This is going to be huge.” Alioth poses with the belt, high in the air as the fans go wild as he hands it over to the referee who grabs it and hoists in the air as the fans go insane as he shows it off to all sides then hands it out of the ring to start this thing off, as he calls for the bell. DING DING DING! This thing is underway and the fans are on their feet, this is like the Super Bowl here in GIW and Alioth is the one with home field advantage. As they look at one another; their eyes locking as they go in for a lock up as is customary. Right away Ace shows off his superior strength pushing Alioth away and then screaming out “You are nothing!” plenty loud for the fans to hear who respond by booing him. Starre rushes at him this time catching his arm and twisting it around his back, tugging on it tightly. The fans now start to clap Alioth on as Ace, pulls his way to the rope telling the referee to break it off. Alioth pulls away as the referee tugs on him; this gives Ace the chance to nail him with a sucker punch.
