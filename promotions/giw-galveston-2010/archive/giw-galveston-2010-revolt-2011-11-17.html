GIW — Card

Revolt, 17 November 2011

17 November 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: THURSDAY NIGHT REVOLT LIVE! From Moody Gardens in Galveston – November 17, 2011 BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! We continue on back here in Galveston Island this time back at the familiar Moody Gardens the home of both ‘Moment of Glory I and II’. Though tonight might not be that big of a night the card is set up to be an exciting one. The fans that have crowded in here are all jazzed up as the camera pans around them finally ending up on Riley and Oliver both with smiles on their faces. RILEY STEVENS: “Welcome back to Revolt, this is a show that never struggles to find excitement. Last week we announced three challengers for Ace Baldwin’s title at ‘Final Solution’ they will be Tony Miranda, Aspen Chaud, and Johnny Violence. Huge news from last week and tonight they’ll all be in action headlined by a huge Lumberjack match between Aspen Chaud and Ace Baldwin what a night right?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Has there even been a champion as mistreated as Ace Baldwin? I mean Alioth practically had a free run as champion yet Ace gets thrown into a Four Way for his first defense? This guy is our poster boy and Blake is ruining it!” Oliver clearly angry is fuming but as he’s ranting Matador’s music hits and all the sudden Ace Baldwin heads out from the back still done up in a suit as he heads toward the ring with his belt over his shoulder glaring around at the people standing behind the barricade heading up into the ring not looking happy as he enters the ring and gets a microphone from a worker. ACE BALDWIN: “You know what kind of a Boss mistreats their BIGGEST star the way Blake does. I am the poster boy, this face…my ability…this charisma it all adds up to the perfect guy. But Blake Beckett is too busy thinking this show is all about him to realize that I AM THE STAR! I am the money maker. But while Alioth faces opponent after opponent, garbage ones at that. I am given a match in a FOUR WAY? This has got to end so I am demanding Blake come out here right now and change it! Or I will walk!” Ace lowers the microphone as Blake’s music hits and he walks out onto the stage chuckling himself at Ace’s words as Baldwin stares up at him waiting for the answer he desires. BLAKE BECKETT: “They told me that you were coming out here and I knew it was too complain, it always is. You go ahead and pretend that you would walk if I didn’t change it. When you know full well that the contract you signed when you won that belt states that if you are to leave you have to pay me a whole lot of money. So, I am not worried about that. As for changing the match…That’s not going to happen. After all, I’ve already been advertising it all over GIW. But you shouldn’t really take it as a bad thing. You’ve been calling yourself the greatest champion from what I hear, so you have the chance to prove it against three of the best GIW has to offer.” There is no doubt whose side the fans are on as they applaud Blake’s response as Ace starts to pace in the ring. ACE BALDWIN: “Real funny Blake. You know as well as I do that this is not fair! I want it changed and I want it changed now. I am not leaving this ring until I get my way. So do it right now!” Ace lowers the mic again, anger clearly in his voice as he’s practically fuming in the ring as Beckett rubs his chin and nods his head. BLAKE BECKETT: “You know what, I am going to change it for you. Let me and the rest of the staff put our heads together and by next week you’ll have an announcement on what exactly it is.” Blake turns and heads on his way as Ace looks around smirking a little bit realizing he might have just gotten his way and then Blake stops before going all the way to the back. BLAKE BECKETT: “Actually on second thought, it stays the same.” Beckett laughs as the fans pop in approval as Baldwin angrily throws his mic down in anger. Blake heads to the back as Ace reaches down and picks the mic up trying to talk into it but it’s broken as he throws it outside of the ring and then rolls out, going after Blake as we take a break. QUOTE Standing in the ring when we come back for the opening contest we have Justin Impact. But the fans look up at the top of the ramp as "Exploding Helmet" done by Videohelper Production Library hits and the fans look up confused waiting to see who it is as Jeremy Baltimore walks out with a smirk on his face as he heads towards the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “New star here in Jeremy Baltimore, I have only heard good things about him. We will see how he does here tonight.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “We’ll see…” Baltimore taunts some fans at ringside as he heads up the ramp and into the ring stepping through the ropes getting ready to go as the referee calls for the bell. DING DING DING! And here this thing starts with Justin rushing forwards at Jeremy only to be met with a big Clothesline that knocks him down. Impact trying to get the early advantage hops back up and this time heads right into a Scoop Powerslam. Rather then going for an early pin Baltimore uses this chance to taunt Justin by taking his time to flex in the center of the ring. This only makes the fans boo him as he waits for Justin to get up only to rather calmly grab him and hit an Overhead Belly to Belly Suplex. As impressive as the move is Jeremy’s choice to get up and hold both arms in the air only makes the fans hate his guts. Turning around and watching as Impact gets to his feet, using the ropes already seeming to be done for as he stumbles away from them right into a Spinning Elbow Smash, that stuns Impact but doesn’t take him off his feet. Showing little compassion he hoists the dazed Impact into the air and hits a Thrust Spinebuster tossing him to the mat. Finally deciding to make the pin 1…2…Baltimore stops and holds Justin’s shoulder himself. RILEY STEVENS: “You know against guys like Impact this may work we’ll see how he does against stiffer competition.” Baltimore points to the top rope as if the fans are going to at all cheer him after some of his actions as he heads on up there. Though quite a bit of time has elapsed Justin is clearly done. Jeremy perches himself up top then smirking as he jumps off going for a ‘Powerdrive Elbow’ a Twisting Elbow Drop that lands flush and puts Impact away officially as Baltimore lazily hooks the leg 1…2…3! That is it. JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…Jeremy Baltimore!” Baltimore hops to his feet helping up Justin to the surprise of the fans, hoisting him up. RILEY STEVENS: “Got to be impressed with this kid tonight, clearly a showman but can he stick around with the big boys? We shall see.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I am telling you, this kid has IT.” The fans seem to be coming around at first until Baltimore spins around and tosses Impact through the ropes which brings back the boos as he holds up one arm in the air showing off as we take a break with the fans booing. WINNER: JEREMY BALTIMORE Returning to the back we see Joshua Acquin walking through the hallway when he runs into Amanda Rowland who turns and smiles at him. AMANDA ROWLAND: “I’ve been meaning to thank you for like being there for me last week when your brother insulted me.” Josh smiles and nods his head as Amanda waits for his answer. JOSHUA ACQUIN: “Listen it’s not a problem. The kid’s gotten out of control lately, not even showing me any respect any more.” But as he’s half way through that sentence Austin walks up behind him, angrily staring at the back of his head. AUSTIN ACQUIN: “Oh I am out of control now? Because I am not being a follower for you any more. I am not being your little tool. I have been hearing that you have some big announcement to make, what is it?” Joshua turns around as his brother confronts him Amanda standing behind him looking worried. JOSHUA ACQUIN: “You’ll hear about it sooner rather then later.” The older Acquin has a smirk on his face as he clearly has something big planned as Austin rolls his eyes and walks past him. AUSTIN ACQUIN: “Whatever.” With that Joshua watches him go, sticking with Amanda as we take a commercial break. ==== From there we head to another area backstage where Tony Miranda is getting prepped for his match stretching out his educated legs as in walks Johnny Violence with a grin on his face. JOHNNY VIOLENCE: “Just the man I was looking for. You know I am not happy about being forced to be on a team with you in fact it’s the last thing I want to do. But here’s the deal let’s face the facts and admit I am our better half. So you sit there and if I need you I’ll tag you in…Otherwise just take the night off.” Violence pats him on the back as Tony stops his stretch looking across at his partner for the night. TONY MIRANDA: “You think I want to team with you? Knowing that I have to shoulder an even bigger load then usual. You want to start? Go right ahead. But when you falter I’ll be there waiting to get in.” Already tension has risen and with these two it was bound to happen as Violence grins and gets up in his face. JOHNNY VIOLENCE: “Bring a paper and pen so you can take notes. After all you get to be one of the few to watch the future Undisputed Champion in action so close…See you out there.” Johnny smirks and walks by him as Tony goes back to his stretches shaking off the confrontation as we take a break. QUOTE "Nightmare" by Avenged Sevenfold hits as soon as we return and the fans begin to boo heavily as Austin Acquin the GIW Television Champion walks down looking confident as usual as he climbs through the ropes and hoists his title up, the belt he’s held for awhile now. RILEY STEVENS: “You know Austin has had issues facing tough competition but when it comes to keeping that belt he has done well. But with the falling out between he and his brother as we’ve seen the past few weeks can he keep it up?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Of course he was always the better brother, but Joshua was just trying to hold him back.” His music trails off as the fans now wait for the entrance of Nero. There is a long awkward wait as the fans look around confused as to if this could possibly be happening again. The referee waits but Austin tugs on him screaming at him to start it, the referee waits but then is forced to start it. DING DING DING! Austin Acquin doesn’t even hand his belt out instead he just waits as the referee again starts the count 1…2…3…Austin walks around hoisting up his belt apparently having no issues winning this way…4…5…6…7…The fans are starting to boo even louder, knowing that this means Austin gets the win as yet again the referee counts 8…9…10! Count out. JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…Austin Acquin?” As soon as the ten count is counted Austin jumps up onto the top rope holding up his belt high in the air as the fans boo him. RILEY STEVENS: “This Nero not showing up business has gotten out of hand, what’s up with that.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Who cares, Acquin gets the win.” WINNER: AUSTIN ACQUIN Before we do anything as Austin is celebrating suddenly we hear loud coughs through the mic as the fans look to the ramp where Joshua Acquin is heading towards the ring with a big sack in his hands. Austin looks over at him letting out a big sigh, mouthing “What are you doing out here!”. Joshua heads up into the ring the fans starting to turn around about him. JOSHUA ACQUIN: “As big of a win as that was for you, huge! I have an announcement to make that concerns you and several other stars here in GIW. You see just last week I came upon something backstage, that hadn’t been seen for a long time. Something that was seemingly lost a long time ago, but I found it.” Joshua pulls open the bag reaching into it and pulling out a belt, when shown to the cameras it is revealed as the ‘GIW Legacy Championship’ that hasn’t been seen since September 2011! JOSHUA ACQUIN: “This belt was one that was supposed to be a prestigious belt before the champion lost it and left the company. When I found it, I knew that we had to restore it and bring it back to being a fixture here in GIW. So I brought it up to Blake…and he agreed. So here is what is going to happen at ‘Final Solution’ we will crown a champion. But this belt just can’t be anyone’s…They have to earn it.” Joshua looks over at Austin insinuating his belt was not earned which offends Austin who screams at him something we can’t make out but Josh just continues. JOSHUA ACQUIN: “A one night GRAND PRIX! Three matches between six guys. The winners all advance to a Three Way for the belt. The winner will be the new face of the Legacy division one that will rival that of the Undisputed Championship if I have my way. Austin you are involved, and will have the chance to get the belt.” Joshua puts the belt back in the back as Austin smirks and nods his head holding up two fingers while mouthing “Dual Champion!” as Joshua shrugs his brother off sliding out of the ring as the fans let this news sink in. ==== Backstage we go where Aspen Chaud has just arrived surprisingly late to the show with her driver Rejinaldo carrying her bags as Ace Baldwin immediately approaches her as she enters the building. ACE BALDWIN: “I was beginning to think you weren’t going to bother showing up. After the last time we fought, wouldn’t be such a bad idea for you.” Ace smiles and chuckles remembering that night in his head as Aspen sarcastically laughs with him. ASPEN CHAUD: “Nope. I just figured if I didn’t show up early I wouldn’t have to listen to you complain at the beginning of the show…Which is how it started tonight right?” Aspen leans forward waiting to hear it Baldwin looks around shaking his head. ACE BALDWIN: “…No.” This brings a laugh from her as she goes to walk past him heading on her way to the locker room. ACE BALDWIN: “We know it’s going to end with you laying down with me on top!” Baldwin nods his head as Aspen walks off and we head to the ring yet again for the big tag match. QUOTE It’s now time for the interesting tag team match up as already standing in the ring waiting is the debuting Altair standing ready to go. "Battle Flag" by Lo-Fidelity All Stars hits and the fans go wild with cheers as Draven has quickly become a fan favorite here in GIW with his high flying abilities really leading the way. RILEY STEVENS: “You know Draven was extremely close to qualifying for the Undisputed title shot last week and he’s relatively new, the sky is the limit for this kid.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “You mean the hospital is the limit for this kid, that high flying crap annoys the hell out of me.” As Draven jumps over the ropes into the ring “Bat Country” by Avenged Sevenfold hits and the cheers in the air completely cut off into boos as Johnny Violence walks out as usual not at all looking worried as he heads down to the ring, looking confident for someone teaming with an enemy. RILEY STEVENS: “Last week Johnny Violence ended the show as the last man standing in the ring, many people wonder if that will be the case come ‘Final Solution’.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Ace Baldwin is still the champion if you forgot.” Violence heads over to his corner as Altair and Draven both try to get to know one another as "World Warrior" by RedRapper hits and though Draven is loved it does not yet compare to the veteran Tony Miranda as the fans go berserk for him. RILEY STEVENS: “This man has been in situations like this before will he be able to handle having an ego like Violence on his team?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Uhhh you mean a talented team mate that will carry him to a victory?” Tony heads into the ring where Violence already points him to the corner. Not trying to stir anything up yet he does so, willingly letting Violence start it off with Draven who seems ready to go. DING DING DING! And here we go this tag match is underway and things are about to get heated as Draven and Violence circle around one another looking like a rematch from last week’s main event. Yet again they go in for a lock up, both men trying to show dominance but neither going anywhere it’s a stalemate until just like last week Violence tries to go for a knee but when he does Draven trips his planted foot sending him to the mat hard. The fans cheer Draven as he bounces off the ropes timing Johnny’s stand up perfectly as he nails him with a big flying Elbow. The fans are on his side at this point as he uses the temporarily stunned Violence and puts him down with an Impaler DDT. Draven hops up knowing it’s not enough to put him away and heads to the side of the ring where he steps outside prepping to go Springboard. Just as Johnny gets up he goes for it but Violence sees him and rushes to his corner where he tags in Tony and quickly exits the ring allowing Tony in as Draven steps back into the ring knowing he’s got to start again. RILEY STEVENS: “How quickly Violence bailed out and allowed Tony in.” Tony now goes in for a lock up with Draven and the fans are a whole more conflicted but at the end of the day Tony still wins out with this one just as he does with the lock up throwing Draven to the back. Draven gets up quickly nodding his head showing the mutual respect they have as he heads in for another lock up but Tony goes for a surprise Spinning Roundhouse Kick. Draven’s speed helps him duck under it, then go for the exact same move but knowing all about those moves Tony ducks it as well but he grabs him right away when he lands back on his feet and puts him down with a Snap Suplex! The fans cheer this on as Tony goes for a cover 1…Kick Out. Not even close here for Tony as he gets up nodding his head, clearly respecting the new guy as he hoists him up and throws him off the ropes but at the last second Draven reverses and throws him off and on the rebound hits him with a Japanese Arm Drag that impresses the crowd. RILEY STEVENS: “Can’t help but be impressed with Draven here.” Draven gets up looking to be getting jazzed up here as Miranda gets up quickly walking right into a boot from Draven that he uses to set up a Spinning Cradled Suplex with a Pin 1…Kick Out. Nobody is surprised he kicked out as Draven gets up, not having tagged out yet having success on his own. Realizing that he might be able to end it he goes for his move the ‘Hail Mary’ as he lifts Tony over his shoulders, the fans going crazy thinking he may pull an upset. But Miranda always crafty rolls right off and just like that hits him with a Spinning Roundhouse Kick after all. The move doesn’t send him to the ground instead Draven stumbles back right into a tag from Altair who heads right in and immediately gets hit with a Rolling Wheel Kick. RILEY STEVENS: “Let’s see what Altair’s got.” The answer doesn’t seem to be a good one to that question as Altair gets up from the kick right into repeated Muay Thai Knees over and over again finally ending it by throwing Altair off the ropes right into an Inverted Samoan Drop! Right away he goes for a pin 1…2…Kick Out. Just barely kicks out as Tony gets up foolishly taking a few steps back into his corner setting up for the ‘Third Strike’ as Altair gets to his feet. Johnny reaches over the ropes and makes the tag. Just as he does this Draven enters the ring trying to stall them so that his tag partner can get up. Rushing at Violence who just entered Johnny side steps and throws Draven right into Tony who has yet to leave sending both over the ropes with one another. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Two birds one stone.” Violence now has gotten rid of Draven and Tony as Altair gets up completely out of it as Johnny like a vulture looks to pick up the scraps waiting as he gets up then holding him and putting him right into a ‘Glory Fades’ a vicious Tigerbomb that puts Altair down and out as Violence now takes the pin 1…2…3! JAMAL JACKSON: “Here are your winners… Tony Miranda and Johnny Violence!” The fans boo, as Violence gets up asking the referee to raise his hand as Draven and Tony both get up on the outside where Violence stands tall having just picked up the scraps from Miranda’s offense. RILEY STEVENS: “Can’t at all take anything away from Tony and Draven who did very well but this Johnny Violence yet again ends up being the one that comes out looking really good.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “As usual.” Violence taunts Tony with a title taunt, supposed to be tag partners but clearly this was always an arms race from the beginning as we head away to the backstage area. WINNERS: JOHNNY VIOLENCE & TONY MIRANDA Backstage we go where Nero is seen looking around for something continuing the confusion as to why he’s been missing his matches but the camera pans slightly to the side where Jeremy Baltimore the man who only debuted earlier tonight is reading the board where announcements are made. JEREMY BALTIMORE: “I’m in the Grand Prix at ‘Final Solution’ against a bunch of losers. Altair, some other bums and a loser named Draven.” Just as he says that Draven is walking back from his match that just finished, staring at him. DRAVEN: “A loser huh? You know I just had to try to carry a bad team mate to a victory. So if you think I am a loser, let’s give the fans a preview of the Grand Prix next week. You versus me!" Draven stares across at him as Jeremy turns towards him with a big smirk on his face as he looks him over. JEREMY BALTIMORE: “What better way to impress management then an easy win, you are on.” Baltimore scoffs at him and walks as Draven looks over at the board now as well as a video starts up. ==== That video starts showing random effects in the background when the following text appears on the screen. Two Time GIW North Beach Champion With that some fans might already have realized just who the video might be hyping as there has only been one man that has ever held it twice. One of GIW’s Biggest Stars Returns… The text trails off as the video shows a face suddenly look up into the camera the light now showing it is Trent Lost as the video begins to show several highlights finally the video ending with the following text. Next Week! QUOTE It is now time for the Main Event and before we get to the intro’s the Revolt theme hits and here comes all of the stars of GIW Revolt. Amongst them are Altair, Austin Acquin, Brody Harper, Draven, Jeremy Baltimore, Johnny Violence, Justin Impact Before he got his walking papers, and Tony Miranda all heading towards the ring followed then by Joshua Acquin and Izzie Dead the Road Agent. RILEY STEVENS: “Here we see the Lumberjacks for tonight’s match a role that will almost surely cause some hectic situations.” Already we see some groups formed as Austin Acquin, Jeremy Baltimore, and Johnny Violence stand on one side of the ring. Draven, Tony Miranda, and Joshua Acquin on another side. Izzie Dead and Brody Harper finding their way together. And finally Altair and Justin Impact on the final side. As they take their places “Bullseye" by Aly and AJ hits and the fans go wild as here comes Aspen Chaud out from the back as these fans have chosen their preferred star as she heads up the steps looking around the ring at the people around. RILEY STEVENS: “You know this match really might end up being about who has more friends, cause these Lumberjack’s are given free roam.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Unless Aspen seduced everybody, I doubt she has any friends.” Aspen enters the ring still looking around as Matador now starts to play and here comes the champion Ace Baldwin as everyone on the roster stares at him, all looking at the prize on his shoulder which he proudly shows off as he heads up the steps and into the ring stepping through the ropes then hoisting up his belt in the air, no better time to show it off then now in front of every star on the roster. RILEY STEVENS: “That’s some way to endear yourself to the people on the outside.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “He’s the champion, he is the top dog.” Ace now hands his belt out to the referee who hands it out as the fans seem giddy about this one as the lumberjacks seem ready as the referee calls for the bell. DING DING DING! And just like that this thing has started as Ace grins looking across at the very serious Aspen Chaud who calls for a lock up. Baldwin naturally does so but right away throws her to the ground before she has a chance to try to put up a fight. Ace has a chuckle at her expense as she hops back up to her feet. Yet again she goes in for something but wanting to test out the lumberjacks right away Baldwin uses his strength to grab her tights and launch her through the ropes and to the outside. Ace screams at them to get on her but Tony, Draven, and Joshua instead listen to the rules pulling her up and throwing her back into the ring. Ace leans over the ropes screaming at them to hurt her which they all ignore as Ace turns around going to reach down to grab her but being rolled up out of no where 1…2…Kick Out! The fans all jump up thinking she had it as Baldwin gets up before even she can, the anger fueling him as he catches her with a big boot while still on her butt that puts her back down. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “End her!” Baldwin grins as he pulls Aspen up to his feet, not having any issues lifting her up and around as he goes right into a Suplex. He doesn’t release instead just lifts her right back up and then hits another powerful Suplex. This time when he rolls over and then to his feet he hooks her leg and hits a Fisherman Suplex with a pin 1…Kick Out. Though he has everything going for him Aspen still won’t go down easy as he gets up choosing now to taunt his opponents pointing over at Tony and motioning the title around his shoulder. Miranda is able to keep his composure as Chaud gets to her feet as Ace continues to look dominant as usual in this one. With that he gets her set up and goes right into a Double Underhook Backbreaker. Rather then going for another pin he drags her over to the corner where he climbs to the second rope. Before doing anything he holds his arms up in the air taunting then going for a Middle Rope Leg Drop but at the last second Aspen rolls towards the ropes sliding under them. RILEY STEVENS: “She moves.” The fans go wild as Ace lands on his butt hard, holding the back of his thighs as he gets up to his feet and here comes Aspen! With the fans starting to get into it for the first time in the match Aspen gets up throwing a big Flying Forearm, followed immediately by another then turning and whipping him into the ropes wasting no time going right into the ‘Tush Push’ a Turnbuckle Flying Butt Shot, the fans go wild as Ace stumbles out and she goes immediately to the top rope. She goes flying as high as her short legs can take her as she goes for the ‘AirBarbie’ a Flying Crossbody! But she is caught in mid-air by Baldwin out of no where. The fans stomachs drop as Ace hoists her over his shoulders and goes for the Powerbomb but out of no where she hits a Hurricanrana that sends him flying out of the ring into the guys on the outside. At first there is an awkward stoppage before its Violence that drops a boot on him, as Draven steps in to stop him as the two start to push each other around. RILEY STEVENS: “Uh oh, here we go!” Just as he says this all hell breaks loose as the two start to brawl on the outside and the sides are drawn as it’s all out warfare on the outside. While all this is happening the referee watching them trying to get control, Ace slides his way back into the ring as Aspen has her back to him watching the stuff on the outside. He waits for her to turn around but perhaps making a noise or something Aspen turns around and just like that goes for a ‘Queen's Wrath II’ a Heel Kick! It lands flush on Ace but while stunned he just goes in for a tackle and it sends him as well as Aspen into the referee both crushed in the corner. Aspen gets up but the referee’s head snapped hard against the corner and he seems out of it as Aspen bounces off and goes for a Clothesline just as Ace does and both take each other out. RILEY STEVENS: “The referee is out, as are Ace and Aspen.” The fans are looking in the ring in shock as the brawl on the outside continues some guys trying to stop others throwing punches when suddenly Johnny Violence slithers into the ring looking at both Ace and Aspen on the mat. He looks at both of them as the fans wait to see who he will go for first and just like that he hoists up Ace Baldwin the champion! With that he hits the ‘Paranoid’ a Ranhei, to the champion. To everyone’s surprise he rolls out of the ring leaving Aspen untouched. The referee gets up just as he slides out just as Aspen does the same, looking around confused as she looks down at Ace thinking he’s been out this whole time. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “This is a travesty!” Aspen looks around at the crowd as the brawl seems to be sort of contained some of the wrestlers heading to the back abandoning this, as Ace gets up holding his back from the previous move from Violence and using this Aspen grabs him and then nails him with a ‘Queen's Wrath I’ a Double Knee Backbreaker! The fans go wild as Ace falls down to the ground, out of it as Aspen crawls on top 1…2…3! JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…Aspen Chaud!” The fans go crazy as Aspen hops up to her feet unaware that the win was a little sour as she goes to hold her arms up when BOOM! A title to the back of the head as Johnny Violence returns to the ring this time crushing Aspen with the belt. RILEY STEVENS: “Johnny Violence is making his presence felt here tonight.” Aspen falls over beside Ace as the fans boo until the last man around the ring slides into the ring…That man is Tony Miranda as suddenly a huge ovation can be heard as Tony stands behind him staring at his back in the corner. Suddenly he starts to rush at him causing Violence to turn around and then boom, ‘Third Strike’ a Running Leg Lariat! The fans go nuts as Tony sends Violence to the mat alongside the other three. RILEY STEVENS: “Tony Miranda is the last man standing here tonight!” The fans are going wild for him as he falls to his knees right in front of the belt, looking at his own reflection in it as the show slowly comes to a close with that being the final picture. WINNER: ASPEN CHAUD
