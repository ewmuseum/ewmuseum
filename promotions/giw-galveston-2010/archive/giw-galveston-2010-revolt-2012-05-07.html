Revolt, 7 May 2012

7 May 2012

GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: THURSDAY NIGHT REVOLT LIVE! From Barnhill Arena in Fayetteville, AR – May 7, 2012 BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! The show is ready to go as “Over and Under” by Egypt Central plays through the arenas as some opening pyro goes off as this new set for Monday Night Revolt is shown off for the fans who are excitingly cheering for the first ever Monday Night Show as the new voice on commentary starts us off as the camera pans around the crowd. JACOB O’CONNELLY: “Welcome everyone to Monday Night Revolt the first ever episode here on Monday Night’s, I am Jacob O’Connelly your new voice of Revolt.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “What?” The camera pans all around them until we come into the ring currently set up with eight stars already in the ring with a red carpet underneath them as the music continues to play as David Sinclair comes out from the back to some more boo’s as he walks down towards the ring with a smile as the eight tournament participants are set up across from there opening round opponents. Sinclair enters the ring with a smile on his face as he walks between the competitors. DAVID SINCLAIR: “Welcome everyone to Monday Night Revolt! We have a very good show for you tonight where we will kick off an eight man tournament to declare the Number One Contender for the GIW Undisputed Championship! Standing in the ring are the eight combatants, ready to fight here tonight.” Starting from the top we have Alex Castellanos across from him is Eliza Gray both seeming to be focused for the night. As David walks further it is then Daniel Starr and Faith Simpson, Faith seeming happy as usual while Starr has a wide smirk on his face. Then we move on to Alioth Starre and Phoenix Matsuda whose eyes are glued to one another the entire time. And finally we see Ryan Cavallari set up in the corner then Matt Kluge leaned up against the corner with his head in his hand, looking rather bored as Sinclair stares at him. DAVID SINCLAIR: “In this tournament are former…Number One Contenders...fast rising prospects, former champions, and all the ingredients to find a worthy contender.” Again Sinclair stares at Kluge only this time Matt reaches out grabbing the microphone away from him as Sinclair backs up. MATT KLUGE: “Really? I find it funny your champion isn’t even here tonight because of the beating she took at my hands before that weasel there at ringside decided to get in my business. You know Sinclair I don’t know who will win this tournament but I am sure that even though they will work ten times harder then Aspen the next month in the end it will be in vain because she’s got you wrapped around her finger. Frankly your kind of her bitch…” Kluge shrugs his shoulders as Sinclair walks across the ring asking for another microphone that he receives. DAVID SINCLAIR: “I don’t have any control over what Jacob does at ringside and frankly you had it coming after you put him through a table a few weeks ago. But just count yourself lucky I gave you a second chance at the title.” Sinclair walks away as Kluge rolls his eyes putting the microphone down as he rolls out of the ring heading towards Jacob only for some Security who was already prepared it seems step in front of him as Kluge points around them at Jacob mouthing something at him as he is backed around the ring by the Security as in the ring Starr walks across the ring grabbing the mic from where Kluge left it. DANIEL STARR: “Listen up, listen up. I am Daniel Starr the poster boy for ‘Aggressive Expansion’ so it only seems to make sense that Sinclair believes I will run through this tournament and challenge for the belt and as I look around this ring I can’t help but think he is right. I mean I am facing a fifteen year old tonight. Then I have to beat Starre or Matsuda? I am your next Undisputed Champion and if you don’t believe me ask Paul Defias in the unemployment line.” Starr smirks again dropping the mic having got his point across as Sinclair doesn’t seem too happy that his planned out opening isn’t going to plan as Faith seems ready to show Starr he is wrong. DAVID SINCLAIR: “Let’s get this thing started then, Starr and Simpson you are up first.” Ryan Cavallari, Alex Castellanos, Eliza Gray, exit the ring pretty quickly as Matsuda and Starre share a bit longer stare down before exiting the ring as Starr and Faith are prepped and ready as Sinclair walks to the outside heading to the back to watch the matches from the comfort of his office. QUOTE After that break we return to the ring where Faith Simpson and Daniel Starr are already in there corners getting prepped and ready to start the match up. RILEY STEVENS: “This is the first of four matches here tonight in the tourney two stars that could really be dark horses in this tourney.” JACOB O’CONNELLY: “Starr is no dark horse.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “So long as Faith doesn’t win who cares.” Starr walks to the middle of the ring offering a hand shake to Faith who walks over untrusting as she prepares to extend her hand only for Starr to spin around and toss her into the ring post with force as the referee runs over pushing him away scolding him as Faith falls to the ground holding her shoulder. RILEY STEVENS: “Just sick…” Simpson holds her shoulder in pain as the referee tries to ask her if she can go, hoping she’ll say know but her Mark Cross like pride won’t allow it as she tells him to start it anyway as he waits a moment the calls for the bell. DING DING DING! As she turns around still favoring her shoulder she walks right into a huge ‘Starrstruck’ a Brogue Kick that sends her flipping around before falling to the ground as the fans unleash there boo’s in fury as Daniel gets up not seeming to have any real care in the world for what he had just done as he points to the top rope, heading right on up taking his sweet time knowing that kick has just put Faith on dream street, as he looks out at the crowd then screams out “Next Undisputed Champion!” then jumps off with a Shooting Starr Press that lands beautifully on Simpson who lies there down and out as the referee jumps down making the count 1…2…3! REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner…Daniel Starr!” The fans boo him for the way he went about this one as he hops up to his feet with a smirk, the first man to advance to the next round. The referee reaches down to help Faith but Daniel pulls him away telling him to raise his arm which he does high reluctantly. RILEY STEVENS: “Three weeks in a row this guy has looked dominant, you got to think he may just have the most momentum in this tournament.” JACOB O’CONNELLY: “He’s my pick.” Starr raises his arms high in the air as the referee looks back at Faith as he takes one final glance at Faith then rolls out of the ring heading to the back having came and conquered. WINNER: DANIEL STARR Backstage we see Alexis Terry looking through some sort of wedding planning book as Alioth Starre walks up behind her heading to the ring for his match as he stops to talk to Alexis. ALIOTH STARRE: “What are you looking at?” Starre making conversation with Alexis in general doesn’t seem right as she look over to see who it is, looking at him like he is crazy. ALEXIS TERRY: “What do you want?” Alexis glares back at him as Alioth holds out his arm to calm her down not wanting to offend it seems. ALIOTH STARRE: “Oh no. no. I don’t mean to insult you at all, just simply wanted to say that I am happy for you…Reminds me of that movie, what’s it called? Where the woman comes from a shady background to find true love what was it again?” Starre rubs his chin for a second as he tries to think about it as Terry stares at him not seeming to enjoy it as Starre walks past her but then stops. ALIOTH STARRE: “Oh yeah that’s it…Pretty Woman.” Starre heads to the ring as it takes Terry a second to think about it then she realizes what he’s just said as she glares at him as we head to the ring. QUOTE It’s time for the second tournament match of the night as we head back to the ring as "Masquerade" by Versailles hits as the fans begin to boo the arrival of the fast rising Phoenix Matsuda as he heads out from the back with a smirk on his face as he walks towards the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Phoenix Matsuda has yet to win a match here in GIW but he has really impressed a lot of people.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “The kid’s got talent.” Matsuda enters the ring these fans seeming to be pretty amped for this one as his music fades out and “Grand Central” by Edward Shearmur hits to a now huge ovation as the fans cheer the longest reigning Undisputed Champion ever as Alioth Starre walks out from the back towards the ring seeming confident in himself. RILEY STEVENS: “These two seemed like the most intense pairing at the opening of the show.” JACOB O’CONNELLY: “That’s for sure, couldn’t keep there eyes off one another.” Starre walks past the fans up the steps looking across at Matsuda as he enters the ring seeming ready to battle his way back to a title shot as he stands across from Matsuda as the referee sets it up and starts the match. DING DING DING! Right off the bat these two meet in the center of the ring slowly circling one another as Phoenix tosses the first punch which is immediately sent back by Alioth leading into a very quick exchange of punches back and forth between these two combatants getting the fans onto there feet as they exchange blows like Rocky as finally Phoenix the bigger man’s punches begin to wear at Alioth backing him up slowly until Matsuda has him up against the ropes where he grabs him and tosses him off the other side, waiting as Starre bounces off the ropes right into a Running Knee to the face as immediately Phoenix starts to taunt his fallen opponent as he looks out at the crowd and asks “This was your champion.” As he bounces off the ropes then goes for an elbow drop only for Starre to move out of the way due to his slight hesitance to taunt. RILEY STEVENS: “Taunting costs him there.” These fans cheer as Starre gets up before Phoenix and he Chop Block’s him back to the mat, these fans making it clear whose side they are on from the start as Matsuda falls to the mat holding the back of his leg. As Alioth takes the chance to drop down to the mat grabbing him by his leg rolling over into a Knee Bar as the fans cheer him on hoping he can draw a quick tap from Phoenix. Matsuda cringes in pain but quickly uses his free leg to kick Alioth until he is forced to break the hold. They both get up but Phoenix is a bit gimpy on his leg and it allows Starre to be up first where he delivers a Heart Punch that stops him in his tracks, teetering him a bit as Alioth hits the ropes and clotheslines him down to the mat making the first attempt of the night 1…Kick Out, still not even close as Alioth gets back up to his feet calling for Phoenix to do the same. RILEY STEVENS: “Alioth may have him here.” When Matsuda gets to his feet Alioth rears back and throws a Super Kick but Phoenix catches it, then takes him to the mat getting on top throwing punch after punch on top of Alioth until Starre is able to throw him off, both men hopping to there feet only for Matsuda to have his back where he grabs him around the waist dropping him back with a German Suplex! The fans cringe in fear as Matsuda lets him go then jumps right into a Dragon Sleeper which gets the fans even more worried as Phoenix gets it all the way in tugging back on Alioth’s neck. However Starre is savvy and doesn’t get to worried in the move instead reaches out to the best of his ability to get to the ropes finally making it there, with one hand as Phoenix makes sure to hold it in 1…2…3…4…Breaking it right before the referee gets to five dropping him down to the mat as he backs up, getting a bit angry as he awaits Starre getting up. JACOB O’CONNELLY: “Phoenix has him right where he wants him.” Starre starts to get up as Phoenix screams at him to get up! These fans wait in anticipation hoping for an Alioth come back as he uses the ropes to get up as Matsuda rushes him only for SMACK! His face to be crushed with a boot to the face as Alioth lands with a Superkick. Matsuda falls backwards into the mat holding his chin in pain as Starre looks to finish this one off as he has done to so many opponents as he reaches down into Phoenix’s legs turning it trying to get his patented Figure Four in but before he can Phoenix kicks him away hopping to his feet but yet again BOOM, a second Super Kick that sends Matsuda back to the mat this time Starre heads to the top rope as the fans climb to there feet in anticipation as Alioth jumps off for a Shooting Star Press but at the last second Matsuda rolls out of the way sending Alioth right into the mat. RILEY STEVENS: “High risk move costs Alioth there.” Starre hits the mat hard and some how bounces right back to his feet stumbling around as Matsuda connects out of no where with a Crane Kick to end it as he hops on top of him for the pin attempt 1…2…Kick Out! The fans go wild as Matsuda gets up, looking at the referee like he is crazy as Alioth rolls out of the way using the ropes to try to get up as Phoenix argues with the referee about it but as soon as Starre gets up he rolls him up from behind this time he grabs the ropes as leverage with the referee out of position to see it 1…2…3! This time getting the win. REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner…Phoenix Matsuda!” These fans are furious as Starre kicks out but just a bit too late as Phoenix releases the hold of the ropes and rolls immediately out of the ring with a smirk on his face as these fans give it to him. RILEY STEVENS: “The Crane Kick couldn’t put him down so he resorted to using the ropes to get the win.” JACOB O’CONNELLY: “That’s a win for Phoenix a huge one over Alioth Starre, give him his credit Riley.” Matsuda looks out at the crowd who are jeering him as Alioth rubs his hands through his hair looking at the referee in disbelief knowing that complaining won’t help as Phoenix walks backwards up the ramp with a huge win over Alioth Starre here tonight to advance in the tournament. WINNER: PHOENIX MATSUDA After a short commercial break we return to the arena where after a few moments "Kiss My Lips" by Dev featuring Fabolous echoes through the arena a bit earlier then expected only drawing more boos from the crowd realizing they’ll have to see Alexis twice as out from the back comes Alexis Terry with Ryan Cavallari and Eliza Gray flanking her as she has a microphone. ALEXIS TERRY: “Later tonight I will be taking back my Legacy Championship but we have great news for all of you GIW fans here in attendance. As some of you may already know Ryan and I are getting married and I feel as though there is no better place for the two of us to get married then right in this ring.” Terry smiles brightly as Ryan who doesn’t seem thrilled to be out here, preferring to prep up for his match as Eliza smiles ready for her own match as well. ALEXIS TERRY: “So if you can afford to come to the wedding in a few weeks time, you are all invited to celebrate our marriage to one another. Carry on with the show and good luck Eliza.” Alexis smiles leaning in and hugging Eliza who starts to head down to the ring for her own match now. RILEY STEVENS: “Congratulations to Ryan Cavallari and Alexis Terry though personally I have no idea how he stands her.” JACOB O’CONNELLY: “A woman of class is truly not your type Riley.” Gray heads into the ring for her match as Alexis and Ryan head to the back to prepare for their own matches as we head to the ring. QUOTE Eliza Gray is now walking into the ring as we come back after announcing Alexis and Ryan’s wedding as she sets up in the corner looking as focused as ever as her music trails off and “Wayward One” by Alterbridge hits which gets a loud applause from the crowd for the man who just recently made his return last week, Alex Castellanos who walks down towards the ring looking focused. RILEY STEVENS: “You know tonight could really be called past and present as Eliza Gray the new girl meets former contender Alex Castellanos in another tournament match.” JACOB O’CONNELLY: “Gray will win, just wonder how she will go about the Aspen situation.” Castellanos heads into the ring looking motivated as per usual as he stands across from Eliza both ready to get one step closer to the title as the referee calls for the bell. DING DING DING! This match gets underway as these two combatants circle around one another Alex with his strong Aspen hatred and Eliza a friend mostly by association with Alexis making this one very interesting. Foolishly she tries to go for a lock up only for Alex to push her back into the ropes using them to send her flying off on the rebound. Trying to end this one quickly Alex takes a gamble going for a ‘Golden Arrow’ a Superkick that Eliza ducks under and uses it to roll him up right off the bat 1…2-Kick Out. The fans give a collective sigh of relief as Alex hops up to his feet, probably just regretting the move a little but as Gray is up first dropping him back down to the mat with a Russian Leg Sweep that again gets the fans a little worried as Gray drops down for another pin attempt 1…Kick Out as these fans continue to pull for Castellanos to get things going as Gray gets to her feet seeming to be getting more and more confident as she waits for Alex to get up. RILEY STEVENS: “Eliza starts this one off on fire.” Once he is up she grabs him immediately around the neck trying to set up a DDT only for him to spin out of it and catch her with a kick to the gut that sets her right up for a Snap Suplex that Castellanos executes perfectly. Hopping up to his feet now finally starting to get some offense going, Eliza steps back up to her feet holding her back as Castellanos decides to target it grabbing her and this time hitting a Northern Lights Suplex with the pin 1…2-Kick Out. These fans were hoping for it big time as Alex gets up not seeming to be all that worried yet as Eliza again gets up holding her gut as this time he grabs her from behind going for a Delayed Back Suplex but before he can get her off her feet, she elbows backwards to stop him then nails him with a Roundhouse Kick that drops him to the mat. RILEY STEVENS: “Boom huge kick.” These fans seem a bit worried now as Alex’s momentum was stunted big time with that as Alex gets back up to his feet as Eliza grabs his Wrist going right into the ‘Deep in Debt’ a Wrist Lock that tears on Alex’s wrist as these fans scream at him to get out, finally using his strength advantage he breaks the hold pulling her onto his back then dropping her down with a Samoan Drop to a huge cheer from the crowd as Castellanos shakes out his wrist for a moment, as he reaches down pulling up Eliza tossing her into the corner where he rushes her and smacks her with a Clothesline then before she can slump over he hoists her up onto the top rope as the fans get to there feet knowing something crazy may just happen as he follows her up there. JACOB O’CONNELLY: “This is dangerous.” Hooking her up by the shoulder then climbing to the top rope he prepares for a Superplex but in survival mode Gray is able to rake his eyes, causing Alex to naturally react by holding his eyes as she pushes him off the top rope sending him falling to the mat in pain as Gray spins around on top rope looking to do it for her Alexis as she goes for a Moonsault just like her friend only for Alex to roll out of the way using his own survival instincts as Eliza now hits the mat hard as well as both are down and out Gray holding her chest and stomach while Castellanos rolls over onto his stomach holding his back as the referee starts the count on both of them 1…2…3…Both start to stir a bit, crawling to the ropes to prop themselves up…4…5…6…Both get a hold of the ropes climbing to there feet…7…8…They are both up at the same time as the referee calls off the count and they head back to the middle of the ring where Gray walks right into Alex’s Double A Spinebuster to a huge cheer from the crowd as he hooks her leg 1…2…Kick Out! RILEY STEVENS: “That was nearly it right there.” These fans want him to put her away as he looks down at her looking to lock her into the ‘Illuminatus’ a Texas Cloverleaf that seems nearly in but Gray manages to grab the ropes right before he can even get it in, as he is forced to break the hold as he gets back to his feet this time as he walks towards Eliza he walks right into a dropkick to the knee and as he falls over she goes for the ‘The Princess' Crown’ an Arm Trap Crossface getting it in but he to reaches out for the ropes both making the mistake of going for it to close to the ropes. They both get up and Eliza goes for the Inside Cradle out of no where 1…2…Alex rolls through it and they end up on there feet and boom a ‘Golden Arrow’ Superkick out of no where as the crowd goes wild and Eliza falls to the mat down and out as Alex pins her 1…2…3! Alex advances. REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner…Alex Castellanos!” Seemingly out of no where this one has ended with a huge applause from the crowd who were all behind Alex in this one as Eliza gets up, seeming to have just been caught by that one as Castellanos rolls out of the ring high fiving a few fans. RILEY STEVENS: “Alex Castellanos picks up the win here tonight, to advance to the next round.” JACOB O’CONNELLY: “That’s what we call a lucky kick, she got caught.” Castellanos heads up the ramp as Gray follows him holding her chin in anger as she heads to the back as well with another semi-finalist moves on. WINNER: ALEX CASTELLANOS Again commercials fill the screen until they fade out and Eliza Gray is seen just getting to the back with a very mad demeanor as she storms through the backstage area only to be stopped by the GIW Television Champion, James Dean. JAMES DEAN: "Whoa there Eliza, good match out there was a really close one." Gray taking it as sarcasm does not give him a moment snapping right back at him. ELIZA GRAY: "Shut up! He got lucky; I have been screwed over three times in a row." Dean holds out his hands trying not to offend as he holds his title over his shoulder. JAMES DEAN: "Wasn’t trying to put you down just trying to say you’ve been doing a good job." Dean trying to be a nice guy despite his competitive nature gets a very angry response. ELIZA GRAY: "I don’t need your approval, I really only care about Alexis and Aspen’s opinion. Because I may just come and take that belt from you so that all three of us are champions, in fact I really like the sound of that." Gray walks off as Dean rubs his hand through his hair wondering what he did wrong there as we head out to the ring. ==== Back to the ring we go where it seems we are ready for the next match as “The Rat” by Dead Confederate hits and walking out in a hurry is Matt Kluge and instead of walking to the ring yet again he seems to be heading around it as Jacob speaks up. JACOB O’CONNELLY: “Is he coming around here?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Sure looks like it, haha.” Oliver seems to be enjoying this as Jacob who is in the middle of the announce table ducks under it to hide as Kluge tells Oliver to move which he does with a smile as Kluge reaches underneath the table pulling out Jacob just as Security and Tony Miranda a Road Agent head out from the back to restrain him as Matt holds him by the collar yelling at him “You made a big mistake! as Security and Tony pull Kluge away as Jacob pushes his way back to the announce table as Security and Tony get him into the ring as Jacob gets his mic back on. JACOB O’CONNELLY: “That man deserves to be fired! That man deserves to be released! That guy is an embarrassment to this company.” Kluge sets up in the ring as he looks over at Jacob as a few members of Security continue to stand beside him as his match is prepared. QUOTE With Kluge funneled into the ring “Brand New Day” by Ryan Star hits and the fans begin to cheer for Ryan Cavallari who heads out from the back with a smile on his face as we have just found out earlier he is about to be a married man as he heads towards the ring with the plans for his wedding ring to be just one of the new gold pieces he has in just a few months. RILEY STEVENS: “This is another good clash of new star and old veteran this time as Kluge takes on Cavallari.” JACOB O’CONNELLY: “I don’t usually root for Ryan but I will tonight I hope Kluge gets crushed!” Ryan heads past the crowd high fiving a few of them then heading up the steps as in the ring Kluge has yet to take his eyes off Jacob, who trashes him on commentary without making eye contact as the referee starts the match in this one. DING DING DING! Both Ryan Cavallari and Matt Kluge stand in the center of the ring staring each other down. Matt says something to Ryan but instead of Ryan saying anything back he threw a punch that sent Matt right down back first onto the canvas. Matt pops right back up and let all of the anger he had built up from what happened at International X out. Matt gives Ryan four HARD chops to the chest followed by an Irish whip. Ryan is sent running across the ring, he bounces off the ropes and runs back to Matt. Kluge nails Ryan with a harsh knee to the stomach causing Ryan to fall to his knees and hold his stomach. Matt follows right up by wrapping his right arm around Ryan's neck and planting his forehead onto the canvas with a DDT. He hooks one of Ryan's legs pressing his shoulders down. 1.....2......kick out! JACOB O’CONNELLY: “Damn it Ryan let’s go.” Matt looks up at the referee shocked. He stands up and walks right into the referee's face and yells with great anger "YOU WANNA SCREW ME OVER LIKE ASPEN DID?" The referee kept holding up two of his fingers trying to explain that it was just a two count. Ryan is now back on his feet and heated up. Matt finally turns around only to be struck by a clothesline so devastating it could be heard all over the arena. Kluge falls flat on his back. He seemed to be knocked out. Ryan decided it wasn't over yet. RILEY STEVENS: “Kluge may be out.” Ryan guides Matt back to his feet and places his right forearm in between Kluge's legs. He then uses his strength to lift Matt off his feet and on to his shoulder. The fans get on their feet and wait for the big move they thought would come. Ryan starts to go for the running Powerslam but Kluge slithers his way off Ryan's right shoulder and drops back down to his feet. Ryan spins around instantly. Kluge lifts his left leg all the way into the air then kicks Ryan right in the teeth with a Superkick. The entire arena began to cheer because Ryan was STILL standing. Kluge's eyes widen. He tries a roundhouse kick but Ryan ducks and then wraps his arms around Matt's waist from the back. JACOB O’CONNELLY: “Beat him! BEAT HIM!” Matt fights to break Ryan's grip but he's unable to. Ryan steps back and lifts Matt all the way up into the air just to slam him down with a back Suplex. Ryan covers pressing his palm against Kluge's nose hard. 1....2...Kluge gets his shoulder up at the same time as the referee counted three. RYAN looks up at the referee with his right arm raised into the air. He thought he had won but tue referee said the match would continue because Kluge kicked out at three. Ryan sighs and attempts to keep Kluge grounded. But suddenly Kluge turns Ryan holding his arm around and locks Ryan into a tight hammerlock. Applying all of the pressure he possibly could. JACOB O’CONNELLY: “NO! NO! NO!” Ryan doesn't tap bit his arm was extended as if he was thinking. Anyone could see the pain in his eyes. Ryan reverses the hammerlock finally and grabs Kluge's left ankle and locks in a hard ankle lock submission. Kluge yells out he's in the middle of the ring with no where to go. The referee asks if he wanted to give up but he says no. Matt rolls forward and sends Ryan into some ropes. Ryan bounced off the ropes chest first. Matt follows and rolls Ryan up from the back he holds tights. 1....2...... Ryan rolls back pinning Kluge this time.1…2...3!!!! He got the clean win over the former tag champion and world championship contender. REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner…Ryan Cavallari!” These fans are going wild for the result going from scared to excited in an instant as Ryan gets the win out of no where as he holds up his arm in victory as he now rolls out of the ring to join the fans as Jacob can be heard getting off the announce table and heading into the ring as Kluge has his back turned trying to argue with the referee. RILEY STEVENS: “What is he doing?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I don’t know but I hope he gets crushed for it.” As Cavallari celebrates his win on the outside Jacob stands behind Kluge going to throw a punch but Kluge turns and catches his hand expecting it as Jacob tries to plea for forgiveness as Security that was on the outside rushes back into the ring to stop him this time grabbing his arms and pulling them behind him as Jacob quickly bails out of the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Does Jacob have a death wish?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Let’s hope he gets it.” O’Connelly scurries back to the announce table as Kluge is again pulled away by Security the second time tonight that Jacob was saved by the big security force now including Jamal Jackson as he is pulled to the back yet again. WINNER: RYAN CAVALLARI After a promo video for GIW’s ‘Aggressive Expansion’ finishes playing we head to the backstage area where standing in his office is David Sinclair with a still sweaty Ryan Cavallari and Alex Castellanos, Daniel Starr, and Phoenix Matsuda the winners tonight. DAVID SINCLAIR: “Welcome guys you four are the victors of tonight’s opening round match ups. I’d like to say all four of you did a wonderful job tonight but only one of you can win so next week we have the Semi-Finals and it will be Phoenix Matsuda versus Daniel Starr to open the show.” Starr and Matsuda take a moment to glare over at one another, both seeming ready and willing for their match up. DAVID SINCLAIR: “Leaving it as Alex Castellanos versus Ryan Cavallari in the Main Event. Good luck...” Alex and Ryan look at one another nodding as Sinclair uses this simply to hype up next week’s Revolt as we take another commercial before the Main Event, this one starting off by hyping up the Semi-Finals as well. QUOTE After a short break we return for the Main Event with our champion Hayden Carter standing in the ring with the Legacy Championship as it takes only a second before "Kiss My Lips" by Dev featuring Fabolous hits to a loud amount of boo’s this crowd isn’t on her side like they were in London as Alexis Terry walks out with a scowl on her face heading to the ring to try to take back her belt. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Here is our great champion Alexis Terry ready to take back her title from that shame of a champion Hayden Carter.” RILEY STEVENS: “Glad you are so impartial.” Terry steps into the ring staring across at Hayden as the referee holds up the belt in the air as both look up at it then the referee turns calling for the bell to start this one off. DING DING DING! The bell sounds and the main event of the very first Monday Night Revolt is underway. The crowd is hot inside the Barnhill Arena, as the fans in attendance have been waiting all night wanting to see this highly anticipated rematch for the Legacy Championship. Hayden Carter and Alexis Terry meet in the center of the ring. The newly crowned Champion has the fans behind her as Alexis just stands there silently, all business tonight having been embarrassed about her loss at International X, in front of her home country and home fans. Suddenly, Hayden makes a move and slaps Alexis hard across the face. Alexis snaps and kicks Hayden hard in the gut. RILEY STEVENS: “Brutal kick by Alexis.” Alexis drops Hayden with a snap DDT that spikes her head into the mat. It looks like Hayden is out cold as her neck lands awkwardly. The referee asks Alexis to end it due to Hayden needing medical attention but Alexis refuses. She picks her up viciously by her hair and delivers the Flawless Victory, an inverted stomp face breaker. Alexis isn’t through just yet. She climbs the turnbuckle and dives off with the Most Glamorous Moonsault Ever. She hooks the leg and we have a brand new Legacy Champion. Alexis Terry is Legacy Champion for the second time. REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner and new GIW Legacy Champion…Alexis Terry!” Alexis is handed the Legacy Championship and she kisses it as “Kiss My Lips” By Dev and Fabolous cuts back in on the public announce system. The fans boo loudly, truly sickened that Alexis is once again crowned Champion. Various medical personnel rush the ring to check on Hayden as Alexis climbs the turnbuckle and raises the Championship high into the air. Eliza Gray, Alexis’ partner in crime slides into the ring from out of nowhere and begins clapping. Alexis hops off the turnbuckle and hugs her longtime friend and student. Suddenly, the duo turn their attention back to Hayden who is still in pain, holding her neck as she continues to be checked on… RILEY STEVENS: “Oh come on leave the poor girl alone…” Alexis and Eliza throw the EMTs out of the way and begin to stomp away on Hayden. Eliza quickly applies the CMB, Canada’s Most Beautiful, a sharpshooter submission hold. Hayden screams in pain, just wanting this to be over. Alexis places the belt in her face and screams, “You will never be me! Your Nothing!” which is followed up by Alexis spitting in her face. The fans begin to fill the ring with various trash items, bottles, wrappers, plastic cups; whatever they have is slowly filling the ring. Alexis calls for Eliza to break the hold and pick her up. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Her fifteen minutes are officially up.” Hayden is lifeless as Eliza holds her in place. Alexis runs at Eliza and cracks her on the skull with the title belt and Hayden crashes to the mat. The fans boo loudly as Eliza and Alexis stand tall in the ring as Hayden rolls out of the ring and is finally helped to the back. Alexis demands a microphone and is handed one. ALEXIS TERRY: “What is standing before you is the most dominate Legacy Champion in history. Hayden beating me at International X was a fluke and I proved that tonight by ridding this company of that pathetic excuse for a professional wrestler. You will never have to see her filthy, ugly face again! You people can boo me, hate me, and wish I was gone all you want but the fact of the matter is, I’m not going anywhere..Anytime soon. You people pay to see me! You can deny it all you want but denial will get you nowhere. With Eliza and Ryan by my side, this Championship will be going nowhere. There is not a person in the back who can beat me. There isn’t a soul in this entire profession who can beat me! So I suggest you bloody pigs get use to it – get use to seeing me standing tall, victorious! Get use to seeing Eliza Gray, get use to seeing Ryan Cavallari, and get use to seeing Alexis Terry. Why? Because we are winners. You can call us…International Glamour and International Glamour are winners. Get well soon Aspen…We miss you babe!” Alexis’ music hits once more as Eliza raises Alexis’ hand in victory as Revolt fades to back, the last shot being one of the two hugging in the ring, blowing kisses to the camera. WINNER: ALEXIS TERRY (New GIW Legacy Champion)
