GIW — Card

Revolt, 29 September 2011

29 September 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: THURSDAY NIGHT REVOLT LIVE! From Kingston, Jamaica – September 29, 2011 BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! The show kicks off and this time we find ourselves in Kingston, Jamaica and the fans here in attendance seem to be enjoying the atmosphere as things get underway with the pyrotechnics that always get things underway as the camera pans around the crowd ending up at the announce table where Riley and Oliver are sitting ready to go. RILEY STEVENS: “Hello ladies and gentleman as you can see we are here in Kingston, Jamaica at this tour continues and the fans here are squished in to watch the GIW stars go at it. And they have a show for them as we kick off the show with Ace Baldwin versus Aspen Chaud in a Submission Match. Following that up will be ‘GIW Television Champion’ Curtis Durrington facing off with three potential contenders in Brody Harper, Jack Waters, and AJ Fairchild. Then we head to the triple threat match featuring Doombringer, Koji Nakamora, and Tony Miranda. And to cap it all off Nick Starks versus Alioth Starre in singles action…That right there is a show Oliver.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Thanks for letting me talk…You little bi--” Luckily things are cut off as "Big Sh#t Poppin'" instrumental by T.I. hits and the fans unleash boos on the Number One Contender as for the third week in a row it’s Ace Baldwin starting the show heading out towards the ring smirking as the fans jeer him into the ring. Once there he asks for a microphone making people wait but just as he gets a mic "Bullseye" by Aly and AJ hits as the fans blow up as Ace angrily looks to the ramp as Aspen walks out from the back with a mic of her own. ASPEN CHAUD: “Please just shut up…I don’t mean to be mean but frankly I think I speak for everyone when I say that you starting the show with your little rants has gotten boring quickly. So let me save you the time and give these fans the little summary…” Aspen takes a second to broaden her shoulders and stick out her chest like a tough guy as Ace watches her shaking his head as she enters the ring. ASPEN CHAUD: “Me name is Ace Baldwin…me is good at the wrestling…me want the title belt…me wrestle for meaningless MWA…me win title in MWA…me think that makes me the best…me will beat everyone in GIW…me hate the establishment…me win.” Aspen finishes up as the fans both laugh and cheer for her rendition of Ace as he angrily glares back at her lifting up his microphone. ACE BALDWIN: “You know I came out here to make peace with you…The two of us we could be allies here in GIW.” This catches Aspen off guard as she mouths “Oh really? in disbelief as he nods his head. ACE BALDWIN: “You know because for a long time I have been needing something…You know with Alex Castellanos gone you could use a new friend. So I am willing right now to hire you to be my new Cook.” As he says this Aspen doesn’t even seem shocked at all as the fans mostly boo except for a few sexist guys who have a good laugh. ASPEN CHAUD: “Aren’t you just soooooo funny. You know Ace tonight you have me in a Submission match and when I’m finished with you, it’ll be that’ll be known as the Number One Contender.” Aspen says this to a loud cheer from the crowd who would not mind that but they wouldn’t mind anyone being the contender over Ace at this point. ACE BALDWIN: “Your going to regret turning down my offer.” Ace throws down his microphone as does Aspen as we take a quick commercial break. QUOTE Coming back the referee has now arrived as this match is scheduled for the start of the show as Ace and Aspen have been waiting to go at it for awhile now. RILEY STEVENS: “This is exciting right here, we’ve all been waiting for this match for a long time now who will get the win?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “You already know that.” In the ring the referee is finally ready to go as he calls for the bell to start this thing starting out the battle. DING DING DING! Like a dog that doesn’t realize its size Aspen runs at Ace going for a lock up and almost expectedly Ace throws her away. Walking around taunting the downed Aspen Chaud the fans boo him as he calls for her to get up. Chaud never one to back down gets back up to her feet rushing at him jumping towards him but almost with ease Ace catches her in his arms shaking his head as she tries to wiggle free but like the jerk he is he lifts her up and throws her over his back as he walks away with ease. This starts to discourage the fans as Aspen holds her gut, the wind knocked out of her. Baldwin walks around holding up his arms bringing the boos onto him as Aspen gets up still trying to get back into this thing going after him but he ducks under her clothesline attempt and uses his size to bend her over his knee. Grinning he prepares to deliver a very literal spanking but Aspen not wanting to be disrespected like that throws a big elbow that connects to his jaw. She hits another one and this allows her to get free and rush off the ropes and on the rebound she jumps into a Thesz Press followed by Mounted Punches, this gets the fans back into it as Aspen is pushed off by Ace as she waits for him to get up and very quickly goes for the ‘K.M.A. [Kiss My Aspen]’ a Heel Lock but Ace not weakened enough is able to kick away sending Aspen into the ropes. RILEY STEVENS: “If Aspen locks that in, it’s over.” Aspen goes back after him but he shoulders her away then as she comes back in for Offense he lifts her up for a Suplex. Rather then let her go he keeps a hold of her, easy with someone as small as Aspen as he lifts her back into the air and hits another one and not done there he goes for a third but before he can hit anything Aspen is able to pry free and turns it right into a Sitout Jawbreaker. Right away the strategy seems to be simple for the both of them, wear one another down before locking in the Submission. Aspen calls for him to get up as things have turned around now and Aspen looks to try to get the advantage. Grabbing him as soon as he gets up she whips him into the corner rushing at him so he doesn’t have time to recover as she hits him with the ‘Tush Push’ a Turnbuckle Flying Butt Shot! The fans go wild as she immediately heads to the top rope looking for the move that always follows it up. Ace seems to be a little bit dazed as he stumbles back from the corner. Aspen jumps off confidently for the ‘AirBarbie’ a Flying Crossbody. But as she is in mid-air Ace jumps up and perfectly delivers a Jumping Dropkick that sends her backwards to the mat holding her gut. RILEY STEVENS: “If she has any breath left in her, I’d be surprised.” Aspen holds her gut which is where the dropkick landed struggling to catch her breath but Ace shows no sympathy picking her up from her knee and spinning around throwing her into the Corner Post! The fans boo as Aspen’s shoulder hits hard as she bounces back holding it and due to that she gets crushed with a Spinning Spinebuster. That seems to potentially put away Aspen whom seems to have taken enough damage but there is no pins in this one as Ace takes a second to think about his next move before he kicks Aspen over onto her chest as he goes in for the Texas Cloverleaf. The result is an almost scary stretching of Aspen’s body as she lets out a big groan of agony as Ace has the move sunk in deeply. The fans cheer her on to get out and surprisingly as Baldwin seems to sort of loosen up she’s able to spin the move over and end up trying to grab the heel of Ace! The fans go wild but Ace jumps up and out of harms way. RILEY STEVENS: “There he goes getting out of that maneuver yet again.” The move seems to be Aspen’s last ditch hope as she’s barely able to stand after the Cloverleaf seemed to stretch her out badly as she gets up but as she does Ace comes up with a huge Running Knee that stuns her once he hits that the transition into the ‘Smokin’ Aces’ is seamless the move being a Omoplata Crossface! The fans go wild with boos as Aspen tries her best to get free but the pressure on her neck is too much as eventually she is forced to tap out. JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…“Amazing” ACE BALDWIN!” It takes a few moments longer for Ace to break the hold pushing Aspen down as he gets up as Aspen falls to the mat holding her neck in pain as Ace holds up his arms in the air. RILEY STEVENS: “Any doubts that were put in the minds of people last week due to his performance should be erased after this one as Ace Baldwin just made Aspen Chaud tap out.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Soon he’ll do the same to Alioth.” Ace stands over Aspen throwing some sort of verbal insults at her before heading out of the ring having done what he came here for as this match ends and we head to the back. WINNER: ACE BALDWIN Heading to the back after a great opening bout we have Curtis Durrington getting ready for his match stretching out with his GIW Television Championship laying not to far away from him, his match is next and as he stretches out one of his opponents walks up noticing him and stopping…it is AJ Fairchild. AJ FAIRCHILD: "Hey champ." This causes Curtis to become alerted knowing that you have to protect yourself at all times but when he realizes it’s AJ he eases up getting up to his feet looking at his belt and nodding. CURTIS DURRINGTON: "Hey…" Naturally he was pretty stand offish knowing that he was about to face this man and knowing the belt made him a target. AJ FAIRCHILD: "It’s a shame I got hurt back at ‘Blackout II’ we never really did get to see who was the better man…Who really would have won that match…" AJ says this, his eyes still falling onto the belt the entire time as Curtis picks it up throwing it over his chest. CURTIS DURRINGTON: "I suppose…that’s what we have tonight for." Curtis nods his head as this very awkward yet telling confrontation comes to a close as Curtis heads on his way as the segment closes. QUOTE When we return to the ring for the second match of the night already standing in the ring ready to go is both Brody Harper and Jack Waters both stretching out as “Sirius” by The Alan Parsons Project hits and the fans break into cheers as AJ Fairchild jumps around out from the back with a smile on his face as he heads toward the ring high fiving the fans all the way down towards the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “You know AJ Fairchild was ever so impressive in that Television title tournament but in the title match he got hurt and had to leave the match. Now he’s back ready to get back in the mix.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Yeah…hurt…” As he jumps up into the ring his music cuts off as he poses on the top rope as now "Country Heroes" by Hank Williams III hits and the fans get even louder as out from the back fresh off a defeat comes Curtis Durrington GIW’s Television Champion holding his belt over his shoulder as everyone looks on at him with envy in their eyes as he heads to the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “You know Curtis has been impressive since getting the belt but last week was surely not his week losing quickly to Doombringer. Can he bounce back against three men or will the odds be against him?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I’ve got Harper winning.” Durrington enters the ring and as he hands the title to the referee everyone’s eyes can be seen following the belt out of the ring as the referee turns and calls for the bell to start this thing. DING DING DING! Almost as soon as the bell rings things pop off as Brody Harper runs full steam absolutely crushing Waters with a Clothesline. Catching everyone off guard he takes a second to pose over him as AJ follows up on him with a big European Upper Cut then as he is stunned puts him down with the Implant DDT. Almost like a train as AJ gets up taking a second to scream out for the crowd the champion stands behind him grabbing him around the waist and lifting him up and letting him go with a Release German Suplex. The fans go wild for him as Waters is back up now trying to take a cheap shot on him but Curtis ducks it and then grabs him and picks him up and puts him down with a huge Northern Lights Suplex. The fans are already into this fast paced action as its AJ turns as he runs up kicks him in the gut then jumps up off the ropes for a Sliced Bread #2! This sends the fans into a frenzy loving the move as Harper is up rushing at him for a Clothesline. AJ ducks it and then on the rebound hits him with a Thrust Kick. This doubles over Brody as Fairchild puts him with a Jumping Facebuster! He looks around thinking this is his chance to make a cover 1…2…Curtis kicks him off. RILEY STEVENS: “That’s the issue with a Fatal Four Way Match you have to find a time where everyone is out to make a cover.” Curtis delivers another stomp and AJ gets up to get out of harms way. Curtis immediately whips him into the corner going after him. Fairchild jumps up kicking him away but before he can do anything Waters gets up jumping up onto the ropes catching AJ with a punch looking for a Superplex. But before he can do that Brody punches Waters from behind and looks to try to take both men out. Durrington has different plans as he grabs Harper from behind wrapping him up around the waist and delivering a German Suplex! Just as he does this AJ flips over Waters and right into a Powerbomb! Both men’s moves sending the fans into a frenzy but while AJ admires his handy work Durrington is all business rushing at him and nailing him with a big Clothesline. Grabbing him up he attempts to throw him out of the ring but Fairchild grabs hold of the ropes landing on the ring apron. Curtis rushes at him but AJ punches him away as Durrington stumbles back. Taking his chance he jumps up going Springboard as he lunges at Curtis. The champion ducks under but unfortunately for Waters he’s just getting up and he eats the big Forearm Smash! RILEY STEVENS: “This is a war between these guys.” Curtis waits as this time AJ smartly turns around to find his opponent but he turns right into a Belly to Belly Suplex. Fairchild rolls out of the way as Harper gets up and takes a swing at Durrington only for him to catch his arm then swiftly power him to the mat where he locks in the Arm Bar! The fans start to go wild thinking this will be it but a recovered Waters lunges with his last bit of gas and breaks up the hold just before Harper could tap out. The fans let out a big gasp this match, so fast paced that they can hardly keep up with it all. Jack starts to try to stomp him but Curtis starts to get up fighting off the punches. But out of no where AJ comes into the screen crushing Waters with a Running Leg Lariat. But before Curtis can do anything to him Harper is back up and tries to use his good arm to punch Durrington. He ducks it and drops him to the mat, once there he goes to work as he locks in the ‘Daphne Deathlock’ a Tazzmission! All while he’s doing this on the other side of the ring AJ has locked in the ‘Holy Mutilation’ a Rear Naked Choke! The fans are going crazy as both men have it locked in and at seemingly the same time Waters and Harper tap out! And the referee rings the bell. RILEY STEVENS: “Who won the match? I couldn’t tell who tapped first.” The fans seem to be wondering the same thing as does Jamal Jackson as the referee rolls out of the ring walking over to him as both Curtis and AJ start to celebrate as Jamal takes to the mic. JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…AJ FAIRCHILD!” The fans seem split on the announcement some boo some cheer depending on whom they prefer as Curtis looks at the referee completely confused as AJ rolls out of the ring. Durrington maintains his composure but clearly doesn’t know what to think as he looks over at AJ who has exited the ring and Fairchild shrugs realizing even he couldn’t tell who was first. RILEY STEVENS: “I’m looking at replays and I can’t tell who tapped first…But apparently our referee had his eyes on both at the same time?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “They should both just be DQ’d.” The fans continue to be split as AJ heads back up the ramp as Curtis begins to nod realizing there isn’t a whole lot he can do now as he’s handed his belt realizing the man he just technically lost to might just be next in line. WINNER: AJ FAIRCHILD Heading to the back we go as Tony Miranda is standing by with Zachary Pagonis with a very determined yet slightly happy look on his face. ZACHARY PAGONIS: “I think I speak for everyone when I say it really is nice to have you back Tony we really missed you. Any how as for last week you defeated Jack Waters in singles competition but now you are set to face off with Doombringer and Koji Nakamora in a Triple Threat Match, your thoughts?” Zachary holds the mic over to Tony as he begins to answer with no hesitation at all. TONY MIRANDA: “There is no doubt in my mind these two are a whole lot better competition then Jack Waters but I am still confident. I’ve competed with the best here in GIW and I realize that to get back to the top I’ll have to do just that again.” As he’s talking Doombringer walks into the screen staring over at him as he shakes his head as he talks. DOOMBRINGER: "Better doesn’t even begin to explain how much tougher this week is going to be for you. Last week GIW gave you a gimme’ for your first match back. This week they gave you me and if you didn’t see what I did to Curtis last week…I’ll make sure to show you tonight why that was only the beginning." The message is received loud and clear as Doombringer heads off and Tony looks over at Pagonis realizing this just got real quickly as we head to a break. QUOTE It’s now time for the huge Triple Threat the fans have been dying to see. Already in the ring with his music “Voice of the Damned” by Terror echoing through the speakers is Koji Nakamora stretching out and getting ready as his music cuts out and “So Cold” by Breaking Benjamin now hits and the fans begin to boo knowing just who this is as the menacing Doombringer enters from the back heading towards the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Not often do we see a man debut like Doombringer did last week demolishing our Television Champion. You’ve got to think Blake noticed him and really how can you not?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “He’s the type of guy we needed to take care of some of these losers in GIW.” Doombringer enters the ring and Koji does not seem worried at all not one for fear as Doombringer stares his way and then “World Warrior" by RedRapper hits and the noise is deafening as the fans welcome back Tony Miranda for the second straight week with open arms, even here in Jamaica they love him. RILEY STEVENS: “And here is Tony Miranda what a way to return last week. In fact all three of these men had quick finishes last week…I have a feeling that won’t happen this week.” Tony high fives a few fans then heads up the ramp and into the ring looking from Doombringer to Koji before heading to the top rope posing which gives him an even louder response from the crowd as he jumps down the three men all intimidating in their own right look at one another and the match begins. DING DING DING! The fans are on the edge of their seats for this one. Koji has yet to not be in a match and yet to really not put up a war since he came to GIW. There was an air about Doombringer that scared just about everyone and then Tony Miranda a GIW Legend returning looking to get back to the belt that had eluded him. The three men headed for the center of the ring and in a very rare yet interesting bit of strategy the three go for a lock up, all locking hands with one another. But it was Doombringer’s for the taking as he surprisingly starts to push both men back. Smartly Tony and Koji break the holds so that they can both kick Doom in the knees together. Stopping they both bounce off the ropes going for a Double Clothesline but instead Doombringer rushes forward Spearing them both at the same time. The crowd is shocked but quickly we realize that Doombringer is the strongest in this match and perhaps the favorite. Tony rolls out of harms way but Nakamora always a warrior gets to his feet throwing a kick but Doom catches his leg. He throws it around and when he does that he gets Nakamora to give up his back as he locks in a Sleeper Hold right away trying to choke the fight right out of Koji early in this one. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Choke that Asian!” Never one for equality Oliver’s words can practically be heard making Blake cringe as Doombringer imposes his power starting to look like Koji might fade. But a recovered Tony is back up slamming his leg into Doom’s back causes him to release Nakamora who falls forward to the mat. Tony jumps up onto his shoulders locking his legs away as he starts to deliver elbows that Doombringer can’t really protect himself from. The elbows seem to be landing rather forcefully as the fans seem stunned that Doombringer isn’t going down as suddenly Doom gets his arms free and now Tony is in a predicament as Doombringer grabs him trying to throw him off his shoulders, deciding instead to just drop back sending Tony back first into the mat! The fans boo as Miranda holds his back in pain those elbows not seeming to really do much damage to the big man. Koji is back up and though he’s taken a beating thus far if there’s one thing we’ve learned about him it’s that he’s always in the match as he nails Doombringer with a kick to the stomach as he rushes at him trying to take him off his foot he gets lifted into a One Handed Backbreaker showing off his strength. RILEY STEVENS: “This man is some sort of monster.” But it’s time for the return of Tony as he heads back into the ring as Tony steps in throwing a big punch that actually sends Doombringer off balance. But like a boxer he throws another one right back at Tony. The two go back and forth, seeming to be throwing everything into it to try to take the other but nearly budging. Finally Doombringer gets sick of this game trying to jump in to grab him but he gets caught with a Spinning Roundhouse Kick one of Tony’s famous moves. Doombringer falls backwards to the mat, rolling to the apron. Koji is back on his feet and boom gets hit with a Snap Suplex by Tony. Doombringer starts to get up behind him on the outside of the ropes but this leaves him wide open for a shot as Tony knowing this is a Triple Threat Match turns and hits the Rolling Wheel Kick! The fans go wild as Doombringer falls to the outside and then as Nakamora stumbles to his feet he rushes off the ropes and runs at him hitting the always loved ‘Third Strike’ a Running Leg Lariat! The fans go wild as the first pin attempt is also the last one in this match 1…2…3! JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…TONY MIRANDA!” Tony rolls out of the ring raising his hands and then falling into the fans who embrace him with hugs as the fan favorite walks around high fiving people at ringside as Doombringer returns to the ring angrily glaring at him. RILEY STEVENS: “Tony keeps the winning streak up picking up the pin here in the triple threat match.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “He took Doombringer’s scraps.” Doombringer glares up the ramp at Tony as he reaches the top but angrily he lifts the already hurt Koji to his feet and puts him up into the air then puts him down with the ‘Tainted Soul’ an Elevated Powerbomb that absolutely devours Koji. RILEY STEVENS: “Something tells me that was a message for Tony Miranda that this is not over.” Tony watches shaking his head as Doombringer looks up at him both men clearly knowing this was only a battle in a much bigger war. WINNER: TONY MIRANDA One last time we head to the backstage area where it’s Blake Beckett’s office as the setting of this part of the show as Blake is looking through paperwork. As he does this the door opens and in comes the very oddly absent Joshua Acquin walking into the room with a grin on his face. BLAKE BECKETT: “Hey Josh what are you up to?” Blake looks back at his paper work not feeling this to be much of an urgent thing as Acquin walks over in front of his desk. JOSHUA ACQUIN: “Just wondering what you were going to do about my birthday, it’s tomorrow got anything in mind for me?” Blake chuckles at the statement as he looks up nodding his head. BLAKE BECKETT: “I’m sure the guys have something planned for you…After all your pretty popular around here.” Beckett says this with clear sarcasm in his voice as Josh seems to take a bit of a exception to it. JOSHUA ACQUIN: “Maybe they’d like me more if you actually gave me something to do around here…You know maybe book a match here or there?” Joshua says this clearly wanting it pretty badly but Blake doesn’t even look up from his desk. BLAKE BECKETT: “When I feel your ready…You will.” Acquin looks down angrily that’s not the answer he wanted as he scowls at Blake who doesn’t see it. JOSHUA ACQUIN: “Of course…” Acquin turns around angrily pushing his way through the door as Blake just continues to go about what he was doing. ==== From there we head to the Doctors Office where Dr. House is being visited by Aspen Chaud who is sitting in their designated area for injuries with a big ice pack on the back of her neck. DR. HOUSE: “Just keep it iced and you should be fine.” Aspen cringes as she tries to nod her head, holding the ice pack there but an unwelcome site comes into the screen as Nick Starks walk into the camera angle with his mask still on his face. NICK STARKS: “I hate to see you in pain……that I didn’t cause.” Starks chuckles to himself as Aspen has to try to contain herself from exploding on the man that took out her friend last week. NICK STARKS: “I’m sure your wondering why I didn’t come out and do the same thing I did to Alex to you tonight. The simple truth is…with you I don’t want to be picking up someone else’s scraps. So rest up…I’ll be waiting.” Starks now makes his exit doing enough to anger Aspen whose injury probably kept her from doing anything as we take a break before the main event. QUOTE Heading to the ring for the main event we come back already realizing two things first that in the ring was Nick Starks and just joining the commentating booth is Ace Baldwin just sitting down. RILEY STEVENS: “We are being joined by the Number One Contender, Ace Baldwin here at ringside.” ACE BALDWIN: “The pleasure is all yours. You know I really didn’t want to be here, I could care less about watching an Alioth Starre match but whatever…I suppose it couldn’t hurt.” "Blood & Fire (Out of the Ashes Mix)" by Type O Negative hits and the fans go insane with cheers as out from the back with a smile on his face is Alioth Starre holding the Undisputed belt over his shoulder as he walks towards the ring. ACE BALDWIN: “You do realize every time this guy comes out with the belt in a new location we look like a joke right? Or am I the only one that realizes this.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Your absolutely right.” Alioth heads into the ring holding up his belt for all to see as the fans give him another cheer as he hands his belt out to the referee who hands it out of the ring then calls for the bell. DING DING DING! The bell rang. The first encounter began with a shove to Alioth Starre. Nick Starks then hurried out of the ring for a microphone and pointed toward his opponent as he spoke. NICK STARKS: “Starre! You can’t touch me! I am simply the best! You’re reign has gone for too long and you’re visibly burning out.” The count began and Starre leaned back against the ropes in wait as Nick let it all out. NICK STARKS: “I am from New York, you are nothing to me and neither is anyone else here! I deserve that title more than anyone here! I deserve it! Me!” The crowd began to chant, jeering at Starks, which was enough for Alioth to wince from the abundance of Spanish. Alioth took a microphone as well and invited Starks back into the ring before the count out would have disqualified him. ALIOTH STARRE: “Nick Starks, I’m champion simply because I’m luckier than you. Let me get something straight, did you come down here to wrestle, or act like an arse?---Now, I’ll tell you what I’ll do, Starks. You can do a headlock on me, I mean; you’ve got to know how to do some wrestling moves, yeah? I’ll stand right in the middle of the ring and let you have a free hold. I’m going to give you the first shot at me.” True to his word, Alioth dropped the microphone and bent over for Nick Starks. Hands behind his back and head exposed, he moved to the center of the ring. Nick Starks approached cautiously and made a faux gesture to go for his opponent’s neck, but backed off. It took him another moment before he properly applied a headlock onto Alioth Starre. ACE BALDWIN: “He just lost himself this match, the guy is a joke.” The crowds began to jeer, but Alioth extended an arm to gesture with a finger for them to wait only a moment longer. The moment came when Alioth wrapped around Nick’s waist and then slammed him back with a backdrop Suplex. The crowd erupted in unison and Starre finally pandered to this. He pulled up his opponent between the knees and then with a Piledriver, drove him back into the canvas. Alioth rolled his opponent over for the pin and connected for the full count. JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…ALIOTH STARRE!” With victory in his grasp, he pulled off of his opponent and took the microphone again. ALIOTH STARRE: “You see, Starks, no, everyone, it takes more to defeat me than mockery. I can be defeated as it has been done, but I’m not going to lay down for fools like Nick Starks here that think they can get by on ego and arrogance alone. That is all.” He pandered to the crowd one last time with his hand up as his opponent lay before him but then they started to jeer as sliding into the ring like a jackalope is Ace Baldwin who tore the belt away from the referee as he slams it into the back of Starre’s head! The fans go crazy with boos as Ace glares around at them reaching down and picking up the mic that Alioth dropped. ACE BALDWIN: “You really think your something don’t you Alioth? Let me tell you something you may think I am arrogant but unlike Nick Starks I can back it up! So here is my announcement. On Halloween Night it’ll be me versus you for that belt. And when it’s all said and done you will hear…Here is your winner and the new…GIW Undisputed Champion…Ace Baldwin!” Ace throws the mic down onto Alioth’s stomach as he heads for the ropes sliding out quickly as he backs up the ropes, all that can be heard are the fans boo as the show ends with Ace Baldwin standing tall as the show fades. WINNER: ALIOTH STARRE
