GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS SUNDAY NIGHT REVOLT 6/21/15 FROM KAUFFMAN ARENA IN GALVESTON, TEXAS BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! “Over and Under” by Egypt Central welcomes us to another edition of Sunday Night Revolt! The camera pans around the capacity crowd as the fireworks stream into the night sky high above Kauffman Arena. We see signs such as “Callum Sold Out”, “Violence for Triple Crown”, and “Wow, really Jon decided to get married right during the buildup to the biggest show of the year? I’m really happy for him and all, but he should have considered moving the wedding to the winter when the game cools down a bit, you know? I know I sound like a dick, but that is how I feel” waving high and proud. After a while, we cut to the commentary booth. SARAH SHARP: "Ladies and gentlemen, welcome to another edition of Sunday Night Revolt! We are coming to you live on Fox Sports Southwest, Oklahoma, and New Orleans just one week removed from Category 5. We hype that show as if it is one of the biggest and most important of the year, and it certainly didn’t disappoint. Tonight, you experience the aftermath." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "If you didn’t catch it live, you definitely should go back and watch the show. We saw so many amazing matches I don’t even know where to start! The Sir managed to retain his Deep South Championship in a hellacious match with Johnny Violence. Alioth Starre was the Sole Survivor, outlasting three of GIW’s best… and Chris Callum. Well…" SARAH SHARP: "He is still the champion, but to earn that right, he had to make a deal with the devil. Aspen Chaud made her priorities known, but the real shock of the night came when Alexis Terry of all people backstabbed her former ally Bronx Valescence, joining up with her enemy Chris Callum." “When Angels Fall” begins to play as the fans let loose a slew of boos. They know that Chris Callum is on his way to the ring, kicking off this show, and they are not happy about it. OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Well, it looks like we are about to get the answers to all of our questions. So just shut up Sarah and listen closely because the champion is gonna have a lot to get off his chest." Out from the curtain come the new trio that everyone is talking about. In the middle is the GIW Undisputed Champion, Chris Callum. His black mask looks even more odd given his black suit, but the entire group is dressed for success tonight. Aspen Chaud is on his left, wearing a black dress now, rather than a white one. Symbolic, one would assume. And on his right is the woman of the hour, Alexis Terry – also wearing a black dress. The sight of her elicits more boos than anyone else, and she definitely plays it up. With a big smile on her face, she followed Aspen and Chris down to the ring. RUBY REYNOLDS: “Ladies and gentlemen, please welcome – The GIW Undisputed Champion, Chris Callum…. The Commissioner of Revolt, Aspen Chaud… and ‘England’s Most Glamourous Export’, Alexis Terry!” The crowd welcomes them with more boos as the music begins to die down and the three of them are given microphones from the bell keeper. They face the hard camera in a straight line, and it looks like Chris Callum will be the first to speak. CHRIS CALLUM: "You wanted an alliance, and you got one, didn’t you?" CHRIS CALLUM: "All I have heard about for weeks and weeks is how Aspen and I have some kind of romantic relationship, right? That she goes out of her way to keep the title belt on me? I got asked about it everywhere I went, without fail – and after a while, it really began to get me down. Because I was a true hero to all of you. You repay me by spreading rumors. Lies. You cheer on guys like Bronx Valescence because they are funny on Twitter, and I was starting to get boos for the first time. Well, I had enough of it. And I decided to do something about it." CHRIS CALLUM: "Here is some knowledge for all of you – there was no alliance between Aspen and I. When I called out Bronx, Aspen, The Sir, Seton, and whoever else, it wasn’t some kind of ruse. I was speaking from the heart because I thought that, by now, I had earned the right to do that. I gave everything for you. I had my jaw broken fighting for you. And you repaid me with boos." CHRIS CALLUM: "Everything that happened to Bronx Valescence at Category 5, and everything that is going to happen to your new heroes is payback for you abandoning me. Because that is exactly what you did. When I needed you most, when I was getting ready for the biggest title defense of my career, you all let me down. So I turned my back on you before it killed me." CHRIS CALLUM: "I don’t regret anything. I would do it all again if I had to. But none of this would be possible if it wasn’t for Aspen Chaud. When I was down in the dumps, she was the one who pulled me back up again. She promised me that everything would get better again if I just trusted her. I’ll admit, I was a little bit skeptical, but she came through. Aspen Chaud came through." ASPEN CHAUD: "Thank for that Chris. I’m just glad I could help. And you know, it was easy to reach out and help a man like Chris Callum. He fought his way to the top, and I wasn’t going to let you all be the ones that tore him down again. I respect Chris Callum, because no matter what any of you say, he really is the best this company has to offer. And as Commissioner of Revolt, it my job to make sure that the best have their chances to shine. I consider all of this as a part of my job description." ASPEN CHAUD: "All of those rumors that you took to so quickly – that you believed were true… they weren’t just about Chris Callum. They were about me. They were designed to make me seem like a horrible boss. Well I happen to like being in an authority role in GIW. Though my days in the ring are behind me, I still have a lot to offer. I wasn’t going to let that slip away from me. This is my dream job, and I had to protect my credibility." ASPEN CHAUD: "Credibility is important to me. This company would have no credibility if a man like Bronx Valescence were to hold the title belt. There would be pictures of it all over Tinder… all of his whores, if he can manage to get any, would be thinking of ways they could steal the title belt. I would have to hunt through every pawn shop on this island just to get the crown jewel of this empire back where it belongs. The choice was simple. Bronx Valescence – it isn’t your time, honey." ASPEN CHAUD: "I had to make a tough decision. If I lay a hand on any member of the roster in the ring, I lose my job. That is in my contract, and I’m not going to fall victim to that trap. I needed some muscle to make sure things went as planned. I needed a hired gun. I needed someone who hated the idea of a Bronx Valescence title reign as much as I did. I needed… Alexis Terry." ALEXIS TERRY: "Did you all miss me?" ALEXIS TERRY: "I figured you did. It is great to be back in the ring, entertaining all of you. I’ll admit, it is pretty odd standing next to Chris Callum and Aspen Chaud. I mean, I broke his jaw two months ago, and she was in the ring crying like a baby over it. I don’t regret anything I did. I was right to punish you, Chris. You’re talented. I’ve always admitted that. But you were naïve, and you needed to be taught a lesson." CHRIS CALLUM: "For months and months, Alexis Terry told me that it was no use trying to be a hero, and I wouldn’t listen. I was too stubborn. I wanted to see the good in all of you, but I was naïve. The truth is, I wasted my time. You don’t deserve a hero like me." ALEXIS TERRY: "It is a good thing you learned you lesson, because if you didn’t, you wouldn’t have that title around your waist right now. But now that you see these people for the fickle, disloyal assholes that they really are, I think we can work together just fine, Chris." ALEXIS TERRY: "Speaking of disloyalty, how about that Bronx Valescence guy, right?" ALEXIS TERRY: "I pulled him out of obscurity and I made him a star. I made sure that he would find his way into the Undisputed Championship picture after he helped me win my Deep South Championship. That title, by the way, is still mine. Aspen, you can strip me as many times as you want, but I never lost that title… and I want it back. But I also want a new title, one that isn’t stretched out by The Sir." ASPEN CHAUD: "We will discuss it." ALEXIS TERRY: "Of course. Back to you Bronx – I didn’t forget about you. I made you. I gave you everything you have. But what happened when I got suspended? What did you do? Nothing. You didn’t speak out for me. You didn’t take the fall like you pledged you would. You saw that Undisputed title match coming up and you forgot about the woman who got you there. Because of that, Bronx, you paid the ultimate price. You’ll never get close to the title again, I’ll make sure of it." CHRIS CALLUM: "No one will get close to my title until I decide they can. That is the purpose of this alliance, and as you saw last night, we can be extremely effective." QUOTE RUBY REYNOLDS: “Ladies and Gentleman the following match is scheduled for one fall and is for the GIW Television Championship! Making his way to the ring first from Brixton, London, England. He is the number one contender for the GIW Undisputed Championship, Alioth Starrrrrrrrrrre!” “Starre Crossed” By: NC begins to play as Alioth meanders to the ring. He moves slow down the center of the ramp. Alioth rolls into the ring and begins to run the ropes. “Starre Crossed” slowly fades out and is relapsed with “Imaginary” by the band Evanescence. RUBY REYNOLDS: “And finally, from Calgary, Alberta, Canada. Johnny Violence emerges from the back rubbing his title and grinning. He walks to the ring with the belt hoisted above his head with one hand. Once he reaches, Jessica Tendonin, The two slide into the ring together. SARAH SHARP: “I don’t like how this is looking, Oliver. Tendonin and Violence seem a little to familiar.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “There you go again with your crazy conspiracy theories. Violence is down from the move and Alioth used ever once of strength to get up and hit it. On the outside Tendonin is finally starting to stir. Tendonin turns and is greeted by Spinning Hell Kick that send her staggering backward. She bounces off the ropes and right into a Northern Lights Suplex. Tendonin is down and Alioth bridges for the pin. 1 2 KICKOUT! SARAH SHARP: “Alioth is going to have to do more than that to get his hands on the TV title.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “These matches are always brutal, Sarah. Eventually it's going to devolve to the point where you have to kneecap someone to win.” Johnny Violence slides back into the ring while Alioth has Tendonin in a pin. He waits for the two to break up and then nails Alioth with a wicked Spear. Tendonin and Violence proceed to stomp Alioth Starre mercilessly. There boots move up and down until Alioth's stomach is the color or the cosmos. The ref tries to break up the lynching but is shoved away by Violence. Alioth seizes the moment and sweeps Tendonin's legs out from under her. Alioth rallies to his feet as the crowd cheers him on. Violence turns and Alioth gives him a kick to the gut. Alioth sets Johnny up for a Brainbuster by lifting him into the Suplex position. No! Tendonin blindsides Alioth with a clubbing blow of the back of the head. J-Vi lands on his feet and then he and Tendonin go back to stomping the life out of Alioth Starre. The referee tries again to break up the assault but is ignored. He has no choice but to call for the bell. DING DING! SARAH SHARP: “Pathetic! What a foul way to win a match.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Hey now! When you're champion you gotta do what you got to do. The ref finally separates the three combatants and helps Alioth to his feet. They stand in the center of the ring with Alioth's hand raised and the other clutching his ruined stomach. RUBY REYNOLDS: You're winner due to disqualification, Alioth Starrrrre! However since there was no pin-fall or submission decision your GIW Television Champion is still The King of Anarchy, Jaaaaaaaaaaaaahnny violence! Alioth rolls out of the ring still clutching his stomach. He makes his way to the back but never takes his eyes off the ring. Johnny and his partner in crime, Jessica Tendonin celebrate in the ring. WINNER: JOHNNY VIOLENCE (STILL GIW TELEVISION CHAMPION We cut to the back where we see George Stark talking quietly to a group of people.
