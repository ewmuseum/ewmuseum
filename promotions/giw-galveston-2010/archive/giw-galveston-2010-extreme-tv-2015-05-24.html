GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS EXTREME TV 5/24/15 FROM THE FREEMAN COLISEUM IN SAN ANTONIO, TEXAS BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! The PYROTECHNICS~ make off at the base of the stage in the Freeman Coliseum in San Antonio, Texas. The crowd is ecstatic as GIW makes its triumphant return to the River City. One of the most loyal markets for GIW, the San Antonio crowd will be playing a much larger role in the future of GIW as it begins to tour, and they know it. It all starts tonight with Extreme TV. Signs in the crowd like “LOLSETONWINS”, “LOLCALLUMWINS”, “Stark Strong”, “#RiseAboveTerry”, “I’m Entering the Battle Royal”, “Land Shark” (yeah, just “Land Shark”), and “I’m Joining MEM” can be seen being waved through the audience as we cut down to the commentary booth. SARAH SHARP: "Ladies and gentlemen, thank you for joining us tonight at Extreme TV, live from San Antonio, Texas. I’m Sarah Sharp joined alongside my broadcast partner for the evening, the legendary Oliver Thibodaux. Oliver, how excited are you for tonight?" OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Sarah, I’ve been looking forward to Extreme TV all week. I’m skipping my own mother’s birthday to be here tonight. I’m just that Extreme." SARAH SHARP: "GIW isn’t known for deviating too much from traditional wrestling, so tonight will be the perfect opportunity for the wrestlers you know and love to let out a little bit of steam. And taking one look at this card, let me be the first to say that this could get ugly." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "I don’t think it will take too long to get there either, Sarah. This show starts off with The Sir and Bronx Valescence battling it out for chance to take on the Undisputed Champion at Category 5. Both of these men have been trying so hard to establish themselves as main event contenders in GIW, and this will be the time when they have a chance to prove it." SARAH SHARP: "The Sir has to be the favorite in that match. In the past two weeks, he has defeated Matt Kluge and taken Alioth Starre, a GIW Hall of Famer to the edge and back. I don’t think it would be too much of a stretch to say that there is no one in the company hotter than The Sir right now." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "The Sir may be the favorite, but Bronxy V will have something up his sleeve. He is a member of the Main Event Monarchy, one of the most ruthless factions in GIW history. We saw what Alexis Terry did to Chris Callum, and I wouldn’t be surprised if Bronx does the same thing to The Sir tonight. This is the opportunity Alexis promised Bronx when he joined the group. She said she would make anyone who joined the Main Event Monarchy a main eventer, and I think she came through on her promise. The Sir, Bronx Valescence, and Johnny Violence all have their chance to make a mark on the company that will last forever tonight." SARAH SHARP: "We’ll talk more about Alexis and Johnny in just a second, but how about the TV title match? Terra Nova qualified for a one on one match with the TV Champion, Britney Filmore, at the last iPPV Exile. This gives her a significant advantage, as normally the TV title is defended in four, or even five person matches. Do you think she is ready?" OLIVER THIBODAUX: "The last few weeks haven’t been good to Nova. After she won the Stewart Beach Brawl at Exile, she has gone 0-2, taking losses to Ghetti Ono and Zuri Chastain. Momentum is extremely important in wrestling, as you know, and right now, Terra Nova has none." SARAH SHARP: "Britney Filmore, on the other hand, has been on a tear the past two weeks. Last week on Revolt, she became the TV Champion when she upset the GIW Hall of Famer and current Deep South Champion Alexis Terry. I don’t think she is going to want to lose the belt so soon." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Becoming Television Champion is easy, defending it is hard. Normally everyone is gunning for you, and that’s why we see a typical reign only lasting about one week. But this is a singles match, and it works just as much in Nova’s favor as it does Filmore. We’ll see if she can manage to retain tonight." SARAH SHARP: "Alexis Terry will be attempting to do the same thing tonight – retain her title. Duff Briggs has proven to be a worthy challenger, but Alexis Terry is the still the champion. The Deep South Championship is on the line later tonight in a brass knuckles on a pole match." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Diff was looking forward to having a one on one match with Chris Callum, a man that he respects, but wants to beat. Unfortunately for Duff, Alexis Terry has proven herself to be deadly with a set of brass knuckles. She’s fractured two jaws with them so far in her career, and I think she will be looking to make it three tonight at Extreme TV." SARAH SHARP: "The only way she will be able to get her hands on them is if she wins the match, as the first person to procure the brass knuckles from the pole will be declared the winner. If she can manage to grab them, she could add insult to injury by slamming them into Duff’s jaw after the match, but I’m sure he is aware of the possibility." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "She has to win the match first. I don’t think it is too much of a stretch to call Duff Briggs the favorite in this match. He is the bigger man, he’s stronger, and he’s probably quicker than Alexis too. If he tries to ascend the turnbuckle to grab the knucks, I don’t see Alexis having the strength to pull him away. I’m gonna go ahead and call it right now – I don’t think Alexis will be able to pull this one off. This may be the match she requested, but I don’t think it fits her style." SARAH SHARP: "Speaking of matches that my not cater to a wrestler’s style, after we see the Deep South Championship on the line, we will see an open battle royal where the final four participants in the match will be entered into Sole Survivor. Battle Royals favor larger wrestlers, to be sure, but we are expecting a huge showing for this match including past, present, and future GIW stars." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "We know for sure that Madison Seton will be entering the match. I think it is safe to assume that Alioth Starre and Matt Kluge will enter as well. All three of these folks are veterans of the industry, and because of that, they know how to work a battle royal match in their favor. You’re gonna see a lot of kids go out there and come in hot, but a battle royal is all about saving your energy for the end." SARAH SHARP: "We’ve also got wrestlers like Journeyman Phillips, George Stark, and Zuri Chastain entering the match as well. I certainly think Journeyman and George are uniquely suited to do well in these types of matches, but there are also a number of debuting wrestlers we will see in the match tonight. All you have to do is make it to the final four, so placement, which is determined at random, will probably be a huge part of this one." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "I still think this match favors the veterans, but don’t forget that every single entrant in this match gets to come out with a weapon of their choice. If they choose something that they are particularly good at wielding, well… we don’t call them difference makers for nothing. Zuri Chastain could make George Stark a non-factor with one well placed whack of a steel chair." SARAH SHARP: "And of course, all of these wrestlers will be vying for a shot to the compete in the Sole Survivor match at Category 5. The rules to that match are extremely simple. Four wrestlers start out in a four way match. When one is eliminated, we take a break, and come back for a three way match later in the night. When another wrestler is eliminated, only by pinfall or submission, mind, you, the main event of Category 5 will be a singles match to determine the number one contender for the GIW Undisputed Championship – a match they will be granted in the main event of Moment of Glory V, the biggest event of the year. This is a star making match." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "You’re absolutely right. In four years, there have only been two winners. Aspen Chaud and Alexis Terry are GIW Hall of Famers, and Madison Seton is a future Hall of Famer. Fun fact – Madison Seton is the only person to win Sole Survivor twice, which is an amazing feat. If she can qualify tonight, then she will be the odds on favorite to win the match for a third time. She knows how to pace herself in that grueling test of wills. But first, she has to get there. We will see what happens tonight." SARAH SHARP: "Two people that will be extremely interested in the outcome of that match are Johnny Violence, and the Undisputed Champion Chris Callum. These two men square off tonight with the title belt on the line, so it may be better for each of them to focus on tonight – but I would be willing to bet that both men have their eyes set on Moment of Glory V coming up in the middle of July. These two will compete in a first blood match, as decided by fan voting, so we know that this one will be brutal." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "We have the potential to see two brutal, bloody endings in a row for Chris Callum when he puts his title on the line. Of course at Exile, we saw Alexis Terry crack his jaw with a set of brass knuckles. For the past two weeks, he has been recovering from the injury. Even though he appeared to be in good spirits for the split second we saw him on Revolt last week, I don’t think it is safe to assume anything. There is no way he will be at 100% tonight, and that’s something Johnny Violence plans to take advantage of." SARAH SHARP: "Johnny Violence is so confident that he can pick up the win over the injured Callum tonight that he has spend the last week relaxing on Miami beach. That being said, I don’t think we should assume he will be coming into this match with anything less than full intensity. Johnny Violence is so close to the Undisputed Championship, he can taste it. He will not let this opportunity slip through his fingers easily." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Yeah, well neither will Chris Callum. He may be battered, and he may be bruised, but if he truly is Superman then he will step up to the plate this week and come out with a win. The odds are stacked against him, but a Champion doesn’t make excuses. This should be one hell of a match to watch. We’re gonna find out what these two men are made of tonight." We will see Bronx Valescence and The Sir battling it out for a shot at the GIW Undisputed Championship at Category 5." We cut over to the ring.
