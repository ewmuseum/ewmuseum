GIW — Card

Revolt, 6 October 2011

6 October 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: THURSDAY NIGHT REVOLT LIVE! From San Juan, Puerto Rico – October 6, 2011 BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! As the show starts up we head around the ring where the fans from here in San Juan in Puerto Rico are looking excited as they cheer on the start of the show getting ready for some action as the camera pans around them until it gets to the announce table where the always bickering Riley and Oliver are sitting at attention. RILEY STEVENS: “Welcome everyone we are here in San Juan, Puerto Rico with another exciting edition of Revolt. We are still on our way towards ‘On Hallowed Ground II’ and you can almost feel people starting to get the final bit of momentum into a big match.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “And Ace Baldwin is still looking great.” RILEY STEVENS: “You may be right about that, last week he sent a statement to the entire roster when he defeated Aspen Chaud then attacked Alioth Starre later in the night. Tonight we have a packed card with a lot of fun action. Starting the show off will be ‘GIW Television Champion’ Curtis Durrington versus AJ Fairchild and finally ‘GIW Undisputed Champion’ Alioth Starre in a big triple threat match.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Notice that Ace is in the Main Event spot.” RILEY STEVENS: “Before we get there we have some other matches including Koji Nakamora versus Nick Starks as well as Doombringer and Aspen Chaud doing battle. But just as Oliver pointed out the huge main event is Tony Miranda one on one with Ace Baldwin what a match.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “The Road to The Age of Ace continues!” With that very biased statement by Oliver we head to an early commercial before the first match gets underway. QUOTE It’s the first match of the night and things are about to get underway with the GIW Television Champion currently in the ring waiting as “Sirius” by The Alan Parsons Project hits and out from the back to a loud cheer is fan favorite AJ Fairchild! The fans high five him as he runs towards the ring and slides in. RILEY STEVENS: “This AJ Fairchild kid has looked real impressive as of late. Last week he picked up the win in the four way and this week gets a step up against both of GIW’s champions.” AJ heads into the ring nodding over at Curtis as he heads to the top rope posing as "Blood & Fire (Out of the Ashes Mix)" by Type O Negative hits and the fans get even louder as GIW Undisputed Champion, Alioth Starre. Out he comes looking poised and ready as he walks towards the ring in his always slow and methodical manner. RILEY STEVENS: “Our champion has two men to deal with this week, with Ace lingering we know that he’ll want to send a message to him.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Yeah, a message that’ll be topped by Ace in the Main Event!” DING DING DING! Alioth pulled up both of his black elbow pads and did a few hurdler stretches as Curtis Durrington and A.J. Fairchild circled one another to lock up, but Alioth stood to find the two stop before they could lock arms. They both turned toward the Undisputed Champion and charged him. Alioth avoided contact back pulling himself out onto the rim of the ring and threw his hands up. The referee broke the two off of him and the champion dropped to the outer mats. A.J. Fairchild and Curtis Durrington finally locked up with one another to display a struggle of power. Curtis was stronger, but A.J. was able to maneuver better like a ferret in the hold. It ended with Fairchild giving his opponent a quick kick to the kidney and backing off. Curtis jabbed Fairchild, but it was dodged for another kick to the liver by Fairchild. Another kick and Curtis felt he’d end up with a urinary tract defection. Curtis backed off to the corner of the ring to rest when he noticed the Undisputed Champion finally slid in briefly. Fairchild moved toward Starre. Alioth slid back out of the ring, refusing to lay hands on either men until he was ready to. RILEY STEVENS: “Alioth taking a little breather.” A.J. Fairchild shook his head, but moved to follow up his attacks on Durrington. A.J. stopped a small punching count barrage over Durrington’s head when he heard the noise of the Champion call to them, then the sound of what seemed like Spanish after Alioth’s voice finished speaking. It was an upgrade from the insulting German in Alioth’s promo for the match, but soon Fairchild ignored it to continue on Durrington. ALIOTH STARRE: “So unfocused. How on Terra Nova do you expect to defeat me Fairchild? You’re a nobody to me, just as Trent Lost and others were. Curtis Durrington is a second rate no-shower of talent. I’d much rather force you to grow up man to man than to share the ring with a third opponent.” Fairchild used the rules of the match to knock Curtis Durrington out of the ring to give all of his attention to the champion. He turned to the ropes and tried to fire back at Starre, but without a microphone he wasn’t heard. Starre took advantage of this and moved closer, glaring up at his opponent, microphone in hand. He pointed to A.J. and declared the immaturity of the world upon him. ALIOTH STARRE: “They’ll cheer me for any reason possible, Fairchild. I’m the Undisputed Champion and I fight for them, of course. However, little do the spaniards, and Americans know my new task in life is to quell individuals like yourself for lacking real talent. If you’re going to fight me, I want to see you a more focused individual. Perhaps if we went one on one, perhaps. I want Galveston Island Wrestling to be filled with bright stars that will carry this business forward like myself, Aspen, and others before us. Show me you deserve this, Fairchild!” Fairchild slid out of the ring, but Starre was ready for him and with his free hand, pushed Fairchild into Curtis Durrington to let himself escape back into the ring. Fairchild followed behind, then Durrington. Fairchild dropkicked his opponent out of the ring, but in doing so, opened him up for Starre to take advantage and pull him back to his feet. Alioth threw a hard right chop across Fairchild’s chest, again, and again until the red shown through Fairchild’s flesh. Starre finally let off to allow A.J. to hold his beaten chest and recover. A.J. countered a big boot with an Enzuigiri and took the opportunity to pounce the champion. He maneuvered the two to lock in a reverse chinlock, but Alioth was not going to have any of that. His hand already at the ropes, he tugged hard to pull half of his body over the ring and force off his opponent. Alioth climbed out and threw his big boot kick to keep Durrington down and out on the mats outside of the ring. He then slid back in when A.J. was at a comfortable distance from the ropes though it was still a risk. RILEY STEVENS: “While Alioth may say that he doesn’t feel AJ is worthy he’s proving otherwise in this match.” He pulled himself up with the ropes as protection and the referee in front of him. When Fairchild came to follow up his attacks, Alioth retaliated with a hard right heart punch to buy himself some more time to recover. A.J. returned to be tripped by Starre and with the ropes in hand, he used them to hop up on the first rope and come down on his opponent with a knee drop to the chest. Alioth covered his opponent, but Durrington pulled him off before the two count. Alioth pushed Durrington into the announce tables and then turned back around to slide into the ring, but A.J. was ready with a boot to the face. With both of his opponent’s backs to the tables, he wasn’t sure which one to strike. He chose Durrington and used the ropes to lunge out and through the table, crashing into Curtis Durrington outside of the ring. Alioth shook his head in disappointment. He expected Fairchild to take the opportunity against the champion, but didn’t. He slid into the ring to wait for his two fallen opponents to return. Fairchild was first, but he dragged Durrington with him. RILEY STEVENS: “Can Fairchild get the win?” Alioth had already been across the ring, resting and waiting when he noticed Fairchild lock his opponent into a submission move. It just so happen to be the boy’s finisher. ‘Holy Mutilation’, also known as the rear naked choke. Alioth, flabbergasted and flustered, rushed across the ring and tried to kick Fairchild off, but the boy refused to give in to the beating. Unable to pull him off, Starre instead took Durrington’s legs and set him up for the figure-four leglock, the ‘Four Star Mary’ as Alioth called it. With bottom and top succumb to the worst torture of any individual in the night, even for the first match, Durrington tapped out. RILEY STEVENS: “Who just won?” The referee wasn’t sure whose hand to raise until finally he raised both hands in the air declaring both the winners. JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winners…AJ Fairchild and Alioth Starre?” The fans seem to be a bit confused that this could be the ending just as well as the two men in the ring as Alioth nods his head and smiles seeming to give some slight respect as AJ nods as well almost saying “Still think I’m not talented?” as we take a break after a great first match. WINNER(s): AJ FAIRCHILD & ALIOTH STARRE As we head to the back we see a very angry Joshua Acquin waiting behind the entrance area as Curtis heads back with his belt over his shoulder holding his legs as Josh stops him. JOSHUA ACQUIN: “Wait up champ.” Curtis stops looking over at Joshua drained as Acquin grins and grabs the belt off his shoulder. JOSHUA ACQUIN: “You know when I held this belt it meant something. And while Blake might think I have no power, I still have the ability to vacate this belt and suspend you indefinitely…so that’s just what I am going to do.” Curtis looks surprised trying to talk but before he can Austin Acquin comes into the screen with a smirk as Joshua puts the belt on him. JOSHUA ACQUIN: “Move along…” Durrington seems at a loss for words at this abuse of power as he heads on his way as Josh turns smirking as Austin laughs himself as a new champion has just technically been crowned. QUOTE As we return to the ring for the second match of the night we already have Koji Nakamora and Nick Starks already in the ring ready to go. RILEY STEVENS: “Welcome back to the ring as Koji Nakamora and Nick Starks are ready to go in this one.” DING DING DING! The bell rang and the contestants began their bout. With a quick lock up, Nakamora shoved his opponent back, then arched his leg behind the other’s, wrapped an arm over Stark’s chest and pulled him down to the canvas with a trip. Starks pulled himself up, though surprised at the quick loss of momentum, but he knew he’d gain it back. He threw a right and left jab toward his opponent, but Nakamora dodged and hit Stark’s with a thrust kick to drop him once again. RILEY STEVENS: “Koji has what I’d call educated feet.” Starks pulled himself together once again and charged his opponent. Using a pass, Koji guided Starks to the ropes and then dropped to his stomach as his opponent continued to run to the opposite end of the ring. Upon his return, Starks met a dreaded maneuver that had single handedly ended the match. Nakamora had reared his arm and fist, waited for Nick Starks to be in striking distance, and then as soon as he had been, Nakamora unleashed the dreaded ‘Nakamora Death Drop’ a Vertebreaker. Koji dropped Starks to the canvas and quickly covered him for the pin. A simple one-two-three victory and Koji had swept the arena by surprise. JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…Koji Nakamora!” This was a quick one and it had seemed that Koji could be back on track but we’d said that many times before. RILEY STEVENS: “Koji has been off and on like this his entire career, can he now start to string some wins together?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “He’s Asian I doubt he sees things that far ahead. Haha.” Oliver has a good laugh at his own joke as Riley can be heard sighing as Koji doesn’t hesitate as he heads to the back not really celebrating after the quick win. WINNER: KOJI NAKAMORA Back to Blake’s office we go where he is standing by with Aspen Chaud and Tony Miranda having a conversation. BLAKE BECKETT: “You know I haven’t yet decided the next contender yet. But just know that you’ll both be considered for it…” Aspen and Tony thank him but just as they do in walks Acquin which immediately seems to make Blake angry. BLAKE BECKETT: “What do you think you were doing earlier?” Joshua holds out his hands trying to stop him as Aspen and Tony talk to each other behind them. JOSHUA ACQUIN: “Listen Curtis has not been a great champion and my brother will bring back prestige to that belt. But lets save this for another time the new guy and the returning one are here…” Joshua points to the door as Blake can be seen holding back his frustration with the man as in comes Johnny Violence and Kevin Mero already arguing with each other. JOHNNY VIOLENCE: “Listen Metro, I am going to be the next champion around here not you!” KEVIN MERO: “JohnnyBOY you will be nothing more then a stepping stone.” Blake listens to the two surprised as Aspen and Tony the veterans share a glance at the two’s confidence. BLAKE BECKETT: “Guys, guys calm down. I like the confidence but Kevin your first stint wasn’t great and Johnny your only brand new. Regardless if the two of you think you can beat the best we have…Well next week you two can team up to face two of our longest tenured Main Eventers…Aspen Chaud and Tony Miranda.” Blake points back at them as Aspen and Tony nod walking together between the two as Johnny and Kevin turn to each other angrily glaring at each other before heading out as Joshua sneaks out during all this as the scene fades. QUOTE The third match is ready and set as already in the ring almost fitting for the upcoming Halloween show is Doombringer standing there ready to go looking intimidating as usual. "Bullseye" by Aly and AJ hits and out from the back comes Aspen Chaud with a smile on her face, not something you’d expect from someone about to face Doombringer. RILEY STEVENS: “You ever heard of the term being fed to the fishes Oliver?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Shut up.” Aspen high fives the fans and then heads up the steps stepping over the bottom rope and into the ring where she blows a kiss to the camera then turns into Doombringer staring up at him as the referee calls for the bell. DING DING DING! Aspen looks up at him he’s a full foot and a half or so taller then her and the weight advantage is not even close. The fact he isn’t exactly friendly and does not even pretend to be only made this match up even weirder. The fans were cheering for her but really it was only in that hopeful underdog kind of way. Doombringer went to strike first but Aspen ducked under it and threw a punch of her own…It didn’t even nearly phase Doombringer. He threw another big punch that yet again she ducked under this time throwing her patented ‘Queen's Wrath II’ a Heel Kick to the back of his head. It doesn’t seem to be more then a little bit of a stunner for Doombringer as she rushes off the ropes and goes for the Thesz Press but instead ends up getting lifted with ease onto Doombringer’s shoulders in a Powerbomb position. She began throwing punch after punch before spinning around so that she was facing the other way and then amazingly she turned it into a Victory Roll right into the Pin…1..2…3! JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…Aspen Chaud!!” The crowd goes wild as right as the three is counted Doombringer essentially throws Aspen half way across the ring. Immediately she slides out of the ring with a huge smile on her face. RILEY STEVENS: “Aspen pulled the quick upset!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “That was a fast count…” Aspen falls into the fans who embrace her patting her on the back as Doombringer grabs the referee by his throat screaming at him as he lets go of him seeming to maybe be showing mercy but quickly Doombringer pulls him in and lifts him up hitting the ‘Tainted Soul’ a Elevated Powerbomb that nearly breaks his back. RILEY STEVENS: “That was uncalled for…Frankly I could see Blake Beckett fining him or maybe even suspending him for actions like that.” Doombringer heads out of the ring as Aspen slides back in to check on the referee as we head to break while he gets help. WINNER: ASPEN CHAUD Another backstage segment comes and this time we head to Aspen’s locker room where she is celebrating her victory. When the door opens and in walks Alex Castellanos. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “Surprise!” Aspen gets up excited as she walks over hugging him excited to see her good friend back. ASPEN CHAUD: “Oh my god! Wasn’t expecting to see you back so soon.” Aspen releases the hug and steps back as Alex nods his head. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “Yup I am already cleared to wrestle…I asked for Ace next week.” Aspen’s expression changes as she now seems worried. ASPEN CHAUD: “The Doctor said it was very severe and I mean Ace is actually pretty good…” Chaud does not seem happy at all with the news as Alex smiles having expected it. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “Listen the Doctor said I’d be fine. And you know after the way he hurt you I wouldn’t mind shutting his cocky mouth up. Now stop playing mother for just a second so that we can go have a fun night and celebrate my return and your win.” Aspen seems a bit hesitant but eventually breaks nodding her head and heading off with him as we take a break. ==== For the last time we go backstage where Alioth Starre has just changed into some normal clothes and is preparing to go out to the ring for the main event as a commentator but before he can go AJ Fairchild walks up stepping in front of him. AJ FAIRCHILD: "You know I heard what you said in my match about me not being worthy of fighting you or whatever. And since tonight’s match wasn’t exactly conclusive lets do it again next week…One on one." Fairchild extends his hand as Alioth looks down at it adjusting his title on his shoulders before grinning and then nodding his head in agreement not yet shaking his hand as he heads on his way before the break. QUOTE And it is time for the Main Event of the evening. And just as we come back on air we see Alioth Starre joining Riley and Oliver at the announce table. RILEY STEVENS: “We are being joined at the table tonight by Alioth Starre the Undisputed Champion.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Oh joy.” ALIOTH STARRE: “Barely have any room sitting next to Oliver.” Riley has a good laugh at that one as Alioth got a zinger in on him as the match now starts up as "World Warrior" by RedRapper hits and the fans let out a loud cheer for the man they know as the New World Warrior as Tony Miranda walks down towards the ring with a very ready demeanor heading up the steps and into the ring where he takes his place in the corner. RILEY STEVENS: “You’ve faced Tony Miranda before in fact you beat him for that belt, what do you think his chances are in this one?” ALIOTH STARRE: “Always a tough competitor…never able to get to the top…I’d say mediocre.” As usual Alioth hardly ever showed much in the way of respect as Tony entered the ring heading to his corner stretching out his legs which he was known to use quite often as his music cuts off and Ace Baldwin’s Matador made theme hits and the boo birds immediately begin to fly as here comes Ace Baldwin the Number One Contender. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “There is our next GIW Undisputed Champion!” ALIOTH STARRE: “And there is Oliver the man that’ll soon fit into his old high school jeans…Oh sorry thought we were sharing unreachable goals.” Riley yet again laughs as Ace enters the ring walking over to the ropes leaning over them and pointing at Alioth then motioning the belt around his waist as the referee starts the match. DING DING DING! And here we go the match is underway and that always special feel is in the air as the two men start to circle each other. But Ace stops looking over at Alioth and screaming out “Watch this!” going in for a lock up right after and almost immediately Tony throws him back to the mat with ease feeling disrespected by the way he is seemingly looking past him. The fans here are in the corner of Tony in full force as he looks down at Ace then points to himself advising him not to look past him. Ace gets up angrily jumping up and going in for a lock up. This time it’s much more competitive and Ace being the slightly bigger man starts to get a bit of an advantage pushing Tony backwards but Miranda smartly breaks the hold and pushes him away going for a very early Spinning Roundhouse Kick that misses. Baldwin quickly rolls him up trying to get a quick victory 1…Not close as both men hop up back into the lock up. This time Ace pushes him up against the ropes and propels him off of them. Tony bounces off the ropes and on the rebound ducks Ace’s attempt at a Clothesline. Bouncing off now under his own momentum he swings at Ace now but is unsuccessful as Ace ducks it and almost immediately as Tony tries to stop himself he gets Chop Block’d from behind sending the fans into a frenzy as Tony holds his leg in pain. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “What a thinker.” Ace gets up taking a second to smirk and soak in the early victory as he stomps the knee that he might have just hyper extended dishing out more pain early for Tony. The fans seem to already get worried as Tony gets up to his feet holding the ropes and with a limp. Baldwin taunts him by pretending to have a limp of his own. And Miranda makes him pay with a big Spinning Roundhouse Kick with his still working leg and he makes sure to land back on it to take away the pain. The fans go wild as he hit the move out of no where as he crawls to the ground to make a pin 1…Kick Out! While it clearly stunned Ace it wasn’t enough to put him down for good as Tony gets up still nursing that knee as he uses all of his strength to lift Ace up to his feet. Putting most his weight down on the other leg he quickly delivers a Snap Suplex as he goes for another cover 1…Kick Out! Tony knows that his knee will not favor him in a long match so he knows he needs to get this one over quickly. RILEY STEVENS: “Thus far he is doing ok considering he tweaked his knee.” Tony gets up observing Ace as he gets up to his feet, trying to decide what his options are at this point as Baldwin gets up he seems to have decided as he hoists him right away going for the Tiger Suplex. But Ace is able to shake free from it and he immediately kicks his knee. The fans boo again as Tony falls to the mat in pain as Ace rolls out of the ring pulling him towards the ring post grabbing his hurt knee and slamming it into the ring post. Miranda quickly rolls back in pain as Ace turns walking over to Alioth at the announce table where he looks down at the title in front of him. Baldwin points at it and then himself telling Alioth it’s his as Starre pulls off his headset getting up as things seem ready to pop off. In the ring the fans see Tony getting to his feet by the ropes holding it. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Watch out Ace!” Oliver’s scream for his guy ends up alerting Ace as he spins around but it is too late as Tony is already airborne and he comes down right on top of him. The fans go wild with cheers as Tony holds his knee and the referee restarts the count back at one. Alioth returns back to the announce table as Tony is the first one back up lifting up the clearly out of breathe Ace as he throws him back into the ring as he rolls across the ring trying to give himself some time to recuperate. ALIOTH STARRE: “Focus that’s what he lacks.” While Alioth critiques his contender Tony sizes him up in the ring ready to deliver the move that will put him away as he runs at him and successfully hits the ‘Third Strike’ a Running Leg Lariat! The fans go wild but the air he gets on the move costs him as he ends up falling down awkwardly on that knee. He takes a second or two to hold it as he crawls over to get on time 1…2…Kick Out! The time he took to nurse his knee costs him as he gets up and pulls Ace with him. Wasting no time he lifts him up onto his shoulders and goes for the ‘Second Impact’ a Running Lygerbomb but his knee gives out forcing him to drop Ace. Baldwin wastes no time at all as he lifts him up onto his shoulders and hits the ‘T.F.C’ (Thanks For Coming) a Standing Death Valley Driver with the Cradle 1…2…3! JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…“Amazing” Ace Baldwin!” The fans begin to boo as Tony was within seconds of picking up the win as Ace falls down holding his head from the big Lariat he just took but wasting little time forcing the referee to raise his hands. Alioth gets up again off the broadcast table as he holds up his title. RILEY STEVENS: “These two men’s collision course continues…and we’ll see it end at ‘On Hallowed Ground II’ but for now it remains a top that between the two on a weekly basis.” Ace looks out at him shaking his head and saying the belt is his as the show slowly fades here in Puerto Rico. WINNER: ACE BALDWIN
