GIW — Card

Revolt, 15 March 2012

15 March 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: THURSDAY NIGHT REVOLT LIVE! From Hirsch Memorial Coliseum in Shreveport, LA – March 15, 2012 BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! As soon as the show starts these fans get no exciting kick off instead they get the sound of "Did It On 'Em" by Nicki Minaj sending them into the usual arena shaking boos that come with an Aspen Chaud entrance especially this case as she comes out with crutches and a big boot on her ankle slowly crutching her way down towards the ring, even with all of this she still has her GIW Undisputed Championship. RILEY STEVENS: “How can anyone fall for this?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “She’s hurt Riley stop being an asshole.” Heading to the ring she slides in her crutches then does her best to roll into the ring using the ropes to get back up and slowly head back towards the center of the ring, it would have been easier not to bring her belt but that was not Aspen’s style the fans were booing her even worse then usual not a single person in the place not joining in as she grabs the mic and prepares to talk. ASPEN CHAUD: “I know, I know I am as upset as all of you. I understand why many of you would be mad you are being deprived of seeing yours truly wrestle here tonight but unfortunately I suffered a very serious ankle injury that will keep me out…At least until…April second…” This only makes the fans boo louder as Aspen has just described the day after ‘Exile’ as she looks down at the ground in despair before continuing. ASPEN CHAUD: “I was looking forward to once and for all proving that I am better then Alioth Starre but I sadly have to be the one that has to say that the match at ‘Exile’ is not longer happening. The TLC match will be your new headliner…” Aspen only has a bit more time to look down as though she were sad when “Grand Central” by Edward Shearmur hits and the place erupts with cheers as Alioth Starre walks out from the back stopping at the top of the ramp as Aspen turns towards him the best she can with her crutches as he addresses her. ALIOTH STARRE: “Do you really think these fans and I are that stupid Aspen? This acting job is almost as bad as the one you do when you walk out here every week as the “champion” of this company.” This draws some “Ooo’s” and even more cheers from the crowd who laugh at Aspen’s expense as she fires back. ASPEN CHAUD: “I HAVE A HIGH ANKLE SPRAIN!!!” The screeching of her voice would even make deaf people cringe as Alioth grins and then replies. ALIOTH STARRE: “When that ankle heals on April second and you have to wrestle me, I am going to take that Undisputed belt away from you and return it to the champion that made it what it is today.” Alioth goes to turn thinking that it is the end but Aspen snaps back quickly at him before he can go. ASPEN CHAUD: “No, no, no! You will have a match at ‘Exile’ Alioth because let’s face it you have a lot of enemies around here so I don’t know if you’ll even get a shot at that belt because there’s a man ready and willing to take it away from you at ‘Exile’. He may not like me but he hates you even more…” Alioth turns around intrigued by this but it turns out to be bad timing as Matt Kluge crushes him from behind clubbing him to the ground. The fans boo as Kluge hoists him up enjoying a huge size advantage as he spins around tossing him into the stage areas backing. RILEY STEVENS: “Holy cow! Aspen is out and now we get Alioth Starre versus Matt Kluge at ‘Exile’!” Matt stands over him having dazed Alioth as he screams at him “Stay down, bitch.” Kluge gives him one last kick to the stomach as he turns to the crowd as we head to a commercial on this surprising turn of events. QUOTE After a short commercial break before the opening sequence we get a short replay of Kluge’s blindside attack then head to the ring where we have Faith Simpson standing in the ring with Ryan Cavallari across from her. RILEY STEVENS: “This match was scheduled for Evolve but with Faith Simpson’s inclusion and the talented Ryan Cavallari, Sinclair gave them a shot here on Revolt.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “This little girl is back?” The crowd is hot for the returning Faith Simpson as the former Legacy Champion takes in the noise from the Galveston Island Wrestling crowd who are happy to see the former champ back in action tonight. Ryan Cavallari is hurtled in his respected corner, simply waiting for the bell to ring. Ryan is new and looking to make an impact tonight and he has the perfect chance, facing off against one of GIW’s best of 2011. DING DING DING! Finally, the bell sounds and Faith and Ryan circle each other at a brisk pace, quickly shaking hands to show respect for each other. They quickly lock up and Ryan has Faith in a headlock, quickly tossing her to the ground with a hip toss, making sure to hold the lock in place as he does. The referee asks Faith if she wanted to quit but the teenage prodigy with a large heart refuses to give up. Instead, she manages to counter the move by mustering up some strength and getting back to her feet. She elbows Ryan in the chest and delivers a Headscissors takedown, Ryan quickly is back to his feet though but Faith is back on the attack, connecting with a sunset flip Powerbomb into a pin, Ryan is able to kick out at two. RILEY STEVENS: “Faith is still as fun to watch as always.” Both competitors are back up and Faith Simpson attempts a running clothesline but Ryan Cavallari is able to catch Faith off guard with a European uppercut that rocks her jaw. The crowd could even hear it as they react accordingly. Ryan continues to show his athletic style as he once again clocks Faith, this time with a beautiful looking pele’ kick that sends Faith tumbling into the ropes. She bounces back and Ryan finishes the little combo off with a snap Hurricanrana. He quickly hooks the leg and covers, but this time Faith is able to kick out at two. Ryan plays smart and doesn’t waste any time, quickly applying a one leg Boston crab that sends Faith into a world of pain as she is in the middle of the ring with nowhere to go. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Tap little girl!” Faith screams in pain as the crowd wills her on. The referee asks if she wants to quit but Faith shakes her head furiously and lets out a loud “NO!” which causes Ryan to lock the hold in even deeper. Faith uses her arm to push up and crawl ever so slowly to the ropes. Finally, she reaches out and is in arms length. Her fingers grace the bottom rope before she is able to gather the strength to make the final push and grab the rope. The ref makes Ryan break the hold, which he does after about a count of three. Ryan runs his hands through is hair and places his hands on his hips as catches some breaths and refocuses. Ryan grabs a hold of the legs of Faith, attempting to drag her back into the center of the ring but Faith uses her legs to send Ryan crashing backwards. The crowd is on their feet as Faith is back up and on fire. She hits a nice running cross body, which she follows up with an arm drag. Ryan hops right back up but runs right into a snap suplex. Faith Simpson covers Ryan, ONE – TWO – Only two and a half as Ryan gets a foot on the bottom rope. RILEY STEVENS: “Simpson looking impressive here tonight.” Faith can’t believe it, she was sure she had it in the back. Faith quickly rolls under the bottom rope and waits for Ryan to stagger back to his feet. The fans chant Faith’s name loudly throughout the arena. Ryan is back up and turns around, Faith plunges from the ropes with a springboard dropkick. Ryan steps out of the way and Faith crashes and burns. Faith is quickly back up but from out of nowhere, Ryan connects with the You Give Love A Bad Name, otherwise known as a Running Flying Knee Smash. Faith looks out cold as Ryan hooks the leg. One - Two – Two and a half as Faith gets a shoulder up. RILEY STEVENS: “She kicked out of that? HOW?” Ryan looks a bit flustered, really thinking that was it. The crowd was even sure of it, counting along with the referee but Faith in her comeback match was able to get the shoulder up. Ryan picks Faith up by her hair and from out of nowhere, this time Faith hits her finisher, the Twist of Faith. Faith is able to drop an arm over Ryan..ONE…TWO…NO! Ryan kicks out and the crowd erupts. These two young superstars are laying it out for everyone to see tonight and the crowd is loving it all. Faith uses the ropes to help herself up. Ryan and Faith quickly exchange a few rights and lefts in the ring which, Faith being a bit quicker is able to secure the upper hand. Faith springs off the ropes with another cross body, this time Ryan is able to reverse it and roll through, rolling Faith into a pin. One – two – three, Ryan gets the pinfall victory. JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…Ryan Cavallari!” The fans seem a bit upset about Faith losing as Ryan hops up to his feet as Faith kicks out just a half a second to late. The referee raises his arms in victory but he pulls away to help Faith up, who is a little upset with the loss but then gives him a respecting nod. RILEY STEVENS: “Talk about earning your spot, these two were supposed to be on Evolve and they put on this amazing show here tonight.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Glad she lost.” Ryan raises her arms in victory the two sharing some mutual respect after a great contest that truly got Revolt kicked off the right way here tonight as we go from them to the back. WINNERS: RYAN CAVALLARI After the first match of the night we go backstage where Aspen Chaud is seen walking along with her crutches trying to get around in them when Beck Ramsey walks over leaning against the wall. BECK RAMSEY: “If it isn’t Ms. Broken Ankle over here. You know I watched that video a few times and I couldn’t quite figure out how you hurt it, I mean…Looked more like you just…Fell. But I get the Bailey match…The Starre match…This was sure a bad time for you to get injured right?” Aspen glares back at him not even bothering to reply as he gets closer smiling. BECK RAMSEY: “Just remember you still owe me a match…” Beck smiles and walks by her heading down the hall as Alexis Terry now heads her way as Aspen sees her and now talks to her. ASPEN CHAUD: “Alexis stop, we need to talk.” Alexis stops staring back at her angrily as Aspen gets her to calm down and then starts to talk. ASPEN CHAUD: “I know you think I am trying to screw you over with Bailey but can’t you see what is going on? I am messing with him. Sure we fooled around, but just think what’s he more worried about right now? Your match or his marriage, I mean I have him wrapped around my finger.” Alexis takes a second to process it as Aspen nods her head, trying to get her to get it. ALEXIS TERRY: “Well, I mean that’s probably true but didn’t you hear he got back with Alexandra?” This seems to catch Aspen off guard as she looks back at her getting a little angry. ASPEN CHAUD: “I’ll handle that later but trust me I have a plan and thought we needed to talk about tonight…” Alexis seems now to be starting to believe her again but seems to have something on her mind. ALEXIS TERRY: “Did…did you get the pictures and stuff?” Aspen laughs as the two start to head off, with Aspen using her crutches as she replies. ASPEN CHAUD: “Yeah, I know you were just mad. But whoever edited in Tony’s face was really talented…” Clueless Aspen heads on with Alexis, back on the same page yet again as they head on to set up a devious plan. QUOTE After that little break we come back to a ring full of wrestlers which was originally supposed to be our first match here tonight as Angel Vegnaduzzo, Big J, Eliza Gray, Michael Lobo, and Mya Denton are in the ring ready to fight for a shot at the GIW Television Championship. RILEY STEVENS: “These five stars are all relatively new here in GIW and they are fighting for a shot at the Television Championship.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “What an odd group we have in that ring right now…” All five are set up and ready to go as the referee steps up in between them all as they all stand in different parts of the ring as the referee calls for the bell. DING DING DING! Right off the bat this thing gets started off with a fast pace as Angel Vegnaduzzo and Eliza Gray together agree that they should go after Mya Denton as they head to the corner, stalking her back into it as Mya thinks of what to do. On the other side of the ring Big J and Michael Lobo head to the middle of the ring throwing bombs at one another, and despite his size advantage Big J is losing the fist fight as Lobo sends him backwards with every punch. Mya throws a punch at Eliza to get free but Angel shoves her back into the ropes allowing Eliza to get on her while he sits back keeping himself nice and loose while the others all work themselves. Eliza slaps away at Mya until finally she grabs her spinning and tossing her through the ropes to the outside, as she does this however Angel shows his true colors grabbing her by the tights and then doing the same launching her over the top rope to the outside as well. RILEY STEVENS: “Angel playing it smart early on.” Angel slides out of the ring but as he does this Lobo clotheslines Big J over the top rope, where he falls out right next to them. Vegnaduzzo dodges it and then looks down at the three bodies on the floor, while he does this Lobo dropkicks through the ropes sending him to one knee on the outside. Just like that he looks around and then heads up to the top rope where he gets prepped up, looking down as Eliza, Mya, J, and Angel get to their feet. The fans get onto their feet as he jumps off flying high as he heads down on top of them taking them all out expect little old Angel who shoved his way out of the pile and got away. Lobo gets up admittedly a little excited about what he just did but as he gets up to celebrate Angel returns to the picture grabbing him and tossing him into the barricade around the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Lobo goes off the top, but Angel gets him from behind.” The referee tries to get control as Angel slides back into the ring with all his competitors on the outside looking in on him as he smirks to the crowd, he was enjoying putting on a show like this. But Mya was a scrapper and she was the first one back into the ring ready to fight but Angel just simply toys with her slapping her across the face which gives him the boos he was looking for as he follows it up with a Suplex that puts her down, he spins around to cover her 1…2-Eliza Gray jumps back into the ring to save the match up as she kicks him off. Vegnaduzzo gets up ducking one of her punches then delivering a Dropkick to the Knee. On the outside Big J and Lobo are back up and again fighting this time he jumps up onto the Ring Step then Dropkick’s Big J bringing out some impressed looks from the crowd. RILEY STEVENS: “Lobo has looked impressive that’s for sure.” Back in the ring Angel is now dealing with two girls both really beaten already but trying to just maintain the match. He grabs Mya as she gets up and whips her into the corner then hoisting up Gray and tossing her into the same corner. He runs across the ring hitting a Clothesline on both women. Back on the outside Michael waits until Big J gets up before nailing him with a Superkick that puts him down. Back in the ring Angel goes for a pin on Eliza 1…2…Kick Out. He rolls over onto Mya 1…2…Kick Out. Both are barely surviving as Angel gets up not seeming too worried but Michael re-enters the ring ready to get the win. RILEY STEVENS: “Lobo and Vegnaduzzo are the two stand outs.” Angel is ready to go after them some more but Michael flips him around doubling him over with a knee then following it up with a Enzuigiri that gets him the advantage in this one. Getting up with everyone down he turns his attention to Eliza who gets up as he grabs her up going for the ‘Lobo-timizer’ his version of the Celtic Cross which lands big time 1…2…Big J grabs his foot and pulls him out of the ring. Eliza then does the same, falling to the outside as Angel gets up looking to the outside where Lobo is distracted by Big J as Mya gets up as he floors her with a European Uppercut. RILEY STEVENS: “Angel looking to finish this one.” Big J was getting no offense in but he was sure distracting Lobo well as Angel sets up and then locks in the ‘Sharpener’ a Texas Cloverleaf easily on Mya who tries her best to fight it as Lobo floors Big J with a Flying Roundhouse but as he looks back into the ring Denton taps out. JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…Angel Vegnaduzzo!” These fans are not happy as Angel gets to his feet with a smirk on his face as Lobo angrily glares up at him as the second week in a row he came this close to a win, this time getting distracted long enough for Angel to win the match. RILEY STEVENS: “And it is Angel Vegnaduzzo that gets the win and stamps his shot at the GIW Television Champion, Henry Malkin.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Got to like this kid.” Angel smirks while looking at Lobo almost seeming to know that he did a lot of work in this one only for Mya’s tap to be the reason he lost but at the end of the Vegnaduzzo as the new Number one contender. WINNER: ANGEL VEGNADUZZO Backstage we find Alexis Terry and Eliza Gray talking to two big Security Guards about something but as they do Cassidy Hightower walks up to them smiling as she gets their attention. CASSIDY HIGHTOWER: "Oh hey there Alexis…Alexis Jr. I just wanted to find you and let you know that everything you have done to me these past few weeks is going to be returned here tonight in that match." Gray rolls her eyes as Alexis looks at her with a grin on her face and her Legacy belt over her shoulder. ALEXIS TERRY: “I am going to eat you up just like I did your mom…And by the time I am done with you, you won’t be in that match at ‘Exile’…” Alexis says this smugly as Cassidy chuckles at her joke then responds. CASSIDY HIGHTOWER: "We’ll see about that…" Cassidy smiles and heads on her way as Eliza and Alexis look at one another seeming confident as she goes along. QUOTE Skylar Grey played into the arena. Hayden Carter made her entry to the stage to a mixed reaction. When against a heel like Scar, as she was last week, the crowd preferred her, but her opponent was a popular individual and a proven entity. She was the dark horse competitor in this one. Many individuals only cheered her if they detested her opponent. Either way, Hayden Carter moved down the aisle and slipped herself into the ring. She prepped and shadow boxed, waiting for Alioth Starre to make his entrance. This was going to be Hayden Carter’s first real test. RILEY STEVENS: “This girl has been calling out Alioth Starre for weeks now yet she has yet to win a match here in GIW, you got to think this is a bad match for her to end that losing streak.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Some might just call this a cow being lead to slaughter.” The lights dimmed and the cosmos spilled over the seams of the arena from a projector as Alioth’s theme played and the crowd stood. He appeared onto the stage from the back and headed for the ring quickly. He slid in and then moved toward the ropes, a foot on the lower and one on the mid. RILEY STEVENS: “Alioth Starre was attacked earlier tonight by Matt Kluge but seems ready to go here tonight versus Hayden Carter.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Well duh, this should be a quickie.” He hung there for a moment and then looked over toward his opponent with a piercing glance. He had been milking his entrance, considering he figured it would be a squash match against Hayden Carter. He finally pulled off and readied for the bout. DING DING DING! Alioth scratched at his chin briefly as he tried to size up his opponent. Hayden pulled her fists out, ready for a fight and then took a quick jab at her opponent. Alioth dodged it and snickered. He looked to the crowd and then into the eyes of his female opponent and smacked her square across the right cheek. She didn’t grab at her reddened flesh, instead she jabbed him again in the ribs. This strike connected, and so did the next one. She continued the flurry of blows before Alioth was forced to throw her to the canvas and stomp upon her to end her momentum. He leapt upward into the air and came down on her shoulder with a knee drop. Her body jolted from the impact. Alioth pinned Hayden, but the count only came to one. She was going to be persistent in her desire to defeat him, it seemed, or she just hadn’t learned her lesson yet. Alioth pulled her up from the canvas, as well as himself, and hoisted her up for a shin breaker. She toppled back down to the canvas with her leg in hand and Alioth pinned a second time. One! RILEY STEVENS: “Good opening sequence by these two.” He pulled off of her and seemed puzzled. She wasn’t. She knocked him back with a right hook and turned over to pull herself up with aid of the nearby ropes. She then jabbed him again with the flat of her boot and then performed a dropkick to knock Alioth Starre into the canvas like a ton of bricks. Instead of going for the pin, like he had, Hayden Carter applied the Cross–face Chicken–wing to her opponent, knowing he was a master of submission holds as well. She locked it in tight and he began to squirm with pain and discomfort. RILEY STEVENS: “Wow Carter has Alioth in a predicament.” It appeared as though Hayden Carter’s hold was getting to Alioth Starre. He began to weaken. He showed the signs of wear and tear and she held tight. He refused to surrender and tap, but the referee still insisted in pestering him about whether he would or wouldn’t every two seconds. That was the worst part of being in a submission hold: The referee. He held on tightly to his sense of whimsy as well as she did to him. Neither were going to give the other a chance as long as the struggle of wills was on. Size was in Alioth’s favor. Being 5’6”, it was harder for her to grasp onto him, being 5’10”, but not by much. Only enough for him to eventually find light at the end of the tunnel and rip her hands from him. When the hold was finally broken, Alioth pulled away and then Hayden too. She followed up on the assault, knowing the threat he posed if he got back on his feet. She tackled Alioth and dragged him into yet another submission hold, but this one was the Boston crab. Alioth quickly broke out of it, under the ropes. RILEY STEVENS: “Got to give her props for trying.” He pulled his way up on the rim of the ring and she did as well. They were on their feet around the same time and just in time for them to start jabbing and knocking at one another. A game of janga to pull what punches they could before someone fell, toppling to the canvas or matted floor. It ended with Hayden wrapping an arm over Alioth’s neck to pull for a suplex into the ring, but Alioth refused. He ducked a foot under the rope and made the same arm gesture as she, for a DDT outside of the ring. She refused! She countered his advances with her own, but neither would give way. One would have to bull rush the other, or pull some kind of smug, dirty trick to get the upper hand. Who would sink to the lowest step? The star, or New York’s favorite brawler? Which ever was the first would become the villain of this piece. Hayden wriggled out of Alioth’s grasp as he from hers and sent him a nice message with a knife-edge chop. He imitated her with his own across her chest. She staggered, but didn’t back down. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Is this real life?” Alioth suddenly gave in. He looked down and then back into the eyes of his opponent and sent a wad of vitriolic spit upon her face. The shock was enough for him to gain the upper hand. He knocked her back with a heart punch, and then ran to the top turn post to the left of him. There he ascended and waited for her to wipe the stain of disrespect from her face. She looked to him and he leapt! Alioth crashed down on top of Hayden with a cross body and struck her to the canvas with a pin. Still, however, she refused to give up! Two count! He was flabbergasted and his anger flared. He threw her shoulders back down onto the canvas and forced a second pin, but the results were even worse for him. In what could only be described as a dick move, she gouged Alioth’s eyes and caved in to a disrespectful gesture as he had. She didn’t care though; she was a tough bitch and admitted to it. She gathered herself up and gave Alioth, whilst on his knees, a sharp kick to the skull to knock him off his high horse. RILEY STEVENS: “This is a battle here tonight, even if she loses she proved something to me.” It seemed apparent that it would be difficult for her to perform a piledriver on him, so she improvised. She set Alioth back up on his knees. Dazed and out of it at sorts, Alioth looked up at her with mouth agape and eyes glazed over. Hayden cracked her neck, knuckles, then snapped an arm back and threw a hard right hook into Alioth’s face that knocked him dead flat on the canvas. She kicked him onto his back and then pinned the former Undisputed Champion for a full count of three. She was finally victorious, and against Alioth Starre. JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…HAYDEN CARTER!!!” She smudged his face into the canvas when she pulled off. A grin plastered wide across her face, she spit upon his fallen body and raised her arms to the bewildered crowd. She had just taken down a former champion, and in doing so, ended her losing streak within Galveston Island Wrestling. Hayden Carter was the victor and proud of it. A stone cold bitch and the one to leave Alioth in the ring, a ditched mess. RILEY STEVENS: “Are you kidding me, Hayden Carter wins!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “…” The crowd in the building seems just as shocked as everyone as Alioth lays on the mat in pain as Hayden raises her arms high into the air having just beaten Alioth though everyone in this place is pinching themselves to see if it really happened as we head to the back. WINNERS: HAYDEN CARTER We shoot to the backstage where the GIW backstage correspondent Zachary Pagonis is standing by with a microphone in hand. Over to his side was the GIW television champion Henry Malkin. Henry was in his ring gear with his title slung over his shoulder. ZACHARY PAGONIS: "GIW I am here with your Television Champion Henry Malkin. So Henry, you are set to go head to head against the undefeated Nathan Bailey in just moments, we saw last week you and Bailey had some words, and when Aspen Chaud succumbed to her injury last week, you stepped up to take the match against Bailey. Tell us, what's the animosity between you and Nathan." Pagonis holds the mic over to Henry, one of the few men he didn’t hate interviewing here in GIW. HENRY MALKIN: "Well Zachary there is no real animosity between Nathan and I, this is a competitive industry, and banter between guys in the back is a common thing, now what I don't like is Bailey's sense of entitlement. He may think that he's running the show, but tonight he has a wake up call with the GIW Television champion.” He says that proudly with the belt over his shoulder to prove it as Pagonis takes back the belt. ZACHARY PAGONIS: "Yes and speaking about your Television title, you've probably been watching earlier, and you now know that the number one contender to your title, and your opponent for ‘Exile’ is none other than Angel Vegnaduzzo. Henry what are your thoughts on the Television title division and on your opponent for ‘Exile’." Zachary holds the mic over to him again as Malkin quickly responds. HENRY MALKIN: "I feel that the division is really the diamond in the rough in GIW. You see young hungry talents ready to make an impact, whereas people see the Television division as paying one's dues. I beg to differ, and you're going to find out what the champion of said division is capable of later tonight. As for my opponent. I just want to give Angel a bit of good luck for ‘Exile’; I think we can steal the show." It was not likely his opponent would be as respectful as he heads off with Pagonis finishing it up. ZACHARY PAGONIS: "Thank you for your time Henry and good luck tonight..." With that our champion heads off to get ready for his match here tonight. QUOTE Paul Defias and Daniel Starr both appeared on the stage as the unified tag team that they were. They were jeered and loved it. Both of them made their way to the ring, Paul behind, but Starr not too far ahead. Paul climbed the steel steps as Starr slid in. Both seemed eager for a match, but Paul Defias wasn’t too certain if they were both ready for these two particular opponents. RILEY STEVENS: “DefiaStarr made their debut last week and sputtered a bit but now they have a chance to redeem themselves.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “They were robbed last week, these two are great.” However, they did realize that their opponents had bad blood etched into their history. DefiaStarr then waited impatiently for their opponents to enter. Paul took to the ropes immediately. Tony Miranda was first out for his team. He came out with Hayden Carter behind him. She looked battered and exhausted after having had her match against Alioth Starre moments ago, but she was still ready to be at her partner’s side for support. The two moved down to the ring and as Tony entered, Hayden remained outside of the ring at the request of the referee and the opposing team. Paul glared at her with dagger eyes, but Daniel didn’t pay too much attention. Tony entered the ring and looked to the stage, as they all did, for his tag partner. RILEY STEVENS: “How in the hell are these two going to team with one another here tonight?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I have no idea…” The lights blackened and then faded up to a red hue as ‘Scarred’ played over the audio system. Scar stepped past the curtain, adjusting his black gloves and then moved down the aisle with a quicker step than usual. He wanted to get into this. He removed the long black towel from his masked face and threw it down on the end of the ramp way. He then pulled himself up to the rim of the ring and inside to join the other men and get the match under way. With Scar in, the bell then rang. DING DING DING! Scar didn’t wait for he and Tony to decide who would fight first. Tony took a step forth, but Scar was already moving like a tank in the desert. He startled Daniel with a throat thrust and then the match was under way. Tony backed off toward the ropes. Scar pulled Daniel toward the center of the ring and threw up another throat thrust to keep him staggered and under control. Scar then took several big, swift steps back toward the ropes to use it as momentum going forward into a big boot to the upper jaw of his opponent. Daniel Starr fell back in a loud thud, only for Scar to peel him off like a sticker. He hunkered Daniel close in grasp and performed a hard knee lift, then backed away again. He was going for a final big boot to plant his opponent, but this time Daniel ducked under. He retaliated with a scoop slam and then applied a belly–to–belly suplex to his opponent after Scar rose quickly up to his feet. Daniel yelled to the crowd, pumped up from the match already and then turned back around to find Scar standing tall, looming over him. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Scar is a creepy dude.” Scar grasped his opponent by the throat, but then so did Daniel Starr. Starr kicked Scar’s gut and finished the chokeslam and then pulled away to take himself out of the match, tagging in Paul Defias. Paul hurried in, but unsure if he wanted to go at Scar so quickly, and so brash. He did anyway, as he knew if Scar rose up, he’d have less of a chance to follow up on Starr’s advances. He struck his opponent with a stomp, then performed a rolling thunder maneuver, topped off with a pin. The pin failed, but he felt energized. Scar pushed Paul off of him, dropping his opponent like a rag doll. He then turned toward Tony, not tagging him in, but instead pushing him lightly over the shoulder. Tony didn’t have time to react, but he did give Scar a smug glance that he had something up his own sleeves for his partner. Hayden glared up at the masked man, her arms folded, but ready to ball into fists if needed. Tony met Paul dead center in the ring and the two locked up. Just as Paul motioned to perform a jumping knee to Tony’s face, Tony took down his target with a leg sweep. RILEY STEVENS: “Crafty move there by Tony.” He then dropped down and applied a sleeper hold, but Paul quickly writhed out of it. The two locked eyes and slowly rose to their feet. Tony smirked and held out his hand. An odd gesture to a jeered man like Paul, but Defias slowly rose up his own hand, and extended it cautiously. Tony smacked Paul’s hand out of the away and laughed at him, pointing to embarrass him as if he actually fell for the oldest trick in the wrestling books. His face then dropped into a serious expression and he lunged at Paul with a jumping, ‘Lightning Uppercut’. RILEY STEVENS: “Thus far Tony is firing on all cylinders.” Paul recovered fast and scurried over to tag in Daniel Starr. Tony held up his right hand in protest, not so much to beg that Starr didn’t enter the ring. He merely just wanted Starr to wait a minute whilst he tagged in his partner. Tony threw up a finger, telling Starr to wait and then turned and walked a few paces to Scar. They locked eyes and Tony, using both hands, pushed Scar as he was pushed earlier. Tony snickered and passed under the rope to the rim, no longer the legal man in. RILEY STEVENS: “You had to know these two were just going to be at each other’s necks the entire time.” Scar slowly looked down at his shoulders, then at Tony and entered the ring. He ignored Daniel Starr, who waited with a confused look upon his face. Tony and Scar stood face to face, or well, face to mask. Scar was then pulled back by surprise into an abdominal stretch by Starr. Within arms reach of his partner, Tony just watched and even exchanged a few chuckles between he and Hayden at the distraught state of his tag partner. Scar countered with a hip toss, but Daniel was on him sooner than expected with a Suplex. Scar rolled out of the ring immediately after breaking a quick roll up pin. He slowly advanced toward Hayden, forcing her to move around the ring to avoid him. Tony took note of this and dropped down from the ring, but it was Daniel Starr that stopped him. Starr leapt from the ropes with a leaping bulldog that slammed the masked face of his opponent into the matted floor. He then dragged Scar back into the ring and tagged in his partner, Paul Defias. With Scar in his grasp, Paul quickly seized the opportunity to pin after the maneuver Starr had pulled, but Scar kicked out. RILEY STEVENS: “DefiaStarr has Scar in trouble.” Quickly Paul tugged his opponent into an ankle lock. It didn’t seem to affect Scar as well as he’d hope, in fact, Scar easily turned onto his back and stuck the flat end of his boot up into Paul’s chin. Paul released him and staggered back into the corner turn post. There, Scar positioned him, after getting up himself, into a tree of woe. Upside down, he couldn’t see the hard boot that drove into his stomach, but he definitely could feel it, and then the impact of his head hitting the canvas. Paul held his skull and stomach, it almost felt like a police investigation to determine if he were drunk, but he wasn’t. Just smashed. RILEY STEVENS: “Scar recovers and gets the advantage.” Paul curled under the rope and snuck out. Hayden had pulled herself up onto the rim of the ring to distract Scar, Tony’s own partner. Scar moved to greet her with a cold glare. He gestured his thumb across his neck and reached out to her, but Daniel was swift to smack him across the masked skull with a missile dropkick from the top turn buckle. Scar collapsed over the top rope. Hayden dragged him in time for Tony to tag back into the match. She then pulled his partner out of the ring and threw him to the ground, then her hands in the air with innocence. RILEY STEVENS: “Hayden is getting involved on the outside.” Daniel had aided Paul up during this moment and tagged himself in. He went for Tony, but Tony pulled back. He pointed toward Scar and suddenly Daniel understood. In an odd fashion, Tony and Daniel Starr, with help of Hayden Carter dragged Scar back into the ring. Tony performed his finisher on Scar, then Daniel took a turn at it. With that out of the way, Daniel pinned his masked opponent for a full three count. JAMAL JACKSON: “Here are your winners…DefiaStarr!” Scar jumped to his feet in anger looking around just for someone to take out his anger on but both Starr and Defias bailed out of the ring heading up the ramp in a hurry holding their hands high as he then looked to the outside where Tony and Hayden stood, Tony with a microphone. TONY MIRANDA: “Scar you and I know all to well that this little thing between us, it needs to end. So I have a solution, last time we met you beat me but in the end I got right back up. I propose that this time…The loser doesn’t get up. Scar, at ‘Exile’ you versus me in a Last Man Standing match!” The crowd goes wild for the idea as Hayden nods while patting Tony on the back as Scar looks out at them and then again does the same thumb gesture basically accepting the match, making the fans very happy. RILEY STEVENS: “Last time they had glass on their hands this time anything goes only this time the loser will be the man that can’t stand, this is going to be crazy.” The two continue to stare at one another, they were the losers here tonight but the only match they cared about now was the one they would have at ‘Exile’. WINNERS: DEFIASTARR [Daniel Starr & Paul Defias] Splash! We hear the sound of some champagne opening as Paul Defias and Daniel Starr stand backstage cracking open the bottle in the catering area. PAUL DEFIAS: “First win! DefiaStarr is on the map!” DANIEL STARR: “We beat Scar and Tony Miranda try to tell us we are not the real deal.” Starr takes a big swig of the drink as the two are soon joined by Michael Lobo who heads over to them. MICHAEL LOBO: “So you two must think you’re the top group around here?” Lobo says this clearly with something on his mind as Defias and Starr look at one another then reply. DANIEL STARR: “That’s a fact.” PAUL DEFIAS: “Now if you don’t mind this area is reserved for WINNERS.” Defias chuckles as Lobo looks at them and then nods his head. MICHAEL LOBO: “We’ll see…” Michael walks off as Defias and Starr look at each other then shrug going back to celebrating. QUOTE When we return to the ring we see Henry Malkin standing in the ring ready to go for his next match, as “Busy Being Born” by Middle Class Rut echoes through the crowd to a huge roar of cheers for Henry Malkin the GIW Television Champion. RILEY STEVENS: “Nathan Bailey is undefeated here in GIW and has looked impressive, Malkin was great last week and is looking to bring some prestige to that belt.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Bailey is going to embarrass him.” Bailey stands in the ring ever so confident, as Malkin heads down the ramp high fiving some fans along the way as he heads up the steps and into the ring where he looks across at Bailey who let’s face it is the favorite in most people’s eyes here tonight. Malkin gets ready as Bailey walks across the ring. DING DING DING! Bailey stands across the ring looking at him as he starts to trash him, yelling out “You are not a real champion!” Pointing out at the belt on the outside, Malkin lets him get out of his system before rushing across the ring dropkicking Nathan in the knee. Bailey falls down to one knee as Henry bounces off the ropes and right off the bat hits him with the ‘Euthanasia’ a Shining Wizard! The crowd goes insane as he is not quite done yet, with Bailey in and out of consciousness he grabs his arm and with little resistance locks in the ‘Queen's Vice’ a Crossface! The fans again go nuts as Bailey flails around tapping out immediately sending the fans into a frenzy! JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…Henry Malkin!” These fans are jumping up and around, as Malkin gets up seeming like the only person not surprised here in the building as Bailey rolls around in agony as the referee hand’s Henry his belt and he holds it into the air proudly. RILEY STEVENS: “That couldn’t have been more then a minute long and Malkin makes Bailey tap out.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “First Hayden Carter then Malkin destroys Bailey like this?” Henry grabs his belt showing it to the camera as a message to the doubters as he beat Alex Castellanos last week and then beat Nathan Bailey IV this week as we go to a break. WINNER: HENRY MALKIN Returning from break we have Henry Malkin heading through the back with excitement as he walks right into Angel Vegnaduzzo clapping him on. ANGEL VEGNADUZZO: "Good job champ, really good job." Malkin tosses his belt over his shoulder knowing there has to be a catch as he walks closer to him. HENRY MALKIN: “Thanks, you did well tonight as well.” Angel doesn’t seem to really care about the compliment quite as much as he stares back at him. ANGEL VEGNADUZZO: "Listen I heard what you said earlier about us stealing the show, then you wished me luck? I don’t need luck and if you think this is going to be some back and forth match? It’s not I am going to take that belt and I am going to do it quickly. Ok?" With that Angel walks off and as he goes so to does Henry’s hope that the two could have mutual respect going into the match as the two were now on a collision course with one another. ==== Else where backstage we find Alexandra Rossi checking on Nathan Bailey IV as the two head towards the exit only to be caught off guard by Aspen Chaud crutching her way over to them. ASPEN CHAUD: “Oh isn’t that just too bad, I mean that was embarrassing. You know when I heard you two got back together I thought it was a joke but then I realized that’s just what you two are. I mean that match just lasted longer then most of our nights together Nathan, the one’s where you…ahem finished before you could even get it out.” Rossi glares back at her while Bailey is in too much pain to even know what she is saying. ASPEN CHAUD: “So if you two can’t handle the pressure of GIW any more I am going to relieve you of your duties here, you are fired.” Aspen grins and sadly she had that kind of power, at least when she had Sinclair’s permission as she did in this situation. ALEXANDRA ROSSI: “We already were leaving…” Rossi hands Nathan an ice pack as Aspen grins and smiles. ASPEN CHAUD: “I didn’t agree to that, you are fired.” With that the two head on their way as Chaud happily watches them go as she has just manipulated these two all the way out of GIW. QUOTE It is now time for the Main Event and we come into the scene with a very happy crowd cheering on Cassidy Hightower as she heads up the steps and into the ring looking out at the crowd with a smile she was getting the match she wanted here tonight. "Kiss My Lips" by Dev featuring Fabolous now cut her music off and those cheers turned into angry growls as the crowd’s anger could be felt as Alexis Terry walked out from the back with the ‘GIW Legacy Championship’ around her waist. RILEY STEVENS: “Hayden Carter beats Alioth Starre, Henry Malkin beats Nathan Bailey IV in under a minute, can Cassidy get the win?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Not in a million years.” Alexis strides to the ring glaring in at Cassidy having a whole lot of pent up anger from the past few weeks of this little rivalry of there’s as she heads up the steps into the ring with her belt over her shoulder, it was still hers and she would make sure everyone knew it as she went to the tope displaying it. RILEY STEVENS: “With Bailey fired, it leaves just Beck, Cassidy, and Alexis.” She jumps down handing over the belt to the referee as Cassidy watches her and finally the referee calls for the bell. DING DING DING! Cassidy bursts out of the starting gates rushing at Alexis to get back at her for all that she has done but showing a bit of composure for the first time in a long time Alexis lets her run right into a European Uppercut. These fans are in this one big time so they boo when Terry follows it up with an immediate DDT that slams Hightower’s face into the mat. Terry gets up and all that pent up anger seems to be starting to come out as she walks around Cassidy who gets up quickly only for Alexis to bounce off the ropes and nail her with a quick Big Boot. Looking down at Cassidy she had some sick intentions in her eyes as she glared down at her and then the crowd listening as they booed her as she grabs Cassidy by the hair, the referee telling her to let it go so quickly she slams Cassidy’s face down hard into the mat using the hair as leverage, ripping a bit of it as she lets go. RILEY STEVENS: “Did she just rip some of Cassidy’s hair out?” With that Cassidy rolls over trying to get back up only for Alexis to get on top viciously throwing punches while having her mounted. Cassidy flails around to get her off finally pushing her away as Alexis rolls up to her feet as Cassidy does the same only it ends up a bad idea as Terry nails her with a Spinning Wheel Kick. It doesn’t knock her off her feet however so Alexis finishes the deed this time with a Yakuza Kick that sends her to the mat now starting to show some pain, holding her face which has taken most of the damage here thus far. Rather then even attempting a pin Alexis screams at her to get to her feet. Cassidy does so using the corner to get up so Terry grabs her by the back of the head repeatedly slamming her face first into the corner cushion over and over again until finally the referee pulls her away as Cassidy falls to the mat. RILEY STEVENS: “This is a beating…” Terry pushes away from the referee almost getting herself disqualified a few times now as Hightower grabs the ropes using them to get to her feet where she turns around right into a ‘Flawless Victory’ a Inverted Stomp Facebreaker! Right away the blood starts to spew out of the nose of Cassidy Hightower, the alignment is off as well and it’s clear that nose is messed up. Take into account the cut right under her eye and the blood is certainly flowing now, the referee even tells her just to pin her but it is not enough for her as Alexis head to the top rope looking out at the fans who have lost all their energy not expecting this kind of a beat down at all as they don’t even react as she hits the ‘MGME’ the Most Glamorous Moonsault Ever, landing right on the bloody mess that is Cassidy and the referee jumps on for a really quick count 1-2-3! JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…Alexis Terry!” Alexis gets to her feet, the blood of Cassidy rubbing off on her as the referee gets down to check on her as the fans begin to boo as Eliza Gray walks out from the back walking around the ring grabbing her belt then sliding in and handing it to her as she holds up her arm. RILEY STEVENS: “Beck is locked backstage!” In the ring Alexis screams then rushes forward clobbering her with the belt as Cassidy falls over lifeless in the ring as she stands over her with her belt, screaming something at her as she walks away and tells Eliza yet again to lift her all the sudden the crowd goes wild as Beck breaks the hinges on the door then springs out as the guards go to stop him only for them to receive big punches then a toss into the wall as Beck runs towards the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “You know this may not be the time but it appears as though Nathan Bailey IV and Cassidy Hightower are out…Alexis Terry and Beck Ramsey will meet at ‘Exile’ in that TLC match and I think Alexis just made Beck…Very…Very…Angry.” With Alexis glaring back into the ring with no remorse Eliza urges her to go as Beck glares out at her as the EMT’s come in to check on Cassidy as the show fades.
