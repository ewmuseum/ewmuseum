GIW — Card

Revolt, 15 July 2010

15 July 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Galveston Island Wrestling Presents: Thursday Night Revolt! Live! From Poretto Beach - July 15th 2010 Boom! Boom! Boom! The crowd goes insane as fireworks shoot into the sky from Poretto Beach, the location of this weeks edition of Thursday Night Revolt! The crowd of nearly two hundred screaming fans are screaming so loud, the cameras broadcasting the event over Ion Television begin to shake. The cameras pan around the audience showing signs, and an overall amazing crowd packed around the ring. Riley Edwards: Ladies and gentlemen, welcome to Thursday Night Revolt! The flagship show for Galveston Island Wrestling, and your primary source for sports entertainment! I’m Riley Edwards, alongside my broadcast partner Oliver Thibodaux! What a huge show we have scheduled for you tonight… lets run through it shall we Ollie? The camera pans around for a second or two more before focusing its view on Oliver and Riley behind the announce booth. Oliver Thibodaux: That’s right Mr. Edwards. Tonight’s card has the advantage of being well booked… it actually makes sense, unlike the card of our competition. Riley Edwards: You may be right about that one Thibby, but in the end it comes down to pure wrestling entertainment… with that said, I think we still will come out on top! Earlier this evening, Liviana won the Fusion Battle Royal decisively. We will be looking forward to seeing more of this wrestler in the future, but that’s the past. Let’s turn our heads to the future… tonight’s opener is a singles match between Jaden Daniels and Billy Spitzatoni, what are you expecting from this match Ollie? Oliver Thibodaux: Hey…! Billy Spitzatoni… that’s the guy from that Jersey Shore right? I didn’t know he wrestled. I’ll pick him! That’s an awesome show! Riley Edwards: Uh, Thibby, I don’t think those are the same people. But, regardless of your methods, I agree with your pick. Billy Spitzatoni may not be a large competitor, but he makes up for his size in attitude several times over. The next match on the card is a slumping Mike Danielson vs. a streaking Nitro. Oliver Thibodaux: Oh no! Streakers are never good! We better get him out of here before we get in trouble… this is a family show! Riley Edwards: Ollie, you know that’s not what I mean. Who do you think will win this match? Personally, I was leaning toward Nitro. Oliver Thibodaux: Well no crap Nitro will win. I heard since Danielson lost at the PPV he couldn’t pay for his house and the bank took it away. Now his backyard powers are gone! Oh no! Whatever will he do??? Riley Edwards: Yes, Mike has been called the “Backyard Superstar”. Next up we have a six superstar inter-gender tag team match: Eric Sparrow & Kaiju & Catalina Mendoza vs. Trey Walker & Faarooq Fawaz & Taylor James. This match a bunch of minor feuds intertwined inside of it… neither team has a very decisive edge here, very even match up… but I am going with the team of Sparrow, Kaiju, and Mendoza… simply because they have experience on their side. Oliver Thibodaux: How can you root for a man in a dinosaur robot suit with good conscious. I guess Stoner gave you a few pills before the show tonight. The other team wins. Riley Edwards: Next up is Jake Kennedy against the fan favorite Johnny Stylez. Stylez, of course, is coming off of a huge win at Moment of Glory, he literally Seized the Moment! Oliver Thibodaux: Yeah, and he will demolish Jake Kennedy in this match. A buddy of mine asked me the other day who I thought has gotten the farthest in this company with the least amount of talent… and without thinking I said Megathreat. But, in second place it would have to be Jake Kennedy. Riley Edwards: And then, of course, we have the main event match! Wake Walker teams with Aspen Chaud against the duo of Violet “Velvet” Vanity and Monday Night Affliction’s Joshua Acquin. Who do you think wins this match? Oliver Thibodaux: Well… it wont be Fake Falker and Aspen Hot! No way! Velveeta Vanity and Joshua should take this match in record time! Riley Edwards: OK! Enough chat… time to get the opening contest started… Billy Spitzatoni vs. Jaden Daniels, right after the break! We are now in the ring after the announcers have made there call and almost immediately already standing in there is Jaden Daniels yelling at the fans as they give him no reaction at all. Soon the place erupts with “Young” by Hollywood Undead and out from the back looking completely serious is Billy Spizatoni, staring his opponent in the ring down. Riley Stevens: This kid Billy may be small in stature but I have it under good knowledge that he is quite the wrestler. Oliver Thibodaux: Yup I really like this guy; he’s going to be impressive. Billy ignores the fans who boo him as well hating both of these wrestlers. He jumps up the steps and then quickly into the ring, setting up to fight almost immediately across from Jaden as the referee calls for the bell. DING DING DING! Right away the two head to meet up in the middle of the ring and Jaden begins to get up into Billy’s face, talking trash. Billy smirks and shakes his head as Jaden backs up patting his chest but when he turns back into Billy he is caught with a huge kick to the chest. He holds his head in pain as Billy connects with one to the left leg. Jaden tries to protect his chest again expecting it there but instead Billy jumps up and catches him right across the face with a huge kick. Jaden falls back into the corner as the fans have a bit of unsure reactions. Billy backs up and runs across the ring screaming at the last second before nearly tearing Jaden’s head off with a Flying Forearm! The fans have to be a bit impressed as Daniels seems to be in real trouble, falling to the mat in a lifeless heap. Billy jumps up and connects with a big stomp onto the chest of Jaden, that seems to only further Jaden’s pain now seeming to be hurt any and every where. With Jaden on the mat Billy looks to the crowd and yells “Its over” before lifting Jaden up, barely able to get his lifeless body to a stand before hitting the move he calls ‘Face My Innocence’. The crowd is a bit stunned by this lack of any offense from Jaden as Billy, looks at them knowing full well he has it done before getting down on top for the pin 1…2…3! Oliver Thibodaux: I told you, this kid is great. Riley Stevens: He made quick work of his opponent tonight but up against real competitors, can he do it? Billy gets to his feet forcefully pushing Jaden’s motionless body out of the ring before demanding the referee hold up his arm after a big debut here on Revolt. After the match ends, and the crowd settles down, “Motherfucker of the Year” by Motley Crew blares through the PA system set up on the beach and the crowd bursts into boos. Your new Galveston Island Champion, Scott Rydell, strolls down the ramp with his signature smirk on his face. He has his microphone in his hand, but when it comes time to enter the ring, he simply stands outside of it. Scott Rydell: Well, well, well. Look what Scott Rydell has around his shoulder. Scott rubs his championship belt and smiles to the crowd. Boos could be heard from miles in every direction. Scott Rydell: Yeah, yeah. All of you idiots just keep on booing. It doesn’t change a damn thing… I’m still the Galveston Island Champion. I’m still the face of this organization. I’m still BETTER THAN YOU! Scott smirks some more… every times he smirks or laughs the fans boo him, but none of that seems to matter to him. Scott Rydell: Now, unfortunately for you guys, I didn’t come out here simply to entertain you, I came out here to talk about the new brand on the block, the “new blood” as they call themselves; Megathreat’s illegitimate brain child… Monday Night Clusterfuck. The fans continue to boo, Scott hold a finger up to hear, acting like he has an earpiece and nods. Scott Rydell: Oh, oh, ok. I’m being told that the show name is indeed not Monday Night Clusterfuck, but Monday Night Affliction. With that card and roster, you could have fooled me. Scott laughs at his own joke, much to the dismay of the GIW fans. Scott Rydell: And, to keep the whole “unfortunate” theme of this conversation. Unfortunately for myself… Blake Beckett thought it would be good business to have Joshua Acquin return for to the A-Show for just one week… but Megathreat, being the stingy old man that he is, wont let him come over without someone heading back over there in return. Guess who got picked? It damn sure wasn’t Billy Spitzatoni. There is a mixed reaction with the crowd, some laughs, some boos. Scott Rydell: Yeah. It was me. Monday Night, Scott Rydell will live from some football stadium on the middle of the island. Now, even though you want to, I am begging you not to turn on your televisions and switch to My Network 20 on Monday Night… don’t do it! Obviously, you all would want to, considering I will be there doing my thing. Don’t give in to the Rydell Effect. You can read on the website what is happening, but don’t give them any self-confidence by watching the “B” Show. The crowd boos again. Scott Rydell: I’m serious. If you watch that show… you will go blind! More boos. Scott Rydell: Fine, watch at your own risk. We have to cut to commercial. ============ "The Final Countdown" by Europe plays. All the fans yell, "Command & Conquer!!!" as Mike Danielson makes his way down the ramp. He eventually climbs up the steel steps and poses for the crowd and enters the ring. He begins to hit his head in order to pump himself up. Three Days Grace- The Good Life airs as Nitro comes out to his slow motion entrance. Mike Danielson grinds his teeth, he doesn't seem to like Nitro one bit. Nitro enters the ring and the bell rings. Nitro quickly starts the match with a drop kick straight to the chest of Mike Danielson. Mike Danielson falls down and is now cornered in the turnbuckle. Nitro begins an onslaught of closed fist punches which award him a few warnings. Nitro ignores them and tells the referee how sexy he is. Mike Danielson takes advantage of the fact that Nitro is distracted and kicks Nitro right on the knee. Nitro falls down and goes to the center of the ring. He tries to drag himself to the opposing turnbuckle but Danielson quickly follows up with a baseball slide that seemed to be a low-blow at first that nearly got Danielson disqualified. Danielson tries to go to work on the arm of Nitro but Nitro throws Mike Danielson to the outside. Mike Danielson quickly comes back in and keeps hitting Nitro over and over again, showing a dead-near pitbull type of style fighting. Oliver Thibodaux: Wow, take a look at Mike Danielson! He is absolutely ruthless! Nitro eventually starts getting the upperhand after a while when he executes a drop toe hold. He goes to work on Mike Danielson by hitting a series of suplexes and other high impact maneuvers. After four minutes of the match going on, Nitro hits a devastating European Uppercut that knocks out Danielson out cold completely. The referee looks like he has intentions of stopping the fight but Nitro continues his attack. He then follows up with a superkick. Mike Danielson is done. Nitro finishes him off with a Nitroxide (Saving Grace DDT). Nitro goes for the cover but the referee refuses to count. He declares this a KO and calls Nitro the winner. Nitro celebrates. The crowd doesn't like it but some clap to show that this win was really decisive. David Sinclair comes out with a microphone in hand. Riley Edwards: Here comes the surprise! I wonder what it is! If David Sinclair is here, you know it's gotta be huge. David Sinclair: Well, Nitro... you won the match and we said there was going to be a surprise so just enjoy it while you can. Try to hold onto for a long time because otherwise you're just going to embarrass yourself. A box is lowered down using a pulley from the sky seemingly. Nitro punches the box open and finds a title belt in side. David Sinclair laughs and walks to the back. Nitro holds up the belt and celebrates more as the crowd boos. In one small camera shot, the belt can be seen as the, "Lightweight" belt. Oliver Thibodaux: Wow, how lucky of Nitro! He seems to be enjoying his prize! The cameras switch over to backstage where Sarah Sharp is standing in the GIW Interview Lounge with Trey Walker. Sarah Sharp: Hello GIW Revolt fans, Sarah Sharp here with one of the newest men here in Galveston Island Wrestling. Trey Walker. Trey- Trey Walker grabs the microphone, Sarah runs away. Trey Walker: I don’t have time for an interview; but let me say this. This match, whoever the hell booked it, makes no sense. It doesn’t make sense because I am in it, and it is only in the mid card slot. Do you know who I am, does anybody know who I am? I’m Trey Walker; the future of this brand and the future of this company. If I don’t get some respect… some bad things are going to happen. Trey drops the microphone and scampers off to his match. Back from commercials and the team of Trey Walker & Faarooq Fawaz & Taylor James are already in the ring. Drank By Girlicious hit’s the P.A. and out comes Catalina Mendoza, she makes it to the ramp as Fat Lip by Sum 41 cuts through and out walks Eric Sparrow. Godzilla by Blue Oyster Cult roars through and out walks Kaiju of the Battel to a blaring red hot crowd. Eric Sparrow and Faarooq Fawaz start out the match. Faaroq immediately goes for a Death Valley Driver but Sparrow grabs hold of the ropes. Faaroq tries and tries but can’t break Sparrow off and Sparrow let’s go and bounces into a Hurracanrana sending Faaroq halfway across the ring. Faaroq gets up only to meet a Calf Kick. Eric hit’s a Air Sparrow and tags in Kaiju as Faarooq tags Taylor James. Kaiju unleashes a torrent of big boots and choke slams. He drops her with a DDT and goes for the pin only to have Trey Walker break it up. Sparrow enters the ring and Kaiju and him whip Walker into the ropes and hit a Back Body Drop dumping Trey to the outside. Eric Sparrow hit’s a Asai Moonsault as Kaiju goes back to Taylor James. He hits her with a German Suplex and then a leaping elbow drop. He throws her into the ropes and crushes her with a Avalanche. He tags in Catalina and they hit her with a stereo suplex. Twisting Elbow Drop and Split Leg Drop from Catalina. She goes up for a Corkscrew 450 Splash but Faaroq tries to push her off. She mule kicks Faarooq and hit’s a cross body on him. Kaiju comes in and Powerbomb Faaroq. Taylor James make it up and tries to take Catlina down and apply a Sharpshooter. Catlina wrenches her hand deep into Taylor’s scalp and hit’s a Muay Thai Head Kick. Trey Walker is getting up and Taylor hit’s a desperation tag to him. Trey limps into the ring and gets hit by a Puerto Rican Princess. As he is down Eric Sparrow hit’s a Spinal Tap and Kaiju hit’s a Amphibious Bomb. Catlina pins him only for Faaroq to interrupt the pin. Catalina tags in Eric Sparrow. He hit’s a Gravity Killer on Faaroq, and a dropkick on Taylor James sitting on the apron. Kaiju and Catalina hit a Clothesline From The Depths and a True Beauty on Trey Walker. Eric Sparrow then applies his Purple Haze Rear Naked Choke and Trey Walker passes out. A voice can be heard in the distance…speaking in a foreign language. “それらが(彼・それ)らの罪に対して価格を支払うときが来ました。 私はそれを必要とする視界に入っているそれらのすべてを罰するであろう正直な男です。 まもなく、私の時代は始まるでしょう。” The crowd is confused but they make fun of the foreign language begin spoken. Suddenly, a symbol appears on the tron for three seconds before fading away. At the bottom, the words: COMING SOON… can be seen. [pic] "Cold" by Crossfade fizzes through the speakers. Johnny Stylez cockily comes out past the curtain and completely ignores all of the fans that simply want a hi-five or a handshake. Johnny Stylez walks down the ramp, enters the ring and then his signature pose managing to create a lot of controversy within the crowd. A rowdy crowd in the front begins to chant Stylez’s name which instead of flattering Johnny, it irritates him. Johnny Stylez jumps up and down in the ring and waits for his opponent. "The Way I Am" by Fozzy interrupts everything going on and Jake Kennedy gets a nice applause from the crowd and even some cheers. Jake Kennedy walks down the ramp in a cold fashion and enters the ring. He doesn’t want to pander to the crowd, it seems. He’s more focused on Johnny Stylez, his opponent. The rowdy bunch in the crowd begin to chant, “Kennedy Sucks!” The bell rings. One of the rowdy crowd members throws a rock at Kennedy which hits him right in the head. Kennedy starts yelling at the crowd member when suddenly, Johnny rolls Kennedy up! 1…2.. kick out! Johnny quickly backs away to try and form another strategy to use to defeat Kennedy. Kennedy doesn’t seem to care about what Johnny just tried, he seems more angry at the audience member. Kennedy tells the referee that he isn’t going to wrestler unless that audience member is thrown out. Funnily enough, the crowd member is ejected from the building. Kennedy seems ready to wrestle when some of the audience members (actually, the majority of them) begin to throw trash at Johnny Stylez. Oliver Thibodaux: Oh my GOD! What’s going on here?! This entire place has gone up in smoke and has lost all control! Johnny is blamed for having paid those rowdy members to throw the rock. Kennedy begins to get a chant and he eventually begins to beat down Johnny, creating a lot of cheers from the crowd. There is a beatdown that goes for nearly three minutes with the crowd continuing to throw trash. The referee is too busy picking it up and throwing it where it belongs and making sure that the ring is cleared so in essence he pays little attention to what is going on in the match. After those three minutes pass, Johnny Stylez low-blows Kennedy. Kennedy is hurting now! Suddenly, the lights go off! A green light seems to be flying in the sky and has now perched itself on one of the turnbuckles. The lights go back on- it’s tOWLette, the back-up referee wearing a neon glow-in-the-dark colored outfit! Riley Edwards: What in the world? Is that an owl? Oliver Thibodaux: That’s not just ANY owl, that’s tOWLette! He’s come here to make sure this match goes safely as planned! tOWLette begins to chase Stylez around the ring and tries to punish him for the low-blow. Stylez enters the ring to a Kennedy waiting to clothesline him. Unfortunately, however, Johnny Stylez ducks and dodges the maneuver and at the same time, tOWLette accidentally pokes Kennedy in the eye! Even tOWLette can’t do anything about this! Kennedy is on the floor. Johnny goes up on one of the turnbuckles and hits The Hollywood Impact (five-star frog splash)! He goes for the cover. The referee stops picking up trash and enters the ring! He starts the count! 1…2..3! It’s over! Stylez has defeated Kennedy. Oliver Thibodaux: Due to some unlucky events, it looks like Stylez has picked up the win. Who knows what’s going to happen when he receives HIS title shot! Winner: Johnny Stylez Stylez goes up the ramp and into the backstage area. Kennedy is standing, posing for the crowd despite his loss. Suddenly, the lights go off again! Everyone appears to be confused and by the time they come back on, Jake Kennedy is on the floor in a pool of his own blood! Nobody can explain it and immediately, Jake Kennedy is stretchered to the back. As Kennedy is being stretchered, an evil laugh can be heard over the speaker. The cameras switch over backstage where cameramen are running. The turn a corner and we see Scott Rydell being restrained by security guards, a blood stained steel chair, and Wake Walker down on the ground. The main event is advertized. Wake Walker can be seen in the back trying to get pumped up for his match. He cracks his neck and then begins to walk towards the ring. The crowd can see this so they obviously give this a cheer. Suddenly, however, Scott Rydell comes practically out of nowhere and looks like he wants to start a fight with Walker. The two stare down. Riley Edwards: Wow, we’re back after that strange backstage occurance we just had with Walker and Rydell! Here comes Aspen! "Envy" by Nicki Minaj begins to play. The crowd gives Aspen Chaud a very warm reception. She goes past the curtains and walks down the ring. She still seems to have some of the cockiness with her but the crowd generally likes her. She enters the ring and waits, pumping herself up for her match. The lights go out. Aspen Chaud is confused. She begin s to ask the time keeper and the referee to inquire about what’s going on but no answers can be given. As the lights are off, tOWLette can be seen getting into position. A video begins to play. It is labeled, “Just a few minutes ago…” Rydell and Walker can be seen looking at each other angrily when suddenly, Rydell hits a shot! Walker takes it but quickly retaliates with a clothesline bringing Rydell to the floor. Walker begins a beat down on Rydell but eventually the two are separated. As Rydell is being separated and held back, he manages to get his hands on a steel bat. He breaks away from the guys restraining him and hits Wake Walker square on the head with the bat, sending him to the floor! People try to restrain Rydell but it’s no use. He warms that he’s going to hit them with the bat as well if they don’t get out of his way. Rydell begins to salivate all over Wake Walker and begins to hit him over and over again in the stomach with the bat until other wrestlers such as Kaiju and Stoner come up to break up Rydell and Walker. Rydell warns Walker that he is the top man in the business. Rydell picks up his title belt which was on the floor due to the altercation and walks away, laughing. The lights go back on. Aspen Chaud looks in horror. She begins to talk with the referee. Suddenly, Blake Beckett , Violet Vanity, Liviana and Catalina come out. Blake signals to the girls to go get Aspen. The referee looks like he wants to take a stand here. Riley Edwards: This can not be good for Aspen. Let’s see what the referee has to say about this! I wonder if Walker is okay and not permanently injured! That was a brutal beat down we just witnessed! Referee: Look, I believe Acquin is no longer in this match but one of you will replace him. I don’t care who it is! (The girls agree for it to be Catalina) However, because Aspen’s partner, Wake Walker has been beat to a bloody pulp and now Aspen is at a disadvantage, I’m going to give YOU guys a disadvantage as well! Oliver Thibodaux: Is the referee insane?! The referee rings the bell and begins to count out Vanity and Catalina! Vanity quickly runs with Catalina behind her and makes it into the ring at the count of 6. However, Aspen quickly takes Vanity down and locks in the KMA (heel lock)! Vanity quickly taps out and can’t take the pain! Wake Walker and Aspen Chaud are declared the winners but Catalina kicks Aspen right on the jaw. Vanity gets up whilst holding her leg and they begin an assault on Aspen when Liviana joins in. After roughing up Aspen, Blake Beckett can be seen laughing and walking to the back. All the divas leave. Aspen appears to be bleeding from the lip. Oliver Thibodaux: Well, Violet Vanity and Catalina were clearly robbed! Why didn’t tOWLette say anything! We give him dead rats every day for a reason! Winners: Wake Walker & Aspen Chaud
