GIW — Pay-per-view

International X 2011, 24 April 2011

24 April 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: INTERNATIONAL X! LIVE FROM CANCUN, MEXICO = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! The cameras come on and immediately we find ourselves in the middle of an arena filled to the masses with wrestling fans, some of which may be unfamiliar with the company but know that wrestling is a universal sport. There’s not an empty seat in the place as the opening pyro goes off as the fans begin to cheer knowing that the show is about to start. As we pan around the fans some of which having gotten some GIW merchandise it’s clear there is a mixture of fans that either know about us or are here just for the show. We arrive at the announce table where Riley Stevens and Oliver Thibodaux are ready to call the action. RILEY STEVENS: “What a night. Thanks to Eduardo Santiago, GIW has made the trip all the way down to Cancun, Mexico for another great Supershow with the fitting title ‘International X’! How are you liking the beaches thus far Oliver?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Would certainly be better if there weren’t all of these damn M-…” RILEY STEVENS: “Any how! We got a great card here tonight filled with action from top to bottom. To start the show we have a contest pitting Jacoby Jackson against former ‘Galveston Island Champion’ and frankly a Legend in GIW, Ross Rydell. You see it’s due to that success that Jacoby wanted this match, so that he could make a name off of Rydell. However Ross Rydell has to be looking to put his name back in the spotlight around here and win would do just that.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Sometimes a guy needs to know when to step aside.” RILEY STEVENS: “We’ll see about that Oliver. You see after that match up we will be taken to a GIW Tag Team Championship match up. Brotherhood of Steel came into GIW and have shocked the world week after week. Rising to prominence quickly they have gained the fans respect, along with the belts rather quickly. However their challenge here tonight will not be an easy one. The Acquin Brothers, have returned immediately aligning themselves with Aspen Chaud. They’ll be looking to add some gold around their waist; you have to wonder if they’ll be any ring rust.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Frankly I’ve already gotten sick of Brotherhood of Steel; I’m hoping that the Acquin Brothers can take them down a notch and take those belts.” RILEY STEVENS: “Then in a match I am personally very interested in, Alioth Starre the thus far dominant ‘GIW North Beach Champion’ who has run rough shot through the roster here in GIW defeating such names as Jak Hendricks, Ross Rydell, and Seth Seeker along his way will take on High Voltage’s Trent Lost who has looked impressive just as well. The two have been going at it with their recent feud between High Voltage and anyone willing to stop them. These two as you may have saw fought to a Tie in the GIW Shootout nearly a month ago and it left a sour taste in everyone’s mouth, now they’ll have the chance to settle the question once and for all, who is truly better?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Trent Lost is…” RILEY STEVENS: “Anyways, sometimes a feud gets so intense that a normal match just isn’t fitting to end it as is the case for the feud between Jak Hendricks and Seth Seeker. Not often do we see two guys just immediately hate one another, but with these two it happened the moment they each debuted against one another. Back at ‘Exile’ Hendricks defeated Seth Seeker who went on a minor hiatus. Since returning Seeker has been a man possessed looking to get revenge on Jak. The two have seemingly battled every week, costing each other matches and it’s time to put an end to it all. What better way then to have the two of them, duke it out a First Blood Match where the match will not come to an end until somebody’s blood is drawn, whose blood will it be?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Hopefully that psycho Seeker gets what he has coming to him.” RILEY STEVENS: “Then in what can only be called a dream match it’ll be Eric Sparrow the current ‘GIW Undisputed Champion’ who has been demolishing everyone in his path will take on longtime GIW Superstar…former ‘GIW Television and Tag Team Champion’ Tony Miranda. Known as perhaps the man with the most heart in all of GIW he’s fought his whole career for this opportunity. But the dream may not come as easily as some would hope as Eric Sparrow is looking only to keep his reign alive. But…one late added stipulation is that donning the black and white stripes for this contest will be the always devious Aspen Chaud…” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I’m sure she’ll call it right down the middle.” RILEY STEVENS: “For some reason I doubt that Oliver. However I hope she does because this match will be one for the ages; Tony Miranda and Eric Sparrow will do battle for the gold. We understand that Eduardo has some huge news for us, right now.” Soon enough mariachi music hits as the fans begin to cheer the man who has quickly earned favor with them since buying the company. Out he comes wearing a suit looking very smooth, it’s Eduardo Santiago. He walks out high fiving a few fans; GIW fans and Mexican natives alike cheering him on as he walks up the steps into the ring, with a big smile on his face and a microphone. EDUARDO SANTIAGO: “Hello ladies and gentleman, I have an important announcement to make for all of you. As some of you may know, I bought a lot of stakes in GIW because I saw a big profit in this company. Big stars like Eric Sparrow…Alioth Starre…Tony Miranda all of them, would make great fighters in my company here in Mexico! See, I’ve thought awhile about this and I understand they are having a meeting backstage right now, discussing if…” Eduardo breaks out into a smile hardly able to maintain his composure over the announcement he is about to make. EDUARDO SANTIAGO: “Wait for it…wait for it…They are discussing whether or not GIW will become a permanent Developmental company for my show!” The fans seem a bit caught off guard as he says this with a lot of emotion, as rather quickly the GIW fans in attendance begin booing the news, as he shakes his head. EDUARDO SANTIAGO: “No, no…This is not a bad thing, it’s just…my show is better! Perhaps one day, you’ll see Eric Sparrow on my show, winning the big boy belt? Come on it’s a good idea!” The fans continue to boo as “Demon Eyes” by The Answer hits as the fans welcome the interruption as out comes Blake Beckett with a mic of his own, stopping at the top of the ramp rubbing his chin as he looks down at him. BLAKE BECKETT: “No…that’s not a very good idea. And while you may think that our show is lesser then you I just got off the phone with some people who thought differently. I don’t know what station you’re on around here but needless to say up in the US, there’s a channel with a lot of name value. And through that I have two announcements to make, the first is that starting on May 12th, Revolt will be brought to you by ESPN 2! Oh and that’s not all, seeing as it’s Fox Southwest that hired you…whether or not your allowed in here, is up to me. And I say that you need to leave…Right now.” The crowd goes wild how quickly they have turned on Santiago, as he screams something up at Blake as some Security walks by him to get Santiago. As they grab him we are taken to the backstage area, finishing with the smiling face of Blake Beckett. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = As we hit the backstage area we find ourselves in the middle of what looks almost to be like a family gathering as we see Aspen Chaud in the middle of Trent Lost, Marcus Hister, and the Acquin Brothers all spread out around the locker room area. ASPEN CHAUD: “Seeing as tonight, I do not have a match I expect all of you to do BIG THINGS. You see this past month has been a very big down month for this group. And unfortunately, I can’t help but wonder why. I feel like the motivation is there, yet success does not follow. With the additions of my good friend Joshua Acquin and his brother I expect things to be turned around a bit, because if not I’ll be angry and trust me none you want to see me when I am angry.” Aspen sounds like a fearless leader as she addresses them, though dismissing the fact she got beaten this week naturally. ASPEN CHAUD: “So, here is the deal. The night starts with you two boys fighting for the tag team gold against two guys, who are JOKES! Now if you lose this match I will be…VERY disappointed in you. I expect those belts to be returned to their rightful owners, High Voltage in due time, right?” Aspen turns her head towards Joshua and Austin, who are sitting near each other both nodding their heads as she turns her head to Trent. ASPEN CHAUD: “And you. This Alioth Starre has been a road block since the start. I want his belt taken from him and him taken out of the equation and trust me; big rewards will be given to you.” Aspen smiles as he nods his head seeming to know what she means, as she backs up smiling. ASPEN CHAUD: “As for me, I have plans of my own. So let’s make this a night worth remembering shall we?” Aspen has a big smile on her face, however at the same time she looks to be scheming about the possibilities she has as the Special Guest Referee as the segment ends and we are sent to the ring. QUOTE Back to the ring we go where Jacoby Jackson is wearing a big smirk ready tonight to do as he said before, make a name for himself. When “Motherfucker of the Year” by Motley Crue hits the fans go insane. One of the most beloved men in all of GIW comes out, with that big smile on his face as he walks towards the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “You know I had a conversation with Ross earlier he told me that he wanted to end this in under a minute, anything more was a failure in his eyes.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “What does he think Jackson is a loser?” Rydell stops on his way to the ring, smiling as a female fan in the front row with a ‘Marry Me Rydell’ sign holds it out for him. He stops pulling out a Sharpie from his pocket, she happily hands him her sign. He shakes his head and then quickly signs her chest area, as she goes wild happier with that any way. Jacoby in the ring tells the referee this is ridiculous as Rydell heads up the steps. RILEY STEVENS: “In the bottom of your screen is the ‘New York Minute’ count down clock. We’ll see if Rydell can do it.” Ross steps into the ring, pointing at Jackson and having a good laugh as the referee calls for the bell to start the match. DING DING DING! The clock starts as the fans in Cancun look up at the clock then back in the ring. Jackson comes to the middle of the ring to meet up with him. The two exchange words before Jacoby attempts a cheap shot slap. Rydell blocks it and pushes him away, when Jackson naturally walks forward he catches him with the ‘New York City Speed’ a Savate Kick! The fans go wild as he looks up at the clock which currently reads forty seconds left. He lifts Jacoby up then whips him off the ropes bouncing him right back into a Sidewalk Slam! The fans have not yet stopped cheering for Rydell since the match started as he lifts him up, calling for the finish already putting him into position for the ‘New York Minute’ a Cross Arm Pedigree. He takes a moment, and then hits the move with force as Jacoby falls to the mat out cold. Ross carelessly goes down to the mat and hooks the leg, 1…2…3! With a whole thirteen seconds left to spare as the fans go wild. RILEY STEVENS: “Wow he did it, with time to spare. I guess sometimes we overestimate people here.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Back to the unemployment line with the rest of his family for Jackson, I should of known the god damn…ni-.” RILEY STEVENS: “Whoa Oliver!” It seems as though Oliver’s microphone has been cut as he doesn’t respond as in the ring Rydell looks down at Jackson shaking his head having expected a little more of a fight. Regardless he slides out of the ring heading towards the backstage area as he’s finished off another opponent in under a New York Minute here tonight. WINNER: ROSS RYDELL As we head towards the backstage area, we find a very happy Ross Rydell celebrating with Matt Kluge and a few staff members all of them having a toast to his victory. From around the hall comes Joshua Acquin and his brother Austin, heading towards the ring stopping when they see Rydell. JOSHUA ACQUIN: “Great job…beating a nobody. You see my brother and I we are about to go get the tag team titles, I hope you’ll watch.” A few of the staff members exit out leaving only the brave few behind as Rydell gets up in his face. ROSS RYDELL: “Wow it’s nice to hear that Aspen took your muzzle off.” The few staff members chuckle as does Kluge as neither Acquin’s seems to see the humor in the statement as he angrily responds. JOSHUA ACQUIN: “I just helped her, I’m not her slave.” Rydell grins as Josh looks back at Austin who seems ready to fight as Rydell casually responds. ROSS RYDELL: “You know, while I don’t know if you’ll win tonight, I’d surely not bet on it. I hope you win, because Matt Kluge and I would be ending that reign in a New York Minute.” Again they all have a good laugh as an angry Joshua Acquin barges by him followed not long after by Austin as the two head to the ring for their match as things seem to be heating up. QUOTE In the ring ready for their title defense is Antonio Martins and Pedro Cabrita both showing off their belts. Soon "Monster" by Skillet hits as the fans begin to boo as Joshua Acquin walks out soon after Austin Acquin follows him up. RILEY STEVENS: “These men just recently returned and to a lot of people’s surprise they did so by helping Aspen.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “How is it surprising? They were old friends and he’s a smart guy.” Looking confident the two men head up the steps ignoring the taunts from the crowd who are firmly against the two brothers. They step into the ring as Antonio and Pedro have over their belts. The referee holds up the belt and then hands them as he then turns and calls for the bell. DING DING DING! For both teams they decide quickly who will be starting their match, for the Golden Duo they decide on Austin while the Brotherhood picks Antonio. The two begin to circle then go in for a lock up; the two seem evenly matched in the strength department but Austin ends up out smarting him by rolling around him with a hold of his arm. He tugs on it for a second before Antonio pushes backwards trying to save himself, but Austin slides out of the way and grabs him by the head and tosses him to the mat face first. The fans boo him as Austin walks around Antonio with a big grin on his face, kicking him in the face while he’s down just too taunt him a bit. Austin waits for him to get up, before whipping him into his own corner. Looking over at Pedro he taunts him before running forward and catching Antonio with a Clothesline! The fans boo as Antonio falls face first forward as Austin tags himself in. He steps in as Austin lifts him up, tossing him right into a big Clothesline from Joshua! The fans boo as Josh now drags him into the middle of the ring making the cover 1…2…Kick Out! RILEY STEVENS: “These two are very much on the same page.” Joshua gets up, seeming confident that the kick out won’t matter too much. Instead he sits back waiting for him to get up. Suddenly he smirks as he backs up into the corner looking for the Punt. Suddenly a desperate Pedro jumps in to save his teammate. He steps in front of Josh, trying to keep him away. Austin hops into the ring grabbing him and crushing him with the ‘Crusher’ a Full Nelson Facebuster! The fans boo as Pedro has been taken care of. Pedro rolls out of the ring out of it, as Austin exits the ring. During all of this Antonio has gotten to his feet, walking forward right into the ‘Face Breaker’ an Inverted Double Underhook Facebuster! The fans boo as he immediately hooks the leg 1…2…3! New champions. RILEY STEVENS: “You have to be impressed by the Acquin’s here tonight. Whether Brotherhood of Steel were just unfit champions or not, the Acquin’s dominated them like they were nobodies.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Welcome back guys!” The fans boo as the referee hands in the belts to the new champions, the two hold up the belts in celebration. They both go to the top rope holding up the belts as soon, Joshua steps down from the top rope looking as Antonio seems to be trying to get to his feet. Handing the belt to Austin, he backs up. RILEY STEVENS: “What is he doing!?” His conscious seems to fade as he runs across the ring landing the ‘Last Call’ a Punt to the Head that catches him right on the temple. The fans boo as Antonio goes unconscious as the referee runs over to help him, Josh walks back grabbing his belt as the two head to the back having returned in fashion here tonight. WINNERS: GOLDEN DUO [Joshua Acquin & Austin Acquin] (New GIW Tag Team Champions) As we head to the backstage area we find ourselves looking right at Sarah Sharp who is being joined currently by Number One Contender, Tony Miranda as she has the mic. SARAH SHARP: “Tony as we all know tonight you fight in the main event for the GIW Undisputed Championship, however you’ll face the champion Eric Sparrow a person who you’ve not only been fighting along side with the past month but also a man who has looked seemingly unstoppable thus far in his reign, your thoughts?” Sarah leans over holding the microphone to Tony who nods his head seeming thrilled by the opportunity. TONY MIRANDA: “You know I have wanted this for a long time. Around here I have always been right on the cusp. I’ve watched as people like Aspen, Sparrow, Walker, and Rydell, all these guys won the belt and had their time. Frankly it’s time for me; I’ve done everything else in GIW there is really only one thing left. And if I have to go through Sparrow to get it, so be it.” Tony shrugs as he finishes as Sarah takes the microphone back as she then poses another question to him. SARAH SHARP: “Aspen Chaud was thrown in as the Special Referee, what do you think about that?” Tony lets out a bit of a sigh as she holds the microphone back to him. TONY MIRANDA: “You know obviously I don’t imagine her being a very fair referee, especially considering I just made her tap out. However I don’t think she likes either of us, and when Blake realizes that she’s messing up a good thing, I hope he’ll realize that she needs removed. I’ve never laid a hand on a referee, with her I just might.” With that he walks away as the excitement continues to build towards the Main Event as the segment ends. QUOTE “Down” by Curse Icon hits as the immediate boos begin to show the ring for the man coming out wearing shades looking supremely confident in his abilities. As he walks out towards the ring, taking in all of the jeers and all of the hate filled remarks he heads down the ramp and up the steps, stepping into the ring with that usual swagger. RILEY STEVENS: “This man has been one of our brightest stars here in GIW one has to believe he might be a bit jealous of the fact that Alioth Starre has achieved a bit more success then he has thus far.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Really? Last time I heard Alioth wasn’t in the most dominant stable in this company and perhaps dating the most beautiful woman in all of GIW, so he won a title. When Trent takes that, who will argue that Trent hasn’t achieved more.” As he stops looking out at the crowd with that smug grin “Silly Boy Blue” by David Bowie hits as the fans take a turn to his side. It’s clear they are with Alioth here tonight as he walks out with the belt strapped around his waist, walking towards the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “I don’t think many men have come in and impressed me quite like Alioth.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I’ve never understood a word he was saying to be quite honest.” Alioth rolls his wrists as he steps up on the apron and in through the ropes, stopping to pose with his belt before handing it over to the referee who promptly holds it up into the air for all to see, he hands it out and then calls for the bell. DING DING DING! The match starts as these two men realize just how high the stakes are here tonight fighting for themselves this time. They circle each other until finally they meet up in the center of the ring. Trent begins to over power Alioth due to his strength advantage pushing him up against the ropes. The crafty Alioth uses the momentum to shoot out of his hands, running off the ropes and coming back with a big Clothesline attempt, Trent ducks it. However Starre stops on a dime and then rolls him up, 1…2…Kick Out! Lost angrily hops up flustered by how close that truly was as Alioth gets up back into a fighting stance with a bit of confidence after that. This time Trent comes forward quickly, with a big kick to Alioth’s knee, as he then whips him into the corner. Lost runs at him, looking to go for a kick but when Starre protects his body he goes low drop kicking that same knee as before. Immediately Alioth limps out of the corner in pain as the very unremorseful Lost follows it up with almost a football kick to the back of his knee as he’s walking away sending him falling to the mat. The fans boo him as Starre holds his knee in agony as Lost runs off the ropes and then gives him a dropkick out of the ring and to the outside of the ring. Starre does not have a graceful landing as Trent stands inside of the ring posing for the crowd who boo him for his actions thus far. Realizing he can’t win on a count out he slides out of the ring, going after Alioth who is using the Ring Apron to get to his feet. RILEY STEVENS: “The action has spilled to the outside.” The referee is counting but for now it’s not a big issue. Starre realizing he’s coming grabs him and gives him a big European Uppercut. Trent stumbles back as Alioth goes for him however Trent grabs him and then uses his inability to really put enough pressure on his leg to put the brakes on, to push him towards the steel steps which he hits knee first, falling immediately to the ground holding his knee. With the referee reaching a six count, Trent slides into the ring and then right back out forcing the ref to restart the count. Walking over to an injure Alioth, Trent lifts him up and rolls him inside of the ring. Alioth gets up to his feet, but quickly we realize that he can’t put much weight on it. Lost returns to the ring, looking not too worried about a one legged Starre. Walking towards him he is caught off guard by a big punch by Alioth that brings the fans back into it, cheering Alioth on. Lost angrily goes right back at him this time only he walks right into another European Uppercut. Trent stumbles back holding his jaw and this time a motivated Alioth puts everything he has into a big Body Slam that sends Lost to the mat! The fans go wild as Starre takes a moment to hold his knee, perhaps hurting himself more so with that maneuver but quickly making a cover hoping to end it, 1…2…Kick Out! Yet again a kick out by Lost, as Alioth gets up still bouncing up and down on that leg. RILEY STEVENS: “The longer this match goes the more it favors Trent, as Alioth’s knee will only get worse.” Starre turns around, walking forward towards Lost then stopping. Walking around him now he takes a few steps back then uses whatever he has left to deliver with a Leg Drop with his unhurt leg. The fans go wild as he makes another cover 1…2…Kick Out! Starre gets up, realizing what needs to be done as a beat up Lost gets up, slowly turning into him. Alioth drops him down and then starts to try to line up the Figure Four Leg Lock, however a smart Lost uses all his leg strength to deliver a kick to Alioth’s knee that forces him to bounce back holding his knee. Trent gets up, realizing he dodges a bullet as basically on one foot Starre comes forward only to walk right into a kick to the knee, he cringes and turns around trying to keep protected instead he is rolled up out of no where by Lost, however as the referee goes down to check the shoulders Lost puts his feet up on the ropes 1…2…3! New Champion! RILEY STEVENS: “His feet were on the ropes!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “It’s not cheating if you don’t get caught.” Immediately Trent slides out of the ring not waiting around at all, as he heads over tearing the belt away at ringside. The fans boo as Starre takes a second to get up, realizing what has happened. Barely able to stand, he tries explaining what happened to the referee regarding the ropes but the ref says he didn’t see it. RILEY STEVENS: “I can’t believe this, it was a travesty!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “It’s called winning Riley, deal with it.” Lost holds the belt tightly to his chest knowing full well how happy Aspen will be about this. Alioth realizes that his argument is getting him any where as he looks up the ramp towards Trent who holds the belt high taunting him as this may not be the last time we see Trent Lost and Alioth Starre meet for the title as we head to the back. WINNER: TRENT LOST (New GIW North Beach Champion) Heading to the back we find ourselves in the locker room of High Voltage where a party seems to be ensuing, as Joshua Acquin and Austin Acquin have their GIW Tag Team Championships and Trent Lost has his GIW North Beach Championship as Marcus Hister is cracking out the drinks as the four each get their drinks when out of the bath room area walks Aspen Chaud just having changed into her referee outfit, a slim fitting top and black spandex as she wears a smirk. ASPEN CHAUD: “You boys did it, I knew you could! Trent you beat Alioth! Josh and Austin you CRUSHED those losers as well. I’m so proud of you all.” Trent and Joshua clank together their drinks ready to drink when suddenly Aspen stops both of them. ASPEN CHAUD: “However remember, the night is not quite done yet. Need I remind you that we have business later on. So put the beers down we’ll celebrate after the show…We still have one more thing to do.” Though they seem disappointed that they can’t yet party, they nod their heads seeming to be on the same page as the segment ends. QUOTE When we return the fans can almost smell the blood boiling in both of the men that are waiting to come out for this next match, soon however they’ll have a chance to see Blood drawn. “Forever” by Drake, Kanye West, Lil Wayne, and Eminem hits as the fans begin to boo one of the most arrogant stars in all of GIW, Jak Hendricks himself. As he walks out, he doesn’t seem like a man that is overly scared by the situation he is in. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “This is a damn shame, this man was nearly champion a month ago and now he’s facing this loser Seth Seeker.” RILEY STEVENS: “Well I think I speak for everyone when I say that we all want to see this feud come to an end, these two men hate one another.” Jak steps into the ring walking up to the top rope and taking a second to pose. He hops down and looks at the ramp as “Nightmare” by Avenged Sevenfold hits as the fans go wild as the big man walks out looking just as menacing as usual. Walking slowly towards the ring the tension builds as everyone realize that things are about to go down. He casually walks up the steps, looking into the ring at Hendricks who stares back. RILEY STEVENS: “Wow you can almost smell the hatred in the air.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I think that’s just Seeker.” Seeker steps through the ropes as the fans wait patiently as the two men meet in the center of the ring where the referee explains the rules, they back up and then he calls for the bell. DING DING DING! The two men take a moment to stare one another down, before the war commences as both men meet up in the center of the ring only for Seeker to basically toss Hendricks back onto the mat. Stopping for a moment Seeker has a big grin on his face as Jak gets right back up, not enjoying it at all as he now shakes his head and slides out of the ring calling for Jamal Jackson to get up from his chair, however when he doesn’t budge he walks around grabbing a steel chair from behind him. Seeker stands in the ring calling for him to get in, Jak has the chair as he walks up the steps and then just as he tries to get through Seth walks over grabbing him and pulling the chair out of his hands. Holding the chair with a smirk, Hendricks backs up into the corner in fear. Seeker goes at him swinging the chair back and bringing it forward only for Jak to narrowly slide out of the way causing Seeker to hit the corner. He rears back and swings it again at Hendricks who again jumps out of the way. Seeker finally seems to have him dead to rights but when he brings the chair back Hendricks comes forward with a kick to the lower regions of Seeker that has to take his breath away as he falls to his knees. There are no rules in this contest which means that the move was completely legal. With Seeker downed Hendricks runs off the ropes and nails him with a boot to the face. Seth falls down face forward as Jak sits on top of him, pulling up his neck as he uses one arm to try to scratch his face or tear it open to draw the blood as the fans boo him wanting Seeker to fight back. RILEY STEVENS: “He might just be able to draw the blood right here and right now!” He continues to try to draw blood when suddenly like an awakened giant Seeker starts to get up, with Jak still on his back. Hendricks is lifted up in the air, in fear as Seeker then drops him backwards dropping him hard on his back. The fans go wild as Seeker backs up, lining up as he runs forward crushing him with a breath taking Spear that literally sends Jak airborne! The fans go wild as Seeker steps to slowly pound his chest knowing he just did something great. Jak gets up clenching his chest in pain as he comes out of the corner walking towards Seeker. Grabbing him he puts him between his legs and then lifts him up, Jak is up and all we see is his scared face as Seeker brings him with a thunderous Powerbomb! The fans go wild as Seeker goes to grab him only for a smart Hendricks to roll out of the ring despite being in a lot of pain. Seeker waits for a second, and then heads out of the ring as Jak seems to be trying to get a break by crawling under the ring. Seth grabs him by his legs stopping him, as he looks back at the crowd with a grin. When he pulls him out to his surprise Jak slides out with a big kendo stick that he slams against Seth’s face! The fans are caught off guard as he gets up, as Seeker backs up. Jak uses the kendo stick in any way he can, almost a modified Jak Attack using the kendo stick as he slams it against every single body part of Seth before it eventually breaks in half. RILEY STEVENS: “Wow momentum has taken a turn with that Kendo stick.” Hendricks tosses the broken stick out of the way as he’s able to toss the hurt Seeker into the ring. Walking over to the chair that has been left inside the ring this entire time he grabs it smirking and setting it up in a sitting position inside of the ring. Waiting he stands behind Seeker waiting for him to get up. When he gets up he turns into Jak who grabs him and goes for a Suplex onto the chair; however he underestimates Seeker who suddenly lifts him up using all his strength into the ‘Don't Fear The Reaper’ a Jackhammer that makes the fans go absolutely insane! Whether he knows it or not he brings him down right onto the Steel Chair, sending Jak’s back of the head hard into the chair! A tired Seeker falls down beside the laid out Jak as the referee jumps in looking at the back of Jak’s head where blood is drawn as he calls for the bell. RILEY STEVENS: “That’s it the blood has been drawn but more importantly I don’t think Jak will be able to get up.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “He tried to kill him, this was assault!” The fans go wild for Seeker who seems too gassed to even celebrate. When he finally gets up, he looks down at Jak shaking his head. The fans continue to cheer him on, as he walks around the ring holding up his hand. To the surprise of almost everyone, Jak starts to stir. Seeker stops looking back at him caught off guard as Hendricks gets up stumbling to the ropes. Seth walks over to the ropes, asking for a mic. Which makes us even…You see I did not expect you to come out here and fight me like that but you did…See you and I are not so different…we both want success…And frankly I have a way for us to get it. You see I have been thinking, why don’t the two of us team up...Take this place over leave our mark.” The fans seem to be turning on Seeker a bit, not liking Jak one bit. A hurt Hendricks takes a second to think about it, as a smirk comes across his face. Despite a big cut in the back of his head, he nods his head and presents his hand as the two shake hands as the fans boo the sudden agreement. RILEY STEVENS: “I never thought in a million years we would see this…Hendricks and Seeker teaming up?” Riley’s thoughts seem to be that of the fans as well as the two hold each other’s arms up as Seeker then helps Jak to the back as this new almost scary team has come to reality. WINNER: SETH SEEKER Eric Sparrow is sitting in his locker room looking deep into thought as the door suddenly opens up as he looks up as a big look of disappointment comes across his face as he shakes his head. ERIC SPARROW: “Please say they didn’t send you?” The camera pans out to show a smiling Amanda Rowland who doesn’t seem to get what he meant with that. AMANDA ROWLAND: “Yup, apparently Sarah is having some sort of melt down because she stubbed her toe. Any how they didn’t really give me any notes so ummmm…I think what everyone wants to know is, do you sleep with that belt?” Amanda motions to the belt beside him as Sparrow dealing with the question lifts it up onto his shoulders responding. ERIC SPARROW: “You know what I can work with that. You see because this title and I are almost in a relationship. She’s my belt, she’s my lady. Sometimes a guy comes along trying to steal her away because of her beauty and the prestige that comes with having her. But in the end, I always prevail and at the end of the day she’s back in my arms. This is my belt and nobody is going to take it away from me…NOBODY!” Sparrow lifts the belt up onto his shoulder as he heads out of the room ready for his title defense as the camera pans back to Amanda. AMANDA ROWLAND: “Wow, I had no idea how serious their relationship had gotten.” Amanda seems to think this is big news she is breaking as on that note it is time for the main event. A man who has looked dominant ever since he won the belt. Former Evolve, Deep South, NO-X, and now the Undisputed Champion, Eric Sparrow has done it all. But he does not plan on stopping yet and tonight he looks to keep the Sparrow Era alive, by keeping the belt he so strongly desires. The video fades as “Circles” by Incubus hits as the fans go wild for the champion just as well as he walks out with his belt around his waist, looking around at the packed house with a smile across his face. RILEY STEVENS: “This man has an incredible resume but I don’t believe you can say he’s ever been better then he is right now. Over these past few months’ he’s been rolling through everyone. First it was Jericho then it was Hendricks, the reign has been impressive can he continue it here tonight? I think he has his biggest challenge yet.” Sparrow slides into the ring with a smile as he heads to the top rope un-strapping his belt and holding it high in the air as we get a shot of Tony as well. Sparrow steps down as soon the fans begin booing as out from the back walks Aspen Chaud in her Special Referee outfit, as she does a murmur starts as jumping over the barricades from all direction is High Voltage.
