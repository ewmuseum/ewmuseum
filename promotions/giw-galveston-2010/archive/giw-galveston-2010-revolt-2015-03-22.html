GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS SUNDAY NIGHT REVOLT LIVE! From Kauffman Arena in Galveston, TX -- March 22nd, 2015 BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! As the fireworks stream into the air outside of Kauffman Arena on Galveston Island, the camera pans around the arena to show screaming fans waving signs such as – “The Golden Age Never Started”, “Cowardly Violence”, “Thunder too Loud for NZ”, and “April 19th is Our Night”. “Caged by Wolves” by Family Force Five plays over the announcers. RILEY STEVENS: "Welcome back to another edition of Revolt ladies and germs! We have a big night ahead that will culminate in a clash of titans in the main event." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Two former GIW Undisputed Champions will square off and tear the house down! Alex Castellanos and Alexis Terry will attempt to finally put their rivalry to rest as we are one week away from Seize the Moment, where we will crown a brand spankin’ new GIW Undisputed Champion." RILEY STEVENS: "Nine wrestlers have qualified for the match, and all of them have never been GIW Undisputed Champion before. That means that by the end of Seize the Moment, there will be a new pair of boots on top of the mountain!" OLIVER THIBODAUX: "And with every wrestler wanting the same thing, we are bound to see tensions flare tonight. It is a sound business strategy to take your opponent out before the match begins, so I imagine we will see some brutal action tonight by those who are looking to gain an advantage in the ladder match next week." RILEY STEVENS: "Hopefully not! This will be a big moment for someone next week, and I would hope that it wouldn’t be tarnished by an act of cowardice." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "You just don’t understand wrestling Riley. For everyone in the back, the Undisputed Champion is the reason why they are here. They will do anything they can to win the strap, even the ones these fans think are the good guys. We will probably see that tonight when Chris Callum and Carla Owens try to kill each other." Johnny Violence’s music hit and the crowd began to loudly boo him as he made his way to the ring. RILEY STEVENS: "Well here he comes. The man we all want to hear from tonight. It was just seven nights ago that Johnny Violence ruthlessly attacked Christopher Darren Erns and Michael Lynch. From the way these fans sound tonight, it seems like they will be expecting an explanation… and I have a feeling it won’t be adequate." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "He doesn’t owe anyone an explanation. That is how you make a name for yourself in this business – by taking out the top guys." As his music dies down, we see Violence in the center of the ring, smirking. He holds a microphone up. JOHNNY VIOLENCE: "All week long, I’ve been harassed on Twitter by you people demanding answers for what I did. But for those of you who missed the action, let’s take one more look." He points to the video screen near the ramp. The tape begins to roll as we see Johnny Violence systematically demolish CDE and Lynch. The video ends and we cut back to the ring with the boos louder than ever. JOHNNY VIOLENCE: "I was standing backstage last week during the opening segment, fresh off of my win in the open battle royal, and I saw those two men come out and claim that they are the rightful champions. I thought to myself how stupid it was that they thought they were worthy of the belt, and I was offended that those clowns were invited back to Galveston Island Wrestling when I had to go through the process like a beginner again." While some in the crowd may believe he has a point, the majority of them continue to boo loudly. JOHNNY VIOLENCE: "If you don’t remember, I was a main eventer back in this company a few years ago. I nearly won the Undisputed Championship, but every step of the way I had to fight the management. When I left, it was like everyone here forgot about me. Well, that was a mistake. Because I am back, and I will be just as dominant as I was when I was here the first time." Violence pauses a moment, then continues. JOHNNY VIOLENCE: "For Lynch and Erns, I would love to say that it wasn’t personal. But this is personal. Because you two are being placed back in the main event, and for what? Why are you better suited to be a champion than I am? Why didn’t either of you bring up my name? I was better than you then, and I am better than you now. I’m not going to stop until everyone out there realizes that I am the only man in this company who deserves to be champion." Violence points to himself, stirring the pot. JOHNNY VIOLENCE: "I will prove that next week at Seize the Moment. All it takes is a few rungs and the length of an arm, then I get what I deserve – the GIW Undisputed Championship. But this week, I have a chance to finish what I started last week. Michael Lynch will be taken out and Christopher Darren Erns will be next." JOHNNY VIOLENCE: "This run will not end like the last one. When I leave Galveston the next time, I will leave as Undisputed Champion. Mark my words." Christopher Darren Erns’ music hit and he made his way onto the ramp to loud cheers from the fans. It is clear that they are firmly behind him tonight. CHRISTOPHER DARREN ERNS: "Johnny – You can come out here and talk a big game. Hell, you can keep attacking us by surprise – but that doesn’t make you a main eventer." Erns is standing near the apron now, on the floor outside the ring. CHRISTOPHER DARREN ERNS: "I hope you win Seize the Moment next week. I hope you become Undisputed Champion. I will be rooting for you, because once you win the belt, that means I am eligible to hold it again. I would love nothing more than beating you to regain the title I never lost." Violence moves closer to the ropes. JOHNNY VIOLENCE: "Yeah, yeah... you never lost the belt. You’re absolutely right about that Christopher, but what you don’t mention is that the company wasn’t able to secure a TV deal with you as the face. If you were really worth it, Aspen would have put the strap right back around your waist, but you aren’t. You aren’t worth it man – and Aspen knows it." More boos from the crowd. Erns nods, looking to the fans. CHRISTOPHER DARREN ERNS: "If that’s the claim you want to run with, fine. The company did go off the air during my reign, but that wasn’t my problem. I think the fans enjoyed seeing a man who didn’t have to resort to cheap attacks as their champion." JOHNNY VIOLENCE: "Cheap attacks? I did what I did because no one in this company had any respect for me. But guess what, now I’m opening the show. Now everyone knows my name. And you? Well, you’re just the guy who got jumped." Loud boos from the crowd force Violence to stop for a moment. JOHNNY VIOLENCE: "I’m trying to help you Christopher. I want what is best for GIW -" Erns is quick to interrupt. CHRISTOPHER DARREN ERNS: "You what was best for yourself." Violence replies quickly. JOHNNY VIOLENCE: "In this case, what is best for GIW is also best for me -" Erns steps onto the ring, on the other side of the ropes. CHRISTOPHER DARREN ERNS: "No Johnny. The decision you made last week, that wasn’t best for you." Suddenly the lights go dark. When they pop back up, Michael Lynch has appeared from out of nowhere to be right behind Johnny Violence. It takes Violence a moment to realize that Lynch his behind him, but when he does, he turns quickly and sent to the floor with a stiff right hand by Lynch. From there, Lynch picks Violence up and holds him from behind as CDE enters the ring. Erns is about to throw his first punch when the voice of Devlynn Smith stops him in his tracks. DEVLYNN SMITH: "Hey whoa whoa… Michael, what are you doing?" Lynch freezes, staring at Devlynn, but he maintains a tight hold on Violence. Devlynn continues to make his way down to the ring as CDE turns to watch him. DEVLYNN SMITH: "Michael, I was listening to what Johnny Violence had to say about making a statement, and I thought it was interesting. A man like you, capable of the things you are capable of… chaining yourself to a man like Christopher Darren Erns? I think that’s a waste." Devlynn stops before he enters the ring as the situation inside remains static. DEVLYNN SMITH: "Nah... You’re a ruthless fu*ker. I’ve seen it Michael. I know you live for more than just keeping CDE afloat here. You wanna hurt people, just like me. You wanna win titles, just like me. You are just like me man. Open your eyes…" Lynch grabbed Violence’s microphone and picked it up, still holding Violence. MICHAEL LYNCH: "You need to leave or you’ll get it just like Violence will." Devlynn couldn’t help but laugh. DEVLYNN SMITH: "What are you gonna do? Tickle him to death? Or are you just gonna hold him while CDE does the work. If you want to hurt him… HURT HIM." Lynch was screaming now. MICHAEL LYNCH: "GET. OUT." Erns was screaming at Devlynn from inside the ring as Devlynn came closer and closer. Lynch was fuming, but he wouldn’t let Violence go. DEVLYNN SMITH: "Do you want me to show you how to hurt again, Michael?" Devlynn hopped onto the apron and was quickly met by CDE. Devlynn jumped off, then spoke directly to Lynch. DEVLYNN SMITH: "Call off your puppy or I’ll have to change my plan!" Lynch screamed: MICHAEL LYNCH: "GET. OUT." Johnny Violence broke out of the hold and landed a hard elbow to Lynch before CDE could see what was happening. CDE turns around and was met by a huge clothesline by Violence before Violence slid out of the ring to join Smith outside. JOHNNY VIOLENCE: “You had your chance Erns! That’s the only one you’ll get!" Devlynn nodded. DEVLYNN SMITH: "You made your choice Lynch. Now you’ll have to live with it!" Devlynn’s music began to play as Lynch and CDE were getting to their feet. Out of rage, Lynch pushed Erns to the ground as the fans were in awe. The crowd Ooo's as Erns looks up at Lynch who is seething at the mouth with anger. There was a tense moment between the two allies as Erns looks in shock. Lynch starts to shake his head, trying to get the voices out. MICHAEL LYNCH: "I’m sorry. I’m sorry…" Lynch pulled CDE up, but CDE was still noticebly surprised by the action. Off mic, we can see Lynch apologizing over and over to CDE as we cut to the announce booth. RILEY STEVENS: "CDE doesn’t seem to know what to think." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "There is a monster inside both of these men, the question is, will Violence and Devlynn be able to bring them out." RILEY STEVENS: "Why would they want to?" OLIVER THIBODAUX: "I dunno Riley, but it looks like that is where this is going." RILEY STEVENS: "While we try to make sense out of all that just happened, let’s cut to commercial – when we come back we will see Driver and Acquin take on Averell and Stark." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "You definitely don’t want to miss the tag team clinic that Stark and Averell will put on tonight. Those guys work so well together." Fade to commercial. Alex is able to catch his breath a little and it’s him who is now on the attack. He connects with a few clothesline that catch Alexis off guard. Alexis pops back up and is dropped with a “Starlight Suplex”, Alex’s version of the fishermen suplex. Before Alex can get the pin however Sinclair pulls Alexis out of the ring by the leg. He frantically checks on his client which only draws more heat from the crowd. You can tell Alex is growing frustrated and decides to take matters into his own hand by connecting with a plancha over the top rope! Sinclair goes down and he grazes Alexis as well who was still recovering. The fans show a little appreciation for the high risk move. He has Alexis and throws her into the barricade at ringside. He picks her up and drops her on the railing now. Alex is on fire! He rolls her back into the ring and we get are first nearfall of the match. Alexis is able to get a shoulder up at two. Alex now pin points Alexis’ legs and drops a few stomps on her right one using the ropes for leverage. He drags her over to the corner and places her between the ringpost. He rolls to the outside and smashes her right leg across the ringpost…not once…not twice…but three times before the referee forces him back into the ring. Alex has her leg now and applies a leg-lock grapevine and Alexis is screaming in pain! Alexis however is able to grab the bottom rope quickly. RILEY STEVENS: "She probably just saved herself!" Sinclair is back up now and is on the apron. The referee tells him to get off but Sinclair is complaining about Alexis and his earlier attack. With the referee preoccupied Alexis is able to connect with a low blow to the groin of Alex. Alex is down now and in pain and Alexis hooks the leg. Sinclair points that Alexis has a cover…1…2..No! Alex with a kickout. Alexis screams in frustration! How can this be? She mounts Alex and throws some stiff forearms as the referee begins to count for her to break..1…2…3…4..and finally right before 5 she rolls off. Her leg is sore and clearly in pain as she is forced to use the ropes to pull herself up. She crouches in the corner and waits for Alex to get to his feet. Groggy and on wobbly legs Alex stands and turns - CHAMPAGNE SUPERNOVA wait no! Alex ducks. Alexis is up…GOLDEN ARROW…but she ducks! Alexis connects with a super-kick of her own! Nether Alex or Alexis are favorites but the sheer evenness of the match has forced the fans to lose control and get into it. Alexis and Alex are back up and they both throw haymakers. Alex ducks another super-kick try by Alexis and rolls her up…1…2…Alexis rolls through but Alex hits a nice flapjack and floats over into a Boston Crab! Alexis has nowhere to go! Alex has had enough and connects with The Golden Arrow which causes Sinclair to fall off the apron into the barricade. He begins to check her arm now…dropping it once and making sure the timekeeper knows that this could maybe be over. He drops it a second time and Alexis seems lifeless. One more time now but…yes! Alexis shows a bit of life and is scratching and clawing frantically trying to wiggle out. Finally a questionable poke of the eye does the trick and she is free grasping for air. She hooks herself to the bottom rope and is clinging on for dear life. Alex tries to pull her off by the leg but Alexis kicks and kicks some more. One last pull by Alex and it forces her to let go. WINNER: ALEX CASTELLANOS (VIA DQ) Blade Vega continues his assault as the bell sounds off again but Vega is ignoring the order to stop. The referee is now trying to pull Vega off of Alex but Vega turns and decks him with a right hand sending the referee to the floor. Meanwhile David Sinclair has entered the ring and is checking on Alexis who is in the corner trying to pull herself up using the ropes. Alex tries to fight back and even manages to get a few right hands in but the grueling match has taken to much out of him and Vega lays him out with a huge Gorilla Press Slam! The fans in Kauffman Arena are on their feet booing as Alexis is back to her feet and a smirk appears on her feet. It seems as if she has orchestrated this whole thing. The GIW Undisputed World Heavyweight Championship!” “Hit Me Like A Man” begins to play as Alexis lets out an over-the-top maniacal laugh as Sinclair begins to clap for her.
