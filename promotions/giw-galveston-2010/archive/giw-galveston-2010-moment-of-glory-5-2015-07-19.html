That’s the GIW Undisputed champion!” Alioth crosses Callum’s legs as he protests and then locks in the Four Star Mary on the ring post! SARAH SHARP: “ALIOTH WITH A CHRIS CROSS?!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “No! Not like this!” Callum is stunned as the crowd is on their feet going absolutely insane. Alioth makes the cover quickly. 1! 2! 3! NO!! Callum gets a shoulder up at the last possible second and Alioth has a little knowing smile on his face as he nods a couple of times. Once again Callum gets his shoulder up and Alioth just nods again, knowing what it will take to keep the champion down because he has been there before.
