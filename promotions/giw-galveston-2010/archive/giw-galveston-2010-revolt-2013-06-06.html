GIW — Card

Revolt, 6 June 2013

6 June 2013
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: THURSDAY NIGHT REVOLT LIVE! From North Beach – June 6, 2013 BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! Thursday Night Revolt EXPLODES onto the airwaves of Fox Sports Southwest. The fans go crazy as the purple fireworks stream into the Galveston sky over the Gulf of Mexico. The crowd of 400 are all on their feet as the camera pans around the ring and the seating area before it finally stops on the announce team of Riley Stevens, Oliver Thibodaux, and Amanda Rowland sitting at ringside – the best view in the house. RILEY STEVENS: “Greeting everyone and welcome to this week's episode of Revolt! After tonight, there will be only one more show before out first pay-per-view event, Aggressive Expansion, and with that card largely blank, tonight will be pivotal for some of the newer stars, and even some of the established ones, to solidify their case for a championship match.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “That's right Riley. Tonight, we have already crowned a new Deep South champion, and tonight we will crown a new North Beach champion.” AMANDA ROWLAND: “But Alioth Starre still does not have a challenger.” RILEY STEVENS: “That is true. As of now, our Undisputed Champion does not have a clear cut challenger for his title. Hopefully tonight, our questions will be resolved.” “Heaven's a Lie” hit the PA system and the crowd went wild as Alex Castellanos made his way down the GIW ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Well, this is the man who can answer our questions.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I doubt he will. The corporate figures like suspense.” AMANDA ROWLAND: “Alex isn't a corporate figure! He's the booker!” RILEY STEVENS: “You mean, the General Manager?” AMANDA ROWLAND: “Yeah. And he's really over!” RILEY STEVENS: “The fans sure do love him.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Alex is nothing more than a wrestler who can't wrestle anymore who decided to go and run a wrestling promotion.” Alex has since made his way into the ring. The music has faded out and the crowd died down. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “Last week on Revolt, in the main event no less, I watched helplessly in the back as the match I scheduled was foiled when Eliza Gray interfered and cost Alexis Terry her match against Alioth Starre. Now, I know that interfering has become so commonplace in wrestling as a whole these days that most of you just rolled your eyes and shrugged it off, but to me, to a guy that runs the company, to a guy who had advertized Alexis Terry vs. Alioth Starre for a whole week – when that main event gets ruined, I'm the one who looks like an ass. I'm the one who looks like he can't control the company. I'm the one who has to deal with sponsor after sponsor who wants their money back because the match they invested in was ruined by one little bi*ch with a bone to pick.” Alex steps back for a moment, then continues. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “It wasn't even ten minutes after that happened when I started getting calls. The Daily News called. Blue Bell. Texas A&M every single sponsor. But then the network called. Fox Sports Southwest called me personally and told me that if I didn't deliver a main event as planned this week, that there would be repercussions.” The crowd was silent. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “Starting this week, there will be no more backstage beat downs. No more run ins. No more brass knuckles – Joshua Acquin. Starting this week, no one will be allowed out of their locker room area unless they are escorted by a uniformed GIW employee. There will be no roaming. There will be making friends. To everyone in the back – remember that this is a business. We are here to work, not play.” Alex turns toward the entrance ramp. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “Because here is the honest truth. Blake Beckett is gone. No one knows where he is. He told me that he would be here to help... to supply us with some money... connections... but since he is gone... we don't have as much as we used to have. We don't have Blake's trust fund anymore. Every sponsor, every iPPV buy, every TV deal – now it is more crucial than ever before. I didn't ask for this responsibility. When I signed up for this job, I was told I would just be making the matches and putting on a good show, but fate put me in the position I'm in now. I have Sara helping me, but otherwise, the pressure falls just on us. So – for all you wrestlers in the back that think having a wrestling job is something you just get – let me remind you that if you mess this up, GIW will flop like any other indy company. We are not special. You are not special back there in the back.” Alex turns to the crowd now. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “GIW has the best shows, the best roster, the best matches, the best fans – and I will be damned if I'm going to let that all go to waste because our wrestlers are out of control. Starting this week, if any rules are broken, fines or suspensions will be handed out. If the act is too egregious, I will not hesitate to terminate you.” An audible 'ooooh' from the crowd. “Heaven's a Lie” fades up slowly, but Alex stays in the ring a bit longer, looking down, pondering something. He finally exits as Oliver, Riley, and Amanda are slowly heard over the music. RILEY STEVENS: “Alex Castellanos is not in the mood for jokes tonight.” AMANDA ROWLAND: “I think this is good for the company. People need to stop cheating.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I guess Alex just wants us to forget when he laid down for Aspen in that Sole Survivor match. I bet the sponsors didn't like that one either.” RILEY STEVENS: “Well, the rules have been set in stone now. Let's see if the wrestlers will abide by them. But for now, lets head to a commercial break. When we come back, we will be ready for the first match of the evening. A three way bout between Gabriel Lauren, the fiery Zerotine Valentino, and the grizzled veteran Joshua Acquin. And not meaning to tease or anything, but a former GIW champion will be on commentary for the match. Next, on Revolt!” The camera fades down on Alex, who has stopped in front of the curtain, looking back on the crowd. He is obviously upset about something. The black screen envelops him. QUOTE We are now ready for the first match of the evening and as we get it going already standing in the ring with Gabriel Lauren as the camera cuts over to Kacie Church at ringside, sitting at the announce table with a big smile on her face, the announce table looking a bit crowded tonight. RILEY STEVENS: “We are sitting here with former GIW star Kacie Church sitting in to watch her friend Acquin’s match tonight.” KACIE CHURCH: “Sure am, he’s going to crush some people tonight.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Who the hell are you?” "Darker Side of Me" by The Veer Union hits and the fans start to boo as Joshua Acquin walks out from the back with a smirk on his face as he heads towards the ring, these fans not showing him much love at all as the longtime GIW Veteran, walks around the ring over to Kacie Church smirking and nodding to her, saying something that the camera doesn’t quite catch which draws a response from Kacie as his music fades and “Victimized” by Linkin Park hits and these fans boos only continue. RILEY STEVENS: “What are your thoughts on Zerotine, Kacie?” KACIE CHURCH: “Let me remind you Oliver, I am one of the legend’s of this company, so don’t pretend not to remember you. And Zerotine, Riley? Just a filler for Acquin’s match” Zerotine heads toward the ring shaking off the fan’s boos as one of GIW’s most interesting stars hits the ring, walking up the steps and into the ring fresh off her return costing Ryan Cavallari his match up. Valentino heads up into the ring and gets ready to go as the referee gets ready and then starts this triple threat match up. DING DING DING! It’s time for the first match up here tonight on Revolt featuring a longtime GIW veteran, a new GIW star, and the always…interesting to say the least Zerotine Valentino. These fans make up there mind quickly in this one and that’s that they are going to root for…nobody, booing anything that moves. Right off the bat Zerotine exits the ring, looking over at Kacie at the announce table as Acquin tries to watch only to end up in a fight with Lauren. Valentino walks over to Church, asking her who the hell she even is as the fans may be wondering the same thing. KACIE CHURCH: “Someone calm this girl down.” Kacie talking down to her seems to annoy Zerotine a bit as she shakes her head and proceeds to spit in her face out of no where, the fans not liking either of them so finding spit in either’s face to be something to cheer as Valentino backs away and heads around the ring as Church gets up angrily yelling at her, as the microphone is cut off for censorship reasons as well as speaker reasons, as Zerotine looks in the ring as Acquin has just seen what has happened, telling her to get in the ring so he can get some pay back for her friend but Zerotine shakes her head and mouths “I am better then this!” as she heads to the back, not seeming too worried about Acquin or Kacie following her as in the ring Lauren hits him hard enough just to really annoy him, as Joshua turns his attention smartly back to the ring, as Church settles back into her chair at ringside. KACIE CHURCH: “Who spits in people’s faces, I mean really?” In the ring Acquin’s strength and savvy is really evident as he takes down the young kid shaking his head, and grabbing him whipping him into the ropes and on the rebound he dives down at his knees and hit’s a Chop Block, chopping him down as he lets out a cry of pain, these fans feeling for him a bit as Acquin doesn’t seem to be in the giving mood tonight as Lauren rolls around on the ring in pain, perhaps really damaging his knee there as the referee tries to check on him but Acquin shoves him away then hoists him back up and drops him down with a Facebreaker Knee Smash, as the new star falls backwards struggling to keep his balance, using the ropes to do so. But unable to protect himself Acquin walks right up to him and clubs him with a shot then spins him over walking to the center of the ring to a land a ‘Facebreaker’ his version of the Kill Switch. Lauren is down and out, unable to really move as Acquin goes for a pin 1…2…But Acquin himself shakes his head and pulls up his shoulder, instead dropping down and grabbing a hold of his legs and turning him over into a Sharpshooter, a move that Lauren immediately taps to. REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner by way of pinfall…Joshua Acquin!” Acquin keeps the maneuver held on for a little bit too long as the referee tries to pry him off and finally he breaks the hold, perhaps having done some serious damage to his foe tonight as the referee goes to check on Lauren. KACIE CHURCH: “As I said, a win! Was nice being here, just need to get your rat problem fixed.” RILEY STEVENS: “That was Kacie Church here tonight to watch Acquin, who really dismantled Lauren after Valentino well just…left.” Acquin rolls out of the ring as Church gets up from the announce table; Acquin asks her about the spitting incident to which she shrugs off as the fans boo the two of them as they head around the ring to the back together and we are sent to the backstage area. WINNER: JOSHUA ACQUIN The camera cuts over to the Motel 8, right across from Porretto Beach where Revolt is currently underway. The room is dingy, but it was all that GIW had in place of locker rooms for now. This particular locker room was shared by Devlynn Smith, the new GIW Deep South Champion, and Nathan Starr, a man who he had defeated earlier in the night. They sit on opposite sides of the room, still in their ring gear, when there is a knock on the door. NATHAN STARR: “I'll get it.” DEVLYNN SMITH: “How about not? Why don't you just sit down and nurse your wounds.” Starr rolls his eyes and waves off Devlynn, choosing this time not to be confrontational. Devlynn makes his way over to the door an opens it. Outside is Airica Lynn. DEVLYNN SMITH: “Oh Jesus. I didn't call for a hooker.” She quickly throws him the finger. He smirks, looking her up and down. AIRICA LYNN: “I'm here to talk to Nathan, creep.” Devlynn turns to Nathan, smiling. DEVLYNN SMITH: “You call for her, jobber boy?” Nathan gets up from the chair and pushes Devlynn aside. NATHAN STARR: “She's my tag team partner. And my... well... my partner.” Airica's eyes got wide. AIRICA LYNN: “I just wanted to talk to you.” Nathan was obviously uncomfortable. NATHAN STARR: “About tonight, or about something else?” AIRICA LYNN: “Both.” Devlynn started to close the door, but Starr stopped him. AIRICA LYNN: “He called me a whore, Nathan.” DEVLYNN SMITH: “Well your hoochie outfit had me convinced.” Nathan, in a fit of rage, pushed Devlynn against the hotel wall. Devlynn raised his hands in the air, amused by the outburst of anger.” NATHAN STARR: “Stop talking to her like that.” DEVLYNN SMITH: “Ah... I thought she was just your tag team partner?” Nathan let him go. DEVLYNN SMITH: “Sure hope Alex doesn't see her out of her locker room.” Nathan turned to Airica. NATHAN STARR: “You need to jet. This isn't the time.” Airica nodded, then closed the door. Nathan turned to Devlynn. NATHAN STARR: “Look, congrats on your title win, but you're a joke. You may have debuted in style, but you won't ever be able to outshine me. Not now, not ever. Got that?” Devlynn patted him on the shoulder. There was another knock on the door. This time a man yelled from the other side 'I'm here to escort Devlynn.' DEVLYNN SMITH: “We'll see about that pretty boy. I've got one win over you so far, I'm sure I'll have plenty more in the future. If you'll excuse me, this man is here to escort me to my photo shoot. As Deep South Champion. I'll let you know how it is.” He leaves Starr in the room alone. We fade to a commercial break. QUOTE "Tokyo Takeaway" by Paul Osborne hits and these fans begin to cheer on Saitou Taguchi as he heads out from the back with a smile on his face heading towards the ring, as these fans seem to be getting behind mostly just because he is facing Alexis but also seeming to be getting on his side, as he’s grown on them a bit. The Japanese star heads up the steps and into the ring as these fans continue to cheer. RILEY STEVENS: “Saitou won the Open Battle Royal last week and this week gets a big step up against a former Undisputed Champion, however he also has a chance to really make a name for himself.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Jackie Chan!” Despite Oliver’s racism Saitou poses on the top rope as "Killin' It" by Krewella hits and the fans start to boo Alexis Terry as she heads out from the back with a big smirk on her face riding high after a huge week last week after nearly winning the Undisputed belt and then also defeating long time rival Aspen Chaud. She heads into the ring ignoring the fans hatred, looking across at Saitou probably severely under-estimating him. AMANDA ROWLAND: “She looks pretty tonight.” RILEY STEVENS: “Thank God we have you Amanda, as Alexis enters the ring looking to build on her success from last week having defeated Aspen Chaud.” Alexis steps up into the ring and walks over to the corner across from her up and coming opponent, shaking her head as the referee gets things squared up then calls for the bell. DING DING DING! Alexis is leaning into the corner with a smirk on her face. She eyes up Saitou and looks rather unpleased with her opponent this week. The bell sounds off and Alexis continues to simply lean in the corner, a rather overconfident smug look on her face. Saitou takes center ring and is ready to fight, signaling for Alexis to “bring it”. She finally pushes herself from her corner and walks right up to her opponent. “Do you know who I am?” she screams right into his face. “Walk away before I embarrass you!” she shouts once more. Finally, Taguchi has head enough and slaps her right across the face hard much to the delight of the fans. He follows it up with a hard kick to her gut that leads straight into a snap suplex. He quickly covers but Alexis survives and is able to kick out at 2. She kicks away from Saitou and crawls back into the corner, a surprised and astonished look upon her face. Saitou readies himself in the pounce position ready to strike as the newcomer has the Galveston faithful fully behind him, impressing them with his discipline and no nonsense attitude. RILEY STEVENS: “Think Saitou can get the victory here?” Alexis slowly pulls herself to her feet using the ropes. Saitou Taguchi rushes but Alexis screams for the ref to hold him back and allow her to ready herself. The crowd boos as the ref steps between the two. Alexis brushes herself off and struts to the middle of the ring like nothing happened. She does a little bow, mocking Taguchi who doesn’t take to kindly to it. As Alexis is in the bowed position he kicks her square in the face. The force from the blow sends her backwards in a matrix Esq. position. Saitou does a double foot stomp into the stomach of Alexis knocking the wind out of her. Alexis rolls around the ring in pain holding her chest trying to catch her breath. The smart Saitou takes notice and quickly applies an Indian Death Lock. Alexis is now virtually screaming at the top of her lungs and the fans that have been mistreated by the British brat for so long are eating it up. Alexis looks like she is about to tap but keeps fighting through the pain. She is able to scoot on her butt towards the bottom rope and hold on for dear life. The ref reaches a 4 count before Saitou lets go of the hold. RILEY STEVENS: “Got to do anything to pick up wins here in GIW.” This time he picks Alexis up by her hair but Alexis is able to counter whatever motive he had with a thumb to the eye. She continues her assault with stiff forearm shots. Alexis finishes off the furrier with a huge European Uppercut. Saitou stumbles backwards into the ropes. Alexis Irish whips him into the opposite side of the ropes and as he comes crashing back to her she nails a huge knee into the gut followed by a snap DDT. Alexis quickly covers but only gets a two count. Enraged by the amount of effort it is taken to put Saitou Taguchi away she mounts him and begins to connect with right hand after right hand until the referee has to physically pull her off. The fans boo loudly but Alexis simply screams for them to shut up. Taguchi is back to one knee and Alexis quickly connects with a shining wizard. She once again covers but only is able to keep him down for two. Alexis begins to ascend the turnbuckle and is now on the top rope waiting for her him to stagger back to his feet. He is back up and Alexis leaps off doing her best to connect with a flying cross body but wait, Saitou counters it with a huge dropkick! RILEY STEVENS: “This might be his chance!” He crawls over and is able to toss one arm over her. 1! 2! No! Alexis kicks out. The crowd thought it might have been over there! The pound on the guardrail and stomp their feet, reeling Saitou Taguchi back to his feet. Alexis stumbles up and backwards right into the arms of her opponent. He tosses her backwards and over his head, a picture perfect German Suplex but Alexis lands on her feet! He turns around and is rocked by a huge spinning wheel kick. Alexis runs to the corner and hops all to the top of the turnbuckle. MOST GLAMOROUS MOONSAULT EVER! She hits it perfectly. ONE…TWO…THREE! Alexis picks up the win! REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner by way of pinfall…Alexis Terry!” These fans are upset as Alexis Terry succeeds tonight having won her contest, walking away with another big here in GIW, as though they may not like her these fans have to realize that yet again Terry has proven herself one of the top star here in GIW, as the referee raises her arm. RILEY STEVENS: “A good effort by Saitou but in the end, Terry’s veteran savvy and talent pulls her through.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “He didn’t see it coming.” Terry heads out of the ring as Saitou gathers himself in the ring, upset with himself for the loss tonight as Alexis flips and heads to the back with one arm high. WINNER: ALEXIS TERRY After Alexis Terry's victory, the camera cuts to Alex Castellanos' office, though instead of him in the chair, his assistant GM Sarah Sharp takes his spot. She sits with her legs crossed, staring across the table at Michael Lynch and Darian, the Great. SARAH SHARP: “I called you both in today because you had a tie in the open battle royal -” DARIAN, THE GREAT: “Yeah, we figured that.” Sarah was taken aback by the interjection. SARAH SHARP: “Well, I'm glad you have such incredible senses.” Darian rolls his eyes. Lynch sits silently. SARAH SHARP: “I wanted to tell you two that I was extremely impressed by your work this week -” MICHAEL LYNCH: “It isn't your words that matter. I want to hear it from Alex.” Sarah leans back in her chair, offended. SARAH SHARP: “Excuse me?” DARIAN, THE GREAT: “Why are we meeting with you, and not Alex? Why do we get the number two girl and not the number one guy?” SARAH SHARP: “Well- I ...” MICHAEL LYNCH: “Well?” Sarah stands. SARAH SHARP: “I was going to give you both a shot at the North Beach title. On a pay per view no less! But now, you two are going to have to fight for it. One on one. Next week on Revolt. The winner will get a shot at the North Beach championship at Aggressive Expansion. Now get out.” She points a finger towards the door; the two men leave. Ready to fight now. Fade to commercial break. DING DING DING! New Champion. REV.
