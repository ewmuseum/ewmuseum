GIW — Card

Revolt, 17 February 2011

17 February 2011

GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: THURSDAY NIGHT REVOLT! LIVE FROM THE SEAWALL BOULEVARD ENTERTAIMENT COMPLEX = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! As the show starts the world gets a view of the newly built Seawall Boulevard Entertainment Complex as we pan around hitting just about every single area of the arena. The fans in the arena numbering up to about seven hundred on all three stories of seating. Finally we come to Riley and Oliver both with big grins ready to call the night’s action. RILEY STEVENS: “We are here in our recently christened GIW arena known as the Seawall Boulevard Entertainment Complex! There is a very great feeling in the air tonight, right Oliver?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Personally I just smell your B.O. but yeah that to,” RILEY STEVENS: “Should have expected that. None the less tonight, may as well be called a Super-Show with the card we are bringing you here tonight. First on deck is a match featuring Jak Hendricks who ended his feud with Seth Seeker with a win at ‘Exile’ against the recently unretired Matt Kluge, what a contest that’ll be.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Shame Kluge has to find out the hard way that there’s a new man in the business and his name is Jak Hendricks.” RILEY STEVENS: “Following that will be a triple threat match between Wake Walker, Rob Gamble, and Ash Aiken in what could have contendership on the line, that is if they even show up. Then we’ll see the first of two unification bouts as ‘GIW NOX Champion’ Madison Seton versus ‘GIW North Beach Champion’ Joshua Acquin, talk about a match.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Personally I could care less about that one, when our next match features Sparrow and Jericho.” RILEY STEVENS: “Yup as he said the ‘GIW Deep South Champion’ Eric Sparrow against ‘Galveston Island Champion’ Chris Jericho to become the NEW GIW Undisputed Champion, and speak of the devil.” Just as they are talking about them “Break the Walls Down” echoes through the new complex as out from the back Chris Jericho immediately makes a bee line to the ring, the belt around his waist holding a microphone, as he stops near the ramp area, looking out at the crowd deciding to address them from right here. CHRIS JERICHO: “You fools continue to doubt the fact that I am truly the best this pathetic island has to offer. I don’t have to be here, I choose to be here. You continue to call me washed up, continue to say that I am done when in reality you all know that I am just as lethal as always. Do you really think unifying belts is anything new to me? I mean do you think I can do it?” The crowd boos showing just where they stand on the subject as Jericho walks across the side to where a little boy is being held up by his father, he walks over right in his face and screaming. CHRIS JERICHO: “DO YOU THINK I CAN DO IT?” To his surprise the kid shakes his head, as he continues on to the next fan who is a cocky little middle aged man with his friends, who screams out that Jericho sucks. CHRIS JERICHO: “You want to be a tough guy?!” Jericho grabs him by the collar, causing the man to go a little stiff as suddenly the crowd pops. As Jericho lines up his fist, out from the back runs Eric Sparrow heading right for Chris. Jericho immediately drops the mic then slides into the ring and then back out of it, escaping as Sparrow stops by the fan he just saved as he surprisingly gets a loud ovation from the crowd. Eric reaches down grabbing the microphone as Jericho backs his way up the ramp. ERIC SPARROW: “Hey Jericho! Why are you out here picking on these people, when you have a match with me later on tonight? Seems to me like you are a little over confident. Just because you got one, lucky win does not mean you’re not washed up. I hope you bring your best, but unfortunately it won’t be enough. One way or another Eric Sparrow is walking out of here, with the GIW Undisputed Championship!” Sparrow tosses the microphone down as Jericho shakes his head quickly, Sparrow leans over shaking the man’s hand as we take a commercial break.[/font] QUOTE Just as we come back “Forever” by Drake, Kanye West, Lil Wayne, and Eminem hits as the fans burst into boos. Out from the back with a confident swagger is Jak Hendricks, heading towards the ring with that always present confident look on his face. As he ignores the fans, he heads to the ring seeming none to worried about his opponent here tonight. RILEY STEVENS: “This man was able to defeat Seth Seeker back at ‘Exile’ in what many people thought was a shock.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “When you dominate a guy, is it really shocking? He killed him! He’s the future in this company.” Just as he enters the ring, “Battle Metal” by Turisas now hits as the fans take only a second to realize who it is, popping as out from the back comes Matt Kluge with Scott Rydell not far behind him. He flips off the hood of his jacket as the fans pop as he high fives a few of them, as everyone seems equally excited by his return. RILEY STEVENS: “While you may think Jak is the future, this man still has it.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “He’s old; Jak will make an example out of him.” Rydell takes his place at ringside as Matt walks up the steps and then heads right into the ring, looking across at Jak who still has a big smirk on his face. Kluge heads up top rope where he holds up his arms, before jumping down and stretching out as the referee calls for the bell. DING DING DING! The fans are ready to go, this night an exciting one. Kluge offers a hand shake, however Jak slaps it away showing just how this match will go. They go in for a lock up, which Matt seems to be winning. He backs him up, but Jak smartly turns him around right as they are near the corner, allowing him to deliver some blows while Kluge is trapped. The fans dislike this and show it with their boos, but Matt is able to deliver a kick that sends Hendricks stumbling back. He explodes forward tackling Jak down to the mat where he mounts him and starts to go wild with punches. The fans go wild with him, before Jak is able to push him off as both men scramble to their feet. It’s Kluge that is up first and is able to grab him with a headlock. He wrenches on it for a second before being shoved forward and off the ropes. Kluge hits the ropes and comes back, running right into a big knee to the gut from Jak. The fans boo as he grabs Kluge and lifts him, and then crushes him with a Lariat! The fans boo as he goes for the quick cover 1…Kick out not even close to taking him down yet. This brings a smile to a few fans faces, as Jak quickly lifts him up and Irish whips him into the corner. He takes a second before running forward at him, going for a Clothesline but Matt is able to duck out of the way and school boy him 1…2…Kick Out! It was close but it’s Jak that is able to get up quicker as he delivers a big dropkick to Matt that sends him falling into a seated position in the corner. Sitting there he seems in a bad way as Hendricks runs forward with a big dropkick to his chest. The fans boo as Jak makes the cover 1…2…Kick Out. Not much urgency from either man, as they get to their feet almost starting the match again as they begin to circle around each other. RILEY STEVENS: “What a back forth contest this has been here tonight.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Come on Jak’.” The fans have started a Kluge chant as the two go after each other again but its Hendricks that is able to get the advantage of this lock up, as he turns it right into a headlock. Jak has it tight as he tugs on it, as it seems Kluge may just be losing a bit of his consciousness and he’s fading fast. On the outside Rydell starts a slow clap that the fans quickly join in on. Almost immediately Kluge regains some life, suddenly lifting Jak into the air and hurling him over his shoulder! The fans go wild as Jak holds his back in pain, while still getting up. Matt calls for him to come forward where he walks right into a quick Snap Suplex. The fans cheer as Kluge gets up, slapping his chest as he is getting pumped up. They now have started to chant his name again, as he stalks Jak waiting for him to get up. He seems to have something big in mind as he grabs him around the waist, wanting to go for a German Suplex but Hendricks catches him with a backwards elbow that stuns Matt a bit. He stumbles back and now a recovered Jak comes forward with the ‘Jak Attack’ a variety of punches and kicks that cause the fan some worry. BOOM! He finishes him off with a Yakuza Kick that sends Kluge to the mat. Rather then pin him, he grabs his legs and starts to turn it into the Sharpshooter the same move that made Seeker tap out. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “It’s OVER!” Jak tries to turn him into it, but Kluge uses all his leg power to suddenly spin out of it! The fans go wild as he gets to his feet right as Jak does the same. He comes forward going for a very illegal low blow kick, however Kluge catches the leg and slaps him with the other hand. With him momentarily stunned Matt hooks both arms as the fans go insane. He lifts him up and nails him with ‘The Tiger's Roar’ a Double Underhook Piledriver that seems to nearly kill Jak! The fans go wild, as he hooks the leg 1…2…3! That’s it. RILEY STEVENS: “What a win here tonight by Matt Kluge, but no disrespect for Jak Hendricks whom you have to admit did great.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Even a broken clock is right twice a day, this is a shame!” The fans are cheering, as Rydell gives him a big round of applause from the outside. The referee raises his arm in victory, as he turns around and attempts to help up Jak Hendricks. Immediately Hendricks shoves him away, heading right out of the ring angrily towards the back still holding his head seeming dazed as Matt seems a bit offended by the lack of respect but doesn’t seem all that surprised as we go to the back. WINNER: MATT KLUGE As we return to the backstage area, we see Marcus Hister sitting alone, off to the side in the catering area. Suddenly there is the sound of foot steps as standing in front of him now is Aspen Chaud, smiling down at him. ASPEN CHAUD: “Hey there, thought I told you to make some friends? Didn’t I.” Aspen smiles, clearly joking around with him pretending to be stern with the remarks as he looks up at her staying quiet. ASPEN CHAUD: “Listen, I have to be going to do…something with Justin a moment. I just wanted to see if you were making any progress. I mean all you have to do, is do one nice thing for people and more likely then not you will have made a friend.” Aspen pats him on the shoulder as he clearly takes in what she’s saying as suddenly there is a loud commotion, that causes the cameraman to head around the corner. Just like we saw Jake Avery attacked at ‘Exile’ we see Wake Walker lying there on the floor in a big heap. There is a few EMT’s around him checking on him, as everyone wonders just what happened here tonight and if it had any relation to the Avery attack as we go to break. QUOTE When things return we find Ash Aiken and Rob Gamble in the ring, seeming prepared to get ready to go. Walker’s attack leaves him a no show for this match as the referee calls for the bell. DING DING DING! As soon as the match starts the two go right after each other with a lock up. The fans are ready to go when all of the sudden "To Be Loved" by Papa Roach hits as the fans are utterly confused about what High Voltage could even be doing out here. As they enter the building, the fans boo them a few of them still showing support for Aspen who walks with Justin as they head to the ring. Both Gamble and Aiken stop the match, confused as they watch as Volt and Chaud head into the ring. Justin smirks looking over at Aspen, then suddenly crushing Gamble with a Superkick! The fans boo as he turns his attention right away to Aiken, instead offering him a hand shake to the fans surprise. Aiken seems to happily accept the offer but gets pulled right up into ‘The Justin Concussion’ a Spinning Brainbuster! RILEY STEVENS: “What is this for? They ruined a perfectly good match!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “It’s called not caring about anyone.” Justin stands over Aiken as the referee rings the bell, with a smirk having done all of this with his Tag Team title around his waist, matching that of Aspen’s who is a few feet away. She has a microphone and hands another one to Justin. JUSTIN VOLT: “At ‘Exile’ the two of us stole the show! We became the GIW Tag Team Champions and we became the two faces of this company. You best take a hard long look at the two of us in the ring tonight, because you are going to be seeing the two of us with these titles for a VERY long time. Now some of you may not like what we did. Some of you may still be in shock over the fact that Aspen was willing to go that far for me. But you see it’s that, that will continue to help our success. And it’s what makes us the most dominant tag team, in GIW…no…the world! Right babe.” Justin grins lowering his mic and looking over at Aspen who now raises the microphone up to her own mouth, seeming a whole lot less intimidating then Justin. ASPEN CHAUD: “You know what I’ve tried pleading my case this whole week. I tried telling people how much I love, how much you mean to me. But in the end, they just continued to call me a traitor. I didn’t betray anyone; I joined the man I love. And if I am going to be condemned for taking that side, then frankly I will stop caring about you people. All I need now is Justin and this belt.” Aspen slaps her belt and then looks back at Justin, both of whom listen as the fans boo her realizing that slowly but surely she’s turning on them for Volt. JUSTIN VOLT: “These two guys were just examples of how much power we have. Compared to us, they hold no relevance in this company. So here’s the deal tonight, we have a rematch with Perfect Combination because for some reason they don’t know when to stay down. And I am hearing that it’s for a title shot. Now while, beating them at ‘Exile’ was easy…tonight I figure that we will end it, even faster. You felt the virus, now let it finally give you your final breath.” The fans boo as he drops the microphone, as Aspen does the same. Together they leave the ring, while Ash as well as Rob struggle in the ring having been destroyed at the hands of Aspen and Justin. WINNER: NO CONTEST Tony is standing in the back, his head clutched in his hand and a clear look of pain on his face. Perfection’s Virus was once again working at full force, causing him great pain. He is sitting in a chair in the backstage area, a backstage worker walks to him, handing him a bottle of water, Tony says nothing, but simply nods in thanks as he takes the bottle of water. Tony unscrews the cap and takes a large drink. The water is incredibly soothing, cooling down his body and making some of the pain goes away. Tony starts to ease up, and shakes the rest of the pain away from his head. He stands up from the chair and begins to walk to his locker room to prepare for his match, when he bumps into Sarah Sharp. SARAH SHARP: “There you are. I’ve been looking for you. I had some questions for you about your match tonight with High Voltage.” Tony didn’t want to answer her questions, he wanted to shove her out of the way, but he knew that if he did she wouldn’t leave him alone. He sighed, giving in to her request. TONY MIRANDA: “Very well. Ask away.” SARAH SHARP: “Well, Tony. Last week at Exile you faced High Voltage for the GIW Tag Team Championships that were stripped away from you a month ago at Genesis. During that match, Aspen Chaud betrayed you, allowing High Voltage to win the match, which is now Justin Volt and Aspen Chaud herself. Tonight you get the chance for revenge in a non-title match tonight, what are your thoughts for this match?” Tony closes his eyes and lets a small sigh out. He pondered the question in his mind, slowly forming his answer. TONY MIRANDA: “Because of what happened last week, I haven’t been myself. I’ve been infected with what High Voltage calls “Perfection’s Virus”. Some people think they were only speaking words, but the mind is a powerful thing. They made their words real for me. I spend every waking morning drenched with sweat and filled with pain; I can’t think straight, I can’t even train without Perfection’s Virus effecting me. I know for a fact, Perfection’s Virus will effect me during the match, but I will not succumb to it. I will overcome this…and I will defeat High Voltage tonight.” Tony shakes his head, a bit of the pain returning. TONY MIRANDA: “Shadow and I…we may have lost at Exile…but I guarantee you that is going to be our only loss as a team here in GIW. We will show High Voltage they got one lucky win against us since we did not expect Aspen Chaud’s betrayal. We will not make that same mistake again. The Perfect Combination will be stronger, more violent, more destructive, and we will not let anyone, be they High Voltage, The Killers, or any other team here in GIW…defeat us again. We will regain our GIW Tag Team Championships.” Tony turns towards the camera, pointing into it. TONY MIRANDA: “Aspen, I know you’re watching. I know you’re listening to my words. Listen up and listen well…you will pay for what you did to me at Exile, all this pain, all this suffering that Perfection’s Virus has caused to me within the past few days I will return to you a thousand times over. Aspen, I am going to not only defeat you…I am not only going to destroy you…I AM GOING TO END YOU.” Tony walks away, leaving Sarah Sharp by herself. The scene goes to commercial. QUOTE As we return from commercial, Joshua Acquin and Madison Seton are already in the ring. They are both in their respective corners waiting for the match to begin. RILEY STEVENS: “One of these two stars will be the new GIW North Beach Champion.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Who cares?” As the bell rings they begin circling the ring. Acquin tries to lock-up with Seton, but she rolls past him, not allowing the chance. Acquin turns around, to see Madison quickly rise to her feet. Acquin charges towards Madison, trying to hit a clothesline, she ducks the move, and as Acquin hit’s the ropes and comes back, she hits him with a dropkick, knocking him down. Acquin quickly rises to his feet, and once again charges at Acquin, this time, she jumps into the air, and hits Acquin with a Hurricanrana. Madison quickly pinned, but barely got a one count, before Acquin pushed her off him. RILEY STEVENS: “Madison’s starting this match off very strong, but its going to take her a lot more than that to take down Acquin.” Acquin is the first to rise to his feet, waiting for Madison to get up. As she does, she turns around only to be hit with Acquin by a clothesline, knocking her down. She quickly gets up, Acquin grabs her and Irish Whips her into the ropes, as she comes back Acquin jumps into the air and hit’s a dropkick. As she crashes into the ground Acquin rises once more, he walks over towards Madison Seton and picks her up. He quickly turns her around and locks her in an Abdominal Stretch. Madison screams in pain as she tries to make it towards the ropes. After a good minute of being locked in the hold she grabs the ropes, forcing Acquin to break the hold. He lets go, and tries to pull her off of the ropes, but before he could Madison elbows Acquin in the face, he staggers backwards, clutching his face. Madison quickly tries to take advantage of the situation, and runs to hit Acquin with a cross body. However Acquin recovers from the elbow strike and catches Madison, and drops her with a Backbreaker. Acquin covers. 1... 2... Madison quickly kicks out after the two count. She clutches her back in pain, before quickly moving away from Acquin to try and regain her breath. RILEY STEVENS: “This has been a really even match so far…but I think Acquin’s backbreaker might be the turning point for him in this match.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I couldn‘t care less about who wins this match.” RILEY STEVENS: “You really can’t cheer for at least one of them?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Nope, I hate them both.” Acquin gets up, and sees Madison getting up using the ropes. Acquin charger and tries to hit a clothesline, Madison ducks, and pulls the ropes down causing Acquin to fall over the ropes and crash down onto the floor. Madison watches him as he slowly rises, holding his back in pain, she jumps over the ropes, hitting Acquin with a springboard cross body. 1! Madison slowly rises, picking up Acquin with her, she tries to Irish Whip him into the steel steps, but he reverses, whipping Madison into the steps. 2! Acquin backs up a little, before breaking into a run, he goes to hit a dropkick on Madison, to try and cause further damage to her by kicking her head into the steel steps. Madison however moves out of the way, and Acquin hit’s the steps. 3! RILEY STEVENS: “They need to stop this and get back into the ring.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “…” 4! Madison quickly rises, and begins stomping away at Acquin’s gut. Acquin grabs her foot around the fifth stomp and pushes her foot away, causing her to stagger back into the barricade. Acquin gets up and walks towards Madison, he goes to punch her, but she blocks the punch and counters with a sitout face buster. The referee’s count by this time had reached seven. RILEY STEVENS: “They need to hurry up, or else they’re going to get counted out!” 8! Madison rises, and quickly races to get into the ring, but before she enters the ring, Acquin grabs a hold of her foot, not letting her into the ring. 9! Madison stomps on Acquin’s face with her free foot a few times, forcing him to let go, she rushes towards the ring and slides in…but it’s too late! The referee rings for the bell only a half of a second after he had counted ten. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “HA HA HA! YES! THEY BOTH LOST!” RILEY STEVENS: “I can’t believe this, they both got counted out, what the hell is going to happen now?!” Everyone in the corner is confused, as Madison seems to ask for a explanation as we take a commercial break so all of this can be sorted out. WINNER: DOUBLE COUNTOUT The camera takes us to La’Sarah Audric’s office, where as we come into it we see Sarah Sharp entering the locker room in a hurry making a bee line for her desk, holding her mic with her. SARAH SHARP: “La’Sarah, did you see what just happened out there in the ring, can we get a comment from you on it?” Sarah holds the mic across the way to La’Sarah, who seems none to pleased about the situation that has transpired. LA’SARAH AUDRIC: “I saw it and frankly to me all it means is that those two were not worthy of being the champion. See the new belts, they just arrived and are over there in that box.” La’Sarah points at the big brown box, which the camera gets a site of only making things even more intriguing. LA’SARAH AUDRIC: “Frankly, neither of them won that match which means neither is champion. So while their belts are still merged into that one, the champion will not yet be named. Over the next few weeks, I personally will be scouting the talent to find someone worthy of the shot. As for tonight’s GIW Undisputed Championship match, Blake has decided that we are making it No Count Out’s so that a winner will be named one way or another. Now get out of here, I want to be the first one’s to see the new belts.” La’Sarah basically shoves her out of her office, grabbing up the box then shutting the door ending that little segment. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = We are then back in a different area of the backstage area this time, we are in the catering area where Scott Rydell and Matt Kluge are joined by a few members of staff all of them seeming to be having a good time. Suddenly some loud clapping can be heard, as Chris Jericho walks up behind them wearing that always big smirk of his. CHRIS JERICHO: “Good job! Really, man I mean it. You know I’ve heard a lot of good things about you. I’ve heard you’re a legend, a guy not much unlike myself. The only difference being, that if you asked ten people who we are, they’d all know me and we’d be lucky if even one of them know you.” Jericho seems to think his little remark, was rather insulting as Rydell and Kluge seem to look at one another shaking their heads a bit. SCOTT RYDELL: “You forgot to mention the fact that his hair designer isn’t blind and the fact that all ten of those people would also consider you a complete asshole.” Jericho who hadn’t really been giving Rydell much attention, turns to him patting his shoulder with a grin. CHRIS JERICHO: “You are not still bitter about the fact that I took you out of the Galveston Island Championship match are you? I mean I’m sure it is hard seeing me with the belt, isn’t it? Plus knowing that in perhaps, five minutes tops I will be the new GIW Undisputed Champion. I’m sure that leaves a bit of a bitter taste in your mouth, doesn’t it?” Chris finishes and it seems that his words may have struck a chord with Rydell, who stands up getting in his face. SCOTT RYDELL: “I don’t care what you do tonight, I’d be surprised if you won. But regardless if you are champion or not, me and you next week! One on one!” Rydell waits to hear what he says about the offer as Jericho smirks, walking by him. CHRIS JERICHO: “Then be ready to face the champion.” Jericho heads on his way to the ring, as Rydell angrily sits down as Matt Kluge pats him on the back and the group goes back on their way as the segment closes and we take a break before the next huge match. QUOTE As we return “Circles” by Incubus echoes through the arena as the fans go insane, cheering having pulled a complete one eighty with Eric Sparrow after the events of earlier tonight. Out he walks with the ‘GIW Deep South Championship’ over his shoulder, as he quickly heads towards the ring. The fans are clearly on his side here tonight, as he walks up the steps and then jumps over the ropes. RILEY STEVENS: “Could this man be the new Undisputed Champion?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I really thought he had a chance, and then I remembered who he was facing here tonight.” Sparrow throws up his title in the air for the last time as he hands the belt over to the referee as “Break the Walls Down” turns the crowd upside down as their cheers become boos at the drop a dime. Out from the back this time is Chris Jericho, proudly sporting his Galveston Island Champion around his waist. The fans jeer him as he heads towards the ring, showing the legend little respect. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “There is the legend himself, the man that is simply the best in the world.” Jericho enters the ring now handing the belt over to the referee as he stares across the ring at Sparrow as while we had a failed attempt at a Unification bout earlier in the night, the feeling now is that we all know this will not end in similar fashion. As the referee stands beside them, he holds up both belts in the air then hands them out and calls for the bell. DING DING DING! The fans are on the edge of their seat as the two men begin to circle one another, slowly walking around the ring. They both know what’s on the line here and then just like that they are in for a lock up, which Sparrow is able to spin out of with a hold of Jericho’s arm. He tugs on it harshly, but then Jericho counters and ends up behind Eric giving him a very similar treatment, wrenching his arm. The fans are basically silent, with a little buzz about them knowing this could end any second. Sparrow now spins out catching Jericho with a Hip Toss that causes the fans to pop for the first time here in this match. Eric runs forward attempting a quick leg drop, but Jericho rolls out of the way. Both men are up to their feet quickly, but Jericho is just a split second quicker and it allows him to whip him into the corner. The fans boo as he runs full steam at Sparrow catching him with a big Flying Forearm Smash. Sparrow stumbles forward a little stunned, as Chris takes advantage with a big Knife Edge Chop to the chest of Sparrow. Eric immediately holds his chest, as Jericho grabs him and whips him off the ropes giving him little time to recuperate. On the rebound Sparrow ducks under Jericho’s big clothesline attempt. Then off the rebound he comes full force forward and nails Chris with an amazing Dropkick that sends Jericho flying. The fans go wild as Sparrow gets right up, excited about what just happened as Chris rolls out of the ring holding his chest trying to take some time to recover. Eric looks at the fans nodding at them as he bounces off the ropes, then flipping over the top rope only for Chris to almost immediately get out of the way sending Eric to the outside, landing harshly. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “YES! He may not be the strongest and he may not be the fastest, but Jericho is damn sure the smartest wrestler in the business.” Chris has a big smirk on his face, as he slides into the ring and tells the referee to start counting. It’s clear he was playing possum and it seems to have worked perfectly. However the referee waves him off, remembering the words of La’Sarah Audric who made the ruling that there will be no Count Out’s in this one. He angrily exits the ring, grabbing Sparrow and tossing him into the ring. Sparrow attempts to get to his feet, still a little out of it from his fall. Chris runs at him from behind and slams him down with a big One Handed Bulldog! The fans boo as he makes the cover thinking he has it, 1…2…Kick Out! Not even close to enough to take out Sparrow yet. The fans are cheering Sparrow on as he gets to his feet, while Jericho waits. Jericho goes for a big European Uppecut, but Sparrow ducks backwards then comes forward immediately with a big punch, YEAH! Jericho responds with a Knife Edge Chop of his own, BOO! Sparrow comes forward with a big haymaker of a punch, YEAH! Jericho this time lands a big European Uppercut, BOO! In an act of desperation perhaps Sparrow lands a big Calf Kick, that drops Jericho to a kneel. He follows that up with the ‘Chow Moda’ a Swift Kick to a Kneeling Opponent. The fans are going insane as he makes the cover 1…Kick Out! The fans are a little shocked that Jericho kicked out of it as early as he did. Eric gets up, clearly a little tired but none the less ready to keep fighting. Jericho slowly starts to find his way to his feet, getting up slowly as Sparrow seems to be preparing for something big. When Jericho gets up he wraps him into a Guillotine Choke! The fans are going insane now thinking he can finish it off. He calls it the ‘Purple Haze’, and he has it locked in tightly falling to the mat with it. The fans are going crazy waiting for Jericho tap out. RILEY STEVENS: “Jericho has to tap out, he’s done for.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “NO! Come on Y2J!” The fans are waiting for that tap to signify the end, as Jericho seems to be fading. Suddenly the crowd is shocked as Chris is able to pry Sparrow’s arms apart and free his head. Both stars get to their feet but Sparrow runs right into a ‘Codebreaker’! The fans boo as Jericho falls backwards, to the mat. He slowly crawls to the pin, laying his arm over Sparrow to end this thing, 1…2…KICK OUT! The fans are as shocked as everyone else is. Jericho gets up, angrily pushing the referee unable to believe that this just happened. He turns around, realizing Eric has returned to his feet but he walks right into a Superkick that lays him out! The fans are going crazy as Sparrow immediately looks to the top rope. Carrying his body like its dead weight, he climbs to the top. The fans realize if it hits, he wins and if he misses he loses. Suddenly he goes airborne with the ‘Spinal Tap’ a Corkscrew Somersault! The fans go insane as he lands right on top of him, able to keep his body right on top of him, 1…2…3! RILEY STEVENS: “Eric Sparrow wins, Eric Sparrow wins!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Could this day be any worse?” The fans explode, right as the count hits three as the sudden fan favorite Eric Sparrow falls back unable to believe he has just won the match. The fans are going wild as he, holds up his arms in the air realizing he has one. As all this is happening, La’Sarah Audric enters the ring, carrying the new belt. Slowly she hands it to Sparrow then exits the ring, as he falls to his knees holding the belt tightly to his chest. RILEY STEVENS: “Eric Sparrow is now the top dog here in GIW! He edged out Madison Seton, then he pinned Chris Jericho and with these fans at his back he has reached the pinnacle becoming the GIW Undisputed Champion! What a match!” The fans continue to cheer as Sparrow, clenches the belt tightly to his chest, sliding out of the ring and going to celebrate the fans in attendance here tonight as we cut away to a commercial break. WINNER: ERIC SPARROW (New GIW Undisputed Championship) As we come back from a commercial break for nearly the hundredth time tonight it seems we see High Voltage, Aspen Chaud and Justin Volt walking towards the ring. They walk right into Reyna Silva, who is standing by talking with someone. JUSTIN VOLT: “MOVE! Little girl.” Reyna seems a little offended, but before she can retaliate she’s cut off by Justin who continues. JUSTIN VOLT: “Don’t say a thing, we won’t listen. Why don’t you wander off back to ETV, where you belong.” Reyna seems ready to strike however the presence of another figure, this one being the returning Jacoby Jackson stops the commotion. JACOBY JACKSON: “Listen man, why don’t you leave her alone? As a matter of fact. I have a proposition for you; I know she’ll agree with. Me and her versus the two of you, next week. So she can prove she belongs and we can prove, that you aren’t as good as you say you are.” Jackson holds out his hand for a shake as Silva stands behind him seeming to be in agreement as Justin looks down at his hands and shakes his head. JUSTIN VOLT: “You’re on. But just in case being terrible is contagious, I won’t shake your hand.” Justin smirks and walks by him, with Aspen at his side as Jacoby looks over at Reyna nodding as the segment ends. QUOTE “Taste the Blood” by Shawn McPhearson begins to play, and the Perfect Combination stroll out from behind the curtain. Boos rain down on them from around the arena as they seem in no hurry to make their way down the ramp. RILEY STEVENS: "We could be looking at the Number One Contenders for the Tag Team Titles in action here tonight.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Number one contenders? Really? You know we have The Killers right?" "To Be Loved" by Papa Roach hits the speakers, and sparks the introduction of High Voltage version 2 - Justin Volt and Aspen Chaud. The crowd are clearly still unsure how to react to Aspen following the events of the last few weeks, with the pair being greeted with a very mixed reaction. Their Galveston Island Tag Team Championship belts are draped over opposing shoulders as they approach the ring, and the intimidating presence of The Shadow. OLIVER THIBODAUX: "I like this new partnership Stevens, they're gonna go far" The Shadow seems more than willing to kick things off for the Perfect Combination, as is a rather confident-looking Justin Volt. Tony Miranda has other ideas, he is pointing at Aspen Chaud, screaming for her to come into the ring and face him. The former Galveston Island Champion seems more than comfortable on the edge of the ring apron however, leaving it down to the referee to try and get one of the members of The Combo out. Growing tired of the whole affair, Justin Volt makes the decision for him, taking out a distracted Tony Miranda who wisely chooses to roll out of the ring and regroup. The referee calls for the bell as Volt's eyes follow him for a moment, maybe concerned that he might make an attempt to confront Aspen. The momentary distraction would prove to be costly, turning around to a faceful of boot from the Shadow, sending him staggering back into the ropes. A vicious clothesline on the rebound sends the smaller man crashing all the way back down to the mat, knocking the wind out of his sails. RILEY STEVENS: “Love probably isn't the best foundation to build a tag team on, Justin Volt already caught off guard here tonight.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “You jealous Stevens? I don't blame the guy, Aspen looks smoking HOT tonight!” Meanwhile back in the ring, The Shadow has already unleashed a couple of big elbow drops on the downed Justin Volt, who seems to muster just enough energy to roll away from the third attack, causing the best part of 400 pounds to slam into the mat, giving the ring a nice shake in the process. As The Shadow begins to guide himself back to his feet with some help from the nearest ring rope, Volt begins the long crawl back to his partner, who seems surprisingly willing to make the tag despite the giant prospect of the Shadow to face. Seemingly able to shake off his fall, the Shadow stands menacingly, eyeing up his new challenger as Aspen elegantly climbs through the ropes and into the ring. Much to the big man's disgust, he is slapped hard on the back by his partner, and the referee seemingly accepts it as a legal tag. Tony Miranda comes barrelling into the ring full of purpose, but his damaging martial arts derived strikes look a little wild and ragged, allowing Aspen to duck underneath and land a quick snap DDT to send Miranda down to the mat. Wasting no time she rolls around to Tony's legs, attempting to lock in the K.M.A and finish it early, only to be greeted by a kick to the face for her troubles. The pair return very quickly to their feet, locking in a momentary stare from across the mat. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “She nearly had it!” RILEY STEVENS: “Aspen gets a bit of a bad girl image and you're her new biggest fan? Typical.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Riley, she'll never be yours. Marcus Hister has more of a shot.” Tony beckons Aspen to come at him as the pair circle each other. Aspen scoots in a couple of steps, trying to go low again, but is forced back by a big swinging kick from Tony which nearly catches her in the side of the jaw. Aspen springs herself off the ropes trying to get some momentum, only to be taken down by a hip toss from Miranda. She is able to roll through, surprising Miranda with a pretty decent looking football tackle, sending him back down to the mat. He is quick to return to his feet, but Aspen is already back up to a run, connecting with a flying dropkick that sends Tony flying back down to the canvas. Volt seems keen to come back into the fight, calling for the tag. Aspen reluctantly slaps his hand, and the fired up High Voltage original begins unleashing a flurry of stomps and kicks on the downed Tony Miranda. Knowing he was in a pretty bad spot, Tony digs deep, sweeping out the legs of Volt, buying himself enough time to get to his feet and make a break for the safety of his corner. Volt is up almost instantly, catching Miranda with no trouble, sending him back down to the mat with a reverse DDT. Justin seems to be considering the top rope, and surprisingly makes a bee-line for it. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “This is gonna be electric!” The sound of Riley Stevens face palming can be heard from his microphone. He's not wrong. As Justin begins his ascent, Tony Miranda seems to dig deep into the energy reserves, and is able to muster a tag back into his partner. The lumbering Shadow gets straight to work, pulling Volt off the turnbuckle and tossing him casually across the ring like a rag doll. He places the smaller man between his tree trunk legs and unleashes with a bone shuddering Powerbomb. 1...2...ROPE BREAK! The virtually lifeless Justin Volt is able to take advantage of his position and just manage to get a toe onto the bottom rope. Angered by the call, Shadow pulls Volt up by his hair, delivering a backhand to the face that sent the High Voltage member flying towards his own corner... And is able to get his hand up to tag in Aspen mid-air! The other half the current Tag Team Champions comes flying full pace into the ring. She attempts to unload a series of strikes on the monster, but none of them seem to make their mark, the Shadow laughs it off as he grabs Chaud by the throat. The fans know what's coming as the Shadow lifts her high in the air. Shadow holds her there for what seems like an eternity, before uncorking a SINGLE HANDED CHOKESLAM. Justin Volt is nowhere to be seen as Shadow hooks the leg and goes for the pin. 1...2...3! Perfect Combination wins it! RILEY STEVENS: “What a win, here tonight by Perfect Combination bringing this battle to a tie at one win a piece and you have to think this earns them a title shot.” Justin Volt now returns to the ring, slowly seeming to not realize what happened yet, he’s tossed over the rope quickly by Shadow. When Shadow turns around he turns into Tony who yells at him to do something. Shadow exits the ring and then reaches under the ring, pulling out a table that he slides into the ring. The fans are all in shock as they realize what Tony is planning here, as Aspen lays lifeless in the ring. They get the table set up in the ring, and then lift Aspen to her feet. Suddenly a murmur fills the arena as the camera cuts to Marcus Hister who slides into the ring wielding a chair in hand. He swings it wildly at Tony who ducks it and bails out of the ring. Suddenly it’s Marcus and Shadow, standing across from one another. RILEY STEVENS: “Is Marcus Hister crazy? Confronting The Shadow?!” Shadow seems willing to take his chances with Hister, but Tony screams at him to get out and he does so to the fans disliking. Volt now returns to the ring, checking on Aspen as Hister stands in the ring as Perfect Combination backs up the ramp having done what they came here to do, with Hister just putting a bit of a hitch in their plans as the show comes to a close.
