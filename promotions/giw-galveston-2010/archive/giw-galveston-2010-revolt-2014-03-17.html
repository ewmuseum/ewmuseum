Revolt, 17 March 2014

GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: REVOLT Live from Porretto Beach - March 17th, 2014 BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! The fireworks stream into the air, green, as today is St. Patrick's Day, and the crowd goes absolutely insane. Tonight is going to be a huge night for Revolt and the fans clearly know it. RILEY STEVENS: “Tonight is going to be a huge night! Welcome all to Revolt on St. Patrick's Day streaming live on galvestonislandwrestling.com! Just one week after crowning a new GIW Undisputed Champion, we will see all of the top contenders in action.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Don't try to pinch me Riley! I hate St. Patrick's Day, I hate you, and I hate the fact that Alexis Terry is in our main event tonight even though she just lost her belt.” RILEY STEVENS: “I'm glad you mentioned that. Can we talk about Madison Seton and Michael Lynch for a second?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “No Riley. No. We go through this every damn time. You don't understand wrestling. Stop. You always think we need to cheer for the guys who play by the rules – and LOSE – but guess what Riley, we cheer for the winners. The ones who get stuff done.” RILEY STEVENS: “Well tonight, I have no doubts that Alexis Terry is going to be able to get things done and she will be one step closer to regaining the title she was screwed out of in the first place.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I bet she ends up through the announce table tonight. I should take out an insurance policy on this thing. I have a bad feeling.” RILEY STEVENS: “Well, the show is about to start, so buckle your seatbelts ladies and gents because Veronica Taylor and Tommie Gunn about to take us for a ride.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I'd like to take Veronica on a ri-“ As Oliver's mic cuts out, we cut to the ring for the first match. QUOTE We are ready for the return of a former star now as already standing in the ring having made his debut last week is Tommie Gunn, standing in preparation for his next chance to get a win here in GIW. He’s getting his arms stretched out as “Chick Habit” by April March and these fans are quick to remember just who is going to come out next as Veronica Taylor a girl who made waves during her short stint in GIW heads out from the back with her manager and publicist Melissa Mariano in tow. RILEY STEVENS: “Veronica Taylor is back Oliver and I know you’ve always liked her but I have to ask, you think she’d consider you one of the uglies she hates so much?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Excuse me Riley? I’m a man’s man, ladies love me. Stop projecting your insecurities on me.” Taylor looks over to Melissa who reaches into a bag that she is carrying with her to the ring, pulling out a mirror that she uses to look herself over in the mirror with as she walks up the steps then into the ring, as the referee tells her to set it down as she holds her finger up telling him to wait as she fixes up her hair as Gunn tells her to hurry up as she finally sets it down in the corner then turns as the referee calls for the bell. DING DING DING! Gunn is about to go after her when Melissa jumps up onto the ring apron, with the bag digging through it calling for a timeout as the referee turns over to her telling her to get down as she fumbles with a few items that end up in the referee’s hands. He throws them back into the bag and tries to get her down as Tommie tries to help out with the process as Veronica has a hold of that mirror now, and as he turns around she cracks it over his head shattering the mirror as she kicks a few of the stray pieces out of the ring as it opens up a bit of a cut on Gunn, but only drops him to a knee. She throws the mirror out of the ring and poses for a second, but when she turns around a now angry Tommie is starting to get to his feet as the referee has turned around having to be confused why he’s suddenly bleeding but too incompetent to figure it out. Gunn rushes at her and Taylor quickly backs up sliding through the ropes, Tommie tries to grab her but Veronica begs for the referee to help her and he quickly comes pulling Gunn out of the way telling him to keep off her as she’s in the ropes as he pushes the referee and walks forward only to end up getting slapped across the face with a Bitch Slap as he stumbles back and she realizes she made him more angry as she quickly slides out of the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Taylor may be playing with fire here.” Veronica heads around the ring to where Melissa is, she lets out a scream as Gunn comes around forcing him to put on the breaks allowing Taylor to slide into the ring as he quickly follows her into the ring then she catches him right away landing an Implant DDT. These fans are not having any of it, booing her tactics as she walks around the fallen Gunn posing a bit for the camera as she calls for him to get up looking to have a little fun as he gets up and stumbles back into the corner where she rushes him and hits a big kick to his face, then promptly follows it up with a corner foot choke that the referee starts to count 1…2…3…4…Taylor backs off as Gunn walks out right into a Super Kick that drops him to the mat as she walks over to him, putting just a foot down on him 1…2…Gunn kicks out. This annoys Veronica as she yells something at him then backs up and waits for him to get to his feet, as she hits the ‘Exed Out’, laying him out as he’s done now as she makes the cover 1…2…3! REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner by way of pinfall…Veronica Taylor!” Despite his best efforts Taylor’s plan worked out to perfection as she stands up and calls Melissa into the ring, as she comes up the steps with the bag coming into the ring as Taylor gets down and turns the laid out Gunn over as Veronica holds her hand out and Melissa starts to dig through the bag. RILEY STEVENS: “A dominant win in her return but what she got planned now for Gunn?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Who knows?” As he says that we get the answer as Taylor is handed some lip stick from Melissa as she starts to spread it on the downed Gunn’s lips doing a poor job considering he’s not completely out but just enough for her to slab it on then use it to write ‘Ugly’ across his chest as she throws it back in her bag and holds up her arms victorious after a big win in her return. WINNER: VERONICA TAYLOR We cut to Ian Mendoza's office. It isn't really an office, but rather a tent set up away from the ring and the bleachers on Porretto Beach. Inside were a few chairs and wooden table where Mendoza sat reading something on a laptop. The camera zooms out and we see Wake Walker standing. WAKE WALKER: “I don't like waiting, Mendoza. What do you want?” Mendoza doesn't answer. Instead he keeps reading. Walker crosses his arms. WAKE WALKER: “Is this how Mr. B told you to treat me – whoever that guy is? I'm pretty sure this isn't what they teach you in whatever clown college you got a business degree from.” Mendoza slams the laptop shut and stands up across the table from Walker. He looks him right in the eye and clearly isn't afraid. IAN MENDOZA: “That was an e-mail from my daughter. I don't get to be with my family as often as I would like because I have to fly down to Texas for Revolt. Do you have family Walker?” Mendoza stares into Walker's eyes. WAKE WALKER: “I don't think it matters.” Mendoza leans forward, placing his fists on the desk. IAN MENDOZA: “I think it does. I really think it matters. We all have to make sacrifices for family. We become selfless. We can't afford to be arrogant. When Mr. B offered me this job, I felt like I was above it Walker. I didn't think I really needed to fly down here every week to oversee a wrestling promotion. I was doing something a lot more important, at least in my opinion. But Mr. B cares about GIW. He passionately cares about GIW. Her loves GIW like I love my family. He offered me a pay raise to come here; money that could go to my kids. So I took it Wake. I took the job, I took the pay raise. I made sacrifices so my family could prosper.” Wake has been rolling his eyes through this whole story. WAKE WALKER: “Save your maxim for someone who cares Ian.” There is a moment of tension between the two men that reaches its peak. IAN MENDOZA: “I don't think you have family because you are one of the most arrogant men I have ever come across in my career. The things you do to people Wake, just to advance your own career, its sick. I called you in here tonight to make it clear that there are lines, and if you cross them, there will be consequences. This is your formal warning. I am not the man you want cross.” Wake Walkers speaks as he leaves the tent. WAKE WALKER: “You have no authority. You're just a puppet, like the rest of these people...” Mendoza screams at Walker, halting his exit. IAN MENDOZA: “I am the physical embodiment of Mr. B! Everything I say, you should assume he says. Now don't think I am scared to terminate you Wake Walker. You are not as important as you think you are. Mr. B has plenty of money and plenty of resources to keep this company open and funded as long as he wants – you are not crucial in any sense of the word. I urge, you, Wake, for the sake of your career, watch the people you cross and be careful where you step. Mr. B does not own a charity and we will not keep the parasites employed if we don't have to.” Walker turns and glares at Mendoza, having clearly made a new enemy out of the boss. Walker quickly turns and exits the tent as we cut to the backstage area. Jack Waters and The Sir are in the midst of an argument. JACK WATERS: “I lasted longer than you in the battle royal tonight, if either of us gets a good match on Revolt next week, its going to be me.” The Sir fires back quickly and loudly. THE SIR: “Oh please, you're just a pleb like all the rest. It was made clear tonight that The Sir is the fastest rising star in GIW right now, even though I didn't win the match.” At the perfect time, Amanda Rowland, Ian Mendoza's assistant, comes walking towards the men, eating a churro. AMANDA ROWLAND: “Oh my Gosh! Are you guys in an argument?” The two of them nod. AMANDA ROWLAND: “Oh jeez. What did Colt Cox say to do when I see people arguing?” Amanda stops to think, tapping her chin. The Sir sneaks up close to her. THE SIR: “Taking a trip to candyland always lessens the tension.” Amanda shakes her head. AMANDA ROWLAND: “No. Last time I played candyland I lost sixty dollars. Oh! I know. Colt said that when I see two people arguing, I'm supposed to book a match between them!” The two men seem pleased, but neither of them are as pleased as Rowland herself. JACK WATERS: “Yes! That is a great idea.” Waters and The Sir exchange a glance, then finally part ways. The scene fades on Amanda Rowland patting herself on the back, or at least trying to, while still holding on to her churro. QUOTE When we return back to the ring, it’s now time for the next match up as Harrison Benson stands in the ring waiting for his opponent as he officially enters the deep end as "Another Stranger Me" by Blind Guardian hits and the fans go crazy as Tony Miranda walks out from the back, and Benson seems a little nervous seeing the former champion making that walk to the ring. Miranda high fives a few fans then walks up the ramp keeping his eyes on the fresh face, knowing that he can get in his head early. RILEY STEVENS: “Benson gets a huge opportunity here against Miranda but one has to wonder if it is too much too soon for the young man.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Who cares, this is a business got to go to deep end.” Miranda steps through the ropes staring across the ring at Harrison who does his best to look him back in the eye as Tony walks to the corner holding up an arm in the air and screaming out “Get Hype!” which the crowd yells back at him for, as he jumps down and does it again in the other corner getting a louder response as he jumps down and walks back to the center of the ring as the referee rings the bell. DING DING DING! The two start to circle around one another as Harrison seeming a bit caught up in the lights, charges in only for Tony to hip toss him over his shoulder with ease as Benson hits the mat and pops up running at him again only to yet again be thrown over the top with a hip toss as Miranda looks over at him with a smirk almost just to let him know who he is in there with as Harrison slaps the mat and hops up to his feet, trying to get some offense going as the two go in for a lock up now and Tony starts backing him up showing off his strength but Harrison does his best spinning around with Miranda’s right arm cranking it behind his back. Seeming a little bit happy with just doing that Harrison takes too much time and Miranda throws a big elbow to his head knocking him off and stumbling back as he hits the ropes and displaying his speed as he rushes him with a Running Dropkick. Benson hits the mat and flips over, ending up in the corner where he grabs the ropes and fights his way to his feet holding his gut as Tony rushes him and goes again for the Running Dropkick this time planning to use the corner to even make it worse but at the last second Harrison spins out sending Tony into the corner pads, dropping him to the mat with a thud and hurting his knee a bit as Benson stumbles out and gets his breath again as he hoists Tony up and tosses him off the ropes. He throws a big clothesline but Tony ducks under it then explodes off the other turnbuckle and hits a Running Neckbreaker on the young upstart as he makes the cover 1…2-Kick Out! RILEY STEVENS: “Got to admire the heart but Tony is putting on a clinic.” Benson gets back up to his feet again, this time slower as Tony throws a Spinning Roundhouse Kick that catches him in the jaw. But to Tony’s surprise it only puts Benson off balance a bit, as he turns and goes for the Rolling Wheel Kick again catching him but Harrison stays on his feet rocked a bit and off balance but still up as Tony gets back up nodding his head and going for a European Uppercut but Benson ducks back and tries to grab him only for Tony to spin and grab his head and pull him right into ‘The Knee’ in one flawless transition as that’s the straw that puts him down on the mat, seemingly out as Tony hops down on top 1…2…Kick Out! Shocking to many Benson kicks out, showing off his heart something the man he is facing is known for as Miranda gets up and looks over at him with a raised eyebrow. Seeming a bit impressed by his heart as he continues to fight, Tony none the less knows he has to put him down as he turns and hits him with a Discus Clothesline, again Benson’s body won’t go down as he’s out on his feet and taking advantage of it Tony jumps up and pulls Harrison into a Triangle Choke, from a standing position down to the mat as these fans can tell it’s the end because once he locks this in, you know it’s over. Benson won’t tap and finally his body gives out, passing out as the referee steps in and stops the match. REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner by way of submission…Tony Miranda!” Miranda puts on a clinic in this match and he gets up after drawing the submission win, bouncing back after the loss to CDE the week prior in a war. Miranda raises up his arms as the fans are happy to see him back in the win column as he looks down at Harrison who is coming back to consciousness looking around a bit confused. RILEY STEVENS: “Tony did what he was supposed to here tonight with the victory but Benson was game he did his best but Tony proved he was another level.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “You don’t get anything for heart…” Miranda walks over to him giving him a small pat on the back then walking away as he raises his arms up into the air and rolls out of the ring showing the fans some love as he heads towards the back picking up some momentum here in the hotly contested GIW food chain. WINNER: TONY MIRANDA “Party in the USA” blares through the speakers as the crowd erupts in boos. Madison Seton makes her way down to the ring, microphone in hand. RILEY STEVENS: “There she is, the girl we all have been waiting to see. I'm sure she is going to come out here and apologize for her actions.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Apologize? For what? She did exactly what she needed to do to get noticed. Boring ol' Madison Seton, blah blah blah. She came back to GIW with a new look, a new attitude, and she might be champion next week on Revolt.” RILEY STEVENS: “She took it too far Oliver. Way too far. Alexis Terry didn't deserve that.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Shut up Riley. Look at this girl! She is over six feet tall! And she is coming out to her same old theme music. She has balls.” By this time Seton has slid into the ring and her music is beginning to die down. She stands for a moment, raising an eyebrow at the boos. They seem to be amusing her. She takes a breath, raises her chin and begins to pace around the ring. MADISON SETON: “Alexis Terry—“ The crowd lets out a huge cheer hearing the former Undisputed Champion's name. Madison appears quite confused as she waits for the crowd to quiet. MADISON SETON: “Alexis... Terry...” Again the crowd cheers loudly and again Madison looks confused. Even when the crowd quiets, it takes her a few seconds before speaking again. MADISON SETON: “Really? Did I enter the Twilight Zone? You know, I get back here a few weeks ago—I want to cover this before I get to why I went after Alexis 10 days ago.” She runs a hand through her hair and tosses it over her right shoulder. MADISON SETON: “This place closed in September of 2012. Yeah, it stunk it closed, but I was Undisputed Champion. I was the last one. I reached that top of mountain and I ecstatic—just like you all were.” A number of fans cheer but Madison holds a hand up to get them to stop as a look of displeasure takes over. MADISON SETON: “But something happened. This place opened a couple months later. Then it closed again. Then it reopened yet again. Alioth Starre became a champion. Alexis Terry became champion again… and there was no Madison Seton. What was SO important that made me stay away from Galveston? Finishing school? No, that never made a difference. Playing in the WNBA? No, that shouldn't have impeded anything. So where... was... I...?” A silence ensues as a look of extreme confusion takes over her face. The silence is long enough a few in the crowd begin booing. The confusion appears to turn to anger. MADISON SETON: “NO ONE gave me a head's up! Not a SINGLE person running this show let me know! No calls, no voicemails, no letters, NOT EVEN A SIMPLE EMAIL! I get left out of the loop like I'm some... afterthought. Not ONE person says, “Hey, Maddie. We're opening again. Come on back!” I was the reigning Undisputed Champion. The last time Alexis and I were in the ring, I beat her for that title. I never lost it. I... NEVER... lost it. It was TAKEN from me. The true Undisputed Champion right now is Madison Seton!” Her voice had turned into a whine, which causes fans to boo again. MADISON SETON: “YOU HAVE NO RIGHT TO BOO ME!! I loved being in this ring and I loved everything about this business. I should have been the FIRST person called. I would have been on the first flight out and I would have re-signed in seconds. Instead, I get bypassed and overlooked like I was a big slab of nothing. It was like I never existed... You know what that told me? It told me... you... are all... moronic... fakes.” For the first time ever, the entire arena is now booing Madison. MADISON SETON: “None of you have any right to say a thing to me. You all waived that when this place reopened later in 2012. Not even one of YOU told me GIW reopened. I was on my merry way at K-State and not ONCE did a single person come up and say I could have a chance to defend my title. If you truly cared about me, you would have begged me to come back and take out Alexis and Alioth and the rest of the poison running around GIW. You would have looked to me to again save the day. Instead, nothing. I was never a favorite, was I? You only cheered me because you hated everyone else that much. I was only favored because I was the less of all evils, right? A true fan favorite would have been hit with a barrage of mail asking—begging—for her to return. All I ever got were a bunch of magazines. No one cared. That's why I attacked Alexis. I'll say the name again: Alexis... Terry.” The booing becomes another huge ovation. MADISON SETON: “I don't know what made all of you suddenly start kissing up to her. She's petty. She's a backstabber—but for some stupid reason you all cheer her. I came to ringside because I had to see for myself if she was different. I saw nothing changed and it all rushed back to me. All the nasty things she said about me. All the dirty tricks she ever played. I took her hand and slammed her into the steps and let me tell you... watching her crash into them and lie there slumped over gave me the best feeling I had since I won the Championship. I took out Terry simply because the opportunity was ripe.” The booing has more than returned and a strong “SE-TON SUCKS!” chant rings across Madison simply smirks. MADISON SETON: “Michael Lynch is supposed to be the best? Well, I'm about to beat the best... so fuck him and fuck all of you.” She drops the microphone as we cut quickly away from her, heading to a promotional video for next week’s show hyping up the two top matches, really focusing on Seton and Lynch. After that video we return to a ring filled with Madison Seton and in her corner alongside her is Melancholy her tag team partner for the night as Seth Cohen is now at the ring steps walking up them, his music playing in the background. As he takes his spot on the side of the ring as these fans go quiet then pick up as "Satori, Pt. 1" by Flower Travellin' Band, hits and the fans start to deafly boo as the new Undisputed Champion, Michael Lynch walks out from the back wearing both the North Beach and the Undisputed Championship over each shoulder as he strolls towards the ring probably with more confidence in his back pocket then ever before. RILEY STEVENS: “Some really strong words from Madison Seton moments ago and this match should give us a little bit of an appetizer for what is too come next week when Seton and Lynch go one on one for the belt.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “It’s a win-win both would make great champions! Could be the best match we have ever had.” Lynch walks to the top of the steps and pulls off both belts raising hem high over his shoulders as Seton, Cohen, and Melancholy all eye what he has, though Seton may be the next contender Melancholy and Cohen know they can put themselves in line for the belt as well by making a statement of either tonight. Lynch sets his belts down on the steel steps in his corner as he steps into the ring with Seton, and to everyone’s surprise these two are going to kick it off as the bell rings. DING DING DING! Lynch and Seton are in the ring together and the fans actually remain on their feet, waiting to sit down as Madison and Michael start to circle around each other, each of them having some words for one another as they are about to lock it up when Lynch holds up his hands shaking his head walking over and slapping Cohen in the chest, tagging him in as he looks over at Michael a bit confused why he bailed as the fans go crazy most of them taking their seats realizing that this battle isn’t going to happen quite yet in this match up as Cohen willingly hops in and nods his head, happy to have a chance to really make a statement against Seton as he gets in there and locks up with Seton the two going back and forth, before an angry Madison gives him a shove throwing him back to the mat but he rolls through right back to his feet as he gets right back to his feet. He rushes her trying to catch her off guard but Seton ducks under it throws a kick to the stomach at the same time using his own momentum against him as he doubles over and she grabs him spinning him around and tossing him into the corner. Madison fighting with some anger tonight, the force of the throw bounces Seth off the ropes and right into a clothesline from Madison as she looks over at Lynch in the corner as he says something to her that she responds back with “Just watch this.” as she pulls up Cohen by the back of his neck. RILEY STEVENS: “You had to expect that these two would use this match to send a message to one another.” Seton pulls him up and walks him across the ring, dropping him with a Russian Legsweep to the mat, taking the chance to make an early pin 1…Kick Out. Seton gets up and looks up as Lynch is again trash talking her, this time about her inability to put Seth out with that move as she rolls her eyes and waits for Cohen to get up which he does as he throws a punch missing and getting hit with one leg kick, then one to the thigh, then up to the stomach, then finally a spinning kick to the stomach that doubles him over as she spins and kicks him in the head now which drops him again as she walks across the ring, standing in her corner as Cohen now desperately crawls towards his corner. Waiting for him to get close Seton tags in Melancholy then steps out of the ring, as she rushes to try to get a hold of Cohen but Seton took too much time instead Seth tags out to Lynch who enters the ring and blocks Melancholy’s attempt at a punch then connects with a absolutely brutal Lariat that nearly knocks her out of her boots as she falls down to the mat. RILEY STEVENS: “Going into this match I thought it was a great opportunity for Cohen and Melancholy but I think they may be in the wrong place at the wrong time…” Michael now looks over at Seton as if to give her a look into her future, next week as Seton doesn’t seem impressed at all as Cohen is still recovering in the corner from the kicks he took from Madison as Melancholy stumbles to her feet getting her bearings as Michael wraps his arms around her and lands a Running Bulldog, continuing the attack on her head as he rolls over and makes the cover 1…2…Kick Out. The whole time he was looking up to Seton, as though he was going to do it that easy but with Melancholy kicking out he glares up at her as she smirks at his failure as he angrily gets up and tugs up Melancholy with him, as he plants her with a Tornado DDT completely taking her out of this as the move gets her awfully close to a tag as he grabs her and throws her to the corner as he walks back to his corner and tags Cohen who doesn’t seem to have expected it again as he steps into the ring and by then Seton has made the tag to Melancholy having to reach over the ropes and literally tag her now as she steps back into the ring and Cohen gives it his all rushing her and tackling her back into the corner where he throws rights and lefts tagging her over and over again. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “He may be playing with fire…” As if on cue Seton gives him a big shove sending him backwards as he comes back at her and she hoists him up then drops him face first onto the ring padding in the corner. She rolls him up, 1…But she then changes it from a pin attempt right into a Camel Clutch a flawless transition and only looking more slick because she ends up with Cohen facing Lynch’s corner as he struggles and tries not to tap but at this point it probably isn’t the worst idea as Seton sends a big message with the submission cranking it hard as Seth readies his hand and Madison pulls off pushing his face into the mat, as she grabs him and looking broken and beat she hoists him up and barely able to stand she finishes him off with the ‘Round-A-Bout’ a Roundhouse Kick that drops him to the mat, ripe for a pin but rather then doing that she grabs him, even though he is dead weight and tosses him into the corner as he’s literally just draped over the middle two ropes as Lynch slaps his back and Seton walks over to the other side of the ring where Melancholy is on the corner holding her head laying on the top rope as she tags her and steps out of the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “It’s Lynch’s turn now.” Having already tagged in Lynch pulls Melancholy back into the ring, she doesn’t even seem to want to fight anymore as he grabs her arm and then screams at Seton, as he pulls her into ‘The Ram's Horn’ a Roaring Elbow and all that Melancholy had left is gone as she falls to the mat, down and out as Seton gives him a nod as he slides out of the ring and starts to search for something underneath the ring as these fans are as confused as Seton is as he pulls out a table! These fans can’t help but get a little excited about it as Melancholy still hasn’t moved in the ring, as Lynch slides it into the ring and rather then doing anything at all Madison stays in wait in the corner as Michael gets the table set up in the ring then walks back over to Melancholy and has her by her throat then looks over at Madison as the referee begs him to stop as he hoists her up and tosses her through the table crashing through as the referee calls for the bell right away. REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here are your winners by way of disqualification…Melancholy and Madison Seton!” Seton enters the ring and seeing this Lynch takes his leave, rolling out of the ring and grabbing both his belts and tossing them over his shoulders as he backs up the ramp and Madison looks down at Melancholy as the referee calls for some help from the back as Seton and Lynch share a staredown. RILEY STEVENS: “I get sending a message but not like this, that is disgusting…” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Whatever Riley, this is wrestling!” Lynch stands at the top of the ramp as Seton has now exited the ring as Lynch is about to turn to head to the back he sees Madison throwing a table of her own into the ring now as he turns to watch from the top rope as Cohen who was still in the corner propped up against the ropes falls off them as Seton gets a table set up in the corner. RILEY STEVENS: “Now what?!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Seton doesn’t want to be topped.” The referee is now trying to convince her to stop, Melancholy already a victim of circumstance as Seton grabs Cohen up and in one quick motion spins and throws him right through the table in the corner his body ending up a curled up mess in the corner as she turns back to Lynch. RILEY STEVENS: “I guess just having the match next week wasn’t enough, but regardless of what you think about what they did to these two tonight they will actually have to go one on one next week and it’ll be for the belt.” Lynch hoists up the titles high into the air as Madison stares at him from the ring as if the people weren’t already intrigued by this match before hand they have to be dying to see it now as we only have one more week until this amazing clash as we yet again go to the back. WINNERS: MADISON SETON & MELANCHOLY We cut to the back where Christopher Darren Erns is warming up for his match. Tony Miranda shows up behind him to the audible cheers of the fans. TONY MIRANDA: “CDE. Good luck tonight man. You ready for this?” CDE shakes Tony's hand and nods. CHRISTOPHER DARREN ERNS: “Yeah. I'm ready for Ryan Cavallari. I'm ready for Terry or Walker. I'm ready or Lynch or Seton. Tony, let me tell you, I'm ready to become Undisputed Champion.” Tony nods and pats CDE on the shoulder. The respect between the two is clear. TONY MIRANDA: “Look man, I just wanted you to know that I'll have your back next week.” CDE turns profile to the camera, looking Tony in the eye. CHRISTOPHER DARREN ERNS: “I appreciate the gesture, but do you not think I can do this on my own?” Tony is quick to reply. TONY MIRANDA: “No, of course I think you can do it on your own. But you know how this place can be. Especially if you end up facing Wake. It is always good to know you have someone who genuinely wants you to succeed in your corner. I see myself in you man. I want you to succeed.” CDE nods. They take a moment. CHRISTOPHER DARREN ERNS: “Awesome. Good then. I'll see you next week, if I get there next week. I gotta take care of business here first. See ya around Tony.” CDE walks off towards the ramp, leaving Tony alone. We hear clapping off in the distance as Dennis Driver comes into focus, a grin on his face. DENNIS DRIVER: “That was solid acting my friend. I mean, CDE doesn't seem like the sharpest tool in the shed but you really have him fooled.” Tony turns towards the much smaller Driver and looks down on him. TONY MIRANDA: “What exactly are you talking about?” Dennis chuckles and raises an eyebrow at Tony. DENNIS DRIVER: “You're a former GIW Undisputed Champion. And that guy beat you last week. Do you really expect him to believe you have absolutely no ambition left in you? That you don't want to get even with him? You're going to double cross him the first chance you get – probably because you can't beat him fair and square in the ring. I wish I could sympathize, but I don't have that problem.” Tony laughs and rolls his eyes. Dennis doesn't seem to think it is a joke. TONY MIRANDA: “Look, you have no idea who I am. I barely even know who you are. I've made a name for myself in this company by staying on the right side of the rules. I earn respect in the ring, and I give respect outside of it. Don't think you can roll up into this company that I have been a part of for damn near its entire life and try to call me a backstabber.” Dennis shrugs. Tony is red and fuming now. DENNIS DRIVER: “I just call it like I see it. But hey bud, it seems like I've already gotten under your skin. The truth hurts sometimes. Save some of that rage for our match next week – I just got it confirmed by Mendoza.” Dennis turns to walk away, then pivots on his heel and turns around. DENNIS DRIVER: “By the way, it must hurt to know that guy is on the path to a title shot, and you're facing some guy in his second match with the company. Maybe you aren't such a big shot after all?” Dennis exits, leaving Tony alone once again. This time, Tony looks far less confident and extremely flustered. We cut back to the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Cavallari has made no secret about his desire for the belt and now a rematch with Walker but first he has to go through CDE a real dark horse tonight.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “He’s going to destroy this guy.” Cavallari walks across the ring now walking to the corner and stepping up posing and only getting booed more as he jumps down and "Running Under" by King Black Acid replaces his music and the boos he was getting are replaced by cheers for Christopher Darren Erns as he walks out from the back, looking all business knowing how big the stakes are tonight as he makes his walk down the ring still giving some love to the fans with a few high fives but still with his eyes on Cavallari all the way as he walks up the steps not wasting any time here. RILEY STEVENS: “Erns looks like he is ready to go, he’s making a B-Line for the ring right now.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “In a hurry to lose the biggest match of his GIW career, nice.” Erns steps up and through the ropes and quickly walks over to his corner not really pandering to the crowd this time, more so just preparing for what is at hand as he stretches out his arms and legs as the referee gets in between the two and call for the bell. DING DING DING! Both men are ready to go as the fans are on there feet for this one, which has been highly anticipated since it was announced last week. Christopher Darren Erns has been somewhat of an underdog for his duration in GIW but has proven himself time and time again. Ryan has been nothing short of a stud so the match up is quiet intriguing. The bell sounds and the two men lock up in the center of the ring. Ryan Cavallari applies a perfect side headlock but Erns is able to reverse it by sending Ryan into the ropes. Ryan comes back with a clothesline but Erns ducks – THE UNDISPUTED KICK! Christopher Darren Erns’ version of the Yakuza Kick levels Ryan. Christopher looks down at his opponent and smiles. He lifts him up by applying a chokehold and inverts it into lifting DDT! What a maneuver! CDE goes for the pin! ONE….TWO…CDE picks up Ryan’s head. He isn’t finished yet! RILEY STEVENS: “Erns seems to have Cavallari reeling early.” Ryan is out of it. Christopher uses his strength to toss Ryan into the air – THE UPPERCUT FROM HELL! The crowd lets out a roar. Ryan crumbles to the mat but with quickness Christopher Darren Erns has Ryan set up. PACKAGE PILEDRIVER! He covers…1….2….3! CDE picks up the win and the statement has been made – Don’t overlook Christopher Darren Erns. REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner by way of pinfall…Christopher Darren Erns!” This match a very quick win by Erns and he has earned the right to face the winner of the Main Event here tonight as Erns gets up to his feet looking a bit shocked that he just finished things so quickly here tonight as Cavallari didn’t seem to have his head screwed on right here tonight and CDE proved he was ready to get his chance at gold. RILEY STEVENS: “You know I didn’t expect this match to end that quickly but CDE a week after defeating Tony Miranda has now beaten Ryan Cavallari in quick fashion. And the man that entered this tournament as perhaps the underdog now is a win away from being next in line for the title.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “He got lucky it was a fluke, something was wrong with Cavallari I’m sure of it!” Cavallari gets to a knee holding his neck in pain, perhaps something was wrong tonight but it does mean he is back to square one here in GIW as Erns hops up onto the top rope and these fans are going nuts for him as he celebrates his victory holding up a single finger knowing that’s all it takes for him to be the next Number One Contender.
