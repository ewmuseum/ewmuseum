GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: WEDNESDAY NIGHT REVOLT Live! On North Beach July 18th, 2012 on Showtime BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! As the show begins here on North Beach these crowd members are going crazy for what has to be easily be one of the most stacked Revolt’s in recent memory. As the show kicks off "Tear The World Down" by We Are The Fallen immediately sounds as yet again these fans realize they are about to be subjected to Alexis Terry as they let out their frustrations as the Undisputed Champion walks out from the back with David Sinclair at her side heading to the ring, together. Wasting little time grabbing a mic’, Sinclair is the one to kick things off. DAVID SINCLAIR: “Last week it was announced that if Alexis here wins at ‘Moment of Glory III’ I become full time General Manager around here taking back the position I should have never lost. I realize that with Alexis in that match it is essentially a shoe in considering the circumstances. So everyone join me in a moment for your future General Manager and your Undisputed Champion!” Sinclair holds up one arm using the other to point at Terry who holds up her title in the air with a smile on her face as that moment is quickly ruined by the sound of Kluge’s voice over the speakers as he walks out with Seton as well heading down the ramp. MATT KLUGE: “Wait you honestly think Alexis has a chance in this match David? You realize you can’t play dress up as the referee anymore…I’ll be there to make sure nothing fishy, or should I say Terry happens. Madison is going to beat Alexis, and the days of you trying to make this company into some sort of beauty pageant will be over. Right Seton?” Kluge looks over at Seton who nods her head both Sinclair and Kluge’s futures hinging on these superstars as “Demon Eyes” by The Answer hits as the fans again roar with cheers for the recently returned Blake as Kluge and Seton look up from the bottom of the ramp as do Terry and Sinclair from in the ring. BLAKE BECKETT: “You know the more I hear you guys go back and forth, the more I realize that I may not really want either of you as my General Manager. But I am not the type to go back on my word so I came up with a bit of a change to that match at ‘Moment of Glory’. You both have your representatives well, now I will have one of my own. You see if my guy wins neither of you are General Manager and I will serve as both Chairman and GM. And who is more deserving for a shot at that title then the man that you two…screwed over. So Madison, Alexis let me introduce you to your opponent in the Main Event of ‘Moment of Glory III’…Alex Castellanos!” The crowd goes nuts with the news as out from the back walks Alex Castellanos not even for a second taking his eyes off of Sinclair and Terry in the ring as Blake looks at his guy with a smirk as we go from each…Alex and Blake, Alexis and David, and Madison and Kluge. Your Moment of Glory Main Event! As we head right from that into a trailer hyping up the Four Way Main Event. Looking at the fans shaking his head Starre walks up the steps, stepping into the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “What better way to kick off a Revolt then a rematch of the Main Event of last year’s Moment of Glory show, this is huge!” Starre heads up to the top rope posing as his music fades and "Did It On 'Em" by Nicki Minaj hits as these fans show their disapproval with a loud and angry amount of boo’s nearly deafening as Aspen Chaud heads out but she is not alone Ryan Cavallari stands beside her as the two head to the ring for this epic rematch, this crowd showing their distain for both competitors despite still realizing what a huge match it is. Aspen Chaud and Alioth Starre stand in their respective corners awaiting the sound of the bell ready to do battle. Both are widely considered the “best” that Galveston has to offer and this match should certainly settle that argument once and for all. The bell sounds and the fans are on their feet as Aspen and Alioth circle each other in the center of the ring before locking up. Aspen quickly takes advantage with a side headlock. She applies pressure in hopes of Alioth giving up but there was no way that the former champion was going to give up that easily. An elbow from Alioth allows him the time to escape the move. He sends Aspen into the ropes and throws a wild clothesline but Aspen ducks. Alioth turns around and eats a dropkick to the face. Quick cover by Aspen but she only scores a two count. Aspen is focused tonight with a look of a woman on a mission ever so evident in her eyes. She wastes little time in continuing the attack. Alioth is on one knee and Aspen takes advantage. She applies a sleeperhold and screams for Alioth to give up. Up to this point, Alioth has had little to know offense. Alioth looks dazed and confused, slowly fading away. RILEY STEVENS: “Aspen with a strong start.” The referee raises Alioth’s arm once only for it fall right back down. Aspen looks pleased. She believes that this one is over. The referee does the process again and is issued the same result. One more time and this one is over. The referee takes Alioth’s arm and raises it but Alioth has enough strength to keep it from crumbling to his side. Alioth is able to get to his feet and with Aspen still on his back with the sleeperhold locked in. Alioth looks to have caught his second wind. Alioth is able to break the hold by simply throwing Aspen over his shoulder and to the mat. Aspen is quickly back up but is caught by a pair of arm drags. A standing dropkick rocks Aspen and she stumbles back into the corner. Starre follows it up with a huge running knee to Aspen’s jaw. A groggy Aspen falls right into the hands of Alioth who catches her. He nails a picture perfect Fisherman Suplex into a pin. The referee counts and Alioth gets a two. It was a close two and the fans that were counting along thought that may have been it. Aspen looked out on her feet. Alioth cruelly picks Aspen up by her long dark hair. Alioth follows it up with a huge snap suplex. Once again Alioth pins this time hooking the leg. The referee counts and once again Alioth only gets a two. This match has been back and forth since the beginning! He soaks it all in, cocks his head back and keeps walking down the aisle as Tony paces in the ring, watching Starr like a caged animal, Starr begins to slide in the ring, but besides better of it and does a lap around the ring but stays outside. RILEY STEVENS: “Here comes the self proclaimed, ‘Face of GIW’ Daniel Starr, he has been impressive as of late and has opened some eyes, but I don’t know about this ‘Face of GIW’ stuff.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Of course he is! Listen to the crowd, they love him!” Who really knows what Oliver is hearing, all the crowd did was boo Daniel as he casually leaned against the apron on the outside, Tony had his eyes locked on him to make sure he didn’t try anything funny as “D-7” hits on the house system and Vega comes running out on stage with energy as she points to the crowd, they give her a mixed reaction, but nonetheless all are happy to see the daredevil in the ring, she slaps a few hands as she jogs down the aisle and slaps hands with the fans but as she hits the steps she stares in at Starr with anger glaring at him for what he did earlier tonight. RILEY STEVENS: “And here comes, in my opinion the x-factor in this match. A unique, unorthodox style and look to go along with it, Vega has a chance to prove herself tonight. He didn’t pander, his eyes were locked on the ring where Tony was in one corner, Vega in another and Starr yawned and paced on the outside. Genocide slid in the ring and went to a vacant corner. RILEY STEVENS: “Genocide has also been impressive as of late, another great addition to this match and a good time for him to shine in the main event.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Maybe one of my picks to win this match, I think he has the attitude to win it and isn’t a pandering idiot like Tony Miranda…” As the other three wrestlers itched for the bell to ring, finally Starr eased in and rested on the turnbuckle, sitting on it in the corner as the timekeeper handed the referee the North Beach title and the referee went to the middle of the ring and held it up as the crowd popped. RILEY STEVENS: “And there it is, all eyes go to the North Beach title as we are about to get this thing underway!” The referee walks back and hands the belt to the timekeeper and then signals for the bell. DING DING DING We are underway as all four wrestlers look at one another, unsure of where to start but it was Daniel Starr who would make the first move and slide out of the ring and back to his position at ringside as he paced, the crowd booed him for this as Vega and Tony looked to the cocky Genocide who Vegas rushed at first, the exchange a couple of punches before Genocide shoves her down, bullying her as Tony spins him around and kicks him in the gut to much delight of the crowd and sent him into the ropes. Tony dropped to his stomach as Genocide bounces over him and Vega catches him with a flipping dropkick and sends Genocide to the mat as she stands up and nods at Tony who gets to his feet and turns to Starr, but Vega runs at Tony and forearms him in the back as he leans on the ropes she gives him a few swift kicks and the crowd gives her a mixed reaction. RILEY STEVENS: “Well, I was about to say, Tony and Vega showing some team work, but she quickly shows that it’s every man for himself in this match, and Daniel Starr has yet to be touched.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “It’s good strategy! Why go in there head first like these guys when you can let them beat up on each other for a while!” Vega has control of the match as she whips Tony into the corner and rushes at him, he rolls out of the way just in time as she crashes and burns into the turnbuckle.
