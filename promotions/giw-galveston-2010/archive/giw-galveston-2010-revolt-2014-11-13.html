GIW — Card

Revolt, 13 November 2014

13 November 2014
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: REVOLT! Live from the Galveston Arena – November 13th, 2014 Galveston Island Wrestling opens up this week not to the usual tune of "Bartholomew" by Silent Comedy, but instead we hear the new Undisputed Champion's theme. Christopher Darren Erns steps out from the curtains with a sinister grin on his face as he takes a look at the Undisputed Championship resting on his shoulder. Unsurprisingly, he was met with a chorus of boos from the crowd, but just as unsurprisingly, Erns didn't really care what they thought of him as he climbed into the ring and grabbed onto a microphone CHRISTOPHER DARREN ERNS "A lot of people said I wouldn't reach this point. A lot of people thought I was never going to be the face of Galveston Island Wrestling. And a lot of people in this world are manipulated by what the 'good guys' tell them. They want you to hate me because I'm different than everyone else. Because I speak the truth, because I kick ass, and because I'm simply better than everyone on this roster. And you know what? I proved that when I took this Undisputed Champion from that little bitch. It's funny how all the critics have disappeared now that I'm reigning supreme in Galveston. In fact, I'd love to see those critics come out right now." Erns walks away from the main camera and leans over the top rope, glaring right at the entrance ramp CHRISTOPHER DARREN ERNS "M. Tech Michaud. The poor soul that has earned the right to challenge me for my belt after he beat my messiah. Yet, no one actually cares about M. Tech Michaud, but M. Tech Michaud. He claims to be a humble man, but you've all heard him speak. He's brash, harsh, and cruel to his opponents. Me? I'm honest. I'm fair. While they may not always be able to comprehend that. I'm the good guy. M. Tech is just another misguided fool who think he deserves to be a champion after all the hard work he's put into wrestling. But let's face it, you don't deserve a damned thing. In fact, how about you step out here right now and face me." Several seconds pass by and there's no one to be seen. Christopher just shakes his head and scoffs CHRISTOPHER DARREN ERNS "Why am I not surprised? Everyone is talking about how Michael Lynch is suspended and how weak Christopher Darren Erns is without him. But here I stand. In this ring. Right now. Calling out anyone backstage who wants to fight me. I know I got a lot of critics out there and I'm without my messiah. I'll take on anyone of you cowards! If you really think any of you stand a chance, grow a pair of balls, and step out right now! I'll defend my title any day against anyone and I'm not afraid to admit that. Everyone wants my title, so come on!" Again, more silence as Erns' temper begins to develop at the lack of competitors coming out from the curtain. The situation defuses as the champ begins to think to himself CHRISTOPHER DARREN ERNS "How about the biggest critic of them all. Beck Ramsey. The man who left you all for someone else. The man who returned to Galveston and won the Undisputed Championship with no questions asked. Then proceeded to 'lose' to his wife in a 'fair' match. And then he has the audacity to question my credibility as a competitor? But Beck's an idiot. He's scared of me. He knows that I'm better than him, but even more...he knows his wife is better than him. He can't handle the fact that the only sense of pride he had in his pathetic life was beaten by yours truly. So come on down, Beck!" The crowd popped at the aspect of Beck Ramsey coming down the ramp, but no one actually expected him to come out. Then again, no one expected someone's theme to actually play! Suddenly the PA begins to play 'Everybody Wants To Rule The World.' by Lorde cutting off CDE. He lowers the microphone while turning towards the entrance. He is frustrated by the interruption and begins shouting at the entrance. The crowd nearly blows the roof off while they all wait for The Harlequin to emerge from the back. The cheering only seems to grow a little louder but the current GIW Undisputed Champion can't seem to figure out why. Unbeknownst to him The Harlequin had entered through the crowd and climbed over the barrier. She kneels down to pull out a folded up chair from under the ring and slips into the ring. The chant 'Harlequin' breaks out. CDE figures it out but when he turns, The Harlequin is ready with a chair shot to the face! It makes a sickening smack making CDE stumble backwards and falling to one knee. He tries to shake the cobwebs off fast but she hits him again leaving the champion no room to breathe. The crowd goes wild. Christopher wipes his face with the back of his hand while he struggles to stand up again. Harlequin bends forward screaming something at him, to which he seems to shout something back, before she slams the chair down against his back. CDE refuses to fully fall to the mat. Harlequin hits him for the final time, finally drops the chair down, and scoops up the Undisputed Title and the microphone. She holds the title up in the air receiving a loud pop from the crowd. The Harlequin walks to the ropes. THE HARLEQUIN: “That is how you make someone bleed!” The crowds continues to cheer! Harlequin glances backwards with a short laugh. THE HARLEQUIN: “I know Auva had told you guys that I was going to receive time to speak... To... Discuss my feelings because I am just so damn delicate over my title loss. MAYBE I didn't make myself clear enough! This loss won't destroy my life... Am I angry about it? Of course but at the end of the day it won't matter about this tiny, setback. This title is mine! So, I will allow you to have fun with the title, enjoy it's glory and then I'm going to strip off of you, CDE. Makes it more fun, right?!" The crowd begins the 'Harlequin' chant again which she is soaking up. CDE rouse from the mat. The Harlequin tilts her head with a smirk. THE HARLEQUIN: “No, I don't want to discuss my feelings, I did want the night off though, I have a lot of plans to get in motion, and more importantly I just wanted to beat you, CDE... Like I said I was going to; I'm a woman of my word. Told you I would beat you to prove a fucking point. Here's the point! This is my title!” She holds the title towards CDE who is standing up now. The Harlequin sets the title down as fast as possible. THE HARLEQUIN: “Keep it warm for me, sweetness.” With that she throws the microphone down at CDE who palms it away as he chargers at the clown. She jumps up meeting him as the two clash together wildly throwing punches. Christopher strikes Harlequin in the face with elbow sending her stumbling before he kicks her in the stomach. The Harlequin drops down to the mat, one arm gripping her mid section while it's twin reaches down to grab the BANG! Pistol. The crowd is going insane! CDE reaches down for the clown but is shot in the face with a bunch of confetti! This momentarily sends him staggering back allowing the clown the grab the Undisputed Title from the mat. She puts the pistol away before drilling CDE in the face with it. This doesn't seem to do much harm but gives her enough time to slip out from the ring, leaving the Undisputed title at the edge of the apron. The Harlequin is laughing while she begins to make her way back up the ramp. “Everybody Wants To Rule The World” by Lorde plays. The Harlequin reaches out to touch a few of the fan's hands. CDE grabs his GIW Undisputed Title rubbing the back of his head. He's jaw is clenched while he glares down at The Harlequin who just blows him a kiss before disappearing into the back. The camera cut to a shot of the backstage office, where Auva Skye could be found with a warm smile and gaze toward the camera. AUVA SKYE: "Welcome back, everyone, to another edition of Revolt. Tonight, now that you know the identity of the mystery man that has been, well, cleaning up the back, I've decided to do something special with him. Scar, for the first time ever, will be involved in a tell-all event when he is interviewed by Zachary Pagonis later tonight! You will learn everything about this new transition as we shift from that awful Alexis Terry's regime to my new and improved Galveston Island Wrestling." The camera soon faded out to the ring for the first, official match. QUOTE House of the Rising Sun by Five Finger Death Punch begins to play. The crowd initially goes insane for the returning Joshua Acquin making his second appearance in his new GIW stint. Joshua Acquin walks out, cockily, however and he commands the attention to himself. Some fans begin to turn on him but it’s not enough to stop his old fans from cheering. As he waits in the ring, he tries to pick a fight with a fan in the front row. Du Hast by Rammstein begins to blare through the speakers. The fans immediately begin to boo as Rose Gasm comes from the back, blowing kisses to the crowd. Some of the men in the arena cheer and clap but clearly, it’s not because of Rose’s actions. Rose walks to the ring and enters it. The referee rings the bell. Rose and Joshua circle in the middle of the ring. Joshua shrugs his shoulders and begins to point and laugh at Rose, presumably demeaning her simply because she’s a woman. Joshua tells Rose Gasm to lie down. Rose approaches Joshua and then tries to roll him over! 1…2…kick out! Joshua looks around, surprised but then goes back to keeping his cool. Joshua and Rose circle the ring and they clash hand-in-hand only for Rose to kick Joshua in the mid-section. Rose looks at Joshua, who is clutching his stomach and bounces off the ropes but Joshua delivers a massive high-knee strike that sends Rose back to the ropes. When she bounces off them again, Joshua delivers a massive clothesline and goes for the cover. 1…2…kick out! Rose barely kicks out in time but Joshua immediately places a chokehold on Rose, grounding her. Joshua continuously tells Rose to give up but Rose gets up and lands a couple of elbows to the sternum of Joshua eventually freeing herself. Rose bounces off the ropes and runs at Joshua who grabs her and throws her up in the air. However, Rose uses the momentum as she falls to hit a missile drop kick on Joshua which takes the crowd by surprise. Before she can go for a cover, Joshua rolls out of the ring. Joshua holds his chest in pain. Rose exits the ring and tries to go after Joshua but Joshua punches Rose in the face and then sends her across the announce table. Joshua grabs Rose and puts her back in the ring and goes for a cover but only gets a two count. Joshua begins arguing with the ref, allowing Rose to get up and deliver a drop kick to the back of Joshua, sending Joshua to the turnbuckle. Rose runs at Joshua. Joshua delivers an elbow. Rose walks back, dazed. Joshua goes for a big boot but Rose ducks and quickly jumps on the second rope of the turnbuckle and throws herself to hit a flying crossbody! She goes for the pin. 1…not even a two count! Joshua and Rose both immediately get up and rush at each other. Rose grabs Acquin’s and goes for a roll up! 1…2…3! Acquin looks shocked and hits the mat in frustration as the victor rolls out of the ring. WINNER via Pinfall: Rose Gasm! QUOTE She Hates Me by Puddle of Mudd plays throughout the speakers. The Sir comes out by himself but stands on the top of the ramp and points to the curtain. Out comes Steve Realman who smirks. The Sir bows, causing a lot of the crowd to boo him. Steve Realman claps for The Sir. The Sir smirks and thanks the audience for their support, which ironically, they never gave. The Sir walks up the steel steps as Steve Realman stays on the outside, like a shark in the water. The Sir stands on a turnbuckle, clasps his hands together and begins to swing them back and forth, as if he were demanding praise from the audience. The audience further boos him. Du Hast by Rammstein interrupts The Sir’s little premature celebration as Barbara Weiß walks past the curtain with a slightly injured Rose Gasm on her side. Barbara reaches into her bra and takes out a small German flag which she waves around, causing the crowd to further boo her. From the looks of things, the crowd doesn’t seem to like anyone involved. The Sir pretends to tip an imaginary fedora as Barbara enters the ring. The referee calls for the bell. The Sir immediately runs at Barbara and hits a spear! Barbara quickly rolls out of the ring. Steve Realman gets close but Rose stops him from getting any closer. Rose protects Barbara, buying her some time. The Sir exits the ring and grabs Barbara, which Rose tries to stop but can’t. The Sir puts Barbara in the ring and begins to climb it. Rose begins yelling at The Sir, who is now distracted. As he and Rose argue back and forth, Barbara gets up slowly, bounces off the ropes and tries to punch The Sir. The Sir turns around just in time and blocks Barbara’s hit and pushes her away, sending her flying almost halfway across the ring. The Sir yells at Rose and then enters the ring. Barbara quickly runs up to The Sir and attempts a suplex! The Sir just stands there and laughs at Barbara’s attempt, knowing full well Barbara can’t lift up 300 pounds above the mat. The Sir pushes Barbara away. Barbara runs at The Sir, who tries to clothesline but Barbara quickly gets behind The Sir and attempts a German Suplex. The Sir yet again stands there raising his arms, taunting Barbara. Rose stands on the apron and distracts the referee. Barbara immediately notices this and low-blows The Sir, who topples over. Barbara goes for the pin! 1…2…kick out! Steve Realman tries to point out what just happened to the referee but the referee seems too busy with Rose. Realman approaches Rose and gets her off the apron. Realman pep-talks Rose for a bit, who only looks down as if she was ashamed. The Sir, who is holding his stomach in pain, gets on his knees. Barbara runs and hits a drop kick to his face and goes for the cover! 1...kick out! Barbara quickly gets up and begins climbing the turnbuckle. The Sir gets up. Barbara goes for a flying crossbody but The Sir catches her and delivers a back-breaker! The Sir nonchalantly goes for the pin. It looks like it’s over. 1…2… nope! Kick out! The Sir looks at the referee, bewildered. Meanwhile, Steve Realman has stopped giving Rose a pep-talk and has begun chatting with her. Rose seems to respond every once in a while. Steve Realman looks like he’s hitting on Rose! The Sir notices this and begins yelling at Steve to stop it. As he’s turned around, Barbara tries to hit a clothesline that does nothing to The Sir. The Sir laughs and stands there. Barbara bounces off the ropes and tries another clothesline but The Sir doesn’t seem to react to Barbara’s power, or lack thereof, at all. Steve Realman continues to speak to Rose and begins getting touchy, going as far as to begin playing with Rose’s hair. Rose looks like she doesn’t like what’s going on at all. Eventually, Steve Realman places his arm around the hip of Rose who then looks at Steve in disgust. Rose tells him to get his hand off of her immediately but Steve doesn’t seem to listen. Rose slaps Steve. The crowd reacts to the slap but Steve merely begins laughing. However, Rose follows it up with a kick straight to the groin! Steve Realman falls to the floor. The Sir turns around and starts yelling at Rose. The Sir looks at the fallen Steve and begins shaking his head. The Sir turns around slowly. Barbara runs at The Sir and then takes a high risk kick toward the his skull that knocks him to the canvas like a brick. A quick pin and Barbara steals it. WINNER via Pinfall!: BARBARA WEIß! In the back, cut to a wide shot reveals Zachary seated alone opposite an empty chair. He appears to be worried, uncertain about his own job and well being, but as time passes, Scar does not appear. Though he breathes a sigh of relief, he knows that something is up and turns to the camera, unsettled and uncertain. The camera then cuts to the next segment of the show. QUOTE Our next match is just about to begin as the crowd starts to create a buzz throughout the arena in excitement. And excitement is just what they get when the opening riffs of “Enter Sandman” by Metallica play and the current number one contender for the GIW Undisputed Champion makes his way to the ring! M. Tech Michaud is hot off his first victory in Galveston and it was a very impressive one over Michael Lynch to earn the right to be the next challenger for Christopher Darren Erns’ title. On the way down to the ring, he acknowledges a few fans before climbing through the ropes RILEY STEVENS: "What an impressive match we saw from Michaud just a week ago against Michael Lynch! And now we get to watch him continue to take down anyone who steps in front of him." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "He’s okay." The beginning of “Awake My Soul” by Mumford & Sons plays as Kevin Astro makes his way out to the entrance ramp, a sea of boos raining upon him. Just like M. Tech, Astro was coming off a huge victory from On Hallowed Ground where he beat Mr. GIW Matt Kluge and was looking to do the same to the current number one contender. On his way down to the ring, Astro definitely didn’t wait to start the trash talk game as he was shouting various things at M. Tech before he even got into the ring, but once he did, the two men met in the middle of the ring; having to be pulled apart before the match could truly begin RILEY STEVENS: "Who picks Mumford & Sons as their theme?" OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Champions, Riley, champions!" DING DING DING! The hottest superstar on the independent circuit, M. Tech Michaud, stood in the center of the ring with his fists balled up. It was easy to tell that Michaud was already tired of Astro’s antics and arrogance. The two men just stared down one another with neither one giving up ground during this showdown. A “M. Tech” chant started and was rivaled by a much softer “Astro” chant as these two men finally started the match RILEY STEVENS: "Let’s get this thing started!" Kevin Astro was a man who had evolved multiple times over the course of his time with GIW. He’s grown stronger as the weeks have gone by and he’s definitely shown that he deserves a mention among the top brass in Galveston. Kevin arrogantly stepped up to M. Tech first. As soon as Kevin began to run his mouth M. Tech leaped up into the air and drove his knee right into the jaw of Astro, sending him down to the mat early. As soon as Kevin's back hit the canvas hard the fans leaped to their feet and let their praise be heard for M. Tech. Kevin laid on the canvas, but didn’t waste any time there as he quickly got back up to his feet, looking to silence the harsh critics. Astro gave M. Tech the middle finger in a blatant show of disrespect. RILEY STEVENS: "Wow! Despite being leveled so early in the match, Astro is not disturbed by what just happened at all! Hopefully the censors caught that in time. You’d think you’d know better than to egg on the current number one contender." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Maybe if Astro listened to losers like you, yeah, I can see that, but he doesn’t. The man knows just what he’s doing." While M. Tech was smirking at Kevin, the assault began as Astro launched himself at M. Tech. A quick leg check followed by an enziguri was what we got, but it didn’t knock down Michaud! It did stun him however, Astro hit a second kick sending M. Tech down to one knee. He then ran across the ring, bounced off the ropes, and hit a Busaiku Knee Kick right in M. Tech’s face. With such a sudden combo of attacks, Astro did the smart thing and went for a quick cover 1… 2… KICK OUT! RILEY STEVENS: "This doesn't look good for Michaud." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Finally, something we can agree on!" Jumping back up to his feet, Astro made the poor decision to taunt the crowd as M. Tech stood back up. Turning around, Astro found himself getting lifted up in a suplex position, but M. Tech didn’t drop him just yet. After holding Kevin up in the air for about a minute, letting the blood rush to Astro's head, M. Tech dropped the arrogant challenger down on the top of his head nearly knocking him out. Momentum swung into M. Tech’s corner now and all of the fans were going wild for him. M. Tech went over to the fallen body of Kevin Astro, taking a brief moment to chuckle at his fallen opponent, then lifts him up in powerbomb position. But Astro wasn’t going down without a proper fight! Held up in the air, Kevin began to level with a series of lefts and rights to M. Tech’s unprotected face, causing Michaud to drop Astro back to his feet. A quick super kick from Astro and we see a second Busaiku Knee Kick! However, M. Tech was ready for it this time and rolled out of the way at the last second. Stumbling into the ropes, Astro fell right back into a German suplex from Michaud and slams him down with all of his might on top of the fallen Kevin Astro. Walking over to his fallen opponent, M. Tech grabbed onto Kevin’s legs and began to lock in a Texas Cloverleaf! RILEY STEVENS: "That's all she wrote." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Well, let’s not jump to conclusions here! Astro hasn’t tapped ou-" Putting on a strong fight, Astro finally gave in and slapped the mat as fast as he could. M. Tech released the lock and stepped away from Astro in victory. WINNER via SUBMISSION!: M. TECH MICHAUD! QUOTE Two individuals stood in the ring. Time had taken them here, but it didn’t matter. The flash of their entrance and the style of the music, all glossed over by the time they entered the ring and stood face to face. Christopher Darren Erns versus Jack Waters, and both were top predators on Revolt. Jack had ascended high, whilst CDE cruised the high end of low to destroy his opposition, hoping that Waters would be the next one down. He knew Waters was fresh off of a five-for-fighting war against Tony Miranda, whom now seemed as dead and buried as the alumni roster. Despite presumptions, Jack Waters started the bout with a counter to a swing that he transitioned into a wrist lock. Basic, he knew that, but sometimes it helps to wear your opponent down from the start, even with the little things. This little thing could potentially become something greater, as Jack knew he too couldn’t contend without both wrists working at optimal capacity. He twisted and applied pressure, and twisted some more until CDE managed to push away with a run toward the ropes. That run became more aggressive and as he launched off the ropes and headed back toward his opponent, Jack ducked over in vain. CDE stopped, looked to the crowd, then sent a hard hitting boot to the face of his bent opponent. Whatever Waters thought he’d do was foolish, and now CDE had him in an abdominal stretch. If Waters wanted to start small with a potentially vile submission hold, then so would he. His wrist was already fine when he pressed it against his opponent to stretch the body, bones, and organs of Jack Waters. Jack yelled out, not too loudly as he tried to stay his pain, that, and he found a loop hole which he hooked and sent CDE over with an over-the-shoulder toss. After the thud echoed, Jack dropped down with his very accentuating elbow. The game was old school submission and tech, and these two were playing it well. Borrowing moves and weaving through chain-link penances, Jack and CDE expertly maneuvered with one another a combative dance that captured the audience. After the drop, Jack transitioned them both into an armbar. CDE, however, was not going to have any of that. Using what power he could gather from the depths of his black heart and strong muscles, he hoisted up Jack and then dropped him down onto his back like an unwanted, cradled child. A sickening metaphor, but the image worked. It was now time to perform a screen wipe. He could go on to perform some more old school maneuvers, such as the sleeperhold, stalling vertical suplex, or the single-leg Boston crab, but he was looking for inflicting the most pain in the shortest amount of time. First, he came down on his opponent with a standing knee drop, just to keep him in check. Then, CDE set up his opponent for a spinebuster. As he hoisted his opponent and in the seconds it took for him to swing Jack back down, Waters transitioned it into an implant DDT. He was quicker to apply it and soon it was Jack being able to complete and perform the spinebuster on CDE. Laid out flat, CDE was ripe for the pin, but Waters milked his comeback. He turned toward the crowd at all four angles and pandered to the energy they fed him. When he turned, CDE was already up and ready to strike like a cold viper. It was an uppercut from Hell he performed that wiped the memories right out of Waters’ heavenly state. It was Jack’s turn on the canvas and CDE took the chance for a pin. The unfortunate thing about that was that CDE had only managed to get a two-count before the lights blackened. As soon as they did, they became a dark crimson shade and Scar was in the ring behind him. CDE pulled himself from the canvas and turned to Scar, face to face with a man of Galveston’s dark past. The two shared a glare that pierced one another like daggers, but Scar’s intentions were known when he pushed CDE out of the way. He wanted Jack Waters for himself. Unable to control the situation, the referee turned to end the match just as Scar grabbed hold of CDE’s opponent. Before the call could be made, however, CDE swung Scar’s arm and turned him round for a hard kick to the gut. In this moment, Jack became hyper aware of his surroundings. He tried to rush up to his feet to back away, or defend himself, but CDE had it under control. Scar took a swing, which was ducked under and CDE threw at him another hard boot, then in the second after, kicked Jack Waters in nearly the same motion. Both men reeled back, but only Jack returned for more. Scar had retreated to the outside of the ring and pulled himself away toward the spot where the aisle met the squared off area for the ring.  Still drowned in crimson, CDE ducked under a swing from Jack and performed his Malice Acrimony. The pin was sure and the victory was his. After the match, CDE pulled himself to his feet and turned back toward Scar, who had now entered the ring on the opposite end. The two locked eyes, but Scar, adorned in his black leathers as he did when he was the mysterious figure, backed away. This let CDE take control of the ring and lay claim to his victory in the main event of Revolt. WINNER via Pinfall!: CHRISTOPHER DARREN ERNS!
