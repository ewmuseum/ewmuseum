Pre-show, 14 April 2014

GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: REVOLT PRE-SHOW Live from Sea Wolf Park - April 14th, 2014 QUOTE Coming back to the ring now we have a ring full of several different women in one corner known as the Machinas as across from them we have a returning Chris Callum as well as the debuting Kingston Gray. In one corner the four women, stand conversing as the two men each in their own corner seem to be setting up a game plan in their heads. COLT COX: “A new batch of faces tonight for the Open Battle Royal, one team included in the Machinas, but only one can win. Seems hazardous for a team making their debuts. Especially with a crafty veteran like Kingston Gray in the ring.” AMANDA ROWLAND: “Chris is cute. Glad he answered my call to come back.” Another sigh from Colt as in the ring the four girls have pulled apart from their makeshift huddle to look at their opponents as we ring the bell to get started. DING DING DING! As soon as the match starts it seems that the Machinas have decided to divide and conquer as two of them go after Chris and two of them go after Kingston. Gray who promised not to strike them, seems a bit caught off guard here as Enigma and Orianna start to attack him in the corner as Pearl and Machina do the same to Callum both men clearly not expecting this as they struggle with the numbers. Finally Callum is able to give Pearl a shove to clear some space as he ducks under a Machina punch and throws her back into Pearl who was rushing back at him. Chris charges them both, and takes them out with a double clothesline as these fans seem to be having a hard time picking a favorite as in the other corner the oldest man in the ring Gray locks up with both Orianna and Enigma and starts to push them back then gives them a big toss backwards. Walking back to the ropes Orianna charges him and he ducks under it using his shoulder to throw her over the ropes. These fans think it’s over for her but she manages to land on the ring apron as Enigma charges him and he ducks to the left looking like he’s about to lead her into eliminating her own team mate but she stops herself right before she connects with Orianna on the ropes. COLT COX: “Close call there.” Gray thought he had her there and he starts to walk towards them now but the two connect with a rather well planned tag move out of no where as Orianna clotheslines him from the outside while Enigma takes a shot at his knee in the ring dropping him down as these fans go wild. On the other end Callum has gotten back up after some double team action as Machina and Pearl whip him but they make the mistake of throwing him right at Orianna as without even realizing it Callum shoulder checks her on the outside of the ropes leading to her elimination. Orianna has been eliminated! Just like that these fans watch one of them go down as Quinn Machina who came down with the Machinas runs around to check on her. Callum looks over the ropes surprised a bit by what happened but when he turns around he is met by a double clothesline from Pearl and Machina. The two watch as Gray has got a hold of Enigma and is about to toss her over. He gets her over the ropes but she as well manages to end up catching herself on the ropes as just then Pearl and Machina grab a leg each and start to push him over as on the outside Enigma helps. It takes all three because of just how big Gray is and finally they get him over but he grabs the ropes with one arm and now is on the ring apron along with Enigma as they are dangerously close to being eliminated. He gets up to his feet as all three start to punch and kick him, trying to break his grip as he finally gives both girls in the ring a shove back into the ring. COLT COX: “These three can’t seem to get him!” Despite the numbers, Gray is doing a good job fighting them off. In the ring Machina bounced back into Callum who whipped her into the corner as on the outside Gray caught an attempted kick by Enigma leaving her with one leg, as he walks along with her as she tries her best to keep her balance finally he pulls her leg to the side sending her falling to the outside. Enigma has been eliminated! Just like that as in the ring Callum made the mistake of trying to throw Machina over the ropes near the corner as she kicks him away then comes off the top rope hitting him with a dropkick from the top rope. After getting rid of Enigma, Pearl charges Gray on the ropes now but he sees her coming at the last second and pulls down the ropes and with her momentum going she can’t stop herself and she goes right over to the outside but while Gray is looking out at Pearl, Machina runs across the ring and connects with a Dropkick, getting rid of him as well! Pearl and Kingston Gray have been eliminated! These fans are shocked, Gray was doing so well fighting the numbers but he took his eyes off the ball to make sure Pearl was ok for just a moment and Machina got rid of him. She turns around back at Callum, who gets up in the middle of the ring from her dropkick. She charges him and he comes to life out of no where hoisting her up and then down with a Spinning Spinebuster as these fans are pretty split on who they want to see win as the rest of the Machinas stay on the outside to watch their team mate as Gray starts to head to the back having given a good showing but coming up just a bit short due to the numbers. With just one left now Callum, hoists up Machina and puts her on his shoulders as he looks to dump her out of the ring but right as he’s near the ropes she jumps into a Hurricanrana, nearly sending him over but he manages to hold onto the ropes as she nearly has him over but catching himself he’s able to break her legs and send her over the top rope to an elimination. REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner of the Open Battle Royal, Chris Callum!” These fans are shocked as Machina almost had him with that Hurricarana but he caught himself and Callum is leaving here tonight as the victor as the Machinas gather around her, helping her up as Callum falls back holding up his arm in victory having fought through it all to get the victory. COLT COX: “I got to say everyone in this match showed some promise, Kingston Gray whom promised not to strike them was doing well but the numbers caught up to him. But he took out a few of them clearing the way for Callum who just narrowly avoided elimination before eliminating Machina.” AMANDA ROWLAND: “Call me Chris!” Amanda has stood up now at the announce table to ask him to call her as Callum doesn’t seem to notice as the Machinas gather the troops and head to the back as we fade just a few minutes away from the Main Show. WINNER: CHRIS CALLUM
