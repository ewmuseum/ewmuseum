GIW — Card

Revolt, 30 September 2010

30 September 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: Thursday Night Revolt! LIVE SEPTEMBER 30TH, FROM SEA WOLF PARK IN GALVESTON, TEXAS = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = The noise in the Revolt arena comes to a halt as an all to familiar theme begins to play over the speakers. The once celebrated tone had turned ominous as Blake Beckett, the GIW Chairman made his way down the ring ramp. The crowd watched him with intent, their heads following his every move until he came to a stop square in the middle of the ring. He looked out into the audience for a moment before a microphone was slid into the ring, slamming against his foot. Blake stared down the bell keeper for his foolishness with the technical equipment before quickly bending over and retrieving the microphone. He straightened his jacket as his music slowed and eventually dissolved. Once the music stopped, the boos were more apparent from the crowd. Blake chuckled as they poured into the ring. He began to speak in a mocking tone. BLAKE BECKETT: “Wow. It’s nice to see you worthless people finally so passionate for a cause! It’s nice to see you GIW fans actually not acting like zombies at a GIW show! Well, its to little to late. GIW is about to be a defunct corporation. Finally, we can let it die in peace.” The boos are louder now, seemingly every fan is giving some sort of input. Blake smirks and chuckles to himself. He checks his watch jokingly. BLAKE BECKETT: “For those of you who didn’t watch Unscripted on Sunday… I basically announced that Wake Walker’s secret prize in the case was a one way ticket to EPW, and he could randomly choose five people to go with him. That’s right fools… I stole the GIW champion!” The boos are echoing now, Blake even seems stunned at the onslaught of the GIW fans in attendance. The snickers to himself, and carries on his speech. BLAKE BECKETT: “I brought this company up from nothing. I made the company the powerhouse it is today. The success of Galveston Island Wrestling can be attributed to me, and only me! So, you ask, why would you leave it at its peak? The answer to that is simple… I am not a fool. Galveston Island Wrestling has been on the road to failure ever since I brought in Megathreat and Rowland. The moment I employed them, I began taking a back seat. They were the ones calling the shots, they were the ones raking in the cash! What did they do with their power? The ran a perfectly good brand into the ground. Their brainchild, Affliction, was only the start of their combined incompetence. Then, when Sinclair betrayed me and everyone else… they wanted to merge back. Cut their losses. Make it look like they ended up on top. When GIW went back to one brand, they made even more decisions. They decided who GIW hired, they determined tour dates… they seized my position. What did they think would happen? Did they think they could run a company? Rowland cant run anything… Megathreat had his chance with Undisputed Wrestling Alliance and failed, even I couldn’t save the corporation. The one thing I can applaud Megathreat for is his ability to look toward the future. He KNEW GIW was DOOMED, so HE PACKED HIS BAGS AND LEFT ME WITH THAT FOOL ROWLAND! Sinclair offered me a mirror position in EPW, and I could not logically turn him down. A spot with equal money coming in, no Megathreat? No Amanda Rowland? No ASPEN CHAUD? That sounded like Bliss to me… so I devised a plan.” The crowd gasps when they finally realized Blake was stopping his speech for a moment. They realized how long this had been planned, and how they never noticed it. BLAKE BECKETT: “I realized that I had a clause in the contracts of all the GIW staff and roster that would basically force them to follow me. I COULD have stolen the whole roster. I COULD have just killed this company… but I didn’t. I have a soul, somewhere inside my body. I’d much prefer to watch GIW rot in front of me. I brought this company up from nothing, and you all supported those posers. You make me sick. I hope this company rots in hell.” The camera fades with Beckett looking deep into the camera. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = As we return from the commercial break, Charnel is in the ring with a local wrestler from Galveston. The bell rings, and within seconds Charnel tosses the local star out of the ring. He chuckles at how easy this was and raises his arms confidently into the air. WINNER: BROCK CHARNEL His music is replaced, however, by the music of the acting GIW Revolt General Manager, Amanda Rowland’s. AMANDA ROWLAND: “Why don’t you make your way out of my ring?” Charnel seems confused at Rowland’s sudden demand. He slowly made his way out of the ring, up the ring ramp, and passed Rowland to get backstage. AMANDA ROWLAND: “I don’t want to be here long, but I feel I need to reassure the fans that GIW will still be here next week, and week after, and the week after. I don’t care if we have Beckett or if he is off doing some sort of ridiculous thing… we don’t need cancers like him. All Blake does is tell us what to do, and then chuckle as we do it and he sits on his ass. With Blake gone, ladies and gentlemen, we actually gain 30-40 minutes of television time, now that Blake can’t hog it all up. GIW will be fine. You have my word on it.” And with that, the scene fades away. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = We come in on Blake Becket taking on an important call in his office. BLAKE BECKETT: "Yeah. Yeah. It's all set. Yeah. Talk to you soon. Yeah. Bye." As Beckett concludes the call Alexii, clad in a powder blue buisness suit, enters right. BLAKE BECKETT: "Ah, Alexii. Glad you're here. Take a seat." Beckett gestures to a set of chairs but Alexii refuses. ALEXII RAVYNOVA: "Look, Beckett. I don't know what's so urgent that you need interrupt my business lunchon with my staff, but I don't appreciate it. So, if you'll excuse me, why don't you cut formalities and tell me what you want?" Alexii wasn't ever mistaken for a nice girl, that's for sure. BLAKE BECKETT: "To the point as always, Alexii. Well, I guess I have no choice but to be blunt. This campaign your running to unseat me? It's going to stop." An interceding cheer from the crowd is quickly stifled as Alexii speaks. ALEXII RAVYNOVA: "Oh really? Why on Earth would I stop when I'm so close to winning the hearts and minds of the GIW booking committee?" BLAKE BECKETT: "Because I'm giving you a job. I see a lot of potential in you, Alexii, and I think your skills would better serve working with me than against me. Think about it. You could be my secretary of state. How does the title Senior Vice President of Talent Relationship sounds? I can make it happen, as my last act as GIW Chairman." Alexii narrows her eyes not trusting him completely. ALEXII RAVYNOVA: "What's the catch?" Beckett smiles thinly clearly having something on his mind. BLAKE BECKETT: "No catch. Just a promise. Work with me and I promise you that you'll eventually get a seat in the committee. All you need to do is shake my hand and it's done." Alexii looks at Beckett, looks at his hand, then looks down and off to the side. Finally she looks up Beckett and takes his hand in a firm shake. ALEXII RAVYNOVA: "Alright, Beckett. You got yourself a new V.P." The jeers of the GIW faithful almost drowns out Beckett's address to Alexii as they shake hands BLAKE BECKETT: "I promise you, Alexii, you won't regret this." Alexii exit right and we pan in on Beckett. BLAKE BECKETT: "Keep your cellphone on. I'll call you." The crowd is a bit uneasy now after all, Blake no longer had GIW in his best interest. And this new partnership seemed to scared them just as much as we fade out on the scheming eyes of Beckett. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = We return to the ring where we are already set up for a four way match up between four starts. We have Zen the newcomer with Naomi his manager on the outside. Then we have Chicken one of the comedic wrestlers in the company looking as fierce as ever. The two real stars of the match are also here Joshua Acquin coming off the momentum of beating EPW at ‘Unscripted’ as Tony Miranda is the opposite having lost his belt at the same show. They are all nearly ready to fight when suddenly "I Will Not Bow" by Breaking Benjamin hits as out from the back walks the ‘ION Television Champion’ Jake Kennedy. RILEY STEVENS: “Kennedy isn’t supposed to be out here right now, what is he doing?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Who cares he’s the biggest champion we have left.” Kennedy walks down towards the ring with Tony Miranda glaring out at him the entire time. The two may have given a shake but Miranda still isn’t completing happy about it. Kennedy has a limp as he walks around and sits down next to Riley and Oliver at the booth. RILEY STEVENS: “Looks like we are being joined by Kennedy now.” JAKE KENNEDY: “Your damn right you are.” RILEY STEVENS: “Here I was thinking I heard something about an injury, you seem to be walking just fine tonight.” JAKE KENNEDY: “You didn’t see the limp? I mean come on; the Doctor said I can’t wrestle so…I can’t wrestle. But I figured this show would be missing something with out me.” Back in the ring we see the four of them getting set up to fight with Miranda still staring over at him every once in a while as the referee calls for the bell. DING DING DING! RILEY STEVENS: “You out here to watch Tony Miranda?” JAKE KENNEDY: “Who?” RILEY STEVENS: “Your opponent at ‘Unscripted’ the man whose hand you shook after the match?” JAKE KENNEDY: “Oh yeah that guy, frankly I put him in my rear view mirror. I’ve sort of set my sites a little higher then him, he had his chance.” Back in the ring this thing has started up, with two fights breaking out on both sides of the ring. On one side we have Joshua Acquin beating down Chicken in what is a rather one sided affair the entire time. On the other side of the ring we have Tony Miranda laying into Zen as Zen’s manager tries to motivate him on the outside. Joshua sets up Chicken on the ropes and then crushes him over the top with a big clothesline as on the other side Miranda grabs Zen by his tights and the back of his head and thrusts him out of the ring. The two turn around and it’s Miranda and Acquin the big two in the match and the crowd begins to cheer wanting to see these two guys go at it. They are about to fight when Chicken and Zen slide back in and the two now switch guys that they are fighting. Joshua crushes Zen with a big knee and corners him while Miranda whips Chicken off the ropes and then connects with a big Yakuza Kick. In the corner Acquin sets up into a Flowing DDT! It’s just these two again as they meet up in the ring and begin going at it with punches throwing them all over. They both push each other away and at the same time back up into Zen and Chicken. Joshua tackles Chicken only to fall onto the outside. In the ring Miranda dropkicks Zen in the face. Acquin on the outside connects with a monstrous Running Clothesline! As he gets to his hands and knees he runs forward and hits a huge ‘Last Call’ a Punt to the Skull knocking Chicken clean out. Miranda however has Zen set up before delivering a ‘Lightning Buster’ a Crash Thunder Buster and immediately makes the cover 1…2…3! That’s it Miranda gets the huge win. WINNER: TONY MIRANDA RILEY STEVENS: “I’m thinking he may be right back in line for your belt Jake.” JAKE KENNEDY: “He beat a NOBODY.” Kennedy gets to his feet and holds up his belt taunting Miranda with his belt as Tony stares out at him shaking his head and yelling that it’s his. If Kennedy wasn’t injured these two may just go at it again however suddenly Joshua Acquin spins Miranda around and turns it right into a ‘Face Breaker’ a Kill Switch. The crowd is a bit stunned not thinking that would happen as well. RILEY STEVENS: “What is Josh doing?” Acquin stares down at Miranda as he stares to the outside and calls for what he really is after the belt. It seems as though we have a three way tussle over the title now as Joshua Acquin has just entered himself into the fight as the two stare each other down before we fade away. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = We head to the backstage area where it appears that Blake Beckett has finished his business and is now leaving GIW perhaps for the last time ever. It’s a shocking site for everyone as he strolls through the backstage area, but suddenly he stops. He turns watching as Aspen Chaud is stretching in the area next to him, he takes a second to gaze at her before approaching her. BLAKE BECKETT: “Hey there.” He said nothing else just wanting to see how she’d react as she looks up at him, almost seeming ready to attack him right then and there. But she seems to control herself trying to be the better person. ASPEN CHAUD: “Hi…” Aspen looked forward trying to ignore him a bit as she stretches out her arms, however he doesn’t move clearly poking the bear before he goes. BLAKE BECKETT: “You know it’s a shame that Wake didn’t call your name, it would have been nice having you on our show. I hate to see someone like you in a dying company.” You can tell inside Aspen was angry but she did a good job keeping it in, as she just shook her head a bit as Blake grinned at her for ignoring him. BLAKE BECKETT: “After all I always did feel like we had something, going. I mean before I handed that chair to Chris back at ‘Unscripted’. It really is a shame that a girl as beautiful and talented as you is being wasted like this.” Blake tries to seem sincere but nobody is buying it at all, these two’s long history of hating each other has come back now. BLAKE BECKETT: “Who am I kidding, I won’t miss you. You’re just a pretty little girl who got lucky a few to many times and now people think you’re talented. If I wanted you I would have asked, but you see I am going to enjoy seeing you rot away here.” Blake smirks laying it into Aspen now who seems to be letting it get to her more and more. And before he can say anything else, Aspen turns around and slaps him square across the face and walks off for her match. The crowd cheers its approval, as Blake holds his cheek embarrassed and angry as we head to the ring for the main event. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = After a night of seeing all too much Blake Beckett the fans were ready for the main event. It was time for the match that the entire crowd was looking forward to. We head to the ring where the crowd was a bit relieved to watch some more action tonight. "Bullseye" by Aly and AJ hits and the crowd goes nuts as out from the back comes Aspen Chaud, having just seen her slap the crap out of Beckett they were even more so on her side as usual. She runs out with her arms up, running down the ramp and high fiving the fans on both sides of the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “You saw that slap she just gave Blake, got him right in the kisser.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Oh yeah it’s totally smart to slap the man that has the ability to send several people after her, was just brilliant Riley.” She heads up the ramp and then bends down over the bottom rope, then walking across the ring she leans through the ropes and blows a kiss to the camera as behind her "Impossible" By Anberlin hits and the crowd changes its tune to boos. Out from the back now is Kacie Church with her usual plenty of confidence. She walks down the ramp looking as intense as usual, Aspen turning around and staring out at her. Kacie unlike most girls doesn’t look scared at all, instead she looks completely confident. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I’m just glad that Blake didn’t take Kacie, she’s one of our best for sure.” RILEY STEVENS: “Not often we hear you give women credit.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Well, she’s my type of girl.” Kacie now heads up the steel steps and into the ring, stepping through the ropes as the crowd continues to give her boos. She stares over at Aspen and then steps up onto the ropes holding up her GIW Women’s Championship or what she’d call it the Women’s WORLD title. She steps down and hands it to the referee who hands it out of the ring as the crowd now seemingly realizes just the type of match we are in store for, it’ll be a classic. The referee steps between them and then calls for the bell to start this Dream Match. DING DING DING! The crowd gives one last set of claps cheering both members on showing their support for GIW. Both girls walk around the ring staring at each other. It’s the case of one of the best Technical Wrestlers against easily the best Submission wrestler in Galveston. Both girls seem to have mutual respect, as they go in for a lock up almost as if to see who has more strength. As some may expect it’s Kacie who begins to push her back to the corner. She gets her to the corner and then whips her across the ring into the other corner. Aspen stays there as Kacie runs at her only for Aspen to slip out of the way and go in for a Roll Up 1…2…Kacie pulls over and gets into her own pinning combination 1…2…Aspen again rolls through getting back on the pinning side 1…2…Kacie hurls her off and both girls rush back to there feet. Both of them seem ready to go at it realizing that the other is no slouch. They again go for a Test of Strength which Kacie again wins pushing her back to the ropes. She again hurls her off but as Aspen comes back she ducks a big fist from Kacie. She runs off basically hitting the second rope only as she comes back she’s caught by the ever so smart Kacie into a big Hip Toss. Aspen however gets up right away going back on the attack, stepping into a Dropkick however that sends Aspen reeling to the ropes. Kacie walks across the ring using her foot to force Aspen’s neck up against the bottom rope, pushing her against it. The referee tells her to get off and then begins the count 1…2…3…Kacie backs off just at the four count. Chaud is not looking to great in this contest that’s for sure. She however starts to get up on the outside of the ropes. Kacie turns back to her and then runs at him only for Aspen to get a knee up into her face. Church stumbles back into the ring as Aspen goes Spring Board into a variation of the ‘AirBarbie’ a Crossbody that she takes Kacie down with. Aspen falls to the mat as well both girls down Kacie getting taken off her feet with the big move while her attack has seemingly did a toll on Aspen. RILEY STEVENS: “Kacie sure has been impressive, but Aspen may have just swung the momentum in her favor after that move.” Back in the ring we return where both girls are now getting to there feet. Both are using the ropes as they turn and Kacie runs forward hitting a big punch. Aspen bounces back and responds with a big forearm. Kacie pulls back but this time she outsmarts Aspen hitting a High Leg Clothesline! Aspen falls to the mat but starts to get up but Kacie is waiting, she grabs her and hoists her up and hits a flawless Suplex. The crowd is a bit worried but they know a lot of times Aspen comes from behind in her matches still that’s not generally against great wrestlers like Kacie. Aspen gets to her butt however Kacie takes advantage and locks her in a Rear Naked Choke a move that has made many athletes pass out. The crowd is a bit in doubt as Aspen seems to be ready to pass out but not before being able to very luckily be able to stretch far enough to get her foot on the ropes. The crowd claps as the referee calls for it 1…2…3…4…Kacie releases the hold not to cautiously tossing Aspen out of the way. She shakes her head getting to her feet and backing up. She starts to yell at Aspen to get up and as she does she nearly turns right into ‘The Ice Breaker’ however she shakes free and then doubles Kacie over with a knee to the gut. She runs off the ropes and prepares to end it hitting a ‘Dream Ride’ a Running Axe Kick! The crowd goes nuts this is it, it’s over 1…2…Kick Out! The crowd seems stunned as does Aspen that Kacie kicked out of a move that has ended several girls. RILEY STEVENS: “Wow did not expect that, I thought it was over but instead Kacie kicked out. Can Aspen put her away?” It seems stunning for everyone but Aspen gets to her feet not wanting to waste any time. Aspen looks over at Kacie who is already getting up. She runs at her going for a kick but Kacie rolls out of the way and hops up to her feet. Aspen turns around and runs at her only to be given a stiff kick to the face. Chaud falls to the mat holding her face as Kacie looks down at her and then to the crowd with a bit of a smirk. She gets down and starts to set up; it’s time for The Sharp Shooter! She starts to turn her over getting it set up only for Aspen to give her a big kick in the butt that sends her forward. Aspen gets to her feet and waits for her to turn around and gives her a big Jump Dropkick! The crowd is cheering as she gets to her feet. However suddenly there’s a loud roar as the camera struggles to find what it is. Finally heading down the ramp is Chris Armstrong! The crowd is booing him as he hops up onto the ring apron and calls her out. Aspen turns around getting into her face as Kacie gets up from behind her. The crowd realizes that she’s distracted and wants to take advantage. She runs across the ring at Aspen however Chaud ducks at the last moment and Church crushes Armstrong off the apron, Aspen ducks behind him and catches her with a roll up out of no where 1…2…3! Out of no where! WINNER: ASPEN CHAUD RILEY STEVENS: “That’s it out of no where!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “We got bigger things to worry about…” Back in the ring Aspen hops to her feet as Chris Armstrong now leads the new arrivals of Marcus Hister, Bret Battery, and Jack Prowler into the ring! The crowd is booing the hell out of them as they corner Aspen and star to beat her down. It’s a four on one attack as the crowd gives there boos to them. However Kacie Church soon gets up and to the surprise of many she defends Aspen taking it to Hister. Soon though she is caught by Hister and Prowler who begin a beat down. Suddenly there is another mass of cheers as running out from the back as Tony Miranda and Joshua Acquin even after the earlier fight running down towards the ring alongside Eric Sparrow as they slide in. Miranda crushes Prowler over the ropes as Acquin tosses Battery out of there and finally Sparrow dropkicks Hister out of the ring while Armstrong bails. Acquin, Sparrow, Miranda, and Church all stand tall in the ring as the EPW guys bail to the back as Aspen Chaud gets a microphone and talks. ASPEN CHAUD: “REALLY Blake? I mean REALLY? You thought you could send a message before you go? Did you forget that we have some of the best wrestlers in the world still on our show? See Blake I heard what you said earlier and now that I realize that you won’t go quietly or with honor, then let me tell you this. GIW is STILL the dominant show and if you don’t realize that you’re going to soon enough. You made a big mistake in leaving this much talent here, because Revolt will be JUST FINE! So Armstrong take your little friends and next time you better bring a lot more guys because if you haven’t realized it yet. As much as we all may not like each other, we hate you even more. So go ran back to Daddy and tell him why you failed this time. And if you think about attacking again you best realize that just like ‘Unscripted’ we will always come out on top!.” The crowd cheers as Aspen drops the microphone and watches as the EPW guys back their way out of here as Aspen looks at her teammates nodding realizing that she’s got some back up in this fight.
