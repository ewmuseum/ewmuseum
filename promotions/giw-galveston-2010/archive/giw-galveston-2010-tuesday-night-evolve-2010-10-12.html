GIW — Card

Tuesday Night Evolve, 12 October 2010

12 October 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
EVOLUTION PRO WRESTLING PRESENTS: EVOLVE LIVE FROM THE FOUR STATES FAIR GROUNDS ARENA IN TEXARKANA, TEXAS BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! We come into the ring area where the fans are piling in for another huge Evolve show, the show that in everyone’s minds is the one on the up and up. We come to the announce table where the familiar faces of Austin and Colt are set up for the night. AUSTIN ST. AUDE: “Colt, you ready for a big night because I am. From top to bottom we got a big show for the world. I mean we have Joseph Erns our current Evolve Champion’s little brother making his debut against one of our new stars Ryan Daniels to start off the night, come on that’s pretty cool.” COLT COX: “I suppose it isn’t a completely dreadful match but we actually have ANOTHER GI-Championship defense this week as it’ll be Bryan Battery forced into the deep end going one on one against Wake Walker, think he can do it? Who cares what you think I don’t think he can.” AUSTIN ST. AUDE: “We’ve seen crazier things happen around here. But one of my personal most anticipated matches would have to be Bret Battery versus Austin Acquin. These guys go so far back it’s crazy and this time it’s all about the new and the old in EPW, this one is personal.” COLT COX: “Speaking of personal, our main event is just that and more. I mean we have Jack Prowler and Chris Armstrong former enemies going up against the new blood of EPW, the man that last week defeated Armstrong for his title; Christopher Darren Erns teamed up with Travis Tate. Personally I am rooting for the two men that brought this place up, Prowler and Armstrong.” AUSTIN ST. AUDE: “Your right about that there does seem to be a little battle going on inside between the New and the Old, when I think they should all be focused on GIW, regardless let’s start the show!” = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = And here we are, this first match of Tuesday Night Evolve at the Four States Fair Grounds Arena in Texarkana, Texas! With the crowd buzzing, the first wrestlers’ music to hit the P.A System is nineteen year old Joseph Erns. With 12/21 by AFI blasting throughout the arena, Joseph Erns steps out of the entrance curtain in a very white robe. He makes his way slowly down the entrance ramp, almost god like. He walks up the entrance steps and takes off his rope, getting set for his opponents’ music to hit. AUSTIN ST. AUDE: “It’s interesting to see what the man who calls himself Our God will do against an opponent like Ryan Daniels who has been around here for a while now!” COLT COX: “You bet Austin! We’ll be seeing a great match as Ryan Daniels is looking to back on top here in Evolve, but I doubt that’ll happen, this Joseph Erns looks ready to win!” AUSTIN ST. AUDE:: “You never know what can happen here at Evolve though!” Just then Ryan Daniels theme song Pursuit of Happiness by Kid Cudi blasts throughout the P.A System as he makes his way out of the entrance curtain wearing an Evolve T-Shirt with the usual DC hat. Ryan Daniels looks set and ready for this match with the fans chanting RD as he walks down the entrance ramp, slapping fans hands. Finally he slides under the bottom ring rope as the tension between Joseph Erns and Ryan Daniels begins to build up as the crowd is fired up and ready for this match. The referee looks at both men, assuring they are ready to go as he calls for the bell. DING! DING! DING! The crowd goes into an uproar the two men begin to circle each other Joseph Erns jerks forward looking for the grapple but Ryan Daniels moves backwards, they continue to circle the ring as Ryan Daniels drops to try a grapple but this time Joseph Erns sees it and avoids it too. The two men move together in the middle of the ring as they grapple. Joseph Erns wins the first encounter and puts Ryan Daniels in a headlock. Ryan Daniels breaks it and locks back up into a grapple before Joseph Erns counters with a quick Snap Suplex. AUSTIN ST. AUDE: “Looks like Our God Joseph Erns is going to take away with this!” COLT COX: “Austin, the match just started, let’s not jump to conclusion here.” Joseph Erns quickly drops to the floor and drops a knee to the shoulder of Ryan Daniels before climbing back to his feet and following up with a second, he goes to lock in an Armbar, but before he gets chance Ryan Daniels deals a few punches to the head and climbs back to his feet. Ryan Daniels punches Joesph Enrs in the head before trying to bounce off the ropes walking straight into a Tornado DDT, one of Joseph Erns signature moves. The crowd cheer somewhat as he goes for the cover. 1...2. Kickout! Joseph Erns goes and begins to beat down on the face of Daniels but Ryan pokes him in the eye before jumping back to his feet, he bounces off the rope and comes back with a Spinning Wheel Kick catching Erns by surprise as he falls to the floor. COLT COX: “See Austin, don’t count Ryan Daniels out of it after the first minute of the match!” Joseph jumps to his feet and tells Ryan to bring it and Ryan goes to kick Erns but he catches his foot only to receive an Enziguri to the back of the head. Joseph drops to the floor holding his head as Ryan Daniels runs against the ropes and drops a High Drop Elbow to the middle of Joseph’s back as he yells out in pain. Daniels continues to work on the back. COLT COX: “This is wrestling with strategy; take out an area of strength and well your opponent will struggle.” Daniels pulls Joseph Erns up before Back Body Dropping him to the middle of the ring. Ryan Daniels points to the top rope as he jumps to the top while Joseph climbs back to his feet and using all his strength he throws him off the top but Daniels lands on his feet, the crowd cheers as Joseph follows up on Ryan with a Bulldog. Joseph Erns stretches his back before going for the cover once more 1...2.. again another kickout. Joseph Erns pulls himself up and Ryan Daniels does the same as they have a standoff in the middle of the ring. COLT COX: “Well that was a waste we are right back where we started.” AUSTIN ST. AUDE: “Well, as of right now it’s been pretty even!” COLT COX: “Never has a more obvious statement been uttered.” Joseph Erns goes to kick Ryan Daniels in the legs but he blocks it and kicks back, Erns tries again Daniels blocks it with his shin and goes to kick Joseph again but he grabs the foot and before Ryan can hit an Enziguri again Joseph Erns ducks, his head sending Ryan Daniels to the mat and he quickly puts Daneils in a Sleeperlock. Daniels tries to fight it but Erns locks it in. AUSTIN ST. AUDE: “This can really drain a wrestler this move.” Ryan Daniels continues to try fight it but gradually he begins to fade as the referee goes to move in and grab Daniels’ arm to see whether there is any motion but before he has chance Ryan Daniels climbs to his feet and falls backwards causing Joseph Erns to break the hold, Ryan Daniels follows up with a Standing Somersault Leg Drop across the throat of Joseph Erns. Ryan drops into a cover 1...2..Kickout! Ryan tries it again 1... not even a two. COLT COX: “I never understand why if you fail with the cover the first time. Why try again?” Ryan Daniels puts his hands against his mouth wondering what he has to do to get the job done! He begins to stomp on the chest of Erns knocking the wind out of him before grabbing Erns’ head over the middle of the ropes. Daniels runs off the far rope and attempts to drops a leg over the back of the head of Erns, but is unsuccessful! Joseph gets to his feet quickly and hits Daniels with a textbook Dropkick. With Daniels laying flat out on the mat not knowing what hit him, Joesph runs at the ropes then executes a backflip and lands on Ryan Daniels, a perfect Springboard Moonsault! Joseph Erns then covers his opponent! 1...2... AUSTIN ST. AUDE: “Could this be it?” The referee stops the count. COLT COX: “Great ring presence from Ryan Daniels he got his foot on the ropes!” Joseph Erns cannot believe it as Ryan Daniels begins to climb back to his feet he uses the ropes to get to his feet as Joseph Erns goes to hit him with a Flying Cross Body but Daniels spots it and ducks to the canvas as Erns won’t let him off the easy and hits a Double Knee Drop to the back of Ryan Daniels. Erns picks up Daniels by the hair and tosses him over to the ropes, and jumps up just in time so he lands on Ryan Daniels to execute a perfect Hurricanrana! AUSTIN ST. AUDE: “Joseph Erns has takin’ this match; it looks like the end is near!” Will his pray lying flat out on the canvas, showing no signs of moving, Joseph Erns heads to the top turnbuckle. With the crowd somewhat behind him, he stands up and jumps into the air executing a backflip in mid-air followed by a stunning twist then landing elbow first on Ryan Daniels, an amazingly stunning move Joseph Erns calls Shining Moonlight Strike! He then hooks the leg of Daniels. 1...2...3! AUSTIN ST. AUDE: “Joseph Erns dominated that match!” COLT COX: “No he didn’t Austin, Ryan Daniels was just off his game tonight” AUSTIN ST. AUDE: “Well, congratulations to Joseph Erns to winning his debut match! Don’t go anywhere, we still have a lot of great wrestling to come!” Joseph celebrates to the back however suddenly from the back come Bret Battery and Chris Armstrong both of them making a B-Line for the ring as the crowd starts to murmur. Ryan Daniels is just now getting to his feet when Battery kicks him in the gut hard. The fans boo as Battery lifts him up and pushes him back into the corner as Armstrong is in the corner waiting as he pushes him up there and right into a huge ‘Money Maker’ a Top Rope Powerbomb! AUSTIN ST. AUDE: “What is this about!” COLT COX: “I think you know what it’s about, these two Originals are fighting back.” With Daniels out its Bret’s turn as he heads up to the top rope and then nails him with a huge 450 Splash! Both men stand over the fallen and now seemingly done Ryan Daniels both of them screaming out “This is our show” as we take a break. WINNER: JOSEPH ERNS = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = We cut to the parking lot area where David Sinclair is standing next to a group of maybe seven guys donning Security get ups. DAVID SINCLAIR: “They think that they are going to sneak in and pull a fast one on us by taking us out. As far as I know it’s supposed to be a pretty girl and a stoner, trying to sneak in here./ So make sure that neither get in here. They’ll make no statement at our expense.” The security guards nod there heads as Sinclair walks away as the segment comes to a close quick yet effective. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = Bryan Battery can be seen in the ring already with his music playing in the background. He is announced by the ring announcer and poses for the crowd. He looks excited for this match. “Dazed and Confused” begins to play. Wake Walker does his usual entrance which takes approximately three minutes. As he is walking down the ramp slowly with his title belt around his waist, the match is announced to be a match for the Galveston Island title. Some of the fans cheer for this since they don’t appear to like Wake Walker so much in this brand. Once the two men are in the center of the ring, the referee checks both of them for weapons and starts the match. COLT COX: “This‘ll be an interesting match.” AUSTIN ST. AUDE: “Biased on what I saw last week from this new Walker guy, Bryan better pull out every single inch of his being out in this match if he wants to even see any sort of possibility of winning.” Wake Walker and Bryan Battery starts in a test of strength but Wake Walker quickly swats away Bryan like a fly. Bryan charges at Wake Walker only to be hit by a big boot. Wake Walker then picks up Bryan Battery and hit’s the Temple Of Love. Wake Walker puts his foot on top of Bryan. 1...2.…3. Well, that was fast. A record 14 seconds. What a great showing by Bryan Battery. COLT COX: “What? That‘s it?! Jesus Christ! ” AUSTIN ST. AUDE: “Wow. I continue to be impressed. This guy may just be what EPW needs. With him, EPW will certainly destroy GIW soon enough. However… who‘s side is he even on?” WINNER: WAKE WALKER (Still Galveston Island Champion) = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = We head to the backstage area where it’s Joseph Erns and Christopher Darren Erns having a minor celebration of a sort both talking when it’s Travis Tate that pops up in between them with a big smile. TRAVIS TATE: “Hey there guys, just thought I’d run by and see how my partner is doing tonight. I didn’t think we needed too much pre-game because after all, the two of us have already teamed and we did a good job at that. Besides our opponents can’t even spend a second in the same room without fighting should be a cake walk.” CDE looks over at the always comedic Tate with a smile looking at him with his brother who doesn’t seem to willing of the advice, as Erns gives Tate a small fist pound as they’ve become friends due to there teaming at ‘Unscripted’. CHRISTOPHER DARREN ERNS: “I was just trying to tell my brother not to get to confident way to go. But seriously you do know what’s at stake tonight right? Not only do these two guys want to crush us, but if your pinned you don’t get a title shot. However don’t think that’ll change how I wrestle, I consider you a friend and if it means having less odds at keeping this belt then so be it I’d rather beat these guys. Erns taps his belt still happy about the win in the first place as he begins to walk off with Tate getting prepared for there match as the segment ends. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = ”Eruption” by Van Halen begins to play. The crowd erupts into a series of cheers as their hero, Bret Battery walks down the ramp. He make sit onto the ring and then stands on one of the turnbuckles and is so kind as to pose for the crowd. The crowd eats it all up and he goes back onto the canvas. He waits for his opponent. "Re-Education" by Rise Against The Machine blasts through the speakers. Austin Acquin, trying to get revenge on Bret Battery runs to the ring. As soon as he’s in, the referee starts the match since he already knows there will be little to no control in this match. COLT COX: “You can expect chaos here..” AUSTIN ST. AUDE: “No kidding.” Both men begin to brawl it out. The referee tries to control the match but eventually finds that it is useless. He lets the match carry on until finally, Bret Battery kicks Austin on the knee and then gets up and hit’s a drop kick. Austin bounces off the ropes and tries to hit a clothesline. Bret dodges it and then hit’s a rolling kick square on the face of Acquin. Cover. 1...2... Kick out! Acquin slowly gets up. Bret Battery goes for a knee smash to the face but Acquin counters it with a back body drop. Acquin then slowly begins to wait for Battery to get up. He can feel the momentum shifting in his favor. He tries to hit the Killswitch on Bret Battery but Bret manages a small package! 1...2...3! Battery wins! AUSTIN ST. AUDE: “A rather quick match. Looks like Acquin wasn‘t able to get his revenge, after all.” However after the match rather then letting Austin Acquin get to his feet Bret wastes little time, in setting him up for a Canadian Destroyer. The fans boo as Battery crushes him with the move, knocking Acquin out cold having taken out two new guys tonight perhaps for good. WINNER: BRET BATTERY = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = We head to the backstage area where it’s just before the main event and Jack Prowler is getting ready for the match. However in a almost coincidental resemblance to last week’s events, he’s about to leave when it’s Chris Armstrong he runs into. JACK PROWLER: "What are you doing here?" Prowler seems just as annoyed as Armstrong did last week by his rude entrance however this time it’s Armstrong keeping his cool clearly having come here with a message. CHRIS ARMSTRONG: "Calm down, I know that you don’t like me just like I don’t like you. However as you may have seen earlier Bret Battery and I are taking a bit of a stand around here. We took out Ryan Daniels and he’s not coming back. Tonight I say we do the same thing with CDE and Travis Tate, because we were the one’s who made this show not them. I may not like you Jack but we’d both be lying if we said that we both benefit from each other’s presence. Without you I’d have had no competition around here. But regardless I thought I’d swing by and send a message because I know you hate them coming in here just as much as I do." Armstrong pats him on the shoulder and walks off having said what he came here to say as Prowler glares at him soaking in what he’s said as they both seem ready for the big main event. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = The crowd is smiling as Travis Tate stands in the ring stretching out his arms ready for the match when “Bring It On” by Nathaniel Dawkins hits as the crowd roars CDE may well be one of the most popular stars in Evolve as he walks out from the back, with a big smile on his face as he heads down towards the ring. He high fives a few fans and then slides into the ring as the crowd continues to clap him on. He undoes his Evolve title and then heads top rope proudly showing the belt off to the fans. AUSTIN ST. AUDE: “There is our new champion, Christopher Darren Erns! Talk about a big impact the guy has exploded his way onto the scene here in Evolve with that title win.” COLT COX: “Have you been seeing what has happened to the new guys tonight? Ryan Daniels GONE. Austin Acquin GONE. You really think he’ll be any different?” Erns jumps down and hugs his partner Tate both sharing a mutual respect for one another as “Turn Up The Trouble” by Airbourne hits and the fans begin to throw out the boo birds as Chris Armstrong as he walks out from the back. We cut to a split screen of CDE as well as Armstrong’s faces and the hatred is very much so mutual. Armstrong walks down to the ring and then heads up the ramp, watching as the referee hands out his old title to the man on the outside. COLT COX: “Let’s go Chris! This guy is what made us and now he’s just a contender to Erns? This is a joke.” Armstrong walks to his corner glaring over at Erns as Jack Prowler’s theme song hits and the fans begin to give a very mixed reaction as the good looking star heads out from the back heading right for the ring. He ignores the fans and then jumps up onto the ring apron and joins his partner in the ring, these two as a team is a little comedic. COLT COX: “I sincerely hope that Prowler joins Armstrong and Battery in taking out the new guys tonight, they are the real stars around here.” The two have a minor fighting over who starts however it’s Prowler that gets to start as Armstrong heads to the apron as CDE will start on the other side as we ring the bell to get this thing underway. DING DING DING! The two men in the ring begin to circle each other as the fans cheer on what should make for a very good match. In fact as far as we know for now these two may soon be competing in a title match if things go how they seem. The two go in to start the match as they lock up however it’s CDE that gets the early advantage as he is able to spin around him and lock in a Sleeper Hold. The crowd cheers as Prowler struggles to get out before backing Chris back to the ropes and then tossing him forward off the other ropes. However Prowler’s big clothesline attempt is ducked as CDE stops on a dime and catches him with a big Superkick! The crowd cheers showing it’s approval as Christopher gets to his feet nodding his head getting some steam behind him as he waits for him to get up. Prowler gets up and is immediately whipped into CDE’s corner. Where Darren Erns runs and nails him with a big clothesline and then tags in Tate. Tate jumps into the ring and with Erns holding Prowler, he allows him to hit a big kick to the mid-section. CDE heads out of the ring and then Tate tags him back in and holds out Prowler’s arm as Erns goes top rope and jumps off with a Double Axe Handle to the arm of Prowler these two showing off some early tag work. As Prowler falls back into the ring and tries to head towards his corner only for Erns to run across the ring and tackle him to the ground. The crowd cheers as Erns waits for him to get up however Prowler catches him off guard when he gets up with an Enziguri! Prowler gets up and angrily tags in Armstrong, upset about the way he started the match as the always cocky Armstrong hops in and begins to circle around Darren Erns. Erns gets to his feet and turns right into Armstrong who attempts to end this early with a ‘Loaded’ however Erns is able to roll out of it and make a pin attempt 1…2…Kick Out! The fans let out a big sigh as Erns gets to his feet and with Armstrong getting up a split later in front of him he grabs him and nails him with a German Suplex! The crowd goes nuts as Erns turns and sees Tate calling for a tag. Erns walks over giving his guy a chance as he makes the tag. AUSTIN ST. AUDE: “Erns has them reeling and he tags in the fresh Travis Tate!” Tate jumps into the ring and with a head of steam nails Chris with a big clothesline and then follows it up with another one! The crowd is getting behind this team as he waits for him to get up. However as he reaches down to try to get him up its Prowler that distracts the referee long enough for Armstrong to nail him with a Low Blow! The crowd boos and when CDE tries to get into the ring the referee runs over and stops him as Prowler jumps in and grabs Armstrong and the two hit a flawless Double Suplex! Prowler quickly slides out as the referee turns around as Armstrong goes for the pin attempt 1…2…Kick Out! The fans cheer happy that wasn’t it as Armstrong angrily lifts him up and then whips him into the corner. Armstrong walks across and starts to lay into him in the corner until the referee pulls him away however when he does Prowler leans across and rakes the eyes. Armstrong makes the tag as Prowler jumps in and as Tate beaten tries to walk to his corner he runs across the ring stopping him with a Running Bulldog! The fans boo as CDE isn’t able to get in. However in a last bit of effort Tate kicks him away and makes one last effort to get to his corner with the claps of CDE and the fans cheering him on. However Prowler whips him right next to Armstrong. Then in a huge cheap shot runs across and nails CDE off the ring apron. He walks back and sets Tate up in the corner and then nails him with a ‘Wolfenstein’ a Reverse Frankensteiner! The crowd is actually in awe after that one however when he goes to make the pin the referee forces him out of the ring as Armstrong jumps in the ring and jumps on Tate for the pin 1…2…3! AUSTIN ST. AUDE: “If he was trying to get Prowler on his team, I can almost assure you that wasn’t the way to do it.” COLT COX: “It’s called teamwork Austin.” Erns gets up on the outside of the ring and then slides back into the ring to check on Tate as Armstrong slides out of the ring holding up his arm like he just did that all by himself. Prowler stands on the outside glaring at him, Armstrong’s ego perhaps ruining any chances of this team working. We head back into the ring and with Tate out of the picture now we get a odd look at Erns in the ring, Prowler on the outside, and Armstrong at the top of the ramp these three soon ready to face off as we head to a commercial break, this show seemingly not over yet. WINNERS: CHRIS ARMSTRONG & JACK PROWLER = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = Evolve’s theme song begins to play. The entire crowd begins to cheer. They give a standing ovation to Evolve’s leader. Sinclair begins to walk down the ramp, with a really cocky strut. He goes up the steel steps and enters the ring. He starts having a chat with some of the members of the front row until his song dies out. Once it does, Sinclair waits for the crowd to simmer down. Once it does, he gets a microphone and then begins to speak. DAVID SINCLAIR: “It has come to my attention that a lot of you are wondering whether Wake Walker will actually serve as a means to get the upper hand on Revolt and I tell you all, let this be heard from the source himself - Wake Walker is indeed going to help us defeat Revolt once and for all. You see, it’s not about agreement… Walker and myself have come to terms. Walker has said that he wants to prove how much better he is than those losers back at Revolt by helping us beat them. Not only that but he also said…” Led Zeppelin’s, “Dazed And Confused” begins to play. The crowd immediately begins too and cheer at the same time. Wake Walker makes his way down the ramp. He walks up the steps and enters the ring. He quickly grabs the microphone away from Sinclair’s hand and then begins to speak angrily. WAKE WALKER: “None of what you’re saying is true. You see, you’re just showing all of these people just what type of coward you really are. I have not agreed to helping EPW and never will. EPW is an absolute joke of a company and I proved that tonight when I destroyed your previous Battery brother. You need to show me some respect and maybe down the line, I might lift a finger for you. In the meantime, I’m just going to keep on destroying your little pathetic wrestlers.” Sinclair doesn’t seem too happy with what he’s hearing from Wake. He snatches the microphone away from Wake back and then begins to speak. Eerily, he does so with a ton of confidence that could only possibly lead to one thing - some sort of plan that has been formulated in his mind at this very second. DAVID SINCLAIR: “Is this really the way you want to act towards me, Wake? Is this really how you want it to go down? You will have to help me, whether you like it or not. And if anything, the only person showing anybody respect between us two is YOU to ME.” Sinclair punches Wake Walker in the face which literally does no damage. Wake takes a step forward. Sinclair cowardly runs away from the ring. As he’s running up the ramp and Wake is laughing at him, two people are behind Wake! Wake turns around. It’s Eric Sparrow and Kacie Church! Sparrow charges at Walker only to be hit by a clothesline and foiled. However, Kacie Church lands an amazing flying kick to Walker which sends him to the floor. Eric Sparrow recovers from his clothesline and starts hitting Walker along with Kacie. After about thirty seconds of ambush, Kacie goes to the outside and grabs a chair. However, Wake quickly stands up, grabs Sparrow and throws him to the outside. Kacie Church swings the chair at Wake who dodges it. Wake tries to hit Kacie but then just stares at her for a few seconds, possibly debating amongst himself what to do. However, Kacie pulls the trigger and smashes Wake across the head. Kacie keeps hitting Wake until he appears lifeless. Eric Sparrow goes to the top rope and hits a flying splash on Walker. The two leave the ring with Wake practically left dead in the middle of the ring. Sinclair runs back to the ring, grabs a microphone and starts making fun of Wake Walker. Wake sits up, holding his head as Sinclair speaks. DAVID SINCLAIR: “Hah! You see what happens?! I didn’t even have to ask them for help…Walker! Even our enemies hate you! For the sake of avoiding more situations like these… you have to pick a side Wake, and the side for you to be on is Evolve. Face it!” Wake Walker is given the microphone by Sinclair who is crossing his fingers, hoping Walker joins. Walker laughs at Sinclair, pushes him to the ground and says, “no.” Sinclair stomps his way to the back as Walker has to be helped to the back. Although he’s hurt, Walker appears to have a smirk on his face.
