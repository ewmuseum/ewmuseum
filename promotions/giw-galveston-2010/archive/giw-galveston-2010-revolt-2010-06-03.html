GIW — Card

Revolt, 3 June 2010

3 June 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: REVOLT! Live: From the San Luis Hotel and Resort. “Here's to the crazy ones, the misfits, the rebels, the troublemakers, the round pegs in the square holes... the ones who see things differently -- they're not fond of rules... You can quote them, disagree with them, glorify or vilify them, but the only thing you can't do is ignore them because they change things... they push the human race forward, and while some may see them as the crazy ones, we see genius, because the ones who are crazy enough to think that they can change the world, are the ones who do.” This is Galveston Island Wrestling: Dare to Defy BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! Fireworks crackle above the San Luis Hotel and Resort… the jam packed crowd of 650 wrestling fans have finished filing into the outer pavilion area of the San Luis Hotel and Resort, and all of them are more than happy to be there. Interest has been booming since the federation began putting out magazine, television, and radio ads, and most of the people are just here to see what the fuss is about. The crowed is situated on the North, West, and South sides of the ring, with a rough looking entrance ramp coming from the Hotel Plaza to the east. On the ring apron, right in the middle of the ring there is a SAN LUIS HOTEL logo, and on the sides there is the 104.1 radio station logo. Hanging around the arena-area are 42” LCD TVS flashing the GIW Logo. Inside the ring, three people are standing, the three commentators for the show: Paul Marshall, Allison Beckett, and Colt Cox. Paul has the microphone, and after testing to get the amps just right, he begins to speak. Paul Marshall: Ladies and gentlemen! Let me be the first to welcome you to the future of the wrestling world as we know it… welcome to Galveston Island Wrestling, right here from the San Luis Hotel and wonderful Spa! Tonight, you witness the dawn of a new age, the birth of your wrestling, the establishment of the soon-to-be finest wrestling promotion on the Earth… how does that make you feel? The crowd blows up with cheers, whistles, and applause. Several fans let loose streamers into the ring, others were to busy clapping. Paul Marshall slips the microphone over to Colt Cox, who clears his throat rather loudly before he begins to speak, or tries to over the madness. Colt Cox: Not only is this fed the future of wrestling, but the young talent inside it make up the future of wrestling! Tonight you will see old faces such as Aspen Chaud, Paul Defias, Scott Rydell, and the Acquin brothers… but you will also see the future: Jake Kennedy, Rocky Davis, James Pheanox, and Ima Goner. This fed is great because we do not discriminate based on how well you are known, as we aren’t well known yet either, but we judge you based on character, charisma, and of course, in ring ability. Everyone here is given a fair shot… regardless of your past. Colt Cox hands the microphone off to Allison Beckett, who seems to be just giddy to even be standing in the middle of the ring. She smiles a lot, and after a while regains her composure over the roar of the crowd. Allison Beckett: I don’t know what to say… you guys are just awesome! Tonight is a night where we crown our first champion ever! Awesome right? Will it be the beautiful Aspen Chaud, the feisty Paul Defias, or the talented Scott Rydell? But hey! That’s not the only match, is it Michelle? Suddenly, all of the televisions hanging around the arena-area light up with the face of backstage interviewer Michelle Perez who is standing in front of the hotel pool, behind her seems to be a dressing room area with random stars, trainers, and technicians walking in and out. She seems to be ignoring the chaos that’s going on behind her, including the overweight man in the hot tub for no reason. Michelle Perez: It sure isn’t Allison, in fact the show will start off with a backstage talent battle royal! Anyone who didn’t get placed on this weeks card had a chance to apply for the match, even we don’t know who is in it, it will be a surprise. After that, we have a match between the newcomer James Pheanox and the grizzled veteran who is slimming down a bit, Wake Walker! But that STILL isn’t all GIW has to offer on the debut of Revolt, is it Mitch? The televisions change over to the image of Mitch Conner, standing on the Galveston beachside. He is wearing a suit, and looks really out of place with the groups of tourists wading in the surf behind him. Mitch Conner: That’s right Michelle, we also have Austin Acquin, a grown man taking on a Ladder and two zombies… that’s a match that shows people this fed is really serious about becoming a powerhouse! Then, of course, we have the new guy who idolizes Chris Jericho, Jake Kennedy against the meathead walking muscle bag that we call Rocky Davis. This looks to be a ‘great’ show… quite frankly.. I’m glad I get to sit on the beach interviewing bystanders. Back to you Paul. The televisions turn off and the crowd redirect their attention to Paul Marshall, standing in the ring with an amazed look on his face. He brings the microphone back up to his mouth. Paul Marshall: And with that, let me get out of the ring, and the show will begin! Ring the bell Allison! Ring……..ring..ring…ring……………..ring..ring….riiiiinnnnggg!!! Paul Marshall: We will work on that, OK Ally? BATTLE ROYAL MATCH Heading out to the ring with little real appeal comes Shayne Helms as the fans give him little attention even as he yells out at them. They give a half hearted boo as he steps into the ring and cockily pretends to Air Guitar as the fans boo the man known as ‘The Rockstar’. Soon the fans are much happier when “Kings Will be Kings" by Helloween hits and out from the back comes Stoner! He brings some excitement as he high fives the fans on his way to the ring, he slides on in and points over at Shayne and says he’s going down then heads top rope and pounds his chest. Both men seem ready to go as the fans have chosen their guy. Colt Cox: Here we go Stoner a Texas City boys against The Rockstar, no secret that the fans are behind the guy from Texas. The fans hear this and only seem to get even more so behind Stoner as the two seem about to lock up. Before they get their Shayne stops and start to play the air guitar again while Stoner sits back and laughs. Stoner chuckles and points at Helms, then connects with a big forearm to Helms face shutting him up to a big cheer from the fans. Helms bounces off the ropes and runs back at Stoner only to be caught with a huge dropkick! The fans cheer him on as he hops up with Helms and connects with a big throwing hip toss. Helms ends up on the outside of the ring getting to his feet and seeming upset by the fact that he’s not doing well at all while Stoner panders to the crowd. Shayne heads back into the ring at the referee’s six count. He calls for a lock up which Stoner agrees to, but Helms instead connects with an eye poke. He runs off the ropes and seems about to Lariat Stoner’s head off, but Stoner ducks it and on the rebound walks right into The Knee! He goes for the cover 1…2…No! Stoner a little weary of this even though he’s dominating gets to his feet and heads up to the middle rope in the corner Helms gets up and off the top Stoner hits a Dropkick of Doom! Helms seems done but regardless Stoner pulls his lifeless body to his feet and he hits The Roachclip a Vertebraker! He gets on top 1…2…3! There he is with a very dominating win. Paul Marshall: Your winner of this open battle royal is Stoner! The fans cheer as Stoner with the dominating victory exits and high fives the fans at ringside all the way to the back as we continue with the show. As the fans were calming down after the match, the video feed of Blake Beckett begins to show on the TVS. He is sitting behind a desk in the building we saw earlier in the fed’s time. There is a filing cabinet and bookcase in the right corner, and the window on the side of the room traded in sun rays for moon beams this time around. Blake’s office is pretty dark, only a lap in the corner is lit, casting a shadow on one side of his face. Blake is leaning back in his chair with a smirk on his face. Suddenly, the sound of knocks on the door breaks through the silence, and Blake seems annoyed. He turns to the door and yells, “Come in”! The door slinked open and Paul Defias poked his head through the door. Paul Defias: Well, hello there Mr. Beckett, I was just in the neighborhood so I thought I would pay you a visit… why is it so damn dark in here? Paul Defias flips the light switch on and the room is instantly filled with light. Blake winces and holds his hands above his eyes. Paul winces as well. Paul Defias: Aw…dammit! Jesus, where did you get these bulbs? The sun? You better call the maintenance staff and get some new ones in here… people are gonna think we are making stars. Blake laughs as Paul flips the switch, dimming the room once again. Blake motions for Paul to sit down in the chair on the other side of the desk. Blake Beckett: Paul… can I ask you something? Blake gets an evil smirk on his face and cocks his head toward Paul, who is now sitting with his legs crossed in the chair. Paul Defias: Uh…yeah? What about? Blake clears his throat. Blake Beckett: Aspen and Acquin… whats up with them? Are they dating? Married? Is there something I’m missing? I really don’t want Aspen to win the match because of some chump guest referee with a crush on her screws the rest of you. Paul laughs, uncoils his legs and leans forward in the chair with a smirk on his face that matches that of Blake’s. He peruses his lips before speaking again. Paul Defias: Ya know Blake, I don’t know the answer to that question.. Maybe a sense of loyalty or something because Aspen carried Acquin in other feds, maybe there is something more. Honestly, I don’t care… but I agree that we cant let Aspen and Acquin handicap this fed with their little group like they did in Legends. That’s why I really came. Because I need your help. Blake seems interested. Paul Defias: Aspen and Joshua are going to do whatever it takes together to win this match and start taking over… you don’t want that to happen do you Blake? So, IF, and I emphasize IF, Aspen wins this match… strip her immediately. I’m not saying you should then hand the belt to me… but you can at least trust me with it… unlike Aspen… she is shifty. Blake tries to control himself from bursting out with laughter. Finally he cant contain it, but he only lets out a few muffled chuckles. He regains his composure and turns back to Paul. Blake Beckett: Give the title to you?!? I’d rather give it to someone who was a bit shady than give it to a wannabe con-man like yourself. Look at you, trying to convince me to give you free titles when you know your screwed in the match… but hell, you just earned some major karma points just now. Paul, I’m not gonna give you free anything… we are in Texas now, you gotta work for stuff here. However, I think we could work together… Aspen has got to go down… we can do it as a team. You need someone in the front office and I need someone to do my dirty work in the ring. Tonight, only you or Scott has a chance to win gold… Aspen is just SOL. Deal? Blake smiles and extends his hand to Paul. Paul looks at it, evaluating the situation… but finally gives into temptation and shakes Blake’s hand. The TVS fade out, and are replaced by Paul Marshall who is outside the ring with a microphone in his hand. Paul Marshall: Ladies and gentlemen, get ready for one of the most anticipated matches of the night… Wake Walker vs. James Pheanox! James Pheanox comes out. He seems to be a little full of himself. Despite nobody making a single noise, he yells at the crowd and says, "Shut up!". He enters the ring and then snatches the microphone away from the announcer. He introduces himself by saying, "I'm James Pheanox, and be prepared to be impressed". James watches as Wake Walker comes out. "Horsepower" begins to play. Wake Walker comes out and puts his head down. Then he looks up in a cheerful way and begins to circle the ring, slapping the hands of all of the fans. He looks at James Pehanox and then enters the ring. He gets on one of the turnbuckles and then yells, "Watch the new Wake Walker!" He gets off and then has a stare down with Pheanox. The bell rings. Wake immediately rushes at James but it appears to be a faint. James flinches. Wake laughs at James and then tries to scare him again only to be hit by an enormous clothesline. James picks up Wake and throws him to the turnbuckle and begins to hit a couple of heavy blows to the body and the face. The referee tries to break them up both because he has cornered Wake on the turnbuckle and is using closed fists. James stands in the middle of the ring when suddenly, Wake rushes at him once again only to be carried and hit with a devastating spine buster. James goes to the top of the ropes and then poses for the camera and the crowd. He flies and hits a large elbow drop! He goes for a pin and only gets a two count! James proceeds to lock in a chokehold on Wake, which lasts about 45 seconds in which the referee tests Walker three times to see if he still is awake. Wake begins to gain power from the crowd and tries to elbow the gut of James to get out only to be hit a clubbing blow. James sets up Walker for a power bomb and hits it! James goes for the cover and only gets a two count! James seems to be growing a little frustrated. He picks up Wake, expecting him to counter in some way but he doesn't. Eventually, James just finds himself standing there. He kicks Wake in the gut to stall a little bit and then follows it up with a neck breaker. He begins to start stomping on Wake a bunch of times. Pheanox keeps the assault going until Wake rolls to where the ropes are, forcing the referee to break up the stopping. James picks up Walker slowly and then begins to yell at the crowd, "This is the great Wake Walker? Is this all he's got?". Wake punches Pheanox once in the gut but then is hit by another clubbing blow to the back. Pheanox gets on top of Walker and then locks in a camel clutch, which is quite unusual considering his fighting style. Wake tries to roll his way out of it and succeeds! He has somehow managed to get Pheanox on his back, however, Wake only gets a two count. Wake begins to crawl towards the turnbuckle. James quickly gets up and then hits a flying forearm on Wake. Wake seems really dazed. James follows up by Irish whipping Walker to the other turnbuckle. James rushes at Wake and hits a clothesline. Wake walks in a dizzy way to the center of the ring whilst Pheanox bounces off the ropes and hits a big boot! Wake is on the floor! James goes for the cover. 1...2... no dice! James gets on his knees and then looks at Walker. He looks like he has something in mind. He picks up Walker and then goes for the third degree (underhook DDT). He puts Walker in position and then lifts him, only to be locked in a triangle choke! Pheanox did not see this coming at all! He fights out of it and somehow transforms it into another powerbomb. Pheanox falls as does Walker. Walker tries to get up quickly, but can't due to his back. Pheanox seems to be too busy coughing. James recovers first and tries to pick up Wake again. Wake hits an uppercut out of nowhere, however and then a roundhouse kick! Wake follows up with a drop kick! James doesn't look like he was ready to be hit from all sides all of a sudden! Wake Walker rushes at Pheanox but James counters by hitting an elbow which sends Walker to the floor, leaving him in perfect position for the Zero Gravity (shooting star elbow drop). James climbs the ropes but Wake quickly climbs it as well and tries to make this into a super plex. James resists against it but eventually he gives in and the superplex is hit! The crowd goes nuts! Wake Walker gets up and sets himself up at the corner. He waits for Pheanox to get on his knees. Once Pheanox attempts to get up and is on his knees, Wake Walker hits The Trickster (drive-by kick)! Wake Walker then looks around and the crowd looks like it is gaining as much steam as possible! Wake bounces off the ropes, clucks like a chicken (and pretends to have wings) and hits a splash, which deals no damage to Pheanox whatsoever seeing as how he's already knocked out (or is he?)! Afterwards, Wake flops like a fish, ending the move that is Chicken Fish. Wake goes for the cover. 1....THAT'S NOT POSSIBLE! James has kicked out! Wake looks as surprised as everyone else! James gets up and doesn't look too happy. Wake looks scared. James rushes at Wake but Wake hits a low drop kick, causing James to fall. Wake follows it up with a slam. Wake begins to think for a few seconds and then looks at the turnbuckle. He smiles and nods his head. He goes up the turnbuckle and imitates James Pheanox's Zero Gravity maneuver and he hits it! Walker goes for the pin. 1...2..3! How embarrassing, losing to your finish move! Wake gets up and celebrates with the crowd. He even picks up the referee, kisses him and carries the referee to the back. The TVS above the arena fizzle in and we see Allison Beckett standing somewhere in the crowd with Izzie Dead, Ima Goner, and El Ladder… the Greatful Dead. Allison Beckett: Hi! Allison Beckett here with the big bad Grateful Dead for a pre-match interview!!! So… Izzie… how have you prepared for this match against Austin? Izzie and Ima laugh… Ladder remains still. Izzie Dead: Grah…mah…la brah. Ima jumps in… Ima Goner: BRAHHHH!!! Several fans run away in horror, Allison turn to El Ladder. El Ladder: ………………… ………………………………. ………………………………................. ……………………………….. …………………. Allison wipes a tear from her eye. Allison Beckett: Thanks so much for that! You have such a way with words.. L.O.L!! Izzie grabs Ladder and the trio begin toward the ring. “Dead and Gone” by T.I. is played through the arena as the fans seem completely sold on the idea of two zombies and a ladder wrestling. Ima Goner takes her place on the apron while El’ Ladder and Izzie Dead talk over who will start. Paul Marshall: Looks like these fans are behind this team tonight, Grateful Dead always seems to get them going. Soon “Papercut” by Linkin Park hits through the speakers in the resort as the fans turn their attention to the entrance way where soon Austin Acquin comes out and almost immediately begins to bad mouth them they realize quickly this is not a guy they’ll like, he heads into the ring and takes his place across from the other three. Colt Cox: And these fans have quickly realized their hatred for the younger of the Acquin brothers however he’s my type of guy I think he’ll take this match for sure. Allison Beckett: I think the Grateful Dead are going to win, El’ Ladder is just so inspirational. Cox sighs as the fans wait for the match up to start with Austin yelling out at a fan who is speaking up while Izzie Dead has convinced Ladder to head out of the ring. The bell rings and the fans are ready to go. Paul Marshall: Here we go looks like Izzie Dead will start it out. Austin Acquin quickly walks right up to Izzie Dead and begins to laugh right in his face seeming to have little respect for the team across from himself. Austin points towards Izzie and slowly pretends like he’s a zombie himself, which Izzie seems to take with great offense. Izzie screams out at him as Austin turns and continues and quickly Izzie catches him from behind with a big clothesline to the back of Austin’s arrogant head. The fans roar with approval as Austin hops back up and goes for a big clothesline but he’s caught with a big Shambling Wheel Kick! Austin holds his head in pain after two quick shots while Izzie Dead hops up and goes to the corner and makes the quick tag to El’ Ladder. Ladder sets up in the corner while Izzie Dead grabs Austin and whips him right towards Ladder. Austin hits hard into El’ Ladder who seems himself to have taken a bit of harm from the shot. Austin falls to the mat and Ladder seems to be heading down right on top of him, and Ima Goner makes the tag as he falls. Ladder crushes down on top of Joshua as Ima gets right in to a few cat calls from the crowd. Colt Cox: Our female announcer as you can see is currently off with El’ Ladder, which I sadly see as only a good thing for you fans sake. Jake Kennedy punches Rocky Davis a couple of times and then bounces off the ropes. Rocky Davis tries to hit a massive clothesline (which arguably, could have killed Kennedy on the spot) but misses. Jake Kennedy goes for the cover. 1...2...3! Jake Kennedy has won! Jake Kennedy can't seem to believe it but eventually, it all hits him. He then stands upright and smiles in an evil way. The crowd seems to be completely behind him. Rocky Davis begins to get up. Jake Kennedy turns around and hits a cheap shot and then follows it up with a DDT! Some of the crowd boos for this whilst others are impressed even more by this man. Jake Kennedy walks up the ramp whilst celebrating. The fans seems to have warmed up to him already. The TVS change away from the GIW Logo that was on it during the match and fade in on Amanda Rowland, the GM’s dressing room. Amanda Rowland, who looks like she hasn’t even acknowledged the fact that there is a beach behind her… is standing there in a business suit arguing with Aspen Chaud… in full ring attire. Paul Marshall: The fact she may be sleeping with the referee doesn’t mean she is smart… The fans seem ready for this match have seemingly forgot about the last part of this match as soon “Reise Reise” by Rammstein hits and from the back holding the GI-Championship and wearing a full blue and white referee attire comes Joshua with a big smirk on his face as heads into the ring, Aspen claps while Defias and Rydell shake their heads not wanting to see him out here at all. Defias and Rydell seem to be willing to team up for a bit cornering Aspen Chaud much to the fans happiness. Defias hits the mat hard and Aspen does as well as Rydell seems to be getting a second wind! Aspen gets to her feet and Rydell connects with a Roaring Elbow. Both of them back him up against the ropes seeming ready to attack him but Joshua points away catching their attention as Aspen comes across the ring and connects with a brutal kick to Rydell’s face, The Dream Ride! Aspen goes back on the offensive starting to stomp away at the downed Defias. Soon however he catches her leg and again rolls into a Sharpshooter this time he gets it locked in. Aspen screams in pain but soon Rydell returns to the ring seeing that this thing could be over. He runs off the ropes and connects with a big knee to get Defias off. Rydell tosses Aspen out of the way and goes on the attack of Defias hitting with punch after punch. Finally he seems ready to finish this thing right here and right now as he sets up for the New York Minute! However Defias battles his way out of it and kicks Rydell in the gut runs off the ropes and connects with a HUGE Bad News to Rydell! Rydell is out cold and the title will be Defias. He bends over to pull Rydell closer but is caught with blatant low blow from Aspen Chaud! The fans are beside themselves as Aspen shoves Defias out of the ring and pulls Rydell closer. She lies on top for the pin while Defias attempts to get back into the ring, 1.2.3! A very quick count from Joshua as the fans roar in disapproval, of what just happened. Colt Cox: There you have it fair and square win by Aspen Chaud our new champion! The fans are screaming at the travesty that has just occurred as Joshua his handed the title and he hands it over to Aspen. Aspen hugs him with the belt and then falls to her knees holding the belt up high. The fans aren’t letting up at all as Joshua holds up her arms in victory. She continues to hug her title tightly as Defias on the outside seems very upset with what just went down. The fans are left in anger as this seems to be the end of the show until Blake Beckett comes out and makes a b-line for the ring. He climbs in and Aspen seems a little worried he already has a microphone in hand as the celebration ends. Blake Beckett: Congratulations Aspen on becoming our first Galveston Island Champion! You know these fans may not like it but you deserve it… Aspen nods her head seeming to be relieved this wasn’t about what she thought it was. Joshua seems just the same as Blake then smirks and shakes his head. Blake Beckett: Now that I’m done being sarcastic, you may be the first champion ever. But your reign will be short lived because due to the action of Joshua Acquin helping you, I’ve decided to strip you of that BELT! The fans go wild as Aspen seems up in arms over the decision yelling back at him trying to keep her belt from him but eventually he wrestles it away and holds it himself. Blake Beckett: And next week we are going to start a big tournament to decide just who will be our next Galveston Island Champion so maybe this time you can prove you earned it. See you next week ladies and gentleman because we’ll crown our first REAL champion! Blake heads towards the back as the fans scream and yell patting him on the back as Aspen falls to her knees in anger screaming as Joshua attempts to comfort her as the show comes to an end.
