GIW — Card

Revolt, 25 November 2011

25 November 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: FRIDAY NIGHT REVOLT LIVE! From Galveston ISD Gym – November 25, 2011 BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! Here we are back in the Galveston ISD Gym for the first time in a very very long time. The fans here are ready to go and they have packed the small gym to capacity some people having to find seats in the isles but they don’t seem to mind as we pan around them then finally over to the announce table where Riley and Oliver are ready to go. RILEY STEVENS: “Welcome ladies and gentleman to the show tonight and we have a good one for you. The card is full of excitement from top to bottom. First we start with a Trent Lost Open Invitational that will surely be a grand return for him. Then from there we go in to two matches that are considered tune ups for Aspen Chaud and Tony Miranda as they face Austin Acquin and Nero respectively.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Naturally! They get a soft night while our champ has to fight a tough opponent.” RILEY STEVENS: “Oliver we’ll get to that before that we see two new young guns step into the ring as Jeremy Baltimore steps into the ring with Draven for a chance to really show what they are all about.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “And then in the Blake Beckett screws Ace Baldwin match of the week it’s Johnny Violence versus Ace Baldwin. For no reason whatsoever!” RILEY STEVENS: “We’ll see how things go…” Suddenly the tron lights up with the face of Johnny Violence on the screen as the fans all turn their attention to him, many booing. JOHNNY VIOLENCE: “You know this company is starting to get on my nerves a bit…I ask for a bit of time to go out to the ring so that I can get my point across and they tell me…We don’t have time Johnny. That’s how it’s going to be? After all I am supposed to be the forth wheel, the one that they think they can replace if something goes wrong. But…what they don’t realize is that I am the favorite…I am the top dog around here. Not some loser whose been here for two years and never won the belt, the little girl whom gets chance after chance, or least of all the champion who claims I’m not even worth remembering. So I made sure that I could get on here and send a message, a message to you people…this company…and my three opponents next Sunday…” Clearly angry at this point Violence breathes in heavily as the camera which he’s apparently stolen or forced into recording sways a bit. JOHNNY VIOLENCE: “Soon this company is going to revolve around…ME!” Violence now walks away from the camera as a cameraman scurries into focus turning it off as we take a quick commercial break. QUOTE When we return to the ring the fans are still going wild for Trent Lost as he poses on the top of the ropes holding up his arms for the people who give him a huge ovation and welcome back. In the ring already is an odd pair as Altair and Faith Simpson stand beside one another, both paling in size compared to Trent. RILEY STEVENS: “Tonight in the Trent Lost invitational we see that debuting Mark Cross protégé Faith Simpson as well as the returning Altair have taken the call to fight Trent.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Talk about getting a win fed to you on a platter they both weigh a buck o’ five soaking wet, Trent has it easy.” Lost jumps down observing his opponents as the referee calls for the bell. DING DING DING! Faith looking a bit nervous takes a second to look around the crowd all of which don’t even know whom she is. As for Altair he wastes no time at all going in for Lost as he rushes forward and kicks him with an Enzuigiri. Lost stumbles back a bit stunned holding his jaw wondering where that came from. Both men then go in for a lock up with Faith essentially watching as Trent pushes Altair away with ease, but Altair shows no signs of backing down going back in for a lock up as this time when Trent goes to push him away he tricks into giving up his arm which he grabs and drops Lost right into a Inverted STF! The fans seem shocked a bit not a soul in the building expecting Trent to get pushed around like this. Altair has the lock in well but Faith now snaps out of her daze walking over and kicking Altair off. The fans gasp as Altair gets up slowly walking towards her as her training now seems to go out the window with all the nerves of her first match coming together as the seemingly heartless Altair stalks her into the corner. Once there she jumps up trying to kick him away but he catches her legs. Shaking his head no but suddenly an enraged Trent returns grabbing Altair from behind angry about how badly he was getting it. Lost pushes him off the ropes and then lifts Altair up but out of no where Altair wraps him up into a Standing Guillotine Choke! RILEY STEVENS: “Can Altair finish him?” Lost starts to fall to one knee in pain but the crowd starts to get to the edge of the seat as Faith may have found where she is most comfortable stepping outside of the ropes and then grabbing them as she jumps up going Springboard flying through the air and Dropkicking, Trent’s back this sends him to the mat and Altair to break the hold when that inevitably sends him there to. She pushes Altair aside and goes for a pin on Trent 1…Altair pulls her feet off and drags her over to the side of the ring from there he hits a Side Slam in the corner of the ring turning his attention back to his real victim Trent Lost. He walks over to him as Trent gets up looking like a shell of the man a lot of fans remember as he stands up as Altair hoists him up with the ‘Ruiner’ a Lifting Inverted DDT. Lost is out right in the center of the ring however rather then pin him he rolls out of the ring to the outside. Back in the ring Faith gets up to her feet, looking around not seeing Altair who is behind her as she heads to the top rope the crowd behind her as she nails Trent with the ‘Have A Little Faith’ a 630 Senton! The fans go wild for the move as Simpson gets the cover 1…2…3! JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…Faith Simpson!” The crowd goes wild as Faith quickly rolls out of the ring, jumping up and down a bit as she heads towards the back her emotions sending her there but back in the ring we watch as Altair slowly walks up the steps, now wielding a mic as he steps through the ropes looking down at Trent who rolls over in pain. ALTAIR: “You want to know why I didn’t finish you off? You want to know why I didn’t just end you today? Because…here right now? It wasn’t big enough for the end of your career. So here’s what is going to happen at ‘Final Solution’ it will be you and I, one on one and this time things are going to be a whole lot more brutal…” Altair walks around the fans have begun to boo him now, realizing he might be more heartless then originally thought. ALTAIR: “For you.” With that he drops the mic right on the back of Trent as he heads out of the ring as we take a break. WINNER: FAITH SIMPSON After the break we head to the backstage area where Faith Simpson is just arriving from the ring, with a big smile on her face unable to believe what just happened. But as she is walking along she bumps right into Jeremy Baltimore who is standing by with a few members of the backstage crew. JEREMY BALTIMORE: “You best watch your step little girl…” Jeremy looks down at her with a big scowl on her face as Faith looks up at him struggling to find her words. FAITH SIMPSON: “I’m really sorry, just can’t believe I won.” Faith says this with a big smile on her face with some big time jitters as Jeremy looks over at the backstage crew with a big smirk. JEREMY BALTIMORE: “You sure did, congrats on that…But here’s the bad news I just got word that if I win my match later tonight, I face the winner of that little Trent Lost Invitational thing for the Legacy Championship…So that means it’ll be you and I…” Baltimore laughs a bit just at that statement as he looks around at the guys around him who all seem like tools that look up to Baltimore. JEREMY BALTIMORE: “So I’ll see you then, now run along.” Simpson looks up at him mad but not showing it as she heads on her way as Baltimore starts to crack up with his friends. QUOTE When we head back to the ring "World Warrior" by RedRapper hits and the fans go insane for Tony Miranda as he heads out from the back ready to go as he makes his entrance looking focused as usual. RILEY STEVENS: “Last week Tony Miranda put everyone on notice after the main event when he ended up the last man standing and tonight he vowed to not even get touched here tonight by Nero.” The crowd does not let up with their cheers as he heads up the steps and into the ring. But just then they begin to wait as yet again Nero is no where to be seen. Tony says he’ll go get him but the referee says he’s got to start it. DING DING DING! Surprisingly right as the match starts Tony slides out of the ring making a b-line backstage as the fans go wild. The cameras follow him to the back as the referee starts to count 1…2…Once in the back we see Tony looking around back there, as the people in the back start to stare at him curiously. TONY MIRANDA: “Nero! Where is he?” Tony screams it out as one staff member points down the hall, 3…4…We can hear the count from the crowd who want to make sure Tony knows how much time he’s got left. Suddenly he stumbles right into Nero throwing a huge punch that catches him off guard. Grabbing him he pulls him towards the ring, fighting off Nero’s attempts to stop him…5…6…7…Tony enters back into the ring area heading down the ramp…8…9…Tony heaves Nero in and follows him right in. Nero is first up and he rushes at him only to kicked in the gut. Nero stumbles back and Miranda runs full steam then BOOM ‘Third Strike’ a Running Leg Lariat! The crowd goes wild but Tony is not done there despite Nero being quite clearly out, Tony hoists him back up and nailing him with the ‘Second Impact’ a Running Lygerbomb that sends Nero to dream street as Tony hooks the leg 1…2…3! JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…Tony Miranda!” Tony gets to his feet nodding his head as the crowd goes wild for him as Nero rolls out of the ring as the referee hoists his arm up. RILEY STEVENS: “Well Tony went back and got him and then ended his night early, Tony might just be a week away from winning the title he so truly covets.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Yeah right!” Tony slides out of the ring not sticking around to celebrate much because after all he’ll celebrate when the belt is firmly in his grasps. WINNER: TONY MIRANDA From there we go to the backstage area where we see Blake Beckett standing outside of the gym, where the staff and such parked standing with him is at least ten security guards all of which he seems to be training. But as he does here comes the champion Ace Baldwin with his belt. ACE BALDWIN: “Really Blake? You that worried that I am going to attack you?” Ace chuckles as Blake turns around looking at him, with distain in his eyes. BLAKE BECKETT: “I am just preparing for what I consider the inevitable later tonight…” Blake hints at the match up between Violence and Baldwin as his security guards drift back into not caring mode. ACE BALDWIN: “We’ll see won’t we? Regardless, I just wanted to ask you what happens when I defeat three of your best and there’s nobody left for me to fight, what will you do then?” Ace smirks loving to talk himself up as Beckett grins back at him not one to be outdone. BLAKE BECKETT: “Oh I really doubt that, but if by some miracle you do win. I’ll make sure that you don’t go too much farther with that belt.” Blake smirks and heads on his way as Ace watches him, realizing he’s a week away from really running this company. QUOTE Back to the ring we go where Austin Acquin the GIW Television Champion stands in the ring ready to go as "Bullseye" by Aly and AJ hits and the fans go nuts for Aspen Chaud who heads out from the back with a big smile on her face heading to the ring excited to teach Austin a lesson here tonight. RILEY STEVENS: “Joshua Acquin booked this match here tonight and I expect him to be watching closely.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Yeah he’ll probably screw him.” Aspen heads into the ring pointing at Austin and then bending through the ropes to blow a kiss to the fans at ringside. Stepping back into the ring she looks back at Austin with a grin ready to go here tonight. DING DING DING! Right away Austin walks over to her screaming into her face something, talking some big time trash to her as he continues to berate her, but she throws a big Forearm to his face then whips him over to the ropes. Already just moments into the match she rushes at him and crushes him with the ‘Tush Push’ a Turnbuckle Flying Butt Shot. The fans go wild for her as Austin bounces off stunned and as usual she follows it up by going to the top rope. But Acquin recovers quickly rushing forward and pushing her leg sending her falling down on to the top rope. The fans boo as Austin backs up, talking trash when Joshua heads out onto the entrance area as Austin looks over at him screaming at him to go away. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “What in the hell is Joshua doing out here?” Austin takes his eyes off the ball as Aspen recovers and gets back to her feet up there jumping off at him just as he turns around into an ‘AirBarbie’ a Flying Crossbody. The fans go wild as she rolls off of him and right into her next move, her infamous ‘K.M.A.’ a Heel Lock that she gets in tight! The fans go wild as Austin finds himself in the middle of the ring he tries to fight it for a second before tapping out. JAMAL JACKSON: “Here are your winner… Aspen Chaud!” Aspen hops up to her feet with a smile as Austin rolls over holding his heel in agony as Aspen heads out of the ring where she high fives the fans at ringside, not taking this match as seriously as she will the title match. WINNER: ASPEN CHAUD Aspen heads up the ramp high fiving the folks here as she walks by Joshua, motioning that he’s all his as Aspen heads to the back and Joshua gets on the mic walking down towards Austin who crawls in pain towards the ropes. JOSHUA ACQUIN: “Can’t say I didn’t enjoy watching that. Austin I know I told you that you’d be fighting for that Legacy belt but there has been a change in plans. You see I talked to Blake about that Television title, and he agreed with me when I came up with this idea.” The fans are on Joshua’s side for the first time in awhile we don’t hear a single person booing him. JOSHUA ACQUIN: “That Television belt has been made a joke of ever since it fell out of my hands. So here’s what is going to happen at ‘Final Solution’ it’ll be…ME versus You! And here’s the stipulations brother. If you win that belt continues and you can hold it proudly but if I win that belt is retired with me. So Austin you’ve wanted to prove whose the superior wrestler for a long time, so next Sunday we’ll find out!” The fans cheer as Joshua drops the microphone and Austin hugs the title belt close to his chest still struggling to get to his feet as we take a break. QUOTE Once the commercial break is over its back to the ring for the second to last match of the night and already in the ring is Draven ready to go as "Ain't No Stoppin Me Now" by Axel hits and the fans look to see who it is but are disappointed when it’s Jeremy Baltimore a man whom they have quickly come to hate heads out from the back. RILEY STEVENS: “As stated earlier by Jeremy Baltimore we are getting word that the winner of this match will earn a title shot for the Legacy Championship against Faith Simpson.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “So basically if they win they are champion…” Baltimore looks around at the crowd shaking his head and laughing at their attempts to insult him. Baltimore heads into the ring looking across at fan favorite Draven as the referee calls for the bell. DING DING DING! Draven uses what he’s known best for his speed right off the bat rushing at him only for Jeremy to use what he’s known for his strength as he hoists him up with a Thrust Spinebuster. The fans cringe, starting to fear that Jeremy the cocky young star might just beat their hero right off the bat. Baltimore grins down at Draven as he pulls him up to his feet and yet again whips him off the ropes right into a brutal Clothesline. Screaming out loud enough for the crowd to hear he says “This is too easy!” as Jeremy looks down at Draven and hoists him up to his feet. RILEY STEVENS: “This is ugly…” Baltimore looks around at the crowd all of which started out as Draven fans but now seem to be giving up hope for him in this one. Rather then take it easy on him he spins hitting a Spinning Elbow Smash that completely dazes Draven as he stumbles back into the corner where Jeremy follows it up with a Corner Shoulder Block. The fans watch as Draven falls down to the mat in the corner. Draven crawls forward looking for salvation what he finds instead is the ‘Dragon Clutch’ a Dragon Sleeper. He flails his legs and tries to get out but Baltimore has it locked in tight but he will not tap instead his body gives out on him and Draven falls asleep and the referee calls for it. JAMAL JACKSON: “Here are your winner… Jeremy Baltimore!” Jeremy takes a second longer in the hold then releases it as Draven’s face falls into the mat as the referee goes down to check on him as Baltimore demands a microphone which he’s given as he heads back to the middle of the ring. JEREMY BALTIMORE: “Shhhhhhh, be quiet he’s sleeping.” Baltimore chuckles at his own joke as the fans respond with boos as the referee tries to get Draven back into consciousness. JEREMY BALTIMORE: “You people need to realize I am the REAL DEAL! With this one I have gotten myself a title shot at the recently revived ‘GIW Legacy Championship’. And I am going to be facing a fifteen year old girl for it…” Baltimore chuckles at this clearly not taking her too seriously as he continues. JEREMY BALTIMORE: “So I am going to make this belt the one everyone covets around here, because soon I will become the biggest star here in GIW.” Baltimore drops the microphone as Draven returns to consciousness now as he heads out of the ring as we head to the back. WINNER: JEREMY BALTIMORE Aspen has already changed out of her ring gear and into a blue dress, she is watching the show regardless as Tony Miranda walks up beside her watching the TV as well. The two share a awkward silence before Aspen speaks up. ASPEN CHAUD: “You know I am pretty sure that I beaten Austin quicker then you won your match…” Aspen says this with a little grin on her face as Tony shakes his head not agreeing with the statement. TONY MIRANDA: “I had to go back and get my opponent and I still think I beat you….” She shrugs having to agree with the fact that at least her opponent was in the ring waiting for her. ASPEN CHAUD: “I just want you to remember that when you beat me at ‘On Hallowed Ground’ that I was still dazed from that chair shot by Johnny.” Aspen turns towards him things getting rather serious quickly. TONY MIRANDA: “There’s always an excuse from both sides when we fight. But next Sunday, none of that matters. Because I am the one that’ll walk out with the belt.” The two stare one another down before Tony turns and heads on out as Aspen looks back at the TV waiting for the main event as we go to commercial before the Main Event. QUOTE Here we go it is time for the Main Event of the evening as the fans here are ready to go knowing this thing could get wild. As per usual it’s the non-champion first as ‘Bat Country’ by Avenged Sevenfold hits and he’s greeted as usual by the boos that so often follow him wherever he goes here in GIW. RILEY STEVENS: “When this four way was originally made I think a lot of people were doubting Johnny Violence I think as the match has neared he’s gained a lot more supporters.” Johnny heads up the steps and into the ring, looking angry and prepared as usual as the unpredictable star of GIW takes his corner as Matador made music hits as the fans continue to boo, apparently no side will be chosen by the crowd. RILEY STEVENS: “Ace Baldwin is still the champion but last week he was defeated by Aspen mostly because Violence attacked Ace, causing him to lose.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Otherwise Ace would have won.” The fans boo him as well as his belt sits around his waist as Baldwin heads up the steps and into the ring where he holds up his title in the air as the fans only boo louder. He hands his belt over to the referee who hands it out of the ring and starts the match. DING DING DING! These fans are in for a treat as Violence and Baldwin begin to circle around one another. The two not sharing many physical traits as Ace is the groomed good looking boy as Violence is tatted up like the King of Anarchy he is. The two go in for a lock up and it’s Ace that gets the advantage being the much heavier and stronger star as he pushes him back into the ropes. Baldwin steps back and then boom delivers a slap to his face backing up and screaming “Nobody costs me a match!” but the slap only angers Violence as he runs forward delivering a huge Spear! The fans seem to be trying to pick a side as some have begun to cheer for Violence, just by force essentially. Violence stares down at him clearly out here with something to prove as Ace stumbles up to his feet caught off guard with that one. Johnny grabs him up going for an early ‘Trashed’ a Torture Rack. He gets him up there but Ace is able to slide out before he can really do any damage, once behind Violence, Ace puts him up into an Atomic Drop then with Johnny holding his butt he runs off the ropes and hits a Spear of his own. RILEY STEVENS: “I think we all got the feeling this would be a close one.” Baldwin gets up instead of going for a pin quite yet as he looks around at the crowd with a big smirk on his face seeming to think that this last move was all he needed to turn the tide his way. Baldwin calls for him to get up and when up goes right into his Suplex machine as he puts him down with one, then another finally hooking the leg for a Northern Lights Suplex only to all the sudden be countered as Johnny turns things around right into the ‘Critical Acclaim’ as he himself hits a Suplex…then another…but yet again nearly man will let the other hit the third as Ace pushes him away and then lets him run right into a Dropkick. Baldwin hops up angrily running his hand through his hair as Johnny gets right back up and is whipped into the corner by Ace. Baldwin rushes at him going for a big clothesline but Violence ducks out of the way and ends up behind him where he hits a German Suplex with a pin 1…Kick Out. Both men hop up and it’s Violence that’s naturally up first and he hits a Bulldog to stop Ace from doing anything if only for a second. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Come on Ace!” The fans are into this one now, they may hate both guys but they respect a good match regardless. Baldwin gets up near the corner as Violence rushes at him right into a Spinning Spinebuster by Ace. Neither man can really put much together as Ace turns towards the ropes heading to the second rope where from there he lands a Leg Drop! The fans don’t really show much care just instead watch as Baldwin makes the cover 1…2-Kick Out. Baldwin gets up, as usual getting annoyed when moves he thinks should put them away don’t. Ace complains to the referee telling him to count quicker as Violence gets up behind him going for a low blow! But Ace catches it and then spins around holding onto that hand as Baldwin now goes for an eye poke, but Violence catches it….These guys are such good cheaters they can actually see them coming. Both now back to square one in a lock up struggle for a second until Ace starts to get an advantage. But Johnny ducks down and wraps up Ace tackling him through the ropes sending both men to the outside! RILEY STEVENS: “These two have stumbled to the outside.” The fans get up to watch them out here as the barricade is the only thing to separate them. Johnny gets up but Ace does as well only he does so right into a tackle of his own tackling him right into the barricade as the referee after warning them starts to count 1…2…3…Ace and Johnny don’t seem to care as Baldwin gets on top throwing punch after punch….4…5…6…Violence rolls over on top and does the same…7…8…Finally they both get up to their feet still throwing punches…9…10! JAMAL JACKSON: “This contest has been ruled a Double Countout.” The two stop with the announcement of the winner then go right back into fighting throwing punch after punch. Violence now gets an advantage with an elbow that dazes Ace as he throws him towards the announce table. Both Oliver and Riley bail as Violence puts Ace up on it. The fans actually start to cheer realizing that they may see something big as Johnny starts to pull him up when Security the ones we saw earlier rush into the Gym right towards Violence grabbing his legs to stop him. This allows Ace to get up with Johnny held there as Ace jumps up Clotheslining him off the top into the security! RILEY STEVENS: “This is wild these two guys are brawling!” Out comes the road agents and Aspen Chaud and Tony Miranda stepping in to try to stop them. Tony pulls Ace away but when he does Baldwin throws a big elbow that now infuriates Miranda who throws him to the ground and now becomes another problem as he throws punch after punch. Aspen in a dress not in ring gear helps the security hold Violence until he slaps her across the face. Then she goes wild as well kicking off the heels and jumping on top throwing forearms! RILEY STEVENS: “Come on get some control!” The security are back up as they pull Aspen away and it allows Violence to attack her as Ace and Tony are on the other side throwing punches back and forth. Finally some more stars head out and they get some control, as each of the four is pinned down by at least three members of security. RILEY STEVENS: “Blake’s telling me to cut the show off so everyone tune in for ‘Final Solution’ to see which of these four stars wins the Undisputed title!” The cameras watch for a few more seconds this crazy scene before they cut off to static before the commercials hit. DOUBLE COUNT-OUT
