GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: REVOLT Live from Sea Wolf Park - April 14th, 2014 BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! The show begins as fireworks stream up into the sky above Sea Wolf Park – the fans are screaming and the scene is electric as the camera pans around the ring before finally stopping at the announce booth. We zoom in on Riley Stevens and Oliver Thibodaux as the crowd continues to scream in background. RILEY STEVENS: “Ladies and gentlemen, welcome to another edition of Revolt streaming LIVE on Galveston Wrestling dot com. This is a big night, just TWO shows away from Aggressive Expansion, and we have a lot to cover in just two hours. Tonight, in the main event, we will see six of our top stars collide in a tag team match pitting rivals against each other.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “The whore Alexis Terry teams with the boring Christopher Darren Erns and the scrawny Dennis Driver to take on the mighty team of Madison Seton, Wake Walker, and Michael Lynch. As far as I’m concerned, it must suck to be on Team Terry tonight.” RILEY STEVENS: “Over the past few weeks we have seen the rivalry between Alexis Terry and Wake Walker bloom into one that is founded on hatred for one another. But those two aren’t the only ones who have issues in this match, Madison Seton tore apart one of CDE’s best friends last week, Tony Miranda, and I am absolutely sure that CDE can’t wait to get his hands on the champ. But I bet he wants to meet up with Wake Walker too, the man who used his influence to screw him out of a number one contendership for the belt.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “But when Ian Mendoza made the boneheaded decision to fire Walker, CDE became the number one contender. CDE better just stay at home for a few weeks and wait until Aggressive Expansion, because Walker is on a mission to get that number one contendership back.” RILEY STEVENS: “Wake Walker isn’t a legitimate contender! He literally had to pay off the guards to finish off CDE, and even then Tony Miranda was the one who accidentally got CDE disqualified.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Regardless, a winner is a winner. You can’t argue that Riley.” RILEY STEVENS: “You know I’m right!” He is quickly cut off the sound of Ian Mendoza’s music. The man in charge steps out onto the ramp to the cheers of the crowd. He begins to make his way down to the ring as the announcers continue to discuss. RILEY STEVENS: “Here comes Ian Mendoza who apparently has an announcement to make.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Holy crap. I thought he was a Mexican? He looks as pale as a ghost.” RILEY STEVENS: “Well… I mean I don’t agree with the way you said it, but you’re right…” Mendoza climbs into the ring slowly as his music fades out. He is handed a microphone by the ring announcer. The crowd starts to quiet down. IAN MENDOZA: “Last night, I received a message from Mr. B. Before I read it, I would first like to make something very clear. I am the physical presence of Mr. B here on Revolt. Obviously, I make decisions that I do not have time to pass through Mr. B. But, regardless of that fact, my word should always be considered his… unless he decides to speak.” Mendoza pauses for a moment, rolling his neck around. He continues slowly. IAN MENDOZA: “At which point, his word obviously overrides mine. Two episodes ago, I took it upon myself to fire Wake Walker and void his number one contendership for the Undisputed Championship. Last week, when Alexis Terry returned to Revolt, she convinced me to rehire Wake Walker with the condition that she gets a match with him at Aggressive Expansion. Everything has fallen into place nicely, in my mind anyway. Alexis Terry would be able to get her revenge on Wake Walker for nearly ending her career and CDE would get the title shot against Seton that he rightfully deserves.” The crowd goes wild. Mendoza holds up a hand, calming them down. IAN MENDOZA: “However, Mr. B doesn’t share my vision. He says so in his message to me…” The crowd begins to murmur. Riley and Oliver can be heard murmuring as well. IAN MENDOZA: “My friend Ian, I applaud your tenacity and passion in trying to restore order to Revolt. I too find Wake Walker’s actions to be reprehensible; I believe that the course of action you have chosen in disciplining him will be ineffective and detrimental to the growth of this company. Not only have I signed Wake Walker to an extension with Galveston Island Wrestling through the end of August, but I have also named him the number one contender for the Undisputed Championship once again…” Boos poor out from the crowd. Mendoza nods his head and holds up his hand again. IAN MENDOZA: “The message continues… I have personally re-booked next week’s edition of Revolt as well as Aggressive Expansion. Next week, we will see Alexis Terry versus Wake Walker, and Madison Seton versus CDE in a non title match. At Aggressive Expansion, we will see Wake Walker versus Madison Seton for the Undisputed Championship, and Alexis Terry versus CDE in a number one contenders match. These changes are drastic, yes, but I believe they are the best decisions for the company.” “THIS IS BULL-SHIT” chants pour into the ring from the crowd. Mendoza has to speak much louder to make his voice audible over the noise. IAN MENDOZA: “Mr B. continues to say… Wake Walker, I personally believe, is the future of this company. There is not a more polarizing or impressive figure here right now, and he should he given every opportunity to become champion…” Several fans throw objects into the ring – programs, cups, popcorn. Mendoza nods and shrugs, there really is nothing he can do. “Gunboat” by Vixtrola hits and the boos get even louder. Madison Seton steps out onto the ramp, her belt over her shoulder. MADISON SETON: “Are you kidding me? Is this some kind of joke?” She begins to make her way down to the ring. MADISON SETON: “What kind of promotion are you running here? This is the kind of nonsense I am talking about. I am the Undisputed Champion and no one here seems to respect that.” Seton begins to climb the steps, as she does some fans begin to cheer her for pointing out the hypocrisy, though they clearly still hate her as a person. MADISON SETON: “You want to pander to Walker? You want to play his games? Wake Walker is everything that is wrong with this company. He does nothing and gets all the rewards.” Mendoza takes a few steps away from the fuming champion, who in turn follows him around the ring like a predator stalking its prey. MADISON SETON: “Some of these people actually cheer him, and they all boo me? They ALL boo me Ian. I am a fighting champion. I won this belt clean, no guards, no games. And they still boo me? And now Walker is the new number one contender, after I have spent all of this time preparing for CDE?” “CDE-CDE-CDE” chants now. Seton hears these and quickly turns towards the crowd, telling them to shut up. She is furious. MADISON SETON: “And not only that… but I have to face CDE the week before my title match with Wake, and he gets fed the little shrimp Alexis Terry? Mendoza, you tell Mr. B that if he wants this belt off of me, he might as well just strip it from me so I can walk right out the front door because I do NOT have time for these games.” “STRIP-THE-DUMB-BITCH” chants begin in the crowd. IAN MENDOZA: “Madison… calm down. I understand why you are frustrated… you have every reason to be… but this is completely out of my hands.” Madison is visibly enraged by this statement. MADISON SETON: “Who is Mr. B? Why won’t he come and say it to my face? Why does he just use you as his little puppet? Maybe I should hurt you to send him a message…” “The Waiting Makes Me Curious” hits the PA and more boos pour in from the crowd. Wake Walker stands on the ramp and speaks. Seton makes her way to the edge of the ring, getting as close to Wake was she can without leaving. WAKE WALKER: “Madison… Madison… calm down…” The majority of the fans boo Wake, though a few cheer him on. WAKE WALKER: “Mr. B is not your enemy. He is not out to get you. Mr. B simply understands the concept that the best man should be the number one contender. I am the best man.” “WALKER-SUCKS” chants. Seton rolls her eyes. WAKE WALKER: “But you are the best woman. Madison, if there is anyone on this company even close to my level…” Seton leans over the ropes as she shouts. MADISON SETON: “No Wake, let me be clear. I am NOT on your level. I am ABOVE you. I am the Undisputed Champion…” She raises the belt high to a cascade of boos. WAKE WALKER: “For now.” Walker winks. Seton is furious. MADISON SETON: “Forever!” There is a pause as the arena erupts in boos and cheers for both of these competitors. WAKE WALKER: “If you say so. I must say that is whole ordeal is… unexpected… but I am pleased that finally someone in this company realizes that I am truly its most valuable asset. Keeping me happy and keeping me in the title picture always should have been the number one priority, and I’m glad that has been clear to you now Ian.” Mendoza steps forward now. IAN MENDOZA: “I am still in charge Wake. There will still be order here in GIW…” Walker is quick to interrupt, slick grin plastered on his face. WAKE WALKER: “I will do as I please. At least until August. There isn’t anything you can do about that Ian.” Mendoza shakes his head. He knows Walker is right. MADISON SETON: “But you’ll be doing it as a challenger. Never a champion.” She raises her belt high. The arena erupts once again. Seton’s music picks up as Walker slow claps from the stage, finding that whole presentation amusing. We fade into a commercial break. IAN MENDOZA: “You two are going to square off at Aggressive Expansion in a number one contender’s match. That is making the best out of a bad situation. I’m sorry that this happened to you both, but like I said... My hands are tied.” There is a long pause. Terry shakes her head. ALEXIS TERRY: “If that’s the way it has to be… then that’s the way it will be. I’ll see you at Aggressive Expansion, and I’ll see Walker next week.” Erns rolls his eyes. CHRISTOPHER DARREN ERNS: “I’m glad you’re fine with your non title match being rescheduled.” The two look like they are about to come to blows before Mendoza stands between them. IAN MENDOZA: “Enough. Now you two are partners tonight. Go get ready.” CDE and Terry exit as we fade to commercial.
