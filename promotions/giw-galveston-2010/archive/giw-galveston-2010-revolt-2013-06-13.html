GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: THURSDAY NIGHT REVOLT LIVE! From North Beach – June 13, 2013 BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! In the ring, it all began with a table, two chairs, a contract and mediator. Alex Castellanos stood at the head of the table whilst Alexis Terry and Alioth Starre sat across from one another. Neither could stand to look at one another without a glare of hatred, or discontent. Alex was ready to begin the signing and held the microphone to his lips, the other two still rested at each respective side of the table for when Terry and Starre wanted to speak. However, the crowd knew these things never ended with kind words. That much was clear here. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “We are here on Revolt on the verge of Aggressive Expansion and here lays the contract that will bring two great opposing forces of GIW together for the Undisputed Championship title. Alexis Terry and Alioth Starre.” Starre was the first to speak. ALIOTH STARRE: “Alexis Terry, enlighten me, please? What is it exactly that makes you want this title so badly? Aren’t you so well spread throughout this nation that you could get any man to give you anything you want, but here you come for something that clearly you’re unable to get on your own, or at least it seems, and for what? You know that you’re not capable of holding this title. I have made a goal of defeating my own streak, and you’re just a little ant in the of that. So, my dear, quit taking the piss.” She stopped him there. ALEXIS TERRY: “Alioth, you’re an idiot. You’re a pig-headed male that’s so wrapped up in his own ego and ass that you forgot the world around you. There are a lot of us here that could defeat you and strip that title away from you, but you like to pretend you’re the only one here. That you’re the only good person here. You’re not. I’m better than you, and I’m better than everyone here. I did what I had to in order to get this spot because I played the game, Alioth. I played it better than anyone, better than Aspen, and better than you.” She replied but Alex intervened. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “All right, now. Let’s see some signing. Alexis, if you would please do the honors first.” He pulled a pen from his pocket and offered it to her. She took the pen and glared at Alioth. As she signed, Alioth took the microphone again. ALIOTH STARRE: “I don’t remember much about our unfortunate stint together, which clearly went as far over your head as calculus for a three year old. I’m not going to sign this until you reveal to me why you struck me and have been on my case since this whole organization came back from hiatus. You and I, from my knowledge, were tight, Alexis. You and I were firebrads of England. You betrayed me and that relationship you built with me, because now I’m going to have to do what everyone here and everyone in the back accuses me of doing to each of them: Bury you. I will bury you Alexis, because I don’t believe you could be this far gone without doing something dirty.” ALEXIS TERRY: “Something like paying Castellanos to thrust me into the main event? Yeah, what about it? I’m here, Alioth. I got there on my volition, and did what ever it took to get here. Now that I’m in the place to take that title and be the champion of Galveston Island Wrestling, I’m taking it. You will lose at Aggressive Expansion. You. Will. Lose.” Alex looked down, and scratched at his temple. The crowd booed at this revelation. Alioth shot him a cold glare and took the pen from Alexis to sign the contract. With it signed, their match was made, a match made in hell. Nothing was glamorous about what was to go down at Aggressive Expansion, nor would anymore political enlightenments matter between them. He had her, and she had him. Together they’d enter the ring, but only one would leave as the Undisputed Champion. Only one. Through the crowds jeers, Alex broke to intervene and seal the deal. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “I admit, and I’m not proud of it, but what’s done is done. We will see, for the Undisputed Title, Alioth defend it against the challenger Alexis Terry.” He said and took a step back from the table. Alioth stood up, which made Terry stand as well and the two eyed one another carefully, and with violence in their hearts. RILEY STEVENS: “This team is pretty diabolical, and after last week’s stunt with Terry you have to wonder what kind of pull does she really have when apparently our GM is…well backing her.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “It’s called power, deal with it Riley.” Terry enters the ring as past work together alongside Aspen is brought into mind as Acquin and Terry end up walking over to the same corner together as she smirks and turns around as “Victimized” by Linkin Park hits and be it the fact she’s with Starre or rather just there hatred for Acquin and Terry these fans give her a warm reaction as Zerotine Valentino enters the ring, always a real variable in any match she’s in as she heads towards the ring, stopping as she reaches the end of the ramp to wait for her partner as “New Killer Star” by David Bowie hits and these fans get going as Alioth Starre the beloved Undisputed Champion makes his entrance! RILEY STEVENS: “I want to say Terry and Acquin have the advantage here but both have giant ego’s so there past work together may not help….” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “As if Valentino and Starre are expected to work together; they are two nut cases!” That nut case walks towards the ring with his belt around his waist, the belt that everyone in GIW wants as he walks by Valentino and the two head up into the ring together keeping an eye on Terry and Acquin as they enter the ring, and the referee calls for the bell. DING DING DING! This very anticipated match up is now underway and these two teams first trial is deciding who starts off for both teams but the Undisputed Champion is willing to take a step back knowing he’ll get in one way or another, trying to show his trust for Zerotine. As Valentino steps into the ring ready to go, seeming to have no preference who she gets to get her hands on, wanting to send a message to Starre, Alexis enters the ring with a smirk looking past Zerotine right at Starre as the two start to circle one another in the ring, as Terry looks past her she mouths something to Starre that we can’t quite hear as Starre keeps his eye on her. Valentino lets it go for a second but when Terry doesn’t refocus, she makes her with a big kick to her stomach that doubles her over as she hits a big kick to the shoulder area as Terry holds her stomach, as she stumbles back a bit and Valentino connects with a Dropkick that has these fans going, if only because it’s Terry falling flat on her butt in the center of the ring as Zerotine grabs a hold of her by her head and pulls her up as the referee tells her to stay off the hair and Valentino releases it and smacks her with a big punch then whips her over into the corner rushing at her and connects a big clothesline, as these fans cheer her on and she backs out of the corner and lets Terry stumble right into a big Clothesline, that she follows up immediately with a pretty looking Standing Moonsault her athleticism on display with that move. RILEY STEVENS: “Terry’s always had an issue with really keeping her focus on not taunting…Costing her here early on.” These fans are eating it up however as Zerotine goes for a cover but Terry kicks out pretty much immediately as Valentino doesn’t seem to fazed by it as she hoists her up but as she does, she is caught off guard by Terry raking her eyes out of the view of the referee as she pushes her back into the ropes in her corner as the fans boo that she made it there. But as Terry gets vertical Valentino slaps her across the face dropping her back a bit but immediately Alexis favors her eye, pretending it was poked distracting the referee long enough for Acquin to grab over the ropes tugging Valentino to the mat, with extreme force. These fans boo as Acquin looks away as the referee turns back to him, pretending that nothing happened as Terry crawls on top of Zerotine and starts to beat her down with punch after punch, before Valentino finally throws her off. They both jump to there feet and Valentino punches Terry but takes her eyes off the ball for a second, turning to strike Acquin which she does but as she turns back around Terry connects with a big Yakuza Kick that sends her falling over to the ropes, down and out as Terry makes the tag to Acquin and he steps in. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Told you they’d make a good team.” So far Oliver has exactly been right as Terry hoists up Zerotine and tosses her right into a Acquin clothesline, as he stands over her with a big smirk before hoisting her up and making another tag to Terry keeping her trapped in the corner. Terry steps back in and Joshua holds Zerotine and drops her with a body slam, as Terry delivers a elbow drop as Acquin leaves the ring and Alexis drags her to the center of the ring and yet again, shows Starre where her belts going to be, around her waist as he glares at her but as she goes for the tag Zerotine rolls her into a pin 1…Kick Out! But Valentino hops right back up to her feet, rushing towards her corner. Alexis at first attempts to stop her but instead runs across the ring making the tag to Acquin, just as Zerotine does to Starre. Alioth jumps in and runs across the ring grabbing a hold of Terry trying to drag her backwards into the ring, but before he can the referee informs him of the tag and when he goes to release her Acquin comes over top of Terry and clotheslines Starre to the match; as perhaps it’s Josh that can really make the statement in this one as Alioth gets up off the mat and Acquin responds with a big High Knee Strike then immediately lands a big Knee Drop, that gets a lot of boos from the crowd. RILEY STEVENS: “They really put Starre down before he could get any form of momentum.” Starre gets up from the mat stumbling as he does so, some big damage to his face already as Acquin perhaps gets a little bit of ahead himself eager to try to get a victory over the champion but as he goes for the ‘Facebreaker’ his version of the Kill Switch, but as he turns him over Alioth spins out and kicks him right in the jaw with a huge Superkick, as these fans go wild and Joshua keeps his feet under him but just barely stumbling a bit before Alioth hits the ropes then comes off with a Knee Bar that really takes down the former Television Champion as Acquin holds his knee but soon his arms are ripped away and replaced by Alioth, who rolls into a Knee Bar on the ground tugging on the now hurt Acquin’s knee as these fans go wild for Alioth as he goes for the submission, though as we all know Joshua is not easy to submit, always priding himself on his toughness. He holds out for a bit but he gets saved as Terry jumps into the ring and kicks Alioth in the face, to break the hold. These fans boo her as the referee grabs her and starts to escort her over to her corner as Acquin goes for a big low blow but Alioth catches it! RILEY STEVENS: “He caught his hand!” Hoisting him up he spins him right into Zerotine who entered the ring to originally stop Alexis who the referee is having troubles with but now in the perfect position, she is right in line to hit Acquin with the ‘Innovative Bliss’ a Seven Twenty DDT! These fans go crazy as Valentino slips out of the ring as the referee turns back from getting Terry into her corner as Joshua is on an island as Alioth grabs a hold of his legs and starts to twirl him into the ‘Four Star Mary’ a Figure–Four Leglock! These fans know it is over here as Alioth looks back at Alexis, as she makes her choice not to help instead jumping off the ropes as Alioth keeps his eyes on her the entire time as Acquin slaps the mat in pain to tap out, having had enough and having no way out after the DDT left him in the center of the ring. REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here are your winners by way of submission…Alioth Starre and Zerotine Valentino!” This match ends and Alioth Starre gets to his feet as the victor as Alexis backs her way up the ramp having really not done much to get herself hurt tonight as Acquin rolls out of the ring in pain and Valentino joins Starre in the ring, her arm being raised as well the two having worked together well enough to get the win. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Got to love the strategy by Alexis, not going overboard to win this one knowing what matters is next week; when she’s got Alex in her back pocket.” RILEY STEVENS: “That’s very true on the end there Oliver; so be there next Sunday for a huge ‘Aggressive Expansion’ show as Tony Miranda defends his North Beach belt against Airica Lynn and Michael Lynch. And then in our huge main event Alexis Terry will face Alioth Starre for GIW’s Undisputed Championship all with Alex overseeing the match, be there!” With that the show slowly fades with Starre in the ring holding his belt high in the air as Alexis glares at him as she backs up the ramp, wanting nothing more then to get the belt around her waist as the show ends. WINNERS: ALIOTH STARRE & ZEROTINE VALENTINO
