GIW — Pay-per-view

Malice 2012, 15 January 2012

15 January 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS MALICE! LIVE from The Galveston Convention Center – January 15, 2012! BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! The ION Television cameras begin to broadcast the first supershow of 2012, Malice, through the air and into your television sets, but for a moment everything is black. A narrator begins to read the following passage, with slides of the road to Malice playing. “We are wrestlers, we are demented human beings. Our entire lifestyle is based on the physical punishment of another man who stands in our way. We stop at nothing to achieve our goals, even if it means ruining the life of someone else. Bodies have been broken, dreams have been shattered, and families have been torn apart by the nature of our business. But we love it. Even though there is so much at risk for a relatively unimportant prize, it is the infliction of punishment that keeps this sport from becoming stagnant. We are all malicious here, in this business. We thrive on pain. We thrive on risk. That is the power of Malice.” The screen fades away as we slowly begin to see the Galveston Convention Center and the crowd of one thousand people who are stuffed inside like sardines. The theme song begins to play, and the crowd goes absolutely insane. We cut over to the announce booth. RILEY STEVENS: “Ladies and gentlemen, this is the moment we all have been waiting for. It is time for the first supershow of 2012, GIW Malice right here from the Galveston Convention Center, overlooking the Seawall and icy Gulf of Mexico. Tonight’s results have the power to shake the foundation of GIW, we could see people rise to power tonight who have ill intentions in mind for the company. Tonight could very well mark a new era in Galveston Island Wrestling, and it may not be for the better.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Riley, change is good. I’m assuming you are talking about the possibility of Aspen Chaud and Alexis Terry becoming champions, and to that I say, thank GOD! Think about it, Riley! Aspen Chaud and Alexis Terry doing photo shoots on the website wearing only their title belts, my God the possibilities are endless!” RILEY STEVENS: “You are exactly the kind of guy they want in GIW. Ones that are so caught up in their beauty that you do whatever they say, and think they are innocent all of the time. You have to see through it. All they do is manipulate their way to the top, and I sincerely hope that tonight they get in their place tonight.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Oh, I’ll manipulate my way on top on of them, and put them in their place while I see right through their clothes. Am I right or am I right?” RILEY STEVENS: “You’re ridiculous. But, don’t forget, those are only the top two matches of the night. We also have three other matches to entertain you fans at home. For the first time in months, the GIW Tag Team Championships will return to action, and they will be up for grabs in the triple threat tag team match. Jak Hendricks and Seth Seeker take on Nextwave, and The Sin City Express in what is sure to be one hell of a match.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I love tag teaming. Not just in the bedroom either. Man I’m on a roll tonight. But seriously, Jak Hendricks and Seth Seeker have a lot of experience here, and everyone knows what they are capable of, but I don’t think they will be able to overcome The Sin City Express. Did you see what they did to Harper and Fielder last week on Revolt? It was brutal, no, it was MALICIOUS! So, they’ve got my vote.” RILEY STEVENS: “Oh, I thought Barack Obama had your vote? Regardless, that’s not all that we have for you tonight. The number one contendership for the Legacy Championship is on the line when Assassin takes on GIW veteran, Marcus Hister, in a singles match.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I’ll be taking a piss during this match. Alexis Terry is going to win the Legacy belt tonight, and no one is going to ever take it from her. So, if I know that, why the hell would I care about this match. I mean, look at this way, do you think Hister can beat Terry? Who the hell is Assassin? These guys need to do something for once in their career before I even bother to continue talking about them.” RILEY STEVENS: “Well, we also have a casket match for the first time in GIW history. The demented Altair faces a resurging Johnny Violence in a match where you have to actually throw your opponent into a casket to be declared the winner.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Well, this match is perfect for these two men, seeing as how they can’t win a normal singles match on their own. Seriously, how hard is it to put someone into a casket? It’s so simple, you just pick them up and shove them inside.” RILEY STEVENS: “Uh, for some reason I think it is a bit more complicated than that Oliver. I don’t think any GIW opponent will just lay down and let you put them in a casket.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Oh? Alex Castellanos will lay down for Aspen Chaud in a Sole Survivor match. Do you remember that? I’m pretty sure I called it with you, and that is the guy you want to be the Undisputed Champion? Give me a break, Aspen Chaud is a goddess.” Suddenly, “Demon Eyes” by The Answer hit’s the PA system, and the fans blow up knowing they are about to be visited by the Chairman, Blake Beckett. He entered down the ramp in his trademark gray suit, and ascended the steps and entered the ring with the microphone in his hand. The crowd was going wild for Beckett, and he began to speak when his music stopped. BLAKE BECKETT: “Thank you, thank you all. This is a big night for GIW, and it is only fitting that there would be a big announcement on a big night. Right?” The crowd cheers at the notion of a huge announcement, and Beckett, a master at cliffhangers, lets a few moments pass before he begins to shed some more light on the situation. BLAKE BECKETT: “Well, I actually have two to make this evening. Number one, I’m proud to announce that our next supershow, Retaliation, will taking place in the great city, and frequent host of GIW’s shows, New Orleans, Louisiana! But secondly, I’m here to announce that nine GIW stars will be competing in the APW Survive and Conquer match, which will feature the best of the best from wrestling companies all around the United States, and the world. GIW is becoming a household name for independent promotions around the United States, and it is all thanks to you.” “Did It On ‘Em” by Nicki Minaj hit’s the PA system, and the crowd absolutely erupts in boos at the sight of Aspen Chaud. She walked down the ring in a frilly pink dress, designed specifically to be outrageous, and huge heels. Joshua Acquin was with her in his “Worst Nightmare” shirt from his glory days, and a pair of blue jeans. He held the hopes for her as she entered the ring, coming face to face with Beckett. ASPEN CHAUD: “Blake, I was sent out here to tell you something. Something really important. No one wants to hear you open up the show. I wanted to be an assistant GM in the past, do you remember that? You gave it to me. Now, I want you gone, and if it is the last thing that I do in this company, I will get what I want.” BLAKE BECKETT: “Aspen, why do you want me gone so bad? I mean, you are completely right. It seems like you always find a way to get what you want. So why are you so adamant that I resign as Chairman of GIW? I could have fired you so many times now, hell, I could have fired you and Alexis for what you pulled on APW last week. It seems like a never ending with you. You break the rules, then you annoy me enough to where I let you off. So what case do you honestly think you have against me right now?” ASPEN CHAUD: “Blake, you don’t seem to understand. I want you gone because you have been a thorn in my side from the first show and beyond. I believe in solving problems. I’m a problem solver. And you are a problem, so I am going to find a solution.” BLAKE BECKETT: “Aspen, why are you out here? Just to tell me you hate me again? That’s hardly productive, and frankly it is a waste of time.” ASPEN CHAUD: “I’m here to warn you, because tonight your world will slowly begin to fall apart. When I beat Tony Miranda and Alex Castellanos, and Alexis beats Faith Simpson, your two worst enemies will hold gold. I know you are leaving for the APW conference right after you walk out of this ring, and then I’ll be allowed to do whatever the hell I want to do. So, take one good whiff of the air here Beckett, because it won’t be yours to breath much longer… if you are still even breathing when I’m done with you.” “Did It On ‘Em” plays again, and Aspen exits with Joshua guarding her back. We slowly fade away into a promotional package for Retaliation.
