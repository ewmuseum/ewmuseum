GIW — Pay-per-view

Retaliation 2012, 19 February 2012

19 February 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS RETALIATION!! LIVE from the Avron B. Fogelman Arena in New Orleans, Louisiana - February 19, 2012! The show begins not with the normal introduction sequence, but with a simple black screen. Although it is completely barren and without any purpose, it’s presence of nothingness and silence draws you in. For about thirty seconds, there is nothing but dead air. Calm. Then, from the left side of the screen, David Sinclair enters the shot, only his head and neck in view, dressed in what appears to be a suit. The crowd is audibly booing now, causing Sinclair to laugh, but he just uses this as fuel to propel him onward. He begins to speak directly into the camera, piercing through to connect with anyone he can reach. He talks quietly, and purposefully. DAVID SINCLAIR: “It has been such a long time since I have had the opportunity to speak with you directly. I’ve been back for a few weeks now, but I just haven’t found enough time to fully give myself to you. So here I am. I am back now, and this time, I can throw my voice all around the world with this iPPV that we are broadcasting right now. My voice can be heard in England, Thailand, South Africa, and Venezuela, all at one time; all at the same time. I feel the power of notoriety streaming through the body with every pump of my heart, and I love the feeling. In just three weeks with me at helm of Galveston Island Wrestling, we have ditched the shitty, stinky island for New Orleans, Louisiana - the only surviving gem of the south; and we have ditched super shows for a more public iPPV. You all hate me because I don’t play fair. I don’t like to do things the ‘ethical way’, I don’t like to follow the ‘rules’, but really, who gets hurt? Besides Beckett of course! I have given you the most entertaining three weeks of wrestling programming that you could ask for, yet you hate me. Do you hate me just because you are told to hate me, or did you make up your own decision? If anything, I am the reason GIW is still alive. You see, there is an old adage that what doesn’t kill you only makes you stronger; before I was around to found Evolution Pro Wrestling and try to outdo GIW, this company was joke! Because of me, Beckett pushed the envelope to keep up, and he won the first time, only because the people I had on my side were jokes themselves. But as time passed, Beckett became more and more content with GIW remaining in the place it was. A little piss ant regional federation, built on ‘rasslin and ‘rivalries. You would be lying to yourself if you say you honestly thought that Beckett was going to keep this company around. I saw my shot, I picked my allies, and viola, thanks to me any my drive - my intellect - my God given ability to be a creative genius, GIW has doubled in size. In three weeks, a company has doubled in size. Tonight, think of this a turning point, a climax if you will, for this company. We will go from being trapped inside a disgusting floating trash heap of an island, and we will expand our boundaries as far east as New Orleans, as far north as Omaha, as far south as Cancun, and as far west as Phoenix. Our sphere is growing. Our prospects are growing. Our bank account is growing! We are running at our finest form yet, and I will do whatever it takes to make sure the balance is not upset, and we continue to grow in such a great and extensive way. I hope you all enjoy the show.” BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! Air cannons shoot into the crowd getting them amped up for the show, but unfortunately it is mostly filled with boos as the entire crowd is extremely upset with what Sinclair has just said. The camera cuts over to Zachary Pagonis, the interviewer, who is out in the crowd with a microphone standing next to a fan with a “GET HYPE” Tony Miranda shirt and a big cheese head, homage to Matt Kluge of course. The fan looked irate. ZACHARY PAGONIS: “Zachary Pagonis here with Daniel Clemons, a GIW fan from Lafayette, Louisiana who made the trip here tonight for Retaliation. I thought that getting a fan’s response to Sinclair’s ‘State of GIW’ address would be interesting. So, what did you think of what was said?” The fan took the microphone and stared into the camera like he was about to deliver an old school promo. He wagged his finger as he spoke in a deep voice. DANIEL CLEMONS: “Zachary, David Sinclair is nothing but a joke! That’s what we all say! He needs to get hit by a car, because I know someone out there wants to take him out! Him and Aspen and Alexis and Joshua and Defias too, I’ll take them all out myself if I have to!” “Earthquake” by Labyrinth hits the speakers, and crowd erupts in even more boos as we cut over to see Daniel Starr making his way down to the ring. We can hear the voices of Riley Stevens and Oliver Thibodaux chiming in as he makes his way down to the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “This has not been a conventional opening to a GIW show by any means. I suppose that is alright though as we are now coming to you live via an iPPV.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Really inspirational words there by Sinclair. That man has a gift; in fact, he might be God’s gift to the wrestling industry. The man has ‘it’.” RILEY STEVENS: “He has something alright, probably just arrogance. When Blake gets back and sees what he has done, I’m sure there will be hell to pay.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Oh, you mean when Beckett rolls up in his wheelchair because his legs are shattered into a million pieces? Yeah. I’ve seen the pictures. He looks like a mummy he has so much gauze everywhere, Beckett won’t be doing anything anytime soon.” QUOTE As usual the show is going to start with the big Open Battle Royal where the fans will get to see some of the big GIW stars in action. Already in the ring are all the stars but soon “Crooked Man” by Those Poor Bastards hits and the fans go wild as Matt Kluge enters from the back heading to the ring with a grin on his face, he was the only one that got an intro and there was a reason for that he was a legend. JAMAL JACKSON: “Please welcome your Open Battle Royal participants CJ Hayes, Izzie Dead, Hayden Carter, Michael Harris, Evan Clarke, Johnny Steel and Matt Kluge!” RILEY STEVENS: “I don’t think any of us saw Matt Kluge making his return, especially not in this fashion.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Woopty doo.” The fans made no secret who they were cheering for, it would be Matt Kluge and Izzie Dead the only tenured members of the fed. Kluge steps into the ring, immediately now the enemy as the referee calls for the bell. DING DING DING! This thing starts and right away it’s Matt Kluge and Izzie Dead in a corner together as the other guys stare across at them, naturally this was a chance for all of them to get back at him. Izzie being the smart zombie he is realizes this and slowly walks across the ring attempting to slide in on the ‘Attack Kluge’ bandwagon however it ends up back firing as Johnny Steel grabs him and throws him over the top rope to a sad boo from the crowd. Izzie Dead is Eliminated! The fans angrily boo as this starts an all out brawl as now things get going as it’s Michael Harris that goes after Matt Kluge while CJ Hayes and Johnny Steel do the same with Hayden Carter and Evan Clarke being the last duo to be brawling. The fans are cheering for Kluge as Dead hits the back as Kluge spins Harris around and starts to deliver big elbows in the corner. Carter continues to put up a fight but Clarke gets the advantage in the corner, delivering a big Suplex to her in the center of the ring as the fans try to stay focused though it’s hard with so much going on at once. RILEY STEVENS: “The winner here will surely make their presence felt here in GIW.” Back to the ring we go where Harris uses a big knee to the gut to turn things around on Kluge, while Hayes Steel tackles Hayes to the mat delivering punches. In the corner we see Clarke rushing at Carter only for her to jump up her feet to kick him away. The fans start to give her some love to now, seeming to start to like her as she comes forward into Clarke, trying to set him up for a Piledriver but Clarke throws her over her shoulder sending her over the ropes but amazingly she gets one hand on the rope, hanging over the edge showing off her determination to win but Clarke basically boots her fingers sending her to the outside. Hayden Carter is Eliminated! The fans groan again as another of the ones they were cheering for has been eliminated as she holds her fingers in pain. Looking upset with herself, having made a simple mistake which in this type of match will cost you. She heads to the back with fans patting her on the back as Clarke sits back in the corner watching the others fight. Steel spins and tries to throw Hayes over but he spins and ends up sending Steel over instead. But Johnny is able to land on the outside, holding on for dear life as Hayes comes forward only to get punched away. RILEY STEVENS: “On the outside of the ropes is the last place you want to be.” Steel tries to get back in but Hayes is able to run forward using his hips to send him back through the ropes, Steel still holds on. On the other side of the ring Kluge is whipped off the ropes by Harris right into a Flying Knee from Clarke the two deciding to work together to take out the GIW veteran. However on the other side we have Steel trying to stay in but out of no where Hayes starts to slap and punch him repeatedly eventually Steel starts to teeter back and it’s a huge Headbutt that finishes off the ‘M-16’ named moved that sends Steel falling backwards out of the ring. Johnny Steel is Eliminated! That leaves just four left as Kluge gets up in the corner and now as is obligatory in any battle royal all four men head to their corners looking around at one another. Clarke goes for Kluge. But its Clarke’s bad choice that leaves him in a mess as Kluge hoists him up as soon as he gets his hands on him, right into a Snap Suplex. RILEY STEVENS: “Here comes Kluge.” Kluge hops up and now it’s Harris takes his shot now walking towards Kluge only to be given the move that Kluge calls the ‘Hortonville Suplex Special 1’ a Suplex rolled into a Mounted Punching. Kluge jumps up and Hayes tries to rush at him but Matt ducks under his clothesline then when he spins around it’s Kluge that rushes at him with ‘The Matt Cooke’ a Running Knee to CJ’s knee that sends him hopping along on one knee as Kluge grabs him and spins around tossing him over the top rope to the outside. CJ Hayes is Eliminated! But almost immediately Harris and Clarke hop back to their feet and with Kluge’s back turned they hoist up his legs and flip him over the top rope to eliminate him. Matt Kluge is Eliminated! The fans boo as Kluge falls to the mat, looking around angrily, after basically going nuts and taking all three out he is flipped over the top rope. Harris and Clarke head back to the center of the ring to size one another up, getting ready to fight. The two go in for a lock up and it’s Harris that gets the advantage as the much bigger man, backing him up into the ropes where he sends him off the ropes. On the rebound Harris connects with a Double A Spinebuster, as he hops up quickly pumping his fists getting excited now as Clarke crawls to the corner for refuge. RILEY STEVENS: “The last two men here.” Harris starts to follow up on him, as he hoists him up to his feet where he nails him with an Standing Dropkick that sends him back against the ropes where it seems Clarke is a lost cause seeming dazed and confused as Harris gets up rearing back and clotheslining him over the top as he turns around and starts to celebrate however it’s Clarke that kept one hand on the rope able to get a foot on the side of the ring, trying to get his balance when Harris turns around to see him still up. Heading for ‘The Brooklyn Knockout’ a Gore to send him out of the ring but just as soon as he gets his footing Clarke pulls down the ropes and Harris goes over the ropes to the outside! JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…EVAN CLARKE!” The fans are not sure how to react at this point, not really knowing all that much about all these stars yet as Clarke looks down at Harris then gets a smile on his face as he heads back into the ring to celebrate. RILEY STEVENS: “Many stars have been spawned through the Open Battle Royal and on a stage like ‘Retaliation’, Evan Clarke just made a big statement.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Just glad Izzie Dead didn’t win.” Harris looks back into the ring at Clarke angrily heading to the back, having just gotten caught in this match the two really both impressing here tonight along with everyone in the match. Other then Kluge all of them would get the chance to really prove themselves on the next Revolt. WINNER: EVAN CLARKE After a quick video package we find ourselves looking at Daniel Starr walking down to the ring, probably for the next match ready to go however with a microphone, he seems prepped to talk. RILEY STEVENS: “Well, it looks like Daniel Starr has something to say, so let’s here it.” His music has faded, and he stands in the center of the ring with a microphone, waiting to begin to speak. When he gets the cue to begin, he wastes no time. DANIEL STARR: “I arrived in GIW two weeks ago looking for a new place to get famous. A talented wrestler such as myself deserves nothing less than fame and fortune, and I thought Galveston Islands Wrestling was going to be that step that propelled me to greatness. Well, I think it goes without saying that I have been disappointed with the opposition I have reached.” The crowd wasn’t happy with that statement, and they let him know it. DANIEL STARR: “Well, it is the truth. Ever since I have been here, I’ve gotten to face all of the undesirables in the company, I deserve better! There are two warring factions right now, Castellanos and Chaud, and they keep begging me to pick a side. Well I’m not picking, because the fact is that me joining either of those teams would lower my value. I’d have to hold myself back to make them look good. That’s not how I work. When I talk, people listen. When I wrestle, people watch. When I eat a sandwich, people ask what it is so they can go and make one for themselves. I have the power.” He throws his hands into the air, acting like he is ashamed of his so called ‘power’. Then he continues ranting to the crowd, even though they obviously have had enough of it. DANIEL STARR: “So, for everyone who thinks you have to fall into one of the cliques to be successful here - I am going to prove tonight when I become the number one contender for the Legacy title, that all it takes is talent. Unfortunately, not everyone has talent like I do.” “Grand Central” by Edward Shearmur hits the PA system, and the crowd goes absolutely wild at the introduction of Alioth Starre, the longest reigning former GIW Undisputed Champion in history, and winner of the APW Survive and Conquer 2012 event. He comes to the ring dressed in street clothes, obviously not prepared for any action tonight. He carries a microphone and a slick smirk on his face to the ring. ALIOTH STARRE: “It never fails. A rookie comes into the company, wins a few matches, and is suddenly the best thing since sliced bread. But, it takes much more than sliced bread to impress me. It is true that you are undefeated here, other Starr, but it is also true that you will likely be defeated tonight, and even if you do win, you are still only the second best star here.” Starr laughed as the crowd popped at Alioth’s pointed words. They knew this would eventually turn into something if Alioth kept it up. DANIEL STARR: “The second best Star here eh? It’s funny, I seem to be the only one who is still even around anymore. You think you run this place, but you hardly ever wrestle anymore.” Alioth was quick to strike back in their war of words. ALIOTH STARRE: “Oh, you think I have lost a step since my incident do you? Well, I would be more than happy to prove to you that I am still the best in this company.” Daniel Starr smirked and began to exit the ring. DANIEL STARR: “Maybe our paths will cross in the future.” His music began to play, and we faded into a promotional package. QUOTE “The Red” by Chevelle hits as we come back to the ring and the fans immediately begin to boo Austin Reynolds as he heads out from the back with that pretty face and that big smirk, as he heads towards the ring ready to go. Introducing first weighing in at two hundred fifteen pounds, standing six foot two inches tall…from Jefferson, Georgia…Austin Reynolds!” RILEY STEVENS: “Austin has made a lot of enemies quick here, siding with Alexis Terry.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I am trying to be nice with him…” Austin slips into the ring with a big smirk as his music trails off and "Midnight Romance" by A Kiss Could Be Deadly hits and the fans go wild as Cassidy Hightower comes rushing out from the back with her big smile, ready to win this match. JAMAL JACKSON: “And introducing his opponent weighing in at one hundred and nineteen pounds, standing five foot seven inches tall…from Toronto, Canada…Cassidy Hightower!” RILEY STEVENS: “Cassidy has grown a strong fan base here in GIW.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “No idea why.” Cassidy high fives some members of the crowd then jumps up the steps, stepping into the ring ready to go looking across the ring at Reynolds as he stares back at her as the referee then rings the bell. DING DING DING! The tension could be cut by a knife as the bell sounded and Austin and Cassidy circled each other in the middle of the ring. The GIW fans were on their feet for this huge match up. Still Fresh in Cassidy’s mind was the brutal attack on Seth Ramsey that Austin Reynolds just so happened to be a apart of, an attack that left Seth in the hospital. The duo finally locked up in the center of the ring and the much physically stronger Austin Reynolds gains the upper hand with a headlock. Cassidy does her best to fight out of it but is unable to do so. RILEY STEVENS: “These two are fighting to prove themselves here in GIW.” Cassidy is finally able to hit an elbow to the stomach and then sends Austin into the ropes. Austin ducks a clothesline attempt and connects with a huge standing dropkick. The crowd inside the Avron B. Fogelman Arena are hot and on their feet as Austin smirks at the fallen Cassidy Hightower who is on the mat in pain. After wasting valuable seconds taking in his actions, Austin slowly picks Cassidy up by the hair. Austin once again flashes an arrogant and connects with a beautiful gutwrench powerbomb. Austin covers but is only able to get a two count as Hightower is able to get a shoulder up at the last second. RILEY STEVENS: “Cassidy is a fighter, she will never be an easy out.” Austin wastes little time and continues the assault, mounting Cassidy and delivering right hand after right hand to the face of the Canadian wrestling prodigy. Despite her rebellious strikes and struggling, Nathan tore her from the canvas and performed his Pearl Necklace maneuver again in the match. DING DING DING! Surprised Chaud freezes up for a moment before snapping out and getting back down on him, looking around at the crowd as she grabs him and then locks in the ‘K.M.A.’ [Kiss My Aspen] a Heel Lock that she gets in, right in the center of the ring as Alex grimaces in pain as the fans seem to now start to almost lose hope as Alex grits his teeth not willing to give up yet.
