GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: FULL THROTTLE LIVE! From Mario’s Bar and Grill – August 18, 2011 QUOTE Brody Harper vs. “The Wild Boy” Justin McCoy The opening starts off with test of strength between these two heavyweight competitors, unfortunately for Brody Harper this series was a huge disadvantage. He finally lifts McCoy up for a Body Slam with four minutes left but The Wild Boy as he is called slid over him and locks in a sleeper hold, Brody is able to power out and the two men brawl for a while. Brody tries a Powerbomb with 30 seconds left but McCoy blocks it. 5 minutes are up and the match continues. Brody gets mad and snaps McCoy down with a Powerbomb just missing the time limit. QUOTE Nick Starks vs. Tony “The Iron Man” Rodgers Both men “Marvel” at their names for a second before Starks simply rolls Tony up for a two count. Tony tries to use his high flying moves but Starks is able to keep him grounded with his array of suplexes. Near the 4 minute mark Starks locks Tony in a side head lock and with the ref not looking Starks split’s the uprights with his back leg. From there he hit’s the Starkpocalypse for the count of 3 winning the match with time to spare. QUOTE Jak Hendricks vs. The Masked Flipper #9 The Masked Flipper #9 tries to use his Madagascar style of high impact moves but he misses an early Corkscrew Moonsault to a standing opponent and from there Jak dominates the smaller wrestler. Jak grabs the mask of The Masked Flipper #9 and throws him to the ground before locking in his Sharpshooter with 2 minutes left. QUOTE Sean Galen vs. Some Guy Sean Galen dominates this match throwing this Guy around the ring like a rag doll. In two minutes he hit’s the Equalizer for the win, but in all honesty he could have finished this match a lot sooner. QUOTE Christian Rivers vs. “Smart” Marky Mark In a back and forth match with a lot of headlocks, arm bars, and various displays of work rate based matches. Marky Mark tries playing a heel using a thumb to the eyes. He tries to throw a forearm but Christian catches the arm and hits him with a arm drag that he uses to apply the Stay Dead with a fingernail biting 10 seconds left. QUOTE John Rawlings vs. Larry Potter This match is very by the books…. Rawlings hits Larry with a T-Bone Suplex that hurt more then that lame pun. He catches him in the Raw Deal but Larry was able to reach the ropes unluckily for him. Rawlings catches him with a Exit 9 that bounces him into the ropes and finishes him off with a Fall From Glory 2 minutes and 45 seconds into the match. QUOTE Katya Rose vs. Faith The Pride Of St.Petersburg went to town on this local female wrestler. Simply battering her with slaps and power moves. She hits her with an Agent Provocateur and knocks Faith out. QUOTE Curtis Durrington vs. Matthew Dannys The Southern Submission Machine takes apart the Indy wrestler Matthew Dannys with his ground game. He throws him around with suplexes and stiff chops. Matthew tries to mount a comeback but Curtis catches him in the Daphne Deathlock. QUOTE The Pillow vs. Sin Vida In possibly the most pointless match in GIW featuring The Pillow true to his namesake and Sin Vida who is a Luchador Doll. Both objects just sit there for ten minutes until it’s declared a draw. During the match The Grateful Dead came out and entertained the crowd it looked like El Ladder challenged The Pillow to a match.
