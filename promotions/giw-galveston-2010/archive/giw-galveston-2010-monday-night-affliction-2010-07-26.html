He rebounds him off of the ropes, delivering a huge clothesline which sends Chris down to the mat in agony. Aaron picks the wounded superstar up and suplexes his repeatedly before his last one is stopped by Chris. Armstrong reverses it into a standing suplex of his own, before locking in a one-legged Boston Crab. After a minute of being in the hold, Snow manages to grab the ring ropes, causing Chris to break it off. Adam Calloway: This match seems pretty equal to me. Carlos Castillo: You're insane. Chris then hits a facebuster on Aaron, before whipping him into the corner. He then picks him up and places him on the top turnbuckle, preparing for the Money Maker, but a quick and unseen eye poke from Snow causes him to fall back. Aaron then hits the Irish Spring, then goes for the pin: 1...2...Leg on the rope! As he screams at the referee in anger, Chris Armstrong pulls a pair of brass knuckles out, puts them on, and nails Aaron with a powerful KO punch. he slides them off and kicks them out of the ring as he covers Aaron Snow. 1...2...3!!! Carlos Castillo: Brilliant finish! Chris celebrates as the crowd boo, but unfortunately Aaron Snow, jaw damaged and all, hits him with an Irish Blizzard as he turns around. He then begins to mercilessly attack Chris, punching him in the head until he gets busted open, until security manage to pull him off. The crowd give a modest cheer for him as he's escorted to the back by the guards. Adam Calloway: Can't say Chris didn't deserve it! Liviana Valentine vs. Bret Battery ‘F.C.P.R.E.M.I.X’ by Fall of Troy hits the small outdoor venue with full force as the fans begin immediately booing. The entrance theme is that of Liviana Valentine. She makes her way down the entrance ramp pushing a shopping cart full of chairs, nails, pipes, traffic signs, and light tubes. The fans immediately begin to cheer upon seeing the assortment of goodies that Liviana has brought with her to the ring. She has a microphone in hand as she parks the cart with the other outside the ring. The crowd takes up the chant ‘This Is Awesome’ as Liviana Music dies. Liviana does her signature move by slapping her ass too the fans who boo loudly. Carlos Castillo: “Finally we got some great talent on this show as Liviana Valentine makes her presence felt in the Affliction ring tonight. We usually see her on Thursday Night revolt, but we’ll take em’ how we get em’. Isn’t that right Calloway?” Adam Calloway: “You know I look first at talent instead of their heritage unlike you, but I agree Liviana is one of, if not the shinning star of not just women’s wrestling, but wrestling period. Now it’s quite interesting to see the cart filled with dangerous devices, I wonder what she has in store for us.” Liviana went thru the cart looking for a specific weapon; it doesn’t take her long to pull out a yellow, blood stained traffic sign that read ‘Dead End’. Liviana smiles evilly as she displays the sign like a model displays the round card at a boxing or UFC fight. Liviana lays it down and retrieves a microphone; she raises it up to her lips as she begins to speak in front of the booing crowd. Liviana Valentine “Yes indeed, Monday Night just got a whole lot better because I, the new Diva’s Champion Liviana Valentine, am gracing all of you nimrodic Neanderthals with my breathtaking presence. So now I’m going to get down to business. Tonight I will be facing Bret Battery; Bret was originally poised for greatness, until he made a fatal mistake and backed down in fear of the greatness he could have achieved under the leadership of Joshua Aquin and me. He did wise up and try to gain re-entry into our ranks, but you see life doesn’t work like that. For you unlearned and unwashed masses let me school you all on something. Newton, states that with every action there is an equal or opposite reaction. So Bret tonight is the reaction to your flimsy faith so come on out.” Bret Battery walks out of the entrance area. His theme music doesn’t play as he is trying to show how serious he was about his match tonight. He makes his way down the ring. The referee for the match is following him as he walks down the ramp. Both Bret and the referee make their way into the ring. Liviana brings the microphone up to her lips once more. Liviana Valentine “Excuse me Mr. Referee, let me state to you the rules of this match. From the match maker Joshua Aquin, he states that in this match up the contestants can win via pinfall submission, or excessive bodily damage. He also states that as an added stipulation, I may be allowed to use any weapon of my choosing without facing disqualification, but Bret cannot use any weapon other than himself. If he does use a weapon, He will be disqualified on the spot.” Carlos Castillo: “Seems pretty fair to me…” Adam Calloway: “Are you insane? This wont be a match, this will be a massacre…” The referee acknowledges the stipulations and rings the bell. Liviana pick up the dead end sign again. The crowd begins cheering as Liviana and Bret circle each other. They both rush at each other, Bret attempts a high clothesline, and Liviana simply ducks under his arm and nails him in the back with the dead end traffic sign. The crowd cheers as Bret grimaces in pain. Be doesn’t fall to the mat but he reaches for his back. Liviana rushes in and chop blocks Bret off his feet using the dead end sign. Bret hits the mat back first and begins holding the back of his knee. Carlos Castillo “Livi’s using the stipulations to her advantage like any other great champion would.” Adam Calloway: “Or like any other opportunistic cheater would…” Liviana hits the grounded Bret on his back with the sign. She hits him so hard that the sign flies out of her hand upon impact. She doesn’t hesitate as she immediately pounces upon Bret raining in with left and right fist to his head. Bret covers up, protecting his head. Bret then uses his size advantage to muscle Liviana off of him. Bret gets to his feet while Liviana is still on the mat. He runs and kicks her in the stomach. Liviana grabs her stomach and rolls out the ring to regroup. Bret begins to chase her walking to the ropes. However Liviana quickly grabs Bret legs and trips him. She then pulls him to the outside. Bret gets up gingerly while Liviana begins to stalk him. She makes a quick kick to Bret’s damaged knee causing it to buckle. Bret is one knee as Liviana walks up to him. She begins talking trash while slapping him on the head. Bret uses his strong arm to punch Liviana in the stomach. Liviana coils over in pain. Bret then hops to his feet and grabs Liviana by the hair. He then aggressively uses Liviana’s hair to Irish whip her into the ring steps. Liviana hits the steps hard shoulder first. Carlos Castillo: “This is turning into a very physical match up…” Adam Calloway: “Yes, Bret is using his size and strength advantage over Livi.” Bret is in control now as he grabs Liviana and tosses her into the ring. Bret begins posing for the fans who cheer him on. Bret then begins climbing the ring apron, but he is nailed by a unexpected chair shot for Liviana. Bret somehow stays on the apron. Liviana then pulls Bret thru the ropes and hangs his feet on the ropes for leverage. She then nails him on the head with a hangman DDT. Liviana gives a primal scream, and goes on the attack. She walks to the shopping cart and pulls out a kendo stick. She then begins to wail on Bret with the cane. She hits him in different areas as he backs up to the turnbuckle. She then hits him on the head with the cane, and Bret losses consciousness. Liviana sees this and tosses the cane. She walks up to the cart and positions it in front of Bret. Liviana then backs up the opposite way gathering her strength. Carlos Castillo: “She wouldn’t…” Adam Calloway: “She is…” Liviana rushes at Bret with the cart and rams into him crushing him between the cart and the turnbuckle. Bret slums over and then falls to the mat. The crowd screams loudly as blood is seen on Bret’s forehead. Liviana is exhausted as she begins to walk over to the floored Bret Battery. She then grabs him by the hair and pulls him up to his feet. Bret instinctively kicks Liviana in the mid section causing her to back off. As she backs off Bret charges her and knocks her off her feet with a nasty clothesline. Bret wipes the collecting blood on his forehead smearing it. The then picks Liviana up and puts her back on the mat with a snap suplex. Bret rolls over on Liviana going for the pin. The referee rushes over to make the count. Liviana kicks out at two. Bret picks her up and knocks her back down with a European uppercut. He the picks up both of her legs and sling shots her head first into the steel cart. The crow cheers wildly as Liviana as well is busted open. Something comes over Bret as he rolls to the outside and fishes under the ring for something. He pulls out a table and slides it into the ring. Carlos Castillo: “He can’t use that. It would be cheating….” Adam Calloway: “Right now Bret doesn’t even care about winning or losing.” Bret sets up the table as Liviana makes it up to her feet. Bret finishes the table and turns around. He is nailed in the head with the Diva’s championship belt by Liviana. Bret falls back on to the table. Liviana grabs his feet and props his full body on the table. She then limps toward the turnbuckle. She climbs to the top as the audience get on their feet cheering. Liviana balances herself and slightly kicks out her left leg. She then leaps off of the turnbuckle and extends her left leg as she is turning a 360 in the air. She land on Bret upright with her legs. Both of them smash thru the table. And the crowd goes wild. Carlos Castillo: “That’s the 360 leg drop Liviana calls the ‘Elle Deux’ and right thru the table oh my god this is exciting.” Adam Calloway: “This is indeed match of the year material.” The crowd begins to chant Holy Shit. The referee looks upon the unmoving bodies of Liviana Valentine, and Bret Battery. He makes a hand signal and calls the ring announcer into the ring. The ring announcer and the referee have a small conversation as Liviana and Bret were making it to their feet. The ring announcer began to speak in his microphone and several EMT’s rushed from the backstage with stretchers and first aid kits. Ring Announcer “Due to the inability for both competitors to continue further this match has been stopped via excessive bodily damage. Carlos Castillo: “They can’t stop this right now, this was great.” Adam Calloway: “I agree with you but you have to think about the health status of the competitors. This is a safe call.” Liviana and Bret shrug off the EMT’s and circle each other as the crowd chants let them fight. Liviana and Bret both drop their fist and look at each other. The fans boo assuming that they weren’t going to fight anymore. Liviana and Bret then evilly smile at each other. An EMT began walking towards Bret with a first aid kit. Bret performed a spinning back fist that floored the EMT knocking him out cold. Liviana charged the other EMT and Bret tackled the ring announcer. Both Liviana and Bret began savagely beating anyone in sight. Bret the grabbed referee and slammed him down with a nasty Chokeslam Adam Calloway: “What the bloody hell do these two think their doing.” Carlos Castillo: “I don’t know but I don’t feel safe here…” Liviana uses the cart to run over the head of an EMT busting him open badly. The crowd is booing heavily. Liviana and Bret roll out the ring and begin attacking camera men. Liviana takes an audience member’s skateboard and breaks it across the head of a cameraman. Bret tosses a few camera men head first into the steel crowd barrier. Liviana picks up a camera and crushes it across the face of Brooke Ellis who was too slow to escape. Liviana then grabs Brooke’s bag of pretzels and chokes her with it. Bret and Liviana then make a beeline straight for Adam and Carlos. They strike them down heavily with left and right fists of fury. Bret grabs both of them by the neck and choke slams them thru the announcer table. Liviana puts Carlos into the scorpion death lock and Carlos begins screaming in pain. Liviana releases him and puts Adam into the scorpion death lock, and he cries out as well. Liviana lets him go and she and Bret step back into the ring. Liviana raises Bret arm high in the air, as the fans boo tremendously. Body were everywhere in the ring.
