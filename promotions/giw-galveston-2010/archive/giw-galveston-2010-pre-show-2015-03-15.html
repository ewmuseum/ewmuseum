GIW — Card

Pre-show, 15 March 2015

15 March 2015
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
As the GIW logo flashes across the screen and then fades to black, we cut to the ring where the fans are anxiously awaiting the start of the traditional pre-show open battle royal. “Touch the Sky by Kanye West hits the speakers and Gavin Achilles makes his way to the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Alright ladies and gents, we are giving you a little sample of what is to come tonight right here on the Revolt: Resurgence Pre-Show. Here comes a man that a lot of you have been talking about, the wrestler with one arm – Gavin Achilles. He looks to make an impact tonight, but who knows which wrestlers will step into the ring to face him.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Oh lord, this is the guy everyone is talking out? I could step into the ring and throw him over the rope if there is no one else who wants to enter.” Gavin steps into the ring and raises his arms into the air as “More than Meets the Eye” hits the PA speakers and Melissa Stark steps onto the ramp, her brother George closely behind her. As she makes her way to the ring, the fans boo and Gavin watches his opponent. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Melissa Stark? Well, if she is half as strong as her husband, she won’t have any problem tossing Gavin over the top tonight.” George moves to the outside and watches closely over his sister as she enters the ring. “Bat Country” was the next theme to play as the fans erupted in boos at the sight of a man they were very familiar with – Johnny Violence. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Johnny Violence is back! Do you see it Riley? Johnny Violence is back!” RILEY STEVENS: “I see. Johnny Violence was very close to getting his hands on the Undisputed Championship when he was last here in GIW. Certainly a forced to be reckoned with.” “Get Right Back to Where We Started From” hits the arena and the fans instantly know the man they are about to see – Dennis Driver. He steps onto the ramp and makes his way to the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Dennis Driver, the former GIW Deep South Champion, is back on the island!” With all four competitors in the ring, the bell and we were off. George Stark didn’t waste any time getting involved in this match as he grabbed Gavin’s leg as soon as he could, allowing Melissa Stark to charge the rookie and land a huge running forearm that send him to the mat. The very next thing she did was pick him and attempt to toss him over the rope, but he was still too fresh – instead just pushing her off and then tackling her in a very unorthodox way before beginning to throw some punches. On the other side of the ring, Driver and Violence were chain wrestling, and Violence was getting the upper hand on the smaller Driver. With a headlock takedown, Violence threw Dennis onto the mat and took a step back, potentially to set up for the superkick he calls “Turn the Other Way”, but when he went to connect, Dennis ducked and instead pushed Violence forward so that he could land a dropkick that made contact right at the base of Violence’s next, sending him across the ring and into the turnbuckle, but not to the mat. Driver charged his opponent in the corner, but was met with a reactionary boot that sent him backwards, dazed. Gavin Achilles had managed to keep Melissa Stark down for a moment and decided to charge Johnny Violence and attempt to toss him over. A few stiff punches, Gavin began to lift Violence’s leg over the top rope and was soon joined by Dennis Driver. The impromptu duo was very close to tossing Violence over, getting him entirely over the top rope, but Violence slid back under the bottom rope before he could fall to the mat. Quickly from behind, Melissa Stark threw a big axehandle that took Gavin to his knees, but Dennis Driver was closeby and managed to whip Melissa over the rope. With a big clothesline, he took tossed her over the rope. MELISSA STARK HAS BEEN ELIMINATED RILEY STEVENS: “And now we are down to three!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Johnny Violence got into some trouble there, but he looks like he is in a safe place right now.” Gavin and Dennis were upright now as Violence was stuck in the corner. They looked at each other for a moment, and then back at Violence, seemingly deciding the best course of action would be to team up on him. Gavin pulled Violence up from the corner, but Violence landed a stiff left hook that sent Gavin back. Driver was next to try, this time getting the upper hand by hitting a running knee to Violence’s gut. He dragged violence to the center of the ring before hitting him with two leg drops draped across the neck. Gavin Achilles moved in and picked Violence up, whipping him over to the ropes, but Violence bounced off and connected with a huge spear to Gavin, a move that he calls “The Beast”. He went to battle with Dennis Driver, trading shots back and forth with the former Deep South Champion. The crowd was firmly behind Driver, but Violence got the upper hand, stunning him for a moment and then hitting a bulldog that drove Driver’s head into the mat. RILEY STEVENS: “Good work by Johnny Violence to work on splitting up this alliance.” He pulled Driver off the mat and went to set up his finisher – “Paranoid”, which was a ranhei – but Gavin broke it up with a perfectly placed roundhouse kick. With Violence seemingly out, Gavin began the process of trying to toss him over the rope. He was joined a few moments later by a groggy Dennis Driver. Violence managed to ward off Gavin with a poke to the eye, but he couldn’t get Driver to step away. Gavin took a moment to regain his composure, then paused for a moment, seeing Driver in a vulnerable position as he attempted to toss Violence over the top. He looked the fans, who knew what he was thinking and didn’t appreciate it – but nevertheless Gavin swooped in behind Driver and tossed him over the rope. As Driver fell to the mat, he looked up at Gavin and shook his head in disappointment. DENNIS DRIVER HAS BEEN ELIMINATED OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Hey that was pretty smart move!” RILEY STEVENS: “I think that was rookie mistake. There was no reason for Achilles to eliminate Driver there, especially if they had an alliance going in this match!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Well they weren’t working very well together, and if Gavin wants to win this match, he would have had to face Driver at some point anyway.” RILEY STEVENS: “I understand that Ollie, but Gavin doesn’t need to be making enemies this early in his career. Dennis Driver will not forget this match.” Dennis was screaming at Gavin from the outside, asking him why he would do such a thing. Gavin was screaming back, trying to defend what he did. While he was distracted, Johnny Violence came from behind and tossed the rookie over the top rope and right down to Dennis’ feet. GAVIN ACHILLES HAS BEEN ELIMINATED With the match won, Johnny Violence’s music began to play and he gloated to the two men from inside the ring. He pointed to his head over and over, implying that he was smarter than they were. Dennis doesn’t speak to Gavin, instead just slowly making his way to the back. The camera cut to angle where we could see Violence celebrating on the top rope with Gavin leaned back against the barricade in disappointment. WINNER: JOHNNY VIOLENCE
