Galveston Island Wrestling Presents: Thursday Night Revolt! LIVE FROM PORRETTO BEACH ON SEPTEMBER 16TH, 2010 = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = Revolt, the franchise brand of GIW, has begun. BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! Fireworks stream from the top of the entrance tunnel situated on the pristine sands of Porretto Beach. The ION TV cameras are panning around the crowd, showing the creative signs “EPW can kiss my Aspen” and “who’s the Legacy Champion?”. The camera stops zooming around and then zooms in on the GIW Commentators Oliver Thibodaux and Riley Stevens. RILEY STEVENS: “Welcome everyone to GIW Revolt, right here in Galveston, Texas on the beautiful Porretto Beach! I’m Riley Stevens alongside my broadcast partner, Oliver Thibodaux, and we are getting ready for the last Revolt before our next Supershow, Unscripted!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “For once, Riley, you are correct. Tonight’s Revolt will be off the chain, so many rivalries are going to be poked and prodded tonight, we might just have a wildfire on our hands!” RILEY STEVENS: “Yes, and our opening contest is an eight person tag team match, filled with eight of the most promising stars in GIW today. Each of them are looking to make an impact, and winning this match is the best way to do that.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Yeah, but the next match is even better! We have Alexii Ravynova and Pandora squaring off to become the number one contender for the Women’s Championship at Unscripted. Everyone knows that Alexii and Kacie Church hate each other, but Kacie may not even be the champ come Unscripted! The match after that is a Women’s title match against Model C-22 Female Combat Droid, and she is programmed to KILL!” RILEY STEVENS: “Well, I’m not so sure about that… but the match after the championship match is another number one contenders match, but this time it is between Austin Acquin and Ryan Daniels for the Legacy Championship. Jamie Vaccaro will be present for this match, joining us on commentary!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “That’s nice, Riley, but the main event trounces them all! Aspen, Walker, Miranda, Kennedy, and Sparrow are all in a ladder match, vying for some golden briefcase that no one knows the contents of, except Beckett of course. I wonder what’s inside…?” RILEY STEVENS: “Who knows, Oliver. We don’t have time to speculate… its time to start the show!” The cameras zoom in on the entrance ramp, expecting the arrival of the first participant of the eight person tag team match, but surprisingly the chairman, Blake Beckett’s music hits and he walks down to the ring with a smirk on his face. BLAKE BECKETT: “Now, I’m not one to interrupt my own show… but I think there are some changes that need to be made. This match is booked to be an eight person tag match, but upon some careful deliberation… I found that it would be much more beneficial to make this match and eight person battle royal, where the winner gets placed in a match against an EPW superstar at Unscripted. This is your chance to make a name for yourselves, don’t fail me.” Blake exit’s the ring, and takes a seat at the announce table. RILEY STEVENS: “Ladies and gentlemen, we are being joined by the chairman himself right here at the announcement booth! What an honor!” Blake and Oliver both roll their eyes as the lights go out on Porretto Beach, signaling the start of the eight person battle royal. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = After a short break after the huge announcement we return to the ring where it’s now packed with wrestlers. Standing all around the ring are the men who came into tonight thinking that some of the men with them would be there teammates. Now they all stand ready to represent GIW at ‘Unscripted’. RILEY STEVENS: “Well things sure have changed for these eight guys in the ring tonight, they are about ready to duke it out in a Over the Top Rope Battle Royal.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Please Lord don’t let the Chicken represent us at ‘Unscripted’.” BLAKE BECKETT: “The winner will be the one who earns it regardless. But yeah I hate Chicken just as much as the next guy.” The eight men are ready to go as a few referees have gathered around the ring to watch to see if any of them touch the ground on the outside. The referee on the outside sees that the men are ready then calls for the bell. DING DING DING! The eight men don’t run straight at each other instead they seem to wait. Soon however Don Hairston runs at Christopher Darren Erns who grabs him and tosses him over the ropes and to the outside. Don Hairston is Eliminated! On the other side of the ring Zen does the same at Nicholas Frost who side steps him and hurls him over as well. Zen is Eliminated! Those two are gone already and are probably going to be future endeavor’d soon. Back in the ring six remain as they stare each other down realizing how quickly things have slimmed down. Boom all of them go after one another Chicken attacks Brock Charnel, Frost and Angle lock up, and Darren Erns meets up with Travis Tate to do battle. The crowd cheers as the real fight has begun between these guys. Frost pushes Angle back to the corner where he delivers some shots to the ribs. Suddenly the crowd seems surprised as Brock begins to crush Chicken with punches before grabbing him and hurling him over the top rope sending him to the outside with a huge thud. Chicken is Eliminated! The fans laugh Chicken’s departure off while realizing Brock may be a force to be reckoned with here perhaps the biggest man in the match. Darren Erns on the other side is getting hammered with boots left and right by Travis Tate. On the other side after an eye poke Angle was able to get the upper hand and a low blow sent Frost reeling on the mat. Now he turns his attention over to the big man Brock. He doesn’t seem phased as he runs up and hits a Clothesline to little reaction. He runs back off the ropes and tries again but this time runs right into a ‘6 Foot Under’ a Swinging Side Slam! The fans seem a bit stunned as Brock lifts up Angle and throws his lifeless body to the outside. Jason Angle is Eliminated! OLIVER THIBODAUX: “This guy looks like a force to be reckoned with right here.” Suddenly the rest of the men have gathered on one side of the ring. Travis Tate, Christopher Darren Erns, and Nicholas Frost seem to realize that they are facing a beast right here. Tate runs first at him but receives a big clothesline for his troubles. Next up is CDE who goes full steam only to walk right into a Spinebuster. Last up is Frost who perhaps possesses the most physical problems to Brock. He runs at him only to be grabbed and lifted into the air much to the crowd’s shock. However he’s not done there as he proceeds to lift him over the ropes and toss him over the outside roughly to the ground stunning everyone in attendance. Nicholas Frost is Eliminated! Brock steps back and now it’s the other two men ready to face him. Tate runs up first kicking him in one knee as Darren Erns then does the same to the other leg bringing him down to his knees. The two men seem poised to work together as a unit. They then run at him and try to lift him only to be shoved away. Brock explodes up only for both men to duck his clothesline attempt. As he turns around he walks right into a ‘Tossing The Stub’ aka Rock Bottom from Travis Tate! The crowd is stunned he just did that to the man nobody thought could be stopped. As he hits the mat he then starts to stir and get up as Darren Erns runs at him and hits ‘The Knockout’ a Shining Wizard that ends his night. With Brock down and out both men then lift him up and holding his tights they both hoist him over the top and to the outside. Brock Charnel is Eliminated! We are down to two and quickly Tate tries to pull a fast one on Erns by attempting to toss him over only for Darren Erns to land on the other side of the ring apron. Tate takes a swing at him to take him out but Erns grabs his arm and pulls him over using all of his strength. Tate falls over him as CDE manages to lay down on the side of the ring as Tate falls to the ground on the outside. Travis Tate is Eliminated! CDE with the win. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “There’s the man that will represent us, though I’d have loved for Brock to have done so. Could have been Chicken after all.” RILEY STEVENS: “These fans here seem to think otherwise they love this guy. However I’ve got to admit that a lot of these guys impressed me for sure.” BLAKE BECKETT: “But that man in the ring is the one who’ll be representing us, and I couldn’t be happier.” The fans cheer as Christopher Darren Erns hops up holding up his arms in victory as he heads to the top rope and slams his chest pumped up. He looks out at Travis Tate who nearly pulled a fast one on him and it seems there is no bad blood between them as he hops down now knowing he’ll be one of the men representing GIW at ‘Unscripted’. The camera cuts from Blake with a smile about the outcome then back to CDE celebrating as the segment ends. WINNER: CHRISTOPHER DARREN ERNS = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = After the match is over, the ION TV cameras switch over to the backstage area were Alexii Ravynova is warming up. She is stretching and throwing punches to the air when she is interrupted by Kacie Church who is wearing her title belt around her waist. KACIE CHURCH: “Well, hello Alexii… it looks like you are working hard to prepare for this match. I suppose you really want my title?” Alexii stops stretching and drinks some water from her water bottle. She begins to speak between gasps for air. ALEXII RAVYNOVA: “Is that a joke? I believe I have made it clear that I could care less about that piece of grimy scrap metal around your waist. All I want is to beat you.” Kacie laughs and begins to polish her title belt. KACIE CHURCH: “Well, Alexii, beating you again would be my pleasure. If it means anything at all though, I want you to beat Pandora tonight. I’d rather face you than that… thing. But, the sad part is, I think you are incapable of beating anyone.” Alexii gets into Kacie’s face. ALEXII RAVYNOVA: “Well, if it means anything to you, Kacie, I hope you retain your title tonight, so when I win my match tonight… I’ll have the opportunity to demolish you at Unscripted.” Alexii leaves the scene. Before the camera fades off, it zooms in on Kacie Church polishing her title with an evil smirk on her face. ~ As we return from commercial, we see Aspen Chaud standing outside Beckett’s office. The knocks on the door, but there is no response. The knocks louder, but there is still no response. She seems frustrated and leans against the wall. Eric Sparrow is seen entering the scene. ERIC SPARROW: “May I be of assistance to you, Miss Chaud?” Sparrow gets closer to Aspen, but is only shoved away. ASPEN CHAUD: “Get away from me… creep.” Sparrow chuckles and stares at Aspen while he speaks. ERIC SPARROW: “Oh, sassy. I like them sassy. So, what is a lady like you doing in a place like this?” Aspen backs away a bit more, with wide eyes she begins to speak. ASPEN CHAUD: “I need to talk to Blake.” Eric closes in on Aspen a bit more, he licks his lips. ERIC SPARROW: “Oh really? I never would have guess that. You wouldn’t be trying to find out what is inside that Golden Briefcase, would you Miss Chaud?” Aspen looks away. ASPEN CHAUD: “Yeah… yea it is. That’s exactly why I’m here, Eric. I just, you know, like to know what I am wrestling for… I figured he might tell me.” Eric laughs. ERIC SPARROW: “Oh! You see Aspen, that’s why I’m here too! Great minds think alike… lets see what else we have in common. How about you and me after the show tonight… ? My room number is 108.” A voice can be heard from outside the camera view, it is recognizable as Wake Walker’s only when he enters the scene. WAKE WALKER: “Would you leave her alone, Eric?” Walker gets up in Eric’s face, pushing him back. Eric chuckles to himself and exit’s the camera’s view. The scene ends with Walker staring at Sparrow, and Aspen looking on. The camera switches back to ringside where the second match is ready to begin. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = We come back from the break with Pandora standing in the middle of the ring. She paces back and forth as she waits for her opponent. JAMAL JACKSON: "Ladies and Gentlemen, this match is scheduled for one fall, and the winner will become the number one contender for the GIW Womens Championship! In the ring beside me, from Quebec, Canada, weighing in tonight at one hundred and fifteen pounds... Pandora!!!" Pandora raises her hands high in the air and nods her head when she hears her name. JAMAL JACKSON: And her opponent, from Marieville-Quebec, Canada, Her is Alexii Ravynova!!! After the infamous loud drum beats from Amatory's "Butterfly Effect" The fans in the arena start to share their feelings towards Ms. Ravynova. After a few seconds, Alexii Ravynova walks out from behind the curtain as a mixture of red and black lights sling around the stage. Alexii stops before the ramp starts, and takes a deep breath, and looks around the arena while "Butterfly" By "Amatory" blares on the public announcement system. "Ding, Ding, Ding!" RILEY STEVENS: “Alexxi Ravynova has a lot to prove with this match.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “So does Pandora! With a losing streak, I think she might have to do something drastic to stay as a credible opponent!.” Pandora and Alexii start in the ring, they stare at each other before Pandora offers up handshake. Alexii looks at her before telling her “Earn it”. They move in and get into a lock up, Pandora goes into a hammer lock. Alexii reveres it into a side arm bar, Pandora rolls threw for a side wrist lock, Alexii rolls threw that one and pulls her opponent in for a side headlock in the center of the ring. Pandora tries to force it open but she doesn’t have the strength. Then she resorts to using an Irish whip to send her across the ring. As Alexii comes back at her, Pandora jumps to hit a dropkick to the jaw. She goes for the cover, 1... 2... Only a two count. Pandora leaves her foe on the ground and goes to the top rope. While going up Alexii hits her from behind making her stumble and fall down into a tree of woe. Alexii grabs the head of Pandora and grabs it from behind with one arm to swing his second one around for an extreme twist of fate from the tree of woe! Pandora hits the mat hard and holds her neck in pain. Alexii pulls her toward the center of the ring to hook the leg for a pin. 1... 2... but gets as far as a two count before the shoulder is up. Alexii decides she wants to end this early and lifts Pandora to her feet to attempt a 'Ignorance. But, Pandora slides down from behind and uses the ropes to get speed into a strong big boot but Alexii side steps it and gets behind her to hit home a bridging dragon suplex! 1…2…. Kickout! Pandora barely get her shoulders up. Alexii, looking a little upset at that leaves the ring and goes up top to scout out a high impact move. As Pandora pulls herself to his feet the Alexii dives for a missle dropkick!! Pandora ducks down and her opponent crashes and burns in the hard fall. Pandora leaves the ring herself and waste no time to dive and plant the perfect leg drop on the prone Alexii Ravynova. She hooks the leg and counts the pin 1…2… Alexii grabs the arms of Pandora and pulls her over for a crucifix pin! 1…2… Pandora rolls threw, with incredible strength, keeping a hold of Alexii and takes her to her feet in a fireman carry, looking to end this as she throws Alexii forward for the Sit-Down Facecrusher she's made so famous! Alexii hits the mat with her head bouncing back hitting canvas hard! Pandora calls this one done as she hooks the leg. 1......2............! The refs hand came down but he calls it a two as Alexii managed to get her foot on the bottom rope. Pandora isn’t too thrilled with this match as Alexii manages to take everything she has ever had. Pandora pulls Alexii Ravynova to her feet and sets her up for another Sitdown Facecrusher! BUT, Alexii shows her how its done as she slides around her to the back and lifts Pandora up in the Ignorance. As Alexii tries to showboat, Pandora SOMEHOW turns it around into a Sitdown Facecrusher but Alexxi manages to counter half way through and rolls up Pandora for the pin! 1...2...3! RILEY STEVENS: “What a great try by Pandora!.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “She has definitely proved that she's no laughing matter. Ravynova, however...stellar job. .” WINNER: ALEXII RAVYNOVA = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = After the match is over, the camera moves to the backstage area where Sarah Sharp is standing by with Austin Acquin. SARAH SHARP: “Ladies and gentlemen… my guest at this time, Austin Acquin!” Austin Acquin comes into the cameras view, the crowd goes wild. AUSTIN ACQUIN: “Hello, Sarah. Nice to be here.” Sarah seems annoyed, but begins to speak. SARAH SHARP: “Austin, lets be honest… any success you have ever had in any company was simply because your brother helped you out. Now, you have a chance to earn a title shot, and your brother wont be here to help you out… do you think you can be successful.” Austin ponders his answer, before speaking. AUSTIN ACQUIN: “That’s a fair statement, but I will prove it wrong tonight. I am going to prove to you, to Ryan Daniels, to Beckett, and to Joshua that I am NOT a fluke. This is the beginning of a new Austin Acquin. This is the beginning of a better, more focused Austin Acquin. By the next Revolt, I will be the GIW Legacy Champion.” We fade to commercial. ~ We now head to the backstage area where Amanda Rowland is standing by with Travis Tate already in conversation as we come in and Amanda begins to suddenly offer. AMANDA ROWLAND: “So Travis, Blake has told me to go ahead and tell you that he's decided to make you the third member of Team GIW at 'Unscripted'. Congratulations.” Travis nods his head in approval of the news as Amanda holds out a hand for a shake but he instead leans in and hugs her. TRAVIS TATE: "He won't regret this." Tate's hands sort of rub her back before he releases her and walks away as she looks at him with a little grossed out look before shaking her head ignoring it as she then stops Pandora who is none to happy as she is walking by. AMANDA ROWLAND: “Wait Pandora one second. I've also got some news from Blake for you as well, you see he loves your heart and thinks you've been this close to winning each and every week. And he's decided to give you a opportunity to overcome the odds in a Triple Threat for the Women's title against Alexii Ravynova and the winner of the next match, congrats.” Amanda again off her a hand shake as Pandora shakes it eagerly realizing she's been given a second chance. PANDORA: "Thank you both so much, I'll make sure that I prove what I can do against those two, thank you again." Pandora walks away with considerably more pep in her step as Amanda smiles loving to hand out the good news. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = It’s now time for our only title match tonight and after Alexii Ravynova’s contender win earlier we now know that the winner of this match will face her at ‘Unscripted’. Already on her way to the ring is Model C-22 Combat Droid the fans are giving her a mixed reaction as she enters the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Here’s your favorite part of the show Oliver.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “This should be great…” Model C-22 steps into the ring and takes her place as soon "Impossible" by Anberlin and the fans immediately begin to boo. Out from the back comes Kacie Church with her GIW Women’s Championship. She heads towards the ring with a lot of swagger after all she has looked pretty dominant around here. She steps up into the ring unstrapping her belt and handing it to the referee as he shows it off before handing it out and calling for the bell. DING DING DING! The two girls start to circle each other before Kacie jumps at Model C-22 and does a quick Hip Toss. However after hitting the move Kacie holds her back realizing that Model C-22 weighs a lot more then one would think. She shakes it off and waits for Model C-22 to get up, which she does perhaps trying to recalibrate to the attack while doing so. Church waits and when she is up she goes for a Dropkick only for her legs to be easily slapped away. The fans seem to realize that there perhaps is a chance for an upset here as Model C-22 now waits for Church to get up. Church gets up and now angered by what happened gets up into the face of her opponent talking down to the robot. The robot doesn’t take kindly to the words as she delivers a ‘Tech Slap’ an extremely hard slap that knocks Kacie off her feet. The fans are a little wowed by Model C-22’s early offense. However Church is able to catch the slow to react Model C-22 off guard jumping up and tackling her to the ground. She begins to deliver left and rights before the referee pulls her off so they can both get back to a vertical base. However as soon as Model C-22 attempts to do as Church runs across the ring and hits a Dropkick sending her flying out of the ring. The referee begins to count her out as the crowd turns to the ramp where Alexii Ravynova has begun to walk down towards the ring. 1…2…3…Church turns around looking over at her as she walks around the ring and pulls up a chair on the outside of the ring, Kacie has her eyes right on her….4…5…6…7…Model C-22 however has gotten up and just gets in at the count of eight to break up the potential count out title win. RILEY STEVENS: “Alexii Ravynova has no business out here!.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “She is scouting out her potential opponents no harm there.” Kacie finally takes her eyes off of Alexii and then goes back on the attack grabbing Model C-22 and whipping her off the ropes but on the rebound Model C-22 connects with a big Standing Hurricarana that catches everyone in the arena off guard. She quickly goes for the cover as Alexii Ravynova can be seen staring intently into the ring at the two in the ring. Back inside the ring the referee makes the count 1…Kick Out at Only One! The fans are actually a bit more on the robot’s side at this point as both competitors get to there feet. However Kacie quickly gets around her and locks in a Rear Naked Choke. She stares out at Alexii the whole time before dragging Model C-22 to the ground. However as programmed she gets her arm out to the rope and the referee counts it 1…2…3…4…Kacie releases the hold as the referee drags her off. She smirks out at Alexii who continues to show little emotion as she looks into the ring. Church however calls for the match to be over as she waits for Model C-22 to get up. The Combat Droid does this and turns around right into an ‘Ice Breaker’ attempt from Church only for the Combat Droid to push her away. Kacie bounces off the ropes however Model C-22 malfunctions for a split second making her second ‘Ice Breaker’ attempt a success as she connects with it successfully. She gets on top for the pin attempt 1…2…3! Still Champion. RILEY STEVENS: “We were so focused on Alexii and Kacie we forgot about the late addition to the match Pandora. And though she’s yet to win around here these fans have gravitated to her underdog stature to these two seemingly giants. And now it seems that she’s got the momentum, who will be the winner come ‘Unscripted’ when these three ladies meet.” The crowd continues to cheer as Pandora now stands over both girls as she stares down at the Women’s title that Kacie lost after the shot as we head to the back again. "Bullseye" by Aly and AJ causes the entire crowd to uproar into possibly one of the greatest cheers of all time so far. Aspen Chaud comes out and seems focused, forgetting to slap the hand of some fans. She enters the ring. All of the men look at her, they know how dangerous she is. Her 5’1 physique is nothing to laugh about. “Dazed And Confused” begins to play. The crowd almost tries to replicate the previous ovation but falls just short. Still, Wake Walker receives a massive ovation. His normal long entrance is cut and he quickly walks to the ring, eyeing everyone especially Aspen. All competitors separate themselves with the exception of Eric Sparrow who is in the middle. The bell sounds. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “So who‘s your pick to win this match, Riley? Is it Kennedy, the fighting soldier? Maybe it‘s the Tony Miranda…I bet Celina would love to know that her hubby won that case or perhaps it‘s Eric Sparrow, the devilish wrestler. Finally, it could be the vibrant but dangerous Aspen Chaud?.” RILEY STEVENS: “I‘m going to have to go with none of the above, Wake Walker is winning this. It‘s clear as day.” Since Eric Sparrow is in the middle, he is immediately the target of everybody. Everyone beats him up and throws him to the outside. Immediately afterwards, Jake Kennedy tries something on Aspen but Aspen retaliates with a punch. Tony Miranda happens to be next to Aspen and is also hit. Aspen starts attacking both before both of them react and kick her on the gut and do a double suplex and send her to the outside. Jake Kennedy and Tony Miranda both look at each other in anger but in respect as well. Wake Walker runs at both of them but is thrown to the outside. However, Wake Walker lands on his feet on the outside and tries to drag Tony Miranda to the outside too. However, Jake Kennedy bounces off the ropes and hit’s a baseball slide on Wake Walker, sending him, Eric and Aspen crashing into a ladder that’s laying on the floor in the back of them. Jake Kennedy and Tony Miranda are left in the ring. They both stare at each other before shaking hands. It looks like they’ve reached consensus. Tony Miranda exit’s the ring but warily looks back to see if Kennedy is going to try anything. Tony Miranda grabs Eric Sparrow and starts working on him. Jake Kennedy also does the same. Once they’re through with Eric Sparrow, Kennedy and Miranda realize that they’ve forgotten about Aspen and Walker both of which deliver a clothesline to both men. Aspen looks at Walker and grabs him by the neck, attempting some sort of move but Walker just pushes her away to the floor and picks up Kennedy, entering him in the ring. Meanwhile, Tony Miranda tries to help but is distracted by Aspen on the outside. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Looks like Kennedy and Miranda are teaming up.” RILEY STEVENS: “Sure looks like it. Maybe they‘re just being competitive with one another? They don‘t hate each other per say but they‘re rather competitive.” Eric Sparrow grabs two ladders and puts them in the ring. Tony Miranda gets the best of Aspen by throwing her onto the steel steps and then enters the ring. He clubs Walker on the head and saves Kennedy from a possible high impact move. Eric Sparrow enters the ring and goes to the turnbuckle. Jake Kennedy looks at him and Eric Sparrow lands Air Sparrow! Quite the amazing move! Jake Kennedy is laid out on the floor. Tony Miranda tries his finisher the running leg lariat but Sparrow dodges it and goes to the top rope quickly again! Air Sparrow! Tony Miranda is also laid out on the floor! Wake Walker goes for a bit boot, Sparrow dodges it and hit’s a big boot of his own! Eric Sparrow is in control of this match! Aspen Chaud enters and starts an attack on Eric but Eric again, gets the advantage and drop kicks Aspen out of the ring. Eric Sparrow looks around and the crowd begins to cheer and boo at the same time. Eric Sparrow sets up both ladders and begins to climb them. He reaches for the golden case but Jake Kennedy quickly climbs the other ladder and hit’s a rolling kick! Both men fall to the floor but Jake Kennedy gets up and sluggishly begins to climb the ladder. As Kennedy is on the top, Tony Miranda quickly climbs all the way to the top and both men stare at each other, maybe a little too long. Aspen and Wake both grab on to the legs of Kennedy and Miranda but both men kick off the former couple. Kennedy and Miranda stare again. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “This is getting exciting! Look at those two, working together - but now… they have to fight each other!.” RILEY STEVENS: “This will prove to be interesting.” They both reach for the case but realize that they both can’t win. They begin to attack each other. Suddenly a huge CLASHING sound is heard. Wake Walker is seen with a steel chair and Aspen Chaud is laid out on the ground. Eric Sparrow tries an Air Sparrow from the top rope to the outside but Wake Walker hits him in mid air, nearly endangering the life of Sparrow. Wake Walker enters the ring. Miranda and Kennedy both realize that unless they stop Walker, they’re doomed. Jake Kennedy sacrifices himself and does a missile drop kick from the top of the ladder! Wake Walker dodges it and begins to hit Kennedy with the chair several times. Miranda looks at the case and then at Kennedy and decides to jump off and hit a flying cross body on Wake Walker! However, Wake Walker somehow rolls it through and has Miranda on his shoulders. Wake Walker uses a fireman’s carry to throw out Miranda to the outside. Wake Walker begins to walk towards the ladders to climb them but Kennedy grabs on to his leg. Walker tries to shake him off but it’s no use. Wake Walker picks up Kennedy, kicks him in the gut and hits The Temple Of Love. Jake Kennedy is done for. Wake Walker begins to climb the ladders and grabs the golden case! He begins to detach it when a bloodied Eric Sparrow jumps from the turnbuckle to the ladders and hit’s a leaping kick which hits Wake Walker square across the face. Wake Walker is now out cold but at the same time, he has a big grip over the case! Both ladders also fall and Wake Walker is suspended in the air, holding onto the case and whilst knocked out. All the other superstars enter the ring and have no idea what to do. Eric Sparrow tries to set up a ladder again but Kennedy and Miranda stop him by punching him. Aspen tries something but she’s swatted away like a fly by the rest. Eventually, a screeching sound can be heard. The hook where the case is on, BREAKS! Wake Walker falls the 20 feet and onto the floor, with case in arms. With what little energy is left, he slowly unhooks it and holds it up in the air. Everyone looks shocked at how Wake Walker won. RILEY STEVENS: “Now that‘s amazing! Wow…Oliver, look at you…HAH! Your jaw is basically touching the floor right now. Wow, what a night. Wake Walker is now not only the top guy in GIW as well as the GI champion but at the same time, he has that case! With competitors like these, it must have some great prize!” WINNER: WAKE WALKER = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = Slowly, Wake Walker is helped up by referees. Aspen Chaud leaves, disappointed but still with high spirits. The others have no choice either. They leave. Kennedy and Miranda appear to get somewhat along now. Eric Sparrow, is stretcher out due to him fainting on his feet from pain. Wake Walker is helped to the back but refuses medical attention. He gets back in the ring and is handed a microphone. WAKE WALKER: "Let it be known that Wake Walker is officially the strongest and most powerful entity in Galveston Island Wrestling. I am unstoppable and nobody can defeat me." Aspen Chaud, Kennedy and Miranda all look at Walker, defeated - they know that Walker deserves this. Eric Sparrow gets off his stretcher and listens to walker. WAKE WALKER: "I am now not only Galveston Island Champion but the holder of the Golden Case. I am untouchable … but, there’s something missing. For the past few weeks, no…MONTHS…I have been talking about a revolution that was going to undergo as soon as I’d be champion. Now with this case…whatever it holds, I have this confidence… a confidence that has inspired me to do what I’ve needed to do for quite some time. After I defeat Eric Sparrow and open this case at Unscripted, I’ll be a completed man. It’s been three weeks since I became champion and nothing has happened. Aspen… I need your help for this." Aspen Chaud looks around, confused and enters the ring. He asks Walker what he wants. Wake Walker begins to smirk and hits Aspen right across the head with the case. The crowd begins to boo. From the back, out comes Sinclair and Chris Armstrong. Sparrow, Miranda and Kennedy all watch in horror as the unthinkable is happening right now. It looks like Wake Walker has jumped ship. Sinclair has a microphone in hand. WAKE WALKER: "Quiet… for months now…I’ve had to make an impact…so great that nobody could say that Walker is unable to do so. And now, I’ve made it. With Aspen on the floor, this is a symbol… this is a symbol of…" DAVID SINCLAIR: "Unity! Wake Walker, I’m glad to introduce you into the family that is Evolve! You’ll make a great addition." WAKE WALKER: "Of course, Sinclair. Of course. I would make a great addition, seeing as how all your superstars are failures and your champion, the biggest disappointment of all." DAVID SINCLAIR: (Tries to understand Walker) You may be right about that but you’re still with us! You’ll be the top guy! WAKE WALKER: "Who ever said anything about joining you?" Wake Walker hits Chris Armstrong with the case and then briefly stares at Sinclair. Wake Walker hits him with the case too and then hit’s the Temple Of Love on top of the case. The crowd explodes in cheers but some stay silent, not knowing what to make of Walker. Miranda, Kennedy and Sparrow look relieved to know that Walker is still with Revolt but still dislike what he did to Aspen. The show closes with Wake Walker, standing dominantly over all three men and women - holding his title up high.
