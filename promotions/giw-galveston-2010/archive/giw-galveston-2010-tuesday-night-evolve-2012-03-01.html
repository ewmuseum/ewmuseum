GIW — Card

Tuesday Night Evolve, 1 March 2012

1 March 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: EVOLVE LIVE! From the Lakefront Arena in New Orleans, LA – March 1, 2012 QUOTE Our opening contest kicks off with all five participants in the ring. The bell sounds and the opening battle royal kicks off with Brody Harper, Frost, Big J, Carmen Rivera, and Mya Denton circling each other. DING DING DING! Big J finally makes a move and attacks Frost with right hands. The two exchange blows in the center of the ring as Mya and Brody tie up in the corner. Carmen simply backs into a corner and watches the mayhem-taking place in the ring. Frost connects with a suplex on Big J and Mya and Brody Harper are still struggling in the corner, doing their best to throw each other out. Big J is back up and this time ducks a clothesline from Frost and delivers a nice looking Samoan Drop. Brody gets the upper hand and pushes Mya away and the much smaller competitor tumbles into the ropes but remains in the ring. Cue Carmen Rivera as she finally makes a move, easily tossing Brody Harper over the top rope when he wasn’t paying attention. BRODY HARPER IS ELIMINATED Carmen keeps up the attack, kicking Mya who is down on the mat over and over again. Frost and Big J are still going at it and this time Big J is able to pick Frost up and deliver a chokeslam. J turns his attention to Carmen. Big J rushes and attempts a clothesline but Carmen ducks, very matrix like, and connects with the Diablo Drop, a devilock DDT maneuver. Frost is back up and out of nowhere sends Mya Denton over the tope with a clothesline. MYA DENTON IS ELIMINATED We’re down to three, Carmen Rivera, Frost, and Big J who is still down. Frost and Carmen exchange rights and it looks like Frost has the upper hand at the moment. Frost suddenly turns his back on Carmen but for a reason as he attempts a beautiful pele kick. Carmen ducks and the kick connects with the skull of Big J. Carmen takes notice of the stunned state of Big J and easily throws him over the top. We’re down to two. BIG J IS ELIMINATED Frost and Carmen waste no time. Frost rocks Carmen’s jaw with a beautiful European Uppercut. Carmen tumbles into the ropes and Frost lifts Rivera’s leg and uses his strength to send her over but Carmen catches herself using the top rope and is still safe. Frost has his back turned thinking he’s the victor and is already celebrating. Carmen springs off the ropes and just as Frost turns around, she connects with California Sunset. Frost crashes into the ropes and just for good measure; Carmen hurries over and dumps Frost to the floor. JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…Carmen Rivera!” The crowd gives a mixed reaction as the referee raises up her arm in victory with Frost getting up on the outside upset with himself. RILEY STEVENS: “Impressive outing her by Carmen Rivera to get this win, onwards and upwards she shall go.” Rivera heads to the top rope to celebrate her first win here in GIW as the crowd continues to give a mixed back. WINNER: CARMEN RIVERA Heading to the back after the last match here on Evolve and we see Big J and Mya Denton in the backstage area both seeming upset with their loss as Jacob O’Connelly the man running this Evolve show heads over to them. JACOB O’CONNELLY: “Real close, you two. I am the Evolve Commissioner and next week I want the two of you in a match, so that you can make a name for yourself.” Jacob pats them both on the shoulder as Mya looks up at the menacing Big J not seeming to be too scared as we head to a commercial break taking a moment to hype up some of the matches on Revolt tonight. QUOTE First to the stage was Paul Defias to the tune of "Scream If You're Crazy" By D.R.U.G.S. He was jeered in his journey to the ring. Upon entry, his music that played into the arena was cut off by his opponent’s as he posed into the ring too more boos. RILEY STEVENS: “Here comes Paul Defias, hoping to start his rise here in GIW.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “He’s going to soon.” As Skylar Grey played, Hayden approached the stage. The crowd seemed uncertain, but took her side against Paul’s considering Defias was associated with other heels. Hayden stepped down to the ring and slid inside. She stepped up to the center toe to toe with Defias in a stare down before they both backed away. RILEY STEVENS: “Hayden Carter is new but she seems to have some real guts having called out many of the top stars.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Hasn’t made her very liked in the ” As Johnny Steel was announced, nothing happened. His music played, but the curtains remained motionless. A minute, then two, even three passed and still there was no signs of Steel. It was ruled that the match must go on and the bell rang for Hayden Carter and Paul Defias. DING DING DING! Paul pulled Hayden around, diverting her focus from the absence of Steel to his elbow to the chin and knee to the face. She staggered back, but retaliated with an elbow of her own and a backhand chop across the chest. She may have been a women, but she was self-proclaimed tough and she would prove that against any man. Slowly, Hayden began to push Paul back toward the ropes with each knife-edge chop she drove across his reddening chest. At the ropes, she sent him across with an Irish whip and then ran to meet him at the center of the ring. Paul dodged her attempt at a DDT and came back at her after another touch of the ropes. Hayden followed up with a quick suplex that expelled Paul’s growing momentum. She swung her body over for a pin, but Paul pushed her off before the referee could get to his knees. RILEY STEVENS: “These two have had some serious trash talk backstage.” Instead of fall into a discouraged state, Hayden simply tumbled down toward Paul’s feet and tugged him into an ankle lock. She wrapped his body onto his stomach and pulled his leg up and locked the maneuver in tight. He kicked at her with the other foot, barely nicking her fingers in the process, but she kept it locked and would stab his kicking leg with a few stomps to settle it back down to the canvas. She held him in this dominant form whilst the referee bothered him about whether he would like to quit, or not. RILEY STEVENS: “Both stars are putting on a Revolt like performance.” Paul pushed away at the referee and eventually tugged his body with a military crawl toward the ropes. Hayden would try to pull back, but Paul was the stronger of the two. She was forced to let go at that point, but another stomp finished the insult on the ankle she had begun to work on. She would continue to target. Paul crawled underneath the bottom rope and then used it to tug himself up onto his feet. The right ankle was tender, but he could still move on it. It wouldn’t be an issue if he kept it safe. Hayden moved for an attack, but ended up with Paul’s right foot in her grasp. She went to elbow it just as he swung over with an enziguri kick to the side of her rosy cheeks. She smacked into the canvas at the same time he did and cradled her face in her palm. She looked more angered by the kick than hurt, but Paul followed up with an ankle lock of his own upon his opponent. The mind games were on as he pulled her to the center of the ring. The hold was tight, but he was mostly toying with her. It was vengeance. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Finish her Defias.” She tried to turn over, or kick. She knew she wasn’t strong enough to crawl to the ropes with her weight and his, let alone the force of him pulling back on her. Instead, Hayden continued to contort her body in any which way to break the hold, but Paul would just smugly stomp at her as she did to him and put her flat on her chest to keep the hold locked in tight. The referee was now on her case about quitting the match, but she refused to surrender. RILEY STEVENS: “This young woman is brave that’s for sure.” Paul dropped her foot down into the canvas and pulled her from it. She retaliated with a knife-edge chop and pushed backward to lift up into a dropkick. Both were up quickly, though their tender ankles held them back equally. Hayden was the one to get the first hit and threw Paul Defias into an Irish whip. She rushed him and pushed him against the ropes, then pulled back and sent him into a roll up pin. It seemed like she would get the victory, but just as the referee was bringing his hand down for three, Paul pulled out of the ball they had formed and created one of his own in the form of The Ender, his gogoplata variation. She struggled and squirmed like a fish in the hold, but he locked it tight and soon she fell limp. The referee was forced to rule the match a victory in favor of Paul Defias when Hayden could no longer respond to him. JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…Paul Defias!” It took Paul a moment before he would let go of her, but he soon did and rose up in victory. The victor of the alleged triple-threat match that had transformed into a singles bout. RILEY STEVENS: “Big win by Defias here tonight, Hayden refused to tap out and paid for it.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Way to go Defias.” The crowd gives Hayden some applause as Defias raises his arms in victory rebounding from his loss at ‘Retaliation’. WINNER: PAUL DEFIAS Again we head to the back with Revolt stars just starting to show up as we see Alexandra Rossi arguing with a staff member backstage. ALEXANDRA ROSSI: “I know I can’t believe it, I am not booked again. It’s like they LOVE Bailey or something but forget about his better half.” The staff member doesn’t seem too excited to be listening to her but knows better then to argue it as Jacob O’Connelly walks over to her. JACOB O’CONNELLY: “Listen, I am the Evolve Commissioner and I would like to offer you a spot on next week’s card here on Evolve one on one with Frost.” Rossi seems to be thinking it over, before shrugging realizing that she didn’t know if she had any plans for Revolt. ALEXANDRA ROSSI: “Fine but if I win I better get a big win bonus.” Rossi smirks and walks off as O’Connelly watches her go with a smile on his face, pulling all the strings here tonight. QUOTE After a short break we return to the ring with CJ Hayes already prepping to get ready for the match when his music ends and "Born With Nothing, Die With Everything" by Papa Roach hits and the fans begin to boo Matt Matlock’s entrance to the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Matt has lost three straight matches here in GIW but not a single one has been by much, he’s impressed a lot of people.” Matlock walks down towards the ring looking at the fans and smirking at them as he heads up the steps and into the ring across from Hayes, not seeming too worried about this one as the bell rings. DING DING DING! The bell sounds and the Evolve main event is underway. Matlock, who is zero and three in matches in Galveston Island Wrestling, looks ready to finally break the losing streak and prove that he belongs in GIW. Hayes taunts Matlock to “bring it” and bring it he does. Matt connects with a running DDT that spikes CJ’s head into the matt and it looks like CJ is already out. Matt shakes his head and notions that this match isn’t over just yet. He picks him up and continues the assault with a Full Nelson slam that shakes the ring. Matlock arrogantly picks CJ up by the hair and tosses him into the corner. RILEY STEVENS: “I had a feeling this was going to get ugly quickly.” Matt backs up a bit and then uses his boot and smashes it right across the face of Hayes. It’s clearly visible that Matlock broke his nose in the process as blood gushes from the face of CJ. Matlock finally signals for the end. Matt Matlock connects with The Animal’s Instinct finishing move, covers, 1…2…3! And this one is over. JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…Matt Matlock!” Matlock gets up to his feet throwing up his arms in victory finally getting a win but doing so in quick and impressive fashion. RILEY STEVENS: “Don’t let this mans losses fool you, he is a legit talent.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “That’s for sure.” With that Evolve slowly starts to fade with Matlock ending it with an exclamation point, as we take a break now just ten minutes away from live Revolt! WINNER: MATT MATLOCK
