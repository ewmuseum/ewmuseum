Pre-show, 30 April 2014

GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: AGGRESSIVE EXPANSION: PRE SHOW Live from Porretto Beach – April 30th, 2014 QUOTE The time has come for the Open Battle Royal where we have three different stars waiting in the ring as Artemis Kaiser, Kiara Lewis, and Wake Walker, yes Wake Walker stands in the ring here for the Open Battle Royal. These fans have a good time heckling him and jeering him for it as he stands in the ring, glaring out at them as Kiara and Artemis prepare for their huge debuts getting a rare opportunity to beat a big name like Wake Walker in the Open Battle Royal. RILEY STEVENS: “Surprise, surprise here for the Open Battle Royal is Wake Walker stepping in their with two debuting stars.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “This is a sham…” Walker doesn’t looked pleased to be there as Kiara and Artemis get ready then the referee calls for the bell. DING DING DING! This match is underway now as the fans are cheering on both Artemis and Kiara now to take out Wake Walker as both girls turn their attention to Wake who stands against the ropes looking annoyed even being there as they look at one another then back at Wake knowing that he’s talented but also knowing what it would do for their careers if they were to take him out day one. They are about to attack him when he jumps over the top rope, then out of the ring just as both were about to push him off as his feet hit eliminating himself. Wake Walker is Eliminated! Walker heads to the back getting booed like hell from the crowd as Artemis and Kiara watch him go shaking their heads as we head back to Wake who yells out “There I did your Open Battle Royal!” angrily at anyone that will hear as he throws his arms out shaking his head as he walks to the back as in the ring Artemis and Kiara now turn their attention to one another knowing they have to eliminate each other now as they jump into a lock up the two sharing much different frames. Artemis much stockier and shorter, while Kiara is much taller and skinny the two now start to move each other back and forth about the same in the weight department but Kaiser uses her lower center of gravity to push Kiara back to the ropes before launching her off. On the rebound Lewis lunges for a clothesline but Artemis easily ducks under it, then grabs Kiara from behind her rolling her up almost on instinct but when she realizes there is no pins she pulls her up into a German Suplex getting some love from the crowd. As she hoists her back up still with a grip around her waist as she turns her around and whips her over into the corner now, charging her only for Kiara to move at the last second. RILEY STEVENS: “Both these girls know what a big opportunity they have here tonight.” Kaiser bounces off the corner hard as Kiara now takes the chance to jump off the ropes and into a rather agile Spinning DDT using the ropes to launch herself into Artemis. This brings a big pop from an impressed crowd as both girls get to their feet now as Kiara charges her and lands a big Hurricarana sending her across the ring now as Lewis gets up with a lot of energy and charges her once more but Artemis lands a ‘Falcon Punch’a Superman Punch out of no where clocking her and sending her stumbling backwards to the ropes where she charges her with a big clothesline that sends both girls over the ropes but to the fans amazement both of them grab the ropes and are able to awkwardly get their feet down onto the ring apron but still in a dangerous spot now as these fans are up on the edge of their seats, knowing one may fall as the two girls get their balance then start to exchange back and forth on the ring apron. Kiara uses her long legs to kick the hand that Artemis is using to hold the ropes but Kaiser catches her leg just enough to make her lose her balance a bit as finally the two come closer and nail each other with big shots, each losing their balance as the fall off and hit the mat but Kiara’s legs are much longer and she hits first. REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner…Artemis Kaiser!” The fans wait for just a moment as the referee confirms it by raising the hand of Artemis who struck the ground just split seconds later as Kiara gets up shaking her head realizing she was so close as Artemis happily gets her hand raised. RILEY STEVENS: “Real close here but both showed they got some talent, will be interesting to see how they do next week in their big debuts.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Walker should have just destroyed them...” Kiara gets up and as Artemis is about to head to the back, she extends a hand for a shake and Kaiser looks to want to just ignore it and head to the back, but she seems to succumb to her own joy as she shakes her head then heads to the back to celebrate as we take a break before the big Supershow. WINNER: ARTEMIS KAISER
