GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: MONDAY NIGHT REVOLT! Live! From RA Apffel Park on Galveston Island June 25th, 2012 BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! The show begins like many others, panning around the arena. A logo pops up, announcing that we are live on Ion Television from RA Apffel Park, and the crowd of three hundred screaming GIW fans are waving their signs high and proud. We cut to the center of the ring where Matt Kluge is standing, dressed in his “American Gold” t-shirt and blue jeans, and with him are the three announced competitors in the Sole Survivor Match, “Amazing” Ace Baldwin, Alioth Starre, and Madison Seton. MATT KLUGE: “Welcome to Revolt once again everyone, I’m your interim chairman Matt Kluge, and these three here are the announced competitors in the Sole Survivor match.” “Se-ton” chants and “A-maz-ing” chants compete with each other from the fans, Alioth leans against the rope, disinterested in what is going on, an eyebrow raised at the chairman with whom he had so many wars. MATT KLUGE: “Now, these three will make murderer’s row for sure in this match, and I feel bad for the poor soul who is announced as the fourth and final competitor in this match. I can say that by the end of the night, I will have chosen that person - but the four way match has been canceled, as I have chosen to release all four of those wrestlers. They would have only brought the match down anyway.” Alioth nods and steps up beside Kluge, tapping him on the shoulder. Kluge tensed up at the touch, it was clear that the two didn’t like each other. Alioth slipped the mic out of Kluge’s hand. ALIOTH STARRE: “Mr. Kluge, I’m sure that with your extreme intelligence and hatred of things that waste time, that you have something important planned for us three tonight. However, I have a match with Seton and Lokie that I need to prepare for, so if you would hurry it up, that would be grand.” Kluge bit his tongue and stepped away from Alioth, but Ace was there with a microphone of his own. He stepped in front of Alioth as Seton slinked back into the corner, avoiding confrontation.” ACE BALDWIN: “Alioth, I’ve learned something about you over the past week or so, you really are just a disrespectful little prick. You would think you would show some respect to people who have beaten you in the past, but no. You never fail to impress me.” Alioth chuckles as the crowd was enjoying him being put in his place. Seton was holding her hand over her face, trying hard not to make it obvious she was laughing. ALIOTH STARRE: “Okay. Yes Kluge defeated me. And yes Ace, you defeated me as well. Congratulations. I never said I was an undefeatable force, that is just the moniker that you all gave me because I have been so dominant in my tenure here. Perhaps if you focused more on improving yourself, Ace and Kluge, your title reigns would have been as long as mine.” “Kiss My Lips” by Dev hits the PA system now, and the place erupts in boos as Alexis Terry, the number one contender for the Undisputed Championship, arrived at the ramp. ALEXIS TERRY: “Look at you all - bickering over which one of you will win Sole Survivor, the fact is, none of it will matter. Because as I look at that ring right now, and I see you three, I know that when I defeat Alex and I become champ, I could run through all of you challengers in my sleep. Alioth is one of the most overrated in history, Ace Baldwin is only famous because he dominated in a dead federation that operated out of barns, and Seton is about as charismatic as a white door. I am the definition of a champion. I have beauty, brains, and talent, and you all will be bowing at my feet.” Her music hits the PA again, we zoom in on all three competitors, Ace is laughing with a big grin on his face, Alioth shows no clear emotion, and Seton is rolling her eyes. We fade to commercial break. QUOTE Miller and Sunderland arrived first for their bout. The two opted for the far corner of the ring, opposite the aisle. That would be their team corner and it was decided that Sunderland would begin first for her team. Once that was settled, the second team had arrived. Consisting of Val and Shiloh, the two slid into the ring, Shiloh being the first to start in the bout. This meant a lock up between Sunderland and Shiloh had opened the tag match. Shiloh pushed away his opponent, but Sunderland locked up a second time and transitioned it into a wrist lock. OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Really? A wrist lock?" Shiloh countered with his own wrist lock, then a short arm whip into a big boot to the chest. Sunderland dropped to the canvas with a loud thud, but rolled back up soon after to escape a stomp from Shiloh. She had to be swift to avoid him and so she began a few fleet footed attacks. Jabs, kicks, and dropkick to seal the swiftness. Shiloh dropped his smaller female opponent with an armbar and then pushed her away. He didn’t want to fight her, but Miller. He yearned for a male opponent, even though Naviel was reluctant to fall victim to this display. She kicked his ribs hard and used a fleet footed wheel kick to finally drop Shiloh onto the canvas. Shiloh pushed off and went to tag in his partner, Val, but Naviel was quick to stop it. She raced to the ropes and launched herself at him with a springboard lionsault. Shiloh had caught her, but only enough that they both tumbled down to the flat surface of the canvas with a loud thud and kerfuffle. This only deterred Shiloh enough that he began to strike against his female opponent. He struck with a hard right and then pushed her off of him. He pulled his opponent up from the canvas and threw her straight toward her corner where she was forced into a tag by Miller. Miller bolted into the match with a springboard missile dropkick and the momentum war had begun! Reluctantly, however, Naviel returned to the rim of the ring to wait for her turn again, upset about the forced tag. She seemed bitter, but the deed was done. RILEY STEVENS: "WOW!" With the momentum of a fresh tag, Miller used a jumping spin kick, snapmare neckbreaker, and a few other swift and momentous attacks upon his opponent. He wanted victory fast and hard! Shiloh eventually caught up however, after a big boot to the throat, Miller was down and out for the count. Shiloh tore his opponent from the canvas and had begun to set him up for the sitout spinebuster, his ‘Dixie Driver’. He sent Miller to the ropes with a hard Irish whip, then awaited his return. Miller suddenly burst through his plans, leaping over Shiloh, racing to the ropes, and bouncing off with a backfilp kick. Shiloh was caught unaware. RILEY STEVENS: "This match is too close to call!" Miller, taking the momentum into his hands, grappled his opponent, tugging him close and jerking any resilience out of him then performed a swinging fisherman suplex. Val tried to enter the ring to break the inevitable pin, but the referee was already on his heels. With that distraction over the referee went for the count. Val still lunged into the ring, but by the count of two, it was almost too late. Val struck Miller hard in the back of the skull enough to knock the wind clear out of him. He rolled to the side and Val helped his partner move on top, thus sparking a second pin count! This time, the opposing side was victorious In his defeat, Miller rose up in shame and searched for his his partner, but Naviel was behind him waiting and ready to strike. Not necessarily behind him in a standing point of view, but more of an ariel dive bomb in the form of a shooting star press that crashed down upon Miller like a thousand atomic bombs. She pulled off and saw the carnage and betrayal she had just committed. She spat his name upon the canvas in bitterness and spoiled the moment for the victors, but a defeat nonetheless. The winning team retreated from the ring, smirking at the dismay they had left in the ring. WINNERS: THE OXFORD DROPOUTS We fade back into the show, and we see Naviel Sunderland standing with Zachary Pagonis in the GIW interview lounge. Pagonis begins to speak as Sunderland holds her neck in pain. ZACHARY PAGONIS: “Hello ladies and gentlemen and welcome back to Revolt, with me now is my guest for tonight, Naviel Sunderland, who just shocked the world with a brutal attack on Adam Miller, her partner.” Sunderland grabs the microphone from Pagonis then pushes him away, out of the camera shot. She assumes the center - the camera entirely on her now. NAVIEL SUNDERLAND: “NO MORE! I’m not letting anyone hold me back anymore. Adam Miller cost me that match, I could have won, but he was the weak link! He cost me a win! So he paid for it, I ended him and he deserved every single bit of pain he felt tonight. From now on, and I’m talking to anyone in the back, when you look at me, be afraid of me. Be afraid of what I can do, because now I’m not going to waste my time trying to contain all these emotions I have pent up inside. I am here to win. I am here to be a champion, and that is just what I’ll do. At Category 5, there will be a Legacy title contendership match, and no matter how many others I have to defeat, I will be champion.” Sunderland looks directly into the camera with determined eyes, the crowd is shocked as we cut to the ring for the next match up of the night. QUOTE Jace and Eliza stand in opposite corners away from each other as the bell sounds. Eliza, in her first weekly Television Championship defense, makes the first move. She walks into the center of the ring and taunts for Jace to bring it. Jace locks up with Eliza and Jace quickly takes control. He has Eliza trapped in a side headlock and the referee asks Eliza if she wants to give up but she refuses to give up that easily. Eliza uses her strength and pushes Jace into the ropes. Jace uses the momentum and simply comes back with a devastating shoulder block. This time on his own power he uses the ropes for momentum again but Eliza is back up and leapfrogs Jace. Jace turns to face Eliza but eats a huge roundhouse kick. Quick cover by Eliza, but to no avail. Jace is able to kick out at two. Eliza mounts Jace and is able to deliver some forearm shots before the ref warns her and she backs off. As soon as Jace is back to his feet Eliza kicks him in the gut and attempts DDT but Jace pushes her away. Eliza comes back with a clothesline but Jace ducks it. A hip toss from Jace sends Eliza to the mat but Jace quickly locks on an Armbar, a move he is known for and uses on a regular basis. Eliza looks to be in a lot of pain. RILEY STEVENS: "Eliza may not be able to hold out much longer! Could we see the champion lose her title on the first week of this new rule?" Eliza looks ready to tap but her long legs enable her to get a foot on the bottom rope and Jace is forced to break the hold. Jace begins to argue with the referee, which draws a lot of heat from the crowd, many on their feet booing him. Jace picks Eliza up by the hair as she continues to scream in pain. Belly-to-Belly suplex sends Eliza hard onto the mat. Quick pin by Jace but he only gets two. Jace is back to his feet and doesn’t let up. He begins to stomp away on Eliza, which draws more massive heat from the fans. “Shut up! Shut up!” Jace yells at a few fans in the front row. Eliza slowly gets to her feet and she swings a few times at Jace but they clearly miss their mark. Huge German suplex from Jace and this one looks to be about over. Eliza couldn’t possibly take anymore. Jace covers. ONE…TWO..FOOT on the rope once again and it saves Eliza. Jace can’t believe it. RILEY STEVENS: "Jace could have had her right there, but he didn't hook the leg! What a mistake! Eliza knows the ring so well." Jace once again picks Eliza up violently by her hair but this time he eats a stiff forearm from Eliza that connects right to his face. Forearm shot after forearm shot connects and Jace stumbles backwards. Eliza delivers a huge lifting underhook sitout facebuster. Ignoring going for the pin, she scales the top rope and dives off with a huge moonsault, a move she adopted when training with Alexis. She goes for the pin but only gets two. Not wasting anytime, she attempts the CMB, Canada’s Most Beautiful, a sharpshooter submission move but Jace is able to kick through it, a kick connecting on Eliza’s nose. She holds her face in pain. Jace rushes Eliza, going for his “Train Wreck” Spear/Spinebuster combo. Eliza steps out of the way and quickly rolls Jace up. ONE…TWO…THREE!~ Eliza escapes with the win. WINNER – ELIZA GRAY (STILLTELEVISION CHAMPION After we return from commercial break, we see Eliza Gray walking through the hallway with her recently defended TV title around her waist. Kluge enters the shot. MATT KLUGE: “Nice job out there Eliza. I knew if there was one person who could handle weekly title defenses, it would be you.” Eliza is flattered - she nods and smiles. ELIZA GRAY: “Thanks Kluge. It isn’t ideal, but I’m not letting go of this title belt.” She turned to walk away but Kluge stopped her. MATT KLUGE: “That is what I like to hear, but I have a proposition for you...” Eliza turns back towards Kluge, interested. ELIZA GRAY: “Oh yeah? Alright, what is it?” Kluge smiled, Eliza was confused. MATT KLUGE: “Well, as you know we have a vacant spot in the Sole Survivor match. How would you like to be the fourth and final participant?” The crowd cheers out in the stands, Eliza smiles and nods. MATT KLUGE: “Great, follow me and I’ll get you to sign some papers.” They take off down the hallway as we cut back to the ring. QUOTE Ace Baldwin was first to the ring. Trent Lost was second to the stage after Baldwin had settled in the ring. Trent entered the ring and the two closed the gap between one another quickly. Trent then went for a pin, but he too was given the disappointment of a shoulder raised to break the attempt. Free from the hold, but not Ace Baldwin. The loss of one hold only made him try on another one. A crossface chickenwing was just the submission hold to lock Trent in. He rushed his opponent back to his feet and then set him up for the T.F.C. After that, the show was over and Ace Baldwin cleared the win for himself. Victorious, he rose up from atop his opponent and celebrated. Trent, though having put up a tremendous fight, had been bested, just barely, by Baldwin. All he could do was pulls himself up after Ace was finished and move on to the next day and the next fight.
