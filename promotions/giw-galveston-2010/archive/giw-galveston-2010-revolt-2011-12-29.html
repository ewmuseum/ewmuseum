GIW — Card

Revolt, 29 December 2011

29 December 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: THURSDAY NIGHT REVOLT LIVE! From Commodore Hotel – December 29, 2011 BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! The sky above the Commodore Hotel was set ablaze by the magnificence of the GIW fireworks streaming from the roof into the air above the crowd of around seven hundred screaming wrestling fans. Ion Television began their broadcast of Thursday Night Revolt by panning around in the audience with several cameras and simply observing the chaos. The voices of Riley Stevens and Oliver Thibodaux are heard over this picture. RILEY STEVENS: “Ladies and gentlemen, welcome to the second to last Revolt of the new year! Tonight, we will crown a King or Queen of 2011 based on a series of three beat the clock matches. These three matches promise to be highly competitive, as each of of the six participants want to call themselves the best of the year.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Their all going to knock each other’s teeth in! They are going to try to end each other’s career’s just for some pointless crown! What a waste of time and good matches.” RILEY STEVENS: “Actually, Ollie, there is some news concerning that right now. Blake Beckett is scheduled to make an announcement right... now!” Right on cue, “Demon Eyes” by The Answer hit the PA system, blasting through GIW’s outdoor arena. Blake Beckett made his way to the ring to the sound of a massive ovation from the fans. He entered the ring and took the microphone from the ring announcer. He paused for a moment, letting the cheers rain down onto him, the he decided it was time to make his announcement. BLAKE BECKETT: “Tonight, as you all know, will feature three beat the clock matches. The person who beats his or her opponent in the fastest time will be crowned King or Queen of Galveston Island Wrestling for 2011. However, I think we should take this match a step farther.” The crowd begins to cheer louder, understanding the direction this announcement might be heading, and Blake pauses for a moment to listen to the crowd chant “TITLE SHOT! TITLE SHOT!” BLAKE BECKETT: “Due to Ace Baldwin being injured at the hands of Aspen Chaud last week at the end of Revolt, we seem to be without a number one contender for the Undisputed Championship.” The crowd goes wild. They start chanting the names of all of their favorite GIW superstars, somehow thinking that their pleas could directly influence Beckett’s decision. He smiled, and carried on. BLAKE BECKETT: “Tonight, the winner of the Beat the Clock contest will be crowned the number one contender for the Galveston Island Wrestling Undisputed Championship! And if Tony wins, he gets to choose his opponent from anywhere on the roster to fight him at Malice!” The crowd began to cheer even louder than before, and the “TONY! TONY! TONY!” chants probably could be heard all the way at North Beach. BLAKE BECKETT: “And this idea is all fine and well, but this wasn’t the plan. I’ve already spent thousands of dollars marketing a Tony Miranda vs. Ace Baldwin match. That was the main event, that was the match that was supposed to draw people in. But, one person took it into their hands to ruin everything I had been building up, effectively costing me thousands of dollars and more than likely ticket sales and sponsor money. So, I want Aspen Chaud to come out here and apologize to me.” The crowd erupted in boos as Aspen Chaud’s music hit the PA system, and it exponentially increased when she started to make her way to the ring wearing her brand new GIW “Queen of the Island” t-shirt, blue jeans, and horrendously tall heels. He slithered into the ring and stood opposite of Beckett, and the two long time enemies stared each other down for a moment. When Aspen’s music began to fade down, she took a moment then began to speak, never breaking eye contact with her hated boss. ASPEN CHAUD: “Blake, you know better than to do this. After all that we’ve been through, after all that you have done to ME, you tell me to come out here and APOLOGIZE to you? You have the audacity to make me, the undisputed figurehead of this company, apologize to you, the one who have been trying to hold me back this entire time? Well I have news for you Blake, I’m not doing it. I did you a favor by getting rid of that cretin Ace Baldwin, he has been a parasite to this company ever since he brought himself over from that inbred hick federation from Montana. You should apologize to me for making me waste my time tonight, and you should be grateful for all of the wonderful things I do for this company. This ring wouldn’t be here if it wasn’t for me. These people would still be at home shoving corn dogs down their throats and watching The Ghost Whisperer if it wasn’t for ME, and MY talents. So Blake, you can take your microphone, and your fancy little suit, and all of your Ace Baldwin posters, and you can KISS MY-” Blake was quick to stop her from delivering her catch phrase, and the audience applauded him for it. He held his finger to her lip, quieting her down for a moment so he could speak. BLAKE BECKETT: “I remember the first episode of Revolt, I really do. That’s the night you are so bitter about, right? When I stripped you of your GIW Championship the night that you earned it? I only did that because I was protecting my company, Aspen. I didn’t want someone with such little natural wrestling ability and such a horrible attitude to be the GIW Champion. So I stripped you, and Ross Rydell ended up with the belt. I won. What you fail to realize is that I always win. So why don’t you stop complaining, and flattering yourself, and get on your knees and BEG for my forgiveness. Your job depends on it.” “Ooooooh...”’s echoed from the crowd. Beckett was dead serious, and Aspen was astonished at the first sign of a backbone from Blake in a very long time. She held her chin up, and spoke to him in an equally serious tone. ASPEN CHAUD: “I’m not going to apologize to you, I was just trying to help! Ace doesn’t sell tickets, he is a little rat. I’m a GODDESS! I’m a QUEEN! I am the one who sells tickets here Blake, it isn’t Baldwin. It isn’t your champion, it isn’t Castellanos, and it isn’t Faith Simpson... it’s ME! If you are so enamored with your marketing schemes, you would realize that I am the only person suited to hold gold in this company, and I am the rightful choice as number one contender for Malice. So go ahead and crown me NOW!” The crowd was booing again, Beckett simply shook his head and began to yell at Aspen. It was a side we hadn’t seen from him since the days of old. He was pointing at the ground as he spoke. BLAKE BECKETT: “I’ve let you YOU run this place for to damn long Aspen. I’m the Chairman. I’m YOUR superior. YOU ANSWER TO ME AND ONLY ME! So I’m asking, no, COMMANDING, you to get on your knees and apologize to me, or I will make your life in this company HELL. I swear it.” Aspen smirked, the crowd was in awe of what was transpiring. The usually cackling Oliver Thibodaux was wide eyed, and Riley Stevens was munching on a pencil. ASPEN CHAUD: “This is not new to me Beckett. Do your worst. You’ll see how irrelevant you really are when I PROSPER and you lose whatever credibility you thought you had.” “Did It On ‘Em” by Nicki Minaj hit the speakers, and Aspen Chaud backed out the ring, leaving Blake furious as we cut to commercial break in silence. QUOTE To start the show off we have a match up featuring three separate stars as we come into the show with Johnny Violence, Brody Harper, and Marcus Hister filling up the ring the fans all picking sides most of them thinking Violence has to be the favorite others stating that it would be Hister upon his return the rare few pulling for Brody. RILEY STEVENS: “As usual here on Revolt we start the show with the Open Battle Royal and we have a cast of characters this week in Brody Harper, Johnny Violence, and Marcus Hister. Who you got?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “These matches are always impossible to choose.” The match up is ready to go with the three stars all seeming within seconds of just fighting and finally the referee just lets them go. DING DING DING! Right away the match starts and Brody Harper rushes at Johnny only to be thrown into the corner shoulder first. The fans in attendance sympathize with the poor guy as he holds his shoulder as Marcus now heads after Violence the two exchanging blows back and forth in the center of the ring. Finally Johnny seems to get the advantage punching back Marcus until bam he hits him with a kick to the stomach that doubles him over. Quickly he bounces off the ropes and delivers a Spear to Violence to get the early advantage. Marcus bounces off the ropes going for a Leg Drop but as he comes forward Brody meets him half way with a Clothesline. Harper screams out for the crowd to get into it as he drops boots down on both men. Brody bounces off the ropes and goes to drop an elbow on Johnny but Violence rolls out of the way and waves off the match, instead choosing to head to the back. While Harper watches confused he turns right around into a Wheel Kick from Marcus. Hister grins out at the crowd as he senses the win as Violence hits the back as Brody gets to his feet then turns right into a Superkick that puts him down to the mat as Marcus gets the cover 1…2…3! JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…Marcus Hister!” The fans give a mixed reaction to the returning star, not really fully sure what side he is on as the referee raises his arm in victory after a quick match. WINNER: MARCUS HISTER After the end of the Battle Royal, we cut over to Blake Beckett’s office. He is sitting in his chair, rubbing his forehead, then there is a knock on the door. BLAKE BECKETT: “Good God, come in.” It was Joshua Acquin, the assistant General Manager of Revolt. He slid in the doorway, careful not to make any quick movements around the angry Beckett. BLAKE BECKETT: “Do you know why you are here?” Joshua stood in front of the desk as quiet and as still as he possibly could. JOSHUA ACQUIN: “No sir, I do not.’ Blake laughed a little bit, then stood up and stared at Acquin from behind his desk. BLAKE BECKETT: “I’m relieving you of your duties as assistant General Manager. In a time like this, it would be foolish of me to trust anyone else with an authority position other than myself. You will stay with the company as a road agent along with Apollo Calix and Paul Marshall. Good luck. I hope you enjoy your new assignment.” There was a brief moment, and then we cut over to the ring. ==== ‘Cruel Melody’ by Black Light Burns played to the crowd. Altair walked out with a raven perched upon his left shoulder and large shades covering his eyes. The raven fluttered, but stayed on his shoulder relatively undisturbed as the pale figure entered the ring and stood within the center of the squared circle, a microphone given to him. With it, he addressed the crowd. ALTAIR: “I am the Crimson Reaper; the Blood Raven; and soon to be known as the bringer of the Harvest.” He continued, his shades being lowered to eye the crowd with a smug glare and grin. He was hiding something behind that toothy grin. ALTAIR: “It’s time I spoke more about this harvest for Malice. What a glorious sacrifice it will be when the Crimson Reaper descends upon Galveston Island Wrestling with the monster that you fear. That’s right, I am not the only one come to destroy each and every one of you come Malice.” He let that sink in for a moment before he spoke again. The crowd beginning to simmer with jeers toward the pale figure of a man in the ring. ALTAIR: “When you wish upon a shooting star, it makes no difference who you’ll scar, but when it comes for the Harvest, your nightmares will come true. You see, I noticed that Galveston Island Wrestling has a weak link in the chain. The roster is slim and the talent slender in. The worms beneath your spine tingle and devour the very fabric of reality that you have all built around yourselves to pretend that everything is still okay here in this belittled organization. I promise that the end is near, but I won’t bring upon the 2012 apocalypse on my own here in Galveston Island Wrestling. No.” Before he could speak another word, the lights went to darkness. He began to snicker and said, “when I leave this company, there will be a scar that no one will ever recover from. Ha, ha, ha.” After that, the lights raised to a dim, blood red color and the sudden burst of the song ‘Man That You Fear by Marilyn Manson. At the lurching of the lyrics, a man emerged at the entry way. Dressed in black and white attire with a black painted venetian mask with a sparkling red tear drop underneath the left eye. The man slowly walked down the aisle and then pulled himself up to the rim of the ring and inside. The man was more muscular and somewhat taller than Altair. He stood facing the pale vampiric figure in the ring. The raven, disturbed, flew from the shoulder to the rafters of the local arena. The figure remained in mask and his attire lead no clues to his identity. He had short, dark-brown hair and black and white trunks, with the rest of his attire in black. Altair grinned wide with his fangs revealed. He signaled for the lights to return to normal and then turned back toward the crowd. ALTAIR: “Allow me to introduce you to the monster that will help me bring forth the Harvest upon Galveston Island Wrestling. The man that will scar this organization permanently. The man has become the monster you fear.” The man grabbed the microphone from Altair with no remorse for the aggressive behavior. He slowly turned his head toward the crowd around him and then lifted the microphone. JOHNNY SCAR: “You’ve poisoned all of your children to camouflage your scars. Pray now. Pray your life was just a dream.” He spoke slowly and slowly lifted the blackened Venetian mask to reveal none other than Johnny Scar behind it. Returned to Galveston Island Wrestling. JOHNNY SCAR: “You’re all so tangled in your sins that you cannot escape. Someone had to go this far. Born. Villain.” ALTAIR: “Oh, yes! When you wish upon a shooting star and watch the wounds scar over, your nightmares do come true. Altair and Johnny Scar will become the most dominating monstrosity in all of Galveston Island Wrestling to the very end. Welcome, everyone, to the Harvest.” Before the segment ended, an odd interference occurred. The screen revealed a flesh tone color and then a reddening color in the shape of a star. The letters ‘1.15.12’ faded into view and then blackness and the arena returned to normal. QUOTE When we return to the ring for the only booked match that does not have any Beat the Clock stipulations we see Jamie Hayden sitting in the ring ready to go. "South Texas Death Ride" by The Union Underground hits and the fans look up to the top of the ramp and start to boo as Assassin walks out from the back throwing out his arms soaking in the boos as he heads towards the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “This match up features three stars all trying to make a name for themselves here in GIW.” Assassin walks up the ramp joining Hayden in the ring as his music trails off and Bluegrass Banjo Music hits and the fans look confused up at the ramp as The Plumber walks out in all his…weirdness. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “What…the…fuck?” RILEY STEVENS: “Oliver watch your mouth, this is debuting star The Plumber.” The Plumber heads down to the ring completely ignoring the fans who all look at one another asking themselves the same question as Oliver just uttered a few seconds ago as even Assassin and Hayden seem confused as he enters the ring. Three men, all separate corners as the referee starts the match. DING DING DING! As the match starts the fans are on Hayden’s side who apparently got them to like him during the commercial. He stares across at Plumber confused as Assassin wastes literally no time rushing across the ring crushing Jamie with a big punch to the face. The fans boo him as he starts to deliver punches in bunches in the corner. Plumber watches from across the ring not wanting to get involved if he doesn’t really need to. Assassin pulls Hayden away and whips him off the ropes right into a Spinebuster as he gets up looking over at Plumber with a smirk on his face backing up a bit motioning that he is all his as a game of one upmanship now begins. Plumber steps up as Hayden gets up slowly holding his back but right away he is lifted over Plumber’s shoulders as he drops him with a Powerbomb right back on that already hurt back. Plumber steps to the side now as he allows Assassin to go ahead and get in as he no follows it up by hoisting up Hayden and putting him down with a Stalling Suplex that seems to put Jamie out. RILEY STEVENS: “These two are trying to top one another.” But just as Riley makes that statement Plumber stops the entire little back and forth as he rushes in and hits a Big Boot out of no where to Assassin. With his mask on we see little emotion but as he looks out to the crowd we can tell he wants them to take a bit of notice. Assassin rolls to the outside as Hayden gets up after a bit of time on the ground stumbling right back into a chop from Plumber followed up by a Headbutt with that metal mask while the referee has his back turned to watch Assassin. This seems to bust open Hayden who tries to wipe off the blood and this leaves him open for a kick to the stomach that doubles him over as Plumber finishes it off with a Powerslam going for the cover 1…2…Assassin kicks him off. Plumber gets to his feet slowly as he looks around but bam a European Uppercut sends him stumbling back into the corner. Assassin rushes at him going for a clothesline but Plumber raises his knees sending him right into them. Assassin stumbles back as Plumber goes top rope going for a Double Axe Handle but when he jumps off Assassin sets up and boom hits a European Uppercut! RILEY STEVENS: “What a counter!” Even the fans seem impressed with that one as Assassin holds up his arms in victory after that amazing move as he turns around and awaits Jamie to get up, now bleeding pretty badly. He starts to lock in ‘The Career Killer’ a Rear Naked Choke but before he can get it fully locked in Plumber pulls him off and tosses him over the ropes to the outside. The fans boo as Hayden falls to one knee then stands up right into a big punch that knocks him to the mat. Plumber from there goes to the top rope as Assassin recovers on the outside as Plumber jumps off with a Swanton Bomb right onto Hayden then covers him 1…2…3! Assassin slides in a second to late. JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…The Plumber!” The fans boo as Plumber immediately rolls from the ring heading towards the back as Assassin holds his neck angrily glaring at him as Jamie gets up trying to get to the back for some help but with no remorse he locks in ‘The Career Killer’ a Rear Naked Choke falling to the mat with it locked in. RILEY STEVENS: “Plumber gets a win but Assassin is here sending a message.” He keeps it locked in until Jamie goes out cold, when he breaks it up and gets to his feet some of Hayden’s blood on his arm as he throws his limp body to the mat then angrily heads to the back. WINNER: THE PLUMBER When we return from commercial break, we see Alexis Terry warming up for her match backstage. She is doing the normal routine of stretching and all of the like. Suddenly, Alex Castellanos entered the shot from somewhere down the hallway, she stopped for a moment to look up at him. ALEXIS TERRY: “Can I help you, Alex?” Alex smiled at Alexis’ rudeness, then he nodded his head. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “In fact, you can. That would be why I’m here.” She seemed extremely annoyed that he was still in her sight, but she continued to humor him. ALEXIS TERRY: “Well get on with it then.” She stopped and checked her pulse and brushed her hair out of the way. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “I just wanted to warn you one last time about dealing with Aspen Chaud. I know I’m not the only one to tell you about this, but you have to know that the majority of the people around here know she is a leach. She only has allies in the people, like you, that don’t really know her.” Alexis nodded, then pushed past Alex as she was on her way out. ALEXIS TERRY: “Why don’t you leave me to my affairs, and I’ll leave you to yours. How does that sound to you, Alex?” We leave this scene with Alex exhausted with the situation. Quickly, we cut over to Aspen’s dressing room where she is being primped and prepared for her match later on in the evening. ==== There are four women surrounding her applying make up, or curling her eyelashes, or doing something else to make her look like the Barbie that she calls herself. Suddenly, the door flies open, and Joshua Acquin runs inside. JOSHUA ACQUIN: “HE FIRED ME!” Aspen turned around with a shocked look on her face. ASPEN CHAUD: “What Joshua?” Joshua is bright red and enraged. He paced around the room, never making eye contact with anyone. JOSHUA ACQUIN: “Blake Beckett fired me. He said he couldn’t he trust me. WHAT DID I DO? I’ve done everything he has asked of me. I’ve gotten the ratings to soar on this show, and he fires me!” ASPEN CHAUD: “Oh wow... I...” Aspen applied some more lipstick, with a sinister smile on her face. JOSHUA ACQUIN: “I’m not going to let him get away with doing this Aspen. I DESERVE BETTER than to be treated like some interim run of the mill executive. He made me a FU*KING ROAD AGENT!” Aspen shooed her girls away, they shut the door behind them. ASPEN CHAUD: “I’m so sorry Josh.” JOSHUA ACQUIN: “I’m going to get revenge on him.” Aspen leaned back in her chair. ASPEN CHAUD: “Let’s talk.” We fade to commercial. QUOTE Lady GaGa played right into the ears of the crowd as Alexis Terry came down to the ring. The flashes of the crowd captured her in the ascent to the ring and toward the center of it. With the jeer bringing tears of joy to her, she waited for opponent to hit the ring as she had. She knew she’d have to dominate the match to show herself off to the higher ups and the crowd as a big player in Galveston Island Wrestling. The best way would be to defeat the Undisputed Champion – Tony Miranda. RILEY STEVENS: “I know you have grown quite the infatuation with Alexis Terry who has the chance to set the bar here tonight in this match.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “She is wonderful…” Tony Miranda made his arrival to the entry way and held the title high for all to see. It glistened in the flashing lights of the crowd. He lowered the title and made his way toward the ring with his own theme playing into the crowd, a vast difference from the glam of Lady GaGa, but it worked for him. He used the steps to enter the ring and with one last display of the title, handed it off to give himself the last few seconds of preparation before the bell would ring. When it had, Tony looked across the ring toward his smaller opponent. She may have been disliked by the crowd as a heel, but she still looked breakable to him. RILEY STEVENS: “Tony Miranda has looked dominant since becoming champion winning that Tables match last week and now he has a chance to continue his dominance.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “It ends tonight.” DING DING DING! She was eager to start the match after the bell, but Tony seemed reserved at first. He saved face after she hit a thrust kick on him and forearm to the chest. The body difference was obvious, but she seemed determined to make that weakness into a strength. In that fashion, she thrust a low kick to his inner thigh, connecting between the knee and knocked him down a peg, but Tony raised up quickly with a counter strike. Alexis stumbled backward toward the ropes. Tony grasped the smaller female and whipped her across the ring. He bent over at the center of the ring, expecting her to be tossed over, but instead, Alexis Terry pulled him into a DDT. She then took to the ropes again for the propulsion. When Tony pulled himself up to his knees, she shot back at him with a shining wizard. The crowd began to chant for him and jeer her. He realized he couldn’t hold back with this opponent and with that switch flipped, he got up quick from her attack and grabbed her into a lock up. If she wanted to be speedy, so could he and he whipped her into the right corner turn post. He ran his forearm into her and knocked the slender female down, then ascended the turnbuckles. RILEY STEVENS: “Rare sighting, Tony going up top.” Alexis Terry shook the strike out of her mind and was ready to move on, hoping to catch the upper hand, but when she turned, Tony launched himself like a missile from the top turnbuckle and tried to use the Hurricanrana to send her flying across the ring. Like a rag doll sliding for home plate, she slid across the ring and stopped close toward the ropes after being lunged into the air by Miranda’s maneuver. It took Miranda a few moments to collect himself and return to his feet. Alexis was still collapsed like a pancake on the canvas when he moved toward her to follow up the assault. He pulled her by head and hand to her feet and leaned her against the ropes. She slinked like a plush toy against it, but after a Muay Thai knee strike, she was jolted back to her senses. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Don’t touch her face!” Tony turned Alexis around, her back facing his chest and wrapped his more muscular arm around her neck. He bent her backward in a modified chokehold and held her in submission. She slapped his forearm and yanked for the ropes, but he pulled her away and toward the center of the ring with the maneuver locked in. When she seemed to be clearing his arm, he transitioned it into a headlock and applied enough pressure that she couldn’t escape. One submission hold for the other. It took a few moments before Alexis fell limp again. Her arms dangled like drapery that reached for the canvas. The referee checked the right arm by raising it and let it fall once, then twice. On the third drop, she barely held it up and used that raised arm to then jab Tony Miranda in the heart with her pointed elbow. This didn’t seem to affect Tony as much as she would have hoped, but a stomp near the mid-section got him off of her pretty quickly. Though she was now released, she was far from freedom. RILEY STEVENS: “…That hurts.” Alexis pulled off Tony and used an irish whip to counter a maneuver of his. She held up her foot, ready to kick him with a big boot to the face, but Tony saw it coming. He stopped at the tip of her foot and just shook his head, gesturing down to her like she couldn’t knock him with such a move. In response, she dropped him with a spinning wheel kick. Tony had held her for a while in a submission hold and so she decided to widdle him down with a quick elbow drop and knee drop to his head and then apply a tight leg scissors choke. With his head between her knees and pressure tight around, he found it hard to escape her maneuver. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Just tap Tony.” Miranda struggled in the submission hold, but had an ingenious idea to escape from the smaller female wrestler. He had bulk on her and thus shifted his weight to turn them both onto their stomachs. This took Alexis by surprise and halted her focus on the hold enough for him to grab her by the legs and slowly climb back up to his feet with her seated on his shoulders, the leg scissors still applied. He bounced her on his shoulders so her back was to the back of his head, and he fell backward with an inverted Samoan drop to slam her into the canvas belly first. Alexis held her face into the mat and her hands at her stomach after the fall. She writhed as he pulled himself up content with the counter. It was at the time he turned to look over at her, ready to pin her, that he noticed out of the corner of his eye another female at ringside. Aspen Chaud. He pointed toward her and yelled to the referee. The ref took note, but considering Aspen had done nothing but observe when they finally did notice her, he wasn’t about to throw her out. RILEY STEVENS: “Come on ref, throw that bitch out.” Aspen moved to the rim of the ring, but not climbing on top of it. She yelled to him and bought Alexis enough time to roll away and onto her knees. She lunged forth with every fiber of her muscles to drive a pointed elbow into Tony’s shoulder. Now Aspen was a distraction. The referee had his sights on the match going on, however. Alexis let Tony sit up after the attack as she propelled herself toward the ropes and used it to send her back toward him. He was at his feet and only slightly keeled over when she lunged forward and spun with a European uppercut. The maneuver made Miranda stagger toward the rim of the ring. His arms laid upon the top rope and his body slack. RILEY STEVENS: “The distraction gives Alexis back the advantage.” Alexis took in the sight and gauged the accuracies of her next move. She was disheartened to see Miranda come back to his senses quicker than expected, but only in time for him to be distracted again by Aspen Chaud, whom had finally ascended to the rim of the ring and distracted the Undisputed Champion. The referee and Miranda forced Chaud down, but the distracted was enough that it let Alexis charge Miranda and hoist up her right boot for that big boot to the cheek that had been denied to her earlier in the match. The force of the kick was enough to send Miranda over the top rope and toppling onto the matted floor. Aspen pulled back with her arms up in innocence. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Aspen hasn’t done a thing.” With all of the energy left within her, and to the discontent of the crowd, Alexis climbed the nearest turn post of Miranda and performed ‘The Most Glorious Moonsault Ever’ to the outside of the ring and right on top of her male opponent. The arena burst with excitement and jeer. After that maneuver, all bets were off with Aspen as she helped Alexis drag Miranda into the ring; herself included as she watched the pin and the successful count was made 1…2…3! With an upset victory over the Undisputed Champion, Alexis Terry was raised in celebration. She had defeated her opponent, even with the aid of Aspen Chaud. A victory that she knew would stabilize herself as a dominant force in Galveston Island Wrestling. However, it had taken a while to bring Miranda down and though her mind was more focused on the victory, she knew that the match was timed. How high was the bar set? JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…Alexis Terry at eleven minutes and fifty four seconds!” Alexis grins as she gets to her feet raising up her arms in victory getting the win and now having the time to beat as Aspen heads to the back having done what she came out here for. RILEY STEVENS: “Huge win for Alexis here tonight…But with Aspen…” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “It was her that hit the move not Aspen, she won it fair and square Riley!” Terry looks down at Tony with a smirk on her face as Aspen heads to the back to get ready for her own match as Alexis has just stunned the champion. WINNER: ALEXIS TERRY We cut to the backstage where Alex Castellanos is making his way to the ring. He passes a dumpster; sitting on top of it is Johnny Violence. Violence jumps down to intercept Castellanos. JOHNNY VIOLENCE: “Hey... look here. This is Alex Castellanos, the man who has won two matches in ten seconds since arriving in GIW. Do you know who I am?” Alex looked the man up and down, then shook his head. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “No, not really, no.” Violence nodded, he didn’t seem to be offended.
