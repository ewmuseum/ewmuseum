Monday Night Affliction, 24 January 2011

24 January 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: - MONDAY NIGHT AFFLICTION – BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! The Fox Southwest Cameras film the glowing blue fireworks as they soar into the black New Orleans sky. A camera on the other side of the river films the scene as the lights disperse far above the shining lights of the skyline. We enter back to the GIW Arena that holds three hundred and seventy five people at the zoo in downtown New Orleans. We pan around to Garrett Holloway who is standing in the ring dressed in one of his nicest suits with a microphone in his hand. GARRETT HOLLOWAY: “Welcome to Monday Night Affliction everyone, I am very happy you have chosen to join us this evening. Tonight, I come with rather bad news. For those who don’t recall, Megathreat, the then acting General Manager was brutally attacked by someone. It doesn’t please me to say it, but we have no idea who the culprit is, and no one has come forward. Last week, we said that Megathreat would be back on the show tonight, and he will, but it won’t be until nearly the end of the show due to some MRI’s he needs to take being booked at the same time. He has said that he knows who the attacker was, but he wouldn’t alert the officials until Monday Night.” The fans cheer Megathreat, knowing he is doing this only for the pleasure of the fans. Garrett Holloway claps along with the fans before speaking again. GARRETT HOLLOWAY: “Last week on Revolt, the play by play commentator, Riley Stevens, was attacked as well. Now, we don’t know if the attacks are related at all… but there are allegations that some members of the Affliction roster were the ones behind it. This is a preposterous allegation that was made by the Revolt GM La’Sarah Audric. The stars here on Affliction are much too well behaved to even contemplate doing a return attack on Revolt. It is obvious that this is a frame job done by the Revolt stars to make them seem like they are the victims here! I implore you fans to call them out on their audacious claims!” Suddenly, the music of High Voltage hits the speakers. Justin Volt and AJ Lightning begin to walk down the ramp and towards Holloway with their microphones already in hand. JUSTIN VOLT: “Garrett, calm yourself my friend! There is no need to break out your thesaurus for this speech, you poser. Look, whoever attacked Megathreat is a coward. We here in High Voltage just happen to hate cowards. So… maybe if I made a little road trip to Galveston to get even with those pricks over at Revolt, I don’t seem to see an issue. Do you?” Holloway stares down Volt for a moment, who is staring back to match him. Holloway breaks the moment by looking down and around before speaking again. GARRETT HOLLOWAY: “We hold ourselves to a higher standard here on Affliction. We don’t need to get violent to get even Justin, we are smarter than that. All we need to do is put on a better show than they do to get better rankings. If they want to sabotage us by taking out the figurehead, then that is their game. Justice will prevail; Affliction will prevail.” Justin Volt is laughing at Holloway, who is just shaking his head. Volt goes back into one of the corners of the ring and leans back on the turnbuckle. JUSTIN VOLT: “What the hell are you doing Holloway? These fans may be buying you games, but the rest of the backstage knows the real you. You may act like a prep; all smart… using the big words… acting like you the prim and proper figurehead of the brand… we know that isn’t you! We know it! They don’t though… why don’t we give them a history lesson?” Holloway goes ghost white, he takes a very deep breath and starts to walk out of the ring, but Volt gets in his way, keeping him from leaving. JUSTIN VOLT: “Oh, you don’t like that, huh? Come on Mr. Holloway, you can’t keep your past in the dark forever, can you? You see… Garrett Holloway used to run the Extreme Wrestling Syndicate…” Holloway turns around and smacks the microphone out of Justin’s hand. Justin stays standing for moment, seemingly frozen, except still smacking on his gum. Holloway exits the ring in silence, the camera following him as he walks backstage. When he disappears behind the curtain, the camera still follows him as he walks into one of the trailers set up for the upper staff. Holloway slams the door in the face of the cameraman, but it doesn’t take long for Alexii Ravynova to enter the shot from the left. She looks into the camera with a stern look. ALEXII RAVYNOVA: “Revolt… if it becomes undisputable that you were the ones who took out Megathreat, let there be no mistaking us here… actions will be taken. It is truly disgusting that jealousy could provoke you to do such a thing. If I have to, Revolt, I will take you down brick by brick myself.” Alexii leaves the shot as we slowly fade out on the trailer door, heading into our first commercial break. When we come back, we will have CDE vs. Dolo in a Last Chance Match. QUICK RESULT: BOTH CDE AND DOLO WERE FIRED BEFORE THE SHOW. After the match is over and the crowd dies down a bit, we cut backstage were Mark Cross is bandaging up his arm for the match tonight. There is a knock on the door, Cross tells the person to come inside… the door slowly creeps open and Madison Seton walks inside. MADISON SETON: “Hey, Mark… if you can’t talk… its ok… I just…” Mark Cross rips the tape off of the wheel and tightens it around his arm. Without looking at her, he stands up and goes to put the tape in a bag. MARK CROSS: “Go ahead Madison, I’m not busy.” Madison crosses over to Mark’s left. He fiddles with her hands and her hair as then begins to speak, her voice quivering. MADISON SETON: “Well… I just want to make sure that we are on the same page tonight. We want to win, there is no debating that, but regardless of what happens… I think we should make sure that Scott Reave doesn’t get his grubby hands near that title belt.” Cross zips up the bag and walks over to face Seton. He is silent for a moment as he studies her. He laughs to himself and begins to speak. MARK CROSS: “Let’s make no mistake about it Madison, I am in this match to win it… but I understand your point, and I agree to it. May the best man… well, wrestler, win.” They shake hands, it is obvious that Madison is giddy. She walks out of the locker room and the cameraman follows her every step of the way. Finally, she enters her locker room and takes a seat on the leather couch and takes and flips on the TV. From the back of the sofa, we see the head of Eric Sparrow slowly rising above her. He hovers there for a moment before speaking. ERIC SPARROW: “It is a new week, Miss Seton. I’m still here.” She screams and covers her mouth as Eric leaps over the top of the sofa and begins to snuggle with her. Tears are filling her eyes as he continues to talk to her. ERIC SPARROW: “Shh… yelling won’t make me leave little lady. I came to talk to you! I wont leave until you talk to me too… make that choice now Madison.” She calmed herself down before staring down Sparrow some more. He strokes her chin and licks his lips, staring directly into her pupils. He lets out a sigh. ERIC SPARROW: “Oh, I was very upset last week indeed. I could have had a chance to win my match, but you just couldn’t let me have my moment, could you? You’re selfish, do you know that? I just want you to know that I will be a champion before Exile… and I’m hoping you win it so that I can pull it right off your little waist!” Sparrow slowly begins to rise, Madison Seton stays still. Eric Sparrow walks out of the room, never moving his eyes as the scene fades. DING DING DING! The two men in the ring begin to circle each other as the fans cheer on what should make for a very good match. The two go in to start the match as they lock up however it’s Justin Volt that gets the early advantage as he is able to spin around him and lock in a Sleeper Hold. The crowd cheers as Craven struggles to get out before backing Justin back to the ropes and then tossing him forward off the other ropes. However Craven’s big clothesline attempt is ducked as Volt stops on a dime and catches him with a big Superkick! The crowd cheers showing it’s approval as Volt gets to his feet nodding his head getting some steam behind him as he waits for him to get up. Craven gets up and is immediately whipped into Volt’s corner. Where Justin Volt runs and nails him with a big clothesline and then tags in AJ. AJ jumps into the ring and with Volt holding Craven, he allows him to hit a big kick to the mid-section. Volt heads out of the ring and then AJ tags him back in and holds out Slade’s arm as Volt goes top rope and jumps off with a Double Axe Handle to the arm of Slade these two showing off some early tag work. As Craven falls back into the ring and tries to head towards his corner only for Volt to run across the ring and tackle him to the ground. The crowd cheers as Volt waits for him to get up however Slade catches him off guard when he gets up with an Enziguri! Slade gets up and angrily tags in Assasin, upset about the way he started the match as the always Assasin hops in and begins to circle around Volt. Volt gets to his feet and turns right into Assasin who attempts to end this early with a Powerbomb however Volt is able to roll out of it and make a pin attempt 1…2…Kick Out! The fans let out a big sigh as Justin gets to his feet and with Assasin getting up a split later in front of him he grabs him and nails him with a German Suplex! The crowd goes nuts as Justin turns and sees AJ calling for a tag. Volt walks over giving his guy a chance as he makes the tag. EMMANUEL RUSSO: “Justin Volt has them reeling and he tags in the fresh AJ Lightning!” AJ jumps into the ring and with a head of steam nails Assasin with a big clothesline and then follows it up with another one! The crowd is getting behind this team as he waits for him to get up. However as he reaches down to try to get him up its Slade that distracts the referee long enough for Assasin to nail him with an elbow to the head! The crowd boos and when Volt tries to get into the ring the referee runs over and stops him as Slade jumps in and grabs Assasin and the two hit a flawless Double Suplex! Craven quickly slides out as the referee turns around as Assassin goes for the pin attempt 1…2…Kick Out! The fans cheer happy that wasn’t it as Assasin angrily lifts him up and then whips him into the corner. Assassin walks across and starts to lay into him in the corner until the referee pulls him away however when he does Slade leans across and rakes the eyes. Assassin makes the tag as Slade Craven jumps in and as AJ beaten tries to walk to his corner he runs across the ring stopping him with a Running Bulldog! The fans boo as VOLT isn’t able to get in. However in a last bit of effort AJ kicks him away and makes one last effort to get to his corner with the claps of Justin and the fans cheering him on. However Craven whips him right next to Assasain. Then in a huge cheap shot runs across and nails Volt off the ring apron. He walks back and sets AJ up in the corner and then nails him with a 'Texas Two Step’! The crowd is actually in awe after that one however when he goes to make the pin the referee forces him out of the ring as Assasain jumps in the ring and jumps on Tate for the pin 1…2... KICK OUT! Assasin is furious, he gets all up in the referee's face. Volt makes a tag to Lightning.... ROLL UP...1...2....3!!!! EMMANUEL RUSSO: “Teamwork at its finest!.” WINNERS: HIGH VOLTAGE We head backstage where Scott Reave is walking with the newest member of Affliction, Jake Avery. Reeve is talking to him and Avery is listening to everything he says. SCOTT REAVE: “I’m glad you realize that I am your best bet if you want to become something in this company. Some people don’t realize it, but I am the best. To be the best, you have to beat the best… but that won’t be happening any time soon kid… so why don’t you just learn from the best?” Reave stops and Avery puts a pencil back behind his ear and folds a piece of paper into his pocket. He sighs and looks at Reave sternly. JAKE AVERY: “I’m really glad you are helping me Scott… I appreciate it. I’ve always wanted to get into wrestling, and now I have my chance. Maybe you could get me a match next week?” Reave nods his head, but before he could speak Nate Amazing shows up and extends a hand. Avery looks it, but doesn’t shake. Amazing brushes it off. NATE AMAZING: “Hey man, it just so happens that I haven’t been booked for next week! You know, I always like to give the newbies a shot to make a name for themselves; you want a shot at Nate Amazing?” Avery studies Nate Amazing, who is much smaller and weaker than he is. He chuckles to himself and turns to Scott Reave, who is laughing also. JAKE AVERY: “Are you serious guy? I don’t want to make my debut against someone who should be wrestling in the little tikes division on Revolt. I’d like a real challenge.” Amazing seems confused and starts to laugh at Jake Avery. NATE AMAZING: “Uh, who the hell are you dude? I wasn’t talking to you; I was talking to Scott Reave.” Avery seems furious now as his face turns bright red. Scott Reave is equally as angry that this guy would walk up and insult him in such a way. SCOTT REAVE: “Who do you think you are kid? You haven’t done anything to earn the right to challenge me to a match, so I don’t accept. You can take Jake Avery on next week though… how about in a match of Jake’s choice? Is that alright with you?” Nate Amazing seems pumped up. He begins to hop up and down and nod his head. NATE AMAZING: “Yeah, yeah whatever gets me in a spot where I can kick your ass Reave.” Scott whispers something into Jake’s ear. Jake’s face lights up and a smirk creeps across his face. He turns to Nate and extends his hand. JAKE AVERY: “A first blood match. The first one to bleed loses the match. You in?” Nate stops jumping and looks into Jake’s eyes for a moment. He nods his head and shakes Jake’s hand. Reave begins to clap. SCOTT REAVE: “Very good, I will see you both next week then.” Scott Reave leaves the scene as Jake and Nate stare at each other. "Poker Face" to play. The crowd erupts into a series of cheers as their hero, Elizabeth Reeve walks down the ramp. She make sit onto the ring and then stands on one of the turnbuckles and is so kind as to pose for the crowd. The crowd eats it all up and he goes back onto the canvas. He waits for her opponent. COLIN MCKENDRY: “You can expect chaos here..” EMMANUEL RUSSO: “No kidding.” Both begin to brawl it out. The referee tries to control the match but eventually finds that it is useless. He lets the match carry on until finally, Reeve kicks Ezzra on the knee and then gets up and hit’s a drop kick. Ezzra bounces off the ropes and tries to hit a clothesline. Reeve dodges it and then hit’s a rolling kick square on the face of Ezzra. Cover. 1...2... Kick out! Ezzra gets up. Reeve goes for a knee smash to the face but Ezzra counters it with a back body drop. Ezzra then slowly begins to wait for Reeve to get up. She can feel the momentum shifting in her favor. She tries to hit the Killswitch on Reave but Reeve manages a small package! 1...2...3! Reeve wins! WINNER: ELIZABETH REEVE After the match, we cut backstage where the camera is following the feet of someone, we cannot see their face. They walk up the stairs and over to a trailer. We can hear three knocks on the door. The door slowly opens and the feet walk inside. We cut to commercial. Amazing and Sparrow lock up and Sparrow overpowers Amazing with a Knuckle Lock. Mike Bryant comes in and locks a sleeper on Nate Amazing Sparrow Russian Legsweeps Amazing taking Bryant with him as well. Mike gets up and gets blasted with a Wheel Kick. Nate gets decked with a spear. Sparrow tries to Superkick Mike but he catches the leg into a Dragonscrew. Mike applies a El Christo leglock but Eric reaches the ropes. Nate comes from behind with a Wrestling Takedown taking Mike to the mat. Nate launches some Soccer punts into Mike’s skulls and Sparrow makes his way back up. Mike slides out of the ring and grabs a chair. He slides back in and in one shot brings Sparrow down to the mat. Sparrow is out cold! Bryant grabs the mic. MIKE BRYANT: This is my time... and I dont need to waste it with stupid matches." We fade away. WINNER: ERIC SPARROW We head backstage where Malissa Wiggins is standing by with Revolt General Manager, La’Sarah Audric. Audric is dressed in a lime green dress with hot pink lipstick and eye shadow. MALISSA WIGGINS: “La’Sarah, let me be the first to welcome you to Affliction… however… can you tell us why you are here with us tonight?” La’Sarah snickers at the question. LA’SARAH AUDRIC: “I’m here because I know what you, Holloway, and Megathreat are about to pull after the main event. I know that by the end of tonight, Revolt will be blamed for the attack on Megathreat. I know we are innocent, and I won’t stand for it!” And with that La’Sarah storms out of the interview area, leaving Wiggins shaking her head. "Lift Me Up" by Moby begins to play. Mark Cross walks down the ramp quickly, without even caring as to what the crowd has to say to him and enters the ring. He is focused on this match above everything else. “Party in the USA” by Miley Cyrus blasts through the speakers. Madison Seton omes through the curtain and runs into the ring. She starts talking smack to Cross jokingly, who doesn’t appear to care at all. Eventually, the long awaited Scott Reave comes. "Fucking in the Bushes" by Oaisis plays. Scott Reave comes out. Jake Avery is with him. The match begins. EMMANUEL RUSSO: “Here we go! A Deep South Champion will be crowned at the end of the night!” Reave runs at Mark Cross but is quickly hit with a big boot right to the face. Mark Cross throws Reave at Madison Seton. Seton hit’s a slap across the face that sends Reave to his knees. Madison Seton grabs Scott Reave from the arms and starts telling Cross to inflict as much damage as possible. Cross lands a series of blows but eventually, he goes for a hit but Reave gets out of it! Cross and Seton stare at each other. However, Reave clotheslines both of them at the same time and goes for a cover, only to have it be broken up by Seton. Reave tries to fight off Cross now but Cross seemingly easily just pushes him away and throws Seton at Reave, causing the two to fly out of the ring. Cross appears dominant so far. COLIN MCKENDRY: “Cross is well on his way to winning this match and the title! Cross waits as Reave and Seton fight on the outside. Seton slams Reave onto the announce table, which knocks him out cold. Seton enters the ring and then Seton and Cross begin to duke it out. The two exchange blows with the usual, “Yeah!” and “Boo!” chants going on…except there were no boos! Eventually, Seton manages to get the upper hand and Irish whips Cross to the turnbuckle. Seton runs at Cross only to be hit by the turnbuckle after Cross leaves. Cross goes for a roll up but it is broken up by Scott who has come to break up the pin. Reave starts working on Cross but quickly, he is thrown outside again. Seton picks up Cross a few inches off the ground and hit’s a side slam. She goes for the cover. 1...2... Kick out! Madison Seton starts stomping on the chest of Cross over and over again until the referee tells her to stop. Seton backs off. Reave grabs Seton’s leg. Cross clotheslines Seton who lands on the outside. Reave enters the ring only to be hit by a roundhouse kick by Cross! Cross sets up for a powerbomb on Reave, but it is countered into a hurracanrana with a leg hook! 1...2… Kickoout! Madison Seton is back in the ring. She does a baseball slide on Cross, sending him out. She slaps Reave across the face and locks in the FREEZING RAIN! HE TAPS! WINNER: MADISON SETON After the match is over and the commercial break has ended, we zoom in on the ring where Garrett Holloway, Alexii Ravynova, La’Sarah Audric, and Emmanuel Russo are standing. Garrett Holloway steps up to a podium that has been set up in the middle and begins to speak. GARRETT HOLLOWAY: “Last week on Affliction, there was a brutal attack on Megathreat… our General Manager and overall figurehead. This ruthless attack was committed by an unknown assailant, and tonight, we will find out who attacked Megathreat last week. Without further a due, let us please welcome Megathreat!” Megathreat’s music hits the PA system and the fans roar with approval. His music rambles on for about twenty seconds before he slowly walks out from behind the curtain. His face is riddled with cuts and bruises. The group holds down the ropes for him as he enters the ring. MEGATHREAT: “So, I am here tonight to tell you all who attacked me last week. The truth is that I have no idea who did it. That’s all.” Megathreat drops the mic. La’Sarah and Garrett are yelling at each other. Emmanuel Russo picks up the mic and begins to speak. EMMANUEL RUSSO: “Now look Megathreat… we need to know who is responsible for all of this. You honestly have no idea who did this to you?” Megathreat shakes his head no. The fans are booing now, but a voice is heard offstage. The crowd instantly recognizes it as Blake Beckett. BLAKE BECKETT: “Well, I do.” Blake slowly walks down the ramp with the microphone in hand. BLAKE BECKETT: “You know, when I hired the security team for the upper level GIW staff a few months ago… I was crazy! I was being negligent with my money… But look now, look who has the power. GIW’s ALL SEEING OMNIPOTENT GOD is in possession of information that will affect all of you.” Everyone is silent and peering at Beckett. BLAKE BECKETT: “Not only do I know who attacked Megathreat… but I know who attacked Riley Stevens on Affliction. I also know that you two General Managers were the ones who organized the respective attacks, and I know that I respect you all even more because of it.” La’Sarah is acting innocent while Holloway is ghost white. BLAKE BECKETT: “Oh, don’t be alarmed you two. You all know that I respect good television, that’s just what this is… good television! So, what makes television even better… SUSPENSE! YES! Would three days be to long to keep the views waiting?” Boos echo through the arena. BLAKE BECKETT: “I’ll announce the attackers on Revolt… see you then.” Holloway, Alexii, and La’Sarah are yelling furiously at each other as the GIW logo flashes across the screen and we fade to black.
