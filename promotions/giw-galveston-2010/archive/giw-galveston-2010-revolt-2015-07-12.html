GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS SUNDAY NIGHT REVOLT 7/12/15 FROM KAUFFMAN ARENA IN GALVESTON, TEXAS Revolt has a cold open this week, as once the show begins we find ourselves backstage in Aspen’s office with a simple line of text at the bottom of the screen stating “Earlier Today”. Aspen sits at her desk rubbing her temples while Alexis Terry stands opposite of her, barking at the commissioner. Katie Keller is watching intently from the couch as Chris Callum flips though a newspaper in a chair on the other end of the office. ALEXIS TERRY: "Katie Keller was robbed of her North Beach Championship, Aspen. She deserves a title shot more than anyone else in this company because if you don’t give it to her, you appear weak. We run this show, not Kevin Marshall and not Bronx Valescence." Aspen exhaled loudly, turning towards the woman in question as she twiddled her thumbs. Keller looked Aspen in the eye confidently while Callum seemed largely uninterested. ASPEN CHAUD: "Was this your idea? I know you’re new to this whole big time thing, but if you want something, you need to talk to me directly rather than sending Alexis my way." Alexis rolled her eyes while Keller nodded. KATIE KELLER: "I want revenge on Kevin Marshall. I’ll get that tonight. I agree with everything Alexis is saying right now, but it was her idea. Not mine." The two women break their gaze as Aspen turns to thumb through some papers. ASPEN CHAUD: "Well… it is a compelling point, I’ll admit. The Moment of Glory card is practically set in stone though, I’m afraid. But I think there is a way that we can work something out." She tossed the papers in the trash and leaned back in her chair. ASPEN CHAUD: "The contract for your match with Kevin Marshall can be nullified if he is unable to compete. Since I am refereeing the main event six man tonight, because God knows I can’t trust any of you to get something done on your own, I think I may be able to facilitate a way to… incapacitate Kevin Marshall." Alexis turned towards Katie with a smile. ALEXIS TERRY: "And when that happens, Katie Keller can be placed in the Deep South Championship match. That way, The Sir doesn’t have a chance of walking out with that title. And when Chris becomes Television Champion this week, we will control every title in the company." Aspen nodded, speaking before turning to Keller. ASPEN CHAUD: "Just like it should be." Callum folded the newspaper and locked eyes with the commissioner. CHRIS CALLUM: "I would gladly help out with a dismantling of Kevin Marshall. What he did to Gavin Achilles still makes me sick, and he deserves to be punished. I’ll take his title from him, and then I’ll take his career." Keller turned to Callum before speaking. KATIE KELLER: "With all due respect, Chris – I think Alexis and I should be the ones to take him out. He’s messed with us one too many times." Callum shook his head. CHRIS CALLUM: "No. It is important to me that I be the one to do this." Alexis snickered as she crossed the room to face Callum. ALEXIS TERRY: "What? To bring back that Superman, ‘justice prevails’ persona that got your jaw broken in the first place? You sound awful hypocritical Chris… sticking up for Gavin Achilles when you were part of the posse that screwed over poor Bronx Valescence." The color began to drain out of Callum’s face. CHRIS CALLUM: "That’s different. Gavin was innocent. Bronx was not." Aspen stood, slinging a purse over her shoulder. ASPEN CHAUD: "I trust you two can work it out. And Chris – what is this announcement that you are planning on making next Saturday? I don’t appreciate being left out of the loop." He turned his head slightly, as to avoid eye contact. CHRIS CALLUM: "Personal issues. Don’t worry about it." Aspen, Alexis, and Katie glared back at him before Alexis motioned towards Keller. ALEXIS TERRY: "Let’s go get lunch. Chris – I’ll try to remember to bring you back something." The three exited through the door of the office, leaving Chris alone looking down at his title belt in his lap. He rubbed his eyes as we faded into the opening video package of Revolt.
