GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: THURSDAY NIGHT REVOLT LIVE! At Galveston's North Beach Park – May 23, 2013 “And with flesh and blood I commanded an army -Through it all I have given my home for a moment of glory” echoes as the rebirth of GIW starts off with a video package set to the song “In The End” By Black Veil Brides. The package shows many of the memorable moments in GIW history, some of these including Aspen winning the Undisputed Championship, Alioth and Kluge battling it out in one of GIW’s best matches, and footage from the last iPPV Moment of Glory. The video ends and we are live on Ion Television inside the renovated North Beach Park in beautiful Galveston. Close to 400 screaming fans have taken their seats around the ring, packing the place to full capacity to witness this great event and memorable moment in GIW history. “Heavens A Lie” By Lacuna Coil hits and the fans inside the arena jump to their feet, remembering the music from GIW’s past. The camera pans around the crowd for a moment before fading to the stage to reveal Alex Castellanos, who many consider the heart and soul of GIW, standing there with a smile on his face. Instead of the normal wrestling gear, Alex is clad in a black suit. Alex looks out into the packed crowd before walking down to the ring overcome with emotion. “Alex,! Alex!” chants emerge from the fans as he walks up the ring steps and takes center stage in the ring. He is handed a microphone from a member of the ring crew and as his music fades a thunderous ovation overtakes the small arena. He nods a few times before signaling for the crowd to allow him to speak. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “I’m sure if you follow me on Twitter you know by now but if you don’t, my name is Alex Castellanos and I am your new Revolt General Manager.” The crowd explodes again, happy that someone who is on their side for once is in charge. Alex smiles as he paces the ring for a moment letting the news that he is now officially in power settle in before raising the microphone to speak once more. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “Once GIW closed its doors Blake Beckett went MIA. He is nowhere to be found. So, instead of letting Galveston Island Wrestling rot in limbo, I took the wheel. I did what I had to do to make sure GIW reopened its doors for real wrestling. I traveled all over – from UWL to PWX earning all the money I could. It’s with that money that I got the go ahead to allow me, with my own money, to get GIW back on its feet. It took every last penny that I had but I did it and I did it because you people deserve it. GIW is the home of real, hard-hitting wrestling with some of the best wrestlers in the world and I cannot take that from you. This is a new era in Galveston Island Wrestling, the peoples era!” The news of Alex single handedly using his money to reopen GIW hit the heart of most of the fans in attendance. In one single motion, the fans inside the North Beach Park Arena stood on their feet giving Alex a standing ovation. “Thank You Alex! Thank You Alex! Thank You Alex!” chants began in a small rumble but quickly emerged into another loud chant, the fans obvious red hot for the night of action ahead. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “No – thank you for believing in Galveston Island Wrestling these past few years. We would be nothing without the tremendous support from our fans. And, its this support that we call upon one more time. You see, if I could continue to support GIW solely I would but that’s impossible. It took everything I had to even get this show together and get the people who will be performing tonight here. That’s why I’m asking all of you for something in return. Please, if you enjoy the show tonight and I know you will, go to GIW.com and donate. I know that’s a lot to ask but to be able to give you the wrestling you deserve I’m just asking for a small donating. $10 maybe $20 dollars, money that will be used to pay the wrestlers, turn the lights on, and allow the ring crew to continue to do the things that keep this show running smooth. It’s thanks to your support we have been able to do the things we have done and it’s thanks to your support we will continue to do these –“ "Did It On 'Em" by Nicki Minaj cuts Alex off and the expression on his face tells the whole story. The crowd boos as well as appearing on stage is none other than Aspen Chaud sporting a beautiful red dress. In her hand is a microphone and her music instantly fades as she raises the microphone to her lips to begin speaking. ASPEN CHAUD: “Oh my – am I hearing this correctly? Is this what GIW has turned into? A charity case? This is pathetic. Can you do anything right Alex? I mean, if I really wanted to I could afford to support GIW by myself but that’s not going to happen. As long as you are running things Alex that will never ever happen.” ALEX CASTELLANOS: “Good, because I would never allow GIW to be ran with your dirty money. It would be a let down to each and every single one of these fans.” Aspen was now face to face with Alex in the ring when “Naughty Naughty” By Porcelain Black interrupts their conversation. A chorus of boos welcome Alexis on stage now; she was dressed in her wrestling gear already and had a microphone of her own. She waited for her music to fade before speaking, her British accent echoing throughout the arena. ALEXIS TERRY: “Oh my god, is this really what GIW has turned into? Bickering about money? No, not a single cent, no amount of money will save GIW. GIW has lost its glamour. It’s lost its shimmer. The only thing that can save GIW?” Alexis was now in the ring and standing next to Aspen and Alex. ALEXIS TERRY: “IS ME – ALEXIS TERRY AS UNDISPUTED CHAMPION!” “New God Flow” By Kanye West featuring Pusha T hit the PA system and once again the crowd bare witnessed to a former GIW star appearing on stage, this then Ryan Cavallari. He smirked sporting a plain white v-neck, jeans, and sunglasses. The crowd gave him a mixed reaction as he walked to the ring and entered. A microphone was quickly handed to him. RYAN CAVALLARI: “Let’s just be honest with ourselves, I’d already be champion if it hadn’t been for GIW closing its doors. I’m exactly what this company needs. I will hold this company on my back as I climb the ladder. I won’t rest until I’m Undisputed Champion.” “Enough” Walking out onto the stage was none other than Alioth Starre who received a loud ovation. No music, no fancy lights, just Alioth in his gear with a microphone. Alioth received a mixed reaction but more boos than anything. He looked down at the ring full with his peers and continued. ALIOTH STARRE: “The time for talking is over. It doesn’t matter what you’ve done or who you are, the best wrestler will walk out champion tonight. It’s time to shut up and fight!” The crowd cheers the gesture made by Alioth as he drops the microphone on stage and simply walks to the back, allowing Revolt to ease into the first commercial break of the evening. RILEY STEVENS: “You know the alliance wasn’t going to work too long.” Not even seeming to notice that Alexis isn’t really all the way out. Gray catches Aspen with a knee that sends her rattled as she uses the top rope to keep her up on her own two feet but it proves to be her down fall as Gray backs up and connects with a big boot to her jaw sending her over the ropes and to the outside, though she manages to keep a hold of the ropes as well, just barely keeping a hold at the bottom. Knowing she’s got her finished Gray reaches over the top rope tearing at her grip on the second rope, but as she goes for it Alexis rushes over to her grabbing her and using Gray’s own position to force her over as Aspen releases her hold then Terry tears Gray off the top sending her down on top of Aspen on the outside, eliminating two in one. Aspen Chaud is Eliminated & Eliza Gray is Eliminated These fans are shocked as two times tonight Alexis has gotten the better of Aspen as she stands on the outside of the ropes, probably wasting too much time doing just that but lucky enough that Ryan and Alioth are across the ring treating the match like a one on one, the two having this whole time been grappling back and forth. Each knowing each other well enough to stop most of the other man’s offense as Terry returns to the ring, letting Ryan and Alioth do some battle but shocked like most when Alioth finally hits a game changer as he catches him with a Superkick out of no where! But Ryan ever so tactical, even with his head rocked drops to the mat knowing Alioth doesn’t have it in him to throw him over, as dead weight. Alioth shakes it off then looks around the ring to see who else is left and gets caught with a big low blow out of no where from Terry, the fans booing her as Starre falls to the mat in pain, even Bowie fans know that feel. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Goodbye Alioth!” With that these fans are shocked to see Terry waiting for Ryan to get up, motioning towards the downed Alioth. The two’s long history together seeming to be put aside long enough to focus here tonight on Alioth as the two head over to Alioth, grabbing him up and going to throw him over only for Starre to stretch out his hands and hold onto the rope, stopping them from throwing him over before releasing one arm to elbow Alexis causing her to break the grip dropping him down to his feet and connecting with a Heart Punch to Ryan then a Superkick to Terry putting her on dream street. But before he can take her out, Cavallari spins him around out of no where and lands ‘A Heartless Moment’ a Snap DDT that takes him out. Alioth is out cold as Alexis shakes off the move but Ryan shrugs his shoulders and connects with the very same move to Terry, making her dead weight. Having little effort picking her up off the ground, Ryan casually walks over and dumps her from the ring; Terry’s amazing night coming just a few minutes short as she hits the outside with a thud. Alexis Terry is Eliminated And with that we are down to just two, or should we say one as Alioth just now starts to get back up to his feet, hurting and barely able to stand the move really jarring him as Ryan again lines up across the ring, this time rushing at him and going for the same clothesline that cost him the match earlier and this time the move takes Alioth over but the speed and the momentum as well as Alioth leaning on the ropes pulling it down causes Ryan to go over as well. But while Alioth gets caught up momentarily on the ropes Ryan does not sending him to the outside hitting first while Starre hits seconds later as these fans go wild and the referee rings the bell! And after a moment to clarify with the announcer, he makes the call. REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner and the new GIW Undisputed Championship…ALIOTH STARRE!” The fans in the building go wild here tonight as Alioth Starre by the skin of his teeth, rather just the luck of the draw gets the win as Ryan’s own momentum and a lucky break for Alioth makes him the new Undisputed Champion, something he and Ryan probably have yet to even realize yet, but these fans have and they are going crazy as the longest reigning champion in GIW history, is champion yet again! RILEY STEVENS: “Alioth Starre is the champion! He lasts just a little bit longer then Ryan!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “THIS IS A TRAVESTY! IT’S A SHAM! RYAN WON, ALIOTH GOT LUCKY! NOOO!” These fans seem more then pleased however as Alioth rolls into the ring as the referee does the same along side him, handing him the belt just as he gets his wits about him as Ryan has gotten to his feet on the outside and has just realized it as well, glaring at Alioth in the ring, so angry he seems frozen as Alioth holds the belt with his hand, still holding his neck from Ryan’s Heartless Moment as perhaps luck played a factor in his winning. RILEY STEVENS: “What a mome-” Confetti shoots out from a few cannons around the ring but the crowd boo’s as Ryan slides back into the ring spinning around Alioth and dropping him right away with another ‘A Heartless Moment’ this time right on the belt that Starre dropped, planting him down and out the confetti now falling on Ryan as Cavallari gets to his feet, the title now with some blood on the front piece as Ryan stands over the fallen Alioth. RILEY STEVENS: “What a cheap shot!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Alioth got lucky Riley…Ryan beat him but by some sort of miracle he won the belt, twice tonight he got lucky. History shows in a one on one match Ryan would beat him! And he will do that whenever he gets his rematch, which should be SOON!” Even though it’s Alioth that wins the belt tonight, it’s Ryan that ends the night standing over a fallen and bloodied Starre as one can’t help but wonder if Oliver is really right and what will happen when these two meet one on one, but until then Alioth Starre is GIW Undisputed Champion…again. WINNER: ALIOTH STARRE (New GIW Undisputed Champion)
