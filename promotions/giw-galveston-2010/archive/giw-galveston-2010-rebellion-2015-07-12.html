Rebellion, 12 July 2015

GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS SUNDAY NIGHT REBELLION 7/12/15 FROM KAUFFMAN ARENA IN GALVESTON, TEXAS QUOTE Open Battle Royal This match saw Karl Parris and Zebadiah Lewis making their debut in Galveston Island Wrestling, joined by a local talent named Antonio Lopez. Zeb proved to be quite talented in the early portion of this match, managing to remove Parris from the ring and bully Lopez. However, late in the match, Karl Parris took back control and ended up finishing Lopez off with a double underhook DDT that he calls "Dead Weight". WINNER: KARL PARRIS QUOTE Brody Harper & Landshark vs. Shining Hero Ishimaru & Kelsi Ross Brody and The Landshark were unable to work as a team. As a result, The Landshark left Brody alone in the ring so that he could prepare for his TV title match later in the night. Harper looked impressive, however, managing to take out Ishimaru. Ishimaru did manage to get a hot tag towards the end. Ross came in and looked as talented as ever, beating Brody Harper with her finishing move, the Kelsi Lock. WINNERS: SHINING HERO ISHIMARU & KELSI ROSS
