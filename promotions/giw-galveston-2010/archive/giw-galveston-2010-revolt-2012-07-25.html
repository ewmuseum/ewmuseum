Revolt, 25 July 2012

GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: WEDNESDAY NIGHT REVOLT LIVE! On Stewart Beach – July 25, 2012 on Showtime BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! The show starts off with some fireworks as usual as these fans are going crazy ready for a huge night of matches here tonight on a stacked episode of Revolt at least on paper, but before they can get to excited “Notes From A Sunday Morning” by Unjust hits as these fans begin to boo as Ryan Cavallari with Aspen Chaud on his arm head out from the back getting booed by the crowd as they walk down to the ring together, still not yet in ring gear as they have a lot of time before their big main event match tonight. Ryan pulls up the second rope for her as she sits on that same rope for him to get in as they walk across the ring as Aspen calls for a mic which only angers the crowd even more as she walks back to the center of the ring next to Ryan. ASPEN CHAUD: “So! Over the past few weeks here on Showtime there has been only one constant from each episode and that is the simple fact that the two biggest stars in the company are not even in the Main Event of ‘Moment of Glory III’ they are standing right in front of you. The greatest Undisputed Champion in history and the man that will soon take that belt as his own as well. Nobody cares about Alexis, nobody cares about Alex, and nobody cares about Seton either. Each week it’s been us beating them, we beat all of them in a tag match just two weeks ago and then last week I defeated Alioth while Ryan continued his streak by defeating-” On cue "Radioactive" by Imagine Dragons hits as these fans start to mostly cheer as the GIW Legacy Champion, Lokie Lindsay walks out from the back with a microphone of his own shaking his head as he talks with his Legacy belt over his shoulder. LOKIE LINDSAY: “You know I am not usually the type to come out here and talk, I generally just defend my belt but I couldn’t help but hear you about to say that you defeated me last week? I know, you got the pin but if it wasn’t for Aspen distracting me do you really think you’d have had that advantage in the first place? I happen not to think so. In fact she’s out here talking for you, she’s out here helping you in matches, you starting to forget what it’s like to do anything by yourself?” Some loud ooh’s can be heard from the crowd as Lokie enters the ring as Aspen shakes her head as Ryan just simply glares back at him not showing really any emotion to his statements as Aspen steps forward with the microphone. ASPEN CHAUD: “Do you really think you are even close to being as good as Ryan? Frankly the two of you are not even on the same level. Cavallari is moving on to bigger things now, and that doesn’t include you. Because trust me, he has not interest for that little belt on your shoulder. You’ll have to deal with the fact that while Ryan is going for that Undisputed title you’ll be left as simply his side act.” Aspen looks up to Ryan with a smirk on her face as he looks down at her nodding as Lokie chuckles a bit at her statement. LOKIE LINDSAY: “You know I was saying that Ryan needed your help but don’t get me started on what he means to you. I mean since you lost that title when’s the last time you actually won a match without him? He can do it by himself if he was man enough to but you…you couldn’t if you tried.” Aspen holds her jaw down looking up at Ryan as she raises her mic back up now basically screaming into the mic. ASPEN CHAUD: “Who do you think you are! I am the greatest Undisputed Champion ever! I have held more belts here then you have won matches! I AM THE BEST GIW WRESTLER EVER! And you, Mr. One Hit Wonder are going to come out here and talk down to me? Listen up. People keep saying you and Ryan are future Undisputed Contenders but as far as I am concerned you are nothing more then an after thought. Who have you ever beaten? Ryan is the future champion, you are nothing! You’ve never been important and you never will.” Lokie has clearly struck a nerve as Aspen’s anger resonates through the crowd as Lindsay raises his mic back up readjusting his title as he does it. LOKIE LINDSAY: “Who have I beaten? I defeated our current Undisputed Champion to win this belt and have defended it since. Sure you can talk about all your history but the last few months Ryan is the only thing keeping you relevant. And if you don’t think I am a future champion let me go ahead and prove it. Sure you may not mean as much as you would have used to but let’s do this. If I can’t get my rematch with Ryan, I’ll take a match with you. I’ll defend my belt against you at ‘Moment of Glory III’ and show you that while I am moving up you are on your last leg around here.” Lindsay drops the microphone and without hesitation clearly angered Aspen screams out her answer into the microphone. ASPEN CHAUD: “YOU ARE ON! YOU ARE ON!” Lokie grins as he exits the ring as Aspen turns to Ryan breathing heavy now in anger as she tucks herself into him screaming some things at Lokie as he goes as we have just added another huge match to Moment of Glory as on cue we get ourselves a Moment of Glory teaser video. QUOTE "Frontline" by Pillar hits as immediately some boos fill the arena as Daniel Starr the man who long claimed himself as the Face of GIW has some gold to back it up as he has the North Beach Championship title strapped around his waist showing it off with a big smirk on his face as the fans boo his less then stellar way that he won it. RILEY STEVENS: “Well Oliver you said for a long time Starr was a future champion and with the revival of the North Beach title he is the title’s new champion.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “As usual I am right!” Starr strolls down to the ring taking longer then normal this time showing off his belt as he enters the ring with his gold tossing it over his head only inciting more boos as his music fades and immediately the crowd pops even before "Party in the USA" by Miley Cyrus hits as Madison Seton again enters with her big time smile as she heads to the ring looking as usual jazzed and ready to go. RILEY STEVENS: “Seton is one of three Undisputed contenders with a match tonight all looking to get some momentum going.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “As though it matters Terry leaves as Champion, Sinclair leaves as the Boss.” Starr lifts his newly won GIW North Beach Championship high in the air for Madison to see as she enters the ring showing it off for her as she laughs at him and then heads to the top rope for her fans who cheer her on as she jumps down as the bell is rung. DING DING DING! He's in her face bragging about it. Seton doesn't seem to care. Starr then goes as far as to slap Seton right in the face. That set the Undisputed title contender off. Before Starr could even react Seton hit him with a Thez Press and began punching him right in the mouth. Starr uses his strength to turn Madison around and pin her shoulders down to the canvas 1...2..Madison gets the shoulder up before three. Starr looks at the referee shocked yelling "I'm the face of GIW! Count fucking faster you non factor." The referee cuts his eye at Starr. The North Beach Champion turns his attention back to the downed Madison Seton. The overly confident champion guides Madison to her feet slowly. The fans thought a high impact move was coming up. Starr simply spits right in Madison's face showing utter disrespect for his opponent. Madison has an angry humiliated look on her face. She looks as if she's about to rip Starr's head off. Starr then tries to slap Madison but she catches his hand. RILEY STEVENS: “Here she comes!” The contender musters up enough strength to lift Daniel Starr off his feet and body slam him down on his back. The Noth Beach Champion had a surprised look on his face. Madison follows this up and sprints across the ring boucing off the ropes and charging back to crack Starr right in the nose with the tip of her wrestling boot. After Madison drops an elbow on Starr's chest covering him 1...2.....A split second before three Starr gets the shoulder up. Madison tries to turn the pin into a submission hold. Grabing at Starr's head and arm. But the ever crafty North Beach Champion pulls Madison over his body and turns her upside down so her shoulders touch the canvas. 1....Madison rolls out of the pin at one and clocks Starr in the face with another sharp left. RILEY STEVENS: “Back and forth action here.” The North Beach Champion rolls to the ropes. Madison chases him and tries to grab at his ankle but the referee is pulling at Madison's shoulder and telling her to get off. She does. But while the referee is talking to Madison Starr pulls something out of his tights. It looks like a pair of brass knuckles. Madison tries to tell the referee but he's pushing her back. Daniel runs up to Madison and cracks her right in the forehead with the knuckles causing her to fall flat on her back. Daniel slips the knuckles back into his tights. The referee thought it was a regular punch. Starr exits the ring and gloats to the fans telling them "I should take her SPOT and become DOUBLE CHAMPION bitchess!" After the trash talk he slides back into the ring and lays on top of Madison without hooking a leg. ONE! TWO! THRE- Madison gets her shoulder up!!!! RILEY STEVENS: “Starr nearly had her there!” Starr is shocked. He gets up looking at the referee wondering how the brass knuckles wasn't able to win the match. He failed to realize he gave Madison too much time to recover. He bends to try and pick her up but Seton pulls his head down and grabs onto his left leg getting him into a small package pin 1...2..3!!! Madison wins against the North Beach Champion. The referee helps the dizzy Madison back to her feet. She barely knows where she is. Yet, she has more momentum going into the Undisputed title match. REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner…Madison Seton!” These fans are going crazy as Seton stumbles back into the corner with her arms held high in the air after the victory, as Starr hops right back up going right after the referee grabbing him by his shirt yelling at him. RILEY STEVENS: “Seton pulls it out!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Luck, Daniel dominated her.” Seton rolls out of the ring high fiving a few of the fans along the way as she heads backwards up the ramp as in the ring Starr argues that he got his shoulder up walking over to the camera and yelling into it “I got my shoulder up! This is not a loss! IT DOESN’T COUNT!” before shoving it away as he heads to the back yelling one more thing at the referee as he goes. WINNER: MADISON SETON To the back we go with Alexis Terry and Alioth Starre the odd combo together talking in the backstage area with a dark figure sitting behind them in the shadows as the two are discussing something as we come into the scene. ALIOTH STARRE: “I was just told that I have a match with Alex next week, a match I have long waited for and I plan to take him down a few notches.” Terry seems to be happy with this news but quickly that smile turns into a frown as Zerotine Valentino walks up to them, Starre catching a glimpse of her and walking away as Zero takes his spot next to Alexis. ZEROTINE VALENTINO: “Listen I know that last week we sort of let things get heated but I just wanted to say that I did think we teamed pretty well and, you know we’d make a pretty good team.” Valentino seems a bit apologetic as Alexis rolls her eyes. ALEXIS TERRY: “You stole my pin, no thanks!” Valentino nods her head willing to admit that she did just that. ZEROTINE VALENTINO: “I admit that but I want to make it up to you, I got a match with Seton next week. I’ll show you that we could be on the same page.” Terry nods her head seeming a bit intrigued at the thought as the two walk off together as the figure in the background can be heard saying something. ???: “She is next…” And with that we head back to the ring for the next contest. QUOTE Sydney Chase waited eagerly for her opponent. She had already made her entrance, slipped through almost undetected. She was new, so that was to be expected until she made a name for herself. This would be a bout to do such a thing. As she and the referee waited for the opponent to arrive, the lights blackened. The arena fell into a dull roar, then near silence. ‘Scarred’ played over the audio system and the lights turned to a dark crimson hue. The flicker of firelight danced in the crowd. Scar was announced, but he did not make his way to the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Scar is supposed to be in the match tonight.” Instead, after what seemed like forever for the challenger in the ring, he climbed up to the rim of the ring behind Sydney like a tiger in the night. The lights returned to normal and the white figure of a dark man emerged behind her. She took a moment to try to understand what had happened before she even thought to turn. Startled, she staggered back in surprise. This only made him smile behind that dove-white, leather, venetian mask. Scar soon ducked under the third rope and entered the ring, his long white coat swayed as he stopped before her. With it handed off to a crew member outside of the ring and then his mask removed and set at the corner, Scar was ready for the match. His return match. DING DING DING! Sydney received a few encouraging chants, but as soon as Scar’s gloved hands clenched around her feminine throat, the crowd knew that the show was over. He hoisted her up with both arms like a ragdoll and then tossed her flat onto her back in the center of the ring. Sydney writhed from the pain that swelled and pulsed at her neck, and stung her back like a thousand hornets. Scar backed himself into the ropes and then swiftly lurked forth. He leapt straight up over her and came down like a ton of bricks with a knee drop. After that, he figured her story was over and had gone for the pinfall. With a full count and another victory under his belt, ten to be exact, Scar lifted back up from his new victim and looked out to the crowd with a grin on his face. REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner…Scar!” He wasn’t done. Scar grabbed the girl by her hair and pulled her to her feet. Groggy, Sydney could barely make heads, or tails of what was going on. Her head was most likely throbbing and in dire need of vicodin. He signaled with a two fingers held in the air, diagonally that then swiped downward in the same angle like a knife cutting through flesh. RILEY STEVENS: “What the hell is going on…” At that motion, the lights blackened a second time and suddenly raised again. All seemed normal, except the gratuitous amount of a certain crimson liquid that covered Sydney Chase from head to toe as though it had been dumped upon her overhead. Scar simply snickered and backed out of the ring, amused. WINNER: SCAR Into the backstage we go once more this time with Daniel Starr talking to a random staff member, still seeming as upset as he did earlier in the ring. DANIEL STARR: “You saw that earlier right! I SO HAD MY SHOULDERS UP! I talked to Blake and he might change the result, if not it would be a travesty.” The staff member looks up from what he’s doing looking around wondering if Starr is even talking to him but as Vega walks up behind Daniel the staff member scurries off in fear as Starr turns around to look at Vega. VEGA: “Calm down, I am not here to fight you because if I was trust me you wouldn’t have ever saw my face, the same courtesy you gave Trent last week. Just came by to tell you, tough luck on the loss.” Starr chuckles at her sarcasm but makes sure to really make a bit of a thing of him readjusting his title on his shoulder to show it off. DANIEL STARR: “It was a cheat, what are you going to do when you win celebrate with Trent? Oh…Wait.” Daniel has another laugh at his own joke as Vega laughs with him very sarcastically but ends it with a quick snap back at him. VEGA: “Well actually I will have a lot to celebrate though, because I was told if I win I get a rematch with you and trust me this time you won’t be able to steal that title.” Vega walks away with the announcement set, if she can win she’ll get some chance at redemption with Starr as she heads off with Starr looking down at his title with a grin as we go to the ring. Alex coolly side stepped Genocide who rammed himself into the corner. Alex began throwing some punches and kicks towards Genocide who instinctively covered up himself for protection. Leaning his body out of the ropes the referee forced Alex to back off of Genocide. Genocide smirked as he came from the ropes. Alex shook out his arms still loosening up. They both made their way to the center of the ring looking to tie up. However just before their fingers locked Genocide threw a stiff kick to the mid section of Alex Castellanos. Alex doubled over as Genocide began clubbing his back. With a flurry of kicks, back clubs, and punches Genocide was sucking the life out of the fans who came to watch their hero win. C’mon Genocide, wipe the cocky smile from this guys face!” Genocide went to trash talk the fans as Alex was sown on a knee reeling from the pain. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner…Alex Castellanos!” RILEY STEVENS: “What were you smelling again Oliver?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “The stench of disappointment.” The camera pans back to Alex who was in the ring celebrating a quickly earned victory, having returned from his past few weeks off to pick up the victory with that Superkick.
