GIW — Pay-per-view

Breakdown 2011, 29 May 2011

29 May 2011

GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: B R E A K D O W N BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! As soon as the show starts we cut to the parking lot, where a limo has just pulled up. Stepping out of the limo to many people’s surprise is Aspen Chaud wearing a black dress. Her driver closes the door behind her as she begins heading towards the door when suddenly someone steps in front of her it is a bouncer of some sort. ASPEN CHAUD: “Excuse me what do you think you are doing?” The man, whom is a menacing looking figure standing far above her answers back. BOUNCER: “Blake Beckett informed me that you’re not allowed in here tonight.” Aspen looks at him like he is crazy, shaking her head. ASPEN CHAUD: “Are you joking? I AM ASPEN CHAUD! I am this companies best ever. I am scheduled for the Main Event tonight. So if you don’t mind, stepping out of my way.” Aspen plants her hand on her waist as the Bouncer shakes his head. BOUNCER: “Think he said after your little speech at the end of Revolt last week, your fired...” Aspen’s jaw drops, angrily replying. ASPEN CHAUD: “I’m WHAT!? He can’t fire me! I was just upset on Revolt. That belt is mine!” The bouncer shrugs, as she turns around yelling something at her driver before heading into her car. As almost the prologue to ‘Breakdown’ ends. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = As soon as that ends, we cut to the ring here on North Beach where the crowd is going insane as we pan around them finally ending up on Riley Stevens and Oliver Thibodaux, Riley sporting a huge smile as the two start up. RILEY STEVENS: “Looks like we won’t be seeing Aspen Chaud here tonight, what a way to kick off the show.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “That poor girl, let her in. Blake will do anything to keep away from the title.” RILEY STEVENS: “Oh right…After her actions this week on Revolt she deserves this. Besides, she tapped out to Alioth as far as I am concerned that was her tapping out her title hopes. You see tonight’s theme is just that, championships. Every match tonight will be for a championship. Starting with the huge kick off and return of the GIW Television Championship, a title notably held by Joshua Acquin and Jake Kennedy. It’s been known as a title that shoots people up; tonight we’ll see veteran Jak Hendricks, fast rising star Mr. Black, and recently returned Pandora fight for the belt.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “You ready for a Jak Attack?” RILEY STEVENS: “I think you’re the only person who likes that guy, next up we have something of a grudge match. Current ‘GIW North Beach Champion’ Trent Lost defends his belt against former ally Marcus Hister whom personally called him out after Trent turned his back on Aspen as well as Hister last week on Revolt. I personally thought Lost made a wise move, branching out from that team.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “He had it all. The girl, the belt, and he threw it all away. Personally I hope Hister beats him up so badly he’ll wish Aspen was there to pick him up.” RILEY STEVENS: “Following that match up we have a GIW Tag Team Championship match with Brotherhood of Steel defending against the Strong Style Soldiers. Certainly this is a match to declare who really is the best tag team in GIW. The Strong Style Soldiers as we recall were not there when the Brotherhood won the belts. Last week Kluge defeated Martins, so it should truly be some great action as we declare who really is the best.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “No Jak, I don’t want it.” RILEY STEVENS: “Then lastly, DREAM match time. Alioth Starre perhaps the fastest rising star of his time competes with long time GIW veteran Tony Miranda to finally crown a champion. It should be a war as both men want this badly, who you got Oliver?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “…” RILEY STEVENS: “Any how everyone it’s time to start the show! Get ready for a Breakdown!” QUOTE Right away after the break we return to see Jak Hendricks mid way through his entrance, heading through the ropes to a loud amount of boos from the crowd who are showing their distain for the man who had not garnered much in the fan department since his start in GIW. RILEY STEVENS: “Here’s your guy Oliver, a man whom could reinvent himself with a win here to become the new GIW Television Champion.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Why reinvent himself? He’s already perfect.” As Jak takes his place in the ring we take a moment before, “Pretty Messed Up” by Daisy hits as the fans yet again begin to boo as out from the back comes the small sized package Pandora looking confident. She heads down towards the ring, turning to the fans and talking a bit of trash as she heads up the steps. In the ring we can see Jak, laughing a bit to himself. RILEY STEVENS: “Pandora had her struggles her first go in GIW. Since her return, she’s taken on a new less then happy go lucky demeanor. Last week it proved to work, think it’ll help her tonight?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Yeah but lets go Jak!” Riley can be heard scoffing as Pandora steps into the ring, looking over at Hendricks who is laughing at her seeming to be less then worried about her in ring abilities. Lastly “I Don’t Want to Stop” by Ozzy Osborne hits as the fans yet again boo, it’s become clear they have no favorite in this one. As out from the back with that big smirk is Mr. Black, not seeming at all worried or in doubt about his match here tonight. RILEY STEVENS: “Sometimes, I suppose there is no good guy.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Uhhhh…Jak?” The fans continue to boo as Mr. Black saunters his way down towards the ring, looking out at them and rubbing his chin it’s clear that he’s not too worried about them. As he walks up the steps then through the ropes, as he heads to the top rope as Pandora and Jak stand in their corners. He jumps down as the referee holds up the belt, they are all after up high into the air, they all gaze at it before he hands it out and then calls for the bell. DING DING DING! Jak quickly walks between Black and Pandora, holding up a hand to Mr. Black with a grin on his face. Black sits back confused as Hendricks turns to Pandora, slapping his cheek for a kiss from Pandora. Pandora says something to him, that causes Jak to walk towards her continuing to trash talk her backing her into the corner. Out of no where she delivers a brutal slap, then quickly bounces up onto the top rope. Where she dives off landing with a Missile Dropkick to start this match off. She stands over him, as he rolls around holding his chest as she says some words to him. However showing little care for their little battle Black runs across the ring delivering a brutal Spear to Pandora that nearly rips her in half. She rolls out of the ring as Black gets up, slapping his hands together thinking he’s ridded himself of Pandora in this one. Turning around he lifts Jak up who is still struggling for air after having it kicked out of him right to start this match. He quickly whips him into the corner, following it up with a big clothesline. That drops Jak into a sitting position in the corner. Backing up he runs forward and delivers a knee that catches Hendricks hard. He pulls him out by the foot and makes the cover 1…2…Kick Out! It’s not near enough to put him away as Black gets to his feet, taking some time to think as Pandora begins to crawl back in on the outside. Jak begins to get to his feet and he seems a little dazed. Black grabs him setting up for a Suplex. Right as he’s about to deliver it, Pandora delivers a running dropkick to the back of Black’s leg. It gives out on him and it allows Jak to toss him out of the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Pandora with a smart move.” Jak turns right into a kick from Pandora that doubles him over. Running off the ropes she comes back and delivers a Running Bulldog. Flipping him over she makes a cover 1…2…Jak is able to kick out. The fans don’t know who to root for, and it’s evident by the very here and there cheers for people whom are choosing their favorite just by whom is the least evil to them. Pandora gets up, shaking off the kick out. Returning to the ring, after his fall to the outside is Mr. Black who sits in wait. Pandora turns realizing he is there and she receives a Clothesline. Quickly Black turns around and gives one to Jak, going off. As he turns it Pandora that’s back up and she gets a Dropkick almost to the face due to her height. She falls back down into the corner as he turns and plants Jak with a DDT! He turns to make the cover 1…2…Pandora drops down and breaks up the pin attempt. She grabs him by the back of the head, but he spins out of it setting her up for a DDT. She is able to squirm her way out, then jumps up for a big Jumping DDT but he is able to push her away at the last moment. Pandora ends up landing on her feet right in front of the ropes. Black runs at her but she delivers a Drop Toe Hold, right into the ropes. Black falls back turning around on one knee, Pandora runs off the ropes and catches him in the face with a Shining Wizard! It’s over, that’s it 1…2…Black Kicks Out! RILEY STEVENS: “I thought that was it.” Pandora looks at the referee confused how that was not three; not thinking Black could kick out of that maneuver. Angrily she walks over to Jak, for whom she gives a Swinging DDT! Rather then covering him she turns and walks back towards Black with bigger plans. Setting up for her finish ‘The Pandknee’ however as she’s about to hit it Black rolls out and tosses her over the top rope and to the outside with a huge thud! The crowd is stunned, seems like she might be out. Black looks at her, grinning as he turns and goes for the gold. Jak who hasn’t done a whole lot in this match, begins to struggle to get to his feet using the ropes to even do so. Black sits in wait; with a big smile on his face he realizes that this is it. Finally Jak is up and turns barely able to walk right into Black who grabs him and then hoists him up and delivers ‘The Blackout’ a Psycho Driver! The fans realize it’s over. RILEY STEVENS: “That is it, new champion.” Rather then immediately pinning him, Black chooses to motion the belt around his waist while on one knee. As he’s doing this Pandora returns to the ring, running and dropkicking him out of the ring. Right away covering Jak 1…2…3! The crowd goes into a bit of a frenzy with that little tricky maneuver by Pandora! RILEY STEVENS: “Oh wow, just like that. Pandora takes the belt by dropkicking Black out of the ring.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “She cheated! That’s unfair.” The crowd even realizes just how perhaps unfair it was as the referee quickly hands her the title as she slides out of the ring and begins to head towards the back holding the belt in the air. Black returns to the ring yelling at the referee as he then looks up at Pandora fuming as he realizes that she just cost him the belt as we head to the back. WINNER: PANDORA (New GIW Television Champion) Heading to the back we find Sarah Sharp in the interview area where she is standing talking with Trent Lost, seeming not to realize that the camera is rolling. TRENT LOST: “I just wanted to let you know, that it wasn’t me who attacked Alex on Revolt.” Lost seems genuine with his words as Sarah nods her head seeming to accept it due to his recent change in demeanor as she turns seeing that the camera is rolling and begins her question. SARAH SHARP: “Tonight Trent you have a match with former teammate and ally Marcus Hister whom personally called you out after Revolt this week. We all saw you break away from Aspen this week, how are you feeling about your match here tonight?” Lost repositions his title on his shoulder as Sarah holds the microphone over to him. TRENT LOST: “You know I am done with them, I am done being a lackey. You know I was getting sick of being tied to her. My achievements were all accredited to her. I defeated Alioth Starre and it went unnoticed because that same night, I went out and tried to help Aspen in her match and got hurt for it. I don’t need that so as far as I am concerned I can be a lone wolf now. What better way, to officially put an end to that part of my history by defeating my fellow slave to Aspen, Marcus Hister. If he thinks he can just call me out, if he has some sort of issue with the way I left the group, then he’ll realize rather quickly that the new Trent Lost is better then ever.” Lost smirks then heads on his way towards the ring where his match is next, Sarah smiles and stands there as the segment comes to a close. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = Heading else where in the back we see Pandora with her newly won belt, talking with someone on the phone when around the corner comes an angry Mr. Black walking right up to her yelling. MR. BLACK: "That’s my belt! YOU STOLE IT!" Pandora seems to be taking a second to think of what to say but before she can Mark Cross comes around stepping in front of him. MARK CROSS: "She beat you man, nothing you can do now, just calm down." MR. BLACK: "Who do you think you are? All deal with you if I have to as well." Shoving by him he heads in a different direction, going some where else to vent his frustrations as Pandora pushes Cross. PANDORA: "I don’t need your help. So, back off!" Pandora then pushes by him heading the other way as Cross seems a bit confused as to what he just put himself into as we head to the ring. QUOTE “Under The Knife” by Rise Against hits through out the arena as the fans begin to boo as out from the back comes Marcus Hister with that usual, maniacal look across his face seeming like he’s not scared in the least as he heads toward the ring for perhaps the most personal fight of the night by far. Walking by the fans, none of them show any respect but he does not care at all. RILEY STEVENS: “This man was perhaps the odd man out of that group with Aspen and Trent. But I don’t think anyone doubts how often he helped them. Branching off on his own, we’ve seen him several times be in wars in the ring. Tonight will be the most important one he’s ever been in.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I hope he teaches Trent a lesson.” Heading into the ring, Hister sits in his corner looking around at the crowd waiting as “Down” by Curse Icon hits and the fans tone turns to loud cheers. How quickly they have fallen in love with the new Trent who walks out from the back, looking down towards the ring at Hister with determination in his eyes. Readjusting his belt he heads toward the ring, looking at the crowd who are giving him their full support. Jumping up onto the ring, he slides in and then heads to the tope displaying his belt to the audience. RILEY STEVENS: “You may not think so Oliver but I think that Trent made a wise move. It was him that defeated Alioth; it was him that won that belt. He’s better off on his own.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “You know I don’t expect you to realize how important she was to him considering you’ve never been with a woman before.” Trent steps down handing his belt quickly to the referee who holds it up then hands it out to the outside, calling for the bell. DING DING DING! Right away these two men come to the center of the ring, not fighting yet. Instead the two begin to exchange words back and forth. There is no love lost in this one as Hister throws the first punch, backing Trent up. Lost answers right back with one of his own, which causes Hister to grab him by the head locking up a Headlock. Backing up Trent uses the ropes to propel him away from him. Hister bounces off and on the rebound dives down tackling Lost’s leg which seems to almost hyper extend. The fans boo as Lost falls to the mat holding his leg in pain, as Marcus goes on the attack but the referee pushes him away asking Trent “Can you continue?” Lost uses the ropes to get to a vertical base nodding his head to the referee, as Marcus seems to have just put Trent on one leg. Lost walks forward, doing so with a bit of a limp in his step. Marcus locks up with him then gives him some knee to knee contact, causing Trent to drop to one knee holding the other. Showing little care for his leg, he forcefully lifts him up and delivers a Tornado DDT! The fans are booing the entire time, as he goes for a cover with a smile on his face thinking he’s just taken the belt 1…2…Kick Out! It’s not over yet. The fans cheer Trent on as Marcus gets up, stalking him from behind. Trent uses the ropes to get up as the entire time Hister is licking his chops ready to again attack that leg. He runs at it but hearing footsteps Lost side steps him quickly and lowers the middle rope sending Hister right through them to the outside! RILEY STEVENS: “Lost may have just avoided a match ender there.” Lost grabs the ropes watching as Hister gets up on the outside. Using all he’s got in terms of jumping ability he propels himself over the ropes flying with a Crossbody to Marcus on the outside drawing another huge pop from the crowd. The referee begins to start the count, 1…2…3…Lost is now getting up, still limping and this move probably didn’t help him much either…4…5…Not wanting a count out he grabs Hister and tosses him into the ring then begins to do the same. However Marcus was not as hurt as we thought because as soon as Lost steps into the ring Hister drops him with a big Spear! The fans boo as Hister grins and then covers Lost 1…2…Kick Out! Still not enough to put away the North Beach Champion here in North Beach. Getting a bit angry Hister gets up rubbing his hands along his head as he plots what too do next. Suddenly he has thoughts as he turns and begins to glare at Lost who gets up slowly. As soon as Trent turns around Marcus jumps up for the ‘Mystery's Darkness’ an RKO! But Lost is able to push him away at the last second! The fans go wild as Marcus bounces up and walks right into Lost who lifts him up going for the ‘Lost Cause’ a Pump Handle Slam! But just as he gets him up the leg gives out, and Hister falls out of his clutches and to the mat. RILEY STEVENS: “That leg just won’t hold up.” Trent falls down to one knee as Hister gets back up, backing up then right as Trent gets up he throws a Superkick! But Trent catches it then using his good leg kicks out the other leg from underneath Hister dropping him to the mat. Then using his good leg, he quickly puts in the ‘Lost In Translation’ Indian Death Lock! The crowd goes wild as Hister, tries to break free but it’s in good. Finally he taps out, as the fans cheer on the champion. RILEY STEVENS: “He is still champion, on one good leg.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Luck.” The fans go wild as Marcus rolls out of the ring in pain as the referee hands him back his belt. Limping over to the top rope he steps up holding it high in the air for the fans here on the North Beach as we go to the parking lot after this happy scene. WINNER: TRENT LOST (Still GIW North Beach Champion) As the last match comes to a close we return to the parking lot where still sitting out there in her limo with the door is Aspen Chaud refusing to leave. The door opens and out comes Blake Beckett, as he does she pops up ready to yell at him but he speaks first. BLAKE BECKETT: “Shut up. Before you say a word, yes…you’ve been released.” Aspen looks at him like he is crazy, quickly voicing her opinion on the matter. ASPEN CHAUD: “YOU FIRED ME? I am Aspen Chaud. I have kept this company alive since the start. Without me this place would have been closed a long time ago. Do you really want this place to die? DO YOU?” Blake seems to scoff off her threats, as she brushes some hair out of her face as he answers. BLAKE BECKETT: “I’m smart enough to know that yes you’re an asset. As annoying…as stupid…and as unbearable as you have been from the start, I’ll let you back in…IF…You apologize to everyone.” ASPEN CHAUD: “I apologize. There now let me in.” Blake shakes his head laughing at her less then heart felt apology. BLAKE BECKETT: “No, no. You’re not coming in here tonight. You pose too much of a risk to our main event. So be there on Thursday…And then you can apologize…And get your job back. Now have a good night.” Blake waves and heads back inside as Aspen quickly screams an inaudible command at her driver then steps into the limo as it pulls off as we return to the ring for the tag match. Riley, I don’t think the Brotherhood stand much of a damn chance here.” RILEY STEVENS: “Perhaps not, Oliver, as Ross has quickly tagged in Matt Kluge. Things are about to get down to business here!” Kluge prepped his opponent for a quick victory. With his signature ‘The Tiger’s Roar’ in place and executed, he took the initiative for a pin. RILEY STEVENS: “One, two, and well that’s all it takes. I doubt they would have lasted longer in any other circumstance.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Hey, look, there’s the ball Pedro and his partner dropped.” RILEY STEVENS: “I’ll be honest I expected much more of an effort from the Brotherhood of Steel. Regardless the tag titles continue to jump around this time falling into the hands of Rydell and Kluge, whom seem fit to keep them.” Though the fans are a little shocked by the length or the lack there of during this match Rydell and Kluge are handed their belts. Even they seem to be disappointed with how quick this one ended. Heading out of the ring, they hold up the belts for their cheering fans. Together they slowly walk up the ring ramp after their match belts in hand, not tired. The two look back at the ring and then at the crowd. They stare for a few moments, taking all of the emotion in and finally proceed to begin to exit the arena. Before they can go back up the ramp entirely, a man with slicked back blonde hair, wearing a black jacket accompanied with black pants and sinister pitch-black sunglasses appears. Ross Rydell and Matt Kluge stop in their tracks and then look at this man for two seconds before suddenly, the lights go out. A thud can be heard. The lights go back on. Matt Kluge is on the floor and the man in all-black attire is next to Ross Rydell! RILEY STEVENS: “What is going on?” The camera zooms in on the man to reveal an emotion-deprived face. Rydell kicks the man and then punches him, dealing seemingly absolutely no damage. The man retaliates with a headbutt that brings Rydell straight to the floor instantly. Some of the members in the crowd can be seen jumping up and down, as if they are able to identify the man. The man picks up Rydell and delivers a swift European uppercut that brings Rydell back down. Matt Kluge gets back on his knees and shakes some of the cobwebs from his head. He lunges at the man with a clothesline only for the man to perform some tricks on par with the matrix, avoiding the clothesline entirely. The man kicks Kluge on the left leg once and then the right, causing Kluge’s stance to be weakened. Finally, the man kicks Kluge on the gut and within a second and almost seemingly effortlessly, the man performs a devastating Powerbomb to Kluge on the ramp, causing him to roll back down to the ring almost lifeless. Rydell gets up and tries to deliver a big boot to the man. Again, the movement of this assailant is too quick and fluid for any type of offense. Rydell doesn’t hesitate to try and throw more shots at the man but every single one is dodged. RILEY STEVENS: “This man is taking it to Rydell.” Rydell stops and begins to gasp for air, reluctantly throwing a big and reckless right hand that misses completely. The man pushes Rydell away and then does a cartwheel followed by an incredible horizontal spin in mid-air which concludes with a flying spinning kick to the side of the neck of Rydell. Rydell collapses and is essentially now an easy target. The man picks up Rydell again. A massive flurry of shots are landed on the chest and stomach region of Rydell followed by a commanding clothesline. The man looks at the carnage he has caused and begins to walk to the back. Kluge begins to get up and starts walking towards Rydell, helping him up. Once the two are up, the man quickly turns his head and sees that Rydell and Kluge are back up. The man begins to power-walk back to the two. The man goes for a clothesline on his nearest target, Rydell. Rydell ducks down, managing to dodge the maneuvers of the man. In consequence of having missed, Kluge lifts his foot and hits a big boot to the face of the man. RILEY STEVENS: “Kluge with a boot to the mystery man.” The man bounces off the boot. Rydell tries to take advantage and goes for a high drop kick. The man dodges it, causing the high drop kick to hit Kluge, sending him back to the ground. The man quickly picks up Rydell and hit’s a Powerbomb as Kluge was about to get up, eliminating Kluge entirely out of this encounter. The man picks up Rydell and hit’s the Powerbomb a second time, on the steel of the ramp that leads to the ring. Finally, the man hit’s a sit-down Powerbomb and absolutely destroys Rydell. The man looks around and is booed for the most part. RILEY STEVENS: “These men just finished a match, how pathetic is this.” Until, however, he takes off his sunglasses - Kluge can be seen opening his eyes and looking a little confused. “W-W-Wake…W-W-Walker.” He can be seen muttering. The entire crowd knows who he is now. Wake Walker had changed almost entirely to the point of not being able to be recognized. RILEY STEVENS: “I think Walker just took Kluge out of the equation.” Wake Walker stands over the decimated body of Rydell and puts back on his sunglasses. He gives no emotion and walks away. Kluge seems to be a mixture of shocked and angry as he watches as Walker leaves. Before going to check on his partner as we head to a break amidst this very barbaric scene. WINNERS: STRONG STYLE SOLDIERS [Matt Kluge & Ross Rydell] (New GIW Tag Team Champions) The Road to ‘Moment of Glory’ continues as the last stop… The video shows a town, completely wiped out. Scattered debris all over the place. A desolate area… Can only be described as a ‘Category 5’. June 26th! WHO WILL SURVIVE? As the promo comes to an end, we cut to the backstage area where a distraught Matt Kluge walks alongside Ross Rydell being carted off towards the exit; the scene is frantic as whatever is hurting him is clearly serious. They start to put him into the back of a ambulance as Sarah Sharp runs up to Matt, who is standing by as they load him up. SARAH SHARP: “Matt…Matt…Can we get your thoughts on what just happened?” She holds the microphone up to him, as he looks at her amazed she would even ask him at this time. MATT KLUGE: “What do you think my thoughts are? If Walker wants a fight he just found one. I’m better then him I always have been. I don’t know how bad Ross is. But I can assure you, that Walker is going to regret this!” Kluge quickly jumps into the ambulance with Rydell, as they close up the door and begin to drive off as the scene comes to a close. QUOTE As we return to the ring, the tron lights up as a voice over begins to play. “Sometimes…a man comes along that changes everything. Highlights of Alioth Starre begin to be shown on the tron, the entire place dark as we all watch. “Alioth Starre is the real deal.” The video quickly snaps to an end as “Subterraneans” by David Bowie hits to a load ovation from the crowd as slowly out from the back comes Alioth Starre, looking all business as heads to the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “This man has risen to the top so quickly Oliver. Not often do we see a guy win a belt as fast as he did around here. Now he’ll have a chance to win the ultimate prize, the GIW Undisputed Championship.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “If I even understood him one forth of the time, I’d have something to say. But I don’t.” Starre enters the ring, stretching out his back as he walks to the other side of the ring heading to what will now be his corner as again the lights go out and the tron lights up with the same voice over man talking. “Heart…It’s not something you can measure. It’s not something you can count. However it is the most important part of any warrior.” The video proceeds to show Tony tapping out to Sparrow, then getting the double count out again Aspen. “It’s the will to always get back up, no matter if you’ve been knocked down.” The video then shows Tony fighting back, hitting several of his signature maneuvers “Tony Miranda is…Heart. The video ends and “The Human Stain" by Kamelot hits as the fans go insane yet again. RILEY STEVENS: “This man has been here from the start. He’s won several titles, always been right there in the Main Event. Some bad luck here, mostly coinciding with Aspen Chaud have kept him from the belt. Now more then ever, he’ll have to show his heart against Alioth Starre.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Heart is just a nice way of saying he’s been beaten. I admire that he keeps getting up, but the man needs to quit.” RILEY STEVENS: “I don’t think that’s in his arsenal.” The lights come back on as Tony walks out from the back, slowly punching his closed fist into his other hand heading towards the ring high fiving a few fans on the way before heading up the steps and into the ring. Looking across from him at his opponent here tonight in Alioth Starre. RILEY STEVENS: “We’ve been waiting a long time to see these two men go one on one. They are two the most deserving men of this show. However only one, can be the Undisputed Champion.” The referee in the ring is presented the title by a person at ring side. Carrying it into the ring, both men have their eyes on the prize. Holding it up we see it with no name plate yet engraved on it, a champion will be crowned. The referee hands it back out of the ring, looking at both men, as you can hear a pen drop in this place everyone realizing the stakes as the referee calling for the bell breaks the silence. DING DING DING! In this one it is a Mixed Martial Artist against a Wrestling Technician. Both men begin to circle around one another as easily the strongest emotional vibe can be felt through the crowd. Some Alioth fans, some Tony fans. Regardless there is not a person in the crowd that does not have goose bumps and if they don’t, then they just aren’t human. As the two men begin to circle around one another, Alioth goes right in for a punch but Tony catches locking in a Wrist Lock! The fans begin to cheer as he pulls it around; tugging it tight as Starre quickly reverses a move that he himself uses frequently. Almost like the two don’t want to be one upped, Alioth connects with a Spinning Roundhouse Kick that sends Tony falling back into the ropes. The two men yet again begin to circle each other, using each other’s move set’s not wanting to show any weaknesses. Finally it’s time to see which man is stronger, as surprisingly Alioth offers up for a Test of Strength. Tony accepts the offer, locking one hand and then the next. Right away Miranda begins to back him up, pushing him back into the corner. The fans in favor of, Tony are cheering for him as Alioth bounces up onto the ropes then breaks free of one hand and then jumps off Arm Dragging Tony across the ring. Oddly Tony is up first, quickly wrapping himself around Alioth who got up with his back turned to him. Yet again, Alioth is able to spin out of it and get behind him now. Not afraid to throw the elbows Tony catches him with one, then another and it causes Alioth to break his hold of him. Miranda turns and then runs at him landing a Big Boot to the face! Alioth drops to the mat and quickly Tony drops an elbow onto the back of the head! Tony makes the cover 1…Kick Out! Not even close. RILEY STEVENS: “I can’t imagine either man is going to be finished for awhile here. We are going to have a classic on our hands.” Tony gets up, not showing too much worry about that not finishing him. As he prepares his arsenal for whatever he should do next. Quickly he decides as Starre who is doubled over, is wide open for a Running Knee. But Alioth may have just been suckering him in as at the last second Alioth moves his head back and then rolls him up 1…2…Kick Out! Tony nearly got caught but quickly he gets to a standing position, as Starre does as well both men having hit a stale mate thus far into the match. Miranda decides to make the first move, going in on the attack only to out of no where get locked right into a Chin Lock! The fans cheer as Alioth tugs on it, showing off his technical prowess. RILEY STEVENS: “Does Tony even know how to tap out?” Apparently he does not as he is able to break the hold by ripping Alioth off of him. Starre takes an uncharacteristic wild shot at Tony, that he ducks. Then immediately he throws and delivers a Rolling Wheel Kick! The fans that are for him go wild as he goes for a cover 1…Kick Out! That’s not going to do it. He gets up shaking his head as Alioth gets up, shaking off the cob webs as Tony comes at him, as he delivers a brutal European Uppercut that drops him to the mat. Quickly he drops with a Fist Drop, and then makes a cover 1…Kick Out! These moves though effective will never be enough to put either man down for the count. RILEY STEVENS: “Both men at this point are wondering just what they’ll have to do.” For Alioth it seems clear as soon as Tony is up, he grabs him and locks in the Reverse Chinlock! The fans go wild for Alioth whom has it seemingly locked in well. Tony struggles to break free as the people realize that this thing may just be over. Miranda pulls on his hands, hoping to break free from the hold. But it does not seem to be letting up. The referee waits for Tony to tap, when suddenly he seems to realize the stakes and rips Starre’s off then spins around and delivers a Tiger Suplex Pin out of no where! 1…2…Kick Out! Tony’s fans gasp thinking that was it. Both men get up, Tony is up first. Both at once they run at each other only to deliver simultaneous clotheslines dropping down to the mat right beside one another! RILEY STEVENS: “Both men are down, who will be up first?” The fans are going wild for both men as they wait to see who will be up first. Instead it is both men, slowly getting up to their feet at the same time. Each of them seeming to have taken a toll on the other with their past few months. Tony is the first to throw a punch as seemingly half the crowd cheers! Alioth answers back with a punch of his own, the other half cheers! This time Tony throws a bigger punch, his half roars. Alioth answers back with a European Uppercut that sends him stumbling back. Then he explodes forward and lands a Running Leg Lariat out of no where! The fans go nuts. Seeming a bit tired, he takes a second to climb on top 1…2…Kick Out! RILEY STEVENS: “He kicked out of the Leg Lariat!” Tony’s fans look to one another in shock as Alioth’s go wild with cheers because there man is not out of it yet. Tony gets to a knee looking around at the crowd drawing to them the strength to finish this thing off. At the end of the day he knows what move he needs to deliver to end this. It was plain and simple. Getting up to his feet, as Alioth does the same seeming a bit dazed. Tony unwilling to wait any longer grabs him and then lifts him high into the air! The fans are going nuts as he sets it up, it’s readied. But as he begins his run, Alioth is able to slip out of his grasps and push himself over Tony’s shoulders. Tony turns and throws a right that Alioth ducks then drops him down and right away locks in the Figure Four Leg Lock! OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Say goodnight.” RILEY STEVENS: “Can he get out of this?” The crowd in favor of Alioth is going wild as Tony seems to be clinching up in pain trying to scoot his way towards the ropes. But to little avail, hoping to get there he continues to use all of his strength. Suddenly he stops, perhaps ready to give up. Ready to tap out, as he’s about half way to the ropes! The fans are going wild for this huge moment as suddenly Tony jolts up and bridges up and basically crawls backwards to the ropes, grabbing them! Alioth breaks the hold. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Wow.” RILEY STEVENS: “Tony got to the ropes.” Getting up, he uses the ropes his legs not the same after that move as he turns walking towards Alioth. However, without full energy Alioth immediately drops him back down and locks in the Figure Four Leg Lock a second time this time he’s stranded in the middle of the ring! The fans are at a stand still, will he tap out? He has no way to go. Without the energy to get to the ropes, Tony seems to be in a no win situation. However, unwilling to tap out Tony instead chooses to fight through the pain! Almost in a terrible predicament the referee urges him to tap out before he seriously hurts himself. His eyes on the belt Alioth, continues to put the pressure on as due to pain Tony passes out and the referee calls for the bell. RILEY STEVENS: “New Champion! New Champion!” The crowd goes wild as the fans of Alioth explode into cheers. Starre breaks the hold, getting to his feet. The referee hands him the belt, then goes to tend to Tony who seems to be coming to. RILEY STEVENS: “Alioth Starre has risen to the top. Forgetting our past champion we all now start a new era, Alioth Starre is your Undisputed Champion! No slight to Tony, who would never quit.” Clenching the belt to his chest, Starre seems to be gazing at himself through the gold plate or perhaps looking at the place where his name will soon go. With the referee’s help Tony is up. Pushing the referee away he walks over to Alioth, whom graciously gives him a hand shake seeming to have mutual respect. Tony exits the ring, just as he does we watch as confetti is shot down onto the champion. RILEY STEVENS: “What a night! The only champion that kept his belt was the one whose title fit our location here tonight. New Television Champion…New Tag Team Champions…The Return of Wake Walker…And last but not least a new Undisputed Champion. Tune in Thursday as we head to ‘Category 5’.” Back in the ring the confetti drapes over our new champion, Alioth Starre whom has fallen to his knees holding the belt as the show slowly fades to a conclusion. WINNER: ALIOTH STARRE (New GIW Undisputed Champion)
