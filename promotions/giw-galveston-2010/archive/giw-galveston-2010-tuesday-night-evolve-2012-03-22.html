Tuesday Night Evolve, 22 March 2012

22 March 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: EVOLVE LIVE! From the Baton Rouge River Center in Baton Rouge, LA – March 22, 2012 QUOTE In typical fashion, Adonis Ethier, Jester, and Emily Claiborne gathered in the ring for the open battle royal that every has come to know and love. Many of the GIW greats have gone through this process and the three in the ring hope to have the same success. RILEY STEVENS: “Three new stars here tonight trying to make a name for themselves.” DING DING DING! The bell sounded and we’re off. Emily, Jester, and Adonis circle each other. The two men in the match may not like each other or respect each other but they quickly see fit to create an alliance as the two close in on Emily who was backed into the corner. Jester throws a wild punch but Emily ducks only to be met by a clothesline from Adonis. The two stomp away on the much smaller Emily, which in turn gets the fans to erupt into a chorus of, boos. Adonis Ethier eats it up as he mocks the fans. Jester continues the beat down, now down on his knees connecting with shot after shot to poor Emily. RILEY STEVENS: “Figures these two would do a two on one beat down on the poor girl.” Adonis quickly grabs Jester by the hair, which in reality signals the end of their “alliance”. Adonis attempts to throw him over the top but Jester is able to hit an elbow to the chest of Ethier before he can make it across the ring. The two exchange words, Jester seemingly angry at the plan being disbanded. Jester shoves Adonis and in retaliation, Adonis hits a beautiful standing dropkick. Jester tumbles backwards and Emily using her smarts lowers the top rope and Jester crashes out of the ring. Emily Claiborne picks up the elimination with a little help from Adonis. JESTER IS ELIMINATED The final two meet in the center of the ring and Adonis strikes first, rocking Emily with a right hand. He tries another but Emily is able to catch it and from out of nowhere is able to hit a picture perfect tornado DDT. Adonis able to get to his feet a few moments later and shake off the cobwebs but Emily is right back on the hunt, hitting multiple forearms. Adonis looks rocked from the shots. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Ethier toss her over already!” Adonis throws a clothesline widely but Emily ducks, bounces off the ropes, and nails a huge lariat. Adonis is down and Emily realizes her chance. She rushes over and attempts to pick up her fallen prey. Adonis pushes her back and attempts one more huge running clothesline but Emily side steps. Using Adonis momentum against him, she sends him over the top rope, picking up the win in the process. JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…Emily Claiborne!” The fans cheer her on as she jumps up wildly in the ring her excitement taking over her as the fans join in along with her. RILEY STEVENS: “Emily finds a way to get the win here tonight to start off her GIW career right.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Every dog has it’s day.” She rushes out of the ring heading to the barricade to celebrate with the crowd, as we head to a quick Revolt preview. WINNER: EMILY CLAIBORNE QUOTE As we return back to the ring we now have the ring again filled with three stars Big J, Eliza Gray, and Mya Denton ready to go for tonight’s Evolve main event. RILEY STEVENS: “These three are trying to get back into Managemen’t good graces with a win here tonight.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “We shall see what they can do.” DING DING DING! Big J headed straight for Maya Denton as once the bell rang, hitting her with a Samoan Drop that rattled the entire ring, but Eliza Gray quickly exited the ring for safety looking on. Big J forced his attention back to Denton who was in a crumpled heap as it seemed as if he wasn’t done yet. With his back turned, J was jumped from behind by Eliza Gray who had hopped on his back trying to apply a Sleeper Hold but that didn’t last very long as J dismounted Gray as he shifted his body weight throwing her forward releasing her grip she thudded to the canvas trying to crawl away. As if she could get equal footing Eliza ran for the hills, quickly Big J stabbed her in the knee with a boot before grasping onto her hair with a tightened overly sadistic grip upon the lovely locks of Eliza Gray. RILEY STEVENS: “Big J dominant thus far.” Burying a knee into Eliza’s gut he doubled her over without much effort, she looked to be bellowing out in pain as Big J set her up shoving her leg between his legs he paused looking around arena as demented as he could be. Lifting her up without much effort J finally tossed her up giving the fallen Eliza enough even momentum to nail the coarse DDT he always preformed right after the jackknife like toss into the air he often simonized the move as the “Buzzkiller DDT” a move that you weren’t going to wake up from any time soon. RILEY STEVENS: “Big J bouncing back in a big way.” Maya was stirring but not for long as J wouldn’t admire his handy work on Eliza for long, setting Maya up just as he had Eliza only mere seconds ago he preformed the same adding insult to injury already with that Samoan Drop from earlier it seemed that Big J didn’t care who you were you were going to be flattened without question. J was egoistical enough to pull both ladies on top of one another planting a foot onto Maya’s chest and the Referee forced to count…1…2…3! JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…Big J!” The fans give him a mixed reaction as the referee raises his arm in victory as he stands talk after a very dominant performance. RILEY STEVENS: “Big showing from Big J here tonight, very impressive.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Women…” With that Evolve starts to face with Big J being the last thing we see as we now start to the wait for Revolt. WINNER: BIG J
