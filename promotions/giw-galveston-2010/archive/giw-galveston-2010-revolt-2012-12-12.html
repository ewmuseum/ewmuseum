Revolt, 12 December 2012

GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: WEDNESDAY NIGHT REVOLT LIVE! At Galveston’s North Beach – December 12, 2012 on GIW.com As the show starts we get another segment from the backstage area with three stars standing in the backstage area, all three very different wrestlers only sharing one thing in common at this point that being that they are in the lead in this first to five race, each already with two wins. Standing at both ends are Ace Baldwin and Ethan Wrathmore and wedged in between them is Wake Walker as Zachary Pagonis enters from the right of the screen and as the camera pans out a bit we can see Rayne standing alongside Wrathmore on the edge as Zachary approaches Walker first. ZACHARY PAGONIS: “Wake, last week you got to two wins however you did get attacked by Alioth Starre after the match. With a budding rivalry coming, many people think you may not be focused on the belt rather, Alioth Starre. Your thoughts?” Walker smiles at Pagonis the two of them sharing time last week, and clearly becoming a bit close as Wake leans in to respond. WAKE WALKER: “You know Alioth is trying to scare me but he can’t, I don’t worry about him because he doesn’t scare me. I know that tonight I can get to three wins and that’s more then he can say. I am not the slight bit worried about him because he’s not standing up here with me right now. He’s not one of the leaders, he’s just another guy and if I get the chance to beat him sooner rather then later so be it, I am looking forward to it. He can be my forth or my fifth win, I don’t really mind.” Walker shrugs his shoulders showing his lack of real fear in Alioth as he stands back calmly as Zachary now heads over to Ethan. ZACHARY PAGONIS: “Ethan, you have really burst on the scene here the past few weeks and currently are the only one standing here that has yet to hold the Undisputed Championship. A lot of people believe you may fold under the pressure, that you may not have the experience these guys have, what do you have to say about that?” Wrathmore turns around chuckling as he looks over at Rayne, clearly showing no signs of fear just a supreme amount of confidence. ETHAN WRATHMORE: “As far as I am concerned these two had their shots and both of them blew it. I am the future of this company, I am the new generation. You know I keep having to hear about the Ace’s, the Wake’s, the Alioth’s all these people. They keep treating me like I don’t have a chance but frankly I am the best thing going for this company right now, I am the star of the show. Tonight Ace is about to find that out the hard way, I am not only going to get my third win I am going to make a statement to you…” Wrathmore walks in front of the group, pointing at Wake as Rayne follows him as he then takes a step looking at Ace with a smug grin. ETHAN WRATHMORE: “And…you!” With that Wrathmore gives Ace a shove back into the wall but as Baldwin heads back his way Wrathmore steps back with Rayne in his path as Ace stops the punch attempt mid-swing to avoid hitting her as Wrathmore backs down the hall with a grin Rayne following him as Ace shouts out at him. ACE BALDWIN: “Tonight! TONIGHT!” The two having a stare down as Wake watches on as we head from the backstage area down to the ring for the first match of the night. QUOTE Heading down to the commentary both the crowd is rocking for the night’s action as Riley and Oliver get us kicked off here tonight. RILEY STEVENS: “What a show we have tonight ladies and gentleman as we have some HUGE matches with Wake Walker battling Jeremy Baltimore to get to his third win, Alioth Starre taking on Alexis Terry who hopes to get revenge for his attack last week, the GIW North Beach Championship is on the line in a five pack challenge and then nothing bigger then the huge main event we have in store tonight. Somebody is walking away with three wins, as Ace Baldwin and Ethan Wrathmore go head to head.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Did you see the size of that guy?” Oliver points into the ring as the camera pans over where as usual the participants for the Open Battle Royal have crowded the ring. Adrian Ares and E.G. Deal stand together in the corner both staring up at the beast of a man, The Dark Angel a presence that seems to be shocking to nearly everyone in attendance here tonight. RILEY STEVENS: “Every once in awhile we get monsters roaming into these things…” The Dark Angel stares down at the two of them both seeming to want to team up on this big guy as the fans wait to see if he’s the real deal or just a tall guy with no coordination as the bell rings. DING DING DING! Right off the bat Deal and Ares rush across the ring at Angel hoping to take him down only for Dark Angel to hit a Throat Thrust Uppercut to Deal then one Ares as well. Ares stumbles back and Deal rushes full steam back at him only for Angel to hoist him up and put him down with a Sidewalk Slam. E.G. rolls around in the ring in pain as Ares takes his second shot throwing a big haymaker that is caught by Angel, as he shakes his head then hoists him up seeming to not even have a slight problem doing so as he drops him down head first into the corner as he bounces off the ropes and nails him with a big boot dropping him nearly making him do a flip in the mean time with a move he calls, ‘Texas Two Step’. E.G. is back up now but even he seems to realize that this is not his day as he rushes towards the ropes literally trying to eliminate himself only for Angel to grab him by the legs as he’s about over dragging him back into the ring. As he pulls him back in, he has him set up from behind as he hits the ‘Deadhorse Deadlevel’ a Full Nelson ‘Bubba’ Bomb that shakes the ring and sends Deal flying. RILEY STEVENS: “This guy knows he’s suppose to throw them over right?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “He’s sending a message Riley a loud…scary…message.” Deal falls into the corner not really moving as Adrian gets back to his feet, trying to talk Angel down as he corners him hoping to just get out of a beat down as Angel reaches forward grabbing him around the throat, but Ares battles out of it by slamming down on his hand in desperation mode but as he does he gets hit with another Throat Thrust Uppercut that sends him back. Deal is back up only just slightly stumbling his way across the ring as Adrian stumbles out of the corner as Angel grabs both around the throat, hoisting them both up for the ‘South Texas Deathride’ a Chokeslam From Hell! Both men are down and out as Angel apparently done with them hoists up Adrian and gives him a toss out of the ring like a sack of potatoes then walks back over grabbing Deal and sending him over right with him to end this match. REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner…THE DARK ANGEL!” These fans are in awe as for the second straight week the Open Battle Royal has produced a beast of a human being, this one coming in the form of a seven foot giant known as The Dark Angel. The referee slides out to attend to Deal and Ares while Dark Angel stares out at the crowd, the fans really seemingly stunned into silence. RILEY STEVENS: “You know this Open Battle Royal is generally supposed to produce new stars but tonight I have a feeling it just produced a wrecking ball that everyone in GIW better look out for.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I don’t care if he did start late, this man may be the new favorite in the Race to Five!” He’s impressive no doubt as finally he takes his exit stepping out of the ring heading to the back with the crowd silenced as we head to the backstage area for more. WINNER: THE DARK ANGEL Backstage we go with Victoria Rio getting the tough challenge of interviewing a very focused Alioth Starre tonight as he hardly seems focused on the interview rather on something entirely different. VICTORIA RIO: “Alioth, last week you attacked Alexis Terry to seemingly send a message to Wake Walker however many people are wondering if you are really focused on the belt rather getting Walker?” Rio holds the microphone over to Alioth waiting for an answer as he looks from the mic over to her giving her nothing such. ALIOTH STARRE: “…” Giving ample time for him to respond she finally gives up on him answering pulling away the mic and again trying a different question. VICTORIA RIO: “Will you yet again try sending a message to Wake?” Rio leans over holding the microphone to Alioth but this time he doesn’t even give her the light of day instead he walks off the camera, giving her no real hints and not showing his hand tonight whatsoever as the show goes on. QUOTE It is now time for one of the highly anticipated matches of the night as we head to the top of the ramp and after a moment of waiting, "I Can't Keep Still" by KPM Music hits and the crowd pops with cheers as Jeremy Baltimore heads out from the back looking all business as usual as he heads down the ramp to the ring looking eager to get this one started and put that loss last week, in the past. RILEY STEVENS: “Baltimore is only eighteen years old and really has to be considered a top shelf prospect around here, a win tonight would put him right back in the hunt for that Undisputed belt.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I don’t like him at all Riley, he’s a cocky little youngin’.” Baltimore steps through the ropes into the ring looking over at the crowd giving them a nod as the crowd goes quiet until “Dazed and Confused” by Led Zeppelin hits and they get even louder now as the man who has really come back like a ball of fire Wake Walker heads out from the back with a grin on his face walking towards the ring, walking down the ramp letting the fans pat him on the back as he looks at Baltimore who probably looks like a three right about now, a three that would be his third win. RILEY STEVENS: “Walker has come back like a changed man the past few weeks and the wins have been coming with it as he has the chance to be the first to three tonight.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Please no.” Walker heads into the ring looking across at Baltimore these two sharing a common goal knowing that only one can get what he wants as the referee lets them make their final preparations until he steps in and rings the bell. DING DING DING! As this one starts off the two begin to circle around each other as these fans are behind Walker firmly though again there is a section or two rooting for Baltimore for the second week in a row as Jeremy faces a former champion. The two head into a lock up to start this thing off as these two, sharing the same height and almost the same body type begin to push back and forth each trying to get the upper hand, but Baltimore showing his youth and his energy begins to push him back against the ropes finally pushing him against them then whipping him off the ropes. On the rebound Baltimore delivers a big shoulder check to drop Walker to the mat as Baltimore hits the ropes coming off them quickly and taking a swing at Walker with a big clothesline that Wake the veteran ducks under. Jeremy not giving up explodes off the other ropes but runs right into a Clothesline from Walker that gets these people on their feet as Walker gets up looking around nodding his head telling them to get to their feet as he walks over to Baltimore hoisting him up off the mat then hoisting him up over him then dropping him down harshly with a Powerslam dropping down for a early cover 1…Kick Out, Jeremy is having none of that. RILEY STEVENS: “If Baltimore has showed us anything, it’s that he will not go down easy.” Walker gets back to his feet not seeming discouraged in the slightest as he watches as Jeremy gets back to his feet. Grabbing him he pushes him between his legs looking to deliver the hammer as he hoists him up into Powerbomb position only for Baltimore to throw a couple punches while he’s up there causing Walker to drop him down to the mat where Baltimore connects with a kick to the stomach that doubles over Wake as he then hits a Spinning DDT that connects perfectly putting Walker down as Baltimore takes his turn to get that pin 1…Kick Out. These two are just going through the motions at this point really both seeming to know that they aren’t going to put each other away yet, both having enough respect for each other to know that. Baltimore gets up first then hoists up Wake as well deciding to keep on the offense hoisting him up for a Bridging Northern Lights Suplex, 1…Kick Out. Still nothing as Baltimore gets up to his feet, this time looking to go for the kill as he hoists up Walker and delivers the ‘X-Factor’ a Sit-Out Powerbomb and surprisingly it is landed as the crowd goes crazy, however the move causes Walker to roll out of the ring flopping to the outside. RILEY STEVENS: “That move may have been it, he needs to get him back in the ring!” Jeremy seems to realize it as well as he scurries out of the ring trying to hoist up Walker who at this point is dead weight after the vicious Powerbomb connected with him landing on the back of his neck. The referee starts the count now as Jeremy gets him to a vertical base then hoists him up onto the side of the ring, as he pushes him under the ropes back into the ring as he slides back in at the count of four jumping on top of Walker 1…2…Kick Out! These fans go wild as Walker kicks out, the bit of time to recover enough to allow him to kick out as Baltimore only shows concern for a moment before dusting it off knowing exactly how to put this one away as he waits for Walker to get up hoisting him up onto his shoulders but before he can do anything Walker elbows his way off, as Baltimore withstands it for a bit until about the tenth elbow causes him to drop him down as Wake wastes no time tossing him over his shoulder with a German Suplex that makes the crowd go crazy. RILEY STEVENS: “Can he make the comeback?” Walker gets up to his feet shaking off Baltimore’s Powerbomb as Jeremy gets back up holding his back walking right into a punch to the chest followed by another then another finally reaching six as Jeremy favors his stomach as Walker delivers a vicious Uppercut that stuns him long enough for Wake to drop him down between his legs, hoisting him up then putting him down with the ‘Lethal Injection’ a Sit-Down Powerbomb! These fans know it’s over as the referee makes the count 1…2…3! REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner by way of pinfall…WAKE WALKER!” These fans go wild as Walker drops back to his bed clearly showing some signs of fatigue after this war with Baltimore as he crawls to a knee then up to his feet as the referee hoists up his arm in victory. RILEY STEVENS: “Another win for Wake in this hard fought war, getting him to three.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Hope he’s ready for Wrathmore to soon join him there.” Walker reaches down pulling Baltimore to his feet as Jeremy shrugs it off upset he lost, even though he came so close. Walker nods his head and then extends his hand for a shake as Jeremy looks at it for a moment then shakes it, before rolling out of the ring still frustrated as Walker soaks in another victory on our way to the back. WINNER: WAKE WALKER In the backstage area we get a glimpse of Daniel Starr with his North Beach title walking through the halls looking around in anger looking for someone to complain to as usual. DANIEL STARR: “I’m the champion, this isn’t fair! I DESERVE BETTER not to face a bunch of losers.” As he says this he walks right in Kyle Karnage who shakes his head standing up from where he was sitting, as he begins to approach Starr who quickly turns away to get away only to run into the brick wall that is Jack Redd. He stares up at him as he shakes his head as Redd just glares back at him as Starr quickly rushes around the corner to get away but trips over the leg of Kassandra Kayne who is leaning against the wall as he drops his belt, then scurries to pick it up walking away from all three screaming back down at them. DANIEL STARR: “You guys are nothing, this belt is mine!” He scurries off to the ring for the next match as the camera cuts back to Kyle Karnage, Jack Redd, and Kassandra Kayne watching him go then turning to each other, all of them realizing that the others are the real threats as a video begins to play hyping up the North Beach title match. QUOTE After a very long video really displaying the vast differences between our competitors in this title match tonight, we have all five of them packing the ring ready for some action. Each standing in a corner we have Daniel Starr in the middle of the ring walking around showing off his North Beach title to each of the competitors seeming to have forgotten all about the situation he is in tonight as all four glare back at him. RILEY STEVENS: “Well I don’t want to be the guy that says it, but I think tonight is the last night we ever see Starr holding that belt.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Starr is champion for a reason, he’s a great wrestler…the face of GIW!” Starr hands over his belt over to the referee after a few seconds of fight as the referee displays it for the crowd and the wrestlers finally starting the contest off for this one. DING DING DING! This match gets going and immediately Starr makes himself public enemy number one as he looks from one to the next telling them he’s the champion until finally all four challengers collapse on him in the center of the ring crushing him with blows as he falls to the mat and gets nearly stomped into the ring by all four competitors as he gets back up with some fight getting a punch from Kassandra then one from Kyle finally clotheslined over the top rope by Redd to get him out of the way as the rest of the competitors now take a second before splitting off into two separate battles Karnage and Kayne going into a war as Redd tosses Douglas back into the corner with ease. These fans are having troubles keeping track of it all as they have to keep their eyes peeled as the first pin will be the win. Knowing that it’ll be near impossible to do anything with the ring full Redd nails Jeffrey with a Violent Clothesline over the ropes sending him to the outside with Starr, to a huge thud. On the other side of the ring Kayne rolls through on top of Karnage 1…Kyle rolls through so he is on top 1…Kayne kicks him off as he flips right into the waiting arms of Redd who tears at him with a Bearhug trying to squeeze the life out of him. RILEY STEVENS: “Redd is one of the most powerful men I have ever seen in a GIW ring.” Jack continues to tear him back and forth until Kayne takes both of them out kicking out his legs from under him dropping him back with Karnage right on top as he makes the cover 1…Kayne quickly wastes no time kicking him off as the fans cheer on this early action. Kassandra tosses Karnage over into the corner just as she does Redd returns to his feet as she moves in for a Discus Elbow Smash connecting hard on Redd but still not knocking the big man off his feet. Starr jumps back onto the ring apron, as Karnage runs along the ropes connecting with a Clothesline to send him right back out of it. Kayne follows up the shot on Redd with a big elbow to the face again, this time she tosses him back into the corner where she thought Karnage was. Redd hits the corner as Kayne goes to head across the ring for him but Karnage steps in her path and hoists her up smashing her down with a huge Spinebuster that gets the crowd hyped up as Kyle gets up calling for the fans to get into it as Douglas rolls back into the ring and runs at him as he hits a second Spinebuster! With two people laid to waste he doesn’t see Redd coming at him from behind as Jack nails him with a Big Boot as soon as he thinks to look the boot sending him across the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “This action is hard to keep up with.” The fans are getting into it as well all picking sides in this one, none with Starr who again tries to get back in the ring but this time its Redd who grabs him on the side of the ring with a Double Handed Chokebomb over the ropes then out of the ring again as Daniel hits the mat hard unable to even get back in the ring for this match but as soon as Redd turns around he gets smacked in the chin with a huge Superkick! The move stuns Jack as Karnage hits the ropes and then follows it up with a Clothesline from Hell that drops him down to the mat as like a well oiled team Kayne jumps up onto the ropes for a Springboard Moonsault, landing flawlessly on top as she makes the cover 1…2-Karnage pulls her using the trunks off of Redd as he tosses her off the ropes as she comes back going for a Sliding Lariat as Kyle ducks under it and the move ends up connecting flush with Douglas sending him to the mat hard, though he quickly rolls out of the ring falling out as well. RILEY STEVENS: “Who can get the opening long enough to get the three count?” Karnage wastes no time jumping forward going for a ‘Hellbound’ a Double Knee Backbreaker! However Kayne reaches forward grabbing a hold of the ropes, to stop the move. Turning around getting a running start Kayne goes after Karnage with a clothesline but the two of them end up going over the ropes out of the ring together both landing awkwardly as Starr finally slides back into the ring looking around like that was a victory in itself as he raises his arms until he turns around right into an awaiting Redd as he hoists him up ready to finish this thing off as he hits the ‘South Texas Deathride’ a Running Throwing Crucifix Powerbomb! These fans go crazy sensing the end as Douglas is out and Karnage and Kayne are still recovering as Redd hooks the leg 1…2…3! New Champ! REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner by way of pinfall and NEW GIW North Beach Champion…JACK REDD!” These fans go crazy already seeming to grow fond of Jack Redd but even more so they cheer the end of the Daniel Starr reign as champion as there is a new champion here in GIW that man being known as Jack Redd, these fans going crazy. RILEY STEVENS: “Kayne and Karnage did so well but there battle took them out of the ring long enough for Redd to capitalize on the weak link that just so happened to be the champion, however he is no longer champion. That title now belongs to Jack Redd!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Whatever.” The realization now seems to set in for Karnage and Kayne on the outside who lost track of the bigger picture for just a moment and it cost them as the referee hands over the belt to Jack Redd who simply tosses it over his shoulder like he was expecting it the whole time as while the race for a new Undisputed Champion continues, we now have a champion in Jack Redd. WINNER: JACK REDD (New GIW North Beach Champion) In the backstage area we have Aspen Chaud standing at her desk looking down at her desk already with Ace Baldwin in the room, leaning against her bookshelf seemingly talking to himself at this point. ACE BALDWIN: “You do realize you are going to have to put that belt around my waist right?” Baldwin says it but Aspen doesn’t even look up from her desk trying her best to ignore him as Ace picks up a book in the shelf flipping through it then pushing it back in where it was. ACE BALDWIN: “I know you don’t want to admit it but I am your biggest star, go ahead...Just tell me.” Baldwin seems to be enjoying rubbing it in Aspen’s face a bit, that he has to this point really been her big star but as he is talking Ethan Wrathmore walks through the door looking over at Ace who stops what he’s doing to make eye contact with him. ETHAN WRATHMORE: “Biggest star? You’ve got to be kidding me. Aspen knows that I’m her guy, I’m her champion. I am the one that’s going to be making her a lot of money, all you are…Is the guy that I have to beat to get there.” While these two share a little stare down Aspen has yet to even give a glance at the two as the two continue to bicker back and forth until finally she pokes her head up yelling at them. ASPEN CHAUD: “Shut up! You guys fight tonight, that’s all that matters. One of you gets three wins, the other stays at two. So will you please get out of my office!” The stresses of the job continuing to get to Aspen as she screams loudly at the two ending their little back and forth as the two stop their argument, Wrathmore shaking his head and walking out as Ace takes a little longer winking at Aspen before he heads out the door as well as the camera zooms in on Aspen breathing heavily, seeming more and more stressed by the week as she rolls her eyes and gets back to work as the show goes on. QUOTE Back from commercial break we are shown some highlights from the backstage assault Alioth had done on Alexis Terry then again the one after the match with the Figure Four as the video ends we see Alexis in the ring wearing a big knee brace as she leans against the ropes holding up her hands in the air for the fans who cheer her on but as her music fades “I'm Afraid of Americans” by David Bowie as the lights go out and an ‘A’ appears on the ramp, a few moments later Alioth Starre heads through that ‘A’ as the lights return as he heads into the ring glaring at Alexis the entire time. RILEY STEVENS: “You know Alexis has to be looking for some revenge after the attack last week but one has to wonder if she’s fully healed or not.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “She’s not Riley, you saw her limping to the ring. She’s not a very good actor.” Terry glares back at him as the two former allies have gotten caught up in this little feud due to Alioth’s messages to Walker as Terry continues to hold onto the ropes as Starre enters the ring looking across at her shaking his head as the referee calls for the bell. DING DING DING! Starre stands on the other side of the ring as he calls for Terry to come at him, as Alexis looks down at her knee trying her best to keep it hidden just how hurt she is as she walks towards him doing a pretty good job until she gets about two feet away and she stumbles on the knee right in front of him as Alioth looks down at her, seeming to be showing little pity. However to most people’s surprise he offers her a hand helping her to her feet as Terry takes the help seeming a bit taken aback by it as perhaps he really did just use her to send a message however Alioth shows his true colors quickly pushing her back then hitting a ‘Stardust’ a Facebreaker Knee Smash! These fans boo him relentlessly as Alexis falls to the mat stunned and dazed though not completely out but she is completely vulnerable as he drops down for the same move that helped hurt her last week this time at least in a match as he ruthlessly gets the ‘Four Star Mary’ a Figure Four Leglock locked in, a move that draws the tap from Terry after only seconds of trying to fight it. REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner by way of submission…ALIOTH STARRE!” These fans are not happy as Starre gets his second win in the same amount of matches trying to get back into the mix. However rather then release the hold Alioth keeps a hold of it tugging it harder and harder as Alexis continues to tap out her knee feeling the pain from her injury as the referee tries his best to pull Alioth off, but unable. RILEY STEVENS: “This is too much.” The move continues to do its damage but soon enough the crowd goes crazy with cheers as Wake Walker comes out from the back making a b-line for the ring. Starre hears the crowd and seems to know what’s going on as he releases the hold but he abandons it too late as Walker is already in the ring tackling him down to the mat looking for revenge as he drops punch after punch down on Alioth in the ring, as Starre kicks him away and grabs the ropes rolling under them as Walker misses on his attempt to grab him before he can get away. RILEY STEVENS: “I’m not sure what these two guys have against each other, but the hatred is strong and frankly I can’t wait until we get to see them go one on one.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Starre is in his head Riley, he’s in his head.” Starre walks up the ramp not worried about an attack just going on with his day as Walker heads over to Alexis helping her up to her feet as he tells the referee to get some help out here as that takes us to the backstage area. WINNER: ALIOTH STARRE In the backstage area Jack Redd has just gotten his belt back as the workers have quickly gotten the name plate replaced on the belt, his GIW North Beach belt now reading his name as he sits in the back as Jeremy Baltimore approaches him as he looks at it in the table. JEREMY BALTIMORE: “Listen man, congrats. Really mean it, you did well.” Baltimore pats him on the back having a look at the belt as Redd breaks his gaze to look over his shoulder at Jeremy nodding his shoulder. JEREMY BALTIMORE: “Listen I know you just became the champion and all, but frankly I think I deserve a go at it. I should have been in that match and I think the two of us, could have a good match for it. I say the two of us go one on one next week, what do you say?” Baltimore extends his hand out for Redd as Jack turns around not seeming to be scared at all taking only a second to think about it before grabbing his hand and giving him a strong shake as the two have a momentary chance to look into each other’s eyes before heading their separate ways. Before we get ready for the Main Event a video begins to play with Wake Walker and Aspen Chaud visiting some local kids at a hospital. Even though Aspen doesn’t seem to really be hugely enjoying herself she does a good job acting though it’s very quickly realized that Walker is the one getting most the love from the kids, the video cuts quickly to Aspen talking to a camera. ASPEN CHAUD: “GIW is all about giving back to the community and this week Wake Walker and I visited a local hospital and let me just say it was a whole bunch of…fun!” Aspen puts on her best fake smile as she holds up her thumb to the camera as the video shows her interacting with kids, before now showing Walker as well talking to the camera. WAKE WALKER: “You know last week we visited a orphanage and this week we are visiting a hospital I really enjoy giving back and it’s really inspiring to see how brave these men and women are here, these are the true warriors not us.” Walker again is the hit, the young fans and even some of the old giving him most of the attention something Aspen doesn’t seem to mind the entire time as this time the video ends with Aspen and Wake standing beside a sick young boy as it fades away to a ‘Malice’ commercial before the main event. QUOTE After a long night of amazing match after amazing match we are now ready for the main event as a video begins to roll up on the big screen as everyone turns there attention to the screen. “Two men in very different positions have managed to get to two wins. Both having very different histories here in GIW.” The two stars are shown winning their matches the past few weeks from Ace’s amazing triumph in the first Main Event on the return show to Ethan’s two consecutive losses. “One man a former Undisputed Champion looking to get back where he once was…” The video begins to show Ace Baldwin hugging that Undisputed belt back at ‘On Hallowed Ground II’ the day he ended the Alioth Starre reign. “The other looking to prove that he can hang with the upper echelon.” Wrathmore is shown then a clip of the Undisputed title sitting in a case is as well. “Two men enter…One leaves needing only two more victories.” The video ends and these fans are greeted by the unexpected site of seeing Aspen Chaud sitting at the announce table alongside Riley Stevens and Oliver Thibodaux wearing a red dress and a smile along with the headset over her head as “Hit the Lights” by Metallica hits as the fans show no respect for the rising new star Ethan Wrathmore as he walks out of the back with Rayne by his side as he heads down the ramp with a very confident smirk. RILEY STEVENS: “We are joined down here today by GIW Owner, Aspen Chaud. Special interest tonight?” ASPEN CHAUD: “Of course, the winner of this match may very well be our next champion.” Rayne walks up the steps lowering the ropes for Wrathmore as he enters the ring then taking the steps back down to stand at his corner as his music ends and "Who Dat" by J-Cole hits to the loudest pop of the night as Ace Baldwin walks out from the back with that same confident look as he walks down the ramp high fiving a few of the fans at ringside all of them loving the man that they seemingly want to be their champion as Baldwin enters the ring looking across at Wrathmore. RILEY STEVENS: “What a huge match we have in store for all of you tonight, really is a blockbuster.” ASPEN CHAUD: “Thanks, I do my job well Riley.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Yes you do!” Baldwin and Wrathmore share a stare down the two having had some heated exchanges the past few weeks as the referee steps in to start this thing, as the bell rings three times the same amount of wins the winner will get tonight. DING DING DING! This thing gets underway and these two competitors though they may not like each other are smart enough to know that it’s not going to be an easy out at all. The two share nearly the same physique, both very tall competitors in the ring. They go in for a lock up right off the bat to really test one another but it’s quickly realized that this match is not going to be won by the stronger man as neither can get the advantage, so Wrathmore ends that kicking him in the gut to make him release the hold as he doubles Ace over, then grabs him by his neck and trunks tossing him under the ropes out of the ring showing no respect for Ace at all. The fans boo him as he walks back to the center of the ring holding up his arms in the air, showing supreme confidence in his first GIW Main Event. Baldwin gets back up on the outside looking out at the crowd who scream at him to get back in there which he does clearly upset with Ethan’s ego, jumping in and immediately tackling him down to the mat dropping down some punches while on top until Wrathmore spins over on top and then does the same roughing him up with punches until Ace kicks him off and both men jump to their feet but Baldwin gets there first and connects with a punch, that Wrathmore quickly answers back. RILEY STEVENS: “This has turned into a fist fight.” Both men not backing down from the fight as Wrathmore tosses another big punch at Ace as the crowd boos until Baldwin tosses one back, then another as he starts to get the upper hand as he hits another dropping Ethan back into the corner where he climbs up onto the ropes dropping down punch after punch while having him stuck in the corner as the referee makes the count 1…2…3…4…Baldwin jumps back down having landed several punches from the corner as Wrathmore walks out right into a Dropkick that gets the fans going wild as this time it’s Ethan that rolls out of the ring landing on his feet needing some time to recover. Finding his way to Rayne who checks on him as Baldwin finally gives up on waiting, instead deciding to go out after him but as soon as he slides out Wrathmore slides right back in. Baldwin angrily goes to get back in but as soon as he’s up to his feet Ethan shows some ring savvy connecting with a Dropkick, yelling down at him “That is how it’s done!” which gets the crowd going again as Wrathmore shrugs his shoulders not caring at all about what these fans think of him as he waits for Ace to get back up again. ASPEN CHAUD: “This Ethan guy is pretty good.” Calling for him to get back up, Baldwin does just that as Wrathmore tries to disrespect Baldwin a bit by jumping off the ropes going for his move the Spring Board Heel Kick but Ace sees it coming ducks under it and then goes for that same move and as Wrathmore turns you can hear the smack across his face, landing hard as the fans cheer as Ethan falls to the mat out of it as Ace drops down for a cover 1…2-Kick Out. Still not enough to put this rookie out as Baldwin gets back up knowing the momentum is now on his side looking to really get it going as he uses a Snapmare to flip him over then a huge dropkick to Ethan’s back crushing him as these fans get into it now feeling that Ace might have a chance to really put things away with the momentum he has going as Wrathmore gets up again still not wanting to give him a chance to rest even though he’s on the defensive but he walks right into a Suplex! But Ace keeps a hold of him spinning around as these fans knowing what’s coming, counting along with them…ONE! As he turns over and then delivers a second Suplex…TWO! Finally he switches to a Fisherman’s Suplex with a pin…THREE…1…2-Kick Out, still not enough as Rayne claps Ethan on from the outside. RILEY STEVENS: “Ethan is not going to give up on that third win just like that.” Baldwin seems to have really expected Wrathmore to have been this hard of a challenge as he walks around him waiting for him to get back up, Ethan though does start to show that the damage is getting to him as he now very slowly gets back to his feet, gingerly holding his back as Ace finishes the job for him pulling him by an arm up to his feet then sending him off the ropes, on the rebound he seems to be prepping for ‘The Last Blast’ but he’s caught off guard by a huge Spear by Ethan finally getting out of that run of moves by Ace as the fans groan as Ethan gets up stumbling a bit, the move really coming out of desperation. Baldwin gets right back up but gets a Arm Drag down to the mat as Wrathmore jumps up for a Standing Moonsault looking for the pin 1…2-Kick Out! The fans cheer on Ace for his kick out as Wrathmore gets up to his feet both men breathing heavily after this long battle. Ethan hoists him up again this time he connects with ‘The Cut Above the Rest’ a Scoop Legged Stunner out of no where as this match is over, 1…2…KICK OUT! ASPEN CHAUD: “He kicked out?!” Baldwin the warrior that he is, just kicked out as Ethan seems to be getting a little nervous now as he looks over at Rayne who screams at him to go after Ace. Wrathmore waits for him to get up as he hits the ropes heading after him only to run right into ‘The Last Blast’ a Spinning Spine Buster! These fans go crazy as Ace gets up stumbling over in the corner as Wrathmore does the same only in the opposite corner, on the ground. Rayne jumps up onto the ring apron catching both Ace and the referee’s attention screaming something at them. Ace doesn’t give her much attention but the momentary distraction allows a hurt Wrathmore to tug off the corner padding. Baldwin not seeing it rushes at Ethan in the corner only for Wrathmore to hit a drop toe hold sending him head first into it. Baldwin stays on his feet but seems to have a cut as he turns right into a beautiful ‘Hit the Lights’ a Pele Kick as he hooks the leg Baldwin taking two quick shots to the head, looking out of it as Ethan makes the cover 1…2…3! REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner by way of pinfall…ETHAN WRATHMORE!” These fans are both shocked and angry as Rayne slides into the ring celebrating alongside Wrathmore who gets up slowly after a war of a main event breathing heavily in the ring as the referee raises his hand in victory but he is quickly replaced by Rayne who raises his arm in his third victory here in GIW. RILEY STEVENS: “He exposed the turnbuckle, Baldwin took that shot head first.” ASPEN CHAUD: “Win by any means Riley!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “That’s what I always tell him.” Aspen removes her headset standing up clapping on Ethan as Ace rolls out of the ring to the outside holding his head in pain opened up as Ethan and Rayne look over at the Boss who is clearly impressed. RILEY STEVENS: “Looks like Wrathmore impressed the right person tonight as he takes home win number three tying him with Wake Walker for the lead, tune in next week as the race to five continues!” With Aspen clapping him on Ethan goes back to celebrating holding up three fingers as the show slowly fades on that image. WINNER: ETHAN WRATHMORE
