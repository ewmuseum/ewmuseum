Pre-show, 22 July 2014

GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: MOMENT OF GLORY IV PRE-SHOW Live from the Kauffman Arena – July 22nd, 2014 QUOTE We start the pre-show up here at ‘Moment of Glory IV’ with a majority of the crowd still not quite here yet or just filing in of course there is a contingency of die hard GIW fans there to see the always entertaining Open Battle Royal as we return to a ring full of new debuting stars ready to go as we have Anubis and a returning Daniel Elias in the ring for what is now a singles match. RILEY STEVENS: “Ready here for the Open Battle Royal on a night that it may be hard to make noise, they will still be able to get a win on the big stage and maybe get on the path to the Main Card next Supershow. Because we have two stars debuting tonight it has been made a Singles Contest.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Never know but…When does Lynch wrestle?” Though he doesn’t seem to thrilled the fans and the referees outside the ring stand at attention as they get this match started. DING DING DING! Like burning empty rain down on him, Anubis broke the silence quickly bending Daniel to the alter of his ego. A kick to the gut and shove back to put him a rut. A Samoan drop locked in right, but Daniel had put up a fight. He countered with a hip toss and dropped an elbow to keep him down, his opponent turned when Daniel came back around. Daniel pulled Anubis to his feet and threw in a few demoralizing hits to the skull and then set up his next maneuver. He twisted and contorted his opponent just enough to apply the abdominal stretch. An awkward position for Anubis, he had no ability to escape the hold by way of rope, or even tapping. He was under the dark thrall of Daniel Elias. RILEY STEVENS: “Formerly known as Daniel Starr you got think Daniel has the advantage of not having to deal with first time jitters.” Daniel tightened the submission hold, but eventually relented. He pushed his opponent away with a smug shove and continued the assault with a well struck inverted clothesline. Anubis back on the canvas, breaking bad in this, he had to secure a counter here or victory was vain. He drained his opponent’s chances of following up and countered a stomp with a quick leg sweep to knock Daniel belly level with the canvas. Both down, he rolled onto his opponent, positioned himself for higher ground and began a series of strikes that could knock the sensei out of temple. He tore himself off and steeled away toward the corner of the ring. He was ready for the spear, to end this quick pre-show match. When Daniel pulled up, Anubis charged like the winds of Egpyt, and Daniel caught in time to perform a belly to belly suplex. Once successfully countered, the fans cheered and jeered as the Elias Detector was expertly applied. Quickly tapping, Anubis had lost. REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner by way of submission…Daniel Elias!” Elias rolls to his feet as the fans here start to boo as the returning star gets a big return victory and puts himself back in the think of things again as Anubis rolls out of the ring collecting himself having come up short but still making a name for himself here on the Pre-Show. RILEY STEVENS: “What a big win here for Daniel Elias who picks up a win in his return back, how far can he go?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Far he’s always had the talent, now he just needs to get there.” Elias smirks up at Anubis who walks up the ramp seeming angry at Daniel and himself as Daniel turns to the crowd raising his arms again in victory drawing some more boos as we go to another Moment of Glory teaser video. WINNER: DANIEL ELIAS QUOTE Up next in our featured match up we have a classic as "Sky is the Limit" by Rebelution now hits and the fans start to cheer as Mo Love; the combination of Mo Chronic and Chuck Love-man known as the The Lovelutionaries but that is often a handful to type so they stay Mo Love as they walk out from the back as Chuck calls for a hug as the two hug at the top of the ramp before heading down to the ring hugging fans on each side of the ramp as they get a big ovation here tonight as the fans are really starting to pour in now. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “These guys…” RILEY STEVENS: “The fans love them no doubt.” Chuck and Chronic walk along the ring and jump into their corner as “Bodies” by Saliva hits and the fans go to another level with cheers as they mark out hard for the legendary longest running tag team in GIW history but they are confused when the much anticipated return of Nelson Fielder seems to be a sad ruse as Brody Harper wheels out a casket with ‘Neelson Feildr’ written on the top, as the fans give out a standing ovation to the former Tag Team title contenders as Harper pushes the casket to the side of the ring patting it as he slides into the ring, staring over at Chuck and Chronic who seem more confused then even usual. RILEY STEVENS: “Harper has brought out Nelson’s coffin tonight a heartwarming story really.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Got to love it.” Harper points out at the coffin as the fans clap some more as he turns and glares at Chuck and Chronic ready to go, as this First Blood Tag Team match appears ready to go. DING DING DING! This apparent handicap tag team match is now underway as Chuck and Chronic have a small debate over who will start but Chuck pulls out a dandelion and says he’s got this, as he grabs it and turns to Harper who stands in the middle of the ring waiting for him as he walks over with the dandelion walking past Harper and leaning out of the ring to put it on the casket of Nelson Fielder as he turns and walks back to Harper, holding out his arms for a hug feeling sorry for Brody. Harper seems like is about to jump into it when he jumps forward with a nasty head butt to the skull of Chuck trying to draw blood here first as Love-man stumbles back and Harper charges at him taking him down with a brutal clothesline as the crowd goes wild for Brody as he looks over at the casket seeming to be willed on by his team mate even from the grave as he turns and bounces off the ropes and comes at Love-man only for Chuck to dodge him and Brody hits the other ropes coming again at Love-man but this time he nails him with a gnarly chop to the stomach that actually seems to hurt Chuck’s hand more then it does Brody as he lines up for it again and Harper takes it to the stomach and Love-man stumbles back foolishly goes for a third but Brody jumps into it this time as Chuck hits his stomach but goes down in pain as Harper jumps into a cover, the referee reminds him it’s a First Blood match and Brody gets up telling him he knew that as he calls for Chuck to get up grabbing him as he does grabbing an arm but Love-man reverses it throwing Brody to the corner instead as he charges him and connects with a running butt shot in the corner as he makes the tag to Chronic, as he grabs Harper and gives him a noogie before throwing him into a arm drag from Chronic. RILEY STEVENS: “Harper unable to make a tag in this match, while Chronic and Chuck have proven to work great as a team.” Brody gets up quickly and Chronic tosses him back into the corner as he tags back in Chuck and he steps in and the two do a fist bump before punching Brody back to the mat as the tags are coming in bunches now as Chronic steps back onto the ring apron where he tags Chuck again as Harper gets up and the two hit a Double Suplex followed by a team high five, neither team seeming to remember that there was a stipulation to this match as Chronic and Chuck go to hoist him back up but out of no where Harper starts to hulk up as the crowd goes wild as he pushes both back then dives into them with a double clothesline but he falls to the mat with them as he turns and starts to crawl to his empty corner as Chuck and Mo start to do the same…yes…they are both crawling to an empty corner. Harper starts to crawl as he gets closer and closer when the loudest pop ever recorded in GIW occurs as the casket pops open and NELSON FIELDER POPS OUT! The arena is rocking as Fielder jumps onto the ring apron as Brody jumps into a tag as Chuck and Chronic turn around and are met with a double dropkick after Nelson goes Springboard as fans in the crowd are shown flipping out as Fielder hops up going crazy, death not stopping him as he hits the ropes and runs off the ropes hitting Chuck with a Dropkick because I was too lazy to look up his moveset! Love-man is sent out of the ring as Fielder grabs Chronic and tosses him back into the corner where he runs and clotheslines him then tosses him forward going top rope and diving off with a splash onto Chronic still no real effort to cause any bleeding but the fans don’t care they are going nuts for the reunited Killers! RILEY STEVENS: “WHAT A MOMENT WE ARE WITNESSING!” Fielder gets up and looks over at his tag team partner as he walks over making the tag to Harper as they stalk Chronic looking to set up the move that has gotten them their wins in so many of their tag team matches as Mo gets up and stumbles into ‘The Killer Move’ a Flapjack DDT! A move we haven’t seen since their last win…whenever that was as they get up and Chuck slides back in walking right into ‘The Killer Move’ a Flapjack DDT as well as the Killers lay them both out getting up to a loud ovation here as Fielder walks to the corner, as Harper calls for them to get up maybe to actually cause some bleeding. Chronic gets up like a sitting duck looking ready to be finished as Harper charges but Chuck the hero he is jumps up and does a flying high five to Brody’s face! The crowd is stunned as Brody seems rocked now as Chronic recovers in time and throws a fist bump to the throat a double whammy as Brody stumbles back into his corner, falling through the ropes into the casket but Fielder makes the tag and goes springboard. He misses as Mo busts a move doing the MC Hammer to the left as Fielder bounces off the mat as Chuck jumps into a headlock on Nelson, as Chronic locks in the Indian Burn to Fielder who may be in more pain then when he died as Chuck locks in the noogie now VICIOUS! The crowd looks on in horror as the fighter that Fielder is battles when Chuck noogies so hard, a cut opens up as the referee sees the tiniest amount of blood and calls for the match! REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here are your winners the team of the Lovelutionaries, Mo Chronic and Chuck Love-man!” What a contest these fans are amazed as Chuck and Chronic release the devastating hold now as Love-man looks appalled by what he did as he checks on Nelson asking if he is all right, as Chronic and Love-man hug Fielder before rolling out of the ring as Brody steps into the ring with his partner now. RILEY STEVENS: “What a match that was one of our best tag team matches ever between two teams that weren’t enemies I have to say.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “...” Chuck and Chronic head to the back getting cheered by the crowd with a big tag team victory here tonight but they are classy guys and give the Killers their moment as Nelson and Brody hug in the ring as the crowd gives them a standing ovation, as a ‘Thank You Killers!’ chant breaks out in the arena as the Pre-Show fades as it’s now time for the Main Card though few will want to follow that up. WINNERS: MO LOVE
