GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: ETV LIVE! From Bolivar Lighthouse – July 14th, 2011 QUOTE Opener The opening contest known as the Open Battle Royal got a very unexpected guest in the form of the returning Seth Seeker, who immediately got the fans attention. Joining him were Azriel and Demon Night two men who clearly knew one another. Right away it was an all out war with Night and Seeker waging a war with one another that left many thinking there was legitimate bad blood. It was Azriel who was eliminated first when, attempting to get involved in the fight he broke the two up only to be thrown over by both men. Back to the battle we went and while it was Seeker who was the favorite nobody came away thinking any less of Night as he attempted to go for a Clothesline to take over Seeker, the big man ducked it and used Night’s own momentum to flip him over the ropes to win the match in what was a great way to start the night. Night angrily tried to get back in the ring after the match but it was Azriel who calmed him down, as the dynamic between these two became clear as the first match came to a close. WINNER: SETH SEEKER Crysis, Antonio Nappa, Shock Collar, and Tesoro are all in the ring for their match to begin, but they get interrupted by the music of Aerosmith. Richard Youngblood comes walking down to the ring with a microphone in hand. He comes up the steps and joins the men in the ring. RICHARD YOUNGBLOOD: Yeah know, before you guys start fighting in the ring. I thought it would be a good idea to have our first contest before you guys start getting to beat up. I have been having a few phone calls with the agency and it seems we recently got a deal with a certain movie that is coming out soon. The contest is this. Each of you will have to do a quick little promo in the ring, but there is a catch. Every single person, place, and thing will have to be replaced with the word Smurf. The winner will be gettin' himself one point as well as one thousand dollars. Now who want's to go first?” Shock Collar steps up with a large smile on his face. He didn't even take a second to think about this. SHOCK COLLAR: “Well... Smurf thinks that this is awesome. Smurf grew up with the Smurfs. Smurf is very excited about the upcoming Smurf Smurf. Smurf doesn't think that these three Smurfs know what the Smurf they have to talk about. That is because Smurfs are afraid to talk in front of all the Smurfs here. Smurfs be trippin'.” A buzzer goes off and Shock Collar skips back to the line up while Youngblood gets his microphone back. RICHARD YOUNGBLOOD: “Alright, that wasn't too bad. Which one of you guys think you can do better.” The three men just keep looking at each other. None of them wanted to try to compete with Shock Collar. Eventually, Antonio Nappa walks up to Youngblood and gets ready to talk. ANTONIO NAPPA: “I want to go on record and say that this is possibly the dumbest thing I have ever head in my life. Richard, you use to be a wrestler yourself and now you want us to prance around the ring and crack jokes. I am not sure if you have noticed yet, but this is a wrestling ring. You should know what we do in this ring. Do not patronize us and the craft we have developed with these cheap gimmicks. If you want a promo, we give you one. If you want a match, we give you a match. If you want a joke, than there's the door cause you are going to have to look elsewhere cause I am not putting my integrity on the line to entertain you or these people.” Youngblood gets his microphone back and looks somewhat puzzled about it. Nappa goes back and stands with the others. RICHARD YOUNGBLOOD: “You didn't say Smurf.” Nappa just stares him down with a death glare. Youngblood seems to ignore it as he tries to wave Crysis or Tesoro over. Both men are shaking their heads no. Youngblood gives up. RICHARD YOUNGBLOOD: “Well than, it seems like the winner of our first contest and one thousand dollars is Shock Collar.” Shock Collar puts his fist in the air, but his victory has become very salty with what Nappa has sad. Youngblood leaves the ring as the match is about to start. QUOTE Mid-Card Match This tag team match was slow starting up. These men were very unfamiliar with each other. It started out with Tesoro and Crysis in the ring. Tesoro tried to use his speed as an advantage, but eventually the size difference proved to have been to much as Crysis and Nappa were able to double team him down to the mat. Thing looked like they were going to turn around though as Tesoro got a burst of energy. He nailed Nappa with a Hurricanrana and crawled over to tag in Shock Collar while Nappa got in Crysis. Collar ran in and kept taking the men down over and over with big running power moves. Eventually he got Nappa up onto his shoulder to nail the Rolling Rock. When he turned, Crysis nailed him in the face with the Downgrade. He goes for the pin and gets the victory as well as the two points for himself. WINNERS: ANTONIO NAPPA & CRYSIS QUOTE Mid-Card Match In this match up we had the veteran Nelson Fielder taking on the debuting Green Dragon who brought with him a whole lot of hype into the match. Expecting much the fans were let down when rather then seeing a big showing from Dragon they instead got a beating being dealt out by Nelson Fielder. Though the fans didn’t like it, he picked him apart piece by piece until finally he put an end to it with a ‘Kill Shot’ a Springboard Somersault Neckbreaker, that put Dragon away for the three count. After the match The Killers celebrated in the ring together. WINNER: NELSON FIELDER QUOTE Main Event Not often are we given a full females match so this match was very rare. The fans were again behind Amber Stone who yet again seemed prepared to take these two women out. Early on it was Stone that seemed to be at an advantage, perhaps having gotten her first match out of the way she had a bit of an advantage. Regardless as the much wore on it was Katya Rose that took advantage showing the fans her talents while also turning them against her with her very angry demeanor. The match was wore and Lisa who was clearly the weak link, was set up and it was Stone in the ring with her alone. Getting her back, she grabbed her and nailed her with a Double Knee Backbreaker! The crowd went nuts but as she got her wits to go for a pin she was met with a huge Pele Kick that sent her stumbling away. Rose took the chance to make the pin for the 1…2…3, for a big win. The crowd booed her but as she exited the ring she didn’t seem to care as Stone held her head realizing how close she was again as Rose taunted her, as we got the feel this wasn’t over. WINNER: KATYA ROSE
