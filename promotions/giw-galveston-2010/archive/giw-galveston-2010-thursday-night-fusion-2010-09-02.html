Thursday Night Fusion, 2 September 2010

2 September 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Galveston Island Wrestling Presents: Fusion! Chicken Wins Battle Royal On Account of No One Else Showing, his Reputation for Pain Continues WINNER: CHICKEN "Getting Away With Murder" by Jeffree Star hits and the crowd goes bleh to Ashleigh 3 Tears as she walks by a clown falls in love, but her mom admonishes Ashleigh for stealing all her makeup and dressing like a whore. Re-Education by Rise Against cuts it off and Austin Acquin walks out with a smirk. One Cowboy in a fancy hat throws a cup at him yelling that he doesn’t do anything that reasonably sustains his push making him the most smark Cowboy ever. The Security escorts Megathreat and his pet girlfriend/cow Bertha out of the building. "How You Like Me Now" by The Heavy starts playing and the audience answers by not doing anything. The camera picks up on one fan picking his nose as Travis Tate does all he can to stay relevant. "Butterfly Effect" by Amatory starts thrashing around and Alexii Ravynova walks out. One fan goes Tits or Get The fuck out, but then another fan respectively declined on their behalf. It’s ok though because Alexii wasn’t going to oblige. Austin Acquin and Travis Tate start the match off doing what they do best. Leaching of his brother and being extremely condescending respectively. Austin punches Tate in the face but Tate is too interested in winning a match for once. Tate hit’s a body slam and jumps up with a How do you like me now! One fan responds you still suck. Tate tags in Alexii Raynova and the ref checks the rulebook and remembers how Aspen handles the male roster… in wrestling you pervs so he allows Alexii to fight Acquin. Alexii demonstrates why they call her Fountainhead and spits on Acquin. Acquin seems intent to live up to his nickname, but he realized he didn’t have one… Oh damn Heart Punch by Alexii. Austin struggles to Ashleigh’s corner and pays for her services and by that I mean he tags her in but a quarter fell out of his trunks. Ashleigh comes in and Alexii hits her so hard her makeup goes flying. It was actually pretty sweet like a pink fog overtaking the mat, but not nearly as big. Anyway Alexii demolishes Ashleigh making her into Ashleigh A Lot Of Tears. She Curbstomps Ashleigh and tags in Travis Tate. He hit’s a Second Rope Leg Drop on Ashleigh. Ashleigh gets up and looks at Travis Tate. She asks him if he likes Hollywood Undead, spoilers he doesn’t. So Ashleigh bitch slaps him and runs up the turnbuckle. She goes for a Flying Crossbody but Tate was busy Tossing The Stub and by that I mean he hit his finisher. He laughs and throws her to Acquin. Acquin tags her in and comes running Spear but Tate has it scouted by distracting him with a picture of a Stealth Bomber. Tate tags in Alexxi and whips Acquin in the ropes when Alexxi hit’s a Jawbreaker Lariat. Tate picks up Acquin and hits another Tossing The Stub on Alexii’s outstretched knee. Alexii pins him for the 3 count. WINNER: ALEXII RAVYNOVA & TRAVIS TATE “Dragon (Ricky Steamboat/Ultimo Dragon Theme)” begins to play. The entire crowd stands up and then looks on at the fabled Inchiro Hueda. He quickly enters the ring. At the top of the tron, a mechanical dragon appears who spits out fire for a minute as Inchiro Hueda poses. Several men in samurai costumes come out and begin to do a ritual tog ive Inchiro Hueda luck. It is to be noted that nobody in the audience either cheered or booed. Instead, they all looked on. It was very hard to tell what the audience thought of Inchiro. Inchiro’s music fades… “Rick Ross-B.M.F” plays. J.J Evans comes out. The crowd doesn’t appear to be too keen with him. They boo him some as he enters the ring. Once they are both there, they stare each other down. The bell sounds. Inchiro Hueda quickly exit’s the ring and begins to get booed by the crowd. He begins to circle the ring and begins to get booed from the crowd. J.J. Evans comes out and then begins to chase Inchiro. Inchiro doesn’t run but instead drop kicks J.J Evans. The ref begins to count. 1...2... Inchiro picks up J.J Evans and tackles him to the barricade. 3...4...5... Inchiro Hueda throws J.J Evans and all of the samurais surround J.J Evans. 6...7.…8... Inchiro Hueda steps on J.J Evans’ face. 9...10! COUNTOUT! Inchiro Hueda and the samurais begin to attack J.J Evans in the cruelest of ways. After being beaten practically to a bloody pulp, J.J Evans can be seen hardly being able to stand up as Inchiro Hueda laughs and leaves with his music playing. WINNER: NO CONTEST [Due to Double Count Out]
