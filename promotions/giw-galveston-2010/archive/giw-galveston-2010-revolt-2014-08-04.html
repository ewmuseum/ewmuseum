Revolt, 4 August 2014

4 August 2014
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: REVOLT Live from the Kauffman Arena – August 4th, 2014 BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! The camera pans the arena as the crowd inside Kaufman arena is on their feet for the start of another episode of Revolt. The camera catches a few signs in the crowd “Beck Ramsey 4 Life”, “GET HYPED”, and “We already miss Aspen & Cody”. The camera quickly transitions to the ring where already there is Alioth Starre. He was in the ring, stood like a sole solemn statue. He held a microphone in hand with intend to speak. His attire was formal, all black as normal, but with a black sequin tie to tie in the quirk of his personality. Alioth waited for the crowd to finish simmering until he spoke. ALIOTH STARRE: “It is true that I have defeated Wake Walker with little to no effort. However, now that I have completed one of the biggest goals that I had set in my career within Galveston Island Wrestling, I’m ready to see what goes beyond this horizon. I know there is still things I could do here, but my journey north has told me that I’m nothing more than a big fish in a little pond down here. The only way to grow is for the universe to expand.” He continued as the crowds silenced. ALIOTH STARRE: “Alexis Terry is in control now. I don’t just mean in control, but also controlling. She lent a hand down to me, as if I were a child, and expected me to join her in this new era, but I refused. I asked what it would entail, what goals I could achieve, and her response was that I’d have to do what she told me to, without being in the loop, and any suggestions about my own career from me were moot to her and her new regime. To that, I have decided to, effective immediately, resign from this company, and I hope more people in the back do as well. You can follow me elsewhere as I travel the independent circuits to find my next biggest challenge, but Galveston Island Wrestling is completely and utterly tapped. Without the previous powers that be, this place will run as dry as Alexis Terry’s imagination within weeks. I cannot sit idly by while she singlehandedly destroys this company by bullying myself, and all of you, and thus I’ve chosen to walk away until the day, perhaps a long time from now, when the old power comes back into being. Remember to respect the devils you know, and be cautious, very much so, with the ones you don’t. Wake Walker had a vision for our match, and confided in me this vision, but it did not come to pass as he saw because of the change to this new era. Ladies and Gentlemen, all that you have left is Tony Miranda and the last fragments of the past. May they be of comfort to you whilst this place is slowly choked by the controlling, selfish, and fool-hearted Alexis Terry. Goodnight, and goodbye.” He he dropped the microphone, the arena descended into a starry darkness. As the lights lifted, the man had disappeared. Not like a ghost, but like the stars in the cosmos, and the stars that once made Galveston Island Wrestling what it was. Now gone. RILEY STEVENS: “Alioth, a true GIW icon has left and who knows when he will be back.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “How dare he talk about Alexis, our leader, like that. Good riddance.” =============== Already in the ring is Kevin Astro and Anubis, both newer roster additions looking to make an impact tonight on Revolt. Kevin Astro has a smirk on his face as the two stare each other down. RILEY STEVENS: “Kevin Astro claims to 'have the best matches in GIW' and looks to have another show stealer tonight.” Kevin Astro is in the ring and the bell sounds. It’s Astro vs. Anubis one on one in the opening contest and Astro starts the match off quickly with a lock up and side headlock. Anubis pushes Astro off but Astro leapfrogs Anubis. Huge dropkick connects and Anubis is down. Astro from out of nowhere with the HDMI. He covers and gets the win in a quick match up tonight-on Revolt. RILEY STEVENS: “Well the was fast. Kevin Astro with the win in almost record time.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “It's like Anubis didn't even show up tonight.” WINNER: KEVIN ASTRO BY PIN =============== "Hello Zepp" by Charlie Clouser begins to play and the fans inside Kaufman arena stand. Christopher Darren Erns, the newly crowned Deep South Champion, makes his way to the ring in his ring gear with a Singapore Cane in hand and the Deep South Championship around his waist, though he is not scheduled to compete tonight. The crowd gives him a bit of a mixed reaction, which is unusual. He enters the ring and quickly grabs a microphone. CHRISTOPHER DARREN ERNS: "I’m going to make it short and sweet tonight. I know I am not scheduled to wrestle tonight but Michael Lynch and I have unfinished business and I demand he comes out here right now so we can settle this one and for all!" A few moments pass and instead of Michael Lynch walking to the ring to a chorus of boos is none other than Charity Ryan. She has a microphone in hand and CDE looks on in disgust. CHARITY RYAN: "I am sorry to inform you Christopher but everyone knows Michael Lynch is not here tonight." CDE looks on as Charity steps through the ropes and continues. CHARITY RYAN: "So on behalf of Alexis Terry and The System I need you to leave the ring so we can continue on with –" Before she can get another word out CDE connects with a shot to the ribs with cane! CDE has lost it and continues to beat down Charity violently with shot after shot to the back. Charity tries to scurry out of the ring but CDE grabs her by the foot and pulls her back in. He lines it up perfect and connects with a huge shot to the head of Charity that echo’s throughout the ring. This is a much darker and violent side of CDE as he continues to beat down Ryan. Finally, referees and some agents storm the ring and break up the violent attack, finally able to pull CDE away from Charity Ryan who is bleeding now and crying. RILEY STEVENS: "Christopher Darren Erns has snapped! Oh my god!" Christopher Darren Erns looks on with almost a sick smile as he leaves the ring and heads to the back escorted by referees. =============== Daniel Elias is already in the ring. "Electric Sleep" by sHEAVY cues and from the back emerges Sam Hain to boos. He ignores them and makes his way to the ring ready for action tonight. REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Making his way to the ring from West Allis, Wisconsin, Sam Hain!” Sam Hain and Daniel Elias locked up and Daniel had Sam early on, hitting a nice hip toss to the canvas. Daniel had the headlock still applied by Sam Hain is able to reverse it into a head scissors. Both men are back up now and Sam Hain ducks a swinging clothesline by Daniel. Discus Big Boot! Sam covers and that is all she wrote, Another short match on Revolt. RILEY STEVENS: "Another short match on Revolt tonight with Sam Hain getting a nice victory over Daniel Elias" OLIVER THIBODAUX: "It's like Daniel Elias shared a car with Anubis and both decided to not show up tonight. Pathetic." WINNER: SAM HAIN BY PIN =============== We cut backstage where Beck Ramsey and The Harlequin are seen talking to one another in the locker room. THE HARLEQUIN: Look, all I am saying Beck is to be careful tonight. Nether of us know what to expect from Alexis tonight. BECK RAMSEY: It really doesn’t matter what she has in store for me tonight because Alexis finally understands that I am not going away and I am never giving this up. He looks at the Undisputed Championship on his shoulder and smirks. BECK RAMSEY: I will be fine; I’m Beck Ramsey baby. He walks out of camera view. Harlequin turns and walks over to her bag. She picks up the Seize The Moment briefcase and the crowd pops. She holds it in front of her and smiles. =============== Love-man is dancing in the ring as Jack Waters simply looks on unamused with Love-man. Jack Waters attacks and is all over him now as the fans boo. Jack Waters hits a DDT and then mounts Love-man and is blasting away with right hands. The referee tries to pull Waters off but he refuses to do so. The crowd erupts in a roar now as storming down the ramp is....Tony Miranda! Miranda slides into the ring and tackles Jack Waters to the mat! RILEY STEVENS: "Tony Miranda to the rescue!" OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Tony Miranda has no business out here!" The two continue the fight but Waters gets the upper hand kicking Tony "rather low" and Waters is able to escape to the outside and up the ramp and Tony Miranda looks on in pain. RILEY STEVENS: "Tony Miranda has said in the past he wants Jack Waters and he may have gotten a bit more of him tonight then he wanted." WINNER: JACK WATERS BY DQ =============== We cut to the parking lot where a black town car pulls up. The door opens but we can only make out legs as the camera follows them as they walks towards the entrance of the arena. =============== It began with a snap suplex. Something to catch the spark of the match. Once the first body hit the canvas and the arena echoed with the thud of carnage, the match was underway between Tony Miranda and Xombi. Tony prepared his opponent for a submission move, the Nerve Hold, as it were, but as he tried to apply it, Xombi countered and performed an enziguri. With Tony down, Xombi quickly used the ropes for momentum, leapt over his opponent, then on the way back, when Tony had gotten to his feet, he sent out a hard big boot to the face. Tony collided with the canvas again, but he bounced back with more energy. RILEY STEVENS: "Xombi looking impressive tonight." So far it seemed even. Though it was a fast start, and both hoped to reach a quick end, Tony took the offensive with another attempt at a submission hold to slow things down. He wanted to drain the life out of his opponent in order to win by submission. He tried to apply the abdominal stretch, but it was countered into a throw back down onto the canvas, then transitioned into a knee drop. Xombi seemed to be gaining momentum against his surer opponent. Tony dodged the next ground strike, but Xombi was steps ahead. He had rushed to the turn post to climb up and strike from above like a bomber, but even so, Tony was prepared for that! The moment he touched down was the moment Tony applied the abdominal stretch again. He caught him and quickly maneuvered him into position for the submission hold. With the hold finally intact, Tony took the reins, and the pacing of the match which had suddenly slowed to a near halt. He was fine with this, because he wanted to take the time to exhaust his opponent quicker than joining in the reindeer games RILEY STEVENS: "Is that....Waters?" Suddenly, the crowd grew to a roar as Jack Waters slides into the ring and hits a few forearms to the back of Tony. The bell sounds and Xombi rolls to the outside and escapes the mayhem. Tony fights back now and hits a clothesline that sends both of them out of the ring. The two brawl up the ramp and to the back as the crowd cheers the fight on, wanting to see the two fight WINNER: TONY MIRANDA BY DQ =============== From the back the camera panned left. A loud noise had caught the cameraman’s attention and as he spun it around to catch what was going on, Tony Miranda and Jack Waters slammed into the side wall of the corridor. Tony had the upper hand and nearly dropped his rival with three clear strikes to the head, but Jack pushed off and threw a gut kick to free himself. Just as things were getting interesting, the security from the arena began to swarm like blue locusts around the two. Tony got in a shot, Jack managed to slip a kick through, but ultimately the quarrel was torn apart. As the dust settled and the guards held back the two, Alexis Terry was seen entering the scene with her eyes glaring holes through the two new rivals. ALEXIS TERRY: “You want to fight on my time, then you’re going to do as I say, when I say it. Clearly there is something between you two that needs to be worked out, so how about this? Tony Miranda versus Jack Waters, in a ‘best out of 5’ series of matches. I like it, the crowd will like it, and I know you will like getting your hands on one another and inflicting carnage.” The two looked happy with the decision as the camera cut back to the ring. =============== Harlequin and Beck Ramsey our in one corner as Ryan Cavallari and Artemis Kaiser are in the other. The bell sounds and the main event is underway. Harlequin and Cavallari start and Cavallari is on the attack early hitting a nice shoulder block that takes down Harlequin. Ryan tries an elbow drop but Harlequin is out of the way. She hits a nice arm drag that sends Cavallari to his corner. Cavallari tries an arm drag of his own but Harlequin reveres it and hits one of her own. Ryan tries to tag in Artemis but Artemis drops off the apron much to the surprise of the fans. She points to her shoes and pretends that she needed to tie them, which Ryan seems not to believe. Harlequin tags in Beck who comes in to a nice pop and nails a beautiful dropkick to the back of Ryan. RILEY STEVENS: "Artemis looks like she cares less that she is in the middle of a match and its costing her team." Ryan is back up but staggers. Beck Ramsey lands a nice snap suplex. He takes in Harlequin who is back on the attack with elbow drops to the back of Ryan. Ryan is able to push Harlequin back enough for another attempt at a tag but Artemis once again drops off the apron, this time walking over to some fans in attendance to talk some trash. Ryan can’t believe it. Beck is tagged back in and Beck and Ryan exchange punches in the ring. OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Beck Ramsey and Ryan Cavallari are going to kill each other! Ryan Cavallari rakes the eyes of Beck and hits a nice roaring elbow but Ryan stumbles into his corner and is unable to tag advantage. Artemis quickly tags herself in and goes for the pinfall..1…2…but Beck counters and rolls up Artemis! He gets the pinfall on the reversal! OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Was that three?" Artemis can’t believe she was pinned. Beck Ramsey is handed the championship as Artemis and Ryan argue on the way up the ramp. Suddenly, “Dangerous” By Within Temptations cues and from the back emerges Alexis Terry to massive boos. Harlequin and Beck look on from the ring as Alexis stops atop the ramp and smiles at the fans who continue to boo her. "Well, it’s been an interesting night but Beck I know you have been wondering all day what exactly I had in store for you tonight. Well you are in luck because I want to introduce you to your opponent at Blackout…." Beck looks on waiting for the music of his opponent to hit and for them to make their presence known. After a moment he throws his arms in the air. The lights go out. The crowd roars with anticipation and when they come back on Harlequin is laid out. Standing over top of her is Madison Seton! Seton has her Undisputed Champion in hand and when Beck turns around he is nailed with the title. Alexis Terry is laughing on stage and clapping at the attack. "At Blackout it will be Beck Ramsey versus Madison Seton in an Undisputed Championship Unification match!" WINNER: BECK RAMSEY & HARLEQUIN BY PIN
