GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS SEIZE THE MOMENT LIVE! From Kauffman Arena in Galveston, TX -- March 29th, 2015 BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! As the fireworks shoot into the air outside of Kauffman Arena, the fans know that the show they have been waiting for is about to begin. Seize the Moment – where nine of GIW’s best meet in a ladder match to crown the Undisputed Champion, is the first supershow back for Galveston and the crowd is roaring in anticipation. “Failure” by Breaking Benjamin, the special theme song for the event, is playing loudly over the PA system as the camera pans through the crowd. Signs such as “MO’S MOMENT”, “IS HE UNBANNED”, AND “THE NEXT GOLDEN AGE” can be seen as the Fox Sports Southwest logo flashes across the bottom of the screen, followed by the GIW logo shortly after. After giving the audience at home a moment to take it all in, we cut to the announcer’s table where Riley Stevens and Oliver Thibodaux are dressed in their tuxedos, ready for the show to begin. RILEY STEVENS: "Ladies and gentlemen, thank you all for joining us live here tonight from the Kauffman Arena. For those of you joining us on Fox Sports Southwest, thank you; and for those who are out of the area and watching us live stream on gogalveston.com, we thank you as well. Whether you are a loyal fan of Galveston Island Wrestling or you are tuning in for the first time, we have a show that is sure to impress tonight. Before we begin, I’d like to introduce my partner for the night, Oliver Thibodaux." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Thanks for the introduction Riley. The crowd tonight is hot and ready for action, but we have some business to take care of first. This card is LOADED and we are going to go through it match by match to give you an idea of what you can expect tonight at Seize the Moment." RILEY STEVENS: "Stacked may be an understatement Ollie; from top to bottom this show will be full of matches that are gonna tear the roof of this place tonight. Already tonight we saw Ashley O’Ryan debut in convincing fashion as he won the pre-show match against a local talent. The fans were behind him the whole night, and we may get a chance to see him later on in the show. But as for the main card, we will begin with a special treat – the GIW Television Championship match. The title belt is defended every week right here on GIW programming, and the champion, a man known as ‘The Sir’, will seek to defend his belt for the first time after winning it last week. The participants are confirmed: we will see Bronx Valescence and Chuck Loveman challenge him tonight." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "As if it wasn’t hard enough to defend a title every week, The Sir is going to have to outlast two great competitors tonight. Bronx Valescence is someone we will talk about a little bit later in detail, but last week he was victorious in the open battle royal and he will be a tough nut for The Sir to crack tonight. But all eyes will be on Chuck Loveman, the bonafide hippy and man who gave up his opportunity for the Undisputed Championship. He and The Sir have a blood feud that goes back quite a long time, and I imagine The Sir will be focusing all of his attention on hurting Loveman. Because of that, I believe Bronx Valescence is going to sneak in and take the title tonight to continue his winning ways." RILEY STEVENS: "That match certainly has the potential to become very violent very quickly, especially with a sadistic freak like The Sir involved. Gavin, if you don’t already known, is a professional wrestler with one arm. His opponent this week is another veteran of the business, Ryan ‘Kick’ Smith. Though Smith hasn’t picked up a win yet in GIW, considering Gavin’s performance last week, he has to be the favorite tonight." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Oh definitely.
