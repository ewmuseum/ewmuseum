GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: CATEGORY 5 PRE-SHOW LIVE! From Galveston’s Pleasure Pier – August 4, 2013 QUOTE We have now arrived at the time of the night for the GIW Open Battle Royal here at ‘Category 5’. Before the big show begins we are greeted with two new faces in Mason Andrews and Maria Milonas as well as a returning veteran Christopher Darren Erns. RILEY STEVENS: “Ready for the first match of the night, the Open Battle Royal and tonight we have a three way.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “In your dreams buddy.” The three of them all seem to know what is at stake here tonight as the referee gets things started and ready to go. DING DING DING! It’s the time of the night, the ever so annual open battle royal. This week’s crop of talent to take the plunge wanting to make a quick name for themselves happens to be Christopher Darren Erns, a former GIW talent and two rookies to the company in Mason Andrews and Maria Milonas. Since it was only the three that match like normal is changed to just a triple threat. The three circle each other and the bell sounds and we are underway. Mason and CDE exchange a few words before locking up and Mason gaining the upper hand early with a side headlock. Mason clenches the move in but Maria shows that she is no slouch by smartly poking the eye of Mason to break the hold. She hits a knee to the stomach and then spinning heel kick. She quickly covers Mason but CDE is there to break it up. CDE has Maria up and lands a crushing Spinebuster! He covers but Maria kicks out at two! RILEY STEVENS: “These three look good.” Mason and CDE are back at it, this time Mason able to land a swinging neckbreaker. Instead of going for the cover though, Mason applies a one legged Boston Crab! CDE is in the middle of the ring and has nowhere to go. He could tap at any moment. Mason looks to upset the CDE veteran as Mason screams “Tap! Tap!” Mason leans farther into the move and CDE looks to be ready to tap but Maria is back to her feet. Super kick! The sound of Maria’s foot against the face of Mason echo’s loudly and the crowd lets out a loud “OHHH!” Maria looks to have it one as she goes to cover but CDE grabs her by the hair! PACKAGE PILEDRIVER! Maria is folded up thanks to the devastating move. CDE covers..1…2…3! CDE is victorious in his GIW return! REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner by way of pinfall…Christopher Darren Erns!” These fans give CDE a mixed reaction upon his return to GIW as the referee stands him up and raises CDE’s arm into the air as the man that returned to GIW shows no rust as he gets the victory though Andrews and Milonas prove themselves tonight, but will get a chance next week to bounce back from a loss and prove it was just a fluke. WINNER: CHRISTOPHER DARREN ERNS
