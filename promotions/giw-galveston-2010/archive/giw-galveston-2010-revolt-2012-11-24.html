GIW — Card

Revolt, 24 November 2012

24 November 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: SATURDAY NIGHT REVOLT LIVE! At the San Luis Hotel – November 24, 2012 on GIW.com In what is almost eerie way to return to GIW the camera picks up rolling in the parking lot where we are given flashbacks to the last Revolt show, also known as GIW100. However instead of Blake Beckett walking through the parking lot is Aspen Chaud wearing a business dress with glasses on her faces the loud clank of her heels against the ground literally the only thing that can be heard in a uncharacteristically quiet parking lot area as she heads past a few workers, that only seem to go quiet as she passes. Unlike Blake’s walk there are no introductions…There are no shenanigans, just business as Aspen continues to walk her way until she reaches an area where a group of the backstage workers as well as a few wrestlers can be seen gathered around in a circle, staring at Aspen who looks around at the group standing in front of her with just a simple stare before releasing one of her always questionable “smiles” as she pulls her glasses off her eyes, probably just for looks as well as Zachary Pagonis can be seen shaking his head distraught, while others have fallen into silence at this strange site of their new boss. ASPEN CHAUD: “Let’s get to work.” With that some of the workers scatter off to do there thing, some slower then others including Zachary Pagonis who walks up shaking his head seeming to want no part of working for Aspen, a feeling probably felt by more then him. Finally the last of the workers and wrestlers scatter as Aspen places her glasses back over her eyes, chuckling as she heads on her way yet again. BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! From there we head to the ring as the pyro goes off in the Hotel as these fans are shaking the place a bit with excitement, they may not know how Aspen will do as a Boss, but they know that regardless of how long it lasts…GIW is back. The camera pans around these fans all of them rocking the same gear they were back a few months ago, holding similar signs as always, and proudly representing Galveston Island Wrestling even if they don’t trust the new leader as we finally reach Oliver and Riley who are already seated at the announce table Riley looking a lot more clean shaven since the last time we saw him on the announcement show, his smile also taking the place of a frown. RILEY STEVENS: “WELCOME BACK! It has been too long since I could kick this show off like this and frankly it feels damn good to say we are LIVE HERE ON REVOLT for an amazing night of wrestling. Top to bottom this card features some of the best talent GIW has ever had capped by an amazing Three Way in the Main Event! This is a show to really get things going again, time to shake off the rust. Here we go!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “You seem a lot happier since the last time we saw you, was worried you were going to commit suicide.” RILEY STEVENS: “You were worried?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “There has to be worse commentators out there then you…” RILEY STEVENS: “Thanks…Oliver. Regardless we’re back lets get this thing started! As you all know every match counts toward the First to Five race for the Undisputed title, so here we go.” These fans go wild with cheers, as Riley’s voice shows that of a man with hope which at this point is all we can hope for as we take a second to have a look at the Main Event contest, hyping up all three stars and explaining the rules of the contest. QUOTE As we head back to the ring for the contest that has started every single Revolt show in the past; already standing in the ring is Eliza Terry a former Television Champion one of about a thousand prepped and ready to go as “Verse” by New Fury hits and these fans look to the entrance ramp as Kassandra Kayne a debuting star walks out from the back making her walk down towards the ring these fans really showing no real knowledge of her waiting for her to give them something to judge her by but they will have to wait as she casually enters the ring looking around at the crowd with a grin. RILEY STEVENS: “This match is the Open Battle Royal and Kassandra has a huge opportunity to beat a veteran of GIW in Eliza Terry.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “We’ll see.” Waiting for any more participants takes a moment and right before the referee can call for the bell “Dazed and Confused” by Led Zeppelin hits and these fans go crazy in the hotel here tonight the place deafening, not just for the man that enters but at the sheer shock at seeing him as Wake Walker walks out from the back with his usual slow and methodical entrance as these fans are jumping around screaming at each other as if they can’t already see who it is as the former ‘Galveston Island Champion’ Wake Walker heads down towards the ring Eliza perhaps knowing a lot about him has fear in here eyes while Kayne shows little regard knowing nothing of the former champion and showing little fear to the reaction he’s getting. RILEY STEVENS: “If you’d have told me that this man was going to be here tonight, I wouldn’t have believed you. GIW is back and so is Wake Walker!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Wow…” Oliver’s sentiments are shared by most as Walker enters the ring as he stares at both Kayne and Terry both with different outlooks on the chances now as the referee calls for the bell. DING DING DING! This place is hyped up for the return of Wake Walker as he looks around the ring as Kassandra Kayne looks at him unafraid, the two making eye contact realizing that in this one they are each other’s true competition. They meet up in the center of the ring Kayne seeming to be the only one not getting crazy over Walker at this point but before the two can fight Eliza intervenes feeling left out as she clubs Kayne down from behind as she looks over at Walker still fear in her eyes as Wake steps back letting her go at it for now, still one of the most intelligent ring men as Eliza pounds at Kayne on the mat, though she slowly starts to get up. Terry grabs her as soon as she gets up tossing her off the ropes waiting for her on the rebound where she tosses a elbow only for Kassandra to duck underneath it heading on the rebound off the ropes where she meets her in the middle of the ring with a clothesline of her own getting these fans going a bit as Eliza bounces right up walking straight into another clothesline, this time Eliza heads back into the corner favoring her shoulder as Kayne rushes at her and meets her with a running discus elbow smash in the corner putting Terry on dream street falling down to the mat in the corner. RILEY STEVENS: “What an elbow!” Surprising nobody Walker heads towards Kayne to get himself into it, but surprising everybody is Kayne who rears up and smacks him in the chin with a Superkick as he walks over to her as Walker stumbles back into the ropes as she seemed to be just a step ahead of him as she looks at Terry in the corner then rushes at Walker who is recovering on the ropes only for Wake to show his ring awareness ducking under her attempt to send him out of the ring lowering his shoulder and tossing her over the top rope to the outside of the ring Kassandra landing hard her momentum dying off a bit as Walker shakes off the unexpected shot to the chin as he walks back over to Terry who is just now getting back to her feet in the corner walking out of it trying to recover from that elbow but probably thinking she’s Gray again as Walker shows little care for letting her lick her wounds hoisting her up over his shoulder walking around with her over his shoulder with ease before dropping her down to the mat hard, as these fans watch her struggle as Walker doesn’t seem to think that’ll be enough as he hoists her back up into the air then between his legs hoisting her up then down with a huge Powerbomb that will end it surely as he makes the cover 1…2…Kayne kicks him off! RILEY STEVENS: “Kayne stops the pin.” Terry rolls out of the way out of it still as Kassandra uses a kick to his chest to stun him so she can head around him grabbing his arm behind him then setting up into the ‘Divorce Court’ a Hammerlock into a Single arm DDT! These fans are pretty jazzed up about this new girl pretty amazed by what she’s done so far as she hooks the legs hoping to put an end to the Walker hype early 1…2-Kick Out! Not going to be enough to put away a guy like Walker as Kayne gets up not seeming too upset about the kick out instead just waiting for Wake who starts to get to his feet, but Terry catches her eye getting up first and grabs her by the wrist pulling her right into a ‘Ripcord Roaring Lariat’ a Wrist-lock transitioning into a Discus Lariat! These fans going crazy as they realize she may have it. RILEY STEVENS: “Terry is done.” Kayne heads down for the pin 1…2…Walker pulls her up off of the pin in between his legs looking around wanting only to win as he sets her up for the ‘Lethal Injection’ a Sit-Down Powerbomb that lands hard as he sets it right up into a pin 1…2…Kayne kicks out! These fans look stunned as even Walker seems impressed and shocked as Kayne gets her shoulder up but even though she managed to kick out she is still out of it rolling under the ropes then flopping out of the ring as Walker shakes his head and picks up Terry instead hoisting her over his shoulder then dropping her down with the same move but there is not a chance she kicks out of this one 1…2…3! Walker wins. REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner by way of pinfall…WAKE WALKER!” Walker gets up to his feet looking outside of the ring at Kayne who is stirring a bit with a nod as the referee raises up his arm as he has just achieved victory in brutal fashion in his first match back and these fans are happy to greet him with big cheers as he lowers his arm and heads out of the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “What a huge return and big win for Wake Walker who in his return is the first man to get a victory in the Race to Five.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Kayne made it harder then expected though.” Walker helps up Kayne on the outside but she shrugs him off a bit still a little upset she go so close before the defeat but still having to have gained a lot of respect from everyone here tonight in her first GIW Match as Walker heads to the back but as he is going up the ramp the lights go out and the small bit of lighting at the top of the ramp lets us see Walker as an ‘A’ appears on the ground right in front of him. RILEY STEVENS: “Is that what I think it is, the ‘A’ that has been a message from Alioth Starre so many times before.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Is he back?” Walker seems to have done his homework as he doesn’t seem shocked at all as he looks down at the ‘A’ on the ground as he shakes his head and heads to the back as he goes through the curtain the lights return to normal. WINNER: WAKE WALKER Heading to the backstage area we are greeted by the almost saddening view of Aspen Chaud sitting in a freshly set up office area for the night looking comfortable in her chair as she goes over some sort of paper work on her desk, as the door opens and Ryan Cavallari enters through the door looking around at the make shift office as Aspen continues to do her work as Ryan grins looking at her. RYAN CAVALLARI: “You look good in your office.” Aspen hardly even gives him a look up from the paper work she has going as Ryan leans against the table having a look at her little name on the front of the desk as she speaks to him while still writing. ASPEN CHAUD: “Not now Ryan, there’s surprisingly a lot of work that goes along with this job…” She shakes her head seeming to have probably taken this a bit lightly as she moves the paper she wrote out of the way as Ryan goes to walk around her desk when the door again opens as this time its Alexis Terry immediately throwing up her arms, in anger. ALEXIS TERRY: “Should have figured you two would be in here together. So how easy is it going to be for the cheater to win the belt this time? I mean, something tells me you’re going to take in his pins in the bedroom into account for the race to five?” This gets Aspen’s attention as she and Ryan stare over at Alexis who doesn’t seem the happiest about her new boss, as Aspen takes the chance to respond. ASPEN CHAUD: “It’s five wins and if you’ve seen Ryan’s match tonight you’ll know I am doing him no favors. I’m willing to forget the fact that you are a jealous…bitch but you have to just trust me ok? First to five, no funny business. So you better worry less about me, and more about your match tonight.” Alexis naturally gives her some untrusting eyes as Ryan scoffs at her, the tension in the room so high just thinking about what these three have all been through before today as Alexis shakes her head and walks out, unable to believe Aspen could be anything more then a conniving bitch that she’s always been as Ryan goes in for a kiss but Aspen stops him. ASPEN CHAUD: “You should probably get ready.” Aspen seeming stressed and not in the best of mood’s shrugs off the kiss from Ryan, giving him one on the cheek only as he walks off nodding his head knowing she’s probably right even if it is only because she’s in that bad mood as we head back out to the ring the crowd still hot from the first contest. QUOTE Heading back to this excited crowd we are now prepped for the second match of the evening, this one full of intrigue as we head to the ramp as "I Can't Keep Still" by KPM Music and the crowd quickly pops for a familiar face in Jeremy Baltimore, as he walks out with a smile on his face heading down the ramp quickly high fiving a few fans along the way. RILEY STEVENS: “Here we have Jeremy Baltimore a man whose been here awhile but has never got over the hump, in a first to five series he just needs to catch lightning in a bottle to win.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Not going to happen.” Baltimore walks around the ring with a happy smile as he walks over to the corner using it to stretch out a bit as "Darker Side of Me" by The Veer Union hits and again the crowd pops for another familiar face here tonight as the former GIW Television Champion and Tag Team Champion; Joshua Acquin heads out from the back making his slow walk towards the ring much more methodical then Jeremy flanked by Austin Acquin and a unknown female manager. Austin and the girl head around to a corner while Acquin slowly gets in the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Acquin’s never really been a top guy here in GIW but having won the Television and Tag Team belts before he’s now got his eyes set firmly on the Undisputed Championship.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “We’ll see…” Acquin looks out at his brother and manager giving them a nod as Baltimore walks along the ropes prepped and ready as the referee turns and calls for the bell to get this started. DING DING DING! The second match of the night is started and these two go old fashion heading to the middle of the ring for a lock up. The two of them wanting to send an early message Acquin the longer wrestler but Baltimore the bigger one as the two go back and forth before finally Jeremy gets the upper hand tossing him off the ropes but on the rebound Acquin shoulder checks him down to the mat bouncing over him on the ground then hitting the ropes again, this time Baltimore spins over on the ground making Acquin jump over him as he then bounces to his feet hoisting him up then putting him down with a huge Spinebuster! These fans cheer that move on as Baltimore gets up screaming out at them to get on there feet as he stalks Acquin from behind, waiting for him to slowly get to his feet before wrapping his arms around Josh’s waist, going for a big German Suplex but Acquin hooks the leg so he can’t send him over then tosses back a huge elbow sending Baltimore backwards as Acquin spins and brings up a huge knee to his face, sending Jeremy back into the corner favoring his nose. RILEY STEVENS: “What a huge knee!” Baltimore is too busy making sure his nose isn’t broken from the knee when Joshua rushes at him with a big clothesline squishing him in the corner, as Acquin lets him stumble out into him again this time so he can hit a Flowing DDT planting Baltimore to the ground before crawling over on top of him for the pinfall 1…Kick Out. Not even close to putting away Baltimore as he gets up and looks out at his two teammates at ringside giving them a nod to assure them things are going well as he hoists up Baltimore to his feet using one arm but Jeremy perhaps playing possum is able to twist that arm and bring it behind his back. Using it just to get to his back and determined not to let himself get countered again he goes quickly into a German Suplex this time landing it, only for Acquin to get right back up though doing so right into Baltimore who hoists him up onto his shoulders then back down for the Samoan Drop as Baltimore this time makes the cover 1…Kick Out. RILEY STEVENS: “Competitive match up so far.” Jeremy gets up starting to get into it more and more calling for Joshua to get up perhaps making the mistake of underestimating Acquin a bit he sets him up between his legs looking for the ‘Match Killer’ a Sit-Out Powerbomb but before he can hit it Acquin transitions out then spins Baltimore around himself going for the ‘Face Breaker’ his version of the Kill Switch but Jeremy transitions out as well ending up in his stomach tackling him through the ropes to the outside, both of them landing on the outside with a huge thud these fans grimacing at the impact made by the two of them as Austin can be seen checking on Joshua from afar both he and Baltimore not moving too much, Joshua holding his back and Baltimore his shoulder as the referee starts the count 1…2…3…At the count of four Acquin gets up first shaking off the cob-webs, dropping a knee down onto Baltimore’s shoulder, as he rolls around in pain. RILEY STEVENS: “These two better get in the ring here.” Acquin does just that rolling into the ring to restart the count only to walk back out as Baltimore starts to get to his hands and knees as Austin yells out at Joshua to go for the kill as Acquin takes a few step back as the referee again hits the four count, as Josh positions himself for the ‘Last Call’ a Punt to the Head! These fans know that it’ll end it as Acquin rushes at him at the count of six but Baltimore goes into survival mode as he catches Acquin off guard tossing him over the announce table; Riley and Oliver narrowly getting out of the way as Josh hits the barricade. As Baltimore heads towards the ring, the referee hitting nine just as he gets back into the ring as the referee makes the ten count, counting Acquin out. REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner by count out…JEREMY BALTIMORE!” These fans are a little disappointed by the count out as Baltimore is just able to get back in holding his hurt shoulder from the fall out of the ring as Austin and Josh’s new manager walk around helping him up on the other side of the announce table, Austin throwing his arm over his shoulder as he helps him up Acquin able to walk on his own though, as Baltimore gets up drained from this fast paced war. RILEY STEVENS: “In a Race to Five it doesn’t matter how you win, just that you win and that’s what Baltimore did tonight.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Wins a win.” Baltimore looks out at Acquin wanting to show his respect but Joshua always a proud man mouths that this isn’t over as he walks towards the back as Jeremy rolls out of the ring holding his shoulder high fiving a few fans with his good arm as he walks to the back as well. WINNER: JEREMY BALTIMORE After a short little hype video for the next Revolt, back on Wednesdays we are sent backstage just as Jeremy Baltimore gets to the back looking a little beat up holding his neck though still happy having secured a huge victory even if it was by a count out after a big battle with Acquin. Heading back besides the catering area where Ace is digging into some chips, turning around as Baltimore walks behind him patting him on the back. ACE BALDWIN: “Good win.” Baldwin turns back to dig into the chips a bit as Baltimore nods his head, not moving yet instead waiting for Ace to look back at him, raising his eyebrow wondering why he’s still there. JEREMY BALTIMORE: “Thanks, but you know the way I see it. The quickest and best way to get to five is to beat the guys that win tonight next week. So even though I have nothing against you; say you win tonight. We meet next week, one gets two the other falls behind?” Baltimore extends his hand as Ace tosses another chip into his mouth then extends his hand shaking it with a nod the two ready to get it going next wee granted Ace gets the win. ACE BALDWIN: “See you next week.” Getting himself in the right mindset before his huge main event match tonight Ace shows his confidence as Baltimore walks off favoring his hurt shoulder still as we go back to the ring, a ring that’s already full. QUOTE As we get to the ring we already see it full with four wrestlers all four of them completely new faces to this company, leaving the intrigue at a high level. All four have a shot at getting one win closer to being a real dark horse in this tournament. Alexandria Espoir, Ethan Wrathmore, Izzy Lancaster, and Jeffrey Douglas align the ring in each corner all looking ready to go. On the outside is a woman standing behind Wrathmore cheering him on. RILEY STEVENS: “Four new Aspen signings right here, lets see how Aspen has done at scouting.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Knowing her, amazing.” Clearly wanting to get on the Boss’s good side Oliver shows his support for Aspen while Riley can be heard scoffing it off as the four wrestlers are ready to go as is the referee who calls for the bell. DING DING DING! In this battle of four new stars we have four fresh faces to scout as all four of them start the match off as expected going into an all out war all four trying to make a name for themselves but quickly the stand out is revealed as Ethan Wrathmore throws up a dropkick to Alexandria first sending her out of the ring then another to Jeffrey who tries to get the upper hand finally finishing it with a third one to Izzy effectively taking out all three of his opponents in one amazing show of athleticism after the next as the woman on the outside known as Rayne claps for him loudly, watching him do his thing as he gets up to his feet with a grin on his face watching as Izzy and Jeffrey get up side by side wanting not only to win but leave a lasting impression at that. Bouncing off the ropes as the two get all the way up and connecting with a Spear to both of them at the same time getting up to his feet as these fans can’t help but give some applause for the new guy who has thus far made a statement. From the outside Alexandria slips back into the ring making the mistake of walking into the ball of momentum that Ethan has going as she tosses a clothesline at the back of his head that he ducks and then catches her with a Reverse DDT down to the mat. RILEY STEVENS: “Aspen may have found a blue chipper.” Wrathmore heads across the ring to the top rope looking to show his arsenal of high flying maneuvers here tonight as he looks around at the crowd but he waits a bit too long as Lancaster is back up jumping up to the top rope, trying to pull him off but he lands a big punch almost losing his footing and before even really getting that footing back he jumps off amazingly still landing a 450’ Splash over Izzy and onto Alexandria in the middle of the ring. Hopping up to his feet he looks out at Rayne then turns back to Douglas in the ring caught off guard by a Enzuigiri that may have just ended all that momentum he had going. Stunned momentarily, Douglas dropkicks him getting some revenge sending him under the ropes but Wrathmore keeps a hold of them so he doesn’t fall out. Lancaster is back up as she rushes at him as he leap frogs her then when she stops he smacks her with a Superkick to the jaw sending her stumbling through the ropes and out of the ring as Wrathmore is back up going Springboard with a Dropkick to Douglas’s back that sends him flying through the ropes to the outside as with just he and Alexandria left, Wrathmore looks to get that win as she stumbles to her feet and he connects with ‘The Cut Above the Rest’ a Scoop Legged Stunner that puts her out as Wrathmore climbs on top 1…2…3! Game. REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner by way of pinfall…ETHAN WRATHMORE!” Everyone has to admit that Wrathmore not only put up his first win but also looked damn good doing it as he was the stand out in this match up of new stars as the referee raises his arm into the air though he’s quickly replaced by Rayne who has entered the ring to raise Ethan’s arm. RILEY STEVENS: “What a showing by Ethan Wrathmore, this kid could be something special.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “So is his manager…” Despite Oliver’s pervy comment, nobody can take anything away from Wrathmore’s win tonight as he has just put himself in the hunt for the Undisputed title, what a story that could be. WINNER: ETHAN WRATHMORE Right to the backstage area we go where Alexis Terry is stretching out in the backstage area already with her ring gear on her match up next as Daniel Starr is seen walking by heading to the ring shouldering his North Beach Championship the two sharing a stare down for just a moment before Starr walks out of the picture as Alexis stretches out an arm when Zachary Pagonis approaches her from behind looking as upset as he did to start the show, as Terry looks over at him with a smile. ALEXIS TERRY: “Hey Zach, cheer up. It’s not all bad, at least we got a show right?” Terry pats him on the back as Zachary shrugs his shoulders having a hard time finding any light at the end of the tunnel knowing he’ll be used mostly as Aspen’s bearer of bad news as Pagonis goes ahead and does just that. ZACHARY PAGONIS: “I guess but Aspen told me to come and tell you that next week you got Wake Walker in the Main Event of Revolt in a Submission match. She also told me to say “I hope this doesn’t make you lose focus on this match.” Alexis has a small chuckle as Aspen probably won’t be pleased that he didn’t say it near the way that she wanted him to as Terry doesn’t seem phased by the news not letting Aspen get her way with her scare tactics. ALEXIS TERRY: “So be it, try to have a good night Zach.” Pagonis nods and trudges off as Alexis heads out towards the entrance path with a huge smile on her face having come out of retirement and moments away from her return to the ring yet again, that alone bringing a smile to her face. QUOTE Already standing in the ring displaying his GIW North Beach Championship proudly in the air is Daniel Starr walking to each side of the ring screaming at them that he’s the longest reigning champion ever. These fans just decide to give it to him, booing him as usual as the always brash Starr pretends to soak it in as his music fades out and "Clarity" by Zedd hits and these fans go crazy with cheers for the former Undisputed Champion, Alexis Terry! RILEY STEVENS: “You know I don’t like to choose favorites but Alexis Terry has to be one of the front runners. She’s a veteran, she retired before but you can tell she is reinvigorated.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Aspen hates her, there’s no way she gets to five.” Alexis heads down the ring high fiving the fans along the way, easily one of the loudest reactions of the night for the former champion as she walks around the ring making sure to high five nearly everyone on the way as Starr continues to taunt her with the belt in the ring, as she steps up then through the ropes as Daniel heads to the top rope displaying his belt trying to steal the spotlight a bit as Alexis rolls her eyes and waits in the ring as the referee turns and calls for the bell. DING DING DING! As soon as the match starts Starr walks across the ring doing his usual pre-match banter screaming at Alexis that she’s nothing, that she should get out of his GIW ring and that he’s the face of GIW. Slamming his finger into his chest these fans boo him as Terry looks at him then out at the fans as he tells her to look at him, the face of GIW. Finally he tries for a cheap shot but she grabs his hand then drops it down into a ‘Flawless Victory’ a Inverted Stomp Facebreaker showing him what she thinks of that face of his. Stumbling back a bit trying to fix that face of his as some blood can be seen coming from his nose as Alexis grabs him and spins him right into the ‘Glam-Go-Round’! These fans go wild as she has him set up already for the pin 1…2…3! REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner by way of pinfall…ALEXIS TERRY!” These fans go crazy at just how quickly Terry was able to finish this one the fans getting hyped up as Starr bounces up claiming that he kicked out though Alexis probably had him pinned for a four count as she gets up with a huge smile on her face walking over to the corner jumping up to the top rope, while Starr berates the referee before realizing his nose may be broken, reaching up for it holding it trying to stop the bleeding. RILEY STEVENS: “Knowing you have to win five wins and knowing you are going to be in some wars, this quick win may really give Alexis a huge advantage, she just embarrassed Starr.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “She caught him off guard, he broke his nose and still fought like a warrior!” Terry bounces off as Starr rolls out of the ring grabbing his belt tearing it away dropping it then picking it up yelling at the fans that he’s still the longest reigning champion, as Alexis rolls out of the ring hugging a few of the fans at ringside not even ruining her make up or breaking a sweat in this match up as she turns to the camera sending a message to her loving Boss by holding up one finger, though not the one that would get blurred just the one that meant she only needed four more to become her champion. WINNER: ALEXIS TERRY Unfortunately yet again we are sent back to the office of Aspen Chaud where she is set up at her desk with a grin on her face already mid-sentence as the segment picks up. ASPEN CHAUD: “That bitch won? Whatever, this is going to look very professional; this is something Blake never did! I am going to bring this place to levels he never achieved, and the best part is I can have myself on the show as much as I want. So just give me the motion when the promo piece has ended and we’ll get it started.” The camera man takes a second hoping she doesn’t realize he’s already rolling as she fixes up a few things on her desk trying to look spiffy as he then gives her the motion as she sets up not realizing it’s been rolling the entire time. ASPEN CHAUD: “Hello everyone this is Aspen Chaud the new owner of GIW and what a show we have had so far. What great wins we have already seen by Ethan Wrathmore, Jeremy Baltimore, Alexis Terry, and even the returning Wake Walker and we have still one match to go. But I am taking this moment to let you in on a few details for the next huge Revolt.” Aspen again trying to look and sound as professional as possible still wearing the glasses we’ve never ever seen her wear before today, her legs crossed under the table and that same smile we’ve seen all night on her face. ASPEN CHAUD: “GIW will be returning to Wednesday night’s on December fifth in Courville Stadium. As has been announced tonight’s winners Alexis Terry and Wake Walker will fight for two wins in a Submission Match in the Main Event. My new signings Ethan Wrathmore a winner tonight will fight the impressive Kassandra Kayne as Jeffrey Douglas and Joshua Acquin look to get on the board as well. Alioth Starre will also make his return to the ring against Ryan Cavallari. So be there, because GIW is going to just keep going up and up.” With that the cameraman now actually clicks the off button as that promo piece that was supposed to air prior now shows for real as they hype the show that she just announced on December 5th! Alongside that it is announced that Alioth Starre is indeed returning to GIW a fact just sort of spoiled by Aspen. QUOTE It’s now finally time for the match that has so hugely been hyped up a triple threat match between three of GIW’s best and already standing in the ring ready to go are the two superstars who you could argue were the closest to winning the Undisputed title before the companies closing in Attack Attack and Ryan Cavallari two of GIW’s top stars as these fans boo the both of them, no respect at all for them as they stand in the ring glaring off at them until "Who Dat" by J-Cole hits and as soon as these fans see Ace Baldwin this place is rocking, yet another Undisputed contender coming out to the ring! RILEY STEVENS: “You know how I was talking about favorites Oliver? Here is one of them, Ace Baldwin.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Not a chance he’s the underdog in this one; Ryan and Attack are two of GIW’s best; Ace is old news.” Baldwin looks into the ring as Attack and Ryan look back at him both of them looking at him like a meal as they all know there is three of them and only one of them can get closer to five with a win, as Ace slides into the ring looking across at them waiting for them to try something as he heads to the top rope to pose, but out of no where Attack rushes across the ring grabbing him slamming him back hard off the top rope back first into the mat the referee panics and calls for the bell to get this started. DING DING DING! As this thing gets started Attack lays down boot after boot into a downed Baldwin not wanting him to get any sort of momentum to start this match instead just wanting to eliminate him knowing that to win this kind of match you have to get rid of somebody to make it a singles match. The fall clearly stunning Ace and hurting him as well, he tries to get free but soon Ryan Cavallari heads across the ring at first looking like he may intervene but then joining instead realizing just what Attack does that it’s best just to eliminate Ace and do this thing themselves. Baldwin tries his best to break free but the two of them laying boots into him is just too much as finally the two decide that this isn’t going to be quick enough instead hoisting up Ace and tossing him off the ropes, already hurting a bit Baldwin runs right back into a huge double clothesline from both men. Looking at one another they realize that Baldwin is tough and that isn’t going to be enough, as they pick up by both arms tossing him over into the corner he barely gets there already looking like he’s been in a long match from all the bruises he’s already gotten. RILEY STEVENS: “It may not be fun to watch but sometimes in these matches you do have to eliminate somebody to be able to get the pinfall.” Baldwin looking around for an escape route in the corner as Ryan and Attack decide who will go first as Attack does so heading at him going for a spear in the corner but Baldwin spins out of the way sending Attack shoulder first into the corner. These fans cheer as Ryan now tries his shot at him only for Baldwin to shoulder him up and over the ropes, though Ryan lands smoothly onto the other side. With Ace in the middle Attack and Ryan again go for broke from both sides going for a double clothesline to wedge him in between but Ace ducks it and sends Ryan and Attack into each other both clotheslining one another Ryan getting the brunt of it sending Attack to the mat while Ace follows it up with a Springboard Heel Kick to Ryan over the ropes on the outside landing back down in the ring as Cavallari falls to the ground with a bang on the outside. These fans are going wild now with Baldwin’s resurgence into the match as he calls for Attack to get up looking to get his revenge for the cheap shot, grabbing him up and hitting him with a Suplex! Looking to dish back at him for what he did to his back Baldwin keeps a hold of it, then hoists him up then back down with a second Suplex. Finally getting him back up this time switch into a Northern Lights Suplex with a hook of the leg 1…2-Kick Out, Attack is still not done. RILEY STEVENS: “Baldwin is putting it all together now.” But while he was doing the beautiful Suplex’s Ryan has fetched out a chair from ringside knowing that anything goes in a match like this as he gets back into the ring wielding that steel chair as Ace gets up only just in time to see him coming ducking under the huge chair shot by Ryan then getting a huge dropkick to the face sending that same chair into it. These fans go wild after that as Ryan falls to the mat down and out it seems the chair being used against him as Baldwin looks from one to the other waiting to see who will get up first deciding that Attack is his guy hoisting him up to his feet then putting him over his shoulder walking around for a second then dropping him down with a ‘Autodrop’ a Brain-Buster Suplex! Again looking for the pin he gets it 1…2-Ryan pulls him off still holding his face in pain but trying to do anything to save the match. RILEY STEVENS: “As we said, you got to make this thing a singles match.” Both men get up to there feet throwing punch after punch before finally Attack throws a huge low blow to Ace from behind dropping him down to a knee as together Attack and Ryan toss him from the ring through the ropes again working together both realizing Ace is having a good night, as they exit the ring picking him up as Ryan grabs at the announce table pushing everything off as the two get ready to hoist up Ace but Baldwin elbows Ryan off and then Attack as well, as Attack rushes at him as Baldwin gives him a big push up over his shoulder sending him back first into the announce table breaking it underneath him as the crowd goes crazy! And just like that Ryan rolls him up 1…Baldwin catches his arm in and transitions right into the ‘Smokin' Aces’ a Omoplata Crossface! With no where to go on the outside and Attack out cold in what was once an announce table, Ryan tries to fight it before finally having to tap out to the loudest reaction of the night. REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner by way of submission…ACE BALDWIN!” Baldwin gets up to his feet raising up his arm in victory having overcome the pre-match cheap shot and the constant team up to make the former number one contender tap out while also sending a man once though to be next in line sent crashing through the table. Baldwin has just put himself one step closer to the promise land, and regaining that Undisputed strap. RILEY STEVENS: “What a huge victory here tonight ladies and gentleman for Ace Baldwin a former Undisputed contender who has just got his win but perhaps did so sending the loudest message here in the Main Event, to the rest of the roster.” Ace’s happiness seems to help him forget about the pain that he endured in this one as the referee checks on Attack still lying in the rubble of the announce table while Ryan grasps at his neck feeling the pain on the outside as Baldwin ends the return show heading to the top rope. RILEY STEVENS: “We are back and it’s only going to get better from here. Wake Walker, Jeremy Baltimore, Ethan Wrathmore, Alexis Terry, and Ace Baldwin picked up wins tonight but we won’t crown a champion until somebody hits five, tune in next week as we get closer to crowning a new champion.” As Baldwin stands in the ring you can’t help but wonder if he’s going to be that man as he looks out at the crowd arms held high as the return show slowly fades to black. WINNER: ACE BALDWIN
