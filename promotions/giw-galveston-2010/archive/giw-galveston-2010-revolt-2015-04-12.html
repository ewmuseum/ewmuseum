As soon as Duff has his balance back, he hits The Sir with an arm drag across the ring. Popping back up to his feet, The Sir goes after Briggs in hopes for a quick retaliation. But a high angled dropkick from Duff stops The Sir in his tracks, but it isn’t enough to knock him down! Everyone is rather impressed by The Sir managing to stay up after that dropkick. Standing firm in the center of the ring, The Sir mockingly laughs at Duff, who responds to this by bouncing off the ropes. On his way back, Briggs hits a clothesline on The Sir, but again, The Sir barely flinches. Instead of letting Briggs try again, The Sir just slaps the taste out of his opponent’s mouth. An audible “Oooo” is heard from the crowd after the slap as Duff is rubbing his cheek in anger. The Sir uses this time to go off the ropes himself, but Duff Briggs gets payback with a thunderous spinebuster! This time The Sir goes down to the mat and Duff goes for a pin attempt. However, before the ref can get in position, The Sir tosses Briggs off of him and the two men get back to their feet. SARAH SHARP: "An impressive test of strength from both men. The powerhouses are looking to tear down the ring early on." Not wanting another test of strength, The Sir plants a kick to Duff’s midsection that causes him to hunch over. A snap DDT brings Duff right onto the mat. A bit dazed, Briggs is brought back up to his feet and gets hit with a snap suplex this time. Despite the slow start, The Sir starts to control the pacing of the match as he starts stomping right onto Duff’s chest. The referee pulls The Sir away after a near disqualification; allowing Briggs to catch his breath in a ring corner. Seeing this, The Sir rushes to the corner and drives his shoulder into Duff’s midsection. It appears as if The Sir is going in for the kill early on as he starts to lift the dazed Duff Briggs onto the turnbuckle. Once The Sir gets Duff in position, it’s his turn to head up as he positions himself on the second rope. OLIVER THIBODAUX: "If the ring collapses, we all know who wins! Bronx and The Sir via knockout!" But Duff isn’t allowing it to happen as he delivers a strong kick to The Sir’s face. After doing so, The Sir eats a few elbows to the side of the head before falling down to the mat. Shaking the cobwebs from his head, Duff sees the position he’s in and stands on top of the turnbuckle. Duff leaps off the top rope and hits a perfect elbow drop onto The Sir. This seems to have taken a bit of energy out of both men as they’re sprawled out on the mat after the move. Bronx and Callum are calling for their tag team partners to make the tag as the crowd is chanting “CALLUM!” louder and louder. SARAH SHARP: "I think it's pretty obvious who the fan favorite is in this match." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Yeah, I agree. LET'S GO BRONX!" Soon enough, the fans get their wish as Chris Callum and Bronx Valescence are tagged into the match. Both men are running at each other, but Callum is the first to strike with a flying forearm. It isn’t enough to take Bronx down though, as he’s back on his feet. Callum is prepared as he hits Bronx with an arm drag, but one isn’t enough as he hits a second one. Bronx is hit with a third arm drag and rolls out of the ring in the process. Valescence walks over and grabs his sunglasses, putting them on as he places his arms atop the ringside barriers in the corner to catch a breather. Instead of biting the bait, Chris patiently awaits Bronx’s return back into the ring. And as the referee’s count goes on, Bronx finally gets back up, puts his sunglasses back in his corner, and rolls back into the ring. It’s a replay of what we saw earlier as Callum gets Bronx with a another arm drag, but on his second attempt, Valescence holds his ground and instead, tosses Callum with his own arm drag. Popping back on his feet, Callum ducks a lariat attempt from Bronx as Valescence repays the favor by sidestepping a dropkick from Chris. Both men get back to their feet after their counterattacks and dodges. Bronx goes after Callum who’s anticipating an attack, but gets a surprise as Valescence gets a roll up pin 1… KICK OUT! SARAH SHARP: "A kick out at the one has to be a bit demoralizing for a wrestler." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "I don't think I'd consider getting a surprise roll up on the champ a 'demoralizing' moment for a wrestler, Sarah. Let alone for Bronx Valescence." Bronx gets back on his feet and gets behind Callum to wrap his arms around his waist. Chris tries his hardest to get out of the waist lock, but it isn’t enough as he’s brought down to the mat with a release German suplex. Callum slowly gets back up to his feet as Bronx puts him in headlock and just starts to hammer in some stiff shots to Callum’s head. A snapmare by Valescence puts Chris down to the mat in a seated position as Bronx follows up with a stiff kick straight to the back of Callum’s head. Chris Callum is brought back up to his feet again looking like he’s completely out of this match. It seems as if Valescence is going to try for another German suplex, but Chris is able to slide his hands down to break the hold. Once free from Valescence’s grip, Callum hits Bronx with a back elbow that stuns him momentarily. Perhaps shocking everyone, Callum leaps off the middle rope and hits a springboard stunner on Bronx! Valescence is back on his feet, but he’s not all there as Callum hits Bronx with a powerslam and hooks the leg 1… 2… KICK OUT! OLIVER THIBODAUX: "They should disqualify Callum right there! He was holding onto Bronx's tights!" Callum is hyping up alongside the crowd as he gets back on his feet looking to inflict more damage to Bronx. Picking up his heavier opponent, Chris looks like he’s set up for an Alabama slamma, but Valescence is able to wiggle his way out and get behind Callum. Upon turning around, Chris eats a super kick that sends him flying back into his corner. Duff slaps his partner on the back and catches Bronx off guard with a Thez press. Crashing to the mat, Duff starts laying into Valescence with a series of punches to the head before getting off of his opponent. Briggs is hyped up as he’s tugging on his beard and screaming at Valescence to get back to his feet. SARAH SHARP: "Duff looks like he's ready to wrap this one up." Using the ropes to guide him back up, Bronx tries to get back into the match as Duff takes charge at his opponent. Briggs was looking for a big boot, but Valescence pulled the rope down at the last second and sent his opponent flying out of the ring. Stomping his feet on the mat, Bronx anxiously awaits his opponent to stand back up and once Duff does, Valescence bounces from one side of the ring and leaps out with a Tope con Giro that sends both men crashing to the outside mats. SARAH SHARP: "Wow!" Bronx is the first man back into the ring as he catches his breath and allows the official to try and count out Briggs. It’s at around the seven count that Duff manages to get back into the ring and is instantly met with boots stomping on his back. The referee pulls Bronx back as he was starting to get carried away with the stomps. This break allows Duff to get back up to his feet and as soon as the referee is out of the way Bronx charges at Briggs. Valescence gets a surprise however as Briggs hits him with a Northern Lights suplex. Opting out of the bridge, Duff gets ahold of Bronx and lifts him up, signaling for the end. Once in position, Bronx is sent face first into the mat with Duff Briggs’ Roadkill! The belly-to-back inverted mat slam knocks Valescence out and allows Duff to wrap him up in a pin. The Sir tries to break up the pin, but gets met with a clothesline from Chris Callum to allow the pin to go on OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Come on, Bronx! Kick out of it!" 1… 2… 3! SARAH SHARP: "And there you have it! Bronx reigned supreme for a short while after that outside maneuver, but Briggs was able to reel himself back into the match." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "That's a fancy way to say he got lucky! Hell, he probably cheated too!" The Sir slams his fists into the mat in anger and rolls out of the ring holding onto the back of his head. Bronx manages to roll out and follow suit with The Sir as Duff Briggs and Chris Callum stand in the ring victorious. In a good show of sportsmanship, Chris raises the hand of Duff Briggs and allows him to get the attention as the team pulls off the victory tonight. WINNERS: DUFF BRIGGS AND CHRIS CALLUM
