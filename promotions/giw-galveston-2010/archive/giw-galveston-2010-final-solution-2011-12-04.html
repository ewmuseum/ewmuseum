GIW — Pay-per-view

Final Solution 2011, 4 December 2011

4 December 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS FINAL SOLUTION! LIVE from Benno’s on the Beach – December 4, 2011! BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! The fans here at Benno’s couldn’t be more excited and when they see that the show is about to get underway they couldn’t be louder either as the camera comes in panning around the place looking at all of the excited people ready to go, ready to watch some GIW action. Not often is there this high of stakes in the main event, and the fans look like a Harry Potter Quidditch game as the division in the crowd is fitting, everyone picking a side in the four way. Finally as the fans just start to die down we find ourselves looking at the familiar faces of Oliver and Riley both of whom are looking prepared here tonight. RILEY STEVENS: “There is that big fight feel here tonight Oliver. You can almost sense just, a classic coming on here tonight. Everyone is picking a side; Ace Baldwin, Aspen Chaud, Johnny Violence, or Tony Miranda. This is one of those matches that is going to go down in history as one of the biggest ever.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I’m on Team Baldwin!” RILEY STEVENS: “Naturally but the card before that will be just as big. Starting the show off will be the culmination of the Acquin’s rivalry. It’ll be Joshua Acquin challenging his own little brother for the GIW Television Championship. The rules are simple; if Joshua wins he retires the belt if Austin wins he proves he deserves it.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “You know that Joshua ruined these two’s brotherhood, I hope Austin kicks his ass.” RILEY STEVENS: “Right following that we’ll have an exciting match up as long time GIW star Trent Lost yet again meets Altair in the ring. Altair last week promised to dispose of Trent Lost and after the way he brutalized him the first time I can’t help but believe him.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Sometimes you’ve got to trim the fat and Trent is FAT.” RILEY STEVENS: “Then one of the most, perhaps crazy match ups I’ve ever seen. We’ve all seen Aspen Chaud last as a female in GIW. But Faith Simpson a Mark Cross trained wrestler debuted last week on Revolt and now fights for the recently brought back GIW Legacy Championship, here’s the thing she is fighting a man in Jeremy Baltimore that will probably not show much remorse.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “As he shouldn’t. She signed a waiver so this is all legal and I hope that he shows her why she doesn’t belong around here.” RILEY STEVENS: “We’ll see what she can do but then we have the MAIN EVENT. Four stars, one belt as the top four stars in GIW today will compete to see who the top dog really is. It’ll be ‘GIW Undisputed Champion’ Ace Baldwin defending against three challengers…Aspen Chaud, Johnny Violence, and Tony Miranda…This one is going to be BIG!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Not really Ace will crush them all.” RILEY STEVENS: “Lets get this show on the road.” QUOTE When we head to the ring for the one "Monster" by Skillet hits and the crowd goes wild yet again for the first time in a long time for Joshua Acquin as he walks out from the back a whole new man from the one we saw just a few weeks ago as he walks towards the ring with a smile. JAMAL JACKSON: “The following contest is for the GIW Television Championship! Introducing in the ring weighing in at two hundred and twenty seven pounds, standing six foot three inches tall…from Las Vegas, Nevada…the ‘General Manager of Revolt’…Joshua Acquin!” The fans high five him as he heads by them then up the steps and into the ring. As he gets in there his music cuts off and "Nightmare" by Avenged Sevenfold hits Austin’s old theme as he heads out from the back with the belt and a big smirk on his face as he heads down to the ring. JAMAL JACKSON: “And the champion, accompanied to the ring by Joshua Acquin, weighing in at two hundred and fifty pounds standing in at six foot five inches tall, from Las Vegas, Nevada…the ‘GIW Television Champion’…Austin Acquin!” Austin steps up into the ring and looks across at his brother with a big grin on his face, not seeming at all worried about him. RILEY STEVENS: “These two brothers have gone from best friends to this here tonight, where we finally answer the question…Which Acquin is really superior?” Austin hoists up his belt then hands it to the referee who showcases it around the ring to the fans and then hands it out and starts the match up. DING DING DING! While they are brothers there is no doubt whose side the fans are on as they cheer for Joshua as the two circle around each other then finally decide to lock up. There is a slight battle before Joshua throws him to the mat showing off his strength a bit. Austin gets up screaming out “I wasn’t ready yet!” as Joshua invites him back into another one if that’s the case. While Joshua has his hands up in the air Austin kicks him in the gut then whips him into the ropes. Austin rushes at him throwing a big clothesline that lands flush on his older brother. The crowd boos him as he backs up throwing out his arms as usual always taunting. Austin then turns around and tells his brother to get up. As soon as he gets to a knee Austin bounces off the ropes and when his big brother gets up he hits back down with a Spear, then taking the pin 1…Kick Out! Not even close for the younger Acquin as he hops up to his feet not seeming too worried as he bounces off the ropes and hits a Jumping Knee Drop. The fans seem to be growing slightly worried, as Joshua now takes a little longer to get to his feet and now it seems as though Acquin wants to taunt him a bit as he backs up into the corner setting up for his brother’s move the ‘Last Call’ a Punt to the Head! The fans scream at Joshua to get up as Austin rushes forward throwing the kick only for Joshua to bail out of the way at the last second. RILEY STEVENS: “He narrowly missed kicking his brother’s head off.” Joshua hops back up as Austin throws a wild haymaker at his brother that again misses. Then boom, boom, boom repeatedly Joshua throws left and then a right sending Austin reeling into the corner. His brother cowers in the corner as Joshua backs up and begs him to come forward but when he does he runs right into a High Knee Strike that sends Austin falling back through the ropes. Luckily he is able to land on his feet on the outside avoiding a huge tumble. But right away he turns throwing his arms and saying “Screw this!” as he starts to head to the back avoiding the match up. Joshua shakes his head and immediately rolls out of the ring chasing after him. He slams his fists into the back of his head causing Austin to spin around. The fans cheer as Joshua drags him back into the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “He’s not going to let his brother bail on this one.” Joshua tosses him back into the ring following him right in. But Austin plans this out well as he immediately throws a big kick to Joshua as he stands up in the ring then once there he sets him right in place for the ‘Crusher’ a Full Nelson Facebuster! The fans gasp until his brother elbows out and then spins it right into a ‘Face Breaker’ an Inverted Double Underhook Falconwing Facebuster! The fans go wild, they haven’t seen that move in a long time as Joshua hooks the leg for the pin, 1…2…3! JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner and the final ‘GIW Television Champion’…Joshua Acquin!” The fans go wild with cheers as Joshua Acquin is handed back the GIW Television title in an almost too familiar site as he holds up the belt into the air while his brother holds his face on the ground. The fans continue to cheer as Joshua hoists up his brother and extends a hand to his currently hurting brother. But Austin slaps it away and turns around heading out of the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Some thought this competition might have helped these two brothers but judging by that reaction Austin isn’t liking the fact his brother just retired the belt he came into the night with.” Joshua shakes his head disappointed with his brother’s lack of respect as he throws the belt over his shoulder taking it home with him as we head to the back. WINNER: JOSHUA ACQUIN (New GIW Television Champion) Once done with that we head to the back where we see Tony Miranda breathing in and out in the backstage looking to try and stay and tranquil when the door slams open and in comes Johnny Violence with a big grin on his face. JOHNNY VIOLENCE: “Hey Tony…You remember that movie? Where the down on his luck guy overcame the odds to become the champion? Remember that one? Well…it was just a movie. Fairytales don’t exist and they don’t happen.” Tony clearly was trying his best to ignore Johnny who grinned not too far behind him as Miranda continued to try to stay calm. JOHNNY VIOLENCE: “This night isn’t about you overcoming the odds. No it’s about ME winning the belt.” Violence leans down next to him, to send the message loud and clear to him. JOHNNY VIOLENCE: “So when you lose tonight just know this…that dream of yours about winning the belt? Well it’ll finally be crushed.” Violence grins and then heads on his way out of the locker room trying to play some mind games as head heads to the back. QUOTE When we return to the ring for the next contest already standing in the ring is Trent Lost looking ready to go as the fans seem to have lost all care for him. JAMAL JACKSON: “The following contest is scheduled for one fall! Introducing in the ring weighing in at two hundred twenty three pounds, standing six foot three inches tall…from Corpus Christi, Texas…Trent Lost.” No reaction at all as “Cruel Melody” by Black Light Burns hits and the fans start to actually boo. They didn’t like the way Altair acted his first night on Revolt and have already gained a bit of hatred towards the man as he heads out from the back strolling towards the ring. JAMAL JACKSON: “And his opponent, weighing in at one hundred forty pounds standing in at six foot ten inches tall, from Darkly Falls, California …“The Crimson Reaper” Altair!” Altair heads down towards the ring with a smirk growing on his face as he looks into the ring at the man that he has chosen to be his first victim here in GIW. RILEY STEVENS: “This man Altair gives me the creeps, once Trent Lost returned he chose to personally take him out.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I like the initiative.” Altair heads into the ring glaring across now at Trent, who actually seems to be a little scared per say as the referee calls for the bell. DING DING DING! Altair and Lost stood toe to toe. The paler wrestler put on a smug, toothy grin for a display before his opponent that Trent tried to smack off, but his hit was countered with a gut kick. Trent keeled over just as his opponent elbowed him upon the back of his shoulder and pulled him down to the canvas with a Fujiwara Armbar. The maneuver was locked in, but brief. It was like a message to Trent to try harder. Altair backed off from his opponent and wiped the smug grin from his own pale face, the two protruding fangs visible as his fingers passed his lips. Trent pulled himself upward onto his feet and charged forth toward his opponent. Altair whipped his arm across the chest of Lost and kicked his ankle out from under him with a side slam back down onto the canvas. Altair made a cover attempt, but instead of letting Trent Lost kick out, Altair pulled off just as the referee had brought his hand up to make the second count. He was toying with Lost. RILEY STEVENS: “This is hard to watch.” Altair gathered his victim from the canvas and dragged him over toward the ropes. He guided Trent Lost to the rim, the body of his opponent faced toward the crowd. He then used the second rope as a sort of chair to pull his legs up on as one arm wrapped around the neck, his thighs around the abdomen and locking into the submission hold – Bite of the Dragon. Trent Lost began to yell and writhe in frantic motions that seemed only to fuel the pale villain to apply the lock tighter. Eventually, before he knew his opponent would tap, Altair released the hold and let Trent fall slack on the rim of the ring, holding by the nails of his fingers, and the plaque of his teeth. Trent Lost turned, ready to enter the ring, though exhausted and near spent. As he turned, he met Altair in a grapple, this time a Suplex into the ring. A quicker entry for Trent, but one that was much more painful than just slipping under the ropes. Altair was swift and fluidic in his rising from the canvas. He absorbed the confrontational jeering from the crowd an turned around the ring toward them all, his thumb across his neck. The end was near for Trent Lost, or so Altair suggested to them with the gesture. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “End him.” He helped his opponent to his feet and then jerked him awake with a swift wrist lock. Altair once again put on a display for the crowd when Trent’s wrest was in his grasp. He wanted them all to see the boy’s bared wrist and then he delved into the flesh with his fangs and bit into his opponent. It was brief, but the markings were immediately painted over with blood that trickled from Trent’s wrist. A mere flesh wound, but it triggered the next maneuver from Altair: a spray of Trent’s own blood, spat back into the face of Lost. It caught him blinded and off guard, just the right kind of vulnerable for the Crimson Reaper. Altair crept behind his blinded opponent, whom began to frantically wipe his own blood from his face to see. Altair wrapped an arm around his throat, and grasped the boy’s tights to lift him upward and slam him down in his finishing maneuver, the lifting inverted DDT – Ruiner. Altair pinned Trent for the full three count and then rolled himself off. He spat the rest of the blood from his lips up into the air in victory. His first victory, and triumph in Galveston Island Wrestling. JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…ALTAIR!” Altair gets to his feet looking down at Trent Lost just effectively ending his time here in GIW for the time being as the referee jumps down to check on the fallen star as Altair rolls out of the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “You know sometimes guys make big statements and don’t back it up, Altair just did.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Did he just bite him?” Altair heads to the back a bit of Trent’s blood still around his mouth as the referee tries to shake Trent back into consciousness as the scene ends. WINNER: ALTAIR Now we head to the back where Faith Simpson is stretching out for her match up when Ace Baldwin the Champion walks over to her in the hall way laughing it up. ACE BALDWIN: “Wow, this company really will just sign anybody. Shouldn’t you be studying for your multiplication test right now?” Baldwin chuckles as Faith seems to freeze up a bit, it was after all the biggest star in the company talking down at her but coming to her rescue was Aspen Chaud. ASPEN CHAUD: “Really Ace? Picking on a fifteen year old girl? Shouldn’t you be getting ready for our match?” Ace looks over at her, angrily as he was having fun scaring the new star as Faith heads off in a hurry for her match. ACE BALDWIN: “Should I be…Doing Something to get ready?” Aspen chuckles at his clear over-confidence. ASPEN CHAUD: “Whatever just leave this girl alone and go Do Something.” Aspen heads on her way as Ace chuckles at her, pulling out his phone as the segment ends. QUOTE When we return to the ring "Power On Me" by Katy B hits and the fans cheer as the young girl trained by Faith Simpson heads out from the back looking only slightly less nervous then last week as she heads down to the ring stopping to lean over the ramp to hug Mark Cross the GIW Alumni who stands at ring side. JAMAL JACKSON: “The following contest is scheduled for one fall and it is for the GIW Legacy Championship. Introducing first weighing in at one hundred twenty-two pounds, standing five foot and five inches tall…from New Orleans, Louisiana…Faith Simpson!” Faith leaves Cross and heads up the steps and into the ring waiting for her opponent as "Ain't No Stoppin Me Now" by Axel hits and the fans immediately start to boo the heck out of Jeremy Baltimore who walks out with his shades on chuckling at Faith in the ring as he walks down towards the ring. JAMAL JACKSON: “And the opponent weighing in at two hundred and twenty nine pounds standing in at six feet tall, from Washington D.C…Jeremy Baltimore!” Baltimore heads into the ring as the referee looks back and forth between them, pretty much different in every single way as he hoists up the GIW Legacy Championship as the fans watch. He hands it out and then turns calling for the bell. DING DING DING! The fans in this place are both all for Faith and worried about her as the match starts and the two start to circle around one another. Baltimore has a big smile on his face of a man that does not at all seem to be worried about this contest. Jeremy raises his arms for a test of strength just to taunt Simpson. Shaking off her nervousness she catches everyone off guard by running forward and dropkicking him right into the chest. Not expecting it he stumbles back into the ropes where she runs forward and nails him with another this time sending him forward off the ropes where she follows it up immediately with a Bulldog. The fans at this point are going crazy as she gets up and heads out to familiar territory out by the ropes, grabbing a hold of them and now setting up for a Springboard. Baltimore takes a second to get up and Faith looks over at Cross for a split second then jumps up going for a Springboard Hurricanrana but she’s caught right up above his shoulders. She tries desperately to punch her way out of the predicament but ends up getting lifted into the air and thrown down with an Elevated Powerbomb that seems to nearly split her in half. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Enough of this crap, crush her Jeremy.” This shuts all of the fans up and we get a glimpse of Cross cringing at ringside as Baltimore walks around Faith, with a smirk back on his face having completely turned the tides in this one. Simpson crawls around surprisingly to some who thought she was done as Baltimore waits for her to get back to her feet with a smile on his face. Once she gets up he grabs her up and hoists her over with both arms in the air for a Military Press drop casually walking around with her showing little strain at all in lifting her up, finally he does the Press Drop sending her down to the mat, a rather large fall. Baltimore looks back at her and rather then give her a second to recover he pulls her back to her feet and then spins around whipping her through the ropes to the outside. There he follows her out and does so right in front of Cross who watches on, hoping Faith can turn things around. Jeremy kicks her while she is crawling on her knees, as Cross tries to shout out some sort of game plan for her as Baltimore follows her and then looks up at Mark saying “You better not watch this.” as he hoists up Faith and clotheslines her down to the mat as the referee just now hits a six count. Knowing that neither of them can win with a countout he pulls her up and throws her back into the rolling get back in himself at about a eight count. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Just a matter of time.” Baltimore heads right back into the ring this time Faith is up first throwing a few kicks to try to stop him but he overpowers her hoisting her up easily and nailing her with a Scoop Slam. Jeremy hoists her up and this time shakes his head as with Faith on wobbly legs already does the Spinning Elbow Smash which we’ve seen him use to set up the finale several times. But this time it sends Faith to the mat which seems to only annoy Jeremy who walks over pulling her back up and calling for the end as he picks her up and then throws her into the corner where he runs full steam and lands a Corner Shoulder Block that sends her off the padding and into the center of the ring. Baltimore takes his sweet time getting to the top rope where he looks to end things with the ‘Powerdrive Elbow’ a Twisting Elbow Drop. But he takes too long taunting both the crowd and specifically Mark Cross as he jumps off and at the last second Simpson rolls out under the ropes. The fans go crazy as Baltimore hops right back up holding his elbow but Simpson has a sudden burst of energy as she hops up and goes Springboard with a Springboard DDT! RILEY STEVENS: “Here she comes!” The fans are starting to get behind her as she makes the cover 1…Kick Out. Baltimore throws her off which causes a sudden drop in cheers, many thinking that could be it. But when Baltimore gets up she kicks him in the gut causing him to double over where she then hits the ‘Losing Faith’ a Twist of Fate! The fans go crazy as she makes the cover 1…2…Kick Out! The fans basically come unglued thinking that was it. But with Cross cheering her on she wastes no time heading to the top rope. Baltimore kicked out but he’s still clearly dazed as she gets set up and wastes no time jumping off for the ‘Have A Little Faith’ a 630 Senton, that connects. The fans go wild as she crawls on top, they count with the referee 1…2…3! JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner and NEW ‘GIW Legacy Champion’…FAITH SIMPSON!” Faith hops up to her feet throwing up her arms, jumping up and down as the referee brings in the belt handing it over to her as she takes it and hoists it up in the air then rolling out and heading right towards Cross. RILEY STEVENS: “What a huge win for this young girl. You know many people counted her out but she overcame the odds and picked up the win here tonight.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Baltimore was hurt, something was not right. This is a travesty.” She heads over hugging her trainer at ringside who hugs back tightly as she falls back into the crowd all of them embracing the young champion as in the ring Baltimore rolls over slapping the mat in anger as Faith continues to celebrate until we head to the back. WINNER: FAITH SIMPSON (New GIW Legacy Champion) Malice…desire to inflict injury, harm, or suffering on another. JAMAL JACKSON: “Introducing the third challenger weighing in at two hundred and twenty pounds, standing six foot two inches tall…from Chicago, Illinois…“The New World Warrior” Tony Miranda!” This sends the fans into a ruckus many of them wearing there Tony Miranda t-shirts as he heads up the steps and into the ring, entering the ring with Aspen and Violence as he heads up to the top rope posing for the crowd who cheer him on. Now that he jumps down there is a slight stall before Ace Baldwin’s theme by Matador hits and the red lights hit as Ace Baldwin heads out from the back, him with the belt they all want. JAMAL JACKSON: “And the GIW Undisputed Champion, weighing in at two hundred forty five pounds, standing six foot four inches tall…from Hollywood, California …“Amazing” Ace Baldwin!” The fans boo him as he walks down towards the ring with his belt strapped around his waist as he looks out at the haters seeming to only enjoy their cheers as he heads up the steps and through the ropes, unstrapping his belt and hoisting it into the air as the other three people in the ring stare up at it. RILEY STEVENS: “That’s what this is all about, the most prestigious belt in the history of this company.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “And the belt that’s leaving with Ace here tonight.” Baldwin hands it over to the referee having taunted the others long enough as the referee now takes it and shows it off for the crowd and the four wrestlers all of whom stare up at it, with envious eyes as he takes it and hands it out of the ring as the referee calls for the bell. The fans boo that and the boos continue as on the outside Violence gets up starting to cower away from Tony. Johnny now heads back towards the ring as Ace has just whipped Aspen off the ropes. She swings at him but misses and ends up right in position for an Atomic Drop. She holds her booty as Ace heads off the ropes and crushes her with a Spear! Falling to the mat in pain he hooks her legs 1…Violence pulls him off by his foot. RILEY STEVENS: “That’s the hard part about the fatal four way match, you have to find a chance to pin.” Violence allows Ace to get to his feet throwing a big punch that sends Ace reeling back. Aspen ends up on the outside of the ropes getting to her feet. Johnny ends up rushing forward with ‘The Beast’ a Spear that connects with Ace sending him back right into Aspen who in turn bounces off the ropes into Tony who was just then getting up. Both of them are out as Ace falls down to the ground as Violence heads off the ropes dropkicking him on the ground and sending Ace falling to the outside. Now Johnny heads up to the top rope as the crowd all get on their feet realizing that Violence is about to do something crazy. Tony, Aspen, and Ace all start to get up on the outside slowly as he jumps off at them. Like dominoes they all go down to the mat, Violence landing just as hard as anyone else. As the fans have no choice but to give it up for the crazy anarchist known as Violence. RILEY STEVENS: “This belt means so much to all four that they are willing to put their own health on the line to get it.” However much it hurt, it’s still Violence up first followed not long by Tony both of them throwing punches at each other as soon as they are to their feet. Johnny gets up to his feet and starts to rub his hands together thinking this is his chance to end it as he hoists up Tony and then goes for the ‘Paranoid’ a Ranhei. Johnny Violence was not completely out as Ace tried to steal the win there. Miranda is the first one up and he throws a big punch at Tony. Miranda always willing to fight punches him back. Now Aspen gets up throwing a forearm at Tony as this becomes a triangle. Violence gets to his feet and throws one at Ace as this thing becomes a square of punches, each sending each other into one of the other wrestlers. Then Tony takes out Violence with a punch then it’s just Miranda and Baldwin throwing punches back and forth, back and forth, neither man letting up. RILEY STEVENS: “These two men are having a war.” Both Violence and Chaud having been knocked out get back up and decided to end this little battle throwing punches to each of the two’s back. Aspen ends up catching Tony while Violence does the same to Ace. Miranda is able to spin around and catch Aspen with a big Spinning Roundhouse Kick that sends her into the corner downed. Violence ends up rushing Ace only to be lifted into a Spinning Spinebuster. This leaves the two again only it’s Ace with the advantage as he stalks him from behind then Snap Suplex’s Ace as the fans go wild as he is the last man standing. The fans are going crazy sensing the end as he prepares for the ‘Third Strike’ a Running Leg Lariat as he rushes at Ace as he gets up but at the last second Aspen jumps in the way taking the kick and sending her through the ropes to the outside. RILEY STEVENS: “Aspen just took the kick!” The fans are shocked as we are as this sets right up for Ace as he hoists up Tony looking for the ‘Thanks For Coming’ Standing Death Valley Driver but before he can transition him Tony rolls off and kicks him through the ropes sending him to the outside! The fans go crazy but then their hearts drop as they see a dazed Violence get up behind him looking to try to end this thing as he hooks his arms and goes for the ‘Glory Fades a Tigerbomb but Tony spins out and connects with a kick to the stomach that in turn sets up the ‘Second Impact’ a Running Lygerbomb!
