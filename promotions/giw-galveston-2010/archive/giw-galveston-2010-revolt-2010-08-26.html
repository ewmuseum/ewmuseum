GIW — Card

Revolt, 26 August 2010

26 August 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Galveston Island Wrestling Presents: Thursday Night Revolt! Live! From Stewart Beach on August 26th, 2010 BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! The scene opens as the cameras zoom around the GIW Ring, signaling that GIW Revolt has begun! The fans go wild and chant the names of their favorite superstars as the camera focuses in on Oliver Thibodaux and Riley Stevens at the Revolt announce desk placed on Stewart Beach. RILEY STEVENS: “Ladies and gentlemen… welcome to the August 26th edition of Thursday Night Revolt! I’m Riley Stevens alongside my broadcast partner Oliver Thibodaux, sitting right here on Stewart Beach one week after Blackout with a huge card before us here tonight, wouldn’t you say Thibby?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “You’re damn right. This week GIW comes to you LIVE as one unified source, that’s right… Affliction and Revolt are now one being. That means the best of the best are here tonight, and will be in action.” RILEY STEVENS: “Just a reminder for later tonight, be sure to stay tuned from 10-10:30 for the Fusion post-show LIVE from Mario’s restaurant. Anyone who you don’t see tonight will be there, so tune in and root for your favorite stars!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Yep! You will get to see the future stars of Galveston Island Wrestling, right there on Fusion!” RILEY STEVENS: “That’s right Oliver. Now folks, this is normally the time where we would address the crowd about the matches booked for tonight, but General Manager Amanda Rowland has a special announcement to start the show.” Amanda Rowland’s theme hit’s the PA System and she begins to make her way down the ring with a microphone in her hand, and the crowd going crazy. She enters the ring and begins to speak. AMANDA ROWLAND: “On the off-chance that any of you GIW fans didn’t get the chance to watch Blackout… I feel the need to inform you on the major event that went down. Yes, we crowned new champions and yes we merged the brands… but we also were robbed by David Sinclair of seven of our most prized young talents. One of them being the North Beach Champion. Now, as the North Beach Championship is concerned… there is nothing we can do about it. Chris Armstrong may no-longer be with the company, but the title is his and cannot be removed. That is, of course, if we decide to strip him of his belt. When the Board talked it over, we decided that that would be a poor decision, because eventually when EPW returns to GIW… we don’t want to have another vacant title. …That is all.” "Re-Education" by Rise Against The Machine begins to play and out come both Joshua and Austin Acquin. They give each other a high-five and then walk to the ring together, both in quite the cocky way. They help each other in and then begin to pose for the crowd which ends up giving them boos. OLIVER THIBODAUX: These two seem quite cocky today.” RILEY STEVENS: Aren't they always like this? Sum 41 by Fatlip blasts through the speakers. Eric Sparrow comes out and waits on the ramp for his partner. Pursuit of Happiness by Kid Cudi plays. Ryan Daniels stands right next to Sparrow and together they both run towards the ring and begin this epic brawl as if these two teams have had a huge history with each other. The referee manages to control the both of them and the match begins. Joshua Acquin and Ryan Daniels begin the fight. Joshua Acquin quickly hit’s a big boot on Ryan Daniels and then begins to go to work by kicking Ryan Daniels in the gut several times. Eventually, he picks him up and then hit’s a gut buster. He goes for the cover. 1...2... Kick out. Joshua continues to kick Daniels in the gut. Daniels reaches for Sparrow, his partner, and tries to get a tag in but fails and is dragged to the Acquin corner. Joshua tags in Austin. However, Ryan Daniels manages to quickly slither his way to his own corner and tags in Sparrow. The crowd seems quite excited for Sparrow. Sparrow gets some heat and begins to hit a series of clotheslines and drop kicks on Austin. Eventually, Austin pushes Sparrow away and says, “forget it!” and tags in Joshua. Joshua enters but is also beat up pretty bad by Sparrow. Ryan Daniels begins to cheer on Sparrow and Sparrow, at this point, is basically running a clinic! He’s making both the Acquins seem like novices! Eventually, Sparrow hit’s the Spiral Tap on Joshua Acquin. Sparrow begins to lose control. As Joshua is falling on the floor, Austin reaches in and tags himself in. Sparrow, however, hit’s a roundhouse kick to put Austin out. Sparrow grabs Joshua from the ground and throws him at his own partner, Ryan Daniels! Then, Sparrow hits Purple Haze on Austin Acquin and pins him for a three count. Winner - Eric Sparrow & Ryan Daniels OLIVER THIBODAUX: What an amazing showing by Sparrow. He essentially destroyed everybody! Sparrow begins to go crazy and is confronted by Ryan Daniels. Sparrow threatens to attack Daniels but referees break up the fight before it ensues. We head to the backstage area where the fans are greeted with the face of Aspen Chaud which brings them to loud cheers. She is biting away at her nails as she leans against the wall, finally turning and heading into the room as she does we see the name plate ‘Blake Beckett’. She has on a white tank top with jeans on a more casual look for her night off. Blake looks up from his desk, at her with that always smug look. ASPEN CHAUD: "So, what’s with the sudden nice guy thing at ‘Blackout’. As happy as I was about winning the Women’s title you’re right I can do better. But you’re the last person I expected to, you know admit that to me." Aspen walks forward putting her hands down on Blake’s desk then leaning forward as she looked down at Blake who shrugged a bit before responding to her. BLAKE BECKETT: “I figured this petty little fighting between us had to end sooner or later so this time I stripped you for better reasons. Because after all when I realized what was happening with Sinclair, I needed to get everyone on my side including you.” Aspen seems a bit surprised while Blake says this with little emotion, trying not to show that this may be hurting his ego a bit. Though he realizes it’s needed for the companies sake. ASPEN CHAUD: "You need little ole’ me? That’s funny. I could say no or make you beg. But quite frankly I think you’ve changed. Maybe realizing that you’re not always in control changed you for the better. And you can strip me any time if it’s for a good reason..." Aspen smiles while Blake raises an eyebrow seeming to be thinking in the gutter with that last statement. Aspen has to think for a second about what he’s implying then realizes her foe pa’. ASPEN CHAUD: "Strip me of the title, obviously. Regardless I am on board with whatever you plan on doing to combat David Sinclair. Consider us for the first time allies, which is like really weird." Aspen extends her hand to Blake Beckett as he gets to his feet, he to finding this a bit awkward after the two had been rivals for so long, but a situation like this with a common enemy can unite people. He leans forward and shakes her hand, an awkward shake all around. BLAKE BECKETT: “Well then here’s what I have in mind.” Blake looks over at the cameraman and then the door as the cameraman does as told as Aspen takes a seat as the segment comes to a close when the cameraman shuts the door on his way out. The scene then quickly cuts to the Backstage Interview Area, where Sarah Sharp is standing by. A wave of boos quickly fill the arena. SARAH SHARP: "Gentlemen...and Ladies. My guest at this time, "The New World Warrior" Tony Miranda!" The crowd erupts in cheers as The New World Warrior walks into the cameraman's view. SARAH SHARP: "Tony, last week you came just a few inches short from winning the Blackout Battle Royal, but in the end you were eliminated by Megathreat. This week you go up against the incredibly inferior Travis Tate. What are you're thoughts on this upcoming match?" TONY MIRANDA: "Well, first off I'm gonna agree with you, Sarah. Travis Tate is indeed an inferior. He is inferior to me...the only reason why he got to where he got to in the Blackout Battle Royal, is because everyone else in that match were newbies...newbies greener than The Incredible Hulk. They were newbies that weren't even worth HIS time. Now, don't go thinking that I'm complementing you or anything. You are still a piece of shit inferior. Tonight, I am going to prove to you and to the whole world what you are. Tonight, I am going to crush you. I am going to humiliate you, and then I am going to drive you face first into the mat with my Lightning Buster...and then continue down the long road towards the ION TV Championship." Tony lets out a laugh. TONY MIRANDA: "Speaking of which...lets talk about you...Megathreat. Last week at Blackout you succeeded where I could not. While you managed to climb over the massive wall known as Blackout, I failed, and along with me, several other people were knocked into the Pit of Obscurity...but unlike the newbies who fell into oblivion...I managed to save myself. I grabbed onto a ledge, and pulled myself back onto the road, back to the starting point. But, now, here I am...running down the road once more...the road to the ION TV Championship...and to you. Tonight begins my long and arduous journey down the road to the ION TV Championship...and tonight is where I will meet my first obstacle, Travis Tate. And as I said before...his wall is a pathetic wall made out of Lego Blocks, and only retards and idiots trip over Lego Blocks. Now...if you'll excuse me...I have to get ready for the easiest match of my entire career. Tony turns around and walks away, leaving to get ready for his match against Travis Tate. We come back to the ring where the fans watch as Travis Tate stretches out for his match up that’s up next. He has a bit of a grin on his face and the fans basically give him no reaction at all. Soon however they begin to cheer as "Valhalla" by Blind Guardian hits and the fans begin to cheer a man whom they had grown fond of, “The New World Warrior” Tony Miranda. RILEY STEVENS: “These fans have really begun to like this guy and quite frankly I consider him one of the best up and comer’s around here, he could be in line for a title shot soon.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “This guy is awful Travis Tate is the real talent around here.” Miranda heads down to the ring grinning over at the fans then high fiving one kid before heading up the steps and quickly stepping into the ring. Miranda looks over at Tate with little fear in his eyes looking at the referee to start the match. DING DING DING! Travis goes full steam right off the bat at Miranda who connects with a Spinning Roundhouse Kick. If we didn’t know who was the smarter one already we do now as Tate falls to the mat hard but gets up only a bit stunned after the shot. Travis walks up into Tony only to be met with a wicked Yakuza Kick that crushes Tate’s already weakened head. These kicks are landing and they are landing hard. Travis doesn’t have a clue what’s going on at this point, as he gets up and holds onto the ropes hoping to have some time to rest. But to think so is a mistake to say the least as Miranda goes right back on the offensive. Only a stray elbow thrown by Travis lands into the Tony’s face sending him reeling for a second. Travis goes for a big clothesline but Miranda ducks it and then as Travis turns around he hits a big Discus Clothesline. Downed at the edge of the ring Tony looks out at the fans as he leads right into a Rolling Thunder Leg Drop! The fans love this move as it’s ever so impressive. With him downed he pulls him to the center of the ring and goes for the pin 1…2…Kick Out! He’s gotten him pretty hurt but not quite enough to put him down yet. However while some may find this as a terrible thing Tony doesn’t seem to be too worried at all. He gets up calling for a slow clap from the fans as he circles around the ring waiting for his victim, ahem opponent to get to his feet. With the referee checking on him Travis uses the ropes to get up to a vertical base before stumbling towards Tony hoping to maybe hit something. Instead he walks right into Tony’s grasps who sets him right up for the ‘Lightning Buster’ a Crash Thunder Buster! The fans are in awe as Tony crushes him to the mat and instead of pinning him he adds insult to injury as he sets him up into the Camel Clutch a basically perfect combo with the last move. Almost immediately Travis taps out and after a few seconds of having it locked in Tony releases the hold with a dominant victory. RILEY STEVENS: “You were saying?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “So maybe Travis Tate wasn’t as good as I thought.” RILEY STEVENS: “Just admit it Tony Miranda is on his way up around here, quite frankly all he needs is a big time opponent, and after this I don’t see how he can’t move up the ranks.” The referee checks on Travis who is out of it at this point. The fans are cheering on Tony in the ring as he heads to the top rope holding up his arms as the fans cheer him, they’ve come to love him. We return after the break to the ring where the fans are a bit confused as already standing in the ring is Megathreat as well as the Grateful Dead both members. Threat’ is not in ring attire instead holding his ION TV title over his shoulder. With a microphone in hands he begins to speak to the fans who have no idea what he is up to. MEGATHREAT: “Unfortunately tonight you will not be seeing me wrestle The Grateful Dead. Because I have some bad news for all of you. While I wrestled at ‘Blackout’ and won this belt, I realized that I was in over my head. And when it comes down to it if I can’t compete at my best level in the ring, then I shouldn’t be holding this belt. And that’s why this belt will be VACATED! And we’ll crown a new champion soon. At the end of the day it’s for the best of the company that I stay out of the ring, so that the younger guys can do what they do.” Megathreat seems a bit hesitant when he sets down the ION TV title maybe having second thoughts about it, as the fans applaud him showing the Commissioner respect as The Grateful Dead walk up behind him and pat him on the back, showing their love for him as well. MEGATHREAT: “Goodbye everyone.” Megathreat heads out of the ring dropping the microphone as the fans seem confused about if he’s leaving the company for good or if he’s just leaving as a wrestler. He heads to the back as The Grateful Dead seem more confused then usual as we head to commercial. After the match has ended and both wrestlers have gone to the back, the fans explode as “Bullseye” by Aly and AJ hits. Out from the back after having just left Blake’s office is Aspen Chaud. The fans are cheering as she already has a microphone and she stops at the top of the ring looking out at the crowd who go quiet to let her speak. ASPEN CHAUD: "This will only take a minute because as I was back there talking with Blake Beckett. He gave me permission to come out and send a message to David Sinclair and his group of expendables. If you think you can come to our show and do anything, you’re sadly mistaken. Because I am right here waiting to take you out. David if you think you pulled a fast one on us, you’re sadly mistaken. We are still alive and kicking more so then ever. So you, your little lackies, and EPW as a whole can KISS MY…” Aspen lowers her microphone with a smirk as the fans finish her sentence for her then beginning to clap as Aspen turns and heads to the back having sent her message as we go to commercial We return to the ring and standing in the ring already are all three of the woman who will tonight compete for the GIW Women’s Championship. Not a single one of them is liked by the fans so they are split on who they want to win instead just having fun thinking who they think may do so. RILEY STEVENS: “After the Women’s title was vacated away from Aspen at ‘Blackout’ we’ll waste little time in getting into this match which is a three way between three very talented women. In fact Alexii is the former champion who we all know will be hungry to get her belt back.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “It’s a woman’s match…WHO CARES.” Riley can be heard scoffing Oliver’s thoughts as the three girls are in the ring. Alexii is pounding her fists into her hands while Ashleigh is looking out at the crowd and Kacie is eyeing the belt. After the referee has shown it to all three of the girls in the ring he hands it out and then calls for the bell. DING DING DING! The match gets under way and the three girls slowly walk to the middle of the ring. Alexii stares them both down while Kacie shows little fear when saying “You both tap tonight”. However Ashleigh seems to become the target when she gets between them telling them both off. Alexii and Kacie seem to agree on a second of pairing as they both grab her arms and whip her off of the ropes. As she bounces off she grabs them and tries to escape but the girls are to fast as they head over grabbing her by her bottoms and tugging her back to the center of the ring. With there momentary bond settled they grab her up onto their shoulders. She screams out in fear as they drop her down to the mat hard. Ashleigh holds her back in pain as the two girls seem to turn there attention to each other thinking that Ashleigh is down and out. Instead they notice she’s trying to escape and turn over to her as they grab her from her hands and knees and lift her up. She looks at both of them begging them for mercy but instead Kacie goes behind her holding her in the center of the ring. Alexii runs off the ropes and then connects with a ‘Hooligan Kick’ a Arched Big Boot but Kacie quickly rolls her up when she’s stunned 1…2…Alexii Kicks Her Off. Kacie rolls off as Ashleigh falls to the side basically out of it at this point in the match. Alexii stands in the center of the ring staring down at Kacie who gets to her feet not looking scared at all as the two girls now seem ready to square off. On one side you have the Submission Specialist and the other you have the Powerhouse this is the match up the fans really wanted to see right here. RILEY STEVENS: “Things are about to get exciting.” The two go at it first with a big punch from Kacie then one back from Alexii that knocks Church back a bit. Kacie responds with a big forearm then another and another pounding Alexii to the ropes before whipping her off of them. On the return however Alexii crushes her with a big kick to the stomach. Momentarily stunned the fans are caught off guard when Alexii hits the ‘Ignorance’ a Pumphandle Piledriver! This move has ended girls before this. With her downed Alexii goes down for the cover and 1…2…Ashleigh kicks her off. Alexii gets up angry at the fact she may have just had Kacie done if not for this. She gets up and approaches Ashleigh who slides out of the ring. Kacie however has begun to stir as she gets up behind Alexii. Ravynova is begging for 3 Tears to get back in here and fight her. But when Alexii turns around Kacie has recovered and she walks right into a ‘The Ice Breaker’ an X-Factor! This may just be it right here as she covers her 1…2…Ashleigh now pushes Kacie off! The fans are annoyed by Ashleigh’s antics now as she again tries to slide out of the ring but doesn’t move fast enough as Kacie catches her. She drags her into the ring while 3 Tears just struggles to break free. But she ends up being unable to as Kacie lifts her up to her feet. Still struggling she catches Kacie with a back elbow then runs at Kacie. But Church dodges it and she instead dropkicks Alexii out of the ring. When Ashleigh looks up at Kacie she is caught with a huge kick that nearly decapitates her. With her stunned Church sets her up for the Sharp Shooter and locks it in! The referee waits as Ashleigh tries to get away but can’t as she taps out, new champion! RILEY STEVENS: “Kacie Church is the new GIW Women’s Champion!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I have to admit both Alexii and Kacie did good tonight, the other I can’t say the same.” The referee hands the belt over to Kacie who hugs it tight having known it was her time for a long time now. She heads top rope and holds the belt high having done well tonight, as she’s the new GIW Women’s Champion! She slides out of the ring holding her belt tight. However in the ring Alexii has returned, looking down at Ashleigh who the referee is trying to help up. RILEY STEVENS: “This doesn’t look good for Ashleigh who Alexii has blamed for her loss apparently.” When she gets up largely with help from the referee, he walks over seeing Alexii and tries to tell her to hit the back. Instead she pushes him to the side and runs across the ring hitting a brutal ‘Jawbreaker Lariat’ a PendulumShort Ranged Lariat to Ashleigh knocking her out cold. As she gets up she looks up at the top of the ramp at Kacie and it seems these two’s paths may not be done crossing as we head to commercial. [pic] After the match is over, the camera switches to backstage in Amanda Rowland’s office, where Jake Kennedy is standing with a serious look on his face. He chuckles for a moment, then begins to speak to Amanda. JAKE KENNEDY: “You know, Amanda, now that Megathreat is gonna be off staring at the sunset, you have a vacant title. I know how much you… hate vacant titles. So… maybe next week… we could throw a little something together… for the title? What do you say… Madame Rowland?” The camera quickly pans out, revealing Amanda Rowland shaking her head and rolling her eyes. AMANDA ROWLAND: “Well… I suppose that you do deserve a shot…” Jake begins to jump for joy. JAKE KENNEDY: “THANKS MISS ROWLAND!!!” Jake storms out of the camera’s view, Amanda seems annoyed. "Hero" by Skillet begins to play. The entire crowd stands up and the great majority of them begin to cheer and start applauding for the man who managed to do the impossible. Jamie Vaccaro comes out and lifts his belt in the air, as the crowd cheers him. Jamie slowly walks down the ramp, thanking all of the fans that are cheering him. He slowly makes his way into the ring and then waits for his opponent. Meanwhile, he hands his title belt to the time keeper before staring at it for a few seconds. “Dazed and Confused” by Led Zeppelin begins to play. The intro goes on for about 3 minutes, strangely enough. The crowd cheers just as loud for Walker as they did for Jamie but slowly, the cheers die as Wake Walker does not make his way down the ramp. When the song begins to get more upbeat, six cheerleaders come out and begin a chant for Wake Walker. A platform begins to rise from the ground as Wake Walker appears and steps down, championship on his waist. The crowd goes insane and it clearly defeats the cheers that Jamie received. Wake Walker walks down the ramp and appears to be smiling, making contact with Jamie. Eventually, the two find themselves in the ring and the epic showdown begins with the bell. Both superstars don’t do anything for one minute since the chants for both are so loud and noticeable so they pan to the crowd. Eventually, they get into a test of strength that goes on for another 30 seconds. Nobody really wins it. RILEY STEVENS: This match sure as hell is starting off slow and with good reason! Both superstars appear to respect each other in the sense that they know what the other is capable of doing. Both appear to be equally matched. OLIVER THIBODAUX: You've really ran out of things to say as a commentator, haven't you? The two appear to have the same amount of strength. Wake Walker gets in the first shot but then backs up and doesn’t follow through with it. It appears as though these two superstars highly respect each other. The pace of the match goes even slower when the two continues to dodge each other’s moves but eventually, Jamie gets Walker back with one shot butt hen backs off. The two are impressed by each others wrestling ability. The crowd just begin to get adjusted to their seats when suddenly, Eric Sparrow comes out, with steel chair in hand. The referee throws out the match. RILEY STEVENS: What the hell is this?! Winner - NO CONTEST Eric swings the chair at Jamie who is now booed a lot compared to before. Jamie dodges it and pushes Eric to Walker. Walker hit’s a roundhouse kick to Eric who falls straight to the floor. Jamie and Walker both grab Eric, nod their heads and throw them out. Wake Walker then pretends to try and throw out Jamie. Jamie gets defensive and pushes Walker away. Walker laughs it off and Jamie eventually realizes it was a joke. Suddenly, though. “Dragon begins to play. OLIVER THIBODAUX: What now? Inchiro Hueda comes out and then stares at Sparrow who is leaving the ring, with his head down. Sparrow tries to scare Inchiro by faking a punch but Inchiro doesn’t flinch at all. Sparrow shrugs and leaves. The entire show ends with Inchiro Hueda’s music playing and him staring down all three. OLIVER THIBODAUX: That guy sure is scary as hell. The match starts off with all three men in the ring already. Mike Danielson and Chad Munroe try to work on Griz first but Griz keeps on pushing them backwards. 325 lbs are going against the two. Griz is saying, "C'mon" to them. Chad runs up to the Enforcer punching him as Danielson climbs the turnbuckle and hangs on to the back of Griz. Griz can not see as Mike accidentally on purpose kicks the referee and he falls outside of the ring in between the ropes making the effect of Chad Munroe punching Griz in the crotch three times and after that, Mike Danielson hits the Missile Dropkick on Griz then the 450 Splash taking him out for the rest of the match. The two baseball slide Griz out of the ring. Munroe and Danielson stare at each other in the center of the ring. Mike holds out his hand for them two to shake. Chad is about to shake it but he tricks Mike by slapping him in the face. Munroe then gets a mixed crowd reaction after that. Mike smiles and locks in the Command and Conquer on Chad but Chad manages to reverse and hit his finisher! He goes for the cover! 1...2...3! JJ Evans wins the battle royal. after throwing Chicken over. After the match, Chicken has a brief dance party with some crowd members.
