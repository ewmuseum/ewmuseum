GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: GENESIS LIVE JANUARY 9th, FROM SEA WOLF PARK IN GALVESTON, TEXAS = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = The camera pans into Sea Wolf Park where tonight the place is more packed then it usually is as the fans are prepared for Galveston Island Wrestling’s ‘Genesis’. The fans all seem pumped for the event as we come into the arena panning around them as the cameraman runs along the outside of the ring to all of them who cheer until he stops at the Announce Table where Riley Stevens and Oliver Thibodaux are sitting ready to call the action. RILEY STEVENS: “Hello ladies and gentlemen and welcome to a Fox Southwest broadcast our first here for Genesis! We have a packed card tonight and you may see it already but our people are already getting the No Escape cage ready, you’ve got to be excited right Oliver.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I’m just glad Beckett helped us keep our jobs all while getting us a big raise. What a guy.” RILEY STEVENS: “Well after the huge No Escape Match it’ll be the long awaited Tag Team title match between Perfect Combination and the champions Technical Mechanica. After last week’s attack you’d have to assume that Perfect Combination would be looking for revenge as well as the titles.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Yeah…pretty easy assumption.” RILEY STEVENS: “Then we’ll follow that up with a GIW Evolve Championship match between former champion Christopher Darren Erns and the champion Eric Sparrow. I’m not sure about you but I believe that has potential to be the Match of the Night.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Yeah probably.” RILEY STEVENS: “Then the long awaited end of the Mark Cross and Chris Jericho feud, where we have heard rumors that it could very well be Cross’s last match here in GIW. Short lived career right?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Is he the one that likes the money?” RILEY STEVENS: “Not at all…But anyways our final match will be the epic encounter between the ‘Galveston Island Champion’ Aspen Chaud against her contender and very much hated Scott Reave in what many believe might be a classic main event. I’m only assuming that you won’t bother at all commenting on it?” There is a brief silence between the two and after a few seconds it’s broken up by Riley after he’s rolled his eyes realizing that Oliver won’t help him out here. RILEY STEVENS: “Anyways we are going to send you to the back before the first match where apparently Blake Beckett is having some words with Amanda Rowland.” = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = As we come into the backstage area we are greeted with the faces of both Blake Beckett sitting at his desk and Amanda Rowland standing behind it, seeming to be rather angry right now. BLAKE BECKETT: “Listen it’s not your fault that you couldn’t cut it. You see this business just wasn’t for you. But rest assured that I’ll find a place for you, even if that’s just as eye candy.” Blake smiles and then looks at some paper work on his desk, seeming to be giving it to the woman that got him fired not too long ago. Regardless Amanda seems none to pleased with him demeaning her. AMANDA ROWLAND: “I was fine being the commentator earlier tonight but you can’t just fire me. That’s not fair. I mean have you hired a General Manager yet? I was good at that.” Blake seems to sigh a bit shaking his head no, but before Amanda can begin to argue she feels a tap at her shoulder as an eccentrically dressed woman stands behind her with a big smirk on her face. LA’SARAH AUDRIC: “That…that would be me. Hiiii Blake.” The woman leans forward giving Blake a kiss on the cheek before looking over at Amanda again with more of an angry stare now. LA’SARAH AUDRIC: “You still here? You see Blake and I have some things to talk over. So if you wouldn’t mind getting your unwanted self out of here, that would appreciated.” Audric waves her off like a dog as Amanda looks from Audric to Beckett who clearly have a past history before walking out as the segment comes to a close. As we return from backstage; Jacoby Jackson, Bobby Bower, and Landon Ryan are all in the ring enclosed by an impressive looking steel cage. The bell rings and Landon Ryan immediately sinks back into one of the corners, letting Bower and Jackson go at each other. RILEY STEVENS: “It has been announced that the three mystery contestants will be revealed every three minutes. I wonder who they are going to be Ollie?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I’d just like to announce that I got a call to be in this match, but respectfully declined.” Bower takes Jackson down quickly with a tornado kick that is perfectly placed across the chest. Bower climbs the top rope and connects with a cross body that keeps the tempo of the match high. Landon Ryan sees his spot and capitalizes, clothes lining Bower to the ground. Landon sets Bobby up for a suplex and connects. He goes for a quick pin, but Bower finds a way to kick out. Jacoby Jackson is up again and starts throwing kicks at Landon Ryan. Ryan overpowers Jacoby and slams him to the ground with a scoop slam. Bobby grabs Landon and whips him against the ropes, and out of the ring. Bobby follows Landon out; he uses the cage to his advantage; pinning Landon against it while he stomps him in the corner. Before anyone can realize it Jacoby Jackson is soaring through the air, connecting with a cross body to the outside on Bower. Three minutes has passed, and a buzzer sounds. RILEY STEVENS: “It’s time to announce the first mystery entrant… who is it going to be?” To everyone’s surprise… MARCUS “THE PSYCHIATRIST” HISTER ENTERS THE CAGE! OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I wonder if he is going to bring David Sinclair with him?” Hister enters the cage and begins to analyze the damage. He peels Bobby Bower off of the floor and hits him with a spike DDT. He follows up with a German Suplex to Landon Ryan and then a Reverse DDT to Jacoby Jackson. Hister rolls Jackson into the ring and goes for a pin…1...2... KICKOUT! Hister whips Jackson into the corner and connects with a flying knee, sending Jackson down to the mat. Hister climbs the top rope and panders to the crowd, but from behind him Bobby Bower rises and tosses him off the top! Bower climbs up the rope himself and connects with a senton Hister! He goes for a pin, but Jacoby pushes him off. Jacoby is now trading blows with Bower. Bower does a quick and cheap thumb to the eye to regain control. He hit’s a textbook dropkick that knocks Jacoby to the ground. Bobby locks in an ankle lock, but Jacoby spins out of it, sending Bobby to the outside. The buzzer sounds again. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Here we go!” …MADISON SETON ENTERS THE CAGE!!!! RILEY STEVENS: “What a pickup for GIW!” Seton enters the cage and squares off with Jacoby Jackson. She out maneuvers him and does a hip toss that brings him to the ground. Landon Ryan is back into the ring and tries to put Seton in a headlock, but she spins out of it and whips him into the corner. Hister is up now and attempts a super kick, but Seton ducks it and Hister runs straight into a boot by Landon Ryan! Ryan goes for a pin, but Seton breaks it up. Seton pulls Landon Ryan up and locks in the Freezing Rain… a modified gogoplata! Landon looks like he is going to tap! The buzzer rings! ITS SCOTT RYDELL! OLIVER THIBODAUX: “HE IS BACK! YES! YES! YES!” RILEY STEVENS: “He needs to hurry… Ryan is on thin ice!” Rydell rushes to the ring, Seton tightens her grip! Rydell slides in the ring… LANDON RYAN TAPS OUT! MADISON SETON IS THE WINNER~! Scott Rydell is livid and pleads with the ref to extend the match… but it is too late. The match is over! OLIVER THIBODAUX: “He was screwed.” The fans cheer on Seton while Rydell looks in from the outside angry over the fact he didn’t get there in time. Regardless the debut of Madison Seton has come in very impressive fashion. WINNER: MADISON SETON As we return to another promo this time we are taken to the parking lot where Sarah Sharp is currently running up to a limo that has just pulled up, after a second getting out arriving rather late is Chris Jericho decked out in a suit. SARAH SHARP: “Mr. Jericho, have you heard any of the rumors?” Jericho shakes his head no as he lets the driver close his door behind him before the limo proceeds to pull off as another member of his staff grabs his bag and begins to wheel it as Sarah follows him. SARAH SHARP: “Rumors are that Mark Cross is planning his retirement after this match.” Sarah continues to follow him but eventually he stops and takes off his sunglasses dropping them in his pocket as he responds. CHRIS JERICHO: “That figures. You see Mark Cross isn’t in my league and if he thinks his little attack was some sort of morale breaker, I hope he’s prepared because unfortunately for him…I don’t have any and I don’t plan on letting him retire in peace. When and I mean when he taps out and when the fans don’t even bother with him, he’ll realize that I am the one these people pay to see…not him. As far as I am concerned, good riddance to him. People with morals don’t make it far in this business, that’s why he never would have.” Jericho quickly peels out of there heading to his private locker room, as Sarah Sharp looks at the camera with a bit of a smile happy that she got the answers she was looking for as we head back out to the ring. As "Taste The Blood" By Shawn McPherson plays the crowd erupts, letting out some of the loudest boos and jeers they could. The boos get even louder as Tony Miranda and The Shadow walk out onto the stage. Tony looks around the arena, looking at all of the GIW Fans who once cheered for him. Tony and Shadow walk down the stage towards the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Out first is Perfect Combination.” Tony hops up onto the apron while The Shadow uses the steel steps to get onto it. The Shadow is the first to enter the ring, steping over the ring ropes, while Tony jumps over them. The Shadow performs his signature "Suffering Taunt", a double cutthroat taunt, while Tony throws his arm up into the air, with his index finger pointing up. As there theme dies, it is replaced by Stereo Palma's El Mariachi (Lambretto Remix) plays. There is a mixed reaction from the crowd, there are some cheers, but most of the crowd is booing as the current Tag Team Champions Technical Mechanica walks out. They look at The Perfect Combination and raise their titles in the air. RILEY STEVENS: “Technical Mechanica the champions.” Technical Mechanica waste no time, and rush down to the ring. The quickly slide into the ring and jump right up. The champions look at The Perfect Combination, and begin to talk trash to them, which neither of them respond to Techical Mechanica. Damon Ramon looks at his partner Ombio, telling him to go to the apron, which he does. Tony steps forward, and The Shadow walks onto the apron. As Technical Mechanica's theme dies down. the bell rings. RILEY STEVENS: "And here we go, the GIW Tag Team Titles are on the line, Damon Ramon and Tony Miranda will be starting this match off." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "I guarantee you this match is going to be a bloodbath. The Perfect Combination are going to absolutely destroy Technical Mechanica tonight.” RILEY STEVENS: We'll see about that. Technical Mechanica are the champions for a reason." Tony and Damon circle around the ring for a few seconds, then lock up in the middle. Miranda overpowers Damon, locking him in a headlock. Damon moves towards the ropes, and uses it to push Tony off into an Irish Whip. As Tony comes back Damon attempts a Hurricanrana, but Tony counters it, slamming Damon down with a vicious Powerbomb. Damon screams in pain as he holds his back. Not letting Damon get any breathing time, Tony runs off the ropes, and goes for his Rolling Thunder Leg Drop. Damon rolls out of the way, causing Tony to land flat on his ass. He screams in pain, not noticing Damon has gotten up. He runs off the ropes, and hits Tony with a dropkick to the face. Damon quickly covers. 1... 2... Tony kicks out, pushing Damon off him. Damon is the first to his feet, and picks Tony up, when he brings "The New World Warrior" to his knees, Tony punches him hard in the gut. Tony gets up, and throws a combination of Boxing Hooks to the face of Damon Ramon, pushing him to the ring ropes. Tony Irish Whips Damon, and as he comes back, Tony grabs Damon and hits a Powerslam. Tony gets up, pulling Damon by his mask, Tony brings Damon towards his corner and Tags The Shadow in. As The Shadow enters the ring, they both Irish Whip Damon, and hit him with a devastating Double Team Move. Tony hits Damon with a clotheline, while Shadow hits him with a spear. Tony moves to the apron, while The Shadow covers Damon. 1... 2... Just before the three count, Ombio runs in and dropkicks The Shadow, stopping the count. The Shadow quickly rises to his feet. Ombio looks at The Shadow, before running off the ropes and trying anything that would bring the giant down, but before he could do anything, The Shadow hits him with a devastating big boot. OLIVER THIBODAUX: "See? What I tell you. Technical Mechanica has no chance against The Perfect Combination." RILEY STEVENS: "Its still early in the match to call this one, Oliver. Technical Mechanica has speed, and that just be what they need to take down a man like The Shadow." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "You're really calling that thing a man? I honestly don't think he's human." Damon Ramon slowly rises towards his feet, and looks up at The Shadow, who grabs him by the throat. Shadow lifts him up into the air, strangling the life out of Damon, before hitting him with a series of headbutts, and then slamming him down to the floor. The Shadow looks down at Damon, before jumping into the air and landing hard on him with an elbow drop. RILEY STEVENS: "Jesus! Imagine the pain Damon Ramon must be going through right now. He's got to have some form of internal bleeding." OLIVER THIBODAUX: "I gotta agree, I wouldn't be surprised if The Shadow completely crushed his stomach with that elbow drop." Damon rolls around the ring, screaming in pain. The Shadow looks down at him with a smile of pleasure on his face. He grabs Damon and picks him up. The Shadow then begins beating away at Damon's face, with a series of brutal punches, before Irish Whipping him into a corner. The Shadow runs at him, and hits Damon with a vicious clotheline. The Shadow looks at the turnbuckle, before grabbing Damon and pulling him away from it. The Shadow then climbs the turnbuckle, and taunts the crowd. The Shadow then jumps off the top rope, hitting a devastating Moonsault! RILEY STEVENS: "My god! 400 pounds right onto Damon Ramon!" The Shadow gets up, not covering Damon. Instead he walks over to his own corner and tags in "The New World Warrior". Tony slowly walks over to Damon, and picks him up...out of nowhere Damon hits and Enzuigiri on Tony, knocking him down! Damon slowly crawls towards Ombio, and tags him in! Ombio rushes into the ring, and hits Tony with a dropkick, knocking him back down. Ombio begins stomping wildly on Tony, desperately trying to do damage to him, Ombio drops down, punching away at "The New World Warrior". After a few seconds Tony manages to push off Ombio, and quickly rises to his feet. Ombio rushes towards Tony, who lets out a scream, before smashing Ombio's head in with one of the most powerful roundhouse kicks he has ever hit someone with. Ombio falls flat on the mat. Tony then turns around, and sees Damon Ramon hopping onto the ropes to try and knockdown "The New World Warrior" with a Springboard move, but before he could jump off the ropes, The Shadow knocks him down, sending him to the outside. The Shadow drops down, and picks up Damon. The Shadow puts him in a powerbomb stance, before lifting him up to hit his signature finisher, Instant Death. The Shadow delibrately botches the move, driving Damon down on his neck. RILEY STEVENS: "Oh my god...I think Shadow just broke Damon's neck." The Shadow turns towards the ring, and looks at Tony, who was currently punching away at Ombio in a corner. The Shadow rushes into the ring, looking at his partner. Tony looks at The Shadow and nods, he then brings Ombio to the middle. Tony and Shadow then run off the ropes, and hits Ombio with their signature move, the double Axe Bomber Clotheline. RILEY STEVENS: "Cross Bomber! Its over!" Ombio falls towards the ground, and both Tony and The Shadow place their foot down on him. 1... 2... 3! "Taste The Blood" once again plays as Tony and Shadow raise their hands up in the air, standing over the destroyed team of Technical Mechanica. The referee exits the ring to grab the Tag Team Titles. As he gets back into the ring, he hands The Perfect Combination their newly won titles, and then raised their arms in victory. RILEY STEVENS: “We have new champions in rather gruesome fashion.” Tony and Shadow still stand emotionless with their new titles, they look down at Ombio, who was beginning to rise to his feet. Tony raises his title up into the air and drops down, slamming it onto Ombio's head. The crowd boos him as he rises to his feet, he then looks at The Shadow, and looks at The Shadow, he nods, and locks Ombio in his own variant of the Camel Clutch...the move known as The Suffering! The crowd boos even louder as Tony drops down towards Ombio and begins screaming in his face, he then reaches into his pocket, pulling out a pair of brass knuckles and begins pounding away at Ombio's head. After a few seconds The Shadow lets go of the hold, and moves to the outside. Shadow lifts up the apron and pulls out two chairs and tosses them into the ring. The Shadow slides into the ring, watching "The New World Warrior grab the chairs and bring them over towards Ombio. He then begins to place both of Ombio's feet into the chairs. RILEY STEVENS: "Oh no...are they going to do what I think they're going to do?" Tony and The Shadow look at each other with a twisted look of joy on their faces, and they jump up into the air and hit twin leg drops on the chairs. Ombio screams in pain, clutching his feet. OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Yep." The Perfect Combination rise to their feet, and grab their titles, and raise them high into the air, their theme begins to play again, before cutting to commercial. WINNERS: PERFECT COMBINATION (New GIW Tag Team Champions) After a short break we are taken to the backstage area where we see ‘ION Television Champion’ Joshua Acquin and his brother Austin Acquin just showing up at the arena after the GIW.com Pre-Show unhappy with their loss. JOSHUA ACQUIN: “It’s all right bro, tonight was a bad night but next week on Revolt we will bounce back.” Joshua looks back at his brother who nods at him agreeing when they suddenly bounce into one of the teams they faced earlier in the night, he runs into the bigger member Brody Harper while his partner Nelson Fielder stands beside him. NELSON FIELDER: “Look Brody if it isn’t the so called Golden Duo. If I recall correctly it was us pinning you in that Tag Team Rumble tonight. But last time I looked only one of them has a title, talk about riding your brother’s coattails.” Nelson smirks and leans over and pats Brody on the chest as Austin seems a little infuriated by his trash talk but Joshua holds him back and then does the talking. JOSHUA ACQUIN: “Listen we are just here to watch the show. If you want a fight so badly, meet us at Revolt. I believe we are both on that show right?” Joshua nods his head and then goes right between them as Austin follows him giving both Nelson and Brody a dirty look before walking by them as they watch staring down as we may be seeing the beginning of a new feud here. The time has come for one of the biggest matches of the night as the fans all sit at ringside realizing that we are heading down the home stretch. "Welcome Home Instrumental" by Coheed & Combria hits and the fans almost immediately begin to cheer as Christopher Darren Erns in all his glory walks out from the back, with that always serious look as he heads toward the ring ready for action after being out of action for so long. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “What makes this guy get a title shot so quickly? Sure he’s a former champion but that’s not fair at all.” RILEY STEVENS: “Neither is faking an injury but you don’t seem to mind when Eric Sparrow does it. Regardless he looks pumped for the match and frankly he’s got these fans support here tonight.” Erns heads up the ramp quickly stepping through the ropes and heading to his corner, ready and waiting for his opponent here tonight. The fans almost expectantly almost start a pre entrance boo as “Fatlip” by Sum 41 hits and the fans go all out. Out from the back comes Eric Sparrow proudly carrying the ‘GIW Evolve Championship’ over his shoulder as he walks to the ring. The fans don’t let up as we cut back to CDE who is jumping around ready for a fight. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “This is my guy! Eric Sparrow! Should be our main champion by now but tonight he’ll prove why the Evolve title belongs around his waist.” RILEY STEVENS: “There is no doubting that Eric Sparrow is a superb wrestler but that mouth of his has garnered him few fans over the course of his career, though it’s my belief that we all respect him none the less.” If that was true you wouldn’t know it as he walks by the fans jeer him all the way until he steps into the ring then hops up to the top rope and throws his title in the air before jumping down and mouthing some form of trash talk to Erns before handing the title to the referee who shows it to the fans and then both wrestlers before handing it out and calling for the bell. DING DING DING! The fans are pumped up for action as the two wrestlers immediately begin to circle around one another ready for the fight. Finally they go in for a luck up and right away Erns shows his physical strength pushing Sparrow back into the corner. The referee tells him to back off but when he does he gets caught by a big kick from Eric that knocks him back. The fans boo as Sparrow bounces forward absolutely crushing Erns with a Superkick! Right away he goes for a cover 1…2..Kick Out! The fans cheer as Eric shakes his head and grabs Erns and lifts him up tossing him into the corner. Rather then just going for a move he choose to taunt the crowd before running forward and right into a big time Shoulder Block. The fans cheer as Sparrow falls backwards into the ropes. Erns runs at him but runs right into a big boot from Eric that knocks him back. The fans don’t act to happy as Sparrow jumps over the ropes and goes Springboard for an amazing ‘Bailing DTT’ a Shooting Star DDT! The fans are stunned as he flies into the air but immediately cheer as when he grabs the head of Erns he meets a man that he can’t bring down. Sparrow begins to kick around in the air realizing the type of trouble he is in as Erns tosses him over the top rope and onto the outside! The fans are cheering as the referee begins to count him out 1…2…3…4…Nearly half way through Erns realizes that winning by Count out does him little good as he slides out of the ring stopping the count. He grabs Sparrow who is currently getting up and tosses him into the ring. However it appears that the champion was playing a bit of possum because as soon as Erns steps up onto the ring apron Sparrow hops up and catches him with a dropkick to the knee that sends him falling face first onto the edge of the ring. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I’ve said it time and time again he is the smartest wrestler in this company.” Sparrow begins to wipe his hands free of Erns as the referee begins the count 1…2…3…Inside the ring it seems clear that Sparrow has no issues defending his belt this way. As the referee counts he watches as Erns now begins to get up...4…5…6…Finally he’s up and the fans urge him back into the ring, but as he goes to get in Sparrow attempts a Baseball Slide to keep him out. Erns dodges it and pulls him right out of the ring with him, restarting the count before tossing him back into the ring so that the two may fight. Right away both men hop up to their feet and Sparrow runs right at him but Erns ducks under it and locks him right into a Sleeper Hold! It may be old school but it is effective as Sparrow begins to flail around trying to get out. Before Erns can slam him down however Sparrow catches him with a big kick up into his groin area that the referee didn’t see. With Erns hurt he catches him with an uppercut that sends him right back into the corner. With him there Sparrow backs up a bit and with CDE temporarily out of it he runs forward connecting with a beautiful ‘Pineapple Express’ a Corner Dropkick! The fans boo as Erns falls flat on his face in the ring but Sparrow decides to slide out of the ring and gets on the ring apron, preparing for something off the springboard. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Here it is, it’s time to end this.” As Erns begins to get to his feet Sparrow times it perfectly waiting for the right moment to jump off. When he jumps off whatever move he was originally going for is ruined when a recovered Erns does the unthinkable and catches him with an amazing ‘Last Call’ a huge Superkick made even worse by Sparrow’s momentum. He falls flat on his back in the center of the ring as the whole crowd explodes into cheers. He pulls Sparrow away from the ropes and makes the cover 1…2…Kick Out! To everyone’s surprise it wasn’t enough. Erns gets to his feet looking down at Sparrow who seems to have kicked out on instinct because he still isn’t moving. Deciding that if that won’t finish it; he’ll have to go a little bigger CDE heads over right to the spot Sparrow got caught from, readying himself to go springboard. Unfortunately for Eric he’s out and can’t counter as Erns jumps off and hits a jaw dropping Springboard 450 Splash! Right into a pin he goes as the crowd counts with the referee 1…2…3! RILEY STEVENS: “We have a new champion and his name is Christopher Darren Erns and he has reclaimed his GIW Evolve Championship!” The fans all go nuts as the referee is handed the title and he then hands it to Erns who has made a very impressive return to GIW with a big win over the always challenging Eric Sparrow. He slides out of the ring and begins to celebrate with the crowd, as the party has commenced here for the new champion Christopher Darren Erns who will head to Affliction with the Evolve championship. WINNER: CHRISTOPHER DARREN ERNS (New GIW Evolve Champion) As we are taken to the backstage area yet again we come upon a women standing alone in a hallway which brings some cheers and whistles as well, this woman is Amanda Henry one forth of High Voltage. As she stands there suddenly the debuting Dolo walks around the corner over to her with a big smile on his face. DOLO: “Hey there pretty lady, pleasure to meet you. What are you doing back here all alone?” Almost as soon as he says that we hear some foot steps and from both sides come A.J. Lightning, Justin Volt, and Elizabeth Reeve all of them looking none too pleased with Dolo. JUSTIN VOLT: “I think you should back off now. Because if you don’t, you’ll have some big problems on your hands.” Dolo seems a bit annoyed that Volt would talk to him like that but in this predicament he can’t say much while knowing he’s basically surrounded however their argument has caused another wrestler to come over, to see what’s going on. NATE AMAZING: “Now come on guys, he was just flirting with her he had no idea she was accounted for.” Amanda doesn’t seem to like being treated like a piece of meat as A.J. and Justin as well as Nate and Dolo stand starting at one another now. JUSTIN VOLT: “You know what we really don’t have time for this. You two are on Affliction right? How about next show we settle this in the ring, you two against us two.” Volt pats A.J. and then directs High Voltage out of there as Nate and Dolo look at one another, not sure about it but neither wanting to back down at the same time as the segment ends. Right away “Lift Me Up” by Moby hits and though nearly new music to the fans they have already grown accustom to the sounds as from the back comes Mark Cross with no concrete emotion on his face as he marches towards the ring, ready for this huge Submission match. On paper not a favorable match up from him but if you asked him he’d tell you that it won’t be a problem. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Well here he is ‘The Quitter’ Mark Cross!” RILEY STEVENS: “You know what I admire the fact that he thinks that highly of respect in pro wrestling. Do I wish he’d stay? Well yes but you have to admire it.” However as he is only nearly half way down the ramp he is caught from behind with a big clubbing blow by Chris Jericho. The fans boo as he tosses him right into the Steel Stairs on the side of the ring, crushing his right arm. The fans begin to give him some harsh boos as Cross holds his arm in pain, while Jericho lifts him up and tosses him into the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “This is disgusting!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “It’s called getting even Riley, deal with it.” The fans continue to boo him as Cross winces in pain on the ground as Jericho screams at the referee to start the match. At first he tries to say no but Jericho’s angry demands eventually get to him as he starts the match. DING DING DING! Right away Jericho begins to circle the injured Mark Cross talking down to him as he kicks him in the shoulder and then the legs, giving all of his body a little kick or two. When he attempts to get up Jericho in a rather heinous act kicks him right back down to the mat. The fans begin to boo as Jericho finally lets him get up only to grab him by the arm and whip him off the ropes. On the return Cross tries to throw a clothesline but Jericho catches it and begins to twist his arm, bringing some more pain to it. The fans boo this underhanded tactic by Y2J as he pulls the arm and then harshly tosses the arm and Cross to the ground. As Cross lays on the mat holding his arm in agony Jericho leans down and screams at him “You think you can make a name off of me!” before grabbing him and lifting him into the air. As he does he grabs the arm and twists it before tugging it behind his back, which nearly immediately causes Cross to wince from the pain. The fans are trying to cheer him on but even they have to admit this isn’t looking good at all for him. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Just tap already and leave us in peace.” As he holds the arm Cross either loses his ability to stand or he thinks it’ll help him get out but regardless he falls to a knee. Jericho however keeps it locked in as the fans surely begin to think he’s about to tap. Suddenly like a ball of fire Cross spins around ending up with Jericho in front of him before connecting with a big punch with the not hurt arm. The fans begin to cheer as Jericho bounces back but goes right forward trying to grab the arm again only to receive a big kick to the chest that stuns Jericho a bit. The fans are getting behind him as he runs forward nailing Jericho with his unhurt arm with a clothesline. When he gets up he stumbles right into place as Cross attempts to lift Jericho up for the ‘Go 2 Sleep’! However as he does his arm gives out on him and almost by force Jericho tumbles off of him. Having done more harm then good Cross holds his arm in pain, as Jericho turns around looking to do some damage. However he walks right into a sudden elbow from Cross right to the jaw. Perhaps in just a sudden bit of momentum Cross lifts him up not using the other arm hardly at all to hit a big Exploder Suplex making the fans go wild. With him downed he takes a second to go for the cover almost off instinct but just then he remembers that this is a Submission Match, as he gets on top of Jericho seemingly trying to find some sort of Submission to put him in. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “He’s got nothing, that arms trashed.” As he spends a second to think of what move would best with one arm, he is suddenly rolled off as Chris hops back to his feet. The fans boo as both men return to their feet as Jericho tauntingly calls for a lock up. He however doesn’t expect Cross to run forward catching him with a dropkick to the knee! The fans cheer as Jericho falls to one knee, holding it in pain as Cross backs up a bit looking around as he goes forward attempting to hit a Shining Wizard. At the last second Jericho is able to duck out of the way and when he falls onto his butt, Jericho attempts to spin him over. He gets his back and grabs at his legs going for what we all know he wants to finish this with, but just as he grabs the legs Cross suddenly spins over causing Jericho to fly into the corner. Y2J is able to stop himself in front of the turnbuckle. Cross gets up and runs at him trying to land a big tackle but Jericho avoids it tossing him right into the Steel Pole, hurt arm first! The fans sigh as they realize this is it. Rather then falling right into Jericho, Cross is smart enough to hold onto the ropes as he suffers in the corner. The fans have started a bit of a Cross chant, but when Jericho gets sick of waiting he walks forward in pursuit, it turns out to be his own mistake as a seemingly quickly recovered Cross slams him face first into the turnbuckle! RILEY STEVENS: “Perhaps this’ll change the momentum a bit.” Almost as soon as he says that Cross explodes right into a big spinning back fast right to the jaw of Jericho. The fans are starting to rock realizing that maybe this could be his set up. Chris hops right up however running at him only to run right into a drop toe hold and almost immediately right into a High-Angle Fujiwara Armbar! The fans go nuts as Cross uses mostly his uninjured arm to pull the arm back while using his other to offer little pull. Jericho starts to kick around in pain trying to get some where but unfortunately for him he is forced to tap out! Causing the fans to go wild. RILEY STEVENS: “Mark Cross has defeated the legend, Chris Jericho!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “It was luck.” The fans rain down their cheers onto Cross as he hops up, holding his arm in pain but happily accepting the referee holding up his uninjured arm. The fans affection will hopefully keep him here, as he asks for a microphone seeming to have something to say. The fans go silent as he gets it giving him his respect as he looks out at them, readying his speech. MARK CROSS: “Well I know there has been a lot of talk about my...” However just as he says this boom he’s crushed from behind with a big hammerfist to the back. The fans begin to boo as the ever so arrogant Chris Jericho has decided to ruin Cross’s speech. As the fans boo him he tosses Cross right out of the ring, unable to protect his fall as well due to the bad arm. He gets up wincing in pain as Jericho slides out of the ring, grabbing him and tossing him roughly into the barricade wall, before Security grabs him and pulls him away. As he’s pulled away he can be seen mouthing “You’ll never be me! You’ll never be me!” as he turns around and storms out of the arena as a member of security checks on Cross, leaving many to wonder would he really go out on a note like this? WINNER: MARK CROSS Back to the back we go where the final match is about under way. Sitting in her locker room seeming to be thinking through her match tonight is the ‘Galveston Island Champion’ Aspen Chaud. However the door suddenly pops open as she looks up and immediately shakes her head with a look that would only seem to imply she’d seen it coming. ASPEN CHAUD: “I didn’t figure I would get through the night without seeing you…Boss.” As she says this we all realize just who it is, as in walks Blake Beckett with that big smirk on his face as he doesn’t bother getting comfortable instead chooses to stand at a distance as he addresses her. BLAKE BECKETT: “Just came by to wish my champion good luck. Feels a little bit like old times doesn’t it? Awkward tension…realization that I am in total control of your job.” Aspen gets to her feet showing a complete lack of fear because she knew better then anyone it wasn’t what Blake could do physically it was what he could do as the Boss. She grabbed up her belt off her shelf looking at him. ASPEN CHAUD: “Your not here to get revenge on me for making you lose your job are you?” Aspen chuckles to herself but Blake grins and shakes his head no, seeming as usual to have something in that always scheming mind of his. BLAKE BECKETT: “No, no, no. Not right now at least, for now I just came to say good luck. I have some big announcements to make after your match and I think it would be better if you were in the ring to hear it.” Blake leans forward and pats her on the back and then walks out the door leaving as almost per usual a stinging been of thought in the back of Aspen’s head as she thought about just what he had up his sleeve this time as she shakes her head and heads to the ring not having time to give it too much thought. All what we have seen tonight has led up to this point now as the fans realized that every match expect for one had been finished and it was time for the Main Event! As they all stood there looking on in awe waiting to see what will happen out first is Scott Reave as the oh so appropriate “Fuckin’ in the Bushes” by Oasis hits and the fans begin to throw insults, jeers, and boos at him as he walks out soaking it all in, loving it like none other. RILEY STEVENS: “I’m not sure how a man can love being hated so much.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Taking it that you hate it?” RILEY STEVENS: “Very funny Oliver. Regardless Scott Reave may be one of the most hated men in our history, yet even I have to admit that he’s exploded onto the scene and is well deserving of this opportunity.” The fans continue to boo him as he walks down beside them, not a single fan holds out a hand for him to high five, something it is clear isn’t eating at him much as he walks up the steel steps, stopping half way up to look out at the crowd with a smirk before jumping over the ropes and into the ring, preparing for what will surely be a war. In the air is a very real electricity as the fans know that this one can go either way. "Bullseye" by Aly and AJ hits next, causing the fans to change their mood into cheers as our champion Aspen Chaud walks out from the back. RILEY STEVENS: “Here is the current reigning champion Aspen Chaud in her first defense.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “And her last.” Aspen has her belt strapped around her waist as she walks down towards the ring, high fiving the fans all the way down the ramp. On paper this is a no brainer between the lovable and small Aspen Chaud against the hated and smart Scott Reave. But Aspen has pulled off upsets before as she jumps up the steel steps and then under the second rope and into the ring. She’s all business as she unstraps her title and hands it to the referee as the fans are consumed into the stare down between these two. The referee shows off the belt and its Reave that looks up at him, wanting it badly as the referee calls for the bell. DING DING DING! The fans prepare for action as the two begin to circle one another; Scott Reave has that big smirk on his face before he’s even done anything. The fans have gone quiet realizing just how much this match truly matters for both competitors. However a sudden blown kiss from Scott starts the action right then and there. Aspen angrily charges at him only to be tossed to the side with relative ease by Scott Reave. Landing on her butt in the corner Aspen sits down on the mat looking up at Scott surprised that he’s not on the attack instead realizing he’s trying to get into her head. She uses the ropes to get up behind her as the two again begin to circle around each other, both combatants ready to fight yet again. This time Scott chooses to take a shot at her by calling for a test of strength in the center of the ring. To his surprise she seems willing to accept as she puts one arm grabbing his however she instead spins his arm and then goes behind him, tugging on his arm. The fans cheer until Reave is able to seemingly easily hip toss her over onto the mat, as he again backs off the fans a little surprised by this as Aspen gets up, getting a bit annoyed by this thinking he’s trying to insult her with this. She gets to her feet yet again this time, it seems these two are serious as she runs forward going for a clothesline only for him to grab her and shoot her right into the corner. With her there he runs at her going for a running knee but she’s able to move sending him right into the turnbuckle. The fans cheer as she rolls him up right after 1…Kick Out! It goes with little benefit as it’s only a one count as both of them hop up to their feet, Aspen’s up first and she’s able to toss him into the corner. She runs at him going for a ‘Tush Push’ a Turnbuckle Flying Butt Shot. However she’s caught by Reave who grabs around her waist and then brings her forward, only for her to land a victory roll right into a pin 1…2…Kick Out! The fans cheer her near fall, but realize that it still didn’t get her the victory as both of them get to their feet only it’s Reave this time that’s up first and to everyone’s surprise he crushes her with a kick to the mid-section that sets up a ‘R.C.D.’ an Emerald Frosion! The fans are stunned thinking that he may have just ended it so soon 1…2…Kick Out! They breathe a sigh of relief as Aspen kicks out, though Reave seems just as confident. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “That’s what you call finishing it early, she won’t bounce back.” Aspen is moving a bit but it’s clear that the R.C.D. did some damage as she tries to recuperate after that big blow. The fans continue to cheer her on, as Reave gets up seeming to be setting up for an early finish. Seeming ready for an early finish it seems that a Shining Wizard is in his sites right now. As she gets into position he runs only for her to duck down at the last second sending him to the mat with a bad attempt, as she gets to her feet seeming recovered from the move now as she sets up behind him. As he turns around she runs forward hitting him with a Jumping Dropkick that sends him right back into the corner. She gets to her feet with Reave struggling in the corner and then runs forward this time connecting with a ‘Tush Push’ a Turnbuckle Flying Butt Shot. As he stumbles out she goes to the top rope and waits for him to turn around and when he does she jumps off for the ‘AirBarbie’ a Flying Crossbody! The fans cheer as she goes right into a pin 1…2…Kick Out! The fans boo as she sits up nodding her head realizing that she was very close there. As she waits for him to get up she stalks him from behind, with the fans getting behind her. When he gets up she runs at him, only to run right into a kick to the skull. The fans boo as the momentum switches back over to Reave’s side as he tosses her into the corner then runs forward hitting a High Knee that he transitions right into a Bulldog! The fans boo as he rolls over Aspen and hooks the leg 1…2…Kick Out! The fans cheer that she kicked out, however a smirk on the face of Reave seems to send them reeling a bit as he gets behind her. As she gets up she walks right into another lethal Reave move this one being the ‘Brain Damage’ an STO with Anaconda Vice applied! The fans begin to go into a frenzy hoping Aspen will get out as Reave tugs on the hold tightly. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “He’s going to finish her, come on Scott!” He continues to pull back giving it all he’s got as Aspen attempts to scoot around to the ropes. After a second to catch her composure she does just that getting a foot on the ropes causing the referee to make the count 1…2…3…4…Scott naturally holds onto it as long as he can before breaking the hold and getting to his feet seemingly angered by the fact she was able to get to the ropes. Regardless Aspen seems in no shape to truly defend herself and as such she rolls out of the ring. The fans cheer her on from the outside as she attempts to get to her feet. She starts to get up as the referee gets to a four count however Reave suddenly has plans as he slides out of the ring and begins clearing off the announce table of all of Riley and Oliver’s things. The referee continues to count however Scott rolls back into the ring and then right back out, as he lifts Aspen up and smashes her head into the announce table. From there he throws her up onto it, and at this point she’s not responding at all just barely able to move. Right away he slides in and then right back out of the ring before quickly jumping onto the announce table over Aspen…1…2…As she gets up he grabs her and puts her in position for a Powerbomb as the fans begin to realize just what situation they are dealing with here…3…4…He grabs her and then lifts her up only for her to slide over off his back, before dropkicking him in the back sending him flying right into the barricade on the outside. The fans go wild as Aspen immediately rushes back into the ring…6…7…The fans go nuts however seemingly getting up like a machine Reave is able to get up rather quickly and slide into the ring right at the nine count! RILEY STEVENS: “That was very close to being a count out victory for Aspen.” Both fighters hop up to their feet Aspen runs at him first however he dodges her and then whips her off the ropes. On the return he runs forward swinging at her but she’s able to dodge it and then throw a big punch as the crowd screams YEAH! Reave answers back BOO! Aspen catches him again YEAH! Reave gives her another big one BOO! She answers back with one more YEAH! And then another YEAH! But then boom Enziguri out of no where from Scott Reave. The fans boo him as he yet again stalks her from behind realizing that he can try to end this right here and right now. As she stumbles to her knees he runs forward crushing her with a ‘Street Spirit’ a Shining Wizard that nearly knocks her head clean off. He falls down seeming to be a bit gassed after all this action and goes for the pin 1…2…Kick Out! The fans go wild as she has kicked out, as Reave gets up angrily screaming at the referee to count faster. The two exchange some words before he turns grabbing up Aspen who catches him with a big uppercut out of no where! The fans cheer as he turns around setting himself right up for ‘The Barbie Makeover’ a Straight Jacket Sitout Sleeper Slam that sets her right up for a pin 1…2…Kick Out! The fans seem just as surprised as Aspen does as she rolls off, looking around as it’s become clear to both of them that they are going to have to submit one another to finish it. However instead of going for the K.M.A. Aspen goes to the top rope, where she seems ready to go for something. As she finally gets set up a recovered Reave realizes that it’s either stop her or get hit. So as soon as he gets up he runs forward hitting her leg, however in an awkward series of events she falls off right on top of him in a gruesome and awkward fall for both of them, both of them roll right out of the ring Aspen basically tumbling while Reave does the same in pain. RILEY STEVENS: “That fall was vicious; the first person up might be the winner.” Both of them seem out of it as the fans cheer on Aspen to get back into the ring 1…2…3…At this point neither of them have even seemed to have moved from where they were when they fell out…4…5…At the half way point both of them have made attempts to get up only to fall back over unable…6…7…8…Just as it hits eight they both seem to be up…9…Both of them are up however both seem unsure where they are…10! To everyone’s surprise the count actually hit ten, as both wrestlers slide into the ring confused as to where they are as the referee steps between them shaking his head and calling for the match to be over. RILEY STEVENS: “Double Countout?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Reave got in first!” RILEY STEVENS: “It doesn’t matter Oliver, both wrestlers came in late resulting in a Double Countout.” Jamal Jackson announces the draw to the crowd which results in a wave of boos as Reave now seeming to at least know where he is, begins to complain to the referee as Aspen seems just as unpleased as him by the outcome. WINNER: DRAW The fans and Reave and Chaud alike are all angry with the ending but soon the sound of “Demon Eyes” by The Answer hits causing the fans to go into a bit of a frenzy figuring that Blake will come out and explain the situation for them. Aspen glares over at him unsure about this as Reave still is trying to explain to the referee something. As he walks down to the ring it appears Blake has a mic already. BLAKE BECKETT: “Well…Well…Well! I’m sure you fans are wondering what exactly is going to happen. You see I was really hoping that one of you would end up champion because it would have made this announcement all the sweeter.” Blake has reached the ring and he steps into the ring, with Reave and Aspen at attention as the referee holds the Galveston Island title in his hands, having not awarded it to anyone yet. Blake grabs it away from him, holding it in his own hands. BLAKE BECKETT: “You see…the new champion is…” Blake looks from Aspen to Reave, Chaud seems rather untrusting figuring a screw job is on the horizon as Reave screams at him that it’s his belt. BLAKE BECKETT: “Well…it’s neither of you!” The fans begin to boo as Reave screams at him trying to demand the belt as Aspen shakes her head, this feeling all too familiar. BLAKE BECKETT: “In fact…with the move to Fox Southwest tonight and the brand split…EVERY BELT IS STRIPPED! That means that neither of you are champions. Perfect Combination are no longer champions! Christopher Darren Erns is no longer champion! Joshua Acquin is no longer champion! You see ‘Genesis’ means the start of something new which means that we are starting anew! And if any of you want to come to me and complain…well let me just tell you that you can have your walking papers…because I’ve said it once before and I’ll say it once again…I am GIW’s God and you have to deal with that.” Blake drops the microphone and then turns and then heads out of the ring as the crowd begins to boo the hell out of them. Reave angrily pushes the referee to the mat as Aspen basically stares at Blake unable to believe the news as Blake turns, letting the fans trash fall beside him as he holds up his arms soaking it in. As the return of Blake Beckett and the return of a brand split has brought on a ‘Genesis’ here in GIW. No Champions, Two Shows, and Basically a New Day.
