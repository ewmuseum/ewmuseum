Throttle Open Battle Royal Winner: Jack Prowler Throttle Fatal Four Way Winner: Austin Flash & Presley Cruise C-22 Dressing In The Back The camera fades in to show a half darkened room. The Model C-22 is getting dressed with her back to the camera. Once she gets her bra and panties on, she turns around, then is slightly startled by the camera. However, she regains her composure quickly. C-22: A couple weeks ago, I watched Jack Johnson and his girlfriend break up, but before they did, she shoved me into the pool and called me a "stupid sexbot." Now while I may also be decent in the bedroom, there was no reason for her to do what she did. I mean look at her! I may be a robot, but I've never seen a diva as fake as her. That fake hair, fake eyelashes, fake nose... Wait, don't forget about those fake boobs!!! The point is, she's more fake then I am. And I deserve that Divas title. I don't care if we're on separate brands! Until another brand-specific women's championship shows up, it's fair game for anyone. Megathreat, you proved that you're willing to get into a working relationship with Jack, so why not me too? All I ask for is a Divas Championship match at Blackout against Violet Vanity. So what do you say? Will you give me my match? Or (moves hands down sides) will I have to persuade you?... The camera fades out as C-22 winks at the camera.
