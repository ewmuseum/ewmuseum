Thursday Night Fusion, 23 May 2013

23 May 2013

GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: THURSDAY NIGHT FUSION LIVE! At Galveston's North Beach Park – May 23, 2013 QUOTE We are back here tonight at the return of GIW as the fans are mostly here already with some empty seats still filling up as Fusion the Pre-Show for Revolt hits and these fans cheer as they see the show get underway looking at the two stars already in the ring, as usual the opener set up tonight as the Open Battle Royal open to any stars that feel they want to attend; and in the ring stands a grinning Chris Callum a debuting star alongside a man who looks really familiar to Jeremy Baltimore the second, only known as Jeremy St. Louis! RILEY STEVENS: “It feels good to back here tonight on Revolt and in typical Oliver fashion he is running late. But we are going to get this thing started on Fusion anyways.” With every star booked on the card this week these two get the opportunity to get things started big by going head to head in technically the first match for GIW as the bell rings, dusting off the cob-webs. DING DING DING! The fans that have already arrived go wild as Riley gets set to call the match, as these fans are all siding with debuting star Callum right away as St. Louis rushes him only to run right into a big boot to the stomach, doubling him over as he takes Jeremy and whips him off the ropes bouncing him right into an amazing Spinning Spinebuster that gets these fans on there feet, cheering wildly as Jeremy holds his back in pain and Callum gets up calling for the crowd to get on up, which they do just that. Wasting little time as Jeremy gets up he levels him to the mat with a clothesline, letting him pop right back up this time into a Inverted DDT from behind as these fans clap him on as he points up to the top rope, an idea they seem to like as he gets ready just as St. Louis stands up and turns around as Chris comes off the top with a Crossbody dropping him to the mat with a pin 1…2…-Kick Out. Nearly having him, the out matched Jeremy struggles to his feet as Callum stalks him and ends it with a ‘Cyrano Drop’ a Gutwrench Powerslam that puts him down for the count 1…2…3! REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner by way of pinfall…Chris Callum!” These fans seem to like the new guy cheering him, as he get up hardly having broken a sweat in this one as he poses for the crowd, letting them cheer him on. RILEY STEVENS: “Solid debut tonight by Chris and we’ll see how he does next week when he gets a real dose of GIW competition.” Callum rolls out of the ring high fiving a few of the fans as we head to a little highlight video of some past GIW moments, and hypes up the night’s action at the same time. WINNER: CHRIS CALLUM QUOTE Alexis Terry; Ryan Cavallari; Faith Simpson; and Sara Hollister. All of them had in common only one thing, besides the group being mostly comprised of females. Their similarity was that all of them had chosen to engage in the contest for the Deep South Championship, a title that would be defended on the pre-show and certainly lift up any newcomer, or struggling competitor that wanted an edge and the attention of those around them. It had the chance to reward prestige and pedigree its champions. Only time would tell and only the champion could start it on that path. RILEY STEVENS: “This is a new concept here on GIW as star’s stamina will be really tested as we crown a GIW Deep South Champion, a title that will be defended weekly here on Fusion, and I can assure you that holding this belt will really give you high standing in the company. The only hard part, may just be…keeping it.” Right now, there was no champion, just four ready competitors at each corner of their world in the ring. The bell didn’t even need to signal them to begin as they were off like hares in a dog race. Ryan held back for a few seconds to allow the girls to quarrel first, perhaps strategy, or that he didn’t want to hit a woman without reason. If there ever was one, this should have been it, but his intentions weren’t clear yet, just clever. Clever because Terry swung low with her sweet chariot of punishment upon the first to her, Hollister. Faith ducked under Terry’s next right hook and turned back with a kick to the gut. Alexis Terry retaliated with a yakuza kick that dropped her attacker on her back. She looked to the three corners in her view to find Ryan, but he was no where, perhaps the fourth one. She turned and he was ready to enter the fray with a fireman’s carry. Now all three women found themselves on their back before the only standing male. Not quite the position either probably enjoyed, so slowly the rose up one by one, Hollister first, then Terry, and Faith. RILEY STEVENS: “Ryan is a talented star, can get the job done in this one?” Now the real match could begin. Ryan took Alexis Terry as the other two broke off on their own physical tangent. A suplex on the right, and a DDT on the right, two down and two standing. Hollister kept Faith under her control and pulled her up by the fistfuls of hair she could grasp to continue the assault. To her left, Ryan and Terry were both on their feet and tried to control the other, without letting either individual gain any upper hand. Both stubborn, and both talented competitors. Something had to take, or someone had to break. It was clear that Faith had begun to ride overneath her struggle with Sara, a few submission holds and her opponent was worn down enough to mould into what ever fold, hold, or attack she kneaded her into. What she needed was pin, however, but even with Hollister’s lights knocked down the halls, Ryan and Terry were still standing, interchangeably. Faith dropped her opponent and turned to the other two to cut in and raise the heat. Ryan reversed her back to the other side of the ring with a reverse atomic drop and then turned back toward Alexis, whom took the breather given to her to prepare her next move. He may have been bigger than her as a man, but with her big boot to the face, anyone would drop with enough force. Now she stood tall and looked at the pretty little selection before her for bodies to pin. She chose Ryan, the closest and dropped down to him for the cover. RILEY STEVENS: “Can she get the victory?” Ryan refused to drop there and and threw his shoulders up to counter the attempt. Frustrated, Terry tried to retaliate by beating his head against the mat, but a boot was at her own head from Hollister. Sara then attempted to pin Terry, but Ryan had pushed her off. Not on his time would that happen and right after he had just kicked out of one. As punishment, he took her by the head, trying his best not to tug on her hair as that would be a bit too crass, he set this beautiful woman up for a very heartless moment. He wasn’t crass, but Terry didn’t care, she went for the low blow with a kick, but just managed to get the inside of his leg. Ryan held back the expletives and struggled down to his knees after giving his opponent Sara a whip to the corner. He wasn’t about to leave her standing so she and Terry could team up against him. Terry bathed in the wealth of destruction she was about to cause, hopefully enough to win this championship title. Faith Simpson was ready to get involved again and temporarily worked with Alexis to perform a two-person German suplex. RILEY STEVENS: “What a match, one could say proudly that GIW is back with this one!” With that little alliance over after another one of Terry’s big boots, she went for the pin on Faith, but Ryan still refused to give in, that and Hollister was back from the corner for another go. The match had only just begun and slowly fatigue set in, but nearly all of them were still set on fire, it just took one to extinguish the flames. Each one hoped it would be them, but only one could grab a hold of that belt and bring it to the level they wanted to be in. Faith was the first down, her battleship sunk by Terry as Ryan, at her back, swept the minefield of Sara Hollister with his flagging forearm smash. It was like the two had taken the bullets to the grave, fresh corpses to take advantage of, but one other still stood. Alexis Terry, ever confident in herself, whipped Ryan around expecting to end it there and then so she spun for her kick, the Glam Go Round sequence, but Ryan caught it, twisted it, and gave Terry quite a Heartless Moment to remember. It was devastating and like a death in the family, she was down for the count. Ryan rose as the victor over the ladies, but more important, as the first Deep South Championship title holder. He received the title, raised it tall and towered over the wreckage. REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner by way of pinfall and the new Deep South Champion…Ryan Cavallari!” These fans are crushed having pretty much had the one person they didn’t want to see exit the ring as champion, doing just that as the referee hands over the Deep South Championship over to Ryan Cavallari who pulls it in and holds it over his shoulder, the four competitors looking tired and one has to wonder if competing in this match was the wisest for any of them. RILEY STEVENS: “Welcome back to GIW and here on Fusion we crown Ryan Cavallari as the first Deep South Champion, will he be able to walk out with two belts tonight? We shall see.” With Ryan holding that Deep South belt, the show slowly starts to fade to black now giving us a few minutes to take a breather before it was time officially for…REVOLT! WINNER: RYAN CAVALLARI (New GIW Deep South Champion)
