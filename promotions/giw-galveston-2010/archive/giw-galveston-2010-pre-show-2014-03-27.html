Pre-show, 27 March 2014

27 March 2014

GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: REVOLT PRE-SHOW Live from The Waterfront Event Center - March 27th, 2014 QUOTE As per usual the feed here on GIW.com picks up live and free on the front page of the website where we pick up where GIW has on so many occasions, the Open Battle Royal. With the ring full of several debuting GIW stars; from BG Jones, Chuck Love-man, J Nightshade, Jadore Dior, and Ms. Valerie. Right away the…diversity of the group can be seen from a mile away, as they each stand around the ring waiting awkwardly for the match to kick off as the referee is in the ring now, as the commentators the special Pre-Show team start us off. COLT COX: “Here we are again for what looks to be a diverse field of competitors for our Open Battle Royal. GIW has been known for weird…But it doesn’t matter what background they may have tonight they get a chance to start their careers with a bang.” AMANDA ROWLAND: “Yeah! I like them all.” Colt really emphasized the weird part as he turned to Amanda, though she is her usual peppy self as we head back to the ring where our competitors are ready to go and this match gets things started. DING DING DING! As this thing starts off the different personalities are on display early as on one side of the ring BG Jones is taking a second to look up to the skies and doing the FSHG symbol over his chest, while Chuck Love-man has walked across the ring asking Nightshade to stop for a moment as he reaches into his boot and pulls out a dandelion, holding it up as everyone in the building but Chuck seems to realize he is barking up the wrong tree as J scoffs at him and takes it from him before throwing it down onto the mat squishing it between his boot as Chuck mouths “Come on man…” as Nightshade shakes his head and throws a punch as the two now get into it as across the ring Dior and Valerie have engaged in something of a slap off the two exchanging bitch slaps, as BG watches it all unfold shaking his head until Nightshade throws Chuck into him, as Love-man holds up a piece sign then gets punched down by BG as everyone is involved now as the three men are exchanging and the two…women are on the other side of the ring exchanging as well as this rather unique open battle royal has broken down, as Nightshade throws Chuck into the corner. He lands as BG grabs J from behind and spins him then tosses him at the corner as well but Love-man rolls out of the way and as Jones goes on the attack, Chuck levels him with a clothesline. As across the ring Valerie has tackled Dior to the mat and is throwing some big hits while she has her down. COLT COX: “A lot going on here early.” This match continues on as Nightshade comes out of the corner and gets kicked in the stomach by Chuck who grabs a hold of him and whips him across the ring off the ropes. J bounces off the ropes and gains some speed, then Love-man jumps over him with ease but as his boots come back to the mat BG is there waiting with a gigantic clothesline to take him down to the mat and crawling over to the ropes to save himself as J comes from behind him and grabs him and spins him around trying to toss him over the rope but BG gets a hold of the ropes to stop himself from going over, turning and throwing a elbow back over his shoulder that pushes Nightshade back as Valerie catches him with a big elbow to the back of the head. On the other side of the ring Dior is laying boots to Chuck in the corner who begs for peace, but gets none of it from Jadore. BG rushes Nightshade now who is reeling a bit but Nightshade manages to hurl him over his shoulder with a big hip toss as he turns about ready to hit Valerie but she holds her hands up begging him to stop as he does as she gets closer rubbing his arm a bit, he seems to be falling for the ploy however as she asks him to close his eyes and pucker up as she does the same. But as he does Dior comes in from the side leveling her out of the way then realizing the predicament that he/she is in as she looks over at J then leans in giving him a kiss as he opens his eyes. AMANDA ROWLAND: “What a lucky guy, a mid-match kiss.” Nightshade doesn’t find himself so lucky as he opens his eyes and shoves Dior to the mat wiping his lips in disgust as BG is seen laughing near the ropes as an angry Nightshade rushes him and clotheslines him over the top, sending the unsuspecting BG to the mat on the outside eliminated but just a moment later, as he is trying desperately to wipe off Dior’s lip stick Chuck comes up from behind and hoists his legs up and over as well sending him to the outside receiving just a kiss for his troubles in this match as he hits the outside. BG Jones & J Nightshade have been eliminated! Chuck apologizes to him after he eliminates him, still calling for peace as he turns around and receives a vicious slap from Valerie, he holds his cheek for a moment then shakes his head and hoists her up over his shoulder walking around with her for his next move but he gets chopped down from behind by Dior who takes out his knees and he falls dropping down Ms. Valerie who lands on her feet and turns into a Bionic Elbow from Jadore which stuns her, then when she stumbles and turns Dior follows up with a two handed bulldog to the mat. Getting up she lets out a yell as the fans here in Texas aren’t sure what they think of her yet as Valerie slowly gets up and when she does she receives a move Dior calls the ‘Cunt Punt’ a Bicycle Kick that leaves her prone as Chuck takes the chance grabbing her from behind and tossing her over the ropes eliminating another to a loud applause from the crowd who’d grown to dislike Valerie pretty quickly. Ms. Valerie has been eliminated! With just two left now Chuck turns to Dior both looking happy to have gotten rid of Valerie as Chuck offers a hug, as Dior unknowingly shrugs and walks over the two starting to hug one another. But after a few moments Jadore starts to struggle as this hug of his, ends up a rather brutal bear hug though he’d never call it that as he gets her off her feet in a brutal hug that she can’t get out of, ‘The Love Hug’ in tight as she may have tapped in a regular match but knowing he can’t finish her like that he walks back to the ropes and uses a move he calls the ‘Goodluck Chuck's Goodluck Chuck’ a Over Head Belly to Belly Suplex, that sends her out of the ring, which means the peacekeeper himself gets the win! REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner…Chuck Love-man!” These fans let out a huge pop as the Chuck has already won his way into their hearts as he smiles and holds up his hands in victory as the referee walks in to raise his arm but instead he receives a big hug from the Love-man after a huge win here tonight as Dior who gets up on the outside now knows that for Chuck a huge is anything but nice. COLT COX: “I mean I think I’d have been surprised nearly no matter how this ended but Chuck Love-man makes his debut and though all these wrestlers seem like they could get noticed here on GIW, it was Chuck who showed with that hug of his he can do anything.” AMANDA ROWLAND: “I love him, he’s so happy! I may go get a hug right now!” Amanda can be heard setting down her mic as Colt lets out another sigh as Chuck celebrates in the ring, with a big peace sign in the air as he leaves this Open Battle Royal the victor. WINNER: CHUCK LOVE-MAN
