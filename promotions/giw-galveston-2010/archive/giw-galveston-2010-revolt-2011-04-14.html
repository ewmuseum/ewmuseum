GIW — Card

Revolt, 14 April 2011

14 April 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: THURSDAY NIGHT REVOLT! LIVE FROM THE SEAWALL BOULEVARD ENTERTAIMENT COMPLEX = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! As the show starts a very minor pyro show sets off, then we begin panning around to the crowd who realize it’s that time to start the show as they cheer their heads off, but their reaction is caught on camera as suddenly their cheers become boos as soon as "To Be Loved" by Papa Roach hits and the fans angrily throw out their jeers as walking out to the ramp is Aspen Chaud wearing a dress and heels as Trent Lost follows to her right as Marcus Hister trails them by a ways, looking like a maniac. RILEY STEVENS: “Oh great, it’s the Queen and her Knights out here to surely talk themselves up. You’d think after last week they’d know when to admit defeat.” Aspen heads towards the ring, ignoring the fans as Trent heads up the steps in front of her. He holds the rope down for her as she steps in as he then does the same, Hister follows up heading in as Aspen demands a microphone and is handed it by Jackson at ringside as she walks back across the ring with the mic. ASPEN CHAUD: “Shhhhhhhhhh! The Queen demands your attention…” The fans respond with boos as Trent angrily demands that they be quiet beside her, as they die down she begins again. ASPEN CHAUD: “Last week you all witnessed all three of us lose and frankly it was embarrassing for us. But you see every team has a bad night, we are no different. Last week Sparrow, Starre, and Tony thought that they would put us away. Then low and behold at the end of the show, who was still standing? That would be me. Now you all may be wondering just why, we were left standing…Well the answer is simple, a masked man came out and helped us…Now I am not going to pretend to know who it was…And I don’t care so long as he continues to help, any how. Tonight I face…Tony Miranda…” The fans go wild for their guy Tony as she shakes her head just mentioning the name. ASPEN CHAUD: “You see he has a title shot on the line…And I plan on making sure that he won’t be the one walking in for a title shot, I WILL! Most importantly however is that I will be the one to END HIS CAREER! No more TONY MIRANDA, ONCE AND FOR ALL!” The fans boo the idea as Aspen lets out a big smirk as Lost claps his hands in agreement when suddenly “Monster” by Skillet hits as the fans realize just who that means as they go wild with cheers as out from the back comes Joshua Acquin as well as Austin Acquin walking behind him as Joshua has a microphone in his hand as he heads toward the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “We haven’t seen the Acquin Brothers in quite some time, what could they be doing back?” They walk together down towards the ring high fiving a few of the fans along the way as they head up the ring. Inside the ring Aspen doesn’t seem pleased she has been cut off at all as he steps into the ring; Aspen, Trent, and Hister walking to one side of the ring away from them. JOSHUA ACQUIN: “Aspen…Aspen…Aspen. You never were very good at keeping your mouth shut were you?” The fans roar as Aspen immediately seems offended raising the mic to her mouth. ASPEN CHAUD: “What are you doing back?! I thought you got released. Nobody wants you back…” As she lowers the mic in anger Trent points towards the back as Acquin shakes his head raising his mic. JOSHUA ACQUIN: “You see, that’s the thing. I have watched as you and your little buddies ran around here causing all sorts of stuff…And I couldn’t stand it…You know why? Cause I have been wanting to do something for a long time Aspen, I’ve wanted to do something to you for a long time…” Joshua walks forward as the fans seem to be buzzing, ready for him to attack as Trent and Marcus step forward ready to fight when all of the sudden, he reaches into his pocket and pulls out a ski mask holding it up for the fans to see. JOSHUA ACQUIN: “I’ve wanted to come back and help you!” The fans react accordingly booing him as Aspen seems relieved as he smirks, as Aspen walks forward hugging him as Hister and Lost walk forward agreeing with the additions when "The Human Stain" by Kamelot hits and the fans burst into cheers for a man that they know won’t turn on them as out from the back stopping at the top of the ramp is Tony Miranda with a mic as everyone in the ring looks up at him. TONY MIRANDA: “I hate to break up this, special moment but Aspen you and I, we have a match tonight. Now as you recall I agreed to put my career on the line…I even went through Trent Lost as you said I should, so you have to come through on your end of the bargain…” Aspen tilts her head to the side seeming confused as Tony chuckles at the top of the ramp to himself. TONY MIRANDA: “You see I didn’t really expect that you would remember my side of the deal. You see you agreed that I got to choose the match type…So I talked to Eduardo and he let me have what I wanted…See I have backstage right now…Some guys preparing to set up a cage, not the cage your thinking of but an MMA Cage. You see tonight you will be going one on one with me in a MMA Contest!” The fans go wild as Aspen’s jaw drops in the ring as she looks back and forth between her guys as Tony drops the microphone and heads to the back as the fans cheer him all the way to the back as we head to a commercial break. QUOTE It’s time for the first match of the night and though it comes first it is certainly Main Event worthy. “Nightmare” by Avenged Sevenfold hits as the fans go wild for Seth Seeker as he walks out from the back, seeming extremely focused on the task at hand here tonight. He heads towards the ring with complete focus as the fans cheer him on as he walks up the steps heading to the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Eric Sparrow has been on a run lately defeating all on comers but if there was ever a man to beat him, it would be Seth Seeker.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Maybe it’s because he’s a lunatic.” Seeker doesn’t seem happy rather just reserved, looking up the ramp waiting for his biggest challenge to date as “Circles” by Incubus hits and the fans yet again go wild this time the cheers are almost deafening as Eric Sparrow walks out with the gold around his waist as he walks towards the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Here’s the champion, whose opponent for ‘International X’ has yet to be named.” He high fives a few fans then runs up unbuckling his belt and sliding into the ring at the same time as the fans continue to cheer, as he heads top rope holding up his belt as Seeker stares at him the whole time. Sparrow bounces down handing his belt out as he backs up to his corner and the referee calls for the bell. DING DING DING! The fans are buzzing for this one never have we seen Sparrow fight someone with the physical presence of Seth Seeker. The two start to circle one another feeling each other out as they come together, with Sparrow landing a kick to his side. He follows it up quickly with another then backing up and going for a big kick, however Seeker dodges it and when Eric lands, be it on his feet turned to Seeker he grabs him and goes for a German Suplex. He tosses him and amazingly Seeker is so strong he gets him airborne but perhaps more amazingly Sparrow lands on his feet. The fans pop as Seth turns around shocked to see Eric up as he runs forward for a Clothesline. Sparrow ducks under it and runs off the other ropes, bouncing up onto them and then towards Seeker, but he’s caught right into the awaiting arms of Seth. The fans are loving this as Seth positions him over his shoulders, Sparrow slips off and is able to land outside of the ropes on the ring apron. Seeker turns throwing a bunch but Sparrow ducks it and hits him with a punch. Seth stumbles back as Sparrow grabs the ropes and goes airborne for the ‘Air Sparrow’ a Springboard Clothesline! However Seeker explodes forward landing a devastating Spear, as Eric’s momentum causes him to nearly split in half as Seth takes him down. Seeker goes for a cover 1…2…Kick Out! Seeker gets to his feet nodding, perhaps not expecting that to finish the champion. Sparrow gets to his feet as the crowd gives both men a standing ovation for the very energetic and fast paced start. RILEY STEVENS: “What an open to this bout.” The two take a second as Seeker prepares his next move while Sparrow catches his breath for a second. It’s Seth that tries to catch him off guard with a big punch but Eric ducks it and catches him with a Calf Kick. Trying to take the big tree down from the limbs he gives him a kick to the other Calf. Finally he finishes it with a Superkick that only sends Seeker stumbling back into the corner. Backing up he runs forward and catches him with an amazing ‘Pineapple Express’ a Corner Dropkick! Seth stumbles out of the corner as Sparrow goes again out to the apron, going to finish his combo. Jumping up he does it again this time the ‘Air Sparrow’ lands perfectly as he quickly makes a cover 1…2…Kick Out! Seeker literally tosses him off to keep this match going. Both men get to their feet, as Sparrow goes on the attack but a suddenly rejuvenated Seeker gets up and grabs him, using his strength he picks him up and puts him down with a Powerbomb that shakes the ring, going for the cover everyone thinks it’s over 1…2…Kick Out! The champion will not go down that easy, as Seeker gets up backing up a bit as he thinks of what to do next. Backing up he waits as Sparrow gets to his feet, he runs forward going for a Spear! At the last possible moment Sparrow ducks out of the way and Seeker goes right into the referee. Naturally his size takes him completely out. Seth tries to apologize but he turns around right into Sparrow who jumps up going for the ‘Purple Haze’ a Guillotine Choke! He has it locked in, but Seeker is too strong and he can’t take him down, finally Seeker crushes him down onto his back and it seems as though Sparrow is completely out! RILEY STEVENS: “Sparrow is out; we need a referee out here.” Just when it seems a new referee is about to come out it is instead Jak Hendricks running down towards the ring with a steel chair. Seeker sees him and gets ready calling for him to get into the ring. Sparrow is still out as the referee starts to stroll. Jak stands outside of the ring, the no contact rule coming into it. Suddenly Jak tosses the chair to Seeker who catches it, seeming a bit confused. Hendricks backs up the ramp as Seeker looks at him confused. However as the referee gets up he looks at Seeker and then at Sparrow then calls the bell, assuming the obvious as he calls for the Disqualification! RILEY STEVENS: “NO! This was a great match up and I thought Seeker was that close to beating him!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Hahahahahahaha! Jak out smarted him, he out smarted everybody.” Inside the ring Seeker tosses the chair down as he tries to plead his case to the referee. The referee shrugs him off pointing at the chair and then to Sparrow as the fans boo the result. The ref slides out of the ring heading to the back as Seeker shakes his head clearly angry. RILEY STEVENS: “This is a shame.” Back in the ring we have Sparrow slowly getting up, seeming confused as to what happened as Seeker in anger heads towards the back, wanting some retribution as we take a break. WINNER: ERIC SPARROW (By Disqualification) As the show has returned after what was a great match in the opener ruined only by Jak’s involvement, Seth Seeker is seen storming through the backstage area as he turns the corner finding Jak and walking up to him seeming ready to crush him with a punch. JAK HENDRICKS: “Hey! Hey! Hey! Remember the ruling, you can’t touch this.” Hendricks smirks at his joke seeming almost too confident that Seeker won’t do something as Seeker gets very close yelling at him. SETH SEEKER: “You cost me a match with the champion, I had him beat! You think I won’t attack you? It might just be worth it.” Jak grins, seeming sure that Seeker won’t do anything to him as Seeker seems to try to calm himself down a bit a smile forming on his face. SETH SEEKER: “You know what? I have a feeling that Eduardo…wouldn’t mind changing the ruling…So just know I am going to go talk to him see if I can’t change his mind…And if you hear my music you’ll know that it means he did.” Seeker walks off as Hendricks, shakes his head seeming sure that Santiago wouldn’t do that as we are sent to the ring. QUOTE When we head to the ring again we have Nelson Fielder already standing inside of the ring waiting as Brody Harper stands on the outside ready to manage his partner. “Down” by Curse Icon hits as the fans begin to boo as out from the back comes Trent Lost walking with purpose looking very angry, as Marcus Hister follows him to the ring. Wasting little time he slides into the ring, looking across at Nelson wanting to fight. RILEY STEVENS: “You can tell Trent Lost is not in the best of moods tonight, can he rebound from last week’s loss?” Trent angrily tells the ref to ring the bell and though he yells back at Trent he turns and calls for the bell. DING DING DING! Right out of the gates Trent explodes across the ring shouldering him all the way to the corner. Once there he backs up and starts to deliver punch after punch as the referee tells him to back off. He takes a step back then runs forward crushing him with a knee to the gut. Nelson stumbles out of the corner as Lost angrily follows him, spinning him around and slapping him across the face showing little respect for his opponent here tonight. Lost grabs him and whips him into the other corner. Running forward he nails him with a Headbutt and then climbs up the ropes. A hurt Fielder stumbles forward as Lost waits for him to turn around to hit the ‘Leap of Faith’ a Flying Crossbody that lands perfectly as he makes the cover 1…2…Kick Out! Lost gets up seeming to only be angered by the fact that Fielder didn’t just give up after that one. Stepping back he angrily calls for Nelson to get up, finally when he does so Trent feints a dropkick then catches him with a eye poke, as the referee scolds him Nelson walks right into the ‘Lost And Found’ a Lifting Full Nelson Twisted into a Reverse STO! The fans boo him as he makes the cover, knowing it’s over 1…2…3! RILEY STEVENS: “Dominating victory by Trent here tonight, a much needed win for him.” Lost gets up pushing Nelson out of the way as Harper drags him out of the ring to help him. Trent holds up his arm as the fans boo him, as we head to a break after the short yet convincing win as we are taken to a short break. WINNER: TRENT LOST Going to the ‘High Voltage’ locker room we find ourselves looking at what has become a rather daunting group of individuals with the Acquin Brothers, Marcus Hister, a sweaty Trent Lost and an angry Aspen Chaud sitting in the front of them all with a pair of gloves. ASPEN CHAUD: “What in the world are these things?” Aspen toys with them a bit, putting them on as she shows them to everyone else as Trent responds. TRENT LOST: “They are MMA gloves.” Aspen angrily pulls them off slamming them to the ground beside her, as she shakes her head. ASPEN CHAUD: “I’m a wrestler not a MMA person, this isn’t fair!” Chaud slumps down angry as Joshua now speaks up a bit trying to calm her down a bit. JOSHUA ACQUIN: “All you have to do is slap on the K.M.A. and make him tap, won’t be any different then when you usually fight.” Aspen angrily sighs picking the gloves up as she seems completely unsure of herself as we are sent to the ring for the next contest. QUOTE Returning to the ring its Jak Hendricks already in there with a big grin on his face as the fans have still not quite finished booing him as the announcers speak up over him. RILEY STEVENS: “Earlier tonight Hendricks cost Seeker his match against Sparrow and I’ll be honest I am really hoping that Santiago will reverse his decision and let Seeker crush him.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Why cause Seeker isn’t smart like Hendricks? Not Jak’s fault he’s smart enough to use something other then his muscles to get into his opponents head.” As Jak seems prepared “Silly Boy Blue” by David Bowie hits as the fans go wild for the North Beach Champion. Out from the back comes the always unpredictable champion holding his belt over his shoulder as he walks down towards the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “This man has been a great champion thus far. He’s come in and taken the GIW world by storm, defeating everyone in his path. His only loss he wasn’t even involved in the finish, you’ve got to think he’s fighting his way towards another shot at the belt.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “He lost, why should he get another one?” Riley can be heard sighing as Alioth gets into the ring walking over to the corner stepping up onto it holding up his title in the air as out of no where Jak jumps up and clubs him in the back, the title falls out of his hand as Jak goes wild throwing punches and kicks at him, the referee gets in between them as Alioth gets up holding his chest asking him if he can go, Starre nods and tells him to start the match. DING DING DING! The fans boo Jak’s actions as he circles Alioth who is holding his chest. Jak begins mimicking the way that he is holding his chest, causing the fans to boo him. Hendricks goes on the attack but walks right into a huge European Uppercut that causes him to be sent backwards holding his chin. Starre grabs him whipping him off the ropes as he comes back; he gives him a big hip toss as the crowd cheers him on. Alioth seems none to pleased with Hendricks actions but plans on taking it out on him in the ring. Jak gets up, slowly walking now seeming to realize that this isn’t going to be as easy as he might have thought. Throwing a wild haymaker turns out to be a bad choice as Starre catches it and turns it right into a Wrist Lock, as Hendricks cringes in pain, as Alioth tugs on it. Jak reaches out his leg and is able to get it on the rope 1…2…Alioth breaks the hold quickly as Hendricks backs up flipping his wrist as he’s clearly in a bit of pain after the move. Still seeming just off tonight he comes forward at the very technical Starre who this time lifts him up and into a Body Slam, that sends the fans into cheers. RILEY STEVENS: “The cheap shot Jak took prior to the match seems like the only offense that he’s gotten thus far.” The fans go wild as Alioth bounces off the ropes and then brings down a big Fist Drop, right onto the face of Hendricks. Jak gets up slowly stumbling to his feet as Alioth grabs him and attempts to put him in a Chin Lock, but Jak is able to break it and back away holding out his arms finding a way to be cocky after the beating he’s taken tonight. Jak now goes on the offensive only to some how run right into the Chin Lock which this time Alioth locks in with ease as the fans seem to almost laugh it off, that he ran into it. As soon as Starre even gets it in, Jak taps out as the referee calls for the stoppage. RILEY STEVENS: “Alioth with another win, despite the pre match attack.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I think Jak is hurt…or something.” Starre seems almost disappointed by the lack of fighting as Hendricks rolls out of the ring holding his chin as the fans cheer on their guy. The referee hands him his belt as he turns his attention to the crowd, suddenly an angry Hendricks returns to the ring stalking him from behind when suddenly “Nightmare” by Avenged Sevenfold hits and the fans go wild. RILEY STEVENS: “Did Seeker get the clearance he wanted?” Out from the back comes Seeker walking out with force, suddenly he stops as Hendricks seems confused until he turns to meet Starre who drops him down right into a Figure Four Leg Lock having realized what Jak’s intentions were, as soon as he locks it in Jak begins tapping out. RILEY STEVENS: “This is called karma!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “This is an attack, this is unfair.” Seeker has a big grin on his face on the ramp as Alioth releases the hold as the fans are going wild. He pushes him out of the way as he slides out of the ring as we cut from Seeker grinning to Jak holding his legs in pain as we go to the back. WINNER: ALIOTH STARRE Heading to the back we find ourselves face to face with Sarah Sharp with a microphone. SARAH SHARP: “My guest at this time is Jacoby Jackson…” The camera pans out a bit as Jacoby comes into screen bringing some boos from the crowd as he simply smirks. SARAH SHARP: “Last week you attacked Ross Rydell in the Parking Lot, while you said you were going to use him as a stepping stone. Now it’s been announced that he’ll be ready for a match at ‘International X’ are you regretting your actions?” Sarah holds the microphone up to him as he looks at her like she’s crazy as he starts his response. JACOBY JACKSON “Regretting my actions? Really. I expected this to happen and I couldn’t be happier. On the biggest stage in Mexico, I’ll get to make a statement to everyone in the back and out in the crowd that I am the best! By defeating their idol Ross Rydell, I’ll make sure they never forget my name again. I hope Rydell is watching tonight, because I am about to show him what he’s getting himself into.” Jackson hands off the microphone to Sarah as he heads towards the ring for his contest as we are sent to a break. QUOTE After the break we return to see the man whose interview time just ended and he’s in the ring waiting for his opponent to come on out. RILEY STEVENS: “This man Jacoby Jackson picked a fight with Ross Rydell last week and he’ll have to face the man now at ‘International X’.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “That way he can make a name for himself beating a guy who doesn’t have much left.” “Whats My Age Again?” by Blink-182 hits as the fans cheer as out from the back walks the biggest member of Brotherhood of Steel, Sergio Moura seeming focused as he heads towards the ring ready to go. Following him is Gonçalo Martins in his street clothes cheering him on. Sergio high fives some fans and then enters the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “A lot has been made about Brotherhood of Steel, they’ve been great ever since their debut. However we haven’t seen much of Sergio, we’ll see how well he can do here tonight.” Sergio steps up into the ring, with a smile on his face as he walks through the ropes looking out at the crowd who is cheering him on. Martins finds his spot on the outside as the referee calls for the bell. DING DING DING! Right away the ever so disrespectful Jacoby Jackson begins to trash talk Sergio as he poses the question loud and clear “Who are you?” chuckling to himself as the fans boo him. Jackson continues to pester him walking across the ring, giving him a bit of a shove waiting for him to do something. Finally Jacoby sticks out his chin offering a free shot. Sergio looks at the crowd and then back at Jackson and then nails him with a gigantic Big Boot! The fans go wild as Jackson falls to the ground quickly realizing what he’s in for. Jackson gets to his feet he even seems a bit woozy as he walks right into a big Uppercut from Sergio Moura! The fans are going wild as he whips him off the ropes and right into a big Clothesline! This has been all Sergio as he grabs him and lifts him up, preparing himself for a giant Powerbomb that could easily have put Jackson out. Rather then making the cover, Sergio takes a step back looking over at Martins who screams at him something; Moura turns around with a big smile as Jackson gets up throwing a punch at the air, the completely wrong way. When he turns around he probably sees the shadow of the man. But he most certainly feels the hand clench his throat lifting him up and bringing him down the ‘Choke of Steel’ a Chokeslam! The fans go wild as he makes the cover and it’s over 1…2…3! RILEY STEVENS: “What a convincing win for Sergio Moura here tonight. And if he didn’t even get a single move against this man, how in the world are we going to expect him to put up a fight against Rydell?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I don’t even know what to say; sometimes even I put my faith in the wrong people.” Gonçalo joins Sergio in the ring raising his arm as the two non champion members of the Brotherhood of Steel showcase themselves as we cut to a video. WINNER: SERGIO MOURA A video begins to play as a man is slowly panned out on; his face is darkened out as he stares at the ground as we get a glimpse of his entire body, the camera gets a view as he looks up and techno music begins to play in the background as the screen fades on his face as the text ‘Techno Viking’ is shown as the video ends. Just before we head to the ring we are sent to the backstage area where Tony Miranda is slowly getting his gloves taped onto his hands by some backstage staff member. The man finishes as Tony feels them out grinning and patting the worker on the back as he walks off as Tony slams his fist into his hand as we head to a break. QUOTE When we return to the ring, it’s no longer…a ring any more. Instead during the break they set up a cage, as the ring was taken down. They are still tweaking a few things so we are sent down to the announce table. RILEY STEVENS: “Ladies and gentlemen this is not a MMA event however tonight we will feature a MMA fight. You see back when Aspen and Tony agreed to this deal, it was Career on the Line so long as Tony got to choose the match. Well earlier tonight he shocked the world when he announced that it would be a MMA fight.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “So unfair.” RILEY STEVENS: “Regardless the rules of this for those who do not know is that their will be three five minute rounds. You can win by KO, Submission, or a Decision win.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I can’t believe Tony would stack the deck like this.” "Now I'm That Bitch" by Livvi Franc hits as the fans begin to boo their heart out as from the back comes Aspen Chaud strolling along with her posse. Around her are the Acquin’s, Hister, and Lost as she wears her gloves and black spandex with a black sports bra ready to fight…Or maybe not as she tries to turn around only to be stopped by her teammates who reassure her of what she’s doing as the referee checks on her before she gets into the cage where another referee is waiting. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “The poor girl has no idea how to fight in MMA.” "The Human Stain" by Kamelot now hits as the fans roar. Tony walks out by himself wearing his gloves as well as his little trunks for the fight. While he may come alone its clear everyone in the place is on his side for this one. As he stops to get checked by the referee, he looks over at the rest of High Voltage before heading up into the cage. RILEY STEVENS: “This is Tony’s environment now and perhaps most importantly he made it so that no members of High Voltage can get in. Let’s get this thing started; Career vs. Title Shot, lets go!” Into the cage we go as the fans seem intrigued as the referee has both of them come to the middle, he explains the rules and then backs them up as he then waits before starting the fight. DING DING DING! The clock starts as the two start to circle each other, Aspen seeming less then thrilled to be in here right now. Tony comes forward throwing two big punches that Aspen quickly dodges and starts to run away from Tony. The fans are enjoying this, as she has no where to run. Miranda follows her with his hands up, as he throws a big punch missing again as Aspen backs up into the cage. She begs for a time out and to everyone’s surprise he takes a step back only to be sucked punched by Aspen as she goes to try to take him down. However he stops her with relative ease as he picks her up and slams her down. RILEY STEVENS: “That should earn him some points.” The fans are going wild as Tony sits on top of her, slowly messing with her hair which only seems to anger Aspen. Trying to get out but she can’t as Tony is too strong; she flips around not knowing how dangerous that can be. Suddenly Tony grabs her and pulls her in tight for a Rear Naked Choke! The fans are going wild as Aspen struggles then quickly taps out as the fans roar with cheers, as he releases the hold as the referee pulls him off. RILEY STEVENS: “Wow this thing is over already! At only a minute and thirteen seconds into the first round Tony Miranda has submitted Aspen Chaud!” The fans are going wild as Tony stands up waiting to have his moment. However it doesn’t last long as a voice interrupts his celebration. WINNER: TONY MIRANDA Everyone turns to the stage as Blake Beckett walks out with a microphone, as Tony looks up at him a bit confused. BLAKE BECKETT: “This is not MMA! This is not Eduardo Santiago’s show. You see he left early to go to Mexico to start promoting this show. Which means that he won’t know anything about what happens. You know I can’t stand you Aspen but frankly I can’t stand the fact that Santiago runs around here thinking he can control this place. So Tony you’ll have your title shot against Eric Sparrow…But Aspen Chaud will be the Special Referee!” The fans boo as Blake grins and heads to the back, having said what he came here to say. In the ring Aspen has gotten to her feet and as soon as the cage door is unlocked she points at Tony as all of High Voltage comes in storming in to ruin the moment! The fans boo as the Acquin’s, Lost, and Hister all begin to attack Tony, crushing him into the ground. Suddenly the crowd starts to go wild as out from the back comes Alioth Starre, Eric Sparrow, Antonio Martins, Pedro Cabrita, and Sergio Moura! RILEY STEVENS: “This is a war!” Trent Lost exits the cage going right after Alioth Starre! The fans are going wild as the two fight around the cage. Also exiting the cage is Marcus Hister and both Acquin’s who meet up with the Brotherhood of Steel on the ramp as they begin to fight it out! Inside of the cage it’s Aspen Chaud, Tony Miranda, and Eric Sparrow! Tony catches Aspen with a punch then Sparrow does the same. They bat her back and forth before Tony grabs her and tosses her into the cage! The fans are going crazy as Trent and Alioth continue fighting. However back in the ring with all the carnage outside of the ring time seems to stop as Tony and Eric go face to face. RILEY STEVENS: “This is carnage out here! What a way to end the final show before ‘International X’! We’ll settle all of this as we head for the first time ever to Cancun, Mexico! Don’t miss all of the action!” Riley seems to be calling this over the crowd’s cheers and the fighting as we cut from Starre and Lost, to the fight on the ramp, finally back into the cage where Tony and Sparrow still are locked into a glare as the show slowly fades.
