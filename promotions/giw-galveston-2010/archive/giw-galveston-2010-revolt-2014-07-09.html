GIW — Card

Revolt, 9 July 2014

9 July 2014
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: REVOLT Live from the Kauffman Arena – July 9th, 2014 BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! “Dangerous” By Within Temptation plays to kick this show off. The fans in unison boo loudly as Alexis Terry wearing a grey pantsuit walks out from the back with the GIW Undisputed Championship over her shoulder. The System David Sinclair, Charity Ryan, and Ian Mendoza surround her. RILEY STEVENS: “Welcome everyone to Revolt here tonight and we kick things off with…The System.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “You sound upset Riley? Look at this great cast of characters we got coming out here!” The group walks to the ring as the fans boo and the camera catches a few signs in the arena “Terry Fears Ramsey”, “Faux Champion, and “I slept with Alexis”. Terry slides into the ring while the others take the steps in. Alexis holds the championship with one hand in the air as the camera focuses in on the Undisputed Championship. She has a microphone now and her music dies down to allow her to speak. ALEXIS TERRY: “Last week, I proved once and for all that I am the greatest Undisputed Champion of all time when once again, for a third glorious time, I become Undisputed Champion!” Terry holds the title in the air for a moment and then drops it back over her shoulder. ALEXIS TERRY: “Unfortunately Madison Seton proved me right when like the flake she was she ‘got injured’ and was unable to compete at Moment of Glory. A true Champ would be able to fight through all obstacles, take on all challengers, and most of all defend the championship at the biggest show of the year. I guess Madison was more worried about a broken wrist than wrestling for you fans on GIW’s most important show of the year. Well, so be it. Alexis Terry will fight for you. I will be a fighting champion and I will take on all comers. Last week, Beck Ramsey decided to step up and become victim number one. Much like Madison Seton, Beck Ramsey was brought back to this roster because you asked for him. You wanted Beck back and I obliged and gave you Beck Ramsey because that’s just the kind of boss I am. I listened to the fans!” The crowd boos loudly. “You suck!” chants echo throughout the arena but Alexis ignores them and continues. ALEXIS TERRY: “I brought Beck back to please you but I brought Beck back under the assumption that like a go little boy he would know his place. Beck is a fine wrestler. Beck would be the perfect GIW Deep South Champion. Beck would relish in the midcard but not the main event. The GIW main event would eat Beck alive but still, even after I tried to protect him, Beck decided to play outside The System and stick his nose in my business so now I have to teach Beck Ramsey what happens when you don’t work within The System. Beck wants a shot at my championship? He wants a shot to become Undisputed Champion? Fine but I am warning you now Beck Ramsey that this is the big time. All eyes are on you now. You wanted the chance for your name on the marquee so be it but now the weight of the world is on your shoulders. But – if I am going to teach Beck Ramsey a lesson about working within The System I need the night off so I hope you all enjoy the show!” After all the talk about being a fighting champion Alexis giving herself the night off rubbed many of the fans the wrong way as much like before, the boos continue to roll in. Alexis smirks and is about to leave when “Vicarious Devil” the theme song for Beck Ramsey cues. In a much different reaction than Alexis’, the crowd explodes for Beck Ramsey. He walks out dresses casually in a t-shirt and jeans with a microphone already in hand. He stops at the top of the ramp and shoots Alexis an arrogant smile much to the dismay of Alexis who is not fond of being interrupted. BECK RAMSEY: “Alexis, Alexis, Alexis…off spewing your jargon some more, eh? I couldn’t help but listen in the back about how you are a fighting champion and a true, oh how did you put it, main event player? The last time I checked you took that title from Madison Seton, you didn’t win it in a match. Trust me, I hate Madison Seton as much as the next guy but at least Seton won it in a match she didn’t jump someone and injure them because she couldn’t win the championship fairly in a fight. Now I don’t know if its old age Alexis or if its because you really are just crazy but the last time I checked I have been busting my ass week in and week out to earn a shot at the Undisputed Championship. You hid behind Mendoza over there and refused to give me the shot I deserved! But now, now I have you at Moment of Glory and there is no hiding anymore Alexis. I am going to kick your ass and finally become Undisputed Champion and there is not a damn thing you can do about it!” Beck lowers the microphone and the crowd cheers loudly as Alexis is fuming in the ring. Charity walks up to Alexis and calms her down. ALEXIS TERRY: “Is that so Mr. Ramsey…is that so? Well, the way I look at it. I have beaten you plenty of times before, I’m sure you remember our classic ladder match at Exile, yeah? You couldn’t beat me then and I am sure you can’t beat me now. You Beck Ramsey are writing checks that you can’t cash. This, this is how comfortable I am in knowing that I am going to beat you. If, somehow someway god himself drops from the heavens and helps you defeat me and win this championship, I will never wrestle a match again in my life. I will retire.” BECK RAMSEY: “Well, you better call the rest home now Alexis because you are going to need a room after Moment of Glory.” Beck’s theme song plays again as the crowd is in shock over what Alexis has just said. Not only is the Undisputed Championship on the line but Alexis’ in ring career is as well as we take a break here. ==================================== The crowd is hushed now, waiting for the opening match of the night to begin. Suddenly the music of Ian Mendoza hits and the silence turns into a storm of boos from the crowd. They clearly haven’t forgiven Ian Mendoza for manipulating them for over three months, but as Mendoza steps onto the ramp and looks out to the crowd, the smile on his face shows us that he really doesn’t care. RILEY STEVENS: “Here comes Ian Mendoza now. Alexis Terry’s right hand man. I wonder what he has to say.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Whatever it is, I’m sure it is important Riley – so shut up!” Mendoza’s music dies down as the crowd continues to boo him. He stands on the ramp as he speaks. IAN MENDOZA: “Galveston! Thank you so much for the warm welcome. Every night that I step foot in the Kauffman Arena, I feel the love. I really do. Thank you all!” More boos as Mendoza chuckles. IAN MENDOZA: “I have an announcement to make, straight from Alexis Terry herself regarding this open battle royal. You see, we love giving new guys and gals all of the tools they need to thrive in this sport, so we have added a stipulation to this match. The winner will be entered into the Seize the Moment ladder match at Moment of Glory IV. As you know, if you win the Seize the Moment match, you get to main event the next iPPV against the Undisputed Champion. So, Sam Hain, Captain, and Carson Maxwell, our newest stars, will have a huge opportunity. Who will Seize the Moment tonight so that they can Seize the Moment at Moment of Glory?” His music kicks up again as he leaves to a mixed reaction. The Open Battle Royal is next. QUOTE Up first tonight on this edition of Revolt is the Open Battle Royal this time getting a spot on the live show and featuring a whole new crop of young talent looking to get started here on Revolt, on the very final show before ‘Moment of Glory’ and one that with most the top stars absent gives them a real chance to stand out. In the ring we have Carson Maxwell, Sam Hain, and The Captain in the ring as the participants tonight each of which looking ready to throw their opponent over the top rope. RILEY STEVENS: “Quite the talented group we have out here for the Open Battle Royal on the show before the big one, they make their GIW debuts looking to get noticed.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I like this Captain guy…” All the stars are looking ready to go, each eyeing each other up as The Captain poses around the ring as the referee’s on the outside call for the bell ready to go. DING DING DING! This match up gets started with three debuting stars one that made quite the impression with the ladies on his way out in The Captain who walks to the center of the ring still wearing that sailor hat as he pulls it off and smirks as Hain and Maxwell look on confused as he look to a few females in the audience giving them a wave when finally the other two men have had enough collapsing on him, both taking him down to the mat stomping away at him as Captain rolls around in trouble as finally the two kick him over to the side as he hides out underneath the ropes as Sam and Carson now meet up in the center of the ring, both locking up looking to want to prove they got some grappling skills but Hain takes advantage of being the much heavier man pushing Maxwell back up against the ropes before tossing him off, as Carson bounces off back at Hain who spins and clocks him with an elbow dropping him to the mat as he turns and hits the ropes but Maxwell rolls towards him making Sam jump over him before bouncing off the ropes again, this time on the rebound it’s Carson who gets the better of him, landing a big Dropkick on the rebound as he gets up looking out at the crowd who have started to rally around him, as he walks over to Captain as he gets up seeming to be ready to try to take him out of the fray but Captain catches him off guard with a big back elbow dropping him backwards as he spins and grabs the back of his tossing him over the ropes but with ease Maxwell lands on the ring apron, but Captain nails him with a Upper Cut that rocks him nearly breaking his grip but he catches himself and pulls himself through the ropes to shoulder Captain backwards but Captain charges forward kneeing him in the side but Carson manages to get a grip of the ropes. RILEY STEVENS: “Carson in trouble but he may have saved himself there.” Captain seems to have him in trouble on the edge there but Hain from behind clobbers him in the back knocking him off his feet as he gets up and drags Captain up with him. Tossing him into the corner while Carson gets his wits about him, Sam delivers shot after shot in the corner to Captain who turtles up as finally Hain rears back but Captain calls for a time out before catching him with an eye poke a move that unfortunately in a match like this is completely legal. Hain stumbles back blinded as Maxwell whose back up now goes Springboard landing a Roundhouse Kick off the top rope to the already blinded Hain as he hops up to his feet and Captain continues to pick his spots rushing in kicking him in the gut and landing a DDT as he gets up walking over to the ropes with both men down behind him, giving the crowd a little grin winking out at them before he walks back to the two men grabbing up Hain first and tossing him into the corner, before hoisting up Carson as well tossing him over into the same corner stacking them both up as he charges at them but at the last second Hain saves himself pushing Carson forward to take the brunt of a shoulder from Captain, knocking him to the mat and knocking Captain back as Sam charges and spins around into a Sleeper hold cranking it for a second as Captain reaches up to break it leaving his body open for Hain who drops down into a Bridging German Suplex releasing him afterwards as he gets up taunting the crowd telling them he’s the future as he walks over kicking Carson while he is down. RILEY STEVENS: “Really everyone picking their spots in this match, all three looking great.” Hain walks back over to Carson still taunting the crowd motioning that he is going to throw over the crowd favorite as he hoists him up and goes to do so but Maxwell grabs a hold of the ropes and brings an elbow backwards stumbling Hain back as he turns and jumps into a Crossbody taking him down to the mat as he hops up and catches Captain getting up charging at him and nailing him with a big boot dropping him to the mat now as he and the crowd are starting to get it going as the momentum is back on his side as he looks at Captain then back at Hain, rushing across to nail Hain with a Diving Bulldog planting him as he gets up and turns his attention back to Captain now looking to finally get rid of him as he goes to hoist him up when Captain throws up a Low Blow out of no where but Maxwell seems to have it scouted as he catches his arm as it goes up, stopping it as he gives Captain a stiff kick to the side of the head while he is on a knee then nails him with a ‘Maxed Out’ a Jumping DDT from no where, as he plants Captain but the bad news is he has to get him over the top. He hoists him up but he’s essentially dead weight, he uses all the strength he has to muscle him up and over the ropes as he collides with the outside but it takes too long and by the time the Captain hits the bottom, Sam rushes across the ring having recovered using Carson’s own lack of balance against him tossing him over to the outside with both men being eliminated as Hain stumbles back in victory. REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner…Sam Hain!” The fans are upset having been pulling for Carson in this one as he turns around rubbing his hair and shaking his head unable to believe he got caught like that as in the ring Sam smirks out at him, not seeming to care how he did it as long as he got the win. RILEY STEVENS: “Well Sam Hain gets the victory and with it makes some noise, he could find himself getting a big opportunity with ‘Moment of Glory’ just around the corner.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “He looked good but I still like this Captain guy.” Captain has gotten up dusting himself off and getting a hold of the hat as he rubs his chin acting like it isn’t a big deal as he heads to the back while Maxwell does the same but goes backwards watching Sam celebrate almost for his own motivation as we head to the back now. WINNER: SAM HAIN We cut to Alexis Terry’s office where we see Jack Waters, Awesome Xombie, and Tony Miranda gathered around a desk with David Sinclair and Alexis Terry. ALEXIS TERRY: “Alright boys, the cameras are rolling now, and I honestly don’t want you three on TV any longer than you have to be, so I’ll make this quick. You all will be competing in the Seize the Moment match at Moment of Glory IV. Now get out of my office.” She points, but no one leaves the office. TONY MIRANDA: “Wait… are you kidding me Alexis?” Terry rolls her eyes, but before she can say anything Waters cuts her off. JACK WATERS: “I’ll be busy defending my Deep South Championship.” Terry seems like she has no words, so Sinclair answers for her. DAVID SINCLAIR: “You have no idea if you are even going to win the belt. And besides, worst case scenario you pull double duty at Moment of Glory. Are you not up to it?” Waters can’t believe what he is hearing. JACK WATERS: “This is bullshit.” Tony turns to Waters, actually agreeing with him. TONY MIRANDA: “Yes, it is. You mean of all the people I came back to destroy, all of the great matches you could book, you’re putting me in Seize the Moment. With who? These two, some new guy, and Ryan Cavallari?” Terry nods. AWESOME XOMBI: “I’m okay with this. This is okay. I like it.” Both men turn to Xombi. JACK WATERS: “You would be.” The tension in the room is rising. AWESOME XOMBI: “Tony. Don’t get too upset. Just remember you still have to go through me tonight.” TONY MIRANDA: “I’m not upset. I’m just disappointed in the direction Alexis Terry is taking…” ALEXIS TERRY: “I’m sure you’ll find some way to get over it. Now get out, all of you.” ==================================== We cut to the back where we see Carson Maxwell getting examined by the GIW medical team. Kevin Astro enters the scene, shaking his head. Maxwell doesn’t notice Astro until he begins to speak. KEVIN ASTRO: “You are the type of guys they are hiring these days? Man… that’s disappointing.” Maxwell looks up Astro, confused. CARSON MAXWELL: “Who are you?” Astro scoffs at the idea that there would be anyone who doesn’t know who he is. KEVIN ASTRO: “I’m a man who gets things done. Stay tuned and maybe you’ll learn something.” With that, Astro leaves the scene. Maxwell shakes his head, but then turns on the TV broadcasting a closed circuit version Revolt right next to him. As we fade into the TV, we swap to the ring. QUOTE Time for the next contest of the night as “I Hope You Suffer” by A Fire Inside hits and out from the back comes Team Captain, Ryan Cavallari being flanked by his tag team partners tonight in Astro and Jeremy Baltimore both looking none to pleased to be forced to come out to Cavallari’s song, Astro especially looking annoyed as he shakes his head behind the two as Cavallari seems to be pretending he’s alone which he’d probably rather be. Baltimore walks up the steps making it there first jumping over the ropes and heading to their corner, as Ryan makes his walk up the steps next entering the ring as well. RILEY STEVENS: “Cavallari is the Captain of this team but you got to think Astro and Baltimore see this as an opportunity to steal the show.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Cavallari will rally the troops, he’s a winner it is what he does.” Astro is last up jumping over the ropes into the ring each of them posing on opposite ends of the ring as "Heart Shaped Box" by Dead Sara hits next as the…strange team now heads out being lead by Artemis Kaiser the pocket sized star who has been looking amazing of late, as she looks back at her two tag team partners as Chuck Love-man and Mo Chronic walk out as Kaiser lets out a sigh then shrugs her shoulders making her way down to the ring as Chuck walks over to the fans hugging one, as he looks at Chronic as he does the same the two working their way down to the ring giving out hugs to the fans spreading the “love” as Kaiser steps up into the ring first looking across at Cavallari the two soon to be opponents tonight and again at Moment of Glory. RILEY STEVENS: “Kaiser and Cavallari will meet in the Seize the Moment ladder match for a future shot but will look to one up one another a week after teaming up in the ring.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “And Kaiser expects to do it with these two goofs?” Chuck and Chronic spread a little mo’ love before walking up the steps and into the ring standing on the outside proposing a group hug as they enter the ring one that Kaiser reluctantly takes a part in as the referee calls for the bell. DING DING DING! Our biggest match in terms of number of wrestlers is now up as Kaiser steps away from Mo and Chuck’s group hug as Baltimore speaks up on his end saying he’ll start and Cavallari and Astro seem unwillingly but they relent when it doesn’t seem he’ll let it go. None the less Baltimore turns walking to the center of the ring, as Mo and Chuck stand there as Kaiser shakes her head wondering what she has gotten herself into as Chuck first proposes a group hug and Chronic eventually subsides doing it as well as Baltimore shakes his head pushing them both away and Chuck goes crazy on him tackling him to the mat throwing wild shots to Jeremy on the ground as Chronic steps back, to his corner now letting Love-man start but he isn’t showing much love now, offended by Baltimore turning down the group hug. Baltimore finally rolls over to the ropes grabbing them to save himself as the referee starts to count, 1…Chuck breaks it immediately and gets up right as the referee counts two. Holding up two fingers, Chuck sees it and smiles leaning in for a hug with the referee while he holds up the two, though Love-man takes it as peace as he steps back holding it up himself with the referee who seems completely baffled. Baltimore is back up charging at Chuck but he dodges it at the last moment pushing Baltimore into his corner, where he charges him and catches him with a elbow rocking Jeremy. He reaches out and tags in Chronic his tag partner who steps in, and drops a big knee to Jeremy as the two walk forward hitting a Double Hip Toss dropping him to the mat. RILEY STEVENS: “These two…surprisingly working together pretty well.” After the hip toss the two high five then drop a double elbow down on Baltimore’s chest as Chuck exits the ring after patting Chronic on the back as Mo pulls him up and throws him into the corner as well this time tagging in Kaiser who steps in, charging at Baltimore with a big running Uppercut in the corner before tossing him out then following up with a Roundhouse Kick dropping him as she makes the cover 1…2-Kick Out. Cavallari and Astro watch on upset, seeing their partner getting quite beaten up now calling for him to get over there. Knowing that quick tags are key Kaiser tags in Chuck again, a bit reluctantly but knowing that they can really isolate Baltimore in this one. Artemis lands a few stomps before exiting the ring as Love-man hops back in, pulling Baltimore up as Jeremy tries to beg him for some mercy and Chuck actually…listens. Kaiser and Chronic look on confused as Chuck helps Jeremy across the ring, shoving him into the corner after a side hug allowing Cavallari to make the tag and he jumps into the ring destroying Chuck with a clothesline as he offers the new legal man a hug. Kaiser has her hands on her head unable to believe what she just saw as Ryan doesn’t seem to mind how things turned around so long as they do so. Baltimore rolls out of the ring, beaten up getting a breather as Astro seems to shrug him off annoyed by his bad start as Cavallari starts to stalk Chuck while he is down trying to do the same thing to Chuck now cornering him off as he pulls him up to his feet into a Forearm Smash before tossing him into a vacant corner. RILEY STEVENS: “Cavallari now taking it to Chuck…Who let Baltimore tag out?” Everyone seems to having a laugh at that moment still but it’s no laughing matter anymore as Cavallari runs the ring delivering a brutal running dropkick to Love-man in the corner as he throws him out of the corner getting onto the ropes and then jumping off with a follow up Diving Elbow Drop to Love-man knocking the wind out of him now as he makes the cover 1…Kick Out. Still not enough as Cavallari gets up looking over at Artemis with a grin, trying to send a message as he walks over tossing Chuck into the corner where he tags in Astro who jumps over the ropes bounces off the other side all while Ryan holds Chuck before giving him a push into a Jumping Clothesline! The fans aren’t happy clearly rooting for Team Mo Love and Kaiser as Love-man rolls over and Astro pulls him up, giving him a punch then another while holding the back of his head finally grabbing him and hoisting him up into a Northern Lights Suplex with a pin 1…2-Chronic jumps in breaking up the pin attempt as the referee instructs him to get back but Ryan deciding to take matters into his own hand knowing they have Chuck reeling runs across the ring, tagging Chronic with a clothesline over the top of the ropes sliding out with him as Chuck gets up stumbling back to the ropes where Artemis spins around the post to make a tag just before Astro clotheslines Chuck out of the ring as well. Baltimore runs around the ring to even up the score as Kaiser lays low walking around to the other side to slip in unnoticed as Astro looks to be prepping something big as the fans get up to their feet. RILEY STEVENS: “Astro about to be a show stealer!” Just like that Astro charges across the ring jumping up onto the ropes then flipping forward off of them while Chuck. Mo, Baltimore, and Cavallari all wait below as Astro comes down on all four of them causing a mass takedown as these fans can’t help but pop a bit for that as Kaiser slips into the ring now, knowing that the referee saw her make the tag. She slips out of the ring now, grabbing Astro who is the first up but seems to have hit his head none the less as she pushes him back into the ring getting him as far in as she can as she slides back in, getting the crowd into it a bit as on the outside her tag partners recover as she hits the ropes as Astro starts to get to his feet nailing him with the ‘Execution Kick’ a Axe Kick to a lowered opponent dropping him to the mat as she makes another cover 1…Kick Out. Still not enough to put Astro away as he rolls over to his feet the back of his neck hurting even more now after that kick as Kaiser tries to catch him off guard with a roll up 1…Astro now rolls up on top of her 1…Artemis gives his butt a kick pushing him off as he jumps forward landing on the ropes then bouncing off with a backwards elbow all in one fluid motion showing off the athleticism as Kaiser hops back to her feet though in a hurry, but she runs right into a Superkick that jars her back but surprisingly not off her feet as she stumbles back then steps forward nailing Astro with a ‘Falcon Kick’ a Yakuza Kick out of no where but it seems the shot from the Superkick was just delayed as she falls to the mat from her own kick as both start to crawl towards their corners now needing a tag. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Kaiser is an elf, she has a lot farther to crawl!” Despite Oliver’s short jokes the two seem neck and neck now as they crawl and Astro jumps out looking for a tag back to Cavallari who has returned to the side of the ring but Baltimore gets up intercepting it and storming the ring as Kaiser makes the tag into Mo before rolling out of the ring as Baltimore charges across the ring diving at Chronic who dodges then makes the tag to Chuck as he steps in and the two drill Baltimore with a Dropkick and Chop Block combination, bringing Baltimore to the mat as the two go for a fist bump then turns into a slap of Jeremy as they finish the combo off with a Calf Kick and Leg sweep combination as finally Astro has had enough, jumping into the ring and tackling Chronic over the ropes both of them flying to the outside as Chuck makes the tag to a recovered Artemis before charging at Cavallari looking for revenge on him but Cavallari pushes backwards off the ropes landing on his feet as Chuck jumps through the ropes but by the time he hits the mat, Ryan catches him in the stomach doubling him over with ‘A Heartless Moment’ a Snap DDT but at the exact same moment, in the ring Kaiser gets the downed Baltimore and moves him into a ‘DMD’ (Die, Motherfucker, Die) a Package Piledriver as she nails it putting him down just as Astro and Cavallari get to their feet trying to crawl back in but they are too late, 1…2…3! REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here are your winners via pinfall…Artemis Kaiser, Chuck Love-man, and Mo Chronic!” Kaiser pushes herself out of the ring just as Astro and Cavallari get back into the ring as she collects Chuck then walks around with him to get Chronic as well both down, as she grins in at Cavallari who glares down at Baltimore knowing he was the weak link tonight as he just seems to mouth that it won’t happen next time as Kaiser with Chuck and Chronic back up the ramp. RILEY STEVENS: “Kaiser and Team Mo Love get the victory here tonight after Kaiser puts down Baltimore.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “They found the weak link and capitalized big deal, still a bunch of weirdo’s.” Chuck and Chronic seem to be as oblivious as usual as Cavallari just watches them go while Astro angrily kicks Baltimore out of the ring getting rid of him as we head to the back for the next segment. WINNERS: ARTEMIS KAISER, CHUCK LOVE-MAN, & MO CHRONIC We move backstage to the GIW interview lounge where Zachary Pagonis is standing with Michael Lynch. ZACHARY PAGONIS: “Ladies and gentlemen, my guest at this time is the GIW Deep South Champion, Michael Lynch.” Before the interview can begin, Christopher Darren Erns appears opposite Michael Lynch. MICHAEL LYNCH: “What do you want?” Erns shrugs. CHRISTOPHER DARREN ERNS: “I just wanted to see if you were ready for our match at Moment of Glory, Michael.” Lynch seems confused. MICHAEL LYNCH: “Our match?” Erns nods. Smiling. CHRISTOPHER DARREN ERNS: “I got it approved earlier this week. You and I are going to face off, one on one in a hardcore match for the belt, if you can keep it, that is.” Lynch grips his belt a little bit tighter. MICHAEL LYNCH: “Are you sure you want to do that, Christopher? I took you out once, I’ll take you out again.” Erns chuckles. CHRISTOPHER DARREN ERNS: “We’ll see about that. But watch your back Lynch.” He pats Lynch on the chest and exits as we fade in on Lynch, and then cut elsewhere. ==================================== Alioth Starre is seen backstage pacing back and forth. One of GIW’s interviewers comes up to Starre and asks a question. ZACHARY PAGONIS: “Alioth… after having learned about the Walker and Starre match at Moment of Glory, a lot of the fans can’t help but wonder… once you defeat Wake Walker, what’s next for you?” Starre looks down for a few seconds and then fixes his eyes on the interviewer. Starre says nothing for a few seconds and then speaks after having thought very carefully about what he was going to say. ALIOTH STARRE: “I guess you and the audience will find out in a bit.” Starre walks out. Within seconds, Starre’s theme music plays which causes a mixed reaction from the crowd. However, there is no doubt that Starre himself is very easily able to generate a reaction from the crowd. Starre makes his way to the ring, with microphone in hand, and begins to speak. ALIOTH STARRE: “From what I’ve managed to gather, it appears some of you have begun wondering why I’ve returned. What ulterior motives could I possibly have for returning? Well, regardless of what my reasons are for coming back, it’s no secret that I finally am going to get the match I wanted against the critically acclaimed: Wake Walker. Unfortunately, it looks like Wake Walker couldn’t seem to be able to handle the pace of GIW and finds himself with a little problem in his heart. Needless to say, after I defeat Wake Walker, what’s next for Alioth Starre is that I’m going to…” Suddenly, a bright white light appears, blinding Starre temporarily. Wake Walker then comes out from behind the curtain, also to a very similar reaction to Starre. With no music playing, Walker makes his way down the ramp and enters the ring with his own microphone in hand. Walker speaks. WAKE WALKER: “Don’t fool yourself, Starre. Despite all the little poems you like to ramble on with, you don’t seem to have a creative and original thought in your entire brain. Do you really think we were just going to do a typical singles match… at the biggest event GIW has to offer with the best performers GIW has ever seen? We have to spice this up just a bit, Starre, because let’s face it: This match isn’t your typical match. It’s about determining who the greatest wrestler in GIW of all time is. That’s a lot to have on the line, and I feel it’s only appropriate that we add another stipulation into the mix.” ALIOTH STARRE: “…Well, what is it?” WAKE WALKER: “Ah, that’s where it gets interesting. Here’s the thing, Starre. I’ll let you decide what type of match it’ll be … as long as I get to add a stipulation of my own.” ALIOTH STARRE: “And what is this stipulation you plan on adding?” WAKE WALKER: “It’s a surprise. Once you pick the match-type, I will reveal the new stipulation to you. ALIOTH STARRE: “So I get to decide how I’m going to defeat you, eh? Well…I could always pick a steel cage match and tear you to pieces by using the cold, hard steel… or I could always pick a ladder match and send you flying from 30 feet in the air…but no, there’s no satisfaction in that. You’re right, Walker, this match IS about determining who the best of all time is… so that’s why I’m going to go ahead and pick the only match-type that would really decisively determine who the best of all time is…” WAKE WALKER: “Well…what is it? A no disqualification match? A street fight match? What’s it gonna be, Starre?” ALIOTH STARRE: :No… our match… is going to be a thirty minute iron man match.” The entire audience goes into an uproar of cheers and they all stand, being completely shocked that Starre would even pick an iron man match.
