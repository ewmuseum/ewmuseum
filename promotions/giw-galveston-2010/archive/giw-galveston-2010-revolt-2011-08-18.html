GIW — Card

Revolt, 18 August 2011

18 August 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: THURSDAY NIGHT REVOLT LIVE! From Mario’s Bar and Grill – August 18, 2011 BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! The fireworks stream from the top of Mario’s Bar and Grill into the night sky of Galveston, Texas as the fans continue to cram into the plaza around the ring. The ring is completely new - the old rag-tag thing that used to be the focal point of the night is gone. This new ring comes complete with the GIW logo on the white apron, the new Revolt! Blue ring ropes, and the banners on the side of the ring feature the Revolt! Logo and our new sponsor, ASICS Brand Shoes has their logo right beside it. We cut to Oliver Thibodaux and Riley Stevens in the announce booth, ready to start the show. RILEY STEVENS: “Ladies and gentlemen, we are one week removed from the biggest show of the year, Moment of Glory II… and I believe it lived up to every ounce of hype that you fans built it up with. Every match there was top quality.. Alex Castellanos lost the North Beach title to Trent Lost, Matt Kluge defeated the Shadow in a grudge match, Damien Dunn and his posse had a triumphant return to GIW, Mark Cross seized the moment, and Alioth Starre left Moment of Glory II with the biggest prize in the company: the GIW Undisputed Championship.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I can’t think of to many flaws in the show Riley - it was just amazing from top to bottom. Even people that I don’t particularly care for won their matches at Moment of Glory II, and all I can do is congratulate them.” RILEY STEVENS: “Let’s not forget about tonight though, Oliver. We get to see the former number one contender, Aspen Chaud, take on the current number one contender, Mark Cross in a singles match that could be considered a dream bout for longtime GIW fans.” Suddenly, “Lift Me Up” by Moby hit’s the PA System and Mark Cross makes his way down to the ring, carrying the Seize the Moment briefcase. He slides into the ring and grabs a microphone from Jamal Jackson and begins to address the crowd. MARK CROSS: “In case you didn’t know by now, I won the Seize the Moment match at Moment of Glory II against six men and women who are more than capable of being a champion. But after all this time, all this effort, I am finally in a position where I can become the GIW Undisputed Champion - a position to fight Alioth Starre.” The mere mentioning of Starre’s name triggers impulse boo’s from some fans, but huge cheers from others. It is truly a split crowd tonight. Cross chuckles to himself when he hears the reaction, then continues on with his speech. MARK CROSS: “Now, I have said it time and time again that winning a title means about as much as to me as having a long term General Manager means to Blake Beckett - I really could care less. But, the one guy left in this company that I want a shot at is Alioth Starre, and I am not going to get at him any other way than cashing in this case.” The crowd cheers wildly at the proposition of Mark Cross versus Alioth Starre. They start a “cash it in” chant, but Cross just shakes his head. MARK CROSS: “I won’t cash it in now, that’s to cliché. I wont cash it in when Alioth is fresh off a match, that’s how I operate. I will be cashing this briefcase in at Blackout II against Alioth Starre, and I don’t intend to waste that chance.” “Falling Away” by Curse Icon begins to play, and out walks Trent Lost with his new GIW North Beach Championship around his waist and Katya Rose on his arm. The fans nearly boo him out of the arena, but he climbs the ropes and holds the ropes for Katya as they both enter the ring to face Mark Cross, who doesn’t seem excited. TRENT LOST: “Well Marky, it seems as if we both had a pretty good night at Moment of Glory II, wouldn’t you say so? I mean, you won that Seize the Moment Case, and I knocked Alex Castellanos out and won the North Beach Championship. Now I could go on and on about that match forever, but it wouldn’t do any of us any good. I’m out here to congratulate you on your victory, not to gloat about mine.” Lost extends a hand to Cross, who shakes it reluctantly. Lost nods his head and throws Cross’s arm up into the air and leads and clap, the crowd has mixed reactions. MARK CROSS: “I’m flattered Trent, but I know you just as well as anyone else. I saw how you won your match at Moment of Glory, and let me tell you it isn’t anything to be proud of. You took advantage of Alex after Aspen interfered, you know that. So don’t come out here and interrupt my mic time when I earned it, and you didn’t.” Trent laughs at Mark’s comment, as does Katya. He licks his lips and waits for the fans to quiet down before he speaks again. TRENT LOST: “You know, you’re right Marky. Let’s cut the crap and get straight to my real reason to come out here: I’m better than you, and I demand a shot for your briefcase next week on Revolt! I am the North Beach Champion, and if I was in the Seize the Moment Match… I would be carrying that case as well. You know it.” Trent moves back for a moment, but Cross never breaks eye contact. The fans boo Lost, but cheer a potential match between the two men. MARK CROSS: “If that’s how you want it, you’re on.” As soon as he said those words, “Bang Bang” by Nancy Sinatra hit’s the PA System, and out walks Alioth’s manager, Mark Turner. Cross and Lost turn to him. MARK TURNER: “Trent Lost, there is no way you are wriggling your way into this match. Alioth beat you for this title, let’s give Mark a chance, alright?” Trent suddenly becomes furious, lunging at Turner from behind the ring ropes. Katya is screaming at him, Cross just waits. MARK TURNER: “If you two want to have a match next week, have at it, but you will not be getting a title shot any time soon, Trent Lost.” “Bang Bang” hit’s the PA System yet again as we see Trent still fuming, then we cut to a commercial break. QUOTE After the break, despite having just been in the ring out comes Alioth as the tremolod pick of the strings beckoned forth the first competitor of the night. A man in a brown sports coat and jeans stepped first onto the aisle. He took in a deep breath and his eyes scanned the crowd, then they turned toward the entry way. Like a ghost, Alioth Starre slowly lurked forth behind this man, named Mark Turner as he and Starre were announced to the audience in attendance and viewers at home. RILEY STEVENS: “Here comes the Undisputed Champion who will take on a cocky young upstart with this new manager…at his side.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “What…all the sudden he’s a mute?” As slow as the music, Alioth moved behind the man that lead him to the ring. He touched Alioth’s shoulder and pointed toward the corner where Alioth then climbed into the ring. His manager remained outside, but Alioth stood in place at the corner of the ring. Mark Turner guided him from outside to the opposite end just as Alioth’s opponent’s music hit the arena. RILEY STEVENS: “Here comes Nick Chaos who lost to Seth Seeker at ‘Moment of Glory II’ for the Television title. With a huge chance here against Starre.” Nick Chaos seemed worried as he entered the arena. Perhaps he heard the stories of newcomers being fed to Galveston Island Wrestling’s current and reigning Undisputed Champion. Which ever the case, he made his way toward the ring and entered, but his eyes kept on Alioth and this new guy. He knew the kind of foolish tactics managers pulled to ensure their stars won, so he wanted to be extra cautious and focused on this man, but would this new focus interrupt with the real problem in the ring – Alioth? DING DING DING! The bell rang, but Alioth remained where Mark had moved him in the ring. Eyes dull and arms at his sides, it was like a coy invitation for Nick Chaos to take the first hit. He did! Chaos opened the bout with three hard punches to Alioth’s bared face, which only served to send his opponent against the ropes, but that stone-like demeanor ended the moment Alioth was propelled back by the ropes. The champion replied Chaos’ hit with one of his own, which knocked his opponent to the canvas. Alioth looked down at his feet like someone had moved something in front of a dumb creature’s face, but he moved down to pull his opponent up and irish whip him across the ring. Nick Chaos hoped Alioth would bend over and motioned to kick, but when this didn’t happen, he wasn’t sure what to do but to collide into Alioth’s heart punch. Chaos held his chest from the impact on his pressure point and Alioth followed up the physical strike with a technical maneuver. He stretched and pulled his opponent like an octopus until one arm was held back and his leg pulled over the others. Trapped in the champion’s abdominal stretch, Chaos tried not to quit so early in the game. He was only lucky enough that the champion had barely moved from the ropes and this allowed Nick Chaos to grasp them with little effort. RILEY STEVENS: “You know sometimes…people just don’t have a chance.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Since when did local talent get on the show?” Just as Nick Chaos was freed, Alioth grasped around the boy’s forehead and knelt himself down. He pulled Nick Chaos back until spine connected with knee and Chaos’ back knew further, excruciating pain. With the back tender, and his new maneuver known as ‘The Nervous System’s Down’ connected, Alioth prepared his opponent for the figure-four leglock, aptly named the ‘Four Star Mary.’ Why? No one knew, but it seemed enough to force Nick Chaos to tap quicker than corner girl to a two-dollar offer. JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…‘The GIW Undisputed Champion’ ALIOTH STARRE!” RILEY STEVENS: “Well…Starre proves why he is GIW’s very best.” Mark pulled his wrestler from the other and dragged him under the bottom rope to the outside. He threw an arm over Alioth’s shoulder and guided him to the back. Chaos was left with his back in pulsing pain and nerves shot, as this dominating performance led us to commercial. WINNER: ALIOTH STARRE We immediately cut backstage to Joshua Acquin’s General Manager office, there is a knock on the door. Joshua instructs the person to come in, it is Matt Kluge, one half of the GIW Tag Team Champions. Acquin instructs Kluge to take a seat. JOSHUA ACQUIN: “Kluge, we fired Leo James last night.” Kluge sits in silence for a moment, then leans back in his chair. Joshua chuckles, then move on with his little meeting. JOSHUA ACQUIN: “Kluge, if you don’t find a partner by the end of the night, then I will find one for you. Understood then?” Kluge shakes his head. MATT KLUGE: “No Josh. I’m not teaming with anyone else. I’ll carry both belts myself.” JOSHUA ACQUIN: “I’m afraid that’s not going to happen Kluge. You need to have a partner.” Kluge stands from his chair, nods at Acquin, walks to the door, then turns back around. MATT KLUGE: “The go ahead and pick one then.” He slams the door on Josh as we cut to commercial break. QUOTE John Matthews. The self proclaimed Satanist passes through the entry way, his hand steadying against the rails as he moves toward the ring. His eyes dazed and the fans unimpressed with the alcohol on his breath. Halfway toward the ring, Matthews collapsed into floor mat and then came Jack Waters. RILEY STEVENS: “Well…there is John Matthews…” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “…Yup.” “Libera Me From Hell” by Tarantula played him to the ring. The man known as Jack Waters completely voided the drunken and passed out body of John Matthews and entered the ring with focus etched across his face. He stretched against the ropes and watched the aisle for his opponent, or if the other might actually show up. RILEY STEVENS: “This man had a great showing at ‘Moment of Glory II’ and will look to build off that momentum.” A.J. Fairchild was last to enter the GIW arena. He looked to the body of Matthews that began to pull itself up, but on his way toward the ring, Fairchild gave the drunkard a quick stop to push him back down where he belonged. Fairchild slid into the ring and watched his opponent in wait until the bell signaled them to compete. RILEY STEVENS: “Then there’s this guy…” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “We still hire his kind?” DING DING DING! The match began with a lockup, but Jack Waters quickly gained the upper hand with an uppercut that knocked Fairchild reeling and hand over face. Waters elbowed his opponent over the top of his back and then threw him to the canvas. With a rush of speed, he raced to the ropes and used them as a means to bounce up and perform a Moonsault upon his helpless opponent. He pinned! One! Two! Three! JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…‘The King of Darkness’ JACK WATERS!” RILEY STEVENS: “You know what you get when you put two bums in a match with real talent…” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “We just saw it.” It seemed that Fairchild had little to no chance to compete in the bout, or he had no plans to fight for his own sake. Jack looked to the crowd around him with his arms out with annoyance. He shrugged and looked down as Fairchild began to climb up by grasping onto Waters’ ankles, knees, and then a first to the face! Waters knocked his defeated opponent back to the canvas, then looked to his left to see a drunken swing toward him, but a swift maneuver to dodge it saved him from what would have been a shameful tap from the unabashedly shamed. He leapt into action to sting his attacker’s chin with a super kick that sent Matthews flying backward and tumbling through the second and first level ropes, and straight out of the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “You know Matthews may be one of the worst we’ve ever had.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Easily.” Jack Waters raised his own hand, then the referee aided him in this effort. As he came, he went and left the mess to clean itself up within the ring’s interior and exterior. He wasn’t the janitor, and they weren’t what he’d consider competitors with the little effort put forth. Eventually, however, Fairchild pulled himself out and Matthews had been swept like yesterday’s garbage from the arena to ready it for whatever Galveston Island Wrestling’s Thursday Night Revolt had in store for both the wrestlers and crowd that came to see the competitions that took place. WINNER: JACK WATERS Backstage, Alioth Starre is sitting in a chair, staring off into space, as Mark Turner unwraps his wrists in tape. MARK TURNER: “You mental state may be strange Alioth, but you were on top of your game, weren’t you? It sure looked it.” No response from the catatonic Alioth Starre. MARK TURNER: “I really wish you would talk again… when you talk you say such interesting things. I miss that. I know you can hear me though, I trust that you can.” From the right side of the camera, Damien Dunn enters the shot with a huge grin on his face. Turner looks up to him and rises. DAMIEN DUNN: “That’s a screwed up relationship. I always knew Alioth was off his rocker.” Damien waves a hand in Alioth’s face, no response of course. DAMIEN DUNN: “Poor kid. I saw what he did out there against Chaos - I won’t lie, I’m impressed. I just got out of Blake’s office; he told me that the person that picks up the pinfall in the six man tag match coming up next, will be the Television Champ. I have no doubts I will be that champ, and, seeing as Alioth is a champ as well, how about next week… him and I face off in a singles match?” Mark’s eyes get wide. He clenches his jaw. MARK TURNER: “Absolutely not.” Damien tilts his head. DAMIEN DUNN: “You may not like it, but I guarantee he would want a shot at me again.” Damien walks down the hallway slowly. We pan in on Turner as we fade to commercial break. QUOTE As we return for the next match it’s “Paper Planes" by This Century that sends the fans into some cheers as out from the back comes the team of Trayton Deluca, Crysis, and the ‘GIW Television Champion’ Seth Seeker. The fans are cheering them on as Deluca high fives some fans and heads up the steps and into the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “You know sometimes we get thrown curveballs like this one. Seth Seeker the current Television Champion will defend his belt in a six man tag match…where only the person that gets the pin will win and get the belt.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Meaning teams are not really teams…” Three head to their corner as “Cult of Personality” by Living Colour hits as the fans begin to boo as Damien Dunn walks out from the back commanding Johnny Stylez and Kevin Mero as they follow behind him like two willing servants as they take in the boos. RILEY STEVENS: “Who will leave with the belt?” As the three head up the steps Stylez holds the ropes for Dunn who heads to the outside pointing at Mero and telling him to start the match as Deluca does for the other team as we start with the bell. DING DING DING! The bell calls for the start and Deluca and Mero begin to circle around as Kevin rolls carelessly at Deluca right into a big kick to the gut followed right up by a DDT! The fans cheer as Deluca goes for the pin 1…Kick Out! Both get up as Trayton throws him right into the corner. Rushing at him he nails him with a big kick to the face, as he waits for Mero to stumble forward following it up with a big Running One Handed Bulldog! The fans are behind Trayton who may not have a great record but always puts on a show for the fans. Trayton takes a second to start a clap from the crowd as Dunn screams at Mero to get to his feet and fight back. Kevin tries to do just that but Trayton whips him off the ropes and hoists him up into a Sidewalk Slam! Going for the pin; Dunn pushes Stylez into the ring as he rushes across…1…2…Stylez breaks it up. He begins to wildly throw stomps at Trayton to try and take him out. But Trayton gets up fighting back as there is no rush from his partners to help him, the referee losing control as Dunn hops into the ring having to do things himself. As Deluca goes after both men, he clubs him from behind sending him stumbling into the more then willing to tag in Crysis as the fans boo the actions of Dunn as Crysis steps in as Mero gets to his feet being pulled to his corner by Dunn. RILEY STEVENS: “Damien Dunn is definitely directing traffic for his team.” By the time Crysis gets in, it is too late to stop Dunn from tagging himself in as Crysis comes across the ring crushing Mero just after he tags out. But Damien throws a big elbow over the ropes into Crysis then goes Springboard with a great looking Dropkick that even gets some respect from the crowd. Getting up he looks over at Stylez and screams “That’s how you do it!” turning back around and calling for Crysis to get to his feet as he wraps him up and hits the T-Bone Suplex, right into a pin 1…2…Dunn instead pulls off himself getting up and looking over at Seeker pointing at him with a grin on his face as Seth seems more then willing to get in as Damien lifts up Crysis and like a piece of trash throws him into their corner as Seeker tags in. RILEY STEVENS: “Did Dunn just willingly let the champion into the match?” Even the fans seem to be confused by his tactics as Seeker steps into the ring like a freight train going after Damien only for him to throw up a big elbow into his face. The crowd boos clearly behind the champion as Dunn throws him into the corner and rushes at him throwing a big knee to the face and then grabbing hold of his head and running forward with a big Running Bulldog! All of the sudden rushing into the ring is Crysis swinging at Dunn but he ducks under it. In comes Mero and Stylez to help out their boss, rushing at Crysis and clotheslining him over the ropes. But following that up is Deluca rushing at both men jumping into the air and clotheslining both over the rope however going with them! This leaves just Dunn and Seeker as, Seth gets up only to be kicked in the gut and set up right into the ‘Complete Devastation” his version of the Pedigree as he makes the cover with nobody to stop him 1…2…3! It is OVER! JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…and the NEW GIW Television Champion…DAMIEN DUNN!” The crowd boos as Dunn gets to his feet demanding the belt as he gets it from the referee holding it over his head, standing over Seth Seeker. Trayton gets up on the outside holding his hands on his head he didn’t lose this match…Instead was just at the wrong place at the wrong time. Returning to the ring in bad shape is Mero and Stylez. RILEY STEVENS: “Well there we have it Damien Dunn is the champion.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Deserving to.” Dunn continues to taunt with the belt as from the back comes two men heading into the ring with Damien as Stylez and Mero congratulate him on his win as Nick Starks and Sean Galen who were here on Full Throttle earlier look at both Mero and Stylez as Dunn smirks back at them and then mouths “You two…are out.” Stylez and Mero seem confused as they get slammed down from behind. Both turn around to fight back but are quickly disposed of out of the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Not only do we have a new Television Champion it looks like Dunn has upgraded his team known as Syndication as well.” Galen and Starks raise Dunn’s hand as he holds the belt high as this threesome seems to now have some pull here in GIW. WINNER: DAMIEN DUNN (New GIW Television Champion) In the GIW Interview Lounge, Zachary Pagonis is with Alex Castellanos. ZACHARY PAGONIS: “Ladies and gents, please welcome Alex Castellanos to the lounge for tonight. So, Alex, the question on everyone’s mind is… what do you have to say to Aspen?” Alex nods, then takes the microphone from Zachary. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “Aspen just needs to learn when to stay out of my way, and keep to herself. She obviously wasn’t able to do that… what do you want?” Our attention is turned to the left side of the shot as Sean Galen and Nick Starks appear inside the lounge. They are winded from decimating Stylez and Mero moments before, but still manage to have smiles on their faces. NICK STARKS: “Well, well, well, if it isn’t the former North Beach Champion.” SEAN GALEN: “People say this guy is the future of GIW…” NICK STARKS: “Ay, Alex. We don’t have a match next week.” Pagonis wisely leaves, Alex turns towards the men. ALEX CASTELLANOS: “I don’t even know who you punks are, but what you did to Mero and Stylez isn’t going to help your rep around here. I suggest you head on out.” SEAN GALEN: “We want to make a name for ourselves, that’s all, and you are in the right place at the wrong time…” Galen throws a punch at Alex, but he ducks it and pushes him back. Galen is furious and charges him again, this time he grabs Alex’s arms and lets Starks land a few punches. Out of nowhere comes Aspen Chaud with a steel chair, she plants one square on Nick Stark’s back, sending the duo reeling off set. ASPEN CHAUD: “Get the hell out of here!” We cut to commercial with Aspen helping Alex up and staring down Galen and Starks at the end of the hallway. QUOTE First to the arena was the peculiar team of Matt Kluge and Alex Castellanos. First of the two, however, was Alex Castellanos. He carried himself to the ring and stopped at the center just as “Battle Metal” by Turisas took over his own theme. Matt Kluge then entered with his own GIW World Tag Team title over his shoulder. RILEY STEVENS: “You know a week ago this team would have been quite odd. With Alex’s turn back to his old self you’d have to think that they could work together better. But…still.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “They won’t win.” The crowd cheered the man that headed down the aisle. Castellanos pushed to the back of the ring as Kluge entered and the two shared a glance, but as tag partners, there would be no room for tension between the two. Differences set aside, and egos focused upon their opponents, Kluge and Castellanos prepped in the ring as their opponents entered the arena. RILEY STEVENS: “You know this is basically two ‘Moment of Glory II’ rivalries coming to ahead again a second chance if you will for both The Shadow and Alex Castellanos.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Oh yeah, Alex can’t exactly get his belt back.” The first to enter from the opposing team was The Shadow. He pulled himself slowly to the ring and soon entered, but kept back against the ropes until his opponent arrived. His opponent, however, had been late to the show. The Shadow and his opponents, as well as the crowds began to wonder if Trent Lost had gotten lost backstage, or if he would not even show up. After a few moments, Lost seemed absent from the entry way, but Kluge had spotted a familiar sight in the crowd behind them. Lost had failed to spring a surprise attack, but he still charged forth and slid into the ring, right between Matt Kluge and Alex Castellanos. RILEY STEVENS: “Here we go!” Before the bell could even ring, Kluge pulled Lost up after Alex had stomped his opponent’s skull into the canvas and then held him tight and slammed Lost back into the canvas with a side-belly to belly suplex. The Shadow lurched forth with vigor, but Alex took to him as Kluge had taken Lost under his fists. The two met at the center of the GIW ring and Alex fired first with a jab and a hook, all of it lead to a standing dropkick as Shadow stood dazed. This pre-emptive start prompted Kluge to take to the ropes and drag Lost with him, like a giant squid pulls its prey in to that massive crushing beak. The bell rang and Castellanos pinned Shadow, but it was in vain. RILEY STEVENS: “This thing got wild before the bell, as expected.” Shadow pushed himself to his feet and dodged the first array of attacks from Castellanos. He threw his elbow into his opponent’s jaw, but Alex fought back harder; swifter. It took Alex a bit more power to than he would normally muster on an opponent like Lost in order to pull Shadow up over his body, but when he did, he managed to successfully knock down his opponent with a scoop Powerslam pin, but even that pin was in not completed. Shadow seemed to refuse defeat, even if his only partner had already been bruised and battered outside of the ring. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Come on Shadow, lets go!” Trent Lost had barely managed to escape the clutch of Matt Kluge. A German Suplex to the floor mats and Lost’s head against the rim of the ring and the man was seeing stars. He, however, was not among those stars. He grasped at the bottom rope to help himself up; even extending his hand out like a flaccid fish, but his partner was in his own kind of hell brought upon by Alex Castellanos. RILEY STEVENS: “This is all out war.” Alex had worked on the arm of The Shadow until it was near limp and bruised. An almost carbon copy of Trent Lost’s face it was. With it raised and twisted so that he had full control of that arm, Castellanos pulled The Shadow toward the corner post and tagged his partner. Before passing the ropes, however, he ensured to leave the ring with another hard right to Shadow’s chest for good measure. RILEY STEVENS: “It seems that Matt Kluge is keeping the spirit of this intriguing tag team taking full control of the match. They started with the ball high and are showing no signs of dropping it.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Oh, this is crazy, man! He looks like a dead hooker on the ropes!” Kluge pinned, but Shadow had once again escaped defeat. Shadow fought back only enough to pull himself away from Kluge. Alex held out his hand and Trent Lost had finally been tagged in. It appeared as if Kluge had no intentions of tagging in his partner, but instead, he wanted to destroy Lost some more on his own. RILEY STEVENS: “Finally Trent gets in.” Lost, feeling as refreshed as he could after the pre-match beat down took a swing at Kluge, but the fist had clearly missed. Kluge used the momentum to set his opponent up for the Northern Lights Suplex and then a pin, but was interrupted by Alex Castellanos. Kluge rushed up and the two were nearly inches from one another’s faces. Glares sweat, and tension culminated in their heads turning focus over toward Trent Lost. Kluge knocked Lost another tooth out, which forced the groggy opponent to stagger back in just the right amount of distance for The Golden Arrow to strike Lost hard. With the hit connecting, Castellanos retreated from the ring at the order of the referee and Matt Kluge bent the battered Trent Lost’s legs back and pulled his chin back, bringing his opponent into ‘The Rat Trap’. Lost had enough. The Shadow had been too beaten to make a save, and with Castellanos ready to ensure no interruptions but his own were made, Lost had no choice but to tap. JAMAL JACKSON: “Here are your winners…ALEX CASTELLANOS and MATT KLUGE!” Kluge let the hold cling to the boy’s body for just a second longer before releasing him. Victorious, he fled the ring and rolled out of the ring, but Castellanos wasn’t finished. No, it would be Castellanos to pull the beaten and nearly indiscernible body of Trent Lost back on its wobbly feet to perform the Golden Arrow again, but this time the super kick landed square in Lost’s groin. RILEY STEVENS: “Sometimes you have to neuter dogs like Trent Lost!” The crowd goes wild for Alex as Trent falls down to the mat in pain rolling around to the outside as Kluge watches chuckling to himself. Out of no where however Sean Galen and Nick Starks slide into the ring both of them collapsing on Alex with shots from both sides…The fans boo as the two attack them and encouraged by the crowd Kluge returns to even the score but the two men slide out of the ring as soon as the odds are evened up. RILEY STEVENS: “You know; never thought I’d see Matt Kluge save Alex Castellanos. But it looks as though Sean Galen and Nick Starks have made some waves here tonight…Choosing Alex as a target.” The two men head backwards up the ramp both with big grins along their faces as they show off to the men in the ring as Kluge helps up Alex seeming hesitant just to do that as we take a little break. WINNERS: ALEX CASTELLANOS & MATT KLUGE Inside Blake Beckett’s office Christian Rivers, Trayton DeLuca, AJ Fairchild, Jack Waters, Curtis Durrington, Jak Hendricks, John Rawlings, and Katya Rose are all competing for a seat. Beckett looks upon them with a smile on his face from behind the desk. BLAKE BECKETT: “I called you all in here because we need a new number one contender for the Television Championship. You all have proven that you are committed to GIW, and I think you all have championship caliber talent. So, I was faced with a dilemma, but I think I now have a solution. I am going to have an eight man tournament with you folks that will culminate at Blackout II with the match to determine the number one contender. Next week, on the two hour edition of Revolt, the first four matches of the night will be tournament matches, featuring you. So come to work prepared next week, because this could be the big break you all need.” The scene fades to commercial. QUOTE We have now arrived at the Main Event of the evening as the fans seem ready to go. First up we have the sounds of "Now I'm That Bitch" by Livvi Franc hits as the fans begin to give a mixed reaction some still holding a grudge others seeming to have turned over a bit from her actions earlier in the show as she walks out looking rather serious and ready to go. RILEY STEVENS: “You know I will say I am pretty impressed with the way Aspen Chaud helped Alex Castellanos earlier…do you think she’s really changed?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Heck no, she’s a good teammate…that’s all.” She heads up the steps and into the ring seeming to willing take any of the fans boos and not trying to make a huge scene as “Lift Me Up” by Moby now hits as the fans go wild for the man that walks out with a big smile on his face Mark Cross the man who at ‘Blackout II’ will get his match with Alioth Starre. RILEY STEVENS: “You know Cross faces Aspen this week then Lost next week…two very good ways to get prepared for the match with Starre at ‘Blackout II’.” Cross heads up the steps and into the ring looking across at Aspen perhaps sensing a change in her as well as he nods his head and joins her in the ring as the referee separates the two until he calls for the bell. DING DING DING! There is an electric feel in the crowd as many people have been wanting to see this contest for a long time now. Cross starts the match by extending his hand, as the fans wait to see what Aspen will do. To their surprise she extends her hand and shakes his hand. This being perhaps the most surprising part of the night as the two go in for a lock up right off the bat Cross naturally getting the advantage however to our surprise Aspen rolls around him rolling him up, 1…2…Cross is able to pop up…Nodding his head at Aspen showing his respect for that little unexpected maneuver. Both of the two go back into it as they do another lock up this time it’s Mark that is able to roll behind her tugging back her arm however she does a little flip and spins her arm back and then arm drags him. The fans giving some respect as Cross gets up a little taken back early on Aspen grabs him trying to whip him but Cross reverses it and throws her off. She ducks under his clothesline attempt then going off the ropes as he jumps down onto the ground as she bounces over him off the ropes again and this time she runs right into a Fall Away Slam! The crowd cheers for Cross as he goes for a cover 1…Kick Out! RILEY STEVENS: “Some great early action here.” Mark gets up but pulls up Aspen with him spinning and rolling her into the corner. Rushing at her he runs right into her boots as she kicks up. Using this chance she goes to the top rope but Cross is too quick and rushes forward and jumps up to the top with her. She tries to push him away but he gets her set up right into a Superplex position! The crowd is going wild in expectation as he goes for it and both competitors hit the mat, their backs hitting hard! The fans go wild as they hit as Chaud rolls around holding her lower back as Cross holds onto his back which took most of the damage. The crowd gives both competitors a big standing ovation for their efforts with that move as both start to get up at the same time, the crowd clapping for them as they do seeming to enjoy this contest now that Aspen seems to have changed. She throws a big Forearm as he replies with a big punch that sends her stumbling back. Not willing to back down she throws another forearm but Cross answers back with a second punch and it’s clear in a striking match she has no chance. So suddenly rejuvenated she rushes at him and jumps up into a Thesz Press Followed by Punches! The crowd popping a bit for the move as Cross pushes her off and both hop up but she nails him right away with the ‘Queen's Wrath I’ a Double Knee Backbreaker! The fans seem split as he goes for the cover 1…2…Kick Out! RILEY STEVENS: “Cross will not go down that easy.” Aspen gets up nodding her head this face paced action getting her a little tired as she waits for Mark to get up before pushing him into the corner. Rushing at him she nails him with the ‘Tush Push’ a Turnbuckle Flying Butt Shot! As usual she follows this up by going right to the top rope…As Mark stumbles out from the corner. Jumping off for the ‘AirBarbie’ a Flying Crossbody! But when she hits his chest to the surprise of everyone he catches her in his hands! The fans go wild as he hoists her up onto his shoulders walking around with her before putting her down with the Go 2 Sleep, as the fans cheer him on as he follows her to the mat for the cover 1…2…KICK OUT! The fans pop as Cross gets to his feet seeming impressed just then Mark Turner along with the champion Alioth Starre walk out onto the stage. RILEY STEVENS: “Here comes the champion Alioth Starre perhaps to scout Mark Cross?” Cross takes some time to look up at him not trusting him. As he does Aspen grabs him by the ankle not knowing at all about the distraction but seeing the opportunity. She tries her best as Cross falls to the mat on his back smartly kicking her away. Both get up as Aspen swings her leg for the ‘Queen's Wrath II’ a Heel Kick but he ducks it and then right away lifts her up right into the ‘Ketteiteki Desaki’ a Tiger Driver '91! The fans cheer as Cross covers her 1…2…3! JAMAL JACKSON: “Here is your winner…MARK CROSS!” Cross gets up as the referee holds up his hand as Aspen rolls out of the way and out of the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “A great match but Cross proves why he is the rightful number one contender.” Cross stares up at Alioth, who looks right back at him. Both of them looked good tonight as they both size one another up as the show heads to the backstage surprisingly. WINNER: MARK CROSS After the main event match is over, we see AJ Fairchild and Trayton DeLuca talking outside of Joshua Acquin’s office. They seen nervous, but Trayton knocks on the door. Joshua opens it after a few moments pass, stares down at them… DeLuca manages to get a word out. TRAYTON DELUCA: “Uh… Mr. Acquin sir… Fairchild and I were talking; we want a match next week… the winner gets to be Kluge’s tag team partner. We can double it as our Television Tournament Match..” Joshua chuckles, he opens the door wider, we see The Shadow standing in the doorway, with the GIW Tag Team Title around his waist. JOSHUA ACQUIN: “I found someone already.” The show fades with the Shadow sadistically laughing, stroking the title belt.
