The referee orders Christopher into a corner as he checks on the Undisputed Champion for any symptoms of a concussion. Clearing Harlequin, the referee allows the match to continue and is nearly run over as Erns attempts bulrush Harlequin. The wise Undisputed Champion manages to roll out of the way as her challenger crashes into the turnbuckle. Harlequin leaps onto the back of Christopher and turns herself around for a hurricanrana that sends Erns across the ring. She follows this up with another fast strike in the form of a flying forearm that keeps Erns on the mat. Feeling a bit more confident about her position in the match, Harlequin hits a standing moonsault on the fallen body of Erns and goes for a pin 1… 2… KICK OUT! RILEY STEVENS: "Whoa! I can't believe it!" The challenger manages to hang on a bit longer as Harlequin rolls over to grab onto the legs of Erns and pull him in for a Boston crab. Struggle is seen on Christopher’s face for a brief moment before he begins to show off his strength again as he drags himself over to the bottom rope. Hanging onto that rope to catch his breath, Harlequin responds quickly with a double foot stomp right onto his back. An audible yelp is heard from Erns as was unprepared for such a move and grimaces in pain as Harlequin smiles brightly while in control of the match. Harlequin waits for Christopher to slowly regain stability as he uses the ropes to aid him. Seeing this as a chance for another attack, Harlequin charges at Erns, but he’s fast to pull down the top rope. While Harley did go over, she managed to land on the apron, and much to the surprise of Erns, hits him with a springboard leg lariat! Harlequin keeps things swinging as she hits a No Laughing Matter, a lionsault that takes the breath out of Erns. Well aware that won’t be enough to keep Erns down, Harlequin continues to work the body with another standing moonsault onto Christopher Darren Erns. Again, Harlequin stands back to let her challenger find his way back up to his feet and surprises everyone with her Clowning Around! The jumping implant DDT out of nowhere takes down the Deep South Champion and Harlequin goes for a cover 1… 2… THR-KICK OUT! RILEY STEVENS: "How is he still fighting?!" A loud sigh is heard from Harlequin as she shakes her head in disappointment. Erns is still very well into this match even if things aren’t going his way. Looking to change that, Harlequin makes the bold move of lifting her opponent up and it backfires on her as she’s tossed over due to a back body drop. Christopher manages to stand back on his feet, but it’s clear he’s still feeling the effects of the DDT and strikes to his ribs. Still, he’s determined to end Harlequin and steps over the fallen body of the Undisputed Champion. OLIVER THIBODAUX: "There we go, Christopher! That's what I like to see!" Lifting her up, Christopher surprises everyone and throws Harlequin into the air for the Uppercut from Hell, but instead, Harlequin just goes flying out of the ring! A “Holy Shit” chant breaks out as Harlequin hits the mat with a loud thud. Christopher lets out a laugh from inside the ring before eventually climbing out of the ring and meeting Harlequin’s fallen body. The Deep South Champion picks up The Harlequin and makes eye contact with Beck Ramsey. A sick smirk develops on Erns’ face as he sends Harlequin right into the ringside barrier and gets in Beck’s face. The two exchange words before Erns ends the conversation by spitting right in the face of Beck Ramsey! The crowd gasps as Erns just smirks and throws Harley back into the ring. With her body weakened, Erns goes for the pin! 1… 2… THR-KICK OUT! RILEY STEVENS: "That's our champion! You can't just disrespect our guests and get away with it!" The camera cuts to Beck who is just smirking as he wipes the residue left over on his face. Mouthing a few words is the current UWL World Champion. Cutting back to the ring, we see an angry Christopher Darren Erns snap at the referee and bring his officiating into question. Erns turns his head to see Harlequin trying to recover in the corner, but he’s not having any of it as he stomps over to her. For a moment, Christopher just looks down at his opponent before driving the side of his knee into her face repeatedly. After a few strikes, the referee orders Christopher back away from the corner and he does just that. But not entirely. He squats down and gets right in the face of The Harlequin. Blood begins to trickle down from her nose and Erns just laughs. “Is that all you’ve got, Mel?!” Those six simple words are enough to fuel Harlequin as she drives her elbow right into the face of her challenger and causes him to stumble back. The momentum starts to build for the Undisputed Champion as she continues to drive her elbow into Christopher’s face over and over again! Blood begins to form at the forehead of Erns after these strikes, but Harley doesn’t care about that. Out of nowhere, she leaps to the top rope and hits Joke’s On You! The 720 DDT from out of nowhere! She goes for the pin 1… 2… THRE-NO! KICK OUT! OLIVER THIBODAUX: "Look at that heart! The Great One is here to win it all!" The crowd can’t believe it! Harlequin can’t believe it! Christopher Darren Erns has kicked out. The Harlequin sighs in disbelief and drags herself back to her feet. Looking down at her opponent, she stands there in silence. Not really sure what to do next, Harlequin just tries for another pin! 1… 2… KICK OUT! RILEY STEVENS: "How is he kicking out?!" A shorter break occurs this time as Erns begins to regain his energy. Harlequin goes for a third pin, but finds herself being tossed by Erns. She quickly springs back up, runs towards Erns who is on his knees, and hits him with a low drop kick. Harlequin quickly hooks both of his legs for a cover but manages to get just a single count. Frustration is shown on the face of the Undisputed Champion. She decided to put it all on the line, walking out of the ring and onto the apron, she watches Christopher slowly make his way to his feet. At the right moment, Harley leaps in the air, springs off the ropes, and jumps almost as high enough to hit the rafters above! She goes for Joke's On You, but while in mid air The Deep South Champion gets back to his feet and hits the Undisputed Kick!!! The crowd is in shock as Erns managed to hit Harley out of nowhere! The Harlequin crumbles down to the mat. Christopher falls back, finding balance with the ropes on the other side of the ring. A bloody Erns is seen slapping his own face, trying to get himself back in this. His gaze looks ahead of him at The Harlequin who is slowly getting up trying to shake off the cobwebs of that vicious kick. She crawls on her knees to the center of the ring; dazed. It seems as though she is looking right at her husband in the audience who is trying to cheer her on. She hears his words, and finds the will within herself to slowly stand. But she doesn’t find peace as Christopher comes running out of nowhere to hit the Malice Acrimony! The running punt right to the weakened skull of The Harlequin puts her down as Erns literally falls on her for the pin! 1… 2… 3! RILEY STEVENS: "NO!!" OLIVER THIBODAUX: "YES!! HE DID IT!" The crowd falls silent as the Undisputed Champion has fallen. Christopher Darren Erns looks on in shock, even he can’t believe what has finally happened. The referee leaves the ring to grab the title belts, but Christopher remains laying on Harlequin, still in shock. The crowd is still silent, and the speakers remain mute as the referee returns to the ring with both the Undisputed and Deep South titles in his hands. Erns slowly raises his body up into a seated position. The referee offers him the titles, but CDE just looks at them. Possibly looking at his referee in the gold. Sinking it all in for what feels like ions, Christopher finally staggers his way to his feet, and slowly reaches out to accept the title belts. The referee takes Christopher's hand and raises it as a sign of victory. The shocked expression on Erns' face is still shown, but nonetheless he raises both the Deep South and Undisputed Championships over his head. "Miseria Cantare" begins to play, and now he seems like everything is back to reality. The fans in the audience break their silence and begin to boo, some cheer for both of these two competitors who waged in a brutal war. Erns limps his way to the turn buckle, he goes to lift himself up onto the second rope, but hesitates. He looks back at Harlequin he is still laid out on the mat. Christopher then looks back at everyone in the audience and lets out a smile. He climbs the turnbuckle and raises both championships in the air as we fade to the GIW logo. WINNER via PINFALL: NEW UNDISPUTED CHAMPION, CHRISTOPHER DARREN ERNS!
