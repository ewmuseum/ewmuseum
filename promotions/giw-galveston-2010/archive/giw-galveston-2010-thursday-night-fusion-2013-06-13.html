Thursday Night Fusion, 13 June 2013

GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: THURSDAY NIGHT FUSION LIVE! From North Beach – June 13, 2013 QUOTE In this week’s Open Battle Royal we open with some fresh faces in the ring as three new stars to GIW are in the ring; ready and set to go as we have Clayden Arkane, Jacob Jett, and Silas Romero in the ring ready to go. RILEY STEVENS: “Fresh face time as yet again; we have three interesting stars ready to go and though many stars have lost this match and gone onto greatness, the one that wins heads into the big show with a lot of momentum.” AMANDA ROWLAND: “Yay fun!” Jett was first to the ring for the battle royal opener. The one match to begin it all. He would contend against both Silas and Arkane. Neither opponents were willing to let the other win easy, so the match began with a lock up between Jett and Arkane, only for Silas to bust it up with a drop kick to Arkane. Jett was pushed away when Arkane reared to fight back. A strike to the head was all it took to get the two into a small brawl and Silus ducked under the next, using a knee strike to the gut to get his opponent doubled over. Not too keen to be taken lightly, however, Arkane gained the advantage with a northern lights suplex and pulled his opponent up and out of the ring, over the ropes. However, he hadn’t noticed his opponent only managed to fall over the middle rope; instead, he had turned to set his sights on Jett to continue the match. As he did there was action going on outside of the ring behind their backs. Jett and Arkane took to one another with a quick exchange of strikes before Arkane managed a Butterfly suplex to drop his opponent. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “What’s Silas doing, he was out right?” Whilst distracted with Jett, Silas returned to the ring. The discrepancy was raised to the referee by Oliver Thibodaux. The referee allowed it, but the two in the ring hadn’t seen this activity occur. As Arkane ran Jett out of the ring, as the first official elimination, he turned to find Silas had returned and charged for him. Silas, with ramming speed, sent Arkane over the ropes not long after Jett and let the two crash like a horrific train wreck outside of the ring, thus not only having been brought back into the match for a second chance, but winning with flying colors. Arkane pulled himself together quickly, frustrated, aggravated, and in his heated state, punished Jett further by running him into the barricade. He turned toward Silas, the arm-raised victor of the open battle royal with disbelief. He had been taken for a ride and had believed the man to be eliminated. Perhaps it was his own arrogance that was his downfall, but he swore at Silas to have his revenge if ever he had the change. Jett was the message to him not to screw with Arkane. Either way, Silas had won REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner…Silas Romero!” Arkane looks in the ring in shock as the referee shrugs his shoulders as he tried to explain to him as Romero holds up his arms in victory right in front of Arkane to taunt him, getting the better of him this time as Clayden took his eyes off the prize for a second. RILEY STEVENS: “Clayden thought he had it and his early celebration cost him there.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Pre-mature, just like you eh Riley?” These fans seemed intrigued by these two locking horns again as Arkane mouths to him how lucky he is as Romero just tells him to head to the back and the stare down is cut off by some teasers for later tonight but one can’t help but look forward to these two meeting again. WINNER: SILAS ROMERO Cutting to...somewhere. We see the fairly new team of THE Brandonator and "The Great And Powerful" Alexander. They were standing outside some building. Who cares, you came here to watch a SpiritMuscle promo, so shut the hell up and listen. ALEXANDER: "PEONS OF GIW! The greatest tag team in the history of tag teams has once again come to grace you with our presence. We are Team SpiritMuscle, and we are hear to make a simple statement. You see, we have one goal. That goal is to prove to the universe that we are the awesomest and the tag teamiest tag team of all time. And to do that, we had to get something. That something are these..." THE Brandonator and Alexander lifted up from the bottom of the screen two brand new titles, Alexander's happened to have literally biceps coming out of the golden plaque of the belt, while THE Brandonator's has the Spiritface etched into his. ALEXANDER: "These right here are the NEW Tag Team Championship belts, created for two people and two people only. That just so happens to be us, SpiritMuscle. These titles belong to us and anyone who says otherwise better come and say it to our faces, but you had better have some damn good Face Insurance, because the moment you say something we intend to punch your damn face off!" "The Great And Powerful" Alexander smiled, his feeling of superiority oozing from him. ALEXANDER: "The domination of Team SpiritMuscle has just begun, and with these newly created titles they say to the world that we are the tag teamiest tag team in all of tag teaming, and we will prove that by winning this First To Seven against The Killers, for I am "The Great And Powerful" Alexander, and this man is the Wielder of the Spiritface THE Brandonator...together we are..." "TEAM SPIRITMUSCLE!" The fireworks and fanfare exploded behind them, hitting a poor dog which burned off most of it's skin, as well as flying into someone's office, burning all of his documents and causing him to lose his job, but nobody cared about any of that. It was all about one thing...and that was Team SpiritMuscle. The newly crowned and yet somehow still only self-proclaimed Tag Teamiest Tag Team Champions of the Universe. QUOTE After there promo and a short break we return to the ring with the two tag teams already in the ring as the longest active tag team in GIW history, The Killers take on Team Spirit Muscles in a…big…well…interesting tag team match up here in GIW, the two in each corner right now deciding who will start the match for there team. RILEY STEVENS: “I guess we are doing this like…A bunch?” AMANDA ROWLAND: “O-M-G YES! These two rivals, need to settle this.” As the two sides stare each other down from both sides of the ring, the referee gets us ready for this featured tag team match here on Fusion. DING DING DING! This match gets underway and right away it’s the two big men in the ring right away as Brody Harper and Alexander meet up in the center of the ring, and start to exchange blows like two bar hopping drunkards, reckless abandon and no defense being used as the punches land over and over again, these fans seeming into it as the two continue this for a minute before cardio becomes a factor for both men, as they each land one big punch and fall over to there knee’s, each of them breathing heavily as cardio has really become an issue here, as the two are left with no choice but to tag but the ring must feel like a vast desert for these two big men, having to get across the ring to there much skinnier halves; as they both get up and these fans are on there feet, both men crawling to there corners reaching for a tag then falling over down and out, neither able to make it all the way. These fans are shocked as the referee checks on both men, and at the same time they have a second win crawling a few extra feet to the corner tagging in there skinnier halves. RILEY STEVENS: “The heck, this seems familiar.” In comes the Brandonator and Fielder, rushing at each other engaging in a technical battle as Nelson rolls him into a cradle, 1…Brandonator then rolls through as well 1…Then Fielder does it again 1…Brandonator goes through again, 1…The referee looking now just as tired as the two big men as he jumps up then right back down as the two exchange a few more pin attempts never getting past one as finally they hop up to there feet, and look around at the crowd who give a lack luster cheer, the two tag teams both tired and beaten…in the early stages of this one. Brandonator grabs a hold of Nelson and tosses him into the corner, as he rushes at him and clotheslines him before tagging in Alexander who gets in. Alexander rushes across the ring and hits Brody off the ropes as Brandonator hits the ropes as Alexander heads towards, Fielder…Will he have the energy? He does as while Alexander crushes him with the Mother of all Clothesline’s, which name may imply nurturing, though it’s anything but, however as he hits the clothesline Brandonator hits a Spear as Brandonator rolls out and Alexander hooks the leg 1…2…3! REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here are your winners by pinfall…Alexander and THE Brandonator….Team Spirit Muscles!” These fans seem to have no idea what to think in this one; surprisingly pretty split on both sides of this match some die hard Killers fans, others liking the new duo but tonight it’s the debuting team that upset the GIW Veterans. RILEY STEVENS: “That was the same thing as last week right?” AMANDA ROWLAND: “Nope, totally new!” Team Spirit Muscles continues to celebrate there victory as the Killers regroup on the ramp, glaring in at the opposing tag team in anger. WINNER: TEAM SPIRIT MUSCLES QUOTE “Hevioso" by the 69 Eyes is playing through the speakers as we return from break as we watch as Devlynn Smith walks down towards the ring with the Deep South belt fastened around his waist as the camera cuts away from him into the ring where a crowded ring full of two former GIW Champions; in Daniel Starr and Jamie Vaccaro; a GIW veteran in Jeremy Baltimore, then two newer stars in Danielle and Michael Neeley all looking ready to go. RILEY STEVENS: “Rather interesting field here assembled to try to take Smith’s belt as we have Starr and Vaccaro in the ring.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Think Jamie will trash this belt as well?” The ring full of stars as this ring it is Smith with the big target on his back as he looks around at his opponents with a grin then hand over his belt as the referee calls for the bell to get this thing under way. DING DING DING! Who would come out front and center? With a crowded ring of six opponents, it was hard to take those bets, but everyone at least knew that Devlynn Smith was out to retain the Deep South Championship title now on the line. He was the first to act, but all of the other competitors followed suit. They didn’t target the champion, not Starr, Jamie, nor even Danielle. Nope, it seems the pack mentality had targeted the two weaker links as though they knew by getting rid of them, the match would be better evened out and thus any distractions toward obtaining, or retaining the title eliminated. The two weakest links in the match were Jeremy Baltimore and Michael Neely. Though Baltimore tried to put up more of a fight, he more or less had been overcome as much as Neely had. Michael had been clotheslined over the top rope by Danielle just as Starr used a swift missile dropkick form the side to smack him against the ropes. On the other side of the ring, Devlynn and Jamie used a double irish whip upon Baltimore, the man screaming obscenities and curses as he then went over the rope as well upon his return. He had been flipped with a double toss by the duo. Neely fell hard, banged and like a wreckage, then Baltimore in similar fashion against his side. RILEY STEVENS: “Ouch, two men down.” With that taken cared of, Devlynn was swift to eradicate Jamie, but she wouldn’t go down without a fight. Unlike Starr with Danielle, who was a much easier target for him. Eventually Devlynn dropped Jamie with a flurry of heavy kicks, the final one to the head as Star dropped his opponent. The two gave a glance toward one another across the ring and then raised their respective opponents and, in unison, whipped their opponents toward one another to exchange partners. The plan was to tower over the two and then go after one another, possibly, but Danielle managed to get the upper hand over Devlynn just as Jamie did with Starr. The two men were knocked down by a crossbody, and an equally high flying elbow to the cheek bone. Both men were smacked into the turn posts of the opposite sides and then whipped into one another, but not before turning the tides on Jamie and Danielle again to force them into collision. Jamie took the upper hand, focusing on not connecting with Danielle and performed a quick spear to break the momentum. Jamie was the first to take the chance at a pin, but Daniel Starr dropped a boot down on him, just as Devlynn approached the crime scene. He wasn’t pleased that Starr got there first, and threw off his opponent to kick Jamie while he was down, just for good measure. Starr didn’t like this either, but he kept his distance at first. He had the two weak links on his heels, trying to crawl back in like lifeless zombies, ready to take a bite out of their active competitors. RILEY STEVENS: “Devlynn looks capable so far.” Starr thwarted any strikes the two could throw at him and set them up for a double clothesline right back out of the ring, but as he propped them up with a couple maneuvers, Devlynn took the spotlight and towered over the two with a spinning wheel kick after using Starr as a base to roll over. With Neely out, he turned to Baltimore and just a mere look was enough to get Baltimore to take himself out of the ring with fright. With the zombies at bay, Devlynn turned around, kicked Starr up straight and then found himself dropped by Danielle and Jamie as Starr ducked again for the two to double dropkick the Deep South champion. With the champion out of the picture, Starr grabbed the two by the necks and dropped them down with a double DDT. As he readied for the pin over Jamie, but Danielle refused to let up. She rolled Starr over and pinned Vaccaro for herself, only to find the zombies latch onto her in a final attempt to regain control of the match. Starr backed away this time, to let Baltimore and Neely have their last hurrah, all the while scheming a way to dispose of them, and Danielle at the same time. As things unfolded, Danielle was more than a match, having tossed Baltimore whining over the top rope and as she readied to plant Neely with her finisher, Daniel took the opportunity and performed his Starrstruck kick. Neely toppled behind and accidentally tripped her over, on top of Jamie. The tangled mess was almost too much for him to be amused with, but he pulled her off Jamie to break any pin attempt and pulled himself up onto the turn post for his final act and maneuver. The one that would get him the title. RILEY STEVENS: “That was close!” He waited for the right moment. The referee was focused on Jamie trying to get up and pin over Danielle, but Neely was still down and the prime target for Starr’s Shooting Starr Press. Just when he found the right moment to go for the leap, Devlynn leapt onto the rim of the ring and ran the length of it to charge at Daniel, running the title into his opponent’s skull and knocking him off the turn post, laid flat out into the ring. Now that Starr was the best example of a pinned opponent to take advantage of, Devlynn tossed the title as far from his criminal hands as possible as the referee turned to see the carnage and leapt into the ring to pin Neely. With a quick one, two, and three under and another victory under his belt. REV. DARRYL CLINTON: “Here is your winner by way of pinfall and the still GIW Deep South Champion…Devlynn Smith!” Smith rolls out of the ring with a smirk on his face; walking over to the belt he just used against Starr grabbing a hold of it again this time in victory as Starr gets up in the ring shaking his head angrily, trying to explain what happened to the referee who has none of it as Devlynn becomes the first to defend the belt. RILEY STEVENS: “Smith gets the victory here tonight to defend the Deep South belt for the very first time meaning he’ll head into Aggressive Expansion as champion.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Starr was still screwed…” Starr glares back to the outside as Devlynn cockily heads towards the back showing off the belt that he just proved could be defended but for him, things were about to get harder as yet again he’d have to defend it at ‘Aggressive Expansion’ and this time the spot light would be on him. WINNER: DEVLYNN SMITH (Still GIW Deep South Champion)
