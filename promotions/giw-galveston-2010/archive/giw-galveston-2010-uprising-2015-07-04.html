GIW — Card

Uprising, 4 July 2015

4 July 2015
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The opening bout between “The Peacock” Benny Beverly and “The New Golden Age” Elijah Henry starts off in fifth gear. Henry comes out of the gate looking to end things early. He tries for his finisher, “The Golden Arrow” but Beverly easily ducks the kick. A Russian Leg Sweep takes Elijah down with authority. Beverly follows up with a Shinning Wizard when Elijah tries to get back to his feet. Henry is down in the center of the ring, Benny Beverly signals that he's going to the top rope. Missile Dropkick connects! Beverly goes for a pin but Henry gets a shoulder up at two. RILEY STEVENS: “This match has been all Beverly so far. Look out Uprising, this Peacocks gotta fly!” COLT COX: “That's actually a pretty good strategy. The last thing you want to do when your in the ring with a technician of Henry's caliber is get caught in a shoot match.” The match continues to be a one sided affair. Benny Beverly maintains control over Elijah Henry with a series of Uppercuts. Elijah is forced back against the ropes and tries to position for a reversal but gets denied. Beverly hits an Enziguri when Elijah finally comes off the ropes. A surprise Pop-up Facebuster from Beverly ends up leveling Henry completely. Benny Beverly plays to the crowd a bit before moving in for the kill. But no! Elijah Henry was play possum, Roll up! One… Two… Kick out! RILEY STEVENS: “Beverly has to be smarter than that. It only takes a split second for the momentum to shift or a match to end.” COLT COX: “Yup, you have to be aware of your surroundings at all times or you end up on your back for the one two three.” Beverly and Henry get back to their feet at about the same time. They lock up and it's Elijah Henry that gains the upper hand this time. Henry tries for a Starlight Suplex but Beverly fights free. Beverly stumbles back and rebounds off the ropes. Henry goes for “The Golden Arrow” for a second time and again, Benny Beverly manages to duck it. Another rebound off the ropes, “Red Solo Cup” connects! One… two… three. WINNER: BENNY BEVERLY QUOTE “Pain” by Four Star Mary accompanies Amber Stone to the ring. The protege of Alioth Starre slaps hands with fans along the ramp as she struts to the ring. The fans love her and she loves them back. Once in the ring Amber runs the ropes and waits for her opponent to enter. Finn Holiday makes steps out from behind the curtain as “Knight's March” by Hans Zimmer replaces Amber's entrance theme. Finn makes his way to the ring more hastily than Amer Stone did but still makes time to bump fist with a few select fans. He ascends the stairs, whips his feet, and enters the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “This should be a good match. Both of these young talent's were trained by former GIW Undisputed Champions.” Stone and Holiday lock and battle for position. Holiday has the strength advantage but Amber is more cunning. It's that cunning that grants her the advantage. Stone slips a foot behind Holiday's leg and pushes him backward. Finn Holiday is flat on his back and at the mercy of Amber Stone. Stone nails a series of three Elbow Drops. Finn is rolling around on the floor from the barrage. Stone doesn't let up. Instead she chooses to lock in a rear naked choke. RILEY STEVENS: “This could easily be the end. Sometimes the fans get so distracted by the big flashy moves, they forget how effect the simple ones can be.” COLT COX: “I've seen matches ans careers ended with a Rear Naked Choke. I wouldn't exactly call it a simple move.” Finn manages to use his strength advantage to struggle free. Holiday climbs to his feet while clutching his throat. The choke has done some serious damage and he is obviously feeling its effects. Amber lunges at him and Holiday is powerless to defend himself. She slips behind, “Double Knee Backbreaker” for the win. One… Two.. Three! WINNER: AMBER STONE QUOTE Kelsi Ross' music pumps through the building. With her wife Dr. Kolby Ross on her arm, Kelsi stomps toward the ring. Adonis has already made his entrance and is running the ropes. Dr. Ross moves to ringside as Kelsi rolls into the ring. Shinning Hero Ishimaru is next to enter the fray. Hero comes to the ring riding an elaborate amine themed motorcycle. He dismounts his ride and flips over the top rope to enter the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “These three haven't had the best luck since joining the Uprising roster and should be looking to prove themselves here tonight.” COLT COX: “Except for Adonis. He's currently undefeated.” RILEY STEVENS: “That's true he made a splash in the OBR last week by taking out Travis Touchdown. Speaking of which, have you seen Touchdown since than?” COLT COX: “He got carried out on a stretcher, Riley. I don't think we'll be seeing him for a long time.” In the first moments of the match Ross and Ishimaru team up to take out Adonis. The duo forces Adonis against the ropes and nail a Double Dropkick that sends Adonis to the outside. Once Adonis is taken care of, Ross immediately turns on Ishimaru hitting her signature move “The Back Stabber”. Shinning Hero is down and clutching his back. Ross looks to do more damage with some stiff kicks to Hero's back but Ishimaru rolls out of the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Smart move from Ishimaru. No count outs in a three way match and the outside is the perfect place to get a quick breather.” COLT COX: “Keeping fresh is key in a match like this.” Once Kelsi's back is turned, Shinning Hero stealthily scales the turnbuckle from the outside. Kelsi turns back around when she hears her manager trying to warn her. Sunset Flip from the top rope connects! Ishimaru follows up with a Running Lariat and then a Snap Suplex. He tries to get Kelsi to her feet for another grapple but is hit with a Low Blow. Isimaru falls over comically clutching his crotch. RILEY STEVENS: “That had to hurt!” Kelsi Ross moves to lock in her patented submission finisher, “The Kelsi Lock” but is blind sided by Adonis. Kelsi rolls out of the tackle and counters with a Big Boot. Adonis and Ishimaru are both down but Hero is showing signs of life. Kelsi allows him to get to his feet and nails him with “Spoiled Rotten” her own take on The Complete Shot. Kelsi goes for the cover on Shining Hero. One… Two… Three WINNER: KELSI ROSS QUOTE “Prayer” by the band known as Disturbed signals the arrival of Gristle Jim and his manager, Father Erik. The two meander toward ringside while a chorus of boos drown out their entrance theme. Gristle Jim climbs up on the apron and then steps over the top rope to enter the ring. Father Erik watches eagerly from ringside as Jim raises his hands above his head and gives a silent prayer for victory. The sound of Jim's theme slowly fades out and is replaces by “Lights” by Ellie Golding. The music gets the crowd on its feet, they know who's coming. Katie Keller bounces out of the back on the balls of her feet. She quickly descends the ramp but makes sure to touch hands with as many fans as possible along the way. Rolling beneath the bottom rope, Katie enter the ring and then climbs up the far turn buckle. Her music fades out while she plays to the crowd. RILEY STEVENS: “I'd like to remind everyone watching at home that this match is to crown the new, North Beach Champion.” COLT COX: “It's the first title match ever on Uprising and I expect big things out of each competitor.” RILEY STEVENS: “As do I, Colt. Both Gristle Jim and Katie Keller are 2 and 0 in GIW. Tonight one of them is going to taste loss for the first time and the other is going home a champion. The title, pride, and the future of GIW is on the line here tonight.” COLT COX: “You're giving me goosebumps here! Enough talk, let's get this show on the road.” The bell sounds and it's Gristle Jim who gains the upper hand early on. Jim hits Keller so hard with a slap to the chest, that she goes tumbling backward foot over head. That's followed up by a series of stomps until Keller manages to regain her footing. But its to no avail. This time it's a Heart Punch puts Keller down. Jim tries to pin with one foot but Keller kicks out at one. RILEY STEVENS: “Jim is looking confidant tonight. Let's hope that confidence doesn't come back to bite him.” COLT COX: “It's all psychological. He knew that Keller would kick out he just wanted to show her who's boss.” Back in the ring, Gristle Jim continues to dominate. He grabs Keller off the mat by her neck and lifts her above his head. Choke Bomb connects! Keller is down in the center of the ring again. Jim falls to his knees and raises his arms above his head, he's giving glory to God. But that's all the window Keller needs. Katie scrambles to her feet and runs at her opponent. Jim is still down on his knees with both eyes closed when Keller hit him with an Axe Kick. The move isn't enough to put Jim to sleep but he is definitely feeling its effect. Jim tries to struggle back to his feet but a series of stiff kicks from Keller keeps the giant on his knees. RILEY STEVENS: “This is exactly where Jim doesn't want to be. The big man is only has the advantage when he's on his feet. When you're stuck on your knees, there isn't much chance to mount an offense.” COLT COX: “Yup, Keller knows exactly what she's doing out there. She's a former ECWF champion for a reason.” Eventually, Jim is able to get back to his feet by pushing Katie away during her flurry of kicks. Keller bounces off the ropes and Jim tries to hit her with “Moose Tracks” but Katie ducks the Big Boot. She bounces of the ropes again and hit Jim with a Shoot Kick to the back of the leg. Jim's knee gives and Kelly maneuvers to the front. “Subsweet” connects, driving Jim's head into the canvas. Keller goes for a pin but the ref is distracted by Father Erik at ringside. The distraction gives Jim enough time to power out of the pin and get to his feet. Keller tries for a “Royal Butterfly” But Jim is too big for her to get the move off. RILEY STEVENS: “Uh Oh! Keller didn't plan of this. There's just no way her finisher is going to work against someone as big as Gristle Jim.” Jim kicks Keller in the gut and lifts her up for “The Alaskan Ice Bridge”. Keller wiggles free in the air and lands on her feet. Superkck connects! Jim is down and seeing stars. Keller looks to the fans and then points at the turnbuckle. Keller shoots up the turnbuckle and flings herself backward. “The Most Glamorous Moonsault Ever” connects. Keller hooks the leg. One… Two… No! Kevin Marshall dives into the ring and breaks up the pin! RILEY STEVENS: “What the hell is Kevin Marshall doing here?” COLT COX: “Looks like he's trying to get revenge on Alexis Terry the only way he can, by going after her girlfriend.” Senior Uprising referee, Jadore Dior calls for the bell but not before K-Mart hits Keller with a Superkick of his own. Marshall rolls out of the ring and makes his way to the back with a smirk on his face. Gristle Jim and Father Erik are both wear looks of abject rage and Katie, Katie is out cold. RILEY STEVENS: “Wait, does this mean Katie is the new North Beach Champion?” COLT COX: “No, It means there is no champion. Titles can't be awarded on a DQ. Katie picks up the win but there is no champion.” RILEY STEVENS: “I can't imagine either participant is happy with this outcome, Colt.” COLT COX: “I know that I'd be upset if it happened to me,” WINNER: KATIE KELLER BY D.Q.
