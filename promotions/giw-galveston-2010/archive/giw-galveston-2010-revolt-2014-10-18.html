Revolt, 18 October 2014

GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: REVOLT! Live from the Kauffman Arena – October 18th, 2014 HERE... WE... GO! The go home show before 'On Hallowed Ground' kicks off with a bang as "Bartholomew" by Silent Comedy hits the stereos of thousands of wrestling fans' television sets! The GIW logo quickly flashes on the screen, which is soon replaced by the face paced recaps from last week. We see a quick and sudden glimpse of El Muerte's debut in the battle royale, which leads to several different clips of the Hashke & May versus Sinful Sexuality match up, and what transpired afterwards. Hashke hitting two Rez Drivers, then being attacked by Sinful Sexuality, who in turn were chased away by El Muerte. The clips of Loveman and The Sir's match is shown, accumulating with the pie shot from Mo Chronic - who seemed to go under a radical change last week. The match that nearly stole the show, Astro versus Waters, is also shown. Showing high spots from the entirety of the match. The package then comes down to shots of all four members of the main event from last week, each one getting some advantage in their match. Lynch hits Harlequin with his elbow after a blind tag, soon after the image of him and CDE standing over Harlequin and Michaud is what takes us into the arena. A few hundred rabid Galveston Island Wrestling fans yell and scream for their favourite wrestling show! Showing their appreciation, every single one of them have brought their own personal signs! "Mo Love Mo Problemz"; "I'll Go 2 A Movie With U Hashke!"; "The Guy Behind Me Can't See So I Moved Out Of The Way"; "We Want Aspen". The roar of the crowd is soon silenced as "Black Mass" by Electric Wizard hits the PA System. The cheers for the start of the show soon morph into boos as Michael Lynch is the first person to walk out of the curtains, with the Deep South Champion Christopher Darren Erns following behind him. The two waste no time pandering to the crowd as they saunter down the ramp and enter the ring. Michael takes a microphone from one of the ring hands and paces around the ring as Christopher Darren Erns stays silent with his eyes locked directly on the hard camera. Michael Lynch allows the crowd to continue booing him as he just soaks up the negative energy for his sermon MICHAEL LYNCH: “Heathens. Sinners. Nonbelievers! You all worship your false gods and your broken idols! You pander for their attention just to feel alive. And for what?! Only for a moment. And in that moment, it’s gone. No one will remember you when the lights turn off. All of your heroes just see you as walking paychecks. You pay to make sure that their lights stay on when they get home. So they can feed themselves and indulge in their sinful lifestyles. They don’t actually care about you. They care about what you contain in your wallets. They know just how foolish you all are to give into them! You’re exploited like the cheap prostitutes that roam these streets at night. But yet, you insist on coming back each and every week. Maybe it’s your ignorance to the entire situation. Maybe it’s because you don’t want to accept the reality that your ‘heroes’ are just as sinister and evil as you claim me to be.” Lynch lowers the microphone from his lips as the crowd reactions, unsurprisingly, with hatred and boos. The Masked Messiah plays this off with a faint smile on his face; not one to let the crowd get into his mind MICHAEL LYNCH: “But you know why you really boo me? Why you really hate me? Because deep down in your hateful hearts…you know I’m right! You envy the fact that I speak such a truth that you’ve yet to handle. So take my words to heart when I say this. Christopher Darren Erns will leave On Hallowed Ground as the Undisputed Champion!” Again, the crowd reacts with a shower of boos on both men. This causes Erns to even show a little personality as he smirks after Lynch’s sentence. Michael just chuckles to himself as he continues to control the crowd MICHAEL LYNCH: “The Harlequin hides behind her face paint because she knows her time in Galveston is over. She doesn’t want to reveal her face to this crowd. So you can’t see the lies she says. She doesn’t want to show the concerned look upon her face when she steps out to the ring. She cakes herself like the WHORES out there! And you know why? Because she knows how to get what she wants. Just stop and ask yourselves. Who did she beat for the Undisputed Championship? Or even better yet…what’s her relationship to that man!? Now take a look at Christopher Darren Erns.” The camera focuses on the Deep South Champion as Lynch continues to speak MICHAEL LYNCH: “He’s a faithful disciple of mine and look at where that has taken him. Success! The Deep South Championship that he took from me. That he earned! I can promise you that I didn't lay down for Christopher like Beck did for Harley. He fought me and I fought back. Now? Now he’s in a position to be successful. I told everyone here that my disciples would go further than I ever could and look at him now. So go ahead and tell me that I’m wrong! I’d love to hear it! But all you’ll do is just boo. Cry. Moan! Oh, how could Michael Lynch be so cruel?! And you forget that the Messiah had given you all the option to flock to a safe haven. Yet…did you listen? The Messiah has spoken.” Michael Lynch drops the microphone as a look of disdain comes over the mask. He makes his exit from the ring and Christopher follows behind him while the crowd continues to voice their hatred for the men as the camera fades to a commercial break QUOTE “Electric Worry” by Clutch hits the arena and Johnny Hashke comes down the ramp, high-fiving some of the members of the audience and receiving a great amount of cheers, seemingly to be the fan favorite among the two stars competing in this match. Either way, Kevin Astro doesn’t really care as he’s carrying himself to the ring with pride; hyping himself up and sliding into the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “This is a man with a lot of pride. And a lot of arrogance. More importantly, he’s also your opponent at On Hallowed Ground. What’s your mindset heading into that match?” MATT KLUGE: “I feel kind of bad for Kevin Astro, Auva called me for the match, of course I’m going to take it. If those two have problems, I’m sure not going to be the solution. I’m here for a fight.” But the crowd quickly changes into all cheers as “Enter Sandman” by Metallica blares throughout the arena with M. Tech Michaud making his entrance tonight. Looking to pick up his first win this week, the crowd favorite slaps the hands of a few lucky fans before climbing into the ring and heading into his corner with Kevin Astro. The cheers don’t stop as the Undisputed Champion’s theme, “Everybody Wants to Rule the World” by Lorde, hits. The Harlequin makes her entrance with the Undisputed Championship wrapped around her waist; her clown face paint still as vibrant and bright as ever. The camera cuts to Kevin Astro who is looking down at his wrist and taps it with his fingers in a “hurry up” motion in which Harlequin just replies with rolling her eyes. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “I can’t blame him. Just get on with the show already!” The energy of the building takes a detour as soon as “Black Mass” by Electric Wizard plays. Michael Lynch steps out from the black curtain with the Deep South Champion, Christopher Darren Erns walking right behind him. The duo make their way down to the ramp and climb up onto the apron; holding off their entrance into the ring until the third member of their team makes his presence felt. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Now these guys…these guys know how to put on a show!” MATT KLUGE: “Oh yeah, I remember when going through that goth phase in wrestling was cool too.” “People Are Strange” by Johnny Hollow plays and Jack Waters steps out with his manager following him closely. But things don’t go smoothly as Waters heads down the ramp with a sudden pop from the crowd; confusing nearly everyone involved. The reason for the cheer became clear as Tony Miranda had attacked Jack Waters from behind! RILEY STEVENS: “It’s Tony Miranda! He’s back again!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “What?! How can these fans cheer this on?!” Jack tries his best to fight back, but Tony’s surprise offense is too much for him to handle. Puppa screams at Miranda to stop, but he’s not listening to any of it. Lifting Jack up, Miranda hits a strong German suplex on the entrance ramp. But he’s not done yet as he picks Waters up just to drop him with an uranage right onto the ramp. Miranda stops his assault and just backs away from Waters; yelling that he’ll get the same treatment at On Hallowed Ground. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Oh my god. I feel disgusted watching that! And you wonder why I root for Jack Waters!” RILEY STEVENS: “I’ll admit that Tony’s actions were a bit unorthodox, but his presence was felt.” Lynch and Erns watched on in confusion as the medical staff comes down to check on Jack Waters. Before the match has even started Jack Waters was already eliminated from the match; forcing an impromptu handicap match. On the other side of the ring, the trio looks on with slight smirks on their faces as Michael Lynch and Christopher Darren Erns look at one another with a bit of concern. Christopher begins to make his way into the ring, but Lynch holds him back; motioning for him to enter the ring instead. M. Tech Michaud makes the first appearance for his team as the bell rings. A quick lock up occurs in the center of the ring, but Lynch is outmatched by M. Tech’s superior grappling; getting tossed to the side of the ring by M. Tech. Springing back up to his feet, Michael charges Michaud, but is taken down yet again with a Judo toss. Not looking to get taken down for a third time; Lynch hops up to his feet and hits Michaud with a sharp kick to his shin that catches M. Tech off guard and a sudden lariat takes M. Tech down for the first time. However, Michaud is still fresh as he gets back up and grabs Lynch; pulling him into a Muay Thai clinch. RILEY STEVENS: “We saw this last week between Michaud and Erns. I gotta say, those strikes don’t look very pleasing to be on the receiving end of.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “It’s wrestling. Do you ever want to be on the receiving end of a move?” A couple of brutal uppercuts stun Lynch and one nearly knocks out the former Undisputed Champion, but he bounces back with some strong forearm blows to the side of Michaud’s head. At this point, Michaud begins to hit Lynch with some forearm strikes of his own. The two men trade blows back and forth with neither party really taking an early advantage. This battle goes on for what seems like an eternity until Michael Lynch finally breaks free with a cheap poke to the eyes of the newcomer. The referee has some words with Lynch, but the Messiah just brushes them off. MATT KLUGE: “You know, back in my day, we had some respect for the referees.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Do you even know his name?” MATT KLUGE: “That’s not important right now.” Looking to continue the attack, Lynch stands over his fallen prey, but M. Tech manages to grab a hold of Lynch’s tights and rolls him up for a quick pin 1… 2… THR-KICK OUT! RILEY STEVENS: “Whoa! We almost had an upset right there!” The crowd voices their disappointment at Lynch breaking free so early and even the Messiah looks a bit stunned as to what happened. A quick elimination would not bode well for the team. Lynch gets up to react, but M. Tech’s not having any of it as he retaliates with some Muay Thai kicks to the midsection that nearly takes the air out of Lynch. Out of nowhere, Michaud throws Lynch nearly out of the ring with a belly to belly suplex. Capitalizing on that, M. Tech heads over to Lynch and locks in a armbar; hoping to continue the possibility of an early elimination. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “He’s going to break his damn arm! Somebody stop this!” A few seconds go by as Christopher Darren Erns just looks on; his face shows that he’s ready to come in and break up the hold, but Michael keeps shouting at him to stay back. With all of his might, Lynch maneuvers himself closer to the ropes only to be barely a few inches away from hooking his leg on the bottom rope. Michaud is well aware of this as he tightens the hold as the fans get to be a bit uneasy as Lynch refuses to tap. Erns notices this as well and takes action by hopping off the apron to grab onto his Messiah’s foot and physically pulls both men over until Lynch’s foot barely touches the rope. The referee tries to make an objection to this, but just lets the match continue on. M. Tech swiftly looks over at Erns with a look of distain on his face and makes the mistake of taking his eyes off of Lynch. For the first time in the match, Michael gets a huge move in as Michaud turns right into a Ram’s Horn! The ripcord roaring elbow drops Michaud to the mat out cold as Lynch gets on top of him for the pin 1… 2… 3! OLIVER THIBODAUX: “There we go!” RILEY STEVENS: “And there you have it folks. The first elimination of the night coming from the team that already started off outnumbered.” The first official elimination has been made, but Michael Lynch has no time to recover as Kevin Astro runs into the ring to catch his opponent off guard. This move proves to be successful as a T-Bone suplex takes Lynch off his feet. Following this move, Astro looks out to Kluge before looking back to Michael Lynch. Following this, Astro goes for a single leg crab lock on Lynch to continue the submission attack on Lynch. After this first encounter with a submission, it’s clear that Michael Lynch is feeling a bit gassed out at this point, but luck is on his side tonight as his finger tips barely wrap around the ring rope. The entire time this move is locked in, Astro is still staring down Matt Kluge from across the ring MATT KLUGE: “Great move by Astro. I would have done it better though.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Yeah, yeah, I’d like to see you try.” After a near five count from the referee, Kevin lets go of the hold, but with no hesitation locks it right back in. Getting another four count, the referee actually has to pull Astro away from Michael to cool him down. “You think you can do that better than me?!” Astro shouts out at Matt Kluge who just rolls his eyes in response. Lynch takes this time to roll out of the ring and catch his breath. The outside doesn’t provide any safe haven for Lynch as Astro begins to formulate an idea in his mind. Looking back at the ropes then Lynch; a buzz generates throughout the arena as the crowd gets hyped for what’s next. Kevin Astro takes off and bounces off the ring ropes to dive through and hit THE H.D.M.I.! RILEY STEVENS: “OH MY GOD! WHAT A MOVE! The H.D.M.I. to the outside! And I’ll tell you something, guys, those mats aren’t padded that well!” MATT KLUGE: “He’ll have to do even better than this if he wants to beat me!” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Are you kidding me?! That was the most exciting move I’ve seen all year! It’s just a shame it had to be on Lynch.” A suicide blockbuster neck breaker on Michael Lynch generates a “Holy Shit!” chant throughout the building. The camera cuts to Christopher Darren Erns who watches both men with a serious look as Harlequin just watches on with curiosity as to who will get up first. A surprising “Astro” chant breaks out amongst the crowd as they begin to rally for the man of the hour. The referee is even stunned by this move, but comes back to reality as he begins his ten count. Around the six mark, Michael Lynch is the surprising competitor to get back to his feet to slide in to break up the count, but rolls right back out of the ring. A bit of rage flows through Michael Lynch has he grabs onto Astro and throws him into the ring. Following behind him, Michael takes to the top rope for the first time in a long time, looking to hit something big here. And he does just that as he takes to the air with a diving headbutt! Both men roll around in pain, but it’s eventually Kevin Astro who gets back to his feet first. Not wanting to be out done, he takes to the turnbuckle this time and eagerly awaits for Lynch to get back up. Astro takes another moment to shout out at Kluge, but all Kluge does is just point back to the ring. Michael Lynch is waiting for Astro and sends him flying with a avalanche frankensteiner. Michael Lynch hooks the leg of Astro 1… 2… THR-KICK OUT! RILEY STEVENS: “The spirit of Kevin Astro is still alive! What do you have to say about Astro’s fight, Matt?” MATT KLUGE: “So far I’m not impressed.” The crowd cheers in excitement as Astro manages to kick out at the last possible second while Lynch just hangs his dead down in disappointment. Harlequin is holding out her hand for the tag, but Astro just blows her off as he states that he’s got this. However, being lifted up and taken down by a German suplex from Lynch would suggest otherwise. Michael begins to take over the match, but not for long as Harlequin begins to yell at him. Momentarily distracted by the champion, Lynch decides to step over and let her know why Christopher will be taking her belt. Although it’s hard to hear what Michael is actually saying to Harlequin, but “Beck Ramsey” is heard in the process. Taken back by this, all Harlequin does in response is slap the taste out of Michael’s mouth. The former Undisputed Champion rubs his cheek and informs Harlequin that she’ll regret that. RILEY STEVENS: “Nice! Take it to him, Harley!” MATT KLUGE: "Girl power!" Turning back around, Kevin Astro has gotten up ready to lock up again with Lynch. The two meet in the middle of the ring as fatigue begins to take effect each man. But Astro takes control with an enzigurl that takes out Lynch. Looking to redeem himself from earlier in the match; Kevin Astro heads back up to the turnbuckle much to the dismay of Harlequin. As Lynch gets up, Astro doesn’t waste any time bragging at Kluge and hits a second H.D.M.I.! Instead of hooking the leg right away, Astro decides now is the time to take another shot at Kluge, but gets stopped by Harlequin who just looks unimpressed with his arrogance. “Oh, so you think you can do better?” is all that’s heard by Astro as he tags in his partner who looks eager to one up Astro in hopes of shutting him up once and for all. As Lynch tries to recover, he only gets captured by The Harlequin who gets some revenge from earlier in the match and hits him with her Clowning Around! The jumping implant DDT puts Michael Lynch down for good as she just looks over at Astro and yells “That’s how you do it!” The clown goes for the pin, but Astro quickly tags himself in to take the glory. Harlequin glares at her partner while she slowly gets back into the corner and Kevin goes for the pin 1… 2… 3! OLIVER THIBODAUX: “No way! That’s not fair!” MATT KLUGE: “I know. Astro needed help from a girl.” Everyone is a bit taken back by Michael Lynch’s elimination, but Astro just gets up as calm and collected as ever as he yells over the top rope, “Have you ever beaten Michael Lynch before, bitch!” MATT KLUGE: “Well, considering I haven’t actually had a match with him. Matt Kluge stands up and just stares at Kevin Astro; unimpressed with everything he’s seen so far. Knocking his On Hallowed Ground opponent down; Kevin Astro seems pretty pleased with himself as he climbs back into the ring and continues to shout verbal abuse at Kluge. At this point, Kevin seems to be unaware of the Deep South Champion’s presence in the ring and gets rolled up with a handful of tights 1… 2… 3! OLIVER THIBODAUX: “And there goes Astro!” RILEY STEVENS: “And he’s not the only one. Where is Matt Kluge going?!” Astro leaps up to his feet in shock and yells at the referee; calling him unprofessional amongst other names, but his verbal assault on GIW employees is cut short due to Matt Kluge pulling him out of the ring. The crowd explodes as Kluge tosses Kevin Astro into the ringside steel steps! Feeling like his job out here tonight was done; Matt Kluge just makes his exit to the back while Harlequin steps into the ring to take on her On Hallowed Ground opponent. The Yakuza kick sends the Undisputed Champion back and on the mat as Christopher looks for a quick upset 1… 2… KICK OUT! But he’s not so lucky as the champ manages to kick out in time. Christopher begins to wear down Harlequin with a series of knees to the midsection. Harlequin’s small stature begins to show as she takes the majority of the strikes from Erns, but finally slips free with her cunning speed and gets behind the Deep South Champion. Unsure of what to do, Harlequin just leaps up onto the back of Christopher and locks in a sleeper hold! This catches Erns off guard as he tries to grab onto Harlequin to toss her off his back, but to no luck! Several seconds go by and it looks like Erns might just pass out as his movements in the ring start to get sluggish. Just when it looks like Christopher is about to pass out, he falls backwards and right on top of Harlequin; forcing her to let go of the submission attempt. The self proclaimed Great One rolls off of the champion to catch his breath while Harlequin is still a bit dazed from having 240 pounds of muscle fall on top of her. Pulling himself back up with the aid of the ropes, Christopher heads over to lift up Harlequin, but only gets a face full of her nails. The Undisputed Champion rakes the face of her challenger and this causes a bit of blood to draw from Christopher’s left cheek. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “That should be a DQ, ref! "Vicarious Devil" by Tool and Laura Marling plays in a surprising turn of events. RILEY STEVENS: “That’s…that’s Beck’s theme!” Harlequin’s attention turns toward the entrance ramp as she’s unsure of what to expect. A few seconds go by before a man finally makes his appearance through the black curtains. It’s Michael Lynch! Harlequin reacts accordingly, but this distraction allows her to get rolled up by Christopher Darren Erns! 1… 2… THR- KICK OUT! RILEY STEVENS: “How does she do it!? RILEY STEVENS: “OH!” And that’s it! Harlequin is eliminated from the match as Christopher Darren Erns has secured the victory for his team.
