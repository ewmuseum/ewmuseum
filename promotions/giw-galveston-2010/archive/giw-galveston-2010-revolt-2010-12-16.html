GIW — Card

Revolt, 16 December 2010

16 December 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: Thursday Night Revolt! LIVE DECEMBER 16th, FROM THE HARBOW VIEW INN IN GALVESTON, TEXAS BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! We are live returning to the Harbow View Inn where the fans are literally PACKED in this place and after last week’s show it’s no secret why. Being a less then main stream company was hard on them, but overall they had done well for themselves. Tonight however this place was twice as full as the last time they came and even more so the excitement in the air was more evident then ever. After panning around through the crowd the camera quickly cuts to the backstage area. The fans are greeted with the face of Amanda Rowland standing by, seeming to be thinking. AMANDA ROWLAND: “So you’re for sure that he can’t go tonight?” The camera pans out to show Dr. House GIW’s personal doctor nodding his head as we continue to pan out to show Eric Sparrow wrapped up in a sling, with that still very cocky look on his face. AMANDA ROWLAND: “Well then, you’re off the card. Thanks Doctor.” Doctor House makes his exit, as Sparrow steps forward smiling, that always scheming face of his with a big smirk. ERIC SPARROW: “It’s just a shame that, I can’t defend my belt tonight. I was really looking forward to it.” Sparrow smirks holding his title over his shoulder, as he glares over at Amanda who shakes her head smiling at him. AMANDA ROWLAND: “No, it’s ok. Have the night off, it won’t last long. Let’s just say that at ‘Genesis’ you’ll defend your belt regardless, otherwise you’ll vacate it. Don’t think you dodged a bullet, cause at the end of the day I’m going to hand pick a worthy opponent for you. Now get you and your little sling out of here.” Amanda smiles and points him out but Sparrow doesn’t give her the right to feel good about herself as he doesn’t even show he cares as he walks out, having gotten himself out of his match here tonight. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = As we return to the ring we are already witnessing our first action as we come right into the scene of an apparent beating. On the mat in pain is a small little Hispanic man, seeming to be trying to escape what is a beat down. Standing above him is a very spiffy looking man, with a big smile on his face. RILEY STEVENS: “Welcome ladies and gentleman to a scene that has been ugly from the start. What was supposed to be a dark match has now become a big beat down as Landon Ryan our newest star has been crushing Julio Rodriguez a local talent.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “It’s been awesome!” Seeming unable to move we watch as Julio tries to escape the ring, grabbing the ropes and hoping to pull himself out. But Landon doesn’t even let him get half way out before pulling him back into the ring, looking down at him with a big smirk. He demands him to get to his feet and fight, something we see right away is not likely. However to our surprise little Julio gets to his feet, standing in his face. BOOM! A big disrespectful slap to the face, sends Julio back to the mat where right away Landon gets down and locks him into a ANKLE LOCK! The fans boo as Julio immediately taps out. RILEY STEVENS: “This was just wrong.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “It’s called a statement. Not only did he terrorize this guy long enough to get on the show, he made a statement.” Rather then letting guy Landon keeps the hold in on the poor guy who seems to fade rather quickly. Suddenly the crowd starts to cheer as out from the back flying down towards the ring is Bobby Bower another debuting star. He slides into the ring, but Ryan lets go of the hold and slides out of the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “Thank God not all our new talent is heartless.” Bobby checks on him as he stares out at Landon Ryan who backs up the ramp with a big grin on his face. It’s clear we have two new stars, one who is good and one who is evil as we take a break. WINNER: LANDON RYAN = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = We head to the backstage area yet again, this time we are backstage with Aspen Chaud who is getting ready for her match here tonight, she’s got on her ring gear on as she seems hyped up. The door opens as Aspen looks up and standing there is Chris Armstrong with a big grin on his face looking down at her. CHRIS ARMSTRONG: “So, I come back and I am immediately in a main event. Guess I always was a better draw then you. Spose’ that’s why I should be the champion around here.” Armstrong smirks as he stares at Aspen who gets to her feet looking up at him, with a smile on her face not letting him into her head. ASPEN CHAUD: “Think you and I already fought, and it was a certain someone that walked away with the win. What makes you think it would be any different the second time around?” Armstrong grins having completely expected that response as Aspen lifts her belt proudly onto her shoulder. CHRIS ARMSTRONG: “Simply put, I’m young, talented, and I’ve done more things in my career then most do in a life time. You beat me you did, but this time there’s nothing but you and I for the title. So how about this then? I’m going to go ask Amanda and I have a feeling she’ll accept.” Armstrong smirks and walks away heading to Amanda’s office as Aspen shakes her head, trying to stay focused as we head to the ring. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = The crowd is booing as Technical Mechanica the ‘GIW World Tag Team Champions’ stand in the ring both ready to go. Around the ring are a bunch of tables, set up every where. Suddenly, BOOM! They are crushed from behind as the fans and everyone is caught off guard by Perfect Combination. The referee realizing that he has to start it quickly calls for the bell. DING DING DING! RILEY STEVENS: “I guess here we go, Perfect Combination attacked from no where on the champions as they fight with them for a title shot.” Right away The Shadow begins to crush Ombio into the corner as Tony Miranda slowly begins to stomp away Damon Ramon on the other side of the ring. The champions are reeling as the fans seem to be booing both teams not wanting to see either do well. However Tony screams out at The Shadow, as he slides out of the ring going for a table. The Shadow grabs Ombio and tosses him with a big throw into the middle of the ring as he then runs across the ring catching Ramon with a monstrous clothesline. Tony slides the table into the ring as he sets it up in the corner, while The Shadow seems to make sure that Ramon and Ombio do nothing. To his surprise both of them get up, catching him with a few kicks before running off the ropes. However they run right back into a HUGE double clothesline! The fans are stunned as both of the champions, fall to the mat. Tony directs traffic walking back into the middle of the ring and telling him to lift up Ramon! The fans are stunned as The Shadow does just that throwing him up onto his shoulders carrying him towards the table set up near the corner. However Ramon slides off and bails out of the ring, looking scared for his life. He tries to help his partner out but he’s too late as The Shadow pulls him away. Tony points in front of the table where Shadow puts the dazed Ombio as he then goes to watch Ramon. The fans seem to hate him for ditching his partner, for his own good. Regardless Tony lifts him up and then Powerbomb’s him right through the table! RILEY STEVENS: “That’s going to leave a mark; you got to wonder what Ramon was thinking leaving his partner like that.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Why should both men take the fall, he realizes that the match was going no where for them, saved himself.” The Shadow and Tony Miranda immediately turn their attention to Damon Ramon on the outside, as he stares in at his fallen partner as the fans boo both sides, it’s clear that it’s soon time for these two to finally fight for the titles as we take a break. WINNERS: THE PERFECT COMBINATION = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = Back to the back we go where we see Austin Acquin on the phone, talking with somebody. As he talks we see Gavin Masterson walk across the way, seeing him and stopping on a dime as he looks at him, waiting until Austin hangs up to speak with him. GAVIN MASTERSON: “Who was that? Let me guess Joshua? You text him and tell him that Gavin Masterson hopes he gets well soon. I wouldn’t want winning the title being any more easy then it already would have been.” Gavin smirks as Austin seems fully prepared to fight him right here and right now however Gavin speaks before anything happens. GAVIN MASTERSON: “Regardless since he can’t be here, you should go ahead and watch my match tonight. Maybe then you can relay the news, that maybe he should just stay away this time. Because I could put him out for a lot longer, by the way give him this some time I’m sure he can use it.” Masterson smirks and then drops a hundred dollar bill into Austin’s pocket before walking off for his match as we end on a very unhappy looking Acquin as he prepares to pull out his cell as we head to the ring. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = Standing inside of the ring is Kaos Jones a debuting star who is stretching out to a bit of applause from the crowd when suddenly “Seven“ by Boondox hits and the fans begin to boo having just saw what Gavin Masterson had to say as he walks out from the back with that huge smirk on his face. RILEY STEVENS: “This man debuted last week in impressive fashion now lets see what he can do with real competition.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “He’s my guy, you heard it here first this guy’s going to be a champion.” Before the match can start as Gavin steps into the ring looking over at his opponent scoffing at him, as he heads to the top rope. On the ramp we suddenly see Austin Acquin appear, seeming to have taken Gavin’s advice to watch him. DING DING DING! The referee starts the match and right away the two men come together in the center of the ring. Kaos tries to show some class by offering his hand for a handshake however Gavin takes a huge cheap shot by slapping him across the face, before following it up with a big Hip Toss. The fans boo his actions as he looks up at the ramp realizing that Austin is there bringing a smirk to his face. Kaos hops up and goes for a punch that connects however Gavin falls back, holding his eye complaining to the referee. The fans boo him thinking there’s no way that he got poked in the eye. The referee checks on him, as Kaos tries to tug the referee away complaining he didn’t do anything. As he does he’s caught with a vicious clothesline that just lays Jones out. The fans are stunned as Gavin looks up at Austin, motioning a belt around his waist as he waits for Kaos to get up. Looking up at him the entire time he waits until Kaos is up before grabbing him and setting him up, right into a ‘Market Crash’ a Barry White Driver! The fans are stunned as he did it right in Austin’s direction as he makes the cover for the match 1…2…3! That’s it. RILEY STEVENS: “Two straight dominating victories have earned him a title shot.” Gavin Masterson smirks slowly kicking Kaos out of the ring, right in front of Austin laying him out in front of him as he points to Austin and begins to motion a belt around his waist sending a message to him and his brother Joshua Acquin as we have a new contender. WINNER: GAVIN MASTERSON (New ION Television Number One Contender) = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = We head to the backstage area where Blake Beckett is standing in the parking lot, with a big smirk on his face as he leans against the wall running through his Blackberry when Amanda Rowland walks up to him, seeming for the first time happy to see him. BLAKE BECKETT: “Sure seems like somebody is happy to see me.” Blake smirks grinning at the fact that Amanda has no choice but to admit that he did good last week bringing in Chris Jericho as she stands there nodding her head. AMANDA ROWLAND: “Well, you surprised me last week in a very, very good way. The numbers aren’t official yet, but I know for a fact we have more viewers then we’ve ever had before. This is our biggest drawing show as well. Thing is, people are wondering just where he is?” Amanda smiles not wanting to anger him into pulling the plug on Jericho, as she looks around at the parking lot as Blake shakes his phone smiling. BLAKE BECKETT: “He’ll be here trust me. But let’s just say that, I want something for him. You know my salary? Well. Let’s double it, ok?” Amanda at first seems to want to immediately say no but then she begins to think about it, realizing that she really doesn’t have a choice here as she nods her head in agreement. AMANDA ROWLAND: “You’ve got it.” Amanda walks away as Blake’s always big grin stretches across his face as he realizes that slowly but surely he’s starting to get back his power as he suddenly gets up as a limo pulls up but we are taken away to a commercial break. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = Aspen and Reave glared at each other, as the referee stood between them. He sounded for the bell, and the two started to circle each other. They locked up in the middle of the ring, each one trying to over power the other. Aspen managed to gain the upper hand, after connecting with a kick to Reave's stomach. Aspen grabbed Reave's arm and irish whipped him into the ropes facing them, and when he rebounded off them, Aspen knocked him down with a clothesline. RILEY STEVENS: “Aspen is taking control, as always. She really is one of the most dominant wresters here.” Reave got up, and this time got knocked down with a drop kick. While Scott was still down, Aspen dropped to her knees, and grabbed hold of Reave, locking him in an arm trap crossface. Scott Reave immediately started to squirm; trying ever so hard to break the hold, but Chaud refused to budge, and instead tightened the hold. Scott stopped squirming, and at this point, the referee was on the floor in front of him, asking him if he was going to quit. When he shouted out a no, Aspen tried to tighten the hold even more, and it was obviously leaving its damage on Scott Reave. His face was a bright red, and his body was obviously getting limp. The referee grabbed Scott's free arm and raised it up, before letting it go; it fell liimply to the mat. The referee raised Scott's arm up again, and let it go; it fell limply to the mat for a second time. The referee raised Scott's arm up again, and let it go; only this time, Scott tensed it up before it could hit the mat. RILEY STEVENS: “Reave isn’t done yet Ollie!” Showing signs of life, Reave started to squirm again, using all his strength to slightly loosen the hold. Jordan kept on squirming, until he managed to elbow Aspen in the face. He connected with another elbow, and that caused Aspen to break the hold. Reave quickly got to his feet, and connected with a right hand, but Aspen retaliated with an upper cut, that almost blew Reave's head off. Aspen then quickly bounced himself of the ropes nearest to him, and connected with a thunderous spinning wheel kick. Smirking, Aspen covered Scott Reave for the pin. ONE...TWO...SCOTT KICKS OUT! Aspen slammed her fists on the mat, not too pleased with the call. She grabbed Reave and yanked him up onto his feet. she went for a right hand, but Reave blocked it, and instead connected with a right hand of his own. He followed it up with an uppercut, almost blowing Aspen's head off, giving her a small taste of her own medicine from earlier in the match. As Reave played to the crowd, Aspen capitalised, connecting with a thunderous bulldog. She quickly covered Reave for the pin. ONE...TWO...REAVE GETS HIS SHOULDER UP! Aspen looks a little annoyed, but she shakes it off. She gets to his feet, and pulls Reave up with him. She goes for a kick in the gut, but Scott catches her leg and pushes it down, before knocking Aspen down with a clothesline. He grabs him and pulls him up, looking to connect with a DDT, but Aspen blocks it, and instead connects with a...KMA! Aspen quickly hooks Reave's leg for the pin. ONE...TWO... ASPEN IS PULLED UNDER THE RING ROPE! RILEY STEVENS: “It’s that damn Chris Armstrong!” The camera pans out, showing Chris Armstrong pulling Aspen out of the ring. The referee tosses Armstrong out of the arena, and Aspen slowly begins to re-enter the ring… SHE IS MET BY THE STREET MAGIC! ONE…. TWO…. THREE! SCOTT REAVE HAS STOLEN THIS MATCH! OLIVER THIBODAUX: “He beat the champion!” RILEY STEVENS: “Oh come on Armstrong helped him out. Though I have to admit that Reave looked good tonight we got us a great new star.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “He should be the champion.” Reave screams as the fans are stunned as he stands over Aspen the winner of the match, as Armstrong smirks on the outside as Scott Reave has defeated the champion and put his name on the map. WINNER: SCOTT REAVE = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = Back to the back we go where Scott Reave is walking through the locker room area when he comes to Amanda Rowland’s office not knocking instead barging right into it. However he’s caught off guard when already in there standing in front of Amanda is Chris Armstrong, looking back at him with a smirk on his face. AMANDA ROWLAND: “Just the man I wanted to see. You see I have a bit of an issue; both of you think your worthy of a title shot.” Reave stays quiet however Armstrong does not, as he begins to speak up as usual not giving Amanda any time to think. CHRIS ARMSTRONG: “This guy won one match! AND I HELPED HIM.” Reave who was seemingly going to stay quiet snaps back at him turning getting into his face yelling right back in his face. SCOTT REAVE: “I beat the champion, a girl that you could never beat it should be my title shot not yours.” The two seem ready to fight but Amanda steps up in between them, keeping them separated from fighting as she lays down the law. AMANDA ROWLAND: “Here’s what we will do then. We have a big super-show on the twenty ninth, the last show before ‘Genesis’. In the main event it’ll be Chris Armstrong versus Scott Reave with Special Guest Referee, Aspen Chaud for a title shot, that ok with you two?” Amanda says that like it’s a choice however Reave nods his head as he walks out as Armstrong glares at him, before he looks back at Amanda then heads out for his match. = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = It’s that time of the night as the fans cheer knowing the Main Event is here. “Fireproof” by Piller echoes through the arena as the fans go wild for some of the biggest fan favorites, Los Firemen fly out from the back both of them fired up. They run right down to the ring and slide into the ring with Jos C walking behind them. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Talk about being fed to the fishes, these poor guys have a huge challenge ahead of them tonight.” RILEY STEVENS: “That is if Chris Jericho is even here.” Los Firemen jump up onto the ropes and hold up their hands, as the fans give them a big standing ovation. They jump down as “Turn Up The Trouble” by Airbourne hits and the fans immediately rain down boos on the man as Chris Armstrong strolls out from the back, stopping as he heads toward the ring stopping and yelling at the camera “I’m the real number one contender” RILEY STEVENS: “You got to believe Chris Armstrong is looking to get some momentum heading into his huge contenders match.” Armstrong steps up into the ring taking his place in the corner as the fans turn to the ramp waiting to see if it’s about to happen. The lights flicker out and then "Break The Walls Down” hits and the fans explode both into boos and cheers as they seem undecided on what way they are with him. Standing at the top of the rope with his hands spread eagle, he turns and fist pumps as he heads down towards the ring. He looks at the fans scoffing at them, as he jumps up the steps into the ring. RILEY STEVENS: “You can feel the electricity in the air as Y2J! Makes his debut.” Jericho steps up the ramp as everyone in the arena seems in awe of him, the biggest star perhaps ever in Galveston. The referee even seems in a trance before shaking it off as Jericho lets Armstrong star while Los Firemen elect to have Jose start. DING DING DING! Right away the fans seem excited though at the same time want to see Jericho get into it. Armstrong and Jose begin to circle around each other, before going in for a lock up. Right away Armstrong begins to back him up into the corner, slamming him into it and trying to choke him out. The referee begins to count 1…2…3…4…Finally Armstrong breaks away walking back at the referee’s command however he goes forward connecting with a big punch to the face. The ref pushes him back as Jose shakes it off, getting back to his feet and this time when Armstrong comes at him he dropkicks his leg and then runs off the ropes connecting with a big kick to the chest sending Armstrong to the mat harshly! The fans cheer as Jose riles them up, before making a cover 1…Kick Out! The fans boo as Jose gets to his feet, starting a slow clap with the fans as he grabs Armstrong and then tries to whip him into the corner but Armstrong turns it around and throws him into the corner. To his surprise Jose jumps up and then dives off with a Crossbody onto Armstrong, into a pin 1…Kick Out! The fans cheer again at the action as Jose gets up seeming pumped as he lifts Chris and tosses him into his corner, tagging in Jos B who goes to the top rope as Jose holds Chris’s arm. He jumps down and does a killer double foot stomp, nearly ripping his arm off. The fans cheer as Chris runs around holding his arm, as Jos B screams out at them as Los Firemen try to pull an upset here. Waiting for Chris to get up, he sets up for a kick. In the corner however Jericho gets the ref’s attention allowing Armstrong to duck under the kick and hit a nasty low blow to Jos B! The fans let out their boos as Armstrong stumbles across the ring making the tag to Jericho! RILEY STEVENS: “Here we go!” Jericho explodes into the ring, connecting with a dropkick to Jos B in the ring before then turning to the corner and then elbowing Jose off the ropes. He turns and waits for Jos to get to his feet, and when he does he nails him with a Backhand Chop dropping Jos to the mat. Jos seems to be out of it as he struggles to get back up, however this is a huge mistake as Jericho kicks him down to the mat and then turns him over right into the ‘Walls of Jericho’! The fans are stunned and actually start to boo him as Jos B struggles to do anything. Jose now up tries to get back into the ring but unseen to us Armstrong has traveled around the ring, dragging him out of the ring and tossing him harshly into the announce table. Back in the ring Jericho tugs back as Jos B taps out! OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Jericho and Armstrong win!” Jericho locks in the hold a few seconds longer before, letting Jos B go as he gets to his feet and stands there in the middle of the ring. On the outside Jos C checks on Jose as Armstrong celebrates the victory. But back into the ring, the show clearly is coming to an end with the fans turning on Jericho nobody cheering him as he screams “This is my show!” and unless someone plans to stop him, it seems like he’s speaking the truth as we end the show yet again with Jericho standing strong. WINNERS: CHRIS JERICHO & CHRIS ARMSTRONG
