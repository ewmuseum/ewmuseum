GIW — Card

Tuesday Night Evolve, 7 September 2010

7 September 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Evolution Pro Wrestling Presents: Tuesday Night EVOLVE! Live! From Ellerslie, Louisiana from the Mccroft Arena! BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! It’s the second addition of Evolve and tonight the crowd are packed into the arena at least a hundred or two hundred of them, full house for the show this week to see the seven of them go at it. They are all happy knowing the show is just starting as we go to the announce table with the announce team. AUSTIN ST. AUDE: “Welcome all to Evolution Pro Wrestling! Welcome all to Tuesday Night Evolve! Welcome all to the best wrestling program in the world! This is Evolution Pro Wrestling, an Evolution in the wrestling business. And after last week how many of you did what I said? And came right back again.” COLT COX: “Last week was just the foundation and now every single week builds on our dominance, shows why we are the best company around here. Last week we had a full card this week we come to you with two matches. The first is one for the guy’s who need to prove themselves in a tag match.” AUSTIN ST. AUDE: “That’s true because it’ll be Marcus Hister and Bret Battery versus the team of Jack’s, Jack Johnson and Jack Prowler. You know that Sinclair is watching and after last week’s booting of Don Hairston one has to wonder how these guys will all do.” COLT COX: “But the real big match tonight is in our main event as the two men that impressed most last week will fight. On one side you have Cody X and Paul Defias who thus far haven’t shown any turmoil against the match made in heaven of our ‘Evolve Champion’ Chris Armstrong and Lance Hollywood. If Defias could get a win this week, you’d have to think it would make him the Number One Contender.” AUSTIN ST. AUDE: “But right now we are going to head to the ring where David Sinclair is standing by with our newest acquisition Chad Munroe!” The fans clap as we head to the ring where David Sinclair, of course with tOWLette is standing in the ring with Chad Munroe as well as Matt Kluge. Both men are standing on different sides of Sinclair as he raises a microphone to his mouth. DAVID SINCLAIR: “Everyone quiet down because I have a very special welcome for this man here in the ring. He’s the man who I recently acquired to wrestle here in EPW. This man was undefeated, this man has never lost. See last week we trimmed the fat and now we are adding the muscle. And since Matt Kluge’s guy last week was cut, well he will be the new Mentor, of Chad Munroe.” The two men look at each other as Sinclair steps back allowing the two to exchange words before giving a handshake though after Kluge assaulted his former protégé one has to wonder what kind of trust levels there really are. Munroe asks for the mic and with a smirk Sinclair hands it over. CHAD MUNROE: “I came here for one reason because I wasn’t getting the type of respect I deserved. I was crushing people in one minute tops there and they still treated me like I was dirt. So I come here and now I will make them regret letting me walk away. It’s going to be only a matter of time before I become the ‘Evolve Champion’. I’m unstoppable so they will regret not respecting me!” Munroe lowers the microphone while David Sinclair has a big smirk realizing he may have struck gold with this one as the three of them head out of the ring as the segment ends. ASSAMA AL-MANHARI: "I'm here now with "The Mystery" Marcus Hister. Marcus, last Thursday you lost against Aspen on Revolt. My question is, what are the consequences of this lost to EPW?" MARCUS HISTER: "This is a great lost to EPW, but not for the reasons you may think. Let me ask you, do you remember when all the other six superstars where attacking the GIW roster from behind?" ASSAMA AL-MANHARI: "Y-yes." MARCUS HISTER: "Well, I was always kept out of with, because I didn't really care about the ratings and all this crap about being the dominant show. But the rest did, so i thought they would come as their great EPW team to fight Aspen, but that didn't happen. That's why it is a big lost for EPW, because we found out that six out of seven superstars of the EPW roster are cowards. Chris Armstrong, Paul Defias, Jack Johnson, Jack Prowler and, specially you, Bret Battery, you better prove that this company is better. And if you don't, then please,let me handle the rest of it alone. ASSAMA AL-MANHARI: "Ok, great words from Marcus, but before we go, i want to ask you a couple other questions, the first one being: Can you tell us anything about your past? People at home want to know you a little better. MARCUS HISTER: "There's a reason for my nickname, so, no. Well, then bye." With that Marcus left Assama alone. ASSAMA AL-MANHARI: "Well, I guess that's all for today, I still had more two questions though. Oh, well." The scene fades with an sad Assama looking at the questions paper. It’s time for the first contest of the night as we head to the stage where soon “Broken Dreams” by Shaman's Harvest hits and out from the back walking with his Mentor is none other then Bret Battery. Laura Seton is not far behind him clapping her hands together showing her support for her protégé who heads down to the ring nodding he has the fans at his back tonight as he heads into the ring, Laura Seton joins him inside. As the two begin to talk we cut to the entrance ramp as “The Man Who Sold The World” by Nirvana now hits and it’s Marcus Hister who now walks out from the back. Standing not far behind him is Bryson Chandler the two’s connection really unknown though with Hister’s unpredictable ways, one has to wonder how well Chandler can do. Both of them head into the ring Marcus Hister heading right to Battery, while Laura and Bryson begin to chat about the match at hand. COLT COX: “There is the man that disgraced EPW on Revolt this past week, tapped out to Aspen Chaud!” AUSTIN ST. AUDE: “I happen to think he did pretty well, he was dominating her for a long time. Besides he probably just got distracted…she’s pretty hot.” COLT COX: “Something tells me he’s not the type to get distracted by such a thing. But we’ll see how he and Bret Battery can do tonight.” As the four in the ring chat it up it’s soon “Pretty Handsome Awkward” by The Used that hits the ring. From the back walks Celina who then waits as her ‘student’ Jack Prowler walks out from the back. The two stand beside each other then nod and head to the ring with Celina trailing him the whole time. He heads up the steps and into the ring where he steps in and stands in the corner while Celina talks with him about their opponents. "Invincible" by Adelitas Way hits and the last man out is Jack Johnson as immediately two cannons shoot off money into the crowd as he heads down to the ring with that huge smirk while his mentor Mike Pace walks behind him, it’s evident that the two are not exactly on great terms. COLT COX: “This team right here is the team to beat in my eyes.” Johnson heads up the steps and the four are all in the ring as Laura and Bryson head out, Mike Pace already sat back against the barricade with the crowd as Celina jumps down last as the four stand in the ring. These four all want to make a name for themselves and as the referee tells them to get to the corner’s, none of them want to go. They each argue with their respected teammates before Prowler runs across the ring and pops Battery as the referee calls for the bell and Johnson and Hister are forced to the outside. DING DING DING! Prowler pummels him into the corner as the fans boo him; this time Battery has the crowd at his back. Prowler crushes him into a sitting position in the corner as he turns with a grin to the crowd taunting them a bit. Celina yells at him to focus as he turns around and runs at Battery full steam only for Bret to slide out of the ring sending Prowler’s knee attempt into the turnbuckle. Prowler bounces back holding his knee as Battery fires up and crushes him down with a clothesline. The fans cheer as Jack gets up only to be caught with another big clothesline. Now Prowler takes a bit longer to get up as Bret calls for him to get up, getting some momentum. When he’s up he goes for a German Suplex but Prowler elbows out of it, and with Battery holding his head he runs off the ropes and nails him with a Running High Knee. Bret falls to the ground and tries for a tag but Prowler drags him to his own corner tagging in Jack Johnson to get him some. Prowler holds Battery in place for Johnson to connect with a huge slap to the face. The fans boo as Johnson smirks and with Bret falling back a bit he connects with a Scoop Slam. He goes for the first pin attempt of the match 1…Kick Out! Not near enough damage done to him yet as Johnson hops up yelling at Bret to get up. As he’s about up Johnson takes a big swing at him only for Battery to duck it and hit a Knee Facebuster! The fans cheer as he hops up turning to his partner on his knees walking over and tagging in Marcus Hister! Jack Johnson is also trying to get to Prowler but Hister is in quickly and pulls him away. He drags him to the center of the ring. Johnson turns into him going for a kick only for Hister to catch his leg and then connect with an impressive Wheel Kick! The fans cheer him on as he heads to the top rope already. His mentor Bryson Chandler tells him not to do it but he does it anyways, jumping off for a Diving Elbow but at the last second Johnson is able to roll out of the way. COLT COX: “That’s why you listen to your mentor!” Hister holds up favoring his elbow as Johnson is on his knees starting to head for his corner. Marcus gets up still holding his arm and grabs Johnson spinning him onto his back. Only able to hold him with one arm it allows Johnson to reach up and poke Marcus right in the eyes! With Marcus falling back in pain Jack reaches up and tags in the other Jack as Prowler explodes into the ring. He hits a Scoop Powerslam to the momentarily blinded Hister. He smirks to the crowd stopping to look back at his Mentor with a grin as she tries to insist he get back to the action. He sets up in the corner waiting as Marcus gets to his feet. When he gets up he runs up for a Running Bulldog! But instead Hister is able to toss him away sending him to the mat hard. The fans are shocked as Hister heads straight to the top rope again. He looks out at the crowd about to jump off when Celina hops up onto the ring apron. Hister looks down at her yelling at her to get off, he’s not one to be scared to kick her off. But she soon jumps down as Prowler is able to get up to his feet and run over and tag in Johnson. AUSTIN ST. AUDE: “Didn’t take Celina for someone that would do such a thing, but she looked good doing it.” Hister notices this too late as Johnson has already made his way over to him. The fans boo wanting to see Hister hit one of his high flying moves. But they are happy when Hister is able to kick Johnson back and then jump off hitting a Seated Senton type of move into a pin 1…2…Kick Out! As he is pushed off he bounces right into Battery who tags in and jumps into the ring. Battery grabs Johnson who has just gotten up and hit’s a German Suplex. Battery hops up and seems to almost fume as Jack gets up and walks right into a Spinebuster! This seems like it’s about over but out of no where Hister tags himself in. The referee forces him out of the ring as Hister without the referee able to see runs over and kicks Prowler off the ring apron. He turns his attention back onto Johnson who has gotten up and walks right into the ‘Mystery's Darkness’ an RKO! The fans know it’s over now as Celina helps Prowler up to try to get back in but it’s to late 1…2…3! Battery and Hister win. COLT COX: “I guess he made up for the whole loss on Revolt proved he’s plenty capable.” AUSTIN ST. AUDE: “Though you can’t say that Battery didn’t help a lot.” The fans boo Hister’s actions really only wanting to see Battery win. Hister slides out of the ring and joins his mentor Bryson Chandler who pats him on the back while Bret Battery doesn’t seem overly happy about the win, having wanted the pin himself. He steps down to Laura Seton who tries to console him about what just happened. Back in the ring Jack Johnson is basically out cold. COLT COX: “Things are starting to shape around here.” Prowler stands over him his second loss in two weeks and this time he wasn’t even part of the finish. He seems about to attack when Celina pulls him away, taking him to the back. Mike Pace is on the outside shrugging, seeming less then worried about his protégé here tonight. The match was not without a bit of controversy. WINNERS: MARCUS HISTER & BRET BATTERY After the match is over, the camera switches backstage, where Matt Kluge and Chad Munroe are standing in a plaza area. Chad and Kluge begin to have a conversation. CHAD MUNROE: “Mr. Kluge, I can assure you that I am a much more qualified professional wrestler than Don Hairston ever was. Having me is an upgrade.” Kluge snickers. MATT KLUGE: “Honestly Chad, I don’t think you realize what you are saying. Don Hairston was nothing… everyone is better than nothing. Except Blake Beckett… but that’s beside the point. How about you hit the gym and call me later, you look like a skinny little dweeb.” Chad Munroe chuckles to himself, and looks around at the commons area surrounding him. He looks angry, but holds back his temper. CHAD MUNROE: “Yes… sir.” Chad Munroe walks out of the camera’s view as we cut to commercial. Chris Armstrong can be seen backstage on a chair, thinking about his upcoming match. Besides him are some dumbbells which he was obviously using to work out and train for his match. There’s a knock on the door, Chris Armstrong says nothing. The door is kicked opened by none other than Lance Hollywood. Lance Hollywood comes up to Chris Armstrong and slaps him across the face. Chris Armstrong immediately gets angry and looks like he wants to retaliate. However, Lance Hollywood doesn’t even flinch and looks at Chris with those intimidating brown eyes and speaks. As he does, Chris Armstrong looks even more frustrated. LANCE HOLLYWOOD: "If you are as to even lay a hand on me, I’ll report you to Sinclair and you’ll be gone within a few seconds. Do you think I’m kidding? C’mon, I dare you to hit me. Watch your entire career go up in flames. Oh, and you can forget about Revolt… they don’t want you there anymore. Now… when I knock you open the door and you bow and you greet me with a simple, “Hello, MENTOR.” It doesn’t get any easier than that. You think that because you’re a superstar, you think you can do whatever you want? You think that because you have money you can do whatever you want? Well, let me give you a damn wake-up call! I’m the one who sets the rules here, not you! You’re just a little grunt! You’re MY grunt! You have to do everything that I say! Got it? Good. (Glances at dumbbells) Oh you’re kidding me. So you’ve stuck with your routine of using dumbbells? Well son, you’re much stupider than I thought you were. You might as well kill yourself because you’re good for nothing. There’s a reason why there’s a bunch of damn gyms built everywhere - it’s so meatheads like you can go and USE them. You don’t scare me, nothing scares me. Chris Armstrong shakes his head and appears to be holding in his anger. He quietly takes a seat and tries to be the bigger man by just agreeing. Lance Hollywood stares at Chris Armstrong with cold eyes and slaps him across the face. LANCE HOLLYWOOD: "What? Cat got your tongue? You’re supposed to say, “Yes, sir.” CHRIS ARMSTRONG: "Yes…Sir." LANCE HOLLYWOOD: "Good. Now, go to the limo and pick up my bags. You have a lot of learning to do and you sure as hell won’t learn anything by sitting on your ass all day. Go ahead. Chris Armstrong leaves and slams the door. LANCE HOLLYWOOD: "Damn no good know-it-all kids." Chris Armstrong comes out to his music, “I Get Money” by 50 cent. He looks somewhat frustrated as his mentor, Lance Hollywood basically pushes him to the side and walks down the ramp, eventually ending up on the side of the ring. Chris Armstrong shakes his head and looks like he’s ready to destroy Lance. Chris Armstrong goes up the steel steps, enters the ring and doesn’t even bother to pose to the crowd - he makes it clear that he’s upset and that he’s not happy with his mentor. Lance Hollywood begins to yell at Chris Armstrong. Armstrong, at first, tries to ignore it. COLT COX: "Looks like the tension between Chris Armstrong and Lance Hollywood continues to build. I wonder why, however…why is Lance so mad at Chris Armstrong? I mean, Chris has been doing fantastic and he is the Evolve champion! Look at that belt!” AUSTIN ST. AUDE: "Maybe it‘s just tough love, you never know. Anyway, looks like we‘re waiting on Chris Armstrong‘s opponent, Paul Defias.” After a small pause and some tension building up between Lance Hollywood and Chris Armstrong, “Sing for the Moment by Eminem” manages to break the noise. However, the true story here is between Armstrong and Hollywood. The two begin to yell at each other. Armstrong looks like he doesn’t even think that he can trust Hollywood. Paul Defias walks down the ramp along with Cody X who tries to get him pumped up for the match. Paul Defias looks ready to go! Lance Hollywood goes up to Cody X and begins to point at both Paul and Chris Armstrong and he also starts yelling at those two. This, for Chris Armstrong, is slightly reassuring…since at least Chris knows that Hollywood is on his side. The bell sounds. AUSTIN ST. AUDE: “I‘m convinced that Lance Hollywood woke up on the wrong side of the bed this morning. I mean look at him, he‘s yelling at everybody for practically no reason! Do you think he‘s really fit for this job, he seems to be a loose cannon.” COLT COX: “Well who knows! Behind the scenes, he may be helping Chris majorly. With that type of attitude, I’m sure Chris Armstrong is bound to learn a thing or two.” Paul Defias tries to take Chris Armstrong by surprise by rolling him up quickly but to no avail, a two count. Chris Armstrong tries to retaliate by hitting a big boot to Paul Defias but Defias is much too quick! He baseball slides in the ring, avoiding the big boot and then climbs on top of the back of Chris Armstrong and locks in a chokehold! Chris Armstrong tries to fight out of it but before he even can, Paul Defias makes the situation more complicated by somehow turning this into a pinning maneuver similar to a hurricarana! Chris Armstrong barely kicks out, quickly gets up and hit’s a devastating clothesline. Chris quickly picks up Paul Defias and tries to end the match right there and then by hitting vertebreaker but Paul Defias counters it and turns it into an arm drag that sends Chris Armstrong sliding off to the outside of the ring! COLT COX: “The Evolve champion looks like he’s in trouble early in this match.” AUSTIN ST. AUDE: "This is true, to me it looks like he‘s off his game. He needs to step it up or Paul Defias is going to step all over him!” COLT COX: “That was hilarious!” AUSTIN ST. AUDE: “Was it?“ COLT COX: “Yeah, I’m definitely writing that one down!” Chris Armstrong tries to climb back into the ring but Paul Defias goes ahead and hit’s a suicide dive! However, Chris Armstrong catches Paul Defias and turns it into a variation of a power bomb, similar to his finishing move! Chris sluggishly enters the ring at a two count and looks on as Paul Defias is counted out. Lance Hollywood starts calling Chris a chicken and quote on quote, “A pathetic shortcut taker”. This angers Chris Armstrong and compels him to go to the outside, pick up Paul Defias and put him back in the ring. Paul Defias gets up as Chris Armstrong is on the apron. Paul Defias tries to bounce off the ropes to make Armstrong lose his balance so he falls to the outside but Chris Armstrong gets a handful of Defias’ hair. AUSTIN ST. AUDE: “Chris Armstrong has a handful of hair and the referee is saying nothing. Is this strange to any of you?” COLT COX: “Maybe the referee is scared of Chris. He IS the Evolve champion, afterall.” Chris Armstrong Irish whips Defias to the turnbuckle. Defias puts his feet up ad hits Armstrong right on the face. Paul Defias goes up to the turnbuckle and hit’s a flying cross body! The crowd is cheering! 1...2...kick out! Chris Armstrong remains on the floor, barely being able to get up and looks rather dazed. Paul Defias goes back up to the turnbuckle and he looks like he’s going to go for another flying cross body. Chris Armstrong feigns injury. Lance Hollywood begins yelling at Chris Armstrong to stop being such a big baby and to get up. The referee looks to try and care for Chris Armstrong but Lance Hollywood quickly gets on the apron and tries to push off Paul Defias. Paul Defias fights him off by kicking him in the head. Lance Hollywood falls to the floor and Cody X starts talking trash to Lance Hollywood for attempting such cheap moves. Paul Defias looks at the two whilst Chris Armstrong magically is healed and goes up to the turnbuckle and hits Paul Defias across the face. Chris Armstrong looks like he ahs intentions of using his top rope power bomb but Paul Defias fights back again and again, not wanting to give up! Chris Armstrong is kicked on the face and sent further from Paul Defias. Paul Defias goes for some sort of flying leg scissors take down but Chris Armstrong catches him and converts this into a power bomb! The crowd cheers at first at awe of the move but then realize that Chris Armstrong is going to steal this one. 1.…2.… KICK OUT! COLT COX: “What in the world?! There’s no way Paul Defias kicked out of that!” AUSTIN ST. AUDE: “It looks like things aren‘t going to go down that way and Lance Hollywood must be furious as soon as he realizes that Paul kicked out of that!” Chris Armstrong’s eyes widen and he doesn’t appear to be able to believe what he’s seeing. Chris goes for another cover but suddenly, Paul Defias locks in the gogoplata with the rest of his energy! The crowd begins stomping their feet on the floor several times, creating a shaky atmosphere. Chris Armstrong looks like he’s in pain and puts his hand up as if he’s going to give up. Cody X goes back to his corner and Lance Hollywood sees that Chris Armstrong is in trouble. Lance Hollywood gets on the apron again and starts yelling at the ref. The referee diverts his attention to Lance Hollywood and commands him to get back to the outside. Lance Hollywood grabs the referee by the shirt and drags him to the outside, injuring himself in the process. Chris Armstrong begins to tap out! Paul Defias looks like he slowly loses energy the more he keeps his move locked in and eventually releases the hold. Paul Defias drags himself to the outside and punches Lance Hollywood in the face, sending him straight to the floor. Paul Defias tries to revive the referee but it doesn’t look like it’s working. Paul Defias goes back into the ring. Chris Armstrong still looks dazed and lazily tries to kick Paul Defias in the gut. Paul Defias catches the leg of Chris Armstrong. In an attempt to imitate Paul, Chris goes for an énziguri. Paul Defias dodges the second foot and then locks in the ankle lock! COLT COX: “Chris Armstrong‘s going to tap here, but why does it even matter?! The referee isn‘t even in the ring or awake for that matter.” Chris Armstrong begins to yell in pain and tries to drag himself to the ropes but after realizing that it’s no use, he begins to tap out again. Paul Defias looks back at the referee who looks like he was just shot twenty times in the gut and refuses to get up. Paul Defias releases the hold. Paul Defias loses some of his patience and screams at the ref to get up. The referee shows some signs of movement. Cody X goes up to the ref and tries to help him get up. Meanwhile, Lance Hollywood enters the fray and tries to hit a big boot on Paul Defias! Paul Defias ducks and then hit’s a drop kick to Lance, sending him to the outside once more! Cody X helps the referee in the ring. The referee slowly crawls to where the two competitors are. Paul Defias turns around and is hit by the twist of fate! Chris Armstrong slowly goes for the cover, being unable to even hook in the leg. 1.…2.… kick out! AUSTIN ST. AUDE: “This match has been absolutely crazy! One moment you think Paul Defias is going to win and the other you think Chris Armstrong has got this in the bag! I’ve got to say, I’m VERY impressed by both competitors. Paul Defias is showing heart and Chris is showing why he’s Evolve champion.” COLT COX: “ I couldn’t have said it better myself. “ Chris Armstrong looks more shocked than ever and the crowd begins to cheer, not wanting the match to ever stop. The referee begins to pick himself up. Chris Armstrong suddenly goes into a second gear, and then places Paul Defias on to the top rope and hit’s the Cash Bomb. Chris Armstrong hurts himself in the process. After twenty seconds of both of them laying on the floor, Chris looks like he’s going for the cover. Cody X tells himself that, “It can’t end this way” and so he gets on the turnbuckle this time, using one of Hollywood’s tricks! Cody X begins to argue mindless jargon. It’s been five seconds since Chris Armstrong pinned Paul Defias but there is no count. Eventually, Chris Armstrong tries to get up and then looks like he doesn’t know what to do anymore. Paul Defias, however…does! He kicks one of the knees of Chris Armstrong, sending him on all fours on the mat and then Paul Defias locks in the ankle lock again! For two brief seconds, Armstrong looks like he’s going to tap again and the crowd is going crazy. Lance Hollywood, again however… goes to the apron and punches Paul Defies on the back of the head. Paul Defias turns around and he looks like he’s going to kill Lance. Before he can, however… Chris Armstrong gets up despite being injured from the ankle and hit’s a second twist of fate! Cody X gets off the apron and then goes to confront Lance Hollywood about all of this cheating. Meanwhile, Armstrong puts his near lifeless body on the already lifeless body of Paul Defias. The referee counts. 1...2...3. It’s over. AUSTIN ST. AUDE: “Wow.” COLT COX: “Despite the cheating, I think we witnessed possibly one the greatest matches of Evolve history. I am truly proud to be a part of this brand. ” Cody X turns around and looks angry. He stomps his foot and starts walking back towards the ring to help Paul Defias up but Lance Hollywood hits him form behind and runs to grab the Evolve title belt that Chris Armstrong entered with. Lance Hollywood grabs it, enters the ring and looks at the belt. Then, he spits on Chris Armstrong and starts yelling, “You call that a performance?! You GOT LUCKY! IF IT WASN’T FOR ME, YOU’D BE NOTHING!” Lance Hollywood then puts the belt on Chris Armstrong and begins to exit the ring. However, he seems like he has some unfinished business. He picks up Paul Defias and tries to hit a spine buster but Paul Defias turns it into a drop kick sending Lance Hollywood flying over the top rope. Paul Defias knows what just happened and helps Chris Armstrong up. Chris doesn’t mind Paul and begins to smile and then pats Paul Defias on the back for a good showing despite the controversy. WINNER: CHRIS ARMSTRONG We come back after a short commercial as both members of the main event stand by with the rest of the Evolve Stars who are lined up in the ring with David Sinclair standing in front of them. He has a microphone while the rest of them sit there with grins on their faces. DAVID SINCLAIR: “First of all I must say that all of you impressed me tonight, but that's not what we are here to address. Two weeks ago on that other show we were called out by a girl named...Aspen Chaud. And then last week we watched as one of our own Marcus Hister was defeated by her...” David looks back at Marcus looking none to pleased about the actions of his star on that show, however he looks back showing little emotion. DAVID SINCLAIR: “But regardless of what she says I have a proposition for her that I think she can't refuse. Since she and her second rate show thinks that they are so high and mighty, why don't we come to their show at 'Unscripted' and have our best wrestle her, to see who really is the best.” The fans cheer his decision and assertiveness as all the wrestlers throw themselves out there, stating that they are the best. They are about to fight when suddenly there is a loud murmur in the crowd as standing on the ramp is Joshua Acquin! DAVID SINCLAIR: “What the hell are you doing here?” Sinclair stares down at him as his Evolve stars stand behind him yelling down at Joshua Acquin. Joshua however has a microphone that he raises to his mouth. JOSHUA ACQUIN: “This past week on Revolt, I was suspended for the next show for trying to fight one of your guys backstage. And since I won't be busy Thursday, I thought I'd lay down two different offers. One for 'Unscripted' in which you'd put three of your guys against me and two guys from GIW!” Sinclair listens from the ring as Battery walks forward telling him that he wants in, these two having a long history for sure as Sinclair nods and tells him to get back in line. JOSHUA ACQUIN: “But here's what I have in mind for next week. Why don't you let me come wrestle here. And it'll be me versus your 'Evolve Champion' Chris Armstrong!” The fans cheer what is a pretty big blockbuster match made for next week as Chris Armstrong walks up with David Sinclair with his belt, as Sinclair makes the decision for him. DAVID SINCLAIR: “HE ACCEPTS! Now you best get out of here before we make you get out of here.” The fans cheer as Joshua Acquin stares down at the men in the ring all of them glaring down at them as Joshua backs away towards the back as the show comes to a close.
