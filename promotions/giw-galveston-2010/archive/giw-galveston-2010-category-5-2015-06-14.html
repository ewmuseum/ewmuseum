GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS CATEGORY 5 6/14/15 FROM STEWART BEACH IN GALVESTON, TEXAS BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! Fireworks stream into the cloudy sky above Stewart Beach on Galveston Island. For a moment, the fans thought that the event would need to be moved indoors at Kauffman Arena across the street, but the weather decided that this show was just too great to ruin. As the blue and white fireworks explode into tiny bits of light far overhead, the crowd begins to go wild. We see signs such as “Not my Superman!”, “I am the Sole Survivor”, and “Bronxy V is Worth My Time!” We cut down to the announcers at ringside. SARAH SHARP: "Ladies and gentlemen, thank you for joining here for this very special iPPV event – Category 5.
