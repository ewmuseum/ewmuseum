GIW — Card

Revolt, 9 June 2011

9 June 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GALVESTON ISLAND WRESTLING PRESENTS: T H U R S D A Y N I G H T R E V O L T BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! The fans ready for a big night of action are all at the edge of their seats as the opening pyro goes off in the Complex sending them into an uproar of cheers. However almost immediately they are shut down as "Now I'm That Bitch" by Livvi Franc hits and their cheers transform to boos as out from the back wearing a very nice looking work suit, with heels and carrying a briefcase is Aspen Chaud. Coming alone is a big surprise in itself but her rather work like get up is just as weird. RILEY STEVENS: “Well what could this possibly be about?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Aspen and I are good friends; she told me that she had some big news for us.” RILEY STEVENS: “Your friends with this witch?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Of course.” Aspen walks up the steps, picking up a microphone on her way into the ring. Holding the briefcase at her side, she waits for the crowd to die down before she raises the microphone to her mouth. ASPEN CHAUD: “Listen up, people I have an announcement to make! But, to make this announcement I need Blake Beckett out here.” The crowd give a mixed reactions as Blake Beckett’s theme hits and out from the back comes Blake himself chuckling a bit as he looks on at Aspen, heading down confidently to the ring with a mic in hand he steps into the ring quickly talking. BLAKE BECKETT: “If this is about that kiss you had to give that guy last week, I’m not responsible for any diseases you may have caught.” Blake chuckles to himself as Aspen simply shakes her head seeming to be able to let that one go right through her as she raises the mic up. ASPEN CHAUD: “You know Blake usually I’d give you an earful for that. But simply put, I am in a very good mood. I have had some of my best people working on some things for me. People that know how to get information right under people’s feet. You see Blake, we found out some things about GIW. They told me that rather then making money ever since we were taken off of ESPN2, we have been losing TONS of it.” Blake raises an eye brow seeming a bit confused as to how she could have gotten this information as she smiles and pulls out a paper out of her pocket. ASPEN CHAUD: “And according to this paper here, the lease on this Complex is about to run out after next week. When exactly were you planning to tell these people that?” Quickly Blake swipes away the paper, to see if she’s bluffing about it all. Reading it over, he mouths “How did you get this?” throwing it to the ground in a bit of anger. ASPEN CHAUD: “So, since your putting yourself in a bit of debt. In this briefcase is enough cash to get you back all that you have lost plus a little more. All you have to do is sign over GIW…to me!” A few gasps can be heard from the crowd, many of which have been loyal fans from the start. They have basically been given a kick to the stomach, not really sure how to process this all. BLAKE BECKETT: “Sell this company to you? Really? You thought you could honestly buy this place away from me. We have the most loyal fans in the world. Call it a rough spot or whatever you’d like. But this company will be fine, so as for your offer I reject it.” Pushing away the briefcase Aspen had been holding in front of him, Blake rolls out of the ring heading to the back. Aspen seems angry only for a second before a smile goes over her face realizing she’d just humiliated him. RILEY STEVENS: “Wow, I am not even sure what to say. I had no clue how bad it was here in GIW.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “She’d probably do a better job with it any how.” RILEY STEVENS: “Regardless this surely will change some things. We’ll be right back after these commercial breaks with a Six Man Tag match, stay tuned for the GIW North Beach Championship match as well as the huge main event with Matt Kluge and Tony Miranda versus Aspen Chaud and Wake Walker.” We head to a break on the two announcers then cutting to Aspen heading out of the ring. QUOTE The match begins with Mardukas, Scar, and INK on one side of the ring, and Bludge, Hendricks, and Rejinaldo on the other side. The teams talk together to decide who would enter the match first, and they decide on Mardukas and Bludge. They lock up, Mardukas easily gains the upper hand. He throws Herman to the ground and locks in a chin lock. Mardukas pulls him to the corner and tags in Johnny Scar, and the two double team briefly before the referee breaks them up. Scar kicks Herman in the back several times before pulling him up for a Northern Lights Suplex. The move shakes the ring and Scar goes for the pin, but it is a quick kick out. Scar pulls Herman up and whips him to the ropes, but Herman comes back with a head of steam and football tackle that lays Scar out. Herman makes the tag to Hendricks. RILEY STEVENS: “Bludge with an unorthodox move there, but it damn sure got the job done!” Hendricks goes right to work on Scar, he pulls him up and connects with a back suplex, followed by a chinlock. Scar uses the momentum of the crowd to escape from it with elbows to the gut galore. Scar hit’s a DDT on Hendricks. Mardukas and INK run into the ring and knock Bludge and Rejinaldo off of the apron. Scar motions to the crowd, then bounces off the ropes with a spring board moonsault! 1...2...3!! WINNERS: HANK MARDUKAS, JOHNNY SCAR, & TYLER INK Heading to the back we see a very focused Matt Kluge standing next to Sarah Sharp who as usual is holding a microphone ready to interview him. SARAH SHARP: “Kluge, I let of the talk of late has been about Wake’s brutal beating of Rydell and yourself back at ‘Breakdown’. Tonight you have him in a tag match, your thoughts?” Sarah holds the mic over to Matt who doesn’t exactly seem too excited about the mention of Walker, more then likely unable to wait to get his hands on him. MATT KLUGE: “Honestly I can’t wait to fight him tonight. He took out my best friend. So, I plan on ending his little grand return before it can even start. Walker’s always had a big head, always thought he was something great. Now I am no Doctor, but I am just sending a message to the hospital now...I hope you have some of his blood type in stock. Cause, when I’m done with him, he’ll be lucky if he has any left.” Kluge pushes past her ending this interview a little short, as Sarah shrugs her shoulders as the segment ends. ==== Heading back to the ring already standing there, despite not wrestling until a bit later is Trent Lost with his ‘GIW North Beach Championship’ over his shoulder with a microphone in hand preparing to talk, the crowd cheering him on. TRENT LOST: “You know last week I lost a chance at the ‘Sole Survivor’ match. I’m not going to blame it on Aspen because it was my fault I took my eyes of the prize. But it got me thinking…we don’t have a Number One Contender…we don’t have someone ready for a title shot right here and right now…That is except for me. I don’t feel like anyone is more qualified then myself. You see, because I beat…Alioth Starre! So I am asking him to come out…so we can settle who really is the best…at ‘Category 5’.” The crowd cheers, wanting to see this match and after a bit of a wait. “Subterraneans” by David Bowie hits to a very mixed bag of reactions from the crowd as walking out onto the top of the ramp with a microphone is Alioth, who had caused quite a turn from his fans this week. ALIOTH STARRE: “Your still talking about the time I gave you that belt? In case you missed it. You couldn’t beat me on my worst day…So…no.” Starre lowers the microphone and begins to turn around to the boo’s of the crowd. But Lost talking stops him in his tracks. TRENT LOST: “The only person that thinks you lost that match on purpose is you. I beat you…fair and square! Frankly, I think you’re worried I’ll do it again.” These words make Alioth turn around rather quickly, with anger in his eyes. ALIOTH STARRE: “You think I am worried about you? The reason I am not accepting your challenge is just because of that…I want a challenge. Something…you are not.” Starre heads immediately to the back, wasting no more time with this as Trent looks out at the crowd who are booing Alioth’s actions. Trent shakes his head, handing the mic out realizing if Alioth wouldn’t accept he’d have to take it by himself. RILEY STEVENS: “I can’t help but agree with him. He did defeat Alioth and to me, he’s next in line.” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “He should be a bit worried about his title defense here tonight.” Trent exits the ring, the crowd patting him on the back as heads towards the back as we head to a break. QUOTE After cutting back from commercial break, we see Jamal Jackson standing in the center of the ring with his microphone in hand. “I Don’t Wanna Stop” by Ozzy Osborne begins to play over the speakers and the fans begin to boo. Mr. Black walks out of the backstage area and onto the ramp. JAMAL JACKSON: “Now entering the ring, from Birmingham, Alabama… I mean England… Mr. Black!” Black slides into the ring and waits for Cross. Jamal introduces Mark, and Mark begins to make his way down the ring. When he arrives, the two men lock up and the bell rings. Cross and Black are both very strong, but Black somehow finds a way to get the upper hand, He hip tosses Cross onto the ground and puts him in a headlock. Cross fights to stand up, but Black wont let go of the hold. Cross offers up several elbows to the gut that finally get the job done, then he goes straight to good stuff with a several punches that send Black fleeing to the corner of the ring. Black regains himself in the corner, Cross waits in the center of the ring. Black slowly moves forward and the two lock up again. Cross gets the upper hand this time, whipping Black into the corner again. He does a few more shoulder blocks before pulling him up and out, only to slam him back down with an exploder suplex that shakes the ring. He goes for a pin, Black kicks out at one. Cross pulls him up and locks in an arm wrench, but Black flips out of it and hit’s a back suplex on Cross. Black pulls Cross up and whips him into the corner. Black charges into a boot by Cross. Cross charges with a clothesline on Black, which is countered as well. Black hits DDT that lays cross out. RILEY STEVENS: “This has been a good match so far with two very evenly matched opponents. Cross may have the experience factor, but will ring rust play a role?” Black pulls Cross up and goes for a some sort of power bomb, but Cross would have none of that! He flips him up and onto his back and begins to stomp him on the mat. Cross pulls him up and locks in a dragon sleeper, but Black gets his foot on the rope midway through the hold. The ref tells Cross to break the hold, and he does. Cross goes back to the other side of the ring, and when he turns around, Black is up and hit’s a perfectly placed Big Boot that sends Cross to the floor. Black looks at Cross, then to the turnbuckle. Black make the decision to go to the top rope. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “That boy isn’t exactly a lightweight. We might need to get Dr. House out here with the stretcher Mr. Black when he busts his ass on the mat.” Black taunts to the fans and to Mark Cross, who is groggy in the center of the ring. Black launches himself with a cross body aimed right for Cross, and it connects… but Cross rolls right through it! He pulls Black up onto his shoulders and hits his finisher, the Go 2 Sleep! 1..2...3~! The match is over. Black slides out of the ring and Cross gets his hand raised, victorious, as we cut to commercial. WINNER: MARK CROSS We cut backstage to Alex Castellanos’ locker room. Alex is dressed in his red ring attire and is sitting on the bench in the center of the locker room. Sarah Sharp is behind him rubbing his shoulders. Alex has his head down, trying to concentrate, but Sarah was hell bent on talking. SARAH SHARP: “Tonight is your night baby, you can win that GIW North Beach Championship and you can wear that gold with you everywhere. Oh, and thank you for being civil with Trent Lost last week, I really appreciate it. Shows your character.” Sarah moves over beside Alex on the bench and grabs his hand, but he is isn’t ready to talk. She smiles and brushes some hair out of her face before looking up into the corner of the ceiling and beginning to talk again. SARAH SHARP: “After all you have done, all you have worked towards, tonight is your night. You have something that Hister and Lost don’t have on their side; fate. Fate, Alex, that is why you are going to win this match tonight. That’s why you are going to decapitate Trent with that Golden Arrow. That’s why you are going to be the North Beach Champion.” She looks back towards him and nudges his arm. She laughs some more and scoots just a little bit closer to him, trying to get him to acknowledge her. SARAH SHARP: “Do you remember what Lost did to you? How he took you out without warning… sidelined you for weeks, and now he thinks he can walk around and be the good guy… come on now, tonight you are going to show him that you are the…” Alex catches her mid sentence with a big kiss. Sarah is pleased, but very surprised. Alex stands up and extends his hand to Sarah and motions his head towards the door. The two exit holding hands. The North Beach Championship match is up next, stay tuned! QUOTE We return from commercial break to see the GIW ring announcer, Jamal Jackson, in the ring waiting to begin the match. We hear the three bells go off, and the crowd goes wild. They know what match is up next. “Ugly” by the Exies begins to play inside the Seawall Boulevard Entertainment Complex and the place erupts in cheers. From the back, Alex Castellanos… with Sarah Sharp by his side… begins to walk down the ramp making the motion of a belt around his wait. JAMAL JACKSON: “Now entering the ring, weighing in at two hundred and twelve pounds… from Galveston, Texas by way of Cardiff, Wales… ALEX CASTELLANOS!” Alex climbs up the ring and holds the rope for Sarah who enters behind him. The two retreat to the corner to wait for their opponents. Next up, “Under the Knife” by Rise Against blasts through the speakers and boo’s echo through the arena. Marcus Hister begins to walk down the ramp, totally in the zone and not paying attention to any of the fans. JAMAL JACKSON: “Now entering the ring, weighing in at two hundred and thirty-one pounds… hailing from Portugal… a member of ‘The Transcendence’… MARCUS HISTER!!” Hister slides in the ring and stares down Castellanos from the other side. He chuckles to himself and climbs up onto the turnbuckle and screams to the fans “I WILL BE CHAMPION”. More boos echo through the arena. Hister chooses the corner directly opposite of Alex as “Down” by Curse Icon begins to play and Trent Lost, belt around his waist, stands at the top of the ramp. JAMAL JACKSON: “And the champion, weighing in at two hundred and twenty-three pounds. From Corpus Christi, Texas… the ‘Lost Cause Crusader’ and North Beach Champion… TRENT LOST!” Trent Lost slides in the ring and unbuttons the belt from his waist. He raises it in the air and then hands it to the referee who then shows it to the crowd and then hands it to Jamal outside of the ring. The bell sounds three times, and then the match begins. RILEY STEVENS: “Here we go ladies and gentlemen. Marcus, Alex, and Trent are ready to do battle for the North Beach Championship. Honestly, I don’t see a favorite.” A stare down ensues between the three men, but Lost breaks the spell by launching his foot into Hister’s gut, sending him to his knees. Lost moves back, then connects with a karate style kick to the side of Hister’s head. Seeing an open point, Alex charges with a clothesline, but Lost ducks it and sends Alex plummeting out of the ring with a standing dropkick. Lost focuses on Hister again, he goes to pick Hister up, but Hister shoves a thumb into his eye. Trent leans into the corner in pain, and Hister introduces his shoulder to Trent’s gut. He does that a few more times, then whips Lost onto the ground. Hister climbs up the turnbuckle and taunts to the crowd, and out of nowhere Alex Castellanos jumps onto the apron and shoves Hister down to the floor with a THUD! Lost is up now, and charges Alex on the apron, but Alex counters with a shoulder to the gut again. Alex enters the ring against a groggy Trent Lost and sets up for the “Golden Arrow” super kick. Lost turns around and DUCKS the finisher! A back Suplex by Lost follows, then a pin fall attempt that stops at two. RILEY STEVENS: “What a great match so far, full of counters! That just shows how even these three men are… but if Alex had hit “The Golden Arrow”, this would be over.” Trent pulls Alex up from the mat and hits another back suplex, followed by a chin lock. From outside, Hister is back on the top rope behind Trent. He takes a risk and dives with a dropkick aimed at Trent’s head… and connects! Trent might be out cold! Hister goes for a pin- 1...2... ALEX breaks up the attempt. Alex and Hister trade punches for a while, then Hister gets the upper hand, he Irish whips Castellanos and when the momentum send him back around, Hister goes for his version of the superkick, but Alex ducks it and counters it into a sleeper hold. Every second Alex tightens the grip on Hister, who is slowly fading. Hister drops down to his knees in the center of the ring, but out of nowhere, Alex breaks the hold. He does the cutthroat taunt and lifts Hister up, and puts him in power bomb position. He lifts up his legs, but the head stays in place… Alex connects with a cradle power bomb he calls “Gods Wrath”. A devastated Hister lays in the center of the ring, motionless. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “HOLY SHIT ON A PICKLE STICK!! Did you see that move? That was amazing! Hister is done… match over… game over… new champ!” Alex rolls Hister over for a pinfall, but Trent drags a motionless Hister out of the ring at the two count. Trent slides in and begins to land rights and lefts on Castellanos before finally bringing him down with a spear, followed by more bar fight style punches on the ground. Castellanos manages to fight his way out of that mess, only to be taken down by another spear by Lost. He goes for a cover…1...2... Kick out! Trent seems annoyed, so he stays on his knees to regain his breath for a moment, then continues on. He pulls Alex up and whips him into the corner. After a running elbow to the face by Lost, he lifts Alex up onto the top rope in an attempt at a spine-jarring superplex. Lost hit’s a few more punches to make sure Alex is disoriented, then throws his arm over for the suplex. He taunts to the crowd and leans back, driving Castellanos into the mat. Alex rolls out of the ring holding his back, and now Trent is all alone. Trent looks at Hister and Castellanos on the ground and panders to the crowd by using “eenie meenie miney mo” to pick between them, He lands on Alex and slides out of the ring to grab him. He pulls Alex up and throws him into the ring steps, further incapacitating him. Next up is Hister. Lost goes hunting and throws Hister into the ring. Trent waits for Hister to get up, then sets him up for the “Lost Cause”, his pump handle slam. Hister was playing opossum! Roll up! 1.…2.………….KICK OUT! Lost rights out of the roll up. Hister stays on the attack just keeping Trent on the ground any way he can. He sets up in the corner to hit his Superkick, but when he begins to go, Castellanos grabs his foot. Hister turns around and stomps Alex’s hand. Trent capitalizes with a German Suplex that turns into an Indian Death Lock! RILEY STEVENS: “That is his finisher, the “Lost in Translation”! Hister has no way of escape now, unless Castellanos breaks him out, but I think his hand is broken!” Hister screams and tries to break out of it. He reaches for the rope, but every time Lost just tightens the grip even more. Out from behind the curtain comes Aspen Chaud… she runs down to the ring and looks into it from the floor, screaming at Trent. Trent breaks the hold and goes to talk to her. She jumps up onto the apron and the two continue to argue. Hister hobbles up, and with his good leg, he pivots and connects with his SUPERKICK! To the back of Lost’s head. Lost is pushed forward, launching Aspen off of the side onto her butt on the concrete floor. Trent is motionless on the ropes. Hister tries for a back Suplex, but Lost holds onto the ropes as hard and tight as he can. Hister begins to pry at his hands, and Lost just does a backwards head butt that send Hister to his back. He whips Hister out of the ring to regain his composure… then from the back… its ALIOTH STARRE! Starre runs down the ring, acting like he was going to get involved. He starts to yell at Trent like Aspen did, Aspen is still on the floor, shocked at what had happened. Alioth turns around and stops yelling, he just smiles. Trent turns around… ALEX CASTELLANOS HIT’S THE GOLDEN ARROW….1.…..2.….3.…. NEW GIW NORTH BEACH CHAMPION! Alioth offers Aspen a hand, when she reaches to grab it, he pulls it up. Aspen is furious now; she chases Alioth up the ramp and grabs him by his arm, twists him around, and slaps him in the face. He turns around and lunges at her, but she is running back to the ring. She slides in a little before Alioth does, and when he gets to her feet, she is behind Marcus Hister. Alioth shakes his head. From behind Alioth. Trent comes in and cleans house. He tosses Alioth out of the ring, then trades a few blows with Hister before tossing him out. He is face to face with Aspen. The two stare down, but Trent shakes his head and walks out of the ring. The scene fades on Trent walking up the ramp, never breaking his eyes from Aspen. WINNER: ALEX CASTELLANOS (New GIW North Beach Champion) After the break we return heading to the backstage area we find Aspen Chaud, standing by with Damien Dunn with a bit of a now angry look on her face. ASPEN CHAUD: “Can you believe Alioth? I gave him a chance to side with me. And to add to that, Blake is still backstage denying that he’s in as much trouble as he really is. He’s a fool…” As she says this, Damien gives her a little nod to look behind her and when she does she finds Blake Beckett standing there. BLAKE BECKETT: “I don’t know exactly how you got the information you did…But you better you’re your mouth shut about it. We don’t need these fans thinking this company is in trouble, now do we?” Aspen has a big smile on her face; the fact he came here made her realize what she’d said had actually struck him deep. ASPEN CHAUD: “Really Blake? You expect me to keep my mouth shut? We’ll see about that.” Aspen grins as she walks by him Damien follows with a grin on his face seeming to be firmly on Aspen’s side in this one as Blake rubs his chin watching her go. QUOTE Back to the ring we go with the fans ready to go as “Pretty Messed Up” by Daisy hits as the fans begin booing as out from the back walking with the big GIW Television title over her shoulder is Pandora. She doesn’t seem to care about the fans much as; she walks out from the back. JAMAL JACKSON: “Now entering the ring, weighing in at one hundred and fifteen pounds… hailing from Quebec, Canada…the GIW Television Champion…PANDORA!!” The crowd boos as Jamal finishes when out of no where running up from behind her is Alioth Starre grabbing her and using the momentum to toss her right into the ring steps. The crowd boos as Alioth glares out at them, lifting up Pandora whom is clearly in pain. He spins and tosses her into the ring, jumping up and into the ring as well. RILEY STEVENS: “What the hell, this blatant blindside attack from Alioth!” As he gets into the ring pulling off his shirt and tossing it to the mat, he screams at the referee to start the match. The referee begins arguing with him, not agreeing to start it with Pandora hurt the way she is. Grabbing him by his collar, Alioth threatens him and he turns asking Pandora if she can go…Unfortunately her ego makes her say yes. As the referee shakes his head then rings the bell. DING DING DING! The crowd boos, as Pandora continues to favor her hurt leg that took the brunt of the shot from the steel steps. Hobbling to her feet using the ropes, Alioth shows no mercy when he kicks her right in the back of the leg sending her down to the mat. Getting on top of her, he begins to throw punches. Pandora gets her leg on the ropes starting a count from the referee 1…2…3…4…Alioth breaks it off just before the five looking up at the referee angrily saying something to him. Though these fans hate Pandora even they seem to be beginning to feel sorry for her. Unfortunately her lack of allies leaves her stranded out here in the ring. Backing up Starre, holds up his arms bringing some big heat from the crowd. RILEY STEVENS: “This is our champion? I am disgusted.” The fans continue to boo him as he walks forward lifting Pandora to her feet, spinning her around smirking right in her face as she throws a big punch that sends him backwards. Grinning he rubs his cheek and as she tries to advance, he clobbers her with a European Uppercut! Fighting off of one leg, she falls to the mat. Alioth bounces off the ropes and then drops a big Fist Drop! The crowd boos as he makes the cover 1…2…He pulls her shoulder up. RILEY STEVENS: “Oh come on just take the win.” Suddenly there is a loud cheer as out from the back comes Trent Lost running towards the ring. Jumping up onto the ring apron, he is stopped by the referee. Trent points out Pandora’s injury, but the referee explains the situation he is in. Alioth grinning puts in the Figure Four Leg Lock right in front of him. Quickly Pandora taps out due to the injury. But as the referee tries to get him off, Starre keeps the hold in. Trent now slides in; going after him but Starre is able to slide out right away. RILEY STEVENS: “Thank God, Trent was here who knows what else he might have done. Our champion has certainly changed and not a change for the better. I for one hope Trent gets his match and takes that belt.” Lost angrily punches the ropes unhappy he couldn’t get a hold of Alioth’s for his heinous actions here tonight. First costing him the belt then hurting Pandora here in this match. As Alioth backs up the ramp EMT’s rush down towards the ring to check on Pandora as Trent and Alioth stare each other down this time, their paths crossing yet again. WINNER: ALIOTH STARRE We cut to La’Sarah Audric’s office where she is standing, talking to man in a GIW T-shirt and headset on. She is wagging her finger in his face and generally being bossy, but what else is new? LA’SARAH AUDRIC: “Right, then you pull the lights up to the ramp and get ready for the pyrotechnics. If we set another roof on fire you will be fired, got that?” The producer is scribbling down notes as if this is some college class and then walks out of the door, before the door can close, we see Colby and Jamal of Ninja Strike Force slide through the doorway. The charge Audric, who is terrified, and then they slide to their knees only a few inches from her. JAMAL MITCHELL: “OH WISE AND MERCIFUL LA’SARAH AUDRIC…” COLBY HUNTER: “WOULDS’T THOU GIVE US A MATCH…” JAMAL MITCHELL: “THAT ISN’T ON THE WRETCHED EXTREME TV?” COLBY HUNTER: “PLEASE?” JAMAL MITCHELL: “PLEASE?” La’Sarah gets down to their level and pinches both of their cheeks. LA’SARAH AUDRIC: “Oh, you want a real match? Well. I’ll give you one, against the Transcendence.” Audric pushes them both on their backs and walks out of the door. Ninja Strike Force offer the camera a thumbs up. ==== We cut backstage where Wake Walker is walking down the hallway heading towards the entrance to the Seawall Boulevard Entertainment Complex. He passes by the locker room area and onto the offices. One of the office’s he passes is Blake Beckett’s whose door is still wide open from the previous altercation. Blake glances up from his computer to see Walker walking by and rushes out to see him. BLAKE BECKETT: “Wake! WAKE! Wake I need to talk to you! WAKE!” Wake continues to walk down the hallway. Beckett finally catches up and places an arm on Wake’s shoulder that stops him dead in his tracks. WAKE WALKER: “What?” Blake shakes his head and seems annoyed with the returning star. BLAKE BECKETT: “What the hell is wrong with you? Do you think you are the boss here Wake? Do you think you can do whatever you want? Well let me tell you something Wake. I’m the one in charge here, did you forget that while you were gone? If you mess up another one of my matches like you did with Kluge and Rydell… you will be fired.” Walker stares at Blake for a moment, then continues on his path to the main event. Blake can be seen in the distance shaking his head and heading back to his office. The main event is next! QUOTE Dawn Of A Million Souls by Ayreon begins to play. Matt Kluge comes through the curtains, wearing orange tights with some ambient colors and gets excited with the uproar that is created by the crowd. Matt Kluge walks down the ramp. On several occasions, Matt Kluge signals that the camel clutch is the move that is going to end this match. He goes up the steel steps and lightly bounces off one of the ropes, crossing his arms and waiting for his partner. “The Human Stain" by Kamelot plays. Tony Miranda wastes no time and enters the ring. The crowd is behind him, but simply not as much as Matt Kluge. Tony Miranda and Matt Kluge shake hands and give each other a short pep talk about some of the strategies they have in this match. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Matt Kluge and Tony Miranda, two very highly-regarded individuals are sure to make a great tag team. However, their opponents are what scare me. ” RILEY STEVENS: “They will be facing a team consisting of two world champions, Aspen Chaud and the returning but changed, Wake Walker. What do you think about Wake‘s return tonight? Do you feel he has anything to prove to the crowd?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Well, I mean, we’re talking about one of the greatest if not THE greatest of all time. Does he need to prove anything? He sure as hell doesn’t but should he prove something? You bet. He needs to show that he is just as good as he was a couple of months ago and with his last performance against Aspen Chaud, I don’t know… ” "Now I'm That Bitch" by Livvi Franc plays. The crowd instantly starts booing Aspen Chaud. Aspen comes out, wearing a dark-colored outfit encrusted in pink. Aspen takes in all of the hate from the crowd and uses it to boost her ego temporarily. Kluge and Miranda shake their heads whilst both smiling at the type of reaction Aspen is getting. Aspen pretends to go up the steel steps but then jumps off them and waits on the outside of the ring, circling it whilst Kluge and Miranda move their heads in the same direction that Aspen is located. Aspen eventually backs it back to the ramp and waits whilst smirking. She points at the tron. “Dazed and Confused begins to play slowly” RILEY STEVENS: “Here he is…” The entire crowd can be seen getting up to get the first view as the lights go out. An uproar of cheers can be heard for Walker, despite what he did to Rydell and Kluge. Wake Walker comes through the curtain and walks down the ramp, slowly. Walker is wearing all-black, a jacket, pants, essentially not dressed for wrestling. With his sunglasses on, he brushes past Aspen and stands on the apron, indicating that he will not be starting this match. Aspen enters the ring. Looks like Matt Kluge and Aspen will begin the match. Aspen is given no opportunity before Matt Kluge runs at Aspen and hit’s a drop kick, sending Aspen flying back to her corner. Walker gets off the apron and walks around the outside near his corner. Aspen is oblivious to this as she is picked up by Kluge and suplexed. However, Kluge chains this move with some mounted punches! The referee tells Kluge to get off. Kluge does so. Kluge picks up aspen and tries to lock in a chokehold but Aspen quickly does an armdrag and locks in a submission maneuver, stretching the shoulder of Kluge. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “This looks to be a fast paced match already. ” RILEY STEVENS: “Indeed.” Kluge stays in the move for about twenty seconds before he fights out of it and hit’s a regal suplex! The crowd pops for this. Kluge goes for the cover. 1...2... Not enough! Kluge isn’t surprised. Kluge kicks Aspen a couple of times, picks up Aspen and hits her with a closed-fist, dropping her. Kluge tries to go for the Rat Trap early on but Aspen quickly makes it to the ropes and goes to the outside. Aspen walks up to Walker and tells him to get back on the apron. Walker turns away from Aspen. Aspen shakes her head and gets back in and tries to tackle Kluge, only do be hit by a knee lift that sets her seemingly out-cold on her feet. Kluge hit’s a northern lights suplex and points up to the air. The crowd pops. Tony Miranda is seen cheering on Kluge. Kluge waits for Aspen to get up. Kluge launches himself and goes for a missile drop kick, Aspen saw it coming! She rolls out of the way but Kluge saw her counter coming and rolls through. OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Oh, that could‘ve meant a world of hurt for Kluge! Aspen saw the missile dropkick coming and was smart enough to get out of the way, but Kluge saw HER counter coming and rolled through! What skill!. ” RILEY STEVENS: “And it‘s interesting how fast-paced this match has become. Don‘t get me wrong, Aspen and Kluge are fantastic wrestlers, some of the best we have… but they usually don‘t perform with this dead-near perfect type of quickness and precision. Do you see where I‘m headed with this?” OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Are you trying to say that there may be another factor about this match that is forcing these two to go the extra mile?” RILEY STEVENS: “Precisely” Kluge bounces off the ropes, and so does Aspen… Kluge goes for a clothesline. Aspen dodges it. They bounce again. Aspen goes for a crossbody which hits Kluge and stops him in his tracks! 1...nope! Aspen starts kicking and puts Kluge in a neutral corner, stomping on him and putting on some illegal moves on the corner. The referee separates Aspen and Kluge. Aspen rushes at Kluge but Kluge gets out of the way. Aspen is dazed and turning away from Kluge near the corner! Kluge picks Aspen up and hit’s a German pinning suplex! 1...2... Kick out! Aspen, dazed, gets up and tries to hit a wild roundhouse kick but it misses. Kluge punches Aspen a couple of times and then kicks her on the gut. Kluge motions for The Tiger’s Uproar but Aspen counters into the K.M.A! Heel lock on Kluge! Kluge takes some of the pain for fifteen seconds before he rolls on his back and and kicks Aspen away. Aspen bounces off the ropes and tries to hit a knee to the face of Kluge. Kluge dodges it and tries a quick pinning move. 1...2...kick out! Aspen gets up and punches Tony Miranda and poses for the crowd. Kluge gets up slowly but appears to be playing possum. Kluge kicks Aspen in the gut against and goes for The Tiger’s Uproar and hits it but Kluge yells out and seems to be feeling the effects of the K.M.A! He is unable to capitalize. Ten seconds pass and Kluge sees that getting Tony Miranda in would be the smart move! OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Here it comes! The hot tags! ” RILEY STEVENS: “Let’s see what Walker is made of…is he still the same?” Walker walks up the steel steps and waits on the apron. Aspen drags herself toward Walker. Kluge drags himself to Miranda! Five more seconds pass! The hot tags are ready! Kluge tags in Miranda and the crowd goes wild. Walker is tagged in by Aspen whilst still wearing sunglasses! Miranda runs at Walker. Walker stays in one spot and hit’s a very sudden and powerful spinebuster on Miranda, quieting the crowd. Walker goes for a pin. 1...2...3! It’s over? OLIVER THIBODAUX: “Good GOD! Did you just see what I saw? RILEY STEVENS: “Ladies and gentlemen… Walker has truly made a massive statement tonight by single-handedly destroying Tony Miranda in a matter of seconds and with one move, none the less! He’s an even bigger threat than I thought! This man is insane!” Miranda looks like he must’ve suffered some sort of concussion considering how that spinebuster alone was enough to take him out! The referee tries to lift the arm of Walker but Walker ignores the referee. Aspen celebrates on the outside. Kluge is sitting on his corner and looks angrily at Walker. He beckons for a microphone and it is given to him. MATT KLUGE: You’re going to come and get me?! C’mon, I dare you! Take me out! Kluge manages to stand up, visibly showing that he is hurt from his encounter with Aspen. MATT KLUGE: C’mon, FIGHT ME! Are you scared, Walker? Kluge puts the microphone next to Walker’s mouth. WAKE WALKER: You’re such a waste of life. Pathetic. RILEY STEVENS: “Very brave on the part of an injured Kluge but Walker doesn’t look like he wants any of it. Perhaps we will see these two clash another time” Walker looks at Kluge very seriously and shakes his head, slowly backing away from Kluge. The crowd boos. Kluge looks visibly angry and limps over to Miranda. Miranda is still on the floor. Aspen was looking at the encounter between Walker and Kluge and seemed intrigued by the encounter. Walker disappears to the back. Aspen subsequently leaves. WINNERS: ASPEN CHAUD & WAKE WALKER
