Application ----------- --------------------- --------------------- Basic Information --------------------- --------------------- The Net Championship Wrestling is a Booked Federation which means we decide the results of the matches and how the match goes, by your participation.
N.C.W Is an All-Original Wrestlers fed, which means we only allow Fictional or "Fake" wrestlers, we have a pretty good knowledge of existing wrestlers all around the world, so don't try to fool us it would just cause your application denial.
Net Championship Wrestling is an Online GAME, Which Was Meant For Fun and in Order To Succeed you must roleplay in order to gain "heat" face or heel
(good or bad), by doing Good, Original Roleplays, so in other words if you wish to win, participate.
NCW is an Organization which is based in Denver, Colorado, and can be found on address http://netwrestling.hypermart.net , the fed is ran by two guys, as they make all the decisions about the federation. We announce From Now, that the fed has a 30 Wrestlers Limit, Which means everybody after that limit will be put on a Hold List, until a room will be vacated, so join before all places are taking.
This is almost it for this section, here is just the Wrestler Creation
Limits and Add on: When Creating a Wrestler it must be between the limits of 5'3" and 150 And
Not Over 7'4" & 470 Pounds. These are good limits even too much, try to set your wrestler reasonable limits. We want absolutely NO Copies! We Don't want Stone Cold
Jay Thomas,
Or Big Sexy Kurt Bash or "The People's Champion" The Lock or anything like that, I Think you get the idea pretty much, we don't want any Copies from real wrestlers, if we see one we Will have to ignore your application. Also we don't want no
"The Pimp"
or the Whore Slave or any kind of Profanity in the name or in the roleplays either, Unless you want your name to be [Censored] ;) but really no Profanity! Now When Submitting a Wrestler Please try be very descriptive in order we can push your wrestler and give it more time in NCW, Since We Basically don't know nothing about this man joining we don't want a new man comes in and you telling us "his
History
is unknown" don't take the easy road or give us a one line history and description, we want it all, if you don't take the application serious we won't know if you are taking the fed serious and might reject your application. The last thing if you don't intend to roleplay don't bother joining, cause we are accepting only members who are intending on roleplaying.
--------------------- --------------------- Booking Information --------------------- --------------------- This Information tells you What do you need to do in order to get your character wins and basically what you need to do in the federation.
70% - Quality (Of The Flash & The In-Rings) 30% - Stats & Their Quality. The President with ratings of how you think the other wrestlers are over. If You Don't Make all these things good chances you will lose and again until you start, now I don't want people saying I Got a better percent etc. Don't take it exactly it just things you need to do as the Quality of the Flashes you send in are the
2 most important things but if there's a match and both of you got the 80% but one guy submitted a BETTER strats he will probably win.

Flashes - These are Interviews, in NCW we have a rule saying you must submit at least ONE FLASH for week, It will be enforced, and the ones who won't go by it might get them selves Fired or Suspended. There is a Max of 1 Flash for show & 2
For Week,
The Flashes Must be in at least 1 Day before the show take place, in the
Flashes you can
reply to what other people said, and everything you basically want.
Things that are usually in every Flash: (Promo/Talking/Scene/InRings) - Scene Description - Wrestler Comments - Ending In Rings - Much Like Flashes, its a wrestler doing Interview, in the ring, the in-ring should include a Crowd Response, this would be done only on Spotlight.
Strarts (Strategy) - In this section you write what you want to be in your match, what you want to do, special moves, how you walk into the match, thing that develop your character (I.E - Ric Flair Beg - get him over as heel) or attack people,
Maybe you would like to
Concentrate on one body part during the match, or hit lots of power moves, maybe execute high-flying
Maneuvers, etc. The Strats are mendetory,I repeat mendoyory,I dont want any excuses,if the other opponent did not beat you in role-playing but DID send a good strat,and you didn't, YOU WILL
LOSE, STRATS ARE DO AT EXECTLY 10:00 PM - NCW Time! --------------------- --------------------- Basic Rules --------------------- --------------------- We Have only Few Rules, but We Will Enforce Them Strictly. 1. No Cursing in Flashes, I will allow Damn & Hell but the rest will have to be [Censored] and no @ss or F*ck or anything like that, Every Curse word must be in [Censored] or The Flash Would be Ignored, I Hope you don't need to use these words to show your wrestler is talented, now if you break this rule 1 time, you will be warned, 2 times and your roleplay will be ignored which will cause you losing, if you do it 3 times and your Out of NCW.
2. Continuing the above rule, No Referring to a Racial/Sexual Comments about any character or Handler, I don't want to see "I'm going to win cause your gay" or anything like that, break this rule and you would be immediately fired.
3. No Random attacks on Staff/Wrestlers in Interviews, since it is Your Roleplay you can't attack him cause he can't protect, so if you want to attack somebody send it into a card and I Will put it in, also unless it is to develop an angle/character it will not be allowed to attack any staff members(President, Commissioner, announcer,
Interviewers etc.) In roleplays, unless we authorize it. Break This Rule and you are risking in suspension. 4. Please keep in mind that this is JUST A GAME, don't get upset every time something doesn't go the way you wanted it, or you lose a match, instead be dedicated to improving and get it your way by being better then others.
5. NCW Will Have A Close Flash System, meaning All Flashes will be sent to The President & The Commissioner, and we will release them on appropriate time, thus making everything a surprise until the actual Card will be released. E-Mail Address of people in stables will be given out, but No Abuse of E-Mail addresses (I.E. - Sending a guy a message with I'm going to Kill you) on those cases wrestlers will be warned and if it will happen again they will be Fired.
6. All Roleplayers that are Scheduled to Fight in a match, WILL Fight in a match, if you won't roleplay you will be jobbed, if you got a personal issue and you can't flash, tell me about it before the line-up comes out.
7. I Can be contacted at ICQ at the number [detail withheld], If You Have Suggestions Or Comments, or would like to discuss with me about a future angle contact me at that number, if you want to attack or interfere in a match to build an angle it is recommended to consult with me first.
These are the only rules, please follow them so we will all have a good time in NCW, The staff in running it and the wrestlers in participating.
--------------------- --------------------- Titles --------------------- --------------------- The Titles are the Awards Wrestlers get for Hard work, for dedication to the fed, for standing in it side all the way, the People who hold those titles are the people you all want to be like, the people that work hard to get there, of course only hard workers will get them, there for
No Coming in the fed and saying To the Champion " I Want a match" not only the wrestler probably won't accept it, most chances are we won't sign the match.
The Titles listed below are the titles you can go for: NCW World Heavyweight Title - The Iceing on the Cake, the best of the best, the Great One, The Man
The Icon, the Guy who will get attacks, stalked and killed for, If You hold this title it means you are the
Best Of The Best, and everybody else are the rest, means you represent r of
NCW when it comes to publicity,
by holding this title you promise your self tons of respect, But with respect comes more...Hard work, you must Protect your title every Pay Per View, And If You wish on a Big Show as well, if it develops an angle, but the challenger must be out of the top 5, no guys who just joined, and for the Pay Per View the
Head of the Board will set a top contender for you to face.
NCW Madness Title - This Title is the second most important title in Net
Championship Wrestling,
The Holder of this title can brag he is the best of the best and better then everybody in the league, except for "The Icon", This title will usually be fought under No Dq & No
Countout and if wanted
other gimmick, it Comes to tell you the NCW Independent Champion is truly
The Most "Free" Man, he can do what ever the hell he thinks of, and if you hold this title, either your tough, or a cheater.
This title will be defended at least 2 times a month, and on every Pay Per
View (On Months there are PPV There would be at least 3 title defenses). Again for the Pay Per View we set the Top
Contender, and for regular shows you can set your own opponent but try keeping them in the top 10.
NCW Tag Team Titles - These Titles Gives a Chance for the teams in NCW to show their talents and proving to everybody that you 2 are one mean lean fighting machine, that work like one unit, if you got the skill to beat all other teams then you get to say "Were The Best", this title must be defended once a month, and on Pay Per Views.
These are the titles in NCW that you can aim for, we might add more titles by popularity of NCW, But if not it will stay these 3 titles, to increase the competition.
--------------------- --------------------- Rankings --------------------- --------------------- The Rankings will be Constructed out by wins & losts, meaning on Every win you will get 2 points on a lose 0 points and on a draw 1 point.
New Rankings will come out every week, so it will be updated weekly, it will also be set by the place your in, as the world Champion will always be on top, the Independent on second place and then the rest of the wrestlers.
Tag Teams will have their own separate rankings, if they will compete in
Singles as well it will be separated!
--------------------- --------------------- NCW Staff --------------------- --------------------- President Brian Warr - Brian Warr Was first brought into wrestling on the age of 8 he saw his first Monday Night Raw, it fascinated him from the first moment, he decided he will get a career in pro-wrestling, coming in with his first image as The Iceman and then Shadow Warrior the two characters which brought him the most success then he went with unsuccessful gimmicks such as "Splash" and "Author" which brought him out of business.
Then decided to start a federation of his own, he traveled around the world to find the right sponsors for him to begin, and along the money he had from his wrestling day, he started the Net
Championship Wrestling, since he was originally from Detroit he decide to start his league there. Currently Brian Warr is a married man with 2 kids.
He is 6'0" and 210 pounds, which is light cause in his Wrestling Days he weighted for about 300 pounds which is too much for a 6'0" that was also one of the reasons that got him fired.
Vice President - Boaz Sity is also a former wrestler, before joining the managment he used to wrestle in the NCW, he held a fake Television title, and also was the self preclaimed "Kind of Xtreme",
Sity left the NCW Cause of some contractual problems, but soon after came back as NCW Vice President. From that day he made a lot of important decisions for and about the NCW, And even invented, the "Most Vicious & Dangerous battle royal" which is known as
"The Deadly War", Boaz Sity along Brian Warr is without a doubt, 2 of the most creative minds the E-Wrestlers scene has seen in a long long time. Today Boaz is living in Salt Lake City in
Utha, not merried, He puts most of his time and affort to improve NCW, For the wrestlers & the fans.
Tom Madness - The Main Commentator, he always on every show along with another announcer, who changes by the show, Tom Madness is in the age of 34, he wasn't really into wrestling, until one day his Boy bugged him so much he took him to wrestling, Tom
Wasn't was impressed by the Size & Talent of the Wrestlers, and their
Microphone skills, after that he never attended a show, but one day he herd about a Commentator Contest where the winner will get to be a Commentator at a new wrestling promotion, Tom
Decided to study hard the wrestling moves, then he went to that contest and won, the Testers were impressed from his Voice and his knowledge, and decided to hire him. Tom Madness is only 5'5" and 160 pounds.
Chris Dale - Spotlight Commentator, this young man only 23 got into wrestling business by being a Jobber to every man who wanted to get over in a big federation, one day he jobbed to a wrestler named at that time "The Iceman", after the show he bought him a cup of beer, Brian Warr liked the young man very much, when he opened the NCW he gave him a call and they worked a plan together, Chris
Dale would become Spotlight Commentator. Dale was a Cruiserweight so he weight about 180 or so he is 5'8", since he was a Jobber but he wrestled he talks about the in-ring action and do play by play usually with comments about the moves.
Jason Low - Thriller Commentator, This Man is a man who basically hates most people, when he saw an ad about a 'commentator needed' he decided to come, he was very rude in his interview Brian Liked it, since he wanted a commentator who says things as he feels them.
But as they done a preview show to see how the commentators get a long, it was a total failure as he started a fight with Chris Dale, since only Tom Madness can handle him by being rude back, they put them together On Night Shack.
Other NCW Staff: Main Referee: Frank Howard - Used to Referee in all the top-notch & main events matches. Second Referee: Billy Tigerwoods - Usually Referee in the regular matches, and sometimes main events.
Third Referee: Neil Johnson - The Referee for Low-Card, House shows & Dark
Matches, rarely gets to referee in a main event or a regular match.
Head Of Security: Tony Wolf - He Is the chief of the Security, and basically sit in a room with a lot of screens on every corner in the arena and tells his men to take care of the situation when ever one comes up. He is in his mid 30, not very tall but very quick.
Rumors has it he was involve with the Mafia, nothing confirmed though. Interviewer: Richard Smith - A Mid 40 man, with a little bold on top of his head, he is very charming man with a great mind, always comes up with something new to ask his interviewee, he likes the wrestlers and the job, we also know that if you want to get a muffin go to bakery, but if you want one hell of an interview come to Richard
Smith.
--------------------- --------------------- Shows & Pay Per Views --------------------- --------------------- Here are the Shows & Pay Per Views, and what they stand for. Saturday Night Spotlight - The Main Show of NCW, The Wrestler Chance to get them selves into the spotlight, get attention, in Spotlight you Can Attack or Flash, in order to start or develop a story line, and to get more "over" with NCW Wrestlers. This Show will usually have 6-7 matches, so send it in by Friday Night and be sure to watch this show.
Tuesday Night thriller - This show is basically a house show that gets to be on television (I.E -
WWF Superstars/Shotgun) It Will Be Recaps of NCW Spotlight, it would publicize the rumors you send in, and it will have 2-4 matches in Highlight style, you can flash in this show maybe even an in-studio Flash, you can attack someone backstage,
The Show will help develop story line, with rumors (I.E - There are rumors that Mr X will be at Spotlight OR, Some Say that Mr X Is going to set a wild challange on the next show for Mr Y) You can attack guests on the show ( If a wrestler will be a guest in the show you can come in and attack him, or attack the superstars back stage...).
Pay Per Views - Pay Per Views or Supercard, will take place every 2 months, like back in the old days, when a feud was a feud and took
2 good ol' months to be over, and be honest, we loved it, the tension, more room to develop, rather these days we have like 5 matches with the same 2 people why? cause they didn't had enough time to develop for the first match. So here in NCW it won't happen, every 6 or 7 weeks we will hold a Pay Per View, and The Pay Per View will be the most glamorous night in e-wrestling...
These are the Shows for NCW, We Might Add Shows, but this are the basic 2 and the Pay Per View.
--------------------- --------------------- Closing Words --------------------- --------------------- Well that's all the information about ncw, if you think your interested and can handle all the work in NCW, be sure to join us as we would love to have you, and please keep in mind that we might be out of room when you decide to finally join, and you might have to wait on our waiting list, so instead just join now if you think this is the ideal fed for you.
Note: Not ALL Applications will be accepted, we will let you know if they will or won't and why.
