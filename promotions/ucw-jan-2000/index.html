Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Universal Championship Wrestling survives as a single recruitment ad, posted 5 January 2000, that opens by billing itself as a revival: “Universal Championship Wrestling was created three short years ago, it became a monster hit and never looked back until some hacked into the site and destroyed every record of Universal Championship Wrestling, we rebuilt in 1998 but by then the greatness that was or could have been was gone for ever.” No document anywhere in the record corroborates that earlier history; the ad itself is the promotion's own only trace.

The ad frames the January 2000 posting as a fresh attempt at the same name, prompted, in its own words, by “a good friend” asking “if we could bring back the federation that made us both household names.” It asks for a roster before anything else — “we are looking for anyone who is interested in us to join us so we can get a good sized roster” — and promises matches and “the championship tournaments” only once that roster exists.

No further document survives. Whether a roster was ever filled, a match ever run, or a tournament ever staged is not known; the promotion's record stops at its own recruitment pitch.
