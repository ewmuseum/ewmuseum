Welcome to the NWF

7 October 1997 (embedded posting date; see provenance)
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Welcome to the NWF, The Northern Wrestling Federation. This fed will be based out of the North East region of the United States. This Federation is an original fed, no real wrestlers. The Rules are simple, you must role play at least twice a week, no more than 5 role plays per week. You win matches by the quantity, and quality of your role plays and strategies. You can have no more than 3 wrestlers in the NWF, tag-teams if owned by one handler, count as one wrestler. Each can have one manager as well. Since we are a new fed their will be two cards per week, Monday Night War, which will be the big card of the week. Thursday Night Clash will be mostly summary's of house shows that took place the night before, with few live matches. There are 4 titles in the NWF, the World Title, Intercontinental title, T.V. champion, and the World Tag-Team title. Stables can't be composed of more than 5 wrestlers. Any mention of associations with real wrestlers is prohibited, but you can talk about real wrestlers in passing. ex- you can say " Who do you thing you are Bret Hart", you can't say " Well i have beat Ric Flair, and have worked for WCW." As far as language goes, you can say the words you hear on TV, but you can't say other swears. The NWF staff is not going to take the time to proof all flashes, how ever an exceeded amount of profanity is found in flashes, suspensions, and contract termination is at the discretion of the NWF Commission. We want you to have as much fun as possible, and we will listen to all you ideas for possible story lines, please send us a sample role play, along with your wrestlers bio. The application is as follows: Your name: E-mail address: Wrestler's name: Height: Weight: Hometown: Physical description (hair, body, etc.): Gimmick/History: Name of finisher: Description of finisher: Theme music: Wrestler's style: 10 favorite moves of wrestler (used for match write-ups): Is your wrestler part of a tag team?: If so, what is the name of the team?: Name of Partner: Name and description of tagteams finisher: Does your wrestler have a manager?: Name of manager and description: Is your wrestler part of a stable?: Name of stable and members: There will be a webpage coming up within the next week or so for the NWF, which will feature roster, rankings, news, etc. So I will be posting that shortly. Any questions, please e-mail [detail withheld]@aol.com
