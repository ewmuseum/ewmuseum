Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

WCCW was a member territory of the NeWA — the National eWrestling Alliance, a GeoCities-hosted hub that billed itself "the Major League of Professional eWrestling" and staged itself, in fiction, as a continuation of the real National Wrestling Alliance's own history: its own introduction page claims to "share their history up until September 1, 1997," the date its own kayfabe champion took the NWA World Heavyweight lineage from the real one. WCCW's own free-agent roster survives from September 1999, wrestlers signing on for limited windows against a listed renewal date — a fed run, per its own governing rulebook, under an elected president, vice president, and head booker.

What survives beyond the roster is thin but genuine: a Christmas-week "Roleplay of the Week" staging one of its own wrestlers, Kevin Alloy, as Satan himself, brawling a costumed Santa Claus through a shopping mall before being dragged off by his tag partners; and a 1999 ESPN Special, the second annual Lou Thesz International Champion of Champions Tournament, fought for the United States Heavyweight Championship — its own Round 1, sixteen matches deep, staged the real Mitsuharu Misawa and Toshiaki Kawada under their own names among its thirty-two entrants. Its own captured record runs out by October 2001.

The hub's own claimed NWA World Heavyweight lineage survives whole on a title-history ledger: the real National Wrestling Alliance's championship record, inherited kayfabe up to 1 September 1997, then diverging under seventeen of the fed's own champions through the ledger's own last word, 18 December 1999 — Roddy Piper and Dan Severn staged as titleholders among them, real wrestlers within the fed's own invented history rather than its borrowed one.

This same GeoCities address (geocities.com/Colosseum/Sideline/1112) is a shared hub — its own material also names a sister territory, N.e.W.A/British Championship Wrestling, elsewhere on the frontier ledger. The wider NeWA hub itself is not accessioned here; only WCCW's own material is.
