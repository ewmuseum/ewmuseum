RPOTW: 12.18.99–12.24.99

18–24 December 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
RPOTW: 12.18.99-12.24.99 RPOTW: 12.18.99-12.24.99 (The scene is blank. Nothing but darkness. A vast, empty void, much like Matthew Jude's cranium. A red soviet-style sickle and hammer fades in, makes one full revolution, and fades out as the hustle and bustle of last-minute Christmas shopping fades in. A still of a shopping mall follows suit, showing many customers, and a fat guy wearing a devil costume, walking in through the doors. The still begins to move in perfect timing with where the audio track is at. The camera doesn't move until a family of four comes through the doors: a middle-aged man with brown hair, wearing brown slacks, a red flannel shirt, a tan coat; his middle-aged wife, with dark brown hair hanging to her shoulders, a cresent-shaped silver earing in each ear, and a black fur coat; their ever-so-naturally (yeah right) platnum blonde, teenaged daughter in her dumb jock boyfriend's black and orange letterman's jacket; and the five-year-old son, decked out in simple blue jeans, Nike shoes, a denim jacket, and a shirt reading "Austin 3:16". With a teenager in the family, it's bound to be disfunctional. And we're right, it is. The family is in the mall five minutes before the young boy is screaming about being at the mall, and the daughter is sintantly annoyed.) Girl: Can't we just ship him off somewhere? Mother: Why do you always have to say that? Girl: Because I think he'd be better off with his natural parents. Son: I wanna see the toys! Father: Oh boy. Look, son, if I take you to the toy store will you stop screaming? Son: Yes. Father: Well, I guess I'll take him to Kay-Bee's. I'll meet up with you in the food court. (The family parts ways, and the camera follows the father and son as they make a turn into a Kay-Bee's Toy Store. The man holds hands with his son, and is all but dragged whereever the boy wants to go: directly toward a wall full of Pok?mon. The little freak's overjoyed the site of an over-stuffed, little, yellow and brown rabbit-lookin' thing. He sweeps the toy up.) Son: PIKACHU! Father: Pikawhat? Son: Pikachu! Father: Oh. Well, put it down before you break it. Son: I want it! Father: Uh.. It's $29.95. Why don't you take.. Um.. (Grabbing a much smaller, plush, orange, dinosaur-ish Pok?mon) .. This thing? Son: Charmander? Charmander SUCKS! Father: What did I tell you about saying that? Son: I'm sorry. PLEEEEEEAAAAASSSSSSSEEE!? Father: Oh, fine. (The little bugger lets out a joyous "YAY!!" and runs after his father towards the counter, where a man spikey blue hair, a leather jacket, baggy blue jeans, and black boots stands holding a peice of paper and talking to an African-American teen clerk. A closer shot reveals the blue-haired man's face to be that Mr. Jackson Hunter, Esq. The cameraman allows us to evesdrop on their conversation.) Jax: Yeah, I need some handcuffs. Clerk: Well, there oughta be some down there wit the cops 'n' cowboys playsets. Jax: Alright. What about whips and chains? Clerk: Umm.. We don't carry any of those.. Jax: Hm.. What about dolls? Clerk: Well, we've got a good deal of Barbies back there. We also have some WWF and WCW figures and playsets in isle three. Jax: Well, there's no Rock's, 'Taker's, or Malibu Barbie's listed here so I don't guess he wanted those... What about your leather products? Clerk: What? Sir, can I see that list? (Jax gives the clerk the peice of paper. The clerk gets a wide-eyed and startled look on his face.) Clerk: Umm.. I don' think we got tha kinda toys you're looking for, sir. Jax: Damn. Well, thanks anyway. Clerk: Anytime... Uh.. Happy holidays.. Jax: Thanks. (Jax turns, and walks through the father and son behind him, almost on purpose. The kid and his damned Pikachu fall over as the IWA wrestler leaves the store.) Jax: Damn it. That little freak is too hard to shop for. Oh well. I wonder what Syn's doing. Oh wait, I know exactly what he's doing: wasting Syndicate Christmas money playing Crazy Taxi. (The scenery begins to blur past, and the scene shifts to another section of the mall, the Arcade. Inside, Jax's predictions is proved to be true, as the player sitting in the driver's seat of the only damn phenomenon ever, the arcade game known as Crazy Taxi (coming soon on Sega Dreamcast), is revealed to be that lousy, no-good, half-Mexican, son of a bitch known as Syn. Obviously, Syn spends more time playing this game than even thinking about the IWA, as the toll he's collected already is at $4,375.17, and his IWA record is 0-1. Syn wears his heavy, blue, black, and white Nike coat, and his usual baggy black jeans. One of his gray Vans stomps on the gas and his taxi goes over the edge of a bridge, landing perfectly in the parking lot of a baseball stadium. The polygonal, green-haired cabby takes his yellow car through the parking lot, trying not to hit any cars but not being too careful about it. A parked pink convertable flips over on it's side as the driver runs into it, and then he moves on. The driver lets it's passenger off, and immediately picks up another. This time, the driver is told to go to the police station. He drives up a hill, onto a freeway, and carefully guides between two semis without a scratch. Now that's skill. Syn continues to play the game until the time runs down to zero. Syn waits to see what ranking he got, which turns out to be 17th, and a class B license. Syn sighs, saddened by the fact that he's not as good as last time. Syn begins thinking to himself, and we hear his thoughts thanks an overdub.) Syn: A class B license AND a loss to a guy that comes out to Micheal Jackson? Damn it all! (Syn gets out of the seat of the machine and leaves the arcade. Immediately outside is the food court, with a Subway, a Taco Bell, a Fu Man Wok, and an A&W hot dog place, among other fast food places. Syn heads over to the A&W and gets a root beer, some fries, and some chicken strips. He takes his dinner to a table sits down, a few tables away from a couple of good-lookin' chicks. From this angle, we can clearly see the Mall Santa's set-up. The Santa walks into his cottage for his break, as the previously mentioned fat guy in the devil costume looks around the display. The "devil" undoes the velvet rope and a small line of children forms, followed by their mothers. Back at the food court, the girls look over at Syn, who looks back with a cheesy smile and a raise of an eyebrow. The girls burst out laughing at Syn's sorry attempt before leaving in a hurry. Syn's thoughts run again.) Syn: f---in' bitches. Heh, bitches. I'm gonna get Logan, goddamn it. I'm gonna get him good. The guy obviously never watches TV, or else he'd know that everytime his good buddy Dan came over to say something about his family he was really saying "Bend over just a little farther so I can shove this big dick of a conspiracy in a little more." I don't even know why Jax didn't take that belt. It'd save me the trouble from even thinking about a worthless piece of s--- like him. Oh well. Come Christmas Eve I'll take his belt and shove it up his conspiracy victim ass. Lousy piece of shit. (Through all of this, the "devil" demands that a child join his evil legion. When the child refuses, the fat man begins yelling about the empending apocalypse and setting the display on fire. Suddenly, Syn is snapped out of his thoughts.) Jax: Hey! You gonna finish that? Syn: What? Oh. I dunno, you can have some I guess. (In the background, the devil-suited man is heard yelling "HO'S HO'S HO'S!") Jax: Cool. Syn: Did you get Ratt's s--- yet? Jax: No, I couldn't find anything over at Kay-Bee. (Fat Guy: Guess where you're going for Christmas! HELL! And Satan can assure you that you'll all like Hell very very much!) Syn: You went to Kay-Bee shopping for Ratt? Jax: Yeah. Syn: They don't have the kind of toys Ratt likes there. Jax: That's what the clerk told me. (Fat Guy: "There's only one radio station in hell, and all it plays is music that sounds like this!" The Santa lets out an annoying screech, tearing Syn away from his enthralling conversation.) Syn: That f---in Santa is pissing me off. What the hell kind of Santa thinks he's Satan? Jax: KEVIN! Syn: Kev.. What? AH! (As Syn and Jax take off for the center of the mall, where Santa's Cottage is, the scene cuts to that very Cottage, where a fat man wearing glasses and a generic, red, Halloween devil costume complete with a tail and badly set horns stands yelling about Hell. This man is none other than Kevin Alloy, the former Syndicate lawyer than began to think he was Satan for unknown reasons. Kevin waves around his plastic trident as shoppers get too close.) Kevin/Satan: You cannot stop the lord of all which is dark and somewhat moist! HAIL SATAN! HAIL! Oh! (Kevin finally notices the tall Christmas Tree. He slowly walks up to the giant evergreen, and is instantly lost in it's blinking lights and shiny ornaments. Kevin pulls out his lighter, and is elated when the tree bursts into flames. At this point, the Mall Santa steps out of his cottage. Midgets run out behind him, their elf suits aflame.) Santa: Ho ho ho! What do you think you're doing there, Mr. Devil? Kevin/Satan: I'm.. Deviling.. And what's it you.. YOU! Santa: Yes, Satan, it's time we finally met. You've been a very naughty this year, Kevin! Kevin/Satan: What do you expect! I'm the devil! And it's high time I kicked your jolly ass! Santa: Just try it, Lucifer! Kevin/Satan: AHHHH!!!! (Kevin lets out a battle cry as he chucks his trident at the Jolly One. With amazing agility, and in slow motion, Santa runs up the side of his cottage and flips all the way around while Kevin's trident flies past, taking out Rudolph. Santa then flips toward Kevin and follows this up by deleivering many powerful blows to Kevin's stomach, his arms a blur. Kev stumbles back, but doesn't fall. Santa then levitates into the air, and delivers another kick to Kevin. This time, Kevin is kicked into a wall. At this point, the kids are chanting "SAN-TA! SAN-TA!" Santa comes in and begins throwing shots at Kevin, his arms blurring again. Kevin sticks his hand out and moves it slowly, blocking Santa's every punch. When Santa stops out of exhaustion, Kevin hits him right on his cherry-like nose. Children start screaming and crying when Santa is knocked head over heels.) Kevin/Satan: And now Satan shall steal Santa's presents! (Kevin walks over the defeated Saint Nick, and walks over toward his big red bag. Kevin is happy with what he finds.) Kevin/Satan: Oh! Breath mints! (Finally, Syn and Jax have fought their way through the crowd.) Syn: Kevin! Kevin/Satan: Syn! Jackson! Satan is pleased to see you again! Jax: Yeah, well, the police are coming and I don't feel like going back to court! Kevin/Satan: Satan will send them to Hell! We're all going to Hell! (Syn and Jax drag Kevin and his big bag of breathmints out of the mall as the scene fades.)
