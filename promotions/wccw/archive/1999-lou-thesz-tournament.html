The 2nd Annual Lou Thesz International Champion of Champions Tournament Info The 2nd Annual Lou Thesz International Champion of Champions Tournament For the United States Heavyweight Championship August 12 - Myrtle Beach, South Carolina Venue to be announced National eWrestling Alliance's ESPN Special: Round 1 Kai Lewis Vs. Powerhouse The match started out as Powerhouse attacked Lewis before the begining of the match, He continues with an assualt on "Brooklyn's Finest" Appling many power moves, and at one point hitting a Choke slam on Kai. Then after sending Kai to the Turnbuckle, He tried to follow up with a Big Forearm Smash, but Kai got the leg up, Kai went to the second rope and hit with a flying clothesline, and went for a Texas Clover Leaf, but Powerhouse managed to reach the ropes. Lewis managed to get a big Jumping Piledriver, but was not able to get the pinfall, He continue to stomp Powerhouse, but 'House catched his leg, And Sweep him to the ground, He sent Lewis once again to the turnbuckle and ran after him, But Lewis jumped over and got a Full Nelson Slam on Powerhouse. After 2 minutes where Lewis controled the match, Powerhouse was sent to the outside, Lewis came after him but got his head straight into the Steps. Powerhouse sent him back into the ring, and tried to go for a Powerbomb, but Kai managed to kick out with a Backdrop, Powerhouse got up and Lewis kicked him in the back, and then got his "Big Apple" (Reverse Vertical Suplex into a 3/4 Nelson Bulldog) for the pinfall. Winner: Kai A. Lewis, advance to the next round. Byron Tanis Vs. Dizzy Dogg Both men came out to Boos from the crowd, first Dizzy then Tanis. They started out with circling the ring for the first few minutes, which got us to 3 minutes. After hooking up, Tanis whipped Dawg to the turnbuckle, and hammered on him. Byron continue with a SNAPSuplex And start punching out Dizzy Dawg, after the referee almost got to 5 he left him. Tanis went for a Piledriver but Dizzy got a Backdrop on Tanis, and followed it up with a Big Clothesline. A big Fist Fight went back and forth until Tanis racked the eyes of Dawg and got a low blow. The angry Tanis continue with an ilegal choke, but this time Dawg managed to gouge the eyes of Tanis, which forced him to let go of the choke, Dizzy got a big Jumping DDT and went for the pin, but Tanis got his hand up in the last second. Dizzy went to the top rope, but missed with a Diving Fist Drop, and Tanis was able to stomp the legs of The Dawg, after finally getting up The Dawg was sent to the ropes, and catched straight into an Over-The Shoulder Piledriver for the win. Winner: Byron Tanis, Advance to the next round. Vincent Valari Vs. Derek Sweger The match started as Sweger tried to attack Valari before the bell to no success, Valari turned around and hit him with "Mr. Belt". The bell rang, and the match was officially on his way, but Vincent was in control by then, he applied power move after power move, after couple of Elbowdrops on Sweger, He sent him to the turnbuckle, coming after him with a vicious Four arm smash. He sent some knees into the midsection of Derek. As we reach the 7th minute Vinecnt Valari is in total control of the match, Valari then Kick Sweger in the midsection, run to the ropes and Execute a Rockerdropper! But Valari refuse to pin the man, he picks up Sweger and execute his Ice-Freeze (Twisted Piledriver) for the, finally, pin. Winner: Vincent Valari, Advance to the next Round. The Boog-Man Vs. Neurosis The match begins as Neurosis takes advantage of his height advantage and power advantage with kicks and punches, along with Power moves trying to wear Boog Man out. Neurosis control the match for few more minutes, before getting The Boog in a Sleeperhold. Boog Man try to fight his way out, he hits a couple of elbows to Neurosis Midsection, runs to the ropes and execute a cross body press, for the 2 count. Boog Man applys a Figure Four LegLock but The Big Neurosis reaches the ropes in a matter of seconds. Boog Man execute a swinging neckbreaker, and goes to the top, he execute a flying elbowdrop, but Neurosis moves out of the way, Neurosis runs to the ropes, and goes for a Jumping elbowdrop but again nobody home, Boog gets up and connects with a spinning heel kick, he goes to the top, And execute a Moonsault Attack on the standing Neurosis, Boog Man stays in control for few minutes, until sending Neurosis out of the ring, He goes over the ropes for a Plancha, but hit the floor. Neurosis, sends him straight into the Steps, then the Apron, he picks the fallen Boog Man and sends him in, and he gets in only after getting a chair. Neurosis enter the ring, and try to hit Boog Man with the chair, but Boog Man ducks goes behind him and dropkicks him straight into the turnbuckle and the chair. Neurosis comes back and Boog Man hits with a Northern Lights >Suplex for the win. Winner: Boog Man, Advance to the next round. Chris Mann Vs. Nightmare We start things out with Nightmare challanging Mann to put his hand in his hand, Mann pretend to put the hand in there and kicks him in the midsection, Follow it up with a DDT. Mann with few Legdrops to the back of Nightmare head, and then execute a Bow & Arrow Submission, Where Nightmare gets in a bit of trouble, before finally managing to get out of the hold. Mann stays in control for the next couple of minutes with his technical style applying several holds to wear off the TV Champ. Finally His Black Widdow came up to the apron and distract the referee, Mann came to her, and got Low blowed from behind. He continued stomping on Mann and execute a Screwdriver at one point. He then started playing the crowd, and from behind Mann came with a rollup for the two count, Nightmare got up quickly missed with a clothesline, and Mann hit with a Belly to Back suplex. Mann Then executed a big Powerbomb for the Pin. Winner: Chris Mann, Advance to the next round. Buzz Tyler Vs. The Wraith Both Big Men went at it with punches at the begining of the match, with Wraith taking the advantage, He knocked down Tyler with a big Right hand, And then ran to the ropes, Tyler Lay down while Tyler came back, he went for a Backdrop but Tyler saw this and was able to execute a Piledriver. Buzz followed it up with 2 jumping elbowdrops, and a Kneedrop. Buzz got up and picked up Wraith, Sent him to the turnbuckle, from the impact he returned and hit a big time clothesline on Buzz. Wraith then execute a Camal Clutch on Tyler, which he held him in for about 30 seconds before leaving it, Tyler got up and was sent to the turnbuckle, where he was hit with chops, but soon he reversed it and hit several kicks and elbows to The Wraith. Both men continue to brawl around the ring, for another 3 minutes, which leads us to the 10 minute. The Wraith has the advantage, and is going for a Vertical suplex but Tyler catch him and turn it into a DDT. Tyler then went to the top rope, as Wraith came close to him, he execute a big Clothesline, and then went to the top again this time executing a Frog Splash for the pin. Winner: Buzz Tyler, Advance to the next round. Adam Fierce Vs. John Cole The match started in a staredown between the two opponents. Fierce with a wicked smile on his face, Started it out with a big Slap, Cole returned a favour with a big punch, And the match was on his way, The control in the begining went to Cole, with Techincal moves, a lot of Submission holds, after applying a Boston Crab, that Fierce was able to escape, Fierce was able to take control, after ducking a lariat and hitting a big DDT. Fierce played to the crowd for a little while, Cole gets up, And gets Shoulderblocked by Adam. He connects with few elbowdrops and then execute a Hammerlock Chokehold. Cole after a lot of pain, finally manage to escape the hold, Fierce try to send him to the ropes, but Cole reverse it, and As Fierce gets back he gets Powerslamed into the mat, Cole got the pin but only for a 2 count. The match continues With Fierce in control, few minutes of brawling between the two, after punches, kicks, Elbows, Knee drops, Cole got Fierce in a standing Chickenwing, but Fierce with a low blow with his leg, and then execute few diving Guillutine legdrops on Cole. Fierce lift up Cole and hits with a Reverse DDT. He go to the top rope, and execute a diving headbutt onto Cole. Both men are down, Fierce gets up first, Cole is right behind him, Fierce lift up John into what seems like an Outsiders's Edge, And turns it into a Neckbreaker! Fierce then go for the pin and takes the win. Winner: Adam Fierce, Advance to the next round. Jim Dalton Vs. Jason Perry In the begining of this match, both men locked up imidietly, With Perry getting the early advantage, whipping Dalton to the ropes. Perry sent Dalton to the ropes, and Jason came off with a lariat. Jason Hit a powerslam, and a jumping Knee drop, onto the Dragon. He continue with a brutal attack with different moves, getting us to the 5th minute. Perry sent him to the ropes, Dalton ducked a Clothesline and conected with a Crucifix. He followed it up with a flying Lariat, and a Moonsault, but only got a 2 for the count. Dalton continue to control the match with his high-flying aerial moves. As we reach the 8 minute, Jason regains control with a big Powerslam as he catched Dalton coming off the ropes, But he only got 1 for the pin. Perry execute a Doctor Bomb on the Dragon, And a knee drop to the face of Jim. Jim is hurt, and as he tries to get up, Perry kicks him back down. Perry goes to the top rope, but As he comes off, Dalton Dropkicks him. Dalton gets up and start hammering on the face of Perry, Then sends Perry to the ropes, while he climbs to the top rope, As Perry returns, he gets Bulldoged into the Mat! Dalton go for the pin, but don't hook the leg to get a 2 and a half count. Jim goes for a German Suplex but don't pin Perry, instead he plays the crowd a little. Delton then comes and execute a Senton Perry crumple. Dalton picks up the Devastator, and sends him to the turnbuckle, he comes after him and put him on the top rope, then execute a Frankensteine! He picks up Perry and goes for an Hurricanrana, but instead Perry Powerbombs him for the 2 and a half count. Perry lift Dalton and execute a Running Powerbomb, but still can't get the 3 count. All of a sudden the Bell Ring. Winner: This match has Been declared a DRAW. Therefore neither will go to the next round. Horror Vs. HICAL Hical comes out to a mixed reaction, Horror comes out running to the ring and imidietly attacks Hical. He hits with blows to the midsection, then goes to the apron and jumps over it to execute a Flying Clothesline. He goes out and brings a chair, in he puts it over the turnbuckle, and try to send Hical there, but Hical reverses it and Horror hit the chair. While the referee take the chair out of the ring, Horror hits with an eye rack to both eyes, and hit with series of headbutts to Hical Head. He goes for the pin and gets 2 and 3/3 before the referee sees Hical got his leg on the ropes. Horror then put Hical on the top rope, He comes after him, Hical throws him down, But on his way he hangs on to the top turnbuckle, Hical falls to the ground. Horror execute a reverse DDT, and then a Face Buster on Hical. Horror signals for the end, and right after execute a running Powerslam for the Victory. Winner: Horror, Advance to the next round. El Diablo Vs. Mitsuhara Misawa Misawa came out very intense, followed by El Diablo that got lots of Boos. Diablo hit few punches, but Misawa didn't seem to notice it, he came back firing away with elbows and open fists, until hitting a Upper Chop which got Diablo Down. Misawa continue with a Crooked Head Scisors, and refused to let go even several seconds after Diablo reached the ropes. Diablo, after ducking a Short Arm Lariat, got a Belly to Belly Suplex on Misawa, and went for the pin but only got one. Misawa execute a Urange, picks up El Diablo and execute an Exploder Suplex, to get only 2 count. Misawa sent Diablo to the ropes, and executed a Wheel Kick. Misawa execute Ankle Lock submission, but Diablo Manage to reach the ropes. An Angry Misawa Goes to the referee, from Behind Diablo comes with a Clothesline, but he ducks, He connects with a kick to the midsection and hit with a Kamikazi Bomb for the win. >Winner: Mitsuhara Misawa, Advance to the next round. Michael Lennox Vs. Scott Simpson The match begins with Simpson in control, after he attacked Lennox on the entrance way. Simpson throws Lennox outside, and follows him out, he execute a Piledriver on the floor, then enters the ring. As Lennox enters he get stomped by Simpson, who play to the fans a bit. Lennox, in the mean time, recovers and as Simpson turns around he is on the attack, he whips him to the turnbuckle, and sends vicious chops to the chest of Simpson. And execute a German Suplex, he then put Simpson on the top rope, but before he can follow him up, he gets Face Bustered straight into the ring, Simpson goes to the second rope and execute a flying fist drop. Simpson takes the advantage with several Hard Impact moves, and then goes on for a Tombstone, but Lennox is albe to reverse the move and apply a Tombstone of his own. Lennox Kicks Simpson in the midsection, runs to the ropes and execute a swinging neckbreaker, Then goes to the top rope, and comes down with a Diving headbutt. which miss it mark, as Simpson moves out of the way, he gets up and execute a legdrop on Lennox, He then picks up Lennox and goes for a Vertical Suplex but Lennox blocks it, he sends Simpson to the ropes and hit A backdrop. He execute a German Suplex once again, he picks up Simpson and put him on the top turnbuckle, he goes after him and execute Bourbon Street Wrecker (Diamond Cutter from the top turnbuckle) for the pin. Winner: Michael Lennox, Advance to the next round. Jake Rockwell Vs. Jenkins then return to the ring, goes to the Top rope, and an Asai Moonsault straight onto Styles, Then banged his head into the floor. Lightning gets up, and Execute a Spining Heel kick, But Benson Ducks, and goes to the top, to execute a flying elbowdrop! He goes for th pin but getting only 2, he Picks up Texas Lightning, and execute a German suplex into the pin! Winner: Dan Benson will advance to the next round.
