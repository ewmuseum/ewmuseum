The Official Rulebook of the NeWA

revised 28 Jul 1999, captured 14 Dec 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Official Rulebook of the NeWA Info The Official Rulebook of the National eWrestling Alliance Revised 07/28/1999 Version 1.8 Amendments and Revisions Rule Added on 7/29/1999 The NeWA Staff and Operations 1.000 Purpose To be the best ewrestling organization in the world. To accurately reflect and simulate the experiences of professional wrestling both for a promoter and a wrestler. 1.005 Ownership The NeWA is owned and operated by [name withheld] and [name withheld]. 1.010 President Shall be responsible for the NeWA's day-to-day operations. This includes, but is not limited to, admitting new member promotions, wrestlers, disciplinary actions, and promoting the general welfare of the organization. 1.020 Vice President Shall be responsible for operating the NeWA during an absence of the president. This includes, but is not limited to, advising the president of pertinent issues and discharging the duties assigned to him by the president. 1.25 Head Booker Shall be responsible for booking all NeWA Champion's appearances. The regional promoter may request an appearance and send this to the Head Booker. The Head Booker shall confirm such bookings with both the regional promoter and the NeWA Champion requested. After which time, this appearance shall be posted on the Official NeWA Championship Calendar. Head Booker may also schedule appearances in regional promotions for NeWA Champions to insure activity of said champions. Shall participate in regular conversations concerning potential title contenders, angles, etc. with the NeWA Owner, the NeWA President, and the NeWA Vice President via e-mail, ICQ and monthly chats. 1.040 Commissioner Shall be responsible for insuring that all promotions follow the rules stated herein. 1.050 Talent Coordinator Shall be responsible for compiling a list of wrestlers and their vital information that are competing in the NeWA and insuring that these names are not duplicated. Shall also maintain a database of this information for use by the NeWA and its members. 1.055 Director of Recruiting Shall be responsible for seeking out new talent and securing their services in the NeWA. 1.060 General Counsel Shall be responsible for advising the president on legal matters including rights to wrestlers, stable names, show names, etc. that may belong to another promotion in our world. Shall also maintain a list of wrestlers under contract to the NeWA and advise the NeWA President regarding re-signing talent as well as acquiring available unsigned talent. 1.070 Director of Development Shall be responsible for securing locations for regional promotions to hold shows as well as securing locations for NeWA events. Shall also coordinate and define territories for member promotions and determine exclusive and developmental regions. Shall advise the NeWA President of the availability of territories as potential members request such. Shall submit a report each month to the NeWA President and NeWA Webmaster regarding the status and activity of member promotions. 1.080 Webmaster Shall be responsible for updating, site development, and general improvements of the official NeWA website as necessary. 1.090 Championship Committee See 2.050 Championships 1.100 NeWA Wrestler Contracts NeWA Champions shall be under contract to the NeWA upon capturing a NeWA Championship Title. All rules of Sect. 4 shall apply. NeWA Non-Champion Contracts The NeWA may sign wrestlers to long term contracts beyond those of the normal thirty- (30) day championship contracts. Said contracts are to be sent to the General Counsel. The General Counsel shall maintain a list of long term contracted wrestlers. The General Counsel shall inform the President of any long-term contracts that will expire no later than thirty (30) days prior to its expiration. The NeWA may sell, trade, and/or assign the rights of said contract as deemed necessary. NeWA Regional Promoters 2.000 Membership Membership in the NeWA is available to any promotion that requests such and meets the criteria established by the president. Said promotion must fill out an application and agree to be bound by all rules and regulations described herein and any modifications made hereafter. 2.003 Sanctioning All Promotions are required to post all information and links located on our sanctioning page in full on the front page of their website. New members have one (1) week to do so. Failure to provide this information is grounds for revocation of membership. 2.005 Suspension/Revocation of Membership A member promotion may have its membership suspended and/or revoked for failure to adhere to the rules stated herein and where such is necessary for the betterment of the organization. 2.010 Qualifications Qualified promotions will have a website, email address, an ICQ number, and ability to run a promotion to the standards set by the NeWA and its member promotions. 2.020 Existing Promotions Promotions that have been in operation for over three (3) months before applying for membership shall be considered "existing" promotions. Upon acceptance by the president, they shall be given full membership privileges. 2.030 Start Up Promotions Promotions that have not been in operation for over three (3) months and/or have less than ten (10) wrestlers on their roster before applying for membership shall be considered "start up" promotions. Upon acceptance by the president, they shall be given provisional membership privileges. After a probationary period of no more than of ninety (90) days, said promotion shall be given full membership privileges. 2.040 Territory Promotions shall be assigned a territorial name representative of their home office. Promotions shall also be given a designated territory in which they may schedule shows exclusively. Other regional promotions may schedule matches in exclusive territories only upon approval of the home promotion. Territories that are not exclusive may be run by any regional promotion. If a regional promotion fails to run a show in a state that they have exclusive rights to at least once every other month, said state shall no longer be considered exclusive to that promotion. Said state may be reassigned exclusivity in the future to the same or other promotion. The NeWA Director of Development will coordinate the establishment of territories and will assist promotions in securing adequate facilities to promote their shows. 2.050 Championships 2.051 Regional Championships Each member promotion shall have a minimum of one (1) singles championship and one (1) tag team championship. There is no maximum amount, however good judgment should be applied. It is recommended that there should be no more than one (1) title for every ten- (10) wrestlers. Names of championship titles shall not conflict with the names of any NeWA Championship. Existing promotions with title name conflicts are exempt from this rule, however no new titles may be added that will conflict with NeWA title names. No member promotion may have their own "World Title." Any promotion claiming such will have ten (10) days to downgrade the title or face automatic revocation of membership. Each promoter has total rights to book and award their championships any way they choose in their own Region. They can also create "joint" titles with other regions of the NeWA. 2.052 NeWA Board Sanctioned Championships Board sanctioned championships are decided by Championship Committees set up by NeWA Front Office. World Heavyweight World Tag Team International Heavyweight International Tag Team: Shall consist of the NeWA President, Vice President and Head Booker. These three have equal vote on all title matches. World Hardcore: Decided by Hardcore Championship Committee. All Changes must be approved by NeWA President. United States Heavyweight United States Tag Team: Decided by a committee of United States Promoters. All members must notify NeWA President via email of any decided changes. Canadian Heavyweight Canadian Tag Team: Decided by a committee of Canadian Promoters. All members must notify NeWA President via email of any decided changes. World Television World Cruiserweight World Six Man Tag Team Decided by individual regional promoter hosting the title match based on role-plays/angles. Any change must be approved by NeWA President. 2.055 Booking NeWA Champions Promoters must schedule NeWA Champions appearances via the NeWA Head Booker. Promoters should notify the NeWA Head Booker denoting the Date, Title, Challenger (if known) and promotion in order to prevent "double booking." Promoters shall also inform the Head Booker of the results of such matches so they may be updated on the Champions Calendar. These championship matches must appear on the Calander at least ten days before the scheduled match. Any NeWA Title Matches not listed on the Champions Calendar (resulting from the regional promoter failing to properly notify the Head Booker) shall not be considered "sanctioned" by the NeWA and any title changes that occur shall not be recognized by the NeWA. All NeWA Championship Match results must be known within two days of the scheduled match.  If the promoter fails to post the result to his website or email the results to the Webmaster, then the match will be deemed a no contest. Promoters must confirm any NeWA title changes on their regional shows with the NeWA President prior to announcing a title change. No confirmation is required if said champion fails to role-play for the title defense provided that the promoter provided proper notification to the champion. However, it is preferred that a confirmation is given. Upon request, each promotion is entitled to receive a minimum of one (1) date per month each for World and World Tag Team Title defenses. 2.060 Status Reports Each member promotion must submit a status report to the president and head booker every month. This report shall contain current champions, top contenders, who is getting pushes and why, opinions of current NeWA Champions, potential NeWA champions from said promotion, and requests for any NeWA Title Matches. This may be submitted via email or via a form on the NeWA website. 2.070 World Rankings Each promotion is required to submit their official rankings to the NeWA once per month as part of the Status Report.  This shall be done during a time period denoted on the NeWA website. The preferred method is submitting them via a form on the NeWA website. Promoters may submit them via email. Failure to submit your rankings may result in a lower ranking for your wrestlers or possible omission of them. 2.080 NeWA Pay-Per-Views Each promotion is encouraged to participate in the NeWA PPVs. Promoters may enter a match or ask to challenge current NeWA Champions. Promoters may also request to host PPVs. Promoters are responsible for booking matches that include wrestlers from their home office when they appear on NeWA PPVs. If wrestlers are from different home offices, the Head Booker shall determine the promoter responsible for the match. The scripts for each PPV shall be emailed to the NeWA President for review and then to the NeWA Webmaster in a timely fashion to insure that the results may be posted on the NeWA PPV results area no later than the day after the event. 2.085 Non-PPV NeWA Events Promoters may request to host non-ppv NeWA events. Such events include NeWA Championship Tournaments and other special memorial tournaments or events. 2.095 Matches Promoters shall provide reasonable notice of matches to their wrestlers in order for them to adequately participate. Promoters may run their matches, in number, frequency, and type, as they see fit, as long as they do not conflict with the NeWA rules. 2.100 Open Chats Promoters are asked to participate in chat sessions as often as possible. The NeWA requests that you attend at least one (1) session per month. Access to the official NeWA chatroom is via a link on the NeWA website. Many productive things occur during chats including discussing NeWA Title challengers, PPV matches, and hosting of events. See the NeWA website for exact days and time for these open chats. 2.102 Closed Chats The NeWA requires all promoters and NeWA office staff to use ICQ as a method for sending instant messages and to participate in "closed door" chats using this software. 2.105 Roster Promoters shall have a maximum of forty (40) wrestlers on their roster at any given time. These wrestlers must be under a "full-time" or "part-time" contract basis. Promoters may have any number of non-role-player controlled wrestlers under a developmental contract (see section 2.110 for further information). All Promoters, upon entering the NeWA, must submit their complete roster to the NeWA Vice President. This information will be added to the NeWA worldwide roster that is available on the main site. This worldwide roster will be used as a source to see who is currently in the NeWA and where they are.  It will also contain a list of free agents and links to individual wrestler's personal websites. The NeWA administration will use this roster to monitor roster rules. Once each promotion's initial roster has been submitted, said Promoter must keep the Vice President informed with any and all roster changes through the duration of their NeWA Membership. 2.110 Contracts Full-time contract is defined as a wrestler designates a promotion as his home booking office and the promoter adds said wrestler to his roster. Part-time contract is defined as a wrestler designates a promotion as his part-time booking office and the promoter adds said wrestler to his roster. Developmental contract is defined as a promoter gains rights to a non-role-played wrestler in hopes that a role-player will be found to handle said wrestler. Promoter may sign said wrestler and keep him for up to six months as a non-roster wrestler. After 6 months, the wrestler must either be added to the roster or released outright. Promoters may sell, trade, and/or assign the rights of any wrestler signed to a developmental contract. At any time during the 6-month contract, if said wrestler appears on a show, he must be added to the roster on either a full or part-time contract. Promoters may withdraw a non-role-played wrestler from his roster and place him on a developmental contract for up to two months as long as he does not appear on any shows during that time period. Once he does, said wrestler must be added to the roster. Try-out contract is a "non-roster 30-day limit contract" appearance prior to adding said wrestler to the roster. A promoter has 30-days from a wrestler's first appearance to add said wrestler to his roster. After this time period has expired, said wrestler cannot appear again unless added to the roster. Per night appearance contract allows a wrestler to appear for one additional show per month for one non-contracted promotion. Wrestler does not have to be added to the promoter's roster for a per night appearance. Touring contract - promoters may "lend" wrestlers from their roster to another promoter for a tour of that territory. Wrestlers must stay on their original roster. Tours may last up to 3 weeks at a time. Promoters may release a wrestler from their roster at any time to make room for a new wrestler or for any other reason. NeWA Regional Wrestlers 3.000 Wrestler/Role-player The NeWA accepts original and real wrestlers. All real wrestlers must be approved by the NeWA General Counsel before appearing on any RP board or any regional promotion event. Real wrestlers under contract to the NeWA or its member promotions may be assigned to a role-player upon request if approved by the NeWA President if under NeWA contract, or the regional promoter if under contract to his promotion. 3.005 Name The name/character of a wrestler shall not be duplicated with current wrestlers in the NeWA or affiliate promotions. 3.008 Matches Wrestlers shall receive reasonable notice of future matches in order for them to adequately participate. 3.010 Role-plays (language) All role-players must send in RP's via email or on the message system of the promotion they work for as often as the promotion requires. Failure to comply without proper notification will result in the offending wrestler being removed from the roster of that promotion. RP's should be creative but discretion is advised. Language that cannot be on broadcast television should not appear in a RP unless it is either masked or bleeped. Promoters may have more stringent or lenient rules. You should refer to that promotions rules and adhere to those when posting on regional RP boards. RP's should not contain any other person's wrestler(s), promoter(s), NeWA Office Staff, or other NeWA related characters without their prior permission or the permission of the NeWA President. Penalties will vary depending on the severity of the infraction. NeWA announcers and reporters may be used without prior permission as long as they are used in their proper function and no physical harm occurs to them. 3.020 Pay per views To appear on a NeWA PPV, a wrestler should issue challenges on the NeWA RP board and solicit help from his home office promoter to secure the bout. All wrestlers shall receive two- (2) weeks notice of any upcoming NeWA PPV's by checking their promotion's website or the NeWA website. 3.030 Championships No role-player may hold more than four (4) regional titles at one time. Regional championships are determined in any manner the regional promoter sees fit. The NeWA Championship Committee determines all NeWA championships. (See sec. 4.050 - NeWA Title Changes for further details) 3.040 Handling Wrestlers No person shall handle more than five (5) wrestlers at any time. This does not apply to promoters who use non-role-played wrestlers to fill their roster. 3.045 Wrestler Contracts Full-time Each wrestler shall designate a home booking office, which shall be considered as working for them full-time. By doing so, said wrestler contracts to perform for that regional promotion as deemed necessary by the promoter. Said wrestler may give notice to his home booking office and select a new home booking office provided he fulfills all his scheduled matches. A promoter has the option to book said wrestler for up to fifteen (15) days from date of notice given. After such time said wrestler may begin wrestling full-time for his new home office. Part-time Said wrestler may also select a part-time office for which he may wrestle and shall be considered as working for them part-time. By doing so, said wrestler contracts to perform for that regional promotion as deemed necessary by the promoter provided it does not conflict with his full-time promotion's scheduled matches. Said wrestler may give notice to his part-time office and select a new part-time office at anytime provided he fulfills all his scheduled matches. A promoter has the option to book said wrestler for up to fifteen (15) days from date of notice given. After such time said wrestler may begin wrestling part-time for his new part-time office. In the event a wrestler wants to switch his full-time and part-time contracts with each other, there will be no need of a delay, however he must still notify both promoters of his intentions. Scheduled matches are defined as matches published either on a website, via email, ICQ or via chat. If a wrestler fails to give proper notification of his intent to leave a promotion, said promoter has the right to book said wrestler for up to thirty (30) days before removing him from his roster. Each wrestler has the option to terminate his contract with a promoter if said promoter fails to provide wrestler with a minimum of two (2) matches per month. Said wrestler is under no obligation to appear and said promoter is prohibited from booking said wrestler upon this breech of contract. Said wrestler shall simply notify the promoter and the NeWA President of this breech of contract. Try-out Wrestlers may appear as a non-roster wrestler for a promoter on a "try-out" basis for up to thirty (30) days from his first appearance. After such wrestler must designate said promotion as "full-time" or "part-time" and be added to the roster. If not added to the roster, wrestler cannot appear for said promotion again until added to the roster. Per-night Wrestler may appear on a "per night contract" basis as a non-roster wrestler for one show a month for any one promotion. NeWA Champions will automatically be signed to a NeWA contract. Please refer to section 4.000 for rules concerning contracts for such wrestlers as they differ from regional wrestler contracts. 3.050 Disciplinary actions The NeWA reserves to right to discipline, suspend, or expel anyone at any time for any reason. 3.060 Pushes/Title Shots Getting a push and title shots are received either from a promoter, other opponents, and from your RP's. Your rank in the NeWA World Rankings will help determine your participation in NeWA PPV's and title shots. NeWA Championship Wrestlers 4.000 NeWA Champions Champions are expected to make themselves accessible to all promoters in order to defend their title in all applicable territories (i.e.: US Champ should defend in all promotions within the US) and are under no restrictions as to the number of regional promotions they may appear at. 4.005 NeWA Champions Contract NeWA Champions are contracted for one (1) calendar month, starting with the month in which they win a NeWA Championship, to appear at NeWA events and/or NeWA member promotions' events. This contract shall renew itself at the first (1st) of each month for an additional calendar month perpetually as long as said wrestler remains a champion or until said champion provides proper notification of his intent to leave the NeWA. Upon loss of NeWA Championship status, wrestler is still bound to the end of that calendar month in which the loss occurred. If the loss occurs within the last seven- (7) days of a calendar month, the NeWA has the option to renew the contract for one (1) additional calendar month. After such time wrestler shall be considered a free agent and regular contract rules shall then apply to him. In the event a current champion intends to leave the NeWA, forfeit a title, or surrender a title, he shall provide the president with a thirty- (30) day notice of his intentions. The NeWA reserves the right to schedule matches for said wrestler for up to 30-days from the date of notification. The NeWA also reserves the right to book matches with said wrestler for up to 30-days from the date the NeWA decides that a wrestler has failed to give proper notification. The NeWA may assign a new handler for the wrestler that fails to give proper notification. 4.010 RP's for defenses/NeWA RP board Champions are expected to RP on the regional RP board before each match, whether title or non-title. In the case of a title match, failure to do so may result in a title change. Champions are required to RP on the NeWA RP board a minimum of once per month in addition to any RP's for PPV title defenses. Prior notice of a problem must be given to the president or vice president to provide an exception to this rule. 4.020 PPV defenses NeWA Champions are expected to defend their title at each NeWA PPV and to role-play heavily for any PPV title defenses on the NeWA RP board. The NeWA President may exempt a champion from a title defense on a PPV. 4.030 Other titles All NeWA Champions are prohibited from holding any regional titles or additional NeWA titles that overlap in territory and designation. (i.e. cannot hold the United States and International Title, but could hold both US and Canadian Titles). Other titles much be vacated immediately or all their titles held will be stripped. NeWA World 6-Man Champions shall not be prohibited from holding any other titles except other regional 6-Man championships. NeWA singles champions may hold regional tag team championships and NeWA tag team champions may hold regional singles championships as long as these regional titles do not conflict with their NeWA championship scheduling. 4.040 Home booking office participation Champions are expected to stay active in their home booking office they were contracted to under a full-time contract before they captured a NeWA Championship. When scheduling appearances the home booking office shall receive priority. Champions may change their home booking office provided they give proper notification and fulfill all scheduled matches. 4.050 NeWA Title Changes Winning a NeWA Championship is based on several factors, unlike regional promotion championships. Regional titles are controlled solely by the regional promoter and they may base title changes on any format they so desire. NeWA titles are decided much differently. The NeWA Championship Committee usually determines title changes based on the following criteria: 1) challengers recent performance 2) champion's opinion of his opponent 3) regional promoter's opinion of his challenger 4) role-play of the challenger 5) role-play of the champion. Title changes are not based solely on role-plays. Doing so could result in the World Championship changing hands several times each week. This is not good for the wrestlers or the organization. The National eWrestling Alliance is based on, and functions like the National Wrestling Alliance (NWA). By doing so, we are officially endorsed and recognized by the NWA. Much like the NeWA, titles are influenced by running storylines and angles. It is possible to win a NeWA title on any given night, but it is not likely. Sometimes a role-play is just so good that plans are changed. But there are many factors that contribute to a title change as denoted above. It usually takes several attempts to capture a NeWA title...just like in the real thing. Wrestlers need to campaign for titles. This includes challenging champions on their promotion's RP board and on the NeWA role-play board, getting their promoter to "push" them into championship status, and of course, demonstrating an exceptional ability to role-play. 4.055 NeWA Title Matches - Challenge Rules The NeWA President may declare certain matches and/or tournaments to be determined strictly on the performance and quality of role-plays.
