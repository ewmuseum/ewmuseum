ESWP: Founding Post

17 February 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Do you want to be part of a fed with lots of action? Do you want to be in a fed that gives everyone a push towards stardom? Do you want to be part of a fed that has not one, not two, not three, but 20 TITLES!!! If you answered yes, then you should join the ELECTRONIC SHOOT WRESTLING PROMOTION! The ESWP is open to men and women in both singles and tag team competition. It is also divided into four seperate regions, plus the coveted World titles. Everyone who wishes to be part of the action can have a chance at greatness. The titles in ESWP are as follows: ESWP World Title ESWP World Tag Team Titles ESWP World Women's Title ESWP World Women's Tag Team Titles ESWP North American Title (tournament coming soon) ESWP North American Tag Team Titles ESWP North American Women's Title ESWP North American Women's Tag Team Titles ESWP South American Title ESWP South American Tag Team Titles ESWP South American Women's Title ESWP South American Women's Tag Team Titles ESWP European Title ESWP European Tag Team Titles ESWP European Women's Title ESWP European Women's tag Team Titles ESWP Asian Title ESWP Asian Tag Team Titles ESWP Asian Women's Title ESWP Asian Women's Tag Team Titles Right now, I am accepting applications for entrants into the North American Title tournament. I will also be accepting Tag teams for the North American division, though that tournament may not start until a week after the first tourney starts. Once I have recieved 20 applications the tournament will begin, and priority to placement in the tourney will be given to singles wrestlers first. I am willing to keep a waiting list for wrestlers who wish to compete for the other titles except the World Titles which will be determined as soon as all the regional champs are determined. I hope to have 1-3 cards a week during the tourney, and one regular weekly show as soon as the first champ is crowned. Well, that's it for now! Just fill out the application below and e-mail it to me. I will send you confirmation and more information on the tournament as soon as possible. Hope to see you in the ring.
