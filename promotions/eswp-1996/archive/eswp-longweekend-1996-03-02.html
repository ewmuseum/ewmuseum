ESWP LongWeekend (3/2/96)

2 March 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
("You Got Another Thing Coming" by Judas Priest plays as JD Smooth and the Heavenly Hawaiians head down the aisle) "This tag team match is set for one fall. Introducing first, accompanied by JD SMOOTH, from Hawaii, at a totla combined weight of 262 pounds KILLER KIANA AND KIMIKO MANAKEA, THE HEAVENLY HAWAIIANS!!! And there opponents, currently in the ring along with their manager THE TALL TROLL, at total combined weight of 239 pounds, SWEET SUE AND KAREN "BUGS" HUFFMAN, THE SPRINGFIELD SUCCULENTS!" DING! DING! DING! HIJO: And Sweet Sue starts things off with a hiptoss sending Kimiko Manakea to the mat. Followed by a dropkick to the back of the head. LAWSON: Now Sue has a headlock on Manakea. The ref is asking Kimiko, but I think it's to early for a submission in this match. Kimiko is getting back to her feet. And a rake to the face of Sue breaks the hold. HIJO: Kimiko has Sue by the hair, and is dragging her to the corner. Kiana tags in and the two are double teaming Sue. LAWSON: "Bugs" Huffman runs in to help, but the ref cuts her off. The Heavenly Hawaiians continue with a vicious double-team on Sue. HIJO: Kimiko has now gone to the outside. Kiana with a series of knees to Sue's stomach. Another tag brings Kimiko in, and another double-team. LAWSON: And another run in by Huffman, but again the ref stops her as Kiana uses the tag rope to choke Sue. HIJO: Kimiko now punching Sue in the face. These to girls claim to be Heavenly, but they've been wrestling like devils in this match so far. LAWSON: Yes they have, but the ref now starts counting as Kimiko chokes Sue in the corner. HIJO: Kimiko lets go to yell at the ref. Bad move, Sue catches Kimiko with THE JERSEY JAW JACKER! Kimiko goes down. Sue with the cover 1! and a save by Kiana. LAWSON: sue fighting back all she can but Kiana tags out to Manakea. HIJO: Huffman has yet to be in this match. LAWSON: A scoop by Kimiko. She's climbing the top rope and THE PINEAPPLE PILEDRIVER! 1! 2! 3! This one is over. DING! DING! DING! ("You Got Another Thing Coming" plays) "Here are your winners, THE HEAVENLY HAWAIIANS!!" LAWSON: And a win for JD Smooth's stable as the Heavenly Hawaiians score a win tonight. Coming up next is our double main event as both the North American Men's and Women's titles will be on the line! "This contest is scheduled for one fall, and is for the ESWP NORTH AMERICAN WOMEN'S CHAMPIONSHIP! Introducing first the challenger, accompanied by the Tall Troll, from Cinnaminsom, New Jersey, weighing 115 pounds, LOVELY LIZ STROHLHEIM!" ("Black Moon" by Killing Joke plays as the Harlequins head towards the ring.) "And her opponent, accompanied by Tragedy, from parts unknown, weighing 122 pouns here is THE ESWP NORTH AMERICAN WOMEN'S CHAMPION, HARLEQUIN COMEDY!" LAWSON: Comedy in her first title defense against the woman she beat in the finals of last week's tournament. HIJO: And the North American Women's champ starts this match with a dropkick knocking Liz through the ropes. Comedy runs into the ropes, and a tope by Comedy knocks Liz back into the guardrail. LAWSON: Comedy reenters the ring. And Liz follows in only to recieve a series of boots to the back from the Women's champ. HIJO: Comedy whips Liz into the ropes, reversal by Liz, and an elbowsmash by Liz. Liz with a gutwrench suplex, and a cover 1! 2! kickout by Comedy. LAWSON: LIz runs the ropes, whoa she's tripped by Tragedy standing on the outside. HIJO: The ref is warning Tragedy as Comedy hits Liz with an eye gouge behind his back. LAWSON: The ref is back on the job, as a savate kick sends Liz to the mat. Spinning toe hold attempt by Comedy, but Liz kicks her off. HIJO: Atomic drop by Liz, now it looks like a back suplex, yes. LAWSON: Liz with the cover and 1! 2! Tragedy puts Comedy's foot on the rope. The referee is issuing another warning to Tragedy. HIJO: Liz drags Comedy to the center of the ring now. Airplane spin by Liz, I haven't seen that move in a long time. LAWSON: Comedy stumbles near Liz's corner. She kicks at Tall Troll. Troll gets up on the ring apron. HIJO: The ref is trying to get Troll down. Wait, Tragedy is taking advantage of the ref not looking at the match, he's tossed that steel mask he wears to Comedy. LAWSON: The ref hasn't seen it yet. Comedy takes a swing at Liz, AND LIZ BLOCKS THE SHOT! She takes the mask from Comedy and hits her with it! HIJO: OH NO!! The referee saw that, he's calling for the bell. DING! DING! DING! "The winner of this bout, by disqualification, AND STILL ESWP NORTH AMERICAN WOMEN'S CHAMPION, HARLEQUIN COMEDY!!" LAWSON: NO! Liz has been DQed in this match. That's not going to do well for her in the rankings. HIJO: It's not her fault really, Comedy was the one who was cheating. LAWSON: Well, it's a little to late now. Comedy has successfully retained her title, even if it was through unscrupulous means. HIJO: Well only one more match tonight, but first here's what you'll be seeing later this weekend. ANNOUNCER: This Saturday Night, from the meadowlands Arena, we will crown the next of our many champions, as we hold the first ever ESWP NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP TOURNAMENT! 8 teams battle to be the best! Plus, six newcomers battle it out in two triangle matches for a chance to become the number one contender to the titles. Tomorrow on ESWP LongWeekend. LAWSON: That's right, The North American Tag Team Tournament plus two triangle matches this Saturday. But right now, let's get to the final match of the night. "This contest is set for one fall, and is for the ESWP NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP!! Currently in the ring the challenger, from St. Petersburg, Russia, weighing 245 pounds. "THE RUSSIAN BEAST" ANATOLY KAZAKOV!!" ("Mama Said Knock You Out" By Scatterbrain plays over the PA as Rage charges the ring, with his manager Union Kane walking behind him.) "And his opponent....AAAHHHHH!" LAWSON: Once again Rage has scared our ring announcer out of the ring. HIJO: Maybe he should just stand on the outside when he does the intros. LAWSON: You're probably right as These two lock men lock up. HIJO: Kazakov with an armbar brings the champ to one knee early in this match. LAWSON: Rage is trying to make his way to the ropes, standing wristlock by Kazakov. HIJO: Rage attempts to escape the hold, somersault by Rage, and he reversed that wristlock into a hammerlock on Kazakov. LAWSON: Kazakov reaches the ropes, and Rage breaks the hold. HIJO: These two men gained a lot of respect for each other when they last met. They may gain a lot more respect after tonight. LAWSON: Kazakov with a football tackle sends rage down. A cover and 1! 2! and a kickout by Rage. Kazakov tries a German suplex and 1! 2! and another kickout by Rage. HIJO: Kazakov again with an armbar on Rage, but Rage makes it to the ropes. LAWSON: Rage strikes back with a roundhouse right, and another, and another. HIJO: Rage is going back to what he does best, and that's out-and-out brawling! LAWSON: Kazakov reeling from those blows. Rage with a scoop and a slam! HIJO: Rage is getting back in control here as he whips The Russian Beast into the corner. Rage charges the corner, but meets the boot of Kazakov instead. LAWSON: Rage is staggering as Kazakov chicken wings Rage. HIJO: Rage with a jawbreaker makes the escape. I think Kazakov bit his lip, his mouth is bleeding. LAWSON: Kazakov swings at Rage, but Rage ducks. Rage has Kazakov scooped up! HIJO: You know what this means. LAWSON: Rage into the corner, and another... HIJO: And another.... and another BOTH: RAGIN' SLAM!!! LAWSON: A cover and 1! 2! 3! DING! DING! DING! "The winner of this match and still ESWP NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPION!! RAGE!!" LAWSON: What an awesome match!! Well, that's all the time we have for today. Tune in tomorrow for the North American Tag Team Tournament. Until then, this is David Lawson for El Hijo del Diablo Numero Dos saying goodnight everyone!
