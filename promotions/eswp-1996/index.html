Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Electronic Shoot Wrestling Promotion launched on 17 February 1996 pitching a four-region, twenty-title ladder — World, North American, South American, European, and Asian championships, each split into men's singles, men's tag, women's singles, and women's tag divisions — run on TNM 6.12, and opened applications for its North American tournament first.

Two days later its own roster report showed twelve wrestlers filling six of those divisions, including two Asian tag teams already entered, the Japanese Dragons and the Super Japan Fighters, and a women's Asian tag entry, the Ladies of the Orient, while most other brackets sat empty. The promotion switched its tournament format from round-robin to single elimination on the spot, rewarding the earliest sign-ups with byes into the second round.

Its debut pay-per-view, “What the World Is Watching,” on 30 March, crowned two champions: “The McManiac” Susan Meagher won the World Women's Championship, and in the main event The Delinquent beat Ronnie “Rolls Royce” Martin eight falls to one for the Heavyweight Championship of the World, the deciding fall coming after Martin's own injured knee was jabbed by his opponent's cornerman and The Delinquent struck him openly with a ruler in full view of the referee.

The promotion went on hiatus starting 1 June 1996, citing unspecified problems at school, its operator recommending two other feds, the WFSW and the CWL, to members looking for action in the meantime. It returned on schedule that September, resuming with a new fall-season card on 27 September.

It kept running into 1997 and 1998: further weekly broadcasts survive from January 1997, and a further recruitment post from December of that year, by which point its own operator was expanding the same acronym a second way, as the “Elite Shoot Wrestling Promotion.” Community postings and a sibling federation's own broadcast still listed one of its wrestlers as late as April and May 1998.

No closure notice survives. The record simply stops there.
