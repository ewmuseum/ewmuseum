Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Pro Fantasy Wrestling League introduced itself on 1 July 1996, its founder Richie announcing that its first pay-per-view had already run the day before. That show crowned five champions across a night of tournament finals: Dan Fuller took the Jr title, Volcano the Pro title, the Titan Team the North American Tag titles, Endless Destruction the World Tag titles, and Petty Officer Pain the World Heavyweight Championship.

Its record over the following weeks tracked a long-running feud between Matt Moyer's corner and the Rayden Brothers, dating back to the league's own second card, and a jobber gauntlet in which a debuting Monstrosity flattened five straight challengers — Fireman Bob, the Executioner, Graffiti, El Gran Alcalde, and Thunderhawk — before Thunderhawk finally staggered him with a single punch. By September 1996, Petty Officer Pain had lost the World title off-page and rebranded himself the Powerful Overlord of Pain, a darker heel gimmick under which he kept dismantling former rivals like Johnny Dingo, while “Badd Boy” Derrick Stardom, the new champion, defended the belt against Sico Gregg with his own finisher, the Starfall.

The league survived a near-closure and a fan-driven revival mid-run before folding for good on 5 November 1997, when Richie announced he was becoming vice president of a different promotion, WIW; the newsgroup's own year-end awards ballot that December still listed “PFWL shutting down” among the year's notable news items.
