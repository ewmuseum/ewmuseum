PFWL PPV, 1 July 1996

1 July 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This past Sunday, the 30th, saw the PFWL's first PPV. This saw a unique blend of tourneys to name the PFWL's first champions. Dan Fuller was named the Jr Champ after defeating Ted Rayden. Volcano was named Pro Hampion after defeating Thunderhawk. The Titan Team was named North American Tag Team Champs after defeating The Toxic Twins. Endless Destruction was named World Tag Team Champions after defeating The Mercenaries. Last but not least, Petty Officer Pain was proclaimed World Heavyweight Champion after defeating Mastedon. This was a pretty good PPV. Full of butt-kicking action, it set the stage for stories to come. For example, Derrick Starrdom defeated Kyle Bannor to recieve a title shot at the next PPV, Rough House '96!! You can check out the action at www.worldy.com/~kenwa/index.shtml
