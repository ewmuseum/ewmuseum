PFWL Battle Brawl, 7 July 1996

7 July 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This past Friday saw the PFWL's Battle Brawl telecast. It was quite an eventful card as we at the PFWL prepare for RoughHouse 96! Let's get to the action! Kinkos defeated Johnny Dingo in an exciting match. This boosts Kinkos' ranking enought to give him a shot at Volcano, the PFWL Pro Champion. The Marquis, Matt Moyer, and The Demolition Man defeated the Rayden Brothers. This is a feud that is long lived here. Moyer and the Rayden's have been feuding since the PFWL's second card!! At the end of this match, Damage Inc. commenced whooping ass on the Raydens when the Titan Team and Johnny Dingo ran to the ring and cleaned house! The PFWL Pro Champion, Volcano defeated Thunderhawk in an action-packed match. The PFWL Jr Champ, "Dirty"Dan Fuller successfully defended his title against the Terminator. This guy (Terminator) wants a shot at the Worls Title...he can't even hold his own against the Jr Champ!! A little out of his leagus, I'd say. And the Main Event was a Non-Title Steel Cage Match between the PFWL World Champion, Petty Officer Pain, and Mastedon (who POP defeated for the title at the last PPV). Petty Officer Pain claimed another resounding victory after giving Matedon a vicious Deep-Six (DDT off top rope). POP will be defending his title at RoughHouse 96 against Derrick Stardom. This is a VERY hot match-up. Everyone is REALLY excited about it. One other event took place: The Enforcer Of Pain came out with a new attitude...Mr Vain Arro Gant. Hmmm, maybe this can help his record? Anyway, see you in the ring!!
