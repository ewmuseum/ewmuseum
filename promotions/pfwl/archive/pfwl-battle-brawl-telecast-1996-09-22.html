PFWL Battle Brawl (telecast recap), 22 September 1996

22 September 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Battle Brawl-September 20th ---------------------------------------------------------------------- (clips of Trillionaire Terry are shown....suddenly Casey Blain's voice is heard) Casey-...DEATH...it's a scary thing....being dead means to be deceased...extinct...and extinguished... (the camera cuts to the end of Elimination, with Fred Smith talking to President Richie about receiving a new arena) Casey...but when the PFWL was nearly gone, the fans pulled together and saved this great league....the PFWL is not dead...the PWFL is not deceased....for it is just beginning for the Pro Fantasy Wrestling League... (the camera cuts to the NEW PFWL arena, it shows shots of the outside, then cuts to the inside....the fans are screaming, and the camera cuts to Fred Smith and Brian Rundle) Fred-WELCOME EVERYONE TO SAN DIEGO CALIFORNIA!!!! WELCOME EVERYONE TO THE NEW PFWL ARENA!!!!!! AND WELCOME EVERYONE TO THE RETURN OF BATTLE BRAWL!!!!! I am Fred Smith, and joining me here tonight is Brian Rundle! Brian-Well you know, I never thought this day would come. When Trillionaire Terry came into the PFWL and said he has bought the old PFWL Arena, I thought it was over. I still can't beleive that we aren't all working at MacDonalds! Fred-Well folks, we have some great action for you tonight, including the first title defence for Derrick Stardom! So lets get up to the ring to the newest member of the PFWL, our ring announcer Bill Fox!! ---------------------------------------------------------------------- Bill-Welcome everyone to the return of the PFWL!!!! This opening contest is a tag team attraction, and is scheduled for one fall. Bill-Coming down the aisle, from England, and weighing at a total combined weight of 575 pounds, the Cold Knights!!!! (the Imperial March, from the Empire Strikes Back, begins to play) Bill-...and their opponents...accompanied to the ring by Destructor, weighing at a total combined weight of 640 pounds, the team of the Terminator and the Dark Lord, the DEATHSTALKERS!!!! The Terminator and Grey Knight begin the match. They lock up, but the huge Terminator immediately takes control with a punch to the head of the Grey Knight. The Termintor drags him into his corner, and makes the tag to the Dark Lord. He comes in, picks up the Knight, and powerslams him to the mat. The ref begins to count 1....2....but the Dark Lord picks him up and delivers a piledriver. The Dark Lord drags the Grey Knight over to the Knight's corner and throws him into the Black Knight. The Black Knight climbs threw the ropes, but gets ambushed by the Dark Lord. The Dark Lord throws the Black Knight into the ropes, and kicks him right in his face. The Lord makes the tag to the Terminator, and they complete their Dark Death. The Terminator makes the cover and the ref counts 1....2....3... Bill-Here are your winners, The Deathstalkers. Fred-WOW!!!! What a debut for the Deathstalkers!!! Brian-The Brew Crew and Mercenaries better watch out for these boys...I still can't beleive how they manhandled the Cold Knights!!! ---------------------------------------------------------------------- Bill-The following contest is a special six on one match. Bill-Coming down the aisle, here are Graffiti, Fireman Bob, El Gran Alcalde, Kinkos, Thunderhawk, and the Executioner!! Fred-Well fans, in this match...the new comer, known as Monstrosity, will have six matches in a row... Brian-...one after another!!! Fred-That's right Brian!!! However, the only way he can win is if he defeats all six men!!!! ("Purple Haze", by Jimmy Hendrix, begins to play) Bill-...and their opponent...accompanied to the ring by Johnny Destructo, weighing at 502 pounds, Monstrosity!!! Monstrosity enters the ring, and begins to stare at Fireman Bob. Bob runs into the ropes, but gets shoulder blocked down by Monstrosity. Monstrosity picks up the Fireman, and slams him down hard to the mat. He runs into the ropes and delivers a big elbow on Bob. Monstrosity gets up to his feet, picks up Fireman Bob, and delivers his Sky Bomb(power bomb). He covers Bob, and the ref counts 1...2....3.... Bill-Here is your winner, Monstrosity!!! Brian-That's one!! Kinkos dives into the ring, and begins to pound on the back of Monstrosity. He then jumps up, and hooks on a sleeper. Monstrosity begins to step backwards, and delivers a reverse avalance into the corner. Kinkos goes down hard onto the mat. Monstrosity picks up Kinkos and delivers a powerslam into the center of the ring. He then runs into the ropes, and delivers a big legdrop. He covers Kinkos and the ref counts 1...2....3. Bill-Here is your winner, Monstrosity!!! Fred-That's two!! The Executioner climbs into the ring, and meets Monstrosity in the middle of the ring. The two men begin to exchange lefts and rights, until Monstrosity begins to back up a bit. The Executioner throws Monstrosity into the ropes, and hits him with a big closeline, but he doesn't go down. The Executioner runs into the ropes, and hits him with another closeline....but Monstrosity still doesn't go down!!! The Executioner runs into the ropes again, but Monstrosity kicks him in the stomach and delivers his Sky Bomb. He goes for the cover and the ref counts 1...2.....3. Bill-Here is your winner, Monstrosity!!! Fred & Brian-That's Three!!!! Graffiti dives into the ring. He runs into the ropes, Monstrosity goes for a back drop, but Graffiti delivers a sunset flip. Graffiti pulls the legs of Monstrosity, but Monstrosity just sits down on Graffiti. The ref counts 1...2....3. Bill-Here is your winner, Monstrosity!!! Fred-That's Four, WOW!!! El Gran Alcalde climbs to the top turnbuckle, and hits Monstrosity with a drop kick, but Monstrosity still doesn't fall off his feet. Alcalde runs into the ropes, but he catches a quick clothesline from Monstrosity. Monstrosity backs up a bit, then delivers a big splash. The ref counts 1...2....3. Bill-Here is your winner, Monstrosity!!!!! Brian-I can't beleive this!!! Thunderhawk jumps into the ring, and attacks Monstrosity. He throws him into the ropes, and hits a clotheslone, staggering the big man. Thunderhawk, then, climbs to the top rope and hits Monstrosity with a forearm shot. Monstrosity still doesn't go down. Thunderhawk begins to look around the arena, and then hits Monstrosity with a Thunderpunch to the heart....and Monstrosity goes down to one knee!!!! Thunderhawk stands in awe at the preformance by Monstrosity, and begins to deliver punches to the head of the big man. This lasts for a minute until Monstrosity delivers a blow, which stops Thunderhawk. Monstrosity gets back up to his feet and delivers yet another Sky Bomb. He goes for the cover and the ref counts 1...2....3. Bill-Here is your winner, Monstrosity!!!! Brian-I can't beleive he did that!!! Fred-That was really impresive. (the fans begin to cheer, as Volcano runs into the ring) Fred-It's VOLCANO!!! Volcano runs into the ropes, and delivers a clothesline which knocks Monstrosity off his feet!! The big man rolls outside the ring, and the two begins to exchange words. ---------------------------------------------------------------------- Fred-WOW!!! Could you believe Volcano and Monstrosity!!!! Brian-Monstrosity has been challenging Volcano a lot lately, and I guess Volcano just gave his response... Bill-The following contest, scheduled for one fall, is for the PFWL World Tag Team Championship!!!! ("Purple Haze", by Jimmy Hendrix, begins to play) Bill-Coming down the aisle, accompanied by Johnny Destructo, from Parts Unknown, and weighing at a total combined weight of 590 pounds, Endless Destruction!!! ("Saving all my Hate" begins to play) Bill-..and their opponents, accompanied by Agent X, and weighing at a total combined weight of 536 pounds, the PFWL World Tag Team Champions, the Mercenaries!!!! The Mercenaries enter the ring and all four men begin to brawl!!! Fury clotheslines the Sniper over the top rope and Vincent Vega throws Wrath over the top. All four men begin to fight outside the ring, until the ref finally counts to ten. Bill-Ladies and Gentlemen, the ref has counted both teams out of the ring...his decision is a double count-out. The match doesn't stop there, both teams continue to fight back to the lockeroom. ---------------------------------------------------------------------- Fred-Could you believe that!!! Brian-These two teams are hot at each other...President Richie has got to do something about this!!! Fred-Lets get back up to Bill Fox! Bill-This next contest is scheduled for one fall! Coming down the aisle, from Portland, and weighing 287 pounds, Johnny Dingo. ("5 Minutes Alone", by Pantera, begins to play) Bill-...and his opponent, from San Diego California, weighing 342 pounds, the Powerful Overlord of Pain!! Fred-Well at the last PFWL PPV, the Powerful Overlord of Pain, or Petty Officer Pain as he was known then, lost the PFWL World Title. Brian-..and now his focus is pain!!! I'd hate to be Derrick Stardom, or Johnny Dingo for that matter. Johnny Dingo and the Powerful Overlord of Pain lock up, and Pain takes control by pushing Dingo back to a corner. Pain begins to break clean, but sneaks in a shot to the mid-section of Johnny Dingo. Pain continues to deliver powerful punches to the body of Dingo, then throws him accross the ring into the other turnbuckle. Pain runs at him, and hits him with a clothesline. Pain picks up Johnny Dingo and slams him down to the ground, followed by an elbow drop, but Dingo moves out of the way!!! Johnny Dingo begins to kick at Pain. Dingo turns to the crowd and gets a HUGE response. Johnny Dingo throws Pain into the ropes, and goes for his flying forearm, but Pain moves out of the way. Dingo goes down hard. The Powerful Overlord of Pain picks up Dingo and sets him on the top turnbuckle. He climbs to the second turnbuckle and hits his deep-six. Pain covers Johnny Dingo and the ref counts 1...2....3. Bill-Here is your winner, the Powerful Overlord of Pain!!! ---------------------------------------------------------------------- (the camera cuts to the broadcast table, and Fred Smith and Brian Rundle are seen) Fred-Fans, at this time, we would like to introduce President Richie. (President Richie comes into the picture) Richie-Hello Fred! Fred-Hey! Well you know Mr. President, there has been many things happening in the PFWL since it's hiatus, would you care to talk about any ot them??? Richie-Well first of all, I would like to talk about this Monstrosity character...this guy had a tremendous showing a few minutes ago, but I don't think he knows what he's getting into??? I mean, he's challenging Volcano??? Well I still don't know what he's up to, but I'm gonna give him a match with Volcano at September Slam!!! Fred-WOW!!! Richie-...another that concerns me is the situation between the Mercenaries and Endless Destruction. They like to fight, well at September Slam...they're gonna have a fight and pinfalls will count any where in the building!!! Brian-WOW!!!! Fred-Thankyou Mr. President, lets get back up to Bill Fox! Bill-This contest is scheduled for one fall!!! (the theme from 2001:A Space Oddessey begins to play) Bill-Coming down the aisle, and accompanied by Styling Sam Mason, from Philadelphia, weighing 297 pounds, Marc Watts!!!! Bill-His opponent, from New York, weighing 214 pounds, Fireman Bill!! Bill enters the ring, and Watts attacks him. Marc Watts throws Fireman Bill into the ropes, and hits him with a big clothesline. Watts picks him up and slams him down to the mat. Watts, then. runs into the ropes, and hits a legdrop. He goes for the pin, and the ref counts 1...2...but a kick out by Fireman Bill. Fireman Bill gets to his feet and knocks down Marc Watts with a shot to the head. Fireman Bill steps threw the ropes and climbs to the top turnbuckle. However, Marc Watts meets him. Watts sets him up and delivers the Sykes Slam(fallaway slam from the top rope). The ref counts 1...2....3. Bill-Here is your winner, Marc Watts. ---------------------------------------------------------------------- Bill-The following contest, scheduled for one fall, is for the PFWL World Title!!! Bill-Coming down the aisle, accompanied by Crazy Brian, from Altanta Georgia, and weighing 255 pounds, Sico Gregg!!!! ("Cult of Personality", by Living Colour, begins to play) Bill-...and his opponent, accompanied by Sabrina Greed, from Hollywood California, weighing 236 pounds, the PFWL World Champion, Badd Boy Derrick Stardom!!!! Stardom enters the ring, and attacks Sico Gregg. Stardom throws Gregg into the ropes and hits a back body drop. Gregg rolls out of the ring, and Stardom follows him. They run around the outside of the ring, until Crazy Brian trips Stardom. Sico Gregg begins to kick Stardom in the side. He throws him into the ring, and applies a head lock. Stardom withstands the pain for many minutes until he reaches the ropes. Sico Gregg lets go and begins to beat on Stardom. Gregg picks up Derrick Stardom and suplexes the PFWL Champ. He goes for the cover and the ref counts 1...2...but a kick out by Stardom. Sico Gregg stands to his feet and begins to have words with Crazy Brian. Sico Gregg picks up Stardom, and hooks him up in his ddt. But Stardom picks up Gregg and back drops him. Gregg tries to regain his control, but he can't. Stardom, eventually, picks up Sico Gregg and slams him down to the mat. Stardom climbs to the top turnbuckle and hits his Starfall! He goes for the cover and the ref counts 1...2....3. Bill-Here is your winner, and still PFWL World Champion, Badd Boy Derrick Stardom!!! Fred-WOW!!! What a title defense by Derrick Stardom!!! (Trillionaire Terry is seen coming down the aisle) Brian-Here comes Trillionaire Terry!!!! Trillionaire Terry reaches ringside, and asks for the mic. Terry-You know Stardumb!!! A lot of men have crossed my path in my glorious life, but none of them were as DUMB as you!!!! (the crowd begins to boo) Terry-My money can buy anything I want, and you ask ME to buy the best wrestler I can find to face you!!!! I left the PFWL, and I thought that was a nice thing to do, but you want even more of me!!! Well your gonna get it... (a figure is seen coming out of the crowd, and entering the ring) Terry-....at September Slam....I'm gonna finish the job that I'm starting tonight!!!! The large man attacks Derrick Stardom, deliverying many powerful shots to the body of the champion. Brian-Oh my goodness, do you know who that is!!! Fred-It's JoBo!!!!! The man who ended the career of the Daredevil Dude!!! The 400 pound JoBo pounds on Derrick Stardom, then sets him up and delivers his spinning piledriver. Stardom lays on his back in the ring, and appears to be out cold. Fred-What is JoBo doing here??? I thought he was suspended from the PFWL??? Many officials come from the back and escort JoBo and Trillionaire Terry back to the dressingroom. Derrick Stardom, who is still in the ring, gets assistance from the PFWL medical staff as he gets up and leaves the ring. (the camera cuts to the broadcast table, and President Richie comes into the picture) Fred-President Richie, what is JoBo doing here and is he an active wrestler in the PFWL???? Richie-The answer you looking for is YES!!! JoBo is an active wrestler in the PFWL. During the hiatus, I was approached by a number of lawyers, representing JoBo, and they convinced me that JoBo was ready to come back. But I had no idea that this was going to happen!!! Fred-We're out of time, we'll see ya next week....
