Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Independent Wrestling Federation called itself a new force in sports entertainment: an e-mail federation of November 2001 in which every member played real wrestlers, up to two at a time, on a weekly Raw Is War and Smackdown! and the pay-per-view calendar of the day, with Jim Ross and Paul Heyman at ringside.

Its rules asked forty lines of roleplay for every match and counted them seventy per cent on quality and thirty on quantity; a member who did not roleplay could be booked to lose however the results team liked. Its info packets ran the whole federation in a single post — rules, staff, rosters, champions and the next card.

Its storylines ran through the owner’s own on-screen character, who announced the Survivor Series card from the ring and, at the end of a Smackdown! in late November, fired a list of wrestlers by name for not roleplaying. The same show crowned Mankind World Champion inside Hell in the Cell and Edge European Champion, while Big Poppa Pump kept the Intercontinental Title against The Rock.

Its handbook of 29 November warned that the federation would be light for a while and that the month’s pay-per-view, Survivor Series, might be cancelled or moved. A Raw Is War line-up followed in the same post, and nothing later survives.
