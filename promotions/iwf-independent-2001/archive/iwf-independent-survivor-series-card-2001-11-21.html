Angle hits Van Damn with the Olympic Slam! Angle goes for the pin! Earl Hebner: 1, 2, 3! Winner: Kurt Angle and DDP After the match Kurt Angle and DDP are getting ready to go to the back when Stone Cold hits DDP with a chair in the back of the head and sends DDP down to the ground with his head busted open. Angle turns right around to a hard chair shot to the face and his eye and mouth are busted open. Austin picks up DDP and gives him a stunner onto the chair, he does the same with Angle. Big Poppa Pump runs down to the ring only to get a stunner by Austin in return. Van Damn is back to this feet at this time and looks at Austin with the chair and then looks at the three bodies on the floor. He goes outside and gets three tables and stacks them on top of each other. As Austin picks up DDP he gives him another stunner and puts him on the first table, he does the same to Angle and Big Poppa Pump and puts them on the second and third tables. Van Damn gets up to the top turn buckle and gives a massive Frog Splash that breaks all tables and all three men are under the broken tables. Stone Cold and RVD then begin to go at it, they start to fight and head off into the crowd. Then The Impact Players and Tommy Dreamer run to the ring to check on the three but they cant get them to speak or open their eyes. The EMT's run to the ring and place them all on strechers and send them to the nearest hospital.
