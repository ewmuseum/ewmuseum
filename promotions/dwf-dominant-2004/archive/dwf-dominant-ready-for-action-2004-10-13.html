Dominant Wrestling Federation -[ INTRODUCTION ]- What's up people? It's Trent and I'm back in business like Revco. Here to make another run at this E-Wrestling hobby of mine. I've had a feeling of working up the energy to establish another federation, and now I've finally decided to do it.
But all I'm trying to do is collaborate with the various people I've worked with in the past or never got the chance to work with. I may not hardly watch
Wrestling anymore, but I'm still inclined to write and create storylines like I did in the past. This time, though, I'll be controllin' the show. Just want you guys to have fun and push your creative limits.
If you're interested in joining the DWF ( Dominant Wrestling Federation ). Fake wrestlers are only allowed, and I'm basically going to keep the roster between
10-15 so I can have a chance to create a short mixed play-by-play/summary style card. I also think that amount gives me a realistic chance of talking to you'll about angles and other topics.
Quality's the concept behind role-playing, simple as that. Push the creative limits, give your character a persona/dimension, and try to entertain your fellow role-players. I don't have some ellaborate mathematical system in determining the winner of a match. I try to see whether you've if you've attempted to role-play and how well you've put your role-plays together.
<> Concepts; Storylines; Angles Adapting some plot within role-plays and storyline integration keeps some things interesting. <> Characterization/Persona The more clever and witty your character is within his personality's bounds the more rounded he/she becomes.
This is a strictly e-mail federation. We'll have bi-weekly televised broadcasts for our flagship show based on most East Coast cable providers. Every other
Thursday night we'll have REVELATIONS. A two-hour show highlighting most of the roster and the finest the DWF can offer.
There will be six PPVs per year and there titles will be unique ( as in never will there be two of the same shows such as how WWE presents SummerSlam on a yearly basis ). The names of the PPVs will be made according to the current storylines and state of the federation.
I'll exclusively write each show, with the occasional guest writer here and there. If anyone is interested in writing for cards in the future let me know by e-mailing me. You can check below for the address.
That about wraps it up for the introduction. More information I've presented below and I've also made an application. If you're interested e-mail me back with the application of your very own wrestler, not a real wrestler. I'll
e-mail you back as soon as I receive it. Thanks for your time. -Trent -[ STAFF ]- TBA -[ CHAMPIONSHIPS ]- Heavyweight Title You are the MAN if you hold this. Consistent RPing and hard work are the factors that'll propel you towards this belt. Only the best can vie for it, and only the best of the best can hold it.
Junior Heavyweight Title Any wrestler below 230 pounds can be eligible for this highly coveted title. What's thought of as the fast-paced division, many future Heavyweight Champions are bred within this area. Respect comes with a tenure of this belt.
Tag Team Titles The most cohesive and skilled duo holds these titles. You can handle two characters or partner up with another handler to form one. In that situation
RPs are split when two handlers face one for these belts to even the odds. But all regular rules apply.
