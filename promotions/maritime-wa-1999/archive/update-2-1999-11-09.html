UPDATE #2 ------------------------------------------------------------------------ -------- The Show : The show seemed to go well. Even though I was a little late in getting it out. That will improve. Riot King tee shirts out sold all other merchadise at the last show, what the fans are doing with them I dobn't know but there have been reports of fans burning them in the streets after Riot King makes an appearance.
Injuries : Only bumps and bruises comon to the ring. Backroom : Jim Menacho has gotten approval from the booking staff to face
Moser in Charolettetown. As well the booking staff and cahmpionship committee has decided to enstate the 6 man championship option and scedualed the deciding match again for the Charolettetown card.
Jim Menacho was also fined $1000.00 for laying his hands on an MWA official. Jack Roberts' plea for a HeavyWeight Title Rematch has been over turned. Instead Roberts must beat the Riot King in a speacial Grudge match on the next card to gain a title shot on the card following.
Comings & Goings : Kevin Richards has been cleared for ring duty. The booking staff would like to welcome him back to the active roster. In regards to his return Kevin had this to say :
"Yes I'm back, and you know what it feels great. At Charolettetown it will be Judge Jason and myself showing the MWA and the world wo the best 6 man tag team is and who the preimere wrestlign family of Canada will always be."
Title Holders & Top Contenders : MWA Heavy Wieght Champion : the Riot King (1/0/0) 1- "Yukon" Jack Roberts (1/1/0) 2- "Handsome" Harry Hadley (1/0/0) 3- Jim "Polar Bear" Menacho (0/0/0) MWA Light Heavy Weight Champion : Angel (2/0/0) 1- Alexander Maxis (0/1/0) 2- the Arctic Dragon (0/1/0) 3- "Excitable" Edwin Edwards (0/2/0) MWA Tag Team Champions : Fate - Alpha & Omega (1/0/0) 1- the American Amazons - Mathew & Mark Sameuls (1/0/0) 2- the DuMonts - Jean-Paul & Kyle Dumont (1/0/0) 3- Team Canada - Mr Canadian & the Snow Warrior (0/1/0) MWA 6 man Tag Champion : Vacant MWA Hardcore Champion : Vancant Closing : The schedual for the Charolettetown card will be out tonight or tomorrow moring. I'm going to kept running the MWA for another month or so to try and get some more handlers. If after say another two to three cards I don't have a larger roster I may have to really consider what to do with the league.
Anyone with comments or suggestions please feel free to fortward them to me. A complete roster will be out in the next day or so listing the title holders and wrestlers for each division.
