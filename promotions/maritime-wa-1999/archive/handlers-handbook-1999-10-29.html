the Maritime Wrestling Association Handlers' Handbook Welcome : Welcome to the MWA. The MWA is a small regional league that is run soly by myself. I do run characters in the MWA aside from the
"jobbers"; however as a conession to the other players to cover my advantage I do not have my own characters compete for title belts, ever.
I hope you enjoy your tenure here at the MWA. Please read on to gain the specifics of what participation in the MWa entails.
Characters : First off each player is allowed upto three characters in the MWA. However these charcters must work as a stable or team. So if you want to run only singles characters I suggest you only create a single character. However for those of you who want more then one character go right ahead.
The MWA does have a Tag title and a Six Man Tag title for those players who wish to run teams. Singles characters can work in teams aswell if both players wish them to, or if a current angle has them working against a comon foe.
For each character you create I need a singles sheet. Since the Tag and 6 man sheets are very short I need the singles sheet to round out the character's in ring performance. All the sheets are located at the end of this message.
Player Responsabilities : Each play is responsable for flashes for each of his characters when they have a match schedualed. If you want to flash when you don't have a scehdualed match you may, but YOU MUST flash for each match you have or you chances of winning go down.
Players are also in charge of asking for matches. I run the show, and make sure ALL debuting characters get a match the first month, but making sure everyone has at least one match a month gets hard. So after your debut send in challenges.
The only challenges I do not automatically grant are ones for title shots, and HARDCORE matches. If someone wants a title shot it is the handler of the champion that decides if you get it. If someone wants a
Hardcore match then I leave that upto the challenged player to accept.
I will sign a match but it may not be a hardcore match if the challenged party dose not agree. Flashes : Please put life into these. ALL flashes are to be done in a back stage or in ring setup. No exceptions. Flashes MUST be done for each match you have, the only exception is if I'm running a one night tourney. Then I only need one flash from you. Please keep them longer then five lines, unless of course five lines is all you have to say.
Keep them in character. That's about it. Game Plans : These are optional, and simple if you want to use them. Just tell me how you wnat your character to "work" the match. Don't list moves unless they are something speacial you want to use, ie new type of suplex your character has never tried. Also tell me how you wnat to defend yourself aginast your opponet's style. Ie if he is aerial are you going to match him move for move or ar you going to try and ground them.
One nice part about Game Plans is that it will allow you to tell me if you wnat to do something "other then" just winning the match. Maybe you wnat to get DQ'ed to make a point to someone else. Have fun with them but remeber they are not nessary only an option available to you.
Player Policies : Swearing is allowed, but I may sanction against it if players get out of hand. Rascim & Sexism is NOT allowed. I catch ANYONE trying to pull this stuff through a flash and "I lose the flash" and you lose you match.
Severe, yes. Nessary I hope not. Flashes are due at least four days before the show is to come out. Match Stragtigies (Game Plans) are due at the smaetime if you want to send in one. Titles : The MWA sanctions the following titles. the Maritime Heavy Weight Championship the Maritime Light Heavy Weight Championship ( must weight 230 or under)
the Maritime Tag Team Championship the Maritime Six Man Tag Championship* the Maritime Hardcore Champion* * These two titles depend on how many "active" handlers I have who are interested in these titles. I need at least four handlers rounded out by jobbers I create to activate these belts.
ALL CHAMPIONS must delcare who they wish to defend against. Titles are to be defened once every four weeks. Normally I try and schedual at least one singles and one tag title match per card, reotating the titles so each champion gets a wekk off.
The HARDCORE title must be defended WHEN EVER THERE IS A CHALLENGE
FOR IT!!! if there is no challenge you don't have to defend.
Shows : The MWA runs only live house shows. No PPV's or tv shows. So EVERYTHING IS DONE live in an arena with no tv coverage. Hence there are no censors( hence the allowance of swearing)
The MWA has one show every two weeks. This allows me time to write up each match, and gives the players time to send in flashes, and if they want match stratigies. This also allows me the time I need to work the other leagues I am part of aswell as giving me time to actually do the work needed for my career.
If everything works well shows will be out on the date I schedual them. Ie every second Sunday.
By Wed after each show I should have the fairly firm Next card schedual. This schedual only becomes law a week before the card. So if you want to make changes let me know ASAP.
Show Personalties : Interviewers : Brook Beauti Height : 5' 6" Weight : 180 lbs Age : 45 Brook always wears a black denuim suit with a bright red tie, during his interview sessions. Brook does all the in ring interviews.
He plays no games and does not pull his punches. He asks the "tough" questions and wont back down from a challenge. Mathew Masterson Height : 5' 10" Weight : 175 lbs Age : 29 Mathew wears dress pants, and a white silk shirt with black tie to his interview sessions. Mathew does all fans talk sessions, and back room interview sessions with MWA officals and superstars. Mathew while not a "big boy" isn't easily bullied as he is a black belt in akido.
Referees : Johnny "the Outlaw" Nash Height : 7' 0" Weight 320 lbs Age : 31 Johnny once was a wrestler but a back injury removed him from active compition. Johnny is the head ref and a stickler for the rules.
You want a match with no Hardcore or outside interferance request Johnny as the ref. Johnny routinely at first provokation sends managers
& valets to the back with security. Things go too far Johnny can
"restore" order with the help of his own trademark move the Jack Knife
Powerbomb. Ian Thorne Height : 6' 0" Wieght : 195 lbs Age : 25 Ian Throne is a solid referee. He sticks to the rules but allows some le-way depending on the angle and fan reactions. However Ian will when things go too far restore order, and if he has to he will use his own vast array of Canadian Championship Calibre Karate and Tae Kwon Do.
"Mad, Bad" Micheal Throne Height : 6' 1" Weight : 200 lbs Age : 25 Micheal is Ian's twin brother. While Ian is quite level headed
Micheal is not. Micheal is a Hardcore Fanatic and is the ONLY MWA ref that will work a hardcore match. Micheal let's matches sometimes go all to hell, but has a strick sense of fair play meaning the first case of outside interferance he awards the match.
Updates : I should be sending out an Update with each card schedual. This will list the title holders, the top contenders, obverall rankings, injury reports and comings & goings.
If will be also a forum for where I ask you the handlers where I should be taking the shows, or federation.
