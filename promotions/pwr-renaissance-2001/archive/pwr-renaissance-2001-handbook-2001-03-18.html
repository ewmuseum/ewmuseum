==================== PWR - Pro-Wrestling Renaissance ==================== PWR Handbook League Introduction PWR was developed in an effort to bring more stability and structure to what many consider a great hobby and a fun game. In most leagues it seems as if some people get what is called a "push" simply because they are friends with the person who runs the league, or that they are new to the league and are used to put over the other handlers who have been in the league longer. The PWR will be provide every handler who is lucky enough to get in the door a fun place to compete and an equal chance at greatness. PWR will be a simple league that will have strict rules, and if you follow those rules then your e-wrestler's future is truly in your own hands. What we will attempt to do is come up with a way for you to know exactly what is expected in a roleplay, and with only a one roleplay per week requirement you shouldn't have a problem. Rankings & Roleplaying - Why do we have these two sections together? Because roleplaying and the rankings system go hand in hand. The PWR rankings will be determined by a points system. You will be given a certain amount of points depending on how you win or lose your match. How you win or lose your match will be determined by how good your roleplay is compared to how good your opponents roleplay is that he submits. A victory by way of a submission will give you more points than beating your opponent by pinning him. RANKINGS POINTS Win by Submission: 20pts Win by Pinfall: 15pts Win by DQ: 10pts Draw: 5pts Loss by DQ: 3pts Loss by Pinfall: 2pts Loss by Submission: 1pt **SPECIAL NOTE - Rankings will be based on the outcome of up to 10 matches. Once a wrestler has more than 10 matches under his belt the rankings will then be based on the wrestlers last 10 matches. This ensures that if you go on vacation one week, have a few weeks where you are busy with mid-terms, finals or you're busy with your job, that you will not fall out of the rankings too quickly. Also if you go through a slump where you lose a few matches it's easy to turn things around and start climbing back up the ladder. We also believe that this type of system will keep the people at the top of the ladder on their toes because if they slack off in the least little bit they will find themselves falling down the rankings. ROLEPLAYING POINTS Roleplays will be judged in several different categories. Several of these categories will be what we call "simple" categories, if you follow them you are guaranteed 5 points. The other categories are what we call "complex" categories, meaning you can earn anywhere from 1 point to 5 points, this will be better explained below. "Simple" Categories - The easy way to get points! **5pts - Staying in character (By this we mean if you are a heel do you have the fans in the crowd booing you? If you are a babyface do you shake the hands of the fans on your way to the ring? You have to act the part you are playing, otherwise your roleplay looks silly.) **5pts - Not breaking kayfabe (In your roleplay do you say the word roleplay or do you instead use the word interview? Do you include out of character remarks, do you use the word JOB, heel or babyface? These are all things that should never be done.) **5pts - Censoring yourself (I can't tell you how tired I am of reading roleplays where every other word out of an e-wrestlers mouth is profanity. Words you hear on prime time television such as "damn", "ass" or "hell" are considered ok to use. Any other word should NOT be used!! If you need to use such a word to get across that your wrestler is fired up or angry, then we expect you to censor yourself. Your options could be things like the following. Use  "@#$&#@%" or "censored" in place of the word in question.) **5pts - Roleplaying towards an equal opponent (How many leagues have you joined where everyone wants to face the champion? Not everyone can face the champion at the same time, it's impossible. You will earn 5 points simply by trying to work with another handler that is not a champion on an angle or storyline. This means your roleplays are directed at other e-wrestlers who are at about the same level in the rankings as you. Only those handlers whose wrestlers are high in the rankings will be eligible to compete for the championships. In this league you have to earn your way to the top over time.) "Complex" Categories - The harder ways to earn points! **1 to 5pts - Interest (How interesting is your roleplay? If I get bored reading it because you are babbling on and on about something you will get a lower score. Longer roleplays are not always better!) **1 to 5pts - Response (After reading your roleplay, does the other handler(s) write a roleplay to respond? The mark of a good e-wrestler is his ability to get other handlers to respond to what he has written. A handler that is good at this is a very valuable asset to the league.) BONUS POINTS*** If we ask you to submit an extra (in ring) interview for placement in the card then you will earn bonus points. That bonus will range anywhere from 5 to 15 points. CARDS There will be one fully written card per week called PWR Colossal Combat. Our goal for this card is for it to be about 250k. We don't want that card to be too big or too small and at that size we feel that it will be an easy read for everyone. ROLEPLAYING As I said earlier, the PWR will be an easier league with a lot more laid back style. Our only requirement is that every handler roleplay once a week. We will have an open mailing list so that you can roleplay when ever you like, but keep in mind that we will only judge one of your roleplays per week. PAY-PER-VIEWS There will only be 4 pay-per-views per year. There may be a few supercards added in here or there but for the most part only expect 4 ppv's. January - New Year's Dilemma April - PWR Revolution July - No Escape October - Double Trouble TITLES There will only be one title to start the league off and that will be the PWR World Championship. Once the league gets off the ground and begins to make a name for itself, we will then recruit more wrestlers and add titles.
