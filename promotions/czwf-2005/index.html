Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

COmbat Zone Wrestling Federation billed itself, in its own pbm.com directory listing, as heading toward being "the premier wrestling e-fed of 2005." Its own roster blends real-wrestler portrayals (John Cena, Trish Stratus, Chyna, Rey Mysterio, Randy Orton, Andre the Giant) with original characters (Iron Bull, "Super Crazy" AJ Warner, Zade, HippieDude, Corey "Iceman" Morrish), all generated through the Zeus Pro simulator engine.

Eight titles changed hands or stood pat across the fed's own held corpus. The fed's own undated Champions page is its earliest surviving snapshot — every ring introduction on Monday Night Adrenaline (11/21) bills the same three champions that page already names, entering as challengers' own opposition. All three titles turned over that night: Chyna submitted Trish Stratus for the Women's Championship, "Super Crazy" AJ Warner submitted Rey Mysterio for the Intercontinental Championship, and the Undertaker sent Iron Bull through a table to win the Heavyweight Championship, part of a rivalry the fed's own recap calls "a seesaw gone wrong." No further title-change document survives; all three new champions are the belts' own last-attested holders in the held corpus.

Storylines

CZWF's own held corpus is thin — one fully documented show, one undated title snapshot, and a second show attested only in passing — so the arcs below are the small, receipted hand of narrative the fed's own words actually support.

The Heavyweight seesaw Written by AI, 2026

The Undertaker and Iron Bull entered Monday Night Adrenaline (11/21) already mid-feud — the fed's own home page recap frames their rivalry as an ongoing trade, "these two men cannot keep their reign for long because the other takes it away." The held corpus documents one leg of that trade directly: Undertaker won the CZWF Heavyweight Championship off Iron Bull in a Double Tables Match, sending him through a table. How many times the belt changed hands between them before that night, or whether it changed hands again after, is not preserved.

A number-one contender waits on John Cena Written by AI, 2026

Andre the Giant, billed as a newcomer, pinned Randy Orton with a Super DDT on Monday Night Adrenaline (11/21) to become the number-one contender for John Cena's CZWF X-Division Championship — the fed's own home page recap previews the title match itself as still to come. No result for it survives in the held corpus.
