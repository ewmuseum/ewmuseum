A syndication hub offering timeslots and a weekly ratings poll to other newsgroup federations, run by a single operator across the last two months of 2001. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The E-Wrestling Network billed itself to the newsgroup as a syndicator, not a promotion of its own: a place for other feds' shows to air on assigned timeslots and be measured against each other. Its rules post of 14 November 2001 laid the system out at length — one- and two-hour blocks, first come first served, no three-hour shows because “they are not neccisary” — and a ratings poll open once a week per viewer, with the week's winner announced under a “ratings winner of the week” subject line. Joining cost a fed its own banner on TEWN's page and a mini-logo in return, plus a promise of “no real wrestler feds” and nothing “illegal, pornographic, racist, or extremely insensitive.”

The surviving ratings reports show the system running as described. The week of 11 November counted twelve total viewers, split entirely between one fed that aired on time and one that forfeited its ratings for airing late, and named a wrestler and a match of the week; by the week of 25 November–1 December, TEWN's own tally had grown to sixty-one viewers across six member shows, with IGA Combat Zone topping the board.

No founding date survives, and no document later than 3 December 2001 does either — whether TEWN kept running, how long its roster of member feds lasted, and how or when it closed are all outside the surviving record.
