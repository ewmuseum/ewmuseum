[TEWN] The E-Wrestling Network has plenty of time slots left!!!

November 14, 2001, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Any feds interested in seeing their shows on the E-Wrestling Network? Well, we have quite a few time slots left! What can TEWN offer your fed? See our FAQ below... Thanx! http://hal3000.cx/~kingfish/tewn/listings.html IMPORTANT - TEWN's LISTING PAGE IS SET FOR THE CENTRAL TIMEZONE!!!! 1) Timeslots. *They are first come, first serve. The sooner you join TEWN, the more likely you are to get your timeslot. *1 hour slots are for shows that have 4 matches or less. 2 hour slots are unlimited matches. *Each fed can have a 2 hour show, and a 1 hour show. No 3 hour shows, since they are not neccisary. *Someone asked me if they should have a 3 hour show since their show was gonna be very large. The answer is no because while you may want your show to be three hours long in a fantasy sense, it doesnt matter to TEWN. Wether the show you plan is 4 hours in legnth, or 2... it can fit a 2 hour block. How? I dunno.... It just can. =] You could maybe say we clip the matches shorter, or have less commercials, whatever you want. (No you dont have to hack your card up to fit it in... never edit your card in anyway to please TEWN, thats not neccisary.) *Another thing of Importance is if the timeslot you want is taken, dont feel you CANT be in TEWN. Say your show is a wednesday night show, but wednesday is booked up, and a slot is open on thursday. Think of it like how UPN aires Smackdown! Smackdown is taped on a tuesday night usually, but still aires on thursday. Your show, when you do it, and whats on it (no porn tho!) is your business. We just give ya a place to show off your work. 2) Ratings *Ratings arent done in a Nielson system, or percentage thing. Nothing fancy. It's done by number of viewers, straight up. *A viewer is someone who votes for a show in our poll. Simple *The poll allows one vote per person, PER WEEK. So a viewer can vote anytime they want, but only once a week *Voting for the current week starts on sunday, at 6pm, with the first show of the week. It ends the next sunday sometime in the afternoon, when I compile the ratings and weekly awards. *It doesnt matter that you vote EVEN before your show starts! Otherwise saturday shows would get clobbered in the ratings and Sunday shows nearly always win. *Wether you have your wrestlers vote for your show, or play on an "honor" system and only vote once a week for their favorite show out of all the feds. It's cool *Votes a show gets will be DISCOUNTED if that show is not completed by the assigned timeslot. The total number of network viewers will be credited, but your show wont be. *1 hour shows and 2 hour shows get seperate ratings. *THEIR IS NO LONGER A DIFFERENCE BETWEEN INDIE AND MAJOR FEDS, disreguard that line in our FAQ v 3) Awards, rankings *Each week we pick a match of the week, wrestler of the week and then our highest rated shows get posted on newsboards and newsgroups with the subject line of being our ratings winner of the week. Hopefully that will mean something to people in the near future... =] *Other rewards for successful feds could be TEWN produced documentaries on that fed, a special banner ad, blatant plugs... etc etc 4) Requirements * You need to post our banner on your page, and provide us with a mini-logo to put back on our page. * No real wrestler feds. * Nothing illegal, pornographic, racist, or extremely insensitive * Providing TEWN with fed news bytes or fed reports can be done, but is optional
