Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Extreme Hardcore Wrestling opened on 1 April 1997 with a recruitment post from its own president promising “the most EXTREME Match & EXTREME PPV'S” to “only the BEST Roleplayers,” and closing new membership after only a short window: “JOIN WHILE YOU STILL CAN!!!!!”

Twelve days later it posted a character-application sheet capping each writer at three original wrestlers — “All of this stuff must BE MADE UP” — asking for a finisher, three favorite moves, a gimmick and a hometown per entry, and warning off anyone who couldn't roleplay: “if you cant Roleplay then leave!!!”

Its last surviving post, on 22 June 1997, has its president asking the newsgroup for card writers and promoters to help run what he still called “the best Roleplaying league aroung.” No card, roster, or result of the promotion's own survives anywhere in this record; whatever it staged, or whether it staged anything at all before going quiet, isn't in the surviving record.
