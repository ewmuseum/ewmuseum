National Wrestling Alliance: New E-Fed Hiring, Not Firing

5 July 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Scene: The camera fades from black to show a seven foot tall door. The name plate reads; NWA Co-Owner/ President James
Stewart. The camera man walks opens the door and walks into the office. The office is big and spacuos, the camera moves closer to the desk where a tall chair sits. The chair swings around as there sits NWA Co-Owner/ President James Stewart.
The camera focuses on James' upper body. James Stewart: First things first, I want to welcome you into the National Wrestling Allience. The National Wrestling
Allience quite simply is not going to be like all those other e-feds out there. This is not going to another Austin/
Vince power angle. Or at least that's what we're shooting for. If you know my track record you know that I am a former
5 time World Heavyweight Champion. You'll also know that I do have a history of booking and have in some cases stepped in and wrote full results for bookers/ owners. I'm not going to BS everyone here, But put very simply, YOU THE WRESTLER
WORK HARD!!! AND I THE PRESIDENT WILL WORK HARD AS WELL!!! I'm looking for the very best that the WORLD has to offer. And I can see you as a future NWA World Champion. But it's not going to be handed to you. IT'S GOT TO BE EARNED, AND
EARNED THROUGH HARD WORK AND DEDICATION. So enough from me. The rules of the fed are the basic e-fed rules. You break them you answer to me. And we'll see you at the first show.
Peace. Rule #1: Roleplays (RPs) are determined by quality, not quantity. While the Bookers can and will put length and amount into factor when deciding a winner, the judgement will be based on who the Bookers has felt did a better job of RPing. Winners will then be sent out to writers, then attacks, interferances, and other such additions placed into the finalized match product. Any complaints about winners can be sent to the Bookers. If a complaint about a particular winner is recieved, a rematch will be rescheduled. Continous complaints about losses will result in termination.
Rule #2: RPs must not contain any anti-semetic, racial, or other such unneccessary comment. Sexual comments are allowed on a very strignent basis (you are not allowed to continually make sexual referances). Any comment that personally insults a person's race, color, or religon will be deemed null and void. If RPs frequently contain this problem, termination will result. Likewise, searching an opponent's RP to find error such as these too frequently will also result in penalty and/or termination.
Rule #3: RPs must be sent out to the [detail withheld] and [detail withheld] ONLY tHIS IS A BCC E-FED. We and only we hold the RP list. This keeps people from sending out junk, in my history this is the best e-mail system. Rule #4: You are limited to the use of your e-wrestler and him alone. Creation of "temporary" characters (such as your e-wrestler's wife, a friend, etc.) is allowed. Use of managers/valets in RPs is also acceptable.
Rule #5: Spam mail is not acceptable. Informing the roleplay list of your e-fed, site, or anything else of that nature will result in your immediate termination.
Rule #6: You are not allowed to inform AOL's Terms of Service (TOS), Hotmail's TOS, or any other mail client of any roleplays concerning a violation of their rules. If you inform AOL that someone from our e-fed is spamming (when all they are really doing is RPing), you will be terminated immediatly.
Rule #7: No RP lengtheners are allowed. An RP lengthener is defined as anything meant to make your RP look longer, such as a copyright statement, triple spacing, or anything else deemed such by the Board. Failure to comply will result in voiding of roleplay.
Rule #8: Curses are banned from subject headers. Do not make mention of them at all. Curses, however, are allowed in your roleplays. We encourage that you censor the e-mail (**** out the words), but will not punish those who do not.
Rule #9: E-mail switching is forbidden. A definition of e-mail switching would be changing your e-mail address once a week. Stick with one e-mail address, if you must change it then give us ample warning time. If you frequently change your e-mail address, we will simply not add you to our RP list anymore.
Rule #10: Asking the Bookers for an opinion on something is prohibited. The Bookers are required not to give out opinions on anything at any time. Asking, for example, if your RP was good or not, will result in a punishment of some sort.
Rule #11: You must send an application in for review in order to be considered for acceptance. Not everyone is accepted, so do not get your hopes up. Fill out the application as best you can, making sure to include a sample roleplay, even if you have never done one before.
Rule #12: There are no Out Of Character (OOC) e-mails allowed. Any breach of this rule will result in immediate termination, unless permission is granted by the Bookers.
Rule #13: Any and all complaints should be directed to the Bookers, not specific members or in character RPs. We are open to suggestions and improvements, and are willing to do our best to see that you enjoy your experience in our feds, but will not tolerate irrational and immature comments sent behind our backs or within character. If this rule is violated, or abused by being used too frequently, the
