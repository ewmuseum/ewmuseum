National Wrestling Alliance: Currently Under New Management

16 July 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
To Any Free Agents Looking For An EWrestling Organization,   I have just taken over as President of the National Wrestling Alliance. The NWA has been around for almost a month now and now that I have taken over, I am hoping to change the structure of things that were missing or just plain out sucked before. I'm not going to sit here and
BS you with how it is the greatest fed around, because I don't think there is a such thing. Instead, I can tell you that the NWA will be as fun and exciting as you make it. If you want it to be fun and innovative by adding some originality and excitement to the NWA, you are cordially invited to join. I personally want to help any and all of you, once you are in the
NWA, to make it fun for you. I am going to work hard at making your ewrestling experience with us full of surprises and excitement. I truly believe that if you are working harder than I am, I'm not doing my job.
  So, here is what we are looking for....We are currently looking for any and all experienced roleplayers for this fake, email based (WWF style of booking and ECW/XPW style of action) organization. We have a website that is worked on every week and updated often. But hey, don't get me wrong...the
NWA isn't based or worked off the site because the site is strictly for information purposes only. If you'd like to view the website, which has the application and current card info on it, go to
The
NWA Website
and check it out for yourself.
                                                                                                           NWA President,                                                                                                      - [name withheld]
