Signature Wrestling Federation: A Few Roster Spots Left

9 November 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Thank you to everyone who submitted an app, We now have 10 wrestlers in the Signature Wrestling Federation! We would still like 8 more, so if anyone out there is looking for an on-time fed with well-written cards, check out the link below. It seems difficult to fill out the tag team division, but we're accepting tag team apps as well as singles.
Hopefully we will air on the new TEWF network, so people can get a look at us as well. Check us out and thanks to all the people who sent a kind word my way, it is appreciated.
http://www.geocities.com/signaturewrestling/
