Flying Kohler Boys segment

What this is A segment written for the Flying Kohler Boys to run right before their ICW match, e-mailed to the fed’s manager on 11 September 2012; from the Janitor’s own mail archive, donated on 9 October 2026. Names and addresses are marked.

Follows the application. Served whole, as written.

---------- Forwarded message --------- From: [name withheld] Date: Tue, Sep 11, 2012 at 8:23 PM Subject: Segment Flying Kohler Boys segment for right before their match: [The show cuts away to the background, where surely something utterly amazing and stunning most be happening.] [Backstage, a wrinkled old man in a bright purple fuzzy robe is passed out completely unconscious on a shower bench. A young kid suddenly comes around the corner, attempting to tighten down the white wrestling headgear. He groans and kicks the bench that his grandfather is currently sleeping on. Jerry "Pops" Kohler, referee-turned-senile-old-wrestler, wakes up with a start, and swings feebly at the air.] Pops: "What! Who! I'll take you all on! Come at me, boys! Come at me!!!" [Jerry Kohler finally remembers to put on his thick coke-bottle glasses, and squints confused at his grandson before smiling.] Pops: "It's time, isn't it? Is it really time!?!" Johnny: "Yeah, grandpa. They just had some guy come by to say we've got about five minutes before we have to get out there. I'm kind of scared." Pops: "Of those gay cowboys and those skinny jean wearing 90s rejects?" Johnny: "Nah. I just ... you know, there's not usually much of a crowd when I wrestle in high school. I don't know what it's going to be like going out there. I've seen wrestling on TV ... there could be tens of thousands of people out there!" Pops: "Kid, you've been 18 for three weeks, and I'm 81 years old. You really think there's going to be thousands of fans watching the kind of fed that would sign us to a contract? No way in hell this place has insurance." Johnny: "Good point." Pops: "Now what in the name of Frank Sinatra's rotting penis are you wearing?" [Johnny Kohler looks up and down his simple red wrestling garb and shrugs.] Johnny: "What? It's my wrestling gear! What are you wearing?" Pops: "Haven't I taught you anything yet, boy! Wrestling's all about flash! Pizazz! Do you know who gave me this robe?" Johnny: "No, but I bet you're going to tell me, and I bet whoever gave it to you has been dead for twenty years." Pops: "Classy Cain Conners gave me this robe back in nineteen hundred sixty seven, in the year of the lord! And boy, he told me what I'm about to tell you ... if you're going to be a wrestler, you've got to excite! Titilate! Thrill! You've gotta secrete pizzazz from every pore! And boy, sex sells in the wrestling business! That's why I've got ..." [Jerry Kohler throws open his robe, revealing his tiny, withered body, clothed only in a tiny bedazzled ruby banana hammock. Pops tries to gyrate a little, but seems to throw out his back a little, and lets the robe close back as he sinks back to sitting while wincing.] Pops: "And do you know who gave me these tights?" Johnny: "Grandpa, I've never wanted to know anything less in my entire life. Can we just go get this travesty over with?" [Johnny Kohler helps his grandfather up, as the Flying Kohler Boys, who could not make their name more of a lie if they tried, start heading down to the aisle for their big wrestling debut.] Pops: "I got these back during the Great War from ..." [To the ring!]
