[YOKO PPV] FLASH PACKET #1

5 January 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
CCW Presents: The Rodney 'Yokozuna' Anoia Memorial PPV Spectacular! FLASH PACKET 1: . . . . . [A parking lot. And a rather empty one at that. The darkness of the night blankets most of the surroundings, save for a few scattered street (or in this case, parking) lights illuminating various parts of the lot. There are only a few cars left, all positioned in a close proximity to each other; but only one actually has someone standing near it.
The car? Well, it's a red Lexus, circa 1998. And the someone standing near it? Well, it's the big show!
OK, not really. It's actually the blond-haired Florida native known as "The Natural" Adam Rogers. The EMWC employee is decked out in some pretty plain-looking clothes, consisting of a red golf pullover and khakis. The trunk of his car is opened, and he's in the process of loading a few bags into the back when he's interrupted by the man making his glorious return from jail, agent extraordinaire, Scott
Jameson. Let the wacky hijinx begin.] SJ: Hey, hey, hey, look who's back! [Adam's less than thrilled.] AR: I'll try to contain my excitement. SJ: No need for that, my man, because Scott Jameson has done it again! [Oh boy.] AR: Done... what? SJ: Well, got you your next ticket to fortune and glory, homeslice! Check this out... there's this big event coming up called the Rodney
Anoanaiski Memorial something-or-other. And guess who got _you_ a spot on it!
[Scott grins, like he's done something right. Adam just rolls his eyes.] AR: Scott, don't you think my schedule is full enough as it is? SJ: Beats me, dude. I just got out of jail, remember? AR: Unfortunately, yes. So what's the deal with this thing? [Scott fumbles around in his pocket briefly, finally coming up with a crumpled sheet of paper, which he hands to Adam. Rogers unfolds it and looks it over while Scott goes on... and on... and on.]
SJ: You've got a match with, like, three other people. I've even heard of one of them... I think. That Flash Gordon guy, I believe it was.
AR: You mean Andrew "Flash" Tucker, I'm guessing? SJ: Umm... I guess. Cool superhero, he was. AR: Right. Well, thanks so much for you help again. [Sarcasm duly noted by all except Captain Oblivious there, who nods his head.] SJ: Hey, no problemo. Just glad to be back at your side, amigo. Now, if you'll excuse me, I've got to find some female companionship for the night. [With those words, Jameson quickly heads off-camera, thank goodness. Adam watches him go, shaking his head... I mean, who actually expects
Jameson to get some snatch?]
AR: Makes me wish I hadn't bailed his sorry butt out of jail. I just hope he's learned his lesson about messing around with underaged girls.
[~!] AR: Anyway, looks like he booked me without asking again, so let's see what's in store for me.
Andrew Tucker... Vandal Gomez... [A pause, as his eyebrows visably perk up and a smile comes across his face.] AR: And Madison J. Valentine. How about that for the fickle finger of fate? Looks like we get to relive those _glorious_ days of Global
Pro Wrestling.
[Right. A roll of the eyes lets us know how much he actually meant that comment. Tossing the sheet of paper in the trunk, he continues.]
AR: Four corners match for the Bobby Duncum, Jr. Memorial Trophy. I've gotta admit it... Scott actually came through with something good for a change here. Never been in the ring with either Tucker or
Gomez. But Valentine? He and I know each other all too well. [Another grin, and a nod, as he leans back against his car.] AR: GPW. Despite all the crap it gets... and rightly so... it was still the place that got my career going, and Valentine's as well. In fact, it was a win over Valentine that gave me the first major title of my career. But MJ was the real star there, and me? Well, I was the guy who never quite became the true marquee name. Always played behind MJ and Tommy Gunn, because they projected the "bad boy"
image... and I didn't. Now, though... I'm the one going to the bank a whole lot more often than either of those two. Call it timing, call it skill, call it blind luck... but you can bet more people now know who Adam Rogers is than Mad Valentine.
And knowing MJ's super-sized ego... I'd say that doesn't sit too well with him. [MJV? Ego? Naaaaah.] AR: So, I guess you could say me and Mad have some unfinished business to attend to. I'd imagine, MJ, that you're looking to avenge your loss to me from nearly a year back... not to mention the fact that I've made it bigger than you, right? Well, chief, I've got some news for you...
It ain't happening. Gomez and Tucker aside, MJ, time's proven who the better man is. You may have been the man in Global Pro... but you're not now. Yeah, you did alright over in IWWC, and you're making a name for yourself in
N.E.W... and honestly, man, I'm happy to see that... but you also know it's eating you up inside that I've made it bigger than you.
Well, Mad... there's a reason for that. And you can bet Kingsley's salary that I'll show you that reason at the pay-per-view.
[Kingsley makes a salary? Anyway, we get a brief pause here, for dramatic effect, I suppose.]
AR: But this _is_ a four corners match, which means it's not going to be an old GPW "who's better" contest. And thank god for that, because who wants to see more Furious Steele or Chris Page?
[Ouch. Truth does hurt, indeed.] AR: So, we're going to be joined by Vandal Gomez... and one of the most well-known names in the game in Andrew Tucker.
Tucker, it's an honor to be in the ring with him. Like him or hate him, he's one of the best in the game. And considering his two-plus year stint in tag team wrestling's now said and done, you can bet he's going to be using this match to launch that singles career again.
Drew, as much as I respect you, and all that you're accomplished, you can bank on one thing...
You're _not_ going to revive your singles career at _my_ expense. [Well... someone's a little bold, aren't they?] AR: With that said, Drew... I'm very much looking forward to the change to step into the ring and compete against you.
Vandal Gomez, on the other hand... [Rogers shakes his head.] AR: Vandal just flat-out worries me. Not the "run away and hide" type of worried, mind you... more of a "this guy is a suicidal lunatic" type of worried. I mean, let's face it... the guy will do
_anything_ in the ring, which means the remaining three of us could be in danger of who knows what kind of damage.
Not exactly the kind of risk I'm wanting to be taking... but I sure as hell ain't planning on backing away from it, either. Vandal, you can throw your little fireballs, carve crosses in your hands, do all your suicidal dives... but that doesn't mean you're going to intimidate anyone once we're in that ring.
Remember where I make my living, Gomez. The stuff you do? Hell, man, that's child's play compared to the crap I've got to endure every time we put on a show. Are you a few cards short of a full deck? Yeah.
Are you dangerous? Yeah. But that doesn't mean I'm going to hide behind Tucker and Valentine, either.
[Another pause. Not much action going on here.] AR: Nothing's really on the line in this match... no titles, no money, no guaranteed top ranking... just a trophy...
... and bragging rights. [Hey, there's that smirk again!] AR: Gomez, Tucker, and especially you, MJ? I plan on being the one who owns those rights. [Fade.] ------------------------------------------------------------------------ [The scene opens up in a skybox, overlooking a football field, the sun already set and the stadium lights illuminating a jam-packed stadium as tens of thousands of people watch the defensively-heavy Orange Bowl.
Below on the field, Oklahoma linebacker Rocky Calmus gets a hold of
Heisman winner Chris Weinke, forcing the Florida State standout to fumble the ball near in their own redzone. An audible groan is heard throughout the arena and an audible explative is head overhead.]

'Specially fah a guy who doesn't have a "name" ta bring in behind
'im. [Shrug.] AT : All that name stuff don't much matter ta me, though. All I know is that I expect a war from you, Rogers. Nothin' less. You may be a natural at wrestlin', I've seen your type. Hell, you remind me a little bit o' mahself, which may or may not be a good thing.
With that said, though, I can get mahself a little idea o' what you're gonna be bringin' ta the table January the 21st.
Whatever it is, I'll be ready fah it. [Tucker reaches forward, picking a bottled water up, taking a swig, then placing it back down, watching down on the field a bit more, shaking his head.]
AT : We all know what ole' Madison J. Valentine'll be bringin' ta the arena. An ego. Who needs _real_ wrestlin' talent when ya'
can live in some sort o' pipe dream actually believin' that you've got an ounce o' talent inside that 190-pound frame, ya ignorant runt.
[Tucker pauses, almost seething at the thought of MJV's attitude.] AT : I can see it now, Mad... You'll be sittin' there ramblin' on in incoherent sentences 'bout how much classier you are than I am, an' how much talent can fill up that little body o' yours. An'
the sad thing 'bout it is... [Pause.] AT : ...You actually believe it. In that diluted mind o' yours, you actually believe that you're worth a damn ta this business, an' that I'm jes' some washed-up dinosaur or some shiot. Well,
I tell ya' what, Emm-Jay-Vee. If you wanna see if I'm washed up, if I'm worth anythin' anybody has ever said 'bout me, I ask you ta do only one thing.
Show up. [Tucker pauses again, looking out the window, watching Florida State capture a safety.] AT : Well, at least we scored. [Chuckle.] AT : Speakin' o' diluted minds, though, how could ya forget 'bout

[Tucker winks, laughing.] AT : Either way, it's gonna be one helluva match, no doubt. I may go in there as the favorite, I really don't know. But, what I do know is that if these three guys bring everything they've got ta the ring January 21st.

[Fade to black.] ------------------------------------------------------------------------ [The camera opens to a pure white screen, nothing definite is visible anywhere. But, oh, the wonder of audio. Soft sounds are heard coming from what sounds like two people, most likely a man and a woman.]
VO1 : Mmmm.....Yeah... VO2 : Ohh...How's that? VO1 : Perrrrfect... There... Right there... You're doing wonderful... [The camera begins to zoom out and the white background becomes recognizable as some sort of plastic container. As the camera continues to zoom out, more things are visible. "That sinks. Time ta sink or float, Ace. Ah hope you do yer homework."
