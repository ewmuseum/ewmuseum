TITLE LINEAGES

STW World Heavyweight Championship

1-Avatarr the Collector, Bulletin #8, Corestate Center, Philadelphia, PA. Defeated Ground Zero in a tourney final.

2-Mr. Fantastic Geoff, Bulletin #16, Toronto Skydome in Toronto, ON, CAN.

3-Lobo the Crippler, Bulletin #22, Wrigley Field, Chicago, IL. Defeated Universal Man in a one night tournament final to win the vacant championship. He also beat Kane Nevermor in round 1 and Black Mask Horror in the semi-finals.

4-Big Dredd Vedder, Bulletin #27, STW Arena, Phoenix, AZ. This was a ''4-way Dance'' in which ''Glistening'' Vin Avery and Kane Nevermor also participated.

5-Lucien Champion, Bulletin #31, Fleet Center, Boston, MA.

6-Lightning Mountain, Bulletin #35, Pontiac Silver Dome, Pontiac, MI.

7-Kane Nevermor, Bulletin #40, The Paladium, Hollywood, CA.

8-The Mighty Hitman, Bulletin #41, Civic Arena, Pittsburg, Penn. Note that this was a 3-way dance, also involving Lightning Mountain.

9-Mercury, Bulletin #43, Toronto Skydome in Toronto, ON, CAN. This was a one fall 4-way match, also involving Lucien Champion and Kane Nevermor. Mercury pinned Champion with his foot on the ropes to win the title.

10-Lucien Champion (#2), Bulletin #44, Kiel Center, St. Louis, Mo. This was a 4-way elimination match also involving the Mighty Hitman and Lightning Mountain (substituting for Kane Nevermor) in a rematch from the previous show, due to the controversial manner in which the title changed hands.

11-Dangerous Dave Howard, Bulletin #55, Location Unknown.

12-"Time Bomb" Blaze Colton, Bulletin #58, Location Unknown.

13-Lucien Champion (3x), Bulletin #61, Location Unknown.

14-"Ab Fab" Adam Colton, Bulletin #65, Location Unknown. Colton was reigning World Light Heavyweight champion. Unifies the two titles.

15-Jake Rush won bull #67. Rush was Reigning Unified Double Crown champion. He also wins Colton's World Light Heavyweight championship, unifying all three titles. Still champion as of Bulletin #69. STW folded shortly thereafter; he is recognized as the last champion.

STW Supreme/World Tag Team Championship

1-The Spirit Thieves (Avatarr the Collector and Kane Nevermor) were announced champions in the Commissioner's report after beating The Celestials via forfeit in a tourney final in bull #9.

2-The World Wide Celebrities beat the Mighty Rednecks via forfeit in bull #13 at the Tokyo Dome in Tokyo, Japan, to fill the vacant title.

3-Death Warrior & Renegade Cop beat the World Wide Celebrities in bull #15 at the STW Arena in Phoenix, Ariz.

4-Rick ''Rocket'' Smits & Reignman Reggie Miller beat Death Warrior & Renegade Cop in bull #16 at the Toronto Skydome in Toronto, Ontario, Canada.

5-Death Warrior & Renegade Cop (2) beat Rick ''Rocket'' Smits & Reignman Reggie Miller in bull #18 at the Tacoma Dome in Tacoma, Wash.

6-Los Dos Capones beat Death Warrior & Renegade Cop in bull #20 at Madison Square Garden in New York City, N.Y. They pinned The World Wide Celebrities to win the belts; this was a Triple Threat.

7-Frag Master & Big Tex Williams beat Los Dos Capones in bull #22 at Wrigley Field in Chicago, Illinois.

8-The Celestials beat Frag Master & Big Tex Williams in bull #24 at STW Arena in Phoenix, Ariz.

9-Muscle Inc. beat The Celestials in bull #26 at the Georgia Dome in Atlanta, Georgia.

8-The Celestials beat Muscle Inc. in bull #31 at the Fleet Center in Boston, Mass. The titles were declared vacant in bull #35 when The Celestials were stripped following the controversial ending of their ladder match against The Rush Brothers.

9-For a brief moment The Rushes were declared new champions, but they refused the belts and that title change was nulled and the belts remained vacant.

10-"Time Bomb" Blaze Colton & "Dangerous" Dave Howard beat Rob Retribution in a tourney final in bull #38 at the Oakland Arena in Oakland, Calif. Rob Retribution's partner Chris Crono did not show up for the match, as it was later learned that he had left the promotion.

11-The Scrub Club beat "Time Bomb" Blaze Colton & "Dangerous" Dave Howard in bull #41 at Civic Arena, in Pittsburg, Penn. This was a 3-Way Dance, also involving Cherry Bomb. Vacant as of bull #43, champions to be determined.

12-Rasslin Rebels (Atlanta Brawler & Dixie Destroyer) won bulletin #45, defeating the A & A Express, Sex and Violence, and the Blade Runners.

13-Alliance of Violence (Blaze Colton & Mighty Hitman) won bull #52.

14-Tommy Rush & Trinity won bulletin #53. Lineage unknown here until Bulletin #58.

15?-Full Throttle (Jackknife Justin Rage & Shotgun Austin Fear) won bulletin #58 from ?

16?-Demolition 3000 (Brock the Pulverizer & Vlad the Impaler) won bulletin #59 by beating Full Throttle. Last Champions.

STW National Television Championship

Jumpman Michael Jordan won a battle royal in bull #08 at the Georgia Mountain Center in Gainesville, Ga., to become the first champion.

Lightning Mountain beat Jumpman Michael Jordan in bull #11 at Cobo Hall in Detroit, Mich.

King Of Extreme beat Lightning Mountain in bull #23 at Cobo Hall in Detroit, Mich. This was a ''3-Way dance''. Black Mask Horror was also involved.

Bloodscream beat King Of Extreme in bull #25 at Philadelphia Spectrum in Philadelphia, Penn.

"Dangerous" Dave Howard beat Bloodscream in bull #26 at the Georgia Dome in Atlanta, Ga.

Lightning Mountain beat "Dangerous" Dave Howard in bull #28 at the Houston Civic Center in Houston, Tex.

Lucien Champion beat Lightning Mountain. This was a ''Title Vs. Title'' match in bull #33, in which Champion's STW World Heavyweight title was also on the line, at the Alamo Dome in San Antonio, Tex. In bull #34, STW officially stripped Champion of the title, as he at the time also held both the STW and CPW World Heavyweight titles, and thus hadn't the time to devote to the title. Champion is recognized as the last title holder, as in bull #35 the belt was unified with the vacant International title to form the 'Unified Double Crown' championship. The lineage continues there.

STW International Title

Big Time Player won a Battle Royal in Bull #16 at the Toronto Skydome in Toronto, Ontario, Canada.

Telmeku the Mongolian Warrior beat Big Time Player in bull #18 at the Fighting Osaka Gym in Osaka, Japan.

Kerry Garret beat Telmeku the Mongolian Warrior in bull #24 at the Toronto Skydome in Toronto, Ontario, Canada.

Christian ''God Damn'' Cross beat Kerry Garret in bull #27 at the STW Arena in Phoenix, Ariz.

Blaze Colton beat Christian ''God Damn'' Cross in bull #31 at the Fleet Center in Boston, Mass. This was a Triple Threat match also including ''Dangerous'' Dave Howard. Colton pinned Howard for the win. Colton was stripped in bulletin #33, for failure to defend the title and for attacking STW officials. Colton is recognized as the last title holder as the belt was unified with the vacant National Television title to become part of the 'Unified Double Crown' championship.

STW Unified Double Crown Title

*Note: In bull's #33 & #34 respectively, both the STW International and STW National Television titles were vacated. They became unified as the STW Unified Double Crown championship in bull #35 to declare an undisputed secondary singles champion. Both title lineages are recognized as part of the unified championship's lineage.*

Kaos Knight beat Blaze Colton, Lobo the Crippler & Stump Anaki in an 'Endurathon' match to win the titles in bull #35 at the Pontiac Silver Dome in Pontiac, Mich.

"Time Bomb" Blaze Colton beat Kaos Knight at the third annual IWF PPV (STW cycle #37) at Madison Square Garden in New York City, N.Y.

Kaos Knight beat "Time Bomb" Blaze Colton in bull #39 at the MGM Grand Garden Arena in Las Vegas, Nev. This was a '2-out-of-3 Falls Count Anywhere' match, with Kaos beating Blaze 2-falls-to-1.

Hypnos beat Kaos Knight in bull #42 at the HSBC Arena in Buffalo, NY.

Kaos Knight beat Hypnos in bull #45 at the Alamo Dome in San Antonio, Tex.

"Mr. Attitude" Jeremy beat Kaos Knight in bulletin #47.

"the One" Orlando Rock beat "Mr. Attitude" Jeremy Nace in bulletin #49.

Genetic Freak Justin Stone beat "the One" Orlando Rock in bulletin #50.

Jake Rush won the title in bulletin #53.

Genetic Freak Justin Stone won the title in Bulletin #??

Unknown between Bulletin #55 &ndash; Bulletin #58.

Tommy Rush won the title in bulletin #59.

"Howling Mad" Murdock McTavish won the title in bulletin #64.

Jake Rush won the title in bulletin #67.

"Mr. Attitude" Jeremy Nace won the title in bulletin #69. Title lineage is unknown after this.

STW Sheer Hardcore Title

Big Daddy G. beat Hardcore Brandon Camden in the last leg of a "Pier Six Brawl" gauntlet match to win the vacant title in bull #20 at Madison Square Garden in New York City, N.Y. Also involved in the match was Spine Snapper, Mr. Downtown, Ultimate Terror & Bloodscream.

Kaos Knight beat Death Jester in a match for the vacant title in bull #22 at Wrigley Field in Chicago, Illinois. Big Daddy G. was stripped after leaving the league.

Spine Snapper beat Kaos Knight in bull #24 at the STW Arena in Phoenix, Ariz.

Mercury beat Spine Snapper in bulletin #33 at the Alamo Dome in San Antonio, Tex.

Avatarr the Collector beat Mercury in bull #34 at the Market Square Arena in Indianapolis, Ind. Bloodscream was guest referee.

Ajax beat Avatarr the Collector in bull #41 at the Civic Arena, in Pittsburg, Penn. Note this was a gauntlet match. Avatarr wrestled, and defeated Adrian Dango & Hypnos previously.

"Way Hot" Scott Colton won the title in bulletin #53.

Unknown between Bulletin #54 &ndash; Bulletin #57.

"Way Hot" Scott Colton won the title in bulletin #58.

Genetic Freak Justin Stone won the title in bulletin #62.

"Abfab" Adam Colton won the title in bulletin #63.

Ajax won the title in bulletin #63.

Vlad the Impaler won the title in bulletin #65.

Ajax won the title in bulletin #69. This marked the last official reign of the STW Sheer Hardcore Title.

STW World Light-Heavyweight Title

Kensuki Tanaka beat Porkchop Cash in a Tourney final in bull #18 at the Tacoma Dome in Tacoma, Wash.

Porkchop Cash beat Kensuki Tanaka in bull #20 at Madison Square Garden in New York City, N.Y. Everlasting Torture beat Porkchop Cash in bull #21 at the San Jose Shark Tank in San Jose, Calif.

Green Blazer beat Everlasting Torture in bull #24 at the STW Arena in Phoenix, Ariz.

Mercury beat ''Dangerous'' Dave Howard in bull #25 at the Philadelphia Spectrum in Philadelphia, Penn., to win the vacant title. The title was retired with Mercury as the title holder in bull #27 at the STW Arena.

Cardinal Sin/Lightning Knight beat ''Mr. May'' Aaron Duncan in a tourney final to win the newly re-activated title in bull #35 at the Pontiac Silver Dome, Pontiac, Mich. Note that shortly after winning the title, Cardinal Sin changed his name to Lightning Knight.

Wandering Soul beat Lightning Knight in bull #37 at the Sun Dome in Tampa, Fla.

Kabuki Dragon beat Thanatos and Dalen Sickmiller in bull #40 at the Paladium in Hollywood, Calif. This was a match to claim the vacant title. It became vacant when Kabuki, Thanatos and Sickmiller all pinned champion Wandering Soul at the same time in a 5-Way match in bull #39.

"The Sellout" Rocky Austin beat Kabuki Dragon in bull #41 at the Civic Arena in Pittsburg, Penn. This was a Fatal 4-Way, also involving Motoharu Kajitsu & Tommy Rush.

Tommy Rush beat "The Sellout" Rocky Austin in bull #45 at the Alamo Dome, in San Antonio, TX.

"Abfab" Adam Colton beat Tommy Rush in bulletin #52.

Mercury won the title in bulletin #60.

"Abfab" Adam Colton won the title in bulletin #63.

Jake Rush won the title in bulletin #67.

"Abfab" Adam Colton won the title in bulletin #69. Title lineage is unknown after this.

STW Intercontinental Women's Title

Veronica B. was awarded the first title by stand-in commissioner [name withheld] in bull #44 at the Kiel Center in St. Louis, Mo.

Sex Goddess Sunny Beach beat Veronica B. in bull #45 at the Alamo Dome in San Antonio, Tex. This was a triple threat also involving Pandora.

Cousin Becky beat Sex Goddess Sunny Beach in bulletin #48.

Sex Goddess Sunny Beach beat Cousin Becky in bulletin #50.

Unknown between Bulletin #55 &ndash; Bulletin #57.

Cousin Becky won the title in Bulletin #58.

STW World Light-Heavyweight Tag Team Championship

Kol Sokkussu & Nick Octane won the titles in bulletin #67.

Rob Retribution & Gabriel! won the titles in bulletin #69. Title lineage is unknown after this.

STW United States Championship

Mr. Fantastic won the title in bulletin #69. Title lineage is unknown after this.
