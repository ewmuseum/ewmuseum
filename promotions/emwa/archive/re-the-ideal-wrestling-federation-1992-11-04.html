Save your typing... I _know_ there are a lot of fantasy federations (I run one of 'em, the oldest, as far as I know). That isn't what I'm proposing. What I'm looking for here is a discussion of what people think would make the ideal wrestling federation in real life! No computer dreamcards, no imaginary wrestlers--I'm asking who you would want to see wrestling, who you would want to see promoting, who you would want to see managing, how you would like to see things organized, what- ever! Point is, I want a serious discussion of a serious topic which I feel is valid and has very little to do with the e-mail leagues or the FWF. --Ben
