The oldest e-wrestling league on record by its own founder's twice-told account — started in September 1990, closed the following May, and running again by 1992 with a World Champion it put on the line against another federation's. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The E-Mail Wrestling Association is the oldest e-wrestling league anyone in the record claims to know of, and the claim is its own founder's. Asked on 17 April 1992 what the EMWA was, he answered: “The EMWA (E-Mail Wrestling Association) is (as far as I know) the oldest of the E-wrestling leagues. I started it in September of '90, but had to close it up around May of '91 due to finals and summer vacation.”

Seven months later, arguing about something else entirely and not repeating himself, he gave the same date from memory: “back when I started the EMWA, two years ago, I started out posting on the net, and was asked (politely by some, not so politely by others) to take it off the net. In hindsight, I have to agree that this was a fair request.” Two accounts, seven months apart, both landing on September 1990.

By its own founder's account it closed in the spring of 1991 and other leagues arrived while it was shut. It was running again by April 1992 under two managers, “although not very well organized at present”, and the same month it lent its World Champion out: John Girder was carried onto Dreamcard V to face another federation's champion, title against title, and lost by disqualification when his opponent threw him a set of brass knuckles and then acted as though he had been hit with them. One of its own managers said four months later that the crossover was his and the founder's own call — they had let Stilt Man, the Dreamcard's own organizer, use the champion for it. That October the other federation was calling him “at least former” champion.

In November 1992, the count of the fantasy federations then running around the newsgroup named it first of nine. A month before that, someone posted asking whoever had been writing about the EMWA to get in touch, having forgotten to leave an address the first time.

Nothing it staged survives. It was a mail league, and its own founder took it off the newsgroup early and agreed that was right; the newsgroup is where it was talked about, not where it was played. What it left is the talk — and a founding date nobody else in the record can beat.

Affiliations

The Summit of E-Wrestling

Member of EMWA (1990) (14 June 1991 – 12 July 1991, as the served corpus runs)
A founder member of the first Summit, named in its own opening night's roll call of the three federations present and again in its own pre-event Update, which prints the battle royal and tag tournament fields against the federation each entrant represented. Its own World Tag Team Champions were seeded first into the Road Warrior's Cup and its Women's Tag Team Champions wrestled the only women's match on the card. It is NOT among the four federations the second Summit names six months later.
