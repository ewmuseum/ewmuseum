What is the purpose of this fed? Carribean Island Wrestling is what we in the e-wrestling world call a regional fed. This means that we cover only a specific region of the world.
Our region of course is the Carribean Sea. Consisting of many islands such as Aruba, Puerto Rico, the Bahamas, Cuba, the Dominican Republic, and the list goes on and on.
CIW is more considered a standard wrestling orgnization without that "extreme" edge. The standard rules are one can win by pinfall, submission, a ten count count-out, referee's decision, and of course disqualifications.
Disqualifications occur when a wrestler uses a "foreign" object such as tables, chairs, etc., strikes an official, another wrestler interferes, or the wrestler uses dirty deeds such as choking, gouging, and low-blowing to an excess.
CIW houses it's headquarters in Puerto Rico and is funded by Carnival Cruiselines. The majority of our events take place on Puerto Rico, but CIW does go on what we call "Island Hopping" tours where we try to make a stop on every island in the Carribean before making our way back to Puerto Rico.
The overall goal of CIW is to have fun. We'd rather see people wanting to have fun working together and planning out in-depth and fun storylines to make CIW fun. We'd rather not see people coming in and wanting to win every match, win every title and bitch and moan everytime things don't go their way. Nor does CIW like the super-ego, so if you are one with a big ego, it's not likely that CIW is the place for you. So, that is that.
2. Who runs this fed? (Fantasy) CIW as the idea of a man known as Jack Firestone. Mr. Firestone saw the Carribean Sea as an excellent region for wrestling action. However, Firestone had one problem, he had a lack of money, which is due to his last business venture the Mainstream Wrestling Federation. Then in came Carnival
Cruiselines who saw how mainstream pro-wrestling had become and decided that by having ties with pro-wrestling might lead to even more income, so they gave Jack Firestone the financial backing he needed, not to forget the political power needed to compete on the many islands of the Carribean Sea.
After cutting deals with the local television stations of the Carribean islands, DirecTV, USSB, Viewer's Choice, and other sattelite and pay per view companies, Firestone is now able to market his promotion to every person in the Carribean. Hopefully, in the future he will be able to expand even further.
3. Who runs this fed? (IRL) CIW is run by [name withheld] and [name withheld]. Who have both been in the game for two-three years. They have both also run other past feds such as the MWF. That's all you really need to know, we will write, book, and make cookies for all the handlers. Well, not the cookies part, but that would be sweet.
Anyway, we also hope to have many, many writers to help us with the activities of the fed. Whether your a weekly writer, monthly writer, whenever you feel like it writer, or only writes for a big events writer, it doesn't matter, we really appreciate writers, and well we need 'em, cause although we claim to be, we are really not supermen.
4. What are the titles here? CIW currently as it stands sanctions three championships. Two for singles wrestlers, one for tag teams. As the roster and the league expands perhaps we will sanction more gold.
4.1 CIW Carribean Heavyweight Title The CIW Carribean Heavyweight Title is the most valued prize for every wrestler competing the Carribean. The man who holds this title will be sought after by everyman from every island in the Carribean Sea. This title must be defended every thirty days and this rule will be enforced.
This title may also be defended if the champion decides to on his own, but the championship committee will always have a top contender to face the champ every thirty days.
4.2 CIW Island Paradise Title The CIW Island Paradise Title is the promotion's secondary title in that the one who holds this title is usually always the number one contender to the Carribean Heavyweight Title. This title is defended every 14 days and although the Island Paradise Champion is usually the number one contender for the Carribean Heavyweight Title, his number one priority is defending the Island Paradise Title and thus the championship committee has decided that after three successful title defenses then the Island Paradise Champion will be allowed to challenge for the
Carribean Heavyweight Title.
4.3 CIW Carribean Tag Team Titles This is the prize for all of the duos in the CIW. It too must be defended every thirty days. CIW feels that tag team wrestling is very important to it's promotion and thus encourages the entrance of formal tag teams (eg. Road Warriors, The Rockers.) and we also strongly encourage those who find themselves in the bottom of the singles ranks to form teams with other bottom dwellers and try to have a successful run in the tag team division before trying a hand at singles again.
5. What are the shows? 5.1 Tidal Wave This is CIW's live show each and every week and is hosted by the dynamic duo of Jack Cafferty and Jason Keller. It usually contains five-seven live matches with the occasional dark matches. Occasionally, we might expand to have special editions of Tidal Wave where more matches and more action is required.
5.2 Flashwire This is CIW's "flash" show where CIW wrestlers voice their opinions on past and upcoming events. These come in forms of flashes written by the handlers which will be explained later in the packet. This show is hosted by Jack Cafferty and Jason Keller.
5.3 Supercards and Pay Per Views. Every couple months CIW hosts special supercars, sometimes called pay per views. Whether it be a special edition of Tidal Wave or an event with an entirely different name.
5.4 Normal Schedule Here is the schedule of how things pan out, on a weekly basis. Sunday: CIW Update comes out with upcoming events, deadlines, and general news listed. Wednesday: Flashes and Strats due. Thursday: Flashwire comes out. Saturday: Tidal Wave comes out. 6. Who are the staff members? These are the guys who call the action, interview the talent, keep control of the matches, and sign the checks.
6.1 Announcers 6.1.1 Jack Cafferty Cafferty is a middle aged man with a vast knowledge of the sport of wrestling. He's the serious type. He has a wife and kids and takes his job seriously as he has to support his family. He has had experience announcing in such organizations as MWF, UEW, and SOW
6.1.2 Jason Keller Keller is a young, punk color commentator. He's quick with the insults and has an obsession with sex. He is known to poke fun at
Mr. Cafferty as well as scout out the female audience. He has had experience as a color commentator in numerous organizations such as
MWF, SOW, ACWA, NJWC, and many others. 6.2 Interviewers These are the people you use when you wish to have someone interview your wrestler in a flash.
6.2.1 Marcia Brown Marcia is one of those drop dead gorgeous types. Known for wearing form fitting clothing and coming onto the wrestlers which she interviews.
6.2.2 Danny Peterson Peterson is a young man trying to catch a break as a broadcast journalist. He is nervous, makes mistakes, and is easily intimidated.
6.3 Referees 6.3.1 George Grant George is the head referee, who has been in the business for a long time. He's wise and knows the rules. However, he can get lost in the action due to being an old fart.
6.3.2 Harley Rogers Harley is a former bouncer turned wrestler turned referee. His wrestling career ended with a knee injury, so he became a ref. He is still in good shape and usually doesn't get pushed around.
However, he has been known to accept bribes pretty easily. 6.3.3 Keiji Nomo Straight from Japan, Keiji is still mastering the English language. However, he's quick and is quite sharp. 6.3.4 Estevan Sanchez Estevan is by far the most poor of our referees. He is easily distracted and often gets knocked down at the most inopurtune moments.
6.4 Ringside Crew 6.4.1 Chris Haley Haley is CIW's ring announcer. He has a booming voice and the ladies are quite fond of him and his very wild tuxedos.
6.5 Suits 6.5.1 Jack Firestone Firestone is the president of CIW and controls all wrestling operations. It is his dream to make Carribean Island Wrestling a success and he will fine, suspend, or terminate any wrestler trying to prevent his dream. Plus, on occassion he likes to play matchmaker.
6.5.2 Drake Savage Savage is CIW's Head of Security, however, he is more known as Firestone's right hand man. A former wrestler Savage is huge and many men back down from a fight with him. He is known for stepping in when things get out of hand or fighting on the be-half of Mr.
Firestone. 7. What I gotta do in this fed? 7.1 Roleplaying In CIW, you must roleplay as it shows your active and also shows us your character. It also helps us in the deciding factor of who to push or the old who wins this match formula. Here are some general rules.
1. Swearing. We allow that which is heard on television such as asshole, damn, and bitch. We do not allow the other words such as "shit" and "fuck." Nor do we except these words blanked out with [BLEEP.] 2. Attacks. Do not attack other wrestlers, announcers, referees, interviewers, or suits in your roleplays without getting permission first. 3. Kayfabe. In CIW we do not wish to be associated with the real world wrestling such as WWF, WCW, or ECW, so do not mention anything from that realm. Nor do we like for characters to refer to this as "e-wrestling."
The characters should pretend it's the real world and they are a "wrestler" not an "e-wrestler." Plus, excessive mention of other feds can be annoying too.
4. Form. As far as Flashwire flashes, the format of RPs doesn't really matter. However, when submitting something to be aired on Tital Wave, it should be in the following form. For example if Bob Johnson were standing in the ring about to cut a promo here is what it'd be like.
[Bob Johnson stands in a ring, wearing his usual wrestling trunks. He raises the mic to his lips and begins to speak.]
BJ: Blah, Blah, Blah. 5. Limits. As far as now we have one RP a week. Plus, in-rings and other segments to be submitted for Tidal Wave are always welcome. 7.2 Strats Strats if done right strats are very helpful in match writing. They have some booking weight, but not much. Plus, they allow you more control over you character. In CIW, we request that strats me in this form.
Entrance: Your particular entrance for that evening in cut and paste form. High Spots: This is for description of any moves you wish for your wrestler to do or any bumps you wish for your wrestler to take.
Finish: The way you'd like to see your wrestler win or lose. Other: Anything else you'd like to include. 7.3 Angles Angles are what makes e-wrestling fun, therefore, we as fedheads try our best to get everybody involved in an angle. However, we are not full of ideas, so it is up to you the handlers to create and execute angles for your wrestler of course you should also run them by us first.
8. Booking Policy CIW is indeed a booked fed as we make judgments based on RPs, strats, angles, and overall heat. We usually have a reason for a loss or a win, so don't bitch. That's one thing we can't stand is BITCHING, so please don't bitch in your stay in CIW, because you'll probably end-up out of
CIW. We'd rather have you ask why you lost, as opposed to bitching us out. 9. Do you need help? Where can I help? With any e-wrestling league, we always need help. It usually comes in the form of writing. However, if you feel you can help us with booking, a website, image making, or any other special thing we are always open to ideas, suggestions, and help.
