[HIW] NEW fed with a small twist

16 February 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
High Impact Wrestling Yes, it's yet another new fed. By and large, it is your standard e-fed, with one major exception which I will now explain.
The biggest problem every fed suffers from is fed head burn out. Running a fed is an extremely taxing and time consuming operation. One man by himself cannot do it for long, and even those with a small staff of writers burn out sooner or later. This is why most feds don't even last a month, and maybe 1 in a 1000 (or more) last a year.
The solution I propose to this problem is to take the most time consuming function (match writing) off the fed head's hands - and give it to the handlers. Here's how it would work:
Say you have a 16 man roster (one wrestler per handler), and one card a week where every wrestler has a match. That's 8 matches per week. That means each handler would only have to write one match every two weeks.
No suspense would be lost since each handler would only know the outcome of one out of every 16 matches. And of course nobody would write their own wrestler's matches. Also, note that the handlers would only write
the matches - they wouldn't book them. This would free up a tremendous amount of time for the fed head to upkeep duties such as booking, putting cards and promo shows together, and web page maintenance. Also, it should cut down on the card delays most feds suffer while the fed head struggles to find enough time and/or energy to finish writing the matches.
And with one promo show per week, the workload for the handlers would be a manageable: one flash per week, one strat (optional) per week, and one match every two weeks.
The only potential problem I see is handlers flaking out and not writing their assigned match. In such a case, the fed head would fill in and write it. However, since everyone is depending on each other, this cannot be a common occurrence. I think a fair rule is: Every one gets one excuse
- my computer crashed, my girlfriend died, whatever. After that, the handler is dropped from the fed. A bit harsh perhaps, but we all have to be able to count on each other.
If you've gotten this far and are interested in participating in this type of fed, here's the rest of the info.
There will be a 16 man roster. One wrestler per handler. Original wrestlers only. Male singles wrestlers only.
HIW is a booked fed. A wrestler's push will be based on quality of participation. mainly through interviews and strats. Other important factors are how much heat a wrestler draws, and how well he advances the angles he is involved in.
There are two titles: the Heavyweight and Cruiserweight championships. Anyone may compete for the Heavyweight title. Only wrestlers under 240 lbs. may compete for the Cruiserweight title.
There will be one card per week (probably on Monday) and one promo show per week (probably on Saturday). There will be a PPV approximately once very two months.
Still interested? Then you can check out the web page at http://members.home.net/citizenkyle/HIW.html
and sign up there. Thanks. [name withheld]
