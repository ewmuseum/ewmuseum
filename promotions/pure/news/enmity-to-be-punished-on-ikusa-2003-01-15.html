Enmity To Be Punished On Ikusa!

15 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
In a not so suprising call from Jamar Wilson, the PURE owner and president has decided that Enmity must be punished for his post match actions against Mang-A-Hero, which led to aggravated knee damage on the fan favorites knee. As of press time, there is no definite punishment set in stone for the hte Messiah of Mutilation, but Jamar was quite frank with his comments about the situation. "That was total bulls**t. I know that's unprofessional of myself to say, but what he did to Mang-A was totally unacceptable in our style of wrestling. MAH was already taking a huge risk by even entering that match, but the post match beating was uncalled for and it's high time somebody tugged the leash on E. Suspension...fine....contract termination...I have a feeling NONE of these are good enough punishment for his attempt to end a man's career," which was directly quoted from Jamar Wilson during a recent press conference on Sky=Perfect TV. Expect a clear-cut decision on the fate of Enmity at Ikusa this Sunday on UPN.
