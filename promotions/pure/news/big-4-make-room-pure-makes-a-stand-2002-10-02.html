Big 4...Make Room...PURE Makes A Stand....

2 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With PURE's Jamar Wilson suprisingly leading the charge of a new and improved unified roster of PUREists, the cocky Vice President has declared war on the Big 4 of PCW, RUSH, OSW, and FSW seizing now as the time to make the strike for PURE to make it's ascension to the top echelon of federations in the wrestling world. PURE will make their first strike in the "big leagues" by battling 5 of Xavier Kannon's hand-picked PCW warriors against the team that has stepped up to lead the charge against the mega-titan that is PCW. Those PUREists are the "hands-on" Vice President himself Jamar Wilson, the awe-inspiring Cianan, high-flying "S2" Suicidal Stevenson, the volitile Ali Khadaffi, and the vicious newcomer Enmity. These five make a stand for PURE's right to be recognized as among the best and tis push will continue with Ikusa 4, said to be a blockbuster show. Ikusa 4 will showcase the newly signed talent of PURE and Jamar promises it to be an unforgettable affair for old and new PURE fans alike. What does PURE havein store with it's new stream-lined roster? Can they successfully debut in the big time against what PCW can offer? Will they realize their dream? Find out at PCW's Red October this Sunday, October the 6th, only on pay-per-view. And be kept aware of PURE's happenings when the next Ikusa card is revealed directly after the pay-per-view. Let the PURE-ification commence...
