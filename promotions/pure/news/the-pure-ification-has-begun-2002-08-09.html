The PURE-Ification Has Begun....

9 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sean Williams has released the first Ikusa card for the returning PURE and it looks to be a showcase of the star power, new and old, for the returning puro fed! Two Ultimate Junior tourney matches will be decided as FSW's Tim Walden battles against female crusierweight sensation, Gina Muniz of DWO. The other tourney match will be FSW's Chinese Chick Magnet, Ming, against "The Dark Poet" Rayze. Two hot tourney matches to begin the crowning of the Ultimate Juniorweight champion and a white hot main-event between Inphino Blitz and The End look to satisfy the urge for hot puro action. Two other matches also compliment the card as well as answers to the burning question.... Can Sean Williams and his unwanted new partner, Jamar Wilson, co-exist to make PURE the hot international force it once was? Be sure to view the card on the Ikusa drop down menu or via link on the bottom half of the screen and prepare yourself for PURE action...if you can handle it!
