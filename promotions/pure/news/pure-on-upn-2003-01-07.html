PURE on UPN!

7 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
On Tuesday, January 7th PURE owner Jamar Wilson made the final signing for a television contract with UPN. PURE wanted to open up their market to an American audience after previously signing television deals with several Japanese networks. When Jamar Wilson was interviewed, he had this to say: “Our Japanese competition is strict, and our American competition is even more strict. This will be sure to set us on an even playing field with all the promotions out there.” The first Ikusa is still yet to be announced, but rumors are the show is shooting for a near date. All contracts have been signed and even a few former favorites will be returning. We talked to a few of these men and this is what they has to say: “Should be a good experience. I expect it to be something worthwhile, but nowhere near the greatness of the shows Front Office put out.” Said PCW Cruiserweight Champion and former PURE guest Tim Walden as he cockily fled the mob. “Something for everyone to see, that’s all I have to say about this first show.” Said the former PURE athlete and now PCW breakout star Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid. “It was a shame to leave so early last time, but I’m confident this PURE era will last. Now if there is anybody skilled enough to compete against an athlete of my caliber, I’m not sure.” Said former PURE title contender and break through WT Free Agent Vilas Lavagna. Rounding up the crew was the former PURE Junior Heavyweight Champion saying: “Tis this should be a show none of us will forget.” As Romantico puffed some smoke from his cigar in the face of our cameraman.” The stars will be there, the legends will be there, the world will be there, but make sure YOU will be there. And if not you can see all the action live on the main station for all your PURE needs, UPN.
