NYGMA! Say it, love it, live it.

28 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
NYGMA! Say it, love it, live it. It started with a beat *A heart-thumping beat starts to play in the background* And it moved on with a scratch. *A hand is shown in a dark room scratching a record* That scratch became music. That music became life. That life attracted people. People of all ages, some I loved, some I hated. They were my family. The family I called Tokyo Pop. The music went to the head. The love flourished, people came, people went, but during those five hours where I controlled the floor it was heaven on earth. My life was simple. The music would flow, the paycheck would roll, and then everybody would love me in the meantime. I was a God! They screamed my name NYGMA! NYGMA! *Echoes surround the dark room* What could have gone wrong? Well nothing went wrong. I changed. Change happens, but my change wasnt anticipated. I need something to keep me busy, to help me live, to do more for me then please my ears. It was love at first site the day I saw that sign. I swear I could have made love to it right there. I was just walking and it was standing there calling my name. It said in big bold black letter PURE. *More echoes and thumping* What did it mean? I didnt care what it meant. The name, so beautiful, so delicate, so simple, so PURE! I noticed it was wrestling, wrestlers were loves, more loved then DJs, and they were legends. I WANTED TO BE A LEGEND! *The full scene is shown as a dark one-room apartment with no furniture accepts one chair is present* NYGMA! It echoed I loved the sound of the name. NYGMA! Why dont you love me I am the best at the game. NYGMA! I told you, Im not just the same. NYGMA! Its me, the one, the only, DJ Enygma. Now whats left to be done? Prove myself. Give me the chance? Im going through hell. I could be the best, I could be the one, now let me try my goddamn stuff. Im rough, Im tough, Im the meanest around, Tokyo Pop desires my sound. Im a shocka, I leave chills running down peoples spines, people listen so carefully to every one of my lines. SO TOKYO POP, PURE, GIVE ME THE CHANCE? ILL PROVE IM THE BEST WITHOUT A SECOND GLANCE! *The heartbeat starts to quicken up and get louder* My life is simple, it wasnt the best, I went through hell, got to get this off my chest. All I know is the mic and the mic knows me, the day we combined its like my ears could see. Im not crazy I tell you, I just loved it so much, loved it enough to give it up, FOR THIS, NO SHIT! Ill give you respect put me to the test I AM THE BEST! NYGMA! It echoed I loved the sound of the name. NYGMA! Why dont you love me I am the best at the game. NYGMA! I told you, Im not just the same. NYGMA! Its me, the one, the only, DJ Enygma. This isnt a song, this isnt a rap, call it what you want I dont give a crap. I GOT MOVES! Believe me, you got to say yes. I got GROOVE! Im telling you I wont make a mess. Ive seen the best dancers, seen the best babes, heard the best rhymes, played the best games. Its me! The one! The only! The son! NYGMA! NYGMA! NYGMA! *Takes a deep breath and looks around at the empty room* I hope you hear me.
