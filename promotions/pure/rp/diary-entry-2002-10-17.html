Diary Entry Written: 17 Oct 2002 Thursday 17th October 2002 What an eventful few weeks. Hired and fired twice by the same company. And now I find myself back there again. What's the difference this time? I've already been treated like crap twice. What's to say they won't do it again?
Well...nothing. But that's a chance I'll have to take. After Red October, Mr Wilson seems to have taken on a new persona. He seems more...considerate. Whether it's the Japanese morals finally instilling themselves on him, I don't know. But, this time, he seems legitimate.
He's finally realised that he has to cater for all tastes in this market. Not only the entertainment sides, but the traditionalists, and the grunge fans.
I think that sees the future. He's combining all elements into one. And I'm in the middle. Ikusa 5. I have to appear twice. A 'Gentleman's' match. Representing Amber Token against Vito Wynn and Orlando Scott...who in turn, I believe, represent Jaden and Ashley Maximoff respectively.
That's puzzling me. Why did Amber pick me? My PURE career so far is hardly a sight for sore eyes. Two matches, two defeats, two releases from my contract. Granted, I know have to work my backside off to prove that Mr Wilson's faith in me is not ill-fated...but out of the entire locker room, she picks me?
I need a word with her on that one. I hope she speaks good English. My Spanish is a little rusty. Better yet if she speaks Japanese, but it's a little too much to hope for.
However, that's just one of my worries. Rumours of a Deathmatch tournament have been strife, and, after seeing the sign ups, have added my name. The bad thing is...I think it starts on this Ikusa. I've heard people discuss a battle royal. 20 men start, 10 men progress.
Why did I sign up? Oh well...I'm late for my training. I hope to God they've finished the repair work in the changing rooms...the site of those builders trying to show off their 'muscles' is pathetic.
Oh...additional thoughts. Must get some milk...I need to write to Alice. Got to let her know the good news.
For now, peace.
