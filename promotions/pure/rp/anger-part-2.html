Anger part 2 I woke up dripping with swet; the bed dripping yet all I remember from my dream is those red eyes the eyes haunting me, following me, trying to get inside trying to control my thoughts.
Suddenly a nurse walks through the door carrying my food, bacon, and orange juice, some bread and other little things but the best thing I on the tray was the chocolate moose that was the only thing I thought of with out thinking hospital food. Its because when I was feeling like I had earn a treat I would have one chocolate moose.
The only thing I like about today is that I am allowed to go to my hotel not a 5 star hotel because only people with lots of money to spend go there.
I dont have lots of money I have a certain amount to help me live through the year. Thats why I am in A 3 star hotel middle one so people dont think
I am a rich bitch but they think I am an average person like them. Well if you look at me you think an average black man nothing special well then they look slightly closer and they see the physic they see the wrestlers inside of me they normally say wow hes a good fighter or they just think why do people take steroids well sorry to disappoint them but I am %100 real muscle.
I got up and got dresses ready for the greatest moment of the week leaving this hell hole oh I mean great hospital I signed the slip that allows me to leave allows me to go to my hotel buy my own food and lets me go where I want when I want and no one is there watching me no one checking if I am ok.
I like acting by myself I dont care what people think of me because they know they dint have a chance against me. Most people dont really like me and that is mainly because they are jealous they are scared because they know a white guy like me can get anyone I like.
I am just dieing to get my revenge on the guy that put me here the guy that has well is trying to ruin my mind the guy who wants to take me out, so I dont get in his way so I am going to start a fight with him if I have to I will start 1000 until I win he might put me in hospital, might brake my bones but it will be worth it when I beat him all the pain and suffering I have gone through will be over and I will be free to start fight with other people I wont have any grudges against anyone but they will have a lot of grudges on me but life is life. You make friends you make enemies. Yet bold white men looking as cool as me seem to make more enemies then friends for one reason and that is because we can kick your ass, steal your girls, and plane and simple we dress a lot better then you!
I am on the streets yet I feel no different I feel the same as I did in there. I still feel locked up. Now I remember I remember what I have to do, what will make me feel free beat the devils friend beat him bad make him bleed make him scream. I am going there now I am going to end it all I am going to show everyone what a man I am what a true fighter I am. I am nearly there nerves kicking in, sweet dripping wanting to make a good name for myself trying to get people fearing me again. I just hope that I can take him I hope for that one thing just one thing I need to do just so I can live a happy life with a win over my belt. That is all I wish for this is all I want just to kick one guys ass. The one guy that has proven me not to be the best not to be the strongest guy in the pub, he has made me like another no body another old person just sitting and drinking telling there old war stories or telling about there old best fights forgetting details forgetting most of the story. Well I am here lets get the guts up and see if hes in there
