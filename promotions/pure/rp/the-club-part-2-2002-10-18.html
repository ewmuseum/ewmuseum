The Club (part 2) Written: 18 oct 02 (ikusa 5) Flashes of purple, green, red, yellow and blue invade the wooden dance floor, the bar area is a buzz with people shouting their orders at the bar tender who seems to be frustrated, due to the fact that he is working his shift alone. Ric and Johnny stroll into the club with widely spread grins slapped on their young fresh faces.
Johnny: Dude, this is the shit! Ric: To be quite honest… I agree The two continue through the club and make their way up to the bar area, Ric calls the sort stocky Japanese bar tender over to order some drinks for the two.
Ric: Hey man, I’ll have to Vodka’s and cokes. Bartender: You two are big stars from America the ones on TV!!! WOW!!! Ric: Are we? Ric nudges Johnny as the two slightly blush, due to the acknowledgment from the middle-aged greying bartender
Johnny: Play along this guys obviously some jackass, but if the ladies see him making a fuss over us then they might think we’re big stars too!
Bartender: Wait let me get camera. I take picture for family Johnny: Well if you want The two look at each other baffled by the bartender’s foolish antics, as he rushes to the far side of the bar and produces a small digital camera
Bartender: Now you two stand there, I take picture. FLASH! Johnny and Ric wipe their eyes and collect their drinks Bartender: Your drinks free now go enjoy the club The two walk of with their alcoholic beverages and sit in the corner; they slowly sip their drinks and observe the surrounding environment. Suddenly Johnny notices a fine looking woman make her way to the bar and like stink on shit he instantly follows
Ric: Damn there he goes again. The woman adorned in skin-tight leather pink dress smoothly waltzes over and plumps her fine booty on the brown material covered bar stools. Ric pursues her every step and pulls up another bar stool next to the lady and leisurely sits down
Johnny: How you doing? The lady looks at Johnny smirks and looks away Johnny: Oh I see, treat mean keep em keen aye? The lady slows turns her head and looks Johnny straight in the eye and with a low sexual voice replies
Woman: Do you want something? If not leave and stop drooling on my dress Johnny: Do I want something? Baby I want YOU! Woman: Jackass Johnny: Don’t think I didn’t see you watching me over; there our physical attraction is to great for we must not be kept apart and the only way to fulfil our burning passion is for you to join me in my hotel room
Woman: In your dreams, damn where’s all the decent guys in this place? Johnny: In my dreams? You probably will be, but how bout we forget the dreaming part and just get onto the dealing with session?
Suddenly a large hand comes down on Johnny’s shoulder that spins him around quickly Johnny: Do ya mind I am talking to this real hot lady and your ruining the mood Johnny is looking directly into the mans chest. He slowly raises his head to look at the gargantuan bouncer that had previously denied him and his comrade access to the club
Johnny: Oh hi! How can I help you friend, I mean s-s-sir Bouncer: Get away from Boss Lady and take you friend with you understand? Johnny: She’s the owner! Damn beautiful and intelligent I think I’m in love Bouncer: Leave now! Johnny: Ok man chill, I bid ye adieu my fare lady… Johnny looks around the club and notices Ric trying his luck with a few women on the dance floor. Ric looks up and notices Johnny, whom is signally to the exit. Ric and Johnny make their way to the exit and are once again are on the busy streets of Tokyo
Ric: Man why did you do that for I was about this close from getting laid! Johnny: Calm it, will be back don’t worry but we got more important things to worry about like whooping all the others teams in Japan so lets go and get some rest
Ric: True my friend, so very true The two walk of down the street laughing and joking excited at the chance of making a name for themselves in the wonderful world of sports entertainment.
