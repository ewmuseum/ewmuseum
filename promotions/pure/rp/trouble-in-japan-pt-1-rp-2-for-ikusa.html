Trouble in Japan Pt 1 Rp#2 for Ikusa

January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Trouble in Japan Pt 1 Rp#2 for Ikusa Written: Ryu and Otoshi walk down the streets talking amongst themselves about the upcoming match against the black dragons, their arms covered in bruises from the previous Ikusa, their muscles sore. They make their way through the busy streets of Ikusa to a little Restaurant, sit down at a table, and wait to be served
A few minutes after sitting down a young Japanese women aged about 19 or 20 years old walks up to Ryu and Otoshi smiling “hello, how may I help you?” she says in a thick Japanese Accent. Ryu and Otoshi stop speaking to themselves and look up at the beautiful lady, Otoshi’s jaw drops in amazement as his stares at the young girl. She looks at him confused and blushes.
“You must excuse my friend, he isn’t feeling the same today” Ryu says embarrassed at his friends actions “its okay, would you like anything to eat?” she says still blushing at Otoshi who keeps staring “yeah, We’ll have two plates of Nikujaga please” the waitress nods and walks away smiling at Otoshi
“What the hell is your problem Otoshi!”? Ryu yells at him, Otoshi looks at him finally out of his trance and looks at him in confusion “what do you mean?” Ryu becomes upset and shakes his head “with you and that waitress?” Otoshi blushes and looks down embarrassed, he smiles and looks up at her, she glances over her shoulder and catches him looking at her again, he sees her and lowers his head immediately “I don’t know what you mean Ryu” she smiles and goes back to doing what she was doing
“Listen Otoshi, this isn’t the time to be falling head over heels over some girl, we need to be 100% focused on our match!” Ryu says sternly trying to get Otoshi back on track, Otoshi still seems to be concerned mainly with the girl he has now got a crush on, he looks at Ryu “I’m not falling in love with this girl” he smiles cheekily, “oh please, its obvious you are” Ryu says, “okay I am” admits a love-stricken Otoshi
“So why don’t you just ask her out? She seems interested” Ryu asks quietly to Otoshi. Otoshi stares at Ryu in confusion “Ryu! She’s not interested” “you don’t know till you ask” he says slyly, they sit there in silence, Otoshi reflects on Ryu statements and wonders if the cute Waitress is actually interested in him, he smiles as he thinks leaving ryu to only assume what is going through his mind
The Sit there in silence for a few minutes, until the cute waitress is seen by Otoshi who perks his head up at the sight of her, is walking to their table with their order of Nikujaga. She smiles again at Otoshi who looks away in embarrassment, she places the plates down on each side and looks at Otoshi ones again, Ryu bumps Otoshi and coughs on purpose trying to get him to ask the young girl out. Otoshi finally gets the hint and clears his throat “um, I’m sorry and please excuse me, but I was just wondering would you like to go out with me” he says nervous, she looks embarrassed as she walks away without answering.
Otoshi looks in disbelief and the girl walks away without answering his question, he lowers his head and shakes it “I told you Ryu” he says angrily at his tag partner, Ryu lifts his fork and starts eating the Nikujaga. Otoshi looks at the food and pushes it away from him “”I’m not hungry anymore” Ryu stops eating and drops his fork. “Otoshi forget it, you tried okay?” he says picking his fork back up and continues eating. Otoshi raises his head and sees two angry buffed up men walking furiously towards them “oh crap, Ryu I think were in trouble” he says “for what?” Ryu asks with food in his mouth “I don’t know but they look angry” he says starting to get up
The Two Men make their way to Ryu and Otoshi both fuming with anger, “did you ask my 15 yr old daughter out?” he says angrily, “who?” Otoshi says as the cute waitress appears from the back with tears rolling down her eyes “her!” the man points towards the cute waitress “she’s only fifteen!” Otoshi screams out.
“Yes she is, and she’s my daughter you sick freak! “ he yells, yanking up Otoshi by the collar and swaying him around “I didn’t know! I thought she was 19” he tries to tell the man, “Get out of my restaurant you sick freak! In addition, don’t return unless you want to be skinned alive! You hear me!” he says as he drops Otoshi to the ground who scatters out the door running for his life, Ryu runs off not far behind as the man walks back to the kitchen shaking his head
