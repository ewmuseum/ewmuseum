This it? Is this it? That was usually the first thing I said when I arrived at a wrestling promotion in a career that lasted less than eight months. Every time I join a new promotion, I am always disappointedcould be little things like, no toilet paper in the toiletsor it could be a huge thing like bad treatment of wrestlers by the front office...maybe I expected to much Many sports give off the impression of a fast, frantic, wealthy, top of the range lifestyle. Thats the impression exerted by soccerthats the impression exerted by wrestlingan impression that I stupidly believed in. Roy Keane walked out of the Republic of Ireland training camp for the FIFA Soccer World Cup because the field was like a parking lot. Ive done the same with wrestling promotionsnot for the same reason obviously but for the wrestling equivalent I suppose. But its different now that Im in PURE; everything here is what I expected it to be. Even though they are considered a feeder fed , they project an image of excellence. In my opinion, that image projects precisely what this place is about. The day after I signed my contract I made my way to the PURE headquarters. I knew this was a great place from the start, when I was greeted by two fresh, friendly faces of the receptionists, and their manner in dealing with me was just as nice. Lea and Pai I think their names were. They said the formalities and pointed me in the direction I had wished for. On my way I went, trotting cautiously down a corridorI didnt want to make an arse of myself by banging into things left and right, did I? Okaymaybe I was a bit to cautious, but hey, Im a nervous fellow. Anyway, I finally got to the gym, after about ten minutes of wrong turns, asking for directions and getting funny looks. Damn, it had the all the latest and greatest equipmentthey even had some of Mr.Wins productsalthough, they were in a head in the corner, cobwebs being knitted around them. There was only one person working out at 10 a.m. on this mornI thought it best not to interrupt his work out, I know I dont like being interrupted, although it happens very seldom. I walked around the gym, scanning the gear with my eyes, just trying to make my self-comfortable. One thing I noticed about the gym, which was unusual, is that there is absolutely no smell of sweat! For a minute I was puzzled at how this could beit was a gym used everyday by top athletes. Alas, questions were soon answered as I noticed the fans on the roof twirling, two exit doors open, resisting the wind and a few windows open for good measure. As I made my way around the room, I felt I was being watched. I looked around the room and I seen the man working out eyeballing memaybe he was just glancing at this newcomer, Im sure it was all innocent. After feeling satisfiedextremely satisfied with the gym, I decided to extend my adventure. I walked out of the room, my head moving up, down, side to sidejust making sure I seen everything, dont know why I did thisjust a habit I guess. Butone thing I did miss was the security guard I bumped into. He wasnt of Asian extraction, looked American. Sorry ladwasnt concentrating I said, trying to get passed him. He held his arm out, I could easily have pushed past, but that would be impolite. Before ya go anywhere, show me some I.D. CRAP! How dumb had I beenI havent received my damn PURE I.D. card yet. I had gotten past reception because the receptionists were here yesterday when I was finalising the contract. How will I get past this obstacle? SureI could push past and just ignore him, but I was pretty positive he had a weapon of some description and there were other security members around this place. SoI took out my drivers license, hopefully this dude will accept ithe didnt. A lot of people have a drivers license, fellawhat I need is a PURE I.D. card! I explained to him to the best of my ability what my dilemma was and who I washe didnt buy it, I tell the truth and no one believes meisnt that a kick in the sack? Just go to reception, theyll tell ya who I am. He chuckledmore of a mockery chuckle than a ha ha chuckle. How bout this. You get your leprechaun ass out of this building, hop on the next unicorn you see and make your way back to the frickin Blarney stonewhile your waiting for the unicorn, theres a pub down the street, I hear they have Guinness. I was fuming. If this were a cartoon, steam would be shooting out of my ears. One more word outta him and Id kill him. I bottled it though. I turned my back to him and headed towards reception, where I could exit the building. But thenthat blood pillock crossed the bloody line. As I was making my way up a corridor I could hear his voice Good boy, go eat your lucky charms, you I.R.A. loving asshole. THAT WAS IT! I WAS DONE! I couldnt take it anymore. I could handle the leprechaun crap. I could handle all that stuffbut he should have never insinuated I was in the fecking I.R.A. He should have never EVER said anything about Lucky freakin Charms. I turned around. Walked towards him, boots clogging against the floor. I grabbed him by the collar and forced him up against the wall. Im tired of this stereotypical shite With that I started laying in the punches. Bang, bang, bangBOOM! Hes out. HERE COME THE DRONES! I could hear the rush of feet coming my way, I knew it wasnt good news so I ran as fast as I could, up the corridor, left, left, right, corridor, reception, parking lotI made itnearly. I strutted to my car, pushing my hair back with my hand. I had just experienced what I had quit wrestling for at such a young age. I had experienced what forced me out of the business at 22.
