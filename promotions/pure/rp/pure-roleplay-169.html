PURE roleplay 169

October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Written: [Self-conscious. That’s what a lot of people are in the world nowadays. They don’t bother to care for other people’s lives, feelings, or emotions. Only their own. Greed. Another thing; another disease; that overwhelms any person, no matter how righteous you think you are.]
[Amber Token is no different from most people. She wants to succeed in life, and if thinking beyond other people will help her there, then so be it. The only thing different between most people, and Amber Token is, most people aren’t put up to it. Most people didn’t have a knife to their back, being forced to fight, being forced to maul another human being, just for the sake of most people’s leisure, and entertainment. She was. Determination drives her, and a drive to succeed fuels her. What some people won’t do to be free…]
The air was cool, as the clouds weaved in and out one another in the night sky. The stars and the moon illuminated the freshly mowed front lawn, as Amber Token is sat comfortably on a porch swing, a thick, black cotton sweated adorns her upper body, as a pair of long, gray sweat pants conceal her lower body. She stares vacuously into the night sky as blank, empty thoughts swam throughout her head.
“Our heads are so filled with thought…” Amber began, still staring into the black beyond, “we can’t use our imagination…like a sky so filled with stars, you can’t find a constellation...”
Amber picks up a low, almost inaudible squeak, as she turns to her left. The front door of her house slowly, and gently moves outward, the usual grayish colored door, now almost black, is pushed outward even more, until the door is completely ajar. A figure becomes more visible, inch-by-inch. The figure, with their back turned to the door, backs out, until both feet are firmly planted on the porch. What can be made out of the figure is that it is male in gender, with shoulder length brown hair, that is almost opaque in the darkness. In his shaky hands, are two full cups of coffee, he cautiously maneuvers himself to the porch swing, careful not the spill any of the contents. Amber extends her arm, as the man stops in his place, letting her take the small mug.
Amber motions for the man to sit down beside her, patting the soft, pillowed swing. The man strolls past Amber’s folded legs, and seats himself next to the Brazilian beauty known as Amber Token, careful not to spill the hot coffee on her, or himself.
“Thank you, Marcello,” she softly said, not moving her eyes off of the sky. The man, Marcello, gets comfortable on the swing, leaning back and stretching his legs, extending them completely. Marcello was decked out in a bright, white robe, with his name emblazoned across the right breast of the robe. His long, wild brown hair is drenched; fresh out of the shower, as little pieces of paper, stained with blood can identify that he just finished a shower and shave. His feet are adorned by a pair of fuzzy, white slippers, with “’Cello” embossed onto the top of the foot.
Marcello looks up, and gazes into the big, bright brown eyes of Amber, who is still completely focused on the sky, seeing a blinking light of an aircraft in between the myriad of stars. Marcello clears his throat quickly as the inconsistent chirps of the grasshoppers’ block out the low noise.
“Amber…what have you been doing out here, all night?” he inquisitively asked. No response. Amber was still glued to the stars, seeming like she was counting them. The only sound that is heard, are the unrelenting chirps of the creatures of the night. Marcello gently nudged her to the side, snapping her out of her trance.
“What? What is it?” she replied quickly as Marcello, satisfied that he has the attention of his female friend.
“Just wondering what you were doing out here…” he said, not breaking his rhythm and lingo. She slowly put her index finger in the air, and started to make a half hearted reply, but then the thought dwindled.
“I…. I…don’t know. I suppose I was bored, I mean, with you in the shower for so long,” she said, as Marcello blushed a faint red color. Amber smiled, and nudged him back, reclaiming her space, leaning back on the swing. “So I came out here, and was just caught off guard by these stars.”
“How so?” he asked in a curious tone of voice. Amber tried to find the words, as she took a quick glimpse at the sky once more. “Well?” Marcello pressed on, ever the persistent one.
“Well...because they are amazing. And they look so small in the big sky, even though they are huge!” she shot back at Marcello. Marcello looked to the sky, as did Amber. Silence. Not sound was in existence, almost as if the whole world froze in time. The noises from the undetected creatures died down as well. Marcello broke the lapse in time.
“Well, what was the poetry about? Constellations…imagination, what was it again?” he asked. Amber again, was staring at the sky. Marcello sighed, as he gently nudged Amber again, this time a little harder, but not so that is inflicted any pain. Amber turned to meet him face to face quickly.
“What?” she again snapped back. Marcello jokingly backs off several inches, letting Amber quickly get back into a relaxed manner. “What did you want, again?” she asked.
“Do you have a short attention span or something?” he suggested. Marcello lets out a sly smile as Amber grins. “But really, what was all that poem mumbo-jumbo about?” he asked again. Amber thought back to a several minutes ago. Amber takes a sip of her now warm coffee from her china mug.
“Oh…nothing, the night sky sort of inspires me, like it did my mother,” she softly replied. Marcello’s calmed, smiley face died down to one of a serious tone. “But I suppose that’s why it cheers me up…a memory of her,” she replied, trying to get Marcello’s spirits back up, and not feeling bad for her.
“Hey Amber! To liven things up a bit, I got some good news for you!” he enthusiastically shouted, causing unseen animals scurrying. He quickly hushes himself as he takes a quick sip of his coffee.
“What is it?” she politely asks, keeping her voice low, setting an example of Marcello to follow. “C’mon! Tell me! Tell me!” she pleaded, as Marcello smiles, teasing her with the information he acquires.
“Well…” he started off in an almost teasing tone of voice; “…you got a letter from Puroresu Ultimate Ring Entertainment, also known as PURE…you’ve been accepted, and have been booked on the Ikusa card!” A look of shock crossed Amber’s sweet, olive skin toned face.
“They sure don’t waste any time, do they?” she proclaimed, putting a little more aggression behind her voice. “Jesus…so, who’s my opponent?” she quickly asked, as if ready to fight at the said moment.
“Well…she’s a new face on the scene, like you…umm…Ashley ‘Ash’ Maximoff…and it looks to be a great battle with the martial arts!” he proclaimed, with a wide smile creasing his lips.
“What do you mean?” Amber asked, obviously enthralled and excited about the good news. Marcello takes another sip of his coffee before replying.
“She is trained in the martial arts, like you, girl! Kicks, punches, take downs, that type of fighting. No chairs, tables, or anything,” he said, ensuring Amber what she was getting herself into, and ensuring her safety.
“That’s great news…a pure fighter, like myself, in some ways anyway,” she mumbled slowly, and barely audible. From the depths of the house, the telephone goes off, ringing throughout the house. “Could you get that, ‘Cello?” she asked sweetly, accompanied by a smile and a wink. Marcello leaned over and placed his coffee mug on the concrete porch, making sure it doesn’t tip over, before pushing himself off of the deep seat of the swing. Marcello straightens his robe, before dashing into the house, opening the door, and bringing it back in, letting it go as it slowly tries to close, failing. The door remains ajar as Amber again, directs her attention to the sky.
With her attention fully on the night sky, she begins to talk aloud, possibly to herself, and possibly to the world. “Everyone’s so sensitive…to any bad vibration…” she’s cut off as a shooting star darts across the night sky. She follows it with her eyes, as it descends into the horizon. “We’re so impressing, while we’re regressing…” Amber stops again, taking her coffee cup, and bringing it to her natural colored lips, a light pink with a olive colored touch to it, and sips the now dying coffee.
“There’s nothing I believe in more, than my own insignificance. So why does everybody think, my words can make a difference…?” she stops again, as she leans over, catching a last glimpse of the shooting star, as it hides behind the thick forest of trees, disappearing from sight completely, well, to Amber’s sight anyway. “I just don’t have time to think up every social consequence…I’ll just keep on talking, and you’ll keep applauding…” she’s cut off yet again, as the door swings open, Marcello barging out with a yellow camera in hand.
“Who was it?” she asked. Marcello shrugs it off as if it were no big deal, while he looks at Amber, trying to get her attention.
“No one...telemarketer. So, the newest member of PURE…do you have anything to say to your opponent?” he asks in a friendly tone, waiting a response. Amber sits and thinks for a moment.
“Prepare yourself…” the only words she can mutter out, as the flash of the camera snaps, causing Amber to hold her eyes for a moment before standing up next to Marcello. “I’m getting a little tired, ‘Cello…I think I’ll try to go to sleep…although it will be hard with all the excitement.” Marcello nods in agreement.
“Yeah,” he started off as he looked to the sky, “it has been all pretty exciting, eh?” Amber sits up from the deep seat of the swing, and pulls herself up.
“Yeah…a good day, full of excitement, too much for me.” Amber walks past Marcello, giving him a quick kiss on the cheek. Amber solemnly walks into the house, gently pulling the door outward, as she looks at Marcello, who looks back, holding his cheek, unlmoving. Amber smiles, and walks into the house slowly, as Marcello finally takes notice of the beautiful night sky. Marcello leans over, putting his elbow on the wooden railing, as he uses it to prop up his lightly bearded chin.
“Hmmm…it is a beautiful night, isn’t it?” His words trail off into the desolate night sky, as he turns back and slowly walked into the house, shutting, and locking the door securing the house. He walks several yards forward, before coming to rest at their living room, darkened, the only light illuminating; the sky. It ‘twas a beautiful sky, after all.
[Greed…self-consciousness. Not with Amber, not completely anyway. Meditation, and complete though awaits her, as she comes to rest for the night.]
