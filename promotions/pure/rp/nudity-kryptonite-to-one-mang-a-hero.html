Nudity...Kryptonite To One Mang-A-Hero

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Nudity...Kryptonite To One Mang-A-Hero -/Our story begins a little unusually in this case...for usually it begins with the sun rising high into the powder blue skies of Japan...but in this case, it begins with the sun resting itself as it floats its way down behind the huge overlaying mountains out of view as the moon begins its shift. With the rays of the moon cast down over the rolling grassy fields of Sado Island, Japan, we now focus on the traditional training dojo of one Inphino Blitz. He sits in deep meditation on the porch of his dojo, the wind soft winds caressing his naked chest as he sits Indian style with just his black karate gi bottoms on. A paper dragon imprint Japanese lantern hangs above him, giving of a dim light as he continues to sit in deep thought as usual...the world and all his thoughts running through his head at this point. Surrounded in peace and seduced by silence, there seems to be nothing that could break the Black Dragon Master from his mediative state...but in a house with a whacked out superhero, a civil rights activist, and a beauteous pron star...something is bound to happen......-/ -/And just as expected, it does as we now come to find none other then our DYNAMIC DRAGONBALL HERO, MANG-A-HERO, rushing out frantically onto the porch and running about Inphino in a circle like a little child while whining and yelling. Inphino opens one eye, the obvious amount of annoyance apparent in his eye as he sees Mang-A-Hero running about in utter idiocy in his Gatchaman replica costume complete with wings. Inphino takes a deep breath in and tries to be as calm about this as possible, standing up and stopping our hero smoothly with his hand and asking.-/ Inphino Blitz:'What seems to be the problem Mang-A-San...' Mang-A-Hero: *huff puff weeze* 'OH GOD INPHINO....IT WAS SOOOOOO TERRIBLE. I CAN'T BELIEVE THAT HAPPENED...WHY DID MY RAIJIN GAIJIN SAMURAI SENSES FAIL ME IN THIS INSTANCE?!' Inphino Blitz:'What do you speak of friend?' Mang-A-Hero:'Well....I simply returning to the bathroom to recapture my Mini-Disc player that I left in the bathroom because I was listening to 'Nothing's Gonna Stand In Our Way' by Stan Bush...which describes our purpose in the PURE Tag Team Title tourney migth I add...but that's besides the point, we can talk about KARATIMACIZING Team Okiki later. Anyways, I opened the door to just go in and get it and there was...YUKA...and...and...and....SHE WAS NUDIFIED!!! She was as uncovered as the blade of a battling samurai and my eyes partook of her body for mere seconds!! OH THE DIRTINESS!! OH THE FILTH!!' *whines and wipes his eyes continuously* -/Inphino sighs, shaking his head which is now starting to become commonplace when he's around our super-samurai as he places a hand on his spandex enwrapped shoulder, Inphino trying his best to calm him down about the situation he has just experienced as he talks him down, Blitz showing why he's the 'brains' of the team-/ Inphino Blitz:'Mang-A, you MUST calm down about this...it was just flesh. I'm sure she understands it was just an accident and it's not like you meant to barge in and look at her endowments. Please, be soft about this and just face up to what happened...I'm sure it's not a big deal. Come on, who's goi...' Out The Way Voice:'YOU SAW YUKA'S BREASTESSESS?! YOU LUCKY SON OF A BI**H!!' -/Blitz and Mang-A-Hero look back into the arched entryway of the dojo as Jordan X Shabazz, the proud African American civil rights activist and ally of the Dragons Duece. He pulls off his blue FUBU skully cap with excitement as he rushes out to meet his male roomies and he exclaims yet again with excitement, Jordan basically killing the 'calm down' speech just given by the analytical Inphino Blitz.-/ Jordan X:'DA** IT MAN!! YOU SAW THOSE TITTIES BEFORE ME, YOU SUCK GUY!! I've been running her bathroom on purpose for days now trying to get a peek! You go in ONCE and there she is, butt naked! Talk about some BS dawg.' Mang-A-Hero:'GREAT SHINZAKI HOT SAKI JORDAN!! You make it sound like seeing women nude is a pleasureable experience and is the goal of every living, breathing man.' -/Jordan and Inphino look at each other, both knowing deep inside the answer to that statement and agreeing with each other silently, both obviously knowing Mang-A-Hero's mind is still in it's puberty stages. Inphino just leans against the wall, brushing back a loose strand of hair in his face blown out of place by the wind and says-/ Inphino Blitz:'Look, Hero.....just go in there and talk to Yuka. It's better not to leave this lingering friend. We're all roomies and friends here, I'm sure she'll understand. How are we supposed to advance over Team Okiki with our combined ki if your unfocused due to this mental block presented by Yuka...' Jordan:'Or to be more specific woadie, the two BIG, ROUND blocks presented by Yuka.' -/Inphino glares at Jordan with those piercing deep eyes for his side comment as Jordan promptly shuts up, our samurai hanging his head in shame after Jordan's slight reminder of what he saw.-/ Inphino Blitz:'Seriously Mang-A...go in there and talk to her, okay?' Mang-A-Hero:'okay....OKAY....OKAY!! I shall talk to Miss Yuka and explain my actions and their accidental nature! I will not let even the sight of her 'Dragonballs' impede me from being focused on obtaining half of the PURE Tag Team championships with you Black Dragon Master-san-type-person. I shall approach that sultry little vixeness and explain myself face to face...I AM MANG-A-HERO, the SAMURAI OF SILLY ANIME SUPER SAIEN ANTICS....WHY SHOULD I FEAR THIS?! AWAY I GO!' -/Our samurai puffs out his chest, his confidence exuberating and overwhelming as he strikes a silly Tekken style martial arts pose before dashing inside, Jordan and Inphino looking to each other and looking to be seemingly unimpressed by the spiel of Mang-A-Hero as Jordan says-/ Jordan X:'Betcha he'll be back in five seconds...I'll bet a dub on dat.' Blitz:'I'm usually not the betting type and besides, Mang-A has more integrity then that...but I'd be too stupid to pass this up, I bet 15 seconds.' -/They both shake hands to seal this deal, Jordan looking to his shiny platinum Rolex as he eagerly follows the second hand as it patrols about the numbers of his watch...five seconds pass and like clockwork, our hero rushes back, biting his nails and jumping about nervously as he looks like he's about to break into a cold sweat. Inphino curses under his breath as he dips into his pocket reluctantly and passes a smiling Jordan with an 'I told you so' look on his face as crisp twenty dollar bill. Jordan holds it up in the air and exclaims-/ Jordan X:'MOO-SHOO FOR ME TONIGHT!! None of that lame a** microwave mess.' -/Jordan happily pockets the money into his baggy FUBU jeans as Mang-A-Hero paces about with a look as if he could crack at any given moment. He finally breaks down, falling to the floor and gripping at the ankles of his two PURE comrades, groveling like a New York begger before them as he pleads-/ Mang-A-Hero:'PLEASE YOU GUYS...COME WITH ME!! I'm too scared to talk to a girl...ALONE.' *gulps* Jordan X:'Mang...buddy...you sure your not a little...you know...cum-see-cum-sa patna? Your starting to scare me.' -/Mang-A-Hero jumps up offensively, yelling loudly at Jordan for thecomment he just made by replying with conviction-/ Mang-A-Hero:'BY NO MEANS AM I THIS 'CUM-SEE-CUM-SA' RACE YOU SPEAK OF...I'M SUPER SAIEN DARNED IT AND YOUR GONNA RESPECT IT MY BROWN SKINNED ALLY!!! Now come on, please come with me you guys. Even my Galvatron Guardian Powers can't uphold against the powers of that girl.' Inphino Blitz:'If our joining along your side will help you face this problem head on, then you shall have our assistance. Now come, let's get this over with.' Mang-A-Hero:'YES MY COMPATRIOTS, LET US GO FACE THAT NUDE-ILICIOUS VIXEN IMMEDIATELY!! ONWARD!!' Jordan X:'God...if you'll be kind...pllllleas let her be naked when we get there.' -/Inphino sighs at both of his friends, wondering at times how he got involved with them both as he heads inside his dojo, Jordan and Mang-A following behind as they go to face their female porn star roommate about the 'skin show' our samurai accidently walked in on. -/)Shortly...(\- -/Mang-A-Hero, Jordan, and Inphino are upstairs and traversing the solid oak upstairs hallway of Inphino's dojo, littered with traditinal Oriental paintings and weapons as they come to the door of Yuka Hamada's room, a slight crack visible in her doorway as Mang-A-Hero takes the doorknob in hand, taking a deep breath in as he loosk towards his two friends. His eyes filled with a cocktail of wide-eyed innocence and nervousness, he asks.-/ Mang-A-Hero:'Are you sure I should be trying to approach her now? I mean...she might be making herself up, or watching an episode of Sailor Moon for all I know.' Inphino Blitz:'There are no closed doors within my dojo...just announce your prescence and enter, obviously the sight you saw is effecting your thinking and it must be confronted immediately.' -/Mang-A-Hero nods, taking in a breath as he looks towards the wooden door that stands between him and facing his problem. Throwing his caution to the wind, he exclaims-/ Mang-A-Hero:'YUKA!! IT TIS IMPERATIVE THAT I SPEAK TO YOU MY PORNOGRAPHIC, whatever that means, PARTNER OF ROOM SHARING!!' -/Mang-A-Hero opens the door, taking a few steps in along with Blitz and Jordan following behind...but it comes as a complete shock to all three as they find the the lovely Yuka standing before them...yet again in all her nude glory. All three of their jaws drop in a simultaneous sequence as their eyes can't help but take in the sight of her naked body with the rays of the moon beading down upon it, excentuating just how lovely it is. She looks to the with wide-eyed innocence, pinning her hair up with a pair of Chinese chopsticks and seeming to be completely comfortable with her nudity as she asks-/ Yuka:'You guys needs something? Oh I'm sorry, do I need to put on make-up or something' -/The irony of this situation seems to have escaped Yuka, make-up being the first thing on her mind as oppossed to clothing as all three of her opposite gender roommates look to each other, Inphino and Mang-A-Hero falling backwards in cold shock, their mouths still wide open as they crash to the wooden floor with a collective thud. Jordan just stands amazed, looking up to the sky with a smile and saying-/ Jordan:'Thank you God...' *Looks back at Yuka* 'Well...the moon ain't the only thing that's rising...' -/Shortly, Jordan joins his three friends as he falls backward, fainting in ecstacy at her body as he hits the floor as well, all three men in a row with their mouths wide open. Yuka just stands their, curling a loose strand of her hair on her finger, and saying to herself as she heads back over to her large vanity, looking in the mirror.-/ Yuka:'That does it...I'm putting on more eye shadow before anybody comes in.' -/With that said, the scene slowly starts to fade...tomorrow soon to be a new day as we grow closer to Mang-A-Hero and Inphino Blitz battling Team Okiki for the right to advance in the PURE tag team title tournament...but as usual...it will always be an interesting few days before it gets there. WHAT NEW MISHAP WILL TAKE PLACE WITHIN THE DOJO OF BLITZ?! WHAT MORE CAN HAPPEN BETWEEN THE FOUR ROOMATES BEFORE THE NEXT SHOW?! All these questions will be answered and more...SO GO GO TO YOUR TV SETS TOKYO...AND STAY TUNED FOR THE NEXT ACTION PACKED MANG-A-SHOW!!
