No Awareness=No Pain Written: January 20th, 2002 No awareness equals no pain... Hahaha... HAHAHAHA... No awareness equals no pain... I believe there are more benefits from these little pills, then there are drawbacks. Think about it, I am so free and vulnerable when I'm doped up. When my mind is all there, I'm a danger to not only myself...but to everyone around me. That can only take me so far, however.
I feel you need someone in your life, well, not even someone...just something. Something that makes you wake up every morning for. I'm sure it comes as no surprise that every morning I wake up for...PAIN.
Whether it's inflicting it upon myself, or someone that I work with...I enjoy pain. And again, that is where these little 'miracle workers' come into play.
You hear that? S...i...l...e...n...c...e... That is what I hear all the time. And all I have to do to keep it that way, is to pop another capsule into my system.
Sometimes, at night, I just lay here and focus all of my attention and concentration on my medication. I swear if I lay here long and still enough, I can feel the pill contents clouding my blood. I can feel it eating away at the walls of my veins.
You want to know something? I like it. I enjoy the self-mutilation that I encorporate and associate with the pills. In fact, just this morning a stuck a steak knife into my fucking hand. I didn't feel it. I'm sure I will later, though.
Why did I do it? No awareness equals no pain...
