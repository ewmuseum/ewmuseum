I’m not going to be held down like all the other guys that work their asses of twice as harder than those heavy-weights.
Written: February 7, 2003 No respect. I come into the ring and I get no respect. I do what I like to do best and still, no respect. I just had a guy in a bar cutting down on how I perform in the ring. How high-flying is nothing but a “circus act.” But I don’t need that guy. He’s only one guy. I have a lot of fans. I don’t need him. But are there others out there that are beginning to hate me for how I perform in the ring?
Oh well, who cares. One day I will prove them all wrong when I rise higher than any high-flyer in this business. I will take on guys of any size, any reputation, and everything special about those guys that are at the top of this wrestling ladder.
My name will one day become a household name. I’m not going to be held down like all the other guys that work their asses of twice as harder than those heavy-weights. It’s not that I have anything against the heavy-weights in this business though, their great and entertaining. But I don’t care how good they are, they will never work harder than a guy like myself.
They don’t take the risks we take. Their chances of a career threatening injury are very slim. We take those chances, though, that they will never do in the ring. I believe that they work hard, and quite hard enough to be where they’re at now, but why aren’t we up there with them?
I’m not saying that I deserve to be farther than I am in my career than where I am at now, either. I’m just a rookie. I don’t deserve to be up there with the guys that have worked there asses off for a while. I will deserve it when it is my time. My time is not now, though.
At this moment in time, I have been standing outside of the bar, just thinking about what that guy had said. I stood there looking at the dark sky and the darkness within it. I felt depressed at the moment, which I wasn’t used to feeling. I felt sick to feel this way. I felt anger.
But why should I let this get to me? Actually, why not? That guy humiliated me. He cut down all of my pride and every other high-flyers pride.
But I shouldn’t worry. A lot of people have called me PURE’s high-flying sensation before. That’s what keeps me going. It makes me feel like one of a kind like I always wanted people to see me as.
That man may not know it, but everyone else knows it. Apparently my cousin Daniel did too.
“Chase, man, it’s alright,” Daniel said, trying to comfort me as he came out of the bar. “Some people are just ignorant,” I said, “I’m not going to dwell on it all though.” “Yeah, I know what you’re saying,” he said, “it’s better to just not listen to them.” Finally, that was the smartest thing I have heard out of Daniel’s mouth so far. He sounded more understanding than he had before.
“Well, since we’re not going to let this get to us, why don’t we go back into the bar and get some more drinks,” said Daniel.
“Sounds good,” I said. We walked toward the door of the bar and before we could open it, the man who had been making fun of me walked out.
“Oh, so you’re still here?” the man said, “I thought you had gone home and cried to your mommy.”
Usually I’m a very easy going guy. But this guy had really pissed me off. I had the urge to flip the bird to him, which I did as Daniel and I headed back inside. But the guy grabbed my hand and pulled me back.
“Oh, so now you think you’re funny?” he said and then swung his fist at me. I ducked as he tried to strike. He kept trying as I kept on maneuvering his punches. He wasn’t going to stop anytime soon, so I struck him right in the face with my fist which led him to fall to the ground. I had laid him out and he didn’t look like he was getting up for quite a few minutes after that blow.
I then decided to go back inside with Daniel. We went back in and sat down as the people had laughed at me earlier began to stare right through me. I didn’t care. I just sat there and drank was Daniel had treated me too.
I didn’t need them or anyone. I just needed my own amusement of being in the ring. I would have at the next Ikusa where I will be taking on Radiant for the Jr. Weight Title.
I didn’t care too much about the title. I didn’t care about the outcome of the match either. I just wanted to do what I did best, and that was to wrestle in my unorthodox way. I’m going to make an impact and the entire world is going to see it.
But as for the future, I want everyone to know, check the record books and look for Chase Herrera. One day, he’s going to do what no other high-flyer has ever done before…
