No controlling it...(Part 1)

5 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
No controlling it...(Part 1) Written: 10/5/02 The scene opens up on a sunny, hot day at around two o'clock or even three in a crowded neighborhood in Brooklyn, New York. Vito Wynn is seen purchasing a bottle of water in a little corner deli. Vito Wynn then grabs the water and gives the money to the cashier. He steps foot out of the store and begins walking down the street. He then makes a left turn into a alley.
'Well, today's a nice day. A nice day to go to the beach or for me to take a dip in my uncle's pool. But, I don't want to be lectured by him...' Vito Wynn says.
All of the sudden Vito Wynn stops where he is as a big muscular build man bumps right into Vito Wynn as jogging. Vito Wynn then falls to the floor from the impact and drops his water and spills it all over the floor, but the man continues to jog down the alley without apologizing for crashing into him.
'HEY! What the hell is your problem.' shouts Vito Wynn to the man. Vito Wynn then runs straight at him and charges into him. The man stops and hardly even felt a thing by the looks of it. The man turns around to face Vito Wynn and throws him against the wall to Vito Wynn's left. He then nails Vito Wynn to the wall by choking him.
'What the fuck do you think you are doing, huh?' shouts the man who is obviously have a temper tantrum.
'Look, you are so lucky that you are like what, sixteen years old?' asks the man. 'No, more like twenty-two years old.' Vito Wynn responds. 'Well, that still doesn't give me a reason to hit you because you still are a little piece of shit compared to me so fuck off!' the man screams.
The man lets Vito Wynn go as Vito Wynn falls to his knees and catches his breathe. The man now is walking down the alley way in exhaustion. Vito Wynn then stands up and looks down at the floor as he picks up a huge brick from the floor and runs at the man. Vito Wynn the cracks the brick over the man's head as the man falls to the floor in a bloody mess.
'You don't mess with Vito Wynn because if you're fucking with me, you're fucking with the best!' Vito Wynn mentions.
Vito Wynn then walks out of the alley way and continues to walk to the block when he suddenly stops and gets one little twitch in his left eye.
'Wait, can't I go to jail for this?' Vito Wynn asks himself. The scene then suddenly fades to black with the last thing in sight is Vito Wynn standing still in the middle of a crowded neighborhood.
