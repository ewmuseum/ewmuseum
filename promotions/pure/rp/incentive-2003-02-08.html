Incentive

8 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Incentive Written: 8th February 2003 Evening has set in, skies are cloudy and a brisk wind blows through the area. Jason Marah is standing outside the hotel he’s staying in, in Tosu, just a short distance from the Tosu Stadium, the home of Ikusa IV. Jason is wearing a pair of blue jeans and a fleece jumper with a pair of blue-lensed sunglasses. He tries rubs his hands together and looks at his watch, obviously waiting for someone. He looks left and then right, to the right, a blue sports car catches his eye. He keeps his eyes on the car as he it pulls up next to him, the rear seated door opens and Binsei Kinaka hops out with a folder placed under his arm. He walks hurriedly over to Marah, he’s obviously anxious and annoyed at his lateness.
Binsei Kinaka: Sorry for the lateness, Jason. Unavoidable really. Can we talk inside? Jason leads the way into the virtually empty hotel lobby, not even a staff member in sight. The two men take a seat on the three seater blue couch just inside and to the left of the entrance. Binsei lays the folder on the glass coffee table in front of him. He turns to Marah.
Binsei Kinaka: First off, I know about your little run in with Shee-Hong Ganata. He’s just a little man who has personal problems with me. He came after you to get to me.
Jason Marah: Why wouldn’t he just go after you instead of me? Binsei Kinaka: Because he’s an odd little man with little mental capacity, the capacity he does have is deranged. My apologies for the incident.
Jason Marah: Right, fair enough. Now, (he smiles) what did you want to give me? Binsei smiles and turns to the folder on the table. He opens it up to the first page, which shows a picture of a medium sized, red brick building with “Dynamite” written above the door in neon lettering. Binsei points at the building.
Binsei Kinaka: THIS is what I want to give you? Jason Marah: Wha? Binsei Kinaka: I recently acquired this club through a business transaction. Thing is, I have no need for it and don’t have the time to run it. Since you have done me various favours with PURE tickets and what have you, I thought hey, why not hand over the deeds to me good friend Jason?
Jason Marah: Holy feck, are you serious? You want to give me a nightclub? Binsei Kinaka: Indeed I do. Couple of catches though. First, the club is in Sacramento, California, USA.
Jason Marah: Sacramento, eh? Not a problem, I know a man who could run it for me. Binsei Kinaka: Excellent. Second catch…you have to win the Battle Royal tomorrow. Jason Marah: What? Why? What does me winning the Battle Royal have to do with it? Binsei Kinaka: You’ve got a lot of potential and you’re potential can be fulfilled if you win that match. I want the best for you, because you’ve been a good friend since we met. If you win, your career will more than likely skyrocket. I believe heart will get a man to go after something, but incentive will help him get it. You’ve got huge incentive going into this match…a shot at PURE’s World Heavyweight Title…but the thing is, every other man in that ring has the same incentive. I’m offering this nightclub as an extra incentive, that little extra push, edge if you will. I want you to win this title, for you.
Jason Marah: Fucking hell man, that’s putting a lot of stuff onto my mind ya know. Binsei Kinaka: Indeed it is, but the more you think about it, the more you’ll want it and the more you’ll feel the need that you HAVE to win this match.
Binsei hands Marah the folder and he starts flicking through the pages. Binsei Kinaka: All that, Jason, ALL OF IT! Just for winning a match. The money made from it could buy you a home, it could also leave you with the option of an early retirement. You could basically do what you want! And just to get over those start out expenses, I’ll throw in one million American dollars.
Jason takes a deep breath, tyring to take in this once in a lifetime opportunity as he gazes at what could be his if he just outlasts all his opponents at Ikusa and leaves the arena with a title shot…
