Live and Learn!pt1 Written: 21/10/02 Coming back home from a long but challenging debut match in Japan was tough. Orlando was recently in a Junior Title battle Royal and was eliminated near the end. A crucial loss but Orlando fed off it and used it to his advantage, thinks of it as experience. No one expects big in a debut, but it’s a good chance to get your name out. Orlando feels he still has not peaked the federation’s attention, but next event he plans to assassinate.
Immediately, when Orlando arrived back home, he went home and grabbed his bankcard. Knowing Rob would have inserted the money they stole from the same bank a few days before. And just as he had thought, the account balance showed that Orlando Scott has a half a million dollars to his name.
With a smile on his face, he rushes home. Sebastian is still at school, as they have not seen each other since before Orlando left for Japan. Orlando has a seat on the “so called” kitchen table, and looks through the New York Times classifies for a new home for his son and himself.
-{The Next Day}- Picture this, A beautiful gray bricked home, overlapping an acre of property. The green grass surrounding the home likes a golf course, and a price tag of 250 thousand dollars. This was just half of the money Orlando had, and the other half was spent on the yearly payment and new furniture.
Orlando had told Sebastian the money was from wrestling, which Sebastian was suspicious but was too happy to look father into the subject.
The first day was almost too much for the Scott’s, because of the advancement in home, from trailer to Mansion. The home was completely covered in pine, and most walls were glass. Orlando then decides to cal Rob, and thank him for convincing him to become his partner in crime.
“ Hello.” “ Hey Ashley, this is Orlando. Is Rob around?” Orlando becomes suspicious when an awkward pause was made, and finally Ashley answers with a quivered voice.
“ Rob was arrested just before you left for Japan. He told me to tell you to take the money and run, just run away. Once they find out where you are, you will be joining Rob.
Orlando nearly drops the phone, and puts a large hole through the pine wall. Orlando is now very worried, and impatient.
“ How long is he put away, I mean where should I go Ashley?” “ Just take your kid and go back to Japan, and follow the federation like the rest of the wrestlers. Just camp out in Japan and give me a call in a few weeks. All you need to do is camp out for a bit, and hopefully this will all blow over. It was nice talking to you, now get out of town!”
Orlando hangs up the phone, and grabs all his personal possessions, and Sebastian’s and makes his way Sebastian’s school. Orlando pulled Sebastian out of class, and they both drove to the airport, and got into the earliest flight to Japan.
