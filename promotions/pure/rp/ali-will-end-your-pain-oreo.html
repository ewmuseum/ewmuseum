ali.will.end.yo.ur.pain.Or.eo. I move through the streets, rain sluices off the roofs of the red-light district. It patters on my baldhead, creating dark runnels. It looks almost like blood; thick, black blood. The tip of my cigarette glows like a fevered eyeball, and I stuff my hands into the deep pockets of my coat. Its cold out, but I am cold inside. The weather is ungodly, then again, so am I. While my Oreo is training in his nice warm dojo, Im out on the street, splashing through the littered puddles of some shit Japanese city. Lights flash, whicker, and blink. I slide a pair of sunglasses onto my face and keep going. Im searching, but Im not Im waiting for something that Ive already received Smoke burns my throat and lungs, and I let it out through my nose, savoring the nicotine rush. I savor the thought that Im killing myself. Selectively slow suicide. The heart of the sato-masachist beats on, my Oreo. I glare out at the groups of young Japanese dressed so finely in their tight, leather duds; yet, they dont see my eyes. They only see my grins and they shy away; frightened like horses when blood is on the wind. Frightened like horses with blood on the wind Soft like the inside of a freshly baked cookie Their edges brittle and flaky Kind of like this little fucking Oreo I know I havent slept in days because of my rage. Wait! THERE THAT LITTLE FUCK GOES! Im screaming so loud that I feel my vocal cords crack. Im pushing, shoving, screaming, cursing my way through Osakas nightlife. There he went, ducking down that ally. Running from me Fearing me I turn on my booted heels and blast him with a tackle, screaming all the while. We splash into wet garbage, rusted trashcans, fecal matter. My smoke is gone and I dont give a fuck. Jordan X Shabazz lies beneath me, a sweaty, heaving mass of shit. Shit thats about to be wiped out. I raise my fists to the dark brooding sky, pregnant with hopes and dreams of millions of little fucking bhudda heads. Here it comes little Oreo Here comes the rage Here comes the hate Here comes the thing thats going to knock your arrogant, sniveling, self-righteous attitude down your throat and out of your raw ass Personification: Shinobi-Shinobi: Who is this man that just tackled me? Great Bhudda, he just ripped my clothes! My back is cut, and I am bleeding! Who is this wild man? FUCK! He just broke my nose! Blood rushes down my face, and it is filled with sick throbbing pain. My whole face is on fire; my poor nose! It is the nexus of the galaxy of pain that is my face. FUCK! He just punched me again! My teeth! My fucking teeth are spread across the ground! Blood! Blood pouring every where! It hurts! Great Bhudda it hurts! Darkness my friendCome to meHis glasses are coming off GREAT BHUDDA! DEMON EYES! End Personification My hand hurts I dont know who this is His face is a fucking mess I felt his nose go more than I heard it. Holy shit, are those teeth? Who is this fucker? Where are you my little Oreo? Im coming for you Coming to kill you Cumming to kill you My seed of hate Dont run from me, my bitch, my Oreo Let Ali fill your void In the end.we.all.must.die. See you fucking hell, Oreo.
