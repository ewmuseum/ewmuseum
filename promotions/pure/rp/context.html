Context...

1 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Context... Tai Chi, a stirring series of motions designed to relax and connect the mind and body. For the last week he'd seen the group gather every morning in the small park at the corner. Mostly older folks, and a few children. Those who could take the time to focus and engage the world on their own terms, not struggle to just stay afloat like so many in the 'rat race' that life often becomes. He'd watched from the bench on the corner for the last two days. Only yesterday did one of the group ask him to join. The movements weren't difficult to learn. The seconds and minutes seemed to drift by without care as the body and mind connected to create the art. Of course, Kevin had begun to add his own little flairs to the movements. Tradition, with a dash of individuality. Why couldn't more people see the truth? And as Kevin worked through the movements, his body was so relaxed, it seemed as if on autopilot. His mind was clear for perhaps the first time since the move to Yokohama. All people could seem to see was the mask, or the name. And for most, that was enough. To gathered in their own affairs to stand a closer inspection of the truth. Ebola.. A word that seemed to stick out in the mind, singular in nature and definition. But what is a word without context? Kevin wished people could see further than the simple exterior. The mask and the name were extensions of a tradition, handed down or generation. His step-father has been a luchadore. A champion, and later a trainer of champions. His mask was a prized possession, an extension of himself. His uncle had worn a mask. First out of neccessity, and later out of respect for the traditions it held. Kevin felt no different. Traditions have a place and a need. For Kevin, that place and need were in the ring, and only in the ring. It was a relief and a gift to not feel the need to constantly be 'in the mask'. He pityed 'The End', whom had long ago given up that part of himself to become something he thought he needed to be. But the mask was more than tradition. It felt like a catalyst.. When the mask went on, that's when the work began. A transformation, like flipping a switch from worrying about the rest of the world to focusing solely on the task at hand. But later, the mask would come off and life as normal would resume. Keeping at bay the pitfalls of delusion and seclusion. Even in this new place, isolation would be the last thing he could allow. But then, there was still the stigma of the name. Ebola... His uncle had been named so because of his seeming ability to sap the strength of an opponent of the course of a match, and turn that strength against him later. Kevin had taken a portion of that style, and had made it his own. It was only right that he should honor the man whom had given him so much support by carrying on his name. But it was more than that. Traditions evolve over the years, it they are to survive in an ever changing world. Kevin had taken that tradition, and added his own twist to it, making the Ebola name his own. It was his hope that his style of wrestling, a Fusion of lucha and puro would catch on, like a virus. That his energy and effort would be infectious to those around him. And it is to that end that Kevin searches out new traditions. To find old ways of doing new things. Someday, people will see beyond the surface, see past the names and the faces. They will derive their meaning not from the literal, but from the context in which it is shown. Someday...
