Phreaks Iron Chef Apperance

Phreaks Iron Chef Apperance *A commercial is aired advertising 'Iron Chef: Celebrity' as a familiar silhouette figure is shown holding up a skillet. The commercial ends with a subscript that reads... Mystery challenger. The commercial then turns into the show as we see a well trained Japanese chef sharpening all of his utensils and checking out the food items. The cameras pan across all the various items which include rice, chicken, turkey, steak, red, yellow, and green peppers and other assorted vegetables, and a thick rich white sauce. The chef looks assured of himself 'til the lights on the set go out and wobbling sounds are slightly audible. Then all of a sudden, the following words are heard yelled out... 'Listen all you Mother F***ers!' *then on the right side of the stage Phreak steps up, and behind his own grill, and starts to sharpen his own utensils as the Japanese chef looks on with amazement and shock. A count down commences as Phreak and his opponent scout out the food in which they plan to make a course out of. The count down ends as Phreak and his opponent both grease up a big sloped frying pan (wok) in which both men first throw the vegetables into the wok, and begin grilling the vegetables, then both men rapidly begin to fry the rice on a separate burner in a different pot. Phreak is doing this with a few beads of sweat on his forehead, where as the Japanese chef is still grinning like an idiot. Phreak looks over and quickly slides a piece of chicken under his spatula and quickly flings it so that it catches the other chef in the side of the jaw, but he just shakes it off as Phreak is laughing now. Phreak then follows up by sliding a cooked vegetable under it and flings it at the opposing chef again, but this time, the burning vegetable hits the chef across the back of the neck, and this greatly disrupts the other chef as he leaves his station, and retreats to the back. Phreak continues to cook as his opponent is missing, but not for long as he went to get a big bucket of water and ice., He silently approaches Phreak from behind, and proceeds to dump the water on both Phreak, and his work zone. Phreak is very appalled by this as he turns around and clocks the other chef with a right hand, knocking him down. The announcers turn from their calm collective selves into rowdy commentators as Phreak just continues his assault on the other chef. The gong then sounds off, and neither men completed the simple stir fry that they needed to advance. Phreak then turns around as he spots his rice starting to smoke. He grabs his pan by the handle and flings it at the other chef, but the other chef then kicks the burning pot back at Phreak who ducks as a camera is taken out of its place. Phreak then looks at the downed camera and just acts as if nothing happened before he and the other chef get back to fighting. However, as Phreak had his back to the other chef, the other chef grabbed a knife. Phreak realizes this just seconds before its too late and kicks the opponents right hand sending the sharp blade flipping and flying through the air and both men watch it in anticipation as it lands right in between the two point first into the ground. Phreak looks down at the knife and then looks back up at the other chef, and quickly distributes a rapid right hand punch knocking the man back down. Security finally makes his way onto the scene as the last standing cameras fade out, and Phreak yells out...* Phreak: 'BEWARE! TAKE CARE! FOR PHREAK OCOKS, AND KICKS ASS!'
