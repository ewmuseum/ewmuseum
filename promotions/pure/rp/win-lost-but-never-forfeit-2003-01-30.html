Win, lost, but never forfeit.

30 January 2003

Win, lost, but never forfeit. Written: January 30, 2003 Wow, PURE is the greatest thing for me so far. Okay, I’m lying. I told you I came here for fun, didn’t I? Well, I would be lying now if I said I was having fun. Why am I not having fun? I’m not having fun because in my debut here, I did nothing! It was my fault, too. I’m not going to blame it on PURE. I could have done better. I could have actually given the fans what they wanted. It’s not the fact that I lost, either. It’s no big deal at all.
My second match wasn’t that bad though. I got in there and did some work, and I dominated what I could. I hope I impressed the fans. I hope I gave them something to be interested in. Whether anyone knows it or not at the moment, I’m one of the proud few revolutionizing wrestling today.
A risk taker is what I am. With a guy like me, you never know what will be coming up next. I know how the wrestling viewers are with guys like me. They do not take our eyes off of us because they don’t want to miss any of the action.
Next Ikusa will be the best of them all. Why? Because I’m taking on another high flyer! Peter Perez.
“Primo, you’re going to lose again!” Daniel announced, rudely. “So what if I do lose?” I said, not really worried about it. I was in my apartment with my cousin, who was still staying with me. Since he came, I have heard nothing but his bitching and complaining.
“You lost in your first match,” he announced, “then you lost in your second match, and now you take on some loco named Peter Perez.”
“Oh well.” “Oh well?” he questioned. “You might as well just forfeit this one.” “Forfeit? Hell no,” I replied, in fury. “Win, lose, but never forfeit.” “You mean just lose,” he said and then chuckled. I then shook my head and went out the door. I suppose one day he will understand me. I suppose one day this entire world may understand me. But what really matters now, is that I understand myself. I know how I want to live my life. I know how I want it to be in the future. I know what kind of person I want to be.
But no one understands…
