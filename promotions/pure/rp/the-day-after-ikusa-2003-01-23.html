the day after Ikusa Written: 23/1/03 Ryu and Otoshi walk into their house, sore and tired. They got beaten up by Enmity and didn’t even have their much, they grumble to themselves and plant themselves onto the newly bought couch, the both hold ice packs on their bruises and relax
“Crap! this is bullshit Otoshi! We had a match and we should’ve won! What the hell is Enmity thinking interrupting us!”
Otoshi just sits in silence, gathering his thoughts on the night before “do you really want to get involved with the guy?” he finally says Ryu stares at him with fierce intensity “He got involved with us Otoshi!” he yells
“Calm down Ryu, its okay its got nothing to do with us, just focus on this week, the trinity titles are coming to us” he says Ryu gets up and starts pacing around the living room still a mess and dusty.
“Damn right those titles are coming to us, we deserve it, we bled for wrestling, we got injured and now we should be rewarded! Black Dragons, those stupid pricks think that they deserve the title! Who the hell are they anyway!” Ryu screams pacing furiously across the room, Otoshi calmly places another ice pack on his badly bruised arm; he squints at Ryu and shakes his head.
“You better calm down Ryu, you don’t know what could happen if you get too Aggravated just rest up for this match, hell Black dragons are a cake walk for us, you and the fans know it. Even President Jamar knows it, why you think he gave us the title shot.”
“Guess your right Otoshi” he replies sitting back down putting the ice pack back on “ nothing going to stop us from getting the titles, you know we’ve earned it. The fans want us to win it too” he smiles
Ryu turns on the television to see a special report on PURE, “ the new fledging Wrestling company known as PURE has already started turning heads at their first show called “Ikusa” with fresh new talent such as Radiant, Enmity, Eddie Mandrake, and the fast rising tag team, Nu Era and Black dragons be sure to watch this company do big times in the months to come, back to the studio”
Ryu throws down the remote in anger and screams “no respect!” Otoshi rolls his eyes seeing another rant about to be thrown at him “damnit, we don’t we get respect! Did we get out names mentioned? No! They ignore us! We have a huge fan base here in Japan, the crowds go nuts when we come out and this stupid reporter doesn’t even realise that!”
Ryu storms out in a hissy fit, retreating to his room. Otoshi lies back in pain and winces as his muscles ache, he looks down the hallway expecting Ryu to come back out and continue his rant but he doesn’t so Otoshi is left on the couch alone.
“God, ryu is so uptight sometimes,” he whispers to himself shaking his head. ** The Sushi crew get title shots along with their “partner” Shaun Styles, but will get they get respect they want and deserve? Only time will tell**
