Taking a nice little stroll...

20 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Taking a nice little stroll... Written: 10/20/02 .:: The scene opens up in a bright sunny day on a beach in what seems to be Los Angeles, California since a billboard says it. A man in black shorts up to his knees, black sneakers with a big Nike sign on the side of them, and a blue shirt that is very big on him and the collar is stretched out. He then walks to the boardwalks and begins to walk down the boardwalk. The camera gets a closer look on the man and realizes that it's Vito Wynn. Vito Wynn continues to walk down the wooden boardwalk. ::.
'Well, I'm back to the place I belong in, Los Angeles. Damn I love this place...' .:: Vito Wynn is stops speaking as a woman collides into him and falls to the floor from the impact but Vito Wynn remains still because of the difference of power. ::.
'Get out of the way. You see, women are nothing but a bunch of bitches. Only if there were no women in the world, the world would be a lot better without them. Even if it means the end of civilization because of them and being pregnant.' Vito Wynn says.
'Why don't you shut up because without women the men wouldn't be anything. We cook for you while you guys either sit on the coach and do nothing or sit on the coach and eat!' shouts the lady.
.:: The woman stands up and walks forward to the opposite direction as Vito Wynn is going. Vito Wynn then goes over to a store where he order a hot dog with ketchup and mustard. ::.
'That will be $1.50, sir.' replies the man selling Vito Wynn the hot dog. 'What? This little hot dog is $1.50? That's unbelievable, unfortunately I don't think you will give it to me for a smaller pricer, would you?' asks Vito Wynn.
'You got that right I'm not going to give you it for a lesser price. We got the cheapest hot dogs on the board walk and you better believe it that they are also the best, most tasteful hot dogs.' the man says.
'So, are you a new worker here?' Vito Wynn questions. 'Yeah, my Mom made me work here. I could have been a very good business man so far. But, she said that I have to start off small.' explains the man.
'Okay, you are about nineteen years old and you still live with your mother?' Vito Wynn ponders.
'Well, exactly. But, now I'm going to start this job and get my own apartment.' answers Vito Wynn.
'Whatever, I'm out of here, bye freak!' screams Vito Wynn. .:: Vito Wynn then walks quickly up the boardwalk and gets into a parking lot where he takes out a pair of nice and shiny keys and sticks them into the key hole of a red Ford Explorer with the rear and light broken, starts the engine, closes the door, and rides out of the parking space and out of the parking lot. He begins to drive the car very fast and stops after a three minute drive in front of an apartment building. Vito Wynn gets out of the car and goes inside of the apartment building as he takes out his house keys and opens the door to a room as the scene fades out. ::.
