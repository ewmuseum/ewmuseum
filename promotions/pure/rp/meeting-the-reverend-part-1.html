Meeting The Reverend [Part 1]

13 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Meeting The Reverend [Part 1] 'GIVE ME ALL YOUR MONEY! AND DON'T TRY NOTHING FUNNY, 'CAUSE YOU KNOW YO' STINKIN' ASS IS TOO FAT TO TRY AND OUTRUN ME.' Those are the exact words Jordan remembers from his old hometown, Queens, New York. It was a rough place to grow up in. Shootings, rapes, gang violence, none of this was uncommon to the young Jordan. He had to grow up quickly, with Andrew [Drew Hostyle] his older brother, being forced to work a lot of hours to support the family; Jordan learned a few tricks of the street. He learned how to pocket the occasional candy bar every now and then. He learned how to sneak a soda into his coat pocket before the damned Korean lady caught him. He just in general, learned things that most other kids didn't know. He learned about the damages of drugs. He learned about how easy it was to score drugs in 'da hood.' He learned all those things that can't lead you to anything but trouble. His mother saw a kid with a lot of potential for the future, in the ghetto; there aren't too many kids that are like that. But Jordan's mother realized that not only could Jordan grow up to be something, Jordan WOULD grow up to be something... Something positive. Jordan has done that so far, graduating from the University Of Toronto, and being promoted from the PWA. It was all good for Jordan. He worked his ass off in the NTICW in the hopes of a PCW contract, and eventually he got it. Ditto with Drew Hostyle. Both of the Copeland brothers worked hard, and both got rewarded with spots in the two top feds [According to the ratings] in the Wrestling Tattler conglomerate of federations. Now the PCW is reevaluating it's roster, which means Jordan could perhaps be only working for PURE... Is that good or bad? Definitely bad, a young man who needs cash to stay afloat, for a young, rising superstar professional wrestler, with ties in the NAACP.... Is it just me or do I smell a lawsuit? ************************************** 'Hey, this is not a playground!' The voice was loud, large, and in charge. The voice from an Asian man. The camera pulls back to reveal what appears to be a ring. It is in incredible shape. The rope cables look brand new; the mat looks like it's been cleaned recently. Inside the ring stands a proud and angry Asian man. He wears all black, black pants, black t-shirt, black wrist tape, and black elbow pads. We now pan some more to see Jordan X Shabazz running around the ring as a large Asian man chases him. Jordan rolls into the ring he stands face to face with an angry Inphino Blitz... [Who was the Asian man in the ring.] 'Jordan, I allow you in my home out of the kindness of my heart, please do not disgrace this sacred dojo by acting like a five year old,' Inphino says with authority to Jordan X Shabazz. 'Now Ken, get in the ring, we shall continue our training with Shabazz-San.' 'Ken' was Ken Ishii, the Japanese man who Jordan originally was supposed to stay with while he was in Japan. Ishii was 37 years old, he weighed 320 pounds, and he stood 6'9. He wore a grin on his face and he had a short temper. 'Okay, sorry, but there's no way that greasy, old, Asian, man is touching me, *what* the hell? I look like? Ali Khadafi?' Inphino chuckles, which is rare for a man of his stature. Inphino's usually serious, but as of late, Jordan's starting to wear down Inphino, meaning that the respect for Jordan is increasing. Jordan is attired in loose fitting gray sweat pants, and a loose fitting white t-shirt. Both are drenched in sweat which means that they have been training for quite a while. His shoes are black wrestling boots, and he wears white wrist tape, and blue elbow pads. 'Inphino, when can we stop training?' Jordan asked, angrily. 'You know Jesse is coming here later today.' 'Jesse' referred to a huge fan of Jordan X Shabazz, current NAACP member, Reverend Jesse Kintei. Jordan had developed quite the friendship with Jesse through several phone calls and a few email messages. Apparently Jesse loved to see minorities in popular sports because it gave younger minorities, who normally would be involved in gangs and violence, it gave them something to learn how to develop. Perhaps some young children growing up on the rough streets of Harlem, New York would now want to become professional wrestlers too. Instead of robbing banks and stealing things. They'll work hard to find a good job, and work hard at that job, sort of like how Jordan X Shabazz is doing with PURE and the PCW. Inphino nodded. 'Shabazz-san, you may stop training to get your things ready, I hear you are going to have a talk with Reverend Jesse Kintei today, is that correct?' 'Yes Blitz-san,' it sounded weird hearing Jordan try to be respectful of a man whom he has respected ever since he first saw him in December. Remember, Jordan has never wrestled in Japan, all he has done is study tapes of wrestling. Jordan nodded and slowly walked out of the training hall and into the main area of Inphino Blitz's dojo. The place was quiet and peaceful, Mang-A-Hero was gone, and sadly, so was Yuka Hamada, the bootylicious girl that was staying at the dojo along with Jordan X Shabazz was also out. Both had gone shopping for the household. Jordan walked towards the guest room that had become his new home. He smiled at how Inphino had treated him since getting there. His own bedroom, complete with television, and access to the Internet, a nice and bouncy bed with a cold pillow that almost FORCES you to sleep. Jordan sat on his bed and checked the clock. 6:24, The Reverend should arrive any minute now, Jordan thought to himself. He wasn't really nervous, it was just that he hadn't been doing much work in the NAACP due to the fact that he and his manager/lawyer in the United States, Jake Jackson, had started the trial in which Jordan was suing The State of Texas, the National Hockey League, and People Magazine. All for a variety of reasons which I shall not get into now. [For further reference check out some of Jordan's PCW RPs.] Jordan went into his closet and went into the coat pocket of his FUBU jacket. He pulled out a cigarette and he fumbled in his pant pockets for a lighter, and eventually found one. He lit up a cigarette. He hadn't smoked since High School, but with the stress of trying to get his job back in the PCW, and with the stress of the NAACP on his shoulders, he needed something to calm himself down and relieve the stress. Jordan was almost finished his cigarette when... SHABAMM~! The door swung open with an angry Inphino Blitz standing in the doorway. He had just kicked the door open and he stared at Jordan with a stony glare. Inphino slowly walked over and he took the cigarette out of his hands. He crumpled up the cigarette, still lit, and showed no signs of pain on his face. He walks slowly over to the small trash can in Jordan's room, and he puts the now unlit cigarette in it. Jordan doesn't speak, as he's about to fall asleep on his bed, the door booms open. A dark skinned man wearing a black suit, and a grin on his face appears. He's about 6 foot, and appears to be in the 200 pound range. He walks over to Jordan, and he gives him a hardy handshake. 'Mr. Shabazz, I'm the Reverend Jesse Kintei of the N double A C P. Nice to meet you.'
