Blood Ready to Flow

10 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Blood Ready to Flow Voice: Have you ever washed the blood of a fallen enemy off of your hands? How about the blood of a fallen comrade? I, Chen Lei-chia, have led the elite four-member Black Dragon mafia gang for a decade now. Blood has come and blood as gone, from enemies striking out against the Mountain Lord Pai Po to some of my best friends turned traitor because the money was right! I have no regrets, I fear no man, and I have no fear of death! Live or die, I am bound to the Black Dragons and the Mountain Lord Pai Po! The scene opens up in one of the numerous hotels in Sapporo, Japan. There in room 1112 sitting on the balcony with a cup of coffee watching the sunrise is none other than Chen Lei-chia. He sits outside alone as his Black Dragon brothers, Ning, Chiang, and Chan are all sound asleep inside the hotel room. The time as come for my Black Dragon brothers and myself to make our mark upon PURE! It is time for us to show the Japanese world what these Chinese Assassins are capable of, remarks a determined Chen. No more sitting back waiting for things to be handed to us From this point on we will take what we want and woe to anyone who thinks they can stop us! Chen slams his coffee cup down on the balcony railing as he stands to his feet. He walks to the rail and proceeds to drop his pajama bottoms. He stands there exposed only to begin urinating off the eleventh floor of the motel. Japan! Chen screams out into the wind. Get ready, the Black Dragons are here and we are here to conquer! Inside the motel room Chiang Ho Nam, the biggest and toughest member of the Dragons, sits flexing his muscled arms. Chiang begins to start doing pushups as the other two members of the Black Dragons, Ning and Chan, continue to snore the early morning away. Chen makes his way into the motel room pulling up his pajama bottoms as he spots a sweaty Chiang doing pushups. Dont worry Chiang, Chen remarks drawing Chiang from his exercise routine. Our time will come soon enough! Japan hasnt got a thing that will stop the sheer brute force that we bring! Two matches under our belts and we showed PURE we came to fight! Win, lose or draw right Chen? asks a sleepy eyed Chan who is woken up by Chens talking. We will conquer and dominate anything and everything that stands in our way! Ning shifts in his bed rolling over and placing a pillow over his head trying to cover up the noise of the other Black Dragons. Chan sits up on the edge of the bed above Chiang who resumes doing his pushups and Chen who takes a seat in the couch the motel room has offered. Who are the next sheep to the slaughter? asks Chan. Bushido, Cege, and Bloody Tiger, answers Ning as he throws the pillow across the room to an unaware Chan. The pillow bounces off the side of Chans head, which brings laughter from the others. Dont underestimate them I beg Chen! Ning pleads, We may have survived and we may have shown these three what we are capable of last Ikusa, but how long will sneak attacks last? Chen stands up and shushes Ning down with a raised finger pursed to his lips. Sneak attacks will always work in our favor Ning; we are the master of the shadows and the masters of the art of assassination! None in PURE are able to stop us from taking over, not Bushido, Cege, Bloody Tiger, Mang-a-Hero, Ebola, Blitz or even the leader of all of PURE Miya! Chen claims with a set determination in his voice. The time for attack is now, so get ready my brothers, for the blood of our enemy is ready to flow! Chen announces to the gang.
