I am? I AM!

6 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I am? I AM! Written: 6th February 2003 The sun beams through the half-opened blinds on the windows of Jason Marah’s hotel room. Beside his bed is an alarm clock, reading 8.45AM. The beams highlight the blue walls and the lack of Marah’s personal possessions in the room; the majority of the room is filled with hotel property. The streams of light bounce off of Marah’s stomach and chest. He is sleeping in his bed, with the blankets only covering his lower torso. The door creeps open and Marah’s religious guide, motivator and fighting trainer enters slowly. He comes in wearing a short sleeved, buttoned up cream top and white trousers. The elderly man walks up to Marah silently, with his hands behind his back gripping something. He takes up a stationary position beside Marah’s bed and reveals whatever it was behind his back – it proves to be a Kendo stick. He slowly raises it above his head, before suddenly smashing it viciously across Jason’s stomach.
Jason Marah: What the fuck? He continues to mutter in an almost illegible volume of voice. His eyes open and he looks up to his attacker. Craig whacks him again…and again as Marah squirms around the bed.
Jason Marah: WHAT THE HELL ARE YOU PLAYING AT?! Patrick Craig: You’re entering the Open Invitation Battle Royal on Ikusa where the winner gets a World title shot.
Craig halts the barrage of Kendo stick strikes. Jason Marah: I am? WHACK! Jason Marah: Fuckin’ hell, I am! Patrick Craig: Since you are, why the hell are you out drinking to all hours of the morn’, instead of training?
Jason rolls out of the bed and slides on a pair of navy blue tracksuit bottoms. Jason Marah: I don’t know, for the laugh I suppose. Patrick Craig: For the laugh? FOR THE LAUGH?! Damn it Jason, I thought you were serious about this profession.
Jason Marah: I am. Patrick Craig: You’ve got a right funny way of showing it. You shouldn’t be out getting intoxicated with the likes of Binsei Kinaka, you should be training and preparing for you’re first big opportunity.
Jason slides on a black tank top and puts a black case, about the size of a small flash lamp into his tracksuit pocket and walks out of the room.
Patrick Craig: Where do you think you’re going? Jason Marah: I’m going to go train and win my fecking title shot, if that’s alright with you?
Patrick smiles to himself and follows Marah out of the room. Marah walks hurriedly out of the hotel room, with Craig doing his best to keep up with him. Marah jogs down the stairs and into the lobby. He looks left and right and decides just to go straight ahead, onto the street. Craig, realising he can’t keep up with Marah just waves his attempts off and goes for some breakfast.
Marah takes up jogging as his choice of transport today with the expense of receiving odd looks from the natives he passes, but he ignores them. He takes every turn in his passage of way, but this leads him to unknown areas to him of the city. He looks around suspiciously, but then shrugs off all concerns and restarts the jog. A soft sound of a car engine seems to be getting ever closer to Marah. He looks behind him to find a dark blue, unmarked Nissan Almera with tinted windows going at a slower pace than him – so Marah picks up the pace, but so does the Nissan. Marah begins to run as fast as he can and the Almera also picks up speed following Marah through every corner he takes in attempts to loose them. Marah finds himself in a sparsely populated housing estate with a field at the end of the estate. Marah bolts. He runs across the field, with the Almera ever present behind him. The Almera picks up even more speed and clips Marah’s knee slightly, but none-the-less knocking him down. The Almera halts and 3 men step out. Two of them of a big build, standing around 6’3 each and behind them a relatively smaller man, all of Asian descent. The smaller man speaks, in a rough version of the English language.
Small Man: Well, if it not be Binsei Kinaka’s Ooropeen fwiend, Jason Marah. Jason Marah: What? Could ya say that again, didn’t quite hear ya. Small Man: Siwence! You fwiend cause me lot of trouble, I think, me get wevenge by killing ooo! HAHAHAHAHA!
Jason climbs to his feet and shakes the hit leg. Jason Marah: Lad, I’ve got no time to listen to your double-dutch, I’ve got training to do, so if you don’t mind, piss off.
Jason smiles, but the smaller man is less than impressed. He signals for the other two men to draw their weapons, and they do exactly that, extracting a couple of small black pistols.
Jason Marah: Ah for fuck sake. Small Man: Ooo listen ere! Ooo show me wespect! Do ooo understand? Jason Marah: Not really, no. It is blatantly obvious that the man to the left of the smaller man is about to take a shot, so Marah uses his Tae-Kwon-Do expertise to knock the pistol out of his hands with a roundhouse kick and with a quick movement extracts the black case from his tracksuit bottoms, and clicks a tiny button on the side of it, revealing a long, silver samurai sword and puts it to the throat of the large man while kicking the gun out of the other large man’s hand too. The smaller man looks shocked.
Jason Marah: Aye, didn’t think I could do that, eh? Here’s an idea little buddy – FUCK OFF!
The man, obviously new to confrontations of this type scrambles to the Almera. He starts it up and speeds off. Jason retracts the blade and slaps the case into his bottoms again. He talks to other two men.
Jason Marah: Lads, if ye two don’t mind, I have to get any with this little run. The two men nod slowly. Jason chuckles. Jason Marah: Right, good luck. Marah runs off again, smiling. He laughs to himself. Jason Marah: (Laughing) I better have a word with Binsei about this.
