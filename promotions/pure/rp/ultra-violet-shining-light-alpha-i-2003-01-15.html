Ultra Violet Shining Light Alpha- I

15 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ultra Violet Shining Light Alpha- I Written: Wednesday, the 15th of January. Narrated by Minori Kumako. In late 2000, my then boyfriend, Akoshi Kunamara, quit Professional Wrestling, vowing never to return to the sport that gave him enjoyment. After losing his coveted Suicidal Wrestling Alliance United States Championship, Akoshi was enraged at the company that had caused me, Minori Kumako, to be harmed during the match when his opponent Kyle Adams fell on top of me. While in the office of the SWA Owner, Robert Williams, Akoshi trashed the office, screaming at him. Williams shred his contract in front of him, telling Akoshi that he would never wrestle in the United States again.
Akoshi wrestled under the name of Radiant, wearing a mask like his hero Jushin Thunder Liger did. His agent, a woman named Megumi Nagasaku promised to keep him notified if a job opportunity came about.
My wish is for Akoshi to return to Pro-Wrestling, but not in America as I think the silly Americans do not know a think about this great sport.
But now, we’re both almost out of money with no jobs living in a small New Orleans apartment. Our tiny apartment only houses a kitchen, a tiny bedroom and a little living room to watch television on. Together we sit on the bed, staring at each other.
‘Minori, I don’t think this is a very good idea,’ said Akoshi Kunamara to me, Minori Kumako. ‘Megumi rang me the other day and told me the only work that I could get in Japan was with this company that closed two times in 2002. This place called “Puroresu Ultimate Ring Entertainment”; it has a nice name but a terrible string of luck.’ He sighed loudly. ‘This could be my last chance, but I don’t think it’s a good idea.’
I sighed and moved a little closer to him. ‘I understand how hard it must be for you to return home, it is for me as well.’ I explained to Akoshi.
‘And my fam-‘ Akoshi began to say, but I angrily stood up and cut him off. ‘Your family has nothing to do with it!’ I screamed at him. My arms were held tightly on my hips, and a stern look of anger plastered across my face as I glared at him. ‘It is the excuse you use every time I tell you to return home!’
His head fell a little bit, showing shame at his actions. All the anger I felt in my body quickly was washed out. The implications of him returning to Japan are immense, and I know some of it. The secrets this young man keeps from the world are to complicated for even the most intelligent of people. But, I don’t fear the consequences of a return to our home country, and neither does he. The only thing I fear? I fear he can’t do it anymore.
‘You’re not afraid of your family, Akoshi,’ I reassured him, hugging him tightly. ‘I don’t think so, anyway.’ I stopped hugging him, and looked directly into his eyes. ‘I think you’re scared you can’t wrestle anymore.’
Akoshi jumped back a little, sliding off the bed and onto his feet. Anger spread across his young face, and Akoshi clinched his fists tightly. ‘What do you mean I’m scared I can’t wrestle anymore?!’ he bellowed at me, anger flaring up in his again. ‘I can wrestle just fine. I just don’t want to do it.’
‘Can we talk about something else?’ Akoshi quietly asked me. ‘I’m getting sick of this talk about wrestling.’
‘No.’ I defiantly said back to him. Akoshi’s brow raised, angered once more at my defiance to listen to him. I never let anyone walk over me, especially Akoshi. ‘We need money. And you of all people know how hard it is to get a job in this deadbeat of a town; everyone here is racist. What else is there for us to do?’
‘I know that,’ he stated. ‘I just don’t think I belong in a wrestling ring anymore, especially with what happened last time I wrestled.’ Akoshi sat back down on the bed, taking my hand in his grasp and giving me a soft kiss on the top of my hand. Memories of the last night Radiant ever wrestled floated back into my head. Throwing his opponent, Kyle Adams, out of the ring and preparing to do a Plancha over the ropes and onto Adams. Akoshi never liked Adams since Adams was a racist, and didn’t like our Asian heritage. Cowardly, Kyle Adams pulled me in front of him, and a completely oblivious Radiant landed directly on top of me. The result caused me to break a rib. These memories were forced out of my head; I need to get him to wrestle again.
‘Don’t worry about me,’ I reassured him kindly. ‘I can look after myself well enough. You of all people should know that.’
Akoshi sighs softly, getting off the bed. He paced around in a circle, walking around the bed I remained seated on. He was deep in thought. I know how his brain works, and I can almost figure out what he is thinking about. It’s like we’re connected.
‘Alright,’ he said sternly, still standing up. ‘I know I am going to regret doing this. But, you’re right. There is nothing here for either of us, and there is work for me home. I’ll do it. I’ll go back to Japan, and wrestle.
For you.’ I sprung to my feet and leapt off the bed. Immediately I threw my arms around his neck and kissed him hard on the lips. We lost our balance and fell to the floor, rolling around on top of each other while still holding the kiss. Our night ended with passion and pleasure, but the journey has only begun.
