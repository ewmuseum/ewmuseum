Attempt: Failed...But Accused...

26 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Attempt: Failed...But Accused... The sun was shining bright...the birds were singing...and I actually didn't feel too bad. Remember that guest room I woke up in before fighting with my father? Well that's where I was then as a 'guest' of my mother's. Apparently all is forgiven and a WHOLE family is needed when someone, say a father, is trying to be killed. I woke up to the smell of hot, fresh tea and toast. I emerged from the bedroom to see my mother and grandmother sitting around the kitchen table. They were sipping thier tea and passing bits and peices of the newspapar back and forth. I sat down and was handed a cup as well as a plate with two pieces of toast on it. I grabbed the headlines page and starting having my breakfast as if nothing had happened. They just sat there and looked at me dumbfounded. 'I am glad you could take time out of your schedule to come and be with your family.' My mother said putting her hand on top of mine. 'Yeah...' I muttered scarfing down another piece of toast. I was trying to read the headlines but it was all in Japanese...not one English translation. I knew it was about my father because I recognized the two mangled cars. 'Hey. Can you translate this headline for me?' I asked my mother handing her the paper. She began...'On the night of August 20th, a man by the name of Lee Kim Nagato was found in a smashed and mangled Nissan, nearly dead.' Tears began to fill her eyes but as usual, I felt nothing. 'After being rushed to the hospital tests showed a broken leg, broken collarbone, and broken spine. After five days of being in instensive care, Nagato is alive and talking again.' She wiped a tear from under eye and held hands with my grandmother. I sat there in shock...utter disbelief. 'DAMMIT!' I yelled nearly giving my grandmother a heart attack. She screamed at me in Japanese and my mother did in English. 'Akai! What was that for? You said it as if you wanted him dead!' I replied (almost stumbling over my own words), 'Oh...er...no. I screamed because I can't believe someone would try and harm him. I will go see him in the hospital in a few days.' She smiled at me and grandmother calmed down a bit, too. 'But I must be getting to the arena...I have a match tonight.' With that, I grabbed my jacket and left the house. On the road, I kept asking myself where I fouled up and why I didn't kill me. I decided...when I visit him...I will get the job done. I arrived at the arena and made my way into my dressing room. About fifteen minutes later two officers entered and sat down on both sides of me. They were both Japanese, and wearing identical trench coats, but they spoke in broken English. 'You Akai?' The one to my right asked showing me his badge. I nodded and he looked up at his partner and then back to me. 'You been accused of attempt murder.' The one to my left, the taller and dorkier of the two, took out a pair of handcuffs. I have to admit, I was ready to punch both of them in the faces and flee but right then...Jung Loc entered the room. 'There a problem, officers?' He questioned them throwing his bag and title on the ground. 'This man accused of attempt murder of Lee Kim Nagato on the 20th of August.' The right-hand man said rising to his feet. Jung just looked at him. 'That's impossible, you fools. He was with me all week. Jamar Wilson, the leader of TokyoPop, bought myself, Enmity...Akia as you like to call him, and Ali sweets in Tokyo.' The two officers were baffled and just scratched their heads. 'If you don't believe ME...you can ask Jamar, himself.' 'But this man had reserved room where Nagato was almost killed. The only one supposedly staying there at time. He only suspect.' One of them said. 'Yes...' Jung told them. 'But he reserved that BEFORE Jamar treated us.' The tall, dorky officer removed the cuffs and walked over to his partner. 'Sorry,' they apoligized leaving the room. 'Thanks, man.' I told Loc as he gathered his stuff, before leaving. He just nodded and proceeded to his dressing room. He saved me...
