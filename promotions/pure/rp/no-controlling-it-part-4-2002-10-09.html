No Controlling It...(Part 4)

9 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
No Controlling It...(Part 4) Written: 10/9/02 The scene appears with a police van with Vito Wynn and two other police men in the back. The man then stops in front of a police station and gets out of the car. The two police men then grab Vito Wynn and escort him out of the car and close the door behind them as they walk him into the building. They then all stop in front of a desk where a man talking on the phone is sitting.
'Oh yeah, where did this take place?' asks the man on the phone. (You hear little mumbles from the guy on the other line.) 'Oh, okay. I'll send men right there. Did you call the ambulance to come?' question the man on the phone.
(Again, you hear little mumbles from the guy on the other line.) 'Okay, good. I'll send my men right over to investigate.' replies the man on the phone. The man shuts the phone off and puts it on its charger. 'Hey guys, can you guys go over to seventy-third avenue, between eighty-fifth and eighty-sixth street because there was a little accident with a man getting hit in the head with a brick in the alley. Well, who is this man?' the man behind the desk asks.
'This man was also caught speeding on seventy-third avenue, between eighty-fifth and eighty-sixth street for some reason, I was only going to tell him that his back license plate was dented and he drove off really quick so obviously he probably did it...' the police man stops talking.
All of the sudden Vito Wynn gets up and kicks the desk on top of the guy sitting behind it. He then kicks one of the police men in the gut as the other two jump on top of him and start defending themselves.
'You are lucky that I got these cuffs on because I would've kicked all of your asses if I didn't.' Vito Wynn remarks.
'Shut up before I beat you down with this nightstick!' shouts the police office. The police officer who was kicked in the gut recovers from being hurt and helps the man behind the desk by pulling him out.
'This guys going directly to jail, SEND HIM!' the man who was stuck under the desk shouts. The three police men grab him and take him out of the police station and put him inside of the van. The same officers stay with him and the other one drives again. The man quickly drives through the street and makes a few tight turns. After a long trip of about a half hour, the reach a jail. The man stops the car in front of the jail and take Vito Wynn out of the car they then get him inside of the jail and add him into the list of people in jail. The three police men then bring him to a certain room where he changes his clothes into an orange jail suit. The police officers then pat him down to check if he still has anything on him. The police men then walk him to his jail cell as being thrown at by paper from the other people locked up in their jail cells. They then throw him into his cellar as the scene fades out.
