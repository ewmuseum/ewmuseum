No Controlling It...(Part 3)

8 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
No Controlling It...(Part 3) Written: 10/8/02 The scene opens up. It is a bright afternoon in a neighborhood. Vito Wynn appears to be driving very quickly in his black Toyota Carolla. He swiftly cutting the cars and causing traffic. The light then turns red and brakes desperately before being crashed into by another car coming the opposite way. A police car then pulls up in back of Vito Wynn. Vito Wynn spots the two police men get out of the car and walk towards Vito Wynn's car. Vito Wynn gets very nervous.
'Come on, come on, turn green already!' Vito Wynn shouts. The light turns green and Vito Wynn just presses on the gas really hard and zooms down the street. The two police men then run over to their car and get in it. They begin chasing after Vito Wynn.
'What the hell is this guy running for, we were only going to tell him that his back license plate is dented. Well, he must have done something bad then.' the police man says.
The police man lifts his walkie-talkie up and speaks into it. 'There's a man on the run for some apparent reason. The man drove off in a hurry down seventy-third avenue, between eighty-fifth and eighty-sixth street.' the police officer reports Vito Wynn to the station.
The police officer presses on the gas and begins to try and find Vito Wynn. Suddenly, Vito Wynn is seen parked on a street. Vito Wynn steps outside of the car and puts his shirt over his face a little bit to cover his face. He walks into a hotel called 'Hotel Pyramid'. The shape of the hotel is obviously the shape of a pyramid. Vito Wynn walks up a numerous amount of flights of stairs and then reaches a room where he puts the key in and unlocks the door. Vito Wynn opens the door and gets inside of the little room. He lays on his bed a takes a deep breath. Vito Wynn then abruptly hears a knock on the door. He stands up.
'Who is it?' Vito Wynn asks. Nobody is heard replying. Vito Wynn opens the door and is then tackled down to the floor by three men who look like FBI agents. They each pull out their guns and point it at Vito Wynn.
'You have the right to remain silent, anything you do or say will be held against you in the court of law.' one of the police officers remark.
The same police officer takes out a pair of handcuffs, turns Vito Wynn laying on his chest, and cuffs Vito Wynn's hands together on his back. The three men then pick Vito Wynn up and walk him out of the room, down the few flights of stairs, and into a big police van. Two of the police officers stay with Vito Wynn in the back and the other one drives the van. The police officer pulls out of the drive way and drives down the street as the scene fades to black.
