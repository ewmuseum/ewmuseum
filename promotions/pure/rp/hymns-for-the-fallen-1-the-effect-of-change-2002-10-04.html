Hymns for The Fallen [1] ...The Effect of Change... Written: 10.04.02 September 25th, 2002 Wednesday - 23:17 hours Egg Dome in Tokyo, Japan ~~~... For myself, and the rest of the PURE team heading into Red October, its another day broken down in Japan. We may be able to relax, quietly enjoying tea baths, and prepping for the match on the 6th of October. While the diligent workers and staff begin disassembling the pyrotechnic equipment and storing them for immediate transport to the Tokyo Dome, the remaining participants begin to file out, in their own bravado and vigor out of the parking lot.
From my vantage point, there seems to be only a few people left, as a partner of mine on this upcoming faithful Sunday, Cianan, fulfills his duty of executing his arm and leg strength in preparation for Sunday night. The warm Tokyo breeze is a difference in my nasal cavity, away from those falling tree leaves and crisp summer nights ending nights ago. The thoughts of bringing ill-fated memoirs to Christian Xavier Kannon and his consortium of demons, only strengthened the brotherhood between us all. Something out of the ordinary, just a nonchalant nod or gesture to him, forcing them to think that nothing was out of the ordinary.
At the very eve of any Puroresu show, Pay Per View telecast, or simply whenever I sought fit, the need for solitude became a priority. While many of my peers speak whole heatedly of 'teaching those PCW bastards a lesson in PURE actions' at public restaurants, indulging in brief social altercations at a hang out spot here in Party Central, I would simply remain to the decor of my Gaijin house for the night. Its all I need, not any of those bogus commercialized promoters shoving their product in my face, not in this land anyway.
The streets remain slick, almost slippery from the almost known instance of downpour occurring in the last few hours or so. Opting to use the Navigator for the best 'mode of transportation' was an excellent decision, if I may add. Can you not have a CD player in the times like these, then you're mistaken somewhat. This event is big, and listening to a soothing hip-hop instrumental, shall calm my nerves just fime. A slight jarring of your thinking ability can derail you from some of your more inquisitive thoughts. . . .
. . . . The Five on Five, PCW against PURE match at Red October. . . . . . . . A Ikusa match, inside of a nightclub with a former acquaintance of mine in DJ Enygma, as he mixes to the moving sounds of the very own pounding fist. Myself, and the Faithful Youth against him and Nu Era. . . .
. . . . Although I may not partake upon it, let it be certain that the team of Puroresu shall impend some damage upon the set of Friday, as the red moon cascades upon the night. . . .
. . . . To my friends in PURE who cannot participate in the five on five, your time shall come soon. . . .
The combination of streaking traffic past the car, as that hum of tires treading along paved roads and narrow distances. Those noises, they force out those thoughts are now gone, drifted into the wind. The appointed mission for this night was to achieve what was always thought of as 'elusive' in this odd profession of ours, those which we partake: the hymns of the fallen.
Home Dressed in that of a common rock star, I move in away from those wanting to grab a hand upon me, resistance not a driving force in my movement. That dream of nocturnal slumber wasn't at the very top of my agenda, but it happened to be for the very most part. All was left, fiddling with the bumbling alarm clock and resting my very head against the pillow and the clarity of my vision dims. ~~~
[ /\ ] It was slowly creeping into the dim Tokyo skyline as the sun begin to rise on many streets. Lying comfortably in bed, he had his eyes shut, dreaming only of how and in what form he would decimate the competition at Red October. The sultry movement of a woman happened to catch his eyes, as he struggled to view her. The shade in the room had been set back a bit, causing more light to enter the room, than his pupils could handle.
'Morning sleepy head,' the voice called, as she placed her forefinger upon his pink lips. His eyes began to try and flutter open, but the woman budged, positioning herself on top of him, moving her index finger over his eyelids. 'No sunshine today!'
He grunted back in a playful manner, before pushing her towards his side, as her elbow jammed into the exterior portion of his ribcage. For the first time in many a month, Cordozer had some thoughts, some second guesses about his upcoming adventures - first and foremost - the five on five.
'Let me go, let me go!' he laughed as she continued to poke his side, inciting laughter from his stable, yet appeasing form of body. 'Why are you here Botan?'
'Oh, same as always, wanting that bit of information that may force you to rethink your career decisions.' the woman glanced at him before turning back to lie on her back. She had raven hair, coming down past her neck with white streaks covering the sides and back. A few moments ago, these extra stands were locked in by metallic chopsticks, but she had removed them. Wearing a red cut T-shirt, the end of which barely covering over her navel with the word SEXY written in silver glitter. Below, a pair of low cut leather pants flew down her backside. 'Please don't tell me you're thinking of backing out from the most important night in your career.'
She wasn't in the more happier of moods, only a few weeks have past since Cordozer and her abrupt fight at a night spot in Downtown Tokyo.
'I have my explanations,' I replied calmly. Then another elbow darted at my side. I turned my head, before looking over at the man with spiking hair that defys most of gravity. Kaneda stood over him, wearing a red biker jacket with torn blue jeans.
'Don't wanna' go through gettin' embarrassed by some poppy busters, eh?' he chuckled in his raspy, yet cocky voice as Botan rolled her eyes, darting him with a stare.
'And you're here because of what? You know you have not a single reason to be near me at the moment!'
I shrugged my shoulder, hopping out of bed and pulling a blueVega shirt over my head. 'Everyone needs a chance to experience life once in a great while. Otherwise, we'd be much like Timothy Walden and become known to snap.
Botan glared over at the man across from her, busy twirling a lock of his hair. 'Its already too late for you to tell him that.' 'I never said I was going to let this match get away from me.' Stevenson muttered, bringing Botan's attention back to their conversation. She had that intimidating figure, much like Violence Jack, but in that domination sort of thing. Their eyes always had that sort of gleam towards them, brandishing that you didn't look them dead in the eye. 'I merely said that I had confident and insecure thoughts about the match.'
Botan folded her arms and leaned against the wall behind her frame. 'So what's the big deal that it may affect the rest of your career?'
'I don't know...' he replied, 'but I believe its something more than meets the eye...'
