No controlling it...(Part 2) Written: 10/7/02 The scene opens up. Vito Wynn appears to be causing traffic on the crowded neighborhood by standing in the middle of it. He then suddenly shakes his head and gets his cellphone out of his pocket and presses a few buttons on it. He then holds it up against his ear and waits as he hears a loud ringing noise. Someone picks up and begins to speak.
'Hello, who is this, I hope it's good news!' says the man in a pleasant tone of voice. 'Hey Joey, it's Vito.' replies Vito Wynn. 'Who? Wait, first of all, who is Vito?' questions Joey. 'You know, Vito Wynn. The man that helped you in many ways during your life because we are friends.' responds Vito Wynn.
'Oh, hey Vito. It just didn't sound like you for a second because you are usually not breathing heavy so this can't be good news.' explains Joey.
'Nope, it's not good news at all Joey. I just broke a brick over some huge guy's head. Well, if that's good news for you then I don't really care but it's not good news for me because if the police find out I did this I will be sent to jail.' answers Vito Wynn.
'Well, what would you like me to do about it?' asks Joey. 'I'd like you to pick me up and ride me to my house because I just can't walk to my house. I would really appreciate it if you were to send me home.' Vito Wynn mentions.
'Oh, now for the first time you said that you would appreciate it. Well, I knew this time would come. Hell, like they say, there's a first time for everything, am I wrong?' Joey ponders.
'Yeah, but come and pick me up. I'm at seventy-third avenue, between eighty-fifth and eighty-sixth street in Brooklyn, New York.' Vito Wynn tells Joey.
'What? You live in Los Angelos, California, I can't take you from New York to California. Well, I can't waste my time riding all the way from California to New York just so I can pick you up and bring you back to California. That's insane!' shouts Joey.
'Well, how about you take a little vacation to New York then. But, may I include, I rented a room in a hotel for two months for a vacation in New York and it is just awesome. No joke, it's really cool out here in New York.' Vito Wynn says.
'That still won't make me come there. Sorry, but I guess you'll have to walk then. Wait, where's your car?' asks Joey.
'Right in front of me, why?' Vito Wynn asks. 'Then take your damn car!' Joey screams. 'Oh yeah, I'm a dick. Hahahahaha! Well, I'm out then, peace.' Vito Wynn includes. Vito Wynn then shuts his phone off and puts it in his pocket. He then gets his keys out of his pocket and spots the man walking out of the alley slowly in pain and is all bloody. Vito Wynn quickly gets into his car and drives away as the scene fades out.
