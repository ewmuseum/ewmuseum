Ultra Violet Shining Light Alpha- VI A

5 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ultra Violet Shining Light Alpha- VI A Written: Wednesday, the 5th of February Narrated by Minori Kumako. Two green and red Parrots chirped softly in the cool February air. Their beaks clipped each other’s feathers in an affectionate grooming session. I watched the two wild birds frolic with each other, smiling while their beaks clipped their feathers. I stopped looking at those two playful birds and snapped my attention back to my grunting fiancé. From the look in his deep brown eyes, they gave off the look of daggers. Akoshi slowly stood back onto his feet from the damp rocks we had seated our selves in. A grunt came from his mouth, angered by his elder brother Yukio interrupting a special moment between he and I.
‘I must see my father,’ he said in a sombre tone. A weary look was in his eyes. I was completely unaware, but he could sense danger. Yukio still stood on top of the balcony, smirking disdainfully down at our ruined moment together. Akoshi took a step forward, but he suddenly stopped walking and turned back to face me. ‘Minori, please, stay here.’
‘I won’t let you go on your own,’ I said back, standing back up. My hand wrapped around his left one. ‘Listen, I have this feeling in my stomach.’
‘Oh?’ Akoshi said in return, raising a brow. ‘Yes,’ I returned back to him. ‘There is just something tell me that there is something wrong with this whole situation. I don’t trust Yukio at all, he might have something planned for your meeting with your father!’
‘Just keep yourself busy, Minori.’ He said, turning his back and walking toward the stairs which Yukio stood atop of. His slick hair was even further oiled up, and his suit was free of dirt and grime. Akoshi glares hatefully at his brother, the look of daggers in his eyes. ‘I will be back within the hour, believe me, I do not like this as much as you don’t.’ Akoshi climbed the stairs; walking right passed the smirking Yukio.
Akoshi slid the door that led into the main area of the first floor of the central house. He soon disappeared from my sight. The triumphant smirk had faded from Yukio’s face. He looked down to where I sat on the rocks near the hot springs, and quickly descended the stair case in a wave of arrogance that could be matched by no one. The sole of his shoes slapped down against the damp ground, ringing into my ear each time he takes a step toward me. I looked over my shoulder a little bit, frightened at what he might do to me.
‘What do you want, Yukio?’ I said timidly to him. ‘What I am wanting is simple, woman!’ Yukio said in his unforgettable broken English. Yukio approached closer, his dark skin glimmering in the sun that has cracked through the clouds over Aikawa Island. ‘All I want with you, woman, is to talk to you! I am not a bad buy, brother just make lies!’
‘It would be appreciated if you did not call me “woman”.’ I said back spitefully to him. Akoshi wells know enough that I do not like being looked down upon, especially by someone I despise. A sneer crept across my face as Yukio looked back at me, not expecting an answer like the one I gave him. I presume that for all of his life, Yukio was used to being able to order the maids around as he pleased, and thought I would be exactly the same as them.
‘I man! Woman speak to me with respect,’ Yukio replied, putting his hands on his waist. ‘It rule of Kunamara house, woman treat man with respect!’
‘But I am not a member of the Kunamara house,’ I told him, smiling at my triumphant revelation. ‘Yukio, you very well know that I am not married to Akoshi. My surname is still Kumako, so you can not boss me around like you can to everyone else!’
Yukio, at first, was taken back in shock. But his shock was soon replaced by a look of anger. His look of anger still kept his sly and sleek look, as if he was about to release the ultimate secret.
‘I tell you things about brother,’ he said slyly. My brow perked up at what he just had said. I was interested in finding information out about Akoshi that he just refuses to tell me.
‘What things?’ I asked back, interested. I did not know why I was interested in finding out useless information that no doubt is all lies. Yukio smiled, and to me, that was the first time he had ever smiled in my presence.
‘Well, I tell you that brother hurt people as child.’ He said, walking over toward a nearby bench and sitting down. He motioned for me to join, but I was hesitant. ‘Sit down, I tell you other things about brother.’
Reluctantly, I sat down next to him. ‘I do not want to hear any lies, Yukio,’ said I sternly. I faced him, staring directly into his cold, dark brown eyes. ‘If you tell me any lies, I will not be happy.’
‘Brother hit servants as child.’ Yukio said, shaking his head as if he cared. ‘Mother and Father not like it, but brother still hit servant if they not give him what he want when he want. I not like.’
‘I do not believe you,’ I said back. ‘Woman should,’ said Yukio again. ‘I not lie. Brother not nice person, he hit people and steal money from people.’ I did not believe a word of it. I shook my head in anger, trying not to just leap from the bench and slap Yukio in the face so hard my own hand would start bleeding.
‘LIE!’ I shouted, startling one of the maids in a far away garden, pruning a flower. My feet leapt into the air and back onto the ground, extending to my full height. My fingers crunched up into a tight ball, but flexed out and slapped against Yukio’s skin. An almighty sound of flesh colliding with flesh sounded out in the Kunamara house Garden.
Yukio sprung to his feet, anger running through his eyes. He clenched his feet, and I was sure he was about to hit me in the face. But for some odd reason, Yukio Kunamara paused. He released his fingers, and took a deep breath.
‘GUARDS!’ he bellowed, and I became quite worried.
