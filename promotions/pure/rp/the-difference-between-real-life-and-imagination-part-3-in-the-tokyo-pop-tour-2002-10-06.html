The Difference Between Real Life and Imagination (Part 3 in the Tokyo Pop Tour) Written: 10/6/02 Part 2 It just seemed to be some mild stress. We see things like this all the time. In a day or two he should be just fine.
We don’t have that kind of time; he has to be ready tonight. We have thousands of paying fans out there and were already rescheduling things we can’t let this happen.
It doesn’t matter; he just isn’t in any shape to be performing. ************* Fade ***************** Hello???? Is anybody there? *********** Heavy Breathing, shuttering, teeth chatter *************** What day is it? *********** Exhale, sniffle, sniffle, and cough ******************** Let me go check up on him and get him ready for his shower. All right, just see what you can do. I really want him there tonight. ******* Stepping closer and closer, door creaking open ******** Okay Nygma let’s get you cleaned up. I don’t need to be cleaned, I feel just fine. ********* Cough ************** Nygma, are you all right? **** Falling glass, slowly shattering, disgruntled look of anguish ***** DOCTOR! DOCTOR! COME IN HERE, HURRY! What’s going on, why is she surprised? ***** Rushes in, deep breath, concern ******* How could this happen, I thought he was sleeping. What happen, what’s going on I’m right here. ***** PUMP…one…two…three…four…PUMP***** My body what are they doing to my body. *FLASH* Come with me. What??? We can be happy it’s better here. No more hate. Hate??? Feel emotions my brother, emotions. Emotions! Ju…. *FLASH* He’s back! Oh thank God, what could have possibly happened, I though he was just sleeping. Don’t ask me, there’s no medical explanation, I’ll have to go through some research in the labs.
Hello???? Good Morning Mr. Nygma, may I get you anything? What happened? Nothing, it just seemed you had a little panic attack, nothing to worry about. The boy…where is the boy? What boy? He had a concussion and a bloody nose; he was in here just the other day. There was no boy like that in here the other day. ********** Confusion ********** Knock…knock. Hi, Tommy bring that water right here. THAT’S HIM, THAT’S HIM. Excuse me. The child yesterday who was in the field unconscious, that’s the child. No, this is Tommy he’s working here this summer. He was never at any soccer field. She’s right I wasn’t. The reason I passed out at the concert the other day. It was because of this kid and how he was hurt.
No you fainted from the extreme heat, there was nothing more to it. I remember…. I remember it so clearly. Maybe you should get some rest. ********* Leaving, door slam ****************** It was so real, the boy reminded me of Ju………. Oh God!
