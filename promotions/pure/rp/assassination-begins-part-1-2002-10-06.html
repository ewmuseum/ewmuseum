Assassination Begins!Part 1 Written: 06/10/02 The Making of A Legend! Part 1 Tuesday, September 10, 2002 Harlem, New York ************************************************ Make this a rule, before you have a child, you must have the proper finances before delivering a child into the world. On April 15th/1978, A young couple that went by the names of Stephen Scott and Abby Moore. It was a sea section surgery, which lasted only thirteen-minutes. They named the young boy Orlando, after Abby’s father. In about three days young Orlando and Abby were released from St. Mary’s hospital in New York. Stephen Scott, the father or Orlando had no attentions on accepting the duties of becoming a father as a teenager, so he parted from Abby. Abby was a strong woman, never complained about anything. Abby and Orlando began their lives at the home of Abby’s parents. A small semi home, which was located in a rough area of New York. Abby kept young Orlando isolated from the outdoors, due to the rough streets of Harlem.
Orlando showed signs of intelligence young, he was crawling, walking and talking above the expected age. When Orlando was two, he was able to write his own name, which was rare at that age. He became anti-social to the other children on the schoolyard, which came from his father’s side of the family. Abby also never allowed Orlando to associate with any of the neighborhood kids, hoping he will not pick up any bad habits along the way.
Orlando drifted through school with ease. Never being top of his class, but never being the lowest student. It was not until high school he broke out of his anti-social shell. Orlando was getting a lot of attention from the opposite sex. Orlando based most of his youth years, lifting weights in his grandfather’s weight room, located in the basement of the small home. After several years of working out, his hard work finally had begun to pay off. Orlando’s body was above average, having a great muscular build to show for his hard work. He did not play many sports, but he was always interested in wrestling, which is why he joined the school team. Orlando excelled in wrestling with ease, becoming well known around the school boards, under his weight class. He graduated at the age of eighteen, and decided to not proceed with his education, and stay in town with his girlfriend, and join the navy.
Life was finally beginning to shift for the young Orlando. He now has his own apartment with his new fiancée, Holly. Both at the age of twenty, they had already achieved their life long goals. Holly was already a nurse, and Orlando was a Navy Seal. Orlando was now a weapon, a weapon not to be used unless in a battle formation. He was living life to his fullest, Holly and Orlando were to be wed the following summer.
They were joined in holy matrimony on July 21st/1998. They had a child on the way, and their financial situations were doing great, so they decided to move into a small home. A great home to start a young family. The child was born seven months after the wedding. It was a small baby boy, Sebastian. Just when Orlando thought life was perfect, the unexpected occurred. Holly was gunned down at a shooting raid, which was between some bikers. This was above all the worst time for this passing. Young Sebastian was only at the age of two. Orlando and his mother Abby were parting every day, more and more. Orlando and his son Sebastian moved back into a small apartment. Finances were beginning to shift in the opposite direction. Work was fading.
Years passed, with a low budget. The two were able to get by, Orlando expressing all the love he can into his son. The two were very close, Sebastian now hitting the age of six. On that same year, Orlando let his actions get the best of him one night, which costs him his job in the Navy. At a nearby bar, he was surrounded by a pile of drunks, and without thinking, engaged a powerful blow to the opponents neck, making the man a vegetable for his remaining lifetime. Orlando was let off reasonably easy, just the loss of his job, and a short time in prison.
Orlando now at the age of twenty-four. Orlando and his son Sebastian lived in a small trailer located at the Cedar Ridge Trailer Park. Orlando called it a temporary substitution for the two. But this substitution will be permanent, as Orlando had no attempt of getting a job. Orlando was now only good at one thing, using his body as a weapon, which he believed that time had passed. Although he did not realize there were alternative options to having a skill such as his. Which was brought to his attention by his son, by giving his father a flyer that he received off the Internet from school. He had watched his father at work, and he knew he would become a great professional wrestler, if given the effort. Sebastian knew his father had the physical ability for the game, but the mental ability was still to come.
Another year passed, with the PURE flyer still resting on the coffee stand. At this time, Orlando was tired of unemployment. He wanted a better life for himself and betters yet his son. He had finally applied to the academy, which was granted and he was officially a rookie in professional wrestling. Orlando was still in his muscular build, just needed to rust of the basics. He had prepared for this for a few months, now he has notified the academy he is ready for battle, and only time will tell if this young talent has what it takes.
********************************************** ==Begin Trans == A muggy day is up us in the trailer parks of New York. The gray clouds surround the entire sky, which is mixed with rain, which has later on excelled to fog. The sound of music blaring kids screaming and people yelling, were the usual sounds of a trailer park lifestyle. The yellow painted trailer was the home of the Scott’s. The trailer was beginning to rust, which is why Sebastian and Orlando painted the entire trailer yellow, to stand out. The two have been doing lots of things together lately, since the signing into PURE. The two had never been so close since Holly was alive. Orlando and Sebastian have been playing catch, been swimming and other recreational programs, two keep them both active.
On this particular day, on the faded plaid couch, that rests in front of the 13’’ television. Sebastian lies, while watching television. Orlando in the bedroom, doing squats to be ready for the PURE. Most of the days were passed by this routine, but they would spend at least two hours playing outside together.
Life was beginning to seem a little better for young Sebastian, now that his father has got off the couch and has a goal again, which is not only to be a successful wrestler, but also to be on the top of the charts. With all the training Orlando has received over the years, it would seem to be unfair for his opponents, due to his Navy Seal tactics. Orlando was granted the permission from the state to wrestle in the PURE.
After Orlando had his long work out session, he joined Sebastian on the couch. In between commercials they socialized.
“ So champ, how has been the first few days of school?” Sebastian slowly looks away from the television. Getting more comfortable in the spring popping couch, also known as his bed.
“ I guess it’s alright dad, it’s school! A great teacher, she is young, so she is not a bitch yet or anything. Like last year, Mrs. Honeywell was a cranky old bitch. Thought it was funny to get my friends and I in trouble. Thank god she died in the summer!”
Orlando shakes his head. “ Sebastian never say that about a human being. Believe me all teachers have a brutal side to them also, wait until you get a little more into the school year, then the truth comes out!”
“ Well enough about me dad, so is that new job in PURE going to get us out of this hellhole? How much is it paying?”
“ Well it’s a feeder federation, not until I am scouted by a big fed I make big money, it’s low paying for now. But this academy will prepare me for the worst in the ring. Once I get a few matches in, I will settle in and be ready for the bigger opponents in the bigger federations. “
“ Well I believe in you dad, just do what you were taught as an Navy Seal! You will do fine, I have high hopes for you dad.”
The rest of the day consisted of watching television, playing a neighborhood baseball game and renting a few movies for the night. Sebastian hit the bed early because he was preparing for school in the morning. Which left the television off limits because Sebastian’s bed was directly in front of the television. Orlando finished his day off with a long run, and then hit the bed, getting ready for another day, which consisted of the same thing. Just a week or so away from his debut as a wrestler, and he feels more ready then he has ever been in his whole life. Orlando needs this profession to succeed not only for him, but his son. He wants his son to have a life he was unable to have, which means he needs to make a name for himself. So there is only one more step to stardom, the question is, will Orlando Scott take it?
************************************************ The Next Day A better day is ahead, the clouds are still up, but have calmed down compared from yesterday. It nears noon, Sebastian has already disappeared to school hours ago. They live within walking distance from Sebastian’s public school. Orlando usually wakes up around this time, it is usually the sunlight seeping through the blinds of the trailer that wakes Orlando up. But lately those days were only a dream, so it was usually extinct. But today he was woken up by a knock of the trailer screen door.
Orlando not happy when he in woken up, he usually enjoys waking up himself. He slowly makes his way to the door, makes sure he is decent by throwing a shirt on with his boxers. He stays inside the trailer, but on the other side of the screen door of the visitor. He notices the young adult, with a clipboard and a cameraman standing next to her. He then realized he is getting his introduction interview, to let the fans know whom they are dealing with. He invites the interviewer in, and the cameraman. It is a tight squeeze with the three in the trailer, as it is a single person home, it was tight enough with just Sebastian and Orlando.
Orlando goes back to his room, shutting the sliding door that separates his room from the living room. He puts some blue jeans and his plain white shirt. He does not have to worry about his hair, because it is buzzed with just bristles on his head. His slimming body and his muscular arms stand out more because of that. He would obviously fit under the middle weight class. Orlando has a bit of a tan, but is fairly white skinned and in ruggedly good looking.
The interview takes place on the plaid couch, and the cameraman sets up in front of the television. The interview introduces herself and her cameraman.
“ Hello there, I am Ann. I work for the PURE as a lead interviewer. And this is my cameraman Charley. Were just going to ask you a series of introduction questions, so do not be alarmed.”
Orlando nods his head, addressing that he is set for his introduction. Ann gives the cue to Charley and they begin the interview.
********************************************* ==Begin Interview == “ Hello sports fans, I am here with newly discovered PURE wrestler Orlando Scott. He has plenty of experience with physical contact with an opponent. He was once a Navy Seal. A skill that is beyond tactics, but he was given the okay to compete in the wrestling world, and here he is, Orlando.”
Orlando sweating a bit, as he has never been on the spotlight before, especially on television.
“ I am happy to be here, I was happy about the states decision on letting me wrestle even though I have been a Navy Seal. I don’t believe you should be held back. Because I have learned Navy school tactics, does not mean I did not work hard to get the skill. The tactics are not easy to mater, even when taught. I was top in my Navy class, and in a few I will prove my talent, once I get into the ring.”
“ And this also has been brought to attention, you have a young son Sebastian.” “ Yes he is a great kid, in fact he is the one who motivated me to become a wrestler. I joined to show my talent, but money is also key, I hope to advance in the WT federation, and make money so I can give my son the life he deserves.”
“ Well that is just the strategy that may just put you over top of your opponent, A goal is always key. Well it was great getting to know you Orlando, And I cannot wait to see you in action, It should be a thriller.”
==End Interview == *********************************************** Ann wraps up her things, and they all say their good-byes. Once Ann and the cameraman drive off, Orlando goes back into the house, and gets back into his boxers and goes back to bed. Orlando may have the strategy of giving his son a better life, but can he perform with these other upcoming stars? Only time will tell.
