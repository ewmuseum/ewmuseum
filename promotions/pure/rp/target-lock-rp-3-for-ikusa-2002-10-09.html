Target lock (Rp#3 for Ikusa)

9 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Target lock (Rp#3 for Ikusa) Written: 10/9/02 Exit 13, I can see it clear as day on the large, green and white sign to my right. That is my exit. I cut over two lanes of traffic and a bunch of Neanderthal skid to a stop behind me with accompanying blares of their vehicles' horns. I travel a short freshly paved road and the forth one is exactly what I want to see. I stomp the gas pedal of my Ford and power slide into the turn, narrowly missing the front end of an oncoming automobile. More horn blaring followed, as I sped down the empty street. I went for miles with out seeing a prehistoric dwelling. It was like my target did not want to be bothered by anyone. After about ten minutes, I finally notice a massive house in the distance. I slowly let my Ford creep up on the front gate and I examine the nameplate on the right side of the gate.
'Clinton,' I read. The name rings through my mind and all the knowledge that I have of the horror my target caused, slowly creates a rage deep in my soul. I can barely contain the fury, as I pull my vehicle into a small patch of open land just a quarter mile from the dwelling. I slowly creep back up to the property and make my way into a set of thick hedges. I lay on my stomach and crawl deep enough into the bushes to get a clear view of the area. I reach into a pocket of my trench coat and pull out a pair of binoculars. With them pressed against my eyes, I carefully examine the area. A pair of men in black suits patrols the front door. There are three more pairs at the back door, and each side door. Three single guards are carefully guarding the front gate. This place is sealed tight. I slowly creep out of the hedge and stealthily rush over to the opposite side to see if there is an easier way to get in. I look down at the contraption on my wrist. I am so ahead of the deadline that I could go back and buy more clothes, with still hours to spare. With the area scouted, I silently make my way back to my transportation. I take off my trench coat and toss it across the hood of my vehicle. There is nothing to do but wait. For me this is the most strenuous portion of my mission. I have never been one to just sit around and wait for things. Whoever said, 'good things come to those who wait,' was obviously a slacker. Minutes seem to turn into days and I do everything in my power to make the time go by faster, but with no luck. Finally after several hours, I look down at my wrist and see the time to strike has descended upon me. I tighten my boots, make sure my Neostryker is activated, and slip my arms into my black trench coat. I gaze into the rear view mirror and give myself a sinister grin.
'Time to do what I love,' I say to myself. Like a shadow in the darkness, I travel up to the perimeter wall of hedges around the property. I slither through a small opening in the plant life and quickly duck behind a tree, as a free roaming guard makes his way towards me. Just as he steps past me, I creep out from behind the tree and grab him from behind. With a quick twist of his head, I snap his neck like a twig. Making sure no one would notice his lifeless body, I dragged him behind the tree and propped him up against it, just out of sight. One down. I have decided to make my way in from the side. It is much easier and safer than trying a frontal assault. Using basic guerilla tactics, I obtain a rock and toss it into the hedges just off to the right of the pair of guards. As expected, one of the fools goes to find out what the sound was and leaves the other all by his lonesome. I examine my foe closely and see that he looks like one of those anal-retentive guys, who is too caught up in his job to have a social life. Therefore, I know exactly how to get his attention. I mess with my shirt and then, close my jacket. Right out in the open, I approach the lone guard. He is shocked to see anyone.
'Identify yourself,' he orders loudly, 'or I will shoot.' 'Sure thing, stud,' I respond back. 'However, you wouldn't want to shoot these would you?'
I open my jacket widely and expose my bare breasts to the security guard. His eyes open widely and his jaw drops to the floor. A sucker is born every minute. As he stares at my cleavage, I slowly remove my Neostryker and place a softball-sized hole in his chest. I let out a small chuckle and pull my shirt down, as I walk past his still convulsing body. I head towards the complex's side entrance. I stop in my tracks, as the sound of footsteps is heard coming from my right. In the blink of an eye, I am out of the open and leaning up against a pillar. I stand perfectly still and then a thought hits my mind like a ton of bricks. I peek around the pillar and notice the guard's lifeless body still in plain site.
'Sh*t,' I quietly exclaim. Now, things may get a bit tougher. 'What the hell,' the returning guard questions. 'Jackson...' I hear the static of his communicator and spin out from behind the pillar. Without a moment to aim, I fire off a shot that blows away the communicator and part of the guards hand. Luck is on my side. Thank God because I cannot afford to have him alert his fellow guards to my presence. He cries out in pain, as blood shoots from his destroyed hand. He screams twice before I make him quiet, forever. With a thud, his body hits the floor. I can over hear his communicator and the being on the other end asking for the now deceased guard to respond. Things have just gotten a little worse. I rush to the side entrance and luckily there is no one in sight, as I enter the building. Quiet as a church mouse, I gingerly walk up the hallway. My Neostryker is ready to blow a hole in anything that even thinks about popping out in front of me. I hear footsteps. They are coming from the hallway on my left. I quickly slide into the closest room and peek my head out to see who the person is. It is the maid. Totally unaware of my presence, I easily walk up behind her and knock her out with one stiff blow to the back of her skull. I catch her, as she falls and drag her into an empty room. Moments later, I re-emerge from the room. I know I am not a duplicate for the ugly, old maid, but it is better to have a disguise then to stand out like a sore thumb. I slowly make my way up down the hallway, with my weapon hidden in my outfit. Too bad they don't have taller maids in this damn place. This outfit is like 2 sizes too small. I fight to pull the skirt down enough to cover my rear end, as I approach an open door. A myriad of sounds radiate from the room and the sound of a deep voice laughing is also heard.
'Now, that is some funny stuff,' the deep voice echoes. I do my best to try to avoid being seen, as I pass the door, but walking in high heels doesn't make help matters.
'Lupe, is that you,' the voice calls out with a southern accent. 'Come in here, now. I need you help.'
Damn. Nothing ever goes as I plan it. I put on my best airhead persona and bounced myself into the room.
'Yes, sir,' I responded. There he was. The biggest pig, I had ever seen in my life, Bill Clinton. He sat in a recliner with a saxophone at his side and a hand in his pants. A cigar was still smoking in the ashtray and his abdomen was covered in broken potato chips. I quickly glanced to the crude entertainment machine and saw the beginning of a show entitled, 'Green Acres.' I looked back at the Neanderthal and saw him drooling, as he stared at me with a glazed look in his eyes.
'Who are you, darling' he asked. 'Lupe was sick, so the service sent me to replace her for tonight,' I respond. He rises up out of his chair and walks towards me, with a crazy look in his eyes. I back pedal, trying to keep him at an arm’s length away. He chases me around the room, without saying a word. Just with a frightening look in his eyes. I did my best to stay away, but fell into the recliner. He was approaching fast and I knew this could bring a new meaning to the phrase, 'bumpin' uglies,' if he were to get his filthy paws on me. Therefore, I improvised like my training taught me. Out of his line of sight, I obtain his saxophone and clench it tightly in my hand. Once he is within reach, I blast the Neanderthal right in the side of his head, with his musical instrument. Blood splatters against my disguise, as I repeatedly smack him in the cranium with his instrument. When his body stops flinching from the pain, I throw down the saxophone and look down at my victim.
'You are to blame for the future, as much as your wife is,' I say with pure anger coursing through my veins. 'You are beyond a Neanderthal.'
I kick off my high heels and let my long golden locks drop out of my pigtails. As I quietly close the door behind me, I look down at my wrist and see that I still have some time to spare. I have to find Hillary Rodham Clinton. She is the reason I have come back. She is the one who started the third world war. The war that nearly destroyed everyone and everything. Men always feared a woman president and I would never have believed it either, if I didn't know what the first one did. From what I learned, people could tell right away that something was horribly wrong hen she stepped into power. She ceased talks with various nations that would not comply with our demands and laws. Then, she did the unthinkable. She instigated a war with China; due to them being one of the last communist countries. The domino effect brought every country into the fray and eventually, the entire world was one big mass of strife and death. Nature was nearly eradicated from the world from all the radiation. Now is my time. My time to save the world from a horrible fate. I searched over the entire house, destroying several guards in the process, and yet, still no sign of my target. I am just done splattering Chelsea's brain all over her bedroom, when fate smiles down upon me. I look down from the second floor and see the front door open. I cannot tell you the hatred I felt, as I looked upon her face. I want to rip her still beating heart from her chest and hold it in front of her, so she can see how black it truly is. On each side of her, a security guard stands. I look down at my timer and realize that two hours have been taken off.
'What the hell' I ask myself. Then, it dawns on me. With the killing of Bill Clinton or Chelsea, I may have altered the near future. Too bad it is not in my favor. Now, with no time to waste, I am forced to do things the messy way. I look down at the b*tch.
'I, Ashley Maximoff,' I scream, 'find thee, Hillary Rodham Clinton, guilty of the future death of billions!'
'What the hell,' Hillary says in shock. I sprint towards the banister and somersault over it. After one full rotation, I annihilated the guard to the right with a well-placed shot to the throat, decapitating him. My feet land securely upon the floor and I look up at my target from a crouching position. The remaining security guard draws his gun from inside his jacket and like a flash, I spin kick the gun out of his hands. He drops to his knees in fear, as I point my Neostryker at his face.
'That's a good boy,' I say arrogantly. 'You make one move and this will be your last night on Earth.'
I catch Mrs. Clinton trying to escape, out of the corner of my eye. With one blast, I drop her to the floor. She clutches at her right leg and blood flows though her fingers. I take a step to go after my target and the guard tries to be a hero. He quickly hops up to his feet and grabs me in a hug from behind. My arms are pinned to my body, but I am not panicking. One sudden whip of my head backwards and I dim the guard's running lights. He releases me and staggers back, with his hands covering his nose. I silence his cries of pain with a single shot to his chest. I hear his body hit the floor, as I go after my prey. Mrs. Clinton tries to slide her injured body away from me and leaves a trail of crimson life for me to follow. I stand over her whimpering body.
'Please, I will give you anything you want,' she cries with tears streaking down her eyes. 'Just do not kill me!'
She could offer me the universe on a platter and I would have spit in her face. She deserves to die, before she can ever get the chance to do fate has destined her to. I look at my timer and notice that I have two minutes. Like I said before, 'I am no slacker.' With one final blast, the future is saved from its greatest horror. My mission is complete. Now, I can go on to the new world that awaits me in the future. I go to blow the smoke from the nozzle of my gun and something amazing occurs. My gun vanishes into thin air. I cannot believe my eyes. The future definitely is changing. I guess they didn't invent the weapon in the new future. I look to my wrist and watch the timer dissolve into nothingness. My heart nearly jumps up my throat. This cannot be. That timer was the transmitter for them to find me. With it gone, I cannot get back home.
'I cannot be trapped in the Stone Age,' I say to myself in disbelief. The realization hits me and my emotions go over board. 'No,' I scream into the night at the top of my lungs. 'No!!!' Fade to black.
