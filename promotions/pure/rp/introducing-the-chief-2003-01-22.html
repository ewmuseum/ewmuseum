Introducing the Chief

22 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introducing the Chief Written: January 22, 2003 Someone once told me that no matter how big you got, how tough you got, or how old you got… no child could EVER beat up their dad.
Of course, this isn’t true. There are many situations that could prove this statement wrong. However, in my case, this was true. Despite being in the best physical condition of my life, I could never get the best of my father.
Then again, it was just play fighting. My dad has always been my one and only trainer. I owe all the success I’ve gotten thus far to him. Still, whenever we tangle, whether it be at a gym, or just around the house, I can’t help but think that he taught me everything I know… not everything HE knows.
Today, we’d go at it again. Round 115. And once again, I would try to put a end his seemingly endless win streak against me.
My father was twice my age. 50 years old. And his age isn’t the only reason that it’s embarrassing that I can’t beat him. He’s 6’1”, and about 350 lbs. Basically, Kohana, PURE superstar, can’t take out an out of shape old man. Well, it’s embarrassing if you view the stats, anyways.
I cared a great deal for him, but I could only take him in doses, if you catch my drift. He too, was a wrestler in his youth. And because of one too many concussions, most of them ignored… well let’s just say things aren’t quite right in his head. He still has all the know how, though. He can teach me how to apply a near perfect STF, yet he can’t even remember his real name. His real name is Dyami. He refers to himself as Chief Squatting Dan, however. His wrestling persona. Pretty much, your stereotypical Native American.
He’ll also make ludicrous claims, most about his contributions to the wrestling business. Claims like he invented “The Kickout.”
“I invented the kickout!” proclaimed my father, a many different times in my life. “Before me, people get pinned, and they don’t know what to do! So they get pinned! I changed that! I kicked out of pins! It made me unstoppable! I was the man who kicked out of pins!”
He bragged to me often about this, among other things. He would also take every opportunity to remind others of his name and accomplishments, even if they were false. It was rather embarrassing at times. He also claimed pins used to last for a “4” count, but had to be reduced to 3 so someone could finally pin my father. Damn them dad… Damn them.
It was odd behavior like that which would get on my nerves. Still does. If I never left his side, I’d likely be as loony as he.
Still, he managed to raise me on his own, in a poor Native Reserve. I say that as if there’s a RICH one. So I have to give him credit. I also feel like I had to return the favor.
And speaking of returning the favor… It was time to return the favor right now! All 115 favors he game me! RAAAAAAAAA!!! We locked up and he immediately got me in a side headlock. I grabbed at his long black hair, hoping he’d ease up a bit.
I couldn’t see it for myself, but I’d have to think that we’d be a hilarious sight to see right now. We were in a Japanese hotel room, which is a bit smaller than your American hotel. And here we were, two massive men, scrapping in between our two single beds. Not lot of room.
He then took me down with the headlock, and I was trapped underneath him. I could only imagine what the people in the room directly below us were thinking right now. I then balled up, and was able to get a head scissor lock on him, forcing him to break the hold. We then got up at the same instance. He charged at me, but I sidestepped him, and dropped him down face first into the ground… The perfect timing for the STF, or as I called it, “The Swift Kill.”
“This is it!” I thought to myself. “You’re finally going to beat your father!” I locked his leg, bent it forward, and went straight for the face lock! “LOCK IT! LOCK IT!!!” I urged myself.
What would normally take 3 seconds at the most, all seemed to be going in slow motion now, as I was a mere face lock away from forcing my father to submit, for the first time in my life. Then…
Just as I went for that very face lock, he grabbed my arm while I was reaching forward, and locked in a wicked arm bar!
I still had that leg locked, however, and we found ourselves in a double submission predicament. My arm was locked and his leg was locked.
“Give up!” My father yelled. I winced in pain as he said this. “Why don’t you?” I replied. He then burst out laughing as I finished saying that. “Give up to this leg lock? Please. I could withstand this for hours!”
“BULLSHIT!” I retorted, shocked after hearing this. “You’re bluffing!” “I don’t bluff. It doesn’t hurt enough to make me give up… you lost some of the pressure.” He said calmly.
“Well yours doesn’t hurt enough either to make me give up!” I shot back. Of course, I was lying, as it felt like he was going to break my arm… I was simply banking on him bluffing.
So we stayed that while, in a stale mate of sorts. Neither one of us adding or releasing any pressure on our respective holds, for fear of slipping up. Bored of this, I decided to instigate a conversation a little off topic.
“See I got a title shot this week? All Asia belt or something. Yulav’s got a shot at it too… cheating bastard,” I stated.
“Don’t like this man, do you son…” he replied. I chuckled to myself, hearing this. “He’s a cheater! I HATE cheaters! He had to pull my tights to beat me!”
Unfortunately, I couldn’t prevent that from happening in the future, unless I wrestled naked. I already wore the least amount of clothing in the ring as it was!
He didn’t answer me, which was fine by me. My mind was elsewhere now. It wasn’t all on one specific thing, but images of Yulav beating me with a reverse roll up would haunt my thoughts at random times. The nature of his win only made my hate for him swell more.
Why did I hate him so much though? Was it his hate for America? Hell, I could almost relate with the guy about a month ago. Perhaps I hate him because he reminds me of myself, only with much more extreme methods. Do I hate myself?
Or perhaps I just don’t like to be reminded of the sadder times of my life. Something Senji Yulav reminds me off, every time he rants about the wrong doings of my country.
What could possibly be bugging him so much about the United States… that he has to whine to JAPAN about it though?
Or perhaps he would just prefer to rid PURE of me, and my fellow Americans… Perhaps, I don’t give a rat’s ass. “Give up,” my father said, knocking me out of my trance. He surprised me, as I once again became alert, only to feel that sharp pain in my left arm once I moved. The leg lock I once held, was long gone.
“Shit…” I muttered. My father laughed to himself before he spoke. “Never let your guard down, Kohana. If you’re focused, no contemptible acts can defeat you. Let your guard down, and those very acts will be your demise… now give up.”
Despite the pain I was feeling, I had to smile about all this. Realizing I could do nothing to better my situation, I tapped out slowly. He released the hold and helped me up, while I laughed to myself, shaking my head.
