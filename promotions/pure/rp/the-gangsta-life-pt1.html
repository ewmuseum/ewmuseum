The Gangsta life. [pt1]

20 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Gangsta life. [pt1] The scene opens up on a cold rainy night. The new york streets are so quiet, that you can hear a pin fall. There are lots of building's going all the way down the block, across the street there is a parking lot with a bunch of old dirty car's, There is one car that stands out, Its a black pathfinder jeep, with tinted windows. The head lights are on. The driver door of the jeep opens up and smoke comes out of the car. Then a young man who looks to be in his 20's emerges out the jeep. He is wearing a red t-shirt with blue jeans, and on top of his head he has a red bandanna, and a black and red 76er's hat that he wears tilted to the right. You can also see that there are two other people in the car. By this time the smoke is all cleared. He enter's back in the car and closes the door. They begin to speak while in the car 'Yo. Why we still here?' One of the other guy's in the car asked. 'Why don't you shut the fuck up.' PosterBoy replies. 'Damn, It's boring like fuck up in here.' The other guy in the back scream's out. 'I am going to put some music on, so you guy's can calm down.' PosterBoy say's. PosterBoy turns on the music, and lower's it so that they still can speak. 'When is he going to get out the building?' PosterBoy ask's the two guy's in the back. 'You act like we up in the shit with him. We don't know!' The two men in the back reply with a sarcastic tone of voice.
'I can't believe this shit! Pass me the glock [9mm] so I can go inside and get him.' PosterBoy shout's out. One of the men in the back lift there shirt up and takes the gun out of his boxer's and passes it to PosterBoy. PosterBoy quickly grabs the gun, he cocks it back and rushed out the jeep. He doesn't even close the door cause he is in a rush, He begins to walk to one of the taller buildings. He begins to cross the street but is stopped in his track's because he just heard 5 shot's ring out. He begins to panic and look's around to see where the shot's came from. Then the door of the building that he was walking too quickly swing's open. Then PosterBoy's friend comes out limping holding his leg, with his hand full of blood PosterBoy begins to quickly speak's confused. 'What the fuck, happen Jay?' He asked in a scared tone of voice. 'Ahh he shot me!.' Jay replies to PosterBoy with a look of pain on his face. 'Shit. let's get out of here.' PosterBoy tell's Jay. 'Take me to the hospital.' Jay tell's Poster. 'Ok. I know.' Poster replies. PosterBoy and Jay quickly rush to the jeep. PosterBoy get's into the car while Jay limp's to the other side of the passenger seat, He open's the door, and they begin to speak while in the car. 'What happen to Jay?' The two men in the back ask. 'He got shot in the leg.' Poster replies to the two men in the back of the car. 'OH NO! We going to die' One of the men in the back scream's out. 'Shut the fuck up and let me think!' Poster screams out 'Can we hurry up?' Jay asked. 'Why you such in a hurry.' Poster replies. 'I got shot in the leg. And I am still alive, what make's you think that he didn't want to kill me?' Jay asked Poster.
'I don't know, why?' PosterBoy asks. 'Cause I killed him first.' Jay tell's PosterBoy in a scared tone of voice. 'Ah shit, we going to die!' All three men scream out except Jay. 'Let's Go!' Jay screams. PosterBoy turns on the car and zooms out the parking lot. In the background you can hear police siren's getting closer. The black jeep quickly head's to the hospital, while the camera fades to black -Scene ends-
