The Warrior Scandal Part One: Wrestling with Reason... Written: 12/3/03 The Warrior Scandal Part One: Wrestling with Reason. The die was cast. The decision had been made. The Tag Team formally known as Justice Incorporated have parted ways with the mighty wrestling organisation known LOW. Why? That was the question many of their fans inquired. Simple. When they entered the world of wrestling, they did so simply as stunt. A stunt that would allow them to publicise their business as professional superheroes. However, there was no longer any need of such publicity stunt.
Justice Inc had gained enough popularity to gain the company work for the rest of their careers. Therefore, wrestling was no long an element in the equation. Yes, Warrior and Blind Rage would miss the valiant sport, they felt the rush of adrenaline every time they stepped out in front of a live audience and every time they stepped into the ring.
It was debatable whether this rush could be left behind altogether, but the team of Justice Inc had to try, their company was the most important thing to them at this juncture. Blind Rage was undoubtedly reluctant at first to simply quit wrestling altogether, but Warrior managed to talk him around. The problem with wrestling was, it seriously eat into their free time, forcing them to turn down some jobs. This placed the public at a great risk and Blind Rage could not argue against that point.
Blind Rage stood on the pine wooden floor panelling, within the luxurious Justice Incorporated lounge area, simply staring out of the window, contemplating his life, the future and his wrestling career. Or at least what was his wrestling career. He was so engrossed in these thoughts he didn’t even hear The Handbag Warrior enter the room.
“What’s up sugah, still havin’ a hard time givin’ up you’re wrestling’?”, Handbag Warrior asked, his high, Mississippi twang making Blind Rage jump a little.
“I’m sorry Daniel I didn’t hear you come in. What did you say I again?”, Blind Rage replied, with a frown on his face.
“I said, are you still having a hard time givin’ up you’re wrestlin’ thing?” “Indeed. The craving for the sport will produce a deep hole within my heart, hell it already has. It didn’t seem so at the time, but the adrenaline rush each time I step into that ring is unnaturally addictive!”
“Addictive? Wrestling is just a couple of guys rolling around on the mat half naked! I…actually yeah, I can see why it could be addictive!”
“Is that all you think about? Sex?” “I’m a man aren’t I?” “Yeah, but you’re a homosexual!” “I’m still a man though. My little soldier still makes all of my decisions for me!” “That could possibly be the worst mental image I’ve had since Warrior got his balls stuck in his zip.”
As if by cue, Blind Rage’s former tag team partner and current Justice Inc team member entered the lounge area, via the door from the reception. Blind Rage and Handbag Warrior simply stared at him as he sat on the Zebra-skin couch. Warrior noticed them staring at him and consequently developed a confused look upon his face.
“What?”, he questioned. “You caught your balls in your zip?”, Handbag Warrior asked, with a devious smile on his face.
Warrior stood up from the couch, with anger and looked at Blind Rage intently. He raised his arm and pointed at Blind Rage. “You swore to me that you wouldn’t say a word!”, Warrior shouted with rage in his voice.
“I’m sorry it just slipped out. I was simply reminiscing about the good ol’ days on the road and the memory of you catching you balls in your jean after our first ever match instantly popped into my head.”
“Still can’t get over the crave of adrenaline huh? I sorted that out a couple of weeks ago when quitting the wrestling industry became a realistic option.”
“Really? What did you do?” “I…huh…I…can’t tell you that!” “Why what is it, sugah? What takes the place of the adrenaline rush for you now, hon?”, The Handbag Warrior quizzed.
“It’s none of your damn business, man!”, Warrior replied, getting very cagey indeed. Blind Rage walked over to Warrior with a concerned look upon his face, taking his former tag team partner by the shoulders. “Warrior tell it to me straight. Don’t lie to me. Are you taking some kind of drug? Is that what you’ve replaced the adrenaline rush with?”
“No! It’s not!”, Warrior replied with conviction. “Tell us the truth, hon!”, The Handbag Warrior demanded. “That is the truth, you limp wrested bastard!”, Warrior replied with venom in his voice. “No you’re not!”, Blind Rage said. “Yes I am. You just won’t accept that the truth doesn’t involve my intake of drugs!”, Warrior replied.
“Well if it’s not drugs sugah, why don’t you just tell us what you’ve compensated the adrenaline rush with?”, The Handbag Warrior asked, looking extremely confused.
“Because it’s no business of yours! It’s my business.”, Warrior replied, leaving the room in a huff.
“Well…that went well.”, Blind Rage stated sarcastically, a stern look etched upon his face.
“Mwhahahahahah…mwha…mwha…mwhahahahahahahahahahah…” the loud, insane laugh bellowed, echoing through the entire room.
“What is it o’ master?”, the soft voice asked intently. “Well Willy, I’ve devised a plan so devious you could stick a dress on it and call it a ‘woman’.”, The ‘Outrageous’ Owl-Man declared.
He was stood in front of a large metal console, which took up the majority of the back wall, with an even bigger monitor taking up the wall adjacent to it. His fingers glided over the controls, as the grin on his face began to get wider and wider. The ‘Outrageous’ Owl-Man is dressed in a giant owl costume, and therefore found it quite difficult to press the console buttons. Willy is a lot smaller than the owl, in fact he’s a midget in a mouse costume.
“Why don’t you just take the damn wings off ya moron?”, Willy muttered under his breath. “What did you say to me?”, Owl-Man bellowed at the top of his voice. “I said, ‘what is your plan sire?’”, Willy replied, with a grin on his face. “Well, little Willy, with the information from the bug I placed within the Justice Incorporated building, I will render the company out of order with the general public, and then I will destroy them!”, Owl-Man declared, triumphantly.
“Wile E. Coyote would come up with a better plan than that!”, Willy muttered, again under his breath.
“What did you say?”, Owl-Man asked, eyeing up Willy once more. “I said, ‘It’s a great plan o’ master.’”, Willy replied. “Very good little Willy. That will be all for today.”, Owl-Man said, turning his back on his miniscule friend.
