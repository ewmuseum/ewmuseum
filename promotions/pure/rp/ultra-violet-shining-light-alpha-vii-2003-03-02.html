Ultra Violet Shining Light Alpha- VII

2 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ultra Violet Shining Light Alpha- VII Written: Sunday, the 2nd of March Narrated by Minori Kumako. Akoshi stood angered. He had just watched Yukio, his own brother, steal me away from him in his black sedan. He clenched his fists tightly together, turning his olive skinned knuckles into a ghostly white. The anger etched on his face was truly frightening. Prohibited curses of the Kunamara family were thrown through the air as Akoshi kicked part of the flowerbed into oblivion, pink and white petals bursting into the air. They spattered against the damp bricks. An angered, young Japanese man had now vanquished the former beauty of the flowers in a swift kick. The soles of his boot trampled the deformed petals as he stepped a thousands steps forward at once.
‘Why?’ Akoshi said to himself softly whilst his dark brown eyes travelled down to the crushed petals on the ground. Akoshi realized he had just kicked his Great Grandmothers famed flower garden, a somewhat of a legacy in the old Kunamara family. ‘Why, why does Yukio have to do this to me? I do not care that he has brought me back to this… this prison, but he has taken Minori from me. Why must he be this way?’
Akoshi sighed, stepping forward a little bit toward a nearby bench. His body sat down on the damp wood, resting itself for a brief few seconds. While I was being stolen from him, in the backseat of Yukio’s car, Akoshi was left in a void of nothing all alone on the island he hates so much.
‘There he is,’ a group of voices shouted out, coming outside of a black door. Akoshi barely heard them, his head held in his hands. Akoshi did not turn his attention to the advancing group of henchmen, ignoring them at the fullest of his power. ‘GET HIM!’
Akoshi finally recognized their pitiful existence, slowly standing to his unwanted feet. The leather soles of the henchmen’s feet tapped against ground, but there was the sound of more coming. They all smashed the bones within their skin encased knuckles together, cracking the airwaves. At once they pounce, lashing a set of five punches into Akoshi’s ribs. He tried to fit some of them off, using all his strength to Mule kick one of the henchmen.
Another stray kick landed in his side, but he wasn’t afraid to use his wrestling training to fight his way through the sea of black suited arms and legs. A few spin kicks later and two of the gutless, spineless henchmen were flat on their backs holding onto their noses. It didn’t how they fell, as long as they fell.
I don’t care how I have to beat them, Akoshi thought to himself in silence, ducking an attempted Roundhouse kick to the side of his cranium. There were only one more henchmen he could see left, another had just had their dreams of ending his life abruptly ended. I was glad I was not there, the sight of one man fighting off a gang of dogs is just not pleasant. The last of the gang of henchmen fell with a thud, holding onto his head, ignoring the flow of blood from his broken nose.
Around the corner came a fast approaching Suzuki Motorcycle, ridden by another one of Yukio’s many, many henchmen. Akoshi saw him coming, and didn’t hesitate to use his training in the fighting arts to kick the rider in the face, causing him to crash off his Motorcycle and into a nearby flower patch.
‘Minori,’ Akoshi said to himself softly, looking at the downed Motorcycle. He pulled up the Bike by the handles and climbed up on top, adjusting the spare Helmet around his head. ‘Minori, I must find you… at any costs.’
My only concern is will he reach me in time before Yukio can do anything ghastly to me?
