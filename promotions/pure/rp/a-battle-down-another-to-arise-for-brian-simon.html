A battle down, another to arise for Brian Simon

15 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A battle down, another to arise for Brian Simon .........a day after Ikuza.....a day after a battle.......a day after a defeat. As the voice fades, the face appears, towel wrapped around neck, hair freshly dried from a hot shower. The face slowly arises, the face not of a defeated man, but the face of a wrestler. The face of a warrior in the squared circle and a face of a man These qualities represented myself in my PURE debut, but I did not let them daunt me. I am a ever changed individual, and the event that transpired on Ikuza will not deter my ability. I gave it my all, yet in the end, I was not the better man in this battle of sorts. But for the first time in a long time, I did feel something good, that is being appreciated. The appreciation factor led me to more appreciation. The appreciation I give to my opponents on Ikuza, Kimbell and Windsor, who both truly gave a fine effort in the ring. We all showcased our own abilities to the fans of PURE, and they responded well by it. Hopefully the officials in PURE also recognized this, as being gaijins here in PURE, we are sometimes midjudged in our reasons for being here. But each one of us gaijins came here to PURE not to become superstars for the most part, but to gain the respect of a international audience and where our talents would be appreciated no matter what. I feel some pain after my defeat. My back aches and my muscles hurt, but one thing that will not be taken from me is my pride. My pride and passion for wrestling is something that burned inside me ever since my youth days in England. The path that took me to PURE was a long and tiring one, but I have found friends in PURE, and I need no enemies. Next Ikuza though, I do get a chance to shine in the first ever PURE Tag Team Tourney, to crown the first ever PURE Tag Team Champions. And I do have the honor in teaming with a man who defeated me less than 24 hours ago. Steven Windsor, I have all the respect in the world for you, as yesterday, you were the better individual, but now we put our talents together. I looked over your bio the other day, and I say we did have a few shared practices. First off, we're about the same age, in our mid 20s. We both stand in the upper 6 foot range, as you do have the slight height and weight advantage. Then take our finishers, basically the same, as only great minds would think alike for a finisher. So you see, Windsor, I hope you do listen to me, and I hope we do both work together, because with our great wrestling ways, we can succeed in the tournament and be crowned not only the first ever PURE Tag Team Champions, but the first ever gaijins to win a title in PURE. Rest on that, and let your conscience do the rest.
