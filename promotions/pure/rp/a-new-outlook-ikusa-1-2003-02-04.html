A New Outlook (Ikusa #1) Written: 2/4/03 It is a gorgeous day in Wuhan, China today. The sun is out, the temperature is mild, and Noi's green house is beginning to bloom. Well, actually, it's not Noi's; its his grandfather's. Attached to the back of the house is a small patio with a few chairs and a small table located against the railing. There are two steps branching off of the patio to the backyard which still looks quite fertile and healthy.
The yard is full of plants and trees, that for some reason, have not been wrecked or even tampered with. In the far end of the yard, kneeling under a tree, rests Noi. He has his hands rested in his lap and his eyes closed, as if he's meditating. Certainly a side of Enmity we've never seen.
'Like the wind,' he murmers to himself as he takes a deep breath in. The sliding door on the back of the house as an elder makes his way out onto the patio with a watering can. He looks out in Noi's direction and smiles as he begins to water his shrubs. A strong breeze comes by, waving the old man's scarce hair and making Noi open his eyes.
'Noi, my boy!' The eldery man calls out and Noi rises from his kness. He looks very different today, his hair pulled back in a ponytail, a long sleeve tee shirt, blue jeans, and he isn't sporting any shoes.
'Be right there,' he calls back to the man, before taking another deep breath and making his way to the house.
'So...' the old man lets out touching Noi's face and examining it. 'So...?' Noi questions. 'So, is the meditating working better than the pills?' He asks looking concerned for Noi's well-being.
'I don't know, grandpa. I'm a lot more aware without them, and with the meditation the pain and symptoms DO go away.' He tells his grandfather before heading back into the house.
His grandfather follows him and they head to the living room where Noi puts on the television. A commercial for the next PURE Ikusa airs, which shows Enmity being pumelled by Mandrake. Noi clenches his fist and hurls the remote at the television.
'What's wrong now, my son?' His grandfather asks bringing a tray of tea into the room. 'I still feel no one takes me seriously. I still believe that because I'm a damn manic-depressive...I'm treated like a joke!' Noi's voice raises as his grandfather senses anger growing.
'Calm down, calm down. You are not a joke. You are one of PURE's top talents, believe me. Everyone respects you...' He tells Noi before heading back into the kitchen. 'I'll be right back.'
Noi leans forward resting his chin on his cupped hands. 'Bullshit...they don't respect. But they will...FEAR ME!'
