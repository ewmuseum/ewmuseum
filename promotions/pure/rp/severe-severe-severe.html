Severe...Severe...Severe....

18 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Severe...Severe...Severe.... The scene opens up where we last left off. Sapporo, Japan. It's the Sapporo General Hospital. We see a small office. It's cluttered with papers, and empty coffee cups. It smells like smoke. The doctor is sitting down, reviewing some papers; he's also on the phone. From the loud voice heard through the phone we realize who the person on the other end of the line is, none other than Jordan X Shabazz, PURE wrestler extraordinaire. '...Mr. Copeland, I am warning you, this concussion is serious, and severe. You should not wrestle for another month, let alone wrestling in America and Japan.' Jordan's loud voice comes through the phone, we can't make it out, but we hear something to the effect of, 'No way in hell.' The doctors face gets more and more grim. 'Mr. Copeland, allow me to fax this to your office in Dallas, I'll show you exactly what I am talking about.' The doctor moves over to his fax machine and he puts the paper in. Shabazz yells his fax number at the man; the man sends the papers to Jordan. We now cut to a shot of Jordan X Shabazz, high atop his office building in Dallas, Texas. The N.I.G.G.A. offices are looking nicer and nicer everyday. Shabazz has numerous leather chairs and brand new computers in the area; it is easy to see why Ali Khadafi calls him a sellout. As the papers come out of the fax machine, Jordan puts the phone on speaker, so we can hear what both men are saying. 'Mr. Copeland, are you checking the charts?' says the Japanese man with a tint of a Japanese accent. 'Yes,' replies the Civil Rights Activist. 'Okay, do you see that small hole right there?' 'Yes.' 'That's what's in your head. You have a severe concussion, and you could suffer from post concussion syndrome if you continue wrestling at the pace you are right now. I am going to talk to Mr. Miyagawa about this, somehow he can't let you wrestle.' Shabazz becomes enraged and begins screaming. 'YOU'VE GOT TO BE FUCKING CRAZY! I FUCKING WORKED HARD HAVING TO STAND THIS FUCKING RETARD, ALI KHADAFI, I HAD TO WORK WITH THAT DUMB FUCK, I HAD TO WAIT FOR MY FUCKING TURN WHILE THAT LAZY FUCK, CIANAN, COULD RUN AROUND FUCKING WITH MIYAGAWA AND KENSUKE SUZ WHATEVER THE FUCK HIS NAME IS! UNLESS YOU WANT TO COME TO THE NEXT IKUSA, AND FUCKING PHYSICALLY FORCE ME FROM NOT WRESTLING! GOT IT? THAT FUCKING TITLE IS MINE!' The verbal outburst from Jordan X Shabazz is not something we are used to. He doesn't usually scream obscenities at that. He has obviously been holding the pressure if a missing friend, two title shots, and a failing company on his shoulders. He knew it, PCW knew it, PURE knew it, and this man was on the verge of a Mental Breakdown. His company was almost in the dumps; his financial backing had been weakened. It was a mere few weeks before they went up on the NYSE, that's if they survived. The doctor and Jordan continued their conversation; neither convincing the other about the argument... the scene fades to black.
