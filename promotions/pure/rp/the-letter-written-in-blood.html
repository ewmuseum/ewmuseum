Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The letter written in blood 'Blood...The true essence of life, one must have blood to continue on with his ever so precious life. Yet most people don't deserve the blood that runs through their weak and useless body.' The scene zooms in to a small dark room, dimly lit by the flickering flames on the hancrafted wax candles, the flames struggling for the air that is in small supply in the room. There is a strange pugnent and grotesque smell running wild throughout the room like a women running from her brutal rapist. In the center of the room, there is Takumi Umeshita sitting comfortably on a old and beat up tatami mat, whispering the words 'shinda tanoshii' over and over as if in the trance of an african witch doctor who is embedding dark and dirty thoughts in the already demented and sick mind of Takumi. Takumi slowly reaches over to a small wooden table that has a dark satin blanket spread over the top of it, hiding the space underneath the table.On top of the table is a old fashioned quill pin and a small bottle of ink, he slowly grabs the two and then stares at them with the look of uncontrolable exstacy on his face and says ' atarashii Homeless Shinda, Arigatou Boku no Atama Akuma'. Takumi then brings the light quill pen and flask that has a small taint of green to it and sets it slowly on a small wooden desk in front of him,that already has a white long scroll of paper spread across the surface of the desk.He slowly takes the quill pin and dips it ever so gently into the ink and when he takes the pen out instead of black, the ink is a thick dark red color, like the color of fresh blood squirting out of the jugglers vain when it has been freshly slit by some sharp object. He takes the quill and brings it over to the scroll and slowly starts writing and ever so often dips the quill back into the jar. ' Dear MR.Shigeharu, I'm sorry to inform you that there is still going to be a few homeless people running on the streets of the once beautifiul streets of Tokyo tonight because i was delayed a little in my affair of disposing of these homeless. I never thought one would put up a fight like this one did today, I'm really sorry i couldnt meet my wanted quota of 4 dead, it has to rest at 2 today. I know what your going to be thinking, how could i do this stuff? It is quite simple, these people are worthless, join in with me and the killing spree before im going to have to kill you. Anyways i dont have much time left as I have plans tonight with someone special but i will leave you of the address of were you can find the body and also some photographs so you can also enjoy the beautiful scene of disembowling people like I do. Well Ja ne and the address is 540-4 kamata, Ueno ward, Tokyo.' Takumi slowly sits up and wraps up the scroll and slowly fastens it into a small rounded case and then slowly lifts up the dark satin blacket, exposing the body of a dead man that looks like if his neck was violenty ripped open by some rigged object. Takumi then slowly grabs a surenge with a thin needle already attached and violenty jabs it into the decomposing neck of the dead man. He quickly fills up the surenge with blood and then takes out the needle and then brings the needle over to a small opened plastic bottle and emptys the blood into their. Takumi then stands up firmly and puts the bottle of blood into his pocket and the letter and quickly leaves the dark room without blowing out the candles and as he walks out the door, the last words heard out of his mouth are ' can never get enough of this magneficent substitute for ink'.
