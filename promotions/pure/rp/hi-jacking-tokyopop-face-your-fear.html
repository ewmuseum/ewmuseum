Hi-Jacking TokyoPop - FACE YOUR FEAR!

29 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hi-Jacking TokyoPop - FACE YOUR FEAR! 'Welcome to another award winning show from the TokyoPop Network! TNP proudly brings to you...FACE YOUR FEAR!' **The Japanese voice introduces the show, the English subtitles rolling underneath as a video package rolls on the screen, showing various Japanese men and women sat inside a packed studio. The camera pans around for a few seconds, before cutting to a host stood on an elaborately designed set. The host is male, in his late forties, with a receding hairline; his black hair brushed forward to try and hide it. He's wearing a grey suit, with white shirt and a blue tie, and is holding a microphone close to his face. His high pitched voice shrills around the arena as he addresses the crowd, English subtitles again rolling through the bottom of the shot.** 'Welcome to FACE YOUR FEAR! I'm Sajito Konotami! Tonight, we see two men facing their fears of spiders!' **The word 'spider' is met by a cheer from the captive audience.** 'We see woman facing goat!' **Another cheer from the crowd.** 'And four members of audience face fears!' **The crowd 'ooh' and 'aah' at the announcement, as the opening credit music plays again. The host walks across the set, and stands next to a tank, full of arachnids, as two scantily clad Japanese females lead on a man, blindfolded, to the set. The man, dressed in blue denims and a black t-shirt, is sat next to the tank as the females place a black cloth over it, and has his blindfold removed. He's in his mid-twenties, has a slightly 'western' complexion, and looks nervously at the audience.** 'Ladies and gentlemen! This is Sami Tomokai! He face fear live on TokyoPop Network tonight! Sami...you have fear? Correct?' **Sami looks at the host with a nervous look, and nods his head.** 'What is fear?' **Sami shakes his head, and responds with one word, looking more and more nervous as he does so.** 'Arachnophobia!' **The crowd cheer again as the two females pull back the cloth, revealing the tank to Sami. Sami in turn screams, and tries to run off of the set, but two burly security guards appear and hold him down.** 'Sami has fear of spiders. Tonight, he conquers fear!' **The crowd cheer again, as Sami is stood up, and stood next to the tank. The host steps to one side, and takes a bowl from behind the tank, with hole cut in the bottom, and cloth draped inside the hole. Sami continues screaming as the large bowl is placed over his head, it just fitting through the hole. Sami starts to shudder as he realises what is happening, and the crowd erupt into applause.** 'Tonight...Sami...You literally face spiders! FACE YOUR FEAR!' **Again Sami is held down by the security guards as the two females pick up a spider each and dangle it over the top of the bowl. As they are about to let go, the crowd going wild...our shot cuts to static.** 'Are we on?' **The scene slowly fades in, static still showing on the screen, and shows a man stood in a dark alleyway. He is obviously holding the camera himself, as his arm disappears under the bottom of the shot. The black and white picture clears a little, and we can just make out the facial features of Joey Flash...the man who made himself known on PURE Ikusa a few nights ago.** 'I don't know how long I've got. I've cut their feed. They can trace me. I have to be brief.' **Joey's free arm shoots out, and fiddles with the side of the camera, before returning to his side. Joey casts a glance to his right, and then turns to face the camera.** 'So here we are. PURE. And I'm fired already. I was FORCED into being Siena. Something that will get me over, they said. Yet Wilson still fires me. Not for long though. I got my shot back. Darkstar. PCW's resident dreamer. A tough challenge. One I must overcome.' **The static increases on the screen slightly, and Joey mumbles an expletive.** 'They're finding me quicker than I thought. I better get this done. Jamar. What you did was wrong. I'm out for revenge. I may be a foreigner on Japanese soil as far as nationality goes. But so are you. The difference is...my roots lie here. I was raised in Japan. I was raised PURO!' **Joey again glances to his right as a small thud can be heard, echoing down the alleyway.** 'I know Japan. I know its traditions. I know what it means to have Pride and Honor. You don't. Entertainment should stick to America. People over there have lower attention spans. They can't watch a PURE art form. You're on foreign soil Jamar, and the residents aren't going to take this well...' **A deep voice cuts across the transmission...** 'FIND HIM!' **Joey looks to his right again, before looking up briefly. He curses again and continues.** 'You spit on tradition. You spit on Puroresu. It won't stand. People don't like the American way. Tradition is strong amongst the Japanese people. I will make sure I defend their honor. I fight for my career, and maybe my life on Ikusa. The Mourning Star and me in a Japanese Death Match. I win, I get my job back...and I get to join the fight against TokyoPop and the 'Entertainment' ideal...' **Sirens interrupt Joey in full flight. Muttering a few more expletives, the camera is suddenly dropped from his hands, static re-appearing as it hits the ground. The transmission continues briefly as four pairs of black boots run past the sideways, monotone screen...** 'THERE HE IS!' **The static continues to increase, before filling the screen. It holds for a second before the transmission cuts, and a blank TokyoPop test screen appears, a single tone airing as a sound. We slowly fade out.**
