Shunned by Tokyo Pt. 1

12 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Shunned by Tokyo Pt. 1 Written: 1/12/03 The Scene opens up outside the 'Yum Yum Dim Sum' resturaunt where Erik Blake is standing in his white karate gi with his arms folded.
'This looks like a good place to eat, and I'm around my people as well.' Blake says as he walks in.
As he walks in he sees nothing but people of Japanese decent, well from the faces at least, sitting and eating. Suddenly, a 4 foot tall Japanese man runs up to Blake in somewhat of a frightened way.
'No, No, no soul food here! Only Asian food! No soul food!' the small japanese man says. 'No, my friend. I have come looking for your greatest set of noodles. Can you supply me with my request?' Blake says.
The small japanese man looks at him funny and shrugs it off. 'Ok, fine. Come right this way.' the small japanese man says. The small japanese man appoints Blake to a table and hands him a menu. 'Thank you, my friend. May your daughter end up with a charming prince!' Blake says, but the small japanese man just looks at him dumbfounded.
'What shall you be having today?' the small japanese man says. 'I want a cup of your best noodles, with a scent of squid, your best egg rolls, hold on the soy sauce, and bean juice.' Blake says as a young female waitress comes over and fills a cup up with water. Blake looks at the glass and quickly grabs the girl's hand.
'This cup is dirty. Get me a new one.' Blake says. 'Sorry, sir. I shall get you a new one.' the young girl says as she grabs her arm in slight pain. She grabs the cup and leaves.
Suddenly Blake starts to look around and see various men staring at him in a funny way. He stands up and unfolds his arms.
'Do you all have a problem?' Blake says. One man stands up from behind him, bib and all, and points at Blake. 'You are without honor! You did not need to disrespect that girl and this resturaunt!' the man said.
'Disrespect? I asked her for another glass, and how am I disrespecting the resturaunt? I am of Japanese decent just like the rest of you.' Blake says.
The females in the resturaunt start to shake their head and the man start scooping up chunks of food and start heathing them at Blake. Blake manages to dodge a few but the barrage keeps coming. So Blake finally runs out of the resturaunt with the men still throwing food byproducts at him.
'Damn, how could they treat their own kind like that?' Blake says as he walks down the street and the scene fades.
