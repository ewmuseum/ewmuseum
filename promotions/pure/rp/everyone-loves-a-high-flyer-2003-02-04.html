Everyone loves a high-flyer...

4 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Everyone loves a high-flyer... Written: February 4, 2003 “Your winner by pin fall, CHASE HERRERA!” The same thing kept echoing through my mind. I had won my first match here in PURE. I know I have said before that winning doesn’t matter to me, and it still doesn’t, but it just feels important and pretty exciting.
From my point of view, I did quite impressive in my match against Peter Perez. I also had a lot of fun in the match. I got to show off exactly what I could do and what I had always planned to do in that ring.
My popularity has also risen. The people obviously want what I have been giving them in the ring. I’m quite proud of myself that so far, in the little success I’ve had so far, that so many people stick behind me. But what will happen with my cheers when I take on Radiant? The fans like him too, don’t they? Oh well, it’s really no big deal. It just gives me another opportunity to impress everyone.
They’re impressed with my high-flying skills. “Hey Chase!” a deeply tempered voice blurted. “Yeah, I’m talking to you Herrera! You suck! People like you give wrestling a bad name!”
I was in a bar with my cousin Daniel. Since I had won my match, he decided to buy me a drink. But tonight was supposed to be a good night, it was a good night, but this guy really made me mad.
I sat at the front of the bar with Daniel, and the guy shouting things at me sat at a table in the back.
As I sat there, I heard the laughter of the guys back there from what the man had said. But I wasn’t going to let this bother me. It was better for me to ignore it.
“Hey man, who the hell do you think you are talking to the toughest guy in PURE right now?” Daniel shot back at the guy yelling cruel things toward me.
Wait, did he just say I was the toughest guy in PURE? I suppose that since I won that match, he’s given me apart of his ego. God damn it.
“Boy, who the hell do you think you’re talking to?” said the man. “And what do you mean the toughest guy in PURE?”
“Sit down,” I told Daniel. “No I will not sit down, this guy is bad mouthing you.” “Still think he’s the toughest guy in PURE? Apparently from what I hear, little Chase Herrera just wants to have fun in the ring and doesn’t care about winning or losing. A wrestling ring is not a playground, Herrera, it’s a war zone.”
I then stood up with a few words of my own to say to him. “Ask any high-flyer what they care about more in the wrestling busine—“
“A high-flyer? Boy, guys like you are a waste of space. That’s not wrestling; it’s nothing but a god damn circus act in a wrestling ring.”
Laughs were heard throughout the bar. This guy was making a fool out of me. “But we still get the job done,” I attempted to counter what he had said. But I was tired of all these people laughing at me. I was tired of that one man. I was tired of this bar. I wanted out, so I did get out. I just walked away, blocking out all the laughter and the next few things the man had to say.
