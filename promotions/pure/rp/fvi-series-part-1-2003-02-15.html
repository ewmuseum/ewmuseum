FVI Series (Part 1) Written: 2-15-03 …Dark…. cold…. windy…uncertain…strange…bizarre…some many words…. Yet none of these words explain the night of February the sixth of two-thousand and three.
BLADED! Bladed: Huh? What the….. KABOOM! This once, old, haggard, but very stable building… the Fusion of Vernacular Intelligencer (FVI) was just annihilated like no body’s business, but actually it is somebody’s business. It is Bladed’s business… You are thinking. Wait a second! Wasn’t he in the building? Well that is where you are wrong…
Dun! Dun! Dun! Bladed was outside the building getting ready to go in. He was supposed to be on the job retaining documents on a top-secret plan of nuclear warfare movement in Sydney. I guess that is going to be happening. Just seconds after the blast a helicopter is seen circling around the once highly respected building, but we will get back to the FVI later.
Bladed was about two miles away, which really isn’t a lot, but managed to avoid being hit by anything. The big question now is who blew the building up, how, and why. There is no way it was anyone on Bladed’s side of things because he was going there for documents that were essential to the US of A as we know it.
Before we can get to any of this Bladed is going to have to venture on down and try to find some clues of evidence to solving the problems of this mystery. Only he can’t really talk to the Japanese Bluecoats (Police) because he is on duty. Basically, if Bladed is spotted his career and life could be put into jeopardy. He isn’t supposed to be around any of this. He isn’t a spy; he is just “inside work” for the NMA.
Bladed watches very closely has he observes everything that is taking place. He can see the police and other affiliates taking pieces of the building that are left and putting them into huge trucks. The obvious thing would be they are taking it back to labs to study, so they can try to get this problem solved. Messing with the FVI is just as bad as messing with the White House, so this will not end up ending in a pretty matter.
B|_/\DeD B|_/\DeD B|_/\DeD B|_/\DeD -/\- The scene cuts over to the remains of this once very important building. Officers and FVI officials a like are on the screen. The explosion just occurred, so dust has been flying everywhere, which is making it hard to see, and it is also unsafe to be around the building at this time. FVI officials are trying their best to talk police officers into letting them into and around the building to see what is left, but at this time it is unsafe. Police officers haven’t had the chance to check the whole building, well the remains, to make sure the people that did aren’t around. Suddenly out of the clear blue FVI Senior Member Jack Charles III gets shot and falls to the ground. -/\-
Officer #1: Mr. Charles! Are you ok? Mr. Charles can you hear me? Mr. Charles! Come on Mr. Charles don’t die on me! Please Mr. Charles!
Officer #2: I can’t believe this! How could this of happen? Look he is dead! He doesn’t even have a pulse anymore!
-/\- Paramedics were already on the scene because they were waiting to see if there were any bodies that needed to by taken care of -/\-
Paramedic #1: Ok, ok! Let us in, so we can get the man to the hospital! Brainy Paramedic: Look at this gunshot wound! Paramedic #1: Yea it is just a gunshot wound. Nothing special. Brainy Paramedic: Actually, there is something special about it. Paramedic: And what would that be? Brainy Paramedic: Well, if you were smarter, then you look you would know that this is a sniper shot wound, which means we need to get out of here as fast as we possibly can before the rest of us go down with him.
Officer #1: Are you sure of this? Brainy Paramedic: 110% positive sir. Paramedic: He is brain of our hospital, so I wouldn’t doubt him for a second. I swear this boy could call the bullet it entered the body if he had the chance.
Officer #2: Well, then I guess we better go ahead and get out of here. Officer #1: All right men, let’s head out! -/\- Wow. Just like that the scene ends. Where is Bladed right now you are thinking and you are thinking what Bladed is thinking about all of this correct? That means you are going to have to continue on this journey to find out the rest of the answers to all of the questions you may have at this point in time. Scene out! -/\-
