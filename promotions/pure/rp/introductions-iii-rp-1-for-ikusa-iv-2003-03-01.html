Introductions III (RP #1 for Ikusa IV)

Introductions III (RP #1 for Ikusa IV) Written: 1-3-03 The scene slowly begins to open up in the backstage area of the Ikusa III arena. We find ourselves waiting around the curtain area as Jesse Ramey makes his way through the curtains; bloody, battered, slammed, and barely able to walk. A smile still shines through the bloody face as Jesse begins to make his way down the hallway and into his locker room. The camera tails him quickly and slips in before the door closes shut. Jesse stands before us, in shock. Curious, the camera man begins to step sideways until he reveals the little old man that has seemingly been stalking Jesse sitting a steel chair.
Jesse: What are you doing here? Man: I’ve come to congratulate you on a well-fought match. Jesse: (Looking weirdly at the man) There has to be more to it than just that. I know you’re not just going to show up here for something like that.
Man: (Smiling) My intentions are still unknown to you and you’re only making it harder on yourself week in and week out.
Jesse: (Thinking) How in the world would you even know I had won?! I don’t have a monitor here in my locker room, how did you even know I won?!
Man: (Nodding) I knew you would pull through this week, young one. (Shaking head) Then again, I also knew that it was going to be another point in your career that you would only slip away from the Puroresu style of wrestling.
Jesse: Old man, listen to me right now. I’m not slipping from anything, it seems as though your mind is slipping. I’ve been going out there fighting for all the right things when it comes to Puroresu. I just made a man say “I Respect You”. How much more Puro can you get?!
Man: (Shaking head) Has it really been that long? Jesse: (Beginning to get really frustrated) What in the world are you talking about?! Man: (Lowering head) Aye, it most obviously has been that long. You’ve been brain washed by the American society and their ways of wrestling.
Jesse: (Sighing) Old man, I’m getting ready to call security in here in about five seconds, so unless you want to be locked away in jail. You’d better leave.
The old man pulls out another folding chair and slides it over in front of Jesse’s feet. Man: Have a seat and listen to me for a few moments, please. Jesse: (Pulling the seat out and sitting in it) You’ve got five minutes, use them wisely, because this is the last you’re ever getting to talk to me.
Man: Aye, I understand. (Nodding his head) Do you remember the first time you came to this island to wrestle?
Jesse: (Shaking head) Yeah, of course I remember it was some of the worst times I had ever spent in wrestling, period!
Man: Aye, but was it not the most influential period in your training ever? Jesse: (Thinking) Well, only a little bit and it really wasn’t the competition that helped me train the most. It was my Sensei.
Man: Aye, and do you remember this man? Jesse: (Shrugging his shoulders) Only vaguely. It was a long time ago and I tried to block out most of that time in my career.
Man: If you know this man helped you out in any way, then you know that you need him once again.
Jesse: (Nodding) Yeah, I guess I really could use his help, but how am I supposed to know where to find him? I don’t even know if the guy is still alive.
The old man stands and walks to the door. Man: My time is up and you never came to the location on that piece of paper I gave you. Come there and all the answers will be revealed.
The scene begins to fade as a puzzled look comes across Jesse’s face.
