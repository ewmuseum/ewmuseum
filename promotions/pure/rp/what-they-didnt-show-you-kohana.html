What they didn't show you: Kohana

March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What they didn't show you: Kohana Written: Joint RP Kohana paces back and forth in his separate locker room from Senji Yulav, shaking out his arms. His large, solid, tanned frame ambles back and forth against a white brick wall. He is already washed up, and has on his black tights and boots. He stretches out his neck as he moves. His eyes dart from one place to the next. He must be focused on Senji Yulav.
#16: Hello. Kohana stops, looking up at the ninja. Then, brushing him off, he steps past and contniues his pacing.
#16: Aww... preoccupied with Champion Yulav, are you? Rightly so, this will be no easy task for you. Your losing is inevitable.
Kohana ignores #16 and keeps pacing. #16 stands at the side, his arms folded behind his back.
#16: But what I have trouble figuring out... is why you would even be in PURE in the first place? You don't seem to have any declared love for puroresu... you're not actually that good of a wrestler. One is at a loss for determining exactly what brought you to PURE... to Japan...
Kohana is starting to eye #16, but his mind seems to drift back to Yulav. #16: Is it perhaps to open a casino? That wouldn't work. Japan already has casinos. But even if not, that US government subsidy that gives you land and allows you to operate it virtually tax-free does not apply to this country. You must make your own money, buy your own land, and Japan's high tax rates would take much of the money that you would make for public funds. That is certainly not the best 'get-rich-quick' scheme.
Kohana shakes his head from side to side, trying to ignore the ninja. #16: Or perhaps it is as you say, that you are bringing pride to your people. Not that your people would care. They are so broken that at this point you could become the ruler of the world and they wouldn't care. What would a pathetic tribe of reservation-bound drunks know about pride? They have not tasted glory in so long they have no doubt forgotten the entire concept. You're campaigning for a lost cause? No, you're just in denial. You want to climb out of the never-ending downward spiral that is the existence of the Sioux people. And instead of mercifully killing them all off, you want to bring attention to them. You want them to watch you on television and say 'There goes Kohana, we are so proud of him.' But you are wrong. Your people are never gathered around the television, if they even have one, watching your triumphs, or lack thereof. They are too busy sharecropping or selling oil for $2 a barrel. They have their own miserable lives to lead, they have no time to give you an ego boost by pretending that you are important to them.
Kohana's breathing changes, getting heavier. He is now eyeing the ninja more consistently, shooting him very noticeably hostile glares.
#16: Or... The ninja leaves his spot, pacing alongside Kohana. The Sioux Nation Sensation's every movement is mirrored.
#16: Or... what if you were just trying to be the white man? Kohana skips a step at hearing this. His face grimaces. #16: What if you were so ashamed of your own race that you would try everything in your power to escape the fact? It seems to be so. You chose wrestling, an industry that your people typically shun. You dyed your hair blond, because the natural black of your race's hair is too much of an eyesore for you. You went through extensive physical training to make yourself look as large as a white man, when there is nothing wrong with being less bulky. You even came to Japan to wrestle instead of staying in America, where you were trained in the art of the white man's wrestling. That is the last step to completely erasing your heritage, except to shallowly use it as a marketing tool. You wish to live like the white man, you wish to look like the white man... all that's left is winning the All-Asia Title. Then you will be the conquering white man.
Kohana bursts with emotion, grabbing #16 by the throat and charging with him to the opposite wall. The back of the ninja's head slams into the wall, and Kohana lifts the ninja a few inches off of the ground. Kohana's breathing is heavy, his eyes are wide with rage.
Kohana: If you ever disrespect me or my people again I will end you. The ninja's throat is clutched tightly, constricting his air passage. He still manages to loose a laugh.
#16: Hahahahahahahahahaha... Kohana drops the ninja to the ground. He lands on his feet, and just leans back against the wall, folding his arms.
Kohana: Leave me be! With a shrugging of his shoulders, the ninja calmly walks away, waving Kohana off over his right shoulder.
#16: Something to think about... would my remarks truly have angered you as much as it did, were there not the slightest bit of truth to them? Good luck in your loss to Yulav.....
Kohana is left alone, staring down to the ground, trying to bring his breathing rate back to normal.
