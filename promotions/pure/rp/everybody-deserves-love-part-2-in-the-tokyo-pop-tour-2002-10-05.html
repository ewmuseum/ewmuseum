Everybody Deserves Love. (Part 2 in the Tokyo Pop Tour)

5 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Everybody Deserves Love. (Part 2 in the Tokyo Pop Tour) Written: 10/5/02 Sweat is pouring from the face of Nygma, seeing doubles and triples of everything around him. There’s a cut on his arm that’s he’s trying to cover up and his nerves are so present that even a blind man would be able to see it. Something’s wrong and something gave in, but will DJ Nygma take the stage and sing.
Nygma you take the stage in 5……………4………………3………………..2…………….1!!!!!!!! The scene goes into slow motion, and in reverse to earlier that day. We’re in Okasa, Japan where the second leg of Nygma’s Tokyo Pop tour is headed. The scene opens to the afternoon before the show.
Nygma is rolling through the city blasting music, when he sees some kids playing soccer at a local park and pulls over. For some reason he seems to like soccer, even though it’s so much different from the other things he’s into. As he watches the young children kicking the ball around flashes enter the mind of the poetic genius.
“Haven’t you ever wanted to run on an open field and listen while the world calls your name.”
CRASH! The memory ends with a tear rolling down the eye of Nygma. Nygma never shows much emotion, so the tear quickly disappears.
I better get out of here… As Nygma makes his way out of the park he hears a scream of a young child, and turns around faster than a bullet leaving the barrel. Falling hard is a young boy with his body colliding with the hard ground. On top of his comes another boy of much larger proportion pushing him and screaming all types of things.
STOP THAT! The child turns around and the one on the bottom kicks him in the gut, but what happens next makes sparks the flame. The larger child pulls out a knife and Nygma reads it. Without thinking or knowing what he’s capable of Nygma goes flying in the middle and taking a stab to his left arm. He screams in mercy throwing his right fist in anger at anything near him. What he hits is the boy with the knife, and what he does is critical damage. The smaller boy runs off not looking back.
Nygma makes his way toward the bigger boy, and sees him completely knocked out with blood dripping from his nose. The boy flied through the air and it’s a miracle he was still breathing after what Nygma did to him.
HEY YOU! What did you do to that boy? As Nygma saw a group of men draw to the scene he thought of all the things he’s worked for to get this far and knew he couldn’t throw it away. So he ran, ran the other way and didn’t look back.
As Nygma drove off into the late afternoon another flash went through his blood stream, causing him to swerve and almost hit a curb.
“I was alone so many years, violence was the answer. I didn’t want to hurt other kids but I had too.”
For that split second Nygma thought of the child lying there on the ground without anyone in the world to care for him. Sure he was a stupid kid, but did stupid kids deserve to be hurt. He doesn’t know what made him snap like that, it wasn’t the cut he’s had much bigger. The fact that life was about to be taken to easily sent chills up Nygma’s spine. What’s the world coming too, where life is taking through violence?
“ I won’t let it happen again. He won’t slip through my fingers.” Screech!! The sound of the tires as Nygma makes the sudden U turn headed straight for the nearest hospital. He sprinted inside, and rushed his words out to a nearby nurse.
Excuse me, do you know if a boy came in here a little while ago. He was unconscious and bleeding from the nose.
As soon as the words poor out of Nygma’s mouth he, his agent taps him on the back. Nygma, what are you doing we have to get to the show, I’ve been looking everywhere for you.
Nygma pushes him to the corner and recites the story. Chan, I think I may have critically injured a child.
WHAT! Are you crazy, do you know what could happen if this gets out. Oh man I have to sort this out ASAP.
Nygma’s agent goes to work as Nygma sits in the nearest seat wishing he had enough strength to just pour out in tears. Time passed and about twenty minutes later Nygma’s agent was coming back with a smile on his face.
O God is he all right! I got great news; you’re off the hook. What do you mean? He’s going to be fine? Not exactly he’s still critical, but it doesn’t matter he’s just a scumbag hooligan the police were after. Doesn’t even matter if trash like that were killed.
As Nygma heard those words he felt like the only trash present. *Fast forward to the show* Introducing DJ NYGMA! Nygma comes out to smoke and lights and the seconds he takes that step out in front of the thousands and thousands of screaming fans, his microphone drops in a sudden thump. Followed by the fall of his whole body.
Nygma lied there passed out on the floor with paramedics rushing the scene. There was no smiles in Osaka Friday Night.
