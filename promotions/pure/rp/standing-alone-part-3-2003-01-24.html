Standing Alone Part 3 Written: 24/01/03 'Powers. Calum Powers.' Why I said that name, I don’t know... well, I suppose it was because he was on my mind so much. I had nothing else I needed to think about until now. What startled me was her reaction. Her arms tightened around me for a split second, and in that moment, I felt the hairs on her arms and on the back of her neck stand straight on end. As if I had slapped her, she backed up against the other side of the cab, staring at me. The look in her eyes... she wasn't afraid of not knowing me anymore. What I had told her made her even more afraid... no... she wasn't afraid. She was terrified. She knew who my father was, but I didn't recognize her... who was she?
She teeth were clenched, and I could hear her breathing through them, hissing every so often. I could tell she wanted to say something, but it was as if she too frightened to do anything more than hug her knees to her chest. The rest of the trip was deathly silent.
The second the cabbie stopped the car, she sprang out and threw some money at the driver, something to the amount of a hundred dollars. The driver called out to her, informing her that he didn't need that much money, but she didn't even turn around. She made a beeline to her door and locked it behind her, staring out at the cab she saw it leave through the window. She wanted to make sure that I was gone. I looked around and checked the surroundings. I was in the suburbs... I knew this place... and...
'Oh god damn it...' I knew her too... and I had told her that I was Calum Powers. But she... she was an American. The place I had found myself in was where my father had grown up. It was the first place he looked for me after my mother died. Why on earth he thought I would surround myself with people like him is beyond me! I went to the closest place to home I could find and stayed above a resteraunt in little China. The pace still stunk of Americans but at least I wasn't surrounded by them.
You could be left wondering why the sudden change in Senji's character. He is not by nature a spiteful man and will attempt to help as his code orders. This time though it was an American, someone who, through their veins flowed the blood, that contaminated his own.
My shoulder ached as I recalled her final question... 'Who are you?' I should have told her. I should have pulled her into the light and said, 'Look at me!' But no... she believes I am a monster. All she knows of me is that I am Calumn Powers. I reached into my pocket and pulled out the ring I took from my mother, bringing it near the window. Every so often, light would flick past from the street-lamps, giving me some means of reading the inscription.
'Till Death Do Us Part' Yeah... that sounds morbid enough for him. It should... if it wasn't, then he wouldn't be the man I know. He wouldn't be mt father. He wouldn't be Callum Powers.
I still can't believe what just happened. I suppose it's just another mask I show to the world... Another mask I use to hide from my past, to protect myself from the present, and to prepare myself for the future.
I don't want to remember who I was before. That man was impure, and was being destroyed from within. I am not that man... not that man, nor Senji Powers. I am nameless as I am faceless until I am ready to face the man I once aspired to become.
When I was young he was not American, he was dad. When I was young he was the man I was supposed to be like.
That man is dead to me… and I will never again see his face. I will never find peace. Vengeance is the next best thing. She Knew. She knew I killed my father and would forever be a Universal Outlaw.
