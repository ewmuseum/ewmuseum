Emotion............Returning?

4 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Emotion............Returning? (OOC Note: This will be a first-person roleplay) -------------------------------------- So hard...it's so hard to try and live up to someone else's expectations. It's so hard to look someone in the eyes who you supposedly let down. It's the hardest fucking thing to do...it's so hard to sit there and watch someone die in front of your eyes because you didn't know it would hurt you so much. You had no idea...yet you could do nothing about. I decided to go and visit my father the other day in the hospital, somehow he got worst and has been staying in ICU. I have to admit, a small feeling of remorse has been circulating through me ever since I saw how it destroyed my mother and grandmother. It was a great day, though. Not too warm, not too bright...pretty perfect if you ask me. I went to the intensive care unit and a young blonde was sitting behind the counter, but she didn't really look like she knew what she was doing. 'I'm here to see Nagato,' I told her. 'I'm sorry, he's not taking any visitors.' She replied...but I could see right past the bull shit. 'My apologies.' I bowed, flipped her off, then walked through the open doors leading into the ICU corrider. I did catch a glimpse of his room number...2...3...6. I made my way right to his room, not even bothering to acknowledge the nurses and doctors telling me to turn back. I just brushed them off. I came to his room...I took a deep breath and opened the door... I didn't have on my mask but I was sporting my jumpsuit. There he was...lying on the hospital bed with tubes running every which way. He looked cold...so cold. His skin was very pale and very grotesque. The sheet covered his body up until his neck but you could still see how mangled his body had become. He opened his eyes and looked up at me...a very confused and blank look but for some odd reason, it sparked a feeling in me. I a feeling of sorrow...a feeling of compassion. 'Son...' he breathed out, barely having the energy to speak. I walked over and put my finger over his lips. If he kept going...I know all emotion would come back to me. Very slowly he raised his arm, put his hand on mine, and removed my fingers. 'Son..' he began again. 'I...don't...have...much time...left.' 'Shhhh...you need to rest,' I whispered to him. 'No...' I could tell, with every second that passed, he was dying more and more. 'I want...to let...you know...I...am...sorry.' I looked at him as if a question mark was just stamped onto my forehead. He continued, 'For...everything. For...making...us move. For...hurting you and...your mother...for everything...' My eyes start filling up because I knew he was serious and really meant this. 'I am sorry, too.' Although I didn't tell him what I was sorry for. 'Son...' He began to cough and hack...and...and cry. 'I.....lo.....ve.....you...' I grasped his hand as he faded out of this world. I honestly did not know his death was going to tear me apart this much. I kissed his hand a tear ran down my face as if one lonely drop of rain just fell from the Heavens. What...Have...I...Done...?
