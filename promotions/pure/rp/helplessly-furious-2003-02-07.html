Helplessly Furious!!

7 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Helplessly Furious!! Written: 2/7/03 You would not believe the amount of s*it I've gone through in the past few days. Hell, even the staff here at the hotel have been pointing their fingers at me. Only cause they know I'm not feeling up to chasing anyone down right now. So the son of a b*tch got lucky, managed to get a few good slams on me during the match, I was just too disgusted at what I saw in that old man to let the match continue, so I said the words I didn't really mean so he didn't humiliate himself even more than he already was. And I thought he was going to put up a good fight! He did nothing but make me feel sorry for his a*s.
The few bumps I did take did enough to keep me inside for a while, and that's where I was, in my hotel room, sprawled out on the couch. For some reason the hotel room was excessively cold, and I had a blanket covering myself up, and a pillow behind my head. Yoshikiro was in the kitchen with the little guys making something. Although the TV was on, and the volume was up pretty loud, I managed to keep my attention either on the floor, or on the small coffee table sitting in front of the couch. My mind obviously thinking about other things.
Yoshikiro: 'Hyu wo yi lyk yir guo?' Lee: 'Bloodied, and motionless!' For some reason those were the first words to come out of my mouth. As you could have guessed, my mind was on nothing other than Jesse Ramey. Trying to figure out what all I could do to him next week. What I could do to him to prove that I am better than he is. I looked up at Yoshikiro who had this blank stare on his face, however, and I decided to change my remark.
Lee: 'Just make them sunny side up.' He nodded, and I wiped the sweat from my forehead. I threw the blanket off of my, but continued to lay on the couch. I felt a tug on my left foot, and looked down to find Frank crawling up to come sit with me. He walked up my left leg, and plopped down on my stomach.
Lee: 'What the hell? Thought you were making supper with Yoshi.' He just looked at me, and almost pathetic look came to his face as he said nothing. I gave him a glare, hoping what I was thinking wasn't going to happen. He wasn't going to turn sour on me now, he's the only one keeping my sane.
Lee: 'Don't you dare turn into Bob! I will have to leave you both on a corner somewhere.'
He remained silent, or maybe the sizzling of the eggs in the fryer was drowning him out, but his mouth wasn't moving, so I took it the way I wanted to.
Lee: 'Well say something! I'm laying around, pathetic as hell, and you don't see me acting like a damn pansy!'
He opened his mouth, no words came out, and he closed it again. This was repeated two or three times before noise came form within. 'Does it hurt when I do this?' he said as he jumped up, and came down on my stomach. Knocking a few breaths out of me, and aggravating my sore abdomen. I grabbed him and threw him back into the kitchen, noticing Bob and Yoshi both laughing at me, and Frank as well when he reappeared.
Lee: 'Should have known you were up to something. You guys are sick, sick I tell...' The sight was disturbing. Yoshi was never able to see, hear, or converse with Frank or Bob before, what happened to him? After all these years, the two guys that are supposedly only in my head are finally seen by someone else.
Yoshikiro: 'Wy re yi toidn bui?' Lee: 'Why the hell are you laughing at me?' Yoshikiro: 'Yi noy!' I was fairly confused, along with Furious at Frank for the stunt he just pulled. I looked straight faced at Yoshikiro, along with a glare or two in Frank and Bob's direction.
Lee: 'Then what the hell are you laughing at?' Just then, a couple of girls stood up, both wearing no other than 'I'm The Next Ikusa Girl' shirts, different from the last time I seen Yoshi with a couple of these girls, and they both smiled at me. Yoshi looked at both of them, smiled, and then they both walked off, heading out the hotel room door.
Lee: 'What the... What the hell was that?!' All he did was laugh at me as he finished up supper, and showed me that my plate was ready. I got up off the couch, and walked towards the kitchen, glaring at Bob and Frank the whole way, and I grabbed my plate. Frank started mocking me though. 'I'm Mr. Furious, I will kill you!' he said as I walked by him. Them two must have been in the beer or something. I grabbed my plate of eggs, scrambled when I asked for sunny side up, and just walked off shaking my head.
Lee: 'You know you two, keep the shenanigans up, and it'll save Ramey from all the trouble of dealing with me and Yoshikiro on Sunday, cause all my anger for him will be unleashed on your asses. I'll be showing you Mr. Furious!'
The two did nothing but laugh at me as I say back down on the couch and watched some television. The eggs tasted like s*it, but I ate them anyway. I needed the energy to get better by the end of the week. Yoshi came in with his pile of eggs, and a glass of milk and sat down on the other end of the couch. Frank and Bob standing on his shoulders, making faces at me. I shook my head at them, and changed the channel.
The scene fades to black.
