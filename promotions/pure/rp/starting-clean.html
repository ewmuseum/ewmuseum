Starting Clean

3 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Starting Clean Courage, a perfect example of something I had always wanted in my life but didn't have. I was never courageous enough to stand up for what I believe in or even care that anything was wrong. I just went through life as if nothing mattered. After my dad died, nothing mattered to me anymore. It was some of the worst times of my life. I felt isolated and all alone. 'Why is it so important to have courage?' some may ask. Courage is a valuable trait that will help you out in a time of need and without it, some things go undone--like me getting over the fact that my dad didn't love me and probably never would. I always held on to a slight shred of hope that some day my dad would forgive for whatever I had done to make him mad. I wanted him to finally love me. That day never came because it never had the chance to. I didn't have the courage to be a man about it and go after what I wanted. Now it's time for a change. Now is the time I need courage the most. It's a new chapter in my life and its time for the past to be just that, the past. I will live on...breathe on with a passion in my heart. It's time for me to start caring and standing up and having a little bit of courage. Life is a thing that needs to be enjoyed while it lasts. Not to sit around and drowned in my own pool of self-pity and hatred.
Today I finally become a man in my own eyes; today I start a new life: A life that will be filled with dreams and hopes of aspirations. I am finally free of my hate filled past. The trials and tribulations of my life were long and plenty full and it's time to wipe the slate clean. I am a new man today and will forever be. Now that my past life is over and the hatred is gone, it's time to focus. Focus on what I ask myself, at this point in time there is no what. The answer of what to focus on will come to me in due time, until that fateful day I move on. I move on just like the rest, I go about my life as a daily thing and hope it goes well until all else fails.
