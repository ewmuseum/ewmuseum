The Envelope...

16 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Envelope... Jordan X Shabazz is in a concussed state. Ali Khadafi had hit him in the back of the head with a shovel. On several occasions... A fucking shovel. Do you know what that feels like? The cold shovel hitting the back of your head, then the ecstasy of unconsciousness. Immediately after Ikusa Jordan X Shabazz was taken to a hospital. For the second time in two weeks Jordan was giving stitches. Over in the PCW Jordan was attacked from behind by John Crichton, who was angry over Jordan talking about him in an interview with the Pimply Faced Intern Kid. But to add insult to injury, Ali Khadafi was dressed in a KKK suit. That's right, a black man in a Ku Klux Klan suit. Ali Khadafi hates Jordan X Shabazz with a passion, Jordan tried to plead with Khadafi, and he tried to ask him to return his friend and associate, The Reverend Jesse Kintei. But that didn't work. Jordan is left with no choice but Brute Force. The scene opens up with Jordan X Shabazz sitting in a hospital bed. He's still dressed in his wrestling attire, except now he wears a Duke Williams shirt. He's holding some ice to the back of his head. He's already been stitched up. It had been a pretty bad week so far, except for the fact that he was granted two, counts 'em, two title shots! The first title shot would take place in the PCW at their Pay Per View, Gold and Glory. Jordan X Shabazz had worked his ass off and shown that he was ready to jump two divisions from the Rising Star division to the Television Title division. The second would take place in the friendly confines of a Japanese wrestling ring. Jordan X Shabazz had been booked along with Cianan and Ali Khadafi by PURE CEO, Miyagawa, for the Asian Title. He wasn't Asian, but he had a lot of respect for Asians. The Asian title would mean a lot to him if he could just hold it. Jordan fantasized about holding the gold around, parading around in The Blitz Dojo, showing Mang A Hero and Inphino what living with them and training with them gave him. But his euphoria was short lived. When Ali Khadafi attacked Jordan X Shabazz he dropped an envelope on him. What was in the envelope was the question that Jeremy Johnson. Shabazz takes the ice pack off of his head and he opens the envelope that was in his pocket when he awoke. A finger. A human finger. Jesse Kintei's finger. Shabazz was enraged. 'SON OF A BITCH! I'LL KILL THAT FATHERFUCKER!' Shabazz screamed. This wasn't over by a long shot; the Asian Title was a mere prop in a feud that was over hatred. A prop that Jordan X Shabazz desperately wanted to own.
