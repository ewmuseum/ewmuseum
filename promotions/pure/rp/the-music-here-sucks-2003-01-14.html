The Music Here Sucks!!

14 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Music Here Sucks!! Written: 01/14/03 So there we were, finding ourselves in yet another boring night club in the middle of Tokyo. I think we had been to about twenty already, and still no sign of Yoshikiro anyway. The description of a short, crazy, and drunken Japanese man doesn't quite work here, cause that could be just about anyone after three in the morning right now. My watch said three forty-eight, the neon green, pink, and orange lasers shimmering through the club we were in currently were giving me headaches, and both Frank and Bob had crawled under the collar of my shirt to muffle the loud, crappy music they were playing.
Lee: 'Excuse me, have you seen any unusual looking Japanese people in here tonight? And when I say unusual, I mean REALLY unusual!'
The waitress just kind of looked at me, with a blank stare. No speak-a English I take it. So I waved her away from me, and began to scan the group of people on the dance floor hopping up and down to the s*itty music, to see if Yoshi was among them in the crowd. I didn't see him, and I had already scoped the bar out. He wasn't in here. I tapped Frank and Bob, telling them to come out as I left the noisy place, and made my way back out into the cold, empty street.
Lee: 'That's the twenty-third place, and still no sign of him. So much for your plan there Bob.'
He gave me the dirtiest glare I've seen out of him before. 'So what, everyone messes up once in a while!' he exclaimed. I just shook my head and turned to Frank. He shrugged his shoulder with a confused look upon his face, and it left us no alternative plan. We were stuck, probably lost, in the middle of Tokyo, and still no sign of the person we were looking for. It was a surprise to me, though, that should Yoshi himself come prancing around the street corner we were standing on, and walk by us without saying anything. I detached the trash can from the light pole, and followed him. Pulling the lid off, and raising the can above my head, I dropped the can over Yoshi's body, and pushed him to the ground.
All I heard were a bunch of Japanese words muffled inside of the trash can, and I decided to remove the trash can.
Lee: 'Why did you run away from us you b*tch? We've been looking for you forever.' He was struggling, not letting me get the can off his head very easily. Lee: 'Geez, would you hold still! I'll have you out in a second if you'd quit your damn wiggling!'
He stopped moving, and I pulled the can off the top of my head. 'I don't think that's who we thought it was.' Frank said to me with a somewhat confused look upon his face. I looked, and looked close. Sure enough, the little man was right, we had just attacked a damn look alike. The poorly lit sidewalk was kind of hard to see, so I guess we made an easy mistake.
Lee: 'Sowwy, so sowwy!' Trying to say it in a tone he'd understand, the man took a good hard look at us, and walked off with anger filling his face. He literally had steam rolling off his hair as he walked away. Who knows why we p*ssed him off so badly, but we were the worse of the two scenarios here. We had to keep looking for someone that we had no clue where to look anymore.
Lee: 'I guess we should just keep checking out the clubs and stuff. He's bound to show up sometime.'
Frank and Bob both looked at me, I looked back at them. 'Why don't we just go home and get some rest. We all need it you know.' Bob said to me as he yawned. I shook my head. 'You want me to do something to him, get him to shut up or something?' Frank asked with pleasure. I shook my head again, and began walking again. Looking for the next club to check, we passes a small pool hall, and the idea hit me, he always enjoyed the beer and pool all in one. I threw myself into reverse the three steps I took past the door, and opened it.
Inside now, we were lucky if there was even a bartender in the place. It was dead. No music, no drinkers, no pool players, and you guessed it, not a damn bartender to be seen. I walked over to the bar, reached behind it, and grabbed a bottle of Rum. Popping the little pourer off the top, I took a quick swig.
Lee: 'He, damn it, is there anyone in here?!' The bartender came through a doorway positioned behind the bar, towel in hand. Bartender: 'What would you like sir?' I took another big, long swig of the rum, a bit strong it was, and decided to tell him what we were looking for.
Lee: 'Well, since you are the only person who seems to know English, I'll ask you this. You seen a crazy a*s Japanese person in here tonight. About this tall, wanting nothing to drink but Vodka, and wanting to play a lot of pool with any and all ladies. He'd more than likely be trying to poke them in the crotch with the end of his pool stick as well. Wouldn't have happened to seen that person would you have?'
I hadn't drank in a while, and it didn't take long for the Rum to start getting to me. The bartender though in the dim lighting of the pool hall, moving the toothpick around in his mouth, he nodded.
Bartender: 'I have actually seen someone like that in here tonight. He wasn't here long, that whole poking chicks in the crotch with his pool stick gag really works. He left here with about three ladies over an hour ago.'
He's serious? The trick actually worked? He'd get nothing but hand print after hand print on his face back in he US for s*it like that.
Lee: 'Didn't mention where he was going did he?' Bartender: 'Something about... 'Hyu nuer fwe oun byr!' ... I think that's what he said. I'm too much English to understand any of that gibberish.'
Lee: 'And here I thought all business owners, and workers here would need to know Japanese. Stupid a*s country anyway.'
That left us with a hundred more options. Cause clearly he was taking them to a private place to get him a little action. Or at least if the words he told the bartender were true that's where he was going. I looked to both shoulder to find Bob passed out, and Frank on the verge. I could do nothing but be disgusted as I walked out of the pool hall into the dark cold street again.
Lee: 'At least he's safe... for now.' With an idea in mind, I walked back the way I came, letting Frank and Bob sleep, and continued on down the sidewalk until I was to reach my destination. Hopefully I'd find him before the night was over with. More than likely not.
The scene fades to black.
