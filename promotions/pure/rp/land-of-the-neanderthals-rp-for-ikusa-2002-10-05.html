Land of the Neanderthals... (RP #! for Ikusa)

5 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Land of the Neanderthals... (RP #! for Ikusa) Written: 10/5/02 I can feel my body slam into a wall. My entire being tingles from the journey, except for my head, which is causing me much pain. I open my eyes from the darkness and partake in the light. I find myself pressed up against a brick wall in the middle of some alley. Does not seem like that stupid contraption worked. Looks like I am still in Liberty City. I climb to my feet and stagger out of the alley. My body is very weak from the 'traveling', I did and goddamn, does my head hurt! I make it to the entrance of the alley and I cannot believe what I see. A busy metropolis filled with numerous vehicles fueled by fossil fuels, people in weird clothing, and not a soldier in sight. No way that stupid, over-sized, food replicator could have possibly worked. I make my way down the sidewalk and receive a myriad of stares from the people of this world. They watch me closely like they have never seen a blonde bombshell in up to date fashion before. I mean these threads are made of the highest quality, replicated, leather and cost more than they would ever have in their life. A middle-aged woman gives me a derogatory look and I give her one right back.
'Stupid Neanderthal,' I mumble to myself. I have to find out where I am. That stupid machine could have sent me to the wrong time, for all I know. I walk up to a newspaper stand and examine the front page of one of the publications. Who would have guessed that these fools could do something right?
'October 2nd, 2002,' I say to myself. A portly man, who reeks of cigars and alcohol walks up to me and tries to snatch the publication from my hands. I easily remove it from his reach.
'Hey,' he exclaims. 'This ain't a library, doll!' I could have easily ripped the Neanderthal's head from his obese body, but I do not want to make a scene. Therefore, I use what the scientists gave me, a nice pair of breasts and a calendar girl smile.
'Can I please have it, sweetie,' I ask in a sweet and innocent voice, as I flash the baby blues at him and give a little pout.
His eyes lock with mine and he is putty in my hands. His jaw drops to the ground and a trickle of drool flows from his fat mouth. Men are so easy to control. Just give them a glimmer of hope and you own them.
'Sure, babe,' he answers. I wink at him and with my free publication, I bounce down the sidewalk like an air headed schoolgirl. I sit down upon a bench outside, what I believe is called a restaurant. I open the publication and take in all the knowledge I can. Out of nowhere, a thought pops into my cranium and I pull back the sleeve on my right arm. I see a small clock attached to my arm. It is counting down to zero and I recall the scientist telling me, that it will keep track of the time until the event occurs. As if I suffered from amnesia, a surge of information enters my mind. All the memories from the future return to me and my mission becomes clear as can be. I can picture the target's face in my mind. Her fake smile, her eyes filled with horrid visions of grandeur, her pathetic excuse for a husband standing behind her. She must not be able to complete her goals and I must make sure of it before the point of no return. There is no time to waste. She must be put 6 feet under, pushing up daisies. I quickly sprint to another near by alleyway, so I will not gain suspicion. I reach into my black trench coat and pull out my weapon of choice. It is a Neostryker, prototype handgun. It shoots energy projectiles, dubbed 'Brimstone.' And to make it even more useful, it never runs out of ammo. How you ask? It absorbs loose energy particles from air and every form of matter and forms them into projectiles. It is my best friend and has never let me down. I lay my weapon upon the lid of a trash receptacle and dig into another one of my pockets. I withdrawal a package in a brown wrapper. I tear it open and discover a large sum of ancient money. The artifact, I possess, was stolen from the last remaining museum in the United States of America, the Museum of Natural History.
'Franklin,' I ask myself. 'Who is hell that?' I shrug and slide all my items back into my coat. I look down at my attire and I am in fashion, in the future. However, I stand out in this time. I look across the street and see a store, marked 'Rave.' I quickly make my way into the store. I pick up a couple pairs of pants, a few skirts, and several shirts. Despite what most men would think, I was out of the store in under a half of an hour. The sun had begun to set, as I chose out my new attire and the sky is now dark. I happen to catch something out of the corner of my eye. Something was radiating light from the pitch-black sky. I glance up and see a large, round circle. Could it actually be what I once heard of? Could I be viewing the moon?
'Wow,' I whisper to myself. The moon was just a legend in my time, for it has been forever hidden behind an artificial O-Zone, that was created after the world had the original become to damaged to protect us back in 2306 a.d. It was so big and beautiful. I began to trek down the sidewalk towards where ever it would lead me. I walk for several blocks, just following the heavenly body. Suddenly, I take a step and kick something lying on the ground. A voice rings out from my left.
'Yo b*tch,' the voice calls out. 'You kicked my boom box.' My eyes slowly lower and gaze to my left. I see a young man in a red bandana walking towards me. On each side of him is a young man with a red bandana of his own. I smirk at the trio and shake my head in disbelief.
'You think this is a game, b*tch,' he quizzes. 'I will give you something to laugh about, chica!'
With a quick flick of his wrist, a switchblade opens to reveal its sharp metal blade. These degenerates have no idea what they are dealing with. I turn to face them, as they are just steps away.
'I am going to make you my whore, chica,' he says to me. 'Is that so,' I question. He quickly thrusts his knife towards my midsection and I easily avoid it. He swings wildly in an attempt slice me across the face. With out even looking, I catch his arm and shatter it at the elbow, like it is a toothpick between my fingers. He shrieks like a little fairy and drops to his knees. He has a compound fracture of the elbow and blood flows like a raging river from the point where his bone broke through his skin. A small chuckle escapes my lips and one of his associates takes offense to it.
'You are going to pay for that, b*tch,' he shouts. 'Why don't you come and make me,' I ask with my best innocent girl voice. The second hoodlum races towards me. In the blink of an eye, I thrust a kick into his midsection, knocking the wind out of his wretched excuse for a body. I take is pathetic *ss and throw him head first into a brick wall. His skull cracks open on impact and leaves a nice trail of blood on the wall, as he slides to the ground. I am liking this little warm up way too much. I am such a sadist. I make my way to the final member of the trio and he begins to back pedal with a look for fear etched on his face. I kick the first hoodlum in the face, as I pass him to chase his friend. The punk begins to sprint away as fast as his legs can carry him. Who is this guy fooling? With all the drugs and cigarettes, he is not out running a turtle let alone me with my advanced genes and training. I catch him by the collar of his plaid shirt without even hitting a mild trot and he gets joked by the lone button that is done on his shirt. He turns to look at me and I slam my forehead into the bridge of his nose. I can hear the breaking of his bone and it is music to my ears. He is whimpering like a little baby.
'What's the matter,' I quiz mockingly. 'Not so tough when your friends are bloody messes, are we?'
I am starting to grow tired of this little gathering and I slam my knee into the punk’s face, possibly making it uglier than it does already was. I drop his lifeless body to the ground. I look down at my new pants and find a bloodstain.
'You got blood on my new threads,' I scream in anger. I kick the punk swiftly in the chest and the impact booms like a cannon going off. Behind me, I hear a footstep and the sound of a gun loading.
'I do not know who you are, b*tch,' a voice sounds, 'but you are about to be filled with so much lead that they will be able to use your *ss for a pencil!'
I quickly turn around and see an unknown hoodlum standing before me with an ancient weapon pointing at me. His hands are trembling and it is obvious he has never shot a person before. However, I do not have time for anymore of this sh*t and have a tight plan to follow. Therefore, I quickly unholster my Neostryker and blow a hole in his cranium the size of a grapefruit. I walk away, as his body still quivers. I look down at my timer.
'Damn it,' I utter to myself. 'That cost me 15 minutes I could have used to relax.' I walk into an establishment, named '7-Eleven,' and I get myself a container of spring water. This really is the dark ages. These Neanderthals do not even have food replicators yet. How pathetic. I could tell them of things that would be so common to me and yet, so fantastic to them that their feeble minds could not even comprehend the simplest description. I pay for the water and exit the establishment. A man pulls up in front of me with a red vehicle. I look at it curiously, while I wonder to myself how these fools could not know about dilithium crystals and their abundant power. The man walks up to me and I lock eyes with him.
'Like what type of vehicle is that,' I ask in my best airhead voice. 'Honey, that is a 2002 Dodge Prowler,' he answers. Is that so? I wait for him to enter the establishment, before I make my way to the driver's side of the automobile. The top has been left down and keys are in the ignition. I climb right in and start the vehicle up. It is awfully loud and nothing like the silent, hover vehicles of my time. From the pedals to the steering wheel, the controls are identical, though. I slam the annoyingly loud car into reverse and pull out of the packing place. I stomp the gas and thrust the stick into first. The tires squeal, as they emit smoke into the air. The tires finally catch and I am off like a rocket. Out of the rear view mirror, I see the man rushing out of the store and sprinting in a vein attempt to catch me. I laugh softly, as I throw the car into 5th gear. The green, road sign reads, 'Westchester County 250 miles.'
Fade to black.
