No Worries!!

29 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
No Worries!! Written: 01/29/03 So here we were, sitting in a cab back in my home town of Denver, Colorado. I had decided to return home for a couple days since it had been a little over a month since I had been there. It was cold as hell outside, and with all 6 windows in my car smashed out, I wasn't about to attempt to drive it. How anyone got into my garage was beyond me since none of the windows were smashed out, and all the doors on it were locked.
It was a slightly overcast kind of afternoon, my kind of weather, and the two hellions wanted to stay at home and sleep. Such lazy bastards! Yoshikiro wantd to tag along, and he didn't even bother to ask where we were going.
Yoshikiro: 'Gri gow duy goi fey vu ping tuyn?' Lee: 'What do you think? Against Ramey? I was looking for an excuse to kick the s*it out of him again, and it looks like I get to.'
As if he even needed to ask me if I was ready for my match or not. Of course I was. Especially with all the 'controversy' people have been talking about, I have wanted to prove I am the better man. Plus, if he is such a vet, like he claims to be, he wouldn't have to b*itch and complain about losing, and then making up silly stories about how he lost when they never happend.
Yoshikiro: 'Gua afi agud, aei cuw mo fe? Lee: 'Ha, no matter how I beat him, he will complain no matter what. I just don't see why he tried to pull what he did to me anyway. Kicking the s*it out of me after the match, then making everyone believe I cheated to win. And he is supposed to be the better man here, right! But if he wants me to accept that match with him so bad, then I guess he's got it!'
I shook my head at the stupidity of Ramey and his little hardcore match. If he wanted me to beat him again so bad, he should have just let me do it in a regular match, instead of letting me tear him apart in this match.
The taxi driver took a sharp turn, and I moved around in the backseat. There wasn't much traffic today, and I figured we'd arrive shortly. I couldn't wait to get inside to see what all they had for me to use.
Yoshikiro: 'Sy goi mu fre loi neywy?' Lee: 'Oh, hah... We are going to the hardware store to pick up a few things. Hopefully Ramey will be glad to see them come Ikusa. I know I'll enjoy having them in my possession.'
I knew exactly what I was going to get too. But everyone else, besides Yoshikiro, will just have to wait and see what damage I pick up later. The cab stopped outside of the Home Depot, me and Yoshi got out, and made our way inside.
The scene fades to black.
