The Streets is Watching... Written: The streets is watching… You think you’re getting away with it…but we’re watching. We’re always watching. It’s nighttime…you think you’re all alone when you do the deed… When you commit that crime… …but you’re not… There’s a code to the streets that you new people don’t know about…like there’s a code to battle that most gaijins or weaklings don’t know about in Japan. You walk into the wrong zone and do the wrong thing…and you’ll have to pay a price.
The park is a main place to do business. Its dark, shady, a haven for activity under the cloak of darkness…the perfect place to deal your merchandise.
The street pharmacist Before your customer arrives, I already know about it…where you stand, your merchandise, your prices, and how much you’re carrying.
Like I told you, the streets is watching… And you think you’re a hustler out there in the bush…you’re just a kid. A youngster. You don’t know what you’re getting yourself into…like that woman doesn’t know what she’s gonna get in the match. Whatever the chick’s handle is…I’ve got better things to worry about than some joro’s name.
By the time your customer arrives, me and my platoon are already on their way. Three black cars, shiny like onyx, sleek like the boot I’m set to stick up anyone’s ass that pisses me off. The engines are in the distance…but you think it’s the midnight train passing through
Money is exchanged… Goods are given… And the cars pull up. They’re surrounded…what’s going on? All they know is that there’s 9 guys to their two, and I’m standing at the point. They hear knuckles crack…the beatdown is imminent. “You’re in my part of town, kids,” I say with a grin. “You have to pay a penance.” And while my hands are being tinted crimson… The streets is watching…
