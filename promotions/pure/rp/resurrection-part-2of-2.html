Resurrection (part 2of 2) Is this the end? Or shall I continue on? Have I reached my breaking point? Is it best to just give up? Cege storms into the arena searching for answers. It's just his luck that it s comes to him the instant he enters his locker room. There is a message stamped on his locker with a knife. He yanks the knife from the locker and it began to read it. Poor Poor Kid, Dont you get that this will never end for you? This will be your life until you die. You will be hated whenever you come here. You don't turn away from family here. Everyone will look at you as a man with no name and a man with no honor. All you have to do is come home and it will all end. I know that it must be hard for you running around trying to keep me from finding you. You will not dishonor our name anymore with your actions. You choose to be such a difficult person and we will have to crush you. But most likely you know exactly what I have known for a long time. You desire to live the good life. It fits you well. You love to be respected. Believe me you will gain it all back with no repercussions for your foolish action. Take it all in and make you decision or keep running. Hopefully you see it my way. Cege stares at the last words with rage in his heart but realizes the truth in everything that was said. Most of the people he has met on every trip have treated him very cruelly for no reason. Even if he treated them with no rudeness at all they displays a great deal of disrespect to him. Wherever he went he would receive the worst service or no service at all. Maybe his father is right maybe he should just give in. His father was too strong and too much of an influence on everything around here. How was he going to go against him and win? He goes out of his locker room and there is a small Japanese boy standing in front of it. He is wearing a long red wig and blue jeans and a red T-Shirt. He takes out an autograph book and a pen and signals for Cege to sign his autograph. Cege grabs the pen and signs the book. The boy becomes ecstatic and skips across to his waiting parent to show off his new prize. Cege becomes very moved by this and gives the kid and his family front row tickets. Thanks kid, you may not know this but you have helped me very much. Cege says in Japanese as his heads to the ring. The thing that brought him to wrestling was the thing that the young kid showed him in that moment. He wanted true admiration not for what people know about his past but what he does in the present. Knowing that there is at least one person out there who feels that way he just cant let them down by giving up. He will fight forever if hes has too because he knows that it is worth it to see people like that young boy looking up to him.
The light of the arena shine upon his face and he fights on.
