Safe in my arms pt.1... He stood looking down upon her in the hazy light of pre-dawn. The constant drizzle that had so personified the night and his mood had dissipated. His head ached, but it was a low, pleasant buzz that could easily be ignored. But there she was. Easily the most beautiful thing Ali Khadafi had ever seen. Blood ran from her crown in great rivers. A clump of hair dangled from one side, dripping a long swatch of flesh and gore. More blood ran down her thin body, covering his hand where he gripped her. Ali couldnt make out her face, because there was just too much blood. Look upon me little man Look upon the one that will make you who you are I already am who I am, women I am he of theDem.on.E.ye.s. Yet, I am intrigued Who did you say you were? Silence reigned king, but in his ears, Khadafi could hear a faint something being carried to his ears on the breeze. It sounded like a song, but he wasnt quite sure. It could be the buzz of neon lights, or the dim hum of the constant Osaka crowd. He wasnt sure. Turning to his right, he found that he could hear the noise better finding comfort in the realization that it was a song after all. Slowly, still gripping her along her thin, bloodied breast, Khadafi began to walk. He stepped over a husk of a form, but didnt look down. If he had, he would have seen the silent for of a young Japanese hooker. Her head had been smashed in, seemingly by a blunt object. It had torn a large chunk of her skull, allowing gray matter to flow out onto the dirty alley ground. Her body was bent in an odd angle, and a coroners report would confirm that half of her chest was caved in. One breast shorn almost completely off of the body. Khadafi groped down the alley blindly. His movements could be paralleled to that of a religious zealot in the throws of divine contact. Ali was slightly stunned to find out that he had a raging hard on.
