Discussion with Myself..

29 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Discussion with Myself.. *I lay there, peacefully on my bed, my eyes open as I stare up at the plain ceiling. The fan spins, throwing a light breeze across the room, keeping everything cool. This part of Japan got rather warm, especially during this time. The one, small window was open, the sounds of the packed streets, filling up the small room. Commerce,
Trading, and general business bounced off the walls of the small hotel room.
Everything seemed peaceful.. Everything seemed calm. Things are not always as they seem, as noted many times, in history, and in people. My mind was in shambles..
foreign agents manipulating it, causing stress between the three main functions of the mind. The Id, Ego and Super
Ego. These three Freudian Theories, are very true, and have certain roles in the mind.. The Id, being the child, wanting now. The Ego, knowing right from wrong, the adolescent, and then the Super Ego, being able to judge between the two, and plot and course.
The Adult. These three are supposed to work together in the mind, but in cases of Trauma, or stress, may separate into separate personalities. To digress, there is no such thing as a split personality, because the human consciousness is divided into three, therefor, you may only have multiple personas. Which, is what I happen to suffer from.* *Inside my mind, looking deep into the representation that we as human's are able to understand. The three are separated into separate area's. The
Id, being in chaos, a thundering tempest, on one-third of the portion. The Ego, being in a nice little spot, a Forrest type area, sun shining, calm. Everything happy. In between the two, is a null space, a place designed where the Super Ego mediates between impulse and law.* Cianan: This place.. is always so cold.. [with a shiver, he tries to cover himself from the pouring rain. The thunder, causing him to crouch down a bit. His damp hair falls into his face, but he brushes it aside with small gesture, to keep it away from his eyes.] Child: Are you proud of what you did? Cianan: Huh? What..? [He blinked, looking up at who spoke, peering across the void, into the happy valley across the way.. He frowned as he looks at the child.] Cianan: Proud of what? Child: What.. You did to Kensuke? [Cianan cocked his head to the side, glancing at the child, with a raised eyebrow.] Child: What did he really ever do to you, that warranted what you did.. Or tried to do to him? [Cianan scoffed, crossing his arms over his chest, and resting his ankle on his knee, turning his head to the side, looking away from his younger counterpart. He replied with much indignity, as though it pained him to be brought down to such, trivial matters.] Cianan: Of course, he overshadowed me at every turn.. He's a glory hog. He's younger, faster, a better martial artist.. But he's not me. I put up with shit beyond what he could ever think. I went through everything the hard way.. He was given it on a silver platter. [The child nodded, a frown on his face, obviously upset with the harshness in
Cianan's voice.] Child: Does.. That make it right? Cianan: Of course it does. He got in -MY- way. He over shadowed -ME-. Not to mention everyone else that came in fresh to CCW. Switchblade, Bryson.. Hell, even that windbag Ace Hawk was overshadowed, going for the Pacific Title. Child: But.. You almost ended his career.. You could have really hurt him.. That wouldn't be nice. Cianan: Not everything in this world is nice, kid. Child: You hate him, don't you? [Cianan paused, looking into this kids big blue eyes, it kind of made him shutter. His the hurricane behind him grew worse, and finally snapped him out of his little pause, but by that time, the kid was already smiling. So he just gave up.] Cianan: ....I just hate what he does.. I don't hate him. Child: He seems like a nice guy.. Apart from the time he went bad.. Cianan: Everyone goes bad from time to time. Child: Oh? Cianan: It's the way the world works. [As he said that, he took a small notice, of hollowness in his voice. It surprised him, he was hardened.. And it, well disturbed him. He remembered the same tone in his father's voice, back when Cianan.. Was little Akira
Dewitt. A small mixed kid, living in a tough off shoot of New York. His father was never there, emotionally, physically. He was unreliable.
Unstable.] Cianan: You..you're me.. I've seen you before... Akira: -smiles- taken you that long to realize it.. -he gives a small wave to his elder-self.- Cianan: Why.. Are you here..? Akira: I'm apart of you silly! [Cianan frowned, standing up from his perch on a rock, he finally looked around, to see what truly exists, the three separate sections. His section looking horrid, and dreary.. Why only his?] Cianan: Why am I over here? Akira: Because, you are what you are.. Cianan: And you're over there..? Akira: Because, I am what I am.. Cianan: And.. what's in between? Akira: He's gone, right now.. Cianan: Then why can't I move over there..? Akira: Because, you aren't him. Cianan: Who is 'He'? Akira: The Peacemaker.. suposabbly.. Cianan: Supposedly, you mean. [Akira grins, and gives a little nod.] Cianan: You seem nothing like me, when I was a child... Akira: Oh, you remember much? Cianan: I remember why I left.. Akira: Are you doing any better now? Cianan: A bit yes.. Akira: Are you a hero? Cianan: No, not really.. Akira: Oh.. [He frowns, and looks very disappointed, a pout on his lips.] Akira: I've always wanted to be a hero.. A guy everyone looked up to and admired.. [Now, it was Cianan's turn to frown. He was feeling, only slightly guilty at this..] Akira: Cianan.. Did you take a bribe from Mr. Miyagawa, to hurt Kensuke. Cianan: I guess, I can't hide anything from you, eh? [Akira nodded, solemnly, at least as solemn as a child can get.] Cianan: Yes, I did. Akira: I see.. [Another look or great disappointment, made Cianan wince.] Cianan: But, he betrayed me.. So now I'm going to have to destroy him. Akira: ...why? Cianan: Why did he betray me? Or why do I have to destroy him? Akira: Why.. Do you have to hurt him? Cianan: He wants to play games.. with me. I don't like that. I want to be feared! I want to be respected! He will learn, never to play that little game with me again. Akira: You want to be feared..? Cianan: I've spent the majority of my life, being fearful.. never having strength.. Now, I have it. Now, I have to use it to make sure, no one fucks around with me. Don't you understand
Kid? This is for us. To make our dream of never being afraid, or being used again true. Akira: ...Do you have to hurt him..? Cianan: Yes. Yes, I do. [Akira, looked down at his feet, and shuffled them in the green grass on his side. He finally turned around, and started walking away. After muttering only a faint..] Akira: ...oh... [Cianan watched him walk over the grassy hill.. holding his hand out.. wanting the kid to stop, because, when the kid left.. He would be alone. Alone was a very bad, and hard thing.. when you hated yourself.] [Cianan watched the kid walk, his head lowered, and he clenched his fist, driving it into the rotted corpse of a tree. As the rain kept falling..] *Then I woke up, angry.. and alone. Tears, were running down my cheek, and I angrily brushed them away, and stood up. I hit the thin walks of the room, in a blunt hammer like motion. Angry..
definately angry. Confused, and Angry.. Miyagawa was going to pay for doing this to me. Definately.* [Fin]
