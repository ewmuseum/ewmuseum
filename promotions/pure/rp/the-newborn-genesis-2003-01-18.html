The Newborn Genesis

18 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Newborn Genesis Written: January 18th, 2003 Think for a second, way back. Before all of the hoopla and parading of the new PURE, its ideas and style of life. I bet you don't remember the second edition in which it did close, temporary and only for a few seconds but happened to be brought back by the ever egonizing Jamar Wilson.
During that time, a young man by the name of Cordozer Stevenson, known to the fans then as Suicidal Stevenson, or S2 for short happened to be a gem to the family, known for his theatrics and ability to show the crowd a show, whether it be almost killing himself in the process. The war betweeen himself and Vito Wynn during one said match is legendary, shown last week for American television stations as just how sickening, how violent, and how talent Stevenson is and always will be.
But now, the times have changed and the ideal mindset for any man logged into the sport of wrestling is now known as he faces off against five other men, five other hungary contenders who all have a piece of gold on their minds. From there, you have Erik Blake, one man known to either bring his skills wrestling, or on the microphone trying to be atleast a poor man's Dante Jackson.
The only thing Cordozer knows about him, Dante Jackson lost to him in early two thousand, a week after Cold Fury in which Jackson won the IML Television title, a now dead and discarded title in the profession.
Each man to S2 is a growning enigma, some of whom he doesn't know and some he has a feeling for knowing since they grew around him. But none of them, not a single solitary one can counter to the statement that they've known what Stevenson is, what he was, and now what he will be in the future.
Its all a matter of time, before things start to break around him. - Excerpt from the Journal of Cordozer Stevenson January 18th, 2003 'Its an odd feeling to be back Desmond...' 'Same here, can't believe you decided to come back after all these years.' Running subway trains connect left and right, the sounds and screeching an all too familar sound for Cordozer, as he fixates his mind, leaning back listening to the lastest techno music by DJ Engyma as his confidant and PURE pyro technician Desmond Lewis stands on the opposite side of him, hugging to a train pole.
Reading his watch, approxamitely four thirteen snaps onto the face as the minute hands creaks slower towards the thirteenth dial as the hour hand comfortably rest on the fourth one stretching diagonally facing the nine a few inches away from it.
'Jeez, when's the last time you've ever ridden a subway?' the elder man remarks with a smirk on his face as Stevenson holds a walkman in his hand, previewing the next Flatlined show Sunday night on HBO. 'Two, three years? Hey, you hearin' me over there?'
'Wha?' Stevenson remarked dumbly, before shaking his head into amazement, a smile crossed against his face however as the track skipped ahead to an old song of Nasir Jones, 'It Ain't Hard To Tell'.
He nods to the beat, a slow caressing movement of not only the head, but contorting the body as well. 'Desmond, think anybody will ever take rap as serious as they do now?'
'Whaddya mean? Rap's at the forefront for not only culture but video airplay! Dante's even ganared a great -'
Cordozer shakes his head, 'No, I mean will it ever gain the same respect it did before? You can't flip the televisions here in Japan and not see a rapper questioning whether or not he should kill, have sex with loose women, or talk about his wealth.'
'Her wealth too, remember that.' 'Yeah, yeah, but still Erik Blake is back in rewind focusing on the Japanese culture, not me.'
Desmond shakes his head before taking a seat. Forty one years he has lived on this plane of existence, the only one known in the entire galaxy at the moment. And for all of those forty one years, he's known about wrestling all of three childhood years during his infancy. Since Dante had no time to pay attention towards him ever since the bitter rivalry with Chet King broke out, he enlisted Cordozer for friendship.
'Hey, that's him.' Desmond remarks. 'You can't tell a man what to do, he'll go off and do the opposite.'
A smile from Cordozer is all he gets for the moment, with a full lock of hair on his head, not to mention his compainion's flourishing look that gets him mobbings every time he steps into public square.
'So, who else is there?' 'There's Raident, but in the back he hasn't said much about anything to anyone, a complete silent person. I tired to get with him to break down what would occur, but he brushed me off.'
'I used to be like that, before I began opening up.' Stevenson taps his head as Desmond continues.
'Him, Blake, Chase Hererra one of those promising rookies from the last show in which him and a tag partner of his took on a waning pop group. Hoshi Akagawa, but he hasn't done a thing lately so count him out.'
S2 jokes, 'Three people to worry about? Then why the five man?' 'Ask Wilson, he knows.' Desmond responds quickly. As the train rattles on, the mindset of Cordozer is pretty focused, just tired from waiting on where he'll go. If not there, then to where?
The smile and confident jesture given to Desmond earlier returns as he sulks back in the seat.
'You have a plan?' 'Not a plan, but something like that...'
