Look who is back

18 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Look who is back Written: 2/18/02 ““FDW, FDW, FDW, FDW it is GONE!!!!! Get over it you son of a blank. Hello again I know it has been about 2 months and you all have been waiting to hear the golden voice of me, Pablo Rogers with the news on are young friend Furinto Age. Well he took the shutting down of his first fed pretty hard that is for sure. 2 months now the 18 year old has been moping around and I thought he decided to retire at age 18 when I got this shocking Video from him last night. I thought I’d never hear from him again and not only did I hear from him, but he rehired me and told me one thing and that was I have found my inspiration again. That word Inspiration he hasn’t used after he snapped in FDW and tried to kill Ryojin Tachikawa. I will not boar you any more as I will now play the tape. Just so you remember you will only hear my voice translating what young Furinto says.,” said Pablo Rogers
Static The scene opens up out side of an airport we are not sure what one it is till we see the welcome to Kendy airport sign. There is a bunch of people coming into the country. One family has 11 kids and the mom looks to be about due again. She looks over at Furinto and his girl friend. They are holding hands and share a quick peck on the cheek and she looks disgusted. She doesn’t say anything to them though and she walks off. Oh and by the way Furinto is wearing blue win breakers with black lines down the side of them. He also has on a FDW t-shirt. You wonder why for FDW has been closed for about two months now. He is also wearing a black shades with small FDW logos on them. His girl friend is wearing a black low riders and a red belly shirt* she is 16* they start to talk.
“Dang Americans can’t believe they consider you a sex predator just for loving me. I know you are older and all, but if we was in Japan where we belong we wouldn’t have to put up with this kind of junk. I mean think about it Furinto they are deporting you because of my parents. I don’t know what I’ll do , but it won’t be pretty my parents will pay I swear It.”, cries Raiza
“Calm down baby no matter how far they make me go away I will always love you and this plays right into my hand any ways. You see not so long ago I saw something on UPN and only way I can get in it is to go back to the motherland. It’s called PURE. I want to get purified. One thing Raiza that you need to remember nothing , I mean nothing can keep me away from me. Not an ocean not your parents and not even the government you are mine and I am yours forever. My sweet not even the big man him self can separate us. My plane is about to leave. “, said a hyped up Furinto Age.
As he walks away from here he grabs his suitcase and he takes his FDW t-shirt off and FDW sunglasses and throws them out in the trashcan. He forgot something though so he runs back to Raiza.
“Raiza will you take this ring as a promise to me and you that we will be a item now and till gods green earth turns red and burns up in hell?” , says Furinto Age
“ Well I don’t know FA my family would kill you if they found out about this , but how can I turn you down. I will always watch you were ever you end up and I mean it with all my heart ”,cried Raiza
He walks away again with only one last FDW thing left and that is the FDW featherweight champion ship that he beat Ryojin Tachikawa for at La Vendetta it never aired though so no one knew he held the FDW featherweight title. He holds it up and looks at it with his eyes cold as ever and he spits on the title. He takes his hair out of the ponytail and lets it hang free. He is still looking at the title as he sees the other FDW stuff he put in the trash and he dose the same thing with a solid gold title.
“You did this too me. You and fame and money .glory you courpted me. I saw you shine in the light and I would do any thing to get you even make another man bleed and forget my beliefs for gets my goals , desires. You did this all you made me another statistic that sold out. Well you know what you can burn in hell with all of my FDW past. I am not the taker of dreams no more oh no I am the maker of dreams again. I am the one and only lifeohlic again. I am free from your trance you are nothing to me and you know what I found something better and something I can do and keep my beliefs and get my fans back. Something that will not only shock America but the whole damn world will soon see just what I can do. When I get in the Pure ring it will be my Final Destiny not you. You are old news just like this whole crummy situation one thing though it did was give me the chance to go back home and well get Purified.”, says Furinto Age
He looks at the trash one last time and then pulls matches out of his bag and burns every thing in the trash. It starts a small fire. He looks at it and laughs as he sees his past burning up and his dragon tattoos no is flashing out for every one to see. The eyes are different though some how they look like they are at peace not war like they did in FDW. Flight 811 is leaving to Tokyo Japan in 3 minuets. He looks back to see Raiza face one last time as they are in tears. He starts to tear up a lil bit too , but he can’t look into the past and see the bad he has to look into the future and see the good.
He walks past a little kid wearing a Furinto Age I’m the Dragon of Life T shirt and he gets a smile on his face as he sees the kid is going the same place as him.
“Yo, Kid where you get that killer t shirt?” said Furinto Age He wouldn’t recognize me with out my wrestling attire I hope.
“ Sweet , Furinto Age you beat my teacher up along time ago. Her name was Amy Grunnel You showed her a thing or two she was a no good b!tch always giving us homework. “ , said the Kid.
“Yes I am Furinto Age and I did beat her up, I regret it I regret every thing from FDW I lost all my fans boy. I lost it all I even lost my self respect I was on top of the world well FDW was around , but at what cost I ask you?”
“Dude, Chill out I am going to Japan with my family to see Pure. I hear you have just signed with them and that is indeed why you are going back to your mother land of Japan.”
“Fluck that is suppose to be top secret… How you find out” “The Internet, it was only a rumor though are you saying it is true?” “I got a meeting with Jamar , but nothing is official. I want in PURE though it could do good things for me and I could do good things for them. I am however mostly looking forward into living at home again with mom and dad it will be like it was before. Raiza will come back too when she turns 18 so every thing that means any thing to me will be home. PURE I hope you are ready because Jamar has ever thing I just need to sign it all and unlike FDW. My wrestling life is not even close to being over with.”
“ Sir , I am still here so stop talking to you self.” “ Huh , what oh sorry so son. Just when I think of the future I can’t help but daydream. I am taking first-class so I’ll see you at Ikusa I just might have a match. Bye”
That almost brought a tear to my eye Furinto Age is indeed coming back to wrestling , PURE. Yes Pure the greatest Japanese fed of all time. Now they got the Dragon of Life. Well latter days Pokemon is coming on, after that Dragon Ball Z.
