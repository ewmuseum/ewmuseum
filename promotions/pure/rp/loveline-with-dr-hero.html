Loveline with Dr. Hero...? (Yes, it is that serious) Can things get any more complicated than they already are? Maybe... ...Maybe things arent as complicated as they seem. Maybe its the understanding that is complex...not the situation. A question that cannot be dealt with intellectually... A Koan... What drives a man like Ali Khadaffi to commit such deviance against someone that has not wronged him, nor has he ever met in life? Who even knew the answers to lifes queries these days? Religion? Government? Society? No...it is the individuals job to answer his own questions... Lest the individual remain a part of the mass...always depending upon the help of others. Why in the world was this Mang-A-Hero teamed with me in this tournament? Two opposite men from different worlds...drawn together by two goals... ...Victory... ...and justice. What good does it do to ask yourself these questions anyway? No ones asking you for answers. Her questions...she questions his questions? Only because she knew nothing of what mental preparation went into his battles every match he participated within. Of course she was also of a different world than Inphino Blitz. Mina de Margo...Mang-A-Hero...Ali Khadaffi...Carl Kimbell...they were no way near the realm where the existence of the Black Dragon Master resided. In order to know...you would have to be bred for the fight from birth. Shhh.There is no need for words... His serious, yet solemn voice crawled along the thin walls of the room like ivy...it was unforgettably saddening, yet charismatic...even depressing somewhat. Hers...it remained sociable...as if she had grown up with the natural gift of knowing how to talk to people. The days were fast approaching...the tag team match that could either start their new journey...or cut it short. In this tournament, we shall see where the path leads Mang-A-Hero and Inphino Blitz...two heroes of sort within a battleground of honor. And in the quest to keep this place honorable...they were going to have to put Kimbell and Khadaffi in their place. Prepare well, gentlemen...for this Sunday... you know what it means to be PURE... The scene opened upon a sunlit training room within the dojo of Inphino Blitz. Kneeling in the middle of the room before the tapestry of a spiraling black dragon was the Black Dragon Master...Inphino Blitz. Dressed as always...all black...he had his eyes closed and his blue and black hair tied back in a spiky ponytail atop the back of his head. His black nailed hands were folded in his lap as he meditated intensely. The screen to the left of him was open, allowing a small view of the garden outside the room. The natural relaxant of chirping birds and trickling water gave a serene atmosphere, helping him focus more. Sitting on a stand underneath the tapestry was a black sheath with silver design. The katana within the sheath had a black handle, and the guard was made of silver as well. It was as if everything was tranquil in his realm of fighters focus... ...As if... A sigh came up from behind him. Standing at the doorway was Mina, leaning against the edge of the screen with her arms folded across her chest. She was wearing a pair of gray sweats, black sneakers, and a white baby tank top with a white thong underneath. Her light brown-red streaked hair was tied back in a ponytail as well, though hers was more lengthy and flowing down to her neck. She watched with wavering eyes as Inphino slightly turned his head towards her. Hmmm...? his eyes remained closed as he addressed her. She smacked her lips in a expression of losing patience. You didnt hear me, did you? Depends on what you said... he replied, turning his head back towards the tapestry.  I asked you if you wanted to walk with me to the market so I could buy some things. Take our new friend, his head tipped a bit to the side, directing her towards another room. He could use the fresh air. A look of disinterest came over her face. I am NOT going anywhere with that...guy. If hes not talking like hes in a cartoon...hes dressing like he is. Inphino...I have a style reputation to maintain...I will not have it tarnished now. Image over interaction... he muttered. How interesting. Dont give me that, Phino! If it came down to a bunch of guys trying to mug me, Id rather have you there than the Comic Crusader there. You should know that he is more than capable of holding his own in a fight. I have been training a bit with him in the gymnasium, and he possesses skill. Mina rolled her eyes. Whered he learn them from? Street Fighter or Tekken? It does not matter where he obtained skills from...as long as he has them... Inphinos head lowered a bit more, trying to focus despite the conversed interruption. Aw, come on! Just take the walk with me, and then you can come back and train! she was getting irritated, stamping her foot against the pine floor in the hallway outside the training room. Can you just do this for me...PLEAAAAAASEEEEEEEEEEE????? Either it was the whining...the pleading...or the realization that she was not going to leave him alone...but Inphino stood to his feet with a sigh. He turned his head towards Mina, who held a smile of satisfaction now. His mellow eyes met hers and asked one question. Where is Mang-A-Hero anyway? TAKE THIS FUSION SWORD!!! HA-HA!! DONT YOU OPPRESS ME, CREST GUARDIAN!! YOU CANNOT STOP ME FROM SAVING ALLUCANEET KINGDOM!!! Mina and Blitz stood at the entranceway to the den, where Mang-A-Hero sat on the black-carpeted floor playing a game on the Playstation 2. His back leaned against the black leather sofa as his hands mashed against the buttons quickly. Mina shook her head as Inphino simply stared at him. Hmmm...is this what he has been doing all day? he asked his ally. Mina nodded. What is he wearing? Inphino turned his attention to his outfit...a white martial arts gi with a red headband across his head and a red spandex faceguard covering the lower portion of his face. A gi...nothing wrong with that... with that, Inphino headed for the door. Mina shrugged as she looked down at the hero. Phino and I are going to the market for a moment. Well be back in a few. The game paused, as he looked back, a confused look in his eye. Who? A sigh broke free from her lungs. The Black...Dragon...Master...and I...are going to the market. she broke it down for him good. Do you need anything? Well...the Black Dragon Master and I could use some Senzu Beans for after our battle with Ali and Carl on Sunday...though I doubt that they even be enough challenge to even expel a trickle of our energy! She stared at him blankly. Yeah...sure...whatever. she walked away, grumbling to herself. Stupid idiot...thinking Japans all about anime...Ill show him anime...get DBZ on his rear... As she walked away, the game came back on. A voice rang out from the screen, youthful and energetic. Im the Legendary Brave Fencer Musashi!! LEGENDARY??? YOURE NO WAY NEAR THE LEGEND THAT THE BLACK DRAGON MASTER AND THE BACK SMACK DRAGON HERO ARE, BUSTER!!! Brave Fencer Musashi...IM THE BRAVE FIGHTER MANG-A-HERO!! Inphino and Mina are shown walking along the side of the road leading into the town below their mountain residence. Along both sides of the road are pine trees, green and brisk. The forest around them seemed to breathe purity and freshness. Side by side the two of them walked in silencethe birds and the gravel crunching underneath their shoes the only thing breaking their quiet. Inphino had his arms folded over his chest, while Mina had her hands clasped together behind her back. De Margo looked up at Blitz. His head was lowered, and his eyes were closed; yet he was still walking along a straight path. She looked forward again, and then she too closed her eyes. Inphino opened his when he heard her stumble to the ground. Silently, he reached down and pulled her back to her feet. She thanked him, getting a nod in reply as he walked on. How come you can do that and I cant? she asked a bit disappointed. His eyes closed again by the time he answered. BecauseI have been trained my entire life to walk the path set for meblindly if I must. What path is it anyway? Mina saw his head tilt up a bit, as if he were going to look at her. But instead he lowered his head once more.  Oh, forget it. She sighed as she turned back towards walking. Their destination wasnt far offjust a few more minutes and they would be at the limits of the town. I have a simpler question for you thenwhy do you keep me around all the time? Pardon? she saw his left eyebrow twitch slightly. She must have caught him off guard with that one. I mean, when we first met, I wasnt even a real manager. I was just some groupie trying to bank off a journalism internship. But after all this time, weve managed to stick togetherexcept for that time when Akiyama  You could see the vein pulse in his neck at the sound of that name. Mina thought a change of topic should occur quickly. But besides all thatwere still working together. And what Im asking is why do you always keep me working for you and hanging around you? Blitz exhaled deeply. She knew that he would respond with something thought provoking. Minawould you suspect that I keep you around just because of your looks? What? No? Would you think that I keep you around for the conversation? She took a moment to ponder that option. Its possible. Would it cross your mind that I would be working with you simply for the fact that you are good at what you do? That one made her cheeks hue a bit. I hope so. Smile beaming on her face. Then Inphino opened his eyesand looked up into the sky. Or maybe it is because you remind me of someonesomeone that I would rather have nearbyinstead of off somewherein the unknown Your sister? she asked, thinking she had the right answer. Soteria? Inphino sighed before looking back down. His eyes snapped shut slowly, leaving Mina answerless.  Mina looked on aheadthe town was within jogging range. Silence provided the rest of the journey It was about three in the morninga shadowed figure padded groggily down the hallway. It made a right and stepped into a room, sliding the screen behind him. Inside, he plopped down on the mattress and submerged himself underneath the covers. The moonlight shined on the masked face of Mang-A-Hero as he went back to sleep. His eyes flickered open, and then closed back. Before he snapped them back open in shock. You are surprisedcaught off guardyou should always have your guard upyou never know where your next attacker will come from. Blitz was crouched at the sliding doorway leading into the garden behind the house. MAH stared with frustrated eyes at the Black Dragon Master sleep in his eyes heavy like weights. What is it, man? he asked lazily. Sleep I needmatch to fight evil This will not take long. I just need some advice. MAH raised his head, sleep leaving him slowly, but surely. You need advice from me? It shows the severity of the situation, does it not? Yeah, now come on and tell me whats upso I can get back to sleep. Inphino nodded, a sigh escaping his lips. WellMina asked me why I keep her around, and I told her that she reminded me of someone else. Then later on she was upset with me, asking me questions about different topics. When I did not reply, she grew angrier with me and stormed off. So now she is really upset with me, and I have no idea what she is thinking. Mang-A-Hero stared at him, mouth gaping open at the Black Dragon Master. He stared back awaiting an answer. WHAT WERE YOU THINKING ABOUT??! Blitz shushed him quickly. Quietyou will wake her. These walls are not that thick. Well what were you thinking?? he hissed lower than before. I was trying to concentrate on our match coming up. Her chatting really was not assisting my focusing. MAH shook his head at Blitz. Dudeyou compared her to someone elsethats not a cool thing to do when talking about another girl. When you speak to girls, they want to be #1 on your mindnothing else! Blitz had a blank look on his face. Waitshe just wanted my attention? Yeah! She did not have to get angry about itgeez MAH exhaled deeply, trying not to grow any louder. Blitzshe was playingthats allgirls do things like that. Its like a tease. Girls tease like that all the time just to get a reaction, like when you play fight with a honey and the girl says, Stop but she really doesnt stop! But if she doesnt mean stop, why does she say stop? Blitz responded in question. Dudedont ask questions about these things. You just play by the rules and you wont cause anyone grief. He nodded finallyan understanding look on his face. HmmmI think I get what you are saying. Thanks a lotyou are a lot better to talk to when you are not rambling on like a madman on sugar injections. Blah, blahnow let me sleepI have to defeat the Crest Guardians and punish Colonel Capricola and Soda Fountain tomorrowLumina will be free! As MAH returned to sleep, Inphino shook his head, standing back up to return to the training room. Well not much betternevertheless
