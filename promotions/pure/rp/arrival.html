Arrival Please fasten your seat belt as we are about to land at our selected destinationTokyo Japan, stated the lifeless voice of the planes P.A system. Ric Sensational Savage removed the hot flannel that had been covering his face and place down onto his black leather pants, as he rubbed his eyes to regain his sight, he glanced to the seat next to him and silently gazed at his slumbering partnerJohnny Scrumptious Starr. He gently nudges Johnny on the shoulder and whispers in his ear Wake up sleepy head, were nearly there, Johnny tosses back and forth before his eyes slowly begin to open, he stretches both arms high above his head and releases a large yawn. Whatcha want I was just having this great dream in where I was the Red Indian from the village people and was at MSG performing Y-M-C-A. Johnny said displeased. Were nearly here. Replied Ric What you mean where in the land of the rising sun, Johnnys tone had an air of excitement in it. Duh! Where else would we be going, you fool. Ric answered back, Johnny raised his right hand and place on his pink satin shirt on the chest area Ouch baby, that hurt, Im sorry that was a little harsh, here repent my sin, Ric places his left hand out in front of his partners, Johnny gives it a swift, soft slap. Owwzzies, I didnt mean that hard, bitch! Ric soothes his poorly hand its beginning to bruise! slowly tears begin to generate in the corner of Rics eyes. Oh, sorry honey-buns shall I kiss it better? Johnny pronounced in an apologetic tone. No, the damage has been done, weeps Ric, Anyways what do you think will be in store for us when we reach Japan? Who knows, I dont know how we are going to convince these traditional loving Japanese folk that Gay is the way, I mean what if they cant see past our homosexual antics and see the real people that we are? This is a more serious side to Johnny and one that is not often used. I guess the best way to go about it, is to gather every Gay Japanese man that is to afraid to admit his sexuality to the traditionalists of this rinky-dink island, and we parade the streets of Tokyo and spread the word that Gay is the way! Ric has now risen from his seat and has he hand on his heart as if he was pledging allegiance to the flag. Or not! Man that idea totally reeks, who care what they think. Therell have to love us or hate us coz were gonna be round for a long time, and no one can change that! I guess so, Ric returns to his seat What ever the weather its gonna be a NU ERA in the land of the rising son. Amen, brother! The plane began to rock unsteadily as the wheel s screeched along the tarmac surface, the dynamic duo gripped each others hand in a display of fright, as the plane sped towards the awaiting terminal, the two knew that this was it, there time to shine.
