Ali Khadafi v.2.0 Yo, Yo, Yo, DAWG! You gotta be droppin dat dope shiz!- VP Jamar to Ali Khadafi Monday afternoon over a couple of beers. Crying. Whining. Bitching. Moaning. Royal Hissy Fits. Thats all Im hearing. All Im getting is this whack static. I sit here with a smoldering roach of a Camel and listen to the big names, in quotes of course, of PURE cry. End, Blitz, Loc, you name em, they all sound like they got some serious diaper rash. Maybe a righteous outbreak of hemorrhoids. Sore throat, Inphino? Raw Rectum Loc? Seeing Eye dog piddle on your Keds, End? Lifes rough, eh? Yeah. Im back. No fan-fair for me. No big Welcome Back to Japan Ali! or We love you ALI!- hugs and smooches from the Underaged Oriental Girls for Unprotected Sex with Black Males. Just a big middle finger from all the fans and followers of the federation. Nothing but static. Why did Williams bring him back, hes nasty! ---First off, it was Jamar, a bag of primo bud, and instructions on how to use those handcuffs Mellisa slipped him. Second of all, you cant have PURE without Ali Khadafi. Its just like having Inphino Blitz without the long drawn-out promos where he relates everything he comes across- be it the cold pizza he had for breakfast or the color of his shoelaces- to his soul and how that effects his wrestling. You see, Ive put ten times more into this company than anyone else has. I dont care what any of you have to say. I rose up from being some black guy who had a penchant for beating other black males, to main-eventing at the last card. I wasnt given jack when PURE first opened, unlike some wrestlers, and I made a name for myself through blood, sweat, and tears. Those bastards you cheer for, Blitz, Loc, End, HIT; everything was handed to them on a silver platter with decorative and customized nameplates. Who did Blitz and MAH beat to win the Tag-Titles? Me and a jobber? A tag-team drafted into PURE for the finals match so MAH could score another undeserved pin? Good job guys, you really deserve the accolades youre being given. The PURE Tag-Team titles. Hot damn, thats something to brag about.--- What about Chuck Jung-Face-Mic-Skills-Charisma-like-Dung Loc? What about him? He spent half of the time he was in PURE laid up on the injured list. In other words he was worth less than me when he came in, wrangled a bit with Miyagawa and scored a win over a blind guy. He cant even speak in complete sentences. I cant wait for him to spit his ill game. You racist white cow licking bean! I kill you! I choppy choppy! I got this belt cause I great! And people diss blacks because of Ebonics. This kid sounds like hes got his nuts in a vice and hes trying to sing the star-spangled. Not to mention hes always threatening to stab you, furthering the stereotypical Asian image. Lets not forget the fact that everything in the world revolves around race and all white people want to beat him. God Forbid he see a group of black males walking on the same side of the street as him. I KEEL YOU BLACK PIG BANGING WHITE WOMEN TAKING HATERS! Yeah. Anyway. Im sick of talking about wrestlers like this. Thinking of them strategically instead of thinking them of slabs of meat cooling in a morgue. Trying to attack them like this. Its hard. I catch myself wanting to cuss them out. Im sweating so hard Ive soaked my shirt. Ive smoked half a pack and its only been ten minutes. Happy Jamar? People want me to drop shit. Want to see me flip out, but do it tastefully. Gotta keep it down. Were not trying to censor you, Ali, but dont swear.--- Unnamed owner of PURE, and theres only two so take your pick. Gotta get that mass appeal, Ali. Cant have you saying all those dirty words, its bad for our image. You dont see Cassius Deamont or Rhett Locke saying the f word as much as you, now do you?--- The other unnamed owner of PURE. So what do you want from me? I cant cuss, so what do I do now? Stare deep into the depths of my soul, go blind, change my race and try to act like a stereotypical black while pinning my eyes back so they slant? Maybe talk for hours until drool pours down my lip about how the stars have relevance to my bowel movements and what time my anime comes on. Sound good, Williams? Maybe I should gouge my eyes out? I could be the first black blind wrestler in the history of PURE! Think of the ratings, guys! Doesnt that sound great? This is frustrating. Not swearing. Not screaming. Not being in your face. I got it guys.I know how I can fit in So I can be just like Blitz, End, Loc, Prez Williams, and VP Jamar Ill castrate myself! Grow feathers and paint a yellow streak straight down my back! Aint that something folks! Hey, kiddies from the PWA, if you want to be recognized in this fed make sure you kiss Williams ass, Blitzs CLIT, feed Locs ego, and tell Jamar hes got a big dong! Dont forget to lop off your jewels too. Thats a must. If you dont then youre a disappointment. And youre going to be censored with the blind hopes that youll be promoted to the bigs. Buy into that lie and Ive got a bridge to Hawaii that Id like to sell you. What about the feathers? Dont know kids, ask Shabazz, cause he flew the coop like the big black FAGGOT that he is. Damn that felt good. Paint? Theres a can of yellow paint and a brush down the hall Just ask End But it might take a while, hes blind you know.
