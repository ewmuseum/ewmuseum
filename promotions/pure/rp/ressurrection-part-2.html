Ressurrection(Part 2)

9 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ressurrection(Part 2) ** The scene opens up. Vito Wynn and his buddy Randy who is supposedly his lawyer appear inside of a car. Vito Wynn is sitting in the driver's seat and Randy is sitting right next to him in the passenger's seat as Vito Wynn is driving. ** 'Well, here we are.' says Randy. 'Yeah, here we are. Now, tell me why I wouldn't know where we are, do you think I am as stupid as you are? Well, anyways this isn't the court house dick.' asks Vito Wynn rudely. 'Remember, I will be the one backing your ass up with all the evidence and proof I can remember or luckilly think of. So, you better shut up before I make you lose your trial. I hate your uncanny and arrogant self, okay.' shouts Randy. 'Listen up Randy, you don't look at me, glance at me, stare at me, talk to me, communicate with me any way possible, and the most important thing you need to know is that you never scream at me! So, get that through your thick head, okay.' Vito Wynn shouts back. .:: Vito Wynn continues to drive down the avenue and makes a tight left turn and picks up his speed. ::. 'Hey man, sorry about this whole argument. I'm only saying this because I really want to make you win this trial because good things happen to the both of us. I get payed more and you don't go to the celler. You started it though.' says Randy. 'Listen up, I can do what I want, where I want, and how I want to do it, okay! If there is any chance in hell that I get to kick your ass, I wouldn't have a second thought. How about you, would you do the same to me?' asks Vito Wynn. 'In a heartbeat, I can't believe that you aren't nervous or frightened at all that you are going to trial for attempt of murder which you deny. Without me you are nothing so you better pay attention and pay attention good. You are with me, I am not with you. Get that through your thick and hard head already. If it wasn't for me you would've just been sent to jail on the spot but I found you evidence that kept you out of jail, atleast for the time being.' explains Randy. 'You were in jail for fifteen years for not paying your taxes and you just got out about a month ago. I am pretty damn sure that you don't want to go back in such a short time for something you claim to not have done, or do you? If you do then you must be some sort of an idiot but I don't think you are one so I suggest you make the right decision for the both of us. Now, either we become friends and forget this ever happened or we remember this for the rest of our lives and I let you go to jail.' retorts Randy, in a low-key tone, politely stating it. 'Well, I see that now isn't the time to mess around with anybody, especially you so I'll stop fucking around with you, little man.' says Vito Wynn as he laughs. .:: Vito Wynn all of the sudden stops in front of a big court house where he takes out the keys from the car and steps out of the car, as does Randy. The two walk side by side next to each other while walking up a flight of stairs. They walk into the door of the court house as the scene fades to black. ::.
