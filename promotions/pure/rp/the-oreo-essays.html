This is getting tiring Shabazz. You are getting tiring. I turn on my television, and there your nappy ass is, bouncing all over my screen like some kind of Afro-Puff of crack. Do I spout off a queered form of ghettoes and reminisce about the days in the hood where I raped someones mother, shot their father, and sold two kilos of cocaine? Do I fucking look like Status Quo? Are you fucking retarded? Not every black man is a thug. And not every black man rages against the condition his life is in. Not every black man is concerned with the plight his people are in. Not every man has to sit on his self-righteous ass and spout racial slurs at every single white person that goes by. Dumb Cracka! You want a bullet in ya fuckin dome? Im waiting, praying, hoping for Mark Makonie to call you a nigger. Not some fucking cheap Word up, Nigga with that little Ah sound at the end. An upstanding member of the NAACP like you would probably have some kind of fucking aneurysm if a white guy told you that. You fucking cunt. Look around you the next time youre in the hood. How many of us are bling-blingin? How many of us have those big ass chains dangling around our necks? How many of us have the time to bitch out some white guy about an article in the newspaper? The only part of the newspaper were in is the fucking obituaries, and thats only if the police have enough time to dig up or rotting corpses. So what if Im black? Im too busy trying to make a living, put food on my table, pay my fucking bills, to bitch about it. Do you think people even care about color anymore? Sure, in fucking Hicksville, Alabama they might, but were in the motherfucking Orient for shits sake! None of these Mao ZeDongs give a fuck about the black mans plight. All they care about is how much money you have in your wallet. Cash or Charge? American Express of Visa I wish I were a fucking Arabian. I could blow myself up and take dozens with me and say it was all in the name of God. Or Allah Or Buddha Or the NAACP At least I wouldnt have to inhale your stench Shabazz. At least I wouldnt be forced to breathe in the ripe decay of your ignorance. At least I wouldnt be forced into a fight with a stumbling, bumbling jackass who needs to spats and congrats of his friends to make him feel good. Running around with your fucking Malice Crew; cant even stand on your own two feet, you pussy. Correct me if Im wrong, but arent all of the members in that little clique white? Are you the token black person, Jordan? Hasnt the NAACP fought against that very thing? Im sure if I cared enough about shit like that, I could find the instance that would prove my unerring logic. Then again, if I just let you go, little Oreo, youll hang yourself. Fuck. Youre already twisting in the wind, you dumb shit. The flies are already starting to fester in your eyes. I can see the maggots squirming around inside your mouth. The buzzards have gathered, waiting to pick you clean, and you have no one to thank but yourself. Fucking Retard. Im going to wait now. Im going to wait for you to put another one of your homosexual confessionals on the television, just so I can laugh when some big Okinawa shit starts beating you with his belt. Im waiting for you to start verbally jerking off, so I can kick you right in the dick.
