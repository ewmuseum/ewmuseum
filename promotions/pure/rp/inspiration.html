Inspiration

20 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Inspiration It was late Sunday night and finally Andrew Riven or better known to the world as Faithful Youth from his books of poems sold world wide finally arrived to his destination. Tonight was a time to relax and just take it easy. Of course he came for some inspiration into his new work but now was a time to just settle down. So natural he went to a local coffee shop. Lucky for him the people spoke English. He orders his coffee and looked along the walls of books for something to read. Nothing interests him. So he sits down and pulls out a pad of paper and pen. Well I came here for some inspiration. I guess there is no time like the present to get started, Andrew says to himself as he looks around. The little shop was almost empty. Just a few people who stayed up all-night and talked. Andrew stayed alone in a corner and looked around for something to catch his eye. He looked out the big open windows and into the Japan night. Then down at the circle table with a napkin holder and salt and sugar shakers. He then tapped his pen on his paper which singles he had something to write. I guess this wont take along. I at least thought it would have taken hours before I found something, he says quickly writing down the few sentence that pop into his head. He taps his pen some more looking for that write word to fit the sentence. He was told millions of times that not all his poetry has to rhyme. He would always answer with the same saying. When you sell a few million copies of books then you can tell me what to write. He wrote down the next lines and actually finished the poem in record time. Talk about inspiration. He read it over a few times making changes where needed. He finally gave it the Faithful Youth signature and left it on the table. He looked at the group off people beside them just to see what was going on. Would you like some more, a voice asked startling Andrew. He looked up at a young woman with an apron on holding a pot of coffee waiting for an answer. Andrew collected himself and looked back. Excuse me, he asked not knowing what she wanted. Would you like some more coffee, she asked again smiling at Andrew. Andrew nodded and she poured him another cup. She glanced down at the poem he just wrote and looked at it carefully. She almost split the coffee all over the table. Are you Faithful Youth, she asked. Andrew looked up. How did this girl know him? How did you know who I am, he asked knowing that she just read the bottom of the poem where his signature was. She pointed down at the signature and Andrew just laughed. She smiled and returned back to the counter. She placed the coffeepot back in the holder and walked back out to where Andrew was sitting. He didnt see her as he already had another piece of paper out ready to write again. Hello my name is Rebecca, the girl says as she puts out her hand in front of Andrews Face. This shakes him and he looks at her. Andrew, he responds back shaking the girls hand and not even looking up at her face. She sat down and just looked at Andrews eyes waiting for him to look up. He did look up and flashed a small smile. She gave one back and Andrew continued to write. She stopped smiling and tried to get his attention. So why did you leave Canada and come here, she asks. This got his attention. Was this person some kind of stalker? He looked at her. She was very attractive all right, with short black hair and a pink face. Andrew didnt say anything back for a while. He just was looking for the right words. Inspiration, Andrew responded back. A simple answer. Rebecca looked at him funny and waited for more then just a single word answer. Inspiration. That is all you came here for, she asked again. Andrew shook his head yes and continued to write again. Rebecca waited for something more. She tapped her finger on the table and waited. Why are you here, she asked again with a stronger ton of voice. Andrew looked up at her and she looked back. He grabbed the poem he had written before and gave it to her. She didnt know what he wanted until he pointed at the piece of paper. Her eyes started to gaze at the paper as she read the poem. I am a lonely man walking in a fearful place The only thing that brings me joy is the expression on your face I started to write not that long ago To control my anger that I would always show I lived in darkness, in fear and no hope It was like against ten men against me pulling the rope. I tried to fight back but it was without fail The odds where against me, it was tipping the scale As I grew older my mind started to turn I started to give, I started to take I started to learn But without warning hell came crashing down The ghost of my past came circling around I tried to run I tired to hide But the fear of my thoughts was killing me inside. Now I am here alone in this land Waiting for someone to lend me a hand She looked back at Andrew still writing his new poem. She wanted to say something but nothing could come out. Andrew spoke up first. Let me guess. You have no idea what I am trying to do or what any of this means. Youre not the only one. Even some of the best poets I know have no idea what I am talking about but that hasnt stopped me, Andrew says still writing his new work. The girl nods her head in acceptance. She still cant get any words out of her mouth. Let me just make this as simple as possible for you. I am in a New World, a new country with just me and my fears and thoughts to guide me. I have no where to run and no where to hide so I just intend on sticking it out. In this day in age everyone is against me. They believe they can write better and I really dont care if they can. I am happy with what I can do, he says shocking the girl in her place. There has to be something else. You wouldnt just come here to write, she says in a sarcastic voice. Andrew looks up a little annoyed that he isnt being left alone. He decides to tell her why he is here. I am here to write but Im also involved in that Japan wrestling promotion you people have here, he says back keeping his head down while he talks. You mean PURE, she asks getting a nod from Andrew as he continues to write. She tries to look for something so she can believe his story. She finds nothing that makes him look like a wrestler. How do I know your not lying to me, she asks. Andrew just shakes his head and finally looks up at the girl. He looks her dead in the eyes and speaks. Do I really have anything to lie to you about. We only just meet so why would I lie. She agrees and he goes back to his writing. He taps his pen a few more times looking for the right words. Rebecca looks around for something else to talk about. She looks out the window and over at the group of people near by. So why are you her, Andrew asks. Rebecca looks shocked that Andrew asked a question. She tries to answer but cant find the words to say. Andrew just laughs to ease the tension. I came here from the States looking for something new. So I guess I am like you. Looking for that little bit of inspiration. I tried my self at writing but it wasnt any good. I really enjoy yours. I wish I could be like you, she says. Andrews head shoots up and he looks at Rebecca with a serious look. She gets a little scared. No you dont. Why do you want to be me? Because I have the ability to write and make words come out beautiful. She the problem is you dont have faith in your own work. You should be Faithful in your own work before you have faith in something else, he says back. Rebecca again looks for more then what he says. He puts down his pen and looks at her. What I mean is, if you dont even like the work you put out how can you be like me. I love the work I put out. To many people in this world hate what they do. But yet they do nothing to fix it. If people would just do what they wanted everyone would be happy. Rebecca nods her head and looks to add something. Its not that I am bad its just it sounds a little childish, she replies. Andrew laughs and added, I was twelve when I put out my first book. That was a little childish but yet people still bought the book. You just got to practice and keep at it. Rebecca shakes her head and doesnt smile. Andrew looks into her eyes and waits for her reaction. She just keeps her head down and doesnt seem like talking. You probably didnt have any confidence in your own work. I was like that when I first started and look at me now, Andrew adds. Rebecca looks up; Yeah but you got started at a early age. I am twenty-five and I am just starting now. I bought even you can make up something just by looking at it and then just start writing. I know I cant Rebecca gets up from the table and walks back to the counter. She stops and turns around looking at Andrew sitting there writing. She walks back over. She tries to get a glimpse at what he is writing but cant make out anything. What are you writing about this time, she asks wondering where he got the idea. Andrew gets up and hands her money for the coffee. He looks down at her and hands her something. A piece of paper, the one he was working on all night. He closes the paper and whispers into her ear. Lets just say a little of everything tonight, Andrew says. He smiles and walks out with Rebecca still a bit surprised. She watches him leave and then opens the paper. She starts to read the paper from top to bottom When I look into your eyes Theres nothing there to see Only the pass of time And the faith I have in me The day I see my fate in you Is the time I came to see Theres nothing but my own mistakes Peering down on me. The promise I see upon your face Puts the weight of the world on my dreams But in the end we will truly see What my soul will become to be. The signature Faithful Youth is at the bottom and she looks out into the night looking for Faithful Youth who is gone. She smiles and returns to her work. Outside Faithful Youth peers through the window at the girl. Thats all everyone needs isnt it. Just a little bit of inspiration. With that he walks off into the night with his coat waving in the wind. He leaves the shop behind in search of his next inspiration.
