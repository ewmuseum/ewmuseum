To Honor the Dead... Its times like these that dreams are made in the eyes of the aspiring Times like these where one event can leave such a lasting impression upon someone that their life will never be the same again. Its times like these that legends are bornand the unbelievable happens its times likenow The finale of the PURE Tag Team title tournament The two teams with nothing to lose and only immortality to gain. Ebola IIIAlexander Miyagawa Mang-A-HeroInphino Blitz Two masked mentwo friends This match can be nothing more than spectacular. One for the record booksone for the agesone spoken of for years to come Ebola IIIa lasting impression hes made in PUREdonning the family mask and making it to not only the finals of this tournament, but the Jr. Heavyweight title as well. Hes a formidable foenothing more than great stuff from him. Alexander Miyagawathe bossMr. CEOPURE Ownertheres not much you can even say about this man. PCW legend, soon to be Hall Of Fameowner of the hottest Japanese Puroresu federation in the world. Hes made many accolades in his timeand made many friends as well as enemies. Theres nothing but greatness flowing through his Japanese-American veinsand do you know why? Because.hes.EXCELLENT. Mang-A-Herozany masked talent who, along with Inphino Blitz, has been tearing up the PURE world. The two have been just about unstoppable in every bout that theyve been in together, and even alone. MAHs unique (if not delusional) outlook on life pumps the crowd into a frenzywith his popular costumes similar to Anime characters and his quirky personality. Yet his talent is just as top notch as his persona Inphino Blitzthe Black Dragon MasterMaster of the Forbidden 999 Styleever since his arrival back into the PTC world, hes done nothing more than train. Hes been working hard to make sure that he gives his opponents the fight that they naturally deserve. To bring forth unworthy battle is dishonorable to one and ones opponent. Inphino looks at everyone with equality, knowing that everyone is capable of reaching their peak potential. He wishes to reach his own peak potential one dayand as he trains and forges his body into the fighting vessel that it is, he hopes to cause others to strive for their potential as well. This placeits not about beltsits not about egos and personalitiesits about nothing but the fightand the love of it. Every time when Inphino steps out into the arena that night, its like every single person has their adrenaline rushing through them. Its amazing how one man so solemn can bring forth the most extreme of emotions from fansits amazing how they respect him so muchjust for doing something that he was born doing. Theres a lot of respect for himoverwhelmingto say the least. The people respect him because hes definitely not like them. They can see it clearly. Inphinos life is something that most people could not go through. Before coming to PURE, he lived in the woodsthe beachesthe desertsthe tundrayou name it, hes been there. And why has he gone to all these places? For the mere goal of training his body in different atmospheres. He trains more than 6 hours a day and meditates for at least 3. Before PUREhe lived without the luxuries that we live with now. Why would he do such things? To perfect his art The Forbidden 999 Stylenot much is known of this techniquewhy it was made and why it is forbiddenbut Inphino utilizes it to the utmost degree. His unorthodox martial arts methods is what constantly keeps his opponent either on his toes or on his back. Acrobatics that would make Jackie Chan, Yuen Biao, and Samo Hung enviousSpeed that would have Jet Li looking twiceTalent that would match if not excel the immortal Bruce Leeand the brooding silent Demeanor that would make Sonny Chiba keep his distance. Hes accumulated so many allies within his stay herethe strong-willed Jordan X Shabazzthe heroic Mang-A-Herothe beautiful Yuka Hamadaand his current friendMina De Margoa woman whos been there since this whole adventure in PCW began. She has been there, seen it all, and personally felt some of the pain and hardships that go into being in this business. Saying business, were not referring to the wrestling businesswe mean the martial arts businessa business where talent and skill surpasses whos got the biggest mouth or whos the bigger man. This coming Ikusatheres nothing but magnificence about to happen in that ringa battle of the ages. PURE history in the making. The scene opened upon a cliff overlooking the Sea of Japan. Standing there with their heads lowered were Inphino Blitz and Mina De Margo. They were both in their usual attire, Inphino in his all black and trenchcoat, and Mina in her blue jeans, tank top, and bandana. The wind began to die down slowly as Inphino lifted his head slowly, brushing away a few strands of the black and blue locks. Fathers Daya day to reverence half of the couple that gave us life. He looked over to Mina, whose eyes were still lowered at the sea. Unfortunately, I never knew my parents She handed Inphino a black rose, which he held under his nose and sniffed it lightly. StillI honor you in death, as I know that you and mother died honorably in battle. I honor you in this bout as well that I am about to go througha bout where a championship will be decided. Everyone is skilledbut in the end I will have no other choice but to winso that the name of Blitz lives on despite your death. Inphino held the rose between two fingers and bowed his head. A breeze began to pick up as he tossed it like a dart towards the ocean. Mina placed her hand on his shoulder comfortably as he looked on into the waters below. The sunset reflected, bringing an orange haze to the area. The Black Wind howlsgive me the strength to prevail. With that said and donethe Black Dragon Master has spoken.
