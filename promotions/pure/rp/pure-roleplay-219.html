PURE roleplay 219

October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Written: It was a few days before PCW's Red October Pay Per View.  Lady Cat, also known as Savanda Carlson, and presented me with a harsh little conundrum, my past enemy, brought before my face once more, not a person, but a thing.  My addiction, I had fallen to it once again.  Injected myself, and nothing.
Nothing at all. Needless to say, I was heart broken, all that inner turmoil, for nothing.  The shit wouldn't even work now.  I was afraid, performing in front of a fan base I didn't know, and I had nothing or no one to help me.
Tears flooded my eyes, and I shook my head, I clenched my hand, slowly looking at the pierced vein, bleeding lightly.  I didn't know what was wrong.  I needed this for Red October.  I needed this to win.
'WHY WON'T IT WORK?!' I shrieked at the top of my lungs, that self deception had now wormed it's way into my brain.  I just sat back, trying to control my hyperventilating.  I tried to control myself, and think clearly.  There just HAD to be an explanation for this.
I was scared.  Afraid I have no hope. There was a reason I felt this way.  Ever since I was young, I had been told, I'm stupid, lower class, worthless, not good enough, a freak, something that shouldn't exist. 
There are only so many times that you can hear that, before you start to believe it.  Before it worms it's way into your heart and your brain, and it corrupts your self-image, and you start to believe you're not good enough.  You become insecure, and lost, in your own mind.  It makes you afraid to try something new, to fight without taking every stupid little advantage, instead of relying my own abilities.
When I look back on it.. What happens next in this story seems very fitting, and I blame no one for myself for what happens.
I sat there, tears streaming down my face, sitting on the floor, afraid, scared.  Yeah, I'll admit.  This was my first time performing for American's again, even though the event was in Japan.
It was only then, when I realized how stupid I was.  The door to my small apartment slowly creaked open, the girls were home from their shopping, and Savanda was the first to see me.  After one look, she crossed her arms over his chest, and shook her head slowly, pinching the bridge of her slender nose.
'Oh Cianan..' Was all she muttered. Akiko wasn't that calm, when she saw me, she shrieked, and slapped me across the face. 'You throw away everything we had? For this? For one stupid PCW event?  I don't believe you!  You bastard!' Again, she slapped me across the face, my head turned, a red mark appeared, and she started to cry.  Of all things, that's what hurt the most, Kelly Bowe, chased after her, and the door was slammed behind them.  To say, I felt rotten, horrid, like the scum of the earth, was to be too kind.  There was no word for how low, I felt.  And the anger I felt.
'Cianan,' Savanda started slowly, and carefully, 'These,' She picked up the briefcase, and hurled it across the room.  Her face was red, her eyes were an intense steely blue.  I had never seen her like this.  'Was your test.  In those little syringes, was a placebo.  Sugar water.  You threw away everything, because you have no respect for yourself, or anyone else.  You're a very stupid man.'
I winced, the harsh words more then just scathing remarks they hit my heart.  Savanda was a long time friend.
'You threw away Akiko, for this shit?'  Savanda continued, just staring a hole through me.  I couldn't help by look to the floor, as though it were my salvation.
'Noir ruined your life.  It ruined hers.  Because of this shit, Akiko had a miscarriage.  Because of your GOD DAMN insecurities, she lost her child.  Do you UNDERSTAND that?'
I muttered something, I don't remember what.  But, whatever it was, it was an unsatisfactory answer.
'I don't think you do.'  She sighed again, 'You made her cry.  You broke her heart.. AGAIN.  Twice, just because you're a stubborn fool.'
Savanda turned on her heels, stomping on the carpet, to the room, which held Akiko. I couldn't think straight.  Emotions swirled in my brain like a hurricane.  What was I going to do?  PCW.. no, fuck PCW.  PCW wasn't important, what was important was Akiko, and only Akiko.
I stood up, shaking my head, still trying to sort my thoughts, and walked slowly to Akiko's bedroom.  I opened the door, and saw Akiko packing.  I reached out, and grabbed her hand, 'No, don't!'
I was responded to with another slap, this one sent me staggering back into the wall, the rattle of the wall, set a picture loose, to crash down on my head, but I just threw it aside.  I'd deal with the blood later.
'Don't go Akiko.  I'll change!' She snorted, and grabbed her back, storming out.  Savanda and Kelly were left behind staring me in the face.  Kelly was the one that spoke first.
'You want to change, Cianan?  You prove it.  You show us that you can change.  I don't think you can.  So, just give up.'
I grabbed Kelly's arms and looked into her eyes, my long hair a tangled, and bloody mess, my eyes wide in desperation.  Kelly just pushed my hands away.
'We'll be watching you Cianan.  We'll be watching PURE.  You show us, you can change.  No Noir, no cheating.  Show respect, for once in your rotten life.'
'I have plenty of respect!' 'But none for anything important!' She shot back at me, 'Show respect for your fed, for your friends and, your opponents.  Show respect for yourself, and your own god damn abilities.'  She snarled and pushed me back.
'What.. Do you mean?' Kelly just growled, 'You mean you don't know?  Ever since we've known you, you've been taking the short cuts.  The cheap little shit you pull in every match.  You've cheated through everything: CCW, PURE, your life.  Stop it, be honest, be respectful, hell if not for yourself, then for the person you SUPPOSEDLY love.'
'I do love her!' 'Then prove it.  Until you do, goodbye.'  Savanda added in and with that, Kelly and Savanda pushed past me, and out the door, and out of my life, following behind Akiko. 
Three of the people who always supported me, and were always behind me, were now gone.  How was this fair?  How was this right?  I closed my eyes, and stormed into the kitchen, my emotions ablaze, as I snatched a knife from its block.  I grabbed my hair, and with a slow motion, I cut it off.
After I left home, I had started growing my hair out, as a sign of rebellion to my father, and mother.  It had NEVER been cut short, since I was eighteen years old.  I threw the lopped off pony tail into the garbage.
This was a new start, with new emotion.  This was a new game.  The prize was worth everything in the world to me.
This is how I ended up.  This is how I started, with my first match of my new life style, my new thinking process.  I lost Red October, but I didn't cheat.  I didn't cause any trouble, I tried my hardest, and even though we failed, I felt good about the effort I put out.
This is why I made my challenge to PURE. And, End.  I'm glad it was you that took up the call.  I have a lot to learn, and I hope you can help me with it.  Through the matches that we will have together, to be honest, this is new to me, and I need all the help that I can get.
