A Introduction To Yuka...

5 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A Introduction To Yuka... Yuka was born into a very rich Japanese family back in 1974, the Chinese year of the Tiger. Living in a wealthy house, Yuka quickly learnt that power = money. Her dad was an important leader of the Yakuza, and her mother was the rich wife of said father. She has two other siblings: her sister, Yuma, is a spoilt rich brat who always gets her own way, while her brother, Hiedo, was a very caring man who loves his sister very much. And the other one (but not by choice). As a young girl, Yuka never had any attention from her family. She was always the forgotten one, except by her brother. He always made time to play and look after his youngest sister. She was a very beautiful girl, and so always garnered the boys attention. Heads would turn as she walked past. So, as she approached her teenage years, she began to get fed up at the way her family would never pay attention to her. She decided, as she couldn't grab their attention, she'd grab the attention of the boys. She'd became a very flirtatious girl, always luring the boys on. Her sister venerably disapproved of her younger sister moving in on her territory, and would regulary humiliate her younger sister. Hiedo would always stand up for Yuka when her parents questioned her, and recieved many bruises because of it. Yuka has been forever grateful for his love, and gradually began to turn her flirtations off so that Hiedo would never have to be beaten by her father. But, one day, as she was simply talking to a young boy, Yuma had told her father that Yuka had been sleeping with boys for money. Yuka's father was very upset by this, and as Yuka talked to this boy, Yuka's dad ran up and dragged her by her hair, throwing her into his limo and driving off to home. He continued to byrate her. As they entered home, Yuka was thrown across the room by her father. Hiedo tried to stop his father from hurting Yuka, but he had had enough of the meddlesome boy. He pulled out his handgun, and repeated to shoot poor Hiedo. Yuka was shocked and scared... her only line of protection was gone. What would she do? Yuka did the only thing she could do... Yuka grabbed a nearby vase, and threw it at her father. As he ducked, she ran up and kicked him in the 'Genital area' and ran up stairs. She ran into her room, grabbed some clothes and provisonal supplies, and packed. As soon as she packed, she said a prayer to her brother... 'I am going to realize my destiny Hiedo. I love you.' And with that, she jumped out of her window, and flipped one finger at the house of hell. And she ran, ran towards her destiny. What it was, she never knew. But, it was going to be better than the death of her brother... As she tried to hitch a ride to Tokyo, she was shocked to find that her only ride out of town wanted something sacred from her as a payment. 'Give me some lovin' babe' Desperate to get out of Akita City, she agreed. After her deed, the man commented on how much of a great pornographic star she would make. She began to think of how much she wanted to be loved and wanted. So what if it was just her body being lusted for? She knew at least she had something going for her. And so, ever since she was 16, she has been a hooker/porno star. As her 28th birthday approached, she began to think. 'I've been doing this for a long time. And what have I gained? I haven't gained any worthwhile respect, or admiration because of her personality or any true talent.' And so, she began to think of what she could do. She was watching late night TV, and she saw... HCW. It made her think. Wrestling would be a good idea! She could be admired for something that requires true talent, not just a good body. And so, she continued to watch the man on the TV. Tecno. Tecno was HCW's 100 day-long Internet Champion. He has moves that were sheer beautiful. He had a grace of movement. And so, she decided: 'That man will teach me to wrestle!' And she took her money, and her clothes, and her life, and headed off to America. As she reached America, she learnt that HCW had manifested into EWA. And that Tecno was going to be leaving that very night. So, she had to get to Brooklyn quick! Her knight had to be found... Tecno was just leaving the arena. Yuka saw him, and ran. As Tecno had reached his car, he saw the stunning beauty drapped on his car hood. As he gave a quaint smile, Yuka explained her situation. 'Hello. What are you doing on the hood of my car?' 'My name is...' 'Look, I know what your name is. Your Yuka Hamada, Japanese Porn Star. But, why are you on the hood of my car? Wait... is this a going away present from Paul? MAN! He is a god! Alright, how much do I pay ya?' 'Hey! I'm not here to relieve you of your stress!' 'It's not stressful, actually it's very pleasurable...' 'I'm here because I am fed up with being a Porn Star. I want a change in direction! I need a new job!' 'Er... I don't have any openings as of now for TeCom, but that chicken guy over there needs someone to dispense the cola.' 'No, Listen! I want to become... a wrestler.' (Smile from Tecno here) 'Really? You? A wrestler? Well...' 'Please! I'll do anything!!!' 'Anything?' 'Not that though.' 'Ah poop.' 'Please?' 'Well... alright! Just come around to a big glass building. It'll say TeCom E on it. Ask the receptionist for the Risk Disk. She'll do the rest.' Looking forward to having to teach her a lot of 'grapple' moves (hey! he IS English!), he agreed to help her. And so, they arranged to meet. And Tecno taught her a very BIG crash course in wrestling. 'Oh, come on! It isn't that hard! You've done so damn much with your ass know, why be scared of falling on it!?!' Teaching her the style of Tecnology (Luncha mixed with Martial Arts and Technical Wrestling), Yuka began to reveal a new talent, besides her usual talents that Tecno was a big fan of (!). Her birthday approached, but rather than go to Japan and spend it with her friends, she chose to wrestle. Tecno arranged for her to wrestle in a match for her birthday, but never knew what she wanted to do with her wrestling. He realized her Japanese values would of meant she would most likely of gone to Japan to wrestle (in which case, she would have to wrestle someone who has a knowledge of technical wrestling). But, her American attitude would suggest a career in Sports-Entertainment would be very profitable. As he asked her the question, she stopped and thought: 'what DO I want to do?' After much deliberation, she decided that Japan was a much better option. If she was going to prove she had other talents, then she must prove it in Japan. Tecno knew of her past, and didn't recommend New Japan Pro Wrestling, like she wanted. 'I want to go to New Japan.' 'What? With your past?' 'Why not? Would they not accept me?' 'It's not that... it's just Antonio Inoki has connections...' 'Who is he?' 'Who is he!?! VERY famous Puroresu legend. He's on the New Japan board. Owns shares in them.' 'So?' 'Well, the Yakuza has big ties in wrestling. And Inoki has contacts that MIGHT lead to the Yakuza. I wouldn't want to find out if he has by seeing you in a body bag to be honest.' 'Well, where else is there?' '...I'll sort it. Don't worry. Now come on!!! Only 26 more press-ups!' 'I thought we were having a break?' 'Yeah, and break's over! Get to it!!!' He was unsure of Antonio Inoki's connections with the Yakuza, so he agreed to help her get into another fed that he knew of. As Yuka wrestled her first match, she quickly impressed with her speed and agility. Tecno, meanwhile, talked to an old 'friend' in HCW: Inphino Blitz. He told him of what to do to get Yuka into PURE, and after Yuka lost her first match (but heavily impressed the crowd and Smart fans all over the world), he told her she'd be going to PURE. 'Right. We've got a fed for you to go to! PURE. It stands for Puroresu Ultimate Ring Entertainment. It's very classy and has a great emphasis on wrestling. An old co-worker in HCW is there. Inphino Blitz. I helped beat his enemy up with a laptop once.' 'Right...' 'You'll like it, trust me.' 'What's the competition like?' 'They have few women there as of now, but there is quite a big roster compared to other feds right now.' 'So I'd be fighting men!?!' 'Look, you were trained by a man, in a man's enviroment, and with a man's mentality. What you do there will be just like this.' Tecno and Yuka both went off to PURE to see if she liked it, and she didn't like it... 'Do you like it?' 'No...' 'WHAT!?! I HAD TO PAY ¥3000 FOR THESE FIRST CLASS SEATS, AND ¥60000 FOR THE HOTELS!!!!!!' 'I LOVE IT!!!' 'I MEAN... what?' 'This is wonderful...' 'Oh.. kay! Well, I knew you would like...' 'Love!' 'Sorry... LOVE it. So, I can let you lose on this place without me then?' 'No.' 'WHAT!?!' 'Join me. Be my manager! You know how much you love Puroresu, and Japan! I can learn off you, and you can learn off the Japanese!!!' '...alright. I'll join. BUT, I ain't carrying your clothes or anything else!' 'Who said I'll wear clothes? *wink*' 'HUH!?!' She immediately felt as if she had found a family to call her own. They quickly signed a deal, and here she is! Yuka Hamada, PURE Superstar, and PURE of heart! 'Hiedo, I'm home!'
