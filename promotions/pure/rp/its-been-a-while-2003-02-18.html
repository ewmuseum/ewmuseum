It’s been a while…

18 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It’s been a while… Written: February 18, 2003 It’s been a while… Since I’ve had to fight for my life. Since I’ve had to worry day and night. Since I’ve had to live my life in fear. Fear? You fear, Chase Herrera? Well, of course. Doesn’t everyone? I take a lot of risks, I understand that. But there is always the fear that wells up inside of you.
But a person has to understand how they want to go out in life. How they would want to die. I would want to die doing a crazy risk. I wouldn’t want to be murdered.
I guess there are some things that many of you may know about my start in PURE. Before PURE’s reincarnation, someone, something, he was haunting my entire life.
“AHH!” someone screamed. It was the scream of a woman. I was at a club. But it felt like I had been here once before, not too long ago. At the time I had heard the scream, I had been dancing with a beautiful, young, latino woman.
I thought nothing of the screams I had heard at the moment. I swung her around… Then, I saw it. I stopped dancing. She stood there and looked at me. “What is it?” she asked. I saw a shadow. It had gleamed on the wall right in front of me. I could see two figures within the shadow. One had been struggling.
It’s him, I thought to myself. Why did this feel familiar? I ran through the crowd and headed toward the screams I heard from the woman. I made it to one of the corners of the clubs and the screams grew louder. I could hear a knife tearing the skin of the woman.
He was now clear in my sight. I ran… I jumped… I jumped right on his back, hoping to bring him down. But he was too big… too strong. I had then noticed the woman’s screaming had ceased. I supposed she was now dead and I had been too late.
He swung me around, struggling to get me off of his back. WHAM! He slammed me into the wall, but I still held on. This fight, it seemed so familiar. But why is my memory so blurry? SLASH! He slashed my with his knife on my arm. No big deal to me. I wasn't scared of a cut on the arm, but there was no way I would take him down. Sooner or later he would end my life instead of giving me a cut on the arm.
He flipped me over his back. He looked at me straight in the eye. I could see him clearly now. He wore a mask. A white mask, which represented a sad face. The facial expression on the mask showed it. The mouth hung low along with a black tear that could be clearly seen below the eye. He looked at me with the eyes that have haunted me for so long. It had come down to this.
I was about to die. Everything, my life, all coming to an end. He raised his knife... I woke up… It had just been a dream.
