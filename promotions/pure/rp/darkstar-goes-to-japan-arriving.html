DarkStar Goes to Japan: Arriving.

22 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
DarkStar Goes to Japan: Arriving. '15 minutes until we reach our final destination, please remain seated and buckled up during our descent.' Talena looked over at her sleeping lover and gently nudged him with her fishnet clad elbow, he muttered something intelligible. She just sighed and looked forward as the in-flight movie wrapped up, she hadnt even been paying attention to it. The brutally long flight had taken its toll on both of them... but it was worth it. DarkStar had the chance to prove himself as a international sensation. Talena couldnt seem to believe that she was more nervous about this stuff then he was. Then again he had alot more of those little airline drinks too. First class totally rocked it. She nudged him again too no avail, noting the fact her lover always slept like a rock. She knew how to stir him though, 'Oh my god! Anime!!!!!', DarkStar woke up with a shot, looking around him for people with large eyes and mech suits. 'Wha! I! Neon Gene-... fuck. That was mean Talena.' She smiled, you could almost picture the halo over her head. Well... had she not been evil. 'So, you're actually serious about wrestling over in Japan for a little while.' He nodded, half tempted to say he wasnt serious at all, and he just took this plane ride for the hell of it. 'I figured it wasn't fair to other cultures that they never had a chance to see me wrestle live. Its the least I could do as a ambassidor from the states to the small country of Japan.' It made sense. In Japan wrestling was alot different then in the states. In the states, DarkStars talent has gotten him high on the cultural standing. He entertains teleivion offers, commercials, movies, and sponsership deals. He has toned down his image to the marketable point it is at now. Not here though, in Japan it was different. This was about respect, he could spin it any way he wanted to, in Japan he could become a legend. Hallowed in the books of lore that the people in the crowd will speak to their grandchildren when they take them to their first wrestling event in a few years. DarkStar smirked as the announcement came over the airplane that they would be landing in Tokyo now. He felt Talena grip his hand tighter, for she had a almost terrible fear of flight. Kind of funny if you think about it, shes been to hell and back (literally) with DarkStar, and yet a large machine that floats through air just disturbs her. The landing gear touched the ground, and the plane bumped up and down a little. As it slowed and began to coast towards the awaiting terminal, DarkStar new that this was the start of it all. He may be stuck up, he may be cocky, he may be evil but he may be cheered, but he knew that he would go into that ring and put on a clinic the likes of the Japanese havent even seen. Now he has a chance to become a bigger legend. Now he has the chance to become a bigger god.
