'Don't disappoint us.'

28 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
'Don't disappoint us.' - Everyone has a moment in their lives that they can look to and call the moment that changed their lives. That one single second, minute, hour, or what ever the time might have been when they looked at the way they had lived their lives so far and realized they must change. They then spend the next few months really beginning to understand the power of their momentous change. For some that may consist of how they look at a certain person of group of people. For others it is the way they choose to see a certain event or chain of events. The changes can range from just the a small change to an entirely different outlook on how they interact with the world. For McCain his change was very important. His has caused him to not only change the way he views the world, but also to feel remorse for all the things that he has done in his life. For years he never knew how it felt to be on the other side of the line. The poor, under fed, at times homeless side. The type of guys he made fun of in high school are the ones he has begun to help since that faithful night just after graduation. Questions rose...Questions were answered...Still there was much to learn and much to see...- The sounds of gears jamming, tires squealing, and glass shattering ring out through the otherwise quiet night air. Slowly the focus begins to fade in and all that can be seen is the stars and bright moon shining brightly in the dark, almost black, sky above. Slowly the sky begins to focus in more and more clearly until the young man begins to slowly move around. His head aches, but other than that he seems to be just fine. Finally he slowly sits up and looks down the road, in the direction where the car had come from and they were headed in, and sees nothing but a long strip of back road. Trees fill the view all along the side of this road. Finally the young man stands up and tries to clear his head enough to think. I was pretty lucky. I hope the others are ok. Man where did that guy come from? the young man began to think to himself as he slowly turns around toward the direction of where his car hit. His eyes fill with shock as he surveys the scene before him. His car had indeed struck the big old oak tree on the right side of the road. His car seemed to almost wrap totally around the huge trunk from the impact of the accident. Tears slowly began to fill his eyes, as he noticed the bodies of his four friends that had been in the car with him. Bloody...Torn...Broken...Lifeless bodies. No this cant be! They have to be all right. How could this happen to them? Why them and not me? the young man yells as he starts toward the car. Suddenly, however, his stops dead in his tracks. His legs seem unable or unwilling to make a move. He cant be sure which one it is that is holding him in his place as his mind is on only one thing at the moment. He fills himself grow cold as he notices the body in the driver seat. The drivers head smashed into the steering wheel and blood pouring out. The figure is...was HIM! He quickly hit his knees and began to cry uncontrollably. His face buried in his hands so all that is scene is the darkness of his closed eye lids in his hands. More questions began to rise in his head...or the head that used to be his. Why has this happened to me? Why them, what had any of us done? What is going to happen to me now? As the fear of what would happen next filled his spiritual body, a soft voice could be heard in the distance. Very low and gentle at first but gradually getting a little louder as it seemed to get closer. The young man raised his head and began to look around but could see nothing more than what he had before. Still the voice got closer. Finally he was able to make out what the voice was saying. You will have to go back Zander. You must right all of your wrongs. the voice softly came forth. What wrongs? And go back where? McCain managed to get out of his grief torn mouth. You must go back to your life. You must change the way you are and show compassion to those you once shunned. This is the price you must pay for your wicked ways. The voice now closer than ever and still very gentle. Price? What is the price my soul? McCain asked the air around him with complete fear and confusion. The price was the lives of your friends. The ones who, along with you, made the lives of countless others very miserable. This is your price. You have been chosen to change your ways and better your life. Chosen one? But what of the others? What happens to them? Zander asked very weakly/ They have had to pay for their sins with a eternity in Hell. You have been given a second chance because there is a bigger plan for you. You will have the ability to help a great deal of others and make their lives better. You also have in you the will to do so. The others...They simply didnt have the will, therefore they have paid for their sins in another way. Do you wish to end up like them Zander? The voice seemed almost amused at the thought of even asking this question. NO! I dont wish to be dead or to go to Hell. I am only eighteen! I will change my ways, and I will be a better person. Please just dont end my life here! McCain spoke with great fear in his tone. Very well then. You have accepted your place as the chosen one and will be given a second chance. Dont disappoint us! Suddenly the voice was gone. McCain began to look around until suddenly a great deal of pain shot through his body. He quickly went to the ground screaming with blood pouring from his head and cuts and scratches now covering his body that had not been there before. The sound of an ambulance can be heard coming from the distance as well as police sirens. As the lights of blue and red begin to fill the sky Zander begins to fill as if he is slipping away. He struggles to stay awake, but his eyes begin to close until... :::The out shot opens to McCain suddenly sitting up in his bed. Sweat is pouring off of his body and he is breathing hard. He is in his bed in Japan. He looks around and begins to shake as he places his face in his hands again. His blue pajama pants are drenched with sweat as he gets up and goes toward the bathroom. ::: McCain: These nightmares are going to kill me one day. I only hope that I havent disappointed them. ::: He walks into the bathroom as the scene once again fades out to black on the sound of rushing water coming from the sink in the bathroom. Then there is no sound. :::
