Vito Wynn Written: 10/11/02 The scene opens up appearing Vito Wynn sitting down in a jail cellar on a little bed thinking about something very closely.
'How can I get myself out of here?' Vito Wynn asks himself. 'Hey, how are you doing?' asks a voice above Vito Wynn. All of the sudden Vito Wynn looks up and sees a man on another bed on top of him. 'I didn't know there were bunker beds as cheap as this.' Vito Wynn says. 'Well, this is jail dude. Oh, and if I were you, I wouldn't try and think about ways of getting out because when I first got in I also tried to think of ways to get out. Well, I'm still here so obviously it didn't work. So, I wanted to do my time in here. So, what are you in for?' asks the man.
'Possibly murder, or maybe because I almost murdered someone. Which I didn't do, so don't be afraid of me. What are you in for?' questions Vito Wynn.
'I'm in for drug abuse and trying to carry twenty pounds of cocaine over the Mexican border.' the man replies.
'Wow, have you ever accomplished to do it before?' asks Vito Wynn. 'Millions of times. That's easy to do. I even did forty pounds before.' answers the man. 'Did you ever get caught before?' questions Vito Wynn. 'Nope, it's just that I'm sort of a starter though. A starter compared to all the other people I know who do it. Why are you so upset.' asks the man.
'I'm upset because I have a match with some guy called 'S2' Suicidal Stevenson on Saturday which is tomorrow and if I'm in jail then I will be declared the loser by a forfeit and I don't need that. I know I can really kick this guy's ass, too.' Vito Wynn mentions.
'So, you are a boxer or a wrestler, right?' asks the man. 'Yeah, I'm a wrestler and in a wrestling career it isn't good to lose not even one match.' answers Vito Wynn.
'Well, I'm going to take a little nap since I don't have anything else to do.' explains the man.
'Okay, bye.' Vito Wynn says back. Vito Wynn then begins to think really hard on how he can escape out of this nasty jail. All of the sudden he abruptly smiles evilly as the scene fades to black.
