A return to WRESTLING

2 April 2002

A return to WRESTLING A return home, a return to Japan...... 'My bags are packed, and I am leaving my future, my family, and all my past experiences back in the states' Brian Simon awaits at the LAX Airport, his luggage by his side. After a multi hour flight from Philadelphia, a tired, yet alert look is on his face. He takes a seat at terminal BB, where his Air Japan Flight 234 in route for Tokyo, Japan is about about a half hour away from departting. 'I gave my all here in the states, and the fans didn't care one bit. I wrestled injured, I gave 110% each and every night, only to have the fans boo me because I didnt have a flashy gimmick.' While in the states, Simon relied on his wrestling training from Dory Funk Jr and his hours of watching Japanese wrestling tapes. However, the American fans did not take well to Simon's approach to wrestling, leaving him distraght at times. 'I know I was not respected, but you see, that is why I am going to Japan, because that is the only place in the world where they respect WRESTLING!' Simon glances around the airport, crowded as it is. People walk by, some hurrily and some not. No one recognizes Simon as he stares at the people. 'They dont care. If I raised a eyebrow or had a silly catchphrase, then maybe I would be recognized, but ableit, the effort is not worth trying for. The states have left nothing but pain and despair in my heart, time and time again, opportunities were promised, but never granted. I do not care how much success I do have over in Japan' As a kid, Brian grew up watching stars like Inoki, Fujinami, and Baba. But, the aspect he cared about the most were the Junior Heavyweights. Names like Dynamite Kid, Tiger Mask, and The Cobra all were familiar faces to him, yet none of those stars are active anymore. Brian knew that in Japan, stardom was a second nature, but that respect came first. 'I truly do respect everything in the Japanese culture....the people are great, as I found out with my tour there two years ago. I found that Japan is a peaceful place, not full with the corrupt politics of the United States. ' Simon slowly gets up from his seat and glances at the screen, looking for his flight once again. An announcement comes over the loudspeakers.....FLIGHT 234 TO TOKYO IS NOW BOARDING, FLIGHT 234 TO TOKYO IS NOW BOARDING, THANK YOU. 'It seems the time has come to leave America behind me and to start a new beginning. PURE fans, my arrival is imminent, as only a few hours away, I step on Japanese soil, and begin to make my mark, step by step. I'm not going to spit green mist in the air or wear a mask, I'll do the one thing I do best.......WRESTLE!' Simon slowly turns away and picks up his carry-on bag. As he walks into the hallway that leads into the airplane, he takes one last look at America, before shaking his head and entering the hallway. A few minutes later, the airplane takes off, leaving all the memories that Simon left in the states at the LAX airport.
