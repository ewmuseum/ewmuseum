Untitled roleplay 152 *Cameras turn on to a set of wooden doors, and pan back to see a sign that says: Knights of Columbus: Frederick R. Kealy; council 3548 With the camera still focused on the door we hear Phreak start whistling as the 6 6 man comes slowly walking towards the door. The cool night wind blows as Phreak is still wearing his shades at night, along with green camouflage attire and black boots with a camouflage headband on. He stops in front of the door, where a security pad is involved. He raises his hands and quickly types in the code as the camera approaches his shoulder. Phreak looks back after opening the door and motions the cameraman to enter the room. *The walk all the way through a small lobby and down a long hallway where two doors are on his right with a water fountain separating them. He opens a big metal door where he sees many patrons lined up at a bar, sitting on barstools of course, and all watching a TV at either end of the bar. The smell of tobacco & alcohol is strong, and the room is a little drafty. The patrons all momentarily stop their conversations and all turn their head over their shoulders and look at Phreak who gives them all a wave. * Phreak: Hows everyone doing tonight? *Phreak must have been here many times before as everyone answers him at once, yet he is able to pick up everyones response. He walks over to a heavyset guy who appears to be about 31. He takes a seat beside him and they start talking. * Phreak: Hey Andy, hows your brother doing? Andy: Hey Zach. Hes alivethats it. How about you? Hows your wrasslin career coming? Phreak: I was currently sent to a wrestling academy. Its helping me out for when I get re-hired by another company. Its going good though. You up for a game of pool? Andy: Dude, Im wasted, and you want to play pool with me? All right. Ill play. *The two men descend from their stools and turn to their right, and continue into the next room is where a green pool table is not in use. Phreak puts two quarters in the mechanical device and pushes them in until all of the pool balls come fall down, then re pulls back, causing them to fall out of their holder. He gets to the end, and racks up the pools balls in his specific way, which also happens to be the pro way. He grabs a cue stick, and with a smile on his face he grabs another stick and hands it to Andy who tries to grab it, but misses. The alcohol he has consumed is now taking an effect on him. He tries one more time to grab the stick, but successfully takes control of it. The drunken friend of Phreak tries to take a shot, but in doing so, he passes out on the table, bent over. * Phreak: Andy? Andy? Are you alright man? *A patron from the bar yells across from the other room. * Patron 1: HOLLYWOOD! *A smirk comes across Phreaks face as he replies* Phreak: Hey Gil, how are you doing? Gil: Not bad, not bad. Your dad was in here just a minute ago, he was checking up on the machine. He seems to be doing all right, but I dont think everythings fine with him. *Phreak doesnt have a look of surprise or anguish on his face, but just merely tells Gilbert whats going on. * Phreak: Gil, hes a diabetic, and hes deaf in his left ear, what more do you want from him. Waitis tonight Karaoke night? *Phreak starts to get a little eager as Gil starts nodding his head. * Phreak: Ill start! I already know what song Im going to sing, and it isnt country by a long shot. *Gilbert now gets an awkward look on his face as Phreak descends to the karaoke machine. He puts in his own tape, and grabs the microphone and places it in his hand. He starts tapping his foot and keeping a count as a small amount of volume plays through the speakers, but Phreak quickly changes that as he turns up the bass, and volume and starts to sing Monster Magnets Space Lord. Phreak: Ive been stuffed in your pocket, for the last hundred days, when I dont get my bath, I take it out on the slaves, so grease your baby, for a ball on the hill, and polish them rockets now, and swallow those pills, and sing awwww (continuing) SPACE LORD MOTHER MOTHER... *Phreaks microphone is cut and he looks around to see an upset bar, and Gilbert hanging onto the microphone cord. * Phreak: I guess you didnt like that. I must go home nowI think I left my oven on. *Phreak quickly exits the room and proceeds out of the whole building back out into the darkness of night, Phreak walks back to his car as the cameraman follows. Phreak looks into the camera with a laughable look and starts laughing as he starts to talk again. * Phreak: They had no taste in music. Oh well, this is proof that a criminally insane mind, and a private bar that has a karaoke night, dont mix. Oh well. Beware, take care, for Phreak, comes out at night. *Phreak gets in his car and starts the engine, then proceeds to drive off as the camera fades out. *
