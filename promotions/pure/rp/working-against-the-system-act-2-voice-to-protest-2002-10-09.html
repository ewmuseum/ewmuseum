Working Against the System: Act 2 - Voice to Protest

9 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Working Against the System: Act 2 - Voice to Protest Written: October, 9th 2002 I have been fine with the system up until now. I don’t mind if they ask me questions but when they call me wrong or say I am not good enough to be with them that is wrong. But of course when they need help, the first person they come to is me. It’s like they need me to send a message to them. But once the message is received and they get their point across I just get tossed aside.
Well not this time. Enough is enough, it’s time for me to make a stand and shout out and say for once that I am the force behind all that is good, evil and anything in between. When have I ever cared what someone else thinks? I have had people telling me to stop and quit applying more pressure all my life, but do I listen? Of course I don’t because I am in control and not easily taken advantage of by others.
Unlike the rest around me. People being told where to go and what to do by so many. If I want something done I won’t wait for permission for the higher person to do it. I will take the first hand and do it myself in any shape or form I see fit. Who here will actually say to me that is the wrong thing to do? Of course, everyone will say it because they believe they are correct and that I am wrong.
So where does that lead us now? Right back to square one where I am being controlled. Look at it through someone else’s eyes for a change instead of your own. To me you are all just following a system that was set up so you wouldn’t worry about the world’s problem and that everything would have an answer. Every question would be answered and the human race would live in peace.
Now look at the world as it truly is. Every day someone dies or is injured, all for what. That’s right, the system. The very same system designed to keep us all safe is what is killing us. Whole countries and entire governments depend on the system to guide them through tough times. No wonder the world is at war. Everyone goes after the same thing because the system says so.
So my question still remains. Why must I have to put up with any country or group that will not allow me to live my own way and that forces their people to go by and guide by the book. I spit on the book and say who needs it. I am my own person and don’t need some book to tell me where to go and how to do it. I am free and believe I am in control. I go where I see fit, punish whom I see fit and react any way I see fit.
So what happens to those who don’t go by the same system I do? Nothing that is what. I don’t follow any system. I am completely free to do what I won’t. If I feel like punishing those for their crimes, I will without caring what the man says. I do what I want and there is nothing that can stop me. Or is there?
Voice to Protest It’s your typical day, with a nice warm sun, bright light and blue sky. Oddly enough this turns into the way of voices. Vilas is alone walking again trying to get a grasp of where he is and why he brought himself here on this day. A normal walk down the street with the warm brisk air passing through his hair and his body filling with the light of the suns raise. Minding what was only in front of him Vilas carefully walks on, always at alert just in case the system pops up in front of him.
Nice day it is, but of course there always is a catch. With a positive action there always is a negative reaction.
Passing by big store windows and shops lining the street, Vilas carefully makes his way down the town to some place. Still not deciding where he is going, Vilas just walks up and down the city streets. Last time his turn took him into a situation that the system was against but couldn’t contain. Of course Vilas could have done something but he wasn’t asked for help.
Stay away from trouble. You don’t want to get your ideas and thoughts in another death match do you?
Vilas slowly walked passed people that didn’t look like any type of threat. To bad you can’t judge a book by its cover or Vilas may have stayed home today and not let himself be dragged out of his home by his own self confidence. Faster and faster seemed to pass by, one after the other. Each one with something unique about them but more then likely, they where all part of the system.
Each person alone is smart, but together in a group they are more ignorant and rude then anyone could imagine. How true is that?
“Excuse me, but would you like to hear his voice?” Vilas slowly turns around and faces two men and a girl. Maybe in their late teens, early twenty’s. So alive but wasting there time on a system that uses them. Vilas already know what they where talking about, but he was playing their game. He had them in his palm and he hadn’t said a word yet. It was just child’s play really.
Here we are again. More people that believe they can change my views and make me work for their greater power.
The three people stood on a single space as Vilas slowly turned around faced them. They looked at him smiling and thought it was all right to speak. One of the men decided to continue on with his words. He just opened his mouth when Vilas quickly put his finger up to issue silence. All three faces went quite and all emotion was wiped clean. Vilas moved his hand and slowly moved on his way.
Don’t turn me around again kid. I gave you a warning now just take it and leave. “Excuse me sir, but you never did answer us. He wants to speak to you,” said the young girl. Vilas stopped and shock his head. He was already a few steps away, maybe about ten or twenty, but yet he still was able to make out what they where saying. He turned around and walked back and looked down at the three.
I told him and he didn’t listen. I gave him a warning and he decided to listen to his systems rules and go on till I either tell him to go away or shut up, which ever came first.
Vilas closed his eyes and took a deep breath. He calmed himself down and looked for the words. He smiled at the three and they smiled back thinking he wanted to actually hear what he had to say. He looked at what appeared to be the leader of the group of system worshipers.
“I know what you are going to say and really I have heard it enough times,” Vilas said as the three looked up at him with a question mark on each face.
“Just to let you know I don’t need you trying to change my views and ideas. So if you wouldn’t mind leaving,” he added before taking a few steps forward.
I knew what would happen. They would try to change my views yet again and once again I would have to make a scene and make an example of them even though I did much care if anything happened.
Vilas face became angry as one of the men asked him to please turn around and talk to him. Vilas laugh an angry laugh to himself and quickly pivoted around to face the three. He slowly walked towards them and they backed up towards the wall of a bank made of solid brick. They had no where to go. They where trapped and had to listen.
“Before you go on about how great he is and how wrong I am, look at yourself and see how wrong you are for stopping me and asking me to change,” he shouted out, making a few heads turn. But not enough to make anyone stop and see what was really going on.
“But sir we just wanted to talk to you,” the women responded with a hint of fear. Vilas smiled down and responded, “Yes, but talking leads to discussion and that leads into you trying to change me. I have done this more times then you have ever came outside. Look at yourself. I thrive on being my own person and don’t let my job; my employer or even my faiths get in the way of anything. You on the other hand believe nothing of your self and are just like everyone one else. A program running in this system.”
Calm down and just gets out of there. You have done this before and don’t need another violent outbreak.
Vilas slowly just let go and looked way. By now they’re a few people looking right at him, but when did that ever matter to him. All that he cared for was himself and that is what he looked out for. What happened to the three people where up to the system now. They where controlled and Vilas didn’t want to free them.
Don’t free someone that doesn’t want to be freed. That just makes matters worse for life and for them as well. I got out when I was young and now I am on my own. I regret coming into contact with them but like normal. I chose to come out and they chose to talk to me. But I think I got my message by clear enough. They got the idea that I am not the person who is to be treated like a program. I am my own free will and don’t need something to keep me alive. Message delivered and I believe the message was received loud and clear.
