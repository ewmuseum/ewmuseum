Those evil eyes will forever haunt me.

16 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Those evil eyes will forever haunt me. Written: 10/16/02 Fear is the worst thing you have to fear Because the fear you fear is always near No matter what you fear the most it will haunt you until you die It will rip at your soul and tear at your heart until you break down and cry
If you fear a fear that's stupid
or one that makes perfect sense
Then over come your fear as you live your life I promise you will be less tense Have you ever feared that when you die it will be one of your fears that caused it
So suck up, be brave, and encourage yourself so you can overcome it.
*** Some people constantly claim to be the best wrestler in the business. Maybe some of them are. I don’t claim to be the best. I know that I’m not. But as much as people want to think about how I, Chase Herrera, am a rookie in the business, no one will ever do the things I will. No one will ever take the risks I will take. But then again, maybe some will. Maybe there are people out there just like me. People with no fear…
They called me one of a kind… Through some eyes I was. But who else is out there? There are other risk takers. Are they going to go out and take my spotlight?
Why are you thinking like this Chase? Why was I thinking like this? It wasn’t like me to think like this. Usually I don’t really care. I don’t care to be the best.
I just wanted fun. I didn’t care if there are other risk takers. I didn’t care if there was someone that may show me up one day by their performance against me in the ring.
I just wanted fun. Life was fun. That’s all life is to me. Usually I never thought too much, or ever tried to think too much. It was boring, and it got depressing.
I bet I was only thinking because I was on this stupid airplane on my way to Tokyo, Japan. I looked over to my right. There, my manager, Dave, sat, his face red, hands shaking, and looking around as if he were paranoid.
I nudged him on the shoulder. “What’s wrong ‘migo?” “You know how planes and I don’t mix,” he said in a shaken up voice. “Oh, you’ll be fine,” I tried to assure him. “It’s not like the engines are going to stop and we fall toward…”
“Enough!” he demanded. “Okay, fine, fine,” I said. “But anyways, what match am I in again?” “You’re in a tag team match, your tag partner is this guy named Damian, and your opponents are these guys that call themselves H.O.T.” he answered.
I was never too in to tag team matches. It required having a partner. I liked to work on my own. It wasn’t about who got the spotlight, but it was about who got pinned, or who you can trust.
Stop thinking so much. Why am I still worrying? This plane ride made me think, probably because I had nothing else to do. Usually I do have something to do, and it would just wash away all of my worries. I couldn’t wait to get off of this plane.
“H.O.T.?” I questioned. “Oh, never mind, I know who you mean now.” He stiffly sat up in his chair. “Well?” he questioned me. “Do you think you’ll win it?” “Who knows, but I will make a promise,” I answered. “I will promise you that I will put on the best god damn show I have ever put on. I don’t care if I win or lose, I am in it to show what I can do, and that’s all that matters.”
Did I tell the truth right then? Did I really care? “We are now going in for a landing,” said a feminine voice over a speaker. “Thank you for choosing Southwest Airlines.”
A few seconds later, we came to a landing. Dave and I stepped off of the plane and went to retain our luggage.
The weirdest thing happened after that. I turned my head for one split second; I looked over to my right. Then I saw him. I saw those devilish eyes. Eyes that I would never forget. Eyes that I could have sworn were possessed.
It brought back the memory… ***FLASH*** I walked over to my car. I opened the door and got in. I started up the car and headed for the starting point. I looked over and saw one of my opponents. He sat there with a cocky grin, sunglasses, and a black bandanna. I’m not one to study someone, but this guy looked confident.
VROOM! VROOM! We sat there waiting for the flag to wave. Suddenly, I saw the flash of a white flag. I lunged my foot onto the gas pedal. I was off.
The rush. The danger. It felt good. I loved it. This was my life, to live the danger. I was winning with the guy that wore sun glasses and a bandanna not too far behind. Suddenly, a shadow crossed my view on the street. My headlights flashed on the shadow to reveal a very big man, not bulky or overweight, but muscular and tall. He looked straight at me as I sped toward him. He just stood there. He stopped right in his tracks. He looked right at me with eyes that I could have sworn would be of a possessed being. He did look like he was going to move. He was going to make me lose.
I struck the brake with my foot trying to come to a halting stop, but I was too close to him. I swerved over to the right.
ERRRRRRRR! CRRRRAAAASSSSSHHHH! ‘Mr. sunglasses and bandanna’ rammed right into me sending my car a few feet forward. I wasn’t hurt. I just hit my head on the steering wheel quite hard. No big deal. A broken bone wasn’t even something that bothered me, it was like a scratch.
***FLASH*** I looked over to my left, the dark figure walked on after the chaos. Those evil eyes will forever haunt me. He stood there in the shadows, I couldn’t exactly see him, but his eyes, I could see them. I wasn’t usually afraid, but his eyes freaked me out.
“Hey, grab your bag,” Dave said, noticing I wasn’t paying attention. I turned my head to look at him, and then turned back to the devilish being. He was no longer there, he was out of sight.
Had he been following me? If so, why? I had a feeling I would find out sooner or later…
