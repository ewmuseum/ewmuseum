Teaching the Young

22 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Teaching the Young Written: 22nd Jan 03 Newspapers scattered variously around the scantily decorated room, wrappers of snacks intrude the floor and other bits of rubbish clutter the apartment in the heart of Japan, Tokyo. The front door opens as Ric Montana and Johnny Quinn enter the room fully clad in exercise clothes, sweat dripping from their intense stricken bodies.
“What the hell happened here!” bellowed Johnny as he looked around the ‘trashed’ room. “I’ll tell you what happened.” Ric replied firmly, “it’s that adolescent half witted brother of yours, that’s what happened!”
“Wait till I get my hand on him. JOSH! JOSH! Get out here now.” Look of seriousness on Johnny’s face was quite intimidating.
One of the bedroom doors creaked open slowly and out stumbled a dazed and confused Josh, scratching his head he looks at Johnny and Ric in a baffled way. He manoeuvres over to the sofa and slumps down landing on an old pizza box.
“What’s all the screaming and hollering about?” a puzzled Josh asks. Johnny looks just about ready to explode and goes to lunge himself at Josh but is stopped at the last second by Ric.
“What’s the screaming about!?! I’ll tell you what it’s about, it’s about the mess you have caused in this apartment, the rubbish, the anarchy and the sheer disrespect you have for me and Ric, your no the only one living here you know.” Johnny concernedly said though Josh did not look like he was paying attention to Johnny’s ‘big brother’ lecture.
“Whoa, whoa, whoa. Hold on a minute bruv this ain’t all my doing ya’ know!” Josh replied in defence.
“So what all this magically appeared did it!” “Might have.” “When are you going to grow up a little Josh? I give you the opportunity of a lifetime by flying out here to Japan and taking part in a internationally known wrestling promotion and this is how you repay me.” Said Johnny with a certain amount of sternness in his voice.
“Damn right.” Ric chirped in “The only reason I’m out here is because with the new introduction of the trinity division you needed a third man, and even though you don’t like to admit you know there’s no one better than me, and it just eats up inside that your lil’ bro is better than you.” Josh scornfully retorted
“Oh is that so.” “You know it’s the truth.” Snidely replied Josh. “I admit you’re a great talent, but you keep up this attitude and you aren’t going to get very far. PURE is about earning respect, and if you show none towards others then expect none to repaid.”
“Whatever” Josh casually replied as he slumbered off back into his room before the slamming the door hard behind him.
“Don’t you think you were a little harsh on him?” asked Ric “He needs to learn, or he’ll end up flat on his back with no help from anyone. We do a tough job and without the right mental as well as physical tools your not going to get anywhere in this business. He’ll learn I’ll make sure of it!” responded Johnny.
“Well he’s your Brother you know best.” “I hope so, for all our sakes!”
