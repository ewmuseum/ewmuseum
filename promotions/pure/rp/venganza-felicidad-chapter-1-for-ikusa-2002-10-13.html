Venganza= Felicidad! (Chapter 1 for Ikusa) Written: 10/13/02 It was a night I’ll never forget…………… *whistling of the wind* Victory had come and crossed my path countless times in my career, but I was never so pleased with it as I was last night.
Last Night… (Sigh) The scientists say I’m unreal, the doctors say I had some genetically mistaken disorder. I…well I think I’m just normal.
My past…I have no past. A future is all I behold, but even that is something I don’t know much about anymore. My brother had a future, or at least he though he did. Mind games are what dug his grave. May he rest in peace?
I had a mother, that’s all I really know. What kind of child has no past? That’s what everyone says. Well I guess I don’t. What kind of man has such unreal strength? My answer was always a man with nothing to lose, because I have nothing to lose, until now. Romantic, well that’s what my fellow Spaniards called me. It’s something about woman that I can’t be without. Loneliness is what I fear, and what I wish to have. Confusing? Not for me.
Woman love a brave fighter and a great lover. Not to be vein, but I am, I was born like this. The only thing that makes my love different is that I HAVE NO EMOTIONS. My heart, well I don’t even know if I have one. The first feelings I ever felt were ones of sadness when my brother died, but even those were self controlled. I wish I didn’t have to tell myself to cry, but I do. I wish I didn’t have to tell myself to love, but I do. There are a lot of things about me that are mysteries, but there is one thing that shall never be…
His name is Jung Loc, I never enjoyed brutalizing a man like I did him. It fathoms me why such a righteous God creates such an evil and coward figure to enter a complex universe. The day he dies is the day I’ve done my job.
When people look at me, they think of a few simple things. They think of me as a God among woman, a mysterious figure, and as the best Spanish fighter to ever enter the underground circuit. It’s true that I am all of those, but so much was mistaken. There’s more than meets the eye, but unfortunately the eye is all you’ll see.
Grappling… ¡Ah Romantico, está con mí para siempre, PARA SIEMPRE! (Oh Romantico, be with me forever, FOREVER) Those were the things I heard all my life. To me forever was a word; I never thought of it much. Then I realized forever might not be long at all, so I took things at a quicker pace.
I devised the plan of all plans to come here in PURE. My goal was to kill Loc. That goal has become a little more distant than I hoped, but it will eventually come. Like I thought he’s ran in fear, leaving the money and luxury behind. Poor Junzo Muto trying to come out as a brave soul, does he not know why Sir Loc was running? Well Muto will soon realize why.
*Nods* Tan Joven Tan Estúpido As for now, well now it’s one day at a time. My life, it’s nothing special, some may think it is, but those who do are living in a delusion.
I… WILL… GET………. MY…………… REVENGE………… And that’s the only thing I care about. A title is just a pretty gold trophy, revenge is happiness and happiness my friend beats all. Happiness is something I’ve heard about so much and wish I could feel just once. The day I feel one second of happiness is the day I die a happy man.
The scene zooms in on the cold full lips of El Romantico. Venganza! Felicidad!
