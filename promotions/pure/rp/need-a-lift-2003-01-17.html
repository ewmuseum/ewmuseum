Need a lift?

17 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Need a lift? Written: 17 January 2003 The sky is red and the air is warm. It’s the transitional period where day becomes night. On the right side of a road in the Japanese countryside, Jason Marah and Patrick Craig are slowly walking along. Marah is wearing nothing but a black tank top, blue jeans and a pair of walking boots. He’s carrying a large sports bag in his right hand and a long, thin plain black bag (which we can only presume is the holding place for his sword.) and on his back he is carrying a cream rucksack. Craig is a little more concealed wearing a pair of cream slacks, a grey sweater with the collar of a white t-shirt showing around the neck rim, a grey cap and he is also wearing a pair of walking boots. Craig is only carrying a black leather suitcase. The road is dilapidated, obviously minimal attention has been paid to it from the local government, as potholes are present, along with cracks and the remains of “road kill”. The pathway Craig and Marah are on is seems like pure clay. As they walked forwards, Craig and Marah scan they’re pathway and surroundings and a less-than-impressed expression plastered onto their faces.
Jason Marah: Hardly the great oriental countryside I expected. Patrick Craig: I didn’t envision this either. Disgraceful really. As they continued to walk, they could here a motor engine slowly getting louder. The noise they haven’t heard since walking down this path. Jason and Patrick looked around inquisitively. A black object was slowly coming towards them and the image began to get clearer. The object turned out to be a long, black limousine with a newly washed shine to it. The limo pulled halted beside Craig and Marah. The driver got out and so did the man in the passenger seat. Both of oriental descent and both clad in a solid black suit. The driver’s much larger than the passenger. The passenger tried to grab Craig’s suitcase but Craig put his hand on the man’s chest pushed him away. At the same time the driver tries to retrieve the bags in Marah’s hands. As he tugs, Marah tightens his grip on them, the veins in his forearm starting to pulsate. As the man tugs harder, Marah’s grip tightens again. This tug o’ war continues until one of the tinted backseat door glasses lowers to reveal Binsei Kinaka.
Binsei Kinaka: Ah Jason, how are you? Jason Marah: Was in a bit of a fighting mood, heh, with these guys here trying to take me stuff.
Binsei chuckles as Craig looks on with concern written on his face, not knowing Binsei or his relationship with Jason.
Binsei Kinaka: Sorry about that. I just told them to put your stuff in the boot and I could give you a lift to wherever you’re going. I guess I should have asked but I’m not used to asking.
Binsei grins and Jason gives a half-hearted laugh. Binsei Kinaka: Do you want the lift or do you want to continue to slum your war through this dirt pile? *Binsei smirks*
Jason Marah: Sure, why not? Jason hands his bags over to the large man, except for the sword carrier and Craig gives his baggage to the other man and they are put in the boot. The driver opens the backdoor and allows Jason and Patrick to step in.
Binsei is the only person in the back of the limo. The limo is perfectly furnished. Brown leather seats, beverage machines, television – the whole works. Marah is amazed by the amount of functions available in the back of this car. Jason mutters “Unbelievable” to himself. Craig looks around cautiously.
Binsei Kinaka: Binsei Kinaka, nice to meet you… He says as he extends his hand in friendship to Craig. Craig returns the gesture. Patrick Craig: Patrick Craig, feeling’s mutual. Binsei looks at Jason, amusingly. Binsei Kinaka: I take it you like it, then, Jason? Jason Marah: More than you could imagine. Binsei Kinaka: So, where are you gentlemen headed anyway? Patrick Craig: Kawasaki. Binsei Kinaka: Ah, the host of Ikusa tonight, I believe. Patrick Craig: Yea, just by coincidence though. We have to catch a train from there tonight to the home of the next Ikusa.
Binsei Kinaka: Oh! I’ll tell you what. I’ll bring you to the Ikusa tonight and the one next week in this limo IF you can get me and my friends in the front here good seats at the show. What do you say?
Jason Marah: I dunno, I think the owners of the arena would be pretty pissed off if we started stealing their seats ya know?
Craig clatters Marah across his baldhead with a slap. Patrick Craig: He doesn’t want the actual seats, he wants tickets you ninny. Marah nurses his head as Binsei nods in agreement with Craig. Jason Marah: Oh, okay. Sure, I’ll go to the show and you can come with me backstage. I’m sure it wouldn’t be a problem. I’d get ya tickets but it’s all sold out.
Binsei Kinaka: Backstage, eh? Um, sounds good. Deal. Jason Marah: ‘Tis indeed. Scene fades to black with Jason trying to find his new favourite drink, Jerau, on the beverage machine after failing to find Guinness, of course.
