Getting to PURE after PCW Fusion

13 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Getting to PURE after PCW Fusion The scene fades in with The End getting out of his Silver Navigator in front of the Marriott Hotel near the arena where PCW Fusion was held. The End is coming from a match with Bobby Stormbringer and Charlie Loc with fellow Dark Age Member Darkstar. The End: I dont think I need to remind you that Im blind and shouldnt be driving a car Handler: Your other senses make up for your lack of sight! Now shut up and stay in-character! The End opens the trunk. 'One day away... one day until the match, and everyone who doubts me is proven wrong,' The End thinks to himself. The End checks his SUV at the front loop at the door of the 30-story hotel/resort, graciously provided for by PCW. The building is large, painted beige with Spanish architecture. Behind the building, the nature path leads to an Olympic-sized recreational pool, a cabana bar, and a game room with table tennis, billiards and standard 8-ball pool, and bumper tables. The green-suited bellboy that looks exactly like Gary Coleman (wait a second, it IS Gary Coleman) takes The End's bags while Bobby Brown (yes, the REAL Bobby Brown; working at the Marriott to fulfill parole) parks his car. The End steps into the hotel and walks over to the counter. There arent too many guests in this hotel. There are more ex-celebrities working menial jobs for Marriott since they cant get work elsewhere. The employees of this particular Marriott include RuPaul, Marty Janetty, and Pauly Shore. Theyre all standing around at the entrance, waiting for people to come so that they may take their bags (and some even put them in the guests rooms). Back at the counter, he presses the bell since no one is around. It takes a few tries, but someone finally answers. From the back area behind the counter, a woman stumbles out half-drunk and with her blouse un-buttoned (and if she had a bra on, she doesnt anymore). The woman is Rosie Perez. Rosie Perez greets him and does her usual routine without much effort. Her hair is worn up and is very big. Her dress is a green womens suit, the company standard. Rosie Perez: 'Welcome to the Marriott, how long will you be staying with us?' The End does a double take at the sound of her voice, making sure it's Rosie Perez. He does indeed recognize the voice, and is surprised. 'You're Rosie Perez,' The End comments, speaking as though he's made some discovery. Perez perks up a little bit, happy that someone recognizes her. With a smile on her face, Perez answers more affably than before. Rosie Perez: 'Yeah. It's nice to see that someone recognizes me.' The End continues the small talk. 'Well, after that horrific performance in White Men Can't Jump, how could I not recognize your obnoxious voice?' Perez's smile shrinks a bit. The End continues speaking. 'What are you doing here?' he asks. Rosie Perez: 'Well, you know, with J-Lo taking all the work, and Penelope Cruz being around, I don't get much work.' The End waves off her reply by clarifying his point. 'No,' he says, 'I meant what are you doing working at the Marriott? You're at most Red Roof Inn-quality, but no more.' The End thinks, 'I'm not exactly being nice. I dont have to be; its okay seeing as how I'm a heel. I really dont like any of these people. Theyre filthy and vulgar. But just wait until I get to PURE in Japan if I annoy these people, just imagine how bad I can be with the Japanese Rosie Perez grimaces menacingly at The End. She reaches inside her big hair and pulls out a 5-inch bladed bowie knife. Rosie Perez: 'Don't f*ck with me; I'll cut you.' 'That's cute,' says The End, taking the knife from Perez's hand and tossing it behind him. 'ARGH! F*CK! WHO THREW THAT KNIFE?!' The End turns around to see bellboy Gary Busey curled in the fetal position on the floor, holding his right leg in pain. Fellow bellboy/washed up actor Burt Reynolds is trying to pull out the knife. The End turns back to Rosie Perez. '... Anyway, I'm The End with PCW. I'm supposed to have a room booked here.' Rosie Perez checks the reservation files. Rosie Perez: 'Um... right, we have a room here for you. But that one's taken. I'm gonna have to put you in the boiler room for your stay here.' 'You can't be serious...' The End says, shaking his head and slapping his forehead with his left palm. Perez calls over another bellboy and tosses some keys to him. Rosie Perez: 'Hey, you, take Mr. The End here to the boiler room.' The End turns 180 and sees that Robert Downey, Jr. is to escort him to his room. 'WHAT THE HELL KIND OF HOTEL DID PCW SEND ME TO?' The End exclaims at the top of his lungs. The camera fades out. There is a time compression of half an hour before the camera fades in once more. The End is sitting on the floor of the dark, hot boiler room, sitting in a meditative position, trying to take his mind off of what has just happened. A little voice inside The Ends head speaks to him. Voice: Well, since this is a PURE roleplay, might you want to address your opponents, Ebola III and Billy Larkin? The End answers the voice verbally. Who? *Time Compression: 21 hours* In the morning, having escaped the hellhole that was the Marriott that The End had the unfortunately displeasure of visiting for 5 hours, The End has had to take quite a few plane trips to get to where he needs to go (obviously Japan hes got a PURE match coming up). Each trip had something weird happen. First, there was the plane out of New Orleans. {New Orleans  Southwest Airlines Flight 117) The End is waiting inside of a 747 that has been waiting on the runway for approximately 74 minutes. Everyone is VERY upset about this. The End, sitting in the first class section with four other people (a middle-aged Caucasian businessman wearing a black two-piece suit, Jay-Z in a Lakers jersey and black baggy denim pants, Bill Clinton (wearing a blue Hawaiian shirt and white Bermuda shorts with yellow sandals), and Josh Hartnett (who still hasnt returned his outfit from the battle scenes in Pearl Harbor). Clinton and Jay-Z are playing strip poker with the flight attendants and winning. So far, theyve gotten their shirts off, and are working to remove the undergarments. The businessman is clutching his briefcase, curled up in a ball on his seat while rocking back and forth and repeating the words, THE WALLS ARE CLOSING IN I CANNOT BREATHE ANYMORE THE MEN THEY COME FOR ME THOUGH I HAVE WRONGED THEM NOT. As for Hartnett, hes too busy studying the script for his next movie. Its about a movie star whos still young and attractive to women everywhere, yet somehow his fifteen minutes of fame are over before hes ready for that to happen. The coach section is much more uncomfortable. One hundred fat, smelly people and their annoying children jammed together in a space that doesnt allow for much movement anyway. The only thing bad about this trip is the man that kept bothering The End A man in white, a cook, approaches The End with a steaming pot. The End is trying to just get a quiet moment, but the mans talking and the stench of the pot force him to get loud. Get away from me! The End shouts at Emeril. I f*cking hate crawdads and you suck as a cook! And then there was the stop over in Texas (Texas  Southwest Airlines Flight 398) The End is at the airport in a short first-class line behind Rudolph Giuliani. Giuliani steps up to the counter. An Asian-American woman greets him with a friendly voice. Attendant: Where are you flying today? Giuliani leans over onto the counter and winks at the woman. Giuliani: New York City. At the mention of NYC, the local Texans that make up the vast majority of the coach-class line turn to Giuliani and all call out in unison these three words. Texans in the coach line: NEW YORK CITY?!?! I thought for sure that they stopped airing those commercials The End says in a low mumble. Attendant: Okay, just let me see your ticket, or you may buy one here for $1,045, please. Giuliani pulls out his wallet from his oversized white fur coat, his pimp cane held under his right arm. Giuliani gives the money to the attendant in wads of hundreds, handing her about $3,000. Giuliani: Keep the rest for yourself, sweetcakes. The attendant looks confusedly at the former New York City mayor, but pockets the extra money nonetheless. Attendant: Thank you, sir. Heres your ticket, have a pleasant flight. Giuliani walks off, with Stayin Alive by the Bee-Gees playing from some unknown source. Everyone can hear it, but no one knows where it is coming from. The End then steps up to the counter. Attendant: And where shall you be flying today? The End half-pays attention and gives an answer thats so casual as to be indifference to everything around him. The same as him, The End says, pointing at the departing Giuliani, who is disappearing down the extendable corridor that leads to the planes entrance. New York City. The Texans in the coach line cue up again at the mention of NYC. Texans in the coach line: NEW YORK CITY?!?! The End turns to the Texans and yells back at them. Shut the f*ck up you slack-jawed yokels! The End returns his attention to the attendant at the counter. Anyway, he says, Im on the flight to NYC. The End gives the attendant his ticket, and she allows him to go onto the plane. (Los Angeles  Southwest Airlines Flight 836) The End is stuck inside of the security room. Hes been checked for suspicious behavior, and brought into this dark room with just a table while his identity is verified. Another man is there with him. It turns out to be Lexicon of the Misfits. You remember Lexicon (James Brolin with a brown leather jacket, dusty jeans, and cowboy boots), right? Good, because I dont feel like explaining how much he has a mastery of the English language, or how blunt he can be at times. Hehe. The End is pacing back and forth while Lexicon is sitting at the white metal table, his hands folded on the surface and his eyes following The Ends every move. Why the hell am I being held here? Im going to be late for my first PURE show because they think I wore an I Love Osama shirt or something. What, do they think the licorice I was eating was plastique? Paranoid bastards. Lexicon responds to The End calmly from his seat. They wont hold you long. Youll be out in no time, and then you can deal with Ebola III and Billy Larkin. Suddenly, the security chief walks in. Hes big and burly and muscular, but The End stands 9 inches taller. The Chiefs body language and tone command respect. Too bad hes not going to get it. Chief: So, whats your angle? The End scratches his chin and thinks about it. Actually, The End says, despite the fact that Im working in an angle-fed being PURE, I dont really have a definite angle to go on at the moment. Thats okay, though, because for this first match with Ebola III and Billy Larkin, only one of them Ive actually heard of, its all about who can RP the best. Or at least I hope so. The security chief is thoroughly confused by The Ends dialogue. Chief: I am thoroughly confused by your dialogue. Well, The End says, poking the Chief in the head when his pacing brings him close enough, I think weve discovered the weakest part of your anatomy, save for the groin. The insulted Chief gets upset at The End. Chief: Thats enough. Very much enough, snaps The End. Chief: Go ahead, press your luck Oh, well thank you. Hey, is Alexander Isawa Miyagawa Bob Barker going to get here soon? Ive got to fly to Japan for a wrestling appearance with his company, and Im guessing he'll be calling on me to come on down since I appear to be stuck in a room at the moment, preventing me from going on my own. The Chief holds his hand out at The End in a simmer down motion. Chief: Hold on. You dont seem to be Muslim or from the Middle East, so if you just watch your mouth you might get out of this with no problems. The End turns his back to the Chief and continues on his chosen path for the day. So Im guessing you had to kill Andrew Dice Clay? The End asks in a very sarcastic voice. Chief: Keep it up. Very well. A white guy, a black guy, and a Spanish guy are all walking on the beach Chief: I saw Boondock Saints. Okay then. The End actually shuts his mouth for the moment. The silence doesnt last long, but it is Lexicon that breaks it. Lexicon: So when and/or how are we getting out of here? Chief: We have to check your ID. If everythings fine, you go free. If theres something wrong, you go to a prison that makes Oz seem like a Girl Scout slumber party. The End, who by this point just doesnt care what happens no matter what he says, since he feels that hell already be late for his first PURE show, mocks the Chief again. They have digital cable? The Chief plays along this one time, but still in a derisive voice. Chief: If you consider the hallucinations youll be having from years of solitary confinement as digital cable, then yes. Man, this keeps sounding better and better. Id never have to deal with any of you *ssholes again! Lexicon breaks in, reminding The End about his wrestling obligations and ambitions. Lexicon: You'd also never get to wrestle in PCW or PURE, and never get to a title belt! The End takes back his last statement and leans back to sit on the table as his pacing brings him near it. D*MMIT! Such a tough decision. A subordinate security personnel comes in and whispers something to the Chief. He nods his understanding and the subordinate walks out. Chief: Ah, well your ID went through, so you can go now. Your flights not left yet, so just get on and go. Finally, The End says, getting off the table and walking out to get to his plane. Lexicon calmly stands up from the table and goes to leave. The Chief stops him. Lexicon: *confused* Whats the problem? Chief: Your ID didnt go through. So our best guess is that you are a terrorist. Lexicon backs away from the Chief and takes his seat again at the table. He is in disbelief. Lexicon: That cant be! Im James Brolin I mean, Lexicon! The Chief reaches for his gun as he approaches Lexicon. Chief: Its okay, well let you go. I just have to put this special tracking device in your head. Lexicon stands from his chair, knocking it over. Lexicon: What are you doing? Get away from me! HELP! A single bullet is fired from the gun. A loud, sharp BANG! And then all is silent. Barbarians, The End mutters to himself as he exits the security complex, having heard everything that happened in the room.
