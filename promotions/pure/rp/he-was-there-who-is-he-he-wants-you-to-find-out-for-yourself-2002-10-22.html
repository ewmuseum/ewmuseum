He was there... Who is he? He wants you to find out for yourself... Written: 10/22/02 Introduction The sounds…The music…The people… A small club in Tokyo, Japan… Hundreds of hearts pounding…Soon to be subtracted by one… He was there… Who is he? He wants you to find out for yourself… She was there too… Who was she? Her name was Melissa Kimono…and she was drunk… She did what she could to dance. She didn't care any more; she danced with who she could. She could only make out the blur of the lights flashing and the music ringing through her ears. She wanted fun.
Little did she know, evil eyes had been watching her… He stood there, right on the balcony in the club. He watched below as he snuck around through the shadows. No one had noticed him.
She glanced over at the balcony for a second, she saw him moving in the shadows. She was able to look and see his hatefully, possessed eyes. Once she noticed it, she glanced at the balcony again. She saw nothing. The being lurking through the shadows was gone.
Melissa stopped dancing. She walked around, head spinning. She walked with her legs wobbling, and not knowing where she was going.
Suddenly, a big hand grabbed hold of her. She was out of sight… Chase Herrera Well,? What can I say? I love Tokyo. Why do I love Tokyo? Girls. Food. Oh, and most of all, these people were freaking crazy! Their martial arts were just incredible! Their wrestling is just nuts!
I loved it here. I was lucky enough to get the chance to stay in such a great place. Oh yeah, the clubs they had here were just great. That's why I was at one. I loved to dance. It was a part of my Spanish heritage. I had to dance; I came from a dancing type of people. And dancing was exciting.
Then I saw her Spanish just like me. She was wandered through the crowd of people. I walked over to her, and gently took her hand. Out of all the Japanese women I had seen here, she was the first Hispanic I had seen so far.
'Hola!' I basically yelled through all the noise. 'Hola!' she greeted back. 'Usted tiene gusto de bailar?' I said, asking her to dance. 'Si,' she answered. We danced. She was beautiful, and she could dance so well. Right...left...right...left... The old days of learning to dance came back to me. The steps echoed in my mind. I swung her around... Then I saw it. I stopped dancing. She stood there and looked at me. 'What is it?' she asked. I saw a shadow. The darkness of it gleamed on the wall. The shadow was of two figures, a man and a woman, a knife being slashed about.
It's him, I thought. I ran through the crowd of people toward the shadow...closer to the screams of a dying woman.
Would I get there in time? The shadow... Where was it coming from? Follow the screams, I thought. I ran over to this corner, into the shadows the screaming grew louder. Slashing through skin was now able to be heard.
He was now clear in my sight... I ran... I jumped... I jumped right on his back, hoping to bring him down. But he was too big... too strong. I had then noticed the woman's screaming had ceased. I supposed she was now dead and I was too late.
He swung me around, struggling to get me off of his back. WHAM! He slammed me into the wall, but I still held on. SLASH! He slashed my with his knife on my arm. No big deal to me. I wasn't scared of a cut on the arm, but there was no way I would take him down. Sooner or later he would end my life instead of giving me a cut on the arm.
He flipped me over his back. He looked at me straight in the eye. I could see him clearly now. He wore a mask. A white mask, which represented a sad face. The facial expression on the mask showed it. The mouth hung low along with a black tear that could be clearly seen below the eye. He looked at me with the eyes that have haunted me for so long. It had come down to this.
I was about to die. Everything, my life, all coming to an end. He raised his knife... It was about to be over... WHAM! A person, someone familiar, hammered the evil being with a spear into the wall. A man that had saved my life for the second time. Brian Gaines. As Brian had laid the evil being out, he pulled me back to my feet. We ran through the hundreds of people dancing. We went down through this dark corridor, and ran out through a door.
It must have been the back way of the club. I sat down in the dark murky alley. Brian sat down, also.
'So this is Chase Herrera?' a man said appearing out of the darkness. 'What's going on?' I asked, a bit shaky. 'Well, it's actually a long story, come with us,' Brian finally spoke. Where was I going? What was next? I guess there are too many if's in life. I was soon to find out some of them...
