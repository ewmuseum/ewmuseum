The Blame Game

30 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Blame Game It was ruined.. Everything. What happened on Ikusa III.. was horrendous.. it was, horrid. Flashing images in black and white, of his ruined dreams. Everything shattering.. it wasn't how he wanted it to be. It just wasn't right. 'IT WASN'T WHAT I WANTED!!' The images flashed.. Kensuke crashing into the mat, after a snap suplex... 'It wasn't right!!' A black and white, slow motion of Cianan lifting Kensuke up, before twisting, and driving his elbow into Kensuke's throat, in a lifted final cut, known as the Enlightenment. 'He ruined it! It wasn't supposed to be that easy..' The Fall from Grace is hit on the battered Kensuke.. 'Miyagawa ruined everything for me!' Kensuke.. is hurled over the ropes.. and downwards.. what seems like for ages.. down.. down.. and finally he hits. Landing on the steel steps, and bouncing off, with a sickening *clank.* He rolls off to the side, and screams out in pain. What the camera's on Ikusa didn't see, was the slight showing of fear on Cianan's face. He had hated Kensuke.. wanted Kensuke dead. But, he didn't want it here ... he didn't want it like that. Cianan watched Kensuke crash into the ring steps, his eyes widen at the sound. 'No one there knew the history.. Miyagawa ruined it. Alexander Miyagawa..' -The battles I fought with Kensuke.. might have been over there. With nothing more to follow up. All the worse, I feel dirty, having taken money for that.. something I've never done before.- -Dirty.. dirty.. must get clean.. The kid was right..- -Must redeem myself.. - -I am not someone's servant. - 'Feared.. respected.. revered.. I sunk so low just to get my hands on Suzahara. I should have waited.. He would have returned to CCW, I could have gotten him there. No, I was impatient.. ' 'Miyagawa.. He tries to break his deals.' 'I signed up with him.. He tries to hold me back..' 'I won't be held back by him...' 'I need to get out of this fuckin' room. ' Cianan stands, running his fingers through his hair, only to let it fall back into his face. A he inhales slowly, filling his lungs with cleansing air. The used air slid from his nose, and he sighed. Trying to control his anger, trying to keep himself calm. -Calm..- -Cool..- -Collected..- 'I need to go for a walk...' With an ache in his muscles, for an unknown purpose, he stood. Wrestlers always have unknown aches and pains, he thought nothing of it anymore. He felt the muscles in his arms twitch, happy to be in motion. He slowly picked up a pair of jeans, slowly sliding them on leg by leg, feeling a normal stiffness. He grabbed a PURE, shirt.. Hell, he had a million CCW ones, why not go for something different? Slipping on an old pair of tennis shoes, he stretched out once more, not caring about primping up. These were people he didn't know or care about, why should he care if he smelled good to them. He slowly opened the sliding plastic door, to the excruciatingly small room, and had to duck through it. He stepped into the equally small hallway, and grumbled.. Cianan wasn't overly sized, but still, he was bigger then the normal Asian man, which made it difficult to maneuver around, in the steadily decreasing hotel room, and building sizes. Cianan maneuvered, and dodged through the filled hallways, moving from one side to another, avoiding touching the other hotel occupants. Finally making it to the elevator, he pressed the button. He rolled his shoulders, cracking his back, and let out a light sight. -This will be good for me.. I need to relax..- The doors slid open, and he quickly moved out of the way, as several families attempted to cram into the open elevator at the same time. -heh.. Sardines..- Cianan shoved his hands into his pockets, and walked down the streets, the same streets he walked down, during his 'Trash' fed days, when he was 18. 6 years ago, and everything seems to only have gotten smaller. -6 years ago.. Ebola III or.. Kevin.. would actually be around the same age I was, when I came to Japan. The most painful experience of my life. Being part 'Gaijin,' I was treated worse then the 'Gaijin.' They were a lot less open back then.. and things were a lot more harsh.- He walked in front of an elderly man, causing the old man to stumble a bit, raising his hand, and yelling. But, it all fell upon deaf ears.. Well, except for one word, one word that Cianan hated. He knew what he was, but having it constantly thrown back in his face, really, well, pissed him off. '................gaijin!..............' Cianan heard.. That word.. and slowly turned. -Calm, must remain calm..- -Clear, and focused..- The old man didn't stop there, he kept yelling, now apparently having a problem with the way Cianan dressed and looked. -I won't snap..- -I am in control..- The old man continued to yell, but no one else, but Cianan paid attention. It wasn't their business, so they just kept to themselves. Cianan, just smiled, and tried to rectify the situation, having already gotten tired of the yelling. 'Gomen Na...' *SMACK* Cianan's eyes went wide.. anger burst through his heads. His temples started to pound, and his face grew red. A snarl emerged from the depths of his stomach. -Fuck control..- Cianan lashed out, grabbing the old man, and shoving him into a wall, lifting him off of his feet. 'Never.. Ever touch me, you old bastard..' The old man struggled, trying to make the steel grip release, but Cianan had none of it. He pulled the man back, and thrust him back into the wall. 'Stop it.. Miyagawa. I'm not done talking to you.' -Miyagawa?- The old man looked confused, but finally got the message, and shut up. Cianan's eyes narrowed. 'I give my blood, and my sweat to this country. I work for you.. I entertain YOU. You pay to see me bleed. You owe me some respect. Do you understand me? DO YOU HEAR ME MIYAGAWA?!' This time, there was no after thought.. The old man looked like Miyagawa, in no way at all. But, to Cianan's eyes.. He was there. Alexander Miyagawa, in the flesh. He was the one being held up against the wall. 'You think you're better then me, just because of where you came from? You come from big bad.. PCW, and I come from little CCW. You've never seen me before.. You don't know me.. AND, YOU THINK YOU'RE BETTER THEN ME!' Cianan's breath started picking up, and his fingers dug into the mans shoulders, he winced in pain, but showed no fear. Though, his bodily functions betrayed him. Cianan just pulled 'Miyagawa' forward, and glared into his eyes. 'I should respect you because of what you are.. Because of who you are, and where you're from? Fuck that, Al. I respect who I want.. and it certainly won't be on a little bugger, who backs out of deals. Who ruins what I've worked hard to do.. YOU RUINED MY MATCH WITH KENSUKE!! YOU DID!!' Cianan, slammed the man back into the wall again, still no one coming to his aid, they were all good little automatons. He pressed his forearm against the old man's throat, pinning him to the wall, as he pulled his fist back. Tears now formed in the man's eyes, the nonsensical screaming, the threatening of violence, the humiliation he just suffered, it was not just too much, his honor had been stripped from him, by a lowly 'gaijin.' 'You ruined my history.. Miyagawa.. and I will ruin you.' He thrust his fist forward ... but, missed the man's head, and driving it into the cement wall behind him. The man's eyes widened, the tears now visible. and another deification. Cianan leaned in close, a cruel smile on his lips, as he whispered into 'Miyagawa's' ear. 'This is going to be fun.. be afraid..' Cianan stepped back, letting the man fall on his ass. He tried to regain his dignity, but it was far gone. And, being one to kick someone while their down, Cianan looks over his shoulder, a smile still on his face. 'How does it feel to be the lower class, Miyagawa?' [fin] ((OOC Note: the Miyagawa in my RP, isn't the real Miyagawa, just In case some of you missed that.. It's just a little delusion from Cianan's mind. Just covering my ass, so I don't get in trouble!))
