Three Men On A Mission!!

11 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Three Men On A Mission!! Written: 01/11/03 So there I was, walking around downtown Tokyo, Japan, just about like ever other five foot nothing dark skinned person in the whole country. How were the three of us supposed to find Yoshikiro in a place where everyone looks the fucking same? Well, one thing we knew for sure... We had no clue where to begin looking.
Frank stood on my left shoulder, pacing back and fourth trying to think of something, while on my right shoulder Bob laid down, sleeping, and being a worthless waste of space. It wasn't quite night yet, but the sun was beginning to set, and once darkness fell, we would have absolutely nothing to work with. I had given up all hope at this point, and was wanting to head back to the hotel as soon as possible. Frank, still pacing quickly stopped me from turning around. 'Why don't we wait till dark, and then go to all the bars we can in one night, searching for him?' He said with an almost enthusiastic tone into my left ear.
Lee: 'That might actually work. Why didn't I think of it before?' I couldn't even answer my own question. But what Frank had just thought up was brilliant, and would eventually lead us to the horny little bastard we were looking for. I mean, he said he'd return eventually, but when he didn't show up for Acxess, I just had to begin my search. The damn Jap was back home, and probably knew every place where he could hide and not have us find him. But why?
Lee: 'God damn it Bob, quit being so lazy, and wake your ass up! We've got a job to do here!'
The tired and not yet alert good two shoes looked at me with one of those dumbfounded looks that just makes me want to shove him into a trash can. But I wasn't going to be that mean to him, yet. I needed to keep my mind on where the hell to find Yoshi at though.
Lee: 'So we've got a half crazy Japanese back at home. He's been in the United States hanging with me for so long he's more than likely picked up a few bad habits. I'm guessing we'll find him with a few hand prints on his face. A possible black eye is he pisses too many chicks off, and if worst comes to worst, we'll find him knocked out in some gutter from some chick's husband. I'd say we have four hours to find him and take him back to safety before he gets himself hurt.'
I looked at both Frank and Bob with my plan for a four hour rescue so to speak, and Frank agreed, while Bob had other things to say. 'If you let him be, and let the good guys deal with it, he will become pure again, and free of the sin that fills his soul.' I had almost had it with Bob's preaching. But Frank has too much fun torturing the little bastard for me to just flick him off my shoulder into a trash can.
Lee: 'You idiot, would you just mind keeping that mini chapel shut for once, I don't need to hear a chapter form the bible every five minutes. I think I'll take my chances at finding him myself anyway. Give me something to do over here with all these Puro bastards!'
Just up ahead was another vendor selling Got Puro shirts at a small little booth he had set up. The sun was setting, and the lights of the large city were beginning to illuminate the streets of the town where the light had failed to visit now. I scanned the are for the closest trash can, hoping to find a nice metal one this time, so I could show this Puro supporter what I thought about that loud of shit!
Shirt Seller: 'Hey you, big guy, would you like a Got Puro shirt? I'm sure you are one of those big wrestling fans, and PURE wrestling is finally back in town!'
Did I mention he was an annoying Japanese-American, English speaking and all. I figured I wouldn't have to put up with him, but no, he has to go off and speak my language so I get to talk to him before dumping him into the trash can.
Lee: 'If you watched as much as you spoke, you'd know who you were talking to. And the three of use don't appreciate what you are trying to do. So either shove that shirt up your own ass, or I'll do it myself.'
Shirt Seller: 'Whoa man, I see why you are so hostile, but have no fear, I have a double extra large right here.'
As you can see, the guy wasn't getting the hint. So I smiled sarcastically, grabbed the shirt out of his hand with some force, and wrapped it around his head. I then lifted the scrawny thing up, carried him a few yards to the nearest trash can, and dumped him in ass first, leaving his legs and arms sticking out, enabling him to move very good. This time, however, I decided to add to the fun, and I tipped the can over, and rolled it into the middle of the street.
Lee: 'I'd suggest shoving it up your own ass next time you Puro bitch!' I brushed myself off, looked at the disgusted Bob, and the overly excited Frank, and I took off down the street as the natural sunlit streets changed to the artificially lit streets of Tokyo. I had a long night ahead of me, and hopefully we could find Yoshikiro before someone else did!
The scene fades to black.
