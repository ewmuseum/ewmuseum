Scream Out Loud

10 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Scream Out Loud **OOC NOTE: Hi. Welcome to Kensuke. Music video RP, so the lyrics (download the song, please) are indicated with italics, the inner monologue with double-colons, narration with brackets. I'm sure you'll pick up on it just fine. Oh, and Ill credit DarkStar with some help on this, even though he doesnt know it. :p** **Theme Music: 'The Choke' by Saves The Day** ---------------------------------------- [Welcome to Seattle, Washingtons skyline. Not the one you see on postcardsthe Space Needle watches like a malevolent god, over a brick and concrete wasteland. Its raining] ::Its raining:: Don't leave yet [The sky? Gunmetal gray and off-white. The moon is down. The light is fading.] ::on your parade.:: It's still early and I haven't even said a word [A loft, on top of a bagel shop downtown. The front is worn away by time, and tides of wind and raing.] And I'm hoping ::Seattle seems to match my mood. Gotta seem a little more upbeat. Dont want to be mentioned in the same breath as that whiny c*cksucker Blitz.:: [The For Sale/Rent sign in the window is snatched away by a black hand.] ::Time to start over again:: That I might upset you [As suddenly as the hand enters, its gone.] ::Im really not in the mood to be f*cked with. Being suspended without pay from CCW does that to you.:: By saying what I want to [The camera pans away, and we see an alleyway. Dark, and messy. Alleys always are, it seems.] ::There are people on the streets.:: Cause it's not like you don't know I've fallen for you [A young, pretty girl walks into the alley, and turns around, pert mouth contorting into a frown. Shes obviously lost.] ::There are people in this world who deservemore:: But it's in my head and that's where you can't see it [Out of the shadows, a badly dressed man slinks carefully up behind her.] :::Like this man. He inherited the wealth of parentsbut they abandoned him after he dropped out of college. It wasnt for him.:: And I thought that maybe if I had to [She turns to face a large, gleaming knifeblade.] ::He deserves more from life. A pretty wife. Money. The simple pleasures.:: Bite the tips of my fingers [The man licks his lips, and sneers at the woman. Shed scream, but her mouth is quickly covered with an oily, grimy hand.] ::He deserves themso is he wrong to take them?:: I could stumble over words [He throat swells with a scream of agony, as he drags her to the ground.] ::Go ahead, take them.:: And tell you just how far before I hit the ground [Two things hit the grounda wallet, and a pair of lacy white panties.] ::You deserve them. Youre one of my peoplethe disenfranchised.:: And I'm the type to think of all the wrong things to say [Awful noises come from the alley. Horrible noises no person should either hear, nor emit.] ::Take what you deserve.:: I will shut myself up [A rivulet of blood runs from the alleyway. The man, pulling his pants up, bloody knife between his teeth, lifts the wallet and runs off.] ::I used to save pretty girls like that. But you know what?:: And I'll never come out [The scene comes up on the rooftop of the loft from before, where a door sits ajar.] ::F*ck pretty girls. What have they ever done for me?:: Close all my doors [The door slams shut, and we see the form of a man dressed in black pants, and a black leather jacket, from behind. He stands on the edge of the roof, toes leaning carefully off the side.] ::I hate Alexander Miyagawa. More than anyone else whos ever lived. Hes tortured me physically. Hes tortured me mentally. He embarrassed me at a PCW Pay-Per-View.:: Only show you the black spots [The camera zooms around, and we see the lithe, muscular form of our protagonistthe villain of the great PURE opera. His head hangs low, his hair unkempt dangling from his crown.] ::He has everything Ive ever deserved.:: Where my eyes once were [His head raises, and almond eyes flash with an intangible red light.] ::I want to torture him. I want him to scream in emotional anguish. I want payback. Its my right:: I can say this... [His face turned towards the sky, the clouds open up in slow motion, and drop their healing water upon the tortured soul of this man.] ::He has the respect I deserve. The PCW Heavyweight Title reigns I was deserving of. The adulations of thousands of fans more than I have. He has his own company. A slut of a wife. I want them. I want them all.:: I can say this... [Kensuke opens his mouth, and screams into the open sky.] ::MIYAGAWA!!:: I can say this... [The water from above drips down his face, into his mouth, down his hair. A baptism from abovecalled upon by perhaps forces from below] ::I gave up EVERYTHING to be the best in CCW. Do I have respect? Love? Am I the first or only of ANYTHING?:: I can collect myself deep down [More water. This time, from the corners of his eyes. Specks of light emerge in the breaks in the clouds. The sky is going black in Washington.] ::No, Im not. I know what I have to do.:: And then come out punching [A crescent moon hangs like an ominous omen. An axeblade that waits for the fourteenth to fall.] ::When Im through with you, MiyagawaIll have your everything. Im the Messiah For The Disenfranchised. Ill take whats mine:: I can collect myself deep down [As the sky slowly changes, Kensukes form cuts a sharp outline. Its a black and white world sometimes, and Kensuke sure looks dark, even against a sky thats slowly going black.] ::and youll either like it:: And then come out punching [Some things are just bad.] [The view recedes into the sky, but Kensuke, no matter how small he starts to becomeis still visible. His dark shape and form stand out against any background.] ::or Ill destroy you.:: I'll scream out loud [The world falls away from us as we ascend to the heavens with Kensukes scream of rage.] ::I want you at my feet, or dead, Miyagawa. There is no other way.:: Scream out loud ::Scream out loud, Alexander.::
