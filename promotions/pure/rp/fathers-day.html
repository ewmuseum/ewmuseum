Father's Day

15 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Father's Day Another Father's Day is coming up. On this one, I shall be wrestling in Japan. My mother was Japanese, but my father was from Virginia. It's a return to my heritage, but not my father's. Then again, I'm the last surviving member on my father's side of the family anyway, so it's not like I'd have anything to go back to in Virginia. But my father met my mother stationed in Japan, so I suppose this is close enough to a homecoming. I sometimes wonder what my father would think about what I do for a living. From the few journal entries I found by him (before I lost my sight), my father didn't like fighting even though it was his duty. I don't know if that meant in every way, or just in war. I mean, I often engage in battle, but more a battle of wills within physical competition. However, fighting is fighting, and were he alive, I'm not sure that my father would approve of what I do for a living. Of course, I'm 25, so his approval on my choice of occupation really wouldn't be necessary. But to wonder... I never grew up with a father, save for the first four years of my life which are extremely blurry at this point. Who knows how close we would have been? But, that's just my human father. I'm not actually fully human, something I wish not to go into much detail at this moment. But long story short, my biological father is not of this world, as Akira Akiyama's fellow celestial Fate informed me. So then, I wonder on this Father's Day, who was my biological father? What was he like? Why would he come to impregnate a mortal woman? Why my mother? And why would he leave her to raise on her own a child that by the very nature of its conception could have been a terrible man-eating monster? My biological father gave me my physical gifts. My unnatural strength for my low weight, my height, inhuman agility... most likely all of this came right from my biological father. So, my biological father, or the only father I ever knew. Which one to celebrate for Father's Day? I have no connection to either of them, so I'm really considering just screwing the holiday. But if I don't... maybe this will be a celebration through my match. My match on Father's Day will be dedicated to my father. All of them. Every male I've known growing up has been a father figure to me, whether the lessons they taught me in life were good or bad. So when I step into the ring on Ikusa, I shall represent every man who may have acted as my father in one way or another. That's perhaps a big responsibility, but some guy named Dwayne Johnson claims to represent all the people of the world, even those who don't even know about him and even those who don't like him. Now that's a big responsibility. Compared to that, my match will put less pressure on me in that respect. There's still the pressure that will be on me from the nature of the match. So, what will I do when I walk into Ikusa? Simple. I will fight to win.
