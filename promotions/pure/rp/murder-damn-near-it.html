Murder? Damn Near It...

21 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Murder? Damn Near It... Japan... BAH! Jordan wrestled his first match in Japan, it wasn't the best of matches, but it was quite the match. Jordan stepped into the ring against a whole bunch of other wrestlers... In the end, Jordan was upset, and defeated, by his roommate, Yuka Hamada. Even though he lost, Jordan was enjoying himself, everything was going good for him, he'd had his match, less than a masterpiece, but he was satisfied that the fans were satisfied. As he watched the matches on the monitor, he saw Ali Khadafi in the ring, brutalizing his friend, The Reverend, Jesse Kintei. Jordan ran as fast as he could down the ramp, while the crowd was cheering him on. The fact that the fans were cheering him on didn't do much; he just wanted to save his friend. He made it there, before it was too late. He nailed Ali Khadafi with BY ANY MEANS NECESSARY, but it was too little, too late. The damage had been done. Now, as Jordan rides on his plane, back to Dallas, where he was supposed to be given a top-secret mission by his upper heads, but that wasn't on his mind. Instead, he was thinking of his friend, lying in a hospital bed with numerous injuries. A mild concussion, partially broken nose, several stitches in the forehead and face area. Blurred vision... None of this was right... He hadn't done a damned thing... but that sick freak, that perverted bastard... That... That... Fuck that. Ali Khadafi's going to hell... Jordan's thoughts are interrupted as he is offered a drink from a tray. Jordan pours himself hard bourbon with Orange juice. He takes a sip and sets it back down. Damn ungrateful ass nigga. I'm risking my life everyday, by being a public figure, and fighting for OUR rights. The very rights that the fought the damned Civil War over, the very rights that prevent redneck groups like the Ku Klux Klan from dominating America.... But Ali Khadafi doesn't care.... He's staying in Japan... Waiting for Ikusa 4... In which I'll team with my Japanese, male roommates... To face him... and some other guys... I don't even remember.... I hardly care.... I can't stop thinking about what happened to Jesse.... Fuck this. Jordan takes a gulp of his Bourbon and his orange juice. He goes into the pocket of his coat, and he pulls out a green Nokia cell phone. He dials a number quickly. RING! RING! 'Hello?' A voice answers with a heavy Japanese accent. 'Yes, I'm Jordan Copeland, I would like my call to be forwarded to a certain room.' 'Sure, what room?' Jordan reaches into his pocket and he pulls out a small piece of paper with a number that had been obviously jotted down quickly, on it. '666.' Ironic isn't it? Because Ali Khadafi was a sick freak, who probably believed in the man that lay below... 'One moment please.' The call was forwarded through a doctor answered the phone. 'Hello?' The doctor said through the phone to Jordan X Shabazz in perfect English. 'Hello, sir. I'm Jordan Copeland, I would like to speak with Jesse.' Jordan replied, firm, large, and in charge. 'Yes, one moment. Be brief please, he needs his rest, right now we are recommending he sleeps at least twenty hours a day, it may seem like a....' 'Listen, sir, I don't want to hear all that, just let me talk to him.' Jordan answered angrily. The doctor gives the phone to Jesse. Who's lying on his back, facing a small television that is high above him on a shelf? 'Hel... Hello?' Jesse says weakly. 'Hey dog, how you doin', foo'?' Jordan says. 'He... Hey, I'm... I'm... doing okay,' comes the reply, weakly again. Oh shit does he have a stutter? 'Okay, good. Just callin' to check up on you, Khadafi got you pretty damn good.' 'Yeah... But you'll... you'll get hi-...hi-...him at Ikusa IV.' Before Jordan could say another word; the doctor takes the phone out of the hands of Jesse Kintei. 'He has to get his rest now, thank you for calling. Good night.' CLICK! Oh no the fuck he didn't! He hung up on me? DAMN! Oh hell... Khadafi fucks with the NAACP, he fucks with me... Payback's a bitch... At least that's what those white people say...
