Turning American...finally...

5 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Turning American...finally... Written: 05.10.02 [Amber Token and Marcello are both sat together in the classroom, muttering to each other...like they've been for the past week.]
Marcello Dias: Have you any idea who's responsible for putting us through Junior High around here?
Amber Token: No. Yet, it does show something. Marcello Dias: What? Amber Token: Why so many promos are indistinto. It is as if there is a rule saying that you must use certain phrases in order to get attention.
Marcello Dias: What, like it says that if someone has a female friend, you should call them a slut? No, that's just too basic for comprehension.
Amber Token: There is more. Listen to Ashley Maximoff, the human Xerox... Joseph Farrell: Amber, are you ready for your second tutorial into being more American, so that you'll appeal to the masses with a persona they can easily identify with, since it worked for somebody before?
Amber Token: Gostar em projétil em cabeça meu... Joseph Farrell: Now, what did I tell you before? Don't overdo the Portuguese, as you will isolate potential fans and, more importantly, advertisers.
Amber Token: Are you not the one who gave Ashley Maximoff her ocupação, Senhor Farrell? Joseph Farrell: No, that was the Contract Department. I work in Commercial Affairs which, as we all know, is where any wrestling federation gains it's image.
Amber Token: Vocês sinuso... Joseph Farrell: Don't make me tell you again... [Amber folds her hands under her chin, and puts on puppy dog eyes.] Amber Token: I'm so, so, sorry. Joseph Farrell: You see, a little humility is all it takes to get ahead. Marcello Dias: Cool, let me try! Joseph Farrell: If you must, go ahead. Marcello Dias: I'm so, so, the next championship slut. And to think, I actually did a better job convincing people than when Ashley 'Copy And Past Promos' Maximoff does it.
Joseph Farrell: Okay, enough with being a smart alec. It isn't like this scene is being filmed, so you can play up to the camera.
Now, I realise I was a little harsh before, dropping you in the deep end with the whole 'acting American' idea, which is fine. Obviously you're more comfortable speaking the way you do, and if you slip in the occasional Portu-slip, that can be negotiated for now. After all, thinking too hard is never going to make a decent promo...
Marcello Dias: So, that's how Rayda got a job in this industry? Joseph Farrell: Knock it off! As I said, speech is one thing, since you haven't spent a year in this country, and most of the time the dialects can throw you, be it Futuristic, Texan, Australian, Wannabe Dre or Welsh, so we can let that one come along at it's own pace. However, your image needs tweaking.
Amber Token: 'Tweaking'? Ir transar sozinho! I do not need to change, for that is not me, that is Joseph 'tolo estuprador' Farrell's vision of me.
Joseph Farrell: 'Tolo Estruprador'? What a good idea for a pseudonym! Amber Token: Then may I suggest you bestow it on Ashley? Joseph Farrell: Why Ashley Maximoff? Amber Token: She is supposed to be futuristic. It will fit her like a glove. That is how I understand their leasure, anyway.
Joseph Farrell: I don't understand... Marcello Dias: I get the feeling that was the point. After all, you shouldn't be led on like...a sheep because you might get, well, a little bit...stuck.
[Farrell looks very confused indeed, again.] Joseph Farrell: But anyway, about your image. First of all, since you're a foreigner, how about you play up your nationality? You know, come to the ring to the Brazilian national anthem, waving the flag, bragging about...what are Brazil good at?
Amber Token: Futebol, café, música, cultura... Joseph Farrell: Yeah right. I doubt a Brazilian team could beat the 49ers. [Amber looks nonplussed.] Joseph Farrell: But then there's your image. I mean, why wear those baggy shorts all the time, what are you trying to be, Fred Durst?
Amber Token: Are you familiar with Muay Thai? Joseph Farrell: Is that the coffee? Amber Token: It is the most brutal form of kickboxing. Marcello, eu poder pontap dele jumento?
Marcello Dias: Not yet, but keep it in mind. Joseph Farrell: Wait a minute, I got a marvellous idea. How about, you can be...A BRAZILIAN RAPPER! It'll be great, you can come down to the ring to sometyhing like, ummm, I don't know...'Straight Outta Belo Horizonte'!
Amber Token: Eu sentir doente... Joseph Farrell: Of course, you'll have to change the names to your moveset. Instead of 'Lethal Seduction', how about 'Kiss of Death'? 'Bala de Canhão' can be 'Cannon Ball'. 'Furacão'? 'Cyclone'!
Amber Token: You have researched poorly, I see. Two of them are the same. Guess which. Joseph Farrell: Oh forget about that, I've gotta go see Wilson about this and propose this radical change. You two stay here, I'll be back soon!
[Farrell darts out the door like a demented Aristotle, albeit in Farah slacks and sports jacket. And certainly not a Greek genius...on either count.]
[Amber and Marcello sit in the classroom, hoping there is a kendo stick of some description lying around. And notice there was a camera crew there all along...and inconceivable as that sounds.]
Marcello Dias: Well, well, once again the fate of Anber here is left in the hands of an intellectual cruiserweight, who punches above his weight in Kindergarten.
Amber Token: Relativo de seus, Maximoff? Marcello Dias: Oh, sorry, should we let you go fetch that translator for you? Be warned, you probably wouldn't like a few of the nasty things we already said about you.
Amber Token: As if that matters to me. Marcello Dias: Just like his oh so original put downs. Amber's a slut, damn, how original, Ashley! I trust you were up to all hours trying to work out the ultimate insult, and at abvout 4:30am, it came to you in a flash. Slut. Wow. I am in awe of you and your promo abilities, which is no wonder; after all, you will be the next World champion.
Amber Token: E porco força atuar cirurgia... Ashley, you are below the low. You are insignificante; to me, to the patrocinador, and to the Campeonato World. Yet you do not see, as you are too estúpido to see. Of course, it could be a result of all those futuristic steroids you take.
Come to think, for your words, como eles ester divertimento, it is you who has much in common with losers, and personal hygeine deficient rednecks. After all, you sound similar, brag, and make no sense. And, of course, amount to little.
Marcello Dias: Isn't that a side effect of taking Olympic doses of steroids as well? Amber Token: What? Marcello Dias: Being the laughing stock of the embryos after gym class for a start. And the common use, maybe not in your case, of the phrase 'But it IS in!'.
Amber Token: Mais informe do que essencial... Your incompetence resonates. If this does not make sense, you need to see Farrell more than I do.
Marcello Dias: Come to think of it, at least that moron can tell Spanish from Portuguese. Wait a minute...only truly desperate people have used that line in the past, you know, umm...you. Why don't you wait for Valentine's Day, since you are clearly sooooooooo in love with them you imitate them in every way.
Amber Token: Which means I need not bother, I have already won. Marcello Dias: But I've been wondering one thing about PURE recently? Amber Token: Which is? Marcello Dias: Do the promo writers still attend Junior High? I mean, listen to this meathead; and the future ones; 'she's a slut, you're an illegal immigrant, I'm the best.' Purile to say the least. by the way, getting a job means you qualify for citizenship you dumb little bastard with a suspicious amount of acne on their back. Look things up occasionally, before you make a complete ass of yourself while live on national television. I know that may have thrown you, after all, who in their right mind watched the future and all the crap that contained? Which reminds me, didn't you give yourself your first career start? Fine eye for talent you have there, Ashley.
By the way, statistics show that most embryos, such as your charming self, tend to only have careers tending bar. So get to it and give me a Margherita, although it'll probably be 'The Best Damn Mergherita Ever, Because I Made It, I Did'.
Amber Token: But Ashley managed to be right, by fluke. I am not in her league. After all, I am not a member of 'Tolo Estruprador Little League, Liberty City.' And I also manage to have a shred of originality. Liberty City, has someone else used that as video game setting? Why not have your own career, not one of your heroes, who look down at you. I look down on you, as you are absurdo, and will be doing so on Ikusa after you fail to live up to your words, as your words mean nothing. In fact, you are nothing, and mean nothing. Realidade lata ser igual em cadela, 'campeão.'
Marcello Dias: Now, if you have nothing better to do than bully rednecks and strangers and talk cojones all day long, would you find it too impossible to forfeit now? After asll, I have to prepare Amber for defeating the rest of the roster. Remember, words may make a difference, but there is so many you can say before we cannot breathe. Laughter has that problem, you know.
Joseph Farrell: What the? Cut, cut, CUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUTTTTTTTTTTTTTT! [Once again, Amber and Marcello are at the mercy, sorry, 'teachings' of PURE Commercial Affairs Chief, Joseph Farrell.]
Joseph Farrell: Okay then. For the past week, I've been trying my damndest to get you into a position where you can get a greater connection between yourself and the millions of PURE fans worldwide, yet you continue to ignore me and do what you've always done...
Amber Token: Perdão meo... Joseph Farrell: Yes, it's things like that. Talking back is one thing, but in Portuguese just to be spiteful is quite another.
Marcello Dias: Well, at least you can tell the difference between that and Spanish... Joseph Farrell: And you're supposed to be speaking the damn language! Marcello Dias: Perdone mí... Joseph Farrell: Just stop it! Amber Token: You could make your mind up, Farrell. Marcello Dias: Oh, I'm afraid you're now beyond my help when it comes to speaking American.
Amber Token: Do you mean Inglês? Joseph Farrell: I KNOW WHAT I MEAN! Amber Token: Good. Joseph Farrell: Ohhhhhh...RANDY, GET IN HERE! [A nerdy looking kid in a polo shirt and golf shorts wanders into the room.] Randy: Yeah? Joseph Farrell: Give me a hand with these two! Amber Token: Entregar meo por diabólico... Joseph Farrell: WHAT? Randy: He said 'Deliver me from evil.' Joseph Farrell: Oh, it's a fine time to get all Biblical on me now, Token! Marcello Dias: So, let me get this straight; we now have to drag a translator around with us for EVERY promo, just because you think it's needed?
Joseph Farrell: In a word, yes. Amber Token: Wouldn't subtitles be better? Joseph Farrell: No, they cost too much and, besides, I want to see you suffer as you carry this nerdy little runt around with you for months, just to bury your career!
Randy: Hey Joe, I got feelings, you know. Joseph Farrell: Oh who cares about you? I want this little Brazilian bastard to be a bland persona by the end of the month, because I'll get rich off her T-shirt sales!
Marcello Dias: So, it all comes out, does it? Joseph Farrell: It's not like we're being filmed. Amber Token: That would be too fortunate... Joseph Farrell: Wait a minute, I haven't begun! Wait until I show you what the wardrobe department have made for you.
Amber Token: Você tem nunca ter relação sexual, que tu? Joseph Farrell: RANDY! Randy: Erm, she...just..said, oh yes, 'She can't wait to see the costume she's had made for her...she bets that she'll look sexy.'
Joseph Farrell: Okay, that was a little weird, but give me a second. [Farrell walks out for a second...] Marcello Dias: Has anyone ever told you that your Portuguese is really, REALLY bad? Randy: No. Marcello Dias: Does that mean we're the first, and second, to note this fact? Randy: If I told him that you told him that he never had sex in his life, can you imagine his reaction?
[Amber smiles for a second.] Amber Token: Yes, I think I can imagine that. Joseph Farrell: Right, Pele, here's your new gear. Since you're taking on a futuristic, and I think she's Australian, you can make fun of her in these khakis for a start.
Amber Token: Amber Token, 'Caçador Crocodilo.' Maravilhoso... Joseph Farrell: But that's nothing compared to your ring gear. Check out this green and orange singlet. Damn right you'd look sexy!
Marcello Dias: Tío! Buscar ayuda. Seriamente... Joseph Farrell: You see, even he's beginning to play ball. Amber Token: Favorito hobby teu? Randy: Erm, 'Yes, he wants to play balls. BALL! He wants to play ball!' Amber Token: BESTEIRA! Randy: He likes that the best! [Amber gets a wicked smile, realising the fun she can have at two people's expense. Simultaniously...]
Joseph Farrell: So, you think that I'll let you actually get ahead, just because you're playing along? Well, TOUGH! You're taking on the Ice Angel at Ikusa, so you'll be allowed opportunities. You know, useful ones. And maybe you'll be able to use weapons. Blackboard erasers. Tennis balls. Cushions. Try and get hardcore out of that.
Amber Token: I believe I can. Joseph Farrell: Yeah right, Pele. How can you do that? [She glances over to Marcello, who nods, and reaches under the desk.] Marcello Dias: Well, you know how good it is to be prepared? Joseph Farrell: Yes. Marcello Dias: Well, we came prepared in case your midlife crisis co-incided with your nervous breakdown...
[He places a couple of Kendo Sticks on the desk, before he and Amber pick them up.] Joseph Farrell: W-w-wait a minute! Amber Token: Boa noite, José. [They both swing the sticks at his head, but the scene cuts to black just before impact.] [The scene opens up with the camera focused on Marcello, with muffled noises in the background.]
Marcello Dias: As you may have noticed over the past week, myself and Amber have been receiving teaching from Joseph Farrell in order to be more 'American'. Well, we've endured his lessons, and have learned well. Let Amber demonstrate.
[The camera pans 'round, to show Farrell with his mouth taped up, held on top of a desk in the piledriver position.]
Marcello Dias: Now, we learned that in America there is a propensity for violence in public schools, so we decided to join in on this latest fad. Amber, go ahead...
[Amber drops Farrell headfirst through the desk, all the way to the floor, then throws a filing cabinet on top of him for added emphasis.]
Amber Token: Transar você, tua peça de merda insignificante! Marcello Dias: Randy? Translation please. Randy: Well, she just said 'Fu...' Marcello Dias: I wouldn't use language like that on TV, you could end up in hot water. Randy: I think I'd better be going... Marcello Dias: You do that. Amber Token: Adeus! [Randy rushes out the door.] Marcello Dias: Well, I guess school's out for summer, then. Amber Token: I think that Farrell could well be out for that long. Marcello Dias: I don't know. That desk looked suspiciosly easy to break. Amber Token: The floor did not. Marcello Dias: Granted. Either way, I think he won't be bothering us for quite some time. Meanwhile, someone else is.
Amber Token: Who? Marcello Dias: Ashley...what was her name again? Maximoff? Amber Token: That seems improvável. Marcello: What, the name or her being the best? Amber Token: Both. Ashley, please learn your lesson. You do not matter to me, as you are tapado. I do not want to hurt my career by having to carry you to a match that may be barely watchable. After all, look at the facts.
Marcello Dias: Such as the fact you just got punked out by a red. How low can you get? I mean, a redneck?
Amber Token: What is more, you have problems with originality. How many times have you said idêntico exato? Each time, it is 'I'm gonna win' this, 'I shattered his elbow' that, did I miss much?
Marcello Dias: You also missed 'Amber is a slut' this, 'I'm not an accident!' that, and 'You will lose' inbetween. Then there's 'You have no talent', and...frankly, who cares?
Amber Token: It is you that needs to go read some books. May I suggest 'How To Cut An Original Promo'? It may well be on Amazon, and could boost your career to new levels of apathy.
Do you think that I care? Do you think I bothered to notice you before? You are part of your own problema, as you do not see your failings. That may seem impossible, as there are many of them, but that says it. To think I want to impress you is laughable. I want to end you, and earn more respeito than thought possible. But first, please, tell me who else believes that my words are 'made up'? Of course, you will not, as you are desperate. You say desperate words, as you are a desperate man. That should be no surprise, since you have shown no beginning to your 'talent'.
Marcello Dias: Such as your talent for observation. You generally use legal maneuvers to win a normal match, which will put you away all at the same time. Is your Amazon bill going to include 'How To Understand Basic Rules Of Matches' as well? As for your viewing of the gangster/redneck match, didn't you notice for the previous two weeks that they single handedly took farming AND drive bys out during the interregional tag matches, and a singles match? Then again, you did not seem to be watching the same match as every other person. It's strange that your research misses these points just like you miss points on an IQ test. Another side effect, I understand...
Amber Token: As are having no neck, dispropostionate muscle mass, pênis cuidadoso... Marcello Dias: No wonder you don't want anyone near your accidental genitalia. I'd be there for ten minutes repulsed before I could move. Then again, why are you so repulsed by the thought of having a woman, like Amber near your nuts? Of course, I can think of an answer: BAAAAAAA! Well, at least that's the rumour that's going 'round.
Amber Token: As for being too afraid to confront anybody...well now, you're grasping at straws with alarming regularity, aren't you? She hasn't backed down once, and stepped right in the face of those bullies just a day ago, for a very recent example. As for taking out paid performers, maybe she was trying to give you advice; THIS is how you beat somebody up, rather than some of the guys in the back infamous flex your muscles in a way that will appeal to certain Germans.
Try not to be so ridículo, it is saying more of the state of my opposition than the fact I can beat every person put in front of me. To hear you claim that it was the happiest day of your life when you were sent off to work proves this; you live in hope that maybe I have fallen. Um problma, if I have fallen, it is not nearly as low as your level. You had better hope you are in a car crash, Ashley, as it will be far less painful for you, and will gift me a night a fraction more easy. You are right there is no room for us on this planet; you do not seem to inhabit this one.
Marcello Dias: Just like what she does to 'your fire'; she'll get me to piss on it. In fact, you can spit on your fire to extinguish it, as it isn't as large as a match. Sorry to tell you pal, but you just crack me up with quotes from the only book you do seem to read; '101 Wrestling Promo Cliches', which is used by kids new to the business hoping to catch on, until they come up with something better and more entertaining. How long has your career lasted, you say? Was it 20, 21 years of slavery labour? And you have used one promo between the lot, no doubt.
Amber Token: There is no chance for you to achieve greatness, Ashley. That is for people capable of the minor first. You will not get near what is important to me, as that requires sense. Maybe if I took you to meet my família, just so they can gain amusement at how easy my employment is, and we can all 'confuse the Hell' out of you. Before having fun at your expense. We will see how long you last in the favelas, it may be longer than you last in the ring with me, by a hundreth of a second. You may think you are tough, but you have convinced yourself and nobody else. And this will be exposed at Ikusa.
Marcello Dias: Just like your plan to kidnap somebody when you're unconscious is doomed to failure. And don't think I'm some homemaker. I know that, being an poorly cultured ape, AKA 'Futuristic Australian, ala Joseph Farrell', you might have trouble joining the 19th Century, let alone the 20th or the 21st, which we just so happen to be living in outside your personal bubble. I can take your sorry ass to the cleaners just as easily. Women, women like Amber should wrestle, as they will also show you up as the waste of spandex that you are.
Amber Token: I will need to learn the phrase 'I Quit'. After all, how else shall I understand your screams?
Security Gorilla: Wait a minute, what happened in here? Marcello Dias: What? Oh, Joe Farrell had a head rush, and banged his head on the filing cabinet. It fell down with him.
Security Gorilla: What about that desk? Amber Token: It was like that when I got here. Security Gorilla: Okay, you better go, so we can get the EMTs in here for him. [Amber and Marcello walk outside into the hallway, followed by the camera.] Marcello Dias: 'It was like that when I got here'? Amber Token: He believed it. Marcello Dias: You know, Amber, you may be getting American after all... [He puts his arm around her shoulder, and they both walk off down the hallway.]
