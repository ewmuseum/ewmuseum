Ordering the First Belt

14 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ordering the First Belt Miyagawa looked down at the belt lying on the floor in front of him. It was a complete mess. He bought several title belts from a backyard fed in Kansas... they were only ten bucks a pop, but they had been put through hell by sixteen year olds with death wishes and now they were just falling to bits. But they had pride, something that the generic PCW belts didnt have. They all looked like something a man with a cheap goggle gimmick might aspire to wearing, but no Miyagawa had an expensive goggle gimmick, and therefore it demanded a much better belt. He looked up and took in the sight of the office completely, with its fine ornate chairs, and a petite desk resting solemnly on the other side of the room. He breathed the chilled silent air quickly as he awaiting his appointment. He wasnt sure why he was here, Steve had set this whole thing in motion and he could fathom exactly why he got out of bed this morning to sit in a cold office with old furniture, trying to get a spot in a TV commercial. Commercials were JJs department, although then again I am the former TV Champ, Miya thought. He picked up a magazine from beside him on a short table; it was an old PCW Insider mag, about sixteen months old. Those were the old days, he thought. Back when I first joined the PCW, back when men were men and jobbers were jobbers. Back when Kid Wonder first won the Cruiserweight belt at the Old Country Buffet. That was a step back in time. Cruiserweights... Light Heavyweights... Junior Heavyweights... Back when the New Blood instead of repeatedly having tournaments, were instead stuck in one big ring and told to beat each other stupid. That first match certainly made or broke the men that were in it. The winner Lackostress, became a runner in the World Title stakes before leaving PCW for good. Jon Kano, a man who managed to cheat his way to the Continental title before ending up doing virtually nothing. And of course Miyagawa, himself the owner of a federation at last. He sighed at the thought of his own name, and looked back at the belt he had dumped on the floor. It was dammed ugly, so ugly that it made Clydes mother look quite pretty. He taped the metal plate on the front with the tip of his boot, and looked amused as a lump of it fell away from the belt. Reaching down he picked up the lump of belt that fell off it read Champion. Miyagawa stood up abruptly, and put the piece in a pocket. He reached back down and grabbed the belt itself, before walking over to the secretary. As he approached, so looked up and spoke; Sorry sir, Mr Adams still isnt ready to see you, itll be at least another ten minutes yet. Miyagawa looked down at her, his minor concussion from Fusion still making pounding noises in his head. Luckily he had managed to pre record his match against Frisco, so he could get to Japan to host Ikusa I, the first ever Puroresu Ultimate Ring Entertainment card. Good, Im glad He replied, Whose he got in there at the moment? The secretary looked down at the list of people in front of her on the desk, Drew Carey. Miyagawa started to chuckle, until it got too painful for his ears to continue. Drew sodding Carey, eh? I suppose vaguely he does have something amongst experience in the wrestling industry but nothing I would call solid, right? The secretary nodded whole-heartedly. Whereas, Im a multiple title holder in PCW and Im the owner of PURE, the best damned Puroresu Federation out there. Can Carey say out loud without lying that he is the best comic in the world? No. So Chef Boyardee better get someone like me to star in his new adverts. The phone on the desk suddenly rang, and the secretary snatched at it straight away, lifting the handset to her ear, she listened for a few seconds before replying Yes sir. Placing the handset back down, she looked at Miyagawa. Sorry, but Drew Carey has been hired, the boss said that he would give more options. Miyagawa nodded at this, before dumping the rotten, melted beaten up Cruiserweight Belt on the desk. Look at this belt. What does this say to you? Thats right, it says that some sixteen year old moron has more testosterone then brain cells and that is never a good comination. Well Ill tell you this, you can keep the belt. Sell it on ebay or something, you might be able to get thirty bucks for it. Miyagawa turned and walked towards the door to the outside, the secretary stood up to call Miyagawa back, but he was already out the door when she started to call. The rain was pounding down as the goggle-wearing wrestler bunched his raincoat up to try and stay a little bit warmer and dryer. He wiped the raindrops off his goggles as he looked up at the grey San Jose skyline. He grinned, California, famous for its warmth and its sun was now trying to be different. Trying to make English people feel at home. This place felt almost like London. Miyagawa noticed a few subtle differences as he walked, for instance a number of people here could speak a foreign languages, while the Brits constantly insisted that they werent part of Europe, because of course, Europe is as foreign to the United Kingdom as the USA being part of North America. He walked along the sidewalk, mixing in with the crowds, trying to avoid the splashes of water as the cars ran through the puddles along the sidewalk, soaking pedestrians as they went. He finally found the shop he was looking for, a shop he hadnt visited in almost two months, Jamess Wrestling Gear was the shops name, and this guy did everything. He gently pushed the door open, and stepped inside out of the pouring rain. His eyes darted about, as noticed that the shop itself hadnt really changed that much, in fact, it hadnt changed at all, there was still a rack of various outfits on the far wall, and the counter was still right by the door. And Joe also still falls asleep on the job. Miya reaches over and pokes him in the chest, to which Joe awakes, startled. Huh! What? Joe blurts out before he opens his eyes, but when he does he recognises Miyagawa immediately. Oh my god, youre Miyagawa, from the PCW, arent you? Miyagawa nodded straight away, after all, when youre wandering around wearing goggles on your face in the middle of California in the middle of a rainstorm, there isnt really any point trying to make excuses. You either play a really bad game of basketball or your Miya. Joe looked at Miyagawa before grinning, and then speaking, When you came here two months ago and placed that order, I thought you were just a fan, but when I saw you on PCW a week later I thought you were stupid. Whereas now, I think youre great. Hell, have you got any idea just how many pairs of swimming goggles Im selling now? Dont suppose you could wear some nose clips, its just that my mate Jimmy can get them real cheap, and I could make a total killing with them. Joe awaited Miyas answer, but Miya didnt answer the question, instead he had a look around the shop, looking at the various feds merchandise. Hell theres even Phantom Booker branded tights to wear on your head, should the fan want to go for that I really, really like hosiery look. He looked through the t-shirt rack, noting that NTICW had released a Steve Jabowski shirt now hell he was surprised that Steve wasnt boasting about it probably something to do with the fact that there was at least five Miyagawa shirts, four old Bang shirts, one Bangs Mother shirt, two Kensuke Suzahara PCW shirts and about nine Trashcan Man shirts. Oh, and three Asylum shirts... And an official Asylum straight jacket... And the official Trashcan Man Trashcan.... And Bangs toy tractor. Although theres probably a Flaymin Aymin tractor in the works now.... And surely Soy Boy Soy Sauce cant be too far away. God I miss the old days. They were fun. Miyagawa laughed out loud as he saw the next item, in the Rent-a-Costume section he walked up to it and saw it was a Clyde Costume. Complete with mangled title belt of no description, sledgehammer and dark clothing lots of. Joe called out as he saw Miyagawa looking at the costume; That comes with a free grey beard this week. Miyagawa chuckled, he always knew that Clyde was beardy, but I guess that proved it at last. He seemed sated, and with that, he walked back to the counter, placing the small part of the Cruiserweight Belt on the table. Have you done that belt to my specifications? Joe nodded, and Miyagawa continued How long to incorporate this into the belt, but I was it to be embedded into the belt, so that it looks like its supposed to be there. And I want the actual title name to be Junior Heavyweight now. Joe nodded at all of this, and picked up the piece, before going to walk out the back of the shop, before turning back to Miyagawa and speaking, Give me half an hour, Ive got a couple of videos behind the counter, watch those if you want to keep amused. Joe turned back and went out the back of the store, as Miyagawa watched. Ten minutes had passed, and Miyagawa is now sitting behind the counter, watching a video on the TV monitor placed on the counter. Sitting there, eating a bag of Worcester Sauce crisps he laughed happily at the PCW 2000 highlights video. A moment ago it was showing the Trashcan Man defeat Ed in a Stairway to Hell match, and now it was showing Kid Wonder winning the Old Country Buffet match to become PCW Cruiserweight Champion. How fitting that was, the thought drifted through Miyagawas head, that the man considered to be the greatest Cruiserweight Champion ever, didnt hold the belt for the longest time. Both Dan Hollywood and Jake Keeton held the belt for longer, and Miyagawa was also amongst that list... and the same with the Television Title too. Joe burst through the door the back of the shop, with a black bag in his hand, which he placed on the counter. Miyagawa looked him in the eye, and Joe nodded. Miyagawa reached down and opened the bag, pulling out a championship belt; a championship belt that was all dragons and Asia and Japan and imagery.. Its perfect, exclaimed Miyagawa. Joe wiped a tear from his eye, before hinting at Miya to get out of his chair, to which Miya did in fact move out of the chair, before strapping the belt around his waist, closing his coat and walking out the shop. Ill wear it for now... keep it warm and all that. Joe was about to run after him, but he found a wad of notes had been thrown in the bag, where Miya had left it. As Miyagawa left the shop it stopped raining, and a ray of sunshine arced through the clouds, hitting a building in front of him. He looked up at the building and smiled, Its time, Sukey, its time.
