The Beginning...at last

5 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Beginning...at last It was, perhaps...too simple. [Fade up.] [Masanobu Akazi sits on a wooden bench; his knees, wrapped in ice, are straightened with his legs propped on an overturned bucket. He's wearing black tights, that go down to about mid-calf. He's shirtless, sweat dripping down the just starting to sag skin around his breasts and shoulders. His eyes are squinted, obviously in pain, and his long hair, soaked, hangs down his back. This is the 'Jido-san' no one sees.] I was looking at my beginning here...a match against the unknown, not only my opponent, but from within myself. Closing in on twenty-five years in the ring...and although I can still fight...I know that, can I carry the federation like I aspire to do? And, the question that burns at me, day and night...can I continue to put in the effort if I can't? [Akazi leans back, putting his hands behind his head, and cushioning the rest against the steel lockers behind him. He groans, until he settles.] That's all I've wanted for the last seven years of my career...is to be that guy that was the end-all and be-all of his federation...The Great Azuka was that...Senzuke Kimaro was that...GIANT Denton was that...and there I was ready to step in...and it lasted three months, before a man named Kaan, all of twenty-five years old, tore down the walls around the castle that I had built...before the mortar had even dried. [Akazi looks up, his eyes glazed over, looking for answers that aren't there.] And, so, that was it...the empire...*my* empire could never begin...and relocation was my only option...somewhere where I would be relieved, relaxed... That place where my blood will spill, where my sweat will pour, and my tears will undoubtedly flow...that place where my pride will be tested...where my respect will thrive...where integrity is all that I need to survive. That place...where I'll make my knees suffer, where I'll endure all obstacles, where my life will be lived or killed, where the past will be become my future, and my future will be the past of something great... That place...is...this...place. And, this place is PURE. [Fade out.]
