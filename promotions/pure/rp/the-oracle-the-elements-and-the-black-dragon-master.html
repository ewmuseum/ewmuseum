The Oracle...The Elements...and the Black Dragon Master...

undated

The Oracle...The Elements...and the Black Dragon Master... Never regard this world as The only one; The next world And the one after the next... All the worlds are here now. Meditation is a wonderful thing. It prepared you for many things...enlightenment...understanding...tranquility...and stability. It was the inner being coming to peace with itself. It was how emotions were controlled...it is how impure thoughts are killed... It could be very easy to think impure thoughts about Team Okiki... It is no trouble for Inphino to think about how these two would be no challenge for him...how theyre not even up to the level that he possesses. He could always think about how he would tear at the nerves of his opponents this Ikusa with the Void of Sorrow...Drive unconsciousness into the enemy with Black Dragon Smasher...and inflict pain into the hearts of foes with My Angelic Suicide. Imagining each and every one of his moves to cause suffering upon Team Okiki would be such a nice thought...imagining their cries of pain... But then...that would be giving into the Darkness... Giving into the Void... And Inphino is not going back down that path... ...Not again... This is PURE...to bring about the reminder... Blitz was the epitome of what this place existed for...and giving into the inner demons would not establish his position as the spokesman of PURE. Being the top name people think of when they think of PURE requires not only strength physically...but mentally as well... Know this...and you will be able to improve yourself...both in combat and in life. It is an never-ending war inside us all... It is a forever for Inphino... But for you... Let the battle...begin... Sunset was falling upon the dojo...the dwelling of one Inphino Blitz...the Black Dragon Master. But this time we overlooked the residential dwelling, skimming over the roof, passing the garden, and focusing on the training hall. Inside, there was a wrestling ring, sandbags, a Wing Chun wooden practice dummy, a few sandbag dummies, mats, parallel bars and gymnast rings. But no Inphino... There was a sliding screen door to the back of the hall. On it is a black dragon coiling around amidst the clouds, a faint light creeping through the screen and the cracks of the doors. Inside, a black candle burned with a orange flicker upon a small table sitting at the wall of the spacious, yet dark room. A white taped hand reached over it, gently holding what looks to be a red rose petal. The flame lapped at the peal, consuming it like a starving man would food. The hand traveled back to rest on the muscular thigh of one...Inphino Blitz. Much like this flame... he thought, whispering to himself. ...men are hungry for the destruction of others. His right hand cupped a few more rose petals gently, his black fingernails curling around them. His left hand plucked another from his hand and fed it to the generous flame. This flower...plucked apart by the selfish will of man...by time or by force. That...is how man wishes to do to his foes...take him apart. Yet man is never patient enough to let time take its toll, so he takes the initiative himself to dissect his foes. Picking them apart...one...by...one. Another petal burned... But while he is plucking at the competition...he never truly realizes what he is destroying. Taking the petals from the rose destroys the rose...the beauty and the smell...what made a rose a rose. Appreciation is a part of understanding. The last petal is tweezed between his two fingers, held at his face. Appreciate what makes a rose a rose...what makes one on the inside...and then you will understand how to take him apart from the outside. With a bow of his head, he incinerates the last rose petal. Then he rested both of his hands across his lap, folding them near his lower abdomen. With a few slow, deep breaths, you could almost see relaxation melt over him. Inphino was set on preparing himself mentally for the battle soon to be at hand mentally. A few minutes passed by in silence. Then the room was bathed in darkness. The candle had gone out...actually, it was more like the flame was blown out. Inphino did not stir at all amidst the darkness. But silence did not mean the lack of movement... The candle was lit again, revealing a point of light within the room. Inphino stood before the candle with a lit match in his hand, his back to the rest of the room. With a soft breath, he extinguished the flame on the match and placed it near the candle. The Black Wind howls... he muttered to himself. Then his head looked up from the candle, looking straight at the calligraphy wall scroll upon the wall. Since when did you align yourself with the Void Elements...Omina? Behind him stood a woman dressed in a silver vest, blouse, and long flowing skirt with heels. Her silver hair flowed down her back, much like her silver silk covered legs flowed visibly from the long slit in her skirt. Behind her stood seven figures in black attire, ranging from a basic hood and cloak to black silk. Inphino didnt look back at them...but he knew who they were. The past leaves no better reminder than memories. The woman in silver stared at his back, a melancholy look over her face. Hello, Inphino. Its been awhile. Indeed... he responded, agreeing with her. Long enough to see youve made some new friends. She looked back slightly at the black garbed individuals behind her. A humorous scoff came about her, though it could hardly be called such. Necessity makes strange bedfellows, Inphino. There is a matter that requires both the Void Elements and the Celestial Oracle to take care of. Inphino almost turned around to look at her. Celestial... Oracle? Congratulations...progress in ones work is always a good sign of improvement. Omina nodded her gratitude. Thank you. But I will not waste time with you. I know how thorough you are with conversation. Nice to know you remembered. a sigh came out of his lips as he folded his arms across his chest. So...what brings Omina and the Void Elements to my dojo? Omina looked to her left and right, nodding at the dark individuals behind her. Then she returned to answering Inphinos question. Akiyama. If you could hear Inphinos breathing, you would know that it stopped for a second. He stood there frozen in silence for a few seconds before responding. ...What of him? Hes missing. the deep voice of Darkcyde came from behind Omina. The giant leader of the Void Elements stepped forward a bit, revealing his grim, shadowed face. His black cloak billowed as he walked forward. If you do not mind me asking...what is the dilemma? A growl came up from Darkcyde as the white haired Shinnosuke stepped forward as well, a slim blade slung over his shoulder. He wore a black turtleneck and black slacks and boots. Before he left, he managed to infiltrate the DDE compound and disappear with the Divine Prophetess. Then he disappeared in a white flash of light at PCW Supremacy this passing Sunday. Hmmm...the Department of Divine Examination could not stop him...sometimes you wonder what good the government is nowadays. Cute Darkcyde muttered angrily. But if you cut out the wisecracks, youll realize that somethings going on! If you say so, Darkcyde. Inphino rubbed his chin for a moment. I mean, the sole person in existence that has managed to enrage me is missing. It is not the end of the world. Actually...it is. Omina countered seriously. We suspect that Akiyama and Cyclonica are searching out the Savage Tranquility. Another wave of silence hit the Black Dragon Master. He stood there for a moment, his hand pressing against his chin. Hmmm... was the first thing heard from him. I dont believe this sh!t. Kiyone stepped up from the darkness, her dark eyes glaring fire into the back of Inphino. She was dressed in a black leather halter top and black leather pants. We have the Neo Void God and the Divine Prophetess out to destroy the entire f^cking universe, and all this b!tch has to say about it is Hmmm? You suck!! Actually...I was thinking. he responded. So Akiyama and Cyclonica seek the Savage Tranquility to destroy the universe...what brings you to me? Chronic stepped out of the darkness, dressed in a black tank top and black jeans and boots. Weve been ordered to search for them both along with Omina here...but we need you to search for the Savage Tranquility. Yeah...out of all the bleeding servants under the will of the Celestials, were currently understaffed. muttered Darkcyde begrudgingly. Rat bastards. Inphinos brow wrinkled. So...you...the Void Elements...the very same people who kidnapped Soteria from me...want me to help you to find the Savage Tranquility...the Word of Chaotic Serenity...to stop a psycho god and the Divine Prophetess from destroying the universe. Am I correct? Shinnosuke grinned; he was one of the few Void dwellers who could do so. Pretty much! I may sound pretty shallow when I ask this of you...but what will I get from helping you? Hypocritical f^cking bastard... Kiyone began swearing up a storm as Omina looked rather surprised at his back. Boy...didnt see that question coming... she admitted embarrassed. Shinnosuke chuckled at her. Some oracle you are! Omina glared at him before answering Inphino. Well...Im sure that lord Kirin and lady Hinata would be more than willing to grant you godhood upon completion of the task. It seems like godhood really is not all it is cracked up to be nowadays. he muttered lowly. See Akiyama... Oh sure, bring up the one flawed god you know. Shin responded. Besides...I kinda like the sound of Black Dragon God myself! Ill pass...thank you. Omina sighed. Very well...Ill see to it that Soteria is returned to you upon completion of this job. Two large figures loomed in behind her, towering over her like two monsters. The first was Mystic, a large blonde amazon-like woman wearing a black bikini top and black shorts. Every inch of her body was solid muscle. The other was X, a mysterious being in a hooded robe. Growls escaped from both of them as they glared down at Omina. Omina looked back at them, challenging their looks with her own. If that is your price for your help, then so...be...it. Her words still didnt push back the two monsters. Darkcyde and Kiyone were near animosity themselves at the promise Omina made Inphino. But then a small teenaged girl wearing a silk gown stepped between Omina and the other Void Elements. Her black hair veiled over her face completely, but underneath her dark locks a blank, yet authoritative stare pierced into them. Omina gave them a smug look before turning to Inphino again. Will that be all? They could see Inphino nodding from their view behind him. That...is all I ask of you. Omina nodded. So youll take on the task? There was no reply... Inphino merely sat back down, getting into his meditation position and lowering his head. His eyes closed at his final words flowed from him. The fate of the universe rests in my hands... There are those who are destined for greatness... Then there are those who are chosen to become great. And then some have greatness thrust upon them Somehow...Inphino Blitz always seems to fall into greatness by circumstance... Be it by destiny or be it by fate...the path you are placed upon will have ups and downs...but it is you as the traveler of that path who decides how you will take on the obstacles in your way. You can either let them stop you...or you can plow through them. Either way...you make decisions on how you will travel... If this path leads him towards eventually saving the world...so be it... And if this path leads him towards becoming PURE Tag Team champion...that would be nice too... Guess well just let fate decide... Mina de Margo sat at the kitchen table with a laptop typing furiously. She was dressed in a pair of drawstring gray sweats, white socks, and a white short-sleeved T-shirt. Her blood red hair was held back by a black bandana. On her face was a pair of black-rimmed glasses, which she peered at the screen through. From the backyard, Inphino came walking into the kitchen. He was wearing his old trench coat, and carrying a black knapsack slung over his shoulder. Mina looked up at him and smiled. Going somewhere? she asked. He nodded slowly. I have a task to complete...it may take me a while. She looked up at him, her eyes full of worry. Then she reached across the table and grabbed something small and black. She turned back to him, holding the object out to him. My cell phone... she revealed to him. If you...ya know...ever need anything. Blitz stared at the phone for a second, and then turned his gaze to her. Their eyes met, staying locked on each other for some time before Inphino took the phone from her and slipped it into his coat pocket. Thank you. I will do so if the need arises. he bowed to her respectfully, his black and blue hair falling over his face. Mina reached out for him, brushing away the strands of his hair to look at him again. Please... she said softly. No more formalities... An awkward silence filled the room as they met eyes again. Clearing his throat, Inphino leaned back up and looked at the laptop. What are you doing? Mina turned back to the computer, moving it to show Inphino. Oh...well PCW all of a sudden fired everyone, so I figured that maybe if I send in an application for you, I could get you back into PCW! Sounds like a good plan, for sure? He sighed...not the answer she really wanted to hear. PCW... I will return to that place only if my path takes me there. Oh... she said, her voice lowering a bit. Inphino sighed again, knowing she was disappointed. But I thank you nonetheless. Who knows...maybe it is my destiny to return to that place...bring about the honorable fighting spirit that the place of battle has lacked for so long. =/=/=/= They stood there in an embrace, the both of them... holding each other as if they were never to see each other again. Anyone who knew Blitz would know that he wasnt big on hugs, but Mina suddenly wrapped her arms around him once she had walked him to the door. Sentimental moments...they were rare. Inphino patted her on her back, unsure of what to do exactly. She leaned back and looked into his eyes, hers almost to tears. What drove her towards such emotions? Blitz bowed his head to her, his way of saying goodbye for now. She smiled, trying to keep her emotions under control. Then as he made it down to the driveway, he stopped when Mang-A-Hero, Jordan X Shabazz, and Yuka Hamada came running up to the house. They were sweaty and out of breath...having ran for miles as a part of their training. They were confused about what was going on, but Inphino said nothing as he bowed to each of them and began walking down the road leading towards town. Nightfall was almost upon them...the last sliver of sunlight fighting...not trying to allow darkness to overpower it. It was so much like the essence of Inphino...he existed in that sunset...the area where dark meets light...with neither truly having the upper hand. Total...and complete...equality... And is that what made him great? Who knows... All that was known at this moment was that he was chosen to save the universe...he was to face Team Okiki this Sunday to advance to the finals for the PURE Tag Team champions... Pretty tough to balance out your priorities...but he would do both...hes required to do both... Why? Because...his path requires him to do so... With that said and done... he muttered to himself as he walked down that gravelly roada parting muse if most ...The Black Dragon Master has spoken.
