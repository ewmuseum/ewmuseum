Calender Series: November 2001 Written: --/--/-- (Ikusa #4) Columbia University. College in general, is just one big fat party. Now I’ll admit that Columbia isn’t really the biggest party school there is, but New York City is definitely the Lord of all parties. If you want a good time there’s no place better than NYC, baby! Oh hell yeah!
But this story isn’t about Cornell or even New York City. It’s about a little, tiny, insignificant spec of an arena… Madison Square Garden.
It was there that my dream was founded. It was there that this story truly begins. It’s not a story of honor. It’s not a story of love. It’s not a story of a falling sky when really the dumb chickadee just had a piece of an egg fall on her head. Pff, chicks, can’t trust them to think anything logically.
No, this story is about a boy. A young boy who strived to be one thing… first. It all started in November where this youthful college senior saw his first glimpse of his soon-to-be profession. It was something many call barbaric or for rednecks. It’s something that’s meant to be for only certain people. Big muscular tan guys. Guys who you would see on the cover of Playgirl or Muscle & Fitness magazine. These guys are thought more of as Gods. They are in a business that is all about respect and politics. It’s all about getting help and if you make a mistake or don’t kiss the right ass you’re ass is kicked away to the curb. You’re never to be heard of again. It’s a business where careers and lives are lost in an instant every day. It’s life or death. It’s about the reactions you get. It’s about going through fifteen minutes of magic that is remembered for years and years to come only to try and outdo yourself two nights later. It’s about doing it for the entertainment and getting a little glory along the way. It’s pro wrestling and a few college buddies wanted to see if they could “cut it.”
--- Every story must have its beginning. Without a beginning there is no story to tell. This story begins in November. A lovely, wintery, November night it was. Five friends from college went to see a nice of blood, sweat, and tears and along the drink a couple of beers. Five friends, all from Columbia University in for a night of action. Five friends all in it together.
The first, the so called, leader-of-the-pack to this quintuple set, was an egotistical man named Oliver Watson. Oliver was the guy who had a different chick every night. Night after night after night. Over and over and over. Lack of sleep was a common thing for everyone else in his dorm rooms as moaning wasn’t quite a rarity. He was the guy who always would yell, “Grab me another brewskie! I’m taking off my shirt!” and then he would. He’s a barrel of laughs and filled to the brim with hangovers. Your classic party animal.
Next on the list was James Amoto Ford III. Jimmy as most every soul who knew him called him. Jimbo was half-Filipino, half-American. His mother was Filipino and that is where he and his three brothers spent the first part of their lives. Each of them were obsessed with cars and being that their last name was Ford not a single car in their driveway was anything but. He was a quiet kid with a small stutter, but it didn’t make much of a difference to anyone. Just something that people noticed, but didn’t care about. Jimmy was the guy who everyone liked. He’s just that kind of guy, muscular, cute as hell, and with a skin color that looks like a year round tan, how can you not dig this brutha?
The third guy in this small group was the comical Irish one. Compared to Jimmy’s tan skin this boy looked like a piece of paper. His skin color shines bright lights off of it. Actually, he is possibly the funniest guy there is. Always with a smile on his face, but is logical in every way. He always knows just what to say and do. When other people make wrongs he rights them. His name was Patrick William Morris. Pat was a great guy. He is the guy that everyone knows and loves. Everyone.
Fourth was the eldest of the group by a few months to Oliver. Roland Wilson was just the guy who… well, it’s hard to describe him. He’s very smart. Smart does not begin to describe him, and sometimes he acts a little queer, for lack of a better term. He’s not, but some of his gestures are a little out there. He’s funny as hell and is a great guy to talk to. Always speaks the truth, but tries not to hurt you or anyone. What happens, happens. It just does. That’s who he is and he’s damn proud of it.
The last guy in this group is impossible to describe as this boy is me. I am Ilan Levin. I’m Jewish, I’m proud, and my motto is, “Take everything with a grain of salt.” It gets me in trouble often enough with my friends. But what can I say? I don’t try to hurt people, it just happens and I am the first person to admit my mistakes. No matter how many there are. I make mistakes quite often in those senses, but I try to make up for them. I try. That’s all I can really do, right? Try?
So these five friends went on a trip up to MSG for a night of wrasslin’! Oh yeah! It was energetic. It was fun. It was fantastic. It was unforgettable. The night of wrestling wasn’t exactly the most important part of this night though. No, the most important part was the walk away from the arena. As these friends went over what they had just seen in their minds and with their hands and sound effects a subject came up. Could they do it?
“Hey!” Oliver shouted. “I could so kick those guys asses in there! Every last one of ‘em! Hell yeah!”
“Uhh, sure Ol.” Pat was just a bit doubtful of Oliver’s confidence. They all were actually, but Oliver just kept on going as he most always does.
“Me and you man. Me and you,” he challenged in a semi-drunken rage that was bound to get more drunken later on.
I just shook my head. “Idiots…” “Hey,” Pat defended, “He’s challenging ME!” “True, true.” “Fact is, I could k-kill all of ya.” Jimmy wanted in on this action. “Dude, we could so do all of that. All of us.” I don’t remember who said this. “What?” asked Oliver. “We so have got this.” “What?” I asked. “Yeah, what are you talking about, man?” “Let’s sign up to wrestle!” Okay, okay. Now none of us are slackers or overweight or whatever, but c’mon! Jimmy and Oliver are the biggest of all of us and they’re not even 6’5”. I myself am 5’11” with shoes on and just barely reach 185 pounds. I actually did wrestle in high school, but there’s a difference between this and that. “Are you kidding me?”
He shook his head. “Hell no, we can do this!” “Hey, I’m in.” “We all gotta do it.” Whatever, “All right,” I agreed. So we all put our hands in a big circle and Oliver spat out a pitiful cheer. “All for one and one for all, we see this through until all of you fall.”
And so the cards were set and we signed up later in the month, all five of us. It was the beginning to this story for every story must have a beginning to be a story worth telling…
