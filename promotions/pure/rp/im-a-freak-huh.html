I'm a freak, huh?

October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I'm a freak, huh? Written: My first match on a PURE show, what a great success it was! I bet the piss out of Phreak and never felt better. The bloody nose? Nah, I don’t mind bloody noses, all part of being an entertainer in my books. I have to say Phreak is one tough little fucker, especially for a sixteen year old but still, he’s no match for this warrior.
But not everyone seemed to appreciate me coming out to the ring, putting my life on the line, to boost the company’s standings and to entertain the crowd…
“We got our selves a freak and Phreak. Let’s get this junk over with.” Is that right now, Matty boy? Huh? Ya think I’m a freak? You twat wanker! Piss off and get a real bloody job! At least MAH and Hitomi can do a poor job well, you just make it worse. You’re a disgrace. You hope I die? That’s what you said, wasn’t it? Heh, right lad…right…
I come to PURE thinking I’ll be welcomed…but noooo, a bloody commentator bad mouths me which incites the crowd to treat me like the devil while I wrestle. And when I’m leaving the god damn building to get to my car, I have to deal with a bollocking from so-called fans jeering me with profanities, hand gestures and throwing some garbage…what a life, eh?
I’ll soon change the way I’m treated around here, no worries about that, pal. Soon enough…those wankers out there won’t even dare use my bloody name in a negative way, ohohoho…won’t even dare.
Next week I’ll probably be putting up with this crap again…probably be the same the week after, but as long as I keep throwing their boos and jeers back in their faces by having Hanza Kibgame announce my name in victory week after week they’ll have no choice but to cease the jeers…and if they don’t…well, lets just say they better bloody not…
