Sleep with one eye open...gripping your pillow tight.

10 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sleep with one eye open...gripping your pillow tight. Written: 10.10.02 [I want to fight.] [There’s been a fire burning in my gut for so long now. One match is not enough to satisfy my needs to release my emotions in a physical form. I’ve had only one match, only one chance to inflict harm on another innocent person. God, it felt so good. Kick, kick, punch, jab. The screams of agony that my only opponent let out was like a symphony…a symphony…of destruction.]
[Look into my eyes. What do you see? What do you feel? What do you sense? I want to be wild, untamed, and bad. I want to look into my adversary’s eyes, and make the water, tears. I want their salty tears to roll down their cheek, and plummet to the ground. Pain.]
[I’m not turning into a malicious, psychopathic person. No, I just need to unleash myself every now and then. I don’t hurt people for the fun of it. I’m not my opponent, Ashley Maximoff. I was forced into this hell, and the only way out is to obey the cruel people who have banished me into the world of fighting. So be it. I’ll fight, but not for them, but for me.]
The rising sun broke the horizon as a dawning of a new day started. The dim lights crept their way into Amber’s room, shining between the cracks in the white curtains and blinds that were supposed to block the sun’s rays. A familiar, yet annoying buzzing sounded her day off. The alarm cock continued to ring as Amber pushed the colorful quilt off of her lithe body, with nothing on but bra and panties. She rolled to the side of the bed, sitting up as she stretched her body. She stood up, rubbing her eyes free of sleep as she grabbed the alarm clock. With the clock in her hand, she looked at it inelegantly before launching the clock across the room, with it crashing into the bedroom door, which was slightly ajar. Amber strides across the room to her black and white colored, spherical dresser. She opens the two top drawers, and carelessly picks out two sets of clothing: a lacy low cut bra, and a pair of green boxer shorts. And in the other hand she had a white shirt, and pair of hot shorts. Amber looked around the lightening room before shrugging and wandering off to her left, where another door is slightly open. She pulls it out, and takes a step in to reveal a classy shower and bathroom.
Several minutes pass before the sound of running water stops and Amber opens the restroom door, her hair drenched and darkened because of it. Her body is wrapped in a white bathroom towel, covering her upper body and lower body, stopping at mid-thigh. She shakes her head to both sides swiftly, letting her hair fly through the air before resting gracefully onto her shoulders and back.
“Ah…I feel so refreshed,” she said in a calm, soothing tone of voice. Amber walked back to her dresser and opened it again, this time pulling out a soft, plush black robe. She puts herself into it, and ties it up before reaching down her chest, and pulling out the towel. She looks at her bed, and sees the clothing she picked out earlier. “I’ll put those on a little later,” she thought to herself, as no one was around to hear her. Still in the robe, she walks out of the room for the first time since her previous slumber.
As she opens the door, light floods the scene in the big living room leading out of the master bedroom. She turns around to see a yellow “sticky” note stuck to the door. With a slightly confused look on her beautiful face, she reaches out, and pulls the note off of the door. She holds it several inches from her eyes, and reads aloud.
“Gone to town. Be back in a few hours. Marcello,” she read slowly, trying to make due with the crude handwriting. “…That’s cool, I can just relax for the day,” she thought to herself as not a sound can be heard in the large house.
[Relax for the day? What a nice thought, isn’t it? But with thoughts swimming through her head, and events occurring in her life, what are the odds of having a “relaxing day”? Think for a moment, would you?]
Several hours have passed, and Marcello is yet to arrive at the house. Amber is sat quietly on the sofa laying down, now completely dressed and out of the robe. Her focus is nowhere, just staring straight up at the white, white ceiling. A variety of different bird chirps are heard through the window, from the large yard that surrounds the house. Random neighborhood cars pass the front of the house, red, blue, white, black…all different types, styles and looks. Amber sits up on the sofa, leaning over the sofa to glance out of the window.
[Finally…the scene of action. There is no “normal” day in the life of Amber Token…ever.] All of sudden, a black limousine streaks exceedingly fast down the friendly asphalted streets, as a burly man stands up through the sun roof, yelling inaudible curse words at the house, as he pulls his arm on top of the roof. He cocks his right arm, and launches it forward, with an object soaring to the house. The man smiles as the object, a heavy stone with a paper strewn to it, breaks the window pane next to the one Amber was gazing out of it. He sets up and shouts at Amber. “You better get that done, or you’ll be sorry!” the words drift off as the car rockets down the streets, with the man slipping back down into the seat. Amber, obviously distraught on the happenings of a few moments ago, reaches over and calmly picks the stone up. Amber turns back around, and sits down properly on the sofa, and places the heavy rock on the coffee table nearest to her. She unties the strings with her fingers easily, and pulls the slip of paper out. On it, roughly written out, with several words stretching across the paper, obviously written in the car, Amber translates the letter orally.
“Hush little baby, don’t say a word…and never mind that noise you heard. It’s just the beasts under your bed, in your closet, and in your head…” she starts off slowly. Amber coughs, with a tear rolling down her cheek as she keeps reading. Your first job is upon us. Your first chance to pay us back for all the helpful services we have given you in the past several years. The job, take out your first PURE opponent, Ashley Maximoff. Reasons will be given later in life. Oh, and sorry about the window…but we’re not deliverymen. Signed, Anthony Mancini,” she finishes the last lines with a look of shock on her face.
She was humbled. Her lips were quivering at a rapid pace as she reread the letter time after time again. “Take…out…Ash?” she questioned herself. “But why? I’m not like her…I don’t kill for fun. I don’t hunt for the sport of it,” she again, had trouble letting the words glide through her lips.
Amber stands up to a vertical base, with the letter still in hand. She looks around the house for any other weird things, but finds none. Amber stumbles several feet forward, but regains balance. “Why is life so hard on me? What did I ever do to deserve this?” she asks herself once more, as her voice echoes off of the walls of the house. A loud knock broke the eerie silence of the house. Amber lifts her head up completely, and dashes to the back door, where the sound was ringing. Amber quickly tries to make herself look professional, wiping off the tears that are now stalling on her cheek, with the sleeve of her shirt. Amber quickly opens the curtained door, which blocked view from outside, hoping that maybe Marcello forgot his keys. How wrong can Amber be?
Amber’s face turns pale white, as she staggers back a few feet. The door opens the rest of the way as a black shoed foot enters the house. The rest of the figure waltzes into the house. Anthony Mancini. His jet-black hair shines in the light of the house, as he adjusts his Armani suit. He smiles a wicked smile as Amber backs up, and comes open a chair, which she falls back on. “Why are you here? You have no business here…leave!” Amber commands the much larger man.
Mancini just smiles and gazes at Amber. “What, no greetings for your favorite mob leader?” he asks in a mocking tone of voice. He walks the rest of the way into the house, and slams the door behind him. “I see your little friend, Marcello isn’t here…you don’t understand the lengths a police officer will go for a fifty. In case you were wondering, Marcello is being stalled in traffic right now, and you’re all alone,” he wickedly snarls. “Now…allow myself to make myself at home…” he arrogantly remarks as he looks around the house. “Hmm…pretty good crib, eh? Can you believe it’s from us? From your superiors?” he menacingly asks Amber.
Amber is perfectly still. “Why?” she asks…the only word she can muster to ask. “Why what?” Mancini snaps back at her. Amber, still standing completely still, stares a hole through Mancini. Anthony turns around to her, and puts on a fake smile. “Let me guess, Amber…you want to know why we told you to take Ash out, am I correct?”
“You are,” Amber calmly replies. “Well…you see, Ash has this, well…little…problem. Every time you see her, she is killing someone, am I right?” he asked in a more serious tone, one that Amber has never heard before.
“Yes…yes, you are,” Amber replies once more as Anthony Mancini moves toward the wall, and leans against it while staring at Amber.
“Well…you see, I, Tony Mancini am frankly, sick of her actions. Killing, and slaughtering innocent people is not cool by me. Sure, you may seem shocked at this, but it is the truth. We kill for reasonable causes, but she does it for the joy of killing,” he stands up straight as the words slip through his mouth. He makes a point there. His gang doesn’t kill for the fun of it, but to claim their territory and agenda. But Ash does it for sport.
“And that’s why you want me to take her out? For the good of the community? For the good of the world? So I can finally let out some of these pent-up frustrations?” she asks him in a lighter voice. He nods.
“Exactly, my beauty…” he laughs as he nods once more. “Now…I must be going. You know what to do. So do it,” his last words ring throughout Amber’s ears as he makes a quick escape from the house, going through his entrance door. Amber grew a sly, almost cheerful smile.
“He didn’t hurt me,” she thought to herself once more. “Good…” Amber walked back to the sofa and sat down on it, crumpling up the note she had received via window mail. She laid back down on the sofa, thinking up a good excuse for the damaged window. “Baseball…” she thought, “an American past time.”
[Her mission was set in stone. The writings on the wall came to her. And she’ll scream it from the mountaintops; pride comes before a fall for Amber. She needs to take Ash out, for the good of the world, and future.]
