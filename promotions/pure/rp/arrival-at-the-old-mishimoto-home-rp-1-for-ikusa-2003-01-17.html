Arrival at the old Mishimoto home (rp#1 for Ikusa)

Arrival at the old Mishimoto home (rp#1 for Ikusa) Written: 17/1/03 The streets are buzzing with cars and bikes as usual in Japan, the hot red sun burning down on the ground as Ryu and Otoshi get out of a Cab and pay the driver, the walk onto the pathway and look at the house before them, they smile carrying their bags. Ryu opens the gate and walks into the front yard, they walk to the front door and drop their bags off and look at the house, Otoshi looks around “my god this my old house”
They look around, Otoshi beaming with happiness to finally be home after all these years, they walk in and inspect the old home, everything seems to be covered in dust and cobwebs, Otoshi wipes down the seat and sits down, Ryu brings in the bags and looks at the inside “this place is filthy!” Otoshi glares back at his best friend “well my when my mother died, the house was turned over to me but I was in America with you! So yeah I was expecting it to be filthy!” Ryu walks over, sits opposite of Otoshi, and looks at him “can you believe it were home after so many years in the states were finally home and wrestling in front of our own people” they sit their basking in the glory of being home.
“Did you see the card for PURE Ikusa?” asks ryu moving the bags inside, Otoshi barely can understand him and is too busy concentrating on cleaning the house up “uh, no don’t think so why are we wrestling on it?” Ryu comes walking back into the room smiling “we got a mystery partner”, Otoshi stops and looks at him confused “what you mean mystery partner?” looking confused, Ryu walks up to him and holds his shoulders “what you think it means! We got a partner and it’s a mystery? So what? Doesn’t matter who it is, we can win the titles by ourselves”
Otoshi wipes down the table and benches, thinking of the partner, wondering whom it might be. He stops and looks at Ryu “why would it be a mystery? Why would the boss do that?”
Ryu glances back at otoshi “I have no idea, but it doesn’t matter” lying on the couch but as soon as he lies on it, the boards under it snap and the couch splits in half causing ryu to fall to the hard floor “what the hell!” he yells out in pain. Otoshi peers at the folded up body of ryu, who is covered in dust mumbling to himself “Otoshi, this place is a wreck! We need new furniture and shit!” he yells out whilst trying to pry himself out of the broken couch, Otoshi ambles over and gives ryu a helping hand once up ryu dusts himself off and continues mumbling walking to the bathroom. Otoshi goes back to cleaning the kitchen when Ryu gives out a loud cry followed by a loud crash; Otoshi looks down the hall curious “Ryu! You okay.” No answer, Otoshi starts to get worried and cautiously he swings the door open to see Ryu lying on the cracked tiled floor dazed and confused, the bathtub crack wide open and tipped over, water running into the hallway. Otoshi picks up Ryu and helps him back into the living area and lies him on the floor, he goes and puts a ice pack on his head and watches him “god, your full of bad luck Ryu” he says chuckling, “its not bad luck, its this house” he says incoherently Otoshi smiles back and goes back to cleaning, “we gonna…. gonna…win titles?” Ryu blurts out. Otoshi glances at him “trinity titles?” Ryu thinks for a while “yeah” Otoshi smirks “hell yeah were the sushi crew aren’t we? Were going to dominate!” he says going back to cleaning. Ryu lays his head back down trying to rest as Otoshi tries his hardest to clean up the house
** Now the sushi crew are back in Japan, wrestling for the up start company PURE, they’re back in their old house which is now a dump and is falling apart and everything ryu touches breaks. ah great to be home**
