Meeting Binsei Kinaka

11 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Meeting Binsei Kinaka Written: 11-01-2003 The sun is splitting the clouds today and blaring through the windows of high-rise office buildings and virtually burning the ground beneath people’s feet. It’s just a regular day in the home of Puroresu Ultimate Ring Entertainment – Japan. Walking the busy streets of Tokyo are two new arrivals to the country but not here on their first visit are Jason Marah and his religious guide and fighting trainer, Patrick Craig. Craig is sporting a black tracksuit bottom and a loose, plain white t-shirt along with a white cap on his head while Marah has a pair of sunglasses on, a blue bucket hat protecting his bare skin cranium, long blue shorts and a sleeveless green t-shirt, the sweat on his biceps give them a glistening effect in the sunlight. Marah, standing at 6’6, seems like a giant to the natives of Japan, which are not renowned for producing tall people whatsoever. Both Craig and Marah are constantly looking down their paths above the heads of those in front of them – almost ignoring everyone else using the walkway.
Patrick Craig: Tell me, what are the two most important things in life? Jason speaks without a moments thought. Jason Marah: First, it’s honour. A man is as good as his honour, no better, no worse. If you don’t have honour, then what do you have? Absolutely fuck all. In a business like wrestling, honour is important for the simple fact that if you are dishonourable to your opponent you will most likely reveal your weaknesses. If you attack someone from their blindside, you reveal your cowardice. If you use a weapon in a straight up, one on one, normal wrestling match you reveal your lack of strength, your lack of composure, your lack of skill and most importantly, you reveal the fear you have for your opponent.
Patrick nods approvingly. Patrick Craig: And the other? Jason Marah: The other is pride. If you don’t have pride in yourself and what you do, then you are walking a path to self-destruction.
Patrick Craig: And which is the most important? Jason Marah: No competition. Of course it’s honour. Without honour, how can you be proud of yourself?
Craig rubs his hands together and smiles. Patrick Craig: You’re learning well, Mr. Marah. You know all there is to know about Grandism and Bushido code of honour. Now, the only thing you have to do is perfect your Bujutsu and your samurai techniques.
Jason Marah: Ah, sure I’m already better than most. Patrick Craig: Listen here, you big freak. Craig stops walking and halts Marah by roughly placing his hand on Marah’s chest. Patrick Craig: You’re better than most but you should be thriving to be better than all. If you say anything that would insinuate that you have done all the work you need to do, I will rip your heart out with my bare hands. If you think I am bluffing, you are sadly mistaken Jason, very fucking mistaken.
Jason wipes his tongue over his teeth and looks at Craig, dead straight in the eyes. They both are silent as they look at each other. Jason shrugs.
Jason Marah: Sorry. The teacher and student restart walking. The tension is obvious; the disappointment isn’t as obvious in Craig’s face as it is in Marah’s. Disappointed with himself. Jason swiped his hat off and rubs his baldhead vigorously.
Jason Marah: Bloody hell man, I think I made the wrong choice coming here. It’s not like I enjoyed myself last time around in PURE. It drove me literally nuts last time about.
Patrick Craig: It’s just a little bad weather before you get to the good times. Remember, you’re a development wrestler in PURE…if you impress you go to PCW. PCW! That’s where you want to be isn’t it?
Jason Marah: Of course, it’s my dream. Patrick Craig: Then stop bitching and go through the course. You’ll get there…eventually. Anyway, it’s not the same PURE as before. New owner, improved front office. Jamar’s got one hundred percent control…trust me, it’ll be better than before.
Jason Marah: Aye, suppose your right. Jason takes of his cap and wipes the sweat off his head then replaces the hat. Jason Marah: Jesus man, I need a feckin’ drink. Craig takes a plastic water bottle out of his pocket and offers it to Marah. Jason looks at the bottle and then at Craig…then lets out a bellow of laughter.
Jason Marah: Water? Fuck no. I need a proper drink. Patrick Craig: Ya need to take care of yourself if you want out of PURE and into PCW, Jason.
Jason Marah: Four or five pints won’t hurt me. I know where the hotel is, I’ll catch up with ya in the morn’.
Patrick drops his head and smiles as he walks on down the path. Jason looks up to his left at a building. Thinking it at least looks like a pub from the outside he decides to go in. The pub is quiet, with soft music playing throughout. A large bar stretches the left wall. Only one of the tables in the main floor of the bar is occupied, and only a few more are on the elevated area. A wooden banister surrounds the elevated area; three steps lead up to the area. Jason passes the occupied table on the bottom floor on his way to a barstool. 5 men sit around the table; all of Asian extraction and the same number in some class of formal wear. One man, a broad shouldered, short black haired man in a grey shirt and black trousers seems to be the “centre” of the crowd. Each man “eyes” Marah as he goes past them before returning to their round of drinks.
Jason takes off his hat and puts it in his shorts cargo pocket as the bartender, tall and skinny man, also of Asian origin and the only clothes visible on him are a white shirt and black tie, approaches to take Marah’s order.
Jason Marah: Lager, please. The bartender gives Jason an inquisitive look. Jason curses to himself. He scans the back of the bar and the tap handles for some kind of lager logo but comes up with zip. When all looks dreary for the alcohol deprived Irishman, a man puts his hand on Marah’s shoulder and comes around the side of him. Jason looks at him and it’s one of the men from the table of five on the same floor. He’s got slicked back black hair, down to his shoulders and is wearing a black suit with black shirt. He smiles at Marah and in perfect English speaks.
Man: They don’t sell lager here, don’t know why. Let me order you a drink. Jason nods slightly and says “right so”. The man orders the drink. It promptly arrives in the form of a solid black liquid in a short glass. Jason circles it with his eyes and then knocks it back in one go. He quinces – closing his eyes firmly and basically trying to shut his face. He lets out a gasp and opens up his eyes, which are full of water.
Jason Marah: Beat the lord that’s bitter! Fucking hell, but nice though. The man chuckles. Jason extends his hand and introduces himself. Man: I thought it was you, PURE’s only Irishman, haha. I’m Binsei Kinaki. I’d say ya wouldn’t mind another one of those drinks, right?
Jason smirks as Binsei orders another drink. Jason eyes trail down to Binsei’s left hand – in particular his pinky finger. The tip of it seems to be have chopped off. Jason, curious as usual, asks Binsei about this. As Binsei hands Jason the drink he answers.
Binsei Kinaki: Ah, what happened there is nothing to exciting. I accidentally chopped it off while cutting carrots. You know how it goes.
Binsei chuckles and Jason shares the laugh…then recoils once again after swallowing the black alcohol.
Jason Marah: Whoo! That’s some good shit right there, Binsei. What’s it called? Binsei Kinaki: It’s known as Jerau. Only available in this here fine establishment. Anyway, come over and join in with my friends. I’m sure they’d like to meet you.
Jason Marah: Surely will. Jason picks up his third glass of “Jerau” and follows Binsei over to the table. As he sits down on a spare seat he notices that the tip of one of all men’s pinky fingers have taken the chop at sometime or another. He swiftly wipes any curiousity out of his head as he knocks back his newfound favourite drink.
