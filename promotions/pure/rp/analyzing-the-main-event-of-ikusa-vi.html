Analyzing The Main Event Of Ikusa VI

27 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Analyzing The Main Event Of Ikusa VI The Asian Title. It's something that you'd expect an Asian to hold. But Jordan X Shabazz is not an Asian. In fact, neither is Ali Khadafi for that matter. And the two men that are in the Main Event for the upcoming Ikusa, that are battling for the Asian Title, in the Main Event, Ali Khadafi, and Jordan X Shabazz, loathe each other. But, there is a difference between Jordan X Shabazz and Ali Khadafi. Jordan X Shabazz respects Japan. He respects their tradition, he respects their rules. Ali Khadafi on the other hand, doesn't give a damn about Japan, PURE, or the Japanese People. Ali Khadafi walks in the arena, threatening to attack fans, he kidnaps people. He disrespects the tradition of Puroresu by fighting, not wrestling. In fact, it has been said that Ali Khadafi cannot execute anything more than the fundamental wrestling moves. It's not so much that Jordan and Jesse Kintei (The person Ali Khadafi kidnapped) were good friends... it's more that Jesse came to Japan to see Jordan in action. He wanted to see a Civil Rights Activist, get into the ring, and show some true respect and professionalism. But Ali stopped that. The chances of Jordan and Jesse becoming friends ever again, are slim to none. But that doesn't meant that Jordan X Shabazz is going to stop looking for his fellow Civil Rights Activist. Cianan on the other hand, is Asian. He's a dangerous wrestler, who is currently embroiled in a feud with PURE's CEO, Alexander Miyagawa. Jordan X Shabazz and Ali Khadafi are liable to start brawling from the start of the match, to the end of it, where does Cianan place himself in all of this? Jordan X Shabazz wants the Asian Title, Ali Khadafi wants to hurt people, Cianan wants to show Miyagawa up, and Miyagawa wants to screw Cianan (Not like that, you SICK FREAKS!). Everybody in this match has their own agenda... but who will prevail? We'll have to wait and see.... won't we...
