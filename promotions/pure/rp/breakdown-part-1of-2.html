Breakdown Part 1of 2 A simple job thats what it was supposed to be. Just a quick pickup and nothing more he said. Funny how things are made less then they actually are when speaking to those reluctant to do it. But of course deception is one thing that is To be expected in the business. The camera comes into a beautiful oak-walled room with a nice Persian rug place in the center. Around the room are different pictures, which from the similarities look to be a passage of generation with each one. In the middle of the room is marble desk covered only with a portfolio and a fountain pen. Inside the chair is a middle aged Asian man. He is wearing a slick black suit and has a huge grin on his face.
Its nice to have you back. He says as he stares across the room at the person that we all know as Cege. Unlike his usual appearance his hair has been cut short and he is wearing a white silk shirt with black pants and black shoes. He seems to be uneasy about what the man is going to say.
Well since you just came back Im going to give you an easy job a simple job for you to do. All you have to do is go over to Mais flower shop and pick up some money for me. They should know that is time for their payments so there should be no issues. The middle-aged man says as he points to the two men coming through the door. And if you have any trouble these guys will help you.
Cege stares at the two men and realizes that these guys are prepared more for a gangfight than a pickup. They are carrying guns in every single pocket that they have visible and who know where else that could be storing a gun. Cege looks at it all with an uneasy look as the old man waits for Cege to give his response to the offer. Cege only nods and begins to walk towards the door. He doesnt understand why he has chosen to waste his time doing this because he could spend much more time preparing for his match. But something has told him to just give this a try. Cege heads outside to find a blue corvette waiting for him. He goes hops into the car and begins to drive down the road. After a while he decides to turn on the radio. An upbeat 70s style song blared across radio with a guitar line that similar to those you would see in blackploitation films. After a few seconds of this the guitar dies down to slip the lead vocal into the mix. Im working for the sake of it and Im on all the money that they gotta give. I go out getting laid but I play it safe in a safe place and I know that I know that I wanna live. I said to you (oooooo) Sometimes I feel like I can (Do something) And now it feels like I am (All set for a good thing) Come clean unused be free, just good Been caught, been bruised, been cleaned been used Come clean unused be free, just good Been caught, been bruised, been cleaned been used The music continues back into the guitar and Cege is rocking his head to the music. He feel a bit relaxed and like the feeling of having a nice car to ride instead of his old broken down car. A few minutes pass and he finally makes it to the florist. The two man pulled up behind him in a black sedan. As he steps out the car the two man assumes a defensive position behind Cege. Cege is taken aback by this action and is wondering if something more is involved. As he steps into the flower shop a little girl comes up to him with a most frail looking smile. She is wearing a flowery dress that seems to be faced by the repeating washing.
My mother will be help you shortly, please wait a moment and she will be here soon. She says
Cege gives a candy bar to the young girl and gives her a thank you. The young girl smiles as go away to the back. After a moment a very beautiful lady comes to the front. She is wearing a pair of blue jeans and a white button shirt. Cege cannot help but blush at the women as he tries to talk. The young lady notices and blushes a shade of pink as she go to the front of the desk
I have come to pick up the money from you. Its nothing personal its just business Im sorry sir but I have nothing to give to you. My little girl is sick and I had to take her to the doctor and get her medicine. I have absolutely nothing to give. Please let me have more time and Ill promise to give the money. Mai cries
Ceges heart is crushed by the sad plight of the women and her daughter the only thing that he can say is Come on guys let just forget this one.
But his guards were not at all willing to just forget. They begin to demolish the store knocking down flowerpots and picture. Mai runs up to one of the man and try to stop and with one swift swing he takes his hand and smacks her to the ground. Cege looks and becomes enraged he charges toward the man and knock him down with a hard smash across the face. After he does he turns to the gun of the other.
If you ain't with us. The gunman says as he pulls the trigger blowing Cege away. Cege falls backwards and collapses to the ground. The last image that he sees is the little girl staring at all the mayhem with a jaded face.
Ring ring ring The loud ringing of the gun blast is still fresh in Ceges ears. He quickly takes off the helmet and tries to orient himself back to reality
It was just a game. He tells himself as tears run down his eyes. The scene returns inside Ceges apartment for a moment and then pans quickly over to his fathers mansion. The virtual reality machine did not do what his father planned for it to do. It was a simple idea, send him a gift and see if his heart has become cold. But by this reaction he realized that it was a failed experiment. The father sits stares at the surveillance camera in his office
His father realizes that it was a failure and turns off the screen. Meanwhile Cege cannot get the image out of his head or those two poor souls. Even though he know that all of there ideas and dreams were digital. But still he couldnt play the game. He could not push himself towards the goal of advancing if it meant causing the pain of those two virtual humans. A runoff sheet comes out of the side of the machine and it says these words
Ambition and Heartlessness go Hand and Hand Cege bolts out of his hotel room and could only think how much things that could be simple in one person's eyes can be seen differently in others. He gets in his car and bolts to the arena.
