Changes.

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Changes. You wonder who I am. Well heh You know who I am. My presence was shown. My presence was felt. And in the end the cards were dealt. It was in MY FAVOR! I won the hand when the competition had two aces; I stood high and clear above hundreds different races. My face is hidden, but my soul soars, DJ Nygma is now each and every one of yours. Im an idol and here I am on this wondrous sunny day, your watching the sun the birds chirping and then theres a sudden delay. BOOM! A bomb, where it at, did the world just come to an end, no it didnt there just stands a man, NYGMA MAN OF ALL MEN! Cut! And we are finished. Man Nygma thats some good stuff. Club Tokyo Pop is going to be hopping the next few nights after seeing this. What better could we ask for, a hip-hop and pop DJ mixed with a fighter marvel. Thanks man, but Im going to have to be backing up my confidence come next week. PURE is my new home, and Im taking it by storm. Im gonna be working by ass off to being just as good a fighter as a DJ. Well that, my man will be one hard task, take care. I walked home that day and my mind wandered to the point where I had no control of it. I thought thousands of Japanese traditionalists and thousands of screaming girls coming together in one stadium to see men fight for honor, respect, and money. Who would have ever though a place like PURE would exist? I feel like an outcast. How am I, just one man going to affect others? Thats when I looked up on a Billboard and saw an album cover that said  Zen Wen Brothers Immortals, mixed by DJ Nygma. I thought about it for a while and realized thats mostly my work. People bought that CD to listen to how I changed it up and added my own rhymes, but yet no one knows what I look like and all I get is a little mixed by DJ Nygma in the lower right corner. WHY WAS I MADE TO BE UNIMPORTANT? I just wished that one second that Id look up and see my photo enlarged on that billboard with my name in huge letters and on fire. Then a bunch of television shows wouldnt say Zen Wen Brothers number one on the billboards, but theyd say NYGMA number one on the billboards. Sure I make the money, sure I star a club, but until I see my face on a billboard instead of just my name will I really be pleased. As I came closer toward home a few more things entered my mind, like the game. When you mention the game, people suddenly back off and not know what youre talking about, but its quite simple. In business, law, and in anything you play a game to get what you want. How will I do in this game, and whom do I need to please in order to rule the game. When I think pure, I think Jamar, the man who controls the show and makes the money. Sure others may fight and give it there all, but its up to that one man who he wants to see doing well and doing bad. Hell make you and break you in a heartbeat, but I will not be broken. As I stepped up those steps to my apartment in the sky and I turned the key ninety degrees, I thought of changes. The change from music to fighting is something that might seem drastic. In my case I looked for change, but now what if change looks for me. Will I be ready for what lies ahead? Will I fear the unknown? Will I live off others glory? Or will I be my own glory? Its up to me to choose my path, and its up to God to make the path. Life is life, and a fight is a fight, but when I clinch my fists be ready to die tonight. The scene fades as Nygma enters his house and blares his radio to the sweet beat and rhymes to no one other then himself. The time will come. The time will find you. But, if you try to find it. Time will haunt you. WORD!
