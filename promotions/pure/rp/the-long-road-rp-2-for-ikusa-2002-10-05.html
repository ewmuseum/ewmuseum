The long road... (RP #2 for Ikusa)

The long road... (RP #2 for Ikusa) Written: 10/5/02 The dawn of a new day looms on the horizon, as my mode of transportation streaks down the parkway. I do my best to keep my mind focused on the mission, but when you have no one to converse with and cannot sing along with the songs that radiate from the ancient music system. The air blows through my pigtails and I begin to ponder questions that I have should have asked myself before I was sent here. The questions echo in my mind like a scream in a cavern. How do I know I am doing the correct thing by breaking the laws of all knowledge and changing time? I could save the future from the terror I know of, but I could be condemning it to a future, which is far worse. It is kind of late to be having these doubts and I do my best to block them out. I mean could the future possibly be worse than the one I already viewed? No. Any future is better than that one.
I see a 55 miles per hour, speed limit sign peeks out from behind a tall, brown evergreen, out of the corner of my eye. The more I view of the scenery, the more I cannot believe what the world is going to become. My time has almost no plant life or wildlife. Vast deserts cover the land. The few oasises turned into large cities or penal colonies. To see the smallest plant grow is like catching site of a full lunar eclipse, rare and far between. The cities are over crowded and resources are becoming few. The government is too into keeping the peace than helping the situation. I don't blame them really. The human race has never seen such a great amount of crime in all its years. Large gangs rule over all cities that are without government protection. The government has become more like a business than a governing body and I am one of their products.
The ancient mode of transportation, known as a 'Prowler,' makes an awkward sound and creeps to stop. I throw my hands up in disbelief and punch the steering wheel out of frustration. I step on to the driver's seat and hop over the closed door. A thought pops into my head and I lean back into the car to get a view of the dashboard. The contraption that monitors how much fossil fuel I have left indicates that the vehicle has run out of the needed fuel.
'Figures,' I mumble to myself. I retrieve my black trench coat and fold it over my shoulder. After delivering a swift kick to the automobile's door, I continue my journey down the parkway. The sun has just risen above the horizon and the pavement is beginning to grow warmer beneath my feet. As I walk, my mind begins to wonder once more. I begin to think about my mother. Well, at least the woman who gave birth to me. For I was not created out of the love between a man and a woman, but out of the scientific study of numerous scientists. Specifically, one doctor in particular, Dr. Caine. He is possibly the greatest genetic physicist to ever grace the earth. He spent decades working for the United States government in their research sector. After numerous years of study and testing, the dear doctor formulated a DNA code, which consisted of some of the greatest leaders and soldiers in United States history. His first test subject was Adam or as he like to call him, the prodigal son. Adam's embryo was created in a laboratory and was placed in a host mother. After his birth, Adam was taken studied. He had superhuman abilities in strength, speed, and intelligence. He became the first of their super soldiers. 100 more embryos were developed and 99 went according to the plan. The one error, as they put it, developed differently than all the others. The 99 perfect subjects were all male and that one was not. The embryo administered the 'Y' chromosome out of human error and grew into a bouncing baby girl. How do I know? I am the error.
A large, red automobile passes by. I only catch a quick glimpse, but due to my super senses, I can easily decipher everything. It was a middle-aged woman singing along happily with her two children. They seemed so happy. They were singing as a family without a care in the world. Mother, the word rings in out in my skull. My thoughts travel back to my time and my childhood. The five years, I spent with my mother, were the greatest years of my life. Be it only childhood, but my life has only spiraled down after that fifth year. On my 5th birthday, my makers came calling. It was time for the training to begin. However, I did not wish to go and my mother did not wish to part with me either. My innocent eyes watched every moment of that day and I can replay it in my mind as if it just happened. The man with the patch over his eye, I would later come to know as 'Harrison,' came to collect me. My mother refused each and every time he came calling. Then, one day he arrived, but he was not alone. I remember dropping my teddy bear and rushing into the kitchen, after hearing the door being broken down. I watched in horror, as my mother struggled to stop the men and was beaten to a bloody pulp. With ease, a man grabbed me and carried me from the building. Tears and blood streaking down my mother's face, as she was restrained. I burned those images into my mind. I would never forget what these men did to her and how they took me from my home. I swore.
I walk for miles on end and yet, I am still not have broken a sweat. Be it my genes or my training, this never-ending walk seems like a trip to the food replicator for me. My training, I believe is the answer. After the men forcibly ripped me from my home, I was taken to an underground, security base just outside the city limits of New Liberty. The next major portion of my life I spent training. We ran for what seemed like days in the desert. We lifted weights every morning before the run and every night before lights out. We were piles of clay being molded into works of art. With each day, we grew in strength and speed. Once we were up to their required specifications, we spent all day and night in virtual reality simulators. We were taught how to kill in every form. Be it hand to hand combat, being a sniper, or mass destruction, there was no way of killing that we did not have genius knowledge of. We became living, breathing, and thinking weapons. Our job was to achieve our goal by any means necessary and we were molded to perfection. I followed orders and learned everything that they would teach me. All the while, keeping the images of my mother in my mind. I would turn their dreams of peace into a never-ending nightmare of death.
This mission is important to me. If I succeed, I will save many from the horror of the third world war that the earth would never recover from. My target must die before she gets the chance to cause it. I have only failed one mission in my life and it was well worth failing. Due to the fact that I was the only girl, I was trained in specifically in espionage. They believed the targets would never suspect a cute, innocent, blonde-haired girl to be the one who would destroy them. I completed my first mission at the age of 13. With ease, I went through all of my following missions like a laser beam through an ice cube. I took more lives than the greatest plague and I ruined more lives than the media did. However, little did I know, the mission I would fail would be my last for the U.S. government. I was assigned to infiltrate a feminist gang and assassinate their leader. I did my job perfectly and it all went with out a hitch. I had conned my way up to a top-ranking member of the faction. Some would say that I was second in command, only just a step below my target. I gained her trust and learned everything about her. I knew where she slept, where she ate, and where she relaxed. I could predict her every move as if I was in control of her body. I set the date and planned my attack out perfectly. I would wait until just after she put her daughter to bed and I would strike just as she hit the stairs. All did not go as planned. I watched closely through her child's bedroom window, as she tucked the little girl into bed and read her a bed time story. As I monitored their actions, an old thought crept into my head. I did not want to break this family up and there was no way anyone was going to make me. I discarded my communicator and fled from the area as fast as my legs could carry me. I am no fool. There is only one punishment for not completing a mission, death.
I walked the streets of Liberty City like I am this parkway, alone and with no where to call home. I survived by doing jobs here and there for the highest bidder. Then, my past caught up with me. I aligned myself with an anti-government faction, after they assisted me in escaping the men, I used to call my brothers. Spending so much of your life in a top secret, training complex, you do not learn how the real world works. Just like the government before them, the rebels tried to exploit my abilities. Having me destroy pointless government property in protest against the supposed horrible treatment of the people. Maybe it was all those years of working for the government, but I could not follow the ways of the rebels and tried to leave. However, they showed me the truth and how the government was trying to fill the population with soldiers like me, in hopes of world domination.
I would not be walking my cute, little *ss down this road if it were not for a good reason. The rebels were treated me like a person and not another soldier in the barracks. They earned my respect and my backing. When they first told me of this mission, I was a little cautious. No one had ever attempted time travel before, due to the major consequences of every action in the past. I spoke with them long and hard about their plans and they were never so sure of anything in their lives. The believed in their hearts that if my target did not cause the most destructive war ever recorded, my time would not be so bleak. I knew this wasn't a game to them. I could see it in their eyes.
The sun is slowly setting to my left, as I march towards my desired destination. A few more miles come and go. The sun is just moments from hiding behind the earth. A blue and white automobile streaks by me and comes to a screeching halt. White lights radiate from the back of the vehicle, labeled 'Ford,' as it changes direction and heads back towards me. I cease walking and it pulls up along side me. The window drops into the door and a rough looking male smiles at me.
'Hey there, pretty lady,' he says in a loud voice. You have to be kidding me and what does the name John Deer mean on his hat anyhow? I am on the verge of telling him that I will rip his testicles off and shove them down his throat, when a light bulb goes off in my head.
'Like hi there,' I exclaim in my best airhead voice. I lean against his vehicle and let my breasts stick out to gain his attention. As expected, his eyes instantly focus in on them. Men are so simple minded.
'You need a ride' he asks. 'Sure do, baby,' I coo back. 'Climb on in,' he exclaims, as he opens the passenger's side door for me. I climb in and shut the door behind me. He glances over and gives me another smile. I nearly vomit as I see his repulsive teeth up close.
'Where are we off to, doll,' he quizzes. 'Like we are so not totally going anywhere,' I respond, as I slowly pull my weapon to his head. 'I am completing my mission. You, my disgusting friend, are hitting the road to burn off some of those calories. You are a pig.'
'Just please don't kill me, lady,' he whimpers, as he exits the vehicle with his hands raised.
I close the driver's side door and take off down the road in my new Ford. First a newspaper, then a Prowler, and now a Ford, this time is great. Everything is free. I should have just gotten my clothes for free too. Now, no girl would want to miss that sale.
Fade to Black.
