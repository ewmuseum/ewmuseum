Ressurrection(Part 3)

10 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ressurrection(Part 3) ** The scene opens up with Vito Wynn and his so called lawyer Randy who both don't really get along too well appear in a court room that is very crowded. Many different people are inside talking to other people. ** .:: Vito Wynn and Randy walk over to the front desk where there is an overweight police officer drinking a bottle of water while sitting down in a chair that looks uncomfortable because it is wooden. ::. 'Hey, I need to go to court room six B. Can you inform me where I have to go to get there?' asks Vito Wynn. 'To get to room six B you have to take either the elevator or the stairs to the sixth floor and look on the door, there will be tags on them telling you what room it is, okay.' answers the police officer. 'Got it!' replies Randy. .:: Vito Wynn and Randy immediately walk over to the elevator where they both click on the button. ::. 'So, now's the time.' comments Randy. 'Time for what, how the fuck are you a lawyer, you have to be a lot more descriptive you know.' Vito Wynn informs Randy. 'Yes, I know that. But, I only have to be very descriptive to what I say in the court room. Not when having a one on one conversation with my attorney or anyone else for that matter. You should know that yourself. Well, at least I thought you did.' says Randy. .:: All of the sudden the door to the elevator opens and about twenty to thirty people come barging out of that little space in the elevator. Vito Wynn and Randy or both bumped into and shoved by the people rushing out of the elevator. The stampede of people then spread out into the entrance. ::. 'Watch where you're fucking going! This isn't your court house!' Vito Wynn shouts at all the people rushing out. 'Something that should be watched more is your filthy mouth. This is a court house, if you use any curse or any word that can offend the judge or anyone else in the courtroom for that matter you will lose and go to jail while I get paid but not as much as I would if I make you get out of that court room not guilty.' Says Randy as they get into the elevator. .:: Vito Wynn then presses the button six and the doors close as the elevator starts going up. ::. 'Look, I am looking forward to getting out of that court room not guilty and making you get paid a little more but just because I want it happening doesn't mean that it's going to actually happen. We aren't in a fairy tale Randy, this is a real life situation. Just because we wish for something doesn't mean it'll come true in an instant or later on 'for that matter'.' explains Vito Wynn as he laughs in a low key-tone voice. 'What's so funny, do I have something on my suit, my face, my shoes, or anything else?' asks Randy. 'No, none of those, damn you are slow. I was making fun of your quote that you seem to use for every sentence you say.' answers Vito Wynn. 'What quote do I keep on saying?' asks Randy. 'You don't know, it's the one where you say, 'for that matter'. Hahahaha!' explains Vito Wynn. That's not very funny you know, actually it wasn't funny at all.' Randy says in a nervous way. Don't take it personal man.' says Vito Wynn. .:: The elevator stops as the doors abruptly open as Randy and Vito Wynn walk out of the elevator. ::. Are you ready, Vito?' asks Randy. Yeah, I'm ready when you are so if you are ready so am I.' answers Vito Wynn. .:: Vito Wynn and Randy then walk forward as the scene fades to black. ::.
