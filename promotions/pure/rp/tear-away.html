Tear away

25 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tear away (Cege is actually dressed up at a banquet. He is wearing a dark purple Armani suit with his hair tied back in a ponytail. Underneath the suit He is wearing a brace on his knees from a knee injury he received during a match. Today is his mothers birthday and his father took out all stops to make sure that he would come. This didnt take much coaxing because Cege admires his mother very much. If it wasnt for that he wouldn't be caught dead around this place. The place is filled with many associates of his fathers business. From the look of the men you would think they all just came in from the stock market or some other office job but they are all involved in much shadier dealings. From the cross that he is receiving from them he know they dont admire the fact that he is here. The jazz band begin to play a very energetic rendition of Buddy Richs Groovin hard as the guest of honor comes down the stairs arm and arm with Ceges father. She is wearing a nice emerald dress with a stunning jade necklace. She looks much younger then she actually is and this is without any makeup or plastic surgery. ) Beauty always shines through naturally; doing anything else will just expose the ugliness that you think you are hiding. That is one thing that Cege always remembers his mom saying and in her case it seems to be true. As she makes it down she sees her son she comes down and gives him a very large hug. His father looks on disgusted by the whole situation as onlookers stare at his mothers acceptance of her son. Ceges mother: I have missed you so much sons. Cege: So have I, mother Ceges father: Lets go Yuriko, we should get to our seats now there is much time to go talk about trivial matters. Cege (sarcastically): Yes why should she waste time talking to her son. Ceges father (Angered): You are no son of mine! Ceges mother (Stepping between the two): Lets just go and sit down. (They all go towards the seats set aside for all of them. Around them are people that Cege recognizes from when he was younger. Some are neighbors who were very kind to him while the majority of the people are his fathers associates. The food is served and everyone is eating to their fill of roast duck, orange chicken Cha Siu rack of lamb and a great deal of other good food. Cege has not eaten this well since he left and it is noticed by all that are looking. One man decides to make note of his eating and looks at him with disgust.) Associate: Well it seems as if someone hasnt been able to appreciate the finer things in life has he? (Cege just looks up with a fake smile and continue to eat his food) Ceges Mother: So how is the work going, son. Ceges Father (speaking loudly): Well if you call being beaten by a person who thinks he a Peanut Butter and Jelly sandwich a job then I guess he is doing well. (Everybody around burst out into laughter at his father comments while Cege is brooding silently while eating his food. Ceges father looks at his son and realizing that he is getting under his skin. His mother begins to get nervous but remains silent. But of course his father is far from done. He stands up and gets the attention of the crowd.) Ceges Father (taps glass): Everyone listen up and Ill tell you something about my disgrace of a son. Now he not only is he a disappointment of a wrestler in America but he is also horrible fighter in our homeland. (He walks over to Cege). But of course he was never much of a fighter when he was younger so what can we expect and (pointing at his brace) and from the look at this mark of disgrace he cannot take pain either. Its sad really you would think that someone in his business would try to be much stronger than he is. But of course that takes common sense to know which this boy lacks. We all know that he cant have any if he thought leaving this family was a good idea. But of course he has to have his sense of honor and respect. But of course from the look of his face it doesnt look like honor is putting food on the table. (By now the whole crowd is roaring with laughter at Cege. His face has turned pale with embarrassment and anger towards his father. All he can do is stare coldly at his father as he continue to attack his character .He turns to his mother who is also looking quite embarrassed which fills him up with more rage. Ceges father pulls out a handkerchief from his pocket) Ceges Father (Condescendingly): I know that you have been gone for a long time but you should mind your manners (throws it at Ceges face) clean yourself up. Cege (enraged): You heartless bitch. (He tosses it back to his father's face.) (The crowd lets in a simultaneous gasp of shock as Cege fathers once smiling face is turns red with anger. He smacks Cege across the face and then spits on him.) Ceges Father: Dont you ever try to humiliate me. You are inferior to me and I can strike you down whenever I want to. I will always be your superior and you will have to just realize that. I am a success and you are a failure. You cannot succeed without my backing and once you realize that you will be much smarter. Your honor is worthless and has gotten you nowhere. You want results all you have to do is look at me. I have created an empire and you dont even have a house. You are less than human and will never make to my level. So dont think of trying to be again. (Ceges anger is at a level that it has never been before. He has never been humiliated to this level before in his life and what makes it worse is that its his father who is humiliating him. He pulls the band from his red hair and it comes back down to his shoulder. He wipes the spit off his face with his hand and clenches into a fist, he then gets up and hits his father with a hard punch across the jaw. He falls to the floor grabbing his face in pain. He then takes the spit in his hand and wipes it across his fathers suit. Some of his fathers associates begin to pull out their guns but put them away when Ceges mother tells them to. As Cege walks to the exit he tears off his jacket and shirt and tosses it to the ground. When he makes to the door he turns back to all the onlookers. Cege: You can all keep your finer things. All I need is my pride and no is going to take that from me. Not even you father. I give 100 percent out there in that ring win or lose. And I fight those who are equal in strength and ability. Unlike all of you who live on those who are weaker. When things come my way I stand alone and fight without an entourage. So before any of you leeches think of me as a disgrace you need to turn up your damn noses at yourselves. (Turns to his mother) Mother I wish you the happiest of times and many more years. You are the only rose that I see in this field of thorns. I dont know how you do it but stay the way you are and dont let these jackals change you. Don't worry about me Im fine and will always be. Now I leave you all you your time and bringing down your next target but just know that I got ammo to add to the hunt so be careful who you try to mess with. (After that he exits the banquet hall, it is pouring outside. A taxi pulls up and stops to give him a ride, yet he chooses to walk home. He got through this ordeal without lowering himself to anyone. He show his father that he is not just someone that went on his own and died off, but is very much alive and will continue to get stronger.)
