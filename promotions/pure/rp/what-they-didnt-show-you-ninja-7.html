What they didn't show you: Ninja #7

March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What they didn't show you: Ninja #7 Written: 'My Final Destiny' A ninja sits down in the darkness of the confessional booth. The lights are turned off. He doesn't have his mask on, all we see is a slight silhouette, a glimmer of light on his shoulders from an indeterminate source. He slumps back in the chair, his arms hanging limp down at his inner thighs.
#7: I'm Ninja #7. Yeah, big deal. What's the point of being a high rank in the Clan when nobody knows who you are? When you can't go out into public and have a soda and a meal; when you can't get close to anyone outside the Clan? Technically, I'm not even supposed to be doing this. Everything for preservation of the secrets of the Clan. I was born into it, and I'm supposed to keep up the air of mystery.
The ninja releases a sigh. #7: Think about it. I'm well into adulthood, and everything I've done, everything I am... it's because I am cemented in the Clan. Can I leave? Sure, but at this point I've probably not the social ability to function in the world outside the Clan. It's so isolated, so insulated. We've only dealt with ourselves. If we do go into the outside world, it's for assignments the nature of which I cannot reveal.
His voice goes to being even more agitated than just the slightly depressed tone. #7: You couldn't possibly understand the boredom I go through on a daily basis. I can't even really be friends with the other ninja, I certainly can't be friends with outsiders. Isolation. That's my fate, given to me from birth for the sins of my father and his fathers before him.
The ninja leans forward, though the darkness still obscures his face. #7: My goals? I don't have any. I can't have any. The Clan is my life, whether I like it or not. My Final Destiny? I'll live with the Clan, I'll die in the Clan. Possibly... die by the Clan. Whatever. It's not that big a deal. I'm used to things not going to the way I prefer.
Leaning back into the chair, the ninja slumps once more. #7: I suppose you were looking for something a little more inspiring. A feel good story, talking about all the things I wanted to accomplish in life. Well, sorry to disappoint you. It's a depressing story. It's a depressing life. You can't sugar-coat everything and still consider yourselves mature adults. Sometimes things are just bad. I know that. Am I happy about it? No. Am I going to have to live with it? Yes.
The ninja stands up and walks away. #7: My past, present, and future are all of the same, singular concept -- nothing....
