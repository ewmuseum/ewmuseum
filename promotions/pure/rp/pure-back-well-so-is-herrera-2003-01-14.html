PURE? Back? Well, so is Herrera.

14 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PURE? Back? Well, so is Herrera. Written: January 14, 2003 PURE? Back? Well, so is Herrera. Did you really think you would never see me again? Well, actually, you never really even did see me in the first place. I came to PURE, and I didn’t even get my chance to compete. But this time, I get a second chance. Not to shine in PURE. That’s not a goal at all of mine. My goal is to go out and have fun.
I’m what you would call a risk taker, the guy who has all the fun in the ring, of course. Who wouldn’t want to be a risk taker? Perhaps they are the guys that go out for the gold. The ones that won’t even think about the fun they could be having. Forget the gold.
Even though I’m going to be participating in a mini-tournament for the JR. Heavyweight Title doesn’t mean I’m after the gold. It’s not a decision I made. It wasn’t my choice. But I’m not going to pass this match up. I’ll be in the ring with four other people. I’ll be working harder than I usually do. I’ll be taking more risks than I probably ever have before. It’s going to be great.
I already had the rush of life outside of the ring. Now I’m back to the rush inside of the ring.
“Are you really going to do this?” Daniel asked in curiosity. He looked at me stupidly as he said it, too. He thought I was nuts.
People gathered all around, for I was about to jump from one huge office building to another on a dirt bike.
I snapped my helmet on and headed over to my dirt bike. Daniel followed. “You could break a bone,” said Daniel. “They heal,” I replied, not scared at all, “a broken bone isn’t even a big deal.” “You could die!” “I could,” I said, “and I’ll be glad if it happened while I’m doing this, I wouldn’t want to die any other way.”
Daniel’s mouth hung wide open. He thought I was nothing but a lunatic. I bet you’re wondering who Daniel is anyway. Well, he’s my cousin from Colombia. He had just come here to visit me and at the moment has been staying with me. Of course I’ve still been doing my crazy stunts and all, but I guess this one topped them all.
I climbed onto the dirt bike and started it up. VROOM! I looked over at the ramp, further on the jump, and then the other side. I could make it. I raised my hand up in the air and received applause from everyone crowded around me. I accelerated on the bike and headed for the ramp. I hit the ramp and accelerated off of it.
I was in the air for what seemed to be a long time to me. I could feel the rush and the sensation of being within the range of danger. The crowd grew silent. I then landed on the ramp of the other side. The crowd clapped and cheered quite loudly. I then came to a halt and looked over to the other side raising my helmet over my head. As I looked over there, I could feel the relief on Daniel’s face.
I loved it all. Life can be so great when you want it to be. This is what I wanted for myself. I wanted to be the man that everyone knew made such risks. I wanted it all for myself. It was just fun. Life is fun.
This is what I leave you with at this moment in time. To know, that I am the man that will take some of the biggest risks you could ever know. I’m Chase Herrera, a man who just wants to have fun.
