A peaceful place

2 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A peaceful place I take a few steps forward onto the cold grass that lies ahead. One foot after the other walking in a sea of dark night and endless rows of rooting flesh. Stones go up and down rows marking the place of the loved one below. I look around and find my self-trapped by choice in the middle of what people would call a frightening place. But to me its just another place for enlightenment, a place of reflection and a place of beauty. I take a deep breath in catching all the smells of each rooting corps barred below. The sweet stench is strong enough to make any man faint, but its just a sweet as a rose. I look side to side, catching glimpses of names and dates. Each stone unique in its own mind. I looked down at one. A rounded stone peaking just above a small bush. Samuel Adual 1917  1986 A carrying man, and a loving husband. Comforting words to someone who will never love again. A push the bush back into its original place and continue to walk on. I close my eyes trying to bring the full effect into my spirit. The still of the night and the screams shatter all fear in my body. To think that this was the most peaceful place this little world could ever realize and yet we are afraid to enter. Whats there to fear? Is the smell of rooting flesh so overwhelming that you may actually die? Is the feeling of being watched by countless numbers of drifting souls too much for your mind to handle. Sara Johnston 1960  1990 A women full of joy but life cut short by hate. Poor women, her life cut short. But is that such a bad thing. Look what should mist. War, terror, and endless fighting. Cut short I dont think so. I think it was just in time. She was luck. She didnt have to face the world we now live in. But I do, and all the terror, and the violence looks good. Peoples joy and love replaced by fear and anger. Couldnt make for the more perfect world. Tomas Borden 1985  1995 A beautiful child with a loving attitude. Just a child and not even able to smell the roses. A pity isnt it. His parents must have had high hopes for him. They saw him reaching his goal and become someone special. They saw he succeeding in everything he did and then suddenly, SNAP. It all comes crashing down to nothing. All hope and all dreams end just like that. A few more steps forward and I find my self-peering into a dark whole. Lined perfectly, and just deep enough to make sure the soul can escape. Something then catches my eye. A box, no it cant be. I have to take a closer look. I jump down into the craves making sure not to disturbed the surrounding area. Yes, its what I came to see. I glide my hand across the box. Still warm, not the usually in a place like this. I look around looking for a hatch. I then proceed to lift up and reveal what I came for. A beautiful sights that only I get the honor to see. A push the lid up and crouch down beside the box. Only having a few inches of space to rest. A glide my hand across the face. Cold and hard as stone. This person appears to be still young. I take in a deep breath making sure to get ever sent into my thoughts. A power rushes through my body and fills me full. A close my eyes and push out all the negative thoughts. Slowly I climb out of the grave making sure to replace what I had disturbed. I had found what I came for. Something told me to go back and look at what I was waiting for. I turned around and looked down at the stone covered with flowers. Tara Myers 1960-2002 Different then most. Thats what I came to see. Someone who was different. I smiled and got up. I looked around again. Rows and rows and rotting flesh and the stench of each individual person surrounded me. Each loved one marked out by a stone. A stone that would give past, present and future in three lines. A cold life indeed. I walk down the rows and into the night side. The spirits leave my thoughts behind and I walk out UN-touched like nothing had happen. I look back and thank the world for giving me such a peaceful place.
