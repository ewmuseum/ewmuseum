It Starts now!

8 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It Starts now! Written: 8th February “Why are we here?” “Why are we where?” “PURE.” “We are Wrestlers, that’s our job we wrestle.” “But why PURE? Why did we travel thousands of miles to do something that we could have easily been doing back in the states?”
“Think about it. Would you rather be wrestling here, or be back there having to create crappy angles to entertain because of the lack of wrestling skills that American promotions have?”
“Good point but sometimes I just get a little home sick y’know.” Suddenly three men came into view all lying in separate beds in their own train of thought. The three men were PURE trinity team Nu Era Ric and Johnny lay awake whilst Josh was playing with his portable game system and listening to some kind of a racket that somehow was passed of as modern music.
“The last couple of weeks we’ve been having to deal with Enmity and getting our ass handed to us each time.” Said Johnny as lifted the bed sheet of him and drew the curtains allowing a vast amount of light shine through the window.
“Hey man I can’t see the screen!” Bellowed out Josh as protected his eyes from the incoming daylight.
Johnny walked over to Josh and grabbed the game console from his grip and threw it to the floor.
“What the hell are you doing!” cried Josh “We got no time to be laying around playing games, we gotta get down the gym and train.” Stated Johnny
Ric had now too left the comfort of his own bed and was putting on some clothes, he looked over at Josh and giggled slightly
“Something funny?” Asked Josh “Yeah, the fact that a 20 year old man could get so annoyed that he is not allowed to play a kids game jus made me laugh.” Retorted Ric
“Would it be even funnier if I stuck it up your ass!” Shouted Josh “Hey! Stop all that arguing shit. You know why we ain’t booked on Ikusa this week?” Said Johnny
“No I don’t know please tell me, I’m just dying to know.” Sarcastically replied Josh “It’s because we don’t put the effort in, so starting today we gonna train real hard to become big time players here in PURE.”
“Whatever.” Josh countered, seemingly not bothered by anything his brother has to say. As all three continued to get ready for there hard days work, they apparently need to get into good enough shape to combat the likes of The Black Dragons and PURE trinity tag team champions The Sushi Crew and Shaun Styles.
