Reckless Reality

28 February 2003

Reckless Reality Written: 28/02/03 And just when I thought I was sure about my life. Just when I was certain that things would never go wrong. Suddenly that is taken away, my thoughts proven wrong. My career worthless in the eyes of others. Now I sit at home, and I ponder what to do next. My next plan of action! Is it to merely sit back and relax. Enjoy my time of solitude, enjoy the money. No more pain all because of one little game I used to play. I can enjoy life now, without the hospital appointments, straining my body to be the best. All of this I can put behind me! I no longer have to watch my back every day. No more fear, worry and sorrow. No more lying on my back, looking up to the lights above.
This, could turn out to be a happy ending. This could turn out to be a gift from above. But your not so lucky! This would all be good and well, if this in my mind was a happy ending. However, for me this is not the life I want to live. If you thought I was just going to disappear into the face of the earth, never to be seen again then you will find yourself staring into that fantasy and seeing it disappearing before you. Like I've said before, PURE is part of me! I'm not going to just give it up. Not without a fight, not without struggle. You see, Kohana thought he had all the power. Kohana thought he was in charge and control. Well, far from it Kohana . Your power, was never substantial in the first place. It's been slipping ever since. You think you can fire me, just like I've never done anything for this place. Well that's never going to happen, without it backfiring on you.
You see, I don't want to sit back and relax. In my eyes that is a wasted life. I need to be doing something, and it might as well be doing what I want in the realms I made my name famous in. I love the pain from this little game. It's more than just a little game. It was my life and I'm not just letting you rip that away from me. I need to be the best, that's why I put myself through all of this. I feed off the fear that can be sent through me. I love it and because of that, I'm back.
You see, I wondered just how hard it would be to get my job back, but I didn't need to think for very long. Just one phone call and suddenly everything was looking as good as it always had. And I've come to the conclusion that Kohana , you have absolutely no power. You have nothing against the likes of me. Not when you've got someone who knows what's good for the business. Not when you have someone who will just give me my job back when you fire me. The clouds are gathering above your head Kohana , and the sun is a long way away.
And I come back, and it seems everyone has truly realized how much I'm needed around here. I step into the door, let everyone know that I'm back and look what happens. They need me as a champion and that's basically what they are making sure happens after the PPV. You see, this match isn't there to be a great match for the fans to marvel at. This match has been booked to guaranteed I hold that title when it's all finished, and in the record books. This is all so I bring back pride to that title. It's all so that they can throw me into a winner takes all match once again. It's all so I can bring in the ratings. You see, the cycle is going to be exactly the same as it was before I left. More titles to add to my collection.
Still disagreeing? Prove me wrong!
