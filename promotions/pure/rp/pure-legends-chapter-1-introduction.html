PURE Legends: Chapter 1 (Introduction)

25 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PURE Legends: Chapter 1 (Introduction) The torches upon their tri-stands light the encampment outside Shoiichi Pass. The large tent on a raised platform of wood is flanked on all sides by these torches, each one burning bright and illuminating the drab grey camp a lively, dancing array of reds, oranges, and yellows. The warrior steps up the stairs toward the tents opening, not checking his back-sheathed katana with the front guards as there are more guards within. The warrior is lightly-armored, wearing all gold from head to toe with silver chain mail underneath that. White cloth covers his body under that, just so the metal wont chafe. The armor is scaled like a reptile; his helmet itself is designed in the shape of a dragons head. He wears a white facemask that covers his whole face. Taking off his helmet as he approaches the tent, his hair falls out through the top, a hole provided for it. Unlike the style of wearing it tied up above the head (nobles) or in a shoulder-length ponytail (commons), this warriors shoulder length hair falls freely to his shoulders. On his back his back is the sheathed sword, Wild Fury. Smithied from an unknown metal that fell from the heavens, the sword is sharp enough to cut through copper, yet in the hands of its owner it could be swung full force at a childs arm and not produce a single cut. The sword alone is a fearsome weapon, but even moreso in the hands of its owner. He reaches behind his back to his belt and produces a small scroll. It is intricately decorated and bears the Imperial Seal. The warrior steps forward with the scroll out ahead of his body. Confidently, yet humbly, he announces himself with a bow to the lieutenants guarding the front entrance to the tent. Warrior: I am here to see Emperor Tokugawa Ryosuke. The first lieutenant steps forward, his clanking armor awkwardly fitting over his body. The bulbous armor seems like red porcelain, though it is made of copper. His left hand is on his hip-carried sword, his right reaching out to take the scroll. To be safe, he opens it himself to make sure that it is not laced with a poisonous agent, or rigged to release a dart upon opening. It turns out to be an ordinary scroll. The Imperial Seal has been forged recently, and false scrolls delivered by assassins have been delivered to high-ranking officers and officials in the region as of late. The first lieutenant reads the scroll. First Lieutenant: You are Eiji Endo? The warrior nods his head as he rises back to an erect stance. Eiji: Yes. The first lieutenant goes into the tent. The second waits out with Eiji. Above, a single falcon flies through the night sky. A faint speck of light reflects down from its leg. Eiji: Do you normally send carrier falcons out this late? Second Lieutenant: We do not have carrier falcons. Eiji focuses on the falcon. It flies fast to the east in the direction of Shoiichi Pass, where the fierce and outnumbering Yamatoshi forces gather their forces, expected to attack in a week. The Emperor Tokugawa Ryosuke and his forces are waiting for reinforcements from the Emperors brother, Viceroy Tokugawa Kazuhiro, to arrive. Eiji: *Thinking* Carrier birds are taught to fly slow while scouting ahead, and to return as quickly as possible... The red armor-clad guard steps out from the tent, wearing his broadsword on his short broadsword (a western design, made for hacking and slashing as well stabbing and slicing) on his hip. His plain, calm face belies his inner-worry; the entire battalion seems to have taken on an air of uneasy anticipation, what with the fierce Yamatoshi forces gathered a distance away, preparing to strike. Guard: The Emperor shall see you. Eiji breaks his stance into a run as he charges ahead into the tent, urgency on his face underneath his mask. He rips into the tent and stops in front of the Emperors fire. Emperor Tokugawa Ryosuke: You are Eiji Endo? Eiji: Yes, Emperor. I apologize for my entrance, but you must ready your troops! Retreat or fight, either way the Yamatoshi forces will be upon you by mornings light!
