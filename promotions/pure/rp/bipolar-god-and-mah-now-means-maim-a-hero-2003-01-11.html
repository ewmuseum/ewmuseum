Bipolar God... (And MAH now means Maim-A-Hero)

11 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Bipolar God... (And MAH now means Maim-A-Hero) Written: January 10th Posted: January 11th [i]Ahhhhhhh... It feels so good to be back in the swing of things, doesn't it? The birds are chirping, the sun is shining, and the organization known as PURE is back on it's feet. [/i]
This shit...this fucking shit has me seeing the good things in life. All my problems go away with these fucking little pills.
Tegretol... Surmontil... Trilafon... Halcion... Each little pill, 'helping me become AND stay a stable person.' That's what the doctors keep telling me everytime I get a new prescription. B-U-L-L-S-H-I-T. If they work, how come I need to keep getting more and more irrelevant drugs? What am I? A fucking test tube?
[i]Wow, will you look at that? I never noticed so many clouds in the sky at one time. Fuck, I am so doped...[/i] The doctors keep telling me it's not a big deal.They tell me that many people suffer from manic depression, as well. That being bipolar is nothing to fret about...as long as stay on my medication. But they don't understand...I am at my best when I'm NOT taking my pills. I think, I react, I AM, much sharper and more dangerous when I'm fully aware of the things around me.
I am at other's mercy when I'm doped... They are at mine when I'm f...r...e...e... [i]This is the perfect day to just relax and gaze into the sky. Let my mind wander and let my body go numb. [/i]
I have actually thought up a plan where both the doctors and myself are happy. Every day that I am not working...I get drugged out of my fucking mind and become a puppet. Every day that I wrestle in PURE, I let the mindset that got me into this state take over.
[i]Today, I am a pawn... Tomorrow, I will be a pawn... Sunday, I will be the puppet master...[/i] I tend to think more about wrestling when I'm drugged, and I'm still uncertain on whether that is a good thing or not. You see, for the past few days I have been thinking non-stop about Mang-A-Hero and Axcess.
I applaud and respect him for competing after a career-threatening injury, I really do. But I think he's a stupid fuck for wanting the return match to be against me. Don't get me wrong, if I was in a sedated state on Sunday I would be his bitch. He would be able to have his way with me.
But I won't be sedated...I'll be looking for another victim. I've put many a stars on the shelf in my short time, here. Hell, I took Jamar Wilson, the PURE president out of this game. Does Mang-A-Hero think I won't do the same to him? Does he think I'll take it easy on him because he's a lowly announcer? Does he--
[i]Ringing? What the hell is that ringing?[/i] Oh yeah, this little censor on my arm. Whenever my stress level rises it goes off. It picks up other bodily signals but I didn't listen to what the doctor was saying. All I know is that when it starts ringing, it's time to take more pills.
[i]That's cool...[/i] Time for more drugging. Ahhhhhhh yeah...[/i] Where was I? Oh yeah, Mang-A-Hero. Bah, I'm tired of talking about him. There is one benefit of these drugs...I feel nothing. I feel no pain, no emotion, no nothing. That might be because I take half of the bottle at a time. No pain? I'm going to go play with a blade...
[i]Hahahaha... I'm a fucking bipolar God... And I'm going to be Maim-A-Hero after Sunday... Fuck yeah...[/i]
