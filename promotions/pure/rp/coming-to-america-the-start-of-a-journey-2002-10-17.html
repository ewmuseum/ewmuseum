Coming to America: The Start of a Journey

17 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Coming to America: The Start of a Journey Written: 10/17/02 ::Introduction:: Aryim is a man who is not known at all. He has no friends in the world right now and he has been around since people can remember. He has seen the deaths of kings and have seen the wars around the world. No one knows where he has come from and how he has got here. He has no parents and lives in a deep dark hole most of the time waiting for people to live their lives. Aryim has never been to America and on this day he will find out what it is going to be like.
::End Introduction:: The scene opens up outside of a Chinese small village. A waterfall is seen as the sound of splashing water from the waterfall going into the little pond is peaceful. There is no one around and there are some birds chirping. There is a cave down in the middle of the waterfall that only a few people have visited. Deep into the cave it is dark and cold inside with some water dropping down onto the hard dirt can be heard. A shadow of a man can be seen entering the cave, and he looks as if he has been drenched with water.
'Hello is anyone here?' the deep voice of an old man is heard as he walks in to the cave more. 'Monkey God are you here?'
No one responds to the man as he is walking towards one of the walls in the cave. Some water is coming out of the walls in little holes as it is running down the side of the cave as well. Then the man hits something iron as it falls down to the ground and makes a clank sound as it hits hard. Some yellow eyes can be seen from the top of the cave and a figure is seen right in front of the man's face.
'What are you doing here?' asks the man who just came down from the top of the cave. 'I am here looking for a man that may be able to help me.' says the man with a deep voice. 'What kind of help do you need?' asks the man who was in the cave with the messed up eyes. 'I am looking for a man who is names Aryim so that I may take him to America with me.' the deep sounding voice man said as he looks startled looking into those colors of eyes.
'What is it that you need Aryim for?' asks Aryim himself as that is the man with the yellow colored eyes.
'Well I have heard the stories about him and how he can fight very well.' The man starts to say as Aryim looks at him in a gaze. 'I wanted to know if he wanted to come to America with me so that I can show all of the American boxers over there that there are actually Chinese boxers that can box.'
'Follow me.' Aryim says as he takes him iron pole off of the ground and he sticks it in the sling on his back.
Aryim has the pole on his back as the man follows him out of the cave. They take a right from the cave and into the forest of trees and wild plants/flowers. Aryim starts to head up the hill and he uses his pole for a walking stick. From the looks of the pole it looks as if it weighs at least thirty pounds at the most. He is using it as if it weighs about an ounce.
'So what do you say Aryim?' the guy who has the deep voice asks. 'Who are you first of all?' asks Aryim as he is reaching to top of the hill. 'I am Wei Chang and I go to America on a boat for shipping items over there from China.' Wei Chang says as they reach the top of the hill.
'What stories have you heard about me?' asks Aryim as he looks into the stream of water going to the waterfall.
'When I was young and living with my master he taught me how to read and write.' Wei Chang says as he is watching Aryim look at the water. 'I read a book the name was The Monkey King Journeys. It was where you and Minay-ching went on journeys in the desert and how you fought off the whole army and everything like that.'
'How do you know that I am that man that is in the books that you read?' Aryim asks as he is sitting on a rock watching the waterfall go down into the little pool of water.
'You look just like him, you have the iron staff and the wardrobe just like what he did.' says Wei Chang as he is looking at other features. 'You have the same skills as he does and you have the same eyes.'
'How do you know what my skills are like if you have never seen them?' says Aryim as he is still looking into the waterfall.
'Well let me see you use the iron staff that you have.' says Wei Chang as he is looking at Aryim still and his iron staff.
Aryim gets up off of the rock and he goes over to a bunch of trees clumped together. He grabs the iron staff out of his back sling and he takes it with both of his hands. He goes up to one of the biggest trees around in the trunk and he sizes it up. He swings the iron staff back and he then strikes the tree using a baseball swing. Some of the bark on the tree comes flying back in pieces as the tree falls down to the side.
'That is the same thing that Monkey King would do in his book.' Wei Ching says as he is looking at some of the shattered bark on the ground.
'You needed a Chinese boxer right?' says Aryim as he looks at Wei Chang. 'Yeah that is right.' says Wei Chang as he gets all excited about thinking Aryim will do it.
'When does the boat leave?' asks Aryim as he is putting his iron staff back into his sling.
'It leaves in two days.' says Wei Chang as he is looking at Aryim put the staff back in the sling. 'That should give me enough time to get you all trained for boxing and teach you all of the rules.'
'Well since you woke me up from my sleep as you bumped my iron staff.' Aryim says as he is walking over towards the end of the hill, or cliff. 'I will not be able to get back to sleep for a while so the only other choice is to go with you.'
'Well come back to my place so that I can teach you some boxing rules.' says Wei Chang as he looks as Aryim at the waterfall.
'How do you know so much about boxing?' Aryim asks as he is interested. 'I was once a Chinese boxer and my first trip over to America was for boxing.' says Wei Chang as he is moving over to the end of the cliff to meet Aryim.
'Why are you a guy who goes over to America for products instead of a boxer?' asks Aryim as he is throwing little pebbles down to the little pool of water.
'Well I found out the hard way in America that you are not always as good as you are in your hometown.' says Wei Chang as he picks up a few pebbles himself. 'I went over to America thinking I would win all the boxing fights and go into all of the wealth that came with fighting. But that didn't happen as I never ever made it big time when I was in America. I just was in the gym all of the time and I decided that I would hang up the gloves and pursuit other things. But I think you will get recognized over in America for your abilities with the iron staff you just showed me.'
Aryim just shakes his head as he gets up off of the end of the cliff and he heads into the forest. Wei Chang just heads off down the hill once again as he sees Aryim disappear into the woods even more.
'He will come back.' Wei Chang says as he heads down the hill, back to the village.
