Head west, young man...

9 April 2002

Head west, young man... **OOC note: I hope you weren't expecting the confrontation with the father here. No way I'm giving up the pivotal moment in Kevin's life for free. That's why it called PAY Per View..** The quiet mists of dawn wet the needles of the pines and leaves of the broadgrass as the first rays of the rising sun glint in the rear-view mirror. In the rocky mountain heights, the sandy canyon floor, and in the long shadows of the pine forest that stand between a new day begins. The earth wakes from her sleep as a young man charges east while heading west. Now, the journey begins in ernest. The cool morning breeze on his cheeks helped to refill the spirit in Kevin Sandusky. The events of yesterday had drained him. Even still, the emotional 'backlash' washed over him like a water rushing down a ravine after the monsoons swept across the Santa Catalinas. He'd said his piece, then made his peace. But something told him that it'd never be truely finished, no matter what. A little piece of it would return every time he signed his name to paper, or someone addressed him formally. But he'd made good on Jose's wishes, and that was enough. Even hours later, it scared him though. The malicious force that was still resident in his father, even after all these years. Was it with Kevin as well? He prayed not, lest he fail in his attempts to step out of the shadow of his father's wrath and his uncle's rebellion. Still, it was a good day. He was his own man now, on his own way. In a few short days he'd be half a world away from all the family troubles and drama. Now was his time to sink or swim, and there would be no one to boast or blame but himself. The road's twisting route through the mountains kept his senses actively employed, but the excitement of the next week swept Kevin's thoughts to the future, and his opponents. The End, a supposedly 'big-time' player from PCW. Tall and lean, surprisingly strong despite his outward appearence. From what Uncle Joey had said, he was also something of an 'elitist'. :: Great, just what I need. Another guy to look down on me just because I'm not what he'd like me to be. :: End worked in a swooping and fluid style that suited his range and speed. Best tactics against would be a strong defense and explosive counters to break his rhythm and momentum. His other opponent was Billy Larkin. He hadn't learned much about the man other than he was supposed to be at the show. His bio said he was originally from Iowa, so most likely he had a strong technical base to build off of. At least that's what you'd have to go on judging by the few wrestlers from Iowa that it's been Kev's privledge to meet. ::I sure hope he's not a younger version of Jack 'Crash' Williams, or I'm in trouble. Of course, if he was, I'd have heard of him by now. :: The thought was somewhat reassuring to Kevin. Another unknown there in the ring against a vet like End. Hopefully, End would underestimate the match and come out cold. But odds of that are slim, because anyone who's ever stepped between the ropes knows what can happen once the bell rings. Anything... And that's exactly what Kevin has hoping for. A young man seeks the unknown, hoping therein to find answers to questions that linger in the back of his subconscious. So the young man takes up courage's challenge and leaves behind everything that he has known. Head west young man, head west..
