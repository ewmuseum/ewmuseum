Sue Esyde Written: Ikusa #1 The blade was to her skin… She could feel the chilled teeth biting at her wrist, like a metal animal threatening her. She felt helpless…alone…weak… But she was a hostage. He held her there, against her will, keeping her at bay with fear. There was nothing she could do. She was his prisoner…held at bay by the blade.
Her life has lead up to this point…she knew it the day she found her boyfriend in the classroom, alone with one of the sluts in school.
She knew it the day that she failed her midterm…the one she spent all month preparing for. She knew it the day she thought she was better than the rest of them…and found out just how much of a loser she really was when she fell down the stairs in front of everyone.
Ouch… They told her to her face what she was… Loser… Freak… Waste of space… Worthless… N O T H I N G Well…nothing was being threatened by the teetering pillar of life and death now. No one liked to be forgotten… At first, all the attention is on them, the most popular person in the place, and suddenly you’re pulled out of the light because someone else walks in and does something better.
Someone who thinks they’re better…someone who just may be b e t t e r Saying who’s better…saying who’s superior to another…it’s all a matter of opinion. There is no true way to measure skill…
But you can measure blood…in pints…liters…quarts…gallons… Maybe she would have to measure her own…that is, if he who held her hostage decided to spill her fluids.
Everyone was gone…family outing to see a movie. The Ring…based off the Japanese movie/novel. She was the only one there…no one would hear her scream…no one would hear her body collapse on the ground from the floor below…no one would hear her heart stop beating.
But he would… He held her tightly…no escape from her fate. His cold touch groping her body like a sex-raged fiend molesting her frame. There was nothing she could do but sit there…and feel him hold the knife closer to her skin.
She felt bad…she didn’t want to be so helpless…she didn’t want to leave like this…she thought maybe there was another way.
But he…with his cold touch and his emotionless persona… He said this was the only way… She nodded as he asked her if she wanted to feel better. She nodded again as she agreed with his statement about no one should feel the things she felt. She found herself nodding over and over, no matter what he said to her…the sympathy he began with morphing into insults that stung at her skin and made her eyes bleed salty drops.
He spoke words that led her into not fighting back…remaining his hostage… But there was still something within her…something that wasn’t listening to his propaganda.
And as he continued to preach on how the blade was the only way…on how she should let him do his job…
Something just said N O No to his power…no to his suggestions…no to his demands… No to it all. She wasn’t going to take anything else. It’s time to fight back. They wrestled…with words…with wrists…with will… He was strong…she had never seen him so strong before. Maybe because he had the knife…that gave him power.
But soon…the knife was out of his hands…and into hers. He was now the hostage…and she was on top. She could feel the adrenaline pumping through her, a smile slowly forming on her face as she stalked the man back into the corner of the dark bedroom. She felt the power now…yes, the blade did hold power.
And she felt how much power she could have over her life…as she ended his. -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- The house was dark…silent… It looked like a murder scene. She laid face down on her bed in the dark…tears soaking into the pillow for what almost happened to her.
In the corner of the room, the corpse lay…with knife stained with his death. She would never forget his name… S U I C I D E
