Enter The Dragon(s)

23 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Enter The Dragon(s) The Black Dragons make their way down the busy streets of Tokyo, Japan as the streets are bustling with Japanese business people walking briskly to and from their destinations, along with other Japanese pedestrians walking to their own accord. The Black Dragons look a bit out of place as they walk down the streets into the oncoming traffic of people. People move out of their way as they walk down the streets striking fear into many Japanese pedestrians. This is a waste of time Chen, remarks Chan Tin Yeung of the Black Dragons. Why would Mountain Lord Pai Po want us to come to this filthy place? And here I thought America was bad Ning Yung-ho comments as he looks from the Japanese crowd to his big buddy Chiang Ho Nam. Chiang just nods his head in agreement as the four make their way down the street. Mountain Lord Pia Po requests our presence in PURE, so we will go to PURE, replies Chen Lei-chia, the leader of the four-man Black Dragon band. If Lord Pai Po wants us to do something, we will do it! That includes our current mission! The four Black Dragon members continue on their way as they walk past women, children and men all of which move out of the dangerous and volatile men. Hey, you guys remember the last time we were in Tokyo? asks Ning as they notice some tattooed businessmen that resemble the notorious Japanese Mafia the Yakuza. How can I forget? remarks Chen as the four Black Dragons stop walking and look on at the group of Yakuza members who are standing around across the street. The question is, do they remember? The group of Yakuza stop their talking and notice the four Black Dragons standing across the street looking at them. The four Black Dragons notice that they are caught staring at the Yakuza guys, and with that, they begin walking on their way every now and then looking back behind them. I guess they did forget! remarks Ning as the four walk on. Chen stops the group and points over down the street at the first Chinese Restaurant they have seen since they got in Japan. Time to eat Chen says as he begins his walk down the sidewalk to the Gran Hunan Chinese Buffet. Home sweet home! The four walk into the establishment noticing a few sets of eyes look up and follow their path to an empty table in the corner. A waitress comes up to the table that the Black Dragons are sitting at with a pad of paper in her hand and a pencil in her hair. What can I get you to drink? asks the waitress. Just bring a big bottle of Sake with four glasses if you can, remarks Chen as the waitress writes it down. As the waitress leaves the Black Dragons begin to talk. Its time we get down to business, remarks Chen. What do any of you know about PURE? The four sit and think as they realize that they dont know much about the Japanese based organization known as PURE. Chen looks at each Black Dragon, and with that look from their leader they each shrug their shoulders in response to the questioning look. I know that it is full of the Japanese scum! remarks Ning snidely. But besides that, I am as clueless as a Geisha whore on crack! Well it looks like the first thing on our agenda is to do some research, Chen orders. Chiang I want you and research the wrestlers in PURE. Chan I want you to come with me as we research this whole puro wrestling thing. Ning, I want you to go see if those Yakuza remember us or have noticed that we are back in Japan. They sit back as the waitress delivers the four glasses and the bottle of Sake to the table. Chen pours the potent liquid into each small shot glass and hands it to each person. They raise their glasses and puts them in the center. To the success of the Black Dragons and to the demise of PURE! Chen toasts. The four clang their glasses together and down the potent liquor together. Chiang takes the bottle and begins to chug the stuff as the other three begin to laugh at their fellow Black Dragon. Chen stops laughing and sits back with a look of thought as Chen thinks of the mission at hand.
