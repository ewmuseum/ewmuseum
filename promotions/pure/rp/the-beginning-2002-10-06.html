The Beginning

6 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Beginning Written: 10/6/02 Friday night. Uneventful. My love and I sit on my shitty neon green couch watching the boob tube. I so do loathe the television. None the less, it does serve well as a tool of time wasting. Ten, eleven, twelve, 13. The news. Might as well stop here. Watch one of our government's many propaganda devices.
Laura Demoz reporting for channel 13 Fox News. I stand in front of a home in Downtown Philadelphia where yet another early morning murder has been committed.
Our eyebrows rise in unison. A chuckle escapes my lips. It's here where Maria Vascaz was slain in another act of senseless violence. Senseless? Tonight a major breakthrough has been made by the police. They now believe they have found a connection in this murder and ten others committed over the last six years.
Uh oh. And here we were taking the government as a loud of cretins. Yo- Wait, excuse me officer. Would you mind filling in our viewers into exactly how you made this connection?
Well. It wasn't easy. These horrific acts have been occurring in recent years. At first, we cold make no attachment. All of the victim's bodies vanished. No fingerprints or DNA were ever found. But tonight we noticed something that I quiet frankly am dumbfounded as to how we missed. A small body part was always left tonight, like every night. Before it had been slithers of tongue, pieces of flesh, and fingernails. Tonight, it was 13 drops of blood formed in the shape of a pentagram. It seems that the perpetrator of these crimes is interested in some kind of dark cult, and or sub-culture. Possibly witchcraft, Satanism, or Goth even. A small clue, but a clue non-the less. It's far too early to identify this disgusting individual with many characteristics but I can tell you this. We figure the assailant to be young, between the ages of 18 and 24, quiet, suspicious, paranoid even, and highly intelligent. I have no doubt that they will strike again within the next few weeks.
Thank you officer.... Click Of goes the television. Why did we do that? .... I have no idea... We broke our most sacred of all guidelines. We underestimated our opponent... We showed our ignorance. We disgraced the entire serial killing industry.
Fuck. Fuck! FUCK! We have time before they come after us. We need to pack. We must get the fuck out of here Damian. We need to go far, far away.
Is it really necessary? I mean, they have no PHYSICAL evidence and they're no where near coming after us.
Damian... Of all people I'd never expect you to be so naive. Do you honestly think the government gives a fuck about EVIDENCE of all things? They know how dangerous we are. Don't take the pigs for idiots. They're far from stupid. They will find us unless we leave within the next few days.
Yes, yes I know... Let us pack... fin
