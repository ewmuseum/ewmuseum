Decision at the expense of honour? Written: 5/10/02 Junzo Muto RP#1 The blue tint of the moonlight, piercing the window, emphasised the stern look etched upon Junzo’s face, as he stared out at nothing in particular. He was still reeling from the phone call he had not long received. It was an invitation to join the Japanese based wrestling company known as PURE. It was the kind of decision that could either make or break his life, but he already knew the answer.
He was going to join PURE, no question, but on the other hand he could not escape his duties for his master. The man named Dane Lochlan was his master now, had been since he found Junzo over a decade ago, just after his father died. Dane looked after him and groomed into the company’s top assassin. In reality, Junzo tired of the trade, but he was honour bound to stay by Dane’s side and could not, would not, dishonour his or his families name. Plus, besides the honour factor, Junzo owed a lot to Dane, after all he saved Junzo all of those years ago and without Dane, he surely would have died upon the streets.
Junzo’s thoughts were shattered, by the piercing sound of the telephone. He glanced at the phone for a second, before slowly making his way over to the pine desk, and picked up the receiver.
‘Yes,’ Junzo said, curtly. ‘Yagos? That you?’ The voice replied. It was Dane, he didn’t know about his alias, his assumed name he used to disguise himself from the general populace, when he fought. ‘Yes sir, what can I do for you?’ he asked, sincerely.
‘I have a new target for you. He lives in an apartment on Sycamore and 33rd,’ Dane stated. ‘What is the target’s name?’ Junzo asked. ‘David Mangiello.’ Dane stated, bluntly. ‘Mangiello?’ I questioned, almost in disbelief. ‘I know…I know. He’s the son of John Mangiello. The truth his I’m declaring war on their family and I want you to do what you do best, kill the man and scare their family into submission. You never know, you might even prevent a gang war.’ Dane explained.
‘I very much doubt it Sir, but your orders will be executed, without question.’ Junzo replied.
With that Junzo placed the receiver down and walked over to the pine coat rack. He then unhooks his silver sword he dubbed the ‘Lingos’, named after his father. The sword was holstered in leather, decorated in an expensive oriental material. He strapped the sword holster to his back, then reached for his long, black, trench coat. The trench coat slipped seamlessly over his back, with not indention to show the holster sword – exactly how expensive Italian manufacturers, done as a favour to Dane, had designed it to pay off old debts.
The PURE decision would have to wait for another day – for he had work to do and by the strain upon Dane’s voice, there would be a lot of work in the near future. PURE was exactly always what he had wanted, but to do so without honour would be worthless. He had to find a way to do both, then his honour would not be questioned. The question was; how?
