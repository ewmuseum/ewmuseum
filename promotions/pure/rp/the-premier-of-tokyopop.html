The Premier Of TokyoPop

21 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Premier Of TokyoPop As the federation of PURE re-opened its doors no less than a week ago, many old faces emerged from the past and stepped back into the ring. Old feuds were united, new friends were made, allegiances were formed and oddly enough a new Champion walked through the doors holding gold in his hand.
Under strange circumstances a new enemy stepped through the doors of PURE and managed to somehow corrupt an important and fragile young mind of its family.
The man himself, the Vietnamese Viper known to many as Charlie but now called Jung seemingly sold his soul to the devil on the debut of Ikusa v2.0 by siding with this evil that was TokyoPop.
It would seem that the huge corporation has somehow gained the upper hand, snapping up and corrupting the mind of PUREs only Champion and some of the best young talent that it has to offer.
Jung has proved to many that he is a formidable foe and that he has what it takes to be the best, but the question is, can he farther this with evil? PURE has re-opened its doors trouble free, or more so opened a Pandoras box! Time will only tell whats going to happen within its walls, but one things for sure, they have been hindered with the loss of a key player who may have just turned into a PURE evil enemy! The Future: A Commercial Proudly Bought To You By TokyoPop (.Welcome To The World Of TokyoPop, The Corporation Which Is On The Rise. Here Is Our Latest Commercial With Our Newest Endorsement. The World Is At Our Hands And Soon Enough The World Will Be At Our Feet.) A small message written in white Japanese characters appears on the bottom of a blank television screen along with an English translation running shortly under it. As it rides across the screen it disappears and everything turns into darkness when a thudding sound suddenly cuts through the silence like a knife. Thud~! Thud~! Thud~! Thud~! All of a sudden a fluorescent green line of a heart rate monitor appears traveling along the center of the black screen, illuminating the darkness in time of the thuds. As the line runs across the screen it leaves a soft trail of white light before it fades out and runs across again. Strange music then begins to overcome the thudding of the heart rate monitor as the voice of a young Asian girl can be head muttering away quickly in the Japanese language with no translation running across the bottom of the screen. 'We Are The Future!' the young Asian girls voice can be heard in clear English as she then begins to chuckle as a familiar beat hits the now dark screen. The whole thing is somewhat similar to a Playstation commercial as the song begins to pump base and an Asian sounding voice begins to rap in time with the words as the song becomes clear to be a Japanese cover of '21 Seconds' by So Solid Crew. BOOM~! BOOM~! The base begins to pulsate as the song cuts into its chorus and the screen slowly lights up to reveal a spinning image of a black silhouette with something slung over its shoulder with a TokyoPop logo and a stunning image of its headquarters behind it. The verse cuts into the song and the image stops spinning as a fluorescent green line comes and lines up from head to toe with the dark silhouette as it seemingly runs a profile. Japanese text appears up along side of the line. (English Translation: 58 'Good Things Come In Small PackagesWe Have Living Proof') The text slowly fades out as does the image, which quickly changes to a PURE wrestling logo on the familiar dark screen as it begins to shake. CRACK~! A huge baseline pulsates from the song as on the screen a TokyoPop logo blasts through the PURE one and shines with radiating light, the voice of the young Asian girl can be heard laughing in the song as it begins to go back to the chorus. Once again the image fades to black before being quickly replaced by another one of a pair of two evil looking eyes with some jagged bits of reddish orange fringes cutting down across them. The image is in wide screen as the eyes dart left to right, in the left eye the word 'Tokyo' can be seen. In the right the word 'Pop'. For the second time some Japanese text appears underneath the image of the eyes darting from left to right as the jagged fringes begin to be blown around as the eyes get a sudden 'evil' glint in them as the image slowly begins to fade out. (English Translation: With The New Dawning Of TokyoPop A New Image Has Been Created, A New 'Look' On The World! These Are What We Use To See Our Bright And Prosperous Future) Suddenly the song begins to skip and play the same beat over and over, faster and faster as yet another image appears on a black background, this one looking somewhat familiar. The shiny, gold plate of the PURE Jnr. Heavy Weight Title becomes focused as out of nowhere two stamps on either side of it appear on the straps, the words 'Tokyo' and 'Pop' somehow making it clear of their plans to dominate PURE and exactly who theyve aligned with. As the beat stops skipping a picture of Viper emerges in soft black ink behind the words 'PURE Jnr. Heavy Weight' as the voice of the young Asian girl can be heard laughing for the third time during the commercial. Once again the image begins to fade out as does the music. As the song fades out the screen goes completely black and just flicker for a second or two before becoming still. Suddenly BOOM~! There is a loud explosion sound on the screen and the song begins to play itself from the beginning again as the scene opens up to a posh looking photo shoot with a familiar man wearing sunglasses and black pants with a Title slung over his shoulder standing amidst a group of mean looking business men. All the business men move out the way as the man wearing the sunglasses and brandishing the gold over his shoulder flips his thumb over the shoulder of his muscly body as a curtain is suddenly dropped revealing a globe with the words 'TokyoPop' covering it in true Hollywood style fashion. If there was an audience to this commercial they would be gasping in shock as the man standing in front of the logo looking smug takes of his sunglasses and smiles towards the camera behind a sinister grin revealing himself to be none other than Jung Loc. Footage of Loc then begins to cut in front of the previous image of him standing in front of the huge globe with the logo into images of him signing autographs, drinking at bars with beautiful women and posh looking business men and even pictures of him winning wrestling matches. While all this is suddenly flickering over the screen the voice of the now irritating young Asian girl begins to talk quickly in her Japanese dialect as a translation appears on the bottom of the screen. (English Translation: This Is What The Future Holds! Jung Loc Is The First Of Many To Realize Where The Power Lies! TokyoPop Is The Wave Of The Future And Nothing Can Be Done To Change It! We Are Where The Money Lies!) The Asian Girl begins to laugh after she finishes speaking as a picture of Jung Loc standing in front of a black background with a TokyoPop logo behind it appears on the screen as the ending beats of the song '21 Seconds' are played as the commercial slowly comes to a close. Jung stands muscles tense holding his Title with one hand and pointing towards the screen with the other as he begins to mutter something in Vietnamese. The Young Asian Girls voice can be heard translating into Japanese as an English translation appears at the bottom of the screen as Jung begins to laugh, before he raises his Title above his head and points behind him to the TokyoPop logo before nodding and smiling. The song slowly fades out as a TokyoPop logo appears next to a PURE logo and the Asian girl can be heard laughing as the scene fades to darkness. (.TokyoPop Is What Dreams Are Made Of! This Is The First Of Many! See Where The Power Lies! The World Will Soon Be Ours.We Are PURE! We Are The Reality.)
