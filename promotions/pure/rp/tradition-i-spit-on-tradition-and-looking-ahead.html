Tradition? I Spit On Tradition; And Looking Ahead

28 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tradition? I Spit On Tradition; And Looking Ahead 'Hmmm...you never cease to amaze me, E.' Jamar Wilson exclamed to me. We were sitting high above the arena, now an empty house, and he had calmed down a bit. 'Well, thank you.' I replied accepting the praise, but not feeling it was truly necessary. 'No, thank you. You have made quite the sudden impact in PURE and I am VERY happy you decided to side with TokyoPop.' I didn't know where this was going, so I just continued the conversation. 'Well...what else was I going to do? I sure as hell was not going to side with Williams and his 'tradition'. To be blatantly honest, I spit on tradition.' I told Jamar, hoping he would see I was truly on his side. He smiled, 'excellent answer...I must say. However...I don't know what he has in store for you for the next Ikusa. I want you to be extremely cautious.' 'Cautious?' All of a sudden I WAS getting worried. 'Yes...cautious.' He slowly began to tell me. 'This is the one time I will tell you NOT to do anything drastic before your match. I cannot afford to lose one of my top stars to injury.' 'But-' I was then rudely interrupted. 'No buts!' He was getting very serious, I could tell. 'I don't think it was a wise idea for us to take Sean so lightly in the past. Just come to Ikusa, get ready, and lay low.' I just nodded and began to leave the room. 'Hey, E.' He called. 'Yes?' I answered wondering what piece of outrageous advice he was going to offer now. 'Way to give them hell out there tonight! HAHAHA!' He was back to his usual self but I still just left. I made it out to my car and stopped right before getting in. I started thinking, and talking aloud to myself. 'I know he's my boss...but who the hell is he to tell me what to do!?' I shouted and banged my fist against the roof of my car. 'Pfffttt...be cautious. We'll see how cautious I am when I'm ending yet another career on Ikusa 3. He'll see...THEY'LL see...I'm not just another face in the crowd.' I jumped in my car and started the engine. 'I'll show them all...' I muttered to myself before driving off into the night...
