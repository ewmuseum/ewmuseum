Sellout...

11 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sellout... On Ikusa IV Ali Khadafi called Jordan X Shabazz a sellout. He called him a dirty, nasty freakin' sellout. Does this statement have any truth in it? Jordan X Shabazz was born Jordan Andrew Copeland in a tiny hospital in Queens, New York. His older brother, Andrew Copeland, better known to professional wrestling fans as the OSW's Drew Hostyle. Jordan was dirt poor growing up in Queens. His father had to sell drugs to make his money, and his mother was had to stick around at home raising Andrew and Jordan. Then one day, Jordan and Andrew's father decided to just pick up and leave. He took everything they had. Jordan's mother was forced to make a decision, send one of her children to Children's Aid protective services, and keep the other one. Since Jordan was younger, and his mother thought it would affect him less if he left, his mother decided to give him up to a nice, white family in Canada. Suburban Toronto, Canada, the rather large town of Scarborough. Jordan grew up with only white friends, the neighborhood he lived in was the 'Above middle class' neighborhood, for once in his life, and he could actually have what he wanted. After he moved on to College, he decided that he didn't want to take his adopted parents money anymore. It just wasn't worth it. He wanted to make his money on his own. Shabazz moved back to Queens, and met up with his now grown up bigger brother. Shabazz was dirt poor this was before professional wrestling. So Jordan decided to do what anybody in his position living in that area would do, he began selling drugs. This wasn't the life he wanted. He took the risk of being shot and killed everyday. You never know when you sell something to somebody if they'll go into their coat pocket and pull out a gun to blast your freakin' brains all over the wall. But that's a risk he took for the money. Enter professional wrestling. When Jordan began to use the money that he made for professional wrestling training, like his brother Drew Hostyle suggested, it was different. He was broke again, but he was happy. His goal was to make it to the PCW and make big time money. When he reached his goal, he realized that money was the only key to happiness. He was different, very different. He decided to try and help his own people. He slowly began developing a hate for most white people. He wrestled as Jordan Crazy in the NTICW. One day, on PTC Malice, The Phantom Booker suggested Jordan get a name change... A name change... A suggestion from the Phantom Booker was excellent! Jordan changed his name; this was before he actually took Civil Rights seriously. He had white friends away from the ring; it was just a wrestling gimmick. One conversation that took place between Jordan and Jesse Ramey ended in Jesse calling Jordan a sellout. Now look at Shabazz, he runs N.I.G.G.A, fights for the black man, supports Duke Williams in the Malice Crew, and he is getting richer, and richer everyday. As a child he didnt originally have what he wanted, but now, through hard work, hes gotten the money that he rightfully deserves. Sure he has turned his back on the Hip black scene, but is a sellout? Hell fucking no. Hes just a rich black man who fights for his people.
