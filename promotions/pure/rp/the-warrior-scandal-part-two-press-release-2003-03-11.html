The Warrior Scandal Part Two: Press Release...

11 March 2003

The Warrior Scandal Part Two: Press Release... Written: 11/3/03 “What’re you gonna do about this sugah?”, Handbag Warrior, questioned in a soft tone. “I don’t know Handbag Warrior. Justice Incorporated have never faced this kind of threat before in our illustrious career. Since we set this company up, we’ve been above board and now with the slightest hiccup their all over us like vermin.”, Blind Rage replied.
“But boss, they’re only really doing their jobs. If we keep this a secret from them for too long, it’ll gain more and more adverse publicity. It could bury us.”, Badger Man explained, a concerned look firmly placed upon his face.
“I know that. We have to hold a press conference in order to state our intentions and explain what is going on. The only problem is, we don’t know what is going on.”, Blind Rage replied.
“I know. Warrior still won’t tell us what he is actually ‘on’, but he insists that its not illegal.”, Badger Man confirmed.
“I believe him, sugah, but I don’t blame the press for reacting this way, given the information they actually have, hon.”, Handbag Warrior stated.
“I know Daniel that is why we must obtain the information from Warrior and reveal it to the press.”, Blind Rage declared.
“But Rage, what if Warrior is on drugs?”, Badger Man prompted, with a decidedly worried look upon his face.
“Well…we tell the press and the public the truth. We have no lies from them.”, Blind Rage stated sternly.
-| |- Warrior sat in the Justice Inc living quarters with a stern look upon his face. This was a bad time for Justice Incorporated and it was largely his fault. The pressed seemed to have received the dialogue of the argument he had with the others a couple of days ago and drawn their own conclusions. Damn paparazzo. How did they get the information? In Warrior’s mind there were only two viable options.
Either the press had this place bugged or one of his fellow superheroes blabbed to the press. He hated himself for thinking such things, but what if it is was the latter option? This therapist had always said that Warrior was slightly paranoid…however, he just dismissed the accusation stating quite clearly, “You don’t want to listen to him, he’s always had it in for me!”. However, now Warrior was aware of his paranoia. That’s if it was paranoia.
Warrior shook the thoughts from his head. None of his friends would blab to the press on purpose… he was certain of that, so therefore this place had to have been bugged. Come to think of it there was a extermination crew in here earlier in the week, despite the Justice Inc building not needing exterminators.
Anyone else would have been worried. Not Justice Inc. Why would they worry? Who the hell would want to mess with four superheroes in their own home? A Psycho that’s who, but still why would a guy who prefers to dress in his mothers clothes frighten four of the strongest people in the world?
None of them could have predicted this. Warrior doubted that even the villain who implemented the listening device could have predicted this. The villain probably just waited to record something incriminating, something that he could hold over the company, something he could destroy Justice Inc’s reputation with. Judging by the news it looked as though this super villain was succeeding where many others had failed before. Warrior grabbed the TV remote and clicked the “on” button. Lets see what these bastards have to say about me today!, he thought with serious contempt.
“It has been confirmed by the front desk that CBBN has received evidence that will cause a scandal in New York’s very best Superhero agency! Sandra Coil is on the scene.”
“Yes, Mary, I’m right here outside of the Justice Inc building, where a controversial issue has arisen concerning the superhero team Justice Inc…
“We have received information from an anonymous source that depicts the superhero team talking about a possible “addiction” that one of them has concurrent to the so-called “Wrestling Addiction”…
“The member of Justice Inc didn’t reveal what the addiction was too, but he was not forthcoming in revealing the substance so we can only assume that it’s something illegal, terrible for a role model to thousands to do…
“The Justice Incorporated team have issued a press conference scheduled for tomorrow, which they declared ‘would deal with this mess and conclusively prove that the team member in question did nothing wrong.’
“We can only speculate what the future of the team will be, after this press conference, but this reporter suspects that the situation doesn’t look good for Justice Incorporated.”
Warrior clicked off the TV in disgust. He suspected that the next 24 hours will be the toughest of his entire life.
