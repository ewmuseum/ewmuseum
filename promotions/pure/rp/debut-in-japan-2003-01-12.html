Debut in Japan

12 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Debut in Japan Written: 1-12-03 “It’s like being in total lockdown,” comes the sound of a very familiar voice stretching throughout the inner walls of the small hotel room we are enclosed in, “I mean, I really do not think much of it, but this place is nothing like what it is back home, mom.”
Slowly we make our way through the room, the walls featuring very pleasant looking pictures of mountains, tigers, dragons, and other very festive designs as we make our way into the one room where we get a glimpse of a hand holding a telephone and swaying the other around in the air.
“Have you ever been to this place?” The person asks as he waits in silence, getting his response, “No, I don’t think you would like to come over and visit, especially not this time of year.” The voice begins to get a little angered as we slowly begin to come into the picture with them finally and see Jesse Ramey sat back in a little bamboo woven chair. “There is nothing here, it’s cold all the time and there isn’t even any snow on the ground. I mean, sure I can look up at the mountains and see all the snow I want, cause that stuff never leaves, but I want to see what I did back home.”
Jesse begins to get a little uncomfortable and begins shimmying himself around in the chair, the obvious look of pain on his face, “What?” He pauses, “Michelle? Yeah, she’s supposed to be coming out here sometime soon.” He says still trying to find his positioning in the chair, “What happened between her and Josh? I’m really not sure, mom. She usually is a talker, so I’m sure I’ll hear the whole story as soon as she gets out here.” Jesse gives up on the chair and comes to his feet with a look of relief on his face and letting out a deep sigh, “No, I don’t believe they did break-up, mom. Last I heard the two were still engaged, she just needed a place to go while Josh tries to find work. Being let go by RUSH wasn’t the best thing in the world for his career and working over in $W really isn’t going to do much for the kid either.”
Jesse continues to pace around the room looking for something to sit down in, but practically everything is made of bamboo or wood related materials, “Mom, I’m sure Michelle would have loved to come and stay with you. I know you love her and all, but I don’t know what was wrong with her, she said she just wanted to come and stay with me.” Jesse pauses as he finally finds his bed and flops down on it, “Yes, mom, I’ll be sure to tell you if she has anything against you, but I’m telling you she doesn’t. She just has some issues running through her mind right now that she really needs to work out on her own.”
“Mom, do you remember when I was eighteen?” Jesse pauses just staring up at the ceiling, “Yeah, I know it seems just like it was yesterday, pretty much because it was basically just what? Ten years ago? Well, back on subject here and the reason I brought that up. Michelle is longing for something, I know, because I was in her position at one time and I know something is missing in her life and tell me what I did when I was eighteen?” Jesse waits another moment as he begins kicking his feet up and down lightly on the edge of the bed, “That’s exactly right, as soon as I got out of high school I came to Japan to work in a wrestling promotion. Japan did me well and taught me so many things about life that it wasn’t funny. Maybe it’ll do the same for Michelle.”
Jesse begins to roll his eyes, “Mom, don’t tell me you’re starting to cry.” Jesse pauses as he sets up on the bed and pulls his hair out of his eyes, “Yes, mom. No, mom. Don’t say those kind of things, mom. Mom. Mom. Please, just stop saying those kinds of things, you and dad didn’t bring me up wrong. You’ve helped me out in life more than probably anyone else could. Yes, I know I said what I said, but that was only later on in life. You all did all you could for me when I was younger. When someone turns eighteen they’ve really already learned everything they can from their parents and have to set out on their own.”
Jesse begins to rub at his goatee, “Well, you all were always there for me, if you really think about it, I was never really there for Michelle. So, I’ve still got a whole lot more I could teach her in life. I’ve not been a really good father to her, mom. Hopefully I’ll be able to make that up to her once she comes out here and stays with me.” Jesse begins picking his nose and bit and flips a booger off into the room, “No, I’m not sure how long she is going to be staying with me. Doesn’t matter though, everything down here is paid for and there is plenty of room in this hotel room, just not any comfortable furniture. Oh, nothing mom.”
Jesse stands up and walks over to the window in his room, which shows off a very beautiful Mito, Japan. “Yeah, I’ll be sure to tell her you said you loved her and all that other stuff mom. I’m going to have to go as well. I love you, too. You and dad take care. Okay. I will mom. Bye.”
Jesse walks over and sets his cell phone down on a little wooden table as he walks around the room, looking at all of the wood furniture. “I’m really going to have to go shopping this weekend. After all, I do have the company credit card. Boo Yah, Baby!”
Jesse slowly makes his way out of the room as one of the sliding doors in the rooms begins to come closer and everything fades into the small, white mountain.
-End-
