April 20th Promo

20 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
April 20th Promo Filmed 4/20/02 The scene opens up with The End sitting in a dimly lit room, the yellow light only half-illuminating the area. There's nothing but a chair by the back corner adjacent to the door, a sink on the right wall, and a bed-frame with no mattress on the left wall opposite the door frame touching the back wall. In the background, muffled as though not coming from in the room but another, Nas's 'All I Need Is One Mic' plays as the only sound ambiance aside from the sputtering of the aging room heater trying to work properly. The End is slumped back against the chair, his hands resting limply on his legs. 'Well going on into my second PURE match, I face The HIT and Kensuke Suzahara for the Junior Heavyweight Title.' The End leans forward in the chair. 'I must say that it is disappointing that I do not have the PCW Cruiserweight Title around my waist at this moment, but there was some inexplicable interference that resulted in the match being thrown out.' A pause. Derek clears his throat, prompting more speech from The End. 'Oh, yes, let me introduce Derek. Hes the American cameraman that shall be recording all of my promos. Hes different than Jeremy Johnson in that he has an exclusive deal with to cover any puroreso promos. The End again finishes his speech and just sits on camera. Derek: Youre supposed to keep going. What, you want me to keep talking? asks The End in s half-question. What's the point? It's useless. The World Title match is signed, and now all that's le--' The End stands up and moves to the right wall and bangs loudly on it with his fist. 'Shut up! I'm trying to cut a promo in here!' The music stops. The End returns to his seat and sits back down, lounged with his legs outstretched right over left. 'Anyway, the Junior Heavyweight Title match is signed and now all that's left to do is wait for it.' Another pause, but this one a break in speech to change thoughts. 'Are you smoking marijuana, Derek? Derek: Yeah. You want some? The End is appalled. Why are you smoking marijuana? The End asks in an interrogative attack on Derek. From off-camera, Derek makes inhaling sounds and coughs. His smoking is easily detectable by sound. Derek: Because. Its 4/20. Earth Day. Gotta celebrate. The End is taken aback by Derek's comment. Hm so I suppose after marijuana, youre going to go ahead with the ethnic purge in honor of Hitlers birthday? Derek laughs. The music starts up again. Derek: 'Maybe. After I started smoking the weed I forgot about everything else I had to do.' 'Its okay to smoke pot on April 20. Good thing theres no January 87.' Derek: 'Huh?' The End releases an agitated sigh. 'Nevermind, I have to go an deal with something.' The End storms out of the room, flinging the door open. The camera stays in place, but the noise is easily heard. 'Who are you? The director? I told you to keep the music down! I don't care if you're shooting a video! Sit down, Nas, no one's talking to you!' There is the sound of gunfire. Screams. Then running. The End walks calmly back into the room. Lets cut it there, Derek. We can pick up later. Derek: Are you sure you dont want a hit of this bong, man? Youre lucky I dont hit you, know just turn the camera off. Derek: Fine. Your loss. The scene fades out. The next day *Rings* *Rings* *Rings* *Click* *Beep* Message: Yo. This is Derek. Ive probably got a major hangover right now, so Im not gonna be good for anything. Try me tomorrow. Just leave a message. *Beep* The Ends voice comes over the answering machine. Derek, get up. I have to finish the promo! You lazy son of a I know youre home. Get up! Dont make me come over there! The next day Along with Derek filming, The End meets Yuri Takayama for an interview for her next assignment. They are sitting in a restaurant in Yamanashi, an area less populated than Nagano, but still gets its fair share of visitation domestic and tourist. There arent many other people in the restaurant, those that are present sitting away in the corners speaking between themselves. Yuri wears here usual silver silk blouse with tight black slacks and black heels. The End appears without his mask or ring attire, instead opting for a much more casual black shirt and jeans with white Nikes. So, The End begins, what magazine is this for? Yuri: I cant remember right now. Thats not important. Anyway, lets get down to the questions. Fair enough. Yuri: So, your ring name is The End. You dont give away your real name? Why? That was just laziness on my part. I keep having to give out all my information. I actually just wrote, Look it up in PCW which Im sure you have noted. Yuri: Yes. About your match with Ebola III and Billy Larkin on PUREs first Ikusa card: were you afraid of them? So you jumped out of the ring to let them fight? The End laughs. Not nervously, but he finds the comment funny. No, he says. I like to see, forgive the pun, what my opponents can do, and given the triple threat format, I figured Id let those two fight between each other to get a grasp on what their strengths and weaknesses were. Yuri scribbles some notes while her own digital sound recorder tracks The Ends statements. Yuri: Yes, it says here that youre blind. Is that just something you say to get more of a reaction from the fans? You couldnt really be blind The End says nothing, he just removes his dark sunglasses and opens his eyes wide to show Yuri that there are no real optical devices there. Just big empty pools of white with an incredible small, nearly undetectable black dot in the center. If Im not blind, then theres something weird with how I can see without any of the devices necessary for it. They were burned out from the inside because of a tracking implant I received working for an American wrestling company. An old woman in a blue Japanese gown comes up to The End. He says something in Japanese to the woman. She responds, and they talk back and forth. The woman looks and points over to the camera. The End points off in some direction, and the woman shakes her head and leaves. Derek: What was that? The End: I ordered some food. I said that you wouldnt want anything they serve here, but if they could find you a Coke youd drink that. Shes sending her grandson to fetch one. Derek: How nice. The End turns his attention back to Yuri, ready to ask her next question, her pen resting on her ear under her dyed sandy-brown hair. Yuri: You won your match on the last Ikusa card, and now you must face Kensuke Suzahara and The HIT. HIT has quite a varied and dangerous offense and Suzahara brutally beat the owner of the company. What do you think are your chances in this match? Rather good, really, The End says. The HIT trained me I was his student in a wrestling school, Primetime Wrestling Academy, for about a week, give a few days. I trained with him, and have watched him wrestle, so I believe Im competent enough in what he can do. As for Suzahara, weve gotten into verbal spats before, but this shall be our first match together. Frankly, Im surprised hes settling for the Junior Heavyweight Title match, Suzahara no doubt feels that he is already worthy of the Heavyweight Title. Yuri: Are you going to run away from your opponents in this match? Heh. No, I believe this one will be all action on my part from bell to bell. The old womans grandson gets in the way of the camera, giving Derek his Coke. Derek: Youre blocking the shot! Thats nice, thanks for the Coke. Move it! Derek calls over to The End. Derek: Yo, End! Get this guy out of the way, I cant give AOL footage of some Japanese guys stomach! The End calls over to the old womans grandson. He moves over to The End, who gives him the Yen that the Coke costs plus extra, and the grandson leaves. Yuri: And should you lose your match what are you going to do? Continue your quest for the belt? Find a tag partner and do that? Run back to PCW and cry? The End shakes his head. It certainly wont be the last time you see me. And I wont cry about it. Id most likely keep trying to win the title, as Ive not really any tag team prospects at this time. Yuri scribbles some more on her notepad. Yuri: I have no more questions at this time, but Ill run you down if I have any more. Very well. Ill be in town enjoying the sights until my match. Yuri: You do that. Yuri stands up, as does The End. They shake hands and Yuri leaves. The End sits back down as a plate of food is brought to him. Derek fades out the scene.
