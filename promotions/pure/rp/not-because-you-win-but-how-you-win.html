Not Because You Win...but How You Win...

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Not Because You Win...but How You Win... Have you ever looked at waterand noticed its clarity? It almost doesnt exist at some pointsyet if you place your hand in it, ripples form First theres nonexistencethen existence Peacethen chaos And it was all done by just your hand I think upon this theoryand I wonder if it applies to the art of fightingthe thing Ive given my entire life for. Placing a hand upon someone equals disharmonyyet never striking at all leads to peace? Not in this day and ageanarchy is dished out with the push of a button nowwith the pull of a triggerwith words Society and their advanced thoughtsyou can admire them for certain things, but for some reason they only see how they can tear down, not how they can build up. But in their eyestheir destruction is seen as construction. We are the revolution of wrestling in Japan! We are the new age! People can never deal with whats here and nowthey think it must be changed to suit the futuretheir future. The way wrestling is now in Japan is weakoldancientfutile! It must be changed! I remember a man who said change was neededand he took action upon the change needed. In his change he slaughtered millionsin the name of change Now I look upon a new Hitlerso to speak Just as a computer goes out of date, the software becomes incompatible! We are the upgrade that has been long overdue! Just another set of radicalsthinking their way is rightwhen theres nothing wrong at all. WE ARE WE ARETHE FUTURE OF JAPANESE WRESTLI---. Mina de Margo held the remote level, aimed at the television set. She was dressed in a pair of gray drawstring pants and a white tank top, a black bandana wrapped around her black and red hair. She glared on at the TV set, her brow wrinkled in disapproval. I cant believe these self-righteous bastards. She remarked as she put the remote down. Theyre talking like theyre the second coming of Christianity or something! I sat next to her, my arms folded across my chest. I was in black bondage pants, and a black long sleeved mock tee. With eyes closed, I still paid attention to the dialogue the man spoke. Thats the path theyre takingand were not the one to say if its right or wrong. So youre content to sit back and let them run over us? Mina asked, turning towards him with her own arms folded. I shrugged. I am not the person to dictate anothers actions. If they think they can do such things, then let them think that. I will just continue on with my own actions. She shook her head. And what if those actions conflict with theirs? I didnt respond right awayjust a bit of silence. Then we shall see whose convictions are stronger. Mina smiled at me, that glint in her eyes Ive seen before. I thought so. Sometimes you should just say youre not going to stand for something instead of beating around the bush. If youre going to fight the power, go ahead! Sigh came from me You should know me better than anyone else, so you should automatically know my positions on things. Yeah, I do, but sometimes other people dont! she exhaled deeply, preparing for an explanation. Look, I was watching Ali Khadaffi on TV the other day. Why? I asked, quite sincere with the question. Because I scout your opponents. I watch their promos to see what they have to say and what their motives are. Hmph, was all I said thenI usually didnt pay much attention to my opponents promos. I just watched them in combatand that was it. Promos didnt really seem effective to methey seemed scripted much of the time and fake. If you are content with listening to cursing and negative talk, then that is your prerogative. Mina shook her head, her eyes lowering down to her feet. Noits not always about that. Most of it is. The ones that I looked at were nothing but rudeness, vulgarity, and unmotivated animosity against people who have done them no wrong. Therefore I stopped paying attention. Anyway she said, trying to jump out of the lecture. I was watching it and Ali has a real problem with you! I deciphered that from the attack last week. If that sounded drollit was. Yeahsurewhatever. As I was saying, Ali has this whole recognition deal with you and the other Puro stars. He thinks that just because he comes in and wins matches that he deserves to be the leader around here. He thinks he deserves the fans, and not you, or End or the other guys. Have you ever seen high school jocks? I asked her out of the blue. She stared at me with a surprised look before nodding. Yes. Welltheyre no different that what you saw there. There are certain people who just live by the code that winning is everything, and if youre not a winner, youre a loser. He thinks of himself as a winner, but is becoming jealous because hes not being recognized. What he needs to learn is that there really never is a real win or losspeople do not cheer because you win, they cheer because of HOW you win. Its a very simple theory that most cannot comprehend. Mina nodded her head, like she finally realized something. Iknow that. He also has that problem with the whole thing that youre metaphorical and such. My shoulders shrugged. Theres nothing I can do if hes not able to understand what I say. I must be getting the point across if there are so many fans Mina giggled at him. Thats what Im saying. We cant please everyone, now can we? I was never interested in pleasing anyoneI just wanted to train. Mina gave him a blank stare. Dont rock the boat, Blitzy. I shook my head as I handed her the remote. In any caseIm going to the training hall for meditation. Enjoy your viewing. She watched me as I got up and leftI could feel her eyes upon my back. I would feel the eyes of all those people in Ikusa Halltheyd be locked on mewatching me, and cheering for mejust because of how I win not because I just win With that said and done, the Black Dragon Master has spoken.
