A Rhyme for YOU!

29 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A Rhyme for YOU! Yo, DJ Enygma I want you to get some of the boys and hit me up with a fine freestyle. I know you got mad rhymes, you think you can do it. Word! Klakkety, klakkety, klakkety, klakkety, klakkety, klakkety, klakkety, klakkety, klik. NO SHIT! And it started right there A blind stare Going to the back of my head Looked around felt the sound Surprised I wasnt actually dead From that moment I knew life was more than fun I always carried a gun Fuck friends cause I had none. There was one man He was close to my heart When he left my side It was like a ricochet dart He didnt mean it He could never of seen it He thought it was all games His opponent didnt think the same He was lame. He was so damn young I woulda ripped out your tongue But you shied away You shifted the blame And now its time for you to scream my name. brrrum-de-rum, brrrum-de-rum, brrrum-de-rum, brrrum-de-rum. Im Nygma, trigger loving, never running, keepin the beat, so light on my feet, the maddest DJ on the street. Im Nygma, Mr. N, the perfect ten, a man of all men, so cool, no fool, I use my hands as my tools. brrrum-de-rum, brrrum-de-rum, brrrum-de-rum, brrrum-de-rum. How about that dog? Its tight. Use it tomorrow come club time and youll be the new god of Tokyo. Shit man, I AM THE GOD OF TOKYO. Whatever you say, Ill see you tomorrow, for now close everything down and get some sleep. Youll need it. The night progresses and just as Nygma gets ready to go home something happens. *Glass shatters* What the fuck! Nygma goes to the front room and notices a rock on the floor and shattered glass all around it. It has a small paper attached and in dark letters it says. Stay away from PURE, you people are no good. Ill show them, Ill show them real soon.
