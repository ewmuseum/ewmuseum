Get 'It'....Or Die Trying

11 January 2003

Get 'It'....Or Die Trying Written: 1-11-2003 'In other news...Mang-A-Hero will be returning to the ring this Sunday for the big fan festival and introduction show 'PURE Axcess'...'
Ryoko Ranbu Mang-A-Hero....it's almost Sunday already and your filled with a jumble of emotions you've NEVER had before. It's like for every emotion you feel, your feeling EXACTLY the polar opposite. Your excited....but your insanely nervous...your confident....but your filled with so much doubt....you feel no pain...but it travels with you constantly. This Sunday...after six months of laying on the sidelines and watching so many do what you love so much, you return to the ring. To the think six months ago that you took the gift to dazzle the crowd for granted. Six months ago, you would slice through the air with a moonsault rivaled by few, making it look like child's play...
Six months ago...you felt no pain and you needed no aid. Six months later, your hearing this... 'You'll be able to walk comfortably and run with a little hinderance...but you HAVE to wear this protective knee brace. It's going to take quite awhile for this kind of injury to heal properly.'
You look down at your right knee...and what now lies there...a thick, black knee brace that HARDLY compliments a spandex Gundam Wing replica suit. All jokes aside, this brace is now a permenant fixture of you. Who would have thought that you couldn't even CRAWL INTO BED without the slightest naunce of pain without it. This brace is a permenant crutch...but then again, it was your only way of ever touching the ring.
It's your gift, yet your never-ending curse. You always contemplate whether you took one risk too many...one move too far....an unnecessary bump that was never needed. Then you remember, you didn't care what moves you did...just as long as you were popping the crowd...as long as you were hearing your name exclaimed from the lips of thousands of PUREists.
'Your a big star playa, I'd never think about just cutting you loose becauseyour injured. How about a nice comfortable announce spot? Sounds damn sweet huh?'
It wasn't winning the PURE Tag Team titles with Inphino Blitz in the Sapparo Dome....it wasn't the rush you felt coming down the aisle, slapping dozens of hands on your way to the ring....but it was close to the action. You were content sitting at the announce position, calling the exciting brand of PURE matches with your own little style. I mean, who could resist you saying 'GREAT DESTRUCO DISKS OF KRILLAIN' after an especially awesome move? You sat at the announce table....dormant...sedentary...calm. But as you witness the young stars of PURE pulling off death-defying maneuvers and wowing the crowd...what was all you could say?
'I can do better.' You just couldn't be content with just sitting at the table in-between the obnoxious Mat W. and the beautiful Hitomi Asami and taking easy money could you? You couldn't just wait a few years until your knee was FULLY healed and then possibly try again...older, yet in better condition. You couldn't just watch others outdo you as you had to sit back and CALL their amazing moves could you?
No.... It may sound selfish, but all you yearned and crave was the sound of the fans... 'MANG-A!! MANG-A!!! MANG-A!!! MANG-A!!' Their voices never escaped your head. Just like the last song you hear before you leave the house, it stayed with you all this time and never let go. It grew inside you until one day....you were willing to do whatever it took to step between the ropes just ONE...MORE...TIME.
Now you have a clunky knee brace attached to your right knee and your sitting, thinking hard at the edge o your bed with your mask tight to your face. You took the risk of coming back....you even came back against quite possibly the most dangerous force in PURE...Enmity.
He's broken countless names in PURE. Ended careers before their prime. Turned people against the business after dealings with him. Scared even the most hardened.
But it's not Enmity that scares you....it's finding out that you still don't have 'it'. The intangible.... The X-Factor... The special 'something' that makes you that much better, stronger, faster, charismatic....then the guy across from you.
You cut off the Dragonball Z Krillain-head lamp that stands tall on your nightstand and recline to a horizantal position on your bed....your thoughts running a mile a minute as you realize you have two choices tomorrow, when you make your return outside PURE Studios.
Get 'it' quick.... Or die trying. Go Go Tokyo....Go Go Tokyo.
