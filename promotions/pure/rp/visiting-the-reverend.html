Visiting The Reverend.

24 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Visiting The Reverend. Jordan X Shabazz had been spending most of his time in Dallas, Texas working at NAACP headquarters. And basically, the only time he left was when he needed sleep, and when he needed to travel for the PCW. But now, he's back in Japan, if only for a short time. Shabazz was back in Japan for one reason, and one reason alone, to visit The Reverend Jesse Kintei. Our scene opens up on the outskirts of Tokyo, Japan. We are at the Tokyo General Hospital, or at least outside of it. The moon shines a little bit of light onto the streets outside the hospital, where relatives of injured people are walking hurriedly into the hospital. Jordan X Shabazz sits in the backseat of a taxi, pondering whether or not he should enter the hospital. Whether or not he could take seeing another Civil Rights Activist, such as himself, lying in a hospital bed. Should I go up there? To check on that cat? I guess in a way it is my fault that Ali Khadafi made him his 'Fuck rag', I should have run down the ramp sooner... But then again, it was his idea to come to Japan in the first place... But then again, all he wanted to do was support another famous Negro... Jordan gives the taxi driver in the front seat some money and he opens the door. He slowly gets out, taking in the fresh Japanese atmosphere. Jordan is wearing baggy blue Phat Farm jeans, some white old school Nikes, a large Yellow And 1 basketball t-shirt, and his trademark Toronto Raptors NBA Basketball baseball cap. Worn backwards. Jordan quickly walks into the Hospital and begins searching for room 666. He walks up to the front desk and immediately feels out of place. Everybody in the Hospital that is visible is Japanese. 'Hello? May I help you?' A secretary who speaks in broken English asks Jordan with intent. Jordan takes a minute before he comprehends the question, when he understands it fully, he answers. 'Yes, I'm looking for room 666,' replies Jordan without changing his facial expression whatsoever. 'Are you friend or family? Because visiting hours over. Only family allowed in now,' the secretary tells Jordan. 'Family.' The secretary nods as she beckons Jordan to follow her by waving him over. She walks quickly up six flights of stairs, with Jordan following close behind. When they reach the top of the stairs, the secretary looks at Jordan. She points towards the end of the hallway 'Go right there, that room.' Jordan nods and politely says thank you in Japanese to the lady. She walks back down the stairs as Jordan begins his short trek to the room where his newfound friend, and fellow Civil Rights Activist, Jesse Kintei, lies. Jordan peers in through the window. He's asleep. Jordan slowly opens the door, careful not to wake up the sleeping Reverend. His face is battered and bruised. His nose is heavily taped; staples and stitches line his forehead. Ali Khadafi did a number on him at Ikusa III, and the after affects are evident on his face. One of his hands appears to be broken as well, perhaps from fighting off Ali Khadafi in the ring... Or at least trying to fight him off. Jordan looks at Jesse, and he thinks of Khadafi. 'Final Destinies will be the final destination for Ali Khadafi... You mess with a nigga's homeboys, and you end up with a bullet in yo' frame,' mutters the angry Civil Rights Extremist. 'But before then, I've got Ikusa IV, another place to get some retribution... Payback's a bitch... Payback's a bitch.' The scene fades to black with a shot of Jordan X Shabazz staring at the almost lifeless body of Jesse Kintei, while thinking of Ali Khadafi and PURE.
