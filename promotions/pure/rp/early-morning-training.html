Early Morning Training

28 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Early Morning Training -/Today is a new day...and a new day brings a new adventure for the master of manga...MANG-A-HERO!! When we last left off in his exploits, our hero had informed his tag team partner and roomie, Inphino Blitz, that they would be going through a grueling training session the upcoming day to prepare for their battle against Ali and Carl Kimball...the two men standing between the unlikely duo of the Black Dragon Master and The Drunken Kool-Aid Master from advancing in the first round of the PURE tag team title tournament. Inphino Blitz seemed to have taken the news of a 'training session' led by Mang-A-Hero with a grain of salt and went to bed easy that night...but the past is the past and today is the day Mang-A-Hero had spoken of...the day of the training session he would lead to make the two stronger as a team and as individual wrestlers and warriors of PURE. But at the moment, Inphino is having no thoughts of training or battling as he lays comfortably in his wooden bed, the soft silk covers neatly over his body as he sleeps in utter peace, a peace that could only come to The Black Dragon Master seeing as how the man is so enlightened and well in tune with life...and life allowed him to rest with no signs of tossing, turning, or waking up....the perfect slumber we all try to achieve when we lay to rest. The peace however, looks to be in serious jeopardy as we look to find Mang-A-Hero right around the corner of his door frame, Inphino a non-believer of doors in his dojo with our hero taking full advantage of this. He quietly creeps to bed side of his tag team partner, donning a full body replica costume of Kamen Rider Zo(The Masked Rider) and the red and green spandex material hugging his body making slight high-pitched scrunches and squeaks until he finally is at the edge of his bed. Our hero looks down over the sleeping body of Blitz, so peaceful in his slumber you could mistaken him for a corpse, Mang-A-Hero looking to the camera with a smile as he speaks with a cheesy, announcer sounding voice to the audience-/ Mang-A-Hero:'Hello kimosabi's and kimosabettes....as you can see, our good friend Blitz seems to have forgotten about my training session and now, YOUR SAMURAI gets the pleasure of waking his tired hiney up! Now if you think your a true MANG-A-MANIAC(tilde pending) why don't you use your Ranma Senses to figure out what I'm about to do next! Will I....' A) Leave him to rest until he is ready to get up and just not train, whereas I'll go play a spirited game of Shinobi 2. B) Quietly tap him or whisper in his ear and request for him to get up, and while he washes up....play a spirited game of Shinobi 2 or C) Comedically wake him up by connecting my Game Boy Advance to a set of dangerously loud amps and blasting the theme from Shinobi 2....while playing a spirited game of Shinobi 2. Mang-A-Hero:'If you said 'C' then GO GO TOKYO FOR YOU!! Give yourself a pat on the back and consider yourself a MANG-A-MANIAC!! Now where am I gonna get loudspeakers from...must you forget that this is a Mang-A-Hero RP...logic is an afterthought!!' *Throws a cheesy peace sign as anime graphics engulf the background a'la Pokemon* -/Our hero quickly exits the room and returns, hauling in two amps and laying them down on the floor. He then takes a plug and connects it into the back of his 'Liquid Crystal' colored Game Boy Advance...looking to the audience with a smile as he pops in Shinobi 2 and holds the tiny speaker to the ear of the Black Dragon Master He shoots a wink before cutting sliding the 'On' switch, shortly followed by the incredibly loud blaring of Japanese techo emitted from the Game Boy Advance, the decibels of the music so high that the Game Boy Advance shakes violently in the grasp of Mang-A-Hero. Upon the music invading the ears of Blitz, he shoots up out of the bed and hits the floor with a thud, holding his head and back in pain upon rising up as our hero struggles for the 'On' switch, the room rumbling violently due to the music until he finally sldies it off, the onslaught on Blitz's ears finally over. His tag team partner stands to his feet, incredibly annoyed by Mang-A-Hero's method of waking him up as he does not exclaim...but takes a stern tone with our hero as he says-/ Blitz:'Mang-A...do you have SOME explanation as to WHY your in my room early in the morning, blasting that music in my ears? Is your goal to give me a nervous breakdown or a heart attack before the end of this partnership?' Mang-A-Hero:'Oh pish-posh you silly Black Dragon Master you...we have more important things to worry about. I told you yesterday that we would be training so we could battle the forces of evil that await us on the next Ikusa, that evil being the faces of Ali and Carl Kimball! We've got millions of screaming Dragonites and just as many Mang-A-Maniacs expecting victory from us and who are we to deny them that sight!! That is why today we must train to the fullest and hardest in order to bring that evil Carl Kimbell and Ali to their knees and have them face the justice of the dragon and the punishment of a good BACKSMACK ATTACK!! They are the first roadblock that stands in our way of making claim to the PURE tag team titles, and we shall not let them stand in our way partner...for when roadblocks stand before us, we break them down with our combined powers of the SUPER KAMEHAMEHAIN!! Now get yourself washed up and in gear mister...we have training to do!! I shall await you downstairs...AWAY I GO!!' -/Our hero does a traditional bow Oriental bow to Inphino Blitz, following that with a silly little two step dance before sprinting downstairs...Inphino simply plopping on his bed and looking to his window, the first rays of the morning sun striking his face as he says to himself aloud, flexing his ripped arms while doing so-/ Inphino Blitz:'What does not kill you makes you stronger I would venture to say...but at times you have to wonder if the strength is really worth the torture...alas...that is the life of Inphino Blitz...' -/Inphino Blitz rises from the bed and begins to make it up neatly...his training to begin as soon as he is ready.....-/ Later... -/The sun is in full bloom like a gorgeous sun flower in a garden of the greenest grass, it's rays seeping into the bottom level of Inphino Blitz's dojo. Inphino's dojo is fully loaded with martial arts weaponry, training dummies, and other equipment...making it the perfect training facility for any warrior honing his skills. We now find our hero and the Black Dragon Master, who is now within black karate gi bottoms, kung-fu shoes, and wearing a solid black slash with a white dragon on the loose end of the sash. He stands quietly, allowing Mang-A-Hero to lead the way in this training session as our hero stands tall, a red bandanna wrapped around his head with a Poke' Ball in the center as he looks to Blitz, feigning seriousness as he says-/ Mang-A-Hero:'Now Blitz...let us begin what should be a grueling training session...but first, beign we are partners...we gotta have the ONE thing Carl Kimball and Ali have no sembelance of...teamwork and TRUST!! So what I DEMAND Blitz is that you display your trust in me...' *Our hero runs behind Blitz and holds his arms out* 'Lay back and allow your hero catch you...an OBVIOUS sign of trust from you to me right?' Blitz:'I suppose Mang-A...' *sigh* 'Alright here I go..' -/The Black Dragon Master leans back, the awaiting arms of Mang-A-Hero right there for him as he elevates on his heels, slanting in mid-air. Finally, Blitz falls over...but suddenly, a electronic ring overtakes the dojo, Mang-A-Hero moving out the way as he whips out a tiny yellow cell phone. Blitz rips through the air and hits the wood floor with a hard !!!SMACK!!! as Mang-A-Hero flips the face on his phone and puts his ear to the reciever-/ Mang-A-Hero:'Hello?' -/Our hero is suddenly barraged by a female voice on the phone, our super samurai backing his ear away from the phone and gritting his teeth nervously as the inaudible yelling continues, Mang-A-Hero finally replying when silence overtakes the phone-/ Mang-A-Hero:'Well...yes I know Amy...I did tell you I was going to find some milk...and believe you mean, it's a BIZNITCH to find! I'm looking as we speak...I know it's been a few days now, but that milk is important. It's not like I'm in another country or nothing...' *laughs nervously as more inaudible yelling continues* 'WAIT MAY YOU...FZZZZZTZTZTZTZTZTZT...YOUR BREAKING...FZZZTZTZTZTZTZ...UH OH, GOTTA GO!!' -/Mang-A-Hero promptly hangs up the phone after coping out from his conversation with his 'significant other' and immediately begins to remove the battery from the phone in fear, looking down at the fallen Inphino Blitz and saying-/ Mang-A-Hero:'Piece of advice...NEVER say to your red-headed girlfriend your going to the grocery store as an excuse to take a flight to Japan. And that fall back thing was just a practice...can we get a do-over?' *smiles nervously* Blitz:'This is gonna be a loooooong session...' Mang-A-Hero:'CHEER UP BLITZ...WE HAVEN'T EVEN GOTTEN TO THE TAE BO WORKOUT YET!! AND DON'T EVEN LET ME GET STARTED ON POKE'MON PUSH-UPS!!' -/With all that said...Inphino Blitz lacksadazilly stares at the camera with a 'why me?' look as Mang-A-Hero drags him off further down the dojo, no telling as to what other silly 'training antics' our hero has set up...but will it be ebough for Inphino and Mang-A to have enough of a repore as a team to advance in the tourney?! GO GO NOW TOKYO...AND FIND OUT THE SAME MANG-A-TIME, SAME MANG-A-SHOW!!!
