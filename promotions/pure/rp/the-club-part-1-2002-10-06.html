The Club (part 1)

6 October 2002

The Club (part 1) Written: 6th, october (For Ikusa) The Japanese nightlife was an experience like no other; the streets are a buzz with people and the roads were jammed pack with traffic tailing back as far as the eye can see. A minicab pulls up on the sidewalk as the back doors swing open out steps Ric & Johnny, two men that form one of the hot new teams in the wrestling organisation known as PURE. Ric makes his way to the driver’s window and hands the driver what seems to be a small amount of yen, the driver nods his head and rolls back off into the overflowing congestion of Tokyo’s busy streets.
Johnny: So my man where we headed for tonight? Johnny brushes of his black jeans and adjusts the collar of his white polo shirt, trying to make himself look as sharp as possible for tonight he was out to impress.
Ric: Well before we left the hotel, that cute little secretary told me of a club just down the street it’s called the ‘Boom Boom Room!’ I thought we might go and show face, as I heard it was a hot spot for single chicks.
Ric who was adorned in a black shirt and black pants, looking well groomed as always lead the way to aforementioned club the ‘Boom Boom Room! The two made there way down the eventful street absorbing the energy that was flowing through the air, this was a new experience for the two men. A new country and a whole different lifestyle they had to adapt to, would it be easy? Time will tell.
The two carried on down the street but could see no sign of their selected destination. Johnny looked around frantically, he needed to find this club, he needed to get laid because that’s the way the he thinks of himself, as a genuine ‘Casanova’, what did the ladies think of that self assessment, well ask him tomorrow morning. He looked about until he spotted a little gang of Japanese teens laughing and joking walking in the direction of him and his comrade.
Johnny: err…excuse me, do-you-speaky-Englishy?!? Japanese teen1: 私を許しなさい (Excuse me) Johnny: Club ‘Boom Boom Room’? Japanese teen2: 何をこの愚か者は, 狂気のアメリカ人約継続しているか. (What’s this dude going on about, crazy Americans eh?) The little teen gang let out a snigger at the boys’ remark, Ric steps up and into the kids face.
Ric: Something funny chump!?! The kids form a circle around the two as Ric and the teen stand face to face, Johnny now a bit weary of his situation stands back to back with Ric and softly whispers in his ear…
Johnny: Come on dude this is their turf lets just walk away, while we still can. Ric: I guess your right; we’ll find the club on our own who needs these dickless kids anyways.
Ric and Johnny make their way out through a hole in the circle that the gang had formed and walked off down the street, they can hear the teens laughing and chatting, strolling off in the other direction.
Johnny: Do you see that? Ric: What? Johnny: That sign, it looks like…wait I need to get closer. Johnny walks a few steps more before turning back to Ric with a smile curling his lips. Ric: Well what is it? Johnny: That my friend is the ‘Boom Boom Room’! Ric: Well what are we standing around here for lets go. The two make their way to the entrance of the club they move to go through the big glass panelled doors but are stopped by a bald Japanese man of a pretty imposing size, he is dressed in a silk black suit with a white dragon crest displayed on the breast of the pocket and is holding a clipboard in his large left paw.
Bouncer: Where u fink you’s go? Ric: Huh? Us, we’re just going to have a little look inside if that’s ok with you? Bouncer: Names Johnny: We are the infamous team known the world over as Nu Era, I’m Johnny Quinn and this is my amigo Ric Montana.
The two stand side by side and pose in front of bouncer who looks on in a confused manner. He raises the clipboard and searches up and down the slip of paper attached to it, before looking back at the two.
Bouncer: You’s names not on list, you’s no go in! Ric and Johnny look at each other contemplating what the large man denying access them to the club had said. Ric’s eyes opened widely as you could just picture a small light bulb pop above his muffled blonde hair.
Ric: Well good sir, I think I have something that might persuade you into becoming interested in some sort of agreement.
Ric produces a large amount of yen from his rear pocket, strides up to the towering man and places the cash in the behemoths inside pocket. He gently slaps the bouncer in the face three times and winks before stepping back. The bouncer looks at his pocket and then back at the two, he steps aside allowing the two entry to the notorious ‘Boom Boom Room.’
Ric and Johnny high five each other and ascend of into the club, both with ample grins aptly plastered on their faces.
