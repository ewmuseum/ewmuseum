Ultra Violet Shining Light Alpha- IV

21 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ultra Violet Shining Light Alpha- IV Written: Tuesday, the 21st of January. Narrated by Minori Kumako. Yukio Kunamara is one of the most evil, and despicable human beings alive. Akoshi and Yukio are about three years apart, but they’re completely different people. Yukio runs the drug market in the South-Western business district of Tokyo and Osaka, and owning several adult striptease night clubs. Akoshi often told me that Yukio inherited his father, Masanori Kunamara’s mean spirit and black heart. His slick greasy tied-hair and thin moustache a common feature on his black suits. Yukio always gives off the image that you don’t know what he’ll do, or where he’ll be next. Akoshi and I have wondered how he has escaped Police notification for so long, but our main guess is that when he is being wanted for, he hides inside of Aikawa Island’s many hidden cabins. Akoshi and Yukio have never really got along, since they’re both completely different people.
Growing up, Yukio wasn’t interested in the same Martial-arts training his siblings were taught, opting to learn the deadly art of Ninjutsu. Akoshi, like the rest of his brothers, was taught Jujitsu. I think in a fistfight, Yukio could beat Akoshi!
Anyway, Akoshi stepped out of the taxi, cracking his knuckles in case there was a fight. The doors to the black sedan parked behind this taxi opened, three large men and a smaller man stepped out. Akoshi glared at the smallest of the group of four, daggers appearing in his eyes. Akoshi walked forward at the group of sharply dressed men.
‘What do you want, Yukio?’ Akoshi said with a frown, and flexing his muscles hidden underneath a layer of clothes. Akoshi has never been afraid of a fight, but I didn’t think even he could win this fight, if there was one. Akoshi clenched his fists tighter, whitening the knuckles as much as he could. ‘I’m not kidding, what the hell do you want with me, Yukio?’
Yukio Kunamara chuckled. Not a friendly chuckle, but a quite sinister chuckle. He straightened his tie on his black suit and took a step forward. He walked as close as Akoshi would let him, only a few centimetres from touching Akoshi’ nose with his own. ‘What I want with measly brother?’ he said in broken English. ‘HAHAHA! It simple, brother.’
Akoshi blinked. ‘You haven’t changed a bit,’ he said to Yukio, letting go of his clenched fists. Yukio smirked, but his brother just remained straight faced, keeping his eyes on the three large men accompanying Yukio. Yukio took a step forward again, but he didn’t attack Akoshi one bit. Yukio wrapped his arms around his younger brother and hugged him tightly.
This all seemed very strange to me. Yukio and Akoshi and are never like this around each other, or so Akoshi told me all those months ago. Yukio still had that superior smirk on his face like always, acting as if nobody in the world could touch him. When he released the hug on Akoshi, he snickered, making my fiancé frown. Akoshi’s head turned slightly, turning around to look at me in the car, but I wish we hadn’t.
Yukio suddenly leapt forward, striking Akoshi in the stomach with a Knee-Strike. Akoshi was completely caught off guard. I gasped, horrified that Yukio had gained Akoshi’s trust and betrayed him so quickly. The two large men that accompanied the slick dressed Yukio sprung into action, clubbing Akoshi in the back of the neck with their arms several times. Yukio stood behind them, laughing to himself in his high pitched, snide laugh. The larger of the two men tried to Karate Chop Akoshi in the stomach, which could be a fatal blow if done right.
Inside of the taxi, I bite my lip softly. I kept true to his word and didn’t leave the taxi at all, despite all the emotions in my heart wanting to leap out the door and hit them all in the face. Alas, I didn’t. I kept hidden; watching through my hands guarding my eyes, just as a foot hit Akoshi in the stomach. Yukio continued to snicker, shouting snide comments to his fallen brother.
‘You… you haven’t changed one bit!’ Akoshi said to his brother, after blocking a hard punch from one of the large bodyguards. Akoshi suddenly fought his way out of their rampant punches, striking the largest of the bodyguards in the temple. The other bodyguard was a lot harder for him to fight off. ‘Why did I ever trust you, again!?’ he bellowed whilst dodging a side-kick. Akoshi quickly turned to the only remaining bodyguard, and kicked him squarely in the throat. I watched from the shadows of the backseat of the taxi as the bodyguard fell, watching Akoshi’s expressionless face when the giant of a man hit the ground, blood drooling out of his mouth.
‘Please don’t let him have killed someone,’ I muttered to myself, ducking my head down a little to wipe a tear in my eye. I cleared my eyes of the salty water and looked up, only in time to see Yukio spring forward and attack his younger brother with a lead-tipped shoe. Akoshi fell to the ground in a heap, blood starting to appear from his nose. I could feel tears running down my cheek again. ‘NO! AKOSHI!’ I screamed from inside the Taxi, watching while Yukio picked up my fallen fiancé.
Yukio snickered and looked at me wickedly, opening the door with a lot of force, and throwing Akoshi onto the backseat with me. I immediately cradled his head in my hands, smearing the blood off his face and wiping it on my own pants. Yukio climbed into the front seat, laughing to himself.
‘Time to go home, brother!’
