The tides are turning... and so is my breakfast!!!

5 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The tides are turning... and so is my breakfast!!! Yuka sits and drinks her water as Tecno stretches out for the day's training. He just got back from America after buying EWA from the CEO, and looks worn out from the trip. Nevertheless, he must train his protogé Yuka. As she reads the local paper, she looks up to find Tecno has fell asleep while doing the splits!!! Yuka throws a paper cup at him, making him do some type of breakdance not invented yet. 'I wasn't sleeping...' 'I don't care what you say, your sleeping.' 'Okay...' Tecno thens collapses and begins to drift off in the cold, dingy gym of Akita City. Yuka didn't even want to come here, but Tecno made her. He does look cute sleeping... but so does Yuka. She goes back to drinking her drink, and notices a copy of the day's newspaper. Looking down, she notices a familiar face on the front page. It's her dad. He's just been elected mayor of Akita City!!! 'Oh god...' 'wha... I'M UP!!! DON'T LEAVE ME DADDY!!!' 'what are you doing?' 'sorry... what ya reading?' 'My dad's just been elected Mayor of Akita City.' 'Oh right... well were in...' 'Akita City. You insisted we come here, remember?' 'Yeah, well I was probably high on Pepsi at the time.' 'Look, there's a gym in Tokyo. Besides, that's not only where the next show is but it's also where you can go straight home afterwards. Isn't that better?' '*sighs* Fine... we'll go.' 'I'll drive' 'Whatever.' And so, off to Tokyo they go. Why should they go to Tokyo Tecno would never know, but Yuka can't be in the same town as that jerk... her father. A cold-blooded killer who has no remorse for anything. Just thinking about him makes Yuka shiver with fear and rage. Tecno just falls asleep in the back as Yuka drives on. The reoccuring thoughts go through her head... what will father do to her? What will happen? And who is she facing at Isuki? If only Tecno would tell her... Time flies... thoughts pass... and Porno Star by Sads comes onto the radio. Well, at least we know what theme tune she wants. LATER IN TOKYO 'Hey! Wake up' It's 6AM, and they've arrived at Yuka's appartment. Tecno still looks visably tired, as well as dying for the toilet. He and Yuka head inside, and after the toilet, checking the mail, feeding the dog (robo-kind) and unloading the car, Yuka and Tecno go to their rooms and sleep. Sleep, sleep, sleep... until 5:30PM that is. 'Yuka! Wake up! We've gotta train!!!' 'Uhhh... can't we just...' 'No! Come on!!!' 'God... for someone who doesn't train a lot you certainly like to push me...' Off to the gym they go. As they get ready to train, Tecno let's Yuka in on who she'll be fighting at Isuki. 'Listen up. You'll be fighting in a Fatal Four-Way Match against Jordan X Shazbaat, The End and The Hit. The winner goes and fights for the PURE Asian Title sometime. The next show I think. Anyway, point is: this is an important match. Not only is it your PURE Debut, but it's for the 2nd top title in PURE. Now, obviously winning is going to matter. BUT, what matters more is that win, lose or draw, you get the respect of the fans and the locker room. If you don't win, then no biggy. As long as you hold your own and show that you can wrestle and do it competantley. I'll be proud of you no matter what. Well, unless you began to strip and start screwing in the ring, cause that'd just be stupid...' 'I get it. Thankyou. So, any tips?' 'Ermm... don't lose.' 'Seriously!!!' 'I'd of thought not losing is serious...' 'Tom...' 'Alright... I've taken tips on these guys and to tell you honestly...it'll be tough.' 'As if it would be easy!' 'Just make sure you're ready. I'm going to teach you some good techniques for a Fatal Four-Way Match that I've learnt over the years...' 'Okay. Shoot.' 'Stay out of their way, and they're all incapacitated go for the pin.' '...' Yuka just stares, as if it's a joke. 'What?' 'You think staying out of the way is going to work?' 'It has done for me in the past...' ' *sighs* ...where's this gym?' 'Oh, well were going to go to Inphino Blitz's gym. It has everything we'll need.' 'Inphino Blitz? Hmm...' 'Heard of him?' 'Vagley.' ' *pfff* ' 'Your on the Charged roster, right? In EWC I mean.' 'Yeah. But so is my new tag-team partner Mathers.' 'Ah... but of course. Team FSW.' 'That's Team 5Star Wrestling to you, thankyou! All we're looking for now is a manager.' 'Well good for you. What type of guy is Inphino like... is he good looking?' 'I dunno, see for yourself. Besides, that's not important. What's important is that you hold your own against...' 'Yeah, yeah... Inphino: is he good looking?' 'Oddly enough, I don't pay much attention to those details.' 'He's ugly isn't he...' 'Oh for god sakes! You decide. I don't usually look at men like that...' 'But you do it to the women!' 'There's a difference: women turn me on, not men.' 'Fine... we'll see if he is.' 'Typical...' And so, off to Inphino's gym they go. As they arrive, they notice the man himself working out. Tecno pulls Yuka along and begins to speak to the Black Dragon Master... 'Yo! Inphino! How you doing?' 'Decent...are you well?' Inphino stares at the woman for a second blankly, then he bows his head towards her. 'Nice to meet you.' Yuka blushes as the dazzling Inphino bows. 'So, how's life been treating you?' 'Life does not treat me. It is a path that I must follow until my training is complete. That...is my life.' Tecno raises his eybrow at Inphino. 'Good.' Yuka speaks up. 'So, Inphino. I heard your a good wrestler. Is there any ladies in your life?' Inphino gives her one of his melancholy stares. NoI have never taken pleasure in the physical lusts that exist in society. People tend to make love a physical thing more than an emotional thing, and I find that degrading. It gives a bad name to loveand then people will grow to hate love and urge their lusts more. Then he sighs, looking away from Yuka. Of course that is only my opinion... It is no way a threat towards your former career. To answer your question, I possess talent...there is nothing about me that makes me good.' Yuka looks on mesmorised, while Tecno looks confused and says so. 'So that's a no, then?' Inphino sighs again. 'Indeed...that is a no.' 'Right...' Yuka speaks again. 'I hear your quite an acomplised champion. Tell me, are you going to try for the Asian Title? Only you'd have to fight me for it. And that would be a shame if you lost to a girl.' The Black Dragon Master shrugs his shoulders at the question. Whichever direction the black wind blows me in, I will go there. If it is in that direction to cross paths with you, so be it. Tecno continues to look confused. In an attempt to save his brain from over thinking, he whispers in Yuka's ear. 'We've got to go. But it was nice meeting you Inphino. I wish you luck in your future endevours.' 'Yes...good luck.' Tecno immediately realises something. 'Er... Inphino. We kinda need a place to say... is it possible if we crash here? It's a safe place for her to stay while I'm in America...' Inphino sighs and waves his hand at them. 'So let it be.' Yuka bows to Inphino and immediately scatters off down the hall. Tecno raises his eyebrow and walks off after her. 'So, when shall we go into Tokyo Tom?' 'Um... were on Sado Island.' 'WHAT!?! You said...' 'I know, but I then I realised it was here.' 'oh... okay.' 'Look, let's just train. BTW, he get's up at Sunrise.' 'So?' 'So... does everyone else.' Tecno runs off as Yuka's eyes widen. She runs after him off to the gym... Tecno and Yuka train hard in the gym. Even Jordan X Shazbaat is there, checking out his competiton. Yuka trains hard, and looks similar to her films. Not nessecarily a good thing really, but nevermind. Night time draws closer, and they eventually retire for bed. Tecno gets in, and gets a strange feeling that he is in a convent... 'Don't be silly Tom. This is nice!' 'Yeah. I've always wanted to be in the Clu-Clux Clan...' 'Be quiet. Jordan is black!' 'He's also your opponent.' 'I can check him out!' 'You did that with Inphino... honestly. He's a good guy, but the stuff he comes out with! You need a flipping Enigma machine!' 'Goodnight Tom...' 'Yeah.' Yuka turns over, and rests for the night. Tecno carries on and stares into space. He looks into space, and asks himself: 'What was in that hamburger? Jesus! I feel like I ate a gorrilla with Diahorrea!' Hmm... they sleep for the night.
