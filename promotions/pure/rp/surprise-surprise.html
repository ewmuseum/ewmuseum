Surprise...Surprise... PURE Ikusa...April 14, 2002... Victory belongs to him... ...yet there's someone who thinks of himself to be the victor over him... Ali is his name... What does he think he can accomplish by attacking the Black Dragon Master from behind? Whatever his misguided ideas and morals may be...Inphino Blitz will have to put an end to his goals. Coming up on Ikusa II... Ali and Carl Kimbell...facing Inphino Blitz... '...and who?' 'Mang-A-Hero...his name is Mang-A-Hero.' '...' The scene opens upon the Black Dragon Master standing backstage on Sunday, the night of Ikusa. Dressed in his black wrestling attire, he leans forward against a wall with his hand holding the side of his head. Standing by with an icepack is Mina de Margo, dressed in her blue hip hugger jeans with red thong peeking out, white halter top underneath a red mesh long sleeved shirt, and a black bandana. She tries to attend to Inphino, but he holds his hand to her to keep her back. 'Where did Miyagawa find this...person?' Inphino asked, a bit shaken by the attack Ali placed upon him. Mina shrugged her shoulders as she placed the icepack on the bench nearby and folded her arms. 'I have no idea. But I heard that he's teaming you with this Mang-A-Hero so that you could compete in this PURE Tag Team title tournament. You're going to get your shot at that goon who attacked you, too...Ali...with his partner being Carl Kimbell.' Blitz looked up slightly at the mention of that name. 'Kimbell? Hmmm...' Mina watched as he stood upright again. Then she saw him head for the door of the locker room, grabbing his trench coat. She grabbed his arm, drawing his eyes towards her. 'Where do you think you're going...huh?' A sigh came from the Black Dragon Master. 'I am going to see Carl Kimbell.' 'Are you crazy??' Mina shook her head, realizing she asked a question with an obvious answer. 'Never mind, but you think you can just walk up to this guy and ask him whatever you feel like about him or Ali?' 'Why can I not? Is there some action that prohibits me from speaking with this man?' Mina shrugged again. 'Nothing...except for the fact that he's over 300 pounds and said to be a super tough son of a...' Blitz gave her a warning glance, snapping her back into place. '...whore.' 'Mina...' the stern gaze set upon her innocent face. 'It's true! His mother was a whore! I'd tell you to ask him, but knowing you, you'd go up to him and a huge fight would break out!' her face grows serious as she mimicked Inphino. 'Pardon me, Kimbell, but I have come to understand that your mother was of whorish proportions.' she turns to face where she was previously standing, hunching her shoulders up pretending to he Carl Kimbell, a ugly snarl on her face. 'I'm gonna break you, fortune cookie!! I grew up on the streets beating pigeons down that were tougher than you!!' Inphino gave her a long, hard, blank stare with his dark eyes. She stood in mid-mimic, but she lowered her shoulders and bowed her head. 'Mina, it is not your place to speak ill of this man, nor is it mines. His past origins are none of my concern, no matter how filthy, rugged, or ill-fated they may be. What matters is that this man associates himself with someone with such low moral fiber that he would attack me from behind, instead from the front like a true fighter would.' 'And as it's been said before,' Mina began, sounding droll as if she's heard this before. 'Unmoral association leads to moral obliteration.' 'Correct.' he turned around entirely, raising a hand to her temple. 'How are you feeling?' She pushed his hand away, nodding slowly. 'I'm fine. I've taken my share of bumps and bruises since I've began working with you. Thanks again for...you know...saving me and stuff.' 'I would have done the same for anyone.' he replied blatantly. Mina gave him a look quickly before pasting on a smile again. She grabbed his arm and cradled it while leading out of the locker room. 'Well, let's head back to Sado Island. I've got a surprise for you waiting there.' A hop, skip, and a jump away, a black 4-door sedan sat with its trunk open in front of the door leading inside the arena. Just then, a figure dressed in a black cape, black suit, white face mask, and a black top hat lands before the trunk of the car with a cane in hand. He looked around in all directions before tilting his hat back and scratching his head with the cane. 'Where on the planet Vegita is the Black Dragon Master? His presence was supposed to be here nanoseconds ago, but he nor his hentai honey is nowhere in my super-sight!! NO BOTHER...for I shall wait here with the patience of a pubescent boy waiting for his first glimpse of Miko Mido's Manga Melons!!' His eyes turn towards the inside of the trunk, and they light up with sparkles. 'OH BOY!! RICE BALLS!!! DELICACIES OF THE MOON PRINCESS SERENITY!!' He climbed into the trunk and disappeared as the exit to the arena opened. Inphino and Mina walked out with their bags in hand. They approach the trunk of the car in mid-conversation as Inphino begins placing the bags in without looking inside. 'Who does this Ali think he is anyway for attacking YOU?' Mina asked, a tad outraged by the man trying to threaten the Shadow Dragon in battle...something no one has any hope of accomplishing. 'I bet he's just another one of these Black Dragon Marks that popped up after you were 'dead'.' she quoted that word with her fingers before Inphino could correct her. 'And now that you're alive again, he figures he can take you out and become a bigger name than you!' 'Nonsense.' he replied, placing the final bag in the trunk. The man in the top hat managed to poke his head out from underneath the bags and stared up at Blitz and Mina talking. 'Ali seems like he wishes to learn firsthand of my fighting style...or he may want to train with me, hoping that he could also grow to become as skilled as I am. Unfortunately, he has gone about the entire method of asking me to teach him the wrong way. Therefore, he shall receive a sample of the lesson I plan on teaching all those who think that doing things the... evil... way, is the wrong way.' 'Excuse me, Black Drago---!!!' the voice was cut off as the trunk lid slammed down hard. Inphino and Mina looked around the parking lot before staring back at each other. 'Did you hear something?' he asked her. She shook her head unsure. 'Thought I did...but maybe it was the echo in this place...or maybe the announcers inside the arena.' they both shrugged as they turned to enter the car. Mina climbed into the driver's seat, while Blitz into the passenger's side. The motor roared to life and the car gunned out of the arena parking lot... ...with extra baggage... Hours had passed...the sunset was hued the sky in a blaze of reds and oranges. It was at this time that the black sedan had pulled out from the auto transport area of the ferry crossing the watery depths between Sado Island and the mainland. The car traveled on gravelly paths through small villages towards their destination. Inside the car, Inphino sat with his arms folded at his chest and his eyes closed in meditation. Mina drove on silently, her eyes focused upon the road. Blitz felt the car slow, the motor growing softer and the speed of the auto dropping. Then the car shut off entirely. Here we are!! Mina said as if she were unveiling a surprise. Most surprises were nothing but bad news...but some arent even surprises at all, but already known revelations that people wish not to believe. Ali and Kimbell knew they didnt have a chance, but they chose not to believe...therefore making it a surprise when one of them is nailed with the Saishuu Kessen Ougii...or the other two finishing maneuvers Inphino wields. Inphino opened his eyes and looked up. Sitting atop a steep slope was a gray building that looked like a large Japanese-designed house. He turned to Mina with his usual stare before looking back up to the building. What is this? What an obvious question. Its a house, silly! What an obvious answer. I know that much, Mina...but to whom does this house belong to? YOU! Mina pointed to him with a big grin. Then she stepped out of the car held her arms out towards it. Welcome to your new training hall...Blitz Dojo! He gave her one of his stares, drawing out a sheepish grin on her face. Sorry...I couldnt think of a better name at the time. Youre free to rename it whatever you like. Thank you for that priveledge. he climbed out of thecar and walked to the back of it. Well, no use wasting time staring at the building. Lets get the bags inside to I can begin my training. Mina joined him at the rear of the vehicle and unlocked the trunk. Then she shrieked in surprise, leaping into the arms of the Black Dragon Master as a black figure leaped out of the trunk and fled wailing into the woods. Inphino stood holding Mina in his arms, both of them dumbfounded at the event that just happened. A few seconds later, the top hat wearing man came walking back out slowly, a relaxed look on his face. Ahhhh...citizens...if you ever see mini-riceballs in the trunk of someones car...DO NOT...EAT THEM!! He looked over towards the staring Inphino and Mina and smiled. AHA!! BLACK DRAGON MASTER!! Ive been waiting to meet you!! I...AM MANG-A-HERO!! He flashed a smile, his hands on his hips, before going back to speaking. I would suggest next time you leave your delicacies out of the rear compartment of your vehicle. They tend to go stale...and then they tasted funnier than the maiming of the YELLOW DEMON PIKACHU! There was no food in the trunk! Mina said, rather confused. Only moth balls! Never mind that, Inphino cut the two of them off before anything further could escalate into further madness. Mang-A-Hero...what is it you want of me? Why...to train with you, of course!! AHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA!!!!!!! he popped in a fit of laughter, like the question was as humorous as Alis belief in being better than Inphino Blitz. Good Digital Warlord of PURE MIYAGAWA has assigned you and I to be partners in battle against the villainy of Ali Khadaffi and Carl Kimbell!! Inphino had some trouble swallowing this fact...much like Ali would have trouble swallowing his boot. Miyagawa teamed me with...you? In-DEED he did!! And what a team we will make! We will be the next best thing in the line of HEROIC DUOS IN JAPAN!! LOOK OUT GOKU AND PICCOLO!! MAKE WAY RYU AND KEN!! BEAT IT MIHOSHI AND KIYONE!!! he stopped his rant for a second, rubbing his chin. Well...not like Mihoshi and Kiyone, because theyre super hentai honeys....but the meanings still the same!! Inphino looked on, emotionless as always. Mina remained sitting in his arms, her arms wrapped around his neck to hang on. ... Come now! Lets not waste any more time!! We have a match to train for! he leaps over to the trunk of the car and begins unloading the bags. Black Dragon Master, put down your Kawaii babe of Amy Campbell proportions and help me with the baggage! Theres WORK TO BE DONE!! Mina dropped down to her feet lightly as Blitz folded his arms. He sighed as he walked over to join Mang-A-Hero with the bags. And my life once again becomes interesting...surprise, surprise...
