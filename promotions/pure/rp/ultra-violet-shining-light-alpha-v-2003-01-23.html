Ultra Violet Shining Light Alpha- V

23 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ultra Violet Shining Light Alpha- V Written: Thursday, the 23rd of January. Narrated by Minori Kumako. All I can remember from the day before is watching my fiancé, Akoshi Kunamara, or known as Radiant in the Puroresu world, get hit in the face by a lead-tipped shoe. It was his elder brother, Yukio, who hit him in the face with the shoe, stunning Akoshi. Before I blacked out from an unknown source, I remember Yukio yelling to Akoshi and I, “It time to go home, brother!” and then blacking out as he laughed wickedly, driving back onto the highway again.
Where I am now is completely foreign to me. I have never seen this place in my life. I roll over a little bit, now not facing a wall and looking down at this steaming Hot Springs below. I blinked my eyes a few times, wiping away some dried up tears that I never bothered to clear off yesterday. Damp rocks surround the Hot Springs, with plant leaves dipping in and out of the dangerously hot water. My head peels up slightly, arching my back up. I swung my legs over the hammock and looked around, still quite confused. My feet slap against the damp wooden floorboards on this porch when I walked toward the small staircase that led toward the Hot Springs.
My hair flopped forward in front of my eyes when I went to look down at the boiling hot pool of water. The steam made my skin feel all clammy, as I grew closer to it. I just want to bend down and feel the hot water on my skin. I do not care if it burns me alive… I just want to touch it.
I was blissfully unaware of what was going on around me. Standing above me, on a large patio surrounded by plants stood Akoshi. He had his eyes closed, standing peacefully and unaware of anything going on around him. I looked up at him, staring at the small bandage on his cheek from the fight on the Tokyo highway yesterday, I kept wondering; what was he doing up there?
‘Ak… Akoshi?’ I stammered out, desperately trying to get his attention toward my hapless self. He didn’t respond immediately, like I had wanted him to, but he did respond. After waiting a few seconds, he blinked and looked down at me. I smiled warmly, inviting him to join me with the hot springs. He gradually walked over toward the steps that led to this water inferno. I looked at him, eager to say something. ‘I.. I don’t remember much about yesterday, do you?’
He looked at me solemnly, waiting a few seconds to reply. ‘No, not really,’ is all he could say. Part of me knew he was lying, not wanting to tell me the truth. The other part of me knew he wasn’t saying anything about it so I don’t find out too much, and get hurt.
‘Akoshi?’ I said looking at him while he stared down at the intoxicating warmth of the Hot Springs. He looked at me again, frightening me a little bit.
‘Yeah, Minori?’ he said back. ‘Where… where are we?’ I said back, confused more than ever. His head turned to me again, taking my hand in his. Our warm hands combined together felt more than great, especially with the warm steam pouring from the Hot Springs. ‘Minori,’ he began to say, a sadden expression growing on his face. ‘This place is Aikawa Island.’
I blinked, and then realized; didn’t Yukio say something about taking us back to Aikawa Island? I don’t remember that well, he may have. ‘So, this is where you grew up? On this island?’
‘Correct,’ said Akoshi, taking a few steps to my right. ‘My family, the Kunamara family, owns the largest estate on this island. Aikawa Island isn’t very big, and most of the people living here work for my father in some fashion.’ He walked around a little further, kneeling down on one of the many damp rocks surrounding the Hot Spring. I paused for a split second, and then joined him on the rock. ‘Do you see that small “house” through the trees, Minori?’
‘Yes,’ I said back after peering through the trees for a few good seconds. I need my glasses. ‘Was that your Room?’
‘Not quite,’ Akoshi said back, slightly smiling. ‘When I turned ten, my father told me I that I would now live in a separate room to the estate. I had my own maid who cooked and did my washing.’ He said with a smile. ‘The privacy was nice.’
I can easily imagine how much he would have liked it, always liking his own privacy. His mouth opened again, wanting to continue his story.
‘I rarely ventured into the main house,’ he explain to me, looking over at the bridge that crossed over a small creek currently in near freezing temperatures. ‘Only time I was ever inside the main house was when father wanted to speak to me, or my “mother” wished to give me private tutoring on the fine things in life.’
I looked into his eyes again, and gave him a soft kiss on the cheek. I wanted to take it further, but I felt that this was too foreign of a place for me to feel comfortable. Our heads rest against each other’s, and ours closed at the same time. Nothing could ruin this moment between two lovers like us, except for Yukio Kunamara appearing on the balcony Akoshi stood on just a few minutes ago.
‘Brother!’ he shouted, getting both of our attentions. ‘Father want see you inside now!’ Akoshi grunted, not wanting to let go, but he had to go and see his father for the first time since he was just sixteen years old.
