Fate: Self-Affliction Fates Written: October, 18th 2002 Vilas all weekend was questioned for his action, and asked for a reason why he got involved. Vilas really thought he didn’t need any reason. People believed it was fate that he was there at that time but really he just replayed what he thought over in his mind. There was no true answer to why he did what he did, but it really didn’t mean anything to Vilas. What he done was now in the past and nothing mattered about the past.
Vilas took one of his usually walks trying to get to know the area around him. So far things hadn’t gone so well. Death crept on his shoulder at every turn and haunted his thoughts every second of the day. Not in a scared fashion but more of an anger fashion for being in the situation. Why did he allow himself to go out that day? Fate didn’t decided it, because Vilas had to leave. It just so happens bad things happen.
The next time he almost tore the heads off three people because they wouldn’t leave well enough alone. They believe fate brought the to Vilas, but to Vilas it was just their mission to talk to people rather then just the luck of the draw.
So many people believe they are here for a reason. What is wrong with this world? Why can’t people just see they make their own path and don’t have it chosen for them.
Vilas continues on his way and comes again to a cross section in the road. Left or right which way does he turn? Last time he turned the wrong way he was almost killed, but of course he had chosen his path and it was his problem. He turned left and kept on the path that moved around the city.
So little time to make the right choice in life but the right choice always has to be made. Maybe I didn’t send a strong enough message before and I should send a better one. Maybe I could have done something different and saved my self-this ridicule
Thoughts kept going over in his mind. Normally he wouldn’t behave in this matter but this wasn’t a normal matter. Once in a life time chance and he wasn’t about to just let it mess up. Different faces would walk back trying to get a glimpse at the man trying to think. To Vilas they were looking right at them, but they didn’t even notice him. He was lost in his own thoughts.
This is the way I do things in my life. I lead by example and worry about talking later. Why talk when you can just show someone what you want to do and worry about the punishment later. I only want to lead by my way and let the rest of the world worry about explaining what I mean.
Vilas mind was going a mile a minute and wasn’t slowing down. He turned corner after corner beating him self up for doing things wrong. He slowly thought up his next move and then would quickly erase it when he came to another point.
I could do this, or maybe that. I have to send the right messages some way. He stopped at a shadow of what appeared to be a large building. He stopped thinking and looked up at the top of the sky and saw where he was. He went in circles and was back where he started. Just like his plain he had to start all over again and not get lost.
I lost my train of thought. I have to be more focused on my task and not worry about what message I send just as long as I get it through. The message won’t just pop up out of thin air, but rather I will have to send it. One way or another he will receive the message loud and clear.
