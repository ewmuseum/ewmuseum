Taking The Mind Off

28 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Taking The Mind Off Written: 01.28.03 Let me fill you in on some of the current events in my life, before you jump the gun in asking things that I really have no answer towards. I just got out of jail Thursday night, from some actions committed during the tenth day of the PURE show. Killing your former employer is kind of a thrill, right after a car chase and some death on my part.
The phone jumps off of the hook at 7:29 this morning, me being up at a good hour before that. I turn my head, before picking up the phone. 'Hello?' Notice that I don't like random phone calls right before I've had my coffee. It sucks when that happens.
'Good morning, I am -' I cut this guy off before he tells me something I really don't want to know. 'If you're selling me a Polish FrenchFry French Fry, then I ain't interested.' I watched the PCW in my sparest of time, being known very little by some of the people who were in the tournament, not to mention my current affairs. Vito Wynn could have been in this match, but he's busy with running the MMA and wrestling in other federations. 'I'm not selling you anything, this is an official here at the Hyman Offices. Are you Cordozer Stevenson?'
'I'm him,' If I weren't, then why the hell did you call this motel? That's what I wanted to say to him. 'Well Mr. Stevenson - we here are calling for your latest actions inside of the ring, it is plausable that you get a drug-'
SLAM! All that was happened to be a reminder of what the hell I need to do in this entire word and once again, I get called upon to try and vester away with the gold. Two title shots in a single two week span happened to be the hard part, and I saw it. Picking up my car keys and heading out of the Motel Room door, I saw it.
'Cordozer!' That voice screwed with my past, and once again I had to deal with it. That weed smoking bastard - Kenny. Where Kenny goes, the Phatheads are sure to follow, and I'm already pissed at these bastards for putting me in jail anyway.
'Why the hell are you talking like that? That shit ain't flying with me! And who the fuck are you talking to?' Racks, the one with the cursing problem yelled out.
The nerve of him to tell me what I had to talk to, and it robbed me and haunted me to the absolute death with him - and Will, and Kenny. 'Oh no, you still HAD to be in Japan didn't you?'
Where else would have they gone? They followed me to the depths of the earth, and I don't even really find it a problem, 'Don't even try to change the subject. Whereever weed goes, we go. I thought you knew!'
I sighed, before it came into the point of mine that really these men were loaners to me. Seriously, I could have found friends other than these guys, but sorry I gotta stick to my people. Damn, it kinda sucks for the things that life brings you but really I don't care what the hell people think.
Rack's followed me, with Kenny, Buddha, and Will strictly behind him, following in formation. Looking up at the Denny's Breakfast diner sign, I could only water my mouth and try to hold these guys from fucking things up and making me go to jail; AGAIN.
'Where are we?' Racks asked while chipping away on a carrot. In such a Bugs Bunny way, he was the true sly fox of the bunch.
'Denny's, and you four aren't needed to be in here. I know you bitches will seriously try and mess things up.' I said to him, chuckling t the comment before entering the eatery.
'Who the fuck?' I called out in a hushed whisper. In a whisper, my phone began to ring, an awkward stare towards it, before I flipped open the door. An undisclosed number, somewhere in the Los Angeles area. I could tell by the 555 digits, its a L.A. notoriety, kind of like you always want a certain style of pizza or whatever.
'Who's this?' The voice didn't click within me. It was gargled, to the point of whether they became one of those men or women with holes in their throats because of smoking. 'Cordozer...nice to hear you're still alive...'
I laughed, before muttering something inconherently. It made you feel big that way, and in the true spirit of that, the guy just didn't know when to stop. 'How's life? Got a title coming soon? That's good - real good to know. Just remember, you put me like this.'
'Okay, first off - stop being a repetitive baboon, because frankly, I think you should have just dialed the wrong number or something.' My trash tallking skills were high, only when I needed to. Other than that; I was remedial at best. 'You owe me life boy, everything you have is mine. YOU HERE ME? MINE!'
That right there was his best attempt to scare me, frankly I thought he'd be more up to it than that, being EVIL and all. Hell, I think a retarded monkey on crack could be scarier than him. 'Damn,' I tried not to laugh at his weak threats. 'seems like you've royally screwed up nicely, care to have a drink on that?'
'Laugh about that shit when you're six feet deep, BIATCH!' Then I ended up seeing this one brother, I knew - Preach. One former Gangbanger turned Civil Rights Activist. He was confused, 'What's up Preach, I think a nugga like you'd fit right into wrestling.'
'Now look, you have to stop using the world nigger. Its words like that that put one race over one another!' Preach was straight up confused, he'd had so much white people on his nerves he ended up become sorta white himself. 'Oh, remind me to pick up some liquor from that cracker muthafucker up the strret, and that chink!'
'There's about NINE MILLION CHINKS AROUND HERE!' Then I looked back at the phone, before I could only wonder who had called me. For a pretty bad month upcoming, the time for cat and mosue games were only for the idiots back down in waiting to get where I am at.
That's when it hit me square in the face, about as hard as a brick, but weaker than a Senji Yulav punchline aimed to hurt me. I know, I'm hilarious; call it a gift. Something from my past was trying to claw back into my presence. I laughed, before beginning to eat my breakfast- tomorrow; I'd have to get ready for the title matches coming up soon.
It's not a plan, its a trek to find myself.
