Who Flung Poo?

9 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Who Flung Poo? Written: October 9, 2002 [Crouching Monkey….10:47] [The location for this evening is the Crouching Monkey. A club somewhere in Japan for the higher ups of major companies to come out and loosen their ties, drink a little, and generally just be themselves. The thick smell of cigar smoke and sushi is overwhelmingly strong for such a small and dimly lit night club. Japanese men from all around the city gather here after a long day of work filling all black leather booths from wall to wall. Only a low buzzing is heard from the businessmen over the half-naked Japanese Pop Princess singing and dancing up on stage. Slowly but surely all eyes turn toward you dazzled by her beautiful smile and her long Barbie like legs. Suddenly their dreams are shattered as the music is abruptly halted. The Pop Princess gives the crowd a confused look but is replaced by a weak smile as she sees who is coming from behind the curtain. Looking like a vintage SNL character, a man emerges. Dressed in Swiss black shoes, skin tight red bell bottoms, loose red silk shirt with flames, glittering red glasses, and a 13.5k gold D medallion the afro adorned warrior prances onto the stage, ignoring the confused Japanese men, gasps as he reaches the Princess, still on the stage.]
That 70's Guy': -gasp- 'Where have you been all my life, sweat thang? Ouh!' Pop Princess: -giggles- '-Insert random Japanese-' 'That 70's Guy': 'Come again, Honey' Pop Princess: 'I mean, who are you?' 'That 70's Guy': 'Watch yourself now. I'm the new act for this evening. I'm just here to entertain, The name's Disco Dan. Now if you would be so kind. Step aside, woman'
[By now random Japanese rants are coming from the rich foreign men as the hot Pop Princess is replaced by an arrogant hippie. Back on stage the 'Disco King' does a Michael Jackson crotch grab as 'Macho Man' by the Village People hits and Double D begins his routine]
'Every man wants to be a macho macho man to have the kind of body, always in demand Jogging in the mornings, go man go works out in the health spa, muscles glow You can best believe that, he's a macho man ready to get down with, anyone he can! Oh!' [Yells from the crowd begin to drown out the music...yells like --insert Japanese for 'What the Hell'--
'Macho, macho, man! Da-da-da-da I want to be a Macho Man! Do-do-do-do-do' [Some old man from the crowd jumps up and shouts 'Muta!'...Along with him rise his entourage of four big, burly, bald Japanese men. All of whose arms are thicker than Double D's waist. The men descend upon the stage will blood lust in their eyes the frail old man now sits at the table. double D looks at the angry men and finally gets it. They aren't down with the funk! Maybe he has realized the truth as the four man beasts are encircling him]
Double D: 'Yo, yo, yo! There is no reason to be jealous of me just cause I'm an American. Just cause we nuked you in the war...have Britney Spears.....and not to mention the um...'size difference', if you know what I mean.'
[This just seemed to enrage the men even though they might not have understood it. How will the Disco King get out of this mess? It looks to me like he needs a distraction. Ah, speak of the devil]
[A sharp, long whistle cuts through the air as everyone turns their attention to the middle of the room. Standing tall up on a table is the Pop Princess and she has just raised her shirt!.................--10 minutes later-- Huh, the men in the room have snapped out of their trance but Double D and the girl have already escaped!]
[Outside The Crouching Monkey] Pop Princess: 'Hey, thanks for rescuing me...I was being held prisoner to dance for their pleasure.'
Double D: 'What makes you so sure that's not what you'll be doing for me?....Just kidding, girl'
[She gives him a 'maybe' look before continuing] Pop Princess: 'So where are you headed?' Double D: 'A little place called Pure. I'm supposed to wrestle there. I guess my career at The Crouching Monkey was doomed from the start.'
Pop Princess: 'I see, did I ever tell you that by freeing my from The Crouching Monkey, you have let me lose upon the world, meaning I must come with you....sort of like a genie. And the name is Nanoka.'
Double D: 'So you're doomed to tag along with the Disco King, huh? And if you're like a genie, doesn't that mean I get like three wishes?'
[The Disco King and his companion walk off into the sunset as a giggle can still be heard] Nanoka: 'Is it true what they say about American 'Hot Dog' being bigger than Japanese 'Egg Role'?'
