Court Date 'All rise, for the honorable judge Noah White.' There was a small rustling in the otherwise silent court room.  In the defendant seat, Cianan, and his lawyer rise.  Cianan's head is down, his hair is tied back, and he shifts uncomfortably in his suit.  'This is the People versus Cianan, please be seated.' The rustling again as the masses slowly sat.  Cianan brushed his hair from his face staring at the table, in a bit of disbelief. How did I get myself into this mess?  Was I really the overboard?  I've embarrassed myself, and Akiko with this. The judge spoke with a strong commanding voice, obviously not the one who lets himself be hassled. 'Cianan, you have been accused of assault and battery, and destruction of private property.  How do you plead?'  With a stern look he glared a hole into Cianan. Cianan's lawyer, Paul Moriarty stood, and pushed his glassed up with his index finger, to keep them from sliding down his nose. 'My Client pleads, that there are circumstances to get him off on temporary insanity.  We plead guilty with reasoning.  We would like to work out a deal with the courts.' It all started out, in that Lyon's.  From what I was told, I was sitting there in the fetal position rocking back and forth, and as soon as two boys tapped my shoulder, and I immediately leapt upon them, in a rage.  I have no memory of this.  Apparently, I damn near mauled them.  I had no control over myself, and I did that kind of damage.  Apparently, I went beyond that, destroying the inside of the Lyon's before I passed out.  I remember nothing.. from that day, or days after and before it.  It's all a blank.  It's all blank, as though I was never there.  It was the drug.  Kyle warned me about it, but I didn't listen.  I was addicted, I needed it.  Why wouldn't I listen?  I could have pulled my head out of my ass and helped save CCW?  I could have helped Akiko through her tough time.  It's all my fault.  No more passing the blame, no more throwing it on Kensuke.  I did it myself, I am to blame.... A few days later, as the Jury was called back to make their deliberation over the evidence and the charges against Cianan.  He stood there, his eyes lowered, as they read their results, his eyes squeezed tight, still frightfully embarrassed and angery with himself that he'd be in such a position. 'We, the jurors, find the defendant guilty.  But, with special circumstance of temporary insanity, due to the mind altering substances taken.'  The lead juror spoke, with a shrill voice. 'It was the best we could have hoped for, Son.' Paul Moriarty said with a sigh, patting Cianan lightly on the back. Noah White, sank slowly back in his seat, a frown almost seeming engraved on his elderly face.  He watched Cianan with a close eye.  He pursed his lips, and narrowed his harsh steely eyes, 'Well,' He started, with a slight amount of venom, 'Quite frankly, I think that you got a break because of your celebrity.  I don't approve of that, but I will react in accordance of what your equal peers have suggested.  You will pay the medical bills of those you assaulted, and the bills for the property you destroyed.' Cianan gave a slight nod. 'Yes, your honor.' 'I'm not done yet!' Noah nearly shouted, quite frustrated with this kind of thing. 'You will be placed on probation, and serve  community service for no less then two hundred hours.  Also, you will talk to your probation officer about seeing a psychologist, to work on those problems of yours.  Do you understand?' 'Yes, I do.'  There was a slight sting in Cianan's voice.  A little snarl to his voice, this judge was being a bit too snippy. 'Good.'  The judge rocked back into his chair. 'I never want to see you in here again.  If I do, I will personally make your life a living hell.  Do you understand THAT?' 'Yes.' 'Court dismissed.'  The slam of the gavel echoed around the room, and people slowly started to rise, and Cianan just stood where he was, with a slight sigh.  His lawyer grabbed onto his hand and shook it.  Cianan snapped it back with a glare. 'What's wrong?  We got a good deal, all things considered.'  Paul asked, wondering more about the harshness of Cianan demeanor. 'What's wrong?'  Cianan snarled, as he snatched his briefcase from next to his chair.  His face gaining a light red tint with anger, as he left his bewildered lawyer to catch up to him. What's wrong is that I was here in the first place?  Me of all people letting go, and relying on something artificial to get through, after everything I've done and been through.  He stormed through the doors, and out through the small group of paparazzi, which he pushed through without a word, climbing into a cab. I am such an idiot.  Cianan pulled the band from his hair, releasing the beautiful black mane of locks, down his shoulders, and he shook it out, running his fingers through to smooth down any strands that were in the way.  His blue eyes closed slowly. I have nothing now.  'To Terrington and Vinyo, please.'  He said to the cab driver, who gave a small nod for acknowledgment, before the car shuddered and started to creep into traffic. No federation to work in.  No drugs to escape reality.  No enemies to fight.  No one to place my blame, now I have to be unhappy in everything I made for myself.  It hurts. He pinched the bridge of his nose, before moving away to rub his temples in a slow clockwise motion, trying to relieve some of the stress from the day. I've been inactive far too long.  But, where to go now?  PURE closed down.. Rush has some interest in me, and so does FSW.  I just don't know. The cab stopped at the requested intersection, and Cianan stepped out of the cab slowly, paying the cabbie, what he owed, plus a little extra for a tip.  A lengthy walk, took Cianan to the front porch of his house, with a quick dig for his keys, he unlocked the door, letting himself in.  He grabbed his mail from the floor, sorting through, tossing the mail one by one, onto the kitchen table, as he leaned against a wall.  He sighed, rather bored, until, his blue eyes caught onto one piece of mail.  He quickly ripped it open, and raised a brow as he read the note inside. So.. PURE wants me back?  Excellent.  A perfect chance to work off this embarrassment and frustration.  No more anger to rule me, they will see what I can do.  A slow grin crossed Cianan's face, as he crumpled the letter in his hand. A perfect chance to prove what I really am.  A monster with a brain.
