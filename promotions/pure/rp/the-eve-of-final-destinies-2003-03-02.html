The Eve of Final Destinies

2 March 2003

The Eve of Final Destinies Written: March 2, 2003 I’m a night person. The morning is so… boring. Nighttime T.V. is better. There’s more fun places to go at night than in the morning. There’s a reason most people hate the morning.
And although the night was a more exciting time, I’d be lying if I said I was having a good time right now. I couldn’t sleep. I never really could. I’m not an insomniac by any means, however. When I get to sleep, I can sleep nearly half the day. The boring half, that is.
It was hard to have a good time right now, though. It’s always recommended to “Get some sleep.” “Get to bed early.” Especially when you’ve got an athletic competition on the following day. Something like, a wrestling match? How about, a title match?
Even if I normally had no troubles sleeping, I have a hard time believing that on this particular night, that I could sleep anyway. After all, I have an All Asia Title match! And I HATE its champion…
I was walking down the streets of Tokyo at 4:00 AM. Actually that makes me sound like a morning person. The streets were by no means crowded, nor were they deserted. For the most part, the people who passed me by didn’t acknowledge me. There were a few times, however, where someone would bother to look at me, smile, and nod. Kind of a nice feeling to be recognized by complete strangers.
I had no purpose to be out there, other than to think to myself. Why I needed a walk to do so, I didn’t know. I thought about my match, my father, and Yulav.
He got on my nerves because of his irrelevant hate for America. He annoyed me because he pinned me twice. I hated him because of his actions.
Everything I was thinking has been said many times over. We all saw what he did. We all heard what he said. We all know how I feel about it. I was on a mission. Never before have I hated an individual this much. I’ve never had a reason to. I couldn’t have him beat me again. Not after what happened.
Then, suddenly, I stopped dead in my tracks. A thought came to my mind that scared me to death… What if I COULDN’T beat him?
I mean he’s beaten me before, but think about it. He hurt my father, the only person who means anything to me at the moment. And I want vengeance. I NEED vengeance! But what if I can’t get it?
After all… if I can’t beat him now… after all that’s happened… when can I? And THAT’S what scared me. I turned around to go back to my hotel room. I was starting to get tired at this point anyway. But on the way back, I tried to answer my question. 'If I couldn't beat him now, when could I?' Only one solution came to mind.
Don’t let yourself answer that question.
