PURE Legends, Chapter 2: Attack

17 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PURE Legends, Chapter 2: Attack Written: 09/17/02 Chapter Two: Attack The falcon lands on the arm of a bulky, muscular warrior covered in fur. He is naturally dark, but dirty from travels without bathing, darker by the moonlight with no fire. His campcannot even be made out, save for those whose eyes are accustomed to the darkness of night.
Captain Tsuyoshi Nasudatsuya’s forces, the night guard, pace around on edge. Even the sleep is nervous. They are the first line of defense against the Yamatoshi marauders. The Emperor was not required under the military code to accompany the troops, but a rebellion against a peaceful rule of tradition was something that the Emperor felt personally responsible for quelling.
The three night guards at the front of the Captain’s forces, spearsmen though dressed in the same unwieldy pot-armor as the swordsmen, huddle together for warm.
*boom* Private Takeshi Kita: “Did you hear something?” *Boom* Private Shigeki Nagata: “No.” *Boom!* Private Galuda Wakagame: “I heard it.” *BOOM!* Private Shigeki Nagata: “What was that?” The Captain runs out of his tent, waking up agitated. Captain Tsuyoshi Nasudatsuya: “What? Cannon fire! Why did you not wake me up?” Private Takeshi Kita: “Forgive us, Captain!” Private Galuda Wakagame: “What is a... cannon?” Captain Tsuyoshi Nasudatsuya: “It will be the death of you if we stay here, wake the men!” The privates quickly scramble to wake the Nasudatsuya forces. They are fierce fighters, but no match for cannons.
Captain Tsuyoshi Nasudatsuya: “YAMATOSHI FORCES! RETREAT!” The Captain’s voice is muffled by the blood expelled from his throat, a fired cannonball taking off his head. His sergeant catches the head as it falls his way. He drops it to the ground and continues the order of retreat.
Sergeant Kouji: “Poor man. Such a fate is no way to die...” And with that the Yamatoshi forces, representing the foreign influences of SE, invaded Japan under the Tokugawa Shogunate. It’s current Emperor, Tokuagawa Ryosuke, has been aware of the dangerous, corrupting nature of SE and has done what he could to keep the tradition of Puro alive and intact in Japan. But somehow, the foreign SE has infiltrated the Emperor’s curtain of iron and taken hold in the northern regions. The Yamatoshi are not the first to attack on the behalf of SE, but they are the largest.
Their numbers are vast and powerful, and they have some of the most fiercesome warriors in the land as generals.
Qalid Sadaffi, a Moor raised in the northern deserts of Africa, his family was sold as slaves by Portuguese traders before Tokugawa Ieyasu came to power. He is in no way a practitioner of the old ways. His fighting style is crude and vicious, but effective.
Kim Sung-Kwen, a man whose family hails from the mainland. A distant student of SE whose base is in the Khmer region of the southern peninsula, though he himself is from Saigon. He believes himself to be the greatest fighter in the world. He is skilled, but his arrogance has cost him in a number of battles. Kim Sung-Kwen is perhaps the most vain of all Yamatoshi.
Burning Hatred, a man born in Japan, though raised in the West by his family of merchants. For one reason or another he has developed a strong desire to lash out at Japan, though it has never wronged him. Promoted based solely on his extreme hatred of Tokugawa, he has somewhat earned a measure of respect in early battles, but is still highly untested.
The leader of the Yamatoshi, self-imposed ruler of SE, is only known as The Emptiness. Nothing is known about him. Rumors circulate about the supposed madness from which he suffers, which causes him to inflict cruel and unusual acts against his foes.
There are others, but they are too new, and have yet to gain any measure of reputation. *** Eiji: 'Thank you.' The messenger turns his horse around and rides back to the front lines. Eiji hands the message over to Emperor Tokugawa Ryosuke. He opens it and reads it.
Tokugawa Ryosuke: “The Eastern Front is being attacked with cannons!” Eiji: “Already?” Tokugawa Ryosuke: “I fear you won’t be able to help us now. Cannons are powerful weapons, we should be under bombardment soon. Retreat would be as ineffective as attack.”
Shaking his head, Eiji rushes out of the tent. Eiji: “Do not give up yet!” The Emperor and his imperial guard follow in tow. Eiji is outside, surveying the area. Eiji: “You have catapults...” Tokugawa Ryosuke: “Yes, they give some measure of a long-range attack, but they are no match for cannons.” Eiji: “They don’t have to be. Order your high-ranking officers and privates to retreat, leave me with a large troop of lieutenants.”
Tokugawa Ryosuke: “Would could you accomplish?” Eiji: “Forgive me, Emperor, but I have not the time to explain.” *** The retreat order has left the encampment running with the advancing Yamatoshi forces on its heels. Eiji Endo and the Emperor are left behind with a number if soldiers, yet not enough to fight off the Yamatoshi, especially with their cannons. But the plan was not to fight...
Tokugawa Ryosuke: “I pray to my ancestors that your strategy is successful.” Eiji: “Trust me, Emperor.” The Emperor releases a deep sigh. Tokugawa Ryosuke: “RELEASE!” The catapults are pulled, their payload flies through the air at the Yamatoshi. Though the cannonfire draws ever closer to their position, they stand their ground. Then it slowly dies down before coming to a stop.
Tokugawa Ryosuke: “It worked? It worked!” Eiji: “Gunpower weapons cannot operate when drenched.” Tokugawa Ryosuke: “You have made the retreat far easier!” Looking off to the east as the last of the Tokugawa stragglers flees the retreating front. Eiji: “They are led by Qalid Sadaffi. They will continue their advance until they meet some fighting force.”
He draws the Wild Fury from its sheath and dons his helmet. Kneeling to the ground, Eiji mentally readies for battle.
Eiji: “I will stay and fight. You should leave now, Emperor.” Tokugawa Ryosuke: “May the ancients protect you...”
