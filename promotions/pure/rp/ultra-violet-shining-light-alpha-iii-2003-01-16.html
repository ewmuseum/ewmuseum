Ultra Violet Shining Light Alpha- III Written: Thursday, the 16th of January. Narrated by Minori Kumako. Bitterly cold wind blasts through the air in the Japanese capital city, Tokyo. After only falling asleep a few minutes ago, I am jolted awake by the shuddering of the airborne craft we’re in. I’m not known as a heavy sleeper, only needing five hours each night to be revitalised and energetic for the next day. My fiancé, on the other hand, is quite the opposite. Akoshi will sleep for obscene amounts of time each night, far longer than I am. It’s one of the few differences our special bond has.
After being suddenly woken, I spent the remaining time on the flight looking out at the dense grey clouds just beneath us. I have never been one to fear air travel, never fearing heights. The plane began to slowly descend, the warning lights popping on above our heads. My hands drifted over to my lovers still body, shaking him awake from his peaceful slumber. Akoshi’s eyes opened slowly, fluttering several times as he woke.
‘How… how far till we land?’ he sleepily said to me. ‘I don’t even know what time it is.’ ‘Not too long, Akoshi,’ I said back, sliding my dinner-tray back into it’s position. I smiled and looked over to him while he buckled his seat belt back up. ‘The time is about two o’clock in the afternoon, judging by the sun being very bright.’
He smiled, and I smiled back to him. I yawned softly back, feeling a little tired like I always do after a night of barely of any sleep. We both rested our heads against our chairs, patiently waiting as the aircraft descended down to the ground. To our homeland, Japan. I could feel the jets winding down as the tires skidded along the tarmac, signalling the end of our trip from the United States.
We exited the plane and quickly made our way through customs, the cool Japanese winter making me shiver for the first time in years. Customs was tiring for my already sleep deprived body. Customs was a bore, I just wanted to leave it as soon as possible. Akoshi wasn’t all that fussed, sipping on a cup of warm coffee while we waited. As we approached the front of the queue, I couldn’t help but notice this teenage boy staring at me like a sex object. I growled silently, crossing my eyebrows at his actions.
‘Get someone your own age,’ I spitefully said as we passed through with all things cleared. Akoshi picked up as many bags as his muscular frame could carry, leaving me with only a small bag. He struggled with them, dumping them all into the trunk of the first Taxi that came by. We both climbed in together, sitting on the backseat next to each other.
‘What was all that about, before?’ Akoshi asked me, waiting a few minutes to let my wild temper to calm down. I looked up at him from my nested spot under his arm, smiling warmly so too not suddenly yell at him.
‘Nothing,’ I sheepishly said back, kissing him on the cheek. ‘I was just tired, I guess.’ I paused for a second. ‘I probably should have slept like you did on the flight, I wouldn’t have been so sleepy.’
Akoshi smiled lightly, turning his head to the left and staring out at the window. I looked at him and bite my lip. ‘Maybe I should have apologized to that kid,’ I mentioned loosely. Akoshi didn’t turn his head, just stared out at the mountains in the background.
‘Do not worry about him,’ he said back coldly. ‘We’re not here to worry about the feelings of a sexually charged kid, remember?’ I scoffed a little, my eyebrows frowning down a little. Just two days ago I had to drag him out of the house to get him here. I sighed softly, ignoring him. My eyes looked ahead and into the reflected gaze of our Taxi Drivers, who carefully looked over his shoulder at the travelling cars behind us.
‘What are you looking at?’ I asked the driver, while peeking my head through Akoshi’s tight grip.
‘Car behind us,’ he said in broken English. I turned my head around slightly and saw a black sedan carrying four Japanese men. The Taxi driver tried to keep an eye on the road as well, but it was all too distracting for my prying eyes to keep off of. ‘I think he want us to pull over.’ Our Taxi Driver drove the car down into a small turn off area, and quickly unbuckled himself and left.
‘Uh, Akoshi,’ I stammered out. ‘What are you going to do?’ ‘Stay here,’ he said sternly. ‘Do not leave this car at all, especially if I get into trouble. Stay here.’ Akoshi quickly undid his seatbelt and opened the door, stepping out and cracking his knuckles. Akoshi walked around to the back of our Taxi as I looked over, watching while four men stepped from the black sedan. Akoshi looked at each one, three of the four far larger than the remaining one. Akoshi smirked and cracked his knuckles, glaring his eyes toward the smallest one and saying,
‘Hello, Yukio.’
