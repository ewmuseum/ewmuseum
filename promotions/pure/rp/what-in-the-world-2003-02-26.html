What In The World

26 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What In The World Written: 2-26-03 The scene slowly begins to fade into a small gymnasium type arena. Vacant, we find noone around, except the back that is facing us now; a cloth like matter is draped over the man’s knee. His hands are placed on his knees and in his air, he looks to be in deep thought and we’re not even facing his face. Something obviously must be troubling him as a door is heard opening, he turns and we see that his face is that of Jesse Ramey, the piece of cloth falls to the ground and we see it as being the mask that he wore on Ikusa during his turning into Optimum Violence. Jesse’s face is rather confused as a huge smile comes across his face and the sound of footsteps is heard. Jesse comes to his feet quickly and begins to walk past the camera and the other set of footsteps are revealed to be none other than his daughter Michelle-Marie’s. The two embrace in a huge hug.
Jesse: (Still holding onto Michelle) When did you get here? Michelle: I’ve been here for about three hours now; I was lucky I found you. The two release and Jesse’s smile only gets bigger. Jesse: Mom said you were going to be coming over about a month or so ago, but when you didn’t show I was beginning to think you had gone back to Josh.
Michelle: (Chuckling) Dad, you know that Josh and me aren’t dating anymore. Jesse: (Putting his arm around Michelle) But you never can tell with you Michelle, I mean for goodness sake, you’ve dated everyone it seems.
The two begin to walk over to some chairs as Michelle shoves an elbow into her father’s side, Jesse jumps a bit and begins to chuckle.
Michelle: That was really rude of you. Jesse: (Smiling) I know, but I couldn’t resist. Michelle: (Smiling) Well, in that case, at least I didn’t date that nasty whore Chas Loraine.
Michelle winks at her father as his mouth nearly drops and she takes a seat. Jesse: (Seating down as well) Well, she did do me pretty dirty, so I’m not going to argue you on the fact of what you said.
Michelle: Of course you’re not. Dad, I knew more about that woman than you did, I’m just glad the two of you never did anything.
Jesse: Eh, I’m glad we didn’t either, but let’s not bring up the past. Why are you here? Michelle: I came to support you; you’ve got your chance at the PURE World Title at Final Destinies.
Jesse: (Rubbing his head) Yeah, I know and I’m really worried about that, Michelle. Michelle: (Looking confused) Why in the world would my father be worried about anything? Jesse: Because I’m not sure I’ll be able to defy the odds. (Looking to the ground) Michelle: (Still confused and shaking her head) What odds? Jesse: I’m outnumbered here; it doesn’t matter if I make it past Lee, and onto the Main Event for the title. I’m really at a disadvantage because of Raizo Tenzan. Lee has Tenzan backing him and the rest of their group and at anytime during my match they could easily take me out and cost me my chance.
Michelle: Dad, you’ve never worried about these things before, why start now? Jesse: (Looking up smiling) I don’t know, babe. Maybe it’s the fact that I’m getting so old, I’m beginning to doubt myself.
Michelle: You’re only twenty-six, dad. (Chuckling) Jesse: Yeah, I know that Michelle, but I’ve been wrestling for nearly sixteen years, so that pretty much does make me a little old in this sport.
Michelle: You know I’ve always thought you acted really silly about these type of things, why not consider yourself in your prime?
Jesse: Because I haven’t been in a Main Event for the World Title of a promotion in nearly three years now Michelle. You can’t be in your prime unless you’re able to hang in those types of matches at least once every six months. It was like I had my chance to succeed in this business and then it was all downhill from there.
Michelle: You’re joking right? Jesse: No that kind of stuff happens in this business all the time, once you’ve had your chance and you can’t perform at the rate that your promoter wants, you’re gone from that division.
Michelle: Dad, you haven’t held much of anything in the past three years because people are too stupid to see your talent or they’re jealous of it.
Jesse: It doesn’t seem that way to me. Michelle: Okay, look at it this way. When you were in OSW, when you didn’t win that number one Contendership match for the International Title, why didn’t you win?
Jesse: (Rubbing his chin) Because Michel Krieg interfered and cost me the match. Michelle: All right, in FSW when you had the Primetime Championship Match against Angelo Deville, why didn’t you win?
Jesse: Well, I did technically win that, but I didn’t win the title because of interference by Politically Incorrect.
Michelle: I rest my case then, dad. The only reason you don’t win these things is because of people costing you the matches. If you were given a clean match then you’d have it in the bag.
Jesse: That’s not the case here though Michelle, there is a chance that Raizo will interfere and cost me the match.
Michelle: Then you’re going to have to do something you haven’t done before. Jesse: (Looking at Michelle) And what would that be? Michelle: You’re going to have to grow eyes in the back of your head, because as long as you can keep someone from interfering in either of your matches, you’re going to go all the way.
Jesse: I don’t know if I can handle the World Title though Michelle. Michelle: Listen, dad, people have faith in you. Not only in the Japanese community, but back home in the states as well. We know you can do this; all you have to do is believe in yourself.
Michelle stands and begins to walk away. Jesse: Where are you going? Michelle: (Turning around) To get something to eat, you want to come with me? Jesse: (Standing up) Sure. Jesse and Michelle slowly exit the building as the camera zooms in on the fallen mask of Optimum Violence and the scene fades to black.
