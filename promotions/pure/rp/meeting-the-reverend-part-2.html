Meeting The Reverend [Part 2.]

17 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Meeting The Reverend [Part 2.] When we last saw our Civil Rights Extremist; he was meeting The Reverend Jesse Kintei for the first time. Now Jordan and Jesse have become formally aquainted, and they are becoming good friends. They have decided to go out to some Korean barbuecue via the bullet train in Osaka, Japan. The scene reopens with Jordan X Shabazz on The Reverend Jesse Kintei on a crowded Japanese subway. The place is hot and sweaty. There are people of all shapes and sizes there. Little children, old men, young ladies, old women. Somewhere in the sea of humanity and madness, we manage to see Jordan X Shabazz and Jesse Kintei sitting, carrying what seems to be a very animated conversation. Jordan is decked out in his nice clothing for Japan, baggy khaki shorts, some white Nikes, a khaki colored And 1 basketball t-shirt, and his black Toronto Maple Leafs fullback, baseball cap. Jordan's face is clean-shaven and he appears to be in a good mood. The Reverend Jesse Kintei is dressed in a black suit. His hair is picked out in what appears to be a decent looking Afro. We now move closer as we hear the conversation... '.... Trust it, I know that the history of the black man is a great thing, I know all of that, but when people like Ali Khadafi come and start fucking up my movement, it annoys me,' Jordan says with anger in his voice towards The Reverend Jesse Kintei. The Reverend nods politely as if he understands where Jordan is coming from. Jesse ruffles his suit and he speaks. 'Jordan, I totally agree with what you're doing. Yet I don't see the need for you to fight another one of us, especially because we need all the youthful Negroes on our side,' the Reverend replies to Jordan with a tint of fear in his voice. 'Screw that dog, I ain't even sure if Ali Khadafi is black anymore. Sure he's a dark skinned mutha, but have you heard the things he's said? This cat is making jokes about slavery and whatnot, and he has the gall to call me an Oreo? Come on, that nigga's dark as hell on the outside, but white as snow on the inside.' Jordan says with affirmative action in his voice. Jordan obviously has an intense dislike for Ali Khadafi, and if you were a Civil Rights Extremist, wouldn't you too? The Reverend decided not even to argue with Jordan anymore. Jordan had made his point, and it was obvious he wasn't going to back down from his opinion. Coming to Japan was something Jordan wanted to do for quite a while, he watched PURE on television at home and quickly became a huge fan. He chose his favorite wrestlers, and followed them religiously. Jordan also chose the type of people he thought were 'Fake' and he thought that didn't deserve to be in the spotlight of PURE.... Ali Khadafi was one of those men. Ali talked trash to the extreme, Ali talked about things that Jordan didn't necessarily wanted to hear about. Ali talked about ejaculating in the mouths of other men... Yeah, queer eh? Ali talked about rapes, and crimes. But what angered Jordan the most is the way that Ali talked about slavery. He talked about it as if it was a joke, like any normal racist, redneck, fat trailer park KKK member would. He talked about racism as if it's something that happened hundreds of years ago and it was long gone and dead. But Jordan, like everybody else with a brain knew, slavery was still alive. It was still alive and doing what it's always set out to do... Hate people. The common criticism of Jordan X Shabazz was that he was nothing more than a common hypocrite. A man who would say one thing, then do the other. For instance, Jordan claimed to have no respect for the white man, Jordan claimed to hate every single white person there was in the world. But then, while in the PCW, Jordan decided to team up with Duke Williams, Jason Core, and Legend Inc. Three white men. Why would Jordan risk the credibility and the respect that he had from the underground black community to team with three men? Why would Jordan do such a thing? The answer is quite simple when you read between the lines. Green. Nothing but green is all Jordan wanted. The cash, money, hoes, money, cash, hoes. It's so simple. Being a Primetime Championship Wrestling superstar is something that almost every wrestler dreams about, no matter how much they talk trash about the organization, and how it's run, that's exactly what they want to do. They want to wrestle in the organization and make big cash, and run around with the big boys. Jordan X Shabazz was, and is, no different. He wants to be the guy that walks back to his old home in Toronto, Ontario Canada, and walk up to those white kids that bothered him as a youth living in the rich white neighborhood, and he wants to flaunt the cash. Gloat, and laugh at the men who still live at home with their parents... Prove to them that he WAS something, and he WOULD become a household name. But to do that... He had to take out The HIT, The End, and even, the bootylicious housemate of Jordan X Shabazz.... Yuka Hamada. His first ever match in Japan was coming up, and it was rather important to him that he won. He had to make a good impression not only for the fans, but also for Alexander Miyagawa, the owner of PURE. Miyagawa had to see that Jordan wanted the Asian Title bad... Not because he has mad respect for the Asians, but because it'll help him reach his goal. The goal of being regarded as one of the most respected black men in the world.... Move over Jesse Jackson, step aside Al Sharpton; a REAL black man is coming through...
