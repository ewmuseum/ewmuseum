Childhood's Ills.. Kyoto, Japan. A city an ocean away from Salt Lake City, Utah. Yet, for one man, it is quite similar The Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid has made his new home in Kyoto. A small one room schoolhouse is his new domain for teaching children. Yet, this is not on his mind. His student, The End is. At Ikusa II, student defeated teacher. One last time. Never again. ::Inside his schoolhouse, the Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid stews. He is experiencing a rare emotional combination of pride and dissapointment. Pride, in realizing how well he trained Skylar Green, a.k.a. The End. Disappointment? Well, he DID have to tap out while locked in End's signature submission finisher, 'Never Again.' The Dragon Sleeper style camel clutch undoubtedly has had its ill effects, as the Sultan of Sides is clutching his neck.:: Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid: I knew I did too good of a job....you know, most people say that when they train someone, they don't teach them everything. I believe that I taught Skylar quite a bit too much...whether or not I like to admit it...he dominated at Ikusa II. He beat Kensuke Suzahara and myself from pillar to post...and he deserved the championship. ::Trapezoid grins....his expression evident even through his x-emblazoned mask.:: Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid: Of course.....Skylar...don't forget who you are dealing with. I am a man who has kidnapped the loved one of an advisary, and poured her blood down my throat in order to gain a matchup. As you know....I'm slightly demented....that's been determined psychologically. And, as such...Skylar..you know I want the title around your waist.... ::His voice begins trailing off...slowly..the madman continues to speak. A touch of steel enters his voice.: Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid: And, I have the ultimate chance to get revenge....not in a one on one match...but against three other opponents..... ....The militant black man.... Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid: Wait....where the hell did that come from? ...The former adult film star.... Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid: What's going on here?! .......and The End...... Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid: What is happening to me?! ......I am your subconscious, Ian.... Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid: Why are you calling me that? ....Come on now, stop denying who you are. You know full well your name is Ian... Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid: I am no longer Ian.... ::He realizes that he has let his name slip, and curses briefly:: Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid: I am now LEGALLY The HIT! Why are you doing this to me? .....I am your subconscious. I am your heart....I am your desires. I am not your mind....your mind has created this Trapezoid character for you....as a shield....a shield from the truth.... Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid: What truth? .......The truth...of......who....you are.....and why.....you......are.....this....way. ....Ian.....I am inseperable from you now....if you wish to know more...open your desk..and read the letter there, the one you never opened... ::Fearfully...the HIT, or Ian now...reaches into his desk...pulling out the letter....as the scene fades in a purplish haze...::
