Preparations

2 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Preparations Tuesday Turmoil didn't work out like The End believed. Juan Borro won the PCW Cruiserweight Title in a grueling ladder match. The End still has the PURE Jr. Heavyweight Title, but that's going to be put on the line later tonight against Yuka Hamada. Sitting alone in the dark locker room, The End contemplates his next match. End: 'So, for my next match, I shall finally defend my title after a month of holding it, waiting for a challenger. And the first challenger is Yuka Hamada. 'I've heard much about her past, but I care not. I am not a character judge. All I care about is wrestling. And if she can wrestle, then I'll meet her challenge the same as any.' The End gets up and leaves the locker room. He walks down the wide, empty halls, around the backstage area where there are technicians setting up for the show. Some of them greet The End, PURE's first, and to date, only title-holding champion. With his hands held up cradled behind his head, The End continues on his train of thought. End: 'I failed to capture PCW gold, but I still have the Junior Heavyweight Championship in PURE. This is my first WT-fed title, and I'm not going to give it up easily. Sure, I let Yuka pin me on the last Ikusa, but she was new and needed to win the match more than I. I already had the Junior Heavyweight Title, there was no need for me to keep three other wrestlers from their title ambitions by challenging another one. I'm not greedy, after all.' The End makes what would in America be a bold move in walking out from the backstage area to the audience areas, near the entrances. The Japanese fans see The End and flock to him. However, unlike Americans, they give him his space to walk, forming a line that, although cheering him, stands orderly in appreciation and respect for him. End: 'These fans treat me better than any American fans have. I'm a god amongst men in their eyes. I haven't ever felt such appreciation for me. Americans are so fickle, and flock like sheep to the flavor of the month. But these people respect my skill and ability; doubly more given my lack of sight.' The End waves at the crowds as he walks past them. End: 'And that is why I will fight tooth and nail to perform to the best of my ability in this federation. I've never put any more than half of my effort into a PCW match, but here in PURE, I feel differently about it. I want to put my all into my matches. And I will.' The End circles back around the arena to the backstage area, returning to his locker room. On his way, he passes by some local wrestlers that were invited for dark matches. Wrestler 1: 'To think that Yuka Hamada, a porn star, gets into PURE and gets a shot at a title before I can!' Wrestler 2: 'It's a shame. She is a disgrace to our profession.' Wrestler 3: 'Here is the champion. What do you think, The End?' The End doesn't stop walking, and doesn't turn his attention to them. He merely strides by. End: 'She wouldn't be my challenger tonight unless she could back herself up in the ring. And if you haven't been officially signed to a PURE contract yet, then perhaps you should reapply after gaining a bit more in the maturity department.' The End reaches his locker room and goes in, sitting on the floor in a meditative position. End: 'Yuka Hamada... don't disappoint me.'
