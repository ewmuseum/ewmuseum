A New Criminal On The Rising!

11 October 2002

A New Criminal On The Rising! Written: 11/10/02 Sometimes life does not workout the way you plan it out to be. There has been plenty of people that have done things that are on the opposite sides of the law, just to make their lives better, and the people around them.
Orlando Scott has just begun his wrestling career. The young star had started out in the wrestling academy, PWA. Orlando had a match his first few days in the academy, and was victorious with ease. Immediately he was picked up to a feeder federation looking to make it big, PURE. PURE is a great federation with loads of talent, and some talent has not been discovered yet. Orlando Scott is feeling like he is being underestimated, which has only made him train harder, and have more of a reason to go to the ring and win big.
Orlando Scott is preparing for this match, it is his top priority. Since his dismissal of the Navy, he has never shown so much intensity. But besides working out, PURE and his match is the least of his worries. The park landlord has threatened to dismiss the Scott’s from the park. Orlando has tried to level with Lori, the landlord. Orlando has failed, Lori does not agree with PURE being a better paying job then UN-employment, which he was on before he had, began his wrestling career.
Orlando has thought long and hard, and has even shared with his good friend Rob. Rob is a more upper class person, lives in a nice home with his family in the nicer area of New York. Orlando has had the opportunity to have the life of Rob and his family. Rob has offered him a job as a co-worker with him. Rob is a criminal mastermind. A job where Orlando never thought he would turn to. Although he has never respected Rob’s line of work, Orlando has always liked the friendship of Rob.
Rob has a beautiful young wife Lindsey, also has two boys. The boys are sent off to Boston to boarding school all year around. It is mostly because Rob and his wife Lindsey does not want his kids to find out his line of business.
Orlando has finally agreed to become a part-time co-worker with Rob, because of his recent finance problems. Luckily for Orlando it’s a quick job, which takes little time, and little training.
{Begin Live Feed} The Night sky is pure, not a star in the sky. With the winter approaching, the nights are often cold, and this Thursday night was no different. It was edging midnight, as Sebastian has been asleep on the couch for over two hours. Orlando was given the instructions to meet Rob at his house, wearing all black.
AT this time, Orlando was in his small bedroom, leaning on the bed while tying his pure black Dockers. Orlando is dressed in black jeans a black shirt and a black jump suit jacket, with his pullover mask in his jacket pocket. The sound of the annoying beeping when a station is no longer providing shows. Orlando decides to leave the television on, and quietly sneak out. As Orlando is tip toes across the small living room, and opens the trailer screen door, the screeching of the screen door opening Sebastian from one side of the couch, to the other. But Sebastian is still asleep. Orlando closes the door behind him, and quietly begins running out of the trailer park. As he passes the beat up trailers, and he listens to the yelling, and arguing going on. This arguing is helping him go through with the criminal job, knowing he wants a better life for him, and better yet Sebastian.
Finally, he reaches the bus station. The bus is pretty much empty, as the quick ten-minute ride passes by with ease. When Orlando reaches Princess Ave, Rob’s street, he notices Rob waiting outside a black van. Rob approaches Orlando, and discusses their job for the night.
“ Okay Orlando, glad you showed up. You are making the right choice, for you and your kid. Now tonight we will be breaking into the bank down the street. Now I have done some research on the situation, and if done right we can snatch up to at least a million.”
With Orlando stuttering, over the thought of half a million in his possession was nearly a dream away. So Rob went on.
“ I have been saving this hit for a special occasion, and you are the occasion. So as long as you follow my instructions, and listen up, you may make some big money, and learn some things along the way.”
They both get into the black min-van, Rob in the driving seat. They begin making their way to the bank. Orlando bites his nails, as he tends to do when he is nervous, as some do.
“ Now your certain we will be safe, because I have a plane to catch tomorrow for my debut in PURE tomorrow. I can’t be in jail, they will fire a person who does not show up on their debut match.”
Rob roles his eyes with disgust. “ Now Orlando, I have always been there for you, but stop being a pansy. I have been doing this kind of thing for plenty of years now.”
“ Well even the best screw up.” “ I can do this job with Sebastian, just follow me, we will be fine!” So they finally pull up, to an empty parking lot. Rob grabs his gym bag from the back seat, which has the needed tools for the job. The job went quicker then Orlando could ever imagine, within thirty seconds Rob has three garbage bags full of money. And about a minute after that, they were back in the truck, with 10 bags, and two smiles. They were home in a flash, and then it was counting time.
The job was a success, they were able to gather 1.1 Million dollars, a great feeling for them both. Orlando went home with over a half a million dollars. Orlando banks his cash, with a different bank of course. And around six in the morning, Orlando was off to the airport. With the mood Orlando was in, he is eager to get into the ring again, and kick some ass.
{End Feed}
