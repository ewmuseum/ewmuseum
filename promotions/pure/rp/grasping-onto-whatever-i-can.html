Grasping Onto Whatever I Can..

17 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Grasping Onto Whatever I Can.. Driving along, a peaceful and tranquil road. Cianan's car, a beat up 94 Saturn. Small, compact, but got wonderful great mileage per gallon. Sure, it was old, and when you turned on the lights, there was the shriek of fiberglass on metal. But hell, it saved money in the end. The windows were open, and long strands of Cianan's hair flipped about when the strong wind hit the car. His face was emotionless, as he concentrated on the road, moving back and forth between cars, merging, and passing. California drivers were the absolute worst.. They never signal, they never even look. A sudden jerk, and Cianan swerved into another lane, as a car pulled in front of him without a signal, going 20 mph less then he was. The all familiar snarl rose from Cianan's lips, and he closed his eyes. This week, CCW was coming from Sacramento, California. Only a stone's throw from San Jose, where those familiar to PCW/CCW, would realize was the home to a CCW star, and a former PCW World Champion lived. Two members of the Elements. They hate each other now, it was funny they live so close.. Of course, the PURE office was also located in the vicinity of San Jose, run by Alexander Miyagawa. There was a stroke of luck, in the midst of this. Miyagawa, though he could throw me back into the title.. and just have me forget about everything. Have me be appeased by such a small token of self worth. Wrong. Miyagawa had a few things to learn about me. I don't forgive.. I don't let go.. This wasn't over... Cianan flicked his hand, and hit the signal, moving on to highway 680, moving towards San Jose, California. Carl Kimbell.. That man was another problem, he stole a win from me, in an unsavory way. I do all the work.. and he gets the pin after blind siding me. Time to take it back. Asian Title is in my future.. The way it's figured.. if I lock my hands around that Asian Title, Miyagawa will have problems. PURE isn't strong enough based to fire it's champions. It's not as renowned, not as strong. Miyagawa couldn't afford it. My only chance to stay in PURE is to grasp at straws, and lock onto that Asian Title. Or, maybe it was time for something drastic. Cianan slowly pulled onto the off-ramp, and pulled to the side, looking at the street name. Drastic.. Like throwing Jesse Ramey off a bridge. Drastic, like trying to destroy every part of Kensuke Suzahara's life. Drastic, like competing with Enigma in an incredibly stupid, and ill thought match, for a belt he didn't even want. Drastic, like taking Sebastian 'Switchblade' Toomes, Independent Title after the man incapasitated himself, in a match against Neurosis. Drastic wasn't always a bad thing.. But, how far would will Cianan go? To Be Continued..
