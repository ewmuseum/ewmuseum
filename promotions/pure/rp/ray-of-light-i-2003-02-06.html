Ray of Light - I Written: 02.06.03 Fittingly, its known that after Sunday's Ikusa, or Saturday for the Japanese fans customed to seeing the show at the Ikusa Hall. There the Gekito Cup was won by Emnity, who thus became the monster currently running rampage upon television screens today. In all of that aspect, Cordozer Stevenson was not parlayed into the experiment that he was not one of the top men inside of the company.
In a way, he just played by their rules, a simple yes man with a dead tongue, never flapping in front of brass. In a completely innocent tone, a hushed voice whispers through the foggy black backdrop to serve as a precursor for what should occur towards Stevenson next.
'I'm sorry, but you can't continue to take this kind of a dosage, it hurts mentally.' His transcolored eyes, flux with rage boiling through his veins continue to set their sights on Doctor Mark Hyman, a crisp, yet flimsy man at the age of thirty seven, roughly seventeen years Stevenson's senior. His small and frail body is launched through the air like a lawn dart, positined straight near the top of a board as the sickening thud of bone colliding against cold wall is present as Hyman slumps back down to the ground, sightly in pain without a moments rest.
'It hurts?' Stevenson jockeyed his hands with a frim position around Hyman's neck, cutting off air passages from the nasal cavity. 'How? I feel fine right now!'
Stevenson closes the gap with his fingers, pressing them tightly upon the collar of Hyman as he struggles to let air reach into his lungs. His eyes begin turning faded cloros, from black to almost coma white beginning to roll into his head. Without much resistance, Stevenson drops the man as he begins to convulse, trying to catch air all the more trying to keep his consciousness.
Hyman pushes himself over towards the bannister, gripping the skinny edges for deal life as Stevenson mocks trying to pounce on him before stepping out of the office completely, leaving a humid air convey his body. 'Nurse...' Hyman calls out weakly as he twists open an oxygen bottle, holding the latch up towards his face before leaning his head back. All the more letting the air come to him.
Folding his hands into his pockets, Stevenson looks around at the vast Tokyo skyline surrounding by brooding winds and even calmer tones of life. His over glowing red hair sparked with now silver highlights is shown as his feet continue pacing up and down the street. Coming to a rest point with a stop light hanging above him, Stevenson chuckles to himself before announcing to the world with a ovation only in his mind, 'I am your king!'
'King of what? You've lost three straight matches, I don't call that being king of anything!' Desmond called from the alleyway, dragging his feet along the slab of concrete the two men were currently working on together. 'If I'm not mistaken you're more a pauper than a king.'
Cordozer straightens up his voice, cracking his neck back as he leans upwards against a street post. 'Funny, now c'mon celebrate with me!'
'For what?' the calm tone of desmond was becoming more serious as Stevenson batted an eye towards him. 'You don't need that stuff, yet you still take it for whatever reasons you feel towards. Shouldn't you be concerned about that?'
S2 cracks a gleeful smile, 'Nope, not afraid. Besides I think I'm giving a better effort out there.'
'In who's eyes,' Desmond cracked at Stevenson's armor once more with the lash, giving Cordozer something to think about. 'Not mine and not the fans for damn sure!'
'I wasn't supposed to win the All Asia title anyway!' Stevenson bursted out in the open, leaving Desmond to his own silence and crapulence. 'Wilson didn't want the belt handed out quickly so it would become like the Hardcore or 'trash' title of World Wrestling Entertainment.'
'Reason why they canceled the title's life.' the pyro guru remarked. 'Same reason why you'll cancel your own if you keep this up.'
S2 only taps his fingers along the street lamp, 'Don't worry, Ikusa this week will be different, back to the winning ways.'
