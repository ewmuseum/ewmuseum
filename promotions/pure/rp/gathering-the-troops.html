Gathering The Troops

21 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Gathering The Troops - Jamar Wilson has made his intentions to destroy the PURE companies great history, very clear to the whole world. He is hell-bent on the destruction of anything that matters to the PURE Company and its fans. At every turn and chance Jamar is spitting in the face of all those who made PURE so great in the past and present. More importantly he is spitting in the face of all those fans who love PURE just the way it is. They do not want to have their great company turned into another sports entertainment circus. Jamar is smart enough to know however that he will not accomplish this feat on his own. Enter his cronies that he has personally selected to help on his path of sin. Ali...Enimity...Loc... Three men who share the, VP and 20% owner of Pure, same ideas and lack of respect for the men before them. They too wish to condemn PURE to the same fate as other past companies that their great Tokyo Pop has been involved in. Well Zander McCain will not sit ideally by and watch them destroy everything that he loves about PURE. If there is something, other than wrestling, that McCain is good at...Its getting people to rally behind a cause. Many times in the past several years McCain has been able to get people behind a worthy cause that few seemed willing to get behind before. Almost always he has been able to persuade any one he talks to, over to his side of thinking. This time seems to be a much more simple argument. Tradition and honor over sports entertainment. Tradition and honor are things that the Japanese people hold very dear. But will he be able to get them behind him this time? With pride, honor, history, and tradition on the line...It has to!- Rally around me if you truly love PURORESU! :::The out shot opens to a dirt road some where in Japan where we see Zander McCain, dressed in a pair of light blue shorts, a white T-shirt, and sandals, standing on the back of an old flat bed pick-up truck that is slowly running motoring down the road. He has a bull horn in his hands and is really trying to get the people to listen. A few are following the truck slowly down the road as he speaks. ::: WE MUST SAVE PURE FROM BEING DESTROYED BY JAMAR WILSON! ::: More people begin to walk along behind the truck now and are cheering lightly at the sound of stopping Wilson. More people stop and watch what is going on as McCain continues to speak, to a following of about eighty five people now. ::: The time to show Jamar, and his cronies, that their ways are not going to cut it here is, NOW! ::: More cheers now and McCain begins to smile a bit. The rally is working and people are listening to what he says. He feels a rush of adrenaline as he continues on his assault of Jamar and his boys. ::: Jamar wishes to heap shame upon PURE and your fine Puroresu traditions by tainting them with sports entertainment! These are clearly not ideas that are meant to help the future of PURE. NO they are instead a way of...RUINING IT! :::There is some crumbling in both English and Japanese. All of them clearly with Japanese accents but angry all the same. The fans continue to cheer McCain and his thoughts, while cursing Jamar and his followers. ::: Tokyo Pop, the group which Jamar is backed by, are not here in the best interest of PURE and Puroresu. They have shown before that they are only out to spread more destruction and rid the Japanese culture of Puroresu. Tokyo Pop destroyed FMW! :::The people show their displeasure for the destruction of FMW as a they quickly begin to a FMW chant. McCain again smiles as he knows he has the fans right were he wants them now. The time to strike is here. He plunges forward. ::: Together we must put a stop to their treachery. You fans must be willing to do nothing but be completely silent when Jamar or any of his gang of misfits do anything. Dont even boo them. Are you with me? ::: The crowd begins to chant Zander-San, as sign of respect in the Japanese culture being the San at the end of a name, as huge crowd now gathered around on the walk down the road. The crowd of about three hundred are very fired up and about a half dozen actually jump up onto the back of the flatbed truck to fire people up in their native tongues. ::: Thank you very much. With your commitment to this cause I also give you my word, that I will not rest until the threat of Jamar, his cronies, and Tokyo Pop are no longer an issue! This week brings on the beginning of the end for Jamar and his pathetic attempts to destroy the sport we all love so much. Come now we must spread our message even further.! ::: The camera begins to zoom out to get a full shot of the three hundred plus fans following in unison behind the truck continuing their chant of Zander-San all the way. The scene begins to fade to black on the smiling face and sky blue eyes of McCain who is overwhelmed with pride. The scene goes black leaving only the sound of McCains voice to be heard. ::: You will be stopped Jamar. Your ways will not be tolerated by myself, any of the wrestlers with half an ounce of integrity, or the fans. You better change your wicked ways, or you may not have much of a promotion in which to be the 20% owner of. Remember it is all about the fans. Listen to the fans Jamar. They are your only hope for keeping your precious position of power and authority. Ikusa Hall begins this week with Kyle Leman, but shall end with you if you dont heed this one warning.
