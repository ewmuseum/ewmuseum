Slowly Making my way over

20 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Slowly Making my way over The Scene opens up to some sort of home video. In the lower right corner the word REC is flashing. Suddenly on the black screen is a white guy wearing 4 bandanas. Red, black, gray and white, all on his head, over a du rag. He has a fake scar under his eye. He is wearing baggy, baggy clothes. This man speaks, in a Mimic rapper voice Yo mutha fucka! Dis is mutha fuckin Anthony Big dicked mutha fuckin gangsta Balboa. Im fuckin keeping it mutha fuckin real here in the mean ass fuckin streets of Toronto, Err. I mean da T Dot mutha fucka. Earlyer tonight I was keepin it gangsta, I busted on two mutha fuckin white boys. Then I gave props to my home boys mutha fucka! Then on my way to pick up my fuckin crack I was shot in the eye. Thats fucking right. No I wasnt hurt G dog, I took the bullet out and I said Yo mutha fuckin bullet! Get da fuck outta here before I bust a mutha fucking cap on yo ass. Yo man Im so fuckin black Im white! Interesting. Lets cut away from that bit of craziness. Our real scene opens at the Pearson Airport in the T dot, err I mean Toronto. Terminal B. to be exact. Sitting in the middle of the lounge There is man sitting on the bench, laid back, with a couple of bags around his feet. As we zoom in You learn its Anthony Balboa. The Italian Stallion. Who wrestles under BMW Rule. Anthony Balboa is not a big man. 511, 212 Pounds. He looks like a Italian Soccer player more than a pro wrestler, not only because he is wearing a Official Team Italy jersey or the black Kappa pants. Balboas open as an interesting character enters his vision. A midget, With a afro, and a beard, wearing a trench coat and sandals. This man is Anthony Balboas manager and Mentor. He is dragging a bag that is larger than he is. How can you wear that shit? Asks 3 Feet Pete (thats his name, clever huh?) How can you be so short? Retorts Balboa Italy was screwed worse than a 2 Dollar hooker Dont talk about your mother like that Anthony Says Pete grinning Very funny. Very Funny Says Balboa So to Japan I go Yes Japan says Pete Stroking his beard Why are we going again? Dumbass Says Balboa Because I signed with PURE PURE? asks Pete No More BMW? Yes more BMW Corrects Balboa Ill be wrestling under both companys, so you know what that means? Double the money? Says Pete Damn straight Says Balboa I know what your thinking, Why do I have to offer PURE? No Pete says trying to read his own mind I was thinking where can I get a joint Well Balboa says ignoring Pete ok I cant do this shit no more. Im acting like stuck up whore I agree says Pete Nodding like mother like Son When I get to Japan says Balboa playing with his hair Im going to pick up one of Japans world famous hookers Me too! Adds Pete Who you fighting? Umm Balboa says thinking Poster Boy, who thinks hes some sort of gangster and Tal Wong, who I have no clue about A gangster eh? says Pete Didnt you used to be part of the Mafia? yes says Balboa rolling his eyes Thats back when Gangs where noble. Now we got this rap shit. The Mafia could destroy these new age gangs. Bloods? Crips? Ahh Fuck them. But Im out of that shit. Thats the past, Now I hang out with A midget Vertically Challenged Corrects Pete And Ive been known to get a little angry Sure.. Flight 396 To Osaki Japan is now Terminal B. Please begin the boarding process. Thank you very much Well thats us Says Balboa picking up his luggage lets bounce the scene fades to black With Balboa and 3 feet Pete (aka the Odd Couple) heading over to terminal B. Fade to Black
