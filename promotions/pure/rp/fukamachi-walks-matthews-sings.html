Fukamachi walks... Matthews sings!

October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Fukamachi walks... Matthews sings! Written: It was Thursday... The pounding of the rain was all that could be heard. Patter, Patter, Patter... Patter, Patter, Patter... its violent feel would force even the strongest individuals to cower into their houses instead of facing the elements and attempting to survive. The picture of people staying in their homes was going to be common practice among many of the homes in the local community... and the general feel was no better for both Kamui Fukamachi and Chad Matthews, both of whom were confined... to the confinements of their relativly small appartment.
As the picture opened in this appartment, we saw both Kamui and Chad standing at different windows, they were looking out of them... staring at various items in the immediate area as the rain ravashed upon them... crashing down, creating quite the loud noise. Despite the fact that neither of our heroes were able hear this rain inside the house... the sound it made outside was still quite strong, especially for rain.
'Rain, Rain, Go Away... come again some other day...', Matthews sang to himself, continuing to stare out of the window at everything else, ignoring what was happening inside the appartment... ignoring what his friend and PURE Tag Team partner was doing. Fukamachi-san was like Chad, but standing at the other window, looking out as the rain continued.
'Why does it have to rain upon a day where I was supposed to take care of alot of things? Damn the weather... its so unforgiving...', he said to himself, his face starting to decline in emotion as the rain's relentlessness never once held back. Turning away from the window, he clenched both his fists into balls before dropping his arms down by his sides... his hands releasing their clench, his body moving over to one of the chairs some paces away. Slumping down into it, he looks over to Matthews and begins to make another judgement of him, 'Matthews... you seem so interested by the rain... you seem to think that its nothing more than a friend, an ally... a friend who'll keep you interested until the day is through. Why... what do you see in this that I am unable to?'
Fukamachi-san's trail of thought began to vanish into the distance, he moved his attentions away from what Chad was doing and he looked over to the Television set located about three metres away from where he was seated. Searching around for a remote, he managed to find out around his immediate location, picking it up and knocking the volume on the box up a few clicks.
Sooner, rather than later, the screen projected the image of a Japanese Female sitting behind a news desk, holding Papers in her hands, staring into the camera, ready to read out the next story at hand...
'... In other news, it seems as though the rain that we are recieving tonight, may last for another two days or so. Top Scientists of the community have warned citizens not to get out of the beds unless it is an absolute emergency... for they think that even stepping out of your front door raises your chances of death up to around fifty-three percent... thats forty-four percent higher than the risk of death if you stayed within the home. Everyone... please stay inside your homes and don't increase your own chances of being killed...
... and now, in other news...' He aimed the remote at the television again and pressed the 'stand by' switch, killing the power and stopping the female on set telling him anything else about the storm that was occuring upon the outside of their four walls and roof. Glancing back over to his 'friend'... Fukamachi continued to wonder about the fun that Matthews was having by looking out of the window... well, he assumed that he was having fun... but in all due respect, he didn't know if Chad was smiling... crying... or just looking out of the window with the glumness of being stuck inside written upon himself.
The attention swang back over to where Matthews was positioned, looking out of this same window still. He continued to turn away from where Fukamachi-san was... not even realizing that everything he was doing was being watched from the otherside of the room. Chad looked up at the sky, where the rain was coming from... and he began to converse with himself, 'I wonder... if everything is going to be like this on Saturday... when we have to make our debut, infront of all those people from around Japan... and all of those other people around the world. Fukamachi... I don't know if you're thinking about the same thing as me right now, but we've got to face Damian and Chase Hererra... and its a match that we're going to need to win... DAMN IT, I just hope you're confident of what we're going to be getting into Fukamachi!'
Upon ending his line of thought, Matthews brought his fist down upon the window sill that was infront of him and remained still for a few moments. Looking as though he was just stamping his authority down on something... he remained in place, turned away from Fukamachi... his own face going red with pain, looking down at the window sill and seeing that it wasn't a flat surface... but a bumpy one that was also extremly hard.
Fukamachi-san looked over and stared for a few seconds, but didn't say anything as he didn't want to spoil whatever thoughts he believed were going through the mind of Chad Matthews. With the attention of everything shifting back to Fukamachi, he looked to his own right, staring out of the window that he had previously been looking at... and softly shook his head from left to right... not being able to understand the reason why the rain has graced the world in such large and dramatic quantities.
’Why… I am still puzzled to see so much rain fall from the sky… it is impossible for this to be such a logical reason… since I have never been able to see such water drop in quantities quite like this.’, he said to himself, again, trying to think of a reason that could possibly lead to the reason behind this sudden terror of bad weather. Getting up from his chair, he looked over to Chad again… an individual who continued to distance himself from everything else around him. Pondering on whether he should tell Matthews he’s leaving the apartment, Fukamachi decided that Chad would find him missing sooner or later and left without making a sound. As the focus of things turned to Matthews again, the shot shown was the floor… and area where the feet of our ’hero’ were situated. Tears began to drop down slowly… hitting the floor… plop, plop… plop, plop…
Matthews turned towards the window, looking out again to see the few people who dared to brave everything that the newscaster had told them earlier. Pressing against the window, he continued to stare at these narrow minded folks… watching their titanic struggles against everything else that ‘Mother Nature’ had to throw at them.
“Tomodachi ni… tegami wo kaku toki mitai ni… {In Spite that it would be nice if words flow out to me…} Surasura to kotoba… ga dete kureba ii no ni… {Like when one writes a latter to a friend…} Mou sukoshi… o-tagai wo… shiriau ni wa, jikan ga hoshii…
{That the only one who won’t betray you is your family…}
Sabishisugiru yo… Love is asking to be loved… {Is too lonely a thing Love is asking to be loved…} Shinjiru koto wo, yamete shimaeba raku ni natte… wakaru kedo”
{I know I’ll be happy if I stop believing but…}
Without knowing it, Chad Matthews had broken out into singing a verse of Romanji… something that he didn’t know was within him. Maybe it was something that he’d been able to pick up over his times watching Anime and the sort… but it just seemed too weird for him to have been singing this song without any kind of notes as to how that song was performed. Matthews turned away from the window, looking a little more content with himself… looking a little more happy following his triumphant attempt to sing such a song… in such a language on his first go.
Walking away from the spot that he had spent so much time standing at, Matthews walked away… moving over to the other-side of the room… a location where he had expected to find his Tag Partner. Unable to find Fukamachi-san to express the news of being able to sing at least one verse of a song in Romanji, he felt a little depressed… but all the more desperate to reveal his hidden talent.
“Hmm… I wonder where Fukamachi-san could have wondered to in this kind of weather… it’d be stupid for the guy to have left the apartment…,” Chad asked himself aloud, free to bathe in the fact that nobody would be around to call him a strange little man upon hearing it. Shifting through the ‘Main Quarters’ the apartment for another few moments, Chad was unfortunate to find anything with regards to the location of his Team Mate.
Shaking his head from side to side in something of an easy motion, Chad Matthews moved over to a location near the front door, pulling a jacket from the ‘Jacket Rack’ and putting it on, opening the apartment exit and stepping out into part of the weather that was being hailed as some of the most dangerous around the world. This small tidbit of information was unknown by Matthews as he threw his coat upon himself and started to walk down the Apartment platform, looking down at the floor every few seconds to see if Kamui had been as unfortunate enough as to have dropped off the balcony and fallen down to an untimely demise. As the scene began to fade out into the confinements of black, Chad Matthews started to climb down the silver ladder that would take him to the ground floor of the complex.
Would Matthews be able to find the location of Fukamachi-San? Would Matthews be able to survive such terrible conditions? The answers to both of these questions and more, next time…
