Shunned by Toyko Pt. 3

21 January 2003

Shunned by Toyko Pt. 3 Written: 1/21/03 The Scene opens up outside a small cottage in Toyko, Japan. The cottage is very compact with no other houses or anything around it. Just open field with high corn fields. On the top of the door of the cottage reads 'Kami's House'. Suddenly Erik Blake comes into the screen looking at the cottage. He whips out a sheet of paper and looks at the cottage.
Erik Blake- 'Kami's Lookout'. Yup, this is it. This is the creator of the greatest action-packed cartoon show ever to hit television screens. Dragonball.
Blake sighs as he walks to the door of the cottage. He examines the door and finds the doorbell. He presses it and the theme to Dragonball Z. A smile hits Blake's face as he starts dancing the to theme as the door starts to open. A short, bald, half-naked man with saggy nipples walks to the door.
Erik Blake- Good God! You must be Akira Toriyama! Akira Toriyama- Yes, yes, I am. Now, what do you want? Erik Blake- Mr. Toriyama! I am the biggest Dragonball fan! Akira Toriyama- Yea, yea, you and millions of other Americans. Erik Blake- No, no! I am of Asian descent! Like you! Akira Toriyama just staresat him for a moment in a ocnfused look as he starts to shake his head.
Akira Toriyama- Whatever, what do you want? Erik Blake- I wanted to know if I could spend the day with you. Finding out stuff about the genious.
Akira Toriyama- You got 10 minutes...go. Akira Toriyama walks in as Blake hyperly walks in behind him. He walks in to find various Dragonball posters of all three sagas.
Erik Blake- OH! Majin Buu! HA! Teen Goten! PAAAAAAAAAAN!! Akira Toriyama- Calm down. The more you yell the more time you trim off. Erik Blake- Oh, sorry. I wanted to ask you how come you didn't authorize Dragonball GT? Akira Toriyama- None of your buisness. Any other questions? Erik Blake- Just one...how is Dragonball AF going? Akira Toriyama jumps up in startle-ment and stares at Blake. Akira Toriyama- How did you know--uh...I mean I'm not making anymore Dragonball series. Erik Blake- Hmm, then the millions of millions of Dragonball AF pictures and such on the internet must be fake.
Akira Toriyama- Show me, now! Blake reaches in his back pocket and pulls out some folded papers and shows various characters in the Dragonball series a bit older. Akira Toriyama snatches the pictures and starts to edge Blake out.
Akira Toriyama- TREACHEROUS AMERICAN! Blake turns around only to get the door of slammed in his face. Erik Blake- I'M ASIAN YOU OLD FOOL! A-S-I-A-N!
