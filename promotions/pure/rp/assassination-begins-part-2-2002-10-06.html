Assassination Begins!Part 2 Written: 06/10/02 {Begin Trans} Orlando is the type of man who is anti-social to strangers, he is the man that you would see on the street who would be a rude person, but when you get to know him, he is a great person. Not the kind of person that would smile at any given time, it’s a rare occurrence, unless with his son Sebastian. He does not have many friends, except for a few guys around the trailer park. When the two women he saved to come onto a talk show, so they could properly thank him asked him, Orlando made some excuse on how he was busy. He was happy to save he two, but he is not a man that needs recognition for the things he achieves or does. He likes being simple, which is rare that he would like to become a wrestler. The fans will definitely have mixed reactions for the young star, not a guy that likes to express his emotions. He plans to express himself in the ring, and hopefully the fans will respond.
The day is chilly, but the sun was out. The chimes outside the trailer were swaying back and forth consistently. Sebastian is home, he was given permission to miss school because of the anniversary, and they have made today a code yellow. Sebastian is having a nap on Orlando’s bed, while Orlando is filling out a PURE personalization form. The form is some basic questions involving weight, height, gimmick and other needed information.
It takes him around an hour or so filling out the needed information. He revises it and seals it with an envelope, and mails it off to a nearby mailbox. Orlando then makes his way back to his trailer. He sits on the lawn chairs set in front of the trailer, that are placed on top of the hard mud grounds. The sound of the radio is blaring in the back from the inside of the trailer. He silently sits there, thinking of all the recent happenings in his life. This is usually the time he remembers his fallings, such as his deceased wife and his loss of a great job as a Navy Seal.
As he lies back, with a bud in his hand, his neighbor Bill walks by, and has a seat in the lawn chair next to Orlando. He usually is half cut most of the day. So Bill is always interesting to talk too.
“ Hey buddy, what the hell is going on? There is nothing on television but talk shows and news!”
Bill is a rather plumb individual, who at this time is just wearing overalls, no shirt underneath so he is showing his chubby arms and chest. Of course with a beer in his hand, he leans back in his seat, waiting for an answer!”
“ Bill its September 11th!” Bill sits their very confused, becoming quite clear he is not a smart man. “ Is that some kind of war holiday? Either way they shouldn’t take up every channel! That kind of thing can ruin a guys day!”
Orlando smirks, and just shakes his head and takes a long chug of his beer. “ Are you serious? On this day a year ago, the Trade Center was attacked. Please tell me beer has not affected your mind that bad! And if you found a job, maybe you would not have to worry about your shows not being on.”
“ Speak for yourself pal, as I recall you’re also unemployed.” “ Actually I have a job now, I am a professional wrestler. Well actually I am in an academy, but if I perform to a certain extent. Then I will be promoted to a big time federation.”
After Orlando finishes his sentence, the rare day becomes vigil, as the rain begins to pour down in huge portions. Bill says his good bye and wishes Orlando lucked on his new career. Orlando crushes his beer can and throws it into his outdoor fire pit, which was just recently let out, due to the rain. He then rushes inside the trailer, closing the screen door behind him. The rain is very loud inside the trailer because of the thin roof that the trailer has. It instantly wakes up Sebastian and he immediately goes straight to the refrigerator.
Sebastian is making himself a sandwich with the limited produce in the Scott’s refrigerator. While Orlando is walking towards the couch, the phone rings. Orlando picks up the phone, and on the other end it is his newly acquired agent of Orlando’s.
“ Orlando, Jim Ross here. I have some good news. You have a match coming up at the next event. Ikusa 4 is the PURE weekly event, and you are in a PURE Jr. Weight Chaos Battle Royal match. And the good news is that the Jr. Weight title is on the line.”
Jim excited about the news, and hoping for a reaction from Orlando along the same lines. Orlando just takes the news gently, as he flicks through the channels on his 13’ television. And replies in a non-interested tone.
“ That’s great Jim.” “ And you will be facing an assortment of new PURE wrestlers like yourself. It should be a great chance to get your name out in PURE! Here are the lists of names you face in the match. First is Damien, Dr. Disco Inferno, Ilan Levin, Agent X and Junzo Muto. They are all looking to prove there newly acquired talent just as much as you are, you should be training hard for this debut Orlando!”
Orlando then gets up, and looks through the cupboards and grabs a bag of chips, and then goes back and grabs a seat on the couch.
“ I will be ready, as a matter of fact I am preparing as we speak.” Orlando continues watching television and eating his potato chips. Jim realizing his intelligence is being toyed with, so he wraps up the conversation.
“ Well that’s pretty much it, other then that I’ll just keep you updated Orlando. Say hello to your son for me, and I will see you tomorrow at the local gym!”
After Orlando hangs up the phone, he puts his chips down, and realizes he is out of shape. Not that it shows, physically he looks fine, but his endurance will be lacking. Orlando decides to have a run, first time since his Navy Seal days.
About six minutes into his run, he begins walking. Very disappointed, as he used to be able to run for days. Orlando is now realizing he could be beaten in his match. Orlando is usually confident in his skill, and at the moment he knows for the next few days, he will have to work hard to be prepared for his big debut match.
When he returns home from his run, Orlando rushes to the sink, and hurdle’s his mouth under the tap, and has a long drink of tap water. Before he calls it a night, he does a few lunges, sit ups and push-ups. Sebastian already fast asleep in the pull out couch in the living room, so Orlando decides to get some sleep himself, as he grabs a quick shower and hits the sack. The question on all mind is, can Orlando prepare himself on time before his match. Orlando will have to set the bar high, we will all see if his time in the Navy Seal was worth it, by defeating his challenges. Only time will tell. Can the highly anticipated Orlando Scott make a career out of himself in wrestling? Only time will tell!
