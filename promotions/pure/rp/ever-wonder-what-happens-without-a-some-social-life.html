Ever wonder what happens without a some social life? Written: I sit here at home, not a huge luxurious mansion, rather a ‘regular’ three bedroom, two storey house…nothing much. Anyway, I sit on the sofa, hands shaking as they grip with each other, huge swarms of sweat beads flowing all over my body, my eyes wide and my body twitching…and every time I hear a voice or a bang close by, I turn in my seat, look out the closest window and teeth chatter off the tips of my finger nails.
WHAT THE FUCK’S GOING ON JAYO?!?! What the hell’s happening to you?! How the bloody hell am I supposed to know?!? I’ve been grand the last few days…ever since I’ve came here I’ve been grand, not a bother, no trouble, just keeping myself to myself. Maybe Martians abducted me and performed perverse tests on my private parts. Umm…interesting thought, yea that’s what happened.
Damn television, nothing on except all these squinty-eyed people talking gibberish…wait a minute, that sounded racist. I’M NOT A RACIST! There’s nothing wrong with Americans and their squinty-eyes, nothing at all…they’re just like Japanese people, just with different eyes. Did I say that right? Did I say anything at all? No, that was a thought. I’m such a dumbarse. Did I think that right though? Err…no I didn’t. D’UH! Everyone knows Americans are a figment of your explanationisms that are transferred to you mind whenever you step on eggshells…grr…what was that noise?
Sounds like a doorbell. Let me look out the window. Just stay here for a minute, okay? Um..two girls are at the door, they’re Jewish, I can tell because they have squinty eyes. Jewish porn rocks. I think I should let them in, but I kind of smell funny…whew, is it hot in here? I’m sweating like an Irishman in Japan!
Alright folks, I’m at the door, this is it, my big climatic moment. Here I go, wish me luck! Damn, this knob is slippy, oh, that’s my hand, sorry! Ok…twisting the knob, I’m twisting the knob, hiccch, KNOB! HAHAHA!
“Hello, welcome to the neighbourhood.” “Yea, I like Meat Loaf too.” They gave me funny lucks, I felt nervous…I never feel nervous, I’M A MAN! Or a goat, my brothers always called me a goat. Whatever.
“I am Tika Arasi, we are on the local welcome committee.” These Jews are weird. I’d invite them in so they don’t get caught by the Nazi’s, but they make me itchy and wet around my groin….OH SHITE!! Somebody had an accident.
“Piss off Jewish Johova Witnesses. I didn’t come to Jewland to be pestered by you.” I slammed the door and ran up to stairs to change my clothes…but I didn’t have any clean…so I just came down naked, no big deal. That’s what I though until a couple of Jew kids ran away screaming. I closed the curtains and didn’t answer the knocking on the door…little fuckers are like munchens, scare the poopie out of me.
I just sat back on the sofa…. DRIIINNNG! DRIIINNNG! “AAAHHH!!” I was scared shitless…no, I’m not being literal, but still, it was pretty scary. It was only the phone, I answered, because, if I didn’t that’d be rude and I ain’t rude. It was Moma.
“Jason, Jason” she said all excited like, “Mammy, Mammy” I said acting all funny…but I didn’t feel funny, my privates were chilly. A bit chilly on the willy, mwuahahahaha…heh.
“The Real IRA are disbanding, son!” I thought about this for a second before replying. “But what about the fake one?” She stayed silent for a few seconds before letting out a bellow of laughter; I don’t like people laughing at me…so I slammed the phone down. Well, after calling her an ugly old female leprechaun…leprechauns are real, people confuse leprechauns with Americans…Americans scare me…or is that leprechauns?
You get the picture…right?
