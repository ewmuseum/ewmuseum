Treacherous Swine

30 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Treacherous Swine Written: 1.30.03 Voice: Get over here with my Chicken! (Japan is bustling this fine afternoon as hundreds of Japanese tourists and locals walk down the crowded streets of Downtown Tokyo. Sitting outside one of the best restaurants in Japan are Chen Lei Chia and a small group of fellow Black Dragon brothers. They are loud, irritating, and arrogant but the owners of Hunan Express allow them to stay and play their Mahjong and eat their General Tsao Chicken out of pure fear. For everyone in the land of the rising sun knows that any man that wears the logo of the Black Dragon is not to be messed with! For their acts of brutality, power, and their overwhelming numbers are notorious even to the tourists. Chen slams a Mahjong piece down screaming out in victory as his fellow Black Dragon brothers begin to laugh just before their General Tsao Chicken arrives! Four waitresses dressed in aprons, t-shirts with the Hunan logo, and schoolgirl skirts walk up to the two Dragon filled tables. Chen slams his hand on the table surprising many of the tourists and locals that are walking nearby and even causing many to look over at the loud man.)
Chen: CHICKEN! Now we eat! (The Dragon boys slide the Mahjong pieces off the table and make room for the plates of their favorite dish. As the food sits ready to pick at some of the younger Dragon members take up their chopsticks but get slapped across the face by veterans of the “family”. Everyone then bows their heads in prayer in which Chen himself leads.)
Chen: Victory… Defeat… Power… Dominance… Life… Friendship… From everyone here right now… To everyone who has died in Dragon honor… We praise thee Lord of the Mountains! God of the Winds! The almighty…
(Chen pauses as everyone recites the last verse, which is similar to people saying Amen in other types of prayers.)
Dragons: PAI PO! (Chen slams his fist down shaking his glass of newly arrived Sake and lifting his plate of chicken right off the tabletop. He smiles as everyone around looks up at him with surprised looks on their faces.)
Chen: NOW!!! We eat! (The two tables of Dragons take up their sticks and chow down on some spicy General Tsao chicken. They carry on a light conversation compiled of jokes, crude sexual innuendos just your basic talk that a group of guys would have. They talked about anything and everything except for PURE, the Dragons recent defeat for the Trinity titles and basically everything involving wrestling.)
Unnamed Dragon: You guys heard about Kazu? (Everyone continues to eat but they look up at the speaker as he downs some sake before continuing. Chen stops eating and watches the unnamed Dragon wondering if the rumors were true…)
Unnamed Dragon: Well come to find out, Kazu WAS working for the Yakuza this past year or so! He was stealing some merchandise and setting up pick offs with the Yakuza making it look like random hit and runs!
(Everyone gasps in shock at the news. Not many members of the Dragon triad cross their brothers for personal gain! Yet this Kazu character not only crossed his brothers and family, but he stabbed them all in the back stealing from them and setting up his comrades, joining the Yakuza ranks and sealing his fate! Chen smiled knowingly as assassinations are always given to the top ranking members or to the newer, younger, hungrier brothers of the Dragon. He smiled and chuckled to himself tuning out as thoughts of his younger days run through his mind. The day he was assigned to his first assassination. (Read “First Assassination”, which will be up soon to find out more of this fateful day!)
Unnamed Dragon: Hey Chen!? (Chen shakes his head and returns back to the conversation at hand noticing that every pair of Dragon eyes were on him at this point. He almost begins to wonder how long he was daydreaming but the next question prevented him from pondering that. Instead he began pondering something new altogether.)
Unnamed Dragon: So what is going to happen to Kazu? (Chen smirks viciously that only promises retribution! Chen looks into each and every one of his brothers eyes smirking all the while. He finally rests his eyes on the unnamed Dragon who brought this subject to light.)
Chen: Each and every one of us here underwent the Trials; we all took the 36 Oaths… Kazu underwent the Trials and took the 36 Oaths just like the rest of us! It’s a shame that he lost faith and has decided to aid our enemies. Thus sealing his fate as our enemy! The First Oath we all took was ‘I shall not betray my Dragon brothers for if I do may I die by the sword of my sworn brothers! And cursed throughout my afterlife! May I die a million deaths for betrayal is inexcusable!’ You see guys, no matter what, he sealed his fate and it is now up to the Mountain Lord to pass his judgment! You must continue as if you know nothing! For helping those who betray is a betrayal in it’s own and telling him is helping him!
(Everyone around the table nods their heads in agreement and they go back to their chicken and sake without anymore talk of betrayal, Kazu, retribution, or anything else! These men, soldiers, warriors, but above anything they are men! These men took an oath, underwent a trial… These men pledged allegiance to the lord of the mountain and the god of the wind Pai Po and a similar oath to their fellow Dragons who they consider brothers! When a man breaks that bond, that allegiance, they have sealed their fate and they know death is not far!)
TIME GOES BY (After many hours of eating, playing games and just having fun relaxing with their fellow comrades, these men take their leave from Hunan Express. Everyone but Chen goes back to their favorite brothel Asian Delights in which the Dragons base their operations out of. Chen on the other hand takes a walk through Japan’s bustling streets and down a side trail, a dissolute road that leads to an abandoned lake and park. The lake is man made but is abandoned because it is the place where many lost their lives. It’s a place that people on the streets once referred to as the Dream Seeker. This place was once used by young couples that would go to be together and bound their love with intimacy. It is also the place where the fateful battle known as the Dragon Raid took place. Hundreds of Yakuza and Dragon members alike died in the park that lies next to the beautiful pond. The waters ran red with the blood of fallen men as a war broke out between the two gangs. Once a beautiful thriving “hip” place to be is now a dissolute, abandoned, dried up place that everyone wishes they could forget.)
Chen: Dream Seeker… I am here once again… (Chen walks down the road along the bank of the dried up lake as thoughts run wild through his head. The recent defeat to the Sushi Crew and Shaun Styles for the Trinity Titles put a damper in things as Chen thinks how could anyone think the Dragons dangerous, how could anyone think the Dragons a team to be feared when they lose to the likes of Ryu and Otoshi and Shaun Styles! Chen’s face droops in disappointment as he walks along the bank of the lake on his way to the small playground nearby. As he walks it suddenly begins to rain, which is fitting for the mood that he is in. The fat drops of rain ricochet off his gloomy face as he continues his trek to the playground.)
Chen: How fitting… (He looks up at the sky as the rain bounces off his face and even hits him in the eyes and down his open mouth.) As if you mock me! As if you laugh in my face! Well I will show you! I will show everyone! Sushi Crew and Styles will see the true power of Chen Lei Chia as will the entire world!
(Chen walks over and sits down on a swing and begins to rock back and forth leaving him alone with his thoughts as the rain continues to fall atop him. The scene fades out with Chen slightly swinging back and forth with thoughts from Kaku to Sushi Crew and Styles. What can we expect to see from Chen of the Dragons? And what is Chi’ing up to?)
