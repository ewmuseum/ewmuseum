All is Fair in the Game of Evil

27 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
All is Fair in the Game of Evil Written: 2/27/03 So what else was I to expect from a washed up has been such as himself? He's 'old', he even said it himself. He also said he was scared. It's like a cheetah choosing it's prey, the old, weak, or wounded. The thoughts of how easy this match at Final Destinies was going to be ran through my mind ever since I heard that he was scared, and that he wouldn't be able to handle the World title all day as I sat in the restaurant in the hotel I was staying at.
I had no clue why I was just sitting in the corner booth of this little restaurant, but I did. I continually ordered water every time a waitress came over to ask me if I needed anything. Maybe I was waiting for Yoshikiro to show up, or maybe even some hot b*tch to come over and talk to me, but more than likely, this little poorly lit corner was acting as a personal Hell for me right now, allowing me time to think of how to pick apart Optimum Violence Ramey, piece by piece this Sunday.
I couldn't remember how many times I had been given dirty looks all day, and I was pretty sure that I enjoyed it, fuel to my fire as I always say. The waitress, looking fairly annoyed this time, walked over with her little pad out, and her pencil in hand as she stood in front of me, her hips leaning to one side, and she blew a stand of her blonde hair out of her face as she came over to see what else I needed.
Waitress: 'If all you want is water again, just nod, OK? I'm getting fairly annoyed at walking over here, and doing nothing but serving you water.'
So I did what my instincts told me to do... Be a smart ass. I nodded my head, gave a fairly sick and twisted smile that brought a disgusted look on her face as she turned around, and began to walk away.
Lee: 'And bring back a menu too!' I wasn't sure if she heard me or not, but she walked fairly fast away from my table. She returned fairly quickly as well, a big smile on her face, and she gladly stuck her chest in my face as she sat my water down on the table for me. Not exactly, but Frank did mention how nice it would have been, so I decided to drop my fork on 'accident' to try and call it even. She picked it up for me, Bob climbed down off my right shoulder and managed to hold me back from grabbing something I wasn't supposed to.
Waitress: 'There you go, I'll be back for your order.' Lee: 'Yeah.' She walked away, probably smiling all happy and joyful, it was making me sick just thinking about it. She was probably going to go back there, rant and rave about how I'll probably order a shit ton of food since I had been there all day, then leave this huge ass tip for her.
Lee: 'I hope I burst her bubble by the end of the night.' I was going to too. Sit there and order a few more waters before I grab a hamburger and fries, then leave without dropping a tip anywhere for her. I thought about leaving my room number down, OK, Frank told me I should, but I wasn't going to. Even though I hadn't had sex since I was 17, I'd much rather think about how bad I am going to kick Ramey's, excuse me, Optimum Violence's ass at Final Destinies, on my road to becoming the first ever PURE World Heavyweight Champion!
Lee: 'Scared! Ha, who would've thought Ramey would have ever grown enough balls to finally admit it. I'm sure he wouldn't want me putting him through another announcer's table though. But having enough nerve to say anything about being fair? I'm as fair as anyone in this fed!'
Of course I believe myself, didn't matter what anyone else said anyway! Raizo, or anyone else from the Saikyo System interfering in mine and his match? That's f*cking ludicrous, well, maybe not, but since he said something about it, I'll prove his little theory wrong. Just then the waitress came back over, and I hadn't even noticed. I began to rant and rave.
Lee: 'I'll prove to you that I don't need anyone else’s help just to kick your ass. I may had original intentions of having them run down and helping me win, but I don't need them if this is how you are going to be you stupid little b*tch.'
The waitress looked horrified as she turned around, and walked away. Oh well, I wasn't feeling like eating anything anyway. My free twenty glasses of water was good enough for me as I stood up, and headed for the exit of the restaurant. I began thinking to myself as I put my hands into the jacket I neglected to take off all afternoon. I wondered if the wrestler full of pride would buy my whole spiel or not.
Lee: 'Ha, me, not doing anything in my power to win? You've got to be s*itting me!' I was ashamed at myself for saying it. But if it was going to give me the bigger advantage, bigger than what I already had, then I was going to say it, do it, and whatever else. I kept in my same train of thought as I removed my right hand from my jacket pocket, and pushed open the front door of the hotel. I walked out into the cold night, my breath instantly visible as it hit the crisp air, I remained standing in front of the entrance to the hotel. I began speaking aloud what I would say directly to Ramey's face if he were here right now.
Lee: 'Ramey, or whatever the hell you want to be called, it may not be right in your mind to cheat, but in my world, anything is fair. Evil has no right or wrong, it has no shame, and it definitely has no remorse. So whether or not any of the Saikyo System makes their way to the ring during our match or not, remember this! The only thing fair about Evil... Is everything!'
I breathed once more, looking at my own breath as I finally headed out into the dark streets of Tokyo. I wasn't looking for any place in particular to go, I was just going.
The scene fades to black.
