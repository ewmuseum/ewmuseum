Hymns for The Fallen [3]: ...Family and Friends... Written: 09/10/02 It wasn't happening to him towards something in the mouth where the spoon stuck out. Fresh minded, you sense something new in people at times. Like a waterfall falling down upon the face, it cleanses a person from the dirt, from the unimaginable. Vito Wynn wasn't even a person he found as a threat, nobody came to be a threat to him anymore. They didn't even try to phase him out of the deep zone that he was in. Use a quote from somebody whom the rap game knows, but doesn't respect thanks to two faced people.
'I'm focused man.' Its was Vito Wynn, a question of who was supposed to break upon the minds of everyone involved. Think back towards what people meant by carry on and you might find Vito Wynn down as maybe the 8th term of the word. Questioning trust in some people is needed, but guess what? Nine imes out of ten, nobody sticks with the same thing they began their careers with. You wondered about which way he would go later on in his career, in whatever place he could end up. Wondered about the people he could be associated with.
His mind was on a mission, he wasn't rich but kind of royalty in the place. And now the riches shall become bigger and bigger, him becoming richer and richer.
You see it, you want it? Buy it. Own it. He saw Vito Wynn, he wanted Vito Wynn. Forget buying Vito Wynn, he would own Vito Wynn. Because he could do that. ____________________ .:Centauri:. The door slowly creeped open, with the voice yelling out 'I'm home.' The rushing of a person's feet along the beige carpet just to greet him. His arms swing wide open with a smile on his face, a long chain around his neck and a large microphone medallion hanging from his neck. It was a time of rest and something to be in part of. His aunt stood by the entrance to the kitchen as the small child clutching to him tightly yipped and hollered in amazement for his uncle being there.
'How long you gonna be here Uncle Cordozer?' the small kid asked him, hoping it would be a long time as a glimmer of light from the porch shinned through before Cordozer shut the door behind him. As he took a seat at the kitchen table, which was more like a island his knees banged against the wood holding, where on the left side held two cabinets touching the floor. 'Long as it takes for my mind to ease.' he responded.
The young boy's aunt stepped between the two of them with a cup of Sprite and Hawaiian Punch in her hand and a stirrer in the other. 'Good,' she said with nodding approval. 'you need some rest before heading out for that match. Kahil, let your Uncle get settled before you start bugging him.'
'Thanks Aunt Liz, but I think I'll manage to be all right.' Cordozer told her, still wearing his Oakland Raiders jersey and black Nike sweats. A smile grew on Kahil's face, maybe because he always just like his Uncle, music was kind of his best friend. He happened to cut CD's for his friends for money and was making pretty good out of it. Kahil's main problem though was that, his hair happened to be taken out from braids and cut shorter. Unlike Cordozer, he liked braids.
Laying down on the leather sofa, Cordozer stretched out his feet comfortably with sigh to kick in with. He closes his eyes before feeling a hand come along the top of his head. He screams out in shock as he sees his aunt standing over him, her hand receding back.
'What you do that for?'he asked her with an uproar of everything in the point of which, his mind has been cut off from his original serenity. 'Don't put your feet on furniture.' she responded.
'Why?'he asked. 'Because, do you do that at your house?' 'Well yes! It is mine afterall, and I do have a right to be comfortable during these times of stress! So ease off!'
'Well you're in my house now, so follow the damn rules before you end up on I-10 with a split head and a sore ass. I know you understand that.' she snapped after he happened to back talk towards her. His head was still raised in defiance as he huffed.
'I understand, but really do you have to be so...'he said before stopping, watching his words. He turned his head away from her and then settled them on the big screen television in the dining room. Flicking on the vibrant colors with his remote control, everything ended up being bright and luminous with attention to every detail. The color on persons were just true to life and every item had its definite shape and texture outline.
Cordozer didn't look back at his aunt, but kept a steady eye on her incase she ended up popping him on the head again. 'So when did you get this model?' he asked her with silent comfort to himself, acting like a beer bellied father with a cold Miller Lite in his hand. She responded, 'I think August last year, kept having problems with it though.'
Beginning to walk up the stairs, Kahil headed towards his room, stepping on the wood flooring installed about three months ago. Pulling his bedroom door open, Kahil leaps onto his bed, eying pictures on his walls of Lil' Kim, Tracy Bingham, Lil' Flip, and an assortment of pictures from his trips to the Sado Beach Party in 2001 and 2002.
Pushing a button on his stereo equipment, a compact disc tray pops open, waiting for access and music to begin booming out. He loaded in a mix CD he created thanks to the music program KaZaA and loads it in. First single, 'This Means War' by Busta Rhymes feat. Ozzy Osbourne.
____________________ Take a look inside You can run and you can hide If you cross my path I'll make sure you feel my wrath Give and you receive Cherish every breath you breathe Scriptures on the wall Those who betray all must fall ____________________ Kahil begins to nod his head, before stopping and venturing into the other room, looking at the television in the back room, until finding through the window, a shining car light heading towards his and his aunts driveway. He can see the car, a Mitsubishi Eclipse silver with wood indoor rolling up and parking alongside a candy red Mazda 626. He knew the car, as it made frequent visits, but knew the person inside was a person Cordozer didn't want to speak to no time soon.
'Oh...' Kahil was saying before the camera changed the look of the face on Dante's in order for him to complete the sentence, '..sh^t.'
'What?' Liz asked from the kitchen where she slaved over the stove fixing many Southern delights. Cordozer only shook his head, as he peeped out of the living room window, banging his foot along a dining table in the process. He screams out in pain, but concels it to be a small thing. Eyeing the Eclipse, he spots the driver door poping open from its constraints and causing the driver to come out.
Cordozer turns his head facing Liz, 'She's here.' Cordozer waits for the door to creak open, silence throughout the house as he happens to wait in anticipation and only in that meeting his hand reaches for the knob, twisting it open. The seconds of wait aren't enough as Cordozer pulls the door without hesitation and ends up coming face to face, in something which wasn't new to him and that smile on her face. He felt weak.
'Hi.' he said softly, with her dragging her purse around her arm. It kept him quiet to the single moments of what could happen between the both of them. For one moment they stood in front of each other not able to even move. 'Hi yourself.' she repeated to him. Time stood still between them, they looked at each other as he backed off and let her in the house.
Slamming the door behind her. *** He took a seat, his eyes in nothing but a complete focus of whether or not he was conscious or not in order to understand her words. It had been long, four months long and since then Cordozer has gone through some amazing turbulence during that. All he could think about was how in the hell could he be not thinking of her looking like this, at his most timeless thoughts. Yeah it was different from New York for sure, but the game hadn't changed. Besides, this was his new base of operations so he had to get situated.
Kahil busted in from upstairs, 'Cordozer, you gon' help me with my trip Friday?' Cordozer kept his eyes on Botan for he didn't know how long and in that one piece of moment, the two of them struck a chord within one another and he gasped out, 'Yeah, I'll chaperone you kids.' Under his breath, 'while I take care of this beauty right now.'
'What was that?' Botan barked out at him, causing a smile to grow on his face. He knew it was working like a charm, the first way he baged her in the first place.
Snickering, he calmly settled down into the seat, but was close to end up falling out of the chair since it did have no arms and was basically a horse without a saddle. 'Nothing, just talk. So you got a job down here?'
'Yeah, and I see you're still in this rap game, trying to atleast maintain something to yourself.'
'You forgot one thing.' 'What?' 'Wrestling.' 'Wrestling! Like The Rock?!' she exclaimed before he quiet her down with placing a finger on her lip.
'Not like The Rock, but kind of.' 'Oh, PTC?' She was catching on pretty quick as Cordozer rocked out of that chair and they conversated for about an hour and a half. It was running smoothly, until Kahil came rushing down stairs with that booming voice he must have gotten from Cordozer. 'Dude, you've been talking to her for like eternity. I leave Friday and you ain't helpin' a bit!'
'Would you shut up? Please?' he pleaed with him, not in that slave manner but in a fashion for which he knew for a simple fact that he had to be nice. Afterall, a ride to San Antonio took five hours and going to Fiesta Texas wasn't a joke, but when you got there, the drive was worth it.
'Fine, and while you're at it, tell her about the Crucifix match with Wynn.' Her religious beliefs, knowing for her being a full well Christian had broken that chord in half. It was one of those please don't happen situations, but Cordozer had to hear it from the religious group, instead of the Parent Television Council telling him that acting like a crucifed Christ could end up costing him a job.
'Crucifix?!' 'Yeah....but it isn't for one of those dark mantra things, just payback at someone's very own game. Like the trick I played on Thomas for putting shit in my locker back in sophomore year.'
'That was funny, this ain't.' 'CORDOZER!' Kahil's voice cried out in the essence of which he was needed like Superman comes for a screaming victim soon to be. 'Sh^t, guess we gotta wrap this up.'
Kiss on the cheek and then she went up to her room inside of the house. Cordozer sighed to himself before starting to make his own trek up the staircase. If it wasn't for Wynn taking his time with things, then Cordozer would have ran up in Botan sooner. Oh well, guess with the unwinding, Cordozer could add another 3 to his total and be the odds on favorite for the World title. Another thing on his mind afterall, it was the biggest thing in PURE right now and he wanted to be the very first.
So why couldn't it be said for winning the PURE Heavyweight title, he would be the first. First comes First, Vito Wynn.
