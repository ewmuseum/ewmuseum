Playin' some pool...

6 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Playin' some pool... Written: 6th February 2003 (O.O.C : I realise this roleplay isn't the best in the world. It's just to tie up the loose ends made in my previous roleplay. Thanks.)
Scene opens up to the same bar, in the same back room as Marah and his new “friends” were seen a couple of Fridays ago. This time, the only people that are in the company of one another is Jason Marah and Binsei Kinaka. Marah is standing, leaning on his pool cue wearing a loose white shirt and blue jeans. Binsei is clad in a pair of plain black trousers and a silk black shirt, with the sleeves rolled up. As Binsei leans over the table to set up his shot he proceeds to question Jason.
Binsei Kinaka: So, last time we were here was some night, eh? Jason Marah: Lad, I was so pissed that night the only thing I can remember is the bottom of the pint glass.
Marah laughs, while Binsei just sports a smile, knowing that the activities of two weeks prior will remain a secret and pots the ball he targeted but then misses his bonus shot. He moves away from the table and Marah takes his place, scanning the best possible shot.
Binsei Kinaka: Not much happened, just a lot of drinking and foolery, heh. Good night though, I think we all enjoyed it.
Jason Marah: Ah yea, it was probably good craic. Binsei Kinaka: Craic? Jason messes up the shot, hitting the side of the ball, making it spin uncontrollably off the skirting and missing all the pockets in its way. Jason laughs it off.
Jason Marah: Bah, pool’s never been my thing. Craic is Irish slang, means fun basically heh.
Binsei Kinaka: Ah, I see. How’s life in PURE for ya? Binsei steps up to the pool table as Marah goes back to his pint of the “black stuff”. Jason Marah: Fair enough, ya know. Not much happening. Bit of a bitch only been booked once while others are getting their names out there.
Binsei Kinaka: It’ll probably all come in time. You put on a good match in that All-Asia match.
Jason Marah: Cheers, though, not enough to keep me on PURE television evidently. Binsei is potting the majority of his balls, while Marah is left with the majority of his. Binsei Kinaka: I hear there’s some sort of a open invitation match on the upcoming PURE, winner gets a title shot if my memory serves me correctly. You should take part.
Jason Marah: Aye, I probably will. At least it’s some sort of fuckin’ exposure. Like, how the hell am I supposed to be followed by PCW’s front office when I’m not even competing week to week?
Binsei Kinaka: True enough, my friend. Any chance you can get me a couple of tickets for Ikusa?
Binsei cracks a smile and Jason shares the sentiment. Jason Marah: Aye, I’ll do me best. Not promising anything though. Binsei Kinaka: Ah, you’re best is good enough for me, Jason. (Binsei pots the black ball and smiles.) I’ll meet you outside the arena, a couple of hours before hand. I’ve got a little something I have to give you.
Jason looks inquisitively as Binsei just shrugs and smiles.
