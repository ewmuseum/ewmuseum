This must be stopped

19 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This must be stopped - Most of the time a newcomer to a wrestling company has to sit back and watch others get the opportunities, that are few and far between, and prove their worth to the company. McCain however is being given the great chance of proving his worth...Right of the bat for Pure. McCain has been seeded ninth in the current Ultimate Junior Spinning Wheel O Sorrow tournament and will face off against CCW Legend Kyle Lamen. This is one of those chances that everyone hopes for, in fact most guys spend a entire career waiting on, to prove themselves against a legend. Coming off a solid performance in his debut match last week at Gekito, McCain now looks forward to facing off with Lamen in a No Disqualification match up. Can he possibly live up to the tough assignment and be able to survive against a legend in the business? Only time will tell whether or not McCain will rise to the occasion and walk away with what would have to be considered a huge upset by the youngster. However the big time match up is not the only thing that is going through McCains mind as the last couple of days have went by. Those thoughts have centered around a couple of major things or people for one. Over the weekend McCain has thought all about what brought him to Pure and why he so badly wanted to be here and prove himself. The chance to compete in some pure, no pun intended, wrestling competition in front of the great fans over here in Japan. But now there is someone who seems hell bent on trying to destroy this great companies tradition. A man who wants to take the competition out of the company and turn it into a three ring circus of what he calls, Entertainment. That one man is spitting in the face of all the great history and tradition of the Pure company, a history and tradition that McCain very much wants to be a part of. Some how some way, McCain is going to be one of the men who helps to ensure that the Pure VP doesnt get his way, and ruin that traditional Pure image.- What in the heck is he thinking! ::: The out shot opens to the inside of a small and almost vacant little cafe in Tokyo. Sitting at one of the small tables in the far back corner of the shop is Zander McCain and his very beautiful valet and girlfriend Cherri Barrett. McCain sits there looking out the window with a look of disgust on his face, while Cherri, dressed in some casual business attire that is a tan color, looks at him with confused eyes. The few other people in the small cafe all stop what they are doing and look toward McCain. He looks around the small building, with an old musty look and feel and simply smiles shyly. The people finally go back to what they are doing as McCain smoothes a wrinkle out of the leg of his Khaki pants. ::: Cherri: Are you crazy? There was no need to suddenly shout out like that in here. Everyone must think you are some drugged out worthless American. McCain (more somber look now): Agreed. Its just that the VP is going to do his best to ruin everything that the Pure company stands for. This is a very bad thing for business, since the fans are used to and rightfully want to see classic wrestling matches. One on one, with no interference. But NO! The VP has to go out and ruin everything by making stipulations on the matches and then having Ali something another interfere in the main event. He must be completely crazy. Cherri: What is the big deal Zander? You are here to compete and you are being given that opportunity to do so against Kyle this week. Not to mention the match is in the Jr. title tournament. What more could you ask for? ::: McCain nearly spills the cup of tea he had been sipping on at her words. She looks at him like he may just be on drugs as he quickly start to clean up the spilled tea from the table and off the front of his designer shirt. Finally he looks at her like he just might explode. ::: McCain: Of course I am very happy to be getting this chance. It is an honor not only to compete in a tournament for a title with such honorable history, and against a legendary opponent no less. But this about way more than just me and a title tournament. This is about the history, tradition, and the beliefs that the great company of Pure is founded on. This is about competition and who is the better athlete...Not about who can have the most help run in for him, or how many times they can get half naked women on the show. The VP wants to destroy our company and its tradition and he must be stopped. Cherri: What do you intend to do about his ambitions? He is one of your bosses you know and you cant just tell him he is crazy and to change his ways. McCain (cocky smirk): No... But I believe I can be one of the guys who will help to persuade his thinking back to the right track. I dont know yet what I am going to do, but I will think of something before the event. Mark my words.  ::: The camera shot begins to pull back away from the two sitting at the table talking. As the camera gets further and further away the scene begins to fade out. Finally it hits black on the voice of McCain.::: I have got it!
