Calender Series: February Written: Past January is a cold month. Since New Year’s we’ve lost twelve more guys. One was Roland and another was Pat. Roland quit in January, said he couldn’t keep up with the physical regime, which was actually rather obvious. Roland is a great guy, smart as anything, and all in all hilarious, but his stamina isn’t exactly noteworthy. Pat on the other hand was a bit of a surprise. He actually quit for the reason that his schoolwork was failing because of all this extra time. We got a long speech after that one about not blaming your commitments. Pat really did need to quit because of school though. We were graduating in May and having to deal with tons of stuff, and not to mention the fact that he was planning on proposing to his girlfriend at graduation, was not helping his senior year of college.
Now in February we’ve been doing more complicated moves. The thing Crypt has been teaching us most is not just how to wrestle, but even a bit of politics in wrestling. Telling us not just how to win, but how to be entertaining. ‘The better wrestler doesn’t always get the better matches,’ he says. ‘The audience wants to be entertained. You’re not wrestling for you, you’re wrestling for TV, no matter who says what.’ This is an impressive ideal. I mean, winning is great, but winning a boring match is never good. I remember in high school when two guys were in the same positions the whole time stalling or whatever and then just rolled into a pin, it was pretty boring. Guys that really went at it and fought were so fun. That’s the stuff you really remember.
If people remember you that’s how you know you were good. If people remember you when they never even saw you, you’re a God.
February was a trying month though. It was trying mentally and physically. There was a lot of work for me in the ring and in school. I just always felt these back problems from falling on it so many times. I’m a little guy and when I’m in the ring with someone I rarely get a lot of offense in. I can, springboard and jump from places like most every other wrestler my size, but y’know what? That’s not how I play games. I’m original dammit! I’m not going to just do plancha after plancha after plancha or flip after flip after flip, no. No, I’m going to be different. So I like to keep the matches on the ground. I like to wrap submissions on guys. That way I get enough time for my offense to be interesting, and I don’t have to kill myself or be a replica of every other cruiserweight.
But my back… It was hurting so much. One day I was in Psychology III class. I was sitting in those chairs, y’know, those chairs. I leaned back for a second and then, bang! It was like something was sharply ripped out. I literally yelled out.
“Mr. Levine.” Damn bastard always getting my name wrong. Boring professor if you ask me. “Is something wrong?”
I blushed. Most everyone in the gigantic classroom looked my way and gawked. Some laughed, but not many. Now, I’ve taken about four different Psychology courses and the teachers are usually very fantastic, but this one. This one was different. Mr. Probolous. What a name. He seemed to like to make fun of me a lot. “Uhh,” I stammered out. “Just my back, I’m sorry.”
“Well your back must be very important to interrupt a class of over 100 students trying to graduate from this fine institution,” the bastard yelped in his high shrill voice. That graybeard seemed to up and laugh at me. And that word, ‘Institution.’ Why not just say, ‘You’re my patients in this insane asylum!’ Now there’s some psychological thinking for you! I told you, I’ve taken four of these classes. “Well if you’re finished…”
“Of course I am.” “So I can move on?” he asked evilly. “Of course you may.” He frowned, but continued anyway. Maybe I should ease up in the bumps I take even if I am a ground wrestler. Yeah, that’ll make Crypt happy. Let me just dig my own grave for a guy named ‘Crypt’ to put me in, that’s smart.
I ended up getting a C- in that class. My mother wasn’t happy, but my father didn’t seem to care. ‘What’s he gonna use Psychology III for in… pro wrestling?” Those last two words said as if I had decided to become a porn star.
Yup, February, the coldest month of the year.
