Phreaks latest project

16 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Phreaks latest project Written: 10-16-02 *A camera is shown following the unusually familiar shadow of Phreak walking down a some-what busy street with the handle of a packed vinyl carrying case in his hand. He is shown taking a sharp right turn into a tinted, single story building. In this building it has a glass divider. In one room is a grand sythensizer and mixing board with two men pacing around nervously. In the room to the left there is a glass door and a group of four people waiting around dangling microphones. Phreak enters the main building as the six anxious people bum-rush him at the door. Phreak has a sincere look on his face as he starts to speak. *
Phreak: 'It's been a long day.' Man #1: 'Where the hell have you been? You're 20 minutes late. We're supposed to be recording now!'
Phreak: 'Like I said. ..I It's been a long day. The trainer kept me back for a little bit extra for my return at PURE.'
Musician #1: 'All that matters it that you are here. We can't record with out our keyboarder and lead vocalists.'
Phreak: 'Right, now ... where should we start?' Man #2: 'How about with a sound check, followed with 'Unforgiven', then we will start recording.'
*Phreak and the other 4 musicians enter the glass room, as the other two enter the mixing room. Phreak opens up his case and pulls out a full size keyboard along with his stand. It takes him only a matter of seconds to set up and make sure his equipment is set. He then notifies the two men in the mixing room. *
Phreak: 'Alright ... Sound test confirmed. 'Unforgiven' pre-recording... lets do this.' *Phreak and his music group start drifting to their own heavy metal sounds as Phreak provides vocals for the song. After the song ends, Phreak is shown giving the other band members applause as the cameras fade out. When the camera fades back, all seven men are standing around congratulating each other on a well made CD. *
Man #1: 'Great job guys. Checks will be sent to your homes, and we will keep in touch with Soundscan & Billboard to follow your CD.'
Phreak: 'Great. When does our tour start?' Musician # 2: 'Didn't you get the message? Our tour was canceled.' Phreak (with great amount of anger): 'You've gotta be crappin' me. There's no way they backed out.'
Musician #3: 'They claimed that we were a little too rowdy.' Phreak: 'Just because I accidentally knocked out a fan who charged the stage automatically makes us rowdy?'
*there is a long pause through out the building as Phreaks pager is heard going off. To a message that says 'low battery', but Phreak uses this as a way to go ahead and continue with his day. *
Phreak: 'Guy's that's my trainer ... he wants to see me again. Man ... so much rehab, so little time. I will catch up and celebrate with you later. My friends... Beware, take care, for Phreak, will be out at night.'
