S U I C I D E part 1-The End Of All Things To Come Written: October 10th, 2002 AD I know Inphino feels it... I know End sees it... I know Cianan can hear it... But how many... How many live it? S U I C I D E Every pair of eyes I look into... Hides an empty mind... Every facade I experience... Cloaks an empty shell... S U I C I D E Every time I want to kill... Every one I think should die... When ever I throw my arm threw a window... Every time I lie alone at night and cry... S U I C I D E Every circle of Hell I've had to drown in... Every punch I've taken... Every insult layed upon me... Every threat to my God damn face... S U I C I D E All alone... So very fucking alone... S U I C I D E Every blade I've ever held in my palm... Every serated edge to my skin... Every time I draw blood... Every vein... Slit... S U I C I D E Every time I close the lid... Every time I pop another pill... Every trigger I've wanted to push... Every barrel against my temple... S U I C I D E All the hate that builds up inside... All the anger ready to escape... Every breath I've never exhaled... Every throat I've wanted to pierce... Always turns out to be mine... S U I C I D E Every chill that has run up my spine... Every dark corner I've sat in... S U I C I D E The end of all things to come... Never merciful... Never forgiving... No returning... The end of all things to come... S U I C I D E
