Taking the Stage (Tokyo Pop Tour Part 1)

Taking the Stage (Tokyo Pop Tour Part 1) Written: 10/4/02 Event Announcer: Here we are at Toyama, Japan, where rap and mix expert DJ Nygma is live to perform. Let’s go straight to the stage.
This one is for my opponent’s at Ikusa. Keep it real, or prepare to DEAL! Word! *Beats flourish one after another, mixing real fast with tunes take over.* The night is blue and cool, with autumn in the air. Bone chilling fascinations fill the blood streams of an ordinary man on a mission. This is the type of night where only those who love life appreciate it. Music is game, and a man by the name of Nygma owns it.
Echo’s of laughter fill the air, a crowd smolders with a long blind stare, and the in the middle is one brave soul, so hot so cold, not afraid at all.
It takes a big man to face fears, fears kill, bone chill, I said bone chill. You know how it feels when you want to touch the sun, the freezing gets uncomfortable, and you feel like you the only one, with the chills.
No man has what it takes to beat elements. Sentiment, didn’t feel it when blood dripped from my head. I was a fighter, and I still am till the day I die, I cry, like a baby when I hit him in the eye. I am not sad, if you think I am you got it all wrong, I am mad, I was born this way that’s why I’m singing this song.
My freezing Is pleasing Cause I know that in the end I will succeed Stop leaving I still believing That in the end I will succeed In the end I will succeed is what I said, if your thinking bout defeating me don’t let it get to your head. Don’t try, to find out what my life is about, you’ll cry once ya find out how DJ Nygma aroused. I’m mysterious in so many ways; I only showed up on the scene a couple of days, last month. That’s when I mixed the tape that started a lifestyle, a mask on my face is what I call style. You never saw me, you don’t know who I am, DJ Nygma is the only think you can…PREDICT, predicting gets you on types of trouble, you underestimate people when you can’t see the color….of their EYES! So take this to the head when were fighting next week, because DJ Nygma’s moving as far as his eyes can seek.
*Turn it round once, beat it beat it, STOP!* The crowd applauds as the man of a thousand words and beats leaves the stage. A cool huff comes from the mouth of Nygma exiting with so man thought on his mind. Will his rhymes and rhythm get him by what could be the biggest challenge in his life. One title up for grabs, something Nygma has never had.
Reporter from the scene on the concert: DJ Nygma has left after a great performance. He left this one quote for the reporters, “Only time can tell before they stick me back in the cell.” No one really knows what this means, but this mystery of a man, sure can express himself in different ways.
