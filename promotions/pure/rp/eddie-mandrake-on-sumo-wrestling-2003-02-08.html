Eddie Mandrake on...Sumo Wrestling

8 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Eddie Mandrake on...Sumo Wrestling Written: February 8, 2003 [We begin in a packed Sumo arena somewhere in Tokyo. People are busy making wagers on the next match, and the tension in there is high. People are making wagers in the millions of Yen range. In the front row, we see Eddie Mandrake, who looks rather inconspicuous, despite his all dark violet colour scheme. He's sitting there with Takeshi Nomura, his cultural consultant, with his arms crossed in a perturbed pose.]
Eddie: Explain me this crap. Nomura: Well it's very simple. The competitors will be coming out shortly. Eddie: Well, they better man. Their Sashimi sucks here. Nomura: Be calm like a cherry blossom, here they come. Eddie: What the hell do flowers have to do with my....HOLY SHIT! [As Eddie said that, out come the sumo wrestlers. One is about as big as OSW's Kimbusa. The other...even bigger than that. Combined, the amount of Kobe Beef in the sumo ring will be around 1100-1200 pounds.]
Eddie: They've both got bigger titties than my mom! Nomura: Mandrake-San! Eddie: Well he does! My mom is a C-cup, he's at least a Double-D Nomura: They are very sensitive about their weight...besides, they will squash you like a big purple bug.
Eddie: True, even I'm not that nuts. [Both sumo as they enter the ring begin to spread salt around the ring.] Eddie: Hmm...is that salt any good to eat? Nomura: Not really. A crazy man one time nearly killed the Emperor of Japan and hurt one of great stars of sumo with a wooden chair trying to get some put on his pretzel.
Eddie: Uhm...yeah. [Both men finish with the salting and take their stances. The referee signals, and both men charge at each other. The bigger one takes the Kimbusa-sized one and tosses him out like he was a sack of concrete.]
Eddie: Hey, are there any tag team matches? Nomura: No, unfortunately. Eddie: Speaking of which, I have something planned for my opponents. Nomura: I bet you do. I'll also bet that it involves a lot of pain and anguish? Eddie: Of course, there'd be no other way. They won't know what hit them... [As Eddie begins the Doctor Evil laugh, we fade to nothingness.]
