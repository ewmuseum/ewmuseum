Introductions II (RP #1 for Ikusa III)

29 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introductions II (RP #1 for Ikusa III) Written: 01-29-03 The scene slowly opens upon the beautiful surrounds of a small park type area. The looks are exactly what you would come to see in any typical Japanese style park. We find several people walking around frantically, all in a hurry to get somewhere, until the camera begins to focus in on one certain person who seems to be meditating in between two large trees. The camera begins to slowly zoom in through all of the commotion of the park and reveals Jesse Ramey suited in nicely fit silk clothing with a silk bandana wrapped around his head. The thoughts running through his head must be many on account of the position he is being put in this week on Ikusa, until his meditation is ended when a shadow falls before his face and causes him to open his eyes.
Jesse: (Looking up) Don’t I know you from somewhere? The camera zooms out and reveals an elderly man, one that looks very similar to the one we saw Jesse speaking with a few days ago.
Man: Indeed you do, but you hardly took a look at my face in our last encounter that my voice is probably the only thing you remember.
Jesse: (Closing his eyes) Would you mind leaving now and letting me continue my meditation?
Man: You do not need meditation when the problems you have, you are not meditating on. Jesse: (Opening his eyes once again and looking up) Okay, do you have a point? I mean, you criticized me the last time we met and now you’re telling me that my meditating is completely worthless.
Man: (Nodding his head) You meditate on things that need not be in you mind, while the things that do need to be are never brought to your attention.
Jesse: (Coming to his feet) Where do you get off telling me that I’m meditating on the wrong things, who exactly do you think you are and why do you think you’re so much better than me?
Man: (Nodding) Give it some time young master and meet me here. The old man extends his hand and gives Jesse a piece of paper, which he takes willingly. The old turns and begins to walk away as Jesse reads the contents of the paper, which the camera cannot see, before looking up.
Jesse: What is this all ab… Jesse realizes the old man has completely disappeared once he looks back up. The scene slowly begins to fade out on that and the scene goes to black.
