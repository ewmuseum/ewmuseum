Enter the Black Dragon Master... Its been a long time...many of you have forgotten all about him. Most of you do not even know who he is. A new era has passed through the realm of battle...many names have since came and gone...their presences snuffed out just as briefly and swiftly as a lit candle being extinguished. But he...always remains...either in the background or in the spotlight...he still remains... His life has been nothing but training...nothing but battle...he lives for the moment. He was bred to be a fighter...literally...from birth. There have been many who have come and gone who claim similar origins, but as it is always said... There can be only...one. If you can remember him...you remember that he was proclaimed dead. ...For some of you, he still is dead in your eyes. Well, if they wish to sit in the darkness for the rest of their lives in denial...let them do so. For those of you who wish to know the truth...all you have to do is simply turn on the light... They stumbled through the darkness of the forest with heavy footfalls. Two of them...both of them blind as to their destination. The sun had set a couple hours ago, and the moon was halfway visible. The two of them continued onward...amidst the darkness... Do you know where youre going? a female voice asked perplexed...shaken. Of course! What do ya take me fo? A swindla? a male voice responded offended. He sounded husky, and the outline of his shadow amongst the trees declared it. The other shadow was lithe, smaller, thinner, and more curvaceous. She followed behind the man leading into the forest. Are you sure this is the way? she asked, unsure of the unknown destination herself. The outline of the man turned back towards her. What did I say? I said I knew where I was goin! he turned back grumbling, almost tripping over a fallen tree branch. Dumb b!tch...wont quit ya yappin. She followed on aimlessly...sighing to herself while thinking one thought. How did I get myself into this? Smoke floated through the air like thick gray smog of tobacco. Along with the smell of stale alcohol, there was good reason why this place was not marked on the map. This was certainly no five star pub...but she wasnt looking for a good table or a swell wine list. She wanted information...and she knew that only the locals were going to be able to give it to her. Standing before the seedy looking bar, she stared up at the blank black door. The frosted windows were brown, and showed few silhouettes moving back and forth behind it. Outside, there were a few motorcycles, but no cars. As she actually considered stepping inside to pursue her quest, the door flew open. But thats not the only thing that flew... the wide frame of some scruffy local came soaring out of the bar. He crashed into the dirt ground on the back of his head, the rest of his body hitting the ground afterwards. His eyes were shut, taking in the sudden pain to his head. Then he opened them back up once again, and caught the underside of the womans white cotton covered breasts. He-e-ey... he managed to gurgle out of his mouth, a perverse grin gaping his face. His grin looked like it had a few vacancies for incisors...that made her grimace slightly. Well, the grin faded quickly when the heel of the woman snapped the bridge of his nose. He screamed out, blood spurting down his face as she walked on towards the door. With one last look at the bar fly, she buttoned her blue jean jacket over her white tank top and sighed. If the rest of them are worse than him, I shouldve dressed like a nun. she wiped her sweaty palms on the thighs of her tight blue jeans and pushed the door open. All eyes turned on the newcomer as the door slammed behind her. She felt like a deer caught in the headlights as she stared into them...and they stared into her. Slowly, smiles began to appear on their faces...but not friendly ones. Those smiles had wolf written all over them...and they had just found themselves a fresh little lamb. They would have to soon find out otherwise about our sweet little lamb here...she was very much a b!tch draped in wool. She moved through the room towards the bar on the far left wall of the room. Tables were scattered all around the room messily, though drunkards and bar regulars arent too concerned with the interior design of their drinking hole. Some rock music played from the jukebox over in the right corner of the room; the song was unintelligible, for the speakers were old and worn. She pressed on through the herd of lechers, turning a nose upwards at their leers and their gawking eyes. Then one of them decided that hes had enough of a visual aid...he was going for the hands on approach. She felt a meaty palm grip the side of her rear, drawing her to stop in her tracks. She turned around, and in one swift motion, she pulled the man forward with his groping arm, smashed the beer bottle in his other hand against his face, and then kneed him there. The man reeled back bloodied into his chair, his groans slurred from the alcohol. The entire room froze at the sight...and she was enjoying the stares she received from them this time. Never seen a chica who can kick your @sses, have you, she thought to herself. Then the voice of the bartender drew her attention from basking in their drunken awe. Hey! You cant come in here kicking around my customers! she gave him a smile that totally came out of the moment. Well as soon as I get what I came here for, Ill be out of your way. the bartender eyed her suspiciously, wiping the counter with a grimy cloth. You a cop? thats always the first question they ask... Nope...Im not a cop. she had expected them to ask that. The last three bartenders asked that. You a health inspector? Whoa...new question. She looked around the dive and noticed that its accumulated its fair share of filth all over. She wouldnt be surprised if you could scrape the paint off the walls and use it as fertilizer. Please, the dirt in this place would scare the antibiotics out of a health inspector. The bartender still eyed her...he fiddled with the cuffs of his white long sleeved shirt and folded his arms. Well, what are you here for then? A drink? Companionship? What? The men in the bar loomed up from their seats once the barkeep mentioned companionship as an option. She whipped around and gave them a huge stare that would make the ice in their drinks look tepid. They all dropped into their seats before the woman looked back at the bartender, her light brown hair swinging amongst her shoulders. Information. Try 411...phones in the back. the room erupted into laughter, but she wasnt amused. She stepped forward to the counter and slammed her hand down hard. That broke through their laughter, and the barkeeps attention was hers. Im looking for a man... she spun around as the men in the room stood up again. Sit back down you monkeys!! then she looked back to the bartender. Im looking for a man dressed in all black...has dark blue and black hair...goes by the name of Inphino Blitz. Blitz? Inphino Blitz? he repeated the name in his head, scratching the bald spot in the middle of his skull.  Hes dead, sugar! Been dead ever since August of last year! Dont tell me you think hes still-- Alive? she cut him off. Yes...I have my reasons. Do any of you know where I can find him? The bar was silent for a moment. Then from her right a glass clacked against the counter. She looked over to find a burly man preparing to stand off his stool. I dont know much about findin him... he began in his husky voice. ...but I can show ya where hes possed to live. The woman nodded. Im willing to pay for your services. Please show me the way. Keep ya money...Ill show ya the way. But as we always learn in life...things without a price...still come with a price... Whats going on here? Theres nothing here! She stood in the middle of a wooded clearing staring around, anger sounding in her voice. She turned back towards the man with her hands held out, wondering what was going on. Wheres Blitzs home? Well deal with that later. he said, stepping closer to her. I think its bout time ya paid me now. Her body stiffened, the muscles in her arms tensing. I thought you said youd help me for free. Nothins fo free in this life, girlie. he approached slowly, backing her up slowly as well. She finally hit the end when her back pressed against the bark of a tree. A sigh came from her lips, knowing how things were going to have to play out. She tossed out a punch, but the man blocked it with his wide forearm. Then he countered with his own punch...right into her sternum. All the air came rushing from her midsection, doubling her over forward. Another punch to her spine dropped her down to her knees...right where he wanted her. Grabbing a handful of her hair, he pulled her back and used his free hand to fiddle with his belt. Her mind raced for a solution. She was determined to stop this at all costs. The timing was perfect...this was just how things were going to go. With all her force, she threw her skull forward and smashed it against his groin. A squeal of pain and surprise came out of him as he backed away and collapsed to the ground. She jumped back to her knees and dashed off, not caring where she ended up. All she wanted was to get away. Speed was her gift as she ran further and further into the woods. At first she thought she had heard the mans heavy footsteps give chase behind her, but soon she found no sound behind her at all. She stopped for a moment to catch her breath, and while breathing heavily, she realized one fact. Where am I? Her head scanned the surrounding area, but of course she found nothing but shadow and darkness. Swell idea, she thought, lost in the woods with a rapist. Things just could not get any better. Then she turned aroundand stared straight into the frame of someone. She jumped back, sliding into her fighting stance at the masculine figure. The man made no movement, just stood there and watched. The two of them stood there in a standoff for a few minutesthen she got impatient. Well? Arent you going to advance? she yelled in anticipation. if youre so eager to have me, then try and take me! There was no response. The man was frozen before her. Then with a noise that resembled a sigh, he turned his back and proceeded to walk away. She looked on surprised at her pursuer, a bit confused at the sudden change of heart and growth of morals. Whats going on? Before she could even answer her own thought, the blow to the back of her head knocked her into unconsciousness. As her body dropped to the ground, the man walking away turned back towards her. Another male figure stood in the darkness, holding what looked to be a large tree branch. What took ya so long? he asked, a trace of pain squeaking through his husky voice. She nearly got way! This ones toughbut that means shes gotta body on er! Loads o fun there! The other man nodded. Whaddya standin there fo? Help me wit er carcass so we can git her to the van and outta these woods. The other man, more obviously the accomplice, approached the woman, grabbing the feet while the man grabbed her by her arms. He turned around and took the point as they made way through the forest. All that was heard was the scrunching of feet against the dirt and twigs within the forest. A half an hour later, they both stopped next to a short tree stump. The larger man slumped against the trunk of a nearby tree while his helper kneeled next to the womans body, placed atop of the stump. A relaxed sigh came up from the leader. This has gotta be the best thing thats come out here in a long time! he said with pride. Its gonna be a long time fore we git tired o this chick! Whatdya think, eh Taki? The other man called Taki said nothing in response. His head turned towards his partner in crime in the darkness. Whatsa matter wit ya? Ya got somethin stuck in ya throat or somethin? Before he could ask another question, a wet splat sounded in the leaders area. He jumped to his feet, his hands reaching for his face. Whatis it rainin or somethin?? He held his hand out towards the area where he once sat, and another few drops of liquid hit his hand. He rubbed the liquid through his fingers before turning to Taki. Theres somethin up there, Taki. Git the flashlight and shine it up there! Taki stood and reached to his hip. Then he stretched out his arm towards the upper area of the tree. The light panned across the branches of the tree, finding nothing at all. Mustve been some possum pissin on me. The leader grumbled, turning away. Then something caught his eye in the light. He whirled back around and pointed towards it. RIGHT THERE! The light took a sudden turn, and the mini spotlight revealed the object that they were looking for. The larger man stared wide-eyed at the figure draped across a tree branch like a pair of pants on the back of a chair. He was dressed in all black, his black hair cascading over his face. A red substance ran down his hair, dripping from the strands onto the ground. The leaders hand pointed at the body, now shaking as his bloody finger led an invisible path between him and the body. Taki? Turning back towards his partner, he found the flashlight to come torpedoing towards him. A flash of light was all he saw as the pain shot through his face. After that, his lights were turned out When the lights came back on, it was morning. The birds were singing, and the squirrels were active in their usual rumpus. Yet there were two additional dwellers within the trees. Two bodies hung from the branches, dripping in their crimson vitals. If could listen just closely under the twilight of morningyou could hear their weak moans still pleading for mercy. But in their line of work, they were not ones to give mercy. So many women pleaded for mercy, and they did nothing but twist it into their own sadistic pleasures as they gave them painphysically, mentally, and emotionally. It was the same way within the professional realmwhere men were paid to dish out this hurt and suffering just to ease their egos and please themselves. That was one of the reasons why heleft that place Too many people were more concerned with the matter of satisfying themselves, making themselves into great showmen of fame Giving pain to please Pleasing to give pain His exposure to them would only lead to corruptionand therefore destroy all he worked for in his life. That is why he leftor at least thats one of the reasons... She had a long night on the dead shift of the hospital. All she wanted to do now is go home and get in a few hours of sleep before her first class at the school. As she walked through the doors of the hospital, she stopped when she found a womans body lying across a stretcher in the middle of the doorway. Looking back and forth, she sighed before rushing to her side and checking her vitals. She never knew that she was being watched. Atop of the building across the street, one man stood in silence. His eyes gazed into the scene, never wavering, never blinking behind those dark shades. It was a sunny day, but to this man, the skies were always as dark as his attire. His black trench coat fluttered in the wind a bit, his arms by his sides holding some control of his coat. In his hand fluttered a white piece of paper --- 01.24.02 Dear Inphino Blitz, If this letter gets to you, I would like to formally request of you to consider taking up occupation within my new Japanese Indy wrestling federation, Puroresu Ultimate Ring Entertainment. PURE is going to be twenty wrestlers tops, and from our work together in PCW s past, I know that you are a top name to sign to the roster. I am sorry about whatever happened that drove you out from PCW, but please take this as an invitation back into Primetime Central, which may very well blaze a trail for your return back into Primetime Championship Wrestling. Thank you, and I await your response. Sincerely, Alexander Miyagawa, Owner and CEO of PURE P.S. I have sent this letter through an old acquaintance of yours, Mina de Margo. I trust that shell do nothing but succeed in making sure this letter gets to you. She seems just as determined to seek you out as I am. --- The wind picked up, ruffling his black and blue hair across his face. He brushed the strands away with his black-nailed fingers and heaved a deep sigh. He knew of the wind that swirled about himit was the black winds of destiny that breezed only for him. It was the winds that whispered through his earstelling him what he must do now. His new questhis new training ground PURE Soonce againthe battle begins 'Inphino Blitz'
