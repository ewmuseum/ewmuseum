Beginning Again.

4 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Beginning Again. In Primetime Championship Wrestling, careers are started, and ended, in the drop of a hat. Many athletes have poured through that federation, and used it to jumpstart careers. Others leave when it is their time. But, more tragically, others leave before their time. Such is the case with the Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid. Last time he was seen on PCW television, he was receiving the Red Scare from Ivan Stanislav, resulting in him being forced out of PCW. Yet he has returned to PTC, to PURE to demonstrate his considerable talents to the world once more. Here we go again. He speaks, four words that about sum up his feelings. After all, he has wrestled all over the world. Between the IWO and PCW, he has wrestled over 3 continents, from New York to Scotland, from Texas to Singapore. Yet now, he finally realizes he is headed to his ultimate challenge. Puroresuman, Sam Potright always told me about Japanese wrestling, he said, remembrance heavy in his voice. Potright was involved in mostly garbage though..despite the fact that he despises me now, Im sure hed love the opportunity to do what I am doing right now. Silently, he turns to face the camera, his new mask covering his features. His vastly changed attire and appearance is most evident in his lack of afro, as it has been chopped to the point of being able to fit under the hood. He rises, taking a barbed wire wrapped yardstick from its place on the mantle of his home, and places it back down. None of thatquite sad really. For my career, Ive been known as The HITthe H standing for Hardcore. Ive competed in contests that would make the average man cringe. Ive suffered injuries beyond compare. But nowtheres none of that. Its back to what I used to be about Continuing his stroll, the Trapezoid settles down in a chair, above a picture of himself, before the mask. He is poised on the top turnbuckle, ready to leap. Next to this is a replica of the PCW Cruiserweight Title, one of many belts on Trapezoids wall of fame, so to speak. I am a cruiserweight. Before being hardcore, before diving off big giant platforms onto beds of nailsdropping people into thumbtacksusing laddersI was a cruiserweight. I beat people using my intuition and my insanely large moveset. Its time to go somewhere where that is appreciated again. To the Land of the Rising Sun. To Japan. HITs masked face seems to show no emotion. Oh, youre probably wondering about the mask, right? he said, a slight bitter tone in his voice. Its a result of all of those matchesTrick or Treats.Poisoned Pyramids, and the like. Its to cover up the scars. All of the wounds of war that I have sustained over the years. Pain seems evident in his voice. But in this life, you live with pain. That pain is past. So, now I am in the PURE Junior Heavyweight Title tournament. Against a man known as Cege Hayabusa. A man I admittedly know very little about. Ill find out more in due time. But for nowleave me. I have things to ponder.
