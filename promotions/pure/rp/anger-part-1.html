Hers how it all happens. Yesterday, I woke up on the floor with a splitting headache; I am feeling the effects from my work out. Yet I didn't moan, didnt complain I but up got ready had a glass of milk and went back to the gym. I arrive when the owner arrives I get in as soon as the door is open, I am there to break the first sweat I am there to force the first scream of pain. Its all my own choice its all because I want to keep the great shape I am in to keep people wishing they were half as strong as me. So I go through the pain I try to block it out my head I do it quiet well but the 10 hours none stop can really make you hurt, can make you bleed. Yet I love I, I do it because I enjoy it, the more pain the better the more you can take the more you can give out. But back to yesterday. After the gym work out I go home get my clothes on and go to the pub for a friendly drink and I friendly fight! So I arrived and I found a specimen to my physique. I waited, watched wondered if he could stand up to me, Finally I thought he had drunk enough to start I fight with me so I go over and knock his drink over the guy reacted like he thought and the fight was on, it went on and on finally the stronger man prevailed and that wasn't me it was him! You see he is the evil I was talking about because there was something special about him and it was his eyes, they were a red like a dark red, a fire red. Now I am here waiting for the nurse to see if I can go and try again, try to show I am the better man. .... If it was only so easy... The Hospital thinks he has something round with his left arm. My hart starts beating it feels like it is tearing out my body all I can think of is how I first hurt it the memories come flooding back. It wasnt worth it I thought why did I have to do try my skills at fighting why! Blinking, Thinking, Wondering. I lay back on my bed relaxing. He looks at me making sure I can see the fire in his eyes making sure he can scares me before he kills me, He wants to make sure he sees me upset he wants to see me afraid he wants me to say he is the better man. Well I am not going to do that. I Smile knowing god gave me a bone and I took it. There was nothing to do except wait for tomorrow and time passes alot when you are asleep so that is what I did.
