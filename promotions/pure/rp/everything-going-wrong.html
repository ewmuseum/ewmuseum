Everything Going Wrong

23 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Everything Going Wrong Why is this all happening? I can't figure it out. It's killing me, I can't stand it! My parents are dead, my confidence is dead, and I am close to dead. Nothing is good, nothing is happy. It is all black. A black cloud hovers over me. Why? Why me? To top it off, I lose my match against the Fight Club. I will not blame it on Enmity, Enmity means nothing to me. Whatever odds are stacked up against me, I must overcome them. I did not, and therefore I failed. I will not make excuses. I will not say it is because of the death of my mother and father. I just did not execute. The question that lingers in my mind is this: How do I turn myself around? A bad showing at Gekito, an okay showing at Ikusa. I do not want to be on the unemployment line, and I know that my father would not want a death to ruin me. Yet, I see no cure for this. Nothing can fix it, and to make matters worse, I must go up against The Mourning Star, Darkstar. I just don't know, I need help. I need something to get me going, get me fired up. I look in the mirror, and on one side I see a young man, with a wonderful future ahead of him... ...and yet, on the other side, I see a depressed and lonely man, looking for help.
