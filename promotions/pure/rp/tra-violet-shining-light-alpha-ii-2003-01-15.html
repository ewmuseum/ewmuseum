tra Violet Shining Light Alpha- II Written: Wednesday, the 15th of January. Narrated by Minori Kumako. When a man and a woman love each other, they face problems and confrontations in their relationship. Last night, my fiancé and I faced a problem, and a confrontation. Even though we’ve been together for about five years now, it was only one in about a dozen arguments! Akoshi and I are both very stubborn, it is hard for us to change our minds and opinions on things once we decide on something. When I convinced him to return to his love, Professional Wrestling, it was almost surreal.
I looked over at his sleeping body, running my index finger over the scar on his left arm. I felt happy knowing that we would be going home after all these years living in America, and going to home and seeing my man do the one thing he loves and does well. He yawned softly, briefly waking, but he fell back to sleep again. I kept staring at him, my large eyes peering at him in quiet slumber. I didn’t want to wake him, just look at him. My soft beating heart felt warmer every second, my head resting on his rising chest.
The slow breathing caused my eyes to open and close every couple of seconds, trying to not fall back into slumber. I wrapped my arms around his body, nestling my head on his chest. I felt a sudden jolt go through his body. Akoshi rolled over, his eyes fluttering open like a Butterfly. He turned his head to the right and looked at the small digital alarm clock, squinting at the time. The alarm clock read “6:56”.
‘Morning,’ he mumbled, looking at me. Akoshi rolled over and looked at me again. He gave me a hug as we rolled over each other. Akoshi managed to roll on top of me, smiling. A finger waved in front of his eager eyes, but the smile soon faded as I denied his quest for more pleasure. ‘Why can’t we have sex again?’
‘Because,’ I said, covering my upper body with the sheets of our bed. I slide out of the bed and put on a sweater and pants, feeling the early winter chill racing up my body. ‘Today, we’re leaving.’
‘What about Plane Tickets?’ he asked, putting on a pair of pants, a sleeveless shirt. ‘You don’t expect to just arrive at the airport and be handed tickets, do you?’
‘Of course not,’ I said back, smiling while dragging out a second suitcase to shovel more clothes into. I picked up a large ball of sweaters and dumped them into the open bag, never worrying about folding them neatly. There’s no time! ‘I phoned ahead. About two weeks ago.’
‘WHAT?!’ he bellowed back, frowning deeply. He quickly walked around the bed, snatching the pile of clothes from my hand and dumping them on the floor. ‘Why would you book a plane flight from here, to Japan, when I’m still not that enthusiastic about going?’
I growled. Not playfully, angrily. I went through this with him last night. I thought we resolved the issue, and were going home. Where I want us to go, but I guess I was expecting too much from him. ‘Listen, how long have I known you?’
‘About five years,’ Akoshi replied. ‘Yes,’ I said back coolly, kicking the pile of clothes that had landed on my feet. ‘I have known you for about five years. In that time, I almost know everything about you. When I asked you last night, I knew you’d say “yes”. But, I know you’re not going to enjoy it.’
Akoshi took my right hand in his grasp and guided me to sit down. He sat down next to me, blinking slowly a few times. ‘Minori’, he began to say to me. ‘I have complete faith in you, and the decision you’re making. I know I can still do it, it’s just that… they’ll be there.’ Akoshi sighed heavily, running a hand through his thick hair. ‘The moment Yukio finds out, he will send his men over. I know him well.’
‘Akoshi, listen!’ I screamed at him. Akoshi’s head looked up, slightly startled at my sudden and rare outburst. ‘You need to stop worrying yourself silly about your family. Remember why we’re going back home?’
‘For me to wrestle,’ he confidently said back, standing up next to me. ‘If I keep worrying about them, I am going to make a fool of you and myself.’
I smiled and kissed him softly on the cheek. ‘Keep thinking that way and we’ll be fine.’ I hugged him tightly for a few seconds, not really wanting to let go of him. I felt excited. After our bodies left each other’s warm embrace, I turned my back and left our bedroom, making my way into the bathroom to collect some of my bath soaps.
While I was in here, Akoshi continued to shovel clothes into the suitcases. He pulled out a long line of rolled up socks and dumped them into the only vacant suitcase, oblivious to what was sitting behind him.
The red, black and white mask. The mask of Radiant.
