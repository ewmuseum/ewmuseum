What they didn't show you: Senji Yulav

March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What they didn't show you: Senji Yulav Written: Joint RP Back in Locker Room E, Senji Yulav exits the bathroom, drying his hair with a white towel. He wears no shirt, having come out of the shower. He has already donned his black tights, and moves over to the his open locker, from the door of which that his trademark black dragon shirt hangs. Tossing the away into a laundry basket at the corner of the room, he sees he is not alone, though it is a shared locker room with another 1/3 of the PURE roster.
Yulav: You're one of those ninja that are going around harassing everyone, I presume? #16: Yes and no. The ninja has Yulav's shirt. He tosses it to the Universal Outlaw. Yulav: So what do you want? The ninja, leaning against some lockers next to Yulav's, stands up straight. He turns to Yulav and bows respectfully.
#16: Simple. I am here to give my good wishes in your assured defeat of that troglodite, Kohana.
A grin grows across Senji Yulav's face. Yulav: Is that so? #16: Of course. Look at him, as husky as he is, he certainly couldn't keep up with you in the ring. And you're Japanese. You come from a culture and tradition of puroresu. You are proud to be a wrestler, because it is the most glorious form of combat. Athletes, combatants, meeting in a ring in a form of battle that the Japanese have innovated and perfected. It is a showcase of skill and discipline. Such things Kohana has no idea of.
All-Asia Champion Senji Yulav nods his agreement. Yulav: This is true. It is my goal to bring pride back to Asia, and Japan, by holding onto the All-Asia championship. It is truly a shame that everyone from the Western Hemisphere feels the need to invade other federations content to remain separate. Especially Kohana, though he was sent here because PURE has is in the unfortunate situation of being contractually obligated to take at least some wrestlers from the PWA back in America.
#16: A clause written in back when Isawa Miyagawa was in charge of PURE. The ninja laughs. #16: Even so, what makes Kohana think that he can win against a representative of the superior Japanese race? What makes him think that he who represents a race of people who in estimation be extinct within 50 years? He wishes to wrestle to bring esteem to a group of reservation-restricted casino owners that the creators intended to die?
Yulav changes his voice to mock Kohana's. He lumbers around to make light of Kohana's girth.
Yulav: Woooo, look at me! You should all fear and respect me because I went to an American sports-entertainment wrestling school! I am a very serious competitor, after all, I can call myself the 'Sioux Nation Sensation' with a straight face!
#16: Exactly. He is no match for a Japanese man, he is no match for puroresu. The ninja steps forward and does a shorter bow. #16: You, Senji Yulav, are in the good graces of The Clan. May your All-Asia championship reign be long and prosperous.
Yulav: I am honored by your respect. Thank you. They give each other a nod of understanding, and #16 respectfully leaves Senji Yulav to finish his preparations for his match.
