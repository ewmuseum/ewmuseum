Enter The Backsmack Dragon Masta...MANG-A-HERO

25 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Enter The Backsmack Dragon Masta...MANG-A-HERO OOC:RUSHED!! EXPECT BETTER QUALITY THE NEXT FEW RP'S! THANKS! -/All is calm within the walls of the Dojo of the Black Dragon Master, Inphino Blitz. The night sky blankets the wide green fields of the surrounding territory he lives within, lakes as clear as glass in the distance as his dojo, made from the strongest of bamboo and looking clearly Japanese Fuedal style in it's appearance, stands tall and alone within the wide roaming expanses of the fields. The moon quietly smiles down upon all below as the world seems to be in a collective slumber, even the bamboo torches in the front of the dojo lightly flicker in the slight breeze of the wind...the crimson flame close to burning out eternally for it's final sleep. It seems as if NOTHING could disturb this quiet tapestry of serenity...but like all good and peaceful things....they all come crashing to an abrupt end shortly...which is the exact case as the sound of loud shrieks and screams echo through the inside of the dojo. Enter...the Blitz....who immediately has arisen from his bed upstairs, startled by the continued yelling emitted from downstairs as his senses begin to flare up cautiously, Blitz keeping a strange air of cool about him even though this situation would frighten or shock the average man or woman. Blitz tosses back the covers from his bed, his ripped washboard abs slightly exposed in the rays of the moonlight which seep in through the blinds of the window behind him. His eyes are already adjusted to the darkness, the Black Dragon Master walking cautiously out of his room and down to the upstairs hallway, his steps making NO sembelance of a sound as if he was mist, the screams continuing to grow louder and louder downstairs as Blitz begins to raise his arms up sternly, ready for whatever evil it is that could await him downstairs, unseen and unknown in the darkness-/ /-Blitz creeps down the carpeted steps slowly, taking in a deep breath before leaping down the steps with great agility, rolling on the ground with uncanny skill and popping up ready to fight as he strikes a martial arts stance...peeved in that usual cold Blitz way as he comes to find none other then Japan's SAMURAI O' SILLY STUFF and MANGA MASTER....MANG-A-HERO!! The screams are now identified to the anime addicted hero, who continues screaming and yelling like a spoiled child, in the middle of a hissy fit on the wooden floor in front of a TV with a PS2 hooked up to it, Super Street Fighter Alpha paused on the screen and a large 'KO' across the screen with the life bar on his side completely empty, an obvious sign of defeat. Blitz just stands in amazement of the childish antics of PURE's only hero as he kicks his feets about with his fist banging violently on the floor. His girly screams continue angrily until he turns to find Blitz standing cross-armed in front of him, his actions immediately ceasing as he pops up to his feet only to be met by the piercing eyes of the Black Dragon Master himself. Our hero dusts off his Ultra Man Zero replica costume, an exact replica down to the red and blue color scheme and the crest on his mask as he scratches the back of his head, smiling nervously before saying..::\/::.. Mang-A-Hero:'Why heeeeelllo Black Dragon Master Partner type Kimosabi!! I was just ummm....practicing the art of the 'Crying Baby' an ancient martial arts technique that throws off the opponents with it's excessive use of whining and hissy fits. Eh he....' Blitz:'I was once told by a wise man that if you have nothing smart to say...then it is best to keep your silence partner...' Mang-A-Hero:'Well eeeeeeexcuuuuuuuse me...when did you become MISTER MIYAGI! Anyways...ummmm...' *yawns fakely, outstretching his arms* 'Well I guess I better take my much needed re...' Blitz:'Not so fast my friend....I believe I told you no electronics of any kind were permitted in my dojo? So tell me HOW a TV, a PS2, AND a copy os Super Street Fighter Alpha got into my dojo...I await your answer.' -/Mang-A-Hero looks back, his eyes bugging out in the worst act of feigning shock as looks upon the makeshift entertainment set. He then turns to Blitz, his mouth still wide open as he then begins to speak in an amazed tone, still with his usual heroic tone of course-/ Mang-A-Hero:'MY GOODNESS BLITZ, WHO DARED TO SMUGGLE SUCH HIGH QUALITY EQUIPMENT INSIDE YOUR DOJO!!! Do not fear Inphino...I shall confiscate this equipment and 'inspect' it for any traces of the criminal element and give you a report in due time! But who smuggled this equipment is trivial compared to the battle we should be prepared to have with Ali and Carl Kimball, the two evildoers looking to stop the Dragons Duece! I have called upon the power of Escaflowne and I am prepared to stand beside you great Dragon Master and slay the evil that stands aside us across the ring on Ikusa! Think of it....what a pairing this shall be...THE BLACK DRAGON MASTER AND THE BACKSMACK DRAGON MASTER....I CAN SEE THE COMIC COMPANIES HOUNDING US NOW FOR THE ROYALTIES TO THE EPIC OUR PAIRING SHALL BE! Don't you fear Blitz, Ali shall pay for his sins against you last Ikusa and the Gods of Anime called upon me to help you slay that big ol' meanie IN the ring using my pleathora of plutonium packed powers!! And that Carl Kimbell....ooooooooh is he gonna get a reality check, for in his first meeting with me, he shall come to find that we are the force sweeping the nation faster then Pokemon and I'm when I'm not bashing Ali's head in, I'm gonna be making Carl Kimbell my PIKA-CHU!! OOOOO boy....bad me...I just sad a bad word. See how excited I am about this.' Blitz:'Suprisingly...you have a point Hero....we shall combine our forces to defeat a greater evil that stands in our way of conquest of the PURE tag team titles. I'd be a liar if I told you that revenge hasn't been on my mind after the cowardly attack perpetrated by Ali...but he shall be cleansed for his sins with each and every kick I deliver to his soon to be writhing body, and he shall only recieve forgiveness from the Black Dragon Master when he is beaten into the ground with mercy...for no man dares attack Inphino Blitz and expects to escape unscathed. As for Carl Kimbell, he is guilty by association, and he too shall be bathed in my crippling offense until both men have no more will to continue in the match, OR PURE for that matter. Victory shall be ours Mang-A-Hero, but only if we stand tall and cooperate with each other...' Mang-A-Hero:'My sentiments exactly Inphino...which is why tomorrow morning you should expect to awake to an early morning MANG-A-HERO MEGA POWER MT.FUJI TRAINING SESSION!! I am gonna make your body pulsate with NEGATON energy and and your gonna have more sprint in your step then a Samurai Pizza Cat! So attain your rest now Blitz, for there is no telling how long you shall be working when MANG-A-HERO, MASTER OF THE DRUNKEN KOOL-AID ARTS, gets ahold of you. Now if you'll excuse me, there's the matter of this smuggled electronic equipment...allow me to evacuate it from this room so I may further study it's origins! I BID THEE KONI-CHI-BYE Blitz and good night!' -/Mang-A-Hero bows, busting a slight two step dance to go along with it, Blitz just shaking his head in shame of his partner as he begins to pick up the TV and the PS2 and carry it away hurridly into one of the side rooms of the main dojo, seperated by a see-through screen with various caligraphy strewn beautifully upon it, traditional in most Japanse homes and dojos. Our hero flashes a wide smile to Blitz upon putting the valued items down, closing the blind slowly as Blitz stands sternly, not budging or falling for that somewhat TOO innocent smile from his immature tag team partner. Inphino takes only a slight few steps closer as he watches the shadow of Mang-A-Hero begin to hook up the equipment merrily, our superheroic samurai letting out a barely audible giggle as he can be heard crsytal clear by Inphino Blitz as he says-/ Mang-A-Hero:'My SUPERHUMAN SAMURAI SYBER-SQUAD SMARTNESS NEVER CEASES TO FAIL ME IN ACTION!! Never shall my partner come to find that these items belong to me! GWAHAHAHAHA, GWAHAHAHAHAHA I SAY!! The only way he could POSSIBLY figure out my well planned scheme is if this thin wall is see through and if he was still standing outsi...' *Sees the shadow of Blitz behind his screen, shaking his head and with arms folded* '...de ri...ght....nooow.' *Exclaims* 'I'VE BEEN DRAGONBALL BUSTED HAVEN'T I?!' -/Blitz nods behind the screen and just walks the other way, heading back upstairs as our hero folds his arms and pouts in another fit of childish rage within the miniscule room, saying with a choked up and teary voice-/ Mang-A-Hero:'GREAT INSTINCTS OF ARMITAGE, HOW DOES HE CATCH ME IN THESE SITUATIONS!! I think he reads these RP's ahead of time...no matter, for tomorrow when we arise, we BOTH shall awake to the most grueling training ever experienced by two men....fortheenemy we face must be slayed by means STRONGER then a KAMEHAMEHA BLAST!! But enough about tomorrow morning....back to MY GAME!!' *Unpauses the game only to get beaten shortly after unpausing* 'CURSES, CURSES YOU MACHINE OF 128 BIT GOODNESS!!! TO THE PLANET NAMEK WITH YOU!!!' -/The immature hissy fits of our hero continues downstairs, shouts of utter anger filling the normally peaceful dojo as Inphino lays awake upstairs in his meager, yet humble bamboo stick bed...eyes wide open and staring aimlessly in the ceiling as he quietly says to himself, getting this out before he finally drifts into his night's slumber-/ Inphino Blitz:'Something tells me life is going to get a whole lot louder with my anime influenced friend around....at least things won't be boring for awhile. And the Black Dragon Master has spoken...' -/With that, Blitz drifts asleep as the shouts fill his dojo repeatedly, almost seeming to have no effect on his slumber as the scen fades to black and the events of tomorrow morning are left to be pondered until finally revealed. SO GO GO TOKYO....MAKE SURE YOU TUNE IN FOR THE NEXT MANG-A-HERO SHOW!!-/
