Make like a tree and...

24 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Make like a tree and... Written: 24 January 2003 The scene opens up to a mediocre sized room. One large table in the middle of the room, a bar to the right and a pool table to the left, a door at the end of the room and one at the back. Along the walls are various “oriental” paintings and a few medieval-styled model weapons. There is only one party of people occupying the room – The Kinaka Party. Three men surround the pool table, a couple of men drink at the bar while Jason Marah and Binsei Kinaka socialise with each other at the main table. Jason has the sleeves of his dark green short rolled up and the top two buttons open. He takes a gulp of his freshly poured pint of Guinness.
Binsei Kinaka: So, I hear you have a big match this Sunday. Jason Marah: Aye, should be a cracker. For the All-Asia title…that’d be some sight, eh? An Irish culchie representin’ Asia.
Jason chuckles to himself as he takes another drink. Jason Marah: Can’t remember who I’m feckin’ facin’ though. Binsei Kinaka: Quite a few Gaijins taking part, I believe. Jason Marah: I’m fighting a pack of fairies? Binsei laughs. Binsei Kinaka: Not “gays”, Gaijins. Foreigners, non-Japanese. Jason Marah: Ah…well isn’t my face red. Just then the door at the front of the room bursts open. The men in the room all stand to attention, each man with their hand hovering over their handguns (except for Marah who is still sitting down in the chair sipping his pint.). Two men walk in, taking a position at either side of the door; two more men follow them in, these station themselves just beside and a step in front of the previous two. One man then walks between the four men and scans the men opposite them – sporting a rather odd look at the drunk Irish man twirling his index finger in the head of the pint. Binsei slaps Marah across the back of the head and urges him to stand up – so he does. The last man to walk through the door seems the spark a nerve with Binsei. All men are of oriental decent and all clad in some form of formal wear. The last man to walk in seems to be the centre of the group. He smiles at Binsei.
Man: MISTER Kinaka! Binsei Kinaka: Yugi Hano – what the hell do you want? Every man in the room, bar Binsei, Yuigi and Marah (who doesn’t quite know what the hell’s going on), draws their guns and aims.
Yugi Hano: I’m here to finish you off for fucking good! You’ve caused me trouble for long enough, Mr. Kinaka, so I’m going to kill you so you will not be an ISSUE!
Just as Binsei is about to reply, Marah forces his way in front of him. Head wobbly, eyes glazed and arms pointing here, there and everywhere.
Jason Marah: Listen here, lad. Me and me buddies here, we’re just having a quite drink on a Friday night. We’re not causing anybody any trouble. But you have to come in here and point these little yokes (pointing at the guns) at us for no flipping reason. WHAT THE HELL’S WRONG WITH YOU, HUH?! To quote a great film that I just can’t remember right now…why don’t you make like a tree – AND GET THE FUCK OUT OF HERE!
Yugi looks oddly at Jason…he then looks even more odd when his skull is penetrated and theirs bloody and segments of his brain splashed across his “men” behind him, who drop their guns and surrender. Marah turns around to Binsei, who is holding a smoking Desert Eagle .50 in his hand and smile plastered across his face.
Jason Marah: Nice fucking shot. Binsei Kinaka: You do know he was gone shoot you, yes? Jason Marah: Right…no I didn’t. Thanks for the heads up now…he’s dead now so it’ll be hard for him to kill me now.
Jason smiles and winks at Binsei as he sits back down to his pint, while the rest of the interrupters are getting roughed up a bit.
