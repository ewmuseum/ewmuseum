What now?

15 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What now? So, it's come to this. Tyler Nelson fired everyone after he lost the World Title match to Big Alan Steele on PCW Supremacy. And now what? The End is left out of work. He's reapplied to PCW, simply because it's all he knows of in wrestling within the United States. Perhaps he'll be taken back. His contract with Nelson didn't allow for much money, but The End doesn't need much. As long as travel and hotel is taken care of, he doesn't need anything. And if he isn't taken back, it's okay. He'd have more time for PURE. The End is PURE's first Jr. Heavyweight Champion; the first champion PURE has ever crowned. And how did he win it, but to fight off both CCW Grand Slam Champion Kensuke Suzahara and former mentor The HIT. Quite an impressive showing, and this week, he gets to make another good showing as The End moves straight into the semi-finals of the Asian Title Tournament. He is a bit upset that he can't defend his Jr. Heavyweight Title yet, but at least he gets to wrestle on another solid Ikusa card. And here is where we join The End, speaking to former EW/PCW associate and rival wrestler Bobby Stormbringer, eating at Aruba's seafood restaurant in Fort Lauderdale, FL, looking out at the beautiful white-sandy beach on the clear sunny day. Stormbringer: (Chewing his bacon cheeseburger) I can't believe we're all fired. End: (Finishing a drink of Sprite) Not that you were wrestling all that much that month. Stormbringer: Ha ha. I'm back to being unemployed. Good thing I'm rich. But what are you going to do? End: (Facing out to the sea, the breeze cooling his face) I still work for PURE. I'm the Jr. Heavyweight Champion. Although show dates are only once every two weeks, that brings in income. Stormbringer: I see. There is a break in conversation. The two just eat their food. Stormbringer: Yeah, now I remember, I saw your title match rerun on US syndicated TV. Good match. You beat Suzahara and HIT. You showed incredible stamina in that match. A cold feeling goes up and down The End's spine. He shudders, visibly and audially. Stormbringer: What's wrong with you? End: Nothing. But thanks to Viagra's advertising campaign, I just can't hear the word 'stamina' without thinking of Bob Dole's improved sex-life. Hearing that, Stormbringer moves his chair away from The End, just to rub it in. Stormbringer: You're weird, you know that? End: Yeah. As The End takes a bite of his fettuccini alfredo with scallops and shrimp, Stormbringer drinks some of his Coke, before asking a question. Stormbringer: So what are you doing this week? End: (Swallowing the food) I've reapplie to PCW, and I'll have to go back in for an interview soon. But on Sunday, I'll have a match in PURE to get to the finals of the Asian Title Tournament. Stormbringer: You're gonna leave some bit of the ring for the others, right? The two laugh. End: Well, I've gotta go. I'll see you. Stormbringer: No, you won't. More laughs. Stormbringer: You just get re-hired to PCW, so we can have our match. End: You could follow me to PURE... Stormbringer: I'm not afraid of anything, but Godzilla gives me the creeps.
