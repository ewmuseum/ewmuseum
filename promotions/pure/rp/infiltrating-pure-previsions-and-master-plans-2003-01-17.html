Infiltrating PURE: Previsions and Master Plans

17 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Infiltrating PURE: Previsions and Master Plans Written: 1/17/03 Going back to your roots, that’s something every man has to do sometime in his life. Now is my time to perform that glorious task. It won’t be an easy one, it hasn’t been so far. I doubt it will get much easier either.
FLASH! We find ourselves in a marketing environment somewhere in Japan. It is a very crowded region filled with Asian looking men and women getting hounded by nearby sales people. In the midst of the crowd an old broken down car coughs up steam and stops in the middle of a polluted street full of garbage beneath the vehicles. The honking begins from the other cars behind it. Like that’ll get it going again. The door of the broken down automobile opens and Shaun Styles walks out. He looks as if he is at least 2 years younger, around 21 years of age. Covering his body is a dirty white wife beater tank top, a pair of old ripped blue denim Billa Bong jeans, a ripped beanie hat covering his semi long blonde rugged hair, and worn out sun glasses so his ice blue eyes cannot be seen.
Shaun stands by his sorry excuse for a car as the crowd gets a little annoyed with him backing up traffic. With no other choice in mind he grabs the car by the door and interior pushing it up the street and into a nearby car repair shop. The vehicles begin to move again passing him by quickly screaming in a foreign tongue. At the sight of this an employee, a fairly large and muscular Asian man covered in oil wearing dirty overalls, walks out of the shop and confronts Shaun Styles about his problem. This gets nowhere counting the fact that Shaun does not understand a word he’s saying.
Next thing he knows the bulky Asian is raising his voice in a commanding tone pointing at his car. Styles simply stands there confused. After a while he cannot take it anymore and tries to reason with the person.
Shaun Styles: Listen. I’m really not in the mood for this. Do you have anyone American working in there that I could talk to? You know…American? A---mmmmeeeri---can?
Asian Mechanic: (quickly with an Asian accent) American? American? And it all goes down hill from there. The mechanic feels the need to get a little amateurish. Shaun was never able to find out why this happened or what the man was so angry for but it seems that Styles caught the man in a bad mood. The mechanic starts shoving Shaun apparently so he’ll leave. Groups of people begin to look on for they are expecting to see a little bit of action. They get what they came for.
Since the shoves do not seem to be budging Shaun Styles the man decides to throw a punch that is immediately ducked. Shaun comes back up with an uppercut sending the man stumbling backwards before he falls onto the floor. He is lying there not moving for a little while as Shaun Styles is pulled over by a figure not yet identified. The hand bursts out of the crowd and pulls Styles aside. They follow a slight path leading into an alley until they stop and Shaun finally gets a good look at the person.
It’s Jade Taylor. He seems younger as well and is wearing a white button down shirt, some white khaki, and his face is looking rather gentle, completely clear of aging marks of any kind.
Jade Taylor: I happen to be in a business where I could use someone like you. Would you be interested?
Shaun Styles: …Maybe. What’s the job? Jade Taylor: First tell me a little about yourself. What are you doing here anyway? Shaun Styles: My parents are both gone and I just finished high school. I had no clue what to do with my life so I decided to go around the globe trying to find a niche.
Jade Taylor: I hope you didn’t find anything yet cause I have a hell of an idea. Shaun Styles: And that would be? Jade Taylor: Tell me one thing…Have you ever boxed? FLASH! With that said the deal was made. Shaun Styles was destined to go onto great heights and make a hell of a boxer. All until the big fight against J.T. Thunder. Once again his life has began to head down the toilet.
“You mess this up and you’re a dead man”, those words from the mob boss Don Laproni echoed through Shaun’s head during the fight. And mess it up he did. 43 seconds into the match. 43 seconds, that’s all it took to end a man’s life. Now, what a position we find ourselves in, desperate for money and willing to do anything to get some of it.
--*Scene*--Picks up in a dark alleyway in Downtown Tokyo, which is the host for the PURE organization, one that Shaun has had his eye on for quite a while. Not much is seen but the red bricks of the two building with two figures walking in between them. They walk down, making their way to a small opening. A shed of light reflects upon them and they are seen to be Shaun Styles and Jade Taylor. Shaun Styles’ face is not seen as his dark red hair is running wild down his face. Jade Taylor has his bleached almost white hair running down short. He is wearing a multicolored tie-dye t-shirt and beige Banana Republic shorts. Now that you all have a mental picture of our little duo here we can get on with the promo. Shaun is holding a piece of paper in his hand that is revealed to be a map. It is quite obvious that they are lost. All of the sudden Shaun screams out, 'I know where we went wrong! We were supposed to take a left at the transvestite hooker not a right.', Styles just shakes his head and keeps on walking down the path not knowing where he will end up next. After some walking is done they arrive next to a cheap motel. They both gaze at wonder towards it. A sign reads 'Welcome to CheapAsMotel'. The men walk fearlessly into the building that seems to be abandoned. After checking in their new home for the next few months or so they enter a one-bedroom apartment. Shaun Styles’ collapses onto a nearby bed while his hair falls back uncovering his young yet rugged face. Before he has a minute of rest Jade Taylor reaches into his pocket and pulls out a piece of paper. Taylor plants himself down right beside Shaun who sits up and gazes at the paper.
Jade Taylor: It’s time to get down to business. We need a plan to get you into PURE. That’s our last chance to earn you some money.
Shaun Styles: So how do you supposed I get in? I tried last week and Jamar made it pretty clear he didn’t want me in.
Jade Taylor: That’s why I’ve devised this amazing plan. It’s pretty much foolproof…as far as I know.
Shaun Styles: Let’s hear it. Jade Taylor: *Pointing to various parts to the paper* As you can see you will be using the old Santa Claus routine to enter the arena. The chimney is located here…You will ride down it until you hit…right here. As you follow that path it will take you straight to the ring…Now if that’s not a plan, I don’t know what is.
Shaun Styles: That’s got to be the most stupid thing I’ve ever heard. With that said Shaun throws himself down once again staring at the peeling ceiling while maintaining constant thought.
