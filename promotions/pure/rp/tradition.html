Tradition

20 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tradition - Pride...Tradition...Honor...RESPECT! These are all words that come to my mind when I think about the PURE Company, its fans, and it long and rich history of classic WRESTLING matches. When I think of PURE and all the great athletes that have spilled their blood, sweat, and tears here I can only think about how I honestly want nothing more than to be remembered in that way as well. I love this business...Everything about it. From the hard work all the way to the glory of goals reached and dreams realized. PURE is just another step in the long road to the top of the business I love more than anything in the world...And have always loved. From the time I was a small child I always dreamed of being given the opportunity to come to Japan and wrestling in front of the greatest wrestling fans in the world. That is one of my dreams that I have realized. All the years of heartache, pain, suffering, and paying my dues not only for my wrestling career but also for the sins I have committed in my life. Now my time has come to shine in the spotlight in front of the country I dream of giving my all in front of all my life. There is just a simple problem with my time in the spotlight. A little wrench in the workings of the PURE Company. That one small minded being who is threatening to derail my hopes and dreams. The PURE VP...Jamar Wilson. Jamar wants to run around and ruin the great history of the PURE federation with these none sense promises of Sports Entertainment. His ideas and thoughts not only ruined a great six man tag team match, but also caused a history making main event to be cut short by another man with no respect for the rich traditions of PURE. Jamar has went out of his way to destroy everything that Williams has worked hard to keep in place within the PURE company. The Man verses Man aspect...Both testing their skills, courage, spirit, and might. Respect for the competition that is involved with every single match up. The honor of having more than simply a trophy, cup, or title belt on the line. No matter what Jamar and his band of misguided rebels would like to think, the tradition of Puroresu will be honored. No matter what disrespect they decide to try and show toward the histories great competitors there will always be men like The End and myself to stand in their way and continue on the Puroresu ways. Carrying on the banner for what the PURE Company is not only totally based on, but thrives off of. The things that the fans want the most. Shabazz...Blitz...Miya....Loc...History...Honor...The fans...Puroresu... These are what my Ikusa Hall debut is going to be all about. Not Leman. Not a title tournament. Not the glory of advancement. No Ikusa will be about bringing honor to the great style of Puroresu, the fans, and the men who have paved the way before me, so that I could have this opportunity. Finally Jamar I want you to realize something very clearly. PURE is about Puroresu, its fans, and the wills and desires of the men who love this business. PURE is not about your ignorant ideas, youre backstabbing, you attempts to degrade the talent. You will not succeed in your attempt to taint and defile PURE. You may have Ali and Enimity in your back pocket. You may have money, and the power of being VP in your back pocket. But I have some things much more important than all the things you seem to hold dear. Heart...Desire...The will to stop you...and most importantly the support of the fans. Come Ikusa Jamar, you will learn first hand the extent of how far I will go to help insure that you take your bags, your SPORTS ENTERTAINMENT, and your two hapless pals and go back to were you belong...If there is such a place.
