Till you just can not do it anymore

24 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Till you just can not do it anymore Written: 24 January 2003 The large wooden doors creak open, enabling a burst of light to fill the building and visualising all the dust specs floating through the air. Patrick Craig is the first of the two men to walk in. He brushes the tips of his two first fingers of his right hand off his lips and then places the fingers onto the frame of the large doors. Jason follows him in and looks around the building, almost in awe of his surroundings. Large drapes fall down from the ceilings, obviously covering windows as the sunlight peaks through around the edges. There are large benches, curved. Two benches at each side form an almost perfect circle; the only thing spoiling it is the pathway through the front and through the back of it. This fashion is repeated for another fifty rows. They’re surrounding a large circular platform. Craig walks on down the pathway and Marah follows.
On either side of the pathway are finely crafted statues of religious figures from the Grandism bible; most of the figures are also in the Catholic bible since this lesser-known religion is just another form of Christianity. Above the statues are paintings – a painting for each stage of Jesus Christ’s walk to crucifixion. Jason looks down and covering the ground is a thick carpet, coloured crimson. The pattern written on it from right to left, forwards and backwards is the universal sign of the Christian church – a cross – with a majestic “G” wrapped around it. BANG!
Jason looks up, his senses alert. Jason looks at Patrick standing on the platform. He slams his foot down again. BANG! The elderly man beckons Jason with a hand gesture.
Patrick Craig: A little more attention to why we’re here instead of attention to here itself, please.
Jason jogs up to Craig’s position and stands directly in front of him. Patrick Craig: This here is one of the few Grandism places of worship in the world. There’s four of them at this moment in time, this is the newest one. This is your first visit to one of these, but you’re not here to pray. I’ve organised for you to train here.
Jason’s eyes open wide and he looks awkwardly at Patrick. Jason Marah: In a church? Sorry for being so blunt, but why? Patrick Craig: Remember not so long ago when you were in PWA? Remember the training sessions we had in my competition room?
Jason Marah: Aye, that was just makeshift, wasn’t it? I mean, there was nowhere else to train.
Patrick Craig: There were plenty of other places, but simplicity equals improvement. If you train with the very bare essentials, without timers and trackers and just train not till you read that timer and say “Ah, that’s impressive, I’m happy with that” and finish your session but train till you physically and mentally can not do anymore, until you’ve given everything in your body, you’ll improve a hell of a lot more. This Sunday is huge for you and you should do everything in you’re power to repay those people who put you in this position. Not many people get the opportunities you get, my friend. You’ve got a third chance to impress. First time around you did nothing. Second time around you were average. This time you have to want to turn heads and excel above the best – you HAVE to want to!
Jason drops the open tracksuit top, which was covering his naked upper body to the ground. He slips off his runners to reveal his bare feet. He takes off his black tracksuit bottoms so he is stripped down to nothing bar a pair of thigh length white shorts and begins some warming up exercises. Then one of the church doors open…
Marah and Craig divert their attention to it. Binsei Kinaka walks through the doors, clad from head to do in top-notch dress wear with the sun’s beams surrounding him. He continues to walk up, giving room for two of his “friends” to walk in behind him. Binsei is in a jolly mood, huge smile plastered across his face. Binsei removes his sunglasses…
Binsei Kinaka: Jason! Jason smiles…Craig scowls. Not at the fact that Binsei didn’t greet him, but at the fact that he despises Binsei for no real reason – just a feeling really.
Jason Marah: Binsei, lad. How’s it going? Binsei Kinaka: Never better…not real church clothes you’re wearing, eh? Binsei lets out a low laugh, Marah shares the laugh, understanding his clothing is odd for his surroundings…Craig scowls.
Jason Marah: Just training…aye, don’t ask why I’m training in a church. Jason turns his head smiling to Craig…Craig scowls. Jason, a bit shocked at Craig’s reaction turns back to Binsei.
Jason Marah: So, what can I do ya for? Binsei Kinaka: Big dinner tonight, at the Skiminauka restaurant at eight o’clock. See you there?
Jason Marah: Sure thing man, I’ll look forward to it. Cheers. Binsei Kinaka: (smiling) Good, good. Bye for now. Jason waves and Binsei turns around. He walks out the door, followed by his friends. Jason Marah: Good guy, that Binsei. Patrick Craig: Ya think so, do ya? I don’t think he’s as straight a shooter as you think. He seems a bit…off, in my opinion.
Jason “pfft’s” and brushes off Craig’s nonsense, because that’s what it is in Jason’s mind…nonsense.
Patrick Craig: One thing Jason, how did he know we were here? How did he know we were on the way to Kawasaki last week? Why the hell is he been so damn bloody nice to an Irish bum like you, and an old prick like me?!
Jason’s eyebrows closen and his iris’ move to the lower corner of his eye sockets as he ponders about Craig’s points.
