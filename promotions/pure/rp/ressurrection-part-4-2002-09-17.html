Ressurrection(Part 4)

17 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ressurrection(Part 4) Written: 9/17/02 ** The scene opens up in a dark, clean, yet a bit dirty hallway when all of the sudden Vito Wynn and his lawyer Randy appear walk down the hall. **
'So, what room is it again?' asks Vito Wynn. 'It's court room six B. Well, at least it's what the lady told me.' Randy answers. .:: Vito Wynn and Randy look at each door to see what is written on it and suddenly stop in front of one specific door. ::.
'Well, this is it. This is the court room that we'll be in for this trial, Randy.' comments Vito Wynn.
'Yeah, this really is it. Well, lets get inside.' explains Randy. 'Okay, lets go.' says Vito Wynn. .:: Vito Wynn and Randy both step up to the door and go in one at a time. In the court room it is filled with people sitting down who will be watching, people sitting down who will be the jury, security guards, and the judge. It is silent in the court room as Randy and Vito Wynn sit next to each other on separate chairs with a little stool in front of them. ::.
'Damn, I am very nervous at the moment.' Vito Wynn whispers to Randy. 'Don't worry, you'll get used to it.' Randy whispers back to Vito Wynn. 'Okay, all rise!' shouts the judge. .:: Everyone in the court room rises off their seat. ::. 'Court is now in session. I am Judge Tracy Jennifer Brown This trial is to Randy Dale Smith, Jr. and his client Jonathan Bradley McRenolds. Mr. McRenolds is being charged for the murder of William Sean Johnson. You all may take your seats. Now, Mr. McRenolds, would you like to come up and speak to defend yourself first or will your lawyer Mr. Smith?' explains Judge Brown.
'My lawyer Randy Smith will be the one speaking to my defense first, Your Honor.' says Vito Wynn.
'Okay, Mr. Smith, stand up and try and defend your client in this situation as you may call upon people watching to call to the stand.' remarks Judge Brown.
'This incident happened on August 25, 2002 in Jamaica, Queens at around midnight. On that same night, my client claims to say that he was out partying at Club Sheer on that exact time. So, if it's possible to be at two places at once then I say that my client is guilty. But, it cannot be true that my client can be at two places at once.' says Randy.
'Well, I don't think that Mr. McRenolds would want to confess to murdering someone so I cannot use that comment as evidence to this case.' replies Judge Brown.
'Okay, then I call Hillary Rolt to the stand. Since she seemed to be at the same exact place during this tragedy.' Randy says.
.:: A young lady with blonde hair in curls, light blue button down shirt, black mini skirt, and black high heel shoes stands up and walks to the stand as she sits there. ::.
'Now, as far as I know you were at the same place this happened, am I right so far?' asks Randy.
'Yes, you are right.' replies Hillary Rolt. 'Okay, now what did you see, hear, ect.?' Randy asks. 'Well, I was walking around the block because I wasn't tired and I couldn't fall asleep when I abruptly hear a loud gun shot. At first I didn't think it was one but when I felt the vibration of the impact I knew it was a gun. So what I did was I turned around and I saw a young man in his early twenties, he was kind of cute you know. He was laying on the floor with a big hole in the middle of his chest with blood all over him. What I did was called 9-1-1 on my new Nokia cellphone that my daddy just bought me for my birthday.' says Hillary Rolt.
'Then, when the ambulance came they just took him away in the truck and drove away to the hospital without me even asking him out for dinner after he gets out of the hospital...' Hillary Rolt is cut short by Judge Brown.
'Excuse me, but can you just get to the point or the conclusion now?' asks Judge Brown. 'Sure. They took him away and that was the last I heard of him.' Hillary Rolt says. 'Thank you, you may step of the stand and take your seat.' remarks Randy. .:: Hillary Rolt gets off of the stand and walks to her seat. ::. 'Would you like to call anyone else to the stand Mr. Smith?' asks Judge Brown. 'Yes, I would like one more person to come up to the stand, his name is Mr. Jonathan Bradley McRenolds.
.:: Vito Wynn lifts his head up at Randy as he stands up off of his chair and sits on the stand as the scene fades out. ::.
