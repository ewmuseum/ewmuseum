Awake

3 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Awake (OOC: note, time is not accurate to real time) A month in a state of unconsciousness is nothing to the person who experiences it. It is torture to their family and loved ones though. However, Drachen has had two TRUE family members in his life. One is dead; the other was nearly killed, but saved by Drachen himself. The former of the two, is not important at this time. The latter, however, is standing right in front of us. We, the casual observer to this dramatic scene arrayed like toy blocks just dumped from a bucket. There is the plain white bed with white sheets which Drachen is asleep in. There is an EKG beside the bed on a night stand. A wire leads to an electrode taped onto Drachens finger. There is a brown chair next to the bed, and 'Konvenience Store' Kahlil Anil is sitting in it. He is Drachen's manager and best friend. Have you not seen Kahlil before? I am sure you have, he is manifested by an average convenience store clerk. Red polo shirt, green pants, thick glasses. He is a small dark-skinned Indian man of lithe build with a handsome face. He has unusually thin eyebrows, a profound nose, and less inset black eyes. He is clean shaven and is always smiling to show his perfect white teeth in contrast to his darkened features. Drachens eyes are closed, and he has a large white bandage on his forehead which extends all around his head. This is his only injury, yet it is a grievous one. Jonathans eyes flutter open and closed very quickly. There is not a lot of light entering the room, so he has no trouble adjusting. Other than the fact that this is the first time he has experienced the effects of sensory stimulus in the past month, there is nothing unusual or surprising. He feels no pain, and doesnt seem to have any problem thinking. But there is something wrong. He can see, but he can not see. The gift, that Drachen discovered recently. It got ganked from under the Christmas tree of his self. At first, Drachen is shocked. In fact, he is shocked for a long while, but makes no sound about it. Its gone, I cant predict the future anymore, I cant see what people will do. Thats Kahlil over there, I cant see what hes thinking. PRAISE THE LORD, ITS GONE! The curse has been lifted. Wait a second, where am I, and why is Kahlil here? Drachen muses excitedly, yet with circumspect. He looks at Kahlil. Kahlil flashes his pearly white smile, and says, Good morning, nice to see that youre joining us today. Wha-? How did I get here? Last thing I remember, was I I got hit. Dont remember much else. Drachen stammers this out while searching his memory banks. He is laying on his back on the bed, and then struggles to sit up, which comes naturally after some effort. Kahlil hobbles over, due to his limp from his now not-so-recent injury. A couple of gimps we are. Hahaha. He sits on the bed beside Drachen and smiles again. You were out for a month. Some guy attacked you on the street. One shot, to the forehead, caused some brain damage to your frontal lobe. Doesnt matter though, you dont use that particular part, unless you believe in all that psychic stuff. Kahlil winks at Drachen at this little tidbit. Kahlil was the first person Drachen told about his gift. Your former partner, how dreadful that sounds, stopped in a while ago to check on you. Cege is checking into some leads on who might have done it. He is pretty upset about all this. Why? Drachen asks with a look of confusion in his crystalline blue eyes. If you cant fulfill your role as the prophet, there can be no Ministry of Truth. Its like trying to make a PBJ when youre fresh out of Jiff. This whole team thing you two have been orchestrating, it is all over now due to your loss of foresight. I think it is jealousy, personally, he realized you are- Kahlil is cut short by an outburst from the wounded man. WHAT?!!? Kahlil, I know you are defensive of me, but dont be like that. Jeez man. Drachen says with a bit of anger, but it subsides. Sorry Jon, I have to be a bit defensive man. You saved me from being worm-food right now. And I deednt even have to give you deescount on slurpee. Kahlil starts laughing really loudly after he does a perfect Apu impersonation on the last bit. Drachen starts laughing, but it gives him a slight pain in the head and he stops. Will I be able to wrestle again? You could probably do it now. You are pretty lucky, only that very small part was affected. Its almost as if they were aiming for it, Jon. Eerie. Kahlil says this and then turns from where he is seated to look at the clock on the opposite side of the bed. Although, the doctor says not to attempt any strenuous exercise until youve cleared it with him. He was really surprised you were out for so long, considering how light the damage was. The CAT Scans showed that you were all right to wrestle, but the fact that you were out so long made the doctor worried. Drachen takes this all in, and asks with a grimace, Think itd be ok for me to try and walk? Kahlil nods and then adds, Sure, but be careful though, you havent walked in a month. You might be a little rusty at it. Hold on a second though, the doctor told me to call the second you woke up. Your parents said the same thing, my mom as well. Kahlil walks over to the bedside telephone and makes two or three phone calls, then reports their result back to Drachen. Mom says that shell be over later this evening, your parents werent home, and the doctor will be here in 15 minutes. You want to try and go downstairs? Or do you want me to get the temporary nurse I hired to help you out? You cheap ass, you know that means you will call your mom and tell here to get here faster. Drachen snickers after this comment. Kahlil looks around with feigned anger. Grr, you know me too well Drachen. Well, do you need some help? Nahh, I can handle it. Drachen pulls himself further up and pushes his legs sideways. Kahlil unhooks the EKG electrode from Drachens finger and walks over to turn the machine off. Drachen is a little stiff, but he doesnt have much trouble, and swings his legs over the side of the bed and his feet hit the floor. Kahlil loops an arm under Drachens shoulder and then Drachen attempts to stand. He fails the first time, but gets to his feet the second. With Kahlils assistance, Drachen makes it out of the room and down the stairs. Kahlil helps Drachen over to a brown overstuffed leather chair. Drachen sits in it and Kahlil makes his way over to a nearby couch. Well, Drachen. The doctorll be another few minutes. Lets talk about the match you have to take part in. Which one is that? Where I fight Charlie Loc for the in the first round of the Junior Heavyweight Title Tournament? Drachen asks, while rubbing the area around his bandage. Yeah, that one. Dont pretend not to remember. Kahlil says with a bit of sarcasm. What do I think of the match? I think itll be one of the truest contests of wills and strength that PURE will ever see. I dont imagine and cant imagine it being anything less than that. Loc is one of the best that there is, period. I am one of the best wrestlers that there is, period. Even with the elements of self indulgence and arrogance mixed in, I cannot imagine who will win this match. What I can say though, is that I probably have more desire to win this match, to Deliver Loc the death blow and come out victorious. I have the chance to grab hold of gold in the PTC once again, and greed for it will probably drive me to anything. More than that, I am an American from Americas capital and heart, Washington D.C. Arguably the center of terror for all those who oppose America, and that being not such a good thing. I will face Charlie Loc, and he is from a nation ravaged by my country. Unnecessarily ravaged by my country in many cases. He comes from a nation where destruction and death was rained from above for nearly 20 years. I cant imagine what terror it must have been to have been from that country, which was once so vibrant and full of life yet was destroyed because of its chosen political philosophy. But, I can imagine that it must have had a hand in hardening Loc into the iron man that he is in the ring today. It must have had a hand in the perfecting of his technique that makes him the great of the ring that he is today. It must have had a hand in the sharpening of the tongue and mind that causes him to be the personality that he is today. However, I went through a struggle equal in intensity in delivering pizza to areas similar to where he grew up. Areas so rank with destruction and violence, that it was nearly impossible to experience from the outside, let alone from the inside taking part in it. These places, like his homeland, were destroyed by the United States Government. No, not through the use of the death-precipitator known as the B-52, but through the invention of the projects and the lack of effort to make it a safe place. The allowance of the economic structure and safety of the area to rot. The imprisonment and hardening of light criminals. This is how the US government destroyed Washington DC, their headquarters, and then allowed its corpse to fester and become the stench ridden hell that the city is now. The dichotomy of this city made me into the man that I am today, and although it was hell just working there, I never regret a minute of it. It made me the man I am today, and gave me an appreciation of life that few can understand, let alone attain. Loc, is one of those few. That is why this match will be so great. Kahlil is surprised at the strength of Drachens words and is speechless for a second. Is that all? Yeah, it does not matter if I win or lose, it is how the struggle was struggled. Regardless of the winner, this match will go down in history as one of the finest fights ever fought. Hopefully, I will come out the victor, and continue my quest for the Junior Heavyweight Title, and bring back some glory to my city and my country which has become so darkened with the opaque evil which surrounds and permeates it. If I do not, I shall not let it keep me from attempting to do so again and again. One thing Pizza delivery teaches you, is that if you fail to do the job yet make it back to the office to deliver another day, that you are stronger than most because you had the resourcefulness to live through it, Drachen says with pride. Well I wasnt expecting such strong feelings from a guy who has been out for a month. But you are Jonathan Drachen, we can always expect more from less. Kahlil smiles and then reclines back on the couch. Thats what the girlfriends usually say. Drachen says with an audible and visible smirk. The two men laugh heartily as we walk away from the scene. Drachen, what a trooper ehh? Or sorry, this is pure, *n*eh? He seems all right, but will the injury show its effect more than it is now? One can only hope not. Was there another reason altogether why he was out for a month? FIN
