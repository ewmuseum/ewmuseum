Making you care

5 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Making you care Written: 5/02/03 What would I do to have a nice, cushiony little house with limousine drivers, maidservants, and a plush swimming pool in the back yard? I have always dreamed of going out and buying a new outfit whenever I can’t find something suitable in my closet. I would look great. I would feel even better. No fears. No worries about holding out until the next pay check rolls into my pocket. I wouldn’t have to clean or cook or dust. All the chores…I wouldn’t even think of doing those horrid deeds. I would not want to ruin my nails or dirty my hands. Even one day of wealth is unimaginable to me right now. I would be perfectly content with one day of power. Just one day of freedom. One day of luxury.
You know, some people really have it easy. They can do what they want to do, when they feel like doing it. They can drop everything. They don’t have to worry about their job or their family or their friends. They have no responsibilities to tie them down. They have no forces to hold them back. There is no restraint in their minds. No conscious to chastise them when they act irrationally. There are no morals, no rulers to slap their wrists. They are rich. They are lucky. They are free.
Actually, maybe I don’t need wealth. Sure, it would be terrific. Sometimes I think money would solve all of my problems. Money does not always buy happiness, though; or so they say. I wish my mind were easily bribed. I need freedom…freedom from my subconscious…freedom from myself. I would gladly take a day without my mind dictating my every move over a day without poverty. I would do whatever my heart desired. I would never even think twice. I would give anything to wake up without an ounce of guilt. One day to wake up without asking myself “why.” What a beautiful day. How peaceful it would be….
Part of me would truly love a day without ethics. I’m not saying I would kill anyone or steal anything. I am not that vengeful. I would never stoop that low. But I would love to be able to think and act freely on impulse. I want to follow through on that urge. It would be so wonderful…so passionate. There would be no strings, no wrongness in anything.
A world like that would be so colorful…so bright and cheery. No black and white or right and wrong. My heart can judge. My mind would not deserve a say. I can’t stand the interrogation. Self-examination….that is exactly what it feels like when my mind plagues me with questions. I question my every move, my every thought. I just don’t know how to be right any more. There have been so many mistakes that I can’t take back. I wouldn’t actually want to take them back, though.
Could I handle a day without morals? An entire day ridden of all ethics? Twenty four hours without question? Would I be safe?
So, 24 Kohana, who is the top dog now? We both got our asses kicked, but I won the battle. I like to live one day at a time. As far as I see it, the war is not anywhere near being over. But, I am quite pleased to have this battle under my belt. Shall we keep in mind who won? Shall we remember who has dominated this ring for so long? I am not troubled by your presence here. You do not intimidate me. I do not, nor will I ever fear you. You won’t win that hand; not in this federation. Keep an eye out for your own back, that’s the only way in this federation. Take my advice, kid.
You may have absolutely nothing against me, in your own, warped, twisted mind, but I have a problem with you. I will not tolerate this abuse. I will not sit back and smile after every brutal attack. I will not sit idly while you knock the hell out of me with tools. And most importantly, I will never respect you. I will not respect you as a man. I will not respect you as a fellow wrestler. I will not respect you as a human being. You are a monster, Kohana. You are a pathetic excuse of a man. You deserve nothing but hand cuffs and torture. .
I will prove myself to everyone as many times as I must. I am the All Asia Champion. Don’t ever forget that. Until then.. if you still dont care I will make you care!
