But...It...Wasn't...My...Fault... (Or at least that's what I keep telling myself)

6 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
But...It...Wasn't...My...Fault... (Or at least that's what I keep telling myself) Stupid black man. Stupid, stupid, stupid, stupid black man. Shabazz f***ed up royally on Ikusa IV. No, I'm not talking about the victory that he earned with his fellow roommates and good friends, Mang A Hero and Inphino Blitz, I'm talking about the dumb mistake Jordan made by leaving The Reverend Jesse Kintei in his locker room. Shabazz tries to portray himself as a smart black man, but God Damn it, give me a break, that was pure idiocy on his part, and I hope he knows it. Well then again, I'm just a lowly narrator... The scene opens up in a small and cramped hotel lobby in Tendo, Japan. Jordan was waiting for Midnight, the time when he could finally leave the country. He couldn't stand being in it any longer. He sat there in a red couch in the lobby of the hotel. All of the clothing that he brought with him to Japan in his Toronto Maple Leafs sports bag. Inphino Blitz, Mang-A-Hero, and Yuka Hamada were at the same hotel, but all had left. Jordan was alone, to think. Hmmm... I was thinking of playing it like a white boy and doing that self pity stuff... But I can't... There's no way possible I can blame myself for the situation that Jesse put himself into... No way... It was him who wanted to come and see me wrestle, it's not like I couldn't have gotten the tape bootlegged from some fan on a handheld, but he insisted on seeing it in person. Feces smeared all over his face, running around in urine, all of that disgusting BS. I know wrestling's like a game, but Ali's taking this a bit far... Jordan X Shabazz is suddenly stopped by the sound of rain. He begins to think about what's going on in America. I left the NAACP. I no longer work for them, I started N.I.G.G.A, The Negroes International Growing Group Association, but still... Jesse Kintei is still my responsibility, he's one of the guys that I brought over to N.I.G.G.A with me. I had him out and saved, I had him out with me. We were all set... Then I went for a drink... I won't even much lie, I expected my boys to pick up the slack and actually watch for the Ali or something... But what I didn't know is that they had things to do too. I'm not the only one in this world. Shabazz recently has made some changes in his lifestyle, he runs the fledging group, N.I.G.G.A in the United States, in his group, and he had hired several of his friends. Several of the people that were recommended to him by his brother, Drew Hostyle. They weren't exactly of the best quality of workers, Jordan was rather lax at first, allowing them to smoke marijuana at work, allowing them to make their drug transactions on the property, allowing them to wear whatever they want, allowing them to speak however they want... But Jordan changed all of that. He decided that Ebonics was no longer being allowed in his workplace, neither was drugs and or any type of clothing, Dress code was in effect, and if you were caught doing anything illegal, Jordan would call the cops. All of that was on his mind when he entered Japan for Ikusa, all of it. Plus he had just finished a wrestling match, so it was understandable that Jordan would make the mistake of leaving Jesse Kintei alone in the locker room... Ah no it wasn't. Jordan was making excuses for his mental error... They call me the 'White Black Guy Who Hates White People' because now I have denounced Ebonics. I have asked the Black Man to portray themselves as intelligent and trustworthy people... THAT'S KINDA FUCKING HARD WITH THAT IDIOT WALKING AROUND IN A KKK SUIT! That suit and hood... means so much... History of slavery, fighting the damn oppression of the African American youth... Police officers stopping us in cars, we get stopped and searched in stores... but if we show that we are smart, and we don't steal, and we don't smoke weed, and we don't sell drugs, and we don't rape women (Or men for that matter), then we're moving on up in the world... Jordan X Shabazz stands up, his taxi has arrived. It's 10:30, the security checks and whatnot should allow him to get back to America in time, where his friends Rush Mason and Dante Jackson will meet him. As Shabazz walks out the door, he prays to the God of the Crunk for one thing.... Please tell me Ali isn't raping Jesse right now... Fin
