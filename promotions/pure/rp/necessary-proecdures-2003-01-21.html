Necessary Proecdures Written: January 21, 2003 What do you see when you see yourself starring in the mirror, the reflection bouncing off like a ray of light, or kryptonite towards a standing and powerless Superman? Funny thought that I didn’t imagine that my very first match back from hiatus would be in the form of a loss. You never get the feeling that you actually lost until reality begins to deeply sink further and further in.
The eyes don’t really understand when you see a body on top of you; they actually lie to the point of thinking you’re on the opposites of everything. That you’re the one sitting on top, waiting for that stripped judge with a toupee and gruff voice, whether it be male or female to count your opponents shoulders down for the three count.
It was said that Alexander conquered the mighty nation of Egypt, overthrowing pharaohs and leaders of the United Kingdoms before him. The first thing he told his scribes to do for him was to be a city; Alexandria in his likeness. But in all mystery, the city distorts and breaks down as soon as the man dies while on the throne.
Titles in Asia are just about the same thing, everyone cares until you’re knocked off the top and then they don’t know how to accentuate towards the current. But then a new question pops straight into your mind – slowing down any previous thought you had about anything else.
“What if you’re the first one to win a title?” - Excerpt from the journal of Cordozer Stevenson Location: Flaming Longshot Date: January 20th, 2003 Time: 8:36 PM “How in the world…” Desmond began to trail off while sipping on a brew while inside the Flaming Longshot, a drinking establishment located in the bustling section of downtown Osaka, Japan.
Many of the patrons inside of the little bar happen to be dragged out of the many facets of life in Japan, your middle class, your gang members located in the very back with tattoos of serpents crossed around the eyes, and your run out of luck men and women with hard smiles and harder times to drive them to drink. “you had the match in the bag, and you turn your head for a quick second….”
Unless you’re Cordozer Stevenson at the moment, his eyes fixated upon a standing red brew, mixed with various crosses of drinks from soft lemonades to sodas to even hard liquor.
“I mean come on, Radiant isn’t one of those people that you can take your eyes off of and expect for him to do something.” Desmond solemnly pleads with Cordozer, who only smirks, and sips on the glass even more, the drink reaching about the halfway point of the glass.
Desmond shakes his head even further, “What the hell were you looking at, some girl in the audience?”
Stevenson stifles, “Actually, he beat me, straight up. No questions.” “Jesus…” Desmond mutters, slapping the mantel of the bar table, his utter anger almost shown in front of all these people, yet not are really paying truthful attention. Cordozer breaks out in a brief chuckle, only for Desmond to respond vehemently, “the heck’s so funny?”
“You,” Stevenson mutters. “caring over my match.” Desmond backs off from Stevenson at that very instant, knowing full well that he couldn’t get himself mixed up in the boys feelings. “Dante’s brought you out here to shine like a star, the very best.”
“That’s half the story of any man out there. But tell me why do I have to be like them, what makes them so damn important?”
Desmond could only mutter towards Stevenson, “Because you are important.” Stevenson smirks it off, before taking more sips of the drink, each and every single one taking him further and further into intoxication, and proverbial drunken states of mind if he continues to drink at this high rate. His eyes bug out quite a bit, before he sits the drink back down, the clear watery ring forming along the bar.
He gets up from the bar stool, taking a few steps towards the door before pulling it open. At seventy-two degrees outside during an Osaka nightfall, its pretty humid all year, not many people can enjoy this type of weather without trying to butcher it in the process with cosmetic products and such. “Where are you going?” Desmond turns his body ninety degrees to take a look at his prodigy.
“Out.” * - _ - * Location: New Heights Apartments Date: January 21st, 2003 Time: 5:31 AM The day of some usual press conferences for the upcoming Ikusa card were set on schedule today, and yet from all of the drinking last night, Cordozer Stevenson would not be able to get in a good wink of sleep in, thanks in large part to the drinks he had taken at the Longshot, nearly crippling his mental capacity.
Good thing he thought to walk back here last night. Standing upright next to the queen side bed squashed down into a set, cutting off a few risen inches from Cordozer’s body happens to be a trash can, filled with a small content of junk mail, food left out and vomit from a hangover.
His eyes were blood red, nearly causing him to be partially blind when he flicked on the lights inside of the apartment when he ventured his way into the bathroom this morning. Rubbing his face, the morning sounds of “Hasu and Tai’s Morning Show” stir him from his sleep.
“I care about your career,” he slurs out, speaking into the cotton white pillow with his head resting on top. The show conveys itself into its third segment, a talk of the latest sporting events in not only Japan, but America as well. As Super Bowl predictions alarm the listeners on the show, Cordozer slams his right hand along the right hand side of the radio. “Snooze off…”
Pulling back the cover sheets, his smirks to himself, before cracking his neck. Down over near the lamp, a small piece of stick tab lays hand written by Cordozer himself, bolded out in capitals as he moves his way towards the bathroom.
DR. MARK HYMAN 912 – 614 – 5671 Soon the message is ripped off the dresser as Cordozer reads it out to himself as a reminder, before balling the paper up and placing it in his pocket. A light jogging suit with a sleeveless And 1 T-shirt showing off most of his upper arm strength happens to sit on his body, before he opens the door and shuts it behind him.
Looking downward, he checks out to see two women, one skinny with short auburn hair and a nice frame, perky, but nothing Stevenson would place his hands on, while the other happened to be a well-rounded woman, tan skin with curves like a Coke bottle. He made no passes, no moves on these women except running down the stairs.
“Hi,” the skinny one mutters, but Cordozer doesn’t pay any mind towards it before heading towards his destination. The tan woman smiles, “Cute one, isn’t he?”
“If you think so…” The doors jingle on Hyman Offices, a small branch store located in the suburbs of Junta, a few blocks away from the New Heights as Cordozer walks in calmly, not touching over anything as Hyman turns his head towards the American/Japanese hybrid wrestler.
“Oh, Mr. Stevenson. I believed we talked over the phone?” Hyman was a short, stocky man with slick greasy black hair and a lab coat that proved that no matter what you told him, he was certified. “Yeah, um this is like my first time doing this so…”
“Don’t worry, its all legal here in Japan. How milligrams you looking for the juice?” “Juice?” Stevenson asks in a stupid faced question. Hyman laughs to himself, “Steroids man, you never heard them called juice?”
Cordozer shakes his head, “Never.”
