Emotions = Defeat And We Can't Have That

9 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Emotions = Defeat And We Can't Have That Last night...last night I decided to visit the Tokyo nightlife just to see what all the hype was about. Many things made me really think...and changed me for the better. Well, for the better...in my opinion. And Im sure Jamar Wilson shall agree with that statement... I started my venture by visiting the Night Pad and I can honestly say there was really nothing to it. All I saw there was a huge neon sign that almost blinded me...some Japanese sluts...and more people drinking and getting high than I could count. I always thought Japan was this conservative, well-natured country but now I see...thats only a facade. The music was horrible, the dancing sucked, and I couldnt believe the country I have longed to visit since I was a child...was this so into modern culture. Everyone dressed alike, danced alike, and acted alike. I thought I was in a room full of drones that CARED too much about how others perceived them. After visiting a place that puts a bad rep. on nightlife...I decided to get something to eat. It was ten oclock at night and I had eaten since dinner. Right next door to the dance club, was a sushi and pizza place...how does that work? I decided to get some pizza but it really didnt matter where I went...because everything had sauce and/or fish. Here...I started soaking in reasons why emotion wasnt always a good thing... The old man behind the counter could barely hear, walk, and talk. And when he did walk, to get the pizzas, it took him more time just to get it than it did to actually make it. The young couple that was waiting there didnt say anything because I assume they didnt want to hurt the old mans feelings. Give me a break...there were three much younger men behind the counter and the couple didnt ask if one of them could actually make and deliver the pizza. Now if I were standing there...I wouldve ordered old man to sit his ass down and have one of the youngins get my pizza. Needless to say when they got their pizza it was cold. Idiots... Another occurrence that made me wonder why people cared too much actually happened in the street. Another young couple, go figure, were walking by this homeless woman on the streets and she stopped the two of them. The couple told her they didnt have any money but said they would be right back after they went to the bank. What...the...hell? I most likely wouldve just flipped her off and kept going on with my night. SO...about ten minutes later, the couple came back and handed the woman fifty yen. I ran up to them and questioned them. Hey! I yelled catching their attention. Luckily, they spoke English. Yes? The young woman answered. She was a very attractive woman with a beautiful dress on and shiny black hair. Why did you just hand her that money? I asked, wondering why they would give her their earned money. Excuse me? The man cut in. He was actually a pretty tall Japanese man but he was rather unattractive. You just gave her fifty yen...may I ask why? I turned my attention to the woman because she was the one who handed it to the homeless person. Why not? I mean, she NEEDS the money more than I do. It was only being courteous...caring. She said as the two of them walked away. All I could do was laugh my ass of at them...but thats just me. That was the final straw. Right then and there I decided to allow every aspect of GOOD emotions to flow out of me. All the caring...compassion...guilt...and any other type of feeling that made me weak was drained. A few more actions...and then my night would be completed. Remember the club? Well I went back there, made my way to the DJ casually, then proceeded to pull the plug out of the wall and out of the music equipment he had set up. Next, I went to the pizzeria and told the old man how much he was only hurting business and that he should either retire or die off. And finally...I walked past the homeless woman and spat on her. Ahhh...it felt great to be back in control...
