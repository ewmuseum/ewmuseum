Standing Alone Part 1

20 January 2003

Standing Alone Part 1 Written: 20/01/03 [Abandoned by friends in his time of need, Senji was completely alone even to himself. The anger he felt toward those who wronged him was incomprehensible, so much that at times even he could not understand the desires of the rage and pain he felt. He walked on his own, through the dark alleys of the world hoping that any moment, one of his friends would find him, and accept him, and reassure him that life is not without mercy or forgiveness. This was not so. The Universal Outlaw was forced out in the cold, left there to rot in self-loathing and spite while those who pushed him away had not a care in the world. The drones, the army of numbed uniqueness, the cold, mindless hearts who followed but one mind, drank every word of their proud leader, seeking to find satisfaction and happiness in his eyes. They were no longer his friends, nor were they his enemies. In an instant, one conversation, they had become nothing to him as he had to them. That instant when their backs had turned toward him was the turning point.]
[He had known for very long that none of them had ever looked to him as one of them, but only as another soulless fool who could step in tune with their master. He was not like them, yet he had been following the same person blindly. His eyes were opened to the truth. He saw those people for what they were, and he hated what he had not seen in the past. How could he have been so ignorant?]
[Senji continued his march toward the condemned warehouse, focusing solely on one thought: he had been lost to the world, and even to himself. What he never had, could not find, was only an illusion; he could never be whole. He stepped heavily through the streets, splashing water from puddles over the already wet ground, thinking about the futility of it all. A small Japanese bar was the only store open that late at night in the star-lit city, dimly brightening the surroundings with a fluorescent Yin-Yang symbol. Senji only saw the symbol as yet another source of hopelessness; the constant struggle to balance the good with the evil in each person. He found that no matter how hard he tried, the good always fell short of what the evil could accomplish. The good was only a small route one could take, and was nearly impossible to stay on its straight and narrow path. And so came about his disguise; his new identity.]
[Unlike the world-wide symbol of balance, Senji thought that evil always outweighed the good; life was not black and white... it was all black. Life was darkness, pain, and suffering. The only people who were considered 'good' were those who found that white path through the confusion, chaos, and agony which Yulav uses to define life. Through darkness is a ray of light.]
[The Universal Outlaw, alone in a country full of people he hates... or like the only source of hope in his life... thin... alone... abandoned by his friends and left to drown in a sea of hatred.]
