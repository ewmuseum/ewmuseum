Training Day Ebola-n: a virus that causes viral hemorrhagic fever So, the apprentice will team with one who names himself after a virus. A virus is an infection of the body that slowly siphons the health out of the body, slowly but surely. A virus lies in wait anywhere, ready to strike its first victim from anywhere. This is the kind of man that our young hero must team with on Ikusa. Things look better You have grown in skills, young one, but yet you have much to learn. The panting voice of an older man can be heard as the scene comes up. We can see two men of Asian ethnicity fighting back and forth with practice katanas, both panting and sweat dripping from their brows with each movement. The older man wears a shiny purple kimono, open in the front to show a surprisingly chiseled stomach, with matching pants worn underneath and slippers to crouch around silently. Sensei, you grow older every day, but your swordsmanship never fails to amaze me. The younger man has no shirt on, but wears black pants, apparently to a matching kimono, but he wears no kimono, though he does wear the same slippers. As he speaks, his sensei shirks his katana thrust and counters back with a thrust of the thin dagger-like sword. The younger man barely avoids the practice sword into his shoulder, its point blunt but still dangerous. The sensei is forced forward, going down onto one knee, his sword clattering away. The younger man bounds off into the nearby brush, lying in wait for another practice ambush. His sensei is up in a second and calls out in the general area of the brush. Why didnt you take my sword, Little Soldier? You had me beaten for the first time ever. He waits and crouches down, knees bent as he tries to figure out where his young opponent is. A call comes from somewhere in the bushes, a blurred sort of sound which doesnt help pinpoint his location. Master Ko, you should know that my bushido prevents me from taking another fighters sword. It would be to take his very soul. The older man chuckles to himself as he crouches down even further and calls back. Ha, your honor will impale you from behind someday, wakai ahou. Not able to restrain at cracking a joke at being called a young fool, the younger man yells out from the bushes, Senpou junjiru ken sumau. He who follows wins: a samurai proverb. However, with this saying, Sensei Ko realizes his location and leaps towards him, taking a giant swipe with the katana and slicing the tall grass away, almost shaving the black hair from the top of his apprentices head. Rolling away while keeping his sword above him to fend off his senseis blows, the younger man rolls up and does a standing back flip into standard sword position. Both men are still sweating, but now there is silence A drop of sweat hits the ground CLASH! Their swords meet again and the two begin another beautiful dance of swordsmanship, the steel kissing over and over again. Finally the younger man gets a clear view after countering an attack, but the old man is agile and slides underneath the young mans legs to avoid his katanas long sweeping strike. Swiftly, the older sensei is up again, and invisible in the bushes. Haha, your counter blow seems a bit slow, sensei. You must be in top shape if you are to fight off my enemies. Laughing can be heard from the bushes on his other side. Whats so funny? the younger man cries out towards the laughing and receives a quick answer, You should fight off your own enemies before you worry about me being in shape, young one. With that, trying to emulate his sensei, the younger man lunges towards the bushes and towards a sound, only to watch as a white dove flies off into the sky and a chuckling comes from behind him. He goes to lunge out once again, but this time holds his ground. So, who will be your first test, young one? The young man almost replies as he hears the voice so close, but he turns to be held by the neck at knifepoint. Breathing slowly but calmly, his student replies in a sort of strangled voice, I will team with the man they call Ebola against two sheep who call themselves warriors. With that, the young man throws an elbow into his senseis sword, clattering it against the floor. Following his bushido once again, the younger man sneaks behind a large oak tree, waiting for his sensei, but the older man is too smart to fall for things like that. While his student cant see, he sneaks around the other side of the tree and whispers, Sheep are more safe in a pack in the open, apprentice. With that, he swings at his students chest, but the shot is deflected by the younger mans practice sword. Flipping backwards again, the younger man charges at him, swiping his sword to his side, but his swipe is easily dodged and the younger man flips up onto a wall, panting and heaving as his sensei does the same. Come down, young one, you have done well for today. The old man puts his hands on his knees as he waits for the younger man to come down. The young man looks at him for a few seconds, and then hops down to stand next to him. Now, what do you plan to do exactly for this wrestling business? The young man scratches at his black clean shaven hair as he answers, Well I plan to take out those pathetic gangsters and continue to cement my spot as the greatest warrior of all time. Sensei Ko chuckles as he replies, In time perhaps, Little Soldier The scene fades to all but black except for a Japanese character for samurai left on the black screen, but soon that fades away as well, leaving us with nothing but black
