A Great Week...But A Forthcoming Challenge

19 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A Great Week...But A Forthcoming Challenge Here I sit...alone and undaunted. Frustrated at times...angry at others. Abandoned at times...Confused. But the one thing I always am is a loser. Not a loser according to 'winning and losing'...but I have lost many a things. First I lost the only girl that I have ever loved. Then when I came home, well the place I thought was home, I lost my family. Now I lost the one thing that keeps me going everyday...emotion. Last Wednesday I became the first ever Gekito Cup winner but it did absolutely nothing for me. Sure I seemed thrilled on television when I held the cup in 'glory', with my hand held high nect to Inphino Blitz. But that was merely a facade I put on so everyone would think I was happy. But in truth, I would have felt all the same if I was the first to be eliminated. Then Ikusa came around and I recieved the first offer ever worthwhile in my life. I was sitting, alone, in my locker room when Jamar Williams approached me. I was surprised to say the least...but at the same time...very interested. He told me that if I went out there and made a big enough impact...there would be great things in my future. So I did just that. First, I walked out on my partners and showed them I didn't BELONG in the ring with a group like them. Then, I made an immediate impact when I snapped Chris Kanox's leg in two. When I heard that bone snap...when I felt the ligaments tear against my flesh I went cold. It felt like my heart had just been ripped out of my body because I never thought in a million years I would be in the dastardly position that I was in. Never did I think about bringing that much pain upon another person's body. But I will be honest, it did something for me. My heart grew cold and black and suffering and pain ran through my veins. I realized that all I have to do from now on is envision my opponent as someone that has wronged me in the past. By doing that...my actions shall be justified and I will feel no shame or pity. I also know I will need to use this tactic against my opponent this coming Sunday. If I wish to defeat Deacon I know I will need to pull out all the stops. I know I will need to envision him as the person that hurt me the worst in my life... I will need to envision him as... My Father...
