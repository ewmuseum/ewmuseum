Whiplash

27 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Whiplash 'Where the hell had it gone wrong?' wondered Kevin Sandusky as he lay on the incline bench in his cramped Yokohama apartment. He'd done his job, almost flawlessly at that. Three matches so far, and not a single time had he been counted out. Sure, he hadn't won the first one since End had to be a jackass and sit out half the match like a pussy, but he was standing across the ring as End got the three count on Larkin. Standing, not dazed, not unconscious, but standing. The last two matches in the tag tourney had been textbook. Defeating larger and higher ranked opponents with relative ease, things seemed to be going well. Seemed... Then it happened, and Kevin was surprised he didn't have whiplash from the experience. It was dreaded, oft talked about, but he'd never believed it existed until he'd seen the card for Ikusa IV. The moment he read it, he knew. He'd slammed square into mythical 'glass ceiling', and worse yet there was nothing he could do about it. Miya'd been dropping pink slips everywhere. Deservedly so in most cases, as a significant portion of the roster seemed to be riding the gravy train as far as they could before having to put forth any effort. Kevin feared that if he came forward with his concerns, he'd be seen as another of the malcontents and handed his walking papers. Hell, even Tim Walden'd been sacked over in PCW, proving that no one really had job security these days. But still, it ate at him. He'd been usurped, replaced as Miya's tag partner by JJ. Fricken' JJ for Christ's sake! A bumbling wrestler turned bumbling reporter. Sure, cult icon as the lovable loser, but it was fricken JJ. A tone rang out from the tiny device strapped to the middle of the barbell. The tiny computer had determined that the appropriate rest time has elapsed and that it was time for Kevin to begin his third set of reps. And Kevin did so in an almost Pavlovian way, focusing heavily on his breathing. Keeping a slow, yet steady contraction on the muscle throughout the full range of motion, including the decent of the weight back to his chest. Breathe out, contract, push, hold. Breathe in, contract, lower, contract again, hold.. Like clockwork the pattern repeated 15 times. And as the bar returned to it's cradle, the tiny computer calculated the optimum amount of time required for rest till the next set. Kevin had only recently started working out in this manner, and he felt it. His arms and chest felt like they were on fire. If only he'd started on this regimen when his uncle had first brought it to him. There was a reason that Uncle Joey could get away with the gimmick of a chemically enhansed madman. There aren't too many 5' 7', 210 pound men that could bench 450 or leg press more than double that. That was the benefit of having a brother whom worked at the University of Arizona Sports Research Department. Uncle Alex had made public several papers on the benefits of continual contraction and dynamic work/rest cycles. Uncle Joey'd been effectively using the techniques for years, but it wasn't till last week when Kevin had needed to grunt a bit while taking Windsor's lard ass over for a suplex that he decided to look into a strength program more rigorous than standard calistenics and occasionally pushing the weight plates with reckless abandon. It was his hope that a more complete physical package would help him push up to the next level. But fricken JJ? He was going to have to a have a talk with Mr. Reporterman before his match. If Miya was determined to go out there with JJ as his insurence policy, then it was Kevin's job to make sure Mr. Reporter knew a thing or two about the opponent. After all, Kevin had spent an entire summer on the road with CCW's crew as a ring monkey. He'd stood at the gorilla and watched Cianan make his mark on the wrestling world. He's sat with his uncle and ran down possible strategies for countering Cianan's mixed martial arts background. Hopefully JJ would be able to implement or pass that info off to 'the boss.' But then again, there's another match to look to. Who are the Black Dragon's? Most would expect a gang from China to be Triad, yet they were against the Triad supposedly. Apparently, they were against just about everyone. Seems like an overgrown case of schoolyard bullies never growing up. Duplicity was to be expected, as was outnumbering odds. The odds were dependant on a man he'd never spoken with before. Bushido. Would this be another dissappointment looking for a quick yen before fleeing to where ever he came from, or someone worthy of keeping a contract with PURE. He certainly hoped for the later, because 4 on 1 wasn't an inviting option at the time. The lockerroom was awefully disjointed. Inphino and MAH were supposed heros, yet did little as far as confronting any injustice. Mostly they just talked. Whether he liked it or not, Kevin's gut feeling told him he would be alone in the ring come Ikusa, so he'd better make ready for it. The tone rang out again, and Kevin return to his reps. He needed to be stronger, faster, better. But how strong, how fast, and how good would he need to be to break through to his goals? Only one way to find out...
