The Battle Lines are Drawn

15 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Battle Lines are Drawn Cannons to the left of me... And so, with another edition of Ikusa coming up, I am putting my Junior Heavyweight Title on the line against three men, instead of one [woman]. I go from having to wait two weeks to defend it, to having to defend it in progressively difficult matches. At this rate, next Ikusa shall be myself against the Japanese armed forces, since none of them can be over Junior Heavyweight status. Charlie Loc... he interfered in my match last Ikusa. Well, no, he ran in after Carl Kimbell ruined the match. A man as disgusting as him is upset that he was turned down by a porn star. I don't even want to get into that. Loc attacked me after my match. I'm not at all sure what he felt he's accomplished by this. If he was trying to get to my title, he could've earned it. Cannons to the right of me... So now, a six-man tag. Mine is the only title on the line (in fact, I still seem to be the only champion in PURE). How's this work? Well, I'm given two partners, and on the other side of the ring are Charlie Loc, HIT, and Takumi Umeshita. The man who pins me will be crowned the new Junior Heavyweight Champion. The HIT... I've already outshined him, my former teacher at the PWA. I beat him for the Junior Heavyweight Title, and I outlasted him in the Asian Title Tournament Match (and Yuka still hasn't thanked me for letting her win, but I didn't really expect it). He thinks he can beat me for this title, win he couldn't even beat me in the first place to be the first champion? Cannons in front of me... This match will favor me in that my opponents will all try to be the ones to pin me, so they will most likely end up fighting each other to beat me. I could sit back and relax. I doubt they are a solid team enough to work together and let one of them get the pin with the others standing and watching. Takumi Umeshita never did anything to me, and I still respect him. He's alright with me so far. He'll still want my title, most likely, and having nothing to do with the others, he may not even wish to tag them in. So I have three men against me who may not even be against me so much as against themselves. Interesting situation. Cannons behind me... The odds area against me, as well. Jordan X Shabazz is on my team, as well as Brian Simon. I'm still trying to figure this out. Shabazz and I have no connection other than that we are both Junior Heavyweights. But he'll most likely be thinking about Ali Khadafi and won't have his mind on the match, so his reliability is questionable, at best. Brian Simon? No, really, Brian Simon? I don't think we've ever been in the same room together, let alone on the same team in the same ring. He and I have no connection. In fact, I struggle to see Brian Simon's purpose in this match, since he is the only man who is not a Junior Heavyweight, and by my best notion, he has nothing to do witih any of us. And since losses in exhibition tag matches by primarily singles competitors don't count against one's record (especialy when that wrestler is not pinned or made to submit), then I have to think that perhaps Brian Simon may not even participate in this match, IF he shows up for it at all. So, while I have partners for this tag match, I could very well be facing three men by myself. And if these three men do end up working as a team, then that could be trouble for me. But I forget that in winning the Junior Heavyweight Title, I defeated The HIT and Kensuke Suzahara, two men that primarily teamed up to focus their attack on me. Yes, I've got loaded cannons to my left, to my right, and in front of me. But will the cannons on my side fire? I can't say, and I won't know for sure until the next Ikusa. Charlie Loc, we're rivals, sometimes hating each other, sometimes on good standing. It's pretty much like my relationship with your friend, Bobby Stormbringer. I've left you alone up to this point, but you want to come to me and start something? Heh, it ends on Ikusa. HIT, you're angry that I've surpassed you in every way since we first met. And in such a short time, too. Insecure fool, you can never be me, you shouldn't want to be me. The things I've been through are things I wouldn't wish upon my worst enemy. Live up to yourself, not your former pupil. Then again... perhaps you're mad that I gave Inphino Blitz a DQ win over you at the last Ikusa. I assure you, that was nothing personal. I've just got plans for Blitz, and needed to get things underway. Believe me, it would've been a lot safer for you had you just gone on with your life. But now, I have to teach you a lesson about moving on. Maybe moving onto the hospital after our match will give you time to think. Takumi Umeshita, I have no problems with you, and hope for a good clean competition between us. Jordan X Shabazz, you'd better have you head in this match when you get to the ring. I don't care if Khadafi comes out with your father and shoots him in the head, you will stay at the ring and play your role in this match. Brian Simon, if you're even paying attention to this match which I admit has nothing to do with you, should you be handed the ball in this match, don't drop it. I've talked quite a bit now, and may not even have said anything. But that's just how I am. I don't really like to talk, I'm much more eloquent a wrestler and fighter. So what do you say that we skip these little attempts at 'promos' and just go straight to the part where I make you submit, deal?
