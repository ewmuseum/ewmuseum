The Beginning Of The Resurrection

12 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Beginning Of The Resurrection Written: 2-12-03 The scene slowly opens up on the inside of a small training facility. Everything looking to be in tact, and by the looks of things it isn’t an average training facility. Sparring gear, weighting lifting materials, and all forms of every type of fighting equipment are found throughout the building. The lights are on shinning bright and the sound of scampering is heard in the distance, but the camera is caught off guard by the sound of someone walking through the front door. He swings around and finds Jesse Ramey wearing his normal attire walking into the building looking a little confused.
Jesse: (Yelling) Hello! (To himself softly) I sure do hope I’m at the right address. Jesse begins to examine the piece of paper in his hand as he continues to walk through the building, the sound of the noises coming from the other rooms startles Jesse as he stops and begins throwing punches as a fairly beaten up punching bag.
Jesse: That old man had better not have put me up to something that wasn’t going to happen or have any effect on me.
Jesse continues throwing punches. Jesse: I could have easily been off getting ready for this coming PPV, but no! I had to come here and just see what this old guy had to say to me.
Sudden the figuration of a dark shadow begins to approach Jesse as he continues to throw punches.
Jesse: Stupid old man. He’d better show up soon. Just as Jesse says that an old wrinkly hand is lain down upon his shoulder and scares the living crap out of him. Jesse jumps around in fear and realizes that the old man has appeared.
Old Man: Sorry to have startled you. (Chuckling) Jesse: (Trying to not look frightened) Yeah, well, you’re lucky I didn’t turn around and cause you to break a hip or something.
Old Man: Indeed, young master. Jesse: Now, could you explain to me why I had to come here before you could reveal anything to me? I mean you made me drive all the way out here, was what you got that important?
Old Man: Aye, it was indeed. You see, I’ve known you for a very long time, Jesse. Jesse: So? What’s that got to do with anything now? Old Man: It has a lot to do if you’ll take what I’ve got and put your total thought into it.
Jesse: You’ve been jerking me around for a long time now old man, can’t you just come out with it already?
Old Man: If you insist. The old man reaches down to the ground and pull up a dusty looking paper bag. Jesse: What’s in the bag. Old Man: Your history, young one. Jesse: My history? Old Man: Aye. If I give you this, you must not open it until the next show. Jesse: Why the next show? Old Man: Because it will mean nothing to you unless you are in a state of battle. Jesse: What says I will be in a state of battle on the next show? Old Man: Trust in me for once, young one. You will see that all of this waiting has come because of the greater benefit to your career here.
Jesse: So what’s in the bag is going to make me ten times better? Old Man: Aye. Jesse: You’re crazier than I thought, old man. There is no way that whatever is in the bag is going to help me become any better.
Old Man: And what makes you so sure of that? Jesse: Just trust me, it’s not going to make me any better than I already am. The old man reaches down, picks up the bag, and extends it to Jesse. Jesse takes the bag and looks at the old man with a cocky grin on his face.
Jesse: Just to humor you, I’ll go along with this stuff. Trust me though, when the time comes, you’ll see that whatever is in here will do nothing for me. (Shaking his head in laughter) So, is that all you wanted?
Old Man: Aye. Jesse: Can I leave? Old Man: Yes, you may leave. Jesse turns and continues snickering as he walks out the door with the paper bag and the scene fades to black as the old man continues to shake his head in disbelief.
