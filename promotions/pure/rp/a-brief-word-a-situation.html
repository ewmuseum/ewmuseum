A Brief Word & A Situation

1 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A Brief Word & A Situation TokyoPop's controlling interest in Puroresu Ultimate Ring Entertainment has led them to devote a brand new channel for the company to broadcast it's weekly Ikusa, and it's promotional material. Earlier this week, due to restrictions applied by the 20% Owner of PURE, Jamar Wilson, Joey Flash was unable to broadcast a promotional appearance. Joey, taking exception, 'Hi-jacked' the airwaves of TPN's latest cult gameshow, FACE YOUR FEAR. As a result, and to stop any further problems, Joey has been allowed to appear on the PURE channel to promote his match on Ikusa 3. ----- **The scene opens up in a hotel room at an undisclosed location. The bed is neatly made, and the rest of the furniture seems untouched. The sole occupant of the room is stood, leaning against the wall in front of us. Dressed in a pair of off-white combat trousers, white Reebok trainers, and a blue T-shirt, his blonde hair lays resting on his shoulders. We recognise him as Joey Flash, the man who fights against Darkstar for his job in PURE. He leans forward as he addresses the camera, in fluent and precise Japanese.** 'I'm home. This is where I belong. Japan. I was raised here. I spent most of my school years here. I spent six of my ten years wrestling here in Japan. I'm finally back home.' **Joey steps forward, walking over to the bed, and sitting down.** 'I spent so many years here, that English is a second language to me. Me being British is not a problem. I practically have joint citizenship. But my return to this wonderful country has been ruined by someone who treats it like a playground. After working in England for a while, and bailing out my protege D-Kay Calyx while he had an identity crisis, I manage to impress enough to sign a contract with PURE. Granted, I was to wrestle under the name Siena. Used to protect me. Wrestle under a mask as an American athlete. Don't let them know who you are. I, of course, agreed. Partly on an ego trip, partly for the greater good. I wanted to see whether I could still provoke a reaction for my performances. I wanted to see whether some of my moves still got applause. But, after thinking about it, I suppose I did it really to help out a company. They needed gaijin. And in this day and age, it's all American. A true showdown. American wrestling against Japanese wrestling. That's what they want to see. I duly obliged. I stepped into the ring with Kanox, looking to make an impression on the Ikusa crowd. And what an impression I made.' **Joey stands again, and walks over to the window of the room. Looking out over a main street full of activity, he continues.** 'Jamar Wilson. TokyoPop's own ego. Announces that either Kanox or me would be fired. I tried my hardest, but couldn't pull it off. I lost. I was handed my pink slip. But that wasn't enough for me. I was fighting the American way. I wrestled the American way for the good of the company. Jamar warned me before the match that my move set had to be 'Americanised' and that I had to make the crowd believe I was from the US. My performance was poor under those circumstances. I don't make any excuses for it. But, that was Siena. That was not me. Jamar released Siena. I now demand my shot. I am here to wrestle. I am here to show respect to the country that gave me a head start in life. I embraced puroresu more than any other sport, and it is now time for me to give back. I signed with PURE to wrestle. I signed to return to Japan, to prove that I still have pride for what I do. To prove I still honor the traditions of the greats that helped build the basis of puroresu. And I will make sure I uphold those values.' **Joey walks back over to the bed again, sitting on the far side and picking up a holdall, placing it beside him.** 'Now I face my biggest challenge to date. I face a test for survival to earn my place. The countless years I have lived and wrestled in Japan, mean nothing if I fail to earn my place in PURE. This is my meal ticket, to coin a phrase. Without wrestling, I am nothing. PURE gives me a chance to be something. But to do so, I have to compete in a match sanctioned by Jamar Wilson. A Japanese Death Match against an American athlete who is responsible for some of the most brutal attacks in the wrestling world...Darkstar.' **Joey pauses to let the comment sink in.** 'Jamar Wilson thinks he controls PURE. He is merely a 20% Owner. He believes so strongly in his beliefs, he practically forces others to believe as well. He threatens to pull out of the TV deal for PURE, and to cancel the broadcasting needed to keep the company running. He believes in it so strongly, he doesn't see alternatives. I do. When Sean Williams, and the people who hold the puroresu values TRUE to their hearts get rid of the TokyoPop influence, PURE will be on the map. We won't need a network on the rebound from losing FMW. I'm making a promise for tonight. I will beat Darkstar and regain my roster spot as Joey Flash. Then, the fun starts. America claims to have started the 'Sports Entertainment' revolution in the wrestling industry. Well...Wrestling can survive without the Sports Entertainment. Sports Entertainment can't survive without wrestling. The future of puroresu is being challenged. But, like many other challenges laid before tradition in the past, honor and pride shall prevail. Whether it be myself, Sean Williams, The End, Inphino Blitz, The Pharaoh or anyone else who drives TokyoPop out of PURE, it will be a welcome result.' **Joey stands up, and picks up his holdall, placing the strap over his shoulder. He walks towards the door, and turns briefly.** 'Darkstar. Tonight I fight for two things. Survival, and my job. I fear for both. I'm not going to the American thing, and say that I'm going to destroy you. I know your abilities. I've seen what you are capable of in a normal match. This being a Japanese Death Match, I can only imagine what you are going to do. I do feel fear. I think everyone in my position would. But I have overcome my fears in the past, and I will overcome them tonight. I am going to prove my point tonight, whatever it takes. I'm not saying that I have already won...but I am saying that when this is done, I will make sure I'm the winner.' **With that, Joey turns on his heels, and exits the room, locking the door behind him. We fade to black...** **Or do we? An unknown voice cuts in over the blackness...** 'Come on! We have to show it! It's hilarious. Mr Wilson will be so proud of us if we do! Why? It shows him up! Come on...just press play. Here...I'll do it...' **The voice ends as soon as it started, and the scene shows Joey Flash, still in the same clothes, pulling up to Ikusa Hall in a taxi cab. He climbs out, and pays the driver, before turning and walking up to the building. Holdall still over his shoulder, he heads for the 'workers' entrance, but realises he doesn't have a clearance card, and heads for the main entrance. As he approaches, a small group of American fans appear, and rush up the steps to buy tickets. Joey tags along at the end, and pays for his ticket at the booth, before entering the revolving doors. Instinctively, he turns to his right, walking down the corridor towards the locker rooms.** 'Hey! Stop! Who are you?' **A voice speaking in broken English causes Joey to turn around, face to face with three uniformed security guards. The one in the middle, short black hair cropped close to his head, steps forward, and grabs hold of Joey's arm. Joey responds in Japanese.** 'My name is Joey Flash. I'm wrestling tonight.' **The guard looks surprised at the perfect Japanese spoken to him by a gaijin but replies in his native tongue.** 'All wrestlers must enter by workers door.' 'I technically don't work here until tonight. I don't have a pass because I've been fired once. I wrestle tonight to get my job back. Jamar Wilson refused to issue me a pass.' **As soon as he'd said the words, Joey's facial expression betrays his conscience, realising how bad that would have sounded. The guard smiles at him, and motions for the second of the two guards to help.** 'I see. In that case, please come with us.' **The two security guards take an arm each and escort him down the corridor back towards the entrance, turning through a separate door towards the security office. The third guard follows with the holdall. We fade to black.**
