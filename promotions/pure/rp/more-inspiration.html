More Inspiration

20 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
More Inspiration It was early morning on Wednesday and Faithful Youth was up. He didnt get much sleep last night even though he did come in early. The talk with Rebecca really did open his eyes a little. People all over the place wanted to be like someone else and he was one of them. They wanted to be able to write like him thats all. There are so many other people to watch for wrestling and he wasnt one of them. Well not yet at least. He sat up in his hotel room or the first night and sat outside the balcony with a book of paper and a pen. Just him and his thoughts were outside. Everyone else was still sleeping from the long day before but Andrew couldnt sleep. He looked out in the darkness and took in some deep breaths. He came to Japan for inspiration and it was just about ten minutes before that new inspiration would hit. He sat back and waited but kept himself busy by going through old writings he had done before. He was currently re-reading his first book and to see how far he had moved on from the start. Each book was split into sections for different thoughts from love, to hate and fear. But currently he was on the dreams section. Something he read often to keep his spirit high. He read quickly and flipped through pages of poems in a short time. Some where still in his mind and he wouldnt even have to read it to know what the message was. Some of the stuff he wrote was very good while others where there just because he wrote them. He knew which ones where fillers. Hard to believe this was only ten years ago, he whispered like he was talking to the night. He looked around for any movement but nothing still. A few new lights came on but where quickly put out. He just sat back and waited. He was already changed ready for the days events. He had too much time of his hands and sitting out here was the only thing to do. If he went to sleep he would start to think about what would happen tonight and that was not a good thing. He looked at the sky and slowly shook his head. Only about five minutes more, he said to himself putting down his book and picking up his pad of paper and the pen. He was ready for what was going to happen. His inspiration was only five minutes away. He looked forward making sure not to miss anything. He thought about Rebecca, and how she was missing this. The inspiration was right in front of everyone and yet no one took advantage of it. Anything can inspire someone just as long as you look at it in all the different views. One more minute, he said again counting down the seconds till he was in lightened. He took some deep breaths and waited longer. Canada had given this kind of inspiration before but a change of scene might help. He looked down at his watch. It was almost time for it to happen. He looked over the rooftops of the small Japan houses and narrowed his eyes on a spot. The darkness made it hard to see but it wouldnt last forever. He closed his eyes for just a second to soak in all that was about to happen. He opened them just in time. A bright light raised from the rooftops like that is where it lived. Slowly it moved up. Andrews eyes didnt come off the object. He mod his head to the left a bit and tapped his pen. He moved his head down to his pad and started to write. The idea just came to him from no where and was stuck in his head. He wrote a few words down and then looked back up. The darkness started to creep away very slowly. It was still below him but it would be gone soon. He wrote fast making sure it was easy for him to read after. He didnt want to lose any inspiration. Letters flew on the page and formed lines of work. This past time was turning into a career in its self. Every little thing brought an idea. This was just one of the many things the Japan world would bring to the table. He quickly wrote down faster and faster. It was almost over. He wiped his hair from his eyes and looked up for the right word to put in this section. He snapped back into his work when it came to him. He was at the bottom of the page. This being a small book. He was finished after five minutes of writing. He finished it off with the faithful Youth signature and read it over once making sure there where no errors. He smiled when he was complete. He got up out of his chair and looked down at the street starting to fill with people. He placed the poem book on his chair and looked down on it. He shook his head at the work. He was happy with it. A piece of inspiration last only a few seconds but it can last a life time, he said looked down at his poem again. He closed his book up and gathered his things. He placed them all on the bed and started to walk out of the room. He went back and picked up the book. He put it under his arm and left the room. He closed the door and started his day but made sure to keep his book near just in case any new inspirations hit him.
