A Ghost

23 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A Ghost Written: 1/23/03 (There is complete darkness accompanied by complete silence… No scenery, no voices, no sounds, no visibility. A sort of sereneness, a peace of mind, a peace of body, but there is something about this silence that promises something… But what is it? Is it pain? Is it pleasure? Is it victory or maybe defeat? Is it fear? Is it passion? Or maybe it is anger! What is this darkness, this ominous feeling, a feeling of not knowing what is about to come, what is it? And why are we so intrigued to find out?)
Narrator: Tonight’s story follows one man! (The silence is broken as a lone spotlight shines down on a caucasion man standing in a black tuxedo, a small glimmer to the complete darkness. The light hits his face and upper body making his look ghostly and intriguing. More questions come to mind… Who is this man? Who is the man this man speaks of? What does he know that we don’t? Will we find out what this is all about?)
Narrator: You might have questions… HE might have answers! (The darkness is replaced by a shot of a beautiful seventy-foot waterfall somewhere in a disclosed location in Japan. There is a forest surrounding the body of water that the waterfall empties into and a nice blue sky above with the sun blazing its hot rays below. Halfway up the waterfall stands a lone figure in meditation, with his massive arms in front of his body and his hands clasped together. He is perched on the wet stone under the waterfall, his eyes closed, and his bald head, and unclothed body glistening as the water falls on his freely. He stands there in his loincloth meditating as the Narrator comes back to view at the bottom. The waterfall scene dims a bit as the spotlight comes back to shine on the Narrator of our story.)
Narrator: His name is Chiang Ho Nam! A China man who has seen pain, who has witnessed love, he has been through much but cannot speak a single word! Take a closer look at this man…
(The narrator disappears again as the waterfall scene comes back to life. A closer shot of this man meditating is shown and a noticeable scar crosses his throat. The narrator returns to the bottom of the picture.)
Narrator: You may be wondering what that scar is? Why can’t this man speak? You see, this man had his throat slashed when he was but a mere child of 12 years. A rival Triad gang known as the White Lotus crushed his voice box in an attempt to send a message to the Dragons. You see, this man has been through a great deal of pain! Some say it is a miracle to see him still alive, others say he is just too stubborn to die! While quite a few wonder if he truly is alive! The Mountain Lord is known to have magical powers… What is this man all about? If he could speak what would he say? What is running through that pain filled head of his? Will his story ever be told?
(The narrator disappears and we are brought back to the scene at hand, with Chiang still standing strong, perched above the clear blue body of water below. The only sounds that can be heard are the crashing sounds of the water from the falls hitting the pool below, the birds chirping their song, the wind blowing among the trees. But Chiang so deep in meditation is oblivious to it all!)
Voice among the wind: C….H…I….A….N…G…! (Chiang still oblivious to it all continues to meditate unbroken and un-phased.) Voice among the wind: C….H…I….A….N…G…! It is me… “Angel”!!!! (With the mere mention of the name, Chiang’s eyes pop wide! His head jerks side to side as he searches his surroundings to find the speaker of that oh so familiar voice. As thoughts rave through his head, from denial to hope!)
Voice among the wind: I am here…. (Chiang turns slowly on the wet rocks keeping perfect balance. But as he turns around, he sees the familiar speaker. His jaw drops and his eyes pop wide open! A look of fear crosses his face as the floating speaker glides up to him. If a gust of wind came at that point, he would have been easily blown over!)
Voice among the wind: It’s me Angel! (Chiang shakes his head violently as his jaw remains dropped. He blinks his eyes a few times and wipes them with the palms of his hands. When he removes his hands he sees to great amazement that the beautiful speaker is still there.)
Angel: I know… It can’t be can it? I died years ago didn’t I? It is true and no I am not real… In your state of meditation you conjured me up with your heart and your soul. I am here for you my big teddy bear! So speak, for in this “dream” your voice is as strong as it is in your mind!
(After years of not being able to speak, Chiang moves his mouth, mouthing out words but no voice comes to carry his words across the wind! After a few frustrated growls, he finally speaks his first words after 13 years of silence!)
Chiang: I…. Love… You… (With those words, the ghost of Angel, the love of his life, smiles as a tear of joy forms at the corner of Chiang’s eyes. Then a look of pure confusion crosses his face as he puts his hands on his hips.)
Chiang: What is this? Why are you here? This won’t end will it… Angel: I don’t know what this is exactly… I look at you from above and I know in your heart that you are feeling turmoil. This Saturday will be the 6th year anniversary of my death and I know you are still hurting inside…
(Chiang frowns and looks down at his feet trying to keep from crying as tears is a show of weakness to his understanding. Angel smiles and floats closer to him rising a little to meet him eye to eye.)
Angel: You know what pain is! You have lost everything that means something to you! Your voice, your parents, some even believe you lost your soul! But most importantly you lost me! The love of your life!
(Chiang tries to look away again but he is unable to for some unearthly reasons.) Angel: Time for regret and mourning is over my dear Chiang! The time has come for you to channel your pain, channel that anger, channel that sense of hoplesness and loss… Channel it, build fuel to that fire of rage deep in your heart! I see the fires burning in those deep brown eyes…
(Chiang tenses as his corded muscles in his arms bulge. Angel lays her ghostly hand on his shoulder and continues.)
Angel: You are back in PURE… The third trial is about to begin! The war is ready to be waged! There is no one capable of stopping you, Chen and Chi! No Sushi Crew, no Nu Era, No Shaun Styles, no Jesse Ramey, Enmity, or even Jamar! No one is able to stop you for you have felt pain that no one else has even imagined! Time for that pain to be realized and released! Fuck them up Chiang! Fuck them up!
(Chiang smiles and laughs in a methodical way that just promises to do what his Angel said. Angel floats closer as her ghost form kisses Chiang on the cheek but as Chiang tries to wrap his arms around the love of his life, his soul mate, his babies momma, his Angel, she dissipates into nothing, replaced by air. Chiang smiles and nods his head knowing there will be a time where he will be with her…)
Chiang: I…. Love…. You….! (Chiang closes his eyes and shakes his head and when he reopens them he is back standing on the rocks facing the pool below as if nothing had changed, as if it were just a mere dream. But he smiles knowingly.)
Chiang: …. (Chiang spreads his arms out and falls forward off the rock cliff. His body free falls with the water from the falls and at the last second he rotates and hits the pool of water below feet first creating a massive splash!)
Voice among the wind: Fuck them up my Chiang! (The scene fades and is replaced by darkness as a shot of the Narrator is shown again with a spotlight on him making him look ghastly.)
Narrator: He has seen years of pain, torture, and loss. Will he follow the advice that the love of his life gave him? Will he “Fuck them up” or will he fail and lose more than respect? For failure is not an option when you are a member of the deadly Black Dragons! It’s either victory or DEATH! You think this story is over? HA! This story has just begun!
