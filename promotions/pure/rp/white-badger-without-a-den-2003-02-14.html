White Badger without a den

14 February 2003

White Badger without a den Written: 14/02/03 There's a big pro wrestling card coming to a town near you soon. This one is called PURE's Sunday Night Ikusa, and it is being promoted by the WO, a wrestling promotion caught in the eye of a hurricane. Paranoia, fear, jealousy, envy, and hatred.
Top to bottom the card looks great. Lots of matches, too many matches even. A PACKED card. And then there is that ONE match. Kohana versus YULAV! It's on the internet and it's mentioned in the sheets. The Japanese talk about it, one of their own and stuff, but all in all, it's just another match in a promotion that has seen better days and bigger stars. To many this is just another match...
But then it isn't. Not to one man it isn't. His name is Senji Yulav. And today he is getting ready to leave for Ikusa. There, he will face a man called Kohana, and if he can get by him, he will face another desperate man, followed by another.
The USA is on its last legs. Listen to me, my name is 'The Universal Outlaw' Senji Yulav. It's just a different body, but the same spirit. Only this time around, after... what? Close to 4-5-6 years, the spirit is slowly vanishing, decaying, fading. Some say it is the nature of the business. Some say it is the front office. Underneath there is a whole lot more. Pride, competition, hate, jealousy, anger, money. And of course, fame. Who cares... not me. I'm here for the funeral. I'm here to make MY name on the burnt ashes of a dream. No phoenix rises up this time around the USA.... Bush is burning while the front office plays its violin... each player playing with himself. Rome is burning and the gladiators fight on. To them it's life and death however. To Yulav...
... it's a game he CAN'T LOSE. He stands on the edge of a small pond, very still. He is a part of the trees, the grass, the water, the earth.
He grew up in a world very black and white. No gray in between. Win or lose. Live or die. It was not by accident. It was by choice. This is what he wanted. Extreme country. No distracions. Just day and night, eat, sleep, work. Fight and laugh.
He stands there for what feels like an eternity, unmoving. He looks so peaceful, so tranquil. Like he is taking it all in, the beauty, the stillness, the peaceful nature of this almost sacred place.
There is just win or lose here. Alot of people might overlook that fact, see a man with a temper and a propensity to drink himself stupid. Fly off the handle. Talk tough and intimidating. It's not an act, it's not really a philosophy, a way of life. He doesn't know any better.
In the mist, near the water, by the tree, Yulavsinks into the background. He has become a part of it now.
Then... his eyes shift. You don't realize but he never moved that whole time. Not just an arm, a leg, to adjust for balance, to blink. He NEVER moved, until now, his eye shifted to the left. A hand that was leaning against a tree slowly comes to relax by his side.
In the distance, passing through a tree, a small deer plods its wat across wet grass and mud, chewing on a tree limb here, tasting a bush there.
Let's be clear on this, Yulav doesn't know what's been going on, and he doesn't care. All he knows is there is fresh meat to be had, names to destroy, a reputation to keep. He wants to fight. He wants a fucking fight, that\s all. The gold is just iceing on the cake.
Senji Yulav is the Universal Outlaw. But he is also 'Wasicu Hoka Cola Oti'. The Indians call him 'White Badger Without Den'.
Senji Yulav watches as the deer takes off, running and hopping over fallen branches and rocks. He was able to get close enough to give it a nice slap on the ass. Yulav practices... all arts... always.
At Ikusa, All Asia Title Match, the firewater running wild.... the white badger will finally find a den. And he will call it PURE.
Senji turns around and heads back. It's time to go. Ikusa is a day away.
