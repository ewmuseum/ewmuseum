The Voyage - Part one

March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Voyage - Part one Written: Today [Osaka…an ancient capital city of Japan, a place where light and shadow cross paths and annihilate each other. This is the city where Eddie Mandrake resides in. We head into one of the seedier areas of Osaka. Not really for the crime that goes on there…but the fact that it just doesn’t look good compared to the rest of the city. Into an apartment building, where is looks great on the inside, but looks polluted and worn on the outside. In one of the apartments, it simply doesn’t look really moved into yet. Most of the stuff is still in boxes, the walls haven’t been painted a more suitable colour. In walks Eddie Mandrake holding onto a plastic bag and a big pile of mail. He’s struggling to hold onto his keys because of the food and the mail. He turns left and enters the kitchen, and he places the plastic bag on the counter. He walks back out to have a look at the mail. He goes through the envelopes that have the distinctive mark of a bill.]
Eddie: Bill… (Tosses envelope across the room) Bill… (Tosses envelope across the room) Bill… (Tosses envelope across the room)
[The next envelope has a very familiar look. It has the same logo that appears on the upper right leg of his pants. Eddie, in a panic, opens the letter up. It reads as follows…]
Dearest Edward You have been invited to join the Wych Gladiator Training Facility at Lake Yamanaka. It is here that you will learn how to fight with restraint, perfect for those within fighting job field as Police Officer, Bounty Hunter, or Athlete. To join us here is very simple; you must head to our facility immediately but arrive there within seven days of receiving this invitation. You must present this invitation to be granted access to the ferry. We would like to thank you in advance
Yours Truly Taka Kumamoto Archon, Wych Gladiator Training Facility, Asian district. [Eddie then remembers a few years ago, when he first received this invitation in 1999, but he declined it due to the amount of outstanding targets that he had to purge from the planet. This time it is much different. He immediately gets a bag with his work-out clothes, and the bag containing his in-ring attire, and immediately heads out the door locking it behind him. He walks out the door of his apartment building and flags down a taxi. One stops and he enters the cab.]
Eddie: Shin-Osaka station, please. Taxi Driver: (Something in Japanese) Eddie: Oh shit…Uhm…Let’s hope I remember what to say here…Um…. [Eddie for a brief moment thinks, and finally says the right thing.] Eddie: (Directions in Japanese) [Shin-Osaka station, please.] Taxi Driver: (Acknowledgement in Japanese) [The taxi pulls away from Eddie’s apartment building…approximately ten minutes later; the taxi arrives at the train station. Eddie pays the fare with no tip. He then enters the train station. It is a rather modern looking station, looks new. It has a Karaoke bar in there, as well as a few other amenities. Eddie power walks to the only open ticket booth at this time. Eddie puts his bags down and looks at the short, woman dressed in a red suit.]
Ticket Agent: (Greeting in Japanese) Eddie: Do you speak English? Ticket Agent: I do actually. Eddie: Ah, wonderful, I need a ticket to Yamanashi prefecture. Ticket Agent: Uh…that’s quite the large area; you’ll have to be more specific. Eddie: Going to the station that is closest to Lake Yamanaka. Ticket Agent: Ah, going to get a few rounds of golf in? Eddie: Uh, yeah…I’ll be buying my clubs down there. Ticket Agent: Okay then, just give me a second. [Eddie turns away from the booth to admire the scenery. Looking around and looking into the Karaoke bar. He sees his Cultural Consultant in the bar performing. Eddie immediately turns away from Nomura-san who is performing. Nomura-san immediately drops the microphone and heads out of the bar. He’s dressed once again in his discount three-piece suit. He runs to Eddie who has his ticket in hand and he’s trying to power-walk to the train platform. Nomura however, catches up to him.]
Nomura: Eddie, where the hell are you going? You have a Pay-Per-View to attend later on. Eddie: Fuck it. Nomura: The PURE Heavyweight Championship! Eddie: Fuck it! Nomura: Vengeance! You get to kill Enmity, live on International television. Eddie: Fuck him; Fuck it, and Fuck You! This is business that I and I alone have to perform. Where I am going, you cannot follow. I will meet you at the building.
Nomura: You have got to be kidding me? You have two possible matches tomorrow, and you are headed out on a trip? Are you that dense not to train before a big match such as this?
Eddie: I fully intend to train, I intend to take this train to somewhere where you cannot be.
Nomura: Look, I am looking out for you. You think I am doing this for my benefit? Hell no! I was assigned here!
Eddie: Assign you ass out of my way, because you aren’t getting on the train! Security! This drunk is harassing me!
[Security runs up and grabs Nomura-san and pulls him away from the platform and eventually out of the station. Eddie simply waits for the train as we fade to black.]
