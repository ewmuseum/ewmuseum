Little Oreo, Little Oreo Fake Gangster Fake Thug. I run my tongue over my teeth and taste the stale nicotine. I pitch my last smoke into the rainy streets of Osaka, turning my eyes from the harsh neon billboards and struggle. I struggle to quell the rage that burns forever within my breast. I struggle to consume the poison that pumps from my heart and flows through these veins. Fake Fake What the fuck would you know about being fake, fucking Oreo? All wrapped up in your protective plastic plastic wrapper. All wrapped up in the warm glow of your camaraderie, basking in the warm reception that you receive. Fucking Oreo. With your two crumbly cookie sides and your fucking creamy insides Your WHITE inside Your threats and your attacks, little Oreo. I watch you trying to be hard and I scoff because you cant even stand on your own two feet. You have to have some dumb honky shit Some dumb washed-up piece of white trash Bring you in to your first home. You look at me from your protective plastic wrapper, little Oreo, and you try to emulate through violence. Fake Fake Fuck you little Oreo Fuck your Malice Crew Fuck your friends Fuck your parents and your family. Kiss them goodbye and wipe the tears from their eyes, for they will go first, little Oreo. Ill level the house you were raised in, salt the earth where it stood, and shit in the mouth of your mother. Fuck your training It wont help when youre having your own eyeballs fed to you Fuck everything that you work and stand for Fuck your activism Fake Fake Let me ask you little Oreo Ready for a little Mind-Fucking?
