More Complications...

7 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
More Complications... 1 year ago... It was a very cold night when I pulled into the car park just outside of Sleepy Sal's. I had been driving for hours looking for somewhere I could 'wet the babies head.' I knew it was dangerous to drink and drive but what could one little drink do. I turned the key to switch off the engine, it switched off with a satisfactory tone in its mechanical way. I quickly stepped out of the car and as I did the heavens decided to open. As the drops of rain pattered away on the roof of my car, I couldn't help feeling a sense of deja vu. I had been coming to Sleepy Sal's since I was 18 years old, it was a place I liked to call 'safe'. It was 'safe' from the troubles of life, you entered Sleepy Sal's and you left your troubles outside. Sure it looked sleazy with it's neon sign outside, with a neon lighted woman showing off more cleavage than was usual, but it was a place I liked to go now and then when the troubles of the world got too much. I locked my car I huddle under my black trench coat and ran over to the entrance. Harry, Sleepy Sal's bouncer, was stood outside in the rain, suited up in his tuxedo as usual. Harry was one huge guy, a sort of guy you knew it wouldn't be wise to piss off. He looked down at me through his dark glasses. A smile crossed his face. 'How you doing tonight, sir?' I wasn't in the mood to strike up a conversation at this moment, but I couldn't just ignore the big brute. I put on a fake smile and looked up at him. 'I'm great thanks Harry, never been better.' Harry smiled back at me satisfied with my answer. I pushed the door open but before I could step in, Harry grabbed my arm tightly. I felt my heart race, why was Harry holding me back? I hadn't done anything wrong had I? I looked up at him again, I was thankful to see another smile cross his face. Deep down Harry was a nice guy but I still didn't want to get on the wrong side of him. I gulped to try and calm my nerves, and smiled yet again. 'What is it now, Harry?' He smiled one of his goofy smiles. 'Oh I was just wondering how your family was?' There was that word that I hated so much 'family' that word cut through me like a knife. Of course I didn't have a family, how could I have a family with my job? Even though it was already a cold night, I felt an extra chill rush down my spine. The word 'family' made me more nervous than Harry did. I sighed and yet again but on a brave face. 'My family are great Harry, yeah me and the wife are trying for another baby.' There it was one of the biggest lies I have ever told in my life. I couldn't help it, it would keep him going for at least 9 months before he would ask again. His smile grew wider at the mention of another imaginary kid on the way, and did a friendly punch on the arm. Well to him it was friendly to me it felt like a train had just rammed into my arm. I laughed as to hide the pain and pushed the door open as to escape another question. Again as I pushed the door open and stepped into the corridor leading to the bar I got another sensation of deja vu. I mean I knew I had been to Sleepy Sal's over a hundred times but this sensation of deja vu was something I couldn't shrug off. I threw my coat onto the pile that had formed by the side of the door. It was nice and toasty in Sleepy Sal's, just the sort of warmth someone wanted when coming in from the rain. I made my way towards the neon signed bar door. As I approached the door I could already here the music blasting away, I knew the 'night show' had begun. The wolf whistles of the men and the sexy slow music gave it all away. A little grin crossed face as I swung the door open and stepped into the bar. Indeed my first judgement I had been correct, as soon as I had entered I was hit by the image of an already half naked blonde bombshell moving up and down the pole in a provocative manner. I just smiled and moved over to the bar. I never really found it very erotic being surrounded by sweaty men, all who are trying to grab a handful of the lady on stage. I took another glance over my shoulder, just in time to see a bald fat tub of lard scramble his way onto the stage only to be thrown off again by another huge guard. I sat down on the centre barstool and grabbed a handful of peanuts and popped them in my mouth. Jason, Sleepy Sal's bartender, approached me, whipping a glass clean with a dirty rag cloth. He too was a big guy, but looked a little friendlier than the others. He had obviously recognised me as he placed the glass he was cleaning onto the shelf behind and leaned over the bar to talk to me. 'So what will it be, sir?' He said in his gruff Scottish tone voice. I took a moment to think. 'Erm, give me a neat double whiskey.' I said finally deciding on something I knew I would be safe drinking. Jason only took a few seconds before in front of me was the drink. I took a gulp and slammed it down on the counter. It was a good feeling as the alcohol slipped down my throat. I looked up at Jason who was now distracted by the topless dancer. However I knew that he wasn't 'watching' the dancer like the other men, because that the particular topless dancer they were all watching was none other than Jason's girlfriend, so Jason was not drooling over her, more like watching out for her. I decided to distract him as I had a feeling if I didn't he would get so wound up that another wolf whistle would send him over the edge. 'What time you on till Jas?' I asked in a hope to get his attention. 'Hmmm' Jason said still looking at the crowd of men surrounding his girlfriend with a frown on his face. I knew I would regret this but I grabbed his T-shirt and pulled him away from the image of his topless girlfriend. However he seemed sort of thankful, as I repeated my question. 'I said, what time are you on till tonight?' 'Oh, I am on till 7 am. I'm working double shifts so I will be able to take Rebecca home,' He said with a grin on his face. 'Yeah, I don't like to leave Rebecca on her own, she's so innocent sometimes.' Now I had 2 options here, I could have spit out my whiskey and fell on the floor laughing at the thought of a topless dancer being innocent. Or I could just nod my head in agreement and save myself the beating of a lifetime. I decided to go with the latter, and nodded my head. As I did a shifty character sat beside me. He had those sort of eyes that made you wonder if he really was who he said he was. He had slicked black hair and wore a dark trenchcoat like me. Again as he sat next to me I got that strange sense of deja vu. He didn't look at me at first but looked at Jason. 'Can I get a beer please.' He said with a very rough voice. As he spoke I got the sensation of deja vu grew. Where did I know him from I thought? Jason placed the beer on the counter and the man handed Jason the money. I decided I couldn't just sit there, I needed to know who he was. I turned on my stool to face him and extended my hand. 'Hey there. Listen this may sound a weird question but, I was just wondering do I know you from somewhere?' The man quickly snapped round to face me. The sight of me took him back, it was like he had seen a ghost. From the expression of shock it soon changed to anger. As he spoke I could here the hatred in his voice. 'You don't remember me do you, X?' 'No Im sorry I don't.' I said wondering where he was going with this conversation. 'My name is Maverick, Ben Maverick. You knew a friend of mine called Hassem Black. He told me if I would to ever see you again, I was to give you something from him.' I was intrigued. 'Oh and what would that be?' 'This!' In a blink of an eye I felt a sharp pain in the side of my gut. I looked down just in time to see Maverick pull an army knife from my gut. I tried to speak but the shock had taken it out of me as I fell to the floor and everything went to dark. Was It Real? Was It Fake? I jerked awake in my bed, dripping with sweat. It was all just a dream, a stupid dream, or so I thought. I looked down at my torso and was horrified to see a scar where I had been stabbed. It must have been real, but how come I had forgotten about it? Maybe I had just pushed it to the back of my mind, but I never do anything like that. I couldn't worry about it now, I had to get to Ikusa, and I had a match to survive. It's time! I am ready!
