There is no protections.. There is no safety..

25 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
There is no protections.. There is no safety.. ((Note: I wrote this for CCW, but because of the mention of Miyagawa, and other things, that he is feeling, I feel that you all should look. It doesn't need to be counted for anything, I just wanted to share. Thank you.)) The arena was freezing, and I had a little bit of a chill. Slowly walking, treading, down the silent path. No fear, no feeling. The normal for a pre-match. Everything seemed hazy, everything seemed surreal.
What was wrong with this? It doesn't feel right.. I throw the curtain to the side, and step out onto the ramp. I prepare myself, for what doesn't come. I turn my head to the side, to look at the crowd, but.. It was strange. They all sat still, facing towards the ring. No sounds, no movements, not even the sound of breathing. It was definitely unnerving, and rather frightening. Nothing.. Everyone just staring. Staring.. I hesitate, but I go on. I move down the ramp, and head towards the ring, and the center of attention. I duck between the ropes, and move to a corner, climbing up, and staring back at the crowd. Nothing.. But silence. What's going on..? I don't understand.. No reaction, no hatred. NOTHING. I am all but unnerved now. Just glancing about for a sign of life from the crowd. Nothing, no booing, no hatred. No signs, saying 'Cianan Sucks.' Just, blank stares on empty faces. 20,000 people, and you could hear a pin drop from the back. I started to get frantic... 'IF YOU DON'T SAY SOMETHING, I'LL KILL YOU!!' I scream, at the very top of my lungs. I want something, I don't want to be ignored.. 'SAY SOMETHING!! DO SOMETHING, YOU MONGRELS!' Again, my voice starts to give, I don't scream often, and my throat was going already. I already had the scratchy feeling in the back of my throat, and I knew, that the next scream would be raspy. Fuck it, I'll just wait for my opponent. I waited, pacing around the ring, back and forth, occasionally brushing the underside of my nose with my thumb. Where the hell was my opponent? Was I supposed to have a match against myself? Because, that's what this is feeling like. No opponent, no reaction, what the fuck was Sylkk thinking? I glanced to the crowd, once more in an attempt to get some reaction, and slammed my hands into the ropes and leaned over with a snarl. My eyes widen, as I realize who the audience exactly is. CCW. Family. Friends. People that I knew. I stagger back, with an exclamation on my lips. 'W..what?' Slowly they start climbing over the barrier, one by one, the friends, the family. I instinctively backed away, to the center of the ring, where I could hold them all off. A familiar voice rang out, above the silence. 'Cianan! I no love you.. I just use you. I pregnant..' Akiko. 'Sh..Shut up!!' I rush over to the side she's on.. Where her voice called from. 'I.. I'm sorr..' 'Cianan! You're not worthy to be where you are. You're overrated!' A voice screamed out, of Tom Manson. A fellow from PTC. 'YOU DON'T KNOW MY HISTORY!! HOW CAN YOU..' Again cut off, this time by the voice of.. 'You killed me, you ruined my career.. You drove me to OSW.' This time, I backed up, as I heard Jesse Ramey. A pale version of his normal self. 'Look at what you did to me! You drove me from CCW, you fuckin' prick!' Screamed an annoyingly familiar voice, of the first champion of OSW. Sebastian 'Switchblade' Toomes. 'Yeah, ya Sum-bitch.. You busted me up real good too. I bet you're proud, ain't ya?' Jason McAllister now. 'Ya got me in a massive clusterfuck.. what did you do? Nothin'. Ya jackass.' Did I die in my sleep? Is this hell? Am I being tortured for my sins? Forced to relieve all the mistakes and the heartaches? The pain and the remorse? Once more, my conscience, which has been quiet for so @#%$ long, now it wants to give me hell. It stopped me from burning down Alexander Miyagawa's house.. Now, this. 'Shut up..' 'Cianan! You're just a chicken shit.. You're afraid to stand up to us!' Several of them screamed. I back pedaled, and turned, spinning around, trying to find a way out of the ring, but all sides were surrounded. 'You're worthless!' a shriek above all others is heard, the voice is familiar. Mother? 'You never think of anyone but yourself! What about the family that you left behind, to go to Japan? What about your father, no longer able to work! What about me? Forcing me to work to support the family, because you didn't want to! What kind of a son are you? You run away! You leave us!' she shrieked, only to be over run by another voice. 'Boy, you think you should stop runnin' away from your problems. But, you biggest problem is right here. I ain't gonna take this runnin' shit from ya, you sack of shit. GET OVER HERE!' A voice boomed over everything else. His father. What the hell? Why was that old vegetable here?! He was a lost cause, a mentally broken man. This had to be hell. My father was a horrible figure, a man that would strike fear into my heart. The physical and Emotional abuse that I suffered from my father, and mother. Scars that would never heal. They took all control away from me, they made me feel like an animal. Always berating, I was never good enough. Never, no matter what I did. I backed into a corner of the ring, trying to stay away from this monstrosity of a man. A child beater, a violent, drunken, creature filled with pure animosity. The hands of the others grabbed onto my arms. 'Let go!! Don't please!! Let me go!!' 'You're not as good as you were Cianan.' The heavily German accent came, a familiar voice, known as Jack Panzer. 'You were never good, bitch.' Blayze screeched out, 'You're ass is gonna be grass!' 'You're just a lowlife from CCW. A nothing, a nobody.' Alexander Miyagawa's voice sneered. 'It's not true!! I worked hard to get where I was!' Jesse Ramey spoke again, 'No, you just took advantage of your situations! You're not good..' 'Just a little weasel.. Searching for his next meal.' Vennman Pendragon laughed. 'It's not true.. I was.. I wa..' 'YOU'RE LIVING IN THE PAST!! THIS IS NOW!!!' His father bellowed in his face. 'SHUT UP!!' I shrieked, as I predicted earlier it came out raspy. 'You're lucky.. That we don't kill you Cianan.' The soft voice of Devyl came into my ear. 'Get away... GET AWAY!!' I tried to pull my arms free, but there were too many grasping hands, drawing me over the top of the rope, drawing me into their surging masses. Their hands waiting to rip me apart. 'You're going to be beaten Cianan.. I'm gong to hold that CCW World Heavyweight Title.' Panzer boasted once more, with his thick German accent, as he latched onto my arm, and continued to pull. I felt like I was going to be ripped into two, as my hand latched onto the top rope, saving myself. But for how long? I could feel my fingers slipping, being pulled one by one away from the ropes. 'You don't deserve to win, C-boy,' Jason McAllister's hick accent came into play, once more. 'Where are the Cretin Family now?!' Kensuke laughed, rather spitefully. 'Where's your protection?' They called in unison. 'Where's your safety?!' Again, in unison the cried out. I fell into the surging waves of the ghostly bodies, instantly being over run by the hands. I try to curl up, I try to hide, making myself as small as possible, as the tears stream down my face. There was no protection.. There is no safety. I couldn't hide from them, I couldn't hide from Panzer this Thursday. We were destined to fight.. Two patrons in a the restaurant.. 'What should we do..?' A young Steve asked his companion. 'I don't know..' Jon asked. 'I think.. We should call a doctor. He's been rocking back and forth for.. Four hours now.' They continued to watch the rocking, and crying Cianan, who's in the booth, at a local Lyon's restaurant. His eyes squeezed shut, his arms wrapped around himself, in a protective fashion. 'Hey.. Dude..' Jon slowly reached out, and tapped Cianan's shoulder, which caused the former CCW, to jolt, and start whispering. 'There is no protection.. There is no safety..' 'There is no protection.. There is no safety..' 'There is no protection.. There is no safety..' 'There is no protection.. There is no safety..' The deadly hallucination still playing over and over in his ravaged mind. 'There is no protection.. There is no safety..' There was no stopping this, this is how he thought other viewed him. This was all in his mind. But, often enough, the reality one makes for himself, is more real then the real world. For how one perceives the world, is what shapes them into who they are, and what they are. For Cianan, he grows desperate ... for.. There is no protection from his mind.. And there is no safety from the reality he created for himself. Everything and everyone is a danger, close to shattering a fragile mind. '...There is no protection...' '..There is no safety..' [fin]
