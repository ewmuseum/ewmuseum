Coming To The Rescue

1 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Coming To The Rescue It was me who helped him out! I was the sole person responsible for him winning his last frickin match, and what do I get? Not even a word of thanks! Some people, eh? Sure, he was said to be a nice guy, a bit cold, but a nice guy neverthelessbut I had yet to see that side of him. Id come to his aid and helped him out and how does he repay my valiant efforts? Not even a worda single word of thanks Id come to his rescue in a way. Id aided him in a time of need, and therefore, logically, I should have made a new ally. But, No! The Gods didnt want it to be like that, did they? Well fuck him! If he needs help again I know it wont be me who comes to the rescue. A rush of wind struck my face as I stepped out of the coach. So this was Japan, eh? Isiy stood beside me and flinched as the cold city air hit her. The bustling street about us was the first thing that I took note of. Id heard it was a busy country, but this busy? Surely not? Well I hope youre pleased with yourself. Isiy murmured as a man pushed past her. Youve managed to bring us into an ants nest. She sighed. I fancied the challenge, my love. Dont blame me for being ambitious. And besides, look about you! Its a great city! Ive heard there are more shops and small businesses in Tokyo than anywhere else in the world! So what? You know youre the only thing that interests me, She gave me a sexy glare. And I suppose if we get to the hotel soon we couldwell, need I really say more? I looked around. That, Isiy, was if we ever did get to the hotel. First, however, we had to battle the street about us. From above it probably looked like the entire city was moving. Japan: Why had I even listened to Sean? Hed said Id love it over here, and what a deal hed give me for my services! Looking around, I began to regret stepping on the plane. No, my love, you neednt. But look around! I began, waving a hand to further demonstrate my point. First we actually have to get there! Isiy laughed, Come on then. The sooner we start the sooner we finish. She looked around, considered the situation, and then decided the best way to go about this would be to push as hard as she could. I watched for a moment until she merged with the other people. Sighing, I followed her into the jungle. Charlie Loc, eh? Or perhaps I should call him Jung? Isiy laughed as I read through the tattered paper in my hand. Sean Williams had managed to deliver the card for my first show directly to the hotel. Upon it the words Jung Loc Vs The Pharaoh were scribed in bold golden text. What do you think, then? I queried. Isiys opinion meant a lot to me. She could give excellent advice when she wanted to and I just hoped shed give me a sensible opinion on my situation in PURE. Well, she started. Youve seen what he can do in PCW, right? I nodded thoughtfully. So, what do you think? Are you impressed, or what? Charlie was a good wrestler. He was a former PCW Cruiserweight Champion, after all. But he was nothing I couldnt take care of. I smiled grimly and turned my eyes back to Isiys. Hes taken care of, my sweet. It was a methodical answer. It had more meaning than the obvious one. Loc was nothing to me, and I knew it. At my first Ikusa Id take out the self proclaimed Vietnamese Viper. So come on, then. Isiy said, gently taking the piece of paper away from my hand. I thought we had some unfinished business to take care of. I smiled smugly, But of course, my love. Switching off the lampshade, the sound of the city beyond the suits walls was all that could be heardfor a brief moment. Hed better beware me! Hed better dread me! If he didnt then one thing was sure... Jung Loc was stupider than he first appeared! My first Ikusa, eh? Hmmsounds like the perfect situation to destroy something Ive always wanted to destroy. You nothing! An interesting choice of words; then again in the squared circle words were the only things that counted for nothing. I advise you to rethink those words, Charlie. It might just be the most sensible thing youve ever done Locmy first opponent in PURE My first victim I grinned to myself. I realized Id changed. This new environmentit brought blood back into my veins! PURE had better fear me. PURE had better prepare for me PURE had better run to the Godsdamn hills because of me! For, you see, I am The Pharaoh! --- I was just a whisper thenbut whispers do grow ---
