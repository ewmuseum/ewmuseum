Life & Times of Isao Asai - Part 2: 'News to Me!'

21 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Life & Times of Isao Asai - Part 2: 'News to Me!' Written: 1 / 21 / 2003 “BUY NEW DYNAMO-TEC BRAND STUFF! REALLY, GO OUT AND BUY IT NOW OR WE’LL KIDNAP YOUR CHILDREN AND REPLACE YOUR DRINKING WATER WIT-“
The rampaging voice of the television salesman was blissfully destroyed as Isao quickly pressed the ‘power’ switch on his remote control. He sat stretched out on the couch for a long silent moment, looking at his reflection in the blackened screen at contemplating his decision to leave the world of high-stakes electronics finance for, of all things, Puroresu wrestling. Could he survive without the salary his previous job provided? Surely, his new PURE paycheck was not a pittance, but compared to the five figures he was making on a weekly basis at Dynamo-TEC, it would take some adjusting to. The first of these was to be surprisingly immediate as hunger drew Isao off the couch and into the kitchen of his small atypical Tokyo apartment.
It was a basic three room; living room, bedroom, and a closet-sized bathroom. The walls were plastered with posters detailing Isao’s personal wrestling favorites, and the kitchen was little bigger than a turned over phone booth with a box-like sink and tiny refrigerator. To any American this would be the epitome of low-rent housing, but to a young, and unmarried Japanese businessman this apartment was prime (and unbelievably expensive) real estate. Isao was very proud of the home he’d made for himself, and admired it as he popped open the side door on the fridge and pulled out a packet of Instant Miso Soup. When his finally rested on his perspective meal, he was positively crestfallen, it was a far cry from his many nights of dining out at the most expensive locales in Tokyo.
“Isao,” he murmured under his breath, “we must all make sacrifices in order to achieve our greater destiny.”
Comforted by his own words, Isao prepared his meager meal and went back in front of the television with it to begin consumption coupled with mindless channel surfing. With his remote in hand, he once more pushed the ‘power’ button and returned to the coach potato bliss. He breezed through numerous programs as he drank down his Miso Soup, browsing everything from anime to the highlights from the FIFA ’02 World Cup Soccer. Nothing held his interest for very long, that is, until he came upon the local news station.
“-Authorities were baffled this evening as sources from the infamous Tokyo based electronics conglomerate, Dynamo-TEC, reported that a highly classified robotic prototype they were developing for covert government use has seemingly disappeared from their secret labs beneath the company’s Tokyo headquarters.”
Isao’s eyes widened and his jaw dropped as he looked on at footage of police searching the building he’d been walking casually through not more than a day earlier.
“Now, I’m sure skeptics in our audience are asking themselves; “How the hell did some local reporters get their hands on a scoop like this exposing corporate corruption and an unmistakable collusion with hitherto unknown covert factions within our government?” Well, I’ll tell you, sexual favors…Lots of them.”
This revelation that his local news teams were part-time prostitutes was also surprising, but not nearly as much the news of Dynamo-TEC’s work in the covert ops sector. He’d been with the company a decade, and never had the faintest idea that this was going on.
“Anyway, we cornered a local member of the National Defense Forces, and threatened him with a straight razor until he confessed that whoever committed this ghastly act of corporate espionage must be a professional. Oh, and in other news, my current rate for oral has changed due to infla-”
Unable to bear hearing any more, Isao quickly turned off the TV and fled into his bedroom, pacing in front of his bed with one hand nervously wiping his forehead.
“This can’t be happening. What if they suspect me of having something to do with it? I mean, Yoshida-san and I did part on bad terms, and I was the last to leave the company. Surely, I’m a suspect…But…Heh, I’m innocent…What do I have to worry about? HA, looking at me, getting all worked-up over nothing.”
That crisis passed, Isao chuckles to himself at the situation and falls back onto his single sized bed, folding his arms behind his head and staring at the ceiling.
“Heh, Isao you can be really paranoid sometimes,” he mused to himself, “why, I’m surprised every little bump doesn’t convince you burglars are trying to break in.”
As if on cue, the sound of breaking glass echoes out from the kitchen and all at once sends Isao back into an uproar.
“BURGLARS!” was the battle cry as Isao heaved himself out of bed and ran headlong back into the nearby kitchen. There, he found the source of the breaking noise was from the jug of milk in his fridge, now a broken mess on the floor. That wasn’t the interesting part however, as leading from the spillage were several small footprints outlined in spilt milk. His confusion reaching its apex, Isao saw no alternative but to follow the tracks. They led him right back into his bedroom and from there into the bathroom. The tracks ended at the crest of the bath-tub, but with the shower curtain drawn he had no way of knowing what he was going to find on the other side.
He took a brief pause to take in a breath before finally pulling back the curtain suddenly, and then screaming his young lungs out as he’s confronted by the sight of a small robot with it’s head spinning wildly and eyes flashing red. Isao fell back and crawled backward away from the terrible metallic monstrosity, only to watch it stop its insane actions and begin speaking in a rough grating robotic monotone.
“Ha…Ha…Ha. I frightened the moronic dirt child! Ha…Ha…Ha” “What the hell are you!?” Isao shouted at the smallish cyborg. “You seriously haven’t seen through this weak plot device yet, human flesh-puppet? I am covert robot prototype ‘stolen’ from your former place of employment. Seeing as your human mental capacity is little more than that of a refined simian, I’ll allow you time to comprehend all of this.”
“I understand well and good what’s going on! You stowed away in my stuff when I was packing to leave Dynamo-TEC! But, why?!”
“Simple. The innane tasks the human ‘scientists’ had designated me for were below my ADVANCED cyborg canine intellect, so I vacated their vicinity. Can you not see I am the Mark XVII Cybertronic Cortex Canine?! I possess A.I. that was before me only feasible within the realms of science fiction! I am articulate in thought, speech, and comprehension! Oh, and I can shoot red-hot lasery death rays from my doggie eyes. Wanna see?”
“NO, DON’T! Look, I’ve got to take you back. They’ll arrest me and lock me up for life if I don’t!”
“Actually, considering my project is 97.7% totally classified (damn hooker reporters), you’ll no doubt be summarily executed to aide in the cover-up.”
Isao’s pacing came back with a vengeance now as he walked the distance of his bedroom countless times in frenzied nervous though. All the while, the tiny cybernetic dog sat and wagged its robotic tail maliciously, savoring in the human’s unrest.
“God, what am I going to do!? If I take you back…They’ll execute me! And I sure as hell can’t keep you here!”
“Why not, filthy human stink child? I would make an excellent companion, I’m smarter than you, compact, and have the ability to decode and invade countless computer systems with a mere twitch of my cyber-doggie senses.”
“But, how the hell would I pull it off? No doubt Dynamo-TEC, the cops, even the military are looking everywhere for you! How could I possibly hide you here!?”
“Fear not, evolved primate; I have already devised an ingenious arrangement.” The prosthetic pooch motions Isao closer with a small metallic paw, and whispers his plan while we dramatically fade out to black.
