Calender Series: December 2001 Written: The Past... “Keep those feet up!” The sound of a whistle. “Up!” Whistle. “Up!” Whistle. “C’mon, I don’t want to be here all day doing this shit!” Whistle, whistle, whistle.
“Pitiful,” the most muscular man in the room said, shaking his head. Ugh, this is so tiring. I can’t believe I’m going through with this. In high school up-downs seemed so… annoying, now they’re just hell.
“186! C’mon, pick it up!” Whistle. “Less than twenty to go!” Whistle, whistle, whistle, whistle. “Get those feet up!”
I gave high knees. Not that I can feel my legs, but whatever. “Y’know, I’ve seen you guys progress for what? Three weeks now? And I’ve got to say that you’re doing quite well.” He blew his whistle again.
Down I went and then back up again. “But if you want to Make It in this business you have got to Work At It! Hard!” Whistle, whistle.
Down to up, down to up. “You’ve got to put everything on the line out here! Everything!” Whistle. “All right, that’s 195. Five more up-downs to go guys, let’s make them hardcore.” Whistle, whistle, whistle, whistle. “Stay down for this last one.” Whistle. “On your fists.”
Push ups. Great. “One… two… three… four… five…” he continued onto thirty. “All right. Let’s move into the ring guys.”
We all got up. There were twenty-six of us. Five guys in the class were Oliver, Pat, Jimmy, Roland, and myself. We had made it these couple of weeks. Actually, the class had put up well entirely. After we had all decided to join this academy I looked into it and researched wrestling training a bit. In classes of thirty usually one or two people graduate, if any. So far two guys had left in these two weeks and five days.
So now they wanted us around the ring. So we did. What happens if you don’t do more than what the trainers say is not something anyone wants to find out. There are three of them.
“Watson! Klinoff! Get in the ring!” Oliver and Alex Klinoff, a big Russian guy, got in the ring. Ol isn’t that big, but he’s agile and strong. No one in the class is as agile as I am though.
“Go through a match guys. What are you waiting for? Someone to give you a kiss good luck?”
“Well,” Oliver joked. “Get in there and kiss him Ford.” Jimmy shakes his head, smiles, and backs away. “Sorry Watson, no one loves you. Now wrestle you two carps!” The two men lock up. Ol hits a double leg takedown, but is stopped by a double axe handle club from Klinoff. Klinoff lifts him to his feet and hooks his head. He then hits him with a snap suplex.
“Are you kidding me? What the hell are you doing hitting a snap suplex? You’re fucking two-hundred and seventy pounds for Christ’s sake!” He mutters to himself: “Idiots.”
Klinoff whips Oliver to the ropes and gets him up in a body slam. Oliver takes it poorly. These guys need work, it’s obvious.
Coach shakes his head in disappointment. Klinoff whips OW back into the ropes and puts his head down to signal a back body drop. He does it way too soon and then back body drops Oliver, but loses his balance and falls with him, falling directly on their heads. Oliver gets knocked out cold. Klinoff got a concussion from the fall he took. Klinoff was thrown out of the academy immediately and Oliver was in a coma for four days. He never touched a wrestling ring ever again.
And then there were four…
