Who Am I?

5 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Who Am I? My name is Agent X. It's been so long since I heard my real name that even I have begun to forget it. It's no huge problem forgetting my name, as it is all part of my job. Well to call it a 'job' is a bit of an understatement it is more like a lifestyle now. Everywhere I go my job is always on my mind, there is no escaping it, I am trapped in this 'job' for the rest of my life. You see if you haven't already guessed I am a secret agent, I have been for over 10 years. Now before you all go jumping to conclusions, let me set a few things straight. I am not part of the secret service, I do not work for the government, and I am not James Bond. I am part of an elite unit known as the Patriots. We are unknown to the public because of the simple reason that if we were to become known then chaos would surely ensue. Now before you go pointing it out, I realise that I am telling you of the Patriots existence now, but I have my reasons, reasons that will become clear later on. Before I tell you of my reasons, let me tell you a little about the Patriots. Now I am not going to tell you everything about the Patriots, because as I said before chaos would ensue if I did. Therefore I am going to tell you the sort of information that will not tell you everything but will leave you satisfied. The Patriots have been around since the birth of democracy. Whilst Washington and Jefferson were signing the declaration of independence, in the next room, behind locked doors the true political future of America was being decided. You see the Patriots are the true rulers of this country, they decided what is revealed to the public and what is not. For example, you think this country is governed 'democratically,' but the word 'democracy' is nothing but the wool being pulled over your eyes. It is the Patriots who decide whether or not an individual succeeds in life. You think you, the people, decide who becomes the President? Well if you do then you are wrong, the Patriots are the ones who decide who becomes President of the United States. The whole 'Presidential Race' is just a show to keep the public happy. The President himself is just a puppet, a pawn, which is controlled by the Patriots. All this may be hard to take in, but it is the truth. Me telling you this puts my own life in danger, but I no longer care about my own existence, because quite simply I have had enough. I have worked for the Patriots for over 10 years now , 10 years of my life I have wasted working for a company that no one knows about. When I was first assigned to the Patriots, I had a normal life, I had friends, I had a name, and I had my freedom. I had no idea what I was letting myself in for. The things I have seen over the past 10 years would make even the most twisted minds sick. You see the crimes you hear about on the news, such as homicides and small terrorists attacks, are nothing compared to the things that I have had to deal with. You think the threat of Nuclear War has ended? Well you couldn't be further from the truth. Everyday I have to face the nuclear threat, everyday I come face to face with maniacs who above all else desire power. I have seen into these maniacs minds, I have seen what makes them tick, they don't think like normal human beings, it is like sometime in their lives their minds became warped. No longer do they think about meeting up with their friends at the cinema, but they think of world domination, they think of pain and suffering. Now you just think what these sorts of people would look like, what kind of look they would have in their eyes. The truth is they look like regular people, just like you and me, but if you stopped one of them in the street and stared into their eyes, then you would know, then you would see the difference. I have stared into such eyes, and they have stared right back. Those people know no fear, they do not care whether they die or live, because they will do anything it takes to succeed. Even though I despise these people, part of me respects their determination, their will to succeed. Thinking about it, I am more like them than any of my fellow agents. I share the same will to succeed, I share the same determination. However there will always be 1 reason why I will never ever be like them... There I crouched, my 9mm Eagle Revolver in my hand, bullets whizzing past my ears. This was it the final showdown, after 2 years of chasing him down we finally had Haseem Black trapped. Now before I continue I bet you are all wondering what the hell I am talking about? Well it all started when I was given 'Project Venom.' It was 2 years ago when the boss called me and Agent K into his office. I had been working alongside K for a year and a half at this point, we had got to know each other pretty well, and were quite the team. Anyway we entered the boss' office already knowing what he had in store for us. We hadn't had a case for a few months now and we were ready for a real challenge. The lighting in the boss' office was very dim, for some reason the boss didn't like bright light, he felt more mysterious in the dark. However no matter what the lighting the office still looked very lush and expensive, with it's oak wood panelling floor and walls and leather couches. The boss was sat behind his desk looking through a few papers, he was getting quite old now, with his grey hairs beginning to show. He also was getting quite chubby, no longer was he the slim man of his youth. He continued to shuffle through the papers, until K who has always been impatient coughed to signal our presence. The boss looked up from his papers, and smiled. 'Ah gentlemen, good to see you. Please sit down.' 'So what have you got for us, boss?' K asked as we sat down on the 2 leather seats in front of the desk. 'Patients K, all in good time. First of all I want to congratulate you on finally closing the Census case.' The boss said with a tone of pride in his voice. He knew we were the best the Patriots had to offer, and in a sense so did we. 'Thanks boss, it's good to be appreciated from time to time.' I said with a tone of sarcasm in my voice. 'Very funny, X. Now I have a case for you 2, and let me tell you this case is right down your alley.' The boss said with a smirk on his face 'Not another stakeout boss. We have had enough of watching some drug dealer's wife go back and forth from Burger King.' K said, jumping to conclusions, as usual. 'No, K it is not another stakeout.' The boss said smiling before he continued. 'This case is called 'Project Venom.' The boss paused for a moment as he moved over to the filing cabinet and pulled out a very chunky file, packed with papers and photographs. He threw it on the desk causing it to make a thud noise. I looked at it through my sunglasses, not believing my eyes. I took off my sunglasses thinking that it was just the light deceiving me, but no it was still as big as it was. I looked at the boss who was looking at both of our expressions. K had gone into a state of shock, as had i. The boss was expecting this reaction as he once again spoke up. 'Gentlemen this is Project Venom, and from your expressions I am guessing you can see how big this case is. We have been going at this case for over 2 years now, and not once have we ever come close to cracking it. We have just recently lost contact with our Delta team, they are presumed dead, that is why we are handing it over to you. You are our only hope, if you can't close this case, then we might as well call it a day.' K looked at me, and I looked at him, we could tell that this case was the case we had been waiting for. K smirked and nodded his head, I couldn't help but smile back. I turned back to the boss who looked concerned. 'Who's the target?' I asked. The boss pulled out a couple of pictures and handed 1 to me and 1 to K. I looked at the picture. Staring back at me from the picture was a 'ordinary' man. Obviously he wasn't ordinary as he was the target of this case, he had to have something wrong with him, but if you were to meet this guy on the street, you would just think of him as another member of the public. The more I studied the picture the more confused I became. I had been in this business for 10 years yet I still could not understand how such ordinary looking people could turn out to be such maniacs. I was snapped out of my train of thought as the boss spoke up again. 'His name is Hassem Black. He is the leader of the terrorist group known as The Sons of Liberty. Now as I said before we have been after this guy for over 2 years now, and we haven't got anywhere. This guy is like a ghost, one moment he is there the next he's gone.' 'So I am guessing our main objective is to take this guy out?' K said again jumping to conclusions. 'That is correct, K. However there is something else I need to tell you.' The boss said with a worried look on his face. I felt my heart sink, whenever the boss said something like that I knew it wasn't going to be good news. I sighed as the boss explained. 'Hassem Black, is in possession of a nuclear warhead.' 'What?!?! How the hell did he get his hands on a nuclear warhead?' K exclaimed, not believing what he was hearing. 'Calm down, K, it isn't as bad as it sounds.' The boss said holding his arms up to try and calm K down. 'It isn't as bad as it sounds? He has a damn nuclear warhead, how can that not be as bad as it sounds?' K screamed as he stood up and started to pace around the office. Even though K was known as a cool character, the mention of a nuclear threat always panicked him. I stood up and moved over to K who looked like he was going to faint at any second. I tried to reassure him. 'K, listen to me, if Hassem knew how to work the nuclear warhead don't you think he would have already armed it? Now come on get a hold of yourself, we've got a job to do.' K looked at me and nodded, he understood he was over reacting. I patted him on the shoulder to reassure him that everything was going to be okay. However inside I was a bucket of nerves. I knew the danger of this case, but if I didn't stay strong then the case would have been over before it even got started. We sat back down the boss looking even more worried. 'So where do we start?' I asked in attempt to break the awkward silence. 'You are to meet our contact at the warehouse near the docks. Now be very careful, Hassem has got spies everywhere, and our contact is really sticking his neck on the line here.' The boss said making sure we heard every word. 'Don't worry boss we will be fine,' I said not just trying to reassure him but myself as well. I moved over to the door with K just behind me. I opened door and just before we stepped out the boss shouted after us. 'Oh and guys, watch each other's backs out there.' 'We will boss.' I said as I stepped through the door followed by K. As the door shut so began our 2-year struggle. Over those 2 years we saw the death of 3 of our so-called 'contacts.' We faced the threat of a nuclear explosion 2 times each time finding out that they were just empty bombshells. Out of these 2 years, how many times did we get within 20 feet of Hassem Black? Not once! Every time we thought we had him, he disappeared, and again we were back to chasing ghosts. It all seemed hopeless until our last contact told us of an abandoned warehouse just outside of the district. We went in guns blazing... So there I crouched with K by my side, both of us holding our guns tight in our hands. The bullets kept on coming, one nearly taking part of my ear off. I looked over to K, who was breathing frantically, I could see by the look in his eyes that he was ready to finish this, as was i. 'You ready, K?' I asked already knowing the answer. 'You bet I am, X' K said with a huge smile on his face. 'Okay, Hassem is in the back room with 2 guards, guarding the warhead. First we eliminate these guys, then we go after Hassem, got it?' 'Got it' 'After three, 1...2..' '3!!' 'No K wait!!' But it was too late, K had jump the gun and was up and firing away. It was only a matter of seconds before a bullet went straight through his chest, blood spurting on impact. K fell to the ground grasping at his chest. I quickly pulled him to cover, the blood now spurting out at an alarming rate. He looked up at me, his eyes frantic, the life in them fading. As he spoke silence seemed to engulf the warehouse. 'I'm sorry X' That was it. His last words on this earth were an apology to me. At the time I didn't think it over, something more powerful had taken control, an emotion I had not felt for a long time, rage! I quickly picked up K's gun, and rose to my feet not caring whether I live or die.... Bang! 1 dead! Bang! 2 dead! Bang! 3 dead! Bang! 4 dead! I roared in anger as the final bullet took the life of the final terrorists. I only took a second to look over the carnage I had caused before moving on into the room where Hassem Black waited. I kicked down the door expecting to be greeted by gunfire, instead I was greeted by nothing but silence. In the middle of the room lay the nuclear warhead, untouched, and unarmed. I fell to my knees and dropped the guns to the floor. I looked at my hands that had been covered in K's blood, a solitary tear rolled down my cheek, it all seemed such a waste. A year later... I was sat back in the boss' office, he said he wanted to talk about the progress of 'Project Venom.' Ever since the death of K I hadn't got anywhere with the case, in fact I had lost complete interest in the case, I had given up chasing Hassem Black, even thinking that Hassem didn't exist. The boss walked into his office finishing off his lunch. 'Ah, X glad you could make it?' The boss said with a chunk of food in his mouth. 'No problem boss. Sorry to have interrupted your lunch.' I said not really meaning it. 'So how you doing X?' 'How do you think Im doing boss?' I said with spite in my voice. 'Alright X no need to get like that. Now listen I know this is not what you want to hear but I am dropping you from the Project Venom case.' 'What?!?!' I yelled 'I have a reason X.' 'It better be a damn good 1 too.' 'Now just calm down. The reason I am dropping you from the case is because I want you to take up another 1.' I was intrigued. 'What is the case?' 'Wrestling.' 'Wrestling?!?! You better be kidding me?' I said outraged. 'I'm deadly serious, X.' 'But wrestling?!?! What can be so damn dangerous about wrestling? It is just a sport for god sake! And you are just trying to pull me from the Project Venom case, because you think I can't cut it anymore.' I was up now and storming around the office, I couldn't believe what the boss was saying. 'That's not true X, and you know it.' The boss said trying to calm me down as he continued. 'I am pulling you from the Project Venom case because, I believe you have done all you can do in that case' 'Oh that is total bull! You know I am not finished with the case until I bring Hassem Black down.' 'For god sake X, it's over! Let it go, you need to move on. I miss K as much as you do but it's time to move on. Now you are to report to the development federation known as PURE. There you are to uncover the goings on of the Wrestling Tattler Community, your ultimate goal is to be promoted to one of the 'Big 4' and take it from there. All the contracts have been signed all you need to do is to report to PURE. Good luck, X!' 'In all due respect sir, go f**k yourself!' With that I walked out of the door and slammed it behind me. I was angry, in fact no I wasn't angry, I was p***ed off. I had just been pulled from a case which I was so closed to cracking. Was that the real reason I was pulled from the case? Because I was getting too close for comfort. That question raced around my head, as I got in my car and started the engine. I will go to PURE, but as far as the Patriots go, I no longer exist. No longer will I follow their rules, no longer will I do as they tell me! Agent X is who I am, and who I always will be!
