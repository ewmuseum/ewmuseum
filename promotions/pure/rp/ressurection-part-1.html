Ressurection(Part 1)

8 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ressurection(Part 1) ** There is a rumor out that there is a new wrestler coming in to the N.T.I.C.W. and he sure as hell seems confident. The information we have on this wrestler isn't very descriptive and you can't get the right image of he or she with it. The information that we were informed about him or her was that his or her real name is John Jason Norris, so I guess he's a man. His wrestling name is Vito Wynn, he is 6'4', weighs in between 240 and 238 lbs., his setup move is named 'The Total Devastation', and his finishing move is called 'The Direct Catastrophe'. That's about all we know so far about this newly assigned wrestler in the N.T.I.C.W. We will inform you of any information given to us about this man. ** .:: A scene opens up with a man walking in a depleted way through a darkly deem corridor. The man is wearing a loose black short sleeved T-shirt, blue faded out jeans, a gold chain around his neck, black shades a bit below his eyes reaching the bottom of his nose bone, and black leather boots. The man is the seen walking outside of a door of what looks like an apartment building. He takes out a set of car keys and clicks on a red button. You then hear two beeps come from a Honda Accord. ::. .:: The description of this car is that it's black, it has tinted windows on all sides other then the two front side windows and the front windshield. The two rear side windows and the window on the rear end of the car are tinted, it has two lines on each side of the door both about the same size, and about fourteen inch rims. The car seems to be new or has been taken care of very well. The man walks over to the car and opens the door, he then sits down on the seat and closes the door. He puts in the car keys and starts up the engine. ::. .:: He drives out of his parking space and rides down the block as a cellphone tone begins playing inside of the car. The man pulls over in the front of a driveway and opens his cellphone. On the screen of the cellphone it asks, 'Will you receive this message from Mickey Ronald?'. The man clicks the button yes on his cellphone and puts his cellphone up to his ear. ::. 'Hello, what's up Mickey?' asks the man in a pleasant way. 'Nothing much, what about you Vito Wynn?' asks Mickey Ronald in a pleasant way as well. 'Well, you know. Same old, same old, nothing changes in my life. I got to go to court for murder.' answers Vito Wynn. 'No, I don't know about that court business. What's up with that?' asks Mickey Ronald. 'Look, the F.B.I. is convicting me of killing a man. But I swear it isn't true, I am going to court for it right now and I don't know what to expect. Anyway, why did you call?' questions Vito Wynn. 'I wanted to ask if you would like to come with me to go to some clubs and get a few girls you know. But, you have to go to court so forget it.' comments Mickey Ronald. 'Well, you are correct about me not going but anyway, why would you want to go to a club right now, it's only five o'clock in the afternoon you know?' interrogates Vito Wynn. 'I don't know, I'm just bored. So whatever, peace out.' replies Mickey Ronald. 'Yeah, peace.' says Vito Wynn. .:: Vito Wynn shuts the top of his cellphone and puts it in his pocket. He then pulls out of the drive way and continues to drive down the street. After a few blocks he stops in front of a house where a man with a black suit on, leather shoes, and a gold earring on his left ear is waiting. The man stands up, grabs a suitcase, and runs down the stoop. He goes to Vito Wynn's car, opens the door, and sits inside of the car. ::. 'Hey, you ready for the trial?' asks the man. 'Sure I am ready, Randy.' answers Vito Wynn. .:: Vito Wynn rides away with Randy in his car heading for the court house as the scene slowly fades to black. ::.
