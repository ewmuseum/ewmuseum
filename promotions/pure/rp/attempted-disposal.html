Attempted Disposal

21 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Attempted Disposal Like every other night...last night I sat in my room all alone. I have actually grown used to it and the emptiness doesn't really phase my anymore. Sure at times here and there I miss the opportunity of conversing with another; but not to the point where I would go out of my way to do so. I had a plan for that evening...a plan that could ruin my life forever. A plan that would end a life...and it wouldn't be mine. I sat there, on the edge of my hotel bed, staring into my mirror pondering the thought of life in prison. Or even a death sentence...which I would've prefered if this whole ordeal went sour. But again, either one of those options would only happen if this didn't turn out the exact way I had planned it. The victim? None other than my very own father. See, killing him would've only amounted to maybe 1/10 of the pain and torment he put me through. I had many motives for this act...and I felt they would've all been justified. Firstly, he was never there, not once, in my life. Which meant I had to fend for myself through out my existence. Secondly, he abused me as a child. Many a time he would come home and assault me...both verbally and physically. So many instances I wanted to shout back or shove my fist right down his throat. And lastly, he exiled me from my own family and home. Why? Because I didn't move back to Japan with them in 1995...AND I decided to make a living for myself through hurting people. Well last night was my time...my time to do the assaulting...my God damn time to show him how he fucked up my life! Fifteen minutes earlier I had called him and asked him if he could come over because I needed to speak with him (the hotel was only twenty minutes away from Asahikawa). I was slowly counting the minutes away and on the eighteenth...I rushed out of my room, down the hotel stairs, and into the alley right below my window. I had told him to park in there because he would be right next to the stairs leading up. I jumped in a Toyota Celica, recently stolen, and waited... I was very pleased last night because it was pitch black and there was a cloud mass covering the moon. The hotel lights were broken, giving me an even better location for my attempted disposal of a nuisance. There it was! I saw my father's Nissan pulling into the alley and an evil grin ran across my face. He stopped and stepped out of the car with a dumbfounded look on his face. I could tell he was trying to peer through the winshield to see who was in front of him but he couldn't make me out...maybe it was because I was dressed in all black, a black ski mask, and black gloves. I stepped out of the black Celica and he began backing up in fear of something coming. 'Wh...who are you?' He asked. I thought he was going to piss himself out of fear. I didn't feel like responding to such a question...and I didn't. The only thing I did was walk over to him and strike him down with a club to the side of the neck. It rendered him motionless and unconscious. I laid him on his front seat and walked back over to the Celica. Within the next thirty minutes; I had managed to switch the position of the vehicles so the Nissan was back against the wall, and the Celica was facing in. My father was locked inside the Nissan and I had him right where I wanted him. I turned the key and listened to the engine.Chills ran through my body as if a massive fire had just sparked and I was the caveman that discovered flames. I put a brick on the pedal while the car was still in park and was ready to do something I had been wanting to do for a countless amount of years. I put the vehicle in drive and it sped down the one-hundred and twenty foot long alley right into the Nissan that was housing another human being. I looked on as the two bodies of metal clased together sending pieces flying...raining down from the sky. The crunch was a sickening sound that turned my stomach and wrenched my heart... At this very moment I am still not having second thoughts about what I did because he deserved that...and a hell of a lot more. I didn't stay around long enough to see the outcome. I just hope it did the job...
