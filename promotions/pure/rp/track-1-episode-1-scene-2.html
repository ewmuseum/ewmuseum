Track #1 - Episode #1 - Scene #2

October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Track #1 - Episode #1 - Scene #2 Written: Track #1 -> The Birth of a Dream… In the first scene of this epic story, we saw our two newly introduced individuals cheat a little ‘Sushi Bar’ out of the kind of money that would have made a six year old notorious in his local area. Both Chad and Kamui have dreams of making it to the top of the world… as both Professional Wrestlers… and singers of exclusive J-PoP songs…
Their debut hadn’t gone the best of ways and Chad was depressed about this event… Kamui tried to console his partner, but unfortunately made the situation a little worse for Matthews by talking about an individual called “Shindo Hikaru”… a ‘Go’ player who took two years to master everything that he needed to. Matthews exclaimed that wasn’t the kind of time he had… and soon after this, both our icons had left the confinements of the building…
Where had they gone? Where had they run off too? Yes… I know it’s the same question twice, but do you? Episode #1 – We Gotta Power! Scene #2 – Walking the streets… The second scene happens to open in the busy streets outside this conveniently located ‘Sushi Bar’ that had been filled with cardboard cutouts. Kamui continued to run down the street, not even taking the chance to look behind him and see if anyone were coming. Fukamachi-san wasn’t the greatest of thinkers in the world… and his paranoid nature often left him getting the wrong idea of things. Since Chad had given chase in the last scene… Kamui was convinced that Chad wanted to lay into him with an awesome array of punches and kicks.
“Argh… MAN CHASING ME… GET HIM AWAY… AWAY I SAY… AWAY!!!” Fukamachi screamed, continuing to rune down this street, dodging past people like they were bollards or something of the sort. This is probably the only moment that his martial arts skills would really help him… the evasion aspect anyway. With Fukamachi bringing all of the attention to himself, Matthews was finding it harder and harder to catch up with his friend… who still believed that he was out for blood… and nothing else.
“Fukamachi-San… wait for me!! Wait for me!! Everyone keeps getting in my way God Damn It… hold up… JUST HOLD UP!!!” Matthews screamed hoping that his ally would be able to hear the desperate cries for him to slow down… even stop or something.
Kamui began to wonder about what was happening for a few seconds and began to slow down… eventually stopping and turning around to see Chad running him to up, crashing through bundles of people… sending older members of the community down to the floor. Obviously, he couldn’t help any of the things he was doing… just some bollards weren’t fast enough movers. Stopping as soon as he caught up with the other member of H.O.T, Chad couldn’t help but ask what the problem was, “… Kamui, what was the deal back there man? You just burst outta there like a crazed man… looking as though you were going to force the bill upon me again.”
Fukamachi smiled nervously, slowly stepping away from Chad, trying to keep his mouth closed and just avoid the entire situation. Matthews wasn’t going to back away this time… and with every step that Kamui took… Matthews followed, making sure he kept his tabs here.
“Chad… Chad… Chad… amigo, buddy, compadre… I didn’t run because I wanted to leave you behind with the sorry task of having to pay the bill… again. No, I decided to run… because I needed to stretch my legs a little… you know, get the blood flowing around the joints again… after all, we need to be in like… top physical condition for the battle that’s going to occur this weekend at Ikusa V”, Kamui informed his friend, hoping that this ‘explanation’ of the scenario would allow things to blow over… meaning that both members of the team would be able to get back to the more important matters at hand.
“Dude…”, Chad started, slowly beginning to walk away from the position where Kamui was situated, “… I don’t care about meal as much as the match that’s going to be happening within the next few days or so… we need to work on our vocals and our dance steps beforehand… namely because the Robot Dance that we tried to entertain all of those people with… it didn’t really work out to our favor at all did it?”
Kamui nods his head in agreement, understanding that their debut still wasn’t anything near to the best performances that they’re usually able to give all of their loving fans. Walking down the busy street with him, the two seemed to have calmed down a lot. Chad begins the next conversation.
“You know Kamui… I think we need to look at the bigger picture… maybe even get an idea of what the whole picture would look like. We gotta dream for the highspots of this contest… we gotta power… we gotta prove to everyone that we have something else besides this developing talent to sing and dance… you were right, it may take two years… but I know your level of skill friend. From this day forth… when everyone looks out of their windows and stares down to see Chad Matthews and Kamui Fukamachi… THEY WILL NOT SEE THOSE PEOPLE!!! Instead, they will see I… Shindo Hikaru… and you… the legendary Sai!”
Kamui stands still, his mouth wide-open, his jaw almost touching the floor. He remains still, unable to believe everything that had just found its way out of the mouth that belongs to Chad Matthews. Does this mean that Matthews really is a lot worse than he managed to perform at Ikusa IV? Does this mean that he… is a newbie?! Kamui pressed forward and questioned his team-mate… wondering if their association was worth it.
“Chad… does this mean that… that, you’re one of the worst dancers in the entire world?! Does this mean… you’re not going to be able to do your best unless you learn everything there is to learn from me within the next few days?!”
Matthews stood there… he remained motionless and didn’t do anything that would suggest one answer or the other. Instead, he just continued to walk off into the distance, looking back every so often to see if Kamui was going to follow him down the street.
’Chad… a rookie… a complete rookie? But how… he managed to ‘Robot Dance’ on my level… and I’m pretty good. Maybe there is something that he hasn’t told me yet… ‘, Kamui thought to himself, speaking via his mind.
Chad began to disappear into the background, looking back to see that Fukamachi-san hadn’t even started off behind him. Worried about the next thing that was going to happen, Chad thought to himself, ’What… has he figured out that I’m not worth anything except in that ring? My bio was right… but perhaps he’s able to see through everything… why didn’t I just use Vegita and Trunks instead… oh man..’
The scene freezes as Chad hits himself on the forehead with the palm of his head, the picture turning to an illustration before vanishing into the darkness of the night. Will Fukamachi be able to find out that Matthews really isn’t anything… will they be able to really prepare for their debut contest within the walls of PURE?
There is only one way that you’re going to be able to find the answers to any of these questions… TUNE IN… NEXT TIME!!
