Chapters... Written: -------- Congratulations, Nu Era. You have been selected as an offering. Yes, as far as the altar that is Trinity Tag Team Division is concerned, you are PURE's sacrificial lamb. It is quite an honor, do not take this the wrong way.
The Clan has dispatched countless gaijin, you are just the first to face off against the Clan in a sanctioned match. We have taken the liberty of writing up a list of last-minute things you may want to do.
1) Write out a will. You may want to include your family and friends. Leave them your estate (if you even have one) and whatever personal belongings you require. Oh, but you still owe #12 a total of 2 million Yen for the solicitation of his escort.
2) Go to the bathroom before the match. It is a well-known fact that when a human goes through severe trauma that involves loss of their muscle control, they pass all unpassed material. In words you would use, you shit yourself. Our ninjas would rather not deal with that, and it would disrespect the ring for you to relieve yourself in the middle of that sacred ground. Make sure you are 'empty' before coming out.
3) Bend over. No explanation yet, but this is going somewhere. 4) Place your head between your legs. Almost there. 5) Utilizing a puckering motion of the lips, kiss your ass goodbye. We believe that this is self-explanatory. If you need a diagram, then we are truly doing the world a favor by eliminating you.
6) Schedule all press-conferences and autograph sessions before the match. As you shall likely be in a hospital clinging to life shortly after your match with the Clan, we recommend that you not have anything planned.
7) Do everything you ever wanted to do in life. Now is the time. You'll get no second chances. 8) Pray. Whatever you pray to, do that. A clear conscience is better when going into any combat with the Clan. Your creator will be glad to know that you wanted to make peace with him before passing on.
9) Do not forfeit. You dishonor yourselves and us by asking to call the match off. This will happen... 10) Seek new employment. Just in case you don't die, you shall likely need to find another job. We hope this helps.
