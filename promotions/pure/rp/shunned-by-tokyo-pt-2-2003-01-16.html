Shunned by Tokyo Pt. 2

Shunned by Tokyo Pt. 2 Written: 1/16/03 The scene opens up into a local park in Tokyo, Japan. Basketball is being played, around the lower end is a group of teenagers in a circle moving their heads to a beat, and little babies looking for their mothers. Erik Blake appears on the other side of the park, towards the group of teenagers, and listens in.
Teenager #1- Yo, yo, I'm sick with this you whack rappers can't even hang with this, i'll tie you to a cross and give you a death kiss, if you try to step up leave ya confidence in at home cause I'ma get you to messed up like you was a brain wit no cup...
Teenager #2- you know what's up, when I step in the cypha circle it's over, roll you over, get you slobbin' like an arabian dog named rover, you better be holdin' a clover cause I'm causin' mass destruction go back home boy cause ya rhymes is in need of construction!
Whole Circle- OOOHHHHHH!!! The entire circle of teenagers applaud and give compliments to the 2 young emcees as another teen makes a beat with his mouth...and Erik Blake walks over.
Erik Blake- Hey, guys. Mind if I rap with you? Teenager #1- Um...hold on. The first teenager huddles in the middle of the small circle and motions everyone else to huddle with him.
Teenager #1- Yo, this black guy wants to rap with us? should we annihlate him? Teenager #2- I don't know dude, black guys are known to rap real good. Teenager #3- Think of it this way...at least we can elevate. You know get better. Teenager #1- Bah, he's probably weak. I'll battle him by myself then... The small huddle disperses around Blake as he looks slightly nervous as the first Teenager comes his way.
Teenager #1- Since these two guys are such pussies...me and you can battle. Erik Blake- Cool...shall I go first? Teenager #1- Sure...be my guest. The second teenager shakes his head and starts to beat-box as Blake moves his head from side to side.
Erik Blake- Pretty good. Teenager #1- Whenever you are ready. Erik Blake- Yo, I'm not Japanese or Chinese you can classify me as Asian, me and my karate brothers we be shittin' on the cajuns, my punches are like my uncle Bruce Lee, they are always blazin', and like my cousin Jet Li they leave you still dazin', you call me a chink twice is what you better think before my karate punches tear you apart like a fag in pink....OOOOHHHHH!!!
Blake starts to smile at the teenagers as they are caught in a stare. Blake looks at them confusely as teenagers 2 and 3 walk away. Teenager #1 starts to walk away but stops and looks at Blake.
Teenager #1- Dude, your black. The teenager walks away. Erik Blake- Stupid teenagers. Not knowing one of their own brothers... Blake shakes his head and walks off...as the scene fades.
