Freestyle from the Heart (Part 4 in the Tokyo Pop tour) Written: 10/8/02 ***************Listen to the music, for the music guides the soul. **************** If you put all the great musicians word of our time together you make a force that’s unstoppable. DJ Nygma is unstoppable, for he can put together greatness and add his own touch. The power to entertain is the power to make people happy. And isn’t that what it’s all about.
The scene goes to Fuji where it’s a rainy night and DJ Nygma is putting it all out to make the people happy.
What it was, was just a dream But I couldn’t see That the dream was so close to reality I never knew that it would start like this I think of it all as a beautiful kiss I give my recognition Out like old tradition I’m a man up on a mission To follow my long distance vision And in the end it’ll be me as the one No longer the young child that had to run It didn’t get here for fun and games I didn’t work here for fortune and fame All I ever wanted was to be on top From the moment I started I haven’t stopped You may be thinking what’s so special up there Think about it, being #1 is rare Only a few can do it Only two can make it And only one can take it So if you grab that prize Never let it go Let the rhythm flow Defend it blow after blow Nothing can change what you feel inside In my heart, I’ve already died No emotions is what I feel But I only wish my eyes could heal And kneel Is what I do when I pray for you That you live long and stay strong forever too Don’t take life if yours isn’t already done If you pick up the gun The population goes down by one So why isn’t peace the number one element No easy settlement Wishing you were ahead of him So when the dust clears it’s just you two No more crew Your faces are blue With horror Cause in your eyes all you see is hate You know your faith You can relate To what’s going on In there mind Cause right after you’ll find That if you look behind You’ll see all along you’ve been blind. So what I’m trying to say Is play for the win Don’t give in. Don’t treat it like the usual thing. Cause it isn’t WORD! On Friday Night, I WILL BE THE ONE!
