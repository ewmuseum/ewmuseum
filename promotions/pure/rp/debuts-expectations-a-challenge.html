Debuts, expectations, a challenge

18 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Debuts, expectations, a challenge - Gekito has passed. The event was both good and bad for Pure wrestler Zander McCain. The good he made a semi successful debut by giving a good showing in the over the top rope battle royal he participated in. He showed flashes of the various wrestling skills he possess. McCain even helped to eliminate the biggest challenger in the match, Deacon, with a double dropkick along with another competitor in the match. Those were a few of the good things that happened for McCain.- - However there was a fair share of bad things that happened to him as well, most of which, that were basically mistakes on his own part. The attempted alliance with Deacon backfired miserably. He got eliminated and thus not only lost his debut but more importantly lost the honor of winning the Gekito Cup all in the same night. Then again...- - McCain did take a heck of beating from Deacon and some others. He did show he not only has the skills offensively to win matches, but he can certainly take one heck of a beating and keep on coming. He was slammed, bashed, flipped, thrown, and basically treated like a old rag doll at times...But none the less he kept getting up and kept right on coming. If nothing else this match was certainly a learning experience and a great test of his will, determination, skills, and most importantly his HEART, PRIDE, and DESIRE to be a great competitor. McCain may have a banged up and bruised body, but his spirits are with out a doubt soaring.- ::: The out shot opens the inside of the small room where Zander McCain lives here in Chiba Japan. The room is furnished only with a small table, a couple of fur rugs, and a tiny bed witch lays on the floor with no box springs or railings. Near the front sliding door rests his shoes and the shoes of his guest Sandy Wong. As the camera shot panes out to a full view of the room, which isnt much more, we see both Sandy and McCain sitting, on pillow type seats, in the middle of the small room. McCain has a black eye and a bruised cheek, yet is still smiling right at the beautiful Miss Wong. McCain is in a pair of sleeping pants and a rope. Wong is wearing a white silk shirt and some dress casual black slacks. She smiles back at McCain, who hands her a cup of hot tea, as she begins to speak with him. ::: Wong: Well Mr. McCain you seem to be in very high spirits despite your loss at Gekito a couple of nights ago. Could you tell me why you are in such a great mood? ::: McCain takes a sip of his hot tea which has a little steam coming off of it. He places the cup down on the small table next to them and smiles as he crosses his legs in front of him. ::: McCain: Well, Miss Wong, I cant help but be in a great mood. Yes I did in fact lose in my debut match but I came away with some questions answered and a heck of a great amount of experience from the match. I feel pretty good since I was able to showcase a small amount of my wrestling skills. Believe me I have plenty to showcase to the great fans over here in Japan. Wong: You did indeed show the fans a small amount of your high flying abilities, and you helped to make the match a memorable one for the sum 650 fans in attendance. Other than the loss at the end, what was the toughest thing about this match for you personally? McCain:  Well to be honest with you I hadnt fully prepared myself for the physical beating that I had to endure. I made the mistake of not preparing for a monster of a man like Deacon and as such I paid for that lack of preparation. All the men in the ring the other night were phenomenal athletes but that Deacon was just flat a mountain of muscle. He was able to throw me around like I was nothing more than a stuffed animal on some little girls bed. I would love to get another shot at Deacon in a one on one setting and see what I might accomplish. ::: Wong gives McCain a bit of a strange look. In fact it is as though Miss Wong thinks McCain is completely nuts. McCain gives her a bit of a confused look in return. ::: Wong: Why in the world would you want to face that man again? Especially in a one on one setting, you could get your butt kicked! McCain: Well frankly anyone can have their butt kicked on any day of the week. I never worry to much about those type of things, seeing as how the bumps and beatings are just part of the business. In fact I relish the chance to take a good beating, that way I can prove I have plenty of mental and physical toughness. Deacon is a great example of a guy who I would be able to test those attributes against. Wong: Other than a match against Deacon, are there any other immediate goals you wish to pursue here in Pure? McCain (Confident look on his face): Of course. I have plenty of short term and long term goals I hope to accomplish during my tour here in Japan. For one, like I said before, I would love to have a match with Deacon. Beyond that I hope to prove my worth and earn a shot at the honorable Junior Heavyweight Title. One day before my tour here is done maybe I can prove myself worthy of holding the highly acclaimed and honorable World Title. But the most important thing to me, while I am over here in Japan, is prove my worth to the fans. The fans are what this sport is and should be all about. To prove that I am some one to be given respect and honor toward would be something I would be most proud of. ::: Both Wong and McCain lift their cups and each take a couple more sips each. Finally Wong sets her glass back down and folds her hands neatly in her lap as she begins to once again speak. ::: Wong: Pride and honor are two attributes that the Japanese culture really loves to get behind. There have been plenty of new comers who come in here saying all these same things and then after a little taste of success they lose focus. They just become plain arrogant and forget all about the fans and the honor that Japanese wrestling instills in a wrestler. How do you intend to keep from having that same fate fall upon you? McCain: To be honest I have long ago lost the arrogant...I am better than everyone else... Attitude that some guys have. I have been there and that is no longer the type of person I am or want to be. I am over here in Japan to bring honor to the sport, the fans, and myself. Also a tour like this is a very valuable learning experience, and gives me a great chance, to improve my wrestling skills in the Japanese style. I have always been a huge fan of the Japanese style and I try to implore plenty of that into my wrestling style. Wong: Do you ever get scared that you may be seriously injured or one day not be able to walk again? I mean, over here in Japan especially, this sport is very demanding and can be very dangerous.  McCain (Dead serious look): You dont have to be afraid to get your ass kicked Miss Wong. The fact is that whether you fear injuries or not, they can and will happen. I chose the more positive approach and tend not to dwell on the bad things. I focus solely on the good side and all the great things I can give back to the communities, not only in the United States but most certainly here in Japan, in ways of building them better schools, libraries, and museums. Part of the reason I got into this business is so I could take a great portion of the money I make wrestling and from my family business back home and give back to the less fortunate out there. I have been blessed thousands of times over and I feel like I should give back in as many ways as possible to those who need the help more than I do. Wong: Those are very noble causes Mr. McCain, and I wish you all the luck in those ventures. One last question before I get going; Do you know, when and against who, your next match is? McCain: I havent been informed of my next assignment, but I do know it will not be at the Gekito event next week. Hopefully that means I will be able to show my skills on the more highly re-guarded Saturday night event. We will see what happens in a couple of days. Thank you for stopping by Miss Wong and do so again any time you like. Wong: Thank you and I will do that. :::Miss Wong gets up as does McCain. She bows to McCain out of respect and he then leads here the short walk over to the door way, where she slips her shoes on before walking out the door to the hall outside of the room. The camera follows McCain, as he walks back over to the spot where he was sitting moments before. He sits back down again, crosses his legs, folds his arms across his chest and begins to close his eyes. As his eyes close the camera zooms in on them, and when they shut completely the scene fades out to black. :::
