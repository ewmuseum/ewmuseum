Negro Please...

11 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Negro Please... (Excerpted from Jordan X Shabazzs online daily memoirs.) Now, I'll be the first to say that I have no problems with homosexuals... But it's kind of unnerving when them guys start to hit on you and shit like that... Right? Well, professional wrestling tends to attract stalkers and homosexuals alike. Some people are in this for the money, some are in this for the love of the sport, and some are in this for the love of the competition.... But what is Ali Khadafi in this for? I'm kind of worried now about stepping into the ring with him. The more I think about it, the more it becomes clear to me, and this kid isnt right in the head. Now, I'm a big fan of Oreos. I think they're great. From the chocolate covering to the white insides, it's so delicious.... But when people turn Oreos into an object of sex, that kind of worries me... Especially when it's a black guy. Most black guys got mad respect for what I'm doing, and why wouldn't they? I'm using my status as a huge PURE/PCW superstar by helping the people that made me; I grew up in Toronto, Canada for the most part. Living with a white family. I had a good life, but when I think about it, and I look back to where Andrew [Drew Hostyle] and my Mom were living, I feel bad. I got the good life, money, food; the area I lived in was safe.... While they got violence, drugs, robberies, and they lived in an area where you would get jumped for looking at certain people the wrong way. That shit isnt fair. But now, I'm trying to repay 'da hood'. Now that I'm suing People Magazine, the state of Texas, and the National Hockey League for more than $50 Million. I guess that when I win I could give back maybe... a few dollars to the ghetto... Right? That would be the morally correct thing to do? Wouldn't it? But when people like Ali Khadafi, a fellow Negro, who should be on my side, begins to question my motives; I can't help but feel that's wrong... And another thing that's getting on my nerves, this 'mind fucking' thing that he speaks of... I don't know what kind of weird S&M shit he's involved in, but I don't want to be involved with that. He has a fetish for Oreos, and now he's talking about having sexual intercourse with my mind? Is that right? ...FUCK NO! I am promising the entire world that NO homosexual male will be feelin' on me.... Speaking of which, here's something I stumbled upon the other day while reading through a magazine... 'I will ram you raw, leaving nothing more than a gaping, bloody hole... ' 'Watch me rape my Oreo...' Those are two pulled quotes from the magazine... If he plans on raping me, I'll make sure to beg Miyagawa not to hire any more Negroes like this. THESE are the kind of people that become serial rapists, I'd bet some serious cash that this cat would go out and become a Priest just to feel on a n*gga... He wants to become a Terrorist to blow up people like me? I can't help but question if he really is black, with a name like Ali Khadafi, one would think he's an Arab with a long ass beard... But it's all fine and dandy, because I know that besides PURE I got PCW on my hands; I got a FULL TIME JOB! I don't work every two weeks... It seems as if everybody's choking on the haterade now a days, but it's okay... Some of them can't admit that I AM more popular than they will every be. Hell, the only reason Ali Khadafi's got some beef with me is because of the fact that he knows I'm known, he's trying to make a name off of me... Ali just admit it, n*gga please, I'm the Macaroni and the cheese.
