Darkness Within Written: 9/10/02 Junzo Muto RP#2 Nothing but darkness…as I make my way through the inter-twining, dark, murky, alleyways, I am one with the darkness…both in my stealthy advance and in my heart. I knew my job after all, I had been doing this for the majority of my adulthood, and since then I have come to realise the way of things.
People die. People will always die. The only variable in the equation is, how people die, and that was precisely where I fitted in. The majority of the time I only killed when someone had it coming to them, in Dane’s ‘line of work’ the majority of the people I had to deal with, were not nice people. Still, Dane often used my abilities to his advantage, using me to eliminate all of the opposition, or to scare the opposition, if they still have value to him, to submit to Dane’s ideals.
However, when Dane ordered me to assassinate a witness or somebody who could take Dane and upon extension his organisation down, again I was called in. Most of these of people were innocent, yet I never let my master down, but I would always finish the job, quickly and cleanly. They never felt a thing. With each and every death caused by my hand, both the innocent and the guilty, the darkness in my heart grew. I was now beginning to wonder if there was a way to vanquish this darkness, but I fear the darkness has grew too large to dissuade, and when I breathe my last breath, I will accept whatever fate the gods bestow upon me.
I shook the thoughts from my mind as I recognised the markings on the side of the building I just came up to. It was the building in which my next target lived. I looked around a little, before leaping up onto the fire escape, only briefly leaving the dark comfort of the shadows, but I was confident that I was not spotted by anyone, definitely not the target.
Quietly, I advanced up a couple of apartments, before coming to halt directly in front of the appropriate window. Gently, I reached inside my long, trench coat and extracted my shining silver sword. Most assassins from this era preferred to use a variety of automatic weapons, but I felt this to be a little cold. In essence the assassin profession is generally viewed as cold, but using such automatic weapons made the business considerably more frostier.
There were some white laced curtains obscuring my view, I had no idea whether the target was in the room or not, I did not know whether the target was expecting me or not. For all I knew, the man could be sat directly opposite the window, with a gunpoint directly at it.
On the one hand I could sneak in through the window, but if the man was in the room, he could have his gun out and ready to blow me away, before I’ve even composed myself. On the other hand I could burst through the window and take him unawares, but this approach could cause more noise than I originally intended.
After summing up the options, I decided to go for the latter, this would help restore the fear factor, allowing me to do what I had to do easier. I took a deep breath and concurrently a step backwards. With a primal scream I lunged forward, the impact of my leap forward, instantly shattered the glass, sending the small, transparent shards on the floor in front of me, as I elegantly landed jut in front of an expensive-looking beech-wood table.
The window led into the target’s bedroom it seemed, as the man hastily bolted upright in bed, and fell out upon the floor. The man went for a wooden draw of his bedside cabinet, obviously searching for some kind of weapon to defend himself with. Instantly, I leapt at the target bringing my sword down firmly upon the soft wooden container. My sword sliced through the draw, like a knife through water. The handgun fell onto the floor, and I kicked it away before the target could grasp the weapon.
The target instantly tried to crawl after the weapon, but I brought my foot firmly down onto the middle of the man’s back, and left it there, pinning him to the floor.
‘John Mangiello,’ I began ‘your time has come.’ ‘Pl…ple…please. Spare me. I’ll pay you double what you’re getting paid.’ He pleaded. ‘Sadly, it is not about money, it is about honour.’ I replied, getting very impatient. ‘Who? Who sent you?’ He questioned. ‘Dane. Dane Lochlan.’ I said. ‘Ah…I see. That means your him then, doesn’t it? You’re ‘The Death Stalker’, every crime lord in the vicinity are scared of.’ He questioned, realising his fate was sealed.
‘Yes.’ I replied simply. A thoughtful look applied itself upon John’s face, rather strange I thought within this grim situation.
‘Don’t you ever tire of it? Tire of all the killing and maiming you have to do?’ John questioned.
‘More than you will ever know.’ I simply replied, bringing my sword down violently. John Mangiello had indeed breathed his last breath, I only hope he made peace with his maker, rather than being plunged into the internal darkness. My suspicions however, suggested that he will know nothing but darkness for the rest of eternity. What better punishment can there be? I wonder…
