Unknown Displays Of Affection

2 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Unknown Displays Of Affection -/The day begins anew within the confines of Sado Island, Japan, where we find the traditional martial arts dojo of one Inphino Blitz. The bamboo ladden dojo stands tall with the sun and powder blue skies as it's backdrop, the purple poppyseed flowers in full bloom as they sway in a peaceful harmony amongst the lush grassy fields surrounding the dojo. Within the walls of the dojo, we go upstairs to the room of Yuka Hamada, the first lady of PURE and the lovely female confidant of Inphino Blitz, Jordan, and PURE's very own superhero...Mang-A-Hero. She sits daintily at her cherry oak vanity, looking herself over in the mirror, a slight hint of sadness upon her beauteous face as she lightly applies her eyeliner in the mirror, her hair damp and wet as it lays lightly on her white terry cotton robe. Upon finishing her eyeliner, her eyes given a shiny purple aura now, she just sits quietly in thought....not usually a characteristic of the usually adventerous and wild porn star and PURE performer.-/ -/Those thoughts are shortly broken when suddenly, her fellow roomate Jordan X Shabazz comes barging in, headphones over his head and the civil rights activist holding a sky blue CD player in hand as he loudly exclaims, obviously singing along to the song playing.-/ Jordan:'KILL WHITEY, BUST HIM IN DA' HEAD...PUNCH WHITEY IN THE GRILL, STOMP HIS ASS TILL HE'S DEAD!' -/Suprsingly, even that loud and colorful display of musical appreciation isn't enough to startle Yuka, as she simply just lets out a sigh and continue her hard mirror stare. Jordan can't help but see her pain, pressing the silver 'stop' button on his CD player and resting the headphones around his neck. He walks over to Yuka, his baggy Platinum FUBU jeans scrunching with each step before he is right next to Yuka, kneeling down beside her as he asks.-/ Jordan:'Hey shorty...what's wrong with you? It ain't right for someone so fine to be so sad.' -/That comment manages to force a slight smile from Yuka as she turns to Jordan, looking down with a hint of depression as she answers, revealing her dilemma-/ Yuka:'Well Jordan...it's complicated. See...I like this guy...and well...I don't know if he likes me.' -/Jordan's eyes light up with a hint of happiness, Jordan intrigued by where Yuka wants to take this conversation. He leans back on her vanity, starting to slowly transform into that all too familiar 'mack mode' of his as he looks to Yuka and says.-/ Jordan:'Well...why don't you tell J-Dub what's wrong.' Yuka:'Well....God he is just so cute...he has his own style of doing things and I can't help but be intrigued by it...he is sooo not like the other guys in this house..' Jordan:'Oh really?' *To himself* 'YES, this girl is finally starting to come around...I knew all that stalk...I mean 'macking' would pay off in the end.' *Normally* 'Go on..' Yuka:'He's so witty...in a dumb way...and he doesn't take himself too seriously...and most of all...' Jordan: *to himself* 'SHE'S GONNA SAY IT...SHE'S GONNA SAY IT...SHE WANTS TO DIP INTO THE SEXUAL CHOCOLATE!!' Yuka:'HE LOOKS SO GOOD IN THAT SPANDEX!! I MEAN GOD, have you SEEN how put together he is.' Jordan:'Well you know I....SPANDEX?! Wait...your talking about...your talking about....MANG-A-HERO!?!?' -/Yuka give a girly sigh at the very mention of the PURE superheroes name, leaning back deeply on the cushion of her chair as she giddily smiles at the thought of him, Jordan pouting in disappointment-/ Jordan: *to himself* 'She passed...THE CHOCOLATE up for...SPANDEX...man brothers must REEEALLY be outta style ova' here.' Yuka:'Jordan...everytime I see him, he makes me just want to scream...but I don't think he'd go for me and I'm worried I'll make a fool out of myself if I tell him.' !!!!!*CRASH*!!!!! -/Just as she ends that comment, a loud CRASH can be heard from downstairs, Yuka and Jordan alarmed by the sound as they hastily exit the room and head downstairs to find out what's going on within the dojo. Upon finally making it downstairs, they come to find a gapping hole in the left wall of the dojo and smothered in the wreckage of dust and bamboo is none other than Mang-A-Hero, coughing repeatedly and dusting off his Robotech Valkaryie Mark VI costume, complete with cartoon decals and folding wings. Yuka and Jordan are shocked out of their mind at what has just happened, our super samurai throwing back some of the bamboo to reveal a crouch down style silver Go-Kart with 'GO GO TOKYO' written on the sides. Mang-A-Hero turns to find a startled Yuka and Jordan, laughing slightly with nervousness as he tries his best to explain-/ Mang-A-Hero:'NO NEED TO BE ALARMED GOOD KIMOSABI'S...I'm okay! It's only a pity these walls don't have the SUPER SAIEN INVINCIBILITY I HAVE! And by the way, NEVER....EVER EVER EVER EVER substitue pop rocks, Kool-Aid, and distilled mountain spring water in place of unleaded petroleum.' -/Yuka nudges Jordan, quietly whispering to him so Mang-A-Hero is unaware of her words.-/ Yuka:'See Jordan....he even makes a go-kart crash look sexy. I wish I could tell him how I feel!' Jordan: *mumbles* 'I can do everything he does AND fry chicken, run from the cops, and play Domino's...lets seem him ante up on that.' Yuka:'MANG-A, HOW ARE WE GOING TO EXPLAIN THIS TO BLITZ?! He should be back from the market at any moment.' Mang-A-Hero:'Well, I have no idea what you my good friends plan to do....but intend to make like Vampie Hunter D in the darkest of night and use my power of INVISIBILITY...even a SUPERHUMAN SAMURAI CYBER MECHA WARRIOR like me can't fathom an excuse for this one.' Jordan:'Well that works better then 'blame it on the black man'.' -/During their debate to figure out what to blame this uncanny mishap on, the normally calm and collected voice of the Black Dragon Master can be heard from outside, his yell filled with an uncanny anger unknown to most about Blitz as he beckons.-/Voice of Blitz:'MANG-A....WHAT IN THE BLACK DRAGON'S NAME DID YOU DO TO MY DOJO!?!?!?!' Mang-A-Hero:'Umm ummm.....WELL YOU'VE ALWAYS SAID YOU WANTED AIR CONDITIONING...now you have it all year!' *To Jordan and Yuka* 'I think now's my time to make like some screaming, voice dubbed Japanese citizens in a Godzilla movie and RUN!!' -/Mang-A-Hero wisely decided to flee the scene of the crime, leaving Jordan and Yuka standing near the wreckage and watching our samurai race off to the outside fields and safety of the dojo, Jordan fixing his black FUBU scully and saying-/ Jordan:'D**n, does he have some black in him...cause I don't know any white boy that can run like that...' Yuka:'He might Jordan...just look at that butt....mmmmm.' -/Jordan lets out a sigh as Yuka smiles giddily at the departure of Mang-A-Hero, that 'crush' she has upon the whacked out Back Smack Dragon Masta growing just a little stronger in her body.-/ Later That Night...-/The stars are out in full force and the night is young, calling out to three of the four roomies as they have every intention to paint the town of Tokyo red, green, and any other color they possibly can. Upstairs, we find Mang-A-Hero already ready, wearing the classic Gatchaman outfit without the blue and white streaked cape, while in it's place adding a shimmering blue open up shirt. He looks at his Tuxedo Mask watch carefully, the digital time within the two eyeholes of the popular anime character's mask reading '9:25' as he anxiously taps his foot, exclaiming-/ Mang-A-Hero:'LET US MAKE HASTE ALL, THE NIGHT IS CALLING US COLLECT AND THE CHARGES MUST BE ACCEPTED!!' -/Shortly after his anxious call to both Yuka and Jordan, Yuka can be heard responding to samurai sweetly, her voice not seeming to even raise at all but just make a soft echo, a harmony to the ears of Mang-A-Hero-/ Yuka:'MANG-A...can you come here for a minute?' -/Never being one to rebel against the soft beckon of a female, he shortly heads for her room and upon reaching the doorway, is sent into a state of shock...this time, not over the nudity of Yuka...but at the stunning beauty of her as she haughtily stands in a soft spaghetti strap dress with blue roses imprinted upon it, her hair put up in a neat bun with tiny frills of her hair sticking out. She looks to Mang-A-Hero, a shy smile overcoming her face as he is yet to find the correct words to say-/ Yuka:'Is it that ugly?' Mang-A-Hero:'No...no....not at all...byt the sensational beauty of Sailor Moon Yuka...your stunning.' -/She smiles sweetly, taking that short-breathe comment from the usually never lost for words Mang-A-Hero to heart. She takes a seat on her single bed, covered in snow dove sheets and being careful not to wrinkle her dress, looking to Mang-A-Hero and asking in feminine-/ Yuka:'Mang-A...have you ever known what it's like to want something...want to grasp something...but you just don't know if it's meant for you to have?' Mang-A-Hero:'Yuka...never be scared to battle or strive for what it is you truly want. I know you've been kinda shaky about that Pure Juniorweight title match your having...but I know how you feel. I'm feeling kind of shaky about battling the evil forces of the HIT, Carl Kimbell, and Ali Kahdaffi. But you have to suck that overwhelming fear up and battle with all your might. CALL UPON YOUR KAMEHAMEHA POWERS TO DECIMATE YOUR FOES. It is only when they see fear in your eyes when they start to grow strong...and I know you are a fearless woman Yuka, and I admire that. Your remind me a lot of someone I know...' *slight pause* 'Yuka, just give it your all and allwill eb good, okay?' Yuka: *with a smile* 'Yes Mang-A..' font color='blue'>Mang-A-Hero:'Well, I better go check on Jordan...but your gonna be alrigth Yuka...and don't worry, you will have what it is you want. You can bet my spandex on it.' -/The heroic PURE athlete winks to Yuka and leaves her to be in her room, going to check on Jordan as she lays back on the bed, staring up at the ceiling and enwrapped in infatuation by the spandex samurai, saying quietly to herself as she fiddles carelessly with her fingernails-/ Yuka:'You have a point Mang-A...and maybe, if fate is good...I will have you.' -/With that the scene starst to fade witht he sound fo Mang-A-Hero going into Jordan's room, only to shortly exclaim in terror as it echos through the dojo saying-/ Mang-A-Hero:'ONLY BURNT SAUSAGES OF IHOP, WHAT IN GOKU'S NAME IS THAT JORDAN!!' Jordan:'WHAT ARE YOU DOING IN HER MAN!! AND I'M NOT TELLIN YOU...BUT I BET IT'S BIGGER THAN YOURS!!' -/With that said, the scene fades lightly, Yuka looking out her window and at the stars, wishing on one for her crush to be made a reality.
