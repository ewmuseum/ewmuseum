Brotherly Love? I think not!

21 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Brotherly Love? I think not! I cant believe you did that! The scene opens up to an apartment, well...if you can call it an apartment! More like, a one room alcove! The place is one room but a fairly big room, about 12 X 8 metres wide. The room has a blue carpet that looks soft and cuddly and looks as if it was just shampooed or cleaned in some way. On one wall is a door, the exit to the house. Beside the door, there is another door, beside the other one but on a different wall. This door is dead-bolted tight shut for some reason or another. Beside the only door that is useable is a closet that is shut with a wooden door that rolls to open and close. Inside the door is a small washer and planted firmly beside it, a small dryer but behind the closet door, both of the appliances are unseen from the naked eye from outside. In the far wall is a black desk that has a bunch of shelves for storage and more importantly, a computer planted on the desk with a separate slide out attachment for the keyboard and the mouse to lay on. On the computer presently is a black screen-saver that has a bunch of stars and almost like a camera flying through space  the default Windows Screen saver. Beside the computer desk, along the same wall are some blue curtains but more importantly, behind those curtains is a huge screen door, leading out to a pretty big back yard. In fact, a huge back yard! The back yard stretches for miles down and way wide, about a half a mile past the cut-off of the house! The area is just plain green grass! Back inside, on the wall that is opposite to the screen door are big shelves. There are 16 shelves, 4 by 4. Below the shelves are cupboards for alcohol or other things for storage. Sitting in one of the shelves, one of the bigger shelf is a big TV. Not a large TV but a fair 22 inch TV. In the other shelves arent really nothing. One of them has a small stereo and beside it, a large CD case stacked high with CD cases. Beside the computer desk but not blocking the way to the patio is a blue chesterfield and behind the computer desk, kind of on an angle towards the TV is a blue couch that matches the chesterfield.
Overall, the house, or well, room isnt the best equipment but the shocker is the large backyard that has nothing in it but a wide open space to have parties in among other things. Sitting in the Chesterfield, was my brother, Taka. Taka is about twenty-three or twenty-four, a couple of years older than me. Taka has combed back blonde hair like me. It looks as if Taka had the same spiked hair as I but it was combed back and then parted into two parts, like a wedge. Takas hair is the exact same blonde as mine and he has a couple more brown streaks in his hair that are ferally noticeable. Taka has a couple of freckles on his face, probably from being the few years older than me, wisdom, but it isnt too much. Taka has green eyes that are kind of calming, yet mystical. Taka has a bit of a hair growing around his face, probably from not shaving for a day or two but nothing major.
I cant believe you did that! repeated my brother Taka, who was currently occupied in the blue chesterfield that matched the carpet to our very small place that we had lived in for the past three years. Taka wasnt exactly a rebel. He was more like.....well....a loner. He was very paranoid about a lot of things and one thing was last week, our debut in PURE. We were doing a small press conference. As always, I wanted to brighten it up while Taka wanted it to go perfect, fine and dandy. Anyways, during the end of the press conference, I wanted to attract more people so I sang a new song I was working on as I was a song writer. I then ran out of the room, charging after people, trying to attract a better crowd then the rough fifteen or sixteen that were there, a shallow crowd. After the press conference, I got a warning not to do that again but whats the point in having a little fun? I am twenty or twenty-one, something in there. I has spiked hair that is about four or five inches in the air. It doesnt look like a freak but a normal hair due for me which I like. My hair is a light blonde, like a white colour but more of a blonde. I have one or two little strands that are a darker colour that can only be seen from up close on Me. I dont have freckles on his face but my look says that I am a rough neck and no book-reader, which is definitely true. I have blue eyes with normal eye brows. My lips are normal and I am surely nicely shaven. I ignored Taka from talking for a bit longer, to mainly test how mad and what kind of mood he was in. Are you even listening to me! said my brother, Taka. This time I figured I would have a little more fun with him. It was all in good harm and I did this to him a lot. Of course I was! I replied to him. I tried not to sound too sarcastic but it kinda slipped out more then I had hoped. Then what did I say? Taka looked at me with his eyes. The eyes for those eyes like the, Im smarter than you and always will be look. Uh.......Of course I like chickens. Good food! I joked around and this time I didnt sound sarcastic which was sort of bad since I wanted to. I looked over at Taka sitting in the chesterfield, chilling from my spot, sprawled out on the couch. When I looked at him, the sight was not one you wanted to see. I had pushed Taka over the edge. I usually didnt do that but it all depended on what kind of mood Taka was in. Sometimes I could tease and assault him as much as I wanted to but other days he was pissed and this was one of those days. Why do you always have to act like this? Takas voice wasnt a loud voice but a bugging voice kind of, Why? You had to embarrass me and you last week on Ikusa when you ran out after some guy who didnt care about your song! You screw everything up! You would shoplift as a kid, so I could get the shirt I wanted. You beat up a bigger kid at school so he wouldnt bug me, you got us this room which was supposed to be our mansion... I left Taka to his rambling while I looked at him, half listening, half not. The house thing was partly my fault, I had to admit it.
It all started when me and him were searching for a house, about three or four years ago. Taka was looking in the paper for some house that wasnt too expensive and would cover mine and his housing needs. While he was in the papers, I was looking on the internet. On the internet, I had found a house that said huge bkyd which means backyard in housing limbo but I thought it had meant Big Kommercial Young People Dream and then house on the end.
I decided to surprise Taka so I had bought the house. Without even seeing the house myself, I brought Taka to the house and told him about the bkyd initials and right as we had walked, he told me about nkyd meaning backyard. I tried to make it better but we couldnt sell it! We had to keep it.
Beside us, in the house was an old grumpy man that lived in the rest of the house, a big mansion! Actually, it wasnt a mansion but good enough. The man still lived there to this day but wasnt very nice. He had nailed the door to his area shut and had told us to keep the noise down numerous times, me and Taka knowing that when he died, the rest of the house would be ours! I tried to convince Taka that it wasnt so bad because we had a huge backyard and over time, Taka felt okay about it and we were planning to fill it with things like hot tubs and other gaming things. All we needed was the money  something that WAS hard to find, until PURE. And what about the time when you... I stopped Taka abruptly. He could go on for hours on things I had screwed up on and I HAD to stop it! Taka, why do you remember even the tiniest detail? I mean, you remembered that it was April 12th when I screwed up on this house thing! I tried to act like a smart-ass again and try to prove to Taka of how much a dork he was and I was hoping that somehow it might work. It was April 13th! Taka hollered back at me. He didnt say it like he was a smart-ass but as if he was just giving the facts out. I hated it when Taka corrected me with the correct answer. It bugged me so much and I wondered if he was correcting me because I was wrong or he was correcting me because he wanted to make me mad. If he was trying to make me mad, it was working.
There was a pause for a few seconds until I did something that me and Taka had done whenever we argued. You want to prove youre the best? I said to Taka, Prove it! I challenge to our families most treasured game, Ping Pong! Ever since our first ancestors came to Japan, they had been extremely well in Table Tennis or Ping Pong, so whenever Taka got under my skin, I challenged him to a match on our table, right outside our house. Taka never didnt accept because he always had to prove he was better. I usually challenged Taka whenever we were arguing or when I was bored and needed something to do. Your on! shouted Taka. I wasnt sure if he was still mad at me. He was that type of person. He would argue with me for so long about nothing at all and then I challenged him and we were brothers again. Me and Taka looked at each other and then ran at the screen door to play Table Tennis. Who will win the game? Find out next time!
