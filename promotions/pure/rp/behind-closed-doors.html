Behind Closed Doors

29 August 2002

Behind Closed Doors Jaden had her doubts about her career in wrestling after her release in PCW because of its fast downfall. She left PWA prematurely. How was she able to survive this long with out any real training? Was she flirting with her teachers to get an early promotion? How was she able to make a quick jump from PWA to PCW in about a month? This is the behind the scene story of Jaden (Fiction or Non-Fiction) Jaden had and still has many trials and tribulations she has to work out in her life. Those were some set backs in her career. However, those set backs strengthened Jaden; she learned how to lie. No one really knows who Jaden really is. Since the first time she entered PWA, she began her lying spree. After time progressed, she smoothed her lie out to give you a little taste of her natural self but not to far from another lie. When Jaden first entered PWA, she announced to her soon to be PWA fans she was a retired stripper. Oh yes, this would give her many man votes in wrestling, however, fewer female votes. She had to think twice on that one. She had to make a bond with both sides so she dropped the Stripper gimmick, giving people Jaden, whoever she is! A few days later in her career she met her first wrestling boyfriend, Sylo. Sylo was a soon to be rising star in PCW. They met in a bar one night. Jaden walking in with only a nightgown and slippers on would get anyones attention and it happened to get his. Jaden had not even known him for an entire day yet and they wound up in bed together, technically, her mothers couch. Sylo did anything he could for Jaden. He thought they were in love, real love. Jaden did not have that in mind. All she wanted was a fast break to the big league and maybe a few quick nightstands. As soon as Jaden made it to PCW her relationship with Sylo ended, as she handed anything they belonged to him, his new baby, Angel! Jaden new she was pregnant. She even consulted one of her PCW colleagues about her pregnancy, Akiyama. When Sylo left PCW, he wanted to continue their relationship but Jaden did not want that. He tried to convince her plenty of times that the relationship would work. He said, What about RAH and Amy, they are in to different federations and they still are together! However, Jaden did not want to hear a word he had to say. They still keep in contact but more on a friendship level. Jadens second on screen romance was with Cruiserwieght Champion, Big Daveylicious. They met in Jadens first debut in PCW, after the pick of the new PCW roster members. It was a very likely story, Jaden shows up to PCW Orientation class late to be a big stuck up bitch, getting all the attention from the new PCW male wrestlers. At the end of the class, one man was brave enough to approach her, John Crichton. He was the Television Champion. She wanted nothing with him at the time. All she wanted to do was flirt with everyone she could. He approached her in a harsh and aggressive way, which led her to tell him she was gay so he would get off her back, followed by a kick to the groin. That was a fast move by Jaden and a big mistake, Duzza, and old friend told her. Jaden and Duzza then entered the women restroom to have a private chat. They were in there for hours. All the attention from Jadens arrival drew a crowd. As the left, the restroom, Big Davey, asked Jaden out on a date. He was very rude, however he knew it no other way. She accepted his offer to let everyone know she was straight because rumors started to spread already. Months went by with their comical relationship, Big Daveylicious, and Jaden. Jaden dropped the sunglasses wearing pervert. He was not what she really wanted. Unexpectedly she met her next lover, Television Tile Winner, John Crichton. He was what she wanted at the time. He showed, strength, courage, power, but also, weakness. Jaden can spot peoples weakness fast and use it to her advantage. His weakness was his last girlfriend Aeryn Sun. Jaden was always comparing herself to Aeryn, in private or in public with him. Later in their relationship, she asked John, am I like Aeryn. He remarked with fast, non-hesitant nos! She knew he was going to say no anyway she just wanted to hear it from that horses mouth. This was her way getting out of her tiresome relationship with him. He was boring to her. He was never home. John was always out on a mission find Flemmings, crashing into parties as if he was 007, James Bond. Since John went on his little secret mission Jaden made up a secret mission for herself, finding out whom was this Aeryn Character. Jaden searched the city meeting Aeryns father hank that let out the life story of John and Aeryn.
As Jaden wanted out of their stressful relationship everyday, she would do little things to tick him off, but the thing that took it to the limit was the burning of the pictures. This was after a heated fight between the two in a restaurant where Jaden wanted to hear more about Aeryn from John however he was unwilling to talk about it. She then twisted John's words to get in an altercation with John. *At the Restaurant* Jaden, I wish you could have met Aeryn she was a nice person. I think you would have like her. She was very caring, smart. You know what I mean. No, I dont enlighten me, she says, folding her arms, raising an eyebrow, and rolling her eyes. Whoa, is it getting hot in here, he says, as he loosens he collar. Jaden then snaps her finger Yes miss, may I help you. Yes, turn on the air over here for this gentleman. He said he is getting hot! Ok, miss! Ok, you can finish. Well-ummshe is nothing like you, totally different! WHAT! Jaden stands up and slaps John across the face before she throws the water in his face. Wait, Jaden I didnt mean it like that, he says wiping the water from his face and chasing Jaden out the high-class restaurant. That same night Jaden burned all Johns pictures of Aeryn except for the picture in his wallet. This battle between them continued for weeks. Their next surprise was Jaden grandmother showing up at John motel room retrieving Jaden to take her home with her where she belonged. Jaden grandmother then shocked the world letting John know he was messing with a 17-year-old girl, not yet woman. John knew this was his final way out of this relationship. That same day he made Jaden pack her bags and leave out the house. Jaden then began to stalk him, trashing his place and leaving notes to him in red lipstick. After her separation with her 3rd PCW man, he left the scene, PCW. Were they all running away from Jaden or did they have enough of PCW? This time Jaden left PCW to make her debut in PURE and maybe to find another man!
