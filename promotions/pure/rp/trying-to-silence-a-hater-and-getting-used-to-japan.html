Trying To Silence A Hater, and Getting Used to Japan.

4 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Trying To Silence A Hater, and Getting Used to Japan. The entire PCW/PURE experience so far has been quite the journey for Jordan X Shabazz. For a kid who grew up in intense poverty, and had to move to Canada and grow up with parents, living out his dream as a professional wrestler is quite the accomplishment. But, even though hes having the time of his life in the PCW/PURE, there are some people out there that everybody loathes and to some extent, fear. A group of people known to the Hip-hop community as Haters. Every single one of us has experienced them, they hardly have anything positive to say, and theyre always putting somebody down for something. They claim that certain people dont have talent when they actually do, and they tell you things that try to burst your bubble But then again, there are some haters that are truthful in what the say. For instance, when Karl Leblanc was Extreme Champion, a whole lot of haters come out of the woodshed, or from their rocks, and they began to verbally abuse Leblanc. They abused him to the point of no return. They gave Leblanc a reputation that he didnt necessarily deserve. Sure he wasnt the greatest of talents, but they made it sound as if he was the worst wrestler this side of The Super Dooper Masked Jobber King. Not only did that reputation kill the Extreme Championship, but also in essence, it killed Karl Leblancs career. Now we are sure he has no chance of returning to the PCW, or returning to any of the Big Four. How people can live with themselves after doing something like that, I have no idea. ************************* The scene opens up with Jordan X Shabazz in an office. This is no regular office, this office is in disarray. What once was a nice and beautiful office, is now littered with papers, broken computers, torn advertisements from the walls, and a generally sad demeanor on the face of many of the workers. It appears to be the remnants of a newspaper office. The sun shines through the open drapes, some people continue to continue pounding the keys on their computers in their little cubicles. In a distant office on the floor above, screaming is heard followed by the unmistaken sound of somebody being hit. We now pan up a flight of nice looking wooden stairs that lead to the top floor of this office. The screaming gets louder as we near the top floor. We finally get to the top floor and we see a large office with the words Robert Thompson, Houston Chronicle President. We enter the large office to see a steaming mad Jordan X Shabazz staring at the man whom we assume is Robert Thompson. Thompson sits in a beautiful brown leather chair. Theres a large wooden conference table that separates Jordan X Shabazz from Robert Thompson. Thompson is dressed as any head of a company would be dressed, he wears a brown colored suit, which is now ruffled. His face looks as if he is afraid of Jordan X Shabazz, who has now decided to take a seat in the chair opposite the Houston Chronicle head. Both men are now silent. Mr. Shabazz, youve got to understand that the column was an editorial, and editorials are the pages that area least read by our subscribers and people that buy the newspaper, Thompson begins. So in reality, nothing bad is really happening to you, and nothing that is in our hands. Nothing bad is happening to me? Jordan asks. Are you serious?! A man of my stature, a man who is a member of the Malice Crew in the Primetime Championship Wrestling organization, a man who has been fighting for the rights of the BLACK MAN, for YEARS now? Youre one crazy cracka, because let me tell you something, the haters arent going to let me forget this. The haters? Yes, the haters. Like the one in your newspaper who wrote that editorial on me. Nothing but a white, arm chair critic, whos a hater. And you know what happens to haters where I come from? No, what happens to haters where you come from. They catch a bullet in the brain. Are you threatening me? Why are you twisting my words around cracka? So you can have an excuse to send your guards after me? So you can have an excuse when you guards try to pull a Rodney King on me? Mr. Shabazz, if that is your real name, why do you have to bring every back to this issue of racism? Its a very harsh accusation when you accuse somebody of racism. I understand fully, but when they accused Mark Freeman, or whatever that cracka cops name was, when they accused him of racism during the OJ Simpson trial, everybody was quick to jump on Jonnie Cochran for Playing The Race Card as you white people call it. But in reality, that guy was racist, and so is the entire LAPD, and so is the entire free world of America. Except the crackas that I know I would wish for you to leave my office, but I know you would just get more angry. So I think I might as well call security, the paper has already been printed, and theres nothing I can do to retract it. NOTHING YOU CAN DO? WHY CANT YOU JUST PUT A LITTLE TIDBIT IN TOMORROWS PAPER AS AN APOLOGY TO ME? WHY CANT YOU APOLOGIZE TO THE ENTIRE AFRICAN AMERICAN RACE? WEVE WORKED HARD TO BE SEEN AS TRUSTWORTHY PUBLIC FIGURES, AND IF YOURE NEWSPAPER IS OUT HERE WRITING COLUMNS THAT FUCK UP MY HARD WORK, IM BOUND TO ANGRY! Jordan X Shabazz now stands up and stares at Robert Thompson. The look on his face isnt changing, and he doesnt appear to be blinking. Hes really mad, and that is evident. Robert Thompson has the Sunday edition of the Houston Chronicle laid out before him. Its on the page where the scathing editorial about Jordan X Shabazz was written. Jordan walks around the large table and towards Robert. Robert had the inclination to call security as Jordan neared him, but as soon as he realized that Jordans eyes were fixated on the newspaper, Robert decided against it. Jordan picks up the paper and he nods. He rereads the paper for what seems to be the twentieth time.
Jordan Andrew Copeland, known to the masses as professional wrestler, Jordan X Shabazz, is a complete and utter fraud. Nothing he does is for the African American race. Hes a disgrace to African Americans everywhere. His complaints about racism where there is none is disgusting. For every true African American who has experienced racism, its a slap in the face. Us as black people, have tried our best to live honest lives, stay out of crime, and refrain from starting riots. Jordan Copeland has done none of that. Case in point, recently there was a jailhouse riot in which six convicted armed bank robbers, escaped. Why did it happen? Because Jordan Copeland provoked them to riot in the prison. Jordan Copeland was questioned, but then freed after a lack of evidence was found. But everybody in the state of Texas knows that Jordan incited the riot on purpose, it was his goal when he entered that prison, to make sure that somehow, and someway, some white people were injured in the ruckus. Thats the type of man he is. Even more fraudulent, is the way he works with the NAACP. Its a disgrace to a group of African Americans who have worked hard to make sure that our voice is not silenced by The Man, not only is he their spokesperson, therefore seen throughout the world as the way the NAACP wants to be represented. He dresses inappropriately for work at the office, hes loud, abrasive, and cocky, the typical stereotypical black man. So in closing, I ask you, the people of Texas, to run Jordan Copeland out of office at the NAACP, and keep the perception of the African American in the 21st century, wholesome and pure. Jordan finishes reading the article. He shakes his head and realizes that he isnt going to get anywhere arguing with the head of the Houston Chronicle. The desks and offices below that he has rampaged, have done nothing to help his cause. Hes lucky and thankful that security wasnt called. You know what Robert, Ive been around haters my whole life, Ill let this one little editorial slide, but I swear to the God of The Crunk, if I EVER see another article like this in YOUR newspaper, you can bet your ass that there will be a lawsuit. Jordan says with a smile on his face. Have a good day Mr. Copeland, replies Robert Thompson as Jordan leaves the office. Fade. ************************************** 20 Hours later. The scene opens up on the bustling streets of Osaka, Japan. Its hot and mid-day. The sun beats down on the people walking the overcrowded streets. We see a lost looking, tired, Jordan X Shabazz. Hes smiling for some reason. This, is Japan. Jordan says quietly to himself as he walks the streets looking for some elusive place. We see Jordan cross several streets, and then finally, he appears to reach the place. It appears to be a small hut, sort of like one of those Sushi huts or whatever. Jordan enters the place, and immediately is over taken by the smell of rice, noodles, and other Japanese foods. Jordan looks around the small place and finds that it is nice, and packed to the zenith with small items. Pictures of Japanese wrestling Gods such as Tiger Mask, The Dynamite Kid, Kensuke Suzahara, and even the great MIYAGAWA~!. Jordan marvels at the wonders of the room as he walks in without knocking on the makeshift door, which was made out of an Afghan. Jordan sits on one of the many couches with a smile on his face. Though the Houston Chronicle ripped him for all he had done for the black man, that bugged Jordan, but he was going to forget about it, he had the contract with PURE now, he was lucky to be one of the few people who came in to replace the guys who put minimal effort into their matches. He was incredibly tired, first he was in Houston arguing with the newspaper head of the Chronicle, then he had to fly to Fusion where he showed up, then he had to make it to PUREs Ikusa. But all the jet lag, and hard work was worth it, to get his hands on Ali Khadafi Jordan grinned when he thought about the attack he Ali Khadafi. The two men had waged a feud of words through several different ways, with Ali calling Jordan a sell out, and a fake gangster. It didnt really offend Jordan, because he had experienced the haters all of his life, all he could do was follow what Statuz Quo of RUSH wrestling told him to, Shake The haters off Shabazz-san, welcome to my home, the voice boomed from nowhere. Jordans thoughts are interrupted by the man that stands about 6 foot 8, and weighs at least 300 pounds. Jordan stares at the man in complete and utter awe. He cant believe what is before him. The man is Japanese, and he is dressed in some American style clothing. Some khaki colored shorts, and a white Puroresu Rules! T-shirt. Jordan smiles at the man. Shabazz-san? Jordan asks, not knowing what the man means. Yes, its a sign of respect here in Japan. The large man replies. Oh, really? Thanks, Jordan extends his hand out as the large man shakes it and bows. Whats your name? Jordan asks. Ishii, Ken Ishii, you can just call me Ishii-san. Okay. Well, as you know, Ive been sent as your trainer Shabazz-san. And I plan to train you hard. Ishii says to Jordan. What am I going to train on? Jordan questions. Well, mainly striking. You come from America, striking is popular there, but not in the form that it is here in Japan. You will learn like the martial artists learn, and like your UFC fighters in America learn. Ishii answers while heading back into the small room that he calls his kitchen. Jordan follows. Here is where you will spend the majority of your time besides sleeping in my home. This is my kitchen, you will learn to eat wholesome foods, not that cheeseburger garbage that you eat in America. [Uh oh. Thats bad news for me. I LOVE my cheeseburgers and stuff, how does he expect me to survive eating noodles] We will train every morning you are not in America, to make sure that you are ready for the Japanese style of wrestling, Ishii finishes Now, I know you are tired from the plane flight from America, and from your debut in PURE. Have a good night Shabazz-san, Ill wake you up nice and early for tomorrow. Thanks. Ishii points Jordan to a small portion of the hut. Theres a sleeping bag and a pillow there. Jordan sets his heavy bags down, and nods. This is where the real competition begins.
