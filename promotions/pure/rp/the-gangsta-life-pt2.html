The Gangsta life. [pt2]

21 June 2002

The Gangsta life. [pt2] The scene open's up in a hospital room. Everything in the hospital room is white, with bear prints. PosterBoy is seen sitting down on a metal chair, with the same clothes he had on yesterday. He is sitting next to the bed that Jay is laying down on, with his eyes shut. PosterBoy is still seating on the chair, but this time he is on the phone with one of the guy's that was in the back seat of the pathfinder jeep with them. ~.::Phone Conversation::.~ Po$ter: 'Yo, what you up to nigga?' He asked. Mac: 'Well, I just woke up. I am sorry that John and I left yesterday night.' Mac replies. Po$ter: 'That's ok. I know that you was shook [scared] from yesterday.' Poster tell's Mac. Mac: 'Fuck you. I got to go down to the block and handle some business.' Mac say's laughing. Po$ter: 'Ok. No doubt, do you.' Poster replies to Mac Mac: ' I peace. One [bye] ~.::Phone Convo Over::.~ PosterBoy hang's up his cell phone. and see's jay moving around in pain. PosterBoy jump's up to his feet in excitement. He begins to speak to jay to see if he get's a answer. 'Yo, Jay are you aight?' PosterBoy say's and waits for a answer. There is no answer from Jay. PosterBoy continues to talk to him to see if he can get a answer from Jay 'Yo, Jay it's me, Po$ter/' PosterBoy says worried. 'Ahh. Shut the Fuck up!' Jay replies with a look of pain on his face. 'Are you ok, Jay?' PosterBoy asked. 'Nigga I got shot!' Jay replies in a sarcastic tone of voice. 'Where is Mac and John at?' Jay ask's Po$ter. 'They left once we brought you here.' Poster Replies 'Oh. Those fag's' Jay tell's Poster They both begin to laugh, PosterBoy sit's back down on the chair, and Continues to speak to Jay 'Yo, so how did the surgery go?' Poster asked Jay. 'Surgery. What you talking about?' Jay replies to Poster 'Oh. I guess you don't remember the surgery. They put you to sleep once we got here.' Poster tell's Jay. 'Damn I guess I was knocked out.' Jay say's 'Yup.' Poster replies 'Tell me how this all happen?' Poster asked jay. 'I went into the building, to pick up our package. He tried to play me for a fool and I was not having it. He shot me in the leg once, I fell to the floor took out my glock [9mm] and shot 4 round's to his chest. He dropped and I left the package there cause I figured the Popo [cop's] would come and check the place out' Jay explained. 'Wow. You did good.' Poster say's stunned 'Thank's alot.' Jay replies. 'The nurses say that they will let you out in a couple of more day's' Poster tell's Jay A nurse opens the door and tell's Poster that visiting hour's are done with. Poster get's up from the cold metal seat, give's Jay a pound and begins to exit the room and the hospital. -Scene ends-
