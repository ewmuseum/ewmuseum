Fate: Prologue Written: October, 12 200 Why controls my act in this life? Is there some higher being telling me to go and do the right thing and stay on the correct path so life will be easy? And if I did follow the guidance of this being, did I learn anything from it or did I follow his words to the very end. Did my actions happen for any reason beyond my own?
These questions serve no purpose because for one fact, I am the only one that decides what happens and not some being or fate. Fate is just there to help people believe the system. It’s there to get in the minds of everyone and make then think things happen because a god says they should. What kind of world is this anyway, when we listen to someone who can’t see with are own eyes and we believe he does everything for us, for some reason besides are own.
How can one person believe in things happening in a reason? Everything goes around in a circle and for some reason or another you will lead your self back from start to finish just by following a chain of events. Of course the chain could be rough along the way and could be a little twisted but that is life so deal with it.
My competition believes in one thing just like everyone else. Things happen for a reason. I did what I did because I had something to say but didn’t know how to say it. Everyone believes I was told to make my presents felt and make an impact by someone else higher on the chain then I am. This was all a planned stunt is what I hear most of the time.
You see the problem with all that is; I decide what I want to do and when I will do it. I don’t need someone superior to me telling me what I can and cannot do. If I have something to say, I will say it. If I have a reaction to anything I will react and worry about what happens later. If I get attacked I will quickly respond with what ever force I feel like at the time.
You can beg, pray and plead with me all you want to stop, but you cross me your fate will be decided by what I say and not by the luck of the draw. This is life we are talking about and not some poker game where you beat your life, soul and beliefs all on a hand that you know you can’t win. At least use some common sense and maybe feel that the dealer is bluffing and has a bad hand to make you loss without even knowing it.
“I was put on this earth to do something with my life. Sooner or later he will decide what he had planned for me.”
Anyone that waits for life to be handed to them ends up the same way. They all end up fighting for their lives and using their assets for their own personal joy and pleasure. They sit in their happy little lives and just let things happen without taking any chances or risks. Who really wants to live in a life where everything happens for them and they never get to make any choices? The people strapped to the system are the one’s that live like this and it makes me sick to be near them.
I take risks every day just by walking out my front door. I have to put up with the chance of having to talk to you people involved in something I don’t care for. You call it fate that we bumped into each other on the street or that when you called I picked up the phone but I just call it you trying to get an easy answer for life.
“I want to be in control of my life.” Well if so then it’s time to see the light. And not the light of the heavens opening in the sky and beaming down. Look at the light of day and see that you choice what happens. I don’t need you people telling me where to eat, where to live or where to go. This isn’t prison, this is life. And in life things don’t just happen for a reason. They happen because you make them happen. And that is all life is. You making things happen.
