Working against the System: Prologue

4 October 2002

Working against the System: Prologue Written: October 4, 2002 What is real? Are you real? Are this world and I real? Of course to you, you will see many streets, people, buildings, foods and technology. They all have a purpose and they all have a reason. Each is designed to do a task in which are brain carry’s the task out. Are bodies then performs the task and the job is complete. A simple life and no one can even dare mess with the system.
But really that’s what it all is. This world is a system and we are put here to carry it out for what some will call a greater power. This power or God designed us to do his work for us. So where does that leave you and I and everyone else in this world? I can tell you but you won’t be able to take it at first. Put it this way. Have you ever been in charged of something with a group? That’s how this world works. But instead of one group there are thousands and maybe millions working together for a common goal.
This system was designed to help us in the long run. It was designed like a computer in which every part has a job to do. Everything has to be working in proper order or something goes wrong. But one little thing can bring the whole system down. These viruses bring the system down because they do not won’t to work for a great power. They would rather work for human knowledge and understanding. This is where I come in.
While most spend time carrying for a greater god, I spend my time caring for my own intelligence. Why spend half my time believing in my self and the other time believing in something else? Why not just spend the whole time trying to accomplish my own goals and ideas and see if I can not make my self-a success.
“Have faith in thy self, before thee have faith in others.” A quote people should live by. But really can everyone say they believe one hundred percent in them self? I know I can truly say I do. But what about those people who come to your door and ask you to hear the voice of god or to read this verse in the bible? Do they believe in them self at all or are they just ponds in the churches game of chess.
When they come to my door I let them speak their mind. Let them get all their ideas and thoughts out before I add mine. Of course usually it ends in a big fight between their god and my realistic beliefs. But usually I either get them mad at them selves for not changing me or I make them see things through my eyes. They can approach any way they wish but they never should question my beliefs. Mess with someone’s beliefs is like playing with the devil. In the end someone gets hurt.
God has been the greatest thing that has ever happened to me in this life. I don’t know what I would do without him.
When I hear this I got to say people really don’t know what is going on. You made it through this life so far and it was you pushed yourself in things like school and sports. No one helped you but yourself, but yet you give all the credit to something you have never seen.
So where does that leave us now? You believe and I don’t and yet I am the one who is told to leave all the time. But what if you lived a world where you had no choice. What if you lived in a place that only cared if believe in the great good instead of your self? I lived there and I will tell you it’s not the greatest. You can’t have your own thoughts and ideas unless you are with them. So that is where I am now, alone and on my own with my own thoughts and beliefs.
