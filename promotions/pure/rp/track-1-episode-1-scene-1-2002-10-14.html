Track #1 - Episode #1 - Scene #1

Track #1 - Episode #1 - Scene #1 Written: 14/10 Track #1 -> The Birth of a Dream… Dreams… The only thing that is able to comfort so many of the worlds living population… The only thing that allows one to break away from the constraints of their reality… The only place where ‘Fantasy’ is allowed to take that prominent role of a ‘Reality’ of sorts…
The birth of a dream is something that can determine the path that one will walk… If it is successful, then all is good… and they shall parade down the road to joy… to enchantment…
However… Should they fail in their quest… slip up along the way… all will be lost… nothing will be forgiven…
Can Chad Matthews and Kamui Fukamachi afford to lose so much… in the short existence of their careers?
Episode #1 – We Gotta Power! Scene #1 – It’ll happen… later… real later! (|V_V|) We open our scene in the confinements of a Sushi Bar, one that was conveniently located about five minutes away from the ‘Mizhuo Athletics Stadium’. As everything comes to complete picture, we see the team of Chad Matthews and Kamui Fukamachi sitting down at a table, consuming what food they had ordered. Chad doesn’t look too keen on the food he’s been served… but he’s trying to eat it nonetheless… Fukamachi-san on the otherhand, continued to gulp down mouthful after mouthful of the stuff… bringing back memories of the SaiyaJin tribe that would gobble their food down at such a rapid rate.
“Geez… why couldn’t things have gone better for us at Ikusa IV… I don’t know what was wrong with those lyrics… or those dance steps, I was pretty pleased at the way we both worked…”, Chad commented, sighing heavily before leaning back in his chair, looking up to the ceiling… flashbacks of the dead crowd still running rampant through his mind…
“Come on Chad… dude, its only been our debut performance… after all, nobody is great after their first attempt! Look at Hikaru… he sucked when he started to play Go… didn’t he?”, Kamui said, trying to make Chad feel a little better about what had happened at their debut Ikusa appearance.
Chad’s chair plopped down upon the floor again, its wooden feet hitting the ground with considerable force. Thinking for a few seconds, the ideas of Shindo playing “Go” entered his mind… and then, he realized how long it took for him to just learn the basics of the game, “What?!” Chad yelled, amazed at the fact that it could take a year for them to get any kind of recognition, “… I’m not going to suck for an ENTIRE year… besides… we don’t even have somebody like Sai to show us everything!”
He tilted his head to the side and began to cry in an Anime-like way, tears pouring down his face in white streams, his face scrunched up into something that looked more amateurish than anything else. Looking over at Kamui, his face reflected a nervous grin, concerned at the fact that he’d made Chad start crying with reference to Shindo Hikaru. Eager to make his friend stop, Kamui begins, “… Well, look at what happened after a year of training? Shindo was doing well… and then, after two years… he was like, so strong… and Then… he became Pro… PRO I say!”
Chad began to lighten up for a moment… the idea of finally reaching a goal that they could call respectable appealed to him greatly… although thinking about the time that it would take for such a thing to happen burst his balloon… with one swift POP too! Instantly, Matthews seemed to be uncontrollable with his outbursts, continuing to cry while hitting the table with his fist… his own bowl moving closer and closer to the edge of the table.
“Why… oh WHY… WHY can’t we just have somebody that’ll teach us everything… and make our dreams become reality in the fastest possible time?!” he wailed, continuing to scream out. Kamui felt uncomfortable by these tendencies and removed himself from his placing at the table, side-stepping away carefully before beginning to leave the establishment and Chad behind.
Despite being such a foolish customer, Chad could sense if there was somebody near him or not… and unfortunately, he couldn’t quite sense the presence of Kamui infront of him anymore. With that in mind, he sprung up from his seat, twisting towards Kamui and pointing at him as he opened the door, screaming, “HEY… YOU… STOP!!” at the same time! Fukamachi stopped still, poised in the position to lift his right foot up and walk out of the door. Quickly he span around, again smiling nervously, scratching the back of his head and thinking of an excuse to spare him the blushes of being insulted.
“H-H-Hey… I wasn’t going to leave you here to wail or anything!”, Kamui protested, shaking both his hands in a vigorous fashion to try to fool Chad into believing that he was innocent, “No… No, I was just walking outside to get some fresh air… because like, I needed to get a little fresh air… geez, is it me… or is it stuffy in here or something?”
Kamui’s nervous laughter followed, something that Chad saw as a little insult towards his own mentality. “KAMUI FUKAMACHI!!! I AM NOT THAT STUPID!!! I KNOW YOU WERE GOING TO DITCH ME AND START PLAYING GO!” he yelled, bringing all the attention in the Sushi Bar to himself, Kamui continuing to smile… but edging ever so closer to the door that was already being propped open by some convenient force. Unaware of such deception, Chad closes his eyes for a split second… but by the time they open, Kamui jumps out of the shop and waves once before starting to charge down the busy streets.
“HEY… FUKAMACHI!!! COME BACK… DON’T LEAVE ME WITH THE BILL!!!” Chad screams, jumping from his chair over the table… giving chase after Kamui! He bursts out of the door and into the street, turning to his left to see him running down the roads, dodging people like he were some kind of ancient martial artist or something. Attempting to catch up with him, Matthews runs off in the same direction… screaming and shouting at Kamui for him to slow down!!
Meanwhile… We’re back at the Sushi bar about two minutes after both Kamui and Chad had made their escape… the table that they had been situated at was still vacant… well, except for the two dealings of Sushi that remained on the table. The silence ruled over the bar with an Iron Fist, although when the owner of the location came out and looked at the place where the Superstars of PURE had been sitting, he looked confused and turned around to ask somebody something.
“Tetsuo… what happened to the two customers that were sitting at this table over here? Did they actually come in to eat… or did we just imagine the whole thing and serve food to an empty table again?”
A head popped out from behind the corner where the Owner was seated, this was obviously the one that had been called Tetsuo. “Why, Mr Touya… I do believe that I saw at least two people walk into this establishment today… even if it had been made to seem as though there were more of us here… those cardboard cut-outs… maybe they look as though this establishment is always full or something?”
Mr Touya stood still and looked around at the cut-outs that been erected a few days before. Shaking his head from side-to-side in a soft swinging motion, he sighs at the fact that he’d done something so silly. “Damn… looks like we happened to believe that there were two people there again… but if that really was the case, how comes I can’t see the cut-outs?”
As the scene fades out into the abyss of all that is black, the picture stops on the face of Tetsuo as it scrunches up in confusion.
Where are Kamui and Chad headed? I don’t know… you don’t know… so tune in next time… to H.O.T!
