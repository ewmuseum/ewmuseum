Stage #1: Progress

23 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Stage #1: Progress We cut to the PURE general office, in Anaheim, California. Ok, so its the backroom of Miyagawas home but it is the PURE general office. Miyagawa is wearing a suit, complete even with waistcoat and tie. Its probably the second time ever that hes had to wear a suit, the other of course being the day he got married. He struggles with the neck of the shirt trying to get it comfortable. Miyagawa: Just who the hell does he think he is? The camera pans to the side to show none other than PURE reporter, and good friend of Alexander Miyagawa, Jeremy Johnson formerly known as JJ in PCW circles. He stands there without a microphone for once, and wearing one of his typically ugly Hawaiian shirts. JJ: I dunno? Maybe he thinks he was wronged somehow? Miyagawas head snaps to the side and glares at JJ. Miyagawa: I hired him to take out Kensuke. He took out Kensuke. He has no reason to be on the roster now. JJ: Maybe he likes getting paid. I mean, thats why I took this job. So I can fund my movie making empire. Miyagawa: You dont have an empire. Hell, you dont even have a camera at the moment. JJ: True. But I got a cool clapperboard the other day, its only about ten years old. Miyagawa glares at JJ some more, who fidgets with his collar to distract himself from the intense pressure. JJ shrugs at Miya when he sees nothing else to say at the moment. Miyagawa: I mean dammit. He is from CCW. He does suck. But this is my fed and I can do whatever I like. Im the owner dammit, and I deserve some respect! You dont just go smashing my face into expensive cameras. You just dont do that sort of thing. This is Japan, not America. JJ: Actually this is the old back bedroom of your house in Anaheim. Which is in America. JJ gets glared at once again as Miyagawa undoes the tie and pulls it off. Miyagawa: God I hate suits. Pass me the clip-on. JJ hands over the clip-on tie to Miyagawa, how puts it on to replace the regular tie he just removed. Miyagawa: Dammit, Im the boss. Not him. He should give me some respect. You always respect the boss. JJ: What about the time you set fire to Tyler Nelsons hair? Or the time you piss on the seats of his limo. Miyagawa yet again, glares at JJ. Miyagawa: Now is not the time, JJ. Next Sunday is the time. JJ: Oh yeah, we you take on Cianan to get some revenge. Miyagawa: What do you mean you? We are taking him on in a handicap match. I booked it myself. JJ goes pale for a second. JJ: But... but... Im not a wrestler. Miyagawa: You were a wrestler, and you are again now. And so we shall defeat him. JJ: But my handsome face might get damaged. Miyagawa: If it does, then it can only be an improvement. Come, we must go purchase you a suit also, because were going to run Corporate Style all over Cianan. JJ: Doesnt that usually result in someone running us over with a truck. Miyagawa: With the amount of money this company spends on security? Never. And with that, the Corporate duo make their headway to purchase JJ his suit.
