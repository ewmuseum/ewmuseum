>Sidetracked < Part One Written: 1/23/03 This whole PURE business has really been a blessing but yet it is still not enough to fill the plate. There is a more lucrative opportunity out there waiting back home for me that I seem to have forgotten about. But all that was forgotten is now remembered.
The deal was simple: I bring the head of J.T. Thunder to Don Laproni and he will erase half of the price that I owe him. Fair enough.
With that in my head I got the first flight out of Japan back to the States. New York was the destination; one of my top leads was there. I sat on the uncomfortable couch seat while I rested my head back. All I could think about was that glorious match up. Flashes of it at least. The crowd chanting the count, as I lay covered in blood while Thunder had his hand raised. The people being disappointed in my loss and throwing tickets into the rugged ring. Don Laproni walking over my fallen body and promising to make my life a living hell for losing him all that money. I’ve learned over these few months that it wasn’t an empty promise to say the least.
Once the plane ride was over and I had returned to my hometown safely I was so exhausted that all I wanted to do was head to my hotel room and pass out on the bed. Unfortunately that wouldn’t happen.
From this whole experience I was able to find out that sometimes the things we look for and expect to be across the globe are right under our very noses.
That would have to be the situation with the search for J.T. Thunder. I expected to have to look all over the world for his sorry ass but he was right there all this time, waiting for me. Just like I have been waiting for him.
I never worked out how to go about doing this whole operation. It was supposed to be a spur of the moment thing but when the moment comes you blank out completely. You’re whole planning goes to hell as you stand there staring without a thought going through your head.
The decision I make when I come face to face with J.T. Thunder would determine where my life would head. Unfortunately, this is a lose-lose situation for me. I have no way of getting out of this way and being happy with myself. Say I do track him down, I see him, and I draw the gun and shoot him in the head. Let’s say I do all that. Where will that get me? I would be a killer no better than the rest of them. Sure, a half of the sum I owe would be forgotten about but the guilt will stick with me for the rest of my life and there’s still the little matter of the other half.
On the other hand I could not kill him. Maybe I’ll just forget about this whole J.T. Thunder thing and let him live. That’s always an option, right? Then we get kicked right back to square 1, no money in my pocket, owing hundreds of thousands.
Like I said, lose lose situation. How did I find myself in this bittersweet predicament? It wasn’t that long ago that I was a normal guy trying to make a living like everyone else. But now look at me; I have undergone a transformation like no other. There was no way in hell I would have thought this was I if I looked back on the life that I have led.
There are 6 billion people in the world. My task is to reach out and pick just one person from the whole group. Sounds impossible but when I think about it…it wasn’t too hard.
I arrived at my hotel room late at night after my crusade across the world and I remembered what I was able to find out from J.T. Thunder’s promoter before I had left for PURE. He said Thunder was not leaving the United States. Hell, he’s not even leaving the state. I guess that kind of narrows down where I have to look.
Upon walking through the long hallway filled with different rooms at every door I felt a sense of mystery hit me like a speeding train. Something was different then the last time I was here. That was for sure, but what?
A strange noise was coming behind a door that led to my hotel room. It got me wondering who could be behind that door. The first name that ran through my head was Jade Taylor but he was left back in Tokyo to deal with my PURE affairs. So who else could it be? No one else knew where I was staying except for Don Laproni of course. I highly doubt he would be in my room waiting patiently.
Remaining a sense of caution I unlocked the door slowly and quietly while I turned the door know even slower. Then in order to gain a vision of what was inside I pushed the door open notch by notch without seeing a thing no matter how far I opened it. It was very close to fully open already and still a person was not visible. A sweat of worry broke out as I opened the door more and more finding an empty room just like I remembered leaving it. A bed still ruffled from when I took a little rest on it what seemed to be ages ago, the wallpaper loose and dirty with the edges leaning off, and the small bedside table empty with only a telephone on it looking untouched. The only visible difference was that the old brown-framed television was turned on to a movie channel. That was the noise I heard outside of the room and it got louder and louder as I opened the door. Nobody was watching the television and I had no past knowledge of ever turning it on.
Someone was here or worse…someone is here. Ring! Ring! Ring The phone rang off the hook sending a shock to my already worried system. My time was taken picking up the phone, which proved to be a mistake. I put the phone next to my ear and gave the usual greeting pretending nothing is wrong.
“Yeah?” No one answered at first. I felt as if I was a toy being played mind games. Just what the hell is going on here?
“Hello?” … “Hello?” Still receiving no answer I was about to hang up before hearing a fairly loud shout
“Shaun!” a familiar voice called out from the other line. “Jade? That you?” I asked although it was not necessary. I knew my friend and that was definitely he.
“Yeah, you got to get out of the room. It’s not safe. I got a call before from here, there’s no time to explain! Just get out!” Taylor said rather quickly and worried.
“Calm down, what are you talking about? What’s going on?” I asked keeping a calm voice. Before I could hear a reply a hand covered my mouth, keeping me from talking again and a whisper was sent into my ear:
Don’t speak, don’t resist. Participate and you won’t get hurt The phone slipped out of my hands in the process and fell to the floor with Jade still on the other line. I could hear faint yells coming from it for a while…
