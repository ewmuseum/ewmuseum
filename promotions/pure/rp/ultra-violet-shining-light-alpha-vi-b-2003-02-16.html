Ultra Violet Shining Light Alpha- VI B

16 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ultra Violet Shining Light Alpha- VI B Written: Sunday, the 16th of February Narrated by Minori Kumako. Akoshi Kunamara’s feet click against the marble floor of Kunamara House. Maids stare at the little boy they once knew running across the same marble floors as a little kid. Once a young child that was free spirited, and never troubled on in the slightest bit. But now walked a cautious, long red haired man reluctant to be home. While I was taken away by Yukio’s guards, I just wondered what was happening with him. He seemed to be so paranoid about coming back to Japan, and now I know why.
‘Young Akoshi is back?’ whispered one of the maids to one of her co-workers. Her co-worker turned her head, looking up from her mopping duties.
‘Yes,’ the second maid said, slapping the wet mop against her white bucket. ‘I only noticed when Yukio and Chisao were conversing over it and I over heard.’
‘I presume he is not happy at all being home again?’ said the first maid. ‘No,’ replied the maid who was mopping the marble floor. ‘He seems so agitated ever since he returned to Aikawa.’
The two maids looked at each other, brushing a lose strand a hair from their faces. They both watched silently as Akoshi walked back and forth, pondering the millions of thoughts in his confused mind. He stopped pacing as his feet stood in front of a short, red staircase leading to the chambers of Masanori Kunamara’s chambers. Akoshi looked at the red carpet, dreading the flight he will soon have to make up them. One of the maids that were talking just a brief few seconds ago placed her mop against the bucket, she slowly walked over to where Akoshi stood, her hands behind her back.
‘What… what is the matter, Akoshi-san?’ said the maid to Akoshi, who in returned, looked at her oddly. The maid hadn’t spoken to him since he was only sixteen years old, and he had changed so much since then.
‘N-nothing,’ Akoshi said timidly, looking down at the red carpet again. He turned his head to face the maids, eyeing her with suspicion. His brown eyes looked directly into hers, he wanted to tell her how scared he was about talking to Masanori, but Akoshi couldn’t bring himself to. ‘I-I’m just going to s-speak to my f-father, goodbye.’
Akoshi turned his back to the maid, planting his left foot down onto the carpeted staircase. The staircase makes an almost silent creak that echoes throughout the room occupied by the two maids, and Akoshi. Akoshi’s right foot creaked onto the next level of stairs, and then another. Each step made him more intimidated at what was at the top of these stairs. The gold lining on the sides of the carpet kept grabbing Akoshi’s attention, as if they were forcing him to climb another step until he reached the top and his ultimate demon. His father.
‘It will all be finished soon enough,’ Akoshi silently mouthed to himself, ignoring the loud thump of his feet against the carpet. Only half a dozen red and gold steps remained ahead of him, each one scaring him even more than the previous one could ever imagine.
Just above him he could hear Masanori Kunamara’s old, dying raspy voice. Masanori could only speak in short spurts now, having to take a little break in between sentences. Masanori could be heard talking to one of his servants, barking out orders like they were nothing but trash from the poorest areas of Japan.
Akoshi’s foot stood on the final stair, butterflies that were curling around his stomach turned into a sickly green bile trying to force their way up his throat and onto the floor in the most disgusting display of human rejection ever seen. He shook his head, desperately trying to keep the vomit down within his stomach.
He took a deep breath, walking forward as slow as he could. Ahead of him was a curtain, and inside was the old and pathetic Masanori Kunamara. Like almost everything in the main house of on Aikawa Island (the biggest, as well), the curtains were red and gold. On them was printed the head of a Lion, and a Tiger with “Kunamara” written underneath it, in Kanji. The curtain looked so new, as if it was only just made an hour ago. But in this curtain is one of the oldest pieces of clothing on Aikawa Island. Akoshi admired its beauty; the near one hundred-year-old curtain remained perfectly still. His brown eyes looked at the Lion roaring, and the Tiger prowling.
And with that, he parted the curtains and walked through. Immediately Masanori’s head cocked up, to look at his youngest son. Akoshi’s face remained still, trying not to look at his father directly in the eyes. Masanori dismissed the servant, she quickly hide herself in the shadows. His old and tired body remained in his chair, just looking at his youngest offspring.
‘Son,’ Masanori croaked. Akoshi stepped forward, bowing his head. An old family custom that whenever your father addresses you, you bow to him. Akoshi rose from the bow, walking only a mere two inches closer to the feeble old body of his father. ‘Yukio told me that…. You’re back in….. Nihon.’
Akoshi nodded his head. ‘Hai,’ he said, nodding slowly again. Akoshi sat down on one of the small stools in Masanori’s chambers, attentively paying attention to his feeble father. ‘I, I came back to Nihon… to wrestle. It was Minori’s idea.’
‘Who… who is… Minori?’ Masanori asked, confused. ‘Minori, she is to be my wife in the summer,’ Akoshi said, somewhat proudly. ‘We are going to wed in Hawaii.’ He said, watching his father frown immensely. Akoshi went into a hysterical state of unease, his whole body tensed up when Masanori spat a large wad of green phlegm that landed only a mere few millimetres from the servants feet.
‘Wife!?’ Masanori bellowed a jolt of energy bolting through his system. He would have made the attempt to rise from his chair, but the old man was too weak. Akoshi wanted to just run away again, like he had done as a teenager. ‘I.. I thought…. I told…. You…. You…. marry Kaori Kawashima.’ Masanori coughed lightly, not even bothering to cover his mouth at all. Akoshi pursed his lips, as if he was trying to block the germs from Masanori’s mouth invading his own body.
‘But, I love Minori,’ Akoshi said warmly. Masanori glared coldly, his eyes telling the story that he wished one thousand shining blades pierced through my soft skin. Masanori’s eyes still glared, coldly and evilly. ‘I love her with all my heart,’ Akoshi continued to say, desperately trying to not make contact with the black eyes of his father. ‘I never wanted to marry Kaori, father, you wanted me to. You, and her father.’
‘Son,’ Masanori said again to Akoshi, grabbing the attention of my fiancé. Akoshi looked at his father, apprehensive of what he might do or say next. ‘I… know you do… not like… how things…. Work around… here.’ Akoshi nodded slowly, knowing completely what his estranged father was about to ask him. He just dreaded that question, hearing over and over again until he reached a crossroads in his life. Akoshi couldn’t bear to hear his weak, pathetic and dying father ask him the vilest question an unselfish, good person could ever be asked. ‘Please…. Son, I… I am dying. I… want to… give you…. The family.’
Akoshi sprung to his feet. Red flames replaced the brown colour in his eyes, anger flowing in his blood veins all throughout his body. Masanori was slightly started by his fiery young child, not expecting to see his son act this way. Akoshi turned his back immediately, cursing as loud as you never thought he could ever do.
‘Zakenayo!’ Akoshi yelled to his father, making the servant hidden in the shadows shake her mute head. Masanori gasped. Despite the evil old man was not afraid to be clearly as brutal as anyone, he just could not stand any of his children swearing at him or their mother. Masanori tried to stand, but his weak body would not allow him to.
‘Please,’ Masahiro pleaded to his youngest offspring. Akoshi would have none of it, he turned his back to Masahiro, making his father enraged. ‘Son! Please! Just listen…. To me. I… I need… your… help….’
Akoshi just kept his back turned, ignoring Masahiro’s cries for help. He walked through the red and gold curtains, jogging down the red carpeted staircase. The moment he stepped back down onto the marbled floor, I saw him out the corner of my eye. He only just noticed me while Yukio and his guards threw me into Yukio’s black car.
‘Mi… Minori?’ Akoshi said, squinting at the car. That’s all he could say as Yukio slammed his foot down onto the pedal, so hard that I thought he’d kick a hole right through floor of his expensive car. Akoshi’s eyes lit up again, his body broke into a sprint.
‘MINORI!’ he bellowed just as I reached out of his sight.
