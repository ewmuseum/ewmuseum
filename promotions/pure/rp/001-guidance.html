001 - Guidance. - |[ Monday// August 26th, 2002//11:19 P.M. ]| - Our scene begins at first, the seedy reigions of New Orleans, Louisiana, known for its poverty and class rappers coming from there. One of the newest bright stars from the Magnolia happens to be a ESPN poster boy, but more on that later. It was past the late night hour, going down deep into one o'clock. It was only his eyes and the thick brown liquid in the glass. Every moment that he happened to look, there was something going on deep inside. Whether it be by promoters, or even those wanting a sinlge piece of his time, Cordozer Stevenson had his share of things to think about in the Gator Bar, a low class parlor found in the Magnolia district in New Orleans. His light African American complexion made him fit in at some places, most of which made him a popular guest. The brown eyes fixated deep down on the carvings in the bar as a figure came from his back. 'You ready to end this mack?' The heavy and rugged Louisiana accent rolls off of Cordozer's tongue. 'No, I paid for this drink and I'm going to finish it, whether or not you happen to approve.' The figure didn't waste anytime in trying to eat at Cordozer's mind; and body. He was trying to psyche Cordozer out of a fight, but that's what happens when you meet a person like Cordozer. He ends up turning on you in a second. 'Like I said, its time for you to get up and out out here.' Cordozer looked like Vin Diesel, body wise as he wore a white muscle shirt and jeans to match with black Timberland boots to match. 'You want it? Come.' Cordozer wasn't much of an english major, nor did he happen to have one of those known vocabularies. He didn't say all that much except what was needed and relied on strength instead of brains. The figure lunged at him, trying to use upper body mass to send Cordozer to the ground, but the stronger man happens to grasp the figures jacket, lifting him in the air before launching him over his head and the figures crashing into the bar area, glass toppling over him. The object was to prove a point, not to defeat a person for complete fun. Cordozer held his head down, his chin digging deep into his chest and he walked out of the bar - victorious. Not in a sense when it hit the morining papers. The early morning phone call awoke him to the sounds of yelling to the point of which he had not only felt betrayed in essence for happening with the odyssey he was living for the past eight months. Something he didn't think could reach extinction level just yet. The headline read it out to him, 'Stevenson in Brawl, 3 Men Injured.' Sighing to himself, Cordozer only could fear what could happen next, the third time he happened to be apart of a known violent occurrence. He had college, typical of himself to try, and other accomplishments, but as he looked deep down at the picture painted in front of him, he found only one way out. Wrestling. He got up from the pillow and turned over, placing a hand on the phone stationed to his bed and began punching number keys. 451- 9831, the phone began to ring vivdly in his ears and he happened to pick up on the first ring. 'Hello?' Groggy and tired, the voice appeared on the phone. 'Look, it might now help might now, but I'm looking for a Dante Jackson? Are you him?' he questioned. Cordozer's eyes wondered around as he tried to get something out of the predicament. 'I'm him.' the voice straightens up quickly, before Cordozer could even get a nod from his mind, 'So, what do you want out of me?' 'Its..kinda an odd thing to ask, but I need help.' 'Help? I don't deal with people wanting me to help third world countries or major and designer drugs.' Dante told him vehemently stuffing a handful of potato chips into his mouth and crunching loudly. 'Nothing like that. Wrestling is what I need from you.' Cordozer told him with force, the kind you implant on a person to get them to do whatever you want. Dante wasn't feeling the idea. 'Wrestling huh? Who the hell are you to be asking me these types of questions?' Dante shifted around in his room, taking with him a piece of notebook paper and a pair of Nike shoes to walk around in. It was all the same to the thirty four retiree fresh off a ended career with a blaze of glory. He cracked his neck and looked back into the mirror, in front of him a reflection of himself with wrestling gear on. He shakes the feeling out of his mind, before returning to the phone, waiting to see if Cordozer Stevenson was still there, impatiently waiting on hold. 'Cordozer Stevenson.' he repeated to himself. 'That's right, remember I got into it with you over some shit.' Dante's eyes widended for a moment and their scuffle a few months ago flashed before his eyes. The fight, him gaining Stevenson's respect happened to send him on a guilt trip for acting like an asshole. He sighed to himself, before chuckling. 'What gives you the point of calling me?' 'Help, I told you already.' he repeated. 'Kid, I don't give out false answers to people who aren't wanting inside of this game.' Cordozer tapped his fingers along the dresser right next to him. He wanted an opportunity like this and didn't want to waste it. Nobody in his situation ever does. 'Listen, all I want from you is a sign that I will be able to practice under your tutoring.' 'Tutoring? Fuck that, you want in the business.' His eyes shifted away, thinking that this was a truth that Dante was speaking. He stopped rapping along the fine brim of the dresser and spoke on the phone once again. 'Look, I'm not in the game for this shit; and none of your fucking snide remarks.' Cordozer replied as Dante began chuckling to him. 'One with an edge to him; I like. Meet me by the Harbor; 4:13 P.M. You show up 4:14, your ass is left.' The phone went dead as Cordozer rested it upon the handle. Looking at his watch reading 2:26, he hurried out of his apartment complex door, and straight for the harbor. When he got there, it was 4:12, and he happened to sigh in relief of the completion. 'So, you want stardom badly don't you?' Dante retorted bluntly. Cordozer nodded to him. 'Good...' *** -|[ Tuesday// August 27th, 2002//1:36 A.M. ]| - The blinking lights came over him like a blank screen, the moments of which he could not even detect who was after him. There was no light, no gateway to lead him to where he was going, just the things in which he only found resuscitate moments. 'Sleep easy, you've massed enough.' Her voice called out to him in the darkness, leaving him searching for her and wanting to hold her tight. Inside of everything there was nothing but the voices to keep him company in the land of the blind. There was just his exhasperating of breath calling for her name, the silence of everything that lead up to the point in which he could have been something different. 'They want you, they call for you. You've won the titles, the fame accounted for and the lifestyle of the rich and famous.' He heard the over lapping voices as the night was still, silence and movements for salvation had stopped, everything that has led up to this point was all a blur in his position as the day quickly turned to night, the entire forest of satellite antenna and telephone wires were the back drop to everything. He heard the wailing sirens of the poilce, the flashes of blue and red sparking like diamonds. The flames still running rampant through the city of nirvana. 'These want you to confinement; broken into solidarity and hardship.' The constant creaking of different sounds filled his ears as he continually waggered his head like a dog looking and searching for something.The bright lights and the rushing sound of which is a freight train come looming ahead for him. The words painted on the horse of metal and blinking lights: Career Destination. 'This is your path, through the smoke and out of the flame.' He knew who he was, just not of what he was cappable of and now within everything there was this man and whomever what damage he could bring upon Cordozer. The train rushed coming closer and closer waiting for that abrupt moment of impact where Cordozer would only give a stupid look, before his death... It was a scene straight out of the page of a prodigy only months before in the inaugural breath that this fellowship took and all Cordozer did was speak his mind on what was to come, and he did but now he was not one of those dark horse people to break into the game trying to shake it up, he was a favorite. 'Time for your destiny to begin.' The car came inches from his feet and all he could do was try and let out a shriek, a moan for him to just get a voice out but he couldn't. And his eyes opened.
