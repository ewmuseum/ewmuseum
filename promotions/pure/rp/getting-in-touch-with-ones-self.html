Getting In Touch With One's Self...

7 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Getting In Touch With One's Self... 'THWACK!' The sickening sound of leather hitting flesh is heard, followed by a loud scream of anger and pain. The scene opens up to reveal Jordan X Shabazz standing tall, his face is one etched with pain. A large man wearing some gray sweat pants and a gray t-shirt stands before Jordan X Shabazz. He's the same man as we saw in the last Jordan X Shabazz special. The look on his face is one of happiness. Sweat drips down Jordan's body. The large man rears back again as Jordan readies himself... 'THWACK!' Again, more screaming. But following the screaming is the sound of a 228-pound man collapsing to the floor. 'Sorry Shabazz-san,' Ken Ishii, Jordan's Japanese trainer, tells Jordan. 'Sometimes I get carried away.... But that's what it's really like in the ring here in Japan, they don't let up, no matter how much you get hurt.' Jordan X Shabazz doesn't speak a word, he just gets to his feet and limps over to the corner of the wrestling ring that the two men stand in. Speaking of which, the place they are is a dingy little gym. Something you'd see in one of those tough guy-boxing movies like Rocky or Raging Bull. There's some weight training bags, some weights, and a ring. The ring doesn't look to be in good shape, and whenever Ken Ishii takes a step, you can hear the ring creak along with the large 6'9, 330 pound personal trainer. He had been housing Jordan for everyday that Jordan stayed in Japan. Which wasn't many a week. Everyday that Jordan got off the PCW he spent some time in his office at the NAACP offices in Dallas, Texas. But now, Jordan must work from his laptop computer. The training in preparation for his Japanese Wrestling debut was intense. 'Ishii-san, when do we stop training?' Jordan asks tiredly. 'When you prove to me that you are ready for Ikusa,' Ishii replies with no change in emotion. Jordan doesn't reply, he just returns to his feet and awaits another thwacking from the large Japanese man... But instead... He gets nothing. Ken Ishii nods at Jordan and he points towards the door. 'Shabazz-san, now we get you in the right mind state yes?' 'Bah, what am I going to say? Of course yes Ken, of course.' Fade Out. ******************************** The scene reopens in what appears to be nothing more than a simple temple. We see a few people praying to Buddha, or whatever God they worship in this particular temple. Jordan X Shabazz dressed in baggy blue jeans, a white And 1 basketball t-shirt, some white Nike Cross Trainers, and a black Toronto Blue Jays Baseball cap. Jordan sits cross-legged next to his trainer, Ken Ishii, who is silently humming. 'Ahummmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmm,' Ishii begins. 'Ahummmmm.' Jordan looks around and notices the entire room is now doing that... Then suddenly... They all stop. 'Okay Shabazz-san, time to get in touch with our inner being.' Ishii says while bouncing up to his feet. 'Inner being?' Jordan asks incredulously. 'Indeed. Now get up.' Jordan gets to his feet and smiles. He can't help but feel silly. He does a silent prayer to the God Of Crunkness in his mind for having no white people in the room with him.... But there's Japs... Lots and lots of Japanese men. Some old, some young, but they're all speaking silent words to themselves. 'I am a great person, I am a talented being, I am an intelligent master...' the men whisper to themselves as Jordan tunes them out. 'Thank God there's no white people here, thank God there's no fake gangsta's like Ali Khadafi, thank God that they don't notice I'm the only brotha here...' Jordan begins. Fade. ************************* The physical and mental training continues as the scene opens up yet again in the dojo of the great Inphino Blitz. HCW Legend, PCW Legend, a true wrestling legend. We see Jordan X Shabazz standing with his personal Japanese trainer, the large and very violent, Ken Ishii. Inphino Blitz stands, leaning on a wall of baseball 8 X 10's. Jordan stares at Inphino in awe, you'll never see Jordan in awe of somebody... Especially somebody that isn't black. But Jordan, once wrestled in the same organization as Inphino, a small Indy federation called the HCW. When Jordan was there, Inphino was World Champion, Jordan met him once briefly in the locker room where Jordan, like the true mark that he is got Inphino's autograph. Inphino looks really different now. He's more... physically in shape. 'Damn crackas, thank God I haven't seen one since coming to this God forsaken country.' Jordan says aloud to break the silence between the three people. Inphino Blitz slowly walks over to Jordan, no change of expression on his face. 'Hating one kind of man is just as equal as hating all mankind,' Inphino begins. 'They bleed...they cry...they laugh...just as you and I do...' Jordan looks around, not knowing exactly what to say. 'Crazy ass white people eh Ken?' Jordan whispers to Ken. Ken slowly moves away from Jordan, as if to disassociate himself from him. 'All you see is the exterior, when it's the interior that matters more,' Inphino says to Jordan. 'This dwelling is a neutral zone, where harmony can be achieved and fighting techniques can be nurtured into mastered skills. You...will not disgrace this place with your racist hatred.' Inphino finishes. Jordan wipes some sweat from his forehead and he looks away from Inphino, it's not that he's scared, he just isn't used to the this type of man... All...Deep and stuff. 'Erm... Racist?' Inphino walks away from Jordan as Jordan screams out one question. 'Instead of staying with Ken, can I stay her with you and Mang-A-Hero?' 'Only if you promise to train with us every morning at sunrise.' Inphino replies. 'Sunrise? Is this nigga crazy?' Jordan asks Ken. Ken doesn't reply. He just shakes his head, almost as if he's ashamed of Jordan. 'Oh, yeah, no racism. Sorry about that... Sure, I'll wake up every morning at sunrise to train.' 'Good.... Welcome to my domain.' Fade To Black.
