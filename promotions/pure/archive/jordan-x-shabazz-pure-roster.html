Bio

The man who goes by the name of Jordan X Shabazz was actually born Jordan Andrew Copeland. his middle name is taken from his older brother Andrew Copeland, who wrestles in the OSW under the name of Drew Hostyle. Jordan was born in Brooklyn, New York, but after his father left as a child, he was forced to be put up for adoption. He was adopted by a white family in Toronto, Ontario Canada. And Jordan lived in Canada for most of his life. He got good grades and was a very respectful young man. When Jordan graduated from High School, his adopted parents sent him away to The University Of Toronto, perhaps the best University in all of Canada. Jordan studied Broadcast Journalism and graduated with honors. After graduating from University, Jordan took all the money he had saved up from working in a Nestle chocolate Factory, and he moved out on his own. He was at a lost. He didn't know what to do with his life, he got a job with a local radio station called Kiss 92 FM. After spending a few months there, Jordan realized he wasn't liked by some of the people working there. After getting into an altercation with one of the DJs Jordan physically assualted him and accidently killed him. They tried Jordan for involuntary Manslaughter. That's where he met his friend, and current manager, Jake Jackson. Jordan was facing a life sentence in prison, but he convinced his manager that it was Self-Defense, and Jake Jackson, used his brain, and he made sure Jordan got off by paying some of the jury members. Jordan walked out of court a free man, and that began the bond that those two men share to this very day. After getting out of court, and starting over again with a clean slate, Jordan decided to move back to New York. This time not in Brooklyn, but in Queens. After seeing his brother Drew Hostyle for the first time in over 15 years, the two men shared a bond. Drew was a professional wrestler, and he encouraged Jordan to join the profession. Jordan did. Jordan was trained by legendary wrestling superstar Johnny Rodz. Jordan learned all the tricks and secrets of wrestling. Jordan begin travelling all across America, Canada, Japan, Europe, and Mexico wrestling for very little money. After a wrestling federation he wrestled for closed down in Japan, Jordan moved back over to America, and he got involved in the American wrestling scene. After taking some advice from Drew Hostyle, Jordan went in the Primetime Wrestling Academy and he bettered himself as a wrestling superstar.


After spending a month and a half in the Academy, Jordan got promoted to the NTICW. In the NTICW, Jordan started off as a nobody, he wasn't known, and he wasn't given his just desserts. Then, one day, he was listening to his radio, and he heard The Phantom Booker on PTC Malice. Jordan called in, and the rest was history. Jordan screamed, ranted, and raved, then he used his radio show popularity to ride his way through the NTICW. Jordan is a regular guest of the Phantom Booker and Brain Chapman on the PTC's Sunday radio show.


While in the NTICW, Jordan feuded originally with Mark Makonie. The two men engaged in an intense feud which included such gimmick matches as a Billy Club match, a no DQ match, and burn the flag match. The latter match was the last match Jordan ever had in the NTICW, he was defending his Extreme Championship against Makonie. In a brutal 30 minute classic, Jordan prevailed, defeating Mark Makonie for a second time, while losing zero times to Makonie. That ended their feud, and Jordan's career in the NTICW.


The next week, Jordan called into the PTC Malice show like normal, and he begged Xavier Kannon for a job on air. He smothered Xavier with compliments, and after making Jordan look like an ass. He was given a chance to become Xavier Kannon's lackey.


Jordan was supposed to lick somebody's ass for the PCW contract, he was willing to do it, until he realized that the person who's ass he was supposed to kiss was Neo Nazi, and black man hater, Mark Makonie. Jordan immediately refused, which was going to enduce a beating he wouldn't soon forget...But, thankfully, Duke Williams came out to make the save. After Duke and Jordan cleaned house, Duke offered Jordan a chance to become a member of the Malice Crew, Jordan immediately accepted and joined Duke Williams, Rush Mason, Jason Core, and Legend Inc. in that stable.


Jordan originally wrestled under the name of Jordan Crazy, but he changed his name to be one with himself, and to remember his African roots.


Jordan became a Civil Rights Extremist after being stopped in Miami, Florida for a traffic violation. Jordan felt that the stop was racially motivated, so he began to fight for his people, and a hatred for racist white people developed.


Jordan recently moved to Dallas, Texas to get away from what he thought of 'The Ghetto.'

Jordan decided to try his luck in PURE after watching a match between The Great Muta and Jushin Lyger.


Jordan recently started his own Civil Rights Organization called N.I.G.G.A, Negores International Growing Group Association.
