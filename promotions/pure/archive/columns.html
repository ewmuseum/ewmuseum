Friday, May 10, 2002 Tecno #1 5/10/02 Hello to you all here in Japan. My name is Tecno, and I am Yuka Hamada's manager. Yuka as in the Porno star Yuka. Yep, and not only that: but she's PURE's first woman. So, she has to set a benchmark for many young women out there. Quite a feat to achive, but she's already got the lesbien-thing down. Is it me, or are most of the Puroseu women chubby-porky lesbiens? I guess it's my western culture upbringing. Anyway, I am here to write about our various adventures in Japan. And what a country I must say! Even though the toilets are wierd to work out (it's quite a shock to have perfumed water sprayed up your anus), Japan is just such a great country. Everybodies just so calm about life. Well, most people. You get the usual freaky business men rushing down Tokyo's business district looking as if Godzilla's after them, but you get that anywhere. The thing I am impressed with the most though is my Japanese! For learning from the internet, it sure is good! But, let me take you guys back to a couple of days ago... I had just reached the airport. I'd been in the US in recent weeks sorting out some stuff at the soon-to-be-deceased EWA. Yeah, EWA doesn't have much of a future. I am looking to buy it out though, at least for a storyline I have in mind... I also joined EWC with Carnage (were on seperate shows). So, I got to Tokyo Airport. Yuka was waiting for me, typically dressed just like that fit secretary in the office you wished you could have over the desk. It's something of an art for her to do that. Anyway. We said our hello's and everything, and as we were leaving the airport I quickly caught a newspaper. In Japanese as well (^_^). As we got into the cab, I quickly noticed something... "Hey! Yuka... who's this guy?" "I don't know. He looks familiar..." "That is Kentai Kobashi. He announces Isuki for PURE." said the cab driver in his very hesitant English. "Oh yeah! Why is he in the paper?" "It says he's eating raw gorillas!?!" "Give it here..." Obviously my Japanese is not as brilliant as I thought it was. "He's just talking about some people in PURE... and me." "Well that's great!" Not a smile on her face. "Isn't it?" Obviously not. "Not really, because he's ridiculing me." "Well you ARE a porn star..." "FORMER Porn Star, and what kinda excuse is that?" "You've got to expect this kind of thing. Puroesu is a noble form of wrestling. It has a lot of history and standards. It's not to be laughed at." Which is the point I'm trying to make here. Even though having a former Porn star is quite contraversial, it's not a bad thing. I know there are some who think it will bring down the nature of Puroesu, but I don't think it will. I think it adds a flavor to PURE. Not only is she contraversial, but she is PURE's first woman. That is something else! People must realize that she may be a little... different, but remember: difference is good. As DDP would say in America: "That's not a bad thing... that's a good thing!" I'm confident she will rock your world! I mean... PURE Jr. Heavyweight Champion Yuka Hamada!!! Doesn't it sound nice? Give her a chance. I did, and I am the best darn guy in the world! +++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++ Many people have constantly asked me what my relationship to Inphino Blitz is. Well, I can asure you it isn't anything "naughty". Read the sentance and you'll understand. I am only related to Inphino in that I worked with him in HCW. And while we never worked together that much (just the one time actually), I've seen his "friend" Akiyama in HWA, as well as some tapes of the pair from Japan. I've always had a respect for Inphino not just because of his amazing fighting style, but also because of how he approaches life. He treats everything he does as a battle that he must win. He lives on competition, and that is something I can't help but be amazed by. Take this example. We were at a HCW House Show, and I went to get a Pepsi. I was, of course, HCW Internet Champion by then, so people knew me. I just got it, when Inphino showed up to buy himself one. "Hey." "Yo." "How r u?" "sup?" "er... nothing. u?" "Just getting a drink... good crowd tonight, huh?" "Yeah, yeah... really excited." "Yeah..." It was at this point that he began drinking his can. After he drank it, he began to squeeze it. I figured it was normal, but then after about a minute he was still doing it. I figured he must be stressful, but he certainly didn't look stressed. In fact, he looked very calm and at peace. It wasn't until he began to bleed that he stopped. The can had all but disappeared by now, a former shell of a can. He then proceeded to place the can in his bag. "Er... there's a rubbish bin over there ya know. And why did you squeeze the can for so long? You're bleeding now!" "I know, but if I had just put the can in the bin, then it would of continued to mock me. This way, I've drank it's essence... and can lay claim to it for all eternity." "Right..." For a while, I presumed he was crazy. But recently, I've come to understand what he meant. Everything in life is out to beat him. By showing his dominance over the can, he is showing his dominance not only to life, but me as well. It is for that, in which I am in awe of him for. The man is just so complicated to understand. But if you take the time to understand, he's an easy guy to comprehend. That is what amazes me. Until next time... TECNO - Yuka's the next big thing!!! Thursday, May 02, 2002 The Quest #1 5/02/02 Hi to all the PURE fans out there in PTC! I’d like to thank you all for reading this, the first ever column of Puroresu Ultimate Ring Entertainment! My name is Yuri “Quest” Takayama, PURE reporter and Japan’s #1 freelance reporter! During my stay here, I’ll be providing you with the latest in PURE news, rumors, and information. Let’s kick things off with an analysis of the PURE title divisions. PURE Heavyweight World Title: This division looks to be filled with solid competitors. In the top two slots we have Kensuke Suzahara and Alexander Miyagawa. These two have been locked in a grudging inter-fed feud for what seems like forever. No doubt though that their animosity for each other will be just the flagship championship needs. This is a true battle of good versus evil, and after the actions of Suzahara on the first Ikusa, he’s shown that his intentions in PURE are “purely” evil. Ebola III, Cege Hayabusa, and The HIT hold the last three positions in this division. All three of these men have shown excellent efforts in the debut of PURE. Especially from The HIT, who excelled to the finals of the PURE JR. Heavyweight title tournament along with Kensuke Suzahara and The End. PURE fans should expect nothing but good things from this division as of this point. PURE Asian National Title: In my personal opinion, this division belongs to one man…INPHINO BLITZ! Yes, I may be biased because I used to work with him in Tokyo Wrestling League, but there are three reasons why I say this. 1): He is the number one contender for this belt. 2): Out of the other competitors, he has been the sole competitor to show tremendous effort and determination to make PURE all it can be. 3): He totally encompasses the meaning of PURE and what puroresu is this day. Say what you want, but the Black Dragon Master seems to be destined to be the Asian National Champion, and that is nothing but an honor for Japan. Don’t get me wrong; the other competitors are talented as well. Shirata Rinjiro, Masanobu Ozaki, Steven Windsor, and Brian Simon are all great in their own ways. But I think in my opinion it’s a bit of an oxymoron to have a gaijin as the Asian National Champion. But we shouldn’t judge them by their nationality or race, because that’s not the PURE thing to do. In the future, I see newcomer Ali Khadaffi rising up in this division to challenge the Black Dragon Master. After his street-style attack on Blitz on the first Ikusa, we can see yet another battle of good versus evil here. PURE JR Heavyweight Title: This is the third out of four divisions that is being led by a PCW/Ex-PCW wrestler. The enigmatic The End, talented, mysterious, and number one in the division. This blind man claims to be half Japanese, so it could be said that all four belts have Japanese division leaders. It was his excellent record in EW that brought about his known status, sending him to PCW and eventually PURE. From his appearance, he seems ninja-like, but from his actions, he is like a samurai, possessing his own code or morals to abide by…such as Inphino Blitz. I see good things from this man in the future. Following up in the rankings are The HIT, IWO superstar known for his extreme love of dangerous matches and mathematics. He is always a force to watch for. Kensuke, as always, is a threat, but he may be set his sights on the bigger prize of Heavyweight Championship rather than the Jr. Heavyweight title. You never know…he may turn greedy and think of himself worthy of TWO championships. Ebola III, always the skilled opponent, and Billy Larkin, who has shown less than desired efforts inside and outside the ring. PURE Tag Team Titles: PURE’s leading tag team unit, Team Okiki, sets the pace for this division. Taka and Sakato Nazaki have been one of PURE’s better recruitments along with Mang-A-Hero, with no doubt more on the way after Ikusa II. With the PURE Tag Team Title Tournament on the rise, we’re bound to see these brothers from Tokyo shine. In this division we see the likes of Cege Hayabusa/ Hiro Saotome, the gaijin team of Steven Windsor/Brian Simon, the team of Ebola III and PURE Owner Alexander Miyagawa, and ranking fifth, the fan favorites, Inphino Blitz and Mang-A-Hero. In this tournament, expect nothing less but the best from Inphino Blitz and Mang-A-Hero. In the finals of this tournament, I expect to see Inphino/MAH facing off against Team Okiki, and if not them, then Ebola III and Miyagawa against Team Okiki. Either way, the final match will definitely be a match to remember. -*-*-*- To close this column out, I will run off a list of news and rumors that I have acquired through some extensive research and sleuthing. Some of the following facts may be true, while others may not…you make the decision! • It’s been said that Cianan, Kensuke Suzahara’s mortal enemy, will be arriving in PURE soon to stir up trouble for the superstar. • Word backstage says that PCW former champion Chris Sloboda, AKA Boda, will be showing up at a PURE event to further “punk out” PURE Owner Alexander Miyagawa. • After this Ikusa II, Cege Hayabusa has asked for his release, since his partner Jonathan Drachen, has left the company. • Developmental deals have been signed with The Sushi Crew, Yama Tatsu, “The Asian Dream” Toko Asi, The Price Brothers, and Lucha Libre stars El Lunes Mascara and El Spiffy. • A puro women’s division is in the works for PURE, which would include competition from such female wrestlers such as Inphino Blitz’s manager Mina De Margo, OSW Queen of Extreme competitors Bethany Yamoto and Brandi Wilson, PWA trainees Jaden and Heaven, HATE’s Sassy, RSW’s Teresa Tomes, and CCW’s Angelica Lynn. • PCW and OCW super-heavyweights Akiyama and Kimbusa are expected to sign within the weeks leading up to Ikusa III, along with PCW/OSW additions to the JR Heavyweight division of Great Dragon, El Drago, and El Diablo. • Tiger Akamu has been reported having substance abuse problems behind the scenes. PURE Owner A. Miyagawa has ordered him to seek medical attention and counseling for his problem, in hopes that he can fight his addiction and return to action better than before. • The HIT’s archenemy, Sam Potright of IWO, was seen in the audience at Ikusa during The HIT’s match. • Anime and Manga genius Masamune Shirow, creator of masterpieces such as “Ghost in the Shell”, “Appleseed”, “Dominion” and “Orion”, has shown interest in producing works based on the adventures of Mang-A-Hero and Inphino Blitz. It was said that Mang-A-Hero would be immortalized in Anime film alone, and Inphino Blitz would get a biweekly graphic novel deal solely with a future option to expand into animation. No word from the tag team as of yet on if they will accept or not. • Kensuke Suzahara tried out for spring training with the Osaka Buffalos this April. A representative from the baseball team said that he “apparently has a natural speed and can hit for average, and was talked about making the roster as a walk on backup centerfielder.” Other reports say he was holding out for an offer from the New York Yankees and would not sign a contract. A direct quote from George Steinbrenner, owner of the Yankees, states: “Who?” • The mysterious sponsor of PURE, Maxis Inc, has made announcements to unveil their spokesperson to Japan soon at a PURE event. Mori Morishigi had nothing to say at the mention of the possibly rival company. • Word around town says that there is a union forming between the “Elements” of PURE that could very involve a “dragon” …more information later on what could possibly be PURE’s first stable. -*-*-*- That’s all for now, fans! See you next time! Your freelance reporter, Yuri “Quest” Takayama XOXOX Wednesday, March 20, 2002 Testing Columns 3/20/02 Test
