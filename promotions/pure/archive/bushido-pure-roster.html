Description:

Bushido is a small man, even for his ethnicity. The only part of him that appears to be muscular is his chiseled stomach and chest. His arms and shoulders appear of near normal size. His lower body is covered by baggy leather pants(i.e Tajiri) with red characters going up the right pant leg. His feet are covered by normal wrestling boots. His hair is clean shaven and jet black. He has only small remniscence of a goatee on his chin and no other facial hair. He wears a white band across his forehead to symbolize his training process.
