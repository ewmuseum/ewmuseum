Entrance Music: 'Dragon Slayer' by Anders Lundqvist

“Dragon Slayer” plays as the arena begins flashing in black and dark blue lights. Smoke fills the entrance way as Inphino Blitz and Mang-A-Hero step through the fog. Inphino cracks his joints as MAH strikes several martial arts poses. Then the duo runs towards the ring, where they slide in and mount the turnbuckles, MAH raising his hands and yelling “GO GO TOKYO!!” while Blitz raises his hands at his face and roars to hype up the fans.


Status: Face Manager(s): Mina de Margo (see Inphino Blitz bio for details)

Tag Finisher: 1) The BREAKING POINT O DOOM~! (Finisher A- Impact)

2) Thousand Arms (Finisher B- Submission)

3) Dragon Dive Z(Finisher C- Aerial)

(1) Inphino blitzes the opponent with the Shadow Madness, and when he fakes to kick the opponent in his face, the opponent holds his face to protect himself. Inphino shakes his head and steps away as Mang-A-Hero steps up and holds out his hand as if he were charging up his Manga powers. Then when the opponent looks up from his hands, MAH delivers the FINGERPOKE O DOOM~!, sending the opponent down to the mat.

(2) A maneuver where the opponent is running towards MAH and Blitz. The Dragons Deuce reach out and drive the opponent face down into the mat while applying reverse armbars to his limbs.

(3) Inphino nails his Ya Sakazuki finisher from the top rope and rolls towards the adjacent corner. He sits in the corner below as MAH stands above him on that same corner and leaps off with the Dragonball Xtacy Bomb.)

Mang-A-Hero

Shikima’s Craft: MAH and Blitz both leap off the turnbuckle, and MAH performs a Shooting Star Press while Inphino performs a Shooting Star Leg Drop onto the throat of the opponent.

Shinku-Sado/Mado-Hadoken: Inphino begins his Sado/Mado maneuver, but after he completes the Reverse Jackknife Powerbomb and he picks the victim back up for the Sitdown Electric Chair Drop, Mang-A-Hero grabs the opponent’s head and delivers a joint Sitdown Facebuster/Electric Face Drop onto the opponent.

Destiny...Illusion...Sincerity...Duplicity...: Inphino’s Sukumei...Gen’ei...Shinshi move, but with MAH joining in as well simultaneously. Therefore it’s two strong thrust elbows to the sternum, followed by two reverse sweeps to the legs, and concluding with two sharp snapping kicks to the face of the opponent.

(Set Up) Manga½ : A double superkick from the Blitz and MAH, followed by a duo pose where Blitz and MAH strike a martial arts pose together, throwing their hands back and then forward towards the opponent, via Jinsei Shinsaki/Hayabusa style.

Spirit Bomb: Mang-A-Hero nails an opponent with a running hurricanrana while Inphino stands behind him. As MAH flips the opponent over, Inphino grabs the opponent’s legs on his shoulders and pounds him with a powerbomb.
