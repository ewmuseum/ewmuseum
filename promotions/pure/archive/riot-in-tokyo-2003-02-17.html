Riot in Tokyo

17 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
On Thursday night in Tokyo riot police marched in after a group of Radical PURE fans started throwing rocks at the office of Jamar Wilson. Prior to the attack these Radicals were protesting the number of contracts given to Gaijin wrestlers in PURE. We had a word with a few of these supporters.

“PURE has taken all the Japanese tradition it had and just flushed it down the toilet. I, as a long time fan, can not sit around and see another American take jobs from hard working Japanese talent.”

Jamar Wilson is yet to speak thoroughly on this issue, but some of the Radicals were taken away in cuffs. Word is PURE is going to respond to this movement with a change within it’s hiring policy. Most of the men and women we spoke to in Tokyo were in fact pleased with the way PURE has been run thus far. All Jamar Wilson had to tell us was.

“We here at PURE will work hard to please everyone.”
