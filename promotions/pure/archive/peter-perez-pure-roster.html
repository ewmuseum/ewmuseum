Bio

Peter's mother was Irish, and his father Mexican. They were however raised in the Bronx. They never had much, except a warm loving family. Being Roman catholics, they had nine children. Peter was the third youngest, and was also the favourite. He was always extremely smart though never got a chance at college due to his families 'tight budget'.


Peter had another talent however, fighting. He was extremely skilled at fighting and as well as being strong he was fast and skillful.


Tragedy struck the Perez family as a whole during the heated Harlem vs. Bronx war. Most of the Perez family were involved as all Peter's cousins, uncles, aunts and other relations lived in the area.


Though this war grew to more and soon the Perez family owed New York's biggest gangs alot of money, more than they could imagine. They were all kicked out of their homes. They had to live in an alleyway with only a small warehouse room for shelter. All 47 of the Perez family lived there. They deperately tried to pay off their debts, thought everything they got was only a tiny fraction of what they owed.


Peter's brothers and cousins then began fighting for money, they were all very good and brought home alot of money to the family, though still not nearly enough. That gangs had started using huge interest rates.


As soon as he was 14, Peter started fighting for money too. He was exceptional, he never even lost until he was 16, when the competition got tougher, he rarely got marks due to his agility and speed. Be then entered bigger fights and won more money.


Soon he started Underground tournaments where the prizes were up to $250,000! Though still only asmall fraction of what his family owed. He then started joining league's and clubs but still he was not making enough! Then, he saw that a new indy wrestling promotion running out of New Jersey, which was not far from Bus, was starting up, he knew he could make alot of money is a place like that. So he applied, praying he would be good enough. And, he was. Well, almost. He was sent to PWC's affiliate promotion in Japan for further evaluation. He knew what he was up against, and knew that he must fight, and win.
