This posting's own subject line reads “PURE Ikusa from Budokan Hall in Tokyo, Japan on March 9, 2003” — a stale header carried over from a prior use of this board slot. Its own body text below previews a different show: every wrestler and angle thread it names (Itagaki Freeze, Eddie Mandrake, the Sushi Crew, Radiant, the All-Asia title question involving Raizo Tenzan and Mina de Margo, the Yuka Hamada/Hoshi Akagawa angle) is confirmed present on PURE's Ikusa from Tosu Stadium, 9 February 2003 (see that show's own record and its evidence[]); none belongs to a 2002 PURE show.

PURE:Japan
"The Tamashii Tour"

Deadline is late Wednesday Night!

Tamashii...Spirit. That's what this tour is all about. Fighting spirit, the spirit to grow, the spirit to learn, the spirit to live...there's a spirit in everything we do. PURE: Japan will show the people of Japan the meaning of spirit through it's regional tour of Japan.

The first show of PURE:Japan is promised to be laced with the traditional Puroresu action that the fans have been craving for since PURE reopened. Wrestlers said to step into the ring are the newly hired Itagaki Freeze, Eddie Mandrake, The Sushi Crew, Radiant, and El Mesias!

Also to be expected

-Mina de Margo is said to be making an announcement regarding the PURE All Asia championship. What position will the current champ, Raizo Tenzan, play, and what will the Saikyo System have to say about this?

-Word backstage says that Mina de Margo has also signed a mysterious new talent to push talent levels in PURE:Japan, that few have seen lurking around backstage. Who is this mysterious new wrestler, and what mark will they make on PURE:Japan?

-Yuka Hamada, veteran of the first PURE incarnation as well as "the big screen" will be at the show, and says that she'll be having a few words with Hoshi Akagawa in a more...personal matter.
