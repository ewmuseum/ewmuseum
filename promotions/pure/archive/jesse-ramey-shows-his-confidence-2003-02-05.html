Jesse Ramey Shows His Confidence

5 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The word going around the backstage area of PURE is that Jesse Ramey was very pleased with the ending of his match this week against "Sinister" Steven Lee. The backstage workers had said his attitude since the opening of the promotion had changed drastically.

"Mr. Ramey was in a bit of a slump there for a while during the past few weeks. He seemed to be really upset because of the way Steven Lee beat him on the first return Ikusa." Said an anonymous backstage worker, "Then directly after he beat Lee he came into that backstage area and I saw a smile on his face. That was something I hadn't seen since the day he joined with us. I was truly amazed and am thrilled knowing that one of our top talents is happy again."

These comments were taken from Jesse's stepdaughter, Michelle-Marie Ramey who has been taking more of a financial standpoint in Jesse's career, "Dad didn't seem too happy with the way Lee won that match on Ikusa one. I was probably one of the people who talked to him the most and I've been around him for the longest time. I definitely know how is emotions work, he was outraged by the way things went down and he got his revenge."

Word is that Jesse doesn't plan on letting his winning streak over Steven Lee end with that one show as he goes into Ikusa IV to fight Lee along with several other men in an awesome looking 10 Man Tag Team Main Event match. Check out Ikusa IV for more of an update on their situation and we'll continue bringing you more word here on the newswire as it comes off the presses.
