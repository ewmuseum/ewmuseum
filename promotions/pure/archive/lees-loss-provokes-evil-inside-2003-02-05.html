Lee's Loss Provokes Evil Inside

5 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Blood, sweat, and respect were all seen a few nights ago in the Tokyo Street Fight that Steven Lee, and Jesse Ramey found themseles in. The tough loss that Lee took, however, wasn't a depressing moment for him. "All it's done is entisify my hate for the scum!" Lee stated towards Ramey.

Shorty after the match was finished, Lee was approached by many people backstage, trying to ask him questions. No comments where given, instead a few punches were thrown, as the provoked evil inside of Steven Lee started to show not long after the loss.

For the past few days, Lee has taken refuge in his Tokyo hotel room, and we have only gotten a few words with him about the situation. We do know that he has plans for getting his revenge before the PPV, according to Yoshikiro. What the revenge is, nobody will know.

"His happiness won't last for long, I'll make sure of it." Lee stated in response to an article read about the PURE superstar, and wrestling veteran Ramey. And if all goes according to Lee's plan, then we should see some fireworks on the 9th.
