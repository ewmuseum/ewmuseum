Entrance Music: The lights all-lower in the arena, and the fans immediately go into applause and cheer. They all clap their hands in unison for the entrance of Inphino Blitz. Their cheers lower as silence fills the arena. Then from out of nowhere, a mellow beat plays through the speakers, blue lights flashing moderately to the music. Then suddenly, smoke fills the entrance way as hard guitars roar out, and Dragon Ash’s “Glory” breaks into full beat. From out of the smoke walks Inphino Blitz, with Mina de Margo by his side. The people all stand to their feet and cheer as he bows his head, his hand going up in a single palm prayer position. Then he and Mina dash towards the ring, sliding in and climbing up on the turnbuckles. Mina de Margo blows a kiss to the fans as Inphino claws his hands at his face and roars at the people. Mina stands by Inphino as he comes down, and the two of them stare at their opponent(s) across from them. After introduction, Blitz steps forward and bows his head as the entire arena calls out “BLITZ!!” Mina de Margo takes Blitz’s coat and sunglasses and steps out of the ring after tapping knuckles with him.
Wrestling Style: 'Kin Kyu Hyaku Kyu Ju Ku Shiki', or the Forbidden 999 Style. It's an unorthodox method of battle that has been developed by Inphino through his relentless training. Training in this style left Inphino amazingly agile and swift, his martial arts talent only becoming more sharp to strike and heavy in defense. His agility allows him the capability to flip back and forward out of suplexes of various types, as well as flip out of DDTs and twist and turn his body in various ways to avoid and/or aim in mid air. The stylish, 'Urban Gothic/industrial' Japanese movement of the same name was roughly based from his unique methods and motives.

==4== Koto Zuki You--- (Koto Moon Positive): An inverted Fisherman Buster. Bridging is optional.

==5==Orochi Nagi---(Mowing Serpent): A running attack where Inphino comes with a spinning wheel kick, but uses his left leg to kick at the back of the knees of the opponent. Therefore, both kicks force the opponent down on the mat, folding him so that his legs are swept from under him and his head bounces off the mat at the end of the kick.

==6== Sanrai No Fujin---(Three Sided Defense): An attack set where the opponent throws a punch, but Inphino blocks and delivers a qucik chop to the face. The opponent kicks at him, but this time Inphino grabs the leg, steps over with his leg, and delivers a spinning heel kick to the face, yelling out 'Yoshaa!' (I did it!').

==7==Rainbow Knocker: One of Inphino's unorthodox 'Forbidden 999' style maneuvers where he turns his back on the opponent, bends back, and grabs his opponent's throat. Then he flips back and hits him in the bridge of the nose with a flipping back kick.

==8==Rewind: (Reverse Rana) Inphino leaps on the foe's shoulders from behind and flips back like he was doing a normal Hurricanrana. The opponent flips backwards onto the back of his head and folds over with his stomach in his face.

==9==Last Retort: The opponent whips Inphino into the ropes. He bounces back and Kung Fu Rolls over the opponent. When the foe turns around, Inphino moonsaults over the opponent, grabs his head in mid air, and drives him down with a diving reverse DDT.

==10==Black Dragon Wave: Inphino runs at the opponent inside the ring. Then he leaps up in a front flip and smashes into the upper body of the foe in a Swanton Vertical Splash. Bio

Inphino Blitz is a well defined martial artist usually seen wearing black martial arts slacks, black boots, and a tight black spandex shirt that covers most of his neck. His shoulder length hair is dark blue and black, and usually hangs over his face slightly, covering a portion of it, but he randomly tosses in streaks of color given the mood. Often he is seen wearing a black cloak around his shoulders. His eyes are dark gray, and his handsome face is usually solemn and brooding. His nails are painted black.


Inphino is a wandering loner martial artist with a mysterious background and a strive to train and enhance his fighting technique. He's one of the breakthrough Japanese figures to introduce the 'Kin Kyu Hyaku Kyu Ju Ku Shiki' or the 'Forbidden 999 Style' into the Japanese culture. The Forbidden 999 Style, named for Inphino's martial arts form, is the equivalent to the Gothic movement over in the states.


There have been many tales and stories telling about the Black Dragon Master. But there has yet to be any opportunity or chance to ask him if any of them are true. Inphino was one who grew up outside of modern society, dwelling in a secluded dojo until he was eighteen. Then from there he traveled the earth for battle, gaining many accolades as well as nurturing and developing his Forbidden 999 Style. Then he found Primetime Wrestling Academy, an institute of training in the US. After a few days, he was brought into Primetime Championship Wrestling alongside Ketsueki Karasu to form Hyakuhei. Later in his three-month career, he teamed with Miyagawa, Kensuke Suzahara, and Ming to form the Elements. In three months, he elevated from tag team specialist to World title contender through the 64-man tournament, the quickest rise through the rankings for any wrestler in PCW history. But then tragedy struck...


Inphino was forced out of his title run as well as his position in PCW by one man...

Akira Akiyama...

Akiyama had devised a scheme to lure Inphino Blitz back to Japan, where the dark forces called the Void Elements were there waiting for him. He was presumed dead to the world, and Akiyama took his claim to his position within PCW. But little to their dismay that the Black Dragon Master was not entirely dead. The details are foggy, but Inphino somehow escaped death, and reclaimed his work visa so that he could return to the USA. By this time, Akiyama had become PCW Extreme Champion...so Inphino congratulated him...and moved on with his life. He found a small Indy federation called Hardcore Championship Wrestling, where he took his time to train, renew and raise his skills even further...waiting for the one day to return into PTC...and hopefully PCW.


He is a silent man...but also an honorable and respectable one. He always shakes the hands of his opponents, and also helps them up after he has defeated them. But don't let that make you think he's a nice guy. He's extremely dark and hardly shows emotion. If he shows any, it's usually rage or anger...the sorrow that he shows can hardly be called an emotion since he always seems to be saddened.
