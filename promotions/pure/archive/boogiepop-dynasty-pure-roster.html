Wrestling Styles:

-Rampage: Technical/Shoot Style wrestling, focusing on suplex and submission chains, working on specific body parts to set up for the kill.


-Cell: High Flying/Gymnastics, lucha libre-like movement through the ring, flipping and spinning acrobatic maneuvers that will land the opponent on the mat.


-Kyo: Brawling/Martial Arts, a fighter to the end, working on punches and kicks to down his opponent as they do in the streets, hence the name, #1 Slugga


Set Up Move:

Complete B-Dynasty setup: Fusion Beats: Cell performs a spinning legscissors arm drag (Sickle Cell), followed by Kyo nailing a double axe handle uppercut the opponent into the corner (#1 Slugga). Rampage comes in w/ a splash, but grabs the opponent and throws him over with an overhead belly to belly suplex (1/3 of Retroplex).


Cell/Kyo setup: Hip/Pop: Cell pushes opponent into the ropes and leaps up, grabbing them in a jumping hip toss. The throw sends the opponent over sitting up on the mat, but just as impact is made Kyo comes in with a sliding Karate kick to the face, knocking them down on their face.


Cell/Rampage setup: Heavy Metal Queen: Rampage lifts the opponent up for a Brainbuster, and Cell runs in, leaps up and wraps her legs around the opponent’s upper torso. The weight sends all three down, Rampage in a Brainbuster DDT and Cell in a sitting bearhug piledriver.


Rampage/Kyo setup: The Beatdown: Rampage locks in a full nelson from behind, allowing Kyo to come in with a right cross to the jaw, left hook shot to the midsection, and a final right uppercut to the chin as Rampage goes over w/ the Dragon Suplex.


Cell: Sickle Cell: A spinning legscissors arm drag; Cell runs on the opponent and goes for a spinning legscissors, but spins completely around and morphs the move into a spinning arm drag, sending the opponent into the mat and dizzying them as they rise back up.


Kyo: #1 Slugga: An uppercutting double axe handle smash that knocks the opponent back, mostly aimed so that the opponent falls back into the corner or ropes.


Rampage: Retroplex: Rampage locks his arms around the opponent and throws them over w/ an overhead belly to belly suplex, but rolls over, squats over the opponent w/ his arms still locked, and pulls the opponent up with another overhead belly to belly. Pulling the opponent back up once more, Rampage turns 180 degrees and nails a releasing belly to belly suplex.


Finishers:

Complete B-Dynasty finisher: Boogiepop Revolution: Kyo runs in with Da Noise, knocking the opponent back into the waiting arms of Rampage for the Acoustic. Cell comes off with the finishing, jumping up and pinning both of the opponent’s legs to the ground with her own legs and bridges as Rampage bridges the Acoustic.


Rampage: Acoustic: Rampage grabs the opponent in a tiger suplex from behind, locking his hands around the arms in a vertical over horizontal position. Then he locks his hips and snaps the opponent over, slamming them straight onto the back of their head/back. (snap tiger suplex)


Kyo: Da Noise: Kyo runs in with a spinning elbow smash (roaring elbow) into the temple of the opponent, knocking them back onto the mat out cold.


Cell: Idol Hands: Cell locks hands with the opponent and leaps up for a hurricanrana. Cell then rolls over so that she is back to back with her opponent, hands still locked. Then she pulls herself back and locks her legs around the person’s neck, flipping them over for a reverse rana into an sitout mat slam/x-factor.


Cell/Kyo finisher: Gentle Verse: Cell performs the Idol Hands, but just as she goes to complete the final portion, Kyo runs in and delivers Da Noise, adding extra impact to the reverse rana/x-factor from Cell.


Cell/Rampage finisher: Uncut Version: Rampage goes for the Acoustic, and just as he snaps the opponent over Cell flips over with a somersault axe kick into the throat of the opponent, a double impact on the neck from both sides.


Rampage/Kyo finisher: Amp Frequency: Rampage prepares for the Acoustic, and Kyo adds in extra power to the move with #1 Slugga right under the chin before Rampage completes his move.


Kyo Move set-

Kimura Slide: Kyo slides in through the legs of the opponent, grabbing the ankles and pulling them down face first into the mat.


7th Inning: Moving in with a back elbow thrust into the midsection, Kyo grabs the back of the head and holds it as he comes up with a knee into the face of the opponent. Then he closes it with a side shuffle kick into the face, knocking them back into the mat.


Street Pitcher: Kyo throws the opponent into the ropes, and when they come back he nails them under the chin with a thrust kick. The opponent falls back into the ropes, and Kyo catches them on the rebound, grabbing them by the neck and throws them down with a judo hip toss on the mat.


Bases Loaded: A spear into a mounted punch set, Kyo tackling the opponent before kneeling over them and pummeling them with both fists.


Fastball: Known in the streets for his quick hands, Kyo comes around with a quick roundhouse hook across the face of the victim. Usually used around the end of a punching comeback, or as a sudden counter against strikes.


Cell Move set-

White Cell: Jump Swinging Hurricanrana; Cell runs at the opponent, and jumps up, swinging around the person like a jump swinging DDT, but when she comes around to the front of the opponent, she leaps up and nails them with a swift hurricanrana.


Red Cell: Cell leaps up, grabbing the person’s head and leaning back into a DDT, but Cell adds more impact as she monkey flips the opponent over, putting more force into the DDT


J-Pop: A counter as well as an attack; Cell grabs the opponent and whips them away, but holds onto them. She yanks them back and presses her back against the person’s chest, snatching the opponent over in a modified judo hip toss/rotating fireman’s carry. A counter if whipped and she reverses.


Idol Image: Cell leaps up for a hurricanrana, but spins around so that she faces the same way as the victim. Then she hangs down, her ankles around the opponent’s head as she reaches under and grabs the opponent’s legs, pulling the opponent’s head down into the mat as they drop down to their knees. A pin attempt can be made if she rolls over as she has the legs hooked.


Cellibate: A 360 rotating high angle bulldog. Cell grabs the opponent for a bulldog and runs up the turnbuckle or ropes, springing off in a back flip and drives the opponent back down in a high angle bulldog, Cell landing straight on her back w/ her legs up in the air. Can be used as a counter to a back drop or variation of a back drop.


Rampage Move set-

Emo Flux: Rampage tackles the opponent in the back, knocking them forward into either the mat, the ropes, or the corner. Then he wraps his arms around their waist and nails a snapping overhead German Suplex, Rampage arches over and presses them facedown into the mat to add impact. Optional methods include rolling over and continuing the snapping overhead German suplex chain, bridging for a pin, or rolling over and nailing a releasing suplex.


Heavy Metal: The opponent is snatched up onto Rampage’s shoulders in a fireman’s carry. Then Rampage completely drops backwards, crashing down in a bridging Samoan Drop. Optional methods bridging for a pin, rolling over and nailing another Samoan Drop suplex in chain, or rolling over and nailing a front flipping Samoan Drop suplex.


Guitar Vader: With the opponent face down on the mat, Rampage locks the arm in a leg scissors armbar, while grabbing the other arm and locking in a half chickenwing. Then he takes his free right arm and leans over, pulling back on the neck of the opponent. (Crossface Chickenwing Armbar Submission.)


Screech and Thrash: Rampage locks in a sharpshooter, and leans back, lodging his knee into the back of the victim and wrenching on the back in a elevated Boston Crab variation.


Alternative Hype: Locking in a reverse full nelson hold, Rampage throws the opponent over in a reverse Dragon Suplex. Can be chained into multiple suplexes and can be bridged.


History—

Cellacia Otonachi, Rampage, and Kyoshiro…

The sweet and down to earth brown and white haired girl next door…

The big-hearted and skilled blonde muscle-bound jock…

The rough, cocky, and raspy boy from the streets w/ half a foot tall hairdo…

You probably know these three as friends of the infamous Inphino Blitz, martial artist known throughout the world. But now the group known formerly as the Blitz Promotional Team is taking their chance to make themselves known in two very different ways.


These three, veterans of puro from the Tokyo Wrestling League, have taken the opportunity to show their wares in the newly reopening PURE, using their past accolades along with their ties to Inphino Blitz and PURE player Mina de Margo to ensure themselves a position within the company…more importantly, to help establish the unique Trinity Division and make it a success.


Their second chance at stardom is in the form of their music. Each of these three members brings a personal touch of musical genius to the group…Cell the J-Pop Princess, Rampage the Rock Star, and Kyo the Verse Villain and the “#1 Slugga” as he is known on the streets. Rap, Pop, and Rock…merged into one entity that they have dubbed:


“Boogiepop Dynasty”

After auditioning for Naoki Records out of Kamakura, the CEOs of the company, the sibling duo of Debutante and Dilettante Masaki, signed these three right away. It wasn’t soon that the same song they auditioned with was released as the group’s first single; Kagami Kamen, or Mirror Mask. The song’s topped charts through the country, and has made good reviews on the foreign markets. Naoki Records has high hopes that their wrestling career will also bring more publicity to the group, as well as increase CD and merchandising sales.


Of course to Cell, Rampage, and Kyo, it’s all about the fans…it’s all about the fight…it’s all about the music.


You are now under the will of the Boogiepop Dynasty…
