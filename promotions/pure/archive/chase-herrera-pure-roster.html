Bio

Chase Herrera was born July 16, 1979 in Los Angeles, California. Never tended to by his parents, Chase went when and whenever he pleased to. Seeing that the attention that he never had gotten from his parents, it led to a bit of depression. Chase was then out to get any attention he could. So Chase began to live on the edge of life. He wanted to live life to its fullest. He began to do crazy little stunts in front of friends showing off how fearless he had become. But as a child, the stunts weren't as daring as many things that would be set for him in the future. As a child, it only started out to be bike tricks, skateboard tricks, etc.


But as he was growing up, it got worse...

By the age of 15, Chase was into dirt bikes, cliff hanging, bungie jumping, and anything that would perhaps get him killed.


Chase then began to get interested in car racing, even though he didn't have his liscense, he was interested in it. He wanted to do it one day. Chase had to work to get a learners permit. How? Well, he and some friends began to get people to pay to see the crazy stunts that Chase would do. They would split the profits and Chase would soon get his learners and soon moved on to getting his liscense. Once he did, Chase had to work more if he wanted a car. Once he had saved up and gotten his car, an addiction for racing had come to him.


Today, at the age of 23, Chase is a wrestler, but is always out there to do the crazy stunts in the ring and out of the ring.


What will he do next?
