Real Name: Isao Asai Birthdate: December 10th, 1970 Hometown: Akihabara District, Tokyo, Japan Height: 5’7” Weight: 216 lbs. Nicknames: “The Professional” Status: Neutral Manager(s): “The Power Poochie~!” Chu-Chu Current Teams: None. Entrance Music: “Super Bon Bon (Propellerheads Remix)” by Soul Coughing Wrestling Style: Traditional Puroresu, but stronger emphasis on the High-Flying aspect and nearly devoid of brawling.
Signature Moves: As his style dictates, Isao’s move set borrows largely from standard Puroresu, but shines best in terms of his high-flying repertoire.

Tribute Moves:
“Tiger’s Claw” - Isao applies front face lock on the victim. He then lifts them up by their head and neck and falls back, snapping the victim over onto their back. (Ganso Tiger Driver – Tiger Mask Tribute)

“Heaven & Earth” - Isao stands face to face with the victim, slightly to one side. Isao grabs the victim and steps behind their near leg with his one foot. He then uses his arm in front of the victim's chest to push the victim's upper-body backwards while leg sweeping out the victim's leg by pushing it towards his back. This is done in one motion; Isao leans forward and uses the reverse action of pushing the victim forward while sweeping out their leg to take them down. (Space Tornado Ogawa – Naoya Ogawa Tribute)

“Specialty” – Isao handsprings then jumps (sometimes out of the ring), doing a backflip before impacting with the victim, knocking them down to the ground at an angle. (Sasuke Special – Great Sasuke Tribute)

“Bloody Tiger” - Isao handsprings out of the ring and knocks the victim down at an angle. (Flying Space Tiger Attack – Tiger Mask Tribute)

Grappling Moves:
“Punch Card” - Isao lifts up the victim as if he is going to body slam them. He then positions the victim's head down and body to his side and then sits/jumps/drops down, dropping the victim on their head.

“Quota” - Isao applies a half-nelson while facing the victim. Once applied, he lifts the victim up and falls backwards, dropping the victim over onto their face.

“Lay Off” – Isao lands two German Suplexes on the victim before applying a Wakigatame Armbar.

“Cutbacks” – Isao nails a German Suplex, holds for the bridge, but then rolls overtop to the other side of the victim. He then pulls them by a reverse waistlock only to send them back down with a Wheelbarrow Facebuster.

Striking Moves:
“Liability Suit” – Isao executes standard Flash Magic attack, but lifts his knee straight into their nose and backflip to a stance at the victim's feet.

“Shipping & Handling” - Isao turns 360 degrees and hits the victim in the head with the heel of the leg which turned first.

“Supply & Demand” - Isao does a forward somersault roll. As he rolls forward, he hits the victim in the head/chest with the back of his heel/leg.

Submission Moves:
“Red Tape” – Isao does a headstand against a standing victim, pulling the victim’s arms to help support his neck against his victim's body weight while using his feet to push the head and neck farther than they're designed to go.

“Anti-Trust” – Isao executes a standing Double Chickenwing on his victim and holds it while dropping backwards into a Knee-to-Back Rack.

“Hostile Takeover” – Isao executes a Double Chickenwing on the victim, but springboards into a modified version of his Deadline set-up maneuver.

Aerial Moves:
“Upper-Class Warfare” – Isao executes a Twisting Moonsault Stomp on a downed victim’s chest, landing with his arms folded over chest.

“Falling Stock” - Isao jumps over the victim, twisting mid-air and lands back first on them, basically a Twisting Senton.
Set Up Move: Deadline(With his opponent leaning over slightly, he runs off the ropes behind them, somersaulting so his back is against theirs, and locks their arms letting momentum do the rest executing a Reverse Underhook DDT)
Finisher: Asai Special I (540 Spinning Wheel Kick), Asai Special II (Springboard Somersault Legdrop Bulldog), Asai Special III (Wrist-Lock Throat Stomp), Asai Special IV (Somersault Splash w/ Twist), Asai Death Driver **SUPER FINISHER** (Super Cobra Clutch Suplex)

Bio

Isao Asai was born and raised in Tokyo, one of the world's biggest cities, and so he has become accustomed to it's constant ebb and flow of change.


He lived a life identical to most middle-class children in 70's Japan, and didn't really blossom as an individual until he got into the field of business, a profession he shared with his father.


Here he spent the next decade of his life, becoming a dynamo in the field of consumer electronics. He had upper management written all over him, his future seemed assured, and that's when he 'discovered' wrestling.


It started as just an entertaining way to spend an evening after work, but that single exposure was enough to instill a hunger in him for the sport like nothing he'd known before.


He immediately searched down a trainer, and would alternate between work and training in a delicate balance that nearly cost him both.


Finally, with his training complete, he made the hard decision between the business he'd grown up in, or the business he known he'd been born for. With a heavy but hopeful heart he resigned from the corporate world and immediately started working the Japanese Indy scene.


Even as a rookie he still shined like few others, and immediately was picked up in a contract with the budding PURE franchise, who hoped to capitalize on young, fresh talent.
