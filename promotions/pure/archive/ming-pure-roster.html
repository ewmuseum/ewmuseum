Bio

Ming was born into a rich family in Beijing, China. His father was a high ranking official in the Communist Administration, his mother was a dutiful society wife. Ming had everything: cars, land, women. Whatever he wanted was his at the expense of the proletariat his father professed to serve. Ming soon grew bored of his pampered lifestyle and delved into more and more dangerous activities in a futile attempt to stave off the amazing boredom that he felt the world presented him. In the process Ming was gravely injured in a freak Boa Constrictor accident. Ming soon realized that true danger was not for him, and so he enrolled in a pro wrestling school in a misguided attempt to find a 'safe' environment in which to get his thrills. Of course, all did not pan out as he might have hoped. Ming was not offered a huge contract, he was not put over by his various promotors (who were offended by Ming's constant use of the terms 'peon' and references to what he did to their respective mothers the night previous) and he did not make a name for himself. That is, until he joined the UFW where he defeated 'The Scholar' Howard Porter for the Rising Star Title. However, perhaps because of the 'Ming Curse' that he constantly blames for his various bunglings and failures, UFW shut down a week later. Ming moved on to the MWF in a UFW Invasion angle, but soon grew bored. Ming left the world of wrestling to pursue other activities. After his attempt at writing the great Chinese novel failed misreably, Ming decided to make his triumphant return. He joined PCW, tricked the now (in)famous Red Army into accepting him into their ranks, then turned on them and joined the newly formed Elements with stablemates Inphino Blitz, Miyagawa, and Kensuke Suzahara. Ming became a face, but never felt comfortable in that role, constantly being tempted by his inner heel to commit evil deeds. Ming managed to stave off his anti-social tendencies long enough to win the PCW Tag Team Titles with his sometimes friend Inphino Blitz, but soon grew burnt out after being booked on five cards in a row. Ming quit wrestling again, and was not to be seen for 9 months, when he made his triumphant return to wrestling in the fledgling fed known as FSW where he returned to his heelish roots.
