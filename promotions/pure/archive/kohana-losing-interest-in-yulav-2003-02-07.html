Kohana losing interest in Yulav?

7 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After Ikusa III, it seemed as though Kohana had his sites set on Senji Yulav and his All Asia Title, based on his comments after Yulav's match, and during Kohana's Saturday "Just Pop" appearance.

However, it seems Kohana has taken a 180 degree turn. When he was informed about the Battle Royal on Ikusa IV, the winner getting a World Title Shot on the Ikusa after Final Destinies, Kohana had this to say.

"I don't mean to sound arrogant, but I see no reason why I can't win this match. I think my performances thus far have shown I'm quite capable of holding my own. Holding the tights doesn't get you anywhere in a Battle Royal."

When asked about Yulav, he had this to say.

"Yeah, it bugs me that he's got two pinfalls over me. The All Asia Title is great, but if any heavyweight comes into PURE telling you they're not interested in the World Title, they're lying. I plan on taking this match by storm, and then on the Ikusa after our Pay Per View, I'll make PURE's first World Title reign, its shortest. The way I see it, one on one, I can match up with anyone. Enmity, Ramey, Lee, Mandrake, who cares. So while I do have a bit of a grudge against Senji, if we meet again a few weeks from now, and he's holding his All Asia belt, and I'm holding a World Title Belt... well then we know who's the better man."

Senji Yulav, being a champion already, is not entered in the Battle Royal.
