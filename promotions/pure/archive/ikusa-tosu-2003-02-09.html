More wrestlers flash by as the heavy rock guitar kicks back in-

“LOSING MY MIND…”

“LOSING MY SIGHT…”

“LOSING MY MIND…”

”THIS IS MY LAST RESORT!!!!!!!!”

-Derge soars through the air, transferring to Kohana leaping off the turnbuckle with a guillotine leg drop…fading to Chiang Ho Nam diving off the top for a swanton….fading to Enmity with a corkscrew press….finally ending with Inphino Blitz slamming down upon an opponent after a glorious 450 splash-

PURE IKUSA

The cameras swing about the arena as fireworks blast off at the staging, the fans erupting as lights hoot everywhere. Shortly, the lights illuminate to full as the cameras head straight to the ring just in time to catch the end of the challenger for the PURE Junior Heavyweight Championship, Chase Herrera. His theme, 'Singular Indestructible Droid' by Papa Roach finishes playing when Chase bounces off the ropes to warm up.

Hitomi: WELCOME TO IKUSA LADIES AND GENTLEMAN AND LETS GET INTO THE ACTION, as we’re already about to start the JR.Weight title match! I’m Hitomi Asami, with Mang-A-Hero and Mori Mishargi and we’ve got a jam-packed card for you including the 1st EVER Ikusa Royale…. and here comes the Champion!!

“Parabola” by Tool rocks the Tosu Stadium, and a warm ovation comes with it. Radiant steps through the curtain and is met with streamers flying everywhere. The Junior Heavyweight Title is fastened around his waist. Radiant slaps the hand of the fans he can reach just as he reaches the ring. He quickly climbs in.

Mori: My man Isao Asai will get his revenge on that masked dork.

MAH: Masks rock, dude.

Mori: Shut up.

Radiant patiently waits in the corner, while the eager rookie Chase Herrera flexes his muscles. Hanza has now climbed into the ring to do the introductions.

Hitomi: I am really excited for this match, and I think Radiant will win it.

Mori: Blah, blah, blah, Radiant this, Radiant that.

Hitomi: Quiet, Mori.

Hanza: Ladies and Gentlemen, the following match is a PURE Junior Heavyweight Championship match, and is a one fall match with a 30 minute time limit.

The crowd cheers loudly.

Hanza: Standing to my left, and weighing in at 210 lbs., from the city of Los Angeles, America, he is Chase Hererra!

A mild pop from the Japanese fans, but their loyalties still stay with the only native in this match.

Hitomi: The Tosu crowd seem to be heavy favouring Radiant.

Mori: Well, do you expect anything less? Chase is an American!

MAH: So?

Mori: Bah, shut up.

Hanza: And his opponent, standing to my right and weighing in at 219 lbs. From Aikawa Island, JAPAN! He is the PURE Junior Heavyweight Champion, RAAAAAAAADIAAAANTAAA!

The crowd cheers loudly as Radiant holds his arms in the air and steps a few paces forward (ala Hiroyoshi Tenzan). He takes off his Junior Heavyweight Championship Belt and hands it to the referee.

DING! DING! DING!

Hitomi: And we’re underway!

Radiant is the first to attack, leaping forward and delivering a hard Missile Dropkick to Chase Herrera. Chase falls down onto the ring mat, surprised at the sudden attack by the Champion.

Hitomi: Explosive start by Radiant!


Mori: BOO!

MAH: Radiant shall prevail.

Mori: ..wrong!

Just as Chase tries to get back on his feet, Radiant steps back a little and delivers a Spinning Roundhouse Kick to Chases’ head, knocking him down.

Hitomi: Roundhouse Kick!

Chase rolls to the outside, recovering from the explosive start by the champion. Radiant takes this chances to build on his offence. He runs into the opposite ropes, building a head of steam until he reaches the ropes near Chase and dives over them with a Flipping Plancha onto Chase.

Mori: Bah.

MAH: WOAH!

Radiant climbs back onto his feet, and the crowd cheer loudly for him. He pulls Chase back onto his feet, but quickly delivers a hard shot to the stomach of Chase Herrera. Radiant rolls Chase back into the ring, and is about to launch another attack on him, until he is distracted.

By Isao Asai.

Mori: ASAI! My man!

The crowd gives off a mixture of boos and cheers as Isao Asai walks down the ramp toward the ring. He is wearing his wrestling attire, and a slip of paper is in his hand. Asai walks around the ring and to where Hanza stands, handing him the slip of paper.

Hitomi: What is Asai doing out here interrupting this important match?

MAH: This is not a good image for PURE if roster members are ruining matches!

Hanza stands back up to make a strange announcement.

Hanza: It seems as if the Junior Heavyweight Championship match between Radiant and Chase Herrera has been cancelled, and is now a Junior Heavyweight Championship match between Radiant and Isao Asai. And it’s a Moonsault Match.

Hitomi: What is a Moonsault match?

Mori: I have no idea.

MAH: Me either.

Asai climbs into the ring, and immediately kicks the hurting Chase Herrera out of the ring and to the floor. Radiant does not look impressed.

Hanza: This match will only end once Asai or Radiant complete a Moonsault onto their opponent.

Hitomi: How odd.

The Bell Rings again.

Hitomi: And we’re under again. I think.

Radiant quickly attacks Asai enraged at his match being ruined by his new nemesis. Radiant quickly sends Asai flying into the ropes, but catches him with a Headlock.

Hitomi: Headlock by Radiant to Asai.

However, Radiant doesn’t keep the headlock onto Asai for very long. He quickly lifts Asai into the air and slams him down with a Release Headlock Suplex.

Hitomi: Suplex!

But Asai doesn’t stay down. Before Radiant can even get back onto his feet, Asai springs forward and slams his knee into Radiant’s face with a Shining Wizard!

Mori: SHIIIIIIIININGAAAAA WIZARRRRRUUUUUUU!

Radiant falls down on the mat holding his face after the devastating Shining Wizard. Despite not liking Isao Asai all that much, the crowd still pops for one of the most popular moves in Puroresu.

Hitomi: The Shining Wizard hurt Radiant, and it hurt Asai as well. Both men are down.

Mori: Asai will be the first to recover.

And indeed Asai is. Asai rolls back onto his feet, impatiently waiting for Radiant to get back up. It takes a few more seconds for Radiant to get back up.

MAH: Radiant back on his feet now.

As Radiant gets back on his feet, both he and Asai run to opposite ends of the ring. When both meet in the middle again after bouncing off the ropes, they both slam into each other with Shoulder Blocks – but neither man budges.

Hitomi: Radiant and Asai aren’t giving each other any kind of advantage.

Mori: Why would they? That’d just be stupid.

Hitomi: I knew that.

Asai, however, uses his high intellect for a split second, and decides to Dropkick the left knee of Radiant.

Mori: Brilliant strategy from Asai. Take away Radiant’s spring, so he can’t do a Moonsault and win the Match! HA!

Asai bounces off the ring ropes while Radiant holds onto his knee, wincing in pain. Asai delivers another vile Dropkick to the knee.

Hitomi: Another knee shot by Asai.

Radiant holds onto his knees for a second, but shakes it off and delivers a hard punch to Asai. Radiant quickly locks in a Waist lock on Asai, and then proceeds to lift him over his head with a German Suplex.

But somehow Asai flips out of it, and slams Radiant down onto the mat with a quick fire Reverse DDT!

Hitomi: WHAT A COUNTER BY ASAI!

Radiant holds his neck in pain, and Asai is also out of breath. They both take their time to get back on their feet, and Asai is the first to do so. He senses that he has some extra time, and quickly takes off into the ropes.

As he bounces off them and flips over backward, about to hit a Moonsault, Radiant leaps onto his feet and stops Asai with a Dropkick to his stomach!

Hitomi: Radiant counters!

Radiant slowly rises to his feet while Asai holds onto his stomach. Radiant slowly bounces off the ropes, and then leaps into the air slamming his elbow down onto Asai’s chest.

Mori: NO! ASAI!

Hitomi: Radiant could win this right now if he decides to go to the turnbuckle.

Radiant slowly gets back up, and makes his way over to the turnbuckle and climbs up. But the time he took to get back on his feet has cost him dearly, because Isao Asai has gotten back on his feet.

Mori: Asai back up!

Asai runs up and Wheel Kicks Radiant in the back, causing the sore Champion to stumble and lose his footing on the turnbuckle. Asai climbs up top with Radiant, and hooks him around the waist. Asai then picks up Radiant, and slams him off the top turnbuckle with a Release Suplex, but Asai holds onto the turnbuckle with this legs.

Hitomi: Amazing strength in his legs from Asai.

Asai takes a long gasp of air, catching his breath. He pulls himself back up onto the turnbuckle, but instead of leaping off with a Moonsault, he opts for a Top Rope Splash, instead.

Mori: Why didn’t he go for a Moonsault?

Hitomi: I do not know, perhaps he wants to wear Radiant out more so that his chances of hitting a Moonsault later in the match without Radiant moving will be greater.

Mori: Oh yeah, I didn’t think of that.

Asai is quick to his feet; he launches himself up into the air and drops down with both knees straight into Radiant’s chest.

Hitomi: Double knee drop!

Asai applies a reverse chin lock, Radiant struggling in the hold. As Asai tries to ground the hold Radiant snaps his legs up and gets to his knees, slowly pulling himself up onto his feet. He charges forward, pushing Asai so as to release himself from the chin lock and force his opponent into the ropes. As he comes back Radiant jumps and lands a dropkick straight to the jaw, Radiant flipping backwards after impact.

Hitomi: What a dropkick by Radiant.

MAH: The moon may have given him supernatural powers on this night.

Mori: I don’t think the moon has anything to do with it.

MAH: The moon works in mysterious ways, men transformed into beasts of unimaginable strength and power!

Hitomi: But it doesn’t seem to be having that effect on Radiant tonight.

MAH: The night is not over.

Radiant runs to the ropes, his back turned to Asai he clambers back to his feet. Radiant bounces off the second rope and flips backwards, he realises that Asai is up and lands on his feet behind Asai. Radiant is unprepared and is not yet steady; Asai sends an elbow into his jaw. He turns round to face the champion and sends another elbow into the jaw. He then runs back, bouncing on the ropes and charges back with a diving shoulder… but Radiant sends his elbow forward sharply and sends Asai reeling backwards.

Hitomi: What an elbow strike for a counter!

Mori: Asai!

Radiant holds his jaw, stumbling back a little.

Hitomi: Radiant looks like he could have hurt his jaw.

Mori: Will Asai take note of that?

Hitomi: I would assume Radiant would try to hide it as much as he can.

Mori: Depends on how badly injured it is.

Hitomi: True, I mean, how well can you hide a broken jaw?

Radiant pulls himself together and moves to the nearest turnbuckle. He pulls himself up onto the top rope; he takes longer than usual to steady himself as he still continues to nurse his jaw. Asai, grabbing hold of the ropes, charges forward with every last bit of energy he has left and climbs up onto the second rope, hooking Radiant in a waistlock from behind.

Mori: Asai!!!

Hitomi: What could he be planning here?

Asai locks his legs into the ropes, steadying himself before falling back and driving Radiant into the mat with a Spider German suplex (taking a page out of Tenryu’s book).

Mori: SPIDAAA GERMAN SUPLEXXAA!!!

Hitomi: This is most certainly the perfect set up.

Asai untangles his legs and pulls himself up onto the top rope fully. He the holds his arms out to his side, the fans cheering him on as he flips backwards for a moonsault.

Mori: MOOONSAULTTAAA!!!!

Radiant, with the aid of the bottom rope, rolls away to the outside and to safety. Asai hits the mat hard, as motionless as Radiant.

Hitomi: He missed!

Mori: Radiant rolled right out of the ring!

MAH: He must have used some sort of spider sense of some kind to move so swiftly from danger.

Hitomi: He has good mat sense, knowing that he was in danger there, but not a ‘spider sense’ as you call it.

MAH: You don’t accept any of my theories!

Mori: They don’t make any sense, that’s why.

MAH: They are all perfectly valid points that are true to what I am seeing.

Mori: I would not want to see what you’re seeing then.

Asai is the first to clamber back up to his feet, Radiant stumbling around on the outside still. Asai holds tightly onto the top rope and propels himself over it, flying through the air and hitting a body press onto Radiant.

Mori: BODDYY PRESSAAA!!

MAH: Powers of a thousand dragons flow through him!

Asai forces himself up as quickly as his body will allow and climbs up onto the apron, he looks back for a moment to make sure he is lined up correctly and pulls himself up onto the second rope, flipping backwards for a Quebrada… but Radiant rolls right out of the way just in time as Asai hits nothing for a second time.

Hitomi: That was close.

MAH: A little too close, if you ask me.

Mori: How can Radiant be proud of just moving out of the way?

Hitomi: It is better than being hit and losing the title he holds around his waist.

Mori: Where is the pride of it though?

Hitomi: Radiant is very proud of that title.

Mori: He just runs away though.

Hitomi: I think you are looking a little too closely into this.

Radiant stumbles over to Asai again, lifting him up by the scruff of his neck and rolling him back into the ring under the bottom rope, the crowd cheer at this as Radiant rolls back in himself.

Hitomi: Both men are back in the ring.

Radiant forces Asai back up, both automatically going into a collar and elbow tie up. Radiant pushes his arm into position and drops down with a DDT, Asai driven into the mat and flipping onto his back. Radiant then turns to look at the nearest turnbuckle.

Hitomi: Radiant looks like he is ready to end it now.

Mori: Asai does look to be out cold.

MAH: Time for super magic dragon powers to be called forth.

Mori: No, he’s going to go for a Moonsault.

Hitomi: Which would bring the match to an end.

MAH: He might need super dragon powers to do that.

Mori: I think he can manage without.

Radiant steps up the corner, gripping the ropes tightly as he lifts his leg up and begin to climb. He stops, and holds onto his jaw in pain and stops climbing.

Mori: What’s wrong with him?

Hitomi: It looks like his jaw is still hurting him, he shouldn’t even think about going for a Moonsault if his jaw hurts this much.

Mori: He could hit it on impact and cause serious damage to it.

Hitomi: Exactly.

Asai, now up on one knee, sees Radiant leaning against the corner still holding onto his chin and snaps up to his feet. He locks onto Radiant around the waist and lifts him up in one swift motion, slamming him down with a German suplex.

Mori: German Suplex!

Hitomi: That came out of nowhere!

Mori: Radiant certainly was not expecting that.

Asai climbs up onto the top turnbuckle, having trouble steadying himself on the wobbling ropes.

Hitomi: Asai looks to be having a little trouble.

Mori: Those ropes look more unstable than they should.

Hitomi: I think Asai is just tired, that’s more likely it.

Radiant, now on his feet and slowly moving in Asai’s direction, locks onto both legs and hauls himself up onto the second rope. Asai, now up on Radiant’s shoulders, wails as he flies backwards and hits the mat with a boom with the shoulder mount suplex.

Hitomi: Radiant looks to be in some serious pain with that jaw.

Mori: What about Asai?

Hitomi: That suplex looked to be quite a fall.

Mori: He landed hard, they both did.

Hitomi: Radiant had to sacrifice himself a little for that move, yes, but it looked to have been effective.

After a long while both competitors rise to their feet, slowly. They go into a sloppy collar and elbow tie up, Radiant pushes forward, setting his arms in the right position and pulls backwards, keeping his heels firmly on the mat as he sends Asai down with a northern lights suplex. Radiant keeps the bridge tight, but the referee tells him that he cannot win with a pin fall.

Hitomi: Pure instinct there.

Mori: He’s being careless.

Hitomi: I think his mind just slipped, that’s all.

Mori: Perhaps, but if he doesn’t keep focused on what the goal is he could find himself in serious trouble.

Hitomi: He is bright; he shouldn’t find himself in too much danger.

Radiant struggles back to his feet, he stomps down on Asai with the bottom of his boot. He then looks back, running to the ropes and coming at full steam. He jumps into the air and twists his arm round, getting it full exposure before dropping down onto Asai with a his elbow.

Hitomi: Radiant dropped the elbow onto Asai with added style.

Mori: Showing off, he could have been going for a Moonsault and end the match.

Hitomi: It isn’t that simple.

MAH: It is not?

Hitomi: We have seen both these competitors go for numerous Moonsaults but can never hit them, by wearing him down he will have a better opportunity when he does go for a Moonsault.

Mori: You are right, they both refuse to lose.

Hitomi: There is a lot at stake in this match.

Radiant thinks about going to the top rope, but instead he opts to pick Asai up and try another devastating move on him. Suddenly, out of no where, Asai gets his ‘second wind’ and delivers a hard and sudden Shotei to Radiant!

Mori: GOKUAKU SHOTEI CHO!

Radiant falls over, holding onto his jaw after the Shotei from Isao Asai. Asai takes a second to regain his senses after Radiant being on the attack for awhile.

Hitomi: A well needed rest period for both wrestlers in this match, they’ve both been giving 110%!

Asai runs into the ropes after taking his breather. Radiant is still not moving, so this allows Asai enough time to elegantly leap into the air, going to hit a Shining Elbow drop.

Hitomi: RADIANT MOVED!

Mori: Damn it.

MAH: AHH! SUCH SWEARS!

Radiant quickly picks up Asai and wrenches on his arm. He pulls on the wrist a little, and then quickly sends Asai flying into the ropes. Radiant lowers his head, going for a Back Body Drop, but Asai flips over and lands awkwardly on his feet.

Radiant goes for a Lariat, but Asai ducks and tries a Lariat of his own. Radiant ducks Asai’s Lariat, as well. Asai then kicks Radiant in the side, stunning the Masked man. Asai follows that up by sending Radiant into the ropes, but Radiant reverses the attempted Irish Whip.

Asai goes into the ropes, and Radiant jumps over him as he bounces off them at breakneck speed. Radiant falls down onto that mat, going for a Monkey Toss but Asai flips over the move.

Radiant leg trips Asai, and quickly goes for a roll through Armbar but Asai throws him off before he can lock on the Submission move. Asai jumps back onto his feet, and leg trips Radiant. “The Professional” tries the exact some move Radiant, trying to go for a Roll through Armbar. Radiant throws Asai off.

Quickly running into the ropes, Asai is taken down by Radiant after a Japanese Armdrag. But, Isao Asai is quickly back on his feet, and takes down Radiant with a quick and flowing Armdrag. Both men stare down as the crowd cheers loudly.

Hitomi: What an exchange of moves by these two Junior Heavyweight’s!

Mori: That was a joy to see.

MAH: The fans love it!

Asai quickly attacks again, slamming his knee into Radiant’s chest. Asai catches Radiant as if he is going to execute a back body drop. As Asai lifts Radiant over, Asai hooks Radiant’s legs and falls backwards.

Mori: MOOOOOOOOUNTAINOOOO BAMUUUUUU!

Hitomi: NO! HE MOVED OUT!

Radiant somehow managed to flop down and land on his legs awkwardly. Radiant quickly hooks the waist on Asai, and pulls him over his shoulders with a quick fire Release German Suplex. Asai lands on his shoulder, and is groggily back up on his feet.

Radiant quickly gets back on the attack. While Asai stumbles around on his feet, Radiant bounces off the ropes and slams down Asai with one of the most powerful Lariat’s ever in PURE.

Hitomi: LAAAAAARIAAAAAAATOOOOOOOO!

Mori: NO! ASAI!

MAH: I think it is over.

Radiant is the first up, leaving Asai to count his teeth. Radiant checks over his shoulder, but quickly jumps onto the nearest second rope, flipping over with a Quebrada!

Hitomi: MOOOOOOOOONSAULTAAA! Radiant wins the match, and retains his title!

“Parabola,” by Tool begins to play, the feed being cut from the ring as Radiant slowly gets to his feet, and Medical Crew’s come back in to check on Isao Asai laying motionless in the ring.

A Few “Star” Questions-Hoshi Akagawa

The camera shifts to Yuri Takayama standing outside of a locker room, the door of which reads “The Star” in large gold lettering. Ms. Takayama is wearing her usual attire, which consists of a revealing silver silk blouse with tight black slacks and black heels.

Yuri: I sure do not like being patient. I hate waiting on people just so that I can ask them for an interview.

The door of the locker room opens.

Yuri (muttering): Finally. It’s about time. I don’t have all day.

Hoshi Akagawa walks out from the locker room.

Yuri: Hey Hoshi.

Hoshi: Umm...Hello, Yuri. Did you say something as I was coming out of my locker room?

Yuri: No, I did not.

Hoshi: Alright. Anyway, The Star was just about to go and get something to drink. Would you like to come along?

Yuri: Sure. Sounds like a good idea.

The two begin walking down the long hallway where all of the locker rooms are located. All of the other rooms appear to be similar to Hoshi’s locker room...

Yuri: So Hoshi...How are you today?

Hoshi: The Star is alright. Still hurting a little from last week. You?

Yuri: I’m fine.

Hoshi: Is there something that you wanted from The Star, Yuri?

Yuri: Yes, actually.

Hoshi: Well, what is it that you want?

Yuri: Umm...Could I possibly ask you a few questions?

Hoshi: Seeing as you have already started asking, she supposes that you can ask a couple of questions.

Yuri: How do you feel about how your match went last week?

Hoshi: The Star really did not do a great job last week. She should have done better than she did, but she supposes that nothing can be done about that now.

Yuri: That’s true. You cannot really go back in time and change things. Well, I suppose you could if you had a time machine or something...

Hoshi: Which The Star does not have, because time machines do not exist.

Yuri: Therefore, you cannot possibly go back in time to last week and change the outcome of your match with R...

Before Ms. Takayama can complete her sentence, Hoshi quickly cuts her off.

Hoshi: Do not say his name!

Yuri: Umm...may I ask why not?

Hoshi: Because The Star is not going to let her interview be soiled by the mere mention of his name. That’s why not.

Yuri: Fair enough, I guess.

After a good amount of walking, the two come to a stop next to a vending machine.

Hoshi: Finally here. Took long enough.

The Star places some money into the machine. She then presses one of the many buttons on the machine.

Yuri: It was quite a long walk to this vending machine. You would think that the distance between vending machines in this place would be much shorter than it actually is.

Hoshi: Yes. You would think that, wouldn’t you?

After a few rumbling noises, a can pops out. Hoshi takes the can from where it has popped out and opens it up. She then quickly takes a sip from the can.

Hoshi: Ahh...I sure do love Coca Cola.

Yuri: Is Coca Cola your favorite drink or something?

Hoshi: Yes, it is.

Yuri: It’s kind of odd seeing you drinking Coca Cola.

Hoshi: Why is that?

Yuri: Because not very many people drink soda here in Japan.

Hoshi: That is true. The Star is a part of the minority when it comes to drinking soda.

Ms. Akagawa takes another sip from the can of Coca Cola.

Yuri: Anyway, I have just one more question for you.

Hoshi: Alright. What is your question?

Yuri: Are you going to be in the open invite battle royal later on tonight?

Hoshi: You’ll see.

The Star walks back down the hallway in the direction which leads back to her locker room, leaving Yuri standing next to the vending machine.

How Many Of Them Are There?-Suburban Ninja Clan

“So, wait… exactly how many of them are there?”

The familiar voice of Mina de Margo opens up from her office, the door ajar as the camera makes its way inside. Inside she is across from her desk, leaning over the desk with her arms folded on the surface. Across from her sits an old man with a thin gray beard trimmed and tied into something of a ponytail that hangs down to his chest. His head is bald, capped with a hat composed of a small wooden board 3x8 that rests atop his head lengthwise, the back end dangling down with the front end high on his head. He wears a bright red kimono, and judging from the thickness there are no doubt many more underneath.

“Forty,” the old man replies. “Each one a capable killing machine.”

Shaking her head, Mina de Margo leans back into her chair in a slight slumping motion.

“There’s the thing,” she says. “We don’t exactly go for the whole Killing Machine stuff here.”

The old man scratches his chin where the beard begins.

“Could they… maim?”

Mina shrugs her shoulders.

“Have ‘em go nuts, just no killing.”

“As their Sensei, I cannot guarantee that I will stop them. It is their job, after all. This wrestling thing is just more of a side hobby to keep them occupied. Thus the pittance wage that we discussed?”

Nodding her head, Mina de Margo reaches into the desk and brings out a mere 130 Yen, pocket change in the Japanese economy.

“No complaints here. PURE is reaching out to the best young Japanese talent in the business and the terms of your contract with this Suburban Ninja Clan leaves a great deal of funds open to use in courting other talent. Now, let me ask exactly how this is supposed to work, there being 40 of those ninja. I’m not booking all 40 each night, am I?”

The Sensei shakes his head in a modestly negative response.

“No, no, no. All that is required is that you book the clan to appear. We shall send representatives. It would be best served if they were placed in your famed Trinity Tag Team Division. That would offer them the most rotation to wrestle.”

Mori: Sushi Crew are fools to talk smack without the other Trinity champ Shaun Styles not on the scene! Not like he’d be any difference!

“Your Touch” by Kutless cuts over the speakers, and SSL’s attention is stolen immediately. Jesse Ramey jogs out as the arena pops, sliding into the ring but being cut off by Taki Kuonji immediately!

Hitomi: Ramey and SSL are ready to go right now! But they have to wait! There’s an order to how things go!

SSL shoots out curse words at Ramey as Taki orders for him back in his corner. Ramey backs off, prompting SSL to do the same as the lights lower and “Deep Impact: DJ Krush Mix” by Dragon Ash booms into the ears of the fans.

Mori: Here he comes now! The one…the only…

Raizo appears on stage, letting out a low growl as he taps his fist to his chest. The fans boo as he walks down to the ring jumps onto the apron. He climbs in and stands up on the turnbuckle, glaring out at the people and folds his arms at his chest.

Mori:… THE SAIKYO SENSHI!! RAIZO TENZAN!!!

Hitomi: Can you mark out any harder?

Hanza begins as Raizo glares back at him from atop of the turnbuckle. Ramey, Sushi Crew, and Mandrake back into the corner near him as well, the Black Dragons, Enmity, and SSL across the ring from them.

Hanza: First, from Beijing China, weighing in at 175, 190, and 255 pounds…Chen Lei Cia, Chi’ing, and Chiang Ho Nam….THE BLACK DRAGONS!!

The trio all raise their fists to the sky as Sushi Crew glare on from the other side.

Hanza: From Wuhan China, weighing in at 251 pounds…ENMITY!!

Another set of boos coo out as Enmity glares at Mandrake and the others across from him.

Hitomi: He’s a fierce one! Look at those eyes!

Mori: He’s got nothing on Raizo’s piercing glare.

Raizo indeed matches stares with Enmity as Hanza continues.

Hanza: And from Denver Colorado USA, weighing in at 263 pounds…SINISTER STEVEN LEE!!

SSL puts his middle finger up at Ramey, Taki stepping between as Hanza turns to the other team.

Hanza: Their opponents, first, two thirds of the PURE TRINITY CHAMPIONS….THE SUSHI CREW!!!

They hold up their belts as the people cheer. The Black Dragons take offense, but merely glare in spite.

Hanza: Next, from Osaka via Hamilton, Ontario, Canada…weighing in at 209 pounds…EDDIE MANDRAKE!!!

Mandrake doesn’t have long to do anything before Enmity takes a step at him. Taki backs him up before anything can happen.

MAH: There’s a lot of animosity in these two teams.

Hanza: Next, from Mito …weighing in at 225 pounds…JESSE RAMEY!!

Hitomi: The former FSW superstar looks ready to take another shot at SSL!

Mori: Bah, this entire team sucks except for the Strongest Soldier standing on the turnbuckle right there!

Hanza: And finally, from Fukuoka, weighing in at 257 pounds…THE SAIKYO SENSHI, RAIZO TENZAN!!!

Raizo cracks his knuckles as he hops down from the corner. Taki proceeds to check through the pads of the wrestlers as Hanza leaves the ring. He skips over Raizo, the Yakuza giving him nothing but dirty looks as Taki calls for the bell.

Hitomi: This match is underway…just as soon as the teams figure out who’s starting first!

The Ramey team huddles up to see who goes first…except for Raizo who climbs out of the ring onto the apron. Taking that as a request not to start, Ryo of the Sushi crew starts things up as SSL approaches the center of the ring.

Mori: SSL gonna whip him up some Sushi…just as good as we do down at MORI’S !!

Hitomi: Cheap plug!

SSL goes for Ryo, but he slips underneath and grabs him around the waist, SSL reverses and locks in a headlock on the lighter man. Ryo twists out, a wrist lock in before locking in another headlock, then going behind again. SSL reverses and goes behind, pulling him up and hitting him with a back side slam. He goes to pull Ryo up, but the Trinity champ hops up and arm drags SSL over into the corner! Ryo nips up as SSL kicks the ropes annoyed.

Hitomi: The fans are behind Ryo on that excellent thinking over SSL!

They lock up again, but SSL pulls Ryo’s legs out from under him. He goes for a crab, but Ryo breaks free. SSL then goes for a half nelson on the ground, pulling Ryo up to his feet. Ryo twists out, flips over onto his feet to reverse the lock, and then arm drags SSL into the ropes! The fans pop as Ryo motions SSL to bring it. SSL kicks the ropes again as the people clap.

MAH: Ryo is showing SSL up in the speedy arts!

SSL shakes his head as he walks over to his corner with his hand out. Chi’ing slaps hands, and jumps into the ring, across from him Otoshi of the Sushi crew is waiting for him!

Hitomi: We got a Sushi and a Dragon in the ring! Now things are about to heat up!

They approach each other cautiously, both keeping their distance. Chi’ing snaps a kick at Otoshi’s legs, but he backs away too quickly. They close in again, and Otoshi jumps out of the way for another kick. They finally lock up, and Chi’ing kicks Otoshi in his ribs, scooping him up and slamming him into the mat. Otoshi climbs back up and the two of them begin to pace around once more. They lock into a test of strength, but Chi’ing kicks Otoshi and slams him again. He bounces off the ropes, but Otoshi jumps up and forearms him into the mat!

Hitomi: They’re going pace for pace here!

Otoshi bounces off the ropes this time and drops the elbow, but Chi’ing rolls out of the way! Otoshi shoots out a kick, but Chi’ing grabs it and sweeps his legs out from under him. Otoshi jumps back up and grabs another kick, but Chi’ing turns it into a reverse enziguri! Otoshi rolls out of the ring, and Chi’ing springs off the ropes and runs across the ring!

MAH: CHI’ING’S ABOUT TO FLY!!

Chi’ing jumps onto the ropes, but corkscrews around and lands on his feet in the center of the ring facing Otoshi on the outside!! The fans OOOH as the Black Dragon motions for the Sushi Crewman to enter the ring.

MAH: What a fake by that Chi’ing! Otoshi thought it was coming there!

Chi’ing backs out and tags in Chiang. The fans get hyped as Eddie Mandrake gets tagged in from the returning Otoshi. The fans begin to clap as Chiang and Mandrake circle around the ring slowly. They lock up in the center, but Mandrake hip tosses him over. Both men get back up and lock up again, this time Chiang locking in a headlock. Mandrake backs him into the ropes and shoots him off, going for the Dark Lance as Chiang springs back! Chiang ducks underneath, and when Mandrake turns around he gets scooped up for a DVD!

Mori: That’s right! Get him Chiang!!

Mandrake lands on his feet after being tossed off by Chiang! He holds his arms up to mockingly ask what that was, an angry look on his face as both men back off into their corners.

Hitomi: This match is staying fresh as both men keep tagging in their partners. It’s important to rotate in a match with this many men, or someone will be lost in the shuffle!

Chen climbs into the ring to oppose Jesse Ramey, the fans popping for the gaijin face.

MAH: This American has a lot of respect here in Japan.

They lock up, but Chen kicks Ramey in the gut quickly to gain an advantage over the larger man. He locks in a headlock, but Ramey throws him into the ropes. Chen jumps over the ducking Ramey and comes back off the ropes going for a lariat. Chen turns around for another lariat, but gets caught in a reverse armbar!!

Hitomi! FUJIWARA!!!

Chi’ing jumps out from his corner and boots Ramey in the back to save his partner, the fans booing hard at the double team set to start up.

Mori: That’s using the brain there!

Taki yells for Chi’ing to get back, but he whips Ramey into the ropes. Coming back, Chi’ing shoulder back tosses Ramey over, but he flips over and lands on his feet! Chen is waiting, locking in a full nelson as Chi’ing bounces off the ropes himself and runs for the defenseless Ramey. A dropkick is misplaced as Ramey ducks, Chi’ing hitting Chen instead! Chi’ing turns around and gets planted with a running facecrusher!!

Hitomi: Ramey is going well coming off the brutal street fight he came from!

Pulling Chi’ing back up, he powerslams him before pulling him up once more and throwing him into his own corner. Raizo tags into the ring, drawing out a large set of boos as Ramey stares at him.

Hitomi: Ramey doesn’t appreciate the blind tag when he had Chi’ing on the ropes, but now the Saikyo Senshi is in the ring!

Raizo whips Chi’ing into the ropes, but Chi’ing holds on and slides out of the ring. Raizo shakes his head as to the side Enmity steps into the ring.

Mori: WHAT? Get your ass back in the corner! No one asked you!

Hitomi: We’re about to see two of the most pissed off people in PURE get it on right now!!

Raizo growls at him in Japanese, with Enmity spitting in his direction. They lock up tightly, but Raizo turns in and throws Enmity over with a headlock takedown. E gets back up and kicks Raizo in his chest before whipping him across the ring. Raizo comes back and ducks underneath the lariat, but then bounces off, rolls across the mat, and levels the turning E with a lariat of his own!!

Mori: You can’t stop the Strongest!

Raizo taps his chest with his fist from his kneeling position as he gets back up. He picks up E and whips him into the ropes, scooping him up for a flowing brainbuster! E holds his head as Raizo pulls him back up and whips him into the ropes, bouncing off the other side himself and tackling into him!

Hitomi: Wait!

Enmity suddenly drops back and drives Raizo’s head into the mat with Suicidal Solutions!! The booming impact DDT leaves Raizo reeling, his head popping off the mat as he staggers back into the ropes. E runs over and pops him in the face with a roundhouse dropkick, sending him rolling over the top!!

Mori: He rolled over the top himself!!… because he was tired of facing E!!

Raizo mutters under his breath before walking back over to his corner. Enmity glares at him before taking a look at Mandrake and returning to his own corner. Before anyone can notice, SSL and Ramey are in the ring together!!

MAH: Street fight!!

The two of them go chest to chest, staring into each other’s eyes for intimidation. Ramey quickly knees SSL and snapmares him over, dropkicking SSL in the back of the head before he could sit up! SSL gets back to his feet and snapmares Ramey over himself, putting a dropkick of his own into the back of Ramey’s head! Ramey jumps up, and the crowd does the same as the two rivals go chest to chest again!

Hitomi: They know each other well from the recent match they just had! They’re not going to give any ground away period!

Ramey and SSL begin to trade chops into each other’s chest before Ramey bounces off the ropes. SSL is ready as he throws Ramey over with a Belly to Belly suplex! Ramey slides across the ring as SSL comes in with a hard lariat to the chest. Then he whips Ramey across the ring, catching him in a DVD position ready for S FORCE 5!!

Mori: TIME FOR PAYBACK RAMEY!!

The fans begin to boo as SSL throws Ramey’s frame upwards…but their boos turn to cheers as Ramey tucks his legs in over SSL’s shoulders and pops his body back, sending SSL to the mat with a hurricanrana!! The fans are cheering madly as SSL gets back to his feet and runs at Ramey near the ropes, but Jesse back tosses him over the top!

MAH: Bout to see some flying right here!!

Everyone stands as Ramey bounces off the ropes across from SSL. He leaps up and turns around onto the top, his back turned towards SSL as backflips and lands in the ring!! The people applaud the fake moonsault attempt on SSL as Ramey goes back to tag in a new person.

Hitomi: The fakes are just coming out aren’t they!

Mori: We’ll see!

Ryo comes into the ring, opposed to the much larger Chiang. They lock up, but Chiang gets a thumb into the eyes before pushing Ryo into the ropes and aiming a chop at him. Ryo ducks underneath, and when Chiang turns around he gets thrown over with an arm drag! They both pop up, and Ryo puts a dropkick right into the large Black Dragon’s face! Once again they both get up, and this time Ryo throws him over with another arm drag!!

Hitomi: The Trinity champ is showing just why he’s that!

Ryo keeps in an armlock on Chiang, trying to keep some leverage. Then he pulls Chiang up and backs him into the corner, throwing chop after chop into the Chinese’s chest. Ryo goes to whip Chiang out, but Chiang shoves him away!

Mori: That’s the power of the strongest Black Dragon.

Chiang throws Ryo out of the ring, but when he goes outside himself Ryo slides back into the ring!

MAH: HAHA! Too quick for ya!

Ryo turns back around to get booted in the gut before Chiang throws him out again. And just like last time, Ryo slides into the ring before Chiang gets outside! Chiang gets on the apron, but Ryo dropkicks him off! Then the fans get excited as Ryo bounces off the ropes, baseball slides on his stomach at Chiang standing on the outside, wraps his legs around Chiang’s head, and spins out with a leg scissors takedown on the outside!!

Hitomi: Now that was great! The smaller man knows his skill!

Chi’ing takes up the slack as Mandrake comes back into the ring. The two of them begin to trade sharp kicks into each other’s midsection, Chi’ing snaps a kick into Mandrake’s face, but it only stuns him. Chi’ing goes for another kick, but Mandrake grabs the foot and kicks him over, stepping over at the same time and locking in a single crab!! Chi’ing goes for the ropes immediately as the fans cheer on the reversal!

Hitomi: Eddie Mandrake may be unorthodox in stature as well as in the ring, but his technical skill is complete!

Chi’ing comes back as Mandrake whips him off the ropes. He presses the 190 pounder into the air, but Chi’ing comes back on Mandrake’s shoulders for a hurricanrana! Mandrake doesn’t go over however, but as Chi’ing leans back he steps forward and hooks both legs under his arms for an elevated Boston crab!

MAH: What a Robotech REVERSAL!!

Chen dives across the ring with an elbow in the back of Mandrake’s head! Raizo comes out from his corner, prompting Chen to bounce off the ropes and nail him with a flying cross chop! Raizo staggers back as the lightweight bounces off the ropes again and leaps at him….right into a bearhug front hold!!

Mori: SHI-NE~!!! coming up!!

Before the move is executed, Chi’ing comes from behind and kicks Raizo in the knees. Raizo drops Chen and turns around, shoving Chen away by his face. The Sushi Crew leaps into the ring and begins to trade blows with the Black Dragons, throwing them both out of the ring! Then both Ryo and Otoshi nod as they bounce off the ropes and nail somersault planchas simultaneously on both Chi’ing and Chen of the Black Dragons!!!

Hitomi: OVER THE TOP SOMERSAULT PLANCHAS!! OVER THE TOP!!

Mori: Your selling is OVER THE TOP!!

Raizo turns around to see Enmity climbing into the ring for him. Raizo waves him in with a scowl, but Eddie Mandrake leapfrogs over him and cross bodies E!! Both men go rolling from the ring as Raizo stands there staring around at everything unfolding. Taki stands nearby as well watching the carnage!

Hitomi: There hasn’t even been a pin attempt in this entire match! It’s been nothing but chaos!!

Ramey and SSL are on the outside as well brawling in front of the announce table. Raizo turns around to see Chiang walking towards him slowly. Raizo backs towards the ropes, and Chiang runs at him. Raizo fireman carries him over the top rope, and Chiang splashes right into both of the Sushi Crew!!

MAH: That hurt his partners more than it hurt his opponent!

Hitomi: Raizo doesn’t really care at this point! Everything’s going to hell!

With the Black Dragons overtaking the two Sushi Crew members, SSL and Ramey brawling on another side of the ring, and Mandrake and Enmity making their way back into the ring, Raizo stands in the center of the ring looking around like an anxious and confused man. He lets out a loud growl as he turns around and grabs the nearest person…AND EDDIE MANDRAKE IS THE RECIEVER OF A SHI-NE~!!!

Raizo: SHI-NE~!!!

Hitomi: THAT’S HIS OWN TAG PARTNER!!

Mori: Raizo doesn’t care anymore! This match is out of control, and when things get tense, the SAIKYO SENSHI takes over in him!!

Raizo begins to savagely stomp away on Mandrake. Enmity shrugs before joining in on the beatdown. Taki Kuonji rushes over to stop Raizo from beating on his own man, but Raizo scoops him up and sends his body crashing into the mat with a SHI-NE~!!!

Raizo: AH!! SHI-NE~!!!

MAH: That’s unacceptable!!! You can’t hit the official!!

Mori: Raizo… JUST… DID!!

Hitomi: THIS IS HORRENDOUS!!

The fans are livid as Raizo stomps away on Taki’s body before booting him out of the ring. Then he turns back to Eddie Mandrake, stomping away on him with Enmity. The Black Dragons throw Ryo and Otoshi into the ring, Chen and Chi’ing climbing to the top ropes as Chiang grabs Ryo. Otoshi is the recipient of DEATH FROM ABOVE from Chi’ing and Chen, while Chiang double powerbombs Ryo followed by a powerful overhead stomach crusher!!

Hitomi: Death from Above and Assasination!! The Black Dragons have taken out the Sushi Crew!

MAH: Where’s Shaun Styles??

Mori: Securing his scalp for lice! HAH!

Hitomi: This is no laughing matter!! The people demand answers!!!

As Enmity pulls Eddie up and nails him DEPRESSANT I, Ramey turns around to notice what’s going on in the ring. He jumps on the apron and yells for Raizo to do something about Enmity. Raizo spins around and boots Ramey off the apron, right onto the shoulders of SINISTER STEVEN LEE!!

Hitomi: This is not going to be good!

SSL gets a sick look on his face as he turns his back towards the announce table.

Mori: I’m getting out of here!!

The announce team scatters as SSL throws Ramey up and drills him with S FORCE 5 STRAIGHT INTO THE ANNOUNCE TABLE!!! The fans are booing heavily as SSL yells in the knocked out Ramey’s face. Raizo stands alone as The Black Dragons throw the Sushi Crew out of the ring. SSL slides into the ring and stands in one corner, Raizo in the center of the ring while the Black Dragons in another.

Hitomi: This is sheer madness! The heels have cleared out the faces!!

Mori: Evil has triumphed tonight! EVIL Bento Box special tonight with EVIL-EEL…only at MORI’S!!!

Enmity releases Mandrake from the Depressant and stares at the rest of the men in the ring. Raizo points to Mandrake and motions for E to pull him up. E does so, and Raizo glares into his face.

Raizo: Sonomama…

He hefts up Mandrake and plows him into the mat with…

Raizo:…SHI-NE~!!!

Mori: DIE WHERE YOU ARE!!! THAT’S RIGHT!!

Hitomi: The people are going nuts over this! They look rabid!!

Fans throw litter into the ring as Enmity stands across from Raizo. The Saikyo Senshi stretches his hand out towards E. Enmity looks on reluctantly, but has all reason for it…

MAH: SSL HAS A CHAIR!!

SSL slides back into the ring and connects with the steel chair into Enmity’s back! He falls forward right into waiting arms of Raizo Tenzan…pulling him high up into the air and knocking him out cold with the straight right punch! E’s body flops down right onto a positioned steel chair by SSL!!

Hitomi: Raizo and SSL just took out Enmity!! Now they and the Black Dragons are stomping him out!! What’s going on here??

Mori: I think I see the start of something great!!

They throw Enmity out of the ring next to Mandrake, leaving The Black Dragons, Sinister Steven Lee, and Raizo Tenzan standing alone. Raizo goes to the corner and climbs up, folding his arms and glaring out at the fans. The Black Dragons go to another corner and raise their fists to the air, while SSL lifts his arms at another corner. Then they all come to the center of the ring and all at once hit fists with each other, “Deep Impact: DJ Krush Mix” by Dragon Ash not even enough to overcome the deafening boos from the people.

Hitomi: What do these five men have to do with each other?? They hate each other!! This is insane!!!

Mori: I just thing the Strongest Soldier just got himself some more soldiers in his army!!

Hitomi: We’re running out of time, but we’ll be sure to show up next week for Ikusa, where maybe we can get an answer to what just happened here!! I’m Hitomi Asami, with Mori Morishigi and Mang-A-Hero saying GOODNIGHT!!

Mori: And EAT MY BALLS AT MORI’S!!

The PURE 2003 logo comes across the bottom of the screen as Raizo glares dead into the camera, the men behind him showing the same intensity and hatred.
