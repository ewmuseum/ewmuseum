Bio

Lived in Imperial Beach all of his life. Had a very normal child hood living on the beach's with friends, surfing on the waves, etc. At About the age of 16 he dropped out of school because he wanted to just live his life having fun. He worked in a small Pawn Shop in downtown Imperial Beach and lived with his parents untill he was 21.


A few months after his 21st birthday both his parents died when thier house that they all lived in burned down. AJ was with his friends surfing at the time and he has never forgotten that. Having no place to live ,AJ was forced to quit his job at the Pawn Shop and look for a better paying job. To take his mind of things , AJ's friends took him to a local wrestling show.. AJ's eyes grew bigger and bigger with every match he saw.


For the next couple months AJ ate, slept, and dreamed about wrestling. Hell AJ LIVED wrestling. HE studied tapes of his favorite wrestlers and went to the gym to work out every day. He finaly broke into the sports entertainment scene with the same local promotion he say just a few months back. He thought he was ready, he thought he knew what he was doing. His first match was a complete bomb. He worked stiff, he sold the moves way before it was time, and he even fell off the top rope trying to do an arial move. After the match the fans booed and through things at him. AJ never gave up though, for the next few years he worked harder than he had ever worked before.


Getting a steady pay check was no longer his priority. After lossing his first match he never wanted to lose again. He became fixated on winning. Every loss ate at his soul. In the back people all loved him. He fooled around, played cards with the guys, and even watched tapes with them. But when his music hit and he headed for the ring all that changed. He face would go to a serious look and he would show no emotion. The talent in the back and even the fans started calling him 'Ice Man' for his cold ways in the ring. Showing no mercy or consideration for the others well being. The name stuck with him for the rest of his career.


On the side AJ always wanted to be a police man, so in his off time he took class up at the Imperial Beach Community College in law enforcement. He graduated in the middle of his class but saw there was no work in the police field. He overheard some people talking about calling a Private I to help them solve a case and new THAT'S what he would do. Joined a small time groupd of P.I's along with trying to make it to the top in his wrestling career, AJ is ready and willing to do WHATEVER it takes.
