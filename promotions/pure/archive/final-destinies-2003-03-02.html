Strength in Numbers-The Suburban Ninja Clan
Another press conference held by the SNC, this time it is a ninja standing in front of the press.

#40: Hello, I am ninja #40 of the Suburban Ninja Clan. I have a statement to make about the SNC and their future in PURE.

Reaching into his the cuff of his sleeve, he pulls out some notecards.

#40: The SNC has arranged for itself the #1 Contenders match to the PURE Trinity Tag Team titles. It is facing Nu Era, three unskilled gaijin from the United States. There are ninja in the SNC who say that Nu Era have no chance in winning this match.

The ninja looks at the back of the card, then at the front again. He tosses it away.

#40: Sorry, it stopped there. Anyway, the Suburban Ninja Clan has sent the perfect representatives for this match. Nu Era as a team, is flawed, because a) they are from America, and b) they are too alike. Each of them is around 6'2" and 6'3" and they weigh about 230. Their styles are too similar. We know how one wrestlers, we know how they all wrestle. They are somewhat technical, somewhat able to fly. But they wrestle the American style of spot-wrestling. They have no plan, no psychology. They just get in the ring and throw out moves hoping something works. Our ninja are capable of dealing with such an offense and completely breaking down the competition.

Some cameras go off.

#40: Now, I won't be participating in the match, but I shall speak a little on those ninja who shall. I will not name them specifically, only their styles. The first ninja we are sending into the match is a powerhouse. No doubt, much of Nu Era's offense, if not all, will be ineffective against this ninja. He is solidly built; submissions would be awkward to put him in, he'd be too heavy for Nu Era to lift based on file information as to their physical performance in strength tests, and their impact strikes would bounce off of him. Granted, he may not be as fast as Nu Era, but they only have a finite amount of space to work within, quite limited, indeed. Our ring is larger than what the Americans are used to, but they still have nowhere to run.

Yuri Takayama stands up with a question.

Yuri: Any particular attacks this ninja specializes in?

#40: All manner of slams, drops, and bombs. I assure you, you will neer see a more damagin Nodowa Otoshi than what this ninja performs. His finishing move is a Crossed-Arms Powerbomb; the arms of the opponent are crossed over their own throat before the bomb, making it both an impact bomb and a choking slam. Kickouts cannot happen, the opponent would have no air left and be too disoriented.

Yuri: As for the others?

The ninja nods.

#40: Our next ninja is technically superior to any of the Nu Era members. He prides himself on being proficient in all manner of close-range grappling holds and submissions. Unlike the Nu Era gaijin, whose style is often disjointed, anything and everything that this ninja does flows into his next move. He is a master of chain wrestling. His finisher... well, all I shall reveal is that it will look like one move, but go into something completely different.

Some more flashbulbs light up the podium where the ninja stands. The ninja's outfit seems to swallow the light instead of reflect it.

Yuri: That leaves one more ninja...

Ninja #40 tosses away his other notecards.

#40: Yes... the last ninja that the SNC shall commit to this match is a master of mixed martial arts. He does not "wrestle," per se, instead opting to use a striking offense. His kicks and punches are the hardest in the wrestling circuit today. He executes attacks with critical accuracy and surgical precision. His finisher is somewhat a submission. While the arms will not be free and the face bashed repeatedly, the opponent will have about a 1.5 seconds in between impacts to yell "I give."

Finishing, the ninja is finished with the notecards, but not with his speech.

#40: Each ninja specializes in a particular fighting style, and together they perfectly compliment each. Nu Era is in effect a team of three singles wrestlers. They team together because they are not a threat separately. However, as they have demonstrated time and time again, the fact that their heart is not in PURE and in the tradition of puroresu, they are not a threat as a team. The Clan has dispatched countless opponents just the same as Nu Era, tonight will be no different.

Another reporter, looking like a carbon copy Akira as he was dressed in the final battle with Tetsuo in "Akira," stands up.

Taka: Takahashi Nobusaki, PuroInformer. We have seen some other ninjas around the back. Who are they?

#40: Ah, yes. Well, as you can see, I am giving this press conference. There are other ninjas from the Clan who have come to make their appearance. They would no doubt make some sort of conversation with the other wrestlers in PURE. None of the ones hanging around backstage will be wrestling tonight. We like to keep that secret until the match. Nu Era now knows everything that they need to, not that it will help them. Nu Era requires a triple-team finisher. Our ninja, while a formidable combination, can win the match on their own. And now, I shall have to close this press conference. Everything that we wish to reveal has been so at this time. Good day.

Futile attempts at more questions as #40 leaves the podium and exits through the door at his side of the room.

You can see as the sun is very slowly setting as a man walks into the view with a pair of white karate gi pants with a green dragon flowing throughout the pants. The man has his head down with a huge white hat on and is currently topless. He lifts his head slowly and quickly shifts his head towards the camera exposing his red and white eyes. The white portion begins to glow a lime green color as the sun sets even more and the man removes his big hat.

Man: If you don't know by now...I am Ken Misawa. You should already know why I have come here. I've come to weed out the crap and gain some well-deserved respect. I--
