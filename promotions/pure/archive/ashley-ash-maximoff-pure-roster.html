Bio

Born in the year, 2599. Her DNA flows with the traits of the greatest heroes and leaders of the ages. She was one of 100 embryos that were created in a laboratory by Dr. Peter Caine and transplanted into a hand picked foster mothers. It was all a government project to create the greatest military force the planet had ever seen. Stronger, faster, smarter. 99 out of the 100 embryos devleoped into healthy young, male, babies. However, one was flawed, her's. Dr. Caine forgot to give her the 'Y' chromosome that would have made her body develop testosterone, instead of estrogen. At the age of 5, she was plucked from her home and taken into an underground government facility, along with her 'brothers'. They were all put through very extensive training, which taught them various forms of fighting styles, weaponry, and spy tactics. Ash and her brothers spent 15 years locked away from society. However, something was not right with Ashley. With each passing moment, she became increasingly rebellious. One fateful day, the force was given a mission and Ashley would not do as instructed. The executive of the project, nicknamed 'Project Lamda,'ordered her termination. In the blink of an eye, her brothers turned against her and Ashley was forced to slay many of them before she finally escaped.


For just over a year, Ashley walked the apocalyptic world, fightig against the goverment that created her and stripped the people of their civil rights. A leader of a famous rebellion group came calling one day. His scientists had created a time machine, in which they want to use to change the past and Ashley is their only hope. She agrees and leaps into the portal...
