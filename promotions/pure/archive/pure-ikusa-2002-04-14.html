PURE — Card

Ikusa, 14 April 2002

14 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

The screen goes black for a moment before turning Aubergine... yes that’s right... Aubergine. Suddenly in white the words “Expect the Unexpected” flash three times and “Come with me” by Puff Daddy begins to play over the system as one by one every single roster member of PURE is highlighted... Masanobu Akazi... Shirata Rinjiro... Steven Windsor... Brian Simon... Carl Kimbell... The End... Ebola III... Billy Larkin... Cege Heyabusa... The HIT... Naekura Toryama... Takumi Umeshita... Inphino Blitz... Hiro Saotome... Johanthan Drachen... Charlie Loc... Alexander Miaygawa and finally suddenly a man wearing a Godzilla costume appears and as the Godzilla roars at the end of the song and man springs from just outside the camera view and spears the Godzilla to the floor... Kensuke Suzahara.
The camera pans around the sold out interior of the Expo ’70 Stadium in Osaka, Japan showing the wide variety of people who have come to see the first ever PURE card. We pan around the crowd showing various signs such as “Oh My God, They Killed Kensuke!” and “I was paid to hold up this sign” before we pan down to the three ring Hanza s of Puroresu Ultimate Ring Entertainment. They are of course the beautiful and intelligent Hitomi Asami, the rather... rotund Mori Morishigi and the cold hearted Kobentai Kiyashi.

Hitomi Asami: Let’s get straight into it, it is already time for the first match in the history of Puroresu Ultimate Ring Entertainment! Let us go up to our ring Hanza so he may introduce the participants.

Hanza : This contest is scheduled for one fall, and it will be the first match ever in PURE!

Hanza : Introducing first…

“You Get To Burning” by Yumi Matsuzawa plays, as “Jido-San” Masanobu Azaki makes his way to the ring.

Hanza : From Osaka, Japan..standing 6’4”, and weighing 270 lbs… “Jido San” AZAKI….MASANOBU!

The name is pronounced family name first, as is done in Japanese culture. Jido-san enters the squared circle, awaiting his rival as “Tear Away” by Drowning Pool hits.

Hanza : His opponent hails from the island of Okinawa! Standing 6’6”, and weighing 278 pounds….RINJIRO……SHIRATA!

The two men circle each other intently, as the bell rings, once.

DING!

Hitomi Asami: Here we go!

Azaki and Rinjiro stare into each other’s eyes slowly. A handshake is exchanged, as the men quickly lock up. Jido-San strikes first, with a forearm to the forehead. He fires off a second, and then spins around, catching Shirata with a knifedge chop to the throat.

Mori Morishigi: Quick offensive flurry from Masanobu!

Kobentai Kiyashi, the third commentator, seems rather bored with the proceedings.

Kobentai Kiyashi: I wish to see more emotion from both competitors. This is not America... they cannot go out there on this Puroresu television show and punch each other for twenty minutes and expect to be cheered for their pathetic effort.

Masanobu spins for another chop, but Shirata Rinjiro evades the blow, then throws a sharp palm strike to Azaki’s chest. He steps back, and uses his size to take Azaki to the canvas with a standing lariat.

Hitomi Asami: Rinjiro coming back to assert himself early in the bout.

Mori Morishigi: Absolutely! This early stage of the contest is vital to both men.

Azaki gets back to his feet, and lays in another series of forearm shots. He bends Rinjiro forward, following these blows with a DDT. Masanobu aims, and drops an elbow across Shirata’s broad chest.

Kobentai Kiyashi: Now we are getting somewhere.

Azaki grabs Rinjiro’s arm, attempting to apply a Fujiwara armbar. However, this is blocked, as Rinjiro nips up to his feet, a nice display of agility. Shirata grabs Azaki by the left leg, taking him over in a dragon screw legwhip.

Hitomi Asami: Quick takedown from Shirata Rinjiro!

Mori Morishigi: But can he capitalize upon this position of power?

Rinjiro maintains his grip upon the leg of Azaki, repositioning slightly to attempt to cinch in an anklelock, but Azaki uses his hands to drag himself quickly in a belly crawl to the bottom rope before grabbing it, as Rinjiro respectfully stops his hold attempt.

Hitomi Asami: Rinjiro-san almost gained a definite advantage upon Masanobu Azaki.

Mori Morishigi: But Azaki-san was able to avoid the calculated attempt to take advantage of the weakened leg.

Kobentai Kiyashi: Good because otherwise this match might have ended prematurely.

Azaki gets up slowly, gripping his leg from the hold, and leans against the ropes. Rinjiro runs towards Azaki and attempts a spear, but Azaki, using the ropes as leverage, springs up and takes down Rinjiro from behind with a dragon suplex. The referee goes down to count the pin

Mori Morishigi: We may have a winner of match right now!

Hitomi Ashami: The referee is counting, 1...2...We have a kickout at the last moment by Shirata Rinjiro.

Kobentai Kiyashi: I do not wish this match would just end. That is not Puroresu.

Rinjiro and Azaki both take their time recovering from the move and Azaki is the first one up from the move. He picks up the struggling Rinjiro and tries to hit a swinging neckbreaker but Rinjiro counters the move and forces Azaki to make a full rotation of the move and he uses the momentum to DDT Azaki.

Hitomi Ashami: That was a brilliant counter by “Jido-San!”

Kobentai Kiyashi: That is something to aspire to..

Azaki lays on the mat, in pain, as Rinjiro climbs the first and second rope. He then positions himself on top of the turnbuckle and flies off with a guillotine leg drop, but Azaki smartly moves out of the way, rolling out of danger. Azaki now gets up and just as Rinjiro is getting up, and hits a quick dropkick, knocking the man back over.

Mori Morishigi: Very nice use of the dropkick by Azaki.

Hitomi Ashami: He is gaining control of the match very quickly.

Kobentai Kiyashi: That is good. The talent level in this arena might be indeed rising at this precise moment.

Azaki now goes over to the man lying on the mat and lock him in sitting abdominal stretch. Rinjiro tries to reach the ropes, but with every second the pain on his face increases. He reaches, but misses. He reaches again and misses. Now he has stopped the reaching and the referee raises his arm. It falls. His arm is raised again, and again Rinjiro’s arm falls. For a third time his arm is raised, but this time it stays up and Shirata Rinjiro begins to throw elbows into the face of Masanobu Azaki.

Kobentai Kiyashi: A good break away there and the match continues.

Hitomi Ashami: This match does indeed continue, and Shirata Rinjiro has new life within his body!

Rinjiro continues to throw elbows until Azaki is forced to release the hold. Rinjiro whips Azaki into the turnbuckle. He runs at the man and connects with a powerful spear!

Mori Morishigi: Rinjiro-san calls that move Act of Fate!

Kobentai Kiyashi: And in Puroresu we do not Act.

Hitomi Ashami: Shirata Rinjiro is going to try to end this match right now!

Rinjiro goes and picks up a groggy Azaki and sets him up into a vertical suplex position. He goes to slam the man down into a jackhammer, but Azaki escapes and kicks Rinjiro in the gut, then hits a cradled inverted DDT!

Hitomi Ashami: That was the Azaki-Bomb onto Rinjiro-san!

Mori Morishighi- Both men are very close to nailing a move that can decide this match’s outcome!

Azaki tries to lock on Azaki Garame, a head and arm lock finishing hold, but Riniro slips away and quickly hits a Jackhammer. He then picks the man up and hits another Jackhammer! He follows with a third Jackhammer! He is now looking tired and picks up Akazi for one more. He holds him in the air for about ten seconds and then slams him down with amazing force!

Hitomi Ashami: That was an amazing display of power from Shirata Rinjiro!

Mori Morishigi: He just hit Downfall onto Masanobu Akazi!

Kobentai Kiyashi: This should end the match.

Rinjiro covers 1…2…3

Winner: Shirata Rinjiro

Mori Morishigi: That was an amazing start to PURE’s first show! We’ve got much more to come, but first we must see a commercial!

((Commercial: Morishigi’s House of Sushi... bring the family. **cuts to an image of Mori Morishigi eating a lot of Sushi extremely quickly**))

Kobentai Kiyashi: You have no shame.

Mori Morishigi: What? Because I take pleasure in eating... you should take pleasure in something.

Kobentai Kiyashi: I take pleasure in reminding people like you not to embarrass yourselves publically.

Mori Morishigi: Nice hobby.

Kobentai Kiyashi: I thought so too.

Hitomi Asami: Excuse me ladies, but we have some footage shot earlier today to show the public.

Mori and Kobentai: Ladies?

“Earlier Today” appears in blue letters in the bottom left hand corner of the screen. The camera cuts to show the empty arena, where the ring crew toiled around setting up the ring and stage, preparing for the first Ikusa show that night. The bleachers are empty of spectators, and the voices of the stage crew are faint, though understandable from the camera’s position all the way in the back of the arena in the high seats. Then, a voice is heard from nearby off camera.

Voice: This...is the beginning.

The camera turns to its left to find one of PURE’s new superstars, Inphino Blitz, sitting in the bleachers next to the camera. Dressed in his trademark all black shirt and pants, he leans back with his arms folded across his chest and his legs propped up on the back of the seats in front of him. From underneath the mass of blue and black hair, he wore a pair of black shades.

Inphino: Those diligent workers down there...they work with all they have, determined to do their jobs the best that they possibly can to make sure that this night is special. And they have every right...for this night is indeed special. This night marks the beginning of a new era in the never-ending dance of battle. Ikusa, the debut show of PURE, where those who wish to do battle compete in the form that it was created...with honor, fighter’s pride, and pure...skill.

He lets out a sigh as he leans forward, resting his forearms on his knees.

Inphino: The beginning is always special, for it leaves so many questions left to be answered later on. What shall happen...what shall not happen...all of these questions will be answered, while new questions will be forged. The end will always seem like it is never near...for we all are living in the middle right at this moment, and beginnings come about again and again in the midst of our own stories called life. This is yet another beginning for me...hopefully one that will prove to be better than the impromptu ending last time.

Blitz turns towards the camera, removing his sunglasses and staring with his dark eyes into the lens.

Inphino: This is PURE Ikusa...pure war...and like all wars, there shall be winners and losers. What we all must ask ourselves...is if we are not ready for victory...but if we are ready for defeat. If you are pure...then there is nothing for you to be concerned about. If you are not pure...then the Black Dragon Master will lead by example on what it is to be...pure.

The Black Dragon Master sighs again as he slips on his sunglasses and leans back against his chair again.

Inphino: So...the battle...begins...

***Cut Scene***

The scene opens up to show the PURE logo on a white backdrop behind a long table set up on a small stage about two feet high. At the center of the table was a small podium and a microphone atop of it. Below the stage, an audience of reporters and news hounds sat murmuring amongst themselves as they waited for the conference to begin. Then as if on cue, they all began clapping as PURE owner, Alexander Miyagawa, stepped on stage before the podium.

Miyagawa: Thank you all for attending this press conference. As I am sure you know, I am Alexander Miyagawa, owner of the Japan-based PURE Wrestling. Allow me to introduce to you all now a man who name precedes his reputation. Ladies and gentlemen...Inphino Blitz!

Applause fills the room as Inphino Blitz steps into the room along with a light brown haired, black bandana wearing woman following after him. She was dressed in tight blue jeans, a black thong exposed above her waistline, black boots, and a black sleeveless halter top with the words “BLITZ” etched out in dark blue across the chest. Inphino bows to the audience first before bowing to Miyagawa. Then he shakes hands with the owner, giving a small hug, before the woman with him shakes hands with Miyagawa as well. He steps to the side as Inphino and the woman with him take the podium.

Inphino: Let me say that is a honor as well as a pleasure to be doing battle in a federation that holds so much potential and so much talent. I would like to personally as well as publicly thank Miyagawa for giving me this opportunity to compete in this place of battle...allowing me the opportunity to return into the ranks of Primetime Central. Also, let me thank you all as well for taking time out of your busy schedules to speak with someone the likes of me.

The reporters clap for Inphino’s appreciation towards themselves and Miyagawa.

Inphino: Now, with that aside, let us begin with this conference. Are there any questions?

A plethora of hands shoot into the air at once. Inphino looks around at the hands slowly before picking one out of the crowd.

Inphino: Yes...you.

The hands lower as a youthful woman in a black suit and a notepad reads off her question.

Reporter #1: Thank you, Inphino. I think the question that everyone is dying to ask is where have you been since your disappearance from Primetime Championship Wrestling?

Inphino lets out a deep sigh before answering the controversial question.

Inphino: Well...to clear things up, I was informed that my… sister… was declared dead sometime around New Dawning and my world title match. That knocked my focus and preparation for that match off target, therefore forcing me into a defeat at the hands of Trashcan Man. Afterwards, I raced back here to Japan, only to find that my sister was not dead, and it was all a ruse. I was the victim of foul play...all devised by the one who calls himself the Neo Void God...PCW’s Akiyama. With my PCW contract terminated and my life also thought to be terminated... I saw no other alternative other than to train and hone my skills in a small Indy federation, biding my time until I could make my return. And so...here I am this day before you all.

Hands shoot up across the room again. Inphino points out a middle-aged man in a brown suit.

Reporter#2: Inphino...what are your plans for PURE? Any plans on making a return into PCW?

Inphino: My lifelong quest has always been to train my own personal martial arts skill, perfecting my technique as I have been destined to do so. As of now, my plans are to merely exist in PURE as an example, or quite possibly an embodiment of everything PURE stands for. Courage, spirit, might, skill, honor...and I hope by leading by example, I will guide other fighters into what it truly means to do battle on all aspects of the mind, body, and soul. If my quest and my training leads me towards becoming champion of this place, then so be it. As far as PCW goes... I would take my quest there also if they were willing to accept me back after the disgrace I caused them on my curt exit. Next question?

A gray suited man with a black beard stands with a palm pilot in hand.

Reporter#3: Mr. Blitz, who is this lovely woman by your side?

Inphino glances back at her, as she smiles at the reporters.

Inphino: This...is Mina de Margo. She is a long-term acquaintance of mine. She is quite skilled in managerial duties, as well as wrestling. I thought she could be an asset not only to myself, but to PURE as well as being one of their first female competitors...that is, if Miyagawa will allow it.

Miyagawa stands up quickly and grabs the microphone with a smirk.

Miyagawa: We’ll have to look into that, Blitz.

The audience chuckles lightly as Inphino picks another reporter. A woman in a white blouse and black bow stands.

Reporter #4: What are your thoughts on your debut match tonight against “The Dark Lotus” Naekura Toryama, Hiro Saotome, and Takumi Umeshita?

Inphino clears his throat before starting.

Inphino: I look forward to this battle greatly. This will be the first time that I am meeting both of these men, and from the information I have been receiving, they sound to be just the competitors that I crave to pit my skills against. Takumi Umeshita, from what I have learned, is a GWF major superstar, Hiro Saotome is said to be a talented opponent, and Naekura Toryama is said to be a former PCW Extreme champion. Both of these men have earned my respect for their achievements, and I am hoping that I have earned their respect as well. This battle will prove to be most challenging, but I am confident in my skills. Without confidence in one’s self, there is no hope in defeating others, and in the end the only person that will be defeated will be yourself. I do declare now that both of these men will find out first hand just how much training and growth I have been through since my absence.

One more reporter shoots up to her feet, this one dressed in a black skirt, black blazer with the lapels of her brown silk blouse peeking from underneath. She pulls a pen out of her dark brown ponytail and preps to take notes from the Black Dragon Master.

Reporter #5: Hello, I’m Yuri Takazawa, freelance reporter working with Puroresu NOW magazine. What are your feelings on the man who has quite possibly ruined your career in PCW when you had built quite possibly the fastest rise to World title level ever in PCW, Akiyama?

Inphino: I do not agree with the methods that he displayed before to deceive me, or do I approve of his actions thus far. However, my presence in PCW was not forgotten so easily, and it has haunted him for most of his career there, only now to have finally grown out of my shadow. If I am ever to return to PCW...then naturally Akiyama will be a major issue to deal with.

Yuri: One more question. Has the thought come across your mind to reform the PCW stable, The Elements, considering that there are three out of the four original members present within PURE?

Inphino looks over to Miyagawa, who gives the Black Dragon Master a knowing look.

Inphino: We will let the black winds of destiny deal with those matters. That is all for now, I have a match to prepare for. With that said and done...the Black Dragon Master has spoken.

Inphino bows to the reporters before posing for a few pictures with Miyagawa and Mina de Margo. Then he shakes hands with Miyagawa and walks with him offstage.

We return to the three commentators once again.


Windsor versus Simon versus Kimbell
Mori: Looks like we are preparing to move into our next PURE match-up, another triple threat with this one including three powerhouses of PURE.

Hitomi: Those powerhouses being Carl Kimbell, "Golden Bear" Steve Windsor, and "The Future Legend" Brian Simon, all looking to be great heavyweight competitiors in this federation.

Kiyashi: Forgive me if I don't sound impressed at the moment, I want to see what the men have to offer before singing their praises.

Hitomi: I think PURE is all about singing and not bringing Kiyashi.

"Hellbound by D12 blares over the loud speakers as the fans break out into boos as Carl Kimball emerges from the entryway, walking down the aisle with not a word to say and his focus deadset on the ring. He slides in, removing his dark sunglasses and climbing a turnbuckle, raising his arms high within the air as the boos continue for Kimball, who shortly dismounts the turnbuckle and stretches out on the ropes.

Ring Hanza : FROM NEW YORK, U.S.A.....WEIGHING IN AT 320 POUNDS.....CAAAAARL KIMBAAAAALL!!

Hitomi: I guess booing Kimball was their first impression. Says a lot I suppose.

Kiyashi: It doesn’t matter what the fans think, they don't decide who is the best do they?

"God Save The Queen" plays next over the speakers, the fans cheering as "The Future Legend" Brian Simon makes his way out of the entryway, raising his arm in air and pointing out to the Japanese crowd as they cheer him back in return. He crosses sides down the aisle, tagging a few hands before leaping up on the apron and stepping in through the ropes, stretching out his arms as he again waves to the fans.

Ring Hanza : FROM BLACKPOOL, ENGLAND, WEIGHING IN AT 265 POUNDS....."THE FUTURE LEGEND"....BRIIIIIAN SIIIIIMON!!!

Mori: He's what many call a "shooter" a straight up, in your face style of wrestling, ti shoudl beinteresting to see how he meshes with these two powerhouses.

Finally, the roar of a bear echoes through the arena followed by "This Life" by Primer 55 blareing over the sound system. Out from the entryway comes "Golden Bear" Steve Windsor, outstretching his arms and roaring like his namesake as the fans cheer back with excitement. He walks down the aisle, ready to fight, a few hands slapping his shoulders and ripped arms before he slides in the ring. He takes a corner for himself, roaring one more time to the crowd as they respond in cheers.*

Hitomi: He sure knows how to get a crowd response.

Mori: Many say Steve Windsor is quite an exciting athelete to look out for.

Ring Hanza : AND THEIR OPPONENT...HAILING FROM EDMONTON, ALBERTA, CANADA....WEIGHING IN AT 285 POUNDS....."THE GOLDEN BEAR" STEVE WINDSOR!!!

The fans cheer as the referee makes sure all the men are within their corners. He then signals for the bell and all three men begin to encircle each other carefully, each trying to feel out which one they want to go for. Finally, Simon makes the first move, dropping to his knees and attempting a double leg takedown on Windsor, who blocks it by standing upright and holding him about the waist. Simon digs into his power and drives him upward with his strength, only managing to move him back. Windsor then pounds on Simon with forearm blows to the back, finally getting Simon to release the hold before he throws a clothesline, Simon ducking and attempting one of his own, which he misses as Windsor ducks.

Hitomi: Nice catch as catch can display by Windsor and Simon

Kiyashi: Lets see how things degenerate between these three

Upon Simon missing his clothesline, he is caught in a belly to belly set up by Carl Kimball, who snuck in from nowhere. He then tries to power Simon up, Simon blocking Kimball's suplex with his foot and quickly turning it into a sideways body toss to the mat, Simon chaining that toss into a reverse headlock, cranking on Kimball as he tries not to give up. Simon then release the headlock and floats over to the leg of Kimball, taking one as Windsor comes in and takes the other. They both look to each other, both men turning Kimball over and taking a leg, each one sitting back and pulling on a leg as they execute a double Boston Crab on the hapless Kimball, the fans clapping for what they've seen so far.

Mori: Double Boston Crab by Windsor and Simon, the fans seem to be enjoying this display.

Kiyashi: The fans may be motivation, but they are not what gives the win Mori. Remember, it's not how many touchdowns during the game...it's what's on the score board that counts.

Mori: There hasn't been much on your "score" board in awhile eh Kiyashi?

Hitomi: One for Mori.

Both men crank up on the legs of Kimball, the referee not knowing how to call this unusual hold as Kimball is writhing in pain. Windsor looks over to Simon and throws a hard right hand, Simon falling from the blow and losing the hold as Windsor releases his leg as well, leaping up and dropping an elbow on the downed Simon. Grabbing him by the hair, he lifts up Simon and set him up for a backdrop, but suddenly, Carl Kimball sneaks up behind Windsor and sinches him in a backdrop set up as well. Carl lifts up at the same time Windsor was lifting up, Simon and Windsor high up before BOTH men come crashing to the ground with that unusual double backdrop, Kimball feeling the least of the impact. More claps fill the arena and the roar of the crowd follows as the crowd watches on at this heavyweight 3 way.

Mori: INCREDIBLE DOUBLE BACKDROP RIGHT THERE!!

Hitomi: They've all come here to win Mori.

Mori: It's a shame only ONE can win.

Kimball shakes his head, slightly groggy from the move as he picks up a limp Steven Windsor, pummeling him a few times with right hands until he is backed in the corner. Kimball then proceeds to deliver a series of back elbows, snapping Windsor's head back with every impact before Kimball kicks him in the stomach, bending Windsor over.

Hitomi: This can't be good...

Kiyashi: It can't be good for Windsor...but it's great for Kimball.

Hitomi: You sure know how to be a prick about everything eh Kiyashi?

Mori: I'm telling you, a good geisha would clear all that up for him.

Kiyashi: Keep it up Mori...

Mori: And maybe a couple of kilos of Sushi from Morishigi’s House of Sushi as well.

Kiyashi: Mori...

Kimball then sticks Windsor between his legs, setting him up in a powerbomb position before lifting him up, holding him high in air before tossing him back first into the turnbuckle as Windsor crumples within it, Kimball adding a few stomps for added pain.

Hitomi: Windsor has got to be CRUSHED by that turnbuckle powerbomb.

Before Kimball can get in any more offence, Brian Simon gets behind him grabs at his legs, taking Kimball down face first to the mat. Simon then floats over, locking his hands around the waist of Carl Kimball and using all his strength to lift him up and drop him with a German suplex, the ref jumping down to count, but Simon stands back up, picking up Kimball as well with the lock still in place as he German suplexes him YET again, this time bridging for a pin.

Hitomi: 1...2...NOO!! Broken up by Golden Bear!

Mori: Quite impressive series of Germans there.

Windsor begins to trade blows with Simon, both throwing punches repeatedly at each other as they both rise to their feet. Once both are standing on their shaky legs, Simon throws a palm strike to the face of Golden Bear, who ducks it and grabs around the waist of Brian Simon with his head still down, powering him up and tossing him over with a release Northern Lights suplex, Simon slamming against the mat and holding his back in pure pain. Windsor seems to be getting his second wind as he then pounds away on the face of Simon with punches before lifting him back up, slinging him into the ropes and upon him returning, pressing him high into the air, showing off his strength to the fans who "oooo" and "ahhh" at ringside as he holds up Simon effortlessly.

Mori: What a strength display by Steven Windsor.

Kiyashi: I almost feel sorry for the nasty spill Brian Simon is about to take...keyword is ALMOST though.

Hitomi: Have you no shame Kiyashi?

Kiyashi: Have you no brain Hitomi?

As Windsor asks the fans where in the ring he should drop Simon, who screams in fear of the drop he knows he shall take, Carl Kimball sizes up Windsor and throws a picture perfect superkick he calls the Kimball Kick and connects right with Windsor's jaw, Windsor toppling over with Simon falling on his wide chest, making it an inadvertant splash due to Kimball's interference. The ref counts the pin for Simon, who hooks the legs for the pin he now has

Hitomi: 1...2..NO HE DOSEN'T HAVE IT!!

Kiyashi: Smart of Simon to take advantage of the pin, even though he didn't get it.

Kimball grabs Simon around the neck and raises him up off of Windsor, placing his hand on the British Brawler's back and lifting him up high for a chokeslam, Simon's feet dangling in the air, but before Kimball can even drive down, Simon swings himself over and reverses it into an elevated armdrag, Kimball hitting the mat with great force with Simon chaining that reversal into a Fujiwara armbar, Simon pulling back mercilessly on the arm he now has and not willing to let go until he hears the tapping of Kimball, the ref checking the hold.

Mori: Incredible reversal by Brian Simon, his shoot fighting background really helped in that situation.

Kiyashi: Question is, can he make Kimball tap?

Simon continues to hold the submission, Kimball slowly crawling for the ropes as he yells and screams in dire pain, his breaking point close to being met at anytime. Kimball throws his hand out, just making the ropes with his fingers as the ref orders Simon to break the hold. Reluctantly, Simon releases the hold as he dashes over to the turnbuckle, climbing it and awaiting Kimball to arise. Like clockwork, he soon does, Brian Simon leaping off for a sunset flip off the top and nailing it, but Kimball fights falling down from the move, wailing his arms about so he can keep his balance as Simon fights it. Suddenly, Steve Windsor NAILS Kimball with his patented big boot called the Nightlight, Kimball's head cocking back before the power of the boot sends him over and on his back as the strength from that boot topples Windsor over, unable to get up at the moment. Simon holds the sunset slip pin as the ref makes the count, along with the hot crowd, but suddenly Steven Windsor rolls and reverses the pin!!!

Mori: 1...2...3!!! AMAZING WIN FOR STEVEN WINDSOR HERE!!

Kiyashi: I don't like the guy, but he showed that taking advantage of the situation can win you the match.

Mori: I didn’t pay for that advertisement.

Asami: We have an excellent match approaching right now. The next match is a triple threat match in a part of the PURE Jr. Heavyweight Tournament pitting three of our gaijin wrestlers against each other! You can definitely expect some of the Western style of wrestling in this match!

Mori: The End’s half Japanese, so what does that make him?

Kiyashi: A half-breed...technically speaking.

Asami: (sighing) Let’s hand it over to Hanza Kibagame for the announcements.

The PURE ring Hanza stands in the middle of the ring, dressed in black slacks and a white dress shirt and tie. He adjusts his glasses before clearing his throat and lifting the microphone to his face.

Hanza: Ladies and gentlemen...the next match is a triple threat match in the PURE Jr. Heavyweight Title Tournament!! I am your Hanza , Hanza Kibagame, and this match will be refereed by official Taki Kuonji!!

“All Along the Watchtower” by Jimi Hendrix plays as Billy Larkin walks down towards the ring. He waves to the audience cheering and clapping for him as he slides into the ring and bounces off the ropes.

Kiyashi: Larkin’s music is by Jimi Hendrix, a legendary rock artist over in the states, he’s very popular with the conservatives.
