Bio

Born in the same neighbourhood, their mothers were and still are

good friends. at first Ric and Johnny could not stand the sight of each

other. With both of the two trying to out do each other wether it be with

the females or in sport.


This carried on untill Ric's very first day of high school. Johnny was

already at High school as he was two years older then Ric. Ric at the time

was a very shy individual, and did not associate well with the other kids.


Two of the schools bullies took note of Rics coyness and decided to take

advantage of it. Upon seeing this Johnny went flipmode and completely

kicked the two bullies asses. After Ric asked why Johnny did what he did, but


Johnny has not mentioned why till this very day.

The two of them stuck together like glue from that day onwards. Johnny

always had Ric's back and vice verser. They realised that they could not

live up to their fathers standard (who were successful businessmen). So they

decided to join a local gym and become established athletes but in what

sport? Johnny had the braun and an animal like mentality. While on the

other hand Ric was a swift and cunning little S.O.B! One day they was

working out, when Ric noticed a poster on the wall: Who's tougher then you.


No one well come and find out! and thats how Nu Era broke into the

wrestling business. They won various state wrestling tag team tournaments.


But said they were bored of the indy circuit and wanted a piece of the big

time, OSW. So they find them selves signing the dotted line of the most

extreme tag team tournament. Hoping to get have the chance of a lifetime

and climb the mountain of success required to become legends and a force to be reckoned with!


After being eliminated from the OSW MET they were accepted into a well established fed known as NTICW the was there for about a month and had a record of 1-0-1, and were named #1 cntender for the tag titles, but NTICW close down and Nu Era were shipped on the first plane to Japan to compete in the well respected fed known as PURE.


Since the introduction of the trinity division to PURE, Ric and Johnny decided to call upon the services of Josh, Johnny's younger brother to help them attain fame in the hallowed halls of PURE
