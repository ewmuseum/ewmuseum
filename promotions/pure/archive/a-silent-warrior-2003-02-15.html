A Silent Warrior...

PURE has recently acquired many new talent to add to their growing roster over the past week and all look to be pretty impressive and brilliant additions. One of these men goes simply by the name of Naoki. Full name being Naoki Fujimoto of Mishima, Japan. He along with his manager and mouthpiece Nicolas Grimes have signed a contract with PURE after impressing PURE owner Jamar Wilson himself.

Naoki seems to have a great knowledge of the martial art of ninjitsu. Resources say he has trained with his grandfather, the late Takata Fujimoto, since he was able to walk. There is no doubt that he is talented thanks to some videos of his past stints as a pro fighter and wrestler in the independent league of the Mishima Wrestling Alliance. Reporters could not get word with Naoki as he remained silent and just stared a hole through each of them. However Naoki’s manager was more then eager to talk.

“Naoki is quite the impressive guy.” said Nicolas Grimes as he talked to one of our many reporters. “I was paired with him back in our MWA days to be his manager as the booker at that time thought we would work well together. Well he was absolutely right about that. Sure Naoki is pretty impressive but without me he would just simply be some boring guy in a mask and we just can’t let that happen. With my help Naoki will no doubt be the biggest thing in Japan since Godzilla!”

Nicolas Grimes would have said more but he was met with a very infuriated glare from Naoki who was in the room at the time and that pretty much ended the interview right there. Mr. Grimes told us however that we would learn more about this silent warrior soon and that we would no doubt see a star on the horizon. We will keep you updated as we get more information.
