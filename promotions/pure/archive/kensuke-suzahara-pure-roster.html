Bio

History

Kensuke Suzahara made his debut in CCW, expected to be nothing more than a gimmicky Japanese-gimmicked character with some martial arts skill to put over the established talent in CCW. He was Johnny Flame, destined to be nothing more than a comedy wrestler. An enhancement talent. A kid.


Life's funny like that.

Kensuke pulled up his boots, and managed to become a living legend in CCW, due to his demeanor as an old-school face. He formed the New Strangers, one of the longest-running stables in sports entertainment, and had MANY memorable feuds and title reigns.


CCW entered an interfed called FedWars. Immediately, Alexander Miyagawa and Johnny Flame sought each other out, as they were two of the minority of Japanese wrestlers in the United States. A brutal war of words ensued, sparking off a bitter feud that would send Kensuke over to PCW first as Miya's ally...but later, as his enemy. He was fired shortly after their match at 'Back To Old School.'


Reappearing a few months later, after an injury in CCW sent him packing, Kensuke went on a winning streak unparalleled in CCW history. At Group Therapy 2001, Kensuke won the CCW Heavyweight Title to the approval of millions of fans.


But that's just not enough sometimes.

Since then, Kensuke has been discontent with his lot in life, as while loved, he still did not get enouugh respect from the 'boys' backstage. Yet he still played to the fans that adore him. But recently...things have gone wrong. He lost the CCW Heavyweight Title to an archnemesis, Cianan. His most hated enemy, Neurosis came back, and usurped him as the biggest babyface in CCW. His girlfriend left him. And John Jupiter prevented him from going down in CCW history as the first ever Universal Champion.


Backstage at CCW's pay-per-view event Irish Spring, Kensuke brutalized his tag team partner, Kyle 'Velocity' Lamen with a chair, and gave him his finishing move, the Stranger, onto an open chair. Kyle's wrestling career is over.


Currently suspended from CCW.

Currently in possession of a VERY MEAN streak.

And in PURE for two reasons.

(1) To get revenge on his former stablemates, the Elements (Miyagawa & Inphino Blitz).

(2) To get back at the fans of Japan for 'turning on him,' he intends to prove success is his birthright, even though the business has been 'bad' to him. He is the messiah for the disenfranchised, the modern-day Hamlet.


The most loved susperstar in CCW history is destined to become the most hated in PURE history. Do you think you can stop him? He doesn't.


And neither does Alexander Miyagawa, the owner of PURE. Because Alexander has been taken out of commission, after Ikusa I. Who controls PURE? Kensuke does.

Finisher

The Stranger - Inverted Crucifix into Tombstone Piledriver

Kensuke lifts his opponent up from underneath both of their arms, while facing away from them (easily done out of inverted facelock position, with Kensuke as the victim). He holds the opponent overhead in an inverted crucifix, then pulls the opponent down in front of him. Kensuke puts the victim's head down between his legs so the victim is up side down and belly to belly with Kensuke. He drops to a sitting position, driving the victim's head into the mat.


Set-Up Moves/Secondary Finishers

Silver Silencer - Leg Lever Takedown with Leg Drop

The victim is standing but bent forward. Kensuke stands to the victim's side and hooks the victim's near arm. He puts the leg which is closest to the victim's arm over the back of the victim's neck and hooks it by bending his leg. Kensuke then pulls his leg back and pushes the victim's arm forward in the opposite direction. The victim is then spun sideways and lands flat on their back on the mat. As the victim is falling, Kensuke drops down with a leg drop onto the victim's neck.


The Falling Star - Inverted Facelock Swinging Neckbreaker

Kensuke applies an inverted facelock on the victim. He quickly spins to a side, wrenching the victim's neck and dropping them face first into the mat. Kensuke gets more height on his jump than most others who perform this move.


Signature Moves

The Dreamstealer - Drop Toe Hold into STF with Double Arm Lock

Kensuke drops down to the mat hooking the leg of the victim with one foot in front of the victim's ankle and then trips the victim down by pushing with their other leg on the back of the victim's legs. Then, Kensuke grabs the victim's foot and bends it up. He lies on his side, his bottom leg is behind the victim's knee, and hi top leg is hooked around the victim's shin/ankle. Kense throws the victim's near arm in between his legs and scissor locks the arm and the victim's ankle. He reaches across the victim's chest with one arm and around the victim's free arm and locks his hands around the victim's head. The end result has Kensuke pulling back on the victim's neck, one of the victim's arms is trapped in a leg scissor, the other arm is trapped between Kensuke's chest and under his top arm which is applying the face lock on the victim.


Mask Of The Angel - Off The Top Rope Shooting Star Mat Slam

Kensuke and his victim face each other, Kensuke atop the top turnbuckle, and the opponent standing on the mat. Kensuke does a backflip off the top rope, and as he is landing, he grabs the opponent by the back of the head, and drives his face into the mat as he lands in a sitting position.


Star Cycle - Running Super Forward Rolling Fallaway Slam

Kensuke lifts the victim up so they are laying stomach first across the his shoulders (a standing fireman's carry). He then runs, leaps, leans forward and slams the victim down and then rolls over them with his own weight.


Wind Element Suplex - Belly-to-Back Double Chickenwing Suplex with Bridge

Kensuke applies a double chickenwing on the victim and links his hands together behind the victim's back. He then lifts the victim up and holds him there for a moment, putting pressure onto the shoulder blades. Then, Kensuke falls backwards, dropping the victim on their shoulders/neck/upperback. Then, he bridges for a pin.


The Strike - 180 Springboard Spear

Kensuke leaps to the second rope, and as he comes off, spins 180 degrees and dives at a standing victim. He grabs both their legs, causing the victim to fall backwards to the mat.


Disenfranchiser - Half Nelson Standing Mat Slam

Kensuke stands behind the victim. He reaches under one of the victim's arms with one hand and places it behind the victim's neck. Then, Kensuke grabs the victim by his neck with his free hand and slams their face into the mat.


Stolen Signature Moves

Corrective Therapy - Crossface Sharpshooter -Stolen from Paul Arovnik-

The victim is on their back. Kensuke holds up the victim's legs and steps inbetween them. He crosses the victim's legs around the leg which they put through and holds them in place with his left arm. He them steps over, turning the victim over on their stomach. Kensuke leans/crouches back to apply pressure, and at the same time, reaches back with his right arm to apply a one-armed crossface.


Black Dragon Smasher - Super Spinning Body Slam (Through Legs) Piledriver -Stolen from Inphino Blitz-


Kensuke first applies a front headlock, then rotates and underhooks an arm. At this point, he continues his 180 degree rotation and lifts up the victim as if he is going to give them a body slam. Kensuke positions the victim's head down and drops down to his knees, dropping the victim on their head.


Last Caress - Reverse Hurricarana setup flowing into Reverse Gory Special Piledriver -Stolen from Kyle Lamen-


Kensuke jumps onto the back of the victim's shoulders. He falls backwards while wrapping his legs around the victim's head. Kensuke then falls backwards, and in the process hooks both of the victim's arms. He lands squarely on his feet, and hefts the victim in the air, so that he is suspending the opponent, upside-down and facing away, by his arms. Kensuke then sits down, driving the opponent headfirst into the mat.


The Locust Reign - Hurricarana setup flowing into DDT -Stolen from Neurosis-

Kensuke leaps at the victim and locks both of their legs around the victim's head/neck. From there, he pushes up and applies a front face lock on the victim. He then falls straight down, driving the victim's head into the mat.


Therapy - Kataha-jime Variant -Stolen From Miyagawa-

Kensuke stands behind the victim. He reaches under one of the victim's arms with one hand and places it behind the victim's neck and with his other arm he reaches around under the victim's chin and squeezes across the victim's throat. If needed, he drops to the ground and applies a body scissors as well.


Complete PURE Match History

--Singles Match: Kensuke Suzahara vs. Alexander Miyagawa (Ikusa I: 4/16/02) Winner: Kensuke Suzahara


Main event of the first Ikusa. Round One of the PURE Jr. Heavyweight Title tournament. Billed as a PCW/PTC/Fedwars Grudge Match. Undoubtably one of the greatest matches ever seen. Kensuke picked up the pin after Miya sustained an injury, and the uncaring Kensuke delivered a devastating Stranger.


--Triple Threat Match for the PURE Junior Heavyweight Title: Kensuke Suzahara vs. The HIT vs. The End (Ikusa II: 4/30/02) Winner: Undetermined as of yet.


To decide the first ever PURE Jr. Heavyweight Title holder. Another brilliant match in the wings, against two men who sport 'The''s in their name.
