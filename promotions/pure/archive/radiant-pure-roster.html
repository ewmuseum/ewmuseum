Real Name: Akoshi Kunamara Birthdate: February 1st, 1980 Hometown: Aikawa Island, Niigata Prefecture, Japan Height: 6'0 Weight: 215 lbs. Nicknames: Radiant, Ultra-Red, The Masked Man of Submission Status: Face Manager(s): Minori Kumako Current Teams: None. Entrance Music: 'Chasing The Dragon,' by Dream Evil Wrestling Style: Traditional Puroresu of Submission, Aerial and Technical Brawling Attacks.
Signature Moves: Puroresu Wrestling is about Man-To-Man combat. The eternal fight between Good and Evil. Using devasting Technical moves, high impact Aerial moves and bone-breaking Submission moves, Radiant has a plethora of moves at his disposal to finish off his opponent.

Tribute Moves
“Super Heaven Bomb”- Radiant puts an opponent onto his shoulders, into a Torture Rack, while standing on the second rope of the turnbuckle. (Kenta Kobashi tribute) He spins around in a circle, before pulling the opponent down into Head-Spike Brainbuster. (Also Known as Burning Hammer.)
“Golden Space Drop”- After disposing of his opponent, Radiant handspring’s inside the ring into a Moonsault over the ropes to the outside with a twist in mid air. (Great Sasuke tribute)

Grappling Moves
“Shatei”- Holding the opponent’s around the neck, Radiant runs into the turnbuckle and leaps onto the top rope, pushing himself and the opponent off. He lands on his feet, and pushes himself into the air. Radiant then lands them with a Spike Tornado DDT.
“Lion Driver 2003”- Radiant sends his opponent into the ropes. When they bounce off, he picks up as if to Tilt-A-Whirl Slam them, but drops them forward in a modified Michinoku Driver.
“Tekiya”- With the opponent on the turnbuckle, Radiant ascends the turnbuckle with them and locks their head up in a headlock. Radiant then proceeds to pull the opponent into the air and lands them in the middle of the ring with a Brainbuster.

Striking Moves
“Gurentai”- After his opponent is downed onto the mat, Radiant awaits for them to get back onto their feet. As they’re doing so, he spins in a circle and slams his elbow into the opponents jaw.
“Kumicho Wakashu”- Radiant bends his knee and waits for the opponent to either turn around, or get back on their feet. He takes off in a sprint, and slams his shoulder or head into the opponents stomach, spearing them to the floor.

Submission Moves
“Bakuto”- With the opponent face down, Radiant takes the arm and pulls it upwards; with the right arm vertical across the wrist and the left bent across at the shoulder and pushing up from underneath the elbow, hitting a deadly Fujiwara Armbar.
“Uyoku”- Following the Tekiya, Radiant locks on a headlock on the opponent’s neck. If the right arm is free, Radiant will lock that up arm under the headlock to hyperextend the elbow.

Aerial
“Hayabusa Special”- Radiant climbs the turnbuckle after taking the opponent down with any move listed. Radiant waits for the right moment, casually encouraging the fans in attendance. He then proceeds to flip off the turnbuckle with a 450 degree rotation, and then splashing on his opponent. (Hayabusa tribute.)
Set Up Move: Kumicho Wakashu(Spear) Finisher: The Red Rose (Wrist Clutch Exploder Deathbomb)

Bio

In The Beginning...

On a tiny island, west of the Japanese main island of Honshu, a legacy would begin on the 21st of May 1620. Ichiro Kunamara had been thrown off a small boat housed by a nearly a dozen castaways from the Japanese town of Niigata. Kunamara had befouled the captain’s daughter, taking away her virginity while on a night of drinking through the port-town. The enraged captain, Kintaro Kawashima had ordered Kunamara to be drowned in what today is called the Sea of Japan. Luckily for Ichiro, he had managed to fight off the men of Kintaro Kawashima and float along in the cool Sea of Japan water until he reached an island off in the distance.


Kunamara wondered many a night around the island, finding that the small island had plentiful fresh water, trees with fruit on them and several roaming livestock. Ichiro set to work building himself a small home, with the use of a Machete he had stolen from the boat. The construction of his home took Ichiro three solid weeks of construction to build himself a small house for him to sleep in. He had no way of travelling his way back to the mainland, unless he built himself a boat.


So, Ichiro Kunamara went to work again. No rest period from between building his new home and constructing the small boat. The trusty Machete he had been using over the last few weeks was lashed into the nearby trees, Ichiro knocking down three trees to build himself the boat. The boat was only big enough to carry him, some food and the Machete.


Ichiro set sail back to the main land, but he knew of the dangers lurking in the port-town of Niigata. Ichiro was known by many on the western coast of the main island. So, he had a choice. Ichiro pondered whether or not to arrive into Niigata and battle with the outlaws who had tried to cut his life short, or float South of Niigata toward the newly established Toyama. He decided to travel to Niigata, his life had been lacking excitement recently.


It was very early July when Ichiro Kunamara snuck into the quiet town of Niigata, very early in the morning. He would have been very recognizable to the locals if he didn’t disguise himself by stealing a sheet of cloth that hung from the window of a house on the near the jetties.


Ichiro tiptoed through Niigata in the dead of the night. He creaked open windows of closed food stalls to steal Bread, Rice, Additional Clothing, Woks for cooking, A couple more Machetes and Knifes, and a bigger boat that was abandoned, but in good condition, on the shores.


He went unnoticed on this historical night. Not a soul in the port-town knew about his thieving, and would remain completely oblivious for years to come. Ichiro sailed back to his lonely island as the sun came up, watching the town of Niigata come to life as he floated away peacefully in the Sea of Japan.
