Shirata Rinjiro vs. Masanobu Azaki (First Ever PURE Match)

The camera pans around the sold out interior of the Expo ’70 Stadium in Osaka, Japan showing the wide variety of people who have come to see the first ever PURE card. We pan around the crowd showing various signs such as “Oh My God, They Killed Kensuke!” and “I was paid to hold up this sign” before we pan down to the three ring Hanza s of Puroresu Ultimate Ring Entertainment. They are of course the beautiful and intelligent Hitomi Asami, the rather... rotund Mori Morishigi and the cold hearted Kobentai Kiyashi.

Hitomi Asami: Let’s get straight into it, it is already time for the first match in the history of Puroresu Ultimate Ring Entertainment! Let us go up to our ring Hanza so he may introduce the participants.

Hanza : This contest is scheduled for one fall, and it will be the first match ever in PURE!

Hanza : Introducing first…

“You Get To Burning” by Yumi Matsuzawa plays, as “Jido-San” Masanobu Azaki makes his way to the ring.

Hanza : From Osaka, Japan..standing 6’4”, and weighing 270 lbs… “Jido San” AZAKI….MASANOBU!

The name is pronounced family name first, as is done in Japanese culture. Jido-san enters the squared circle, awaiting his rival as “Tear Away” by Drowning Pool hits.

Hanza : His opponent hails from the island of Okinawa! Standing 6’6”, and weighing 278 pounds….RINJIRO……SHIRATA!

The two men circle each other intently, as the bell rings, once.

DING!

Hitomi Asami: Here we go!

Azaki and Rinjiro stare into each other’s eyes slowly. A handshake is exchanged, as the men quickly lock up. Jido-San strikes first, with a forearm to the forehead. He fires off a second, and then spins around, catching Shirata with a knifedge chop to the throat.

Mori Morishigi: Quick offensive flurry from Masanobu!

Kobentai Kiyashi, the third commentator, seems rather bored with the proceedings.

Kobentai Kiyashi: I wish to see more emotion from both competitors. This is not America... they cannot go out there on this Puroresu television show and punch each other for twenty minutes and expect to be cheered for their pathetic effort.

Masanobu spins for another chop, but Shirata Rinjiro evades the blow, then throws a sharp palm strike to Azaki’s chest. He steps back, and uses his size to take Azaki to the canvas with a standing lariat.

Hitomi Asami: Rinjiro coming back to assert himself early in the bout.

Mori Morishigi: Absolutely! This early stage of the contest is vital to both men.

Azaki gets back to his feet, and lays in another series of forearm shots. He bends Rinjiro forward, following these blows with a DDT. Masanobu aims, and drops an elbow across Shirata’s broad chest.

Kobentai Kiyashi: Now we are getting somewhere.

Azaki grabs Rinjiro’s arm, attempting to apply a Fujiwara armbar. However, this is blocked, as Rinjiro nips up to his feet, a nice display of agility. Shirata grabs Azaki by the left leg, taking him over in a dragon screw legwhip.

Hitomi Asami: Quick takedown from Shirata Rinjiro!

Mori Morishigi: But can he capitalize upon this position of power?

Rinjiro maintains his grip upon the leg of Azaki, repositioning slightly to attempt to cinch in an anklelock, but Azaki uses his hands to drag himself quickly in a belly crawl to the bottom rope before grabbing it, as Rinjiro respectfully stops his hold attempt.

Hitomi Asami: Rinjiro-san almost gained a definite advantage upon Masanobu Azaki.

Mori Morishigi: But Azaki-san was able to avoid the calculated attempt to take advantage of the weakened leg.

Kobentai Kiyashi: Good because otherwise this match might have ended prematurely.

Azaki gets up slowly, gripping his leg from the hold, and leans against the ropes. Rinjiro runs towards Azaki and attempts a spear, but Azaki, using the ropes as leverage, springs up and takes down Rinjiro from behind with a dragon suplex. The referee goes down to count the pin

Mori Morishigi: We may have a winner of match right now!

Hitomi Ashami: The referee is counting, 1...2...We have a kickout at the last moment by Shirata Rinjiro.

Kobentai Kiyashi: I do not wish this match would just end. That is not Puroresu.

Rinjiro and Azaki both take their time recovering from the move and Azaki is the first one up from the move. He picks up the struggling Rinjiro and tries to hit a swinging neckbreaker but Rinjiro counters the move and forces Azaki to make a full rotation of the move and he uses the momentum to DDT Azaki.

Hitomi Ashami: That was a brilliant counter by “Jido-San!”

Kobentai Kiyashi: That is something to aspire to..

Azaki lays on the mat, in pain, as Rinjiro climbs the first and second rope. He then positions himself on top of the turnbuckle and flies off with a guillotine leg drop, but Azaki smartly moves out of the way, rolling out of danger. Azaki now gets up and just as Rinjiro is getting up, and hits a quick dropkick, knocking the man back over.

Mori Morishigi: Very nice use of the dropkick by Azaki.

Hitomi Ashami: He is gaining control of the match very quickly.

Kobentai Kiyashi: That is good. The talent level in this arena might be indeed rising at this precise moment.

Azaki now goes over to the man lying on the mat and lock him in sitting abdominal stretch. Rinjiro tries to reach the ropes, but with every second the pain on his face increases. He reaches, but misses. He reaches again and misses. Now he has stopped the reaching and the referee raises his arm. It falls. His arm is raised again, and again Rinjiro’s arm falls. For a third time his arm is raised, but this time it stays up and Shirata Rinjiro begins to throw elbows into the face of Masanobu Azaki.

Kobentai Kiyashi: A good break away there and the match continues.

Hitomi Ashami: This match does indeed continue, and Shirata Rinjiro has new life within his body!

Rinjiro continues to throw elbows until Azaki is forced to release the hold. Rinjiro whips Azaki into the turnbuckle. He runs at the man and connects with a powerful spear!

Mori Morishigi: Rinjiro-san calls that move Act of Fate!

Kobentai Kiyashi: And in Puroresu we do not Act.

Hitomi Ashami: Shirata Rinjiro is going to try to end this match right now!

Rinjiro goes and picks up a groggy Azaki and sets him up into a vertical suplex position. He goes to slam the man down into a jackhammer, but Azaki escapes and kicks Rinjiro in the gut, then hits a cradled inverted DDT!

Hitomi Ashami: That was the Azaki-Bomb onto Rinjiro-san!

Mori Morishighi- Both men are very close to nailing a move that can decide this match’s outcome!

Azaki tries to lock on Azaki Garame, a head and arm lock finishing hold, but Riniro slips away and quickly hits a Jackhammer. He then picks the man up and hits another Jackhammer! He follows with a third Jackhammer! He is now looking tired and picks up Akazi for one more. He holds him in the air for about ten seconds and then slams him down with amazing force!

Hitomi Ashami: That was an amazing display of power from Shirata Rinjiro!

Mori Morishigi: He just hit Downfall onto Masanobu Akazi!

Kobentai Kiyashi: This should end the match.

Winner: Shirata Rinjiro

The Pills-Enmity

The scene fades into a dimly lit area. A room? No. A basement? No. A chamber. The only light present throughout the whole area is a chunk in the dead center. A window is apparently open because you can hear the whistling of the wind, and also the swaying of chains. The light positioned on top of the camera turns on and pans around the large area. Rows and rows of chains hanging from the ceiling are the only objects visible to the world viewing. Each chain, swaying its own way, as if it has its own personality and feelings. In the distance, laughing can be heard followed by chains clanging together. The voice begins getting louder and closer, as more chains are hitting and making noises. Suddenly the sound of a window slamming shut is heard and the light vanishes. Dead silence...

The cameraman’s breath is deep, heavy, and panting as footsteps slice through the air. They stop five feet in front of the camera, but it’s pitch black. A screeching is heard and suddenly the area lights up. The walls are covered in boards and chains and the ceiling resembles a willow tree, but with steel emulating branches. Sitting in front of the camera, is PURE wrestler, Enmity. His head is gazing down at the floor with his black hair hanging over his face. His left leg begins shaking as the laughter is started once again. He combs his hair back and looks up at the camera. With an evil grin, and a sinister laugh, Enmity knows he is already intimidating enough.

“Pills...” The word is let out with a sigh of relief, as Enmity becomes dead serious. “Pills control my life...they control my very existence. What you see before you is the creation of a tiny capsule. The wonders of medication have molded the being that I am today.”

He leans back in his chair, as if relaxing, but is clearly tense. “Why I allow these pills to consume my life escapes me at this time. I figure that I need something to keep me linked to this world...why not let it be lies?”

“I believe Aldous Huxley said it best,” he continued. “ ‘If we could sniff or swallow something that would, for five or six hours each day, abolish our solitude as individuals, atone us with our fellows in a glowing exaltation of affection and make life in all its aspects seem not only worth living, but divinely beautiful and significant, and if this heavenly, world-transfiguring drug were of such a kind that we could wake up next morning with a clear head and an undamaged constitution; then, it seems to me, all our problems would be wholly solved and earth would become paradise.’ ”

He looks up at the ceiling and then back to the camera, “I live my life every day on that statement. You see, I am a sufferer of manic depression and keep waiting for one pill to make everything PERFECT. I keep wondering whether it’s not just one pill...but many that will make everything worth while. Or is the solution to my problems, ending them?”

He stands up and almost presses his face right against the camera lens. “Won’t you come and take a trip with me?”

The camera fades to black.

He’s Still Got “It”

The towering building of PURE Studios is in the background, the ring announcer, Hanza Kibagame, entering the ring calmly as Jamar and Hitomi’s voices now can be heard via commentary from PURE Studios-

Jamar: Well we’re back PUREists and it looks as if we’re ready to bring you our LIVE main event here, that match being the ever loveable Mang-A-Hero versus the rabid Enmity. I’m expecting an excellent match here Hit.

Hitomi: And you should have no trouble finding it from these two, both of them two incredible talents that are PURE exclusive. Although I do wonder about Mang-A-Hero’s sanity, wanting to step in the ring with Enmity. I’m sure you’d vouch with me on that one Jamar.

Jamar: He certainly did put something on my a** Hit. But maybe MAH can show Enmity if he’s “still got it”.

He fixes the black fighting wraps around his hands as he sneers with dead eyes at the fans booing him in the makeshift aisle. Stepping up on the apron, Enmity raises his arms high in the air as the fans boos grow ever louder, entering the ring with very little care for their opinion as he slowly stalks over to the red corner of the ring and stands tall, his eyes fixated on the aisle for his opponent-

Hitomi: I hope Mang-A has his head in the game today….he can’t afford to be taken off guard by Enmity.

Jamar: I understand him wanting to prove he’s still got it Hit, but against Enmity….it’s just crazy. If he’s looking for a test, he’s found it.

Adorned in a white and blue Ultraman suit, he also has the noticeable addition of a heavy knee brace on his right knee as he goes down the aisle, slapping countless hands with incredible enthusiam, happy to be back in action. Catching one last hand at the end of the aisle, MAH slides in the ring and stands in the blue corner, smiling at Enmity confidently as Hanza exclaims-

Hanza: THIS MATCH IS SET FOR ONE-FALL AND WILL BE CONTESTED UNDER A 15 MINUTE TIME LIMIT! FIRST…IN THE RED CORNER…WEIGHTING IN AT 230 POUNDS….FROM OKINAWA, JAPAN….THE ASSASSIN OF PURE….EEEEEEEEEEENMITY!!!

Hanza: HIS OPPONENT, IN THE BLUE CORNER….FROM THE PLANET NAMEK…WEIGHTING IN AT 221 POUNDS….THE BACKSMACK DRAGON HERO….RETURNING TO THE RING AFTER A 6 MONTH LAYOFF…MAAAAAAAANG-A-HEROOOOOOOOOOOOO!!

The bell rings and the match gets underway as MAH and Enmity circle each other cautiously, feeling one another out before one starts off the exchange.-

Jamar: Enmity and MAH BOTH looking in incredible shape, the fact we’ve been off TV for awhile hasn’t gotten to these guys one bit.

Hitomi: Enmity is all about breaking his opponents down before going for the big kill, if I were MAH, I’d rely on that erratic high-flying and explosive style of is to combat it

After a few seconds, MAH executes a crisp front flip counter and grabs a standing armbar of his own, Enmity howling in pain as MAH applies more pressure. Pulling his arm up over his head, MAH pulls down on it with great force as Enmity cuts a flip to the mat, following with a leg drop to his arm before MAH goes for the early cover-

Hitomi: 1…not even a two there.

Immediately, Enmity goes for the bad right knee, lifting it up and stomping it rabidly. Jamar: I’m not saying it’s right, but Enmity’s being smart about his strike.

Hitomi: MAH can’t keep getting in mat situations with Enmity like this!

Fans: MAH!! MAH!! MAH!! MAH!!

Enmity then takes his boot heel, pressing it against the face of MAH and using the ropes for leverage, SLIDING his boot harshly off his face every few seconds as the referee admonishes Enmity, finally getting him off. He moves the referee aside, lifting MAH out the corner and setting him up, suplexing him high in the air before abruptly crashing him down, rolling over with a pin as he arrogantly pushes his forearm on the face of MAH-

Jamar: 1..NO! Why is Enmity’s being so arrogant with this cover?

Hitomi: Humiliation Jamar. He’s out to completely break MAH today.

-Enmity clenches his teeth, jerking MAH up to his feet via the mask and pummeling him with clubbing shots to his back, before cinching his arms in a butterfly set-up. He powers MAH up and slams him down on the mat….yet Enmity isn’t done as he holds the butterfly lock, getting MAH to his feet yet again before powering him up once more and SLAMMING him to the mat. Still with the hold applied, Enmity powers MAH one more time, this time holding him in the air as the blood rushes to MAH’s head…ending the brutal series with a Tiger bomb.

Tapping him with his foot to make sure he’s down, Enmity lines MAH up near the turnbuckle as he crosses over his fallen body and begins climbing, the fans urging MAH to get up-
