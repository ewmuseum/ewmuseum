Bio

Ilan’s childhood was that of a normal upper class Long Island boy. His entire family was Jewish and sometimes he thought of them as if they should all be on a TV sitcom. He has an older brother who always shows off, a younger sister who is more superficial than anyone ever, a mother who cuddles and loves and cooks, and a father who doesn’t speak much, but always has a lot on his mind.


He grew up easily, always getting many good grades in school and having normal friends and girlfriends. Just your average kid. Played a few sports, wrestling and baseball, and really just enjoyed his time from birth to age eighteen in the suburbs of New York City.


Then he went off to college, Columbia University in New York City. He made four friends very quickly and they had been attached throughout their college careers. Ilan, Roland, Oliver, Pat, and Jimmy were soon known around campus as The Huddled Ones for always being together. In college Ilan got himself very good grades once again and decided to major in finance his Junior year. He was going to go into the field of Investment Banking once out of college.


However, there was a small wrinkle in the plan. After just over three years of college in 2001 The Huddled Ones decided to go see a wrestling show. They left with their lives changed and decided to join a wrestling school.


In the school they all did decently at first, but then, one by one they quit. None were fired or thrown out, just couldn’t deal with it for some reason. At least, all of them except Ilan Levin.


Ilan graduated from Columbia and Crypt’s School of Wrestling well in both. He then set off over the summer to train and get experience in Mexico as a Luchadore. Throughout his time in Mexico he was ridiculed and laughed at. A Jewish wrestler, now there was a laugh and a half. However, once Ilan truly showed off his submission skills and his agility people seemed to gradually quiet. In August he was dubbed, “The Jewserweight” and is damn proud to be one of the very few Jewish wrestlers in pro wrestling.


After garnering skills in Mexico he decided to move onto the next great wrestling capital of the world. He’d started his career in Manhattan, then moved onto Mexico, now it’s time to try out Japan. God speed.
