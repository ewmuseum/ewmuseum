Real Name: Amber Token Birthdate: September 29, 1978 Hometown: Sao Paulo, Brazil Height: 5'09' Weight: 149 lbs. Nicknames: The Aphrodisiac Status: Face Manager(s): None. Current Teams: None. Entrance Music: The lights dim down slowly to black, as the sounds of Sepultura's 'Roots, Bloody Roots' hits the speakers, and rocks the arena. Fog starts pouring out of the entrance way, as pink lights illuminate the stage from below. With the music getting louder by the second, twin white pyros shoot up from either side of the stage as Amber Token, the Brazilian beauty walks out onto the stage as red fireworks shoot down the ramp, and back up. She saunters down the ramp, tempting the nearby fans. Amber walks up the steel stairs, and saunters again, across the apron. She leans against the ropes, allowing the audience to get snap shots in, before she leans over, and steps through the bottom rope, to the ring.


Wrestling Style: Excelling in a vast variety of martial arts, Amber tends to use them in the ring, able to fight much larger adversaries. Her kicks are very swift and can be a key part of her matches, as well as her technical and aerial ability. She can counter possibily any move, and turn it into a defensive strike or submission hold. As her skills are already complimentary, she can also take to the ar, giving matches that extra thrill. She is abl to manipulate her body in midair to aim to connect with her opponent. She also often combines her martial arts from the top ropes, flying off with lethal kicks, leaving her opponent and fans in awe.
Signature Moves: Furacão - Airplane Spin into Flapjack.

Retorcer - Running Tornado DDT.

Desarranjado - Forward Roll into Dropkick on seated opponent in corner.

Crescente - Spinning Reverse Superkick.

Padecente - DDT into Chokehold Submission.

Fractura - Armbar Takedown into Reverse Fujiwara Armbar.

Cyclone Kick - Capoeira Helicopter Kick.

Set Up Move: Alice in Chains(Canadian Crossface, able to get it from almost any position.) Finisher: The Lethal Seduction (Amber kicks her opponent in the back of the knee, with a swift sharp kick, unless they are already on their knees. As they are down, Amber throws several types of strikes to the head, be it punches or kicks. with her opponent dazed, Amber crosses to the other side of the ring and makes a claw with her hand, before cutting the air with it, as a taunt to signal the end is near. Amber shows great speed by quickly tearing across the ring, and jumps low from the ground, able to get to the same height as her kneeling opponent. She stretches her legs outward in front of her, and uses her left leg and kick the opponent's head the opposite way, and letting her right leg, or knee area strike their head, taking them out easily. In simple terms, a varied Shining Wizard knee strike.)
