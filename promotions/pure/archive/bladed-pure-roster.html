Bio

This information is basically just a rough draft of his history... Nothing too in-depth. This bio is kinda old and doesn't really explain how he went from Militray to Infantry. That was a much longer story.


BladeD is a man that has lived a life of hate, violence, backstabbing, and anything else bad you could think of... He grew up in a place in New York called Queens. It was for the most part a pretty pieceful place, but like just about everywhere in New York it had it's ups and downs... BladeD is in the NMA(National Militray Assoication). He has been in the NMA since... well.. As long as I can remember. He is the their greatest warrior and has even turned into the CIA's most 'top secret' weapon. He has climbed every federal ladder and has gained just about every medal and award there is in the NMA... He is for sure one in a million and still does all kinds of secret militray type of jobs for the NMA and CIA.. He also still goes to certain wars, but his brother Saber basically does all that stuff now trying to turn his little brother into the man he once was.


BladeD likes to take the free he does get and go out into different feds wrestling and having a good time... He has been in the wrestling business for almost 2 years now and he is really beginning to become a very good wrestler with a lot of fan base.... Because of certain mistakes and things of those sort taken by the men he is suppose to trust he was injury severly six months back, but now after making his return is he 100 percent and ready


to get back to action.

Looks. He has dark brown hair. It is short and thick and the front part of his hair stops just before his eye brows. He has blue eyes. No facial hair. He is a pretty big build. When he comes out to talk to or whatnot his ring wear is a green tank top, short blue jeans, and black boots. In the ring wrestling he wears short blue jeans(with the ends cut and stringy... if that makes sense) and boots.
