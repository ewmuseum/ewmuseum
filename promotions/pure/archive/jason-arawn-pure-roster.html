Bio

December 17th, 1970 will be remembered as the day that Jason Winn Arawn was born in Seattle, Washington. His father was Sean Arawn, his mother Christy Arawn. Sean worked as a judge, Christy worked at home.


At he age of three, Jason was walking. No one knew he could actually talk, for he was very shy. He loved his parents, as they loved him. They were your average family. At the age of 12, Jason had got his first glimpse of wrestling entertainment. He was simply amazed. He had seen all the muscles, and that is when he decided to take action. He wanted to have a body, just like the men on television. By the time Jason was 14, he was fit. He wouldn't stop though, as he kept training.


Jason would join little wrestling promotions, and by the age of 32, he wanted to get into bigger federations. That is when he joined Primetime Wrestling Academy, with his mother and father supporting him all the way. Jason was a wild man in PWA, and after seeing some of his work, PURE President Sean Williams signed Jason. Jason was overjoyed, perhaps too overjoyed. He was easily eliminated on the first PURE card, Gekito...


...Jason has calmed down, and he has his mother and father supporting him...
