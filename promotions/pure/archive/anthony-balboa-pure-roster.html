Bio

Anthony Balboa was born in 1973 to poor Italian parents Giovanni and Zitta. Anthony was oldest of 6 kids (Anthony, Salvatore, Rocco, Andrea, Vita, Silvia). Balboa wents to school and finished high school. Balboa started Boxing illegaly for money when he was 14. He made enough money to support him and his family. Balboa was a amazing boxer and attracted a crowd of rich Italian Working men, who were willing to bet. In 1990, at the age of 17, Balboa joined an even bigger illegal boxing raccet to gain more money for his family. Balboa was a favorite of the men who gathered around to watch the bare knuckles boxing and made triple the money he made in the last 3 years! At the age of 18, this is when Balboa took a turn for the worst. The Mafia was gaining interest in Balboa, and illegal Boxing and wanted a peace of the action. They signed Several underground boxers, and offered Balboa a deal. Balboa left his family for New York. Balboa thought he'd be treated well and fighting in good conditions, but he was wrong, dead wrong. Balboa got his appartment in the ghetto of New York, and was robbed several times. The Fighting was the worst part. The fighters fought in a pit, like a dog fight, in front of crowds of betting men. There was no rules and Many men brought weapons to the Pit. This is where Balboa learned his Shoot fighting skills and learned the lession of Hardcore. Im the 2 years balboa spent in the ghetto he Toughend up and even Started Robbing other people. Some people say the ghetto turns you to stone, they where right. Balboa, a good boy growing up Went to jail in 1993, Balboa Was arrested for assult. balboa got 4 years, but only did two. Balboa got out in the summer of 1995, job less and homeless. Balboa snuck ontp a flight back to Abruzzi. When Balboa returned to Italy and his family, Every thing had changed. His father had died and his mother was getting weaker. Salvatore Was now head of the family. Balboa did not like this. He felt Salvatore was too weak to run the family. Balboa Become the 'pappa' of the family. He took this too seriously. It was a group of working teen agers, who where good people. They knew Anthony Had changed and they didn't like it. After a few month They were tired of Anthony, who has in and out of jail, and spent long hours at the bar. During this time, balboa developed his scene of humor, constantly laughed at his own jokes that not very many people found funny. Salvatore, the mild mannered younger brother, asked Balboa to leave. Surprizing Balboa left. Balboa moved back to New York. Balboa didn't goto the ghetto instead he went to New York City. Balboa tryed a life at several jobs, but they didn't work. By 1998 he was in organised crime. He joined a small Cosa Nostra called 'The Fattoni Family' or the Mafia. Balboa did small crime with them. During the time he deverloped a 'thing' for wrestling. Missing meetings to Watch wrestling. Balboa was the ultimate fan. Balboa was moving up in the Mafia Rankings becoming a Cappo. In 1999 balboa decided he was going to become a wrestler. Balboa went back to Italy to train. But with Balboa's Mafia Success, there was a danger of getting capped, so balboa brought his friend Tony fattoni and his body guards Jimmy and Johnny Fresco, and his booker (Money Management) Chico D'Aruzzi. All 5 trained, but Balboa was the better of the 5 and seemed like a natural. in July of 2000, Balboa left his Mafia buddys behind and signed with NAPWF. balboa's first match was against Rob 'Robzilla' Andrews July 12, 2000. The Rest Is All History...
