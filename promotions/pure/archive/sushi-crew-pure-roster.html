Bio

(sorry its crappy) Originally from Osaka, Japan, Ryu and Otoshi have been lifelong friends and have always had a passion for Professional wrestler, gaining a small following in Japan, the two decided to go over to America thinking they could make it there,they joined up with PWA a local training acadamy and after they proved themselves they were released from there. Ryu and Otoshi soon became champions of numerous feds mainly with the indy fed NTICW in which they got involved in a cult called the Followers of the hidden star, after the closure of NTICW, the sushi crew (as they were called) joined up with global sensation PCW, but quit after one week deciding wrestling wasn't fun anymore, a couple of months past until PURE owner Jamar came to the sushi crew with an offer to join PURE, seeing this as an oppurtunity to go back to their homeland the sushi crew accepted and went back to Japan, after 2 shows the Sushi crew with the help of their unwanted partner Shaun Styles won the Trinity belts which they still hold on today...
