PURE — Card

Gekito 1, 14 August 2002

14 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

(OOC Gekito Note-I had no idea of everyone's alignment and such and I did the best I could off of what little I had. I suggest you all should start filling in your bios if you have your usernames and passwords. I have more on the way, enjoy the work....)

From The Desk Of-Hitomi Asami(Lead PURE Commentator)....

"Hello PURE fans, August 14 was a wonderful starting night for PURE, as we warmed ourselves up for the big Ikusa from Ikusa Hall. Although untelevised, the crowd seemed hot for the TASTE of action we fed them at the Okata Gym in Osaka, Japan. The crowd packed the bleachers and after head count, 650+ rabid PURE fans came to support this house show and the new talent returned that support with an incredible performance in the house show's main event, which was an over the top battle royal for the Gekito Cup, a reward for the piece of new blood who came out victorious in this battle. We had many Oriental indy stars work the undercard and they worked the crowd up right well, and their were autograph signings at the end of the show. Among the participants were The HIT, The End, and PURE mega-star, Inphino Blitz. The line was unreal for the Black Dragon Master and even without a match on the card, Blitz's pop was unreal when his name was among one of those announced. Our next Gekito will be held at the Tokashi Recreational Center and the card is yet to be announced, but we hope for it to be as big of a success as our very first. Now on to the main event, which I had recorded for me by a member of the PURE staff attending the show at the gym. Enjoy and expect more news from me as it becomes available, THE PURE-ification begins SATURDAY!"-Hitomi Asami

Begin Feed For Main Event of Gekito....


*The meager Okata Gym is filled as best as possible, looking like it could burst at the seems from the 650 plus PURE fans cheering wildly, the video camera panning to show the many happy faces pleased with the action they've apparently seen so far. The crowd begins to settle as a suited, middle aged ring announcer begins to enter between the ropes of the light blue-roped ring. He stands in the very center of the canvas, the "PURE" emblem written in the middle proudly as he clears his throat, taking hold of the mic as he looks out to the crowd*

Ring Announcer: Is everyone having a good time?

Fans: YEEEEEEEEEAH!!!

*The ring announcer stumbles back, surprised at the power of their reply as he smiles out to them. He then grasps the mic, holding it to his lips as he unfolds a piece of paper in his pocket and says*

Ring Announcer: Before we move into our Gekito main event...I would like to inform you all that we have three of PURE's FINEST superstars here with us tonight to give free autograph signing's immediately after the show. Our three superstars are....THE HIT!!

*The fans boo with amazing ease, showing their loathing hatred for the evil mathematician and technical expert of PURE*

Ring Announcer:...THE END!!!

*The fans give a good roar for the blind Juniorweight sensation, obviously over with the crowd*

Ring Announcer:...AND....THE BLACK DRAGON MASTER....INPHINO BLITZ!!!

*The fans raise up from their seats, their roar overwhelming as they go crazy at the mention of Blitz's name. Still riled up from that announcement, the crowd chants in unison, only a few from the mass crowd not joining in the overwhelming chant*

Fans: BLITZ!! BLITZ!! BLITZ!!! BLITZ!!!

*Finally, the fans settle down, the ring announcer gaining his bearing as he prepares to introduce the Gekito entrants*

Ring Announcer: NOW....IT IS TIME FOR OUR MAAAAAAAAAAIN EVENT, which is an OVER THE TOPE BATTLE ROYAL FOR THE GEKITO CUP!! Introducing our competitors...

*"Pure Influence" begins to play over the sound system to introduce the competitors, all walking out with gaping space between them to represent no alignment with one another...all except the Fight Club, who walk unified side by side as they stretch their muscles and crack their knuckles. The fans give a welcoming pop to all the new PURE competitors as they begin to enter the ring, the ring announcer naming them off one by one.*

Ring Announcer: OUR COMPETITORS ARE...ENMITYYYYYYYY....DEEEEEEEEEEEACON....ZAAAAAAAAAAANDER McCAAAAAAAAIN....JAAAAAAASON AAAAAARAWAN......AND MICHAEL HAKIASHU, ANDREW MACLELLAN, AND PAUL McMUNSON....THE FIGHT CLUB!!!

*A PURE refereee slides in, the young and hungry PURE competitors all filling the ring as the ring announcer begins to clear the battle field, the music ending abruptly as all the men stare to one another, plotting out strategy on how to win. The camera pans over to a plain wooden table with none other then KOBENTAI KIYASHI AND MORI MORISHIGI sitting behind the desk. Kobentai has his usual pensive look upon his face, fixing his glasses and primping his suit for the camera, whereas Mori just shoots a wink to the camera before scarfing down a powdered donut hole.*

Kobentai: Well as you can see....we've been replaced by two GAIJIN on Ikusa, where we rightfully belong, so we have now been charged with Gekito duty. I am Kobentai Kiyashi...

Mori: *munch* *smack* *gulp* And I'm Mori Morishigi, the REAL "Best Looking Big Man on TV"...well without no TV I guess I'm just the "Best Looking Big Man".

Kobentai: Isn't that a stretch indeed, anyways, it looks as if our match is about to start and what a main event this looks to be, the new signees of PURE showing us what they have to offer in the Gekito Battle Royal! As I've been informed, they will be presented the award by none other than PURE superstar, Inphino Blitz.

Mori: *Sigh* You made it seem like someone IMPORTANT was giving the award or something...

Kobentai: THE BELL HAS RUNG, THE ACTION IS TAKING PLACE!!

*After a long seconds of planning, finally the ice is broken as Paul McMunson of the Fight Club rushes in at Zander McCain, throwing out a stiff right hook that blindsides him off guard as the match breaks loose. The wild brawling begins, the ref staying out of harms way as punches and kicks go flying all over the place, all these men intent on attaining victory and taking home the Gekito Cup. In the corner, we find Jason Arawn delivering furious chops to the chest of Deacon, even the Japanese puro fans knowing how to respond to each one as they exclaim*

Fans: WOO!! WOOO!! WOOO!!!

*Arawn takes a step back, running towards Deacon with arm out-stretched for a clothesline, but Deacon suddenly explodes from the corner and snatches him up by the legs, sending him crashing down on his back with a BRUTAL spinebuster that raises an eye on a good majority of the fans watching. Deacon then mounts atop Arawn and begins pummeling him mercilessly as we switch to across the ring, where we find Enmity trading blows with Paul McMunson of the Fight Club, Andrew Maclellan and Zander McCain doing much the same as their seems to be a stalemate in each fight.*

Kobentai: The action has broken loose!

Mori: So will my belt after I finish this Peking Duck

Kobentai: At least when Hitomi was around I had someone SMART to talk to...

*The two "down the middle" brawls taking place shortly get the tide turned on them as Michael Hakiashu helps his Fight Club teammates, LEVELING Enmity with a high roundhouse kick to the head and helping Maclellan by delivering a series of palm strikes to Zander's chest, Maclellan following by locking his arms around the waist of Zander and powering him up overhead, sending him CRASHING to the ground with a beautiful release overhead belly to belly!*

Mori: NOW THAT WAS A BELLY TO BELLY! He made me want to stop eating...nevermind... *scarf* *munch*

Kobentai: *sigh* The Fight Club showing superb teamwork in this match...

*Maclellan delivers a series of stiff stomps upon Zander, whereas Hakishu and McMunson lock on a a Boston Crab/Front Facelock combo upon Enmity, their victim screaming in wrenching pain as it echoes through the arena. Deacon leaves Arawn laying on the ground after a brutal beatdown, interjecting himself between Maclellan and Zander as he lock in a full-nelson on the shooter and lifts him up high, slamming him on his back as Maclellan rolls about in pain.*

Kobentai: BEAUTIFUL FULL NELSON SLAM BY DEACON!

*Zander shakes his head, getting himself back together as he uses the ropes to pull him up. Deacon lifts up Maclellan angrily, Zander willing to help him out as they both grab Maclellan about the arms and sling him into the ropes, grabbing him about the legs upon returning and hoisting Maclellan HIGH into the air before sending him plummeting front first into the mat with a double pancake! The fans cheer wildly for the display of teamwork, Zander putting his hand up for a high-five from Deacon...*

Mori: This is a battle royal, there ARE NO FRIENDS, right?

Kobentai: Sometimes you have to know when to build alliances.

Mori: Well my stomach wants to build an alliance with this plate of eggrolls...

Kobentai: So your telling me we got kids going hungry in China..YET WE CAN SOMEHOW FEED YOU? *Sigh*

*Deacon slaps Zander's hand...but quickly destroys the alliance with a CRUSHING lariat to his "ally" sending him spinning in air before hitting the mat. Meanwhile, Hakiashu and McMunson have released their double submission and now have Enmity in the corner, McMunson holding his legs so Hakiashu can pummel him with sidekicks to the midsection, Enmity looking extremely winded from the attack. Feeling they've done enough damage, McMunson slings Enmity with all his might into the opposite corner, Enmity's back slamming with pure force against the turnbuckles. He then takes Hakiashu by the arm and slings him with all his power into Enmity, Hakiashu coming at him with full speed*

Kobentai: This double teaming from the Fight Club is quite effective, and I must say the double team they've set up is NOT going to be pretty..

*Enmity regains some bearings about himself, seeing Hakiashu closing in. Thinking quickly on his feet, Enmity bends over and catches Hakiashu, raising up with all his power as he goes PLUMMETING to the matted floor on the outside, his body making brutal impact with the floor.*

MICHAEL HAKIASHU OF THE FIGHT CLUB HAS BEEN ELIMINATED!!!!!

Mori: Incredible desperation move by Enmity!

Kobentai: That was a veteran maneuver just seen by the young Enmity.

*McMunson, enraged about what just happened to his Fight Club compatriot, rushes towards the corner with a fist cocked back. But before he can release the blow upon Enmity, he throws his foot up and kicks him in the face, McMunson stumbling back a few steps as Enmity begins to mount the top rope*

Kobentai: Dangerous in a battle royal situation...

*McMunson turns around, Enmity leaping off with legs spread before wrapping them around the neck of his antagonist. Using the remaining momentum, Enmity swings over and executes a beautiful flying hurracanranna upon McMunson, catching the shooter off guard. Meanwhile, Zander McCain and a now recovered Jason Arawn have Deacon halfway out the ring, Deacon hooking the rope with his arm trying to fight being thrown over as best as possible.*

Mori: He may be at the point of no return here!

Kobentai: He better pray for a miracle!

*Deacon resorts to crude desperation, thumbing the eyes of Arawn as he stumbles back, Deacon regaining his footing as he then begins to pummel Zander McCain.*

Mori: I didn't know you could get a miracle in a "thumb to the eyes" form?!

*He has Zander backed up in the corner, rocking his head with the strongest of side elbows. This assault dosen't continue long before Jason Arawn locks a sleeperhold in on Deacon, dropping down shortly thereafter with a neckbreaker. Just as Arawn arises, he is sinched around the waist by a recovered Maclellan and hoisted in the air, dropped on his back and neck with a powerful German suplex, Jason looking broken down on the mat. Zander McCain sees Deacon rising up, using this as a time to strike as he mounts the top rope, getting positioned as he awaits Deacon to rise fully.*

Kobentai: ANOTHER high risk in this match! I wouldn't advise it...

Mori: You know what I do advise...ANOTHER EGGROLL!!

*Zander leaps off with a crossbody block, but Deacon catches him, displaying his power upon pressing him high above his head, the fans in awe by the man's strength as he nears him towards the ropes for elimination. He reaches the ropes, cockily performing a few presses with Zander. Just before he prepares to throw him over, Enmity sneaks up behind the powerful Deacon and dropkicks him with all his might, Zander falling from the heavens of Deacon's grasp and slamming to the mat, Deacon tumbling over to the outside, still upon the apron. Zander then looks to Enmity, the two forming a plan as it seems.*

Kobentai: He's halfway over, what are they gonna do now?

*The fans go crazy, urging the two young Juniorweights to eliminate the monster, Deacon. He finally manages to stand up upon the apron, but his stay is cut short as Zander and Enmity strike him with stereo dropkicks! Deacon falls to the floor, smacking his hands against the matting as he angrily makes his way to the back, the fans cheering wildly*

DEACON HAS BEEN ELIMINATED!!!!

Kobentai: These battle royals prove one thing...it's not all power, it's brains too.

*Before Zander can even catch his breath, Maclellan catches him with a stiff right to the chin, crumpling Zander to the mat abruptly. It's not long before McMunson helps out his partner, the two focusing their energies pummeling Enmity with stomach punches until he is bent over and weakened from the striking. The two remaining Fight Club members grab Enmity by the hair and tights, signaling their plans of elimination for him. They rush to the opposite ropes with great speed, tossing Enmity over and turning their backs to soak up any "praise", only receiving boo's for their troubles actually.*

Kobentai: Enmity has been el...WAIT WAIT?!

Mori: WHAT?!

*Enmity has hooked onto the top rope, his feet dangerously close to touching the floor, but never getting the opportunity. The fans pop big as he pulls himself up and over the top rope, at the same time as McMunson turns around to check on his dirty work. Enmity avenges getting tossed by wrapping his feet around the neck of McMunson, powering him over to the outside as he plummets to the floor, the fans going crazy for that fancy elimination.*

PAUL MCMUNSON OF THE FIGHT CLUB HAS BEEN ELIMINATED!!!!!

Mori: WHAT THE HELL WAS THAT?

Kobentai: AN ELIMINATION MORI!!

Mori: I wish I could do that...but my size kind of lacks that ability...maybe I need to eat more...

*Enmity powers himself back up into the ring, Maclellan enraged at the elimination of his partner. Maclellan executes a stiff Shining Wizard to the face of Enmity, sending him stumbling into the ropes. Maclellan cocks his fist back to deliver the final blow, but Zander McCain stops him with an elbow to the back of the head. Zander then runs off the ropes, rushing back towards Maclellan for a stronger blow...but his attempts are futile as he is grabbed about the waist before being suplexed overhead over the top rope, his back smacking the apron before he hits the matting face first.*

ZANDER MCCAIN HAS BEEN ELIMINATED!!!!!

*Enmity crumples into the corner, trying to catch his breath as Maclellan slowly stalks toward a recovering Jason Arawn, still shaken form the German he was delivered.*

Kobentai: Maclellan looks like a hungry wolf in that ring...this will get ugly...

*The fans are cheering, excited that this incredible main event is coming to a conclusion. Just as Maclellan is ready to go for a strike, Arawn gives the shooter a powerful gut punch, throwing more and more as he begins to catch his breath and rise to his feet. Arawn is gaining energy from the crowd, his right brimming with power as they crash against the face of Maclellan. He cocks back for a huge right, swinging and missing as he spins around. Maclellan grabs him, lifting him up in an atomic drop set up as he nears him towards the ropes. With no further thought, Maclellan tosses Arawn over the rope and into the floor, Arawn still gasping for air on the floor as the fans clap for his efforts at least*

Kobentai: Arawn with a valiant try

Mori: An eggroll before the match woulda helped him.

Kobentai: Why don't you suck on an "eggroll" Mori?

Mori: Is that possible?

Kobentai: *sigh*

JASON ARAWN HAS BEEN ELIMINATED!!!!!

*Maclellan looks across from him, Enmity doing the same as they lock eyes within the ring. The two slowly circle each other, trying to figure out what is the best strategy to take out the other. Finally, they end the strategy and just begin straight up brawling, the two trading hard blows with one another, neither wanting to give up ground in this fight. The fans are rabid, seething with interest to find out who will be the first ever Gekito Cup winner. Enmity throws a punch, getting caught by Maclellan and dropped to the floor in a reverse armbar, wrenching back for added pain. Enmity then raises up, flipping forward and turning the armbar into a back sinch on Maclellan. After a few elbows to the back of his head, Enmity grabs him by the head and drops him forcefully with a reverse sitout DDT, both men laid out on the mat and winded.*

Kobentai: We're at the finally moments...it's anyone's match...

Mori: Who's gonna get the cup? And can you drink of out it?

*The fans clap loudly in unison, each man slowly rising to their feet, still fixated upon each other. Enmity throws a kick, but Maclellan grabs it and uppercuts him under the knee, dragon screwing him down to the mat. Enmity crawls over tot he ropes, pulling himself up as best as possible as Maclellan looks like he's tired of "playing games" and ready to end it. Waiting for Enmity to rise, Maclellan rushes at him, stiffly driving his arm into his neck as he goes over the rope and to the outside, Maclellan raising his arms in victory...BUT IT IS YET TO BE IN HIS GRASP. Enmity once again saves himself from elimination, grasping the very bottom rope as he pulls himself back up. Maclellan sees this and rushes in to knock him off the apron, but in a sudden feat of daring aerial tactics, Enmity springboards off the top rope and leaps over Maclellan. While in air, Enmity extends his feet back with all his power, dropkicking Maclellan in the back as he goes tumbling over the top rope and to the floor. With the match won, Enmity falls to his knees in the ring, the fans exploding at his awesome victory*

ANDREW MACLELLAN OF THE FIGHT CLUB HAS BEEN ELIMINATED....

THE WINNER....ENMITY!!!!

Kobentai: In an amazing sowing, ENMITY has won the Gekito Cup!!

Mori: That was simply breathtaking, incredible work by Enmity.

*As Enmity begins to gain his breath, a pissed off Maclellan makes his return into the ring, blindsiding Enmity with a right hook before mounting him and pummeling him with vicious lefts and rights. The fans boo this sad display of sportsmanship*

Kobentai: THE MATCH IS OVER, WHY CAN'T HE WALK AWAY?!?!!?

Mori: I guess he couldn't get over that surprise loss to Enmity.

*Suddenly, during this brutal beatdown....the roof EXPLODES off the the Okata Gym, "Prayer" by Sads hitting the sound system as Inphino Blitz rushes out with a mission from behind the curtain, Gekito Cup in hand. He places it on the ring steps, sliding in the ring and catching Maclellan with a VICIOUS sidekick that sounds off through the entire arena, the fans still whipped into a frenzy by the star's surprise run-in.*

Mori: BLITZ IS HERE!!

Kobentai: I GUESS HE COULDN'T STAND TO SEE THIS HAPPENING EITHER!! GET HIM BLITZ!!!

*Blitz punishes Maclellan with a vicious flurry of blazing kicks and punches, sending him into the ropes as Enmity begins to regain his bearings. He soon joins Blitz, both grabbing an arm of Maclellan and slinging him into the ropes...the two then team up, both CRACKING his jaw with double superkicks as the enraged Fight Club member rolls out of the ring, holding his jaw as he walks away to the back. The fans are still on their feet, Inphino looking to Enmity and Enmity looking to Inphino. Blitz reaches out to the beautiful Gekito Cup, a large platinum trophy with kanji written on the front and sporting a regal look to it. He hands the beautiful cup to Enmity, who holds it with joyous pride in his hand as Blitz takes his other hand, raising it high in the air wit his as the fans are on their feet, clapping hysterically for this display*

Kobentai: BEAUTIFUL...simply beautiful. WELL THAT IS ALL FOR GEKITO LADIES AND GENTLEMAN, BE BACK FOR OUR NEXT EXCITING ADDITION AND TAKE THIS ALL IN...THE FUTURE MEETING THE PRESENT!!

Mori: This makes me wanna eat...GOOD NIGHT EVERYONE!!

*The PURE 2002 copyright comes down across the bottom of the screen, the scene fading with Inphino raising the hand of Enmity...the camera gaining a beautiful close-up of the cup, the future, and the present, with the fans deafening cheers as their chorus*
