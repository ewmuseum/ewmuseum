Bio: Aryim is an unknown man. No one knows how long he has been around and people have said he has fought in many historical battles. He spends a lot of his life sleeping in a deep cave until one day a man by the name of Wei Chang came along looking for him. He needed him to goto America with him for a boxing club, and of course Aryim accepted since he is curious. He went over to America to box, but he found out that wrestling was more physical and that is was more bigger than boxing. So now he has got into wrestling trying to find a way to figure out the history to his life.
