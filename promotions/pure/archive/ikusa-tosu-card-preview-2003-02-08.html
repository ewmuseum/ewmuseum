Ikusa — Tosu Stadium, card preview

8 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PURE Ikusa from Tosu Stadium in Tosu, Japan on February 9 ,2003 Deadline-LATE Saturday Night Main Event-10 Man Tag Jesse Ramey, Eddie Mandrake, Raizo Tenzan, & The Sushi Crew vs. "Sinister" Steven Lee, Enmity, & The Black Dragons You can see the obvious rivalries on each side of the ring...all except for one...Raizo Tenzan and The Black Dragons. Raizo has had serious "territory" problems with the Dragons and is looking to team with the ultra popular squad of Mandrake, Ramey, and the Sushi Crew to battle the pure evil team of Enmity, SSL, and The Black Dragons. With Enmity of suspension, Mandrake seething to get at "The Messiah of Mutilation", SSL dead set on getting back at Ramey, and various other heated issues...this match is guarenteed to be a barn burner and without a doubt...someone is going to get hurt without a doubt... JR.Weight Title Match Radiant vs. Chase Herrera Impressive...that is all that can be said about Chase Herrera's win over Peter Perez for his win over rookie rival, Peter Perez last show. For his efforts, he is rewarded a JR.Weight title shot against "The Masked Man of Submission", Radiant. However, with Peter Perez and Isao Asai waiting in the wings...each with a bone to pick with either man, will they effect the course of what should be an AWESOME JR.Weight title match? Open Invite Battle Royal-Open To Anybody...Winner Recieves PURE World Title Shot At The Next Ikusa After Final Destinies Anybody Who Chooses To Participate From The PURE Roster(Simply RP for it, you cannot be booked for any other matches on the card however to participate) You read right. This is not just an open invite tourney for any member of PURE to get the first shot at the most coveted prize in wrestling...the PURE World Title. Anybody who makes the effort to come out to the ring and take a chance has the oppourtunity to get their names in the history books...when they battle the new champion after Final Destinies. Who is ready to step up and battle? We shall see.... Also to be expected -Isao Asai will announce his stipulation for the JR.Weight title match at Final Destinies against Radiant. -Senji Yulav has been heard backstage speaking of making Kohana "care" about their battle...and it'll be a hell of a wake-up call for Kohana. -There have also been rumblings of a MAJOR event taking place that will rock PURE's foundation...and it may not be able to stand up again after it. BE SURE TO WATCH THIS SUNDAY NIGHT ON UPN WITH HITOMI, MORI, AND MANG-A-HERO as PURE brings you their unique brand of wrestling action! Tune in 8pm on UPN and get PURE-IFIED!!
