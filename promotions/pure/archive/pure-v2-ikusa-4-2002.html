PURE — Card

PURE v2.0 Ikusa 4

13 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

This posting holds the title match before the battle royal; the broadcast ran the battle royal first (the title match’s own commentary calls the battle royal “earlier”). Both are served here in the posting’s own order.

MATCH 4

Hitomi Asami: Welcome back to Ikusa 4...Live from Ikusa Hal!

Mang-A-Hero: Where we get to see Mat W. cowering from me live on national television.

Mat W.: Please don’t hit me sir.

Hitomi Asami: What a match we have scheduled next. One man who helped PCW defeat our valiant warriors at Red October, Jung Loc, going head to head with a bright young upstart in PURE, DJ Nygma, with Loc’s PURE Jr. Heavyweight Title on the line….although this match SHOULD be a three-way dance, Ilan Levin’s gang-style beatdown by the Black Dragons has altered our plans!

Mat W.: This match shouldn’t even be booked. We have two of my men going against each other.

Mang-A-Hero: Well deduced Mat. Give this lad a penny.

Hitomi Asami: It was known prior to the battle royal that Loc would defend against the winner. DJ Nygma one the battle royal, ergo, he’s the challenger.

Mat W.: Still don’t like it.

**Crawling (DJ Nygma Remix) by Linkin Park hits Ikusa Hall as the crowd erupt into a chorus of boo’s.**

Hitomi Asami: The Ikusa crowd getting on the back of DJ Nygma from the outset here.

Mat W.: They do every time. These Ikusa morons have no sense of respect for the best athletes in the company.

Mang-A-Hero: They have full respect for those that demand respect. Those that don’t deserve respect, don’t get shown any.

Mat W.: When did you swallow Confucius’ Guide to Life?

**DJ Nygma steps out to the crowd with a record bag slung over his right shoulder, walking down the aisle nodding his head in time to the beats. Leaving the bag at ringside, he slowly slides into the ring, and removes the headphones from his head, leaving them on the turnbuckle as he steps into the centre of the ring.**

Hitomi Asami: DJ Nygma is bringing a lot to the front of play since he joined PURE.

Mat W.: Of course he has!

Mang-A-Hero: Like a fresh face to loathe, for instance.

Mat W.: Gah!

**Crawling slowly fades out as Hate Me Now by Nas feat. P Diddy takes over the arena sound system. The crowd again erupt into a chorus of boo’s as Jung Loc hits the arena, his PURE Junior Heavyweight Title slung over his left shoulder as he slides into the ring.**

Hitomi Asami: Jung Loc should be ashamed to step into a PURE ring!

Mang-A-Hero: He was forced into wrestling for PCW.

Mat W.: But, like all wrestlers should, he enjoyed it! What else could he do, but take pleasure in making sure that this vagabond group of PUREists didn’t get anywhere.

Hitomi Asami: Mat, that “vagabond” group was Jamar Wilson, Enmity, S2, Cianan and Inphino Blitz!

Mat W.: Only Mr Wilson and Enmity in there are worth the entrance fee. Inphino Blitz drags ANY team down!

Mang-A-Hero: Inphino Blitz is one of our flag bearers here in PURE!

Mat W.: See what I mean? If it wasn’t for him being a “flag bearer” we may have had enough talent to beat PCW!

Hitomi Asami: Let’s get back to action...Please!

Hanza Kibagame: Ladies and Gentlemen...

Mat W.: And the crowd in attendance.

Mang-A-Hero: You disrespectful son of a...

Hanza Kibagame: This next bout is scheduled for one fall, with a thirty minute time limit, and is for the PURE Junior Heavyweight Title! Introducing the challenger first. From Tokyo, and weighing in at 210 lbs...He is the winner of tonight‘s Battle Royal...DJ NYGMA!!!

**Nygma takes a step forward, and bows his head to senior referee, Taki Kuonji. A small section of the crowd applaud Nygma, but the rest of them shower him with boo’s as normal.**

Hitomi Asami: As much as this guy irritates me, I have to give him respect. He plucked away and came out top in the battle royal earlier, and gets his shot at a fresher and more alert Jung Loc.

Hanza Kibagame: And his opponent, the current PURE Junior Heavyweight Champion. Currently residing in Tokyo, and weighing in at 207 lbs...JUNG LOC!!!

**Again the crowd meet the announcement with boo’s and jeers, but the same section applaud as Jung Loc bows his head at Kuonji. Kibagame climbs out of the ring, and Kuonji signals for the bell to ring. As it does, Nygma and Loc start to circle each other.**

Mat W.: Finally, these disrespectful Jap’s get to see two TRUE athletes perform.

Mang-A-Hero: They get to see great athlete’s week in, week out. PURE is home to some of Japan’s greatest performers!

Mat W.: So why did they let you in?

Mang-A-Hero: To boost the performance of this announce team. I mean, you’d drag Hitomi anywhere with you, and down would lose us ratings.

Mat W.: I don’t think so. Hitomi going down on me would bring in great ratings.

Hitomi Asami: It would also bring you from your land of dreams back to reality when I slap you silly for comments such as that.

Mang-A-Hero: Now that would be ratings.

**Nygma and Loc finally tie up, and wrestle with each other for position, breaking when Loc backs the DJ into the corner. Jung Loc steps back with a clean break as Nygma looks on puzzled. The crowd give a short burst of applaud for the sportsmanship as Nygma and Loc circle again.**

Hitomi Asami: Wasn’t it you who asked for clean breaks to be banned Mat?

Mat W.: I meant for...SHUT UP!

**Loc ties up with Nygma again, wrenching the DJ back with an arm wringer, switching it to a wristlock, and leaning forward, forcing Nygma to bridge. Nygma manages to muster up strength, and bridges forward again, battling back to his feet, and reversing the move on Loc. Nygma moves his feet back a few paces, using more leverage, and dropping Loc into a pinning combination. Taki Kuonji drops for a cover, but Loc bridges himself, and starts to battle back to his feet.**

Hitomi Asami: This is a great test of power between these two!

Mat W.: Hold on, these moves should only be used by crap wrestlers. This isn’t right.

Mang-A-Hero: Jung Loc and DJ Nygma look as though they want to put on a true wrestling clinic.

**Loc gets back to his feet, and rolls through the hold, popping out with a hammerlock on Nygma. Nygma ducks under the hold, and returns to an arm wringer, twisting it for extra pressure. Jung Loc drops to one knee, but quickly stands up and rolls through the move. Standing again, he rolls backwards, and then cartwheels forwards to come back to his feet, and lock in the arm wringer on Nygma, forcing the DJ to one knee.**

Hitomi Asami: The crowd are warming to this.

Mat W.: This may be Jung Loc and DJ Nygma, but can I go to sleep.

Mang-A-Hero: Will it mean you being quiet?

Mat W.: Yeah.

Mang-A-Hero: Granted.

**Nygma stands back on his feet, and nails a few right hands at Loc, backing him into a corner. Taki Kuonji asks for a clean break, and Nygma holds up his hands, backing off, before nailing a chop to Loc’s chest. Nygma nails another few chops, and then whips “Charlie” into the opposite corner. Nygma smiles, and follows in on Loc, nailing a clothesline in the corner. Nygma goes to whip Loc back into the first corner, but the champion reverses, and sends Nygma in. Loc follows in quickly, and leaps into the air, planting his feet on The Recreator’s chest, and falling back for a monkey flip.**

Mang-A-Hero: Great agility by DJ Nygma!

Hitomi Asami: Cartwheel back to a standing position to counter the monkey flip!

**Nygma charges back at Jung Loc, but takes a flapjack lift for his trouble. Using his martial arts background, DJ Nygma manages to land perfectly balanced on the top rope, and springs back into Jung Loc with a cross body, and hooks the leg for the cover.**

Taki Kuonji: ONE! TWO! NO!

Hitomi Asami: Superb chain wrestling by these two!

Mang-A-Hero: This is a great display of technical prowess! Jung Loc and DJ Nygma putting on a clinic in an effort to go home with the belt.

Hitomi Asami: All bad feelings aside for this one! They’re pulling out all the stops in this match.

Mang-A-Hero: I don’t know if Taki’s going to be able to keep up with this!

**Loc powers out of the cover, and meets a right hand from DJ Nygma. Nygma backs Loc to the middle of the ropes, and whips him off of the reverse. Nygma drops down into the splits, ducking his head, but Loc spots it, and attempts a leg drop. Nygma manages to sit up at the last minute, causing Loc to land on his tailbone. Nygma quickly attempts a spin kick to Loc’s face, but Jung rolls backwards, avoids the kick, and kips back up to his feet.**

Hitomi Asami: Both men misfiring.

Mat W.: I never have that problem.

Mang-A-Hero: That’s only due to the fact that you’ve never had anything to fire at.

**Jung Loc watches as Nygma kips back to his feet, and quickly runs the ropes. Loc springs onto the top, and springs back for a cross body. Nygma manages to catch Loc, but Jung switches momentum, and takes him over with an armdrag. Jung Loc is first back to his feet, but quickly gets flipped over with an arm drag by Nygma. Nygma jumps back to his feet first, and runs the ropes, springing onto the top himself. Showing perfect balance, he jumps again to face the ring, and nails Loc with a double jump clothesline. Nygma hooks the leg for the cover.**

Taki Kuonji: ONE! TWO! NO!

Hitomi Asami: Another great combination of wrestling by Loc and Nygma!

Mat W.: If this is a prequel to maiming, then I’m all for it.

Mang-A-Hero: Well, I can arrange someone to maim you after the show?

Mat W.: Yeah, that would....HEY!

**Jung Loc again kicks out, and rolls back to his feet. DJ Nygma quickly applies a headlock, but Jung Loc shoves him off of the ropes. Loc ducks down on the return, and Nygma skips over. On the second return, Loc leaps into the air, and Nygma ducks through, spinning on the spot. Loc runs the ropes himself, and returns at the DJ, but Nygma side steps and shoves Loc. Jung quickly leaps onto the top rope, and with perfect momentum, spins off into a crucifix manoeuvre on Nygma. The DJ manages to break his right arm free, so in a brilliant counter, Loc drops behind the challenger, and hooks both arms, flipping him over with a bridged Dragon Suplex.**

Taki Kuonji: ONE! TWO! NO!

Hitomi Asami: Listen to the applause for that chain!

Mang-A-Hero: That was impressive! One of the best chains I’ve seen in PURE for a long while.

Mat W.: What? All he did was hit a Full Nelson Suplex. Not that hard.

Hitomi Asami: It’s called a Dragon Suplex, and I’d love to see you try and nail that.

Mat W.: I’d love to see me nail...

Mang-A-Hero: ENOUGH!

**The crowd applaud the previous chain as Jung Loc gets to his feet. DJ Nygma, beginning to show signs of fatigue after the punishment delivered in both matches, slowly gets to one knee. Jung Loc quickly switches to the offensive, and nails a dropkick to the face of the DJ.**

Mat W.: O.K. This is where it can become good. Jung Loc taking full advantage and maiming the challenger.

Hitomi Asami: The tide has changed on this one. Nygma is beginning to feel the effects of the night so far.

Mang-A-Hero: If Jung Loc is smart, the pace will slow down on this one.

Mat W.: Of course he is smart! He’s smarter than you! He’s so smart...he won the Junior Heavyweight Title without even being on a PURE broadcast!

**Jung watches as Nygma falls back to the mat, and measures him for an elbow drop. Jung quickly lifts DJ Nygma back to his feet, and backs him up into a corner, delivering a shot of right hands to the challenger. Jung Loc watches as DJ Nygma drops to a knee in the corner, before picking him up and whipping him into the opposite corner. Loc follows in, and drives a shoulder into his midsection. Loc delivers a few more, before nailing a hard right hand chop to Nygma’s chest. Loc reaches out with his left hand, and grabs Nygma’s right, dragging him out of the corner slightly.**

Hitomi Asami: Now what does he have planned?

Mat W.: It better be something worth watching. Otherwise my home videos of you undressing may have to keep me company.

Hitomi Asami: WHAT?!?!

Mat W.: Oops! Did I say that out loud?

**Loc nails a kick to Nygma’s midsection, then runs at the ropes, springing off of the second, onto the top rope, and then back into the ring, latching onto DJ Nygma with a headscissors, and taking him over with a hurricanrana. The crowd start applauding as Loc holds on for the cover.**

Taki Kuonji: ONE! TWO! THR...NO!!!

Hitomi Asami: Double jump springboard hurricanrana!

Mat W.: Easy for you to say!

Mang-A-Hero: Great skill on the high offence there, but not enough to get a three.

**Jung hits the mat in frustration as he gets back to his feet, and grabs Nygma’s right arm again. Loc again drags the DJ to the corner, and runs onto the second rope, springing to the top, but this time, as he springs back around, he attempts an armdrag. Nygma catches the idea, and grabs hold of Loc’s other hand, kicking the champion in the shin as he lands on the mat. Both men stood, Nygma makes sure his and Loc’s hands are locked together. Nygma quickly asserts pressure, and pushes Loc back into a bridge.**

Hitomi Asami: Excellent counter by the newcomer!

Mang-A-Hero: The springboard, double jump armdrag countered by a kick to the shin? If it works!

Mat W.: A dirty tactic. I love it.

**Kuonji bends down, and checks that Loc’s shoulders on the mat. Loc manages to use his upper body strength to raise his head off of the mat, and starts to bridge back against the pressure. Nygma soon finds himself back at a vertical base, and Loc quickly takes the momentum, bridging the challenger. Nygma manages to balance the bridge so his shoulders aren’t touching the mat. Loc realises, and quickly leaps into the air, driving both knees across the bridged up chest of Nygma, forcing the DJ’s shoulders down for the count.**

Taki Kuonji: ONE! TWO! NO!

**Nygma manages to bridge again, to break the count. Loc again leaps to drive onto the chest of Nygma, but the DJ drops the bridge and gets his knees up. Holding Loc on his knees, Nygma uses his own momentum to push the champion back to his feet, and roll to his feet after him. Nygma quickly takes the initiative, and jumps, planting his feet on Loc’s chest and rolling him with a Monkey Flip. Loc doesn’t break the locked fingers, however, and lands flat out, head to head with Nygma. Nygma senses the stalemate, and flips his body backwards over onto Loc’s. Jung gets his knees up, and again uses momentum to push both athletes back to their feet.**

Hitomi Asami: This match just gets better and better!

Mang-A-Hero: These two are still on the early parts of their game plans! Still feeling each other out.

Mat W.: Remind me never to hit the town with you.

**Loc jumps at DJ Nygma on this attempt, and spins in mid-air, breaking the finger locks, and applying a body scissors around Nygma’s waist. Loc rolls forward, and takes the DJ into a roll up pin.**

Taki Kuonji: ONE! TWO!

Hitomi Asami: Great pinning effort.

Mat W.: It can’t be great. He only got a two!

Mang-A-Hero: But it makes Nygma exert energy to kick out.

**DJ Nygma breaks the hold, and rolls back to his feet, but turns too late as Jung Loc rolls into a armdrag, locking on an armbar as he sits out. Kuonji checks on Nygma, but the DJ manages to shift body position, and get his feet over the bottom rope. Loc breaks the hold without being told, and jumps back to his feet, nailing a few kicks to Nygma’s chest. Loc picks up his challenger, and whips him off the ropes, dropping down with a Drop Toe Hold on the return. Loc quickly floats into a reverse Fujiwara armbar.**

Hitomi Asami: Great ring positioning this time from Jung Loc. Too close to the ropes on one exchange, dead centre of the ring on the second.

Mang-A-Hero: I don’t think Loc will get a submission out of this, but it at least wears Nygma down a bit more.

Mat W.: This match is getting boring. Anyone want anything from the refreshment stands?

Mang-A-Hero: Yeah, I’ll have a...

Hitomi Asami: SIT DOWN! You’re paid to commentate. You can go for refreshments later!

**Mat mumbles something under his breath that we can’t quite hear. Loc rolls out of the armbar, and rolls Nygma onto his back. He locks his right leg around Nygma’s left, and looks into the crowd. The crowd deliver a mixed reaction to Loc, partially in hatred of his previous actions in PURE, partially as an applause due to the excellent match put on so far. Loc slaps the leg of Nygma, and rolls forward. The momentum, and hold of the move causes Nygma to follow the roll, and snap back top the mat spine first in an almost suplex like action. Loc drops the hold and goes for the cover.**

Taki Kuonji: ONE! TWO! NO!

Hitomi Asami: Rolling Leg Snap Takeover gets a two on DJ Nygma.

Mang-A-Hero: I’m impressed with Jung Loc. He’s increasing his arsenal of moves live on Ikusa 4!

Mat W.: So...sleepy.

**Loc shakes his head as he stands, and lifts Nygma back to his feet. Jung quickly locks the DJ into a front face lock, and lifts him vertical, suspending him in mid-air. The crows start applauding as Loc stands upright. After spot on fifteen seconds, Loc shifts position, and drives Nygma down to the mat with a suspended Falcon Arrow. The crowd erupt into applause.**

Hitomi Asami: Shades of the Great Hayabusa with the Falcon Arrow!

Mang-A-Hero: Brilliant extension and suspension of the move. And the execution was spot on.

Mat W.: I could go for a spot on execution right now.

Mang-A-Hero: Put your head in the stocks then.

Mat W.: Not me!

**Loc plants Nygma to the mat, and moves him into place, before walking over to the corner. Backing into the corner, Loc steps up onto the second rope, and looks down at Nygma.**

Hitomi Asami: Another high impact move on its way.

**Loc nods his head, and leaps from the second rope onto the top rope facing outwards, in quick movement he springs back to the adjacent rope facing inwards, and flips off with a Swanton Bomb onto Nygma, floating over for the cover.**

Crowd: LOC! LOC! LOC!

Hitomi Asami: Double Jump Swanton Bomb!

Mang-A-Hero: The crowd is loving this!

Taki Kuonji: ONE! TWO! THR...NO!

Hitomi Asami: DJ Nygma barely kicked out there!

Mang-A-Hero: Jung Loc is taking it to the top to defend his belt!

**Loc shakes his head as he breaks the pin, and slides out of the ring. The cheers of the crowd turn into a mixed reaction as he snatches Hanza Kibagame’s chair away from him, and slides it into the ring. DJ Nygma slowly gets back to his feet on the inside, and Loc, sensing the end in sight, leaps onto the top rope. Watching as Nygma stands, Loc leaps from the top, and catches a perfect Spinning Heel Kick, sending the DJ back to the mat.**

Hitomi Asami: Top Rope Spinning Heel Kick by Jung Loc!

Taki Kuonji: ONE! TWO! THREE-NO!!! NO!

Mang-A-Hero: How in the name of all that is Kryptonite did he kick out of that?!

Mat W.: Just end it already!

Hitomi Asami: Brilliant resilience by DJ Nygma! He’s gone through a Battle Royal and the effects of this match so far! He isn’t going to quit now!

**Jung Loc looks as DJ Nygma lays motionless on the mat, fighting for every breath. Positioning the steel chair across Nygma’s chest, he looks at the top rope, and climbs onto the apron.**

Mang-A-Hero: This could be it. The spinning heel kick is normally a set up for Lead To My Fame, Jung’s version of a 450 Splash.

Hitomi Asami: If Jung Loc hits this, especially onto the steel chair, it could be over for Nygma.

**Jung Loc starts to climb the ropes, looking into the crowd as they start to cheer again. Loc perches on the top rope, and suddenly stands up straight, taking off with a PERFECT 450 SPLASH!**

Hitomi Asami: LEAD TO MY FAME!

Mang-A-Hero: NO!!! Nygma got his knees up!!!

Mat W.: AH RATS!

Hitomi Asami: DJ Nygma got his knees up with the steel chair! Jung Loc just took steel into his sternum! Both men are down!

Taki Kuonji: ONE!

Mang-A-Hero: What a match this has been so far! Backwards and forwards action.

Taki Kuonji: TWO!

Mat W.: Please God someone finish this. I need to relieve myself.

Taki Kuonji: THREE!

Hitomi Asami: I’m sure the crowd really wanted to know that, Mat.

Taki Kuonji: FOUR!

Mat W.: I mean of the tension and boredom I’ve had sitting next to Mang-A-Dork all night.

Taki Kuonji: FIVE!

Mang-A-Hero: I’m putting in a request to Jamar Wilson for a match against you next week. You’ve irritated me all evening!

Taki Kuonji: SIX!

Mat W.: Can’t we settle it like men? Thumb wrestling?

**Both men start to stir on the mat, with Loc showing more signs of movement.**

Taki Kuonji: SEVEN!

Mang-A-Hero: You’re an even bigger wimp than your reputation makes you out to be. If that’s possible.

Taki Kuonji: EIGHT!

**Jung Loc slowly gets to one knee, and his right arm reaches the ropes. Nygma manages to roll onto his knee as well, both hands reaching out.**

Taki Kuonji: NINE!

**Nygma gets back up to a vertical base as Jung uses the ropes for leverage.**

Hitomi Asami: Both men back up. The match continues!

Mat W.: DAMN IT!

**Jung Loc throws the first punch, but has it blocked by Nygma, who nails a right of his own. Loc again throws a punch, but Nygma retaliates. Nygma starts to fire back with rights, before nailing a chop across Jung’s chest. Loc buckles under the shot, and drops to one knee briefly. DJ Nygma helps him back up, and whips him off of the ropes, nailing a front kick on the return. Nygma runs the ropes himself, and nails a dropkick to the side of Loc’s ducked head. Nygma floats for the cover.**

Taki Kuonji: ONE! TW! NO!

Hitomi Asami: Barely a two count! Loc still has plenty of fight in him.

Mang-A-Hero: This match is taking it’s toll on both competitors. It comes down to who wants the gold most.

Mat W.: I’ll say it again...DAMN IT!

**Nygma gets back to his feet, and lifts Loc up. Nailing a few more shots to Loc’s chest, Nygma steps back and nails a sidekick, before leaping into a hurricanrana. Loc manages to hold on to the attempt, but Nygma forces himself into a spinning position, attempting a rolling school boy, but quickly changes back and manages to pull out the Spinning Hurricanrana!**

Taki Kuonji: ONE! TWO! NO!

Hitomi Asami: Brilliant technique by DJ Nygma. Using the fake on the rolling school boy to pull out his spinning hurricanrana.

Mang-A-Hero: This match is surpassing the half way point of the time limit. We’re on just over sixteen minutes now.

Mat W.: No wonder I feel tired. I shouldn’t have to watch more than five minutes at a time!

**Nygma jumps back to his feet, and waits on Loc. As Jung stands, Nygma drives a shin kick into the champion’s side. Loc staggers back as Nygma nails a roundhouse kick to the side of the head. Jung Loc drops to one knee as Nygma squares up another kick to the chest. Loc, trying to fight back gets back to his feet as Nygma throws another roundhouse. Loc manages to catch the second roundhouse, and swings Nygma around. Jung then lunges at him with a clothesline, only for Nygma to duck, spin Loc round, and plant him with a spinning inverted neckbreaker.**

Hitomi Asami: Turnbuckle Slam! That’s DJ Nygma’s set up move!

Mang-A-Hero: The reMIX is due next!

Mat W.: An ending? Goodie!

**Nygma shakes his head loose of the cobwebs, and drops for the cover.**

Taki Kuonji: ONE! TWO! THR...NO!!!

Hitomi Asami: Loc kicked out!

**Nygma shakes his head and stands up, resting his palms on his knees as he holds his head, showing the effects of the matches he’s fought in. Jung Loc slowly starts to rise onto one knee, and Nygma starts to smile. Jung Loc starts to hold his head, with the referee checking on him. Loc shoves Kuonji away, and steps forward, but right into a heel kick from Nygma! Loc stumbles backwards and hits the ropes, bouncing forwards, only to meet a Nygma Spinning Heel Kick!**

Hitomi Asami: THE reMIX!!! This has to be it!

Mang-A-Hero: Cover him!

**Nygma pauses a second before dropping for the cover.**

Taki Kuonji: ONE! TWO! THREE...NO!!!

**The crowd erupt into applause as Jung Loc manages to squeeze his left arm out at the last possible nanosecond.**

Hitomi Asami: JUNG LOC KICKED OUT OF THE reMIX!!!

Mang-A-Hero: This title must mean more to him than we thought!

Mat W.: DAMNIT! END THE MATCH!

**Nygma jumps back to his feet, not believing that the champion kicked out of his finisher. The DJ grabs hold of Kuonji, and starts to shout abuse at the referee. Kuonji stands his ground, and Nygma shakes his head.**

** Nygma attempts a clothesline, but Loc ducks, and steps forward. Nygma spins around, but is met square on by a perfect superkick to the jaw!**

Hitomi Asami: Superkick!

Mang-A-Hero: Loc going for the cover!

Taki Kuonji: ONE! TWO! NO!

**Nygma throws his shoulder up bang on two, breaking the count, as Jung rolls to his knees, punching the mat with both hands. Jung Loc quickly gets back to his feet, and runs at the ropes, leaping to the top rope. Nygma gets back to his feet, and Loc flies off with a cross body.**

Hitomi Asami: Countered!

Mang-A-Hero: Great awareness by DJ Nygma!

Mat W.: End this!

**Loc drops to the mat from the dropkick delivered by Nygma. The DJ looks down at the fallen champion, and rushes to the corner.**

Mat W.: Please put that chair to good use and end this!

**Nygma picks up the steel chair, and waits on Loc to get back to his feet. Nygma slams the chair on the mat a few times as Loc spins, and charges for a shot to the head, only for Loc to duck, spin around and drive the chair back into Nygma’s face with a ROARING ELBOW!**

Hitomi Asami: Credit to Masato Tanaka on the Roaring Elbow!

Mang-A-Hero: That should be it!

Taki Kuonji: ONE! TWO! THR! NO!!!

**The crowd applaud as Nygma shoots up his shoulder, and Loc starts to argue with the ref. Loc leaves the ref alone after a few seconds, and places the chair in the centre of the ring. Loc drags Nygma over to the second corner, and nails a few open right hands to the challenger. Leaping onto the second rope, Loc grabs Nygma in a front face lock, and signals to the crowd.**

Hitomi Asami: This should put an end to this!

Mang-A-Hero: You’d think so, but after the punishment they’ve delivered to each other, can you see either one giving up now?

Mat W.: We can only hope.

**Loc delivers an open hand to shot to Nygma’s back, and then leaps off of the second rope, aiming for a Tornado DDT. Nygma somehow manages to block the shot, and spins to his back, stepping forward, and planting Loc with a NORTHERN LIGHTS SUPLEX!**

Hitomi Asami: EXCELLENT COUNTER BY DJ NYGMA!

Mang-A-Hero: This HAS to be it!

Taki Kuonji: ONE! TWO! THREE! NOOOO!!!!!

Mat W. GOD MOTHER F...

Hitomi Asami: QUIET!

Mang-A-Hero: LOC KICKED OUT OF THE NORTHERN LIGHTS!

**DJ Nygma slams his fist on the floor, not quite believing that Jung Loc kicked out again, before picking the Champion up, and whipping him into the ropes. Nygma ducks his head for a back drop, but Loc takes on a quick burst of energy, and leaps over into a Sunset Flip!**

Taki Kuonji: ONE!!! TWO!!! THREE!!! NOOOOO!!!!!

Hitomi Asami: DJ NYGMA JUST BROKE UP THE COUNT WITH A CHAIR SHOT RIGHT BETWEEN JUNG LOC’S EYES!

Mang-A-Hero: That would have been it!!!

Mat W.: DAMN THEM BOTH TO HIGH HELL!!!

**Nygma and Loc both collapse to their backs on the mat, Kuonji starting the ten count again.**

MAH: Man this match has been amazing, what more do they have left?!
