Bio

Wrestling Attire: Mang-A-Hero wears a full body spandex costume with various kanji imprints upon it and various manga and jap pop culture logos or imprints on it. He also wears a set of shoulder pads (Hayabusa style) with additional kanji written upon it and each one having 'Go Go Tokyo' written upon it. He wears a martial arts style bandanna around his head with MAH imprinted on a rising sun upon it. There are holes in the eye, mouth, and hair area for the mask and it's made from tight spandex which hugs our hero. His hair is golden blonde and he has soft blue eyes. Often always smiling. His body is well built muscularly, which fills in his suit real well form his tight abs to his built arms and legs

Ring Entrance: 'Tokyo 6pm' by Angina P. plays, strobes activating about the arena as it flashes from dark blue, to white, to red on beat with the intro to the music. Upon the beat bursting with speed, laserlights of various colors shoot from the back entryway to the ring as Mang-A-Hero emerges, showered within the lights and striking several silly martial arts poses before dashing down to the ring, sliding in and posing atop a turnbuckle with hands extended high in air in a cross, yelling 'GO GO TOKYO' as the fans chant along.

Bio:PCW superstar Rent-A-Hero truly loved his time in the United states, he could not get enough. But something egged him as he wondered, 'What else can a spandex enwrapped hero of my stature accomplish in the world of wrestling?' With that question pondering him....it came to midn upon watching an episode of his beloved Sailor Moon. WHY NOT TRY A HAND A PURO? It certainly was a stretch, the more sports entertainment enfluenced Rent-A-Hero joining a puro group, known well for it's serious style of wrestling. With that known, Rent-A-Hero made it a point to TWEAK and UPGRADE his beloved persona and that he did. He sat down and broke down every manga, martial arts, and fight movie tape possible...learning and delving deep into their culture more than before until he was blurting out foolish Dragonball Z gibberish and not matching his mouth with his words. With that feat accomplished or Kryptonian hero did the last thing he could think of...when in Rome, do what the Romans do....in this case Japan. So he took a costume and altered it until all his manga knowledge was imprinted into his new suit. Now embracing his new style of 'Manga-whoopering' our hero hasset his goals on becoming hero to a whole new group of people who are yet to even BEGIN to realize the wild card that is the newly christened Mang-A-Hero. With his beloved Escalflowne collection, a chinse resteraunt menu(he claims it's a universal translator), and a love for the buisness and a new group of fans..Mang-A-Hero hopes to slay and defeat all the villains who have eluded him in the East, and with his new dedication and upgraded moves, it's a very serious possiblity, even if he is not one to be taken seriously himself! WILL HE MAKE THESE DREAMS COME TRUE?! CAN HE SLAY THE FORCES OF FOILONDERING EVIL?! GO GO TOKYO...AND TUNE IN FOR THE NEXT MANG-A-HERO SHOW!
