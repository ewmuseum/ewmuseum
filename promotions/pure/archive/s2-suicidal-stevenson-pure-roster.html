History:

Cordozer 'S2' Stevenson is one of the most gifted, well-rounded, and unpredictable wrestlers of our time. Still at the bright young age of nineteen, he's been tucked away by Dante Jackson, and is maybe one of the youngest stars on the Japanese circuit today. The problem is, he can do just about anything just to please the fans. Whether it be from jumping thirty feet to the crowd below, or wrestling one of the most scientific PURE matches, he can do it all. His style tends to focus mainly on keeping his opponent on their toes, catching them off guard with his unorthodox wrestling throws and holds while quietly positioning the opponent to catch them off guard with his spectacular and devastating Endless Waltz . His style meshes well with any other style, and he can often work with several different styles, himself. This makes S2 one of the most respectable wrestlers in the market, today. No wonder when he snagged gold at the 2001 X Games, after pulling a Coffin Heelkicker that people started calling him Suicidal.


Cordozer 'S2' Stevenson is a well defined high flyer who is usually seen with a pair of silver shorts with the ends coming down past his kneecaps and coming close to his ankles. A circular spiked belt is wrapped around his shorts, as he wears a pair of Timberland Chukka Boots. He wears no shirt, showing his Crusierweight/Lightweight physique and multiple Japanese tattoos thanks to his ten years up bringing in Japan. Stevenson is viewed by some in the community as a sex symbol, for his good looks, and bad boy behavior. He is knowing of the Puroresu style, from various watchings of New Japan, and working the 2001 Super J Cup, finishing third.


Stevenson was brought up with a background in America, as well as Japan thanks to his descent. He is 1/3 African American, 1/3 Caucasian, and 1/3 Asian. By adulthood, Stevenson had become one of the more sought after talents in sports after his longevity in the Moto X circuit.


By the turn of 1998, Stevenson, barely even in his teens had grown fond of the sport of Motocross, and signed up beyond the signing age to appear in the 98' X Games in which he finished beyond his years and it earned him two gold medals and one silver. By 99', he had overcome every obstacle and was one of the star attractions, sweeping his events and winning gold. America and magazines were smitten with Stevenson.


After gracing the covers of the majority of extreme sport magazines, S2 ventured over into Japan, enventually coming back towards his roots in Puroresu wrestling.Without telling nay a soul, he dawned a mask, calling himself Black Dragon and compeiting in the New Japan Pro circuit, winning the Jr. Heavyweight tournament and competiting in some of the better matches of the year. Switching over to FMV later in the year, Steveson began to show the nature of a Xtreme star, that same commodity he had in America. He began to compete in extreme matches, showing off his nature of brutality and bringing FMV into a national powerhouse in Japan. Skipping the X Games for the year 2000, Stevenson continued to stay in Japan and continue his training, almost being silent and away from his sport for a year.


He came back to American in May of 2002, running into his now friend Dante Jackson during the Making A Star contest, running on MTV. The two of them grew mutual respect for one another, before Stevenson told Jackson that he was going to Japan once more, dropping the moniker Black Dragon and calling himself Suicidal Stevenson, or S2 for short. He's back in Japan, and out for another extreme show, like he says,


'Putting your body on the line, your life just for the fans is apart of this business. I may not be the biggest, or the smallest but I am the main reason people come to our shows.'
