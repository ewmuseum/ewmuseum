Bio

First, you'll notice that I filled practically nothing in at the top. There's a reason for that. There are 40 ninjas in the Suburban Ninja Clan. each one is fundamentally different from the other. So, I couldn't possibly focus on one, now could I?


So, here is the URL for their website, which is Angelfire hosted so forgive the popups.

http://www.angelfire.com/gundam/abadon

All the ninjas are organized there, follow the links to check them all out. Other stuff like awards, matches, and rivals will be filled in as it happens.


As for their history, it's best shown by the following two RPs. The first introduces them, and it was written by Syko the Goblin, the creator of the SNC. He made up the idea of 40 ninjas, and gave them to me after running them as an RP device in PCW.


But whereas his original idea was for the Sensei to wrestle and the other 40 provide backup, I've taken it a coupla steps further and made them a unit of 40 ninja which will fill in the job of what one roster spot would do as a wrestler. Each ninja has their own personality, strengths, weaknesses, and backstory.


For brevity's sake, I can't present everything about them at once. Part of it is that I give you a blurb of each ninja and develop them as the weeks and months progress. But they're quite varied as it is.


The second RP (in bold) is done by me, and follows Syko's timeline. He was feuding with Venneman Pendragon at the time, and this RP deals with that, as Pendragon took Bertha from Syko and he hires the SNC to get her back. It was actually written before I gave each a personality of their own, but nonetheless it serves to establish them, despite inconsistencies in behavior.


Long ago, there was a time where darkness was a way of existence for some.

Ancient shadows mark the beginning of a silent breed, conceived and birthed in those dark ages.


They were loyal not to themselves, but to their numbers. Swiftly, they would go off into the night, cascading as if the breeze though the darkness. Haste was made. Each to his own would serve well, tapping into dishonor to maintain his own honor.


They had no choice, yet willed it so, that they might become the perfect apparition of a shadow. Successfully, they made due... Never detected... not until it was too late. To see one of these shifting apparitions was to see your maker.


Failure was never an option, unacceptable to the dark race...

They would not live with themselves, having been shamed by failure. However, they solved the problem in their traditional manner.


They were true to their clan, deceitful to their enemies.

They were strong, graceful, and deadly.

They were the enigmatic race of the Medieval Japanese era.

They were Ninja.

Ninja...

The original Pokemon of Japan. In their day, they were huge. Every household had a horde of Ninja's... They went like hotcakes 24/7.


Hell, in that day... 99% of the population was Ninja. (Note: The suicide rate was through the roof.)


But, today with all the modern technology, and what not, in this ever evolving world, there is no need, seemingly for Ninjas... (Except in movies.) But, how could they be replaced?! WHERE HAVE ALL THESE STEALTH ASSASSINS VANISHED TO?!?!


Thursday, Sept 19th, 2002

Glenview, IL

135 Washington St.

8:00 PM

Nightfall has arrived for the small, well-established 'Tree City,' USA. It's quite calm out, notably warm for a fall evening. Inside this cozy little home, there's an electric hum. An old woman residing here sits in the glow of her microwave, watching a bag of popcorn pop.


There is a small creak above... The old gal looks up for a moment, at the slightly swayed hanging light fixture above. Unnerved, perhaps, she decides to ignore it.


As the kernels cease to pop, she rises to stop the microwave, popping the door open...

A rustle of leaves in her front yard catches her attention now... She scurries to her living room window, pulling a curtain aside... Nothing. Wait... A pair of blurs amongst the branches of the pine tree in her front yard scale into the depth of the greenery...


Turning slowly, the old woman heads back for her kitchen.

Little does she seem to know, is that the small circular, slotted vent for her attic has been disengaged from its place, allowing access for two NINJAS leaping from the tree, and diving head first through the small threshold, landing with in the attic as lightly as a feather.


A pack of Ninja gathered at the rear end of the house quietly tiptoe in a straight line towards the suburban home. The first in the line whips out a grappling hook, which is quickly launched, to the roof. A quick tug locks it into place and in a heartbeat they all scale the home, climbing the slanted roof, and heading for the chimney...


Flood windows become doorways for a flood of Ninja...

Meanwhile, the old lady dumps the fluffy popcorn into a big bowl, but suddenly she hesitates again. This time, there was a definitely a sound INSIDE her home... in fact, right inside her living room...


She lets the popcorn, bag and all, fall into the bowl as she creeps into he living room, the area seemingly empty minus herself.


Old Lady: 'You're all late. Such a noisy pack of fools...'

'FOOLS?!'

'SHH!!! We are supposed to be stealth...'

Old Lady: 'Morons... I can hear you.'

'No, you idiot, wait! Ahhh!'

'GAH!'

'I'm slipping!'

There is a loud succession of thuds as the Ninja in the chimney tumble out the mantle, to the living room floor, black ash fogging up the room. As they roll out, they bring a big mess with them.


The is a groan of disappointment all over the house...

Old Lady: 'DAMMIT!! I JUST STEAM VACCED THE ROOM TODAY!'

The old woman's eyes glow red. A pair of Ninja's drop down from the ceiling, some worm in through a ventilation shaft, a few more from a closet... The Ninja from the chimney rise, brushing themselves off.


Ninja #21: 'Oh yeah, so BECAUSE IT'S SO hard sitting around playing old house wife all day...'


Ninja #16: 'Do I smell popcorn?!'

Old Lady: 'HOUSE WIFE?!'

Suddenly, the old lady rips off her shawl, sweater, and blouse to reveal he muscular, bald headed, male body, shrouded decoratively in black.


Big Ninja guy: 'Do you idiots have any idea how hard it is to keep this place tidy, so no one suspects us?! I slave night and day keeping this home in tact for my Clan to come home to... All I ask is...'


'AHHHHHH!!!!'

Suddenly, the large front window is shattered to shards as five shadow knights come crashing through, having swung from the roof with grappling hooks. The Ninja already inside shield their eyes from the rain of glass, as the ambushing attackers release the grappling hooks, hitting the carpet floor rolling...


More hurl themselves in follow, and others smash their way in from the back.

With a mighty roar, the attackers unsheathe their confidence of weaponry... only to be silenced by the sight of already arrived Ninja.


Ninja #3: 'Uhhh... My clock said we were 5 minutes early... Sorry, Sensei...'

The large Sensei's eyes beam red, his nostrils venting steam...

Sensei: 'Shuttup, and make yourselves 'At home'. I made popcorn...'

---

Basement

8:30 PM

'Alright, alright, mouths shut, ears open! The weekly SNC meeting has begun!'

The murmuring of the Ninja slows, almost 40 or so squeezing into the basement. They kneel in rows, before a stage of sorts, the backdrop being a giant bronze gong. The big ninja formerly referred to as 'Old Lady' tries to shut the clan up...


Soon enough, they all are seated, passing the popcorn around. The Clan leader sets up a tripod canvas with a display. The lights dim and a beam of light shines upon the lone ninja.


Sensei: 'Tell your boss what is wrong with this?'

The Clan all squint at the grid on the canvas, on which a line graph is illustrated. A slight murmur circulates.


Sensei: 'You don't know?'

Silence.

Sensei: 'WELL THE PROBLEM SEEMS TO BE RIGHT HERE!!'

The Ninja master attempts to pinpoint a specific location on the diagram, only he accidentally unsheathes his katana...


The canvas hits the floor in two, merely causing the clan to be more confused, as the awkward murmur would signify.


Sensei: 'DAMMIT!!'

Ninja #23: 'What's this all about?'

Sensei: 'WHAT?! WHAT?! IT'S ABOUT OUR FALURE AS A NINJA CLAN!!! THAT'S WHAT!'

Hushed, the room watches the Ninja master carefully.

Sensei: 'We haven't pulled have the weight we've gained on revenues! As far as I'm concerned, this bumbling group of fools is non-existent!'


Suddenly, an outraged Clansmen Ninja flips to his feet with a clenched fist, shaken violently at the Sensei!


Ninja #40: 'BAH! It is this pitiful 'Land of Opportunity's' fault!'

Another follows.

Ninja #31: 'I second that... The homeland was our turf...'

Sensei: 'Indeed, it had been...'

Ninja #31: 'NOBODY HERE NEEDS NINJA!!!'

Sensei: 'Ignorance!!! There is so much untapped business here, hell, we could have saved ENRON, and got rich... here we can thrive with the proper advertisement!'


The entire room breaks into a murmur as yet another outraged Clansmen rises...

Ninja #7: 'YOU MEAN THE SAME ADVERTISEMENT THAT HAS PREVENTED US FROM HAVING THE SAME NEIGBORS FOR MORE THAN A FEW WEEKS?!?!'


Cut to a shot of a front door.

There is a sudden whizzing sound, followed by the impact of 3 arrows impeding themselves into the door. The door opens, and at that point they see the flaming, bleached skull in a basket on their front step, the reaction anticipated...


Cut back to the angry Clan leader, scowling.

Sensei: 'IMPOSSIBLE!! THE GREETING BASKET/CALLING CARD IS A BRILLIANT IDEA!!'

The Clan goes off on a verbal tangent, the uproar lasting a good ten minutes before...

Knock-Knock!

They all heard it. All the way upstairs, from the front door, the sound had echoed deep into the basement when the Clan all pulled their blades halfway from its sheath.


Sensei: 'Oh shit! I forgot to pay the mortgage!!!'

'MORTGAGE?!'

Suddenly, the entire room goes white, a hissing smoke filling the air. The smoke dissipates rather quickly, leaving the Sensei to find out soon enough he's all alone again.


---

We find that dastardly, devious little Goblin on the front step of the 135 Washington St. residence, scratching his chin thoughtfully at the broken front window.


Syko the Goblin: '...I hope this is the right address.'

'Remember, the assignment is to rescue Bertha. She's a female and very large...'

Outside of a castle, lit by spotlights shining brightly inside to confuse passing commercial airliners and make the place feel special, the seven Suburban Ninja Clan members stand in the traditional black uniform of the ninja that has been popularized (or in many cases, bastardized) along a moat which must be crossed...


Ninja #12:

'A moat? I only see these in the movies!'

Ninja #8:

'Dude, this guy realizes it's the 21st century, right?'

Ninja # 31:

'It doesn't matter, let's just swim across.'

One of the ninjas walks forward and steps into the water. As soon as he gets about waist high, a 50-foot-long blue-scaled water dragon pops out of the moat and devours the ninja in one bite. The others run away from the moat and take refuge in the nearby evergreen woods.


Ninja #20:

'Frickin A! A dragon?'

Ninja #6:

'That thing could've eaten us all whole!'

Ninja #31:

'Yes... luckily, I decided to stop along the way and bring an extra.'

The Sensei's best student, Ninja #1, steps out from the woods with his arms behind his back.


Ninja #1:

'Lesson 19 -- If there is a trap, bring along an extra; the extra will undoubtedly be the first to die.'


Following Ninja #1, the ninjas step out of the woods. Ninja #1, much more muscular and a little taller than the others, draws his sword from its sheath and steps forward.


Ninja #1:

'There is no other way to cross the moat. I must kill the creature.'

The another extra taps Ninja #1 on his shoulder. Grabbing his attention, he points over to the left. 150 yards away, a freight truck drives over the bridge that leads over the water to the castle.


Ninja #1:

'Uh... Right. I saw that. I just wanted to make you all realize it.'

He doesn't put his sword away, but waves the ninjas forward toward the bridge.

Ninja #1:

'There is our path across the water, go!'

The ninjas run across the shore toward the bridge. Ninja #1 stays put, clutching the extra by his wrist. The extra turns around, looking at Ninja #1. He, in turn, spins behind the extra and slits his throat. The lifeless body falls to the ground and Ninja #1 kicks it to edge of the water and runs to catch up to his clan.


And the head of the Water Dragon slowly creeps up from the depths of the moat...

Up ahead, the Ninjas, caught up in the moment, begin a battle cry.

Ninjas:

'YAAAAAAAAHHHHHHH!!!'

Ninja #1:

'SHH! Quiet, morons!'

Rushing with great speed and stealth--

Little Boy:

'Hey look, Mom! Ninjas!'

Ninja #1:

'Make a note, the boy knows too much...'

Rushing with relatively great speed and stealth, the Ninjas reach the bridge. Using their spike-palms, they scale underneath the bottom of the bridge and slowly make their way across, the Water Dragon waiting for one of them to fall...


On the other side, Ninja #1 meets them.

Ninja #12:

'How did you get here ahead of us?'

Ninja #1:

'I hitchedhiked.'

Ninja #20:

'That works?'

With that, Ninja #1 holds up his bloodstained sword.

Ninja #1:

'Who's going to mess with me?'

Ninja #8:

'He's got a point.'

From this point, it's only another 10 minutes' walk to the castle...

And so the ninjas stand in front of the castle's high wall. Ninja #1 looks back at his students. They all take their three-pronged grappling hooks off their belts and attached them to the ropes they have tied around their waists. And with speed and precision they throw their hooks up over the wall to grapple onto something. They don't know what it is, but the grip is firm and they start climbing.


Ninja #6:

'Oh, how to tell her I love her...'

Ninja #8:

'What the hell is that!'

Ninja #12:

'Nothing, Number Six is just is just singing Girl from Epanema.'

Ninja #31:

'Well, tell him to stop!'

Ninja #20:

'Would you rather he sung the theme from Lavern and Shirley?'

Ninja #8:

'DEAR GOD NO!'

Ninja #1, climbing ahead of the group, looks back at the others.

Ninja #1:

'SHH!'

The others quiet down, but in a very short amount of time Ninja #6 is singing again, much to the annoyance of Ninja #8, who quietly begins to draw his sword while staying in place on the wall...


Ninja #6:

'Tall and thin and tanned and lovely, the Girl from Epanema goes walking; and when she passes everyone she passes goes--'


*SWIPE!*

Ninja #6:

'AAAAAAAAAHHHHH!'

And down he falls the forty feet thet had climbed thus far...

*BOOMPF!*

Putting his sword away, Ninja #8 looks back up at his disappointed compatriots.

Ninja #8:

'What? He was getting annoying...'

Ninja #1:

'Compared to you, I think I'd manage a stealthier intrusion with a marching band!'

Sans Ninja #6, they climb to the top of the wall. Crouching perched at the top of the narrow wall, Ninja #1 looks down into the area and sees a large female in a cage.


Ninja #1:

'Confirm hostage?'

The other ninjas look down and see a sign that says 'Bertha,' so they agree with him. They all drop down from the wall and run past the various other cages, the captives all asleep paying no mind to them.


Ninja #20:

'How many people does he have held here?'

Ninja #31:

'Should we free them all?'

Ninja #1:

'No, we're only rescuing the extremely fat female...'

Reaching the cage, Ninja #12 removes his lockpick from underneath his right sleeve cuff and skillfully opens the Masterlock keeping her in the cage. She stirs


Ninja #8:

'Don't worry, Bertha. Syko sent us to free you.'

Bertha groans. The ninjas quiet her and lift her up to carry her out.

Ninja #12:

'DAMN SHE'S HEAVY!'

Ninja #1:

'You're all just weak.'

Ninja #20:

'Shut up, it's not like weight training is part of the training regimen. We're ninjas, not Conan!'


Though with much difficultly, the ninjas manage to lift Bertha and carry her out of the castle without being detected...


Back at the house.

Sensei:

'YOU KIDNAPPED A HIPPO?!'

Ninja #20:

'It's female and fat and named Bertha...'

Ninja #8:

'It was dark, what do you want from us?'

Sensei:

'I want you to pay the settlement for pending lawsuit from The Renaissance Zoo, that's what!'


Syko takes the hippo and begins to leave.

Sensei:

'Hey, that's... not your Bertha.'

Syko sighs and looks at the hippo as he guides it away by a leash.

Syko:

'I know... but she'll have to do...'
