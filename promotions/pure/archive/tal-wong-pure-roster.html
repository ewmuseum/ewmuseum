Real Name: Tal Wong Birthdate: February 18, 1975 Hometown: Salt Lake City, Utah Height: 6'1'' Weight: 225 lbs. Nicknames: 'The Jade Scorpion' Status: Neutral Manager(s): None Current Teams: None Yet Entrance Music: 'End of Days' by Fozzy Wrestling Style: Martial Arts/Technical, has a variety of different kicks which he likes to usein one shot blows or in combinations, also has a nice arsenal of submission moves he learned in his UFC days and is not afraid to take to the air if he has to.
Signature Moves:

Striking Moves

Asian Knee Lift: Tal does this move when he is whipped off the ropes and his opponent bends over for a back body drop. Instead of kicking him in the face Tal get's his knee under and jumps up, hitting them in the face.

Flying Dragon Kick: Tal is perched up on the top rope as he waits for his dazed opponent to get up and face him. Tal then jumps off the top rope and hits a roundhouse kick to the temple of his opponent.

Leg Lariat: Used when reversing an opponent's whip and whipping them instead only to be clotheslined by his extended leg

Running Head Kick: Opponent is sitting in the middle of the ring and Tal bounces off the ropes and runs towradshis opponent, kicking him in the face and laying him out cold.

Osaka Elbow Drive: Tal is sitting on the top rope and he rgabs his opponent by the hair and pulls them up so that they are facing out from him and their head is just below his pecs. While holding their hair with one hand Talraises his right elbow and drives it hard on to his opponent's forehead and then drops them to the mat.

Atomic Throat Thrust: Usually done while opponent is dazed. Tal crouches down in a martial arts stance and waits for his opponent to turn around and when he does Tal thursts forward with a very quick and powerful throat thrust that usually knocks people off of their feet.

Varietys of Kicks and Punches: Superkick, Pump Kick, Heel Kick, Throat Thrust, Yakua Kick, Double Chop, Knife Edge Chop, Dropkick, Knee Strikes, Elbow Smash, Back Heel Kick, Scissors Kick, Wheel Kick, Jumping Roundhouse, Spinning Backhand, Right Hook, Double Palm Strike, ETC.

Submissions

Year of Pain: Elevated single leg crab.

Art of Choke: Figure Four leglock sleeper hold around neck.

Deadly Arts: Tal is sitting on the top turnbuckle while hooking an armbar on to his opponent who is dangling perpendicular to him.(RARE)

Yakuza Lock: Cobra clutch with leg scissors.

Other Submissions: Armbar, Sitting Chinlock, Boston Crab, Mexican Surfboard, Bow and Arrow Lock, Guillotine Choke, Camel Clutch, Buffalo Sleeper Hold, Reverse STF, Standing Ankle Lock.

Grappling Moves

Eagle Driver: Tal picks opponent up in vertical suplex and then slams them down face first in a reverse falcon arrow driver.

Setting of the Sun: Twisting Reverse DDT into 3/4 turn bulldog.

Sting of the Scorpion: Very stiff looking spike piledriver.(2nd Finisher)

Catapult of Despair: Opponent is laying on back with Tal at feet. Tal hooks both of opponen's legs and puts his legs underneath his opponents legs then falls backwards. At that time he catapults his opponent off the ground and while still holding on he slams them face first into the mat.

Other Moves: Tornado DDT, Hurracanrana, Face Buster, DDT, Spinning Neckbreaker, Dragon Screw, Snap Suplex, Belly to Belly Suplex, German Suplex Pin, Victory Roll, Abdominal Stretch Pin, Bulldog, Running Flipping Neckbreaker(while opponent is sitting), ETC.

Aerial Moves

Asian Splash: An inverted forg splash only this time instead of connecting with the body on the opponent's hest Tal does an Austin elbow on to him.

Other Aerial Moves: Moonsault, Body Splash, Missile Dropkick, Flying Clothesline, Guillotine Legdrop, Cross Body, Corckscrew, Somersault Plancha, ETC.
