Bio

Orlando Scott Bio

************************************************

Make this a rule, before you have a child, you must have the proper finances before delivering a child into the world. On April 15th/1978, A young couple that went by the names of Stephen Scott and Abby Moore. It was a sea section surgery, which lasted only thirteen-minutes. They named the young boy Orlando, after Abby’s father. In about three days young Orlando and Abby were released from St. Mary’s hospital in New York. Stephen Scott, the father or Orlando had no attentions on accepting the duties of becoming a father as a teenager, so he parted from Abby. Abby was a strong woman, never complained about anything. Abby and Orlando began their lives at the home of Abby’s parents. A small semi home, which was located in a rough area of New York. Abby kept young Orlando isolated from the outdoors, due to the rough streets of Harlem.


Orlando showed signs of intelligence young, he was crawling, walking and talking above the expected age. When Orlando was two, he was able to write his own name, which was rare at that age. He became anti-social to the other children on the schoolyard, which came from his father’s side of the family. Abby also never allowed Orlando to associate with any of the neighborhood kids, hoping he will not pick up any bad habits along the way.


Orlando drifted through school with ease. Never being top of his class, but never being the lowest student. It was not until high school he broke out of his anti-social shell. Orlando was getting a lot of attention from the opposite sex. Orlando based most of his youth years, lifting weights in his grandfather’s weight room, located in the basement of the small home. After several years of working out, his hard work finally had begun to pay off. Orlando’s body was above average, having a great muscular build to show for his hard work. He did not play many sports, but he was always interested in wrestling, which is why he joined the school team. Orlando excelled in wrestling with ease, becoming well known around the school boards, under his weight class. He graduated at the age of eighteen, and decided to not proceed with his education, and stay in town with his girlfriend, and join the navy.


Life was finally beginning to shift for the young Orlando. He now has his own apartment with his new fiancée, Holly. Both at the age of twenty, they had already achieved their life long goals. Holly was already a nurse, and Orlando was a Navy Seal. Orlando was now a weapon, a weapon not to be used unless in a battle formation. He was living life to his fullest, Holly and Orlando were to be wed the following summer.


They were joined in holy matrimony on July 21st/1998. They had a child on the way, and their financial situations were doing great, so they decided to move into a small home. A great home to start a young family. The child was born seven months after the wedding. It was a small baby boy, Sebastian. Just when Orlando thought life was perfect, the unexpected occurred. Holly was gunned down at a shooting raid, which was between some bikers. This was above all the worst time for this passing. Young Sebastian was only at the age of two. Orlando and his mother Abby were parting every day, more and more. Orlando and his son Sebastian moved back into a small apartment. Finances were beginning to shift in the opposite direction. Work was fading.


Years passed, with a low budget. The two were able to get by, Orlando expressing all the love he can into his son. The two were very close, Sebastian now hitting the age of six. On that same year, Orlando let his actions get the best of him one night, which costs him his job in the Navy. At a nearby bar, he was surrounded by a pile of drunks, and without thinking, engaged a powerful blow to the opponents neck, making the man a vegetable for his remaining lifetime. Orlando was let off reasonably easy, just the loss of his job, and a short time in prison.


Orlando now at the age of twenty-four. Orlando and his son Sebastian lived in a small trailer located at the Cedar Ridge Trailer Park. Orlando called it a temporary substitution for the two. But this substitution will be permanent, as Orlando had no attempt of getting a job. Orlando was now only good at one thing, using his body as a weapon, which he believed that time had passed. Although he did not realize there were alternative options to having a skill such as his. Which was brought to his attention by his son, by giving his father a flyer that he received off the Internet from school. He had watched his father at work, and he knew he would become a great professional wrestler, if given the effort. Sebastian knew his father had the physical ability for the game, but the mental ability was still to come.


Another year passed, with the PWA flyer still resting on the coffee stand. At this time, Orlando was tired of unemployment. He wanted a better life for himself and betters yet his son. He had finally applied to the academy, which was granted and he was officially a rookie in professional wrestling. Orlando was still in his muscular build, just needed to rust of the basics. He had prepared for this for a few months, now he has notified the academy he is ready for battle, and only time will tell if this young talent has what it takes.
