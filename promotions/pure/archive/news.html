PURE Card Archive

News

23 April – 17 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hirings and Firings 6/17/02 The following people have been released from the roster because of rather lack luster workrates: Bloody Tiger Yuka Hamada Cege Heyabusa Carl Kimbell Brian Simon Takumi Umeshita And the following three have been hired to PURE: Poster Boy Anthony Balboa Tal Wong. Kintei not Filed 6/17/02 In a story almost as bizarre as Ali Khadafi's kidnapping of The Reverend Jesse Kintei, a friend of Jordan X Shabazz. Jordan X Shabazz and his corporation, N.I.G.G.A (Whom Jesse Kintei now works for), have decieded not to file a missing persons report for Jesse. When asked why he is doing this, Shabazz had this to say "I'm going to have Ali pay by my justice, the only real justice in this world, BLACK JUSTICE!" Shabazz was noticably shaken up and angry. More as it comes to us. Jesse Kintel Update 5/22/02 Following the brutal beating that the Reverend Jesse Kintei suffered at the hands of Ali Khadafi at Ikusa III, he has been placed in a Japanese hospital where he is expected to spend the next month. Several facial injuries might require painful plastic surgery, but doctors were optismistic. "The injuries are unfortunate. But I'm sure the Reverend will make a full recovery." said one doctor. Jordan X Shabazz couldn't be reached for comment. Final Destinies Update 5/21/02 Final Destinies will now be replacing Ikusa VII, not Ikusa VIII as it was found to be conflicting with other scheduling matters. The preliminary card has gone up, but it is definatly preliminary as it only has two matches, and one of them doesn't have any participants named yet. Post Ikusa III Hirings and Firings 5/21/02 As seen and heard on Ikusa III, the following people have had their contracts terminated: Masanabu Akazi, Shirata Rinjiro, Steven Windsor. In addition, Team Okiki have asked to be released. Kensuke Suzahara is undergoing preliminary tests at the moment and it is unsure of when he will be able to return to PURE. Charlie Loc is making slow but steady recovery and we expect to see him back in the ring at some point soon. From the XWA, the Chinese Black Dragons have been hired who are an enigmatic four man Tag Team. Also hired was a free agent known simply as "Bushido". PPV Annouced "Future Destinies" 5/21/02 As annouced on Ikusa III, replacing Ikusa VIII will be PURE's first ever Pay Per View, called "Future Destinies". Promised on his card will be the crowning of the first ever PURE Heavyweight Champion along with title defenses of all tht titles. It has also been indicated that the first ever Asian Champion will be annouced then as well. Future Destinies will be held at none other than the prestigious Tokyo Dome in Tokyo. Matches will be annouced during the course of the next four shows. Kintel To Attend Ikusa III 5/13/02 It has been confirmed that current NAACP Vice-President, one Reverend Jessie Kintei, will be making his way to the next Ikusa card. Apparently Kintei is a big fan of minorities in sports and sports entertainment, citing his zealous following of Jordan X Shabazz as one of the reasons he is to be attending the card. "I want a chance to speak to brother Shabazz. His work here in PURE and the PCW has given heart to many a struggling negro, and I want to extend a warm hand shake from the higher ups in the NAACP." Kintei is scheduled to make a guest appearance, rumored to be off air, at the beginning of the Ikusa card. Signings Galore! 5/06/02 The following people have been signed to PURE contacts: Jordan X Shabazz (from PCW) Yuku Hamada (free agent) Bloody Tiger (free agent) Cianan (from CCW) WElcome all four as they join us here in PURE. Takumi Umeshita Update 5/02/02 Umeshita has now been declared fit once again by doctors and he will be appearing on the Ikusa III card in a first round match of the Asian Title tournament against opponents that are yet to be named. Firings... 5/02/02 PURE CEO and Owner, Alexander Miyagawa sent us this memo earlier. -------- Friends... Romans... Japanese Men... It has come to my attention that four people are on the PURE roster who are not pulling their socks so to say. Therefore because there are more eager people waiting to get into PURE to make a difference I have no choice but to let these four go with the best of wishes in future endevours. Tiger Akumu Billy Larkin Hiro Saotome Dark Lotus OOC: You all no-showed for a month. If your not willing to put anything into PURE, then I won't waste my matchwriters time trying to put you over in matches. Alexander Miyagawa. -------- Ikusa II Update 5/02/02 Because of problems with last week's telecast of PURE Wrestling's Ikusa II show, it will now be shown this Sunday at 8 Eastern. However a preliminary card for Ikusa III will be going up before then so that people may begin to film their promo material for it. We do not expect a recurrence of this situation happening again as it was due to technical faults that were out of our hands. Loc Injury Report 4/23/02 The second wrestler to recieve an injury in PURE's history under-went surgery little less than a few hours ago! PURE wasn't even informed that Charlie not only recieved a concussion but also took a hard fall and cut a tendon whilst a piece of bone in his right arm fractured! Doctors in the Osaka hospital say Charlie is doing fine and should be left alone for re-coperation but will be cleared to wrestle only after extensive phsyiotherapy! His return is expected to be for Ikusa IV or V depending on how soon he manages to heal.....
