Bio

Damian was born on January 22, 1982 in a hospital in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania. His birthweight was 7 pounds 11 ounces, his IQ 199, 39 points above genius level. His parents were poor. They both worked 9 to 9 every day to support their three children, of which Damian was the youngest. Despite the financial problems Damian's early life was relatively happy. He spent most of his time with his grandparents and siblings: Sister Monica, and Brother Shawn.


But as time passed, Damian's happiness began to deteriate. His grandfather Jim died of cancer just before Damian's seventh birthday. The pain was horrible for young Damian but even worse for his father. Damian's father became so depressed that he began to just stop working and was soon fired. Alcoholism quickly followed. Damian's father began taking his anger in frustration out on the young child. His grandmother was slowly dying and he rest of his family did nothing to protect Damian. For the first time in his life, Damian was alone.


Damian's life slowly went by. And by age eleven he was contemplating suicide. He felt he had no reason to live and that no one who loved him. But one night he found an alternative. He crushed his family's skulls one by one with a hammer. The same hammer he carries around in his trench coat pocket today. He inhaled the sweet, sweet scent of his family's blood and he knew his life was changed forever. He knew his true calling. He was a serial murderer. Cold and heartless. 'Soulless.' Before leaving the house he cut a deep, deep gash on his left hand. He whipped his blood all over the small house. He faked his own death, sorta. He outsmarted everyone, sorta. But still, he had nowhere to go. But would homelessness really be that bad? Damian brought along a blanket and pillow. He stole every meal he eat. A couple of years went by and Damian spent his time educating himself.


Around his 14th birthday Damian began to embrace the Neo-Goth subculture. He began robbing people to pay his way into concerts. Around his 15th birthday Damian got a job at a liquor store. He was paid with food and allowed to sleep in the back of the store during the night. But still, he robbed cars and people for cash. His social skills were near nonexistent. He spoke softly and usually just avoided people. But at night, Damian morphed back into his true self. The hammer wielding psycho killer. He created a method, just not enough of one to allow the police to string his murders together. Damian will come across someone in the street. Anyone really. He has no preference. He will follow them home, keeping himself out of view. After he knows where they live he will study them for a weak or two. Than the stalking begins. And finally the murder. Damian soon mastered his trade.


Damian's life pretty much consisted of work, school, and murder, not necessarily in that order. Damian got good grades but kept to himself in school. Everyone pretty much left him alone, and those who didn't mysteriously disappeared... Vanished. Never to be seen again. Of course, no one suspected Damian of doing any wrong. The odd, quiet, little Goth kid was incredibly careful about disposing of the bodies. Heh. I remember the first 'bully' Damian killed. You see, Damian was just sitting around, minding his business, listening to some music. Don't remember who, when this kid came up and kicked him in the back of the head.. Damian followed him as he walked home from his friend's house around 11:25 PM. Damian pulled the fuck into an alley. Bashed his skull in with his trusty hammer. He cremated his body.


Sometime around his junior year Damian met a girl. No not a girl. A goddess. This green-haired woman came to Damian. And soon, the two fell in love. But neither knew each other’s dark little secret... Of course, Damian is a serial killer. We know that. But this girl, Samantha was 'different' as well. She was a cannibal. Damian found out soon enough and the two became partners in crime, so to speak. Damian murdering, and Samantha feasting upon the carcass. Sure, sometimes Samantha assists Damian with his murders but usually just allows Damian to do what he does best, alone.


But Damian needed money. He was about 17 and was growing tired of working at the same damn liquor store. Even if he could steal all the alcohol and smokes he wanted. He quit and went to live with Samantha. It sucked though. She had four roommates. A bunch of stupid whores. Damian wanted to kill them but Samantha strictly forbade it. So Damian needed his own place. He searched for a job, but found none. But one day Damian learned of an underground fighting affiliation. He went into training and in about six months had his first match. He got his face kicked in by some 322-pound behemoth named 'Mug.' But still, Damian got 200 dollars for the match. He had another match and won. Then he got 500 dollars. He found a cheap ass apartment and moved in, bringing Samantha with him. He fought four times a month and made enough money to support himself. After a while, fighting became somewhat stupid. But he still liked the thought of hurting people for money. So he became a wrestler. Even though he had never even seen a wrestling broadcast. He joined PWA and within two weeks was promoted to NTICW. But soon NTICW was closed. So onto CHZ. They closed as well.

Manager: Samantha

Full Name: Samantha Sarah Jezebel

Birthdate: January 22, 1982

Hometown: Philadelphia, Pennsylvania

Height: 5'8'

Weight: 122 lbs.

Entrance Music: 'Land of Rape and Honey' by Ministry

Managing/Cheating Tactics: Can be quite aggressive. Will bite an opponent if she sees any sort of opportunity. Holds Damian's leather trench coat which of course contains his hammer. Will hand Damian's hammer to him if requested. Samantha is not at all afraid to enter the ring in competition. Damian has taught her a small amount of wrestling. She usually sticks to a 'Hit and Run' style. Oh yeah, she has mastered the corkscrew as well.


Moves:

Kiss Of Death (Samantha climbs to the top rope above a fallen target. She leaps high into the air doing a 450 Degree Flip with a 360 Degree Corkscrew landing in a leg drop)


Stigmata (Samantha bounces of the ropes and performs a running 360 Spin Directly into a spear on the standing target)


War (Samantha slides between the targets legs and grabs a hold of their nuts. Only works on men.)


Sodomy (Samantha springs to the top rope and corkscrews onto a target standing in the crowd)


(Signature Moves)

Fall From Grace (The opponent is laying on the mat. -It does not really matter whether they are on their back or not but usually will be- Damian will climb to the top rope and leap into the air doing a shooting star press. Once the SSP is completed Damian will quickly do a frog splash.)


Death From Below (Usually used as a counter. Damian will be lying on his back. The victim will be standing and facing Damian. Damian will tilt backwards so that he is on his shoulders with his legs in the air. He will spring into the air, wrapping his legs around the victim's neck as if going for a hurricanrana, spin so that he is now facing the same direction as the victim and fall backwards slamming the victim onto the top of their head.)


The Prayer (Damian will be facing the victim. He will run to the ropes which are behind the victim, perform a second rope springboard moonsault and land a DDT onto the victim.)


The Crucifixion (Damian's victim will be laying face down on the mat. Damian will hook his opponent's right leg with his left arm, then turn over into a single leg Boston crab, rotate into a reverse leg lock -like an S.T.F.-, pull his victim's arms back, and raise them off the mat as if they are hung on a cross.)


Alter of Sacrifice (Damian will lock the victim's arms between the top and bottom ropes so that they are trapped and facing the crowd. Damian will vault to the top rope and perform a rocker dropper which wrenches their neck and shoulders.)


Decadence (Damian climbs to the top rope while the victim is laying back down on the mat. Damian will perform a moonsault with a 360-degree corkscrew so that he lands stomach first onto the victim.)


Despair (Damian lifts the victim into a crucifix powerbomb. Damian slings them forward and catches them with a DDT.)


Dream to Nightmare (Damian climbs to some elevated surface and performs a 720 degree corkscrew into an inverted bulldog.)


Hammer Time (Damian will be handed his hammer by Samantha. He will hook the victim in a sleeper while he places the hammer under their nose. He will lean himself backwards and the victim forwards before thrusting forward with all his might, shattering the victim's nose.)


Demise (Damian will lift the victim in a fireman's carry. He will then release their arms and swing the around so that it looks as if he is going to land a diamond cutter but instead whips them forward into a fall forward inverted DDT. Damian will then stand, not releasing the victim's head and flip forward into a neck wrench.)


Principle of Evil (Damian will pull the victim to the top rope while he is up there as well. He will lock their head under his arm and perform a 360-Degree Tornado DDT landing on the floor outside the ring.)


Hand Made Flesh (Simple yet deadly. Damian will wrap his hand around the victim's face and pull his fingers down slowly ripping and tearing the flesh as he goes.)


Dusk Arrives (Damian will wrap his arms around the victim's throat before spinning around and slamming them to the mat with full force.)


The Black Goddess Rises (Damian will unleash a series of kicks and punches which push the victim to the turnbuckle. Once they reach it Samantha -whom is standing on the apron waiting- will begin to gnaw on their neck.)


Hell Awaits (Damian will lift the victim onto his shoulders in a fireman's carry. He will position himself on one side of the ring before running towards the other. He will leap over the top rope, and flip forward landing the victim in a samoan drop. Usually threw a table.)


The Tortured End (Damian locks in a full nelson. He sits himself and the victim down before locking on a leg scissors and pushing forward with all his might.)


Unholy Ground (Damian throws the victim from the ring. He climbs to the top rope and leaps into the air performing a 900 cross body or splash suicida.)


Morning Star (Damian throws the victim from the ring before performing a springboard shooting star suicida.)


God Can't Save You Now (Damian runs at the standing opponent, does a forward roll, and leaps at them with a fall forward lariat.


Insomnia (Damian climbs to the top rope and performs a corkscrew body press.)

Mark of the Beast (Damian slings the victim stomach down over his shoulder with their head facing the same direction as him. He bounces off the ropes, and releases them dropping in a diamond cutter.)


Tourniquet (Damian's opponent is lying on their stomach. Damian straddles their back, facing their head. He wraps his legs around their throat and pulls their legs over his head.)


The Labyrinth (Damian places the victim in a standing head scissors. He hooks both arms as if going for a pedigree type maneuver. He flips them into the air so that they are upside down, and drops to his knees, driving them head first into the mat.)


The Abyss (Damian sets up a superplex. He jumps, turning the victim in mid air and drops them in a stunner.)
