PURE World: Hong Kong, China

Hong Kong holds its reputation as one of China’s richest cities in culture, business, and government. Hong Kong is a diverse state of China, cutting through the Asian boundaries and adapting to all type of life. One July 1st 1997, Hong Kong celebrated its independence. People were dancing in the streets and the party went on for days. The entire history of Hong Kong was changed just four years ago. Before the independence and before the growth in economy, Hong Kong was a small land with a fighting spirit. Producing hundreds of great wrestlers through the years, Hong Kong has deemed itself one of the most exotic lands for fighting in the entire world.

Today, PURE takes on the mainland and sees if it could live up to Hong Kong’s impressive history. With four action packed matches lined up and the announcing of the “Battle of the Nations” tournament, PURE may just blow the roof off. May the Purification begin!

A day after St. Patrick’s Day and a shamrock is being twirled in a PURE: World locker room. The camera zooms out, showing the twirling hands are that of PURE’s first ever European employee, Jason Marah. Marah is staring at the three-leaved plant going on a roundabout.

“Good ol’ Saint Paddy banished all the snakes from Ireland many moons ago. But me? Nah…the snakes of PURE: World are pinning me as they please” he says softly as if talking to the shamrock.

“Two consecutive losses to Cordozer “Suicidal” Stevenson and then a loss against PURE World champion Jesse Ramey in a match I could have easily won. So, I’m 3-1 in singles action in favour of me ending the night staring up at the lights. That one win? That win was against some 16 year old fly-boy in lets just say, PURE’s darker days. Not a great flippin’ record, eh? Suppose, the 1-1 record in cluster matches is some sort of conciliation…suppose…”

Jason’s voice slowly begins to get deeper.

“Actually, now that I think about it, it’s no sort of conciliation when SOME PUNK WANNA-BE BOXER GOING BY THE NAME OF JACKSON PALS IS BLOODY RAISING HIS NOSE TO YOU!”

“Tonight, I team with “S2” to take on Senji Yulav and Kohana and every arrow points towards Stevenson acting like a pillock and costing me another loss. You know what…if he does act an arse, there shall be hell to pay. The Bushido Code Of Honour will fly straight out the f---ing window…”

Jason’s voice becomes coarse and he crushes the feeble plant in his hands while raising his head up to the camera, seeing the seriousness and intent in his eyes “…and my foot will fly straight into S2’s face…”

“Then there’s Jesse flamin’ Ramey, our champion…our controversial champion. Jesse, you must know in your heart of hearts, once I’m finished with the louse of S2, that title will make acquaintance with my waist. You’ve got my full respect, Jesse, but you know I can beat you…and I will. I’ve been too damn nice for too damn long…the rebellion has commenced…”

The scene fades out.

Loss Of Restraint

One of the PURE: World locker rooms burst open as “The Irish Warrior” Jason Marah comes belting through with an obvious passion making a B-Line for Cordozer Stevenson. Jason immediately takes S2’s attention as S2 turns to face Marah leaping at him with his fist just waiting to launch an attack.

As Jason collides with Stevenson, Marah’s weight makes Cordozer fall against an adjacent wall. Marah begins pummelling in Cordozer mercilessly with a constant full force right punches to the jaw.

“You bloody bastard Cordozer” he screams as he continues to lay into him, “I fecking warned you, I warned you what would happen if I lost” he continues.

Eventually five security guards attempt to restrain Marah. Marah doesn’t make an easy task as he refuses to hold back by breaking free of the security guards grasps and throwing wild punches at S2 who has raised his back off the ground and is prodding at his jaw, to try and feel what damage has been done.

“You are bloody done, DONE!”

Patrick Craig, Jason’s religious guide, manager of sorts among other things, calmly walks into the locker room and tries to calm Jason and restrain him by talking and placing his hands and Jason’s biceps.

“Calm yourself Marah, calm. Let’s just go, you’re not doing yourself any favours beating up Cordozer. If you’re going to do it, do it properly, in the ring.”

Jason nods, “Aye, you’re right. See you next week Stevenson.” Jason smirks and leaves the room followed by Craig.

S2 gives a sneer and pushed EMT’s away from him, proclaiming he’s “All right”.

Battle of the Nations Press Conference

The scene slowly draws into a jam-packed conference room with a crowd of reporters and cameramen. The scene is quite noisy and hectic, until a man in a red striped tie and black suit with a gray lace took the microphone and began.

“Hello and welcome to the official PURE: World Battle of the Nations press conference. I would like to introduce Jamar Wilson.”

The group of public news people finally calm down as Jamar Wilson comes to the microphone receiving a faint clapping from a select few.

“Hello, as most of you know I am the one and only Jamar Wilson. I’ll stop the chatting and get right down to business with any questions you may have about the tournament.”

Jamar points over to a man in glasses standing in the back right corner of the room. The man from “People’s Daily” in China begins speaking as Jamar’s translator translates.

“Mr. Wilson could you give us all a brief description of how a wrestler will go about picking a country?”

“Yes, well a wrestler will most likely look for a country that will welcome them with open arms for representing them. Most people will probably go with the country of their residence, but to make the tournament diverse we opened it to anywhere. If wrestlers choose other places will be another story. Once a wrestler picks a country they’ll be entered into a drawing, which will begin next week, where a certain country, with the country of their applying getting a first bid at them, will then choose every wrestler.”

Jamar Wilson then goes over and points to a woman wearing a red dress that could be noticed from a mile away.

“What type of events will the wrestler’s go through once they’ve passed the first stage of qualification?” Says the woman from the South China Morning Star.

“The events will be varied. Some events will have to do with strength, some will do with the mind, and others will do with athleticism. We can’t at this time give away any of the events that will go on, but they will surely please.”

Jamar continues as he points to a rather exuberant young reporter in the middle of the crowd.

“Who do you as owner think will give a strong showing?” The man from the Zhongguo Daily News asks.

“I think everyone will work to their full capacity. I see men like Jason Marah, Kohana, and Jackson Palms Jr. doing well through their size, while men like Senji Yulav, Julio Dervante, and Ken Misawa turning some heads with their athleticism.”

Jamar points to an older reporter patiently waiting in the back before saying “Last question”.

“What do you wish to obtain from this tournament?” asks the older man from CCCNews.

“My goal is quite simple. I want to expand the horizon in PURE. We’ve been secluded to one area for a while, but now PURE: World is breaking out of that area. This is the perfect way to break out.”

The older man nods as Jamar Wilson picks up his papers and says his final words.

“Before I leave today I’d like to leave a final announcement. The main event for tonight’s scheduled will not happen just now. To test good ole Jesse Ramey we are going to make this main event an “ANYTIME TONIGHT” Main Event. There’s still three hours in the day, so everyone will keep there eyes open for the match. We’ll see if Ramey can perform in top shape right at the moment.”

The reporters all talk amongst themselves about the news.

“Thanks to everyone who has come today, PURE: World will be sure to please all in the time to come, and Battle of the Nations is just the beginning.”

The scene fades back to the arena, as the crowd is pounding and cheering, as the main event is about to proceed.
