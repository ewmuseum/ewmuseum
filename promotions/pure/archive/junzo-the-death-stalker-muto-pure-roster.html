Bio

In 1974 Muto's father moved to America, more specifically to the Bronx, with his wife, in search of a better life. He quickly established hismelf a small market shop in one of the roughest area's of the city.


Then in 1978 his wife gave birth to a boy, they named him Junzo Muto. They cared for him a great deal and taught him Japanese cultures so that the boy wouldn't be fully corrupted by American society. He learned all of the religious aspects of his native land, the wisdom of his native land, and most importantly the very delicate art of fighting. He was tauight various different varieties of the Martial Arts, which he found constant use for as a boy growing up in the Bronx.


Then in 1988 when Junzo was 10 years of age, his mother and father were killed. He was told that muggers probably did it, but the police had no idea, they found the lifeless bodies of his parents lying in the back alley's. So he was now alone in world.


Junzo couldn't quite remember how the Crimelord Dane Lochlan found him, but his was a relief of sorts. Dane brought him up from 10 onwards, encouraging Junzo to follow the learning of the Martial Arts, whilst secretly training him for another use.


Junzo knew over seven different types of Martial Arts and at the age of 18 Dane announced his plans for the boy. Dne wanted him to be the organisations leading hitman, something to make all of the other gangs and similar organisations could be afraid. Junzo was reluctant to take teh role initially, but felt that he owed Dane for finding him all of those years ago nd raising him to be his own.


And so his path was set for ever more, until one of Junzo's targets was an independent wrestler working the local scene. Junzo melded into the company, under the guise of a new trainee and began to train to be a wrestler, secretly undercover, waiting for the right moment to strike his opponent down. Whilst training, he found a great admiration for the sport and athletism. Junzo still took care of his target, but secretly continued to train, not to Dane's knowledge. When he was fully trained he wrestled under the assumed name of Junzo Muto and wore a mask. And so the story continues...
