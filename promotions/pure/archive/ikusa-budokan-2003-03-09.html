The cameras swing about the jam-packed Budokan Hall in Tokyo Japan as fireworks blast off at the staging, the fans erupting as lights hoot everywhere. Shortly, the lights illuminate to full as the cameras head straight to the ring, Hanza Kibagame already in the ring as he looks set to intro the first match

Hitomi: Ladies and gentlemen it is time to once again start another awesome night full of PURE events!

MAH: I could totally agree with you right now Hitomi! This is the first Ikusa since PURE’s first ever Pay-Per-View in Final Destinies and we’ve FINALLY got a Heavyweight Champion!

Hitomi: Yes we do and that man is none other than Jesse Ramey.

Mori: As much as I must say that the match put on by Jesse Ramey and Eddie Mandrake was totally awesome, I can’t say I’m glad to see the man as the champion.

Hitomi: Of course, I wouldn’t expect someone like YOU to like the fact that we have such a great champion.

Mori: I would have rather seen someone like Enmity or Steven Lee win the title.

Hitomi: Yes, you would have liked to have seen someone like that win the title, but then again, both of them were put out in their first matches.

Mori: True, but they were cheated.

Hitomi: (Shouting) How do you get that they were cheated?

Mori: Well, Enmity was cheated because he had to compete in a type of death match, I don’t know what it was and Steven Lee was cheated because the ref didn’t like him.

Hitomi: You’ve got to be kidding me!

Mori: Nope, it’s the truth. I heard it from a friend of a friend’s cousin who knows the guy that was the ref of the match.

Hanza: The following contest is set for one fall and there will be a thirty-minute time limit.

“Singular Indestructible Droid” by Papa Roach hits the house PA and the fans come to their feet and begin to cheer as Chase Herrera comes out from the backstage area at full speed, running down to the ring, rolling in under the top rope and beginning to bounce around yelling to the fans.

Hitomi: Herrera sure does know how to get a crowd working.

Hitomi: In traditional culture this crowd would be cheering for something like Hoshi who is from Japan, but my goodness, how can they?

Mori: Because she is like the greatest female wrestler ever!

Hanza: Ladies and gentlemen, introducing first in the red corner (points to Chase) hailing from Los Angeles, California, United States of America and weighing in at two hundred and ten pounds, he is CHASE HERRERA!

A Challenger’s Words-Jason Marah

We go to a room in the back of the building. The room is filled with reporters and at the top of the room we see Jason Marah and Patrick Craig sitting at a long table on an elevated platform. Marah takes a gulp out of the Lucozade bottle sitting in front of him as Craig stands up to organise the press conference.

Patrick Craig: First, I’d like to thank you all for coming. Jason will do his best to answer any question as long as they’re reasonable.

The hands of all those in attendance go up simultaneously. Craig points to a “porky” fellow in the front.

Reporter: Yoshihiro Imata, Highspots.com. Last week at Final Destinies, you lost for the second time straight to Cordozer “Suicidal” Stevenson. It’s hardly the best way to go into a World title match.

Jason Marah: It isn’t? Jay-sus, I think it’s a great way to go into a match. People are starting to think my early matches were flukey and Ramey probably underestimates me. I think it’s all good…I have to go out there, prove what I can do and show S2 that his win was nothing more than a bad night for me. Anyway, I wouldn’t count my “loss” to S2 on Ikusa a couple of weeks back a loss, he did cheat to win, remember?

Yoshiro smiles and nods. The hands go up again.

Patrick Craig: You, in the back with the red hair.

The camera scans back to a red haired woman in a dark grey suit.

Reporter #2: Hirotome Nakato, JAPAN: WRESTLING Press. There’s a draft tonight, a split in the PURE roster. One half of PURE will tour Japan, the other will tour the rest of the world. Where do you hope to go and where do you think you’ll go?

Jason Marah: It’s an intriguing event to say the least. Where do I think I’ll go? I don’t know, I seem to be well responded to here in Japan, but I’ve always got a good response to fans in the US, so that’s a stalemate. Jamar and Mina can bring me to whatever side they want really, but I’m sure as hell I’d do great anywhere. They could slap my arse down in the Antartic and I’d make them damn penguins slap their wings together. Where do I hope to go? Ordinarily, anywhere would do, but now this thing with S2…well, I just hope to go wherever his arse is going so I can kick it.

Hirotome Nakato: Do you think S2 will have any involvement in your World title shot tonight?

Jason Marah: I don’t know, I don’t think so, I don’t think he’d f--- with something this big, ya know? If he does, I’d just have to bring him crashing down to earth with the Rebellion, and then go ahead and do the same to Ramey…and bring that gold strap home.

Hirotome Nakato: We haven’t seen the Rebellion utilized by you in PURE…

Jason Marah: Don’t worry, once you do, you’ll never forget. –smiles- Next question please.

Craig picks out another reporter.

Reporter: John Slone, PUREaddict.net. Rumours are flying about S2 having a drug abuse problem, performance enhancing drugs. Do you know anything about this?

Jason Marah: Not a clue, my friend. S2 keeps his business to himself, and even if I did know, I wouldn’t come out here and tell you folk.

John Slone: There are also rumours that your new nighclub, situated in Sacramento, California, USA came way of Yakuza interactions?

Jason Marah: The Yakuza? The Japanese mafia? Me? What sort of f---head question is that man, ridiculous. To my knowledge, and my knowledge is usually the best, I’ve never had “interactions” with any class of mafia or vigilante group or whatever you want to call them. My nightclub ownership came way of perfectly legitimate, tabletop dealings. Stop with the slander.

Jason stands up and walks out of the room, disgusted and is followed by Patrick Craig as a PURE Representative takes over for them.

The Shaun Styles Security Crew-Shaun Styles

The scene opens up in a dark hallway only illuminated by the lightbulb hanging off by a thread from the ceiling. The walls are covered with breaking tiles and dripping water runs down and breaks on the rough floor. In entirety it appears to be the basement of the chosen arena. A number of security guards are lined up standing straight with their backs against the wall. Shaun Styles appears into the scene and is pacing back and forth nervously in front of the crew. He looks up at them and observes the group.

"You all know why you're here, right?" Shaun Styles asks calmly. To his response a powerful Japanese man steps up and replies.

"We haven't a clue."

"That's alright, Wang." Shaun says and follows up with a giggle thinking about his name breaking the serious mood. His face quickly changes into an icey look again as he walks up and down the line.

"As you all know today I, alongside the Sushi Crew, will be facing the Suburban Ninja Crew. It is unneeded to say that we are at a slight misadvantage. If I count correctly there are only four members between The Sushi Crew and I..." before Shaun can finish his speech he is interupted by the sound of one of the guards clearing their throat.

"Excuse me...Three members...Now that puts us against 40. I'm sure you all understand that even with all my skill and talent I cannot take on 40 men. That is why I have called you all here. The way I see it is if Sushi can have a crew and Suburban Ninjas can have a crew, why can't Shaun Styles?" The speech gets a lot of murmurs from his crowd of around 15 other security guards. They look on in confusion at each other before finally one of them steps up.

"What exactly are you getting at?" The burly gentleman says with a heavy Japanese accent.

"What am I getting at? Gentlemen and more gentlemen, allow me to be the first to induct you into the brand new...Shaun Styles Security Crew!" Shaun yells at the top of his lungs echoing throughout the hollow hall. The men all have mixed reactions on their face before starting to question what is going on.

"Do we get anything out of this? I mean surely a raise would be in order...A prize maybe. Like a car or something." One of the men say.

"Sure, you'll get something. Although it's not something you can spend...Or something you can drive...Or see even. All you can do is feel it." Shaun says putting all his emotion into the answer.

"I could've sworn I heard that exact same line in a movie, Mr. Styles." The same man says unaffected by the touching response.

"...Get out." Shaun Styles grumbles annoyed by the man's antics. The short japenese fellow walks down the hall with his head down and climbs up the stairs leaving.

"Anyone else?" Shaun asks the remaining men. They all salute and stand up straight looking forward once again.

"Good."
