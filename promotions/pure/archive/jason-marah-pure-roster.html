Real Name: Jason Marah Birthdate: 20-12-1978 Hometown: Baltinglass, Co.Wicklow, Ireland Height: 6'6 Weight: 280lbs lbs. Nicknames: Irish Warrior Status: Neutral Manager(s): Patrick Craig Current Teams: Entrance Music: 'Nothing Else Matters' by Metallica, orchestral version Wrestling Style: He's a well rounded wrestler. He implies advanced wrestling skills with a mixture of Martial Arts and high risk manouevres.. Never, ever cheats because it goes against his Bushido Code Of Honour. When infuriated, his fighting mentality switches to pure, brutal street fighting.
Signature Moves:
Standing Moves
Drunken Rage (Spinning back rack into neckbreaker)
D.B.C. Death By Crucifixion (Crucifix bomb/Outsiders Edge)
The Weeper (Impaler/Unprettier)
Shattered Reality (Starts out as a belly to belly suplex, but as Marah is bringing the opponent up, he inverts it in to a sit-down powerbomb.)
Exile The Vile (Starts off as a cradle suplex/perfect-plex, but just before Jason brings him over, he drops them on their back. As they're coming down, Marah kneels down so the bridge of their spine/neck slams off the knee.)
Green Fields (Locks on a frontal full nelson. When bringing the opponent up, Jason, instead of just dropping with a sit-down powerbomb, he elevates them further than usual and slams them down with force utilising a face crusher technique.)
Warrior's Blade (Tornado DDT)

Ground Moves
P.O.P - Pain Of Pride(Sharpshooter)
S.O.A.R. - Screams Of A Rebellion(Crippler Crossface)
Marah Manouevre(Marah puts his opponent in a sharpshooter position, leans back and grabs his opponents head and pulls it forward putting pressure on the neck and back.)

Rope/Turnbuckle Assisted Moves
J-M Crash(Van Terminator with or without weapon)
I.A.R. - Irish Air Raid (Senton Bomb)
Marah Massacre (Moonsault)
Guinness Guillotine (Top rope hurracanrana)
Celtic Moonsault (Asai Moonsault)

Attacking Combination Moves
Shocking Rave (Marah executes an Inverted DDT that impacts onto his knee. He keeps the inverted facelock held on and lifts his opponent and spins 180 for a Swinging Falling Inverted DDT. Keeping his opponent in the inverted facelock, Marah stands to his feet and lifts the opponent up to into the tombstone position, then cradles the leg and body of his opponent to drop into a Tenzan Driver cradle kneeling tombstone.)
Crazy Train (Marah executes a Fisherman Suplex which he chains into a Fisherman Buster that gets chained into a Muscle Buster -- a kneeling Fisherman shoulder-to-shoulder Shoulderbreaker.)
The Code (Marah kicks his opponent in the back of the knee with his right leg, then his stomach with his left leg and then jumps and kicks his opponent with his right leg into the back of the head.>
White Noise (Marah powerbombs his opponent, then lifts the opponent back up and positions him across his shoulders in a fireman carry. Marah tosses the opponent's body back while keeping the throat of the opponent underneath his shoulder. The opponent's throat is crushed in by the variation of a samoan neckbreaker. Marah then rolls back and locks the opponent in a reverse Boston Crab. The opponent screams in pain, but thanks to the damage from the samoan neckbreaker, makes no sound.)
All Alone (Marah grabs his opponent in an upside-down front facelock and executes an elbow smash to then chest/throat of his opponent. The opponent falls, but Marah does not. Marah then does standing 360 leg drop, and when he lands it he moves straight into a Jujigatame cross arm breaker.)
Set Up Move: 1916(He picks his opponent up in a Torture Rack position and then flips them sideways onto the top turnbuckle)
