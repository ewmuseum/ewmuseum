Bio

Luis- Luis Cesar Sangre was a small time musician, just trying to lead a good life. One fateful night, a infamous gang rode into town and started some trouble in the bar Luis was working in. Being the good hearted man he is, Luis rushed in to stop them. What occurred next still haunts the man to this day. The gang brutually mangled Luis's right hand with a sledgehammer and torched his home with his mother still inside. After years of rehibilitation, Luis is out to gain revenge on the men who caused him so much pain and one man in particular, Juan Miguel, who has risen through the ranks during that time to become leader of the gang. With the help of the unusual nun, Sister Ash, Luis searches the world for his target. He wrestles along the way to pay expenses and to give him free passage to many foreign countries.


Ash- The young woman, by the name of Ash, awoke one morning in a monestary in Mexico with her memories gone and her past a mystery. With nowhere else to turn, Ash stayed with the sisters and was taught how to be a woman of god. As Ash stayed with in the nunery, she started to notice a difference between her and the other nuns. She was faster and stronger than everyone there. Her youth brought a headstrong attitude and she was much more extreme in her methods than the others. Believing her sisters to not be giving their all to thweir cause, Ash left the nunery and took off into the world to make it a better place. At a local tavern, she met up with a man, by the name of Luis Sangre. She heard his tragic story and agreed to accompany him on his quest for moral support.
