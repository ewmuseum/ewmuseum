Real Name: Titu Charles Xansiv Birthdate: 4th of April 1975 Hometown: Cairo, Egypt Height: 6, 2' Weight: 229lbs. lbs. Nicknames: The Jewel, The New Hope Status: Neutral Manager(s): Isiy Xansiv Current Teams: None Entrance Music: 'Kashmir' - Led Zeppelin Wrestling Style: Luchador, spiced up with a touch of technical wrestling Signature Moves: The Nile High -Spring board star dust press [With his opponents down on the canvas, Pharaoh runs towards the ropes and using his amazing balance leaps onto the top cable, without a moment too soon he leaps back off and using the elastic in the ropes propels himself through the air in the form of a Star dust press before landing perfectly onto his unsuspecting opponent.]

Wrath of Horus -Insane Dive from a high point in the arena [Pharaoh, obviously feeling suicidal, drags his opponent somewhere in the arena that he feels is a suitable place to position them in preparation for this crazy move. After he’s found his place he looks for a place to climb and before long he finds a height he feels fit to jump from and so without further delay Pharaoh dives from the high point landing his opponent with a suicidal splash.]

Treasure of a Pharaoh -Twisting, spinning, springboard plancha [Pharaoh, like the Nile High, runs at the ropes and spring boards off, this time his opponent is standing but they are completely knocked off their feet as Pharaoh spins and Twists his way through the air towards them and finally lands them with a plancha.]

Lotus Spring -Spear driving opponent out of the ring [Pharaoh lunges forward at his opponent and, driving his shoulder into their ribs, continues to compel them backwards in a spear-like fashion until finally both the Pharaoh and his opponent fall from the ring through the ropes landing on the harsh ground below.]

Valley of the Kings -Death Valley driver spun into a reverse falling DDT [Pharaoh picks his opponent up in a basic fireman’s carry position and then using his strength and balance spins his unlucky opponent into a falling reverse DDT landing them on the back of their head with great force.]

Ancient Belief -Hanging Tiger bomb [Pharaoh sets his foe up in the position of a Powerbomb, he brings them up so as it would seem that he were performing an ordinary Powerbomb but as he brings them back down to the mat he stalls so that the victim is hanging in mid arm only by his arms strength. Then when it would seem he isn’t going to drop them in a Powerbomb he drives them down violently onto the back of their neck folding them up like an accordion.]

The Unravellment -Tilt-a-whirl reverse DDT [Pharaoh whips his opponents into the ropes and as they are coming back towards him he scoops them up quickly onto his shoulder in the form of a tilt a whirl. Abruptly he salutes the crowd and then flips them over his shoulder catching them into mid air with a reverse DDT.]

Catacomb horror -Overhead, spinning belly to back Suplex [Pharaoh locks his opponent up in position for a belly to back throw but before he tosses them overhead he spins them around twice and then abruptly throws them over head.]

Cairo Conundrum -450 degree knee drop [Pharaoh ascends the top turn buckle, his opponent out cold on the ground below, he salutes the crowd arrogantly and then without consequence he takes to the air, performing a 450 degree dive before landing knee first in his victims ribs.]

Set Up Move: The Sarcophagus Casing (Back mounted crossed arms chinlock with body scissors [The Pharaoh pulls one of his opponent's arms around their throat before performing this movement on his victims other arm, so that their arms appear to be locked under their chin. He then performs a body scissors on the foe and drags them down to the mat keeping the hold locked on until they submit.] )
Finisher: The Jewel of the Nile (Inverted Sit-out Tombstone piledriver [Pharaoh scoops his victim up in what can only be described as an inverted scoop slam, he holds his foe up in this position for a few seconds before driving them down hard into the mat in a sit-out fashion])

Bio

What is important about him has already been said. Some heard the whispers, and some didn’t…some…were simply told. It is known that he was born in Cairo, Egypt and remained there for years until he had gained total awareness of the world about him. He studied for a scholarship in ancient history and went on to study archaeology. The man they call The Pharaoh is the only one who knows the truth, whatever that may be, and he doesn’t feel like telling it now – maybe he never will. He isn’t your average man. He is something you’ve never seen before. He’s out to prove a point to his Gods – he is his own new hope.


He is Titu Xansiv...The Pharaoh
