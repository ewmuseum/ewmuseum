Bio

Of modest beginnings and decidedly mixed heritage, Senji Powers was born on the Japanese island of Okinawa to a Japanese mother and a retired American soldier living in Japan with his teenaged son and infant daughter. From the start Senji proved a handful as he ran amock and monopolized his parent's time, to the chagrin of his older half sister Alicia. Senji would learn a sobering lesson later as he learned not everyone was so accepting of his mixed heritage and western ways. Senji would retreat further and further from his family… until he finally disowned them completely. Senji reaffirmed his Japanese culture and embraced it… perhaps a bit too tightly. None the less… Senji took his family name of Yulav and left his mother's house when he was barely in his teens. Senji made his own way for a while… but having little or no job skills made things difficult… but his luck would soon change when his sister came calling with an offer that was too good to pass up on.


Alicia Sinn sought out her brother after acquiring her own company and the immense wealth that came with it. Senji… though slow to embrace what he called his 'Gajin' sister… chose to join her nonetheless. In time Senji became quite the inspiring young talent in the ring… though he preferred the no-holds barred style. Senji enjoyed moderate success inside the ring… but his true love of the martial arts was all consuming. Senji dropped off the map after studying many styles in an attempt to create his own. Apparently having achieved his goal… Senji has returned with the hopes of staking claim to championship gold… to prove the superiority of the martial arts and the Japanese culture.


Senji is arrogant and pompous. Believing that no person… man or woman is the equal of the Japanese. Senji borders on being a racial militant in his obsession to prove himself superior to all others. Deep down… Senji is deeply disturbed and sickened by the impurity of his own blood and he sometimes takes his cause to the extreme in an attempt to abate that very fact. For Senji… honor is everything… but his methods sometimes prove the contrary.


Small in size for a wrestler, Senji is however powerfully built. His frame is wrapped in a layer of pure muscle that is a testament to his intense training. Senji's mixed heritage is painfully obvious in his appearance, much like his sister. Senji has coal black hair and a light complexion. He's rather clean cut, with little or no facial hair.
