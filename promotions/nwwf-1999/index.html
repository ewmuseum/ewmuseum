Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The one document New Wave Wrestling Federation served whole is a house-show broadcast, Friday Night Inferno, dated 21 May 1999, and it opens already mid-fall: a brawl already underway between Anthony Mislan, The RingMaster, and a wrestler its own transcript names, inconsistently, as both Xavier and Xavier Mikel.

The referee, Jack Stevens, checks twice for a tap-out that never comes before Anthony Mislan finally puts Xavier Mikel away with a stunner off his own Swinging Neck-Breaker. The moment the bell rings, Hart-Breaker runs in with a steel chair, beats down both Mislan and The RingMaster, and gloats into a live microphone: “You 2 vs me is you got the guts you will show up unless or are chicken like Grim Repear.”

Hitman and Barr call the whole broadcast from ringside, closing it out with a plug for the following week's Friday Night Inferno. How it ended isn't in its surviving record.
