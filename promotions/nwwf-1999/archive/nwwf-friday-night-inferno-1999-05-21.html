Friday Night Inferno

21 May 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Anthony Mislan walks to the ring. The RingMaster delivers a short-arm clothesline to Anthony Mislan. Xavier applies an arm wrench to Anthony. Anthony climbs to his feet. Anthony goes for a Back Breaker but The RingMaster dodges the attack. Anthony puts his hand in the mouth of Mikel. Anthony has The
RingMaster in the Mandible claw! Jack Stevens is checking for a tap out. ... ... The RingMaster escapes. ] Hitman- - We almost had a winner. [The RingMaster delivers a spine buster to Anthony Mislan. Anthony is put in the Mandible Claw by Anthony Mislan. Jack Stevens is checking for a tap out.
... (AHHHH!) ... Mikel tightens the hold. Anthony Mislan escapes. Xavier and
Anthony go to the floor Jack Stevens starts the count (.1) Xavier executes a piledriver on Anthony. (..2) Mikel swings a Steel chair and hits Anthony.
Anthony is bleeding as a result. (...3) Xavier measures Anthony up and drops a closed fist. ]
Hitman- - Fist drop! [Mikel gets up. (....4) The RingMaster fist drops Anthony Mislan on the floor. Mikel is up again. Anthony gets up. (.....5) They head back into the ring. Mikel drives a forearm into Anthony. Anthony Mislan executes a reverse DDT on
The RingMaster. Anthony goes for a Piledriver but The RingMaster dodges the attack. Now The RingMaster standing. Mikel takes Anthony Mislan down with a knee. Anthony Mislan is up again. Anthony uses a closed fist on Mikel. Anthony
Mislan kicks Xavier in the head. Xavier moves back to his feet. Swinging
Neck-Breaker from The RingMaster takes Anthony Mislan down. Anthony Mislan moves back to his feet. Anthony Mislan is put in the Mandible Claw by Anthony
Mislan. Jack Stevens is checking for a tap out. ... Mikel tightens the hold.
... ... Anthony Mislan trys to escape. Anthony Mislan escapes. Mikel drags
Anthony Mislan to the floor. Jack Stevens starts the count (.1) Anthony Mislan takes a slap to the face from Xavier Mikel. Mikel piledrives Anthony Mislan head first into the floor. Anthony and The RingMaster move back into the ring.
They lockup. Anthony sends Xavier to the corner of the ring. Swinging Neck-Breaker from Anthony takes Xavier Mikel down. Anthony Mislan chants start. Anthony Mislan grabs Xavier Mikel's head and connects with a stunner. Anthony goes for a pin. The ref starts the count. ...1 ...2 ...3 Hart-Breaker starts to make his way out to the ring side with a chair in his hand.]
Hitman- Now it is going to get good. Barr- I was afraid of that. [Hart walks slowly down to the ring. Anthony looks at him. Hart begins to give the sign of the Fab Four. When he does Mislan gives him the one and only
"finger". Hart-Breaker is enraged by this and rushes the ring and nails Anthony with several chair shots. The Ringmaster starts to get up and when he does
Hart-Breaker nails him with the chair.] Hitman- Encore, encore, do it again. Barr- yeah, nothing like hitting a man while he is down huh Hitman? Hitman- You got it there, you see you are starting to learn. [Hart-Breaker continues to beat both men. Finally he stops and throws the chair out of the ring and he grabs a mic.]
Hart-Breaker: You see I have had a really bad night and now I think i may get my ealry chirtmas present I challenge both of you to a handicap match against me next friday. You 2 vs me is you got the guts you will show up unless or are chicken like Grim Repear. Hits the Fab 4 Music!!!!!
[Hart Breaker begins to make his way out of the ring. Gloating about what he has done.] Hitman- I hate to leave this sort of thing like this, but we have to do it folks WE ARE OUT OF TIME, catch us next week an FRIDAY NIGHT INFERNO!!!!
Barr- Good night folks.
