Northeastern Pro Wrestling Stampede, Sample Part III (of III)

15 July 1997

Wrestling Alliance, and this is wrestling legend, Tony ‘Klonis’ Riviera. Klonis: That’s right, Briggs, I may be the wrestling legend but you’re not so bad yourself, there, big guy. For those of you who don’t know, I’m set to make my return to the NPW sometime soon in the future and I swear to god I’m a changed man. Anthony B: A changed man? Klonis: Well, I went back to my roots and found myself. I’m tired of all this rulebreaking I’ve been doing. I’m going to start winning matches the fair way because god knows I can. But we’ll get into that during the match. Right now, lets get down to ring announcer “One Ear” Kevin “The Real Deal” Boland Jr. [Brigano laughs a bit as the camera zooms in on Kevin Bolland.] Kevin B: The following NPW Northeastern Heavyweight Championship Tournament contest is scheduled for one fall and has a 20 minute time limit! [Crowd instantly gives mixed reactions as "Mother" by Danzig blasts onto the shitty PA. Ozzy Oakly steps out through the curtain. Ozzy is well built, and has a few tattoos. He wears dark green tights with a bending bar-bell on the left leg, which is gold, also he wears gold boots with ‘Double O’ written on them in dark green.] Kevin B: Introducing first, coming down the isle, weighing in at 245 lbs, from Salt Lake City, Utah, here is the one and only, best record in the NPW ... Ozzy Oakly! [Crowd continues their mixed reactions as he rolls into the ring.] Klonis: This guy isn’t a pushover. He’s one tough son of a bitch, I’ll tell ya straight off. [Crowd gives more mixed reactions this time, but louder, as “the Beautiful People” by Marilyn Manson blasts onto the PA and Kid Vicious makes his way to the ring. Kid Vicious is a white male and and he has a five o’ clock shadow. He has semi-long brown hair and brown eyes. Also, his size and build could be compared to Chris Benoit.] Kevin B: And his opponent, weighing in at 250 lbs, from Toronto, Ontario, Canada, accompanied to the ring by his partner Mark Stone and the lovely Amber ... here is one half of Malicious Intent, Kid Vicious! [Kid Vicious rushes to the ring, Mark Stone takes his time. Stone looks into the camera and begins speaking.] Mark S: Nope, Jay certainly isn’t going to be 100% going into the next round but god damnit I swear to god neither will Ozzy! Anthony B: Fighting words from Mark Stone as his Malicious partner Kid Vicious enters the ring. Ozzy goes straight after him with a charge but KV kicks him in the midsection! They’re picking up where they left off in the brawl! Vicious turns him around and pushes him into the corner. Vicious drives a shoulder deep into Ozzy’s abdomen! Klonis: Now while you’re calling the action, there, I think I’ll tell you about a few things. After my wins over Mr. Deadly and Jay Cal, I decided it was time to hang up the trunks. But when I got to thinking about things, it just didn’t seem right to me. I love wrestling way too much to quit. Anthony B: I’m listening. Vicious grabs him by the hair and nails him with a headbutt. He shoulderblocks him in the stomach once again and ouch! A third time as well. Vicious grabs him by the arm and irish whips him to the other side! Klonis: But what I was doing at that time wasn’t wrestling at all. I was just brawling and fighting. I decided it was time for a change. So I did a little work at my game and became a real wrestler, you know? Anthony B: I wrestle the pro circuits as well, and believe me, I know. Kid Vicious is getting malicious. He charges the other corner where Ozzy is and Ozzy stumbles out of the way! Vicious collides with the turnbuckle and drops down to the ground. Big mistake there. Vicious has a lot of firepower but going into the corner twice is a mistake. Klonis: So I decided to change my ways. I’m a wrestler now, not a fighter, and I hope Jay Cal is willing to sign another match, but this time, it will be different. The both of us respect eachother and I know that for a fact. If Jay will show up for a contract signing next week, I’d appreciate it. Anthony B: What? Oh, okay. Vicious is down and Oakly is up. Oakly grabs Vicious by the hair and pulls him to his feet. Oakly with a stinging right hand and a knee to the midsection. He grabs him and synchs him in for a vertical suplex. Vicious puts his foot up to block and Vicious now pulls him up for a suplex! He’s holding him up in the air! [Crowd cheers as Vicious holds him up in the air with a suplex. Oakly makes a thump on the mat as he’s brought down with force.] Anthony B: Kid Vicious is not in that ring to play around, rest assured. He drops a big elbow down on Oakly and he drags him over towards the ropes, he lays Oakly down neck fist on the bottom rope and steps on his head! He’s choking him out here and using those middle and top ropes for leverage! Klonis: Kid Vicious is not somebody you want to mess around with. He and I have gotten into a little fisticuffs before and I’m not willing to go through that again. Great fighter, as is Jay a great wrestler. Anthony B: I whole heartedly agree with that, Tony. The referee is counting here ... he gets up to four and Vicious lets him go. Vicious locks on that very same choke once again and the referee starts his count again. Klonis: He’s very determined and very malicious, if you know what I mean. KV will stop at nothing to win a match and that’s just the type of attitude that is needed in this day and age in the wrestling world. Anthony B: Vicious pulls him up to his feet and ties him up in the ropes! Now he’s unloading with kicks and punches to the face of the tired out Oakly! The referee tries to step in here and Vicious shoves him out of the way, he’d better watch it or he’ll risk DQ! He lays another couple of shots in on Oakly. Klonis: Vicious had better be careful because the referee’s here in NPW aren’t too loose. Wait a minute, maybe I’d better rephrase that. :) Anthony B: Kid Vicious is ready to try and put Oakly away. He grabs Oakly by the neck and drops him down with a big DDT! [Crowd chants, “DDT!”] The fans love it! Oakley is getting the snot whipped out of him. Klonis: And you’re suprised? Anthony B: Ozzy is about to become a stain on the mat because Vicious is giving that signal for his patented slingshot brainbuster! He pulls Oakly up and sets him up for it! He hoists him up into the air, bounces him on the top rope and down onto the neck with a brainbuster! Klonis: I’ll tell you, that’s a lethal weapon in this sport and most men would be instantly put away if he was caught in that type of move! The referee better get in there and check Ozzy’s neck! Anthony B: Miraculously, Vicious isn’t even going for the cover! What the hell is he doing right now? Vicious gets up and shoves the referee! There was no reason for that and the referee is admonishing him! Vicious throws the reff to the matt and he’s gonna get DQed for that one! [Ding! Ding! Ding!] Klonis: Very intellegent by Kid Vicious! He softened Ozzy up and now Ozzy must go into the next round to face Jay Cal with an injury, thus giving Cal far better chances of victory here in the tournament. Not such a bad idea, man. Anthony B: Still, you can’t hit a referee. I’m not going to be suprised if Mike Langan levies a good sized fine on him for that one. Ozzy is trying to get back to his feet and he’s clipped by Mark Stone out of knowhere! Klonis: Speaking of Langan, I’ve gotta go talk to the big cheese. We need to go over some contract shit, goodnight everybody. [Klonis drops his headset and starts his walk to the back. Moments later, Jay Cal rushes down to the ring and attacks Ozzy. Stone, Cal and Vicious give Ozzy a horrible beating.] Anthony B: Malicious Intent is getting the last laugh here tonight! That’s all the time we have, folks! Until next time, watch CHAOS!!! and ... GOODNIGHT, folks!
