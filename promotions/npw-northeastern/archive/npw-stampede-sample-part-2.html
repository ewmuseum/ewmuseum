[Camera returns from a commercial with... Dean Wright (of course), seeming pre-recorded.] <--The following interview was recorded on a local television station, the tape being forwarded to the NPW--> Announcer: NPW broadcast journalist Dean Wright caught up with NPW newcomers Search & Destroy earlier in the week and filed this report Dean Wright: You two gentlemen burst onto the NPW scene alot to say, but as yet we are still to see you produce, or come good on your promises so to speak Search: Listen up Deano, we had a number of matches scheduled, our first against the Wave Runners became a dark match due to some problem or other, but the fans that attended the show saw that we could indeed deliver on our promises as you put it. Since then the cards have been either late or cancelled, we, like everybody else in the NPW have found it hard to work up any momentum or get a roll on Dean Wright: So you say. So your poor performance, or lack of performance is being attributed to late cards etc Destroy: Yes. We understand that these problems do occur from time to time. If we though the NPW was purposely stuffing us about we would have packed our bags and used the door a week or so back, but after a phone conversation with Michael Langan he assured us that all will be back on track in the next few days. Currently we are training for what will amount to our debut match against the Hellraisers in the tag tournament Dean Wright: OK, everybody receiving match schedules can see that for themselves, but how do you plan to back up on those promises you made after that match? Search: If the results go our way we'll be facing the Rippers in the second round of the tag tournament, but aside from that we are going to take a week off, allowing us to recharge our batteries and to allow the NPW to sort itself out Dean Wright: What?! You wrestle one match and then go on holidays? To me, you two seem like you're running scared, who from, the Rippers? Malicious Intent? Destroy: Listen up Deano! Search & Destroy aren't scared of anybody! We said we're taking a week off and that is all there is to it! We had this break planned for a few months, the NPW got itself in a twist, and unfortunately it doesn't feature in our plans for teh next week or so Search: Scared? Are you for real you silly little man? We have worked in a great many federations, and in each one we have won their major tag team titles. We currently hold tag team titles in two other federations! We work damn hard week in, week out and we are planning a break! Ther is no team in the NPW, in fact there is no team anywhere that scares us. There are some very competitive teams, some great wrestlers, but Search& Destroy dont runscared from anyone! Dean Wright: Mmmmm. Whatever you say. So you two plan to wrestle the Hellraisers and then take off. When will we see you again and who do you plan to wrestle? Search: You'll see us when we're ready and not before. You dont seem to realise the depth we're adding to the tag team ranks of the NPW! Little men like you could jeopardise the good working relationship we currently have with the NPW. You see since we've started wrestling we could count the number of teams on the fingers of one hand who have claimed clean victories over us, together we have wrestled in excess of 100 matches together, and my friend when we talk loud and bold, its because we have the form, the record to do so. DEstroy: As to who we'll face when we return, hopefully we'll cross paths with the Rippers, we've been after a rematch with thenm for months now, ever since they ran from eth WIF with their tails between their legs. We wouldn't mind a rematch with the Wave Runners, that was a good match and I tink the fans should get the opportunity to see teh four of us in teh ring again! Search: I guess there is also Malicious Intent, for some reason or another those guys just seem to annoy us. They may have been running roughshod over other folks inthe NPW, but we're a completely different kettle of fish Dean Wright: Quite a big ask. It seems that there are a few teams in your sights at the moment, you'd better watch out not to put too many offside at once, after all you are only two and some of these guys, like Intent are better equipped with man power so to speak Destroy: I do hope you aren't trying to threaten us. In teh ring we are indeed only two. For quite a long time w've been able to handle everything thrown at us. The only time we have major probs is when someone has a go at us fromnbehind, you know, the classic, 'lets gang up and jump them from behind mentality'. Search: Well that started to wear a little thin with us a long time ago, so we simply went out and hired ourselves not just a manager, but a security manager. YOu see 7 foot and 400 pounds of Roundhouse enables us to concentrate on business in teh ring and he takes care of any other matters that may arise Dean Wright: You mean you trust yourself to that over grown dolt? How's he gonna handle himself against trained wrestlers Destroy: Obviously you didn't do your homework to well Deano. YOu se Roundhosue worked for many years in the security industry in Sydney, gaining a reputation as teh most efficent and effective door man money could buy. As well as that Roundhosue has held, or currently holds three major championship belts in other wrestling promotions. He can more than adequately look after himself, and more importantly us whatever situation rears its ugly head Dean Wright: *sarcastically* Unfortunately I must conclude this enthralling interview, is there anything either of you two gents would like to add before we cut to a commercial? Search: Yeah two things! First, we dont really like you very much you piece of scrotum sweat, and secondly ...... Destroy: We are here to win teh NPW Tag Team Titles, and there aint nothing, there aint nobody that is gonna stop us. The NPW needs innaugural tag team champs that it can be proud of, men who will bring respect and credibility to the titles, men who will defend them regularily and Deano, you tool, we are the men to do it! Dean Wright: *sarcastically* Fascinating. Well I must be off, and these two defintely are. This is Dean Wright signing off. Good night.
