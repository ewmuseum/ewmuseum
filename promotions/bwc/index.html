Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Buckeye Wrestling Coalition's surviving record opens with a bare 29 October 1997 recruitment line, “the Buckeye Wrestling Coalition is looking for YOU!!!!,” and by late November was fielding skepticism from its own newsgroup readers: a roster call naming giants discovered by Stone Cold Steve Austin and cut loose by ECW drew a reply asking, in the community's own shorthand for a copycat fed, “is this a SHEF?”

Turkey Day Tourney '97, staged 28 November 1997 at the Gund Arena in Cleveland, crowned the promotion's first World Heavyweight Champion out of a sixteen-man tournament, its main event called into the ring by Michael Buffer. Gravedigger and the Rowdy One fought to a finish with no count-out, no disqualification, and no time limit, Gravedigger driving Rowdy through a ringpost, the ring steps, and a guardrail before finishing him with a Tombstone into a Chokeslam.

Its first televised broadcast, Monday Night Blast Off, opened ten days later with President Ryan Vaughn setting the terms for who would challenge Gravedigger next: a ten-man battle royal at the promotion's coming Seasons Beatings pay-per-view, drawn only from wrestlers not already committed to one of its three running tournaments — a ruling that pointedly excluded its own top-ranked contender, a “monster” named Jebodiah Hargreaves who had just debuted alongside his brother, “The Ripper” Ezekial Hargreaves, for having, in Vaughn's own words, “just one fluke match.”

Its surviving record ends there, mid-promise, still building toward that 28 December Seasons Beatings card. No further document survives.
