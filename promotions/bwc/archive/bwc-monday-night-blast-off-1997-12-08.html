BWC Monday Night Blast Off / Blast Zone, 8 December 1997

8 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
BWC MONDAY NIGHT BLAST OFF (Rippers music plays as he and cactus walk out from behind the apron, Ripper is holding a mic) Ripper: What I’d like to have right now is for all of you to stand up and gaze upon this unleashed beast that has come upon the BWC to torture, along with me, all of you losers that you call wrestlers! (curtains begin to open as a huge man, bigger than the Ripper walks out from the darkness with a pitchfork in his hand. The crowd becomes silent as the man grabs the mic) Jeb: I have been looking long and hard to find my brother, the Ripper, and now its all gonna pay off as we tear through this federation by beating up anyone that crosses our path...look out all of you so called champions cause we aren’t gonna put up with your $#!% anymore! (the mic is thrown into the crowd as the two walk back into the locker room looking for someone to hurt) “WILD THING” FROM THE MAJOR LEAGUE MOVIES PLAYS OVER CLIPS FROM BWC TURKEY DAY TOURNEY ’97 ENDING WITH GRAVEDIGGER WITH THE WORLD BELT. (fireworks go off all over the area as we pan down to Eric Williams and Craig Wilson down at ringside.) EW: Welcome everyone to the FIRST EVER TELEVISED MONDAY NIGHT BLAST OFF!!! EW: Without any further delay, let’s welcome out, the President of the BWC, Ryan Vaughn! (Vaughn makes his way into the ring. Heckling coming from the crowd.) CW: Mr. President, I understand that you have an announcement concerning the upcoming Pay-Per-View, Seasons Beatings on December 28. RV: Yes sir, I do. As you and the rest of the fans of the BWC know. We are currently holding numerous tournaments to fill the titles we have here in the BWC. Almost everyone is involved in one so I can’t see how it would be fair to give a World title shot to someone who may still be involved in a tournament at Seasons Beatings. So this is what I have done. The opening match of Seasons Beatings will be a ten man battle royal to determine who gets a shot at Gravedigger at the end of the card. The ten men will be the top ten not involved in tournaments at the time of the Pay Per View. CW: So what you’re saying is that we won’t know who the ten men are until they are announced at the beginning of the Pay Per View. RV: That is correct, sir. CW: I’m sorry but if you look at the current ratings, you will see that Jebodiah Hargreaves, you know, that monster that was just out here with the Ripper, is rated number one. He is also not involved in any of the tournaments. Why isn’t he getting the title shot? RV: If you look again, you will see that the top three wrestlers; Jebodiah Hargreaves, Hyan Greycee and Handy Greycee are at the top of the federation but they only have one match each. I cannot and will not give a title shot to anyone who may have just one fluke match. CW: Okay, Mr. President, thank you. (commercial break) EW: Okay, coming up in our first match, it is from the Great Lakes Iron Man tournament. As the newcomer, “The Scotsman” Simon MacKyght takes on the mastodon of the BWC, Armageddon. We have some pre-recorded words from “The Scotsman”) ( "In the Still of the Night" plays in the background as the camera opens to a shot in BWC's main gym. It focuses on "The Scotsman" lifting weights. He is wearing a white tank top with the words "BWC Training", and black workout pants, and black sneakers. "The Scotsman" stops his workout, sits up on the bench and looks into the camera. His music dies down before he speaks). "Well, here I am. Getting ready for my debut in the BWC. Got to train hard and be ready for all you "BIG" boys out there. I looked over the roster at the great athletes that BWC has and "WOW" these guys are "B.I.G. big". That is FINE by me. (He stands up now. Sweating a little, he takes a towel and wipes his face.) "You see, I have been a fighter all my life. On the streets of Culloden, I paid my dues. And in the rings all over Scotland, I have worked at being a better wrestler. I have faced many different types of opponents, so size is not a factor for me. I can mix it up or wrestle you to the ground. Whatever you do I will be ready." (Simon goes and hops on a treadmill, and starts jogging. He looks toward the camera again) "I can't wait for the Great Lakes Title tournament! It is an "Iron Man" tourney, just the way I LIKE IT! BIG Boys of the BWC may take me for granted, being smaller and a rookie. But get one thing strait! I may be one of the smaller men of the BWC, but great things come in small packages!" ("The Scotsman's" music starts to play again, as the camera starts to pull back and slowly fade to black) (“In the Still of the Night” By Whitesnake plays over the speaker as the fans begin to rise to see the new guy.) Announcer: The following match is part of the Great Lakes Iron Man Tournament! It is scheduled for one fall with a thirty minute time limit. Making his way first down the aisle, from Culloden, Scotland and weighing in at 235 pounds. SIMON “THE SCOTSMAN” MACKNYGHT!! EW: Wow! The Scotsman looks like he’s been through a few fights. The crowd here not knowing what to think. If this guy is looking for popularity, he is facing the wrong guy. Armageddon is one of the most loved wrestlers here in the BWC. CW: Yeah, and as we all know, love is very important in the world of professional wrestling! (“Highway to Hell” by AC/DC plays over the speaker as the fans go nuts holding signs saying “armaGODden”) Announcer: And his opponent, making his way to the ring, from Pensacola, Florida, and weighing in at 450 pounds, ARMAGEDDON!!!!!!!!!! EW: And we are set to go. Last week, Missile again pinned Armageddon to go up 20 points on everyone. Tonight, Missile takes on the Warrior of Destrucity. CW: I like the looks of this guy, Eric. The Scotsman may just be my pick to go all the way in this tournament and in the BWC. EW: And there’s the bell! Armageddon reaches out to slap MacKnyght but Simon ducks and THES PRESS!!!! ONE TWO THREE!!! CW: I TOLD YOU! I TOLD YOU! I TOLD YOU! EW: OH MY GOD! SIMON MACKNYGHT HAS JUST PINNED ARMAGEDDON IN 27 SECONDS!!!! WHAT MUST BE GOING THROUGH ARMAGEDDON’S HEAD NOW? CW: SOMETHING LIKE, I SUCK, I SUCK, I SUCK Announcer: Your winner in TWENTY-SEVEN SECONDS!!! “THE SCOTSMAN” SIMON MACKNYGHT!!!!!! EW: Armageddon is beside himself! He doesn’t know what to think and who’s to blame him. He lost in thirty some odd seconds at Turkey Day Tourney to Missile then lost last week again to Missile and now this. CW: A big man that can’t beat the little ones. How pathetic. And you mean to tell me that the Ripper is not the giant here in the BWC? EW: And now, let’s hear from another newcomer here in the BWC, the Reckless Degenerate. CW: Sounds like my kind of guy! <Standing in alley of Detroit> "If you build it they will come". That saying holds a lot of meaning. Well, I'm not Shoeless Joe, and this sure as hell ain't no Field of Dreams. I've been waiting for the right promotion to take over and it's here. Now I don't give a damn whether you like it or not. You see, all of you made me. All you dope heads, bums, losers, and so called "wrestlers" made me. From your distorted minds, your degenerate illiterate minds, came your worst nightmare. No I'm not Freddy, I won't kill you with some knives, I'm going to kick everyone's ass with my own God-given natural abilities. Cause, boys, when you're as great as the Reckless One, you don't need gimmicks. So, Mr. President, line 'em up, I'll take 'em down. And one more thing, when you get in the ring with me, you crack-smokin', coke snorting, heroine shootin m.f.'s, you'll leave with a "Bad Trip". <Gives the double finger and walks away.> EW: We look forward to seeing the Reckless One in action here in the weeks to come. But up next, it’s a first round match from the World Tag Team Tournament! As I understand, the advertisement said second round match. We apologize for any confusion. Announcer: This match is a first round match for the BWC WORLD TAG TEAM TITLE TOURNAMENT! Making their way down to the ring, from Jenero, Brazil, weighing in at a total combined weight of 610 pounds. HYAN AND HANDY, THE GREYCEE BROTHERS!!! (“Roots Bloody Roots” By Sepultura plays over the speakers as the fans boo.) EW: Hyan and Handy, from the famous Greycee clan down in Brazil, I understand that Handy was banished from the country because of illegal tactics in the shootfighting ring. And his brother, Hyan was so distraught that he followed Handy here. CW: I have seen these guys in action before. You are in for a real treat, Williams! Announcer: And their opponents, (“Dirty Little Mind” By Jackyl plays over the speaker as the fans boo some more!) Announcer: From Seattle, Washington and weighing in at a total combined weight of 701 pounds. Sean Champion and Chris Royal, the NEW GENERATION!!!!!! EW: This should be a good match here, Craig. CW: I like both of these guys. I can’t decide who I’m going to root for. EW: On the phone, we have Damon Chandler of the Chandler Brothers. DC: That’s right, what in the hell are you doing, allowing these Brazilian nuts come in here and take out gimmick. Blair and I are the original and best brother tag team to come down the pike since the Mulkeys! EW: That may be so but none the less, Hyan and Handy ARE brothers and since you do not have a copyright on the gimmick, because you are NOT the first to use it, the Greycees have every right to use it if they wish. CW: What about the Hargreaves brothers, Ezekial and Jebodiah? They’re using it, too! DC: I’ll tell you what, I challenge either one of those teams to a match at Seasons Beatings for the right to use the brothers gimmick here in the BWC. I demand satisfaction and justice! (phone slams down) EW: Well, okay, we seem to have lost Damon Chandler on the phone. Thank you for your comments, Mr. Chandler. CW: What a nut! To think he and his brother, Blair are superior to the other brother teams, let alone the entire BWC. What nerve the little punk has. As a matter of fact, Damon. If you want to find out how a tag team is supposed to act, sign a match with me and my partner here. That’s right, Eric and I used to be part of the elite group in wrestling. We were founders of the Ohio chapter of the Four Horsemen. (both guys hold up four fingers.) EW: That’s right, so if the Chandler Brothers want to think that they are the best there is, was and will be in wrestling, come knock on our door, son! CW: And that, as you would say, is the bottom line. EW: Okay, back to the match, thus far, Champion has been throwing Hyan around like a rag doll. CW: You know, I can’t believe the nerve of that guy. EW: Let it go, if he’s big enough, he’ll sign a match. Royal is now in the ring and just pummeling Handy Greycee. CW: Are the Greycees going to show up today? EW: SKULL CRUSHER BY HANDY!!! ONE TWO THR…….. CW: How did Royal kick out of that? A flying headbutt by Handy, normally would knock the man out but Royal just keeps on ticking! EW: Coming up still tonight, Doc Troyer takes on Sean Champion here in a second round match for the Television Title Tourney. Also, the Rowdy One will be here for an interview and to take on the Mauler in the United States Iron Man Tourney. Also, Beastmaster takes on Dillon Chandler, the Death Finders against the Heat Seekers, the Mauler against Iron and the Warrior of Destrucity against Missile. That is all tonight on Monday Night Blast Off! CW: Handy takes Champion down with a clothesline. He’s giving the sign, going to the top rope… SKULL CRUSHER, AGAIN!!! ONE TWO THREE!!! Announcer: Ladies and Gentlemen, at 18:16, the winners of the match, HANDY AND HYAN, THE GREYCEE BROTHERS! EW: What a comeback victory for the Brazilian team. CW: For the better part of eighteen minutes, Hyan and Handy were beaten to a pulp but with one last ditch effort, Handy gets the win and the advance in the tournament. CW: And Beastmaster is walking back to the locker room with a huge grin on his face. EW: I think it’s safe to say that someone enjoyed the outcome of that one. CW: I know I did! EW: Let’s head back up to the ring. Announcer: The following contest is part of the United States Iron Man Tournament. Already in the ring, The Mauler. And his opponent. (“N.W.O.” By Ministry plays over the speaker as the fans boo.) Announcer: Lead to the ring by his tag team partner, Steel. From Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania and weighing in at 356 pounds. IRON!!!!! EW: This is a guy that still impresses me. I would put him up there with Armageddon and the Hargreaves brothers. CW: We have another phone call. BC: Yeah, it’s Blair Chandler, you chumps. EW: What can we do for you, Mr. Chandler? CW: Do you have a contract for us? BC: Damn right, I do! I have it signed for Seasons Beatings but I have another proposition for you. It seems that there is another Tag Team Tournament starting next week. Why don’t the two of you enter and show us how it is done? EW: If we do that, who is going to be the announce team? BC: That won’t be a hard spot to fill. I hear that George “the Animal” Steele is available. Hell, he’d bring up the ratings on this show 100% overnight! CW: You want to see us in the tournament? You got it! Next week, you will see that greatest Ohio based tag team in the 80s come back and show that the new generation of wrestlers can not match up!!! Announcer: The winner of the match at 13:36, IRON!!!!! EW: What happened? CW: I have no idea. EW: Well, Iron apparently will gain twenty points in the United States tournament.
