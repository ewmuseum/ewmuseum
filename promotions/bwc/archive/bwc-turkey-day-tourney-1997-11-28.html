BWC Turkey Day Tourney, 28 November 1997

28 November 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
WELCOME TO CLEVELAND, OHIO!!! WELCOME TO THE GUND ARENA!!! WELCOME TO THE BUCKEYE WRESTLING COALITION!!! AND WELCOME, BWC FANS, TO TURKEY DAY TOURNEY ’97!!! (the capacity crowd is screaming at the top of their lungs as fireworks explode over the ring followed quickly by flame blasts coming from all four ringposts) EW: It’s a history making event here in Cleveland as the BWC, tonight, will crown its first ever World Heavyweight Champion! We have a sixteen man tournament lined up plus three other matches. So without any further delay, I’d like to introduce my broadcast partner, Craig Wilson! CW: Thank you, Eric. The BWC has been hosting cards over the past two weeks to determine the wrestler’s rankings solely for this tournament. As you will see throughout the night, men of all shapes, sizes and backgrounds have flocked to the BWC, but they all have one goal, going down in the history books as the first BWC World Heavyweight Champion. EW: Let’s go up to the ring for the open match of the tournament! (bell rings) Announcer- Ladies and Gentlemen, this is the opening bout in the BWC WORLD TITLE TOURNAMENT!!! (crowd screams. “Highway to Hell” By AC/DC plays over the speakers and the crowd stands up and cheers) Announcer- Coming down the aisle, from Pensacola, Florida. Weighing in at 450 pounds. ARRRRRRRRMAGEEEEEEEEEEDDON!!!! EW: This is a man, Craig, that I feel will have an easy time making his way through the tournament today. CW: True he’s big, he only has one loss so far. But the man who gave him that loss is also in this tournament. That being Ezekial “The Ripper” Hargreaves. (The Theme to Top Gun plays over the speakers and the crowd continues to cheer.) Announcer- And his opponent, also from Pensacola, Florida. Weighing in at 227 pounds. MIIIIIIISSILLLLLLE! EW: Well, like we said, we have men of all sizes here in the BWC and this match is probably the extreme of that statement. (bell rings) EW: And we’re underway! They lock up and Armageddon shoves Missile back and OH GOD!! Lariat! CW: He almost took the head off of that rocket! EW: Armageddon, somewhat of a submission wrestler from what we’ve seen so far applies a chinlock but Missile is close to the ropes. Armageddon pulls Missile to his feet and PERFECT PLEX!!! ONE!!! TWO!!! EW: And Missile barely gets his shoulder up. Wow! That would have been a hell of a start to this tournament. CW: Quit sucking up to that freak! I am telling you, he is nothing next to the Ripper! EW: Armageddon runs into the ropes and Missile comes back with a kick to the midsection! Missile using his speed, takes Armageddon down with a bulldog CW: See, if this runt can take him down, imagine what Ezekial will do to him when they meet again. EW: And I’m obsessed? Please. Missile charges for a forearm smash but Armageddon blocks it. CW: Eric, you suck at this, I’m taking over play-by-play. Armageddon with a hiptoss with crushing power and OHHHH!! Another Clothesline!! And here comes Ezekial!!! EW: Armageddon has spotted him, they’re exchanging words. Why would Ezekial even try to interfere tonight when he knows damn well that they are the favorites to face off in the finals? CW: Look! Missile’s on the top rope!! EW: Armageddon turns around and FLYING BODY PRESS!!! ONE!!! TWO!!! THREE!!! Announcer- At 2:42, the winner of the match, MISSILE!!!! EW: Ezekial is going to pay dearly for his interference in this one, Craig. CW: Come on, Eric. What did the Ripper do? He came down to scout the competition, that’s all. He never touched the guy. IN THE INTEREST OF TIME, HERE ARE THE RESULTS OF THE FIRST ROUND OF THE TURKEY DAY TOURNAMENT!!! · The Warrior of Destrucity was getting pummeled by the former Marine, Blair Chandler but came back to win at 16:02 after the Destrucity Drop. · Doc Troyer, after having the Gravedigger right where he wanted him, lost to the Chokeslam at 9:02 · The Mauler defeated Sean Champion after a sunset flip at 16:09 · Rowdy One won by countout at 16:39 over Tank · Ravenloff was counted out against Ezekial Hargreaves at 14:43 · Dillon Chandler suffered nine Ore Drops but survived to put the USMC-ya! On Iron to win at 34:35!! (Beastmaster Bohley goes to the announcers table with Doc Troyer and the New Generation and grabs the mic.) I don't know what the hell you think you're doing Mr. Williams saying I'm not a headliner. I'm the Greatest thing to ever happen to this two bit federation and you had better remember that. And so far as that blemish on my record by that punk Missile it was only because I wasn't in the main event and I'm going to make sure that doesn't happen again. You'll see who the best is when I sweep through the tournament and win the belt. (Beastmaster throws the mic back at Eric and leaves.) · That must have been on Bohley’s mind because he then went to the ring and loses to Braveheart Mitchell in 1:47 after a body press. And that brings us up to date! EW: And now let’s go up to the ring for the beginning of our second round. Announcer: Ladies and Gentleman, soon to be making his way to the ring, from Pensacola, Florida. Weighing in at 227 pounds, MISSILE!!!!! (Theme from Top Gun plays over the speaker as the crowd holds up signs saying “Missile is a SCUD” and “I’ve got your Missile” with hoots and hollers toward him) EW: Missile with a outstanding upset over Armageddon in the first round. Apparently, the crowd is not happy that Missile took out the favorite, Armageddon so soon. He has only gone 2:42 so far and the Warrior has gone over sixteen minutes. CW: And don’t forget about Beastmaster Bohley’s comments before his loss. With Bohley, Doc Troyer and the New Generation all out of the tournament. That is one group of guys that have nothing to lose. Announcer: And his opponent, (Ultimate Warrior’s theme music plays over the speaker as you see a streak as the Warrior charges to the ring) Announcer: From Parts Unknown. Weighing in at 276 pounds. The WARRIOR OF DESTRUCITY!!!!! (bell rings) EW: And the second round is underway as both men stand eye to eye and Missile calls for the test of strength. CW: That little runt, Missile, sure has grown an ego since he pinned Armageddon. Hell, I can’t blame him. If I defeated someone six foot ten, four hundred fifty pounds. I’d just leave the tournament. You can’t get much higher than that. EW: MISSILE HAS THE WARRIOR IN A BOW AND ARROW SUBMISSION HOLD!!! THE WARRIOR IS SCREAMING IN PAIN!! MISSILE IS GIVING IT EVERYTHING HE”S GOT!!! AND THE Warrior reaches the ropes. That’s got to take a lot of spirit from the Warrior. CW: The Warrior isn’t through, yet. He’ll ground this Missile, yet. EW: Missile whips Warrior to the ropes, WARRIOR RETURNS WITH A CLOTHESLINE! Warrior back against the ropes, HIGH CROSS BODY PRESS!! ONE!! TWO!! EW: Shoulder up by Missile. Wow! What a comeback, Craig! CW: Well, Eric. The Warrior has a lot of heart so nothing coming from this man will surprise me. But you also have to admit that Missile is a marked man in the BWC. Armageddon will no doubt be looking for revenge and Beastmaster Bohley’s crew are looking for the right time to attack so I think Missile should find himself some cover. Not to mention friends. EW: True, Armageddon lives under the Grim Reaper Wrestling Academy, where Missile originally came from. CW: You see, Missile can’t go anywhere without having to look over his shoulder now. EW: Missile again hooks in the bow and arrow submission. The Warrior looks real tired now. No signs of him giving up, though. Missile is going to have to go after a pinfall here. Missile releases and SMALL PACKAGE!!! ONE TWO EW: Warrior with a kickout! Looks like he can hear us, Craig! Missile up to the top rope FROG SPLASH!! AND HERE’S A COVER! ONE TWO EW: Warrior kicks out again. The stamina of this man just impresses me to no end! CW: Eric, I told you. The Warrior has Destrucity on his side. I have no clue what Destrucity is but it seems to work for him so why knock it? EW: DESTRUCITY DROP!!!! OH MY GOD!! OUT OF NOWHERE MISSILE IS HURT!! ONE TWO EW: MISSILE KICKS OUT!! It is rare to see anyone kick out of that maneuver. CW: If the Warrior wants to win, he’s going to have to keep pummeling this guy. If Missile can get away to use his high flying tactics, the Warrior should just lay down a allow the pin. EW: Missile is placed on the top rope. Warrior up….BELLY TO BELLY SUPERPLEX!!!! What a maneuver! Warrior back up on top!! FROG SPLASH! ONE TWO THR… EW: Missile kicks out. REVERSAL!! SMALL PACKAGE BY MISSILE ONE TWO THR… EW: Warrior gets a shoulder up! Missile goes up top! COULD IT BE….. YES!!! GUIDED MISSILE!!! ONE TWO THREE!!!!!!!!!!!! Announcer: Ladies and Gentlemen, your winner in 19:14….. MISSILE!!!!!.. EW: And Missile moves on to the semi-finals against the winner of the following match. Announcer: Ladies and Gentlemen, soon to be making his way to the ring. From Tombstone Arizona! (The Funeral March plays over the speaker as the crowd grows silent.) Weighing in at 281 pounds, THE GRAVEDIGGER!!!!!! EW: If there was anyone in wrestling who can take off with the Undertaker’s gimmick and have success with it, it’s this guy! CW: Come on, Eric! This is a battle of the wanna-bes! A wanna-be Undertaker against a wanna-be Road Warrior! Announcer: And his opponent! (“Iron Man” By Iron Maiden plays over the speaker as the crowd cheers and holds signs like “LOD- Now and Forever!” and “Mauler for President!”) Announcer: Making his way to the ring, from Oakland, California. Weighing in at 390 pounds, the Next Generation Road Warrior, MAULER!!!!!!! CW: The Next Generation? Oh God. Where did the BWC find these egos? EW: And true to form, both men go for a test of strength. CW: While we have a moment, Just a shout out to my buddies Banana Emmis and Mort back home. Yo! STAY OFF THE PHONE UNTIL I GET THERE!!! EW: What? CW: Just call your match! You don’t need to know what that means! EW: I may be better off. Both men back and forth. Using power moves galore. Do you think it’s wise to use such energy here knowing if you win that you take on Missile. CW: Eric, if you don’t give it all you’ve got every match here. You go home and don’t have to worry about Missile or Rowdy One or anyone else still in the tournament! EW: Good point. Gravedigger with a small package! ONE TWO EW: Kickout by Mauler. Gravedigger gets up and tosses the big man out of the ring. Gravedigger going for his choke slam out there. NO!!! PILEDRIVER BY MAULER!!! OH MY GOD! MAULER SENDS GRAVEDIGGER INTO THE STEPS!! Both men back in.. Gravedigger with a thumb to the eye. CHOKE SLAM!!!! ONE TWO EW: KICKOUT!! OH MY GOD! ANOTHER CHOKE SLAM!!! ONE TWO THR… EW: ANOTHER KICKOUT! BOTH MEN GIVING IT ALL THEY’VE GOT! Gravedigger with a frankensteiner, NO POWERBOMB BY MAULER!!! ONE TWO EW: KICKOUT! CW: Good Lord, Eric. I think it’s time to switch to decaf!! EW: CHOKE SLAM BY THE DEAD MAN!!!!! HE’S PULLING TIGHTS!!!! ONE TWO THREE!!! Announcer: The Winner of the match at 13:41, GRAVEDIGGER!!!!!!!!!!!! MOVE ON TO PART TWO!!!!! EW: So we have Missile against Gravedigger in one semi-final match. Let’s fill out the rest of the brackets. Announcer: Ladies and Gentlemen, soon to be making his way to the ring, from Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania. Weighing in at 283 pounds. Lead to the ring by “Stunning” Stacy. THE ROWDY ONE!!!!! (“Chutes and Ladders” By Korn plays over the speaker as fans throw cups and hot dog wrappers toward the asile as fans hold up signs saying “AWI- Another Worthless Idea”) Announcer: And His Opponent! From Lancaster, Pennsylvania! Weighing in at 330 pounds. Lead to the ring by Cactus Jack. EZEKIAL “THE RIPPER” HARGREAVES!!!! (“Living on the Edge” By Aerosmith plays over the speaker as fans grow irate over his appearance. Signs saying “Armageddon rules” and “Ezekial, time for your Armageddon!” are flaunted in his face.) EW: The fans here in Cleveland do not appreciate the Ripper one bit. CW: The fans here in Cleveland believe that throwing objects into the field of play is a good idea. Come on, Eric. These fans are uneducated! EW: And they lock up. Rowdy with a small package! ONE TWO EW: Kickout. Cactus Jack trips Rowdy. The fans really hate the Ripper here. POWERBOMB BY THE RIPPER!! ONE TWO EW: Kickout by Rowdy. And he is hurt bad. He can’t seem to get up. Ezekial runs into the ropes but STACY PULLS DOWN THE TOP ROPE!!! THE RIPPER FALLS TO THE CONCRETE! ROWDY DOWN TO EVEN THE SCORE! EZEKIAL WITH A WHIP TO THE GUARDRAIL! TO THE STEPS! TO THE GUARDRAIL! ROWDY IS DOWN! HERE COMES STACY TO DISTRACT THE RIPPER. HE’S STALKING HER AROUND RINGSIDE! ROWDY GRABS THE RIPPER AND WHIPS HIM TO THE GUARDRAIL! GUTWRENCH SUPLEX ON THE CONCRETE!!! CW: This is what I was talking about! You have to pull out all the stops in order to win this thing! EW: Rowdy rolls back into the ring behind the Ripper. The Ripper is just laying there. Rowdy to the top turnbuckle! HE GIVES THE SIGN!! FLYING ELBOW DROP!! ONE TWO THREE!!!!! EW: The Rowdy One has gotten revenge for Armageddon!! The fans, who were booing him at first, are now doing everything but throwing roses!! (the fans start chanting “Row-dy! Row-dy! Row-dy!) Announcer: The winner of the match at 5:51. THE ROWDY ONE!!!!!!! EW: The crowd’s hatred for Ezekial was far greater then their hatred for the Rowdy One. CW: They’re golddiggers, Eric. Cleveland is notorious for hanging around with the winners and disowning the losers. Look at the Indians through the 70’s and 80’s! EW: That may be so but for right now, the Rowdy One is the Champion of the BWC in their eyes! IN TWO DAYS! THE BWC HITS FULL STEAM AS THEY HEAD TO THE BWC ARENA IN WOOSTER, OHIO THIS MONDAY FOR THE FIRST EVER MONDAY NIGHT BLAST OFF! TUNE IN AS THREE TOURNAMENTS BEGIN FOR THE UNITED STATES, GREAT LAKES AND WORLD TAG TEAM TITLES. PLUS LOADS OF SURPRISES AND AN INTERVIEW WITH TONIGHT’S WINNER. THAT’S BWC MONDAY NIGHT BLAST OFF!!!! EW: Okay, let’s head to the ring for our last second round match. Announcer: Soon to be making his way to the ring area, from U.S.M.C. Boot Camp, weighing in at 245 pounds, DILLON CHANDLER! (“Marching to Mars” By Sammy Hagar plays over the speakers as the crowd reaches out to shake hands with him.) Announcer: And his opponent. (“Jump Around” By House of Pain plays over the speaker. The crowd starts chanting “Free-dom! Free-dom!”) Announcer: From Ireland, weighing in at 250 pounds, “BRAVEHEART” MITCHELL! EW: And Chandler jumps Mitchell before the bell! What has gotten into this guy? CW: Maybe this jarhead has a mean streak after all! I thought he was totally different than his brothers. EW: Actually, I was hoping that he was a mirror image. Mitchell with a backdrop and Chandler is up and over the ropes onto the floor! Mitchell comes out after him… MISTAKE! Chandler rams his head into the guardrail! MITCHELL IS CUT! CW: He’s hardcore! He’s hardcore! EW: Please! We have copyrights to obey! Chandler tosses Mitchell back into the ring. CLOTHESLINE BY MITCHELL! Mitchell runs into the ropes and another clothesline! What determination by Braveheart here. He has been cut open early in this match and still has the strength and desire of a hungry Lion! CW: Lions are growing extinct, Eric. Don’t forget it. Just because this Williams Wallace rip-off connects with two clotheslines after being cut doesn’t impress me one bit. EW: Chandler with a low blow! He sets Mitchell on the top rope…. SUPERPLEX!!!! ONE TWO THR…. EW: Mitchell gets his shoulder up! Chandler whips Mitchell to the ropes. Misses with a clothesline, DIVING FOREARM BY MITCHELL!!! CW: What is your fascination with Mitchell? You were so proud of Chandler last round I thought he was your son. EW: Changed attitudes change minds, my friend. CW: How profound! Mitchell doesn’t stand a chance because he doesn’t ignore the fans like Chandler is now! EW: Both men are outside now Chandler again to the guardrail but it SEEMS TO HAVE NO EFFECT ON MITCHELL! CHANDLER GOES FOR IT AGAIN BUT MITCHELL BLOCKS IT AND DELIVERS ONE OF HIS OWN!!!! (Crowd starts chanting “Free-dom! Free-dom!”) EW: Mitchell whips Chandler to the ringpost! Turnabout is fair play, I guess CW: And all is fair in love and wrestling. Where are you hiding the clique book? EW: In your wife’s underwear drawer CW: You want to give the fans another match to watch? EW: And here’s the Freedom Fall! ONE! TWO! THREE!!! CW: What a crock! Announcer: The winner of the match at 15:37. “BRAVEHEART” MITCHELL!!!! EW: So there are the semi-final matchups! Missile against Gravedigger and the Rowdy One against Braveheart Mitchell. CW: I’ll take a parlay! For fifty bucks I want Gravedigger and Rowdy One! EW: Let’s go to the ring for the semi-finals! CW: Coward! Announcer: Ladies and Gentlemen, this is a semi-final match-up scheduled for one fall. Making his way to the ring area. Lead by his manager the General. From Pensacola, Florida. Weighing in at 227 pounds. MISSILE!!! Announcer: And his opponent. Coming from the ring from the Darkside. Weighing in at 281 pounds. GRAVEDIGGER!!! EW: Missile has pinned both Armageddon in the upset of the tournament and the Warrior of Destrucity. CW: Meanwhile, the Gravedigger has chokeslamed Sean Champion and the Mauler. EW: And by the computer ratings, Gravedigger is ranked seventh in the BWC and Missile is way down at sixteenth! CW: Where and why do you come up with that information? EW: The bell rings and Missile charges the Gravedigger. He’s caught! Double underhook suplex! ONE!! TWO!! THREE!!!! CW: I TOLD YOU, ERIC! GRAVEDIGGER IS WAY TO STRONG FOR THAT RUNT! Announcer: The Winner of the match at 0:35. GRAVEDIGGER!!! EW: So the Gravedigger shows Missile how Armageddon felt in the first round with a one move match. CW: Missile has been grounded! There is a God! Announcer: Ladies and Gentlemen, this is the second semi-final match. Making his way to the ring. Lead by “Stunning” Stacy. From Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania. Weighing in at 283 pounds. THE ROWDY ONE! Announcer: And his opponent. From Ireland. Weighing in at 250 pounds. “BRAVEHEART” MITCHELL!! EW: So to recap, Rowdy One has pinned both Tank and Ezekial Hargreaves. CW: Where Mitchell has beat Beastmaster Bohley and Dillon Chandler to get here. Go ahead, Eric. Tell us where these guys are rated. EW: All right. The Rowdy One is rated number one in the BWC and Mitchell is rated seventh. CW: What a geek. EW: What’s that? CW: I said that was neat! EW: They lock up and Rowdy One goes right for a chokehold. CW: He may be cheered now but he’s still got the edge! EW: Mitchell turns it around, puts Rowdy on the turnbuckle. BELLY TO BACK SUPERPLEX! OH MY GOD! CW: What was that about copyright? EW: My apologies to Joey Styles at ECW. CW: How did you get this job, anyway? EW: Rowdy throws Mitchell outside. Rowdy follows and OHH!!! Mitchell meets the ringsteps. It looks like Rowdy reopened that cut on Mitchell’s head. CW: Unfortunately, this is where Mitchell got tough last time. EW: Both men re-enter the ring. Rowdy with a Perfect Plex! ONE! TWO! EW: Kickout. Rowdy comes back with a small package! ONE! TWO! THR.. EW: Another kickout! Rowdy up top now. FLYING ELBOW!!! ONE!! TWO!! THR.. EW: Mitchell’s in the ropes. What a break for Braveheart. CW: I’ll say. Nobody gets up from that elbow! Ask Ezekial! EW: Mitchell whips Rowdy to the ropes. FLYING CLOTHELINE! CW: I can’t believe this! EW: Mitchell whips him again to the ropes and hiptoss to the outside! Mitchell comes after him and OHH!! TO THE GUARDRAIL WITH ROWDY’S HEAD. TO THE RINGSTEPS! TO THE RINGPOST! Here comes Stacy behind him. Mitchell turns to confront her. SIDE SUPLEX BY ROWDY! Rowdy whips him to the RINGPOST! TO THE GUARDRAIL! TO THE GUARDRAIL AGAIN! WHAT A MATCH! CW: Rowdy with a PILEDRIVER!!! WHAT AN ATHLETE! HE KNOWS MITCHELL’S HEAD IS CUT OPEN AND HE IS GOING RIGHT AFTER IT! EW: Rowdy throws him back into the ring. Up to the top… FLYING KNEEDROP!!! ONE TWO THREE!!!! Announcer: The winner of the match at 7:47. THE ROWDY ONE! CW: You should have bet me, Williams! I’d be going to dinner after the event! IN TWO DAYS! (Bell rings) Announcer: And now, Ladies and Gentlemen, Michael Buffer! Buffer: Good evening, Ladies and Gentlemen. This is the MAIN EVENT OF THE EVENING! (crowd cheers) Buffer: All night, sixteen of the top wrestlers in e-fed history have gathered to fight just so they can say “I am the first BWC World Heavyweight Champion” We are now down to two. So, without any further delay. From the Gund Arena in beautiful Cleveland, Ohio and to the millions around the World Wide Web…. ARE YOU READY? (crowd grows louder) Buffer: ARE YOU READY? (crowd gets a little louder) Buffer: LET’S GET READY TO RUMMMMMBLLLLEEEEEEEEEEEE!!!! (The Funeral March plays over the speaker) Buffer: The first finalist has buried Sean Champion, The Mauler and The Missile. He is from the Darkside. Weighing in at 281 pounds. This is the GRAVEDIGGER!!!!!!!!! (The crowd is quiet in fear) Buffer: And his opponent. (The crowd erupts in cheering) Buffer: From Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania. He has pinned Tank, the massive Ezekial Hargreaves and Braveheart Mitchell. Weighing in at 283 pounds and lead to the ring by his manager “Stunning” Stacy. This is the ROWDY ONE!!!!!!!!! EW: It has all come down to this! Two men, namely Gravedigger and the Rowdy One, will fight in a no countount, no disqualification, no time limit match to determine the first BWC World Champion! CW: Wow! Are you redundant! How many times have the fans heard that this is the final match in the past two minutes? EW: Three, now! CW: What a putz! EW: There’s the bell and ROWDY ONE GOES RIGHT TO WORK. LEFT, RIGHT, LEFT, GUTWRENCH SUPLEX! HERE’S A PIN! ONE! TWO! EW: Kickout! CW: You didn’t think that was going to be it, did you? EW: Well, by the way some of these matches have gone, yes. CW: Not in the main event, it won’t! EW: We’ve had a match go under three minutes and one at thirty-seven seconds CW: And don’t forget, one that went over thirty minutes! EW: What we are trying to say is, stay tuned folks! We could have a champion at any time. CW: What I’m trying to say is you have time to get a sandwich! EW: Gravedigger throws Rowdy out and OHH NO!!! He lands on the guardrail! CW: Stacy won’t be satisfied tonight! EW: Gravedigger pulls Rowdy back in.. PERFECT PLEX!! ONE TWO EW: Kickout. He’s going for another one! ONE TWO EW: Another kickout! CW: Rowdy has too much inside to give in to a Perfect Plex. Gravedigger will have to use his chokeslam to get the belt. EW: SMALL PACKAGE BY ROWDY!! ONE TWO CW: Gravedigger isn’t going to be pinned by a small package. The specialty move will be what wins this belt for either one of these guys. Anything else is just a feel out process! EW: Good analysis! I’m sorry we can’t give you more of a description of what’s going on here but the action is fast; exchanging blows, reversals, ringposts, guardrails, steps. You name it, it’s being used CW: That’s why this is a no disqualification match! EW: FALLING SPLASH BY GRAVEDIGGER ONE TWO CW: NO! I told you, it will be the signature move that wins, Eric! EW: NORTHERN LIGHTS SUPLEX BY GRAVEDIGGER! ONE TWO CW: You’re getting too excited over nothing! EW: Gravedigger wants to take this fight outside. RINGPOST BY DIGGER! STEPS!! GUARDRAIL!! TOMBSTONE!! OH GOD NO!! ROWDY IS MOTIONLESS! ROWDY IS TOSSED BACK INSIDE. THE GRAVEDIGGER IS MAKING THE SIGN AND.. CW: CHOKESLAM!!!!!!! ONE TWO THREE!! EW: THERE HE IS! OUR FIRST BWC WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION! Announcer: Ladies and Gentlemen, the winner of the match at 14:53 and NEW BWC WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION! GRAVEDIGGER!!!!! EW: So there it is, Gravedigger is our first World Champion… until Monday night, I’m Eric Williams CW: And I’m Craig Wilson! Good night! IN TWO DAYS!
