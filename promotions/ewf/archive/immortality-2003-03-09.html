Immortality, 9 March 2003

9 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sunday March 9th 2003 WELCOME! Ric Taylor: LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, TONIGHT WILL BE A NIGHT TO REMEMBER… SUNDAY 9TH MARCH 2003 THE FIRST EVER ELITE WRESTLING FOUNDATION IMMORTALITY IS COMING TO YOU LIVE FROM A SOLD OUT RALPH WILSON STADIUM, ORCHARD PARK, NEW YORK! HELLO MY NAME IS RIC TAYLOR AND ALONG SIDE ME IS JIMMY AND WE WILL BE PROVIDING YOUR COMMENTARY FOR THIS EVENING! Jimmy: IT'S GONNA BE ONE HELL OF AN EVENT! John Dumas -vs- J "When Worlds Collide" hits the PA system and the crowd boo J out onto the stage. He holds up his arms with a smile on his face but receives no cheers or applauds. Mike Mercury: The following contest is scheduled for one fall and is a first round match in the Elite Wrestling Foundation Universal Championship Tournament. Making his way to the ring, weighing in at 278.5 pounds… J! J slides into the ring and awaits his opponent as "Crushed Like Fruit" by InMe begins to play. Mike Mercury: And his opponent, from Manchester, England and weighing in at 265 pounds… being accompanied by the Elite Wrestling Foundation Chairman, Baz… JOHN… DUMAS! John Dumas and Baz walk onto the stage and the crowd cheer. Ric Taylor: John Dumas is one big man… standing at 6'8"… I'm beginning to wonder if J even has the slightest of chances here tonight. Jimmy: Of course he does… what a dumb ass comment… The two walk down to the ring and John slides in leaving Baz on the outside. Ric Taylor: Well here we go… the first EVER Immortality match-up! Jimmy: My moneys on J in this match. Ric Taylor: What money…. The bell rings and John Dumas is the first to strike with a series of punches to the face of J and then follows though with an Irish whip into the ropes. John gets ready to deliver a back body drop but J jumps clean over the crouching John Dumas and grabs his legs. J pulls John down and rolls away. Jimmy: Well J had a bad start but now watch! He's gonna destroy John Ric Taylor: I disagree. It seems to me that John has this match. Although J's last move was impressive. Jimmy: Just you watch John and J both get up with ease. J rushes towards John and hits him with a high kick to the face. John stumbles slightly but regains himself easily. John and J tie up and John Dumas takes J down with a Russian leg sweep. John tries to stomp J who rolls away again. J kicks John's legs out and climbs to his feet. J victory rolls over John and stomps him on the chest. Ric Taylor: Wow what an impressive match! Jimmy: Yeah J sure is kicking ass out there tonight! John Dumas rolls around in pain as J climbs the turnbuckle and elbow drops…the mat. John had dodged the drop and wraps his legs around J in a chokehold. J frays around trying to get loose. John pulls J hard but J just grabs the ropes. J slides out of the ring and the ref begins the count out. (1)…. J climbs to his feet (2) he walks towards the ring (3) he climbs onto the apron and John Dumas meets him with a super kick. J manages to keep on the apron though and head buts John. He climbs onto the second rope and front flips over the third kicking John in the face as he goes. Ric Taylor: J's giving it his all out there but John seems to have an endless amount of punishment out there. Jimmy: Get real! J's dominating out there. John won't know what him soon enough John retaliates with kicks to the midsection and DDT. John makes the first cover of the match. (1)…. (2)…J kicks out and nails a big punch on John Dumas. Jimmy: See! J kicked out of there like John was dust! Ric Taylor: Are you kidding? That was to close to call! J uses the ropes to help him up and as John goes for a clothesline J jumps and nails a drop kick square on the face of John Dumas. J climbs up the turnbuckle but Dumas takes him off with a suplex. Both men stand up and grapple. J whips John into a corner. J kicks Dumas a few times and ties him in the tarantula. Ric Taylor: Now that HAS to hurt…. Jimmy: J! J! J! Ric Taylor: Shut up. You're embarrassing both of us After 4 seconds J lets go and John slides back into the ring from the apron. He grabs J and hip tosses him to the outside of the ring. John suicide dives over the ropes at J just hits it. John picks J up and hits a neck breaker. Ric Taylor: Neck breaker on the outside. Jimmy: Just you wait…J will hit back soon. Or I'm out of one hundred dollars! Ric Taylor: Wow you did actually bet. John climbs back into the ring as the ref counts (6). J stumbles to the apron (7) and slides in. J ducks another super kick and kicks Johns other leg. J makes the cover. (1)… (2)…John kicks out and reverses it into a small package pin. (1)... (2)…J reverses it so he's the one making the cover but John judo flips J off. Jimmy: J will win soon. Look how tired John is! Ric Taylor: They both are, Jimmy. John stumbles to his feet and J stands after a few seconds. John goes to kick J who grabs the foot and reverse DDT's John Dumas. Dumas rolls backwards and kicks J in the gut and sets him up for the crash landing. John hits it and covers. (1)...J kicks out. John lifts J up and plants a few punches on him. J grabs Dumas' fist on the fifth punch and performs the Japanese arm bar on John. Ric Taylor: Well executed Arm bar there. Jimmy: Yeah but way to close to the ropes. Dare I say that J slipped up? Ric Taylor: Yes John makes it to the ropes. Both contenders are now feeling the effects of the match and both are regaining them selves. J regains first and runs over to Dumas who greets him with a knee to the gut. John lifts J up and performs a half brain buster DDT. John covers. (1)... (2)…J gets his feet on the ropes. Jimmy: Go J! Ric Taylor: I admit that was close. John could of won that Both men stand up at the same time and start to trade punches. J gets the upper hand and lays on a punch-punch-kick combo. John Dumas almost falls over and J runs off the ropes and lands a spring board elbow drop onto John's spine. John however manages to keep standing. John stumbles around the ring and J climbs up a turnbuckle. J leaps off and missile drop kicks John over. J however doesn't cover he to falls over, exhausted. John crawls over to J and puts an arm over him. The ref doesn't even make the one count before J pushes it off and climbs to his feet. He lifts john up with him and the men grapple. Ric Taylor: I have a feeling this will be over soon… Jimmy: With J as the winner! Ric Taylor: It's hard to tell. John Dumas whips J against the ropes and goes for The Sudden Stop! Jimmy: ARGH! Ric Taylor: It's all over if he connects with this one! J counters and ducks behind John! John turns around gets hit with a spinning hell kick to the face! John Dumas crashes to the mat, out cold! J signals to the crowd and heads for the turnbuckle. Jimmy: The J Splash! J jumps up top and looks down upon his adversary… J SPLASH! The ref begins he count. (1)… (2)… (3)!!! Jimmy: WHAT DID I TELL YOU! Ric Taylor: J has defeated John Dumas and moved on to the next stage in this tournament! "When Worlds Collide" plays and J celebrates. Baz slides in the ring to help John Dumas but J doesn't seem to like him interrupting his moment. J pushes Baz and tells him to get out of the ring. Jimmy: Yeah! This is J's moment! Get lost! Baz laughs at J and pushes him out of the way and walks to John Dumas but J runs at Baz and drop kicks the back of his knee, bringing him to the floor. J hits an axe kick on Baz, who rolls around in pain. J continues his celebration but his music cuts out and "Come As You Are" by Nirvana begins to play! Ric Taylor: What the… "The Turnbuckle" Jay Krazy appears on the stage and runs down to the ring! Jimmy: What the hell is he doing here? Jay slides into the ring and squares up to J and begins to shout things at him. J laughs and goes for a punch but Jay Krazy blocks it and begins hitting right hands on J. Ric Taylor: Jay Krazy obviously doesn't agree with J disrespecting the Chairman and John Dumas! Jimmy: I ain't got nothing to do with him! SECURITY! GET THIS MAD MAN OUT OF HERE! "The Turnbuckle" grabs J by the head and throws him over the top rope to the floor! Jimmy: What?! Ric Taylor: Ladies and gentlemen, we will be back after the commercial break! :::COMMERCIAL BREAK::: Psyco -vs- Twisted Angel "My Generation" hits the PA system and the crowd boo Psyco out onto the stage. He holds up his arms with a smile on his face but receives no cheers or applauds. Mike Mercury: The following contest is scheduled for one fall and is a first round match in the Elite Wrestling Foundation Universal Championship Tournament. Making his way to the ring, from Orlando, Florida, weighing in at 221 pounds… PSYCO! Psyco slides into the ring and awaits his opponent as "Coma" by Guns 'n' Roses begins to play. Mike Mercury: And his opponent, weighing in at 270 pounds… TWISTED… ANGEL! Twisted Angel walks onto the stage and the crowd cheer. Ric Taylor: Another match up for tonight's immortality. Jimmy: I'd say Psyco has this match. Ric Taylor: I'm gonna disagree with you there and say Twisted Angel has it The bell rings as Twisted Angel lands on some sucker punches to Psyco and a kick to the jaw sending Psyco down in that mini combo. Jimmy: Oh no! Psyco is down already! Ric Taylor: What did I say? Twisted lands a few elbow drops and kicks. Psyco rolls away and stammers to his feet only to be met with a super kick to the gut and a face crusher. Psyco rolls out of the ring and gives Angel the finger. Ric Taylor: That's not exactly the smart thing to do when your getting beaten like a pillow at a 4th grade sleep over! Jimmy: huh? Ric Taylor: It's a traumatising memory Angel climbs the turnbuckle and performs a perfect flying neck breaker. Twisted Angel throws Psyco back into the ring and slides in after him. Psyco lands his first hit of the match with a kick to the gut of Twisted angel. Psyco tries again but angel grabs the foot. Psyco uses the chance to land an Enziguri kick to the skull of Angel who falls. Jimmy: Angel is finally down! Ric Taylor: Hmm maybe I was wrong about Psyco Psyco goes for the stomp but Angel grabs the foot and dragon flips Psyco to the ground and covers. (1)… (2)…. (3) Ric Taylor: Or not! Jimmy: NO Twisted Angel won! Ric Taylor: your prediction was wrong Jimbo Jimmy: Don't call me that Cut to Announcement Table Ric Taylor: What a match that was! Jimmy: You're not wrong, Ric! Ric Taylor: Hold on… Jimmy, I'm getting word in my ear that there has been some sort of action backstage while we were watching this match and we apparently have footage of the goings on! During the Last Match "The Turnbuckle" Jay Krazy is in the EWF bar when he in comes Blood Man! Blood Man: Hey! What the hell do you think you're doing getting involved in the match earlier tonight? Don't you think you should have been concentrating on our match seeing as you're gonna need as much help as you can get! Jay Krazy: Seriously… shut up! You ain't gonna last two minutes in the ring with me. Blood Man: Oh yeah? Well why don't we just get it on right here, right now instead of waiting for the match?! Blood Man throws a punch and Jay Krazy and knocks him to the floor but Jay gets straight back up and kicks him in the gut! Blood Man tries to get his breath and has his head slammed against the bar by Jay! Ric Taylor: A fight has broke out! Blood Man elbows Jay Krazy in the face, making Jay stumble backwards. Blood Man grabs a stool and throws it at Jay Krazy. Jay ducks out of the way and dives at Blood Man! The two begin rolling around on the floor throwing random punches at each other! Jimmy: Oh my God! As the two continue their brawl, several police officers run and split the two superstars up. They are dragged outside and the camera follows and watches as Blood Man and Jay Krazy are both placed into separate police cars! Jimmy: They've been arrested?! Ric Taylor: It looks that way, Jimmy… but I got one question for you… Jimmy: What about their match?! Ric Taylor: Exactly! Back to the Ring "Dive" by Nirvana plays and Baz walks out onto the stage. The crowd cheer at the EWF Chairman who holds his arms above his head. In one of his hands he holds a microphone. Baz signals for the music to cut out and it does so. Baz: HELLO EWF FANS! The crowd cheers and begins to chant EWF! Baz: Now… it's typical that something that is supposed to go right never does… hence the punch up you have all just witness between "The Turnbuckle" Jay Krazy and Blood Man. Now, due to this minor problem I am in a position to postpone the match between Jay Krazy and Blood Man until next week on Immortality. Thank you all for listen and enjoy the remainder of tonight's show! "Dive" hits the arena and Baz walks out to a cheering crowd! :::COMMERCIAL BREAK::: Darren "Big 9" Sin -vs- Turbulence Turbulence walks out onto the stage as "Spanish Main" by The Coral echoes around the arena. The crowd respond with an even mixture of cheers and boos. Mike Mercury: The following contest is scheduled for one fall and is a first round match in the Elite Wrestling Foundation Universal Championship Tournament. Introducing first, from London, England, weighing in at 267 pounds… TURBULENCE! Turbulence runs to the ring and slides in as his music fades and "Don't Push Me" begins to play. Mike Mercury: And his opponent, from The Bronx, New York, weighing in at 232 pounds… DARREN… "BIG 9"… SIN! Darren Sin walks slowly and calmly to the ring and climbs in, not taking his eyes off Turbulence. The bell sounds and the match begins Ric Taylor: Here we go, match two of the first IMMORTALITY! Jimmy: This seems like a pretty even match-up Ric Taylor: yeah, gonna be a good one. Darren starts before the bell by kicking Turbulence. The bell rings. Darren Sin lays on a combo of punches and kicks. Turbulence is thrown around the ring like a rag doll by the Big 9. Jimmy: Darren Sin off to a blazing start! Ric Taylor: Turbulence can't get a strike in! Turbulence eventually manages to get in a drop kick and a sweep kick on Sin. Darren stumbles and trips. Turbulence lays on a stomp-kick-stomp combination followed by an elbow drop. Darren grabs Turbulence's head and smashes it against the mat before he has a chance to stand from his elbow drop. Ric Taylor: Oh man, that had to hurt! Jimmy: The arena shook with one. Ric Taylor: I think the big 9 may have this in the bag! Darren stands up and grabs Turbulence's legs and spins him around. He throws Turbulence across the ring. The big 9 taunts to the crowd and Turbulence runs up behind him and nails a corkscrew DDT. Turbulence covers with a lateral press. (1)…Sin kicks out. Darren and Turbulence stand at the same time trading punches as they both clamber up. Darren Sin gets the upper hand by laying on three punches in a row. Turbulence lays on the old one-two but gets whipped by Darren "the big 9" sin. Turbulence uses the force of the ropes to help him land a massive one-foot drop kick. Darren hits the mat with massive force and Turbulence covers. (1)…. (2)….Darren kicks out. Jimmy: Another close pin Ric Taylor: Oh man Turbulence runs off the ropes as Sin stands and runs right into the arms of Darren who lands a twirling back buster. Darren climbs to the top rope and lands an axe handle smash to the gut of Turbulence. He picks Turbulence off the mat and lands the packin' heat. Turbulence makes it to the ropes ok. though and slides under Darren Sin's legs. He flips up and nails Darren on the skull with an elbow smash. Darren falls and Turbulence covers. (1)…Sin kicks out. Turbulence picks Darren Sin up by the head and the big 9 takes the chance to punch Turbulence in the gut and drop a single handed face buster. Jimmy: Face Buster! Ric Taylor: Darren Sin is once again in control! The big 9 returns to the stomp-kick-stomp combo and doesn't hold out. Turbulence starts rolling around in pain. Turbulence slides out of the ring and grabs hold of Darren Sin's leg and drags him out with him. This backfires and Darren lands a kick right on Turbulence's face just as the ref begins the count out. (1)…Darren lands a few punches in. (2)…Turbulence grabs Darren's wrist. (3)...Turbulence drags Darren Sin over to the side railings. (4)…He smashes the big 9's skull over the railings and Sin begins to bleed. Ric Taylor: He just busted him wide open! Jimmy: Turbulence is taking control! (5)…Turbulence whips Sin to the ring apron and slides him under before performing a second rope-senton dive on Darren. Turbulence lateral presses. (1)… (2)…. (Thr)...Sin just kicks out. Both men climbs to there feet and Turbulence lays on a few punches to Darren "the big 9" Sin and climbs to the top rope. Turbulence leaps off in an attempt at a flying spear and hits Darren Sin dead on! Jimmy: Oh man! Ric Taylor: What a high impact move from the amateur turned pro wrestler! Jimmy: Its gotta be over now Turbulence holds up his hands and signals the end. He pulls "Big 9" to his feet… CRASH! DARREN SIN IS OUT COLD! Turbulence covers… (1)… (2)… (3)! Ric Taylor: And that's all she wrote ladies and gentlemen! The Coral hits the PA system and Turbulence has advanced to the next stage of the tournament! Ric Taylor: Well folks, that's all we have for you tonight! Be sure to join us next week here on Immortality! Good night! Jimmy: See ya!
