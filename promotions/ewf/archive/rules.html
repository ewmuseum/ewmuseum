EWF (Mar 2003) Card Archive

Rules

April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction i - Elite Wrestling Foundation is owned by [name withheld], and no attempt should be made to claim otherwise. ii - No graphics should be taken from the site unless using the main banner for a link to our website in your roleplay that may be hosted on another site. Behaviour i - As an e-wrestling community in it's own, we hope you can work in an environment where you can agree to disagree. We don't like it when there are childish squabbles and feuds between fellow wrestlers. Yes, we all have our differences, but please, don't bring that kind of attitude onto the e-wrestling scene. ii - Racism is not tolerated in Elite Wrestling Foundation. Whether it's in a roleplay, on the Out Of Character board or in an e-mail to a fellow star. If found to be making racial comments, it will result in your immediate firing and being brought into shame. This also includes any homosexual comments. Television i - EWF's main show is Immortality, aired on Sundays. A card usually consists of 2 lower-card matches 3 mid-card matches an upper-card match and the main event. ii - EWF has a Pay Per View event every month, usually on the third week. Each year, the Pay-Per-View names will be the same as the year before... So July's PPV in 2003 was 'WrestleFest 2003.' So in 2004 WrestleFest in July will be entitled 'WrestleFest 2004.' PPV matches usually consist of 3 lower-card matches, 4 mid-card matches, 2 upper-card matches and the main event. iii - Matches will have a Roleplay Limit. The Roleplay Limit will determine the maximum amount of roleplays allowed to be posted for that card by each member. The maximum roleplays for Immortality will be 3. The Roleplay Limit for Pay-Per-Views will be 5 roleplays. Titles i - EWF World Title: The top title in Elite Wrestling Foundation today. Usually aimed at the top roleplayers and contended at every Pay Per View event, and usually once a month on Immortality. ii - EWF Intercontinental Title: The title for upper-card superstars. This title is a stepping stone for stars who have worked hard in EWF, and as a reward, get elevated to the main event. Defended at every PPV and on Immortality occasionally. iii - EWF Title: When defended on Immortality at least twice a month and at Pay Per View events, the World Television Title is an undercard title. Title changes mostly occur with this title. iiii - EWF World Tag Team Titles: The top tag teams in Elite Wrestling Foundation battle for the World Tag Team Titles. These belts are defended usually once a month on Immortality and at Pay Per View events.
