Real Name: Merce Gimmick Name: Merce Height: 7'0" Weight: 330 lbs HomeTown: Louisville, KY Allignment: Heel Wrestling Style: Power, Hardcore & Brawler Entrance Theme: Your Disease - Saliva Stable: Generation X Finishers: (1) Merce Bomb *Throwing Elevated Powerbomb* (2) Merce Gauntlet *Gauntlet Strike* (Merce takes a gauntlet from under the ring and hits the opponent with it) Signature Moves: (1) Full Nelson Slam (2) Big Boot (3) Chokeslam (4) Snap DDT (5) Powerbomb Trademark Moves: (1) Clothesline (2) Chair Shot (3) Lou Thesz Press (4) Facecrusher (5) Hard Chop (6) Samoan Drop (7) Stalling Brainbuster Entrance Description: The lights go dark and an explosion occurs. Your Disease starts & Merce walks to the ring posing for the crowd or attacks the opponent.
Ring Attire: Medium/Dark Skin, Black Pants, occasional black tanktop, sunglasses, golden-brown medium-long hair, small mustache, black boots.
Biography: Been in the wrestling business for quite some time. Well-known for his Lion Den Death match against Tetsuo and Shiva.
