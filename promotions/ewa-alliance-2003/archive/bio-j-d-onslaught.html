Biography sheet: J.D. Onslaught

c. June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Name: J. D. Onslaught Height: 6'1'' Weight: 240 Hometown: Central IL Theme Music: "Let's Do This Now" by KoRn Alignment: Face Wrestling Style: Unrelenting (suicidal) flying brawler Finishing Move: Annihilator While holding oppenent up for an vertical suplex, Onslaught jumps into the air, spinning 180 degrees before dropping into a brainbuster.
Secondary Finishing Move: Too Much Oppression (TMO) With opponent downed, Onslaught climbs the ropes and faces the crowd, then lands a corkscrew backflip splash on the adversary.
Signature Moves: Flyin High(Alternate Finisher)- Top rope dropkick to adjoining corner, foreign objects encouraged.
Suicide Strech- Reverse STF U R Done- Snap DDT, great set up for TMO. Hold On- Ties opponent in ropes, rebounds off of opposite side and spears them, usually ending outside of the ring.
Frequently Used Moves: Snap suplex Varied Head Submissions (For Annihilator) Bulldog Falling Jawbreaker Closed-Fist Head Shots High Dropkick Reverse Calf Clothesline Fisherman's Suplex T-Bone Suplex Aimed Elbow Drop German Suplex Pin Appearance: Like Jeff Hardy. Usually has a black trenchcoat on over a White T-Shirt w/Sagged dank green Khaki's. Hair is mostly colored reddish-black.
Entrance: "Let's Do This Now" Starts to play with the Bagpipes echoing loudly in the arena as the crowd goes nuts. Slowly the lights dim out, the bagpipes end, and the guitars explode along with tons of spotlights shining red and blue as J.D. Onslaught walks out from the back. He stops on stage and throws his arms, headbangs a little, then begins towards the ring. He slides in and climbs the turnbuckle with a Devil horns in one hand before hopping down ready for the match.
