Real Name: Jonathan Lee Gimmick Name: "The Extreme Icon" Jonathan Lee Height: 6'1" Weight: 215 lbs Age: 30 years old HomeTown: Tokyo, Japan Allignment: Face Wrestling Style: He more ground based now but will take to the air at times. Still used weapons and ladders alot though.
Entrance Theme: Linkin Park - Drag Finishers: (1) Icon Impression *Mod. STO* (Lee hooks his right arm over the persons left shoulder. He grabs them by the back of the head. he leans back. He fall foward, while kicking out their legs, driving he back of this head into the mat)
(2) Blood Shot Eyes *Superkick* (Jonathan hooks his leg to hit the opponent right under their jaw) Signature Moves: (1) Kamikaze *Top Rope Shinning Wizard* (Lee jumps off the top rope and hits a Shinning Wizard) (2) Extreme Tapout *Haas of Pain with an Anklelock* (3) Insane 450 *Springboard 450 Splash* (4) Rolling Brainbusters (Three in a row) (5) Triangle Hold Trademark Moves: (1) Dropkick to Knee (2) Reverse DDT into Dragon Sleeper (3) STF (4) Tornado DDT (5) Cartwheel Moonsault (6) Top Rope Missle Dropkick (7) Martial Art Kicks combo (8) Enzugiri (9) Snapmare followed by a Dropkick to opponent lowerback (10) Backbreaker (11) Uppercut followed by Clothesline (12) Roll Through Dropkick (Lee runs at the guy, he rolls under a clothesline, the guy turns around and Lee pops up with a dropkick. Normally follows it with his taunt)
(13) Swinging Neckbreaker Entrance: Blue and Green lights light up the arena. Drag starts to play through the arena. Green pyros go off in fornt of the entrance. Through the smoke, walks out Lee. he runs around on stage, pumping up the fans. he runs down the ramp and sldies in the ring and hops on the nearest turnbuckle. He does his E taunt and hops down and does in in the other three corners.
Appearance: Black hair with red tips. Its short and is spiked up. He wears a black and blue shirt, black wrist tape aroudn his wrists and hands. he also has on camo pants which are either
1. Green and blue 2. Black and Green 3. Black and red with some black boots. Biography: Born in Toyko, he lost his mother at an eariler age. His father had him taked up martal arts. Jonathan trained till he was 15 when he started watching wrestling. His father got a job in San Fran so Jonathan had to move. That is where he found a school started training to become a wrestler. He has broken many of his own bones but he has found ways to get around breaking bones now. He also met his girlfriend April in one of the feds he was in. He also found his best friend, Bouncer in VXWF. There they became tag Champions but under different names. back then they where known as Hardcore(Jono lee) and Punk(Bouncer). Jonathan dropped the hardcore name and started using his real name. He has been in many feds in the past year from XPW to WWO to WXW and now EWA. Well after putting his job on the line against Richard Bison. he lost and is now retired and became an agent. He has also become the GM of Shockers for EWA. Lee got his job back buy beating Bison. He then went on a roll and almost won the EWA world Title. he formed CXI with Rage and Scoot Williams. He then formed CXI with the Powers that Be to form the Coalition and also won the EWA eXtreme Championship. he won the WWO Heavyweight title from Bison. He held it for a month till losing it. he has a lightbulb tube cabin match with Dragonw hich he won. he then called it quits and retired. But now he is back in the game, and ready to go.
Title History: VXWF tag champion 1x(Punk aka Bouncer) VXWF Hardcore Champion 2x IPW IC Champion 1x WWO Tag team Champion 2x (Once with Dragon, Once with Nathan Irvine) WWO InTeRnEt Champion 1x (Beat Camaro and last one to hold the title) EWA eXtreme Champion 1x (beat Cerberus) WWO Heavyweight Champion (beat Camaro & Bison)
