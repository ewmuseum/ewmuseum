Biography sheet: David Boyce

c. June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Real Name: David Boyce Gimmick Name: David"The Widowmaker"Boyce Height: 6'3" Weight: 248 lbs HomeTown: Tempe, Arizona Allignment: Neutral with more Face qualities. Wrestling Style: Technical, Power & High Flyer Entrance Theme: Head for the Barricade by Limp Bizkit Finishers: (1) Boyce Driver (Double Leghook Fisherman's DDT) (2)The Vice '04 (David starts the move by applying a fujiwara armbar from a standing position. With the opponent now on the ground on there stomach. David hooks the opponents arm into position as if he was going for the crippler crossface. David proceeds to hook the opponent into a modified Dragon sleeper like hold while keeping the opponents arm locked in between his legs.)
Signature Moves: (1) Widowmaker Splash *(Springboard) Pheonix Splash* (Can be used as a finisher) (2)The Vice *Modified Camel Clutch* (David applies a sort of Camel Clutch/Full Nelson hold on the opponent. He then knees on the opponent back & pull back to apply more pressure)
(3) Second Rope Spinebuster (4) Pumphandle Sit-Out Facebuster (5)The DBD Picks opponent up into an electric chair drop. Takes opponents right arm with his left hand and holds onto opponents head falls forward driving the opponent into the ground.~See link at bottom~
(6)Widowmaker DDT Springboard pheonix splash swinging DDT. (7) The Hit Springboard Moonsault. Also done with Wolfe. When done with Wolfe he puts his foot in Wolfes hands that are cupped. Moonsaults off and lands on opponent. Sometimes done to the outside to standing opponent.
Trademark Moves: (1) T-bone Suplex (2) Clothesline (3) Throat Thrusts (4) Reverse DDT (5) Top Rope Elbow Drop (6) Missle Dropkick (7) Variations of DDT (8) Fujiwara armbar (9) Many different submissions affecting the neck,arm and back area. Entrance Description: The lights in the arena go black and nothing can be seen. Head for the Barricade begins to play.9 seconds into the song when "Fight" can be heard the lights flash on in a bright blue color and an explosion occurs on stage.Blue mist begins to rise from the stage.David walks onto the stage with his Internet championship around his waist and his Tag belt on his shoulder. After a second Pyper walks out behind him. He looks out at his fans and does his trademark pose. After a few seconds of posing he walks down the ramp with Pyper following shortly behind. He gets on the apron then the second rope of the closest corner and poses again while Pyper climbs the stairs into the ring and points to Boyce. Dave hops into the ring and unstraps his Internet title from around his waist. He smiles as he hands his belts to Pyper who heads out to stand in his corner.
Ring Attire: Looks- (The Messiah) Has short dark brown hair which he spikes up. Has a goatee which is his natural hair color(orange brown.)Wears black cargo shorts which go to a little lower than his knees. Wears a black t-shirt which has a red flame design with " No pain" underneath the flame in red also. Has a superman symbol with a backwards D and a B instead of the S on his upper left shoulder intwined in barbedwire.Wraps his wrists with white tape.No elbowpads.Wears black boots and kneepads.Tatoo of "The Widowmaker" across his shoulderblades in a flaming tribal design.
Trademark Pose: Makes two fists and puts his pinkies up. Raises his hands above his head. (Think John Cena) It makes a "W" for Widowmaker. Always does this pose before hitting the Boyce Driver. Will do the pose while the opponent is turned to him or staggered or getting up off the ground.
Other: Is over with the fans like Eddie Geurrero is in WWE. The fans love him right now and eat out of his palm when it comes to him speaking in the ring. Likes to play off them and feeds off them.
