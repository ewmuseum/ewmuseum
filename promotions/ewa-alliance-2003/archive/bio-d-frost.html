Biography sheet: D-Frost (Dalis Hyral)

c. November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Real Name: Dalis Hyral Gimmick Name: D-Frost Height: 6’2” Weight: 235 lbs Age (Optional): 25 years old (October 18th, 1978) Hometown: Montréal, Canada Alignment: Heel Wrestling Style: Offensive Technician/Brawler Entrance Theme: Requiem for a Dream (Remix) – Howard Shore Stable: Generation X Finishers: 1-DXtreme Tiger Driver ’91: Dalis kicks the opponent to the mid section and bend him over. He then hooks both arms and lifts the opponent vertically, so he is upside down. Dalis then drops to his knees, driving the opponent’s either on his head/shoulders area.
2-Frost Bane ¾ Facelock Bulldog: Dalis turns his back to his opponent while he warps his hand around the opponent’s head, and then drops down to the mat, driving the opponent face first down.
Signatures: 1-Deep Impact (a spin back kick to the face, usually used as a quick turn table during a match)
2-Tiger Suplex (either released or with Pin) 3-Frost DDT (a stiff DDT followed by a kick flip) 4-Dragon Sleeper 5-Superstar Press ( a Shooting Star Press) Frequently Used: 1-Spinning heel kick 2-Roundhouse kick to the head 3-Running enzuiguri 4-Running low dropkick to an opponent hanged upside down on the turnbuckle) 5-Stiff clothesline to a running opponent (followed by a c*cky pin) 6-Multiple kick to the ribs followed by a jumping kick to the side of the head, to an opponent in the corner)
7-Snapmare followed by either a reverse chinlock or a flipping next snap) 8-SleeperHold 9-Spinbuster 10-Spinning backdrop (picks the opponent for a back drop and turns between 180 and 360 degree around before dropping)
11-Snap suplex 12-Legsweep Inverted DDT (set up for an inverted DDT, but sweeps one of the opponent’s leg with his own as he hits the move)
13-Missile Dropkick 14-Diving Leg drop 15-c*cky Pin Entrance Description: The lights start to fade out for a moment. Blue spotlights start illuminating the arena as Requiem for a Dream starts playing. Blue pyros shoots out of the stage forming an X and Dalis walks out on stage. Dalis walks down the ramp taunting the crowd and then rolls in the ring. He climbs on the turnbuckle and opens his arms to the side to show off his body and taunts the crowd some more. He jumps back in the ring and gets ready for the match.
Appearence: Short dark blond hair, and blue eyes. A good tanned skin and a very good body, with well defined muscles. A burning “X” tattoo on the left shoulder.
Ring attire: 1-Black martial arts pants with blue flames at the bottom up to the knees. Black wrestling boots with blue flames around and blue laces. Black armbands and gloves with blue burning designs on it.
2-Black denim cargo pants with a few belts hanging from one point to another, with a burning X design on the right leg side. Black and blue boots, and black leather grappling gloves
Street Attire: 1-Black expensive pants with black shoes and a black expensive shirt, left open to show his torso. He usually wear a gold chain around his neck, with a spiky X at the end of it.
2-Black Denim pants with black sport shoes. A white tank top and sometimes a black leather trench coat (for bad weather) and sunglasses. Gold chain with a spiky X at the end of it.
Biography(Optional): Born in Montreal Canada, Dalis always was the center of attention wherever he went. Hightly charismatic and talented in martial arts, Dalis won various martial arts championships and amateur wrestling tournaments in high school. At the age of 20 years old, he began his pro wrestling career, and skyrocketed to the top in a matter of months and made many friends during his career, and much more enemies. The only thing known about Dalis than can surpass his skills would without a doubt be his c*cky attitude. With many championships on his career of every kind, from hardcore to world champion, Dalis proved that he got what it takes to make it big in this business, and decided to take his shot in Hollywood. After many months away from the EWA, his first big Hollywood role in GunSlingers is a success. Dalis is now back in the EWA and ready to prove to anyone that he still got the guts and the skills to make it to the top.
Title History: BWS: National Champion wXw/EXPW: Hardcore Champion, Darkside Champion, Tag Team Champion (with Chazz Irvine) Xtreme(World) Champion
WWO: Hardcore Champion, Internet Champion EWA: World Heavyweight Champion Misc. Stuff: Dalis is a very c*cky man, and once he gains control of the match, he will lose much time taunting his opponent or the crowd, or show off.
Dalis’s main target is the head, so he will kick his opponent to the point they can’t stand up anymore and mix a few submission or wreslting move to weaken his opponent.
Dalis loves to copy his opponent’s finishin move and follow with the c*cky pin. He usually let them kick out easily only to finish them with the DXtreme.
