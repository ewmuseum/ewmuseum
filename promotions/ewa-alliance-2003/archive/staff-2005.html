EWA (Alliance, 2003–10) — Staff

Staff: the owner, CEO, commentators and interviewers

c. May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Owner of the Extreme Wrestling Alliance Eric Davis started in the wrestling business as the replacement manager of Richard Bison in the wXw. There, he and Bison have had a short success, but became very good friends. After the wXw closed down, he and Bison went to the RWO, but in the mean time, Davis was arranging a loan at the bank to start his own wrestling organisation. After recieving the money he needed to start, he contracted most wrestlers from the WWO and brought them over to the EWA. After a week or so, he began to hire new talent and so far, has made stars out of them all. Eric Davis currently has resigned from active duty as the CEO of the EWA. He's still around to help the people backstage, but he won't be seen on TV anymore.
C.E.O. of the Extreme Wrestling Alliance Jared Stark took over from Eric Davis is active C.E.O. of the Extreme Wrestling Alliance. Jared a half english and half french and that can be heard when he is speaking. Many wrestlers in the EWA find it very annoying that is hard to understand.
Assistant to the C.E.O. of the Extreme Wrestling Alliance Gayla is an experienced business woman who knows how to get what she wants. Doing most of the paper work for the EWA, she gets access to about everything concerning the federation and the employees. She sees herself as a figure of great authority and does not fear to use her powers to be respected.
Commentators Knows every move in the book and is a great play by play commentator. The Mark was a wrestler in the old wXw, he was know as The Enforcer and he became the Commish at one point. He was GM of wXw: Darkside, which was a place for the new comers to make names for themselves before becoming big stars. Mark then had an accident and broke his left arm. Mark disapeared from the wXw, until Ehrgeiz re-opened it under a new name, EXPW : Ehrgeiz's Xtreme Pro Wrestling. He came back as part of the greatest broadcast team ever known in the world of wrestling, with non other than his best friend, Boutch. He thinks that he can do every move performed way better. Many disagree.
Interviewers Paul knows what he is doing and does it well. He is accident prone and likes to eat junk food, but still works out from time to time, but it doesn't show. He really dislikes his collegue Belinda, who from time to time, manages to beat him to an interview. Paul always has Andrew with him, his favorite camera man.
She will do just about anything to get a scoop. She knows that being a woman is an advantage and she uses it to her full potential. Usually this angers Paul McKane alot, her collegue. However, Belinda never gets involved with any of the staff members or wrestlers, but likes to make them think that she does.
Camera man A pretty weird guy, but still a good camera man. Still thinks that he is in the hippie age and sometimes says stuff that absolutely make no sense at all. Andrew always goes where ever Paul goes, even if that means trouble. Mostly films in places where he isn' wanted too and gets beaten up for that, but he knows how to take a hit, but usually sues that attacker for harrasment. Currently, Andrew works along with Alexander as a part time super hero.
Gus is the camera man of Belinda. He follows her everywhere that she goes to and does just about everything that she says. He is also a hardcore rocker and likes to stage dive, usually when he dives of a stage, nobody cathes him. His Favorite saying is "Rock On!".
Ring Announcer Without a doubt the best in what he does. Announcing. He started his carreer in the WWO. After the WWO closed he took some time off for his self, but when he soon heard that teh EWA was opening, he contacted Eric Davis to see if there was an opening. After talking with Eric Davis, he got himself a job at the EWA
