Biography sheet: Brett Cotter

c. November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Name: Brett Cotter Height: 6’7 Weight: 277 Hometown: Green Bay, WI Theme Music: “The Next Episode” Dr. Dre/Snoop Dogg Alignment: Face Wrestling Style: Power :Very strong and technical. Uses brute force mixed with planned moves and setups and loves to work weaknesses down
Finishing Move: The Cheese Grater Finishing Move Description: (from the Original Pakdaddy) Brett picks the stunned opponent from the mat and drapes the opponents legs over Brett’s shoulders with
the opponents back to his stomach, head facing the mat. Then Brett jumps into the air and come down on the mat onto his knees driving the opponents head to
the mat then rolling on top of them for the cover. Signature Moves: Triple German Suplex’s Spear Reverse Powerbomb Top rope Death Drop Frequently Used Moves: Flying Clothesline Boston crab Baseball Slide Takedown (runs, slides at opponents feet knocking him down as he slides past by grabbing their ankles) roundhouse kick to the face Turnaround uppercut punch to the face Appearance: A well chiseled 6'6 framed Caucasian. Brett has dark hair that he wears up and arranged in a haphazard style, ala Colin Farrell. He has dark brown
eyes that possess a gloss to them that can change from compassionate to cold and deadly in half a heartbeat. Thick legged with a rise in the shoulder muscles
similar to Brock Lesnar (yea I miss pelt it). He has one tattoo on the left of his chest, A Wisconsin badgers W in black over the number 87. Fond of wearing
black and tan khakis and baseball style Wisconsin and Packers jersey open around a colored t-shirt. Along with Blue Oakley M-frames.
Entrance: The lights dim then fade to nothing “The Next Episode” begins to play over the PA bringing the crowd to its feet. Scenes of The Legend Pakdaddy play
showing him hoisting his numerous world Championship belts into the air, along with crushing blows on unlucky opponents mixed in with scenes from his movies and his pro football career up until the song reaches The refrain at which point the screen goes blank until the words “the Next Episode” play and the screen
explodes with scenes of Pakdaddy jr. in college and in the ring. MIS: Background: The son of former pro wrestler Anthony Cotter (Pakdaddy)Wild Wrestling Federation Champion and record holder for longest defense. CWA
champion, NWWA Champion and Se-Interfed Tag Team Champ): and his wife Jennifer Love Hewitt. Brett is a rookie wrestler in all aspects. He's a Senior at
University Of Wisconsin who graduates in May with a Philosophy degree. A member of the football team he plays Middle Linebacker like His dad did for the
Packers. He lead the team in Tackles his Junior year one shy of a school record. He's a graduate of the Canadian Bacon school of Wrestling in Montreal, Canada (this was a stable partner of Pakdaddy's in 2 of the above feds).
Brett has led a privileged life by most accounts, his father an ex Football player/Actor/ And wrestler Brett has not gone wanting in life. His father has made him earn everything he's gotten in life, from his Soon to be built house in Madison, to his 2002 Dodge Viper. Brett has learned the working and earning mean far more then having anything handed to you.
Brett Has made his decision to follow his father's footsteps into wrestling instead of football, due in large part to an already once scoped left knee that cost him much of his Senior year of eligibility. The excitement of watching his father's Classic performances against Mangor and others as a child has enchanted him for years.
Currently single, but tied in rumors to Avril Lavrigne and once rumored to have dated Paris Hilton although there was never proof outside photos at events and denials by both.
Brett is a hard charging, and Pride driven All American Child.
