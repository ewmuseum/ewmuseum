EWA (Alliance, 2003–10) — Rules

The official Rules of the Extreme Wrestling Alliance

c. March 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Rules Sign up Staff Roster Champions Hall of Fame Forum Shockers Card Shockers Results Shockers Predictions PPV Cards PPV Results PPV Predictions Posted by :: Bison :: 22nd August 2007 Welcome to the EWA! Please read the rules before you sign up to the EWA. View Rules The official Rules of the Extreme Wrestling Alliance This guide gives you the entire run of the EWA including Matches Allowed. You should read through this before applying.
The EWA does NOT believe in the "3 strikes and you're out" rule. The punishment scale is simple:
THE FIRST TIME YOU BREAK A RULE...YOU WILL BE SUSPENDED FOR ONE WEEK! (IF YOU HOLD A TITLE, IT WILL BE STRIPPED FROM YOU!) THE SECOND TIME YOU BREAK A RULE...YOU WILL BE FIRED! (NO EXCEPTIONS!) The EWA front office demands respect! This means, when a problem arises, you don't post on any of the boards. You email or IM the Administrator or any of the people in the creative staff. If you disrespect the front office, YOU WILL BE FIRED!
The EWA front office will not tolerate trouble makers, complainers, sore losers, and the like here. You people piss off those that love e-fedding, taking the fun out of it. So if you become one of them, YOU WILL BE FIRED! If you already fit into this category, don't bother applying.
The EWA front office will not stand for racism, sexist remarks, or the likes in your posts. Use them, and YOU WILL BE FIRED! The determination of the offensive degree of a post is up to the Creative Staff.
1. Joining a. Just write down your wrestler’s bio and post in the Roster page, also send a pm to any of the Creative Staff members to notify of your arrival. If you failed to send in a pm to any of the staff member’s your bio will be deleted it.
2. Qualifications a. Only original (fake) wrestlers will be accepted into the EWA. Do not go overboard with the height/weight of your character. If you do so, you will be asked to alter the stats of your wrestler before he/she can be accepted.
3. Storylines/Angles a. Storylines and angles will be decided by both the front office and the wrestlers involved with the storyline/angle.
b. Upon joining, your character might be thrown into an active storyline/angle in order to make things interesting.
4. Roleplaying a. You MUST roleplay at least once a week - (that's not asking much) - if you are scheduled for a match. The front office knows all about time constraints, so there is no set number of lines per roleplay. But try to make them somewhat lengthy...at least interesting to read.
b. Roleplays will be judged by the front office, who will look at the following 4 factors: creativity, originality, spelling/grammar, and overall quality (not in any particular order). No one factor will outweigh another so do not think that because you post five times and your opponent only posts twice, that you will get the win!
c. If you cannot RP for your match, PM the Administrator or a Creative Staff member. You don't have to provide a reason, especially if it is personal. The first time you do not RP for a match, and you didn't notify the front office, your character will be severely "jobbed". The second time you do not RP without notifying the front office before or after the results, YOU WILL BE FIRED!
d. You are only allowed 4 roleplays per match per week. Anything else will NOT be counted, this goes for ALL events.
e. No Stacked Posting. You must wait 2 hours before posting a second roleplay, to allow opponents to post.
5. Results & Cards a. There is only one weekly card, Monday Night Shockers, and one Pay-Per-View each month, which will be held on Mondays, and take the place of that week's Shockers. International Pay-Per-View events will be held periodically, every two or three months, in some venue outside of North America.
b. All results will be uploaded by 9 pm EST. the night of an event. ALL RESULTS ARE FINAL (though "preview cards" are subject to change from time to time). PM the Administrator or any of the Creative staff members with complaints, comments, etc. pertaining to card results.
DO NOT BITCH ON ANY OF THE BOARDS! c. Should the results be late, Slayer/Walter Loufie/Treize Delacroix will let everyone know on the OOC forum. DO NOT pm any member of the board, or post on any of the boards asking when the results will be up. They will be uploaded when they are finished.
d. The deadline for posting roleplays is 12 midnight Saturday/Sunday morning (Eastern Standard Time). This goes for Shockers and pay-per-views, since they are on the same day. NO ROLEPLAYS WILL BE CONSIDERED TOWARD YOUR MATCH AFTER THIS DEADLINE, SO DON'T BOTHER POSTING ANY!
6. The Boards a. There is 1 forums set specifically to post r/p’s for (creatively titled...the RP Board,). If you post on the wrong board, your post will get deleted.
There are a number of other boards to help give you the most experience in the EWA. The boards are designated for what they are.
b. The EWA front office encourages you all to keep up with the boards, especially posts on the OOC forum. This is so you don't miss a policy/rule change, the explanation should a card be late, etc.
c. Keep in mind that you may be placed in a match from time to time that you did not request/agree to. This is to either start a feud/angle, fill up a card, etc. It is not because you are being punished!
d. Swearing is allowed in your roleplays, but don't take it too far. Use your best judgment.
e. Do NOT start or contribute to a war on the OOC Board. Getting involved in a matter that does not concern either you or your character usually escalates into something nasty. If there is a disagreement between 2 or more members, let them and the front office sort it out.
f. Do NOT complain on the boards. This is negative criticism and leads to confrontations. If you have a problem with someone, voice it privately to that person through email, chat, etc.
g. No references to superstars or handlers being gay, queer, transsexual etc. This leads to big OOC wars.
h. The front office holds the right to “censor” any messages on any of the boards by either removing or editing them.
i. Do NOT give out your password once you register for the boards. This could lead to someone posting as you. If the front office finds anyone posting as another handler in the EWA, that person(s) will be fired!
j. Do not post other efed's websites on EWA boards, and do not advertise the EWA on other efed's boards! Invite people through email.
k. Deadline for requesting a match is 12 midnight EST Wednesday morning. Send a pm to the Administrator or any of the members of the Creative Staff. They will choose to agree or deny the request.
7. Championships EWA Legacy Title This is the most prestigious title in the EWA ONLY THE VERY BEST WILL HOLD THIS TITLE! Tag Team Title This is the most prestigious title in the Tag Team Division. Internet Title This title is the second most prestigious title in the EWA. Breakthrough Title This is a title mostly for those who are coming up the ranks in the EWA. However, it could be held by a veteran or someone from a higher rank, if there is a storyline involved
King of Violence Not a real title, introduced by Slayer, usually done for used for special occasions and only when Slayer approves of it. The matches are always Extreme rules, and will be gimmick matches.
THERE IS NO RE-MATCH CLAUSE, SO DON'T BITCH IF YOU LOST THE TITLE AND WANT ANOTHER SHOT. TITLE SHOTS WILL ONLY BE GIVEN TO THOSE WHO HAVE EARNED THEM! 8. Rosters a. You can only handle up to 2 wrestlers at any given time. This applies to both members of a tag team (should you handle both wrestlers in the team).
b. If you choose to have 2 wrestlers, there is a very good chance that both could be signed for separate matches in the same card. Make sure you post for both wrestlers, so as not to break the rule of "at least 1 post a week".
c. No dropping and bringing in new wrestlers all of the time just because you lose your "creative-side" with one particular character. This just makes more work for the front office.
d. Under no circumstances may you use any characters (other than your own, announcers, and/or interviewers) without that handler’s permission. You can use any member of the front office in your post, as long as you don't have him making a rule change, honoring a match, etc. And no physically attacking the front office member.
e. If a champion will be unable to defend his title at any event, (due to vacation, illness, internet problems, etc.), the title COULD be stripped and a championship tournament set up to determine a new champion. This all depends on why the champion cannot roleplay.
Matches Allowed The following are the matches that the EWA allows. Even though the list is quite extensive, if you would like a match that is not on this list, email your request to the Administrator or any of the members of the Creative Staff. As long as it isn't too far-fetched, he will most likely grant it...especially if it will boost the ratings ;)
THE FOLLOWING MATCHES WILL USUALLY BE FOR PAY-PER-VIEWS ONLY: AMBULANCE MATCH An ambulance is parked beside the entrance ramp, and a strecher is placed at ringside. The way to win is to strap your opponent to the strecher, and lock him in the ambulance.
BATTLE ROYAL MATCH An allotted amount of wrestlers enter the ring at the same time. Wrestlers are eliminated when they are thrown over the top rope. The winner is the last man in the ring.
BARB WIRE ROPE MATCH There are no holds barred. Ropes are taken down, and strands of barb wire are hung up in place of the ropes. A pinfall or submission will declare the winner.
BURIED ALIVE MATCH The winner is the first man to bury his opponent. DEATH MATCH The match begins with 1 weapon in the ring. A new weapon is thrown in every 2 minutes by the time keeper. No more weapons are thrown in after the 10th one.
ESCAPE MATCH Opponents wrestle in a room either backstage, or even outside of the arena (like a warehouse, or wherever). The first one to be able to get out wins.
FIRST BLOOD MATCH The rules to this one are pretty simple. Make your opponent bleed first, and you win.
HELL IN A CELL MATCH A sixteen foot steel cage is lowered, and it extends around the outside of the ring. Escape from this cell will NOT end the match; only pinfalls and submissions will. They must occur inside the ring.
HELL'S GATE MATCH The ring is surrounded by a steel cage, with a pole at one of the corners furthest from the ring holding keys to a door which bars the entranceway. A second fence encircles the ringside area as well as around the ramp, leaving a corridor up the ramp to the gates (to ensure no outside interference). The object is to 1) get the keys, 2) climb from the cage, 3) make your way to the gates, 4) unlock them, and 5) go through. The first person to make their way through the doors as pull them shut on his opponents wins. Obviously, even if you get the keys and unlock the gates, someone else can go through to win.
INFERNO MATCH Plain and simple, the winner of this match is the one to set his opponent on fire.
LOSER LEAVES TOWN MATCH This is a single match. If you lose, you're fired from EWA! NO HOLDS BARRED MATCH Basically this is a hardcore match, as there is no rope break, anything goes, and falls count anywhere.
QUARANTINE MATCH Both opponents are placed inside a plexiglass cell. First person to escape and lock their opponent inside the cell wins.
STABLE MATCH Two teams of three or more people face off against each other. The winner is either the first team to gain a fall, or in some cases the first team to eliminate all of their opponents by gaining falls on the entire opposing team.
CHAIR SHOT SURVIVAL MATCH In this match, you can use as many steel chairs as you want in as many ways as you want. Falls occur by pinfall, submission, or knockout. You cannot use any foreign objects besides a steel chair, or you will be disqualified.
STRAP MATCH The opponents are strapped together with a rope that connects their arms. The winner is the first man to score a pinfall or submission over his opponent. This match can also be done similarly with use of a chain instead of a rope.
SURVIVOR MATCH These matches occur when three or more opponents face off against each other at the same time. In some cases, matches end with the first fall. In these cases, countouts and disqualifications don't count. Another variation of this is the elimination style match. These matches continue until one man is left. Opponents are eliminated via pinfall, submission, and in some cases, countout and disqualification as well.
WEAPONS MATCH Each Wrestler gets to pick a weapon. They start the match with their weapons.
THE FOLLOWING MATCHES WILL BE FOR EITHER PAY-PER-VIEWS OR TELEVISED EVENTS: 2 OUT OF 3 FALLS MATCH You win the match by pinning your opponent 2 times. CAGE MATCH This match pits opponents against each other in the confines of a steel cage. The match ends when one competitor climbs over the top of the cage or leaves through the door. In some variations, pinfalls or submissions inside the cage will end the match as well.
FALLS COUNT ANYWHERE MATCH The match begins in the ring but you can pin your opponent anywhere (entrance ramp/stage, backstage, the parking lot, etc.)
FATAL FOUR WAY MATCH 4 wrestlers compete, trying to be the first one to get the win over an opponent.
HANDICAP MATCH One wrestler faces at least 2 others. The number can go as high as 4. HARDCORE MATCH In this match, anything goes. Falls count anywhere in the building, and they occur by pinfall and submission only. There are no disqualifications. Some similar variants to this match include No Disqualifications, Falls Count Anywhere, No Holds Barred, and Street Fight.
"I QUIT" MATCH There is only one way to win in this type of match. You must make your opponent utter the two magic words: "I quit"!
IRON MAN MATCH This match can be done in any style, but a regular fall doesn't end the match. The winner of this match is decided after a given time limit. Whoever has the most falls when time runs out wins.
LADDER MATCH In this match, an object, (for example a title), is suspended above the ring. The winner is the first man to successfully climb the ladder and retrieve the object.
LAST MAN STANDING MATCH Anything goes in this match as opponents battle to prevent their opponent from standing. The match ends when one opponent is too battered to get up within a ten count.
LUMBERJACK MATCH Before the match, wrestlers are chosen to surround the outside of the ring, while the 2 wrestlers battle it out inside. The wrestlers surrounding the ring are the lumberjacks. When a wrestler gets thrown to the outside, then the lumberjacks can attack that wrestler.
ONE ON TAG MATCH A form of a handicap match, only one member of the tag team is allowed in the ring at a time.
SINGLE MATCH This is a classic one on one battle between two competitors. Falls occur by pinfall, submission, disqualification, or ten second count out.
SPECIAL REFEREE MATCH A guest ref is placed in a matchup. Usually it is a "third party" involved with 2 wrestlers. In some cases it may be an actor, singer, or some other popular person from the entertainment industry. (the Administrator must approve of ALL guest referees)!
SUBMISSION MATCH Although there are no disqualifications, these matches are usually very technical. Competitors fight to get their opponent to tap out. Though it's pretty self explanatory, submissions are the only way to win.
TABLE MATCH Falls in this match occur when you put your opponent through a table. TABLES, LADDERS AND CHAIRS (TLC) MATCH Take a table match, a ladder match, and a steel chair match and mix them together. Falls occur via either ladder match rules or pinfall and submission.
TAG TEAM MATCH This is a two on two battle between cooperative teams. One man is allowed in the ring at a time per team, and partners enter the battle via tag. Falls occur by pinfall, submission, disqualification, or ten second count out.
TIME LIMIT TITLE MATCH 4 wrestlers compete against each other (and the clock) with a belt on the line.
TORNADO TAG MATCH Tag team members can enter the ring whenever they want. TRIPLE THREAT MATCH 3 wrestlers compete one-on-one-on-one. SPECIFIC MATCH TYPES CAN BE REQUESTED FOR ANY EVENT. WHETHER OR NOT THEY ARE USED IS UP TO THE EXECUTIVE BOARD.
