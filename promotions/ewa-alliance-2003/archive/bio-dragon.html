Biography sheet: Dragon

c. November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Name: Dragon Height: 5'8" Weight: 210 Hometown: Ridgecrest, California Theme Music: Defy You - The Offspring (Dragon Remix) Stable: Generation X Alignment: Heel (even though he only cheats badly when needed) Wrestling Style: Martial Arts (Tae Kwan Do), High Flyer and Speedy. Finishing Move: Dragon Cutter Finishing Move Description: Dragon lifts his opponent into a suplex position then pulls them back down. Dragon falls back landing with his legs spread out and sitting up. He then nails a hard DDT with his opponent’s head smashing on the mat between his legs and his stomach crashing on Dragon's left knee.
Secondary Finishing Move: Flame Kick Secondary Finishing Move Description: Sweet Chin Music (Sometimes with a chair) Signature Moves: D.N.B. (Dragon Neck Breaker): Dragon is behind his opponent and puts his right hand on the mat and pushes off wrapping his legs around the opponents neck. He then falls bringing his opponent with him and slams the back of their head against the mat with his legs.
Original Set Up: Is either whipped or is running from his opponent to the ropes or turnbuckle. Before hitting, he either jumps or bounces off the top rope and flips over his opponent who runs and slams against the turnbuckle or ropes and is sent backwards.
Overkill: Dragon lifts his opponent in a Torture Rack position. He then spins his opponent around his head and wraps his right arm under his opponents left arm and behind their head. He then slams his opponent face first into the mat. (This move will only be used on weaker opponents. This move will never be used on bigger opponents like Warlord.)
Fireball: Corkscrew Swanton Bomb Tornado DDT Dragon Stretch: First, the opponent's in the turnbuckle. I'll hook their legs with the ropes, punches them a few times then goes behind him on top of the turnbuckle. I'd then grab their arms, wrap my legs around their head and slowly fall back and keep pressure on the neck the whole time, but by falling back the lower of their back also gets damaged by the turnbuckle.
Frequently Used Moves: Arm Drag variations Hurracarana variations DDT Kick variations Dragon Sleeper Dragon Screw Monkey Flip Senton Splash (Ground) Flying Elbow Drop (pin) Headscissor takedown Diving Hurracarana Foot Choke (turnbuckle) Choke (ropes & ground turnbuckle) Springboard Shooting Star Press to the outside Appearance: Long Brown hair, tied in a ponytail when wrestling usually. Black wristbands with a green circle in the middle. Metallic Black colored martial arts pants. One big green flame comes up from the ankles of the left pant leg. On the bottom of the flame, a red dragon is pictured with it's long spiky tail spirals up to the tip of the flame.
On the right pant leg, Dragoon is written downward in green. Just under the "n" the head of a dragon in red flames is pictured. A Dragon tattoo is on his upper left back shoulder.
Entrance: The arena goes black as and red and green lights begin to flicker as a the screen goes completely black except a single figure outlined in flames.
"For everyone who said it couldn't be done..." A single drum beat is hear softly. "For everyone who said I couldn't do it..." Another beat comes but louder. "For everyone who doubted me when I did it... For everyone who said I was finished!" A short midevil drum roll is heard then... "I CAN'T BE SLAIN!" The crowd boo's as Defy You then blasts throughout the arena and red and green firworks explode on the stage. After a moment, a single huge burst of red and green comes out from the center of the stage followed by Dragon. Dragon lands on his feet and spins around a bit as Laura walks out from under the curtain and follows Dragon down the ramp who taunts and mocks a few of the fans. Dragon slowly climbs up the steps and looks around at the fans a bit then laughs and holds the ropes for Laura and follws her inside. Once Dragon's feet get through the ropes he spins again then climbs the closest turnbuckle and screams at the fans. After a moment of laughing at their boo's, Dragon drops down, kisses Laura, and lets her out of the ring as his music dies.
