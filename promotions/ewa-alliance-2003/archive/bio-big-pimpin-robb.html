Biography sheet: Big Pimpin' Robb

c. June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Real Name: Robbie Knight Gimmick Name: Big Pimpin' Robb Height: 6'2" Weight: 241lb HomeTown: Las Vegas Allignment: Face Wrestling Style: Primarily a Brawler, with Submission and Technical skills. Entrance Theme: "Good Girls, Bad Guys" - DMX Finishers: (1) 1) One Night Stand *Angle Slam* (Can be done off the top rope) (2) X Rated *Second Rope Full Nelson* Signature Moves: (1) Fully Loaded *T-Bone Suplex* (2) Late Night Thrilla *Full Nelson Suplex* (3) Show Stoppa *Grounded Sleeper Hold w/Body Scissor* (4) Out Cold *Brainbuster Suplex* Trademark Moves: (1) Full Nelson (2) Back Suplex (3) Single Leg Boston Crab (4) Wind-Up Punch (5) Sledgehammer (6) European Uppercut (7) German Suplex (8) Discus Punch (9) Sleeper Hold (10) Double Fist Smash (11) Step Over Armbar (12) Belly-to-Belly Suplex Entrance Description: "Good Girls, Bad Guys" hits as Robb walks out, with another hot chick, both he adn the chick slap hadns with the fans. They both enter the ring and Robb climbs a turnbuckle and taunts. He climbs down and kisses the chick who heads to the outside as Robb remains in the ring.
Appearance: Muscular, tanned body with short, slicked back black hair. He is very good looking and always seems to be smiling. (Probably because he's surrounded by lots of hot chicks )
Ring Attire: Robb wears a pair of baggy black trousers, with a red lettering on the right leg, reading: "X-Rated" and "One Night Stand" on the left leg. He also wears a pair of Black Boots and white wrist tape. He also wears his jacket and glasses to the ring, but removes them to wrestle.
Casual Attire: Robb wears a pair of Black Boots, Black Denim Jeans and a short-sleeved light Blue shirt. He also wears a pair of sunglasses and a black long Silk jacket, with a white trim. He also wears several gold rings and a gold chain.
Biography: Robb grew up living the rich life and was often surrounded by friends and girls at school. When he left school, he was still surrounded by ladies. He only had one long relationship, that ended badly and hurt him, now he just has a different girl each week. Robb has always been a bit of a charmer, but he does tend to be very vain at times. He also likes to speak about himself in the third person.
Title History: EWA European Champion(1)
