Biography sheet: Denim Hyral

c. June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Name: Denim Hyral Height: 6'6" Weight: 315 lbs Hometown: Montreal, Canada Theme Music: Black & White - Alignment: Wrestling Style: Power & Brawler Finishing Move: The Disaster Finishing Move Description: Grab the guy in a pumphandle position, lift him up on his shoulder and nails an Emerald Fusion.
Secondary Finishing Move: The Line of Destruction Sec. Finishing Move Description: Running Big boot Signature Moves: 1- Chain of Power (Denim grabs the guy by the arm and swings the guy with all his might in the corner, A great set up move for his second finisher.)
2- Choke of Domination (Backdrop to Chokeslam) 3- Ultimate Bear Hug (Grab like a bear hug, He moves like crazy and slam him on the floor when he figures that the opponent have enough.)
4- Torture rack 5- Swings of Anger (Spinebuster followed by a Big Swing) Frequently Used Moves: 1- Running Clothesline 2- Short Arm Lariat 3- Gut Wrench Powerbomb 4- Punching submissions 5- Powerslam 6- Multiple Shoulder Block in corner 7- Full Nelson Slam 8- German Suplex 9- Top Rope Big Splash 10- Right punch to stomach followed by a hard DDT 11- Stalling Suplex 12- Crushing Reverse (When Denim is sent in the rope, He give him some speed and jump back at the opponent with a huge clothesline)
13- Military Press 14- Shoulder Breaker 15- Knee Drop Appearance: Denim is tall and good-looking guy. Denim has blond hair. He usually wears leather pants white or black. Wears Tank top to wrestle and wears T-shirts in the back. He really enjoys bodybuilding. He's very muscular and like to show it. He like to show that what he has done to himself witch took him a lot of time and now what he looks like. Denim has a big tattoo in the back.
Entrance: Black and White hit the speaker, After multiple thunder and multiple flashes, Denim comes out of the stage. With some heat from the crowd. Denim walks to the middle of the ramp and stop to look at the fans on the side. Denim trash talks some and continues to the ring. Denim gets on the Apron, walks a bit and enters the ring. Denim stretches himself with the ropes and when he stop in the middle of the ring, some pyros explode on the four corner of the ring.
Miscellaneous stuff that you think is necessary for the EWA writers and staff to know. Denim is Dalis brother. For so long Denim tried to surpass his brother, He thinks that he never need help but will not let his brother on the side. He and him worked together to get along and they did. Denim has a hard attitude. Some peoples don't like him for that, because he says what he thinks. He thinks that he's the strongest but he can be surprised. Denim had an injury a few years ago, Travis accidentally hurt his back. It wasn't too bad but he couldn’t wrestle for a bit. Now he tries not to hurt it too bad. When Denim is Mad and getting the advantage of the match, he takes off his tank top and shows all of his wonderful muscles.
