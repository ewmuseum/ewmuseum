Biography sheet: Richard Bison

c. January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Name: Richard Bison Height: 6'1" Weight: 233 lbs HomeTown: Calgary, Alberta, Canada Allignment: Neutral Wrestling Style: Technical Entrance Theme: Wake Up Hate – Korn Stable: Generation X Manager: Rachel Ellerton Finishers: (1) The BKO *Fireman's Carry to Ace Crusher* Signature Moves: (1) Piledriver (Set up for the Wildebeast) (2) Inverted Firemans Carry (Torture rack) into Knee to Facebuster (3) (Sprinboard) Bison Face Crusher (Bison is whiped to the ropes. When he comes running back, just before the oponent is going to hit a move, Bison jumps and grabs the head, driving it down to the mat)
(4) Wildebeast (Springboard Moonsalt, can be used as a finisher) (5) Bison DDT (Bison places the opponents feet on the turnbuckle. The oponent is face down in a horizontal position and Bison DDT's him down)
Trademark Moves: (1) German Suplex (Either a double, release or pin) (2) Northern Light Suplex (w/ Pin) (3) Frankensteiner (4) Missile Dropkick (5) Diving Moonsalt Pin (6) Whirl Back Breaker (7) Super Backdrop (8) Spinebuster (9) Lou Thesz Press (w/punches) (10) Brainbuster DDT (11) Gut buster (sometimes from the top turnbuckle) (12) Shuffle Sidekick (only used when doging a clothesline) (13) Snapmare followed by dropkick to the back of the head (14) Spinning wheel kick (15) Enziguri Misc: Bison is a head orientated wrestler, so punches, elbows, knees or whatever to the head , Bison does them.
Entrance Description: The light in the arena start to dim a little and some yellow lights light up. Wake up hate by Korn starts to play and Bison walks out on stage! He walks to the ring, acknowledging the crowd on his way to the ring. He slides in the ring and poses on the closest turnbuckle, raises his arms in the air and fireworks shoot up in the air from the turnbuckle opposite of him. He jumps down from the turnbuckle, stops in the middle of the ring and then continues to the turnbuckle where the fireworks came from. He climbs it, raises his arms and fireworks shoot up in the air, out of the first turnbuckle he was on. He jumps down from the turnbuckle he was on and walks over to the centre of the ring and gets ready.
Ring Attire: Short spiky dark blond hair. Build like Jericho, but more visible abs and biceps. Slightly tanned. He wears long black tights with a bright yellow and white lightning pattern on them, black wrist bands with a bright yellow sterak, black boots, bright yellow elbow pads and bright yellow with black shin pads. Has a tattoo (Rose with a tribal around it) on his left shoulder. He has the same symbol printed on his knee pads in white.
History: Richard Bison was maybe the biggest S.O.B. ever in the history off sports entertainment. People love him, people hate him, people love to hate him. He trained all over the world, but mostly in Canada, where here learned his technical and speedy style. He soon was accepted in an independent federationcalled the WECWF
Bison, soon made his debut in the WECWF where he started out as just your average wrestler. But soon after his debut, he captured his first title, the WECWF Intercontinental Title. He held that title for 4 months and in the last two months he also managed to capture the WECWF Tag Team Title. But soon after, the WECWF went out of business and now Bison was out of a job.
After a while, he was approached by the owner of WCWA. That's where he began to develop an attitude problem. In the year that he was an active member of the roster, he managed to capture every title that the WCWA had to offer. Because off this, he became arrogant and c*cky. In the WCWA, he was constantly interfering in matches and just doing things that he wanted too. The owner tried to put a stop to it, but Bison just made it worse for him. Because of a stipulation in his contract, he couldn´t get fired for 2 years. He went to the house of the owner and completely redecorated the entire place. He burnt all the pictures that he could find in the house and he destoyed all his golf trophies. This drove the owner insane, but a replacement for the owner was found. This person was formally know as The Tormentor. A wrestler that was unbeaten for an entire year in the WCWA until he had to face Bison who ended his winning streak. Tormentor challanged Bison to a loser leaves the WCWA-match, which Bison arrogantly accepted. This proved to be a huge mistake on the part of Bison. He lost the match and had to leave the WCWA.
After a period of two months of doing nothing, Bison got bored. He went into town and saw adds spread over the entire town that a new Federation was opening up soon. He went to his favotite bar, The Halo. He ordered a beer at the bar and asked where he could find the adress of the WWO. The bar keeper said that this was his lucky day, because the owner was sitting next to him! The owner, Phil Ward, and Bison talked and after a while he signed a contract to join the roster. Poor Phil Ward had no idea who he brought into the WWO. As soon as he debuted he started to pick a fight with the owner. It resulted in a huge feud. And even in this Federation, he wrecked the belongings of the owner. Burning family pictures, destoying trophies and steeling an old Heavyweight Title from the time Phil Ward was a wrestler. But this time, Bison didn´ take the blame for it and shove it in the shoes of Psyko with whom he was feuding at that time. Psyko paid dearly, for what Bison had done.
At the moment, Bison is the Cruiserweight Champion and has to prove that he is worthy of that title. He has formed a group called Generation X. The first incarnation of GX excisted in the WCWA and had Bison and Whisper as its members. Now in the WWO, Whisper and Bison contimued to team and brought along Camaro and Jamie Wison along with them. Jamie Wison was soon kicked out of GX for no activity and soon after that, added Bouncer and Jonathan Lee to their ranks.
After defending the championshop successfully against The Kid, Bison was left with out any oponents in the Cruiserweight devision. Most of them left the WWO. Bison claims that they were scared of his skills. Phil then changed it into the WWO Hardcore championship. Bison succesfully defended the championship against Hyena, leader of Dominators of eXtreme. Soon after that, Richard Bison found himself in a championship versus championship match against the Internet Champion at that time, D-Frost. First fall was for teh internet championship. Bison won the first fall, but lost the second, due to some interference by Crusher Adams. This interference led to a long lasting battle between Richard Bison and Crusher Adams. Bison defended the Internet championship succesfully a few times against Crusher Adams. The most noteable match they had was teh 20 minute submission match which ended in a draw. Bison claims that he was sick that day, so thats why he couldn't get the win.
In between matches and other business related topics, Bison had enough free time. He helped out his friend Max. Bison and Max made a deal that if Bison would help Max, Max would allow Bison to ask his manager out on a date. Bison helped out and Max kept his word too and Max arranged that Bison and his manager, Rachel Ellerton would meet. Soon after, they went out on a date and have been with eachother ever since.
GX had been taking the WWO by storm and even added Chazz Irvine and Morzo to their ranks, but Morzo stayed true to his backstabbing self and turned his back on GX.
Soon after that, Bison entered the WWO World Heavyweight title tournament. In that Tounament, he only lost one match, to Camaro and as a result, lost the Internet Championship with it. Bison didn't need to worry much as he defeated Morzo in the final at the Pay Per View Time and wrote history, becoming the first ever WWO Heavyweight champion. At that same event, Bison disbanded GX with teh help of his long time enemy and now friend D-Frost.
Richard Bison and D-Frost teamed together and with some of the other WWO Superstars, they were involved in the Tornado Tag Team Title Table match. In that match, D-Frost and Bison debuted their team finisher, The Unforgiven, on Chazz Irvine and broke his neck.
Richard Bison defended his WWO Championship successfully against Morzo again at Crimson Christmas and also against Nathan Irvine at The Rumble. Soon after that, the WWO closed its doors due to some creative differences in the staff and Bison was yet again after 7 months, without a job, but still, undeafeted WWO Champion.
In the EWA Bison continued to be a dominant force. Leading Generation X he at one point revealed that he partly owned the EWA and to top that off, he introduced the WWO Championship into the mix. After Kingdom Come 2, the path of Bison changed. All the WWO Championships were unified and in teh most unexpected turn of events, Bison was kicked out of his own stable. After a month of rehab, Bison is back and on his way to be a fan favorite.
That didn't last long however as Bison quickly became frustrated with the competition in the EWA or the lack of it. A week before Jonathan Lee and Bison were wet to fight in the 8th Sin (Tornado Tag Team Title Table match in a Cell), Bison turned on Lee and robbed Lee of the chance to fight in the 8th Sin. As soon as his turn was complete, Bison reformed the stable tha was the most dominant one in 2003 along with D-Frost and Dragon; Generation X.
Title History: WWO Hardcore Champion (First ever, lost it to D-Frost) WWO Cruiserweight Champion (First ever, turned into the Hardcore title) WWO Tag Team Champion (4x, twice with Bouncer, once with Camaro and once with Dragon) WWO Internet Champion (second person to hold the title. Defeated Dalis for the title) WWO Heavyweight Champion (first ever, beat Mozro in The WWO Title Tournament) EWA European Champion EWA Tag Team Champion (2, w/ Travis) eCHo Intercontinental Champion (First ever) NHTA Tag Team Champion (with Original Sin)
