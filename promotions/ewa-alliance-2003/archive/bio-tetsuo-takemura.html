Biography sheet: Tetsuo Takemura

c. June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Real Name: Tetsuo Takemura Gimmick Name: Tetsuo Height: 6'1" Weight: 225 lbs Age: 23 years old (August 9th, 1979) HomeTown: Shanghai, China Allignment: Face Wrestling Style: Speedy, Martial Artists & High Flyer Hair Color/Style: Black hair, Shoulder lenght Eyes Color: Brown Skin Color: Tanned Skin Entrance Theme: Kaientai Dojo - TAKA Michinoku (Yamato Suite 4) Finishers: (1) Cross-Cutter *Kneeling Hangman Tigerbomb* (2) Oak Revolution *Tetsuo variation of a Death Valley Driver* (Tetsuo begins with a headlock like position. He then turns and lifts the opponent's leg with his other arm. At this point, the opponent should be at a somewhat angled position across the back of Tetsuo. Tetsuo then drops the opponent on their head by falling to the side)
Signature Moves: (1) Sky Twister Press *Corcscrew Swanton Bomb* (2) Flame Sniper *Springboard Tornado DDT* (Tetsuo applies a front chancery, the opponent's head is under Tetsuo arm in front of him instead of behind like a headlock. Tetsuo then runs up the corner post and swings to the other side, dropping on his own back so that the opponent's head is driven into the mat)
Trademark Moves: (1) Capture Suplex (2) Over the Shoulder Arm Drag (3) Tetsuo Special (Tetsuo stands face to face with the victim. He grabs one of the victim's arms and bends it behind the victim's back. He then sticks his head under the victim's free arm and hooks the leg on the same side as his head is place. Tetsuo then lifts the victim up and falls backwards, dropping the victim on their back and arm. Tetsuo may bridge for a pin)
(4) Running Spinning Heel Kick (5) Running Palm Strike (6) Neckbreaker Drop (7) Cross Arms Lock (8) Metal Claw *Gulliotine Choke* (9) Figure Four Sleeper (The victim is laying on their back. Tetsuo sits behind the victim's head facing towards them. He grabs the victim's head and places one of his legs around the side of the victim's head and bends his leg so his calf is under the victim's chin. Tetsuo locks his leg in place with his other leg so their are forming a "4" around the victim's head. He applies pressure with his legs, cutting off the supply of blood to the brain of the victim)
(10) Jumping Inverted DDT (11) Seikan Punch *A hard punch to the lower back* (12) Japanese Ocean Cyclone Suplex (Tetsuo stands behind the victim, he crosses the victims arms in front of them. Tetsuo bends down & sticks his head between the victim's legs and lifts them up so the victim is sitting on his shoulders. He falls backwards and drops the victim on their back)
(13) Crossed Arms Iconoslam (Tetsuo puts his victim on the turnbuckle & executes a cross arm flipping slam) (14) Diving Backbrain Knee (Tetsuo climbs to the top rope and waits for the fallen or dazed victim to get to their feet, but not standing straight up yet. He then dives off and lands his knee on the back of their head to take them down)
(15) Top Rope Somersault Axe Kick Entrance Description: Kaientai Dojo starts playing and a spotlight illuminate the stage as Tetsuo looks at the crowd. Green pyros explode as Tetsuo walks down the ramp. He climbs the turnbuckle, executes a moonsault to land on his feets.
Appearence: Look likes Robin Shou Ring Attire: Medium Built Bare Chest Black Chinese Pants Black Chinese Shoes White Wristbands Title History: WXW: Tag-Team Championship (Gekido) Light Weight Championship EXPW: Darkside Championship Tag-Team Championship (Gekido) WWO: 3 Times Tag-Team Championship (Gekido) EWA: European Championship TV Championship
