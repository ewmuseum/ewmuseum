Real Name: Xavier Creed Gimmick Name: Xavier "Mirror Man" Creed Height: 6'1" Weight: 230 lbs Age: 22 years old Hometown: Seattle, Washington Allignment: Heel (Although he thinks everyone loves him) Wrestling Style: Mid-Power & Brawler Entrance Theme: c*cky - Kid Rock Finishers: (1) Mirror Effect *Twist of Fate* (2) Trick Figure *Springboard Elbow Drop* (The opponent is on there back face up with Xavier standing out their head. Xavier smiles and lifts his arm in the air and points up one finger for a moment then begins flexing his arm. After a bit of flexing, Creed kisses his bicep and runs to the rope, bounces off the middle one and bounces back. But instead of flipping, Xavier turns in mid air and nails a springboard elbow drop on the opponent. After hitting the move, Xavier usually sits up and flexes his arm once before kissing his bicep)
Signature Moves: (1) Absolute Creed (Xavier grabs his opponents head and smashes it into his knee. The opponent bounces off but before he can fall backwards, Xavier grabs him again and hits a snap DDT)
(2) Creedbreaker (Xavier catches his opponent and wrap his right arm around their neck as he moves beside them. He then kneel down and pushes his right arm down hard driving his opponents spine into his knee)
(3) Double Crosser *Standing Armbreaker to STO* Trademark Moves: (1) Various Punching Combination (2) DDT (3) Backbreaker (4) Snap Belly to Belly Suplex (Usually done when the opponent is running at Xavier) (5) Standing Single Leg Crab (6) Leglock (7) Spinebuster (8) Russian Leg Sweep (9) German Suplex (10) Turnbuckle Chop (11) Turnbuckle 10 Punch (12) Eye Poke (13) Low Blow Entrance Description: The lights dim a bit as c*cky begins playing. The crowd boo's as Xavier comes out on stage, arms strecthed out with a huge smile on his face. THe crowd continues booing as Xavier walks down the ramp occasionally flexing his muscels for the crowd. Xeviers climbs the steps and goes to the center of the apron and points his abs out to the booing crowd the smiles even broader and flexes his arms above his head as the other side of the ring behind him explodes in fire works. After a moment, Xavier climbs in the ring as his music dies.
Appearence: Short Black hair. Short wrestling tights (Blue). On both sides of the legs of the tights have a yellow symbol like this XlX (just obviously put together)
"Xavier" is written in yellow on the butt of the tights. Black Boots with blue soles. Blue knee pads with the same symbol on the front. White wrist tape. Misc. Stuff: Xavier is very c*cky and very much in love with himself. A small part of Mirror Man comes from him standing in front of the mirror so much acting like he's a god. He thinks everybody loves him. When the crowd boo's he'll just point to himself confused like then blow a kiss at the fans before returning to the match.
He'll do anything in order to get a win. Title History: EWA European Champion
