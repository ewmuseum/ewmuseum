Kingdom Come 7 Theme song Posted on February 10, 2009 by admin First match has been announced! Posted on February 10, 2009 by admin “This is the events where legends are made! -Boutch.” At the start of the Ascension Battle Royal, it was announced that the winner of the Ascension Battle Royal will face both the EWA Legacy Champion and the EWA Prime Time Champion at the main event of Kingdom Come 7 to unify both championships!
Ascension V: Phil Ward writes history! Posted on February 10, 2009 by admin “This is going to be huge, I can't wait! -Mark.” Survival is the top priority in the Ascension Battle Royal as you have to eliminate your opponent from the ring by throwing him over the top rope. Phil Ward understood this the best out of all and eliminated a record breaking 7 EWA superstars from the ring!
