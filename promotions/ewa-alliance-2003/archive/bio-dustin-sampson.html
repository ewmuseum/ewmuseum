Biography sheet: Dustin Sampson

c. June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Real Name: Dustin Sampson Gimmick Name: Dustin "The Main Event" Sampson Height: 5'10 Weight: 215lbs Age (Optional): 23 Hometown: Kingston, Ontario, Canada Alignment: Heel Wrestling Style: High Flying/Technical (AJ Styles/Guererro like) Entrance Theme: The Ecstacy of Gold - Metallica (Featuring SF symphony) Finishers: Redemption - Double Underhook Piledriver (Kid Kash's Money Maker) The opponent is set up like a Pedigree but instead of dropping down Sampson lifts him up into a pile driver position and then sits out often kicking out leg out first to make it seem more dramatic.
The Sampson Effect - (Raven Effect DDT/Evenflow) A swift boot to the stomach followed by a lightning fast DDT
Signature Moves: Second rope springboard moonsault into a reverse DDT Triple Springboard Moonsault - (The BME) Sampson springboards off the first second and third rope in the corner before executing a Moonsault
Split Legged Moonsault Frequently Used Moves: Hurricanranna, Snap Suplex, martial arts kicking combos, springboard kick to the head in the corner(like Rico), dropkick, standing Shooting star press, tornado DDT, snap powerbomb, corkscrew plancha, asai moonsault, leg sweep kick, calf kick, German Suplex
Entrance Description: The lights in the arena go black. Then the single bell tolling in the intro toMetallica and the symphony's "The Ecstasy of Gold blasts through the arena speakers as a cloud of smoke billows out from the entrance area and a single bright white light shines from the entrance way illuminating the smokey haze. As the music builds up the bell tolls for a second time which is usually when Sampson steps through the cloud of smoke. The ramp lights come to life as Sampson stands there in his long white leather jacket and white cowboy hat. He looks around for a few seconds then begins a slow walk to the ring. When he gets to the ring he walks slowly up the stairs pausing on the final step for a moment then entering the ring. Once in the ring he removes his hat and jacket and is ready to fight.
Appearence: Sampson has short black hair and bright blue eyes. Sampson wears long white shin guard boots with silver accents going down the sides. The boots are so long they look like they connect with his big white knee pads. He wears white tight shorts with silver accents. His wrists and hands are taped with white hockey tape, and he wears silver arm bands. In his entrance he usually wears a long white trenchcoat and a pair of expensive looking silver framed dark black sunglasses. Outside the ring Sampson is usually just wearing casual clothes, whatever he feels like wearing on any given day.
Ring Attire: Sampson wears long white shin guard boots with silver accents going down the sides. The boots are so long they look like they connect with his big white knee pads. He wears white tight shorts with silver accents. His wrists and hands are taped with white hockey tape, and he wears silver arm bands
Biography(Optional): Sampson made himself famous in the original incarnation of the WWW, he achieved much sucess and even won the WWW Global Championship off of Vincent Raines. Now he's back for a second time, what will the unpredictable Sampson have in store for the EWA? Will he be back to his old antics, or will he take on a more serious role this time and try and take that World Championship he longed for in the oringinal run of the WWW? Only time will tell.
