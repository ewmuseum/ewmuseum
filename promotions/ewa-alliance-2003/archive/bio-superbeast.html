Biography sheet: Superbeast

c. November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Real Name: Scott Lee Gimmick Name: Superbeast Height: 6'10" Weight: 310 lbs. Age: 25 Hometown: Chicago, Illinois Alignment: Face Wrestling Style: Powerful Brawler Entrance Theme: Superbeast- Performed by Rob Zombie Finishers: 1. Mark of the Beast (Full Nelson Chokeslam) 2. Call of the Beast (Spear off the top rope) Signature Moves: 1. Flying Forearm (Runs off the ropes and rebounds jumping into the air and sending his forearm into the opponents face)
2. Chokeslam (Signals that he is going for the chokeslam by holding his right hand in the air, he then reaches back and sends his right hand flying into the opponents throat, afterwards picking the opponent up and slamming him on his back with one hand)
3. Sidewalk Slam (Grabs his opponent around the waist with one arm and picks him up, he walks around for a little while and then slams him on the ground)
4. Running Spear (Runs into the ropes and rebounds by hitting his opponent in the midsection with a violent tackle)
5. Spear into the Turnbuckle (Spears an opponent while the opponent is in the corner) Frequently Used Moves: 1. Vertical Suplex (Let’s them hang in the air for a long time before falling) 2. Triple Elbow (Hits them with one elbow in the face, then one into the stomache, and while they are slumped over he hits one on top of their head.
3. DDT (Kicks his opponent in the stomache and places their head under his right arm, slamming it down violently on the ground)
4. 10 Punch Combo (While the opponent is in the corner he climbs on top of them with his feet on the second rope of each side, he then punches them 10 times in the head.)
5. Mounted Punches (While the opponent is laying on the ground he climbs on top of their chest and begins wailing on their head with his fists)
6. Mudhole Stomping (While the opponent is in the corner he throws them onto the ground and begins to stomp on him uncontrollably)
7. Mounted Knees (While the opponent is laying face first on the ground he gets over top of their head and sends 4 knees into their shoulders and head)
8. Spike Piledriver (Jumping sitout piledriver) 9. Double Underhook Suplex (Puts his opponents head under his right arm and underhooks each of his arms, he then flips the opponent over making him land on his back)
10. Running Elbow Drop (While the opponent is down he runs up against the ropes and rebounds off jumping into the air and landing an elbow drop on the opponents neck)
11. German Suplex (Grabs the opponent around the waist from the back and tosses them over him into the air making them land on their backs)
12. Diving Elbow Drop (While his opponent is down he climbs to the top rope and holds his hand in the air taunting, he then points at his opponent and dives off, landing an elbow drop on the opponent)
13. Reversed DDT (Grabs the opponent around the neck from the back and drops them on the back of their head)
14. Back Breaker Drop (The attacker lifts the victim up so that they are laying across the attacker's shoulders. The attacker hooks the victim's neck and leg and drops to their knees, wrenching the victim's back.)
15. Belly to Belly Suplex (The attacker stands face to face with the victim. The attacker locks their arms around the waist of the victim. The attacker lifts the victim off the mat and pivots 180 degrees slamming the victim's back to the mat and landing on top of them.)
Entrance Description: The whole arena goes black as Superbeast begins to play, the beat is light at first. The song then gets harder as red pyro bursts out of each side of the stage, Scott walks out of the pyro and as he is walking down the ramp red strobe lights begin going off all around him. He then reaches the apron and jumps onto it, he turns to the crowd and raises his hands into the air yelling as he does so. He then steps over the top rope and walks to the middle of the ring, as he reaches the middle of the ring he thrusts his right fist into the air, right as he does so red pyro bursts from all of the corners and the strobe lights go off.

Appearence: Bald, Brown Eyes, Muscular, No Tattoos Ring Attire: Leather Pants, Black Sleeveless Shirt (Sometimes he takes it off during the match), Black Boots
