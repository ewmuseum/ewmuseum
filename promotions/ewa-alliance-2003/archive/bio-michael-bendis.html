Real Name: Michael Bendis Height: 6’0 Weight: 234 pounds Age (Optional): 24 Hometown: Alignment: Face Wrestling Style: Power Entrance Theme: He Ya - Outkast Finishers: (1) Violator (Fisherman suplex to inverted piledriver) (2) Bad Vibe (Frog splash) Signature Moves: (1) 3 handled Credenza (2) Axe Bomber (3) Special B (Bendis stands back to back with the victim. Bendis wraps his arm around the victim's head and drops forward on his knee, bending the opponent's back across his own)
(4) Electric Chair Drop Frequently Used Moves: (1) Kitchen Sink (2) DDT (3) Dropkick (4) Swinging Neckbreaker (5) Manhattan Drop (6) Jawbreaker (7) Gutbuster (8) Flapjack (9) Headlock (10) Tiger Suplex (11) Snap Suplex (12) Belly to Belly overhead suplex (13) Powerslam (14) Whirl Backbreaker (15) Guillotine Choke Entrance Description: He Ya by Outkast starts to play and smoke appears on stage. Michael Bendis comes walking out of the smoke and he heads down the ring. He has a 1 dollar bill in his hand and acts as if he is going to give it to one of the people in the crowd, but then quickly pulls his arm back. He then call the person that wanted to grabs the dollar a lame ass. He continues down the to the ring, sniffing his dollar bill. He puts the bill in his pocket, closes the zipper and gets in the ring. He climbs up on a turnbuckle and poses as the musoc dies out.
Appearence: Muscular and tanned. Brown hair, blue eyes. Wears Black boots, Red pants, black wristbands, a red hat and a golden Dollar necklace to the ring.
Ring Attire: Black boots, Red pants, black wristbands Biography(Optional): Michael Bendis has wrestled for almost 5 years in Mexico and 1 year in Japan, starting at the age of 18. He didn’t have much success there though. Only won one title in his career as a professional wrestler. After being contacted by Eric Davis from the EWA, he signed a 5 week trial contract. If he manages to impress Eric Davis, he will get a extension, if not, he’s out!
Title History: JWA Television Champion (1) EWA Cruiserweight Champion (1) EWA Tag Team Champion (1, w/ Brett Cotter) Misc. Stuff: Bendis uses his knees allot. If he gets the chance, he’ll nail a hard knee to the face, gut or even to the back.
