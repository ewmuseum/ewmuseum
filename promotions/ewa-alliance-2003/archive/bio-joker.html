Biography sheet: Joker

c. January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Real Name: Serov Barzahd Gimmick Name: Joker Height: 6'0" Weight: 235 lbs Age: 21 years old (October 30th, 1982) Hometown: Unknown, Germany Allignment: Psychopathic Heel Wrestling Style: All-Around but mostly Speedy & Brutal/Psychotic Hair Color/Style: Silver hair, like Sephiroth hair style's Eyes Color: Light Purple Skin Color: White Skin Entrance Theme: No Mercy - Lapis Lazuli Finishers: (1) Dark Murderer *Tiger Suplex '85* (2) Last Regrets *Pumphandle Inverted DDT* Signature Moves: (1) Chaos Strike *Sit-Out Inverted Suplex* (2) Forbidden Rite *Spinning Headscissor to Neckbreaker* Trademark Moves: (1) Shoulder Neckbreaker (2) Sit-Out Jawbreaker (3) Russian Leg Sweep pin (4) Cartwheel Kick (5) Running Spinning Dropkick (6) Flapjack to Dropkick (The opponent comes running back at Joker. Joker grabs the opponent & seem to perform a Flapjack. But he instead pushes the opponent up in the air & hits a standing dropkick in the face/chest area)
(7) Pumphandle Armbar (8) Reverse Kneelock (9) Spinning Boot Scrape (10) Reverse Hurricanrana (11) Full Nelson Facebuster (12) Wristlock Backdrop (13) Springboard Tornado Arm Breaker (14) Moonsault Double Kneedrop (15) Tree of Woe (Joker whips the opponent in a corner & follows up with a knee to the guts. He then grabs the opponent & hang him in reverse on the turnbuckle. Joker gives a couple of stomps, chokes the opponent for 3 seconds & finishes it off with a vicious soccer kick on the throat)
Entrance Description: Darkness surrounds the arena as a thick gray fog covers the entrance stage. No Mercy hits & Joker walks to the center of the stage while looking at the arena from right to left with a sick smile. He then bow his head while crossing his arms in the middle of his chest. After a few seconds Joker rises his head quickly, brings his arms in the air & burst out with a sadistic laughter as flames cover the whole stage rising up to 30 feet in the air. Joker then walks out off the flames heading to the ring. As he climbs the first step of the ring, red flashing lights invade the arena & again a thick gray fog engulf the ring. Joker goes to one turnbuckle & taunt the crowd. He then goes to the opposite one bursting in laughter one last time before sitting on top as the fog dies.
Appearence: Look likes Xion from Bloody Roar 4/Primal Fury. Ring Attire #1: Wears a semi-height red leather jacket with the design of a 3/4 view of a golden skull. The jacket is attach with two red bands forming an X in front of the chest. There's also 2 stripes of metal bands on each forearms of the jacket. Beneath the jacket he wears a fancy black shirt. He also wear a fancy pair of white pants with the remaining bottom of the red leather jacket attach by the waist & both legs(Look at banner for more details). Last he wears some fancy black shoes with stripes of metal bands on the tip of it & above it.
Ring Attire #2: Same as Ring Attire #1 except that both sections of the leather jacket is now colored white instead of red. The design of the skull have disappear & he also wears some black thin gloves.
Ring Attire #3: Joker has his hair tied up with a double ponytails, one that is higher above the other one. He wears a simple dark blue jacket with designes on the forearms of it. On the back of the jacket, we can see the design of black angels wings with a red rose on the middle used as the body. He also wears his black thin gloves. At the bottom, he wears blue jeans & his fancy black shoes with stripes of metal bands on the tip of it & above it.
Biography: Little is known about his childhood. With the except that Serov trainned everyday to become a wrestler. At the age of 17, Serov became mentally ill. His attitude changed dramaticly, acting like an angel to a demon. His parents sent him to Zann Esu asylum because they couldn't control him anymore. Serov Barzahd had spent 3 years in the asylum under the surveillance of Dr.Peabody. Dr.Peabody came up to the conclusion that Serov shares his mind & body with another entity named Joker. Serov is now coming but he's afraid of what his otherself can do. Joker always say that this world will end up like the "other-side". What he means by that is still unclear.
Title History: EWA Survival Champion(Held it for 4 months)
