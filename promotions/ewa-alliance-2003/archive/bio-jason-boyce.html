Biography sheet: Jason Boyce

c. June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Name: Jason Boyce Height: 6’ 5” Weight: 254 lbs Hometown: Phoenix, Arizona Theme Music: Soil – “New Faith” Alignment: Neutral Wrestling Style: Technical, High Flyer, Brawler Finishing Move: Internal Combustion Finishing Move Description: Grapevine STF Secondary Finishing Move: Inebriation Sec. Finishing Move Description: Spinning Orange Crush Powerbomb (Spinning Suplex to Powerbomb) [This can be used on it’s own or to set up Internal Combustion]
Signature Moves: (1) Detonator: Picks opponent up in a fall away slam drops to a knee hitting a backbreaker. Stands back up and flips the opponent up onto his shoulders in a fireman’s carry. Flips him back over and drop to a knee slamming the opponent into a high impact backbreaker.(Will sometimes stay with the opponent on his knee and push the opponents neck and knees down applying pressure to the back)
(2) 360 Spin Spinebuster (3) Triple German Suplex (4) Pump Handle Backbreaker (5) The Vice: Full Nelson Camel Clutch (With one knee on back) Frequently Used Moves: (1) Body Slam (2) Backbreaker (3) Release German Suplex (4) Short Arm Clothesline (5) Spinebuster (6) Atomic Drop (7) Dragon Sleeper (8) DDT Variations (9) Fisherman Suplex with Pin (10) Various Submissions on the Back, Neck and Legs Appearance: In-Ring Attire – Jason has each of his wrists taped. He wears an elbow pad on his left elbow and a pair of black tights. On the left side there is a marking/design in grey/white down the pant-leg. A cross, who’s sides appear to be inflamed circle around the leg.
Out-Of Ring Attire – Jason wears a “No Fear” Hat, usually backwards and a pair of shades. He dresses casual, hardly ever in a suit. His shirt has the same cross from his Tights and a flannel shirt, unbuttoned.
Entrance: The lights begin to dim as “New Faith” by Soil starts to play throughout the Arena. Jason Boyce walks onto the stage and poses. He runs down to the ring and slides himself under the bottom rope. He slides to the middle of the Ring and poses on a knee one last time.
