Wrestler: Matt Wolfe Height: 6'3" Weight:235 Hometown: N.Y.C. Theme Music: Anger by Downset Alignment:Neutral depending on the time and place Wrestling Style: Brawling, Power, Hardcore, technical Finishing Move: The Kill Finishing Move Description: Falcon Arrow pin Signature Moves: 1) Irish whip to pullback elbow strike 2) Wolfe Slam *Angle Slam* 3) Powerfull Spear 4) Fisherman DDT 5) Head slams onto mat 6) Wolfe Trap *Single Leg Crab with Ankle Lock* Frequently Used Moves: 1) Dropkick 2) Shoulder Lariat 3) Suplexe's variation 4) Boston Crab 5) Abdominal Stretch 6) Leg Sweep 7) DDT 8) Powerbomb 9) Senton bomb 10) Irish whip to clothesline Appearance: Long semi dreaded brown hair,with dark red highlights mephisto goatee and mustache, muscular but not ripped. Wears Jeans and black t-shirts around (Untucked) with a faded black leather jacket over the top.
Ring Attire: Outfit #1 To the ring he wears either a black wrestling singlet with wolfe in blood red across the front with thigh high black boots(Look like combat boots not wrestling boots)No pads , he tapes his wrists and hands up to his knuckles, Black on his right hand Maroon on the left. Outfit #2 Second ring attire is the same except for that he wears cammo shorts and a black t-shirt that says in red letters " I went through hell and back, and all the devil gave me was this Fu*king t-shirt" Kwl Kristmas Kaos '99" Entrance: Matt Wolfe's music hits and fog starts to billow up and he walks through it to the ramp.Standing at the ramp top he raises his arms in a V and then does a double cutthroat taunt. He then walks to the ring and limbers up for his match
