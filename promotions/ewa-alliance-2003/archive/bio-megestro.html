Biography sheet: Megestro

c. November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Real Name: Unknown Gimmick Name: Megestro Age: 25 Height: 6'7" Weight: 267 lbs HomeTown: Flint, Michigan Allignment: Heel Wrestling Style: Power Entrance Theme: Many Men(Wish Death) - 50 Cent Manager(s): Krystal Finishers: (1) Mysterious *Crucifix Sit-Out Powerbomb* (2) FlintTown Fear *Modified Texas Clover Leaf* (Megestro locks in the Texas Clover Leaf on his opponent. He then knees on the opponent neck to inflict more damage)
(3) Over Dose *Modified F-5* (Used on only rare occasions) (Megestro gets the opponent in a Fireman Carry position and locks his arm around opponents head. Megestro spins him around like the F-5 and drops to his butt. The oppenents throat and jaw hit Megestro's shoulder)
Signature Moves: (1) Under The Influence (Like the Regal Stretch, but Megestro sits back with it.) (2) Tilt-A-Whirl Back Breaker (3) Powerbomb Onto Knee (4) Gorilla Press Into Powerslam (5) Torture Rack Into Neckbreaker Trademark Moves: (1) Stalling Vertical Suplex (2) Gorilla Press Slam (3) DDT (4) Release German Suplex (5) Japanese Kicks To The Back (6) Short Arm Clothesline (7) Spear (8) Belly To Belly Overhead Suplex (9) Rear Naked Choke (10) Top Rope Leg Drop (11) T-Bone Suplex (12) Boston Crab Entrance: The arena lights go out and nothing is heard. The crowd is wondering what is happeniong as they stand up and look at the Tron. We see a reinactment of a time in Flint. It is dark outside and Megestro is sitting in a Mercedez Benz. It is very out of place in crummy neighbor. Soon another car pulls up. The man gets into Megestro's car and begins to speak.
[The Teen] You got it? [Megestro] Who are you looking at, I always got it! [The Teen] How much? [Megestro] Five pounds is gonna run you six grand. [The Teen] OK, let me check it out. Megestro hands the man the drugs and takes the money. The man looks at it and looks a little sad.
[Megestro] What's wrong? [The Teen] I'm sorry man, I really am sorry. [Megestro] Don't tell me that, come on. You are kidding. Soon six police cars surround Megestros' Benz and the scene fades. Soon in the arena, "Many Men" by 50 Cent(Gen-X Remix) begins to blast throughout the arena. Six large lime green explosions are seen and heard throughout the arena. Megestro and Krystal then begin their slow decent down to ringside. The crowd boos as the drug dealer turned wrestler makes his way up the steal steps, while Krystal waves to the crowd and moves into Megestro's corner. Megestro quickly hops over the top rope and moves to the center of the ring. The arena is lit up with flashes from cameras as he flexes and his music fades off of the PA system. Megestro warms up while waiting for his opponent.
Appearance: Looks similar to WWE Superstar, Sean O'Haire. Megestro is taller, but more muscular. Megestro has a tattoo of a flaming letters that say 'MEGESTRO' that wraps around his right leg. Megestro also has a tattoo that is in between his shouders(and arched) that says 'THE MOST EXTREME' in Old English letters. Both of Megestro's arms have tribal bands around them.
Ring Attire: Megestro wears only spandex shorts that are black in color.
