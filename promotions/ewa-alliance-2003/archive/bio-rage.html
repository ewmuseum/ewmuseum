Biography sheet: Rage

c. January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Real Name: Andrew More Gimmick Name: Rage Height: 6'1 Weight: 195 lbs. Hometown: Pleasantville, New York Theme Music: Alone - Twiztid Alignment: Heel Wrestling Style: Power & Technical Finishers: 1- Destiny Driver *Inverted Death Valley Driver* 2- Clash of Fate *Flapjack to Ace Crusher* Signature Moves 1- Inverted Atomic Drop followed by a Clothesline Frequently Used Moves: DDT spine buster drop toe hold russian leg sweep butterfly suplex full nelson slam backdrop bulldog Appearance: Rage has long black dyed hair that's sholdure length and is braided. He has the build of a normal man. He has a few tatoos across his arms and chest. He has a skull on his face in black and white face paint. In and out of the ring he wears a blank red shirt, long black cargo baggie pants, and black sneakers.
Entrance: (All the lights in the arena dim. The image of a skull flashes on the titontron as Alone blasts through out the arena. Rage walks out onto the stage and flips the crowed off before decending the rampway. When Rage reaches the center of the ramp red pyros explode on either side of him. From there, Rage runs twords the ring and slides in. Once in the ring, Rage walks to the nearest corner and climbs to the second turnbuccle. Rage eyes the crowed and then jumps off the turnbuccle)
