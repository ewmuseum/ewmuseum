Biography sheet: Lotus

c. June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Real Name: Mike Macleod Character's ring name: Lotus Height: 6'1 Weight: 230lbs Age: 27 Hometown: Winnipeg, Manitoba Canada Alignment: Neutral Wrestling Style: Normal Entrance Theme: Blindside - Pitiful Finishers: 7th Layer of Hell --> Powerbomb Deathblow --> Superkick Signature Moves: Dangerous DDT Suplex to outside of ring Frequently Used Moves: DDT Clothesline Snapmare Bulldog Entrance Description: Music hits as Lotus walks out from behind the black curtain. Lights on the ramp flicker on and off. There's a thin layer of smoke in the air as he walks down the ramp. The video played in the backround is of Lotus hitting his opponent with the "7th Layer of Hell". Lotus enters the ring and moves to the corners and stands on the first rope from the bottom looking out at the fans with his arms at his side (not in the air) and then he walks to the middle of the ring and waits for his opponent to arrive.
Appearence: -White face paint with black designs on it (Sting) -Comes to the ring wearing a leather trench coat -Long dark brown/black hair wet down -Semi-muscular build (average) -No tattoos Ring Attire: -Wears a black wrestling outfit with padding on the thighs -has "LoTuS" written across the lower back of his tights -Black leather boots and wrist high gloves Title History: XCWF hardcore champion XCWF heavyweight champion (XCWF has since closed down) Misc. Stuff: Lotus's personality is very similar to Sting. Very mysterious, not much is known about him however some fans find this interesting (making him popular with some) while others find it annoying.
