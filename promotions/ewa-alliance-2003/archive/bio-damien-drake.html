Biography sheet: Damien Drake

c. November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Real Name: Damien Drake Height: 5'10 Weight: 225lbs Age: 25 Hometown: Toronto, Ontario, Canada Alignment: Heel Wrestling Style: High Impact/Martial Arts/High Flyer Entrance Theme: Hells Bells - AC/DC Finishers: The Skull Crusher - A swift spinning back kick to the base of the skull. Drake usually sets the move up by forcing his opponent chest first off the ropes leaving them wide open for a strike to the back of the head on the rebound. The move can still be executed properly without doing that set up though if Drake catches his opponent off guard facing the wrong way. He will even occasionally deliver the same devastating kick to the jaw.
The Spinal Tap - Drake picks his opponent up from a gut wrench position and puts them across his shoulder in a Canadian Back Breaker then drops to his knees forcing their whole body to whiplash downward from the quick and sudden stop. His shoulder drives into the opponents back and does severe damage.
Signature Moves: Replay Shining Wizard - If his opponent ducks the first kick in his shining wizard Drake continues to spin and nails a mule kick with the foot he leapt from.
Hellicanrana - Drake does a front flip off the top rope, or whatever is high enough for him to get over his opponent and lands on his opponent's shoulders then does a hurricanrana.
STO Frequently Used Moves: Snap Suplex Martial Arts Kicking Combos (like Tajiri and Ultimo Dragon) Springboard Kick to the Head (like Rico) Dropkick Leg Sweep Kick Release German Suplex Running STO Calf Kick Half Nelson Suplex Tornado DDT Corkscrew Plancha Entrance Description: The lights in the arena go out as the bells sound from AC/DC's Hells Bells. The lights remain black until the first guitar riffs start to play throughout the arena, when they do a bright red light shines from the entrance way illiuminating the silhouette of Damien Drake. He stands there for a moment until the next bell tolls in which the whole arena turns a shade of red as the lights come back on. Drake slowly walks down to the ring ignoring the boos from the audience until he reaches the steel steps. He quickly climbs them and enters the ring, walking straight for the opposite coner where he props himself up laying across the top rope arrogantly waiting his opponent's arrival.
Appearence: Damien Drake has medium length jet black hair that usually hangs down in his face, and hiis eyes are a very light blue. He has a tribal Dragon tattooed on his right upper arm and a barbed wire arm band tattoo on his left. Outside of the ring he is usually seen wearing a long black trenchcoat with flame designs starting at the cuffs and going back past his elbows and from the bottom going up to the small of his back. His skin is pale, and he usually wears a thin line of black make up around his eyes (like Undertaker). He is also rarely seen without his gold chain around his neck. He also always wears a pair of black sunglasses with deep red lenses.
Ring Attire: In the ring Drake will wear either loose long red pants with black flame designs going up from the bottoms or Black pants with red flames. He wears fingerless black gloves with red flames and his wrists are taped with black hockey tape. His boots are either red or black depending on the pants he wears. Red with black pants, black with red pants.
Biography: Drake is a former UKWA star, then known simply as "The Dragon" since the UKWA shut down however he has taken a few years off. He has put on a few pounds and is now ready to make his long awaited comeback in the EWA. He has dropped the gimmicky name and intends on unleashing his full wrath on the EWA as soon as possible. The former leader of the Egomaniacs has a storied history with another ex-UKWA'er now EWA superstar Sense Green. Whether or not their fued continues is yet to be seen.
Title History: UKWA World Champion UKWA Heavyweight Champion UKWA Undisputed Champion UKWA Hardcore Champion Misc. Stuff: Former leader of the Unstoppable Force and the Egomaniacs in the UKWA
