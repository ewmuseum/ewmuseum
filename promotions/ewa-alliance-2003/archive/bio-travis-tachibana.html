Biography sheet: Travis Tachibana

c. June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Real Name: Travis Tachibana Gimmick Name: Travis Height: 6'2" Weight: 245 lbs Age: 26 years old (May 30th, 1977) Hometown: Kyoto, Japan Allignment: Neutral Wrestling Style: Technical, Street Fighter & some Martial Arts Technic Hair Color/Style: Red hair, Short Eyes Color: Green Skin Color: Tanned Skin Entrance Theme: Edge of the Earth - 30 Seconds to Mars Finishers: (1) Death Bringer *Exploder '98* (2) Phantom Burst *La Ayakita 2000* (Travis stands at the feet of a fallen opponent who is facing up, and ties their legs in front of himself similarly to a Texas Cloverleaf. Travis then bends forward over their tied legs and places his own feet on their arms before grabbing hold of their head and falling backward to apply the finished hold)
Signature Moves: (1) Gravity Crush *Leg Sweep Cobra Clutch* (2) Merciful Arc *Strangle Hold Alpha* (The victim is on their stomach. Travis applies a scissorlock on the victim's arms. Travis rolls in the same direction on the victim's head, forcing the victim's body up at an angle where their neck is supporting their own body weight. At the same time, Travis is trying to hyperextend the victim's elbow across his body)
Trademark Moves: (1) Fireman Carry Takedown (2) Judo Hip Toss (3) Dragon Screw (4) Running Jumping Clothesline (5) Spinning Back Kick (6) Rolling Spinebuster (The opponent comes running back at Travis. Travis grabs the opponent & make a 180 spin before hitting the Spinebuster. Great setup for the Phantom Burst)
(7) Surfboard with Inverted Facelock (The victim is on their stomach. Travis stands on the back of the victim's knees/thighs and locks the victim's ankles behind their legs. He grabs both the victim's arms, pulls back and sits down, bringing the victim up to their knees. From there Travis applies an inverted facelock)
(8) Reverse Achilles Tendon Hold (The victim is on their stomach, Travis grabs one leg by the ankle pushing it forward so the knee is bent)
(9) Kneeling Racked Leg Lock (The victim is on their back, Travis kneels down to grab the victim's leg & places it on his shoulder/neck. He then applies pressure to the thigh and shin with his arms, bending the victim's knee across the back of his neck)
(10) Throwing Sleeperhold (Travis grabs the opponent in a sleeperhold position. After a few seconds, he spins around & throw the opponent away)
(11) Cross Arms German Suplex pin (12) Half Nelson Suplex (13) Corcscrew 450 Splash (14) Corcscrew Moonsault (15) Top Rope Arm Drag Entrance Description: The lights fade to black and some laser lights dazzle around. Edge of the Earth plays and Travis walks down the ramp as blue/silver lights invade the arena. He gets in the ring and poses on top of a turnbuckle. Travis then jump down & runs at the opposite corner. He jumps on the second turnbuckle while crossing his legs. Travis then taunt one last time before the light returns to normal.
Appearence: Looks like Peter Facinelli Ring Attire: Dark Blue Levis Jeans Blue Adidas Shoes Gray Sweatshirt with sleeves rolled up to the elbows Dark Brown Leather Holdster Dark Purple Fingerless Gloves Title History: WXW: Tag-Team Championship (Gekido) Heavy Weight Championship Hardcore Championship EXPW: Darkside Championship Tag-Team Championship (Gekido) WWO: 3 Times Tag-Team Championship (Gekido) TV Championship EWA: 2 Times Tag-Team Championship (with Richard Bison)
