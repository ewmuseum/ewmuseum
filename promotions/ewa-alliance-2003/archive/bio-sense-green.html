Biography sheet: Sense Green

c. November 2004

Name: Sense Green Age: 25 D.O.B: 11-07-1978 Weight: 238lbs Height: 6'1" Hometown: Fife, Scotland Music: Last by Nine Inch Nails Alignment: Face Gimmick(s): Fun Drunk, Rowdy Scotsman, Crazy Wrestling Style: Technical, Hardcore & Brawler Sense is somewhat of a wild-card in the wrestling ring. His background is extensive in technical mat wrestling and manouvere execution and is also quite an accomplished submissionist wrestler, but over the years he has developed a very unorthodox style which utilizes a more "hardcore" approach and allows him to adapt well as a brawler. During a match, Sense can switch from one extreme to the next and easily pull off a quick succession of technical wrestling moves that catch his opponent unaware, before instantly going back into a rougher style of fighting and beating on his enemy. Sense's only drawback to his unique style is his lack of aeriel ability. Although no stranger to climbing the turnbuckle, Sense's movelist whilst on the top rope is rarely ever more extravagant than a missile dropkick or a cross body etc.
Finisher:The Vandaliser Finisher Description: Inverted Fireman Carry to Piledriver Alternate Finisher: The PeaceMaker Finisher Description:: Double Under-Hook Rotating Facebuster http://www.yucube.com/moves/rotating_faceplant.gif Signature Moves: 1) Sense-ible DDT: 180 reverse spinning DDT 2) The Glasgow Kiss: Forceful Headbutt (used as a setup for one of his finishers) 3) The Raging Hang-Over: FrogSplash 4) Judgement: Dragon Sleeper 5) 3) Big Green Bomb:=Spinning Sit-down Powerbomb, landing with leg across opponents neck: http://www.yucube.com/moves/spiral_bomb.wmv Frequently Used Moves: *Sense has a preference for Suplex type of moves* 1) Neckbreaker 2) side-walk slam 3) Gullotine leg-drop from 2nd rope 4) Standing leg-drop 5) Spinning Belly to Belly suplex 6) Samoan Drop 7) Tilt-a-whirl backbreaker Spots: The Square Go: (Usually started after an opponent punches Sense in the head, Sense will basically no-sell the punch and stare the opponent down and hold out his arms as if to say "You want a piece of me" ..although this spot can also start out of nowhere)
Sense will start off by using both hands to shove his opponent back (as if trying to provoke a fight) before following up with a hook to the face. As the opponent is reeling from this blow, Sense goes in with a series of punches (4 or 5) to the lower body, forcing his opponent either into the corner or against the ropes, then moving up and working the upper body with another handful of punches, before delivering a stiff left hook, then a stiff right hook and finishing the spot with the Glasgow Kiss, Headbutt. Usually following with a finisher. (Note: If a Scotsman ever asks you for a "Square-Go", it means he's challenging you to a one-on-one fist fight)
Appearance: Sense has a very stocky, broad shouldered, broad chested build. He has very short dark-brown hair, shaved down to the shortest possible length with-out becoming completely bald and a brown goatee beard. Green deep set eyes give him his trademark eerie glare. His head sports several healed-up cuts and scars from fights, bar-room brawls and wrestling matches from his past.
Ring Attire: Sense does not wear conventional wrestling apparel and instead chooses to wear a Dark Green and Black basketball jersey. His lower half is covered by a pair of grey and green military-style camo short-pants which reach past his knee's and heavy black boots.
He also wears white gauze wrapped around his hands (not fingers) and wrists whilst wrestling.
Ring Entrance: The Arena sound system emits a crackle of electrical energy and the lights descend to almost absolute darkness. A cheer raises from the crowd as a green-lit smoke bellows from the vents under the Big screen and the distinct guitar riff of 'Last' by Nine Inch Nails, blasts forth from the speakers. As the beat and the rythym echoes around the building, the unmistakable vocal stylings of Trent Reznor fills the ears of every man, woman and child in the arena, and the entrance is cast in an incandescant green light just as the curtain is thrown aside and the deranged Scotsman, Sense Green strides out to the centre of the concourse. The fans holler and Sense begins to work them, stomping heavily to the left of the ramp and banging his head in time with his entrance music enciting a resounding cheer. After a brief second, Sense turns and stomps his way over to the far right of the ramp-way and throws both arms in the air, garnering yet another solid pop. Sense returns to the centre of the entrance walk and proceeds to make his way down toward the ring with a confident and aggressive stride of a focused man. Finally Sense approaches the ring and extends his hands toward the top rope, pulling himself up onto the apron. He pauses for a second, catsing his glance across the arena and over the cheering fans, he throws one arm in the air to recieve another loud pop and then drops backdown onto the floor below. Sense strides around to the opposite side of the ring, playing to the fans as he goes by and rolls in under the bottom rope. Sense gets up onto his knee's and throws both arms back in the air, before pulling himself onto his feet, ready and prepared to fight.
Bio: Sense is a long serving wrestler, beginning his career in various small-time Scottish promotions were he built his name and honed his talents to a fine degree over many years. However after a dissappointing run with a large scale U.S federation, where he was forced to play up to a comical and degrading stereotype of his own Scot's people, Sense became bitter with the world of "Sports Entertainment" wasting the skills of talented athletes and moved to the orient where he further developed his abilities and made a name for himself as a viscous and dangerous force.
In 2002, Sense returned home to British shores, signing with the United Kingdom Wrestling Alliance (UKWA) and still bitter about his humiliation in the States, he began a reign of terror amongst the U.K wrestling roster. His unholy persona and ominous approach earned him the nickname 'The Judge'.
The UKWA did wonders for Sense's career and his irratic, chaotic behaviour even gained him some fan's despite his wicked attitude.
Sense experienced an injury whilst competing in the UKWA after turning against thefederations controlling faction which gained him a bigger fanbase but also put him on the shelf for several months, until his return in December 2002. However Sense returned with a new attitude, taking his previous wild (and almost comical) antics even further and adopting a whole new approach to the fans of the UKWA. Sense had found the appreciation and respect he had yearned for in the UKWA and as such this had a profound effect on him. He dropped his mysterious "Judge" persona and became the chaotic, unpredictable, Deranged Scotsman.
Sense experienced greater success with his new attitude and was even crowned the UKWA Undisputed Champion, a title with which he would hold until his retirement from the UKWA.
However Sense's retirment did not last long as the Scotsman insatiable urge to climb between the ropes and walk the canvas took hold, he made his way back across the pond to the WXW and then onto the WWW. Now the drunken, deranged one has arrived in the EWA and from there......Who knows??
Misc Stuff: Sense is a fun-loving, wild, slightly irrational, slightly unpredicatable, comical, drunken, deranged Scotsman. Yet when in the ring, Sense is an experienced and accomplished athlete, remaining focused and intense. Despite his wayward lifestyle, Sense takes the sport very seriously, never getting (overly) drunk before a match and fighting with a spirit.....He also likes cup-cakes very much.......and that's about all ye need to know!
