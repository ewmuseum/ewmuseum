Biography sheet: Mark Fletcher

c. November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Name: Mark Fletcher Gimmick Name: “Mr Extreme” Mark Fletcher Height: 5’11 Weight: 217lbs Age: 18 Hometown: Toronto, Ontario, Canada. Alignment: Face Wrestling style: Cruiser style but will try high-risk, high-flying moves near the end of the match.
Sometimes Mark will go technical, and work on the shoulder/neck region, and try to get his opponent to submit.
Entrance Theme: One of a Kind, Breaking Point. (RVD theme) Taunts: Pre-match: On apron: Two arms out, look up. Pre-match: In ring: Two arms out, standing on bottom rope, leaning out. Before hitting The Extreme: Two arms up vertically. Post-match: On turnbuckles, two arms raised. However, if it was a brutal match he'll just leave.
Finishers: The Extreme: Reverse Death Valley Driver. Proceeded by a kick to the gut and a taunt, often followed by a pin or attempt to win the match, or sometimes a move off the top. Only usually used on Cruiserweights or light heavyweights.
Spear: Mark will usually do this move after hitting a signature move to knock the opponent down, and then hit him when he gets up (Goldberg/Edge style waiting in the corner.) However, sometimes when Mark is against a heavyweight, Mark will often try to do this move quickly to buy some time and possibly hit a move off the top.
Signature moves: The Fletch Stretch: Mark jumps at his opponent from behind and raps his legs around his opponent’s waist line. Mark then pulls his opponent to the ground while locking in a cross face chicken wing.
Shooting Star Press Asai moonsault Hurricanrana Northern lights suplex Frequently used moves: Sunset flip Arm drag into arm bar Back slide Kicking combinations Drop kick variations Vertical suplex Back drop Neckbreaker Top rope cross body Knees to gut Entrance description: The lights go out and One of a Kind by Breaking Point starts to play. The crowd start to cheer, but soon are drowned out by a big explosion of white fireworks from the entrance straight up into the air as the arena is illuminated, showing Mark Fletcher on the stage.
Fred: Making his way to the ring, from Toronto Canada, weighing in at 217lbs, Mr Extreme, Mark Fletcher!
The fans cheer as Mark walks down to the ring, slowly, waving to the crowd. Mark walks to the stairs and slowly ascends. He walks to the centre of the ring apron, facing the fans. He stretches his arms out and looks up into the air. The fans cheer as Mark turns and gets into the ring. Mark walks over to the other side of the ring, steps on the bottom rope, puts his arms out and leans out. Mark stands in the middle of the ring and takes his coat off and gives it to a man outside.
Appearance: Doesn’t have huge muscles, but is still quite strong for his size. He has black hair which comes down a bit past the back of his neck. He has dark eyes. His closest look-a-like is Johnny Nitro.
Attire: For match: Wears short blue lycro shorts with the letters MF in red on the back. He wears blue boots with white trim. He wears a blue coat with circular shoulders to the ring. The coat is highly decorated with gold tassels and strings. There are gold studs on the back spelling out Mr Extreme at diagonals across the back. There are gold buttons keeping the coat done up. He takes the coat off before he starts to fight. He has MF in gold italics over his left breast.
No match: Mark has abandoned his blue jeans and white top and now wears smarter clothes. Black shirts, expensive jackets, etc.
Biography: Mark has had a relatively short wrestling career. He was taught how to wrestle by his father, John Fletcher. John Fletcher won several titles and had an illustrious career, before falling ill when Mark was starting up in the EWA. Despite this, Mark won the EWA Rising Star Title. He joined the Powers That Be, and helped overcome the Ministry. Mark lost the title to Scoot Williams, and the title was changed to the Cruiserweight Title. Mark lost his re-match, then tried to join GX, but got rejected. Mark then joined The Darkside, but was fired in December after losing to Cerberus in a You’re Fired match, with Camaro giving Cerberus the win. Mark then joined the Canadian Nation Wrestling Federation, CNWF, and won the CNWF Cruiserweight Title in a memorable ladder match. Mark held the title, and didn’t lose it. Mark became the first person to conquer both Cruiserweight and Heavyweight divisions when he won the CNWF Canadian Title. Mark then forfeited the Cruiserweight Title and focused on the Canadian Title. Mark heard rumours that the EWA was reviving its Cruiserweight division, and quit the CNWF, retiring the Canadian Title. For this reason Mark will often refer to himself as the Canadian Champion. Mark then came back to the EWA, back to the big leagues.
