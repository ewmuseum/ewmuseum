EWA (Alliance, 2003–10) — News

News: 27 August to 4 September 2004

4 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
September 4th The first round for the EWA Northern Continental Championship has been decided on the part of Shockers. Dragon and Psyko have both won their matches and will now face off against each other in a match next week to decide who goes on the
Unsanctioned to face the winner from the Wreckoning tournament.
The Regulators (Brett Cotter and Michael Bendis) became the Tag Team champions for the second time after beating Gekido (Travis
Tachibana and Tetsuo Takemura). The win came with allot of controversy however as both The Regulators and Gekido fell victim to a brutal attack by all members of Generation-X. G-X cost Gekido the titles by putting Cotter over Travis. G-X was out of sight when the referee came to and made the three count. EMT’s came to look after the four battered men in the ring, but the surprises were not over as Cross made his way to the ring and dismantled Brett Cotter some more as
Shockers went off the air.
EWA Shockers results - Kavoxx def. Casey “Bonz” Masters - Psyko def. “Superbeast” Scott Lee and advanced in the Northern Continental Championship Tournament - Vincent “Dragon” Crow def. Ryan Ambrose and advanced in the Northern Continental Championship Tournament
- Cross def. Damien Drake - The Regulators def. Gekido to become Tag Team Champions September 3rd New contract signings! The EWA is happy to welcome Dragonfly, Derek “Ice” Portman, Victor Kraze, Michael
James and Matt X to the active roster. There are currently discussions about on what show the new signings will make their debut. Keep a close eye to both shows to see when they will appear!
September 2nd The Superbeast joins the Ballbusters Inc! It’s official. The EWA is glad to inform you that the BBI have a new member. Scott Lee a.k.a. the Superbeast has joined the ranks of the BBI. The BBI is quickly gaining allot of fans and rumours have it that a clash with Generation-X is inevitable!
September 1st The new Wreckoning has kicked off! Wreckoning, now under control of Eric Davis sr. kicked off with the debut of his very own “Enforcer”, Jake Cross. On his first day on the job, Jake “The Enforcer” Cross took care of some disturbances already. He literally threw interviewer Paul McKane out of the building after Eric Davis has banned him from EWA Wreckoning programming. Later that night, he made sure that Rage didn’t cause any more disturbances after he assaulted Michael Bendis, who was having an interview with Belinda. He also took care of Joker whom Eric
Davis senior calls “Just a “nuisance” and easy to be dealt with.
Jake Cross was not the only one that made his debut on Wreckoning. Scheduled to appear for the first time on Shockers, Kavoxx came out and helped Ryan Ambrose win his match, but to no avail as Superbeast put a stop to that.
In other shocking events at Wreckoning, Merce turned on Megestro, cost him the match and joined Generation X! Merce nailed a Big
Boot to the face of Megestro and Bison and Dragon joined in on beating down
Megestro. Afterwards Merce’s explanation for joining G-X was that he was sick and tired of not ever getting a push.
Wreckoning Results - Rage def. Superbeast by pinfall - Joker def. Camo by pinfall in a Street Fight. - Michael Bendis def. Ryan Ambrose - Richard Bison and Megestro fought to a no contest. Special referee: Merce. Afterwards Eric Davis sr. declared Megestro the winner, but the moral loser.
- Sense Green def. Immortal and Damien Drake in a triple threat match for the EWA Survival Championship. August 28th The new championship has been introduced! It'll be called the Northern Continental Championship. Contestants for the title are: "Superstar" Ryan Amborse, Superbeast, Psyko, Hall of Famer Vincent "Dragon" Crow, Michael Bendis, Merce Galivcef, Megestro and last but not least EWA's resident superhero Alexander. The contestants will be put into a tournament that will be held on both Shockers and Wreckoning. The final of the tournament will be held at the Pay per View Synergy.
The Tag titles are coming back! After feeling robbed of the chance to defend the Tag Team properly, Michael Bendis and Brett Cotter started a campaign for the re-instatement of the Tag team titles and with success! Jared Stark finally gave in to their wishes and The
Regulators (Michael Bendis and Brett Cotter) well get their Tag Team Title shot on Shockers the 4th of September.
Shockers Results - Casey “Bonz” Masters def. Bionic Brad Grayston by default - “Mr Extreme“ Mark Fletcher def. Dave “The Destroyer” Douglas by pinfall - Brett Cotter def. Cross by disqualification - Richard Bison & Vincent “Dragon” Crow def. Gekido (Travis
Tachibana & Tetsuo Takemura) by pinfall
