Onslaught Jonathan Lee Slayer Brett Cotter Sense Green Tetsuo Takemura Introduction Hello and welcome to the official site of the Extreme Wrestling Alliance! This year marks the EWA's 7th year and we're not planning on stopping anythime soon! What can the EWA offer YOU? Fully Written Results We provide fully written match results so that you can see your character come to life (Sometimes, fully written results may appear a day or two later due to RL issues, but match resumes will always appear on time).
A great OOC atmosphere! Creative Control If you have an idea for your character than tell the admins! We'll do everything in our power to make it work! It is strongly encouraged that you come up with your own storylines, because it is believed that it will better YOUR RP'ing experience. Of course, you are not required to create you're own storylines if you don't want to.
The Mind Behind Get to know the creator behind the EWA Superstar! Money A concept that we have tried in the past with great succes has been reintroduced recently. In teh EWA, you get paid. Not with real money, but virtual money which your character can use to buy things like gimmick matches for example. Gimmick matches are free when they fit your feud, but if you want a random gimmick match just for the fun of it, you can buy that in the store.
