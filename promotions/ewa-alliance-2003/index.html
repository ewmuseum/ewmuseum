A text-and-roleplay federation on the fwrestling.com hub whose own pages date six titles reign by reign from April 2003 to 2005, show the Generation X, Regulators, Gekido and Ballbusters Inc teams, a Monday Night Shockers weekly and Kingdom Come, Sinners and Adrenalyn Rush pay-per-views, and list the champions of four more belts to 2008. Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Extreme Wrestling Alliance's own title pages date its first champions to the spring of 2003: Dragon is its first European Champion and Joker its first Survival Champion on 5 April 2003, and Camaro, whom his own biography calls the first EWA Champion, won the Heavyweight tournament final at Kingdom Come on 10 May 2003. Its 2004 and 2005 pages date every reign of six belts (Heavyweight, European, Survival, Tag Team, Cruiserweight and TV) and record three unifications with titles of the WWO (the Heavyweight, European and TV belts), a federation whose wrestlers the owner's biography says he contracted for the EWA and whose closing the Richard Bison biography records. The pages show Generation X, the Regulators, Gekido and Ballbusters Inc, and the cards of two pay-per-views, Adrenalyn Rush in April 2004 and Sinners in May.

By 2006 its news calls the fed 'also known as The Nuthouse', welcomes new wrestlers in batches and says that an e-fed database, Roughkut, where the EWA advertised, has blacklisted it. The 2008 pages run a Monday Night Shockers weekly under three administrators, Richard Bison, Treize Delacroix and Slayer, with Legacy, Internet and Breakthrough Championships and a Hall of Fame of sixteen. The rules accept 'only original (fake) wrestlers', judge a weekly roleplay on four factors and allow one card a week and one pay-per-view a month. The Kingdom Come 7 page of February 2009 announces a Legacy and Prime Time title unification, and the front page, copyright 2003 to 2010, says the EWA is in its seventh year. Nothing later is dated.
