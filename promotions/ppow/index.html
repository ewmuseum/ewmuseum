A newsgroup federation, Paramount Powers Of Wrestling, founded 25 June 1997 and attested through one full show script, structured here as a closing battle royal, before its record ends with an August 1997 taping. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Fueled Sports, Inc. presented Paramount Powers Of Wrestling to rec.sport.pro-wrestling.fantasy on 25 June 1997 as a dice-based roleplaying federation, deliberately kept small — “25-35 wrestlers,… TOPS!” — and running two named shows, “Attitude,” its WCW Saturday Night-type program, and “Propane,” its prime-time hour. It pointed prospective handlers at a companion project on the same site, a tournament called the Elite Powers Of Wrestling.

Its one surviving broadcast, Attitude's first edition on 5 August 1997, opens on a staged hillside set catching fire during the intro and hosts Jeremy “Load” Bullinger and Kyle “42” Briggs welcoming a crowd the broadcast puts at over 2,500. The card runs four undercard matches before its main event: the White Knights, Paladin and Crusader, lose by disqualification to the Modern Day Sioux, Tonto Kowalski and Tommy Hawk, in a rematch from the Elite Powers Of Wrestling tournament; Deathmask wins on his American debut over Johnny Storm; and the Forces of Nature, Grizzly Greg Adams and Condor Corey Man, face the Fighting Eskimos, the Mighty Quinn and the Gyrfalcon Nanook Iklaut.

The main event is a ten-man battle royal for a spot in a small tournament to determine a challenger for Mr. Bodybag's championship — Bodybag himself unseen since an earlier Elite Powers Of Wrestling pay-per-view. Four survive: the Violent Librarian, Jack Robinson, Tun Mi Lung Ho, and Fallen Angel, who are jumped immediately afterward by the Yakuza Hit Squad, Toshi and Manu Gowi, before Jack Robinson turns Toshi's own kendo stick against Manu Gowi to clear the ring.

No document past that taping survives. The promotion's own record ends roughly six weeks after it opened, with no closing statement of its own.
