Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The GFWA, fielded out of Ball State University by a founder known only as Tom, was already a little over a month old by its 14 October 1995 recruitment post — “the excitement makes it seem like it has been around forever!” — placing its own founding in early-to-mid September.

By that October it had already crowned a full title slate: Dreadlord held the GFWA World Championship, Men Of Science the World Tag Team Championship, and Lura Silver the World Women's Title. It had also announced a co-promoted supercard with the IEWL.

No closure statement of its own survives, though a trace of the GFWA lingers in the community's own memory as late as June 1996. What became of it, or of the supercard it announced, is not in the surviving record.
