Real Name: Hope Lee Birthdate: 02/02/79Hometown: Charlotte, North CarolinaCurrent Residence: Miami, FloridaCurrent Feds: Anything Goes Wrestling Past Feds: WWE Developmental ProgramTitles Held: Honors: Personal Information: Favorite Food:  Seafood Favorite Band:  Nine Inch Nails Favorite Song: "Tainted Love" by Soft Cell Favorite Movie: The Scorpion King Favorite Color:  Purple Finisher: Hope Chest (Springboard Flying Body Press) Favorite move:  Springboard Jumping Spinning DDT Favorite Actor:  Val Kilmer and The Rock (of course!) Favorite Actress:  Angelina Jolie Hobbies: Swimming, Hiking, Hangin' with Dwayne at home. History: Hope started out as a teen model, TV commercial actress and gymnast. At 18, she was working as a physical trainer for a small heath club where she was approached by a young Dwayne Johnson to help get him in shape for a Professional Wrestling career. She went to one of his School's promotional shows and was immediately hooked. She began her wrestling training almost immediately and took to it very quickly. She was told that at her height (5' 6") she would never be anything more than a valet or manager. Undaunted, she continued wrestling in the independent leagues, making a name for herself as one of the most talented, energetic and brutal female wrestlers in the circuit. She was finally offered a developmental deal with the (then titled) WWF, thanks to a recommendation from Dwayne (now wrestling for the WWF as Rocky Maiavia). She spent several frustrating years in the WWF developmental program, where she was continually harassed and hit-on by the male wrestlers, managers and developmental staffers. Frustrated, she left the WWF in pursuit of Dwayne (Now known internationally as "The Rock"). She followed him to the AGW and is now trying desperately to make a real name for herself in the world of Professional Wrestling.
