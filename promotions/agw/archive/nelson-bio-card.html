Nelson NELSON Name: Nelson Handler: [name withheld] Height: 6’10” Weight: 372 lbs Finisher: Battle of Trafalgar (elevated powerbomb), Regal Stretch. Titles: AGW TV Title, AGW Triple Crown Tag Champion (AGW World Title, Anything Goes Title, Unified Tag Team Champion) Awards: AGW Rookie of the Year Slammy award (2002), AGW Most Potential (2003 could be their year…) Slammy Award. Nelson first burst onto the AGW screen at Ultimate Survivor 2002, impressing in his very first match (a 5-a-side elimination match) in the AGW by utterly decimating the Order of Snakes, eliminating Snake Eye, Paul Freaker, and Serpent Man!  Nelson could hardly have hoped for a better start to his AGW career and it was not long before people began sitting up and taking notice of the newcomer. Nelson, as his name suggests, is something of an expert of British Naval history, his childhood idol always being the courageous Admiral Nelson.  It was a happy childhood for Nelson, living with his father Stephen Regal and mother on the road.  But the rigours of the road soon became too much for Lord Stephen Regal – despite a series of WCW Television titles, Regal turned to drink to try and cope with the enormous stress of a wrestling career.  It wasn’t long before Nelson’s mother divorced her drunkard husband, causing him to spiral off the rails even more, eventually being sacked by WCW.  The young Nelson clung fiercely to his father, even going so far as to totally lose contact with his mother, but it was no good.  Lord Stephen Regal passed away from liver failure when Nelson was 8. He was distraught, but was soon taken into the care of his Uncle – William Regal. It was here that Nelson’s life took a severe shift in direction – one for the worse.  William Regal’s cold hearted, callous ways and teachings began rubbing off on the boy.  It wasn’t long before an imperialistic sneer sat atop Nelson’s lip to match his Uncles, his kind hearted ways forgotten.  Nelson’s father had always encouraged him to stay away from wrestling as a career, but William Regal was different.  He knew a money spinner when he saw one, and began moulding Nelson into the quality wrestler he is today, teaching him everything he knew. Nelson desired adventure though.  He left his fuming Uncle to join the Royal Navy, where he was a huge success – if only for his innate abilities learnt from his Uncle to bully, meaning that those who attempted to pick on him soon found themselves to be the victims.  The entire ship he served on was afraid of him, and he loved it, despite his only being 17 years old.  He had been in the Navy one year when he attempted to bully yet another new recruit, who stood up to him.  Nelson was so shocked and humbled at the youthful defiance, despite horrific bullying, that he vowed never to bully again.  From then on, he was far more pleasant, the memory of his father and not his Uncle once again in his heart. And then he met Jo.  Jo Hardy (no relation to the Hardy Boyz), also 17, sat crying after being dumped by a boyfriend in Portsmouth docks, where Nelson’s ship was stationed for shore leave.  She felt a hand on her shoulder, and she looked up into Nelson’s eyes.  Nelson was overpowered by her beauty, charm and wit, and she his.  They fell madly in love and a whirlwind romance followed.  They were married in Canterbury Cathedral, Jo’s hometown. Their happiness would not last however.  At his 20th birthday, Nelson drunkenly had a fling with a Navy wren serving on the same ship as him – Jo found out before Nelson had a chance to confess. Heartbroken, Jo fled straight into the arms of another – William Regal. But Nelson was unaware of this, and continued to be unaware until recently.  Bitter about losing Jo, he reverted to his old ways and became the precocious brat the Naval world had so come to fear.  He had been winning the annual Land vs Air vs Sea wrestling matches the armed services holds ever since he arrived, but it was not until 2002 that he, at the behest of his Uncle, left the Navy and joined the AGW.  Within a month, he had won the TV title and formed the formidable Britannia tag team with his uncle.  It would not be long though before Regal turned on his nephew, leaving Nelson heartbroken once again.  Nelson found strength within the cheering of the crowds, and resolved to be their champion in an otherwise corrupt federation.  He attempted to exact brutal revenge on his Uncle, but the slippery Regal always managed to escape him. Growing up, Nelson had always been a fan of Most Holy. He was his favourite TV wrestler, even back in the EEMWF days.  Nelson had opposed the Clique and it’s merciless leader Mark Paris since it’s reformation, but was moved to action when they crippled the Mostest on live TV.  Nelson was incensed, and began a programme of bloody vengeance on Paris and his cronies, even at one point chokeslamming Speed Racer through a ring.  Nelson himself was beaten down again and again by The Clique’s superior numbers, but eventually was instrumental in driving Paris back into exile and with it bringing down The Clique’s tyranny. During this period, increasingly deflated by his seeming never ending quest against The Clique, Nelson called on AGW Legend Smooth Operator to help him.  After pestering him for weeks, Smooth agreed to help, and became Nelson’s new mentor.  After driving away The Clique however, the massively popular Smooth shocked the world by turning on Nelson and becoming a fan hating, good guy bashing super heel.  Nelson, once again, was incensed.  These two had a series of long running battles, absolute hatred for the other forcing them on time and time again.  This feud eventually culminated in one of the most brutal matches the AGW has ever seen, a two out of three falls Insane Death Match, which Nelson won although neither man will ever be the same. With Smooth vanquished, Nelson’s body gave up on him and he was taken to hospital.  Despite not being in much condition to walk, let alone wrestle, he answered Smooth’s taunts to come back and was about to step into a handicap match against the big man when he was saved by an unlikely ally – Eric Von Doom. Eric and Nelson went on to become the hugely popular Damage Incorporated tag team, complete with the infamous D-Day Landings finisher.  They captured the AGW Tag Team Triple Crown (the first tag team to do so) at the very first attempt and all was rosy.  Sadly, the deposed World Tag Champs, The Ghetto Boyz, were not finished.  As Eric was attacked in his locker room, Nelson was driven to a lake and pushed into it in an empty car.  He nearly drowned, and was in hospital for almost a month.  He returned, and disposed of the Ghettoz with his usual impassioned zeal, but things were not the same with Eric.  Nelson blamed him for not saving him from the near drowning.  Eric blamed Nelson for being stupid enough to try and take on the Ghettoz alone in the first place.  Damage Incorporated broke up, and also marked the beginning of Eric’s hiatus from the AGW world. Nelson was now faced with a new challenge – Mark Payne, the Blonde Bastard who had pinpointed Nelson out as a great stepping stone to the World Title.  Nelson would not take that lying down, and (with the help of his good friend Paladin) the two squared off in a two out of three falls match, a Texas Death match, and (fittingly) a two out of three falls Texas Death Match.  The latter was again a contender for most brutal AGW match ever, as most of the Blonde Bastard’s matches are.  It was he who emerged victorious.  Nelson, for the first time in his AGW career, tasted the bitter tang of defeat. And he did not like it.  Bitter, angry, and hurt at his failings and the re-emergence of Jo Hardy at his Uncle’s side, Nelson became selfish and miserable.  He tortured himself with his lack of AGW belts. He convinced himself it was the older generation of AGW talent that was holding him back, and it was all of these factors that lead him to accept Speed Racer’s offer to lead the New Blood incursion as revealed at Ultimate Survivor, 2003.  Nelson, marking his anniversary in the only way he knew how, viciously attacked his supposed team-mates before revealing himself as the mystery assailant. The figure head of the New Blood movement, Nelson is the perfect candidate, being as he is only just barely 22 years old.  Who will win out?  Youth or experience?  Battle has commenced – and Nelson is as usual in the thick of it.
