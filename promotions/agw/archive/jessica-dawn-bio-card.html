Jessica Dawn JESSICA DAWN For more pics of the All-American Jessica Dawn, click here: Name: "Queen of Hardcore" Jessica Dawn Height: 5'6" Weight: 133 lbs. Background: Jessica has always been keen on sports, her cat, and the United States of America.  She played soccer throughout her college career- from which she emerged with a major in political science.  Seeing not much open in the job market Jess first became interested in wrestling after a friend of hers introduced her to the sport. After observing for awhile Jess tried her hand in a local indy fed. During this time she was spotted by an AGW rep and the rest is history.  Jess has been training with the Paladin for years, and her hardcore fighting style and intense focus in the ring have only improved because of it.  Her hardcore tactics, cat-like agility, and seductively good looks have gained her the moniker "Queen of Hardcore".  In between her most recent times in an AGW ring she provided training at the Racer Academy for the women wrestlers there. Appearance: Jessica has flowing black hair which she wears in a pony tail and a knockout body.  Her ring attire consists of black boots and tight pants with gaps which allow her stunning legs to be appreciated.  A rainbow bra finishes off her outfit.  She is often seen with a smile on her face. Theme Music: "Maneater" - Hall & Oates Finisher: Hardcore Plunge (Top rope Reverse Frankensteiner) Secondary Finisher: Catwalk Frankensteiner Weapon of choice: Chair wrapped in barbed wire Titles held: AGW: Woman's World Champion (3x) Woman's Tag Team Champion Favorites: Band: Cold Play and anything 80s Movie: "Swimfan" Book: Any mystery or good horror Color: Dark Green Food: A really good cheeseburger and hot fries
