Thank You from the President Himself

15 December 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello RSPWFers, I am the president of Anything Goes Wrestling, of which we had 8 guys in the RSPWF 200. I can't say enough how great it is to see some of our great handlers get the respect many of them have worked hard for. (But be sure to check out last year's poll, when we had a decent turnout as well.) As Steve, handler of the AGW's Chocolate Thunder, said in another post, we are indeed a SIMMED league. We also have some "real" wrestlers in it although 95% of them are totally different than their real life counterparts. I've been running the league for over 3 years now and most of the people who have ever been in it say it is the best league they've ever been in, booked or simmed. Why? Because I DO partially book things, to make angles work and reward those who deserve it. I can understand much of the hatred towards simmed feds because they generally don't last (then again, neither do booked feds) and may seem like the owner's cheap way out. But I have worked my butt off to make the AGW as entertaining as possible and having our top 8 guys in the top 100 of the RSPWF 200 is an awesome feat. Hell, we even won the Netscape Editor's Choice award for best e-fed....not a bad feat for a lowly simmed league, eh? :) Seriously though, I'm not about to berate anyone's preferences as to booked or simmed....since there are so few simmed leagues out there and even fewer good, fair and well-run ones. Booked feds are the kings of e-wrestling, but I don't see why the AGW can't weasel it's way into the upper echelon of the RSPWF. :) I do like booked leagues and have been in some, although they generally do not last. I am proud to say that my wrestler, Speed Racer, has made the RSPWF 200 four years running....most of which wrestling in simmed feds though he did make a stop to the WWO, which was a lot of fun until I ran out of time to be in it. So basically if you're curious to see what the AGW is like, go to http://agw.freeservers.com We actually are looking for a few good handlers right now although we're far from desperate. Even if you don't want to join, come on by and make a comment in the OOC guestbook about what you think of the league, webpage or whatever else you want. And remember, even though we're simmed, don't judge this book by it's cover. We've had guys who were solely in booked feds before who dumped them all to be with us. Come in with an open mind, and I'll appreciate what you have to say. If you fill out an app., it's a bonus to both of us. Trust me, you won't regret it. But I would like to thank the RSPWF and [name withheld] first and foremost. I also want to thank all of the handlers of the AGW, because the league would not exist if it wasn't for you guys. We have easily had some of the best and original handlers throughout the e-wrestling world, guys whose posts put 99% of the others I've seen in my long e-wrestling career to shame (you haven't lived until you've seen a Mark Payne, Chocolate Thunder, Most Holy or Mark Paris post). I also want to thank my westler, Speed Racer, for still being enjoyable to run despite the fact that people insist he's a cartoon, and not a really fast wrestler. :) I want to thank anyone who has had positive things to say about the AGW as a whole here in the dog-eat-dog RSPWF. And I want to thank YOU if you made it all the way through this message. I just want you all to be sure that I was not putting down booked feds, I just don't want a league like the AGW to be characterized along with the other simmed feds which 'constantly' happens. The AGW truly is a league of it's own, not needing to conform to the norm of simmed leagues and yet a far cry from all other simmed leagues. Thanks a lot folks, and I hope Gib can find a suitable replacement, so we can have even more guys in the top 200 come next year. :) Be well. [name withheld] President of the AGW http://agw.freeservers.com [detail withheld] , [detail withheld] Handler of Speed Racer No. 51 in the '99 RSPWF 200 (70something in '98, 45th in '97 and 142nd in '96) Current AGW President and commentator and occasional wrestler Somewhat of a free agent, so if interested.........you know what to do
