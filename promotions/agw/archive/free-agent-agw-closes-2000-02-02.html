[Free Agent] AGW Closes, Chocolate Thunder Becomes A Free Agent!

2 February 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey Guys and Gals, I really don't know how much this little note will mean to all of you, but I am sure there are at least a few of you out there that will read this, take my offer seriously and drop me line. Therefore, I feel it is well worth my time to both write this and post it.
But anyway. [name withheld]'s "Anything Goes Wrestling" league had a heck of a year this year. This upcoming year would have been the league's fourth, I believe. However, at the conclusion of the last pay per view, the inevitable did happen and the fed is closing. Before I go on any further with some of my personal goals and the such, I would like to say a few good things about the AGW and [name withheld].
First of all, this was indeed a grand year for the AGW. We put something like 6 guys on the top 200 list. And all were within the top 100. However we still got our fair share of "crap." People said that we were a simmed league and therefore did not belong. Others even claimed that we were such a big league that we could sort of monopolize the votes. The AGW is a huge a fed with probably over 30 handlers. However, when the truth is told, the facts come out. And one of those facts is this: on our AGW News Site, we polled the league and it turns out that only about 6 or 7 people voted!
That means that their choices were fairly unanimous. Simmed or otherwise, the AGW really was one of the greatest E-Feds of all time. And it was definitely one of the most underrated.
As for Scott: he handled Speed Racer, which could all but speak for itself. He has been on the top 200 list since he started. His simmed character is probably one of the most successful of all time. He pulled double duty as well. Not only did he handle a great character, but he ran the AGW. He dedicated much of his free time to fantasy wrestling. His hard work paid off as each of his endeavors proved to be overwhelmingly successful. My thanks go out to [name withheld] for providing me with a great federation to
handle my wrestler. I also want to congratulate him on all of his success. Now, that being said, I want to remind everybody that the AGW is closing its doors. Many of the handlers are getting together and starting a new fed called the VWA, I believe. However, I personally do not want to go that route. The closing of the AGW is my chance to explore new options. But again, before I get to that, I would like to point out that the AGW can be accessed at
http://agw.freeservers.com . I suggest that you check it out and read over the role plays. I handle Chocolate Thunder. I haven't role played in about two weeks so you may have to do some searching to find my work. You can feel free to advertise your site on the OOC Board as well.
As for Chocolate Thunder. I guess you can call him a free agent. It's been years since I have handled a "free agent." Chocolate Thunder is a character with three years of buildup. I would like to prove to the RSPWF World that my top 25 ranking this year was no fluke. So I am looking for an established booked fed. I need quality competition so that I can really prove my worth. Anyway, if you know of a good fed that could use my talents, or- better yet- if you own one, please email me at
[detail withheld] Again, I handle Chocolate Thunder. I believe he was #18 or so on the top 200. He has been successful throughout his career. I have been in one booked fed in my life. When the fed closed, I retired undefeated. Of course, I need a little better competition then that =) I should point out, though, that while I may join a fed now, I won't be role playing for about a month. The winter is a busy sports season for me. I'm an all state high school basketball player and I only do this e-wrestling stuff in my spare time. Once the season ends, I can be totally active again. Until then, though, I think that I will be a steal just to be signed.
So, if you're interested please get back to me. And, again, my thank you's and goodbye's go out to [name withheld] and all my friends in the AGW.
Thanks, [name withheld] Handler of Chocolate Thunder and his manager, Li'l Flava *Former World Champion in both the AGW and Jason Chandler's Insane Wrestling Federation.
