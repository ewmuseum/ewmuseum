MWWF Rules!

14 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Well allright here we go.... M-WWF sports a number of big name wrestlers, from Stone Cold to the Undertaker, to The Rock to DeGeneration X. When you join the M-WWF you start at the bottom of the ladder and you have to wrestle your way up to the top!
How to be succesful in the M-WWF? It starts with your application! Give us as much as possible information about your wrestler(s)!! You can have 2 wrestlers in the M-WWF or 1 Tag
Team!
Your information will be added to our M-WWF data-center, and you will find out yourself, why it's so important that you give us as much information as you can give!
You can join the M-WWF as an original wrestler (Rookie) or you can join as one of the well known Superstars of the WWF.
When you want to be an existing WWF Superstar, just email us and we let you know if the Superstar is available!
Career mode. You'll start your career in the M-WWF to make a name for yourself by fighting in House-shows
You'll only see the results! As you win matches, you'll move up the rankings toward the M-WWF Hardcore title. The more matches you win, the higher your ranking. Also, as you become more popular, you'll wrestle on TV shows such as Shotgun, Heat and RAW, and on various (B-ranked)
PPV's such as IYH. After winning the Hardcore belt, you'll be ranked #6 for the M-WWF
European belt, #13 for the M-WWF IC belt and #18 for the M-WWF World title and you will wrestle in all the PPV's! If you win the M-WWF Intercontinental belt, you'll be ranked #6 for the M-WWF World Heavyweight championship, and that's where the fun really begins! The current Champ will feel threatened by your high ranking! And sooner or later he has to face you! Don't worry, you'll get a shot at the gold eventually
..... IF YOU SMELL .............. Wrestlers: The M-WWF uses these WWF Wrestlers, when you see an email-adress behind the wrestler, it means that this wrestler is already taken!

When you choose to act in the Tag Team division, you must control both wrestlers, they also can wrestle in the single-division!
These are the Tag Teams: Jeff Jarrett/Owen Hart Big Bossman/Ken Shamrock The Headbangers New Age Outlaws The Acolytes Triple H & X-Pac Too Much ........... Only wrestling isn't enough! You have to do interviews, challenges, statements, etc. I think you already know how to do that! But remember if you not in the top
10 of the ranking
it's impossible to challenge the M-WWF Champ! So know your role.........
You have to send your roleplaying to us [detail withheld] and we will add to our roleplaying pages! Shows: Two man commentary each show, sometimes a guest commentator will join them! Each week we have two shows (Monday -> RAW, Friday -> Shotgun), each month a PPV! The results of these shows can be seen on our Results-pages! Every day there will be House-shows, these shows are only for the jobbers and low-ranked wrestlers, you only see the results of these matches! Title-matches only take place on RAW or in the PPV's. Shotgun will be for the mid-card wrestlers!
So, interested??? Email us with your wrestlers information and at least 20 moves and climb the ladder of succes in the M-WWF!!!
C.J.A. President of the M-WWF.
