Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The BackNet Wrestling Federation 2.0 opened its doors on 30 August 1997 billed, in full, as “A BackNet Interactive Production,” its own president telling the cameras that the parent company was giving the promotion “more money than Ted Turner has in his bank account.” A one-month hiatus that October, blamed on the president's own computer crashing, ended with a relaunch advertisement promising “at least 3 updates a day” under the line “BWF is not a game... it's an experience!”

Its weekly cards, Sunday Night Warfare and Thursday Thrashings, ran behind a three-man announce desk built to argue with itself: play- by-play man Andrew Ranks, a masked former champion named Brett Rose, and a self-declared voice of reason, Jim Burns, who spent broadcasts openly rooting against whoever the crowd was cheering. Its debut card opened on The GodFather, of the newly formed stable the MAFIA, dominating The Rusher before dropping the match to a single neckbreaker — “one mistake,” as Rose put it on air, “and it costs him dearly.”

Six weeks after that debut, on 15 November 1997 at the Evansville Coliseum, President Justin Williams relaunched the same roster, the same three-man desk, and the same building under a new name: the eXtreme Wrestling Alliance. “It's time for the stars of the past, present, and future to unite in one place,” the broadcast announced, carrying every character and angle across the switch without a break in continuity.

Under its new name the promotion opened a United States Championship tournament that December, shown starting but never shown resolving in its own surviving record, while The Crippler emerged leading a rival stable of his own, the DMW.

Its last surviving document, dated 3 January 1998, ends the promotion's record on two beats left hanging: Rictor no-shows a scheduled match and forfeits it to The Crippler outright, and moments later a man named on air as Johnny Smith runs in from the crowd and beats KASH unconscious with a lead pipe as the broadcast cuts to its credits. No further document survives.
