XWA Saturday Night Debut Card, 15 November 1997

15 November 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The camera's cut to the ring where a slender man stands. He is in a black tuxedo and carries a mic in hand and a smile on his face. Hundreds of screaming fans are all around in the Evansville Colliseum as the XWA begins a dynasty that shall continue for the years to come. The man lifts the microphone to his face.) Man: Ladies and Gentlemen. It's Time! (The fans erupt! Even though it is a small crowd for the first show, they sure know how to make the small arena sound like Madison Square Garden. The man waits for the cheering and screaming to calm down so he can continue.) Man: It's time for the newest spectacle in the world of professional wrestling to begin! It's time for the stars of the past, present, and future to unite in one place! It's time for the first ever card in the eXtreme Wrestling Alliance! (The arena is deafening. The fans are going wild jumping up and down, moshing in the crowd, and many other rambunxious things. One only wishes they were there live in person. The cameras go from screaming fans to an office high atop a building. Sitting at a desk is the CEO of the XWA, Justin Williams. He is wearing a very expensive brown suit with an XWA Tie. His hair is slicked back and in a pony tail as he knows he is now the one that will carry an entire federation to the top.) Williams: Hello XWA Fans! You are about to witness some of the hottest action in the world. You will be seeing the likes of AzteK, The GodFather, The Rusher, and more! I want to be the first to welcome you to become an XWA fan for the years to come. Now, without further ado, I would like to introduce to you the XWA Announcing Team. First, we spent a lot of money buying out his contract but hey, he's the best play-by-play man in the business. Here is the one and only, Andrew Ranks! (The fans erupt as Andrew makes his way out of the very cheesy curtain waving to the fans. He has a smile on his face and slaps hands with fans on the way to his seat. He stands in front of his chair and waits for his colleagues to join him.) Williams: Next up, we searched all around the world for the perfect wrestler to give us the inside on the sport and we found him. Here is one of the greatest wrestlers of all time, Brett Rose! (The fans erupt as their hero from the past and present makes his way out. Oddly enough, he is in a suit and tie and still dons the purple mask that has made him so famous. He raises both hands in the air requesting a response from the fans. He gets on heck of one too. He makes his way to his seat slapping hands. As he gets there, he shakes hands with Ranks and then stands in front of his respective seat.) Williams: Finally, the voice of reason so to speak. The man who has an opinion on everything and is just what you need for your high quality announcers booth. I give you the most hated man in announcing, Jim Burns! (The fans begin to boo tremendously as Burns makes his XWA debut as well. He comes out expecting the same intense welcome as Brett Rose recieved but realizes that he is receiving the opposite. Thus, he makes his way to his seat quietly. The Saturday Team is now complete and they all take their seats and put their headsets on.) Ranks: Hello everyone and welcome to the inagural event in the eXtreme Wrestling Alliance. I am Andrew Ranks alongside my tag team colleagues Brett Rose and Jim Burns. Gentlemen, we have one heck of a debut show tonight! Rose: Without a doubt three of the hottest matches ever will take place tonight. In the main event, the former Disco King of the wrestling world now turned crazed lunatic, Deranged Stu takes on Gene Hoffman of BWF Fame. Ranks: Hoffman has taken a bit of a turn since his manager and wife Amanda Wrede left him a few weeks back. He says he has seen the light. Rose: All he has seen is him being left in the dirt by his girl. No wrestler should let a woman get in the way of their career. It's going to be your downfall. Burns: All this talk of turns and women. Take my approach. Leave the women at home to cook your meals and...well. You know. Ranks: Please. This is a family show. Also on our card tonight, one half of the tag team Death Row, Death, takes on one of the biggest men in the sport today. The 7' 1 giant, AzteK. Rose: I have known AzteK for a while and I assure you that he is one of the most focused and toughest individuals to step into the ring. With the brute power he posesses, look for him to rise to the top and claim XWA gold. Burns: The only thing that AzteK is going to claim is a nice big fist right down his throat care of Death tonight! Besides, how could you forget to mention the match of the century. Viva la Familia, XWA. The GodFather has arrived and is taking on Death's tag partner, The Rusher! Ranks: We were just getting to that but it seems that you have beaten us to the job. Burns: As all great journalists do I might add! Rose: Oh God. Here comes one hell of a ride fans. The XWA is about to begin! The GodFather vs. The Rusher (Written by AzteK) (The Cameras return from the commercial break. The lights go off as you here twelve rings of a church bell . The arena starts to fill up with smoke like fog rolling in , in an old fish peer . The fresh smell of roses and cigar smoke fill the air . The lights slowly start to dim so you can only see the aile to the ring . Fire starts to flame up on the edges of the runway to the ring . Just then you see a a white and gold chariot come into the arena being pulled by four giant black horses . The Chariot is surrounded by the Godfathers own security called " the Men in Black " . Behind the Chariot are the rest of the Mafia . The Godfather is wearing his pin striped suit and a gangster style hat . He has a red rose pinned to his suit jacket and is grinning with a cigar in his mouth . The fans are chanting Viva La Familia and throwing roses at the Godfather.) Ranks: This should be a dandy. Two ring veterans in The GodFather and The Rusher to square off in the first official contest in the eXtreme Wrestling Alliance. Rose: Well, when you stick to great athletes like those two in the ring together, you can't go wrong. Look for a well fought match from both. Burns: I got one thing to say to both of you nimrods. Viva la Familia. (The Rusher makes his way out.) Burns: What a lame little entrance by that numskull. Rose: (Sarcastically) Not everyone is as creative as you, Jim. Burns: (An sneer comes across his face) Ranks: Nonetheless, this match is about to begin. Refereeing this matchup will be none other than James Styles. Styles checks both combatants for illegal objects and sounds for the bell. The GodFather immediately leaps for The Rusher. He knocks him to the ground and is pummelling him with punches to the face! The GodFather has come to fight! He is really laying those in! Rose: If GodFather keeps this up, this match may not be very long. Burns: That's the way the MAFIA works. Short and Sweet. Ranks: The GodFather picks up The Rusher and whips him into the ropes. CLOTHESLINE WITH AUTHORITY by GodFather! He leveled him! Now he executes the... Burns: MISSISSIPPI NUT CRUNCH!!!! Rose and Ranks: (Questionable) Mississippi Nut Crunch? Burns: Damn Straight! That's what you do to your wife and kids if they get out of line down south. Ranks: Thanks for that lesson on Southern Culture, Jim. But back to the task at hand. GodFather is still in control and is laying in some vicious slaps to the face on Rusher. He picks him up. I THINK HE IS GOING FOR THE HORSE'S HEAD!!!!!! Burns: YES!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Rose: No! Rusher just knocked him to the mat! He just got that energy out of nowhere! He is now dishing out the punishment to The GodFather! Ranks: Rusher whips him into the ropes. KNEE TO THE FACE!!! THRUST KICK!!!!!! Burns: No!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Ranks: NECKBREAKER!!!!! He goes for the cover! 1....2.....3!!!!!!!! The Rusher get's the win! Burns: How in the hell did he get that win??? That is terrible! What a fluke! Rose: Burns, that was no fluke. GodFather just made one mistake and it costs him dearly! Burns: What do you know! Rose: I am a wrestling champion and have been in the business for a while. That's what I know! Ranks: We need to take a commercial break! We will be right back! Deranged Stu vs.Gene Hoffman (Written by Deranged Stu) The Ring Announcer:"This contest is schedule for one fall!! First making his way to the ring weighing in at... (Ring Announcer gets interrupted by gene hoffmans music.... The ring announcer runs out of the ring... Then song 2 from blurr starts to roar over the speaker system Deranged Stu comes flying out right for Gene Hoffman.... Some fireworks go off but it doesn't look like Stu cares...They meat face to face in the ring...) Brett Rose: "Well are they gonna start or what?!?!?" Andrew Ranks: "Well I sure hope so." (They lock up... Neither are over powering because they are both about the same... Gene kicks Stu in the gut... Stu goes down.. Gene sends him into the ropes.. Belly to belly suplex!! Stu is down ... Gene is going up to the top and boom!! nails Stu with a flying elbow... The cover 1.......2....kick out!!) Jim Burns: "That was pathetic, come on ref learn to count!" (It looks like Gene is going for a suplex from the top... Gene ties nothing happens then stu pushed him down... Stu is still recovering and looks like he is going for a flying elbow... gene landed on his feet when he was pushed down and now he just pushed stu off the top!!! Stu landed on the ring post and his head is split wide open!!! Gene goes out and is taking the mats and moving them out of the way, there is just cement under them... Gene sets up for a power bomb he..... wait stu just threw him over his back!!! Stu is going insane!! Gene landed strait on the cement!! Stu is going to the top ropes!!) Andrew Ranks: "It looks like he is going for that insane flying head bunt!!" (Stu jumps off Gene moves!! Stu hits the fencing around the audience... Stu is on the ground while Gene is celebrating... What's this stu is standing up and he is going wild!!! Stu grabs gene and throws him into the steel stairs.. Gene blocked most of it but is still hurt.. Stu throws gene into the ring... He sends gene into the ropes gene executes a drop kick!! Gene gets up and starts celebrating again.. Stu is laying in the ring out cold... Gene picks stu up and body slams him.. he goes for the cover!! 1.......2.....kick out!!) Brett Rose: "That was a close one!" (Gene pulls stu up and is going for another body slam he can't get him up... Stu kicks Gene in the gut and executes the DISCO DDT!!!) Andrew Ranks: "Wow!! what an impact I didn't expect him to be doing that move anymore!!" Jim Burns: "Oh well there is no way he is gonna beat my good friend Gene!!" (As they were talking stu goes for the cover! 1.....2....3!!!!! Everyone's mouth is open besides Burn's mouth) Andrew Ranks: "Didn't you just say.." Jim Burns: "What??? Just like I said I knew Deranged Stu was going to win the hole time!" (Andrew and Brett both shake there head..) *Main Event* AzteK vs. Death (Written by AzteK) (The camera's return from commercial. They arrive at the announcers table where Brett Rose and Andrew Ranks smile at the camera and Jim Burns fiddles with a few papers nervously.) Ranks: It's now time for the main event of the evening pitting one of the all time greats against a relative newcomer to the wrestling world. Yes, I am talking about AzteK taking on Death! What about it Brett? Rose: Two of the biggest men in the wrestling world collide. What more could you want? Burns: Two big goofs beating the hell out of each other makes my night set as well. (Death makes his way to the ring) Burns: What quality entrances by these wrestlers!!!!!!!! Ranks: Aren't they though. I guess The President and Commsioner think the wrestlers need to prove themselves. (The Lights Go Out. Hey Man. Nice Shot by Filter begins to whale through the arena softly. The fans begin to stand and cheer as they know the greatest of all time is making his way to ringside! Smoke begins to come out of the entrance way and red and green lazers begin to appear all around the arena. The Big Man of the XWA walks out of the entrance way with the look of determination with a hint of Satan on his face. He stalks to the ring with his eyes on his opponent at all times. He walks up the steps and through the ropes. He stands in the center of the ring. He lifts his hands in the air signaling for the AzteK Bomb. Fireworks Explode as the arms reach full extension) Rose: Now there's an entrance! Burns: Yea. A bunch of loud music and sparklers. What entertainment! Ranks: Ok. But, both combatants are face to face in the ring. James Styles checks both wrestlers for foreign objects and calls for the bell. Both men are still deadlocked face to face talking trash to each other. Rose: Talk about intensity. This is sure to be nothing but the Battle of the Titans. Ranks: AZTEK Just spit in his Death's Face!!!!! He is taking him down with a vicious onslaught of punches to the face! Rose: Wait! Over who is that??? (The camera's cut to the crowd where a large man in a T-Shirt and Jeans jumps over the guardrail and heads for the ring.) Ranks: Who is that?? He is distracting the referee. He just threw AzteK something! (The camera's get close to a pair of brass knuckles that lay in the Center of the ring. Aztek picks them up and levels Death with them!) Ranks: He covers Death! One...Two...THree!!!!!! AzteK gets the cheap win! Burns: HAHAHAHA! I love it! Ranks: AzteK and this Huge man are making their way to the back to the fans approval!
