XWA Christmas Chaos '97, Hour 2, 25 December 1997

25 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
/\ =======/ \================================================================== /XWA \ XWA Christmas Chaos '98 / Xmas \ December 25, 1997 / Chaos \ *PitFighter Match* Gene Hoffman vs. Riot /____ ____\ Written by J. Williams =======|__|================================================================== (The camera's return from the commercial to the scenes of Rictor being carried out on a stretcher. His neck his held securely by the two blocks as the EMT's rush him towards the back. The fans stand in silent respect for a man that just risked his life to entertain them. The camera's then focus on Andrew Ranks and Rich Stevenson. Ranks has his eyes closed in silent prayer as Rich just stares at the scene. Ranks finally opens his eyes and Rich turns around as Rictor is finally out of the arena.) Ranks: Fans, what you just saw was one of the most dangerous, violent, and yes, eXtreme matches in the history of wrestling. Rictor may not be able to wrestle again. We will keep you updated throughout the week on the XWA website. Stevenson: Let's go up to the ring. (The camera's cut to the ring where a Stunned James Stewart stands after the actions of the last match.) Stewart: Ladies and Gentlemen, this next contest is a special PitFighter matchup. The rules are simple. There are none. The first one to be busted wide open is declared the loser. Anything is legal. (The distorted guitar of Ironman by Black Sabbath begins to whale throughout the XWA Coliseum. Out of the curtain walks Nicole. She receives an enormous eruption from the fans. She directs the attention of the stadium to the entrance portal where Gene Hoffman, the hardcore legend, makes his way out. Hoffman and Nicole make their way towards the ring arm in arm.) Stevenson: WHOO!!! Look at the body on that fox. Ranks: Rich, don't attract Hoffman over here. I don't want to be the next one escorted out on a stretcher. (As Hoffman is about to make his way up the ring steps, Riot makes his way out of the stands with a steel pipe in hand. He nails Hoffman over the head with it putting a large dent in it.) Ranks: Holy God! He just dented that pipe! Hoffman is down! Hoffman is down! Stevenson: Talk about your sneakiest bastard. I would have never thought of anything that creative! Way to Go, Riot! Ranks: Riot has the pipe again. He attempts to level Hoffman again but Nicole steps in the way! Stevenson: Get out of there Honey! Ranks: Oh My! Riot just leveled Nicole! She is on the ground and is not moving! Riot is insane! He just knocked out a lady with a pipe! Stevenson: LordZero! LordZero! (From out of the Portal comes LordZero walking calmly towards the chaos ringside. The fans are going mixed on their reaction.) Ranks: LordZero is making his first XWA Appearance and it is not the way I had hoped it would be! He punches Riot right in the face! Riot is down. Hoffman is stunned! He doesn't know what to think! Riot isn't moving! One! Two! Three! Four! Five! Six! Seven! Eight! Nine! He's moving! Stevenson: How in the hell! Ranks: Hoffman winds up with the pipe! Stevenson: Homerun Baby! Ranks: Neither are busted open yet and no one has been knocked out. This may live up to the Rictor/Simon matchup! Stevenson: No way in hell, Andy. LordZero and Hoffman are now going after Riot and beating the hell out of him. Hoffman has the steel steps above his head. Not anymore! Riot just got them right in the kisser. Ranks: Riot is favoring that nose. I think it may be broken! Hoffman isn't backing off though. He throws him into the ring. DDT! Stevenson: LordZero in now. DDT by Zero! Ranks: A fan just threw in his chair! Oh No! Oh No! Hoffman just beat Riot over the head with the chair! Stevenson: That's not all he is gonna do with that chair. He is jabbing it right into the knee of Riot! His sorry ass ain't gonna be walking after this match. Ranks: Indeed. LordZero with a snap suplex right on the chair. What in the hell! Stevenson: Hey! How do you get the...What in the hell?!?!?! Ranks: Hoffman just pulled out a razorblade! He just slashed Riot's face wide open! He's gushing blood! Hoffman Wins! Hoffman Wins! I don't believe it! Stevenson: Hoffman just bladed Riot to get the win. That sneaky little punk. I love it! Ranks: Fans we will be right back with the main event of the evening and a special message from President Williams. (The camera's cut to a movie trailer.) /\ =======/ \================================================================== /XWA \ XWA Christmas Chaos '98 / Xmas \ December 25, 1997 / Chaos \ *Christmas Cage* The GodFather vs. KASH /____ ____\ Written by J. Williams =======|__|================================================================== (The cameras return from the commercial break and focus on Rich Stevenson and Andrew Ranks sitting in their announcers seat. Stevenson still dons his festive Santa Clause Hat. Andrew smiles and begins to speak.) Ranks: The next matchup is the Christmas Cage Main Event. For those that do not know what the Christmas Cage is, President Williams has had a special cage made that has Christmas Wreaths wrapped in Barbed Wire, Christmas Lights, and tinsel wrapped around it. The Fear of Severe Cuts, Electricution, or God knows what else has to be on the mind of both KASH and The GodFather. (As Ranks continues to speak from High Atop the arena, the cage begins to come down over the ring. The lights on it are flickering on and off while some of the tinsel falls to the ground.) Stevenson: Oh is that a beauty or what! (The lights go off as you here twelve rings of a church bell . The arena starts to fill up with smoke like fog rolling in , in an old fish peer. The fresh smell of roses and cigar smoke fill the air . The lights slowly start to dim so you can only see the aile to the ring. Fire starts to flame up on the edges of the runway to the ring . Just then you see  a  a white and gold chariot come into the arena  being pulled by four giant black horses. The Chariot is surrounded by the Godfathers own security called "the Men in Black." Behind the Chariot are the rest of the Mafia. The Godfather is wearing his pin striped suit and a gangster style hat . He has a red rose pinned to his suit jacket and is grinning with a cigar in his mouth. The fans are chanting Viva La Familia and throwing roses at the Godfather.) Stewart: This is the Main Event of Christmas Chaos! It is a Christmas Cage matchup. In the ring at this time is the Leader of the MAFIA. The GodFather! (All lights go off and the crowd goes wild as a Green spot light shaped in a dollar sign is beamed at the center of the riing.  Mo money Mo problems starts to play as a spot light hits the entrance, KASH comes out and walks to the ring [The spot light follows him].  As he enters the ring the lights come back on and the crowd goes even crazier.) Stewart: Making his way to the ring at this time is DMW member KASH! Ranks: You can see the intensity on both mens faces. This match could end the careers of either men. Stevenson: So you know, the only way to win this match is by climbing out over the top of the cage. Anything is legal so if you want to strangle your opponent with the lights go ahead! Ranks: There is no referee in the ring but on the outside is James Stewart with a key in his hand to open the cage if it gets nasty. Stevenson: There is the bell and those two are going at each other like bulls! KASH knocks GodFather to the ground and is pummeling him with a series of blows right to the jaw. Ranks: This is the match that will decide it all. These two men have been going at it since the XWA opened. KASH rams GodFather into the turnbuckle. GodFather moves and KASH just went face first into the corner of the cage! His head just got gashed open by the barbedwire as well!!!! Stevenson: Nighty Night KASH! You just got busted open! HA HA HA HA! What was that about DMW 4 Life? MAFIA is GOD!! Ranks: The GodFather grabs a strand of the lights and is choking out KASH! This is brutal! GodFather releases the chokehold! He whips KASH into the cage! Oh My God! Stevenson: KASH just got his sorry ass electrocuted! Ranks: KASH could have had his heart stopped right there. GodFather back on the prowl. Cross Corner whip by GodFather reversed! Oh My! The Barbed wire in that corner just dug into The odFathers back! He just lose a good hunk of skin as well. Stevenson: This is the best Christmas Ever! Total Carnage rules! Ranks: KASH is back on the offense as he DDT's GodFather right down to the mat. KASH applies a Camel Clutch in the middle of the ring. Stevenson: What the hell. That dumbass seriously thinks he can win by submission! HA HA HA HA! Ranks: I think he is just trying to wear down the GodFather so he can have the opportunity to get over the cage without any trouble. Stevenson: Fat Chance of that KASH. Ranks: KASH releases the hold and is making his way up the Cage. He is halfway up but here comes the GodFather to bring him down. Stevenson: Did you see KASH on the way down?? He gashed his leg wide open on a biece of barbed wire! The ring mat aint white no more! Its got a lovely red tint to it now! Ranks: GodFather with a jawbreaker on KASH. He takes a wreath off of the cage and is beating KASH Down with it! His back is being laserated by the barbed wire strung around those wreaths! GodFather now has a strand of those Christmas Lights and is beating down KASH with them. Stevenson: He is beating him like the dog he is! I love this! Keep it going GodFather! GodFather is now ascending up the cage, but wait! He isn't going over! He is standing on top of the cage! Ranks: Oh he isn't! He isn't going to! Oh My! Stevenson: That kicked ass! GodFather with a big leg drop from the top of the cage! That had to be 30 feet in the air! KASH isn't moving, and GodFather has a big piece of barbed wire stuck up his ass! Ranks: Thank you for putting it so bluntly. Stevenson: (Smiles) Anytime, Andy. Ranks: Both men are down and not moving. Wait just a minute! (From high above the arena, Steve Lauria flys down from the rafters and into the ring. The fans are going wild.) Stevenson: That's Steve Lauria! What the hell is he doing here!?!?!? (Steve Lauria takes off the harnesses that hold him from the bungee cord.) Ranks: Lauria is beating down both The GodFather and KASH as they lay there! Oh my God! Stevenson: He has a big wad of the barbed wiree! He is going to hogtie them together! Ranks: Steve Lauria has just tied the ankles of both The GodFather and KASH together with a piece of barbed wire! Now he's leaving the cage! Stevenson: This is insanity! (Lauria makes his way over the cage and out heading towards the back at a rapid pace as the fans cheer him on. The camera's begin to fade to black with a final image of both The GodFather and KASH laying motionless in the center of the ring secured together by a piece of barbed wire.)
