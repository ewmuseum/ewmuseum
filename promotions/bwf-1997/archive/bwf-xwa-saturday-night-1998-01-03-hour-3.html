XWA Saturday Night, 3 January 1998, Hour Three

January 3, 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
__ ____ ____ \ \ / /\ \ __ / / \ \ \/ / \ \ / \ / / /\ \================================================== / /\ \ \ v v / __ \ January 3, 1997 /_/ \_\ \__/\__/__/ \__\ Steve Lauria vs. Gene Hoffman Saturday Night Written by J. Williams ============================================================================ (The Camera's return from the commercial break focusing on the XWA Entrance
Portal. The Hardcore sounds of Ironman by Black Sabbath whale throughout the arena as the fans in the sold out Fleet Center let off their unusual
Pro-Hoffman response. What has possessed the fans of the XWA to have such a sudden change of heart? Is it the fact that he is facing the man that has just announced his attempted takeover of the eXtreme Wrestling Alliance? Or is it just the fact Hoffman has gotten majorly over with the fans of the XWA with his new "Go <beep> yourself" attitude? Nonetheless, Gene Hoffman makes his way out towards ringside, led down by Nicole. Hoffman's initial response to the fans is a nice birdie to everyone in attendence. Abnormaly, this triggers an even louder uproar from the fans.)
Stevenson: Um. Isn't is usually the norm to take offense to someone flipping the bird to you?
Ranks: Last time I checked it was. Stevenson: Damn Boston-ites. Need to get their priorities straight. (As Rich continues to babble on with his useless talk of the middle finger,
Steve Lauria's theme begins to annoy the hell out of everyone in the arena.
From out of the entrance portal, Lauria hightails it out to a very angry response from the fans. They begin to shower him with soda. He is wearing a pair of silver "Ray-Bans" and a black leather jacket. You could compare him to a 90's version of James Dean as he makes it to the ring. He avoids the fans who attempt to touch him because he is just "too cool.")
Ranks: Oh this guy is sick. I don't know how anyone could stand him. And the sad thing of it is that he was supposed to be the one to carry the XWA into the next millenium. Now he wants to take us over.
Stevenson: If you ask me, I think he needs to get a serious ass beating from a few certain wrestlers and this announcer.
Ranks: Add me in on that list too, Rich. (Lauria continues to cockily walk up the stairs of the ring and finally into the ropes. He walks over to Hoffman and taunts him. This antagonizes him and sends him after Lauria.)
Stevenson: Hoffman just leveled Lauria! Sound the bell! Sound the bell! Ranks: Gene Hoffman just knocked Steve Lauria to the ground and is pummelling him with a large arsenal of punches! Listen to this place! They are going crazy! Hoffman with a snap suplex.
Stevenson: At this rate, Steve Lauria's stay in the XWA will be a short one. Boy would I love that too! Ranks: Hoffman to the top! Elbow Smash! He's going back up. He's winks at
Nicole. Lauria got up! He wasted too much time! Hoffman is down and is not moving.
Stevenson: Dammit! Leave the women at home to do the cooking. They are just a distraction.
Ranks: Lauria in control now. He has a headlock locked on pretty accurately. He has it sinked in on Hoffman. The air is just being squeezed out of him. Stevenson: If you ask me, I think that Steve Lauria is BS'ing all of us with his takeover mumbo jumbo. I want to know what army he has behind him because so far all I have seen is him.
Ranks: I don't know who he has, Rich, but I am sure interested in seeing who it is. (Attempting to change the subject) Back in the ring though, Lauria is still dominating over Hoffman in a major way. Hoffman into the ropes.
Clothesline with authority. Steve Lauria is just laying it in on Gene Hoffman. At this rate, I am not so sure that Hoffman will be able to stay in this match much longer.
Stevenson: Right you are, Andy. Hoffman is getting the tar kicked out of him like a red headed step child.
Ranks: (Whispers) That's not very P.C. Rich. We don't need a lawsuit. Stevenson: Oh. Oops. Ranks: (Back in his normal tone acting as if nothing has even happened.) Lauria with a sleeper on Hoffman. He seems to be going out. One. Two. What in the? Steve Lauria just released the hold. Oh my! DDT! That's it! Jameson is down. One. Two. Three! Steve Lauria just got the win over the seasoned veteran Gene Hoffman.
Stevenson: Looks like Lauria is going to be here for a while unfortunately. I just hope there is someone who is going to be able to take him out. Anybody that could do it, I would kiss them right on the top of their head. And if they can't, I will let them kiss my...
Ranks:(Interupting to cease the unnecessary language) Well, folks we need to take a commercial break.
Stevenson: (Pondering a reason why he was interupted.) Is it just me or did you interupt me on purpose?
(The camera's cut to the commercial break as Rich and Andrew get in a large verbal argument. The final scene of Steve Lauria walking back cocky as ever is show. Is there anyone in the world that can stand him?)
__ ____ ____ \ \ / /\ \ __ / / \ \ \/ / \ \ / \ / / /\ \================================================== / /\ \ \ v v / __ \ January 3, 1997 /_/ \_\ \__/\__/__/ \__\ Rictor vs. The Crippler Saturday Night Written by A. Middleton ============================================================================ [The lights go out, Voodoo Chile by Jimi Hendrix comes on. The fans go wild. The lights flicker on and off while The Crippler makes his way to the ring. Crippler is spelled in the middle of the ring with lasers.] James Stewart: Ladies and gentlemen.. this is a match scheduled for one fall. on his way to the ring.. the leader of the DMW... THE CRIPPLER!! [A very loud pop for the Crippler as he makes his way to the ring... he is clearly the favorite in this match as Hell's Bell's by AC/DC starts to play over the arena PA system]
Stewart: And his opponent.. RICTOR!! [As Rictor is called out, the fans begin a thunderous chorus of Boo's. A green light slowly begins to appear over the Entrance Portal and smoke engulfs it.. the crowd continues to boo.. but no Rictor.. after three or four minutes of waiting.. the lights go fully back on and a confused ref and
Crippler are looking around in the ring. The ref goes to talk to James
Stewart]
Ranks: Where on earth is Rictor? Stevenson: Probably trying to phone this one in.. the Crippler is nothing to beat... you think Rictor would waste his time on him?
Ranks: Well only if he want's the winners share of the fight purse.. fans we have been waiting here a little while.. and no Rictor!
Stevenson: You know, now that I think about it.. I don't think I have seen
Rictor all day..
Ranks: I think the referee is about to take action here.. lets go to the ring.. Stewart: Ladies and gentleman.. Rictor has failed to arrive for his contest. The referee has decided to award this match to.. THE CRIPPLER!! [Cheers are heard as the Crippler raises his hands in victory and walks back to the dressing room.. the camera cuts to Ranks and Stevenson
Ranks: Well it is a win.. but where is Rictor? Why didn't he show up? Stevenson: Well it was a CHEAP win.. I can't believe that the stupid ref gave the match to Crippler.. I would have dq'ed Crippler!
Ranks: HUH? Why? Stevenson: It's simple.. DQ Crippler for being stupid enough for showing up against the wrecking machine! Rictor did him a favor
Ranks: [rolls his eyes] oh get real.. lets go to our next match. __ ____ ____ \ \ / /\ \ __ / / \ \ \/ / \ \ / \ / / /\ \================================================== / /\ \ \ v v / __ \ January 3, 1997 /_/ \_\ \__/\__/__/ \__\ Art Middleton vs. KASH Saturday Night Written by J. Williams ============================================================================ (The camera returns from the commercial break as KASH makes his way towards the ringside area. From out towards the stands is running a man. He attacks
KASH.)
Ranks: Oh My God! Johnny Smith just attacked KASH with a lead pipe! He is out cold! Stevenson: HA HA HA! KNOCK OUT THAT DMW TRASH! WHOO!!!!!!!!!! Ranks: Fans, KASH isn't moving. (THe camera's cut to the credits of the show. KASH is laid on a stretcher after the senseless beating he received.)
