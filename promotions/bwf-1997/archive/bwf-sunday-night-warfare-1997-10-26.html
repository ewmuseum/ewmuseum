BWF Sunday Night Warfare, 26 October 1997

26 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The Camera's fade in from a commercial for Bob's Cleaners. A narration is done by an unknown voice. "Tonight is the night that the BackNet Wrestling Federation returns to television. Tonight is the night that the world finds out if we still have what it takes to be the best. Tonight, stars will be made because this is the BWF!") (The BWF Theme begins to play as a a chant of BWF begins over your Television. Suddenly, a large Flaming BWF Logo appears on your screen but it instantly destroyed to make way for the Sunday Night Warfare Logo.) (The Cameras cut to the BWF Arena were fans are going wild as their favorite fed finally returns to action! The Camera Crew cuts to a few Signs in the crowd that read "BWF Kicks Ass" and "Viva La Familia." A voice soon comes over the PA system. The Fans begin to go wild!) Voice: Ladies and Gentleman. Welcome to Sunday Night Warfare! May I introduce to you the BWF Announcing Team! (The BWF Theme Begins to play as fans go wild as the new announcers will soon be making there way to the ring.) Voice: First, the BWF's play-by-play man returns to the Federation with a new love for the sport. Here is Andrew Ranks!!!! (Andrew Ranks walks out of the entrance way. He slaps hands with screaming fans as he makes his way towards the announcers booth.) Voice: Next up, we have the former Iceman of the BWF turned commentator. Here is Jerry "ICEMAN" Richards! (The Fans go wild as Jerry makes his way towards the announcers table slapping hands with fans and having an all out good time.) Voice: Finally, the BWF would like to introduce you to the man who took John Johnson's job. Here is Lord Williams III!!!!! (Fans begin to boo massively as his lordship makes his way out of the entrance way with a snobbish look on his face. He raises his hand in the air ordering the fans to acknowledge him. A few do by throwing soda at him. He begins to yell obsenities at them as he makes his way towards his seat.) Ranks: Welcome everyone to Sunday Night Warfare! The BWF is back and better than ever and we have one hell of a card for you! Iceman: That's right. In our main event, SuperStar will face the Disco King himself, Disco Stu. Lordship: Disco? Ha. Why doesn't that nipple enjoy the finer things in life. Iceman: Because he isn't a pompus jerk like you! He is just a jerk! Ranks: Gentleman, please. Also tonight, Al Marvelous will be taking on The GodFather. Lordship: I will expect to see The GodFather pull off a stunning victory over that waste of words, Al Marvelous. Iceman: But, who can forget the debut of Steve Lauria as he takes on MAFIA member, Frank Rizzo. Ranks: Can't wait to see that one. The MAFIA looks to be pretty dominating in its past experiences. Lordship: Perhaps the MAFIA is looking for a Lord. Iceman: (Just stares at his lordship) Ranks: With that, let's get down to the ring! *************************Steve Lauria vs. Frank Rizzo************************* UM???? (The GodFather theme begins to play over the PA system. The Fans in the BWF Arena rise to their feet and begin to boo.) Ranks: What is this? (The GodFather and Frank Rizzo make their way out of the entrance way and begin to look around the building. They seem to like the place and make their ways to the ring. A few fans ringside have signs the read "Viva La Familia." Frank Rizzo grabs one and holds it in the air for all to see. They make their way to the ring where Michael Richards is hurrying to.) M. Richards: Ladies and Gentlemen, The Mafia! (Fans begin to boo even louder. Frank Rizzo is telling the building to shut up. The GodFather is just standing and smiling) M. Richards: GodFather, what brings you out here tonight? GodFather: Well, Michael. I have brought a portion of the New York MAFIA with me to the BWF. I have been looking to add people to it as well. The BWF is full of talent that has MAFIA written all over it and I have found two who especially have caught my eye. M. Richards: Well, who are they? GodFather: First, may I introduce to you, DeathHead Distruct! (The GodFather theme begins to play as Distruct makes his way towards the ring with a large grin on his face. The fans are booing him and throwing soda and ice cubes. Distruct finally makes his way to the ring where he shakes hands with The GodFather and Rizzo.) M. Richards: Distruct, what has possessed you to join the MAFIA. Distruct: Money. GodFather: He knows where he can get the money as the next new member did as well. Ladies and Gentlemen. The Marvelous One. Al Marvelous. (The GodFather theme begins again as fans start a chant of Al Sucks. Why would the Babyface of the BWF turn on the fans? Al makes his way out of the entrance way to a sea of booing. He has a large smile on his face and a wad of hundred dollar bills. He makes his way into the ring and hugs GodFather.) M. Richards: I thought you said that Marvelous wasn't MAFIA! Marvelous: You thought wrong. GodFather: Now Gentlemen. Let's make this official. Here are two contracts that will guarantee you a very nice sum of money. Just sign here. (Al and Distruct take out two pens and sign the contracts. The GodFather and Frank Rizzo take the contracts and turn to show them to the fans. Marvelous taps GodFather on the shoulder. GodFather turns around and Marvelous HITS HIM WITH A MASSIVE DDT! Distruct and Rizzo are not aware of anything. They turn around and see GodFather on the ground and Al Marvelous running towards the back. The Crowd is going nuts!) Ranks: Oh My! He just turned on the MAFIA! (Marvelous grabs the contracts and tears them in half to the fans delight. The Camera's cut to a commercial.) *************************Steve Lauria vs. Frank Rizzo************************* (The Camera's come back from a commercial. The Fans are going crazy as the cameras cut to different people in the crowd. War begins to play over the PA System as Steve Lauria makes his way ringside ignoring everyone.) Ranks: Our first match tonight is GodFather member Frank Rizzo taking on the man making his way to the ring, Steve Lauria. Iceman: Well, after what Al Marvelous and Distruct did to Rizzo and The GodFather I don't know how Rizzo can concentrate on this matchup. Lordship: Those two stupid Nipples signed their deathwish turning on The MAFIA. I shall laugh at both of them because The MAFIA always gets their men. (Frank walks down the ring all cocky yet still has a certain Mafia look to him, which make him the most feared wrestler in the whole world. He makes his way up the to the ring.) Ranks: Doing the Referee job tonight is Lance Haysmen. Lance checks both combants for illegal objects. The Bell sounds and we are underway. Both men lock up. Frank Rizzo applies an armbar on Steve Lauria who is quick to reverse it. Frank Rizzo flibs over and sends Lauria with him. Frank is back up and whips Lauria into the ropes. VICIOUS CLOTHESLINE by Rizzo. Iceman: Frank Rizzo is taking his aggression out on Lauria. Lordship: Perhaps if we were lucky, he would beat the bloody hell out of the two of you. Ranks: Commentary from his Lordship, Williams III. Frank Rizzo is really taking it to Steve Lauria. He is nailing some vicious kicks to the midsection. He picks up Lauria. Snap Suplex. Steve Lauria is down and he is hurt. Iceman: Frank Rizzo is now attacking the knee of Lauria. Smart move. I always went for my opponents knee when I stepped into the squared circle. Lordship: When you were in the ring, the crowd was either asleep or at the concession stands. By the way, what was your record? Iceman: (Begins to stare at his Lordship) Ranks: Lauria is still down. Rizzo is heading for the top rope. Fans are booing heavily. Rizzo misses a legdrop from the top as Lauria sits up. Rizzo is down in pain. Lauria goes on the attack. He picks him up and executes a neckbreaker! Rizzo is out cold. Iceman: I sense a Figure-Four! Ranks: Lauria grabs Rizzo's legs. He has it on!! Rizzo tapped out. Your winner is Steve Lauria! Lordship: That worthless nipple Steve Lauria had to play dead. If he were a real man he would have fought him toe to toe. Iceman: You know, we have only been working for a little over half an hour and you are really beginning to irritate me. Lordship: Well, little boy. What are you gonna do about it? Put me in a wrasslin' move? Ranks: Gentlemen Please. We will be right back after this commercial break! (The cameras cut to commercial as Richards and his Lordship are locked in a staring war.) *************************Al Marvelous vs. The GodFather************************* (The Cameras comes back from the commercial break. It seems that Jerry Richards and his Lordship have calmed themselves down. The Cameras are focused on the announcers booth) Ranks: This next matchup is indeed a main event caliber one. After what transpired earlier with Marvelous and DeathHead Distruct turning on the MAFIA, this should be an all out war. (Lights always off . The GodFather theme begins to play as fans begin booing and throwing things towards the aisleway. The GodFather walks out with his own Security . Sucurity wear all black with black sunglasses. He ignores the fans as he makes his way to the ring.) Iceman: What an entrance by The GodFather. Very Impressive. (Fireworks go off (Red a Blue) and a white light circles the now dark arena. Then several revolving "Simply Marvelous" lights circle the arena. Finally, the appearance of `The Marvelous One' Al Marvelous. Marvelous will take his time getting to the ring, as he will shake hands with everyone at ringside.) Ranks: Al Marvelous seems pretty nonchalant about this match with that entrance. GodFather on the other hand has the look of Satan in his eyes. What a war this is going to be. Our referee for this match is Bill Freeman. Freeman checks both wrestlers for illegal objects and this match is underway! Both men lock up. GodFather runs Marvelous back into the corner and is doing a number on him! Bill Freeman breaks up both men. Al gets himself back together. Lordship: Hahaha. GodFather really took it to that Nipple, Al Marvelous. Iceman: Is that all you can call anyone? A nipple? Lordship: If you had one speck sign of intelligence in that feeble minded brain of yours, I would be in shock. Ranks: Gentlemen! There is a match going on. GodFather is in control back in the ring. He whips Marvelous into the ropes and THROWS HIM OVER THE TOP! Marvelous is down on the ground and is not moving. The GodFather makes his way to the outside. He is pummeling Marvelous with some vicious kicks. He picks him up and throws him into the steel steps headfirst! Iceman: I don't think Marvelous can recover from that shot! Ranks: GodFather throws Marvelous back in to avoid the countout. He whips Marvelous into the turn buckle with authority. Iceman: The Ring Moved! Lordship: You stupid nipple. The ring cannot move. Its only your dazed and confused mind that made it appear that way. Iceman: Shut the hell up! Ranks: Guys, you can argue all you want but GodFather is going for the pin. ONE, TWO, THR..So Close. GodFather is up arguing about a slow count. Him and Bill Freeman are eye to eye. LOOK! Al Marvelous is up. He has GodFather in a small package! ONE, TWO, THREE!!!!!!! Al Marvelous wins! Lordship: No! The MAFIA loses again! Iceman: Knowing that it makes you upset, your Lordship, it makes me even happier. Lordship: Why I.. (Just then President Williams walks out towards ringside. He grabs a microphone and begins to speak) Williams: You know. You two have been over there all day bickering about nothing. Since it is apparent you both have something you need to settle, you can settle it at Santa Smash!!!! (The Fans go wild at this announcement.) Williams: That's right. Announcers match. Ranks: HAHAHAHA! You both got what you want Williams: Ranks, shut up before I stick you in the ring against myself. Ranks: We have to take a commercial break. We will be right back with our main event. (The Cameras cut to a commercial as President Williams walks away.) *************************Disco Stu vs. SuperStar************************* (The Cameras cut back from the commercials as Chick Magnet by MxPx begins to play. Tiny yellow stars begin to go down the entrance way as SuperStar makes his way out of the Entrance way. He raises his fist in the air to a thunderous boom of firecrackers. SuperStar makes his way to the ring slapping hands with many fans and kissing a few lovely ladies on the way as well.) Ranks: Here comes SuperStar, the BWF's lone cruiserweight. Iceman: This should be one heck of a fight. I am anxious to see if the Disco King can actually stand toe to toe with SuperStar. (Stayin' Alive by the BeeGee's blares through the PA system. Many fans begin to stand and boo for the disco freak while others are disco dacing! Fireworks explode as Disco Stu's logo flashes on the Big Screen. No one comes out of the entrance way.) Iceman: Where is Disco? Ranks: I am not sure. I thought I saw him earlier today in the arena. (The Cameras cut to the ring where SuperStar has just picked up a microphone.) SuperStar: Now, Ladies and Gentleman, do you not see how the Disco King runs from me? (Fans begin to go wild!) SuperStar: Now, I know you all want to see a good match tonight and I want to give it to you so, someone in the back get out here so we can give the fans what they deserve! (The Fans go wild and turn their attentions to the entrance way as For Whom the Bell Tolls by Metallica begins to play. Tommy Starr makes his way out of the entrance way and instantly begins badgering fans and talking trash to SuperStar.) Ranks: Here comes the veteran of the BWF. Tommy Starr is the only one in the BWF that was present in the old one and he is still kicking some major tail! Lordship: This nipple needs to go back to the nursing home. We do not need him having a heart attack in the ring. Iceman: Santa Smash is so far away. Perhaps I should just take you out right now. Lordship: Hardly. Ranks: Nonetheless, both men are in the ring. Our referee for this bout is former BWF Announcer, James Styles. Iceman: Yea. Ranks over here took his job. Ranks: Indeed. Styles checks for illegal objects. The bell sounds and we are underway. SuperStar extends his hand for a handshake. Iceman: Not to smart. Tommy Starr will eat him alive for this. Ranks: Not quite. Starr grabs SuperStar's hand and gives it a-- Iceman: Vicious Clothesline. He suckered the rookie. Ranks: Starr begins to kick SuperStar in the midsection. He picks him up by the hair and executes a neckbreaker with authority. Lordship: This Tommy Starr guy is not as bad as I thought. Perhaps he could be a prospect for his Lordship's alliance. Iceman: If Tommy Starr ever became associated to you, I would quit my announcing job and sell insurance door to door. Lordship: That would be reason enough. Ranks: Gentlemen, you will both have your opportunity to settle it in the ring. But, right now Tommy Starr is taking it to SuperStar. He has him in a very good armbar submission. He is really pulling back on that arm. He finally releases the hold and picks up SuperStar and WHIPS HIM INTO THE TURNBUCKLE! Starr with a splash into the corner. SuperStar is down hard. Iceman: I smell a Starr Jammer! Ranks: Starr Jammer from the TOP!!!!!!! Styles Counts. One, Two, THREE!!! Tommy Starr is victorious. (Just as Tommy Starr's theme begins to play, President Williams makes his way towards the announcers table. He picks up a microphone) Wiliams: Disco Stu, I don't know where in the hell you are but, if you think you can just not show up for a match, you got something coming to you. So, you can have SuperStar in the ring at Extreme Measures in a Steel Cage. Security will be around you at all times to make sure you show up as well. Now, get those cameras turned off before we have to pay for overtime! (The Cameras cut out and goto La Femme Nikita.) Tell us what you think about this card.
