XWA Saturday Night, 6 December 1997

6 December 1997

Williams /_/ \_\ \__/\__/__/ \__\ December 6, 1997 Saturday Night Introduction ============================================================================ (Open to a large luxurious arena in downtown Evansville. A semi-full parking lot is full of scurrying people trying to get the last seats for the Debut of what is to be one of the most entertaining federations of all time. The cars in the lot range from a beat up '77 Buick to a '98 Porsche 911. Indeed, it has to be President Williams's. As the camera's make their way through the sea of fans in the lot on it's way towards the entrance, you see many screaming twelve year olds yelping the popular "Hi Mom!" to the camera.) (The camera's are at the ticket booth as Hey Man. Nice Shot, the XWA Theme begins to silently hum through your television's speakers. The arena doors open and we enter the new XWA Coliseum! The brainchild of Justin Williams has been created:the state of the art wrestling facility. Fans are walking with their popcorn and soda in hand. A few are at the merchandise booth buying memorbilia of their favorite XWA superstar. Randomly scattered are little groups of friends chatting away awaiting the opening bell. The camera finally makes it's way to an entrance way into the coliseum's seating area. A beautiful array of scenery is on your screen now. The seats are beginning to fill up. Mat Technicians are doing a final check for safety. The pyro guys are doing their final check on the fireworks and lazers that are now a tradition in the wrestling world. Suddenly the camera begins to fade out. As the last faint bit of light is seen, a voice speaks the word "Begin.") (Machinhead by Bush begins to blare through your Television as the Saturday Night opening starts. Random action shots of AzteK, Deranged Stu, and others are shown quickly. Their finishing movers are applied to helpless victims. Their brute force shown to the world for the first time. The XWA Logo then appears on the screen and the music begins to die away and the fans screaming is now heard. The logo suddenly blows up as the camera's return to a now darkened arena. It is now lit up by the loud, thunderous booms and lights of the state of the art pyrotechnics brought in by President Williams. The pyro finally begins to die off as the camera's cut from the fans to the XWA Announcer's Center where Andrew Ranks and Rich Stevenson sit calmly while the ruckus of the pyro was just behind them. Rich and Andrew are both wearing their respective grey suits with red ties. Rich has his wet hair tied back in a pony tail while Andrew's business man hair is perfectly parted to the side and gelled back. Andrew shuffles his papers a final time and begins to speak.) Ranks: Welcome to the inagural edition of what is soon to be the hottest program in wrestling history! Welcome to XWA Saturday Night! Hello everyone. I am Andrew Ranks alongside me is Rich Stevenson and we are live from the new XWA Coliseum! Stevenson: Williams sure spared no expense on this money hole! Look at this! I haven't seen a chair without gum under it since the dark ages! This is amazing! Ranks: Indeed it is. Fans, we have one hour of exciting action for you as tonight in our main event, Stable Leaders collide as MAFIA Leader, The GodFather, takes on DMW Leader, The Crippler. Stevenson: The highlight of my night will be seeing that overrated flunky Crippler getting a free nose job care of The GodFather. Ranks: Also tonight DMW Member AJ Simon takes on the 7' 425 lb. behemoth, AzteK. Stevenson: He's got the braun but where are the brains??? AzteK says he is retiring after next Saturday Night when he and the GodFather take on The Killer's, who for you nitwits that don't pay attention is The Crippler and AJ Simon. Ranks: (Staring) Thank you for that information, Rich. Another match scheduled for tonight is not going to take place as KASH was scheduled to face The Rusher but just yesterday, The Rusher, Death, and Steve Lauria were released from their contracts. Stevenson: What? Did they beat up their coach too??? (chuckles) Ranks: Let's head down to the ring and to Ring Announcer, James Stewart! __ ____ ____ \ \ / /\ \ __ / / \ \ \/ / \ \ / \ / / /\ \================================================== / /\ \ \ v v / __ \ Standard 1 Fall Matchup /_/ \_\ \__/\__/__/ \__\ Written by J. Williams Saturday Night AJ Simon vs. AzteK ============================================================================ Stewart: The following contest is scheduled for one fall with a 20 minute time limit! Making his way to the ring from Kitchener, Ontario, Canada, standing 6'8" and weiging 400 lbs. One half of The Killer's. Here is AJ Simon! (AJ Simon makes his way out flashing his popular "Suck-It" sign to the now disgruntled XWA Fans. It is obvious that the XWA, and quite possibly the entire world, hates AJ Simon and DMW. AJ finally makes his way into the ring still flashing the obscene gesture. He makes his way over to a ring corner and takes a seat on the top rope to wait for his opponent.) Stewart: And his opponent! (The Lights Go Out. Hey Man. Nice Shot by Filter begins to whale through the arena softly. The fans begin to stand and cheer as they know the greatest of all time is making his way to ringside! Smoke begins to come out of the entrance way and red and green lazers begin to appear all around the arena. The Big Man of the XWA walks out of the entrance way with the look of determination with a hint of Satan on his face. He stalks to the ring with his eyes on his opponent at all times. He walks up the steps and through the ropes. He stands in the center of the ring, lifts his hands in the air signaling for the AzteK Drop. Fireworks Explode as the arms reach full extension. The fans stand and begin to cheer and hold up their AzteK signs. They know Only two opportunities to see him before he makes his way out of the XWA and these fans are not going to be sold short.) Stewart: Coming Down the aisle from Las Vegas, NV, standing at 7' even and weighing 425 lb. Here is the "Big Man" of wrestling, AzteK! Ranks: What an ovation for AzteK! This sure seems to be a partial crowd tonight, Rich. Stevenson: I have never heard such an eruption for a walking steriod. These fans are insane! Ranks: The referee for tonights matchup is ring veteran Jimmy Jameson. He check AzteK and Simon for foreign objects. They seem clear. The bell has sounded and the XWA is underway! AzteK and Simon collide like two bulls. AzteK gets the advantage and whips Simon into the rope! What brute force by AzteK! Stevenson: Hey. If you were a test tube baby, you could whip people in the ropes like that too. The Walking Steriod is still in control though. He is throwing AJ Simon around like a two year old with a baby doll. Ranks: (Staring at Rich) Stevenson: What?!?!? Ranks: AzteK has AJ Simon in a well applied Full-Nelson but Simon is not going to give in just yet. AzteK finally gives up on the hold and throws Simon all the way across the ring. Stevenson: Smart move by the Steroid. Try to wear him out and set him up for his new and improved AzteK Drop. For those nitwits that don't know about the new AzteK Drop. AzteK now sets you up for a suplex but just as he is about to drop you down, he turns it into a vicious bodyslam. It sure beats the hell out of that little chokeslam of the past. Ranks: You seem to have an opinion on everything, Mr. Stevenson. Stevenson: Indeed I do. Ranks: We'll be right back after this commercial break! (The Camera's cut to a commercial break as Ranks and Stevenson take a five minute break.) (The camera's return from the commercial break. AzteK is still dominating the matchup with AJ Simon out cold in the center of the ring.) Ranks: We're back fans and AzteK has just been giving Simon the beating of his life. Stevenson: At this rate, our friend Mr. Simon isn't going to have much of a career here in the XWA. (As they are continuing to discuss Simon's beating, The GodFather makes his way out of the portal and towards the ring. He is dressed in the typical tuxedo that he has donned for so long. He walks by the fans blowing smoke from his lit cigar in their faces.) Ranks: Oh my God. Here comes The GodFather, AzteK's tag team partner next week on this very show. Stevenson: I don't think he is here just to view some great action. Ranks: GodFather takes a seat next to James Stewart while in the ring, AJ Simon is down to about 0% of energy. AzteK goes for the cover. One. Two. He kicked out! Stevenson: Well slap me on the... Ranks: Rich! This is a family show! AJ Simon with a great deal of courage kicking out of that one. AzteK seems a bit upset about it too. Stevenson: Steroid is going to the top rope?!?!?!?!? Ranks: This is insane! AzteK is going to do a suicide headbutt from the top rope! He missed! AJ Simon somehow pulled up and dodged what was sure to be the move to end the match. Stevenson: AzteK is out like a drunk on New Years Eve. Simon is starting to get up. (The camera's cut to the GodFather's seat. He has a calm look on his face as he still smokes the remains of his cigar. He finally decides it is done and throws it to the ground and smashes it with his feet.) Ranks: GodFather does not seem to be too worried about this one. Simon is in total control right now. I think he is setting up AzteK for The Simon! Stevenson: If he can get his arms around Steroid's neck, I will be amazed. Ranks: BY GOD HE DID IT! The Simon has been applied to AzteK. Simon goes for the... Stevenson: Here comes the GodFather! (The GodFather rushes from his seat as Jameson goes down for the count. He comes through the ropes and jumps on Simon pummelling him with vicious blows to the face.) Ranks: The GodFather has just gotten AzteK disqualified but they don't care. They are beating down AJ Simon. Where is the Crippler? Stevenson: He is smart. He is probably already on the plane with a bottle of whiskey laughing at how feeble minded AJ Simon is for falling into the trap of AzteK and The GodFather! Ranks: Fans, we will be right back. (The camera's go to commercial. AzteK and The GodFather relentlessly beat Simon throughout the commercial and finally back off as Simon lays in the center of the ring motionless.) __ ____ ____ \ \ / /\ \ __ / / \ \ \/ / \ \ / \ / / /\ \================================================== / /\ \ \ v v / __ \ Standard 1 Fall Matchup /_/ \_\ \__/\__/__/ \__\ Written by J. Williams Saturday Night An Interview with AzteK ============================================================================ (The camera's return from the commercial break and focus into the center of the XWA Ring. It is without a doubt one of the nicest looking ones in any federation. The ropes are blue and yellow and the drapes on the bottom are black with blue and yellow letters claiming "XWA." The XWA Interview Man, only known as The Informer stands in the center of the ring with a microphone in hand. He is wearing a black suit and a black XWA Tie with Purple and Yellow lettering saying "XWA.") Informer: Ladies and Gentleman, it is my pleasure to introduce to you the man that next week will team up with The GodFather next week on this very program to face The Killer's. Here is the "Big Man" of the XWA, AzteK!!! (The arena erupts as AzteK's name is announced. No music is playing or special effects. AzteK walks out to a silent arena other than the screaming fans. He has on his street clothes now:a pair of blue jeans and a black T-Shirt with "AzteK" in red lettering. He has a steel chair in his hand and a big grin on his face. He walks to the ring at a very slow pace. He takes time to acknowledge each AzteK sign in the audience.) Ranks: This is unusual. AzteK has always had one of the most exciting entrances in wrestling and now there is nothing. Stevenson: Looks like Williams is acting like a certain promoter up north in Connecticut. Next we will be taped every other week! Ranks: (staring with a grin) Indeed. (AzteK finally makes his way to the ring steps. He throws his chair in the ring barely missing The Informer. He ascends up the steps and through the ring ropes. AzteK walks to the corner of the ring, steps onto the second rope and raises his fist in the air triggering an eruption from the screaming XWA Fans.) Ranks: How can a seven foot giant like AzteK trigger such a response from the XWA Fans? Stevenson: Easy, Ranks. If they don't, he will just go out their and kick all their asses. (AzteK descends from the rope and makes his way towards his chair. He unfolds it and takes a seat. He grabs The Informer's Microphone.) AzteK: I am going to sit here so I ain't so damn tall compared to you. (He shoves the microphone back at The Informer and then slouches down in the chair.) Informer: AzteK, tough loss tonight to AJ Simon. You are pretty lucky that The GodFather came out and only made it a DQ instead of a pin. AzteK: Lucky? Boy, I had AJ Simon out like a light that entire match. Just because he had a little spurt at the end didn't mean I was going to lose. That punk ass got his little Simon on me but I was fully aware of where I was and was about to get up and kick his sorry ass around the ring. The GodFather just came in because he misjudged AzteK. The Informer: Very well. Would you care to comment on next week when you and The GodFather step into the ring with the lone tag team in the XWA, The Killer's. AzteK: Those two dumbass's finally thought of a tag team name???? Hell, that's creative. The fact is, The GodFather and I are going to take those two out like the Turkey Carcass on Thanksgiving. Stevenson: How creative! AzteK: AJ Simon has felt my wrath just a few minutes ago and we left his ass out like a light. The GodFather is about to take care of The Crippler so, we probably won't even have to worry about a match next week because those two punks will be running from AzteK and The GodFather. If they do happen to show up next Saturday, they will receive another beating from us and it will be ten times worse. The Informer: AzteK, next week, you have stated that it will be your last matchup in the XWA. AzteK: Yea. End of Subject. As I see it, this interview is over. You have been addressed. (AzteK stands and makes his way out of the ring towards the locker room area. Yet again no music, no lazers.) The Informer: AzteK! (AzteK turns around and stares at The Informer. He then turns back around and makes his way out of view.) Ranks: We will be right back ladies and gentleman with our main event of the evening! But first, this message. (The camera's leave the arena and head to a battle field. Napalm is flying everywhere. Bodies lay to rest, blood stained grass is all you see. A voice comes out of your television.) Voice: Coming January 24th, 1998, the eXtreme Wrestling Alliance revisits The Armageddon! (The Armageddon logo flashes up on the screen but is swiftly sent away and to the commercial break.) __ ____ ____ \ \ / /\ \ __ / / \ \ \/ / \ \ / \ / / /\ \================================================== / /\ \ \ v v / __ \ Standard 1 fall matchup /_/ \_\ \__/\__/__/ \__\ Written by J. Williams Saturday Night The GodFather vs. The Crippler ============================================================================ (The camera's return from the commercial break and focus on center ring where James Stewart stands with his microphone in hand. He is still wearing the same jacket but now has on a "Viva la Familia" tie. The fans all around the ring are screaming as they know tonight's main event is about to go down.) Stewart: Two Leaders. Two Alliances. Opposing sides. Tonight's Main Event is scheduled for one fall. (The lights go out, Voodoo Chile by Jimi Hendrix comes on. The fans go wild. The lights flicker on and off while The Crippler makes his way to the ring. Crippler is spelled in the middle of the ring with lasers.) Stewart: Making his way to the ring is the leader of DMW, The Crippler! Stevenson: Do you get the effect that Crippler idolizes a certain immortal wrestler in another organization???? Stewart: And his opponent! (The lights go off as you here twelve rings of a church bell . The arena starts to fill up with smoke like fog rolling in , in an old fish peer. The fresh smell of roses and cigar smoke fill the air . The lights slowly start to dim so you can only see the aile to the ring. Fire starts to flame up on the edges of the runway to the ring . Just then you see� a� a white and gold chariot come into the arena� being pulled by four giant black horses. The Chariot is surrounded by the Godfathers own security called "the Men in Black." Behind the Chariot are the rest of the Mafia. The Godfather is wearing his pin striped suit and a gangster style hat . He has a red rose pinned to his suit jacket and is grinning with a cigar in his mouth. The fans are chanting Viva La Familia and throwing roses at the Godfather.) Stewart: Coming down the aisle from Sicily, Italy. Here is the leader of The MAFIA, The GodFather! Ranks: What an entrance by the GodFather! He spared no expense whatsoever. Stevenson: People that can afford to do stuff like that just plain piss me off. Go invest it in the stock market. It is more productive. Ranks: Thank you for that insight Rich. I am sure we will all hurry down to our broker's now. Stevenson: (Glaring at Ranks) Ranks: The referee is again Jimmy Jameson. He checks both The GodFather and The Crippler for weapons and calls for the bell. The GodFather delivers an early blow to the nose of The Crippler. He is stunned. Stevenson: That came out of nowhere. I love the GodFather! He is still in control with vicious rights to the jaw of the DMW Leader. At this rate, The Crippler won't be in the ring for much longer. (As they continue to call the match, President Williams makes his way out of the Entrance Portal. He has his blonde hair flying freely in his face as he wears a blue suit with a solid red tie. He walks towards the Announcer's Center and takes a seat next to Andrew Ranks. William's puts on his headset and prepares to speak.) Williams: Hello Boys. Are you doing the caliber of work I am paying you for? Stevenson: Yes, Sir. As for Ranks over there, I can't keep him in line. Williams: (Grins) No ass kissing, Stevenson. Stevenson: Yes, Sir. Ranks: Well, President Williams, what brings you out here tonight? Williams: A few things. First, I would like to announce that next week on this very program, the Tournament for the XWA United States Title Tournament will begin! Ranks: Oh My! Williams: The brackets can be found on our website right now. The finals will take place at our very first Pay-Per-View on January 24 as the XWA presents The Armageddon live from our very own XWA Coliseum! Now, I must be on my way. Ranks: Thank you President Williams. (Williams takes off his headseat and makes his way back through the portal.) Ranks: The Crippler is in control now and he is giving the GodFather a beating. Stevenson: A DMW Beating my good man. Ranks: The Crippler to the top. Frog Splash on The GodFather! One. Two. He kicks out. Stevenson: How in the.... Ranks: Rich! I'm not telling you again. Stevenson: That was unbelievable! Ranks: The Crippler is going back up! He missed! Stevenson: He went to the well too many times and now both men are down. Ranks: The GodFather is making his way up. He puts his hand on The Crippler. One. Two. Three! The GodFather gets the win! Stevenson: That had to be one of the most exciting matches I have ever seen! Only in the XWA! (The GodFather finally gets up to his feet and goes for a microphone. [name withheld] hands him the mic.) GodFather: OK. KASH, if you want in the MAFIA, come down here right now and we will settle it right here. Right now. (The arena is silent. KASH Finally emerges to a sea of boos. He has on a black leather jacket. He walks to the ring with his eyes focused on nothing but The GodFather.) GodFather: Alright. Here we go. (KASH makes his way into the ring and stands next to the GodFather with a smirk on his face.) GodFather: All you have to do is sign on the dotted line and you are once again a member of the greatest stable in the world. The MAFIA. (The fans are booing as KASH looks around the coliseum. He smiles and then pulls out a pen.) KASH: Can you turn around so I can use your back to sign this thing. GodFather: You got it. (The GodFather turns around and KASH begins to sign the contract but stops, reaches into his coat pocket and pulls out a baseball bat.) Ranks: Oh My! (KASH taps The GodFather on the back. He turns around and KASH decks him with the bat. The GodFather falls to the ground as KASH unzips his jacket revealing a shirt that says "DMW." The camera's role the copyright notice.)
