BWF Thursday Thrashings, 30 October 1997

30 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The Cameras fade in from the Frank's Laundromat commercial. A narration by Andrew Ranks begins to play. "For years, Thursday Thrashings ruled the Thursday Night Wars. Going up against tough challenges like NBC's must see TV, MTV, Thursday Football Games, and ESPN in General. Now, the BWF returns to Thursday Nights with the biggest, baddest Thrashings ever!) (The BWF Theme begins to play as a flaming BWF Logo comes up on your screen. It is blown up and the Thursday Thrashings logo appears on the screen. It is wiped away to the arena with all of the screaming BWF fans waiting to see their favorite wrestlers. They are holding up the "Where's AzteK" or "Starr 3:16" Signs.) Ranks: Welcome everyone to the return of the hottest thing on television today. Welcome to the return of Thursday Thrashings! Hello again everyone. I am Andrew Ranks along side Jerry "Iceman" Richards and his Lordship Williams III. Iceman: Hello fans. Boy have we got a jam packed card for you tonight. In our main event, we have Steve Lauria taking on Pain Maker who is making his BWF debut. Lordship: Pain Maker? What a nipple. But, who could forget, I am the newest member of The MAFIA. Iceman: Don't remind me. Ranks: Also tonight, Tommy Starr takes on KASH. Iceman: After the performance we saw by Tommy Starr last week, I think he is unstoppable. Lordship: If you ask me, KASH will whipe the floor with that Starr boy. As for Starr's performance Sunday. Who couldn't beat SuperStar? (Just as his Lordship says that, Staying Alive by the BeeGee's begins to play over the P.A. system) Ranks: What in the...? (The fans are up booing as Disco Stu makes his way out of the entrance way with numerous disco points to the air.) Iceman: I thought he was fired. Get Michael Richards down there. (Michael Richards cuts down to the entrance way to speak with Disco Stu. Disco has a huge grin on his face.) Richards: Disco Stu, I thought you were fired from the BWF? Disco: Well, Mikey Baby. You can't keep the disco king off the prowl. I got that old hippie President Williams to let me back in so I can mop up all the squares in this federation. Richards: Well, where were you Sunday Night when you were supposed to face SuperStar. Disco: That's for me to know and you to find out. As for SuperStar, I will see you in the cage. That is when the Disco King makes his return to the ring. Now, be gone Mikey Baby. You're crampin' my style. (Stayin' Alive begins to play as Disco struts his way back to the dressing room.) Ranks: What is Williams thinking? Tommy Starr vs. $$$KASH$$$ (As the camera return from a commercial, we see a dark tall figure standing in the cold,dark night of Alaska. We hear the wind playing with the trees. Other then that there is absolute silence,untill we hear a cold voice speaking.) Man: O,'twas a din to fright a monster's ear; To make an earthquake ! sure it was the roar Of a whole herd of lions...Shakespeare The Tempest..act II,scene II. That's what people would use to describe my entrance those days. I ruled a mighty Kingdom and I saw my Kingdom crumble under my feet due to a few savages. And before my wrath could be complete there was a closing of my battle field. Hence the fact that I will be making my return here. There will be no remorse...there will be no regret. The only thing there will be is carnage. Be prepared...for your'e ending". (Suddenly there is a big thunderbolt that rips through the air lighting up the sky. But when the sky is lightened by the lighting we see no one.) Man: Be Prepared. (The Cameras cut to the announcers table. As it does, Mo Money Mo Problems begins to play as KASH makes his way to the ring. Lights go off as a spot light hits KASH in the middle of the ring there is a green spot light shaped in a dollar sign when he get's into the ring lights come back on.) Ranks: What do you think of that mysterious man gentleman? Secrecy seems to be of the essence with him. Iceman: This man seems to be a bit disturbed if you ask me. Quoting Shakespeare all the time? There is something he isn't telling us. Lordship: If you ask me, this man seems to know what he is talking about. The chap enjoys the finer arts of life. MAFIA material definately. Iceman: Anything that we find odd, you find as MAFIA material. Lordship: Hardly. (As the two announcers argue over nothing imparticular, For whom the bell tolls begins playing over the p.a. system as Tommy Starr walks to the ring, badgering the fans and talking "junk" to his opponents.) Iceman: He sures knows how to get a rise out of the crowd! Ranks: The referee for this bout is Eric Stevens. He checks both wrestlers for illegal objects and sounds the bell. Both wrestlers lock up. KASH runs Tommy Starr into the corner. Starr seems impressed by it. Iceman: What brute force by KASH. Ranks: Indeed. They lock back up. Oh my! Tommy Starr just whipped KASH over him. Talk about contradicting someone. Lordship: That stupid nipple Tommy Starr needs to stop showboating and get back to business. Ranks: Tommy Starr has KASH in an armbar and he has it applied good. He brings KASH up, whips him into the ropes, back body drop! Tommy Starr is on the ground punching the living daylights out of KASH! That savage! Lordship: I LOVE IT I LOVE IT! Iceman: Shut up, LordSh$$!!!!! Ranks: Hey Hey Hey! We don't need that. This is a family program. Iceman: Say what you want but Tommy Starr is dishing out one heck of a beating to KASH. Ranks: He whips him into the ropes. CROSS BODY BLOCK BY KASH!!!!!!!!!!! He caught Starr by surprise. He picks up Starr by the neck! CHOKE SLAM!!!!! One, TWO, THREE!!!!! KASH with the upset! Lordship: How did he pull that one off???? Ranks: I don't know but what a display at the end by KASH. (As they say this President Williams makes his way out of the entrance way. He has a mic in his hand and begins to speak) Williams: Alright, everyone shut up for a second. I got an announcement to make. Due to the excess hate mail I am receiving and the fact that I want to see two pathetic people beat the hell out of themselves, Lord William and Jerry Richards will not be fighting at Santa Smash but rather next week right here on Thursday Thrashings! The Announcers: WHAT!?!?!?! Steve Lauria vs. Pain Maker (The Camera's return from the break. Jerry Richards and his Lordship seem pretty delirious after the announcement they have just heard. They begin to wonder why they must wrestle next Thursday. Who will take their spot in the announcers chair?) Ranks: Welcome back to BWF Thursday Thrashings. It's time for our main event. Let's get to the ring. (War begins to play over the PA System as Steve Lauria makes his way ringside ignoring everyone.) Iceman: Talk about Tommy Starr being Mr. Intensity. Steve Lauria has to take the award for it. Lordship: Why don't you just shut the hell up. I am getting sick of your voice you worthless nipple. Iceman: That's it! (Suddenly Iceman takes his headphones off and attacks his Lordship!!! He is beating the tar out of him.) Ranks: Oh my God! Jerry Richards is attack Lord William! Get Security out here! (Suddenly The GodFather makes his way out and pulls The Iceman off of his Lordship.) Ranks: It is total chaos at the announcers booth of all places! (While the little scuffle is going on at the announcers booth, Pain Maker has made his way to the ring and is attacking Steve Lauria. Back at the booth, both men seem to have settled down and Andrew Ranks is sitting inbetween them.) Ranks: Can we please save the rest for next Thursday? Iceman: He started it. Lordship: (Staring)... Ranks: Nonetheless, Pain Maker is really taking it to Steve Lauria. He has him in a corner and is just punching the daylights out of him. The Ref gets to a 4 count but Pain Maker wisely moves away from Lauria but instantaniously return to the punching! Iceman: What an animal Pain Maker is. (As they say this, the camera's cut to the crowd where a familiar man is sitting. No one has seemed to notice yet. All of a sudden the crowd begins to cheer.) Ranks: Isn't that Gabriel Knight???? Iceman: Well, I'll be damned, it is! What is he doing here? Ranks: I don't know. He isn't under contract with the BWF to my knowledge. For those fans who don't remember, Gabriel Knight was a former BWF SuperStar who was with us since the beginning but wasn't brought back with the BWF's return. Lordship: He must not have made the cut then you nipple. Ranks: Thank You for that insight your Lordship. Back to the ring. Pain Maker is still in control and has Lauria in a very well executed Grapevine. The Ref is asking Lauria if he wants to give in but I don't think you will be hearing those words out of Lauria. (Gabriel Knight gets out of his seat and jumps over the railing and moves toward the ring.) Ranks: What is Gabriel Knight doing? He is walking toward the ring. Pain Maker sees him coming and lets the Grapevine go. He is distracting the referee?? Iceman: Knight has a chair. OH! HE JUST LEFT A DENT IN LAURIA'S HEAD!!!!!! Ranks: Look at him, he is going back to his seat now. Pain Maker covers him. One. Two. Three. Lordship: I don't know this Knight guy but I like him! Iceman: You would. (Pain Maker makes his way back to the locker room and acknowledges Knight.) Ranks: What in the hell is going on in the BWF? For Lord Williamd and Jerry Richards, I am Andrew Ranks. See ya Sunday. (The cameras cut to the Thrashings Replay) The BackNet Wrestling Federation http://www.backnet.com/bwf/
