========================= XWA SoundByte December 22, 1997 CEO: Justin Williams ========================= *The GodFather* [The Godfather is at Pinto's Deli . He is sitting in the dark cornor of the Deli that he calls his area , his Cosa Nostra . The area is dark and mysterius . The air is filled with The strong odor of freshly brewed coffee fills the air . There are certain smells that attract your nose though , its like the smells have density . Sort of like currents in the ocean . The stronger smells flow at the bottom , because they are heavier , but the light smelling coffee’s you can only smell them when  you get up . The currents sometimes get mixed around when someone opens the heavy wooden door , and a light breeze comes in . you can tell that it is wooden because it screeches every time someone opens it . You can also tell that it is heavy because every time someone opens the door you  can hear it screech and then a big “ clank” as it is slammed shut . There is a little bell that rings every time the door is opened . It gets a little annoying after a while . You  can also hear someone play the guitar over in the corner of the room .You can’t tell what they are playing but it has a nice rhythm to it .You  can tell he is using a hard pick because there is a twang to the sound that the strings are making .  You hear the little bell ring again as someone walks in . You can tell its a well dressed mafia member without even looking because of his shoes . You can tell because the way the shoes are taping on the  wooden floor . Expensive dress shoes have hat tap to it , any body else would wear sneakers . Sneakers don’t tap they absorb the impact and don’t tap on the floor .You  can also tell it is raining outside . First of all , You  can hear the rain hitting the roof like a tin shack . And secondly You  can hear the squishy sound the business man’s shoes are making when he steps on the floor . That is how you can tell  it is raining . You hear the expresso makers whistle in the background and the workers hustling around the counter . You hear the jars being opened and the aroma of the coffee traveling its way to my nose .You can tell it is fresh because you hear the “swish” that the can makes when opened . Different jars are being clanked together as they make their way to the counter so people can smell or taste the syrup . You can hear the refrigerator door being opened and slammed shut as the workers get the milk to add to the coffee . The Godfather sits and admires all of the coffee and cigars he gets thirsty for a cafe con leche . The Godfather gets up and orders him self a cup of java . The Godfather is waiting for the XWA there so he can give his interview .The Godfather gets his hot steamy cup of java and he takes a sip . The thick foam reaches his lips and he puts it down . He reaches for a cigar in his black armani pocket humador . He takes a cigar out and lights it up .He starts to puff on it and looks around . He looks out the large window that faces the parking lot . A black Van pulls upwith the XWA Logo on the side . A bald man comes out of the passenger side and goes to the back of the van . He pulls out the camera while another gentleman gets out of the drivers side . The driver is a skinny ,young , guy with long slicked black hair . The driver pulls out the tripod and a tape . They start to walk up the steps into the coffe shop part of the Deli . The door swings open and the Godfather motions to the men to come back to his part of the shop .] Godfather : Welcome guys it seems that you two are a little late . What the hell happened ? Interviewer : Well we got delaid because of the traffic jam . There was a three car pile up on third and main . The Godfather : Well I have been here for a while . You are lucky that I didn’t leave . I am in a good mood tonight because of the Christmas spirit . That and the fight tomorrow I am going to kick the living crap out of the sorry excuse of a human being . I am going to pound him into a living pile of shit . Interviewer : Why is there so much bad blood between you and Kash ? The Godfather : Where the hell have you been ? Kash was once a respectful wrestler and was well respected by the mafia and myself . See we where in the BWF  the old XWA . He was a young nobody but I remember seeing a speck of talent in the tub of lard . I took him under the Mafia’s wing . I wanted to make him into a good wrestler . I did that .He was nobody without the Mafia . Hell he still isn’t anything now with out the Mafia . I felt sorry for your sorry ass  you stupid freak. I took you into the Mafia for a feeling of pitty , I think that sign of talent in a pile a shit was just a peice of food you didn’t digest very well . I see no talent in you now ! Your a loser that had to back stab your own Familia ! Now your in a so called stable . Well it might be a stable but its not a Familia . A Familia is better then a stable ,see we are in it for just not for one cause , but for many . We help each other out . They help me and I help them . We respect each other ! We aren’t a bunch of pansies like you guys ! Interviewer:  Did you even think that Kash would even think of turning on you ? The Godfather : Actually I didn’t think he had the balls to do it . But I guess he grew some fake ones no that he is holding on to the his stable mates . I went there hoping that he kept his talent and that he was willing to let t grow . But now its too late my man . You turned your back on us , Noone has ever done that . But don’t worry you will pay and you will pay darely .The Mafia will make your life a miserable life . I will make it into a living hell ! It will be a nightmare for you . You turned your back to us , but I will stab it for real . I will tak your legs and rip them and beat you over your ugly head . See when you join the mafia , your mafia for life .The only way to join the Mafia is dead . So I have to fix that , I have to kill you . Interviewer : Would you really kilhim . I mean that is illeagal . The Godfather : Ya I would kill him . Who cares if its illeagal . That has never stoped the mafia .I will kill him with my bare hands . I won’t bring a gun to the ring . I will torture him with my bare hands . I will make him suffer . I will take him into the ring and just watch him beg for forgivness . I will watch his face twitch in agony and his body twinge . I will make him bleed out of places he didn’t know he could bleed . See you stabbed us in the back , now its our term , This is revenge for me . This is revenge for the Mafia . This is revenge for the Familia ! The Mafia will regain its respect . Hell we  never lost it ! We are the best , we were the best , we will reamin the best , and we will be the best there ever will be ! Interviewer : Are you going to have the Mafia interfare in that match ? The Godfather : Well I might . If Kash brings in his fellow pansies then I have too . I mean if the start it then I will have the mafia come in and beat the living shit out of them . I have to protect myself . Interviewer : How is that jaw . Do you think that it will prevent you from being the best ?Will it cost you the match ? The Godfather : I hope not . I don’t think it will . I will have to be careful . I don’t want to bite off his ear right away . I want to torture him first . I will take that wreath and put it around his scrawny little neck . I will hang him in the middle of the ring after I am done with that punk . After I am done I ma going to take him and decorate him with the Christmas wreath and all the lights and put him in the middle of the ring and Make him beg for his life . Do you think that I will cut him down . Nope ! You think that his so called freinds will come and get him no . Even if the try the mafia will be there around the ring to stop everyone . The don’t care about you Kash . You actually think if your in a jam that the DMV will help you ? The stole their name from the Departmant of Motor Vehicles . They suck . Why don’t you go back and fix my tickets and make my license plate look better . Hell those kids need you to teach them how to drive . Kash your in it for the money . You are not going to last for ong in this , The XWA is for the big boys not the sorry little punks like you . You actually think you have a chance against me ? This is nothing but a Master VS. Pupil match . And guess what the Master always wins . I tought you some of those moves . I tought them to you . So I know how to counter attack ! You didn’t think I would teach all of my students all of my moves did you ? I am going to kick your ass nice and slowly . I will never give you a chance for the upper hand . I am going to take you to the limit . Your a Dead Fuc*** ! Your nothing but a loser . I am taking you down like a rag doll . You can beat your self . If I put you in a wet paper bag you would suffacate because you wouldn’t have the power to get your ass out of it . I bet your mommy stil gives you a bath hugh? Ya mommy brush my teeth ! mommy wipe my ass ! Man your nothing , you were nothing , you will never be anything .I am the best in the in the XWA , your nothing . Kash Get Ready For The Beating Of a Life Time ! [The Godfather tells the interviewers to go away so he can workout for the match .] *The Crippler* (The Crippler is in the DMW locker room, he is standing with a reporter.) Reporter: Crippler, Christmas Chaos is upon us can you tell us what your feeling are. Crippler: Well like I said, Christmas Chaos is the night that the DMW takes control of the DMW.  AJ and Kash they are as ready for there matches as they ever could be.  As for Dustin Buchanon, I don't know whats going on with him, I don't know if he's even DMW. Reporter: Well what about your Death Match with Art Middleton. Crippler: Middleton, you better be prepared for the worst woppin you've ever got.  I've been preparing for this match ever since I heard it would take place.  I know everything about you Middleton, and I will use that to my advantage.  All I can say is be very prepared. Reporter: Crippler a few minutes ago you were talking about Dustin Buchanon and you said you don't know if he's DMW.  So is there some trouble in camp DMW. Crippler: Well since I beat him in that match the other night he's been acting weird.  Not to me, but to AJ.  AJ is my best friend and co-founder of the DMW, and if Buchanon has a problem with AJ he has a problem with me. Reporter: Well are you going to kick him out of the DMW. Crippler: I'm not sure, I'll do what I have to do when the time comes. Reporter: Well that's all the questions I have, good luck in your match against Art Middleton.
