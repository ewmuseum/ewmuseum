Williams =======|__|================================================================== (The cameras fade into the outside of a small warmlooking house in a quiet peaceful neighborhood on a cold, snowy night. A small window is shown with a large fire burning on the inside of the house. The cameras move through the window and into the house. In the living room is a large christmas tree all decorated with lights, ribbons, and ornaments. Nestled under the tree are dozens of wrapped packages just waiting for to be opened. In front of the fire sits a young boy and girl playing together while Mom and Dad sit in their respective recliners reading the paper. Dad puts down his paper and goes to pour himself a glass of egg nog. The children all crowd in front of the television as dad turns it onto scenes from last Tuesday night. Shown are The MAFIA beating down Tower and causing him so much injury that his wrestling career was ended. As the family continues to watch the Tuesday Warfare clips, debris starts to fall from the fireplace. Mom and Dad are in astonishment along with the children as they see Santa fall into the hot hellatious fire. He finally jumps out cursing a blue streak. He begins to talk.) Santa: You call that eXtreme? You aint seen nothing yet! (Santa walks over to dad and grabs the remote and turns the TV to the some of the most violent, gory, and downright twisted matches you could ever imagine. It can only be one show. It can be only from one federation. That being the eXtreme Wrestling Alliance. This year, Christmas will never be the same.) (As the cameras fade away from Santa and his extreme entertainment, Machinehead by Bush begins to whale annoyingly through your television as the XWA logo appears on your screen. A chant of "XWA" is heard in the background of the song. Clips of various XWA Stars are shown. The MAFIA beating down Tower. KASH and GodFather brawling. Rictor attacking DMW. As the action continues, a large red and green logo begins to slowly come towards your screen. The festive logo reads the words name of a card no one will ever forget. That name is Christmas Chaos '98. As the logo stands idlely on your screen, Santa begins to walk across with fully loaded machine gun in hand. He is "Ho Ho Ho'ing" his heart out as he pumps the logo full of lead and causes it to fall to the ground and make the sold out XWA Arena emerge on the screen. Thousands of screaming fans surround the place carrying signs, waving T-Shirts, or just flat out being the nuts that they are. again. As the cameras circle the arena, a sidebar graphic proclaims that this is indeed live! The cameras finally move away from the screaming fans and cut to both Rich Stevenson and Andrew Ranks who both sit calmly at their seats at the XWA Announcers Center. Stevenson: Come on, Andy. Where's your Christmas Spirit? It's the time of loving, sharing, and giving. But, in the XWA it is the time for blood, violence, and total Chaos! Oh I love Hardcore Specials! Ranks: Fans, we come live to you once again from the XWA Coliseum in Evansville, IN. We welcome our viewers who are seeing us for the first time as this broadcast is going out nationwide! What an action packed night we have for you tonight. In our main event, the score will finally be settled. The GodFather takes on KASH in a Christmas Cage. Rich, you seem to be the expert on this cage. What is so special about it? Stevenson: President Williams must have been in a festive mood when he made up this match. You got your normal, boring steel cage, but this time, there are christmas lights festooned around it, Barbwire wreaths hanging from it, and so much tinsel you couldn't stir it with a stick. Oh Williams is a genius! Ranks: Also tonight, in a PitFighter match, Gene Hoffman takes on arch rival Riot in their second battle here in the XWA. For those that don't know, a PitFighter match is no rules whatsoever. First one to pass out loses. Stevenson: Two idiots that should be in Mortal Kombat beating the hell out of each other. (Sarcastically) Oh I can hardly wait. Ranks: In addition, in a submission matchup, the man known as Rictor tries to bounce back from his loss last Saturday as he takes on DMW's AJ Simon. Stevenson: I like this Rictor guy. A bit on the crazy side, but I think he has some potential. As for the punk from DMW. Let's just say that after Rictor is done with him, I got my nine iron in the back and I want a few whacks at him and the rest of his little "suck-it" buddies. Ranks: Just signed Sunday Fans, Art Middleton is taking on The Crippler in a death match. This is going to be brutal. They can go anywhere. Use anything. The first one to be knocked out for ten seconds, loses. And finally, another DMW vs. MAFIA matchup as Distruct takes on Dustin Buchanan in a four corners matchup. Stevenson: Oh what a two hours this is going to be! I am as giddy as a schoolboy! Ranks: (Confused) School Boy???? Stevenson: Hey! I take no responsibility for what all that egg nog in the back is going to make me say. Ranks: With that, we'll be back after this commercial break with the first match of the night! (The cameras fade from the announcers center to an advertisement for the first ever XWA Pay-Per-View.
