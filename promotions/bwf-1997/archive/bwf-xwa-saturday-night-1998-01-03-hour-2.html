XWA Saturday Night, 3 January 1998, Hour Two

January 3, 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
__ ____ ____ \ \ / /\ \ __ / / \ \ \/ / \ \ / \ / / /\ \================================================== / /\ \ \ v v / __ \ January 3, 1997 /_/ \_\ \__/\__/__/ \__\ Interview with President Williams Saturday Night Written by J. Williams ============================================================================ (The camera's return from the commercial break to the thunderous booms of the
Pyro that only the XWA could dish out. Fireworks are going off all over the arena. It is amazine how much money that William's is willing to put into the XWA. Most federation heads would be two cheap to dish out the fireworks twice a night.)
Ranks: Welcome to hour number two of XWA Saturday Night! (Machinehead, the XWA Theme, begins to blare through the PA system as the pyro dies off. The arena stands and cheers as the beloved president of the eXtreme Wrestling Alliance is about to make his way towards the ring. He emerges from the entrance portal in a gray suit with a red tie. He jogs down to the ring and then into it. He has a large smile on his face as he waves to the sea of fans in attendance. William's grabs a microphone he has in his coat pocket and begins to speak.)
Williams: Boston, you have just gotten eXtreme! (The fans explode in cheers!) Williams: I have come out here with some good news and some bad news. The bad news is that the pay-per-view scheduled for January 24 has been put off.
Why? We suits in the front office felt that we did not have the talent base to give off a spectacular yet. But, rest assured! It has been rescheduled!
February 14, 1998 as the XWA Presents Valentine Massacre '98! And, the main event for the card will be...
(As William's is about to announce the main event, his mic goes dead and the lights go out. A mysterious unheard music begins to play throughout the arena as a spotlight shines on the entrance portal where Steve Lauria struts his way down towards the ringside area. The fans in the Fleet Center are very Pro-Lauria after his actions at Christmas Chaos in taking out both a DMW member and the MAFIA leader.)
Ranks: Here comes the man that will lead the XWA into the twenty-first century! Steve Lauria!
Williams: Mr. Lauria. Lauria: You think that you are just going to make up some piddly little main event without Steve Lauria being in it?
Williams: Well... Lauria: Shut up. Listen up everyone and listen up good. I ain't here to make any friends or win any titles. I am here to dismantle this little mickey mouse federation piece by piece.
Stevenson: HA HA HA! Williams: Well, I don't think that is very appropriate talk for out here. We are the XWA if you didn't know.
Lauria: DO you think I care? I am here to knock out every one of you punks and claim the XWA as my own.
Williams: (Becoming very aggitated) Well, we will just see about that. I will tell you what. You want to be in the main event at the Pay-Per-View?
You got it. Ladies and Gentlemen, the main event at Valentine Massacre is an eight man single elimination tournament for the XWA World Championship! The participants are as follows. The GodFather, KASH, STEVE LAURIA,
"ShowStopper" Art Middleton, AJ Simon, Johnny Smith, Wu Fang, and The Crippler. (Williams walks out of the ring and towards the back. The camera's cut to a commercial.) __ ____ ____ \ \ / /\ \ __ / / \ \ \/ / \ \ / \ / / /\ \================================================== / /\ \ \ v v / __ \ January 3, 1997 /_/ \_\ \__/\__/__/ \__\ LordZero vs. Riot Saturday Night Written by J. Williams ============================================================================ (The camera's return from the commercial break. The GodFather theme begins to play as Riot makes his way towards the ring preparing for the fight of his life.)
Ranks: Folks, our next match pits the MAFIA's Riot against the very skillful newcomer LordZero.
Stevenson: I like this LordZero guy. Too bad he has the brains of a chimp. What kind of idiot wants to step into the ring with a MAFIA member? He is going to be killed!
(Frayed Ends of Sanity by Metallica plays as LordZero makes his way out of the entrance portal and to the ring.)
Ranks: Check out LordZero fans. The intensity, the determination. That man will go far here in the XWA!
Stevenson: Yea. Wasn't the last person you said that would do that was
Deranged Stu? Gee. Where is he now?
Ranks: Let's not get into that. Our referee for this matchup is again James
Jameson as he is flying solo on the reffing job.
Stevenson: Why doesn't Williams get his act together and start signing up some more refs? Cheap ass.
Ranks: There's the bell and this thing is underway. Riot and LordZero step up to each other. They are staring each other down. The trash talk is flying folks! Oh My! Riot just slapped LordZero so hard he sent him to the ground!
Stevenson: HA HA HA HA! What did I tell you. The MAFIA Rules! Ranks: Riot with a hammerlock applied on LordZero. This is surprising that
Riot is in control of this matchup. I would have expected more of an offense from him.
Stevenson: It just shows you that the MAFIA intimidates everyone. That no good sack of goo is gonna die! Go Riot!
Ranks: (Wondering what Stevenson is talking about.) OK. Thank's for that insight. Riot releases the lock. He whips Zero into the ropes. Clothesline by LordZero! That came out of nowhere! LordZero follows it up with a whip into the corner! OH MY! Zero Splash!
Stevenson: A What!?!?!? Ranks: A Zero Splash. Stevenson: That might be the most egotistical name for a wrestling move I have ever heard. Ranks: Well, that's your opinion. LordZero has Riot down to the ground. He's going to the top. Flying Leg Drop! What a shot by LordZero! He just knocked all the wind out of Riot's Lungs.
Stevenson: Riot is just playing opposum. He will be up any minute to take out that no good bastard LordZero.
Ranks: You seem to be stuttering a bit Rich. Sensing defeat? Stevenson: (Trying to retaliate a response to hide his fear.) Um..Um..NO!! Ranks: (Chuckling under his breath.) Stevenson: Hey! Shut up! Ranks: LordZero has a sleeper locked in tight on Riot now. Riot is going out like a light. Jameson lifts Riot's arm. One. Again. Two. And again. But no! Riot is still in this matchup.
Stevenson: (Getting back some of his courage) HA HA! I knew it! Come on
Riot! Take him out!
Ranks: Riot is up. Elbow to the jaw of LordZero. Riot whips him into the ropes! Dropkick with authority. LordZero is down. Riot is going to the top rope. No! LordZero is up! Zero is up! He throws Riot across the ring.
He is applying the figure-four on Riot! Riot taps out! LordZero wins! Stevenson: Dammit! Dammit! Dammit! Dammit! Ranks: LordZero with the come behind victory on the seasoned veteran Riot! Riot is sure in a slump nowadays. Stevenson: He ain't in a slump. That match was a fluke I Tell you! A fluke! Ranks: We will be right back with Hour #3 of XWA Saturday Night!
