XWA Saturday Night, 3 January 1998, Hour One

January 3, 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
__ ____ ____ \ \ / /\ \ __ / / \ \ \/ / \ \ / \ / / /\ \================================================== / /\ \ \ v v / __ \ January 3, 1997 /_/ \_\ \__/\__/__/ \__\ Introduction Saturday Night Written by J. Williams ============================================================================ (The End of the commercial for that awful, awful waste of money movie "Spiceworld" is show. The TV fades from a pitch black screen to the logo for Christmas Chaos, the most lethal two hours ever put on Television. A narration by Andrew Ranks begins to play as various clips from the night are shown. "Christmas Day 1997 will be remembered as the day the XWA put itself on the wrestling map. The day that AJ Simon took out the most hardcore bastard in the XWA, Rictor. The day that Steve Lauria stopped what was supposed to be the end of the longest fued in XWA history. The night that we became a contendor. Now that 1997 is past us and we are in 1998, we can only wonder what will come of the XWA. Who will show up? Who will leave? What surprises are in store for us? Tonight, we kick off the new year with a special three hour edition of XWA Saturday Night!)
(The camera's cut to the Saturday Night Intro Sequence. Machinhead by Bush begins to blare through your Television as the Saturday Night opening starts.
Random action shots of AzteK, Deranged Stu, and others are shown quickly. Their finishing movers are applied to helpless victims.
Their brute force shown to the world for the first time. The XWA Logo then appears on the screen and the music begins to die away and the fans screaming is now heard. The logo suddenly blows up as the camera's return to a now darkened arena. It is now lit up by the loud, thunderous booms and lights of the state of the art pyrotechnics brought in by President Williams. The pyro finally begins to die off as the camera's cut from the fans to the XWA
Announcer's Center where Andrew Ranks and Rich Stevenson sit calmly while the ruckus of the pyro was just behind them. Rich and Andrew are both wearing their respective grey suits with red ties. Rich has his wet hair tied back in a pony tail while Andrew's business man hair is perfectly parted to the side and gelled back. Andrew shuffles his papers a final time and begins to speak.)
Ranks: Welcome to three hours of XWA Saturday Night! Hello again folks,
I am Andrew Ranks alongside my tag-team partner "The Man of a Thousand Words"
Rich Stevenson. Stevenson: Can you feel the excitement, Andy Boy? I feel there is blood in the air! Ranks: We come to you live from the soldout Fleet Center in Boston, Massachusetts! The first time in history that we have moved away from the
XWA arena and boy did we pick the perfect place to be! Folks, tonight we have 8 great matches for you as well as a special interview with President
Justin Williams.
Stevenson: I can't wait to hear what Boss Has to say. I hear it has to do with the first ever XWA Pay-Per-View Extravaganza!
Ranks: Tonight in our main event, The Prodigy's "ShowStopper" Art Middleton takes on DMW's very own KASH. Also on the card, we finish up the United
States Title Tournament's first round with two pre-recorded matchups from last Tuesday Night. Due to Technical Difficulties we couldn't show them to you then, but we are tonight. In a rematch from Chaos, Rictor takes on
AJ Simon and The Crippler takes on Riot.
Stevenson: Oh what a night this is going to be! I hope it lives up to the carnage we saw at Christmas Chaos. Oh It was grand!
Ranks: Fans we will be right back after this commercial break. (The camera's cut to a commercial as Stevenson has his satanistic grin on his face hoping for more blood, gore, and violence. The dumbass needs to realize that it ain't gonna happen. Or is it?)
__ ____ ____ \ \ / /\ \ __ / / \ \ \/ / \ \ / \ / / /\ \================================================== / /\ \ \ v v / __ \ January 3, 1997 /_/ \_\ \__/\__/__/ \__\ *Pre-Recorded* AJ Simon vs. Rictor Saturday Night Written by J. Knapik ============================================================================ "At this time, we'd like to welcome our colleague from Tuesday Night
Warfare, Brett Rose. He'll be joining us for the last three matches of the night. Welcome, Brett."
Rose: "Glad to be here, Andrew." Stevenson: "Oh hell, why does he have to be here? He just screws up my perfectly good commentary."
Ranks: "Well, Rictor has said that he had a special surprise for AJ Simon tonight, and knowing Rictor, he could go beserk. Brett is here for the safety of AJ and the fans by request of Commissioner Knapik."
Stevenson: "Brett couldn't do a thing against Rictor!" Rose: "How about I show you what I can do..." Ranks: "Now guys don't start this again! Let's get to the next match! This will be a match to decide who will go on to the semifinals in the XWA
United States tournament!"
Stevenson: "Andy, Julia Child would excite you!" Ranks: "That's Andrew..." (Camera pans over to ring announcer James Stewart.) Stewart: The next match is set for ONE FALL with a 20 MINUTE TIME LIMIT and is a FIRST ROUND MATCH in the UNITED STATES CHAMPIONSHIP TOURNAMENT! First, making his way to the ring at this time representing the DMW...hailing from
KITCHENER, ONTARIO, weighing 409 POUNDS, standing 6 FEET 8 INCHES tall...AJ SIMON! (A mild fireworks spread riddles the entrance as Simon steps through the portal to a tirade of boos and a handful of cheers. He swaggers to the ring arrogantly, acknowledging a few DMW signs along the way.)
Stewart: AND HIS OPPONENT! (Before the announcement can be made, the man known as Rictor LEAPS over the guardrail! The crowd is going nuts! Simon's back is turned as Rictor gets into the ring, wielding a bat!)
Ranks: "AJ WATCH..." (Rictor cracks the bat over AJ Simon's head, shattering the bat! Brett Rose jumps from his seat and onto the apron, but is met with a dropkick, sending him into the guardrail!)
Stevenson: "Told ya Brett couldn't do anything..." (Simon is on the floor, holding the back of his head which is bleeding. Rictor leaps over the top rope and starts to run towards the portal.) Ranks: "Ladies and gentlemen, Rictor has lost it. Let's go to a commercial break, and when we get back, Diesel Power takes on The Killers."
__ ____ ____ \ \ / /\ \ __ / / \ \ \/ / \ \ / \ / / /\ \================================================== / /\ \ \ v v / __ \ January 3, 1997 /_/ \_\ \__/\__/__/ \__\ *Pre-Recorded* Diesel Power vs. The Killers Saturday Night Written by J. Knapik ============================================================================ Ranks: "This next match should be a good one. A tag team match between
Diesel Power and The Killers."
("Diesel Power" by Prodigy booms over the PA system as the lights go down in the arena. Rich Stevenson gets up on the announcer's table and starts to do his "Richie Shuffle" swinging his "bootie" around wildly.)
Ranks: "Oh hell...GET DOWN from there!...What did Commissioner Knapik tell you about DANCING?!?"
Stevenson: "This is for the crowd baby!" Stewart: "The next match is set for ONE FALL with a TWENTY MINUTE TIME
LIMIT. First, introducing, at a combined weight of 485 POUNDS! "Iceman"
JC...BIG DADDY ROB....DIESEL POWER! (A spotlight hits the portal as Rob and JC step through. The fans are going ballistic as they give each other a "high five". The spotlight follows them down the aisle as they slap the fans hands.)
Ranks: "Truly two of the more popular wrestlers here in the XWA." (Stevenson gets down and puts his headset back on.) Stevenson: "Hey, where'd ole' Bretty boy go?" Ranks: "Backstage for medical attention I suppose." Stevenson*smiling* : "HAHA! That moron..." Stewart: And THERE OPPONENTS! Representing DMW...AJ Simon...The
Crippler...they are THE KILLERS!
(To no music, Simon and The Crippler walk down the aisle towards the ring, there eyes fixed on
Diesel Power. Fans are booing all around, one fan having the guts to throw a beer bottle at AJ Simon! They hop up onto the apron and walk into the ring.)
Ranks: "Wow, AJ Simon is being strong, coming out her aster that attack from Rictor. You can tell he is still hurting."
Stevenson: "I say AJ kicks some butt here! If they only had some music I could dance to!" Ranks: "Yeah, that's just what we need." (The Crippler starts things off against Big Daddy Rob. They look at each other cautiously then lock up.)
Ranks: "Big test of strength here, and it looks like Rob is gonna win it! (Rob lifts The Crippler over his head into a VERTICAL SUPLEX, crashing to the ground hard.)
Ranks: "Good power move to start things off!" Stevenson: "Wait till my man AJ gets in there! WHOO!" (Rob tosses The Crippler into the corner and meets him with a BIG SPLASH! With a scream to the fans, Rob gets up on the middle turnbuckle and starts driving hard right punches into The Crippler's face! The crowd chants
"1...2...3...4...5....6...7...8..." but are stopped there as The Crippler lifts Rob up and delivers an atomic drop to Big Daddy Rob!)
Ranks: "The Crippler showing some ring smarts there. He's still a little woozy from those punches though!"
(The Crippler nails Rob's leg with a dropkick to the back of the knee, taking him off his feet. The Crippler runs into the ropes and rebounds into a FLYING SNAP MARE!)
Stevenson: "You could hear his neck snap! HAHAHA!" Ranks: "You're one ick little guy, you know that?" (The Crippler grabs Rob by the hair and tags out to AJ Simon. Simon delivers a series of axhandle chops to Rob's neck.)
Ranks: "This AJ Simon guy is huge! He weighs almost as JC and Rob combined!" Stevenson: "That's my main man! GO AJ! WHOOOOOO!" Ranks: "Yeah, well he's not waisting anytime here! A HUGE POWERBOMB into a pin from AJ Simon! The referee counts- 1...2... NO! What courage from Big
Daddy Rob!"
(AJ Simon lifts Rob up to his side and laughs! The crowd boos as Simon drops Rob into a DEVASTATING BACKBREAKER! Rob yells out in pain.)
Ranks: "This could be it folks! 1.....2.....NO! ANOTHER KICKOUT! AJ has got to be wondering what he has to do to keep this man down!"
Stevenson: "It's okay, AJ! C'MON BABY!" (AJ grabs Rob by the hair and drags him over to "Iceman" JC and smiles. JC tries to get in the ring, but the referee wont let him! AJ is holding out
Rob's hand just out of reach of JC's hand!
Aj turns around and laughs with The Crippler, and JC leans over the ropes, coming off his feet and making the tag! SIMON DOESN'T SEE IT! JC gets up on the top turnbuckle and land a DROPKICK to the back of Simon's head!)
Ranks: "Look at the "Iceman" go! (While Simon stumbles around the ring, JC wraps him up into a small package!) Ranks: "1...2.....3! It's over! Diesel Power wins! "Iceman" JC took advantage of an injured AJ Simon and gets the pin!"
(Diesel Power starts to play, and although he didn't like the outcome of the match, that isn't going to stop Stevenson from doing the "Richie
Shuffle"! Ranks rolls his eyes as the camera goes to a commercial.)
__ ____ ____ \ \ / /\ \ __ / / \ \ \/ / \ \ / \ / / /\ \================================================== / /\ \ \ v v / __ \ January 3, 1997 /_/ \_\ \__/\__/__/ \__\ *Pre-Recorded* The Crippler vs. Riot Saturday Night Written by J. Knapik ============================================================================ Ranks: "Here we are at the final match of the evening, and it should prove to be a great one. We have Riot of the MAFIA set to go againstThe Crippler of DMW. Who do you think has the best chance to win here Rich?"
Stevenson: "The Crippler! Didn't you see how he dominated the last match?" Stewart: The next match is scheduled for ONE FALL with a TWENTY MINUTE TIME
LIMIT and is a FIRST ROUND MATCH in the UNITED STATES CHAMPIONSHIP
TOURNAMENT! First, currently in the ring at this time, represnting Dead Men
Walking...THE CRIPPLER!
(Boos abound from throughout the arena, as the Crippler stands with a cocky smirk on his face.)
Stewart: And HIS OPPONENT! REPRESENTING MAFIA! Weighing 245 POUNDS satnding
6 FEET 4 INCHES, hailing from DENVER, COLORADO...RIOT!
(Sirens sound through the arena, mixing in with the Godfather theme song. Riot bursts through the portal and runs to the ring amid a mixed reaction from the fans.) Ranks: "Both man looking very focused tonight, don't you agree, Rich?" Stevenson: "Especially the Crippler!" (Right as he says that, Rito drops The Crippler with a football tackle and begins landings lefts and rights to The Crippler's face!)
Ranks: "What intensity!" Stevenson: "ILLEGAL! RING THE BELL!" (Riot picks The Crippler up and throws him into the ropes, running at him and landing a clothesline, which knocks The Crippler over the top rope and out of the ring!)
Ranks: "The Crippler is getting rocked! AND NOW RIOT HAS A CHAIR!" (Riot smashes The Crippler over the head, denting the chair and sending blood flying!) Stevenson: "Riot now tosses back the Crippler into the ring then gets in himself! Oh man, The Crippler is hurtin'!"
(Riot grabs The Crippler OFF THE MAT BY HIS THROAT AND DELIVERS A CHOKE
SLAM! The ring shakes with the tremor!)
Ranks: "WHAT POWER! Riot knows he is in total contol of this one!" Stevenson: "It ain't over yet! OH NO! A PIN! 1...2....KICKOUT! This is too much... (Riot lifts The Crippler up and lands a hard DDT , spreading blood onto the mat. Riot then lifts him up again! INVERTED DDT!)
Ranks: "There is blood all over the mat, folks! This is carnage!Riot gives a "slit throat" signal with his thumb across his throat and the fans are loving it!"
Stevenson: "HOOBOY! NONONO! JACKASSDROP! AHHH! Ranks: RIOT SMASHES THE CRIPPLER WITH HIS FINISHER! 1....2....3!!! IT'S ALL OVER! Stevenson: "Nooooooo........" Ranks: We have Riot advancing to the semifinals! Thanks for tuning in ladies and gentlemen! We're all out of time!
(Riot stands over a bloody Crippler as the camera fades out.)
