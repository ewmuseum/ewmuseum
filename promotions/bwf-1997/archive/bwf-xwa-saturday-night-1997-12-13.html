XWA Saturday Night, 13 December 1997

13 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
__ ____ ____ \ \ / /\ \ __ / / \ \ \/ / \ \ / \ / / /\ \================================================== / /\ \ \ v v / __ \ Written by J. Williams /_/ \_\ \__/\__/__/ \__\ December 6, 1997 Saturday Night Introduction ============================================================================ (Fade into the large, luxurious XWA Coliseum. The ring is set up again and the fans are beginning to shuffle to their seats. The camera's focus onto the faces of Andrew Ranks and Rich Stevenson as they sit idlely at their announcing center. The looks of sadness, fear, and anger are on their faces as they are about to make a heartbreaking announcement.) Ranks: Ladies and Gentlemen, last Tuesday Night on Warfare, you saw the last match in the life of Robert Stewart, or to the fans, Deranged Stu. On his way home last Tuesday Night, he was in a fatal car accident and was finally diagnosed dead at 2:24 AM Wednesday Morning. Our prayers go out to his family and friends as this is certainly a hard time for them. Stevenson: It certainly casts a shadow on the night dedicated to AzteK. Ranks: Indeed. (The camera's cut to the Saturday Night Intro Sequence. Machinhead by Bush begins to blare through your Television as the Saturday Night opening starts. Random action shots of AzteK, Deranged Stu, and others are shown quickly. Their finishing movers are applied to helpless victims. Their brute force shown to the world for the first time. The XWA Logo then appears on the screen and the music begins to die away and the fans screaming is now heard. The logo suddenly blows up as the camera's return to a now darkened arena. It is now lit up by the loud, thunderous booms and lights of the state of the art pyrotechnics brought in by President Williams. The pyro finally begins to die off as the camera's cut from the fans to the XWA Announcer's Center where Andrew Ranks and Rich Stevenson sit calmly while the ruckus of the pyro was just behind them. Rich and Andrew are both wearing their respective grey suits with red ties. Rich has his wet hair tied back in a pony tail while Andrew's business man hair is perfectly parted to the side and gelled back. Andrew shuffles his papers a final time and begins to speak.) Ranks: (Calm Unusually) Hello Fans. This is XWA Saturday Night. I am Andrew Ranks and to my right is Rich Stevenson. Stevenson: (Energetic) Come on Ranks! Don't let the man get you down! We got a show to do. Tonight is the night dedicated to the biggest man in our sport, AzteK. Tonight in our main event, the team of The GodFather and AzteK take on The Killer's, AJ Simon and The Crippler. Ranks:(Silent) Stevenson: Dammit, Ranks! I can't do this broadcast alone. I know I am the best in the world but hell, I need help. If you aren't going to help, then get someone else out here. (Ranks silently stands and makes his way to the back. He walks slowly with his head drooped to the ground.) Stevenson: Dammit. Someone get out here to help me. (Machinehead begins to blare through the arena speakers. The fans rise in unison and begin to cheer and hold their creative and at times disgusting signs in the air. Out of the XWA portal walks the most important man in wrestling. The ShowStopper. Art Middleton! But, he does not make his way towards the announcer's center. He is heading straight towards the ring. On the way, he slaps hands with the cheering fans in the soldout XWA Coliseum. He walks up into the ring, through the ropes, and raises his hands in the air signaling the ShowStopper. The fans erupt at the sign. They are indeed behind him. He walks over to James Stewart and grabs a microphone.) ShowStopper: Well, well, well and WELL!!  X Dubaya ehhhhhhh...  The Showstappa is here!  (good pop)  Now.. Godfather.. you have your little Mafia and I just know those scum DMW low lives are in the back somewhere. So I am going to bring a little insurance to help the Showstopper work his way up fair n square.. so without further ado.  Ladies and gentlemen... ("Diesel Power" by Prodigy starts to play as the lights go out and white spotlights move slowly to the music through the arena) The team of "Ice Man" JC, and the seven foot wrecking machine, BDR...Big Daddy Rob! (All three walk out to the ring with a very confident look to a huge pop as fireworks go off) Stevenson: Oh My God! Just when you thought the XWA had seen it all, here comes "ShowStopper" Art Middleton with his rat pack. Alright. We will be right back after this commercial break with our first match of the evening! (The camera's cut to a commercial break. Stevenson is last seen shuffling his papers around and ordering someone to come out and help him with the broadcast.) __ ____ ____ \ \ / /\ \ __ / / \ \ \/ / \ \ / \ / / /\ \================================================== / /\ \ \ v v / __ \ US Title Tournament Matchup /_/ \_\ \__/\__/__/ \__\ Written by J. Williams Saturday Night Gene Hoffman vs. Art Middleton ============================================================================ (The camera's return from commercial with the sounds of Iron Man coming throughout the arena. Before the music starts GO <beep> Yourself is said. The Cowards surprised at what they heard Boo the MEGA Heel Gene Hoffman but a few remember the Face Gene and Clap.) Stevenson: Mixed Reaction from this partial crowd. Typical from such ignorant fools who could never live up to the status of "The Man with a thousand words!" (Breaathe by Prodigy begins to whale throughout the arena. Throughout the arena, the word "ShowStopper" is embalmed into the floor and the center of the ring. Art Middleton makes his way out of the entrance way alone. He begins his ascent towards the ring slapping hands with fans and strutting all the way down. He walks up the steps of the ring and into the ropes. Strobe lights begin to come on as he dances around the ring a final time.) Stevenson: What the hell was that? OK Guys. This is the first match of the first round of the XWA United States Title Tournament. (The XWA Title Tournament Graphic Flashes on the screen.) Stevenson: The Finals will take place at The Armageddon in January. Speaking of The Armageddon, why the hell don't you get Williams out here to help me!?! (In the ring, Gene Hoffman is giving the finger to Middleton who just laughs and makes gestures towards kissing his ass.) Stevenson: Oh isn't this great family programming! The bell has sounded and we are underway. Gene Hoffman starts the match with a kick to the groin area of Middleton. He falls immediately. Hoffman just laughs as the crowd begins a chant of...WHAT?!?!?!? (The arena becomes deafening with a chant of "Hoffman Sucks." Gene begins to spin around seeing all the yelling fans and then grabs the microphone to speak.) Hoffman: Shut the <BEEP> Up! Stevenson: There went our TV-PG Rating. Hoffman goes after Middleton again with an assault of kicks to the face. He picks him up, throws him into the ropes. Flying Crossbody by ShowStopper! That came out of nowhere! (As the action continues at ringside, a large hefty man in black and green tights makes his way out of the Portal and towards the announcing center. He has short blonde hair, a very muscular frame, and black tights with two green stripes going down the sides of the pants. He has a tattoo on his arm that proclaims "Rictor." Stevenson is still unaware that this giant man is coming towards him. The camera man finally points towards him and Rich turns towards the evil man. He is stunned! He tries to get up but Rictor throws him back to his seat.) Rictor: You sit there! Stevenson: (Frightened) Who are you? Rictor: I'm your cohost now you little <beep>. Stevenson: (Gasps) Rictor: Call the damn match. Stevenson: Yes, sir. Art Middleton takes down Hoffman with a snap-suplex. He goes to the top. Big Elbow Drop by Middleton. Rictor: What the hell kind of commentating is that? Put some emotion into it dammit. Stevenson: You don't have a big scary guy sitting next to you. Rictor: No <beep>. I am the big scary guy. Stevenson: Middleton goes back up again. He misses! Both men are down and not moving. Jimmy Jameson is initiating the ten count. He is up to 6 as Hoffman begins to stand. He is going for the Hoffman Hammer! He Got it! Rictor: What the <beep> kind of move is that? Stevenson: He goes for the pin. One! Two! Art kicked out! How did that punk kick out?!?!? Hoffman is angry. He goes for a DDT. Middleton counters it! Stunner! He applied a Stunner on Gene Hoffman. He goes for the pin. One! Two! Three! Art Middleton advances to the second round of the XWA United States Title Tournament! Rictor: That was the most dull, boring, and all out ignorant match that I have ever laid my eyes on. I should kick your ass right now for putting me through it. Stevenson: Hey you overgrown Hemroid! I didn't put you through anything. You better shut up before I... Rictor: You what:? Stevenson: Before I...(Silence) Rictor: Just what I though. Cut to a commercial dip<beep>. (The camera's cut to a commercial as the frightened Stevenson sits next to Rictor who is verbally abusing him.) __ ____ ____ \ \ / /\ \ __ / / \ \ \/ / \ \ / \ / / /\ \================================================== / /\ \ \ v v / __ \ Tag Team Grudge Match /_/ \_\ \__/\__/__/ \__\ Written by J. Williams Saturday Night AzteK vs. Deranged Stu ============================================================================ (The camera's return from commercial and focus on the faces of Rich Stevenson and Rictor. Both sit in their respective seats. Stevenson with a frightened look on his face and Rictor with a snear.) Stevenson: It is now time for the main event of the evening. Tonight is the last match in the XWA for the seven foot giant, AzteK. He has been here since this thing started and he is leaving now knowing that we can go on without him. Rictor: The only reason that AzteK is leaving is because he knows that I am here now and that I would kick the <beep> out of him. (Voodoo Chile begins to hum throughout the coliseum as DMW's very own Killers make their way out of the entrance portal. They are both wearing the cheesy "Suck It" T-Shirt.) Rictor: Suck It? Hmmm. Sound's familiar. Didn't someone up north do that? Stevenson: Good Call. Rictor: Shut the <beep> up. (Hey Man. Nice shot begins to play as the fans stand and erupt with Cheer. The lights go out and red and green lazers fly around the arena. Smoke begins to engulf the entrance portal. First, out walks the man known as The GodFather. The fans carrying Viva La Familia signs are waving them in unison. GodFather moves out of the way and out of the Portal comes the seven foot giant himself, AzteK!) Stevenson: Listen to this crowd! I haven't heard something this loud since I retired from the ring! (Both AzteK and GodFather make their way towards the ring where The Killer's wait. From out of the back runs KASH and he is carrying a baseball bat in hand.) Stevenson: Oh My God! He just knocked GodFather and AzteK in the back of the head with Baseball bats! They are both down. No! AzteK is up. He grabs KASH by the Throat! Chokeslam! KASH is out. Rictor: Here come the Killers! Damn! They leveled that big steroid. Stevenson: The GodFather is back up as five men are at the entrance way beating the hell out of each other. Rictor: AzteK has The Crippler and he throws him into the ring. The bell finally sounds and this match is officially underway. Stevenson: It ain't going to last too long. AzteK Drop! AzteK goes for the pin! One. Two! What the?!?!? (The lights in the arena go out. It is total darkness and silence is eary.) Rictor: What the <beep> is going on?? Stevenson: I am not sure. They are back on and AzteK and AJ Simon are out cold! AzteK has his arm on AJ though. One! Two! Three! AzteK gets the tainted victory somewhat. Rictor: Hey dumass. Look out there. The GodFather is out and The Crippler and KASH are knocked too. Stevenson: Total Chaos. Fan's that's it for us. GoodNight! (The camera's cut to the credits as the show ends.)
