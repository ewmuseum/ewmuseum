XWA Tuesday Night Warfare, 9 December 1997

9 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
__ ____ ____ \ \ / /\ \ __ / / \ \ \/ / \ \ / \ / / /\ \================================================== / /\ \ \ v v / __ \ December 9, 1997 /_/ \_\ \__/\__/__/ \__\ Written by J. Knapik Tuesday Warfare Introduction ============================================================================ Voice of Andrew Ranks: Last Saturday, in front of millions of viewers tuning in to the inaugural XWA event, KASH did the unthinkable... (Scene opens revealing KASH grabbing the MAFIA contract from The Godfather.) KASH: Can you turn around so I can use your back to sign this thing? Godfather: You got it. (KASH pull sout a bat as the Godfather turns around. Ranks yells "Oh my!" KASH taps Godfather on the shoulder and hits him squarely in the jaw with the bat.) Voice of Ranks: That night, the relationship between the two former friends had been severed forever. (KASH opens his jacket revealing a DMW T-shirt. Camera fades slowly to black.) Voice of Ranks: Tonight, they are in the XWA Coliseum together and only imagination can foretell what could happen. Welcome ladies and gentlemen to TUESDAY NIGHT WARFARE!!! (The scene BURSTS to life with shots of various XWA wrestlers at there best. The Godfather executing a perfect vertical suplex...Aztek flexing for the camera...KASH nailing Godfather with his bat...Riot punching wildly at his opponent...Gene Hoffman standing victorious in a ring, covered in sweat. The neon Tuesday Night Warfare sign flashes in between the shots until the last one comes and the Warfare logo stands alone.With a blast of fireworks in the arena the camera pans over the sellout crowd screaming wildly and lifting signs saying things life "AZTEK DROP!" ,"MAFIA FOREVER", and "Deranged Stu for President!". The scene switches to reveal Andrew Ranks, Rich Stevenson, and rookie commentator and wrestler Brett Rose[wearing his purple mask with a black suit] conversing among themselves. They turn and face the camera in unison.) Ranks: This is where the big boys play! The eXtreme wrestling association has a great show tonight. There really is no main event tonight, since every match is among the best in the XWA. Rose: First up, we got the phenom Riot squaring up against one of the all time greats, Gene Hoffman. Stevenson: This kid Riot is arrogant and has no respect for anything...what a guy! Riot faces The Crippler in the U.S. Tournament and I believe he could win it all! Ranks: Well I think he may be overstating Hoffman's age. He is by no means a dinosaur and has everything it takes to become XWA U.S. Champion Rose: Riot is going to get his (bleep) kicked tonight. (Ranks whispers something to Brett.) Rose: Well what CAN I say? Can I say (bleep)? (Ranks shakes his head "no".) Rose: What the (bleep) is this??? (Ranks rolls his eyes.) Ranks: Also tonight, Art Middleton takes on the number one contender to the XWA title, The Godfather. The Godfather has seen moving his jaw awkwardly and reports are in that doctors do not want him to fight tonight on account that he may further injure himself. We'll talk with The Informer about this later on tonight. Rose: I have a hard time believing that The Godfather will let anything stop him from being in the ring tonight. Stevenson: That KASH Moron is DEAD! The Godfather has MAFIA here tonight, so who's gonna stop them from KILLING KASH? Ranks: Well KASH does fight Deranged Stu in our last match of the evening, and in the XWA, nothing is unbelievable. Let's go to the ring for our first match of the evening! __ ____ ____ \ \ / /\ \ __ / / \ \ \/ / \ \ / \ / / /\ \================================================== / /\ \ \ v v / __ \ Standard 1 Fall Matchup /_/ \_\ \__/\__/__/ \__\ Written by J. Williams Tuesday Warfare Riot vs. Gene Hoffman ============================================================================ Stewart: The following contest is sceduled for ONE FALL and has a 20 minute time limit. First, making his way to the ring at this tiime, at 6'4" 245 POUNDS, hailing from DENVER, COLORADO...RIOT!!! (No music accompanies Riot, as he arrogantly walks down to ringside. Fans are booing all around. One fan sticks a Gene Hoffman poster in front of his face and he tears it to pieces with a grin. He hops up onto the ring apron and steps through the ropes, hopping around with smile.) Ranks: What an ego! Stevenson: NEXT UNITED STATES CHAMPION! Riot is THE MAN! If he can't... (The words "Go <bleep> yourselves" comes from out of nowhere while Iron Man by Black Sabbath blares over the P.A. system , interrupting Rich.) Rose: he can say (bleep), but I can't? Stewart: AND HIS OPPONENT! THE LEGEND! GENE HOFFMAN!!! (The crowd boos him almost as fiercely as they booed Riot. Gene jogs to the ring, flipping off fans along the way.) Ranks: I miss the old Gene Hoffman. Stevenson: You probably miss "Hee Haw" too. (Gene steps into the ring, and Riot attacks him before the bell! Riot delivers a series of kicks to Hoffman's lower back as he goes down to the mat.) Rose: Riot is showing some intensity here. (Riot grabs Hoffman by the hair and delivers a knee to the midsection, sending Hoffman into th turnbuckle. Riot then goes up to the second turnbuckle above him, yelling to the crowd. A barrage of boos follow.) Ranks: If you are a fan, who do you go for here? Neither man likes the fans and the feling is most certainly mutual. Stevenson: Riot swings his fist down hard at Hoffman, but Gene slides underneath his legs and is now behind him! (Hoffman picks Riot off the turnbuckle and performs a devastating BACK SUPLEX!) Rose: What a move!!! Riot is holding his back in pain! Ranks: a rookie mistake by Riot countered by an experienced move by Hoffman. (Hoffman lifts Riot over his head and drop him THROAT FIRST on the top rope!!!) Stevenson: THAT WAS GREAT! The old Hoffman would never do that, Andy! Ranks: It's Andrew, but you are right, that would have never been done by the old Hoffman! Rose: Riot now standing to his feet, holding his throat. (Hoffman runs against the ropes and collides with a MONSTER CLOTHESLINE TO THE THROAT OF RIOT! Hoffman walks around Riot and smiles at him. Looking at the crowd, Hoffman lifts him off his feet using his hair.) Ranks: Referee Ronald Johnson warning Hoffman, but he isn't listening. (Hoffman runs against the ropes again, this time hoping to catch Riot with an elbow, but Riot kneels to his feet and RACKS Hoffman with a punh to the groin!) Stevenson: HAHAHA! This is great! A very well executed move by the next U.S. Champion! Rose: That was more of a coward's way out of getting hurt. Stevenson: OH? And what would you have done , o' wrestling master? Rose: Lots of things. Stevenson: Good answer! HAHAHA! (Riot lifts up Hoffman and delivers a perfect BACKBREAKER! Hoffman rolls out of the ring.) Ranks: Hoffman taking some time to recuperate. Very smart thinking by Gene Hoffman. Stevenson: He won't have much time to rest though! Riot is going outside the ring as well! (Riot grabs a chair and swings wildy at Hoffman but he ducks and grabs the chair. He sets it down quickly, then smashes Riot with a left uppercut!) Rose: Now that was nice! Riot is out cold! Ranks:Ronald Johnson counts! 1..2..3..4..5 (Riot slowly gets to his feet) Ranks:6......7.......8 (Riot gets to the apron in time.) Stevenson: Very close! GO RIOT! Rose: Well I think Hoffman knows it's time to finish things up! He goes to the tope rope! (Riot is just lying on the ground! Hoffman lifts his hands then FLYS off the turnbuckle with a flying legdrop!!! ) Ranks: Daredevil move! The COUNT! 1....2....3!!!! Stewart: YOUR WINNER! GENE HOFFMAN!!! Rose: Great match! This Gene Hoffman is impressive! Ranks: Most definitely! Next Saturday he fights Art Middleton in first round of the United States championship tournament. And we'll see Art Middleton in action right after we return! (Camera goes to a commercial showing off the latest XWA T-shirt! Deranged Stu's GET DERANGED T-shirt is sure to be a big seller!) __ ____ ____ \ \ / /\ \ __ / / \ \ \/ / \ \ / \ / / /\ \================================================== / /\ \ \ v v / __ \ Standard 1 Fall Matchup /_/ \_\ \__/\__/__/ \__\ Written by J. Knapik Tuesday Warfare Interview with Dr. Dike ============================================================================ Ranks: Welcome back, folks! Tuesday Night Warfare is in full effect! Next, we have a exclusive report coming from the Informer. He has a hold of Dr. Eugene Dike... Stevenson: *snicker* Ranks: ...and will learn just how bad The Godfather's jaw is hurt. (Scene switches to show The Informer on the phone with someone as Rich Stevenson bursts out in laughter.) The Informer: Dr. Dike... (Stevenson is heard laughing.) Informer: ...tell the people just how injured The Godfather is. Dr. Dike: It is in my professional opinion that he is unable to perform for two weeks. The bruises to his face are extensive enough that I have asked him to refrain from competing until Christmas. Informer: Thank you for your time, Doctor. Dr.Dike: My pleasure. *click* Informer: Earlier, the doctor was telling me that The Godfather seemed cooperative and will sit out his match with Art Middleton tonight, his U.S. Tournament match with KASH, and possibly even his Christmas Chaos match. Back to you guys. (Scene shows the three commentators sitting at ringside thinking over the Informer's comments.) Ranks: Well, that is sad. KASH wanted to humiliate MAFIA and The Godfather pays the price. Now I'm wondering whowill be his replacement. Stevenson: The Godfather has something up his sleeve! Don't you worry! __ ____ ____ \ \ / /\ \ __ / / \ \ \/ / \ \ / \ / / /\ \================================================== / /\ \ \ v v / __ \ Standard 1 Fall Matchup /_/ \_\ \__/\__/__/ \__\ Written by J. Knapik Tuesday Warfare Art Middleton vs. ?????? ============================================================================ Stewart: This contest is scheduled for one fall and has a 20 MINUTE TIME LIMIT. First, already in the ring at this time, standing 6'2" and weighing 234 pounds, hailing from SWIFT CURRENT, SASKATCHEWAN in CANADA...ART MIDDLETON! (Art lifts his arms in the air and receives a positive response.) Stewart: and his opponent! Filling in for the injured GODFATHER..... at 6'11, 317 pounds! SAMMY..."THE BULL"...GRAVANO!!! (Sammy rushes to the ring, focused on the match before him.) Ranks: A big surprise here! I figured The Godfather would show up, but I guess I was wrong. (Just as he says that, the light goes out and a church bell starts to ring. The fans go nuts, knowing that the Godfather wasn't too far behind. After the twelfth ring, The Godfather appars in ring attire, not using the usual entrance the people love so much. Nevertheless, the fans throw roses and chant "Viva La Familia!" as he walks to the ring. When he arrives, he hugs Gravano, whispers in his ear and looks at Art Middleton.) Stevenson: WOW! I KNEW IT! (The Godfather rushes at Middleton with a FLYING CLOTHESLINE!. He quickly follows with a DDT!!!) Ranks: Look at him go! (The Godfather turns to the commentators, revealing the giant purple bruise on his cheek.) Rose: Ouch! This man has guts! Ranks: Yes, he does. Especially with KASH and DMW around. (The Godfather hoists the dazed Middleton up on the turnbuckle, follows him up and executes a BELLY-TO-BELLY SUPERPLEX!!) Ranks: Oh my! Stevenson: Godfather is taking it to him!!! He's going up woth Art Middleton again! He's setting him up!!!! Rose: THE HORSE'S HEAD! A GIANT SUPERPLEX OFF THE TURNBUCKLE AND ART MIDDLETON IS OUT COLD!!!! Ranks: Ronald Johnson counts.....1.....2.....3!!! Stewart: YOUR WINNER! THE GODFATHER!!! Ranks: Well so much for The Godfather sitting out! We'll goto a commercial, and when we get back, KASH goes up against Deranged Stu!!! (Stevenson remebers the name Dr.Dike and starts to laugh again. A commercial showing off DMW jackets appears.) __ ____ ____ \ \ / /\ \ __ / / \ \ \/ / \ \ / \ / / /\ \================================================== / /\ \ \ v v / __ \ Standard 1 Fall Matchup /_/ \_\ \__/\__/__/ \__\ Written by J. Knapik Tuesday Warfare $$$KASH$$$ vs. DeRaNgEd StU ============================================================================ Stewart: Next, THE MAIN EVENT! Scheduled for ONE FALL with a 20 MINUTE TIME LIMIT! First, at 6'3", 255 pounds, hailing from Dallas, Texas...DERANGED STU!!! (Song 2 by Blur blasts over the speakers. Stu walks straight down to the ring looking determined. Green fireworks and red lasers go off for him but he doesn't care. He slaps a few hands and yells at a couple of people. He then lays on the turnbuckle in the ring and waits for his opponent for the night, KASH.) Ranks: Stu looks to be in peak condition. I see a few GET DERANGED T-shirts in the crowd tonight. Stevenson: He's gonna kick some KASH butt. What he did to MAFIA was unforgivable. Stewart: And his opponent, REPRESNTING DMW...KASH!!!! (All lights go off and the crowd goes wild as a Green spot light shaped in a dollar sign is beamed at the center of the ring. Mo Money,Mo Problems by Puff Daddy and Mase starts to play as a spot light hits the entrance, KASH comes out and walks to the ring [The spot light follows him]. As he enters the ring the lights come back on and the crowd goes even crazier.) Ranks: The fans truly love this man! Rose: He showed a mean streak on Saturday with what he did to The Godfather. (Kash and Stu lock up as the bell rings.) Stevenson: WOO! HERE WE GO! (In the middle of the lock-up, Stu lifts KASH off of his feet and into a beautiful vertical suplex!) Ranks: Good show of power by the former disco fanatic! What's he setting him up for now? THE DERANGED DDT! He's got the head locked!NO< Kash picks him up and delivers an ATOMIC DROP TO STU! (Stu hops around the ring in pain.) Rose: He probably went for that too early. Stevenson: He won't be dancin' for a month! HAHAHA! (KASH executes a scopp slam, shaking the ring. An "OOOOH" is heard from the crowd. KASH grabs Stu by the throat!!!) Ranks: He's going for his patented CHOKE SLAM! (Stu turns the move into a spinning toe hold takedown!) Stevenson: WHAT A MOVE! Rose: That was a move worthy of myself! Stevenson: You couldn't do something like that in a million years! Rose: HOW ABOUT I DO IT ON YOU RIGHT NOW!!! Ranks: Men, please! (Stu has KASH in an armbar, twisting with all of his might.) Ranks: KASH appears to be in pain. Stu has a very evil look on his face! (Stu jumps up and wraps his legs around the hammerlocked arm, turning it into a arm-scissor hold. KASH yells out in pain.) Rose: The referee asks KASH if he wants to give up, but KASH says no. No way KASH is giving up here! Ranks: Neverthless, he is in pain. This could affect his chokeslam! Stevenson: Stu knows what he's doin' all right! (KASH reaches the rope and the hold is broken. Stu raises his hands and gets a good response from the fans.) Stevenson: Stu now going up high! (Stu gets up to the top rope and jumps off immediately in hopes of landing a double axhandle, but is met with a punch to the midsection by KASH.) Ranks: KASH, now standing, grabs Stu by the hair and THROWS HIM THE THE MAT HARD! Rose: Ronald Johnson did NOT like that! (KASH jumps over the rope to the apron. He uses the ropes to hurl him into the air and onto Stu with a GUILLOTINE LEG DROP!) Stevenson: NO! C'MON STU! Ranks: VERY IMPRESSIVE! (KASH goes up to the top rope and flip off with MONSTER MOONSAULT!) Rose: The big man gets some air time! He's in full control now! KASH goes for the pin...1...2..kickout! Stevenson: WOW! This is close! (KASH pulls Stu up and applies a full nelson to him! Suddenly the Godfather's theme music starts to play!!!) Ranks: Hooboy... (The Godfather followed by Sammy Gravano, Riot, and Distruct appear at the entrance! They start to move forward, but Commissioner Knapik appears with them!) Rose: The Commish ain't going to MAFIA ruin this match! (They are warned to stay and they do. They just stand at the ring entrance as Commissioner Knapik makes his way to the announcers table! Meanwhile KASH stare at MAFIA with hatred.) Ranks: Glad to have you with us, Commissioner! Knapik: Good to be here, Andrew. Stevenson: Why do you gotta ruin EVERYTHING? The fans wanna see a brawl!!! Knapik: This is the XWA, and the match will be finished WITHOUT interference or else MAFIA will be suspended! Ranks: KASH is still looking at MAFIA, shouting obscenities and making rather...unappropriate gestures! (Stu comes up behind KASH and rolls him up in a small package! 1...2....KASH reverses it! 1....2........3!!!) Stewart: Your winner.....KASH!!! Ranks: WOW! WHAT A MATCH! (From behind the entrance curtain AJ Simon, The Crippler, Dustin Buchanon, and Gene Hoffman blindisde MAFIA! ) Stevenson: THIS IS WARFARE BABY! WOO! (KASH tries to get to the fight but Stu grabs him and knocks him out with a DERANGED DDT!) Rose: KASH IS OUT COLD! (The brawl continues! AJ Simon KO's Sammy Gravano with THE SIMON STUNNER while Gene Hoffman and Riot fight into the crowd! The Godfather smashes The Crippler's head into the railing and Distruct takes out Dustn Buchanon with THE ELIMINATOR! Hoffman grabs some brass knuckles from his trunks and drives them into Riot's face. ) Ranks: THIS IS CHAOS! (Gene Hoffman and AJ Simon now stand against Distruct and The Godfather! Hoffman and Simon get taken out with a HUGE double clothesline, and THE GODFATHER TAKES OFF TOWARDS THE RING!!!! KASH stands up ! Godfather slides into the ring and KASH slides out! He is sprinting for the entrance but is met by DISTRUCT who grabs him and executes THE ELIMINATOR! KASH IS OUT AGAIN! Commissioner Knapik rises from his seat as a group of officials try to separate the Godfather from KASH, as he is now choking the life from him!) Ranks: Well this has been a crazy night! For Rich Stevenson, Brett "Blood" Rose, and the entire XWA, thanks for tuning into TUESDAY NIGHT WARFARE! (Camera fade to a commercial as The Godfather and Distruct leave the arena with their arms raised.)
