Mission Statement WVWVWVWVWVWVWVWV Ok, Yes, I know, there are a plethera o' feds out there and all claim to be the best and the hottest. We here at the AWL dont make any claims and we can't promise anything..well we can promise ONE thing and that is FUN
That is main reason for the AWL we have heard the complaints about e-wrestling "not being fun anymore." I disagree with that statement and have decided to prove that yes, ewrestling can be fun again. Having been around e-wrestling for close to 5 years and having joined many of feds I have seen the good, the bad, and the ugly that is out there. AWL will be a fed that takes the best of all that has been done before [see we dont claim to be origanl] and combine them as we strive to be the best at what we do while having fun and entertaining ourselves and each other
WVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVW 2. Who/What this thing? WVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVW The AWL is of course a fantasy wrestling fed which is made up of origanl hard working roleplayers. The AWL is based in Washington DC. We don't claim to be the biggest, the best, the hottest, or this, that and the other
[Although we will try to be all those things]. The AWL is open to anybody [Well almost anybody] and everything. If you want to get "extreme" we welcome that if you want to have an old school wrestling classic then that is welcome as well. This pretty much means you can do and see whatever you want without fear of major censorship.
As I said earlier, the AWL is American in scope, we will do shows in places such as New York City, Los Angeles, Atlanta, Texas, and even our home in
Washington DC. Although based in the US there maybe overseas events in Tokyo,
Germany and the UK. Although as stated the AWL is primarily based in the nations capital DC. With the corporate office being just down the street from
The White House.
As we welcome all styles we welcome all types of wrestlers. The AWL is open to any type of wrestler...heavies, juniors, hardcores, tags, and yes even the woman...we've got divisions for all of them. you're character will have a place here in the AWL.
2.2~Who Started all this? The AWL was founded by Jax Jordan in 1999. Jax runs the show and pays all the bills. Who is Jax Jordan you may ask..until early 99 Jax was the president of
International Championship Wrestling when he resigned from his duties. During his reign as president of the ICW Jax was known by many as a complete and total asshole. Jax did what he wanted when he wanted and there was no stopping him.
After Jax resigned he sat backed watched, read, and laughed. He laughed as everybody tried to do the job he once did knowing nobody can do it like he did. As Jax moved from his hometown of Las Vegas to Washington DC his urge to control the wrestling world grew stronger..Jax taking the money he made as president of the ICW began to build a new corporation...THE AWL WAS BORN.
Jax who is in is only 27 years old plans to take the game of wrestling back to it's roots. Can he do so with his huge wallet and even bigger EGO?
WVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVW 3. What can I accomplish? WVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVW The AWL currently sanctions four titles championships. There may be more depending on how things grow.
3.1~AWL American Title The AWL World Title is the most valued prize in the fed. The man who holds it, holds the honor of being the hardest and best worker in the AWL at that time .
He also holds the burden of having everybody after his head. This belt MUST be defended monthly (this will be enforced). The champ is permitted and is encouraged to make more than one defense per month, however.
3.2~ AWL Television Title The AWL's secondary title. Holding this makes you the #1 contender to the World
Title (technically), and also a target for a lot of people.
3.3~ AWL American Tag Team Titles The tag team equivalent of the big gold. A lot of feds have given up on the tags, but as a fan of 80's WWF/NWA, I see the potential in good tag team feuds so we keep fighting to keep them alive. If you handle a tag that can't find a home, the AWL may be the place for you.
3.4~ AWL Womans Heavyweight Title Like the tag titles Woman wrestling has had it's up and downs..The AWL will work hard in giving the woman the spotlight they deserve..this belt is just one way we'll do that.
WVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVW 4. What Shows will there be? WVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVW 4.1~ Thursday Night "Between Heaven and Hell" Hosted weekly by Colin Manning and Mike Matthews this is the showcase card for the AWL. This is where the really big matches take place and where the storys will be told. Usually every match is in a full play- by-play format ranging from 6-8 matches and lasting two hours. Every Thursday night wil be an evening you will not want to miss.
4.2~Wednesday Night Inside Edition This will be a news magazine type of show which will preview the upcoming
Thursday nights show. This will also be the "flash show" where the majority of the week's RPs are seen.
4.3~ AWL Update Not really a show as much as a mailing. This'll include things like rankings, card lineups, injuries, roster, etc. Make sure you read this one weekly if you want to know what's going on. Mailed after Thursdays show..
4.4~AWL Road Rage With days off on Saturday, Sunday, and Tuesday off..This show will come out on
Tuesday..it will be basic results from house shows that took place over the weekend..also it will feature roleplays that are sent in...
4.5~ Supercards and PPVs The current plan for five PPVs during the year..They will be as follows January...In High Gear April...Sudden Impact August.. Summer Anarchy December..A Night to Remember 4.6~AWL Showcase This is a special "awards" show that will be sent out every Friday..it will feature match of the week, match of the week, roleplay of the week, as well as any other kind of awards the members fill like giving out..AWLs way of letting lose and having fun for the weekend.
4.7 Weekly Schedule In a dream world, this is the AWL's schedule. Of course expect some delays and such..I promise not that many.
Monday...Road Rage [weekend house show results and roleplays] Tuesday...Day off [work on roleplays strats, in-rings or just have fun] Wednesdays...AWL Inside Edition [news magazine, roleplays] Thursdays....Between Heaven and Hell and update Fridays...AWL Showcase [Award show, anything else] Saturday..Off day Sunday..Off day WVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVW 5. What I gotta do in this fed? WVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVW 5.1~Roleplays and In-Rings The key to success any any e-fed. You MUST RP in order to prevent being cut from the roster. There's no set requirement for amount of RPs, but dont expect to win or even get booked if you don't roleplay. The Roleplays will be sent out along with the Road Rage Report and Inside Edition. In-Rings will of course be placed on the weekly card "Between Heaven and Hell"
The Rules of Roleplays {Flashes] and In-Rings aren't many but do include.. - NO SWEARING: I'm pretty lenient on this one. Anything you'd hear on Raw including "asshole", "bitch", etc. is acceptable. "Shit", "Fuck", and other such colorful words, while fun to use, should be substituted with a (BLEEP).
- NO ATTACKS: Don't beat up other wrestlers/managers/referees/announcers/etc. without getting permission first. - KAYFABE: Don't mention "real" wrestling. I, and other members of the AWL, don't want to hear about Steve Austin on an AWL card, so don't do it. Also, try and keep mentions of other e-w feds to a minimum unless it pertains to the topic. If you're facing Jack Daniels, and you kicked his ass before in
FFWA...fine, that's acceptable. but if you're facing Jack Daniels, and you decide to mention that "Funboy" Dick Leaky is a dirty, egotistical cocksucker
(while it may be true), it's not appropriate. If you really feel the need to do it, let me know and I'll see what I can do.
- FORMAT: I don't really care too much on this one. Correct spelling is appreciated. Some form of grammar and sentence structure. Punctuation is nice. Things like that I appreciate...not whether or not you do onespace or two after you character's name.
- LIMITS: Generally, you get 2 RPs per week when you have a match that count for booking. You can do one for Road Rage and one for Inside Edition. Also some type of locker room prematch piece or in-ring [more on in-rings follow] on
Heaven and Hell. If you don't have a match, I ask that you try to keep your RPs to one a week, unless you really need an in-ring. In-rings are attempted to kept down in number, but usually a good bet is however many matches we have, that's how many in-rings we'll have. If you want to make sure you get a spot, let me know ahead of time you'll be needing one.
5.2~ Strats Strats are another important thing in the AWL. Will it replace a RP in booking weight? No. But it defintely can be the difference maker in a close match. I don't expect strats with counter for every move, but a good general offense, defense, mindset, spots, and finish are worlds of help for the writers. A good strat format should look like this...
Wrestler Name: [who is this strat for?] Match-up: [who v/s who?] Match Date: [date it takes place] Main Gameplan: [this is what will be the main thing to happen during the match...your main focus]
Special Moves: [moves they'll use, counter, execute, etc.] If They Win/If They Lose: [what will happen? how will they react?] Extras: [anything else you'd like to include that we didn't mention] 5.3~ Angles The backbone to any fed is good angles. Without them, you've just got a bunch of imaginary guys rolling around on a imaginary mat. We want to know why your guy wants to win, why he hates his opponent, why he wears an eyepatch in the ring, why he freaks out when his opponent goes after his knee. It's stuff like that that makes people enjoy your character's work, and makes them want to see more.
Angles aren't "required", but without them, I believe you'll find the AWL a very hard place to get noticed in not to mention a boring place to be. If you need help coming up with an angle just ask and I'll be more then happy to get you started.
WVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVW 6. cast of characters WVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVW The guys behind the mic, in the back, in the striped shirt...all the characters you'll get used to seeing weekly.
6.1. Announcers The voices that call the action each and every week. 6.1.1. Colin Manning Jon's been in the wrestling business since the early years, so he's a fountain of knowledge and AWL history. He's the veteran of the broadcast team, the one who calls it is. Colin doesnt take favorites and doesn't back down on his views..
6.1.2. "The Man" Mike Matthews A former World Champion, although which fed is unknown for sure. Mike is an egotistical jerk, an attitude that got him quite the ass kickings during his wrestling career. Colin always claims Mike was the jobber under a mask not a real champion. Also very wealthy if you buy into his "All the looks..All the
Money" gimmick.
6.2~ Interviewers If you do an "on set" RP, you'll want to know these guys. 6.2.1. Jason Jordan The 18 year old younger brother of president Jax Jordan. Is training to be a wrestler but for know is the AWL's on-the-spot reporter as he covers things behind the scenes during the shows. He is also the host of Road Rage and co-host of Inside Edition with Allyson Jefferson. Unlike his brother Jax doesnt have an ego.
6.2.2. Allyson Jefferson The self claimed "First Lady" of the AWL. fired from a local news station co-anchor postion many believe Allyson was hired by Jax due to their "social relationship." A relationship Allyson and Jax dont admit to. Allyson does the in-ring interviews and is th co-host of Inside Edition.
6.3~ Ringside Crew The guys near, in, and around the ring. 6.3.1~ Todd Blackstone The ring announcer for AWL. Graham's as straight laced as you can get...no stumbling through cue cards, no accidently telling things to the crowd. The man knows how to do his job...Think Michael Buffer
6.3.2~ Bob Known simply by one name..Bob is the head off AWL security and senior head offical..he is in charge of making sure the security and the referees do their job. He is the man dressed in black and always around the ring.
6.3.3~ The Job Squad The EMWC truly has no "official" jobbers, but we do have a few very near and dear to our hearts that are forced by Jax to "do the job"...a make the talent look good. They are also the first to hit the ring when a brawl brakes out..
6.4~Suits The guys who run the show 6.4.1 Jax Jordan The AWL President and CEO...The Boss. 6.6.2. Terry Alexander EMWC VP of Talent Relations...and Jax Jordans head flunky basically if any major crisis comes up, and The prez is occupied, Alexander will take over. He's also in charge of character development, marketing, etc.
