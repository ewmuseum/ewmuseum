A Washington DC-set Usenet e-fed whose September 1999 handbook laid out four championships and a full broadcast schedule under a self-billed 27-year-old president, before any wrestler had yet signed on. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

American Wrestling League's surviving record is its own Handbook v1.1, posted 19 September 1999, opening with a mock-newsreel video package moving from “Apple Pie, Baseball” through wars and unrest to declare “a New Revolution has begun.” It names its founder as Jax Jordan, a resigned former president of ICW remembered, in the handbook's own words, as “a complete and total asshole” during his reign there, who took the money he had made running that promotion to build this one instead, in Washington, DC.

The handbook set out two flagship weekly shows out of the nation's capital — Thursday Night “Between Heaven and Hell,” the full-card showcase hosted by Colin Manning and Mike Matthews, and a Wednesday preview magazine, Inside Edition — plus four championships: the World, Television, American Tag Team, and Women's Heavyweight titles.

Part of its own staff write-up carried over, uncorrected, from wherever its template came from: one passage describing its own jobbers still reads, “The EMWC truly has no 'official' jobbers,” the fed's own name left unswapped.

No roster, card or title reign otherwise survives; the handbook itself, dated 19 September 1999, is the last surviving document under this promotion's own name.
