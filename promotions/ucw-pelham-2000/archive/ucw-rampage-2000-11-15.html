UNIVERSAL CHAMPIONSHIP WRESTLING PRESENTS: THE SATURDAY EVENING RAMPAGE 11/4/2000 "THE FRIDAY SUPERFIGHTS #4" THE PELHAM CIVIC COMPLEX PELHAM, ALABAMA [VOICE OVER]: "Stay tuned next for Universal Championship Wrestling's "Saturday Evening Rampage" here on WABM-68 Birmingham!" With that, we are transported to the end of last week's program. We are in the studio with Charlie and Jerry...along with other guests...
[IRVING]: "Well fans...there you have it...we were set for a nice main event but Tony Powers wasn't going to have that. He quickly stole the win from
K.C. Titan and barely got out of there with his title reign in tact."
Suddenly, a voice comes out of the wings... [VOICE]: "Barely? I beg to differ!" The voice belongs to none other than The UCW Heavyweight Champion himself...'Platinum' Tony Powers. Powers is looking like a million bucks as he is decked out in a fine grey Armani suit with a matching silk shirt and tie. The UCW Heavyweight Title belt is in his grasp as well as he walks onto the set...
[MITCHELL]: "Gee Tony...I didn't think you'd have the stack to show your face after what K.C. put you through."
[POWERS]: "And what would that be? A yawner of a match that I had to...like you say...mercy kill?"
Jerry is not amused by that little statement... [POWERS]: "Face it guys...I was just toying with K.C. in that match. I could have ended it when it started. I'm Tony Powers...I can get away with things like that."
[IRVING]: "Even with that in mind...how confident are you that you can keep a hold of the title belt until 'Badlands' where you will in fact face Austin
Anderson and either David Smith and Steve Stone."
[POWERS]: "Look...we all know that I don't have to defend my belt before
'Badlands'...this little title match proved it. As for Anderson...you all saw me tear his ass apart at 'Fright Night'...and I would do the same thing to Smith...if he could beat Steve Stone...WHICH he won't."
Suddenly, out from the wings...none other than Austin Anderson, decked out in a pair of black Adidas pants and a black UCW T-Shirt, comes onto the set and confronts Powers...
[POWERS]: "Well look what the cat drug in." [ANDERSON]: "Funny...is that all you can think of Tony?" [POWERS]: "No but as strange as it sounds...I didn't want to insult you since I'm about ready to go out and party like there is no tommorow...something you couldn't possibly grasp."
[ANDERSON]: "Let's cut the bull Tony. I'm glad you're so confident that you can beat me at 'Badlands'. With that in mind...I want to make a little challenge for next week."
[POWERS]: "Oh this should be good." [ANDERSON]: "Damn right...next week...you get Clayton Gold on your side and meet myself along with K.C. Titan in a tag-team main event for next week's
Friday Superfights. What do you say?"
[POWERS]: "Myself and Clayton against you and Titan...two fools who I've already beaten. Hell...bring Titan on now...I'm feeling lucky!"
Without any warning...K.C. Titan, dressed in the same attire as Austin
Anderson, storms onto the set and CLOCKS Powers in the skull with a steel chair from behind. Jerry and Charlie are stunned by this action as Anderson high fives Titan. Suddenly, Kevin Harte and Axle O'Leary come onto the set as well...and they too are wearing the same attire as Anderson and Titan.
Anderson, though, continues to speak at the fallen champion... [ANDERSON]: "Feeling lucky now Tony? I didn't think so. You know...myself...K.C....Axle...and Kevin are tired of 'The Dynasty' running rough-shod over this organization. Tonight...when you feel that knot form on the back of your head...think of it as a gift from...THE UNTOUCHABLES!"
With that, Titan drops the steel chair down across Tony's head as all four members of The Untouchables high five each other and confidently stroll off the set while the camera fades into this week's edition of Rampage. We go into the studio where Charlie and Jerry are standing by for tonight's program. Tonight, Charlie is decked out in a dark grey business suit along with a red tie and a white shirt. Jerry is sporting a very sharp blue suit combo with a matching satin blue shirt. As the camera fades in, both men begin the program...
[IRVING]: "Good evening ladies and gentlemen and welcome to another edition of UCW's Saturday Evening Rampage! As you just saw...last week's program ended with a bang and the creation of YET ANOTHER group here in Universal
Championship Wrestling...'The Untouchables'...comprised of Axle O'Leary,
Kevin Harte, K.C. Titan, and the former UCW Heavyweight Champion, Austin
Anderson. And, in their first official act together...they made a definite impact as they laid the current champ, Tony Powers, out to dry."
