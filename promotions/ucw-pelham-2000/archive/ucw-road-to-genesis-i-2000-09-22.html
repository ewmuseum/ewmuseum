Universal Championship Wrestling Presents "Saturday Evening Rampage" 9/16/2000 The screen we are looking at is showing the logo for local Birmingham television network WABM-68. Suddenly, a voice over begins to speak...
[VOICE OVER]: "Stay tuned next for Universal Championship Wrestling's "Saturday Evening Rampage" here on WABM-68 Birmingham!" With that, the camera fades away from the logo for WABM-68 into a normal looking television studio. We see a small video screen in the background and, in front of it, sits a desk with a golden "UCW" logo. The same logo is being shown on the video screen as well. Sitting at the desk are two individuals. The man on the left is the smaller of the two. He is wearing a casual navy blue jacket, white shirt, and red tie. His brown hair is combed neatly and he is looking very professional. The man on the right, though, is a bit different. He is wearing a black jacket, a black silk tie, and a dark blue silk shirt. He has naturally blonde hair and looks like he is pretty built under his suit. Without any warning, though, the man on the left begins to speak...
[MAN]: "Good evening ladies and gentlemen and welcome to the first edition of Universal Championship Wrestling's "Saturday Evening Rampage." I'm your host for tonight, Charles Irving and, sitting beside me is my associate and former pro-wrestler himself..."Mr. Alabama" Jerry Mitchell."
[MITCHELL]: "That's right...Mr. Alabama himself is back in Birmingham and heading the charge of the newest promotion here in the Magic City. Although
Charlie...you should tell the people that this is acutally my show and not yours."
[IRVING]: "Whatever you say Jerry. Fans, like Mr. Mitchell just said...we are bringing you the newest wrestling promotion to hit the great state of
Alabama and namely the Magic City. Universal Championship Wrestling, the brainchild of Birmingham businessman Daniel Sebastian Scott, had it's first ever show last night at The Pelham Civic Complex in Pelham, Alabama. The
Civic Complex will be UCW's homebase while this show, The Saturday Evening
Rampage on WABM-68, will come to you every Saturday night to recap what happened the night before in Pelham."
[MITCHELL]: "Like Chuck said, last night was our first show out in Pelham but it really wasn't our big kick-off of things. It was more so a warm-up to our big inagural show..."Genesis" which will come to you on September
29th from The Pelham Civic Complex."
[IRVING]: "That's right...for the next two weeks, 16 men will try and be one of the final four individuals who will go at it on Genesis to determine who will wear the first ever UCW Heavyweight Championship. Last night, we narrowed it down to 8 men. In addition to that, we had our first qualifying tag-team for the UCW Tag-Team Championship Match that will also be held at
Genesis. Fans, we had 9 big matches last night for the first show out in
Pelham. Right now, turn your attention to The Uni-Vision as we will recap the highlights of each match last night in Pelham. To start, we will show you how we kicked off the festivities."
Suddenly, the camera zooms in on the video screen as the pre-taped footage begins to play. We are transported to a capacity crowd in The Pelham Civic
Complex. The whole crowd is cheering like mad as, in the ring, the ring announcer and another gentleman, who is decked out in a pair of khaki slacks and a "UCW" style golf-shirt, are standing by. We can identify one man as the ring announcer...

[SCOTT]: "Mr. Owens...if you would....let's start the first match!!"
