UCW's Magic City Mayhem [3/17/2001]

March 16, 2001, 12:00:00 AM

UCW's Magic City Mayhem: March 17th, 2001 Recapping: UCW's Friday Night Fury: March 16th, 2001 The Pelham Civic Complex Pelham, Alabama The screen we are looking at is showing the logo for local Birmingham television network WABM-68. Suddenly, a voice over begins to speak...
[VOICE OVER]: "Ladies and gentlemen...stay tuned for the return of Universal
Championship Wrestling on WABM-68!"
With that, the camera fades away from the logo and, for the first time in a long time, back into The Pelham Civic Complex where a capacity crowd is on hand. Standing in the ring is a never seen before character, and he is holding a microphone. He looks to be a middle-aged man with dark brown hair, average build, and it looks as if he is enjoying what he is seeing.
The man is decked out in a tan business suit as well. Suddenly, the man begins to speak...
[MAN]: "Ladies and gentlemen....it is my honor to welcome you back to The
Pelham Civic Complex for the return of UNIVERSAL CHAMPIONSHIP WRESTLING!!!!"
The rabid crowd in Pelham becomes electric at this announcement as the man in the ring continues his statement...
[MAN]: "I would first like to introduce myself to you all. My name is Aaron
Edwards and the officials at GCU Industries have tapped me the NEW
Commisioner of Universal Championship Wrestling." The crowd begins to die down a bit as the man continues his statement... [EDWARDS]: "Like I said, it is my honor to welcome you back to The Pelham
Civic Complex for the return of Universal Championship Wrestling. Before we get started with our action, there are a couple of things I'd like to run down. First off... as of tonight...the UCW Heavyweight, Southern
Heavyweight, and Tag-Team Titles have been vacated! To top that off, Universal Championship Wrestling has retired the UCW Heavyweight and Tag-Team Titles." The crowd looks a bit stunned after Mr. Edwards makes this announcement... [EDWARDS]: "Now don't worry. UCW officials, including myself, decided to change the title structure a bit. From now on, the UCW Southern Heavyweight
Title and Southern Tag-Team Titles will be the top crowns in the organization. In addition, UCW will also debut the new Magic City Title.
Now to fill these belts, UCW pooled its remaining roster and came up with two seperate tournaments...one for the Southern Heavyweight Title and one for the Southern Tag-Team Titles. The Magic City Title will be filled in at a later date. However, starting tonight, the road to the Southern
Heavyweight and Tag-Team Titles will begin and it will not end until March
30th, 2001 when UCW offiicially has its....RESURRECTION!"
The crowd errupts after Mr. Edwards makes this statememnt... [EDWARDS]: "And just to let you know...I am nothing like the old management that ran this organization. I would like to tell you know that, in order to prevent anything like before happening again, we have decided to FORCEFULLY break up the team of Tony Powers, Steve Stone, Rory Blaze, and Clayton
Gold...collectively known as 'The Dynasty'. In addition, I would like to state that tonight's main event will be a tournament match...and it will feature Tony Powers battling 'The American Psycho' Perry Vaughn! Powers, I hope you brought your working boots because, from now on, you're going to need them. With that said and done, ladies and gentlemen, I would like to thank you all for coming and boy do we have a show for you!"
Suddenly, the screen fades away from The Pelham Civic Complex and into the
UCW Studios at WABM-68 for the first time in a long time! The studios have been redecorated with a bit of a festive flare. The big oak broadcast desk is still there but, in the center, a mold of the state of Alabama with a star marking the city of Birmingham is present. At the base of the desk, balloons and confetti are present to emphasize the celebration that the league is conducting. In the background, the video screen is still present and the gold UCW logo is being shown at the present moment. However, a new addition is present as the state flag of Alabama is beneath the UCW lettering. The background has been changed to black so the gold is being brought out more so than before. Luckily, our two announce friends are back as well. Charlie and Jerry Irving are standing by for the restart of this promotion. Tonight, both men are decked out in fine black tuxedos for this event. And, with that, Charlie Irving gets the go-ahead and formally restarts our broadcast...

CHAMPIONSHIP WRESTLING! I AM CHARLIE IRVING AND FOR JERRY MITCHELL, LET ME
WELCOME YOU ALL TO THE DEBUT BROADCAST OF UCW'S MAGIC CITY MAYHEM!"

Mr. Scott tried to skim off the top...UCW was not going to be put down.
Thankfully, the good people at GCU Industries stepped in and saw the connection that was being made with the fans and this promotion. Now, we're back on the air and destined to be better than we were before."
[IRVING]: "Fans, as you saw at the top of the program, our new commisioner,
Aaron Edwards, immediately laid down the law with a couple of huge announcements. The first one had to do with the UCW title situation and we'll get to that in a moment. However, the other big news announced was the forced break-up of 'The Dynasty'."
[MITCHELL]: "And for those who didn't follow the behind the scenes story with 'The Dynasty', you missed out on the fact that it was MR. SCOTT himself who was financing 'The Dynasty'. If you remember, Terry Price was trying to tell us something and, well, that is what it was. However, those days are behind us and now Tony, Clayton, Steve, and Rory are going to have to go their seperate ways or be forced to deal with a management group that is none to sympathetic to the problems that 'The Dynasty' caused."
[IRVING]: "Interestingly enough, Mr. Edwards made no statement concerning the other groups of UCW...such as 'The Syndicate' and 'The Untouchables'.
Apparently, Edwards is wasting no time in laying the law down in this organization." [MITCHELL]: "None at all and that is definetly a good thing for UCW's future." [IRVING]: "Speaking of UCW's future, we've got some champions that need to be crowned. Mr. Edwards made the statement last night concerning the elevation of the UCW Southern Heavyweight Title and Tag-Team Titles to top status in the league and he also announced the two tournaments that would be conducted to determine the new champions. Well fans, if you remember what happened before 'Genesis', the same thing will happen now. Tonight, we're going to show you the opening eight matches of the Southern Heavyweight
Title tournament and one of the two matches in the UCW Southern Tag-Team
Title Tournament. Then, next week...we'll continue the tournament up until
March 30th when UCW has it's 'Ressurection'."

'The American Psycho' Perry Vaughn. It's going to be a LONG ride for Tony
Powers with this new management team."

Anderson will go up against Luke Damien and Axle O'Leary will hook it up
with Lance Reeves. Now, before we get to the action, we would like to send our best wishes to the UCW talent who did not want to be a part of the organization again. We lost guys like Kevin Harte, Stone Mountain, Larry

Well fans, I think you would agree that we have done enough talking already. I know Jerry and myself are ready...and I hope you all are too...because it's now time to get back to The Pelham Civic Complex once again for UCW action! Our first match of the tournament, like we said, will feature none other than the first UCW Heavyweight Champion, Austin Anderson, going up against 'The Fallen Angel' Luke Damien! Here we go!"

For the third time, Powers picks Vaughn up and tries to kick his leg out from under him but, this time, Vaughn pushes Powers away! Once Powers comes back in at Vaughn, Perry catches him and snaps him to the mat with a modified powerslam!
[MITCHELL]: "They say the third time is a charm but I think the roles were reversed in that situation!"
[IRVING]: "And, like I said, Powers couldn't let Perry get to his feet. Now, I don't know if Tony can take control of this match once again because
Vaughn looks to be in an ill mood."
Now back in control, Vaughn takes his head-band off and immediately starts to choke Powers with it! The referee tries to stop Vaughn but Perry just ignores him as he pulls and squeezes at Tony's neck. Finally, Vaughn lets up long enough to bring Powers back to his feet. Vaughn immediately whips him into the ropes and, as Tony comes back, Vaughn lifts him up and spikes him back to mat with a vicious spinebuster!
[MITCHELL]: "Chiropractor needed at The Pelham Civic Complex for one Tony
Powers." [IRVING]: "Heh." Vaughn's eyes are, well, pretty vicious looking at this point...take our word for it. As he looks down at Powers squirming on the mat, a sick little smile forms on his face. He quickly picks Powers up by the hair, forces everyone in the arena to look at hi, and then strikes him in the face with a vicious closed fist!
[IRVING]: "Ok fans...I need to warn you now...that move drew blood on Powers as it nearly shattered his nose."
Vaughn acts like a shark once he sees the blood coming from Tony's nose. He is quick to pick Powers back up and he attempts to drill him with another stiff fist shot. Somehow, though, Powers ducks and, unfortunately, referee
Rick Hassle gets in the way! The move cold cocks Hassle but Vaughn really doesn't care as he rebounds by sending Tony to the mat with a lightning quick DDT!
[MITCHELL]: "A referee down in Universal Championship Wrestling....gee...things get interesting at this point don't they?" [IRVING]: "Naturally." All of a sudden, and almost as if on cue, the crowd errupts as Lance Reeves, armed with a steel chair, rushes down to the ringside area and jumps in the ring! As soon as Vaughn looks on at Reeves, he charges in at his bitter enemy but Reeves is one step ahead. He throws the chair at Vaughn and, as
Vaughn catches it, Reeves executes a superkick right on the chair...forcing it into Vaughn's face! Perry falls to his knees after the move but Reeves, for good measure, grabs the chair and cracks it upside Vaughn's head! The move knocks Perry out right in the middle of the ring. Reeves, before he goes, shakes Powers for some reason and then shakes the referee to wake him up. As Reeves leaves through the crowd, Powers slowly gets to his feet and sees that Vaughn isn't moving....
[MITCHELL]: "Tony Powers owes Lance Reeves his life...plain and simple!" Tony Powers knows a good oportunity when he sees it so he revives the referee himself before dropping a weak elbow across Perry's midsection...you know...to make it look like he did something. And, in the biggest show of arrogance, Powers lies across Vaughn...and acts like he hasn't broken a sweat. The referee, still dazed from the punch, makes the count...
[HASSLE]: "ONE.........TWO........THREE!" [IRVING]: "I can't believe it! Tony Powers, thanks to Lance Reeve's hatred towards Perry Vaughn, has advanced in the tournament even though Perry
Vaughn took him to the woodshed in this match."
[OWENS]: "LADIES AND GENTLEMEN...THE WINNER OF THE MATCH....'PLATINUM' TONY POWERS!" Powers gloats in the ring as the referee raises his hand in the air. However, he is quick to see that Vaughn is getting to his feet and, naturally, Powers high tails it out of the ring and back to the entrance area. In the ring, Vaughn has just been told that he has lost the match and he is about ready to kill the referee! Security, though, storms the ring to break up the attack. With that, the camera fades away from The Pelham Civic
Complex and back into the UCW Studios at WABM-68 where Charlie and Jerry are about ready to wrap things up...
[IRVING]: "Welcome back to the studio fans, and, for our first show back...it was definitely an exciting night. As you just saw, Tony Powers, thanks to a little indirect help from Lance Reeves, overcame the dominating force of Perry Vaughn to advance to next week's tournament matches.
However, Powers' troubles are just about to start because next week...he has to face his friend and former associate...Rory Blaze."
[MITCHELL]: "That's going to be a harder test than Perry Vaughn. What will both men do? They both want a shot at the Southern Heavyweight Title so things could get very interesting in this one."
[IRVING]: "And, speaking of two friends facing each other...our main event next week will be just that...we will show you the match between Axle
O'Leary and Austin Anderson. It will definitely be a clinic of wrestling skill and talent and I can't wait."
[MITCHELL]: "Neither can I...plus, in addition to those two matches, we're going to see Clayton Gold try and get past Akira Otagami and, then, Steve
Stone and David Smith, both former Southern Heavyweight Champions, will once again hook it up."
[IRVING]: "Plus fans, our other first round match in the UCW Southern Tag-Team title tournament will take place as The Electric Mayhem and The
Magic City Allstars will hook up. Plus, we will have three other matches that will be announced later in the week. Well Jerry, for our first show back...I think we did quite well..."
Suddenly, from out of no-where, none other than Tony Powers struts onto the set! He is wearing a nice looking dark blue Armani business suit with a platinum colored tie! However, he is also sporting a huge bandage on his head from where Perry Vaughn split his forehead open...
[POWERS]: "Come on now Charlie...you can't think that your performance tonight was that good?"
Irving and Mitchell are none too pleased about seeing Powers on the set... [MITCHELL]: "Gee Tony, I figured you'd be back in Atlanta hiding from Perry
Vaughn after what happened last night."
[POWERS]: "Hiding? Why would I hide from that punk? He showed me that he's all talk and no action. I was just toying with him last night."
[IRVING]: "Oh...we see...care to explain how you got that bandage on your head?" [POWERS]: "Charlie...you're the reporter...you figure out! Anyway...I just wanted to stop by and let you all know that there is nothing to worry about when Rory and myself have to hook it up in the tournament. He knows his place in this organization and I know mine. Everything will go off without a hitch."
[MITCHELL]: "He knows his place? Care to elaborate?" [POWERS]: "No...not really. You and he knows what it means. Gentlemen, I must leave you all know...women are calling and Tony needs to answer!"
And, just like that, Powers struts off the set and Charlie and Jerry actually look happy...
[MITCHELL]: "I think Vaughn killed about a couple of thousand of Tony's brain cells last night."
[IRVING]: "Ten thousand at least. Anyway...fans...we're out of time for tonight. However, we want to thank you all for tuning back into Universal
Championship Wrestling on WABM-68. We are sorry for the void in wrestling that we left in our down time but, now that we are back, UCW's Southern
Style of Perfection is here to stay and if you don't like it, well, too bad because we could care less."
Charlie and Jerry laugh at that statement a bit... [IRVING]: "Anyway fans, so long and see you next week when we bring to you another edition of UCW's Magic City Mayhem! Goodnight and so long from The
Magic City!"
And, with that, the house lights dim as Charlie and Jerry start to talk amongst each other. The show fades to black and we are done for tonight!
