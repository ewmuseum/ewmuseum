UCW — Card

Universal Championship Wrestling presents Southern Justice

May 20, 2001, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
************************** UCW's Southern Justice Friday, May 25th, 2001 The Pelham Civic Complex Pelham, Alabama ************************** 1. "Bilouxi Blues" Larry Cade, The Real Deal" Dustin Neal and "Rebel Rouser" Ricky McDaniel vs. "Superstar" Alec Ace, "The Fallen Angel" Luke Damien and "Mr. Bada Bing" Frankie King -In a match that will feature the future of Universal Championship Wrestling, The Mississippi Two, Cade and McDaniel, will team up with
Houston's Dustin Neal to battle two members of The Memphis Mafia,
Frankie King and the Mafia's leader, Alec Ace, along with Luke Damien.
Benny Goodman can and will be a factor in this one, but Cade, Neal, and McDaniel look to be up for the challenge.
2. "The Pacific Tiger" Ashitaka Takashi vs. "The Cobra" Marco Carlson -Marco Carlson goes into his toughest match to date in UCW as he steps in the ring with a true Japanese superstar, "The Pacific Tiger" Ashitaka Takashi. Takashi originally came to UCW with a beef against former rival Akira Otagami, but Marco Carlson has recently taken exception to Takashi's appearence. Look for this to be one of the stiffest matches in UCW history.
3. The Coast to Coast Connection(Tony Richmond and Chris Colton) vs. The Memphis Mafia(Sid Garret and Jackson McGraw) -The future of the tag-team division lies with the winner of this match. Both The Coast to Coast Connection and The Memphis Mafia have made excellent waves during their short tenure in UCW. With a win in this match, either could could be in line to make a serious threat at whoever holds the UCW Southern Tag-Team Titles at the end of Southern
Justice.
4. UCW Magic City Title Match: K.C. Titan vs. "The Northstar" vs. "Firestorm" Rory Blaze vs. "The Northstar" Lance Reeves(C) -K.C. Titan plans to play a spoiler in the ongoing war over the Magic City Title between Lance Reeves and Rory Blaze. Blaze originally won the belt at UCW's Supernova back in April, but then lost the belt to
Lance Reeves the following week. Now with the massive K.C. Titan in the mix, the title has a good chance of switching hands yet again.
5. Texas Death Match: "The American Psycho" Perry Vaughn vs. "Lonestar" Clayton Gold -In what looks to be the most violent match on the card, the unstable Perry Vaughn will finally get to meet his rival, Clayton Gold, in a one on one Texas Death Match. Last month at Supernova, "Dr. Doom"
Terry Price was added into this equation, and he cost Perry Vaughn a win. However, look for Vaughn to avenge that loss as he tries to make a serious run at the Southern Heavyweight Title.
6. The Syndicate(David Smith and Akira Otagami) vs. The Electric Mayhem(Raymond Kanyon and JaySon Rave) -Former allies collide as The Electric Mayhem, Rave and Kanyon, battle their former friends in The Syndicate, Smith and Otagami. The Syndicate dropped The Electric Mayhem at Resurrection in March, and then replaced them with The Dirty South. Now members of The
Instruments of Destruction, The Electric Mayhem look to gain revenge on Smith and Otagami, while trying to solidify another shot at the
Southern Tag-Team Titles.
7. No Disqualifications: "The Pinnacle" Austin Anderson vs. Axle O'Leary -Emotions will run high as the teacher meets the master in this match. For the first time, Austin Anderson will meet his former friend, Axle
O'Leary. O'Leary backstabbed Anderson back in March, and then alligned with The Platinum Club. Anderson soon went into a career funk, but broke out of it with a stellar performance in the tag-team match main event at Supernova as he teamed with K.C. Titan to beat
Axle and Tony Powers. Look for both men to throw caution into the wind and beat the holy hell out of each other in this one.
8. UCW Southern Tag-Team Title Match: Southern Comfort(Jim Jackson and Jack Daniels) vs. The Dirty South(B.J. Bad Boy and Jay-C-Jay)(C) -The ongoing war over the Southern Tag-Team titles continue at Southern Justice as Southern Comfort will once again meet The Dirty
South for the Southern Tag-Team Titles. The Dirty South shocked everyone by winning the tag-team titles at Resurrection, but then lost them to Southern Comfort at Supernova. A week later, The Dirty South stole the belts back, and now we come to Southern Justice. The last time these two teams met on a major show, Southern Comfort practically destroyed The Dirty South. It will be interesting to see if The Dirty
South can possibly hold on during the onslaught that Southern Comfort will bring.
9.UCW Southern Heavyweight Title Match: "Thunder and Lightning" Steve Stone vs. "Platinum" Tony Powers(C) -This is the match everyone has been waiting for. Since Resurrection, things have been building to the showdown between these two former allies. When Tony Powers created The Platinum Club, he decided not to include his former associate in The Dynasty, Steve Stone. As a result, Stone made it his point to extract revenge from Powers. After winning a guaranteed title shot at Supernova, Steve Stone officially made sure he would get Tony Powers at Southern Justice. However, on the last show before Southern Justice, Steve Stone might have opened
Pandora's box. Perry Vaughn was facing Tony Powers for the title, and he was inches away from winning the title. Steve Stone, though, prevented the title switch from happening so he could be the one to take the gold from Powers. By doing this, Steve Stone is now being hunted by Perry Vaughn, and this in fact could cost Steve Stone his one shot at getting revenge and the Southern Heavyweight Title from
Tony Powers.
