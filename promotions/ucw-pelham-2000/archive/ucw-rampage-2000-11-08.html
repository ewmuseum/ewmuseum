Press but Powers moves out of the way! The crowd gasps once more. As

Champion, Tony Powers, follows them and immediately, a hail of flash bulbs go off. We can also see a small bandage on his forehead but the huge grin on his face is off setting that. Anderson...AND...would you care to elaborate on this whole situation with
'The Dynasty' and Terry Price?"

Price is so freaking old that he was around when referees only counted to two instead of three. TITAN...decked out in a pair of black pants, black boots, and a white dress shirt, along with his hair still being in braids, stands up and the flash bulbs go off. K.C. Titan! As Titan backs up a bit, he looks out at the crowd before throwing his MASSIVE elbow right into Tony's jaw! Tony, clutching his jaw, stumbles out of the corner for a few steps before Titan gets a running start, comes up from behind on Powers, and catches him with a HUGE bulldog!
[IRVING]: "I know Titan's good...but I can't believe he is just dominating
Tony Powers like he is right now."
[MITCHELL]: "Powers might have gotten in over his head with this one." As the crowd continues to cheer for Titan, the big man waits for Powers to get to his feet after the bulldog. In what looks to be some type of spinebuster, Titan lifts Powers up but, before the big man drops the champion, Powers pokes him in the eye and he is immediately released from
K.C.'s grasp.

As the crowd errupts, we can actually see Tony Powers mouth something that can't be repeated here. Titan, though, immediately kicks Powers in the midsection, lifts him up by the side, and drops him with a MASSIVE and lightning quick powerbomb! [HASSLE]: "ONE.........TWO........." Powers, though, is able to get a foot on the rope and, thus, saves his title reign... [IRVING]: "Luck....that was about all that saved Tony Powers right there." Titan doesn't let the foot on the rope bit get to him as he quickly picks
Powers up and drives a forearm shot into his face for good measure. With
Powers stunned, Titan takes him and whips him into the ropes again. As
Powers comes back, Titan goes for a lariat but Powers ducks. As he bounces back for the second time, Titan turns around but his world is shattered as
Powers drives a dropkick right into Titan's right knee!"
[MITCHELL]: "Good lord! That's going to change everything right there. I have never seen Powers pull that move out."
[IRVING]: "Well he had to. His title reign depended on it." The move is effective as Titan breaks his offensive to clutch his right knee in pain. Powers, though, takes the time to get another breather before coming in and kicking Titan once more in the knee. This time, Titan falls to his left knee and it allows Powers to drive what looks to be a closed fist right into K.C.'s face. The entire crowd has shifted their reaction to nothing but boos as Powers continues to punch Titan in the face...
[MITCHELL]: "Come on ref...check those punches!" Powers breaks away from the slug-fest on Titan so he can pick his challenger up and chop him right across his massive midsection. With Titan stunned,
Powers kicks K.C. in the right knee AGAIN and, as Titan doubles over, Powers catches him wth a lightning quick DDT. The move buys Tony enough time to get to his feet again and take a serious breather before making his next move...
[IRVING]: "I must give credit where credit is due...Tony Powers knew exactly what to do in this situation. He's got the big man off of his feet and has gotten enough time to catch his breath and regroup. That is what makes a champion in an organization like Universal Championship Wrestling."
Still in control, Powers goes to pick Titan up and, when he does, he whips him into the ropes. On the comeback, Powers takes a brief running start and catches Titan in the mouth with a high knee-lift. However, Titan doesn't go down. As a result, Powers backs him up against the ropes again, chops him across the midsection, and sends him in the opposite direction once more.
This time, though, Powers catches Titan on the comeback with a drop toe-hold to get him on the mat. With Titan down, Powers quickly drops an elbow to the back of K.C.'s head before making his next move. Powers quickly turns
Titan over...and without any warning...begins to lock The Scorpion in on
K.C.! [MITCHELL]: "Can the champ finish off the big man right now? I don't think so!" As Tony struggles to lock The Scorpion in, the fight in Titan comes out and he struggles to resist. Eventually, the power in Titan's legs is able to push Tony off and back into the ropes. Tony, though, thinks on his feet and, when he comes back at K.C., he drops an elbow towards him. However,
Titan moves out of the way and Tony hits nothing but mat!
[IRVING]: "I think Tony might have just revived a very angry giant!" As Tony gets up from the missed elbow drop, he is met with a huge boot to the midsection from K.C. Titan. Once Tony doubles over, Titan grabs him and lifts him HIGH into the air with a huge vertical suplex. While Powers bounces off the mat, Titan backs himself up a bit, takes a nice little strut, and drops a HUGE elbow across Tony's midsection. From there...Titan goes for the pin and the title!
[HASSLE]: "ONE..........TWO........" Powers kicks out in the nick of time but that doesn't mean Titan is finished with him... [IRVING]: "Things aren't looking too good for Tony Powers in his first match as champion." [MITCHELL]: "And just think what will happen at 'Badlands' when he will have to face Austin Anderson AND either David Smith or fellow 'Dynasty' member,
Steve Stone."
With Titan still in control, he picks the champion up, executes a waistlock, and nearly tosses him out of the ring with an overhead belly to belly suplex! Once Titan gets up...he notices that Powers is right by the turnbuckle corner...how quaint!
[IRVING]: "It might be all over for Tony Powers right now. K.C. is about ready to detenotate The Titan Bomb and this crowd knows it!"

[MITCHELL]: "Can I get a count-down!" Titan begins the first portion of The Titan Bomb but, as he does so, Powers sneaks in a kick into K.C.'s midsection. [HASSLE]: "ONE......TWO.....THREE!" [MITCHELL]: "NO! NO! ARE THE REFEREES IN THIS ORGANIZATION BLIND!?" The crowd can't stand it but... [HASSLE]: "LADIES AND GENTLEMEN....THE WINNER OF THE MATCH AND STILL THE UCW HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION...'PLATINUM' TONY POWERS!" The crowd is irrate at the length of the match and the subsequent ending but
Powers could care less. He quickly rolls out of the ring, grabs his title belt, and high-tails it to the back before K.C. can realize what hit him.
In the ring, though, Titan is up and has just been informed of the ending of the match. Needless to say, Titan is not pleased at what has happened and he storms out of the ring and rushes to the back. Once this happens...the camera switches back to the studio...
[IRVING]: "Well fans...there you have it...we were set for a nice main event but Tony Powers wasn't going to have that. He quickly stole the win from
K.C. Titan and barely got out of there with his title reign in tact."

Powers is looking like a million bucks as he is decked out in a fine grey Armani suit with a matching silk shirt and tie. The UCW Heavyweight Title belt is in his grasp as well as he walks onto the set...
[MITCHELL]: "Gee Tony...I didn't think you'd have the stack to show your face after what K.C. put you through."
[POWERS]: "And what would that be? A yawner of a match that I had to...like you say...mercy kill?"
Jerry is not amused by that little statement... [POWERS]: "Face it guys...I was just toying with K.C. in that match. I could have ended it when it started. I'm Tony Powers...I can get away with things like that."

'Badlands'...this little title match proved it. As for Anderson...you all saw me tear his ass apart at 'Fright Night'...and I would do the same thing to Smith...if he could beat Steve Stone...WHICH he won't."
Suddenly, out from the wings...none other than Austin Anderson, decked out in a pair of black Adidas pants and a black UCW T-Shirt, comes onto the set and confronts Powers...
[POWERS]: "Well look what the cat drug in." [ANDERSON]: "Funny...is that all you can think of Tony?" [POWERS]: "No but as strange as it sounds...I didn't want to insult you since I'm about ready to go out and party like there is no tommorow...something you couldn't possibly grasp."
