A self-hosted Usenet e-fed whose only surviving broadcast crowns a new Heavyweight and Tag Team champion across a barbed-wire cage match, a hardcore weapons match, and a triangle title match. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

HCWF's only surviving broadcast is a self-hosted pay-per-view, “Hell On Earth,” staged 15 February 1999 at the “HCWF Arena” in Anchorage, Alaska, and billed sold out. Two of the fed's own three commentators, Rob Hall and Bobby Sharp, both had matches of their own on the same card, calling the action alongside Leon Gruff between their own segments.

The Hardcore Icons opened the show by beating the Boys Next Door in a rematch Texas Tornado tag match, and Monkey Killer followed with a No DQ, falls-count-anywhere weapons win over Apollo, then billed as the NeWA Canadian Champion, earning himself a future shot at that title with his own “E.B.O.L.A.” finisher.

A triangle match put the HCWF Tag Team Championship back on the Hardcore Kings when Dirk Cassidy pinned his own partner by accident, and a Loser-Leaves-HCWF stipulation forced onto a no-show Cereal Killer was absorbed instead by a late stand-in, Raven Micheals, who beat Mike Starr to keep Cereal Killer's spot on the roster.

Rob Hall beat Bobby Sharp in the undercard's Barbed Wire Cage Match with a Running Lygerbomb, and the main event turned on outside interference: Jim “The Dragon” Dalton won the HCWF Heavyweight Championship from Kurt Wolf after a run-in from Psycho Bob left both men laid out and a disguised referee counted the pin, closing with Buzz Tyler running in to demand a title shot of his own.

Whether Dalton ever answered that challenge, or what became of the promotion after this broadcast, isn't in the surviving record.
