HCWF — Pay-Per-View

Hell On Earth (part 1/3)

15 February 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Please let me now how you liked this event at [detail withheld] . Hell On Earth 2-14-99 ( The camera opens up outside the HCWF Arena in Anchorage, Alaska. The camera looks on the front of the building, where a sign reads "HCWF Hell On Earth:
SOLD OUT". It cuts inside, and scans across the audience, picking up some of their signs. Among them, "I'm a Daltonite" "It's past the Boys Next Door's
Bedtime" "Bobby Sharp is gonna kick Rob Hall's ass!" and "Dalton is the next
Champ!" )
( The camera cuts to the announcer's booth where Leon Gruff, Rob Hall, and
Bobby Sharp are sitting. )
LG: Hello fans, we've got a great card for you tonight! It's HCWF PPV, Hell On
Earth! RH: First, in a rematch from SNC, the HCWF's two newest tag teams square off when The Hardcore Icons face the Boys Next Door!
BS: Then, Apollo and Monkey Killer fight in a No DQ, Falls-Count-Anywhere
Weapons Match! LG: Feral and Mike Starr put the tag team titles on the line in a triangle match against the Hardcore Kings and Storm Lightning!
RH: Cereal Killer and Mike Starr put their careers on the line in a retirement match! BS: I beat Rob Hall in a Barbed Wire Cage Match! LG: And in the main event, Jim "The Dragon" Dalton faces Kurt Wolf for the HCWF
Heavyweight Title! Let's go to the ring!
Texas Tornado Match Boys Next Door Vs. Hardcore Icons w/ Todd Grene ( "God Gave Rock And Roll To You" by Petra starts to play, and the fans cheer as the Boys Next Door walk to the ring. Then the Hardcore Icon's theme starts up, and Doug Wynkoop and Monkey Killer walk to the ring, accompanied by Todd
Grene. The bell rings, and all four men start going at it. )
RH: All four men in the ring duking it out, and that's perfectly legal in a
Texas Tornado match. T.J. Johnson and Doug Wynkoop are battling on one side of the ring, and Monkey Killer is fighting Mark Smith on the other. Killer is dominating Smith by keeping him in the corner and not letting him have a chance to strike. MK lifts Smith up, and set him on the top turnbuckle. Meanwhile,
Johnson is having no trouble keeping Wynkoop right where he wants him. He comes off the ropes, and hits a spinning neckbreaker on the Hardcore King!
BS: Johnson sees his partner in trouble, and makes his way across the ring. He kicks Monkey Killer in the back of the knee! MK stumbles, but keeps himself standing by grabbing onto the ropes. He turns around, and elbows the 'Young
Lion' in the stomach. King runs up from behind and bulldogs Johnson! Smith is on the top, and he jumps off with a double axehandle! Monkey Killer catches him, and powerslams him.
LG: Wynkoop makes the cover on Smith. 1.....2... TJ Johnson breaks the cover. Monkey Killer hits a sidewalk slam on Johnson and goes for the pin. 1.....2... Smith breaks the pin! Monkey Killer takes a breather, and the Hardcore King hits a DDT on Smith! He puts his foot on Smith's chest, and tells the referee to count. Hold on, TJ Johnson crawls up behind Wynkoop and rolls him into a small package! 1.....2.....no! Wynkoop powers out.
BS: As Johnson pulls to his feet, Monkey Killer takes him back to the mat with a forearm shot! King lifts Johnson to his feet, and hoists him into the air for a brainbuster! Monkey Killer sets Smith up for a powerbomb, it looks like they're going for the Hardcore Move, their powerbomb/neckbreaker finisher. They are! King hits the neckbreaker as MK powerbombs Smith, and he holds it for a pin. 1.....2.....3!
Result: The Hardcore Icons defeated the Boys Next Door by pinfall in 9:42. LG: A great match by both teams, but the Hardcore Icons come away with the win. RH: Next, it's Apollo and Monkey Killer, and this one has been brewing for awhile. BS: Let's take a look at what's been building up between these two in the past weeks. ( Video package of Apollo/Monkey Killer airs. It includes MK defeating Apollo for the HCWF Intercontinental Championship, and their second match which MK also won. It ends with Apollo's challenge that if Monkey Killer wins, he will receive a shot at the NeWA Canadian Championship.
BS: Now that you know why they hate each other, watch them beat one another to a bloody pulp! Apollo Vs. Monkey Killer! No DQ, Weapons Match, Falls-Count-Anywhere Apollo NeWA Canadian Champion Vs. Monkey Killer w/ Todd Grene ( "Have A Cigar" by Primus begins, and Apollo makes his way to the ring with the NeWA Canadian Title over his shoulder, and a pair of brassknuckles in his hand. Then "Intergalactic" by the Beastie Boys starts up, and Monkey Killer walks to the ring carrying a trashcan filled with chair, and a baseball bat in his other hand. Todd Grene is carrying a breifcase filled with something, but you cannot tell what. The bell rings, and both men square off. )
BS: This is the knd of match I like! Two guys beating the crap out of each other with weapons for 15 minutes. One will emerge victorious, and the other, the loser, will be crying for his mom.
RH: Sort of like a preview of our match later tonight, eh? LG: Ahem, let's concentrate on the match at hand. Apollo sets the knuckles on the mat, and Monkey Killer sets the garbage can in the corner. He swings his bat at Apollo, who ducks under and hits a low blow on Monkey Killer! The big man bends over in pain, and Apollo pulls the bat out of his hands. He brings it down on the back of Monkey Killer's head, bringing him to his knees. He does it again, and MK falls onto the mat.
RH: Apollo tosses the bat out of the ring, and rolls to the outside. He looks under the ring, and pulls something out. It's a table with weapons on it! A baking sheet, a toliet seat, a chain, this is gonna cause Monkey Killer some pain!
BS: That was really corny. Anyway, Apollo dumps the weapons off the table, and sets it up near the apron. He slides into the ring, and Monkey Killer is to his feet. He whips Monkey Killer off the ropes, and attempts a clothesline. MK ducks it, and waits near the apron. Apollo comes off the ropes, but MK hits a backdrop and sends him to the outside! Through the table!
LG: Apollo holds his back, and MK slides out of the ring. He grabs the two baking sheets, and puts one in each hand. Apollo is on his knees, and Monkey
Killer claps the pans over his face! Apollo falls backwards. MK throws the dented sheets onto the aisle, and grabs the chain. He wraps it around Apollo's neck, and rolls him into the ring. MK throws Apollo over the top rope, he's strangling him with the chain! Apollo's eyes are popping out of his head! MK lets go, and then dumps the chairs out of the trashcan.
BS: Apollo is on the apron, trying to climb into the ring. MK puts the trash can over his head, and nails him with a chair! Apollo falls off the apron, and rolls across the mat. Monkey Killer folds up the two remainings chairs, putting one in the middle of the ring, and carrying one with him to the outside. Apollo pulls to his feet, and grabs the toliet seat as he does. Apollo dodges Monkey
Killer's chairshot, kicks him in the stomach, and suplexes him onto another chair! He wraps the seat around Monkey Killer's neck, and smashes his face into the ringpost. Apollo rolls MK into the ring, and follows behind him.
RH: Apollo prepares to DDT Monkey Killer onto the chair. MK counters with an atomic drop! He kicks Apollo in the stomach, and hits the E.B.O.L.A. onto the chair! He makes the cover, 1.....2.....3! Monkey Killer gets a shot at the NeWA
Canadian Championship!
Result: Monkey Killer pinned Apollo following the E.B.O.L.A. in 13:44. [detail withheld] Handler of Jake Rockwell. "They send one of yours to the hospital, you send one of theirs to the morgue." - Arn Anderson
