NeWA Canadian Championship.
BS: Bah, he got lucky. He'll never beat Apollo. LG: Now, we have a Triangle Match for the Tag Team Championship! ( Video clips airs from SNC when Feral and Mike Starr attacked the Hardcore
Kings and Storm Lightning during their match. )
LG: Let's see if Team Corporate can walk out of here with their titles! Tag Team Championship Triangle Match Hardcore Kings Vs. Storm Lightning w/ Fran Vs. Feral and Mike Starr (c) ( The Hardcore Kings theme starts up, and they walk to the ring. Then Storm
Lightning's music begins, and they come to the ring with Fran. Finally,
"Negative Creep" by Nirvana starts, and Feral and Mike Starr walk to the ring with the HCWF Tag Team Championship. )
RH: And it's Slade and Bolt to start things off. Bolt attempts a frankensteinr right off the bat, but Slade turns it into a small powerbomb. He holds it for a pin, 1.....2.. Feral walks through the ropes and kicks Slade off. Slade starts to argue with Feral, and Bolt lifts Slade onto his shoulders. He walks towards the corner where Sledge is standing, and Sledge hits a clotheslines knocking
Slade off Bolts' shoulders!
BS: Bolt pulls Slade up, and whips him into the corner. Slade tags in the person who was standing in that corner, and it's Mike Starr! Starr hits the ring and tackles Bolt. He lifts Bolt up, and hits an atomic drop! He knees Bolt in the face, and starts taunting Sledge. He shoves Bolt into that corner, where he tags in Sledge. Sledge climbs through the ropes, and Mike Starr catches him off guard with a snap suplex! He turns over and hooks the leg for a pin attempt. 1.....2... Feral hits the ring and breaks the cover.
LG: Sledge grabs Feral from behind as he walks towards the corner, and starts arguing with him. Mike Starr walks up behind Sledge and hits a German suplex!
He pulls Sledge up, whips him off the ropes, kicks him in the stomach, and lifts him into the air. Vertical suplex! He drops Sledge to the mat and goes for a cover. 1.....2... Dirk Cassidy breaks the pin with an elbow to the head of Mike Starr. Starr is now arguing with Cassidy and pushes him into the corner.
RH: Sledge shakes off the pain, and tags in Slade! Starr is still arguing with
Cassidy, and starting backing up. He bumps into Slade, and as soon as he turns around is nailed with a Flatliner! Slade covers Starr, 1.....2... Feral breaks the pin. As Feral is walking away, Slade comes up behind him and hits a legsweep! He starts stomping Feral's chest, and then he tags in Bolt. Feral and
Slade start to brawl, but the ref breaks it up.
LG: Sledge and Mike Starr are going at it. Both men come off the ropes, and they clothesline each other! They fall to the mat, and start crawling towards the nearest corners. Starr tags in Slade, and Sledge tags in... Dirk Cassidy!
Both members of the Hardcore Kings are in the ring! Feral and Bolt hit the ring to keep the match going. Slade DDT's Bolt and Cassidy hits a Flatliner on
Feral! Cassidy rolls Slade up into a small package, and the referee counts,
1.....2....3! Cassidy pinned his own teammate to win them the tag team championship!
Result: The Hardcore Kings defeated Storm Lightning and Feral and Mike Starr by pinfall in 17:18 to win the HCWF Tag Team Championship.
RH: The Hardcore Kings pull off another upset to win back the HCWF Tag Team
Titles! BS: Now, Cereal Killer is gone from the HCWF! LG: That could happen. It's Mike Starr and Cereal Ki... hold on a minute. ( Raven Micheals and Rebecca are backstage. They open the door to CK's dressing room, but they canot find him. )
RH: It looks like Cereal Killer isn't here! BS: Hold on, I've just gotten a message. Raven Micheals will take Cereal Killer's place! But if he loses, it's not him who is fired, but Cereal Killer. Let's go to the ring! Loser-Leaves-HCWF Raven Micheals w/ Rebecca Vs. Mike Starr ( "Heart-Shaped Box" by Nirvana plays as Raven Micheals and Rebecca make their way down to the ring. Then Mike Starr walks from the crowd, and nails Micheals from behind. )
RH: Starr decks Micheals from behind, and straps on a chinlock. Micheals strains to reach the ropes, but he can't make it. Starr pulls all the way back, he's going for a quick win in this match. He's probaly tired from the tag match and wants to get this over with as soon as possible.
BS: Micheals breaks free, and rolls onto his back. Starr grabs his legs, and applies a boston crab! Micheals kicks him off. Micheals pulls to his feet, but
Mike Starr quickly kicks him in the stomach and hits a spinning neckbreaker. He covers Micheals, 1.....2.... Micheals pushes him off. He pulls to his feet, and starts pounding on Mike Starr.
LG: Starr hits a low blow, and follows up with a snap suplex on Micheals. Rebecca is on the apron, yelling at Starr. Starr walks over, and pushes Rebecca off the apron! Starr turns around, and taunts Micheals. Rebecca pulls up by the apron, and pulls Starr's feet out from under him! Micheals walks over, and pulls Starr into the middle of the ring. He rolls Starr onto his back, and hits a kneedrop on Starr. He pulls Mike Starr to his feet, whips him against the ropes, and hits an elbow smash.
RH: Starr falls to the mat, and Micheals hits another kneedrop. He pulls Starr up, kicks him in the stomach, and piledrvies him to the mat. Feral is running down the aisle with a steel chair. He climbs onto the apron, and tosses the chair to Mike Starr. Starr swings at Micheals, who ducks under, hits a low blow on Starr, and hits a DDT! He covers Mike Starr as Feral hits the ring.
1.....2....3! Feral didn't make it in time! Raven Micheals wins! Result: Raven Micheals pinned Mike Starr following a DDT in 8:37. [detail withheld] Handler of Jake Rockwell. "They send one of yours to the hospital, you send one of theirs to the morgue." - Arn Anderson
