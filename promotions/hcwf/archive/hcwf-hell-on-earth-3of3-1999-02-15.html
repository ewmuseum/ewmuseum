Hell On Earth (part 3/3)

Please let me now how you liked this event at [detail withheld] . RH: Well Leon, Bobby and I need to go get ready for our match. LG: Joining me for commentary will be the head of the HCWF Championship Committee, Jack Fing. JF: Hellooo HCWF! LG: God, why... Barbed Wire Cage Match Rob Hall Vs. Bobby Sharp ( The HCWF Saturday Night Carnage theme plays, and Rob Hall walks to the ring wearing wrestling boots, jogging pants and a SNC T-shirt. The theme contiues to play as Bobby Sharp walks down to the ring wearing black boots, shorts, and a white shirt saying: "Rob is going down!" on it. The cage lowers, and Rob is so shocked he faints. )
JF: The shock of the falling cage made Rob Hall faint! That was... disappointing. I'm going to go get a jelly donut. (Headset drops.) LG: Bobby Sharp is turned around, telling the ref to raise his hand. Hold on,
Rob Hall is to his feet, and he hits a low blow on Bobby Sharp! He whips Bobby
Sharp towards the barbed wire! Bobby falls to the mat to avoid getting cut. Rob
Hall pulls Bobby Sharp up, and does an eye poke! And a... nose pull? Bobby
Sharp pushes Rob Hall away. Hall backs up, and gets poked by the barbed wire!
He cringes and looks at his arm. Bobby Sharp walks over, kicks Rob Hall in the stomach, and hits a piledriver! Jack Fing is back.
JF: What the hell happened? LG: Rob Hall was playing possum and sneak attacked Bobby Sharp. JF: Well it looks like Rob Hall is out now. Bobby Sharp makes the cover, 1.....2... Rob Hall kicks out! Bobby Sharp... hey I just noticed something. LG: What? JF: Bobby Sharp's initials are BS! LG: I've known that since the first event. JF: (Laughing) But isn't that just the funnniest thing you ever heard? LG: It was when I heard it four months ago. Anyway let's get back to the match. Bobby Sharp hits another piledriver on Rob Hall. He kisses his hand and starts... um... slapping his... you know.
JF: Hold on, Rob Hall is to his feet, and he goes in front of Bobby Sharp. He lifts him up... RUNNING LYGERBOMB! HOLY ****! ROB HALL JUST GAVE BOBBY SHARP A
RUNNING LYGERBOMB! OH MY GOD! HE MAKES THE COVER! 1.....2....3! HOLY ****!
Result: Rob Hall pinned Bobby Sharp following a Running Lygerbomb in 4:03. JF: Here comes Rob Hall. That was amazing man! RH: Well, let's just say I've been practicing. LG: You executed a running Lygerbomb! RH: Yep. JF: Well I don't think BS... (Chuckles) ...will be able to make it, so I'll stick arond for the main event.
LG: Let's go to the ring! HCWF Heavyweight Championship Jim "The Dragon" Dalton w/ Master Kwon Vs. Kurt Wolf (c) ( "Jesus Christ Super" starts up, as Jim "The Dragon" Dalton and Kurt Wolf make their way to the ring. Then, "All Apologies" by Nirvana starts up, and Kurt
Wolf walks to the ring with the HCWF Heavyweight Championship around his waist.
They square off, and the bell rings. LG: This should be a great match. Wolf and Dalton lock up, and Dalton executes a standing switch followed by a German Suplex. He holds it for the pin, but
Wolf's feet are on the ropes. Wolf slowly pulls to his feet, and it looks like he's favoring his right shoulder. He locks up with Dalton, this time Wolf goes for a standing switch. Dalton with a standing switch of his own, and a
belly-to-back suplex! JF: Wolf to his feet, and as Dalton pulls himself up Wolf hits him with a clothesline. He hits Dalton with a legdrop, and then turns him over and puts him in a full nelson. Dalton strethces to reach the ropes, but he can't make it. Wolf switches to a chinlock, and he straps Dalton's arms around his knees like a Steiner Recliner. Master Kwon is by the apron, telling Dalton to get out. Wolf releases the hold, and pulls Dalton to his feet.
RH: Wolf whips Dalton into the corner, and goes to the opposite turnbuckle. Wolf runs across the ring and leaps into the air for a splash! Dalton moves out of the way, and Wolf is on the turnbuckle. Dalton turns him around and starts punching Wolf as the crowds counts along. 1...2...3...4.. Wolf blocks a punch, and hits a spinebuster off the turnbuckle!
LG: One of Wolf's trademarks, and it's quite an effective move. He grabs Dalton by the legs, and catapults 'The Dragon' onto the turnbuckle! Dalton falls backwards, and Wolf makes a cover. 1.....2...Dalton kicks out. Daltn slowly pulls to his feet, and Wolf is on the mat. He attempts a flying lariat, but
Dalton counters it with a dropkick! Wolf falls to the mat, and Dalton climbs to the top. Dalton jumps into the air and hits a moonsault!
JF: He doesn't go for a cover, and instead lifts Wolf onto the top turnbuckle. Dalton nails a superplex that sends Wolf flying to the middle of the ring! He goes to the top rope, and goes for a flying headbutt. Wolf rolls out of the way! That really hurt Dalton, and Wolf can end this match if he takes advantage. He covers Dalton, 1.....2... Dalton kicks out. Neither man is going to give up easily, this is going to be a great match.
RH: Wolf and Dalton lock up once again. Dalton scoops Wolf onto his shoulder into a fireman's carry, and hits a Death Valley Driver! He applies a cradle and goes for the pin. 1.....2... Wolf kicks out! Wolf is to his feet, and now both men are going all out to win. Frankensteiner by Dalton! Wolf is to his feet immediatly, and he hits Dalton with a brainbuster suplex! Hold on, someone is coming down the aisle. It's Psycho Bob, and he's got a chair!
LG: Bob nails Steven Darty! The ref is out cold! Wolf is in the corner, and
Dalton walks up to Bob, telling him to leave. Psycho Bob decks Jim Dalton with the steel chair! Dalton is on the mat, trying to get to his feet. Bob hits
Dalton across the back of the head! He's pounding the hell out of Dalton!
What's going on here?!? JF: Wolf pushes Bob, and Psycho Bob nails Wolf on the shoulder with the chair! Wolf crumples to the mat, and Bob knocks him senseless with the chair. Hold on,
Mike Steele's coming down the aisle! He tells Bob to get out of the ring! Yes!
Hold on, he rolls Dalton on top of Wolf! What the ****? He pulls off his suit, he's wearing a referee shirt! He makes the count, 1.....2....3!
Result: Jim "The Dragon" Dalton defeated Kurt Wolf by pinfall in 21:03 to win the HCWF Heavyweight Championship.
LG: Someone else is coming to the ring! I think it's Buzz Tyler, he competes in the MCeW and IWA. What is he doing here? He picks up a chair and starts beating
Dalton! Tyler has the mic.
Buzz Tyler: Dalton, Wolf, you two aren't true champions! Dalton, I want you in the ring, I want a shot at that title!
LG: Fans, we're out time, we'll see you on SNC! JF: (Crying) It's so horrible... [detail withheld] Handler of Jake Rockwell. "They send one of yours to the hospital, you send one of theirs to the morgue." - Arn Anderson
