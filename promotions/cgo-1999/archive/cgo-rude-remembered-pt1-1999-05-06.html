Rude Remembered, Part 1

6 May 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
...This is DirectTV. Rick Rood, 1958 -1999 [Camera fades into a black screen. Suddenly, an ominous bell tolls, as the screen comes to life. What is seen is a highlight reel of Rick Rude's wrestling career. It chronicles everything from his early WWF days, to his latest stint in WCW. Every minute, another bell tolls, until the count reaches ten...]
[Suddenly, Metallica's "For Whom the Bell Tolls" breaks out. Another highlight reel is shown, but this time it's a high-octane reel with excerpts showcasing current
CGO talent. The camera cuts away from the screen, and takes in a live-action shot of the TwinDome at Bloomington, Minnesota. Tens of thousands of drunken, screaming, crazy wrestling fans are shown. Most everyone has CGO-related merchandise or signs. "For Whom the Bell Tolls" continues to play, but it's being covered but the official CGO band, the "Barbwire Syndicate", led by the
Apostle.]
[The lights dim, as the lovely Cara Teal makes her way to the ring, with a spotlight on her the entire time. She's dressed in a designer dress in respect for the evening.
Cara slips into the ring, where she's handed a mic.] C. Teal - Welcome everyone, to "Rude Remembered". Before we start, I'd like to request a moment of silence for the man whom we have gathered here tonight to honor... [.........] C. Teal - Thank you. The CGO has asked me to come out here tonight, and tell you about tonight's event. We all remember Rick for planting the seeds back in the
80's, and making professional wrestling what it is today. Rick was a former NWA
World
Heavyweight Champion, WWF Intercontinental Champion, WCW U.S. Heavyweight
Champion, among many other titles...He was also a great man behind the scenes, and has always loved his home state of Minnesota. So we ask of you, for this night, don't mourn Rick's death...Remember all the great memories he gave when he was still with us. This night's for you, Rick...
Also here with us tonight are a few familiar faces, all proud to be Minnesotans... YOUR Governor, Jesse Ventura! [Spotlight goes to Ventura, as crowd gives a standing ovation...] C. Teal - From the WWF, Sean Waltman! [Spotlight to Waltman, as the crowd is still on it's feet] C. Teal - From WCW, Curt Henning! [Spotlight to Henning, and again, the crowd is still on it's feet] C. Teal - And finally, from WCW, the 14-time World Heavyweight Champion, Ric Flair! [Spotlight to Flair, as the crowd finishes out their ovation, and takes their seat.] C. Teal - We still have MUCH more to come! So thank you, and enjoy your evening! [The lights come back on, as Joey Roberts, Van Douglas, and Chris Osmon are seen at ringside. Cara Teal pulls up a seat next to Osmon at ringside. JR and
Van
are both dressed in black tuxedos, hair slicked back, etc. Osmon, however, is wearing a torn "Type O Negative" t-shirt, with uncombed hair and black sunglasses.
The camera then focuses in on Roberts...] J. Roberts - Welcome fans, to "RUDE REMEMBERED"!! We are LIVE from the Twindome here in Bloomington, Minnesota! I'm Joey Roberts, and joining me on this momentous occasion are both Evander "Van" Douglas, my partner-in-crime from the HWA, and Chris Osmon himself, co-owner of the Chicago Grappler's
Organization. Gentlemen, as always, it's a pleasure...
V. Douglas - Shut up, Roberts. C. Osmon - Likewise. J. Roberts - It's going to be a long night. Anyway folks, as the lovely Ms. Teal stated earlier, we're here tonight to celebrate the memory of one of wrestling's most notorious villains, Rick Rude. All proceeds from tonight's event will go to help the Rood family in whatever way possible.
V. Douglas - Shut up about Rude, Roberts. Tell the slobs at home about the CGO already...We don't got all night. J. Roberts - Fans, this is also the premiere pay-per-view of the fledgling federation, the Chicago Grappler's Organization. The CGO was founded by the man sitting next to me, Chris Osmon, and Danny Sundown. Also, the two hired Ryan
Starr to bear many responsibilities backstage...Chris, any words of wisdom you wish to share with us about this upstart fed?
C. Osmon - Hmmm...Nope. V. Douglas - Hold up a sec Roberts, I've got something to say. If most of the Van-aholics at weren't aware, I just got word that they're CANNING me after this broadcast. Looks like 5 years of broadcasting schooling and another 7 years of faithful service to Os-moron over here don't amount up to a pile of BS in this business anymore...
C. Osmon - You can take all your experience, and shove it up your ass for all I care. Any way you look at it, you still suck... V. Douglas - .............. J. Roberts - Uh...Sorry for that, fans. Anyway... We have a great card lined up tonight! In our first bout, Al Morbis will take on "Mean" Scott Green in a
Chicago Street Fight! We look forward to that one, and it will be happening here shortly. Then, we will see Mark Dedmon take on not one, but two wrestlers, in a handicap match. After that, we will see some tag-team action, as
Everlasting Pain reunites for one night only in order to fight the Frat Boys. Another handicap will be taking place after they get through, as the Innovators of Offense team up against the Bolt. We also have a Triple-Threat match scheduled, when Big Dawg, Brenden Bowes, and
Steel
Titania all lock up inside the squared circle. That match will be followed by
Alex Avery vs. Chris Callous...Alex was originally scheduled to take on Jo Jo Johnson, but Mr. Johnson has left the CGO. And finally, our main event...Ryan Starr will step into the ring for one last time as he puts up his HWA Panhandle Heritage
Championship against Richey Razorblade's HWA World Heavyweight Championship! And to top that off, Mr. Fabulous has just been named as the guest referee for the match! Folks, this promises to be one of the greatest matches in recent history...
I suggest you don't miss it. [Roberts pauses for a moment, as he receives a message from his headset] J. Roberts - Fans, I have just been informed that we are READY TO GO! In just a few moments, Al Morbis will take on "Mean" Scott Green in a Chicago Street
Fight! Gentlemen, quickly, what are your thoughts on this match?... V. Douglas - Does it look like I give a rat's ass? I hope they both kill each other... C. Osmon - I say just sit back and enjoy the match, Roberts...No need to overanalyze.... J. Roberts - Fair enough...We now take you to the parking lot, where this match will take place... Match #1 - Al Morbis vs. "Mean" Scott Green Chicago Street Fight! Summery - The match, as said, started out in the parking lot. "Mean" Scott
Green
was the first man out, but Al Morbis was nowhere to be seen. A black hearse suddenly came screeching around the corner, heading straight for Green. Green jumped out of the way, as the hearse came to a stop. Al Morbis jumped out of the driver's seat, and immediately began to pummel Green. Morbis went "ariel"
quickly, slamming Green to the ground, then performing a moonsault off the top of the hearse. Morbis then used an assortment of weapons, beating Green down with everything from a baseball bat to a piece of scrap metal he found laying around. Morbis went to whip Green into the hearse, when Green reversed, taking the offensive.
Morbis suffered a series of a DDT, a Powerbomb, and a Piledriver right to the cement. Morbis looked unconscious, so Green went to retrieve a chair. Morbis showed his resilient side, finding a way to continue the match. Green, not knowing Morbis was up, had his chair slammed right back in his face by a well-placed Morbis
Missile Drop-kick. Morbis opened the hood of the card, and stuck Green's head inside... He went on to repeatedly slam the hood down on Green. Morbis set Green up for a
Flying Head Scissors, but Green caught him in midair, and turned it into a
NASTY Facebuster onto the cement. Green was fed up with Morbis at this time, so he jumped in the hearse. When Morbis staggered to his feet, Green gunned it, hitting
Morbis head-on. He then pinned Morbis, getting the three count! CGO officials and paramedics immediately ran out, fearing for Morbis' life. Green was set to
RUN
OVER Morbis for a final time, when MARK DEDMON jumped out of a casket in the back! Dedmon wrapped the "Eternal Rest" around Green's head, forcing Green to stop the car. Dedmon then dragged Green onto the hood of the car outside, placed them both on top, and delivered a DEVASTATING Tombstone Piledriver!
Dedmon threw Green off the hearse, threw the semi-lifeless body of Al Morbis into the passenger's seat, and drove off.
Winner - "Mean" Scott Green [Camera cuts back to Roberts, Douglas, and Osmon] J. Roberts - OH MY! WHAT A MATCH! If it hadn't been for Mark Dedmon, we may have seen the last of Al Morbis! Scott Green has just won his first CGO match in convincing fashion!
V. Douglas - Stupid Mark Dedmon. I was looking forward to at least one casualty. C. Osmon - Good showing by both...I'd like to see a future feud between the two. J. Roberts - Fans, we're now going to take you to a special segment that will be showing a few times throughout the night...Several wrestlers have been asked to share their favorite Rick Rude memory. And now, here's Richey Razorblade...
[Cut to Razorblade, backstage] R. Razorblade - You know, one of the moments in Rick Rude's career that I remem-ber the most, was when he returned to the WWF back in '97. I'd always been a Rude fan, and I never ever thought I'd see him united with Shawn Michaels, espec-ially considering the fact that they'd both used the 'sexiest man alive'
gimmick. When I saw him walk down the aisle, I literally stood up and punched the air, because it was great to see him back where he belonged, even though it pretty much confirmed a heel turn for HBK. It's terrible how this sport has been robbed of one of it's greatest talents, at what was, let's face it, still quite a young age for a man to die. I certainly wish his family all the best, and I'm sure that as soon as he stepped into that great ring in the sky, he was once again a champion.
[Cut back to Roberts, Douglas, and Osmon] J. Roberts - Thank you Richey, for those touching words. But now, it's time for our second match...We have word that Mark Dedmon has RETURNED to the parking garage, and is set to take on the Jerzy Foundation! In fact, the Jerzy
Foundation reps are making their way to the ring now... Match #2 - Mark Dedmon vs. The Jerzy Foundation ("King Kong" Steve Jerzy and Matt "The Cow" Jerzy) Handicap Match! Summery - The Jerzy Foundation met nothing but boos as the made their way to the ring, where they remained by themselves for a few minutes. Many thought
Mark Dedmon was going to no-show, and the fans were getting anxious. Suddenly, the lights dimmed, as "Christian Woman" by Type O Negative played over the PA
System. Out came Dedmon, walking slowly, methodically to the ring. When he got to the ring, the Jerzy Foundation tried to take immediate advantage of the situation.
They ran at Dedmon, who consequently caught both of them around the neck. He proceeded to deliver a devastating double-chokeslam. Dedmon turned his attention to
Steve Jerzy, by throwing him into the corner and preceding to beat the living hell out of him. Matt Jerzy clawed his way onto Dedmon's back, trying to do any damage that he could. Dedmon flipped Matt over his back, landing him right on top of
Steve.
Dedmon then lifted up Matt in a gorilla-press slam type fashion, and threw him into the fans. Steve then jumped up, turned Dedmon around, and tried to deliver a drop-kick...He succeeded in knocking Dedmon against the ropes, but the big man used it as momentum and laid Steve out with a flying clothesline. Steve then quickly received the same fate as Matt. Dedmon then went out in the same area as the two Jerzy's, and gave them both devastating Tombstone Piledrivers onto the cement. Obviously, by this time, both Jerzy's are unconscious, so Dedmon ended their suffering and covered them both for the pinfall victory. "Christian
Woman"
again played, as Dedmon left the same way he came. Winner - Mark Dedmon J. Roberts - What a showing by the Phenom of the CGO! Fans, I don't know if there's any man here who can stop the sheer superhuman strength of Mark Dedmon... C. Osmon - Hopefully, someone can get Mark to open up a bit more and find out what his exact mission here in the CGO is... V. Douglas - The guy's a freak! He has no mission! He just delights in the misery of others... J. Roberts - You may very well have something there, Van. Fans, our next bout will feature the Frat Boyz, a team new here to the CGO, against Everlasting
Pain, Bloodbath and Hiroshi Mifune. I don't know the extent of Brady J.
McAllistor III's and Trent Kensington, Esq.'s talents, but they better bring their A-games if they hope to stand a chance against a classic team like Everlasting Pain...
V. Douglas - Those Frat boyz are a couple of fruits, if you ask me... J. Roberts - Um, thanks Van. We now take you to our next match... Match #3 The Frat Boyz (Brady J. McAllistor III and Trent Kensington, Esq.) vs. Everlasting Pain (Bloodbath and Hiroshi Mifune) One night Everlasting Pain reunion! Summery - The Frat Boyz were introduced first, as they made their way to the ring. After several minutes, Everlasting Pain was no where to be seen, and the fans grew restless. The Frat Boyz called for a mic, and demanded for opponents. Much to the fans dismay, out came CGO jobbers Redneck Justice. The Frat Boyz immediately went to work, tearing Billy Stevens and Tombo Jenkins apart. RJ tried to mount an offense, but the Frat Boyz proved to be too much. The Boyz threw Tombo Jenkins to the outside, as they set up Billy Stevens for their finisher, the "Keg
Stand".
B.J. McAllistor III made the pin, while T. Kensington, Esq., prevented Tombo from making the save. Needless to say, the fans weren't too happy with the match.
Winner - The Frat Boyz J. Roberts - These fans are none too thrilled at the outcome with that match! But the question remains...Where is Everlasting Pain? Why didn't they show up? V. Douglas - Bah...They're both a bunch of pansies. C. Osmon - I think they may be too accustomed to their new offices... J. Roberts - In any situation, the Frat Boyz have recorded their first win in the CGO! Fans, it's once again time for a CGO wrestler to share one of his favorite Rick
Rude
memories. Here's Al Morbis; Prerecorded, or course...
[Cut to Morbis, taped before the card, when he was warming up.] Morbis - My favorite Rude moment? Well, it was back when Rude was still wrestling in the WWF. It was me and Annie's first date, and we were at a house show. Rude came down the aisle, picked Annie out of the crowd, and took her to the ring!
He then went on to gyrate in front of her. Heh heh, good tim...Hey! Wait a second! He gyrated in front of MY Annie! That son of a... [Quick cut back to the announcers] J. Roberts - Uh, thanks Al...I think. But now, we're ready for our second handicap match of the night! The Bolt will take on the Innovators of Offense! We haven't heard much from any of these gentlemen, but I suppose we'll see what they have in store for us in just a few minutes...
