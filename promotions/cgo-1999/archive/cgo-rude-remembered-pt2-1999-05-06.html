Rude Remembered, Part 2

6 May 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Continued from Part One) Match #4 The Bolt vs. The Innovators of Offense (Angeldust and Roy Irvine) Second handicap of the night! Summery - "The Dope Show" by Marilyn Manson played over the speakers, as the
Bolt came out of the back first. He ran to the ring, awaiting the arrival of his two opponents. Then, "Comedown" by Bush played, as the Innovators of
Offense came out. With them, they carried various weapons, such as glass and barbwire. The ref, at first, stuck to the rules, making Angeldust sit out while
Irvine and Bolt locked it up.
The Bolt took advantage right away, landing a series of punches to the face of
Irvine. The Bolt landed a series of powermoves, wearing Irvine down with a Swinging DDT, Powerslam, and Sidewalk Slam. Angeldust began to taunt Bolt, and when Bolt went over to take care of business, Angeldust smashed a previously hidden glass plate over Bolt's head, cutting him open. Irvine took advantage, planting Bolt with a
Powerbomb. He then went up top, and devastated Bolt with a Moonsault. Irvine then tagged in Angeldust. Angeldust moved like lightning, outrunning the stronger
Bolt.
Bolt went for a clothesline, but Angeldust ducked, jumped off the ropes, and performed a Moonsault Body-block, knocking Bolt off his feet. Angeldust went to the air again, performing a 450 Splash from the top. By this time, Irvine had set up two tables on the outside. Irvine dragged Bolt out of the ring, set him on top of the tables, and went to look for more weapons. Angeldust tried to perform a Suicide Dive onto the helpless Bolt outside, but Bolt moved out of the way, sending Angeldust crashing. By the time Irvine could come back, Bolt had wrapped the team's barbwire around a chair, clocking Irvine square in the head. Bolt took the opportunity and continued to work on Irvine. But, he didn't see Angeldust finally get up, and by the time he turned around to check on him, Angeldust was already positioned at the top.
Bolt's chair was smashed back into his bleeding forehead by an Angeldust VanDaminator. Angeldust wasted no more time, and got a quick pin. Winner - The Innovators of Offense J. Roberts - What a match! Tremendous display of athleticism on the part of all the wrestlers involved! The Bolt looked like he had a chance...But in the end, two men working against him proved to be too tough to handle.
V. Douglas - If the Innovators of Offense have this much trouble with one guy, how do they ever expect to beat a tag team? J. Roberts - Good point Van. Fans, at this time, Mr. Osmon has a special announcement... C. Osmon - Uh, yeah...No big deal. It's just that former HWA superstar Tommy Gunn came crawling back a few days ago, begging for work. So we decided to let him wrestle tonight...
J. Roberts - And that's the match we're going to see next. In fact, his opponent, Jack "Black" Shaw, is already made his way to the ring... Match #5 Tommy Gunn vs. Jack "Black" Shaw Gunn's CGO debut! Summery - "Last Ride In" by Greenday played over the speakers, as Tommy Gunn made his way to the ring. Jack Shaw jumped his earlier, trying to get whatever advantage he could. Gunn then reversed Shaw's attempt at an Irish Whip, and drop-kicked him to the outside. It was all Gunn from there. Tommy started off with a somersault plancha to the staggered Shaw on the outside. He then took the air out of Shaw with a snap suplex, followed up by another ariel move...A guillotine legdrop. Gunn planted Shaw with a DTT to the cement for good measure, and threw him back into the ring. Shaw got to his feet, but not before Tommy Gunn set himself up on the apron. He took one bound to the top rope, and sprung off with a
Missile
Drop-kick. Gunn continued to wear down Shaw, with a double-armed suplex and a superkick. With Shaw still not quite not ripe for Gunn's finisher, Tommy set him up, took off at full speed towards the ropes, and amazed the crowd with a
Tumbleweed heel kick. At this time, Shaw was knocked clean out, but Tommy went to the top, and devastated Shaw with the Gunnshot, a 450 Splash. Tommy then registered the pin. But with his adrenaline still high, Gunn completely snapped on Shaw for no reason, and continued to beat the hell out of him. CGO officials were called down to try to save what was left of Shaw's unconscious body. Gunn was then escorted out of the ring.
Winner - Tommy Gunn J. Roberts - I don't know what the hell has gotten into Tommy Gunn, but it must be working! That was one of the best individual performances this broadcaster has seen in a while! If only he was able to do that against Ryan Starr last summer...
V. Douglas - I'll tell you what, I like this Gunn kid. He's not afraid of anybody, or anything, including Robert's precious Ryan Starr. If you give Gunn a chance, he's going to become one of this league's big players...
J. Roberts - Fans, we have hit the home stretch here in the Twindome! Only three matches remain! First, we're going to see "Astounding" Alex Avery take on
Chris Callous, who is a last-minute replacement for Jo Jo Johnson, who just recently left the company. Avery has an EXTENSIVE history, competing in over 15 federations.
Not a bad track record... C. Osmon - True, but on the other hand, he's Canadian... J. Roberts - What does that have to do wi...Oh, forget it! V. Douglas - Actually, I think Chris has a good point... J. Roberts - What point?! For the love of...No, no, I won't let them get to me. Chris Callous is in the ring, let's go, NOW! Match #6 "Astounding" Alex Avery vs. Chris Callous Alex Avery's CGO debut! Summery - Beethoven's "Fur Elise" played over the speakers, as Alex Avery made his way to the ring. The ref calls for the bell, as the two opponent's started off with a clean lockup. Callous wound up in a headlock, as Avery began to wear him down.
Callous then sent Avery off the ropes, but Avery responded with clothesline. He then picked Callous up, help him high about his head, and slammed him down with authority...Showing an early display of power. He continued to try to weaken
Callous
with a headlock, bringing Callous to his feet. Callous again tried to send
Avery off the ropes, but Alex held on, and planted Callous face-first into the mat with a bulldog.
Working on the neck/spine area, Avery brought Callous to his feet again, and quickly landed a nice neckbreaker. Callous tried to mount an offense from the ground, but Avery slipped behind him, and applied a full-nelson. Callous refused to give up, and worked his way to the ropes. Avery went to the other corner, awaiting Callous.
Chris tried to charge at him, but Alex caught him in an inverted atomic drop. Callous did a 180, giving Avery a perfect opportunity for a normal atomic drop. To
Avery's
suprise, Callous turned around a delivered a lightning-fast drop-kick. Callous mounted a small offensive, with a bodyslam followed by an elbow. He even set up Avery for a headscissors takedown, but the Canadian quickly turned it into his finisher...
The "Canadian Precision". Avery dropped Callous with a Powerbomb, and then used a forward roll to get the pin.
Winner - Alex Avery J. Roberts - "Astounding" Alex Avery has just come away with his first CGO victory, using a unique combination of mat wrestling and power. I see a bright future in this young superstar...
V. Douglas - You see a bright future in EVERY young superstar, Roberts...I think Avery should take that as an insult... J. Roberts - Whatever. Fans, time is quickly ticking away until our main event... But first, we have a special TRIPLE THREAT match! Big Dawg, Steel Titania, and
"The Extreme Playboy" Brendan Bowes will all go at it! Fans, I don't want to sound biased...
V. Douglas - Too late... J. Roberts - ...but Steel Titania is rumored to be one of the most AWESOME athletes in wrestling today, with a combination of sheer, raw strength and ariel ability! Let's see if this mammoth of a man can overcome both Big Dawg and Brendan Bowes...
V. Douglas - Just a question, Roberts...Are we going to have to start calling Steel Titania "JR's boy"? C. Osmon - Heh heh...Maybe we should keep you on staff after all, Van... J. Roberts - Amusing. Fans, that match is just around the corner, but first, we have another "Favorite Rude Moment" lined up for you. Here's "Hollywood" Ryan Starr... [Cut to Starr, backstage] HRS - My favorite Rude moment would have to be his feud with the Ultimate
Warrior,
back in both their WWF heyday. There I was, a 10-11 year-old old mark, watching with bulging eyes two men who were larger-than-life, locking it up in the greatest series of matches that I've even seen. It was also that moment, seeing Rude's arrogant, rugged and lethal character, that really got me involved with the world of professional wrestling. Sure, I had a successful career in the adult-film industry before hitting it big in the HWA, but wrestling has always been my first love.
One of my greatest regrets in life is not being able to shake hands with the man who changed my life so profoundly...Hopefully, one day, in that big ring in the sky, I'll be able to do just that. We miss you Rick.
[Cut back to the announcing team] J. Roberts - Thanks Ry. Fans, it's now time for the Triple-Threat match...And after this, HELL IN A CELL!! Match #7 Big Dawg vs. Steel Titania vs. "The Extreme Playboy" Brendan Bowes Summery - The Big Dawg came to the ring first, accompanied by "Natural Born
Killers" blasting over the speakers. As soon as he reached the ring, "Paradise
City"
by Guns N' Roses played, and Brenden Bowes came running out. The two immediately got it on, throwing out everything they had. Big Dawg took the early advantage, knocking the wind out of Bowes with a kick to the gut followed by a
Pileplex (Suplex into piledriver). Dawg tried to use submission moves early, such as a Boston crab and an anklelock, both Bowes reached the ropes both times before any real damage was done. Dawg then went for a clothesline, but Bowes ducked it, and nailed him with a T-bone Taz-plex. Bowes then wrapped Dawg up in a bow-and- arrow submission...Dawg looked ready to submit, but then "Busy Child" by the
Crystal Method blasted over the loudspeakers. Out came Steel Titania, and his manager. Bowes tried to take Titania down as soon as the big man stepped in the ring, but to no avail. Titania lifted Bowes over his head like a rag doll, and tossed him to the outside. Titania then went to work on Dawg. Dawg gave Titania a few gut shots, but they bounced right off the 7 footer. Titania staggered his man with a head butt, then sent him for a RIDE...With a running powerbomb. Titania then went to the top, and did the unthinkable...He absolutely rocked Dawg with a MOONSAULT!
Thinking Dawg was near death, Titania went to the outside to work on Bowes. Bowes, again, tried to stagger Titania with a chair, but not even cold steel could slow down the big man. In fact, it only made him more angry. He nailed Bowes with a superkick to the chops, knocking the Playboy to the rail. He then knocked Bowes unconscious with his finisher, "Massive Titania" (Reverse Brainbuster into
Reverse
DTT). But even that wasn't good enough for the Big man. He grabbed a table from under the ring, and placed Bowes on top of it. He then got in the ring to set up his move...What he didn't notice, or didn't think about, was the fact that
Big Dawg was
no where to be seen. As soon as Titania was airborne, Dawg popped up from underneath the ring, and in lightning-quick fashion, pulled Bowes off the table.
Needless to say, 400 pounds hurtling through a table isn't going to look or feel good. For good measure, Dawg busted his head open with a chair. From there, Dawg set Bowes up, and tombstone piledrove the already unconscious Bowes into the cement. From there, it was an easy 1-2-3 for the Big Dawg.
Winner - Big Dawg J. Roberts - Oh my! Look at the carnage! Steel Titania tried to end Bowes' career, but in the end, it cost him the match! V. Douglas - Plain and simple Roberts, Big Dawg simply outsmarted that overgrown luchadore-wannabe. Never EVER leave a man until you know he's down and out. J. Roberts - Fans, we're just about ready for our main event! But first, we have yet ANOTHER special guest with us tonight...Former HWA superstar Ike Harris is in his luxury box here at the Twindome, taking in the action. With him from his current fed, the SCW, are Miss April and Hoss Strickland. Ike has been asked to contribute his favorite Rude memory; Here he is...
[Cut to Harris and his cronies in the luxury box. Harris has his SCW T.V. Tag
Team Championship strapped over his shoulder.] I. Harris - Who can forget the Rude/Roberts feud in the WWF in the late 80's? The 'Sexiest Man Alive' accidentally puts the make on Mrs. 'Stone Mountain
Madman', the wife of Jake Roberts. Tres Classique! But my all time favorite
"Rude Awakening" was the night he first appeared in the nWo. I was shocked. I kept hitting the jump button on the remote, hopping from TNT to USA.
Look, Rude and DX. Look, Rude and nWo. DX. nWo. Vince. Eric. Rude. Rude. Hatchie Matchie! [Harris figures the camera is off him, but due to technical problems, it stays on him for a minute...]
I. Harris - Those other two idiots don't know what's in store for them. Hell, did the CGO really think I'd show up in this dump for fun? I'm here for my homeboy if he needs me...
[Quick cut back to the announcers table] J. Roberts - Fans, I think we just heard something meant for Harris' gang's ears only! Without a doubt, he was talking about the main event...But who is he here to support? Logic would lead us to believe his former HWA partner-in-crime,
Richey
Razorblade. On the other hand, he's been known to team up with Ryan Starr in other federations! Or, could he even be planning an assault on the previous two, along with
Mr. Fabulous?? V. Douglas - I think it's great! Finally, the CGO gets a whiff of a REAL man's wrestler... C. Osmon - If Harris was planning something, I sure as hell didn't know about it. I didn't even know he was here tonight until now... J. Roberts - In any case, fans, it's time for our main event! Before we start, we'd like to thank everyone involved with this worthy cause tonight. Rick Rude is undoubtedly smiling down from above this evening. Our deepest condolences to the Rood family, and may they find a way to survive through this tragedy.
........ Fans, our main event has been 7 months in the making. The winner will recieve both the HWA World Heavyweight Championship, and Panhandle Heritage
Champion-ship...Retiring both belts. SOMETHING HAS TO GIVE...And we're about to find out just what that something is...
