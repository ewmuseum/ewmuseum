Rude Remembered, Part 3

6 May 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Continued from Part Two) Match #8 Ryan Starr vs. Richey Razorblade with Mr. Fabulous as special guest referee Main Event - Hell in a Cell!!! Summery - The first man introduced was the special guest referee, Mr. Fabulous. "Are You Experienced?" by Jimi Hendrix played over the speakers, as Fab met nothing but boos walking down the aisle. Next, "Roll with It" by Oasis played, as Richey Razorblade walked down to aisle to even MORE boos. Razorblade settled in the corner directly across from Fab, as the two had a stare-down. Finally, the crowd erupted in cheers, as "Crazy Train" by Ozzy Osborne played, and Ryan
Starr
came running out. He immediately rushed into the ring, after Razorblade...Fab tried to play it clean, and tell the two to wait for the Cell to be lowered. Starr didn't listen, and went after RR like a house of fire.
Fab called for the bell, and for the Cell to be lowered. HRS started out with rights and lefts, backing RR into the ropes, and whipping him to the other die. RR grabbed the ropes, stopped himself, and pointed to his head, mocking
Starr...Not a wise decision. Starr ran full speed at RR, drop-kicked him right in the chops, sending Double R hurtling to the outside. Razorblade barely got to his feet, but HRS used this momentary advantage to latch onto the ropes, and perform a somersault plancha that WOWED the crowd. The Cell was 3/4ths of the way down at this time, and Fab was letting the action continue on the outside. HRS had Razorblade on the ground, so he decided to drag his body over to the spot where the Cell was going to come down. Richey screamed for help, but Fab just smiled. Just as the
Cell was about to separate RR's head from the rest of his body, he punched HRS squarely in the jaw, and rolled out of the way. As soon as both of them men got to their feet, Richey charged first, knocking HRS down with a wicked clothesline. Razorblade, seizing the opportunity, picked Starr up by his hair, and flung him bad-shoulder first into the steel steps. Starr tried to get up, but RR struck again, this time whipping Starr's bad shoulder into the confines of the Cell. Razorblade continued his assault, raising the steel steps over his head, and bringing them down on Starr's shoulder. Starr looked like he was pretty much done, so Richey turned to the crowd and taunted them.
While he had his back turned, Starr mustered up enough energy to pull a chair from under the ring, and get up. As soon as Double R turned around, Starr whacked him over the head, busting him open. Starr then took to the offensive, quickly picking Richey up and slamming him back down to the pavement. Using ingenuity, HRS retrieved another object from under the ring, this time a table.
He propped it up diagonally, like a wedge between the cage and the ring...Then, he picked up Razorblade, and using his good shoulder, he whipped Double R as hard as he could towards the object. Needless to say, the Englishman went through it like a bull-dozer, smashing it to splinters. Meanwhile, Fab was in the ring, flirting with a buxom blonde in the front row.
Starr finally dragged Double R back inside the ring...Quickly, he stood Razorblade up, and gave him a standing side kick to the face. Using his second wind, Razor-blade popped right back up, and bounced off the ropes...Starr leapfrogged him on the first run, and when Richey came around the next time, HRS put him right back to the mat with a kick to the gut followed by a DDT. Even with the bad shoulder,
Starr then went ariel...He climbed the turnbuckles, and knocked the air out of
Razorblade with a Shooting Star Press. This time, Starr did the posing, and
Richey made him pay. Double R snuck up from behind, and when HRS turned around, he caught him with a NASTY Rock Bottom-type move. Richey then went to work on the legs of Ryan, locking him up with a Figure Four. Fab came over, asked Starr if he wanted to quit, and when Starr said no, Fab went back and continued his conversation with the blonde. Richey broke the hold, and went over to get in
Fab's face. After a few moments of the two squabbling, Starr snuck up behind
Richey, turned him around, kicked him to the gut, double-underhooked the arms, and delivered his finisher...The BOX-OFFICE BLOCKBUSTER! Richey looked like he could've been down for a ten count. Fab went down, counted one, two...But then he stopped.
When HRS screamed at him to count to three, Fab got up, and drop-kicked the kid right to the face! And then, to the fans disapproval, he and Razorblade double-teamed Starr! For a few minutes, the two literally kicked the life out of HRS. Double R then retrieved another table from the outside...He slid it into the ring, as
Fab set it up and placed Starr on top of it. Fab laughed hysterically as Double
R
went to the top, and nailed the helpless Starr with a Moonsault...A move that looked like it nearly broke the kid in half. To finish him off, Double R locked on the Figure Four again, while Fab used a cross-arm breaker on Starr's left shoulder. Fab, still the official ref, half-laughingly Starr if he wanted to submit. To the duo's suprise, the kid hung on, as the fans cheered to try to rally him. For what seemed like an eternity, the duo kept the hold on...And everytime he was asked to submit,
Starr
screamed "No!".
Finally, Razorblade said an audible "screw this". He broke the hold, told Fab to do the same, and dragged HRS to his feet. As Fab held the half-conscious
Starr, Razorblade nearly took his head off with a United Kick-dom. Fab, having the basic same move, which he calls the FabuX, told Razorblade it was HIS turn.
Richey was reluctant, wanting to pin Starr and get it over with. Fab assured him this was the last one, so Razorblade agreed. However, when Fab went for the sidekick, Starr found SOMEWAY to duck...The end was result was Razorblade on the mat, Fab with a gaping jaw, and tens of thousands of fans SCREAMING for
Starr to get up!
Even though Starr was basically out cold, Double R totally forgot about the pin, and went right after Fab! They each exchanged numerous blows, as the hatred between the two finally surfaced. Fab went to whip Double R to the ropes, but Richey reversed it...And when Fab came back, Razorblade backed dropped him OUT of the ring, OFF the Cell, and right smack DOWN to the pavement! Even the fans were shocked at how high in the air Fab was. Richey looked on in amazement at his work for a second...When he heard the crowd blow the ROOF off the Twindome!
Confused, he looked around for a second...But he soon found out why for himself. Ryan Starr had once again showed himself one of the most resilient sonuva bitches alive, by dragging everything left of his body back up, and staggering over to Razorblade. When Richey turned around, he stalled for just a moment, shocked
Starr was still ALIVE...And that's all the time HRS needed. Double R went for a punch, but Starr blocked...Followed by another kick to the gut, and the SECOND
Box-office Blockbuster of the night! Starr has just enough left to lift
Razorblade up and put him down hard...Razorblade was out, alright, but Starr's weakened state couldn't finish up the move in a pinning combination.
At this time, DANNY SUNDOWN was running down to the ring in a ref's shirt! He bursted through the Cell door with unmatched quickness, and positioned himself in the ring to make the count! Problem was, Starr was having trouble crawling over to Razorblade. He came within inches...But his body wouldn't allow him to go any further. Starr collapsed, not even being able to lift his head. The crowd fell silent, as Danny ran his fingers through his hair. He looked around at the sober faces, gave a determined look...THEN THREW STARR RIGHT ON TOP OF RAZOR-
BLADE!! The fans EXPLODED, as Danny Sundown registered the three-count!! Fab, who had just woken up, stared at the scene from the outside with absolute
HORROR in his eyes! Danny Sundown picked up Starr, called for the two titles, and carried HRS out of the ring on his shoulder. Halfway back to the dressing room,
Starr woke up...And realizing what he had just pulled off, he acted like he HADN'T just been through a half-hour from hell! Starr took his two titles, and without heeding Sundown's warnings, climbed up the Cell with literally one arm. From the top, Starr celebrated for the thousands in the stands, and the hundreds of thousands watching on PPV. The
Twindome seemed like it was SHAKING from noise...As this was happening, Double
R, who had just woken up, and Fab had yet another confrontation in the ring! It quickly spilled to the outside, as the two went at it like never before. They didn't know Starr was on top of the Cell, nor did they care. But then, tragedy struck...Without realizing it, Starr was as close to the edge of the Cell as possible. Fab, determined to get the upper hand, sent Double R FLYING into the outer Cell cage...He hit with such force, that Starr lost his balance. It looked like he was going to recover in time to keep himself from falling, but his adrenaline rush finally ended...The fans gasped, as it looked like a
225 pound dummy was taking the 2-story fall. Starr fell right smack on the announcer's table, shattering it to sawdust.
The announce team pulled the rubble off, as they saw HRS laying there, no movement, with his shoulder/arm bent behind his head in a fashion that even makes contortionists sick. The thousands in the stands had to turn away, as white slivers of bone appeared to have broken through the skin. This didn't stop Razorblade and Fab, who brawled back to the locker rooms. Immediately, a dozen paramedics burst out from the back, as they carefully placed Starr on a stretcher. The crowd again fell silent, as a sober Danny Sundown quietly picked up Starr's belts, and followed the stretcher. Even Chris Osmon and Van Douglas were shaken, as Cara Teal rested her head on Osmon's shoulder. Joey Roberts then picked up the mic...
J. Roberts - Fans...Please keep that brave man in your prayers tonight. That's... That's all I can say. [The camera fades out with a scene of many of the fans quietly bowing their heads. Another picture of Rick Rude flashes, as the screen goes black] http://members.aol.com/ozzbws/CGO.html
