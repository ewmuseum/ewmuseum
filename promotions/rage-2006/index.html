Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

RAGE ran its own storyline out of e-wrestling.com, still active by the time of its surviving pay-per-view, RAGE In Hollywood, on 15 June 2006. Staged from the Staples Center, the broadcast wove celebrity cameos into its own arena footage: Halle Berry ejected mid-show after a confrontation with a security guard, Angelina Jolie and Brad Pitt interviewed backstage on a segment called Cyn City, and Clint Eastwood seated at ringside.

The show opens its own Gauntlet Tag Team Championship program with a title change: the Happy Bastards beat the reigning champions, Perfect Chaos (Zee Jensen and Kron), to take the belts.

Its main event puts RAGE World Champion Apache Assassin inside a five-sided glass structure, the Glass House, against two former partners, Cage and The Christian Enigma, in a match its own color commentator, Daniel Dare, introduces as one where he “expect[s] blood.” The Christian Enigma piledrives Cage through the structure's own glass ceiling near the finish, and Apache Assassin retains the title when neither of his challengers can continue. Dare himself turns on the fallen Cage afterward, grinding his face into the broken glass on camera.

A same-day post points prospective wrestlers to the promotion's own site for applications. No later document survives here to say how the promotion's own run continued.
