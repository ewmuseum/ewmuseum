Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Ultimate Championship Wrestling's surviving broadcast, Friday Night's Main Event for 17 April 1998, opens on a backstage assault — Kuwasi “Black Fist” Shakur, in shackles, breaking free to attack a locker-room rival, Sinister, with a fire extinguisher — before cutting to commentators Jerry Jenkins and Thomas “The Beer” Stein, a team the broadcast introduces as unemployed for six months before this call.

The card's own opener was a battle royal for the UCW North American Tag Team Championships, drawing four teams into the ring at once: Chaotic Rage, Hot Shot Donovan and The Equalizer, The Underground A-Team, and a Canadian pairing managed to the ring by Grapes Gilmour. The surviving document ends with the introductions still underway, before a single fall.

No result, and no later document, survives to say who left that ring with the titles, or how the promotion's run continued afterward.
