PWU — Founding

"The start of an era!"

Introduction Hello everyone I am Eric Cooper. Some of you may know me as Aaron Anderson from MVW and OWC. Or some of you may know me as Christian Hawk from COW. Or even as the "ICON" Eric Cooper from IWC, LAW, PWA, SWF fame. Well I am at running another federation. I am greatly hoping to make this one a success. I just hope you all take the time to read this and please join. -Coop =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- =-=-=- 2. Championships [PWU Heavyweight Championship:] Here in the Philedelphia Wrestling Union, the PWU Heavyweight Championship is the main prize, the top dawg. Rarely defended on the weekly card PWU holds, and to get a crack at the gold, you must prove yourself. The man who holds the belt proves to the people that he's the _man_ of the PWU. [PWU Pennsylvanian Championship:] Second best is not bad. The PWU Pennsylvanian Champion is most of the time recognized as the number one contender for the PWU Heavyweight Championship. This belt is defended every week at the PWU weekly card. If you are able to aquire this championship and be able to hold on it for a long time, you are dubbed "Mr. Endurance" because the competition is _tough_ for the belt. [PWU Tag Team Championship:] Basically, the same goes for these championships with the PWU Heavyweight Championship. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- =-=-=- 3. Cards WEEKLY CARD - Philadelphia Wrestling Union presents one weekly card. It comes to you live every week from Phillys own Electric Factory. PCW presents Saturday Night RIOT EXTRAVANGZA!!! Usually 5-7 live matches and 2 darks. Hosted by PWU's main announcers. PAY PER VIEWS - Once a month. Usually LIVE from the Electric Factory also. Every title on the line, and big feuds are ended or either start up. Usually happen the last weekend of the month. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- =-=-=- [The remainder of this handbook -- generic roleplaying advice, an application template, and the fed's own out-of-character staff-and-contact list -- is withheld from this served excerpt; see the provenance note below.]
