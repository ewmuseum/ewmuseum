EWF -New Fed for Good Roleplayers

29 June 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Looking for a new fantasy wrestling to join? Well check out the EWF. We just opened and are accepting new apllicants. Please check out our web page at: http://members.tripod.com/~Anakin/info.html Though this page may not be as flashy as other league's (at least not yet), you will find the cards to be on time and of high quality. My Vision: This is what I expect out of the EWF, it's handlers and myself. 1) This is a booked fed. Winners will be based upon quality of flashes and development of angles. Don't ask me to let you win cause you won't for a long time. 2) Cards will be sent out on Saturdays. There will be a weekly show consisting of four matches. On every fifth week a PPV supercard will be held. 3) Strategies should be sent to me by friday. I can't promise all strategies will be used but I will try to accomidate eveyone equally. 4) Flashes can be sent out when ever. It's quality not quantity that I'm looking at. I will not be strict about censorship but keep it within reason or I will be forced to take action. Cards: Weekly show will be called EWF Saturday Live. It will consist of four matches and possibly an interview or two. It will be sent out on saturdays. On every fifth week a PPV will be held. It will most likely contain eight to ten matches. Titles: Heavyweight- Must be defended at least once every thirty days. Opponents will be chosen according to currnt angles as well as rankings. North American- Must be defended at least once every thiry days. Matches must be gimmick matches. Opponents will be determined by current angles as well as rankings. Gimmicks will be determined by champion. Cruiserweight- For wrestlers 250 lbs and under. Must be defended at least once every thirty days. Opponents will be chosen according to currnt angles as well as rankings. PPV- Must be defended every PPV. Can only be defended during a PPV. Opponents will be chosen according to currnt angles as well as rankings. Tag Team- Must be defended at least once every thirty days. Opponents will be chosen according to currnt angles as well as rankings. Ranking Point System: All wrestlers will be ranked including champions. Champions may not nessesarrily be ranked on top. Points are earned by defeating a wrestler. Defeating #1 ranked earns you 20 pts, #2 19 pts, #3 18 pts and so on. Bonus points will be awarded for defeating champions(Title Match or not). 5 bonus points for Heavyweight champ and Tag Team champs. 4 bonus points for North American champ, 3 bonus points for Cruiserweight champ and 2 bonus points for PPV champ. If you have any other questions, please visit the website. http://members.tripod.com/~Anakin/info.html
