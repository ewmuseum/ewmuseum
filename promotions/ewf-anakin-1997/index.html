Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

This Extreme Wrestling Federation announced itself on 17 April 1997 in modest terms, its founder saying only that it would start “if I get some people to join,” with results decided by the quality and volume of each member's own roleplays.

By 29 June the promotion had reopened under new management, hosted on a fresh Tripod site and pitched as a booked federation judged strictly on writing: “Don't ask me to let you win cause you won't for a long time.” It planned a weekly four-match show, EWF Saturday Live, with a supercard every fifth week, and a five-title slate — Heavyweight, North American, Cruiserweight, a PPV-only title, and Tag Team — each due for defense every thirty days. A ranking system awarded points for every win, weighted toward whichever champion was beaten, so a wrestler could climb the ladder without ever holding a belt.

Its last surviving post, on 15 July, was still recruiting cruiserweights and a heavyweight or two ahead of a first card announced for 26 July 1997.

No card, roster, or result of this promotion's own survives. Whether that 26 July card was ever held isn't in the surviving record.
