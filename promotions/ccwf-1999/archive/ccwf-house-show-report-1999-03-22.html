CCWF House Show Report (03/23/99)

22 March 1999

---------------------------------------------- ||||||||||||||| ||||||||||| |||||| |||| |||||| |||||||| |||| |||| |||| |||||||||| |||||| |||| ||| ||| ||| |||| ||||||||||||| |||||||| |||||||||||||||| |||| --------------------------------------------- *********************************************************** the Canadian Championship Wrestling Federation ++++++++++ House Shows - 03/23/99 ++++++++++ ************************************************************ ********************************* Central Canadian Wrestling (03/23/99) Maple Leaf Gardens - Toronto, Ont. ********************************** the Toronto Express {J} <vs> the London Lover Boys {J} The match started slow but quickly built itself up as both teams worked the double teams. Back and forth it went with several near falls, until a misque between Roberto and Fredrick of the Lover Boys set them up for the
Toronto Express's Highway to Nowhere (double stunner) and the three.
Results : Winners via pinfall the Toronto Express @ 15:02.58 the Silver Jem <vs> "The Future of Pro Wrestling" Derek Sweger In a high impact contest Jem and Sweger battled back and forth. Jem used an impressive array of high flying tatics tried to keep Sweger down. Sweger took over after Jem missed a corkscrew moonsault. Sweger dominated using suplex variation after suplex variation to plat Jem repeatedly into the mat before locking on the Sweger Scream (texas cloverleaf) for the submission victory.
Results : Winner via submission Derek Sweger @ 14:23.04 Jem "Big J." Moser <vs> Jason "the Jaguar" Richards Without a full roster Jem Moser and Jason Richards volenteered to round out the card in an explosive martial arts match. Moser and Richards quickly went to work on one another using both legal and illegal holds to wear the other down. Showing his expertise richards quickly came back from a near fall and unleashed a devistaing seris of palm and nerve strikes before finishing Moser off with a picture perfect Hooking Round house kick. the rest is history.
Results : Winner via pinfall Jason Richards @ 20:21.01 ******************************** Canadian Pacific Wrestling (03/23/99) GM Place - Vancouver, BC ******************************** the Rocky Mountain Reckers {J} <vs> the Drifters {J} Two power teams tore into each other in a destructive show case of raw power. Back and forth the action went often spilling out to the floor. The
Wreckers took fianl control when they hit Drifter #1 over the head with a chair as the ref's back was turned. a mommnet later they nailed the Wrecker
(powerbomb/neckbreaker combo) and got the easy three.
Results : Winners via pinfall the Rocky Mountain Reckers @ 18:43.00 Willow Jackson {J} <vs> Judge "Dredd" Richards Again the Richards family filled in to keep the fans happy. Judge, kevin's youngest brother stepped into the ring against Willow Jackson.
Willow gave Judge a run he will never forget. Using EVERY part of the ring as a spring board he kept Judge down and off balance with dropkicks, lariets, bull dogs, arm drags and flying tackles. Judge took over when
Willow missed a step on a springboard and fell from the ropes to the mat.
Using pure power Judge pummeled him with slams and brawling shots before attempting for his finisher the Final Judgement (spiked powerbomb) HOWEVER
Willow was one step ahead and as Judge swung him up Willow reversed the motion pivioted about Judge's shoulders and snagged a three with a victory
Roll.
Results : Winner via pinfall Willow Jackson @ 12:13.44 Max <vs> Mac Deciding that the fans weere more important then having a shower Mac and
Max of the Rocky Mountain Reckers did double duty as they came out and TOOK
EACH other on in a classic brawler match. neither really kept the advatage as the action was in and out of the ring resulting in a double count out after about fifteen minutes of pure testoseron action.
Result : DRAW - Double Count Out @ 15:10.10 ************************************************ Northern Territories Wrestling Federation (03/23/99) Northern Arts & Cultural Center, Yellowknife, NWT. ************************************************* the IceBreakers {J} <vs> the Drifters {J} The second pair of the Drifters team tried thier luck in the NTWF against a tough and rough pairing of Lazarus and John A. The match was short and sweet. As Drifter #3 and #4 tried a quick attack the Ice Breakers dropped them with a double spear. Sending Drifter #4 out over the top rope they quickly brutalized Drifter #3 before applying the Berg Buster (double brain buster suplex) for the three.
Result : Winners via pinfall the IceBreakers @ 4:01.00 "Yukon" Jack Roberts {J} <vs> Snowbird {J} Snowbird started off the action fast and furious with his tradmark daredevil style. His onslought lasted maybe five minutes before Roberts caught him during a tope rope cross body. A quick switch and Roberts planted him with the Yukon Slam (inverted running powerslam) for the three.
Results : Winner via pinfall Jack Roberts @ 6:15.49 "Yukon" Jack Roberts <vs> John A. Roberts pulled a double duty as John A decided to challenge him to an impromtu match. Never one to back down Roberts quickly accepted and the two got it on. The action was hard hitting and pianful to watch as John A and
Roberts traded power moves and brawling shots for a good portion of the match. Roberts puleld the win out of nowhere when he suprized the equally big John a with a Dragon Suplex for the three.
