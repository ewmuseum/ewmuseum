CCWF Handbook & Rules

11 February 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
CCWF the Canadian Championship Wrestling federation Over view -- The CCWF is a regional Canadian promotion with three Canadian titles and three divisons. The CCWF is run solely by me ([name withheld]) and will have weekly house show reports done in synop style and a monthly Televison premire show either at the begining of each month or the end.
The CCWF is under contract by the NCWF as a regional fed. The CCWF roster will be participating in NCWF PPV's and our champions will get shots at the
NWCF titles. The CCWF is a mixed fed so men and weomen will compete against each other for ALL the titles. Canadians are tough and women can be just as strong as a man so why not mix it up between them?
The CCWF is also a Hardcore open fed. The owner is a Hardcore fantic and there will be at least one hardcore match a week.
Job openings - Right now I have staff consiting of one person(myself). I am looking for anyone reliable to help out with the writing and booking of matches. I'm looking for people who can comit to at least one match a week, and if I get enough writers I will make all shows pbp formate.
If you have writing experince great if not just as good. You only get xp if someone gives you a chance and if your willing to be reliable (getting matches in on time) then I'll give you that chance.
As for match deadlines. They should be in my mail box by monday moring. In turn I will hopefully be able to give you the next card sechedual at that time so you have a week to write it. In most matches it will be the writer's choice on who wins, but if I feel someone should win in a particular match I will let you know when I assign the match. How they win is up to the writer.
Shows -- The CCWF will be run mainly in synop style. The wrestler inteviews and introductions will be written out before each match. The shows on the weekly baisis will be out either Monday or Tuesday, depending on the day off I have from work that week.
Challenges and interviews/flashes are due the Firday before the show is to be aired. I must stress that even though the formate is synop flashes will play a major part in winning matches. Strats for matches are not required by if you want to right one I will incorperte it into the match. This is a booked league.
Once a month the CCWF will host a Televised program. This is sort of our main event for each month. These shows will be done in play by play style, and will most often feature CCWF title defenses. The comitators for these shows are owner Kevin "the Cobra" Richards and his uncle Brook Beauti.
The shows will ALL be done in Notebook formate. Divisions -- There are three divisons in the CCWF. With a possible fourth ebing added if I have enough support. Each one has a title to crown the champion and each champion will have the chance to face the NCWF champion of that division during the NCWF joint PPV's.
CCWF Heavyweight Mens Champion - This division is for anyone in the fed but is strctured for Men weighing in excess of 220 pounds. The title will be once a month usually on the
Televised show. But the champion can defend more often if they wish. CCWF LightHeavy weight Men's Champion This division is structured for those wrestlers wieghing 220 lbs or less. Again the title will be defended once a month, or at the champion's descretion. CCWF Ladies Champion - For women only. With women's wrestling slowly rising back into the main stream there is only one title available for them until the CCWf gains enough of a roster to warrent the insatement of second ladies title.
CCWF Canadian Tag Champions - This division is for tag teams, IT IS ALSO MIXED!!! Players can create a team of thier own or pair up with another player to creat one. A team can be made up of one of the following combinations :
two men one man & one woman two women However it is done tag teams must have a current tag sheet in my files to qualify for the division. again the title is schedualed for one defense a month but can be more if the champions wish.
Canadian Hardcore Champion -- This is an open title. That means if I have enough men & ladies to make up a roster of at least eight hardcore wrestlers then I will establish this as a sacntioned title.
Note on Titles : The Champion is required to decide who they wish to defend agianst and when they wish to defend the title. The minimuim is once a month, but the champion can also defend upto once a week if they feel inclined to. Please note this however. If a champion fails to pick a challenger from at least the top five in his division for thier monthly title defense they will be stripped of the title. The Champion can accept challenges from other players not ranked in the top five HOWEVER the Champ MUST defend against a top five ranked opponent once a month to keep thier title.
Hardcore Policy -- This fed is Hardcore friendly, so Hardcore wrestlers can get just about anytype of match they would like here. BUT to get a hardcore match those involved MUST AGREE on the match style and stipulations before it is schedualed. I do this for one reason. Injuries WILL happen in Hardcore matches and therefore those players involved will end up with varing times
"on the shelf" due to these matches. It is unfair to put another's player on the shelf if they don't wnat him to be. So therefore those people participating in thses matches must agree to the chance of injuriy caused by whatever stipluations they come up with.
Those players who wish to open up the Hardcore division (If I have enough interest) will be bound by that decision when I start schedualling matches for the divsion. This means if I have enough players to warrent a seperate
HD then I will set stipulations under the assumption that the players will abide by the injuires incured by them. NOT all Hardcore matches will end in serious injury. More often then not it will result in stiches and no shelf time. BUT you have to be prepared for the worst cause after goin headfirst through a table after being hit with a powerbomb off the apron you wont be getting back in the ring TOO soon.
I WILL however not setup or write in career ending injuries unless a player asks me to do so. All injuries I create will have a short shelf time before the playerb can comeback.
Flash Policy -- There WILL be no Racial, Homophobic or predujical statements allowed. I get a flash that I think pushes this line and you'll get one warning.
Questionable statements I will ask you about, before decideing what I intend to do. Swearing should be censored. Meaning if you want you man to swear place it a <BLEEP> over the word.
I really have nothing against swearing but this shows is setup to be for a regional area and under that premise we have to consider that tv censors will be watching for inappropiate language. Again just put a <BLEEP> where you want to swear and keep going. You don't need to find words to replace what you want to say. Just cover the swearing and we'll get the picture.
Run ins & Back stage Attacks -- No problem with these. Just keep in mind I wont be injuring other players due to them. This is a great way to get heat for fueds and to keep it up.
For a run in or and attack just email me with what you want to do and when. I'll take care of the rest. Match Strats -- When you want to you can send in a match strat telling me how you man wants to wrestle this match. It dose not have to be long all it has to have is how you want to wrestle offensivly (ie do you want to be mainly aerial when your attacking, or technical) and how you want fight defensivly (ie you wnat to counter your opponents technical skills with brawling, etc.)
You can list some example moves you want to do which would be great. also tell me how long you want to try and make the match last (ie you want to push the match for 20 minutes or you wnat to go for the quick 3 minute pin.). Please note LighHeavies can wrestle longer then Heavies. Also in style wise technical men/submission artists are better at going long matches then say martial artists/brawlers. Keep this in mind when you send in a strat, that is if you do.
Challenges -- Challenges are due the Friday a WEEK before the show that you want to be on is aired. For a challenge just email me with who you wnat to challenge and what and if any the match stipulations you would like for the match. These
MUST be in to me a WEEK in advance for me to have time to setup the match. Deadlines -- All flashes, match strats, and run ins MUST be in to me by the Firday before the show in order to be included for the show. I decide who is winning what match on Saterday and Sunday so if I don'rt have it by Friday night or possibly Saterday afternoon I wont take it into consideration when writing up, the match.
Updates -- I will send out the next card as well as updated rankings, injury lists and who has left or just joine dthe league on the same night or the day after I send out the cards. Rankings will take in your win/lose records along with yoru activity in the league.
The card I send out is prelimnary one. It means I can be changed and will most likely be changed by the time the card is put out. The challenges made last Friday will appear on this "card" if they have been accepted.
Roster -- The roster I plan is fairly small about 20 singles and 10 to 12 tag teams. The complete rosters will be listed with each update as I will rank everyone involved in the league for the divisions in which they fit. Tag teams will be listed also as singles unless the player specifically lists them as tag team only.
Please note I am looking for original characters that have yet to WIN ANY major title in any federation. Newbies are more then welcome.
The roster limits do not include the two jobber teams and the four jobber singles I will create for the league. Jobbers will be listed with a {J} after thier name & win loss record on the updates and roster.
Creation -- Creation is simple. Just follow the sheet (please make sure you fill in everything) and your in. I need a singles sheet for both members of a tag team and the tag sheet in order to accept them. Tag teams as mentioned above will be listed in the singles divisons as well UNLESS you mark them as TAG
TEAM ONLY. * When you pick your Trade mark moves please leave out the basic moves everyone knows. You choice of wrestling styles will tell me what the baiscs your character is good at. These moves should be the speacial moves that are near finisher style but don't quite put your opponent away.
Example - Goldberg's Spear, Six Pac's Bronco Buster, HHH's chin buster, Bret's elbow off the second rope, etc . . . Tag Examples - Hart Foundation's powerslam onto an opponent, Too Hot's Atomic Leg Drop, the Quebecer's Slingshot cannonball, etc . . . Wrestling Styles w/ thier atvatages - Brawling -- a strong vertical style. Can handle most match style. Most at home on the floor, or in a straight out brawl.
Martial arts -- quick knockdown power, most able to get the quick wins/kos in matches. Have good stamina and pain tolenrance and depending on the martial arts may have extensive knowlege in submission.
Power -- mid style. Can last a good match and dish out a tremendous amout of punishment. Aerial -- faced past action you like to move and keep moving. Trenedous stamina but have a problem with giving up safty for high risk which can lead to an upset.
Submisson -- ground work is thier speacialty. They work on single body parts preping for a final submission hold to get the win. Fairly skilled they rely on a mothodical weardown style then trying for straight knowdown punsihment.
Technical - an all around style. Skilled in ground work and aerial work. They use the ring for what it is ment to be, taking your opponent apart. They can use submissions, brawling, some aerial and some power moves, but they rely heavily on basic moves to setup more moves, into pining combos when ready..
Hardcore -- A dangerous style but exciting to watch. In normal matches this high pain tolerance style helps push the match long. But the lack of acceptance for rules often causes Dq as a hardcore wrestler is quite happy nailing people with chairs as they are with punches or suplexs.

Possibly If I get the real hang of it or I find someone to run the page for me their will be listsings of upcoming cards as well as title linages.
