[Excerpt from The Seattle Times] (SEATTLE, WA) It all begins the night of September 29, 2003. The community has been buzzing with the rumors and plans of the often times ecentric, local entrepenuer, and now owner of the Ultimate Revolutionary
Wrestling, Anthony Stone. Stone has made it very clear to the public, that URW will not only make money for Seattle, but "URW is here to give this place what it needs! SOME ENTERTAINMENT!"
With the URW Arena being completed a month ago, Anthony Stone now brings his exciting mixture of local talent and (inter)national stars into the wrestling ring. According to many wrestling "insiders", this is quite the list of superstars and promises to be one of the more promising promotions to come along in a while.
The URW Arena, a former glass factory, was not really "completed" but remodeled and redone in preparation for the hard hitting action on its way to the rainy city. The building seats approximately 1,500 wrestling fans and is actually a good facility according to the owner: "You know, most leaugues that just start out, they may be able to grab a building that can fit 600 or so on a good day and they're just _renting_ the place. Not here, URW Arena is the mecca of the
Revolution and this building is owned by yours truly."
Anthony Stone invites the Seattle community to attend the URW's inaugural brawl this Monday Night.
As the fans grow eager with every passing day, and the hype draws to close,
Monday Night, Ultimate Revolutionary Wrestling is here.
