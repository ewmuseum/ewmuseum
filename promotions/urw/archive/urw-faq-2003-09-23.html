-=URW=- We've got high hopes...

Sep 23, 2003, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
URW ULTIMATE REVOLUTION WRESTLING "THE REVOLUTION IN WRESTLING" -TABLE OF CONTENTS- 1. "Introduction to a Revolution..." 2. FEATURES 3. URW CHAMPIONSHIPS 4. URW PROGRAMMING 5. URW STAFF 6. GUIDELINES 7. CONTRACTS 8. APPLICATION INTRODUCTION URW is exactly what the name tells you. This is The Ultimate
Revolution in Wrestling. We are different.
Imagine if you will a federation that is packed with 50+ hard-working dedicated members. Just imagine what that would be like. There's constantly someone to feud with. There are actual rankings to climb. The titles MEAN something to hold because if you manage to grab on to one, you've EARNED it.
You are on top of a true mountain. This is the inevitable goal of URW. What we offer here in URW is a spirit... A spirit that combines fierce competition with incredible action and always the sense of the unexpected. In this day and age in e-wrestling it is hard to find something different, something truly great. Well, URW hopes to shatter what you think of e-wrestling and how it should be. The name says it all.
It is the goal of URW to bring this sense of excitement, adventure, and the sense of being a part of something bigger and greater to you each and every week. URW will be great because it will be incredibly detailed but it will also offer features you won't find anywhere else.
URW has INCREDIBLY detailed cards. The shows in URW are non-stop action. All in-ring interviews, backstage happenings, and all angle developments are a surprise until they happen, and the show is FULL of surprises and full of the unexpected.
URW will change what you think of e-wrestling... FEATURES NON-STOP ACTION - I can't stress enough how URW will feel like a living breathing entity in itself. The cards will lend a feeling of anticipation, excitment, conquest and more. The URW will soon be incredibly full. There will always be someone to talk to, and always action to look forward to.
CONTRACTS - URW, in an effort to establish a more realistic sense of professional wrestling, offers a contract system. Each new-comer to URW is offered a standard one month contract. Once this is up, if you made good on your opportunity, you'll be offered a new contract extension. Break your contract and your wrestler will be jobbed out the remaining length, fulfill your contract and recieve a MONEY bonus. "Empty" contracts can even be bought by other wrestlers...
MONEY SYSTEM - Money intertwines with the win/loss rankings system in
URW to give you an idea of where you are at. But money makes the world go
'round in URW it is no exception. Money will allow you to buy things in URW to increase your stature and significance amongst the rest. Of the many features that can be bought, things like logos, websites and even entire SHOWS in your honor can be bought. Not to mention, other wrestlers contracts can be bought!
More information on this upon entry to URW. AFTERSHOCK NEWSLETTER - URW offers a weekly Newsletter entitled AFTERSHOCK. Aftershock will keep you updated with all the goings on in the federation. Rumor mill, recaps, ratings, weekly awards interviews with URW stars, and the upcoming match listings are all featured in AFTERSHOCK. Also in
AFTERSHOCK are the rankings...
RANKINGS SYSTEM - Win/loss record, role-playing and money all play an integral role in URW's steamin' hot Rankings system. URW plans on being an incredibly huge federation and it is important for bookers and wrestlers to have an idea of where everyone is at. Rankings also consider longevity and experience (as does URW in general).
URW TELEVISION RATINGS - Television Ratings you ask? Oh yes. URW, once again, strives for a level of detail and realism you won't find anywhere else.
This is a cool feature that allows everyone to see the progress of the federation as it grows. I actually have no control over the ratings whatsoever and they will move according to the handlers. More info on this feature upon entry to URW.
AWARDS - Recognition in this game is important. That's why URW offers a series of weekly awards in Aftershock. URW will also have monthly awards that get voted on by the federation that have a monetary benefit.
URW CHAT ROOM - In keeping with the feel of a 24 Hour feeling of the federation, URW will have it's own private chat for it's members. Once we get as big as planned, this room should almost always be occupied with other members to talk to.
TITLE LINEAGE - Titles in URW mean something. If you win a title in URW you will forever go down in history as a champion. Our histories, like everything else, are highly detailed. Speaking of titles...
CHAMPIONSHIPS In URW, Championships are highly prestigious. URW belts means you are a top star in the league. Despite some titles being on a higher level then others, holding a title means you are a top star. For example, the TV Champion has more stroke then the #1 Contender for the Universal because he actually has a belt. URW will only start out with three of these titles and will add more as roster size allows.
URW UNIVERSAL TITLE - The Grand daddy of them all. If you manage to conquer all to capture this title, you have earned your title as the greatest wrestler alive. You are truly the king of the mountain.
URW INTERNATIONAL TITLE - URW's secondary title. Hold this title and you are one of the premiere stars in all of wrestling.
URW UNITED STATES TITLE - The US title is URW's third singles title and like the International title, shows you are a stand out performer and potentially one of the best.
URW TELEVISION TITLE - The Television Title is for the working man's wrestler. This title is defended more often then any other title and is held by those that can endure.
URW SUICIDE TITLE - Going after the Suicide Title is just that... SUICIDE. This championship is for the craziest and most extreme competitors in URW. All matches will feature some crazy gimmick that will test a wrestlers pain treshold and their very limits. Only the insane need apply.
URW WORLD TAG-TEAM TITLES- These titles are for the best two man tandem in all of the land. The Tag-team titles will be some of the most exciting feuds in
URW and will share the high status of titles such as the International and
Universal.
More titles will be included as the roster size permits... Six Man Titles, The
Submission Ring and more...
URW PROGRAMMING Once again, angle developments, action, and drama are what drive URW shows. This has been a long forgotten idea in the realm of e-wrestling and URW promises to change that. What URW promises ... IT DELIVERS.
URW MONDAY NIGHT REVOLUTION The hottest and most exciting two hours on television! REVOLUTION is is extreme to the hilt with greatest stars in wrestling stepping toe to toe.
5-8 live matches with dark match recaps featuring URWs hot new stars. This action is fast and furious and ALWAYS filled with surprises.
URW AFTERSHOCK URW feels the Aftershock directly after REVOLUTION. Aftershock is a bulletin sent out in the form of a newsletter highlighting the weeks event, recapping all the tumultuous action of the night before and gets you ready for the week ahead.
More programs will be created once the roster size requires it. PPVs in URW are an _extremely_ important event. In order to keep the prestige of these events, they are held every other month. In their place,
REVOLUTION will feature a bigger then average show on those months that don't feature a PPV.
URW STAFF Head Honchos URW CEO and President - Anthony Stone The mind behind the madness. Anthony Stone is the creator of URW and drives with a unmatched passion. Some say Stone is genious, other say he's obsessed, whatever the case Stone makes the final calls when it comes to URW.
URW Commissioner, Head of Talent Recruiting - Orlando Robinson Orlando Robinson is long time friends with Stone. Ever since their days together in past federations, the two have been close. Orlando oversees most of the smaller daily happenings of the federation, is head of talent recruiting, and holds a seat in the booking committee.
Orlando Robinson is a big, black man, a former weightlifter and wrestling champion. A very dapper individual, he always likes to look his best and dress for every occasion. His short fuse harkens back to his wrestling days and he's not always the calm, smooth, guy you'd see walking around in the office.
Orlando, now semi-retired, perfers to stay out of the spotlight as much as he can and keep it where it belongs, on the wrestlers.
URW Announcers - Felix Rose - Felix is the main announcer for URW programming. A former Pac-10 collegiate football and basketball announcer has brought his skill to the URW. Felix is no rookie to the world of wrestling, however, as he has been announcing for the past five years in various, smaller promotions.
Felix prefers the good guys, or the babyfaces, but being the professional he is, tries his best to call things straight down the middle. You can see him on
Monday Night Revolution.
- Stevie T. Dee - A loudmouth if there ever was one, Stevie is the dysfunctional glue that holds the two man Revolution team together. Stevie T. Dee wears the craziest outfits, and brightest clothes. He prefers the bad guys, or heels and will certainly let you know it. He can be seen on URW Monday Night Revolution.
- "Lethal" Larry Cobalt - "Lethal" Larry is a long time wrestler, in his late forties. He brings to the announcing table 20 years of wrestling experience and knowledge. Larry, a throwback to the good ole days, always prefers a great technical contest.
"The Lethal One" called it quits last year at the tender age of 47. A very proud man, he's a great addition to the announce team. He can be seen as on occasional guest filling in for Stevie T. Dee or Felix Rose on URW broadcasts.
URW Interviewers - Lindsey Florentino - If there are any backstage happenings or a wrestler feels the need to speak, the adorable Lindsey Florentino is there. If you ever need an interview to be done, there's only one woman for the job. :)
- Michael Bay - Only 22, and straight out of college, Michael Bay is a wrestling fan at heart, but somewhat new to the scene. However, Anthony saw potential in this young kid, and Michael is one to take the ball and run with it.
URW Ring Announcer - Arnold Myers Those closest to him call him Arnie. Arnie is in his late thirties and is showing signs of male pattern baldness as you can tell with his constant changing of comb-overs, toupees, and Rogaine treatment. Despite being only
5'6", Arnie, with his deep voice, does a great job announcing the superstars of
URW. URW Timekeeper - Julius Jackson Some say he has been weird ever since losing the Ring Announcer job to Arnie
Myers. Whatever the case may be, Julius is as annoying as can be. Always trying to elevate his status and garner attention, Julius Jackson gets into the spotlight wherever he can. In fact, he likes to call himself "The Worlds Most
Famous Timekeeper".
URW Head of Security - Jim Johnston A former independent wrestler, sidelined with a career-ending neck injury, Jim
Johnston and his ever-ready staff are always there when a fight gets too out of control in or out of the ring.
URW Referees Kevin "Swift" Justice - Always in tip top shape, and giving a 100%, this is the usual Main Event referee and URW's best ref.
Mark Ellis - A good ref, who always calls it straight down the middle. "Road" Gill Jones - Gill is URW's oldest ref at the age of 62, and although, not the fastest, or the most in shape, or even the most able to see, Gill does his best.
GUIDELINES 1. You will lose here at some point in URW. I tell you that now, so that we can weed out whiners and not waste anybodies time. 2. Role-Playing in URW is an open system. Meaning, a mailing list is sent out, and you can reply to all. There is a 3 RP Limit for our weekly shows and
5 for the PPVs. There is no line limit in URW. Why? Because URW is out for the best... We're out for people who know what they're doing and don't need a line minimum to worry about. Keep in mind, in URW QUALITY will ALWAYS win out over length. Quality of interviews, just like in real wrestling, is far more entertaining then watching drawn out snooze fests.
3. SPAM MAIL, & TOSing will result in termination of your URW contract and you will be immiediatly black listed from URW. No unauthorized spam mail will be sent on a URW list.
3. NO TOSING. Hopefully, nothing will ever come to this, but if it does, come to me first. My IMs are almost always available (except when writing cards) and I'm always willing to talk.
4. Get involved! E-wrestling isn't just about wins and losses. Angle's are very important here in URW. Feuds are incredibly fun and with the URW pace of excitement they will be something to remember. Get invovled and if you need ideas, the door is always open for some booking discussions :-)
CONTRACTS Every prospective signee is given a 1 month contract. During this one month contract you can bow out at any time without any jobbing or black listing involved after the 1 month period. Leave before the contract is up and any and all matches you were scheduled for will be jobbed out. Should you leave on good terms, there will be no black listing and you are welcome to come back at a later time.
After the one month, your contract will be up for review and lengthier deal will be provided. Depending on your value to the leaugue, a contract will be made accordingly. Fulfilled contracts will provide monetary benefits once they are fulfilled and up for review again.
The One Month Contract includes at least one on screen appearance every two weeks (more with more participation) and can be terminated at any time by either side. If you break one of the URW guidelines, you are subject to contract termination.
APPLICATION URW accepts original wrestlers. You can have up to two wrestlers. Either one tag-team or two singles. You'll see questions in here may not have filled out in an application before. That is because in URW we want you to get to know your character on a higher level now.
Once your application is reviewed and accepted you'll be notified with a standard two-week opening contract. If URW is currently full, you'll be notified of your placing on the waiting list.
Also, we're only looking original creations. Don't expect to get in with "Stone Cold" Steve Texas , "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn McMichael or "The
Hitman" Bert Hart.
