URW Monday Night Revolution: The Inaugural Brawl (Part 1 of 4)

Sep 29, 2003, 12:00:00 AM

It's finally here... URW ULTIMATE REVOLUTIONARY WRESTLING ~~~~~MONDAY~~~~~ ~~~~NIGHT~~~~~ ~REVOLUTION~ ________________________________ URW Monday Night Revolution Attendance: 800 Location: Outside the URW Arena Time: 9:00 PM PST 9/29/03 LIVE on The American Sports Network ["Come With Me" by Puff Daddy and Jimmy Page begins to blast through your speakers. We open to a shot soaring through the orange and purple clouds of a beautiful sunset. The shot begins to move from the clouds, and we're able to see down below. We see a desolate, war-ravaged field. The once bright green grass is now just a dead brown as empty foxholes and abandoned war vehicles dot the landscape.]
[ The figure of a man walking off into the orange horizon is seen. The camera begins to soar, getting closer and closer to this man. As the view gets nearer, we see this man as a soldier. He is wearing a green and black pair of fatigues, a helmet, with a pack slung over his shoulder and a gun slung over the other. ]
[The camera gets up of his face. The solider looks intensely off into the distance. A new scar adorns his cheek. He knows his purpose, he knows his duty.... He knows his destiny. ]
#UH HUH, YEAH# [The scene explodes as the music picks up. A shot of a torn up, bloody, red white and silver American flag slowly flies across the screen. The words
"ULTIMATE REVOLUTIONARY WRESTLING" fade on to the screen.]
#Hear my cries,Hear my call# #Lend me your ears, See my fall# [ A clip of The Sliencer hitting The Assault on some poor jobber.] #See my error, Know my faults# #Time halts, See my loss # [ A clip of "Playboy" Emerson Favreau hitting the Playboy Special. ] #Know I'm lacking , Backtracking# #Where I met you# [ A shot of Jimmy Michaels hitting a devastating Kiss of Death moonsault on his opponent.] #Pistol packing, Itchy finger# #Trigger happy, Try to trap me# [A clip of "The Legend" Joel Johnson flexing his pectorals with a smirk on his face runs across the screen. ]
#Close your eyes# #COME WITH ME# [The flag reappears, slowly flying onto the screen. A few words form on to the screen.] _Monday_ _Night_ __REVOLUTION!__ [ The scene opens to outside of the URW Arena in downtown Seattle, Washington on an usual bright and sunny spring day. The world famous Space Needle and the rest of Seattle's buildings offer a nice surrounding. A ring has been set up in the middle of the street, and roadblocks have been set, not allowing traffic to enter the large area. A large crowd of fans, approximately 800, have come to see the first ever broadcast of Ultimate Revolutionary Wrestling. Most are sitting in chairs provided by URW, while the rest are standing. Guardrails have been set up, around the ring and a broadcast table sits a few feet away from the ring. The camera pans the crowd, some of the fans are holding up signs like
"WELCOME TO THE NIGHTMARE!!!" "ROCHESTER ROCKS!" "BE MY PLAYBOY!" "FRANCHISE
THIS!" "Come With Me" by P. Diddy slowly fades. A makeshift entry aisleway has been set up, with a smaller version of the URW Videowall above the entrance.
Cut back to the commentators, Felix Rose and the one and only Stevie T. Dee. ] FELIX ROSE: Well, ladies and gentlemen, the URW has finally lifted off, and boy oh boy are we excited! I mean, tonight at the debut outside the URW arena here in Seattle, Washington, we have a literal standing room only crowd here of about 800 crazed fans. The atmosphere is indescribable, isn't that right,
Stevie?
STEVIE T. DEE: Rose, you couldn't be more right! And if ya didn't know, the reason we're _outside_ the URW arena tonight, is because of the damn Fire
Marshall, but don't worry, we'll be in doors next week! But Kiddies, if you're looking for the hottest new joint in Seattle today, hell, in WRESTLING today, look no further than URW Arena! We've got everything ya want, nothing ya don't! And the one and only SHAWN JOHNSON!
FELIX ROSE: Not only that, but tonight we have an INCREDIBLE line-up for you fans. To start us off we'll be seeing The Enforcer take on the man known only as phYre! After that, we'll see, "Playboy" Emerson Favreau take on High Voltage !
STEVIE T. DEE: That should be an amazing encounter between two guys who can turn up the heat! And speaking of heat, I don't think there's anyone else on the URW roster attracting as much fireworks as The Playboy Emerson Favreau!
FELIX ROSE: Then, URW's first Triple Threat match will see "The New Sh*t" Tommy
Owens going toe-to-toe with Mavis and "Loverboy" Jimmy Michaels. The action is gonna be fast and furious to be sure!
STEVIE T. DEE: But howabout that Main Event, Rosie! FELIX ROSE: That's right! A Seattle Streetfight contest between "The Franchiser" Shawn Johnson and "The Anybody Killa" James Lowery. You do _not_ want to miss that one! Oh.... we're getting word that, The Enforcer is just arriving to the arena...
STEVIE T. DEE: He's got a match in ten minutes! FELIX ROSE: Michael Bay is with The Enforcer! Take it away! [The Enforcer arrives outside the URW arena hopping out a nice limo, as he comes to a stop in the parking lot a camera crew approaches him about an interview. He opens the doors and tell them to get out of his way. He walks around to the back of the jeep and gets his bags and slams the door. The
Enforcer has a slight smile on his face as he walks to a room that says "THE
ENFORCER" on it in bright bold letters. The Enforcer opens it up and there is a
_HUGE_ stock of metal steel chairs. At least a hundred in this room. The
Enforcer smiles and tosses his bag in the room, he goes to leave until Michael
Bay stops him for an interview..]
MICHAEL BAY: Enforcer I was wondering if we could get a few words from you before we go live on your match tonight and being a part of the birth of the
URW? THE ENFORCER: You want a few words, I got a few words for you. How about getting the hell out of my face. You want a few more words, tell you what I'll do.
[The Enforcer stands up and grabs the reporter around his neck and shoves him into the stack of chair in the room, he looks into the camera and tell the camera man to leave the camera on his way out. The camera man tells The
Enforcer that he cannot leave the camera as the screen fades to black. A few seconds and some kicking and screams later the camera is set up on a chair facing a bench in front of the chairs. The Enforcer sits down on the bench and begins to speak. ]
THE ENFORCER: Before this piss ant Bay left he asked me how I felt about being part of the birth of the URW, my thoughts on that are simple. They are paying me a good chunk of money to come here and kick ass. You see that's what I do, I beat the hell out of people for money. When I got here in URW, I was handed the lineup sheet with tonight matches, I see that I have some guy by the name of
Phyre. Well that's a mighty fancy name you got there, to damn bad I'm gonna have to whip your ass. Phyre I have been in the sport a long time, long enough to know that guys with stupid ass names don't last too long in a tough mans sport. And when it comes to this sport, you _do_ not_ want to piss me off. You see I'm not just The Enforcer of URW, I'm the damn Chairman [he smiles as he gestures to all the chairs in his locker room] And phYre you are not man enough to face me. I am gonna make short work of you, and when I am done I want you to know one thing. It's nothing personal, it's business. Remember I get paid to beat people up, you just happen to be the first person on that list.
URW you have been put on notice that I am here... [The scene fades back to the outside of the URW Arena where Stevie T. Dee and
Felix Rose are sitting ringside.]
STEVIE T. DEE: HA HA! TAKE A SEAT MICHAEL BAY! FELIX ROSE: The Enforcer with some last minute words for everyone' in URW, and when we come back, he'll be taking on Phyre in URW'S FIRST MATCH!!! STAY TUNED!
------------------------------------------------------------- COMMERCIAL BREAK Hey, we've just got a new merchandice! What is it?! DeeZ! DeeZ?!? GET THE DeeZ NuTZ OFFICIAL URW T-SHIRT!!!! For only 24.99! CALL 1-800-ILUV-URW TODAY! ------------------------------------------------------------- [We fade back to ringside.] FELIX ROSE: This is it! Time for our _very_ first match! Take it away Arnie! --------------------------- The Enforcer Vs. Phyre ONE FALL STANDARD MATCH ARNIE MYERS: LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, THIS MATCH IS SCHEDULED FOR ONE FALL. Hailing from Brooklyn, New York, standing at 6'4, and weighing in at 275, ladies and gentlemen The Enforcer!!!!!!!!!!!!!!
[ Natural Born Killerz By Ice Cube & Dr. Dre comes on, then then pyro goes off three times in front of the entrance way in a form of an x. then when the smoke is clearing the enforcer comes out and looks over the crowd. He slowly walks to the ring. When he gets in the ring pyro goes off from each ring corner one right after the other. ]
STEVIE T. DEE: Ya know somethin', Felix? The Mafia have been given such a bad rep over the past 100 years, and that's all the Italians' fault! The Enforcer here is very skilled in the ring, and I think he may bring some honor back to his country tonight in his URW debut!
FELIX ROSE: Ladies and gentlemen, please excuse my colleague, "Mr." Dee, over here. He really doesn't mean to offend anyone.
STEVIE T. DEE: Wait a second, don't put words in my m-- FELIX ROSE: And our sincere apologies go out to our viewers of Italian descent. (To Stevie) I can't believe you just said that on live air-time! STEVIE T. DEE: WHAT?! The Mafia certainly didn't dishonor themselves, it had to be the.........
[Just then, phYre steps from behind the curtain covering the backstage area and walks out on to the ramp.  He pays no heed to the fans booing or cheering, as he rolls into the ring.  He re-laces his boots and mentally prepares for his opponent, then sprints into the ring and starts beating on The Enforcer. ]
STEVIE T. DEE: Ahhhhh, look at the look on Arnie's face! He didn't even get to do his job! Poor kid! FELIX ROSE: Kid? He's forty something years old! Well, it looks like Stevie's a little pre-occupied with the out-of-ring happenings here, so I guess I'll be doing the announcing on my own.
[Phyre is pummeling the slightly smaller Enforcer in the corner. He whips The
Enforcer into the opposite corner with tremendous force...... The Enforcer stumbles out and gets nailed with an Angry Mans Clothesline!]
FELIX ROSE: Ooooooooooh! Somebody needs to check to see if The Enforcer's head is still on his shoulders! That was one helluva clothesline!
STEVIE T. DEE: Well, ya know, this here Phyre kid's a Chee-Ka-Goan himself... FELIX ROSE:Yeah...? STEVIE T. DEE: Yeah. FELIX ROSE: I can't believe you studied for a match beforehand. STEVIE T. DEE: I didn't. I read it on accident. FELIX ROSE: [frustrated sigh] Whatever the case, phYre nearly decapitated his opponent. [By now, Phyre has pulled The Enforcer to his feet. He whips him into the ropes. The Enforcer ducks a big boot and comes back with a devastating SPEAR! The fans actually seemed somewhat impressed by the move as it took PhYre straight off his feet and straight across the ring.]
STEVIE T. DEE: WHOA! Phyre was nearly split in half! FELIX ROSE: You're not kidding! That was amazing explosion with that spear! [After that spear, Phyre slowly rises to his feet. The Enforcer runs into the opposite ropes and delivers a shoulder block to Phyre's chest, which staggers the man but doesn't knock him down. The Enforcer tries the same thing again with the same result. Phyre's turn now as he runs off the ropes and attacks with a hurricanrana, but as he was flipping through The Enforcer's legs, The
Enforcer doesn't fall with him, but rather holds on to Phyre's legs, gets him up in a big powerbomb position, then flips him over his head, with Phyre landing gut-first across the top rope! The crowd is stunned by this awesome display of strength. .]
STEVIE T. DEE: Someone bring out the oxygen tank! The Enforcer just dropped the meatball right across our top ring rope! I get "Das Gooseboompos" just looking
at that! FELIX ROSE: Phyre is just squirming on the top-rope, seemingly gasping for air! [The Enforcer takes Phyre's dangling legs, picks him up until he's vertical, then springboards him off of the top-rope and suplexes him onto his upper back!
Another very impressive move shown here! Phyre is rolling around the ring clutching his neck. He eventually takes the low road and rolls to the outside.
There, he gets up gradually as The Enforcer boasts to the crowd, which actually receives a pop. The Enforcer is back on the attack. Phyre at this time is on the ring apron, recooperating. The Enforcer grabs him from the inside of the ring and sets him up for a vertical suplex. Phyre is up... he flips it around..... LOW BLOW to The Enforcer! The Enforcer hunches over as phYre points to the fans.]
STEVIE T. DEE: Ah yes, the ultimate equalizer! I don't care how big you are, if you're hit in the ole' "meat and two-bits", you're gonna feel it... and prolly the day after too!
FELIX ROSE: Well, Phyre may need to take the low road to get an advantage over this ruthless man. I'm sure he's still feeling those two creative maneuvers by
Enforcer from earlier on!
STEVIE T. DEE: Sh[BLEEP]t, son, _I_ CAN STILL FEEL THOSE MOVES! Those were downright spectacular! [The Enforcer finally stands up somewhat right next to the ropes, so Phyre decides to take a risk, he leaps onto his shoulders from behind, spins around to the front, and HEAD SCISSOR'S HIM OVER THE TOP ROPE AND TO THE FLOOR! The
Enforcer took full impact of that blow!]
FELIX ROSE: Ya know, Stevie, if you want to talk about spectacular moves, I think you'd better mention that Chicago native in the same sentence too! This guy is a bonified daredevil!
STEVIE T. DEE: Don't count him out just yet, when you fight a guy like The
Enforcer, you go in to the match knowing you're gonna have to pull out all the stops. And Phyre has got the fire, and if you ask me, one of these crazy moves he pulls off can take out anyone on the URW Roster!
[Phyre gets back into the ring from the ring apron where he landed, where he looks out at his opponent to see that The Enforcer is already starting to get back up! You can see the blood inside Phyre boiling with frustration, as he runs into the opposite ropes... leaps to the top rope... and SUICIDE DIVE ONTO
THE ENFORCER. Wait! THE ENFORCER CAUGHT HIM! Enforcer has Phyre by the arms.
Enforcer rams Phyre's back into the steel ring post! Phyre shrieks out in pain! But The Enforcer hasn't dropped him yet! He turns Phyre vertically and
.... TOMBSTONE PILEDRIVER ON THE OUTSIDE!]
STEVIE T. DEE: OH NO! Someone, quick, get a EMT, Phyre needs go to a hospital now! Someone get him outta there!
FELIX ROSE: Well, I guess you could say that you were a little off on that prediction, eh Stevie?
STEVIE T. DEE: HAVE YOU KNOW COMPASSION, FR? You are a cold, heartless man,
Felix Rose! Your mother should be ashamed of you!
FELIX ROSE: Whatever you say, chief. [The Enforcer rolls Phyre's limp body back into the ring. He dives under the ropes. The Enforcer grabs Phyre, as the crowd appears to be cheering him on somewhat! Then he whips Phyre to the ropes and htis DEVASTATING spinebuster!
The Enforcer goes for the cover.] FELIX ROSE:It's academic from here ladies and gentlemen! REF GILL JONES: 1!!!! 2!!!! 3!!!! DING DING DING!!!!!!!! MYERS: THE WINNER OF THIS MATCH... THE ENFORRRRRRRRRRRCER!!!!! FELIX ROSE: Your winner, this heartless man, The Enforcer! STEVIE T. DEE: The winner of URW's first _EVER_ match no less! FELIX ROSE: Wait just a second.... We are NOT done here! [The Enforcer looks at Phyre with a sinister grin and rolls out of the ring. Phyre begins to stir, recovering from the spinebuster. The Enforcer grabs a steel chair from underneath the ring.]
STEVIE T. DEE: Uh oh. This could be bad... [The Enforcer gets back in the ring and waits for Phyre to get back up...Phyre does and ...]
THWACKKKKKKKK!!!!!!!!!! FELIX ROSE: A VICSIOUS STEEL CHAIR SHOT FROM THE ENFORCER! STEVIE T. DEE: Holy crap!!!! FELIX ROSE: We're gonna try and get Phyre some medical attention here, Stick with us folks, up next is High Voltage versus "Playboy" Emerson Favreau. We'll be right back!
[Scene cuts to commercial.]
