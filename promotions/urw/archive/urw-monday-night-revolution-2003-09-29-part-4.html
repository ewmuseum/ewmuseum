URW Monday Night Revolution: The Inaugural Brawl (Part 4 of 4)

URW ULTIMATE REVOLUTIONARY WRESTLING ~~~~~MONDAY~~~~~ ~~~~NIGHT~~~~~ ~REVOLUTION~ ________________________________ URW Monday Night Revolution Attendance: 800 Location: Outside the URW Arena Time: 10:45 PM PST 9/29/03 LIVE on The American Sports Network [The scene fades back from commercials to the announcing desk, where
Felix Rose and Stevie T. Dee are looking at the camera. ]
FELIX ROSE: How about that HUGE announcement from Anthony Stone! Next week, here at the URW Arena, the UNIVERSAL Title will be decided in a 20 Man Battle
Royale!
STEVIE T. DEE: Anthony Stone might have a pair of balls afterall, he knows what these fans want and what they've been waiting for, and thats a UNIVERSAL
Champion! Come next Monday Night, the entire URW Roster will be duking it out inside the URW Arena for the ultimate prize in wrestling. The heat has been turned up and URW is begining to sizzle!
FELIX ROSE: No doubt at that, STD, but now Ladies and gentlemen, it is finally time for our MAIN EVENT! Takie it away Arnie!
AM: Hailing from Detroit, Michigan, standing at 6'1", and weighing in at 219 pounds, ladies and gentlemen THA ANYBODY KILLA … JAMES LOWERY!!!!!!!!!!!!!! GANG RELATED [ "Gang Related" by ABK blared over the subwoofers surrounding the stage, the crowd giving off heavy, yet mixed reactions. Out from the back emerged none other than Anybody Killa, himself, clad in that signature Hatchet Man hockey jersey, and a pair of black jeans. His hair was down in the dreads, like normal. A bottle of Faygo held in his hands as he shook it up powerfully, then promptly twisted the cap off, shooting the soda into the crowds. He'd toss the soda bottle right into the audience, fans diving for the remains of it. Those lips tugged up at the sides into a menacing grin as he continued down the the ramp. Lowery kipped up as he reached the ring, hopping up and onto the apron.
He entered the ring, grabbing that Hatchet Man pendant and tossing it into the crowd. A large woman grabs the pendant and goes absolutely wild..
] STEVIE T. DEE: JEESUS, what sewer did that thing crawl out from? FELIX ROSE: I thought that to be a very attractive young woman, STD. STEVIE T. DEE: Well, we all know about your "habits". FELIX ROSE: [Takes a deep breath.] ... How long do they have you under contract for? STEVIE T. DEE: Six years, baby. [Suddenly, "Hey Ya" by Andre Three Thizzle begins to blare over the sound system.] STEVIE T. DEE: YES! "The Franchiser" Shawn Johnson, the new official role-model for today's impressionable young youth, is right here, tonight! [The camera focuses in on the entrance way. A few moments pass, and nothing. Another few moments, the music still playing ... and nothing. ] FELIX ROSE: Hey, what's this?! [ From behind the large mass of fans, a large slick black limosuine screeches up and comes to a quick hault. Jim Johnston (head of security) and the rest of the crackURW security staff begin to part the sea of fans and create a make shift path to the ring from the limo. ]
STEVIE T. DEE: Fashionably late, baby. [ The limo door slowly opens. Out comes Shawn Johnson, slowly with a smirk on his face. He slowly stands up, walk a few feet and stands with his hands on his hips allowing the crowd to admire him.]
STEVIE T. DEE: Now, you see Felix. This is _exactly_ what I'm talking about. Wrestlers like "The Franchiser" are the ones who garner _my_ respect. FELIX ROSE: Well, I'm sure Mr. Johnson can die a happy man now, Steve. [Shawn struts down to the ring, directly through the crowd. He stops at the ring steps, steps half way up, and poses for the fans with his hands around the back of his head. Finally, he steps into the ring.]
Arnie Myers: "Ladies and gentlemen, in the ring at this time, hailing from Las
Vegas, Nevada, weighing in at 240 pounds and standing at 6'8", HERE IS "THE
FRANCHISER" SHAWWWWWN JOOOOOOOOOOOOHNSON! [At that, Shawn stands in the middle of the ring, and mockingly waves to the fans.] STEVIE T. DEE: Look at this guy! A class act all the way. If I had kids --who lived with me-- I would make sure they someday lead good, productive lives by having them look up this good ole American, Shawn Johnson. FELIX ROSE: He's one of the most arrogant men in URW right now, and he will have his hands full tonight with "The Anybody Killa" James Lowery!
[DING!!! DING!!! DING!!!] FELIX ROSE: Here we go with our main event for this evening! Remember, folks, this is a big opportunity. All eyes are on this one as we get ready for that big Unviersal Title Battle Royale next week!
[ The two men slowly walk up to each other. They are soon face-to-face, toe-to-toe. James Lowery, looking as intense as ever says a few words to Shawn
Johnson. A frown grows on Johnson's face as he looks out to the audience. ]
[SLAPPPP!] FELIX ROSE: Oh man! Johnson lays a literal smack down right across Lowery's face! James quickly strikes back with a right fist that staggers Johnson.
Another! And another that sends Johnson flying! ["The Franchiser" stumbles and finds himself in the turnbuckle.] STEVIE T. DEE: The "Anybody Killer" with a _HUGE_ clothesline in the corner! FELIX ROSE: Lowery wraps his arms around the waist of Sean Johnson and slams him down to the mat with a belly-to-belly suplex.
[Lowery rises to his feet, and shouts profanity to the Seattle crowd.] FELIX ROSE: Lowery starts to put the boots to Johnson! STEVIE T. DEE: Just stomping away with some well placed, boots to the chest of
Shawn J! ["Anybody Killa" now gaining momentum, drops an elbow across the neck of Shawn
Johnson. The brawler Lowery lifts his opponent to his feet and _levels_ him with a DDT! ]
FELIX ROSE: Lowery is now implementing a methodical pace, keeping this match at his speed. A wise decision when you're facing someone as quick and agile as
Shawn Johnson.
STEVIE T. DEE: Good point, _but_ Lowery is known for a fast pace himself. That's the problem with facing Lowery, he's unorthodox and you never know what he's gonna bring to you. But with "The Franchiser", once Lowery makes a mistake, once he slips up, Shawn will take advantage!
FELIX ROSE: Lowery has picked up Shawn, whips him to the ropes, and hits himself with a beautiful spinning heel kick!
["The Anybody Killa" grabs a hand full of hair and pulls Shawn Johnson to his feet. ] FELIX ROSE: James Lowery sends Johnson to the ropes again. Shawn bounces back, ducks a clothesline, and CONNECTS WITH A DROPKICK DIRECTLY ON THE KNEE!
STEVIE T. DEE: HA! HA! What'd I tell ya? [A close up shot of Shawn Johnson as he stands and smiles brightly at the crowd.] "The Franchiser" Shawn Johnson: YES!!! [CROWD: BOOOOOO!] FELIX ROSE: Shawn Johnson gets the attention of the ref and points over into the crowd. The ref looks and Johnson with a fist to the nether region of
Lowery! CHEAP!
STEVIE T. DEE: SMART! [Once again, the crowd let's "The Franchiser" know how they feel about him. Johnson gets a questioning look on his face as if he wonders why they're booing him.] FELIX ROSE: Johnson grabs a hold of the Lowery's leg, and connects with a knee slam to Lowery! And another! He's getting as much height on these as he possibly can!
STEVIE T. DEE: Ah yeah, and now he's going for the FIGURE FOUR! [Shawn Johnson grabs hold of Lowery's right leg, twirls around, and locks in the tortuous figure four. ] "The Anybody Killa" James Lowery: AGGGHHHH! STEVIE T. DEE: Ha! That has _got_ to be painful! FELIX ROSE: Shawn Johnson is really cinching this thing in. [A closeup shot of Johnson's face shows a mixture of a faint smile and pure agression as he adds on the pressure.]
STEVIE T. DEE: Shawn knows what he's doing! He's focusing on this right leg of the Anybody Killa! Takin' him down systematically!
FELIX ROSE: Lowery's shoulders are on the mat! 1... 2... Lowery gets his shoulder up! [Lowery seemingly gets a surge of energy and raises up off the mat. Suddenly, he cocks his fist back and connects with a right directly to the face Shawn
Johnson. The crowd bursts into cheers with Lowery's effort. ]
FELIX ROSE: "The Franchiser" quickly lets go of this hold and man, is he in pain! [Johnson, holding his face, gets up and stumbles to the corner of the ring. ] FELIX ROSE: Lowery is getting up, but with a slight limp in his right leg! What's this?! Lowery get's himself in a 3 point stance! STEVIE T. DEE: Oh, crap! [With a deafening yell, James Lowery charges at "The Franchise" and GORES him with a devastating football tackle!]
FELIX ROSE: OH MY GOD! [CROWD: HOLY SHIT! HOLY SHIT! ] STEVIE T. DEE: Somebody call a medic, ambulance, surgeon general! SOMEBODY! [The instant replay shows just as Shawn Johnson turns around, Lowery levels him, whipping his neck forward and slamming him to the mat. ]
FELIX ROSE: Shawn is _not_ moving! STEVIE T. DEE: Get up, "Franchiser"! [Lowery slowly arises to his feet, as the crowd is still buzzing after his spear. Lowery places a stomp to the stomach of Shawn and then bends over, picking him up. ]
FELIX ROSE: Lowery pulls Shawn Johnson forward, and lifts him up and holds him momentarily in a fishermen DDT position! You'll notice that Lowery's move are all incredibly pain inflicting! He's a former backyard and "garbage" wrestler, wrestling int he most crazy of matches where you HAVE to be sadistic to beat your opponent!
[After holding Johnson for about two seconds, Lowery drops Shawn with a BAM! Directly on his neck! Lowery gets the applause from the fans for the move. Shawn dizzily and very, slowly gets to his feet and leans on the ropes as
Lowery soaks up the claps. ]
STEVIE T. DEE: With as much talent as Shawn Johnson has, I honestly don't know if wrestling Lowery before next week's big royale is a smart thing! Lowery : AGGHH! [ Lowery runs forward toward "The Franchiser" but Shawn pulls the top rope and
James Lowery is launched over to the outside! ]
FELIX ROSE: OH! "The Anybody Killa" lands _very_ awkwardly on his right leg! STEVIE T. DEE: Ow! [ Lowery winces in pain as referee Kevin Justice climbs out of the ring and checks on the leg of Lowery. Shawn looks as if he's about to follow when the ref tells him to stay in the ring. ]
FELIX ROSE: Yeah, right! As if Shawn Johnson is going to listen to the ref! STEVIE T. DEE: Look, he's staying back. A good man, that Shawn Johnson. FELIX ROSE: Lowery is trying his best to get back to his feet. He's holding on to the guardrail as he tries to walk. His right leg has been through a lot of punishment throughout this match and this just adds to it. It doesn't look like he's able to walk on it.
STEVIE T. DEE: SHAWN JOHNSON WITH A TOP ROPE SUICIDE PLANCHA! FELIX ROSE: JAMES LOWERY PULLS THE REFEREE INFRONT OF HIM AND SCB CRASHES INTO KEVIN JUSTICE! MAN! STEVIE T. DEE: HA HA! Look at that! Lowery walks on that leg as if it has never been hurt! He was playing possum!
[ Lowery rolls back into the ring and reaches down into his tights. ] FELIX ROSE: Johnson is slowly getting back up ... he climbs up on the apron, gets into the ring, and A FISTFULL OF POWDER TO THE EYES OF SHAWN JOHNSON!
STEVIE T. DEE: Lowery kicks him in the stomach AND PERFORMS THE SWINGING
TOMAHAWK!!! (Brain Buster!!!)
FELIX ROSE: Lowery with the cover! ... No ref! Kevin Justice is out! Lowery has him beat! [James, visibly upset, hits the mat in frustration. He rolls out of the ring, and walks over to the fallen Kevin "Swift" Justice. ]
FELIX ROSE: Lowery is kicking around the ref! Come on, don't take it out on him! STEVIE T. DEE: I kinda like it! [James is looking out into the crowd, as Shawn rolls out of the ring. Johnson runs and dropkicks Lowery directly in the back, knocking him into a ringside fan. ]
FELIX ROSE: Oh! Lowery just knocked into that fan and knocked his pop all over him! STEVIE T. DEE: That's no regular fan! That's The Enforcer out here watching the match!!! [ The Enforcer stares into the eyes of Lowery and then practically jumps on him with a arrage of fists. ]
FELIX ROSE: These two are going at it! And there's High Voltage and Steve
Calgary trying to pull The Enforcer and Lowery off of each other! The
Enforcer throws a fist at High Voltage! AND HIGH VOLTAGE FIRES BACK! Calgary trying to pull these guys off each other!
[A man is seeing running down the aisle, and to the action. He's recognized as newcomer Nathan Brady. ]
STEVIE T. DEE: It's Nathan Brady! FELIX ROSE: What's he doing here?! HE ATTACKS HIGH VOLTAGE! STEVIE T. DEE: And look! In the crowd, there's Jimmy Michaels pounding away on
Emerson Favreau!
FELIX ROSE: And DeeZ NuTZ joins him! Putting the boots to the "Playboy"! There's J-Ra! He jumps on the back of DeeZ NuTZ! [The camera angle changes, showing the entrance way where Victor Ivan Price and The Silencer are trading fists.]
FELIX ROSE: The Silencer dropkicks Price into the crowd! And there's
NightMare hitting The Last Dream on Phyre! This is total mayhem!
STEVIE T. DEE: I LOVE IT! FELIX ROSE:We have a near riot going on here! [The scene switches back to the melay between The Enforcer and High Voltage. Steve Calgary, still trying to break things up, is tapped on the shoulder. He turns around, and sees man, dressed in a suit. ] MAN: Da name's UPTOWN FRANKIE CAPONE! FELIX ROSE: THIS MAN BLASTS STEVE CALGARY IN THE FACE! THIS IS MASS CONFUSION! [The scene switches to the limosuine. Jimmy Michaels appears once again, shouts something in to the Seattle crowd, and moonsaults off the limosuine on to "Playboy" Emerson Favreau! ]
FELIX ROSE: MY GOD! THIS CROWD IS GOING ABSOLUTELY NUTS! STEVIE T. DEE: SO ARE THESE WRESTLERS! I LOVE IT! [The scene switches to inside the ring where The Silencer is seen standing on the apron trading punches with newcomer Victor Ivan Price. The Silencer knees
Price in the stomach, and suplexes him up and over and Price lands hard on the concrete below! ]
FELIX ROSE: MY GOODNESS. JIM JOHNSTON AND SECURITY ARE TRYING DESPERATELY TO RESTORE ORDER! [The camera switches to a view of the entrance way where "The Legend" Joel
Johnson is seen shaking his head at the mayhem before him. ]
[CRAAAACK!!!] ["Dashing" Danny Rochester levels "The Legend" Joel Johnson with a chair shot to the back!]
FELIX ROSE: OH MAN! STEVIE T. DEE: Look! Shawn Johnson and Lowery are back in the ring! They are absolutely sorrounded by chaos here!
FELIX ROSE: Lowery sends Johnson to the ropes, HITS HIM WITH THE CLOTHESLINE -- NO! SHAWN CRUCIFIXES LOWERY! KEVIN JUSTICE IN THE RING! 1!!! 2!!!! 3!!!!! "THE FRANCHISER" WINS IT! AS THE ENTIRE URW ROSTER IS BRAWLING IN THE STREETS OF SEATTLE! [Shawn Johnson, in a fit of joy, jumps up onto the second turnbuckle as
Lowery slaps the mat in anger, yelling out a sailors list of cuss words. Shawn
Johnson, with a smile on his face, raises his hands into the air.]
STEVIE T. DEE: SHAWN DID IT! HE DID IT! FELIX ROSE: Ladies and gentlemen, thanks for joining us on the first ever broadcast of Ultimate Revolutionary Wrest- Wait!!!
[Shawn Johnson is celebrating on the turnbuckle when DeeZ NuTZ runs into the ring and CLOTHESLINES HIM OVER THE ROPE TO THE FLOOR! DeeZ NuTZ raises his hands in the air as chaos sorrounds the ring. ]
FELIX ROSE: OH MY!!!! THANKS FOR JOINING US FOLKS!!!! BE HERE NEXT WEEK! [The scene fades as Jim Johnston and the URW security force desperately attempt to restore order.]
