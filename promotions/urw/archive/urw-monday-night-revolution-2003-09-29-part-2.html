URW ULTIMATE REVOLUTIONARY WRESTLING ~~~~~MONDAY~~~~~ ~~~~NIGHT~~~~~ ~REVOLUTION~ ________________________________ URW Monday Night Revolution Attendance: 800 Location: Outside the URW Arena Time: 9:48 PM PST 9/29/03 LIVE on The American Sports Network [We fade backstage to Lindsey Florentino.] LINDSEY FLORENTINO: Welcome back! URW camera crews and the wrestling press had a chance to catch up with Jimmy Michaels yesterday for some pre-recorded comments.
[Loverboy is approached by several members of the press as he was seen leaving the URW work out facility in Seattle.]
PRESS:  Loverboy how about a few words about your upcoming match tomorrow night? "LOVERBOY" JIMMY MICHAELS:  Well what is there for me to say?  It is going to be a breeze neither of these two morons I am fighting has said a damn word I don't even know if they are hear or if they will show up tomorrow.  I would not be surprized if they decided it is safer for them not to show.  That's fine with me come one, come two, come all I will be victorious over who ever dares to enter the ring tomorrow night just one block from where I stand now.
PRESS:  Loverboy do you have any comments on... "LOVERBOY" JIMMY MICHAELS: SHUT UP!!! I am talking and I am going to comment on everything I want just let me get to it.  Mavis and Tommy Owens tomorrow when you leave to come to the arena give your family a long hug and kiss good-bye because after the fight is over they will need dental records to recognize you.
I have every intention on having my own little triangle match back at my hotel with your girls and as for your kids don't worry off to boarding school they go...
    Now as for the man (and I use that term lightly) who calls himself "Dashing" ha ha ha... Mr. Dark match himself, you don't know me who do you think you are coming in her like you shit don't stink and calling out not only me but almost every member of this fed.  You have a lot of balls running your mouth  when no one will even see you match including you I am willing to bet your punk ass goes down for the count with in 10 minutes of the starting bell.
Of course you seem to have a thing for "The Gayboy"  oops I mean Playboy what is that all about do you want clean his knock off suits after they are blood stained from the beating I hand out?? Too bad his whore has that job locked up.  Choose carefully who your friends are they might get you hurt and trust me
Playboy WILL get you hurt.
    Playboy, I noticed you have stopped running your mouth about me good choice but the fact is now it is to late for you.  We are both in Seattle now and this place is not big enough for the both of us.  God help you if we cross paths and keep Michele locked up in a room I would hate to have to donkey punch the bitch right in the middle of down town Seattle.  URW watch out Loverboy is ready to start his quest for the Universal Championship I just hope the competition gets a little tougher than Mavis, The New Shit, and The Gayboy.  Bring it on URW all you have to offer bring it on...
[Jimmy gets in his black Corvette and drives off. The scene fades back to Felix and Stevie at the desk.]
FELIX ROSE: Wow! Jimmy Michaels basically handing out an open challenge to any and ALL comers here in URW.
STEVIE T. DEE: Needless to say Rosie, but "The Loverboy" and "The Playboy" Emerson Favreau have not seen eye to eye since day ONE. FELIX ROSE: It's quickly becoming one of the hottest rivalries around, and we're about to see "The Playboy" himself in action here against High Voltage!
--------------------------- "The Playboy" Emerson Favreau Vs. High Voltage ONE FALL STANDARD MATCH ARNIE MYERS: LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, THIS MATCH IS SCHEDULED FOR ONE FALL. Hailing from Tampa, Florida , standing at 6'6" , and weighing in at 265 lbs, and accompanied by his fianceé Lynx, ladies and gentlemen High
Voltage!!!!!!!!!!!!!! [Lights go out for about 10 seconds and then a massive [pyro] explosion goes off at the stage. A blue spot light follows High Voltage and Lynx down the ramp. Lynx walks up the stairs to the left and enters the ring. She gets to the middle of the ring and raises her hands. As she is doing that, High Voltage slides into the ring and jumps up on the middle ring rope and pyro/fire explodes from the ring posts.]
STEVIE T. DEE: Check out Lynx, she's one hot piece of a- FELIX ROSE: No need to get into details Stevie. ARNIE MYERS: His opponent, Hailing from Tampa,Florida standing at 6' 4' and weighing in at 265lbs, ladies and gentlemen The Playboy Emerson
Favreau!!!!!!!!!!!!!! [ The lights go out at ringside as the fans begin to boo..scene of emerson chilling at his mansion in tampa go across the screen..with the playboy logo appearing..white strobes scan the crowd as emerson appears on the ramp way smiling his cocky one in a million smile ]
FELIX ROSE: Oddly enough, we have two competitors here BOTH from Tampa, both nearly the same height, and both weighing in at the same weight. Both are incredible wrestlers and this should be a spectacular bout!
DING!!!! DING!!!! DING!!!!! [Both wrestlers stand in the ring at different corners. And the match is off to a start. Both men come out of their corner and stop in the center with a collar elbow tie up. Both men jocking for position. Favreau backs High
Voltage in the corner. Both men break the hold, Voltage calling for a clean break with his hands high above his head. Favreau with a backhand chop and the crowd responds with a loud "Wooohhh" a second, and third, and fourth, and
Voltage ducks under the fifth. Favreau turns around and Voltage is there with a series of lefts and rights and backhand chops. The crowd is starting to come alive. Voltage grabs Favreau's hand and sends him for an Irish whip. Favreau holds onto to High Voltage's hand and tries to reverse it, but Favreau continues to go around in a circle while both men and holding onto each other's
and jocking for position, Voltage tries to snag a German suplex. Favreau takes a leg out from under Voltage however. Favreau tries for a spinning leg lock, but Voltage kicks him off into the turnbuckle, and Voltage springs up.
Both men are down to a face off. The crowd cheers in anticipation.] FELIX ROSE: Amazing. The opening minutes of a match can usually be a good indication of where we're going to go in this match, and all I have to say is damn.
Stevie T. Dee: Something that I always liked to do is start off the match with a bang Rosie. And I think most of these guys know that they'll lose the fans attention really quickly. That however was one of the best exchanges of the night.
[Both men circle each other, and tie up once more. This time Voltage slips behind Emerson Favreau and hits that German suplex he was looking for earlier. Favreau is down, Voltage up to his feet, goes for a leg drop, but
Favreau rolls out of the way. Behind Voltage now, Favreau gets up, Voltage is up to his feet, "Playboy" hits a side Russian leg sweep. Both men get up to their feet, Voltage with a round house kick, ducked by Favreau. Emerson grabs
High Voltage by the waist, lifts him, and drops him neck first into the ropes.
Voltage stumbles around and Favreau hits a neck breaker. Followed up by a cover!] ROSE: ONE!!!! TWO!!!! KICKOUT!!!! "Playboy" Emerson Favreau is putting on a good showing here against High Voltage. And the crowd for the most part just seems to hate everything that he does.
STEVIE T. DEE: Well that's a real shame, Felix, because "Playboy" Emerson
Favreau, is pound for pound is one of the best overall high flying wrestlers here in URW.
FELIX ROSE: Well, pound for pound he's in there with one of the best technicians in the URW.
[High Voltage is brought up and taken down with a snap mare take down followed up by a dropkick to the back of the neck and head. Favreau looks at his prone opponent and picks him up taking him over to the corner. Favreau goes to the second turnbuckle and hooks High Voltage up for a tornado ddt. He leaps as Voltage gets a good lock around his opponent. High Voltage uses
Favreau's momentum and is able to block the tornado ddt. Voltage lifts Emerson
Favreau up and hits a northern lights suplex. 1..Bridge by Favreau. He turns this around now and Voltage is in a dangerous position, Favreau lifts him up for a powerbomb. Voltage with a few desperation hits, Attempts a hurincanrana, but ends up just slipping off of Favreau's shoulders. High Voltage down to his feet and tries to kick the legs out from under Favreau with a leg sweep to the back of Favreau's legs. Favreau jumps however avoiding the kick. High Voltage is up, and ducks an oncoming Favreau clothesline. Dropkick by Voltage misses.
Favreau rushes in, but is tripped by that same leg sweep and now both men are down. Both men are up to their feet. Favreau with a boot to the midsection, and he throws High Voltage to the outside and follows suit.]
FELIX ROSE: Oh boy, Stevie, you should know better than anyone, the outside is a very dangerous place no matter what kind of match it is.
STEVIE T. DEE: Of course, there are chairs, tables, the railing, and after that the fans. And one or two mistakes out here and it'll cost you your career. Not that it would matter to High Voltage. BWA HA HA HA!
FELIX ROSE: You are very strange at times Stevie. [High Voltage throws Favreau into the ring post and the crowd groans. High Voltage goes to pick Favreau up, but Favreau grabs High Voltage's trunks and pulls him into the ring post as well. High Voltages stumbles and falls over the guard railing and into a chair. "Playboy" Emerson Favreau pulls the guard rail close to the ring, the fans scatter as Favreau tries to gather himself while sprawled out on two chairs. Favreau ascends the turnbuckles from the outside. With his back turned, he leaps, and hits an almost textbook corkscrew plancha. He takes out himself and High Voltage, the crowd is going nuts and somehow, Emerson Favreau is getting to his feet!! Favreau drags himself and High Voltage back into the ring. High Voltage tries a desperation clothesline, but Favreau goes under hooking the full nelson. Favreau lifts up once but Voltage fights it. He lifts up a second time, arching his back, both men land, Favreau's hit the dragon suplex with a perfect bridge. High Voltage is out of it, 1...2 Kick out]
FELIX ROSE: Good God, Good God. Stevie, I don't believe it! He kicked out. High Voltage KICKED OUT!!! STEVIE T. DEE: Don't be so surprised Felix! [Favreau gets to his feet and quickly goes outside of the ring. He grabs a chair and goes back into the ring. Before he can use it, High Voltage hits a low blow. Small package, 1...2..NO!!! Favreau out at the last second, the ref's hands was almost inches away from ending the match. High Voltage up, he has that chair. He's about ready to knock Favreau's head into section S row E seat X. Favreau, is up to his feet, High Voltage with two steps, and a big swing. Favreau ducks, High Voltage comes full circle, a kick to the gut. The chair is dropped, Favreau grabs High Voltage's head and drives it into the mat ala X-Pac's X-Factor. Favreau with a sloppy cover. 1...2..KICKOUT!!!!]
FELIX ROSE: It's almost as if "Playboy" Emerson Favreau had nothing left in him, and it was just desperation.
[High Voltage up, Favreau sets up a chair. Voltage gets another kick to the gut, Favreau grabs High Voltage's wrist, springs off the chair, to the top rope. He bounces off the top rope, turns in mid air, and is standing on it once again as the crowd pops big time. High Voltage pulls Favreau down off the rope, but Favreau rolls through the fall. The chair has been flattened,
Voltage grabs Lee and lifts him for a powerbomb. Favreau, flips it into a huricanrana onto the chair. Favreau holds onto the legs of High Voltage.
1...2...3!! NO!! Kick out at the very last second.] FELIX ROSE: In human, in human. STEVIE T. DEE: Come on, Playboy!!! [High Voltage is up as Favreau swings with that chair. Voltage ducks, snags a sleeper. Favreau is kicking and swing his arms. The fans all begin looking to the side as someone runs down from backstage. ]
FELIX ROSE: Now what is this about? [Almost as if on cue, Michele Muroaka begins distracting the elderly referee Gill Jones with her vivacious looks. The man running down from ringside is recognized as The Silencer. High Voltage still has the sleeper on
Favreau as Favreau begins to fade. The Silencer stealthily slides into the ring and pulls out an object from his leather jacket.]
FELIX ROSE: THE SILENCER HAS BRASS KNUCKLES! STEVIE T. DEE: He's resourceful! BAMMMMMMMMM!!!! [The Silencer hits a direct shot to the back of High Voltage head. Voltage wearily turns around to see who hit him before he drops to the mat. The
Silencer pulls Favreau on to High Voltage. The Silencer exits the ring and heads backstage as quickly as he came.]
FELIX ROSE: What the hell is this?! [Michele stops distracting the ref and points to the cover in the ring.] ONE!!!!!!! TWO!!!!!!! THREE!!!! STEVIE T. DEE: AH HA HA HA!!! BEEE-A-UTIFUL! ARNIE MYERS: LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, YOUR WINNER.... "THE PLAYBOYYYYYYYY" EMERRRRRRRSON FAAAAAAAAVREAU!!!!! [The crowd boos insanely.] FELIX ROSE: And Playboy Emerson Favreau with the help of The Silencer has just
ROBBED High Voltage of a victory!!! This is ridiculous!
STEVIE T. DEE: AH HA HA HA!!! "Playboy" knows how to get what he wants! Ingenious is what this is FELIX ROSE: No, this is disgusting. Folks, we will be right back after this commercial break!
[The scene fades as Michele is tending to a limp Emerson Favreau and the ref is doing the same to High Voltage.]
[We fade backstage to President Anthony Stone getting ready for tonights big announcement. He's sitting at a desk, signing some documents and going over important papers. Suddenly, the door swings open. None other then
"Dashing" Danny Rochester is standing in the doorway.] "DASHING" DANNY ROCHESTER: MR. PRESIDENT! [Stone looks up quizically, and then starts shaking his head at who he sees.] ANTHONY STONE: I'm very busy here, Danny. "DASHING" DANNY ROCHESTER: You can never be too busy to meet with the Dashing
One, Stone. ANTHONY STONE: ACTUALLY, I am, so if you could just do me a favor and- "DASHING" DANNY ROCHESTER: NO way, Stone. _WE_ got some business to talk about...Me and you buddy, we gotta get this straightened up!
ANTHONY STONE: I need to get ready- "DASHING" DANNY ROCHESTER: What, may I ask, where _YOU_ smoking when you put me in a dark match tonight?! ARE YOU INSANE?! "Dashing" Danny Rochester is not some curtain jerkin slack jawed yokel. I am TALENT! YOU DO NOT PUT TALENT IN
DARK MATCHES!!! ANTHONY STONE: .... [A long awkward pause as Anthony Stone stares at Rochester. Rochester gets slightly uneasy.]
"DASHING" DANNY ROCHESTER: Stone...? ANTHONY STONE: GET THE HELL OUT OF MY OFFICE!!!!!!! [Stone explodes and Danny quickly gets out of the room. The scene abruptly switches to Michael Bay sitting at a desk inside of the nearby URW
Studios.]
MICHAEL BAY: Welcome to the first ever edition of... THE DARK MATCH REPORT MICHAEL BAY: And I will be your host here, each and every week, giving you fans the details of what goes on BEFORE the Revolution hits the airwaves. Dark matches are for new talent in URW to test their skills infront of a live audience or for some veterans who need a brush up before getting some TV Time.
So without wasting anymore of your time, let's get this started! The Silencer vs. "Heavy" Hank Hedsaw MICHAEL BAY:The Silencer came into this match being outweighed by nearly two hundred pounds by journeyman wrestler "Heavy" Hank Hedsaw. The Silencer, however, showed how extremely methodical he can be by taking Hedsaw apart limb from limb. The Silencer started on the massive legs of Hedsaw until he had a hard time getting around. The Silencer then took out the arms of Hedsaw with arm bars and various locks. From there The Silencer put Hedsaw out with The
Silencer (Stone Cold Stunner) and got the win in a suprisingly one-sided contest.
You winner: In 7:58 The Silencer "El Guapo" Carlos Gonzalez and The Masked Cabana vs. J-Ra & NightMare MICHAEL BAY: The odd pairing of J-Ra and Nightmare took on URW's resident luchadores in a tag-team brawl. Nightmare seemed to be the muscle of this team while J-Ra was content to watch from the outside. As NightMare hit The Last
Dream (a pump handle slam) on The Masked Cabana, J-Ra tagged himself in and got the pin for the victory. NightMare took offense to this action and got in the face of J-Ra. J-Ra slapped NightMare as hard as he could and NightMare responded by wrapping his hands around the throat of J-Ra. Suddenly, from the back came Playboy Emerson Favreau who slides into the ring and hits a low blow on NightMare, saving J-Ra from potential harm. The two get away safely, while exchanging words with NightMare.
You winners: In 2:33 J-Ra and NightMare DeeZ NuTZ, Steve Calgary & Tiny vs. Trash Losagain, Frank Furter, & Turd Ferguson MICHAEL BAY: DeeZ NuTZ came to the ring with a bottle of malt liquor. After a quick game of rock, papers, scissors, Tiny was the one to start the match off.
Surprisingly, Tiny was getting his ass handed to him by perreniel wrestling losers, Trash Losagain, Frank Furter and Turd Ferguson. Try as he might, the seven foot tall Tiny could not make a tag. Finally, Steve Calgary yelled at
DeeZ NuTZ to do something. Finally DeeZ jumped into the ring, kicked Trash
Losagain in the balls, and smashed the bottle of malt liquor on Tiny's head.
The massive Tiny collapsed on top of Trash for the cover. You winners: In 9:46, the team of DeeZ NuTZ, Steve Calgary "Dashing" Danny Rochester vs. "The Legend" Joel Johnson MICHAEL BAY: In a match that could have quite possibly stole the show here tonight, Danny Rochester took on Joel Johnson in a heated contest. These two absolutely brought the house down in a 20 minute contest that had the fans on their feet. Unfortunately, all dark matches have a 20 minute time limit, and by that time, these two were still going toe-to-toe in an epic encounter. If you were lucky enough to see this live, you got your moneys worth before
Revolution even started! If this is just a dark match, I can't wait to see until these two get on a live Revolution broadcast! Watch out for these two, folks!
You winner: Time Limit DRAW at 20:00 MICHAEL BAY: That's all for now, be sure to meet me back here next week for the
Dark Match Report!
[Michael Bay smiles as the scene fades from the URW studios to backstage area. We see Emerson Favreau standing with The Silencer.]

"PLAYBOY" EMERSON FAVREAU: HEY! GET THAT CAMERA OUTTA HERE! [The scene abruptly switches to commercials. ]
