NCWA Saturday Night Revolution

28 January 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

Taylor. As they stand side by side, Kidd raises his arms in the air, inviting the crowd to celebrate him. As he walks the lights converge from the ceiling in a diagonal fashion, letting off a mini-exploision on the rampway behind him. circle...:: Lewis: Kidd seems very confident tonight! Canadian Maple Leaf beaming down upon him from above as he strides to ringside. Avery takes his time in order to stall...::

Gradwell to grab a hold of him and snap his throat across the top rope with a guillotine. Gradwell drags Kidd out onto the floor and starts nailing with hard roundhouse rights to the side of the head. He then throws Kidd face-first into the barricade. He attempts to whip Kidd into the ring post, but Kidd reverses the whip and it is the big man's face who smacks the steel...::
Adams: All hell is breaking loose here in Madison Square Garden! Jones: Someone's gotta get this thing under control, brother! Lewis: This is all Trevor Kidd's fault! Did you see those cheap shots?! Avery is right back to his feet again, and this time Page kicks him in the gut and reels him in for a short clothesline. Avery ducks it, applies a waistlock, and attempts a German suplex. Page is able to block the suplex by hooking his lower legs behind Avery's knees. Avery drops him back to the mat, and Page starts to fight off the waistlock with back elbows to the side of the head. belly-to-back suplex...:: Adams: What a suplex! One! Two! No! Jones: You ain't gonna beat the American Rage like that, brother! Lewis: I agree! I think that Alex Avery is gonna have to knock this man out cold to get the 1-2-3!
Jones: Easier said than done, brother. ::A frustrated Avery pulls Page to his feet, shoots him to the ropes, and nails him right in the face with a standing dropkick. The fans groan at the sound of boot soles smacking flesh, and Page is writhing around on the canvas, clutching at his face. Avery covers Page with a lateral press, but only gets a count of two and a half. Avery yanks
Page back to his feet, hits him with a Russian legsweep, then goes for another cover, this time hooking the leg. Page gets a shoulder up, and
Avery can't believe it. Avery heads upstairs, wasting no time to argue with the ref, and crouches on the top rope like a bird of prey. He waits for Page to get to his feet, then stands and launches himself at
Page. He goes for an off-the-top-rope huracanrana, but Page catches him in mid-air and counters with a powerbomb that nearly breaks the ring floor and completely knocks the wind out of Avery...::
Jones: Sweet Jesus! Adams: That's gotta be all! It's gotta be over! One! Two! Three...no! Jones: I don't believe it, brother! Alex Avery got a shoulder up! Lewis: Quit jumping the gun, Adams! Jones: I didn't think the Astounding One had that kinda resilience in him, brother! ::Page drops a leg across Avery's groin area, to the delight of the fans, then rolls him over onto his stomach. The ref threatens Page with a disqualification, but Page just brushes him aside and heads upstairs.
He steadies himself on the top rope, measures Avery up, then takes flight and drives his head into Avery's lower back with a diving headbutt. Page covers Avery with a lateral press, then decides to pull his shoulders off of the mat at two and a half. He yanks Avery to his feet and sets him up for his patented triple powerbomb. The crowd roars
approvingly...:: Adams: I have to question the wisdom of that...oh, wait a minute! Jones: Here we go, guys! Triple powerbomb comin' up! Lewis: He won't kick out of this! Jones: No one kicks out of this, brother! Lewis: Alex! You've got to counter! Back body drop! ::Page executes the first powerbomb with ease. He strains and struggles to lift Avery's dead weight off of the mat for the second powerbomb, but is able to execute it. On the third powerbomb attempt he reaches down deep, grunting out loud, muscles ripped. He is able to power Avery all the way up onto his shoulders. Avery grabs a handful of Page's hair, and tries to fight off the third powerbomb with a flurry of hard right hands to the top of the head. Page staggers backwards, but will not let go of Avery. Once he is within close proximity to the ropes,
Page drops straight back, dropping Avery throat-first across the top rope with a guillotine powerbomb. The impact of the maneuver sends the
Astounding One flying backwards. Page tries to pin Avery with a jackknife, but Avery somehow is able to kick out at two and three
quarters...:: Adams: Give credit to "Astounding" Alex Avery for kicking out of that pinning predicament! Jones: I think that the tank is on empty, brother! I think the American
Rage is one big move away from winning this thing!
Lewis: This is the same man who once kicked out of two Ice Breakers,
Jones! Do not underestimate him!
Adams: He may be somewhat of a jackass, but I have to hand it to him -- he is a tenacious competitor!

He stomps at Page's ribs, then drags him to his feet and sets him up for what appears to be his patented double underhook backbreaker, but turns out to be a double underhook gutbuster. Page is writhing around on the canvas, holding his ribs, but he also has a smile on his face. He signals for Avery to bring it on, and Avery obliges, stomping away at his ribs with all he's got...::
Adams: Chris Page has...he has a smile on his face! Can you believe this man?! Jones: Mind games, brother. Lewis: Mind games?! The man is sick! Stomp him, Alex! If he likes pain...give it to him! ::Avery yanks Page to his feet and shoots him to the ropes. He scoops
Page up and punishes his ribs with a tilt-a-whirl gutbuster. Page starts crawling towards the ropes on his stomach, and although he can barely draw in a breath, he still has that sick smile on his face.
Avery looks unsure of what to do next. He waits for Page to get to his feet, then scoops him up and drops him, ribs-first, across the top rope. Page drops to the floor, and Avery follows. He irish whips Page into the edge of the steel steps ribs-first. The sound of flesh and bone meeting the hollow steel is sickening. Avery continues to stalk
Page, scooping him up and dropping him ribs-first across the barricade.
Page, however, will not stay down. Avery mutters something under his breath, then starts to stomp away at Page's ribs while he is down. Page is starting to bleed from the mouth...::
Lewis: Ugh! Look at that! He's coughing up blood! Adams: Oh, no... Jones: The referee needs to make a judgment call here, brother. I respect Chris Page and I respect the man's tremendous threshold for pain...but you gotta know when to throw in the towel, brother.

The fans are on their feet, and The Garden is literally shaking. Page's face shifts from a grin to a grimace of pain. Blood spews from his mouth with every breath he takes. The referee is so busy looking for the submission, he doesn't notice Avery reaching back and grabbing the top rope for additional leverage. The fans respond with a thunderous chorus of boos. Page is still grinning, his teeth clenched, trying to block out the pain...::
Adams: The American Rage has got to give it up! He's got to! Lewis: I think I heard him say, "I quit." Jones: Oh, knock it off, Mikey! You didn't hear a damn thing! Lewis: I hear ribs splintering! His ribs have to be screaming in pain! ::Page closes his eyes, clenches his teeth, lets loose a scream, and hip tosses Avery to the mat. The crowd erupts. Avery is in disbelief.

Avery, who does a full 360 spin before hitting the mat. Page's ribs are obviously bothering him, but he toughs it out, yanking Avery to his feet and snapping off a quick snap suplex. He rolls Avery onto his stomach, then starts dropping headbutt after headbutt onto his lower back. The sky blue mat is covered with the blood dripping from Page's mouth. Avery tries to crawl away, but Page waistlocks, dead lifts him off of the mat, and dumps him onto the back of his head and neck with a released German suplex. Page drags Avery to his feet and goes for The
Red, The White, and The Blue, but Avery counters with a swift knee lift to the groin. The fans groan, and both men collapse to the
canvas...:: Lewis: What happened? Did Page have a seizure? Jones: That was no seizure, brother! That was a low blow! Adams: What...what a heinous thing to do! I can't believe what we just saw!
