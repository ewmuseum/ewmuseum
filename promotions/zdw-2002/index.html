Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Zero Degrees Wrestling was an angle federation run by a council and its members, its shows meant to be about the lives behind the squared circle as much as the matches in it. Its own tagline was 'ZDW - My God We Suck'.

It returned in May 2002 with Aeon 3 in Chicago. Crono Tecknic won the first match of the return with brass knuckles, Ronnie Versace won by disqualification after Dave Cortez hit him with a chair, and Luna Hersilia won the first women's match the federation had held.

Around the matches ran its stories: Tony Vigilante searching Chicago for the vanished Dilly Dawg, Magus Crowitz plotting to destroy the federation from within, and the stable FAT blowing open a locker-room door to issue a challenge.

In the main event the 32 Degrees Title was handed to Allen Richards when Kendahl Mack's chair cost Sikanah the match by disqualification, and Dilly Dawg walked out wearing the Zero Degrees title to announce that he was back.

That card and one recruiting pitch survive.
