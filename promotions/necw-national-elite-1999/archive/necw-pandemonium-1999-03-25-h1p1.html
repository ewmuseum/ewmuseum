Viewer Discretion is Advised _____________________________________________________________ The NECW PAnDeMoNiuM logo comes up on the screen as "The Beautiful People" by
Marilyn Manson starts to play. As the guitar kicks in, the logo shatters and highlights of NECW action begin to flash across the screen. We see a clip of the Big Fat Pimp flying from the side of the cage and crushing Midnight with a
Pimp Bomb; "The F'n Show" Luke Van Dam nailing Death with a Van Daminator on the urn containing Crypt Keeper's remains, sending ashes flying everywhere;
The Carnage Cult using the Bloodmoney on a referee; Romeo Fesselt hitting a bloody Ten Ton with a springboard spinning heel kick, knocking him over the top rope, to become the first NECW World Champion; The Corsiars of the Red
Brotherhood sending one of the Hell's Angels over the top rope, crashing through the broadcast table; "The Freebird" Chris Borton hitting a bloody
Romeo Fesselt with the Freedom F'n Rings inside a Hell in the Cell match;
"Superstar" Doug Atlus hitting a Superstar Splash; "Dangerous" Danny Hart nailing Tommy Craven with the DangerZone Death Driver on top of a Hell in the
Cell, sending both men crashing through the Cell and to the mat; "The
Unibomber" Jeffrey Manson stomping around the ring, chasing a referee with his screwdriver "Betsy"; Tommy Craven nailing a fan with the Hardcore Hangover;
and finally a shot of "Special K" Kevin Bledsoe firing the Boston Strangler through the side of a glass cage, shattering the cage in a million pieces and sending glass showering onto the camera. The NECW PAnDeMoNiuM logo is shined onto the shattered glass on the camera lens, giving it a jumbled affect as "The
Beautiful People" starts to fade out.
PAnDemONiuM Live from the Rose Garden in Portland, OR The camera then cuts to a shot from inside the sold out Rose Garden in Portland, OR. A graphic comes up on the upper left hand corner of the screen that says "LIVE - Rose Garden" and then underneath that "Sold Out - 18,522".
The fans are on the feet, going crazy, yelling their asses off and holding up their signs: Borton's the Motha Fuckin' THREE-bird; DDH is the REAL World
Champ; See Ya Later Jews; Brent fears Betsy; SeXy & StuNNinG; LVD 4:20; BA
Rules; Welcome Back Strangler; Warrant fears Lasher; BFP vs. Henry - 6.9 on the Richter Scale; Manson is da BOMB; DQ - Double Quacktastic. Fireworks are exploding all over the arena. The area around the wrestlers' entranceway is ablaze in all sorts of colorful fireworks, and flames are shooting out of each of the four ringposts. Rockets scream through the air and loud explosions are going off all over the place. A huge 20 feet by 30 feet JumboTron is hanging right above the wrestlers' entrance way, and it's flashing "PAnDeMoNiuM" in all different colors. The ringside fans are right on top of the action, as the steel barricades are set up pretty close to the ring. There is no padding on the floor around ringside, and the concrete is exposed. The ring mat is grey with the NECW logo in the center of it. The ropes are red with black turnbuckles, and the apron hanging around the ring is black with the word
"PAnDeMoNiuM" in red. The fans are going absolutely BESERK. Finally the music and fireworks begin to calm down and the voice of "Daredevil" Dick Van
Hype starts to speak over the din of the crowd. As he does, the camera cuts to a close-up of the broadcast table, where Van Hype and TTP are sitting, with an empty chair in between them. Van Hype has on a grey Polo shirt, black GAP carpenter jeans, and black Doc Martens. TTP has on a t-shirt that says "EULW", a pair of blue jeans and sneakers, which are currently resting on the top of the table as TTP is leaning back.
Van Hype: Hello wrestling fans and WELCOME to PAnDeMoNiuM!! We're here LIVE from the sold out Rose Garden in Portland. I'm "Daredevil" Dick Van Hype and with me is the second greatest broadcast journalist in NECW, The Total
Package... TTP!! TTP: Come on Dick, I told you not to start that crap again.... Van Hype: Tonight, we will see two of the top ranked competitors in NECW, not to mention the two biggest, COLLIDE in the ring, as the Big Fat Pimp takes on
"The Jewish Juggernaut" Henry Schnitzleheimer! The top three contenders for the Hardcore Title -- Madman Pondo, "Ice Heart" Chris Matthews, and "The
Unibomber" Jeffrey Manson -- will meet in a Three Way Dance where anything goes, and the winner will get a Hardcore Title shot at High Impact against "The
Whole F'n Show" Luke Van Dam!
TTP: And let's be honest Dick, whoever wins this match is just another victim for Van Dam at the pay per view. Van Dam doesn't lose on pay per views, he steals the f'n show!
Van Hype: Also tonight, The Carnage Cult will defend their World Tag Team titles against the Boston Bad Boyz....
TTP: Whoa whoa whoa Dick, let's not jump to conclusions. I'm still sticking by my theory that "Crippler" Ken Fielding didn't have the Bad Boyz in mind when he made that challenge to the Carnage Cult last week.
Van Hype: That remains to be seen. "Playboy" Romeo Fesselt will be taking on newcomer "Too eXtreme" Ryan O'Neal, Sexy & Stunning will be facing Wicked
Alternative, and so much more!
TTP: Hey Van Hype, what's up with the empty chair? Where's The Marvelous One? And I don't mean Charles Kaine.... "Bring the Pain" by Method Man starts up over the sound system and the crowd pops as the Boston Strangler starts to make his way to the ring. Most of the fans give him a standing ovation, and he smiles and waves, slapping hands along the way. He has on his trademark green Larry Bird Celtics tank top, red
Abercrombie running pants, and black Nike sandals. There are still some visible scars on his arms from CageMania, but other than that he seems to feeling no ill effects from his rough night.
Van Hype: Marv Albert won't be joining us tonight, and here's the reason why
TTP! The Boston Strangler is back!
TTP: Oh great. I don't know whether I should be happy Marv's gone or pissed that the Strangler is back...
Van Hype: Oh come on, you're not STILL upset about the Strangler being Full
Owner of NECW again, are ya? Anyway, the Strangler is in the ring, and he's got the mic!
Boston Strangler: [raises his hand to quiet the crowd down] Thank you, thank you very much. You don't know how much it means to me to hear your cheers once again. There are so many things that I have to address, but I'll try to do it as quickly as possible so we can get to some of the great in-ring action we have scheduled for tonight. Let's start with the man....no, I won't even call him a man....let's start with the PUNK that caused me to miss two weeks of
PAnDeMoNiuM, the punk that has absolutely no respect for the people that have come before him in this sport.... and of course I'm talking about the World
Cruiserweight Champion "Special K" Kevin Bledsoe. [crowd boos] Bledsoe, what you did to me at CageMania was uncalled for....it was unthinkable, and it was almost unbelievable. I came to the ring to give you the belt that the best cruiserweight wrestler in NECW deserved, the belt that Harold Bergmanstein had done away with to save a little money, and what thanks did I get?
Seventy-eight stiches all over my body to close up the wounds you gave me by tossing me through the side of the glass cage...
TTP: That WAS one of the funniest things I've seen in years, you gotta admit it Dick.... Van Hype: Be serious... Boston Strangler: ...but to be honest with you Bledsoe, I got bigger fish to fry. I won't waste my time and energy trying to get you back for what you did, because from the looks of it you've already got enough people on your ass!
[crowd pops] If "Playboy" Romeo Fesselt doesn't take you out, I know there are ten other guys lined up behind him to get the next shot, so your days with that belt -- and probably your days in NECW -- are numbered anyway, so you don't need me breathing down your neck. And besides, now that I have FULL CONTROL of
NECW back.... [crowd pops] ...I won't have much free time to deal with you.
Like I said, I've got other things to worry about. Things like "Dangerous" Danny Hart....[mixed reaction from the crowd] TTP: He calls himself the REAL World Champion! Boston Strangler: ....Danny, I really don't know what's gotten into you the last few weeks and months. I hear the guys in the lockerroom telling you you're the best, that you're one of the best wrestlers in the world, that you should be World Champion. So what did you do? You let all the talk go to your head, and now you've got a title belt of your own -- a worthless title I might add -- and you're calling yourself the REAL World Champion? I don't think so
Danny. The REAL World Champion is none other than "Freebird" Chris Borton....
[crowd pops] Van Hype: Who won the World Title last week by beating Tommy Craven... Boston Strangler: ...but if you've got a problem with that Danny, just let me know and we can work it out. You don't need to lower yourself to wasting a
Sunday going to some high school gym with a wrestling ring in it and going on pay per view television to "shoot" on me. Hell, how many people do you think saw your little challenge to me anyway? A few dozen? [crowd laughs] Maybe a couple hundred, tops? If you want your challenge to be seen and heard, do it on PAnDeMoNiuM, or on a NECW pay per view. That's what the people are watching. If you want to carry around a worthless piece of tin and call yourself a World Champion, then so be it. But like I said, there's only ONE
World Champion, and -- thanks to me -- it's "The Freebird". But, everything isn't how it should be with Mr. Borton, and that's why I'd like to call him out right now....[crowd pops]
Van Hype: Whoa! Strangler just called out the World Champion, and here he comes! "My Kind of Bitch" by ICP blares over the sound system and the crowd pops, and out from behind the curtain comes "Freebird" Chris Borton, followed by "The
Extreme Studd Muffin" Eric Broff, the Big Fat Pimp, and "Superstar" Doug Atlus with The Hand. All four Bitches Anonymous members walk to the ring and climb in, standing in front of the Boston Strangler, who still has the mic.
TTP: This outta be interesting.... Boston Strangler: Now, "Freebird", there are a lot of things that need to be cleared up right here and right now. At CageMania, you got the pin on Tommy
Craven in the main event, as was our original agreement....
Van Hype: Agreement?
