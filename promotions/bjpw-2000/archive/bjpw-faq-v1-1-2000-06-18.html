Due to its purorseu roots, BJPW isn't your typical run of the mill e-fed. Fans of today's US wrestling scene which promotes weekly "crash TV" that build to anticlimactic pay-per-view events featuring screw job endings in basically every match may not find BJPW's style very appealing.
Just like real purorseu promotions such as All Japan and New Japan Pro Wrestling, BJPW focuses on the action in the ring delivering high drama, competitive matches, with clean decisive finishes. So if your looking to be a part of an e-fed that features weekly mud wrestling matches along with title changes taking place every other match, this probably isn't the fed for you.
2 - Success Through Dedication ----------------------------------------------- Success Through Dedication is the motto of BJPW. In other words the more you put into the fed and your character, the more you'll get out of your experience in this fed. If your looking to obtain success in BJPW you must be willing to regularly roleplay, communicate with other handlers, and send in strategies for your matches in order to win. The more thought that goes into the roleplays, strats, and angles you develop/send-in, the more time the match writers and staff will take to make sure that the aspects you want to get across are portrayed in your character(s).
3 - The BJPW In-Ring Style ----------------------------------------- Burning Japan has a distinctive in-ring style. We like to describe it at a combination of the traditional All Japan style with the New Japan Strong
Style/Worked Shoot style.
Basically this means that matches start slowly and build to an exciting finishing sequence full of dangerous high spots, big come backs, and extremely close near falls.
The style features solid mat wrestling and the use of submissions in the early stages of matches with alot of stiff strikes (kicks, punches, chops, elbows, etc.) incorporated throughout.
Submissions are also accepted as finishes in this style and honorable ones at that. You won't hear any announcer lauding the fact that a wrestler has never tapped out or submitted in this promotion.
Keep the general style in mind when filling out your application. 4 - Getting Matches ----------------------------- The Producer of BJPW is responsible for making the matches on BJPW cards. Many matches on BJPW cards will be made based on ranking in the fed, past conflicts, (i.e. a time limit draw the previous week, wrestler A pinning wrestler B in a Tag Match), and various other factors.
Sometimes however, two handlers may challenge each other to a match. If both handlers are in favor of the match, then it will generally be made, and take place as soon as possible.
5 - How matches are Determined ------------------------------------------------ BJPW is a booked e-wrestling league. Match outcomes are determined by the producer and a select few consultants based upon quality of roleplays, strategies, and angles. Often this process is EXTREMELY difficult, but you'll never hear the staff complaining about having to make difficult decisions, since that is an indication that the league is very competitive. There is almost a 100% chance that at sometime during your BJPW experience, you'll disagree with a decision in the outcome of a match. Regardless, we hope that no matter what the outcome of your matches, you'll still enjoy BJPW.
6 - Looking Long Term / Building for the Future -------------------------------------------------------------------- The focus of BJPW is on long term booking. In BJPW major storylines are laid out in advance and take weeks (maybe months) to develop. This focus on long term booking makes handler cooperation in order to develop exciting, detailed angles crucial for the success of the fed.
This approach is not only encouraged, but expected. Infact in some instances, the producer will contact two handlers with an idea for a long term angle.
This is especially true for title matches. Through cooperation, handlers can work out a series of planned events/finishes which elevates both wrestlers standing in the fed while building to a big match showdown. Two handlers get together and agree that
A very basic example of how this system should work would be as follows. Two handlers decide to began a program with their wrestlers. They decide that the first logical step to begin the "feud" or "program" (which ever you prefer)
is through a tag match. It is agreed that Wrestler A will pin Wrestler B just before the time limit expires in the tag match thus establishing a rivalry.
They then decide that on the next card the tag teams will meet again with
Wrestler B on the verge of making Wrestler A submit when time expires, thus resulting in a draw. The two wrestlers are then scheduled in a singles match on the next card with the storyline going in "Can Wrestler B score a victory over Wrestler A." At this point the handlers can either agree on a finish for the big match (Perhaps the handlers agree that Wrestler B should win the match in dramatic fashion in order to extend the program/feud, or perhaps handler B wants to see Wrestler B lose in order to setup a long term storyline of Wrestler B trying to beat Wrestler A) or they can agree that they will enter the match with no prearranged finish and let the producer decide the match outcome based on their RPs and strats.
This system will also be used in building up title matches. For instance, based on RPs the BJPW Producer may select a certain wrestler as the next title challenger, but rather than simply announce the match the producer would get in contact with the champion and the future challenger and establish a series of events/finishes that establish the challenger and build excitement for the title defense.
This system makes for a much more exciting fed and allows handlers the ability to fully develop their characters and have fun executing interesting storylines.
7 - The Importance of Tag Matches --------------------------------------------------- Other e-feds revolve around singles matches with tag matches and the tag division in general used as little more than card filler. Due to its purorseu nature, tag matches take on a much more important role in BJPW. Generally almost every match on the weekly cards will be tag team or 6-man tag matches.
It will be these matches in which BJPW wrestlers will prove their worth and earn opportunities to advance in the singles scene. This focus on tag wrestling makes the singles matches even more important and exciting.
It is said that a man's worth can be judged be the company he keeps, namely, you're tag team partner. Everyone who joins BJPW will be expected to form some kind of an alliance with another wrestler(s) on the roster. Make sure you keep in mind your wrestler's personality, his background, his training etc. before making the decision to become someone's tag partner. Because once that alliance is formed you will both be responsible for the wins and losses you suffer from that point on. If you can't find a regular partner or would prefer not to tag with one wrestler regularly then the match maker will pair you with various wrestlers in tag matches. Keep in mind however that such an approach will probably not be as successful as simply finding a regular partner.
8 - Titles ------------- BJPW currently recognizes two titles: 1)BURNING JAPAN WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT TITLE - This is BJPW's top prize and is reserved for BJPW's top singles wrestler.
2)BURNING JAPAN WORLD TAG TEAM TITLES - These titles are reserved for the best tag team in BJPW.
3)BURNING JAPAN WORLD JUNIOR HEAVYWEIGHT TITLE - This title is reserved for the top wrestler 225 pounds and lighter.
Additional titles may be added as the need arises. 9 - "The Leather Jacket" & Other Championship Traditions ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ ------- As stated before BJPW isn't your typical fed. In BJPW the World title belts actually means something. In order to emphasize the prestige of the titles their are a couple of traditions that are expected to be strictly adhered to following a title match (singles or tag team).
Following each title match their will be a short ceremony of sorts. Following a successful title defense, the champion will be presented with a trophy, plaque, or dish commemorating their defense with the date, opponent, and venue's name on the trophy. This award will be presented by President
Tamura. Following the presentation the champion will be allowed (i.e.
expected) to give a few brief comments to the crowd. These comments can be about anything the champion wishes (thanking the crowd for their support, giving credit to his opponent, issuing a challenge, or just talking general
smack :-). Following a title switch, the new champion will be presented with a custom made leather jacket bearing his colors and last name across the back of the jacket (think of a high school varsity jacket if you will) by President
Tamura. This is the symbol of a wrestler's arrival. With it your a member of a very exclusive club. This jacket then serves as your ring jacket and a symbol of your BJPW stardom for the rest of your BJPW career (so pick those colors carefully!). Following the presentation of the jacket, the new title holder will also be expected to make a short speech to the audience/media.
We feel that these details help to establish the importance and prestige of the BJPW titles, and also serve to set apart BJPW from other promotions. This is a tradition special to BJPW.
10 - Rankings & Title Shots --------------------------------------- The Official BJPW Rankings are based upon a variety of factors including: Win/Loss record, push (determined by Roleplays & strats), current angles, and quality of competition. After each card, a BJPW update, including singles and tag team rankings will be released via email.
Title shots are generally awarded based upon rankings, however title shots can be gained through other means such as: Victory over champion(s) in a non-title match or earning a fall over a champion in tag match.
Champions can also ask to defend there title(s) against certain competitors and can decide to accept challenges issued by other wrestlers.
Their is no set number of title defenses that have to occur in a certain amount of time. Title defends will take place when and as often as is deemed appropriate by the BJPW Producer.
11 - Cards ---------------- BJPW holds one card per week which airs on TV-Tokyo's BJPW program entitled
"GOUKA" (hell fire). Each card usually features 3-6 matches along with some interviews, highlight packages, etc..
Cards are released with full commentary in Action Block Style. Supercards will generally be held every 2-3 monthes in major venues around
Japan. BJPW's crowning Supercard is "STRONG STYLE EVOLUTION" which takes place each August in the Tokyo Dome. This is BJPW's WrestleMania and features the best action of the year with the climax's of BJPW's hottest angles and feuds.
12 - Roleplays ----------------------- Once per week BJPW will release a roleplay packet entitled "Warrior Spirits" to members of the league mailing list containing the roleplays submitted that week by handlers.
Roleplays should be quality flashes and not just simple one paragraph statements consisting of 5 lines of dialogue. All talent participating on upcoming cards are expected to roleplay. Roleplays are judged on quality, not quantity. Therefore take your time and produce at least one quality roleplay each week.
Their is no need to rush out two roleplays or ramble on without purpose just to put out a long roleplay. If you have been scheduled for a match and are unable to roleplay due to time constraints, please inform BJPW's producer so that any needed adjustments or changes can be made.
In ring interviews are rare in purorseu. You may request to make comments following matches (which occurs more frequently in real purorseu promotions).
An example of an appropriate post match comment would be to challenge the champion to a title match following a win over a top contender or defeating the champion in a tag match.
13 - Strats ----------------- Strats or Strategies are also considered when determining the outcomes of matches in BJPW. A short strat should be sent in before each match containing your wrestlers strategy for the match along with moves or tactics he will employ.
While Strats don't count towards match outcomes as much as Roleplays they can be the deciding factor in close matches. Strats are also a good way to help make sure your character is accurately represented in his matches.
14 - Staff of BJPW --------------------------- BJPW has assembled a fine collection of officials, announcers, and referees. Masahiro Tamura - the owner/president of BJPW. Tamura has no real background in the wrestling business. He's a 35 year old millionaire who made his money in the stock market. A life long wrestling fan, Tamura decided to open BJPW to have some fun. But hes made it clear that while he wants to enjoy himself with BJPW, he still expects his investment to make money. You'll see him pop up from time to time in crowning a new champion and on "GOUKA" making official announcements
Hadi Moditian - The lead announcer or "Voice of BJPW". Moditian is a veteran of the purorseu business and generally considered to be the best announcer of his time. In addition to having served as television announcer for Pro Japan
Wrestling Moditian also has experince in promoting, having successfully run
Pro Japan Wrestling & Super Hyper Visual Fighting. Moditian is the model of impartiality and a real call it like he sees it kind of guy.
Shishi Fujiwara - BJPW's color analyst. Fujiwara is a 28 year old former wrestler who has wrestled in the past for the now defunct Pro Japan Wrestling
& Super Japan Wrestling. His career began under a hood as the Golden Spirit
Lion with Moditian's Pro Japan Wrestling. Following PJW's closing Fujiwara showed up in Super Japan Pro Wrestling sans the mask and wrestling under his given name. Fujiwara was very
impressive in his stint with SJPW and was recognized by many insiders as the next great purorseu star. After joining Team Tiger it appeared that Fujiwara was ready to prove the insiders right but nagging knee injuries soon forced him into an early retirement. Fujiwara brings great insight to the announce team and a burning passion for the sport.
Hideo Demura - the official BJPW correspondent for the Tokyo Sports Wire. He'll pop covering various topics in the league from the latest signings, to behind the scenes power struggles, to the financial status of the promotion.
Hes not employed by the company so hes able to tell it like it is. If the promotion is claiming 12,000 paid and the real number is more like 5,000,
Demura will let you know. Demura is the model of impartiality and a real call it like he sees it kind of guy. Due to this trait, sometimes BJPW officials aren't very appreciative of his views (i.e. they don't like it when he doesn't go along with the company line).
Kunihiro Nobu - BJPW's head ref. Hes in charge of all the action that takes place in a BJPW ring. Hes 40 years old a veteran pro wrestling referee. Hes a no nonsense kind of guy who won't tolerate any bending of the rules. You choke a guy with the ropes in another promotion and they give you a 5 count to stop; Nobu DQs you on the spot! Hes light on tolerance and heavy on enforcement of the rules and thats exactly how Mr. Tamura likes it.
15 - Application Analysis -------------------------------------- When filling out your application to join BJPW, please take your time and be as descriptive as possible. Your application is the main piece of information used when writing matches involving your character. Virtually every aspect of the match is taken from your application or strat. Therefore it is VERY important that you take your time when filling out the application. Take special note of each section of the application, to ensure that the match writers are able to fully understand your wrestlers personality and ring style.
Also please keep in mind the general ring style of the fed. While all types of wrestlers have a place in BJPW, every character should have the ability to work BJPW's fundamental "AJPW meets Strong Style/Worked Shoot Style."
For example, this means that while your character may be a high flyer, he'd better be able to throw some strikes and work some submissions as well.
