BJPW — Roleplay

"Airborne" Shawn Foster

26 May 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey you guys. I just wanted to post some of my latest work. I e-mailed it to a few friends that were online at the time I had just finished sending the role-play in and got a lot of good responses. You may respond to it or not.
I'm not asking you to. I just felt I should post this because I myself thought it was one of my most well produced RPs since I first started in the AWMC.
Later. [We start off with a scene of the Pacific Ocean off the Yokohama shore. Next, we cut to the streets of Yokohama where we see flea markets and people selling fruits at the fair and people riding their bicycles around. We cut to the beach shore again. A man is shown playing volleyball with a bunch of Japanese men. He stands out because his skin is darker than that of the Japanese men and his eyes aren't cut low.]
[As the shot gets closer, we recognize the man as "Airborne" Shawn Foster of the Burning Japan Pro-Wrestling organization. Shawn is wearing blue surfer shorts and a white tank top with nothing on his feet. He is sweaty from the volleyball game. People are on the sidelines watching. Shawn notices the camera and stops the game briefly. He points at a random person and signals for that man to come in for him. A woman stands forward, wearing a pink thong and top swimsuit. Shawn stares at her for a brief signal then points towards the game so she could replace him. She does so. Shawn jumps off the court and jogs his way towards the camera. As he catches up he stops briefly to catch his breath.]
[He sits down on the sand and sinks his feet into them. He reaches into his pockets and pulls out silver frame colored "Oakley's" sunglasses with blue lenses. Shawn looks up at the camera with an unknown grin on his face. He puts his hands against the sand for support as he sits.]
Shawn Foster: You know something..... As if it isn't hard to tell, I am very disappointed at the outcome of the first ever Gouka. I feel as if I was robbed. Here I am, six-foot, two-twenty four pounds with a man that is only six-foot, four inches and weighs only two-hundred and forty-seven pounds. Who do we get to face? Some six-foot, four inch two-hundred and eighty-two pound fat bastard and his even fatter tag team partner, standing in at the same height as all of the other men, but weighing in at three-hundred and fifty-five pounds. Do you really expect me to be able to knock a man that big down? A man whom not even a forklift could move? What the hell kind of match was that?
But that's not even the worst part. I get manhandled by these fat, cow eating, chicken blood drinking bastards and my partner doesn't really do anything to help me. Just breaks a count here and a count there. I'm thinking that things can't possibly get any worse. When I finally get enough energy and strength to tag in this no good tag team partner of mine, he back-stabs me.
Punches me and leaves me laid out to be eaten alive by those two fatheads. I end up losing the match. Its okay though. All possible odds were up against me. David Brannigan, Ed Dean, I'll deal with you two separately some other time. Now I get revenge on the first who deserves it the most.
[A man with an ice box is wheeling it right passed Shawn.] Shawn Foster: Hey! [The man turns around quickly with a startled look on his face.] Shawn Foster: Lemme get a......... Man: Sorry, no speaking English. [Shawn laughs a little.] Shawn Foster: Coca-Cola. Man: Oooh. OK. Coca-Cola. [The man opens his ice box and pulls out a Coke. He hands it to Shawn.] Man: Ichiban yen. Shawn Foster: What the hell is that? Man: Sorry, no speaking English. Shawn Foster: Whatever. [Shawn reaches into his pockets and takes out three coins. He hands it to the man. The man smiles and gladly take the money. Shawn then grabs some sand and begins to throw it at the man and pointing for him to leave.]
Shawn Foster: Get the hell outta here. I'm trying to finish my interview. Man: Baikayoro! [The man leaves as Shawn stops paying attention to him. Shawn uses his teeth as a bottle opener to open the bottle. He succeeds in doing so. Shawn spits out the top and drinks some of his Coke. He places it into the sand, next to him. He now wraps his arms around his legs and puts his chin in-between his knees.]
Shawn Foster: Anyway, let me continue where I left off. Revenge. What is the definition of revenge? According to the dictionary definition, there are too many ways to explain it, so I shall give in my own explanation. To me, revenge is when you get pay back for when someone did something wrongful to you, as in lying to you, or back-stabbing you. On Monday, June the fifth, I will get my revenge on that mysterious jackass, Razan Gunshin. Gunshin, you made a huge mistake. You may be taller and heavier than me, but not by a whole lot, so I can still get my payback and payback's a bitch. You will get the match of your life on the upcoming RISE. You happened to see an old interview, prior to when you were under contract with the BJPW. I wasn't calling you Oleg, dumb ass. I was calling my ex-tag team partner Oleg, or Leg even as I changed it.
Unfortunately he got injured and couldn't compete. Real sad. [Shawn wipes a fake tear off his face.] Shawn Foster: Then you came along. I don't think you really care for a victory and to gain better chances at titles. All you care about is screwing people over. At least that's what I think now that I have tagged with you before.
Gunshin, you will pay three times for what happened to me at Gouka. One time for each man who hit me on that show. You messed with the wrong man. Smaller than you or not, I can still kick your ninja warrior ass. I am a far greater wrestler than you will ever be. I know where I stand in the BJPW and it is obvious that you don't know where you stand. That's okay though. I'll help you with that. In about one and a half weeks you will find out why you shouldn't have messed with the Airborne one, because had you not back-stabbed me, we could've probably found a way to get the victory and we would've probably been on good terms. But now...... Now, I'll just enjoy inflicting pain and anger into you from the first ever Gouka. As for the following round, the quarter finals, which will occur on June twenty-first, I will most likely be facing one of the men I faced in the first ever Gouka. I'll probably be facing the fattest of them all, Ed Dean. Well, Deanny, I know just a little bit more about you now, so it'll be easier for me this time around. I promise that if I do face you in the quarter finals, I will slap the fat off your face and make you pay for being on top of me at a point where I was weak. As for
Takeshi Takizumo, I don't think you'll be going passed Dean anyway, so I won't even mention you just yet. Right now I won't concentrate on Dean or you. I have quite some time before I face either one of you two anyway. I will just concentrate on that back-stabbing son-of-a-bitch, Razan Gunshin.
[Shawn picks up his Coke off the sand and wipes the sand from the bottle. He drinks some of it and slowly stand up. He turns around and begins to walk back towards the volleyball game which he left. As he is walking and drinking his
Coca-Cola, the screen slowly fades away to black.]
