=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= B U R N I N G J A P A N P R O W R E S T L I N G =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= UPDATE: 06.17.00 FROM THE PRODUCER =================== Greetings I'm back from my trip and ready to get back into the swing of BJPW. I'd like to extend my gratitude to Mr. Scott Daugherty for a job well done with the card. Just to clarify, the deadline for this weeks supercard, The Coronation (thanks Mark and Scott :-)), is Monday Morning at 9AM eastern. As far as the card goes I'm looking to make this card something special. With that in mind don't be shocked if the card doesn't come out until Thursday or
Friday. I'm not going to rush to get it out Wednesday night because the rest of the week will be pretty much dead anyway so if I need to wait till Friday or even Sunday to send thats what I'll do as it won't affect our schedule at all.
If your one of the NJPW guys who has just entered the fed, I need your app. ASAP. Your old NJPW app will work if you don't want to take the time to fill out a BJPW app.
BTW - I'll be sending out the card again, this time through egroups, for the purposes of keeping an accurate archive on the mailing list site.
Questions, comments, etc. are welcomed. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= S C H E D U L E =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= THE CORONATION LIVE!
