e w r e s t l e . c o m [Intro] Hello, and welcome to the Universal Xtreme Wrestling Alliance! The future in fantasy wrestling as we know it. Since day one, of me being in this industry, I have worked at nothing but coming out with the greatest promotion to ever hit the scene..and now, the return of something great! Back in the summer of 99', the UxWa was easily one of the biggest feds on the net. With great talent, like Rob Stylz, Dave Taylor, The Outlaw, Malik Mustafa,
Hustler, Anthony Kutler, and many more.. Now, the UxWa will give it one last run! In this you will find a small bit of information, as you will recieve more upon joining!
[Titles] UXWA Universal Championship Belt- This Belt is the biggest belt around. You hold this belt your the top Dawg. The wrestler that is going to get the most respect from the federation. This belt is for only heavyweights. This belt will be defended every payperveiw and at least two cards a month.
UXWA North American Championship Belt- This belt is another one just like the World belt. You hold this belt and you will be somebody. Just get ready for match that you won't believe. This belt is defended sometimes at the PPV and 3 cards a month.
UXWA Cruiserweight Championship Belt- This belt is for only cruisweights less then 231 pounds. This belt is for wrestlers that actually think they can perform. Make daring moves. To keep the crowed worked up. This belt is defended every other card.
UXWA Television Championship Belt- This belt fans will know about you. You will defend your belt at everybody
T.V card. This belt will show that you that you are Mr. Showtime. This card is defeneded once a week on a telivised card.
UXWA Intercontinental Tag Championship Belts- These Belts are the highest in Tag Team competion. You and another wrestler gets to team up against the high competion of the UXWA. These Titles are defended every 3 cards.
