The Rebirth of the Greatest Promotion Ever Is An Illustrious Thing Information Packet V1.0 [ | X | ] Opening Preface [ | X | ] The UxWa is a classic promotion which originally opened in the summer of 1999. The promotion went to the top in no time, easily becoming one of the best on the net. It was soon thereafter short-lived as CEO [name withheld]. The return has now become evident. Bringing back the Universal Xtreme Wrestling Association, means only one thing..Complete control over the e-wrestling world. From it's past legends, The Reaper, The Outlaw, Rob Stylz, Malik Mustafa, Thunder, Sleep
Stalker, Hustler, Brian Sillege, Anthony Kutler, Ian Parker, only to name a few. We now step into the new millenium, this time..the new breed will take over. No more main eventers getting all the shots, from here on out you will work your way to the top. The E-Wrestling Mecca will now dawn over the world for one final ride. Here at UxWa we pride ourselves on our amazing roleplayers, quality cards and all around participation and dedication. Another big plus of the UxWa is the quality of our weekly cards. We don't use TNM or Zeus or any other simulator, but we use clean-wholesome hand-typed cards. With our writing staff, which can range anywhere from five to ten writers, we put out the most quality cards that you'll find anywhere in the world of E-Wrestling. Finally, the participation factor is what makes us what we are. Our handlers work together to make feuds and angles the best they can be. They work together to develop stables and really put everything they can into the UxWa. The past handlers of the UxWa have shown their dedication.
Everyone works together to make the UxWa the best thing around. The UxWa has gone through four Co-Presidential switches through its time, showing that whenever the UxWa looks to be in dismay, someone steps up to keep the UxWa going.
[ | X | ] Titles [ | X | ] UXWA Universal Title- This Belt is the biggest belt around.You hold this belt your the top gun. The wrestler that is going to get the most respect from the federation. This belt will be defended every payperveiw and at least two cards a month.
UXWA North American Championship Belt- This belt is another one just like the Universal title. You hold this belt and you will be somebody. Just get ready for a match that you won't believe. This belt is defended sometimes at PPV's and 3 cards a month.
UXWA Television Championship Belt- This belt fans will know about you. You will defend your belt at everybody T.V card. This belt will show that you that you are Mr. Showtime. This title is defended once a week on a telivised card.
UXWA Intercontinental Tag Championship Belts- Only the best tag teams have ever held the UxWa World Tag Titles. Such legendary team-ups as Hustler and
Malik, 187, Rob Stylz and Outlaw, and Sleep Stalker and Thunder, have held these titles. The World Tag Team Titles are the pinnacle of any tag team, showing their rewards in team work and determination.
UXWA Insane Championship Belt- The king of hardcore will hold this title. Your willing to put your life on the line night after night for the sane cause of being the most suicidal star in the promotion. Held by such hardcore legends as
Anthony Kutler, Sleep Stalker, Ian Parker, Brian Sillege, and many more. [ | X | ] Cards [ | X | ] UXWA Tuesday Night Altercation!- Once a week, every Tuesday night! Usually ranges from 7 to 10 Matches!
The UxWa will also be holding bi-weekly Houseshows, and sometimes weekend SuperCards! Pay-Per Views are once a month, and we will kick it off with Top of the Food Chain II! Trust us, this is the best way to operate. Altercation's are
CONSTANTLY exciting! And if you think TNA is good, our PPV's absolutely blow the top off the UxWa Arena! This also makes it challenging...with one card a week, you must roleplay very well to ensure your push over "the other guy"!
Either way, the UxWa runs its gears on this format. [ | X | ] Anouncers/Play By Play Men [ | X | ] The men who call the action every Tuesday night of the year...the two legends in the sport...
Jimmy LeVan: Technically sound in wrestling, he can identify the best moves performed by the best wrestlers. Though he tends to like the babyfaces more than the heels, Gates gives his props to the heels when need be. Jimmy is famous for calling the best matches in UxWa history. Eat your heart out, JR!
Victor Cross: Our resident problem child has always been a foil to LeVan since the birth of the UxWa. Victor loves the heels and relishes extreme and hardcore matches. Cross is also technically sound and doesn't take half an hour to hype the next big card of the UxWa! *cough cough * Tony "I'm not fat, camera adds 25 lbs." Schiavone *cough cough*
And of course...the other PBP men... Taj Nyquest: Nyquest has also been here since the birth of the UxWa. Very neutral in his views, Nyquest likes to look at both sides of the fence before even thinking!
Darrious Simmonz: A former UxWa star, Darrious will love to tell you how much better he is then whoever is out there wrestling. Simmonz is even known for busting out the boots and going at it with anyone who declares he's wrong.
He'll give you a piece of his mind.
