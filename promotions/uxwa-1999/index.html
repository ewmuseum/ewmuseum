A San Antonio-based federation whose debut broadcast from the Alamodome opened with battle royals for its heavyweight and cruiserweight ranks. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

UXWA staged its first broadcast, “Saturday Night Sacrafice,” live from the Alamodome in San Antonio, Texas on 8 June 1999, announcing itself on air as “the UXWA's first card.” A three-person broadcast team, Kelly Price, Jimmy LeVan, and Victor Cross, called a show built around two battle royals seeding the promotion's heavyweight and cruiserweight ranks, headlined by “Bad Ass” Terry Woods against Warren Wallace.

A second surviving document, an application form dated 16 June 1999, shows the promotion still actively recruiting wrestlers by mail a week later.

The last surviving document for UXWA runs to 21 June 1999, less than two weeks after its debut. No closure notice survives, and how the promotion's heavyweight and cruiserweight divisions developed from there isn't known.
