Help Save the IWF/WOW!! (18 May 2000, A)

>Really? I was not aware that that was done without your >knowledge. Well, I can't speak for [name withheld], so I don't know why he >did that. Unless it was Jedah's idea perhaps, but still.... Yeah, I actually would have never even known about it if [name withheld] wasn't still on the mailing list at the time it occured. I mean, if [name withheld] would have came to me and ask me if I would put this guy over, I would have done it. However, the way it was done pretty much illustrated the attitude of "Yeah, we don't need you, get out of the federation and don't come back."
>I've always been treated fairly, even back in the days when I >was getting my ass jobbed out like a madman :-) But that's your >prerogative, Ro, it's cool... Yeah, being jobbed is one thing. That's no big deal. Matter of fact, I don't even know if I've won a single match with Knick
Man since I've gotten back to e-wrestling :-) However, it goes back to the differences between IWF/WOW with [name withheld] in the forefront and without [name withheld] at the head. When I left IWF/WOW the first time, I told [name withheld] to job me for all matches already booked and that was that. That was cool. It left me with the option to come back at a later date. However, the way things went down this time basically eliminated that option. I was booked in a Loser Leaves IWF/WOW match without my permission so now "technically" since Knick Man lost, Knick Man can never come back to IWF/WOW again.
>Someone got shot and killed?? I know Alex Extreme and Derrick >DaMann did some kind of angle a few years back where I believe >DaMann bit it. When'd someone get shot? :-) I'm not saying it >didn't happen, but I must of missed that and I'm curious! :-) >There's no question that things were better when [name withheld] was >around, however there was also a much deeper roster when [name withheld] >was around as well. The shuffle of presidents last summer >really screwed things up, especially when they gave it to that >[name withheld] guy, and things have never really recovered completely >since then. A lot of people left, including ME at the time :-). Yeah, that was a Pain Inc angle. Sure I understand that some people don't do the greatest of angles. I'm all for giving people creative control over angles. However, when people are doing angles that make the entire federation look bad and "bush- leaguish" it's up to the President to step in there and not allow it. When you try to get people to join your federation, when they see stuff like that, they are going to go on to the next federation. If the guy is a good guy that's notorious for bad angles, somebody should help him out and get him into a good angle. When I was in the AICW, we had this guy named The Great
Salami who was pretty bad. So we got together with his handler, had him changed into The Notorious G.S. and had him join the Gay
World Order as a type of "tough" homosexual type character, and he actually became pretty entertaining after that! It's up to the fed head to push the strong characters effectively and work with the weaker ones and get them up to speed. That is what's going to have to happen to save the IWF/WOW.
