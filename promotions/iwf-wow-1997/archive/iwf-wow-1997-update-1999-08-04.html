ROLEPLAY! - Okay, I've come up with a NEW RULE! Gotta love those. Here's what it is! From now on there will be a Trash Talk before Mayhem, and one on Wed., then CC before BZ. Well, from now on all members will only be able to RP a maximum of 3 times per week. 1 for each packet. Some of the members have full time jobs and families, believe it or not, so to make it fair for them I'm limiting them. It's not a gigantic problem, because only on a few instances have I had the same guy send me more than one roleplay. I'm just trying to have some foresight here. :) - About the Women's Division. We've got a few applicants, and a few people who have expressed interest, however we could use more Women Wrestlers. So if any of you would like to create a new female character, or have friends who would be interested, REFER THEM TO THE I/W! Thanks... - I added profiles to the roster page. Click your name, and wow! A profile. Check 'em out and please report any dead links to me. - I could always use another match writer. If you're interested, email [detail withheld] - The new set deadline for all RP's and strats for ALL CARDS (PPV's not included) is Friday at Noon, 12:00pm EST. This is because BattleZone is sat. night, and the match writers need their match assignments on Friday night. That means I need all the roleplays/strats to decide the winner. Sorry to make it such a tight squeeze, but I see no other way of going about it. - That's all I can think of right now, on w/ the update... <><><><><><><><><><><><> III.
