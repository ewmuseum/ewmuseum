==================================================================================== [ Scene opens up with Todd Hendrix standing outside the SkyDome with some fans around him. ]
Todd Hendrix: Hello everyone and welcome to the WrestleFest countdown show! On the show we will take you for a preview of WrestleFest, have some comments from IWF/WOW Superstars and we will also have Slot Machine Frank take on Shooting Star! First lets go to some pre-match interviews with
IWF/WOW wrestlers! ================================================================================ ( Screen fades into the locker room of Destroyer where Anthony Edwards is sitting getting ready to do an interview and Destroyer comes from around the corner and looks into the camera and then goes and sits down beside
Anthony Edwards. ) Anthony Edwards: Destroyer, I'm glad you could join us before you go out there for your big match against KKP for the IWF United States Title. The first question I would like to ask you is what do you think about your current run of things here in the IWF/WOW?
Destroyer: It started when I defeated Tommy Nightmare at the beggining of the month and it kept up until I had a couple of bad house shows but I only lost once to Desert Scopion, Crusher you were my wake up call and after you woke me up I went on a role and now Desert Scorpion might have beat mean and ended the role I was on but he also woke me up and told me that I got to work a bit harder if I look to become a champion so that's what I'm going to do. Starting here tonight.
Anthony Edwards: Can I have your comments on the alliance that you are apart of Pain Plus? Destroyer: Pain Plus is an alliance that I can say won't stop growing for awhile. It will just keep on getting bigger better stronger and more ready to fight. It will also become a team that can take on anyone of thing in its path. So I'm a proud member of Pain Plus and right now I can tell that it will just keep on growing because the stars is the limits!!
Anthony Edwards: Next question I want to ask you then is concerning your match here tonight, what part do you think Pain Inc. and Pain Plus are going to be in it?
Destroyer: That is a question that I have given much thought to. Iron Mike I know for a fact that the members of Pain Plus won't interfere unless your goons from Pain. Inc get involved. So I want to tell you know if you or any of your members of Pain.Inc cause me to be DQ'ed of for KKP to be DQ'ed I will hunt you Iron Mike and that person down and beat them into a pulp so right now I'm giving you a warning don't get in my way or you will deeply regret it.
Anthony Edwards: Now the next thing I would like to know, is what are you going to do about the match tonight?
Destroyer: Well King Kevin Payne I'm ready for you and in just a matter of time and you walk down that isle don't forget to bring the belt that will soon be around my waist and I will tell you right now good luck becuase when that bell rings you will be hurting in a lot of ways. So get ready toninght KKP to feel an new level of payne because you might be a member of
Pain.Inc but you will find out tonight why I'm a member of Pain PLUS!! So look out because when the time comes I will strike. So get ready to fight!!
Anthony Edwards: Now I must ask what will you do if you win the match here tonight and get the US Title?
Destroyer: The first thing that I'm going to do is go and celebrate my first title in the CIWF and for the fact that I clocked a member of
Pain.Inc !! Then after that I will start giving anybody who wants a match all they got to do. So if you want a shot at the title then you better get in line because I'm prepared to knock you all down one by one and don't forget that I'm not afraid to do it. So look out you will soon all find out after I get the title, why they call me DESTROYER!!
Anthony Edwards: Do you have any last comments here? Destroyer: Unforgiven don't worry I haven't forgot about you and Dingo pack you will all fall to my hands down the road but tonight I start my US Title reighn after I take care of KKP So Payne you better run and hide because you will be Destroyed tonight!!
Anthony Edwards: That all for now, thanks Destroyer for joining me here today. ( Destroyer gets up and walks away and the the screen fades to black.) ================================================================================ Todd Hendrix: Some intresting comments from Destroyer! Another title match that we will be seeing is America's Least Wanted taking on Body Count! Lets go to ALW!
================================================================================ Todd Hendrix: "I am outside of the dressing room of America's Least Wanted. They will be facing Body Count for the IWF/WOW tag team titles shortly in what should be a classic tag team match. Let's get some last minute comments from ALW."
KNOCK KNOCK Todd Hendrix: "Gentlemen, it's me! Can I get a few words?" (the door opens and Octane is filling the doorway) Octane: "What do you want?" Todd Hendrix: "I was hoping you could answer a few questions." Octane: "Forget it!" Todd Hendrix: "Look at your contracts .... " Octane: "Get it over with." Todd Hendrix: "Do you think that you will beat Body Count?" Octane: "What kinda question is that? Geez. All you reporters are the same. No originality."
Todd Hendrix: "Umm ... next question. There appears to be some tension between you and your partner. Is there any truth to the rumours that you aren't getting along?"
Octane: "There are no problems between us. We are focused. Well, I mean sure there were some disagreements but nothing major."
Todd Hendrix: "I heard that the two of you haven't been fighting recently because you were getting along."
Octane: "What? No there is no truth to that. We are fine." Todd Hendrix: "You were the UEW Ultimate champion at one point in time. There were rumours that Joe Kidd didn't like that you were focusing on your own career rather than your tag team. Rumour has it, you two still aren't seeing eye to eye."
Octane: "Will you stop with the questions! There are no problems between us! We are going to prove it to you when we win the titles!"
Todd Hendrix: "My sources say that this will be your last match in IWF/WOW. Any truth to those rumours?" Octane: "I can't answer that." Todd Hendrix: "Ok. Joe Kidd has been stating or at least hinting that there are problems between you and the Inner Circle ..."
Octane: "There are no problems between me and the IC!" Todd Hendrix: "Ummm ... let me re-phrase that, Joe has been hinting that there are problems between him and the other members of the Inner Circle ...
including you." Octane: "Didn't you watch Monday Mayhem? Didn't you see what we did to Joe Mills? There was unity there." Todd Hendrix: "My sources say that Joe Kidd didn't want to be there. The Inner Circle forced him to help with the attack." Octane: "Look! There is no dissention between us." Todd Hendrix: "I also hear that you two are joining Pain Plus. Any truth to those rumours?"
Joe Kidd (in the background): "END THE INTERVIEW!" Octane: "This interview is over!" (The door slams shut) ================================================================================ Todd Hendrix: I have to get into the SkyDome so lets go to Anthony Edwards who is LIVE in the Control Center for a run down on the card!
