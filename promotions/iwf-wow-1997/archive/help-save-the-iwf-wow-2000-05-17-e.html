> [name withheld], but I wouldn't come back to the IWF/WOW not after the way my >character was treated after I announced I was leaving. I was booked >for two matches at the time and I had no problem doing the job for >those matches. However, later on, I find out that my character was >used again, after I had already left, to get completely destroyed to >Jedah, I think his name was, without my permission or knowledge. Really? I was not aware that that was done without your knowledge. Well, I can't speak for [name withheld], so I don't know why he did that. Unless it was Jedah's idea perhaps, but still.... > >I actually would have came back eventually, because one of my feds, >NJPW just recently closed down. However, there's an old saying that >goes "never burn your bridges" You don't curse out your boss and >fellow employees when you find out you're leaving a job because you >never know when you might need them again. In the same way, you don't >misuse the characters of handlers leaving your federation, because you >never know when those handlers might want to make a comeback to your >federation. With the history that the IWF/WOW has, doesn't it make you >wonder why the roster size is what it is now? Perhaps if they treated >their former handlers better, this would not be the case. I've always been treated fairly, even back in the days when I was getting my ass jobbed out like a madman :-) But that's your prerogative, Ro, it's cool... > >That, plus I tend to shy away from federations where wrestlers get shot >and killed on the cards. Sorry man, nothing personal against [name withheld] and >all those who are running the IWF/WOW right now. But, in my opinion, >IWF/WOW was WAY better a year or so ago when [name withheld] was running things. Someone got shot and killed?? I know Alex Extreme and Derrick DaMann did some kind of angle a few years back where I believe DaMann bit it. When'd someone get shot? :-) I'm not saying it didn't happen, but I must of missed that and I'm curious! :-) There's no question that things were better when [name withheld] was around, however there was also a much deeper roster when [name withheld] was
around as well. The shuffle of presidents last summer really screwed things up, especially when they gave it to that [name withheld] guy, and things have never really recovered completely since then.
