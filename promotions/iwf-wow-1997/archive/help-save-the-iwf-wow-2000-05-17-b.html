Help Save the IWF/WOW!! (17 May 2000, B)

17 May 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sorry [name withheld], but I wouldn't come back to the IWF/WOW not after the way my character was treated after I announced I was leaving. I was booked for two matches at the time and I had no problem doing the job for those matches. However, later on, I find out that my character was used again, after I had already left, to get completely destroyed to
Jedah, I think his name was, without my permission or knowledge. I actually would have came back eventually, because one of my feds, NJPW just recently closed down. However, there's an old saying that goes "never burn your bridges" You don't curse out your boss and fellow employees when you find out you're leaving a job because you never know when you might need them again. In the same way, you don't misuse the characters of handlers leaving your federation, because you never know when those handlers might want to make a comeback to your federation. With the history that the IWF/WOW has, doesn't it make you wonder why the roster size is what it is now? Perhaps if they treated their former handlers better, this would not be the case.
That, plus I tend to shy away from federations where wrestlers get shot and killed on the cards. Sorry man, nothing personal against [name withheld] and all those who are running the IWF/WOW right now. But, in my opinion,
IWF/WOW was WAY better a year or so ago when [name withheld] was running things.
