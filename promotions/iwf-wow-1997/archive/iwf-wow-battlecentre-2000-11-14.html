IWF/WoW BattleCentre, 11/11

14 November 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(In the background we hear "Beautiful People" by Marylin manson as the camera fades in on the set of the NEW IWF/WoW BattleCentre, a large desk with the
I/W
logo across the front and a large monitor behind. Also behind the desk are the two familiar figures of Anthony Edwards and Jack Anderson...the lights come up as the music fades out...)
Edwards: Good evening folks and welcome to anothe redition of the NEW IWF/WoW BattleCentre, from the nerve-centre of I/W headquarters! Anderson: Yup! You ever see "Beyond the Mat"? Remember that scene when Joey
Styles was cutting promos in Paul Heyman's basement? Well, we're in a similar situation, only not so classy, eh?
Edwards: He's just kidding, folks...he's been a little edgy, since they cut his interview out of that documentary.
Anderson: I made that film and I ended up on the cutting room floor...it's an injustice, I tell ya!
Edwards: Another injustice this week is the shockingly low amount of interviews we have from the Superstars of the I/W this week...I don't know if it's because of the holiday weekend or what, but we have only 2 pieces of video to show this week.
Anderson: So, you know what that means? It gives us more time for something
I
like to call "Hand Shadow Puppet Theatre"!!
(Anderson reaches under the table and grabs a small lamp, shining it against the wall. He holds his hands up in front of the lamp and starts making hand shadows against the wall...)
Anderson: See! Here's a dog! Ok, ok...how about a bird? See it's wings? Ok, ok...how about an old man's head?...(in an old man's voice, moving his hands in synch with the words) "Hey Edwards...you suck!"
Edwards: Will you stop that!? Anderson: Ok, here's some images of my last vacation...Here I am, sky-diving! And here's me doing some white-water rafting...that was a fun trip down Snake Canyon... Edwards: Ok, that's enough! (Anderson puts the lamp away, muttering "spoil-sport" under his breath) Let's get to the interviews we have this week. We're starting off with another offering from a man who's scheduled to make his debut this coming Monday. Let's hear from Jonhhy Spades!
BattleCentre - BattleCentre - BattleCentre - BattleCentre - BattleCentre
"And action!" was all you could hear as the director sat back in his producers chair and watched with intensity making sure everything ran smoothly. Moments later Johnny Spades walked out onto a make shift fashion show runway wearing a dark black suit, black italian shoes and a silver shirt with a black tie underneath. His long brown hair was tied back in a ponytail and as always his grayish blue eyes were covered by chrome shades. Spades walked to the entranceway, stopped folded his hands in front of his crotch and stood their as cameras flickered and woman looked with awe. Spades shot a small smirk then walked to the back.
Director: Cut! The somewhat small director jumps down off his chair and walks to the back patting guys on the back telling them they did a good job. The director walks into Johnny Spades room where Spades is now red Adidas warm-up pants, sneakers and just putting on a white muscle top.
Director: Johnny boy great job, just a great job. I'm telling you Johnny you keep this up and you will be a big name in Hollywood in no time at all. You got the right ingredients to do it.
Spades smiles. He adjusts his shades then looks down at the roundish director. Spades: Thanks allot Frank. But you see i did this suit deal for the multi-millionaire piece of crap clothing company cause i wanted the extra money and a chance for my face to be in GHQ. As for Hollywood.
Hell yeah i could be a big name attraction in that place but instead Johnny Spades would rather become the biggest name in the history of wrestling. Then after that i will make the big time movies and have to listen to overweight wane big names like yourself bitch and moan.
Spades stares at the little fat man who looks ready to explode but knows better. He looks flustered and searching for words.
Spades: Don't worry. Don't take it personally. Just because your a divorced, 45 year old man who hasn't been able to break into the big time yet but still insists he's a great director is no business of mine and no i'm not singling you out. Just have my check ready when i'm finished getting dressed here. Now be a good little flop and leave my room.
Spades chuckles as the fat little guy's face is now red as a beet from anger and frustration. The little man storms out of the room and walks down the hall screaming at his workers to get the "BLEEP" out of his way. Spades refixes his long brown hair then catches the camera man walking down the hall.
Spades: Hey you (man points at himself. Spades nods) Yeah you. Come here and bring a blank tape and that camera with you.
The man agrees and walks into Spades locker room. Spades takes the blank tape, swipes a black marker off his small table and writes "IWF/WOW" on the side of it. He then throws it to the camera man who puts it in with a weird look then hits record. Spades stands in the middle of the room with a somewhat cocky grin across his face.
Spades: "Vicious" Vicky i didn't catch you on Battlecentre. Perhaps it was because i fell asleep listening to all those losers carry on or perhaps it was because you knew damn well that on November 13th your @ss was as good as kicked at my hands. Well i could care less if you do the silent treatment or talk till the cows come home cause like i have already said (smiles) this Monday Mayhem i am out to prove a point. See
Vicky unlike you i don't feel like being a curtain jerker here in
IWF/WOW. I don't feel like having to make minimum wage week in and week out and lose every damn match just so i can say i hit the big time. I'm gonna put that IWF/WOW World Heavyweight championship around my waist and some punk like you doesn't have what it takes to stop that from happening.
I've paid my dues in this damn business. Waiting for my chance at the big time and now i'm in one of the big feds and you can bet your @ss that some "Johnny come lately", "Dime a dozen" born loser no talent like you is gonna stop me. I'm here to prove to everyone that Johnny Spades isn't just some big talker but a man who can step through those ropes and get it done in the ring as well. Unlike 90% of the punks in this federation when Johnny Spades says he's going to do something (looks into the camera with a look of determination) i go out there and damn well do it. I told everyone that would listen that this November 13th i would take care of Vicky Valiant in mere minutes and prove my dominance right away. I will show all you idiots in IWF/WOW, who seem to think your the only "big dogs" that the biggest, sexiest and most talented
"big dog" of em all has arrived. You all can laugh. you can all make idle threats just like before when its time to put up or shut up we'll see who the real deals and the punks are. I'm the real deal, Vicky your nothing but a punk and at Monday Mayhem you better be ready for what will be the fastest, most decisive loss of your pathetic career. Don't hate greatness Vicky, Hate yourself for being a no talent loser.
Spades motions that the segment has ended. He walks over to the camera man. Spades:(Taking tape and handing it to camera man) Take this tape. Go get one of those yellow evenlopes and send it to IWF/WOW headquarters. you think your brain can handle that.
Camera man nods "yes". Spades: Good the address is................ <Camera Fades> BattleCentre - BattleCentre - BattleCentre - BattleCentre - BattleCentre
Anderson: Well, Johnny Shades seems pretty confident about his chances here in the I/W. I'm thinking I like that attitude, it'll take him far!
Edwards: He'll need that confidence to face some of the Superstars of the I/W. Let's now take you back to last Monday Mayhem, and the closing minutes of the Redding/Colby match... BattleCentre - BattleCentre - BattleCentre - BattleCentre - BattleCentre
Jim Robson: Redding lifts Colby and whips him into the corner...
REVERSED!!!... REDDING KNOCKS DOWN THE REFEREE!!! Colby grabs a stunned Redding... BELLY TO BELLY SUPLEX BY TREVOR REDDING!!! AND HE
DROPPED COLBY'S HEAD ONTO A STANDING STEEL CHAIR!!! WHERE DID THAT
CHAIR COME FROM!!! Jack Anderson: I think one of Redding's two hotties put it there!! I hear that's called "Beauty"!!
Jim Robson: What's Redding placing on the mat!?!? Is that a nail!?!? Jack Anderson: Yes, and pointy end up!! Jim Robson: Redding lifts Colby... BRAINBUSTER ONTO THAT NAIL!!!! THAT WAS VICIOUS!!!!! Jack Anderson: I like this guy!! Jim Robson: Redding has Colby out of it, as the ref comes around!!! Redding with an STF!!! HE CALLS THIS "POWER"!!! REDDING HAS A HOLD ON
COLBY'S... "GUYS" TOO!!! THE REFEREE IS CALLING FOR THE BELL!!!
[DING!!! DING!!! DING!!!] Jim Robson: I don't think Colby submitted, but he's out cold!!! Jack Anderson: What an upset!! Francine: The winner of this bout, TREVOR REDDING!!! Jim Robson: And look at this! Redding is hugging his two valets. If he lost, I wouldn't be surprised if he punch one of them in the mouth!!
This guy is sick!!! BattleCentre - BattleCentre - BattleCentre - BattleCentre - BattleCentre
(Both Anderson and Edwards look a little uncomfortable after viewing the video.)
Edwards: Ok, is there any reason to grab a guy's "guys" long enough for him to pass out? It's uncalled for!
Anderson: Well, it got the job done, didn't it? He won the match...and, now we get to hear from the man who beat Magnus Colby last Monday night...Trevor
Redding!
Edwards: Parents, get your little ones out of the room...just in case! BattleCentre - BattleCentre - BattleCentre - BattleCentre - BattleCentre
[The scene fades in to Trevor Redding on the phone. See, he must be talking to the local hospital paying off Magnus Colby's overnight bills. For it was
HE who spent the night, not Trevor. It's not every day you meet a man who squeezes your nuts and shoves you head first into nails, after all. Trevor gets off the phone and speaks...]
Trevor Redding: Don't be fooled by what you just saw. I'm not a big phone guy. Just had a little <business> to take care of. Speaking of business...... I made it mine to shelf that overrated glorified washed up professional wrestler, Magnus Colby last Monday. Mag.... there aint no shame, boy. You fell just like the rest of them. Just like the Jason
Reigns....... and the...... you get my point. I can go all day. I arrived just a month ago and I've already speedwalked through the rankings here.
But like I said, no shame. You got beaten by the better man..... badly at that. Okay, maybe a little shame. [Trevor gets up and paces around.] But see it wasn't your fault. You believed you were a big star, a legend...... dare I say it, an icon. You were wrong. But you were mislead.
You were lead to believe these falsitudes by getting put over by the steroid wreaking bums we found off the street who were looking to make quick money by laying down. All these years. You've lived a lie. Sure, you're good. But only in a mediocre way. You're marketable. You can cut a decent promo when called upon, but your workrate has dropped these past few months and frankly it shows. You aren't really a man and I proved that to the world. Now I don't see any shame in that, do you?
[At this point, Vickie and Vanessa enter into the view of the screen and put their arms around him almost religiously with expressionless emotions on their faces.]
I guess that brings me to you, Chris Carter. You're the next on this ultimate list that the IWF/WOW as well as myself are in the middle of conquering. You can rant and rave and pretend what's going on between you and me is some quote feud, but I don't see it that way. I don't even remember what you look like, junior. You get on that microphone and blast me for the way I treat my women......
[Trevor reaches over to Vickie and tightly grasps her chin.] Well that's none of your business. I will continue to treat my ladies the way I feel they deserve to be treated. And it aint gonna' take you pinning my broad shoulders to the mat for three seconds to stop me. You can't stop me. So if you wanna' build a feud on this than its going to be a long one.
The only thing stopping me from lashing out is death. [He lets go.] But lets call an ace an ace and an ass an ass. You're not gonna' kill me, let alone beat me. You're gonna' take one look at me and shit your pants.
No, I aint much to look at. But you're gonna' take one look into my eyes and see nothing there. At this point you'll realize that there aint a damn thing you can do to hurt me, or even get the better of me. I am a living breathing constant. I will do what I can to hurt you to the point you can not move and then pin you. Either that or force you to give it up. My choice.
No one has been able to put a stop to me yet. What makes you think you can? You are just another name in this world. I will beat you then move on and meet up with the next victim. Until their are none left. Maybe then I can finally get a good nights sleep. Maybe.....
[The scene fades to black.] BattleCentre - BattleCentre - BattleCentre - BattleCentre - BattleCentre
Anderson: (pointing to his arm) Goosebumps! Look, I got goosebumps!
Edwards: He's been tearing up the ranks of the I/W and I don't see him stopping, or even slowing down anytime soon. Let's look ahead to this Monday and see what's in store for Mayhem!
BattleCentre - BattleCentre - BattleCentre - BattleCentre - BattleCentre [Debut] Johnny Spades vs "Vicious" Vic Valiant "MeatMachine" Mike Marconi vs Jason Roth "HeadHunter" Richard James vs Magnus Colby Trevor Redding vs "Countdown" Chris Carter [North American title match] "Smooth Criminal" Charlie Vahn vs Daemon Winters BattleCentre - BattleCentre - BattleCentre - BattleCentre - BattleCentre
Edwards: We have the debut of Johnny Spades coming up...
Anderson: Which I'm really looking forward to. Edwards: We have Redding and Colby in the building, but in different matches... Anderson: You know that this isn't settled between those two...they're be some extra-curricular activities happening there. Edwards: And, our main event is a North American title defense! Anderson: I guess the Epic one doesn't have to work every Monday, eh? Well, he's the champ, he should get some time off!
Edwards: Well, I guess that's it for this week. Remember, if you don't make yourself known by way of interviews and promos, the booking committee won't know you exist...and, if they don't know you exist, you won't be seeing much action!
So get those interviews in, we have camera-crews standing by, ready to inteview you! Anderson: (getting out the desk lamp again) Good night, everyone! (As the music comes up and the lights fade, the desk lamp is shining on the wall and Anderson starts with the shadow puppets again..."Look, this is where the
Epic one won the World title!"...fade to black.)
