IWF/WOW — Card

Control Center

16 June 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(An instrumental version of "Perry Mason" by Ozzy Osborne plays as the Screen fades in to various clips of IWF/WOW Wrestlers performing their finishers. The camera then fades in to show the set for the Control Center. In the background is an IWF/WOW banner. A man is sitting behind a black desk. On the desk are some papers, and a black telephone. He has short dark blond hair, and is wearing a black IWF/WOW MONDAY MAYHEM t-shirt. The man looks up to face the camera.) Anthony Edwards: Hello there fans, welcome to this week's edition of the Control Center, I'm Anthony Edwards. On today's show, I will show you some very interesting interviews I conducted this week. Most notably, interviews with Sudden Death, and Dark Vanity. Today's show also contains the Rumor Report, Rankings, and to start things off, a special LIVE interview. A couple of week's ago, my guest surprised most of the IWF/WOW by joining the Syndicate. Come on out Redback. (REDBACK, wearing his wrestling tights and ring mask walks onto the set of the Control Centre) Anthony Edwards: Thanks for making the trip into the Control Centre studio. Redback: Actually I think this is the first time that I have appeared live in the Control Centre. It is a nice set you have here Anthony. Anthony Edwards: You have been quiet lately but that is about to change as you have a busy scheduled of matches coming up. Firstly what have you been doing lately? Redback: Since joining the Elite of the IWF/WOW, the Syndicate, I have been working my ass off in the gym. I have been in intense training not only with Dingo Dave Dalton but also with AoD and Body Count. I have learnt so much and now is the time to start showing what I have learnt in training. Anthony Edwards: So what can we expect in your next series of matches? Redback: Look I am not going to say that I will become a champ overnight but I guarantee you will see a much improved REDBACK. I know it will take time but I predict that in 3 months I will be among the VERY BEST of the IWF/WOW. My road to greatness starts again on Sunday against Martin Richards and I will be showing the fans that I mean business. Anthony Edwards: Does that mean that you will be more aggressive in you wrestling style? Redback: Anthony, I am still the same wrestler I have always been, that is I am a wrestler in top physical shape, I know all the fundamentals and I have great ring sense. None of that will change, what will change is my DESIRE, being surrounded by the Elite members of the Syndicate has rubbed off on me and it can only make me a better wrestler. Anthony Edwards: Well it sounds to me that you will be more aggressive. You have a match on Monday night against Lord Robert d'Artois on Mayhem, that will be a tough match? Redback: Monday Mayhem is the best show in the wrestling world, you have to fight hard to get a match on Mayhem. In the past I have got many matches due to my huge popularity with the fans, in short I guaranteed bums on seats by being their. Starting this Monday against the Lord Robert d'Artois, I will prove to the promoters and the fans that I deserve to be on Mayhem. You are right it will be a tough match, I notice the Lord is talking about doing whatever it takes to win, well great because I am going into the match with the same attitude. Anthony Edwards: You have made a real enemy in Destroyer you must be looking forward to your match with him? Redback: I am Anthony I am. Destroyer let's face facts we hate each other, always have always will. Anyone that lives near the arena when me and Destroyer fight get your ticket now, as this match will be a full on WAR!!! Destroyer you know me well enough that I don't need the Syndicate to help me against you but I have noticed that you are surrounded by a pack of morons. Destroyer you will soon be feeling the venom of the REDBACK once again. Anthony Edwards: Talking about venom that leads us to Snake Eyes? Redback: I would always back a spider against the snake, ANYTIME!!! Desert Scorpion I know about Snake Eyes, I will not take him lightly. If you say he is dangerous that is good enough for me. But Desert Scorpion, I RUN FROM NO-ONE, I am a tough wrestler and trust me I can be as dangerous as anyone in the IWF/WOW. Snake Eyes I can't wait to meet ya in the ring!!! Anthony Edwards: What about all the rumours involving the Syndicate, I have heard that you are not joining Power Alliance and will help the Inner Circle, what is happening? Redback: Look Anthony I can't tell you anything on my own, we are a unit and make our decisions as a unit, but I am sure that these 3 gentlemen and me can help any questions you may have.... (AoD and Body Count walk into studio. The AoD has his black mask on, as well as black shorts, and a white Syndicate t-shirt. Al Barber and Anthony Thomas are also wearing white Syndicate t-shirts, but Barber is wearing white shorts, and Thomas is wearing blue jeans. The AoD and Body Count sit down beside Redback and Edwards. Edwards is looking uncomfortable.) Anthony Edwards: I banned you guys from this set, you can't be here. AoD: We can do whatever the hell we want to Edwards. Just relax, we aren't here to take the show over... (under his breath) this time. Anthony Edwards: What was that? Barber: Nothing. Ask us what you want to know. Anthony Edwards: I was just asking Redback to shine some light on this Inner Circle/Power Alliance situation, you guys have become involved in. AoD: We stated our position on Monday night, and we are waiting on a response from the Inner Circle. Some things have changed since then, but you will find out about that in due time. Anthony Edwards: What about AJ Stanson's comments after Mayhem? He came out on Trash Talk and revoked the offer he made on Live television. Did this bother you? AoD: Listen Edwards, Stanson had just lost the North American title, Mark Watson was badgering him, and he got emotional. Stanson, you should know as well as anyone that making such hasty decisions can be very harmful. I know you believe in the Power Alliance, and your combined abilities, but I have to STRONGLY disagree that the PA alone can wipe out the Inner Circle. The numbers just don't add up in your favour big man, especially if the Inner Circle decides to present a great offer. If you don't understand where we stand in this situation, take a second to think about it. As a unit, we are responsible to each other for doing what is best for the group. You of all people should understand that Stanson. This entire war, from all _3_ sides is about one thing... Power. The Inner Circle has it, and our two stables want it. We all want to be at the very top of the heap, and I guarantee you that our final decision will ultimately have to be what is beneficial to _us_. And by the way Stanson, you should know me well enough by now to know what kind of man I am. If we decide to help you, you won't have jack shit to worry about. If The Syndicate and the Power Alliance team up to take on The Inner Circle, I can guarantee you that we can be trusted. Anthony Edwards: Body Count, leaving the stable topic for a moment, you guys have a match coming up this Friday against a very dangerous team in Sudden Death. Thomas: Edwards, we know that Sudden Death are a good team, and we also know that they are beatable. After all, we've accomplished that feat already, and this Friday night will be _NO_ exception. Barber: War Machine, and Genocide are going to feel the wrath of Body Count and _our_ doctrine. Anthony Edwards: What would this doctrine be? Barber: That anyone who steps through the ropes and takes on Body Count will get the piss stomped out of them. We aren't kidding around here. We want the World Tag Team Titles back, and we are going to have to go through Sudden Death to get that opportunity. And trust me when I tell you, that that is EXACTLY what we're going to do. Anthony Edwards: Thanks for your time gentlemen. Redback: Thank you Anthony, it was a pleasure. AoD: Later Edwards. (The Syndicate rise and leave the set.) Anthony Edwards: Thank God they left. I was worried I'd be sitting in the corner picking my nose again. (Scene fades into Anthony Edwards in a limo with "King" Kevin Payne right after Thursday Thunder. Payne is dressed as usual with the UWW World Title aruond his waist. Edwards starts the interview) AE: Fresh off yet ANOTHER loss, what's with you? I thought table matches were your specialty? KKP: I'm not in the best of moods all right?? I keep thinking what happened with Iron Mike was just a bad dream. I've been loyal enough to Pain Inc. to know that it wasn't intentional. AE: Are you getting back at Coop? KKP: No, I have other fish to fry...Redback, yes-man to the Syndicate. This will be our FINAL match, buddy. I'll make sure you never wrestle again afterward. Just like Jeremy Thranton... AE: OH NO!!! Will you get OVER that? He beat you fair and... KKP: SHUT UP!! What I mean is, I'll be glad when it's over, just as I was then. You know, it doesn't matter how manny times I lose, each loss fuels me for another win. So then I'll be pumped for the big rumble. AE: But you haven't won many matches as of late. If you're being "fueled up" I'd love to see you burn out. KKP: Ha ha, good analogy, but WRONG! You will be seeing more of my wins very soon Edwards, and the one that will count, will have me winning the gold. AE: It's nice to dream, isn't it? KKP: You want out of this car? AE: To be honest, I would! KKP: Well, not yet. I still have to talk about the biggest match of my career, the IWF/WOW ID4 Rumble! AE: Oh, here it goes! (sighs) KKP: What was that? AE: Nothing... KKP: Oh, I KNEW I heard something! If you don't like hearing me talk, you can leave now! AE: But we're in a moving vehicle! KKP: Who cares? (Payne opens Edwards' door and literally tosses him out of the moving limo onto the street. He rolls around, but lands safely on the shoulder of the road, looking disgusted) KKP: (speeding away) That'll teach you to wear hate-filled t-shirts! HA HA HA HA HA!! (Scene fades out as Edwards curses Payne and starts walking away) Anthony Edwards: I just showed you video evidence of Kevin Payne committing announcer abuse. Presidents Jurkschat and Ishikawa, I want Payne fined, suspended, and I want this retarded clause in his contract removed, AND I WANT IT DONE YESTERDAY!!!!!! ASSHOLE!! Here's a very interesting interview I conducted with _the handler_ of Dark Vanity. (Scene opens inside a IWF/WOW interview set. Anthony Edwards is sitting down. Three empty chairs sit to his right. He sits looking at his watch as if his guests were late. "Pepper" by the Butthole Surfers plays and an unexpected guest walks onto the stage.) Anthony Edwards: Ladies and Gentlemen, breaking away from the traditional e-wrestling interview, tonight we are going to welcome the HANDLER of the Higher Power, that being DARK VANITY, MATT BOOTH, and SANDY FOX! Tonight, we welcome to the IWF/WOW interview set..Mr. Mike Bilodeau and his girlfriend Sandy "Fox" Calabrese. (Bilodeau and "Fox" enter the set and sit down in the chairs.) Mike Bilodeau: Thank you Anthony. This is a little weird, so bear with us. We never got to sit down and give our feelings about our "Characters" before. Anthony Edwards: Mr. Bilodeau, you are best remembered for you participation as the President of the Deadly Wrestling Organization. But, this past week, you retired from Fed-Heading. What made you come to that decision? Mike Bilodeau: I thought I was being unfair to my participants. The DWO had a VERY small crop of writers, and many times it would be just ME writing the matches. I couldn't handle it. I was suffering burn out. But, now I have found a replacement and he has his crew of writers and the DWO will be all the better for it. And now that I have the opportunity, I would like to thank Chad Ishikawa for the kind words he passed along in Trash Talk. I wish the DWO all the luck in the world, but that chapter is over. Now, I'm fully concentrated on handling my character, Dark Vanity. Anthony Edwards: Dark Vanity? Where did that character come from? Mike Bilodeau: Honestly? Well, like all inspiring little boys that wanna go somewhere in the wrestling world, me and my friends had a backyard wrestling league. We would all beat each other up and claim these fake titles. Sometimes I would play Hulk Hogan, or the Ultimate Warrior. But, as I got older, I wanted to be the bad guy. I didn't have the Randy Savage character down. So, I created a wrestler named King Prankster in a tribute to the Evil Doink. My friends started creating all these bogus wrestlers and I felt like I needed something new. So, that's when Dark Vanity came about. A wrestler that was so crazy, a wrestler that was so unafraid, and a man after my own heart. He wasn't a satanic character when I used him back then. He was a Rick Martel type character. But, when I entered him into E-Wrestling, he needed a face lift and I came up with the Satanic Cult story line. Anthony Edwards: When did you start Fantasy Wrestling? Mike Bilodeau: Last year. But, I was one of the suckers who started in the Snail Mail leagues. I filled out this form in the PWI Magazine and sent in $15 to join. Needless to say, the company was a Fly By Night and I was out of $15. I actually started my own Snail Mail League back in 1992. I only got to advertise in my school newspaper. I got some interest, people sent me checks to join. I cashed the checks and mailed them their first announcement. Somewhere in that process I fucked up and I sent my Snail Mail League wedding invitations for my mother's sister's wedding. And all the older people in my family got a newsletter from my E-Wrestling League. Aunt Maize never knew why kids were all her guests that day. I kept my mouth shut. The next day, I got my ass beat by the chess club. Anthony Edwards: Why did you enter E-Wrestling? Mike Bilodeau: I had to prove something. Mike Mavis, of the WCWF, sent me an application for his league after I heard about it in a newsgroup. That was Dark Vanity's first league. His first match was for the league's US Title. He lost match to one of Mavis's JTTS'ers. At that point, DV was still a face. When I found out that his jobber was indeed a jobber, I changed my character and went back after that US Title that Devilman held. DevilMan was one of Mavis's best friends, so I never won that belt. I tried to go after the tag titles, I created the Gatekeeper. I made him bigger than DV, in an attempt to have a Sid/Sean Waltman type team. They made it to a tag title shot, and the league closed. I was confident that they would win. It was a match against the jobber that won the US title and another goof, I forget his name. Anthony Edwards: Where did DV go next? Mike Bilodeau: I created my own league. This was out of spite for Mike Mavis. It was called the EWCW. I also put DV into the APW, run by a good friend Shawn Roy. The EWCW went well, we had Robski and John Jennings and Sick Rick. But, out of spite and anger and frustration I gave DV the TV Title. That was the only title DV has held to this date, and that worries me. Why do I waste my time flashing for this character that Fed-Heads never see as holding a title? Sometimes I think that I may be just as bad as the Immortal Havoc or the Immortal Gangsta! But, even the EWCW, I closed. I guess I am just not cut out to be a fed head. But, Shawn Roy called me out of my resting time of only flashing and we created the DWO with an impressive caliber of talent. But, that meant DV would no way in hell hold any titles. I had to job him out into a commentator role. So I joined the WOW and the NICW. Anthony Edwards: When was the best moment of Dark Vanity's career? Mike Bilodeau: During the Christmas winter months. When DV joined the Inner Circle in the NICW and he had that angle going with the WOW Inner Circle. That was a good time. At that time I felt DV had made it and would be getting a title not soon after that point. In NICW, I got a tag title shot with Shawn Riser as DV's partner. It didn't happen that night. In WOW DV got IC title matches, but who could stop the steam roller known as Derrick DaMann? Dark Vanity is never going to amount to anything if he doesn't go back to his roots. Anthony Edwards: What are his roots? Mike Bilodeau: Me. Me and Sandy. I created him. I handle him. So, it's my fault he has been passed over by federation after federation. Anthony Edwards: To tell you the truth Mr. Bilodeau, DV is an inconsistent flasher. Mike Bilodeau: You are right. But, can I explain something to you? Why? Why did Shawn Roy INSIST that Apollo hold the DWO World Title, even though he sent in one flash a month? Because of his name! Dark Vanity does not have that kind of name yet! But, I do! Maybe I requested this interview to use my name to get my character over. So Sue me! We did it all the time in the DWO! Rob Hill held the title even though he was on the verge of retirement and only sending in flashes when he felt like it. HELL! Look at professional wrestling. Hulk Hogan hasn't defended his World title in weeks and never said a word, except on Monday Night's when he's not busy filming another Box Office Bust. Anthony Edwards: You are mentioning a lot of names in this interview. Aren't you afraid of sevvering ties? Losing friends and allies? Mike Bilodeau: Anthony, it's time for my wrestler to shine. I could care less about all these so-called friends. How could I call people I never met in person, a friend? I don't know what these E-Wrestling handlers are doing! They could be sicko serial killers or fat 700 pound men that live in trailer parks and are constantly on computers. The point is, I don't NEED allies and Dark Vanity certainly does not NEED them either. What have they done for me lately, besides send me chain letters with corny love potions? Anthony Edwards: Do you respect anyone in E-Wrestling? Shawn Roy? Mike Bilodeau: Sure. I respect many people. I respect Razi and Jawaad Mahmood for the polite people they are. I respect Martin Richards for the competitor he is. I repsect Derrick Damann for the character he is. But, no, not Roy. I lost respect for him when he quit DWO and brought back this Rage Wrestling League. It was a slap to my face. I also lost respect for many handlers who quit because their guy was not getting title matches. Rob Hill is at the top of that list. He killed his character after I called him a Legend. He retired Sir Giles after we took the title from him and gave it to J'ster. I could sit here for days saying all the people I respect, but I could sit here for years telling you all the people I hate. I'll save the time and I will tell you that I get revenge on People that I hate. Ask Mike Mavis. Ask him what I did to his stable in the DWO. I hate Mavis very much. He's a smug ass punk who tried to ruin my character. He was the first, and not the last. Anthony Edwards: Let's back track for a second and talk about character development again. Where did you meet Ms. Calabrese and how did you work her into the Higher Power character? Mike Bilodeau: We met off the internet. We went to the same high school. She looked me up on America OnLine 2 years ago. We agreed to meet in school. I stood there waiting for her to show up. She came up to me in this little mini skirt and make up, she looked like a hooker. We talked, and talked. She liked wrestling and I liked wrestling. Maybe it was a perfect match. Our first date was a WWF Card in Utica. I got her front row seats and we watched Bret Hart fight Shawn Michaels in a steel cage for the WWF Title. Shawn replaced Jerry Lawler, who was down raping a little girl. I will always remember this match because when Bret gave Shawn a right hook, we were covered in spit. As I got a napkin and wiped the spit off Sandy's face, our eyes locked and then our lips. We've been together ever since, linked by Shawn's spit. Anthony Edwards: Ms. Calabrese, could you tell us a little about yourself? Sandy Calabrese: Tony, I'm just a small town girl. I am not the evil slut that Mike portrays in flashes and strategies. I enjoy e-wrestling with Mike. But, I hate seeing "myself" being talked about as a slut, a whore, or anything else by these handlers. It makes me cry sometimes to hear myself being called that. I never wanted to be portrayed that way. I told Mike to have my character like a Miss Elizabeth, but he told me about his vendetta with that Mavis guy. I tried to understand, but maybe Mike wanted to run this character all on his own. Last summer we had a falling out and I left him. I realized he was the only person that understood me. Mike was never trying to hurt me. He was always nice to me. He's my sweetie. I came back to him on my hands and knees and he took me back. Then last winter, we got in that terrible accident and the doctor thought I was going to be crippled for life. Mike was right by my side all the time I layed in that bed. I love him. Please leave this interview to him, because I don't know too much about E-Wrestling anymore. Anthony Edwards: Mike, who created the Matt Booth character? Mike Bilodeau: It was a combination of my friend Tim and myself. I just wanted a Disco Inferno type character, but Tim said, "Make him gay! Make him gay!" So I did. In the DWO, it got such a reaction, so I'm toning it down in this federation. Matt Booth helped me get revenge on Mike Mavis and Devil Man. And now this character is going to help me open some eyes and take a tag team title. I mean, how long could Chad keep his men as the Tag Champions? You heard me Chad, I respect you, but how long are these promoters going to keep us all down to make their own men champions. That's the only reason some men make federations. I did it. I know how it goes. Chad, I want the tag team titles. I respect you, but I can't let you continue this facade that some unknown handler is the Tag Team Champion. Anthony Edwards: You are getting a US NATIONAL TITLE MATCH with the Franchise aren't you happy? Mike Bilodeau: Do you know how much Jason Dickess kisses ass? The Franchise will not be dropping a title to me. Anthony Edwards: You said earlier you joined E-Wrestling to prove something. What is it that you have to prove? Mike Bilodeau: I'm here in the IWF/WOW to prove one thing. That Dark Vanity is not what most promoters think he is. He is going to take a title here, there's no doubt. Anthony Edwards: You have been admonishing the promoters in this interview, do you honestly think they will give you a title because of it? Mike Bilodeau: They will, or they will lose the Higher Power. It's that simple. Do you know how much the ratings went up in this little interview? I just want to say one last thing before I cut this interview. Shooting was fun, but shooting like a star to the TOP of this federation will be more fun. (Camera fades to black and "Perry Mason" plays. The IWF/WOW tag team rankings roll up the screen in descending order in white letters. IWF/WOW World tag team champions: America's Least Wanted 8-1-2 IWF/WOW North American tag team champions: Horror 7-7-1 1 Southwest Lawmen 5-1-1 2 Sudden Death 4-1-1 3 Vegas Connection 7-4-3 4 Body Count 5-3-1 5 Wrecking Crew 7-5-4 Screen fades in to the first commercial. The commercial is for Nike Zoom Air, the one featuring Kenny Lofton and Michael Johnson. The second commercial is for Krazy Glue. The announcer says "Buy Krazy Glue! It can even suspend this construction worker 50 stories in the air! Cooper Concrete is holding on to his hard hat and hanging underneath a steel girder. Buy Krazy Glue..because It Works!". The next ad is for Copper Tone featuring the Desert Scorpion. Finally is an ad for the IWF Hotline. Rick Wright says "Can't tell you about the IWF here. Call the IWF hotline at 1-900-IWF-RULE. $245.99 per minute, kids do NOT get your parents permission before calling. SURPRISE THEM!" "Perry Mason" plays and the screen fades back in to Anthony Edwards.) Anthony Edwards: Welcome back. It is time for this week's Rumor Report, brought to you by The Ring Report. First up this week is a rumor that is circulating concerning the tag match between the North American Alliance and Cooper Concrete and his mystery partner. Although the rumor doesn't name names, the rumor does state that his partner is NOT a member of the Power Alliance. Next is a rumor that states that Snake Eyes and AJ Stanson are considering teaming up and taking a run at the World Tag Team Titles. Stanson and Snake Eyes would make a very formidible team, and they could take a serious run at those titles if they wanted to. Finally today is a rumor that the Aristocracy will not be lasting very long. Weak locker room sources are reporting that Dan Watterson and Robert d'Artois aren't getting along. These sources, who again are very weak, are reporting an affair between Watterson, and Marie de Cressay. Take this one with a grain of salt. That's it for today's Rumor Report, brought to you as always, by The Ring Report. Back to the flashes now, and here is Martin Richards. (SCENE: Inside of the CRYPT; the private training facility used by MARTIN RICHARDS. It is a dank, dark dungeon of a room that has never seen light outside of one bare swinging light-bulb ten feet over the center of a well-worn wrestling ring. The mat is covered with rips and bloodstains and most of the turnbuckle pads are missing. Pacing the ring is the TROUBLE MAN, leaning and bouncing off the ropes as he talks...) Richards: People seem to want to think I've gone away. Some people also want to think they've made me disappear. Some of them are even right. I've disappeared, all right. Straight into my Crypt. (Richards pauses as he looks around a minute) Richards: Look right here... (He points to the floor and walls) Richards: This room is barely big enough to contain this ring. Too small to put pads around the outside. You go flying outta this ring, junior, and you do a header straight into the brick wall. Right here... (He points to a garbage can, filled with objects. The ends of ax handles and baseball bats are visible, as is a length of rope and wad of balled-up barbed wire) Richards: ...are the tools of the trade. Everyday objects made into lethal weapons. By my old man. (Richards sneers a bit) Richards: My old man. You wanna talk about paybacks, Riser? You wanna talk about paybacks, Coop? Every f**kin' day from the age of ten on my old man brought me down here. And every f**kin' day my old man beat the living hell outta me. (Richards pulls his shirt off and throws it out of the ring. He looks down at his chest) Richards: Look at these (He traces his fingers along a few of the big scars that run along his torso). Some of you can take credit for some of these. Dancing Lion, Dark Vanity. But most of these stitches belong to ol' Alligator Richards. Pops was determined to make a tough man outta me. Made me take him on in a barbed wire match when I was 15. Not even old enough to drive and the f**ker was tossing me against barbed wire; smacking me with ax handles and damn near lynching me his bullrope. He made sure I HAD to brawl on the school ground, just so I could come up with a reason why I always bruised and bloody. (Richards claws at his belly and balls both his hands into fists) Richards: Paybacks. Alligator wanted his legacy to continue. Wanted his only child to follow in his footsteps as the toughest man on God's Green Earth. When I was 17, I went pro. After all I went through, I was a veteran before I even left the Crypt. I started off by tag-teaming with Pops. (He smiles a bit) There was no "Trouble Man" back then. It was Alligator Richards and his boy "Magnificent" Marty. We left bodies all over the Midwest. Time was catching up with the 'Gator. Broken bones took longer to heal, injuries really began to weigh him down. So I had to go through a trial by fire. I had to wrestle entire tag matches while he sat on the apron. I took a beating - BAD beatings - but after a while I caught on. I figured out what it took to take out two or three guys. A devious mindset; which I made sure to cultivate. Why did I take so many lumps for my old man? Respect? No. Who in their right mind would respect a man who put his ONLY SON through such Hell? Christians, I guess. (Richards smiles again, running a hand through his short brown hair) Richards: But I beat that fear. Beat it DAMN good. A few years down the road, Alligator Richards decided to hang up his rope. Like any other man who lived, slept and s**t his livelihood, he wanted to leave the sport with a bang. Scheduled a retirement match, right in our backyard. The Dearborn Civic Center. Over five-hundred people crammed into the small arena to say farewell to the defining heel of the Midwest circuit. And who did the old man wanna face in his last match? His own son. (Richards pauses again, covering a slight smile with his hands as his eyes get a very vacant look to them) Richards: I remember the night before. Right here in the Crypt. The 'Gator and I had a few beers. The ONLY time in my whole life that my dad tried to bond with me. He got all misty-eyed. Told me how proud he was of me. His boy, made hard as f**k by years of physical and mental warfare. His boy, turned into a monster by his own hands. Never had close friends, never able to hold onto a job or a girl. All he could do was fight and take pain. More animal than man. He almost touched me with his sincerity. Then, he decided to cover his ass. He knew what he had made me. He knew he'd have to take on his own creation the next night. And he knew he couldn't win. So, he asked me to take a fall. But not as the friend he tried to become that night. He asked me with those eyes. Those eyes that read of twenty years of contempt towards the burden that my mother left him with. And I agreed. He knew I would, with those eyes. He never even thanked me. (Richards drops his hands, smiling widely and still unfocused) Richards: It's a good thing he didn't, either. Main event time, the bell rings. I felt the old man out; I knew what he could and couldn't do. That was the advantage I had over the so-called "Devil from Detroit." The crowd roaring as we went blow-for-blow. And I held back for the man...for a while. Something in me snapped. Years of everything, built up on my hardened heart managed to pierce the wall. I saw red as I unloaded everything on the stunned old sap. (Richards eyes retain their focus, as he stares right into the camera) Richards: I broke the f**ker's neck. Right there, in the middle of the ring. On his night. The night he chose to leave the sport, a winner for the last time. The night EVERY wrestler builds up their career for. The night he thought he'd win. And I took it all from him. A series of reverse neckbreakers, applied in the brutal fashion that he taught me, cracking his old vertebrae.
