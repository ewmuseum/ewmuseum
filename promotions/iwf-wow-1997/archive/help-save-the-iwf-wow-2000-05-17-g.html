A lot of people left, including ME >at >the time :-). > > > >[name withheld] >Handler of HADES, God of Wrestling ># 29 1997 RSPWF 200 ># 131 1998 RSPWF 200 ># 94 1999 RSPWF 200 >Former 11 Time, 11 Time, 11 Time World Champion >Current Fed(s): IWF/WOW, MotR III tourney >Current Title(s): None > > > > > > > > I believe he was refering to the whole Pain Inc. angle. I can't remember exactly what happened but I think the leader of Pain Inc., Payne was shot by fellow member Richard Blair, Thus making Blair the leader of Pain Inc. There was supposed to be more to this angle but they just left it as Payne getting shot. In my opinion, I didn't think this was too good of a move for the federation or for the character. If somebody was murdered on live TV, I certainly don't think that Richard Blair or whoever would still be wrestling. He would be in prison. And the IWF/WOW really didn't make a big deal out of the situation either. They just kept going like nothing ever happened. And what if Payne ever wanted to come back? How could he? He was murdered.
