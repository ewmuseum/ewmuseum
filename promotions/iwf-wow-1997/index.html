A merged Usenet federation whose WrestleFest '97 supercard, live from the SkyDome in Toronto, crowned new IWF and WOW World Champions on the same card as a twenty-man battle royal and the first-ever IWF/WOW North American Tag Team Champions. Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

IWF/WOW formed in February 1997 from the merger of two federations already running since 1996, one from that April and the other from that summer. No founding document of the merged promotion itself survives, but its first supercard follows within weeks.

That supercard, WrestleFest '97, ran live from the SkyDome in Toronto on 17 March 1997 in front of a crowd the broadcast put past 60,000. Derrick DaMann won the IWF World Heavyweight Championship from AJ “Black Ice” Stanson after the newly crowned WOW World Champion, Jeremy “Roadkill” Thranton, ran in and grabbed DaMann's leg mid-move, setting up the victory roll that won the title. The same card crowned the first IWF/WOW North American Tag Team Champions and closed a twenty-man battle royal with Joe Mills the last man standing.

The two rival lineages ran side by side for a year before folding into one IWF/WOW World Heavyweight Championship. The Prophet, already a three-time champion, lost it to Christopher Stonebreaker at WrestleFest II (22 March 1998) when a stablemate's turn sent a kick meant for someone else into the champion by mistake. Stonebreaker held the title through two further pay-per-views before losing it to the Idol at Friday Night WARFARE (30 October 1998), wrestling that night under a special guest referee the record calls biased against him.

No document narrates what happened after that: the promotion's own periodic bulletin of 4 August 1999 instead bills Cooper Concrete, already the reigning US National Champion since 28 July 1998, as the new World Heavyweight Champion, with the transition itself unrecorded.

The last dated document in the surviving record is a Mayhem broadcast of 24 July 2001, opening on a highlight reel of the promotion's own past matches and closing on a preview of an upcoming Summer Explosion main event pairing Shane St. Clair and Razor Ron Jeremy against Justin Arcola and Robert d'Artois. Nothing dated later than that survives, and no closing notice is on record.
