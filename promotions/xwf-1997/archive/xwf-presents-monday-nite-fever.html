XWF presents Monday Nite Fever

Jun 29, 1997, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Fireworks flare and the Monday Nite Fever blimp is flying high above Marsh Stadium) HH: Helllllllllooooooo!!!! XWWWWWWWFFFFF fans!!! Welcome to XWF's Monday Nite Feverrrr!! I'm Harvy H and sitting next to me is Bob Winkle BW: Tonight we have the XWF World Hammerfist Title and the XWF Light Heavyweight on-the-line!! But enough idle chit-chat lets cut to a promo of XWF's Ring Rumble: Kings of Chaos '97 (screen fades to black, the screen then opens up on an empty ring with Jerry "The King" Lawler standing in the centre of it) JL: Hello it's me the KING but thats in reality, not e-wrestling so if you wanna see the e-wrestling king made KING then watch Ring Rumble: Kings of Chaos live via e-mail and rec.sports.pro-wrestling.fantasy!!!! Harvy H Bob Winkle and I will be there dishing out gourgous play-by-plays as it happens when it happens so if you wanna a chance at being e-wrestling king let the President of your fed know now and say you want in the XWF PPV and represent your fed today because it's INTER-PROMOTIONAL!!!!!! (screen fades out as THE KING piledrives a man selling hot-dogs, and sounds like "gimme a free hot dog ya punk" and "ARghRdDGh" sounds) Ring Announcer: And now the first match on XWF's Monday Nite Fever is Stevie Richards Vs Mr Mad!!! And now coming to the ring, being accompanied by MRS.SANE!! and from the dentists chair weighing in at 290 pounds he is MR MADDDDDD!!! (histerical laughs play over the PA system as Mr Mad's music) And his opponent being accompanied by the BLUE MEANIE, he is 6'3" and 225 pounds he is STEVIEEEEEE RICHARDDDSSSSS!!!! (Fireworks explode all the way down to ring side as Stevie Richards and the Blue Meanie climb in the ring) (ding ding ding) HH: Both men are circuling inside the ring. BW: Mad is coming off the ropes at Stevie but Stevie kicks him in the mid-section and then delivers a europieon uppercut which sends Mad crashing to the mat, Mad soon kicks up and creeps up behind Stevie who is show boating to the crowd HH: Ohhh Mad just kicked Stevie in the balls which sent him over the top rope BW: Mad is now distracting the ref while Mrs.Sane hits Stevie with a high heel she is now looking under the ring HH: She pulls out a chair as the Blue Meanie creeps up behind and POW the Blue Meanie is out cold on the floor. BW: Stevie is on the ring appron but Mad is comming off the ropes and has dropkicked Stevie into the 3rd row!!! HH: The ref is having a go at Mad while Mrs.Sane is holding a chair and she is about to hit Stevie when 3M and Miss Mel jumps out of the crowd and 3M starts hitting Stevie and Miss Mel works on Mrs Sane but the Blue Meanie is now in the crowd and is kicking the **** out of 3M. BW: 3M and Miss Mel are now running into the locker rooms!! Stevie is helped back into the ring by Blue Meanie and the ref. HH: Mad is on the top rope and goes for a cross body and he lands on Stevie but he did it to fast and he rolls over...Stevie is now on top of Mad....1-2-kick out from Mad both men struggle to there feet. BW: Mad kicks Stevie in the mid-section and then powerbombs him for the 1-2-3!! Ring Announcer: The winner by pinfall Mrrrr. MADDDDDD!!!! HH: Woal what a match that was, Stevie must be livid after that loss. (Stevie and Blue Meanie run after Mad into the locker rooms) BW: The next match should be good!! Ring Announcer: This match is for the XWF Light Heavyweight title !!! The first man is making his debut in Xtreme Wrestling Federation he is being accompanied by "beautiful" Brooke he is Scorpion (fireworks flare and lights flash all around Marsh Stadium) And the XWF Light Heavyweight Champion, being accompanied by 3M he is Funki H!! (Fireworks explode in sequence to the music of "Oh H your so Funki") HH: While Funki is dancing on the apron Scorpion grabs his neck and suplexes him into the ring then comes off the ropes and delivers an atomic leg drop he then comes off the ropes again and delivers another leg drop...then another he has Ko'ed Funki and has busted him open....Scorpion turns Funki over and then locks on the Boston Crab......The ref is asking FH is he wants to give in... BW: But how can he if he's KO'ed the ref is asking FH again...FH is coming to and he screams out in pain that he gives up!!!! Ring Announcer: The winner by submission and new XWF Light Heavywieght Champion Scorpion (Mr Marsh is walking down to ring side with Mr Hunter) MM: I would like to introduce the newest member of the XWF executive board, he is new Vice-President of XWF he is Mr Hunter!!!! MH: Hello! I hope everyones enjoying themselves!! (crowd goes nuts) (Mr Marsh and Mr Hunter take Havry's and Bob's places for commentery) Ring Announcer: Now coming to the ring and weighing in at 300 pounds he is Joe Atkins (no fireworks or music go on because Joe Atkins is a div and didn't want any) And the XWF World Hammerfist Champion being accompanied by Funki H and Miss Mel he is 3MMMMM!!!!! (fireworks flash the colours of the spectrum while red green and all other funky coloured lights flash around Marsh Stadium) MM: 3M sticks his middle finger up at Atkins so Atkins answers with a boot to the head which sends 3M into the corner. MH: Atkins is taunting 3M with fingers and jeers, 3M shakes off the boot and delivers a hard right hand to Atkins, 3M goes for a pin.... MM: He doesn't even get a 1 count, 3M picks him up and throws him into the ropes, 3M picks Atkins up for the powerbomb but Atkins reverses it into a hurra carana (Frankensteiner) Atkins goes for the pin 1-2 kickout from 3M MH: 3M is setting him up for the Meanie (a spine buster off the top rope) but Atkins gets out of the head lock and choke slams 3M to the canvas and Atkins is coming off the rope and delivers an Atomic leg drop MM: Atkins is going to the top rope, he's jumped off and has...yes he planted the Belly Up finisher (Belly Up is just a big splash) 1-2-3 Ring Announcer: The winner by pinfall and NEW XWF World Hammerfist Champion Joe Atkins!!! MM: Woal that was an upset win 3M does not look happy!! MH: Well I hope 3M gets a re-match because the Atkins is a thick kid and doesn't deserve a title. MM: Hey Hunter calm down that's the way it goes.....well till next Monday this is Mr Marsh saying goodbye from me Mr Hunter Harvy H and Bob Winkle!!! _________________________CREDITS_____________________________________ Commentaters: Harvy H Bob Winkle President Mr Marsh Vice-President Mr Hunter Venue Marsh Stadium Wrestle Isle VSA* *Virtual States of America [name withheld] [detail withheld] XWF* President *Xtreme Wrestling Federation is an email fantasy wrestling fed is you want to join email me
