The Story So Far

26 August 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The Scene is inside a pristine television studio. Several monitors adorn the walls, and a mural of past and present XWF wrestlers is splashed across the back wall. Sitting at a black table, dressed in formal tuxedos and bow ties, are two men. One of them is obviously tall, even though he is sitting down. He has thinning dark hair neatly combed across his head, and a beard covering his face. His name is Joe Jones, World renowned wrestling expert and first choice commentator for the XWF. Next to him is Harry Havoc, short spiky blonde hair atop a craggy, time worn face that is possessed with a permanent scowl. He is an ex-wrestler, the very first ever XWF World Champion, a man who, despite his caustic and often brutal colour commentary, remains first and foremost, a fan.) Jones : Good evening everybody, and welcome to this week's edition of 'Inside the XWF' - your weekly magazine show of the events and action that have taken place inside the best wrestling federation on the Planet over the past seven days ! I'm Joey Jones, and with me here is three time XWF Champ, the recently retired Harry Havoc. (Havoc nods once at the camera) Jones : Tonight, we recap what's been a remarkable few weeks for everyone's favourite wrestling Fed by examining the issues and politics that have led to our top two Title Belts becoming vacant : The prestigious XWF World Title and Hardcore Belt. Havoc : You know Joey, it makes me sick to see the World Title just sitting around gathering dust, especially after the shame that scum bucket McVries bought on it. It's lucky for him he's now gone from the XWF, 'cos if he wasn 't, I'd kick his ass but good ! Jones : Taking a step back for a sec, I guess we'd better explain what's been happening recently. Firstly, Marc 'The Maniac' McVries has been fired from the XWF after failing a drugs test, and as a result, the Belt he was holding, the XWF World Title, has been declared vacant. President Logan has promised an announcement regarding the future of the Belt by the time this programme ends. Secondly, let's take you back to last's Thursday night's show, where, in the Main Event, 'Gorgeous' Ian Gregory took on the mysterious and deadly Abbadon for the Hardcore Title Belt. (Scene fades into a shot of the ring, where the seven foot tall Abbadon is grappling with Hardcore Champion Ian Gregory. Jones and the resident weasel of the BWFE a small, balding man who demands he be called 'The Brain', are commentating.) Jones : Gregory with a takedown now, but Abbadon is up almost immeadiately. Gregory is giving away almost six inches in height here, and about the same in reach ! Unless he stays out of the big man's way, he's going to get hurt, and hurt bad. Brain : Hardcore matches are all about pain, and let me tell you, nobody takes pain like Abbadon can - the man is not human ! Jones : Abbadon lunges at the Champ, and grabs him around the throat, picking him up...and down to the canvas with a vicious powerbomb ! That one had to hurt ! Gregory clutches his back now, as Abbadon stomps away, a look of pure evil on his deathly pale face. Abbadon hauls a dazed Gregory to his feet, and hurls him into the ringpost. Abbadon now..charging in towards the corner anddddddd...boom ! Massive, massive clothesline there from the big man ! Brain : Stick a fork in him, he's done ! Jones : Abbadon is signalling for his finisher here....here comes the Absolution !!! BIG face first DDT on Ian Gregory, and the Champion's nose has just exploded, spraying blood everywhere ! Brain : Ha ! He don't look so 'Gorgeous' now, does he ?!! Jones : Abbadon goes for the cover..but wait ! Wait ! The lights have just gone out !!!!! What's happening ? (The lights flicker out, and the arena is in pitch black darkness. When they come on again, President Logan is standing in the ring, alongside a muscular looking wrestler who has Abbadon locked in a sleeper hold. Logan has a microphone.) Logan : Listen up you peasants ! I told all of you when I finally ran Hart's lame duck ass out of this Fed that if anybody crossed me ever again, I would break them. And just last night, Gregory went against my explicit orders by giving a Title shot to Abbadon. And that makes me angry. In fact..it pisses me OFF !!! (Loud crowd boos) Jones : Who the heck is the man in the ring with President Logan ? Brain : I don't know but he must be strong, he's got Abbadon locked in solid with that sleeper hold ! Logan : I said that NO-ONE was to give Abbadon a Title Shot. Ever. And I also said that any new wrestlers who entered the XWF would get a push that would make them superstars in a matter of weeks...and that promise begins with the man you see before you tonight ! Jones : Yes, yes, but the heck IS he ?!! Logan : As punishment for breaking MY law and going against MY wishes..I hereby strip Ian Gregory of the Hardcore Title and declare it vacant !!!!!! Furthermore, both Gregory and Abbadon are hereby FIRED from the XWF !!!!!!!!! (Crowd gasps in shock before beginning to boo loudly) Logan : Shuttup !!!!!!! This match, and this damned show is OVER !!!!!! (The mystery wrestler releases the sleeper hold on Abbadon, who sinks groggily to the canvas. Logan and the mystery man leave the ring, and begin to walk up the aisle, but a sudden cheer erupts from the crowd.) Jones : Dread is here !!!! Former XWF Champion Dread is at the top of the ramp !!! (Dread, the near seven foot tall crowd favourite, lifts a microphone to his mouth.) Dread : Now hold on just a sec, son. I'm a little bit sick and tired of you running around this here Fed saying this, and saying that, and it's going to stop. You'd best turn that skinny whitebread ass of yours and hightail it out of here, or I'm going to fuck you right up !!!!!!! (Huge crowd pop) Logan : It's up to you, steroid boy...you either move out of the way..or I' ll have you moved !!!!! (Dread smiles, and suddenly drops the mic, running towards the President. The mystery man steps in the way, and the two men begin to brawl. Logan stands back, watching. A second newcomer runs from the ring entrance, and joins in with the first mystery man in beating on Dread. Logan watches this with a satisfied smile.) Jones : And who the heck is THAT ?!!!!!!! Folks, this is incredible !!! A second mystery man has just joined the first and started to attack Dread !!! This is unbelievable !!!! (Suddenly, yet another man jumps from the crowd with a chair in his hand. He swings the chair as hard as he can, and it cracks across the back of Logan's head. The President goes down hard, hitting the floor with a dull thud. The crowd erupts as they realise who the chair wielding man is) Jones : THAT'S ALEX HART !!!!!!! EX-PRESIDENT ALEX HART !!!!!!!!! MY GOD !!!!!!!!!! And the man who's company was ripped from him by Logan has returned to take his revenge !!!!!!!!!! Brain : How in the Hell did he get into the building ?!!!!!! Jones : And the two mystery men have broken from beating on Dread, and as Hart dives back into the crowd, the two men help a groggy and dazed President Logan to his feet ! Here comes Xanthus and Chris Hoff !!!!! Xanthus and Chris Hoff have come up to make the save for Dread, and as all four men brawl up and down the asile, we are out of time !!!!! Tune in next week folks !!!!! (Scene fades back into the studio, where Joe Jones and Harry Havoc are waiting.) Jones : Well, there you have it folks. For those of you who are not up to speed on current events, allow me to explain. A while ago, President Hart had Logan bought in as his new VP, and Logan proceeded to try and take over the entire XWF. He enlisted the help of Shawn Maverick and Adam Edge, both of whom have now left the Federation, and set about trying to destroy Hart. One PPV, during a vicious blow off match just two months ago, Logan and Hart fought for Presidency and Ownership of the company. During the match, Maverick and Edge interfered, and helped Logan get the pin, and the win. Hart left the company, and until last night, hadn't been seen since. Havoc : Except now it looks like Alex Hart has come back to gain revenge, and judging by the size and shape of him, he's been working out. I tell ya, for months now, those wrestlers have had to stand by and watch Logan run the company into the ground, and now it looks like they're out to put a stop to it. Jones : It won't be easy. Logan has made his position clear by stating that he intends to bring in a new generation of wrestler, one's who he hopes will take the XWF to a new level. In doing so, he claims he will rid the XWF of the 'old guard' who have served the Fed so well for so long. Havoc : It can't be allowed to happen ! It can't ! Logan : It can't be stopped, old man. I can't be stopped. (Logan enters the studio, flanked by the two mystery men from the earlier clips.) Logan : Allow me to introduce the two newest members of the XWF. This is Sulfer, a former SIW World Champion, and he's the man who was in the ring teaching Abbadon not to mess with the Prez. This other man is Steve Kragen, and he is the baddest man on ANY Planet ! Jones : Why are you here Logan ? Logan : You watch your mouth Joey ! Just remember who pays your goddamn wages ! This here is the card for Tuesday Night Fight. As you can see, the now vacant Hardcore Title will be contested between Dread and Sulfer. Also, there will be a Battle Royal for the vacant World Title, and Kragen will be entered into it. Havoc : You can't do this ! These two 'rookies' have done NOTHING to deserve such a push ! Logan : Zip it old man. I told you already, anybody who accepts my offer to enter the XWF will be pushed above the bunch of losers who are here already, and there is nothing you can do about it ! (Havoc moves as if to get up and confront Logan, but Kragen stops him.) Kragen : You best sit the fuck back down mister. Now. Havoc : I don't respond well to empty threats....... Sulfer : Good. Because we don't make them. Jones : Uh, folks, we're out of time.....but be sure to join us Tuesday night for what should be one of the biggest night's in XWF history so far !!!!!!
