PWC TV Results for May 31st, 2003 Live from the Wildwood Convention Center in Wildwood, NJ The show opens as we watch a buoyant Karina Wolfenden arrive. With bunches pulling the bright hair from her eyes, the smile her lips carry is plain to see... And as she removes her golden-lens glasses, she shows there is a glimmer in her eyes to match. Karina: Always good to check in for my Saturday night fight therapy... The love for wrestling is back with Karina Wolfenden. After the finale to last week's PWC TV where she stood side by side with the Champion, it's fun again... It's a rush. It seems that every loose end has been tied up. Karina: ...Now, time to see who's coming my way... Eager to see who she'll draw in the 2nd round of the Last Man Standing, her stride is now a mix between a walk and a jog... Until she's stopped dead in her tracks. Karina: Oh, er.... Hey... Emerging in front of the K-Wolf is Paula Michelle Smith. The way the two women act around one another gives away that this was not a planned encounter, and despite their brief moment of solidarity 7 days ago, they're uneasy in the other's presence. Karina's care free demeanour becomes something a little more uncomfortable, hands in her pockets and struggling for words. Karina: I was going to call you, but... Well, I didn't know what to say... Karina: Seems to be becoming a regular thing, huh? Both woman still look ill at ease with being in the other's presence. There isn't the tension, or aggression... Just a distinct lack of what to say.
