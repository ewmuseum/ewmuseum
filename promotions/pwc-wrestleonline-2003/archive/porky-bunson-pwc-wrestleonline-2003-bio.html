Porky Bunson Biography Nicknames 'Hard Luck' Hometown Chicken Lick, Kentucky Birthday November 15, 1966 Height 6'6 Weight 271 Biography Earl Roy Bunson and Beth Bunson were a newly married couple in March of 1966. The couple, rich in Southern Heritage, inherited the Bunson Family Farm in Chicken Lick, kentucky following the death of Earl Roy's father. The 120 acre farm was primarily cornfields and cattle and the 30 year old Earl Roy hired farmhands in an effort to effectively farm the large area. One farmhand had a ten year old son named Delmis Cratchett and abandoned his son to the Bunson Family one evening never to return. Beth Bunson gave birth to a baby boy in November of 1966. A huge infant that weighed 16 pounds as a newborn. Earl Roy held the child close to his chest and named him Porky Jack Bunson. As Porky Jack was learning to feed himself, Beth Bunson was affiliating herself with a financially set businessman named Lars and left her family to persue a more lucrative relationship. Earl Roy was saddled with the responsibilities of taking care of his adopted ten year old son, Delmis and Porky Jack. Earl Roy was not an educated man. He had no schooling at a young age and as an adult believed that children should learn a 'hard day's work' rather than learning when a historical figure invented bathroom tissue. So, he gave Delmis and Porky Jack a choice of education. Delmis chose school. Porky Jack, who was five years old, chose to work around the Farm with his father. Thus, a strong bond formed. Because of the lack of schooling, Delmis at the age of ten years old managed the money. Farming had not been lucrative and the Bunson Farm lost thousands of dollars a month in operation. Porky Jack lived a straight forward life. He held no interest in women. Many believed his mother's abandonment was the cause of his anti-social behavior. Years passed. People age and in the year 2001, Porky Jack was 35 years old and still living with his father and Delmis and were still struggling to make a living. Early March 2001, a Realtor named Ian Trapp had bought farmland under the market value with devious cons. The Chicken Lick community consisted of thirty farmhouses. In the span of one week, the Farmers had cashed in either by force or by necessity. Ian Trapp focused his eyes on the Bunson Farm. By Mid March, Earl Roy Bunson died from a massive heart attack and was buried in the backyard of the Farm by Porky Jack and Delmis Cratchett. Porky Jack Bunson inherited the Farm and tried in desperation to cope with the enormous pressures. He began to take pep pills as he would work eighteen hours a day, six days a week. Ian Trapp kept bargaining with Porky Jack to sell but Porky would not budge. This was his home and life. A year goes by and then on October 21st of 2002, Ian Trapp gave Porky Jack Bunson his final offer of purchase for the Farm. Porky Jack refused. Porky Jack awakes a few weeks later and does his morning routine of taking a pill to get through the hectic day. At the breakfast table, he collapses and is threatened by a Sheriff that arrived to his home to get Rehabilitation for drug addiction or serve jail time. Porky admitted himself into St. Henry's Hospital for treatment and met several individuals who insisted on giving Porky Jack the address to a Wrestling Academy due to Porky Jack standing at 6'6 and weighing 271 pounds. That night in rehab, he learned through a local Real Estate Newspaper that his Farm had been sold to Ian Trapp without his consent. Escaping that night on a bus to home, he found his home chained off and inaccessible. With the Primetime Wrestling Academy address written on his forearm in ink, he went by bus to Trenton, New Jersey where he would study to be a Professional Wrestler. He was homeless and the PWA paid no expenses. He stayed at a park and scrounged for food. While in a Truck Stop begging for peanuts on the promise he will pay the clerk back, he had a heart attack. The thirty-six year old was treated at a New Jersey Hospital by Dr. Taylor, who released him that night. Porky Jack continued to work tryout matches in PWA despite homeless and a heart condition. He began experiencing more attacks on his heart and his blood would boil. Little did he know that he was being watched. Laying in an alley he called home, a hired hand named Deets who worked for Mr. Trapp brought him to Manhattan, New York. Ian Trapp told Porky Jack Bunson that Delmis Cratchett had sold the farm to him by forging Porky Jack's signature. Ian Trapp told Porky that the Sheriff who told Porky to get rehabilitation was an actor hired by Ian Trapp. The individuals who gave Porky Jack the PWA address were planted there in a conspiracy. The biggest bombshell came when Ian Trapp told Porky Jack Bunson that his heart problems following the Hospital stay were Microchip enhanced! With the turn of a knob on a remote in the hands of Ian Trapp, Porky could be given a heart attack at Trapp's mercy. Ian Trapp explained how Porky Jack bunson will be his 'personal toy and moneymaker.' He wanted to own the thirty-six year old man and he felt he could make money by wrestling affiliation. Delmis Cratchett and Deets were then ordered to keep him under watch. That changed in November. While Porky Jack trained in Hannibal County, New York at a secluded area he was told by Ian Trapp that Delmis Cratchett has stolen $30 million dollars. Now, Porky Jack must juggle his wrestling dream and the hunt for the money that the deceased Delmis Cratchett hid.
