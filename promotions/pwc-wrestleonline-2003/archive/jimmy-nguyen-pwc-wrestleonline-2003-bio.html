Jimmy Nguyen Biography Nicknames Jim, James, New-Win Hometown Philadelphia, Pennsylvainia Birthday February 8th, 1976 Height 6'1' Weight 182 Fed Titles Current 1/2 Of Tag Team Champions Biography Jimmy Nguyen's life growing up was typical. Nothing majorly out of the ordinary, for growing up in Philadephia at least. He lived in a small city section with his father, who was half Vietnemese, and his mother, whos ancestry traced back to Germany. They lived rather close to Chinatown. So on days as a child when he wasn't attening PS 112 he would wander around Chinatown, mostly by himself. His parents never really paid much attention to him, but that doesn't matter. He eventally became known as a regular to those parts of Chinatown, most everyone knew him and was friendly with him. From that he became soo emfatuated with the Aisan lifestyle that as he grew older he decided he wanted to learn Martial Arts. Being one quarter Vietnemese he had his grandfather who had immigrated from Vietnam help him enroll in a class. He then first enrolled in a Tae-Kwon-Do class when he was 11, by suggestion from his Grandfather. He studied Tae-Kwon-Do until he was 17 and he left with a 2nd Degree Black Belt. He then began college at Temple University, majoring in Communications and at the same time began to learn the Brazilian Dance Martial Arts form of Capoeira. After learning much of the Capoeira form he jumped in the pro-wrestling profession, to work his way through college and on a request from a friend. Many didn't think he would do well due to his lack of self-confidence but somehow, he has.
