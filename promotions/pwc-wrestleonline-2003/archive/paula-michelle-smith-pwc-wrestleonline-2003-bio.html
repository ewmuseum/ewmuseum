Paula Michelle Smith Biography Nicknames PMS, The Punk Princess, The Maiden of Pain Hometown Seattle, Washington Birthday May 25, 1986 Height 5'3 and 1/2' Weight 101 Biography According to Dante, the inscription above the gates of Hell reads, in part: “Abandon all hope, all those who enter here.” You may be thinking to yourself: I’m not entering Hell, but only the mind of a little 16-year-old girl. Don’t kid yourself, for the warning still applies. My life and times are no carnival, no picnic, and definitely no Sunday retreat. Look through me, and you shall find suffering, pain, rage, hate, and other nasty things. The path I walk is the way of the lost, with drugs, alcohol, rape, and all the evils of all the world’s men as mile markers. I was created through lust, not love, and the emotion remained absent throughout my life. We are entering me… abandon your hope. I was born late in the evening on what I am told was a cold, rainy night during an unusually wintry May. At the time, my father was a salesman for a small fastener (you know, nuts and bolts and washers) company. He was often traveling up and down the west coast, and wasn’t even there at the time of my birth. My mother, a high school dropout who worked part-time as a waitress at a particularly sleazy Denny’s, almost died during labor. She survived, but only with her ability to bear more children forever destroyed; somehow, I got blamed for this. I finally met my father when I was just over two weeks old. He had always wanted a strong boy, and I was a very frail little girl. Years later, when I was once holding my mother’s hair back as she threw up a night’s worth of alcohol, I heard her moan something about him almost divorcing her at this point. When I was young, he tried to mold me into something of a tomboy. For a while, desperate for his love, I went along with this… and for a while, things seemed to be all right. Granted, the older I got, the more depressed my mother seemed, and the more she turned towards alcohol and drugs, but things between father and me were good once. He used to promise to protect me, and to keep me safe. He lied. The more I grew, matured, and became an individual, the further I got away from what he expected and wanted of me, until eventually, I had rejected all of his ideals. We fought often, and it often ended in physical violence against me. He would go on business trips, and come back to find my mother in the arms of another man. He would then direct his anger and sexual frustrations towards me… Meanwhile, my mother had lost countless jobs, finally dedicated herself to unemployment, and deteriorated further daily. Her drugs, alcohol, and adulterous relationships were too much for me to take. The two of them were killing me slowly. I ran away often. The police would bring me home, and wouldn’t believe any of my “stories.” Maybe it’s because of my father, but maybe it is because of these police officers that I hate authority figures to this day. At school, I had joined and then quickly dropped out of Drama Club. While I enjoyed acting, and was quite good at it (as a freshman, I had the starring role in the senior play), I hated all the stupid people there. I ditched, and failed, classes. And there were teachers that were willing to make… sick, sick deals for me to get “extra credit” in some of those classes. And I took them. As wrong as they were, I agreed. The only person who brought me any sort of happiness was my best friend, Charlotte Manning. Of course, my parents hated her. Now, here I am. I got into wrestling to get away from Seattle, and my wretched family. I came here, with my moronic little follower, so that I could see somebody else suffer for once. I don’t care if it’s Ariath, or the other wrestlers on the roster, I just want somebody to hurt like I have… and I want to see it… and maybe, I want to inflict it. And you could try to accuse me of running away from my problems, but know this: in the back of my mind, I still plot revenge on all those back home that have wronged me. Also, if anybody here crosses me, there’s plenty of room in my mind to plan their demise as well. CAST OF CHARACTERS Charlotte Manning – My best friend, and practically my polar opposite. She is light, happy, and optimistic. She says that she sees “the angel” in me, and while I completely disagree, I have to admit that unconditional love (as dumb as it can be sometimes) is kinda nice. Jonathon Norton – Also known as Ariath, he is the wrestler that I am both managing and acting as tag team partner for. Andrea Jones (formerly Andrea Norton) – Ariath’s ex-wife, she is a selfish, stupid, condescending bitch. Sure, she once took me in and cleaned me up after my father had beaten me, but she had an impatient and ‘holier than thou’ attitude the entire time… like it was the end of the damn world to help me. Christopher Norton – Ariath’s son, who lives with Andrea. I don’t know much about him; just that Ariath never really utilized his visitation rights. Mr. Smith – My father, a large, brutish, red-headed man who resents me for being a girl, and resents my mother for being unable to have another child. He always seems to be away on business, but the little amount of time I’ve spent with him was always hell: verbal, physical… and, once, ‘other’ abuse… I don’t want to talk about him anymore. Mrs. Smith – My mother, who lives off of unemployment checks. She’s currently in jail, awaiting possession charges, with intent to sell. I made a deal to get Ariath out of prison, and keep her there. Last we talked, she wasn’t too pleased with me. Heather Reed – The District Attorney back in Seattle who will be the prosecutor in my mother’s trial.
