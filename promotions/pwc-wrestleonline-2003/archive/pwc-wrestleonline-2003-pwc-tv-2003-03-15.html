The show opens in a dingy, dark office in the annals of Drozdov gymnasium, where a single desk lamp serves to illuminate a bright yellow legal pad, where Pro Wrestling Circuit CEO Ron Starr is busily putting pen to paper. Reaching over to grab a swig of Coca-Cola from a already open can, Starr taps the pen rapidly against the side of his head in thought… …Thought which is broken by the door to the office suddenly being opened. Starr looks up in surprise, until he sees who is in the doorway, then his tension breaks and frowns a bit. Voice (from off camera): Don’t look so happy to see me, babe. With that, the man hated world around, the STAR~!, and lastly, the first ever OSW World Heavyweight Champion, Sebastian Toomes slides into the chair in front of Starr’s desk, with his trademark sly smile on his face. Starr ignores Toomes, and goes back to his writing. Toomes, perplexed peers over the desk to see what Starr is penning, only to have Starr suddenly look up, and put his forearm over the contents of the manuscript. Toomes: Watcha writing? Starr, pulls the pad off the desk, and down to his lap. Starr: I’m making a list of all the people I plan on firing from this promotion in the not-so-distant-future. Toomes raises an eyebrow, and once again begins to peer over at the pad with much curiousity. Toomes: Oh yeah? Well, do tell! Starr opens up the drawer to his desk and tosses the pad in side. Toomes makes a quick grab for the pad, but ends up getting his fingers slammed in the drawer. Toomes: OOOOOOWWWWWWWEEEE!!! Starr: Serves you right. Toomes sucks on his fingers for a moment, then frowns. Toomes: Come on! I just wanted to know who is getting shit-canned!! Starr sits back in his chair and smiles slyly at Toomes. Toomes suddenly forgets about his throbbing fingers, as a look of agitation and perhaps even fear crosses his face. Toomes: (swallowing hard) Wait a minute…. MY name isn’t on that list, is it? I mean, you wouldn’t fire the STAR~! would you? Starr leans back in his chair and folds his fingers behind his neck, still silently smiling. Toomes: I mean, COME ON, afterall I was the FIRST OSW WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION!!! You wouldn’t fire me, would you? Starr leans back forward, when his cell phone suddenly rings. Starr reaches into the pocket of his leather coat, and answers the phone. Starr: Hello? After a moment’s pause, Starr removes the phone from his ear, and puts his hand over the receiver. Starr: Seabass, I really need to take this call. Go get ready for your tag match tonight. Toomes looks on in anxiousness as Starr returns to his phone call. Toomes: Bu… Starr cups the receiver once again, then backhandedly waves at Toomes. Starr: Mu-mu!!! Toomes grimaces while he rises from as Starr continues to backhandedly wave him from the room. Toomes looks back at Starr one last time before backing out of the room, but finds the CEO’s desire for him to leave unwavering. Stamping his feet like a little kid, Toomes huffs, then storms from the room. Starr smiles and shakes his head, then returns to his phone conversation. Starr: Sorry about that. We still on for tonight? Starr pauses as he listens to the person on the other end of the phone’s response….one that makes the already smiling Starr’s grin widen. Starr: Good, good. I will see you in a few…. Ciao! Starr switches the mobile phone off and nods to himself contentedly….as the scene fades to black. . . . . . . . . The scene reopens backstage, where it’s plain to be seen that the camera is now focused on the part of this small arena that is directly connected to the parking lot. By now, the parking lot’s activity has been reduced to a few assorted sports cars darting this way and that, but for the most part, it’s exactly as it should look, the show having already been kicked off. “The hell…When’s he gonna get here?” As we hear a growl from offstage, the camera whirls to show Jimmy Nguyen, dressed in street clothes, leaning against a cement wall. The young martial artist looks down frequently towards his watch, apparently waiting for someone. Being as Chandler Tsonda, the ever-cynical and older counterpart of Nguyen, is not present, it would not be surprising if he were the exact man Jimmy is waiting for. Jimmy: C’mon, Chandler, man. We’ve got a tag title shot later tonight… Indeed, Chandler is the cause for Jimmy’s anxiety, as the former PWA standout taps his foot irritably against the concrete. Hearing the screech of wheels nearby, Nguyen looks up to see none other than the man whom he is waiting for: Chandler Tsonda. Chandler: Ah, Jim, just the man I wanted to see. Calling across the parking lot, Tsonda begins to hurry towards Nguyen, who is breathing a sigh of relief as he sees the leather-jacket clad ex-model heading his way. As Chandler nears, Nguyen reveals what his nervousness was about. Jimmy: Chandler! Dude, I thought you might not make it…but then again, I mean, who’s gonna miss us becoming champions? As he reaches Nguyen, the two men, together making up 2GVC, begin to walk down the hallway that leads into the building’s bowels, where their locker room…and the monster Penance, are situated. Chandler: Ehh…that whole “becoming champions” bit? We tried that once before and it didn’t really work out. In fact, I’d say-on the relative scale of working out-it was probably into the negatives. Giving Jimmy a look of “I told you so,” the two men continue to walk, as Jimmy explains his new idea to his tag partner and comrade. Jimmy: No, no, no! That was last time. This time, we’re sure to leave Penance lying and to walk out with the tag titles firmly around our waists! The enthusiasm being exuded by the youngster (in relativity to Tsonda) brings a hybrid sneer-frown upon the face of Chandler. He stops walking for a moment, the two men looking each other in the eye as Chandler voices his opinion on the matter. Chandler: Listen, Jim…I’m not one to dwell on the negative, but Penance is like…freakishly big and while we may have looks, wit, charm, and the list goes on working for us…but umm…. Before the Model Citizen can finish, Nguyen lashes the air in front of Tsonda, as if to wave away the idea of losing another match to Penance. As he begins, Jimmy’s eyes light up with excitement. Jimmy: Dude, I’ve got a killer idea. By using it, we’re totally ensured gold around our waists by the end of the night. Rolling his eyes, Chandler continues to listen to this new ingenious plan of his fellow countryman, albeit without total keenness on the idea of any encounter involving Penance that doesn’t involved a large metal object of some sort in his (Tsonda’s) possession. Jimmy: Okay, you saw, in the midst of our making Tota and Axtell our whipping boys, how Penance got involved. At this point, the scornful and contemptuous part of Chandler’s mind can’t resist butting in with the usual rapier wit he possesses. Chandler: Yeah, so the giant swine got involved. He’s got a nasty habit of doing whatever the little Eden geek says. However, Nguyen holds up both hands in a look that says, “Just wait a second.” He then continues to explain, still full of vigor in his explanation. Jimmy: Okay, so Axtell hates Penance…probably a little more than he hates us, right? A confused look on his face, Chandler answers a most likely rhetorical question with a question of his own, revealing his puzzlement. Chandler: Umm…great. Yeah, what does Miss Nebraska, Jimmy Axtell, have to do with us finding a way to take the titles off that mammoth headache? That being the response he was looking for, Nguyen continues with his little schpiel, continuing his explanation of this seemingly un-related part of the “plan.” Jimmy: Okay, we go to Starr…he doesn’t exactly love us either, but he wants Penance away from those titles as much as I….we do. At the mention of this small company’s owner, Tsonda’s face go into a look of uncertainty, now realizing this plan is going to most likely lead to a conclusion which he won’t be too fond of. Jimmy: He’ll give us a No DQ match that we want…well, not that we really want, but that will allow us to make the plan work… Of course, not able to resist the urge to speak his mind-and loudly-Chandler interjects with his own version of what the plan should happen like. Chandler: And we stereo chair shot that freak before he makes it to the ring, bend some more steel on his already dull noggin, pinning him before Axtell and Tota can pull their hero act! Brilliant! While Chandler is already almost celebrating a victory, Jimmy shakes his head, explaining his true plan to Chandler, who’s eyebrows pull a Rocky Maivia and rise about three inches in surprise. Jimmy: No…we talk to Tota and Axtell…put aside the differences for one night and through our combined forces we take out Penance, then finally settle things with those two in the ring next week, two on two, no gimmicks…for the titles! The crowd back in the arena lets out a huge pop at this, strangely cheering some for Jimmy Nguyen, who seems to have gotten an attack of conscience in this idea of a fair fight next week. However, as is usually the case, Chandler Tsonda severely disagrees with this crowd. Chandler: We…WHAT?! You’re suggesting that we TEAM with those two morons who tried to stop us from getting the title shots just last week?! Have you gone absolutely mental? Shaking his head, Nguyen takes his opportunity to clear up this misconception of his friend’s. Jimmy: No, dude, you’re seeing this all wrong. I mean, we don’t like them two, but we all want those tag titles. They scratch our backs, we scratch theirs. After that, all bets are off. I mean, it’s not like you’ve gotta be friends with them. The voice of Chandler Tsonda only rises, getting louder, now being heard all throughout the hallways of the arena. There is also an accusing tone that Chandler doesn’t try to hide as he near-screams at his tag partner. Chandler: After that…bets are off? There should never have been any bets with those spotlight stealers! We should run the tables on those two, my idea will work just fine and won’t involve the Good Ol’ Boy and the Amazonian Wonder! Jimmy lowers his head, muttering. He seems to have taken offense at Chandler shooting down his idea, but Tsonda doesn’t stop there, continuing to rip into Jimmy’s idea. Chandler: Yeah, real great idea, Jimmy. You think I wanna fight that big lug this week then get cheated out of my fairly won titles the next week? We’re going to steal….no, collect our titles tonight, screw that! At this point, Jimmy, a grown man and one of PWC’s toughest fighters, looks very hurt by Chandler’s words. He looks up, his face a portait of seriousness, as Chandler begins to walk down the hallway, motioning for Jimmy to follow. Chandler: Let’s go…Dude, don’t feel bad just because my plan is gonna get us the titles with no hassle. But Nguyen doesn’t follow. Chandler looks back, then stops again, holding out his hands, wondering what’s the matter. Jimmy just shakes his head and follows this with a defiant voice. Jimmy: No. At this, Chandler begins to chuckle to himself, but as Jimmy’s expression shows no signs of laughter, he looks at his younger comrade, scrunching up his face, confused as to what’s going on. Chandler: No? What, you don’t think it’ll win the titles for 2GVC? Dude, you gotta have more faith. The former model tries to start walking once again, but there is no movement from a stone-face Nguyen, who just stares up ahead. Jimmy: No. No it won’t win any tag titles. It won’t win the titles for 2GVC…because there is no 2GVC any more. The crowd back in the arena’s pop can be heard even on scene where Nguyen and Chandler are now arguing. Chandler looks to Jimmy, finally realizing that he seems to be serious. Chandler: Listen, Jim- Jimmy: No, YOU listen! The defiance brings yet another large pop from the crowd, who now seem to be whole-heartedly behind Jimmy. Jimmy: I’m sick of having to play mascot for this tea, I’m sick of taking orders, I’m sick of having to cheat to get what I want, I’m sick of pretending to enjoy others’ pain…but most of all, I’m sick of YOU. I only let you hang around me because I needed a second fiddle, but you were useful at that, at least. Jimmy just stares at the man who, minutes ago, he was calling a true friend and ally.
