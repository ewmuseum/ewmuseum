Onslaught Biography Nicknames The Epitome of Greatness, The World's Greatest, IOW's Finest, The King of Wrestling Hometown Albany, New York Birthday October 14, 1976 Height 6'4'' Weight 245 Biography So, you want to learn about Onslaught's past. Well you've come to the right place. Below you can read about his child and major events in his life... Born October 14, 1976 into a loving household, good parents. Lived in the Bronx (Riverdale). Sheryl (Mother) worked as a nurse and became a psychiatrist five years later. Marcus (Father) worked at Acclaim as a Senior Art Director. Got a younger brother on September 18, 1977 named Trevor. Loved watching pro wrestling as did Trevor. Beat the hell outta kid bullying Trevor who was only in Kindergarten. Parents enrolled him and Trevor into Jeet Kune Do due to the incident. Darren controlled his temper and Trevor began to stick up for himself as a result. Both attended Cardinal Spellman and joined the Cardinal Spellman Falcons Football Team. The Falcons owned the Catholic Archdiocese Football league in 1994. Trevor and Darren were both on the Varisty Team, so yeah. Darren became an All-American Halfback. Ran a 4.2 and racked up 2,045 yds rushing. Trevor became an All-Amrerican Wide Receiver. Ran a 4.2 as well and racked up 1,024 yds receiving. Pissed off the opposing coaches, players, and their parents with their TD celebrations. Protests and such forced TD celebrations to be banned. Darren dated the cutiest girl in school (Alyssa Summers). Trevor a playa in H.S. All three had great GPA's in H.S. and went to UConn on scholarships. SAT Scores: Darren (1560), Alyssa (1559), and Trevor (1492. Note: He didn't focus much). Neither played football in college to their father's disappointment.. Too much was happening, like parties. Both never took their black belt test in order for their hands to remain unregistered as lethal weapons. Trevor was constantly stealing someone's girlfriend. Darren on the other wanted to be able to protect himself and Alyssa (Maybe a family too, one day). Now that we got that outta the way, let's move on to his history in professional wrestling and what brought him to here today to IOW to compete... During the time period of 1982 to 1995, the wrestling industry watched the growth a great superstar in Epic Wrestling known as Wayne Carter. Wayne was one of the first individuals to ever hold a World Championship five times. Carter eventually became a legend in his own time, but his career was unfortunately cut short due to a knee injury he ironically suffered in a match of his own creation. After his injury he decided on opening a wrestling academy to teach youngsters who so longed to break into the business he loved. Wayne would name the academy after himself, The Carter Academy. In his junior year in college, Darren had finally selected his future profession - Pro Wrestling. Darren and Alyssa enrolled in TCA shortly after it's opening. Unfortunately, this decision didn't hold too well with his parents, especially Marcus. He'd hoped Darren would take a career in Football. They feared him being forced into an early retirement thanks to the increasing extreme and hardcore trends. Darren being trained by an individual who had opening the training facility as a result of his injury didn't help his argument in the slightest. Not only was it hard to crack into the business, but cracking into the business and becoming the next Bret Hart was a lot harder as later proved by fWo Survivor and WWF Tough Enough. On top of that, the African American population in Pro Wrestling at that point was an extremely short list that included Vincent/Virgil, Stevie Ray, Zeus, Ernie Ladd, Tony Atlas, D-Lo Brown, Kama/The Godfather, Ahmed Johnson, Faarooq/Ron Simmons, and Booker T. Regardless, he was determined to make it. In order to strengthen his students, Carter would often place them in submission holds after gagging their mouth with a hankerchief and tying their hands behind their back, making it impossible to tap out. Needless to say, TCA didn't have many graduates. Someone making it past a week was a rare occasion. Much to the dismay of their parents, Trevor attended in his junior year as well. Reading a Marvel Comic spectacular mini series, Darren had found his name... Onslaught! With the exception of the character's evil side, Onslaught symbolized everything he wanted to be as a wrestler: fast, strong, and intimidating. Trevor decided to pick up an issue himself was amazed by a character named Post. Trevor took the name for himself as an in ring name. Alyssa however, competed using her real name. Taking the next step in their training, the three then began touring with Wayne and The Carter Academy. Darren made some friends at TCA, who had also spawned names from the Onslaught Comic Saga. They took the names HaVoK and Holocaust. Onslaught and Post were never ever able to beat each other. Trevor had a tendency to constantly pose during matches wouldn't favor to well with Wayne, whereas Darren would leave such things out of the ring. This slight difference would lead to Darren graduating earlier. Alyssa, Trevor, HaVoK, and Holocaust all graduated soon after. In late 1999, Onslaught along with HaVoK, Post, Holocaust, and Alyssa Summers were recruited up by the SCW Owner Michael Mason and were placed in a stable led by Onslaught called the nWo Gold and Black. Mr. Mason paid close attention to graduates of The Carter Academy because to graduate their proved you were up there with the best. Out of respect for his mentor, Wayne Carter, Onslaught used the finisher his mentor used, Courage Required. Despite being signed to a lengthy contract, Onslaught and the other nWo members were also wrestling in other leagues at the time as well. On one fateful Sunday, Darren's relationship with his parents would draw one step closer towards reconciliation. He was in the midst of a serious feud with Blackula, leader of the rival stable known as the Hellions. The feud would reach a final conclusion on Pay Per View in what would was Onslaught's first World Heavyweight Title shot. His mother, Sheryl attended the show, sitting in the front row. Countering Blackula's finisher - a version of the uranage suplex with an Implant DDT, Onslaught picked up the win. Since that match, his mother slowly began to embrace and support his decision. However, he and father still couldn't see eye to eye on the subject. Later in his career, Onslaught won his second World Championship in the SCW and his 6th overall World Championship. Alyssa reached success as well, becoming the SCW's first Women's Champion! She remains the only woman to stay undefeated in SCW or Pro Wrestling for that matter and win the Women's Championship, twice in one federation. After winning the SCW World Championship for a second time, Onslaught felt he was starting to live up to his mentor's aspirations as well his own, by dominating the federation in every possible way. Onslaught had the federation's largest and unmatched fan base with the nWo Gold and Black sleeveless and Onslaught 'Courage Required' t-shirts selling tremendously well. Upon this success, commentators and interviewers began refer to him as 'The Epitome of Greatness.' Soon after, Onslaught made SCW History by being the only man to capture the SCW World Title for a 3rd Time! The nWo Gold and Black were the hottest thing in Wrestling! This unmatched, unbelievable, and outstanding group had captured twenty plus championships in a period from September to February! The stable even successfully took over the EAWF and SCW/IWF, declaring it their own. During their remarkable reign in Suicidal Championship Wrestling, problems with staff ensued and the federation was shut down in Mid-December. Between January and early March, Onslaught joined numerous failed federations while gaining titles along the way. Onslaught stumbled across XCW. He joined the federation with Post to see if the place would be good for nWo Gold and Black to compete in. In his first match, Onslaught made short work of a wrestler by the name of Angel. After winning the Tag Team Titles with Post, by defeating Y2Jay and Icon, they were stripped of them few days later. After a week Onslaught was placed in a comatose angle for unknown reasons. He soon become weary of this and resigned. Post decided to stay in the federation and with WCW pushing for money to use the nWo name due to the group's popularity, Onslaught disbanded the nWo Gold and Black. Onslaught would start his career in the IWO Leagues after being offered a contract from then IML2 President now IWO President, Thomas Ford. President Ford would learn of the young superstar from various recommendations by likes of IML2 and IWO Competitor, Silverchair, IWO Competitor, Former IML2 Vice President and Former IML President, Gunnar Smith, IML2 and IWO Competitor, Phyre, and IML2 Competitor, JJ Nuclear. Onslaught wasn't the type to join some federation's Minor League, but the IML2 was different. They just may have had a better roster than that of the actual IWO with a roster compromising of such names as Shawn Arrows, Mike Extreme, Joey Malone, Daniel Philips, Silverchair, Kyle Kash, LiGiL, Phyre, Lance McNally, Carlos Lopez, Jack Night, Enfirno, Mac D., and Kurt Zoom. After entering the IML2, Onslaught quickly became one of the Top 10 Current Wrestlers in the IML2 during his short time there. His debut match would have him squaring off against the No. 1 Contender to the World Championship, Kyle Kash. Although Onslaught would lose the match, he proved he would be a force to be reckoned with by withstanding a never before seen or repeated 8 Too Late Facebusters (Double Underhook Face Driver) before he would finally go down for the 3 Count. Impressing the IML2 'Suits', in his second match, Onslaught would take on the IML2 World Champion 'Simply Perfect' Shawn Arrows on Monday Night Melee, in what was a Non-World Title Match, Onslaught had handed Arrows his first defeat in 18 Months! IWO President John Maples closed the IML2 and IML3 in an effort to improve the IWO and IML and to make them more elite. Onslaught with a few other IML2 and IML3 Wrestlers was sent to the IML. However IML2 President Thomas Ford wasn't the only one who made this decision as did Shawn Arrows (A reward for his loyalty and hard work), who had persuaded John to send Onslaught to the IML instead of the IWO, had a part in the decision. Shawn on the other hand, had himself sent on to the IWO. Onslaught entered the IML with a renewed rage. Onslaught made his presence widely known by defeating the likes of former IML2 Standouts, Geoff Paul and Mac D. and former Television Champions, Azrael Damodread or as Onslaught would call him, Azrael Damobread and Adam Wars. It didn't take long for Onslaught to capture his first Championship in the IWO Leagues, but Onslaught didn't win gold. The championship would come in the form of Platinum as Onslaught became the second and Last Internet Wrestling Organization Minor League Platinum Champion! Onslaught won the match in his home state, the same place he as yet to lose a match, Madison Square Garden by defeating another Undefeated Man in Michael Nash. This became Nash's first and last title defense when Onslaught tossed him off a 50 Foot Scaffold. Nonetheless, to add insult to injury the losing stipulation would have Onslaught then cover him in tar and feather him like a chicken! Onslaught would defend the championship against anyone foolish enough to face him, including Dean Fiore and The Reaper (Who took off his cheap booster boots and now goes by the name Sabasatian Crow). Destined for a World Championship, Onslaught's arrogance surely won him fans, but pissed off the Executive Board in the process. Due to this, he was suspended for a month. Without The Epitome of Greatness, on the cards, the IML went downhill and had it's first card on October 23th, 2000 since Onslaught was suspended which was September 30th, 2000. They eventually closed. However, what the IML Executive Board saw as unlimited arrogance, the IWO saw as unlimited potential. After finishing his month of suspension, just as soon as he would be reinstated back into the IML, he was promoted to the IWO! Onslaught would debut in the IWO at their biggest pay per view of the year, Ice Age 3 and go on to defeat former US Champion, Ash Robinson in the first ever Vertical Limit Match (Onslaught's creation). Onslaught would establish himself that night as a force to be reckoned with. He continued to make impressive showings even one night on the January 5th edition of Hostile Takeover, Onslaught defeated Donnie Daze, Ash Robinson, and then United States Champion, Billy Ray in a three on one Handicap Match! During Onslaught's stint in the IML, he and Shawn had patched their IML2 differences. As their friendship grew, the two realized they'd posses a great potential as a tag team. The IWO Executive Board allowed the two to teamed up against Former IML World Tag Team Champions, IWO Intercontinental Tag Team Champions, and IWO World Tag Team Champions, the Suicide Kings. The crowd couldn't believe it, not only did Onslaught and Arrows win, they practically dominated. Despite his quick success, Onslaught grew weary of the political and backstage *censored* that happened in the IWO. Onslaught handed in his resignation to the federation. Once again, Onslaught found himself in a search for a new federation. Except this federation, wasn't so new. While Onslaught was competing in the IML, he was working at an upstart federation called Five Star Wrestling. With his reputation, FSW quickly grew to respected status in the wrestling community. However, Onslaught parted ways with the company due to personal issues with some of the roster. He was contacted by Co-Owner Painkiller and Vice President Ryan Lockheart, that they were starting over and with more new faces than old among those accepted, Onslaught accepted their offer. His impact grew more and more with each day. At Five Star Wrestling's first Pay Per View back, Love Hurts, Onslaught made quick work of 'Five Star' Mike Marvelous and Former FSW World Champion, Epitome of Evil to become FSW's First Standard Bearer, Flagship, and World Heavyweight Champion of it's New Era. That night, Onslaught won his 10th World Heavyweight Championship and proposed to his longtime girlfriend, Alyssa Summers. The two would embrace and share a kiss after she said yes. Following Love Hurts, Onslaught mowed over anyone his path. From #1 Contender to his World Championship, Lucky McKnight to Intercontinental Champion, Johnny Mayhem. Others included FSW's First World Champion, Steve Johnson, WWR, IML, and IWO Superstar, Ian Kain, and Sadistic Madman, Ironfist. He also became the first person to ever defeat Epitome of Evil via Tap Out. The Owner, Painkiller kept sticking his nose in the champ's business and Onslaught quickly took exception to it. The two again ended up at odds with one other, with Onslaught constantly embarrassing him on his own shows to the delight of the crowds. Realizing he bit off more than he could chew, Painkiller found out he just couldn't take it and decided to release him. During his time off from the business, Darren finally decided to get married to Alyssa. The wedding was held in St. Patrick's Cathedral. As would be expected, numerous people from the wrestling industry attended the wedding. Faces included names such as then Tag Team Partner Jason Winters, future Tag Partner Shawn Arrows, IWO Hall of Famer Phelen Kell, former jOlt Champion Brandon Blade, Fellow TCA Graduate and former Manager Lance Titan, IWO Grand Slam Winner High Flyer, former HWF World Champion Lance Sterling, and BWM Host Claude Mitchell. Within a few months, Onslaught received a hefty contract to join oWa. As luck would have it, the blasted company closed before his first match. Five Star Wrestling began to take a turn for the worse. He was given numerous offers (four to be exact) to return back to the crumbling company, but rejected every offer. In Onslaught's view.. 'They had their chance and decided to end it. Now they come crawling back when the times are hard? Too bad.' Onslaught rejected numerous offers everything from wrestling organizations like IWO, GPW, WWR, HYPE, and IOW to fighting groups such as Eclipse and The Fighting Zone. He never quite gave any specific reasons. For months the industry had speculated Onslaught had called it quits. Other suspected that 'The Epitome of Greatness' hadn't taken the offers because he felt he wasn't as he good as he used to be, that with the new wave of talent, he just couldn't cut it in the big leagues anymore. In November, Onslaught was contacted by a man named William Morgan. William revealed to him that he was reopening the company his predecessor competed in, Epic Wrestling, with the financial support of his late uncle, former Epic Wrestling World Champion, Trent Morgan and support by the living members of the company's former Executive Board. Morgan asked if he could join the federation, as part of the tag team division. Before hanging up, Onslaught just replied stating 'If I accept your offer, you'll be among the first to know.' Following the announcement of bankruptcy by a promotion called the CWF, William would quickly purchase it and offer the CWF wrestlers contracts with Epic. Unfortunately, the hiring of one CWF wrestler would cause Epic a host of problems and leave the wrestling world in terror. Following a screwjob loss to Ben Archer, Seth Anson would snap putting Archer through a table and giving the referee what so far appears to be a permanent hospital visit. His trainer would soon be revealed to be perhaps the most sadistic son of a bitch walking the Earth in four time former Epic Wrestling World Heavyweight Champion and Carter's long time rival, Akira Akiyama. Unaware of a clause from his CWF contract that Jessica Jenkins (Used to run the CWF) was the only person who could had any authority over him, Morgan agreed to let him keep the clauses when signing him over to Epic. Due to William's decision not to hire Jessica as a staff member in the promotion's early stages, she refused to help. With Anson's master plan of a faction simply known as MALICE, he had created the most ruthless group of individuals ever seen in the wrestling world. MALICE included former FSW World Heavyweight Champion 'The Genocidal Phoenyx' Steve Johnson and a mysterious monster of a man who was only known as Anubis. Seth Anson had expected Onslaught to join Epic. Realizing Morgan hadn't managed to convince The World's Greatest, Anson decided to take things into his own hands. On the January 14th edition of Ground Zero, Seth Anson shocked the wrestling world murdered Onslaught's former tag team partner Jason Winters. He had broken the late Jason Winters' neck with his deadly Backdrop Driver. A week later, Damon Trask wrote an article berating the company believing it be fake. This only provoked Anson even further. Two weeks later, Anson struck. He hung Winters' decaying body from the rafters by his neck with a noose. The crowd fled in a disgust and fear as maggots and other disgusting objects fell from off of and out of his body. Unfortunately, the non-wrestling media and police passed this off as an angle much to Anson's delight. Knowing he was so untouchable, Anson even interrupted the tribute to the late former North American Champion mocking William Morgan after a threat on his life stating: 'Unlikely. Very unlikely. What happened to Winters occurred in the ring. You attack me right here, right now and manage to successfully kill me; your only reward will be getting fucked up the ass during a life sentence in prison. The American Legal System... sucks doesn't it? Then when your death comes, you won't just welcome it, you'll embrace it as if you were addict long overdue for that one little shot up the vain. You would have let down your family, friends, these foul smelling pieces of shit here, and most importantly, Trent. Your dear uncle. The one thing he ever asked from you with god knows how many billions of dollars as financial support, you would have screwed up. So go ahead... do it.' Anson would continue to make Archer's life hell for four months toying with him until Vengeful Hearts. He was going to end his career or worse, having gotten the largest combined (Live and PPV) audience Epic had gotten so far since it's existence since 1982. Concealing their identities to the crowd, Shawn Arrows and Onslaught helped Ben Archer (Shawn's close friend) win the match along with the Epic Wrestling World Heavyweight Championship, and might have saved his life that night from Seth Anson and MALICE. The next night on Ground Zero, the two revealed themselves to the crowd, forming a tag team known as Malevolent Youth - The exact same name Onslaught used with his tag team with Winters. Despite their resolve to help Archer rid Epic of the trio, the Fox Executives were angered at the company's decision to hire Onslaught who once ended two careers within a span of two months and Arrows who had fellow Epic star, Carlos Lopez confined to wheel chair after a vicious beating in the IWO. This proved to be the straw that broke the camel's back. Fox has terminated Epic's contract as they felt the presence of both men would only contribute to the growing trend of violence within the organization. Fox's decision made Morgan rethink Epic's current situation. The promotion's fans had been exposed to in just a few short months and over the years as well and he knew it wouldn't end anytime soon due to the eventual Onslaught/Seth Anson battle. Wanting no more blood to spilt and careers ended under the Epic Wrestling name, William closed down the promotion.
