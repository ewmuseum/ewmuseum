PWC TV Results for April 26th, 2003 Live from Philadelphia Community College, in Philadelphia, PA The words “Last Sunday Night: Capitol Punishment” appear on the black screen, before an image of Bob Kirby in the ring appears. DING! DING! DING! Bob: The following contest is scheduled for one fall and is a triple threat match to crown the first ever PWC HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION OF THE WORLD!!! We see quick clips of the entrances of Sebastian Toomes, Clay Vendetta, and Angelo Deville to a backdrop of cheering fans. The scene then flashes from high spot to high spot. We see Deville apply the Soprano to Toomes, only to be pulled away by Vendetta, who delivers a Cobra Clutch suplex. We see Vendetta grab Toomes, who has a steel chair wrapped around his head, and throw him back over his head with a release German suplex. We see Toomes pull Vendetta up out of the Crowd Control on Deville, nailing the Classic One with an Osaka street cutter. We cut to later in the match, where we see an extended clip of the action. Toomes goes to leap over Deville to finish the maneuver, but Angelo holds onto the ropes and stops his opponent from hitting it. Instead he manages to catch Seabass on his shoulder. Holding him there, Deville steps back up onto the first rope…then climbs to the second, before adjusting Toomes in a tombstone piledriver position. Mark: Oh my god…don’t tell me Deville is actually going to hit this. Bang: He’s gonna destroy Sebastian Toomes! Leaping off, Deville impacts Toomes’ head into the steel chair with a tombstone piledriver off the turnbuckles! Mark: TOMBSTONE PILEDRIVER FROM THE SECOND TURNBUCKLE! Bang: TARNATION! DID YOU SEE HIS HEAD BOUNCE OFF THA MAT! Mark: TOOMES IS OUT! HE’S FINISHED! NO MAN IS KICKING OUT OF THAT! Exhausted, Angelo looks across the ring to see Clay Vendetta still unconscious on the mat. Sensing victory, he falls across the frame of Toomes, as Bobby Howard hits the canvas for the pin. One… Two… Mark: He’s got it! Angelo Deville is going to be our first world champion! Bang: What a match! And then, as Howard’s hand goes down to the mat for the third count, the lights go out. There is no sound of a final slap as pitch black darkness envelops the arena. Then, suddenly, the opening chords of “Antichrist Superstar” by Marilyn Manson are heard reverberating through the darkness. Mark: That music! That’s Penance’s music!!! Bang: Oh shit… Mark: GOD DAMN IT!!! The lights come on…revealing Penance already in the ring, with Vendetta on his back in crucifix powerbomb position. Solomon is standing on the ring apron, shouting empathically at the monster to purge and destroy. With a swift motion, Penance tosses Clay over his shoulders and drops him to the mat with a sit-out piledriver that folds him in half! Mark: IDEOLOGICAL CRUCIFIXION ON VENDETTA!!! The screen flashes, to reveal Jacob Solomon in the middle of the ring, with microphone in hand. The bodies of Sebastian Toomes and Clay Vendetta are at his feet as he addresses the booing crowd. Your stubborn hatred…your ignorance…your pride…your disregard…all factors that have contributed to your spurning our message. I do not appreciate being spit on for attempting to commit the ultimate altruistic gesture. You people should be ashamed of yourselves for acting in such a matter. You deserve it not. I will not martyr myself to save an unthankful flock. That is way tonight my quest turns from a holy mission…to a HOLY WAR!!! If I cannot convert the masses, I shall destroy them, thus cleansing the world and leaving only the sanctified and pure! It is because of people like him that I chose wrestling as the starting point of my moral crusade…and it is on him that I will fire the opening shots of this holy war! We flash to show clips of Penance twisting Toomes’ leg, apparently snapping it. We see Toomes screaming in pain, before being lifted up and tossed over the rope by the masked monster. The back of his head and neck hit hard into the steel, as the curved back of the chair becomes dented inward from the brutal impact. Salvador has now pushed himself to within only one solid movement of ending the count. Mark: This must seem like mission impossible to Totakoxe! Bang: Or Mission impossible for poor Jacob Solomon. The crowd’s nerves are in abundance as they feel ‘The One in Demand’ will fail to get his feet, lose the match and then be destroyed by Penance. Nonetheless, they keep clapping and stomping their feet, eager to help Salvador. Gil Mc Goosen raises his hand for the final count… T-E-… Mark: WHOA! Bang: How’d he just do that?! Mark: I don’t know, Bang. Folks, Totakoxe Salvador just ended the 10 count in time as he quickly jumped to his feet and stormed towards Penance, colliding with him and knocking Solomon from the ring to the floor. The fans at ringside point and laugh at the unfortunate Jacob Solomon, who was in the wrong place at the wrong time. Mark: Just when we thought there was an ounce of hope for Totakoxe in this match, Penance just kills that hope. Bang: Or eats the ounce. Mark: AND THE HOPE RETURNS! The fans urge Totakoxe to capitalize on Penance’s position and try to finish him off. Mark: I’ve said it once, and I’ll say it again… Jimmy Nguyen is a coward! Totakoxe leans against the ropes and shouts at Jimmy Nguyen to ‘bring his ass back into the ring’.
