PWC TV Results for April 12th, 2003 Live from the Trenton Convention Center in Trenton, NJ The show opens in the back parking lot of the Trenton Convention Center, where a yellow taxi is just arriving. A tall, lanky driver, probably in his early forties, pops out of the front seat and strides around to the trunk of the vehicle. He pulls a duffle bag out just as the rear passenger door opens. The first things out of the back seat are a pair of crutches, followed by the head of ‘The Axphyxiator’, Jim Axtell. A tremendous pop from the interior of the arena is heard at the site of the Working Class Hero, as the fans are apparently able to see his arrival on the stacks of televisions that serve as the Pro Wrestling Circuit’s “Tron.” As he exits the cab, Axtell strains to get out of the back of the taxi as the driver brings his duffle bag to him. Cabbie: Here ya go, young man. Axtell slings the bag over his shoulder, balancing on his good leg while the other one is wrapped in a heavy full-leg brace. Jim digs in his front jeans pocket and pulls out some money, handing the driver a few bills. Jim: Thanks, man. Keep the change. The taxi driver nods his head and closes the rear door behind Jim. As he makes his way around to the driver’s side he slams the trunk shut, the slides back into his vehicle. The light on top of the taxi turns on as the cab exits the parking lot. Jim grimaces in pain as he positions his crutches under his armpits. Jim: I knew there was a reason I hated leg injuries. Axtell begins to make his way toward the back door to the building, moving carefully on the pair of crutches. His injured leg, forced straight by the full-leg brace, barely misses scraping on the ground each time he swings on the metal walking aids. Jim arrives at the rear entrance to the building, and just as he reaches to grab the door it comes flying open. Axtell quickly hops out of the way so as to not get clubbed to the ground by the door. Jim: WHOA!! Watch out! A startled Jason Maddux’s eyes widen in surprise, and he promptly apologizes. Jason: Jesus! Sorry, Jim! I didn’t see you there. Axtell chuckles and shrugs the incident off. Jim: You couldn’t have seen me unless you were Superman or something. I’m okay, Jason. Maddux sighs in relief and heads toward a large dumpster in the parking lot, carrying a bag of garbage. Axtell watches with a half smirk on his face. Jim: Taking out the trash? Maddux turns and frowns at Jim, making it obvious that he’s not at all pleased about it. Jason: Yeah. Ron Starr calls me into his office like he has something important to tell me, perhaps a scoop moving forward into the Pay Per View. When I get in there he looks up at me and nods his head toward his trash can, not saying a word. I couldn’t believe it! Jim: So why did you do it? Jason: Come on, man! You know he would have fired my ass on the spot if I said no. He doesn’t like people to defy him. Axtell acknowledges with a nod of his head. Jim: I guess you’re right. Maddux then looks at Jim with a puzzled expression. Jason: What are you doing here? Aren’t you supposed to be off for like four weeks? A fire kindles in Jim’s eyes. Jim: That’s exactly why I’m here. I’m going to go see Ron Starr and demand that I be allowed to compete against Penance tomorrow night at Capitol Punishment. The crowd explodes at the thought of the Axphyxiator wrestling the beast known as Penance tomorrow night. Axtell: (continuing) I’ve come too far and sacrificed too much to let this chance go by. There’s no way in hell, injury or not, that I’m going to miss that match. Ron is just gonna have to realize that. Maddux shakes his head in dismay. Jason: I wish you luck, Jim, but he’s not going to cave in. You saw how he handled Jay Phoenix. Your own doctors called him and told him you couldn’t compete. I overheard him talking to Carlisle about the insurance rates and the fact that you didn’t put enough butts in the seats to justify the added premiums. The fire that had kindled in Jim’s eyes is now a full blown inferno. Jim: Is that so? Maddux starts to respond, but Axtell is already gimping his way into the building on his crutches. His pace is increased vastly as he disappears down the hallway. Maddux watches on as Jim fades around a corner, then re-enters the building, allowing the door to shut behind him. . . . . . . . . . The scene reopens elsewhere backstage, where we can hear loud blaring music and yells from somewhere near, but off, the camera’s view. As we pan, we can see that the locker room labeled ‘2GVC’ is the approximate source of all the noise. As the loud noises continue, the camera moves the door open a bit, so that a clear view of the room can be had. However, the first thing noticed about the room is that, despite its grungy appearance and confined innards, seems to have a festive feel to it. Inside are not only Jimmy Nguyen and Chandler Tsonda, but more than a few scantily clad young women, none of whom can be older than their late 20’s. Jimmy: Uhh…Chandler…I can’t feel my legs.
