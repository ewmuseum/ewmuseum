Sublime Biography Hometown Albuquerque, New Mexico Birthday March 17, 1983 Height 6' 5' Weight 248 Biography The son of an East German prize-fighting father, Jurgen Helmstaedt, and Apache Indian mother, Eve Sonorra, Sublime was born Aachen Helmstaedt in the spring of 1983 on a small Indian reservation just outside of Albuquerque, New Mexico. While Sublime's arrival held great joy for both parents, they each had their own reasons for wanting to see their son grow and thrive. It soon became apparent, however, that his father's motives were far more malicious and self-serving than a father's should've been, especially as Sublime grew into adulthood and began to display the same exceptional ability his legendary father once wielded: ability that would later prove to be both a blessing and a curse. At two months, tradition dictated that Sublime be given a birth name befitting his Apache heritage. Dave Dream Strider, his grandfather on his mother’s side christened him Oneida Elohi or ‘Astride Two Worlds’ to acknowledge his mixed heritage, but the old man was also aware of Jurgen‘s past and sensed Jurgen’s true intent for Sublime. Thus the name also held a second, deeper meaning; that of ‘Burden Carrier’ because Dream Strider knew sometime in this child’s future he would be forced to carry the Helmstaedt legacy and bear the brunt of his father’s weighty expectations. Sublime’s childhood essentially served as a grace period spent in relative normality. Fulfilment of what Jurgen considered to be Sublime’s inevitable destiny wouldn’t begin until he was of suitable age. Nonetheless, it would be fulfilled. His distorted reasoning for this sprung from his own failure to conquer the American underground scene almost twenty years previous. It was the rationale of a man consumed by bitterness and failure to let past disappointments lie: if he couldn’t make it where he rightly should have then he’d make absolutely certain Sublime did in his place. With Jurgen as hawk-eyed overseer, Sublime’s training began in earnest soon after his sixteenth birthday. Over the next three years classrooms gradually gave way to weight-rooms and the ring. Instruction naturally covered the gamut, both amateur and professional forms of wrestling, various hand-to-hand combat styles, psychological coaching… nothing was spared in Jurgen’s relentless pursuit to ready Sublime for his unavoidable future. Sublime flourished in those three years, regardless of whether Jurgen was coercing him or not, and Jurgen was pleased with Sublime’s progress. A model student, during his time at The Factory, a respected wrestling academy located in Albuquerque, he displayed such a meticulous work ethic that tutors dubbed the young man Sublime. Convinced of its accuracy, Sublime decided to take the moniker as his own. Even Jurgen seemed to agree with the decision: a rare moment of approval for someone who never bestowed anything upon his son except constant scorn and a very heavy hand. In the meantime Jurgen began the search for opportunities to launch Sublime’s professional career. He found a number of ideal candidates in the much touted, highly successful WT conglomeration. Powerhouse organizations such as Old School Wrestling and Primetime Championship Wrestling seemed to fit the bill perfectly. They offered extremely lucrative contracts and excellent prospects as a free agent if Sublime found himself back on the market. A place in either one ensured the best possible start. Sublime attended open trials for OSW’s new fighting division but failed to make the final cut, and not through lack of talent or effort either. Priority rightfully went to graduates of WT’s wrestling academy and more established individuals. Both possessed an important advantage over Sublime: recognition in the community. Jurgen would be forced to find entry elsewhere. Unwilling to put Sublime back in training in their feeder system Jurgen was instead referred to a small, independent operation basing itself out of New Jersey. The Pro Wrestling Circuit was a far cry from what Jurgen had envisaged for Sublime, but he had little choice in the matter with the more prominent federations closed to him. It would be a huge risk accepting a spot on what was little more than a bush fed on a shoestring budget. There certainly wouldn’t be any recompense over the federation’s formative months, that much was certain. Nevertheless, Jurgen staked his chances on WT’s reputation for building successful ventures. He was sure the rewards and financial returns would come... eventually . The decision had been made. Sublime was set to make his professional debut in a Pro Wrestling Circuit ring. Overview: Inside the ring Sublime can best be described as multi-faceted. His adaptability enables him to adequately compete with most opponents and most styles. Sublime’s greatest advantage, however, lies in sound technique and explosive power (as opposed to brute strength.) When he delivers, even simple moves carry much greater impact than normal. On the downside is Sublime's inexperience. He's relatively untested, could be susceptible under pressure, and being a rookie, liable to make simple rookie mistakes. Overall ring generalship is still something he lacks and will need to develop further. Entrance Music 'Diamond Sea' by Sonic Youth
