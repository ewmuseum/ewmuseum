PWC TV for March 22nd, 2003 Live from the Wildwood Convention Center, in Wildwood, NJ As our show opens backstage at the Wildwood Convention Center we witness a doorway swing open. Striding through the door is a stone faced Jim Axtell, wearing a black leather coat over a red, white, and blue shirt and jeans. His lip is slightly swollen and there is a knot visible on the side of his head from the beating he took from Penance last week. Gripped tightly in Jim’s right hand is the wooden handle of a ten pound sledgehammer. The crowd inside the convention center pops as they watch on the two video walls. Axtell moves down the hallway, the sledge swinging forward and back with each stride. As he makes his way through the backstage area, Jim comes across his friend and partner Totakoxe Salvador.
