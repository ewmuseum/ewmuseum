The Leprechaun Biography Nicknames Lep, Formerly Known as Conan The Leprechaun Hometown Dublin, Ireland Birthday 12th April 1976 Height 5ft Weight 208 Biography Bio Here begins the tale of a young man, a young man with huge talent, a young man with a big heart, a young man with a strong will, a young man with silver buckled shoes and a healthy obsession with drink, a young man known to many as the Leprechaun. We will begin at a ripe young age of 20. Why? Well who needs to know how someone was picked on as a kid, how someone was bullied by kids 2 years younger than him. Precisely. It was time to look forward, Sean managed to get his life in gear, he knew exactly what he wanted from his life. He had his new start, he had moved to America the home of the free. His dad had been offered a job as a corporate executive in some business in New York. Sean didn’t really care for details, it was a chance for him to get away. Sean didn’t know anyone. He tried his best to make friends, but it was hard for him. Everyone else was all wrapped up in there own lives to give him a chance. The only person he had was his brother. Marc was an upbeat guy he was oblivious to what Sean had been through as a child. That wasn’t his fault, it’s the type of guy Sean was. The way he saw it, he was suffering, he felt no need for anyone else to suffer with him. The Rocking Horse ended up like a second home to Sean. It was the local drinking hole. Many a long drink filled night was spent in that place. The Ale didn’t seem to taste as good as that back home, but he couldn’t knock it. It became the fuel which allowed him to transform his life. Nobodies became friends. Nights in became Nights out. It was like he had been reborn. He gained confidence each day and was renowned there for his sense of humour and the practical jokes he played. It was here where he began to enjoy his life. This led to his first job and the beginning of what can only be described as a remarkable u-turn. Inside of him was his future , like an alien set to burst through his stomach walls the Leprechaun was ready to come out. To begin with he worked for a local Wrestling Federation known as the NPW. I doubt you can say known; it had an average attendance of about 100 people. Sean was a stagehand there, he helped to set up the ring, railings mat. At least that is how it began . Only a few years later the world of wrestling led him to the CWL and then PIW. These were two corporations, which allowed him to preen his moves, improve his physical fitness and get his face on t-shirts. Not only that but it paid for him to live how he wanted, forget about what he used to go through, forget about the dingy little shit holes he used to live in. Now he could sit in his nice little apartment and drink the Black stuff until he was completely buckled. Since then lots of things have happened. When you die...you change...People dont like change. Will PWC ?
