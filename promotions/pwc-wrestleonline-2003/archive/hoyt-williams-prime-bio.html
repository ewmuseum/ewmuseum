Hoyt Williams Biography Nicknames 'Your Personal Jesus' Hometown Chicago, IL now living in Altantic City, New Jersey Birthday July, 4th 1981 Height 6'6' Weight 322 Fed Titles Work In Progress Fed Awards Work In Progress Biography “Your Personal Jesus” Hoyt Williams Physical stats and appearance: Hoyt stands 6’6 and weighs in at 322 pounds he is built solid almost monstrous in muscle mass. He is clean shaven with his blonde hair shaven to about a two on the shaver. He dresses preppy with two gold rings in his left ear. In the ring he wears black shorts with raunchy printed in gold on the back and black boots with HW on the sides. Background: “Your Personal Jesus” Hoyt Williams is a man full of energy and raw power. He preaches the way life ought to be. He is opinionated, passionate and proud, independent and irreverent. He believes his way is the only way and everybody whom doesn’t follow is a sinner. Crafted from the finest in the business Hoyt was born the only known son of wrestling outlaw “The Malice Man” Duke Williams. Many kids have wished upon falling stars on late summer nights dreaming of being the son of fun loving Duke Williams. Hoyt however finds it more as a course then a wish come true. His earliest memories of his father come from when he was seven year old and are of his dad sorting coke from a century note while he was supposed to being visiting young Hoyt for their monthly get together. Hoyt doesn’t blame his father for their relationship or lack of but vows never to be like him. One advantage of having a famous father was connections. As Hoyt got older he started to train his body to be a machine. The bible refers to the human body as a temple and Hoyts is polished gold. His relationship in his teen years with his dad where better and Hoyt took advantage of that to be sent to the best wrestling schools. Trained by his mentor and preacher former WWF wrestling star Ted DiBiase, Hoyt got an early start at the age of 16, wrestling on the Christian Wrestling Circuit by 17. Hoyt has visited his father backstage at many PCW show over the years and his learned some moves from some of the feds best. A close line from Ivan, a pile driver from Kannon, a chair shot from Howard, the list goes one. Hoyt is one of the most dedicated and focused young men in the business today. His goal is simple, be the best in the business. Easier said then done but Hoyt doesn’t let that stop him. Like his father the young man has a weakness for a great set of legs and a nice rack. Will the vices that are a family tradition break the focus of the young powerhouse or will he be able to over come the shadows of famous father and make an independent name for himself.
