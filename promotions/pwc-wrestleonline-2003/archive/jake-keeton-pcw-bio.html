Jake Keeton Biography Nicknames The Solution Hometown Lexington, KY Birthday June 9th, 1977 Height 5'9' Weight 221 Biography Jake Keeton was born June 9, 1977 at the University of Kentucky Hospital. His father was a drunk and left when he was seven years old leaving his mother to raise him and his younger brother. His mother worked hard and had two wonderful sons. Jake excelled in all sports in Junior High School. In High School he was all-state in Basketball and Football as a freshman. When his junior year was set to begin the Football coach told him he was to skinny for football, and the Basketball coach said he was to short for Basketball. Jake began working out hard every day and soon found his sport, Wrestling. Jake began wrestling mid way through the season of his junior year. He won the state championship and went on to the Nationals, coming in second. By his senior year he was the top amateur wrestler in the world, and won the National, and World Championships. After High School, Jake received a full scholarship to the University of Mississippi. A regional pro-wrestling promoter, known only as Prez XXX, saw Jake in the gym one day. XXX immediately offered Jake the highest salary ever offered to a rookie in pro wrestling. Jake quickly dropped out of college for the spotlight of the wrestling business and hasn't slowed down since.((more to come)) Other Feds PCW(both Primetime and Pro), PSW, Advantage, Titans of Wrestling, FSW, ICW, many more... Other Fed Awards Advantage World Champion, PSW World Champion, 2x PCW Cruiserweight Champion, many more...
