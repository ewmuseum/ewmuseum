Caleb McGlocklan Biography Nicknames Mick, Cabby, The Crusader Hometown Originally from Waterford, Ireland... moved to Manhattan, NY at a young age. Birthday 1/2/1978 Height 5'11 Weight 197 Fed Titles No Titles Fed Awards No Awards Biography I’ll be blunt – we are all living in a proverbial shit hole. A bidet filled to the brim with corruption and sin. I stand alone, cross dangling from neck and morals on the mind, prepared to wage war with all those who oppose the good in human nature. No longer will I sit back and watch as the weak and frail are drowned in a deluge spawned from those who follow Satan’s Guide To Life. I long for the days of the past. Back when pornography wasn’t available right under your fingertips, narcotics weren’t blooming like flowers, and education was considerably far more important than it is today. Back when a dollar bought you a burger and VHS was considered a luxury. Back when you didn’t have to give a damn and you could turn out all right. Back then I was the man: young, healthy, athletic… unwise to the horrors of the world. I was gifted with intelligence and eagerness, figuratively blinded by my mother and father’s care, unknown to the constant threat of sin. I guess that’s the plus side to being a member in a religious family—protection from the real world. That went well, but then shit hit the fan. College. Alcohol, sex, drugs. The works. It’s amazing how people turn out without some guidance and supervision. Assholes were constantly on my back... “Hey man, let’s hit the town!” Yeaaa-no. “Bro, that girl is totally checking you out.” How wonderful. “Dude, want a hit?” Want my fist through your head? Fuck you! I was surrounded by that bullshit, and it’s a damn shame it’s carried over to current times. I’m strong, cynical, and pissed off. And I find myself, one of the few courageous evangelists left on this earth, facing a swarm of proverbial demons, one hand clutching the hilt to my sword, the other flipping the pages of Psalms. Corruption? Sinners? The taste of your own blood waits. Yeah, I’m bitter. But it’s all for the best. Other Feds PWA, NTICW, RUSH Other Fed Awards PWA Top 5 Students For 3 Weeks. Wrestling Style Intelligent, stealthy, and hard hitting. Suplexes and submission holds take up his arsenal. Finisher Move Celtic Bloodshed Finisher Description McGlocklan shoots his fists into his opponents face several times before rearing back with a vicious haymaker, which twirls his opponent around in a 180-degree spin. He then latches his arms around his foe's neck and locks in a Dragon Sleeper. Finisher Setup Move Mixes with the finisher itself. Other Moves Guinness - McGlocklan hoists his opponents up into a suplex position and drapes them over the top rope. Keeping the hold, he drops down, spiking his foe's head into the mat with devastating DDT. Entrance Music Vast - Touched
