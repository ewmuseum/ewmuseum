Bobby Howard Biography Hometown Indianapolis, Indiana Birthdate January 15th, 1984 Height 5'9 Weight 167 Biography Bobby Howard didn't want to be a referee. He wanted to be a wrestler. Unfortunately, while Bobby has the heart of a lion and the desire that a middle-aged, balding, divorced, Indian man has for a young, blonde, he didn't have the physical skills to make the cut as a wrestler on the Professional Wrestling Circuit...this time. Impressed with his passion for the sport, PWC officials offered to keep him around and let him get his feet wet as a referee and an extra pair of hands to help out the ringcrew. Bobby jumped at the offer, and plans to do the best damn job he can do as both a ringcrew and manual laborer in order to show PWC officials he would be a great addition to the roster the next time PWC looks to add talent.
