Seth Anson Biography Nicknames The Forgotten Angel, The God of Violence, The Sickest Bastard Alive Hometown Los Angeles, California Birthday December 25, 1976 Height 6'3' Weight 238 Biography Trained by the most sadistic man, wrestling had ever seen... Until now. In Japan, he throughly orchastrated the most violent acts that nation had known since World War II. Little did anyone know, that was merely for practice. Since returning to America... Joined the CWF which was brought by Epic Entertainment, parent company of Epic Wrestling. Joined the promotion where his trainer made himself a legend, Epic Wrestling. Ensured he could not be fired due to a stipulation in his contract that was transferred from the CWF. Seconds after his first match resulted in a loss due to a referee's incomptance, he crippled the referee. Defeated and Hospitalized a multiple time World Champion and DWF Hall of Famer. Won the World Heavyweight Championship. Defeated and Hospitalized a multiple time World Champion and hardcore legend. Avenged his first loss and would have killed the man if not for the interference of the entire roster. Sent him flying into a tree trunk 70 MPH via car during that match. Killed a man on live television for being close friends with the protege of his trainer's long-time rival. Hacked the News website for not making him the no. 1 newsmaker.as they believed it was an angle. Dung his grave from it's resting place in The Saint John Norway Cementary in Canada. Hung the decaying body from the rafters with a noose on live television to prove a point.. Fooled the police to pass it off as an angle and got away with murder. Interrupted the tribute to the late former Epic North American Champion, Jason Winters. Made his words more painful than any weapon. Convinced the crowd there were bloody savages and Winters' death was their own fault. Labeled himself their gladiator. Literally fell out laughing when the owner made him face a 7'1' and 368 pound monster for his title. In the words of No. 1 fan, Dean Styles: 'HE CAME... HE SAW... HE KICKED THAT JOBBER'S ASS!' Defeated and Hospitalized a multiple time World Champion and IWO Hall of Famer. Hospitalized a multiple World Champion and harcore legend, again in a hardcore match despite losing. Used him as a human step ladder after laying him on a staircase to re-enter the ring during the match. Nailed him upside the head with a PS2 that he was swinging by the adapter cord during the match. Single handedly annihilated six security guards and the commissioner in hand to hand combat. Defeated and Hospitalized a multiple World Champion for helping to cost him that match and title. Forced the promotion's closure due to his excessive violent acts. That took a mere five months. Couldn't get signed to another promotion due to his past then... Fooled IOW into signing him simply to continue his reign of malice. Won the IOW World Heavyweight Championship. Defeated a former FAW World Heavyweight Champion, sending him packing with his tail between his legs. Defeated a former IOW World Heavyweight Champion, sending him packing with his tail between his legs. Got screwed by former co-owner and former XWF World Heavyweight Champion who fast counted away his title. Kicked his ass so bad he switched sides. Had the girlfriend of one of the two men who cost him that match in the other promotion, kidnapped. A former IOW Champion left the promotion for the fWo rather than face him. Had the other co-owner renegotiate his contract so he could not be fired. Injurying a future IWO and Pro Wrestling Hall of Famer, IOW's first show back. Defeated and bitched a FAW Grand Slam Champion. Made the IOW World Champion and owner look like his personal bitch. Defeated and hospitalized a Pro Wrestling Hall of Famer and two-time SJPW World Heavyweight Champion. Pissed off every man, woman, and child in a five million mile radius. Sponsors wanted nothing to do with IOW with him around and the true owner, Lisa Tien was forced to close the promotion. Ron Starr = Dumbass The God of Violence is now in PWC as a result. The chaos will continue, be assure of that.
