Last Man Standing Sunday, June 15th, 2003 Hershey Park Arena, Hershey, PA Live and Only on Pay Per View The thunderous opening riffs of MC5's "Kick Out the Jams" explode out as the PWC graphic gives way to the Last Man Standing intro sequence. All 32 men and women who have fought in the tournament flash up, with the images of Paula Michelle Smith, Karina Wolfenden, Rhett Locke and Clark Carlisle left at the end, before highlights of all four begin to play, ending with they respective quarter final winning moves. The four LMS hopefuls' images are then shattered as the giant Masked Monster, Penance is showcased... Shown destroying the tag team division, as well as Karina before Jim Axtell fells him. Multiple clips of Champion and Challenger follow, each man getting the upper-hand against the other many times... But ending with Axtell holding the PWC Title aloft. As the Last Man Standing image fades, we see the interior of the Hershey Park Arena in Hershey, Pennsylvania where a capacity crowd rise to their feet to cheer in the 2nd PWC pay-per-view.
