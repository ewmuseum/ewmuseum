Chandler Tsonda Biography Nicknames 'The Model Citizen' Hometown Portland, Oregon Birthday 2.29.70 Height 5'11 Weight 195 Fed Titles PWC Tag Champion (4/5/03-Current) Fed Awards In First PWC Match Ever, (Collectively as 2GVC) 59th in Jan/Feb '03 WO Polls Biography Chandler Tsonda was born in Portland, Oregon on February 29, 1970. He didn’t have an oppressed childhood. In fact, the most oppressed thing to him was the fact that technically his birthday only occurred every four years, a fact that brought childish fits up until he was…well he actually hasn’t ever stopped complaining about it. Anyway, his mother was a Vietnamese immigrant, who had come to the States in the 1930’s to escape poverty in her home country. Her name was Mei Tsait (pronounced ‘sigh’). She was a young woman, who spoke only a few words of English, and was lost in the large city of San Diego, California. Luckily, in the city at the same time was a half-Italian, half-English lieutenant based in San Diego named William Calvarès. A member of the American Air Force, he was serving his term on the homeland, working as a air traffic controller for B-52’s and other fighter planes that patrolled the Far East, mostly over Japan. By chance, on one of William’s days off, he visited a small massage parlor, that was known for giving its customers a bit more than just a massage. William decided that his post here hadn’t been getting him much tail, so he’d try this lovely little Asian massage parlor. In he went, and of course, his masseuse for the day was Mei. They talked for a while, and then they did several other things that men and women do. William tried to think not too much of it, but she stayed on his mind, and he continued to go to the massage parlor. After a while, William proposed and brought Mei into his home. There were married soon after, and a young son, by the name of Chandler was born not much after that. They both had different views on how to raise a child, but they did a reasonable job of raising the small boy. At age 2 for Chandler, William was forced to leave his base in San Diego for a cruiser off the coast of Oregon, where Chandler followed, and that’s where the boy would grow up. As a boy, he never truly looked up to his father. He loved his father, of course, but he didn’t like the military life, and never liked violent sports, although he was a terrific athlete in tennis and squash, and stayed in terrific shape into his teen years, while his father was almost never home. His mother promoted lots of exercise and healthy food for her only child. Though one of the most fit boys in his school, and very handsome, Chandler didn’t dig the whole girlfriend scene. He wasn’t gay either though, because he didn’t even want to be friends with girls. In his opinion, girls at his school weren’t up to his level of perfection (although arguably, no one was). Chandler was an average student, and went to the University of Vermont, where he majored in industrial scienes, but really only one field interested Chandler, the field where he could make a living based entirely on his good looks and perfect body: modeling. His modeling career is famous, and you’ve obviously heard of it, so we won’t go into details about it, but he is definitely the most famous male model of the past twenty years, and you’d just disgust him if you told him you hadn’t heard of him, so don’t even try. Somehow, after he got kicked out of modeling, he found his way to wrestling, despite it being the total opposite of Chandler’s lifestyle and opinions. He has now found a place that he wishes to call home with PWC. A place that he can declare his excellence and rule over all other normal ugly people who dare call themselves men of the same profession as him. Yes, he’s THAT egotistical.
