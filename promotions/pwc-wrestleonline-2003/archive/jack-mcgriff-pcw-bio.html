Jack McGriff Biography Nicknames 'The Stalker' Hometown Chicago, IL Birthday July 16, 1969 Height 7' 0' Weight 297 Federation Awards -none- Biography As part of 18 Hole Assault, Jack McGriff was riding high in PCW. However, the mysterious disappearance of his partner, Putter Mayhem, cost him the PCW Tag Team Titles and left him stranded and despondent in PCW. Resigning from the federation, the depressed McGriff wandered from odd job to odd job... flipping burgers... volunteer fireman... quality control inspector... Finally, while working as an Assistant Manager at Home Depot #7462 (Topeka, KS), he recieved a call from the offices of PWC. It seems that the owner of the fed was looking for a big man to head up the federation's security detail; would McGriff be interested in the job? Throwing down his orange apron, McGriff immediately went to PWC Headquarters and took the post. Now, with his will to live refreshed, McGriff is ready to take no nonsense and protect the wrestlers and staffers of the PWC at all costs. He may not have had the most accomplished career, but he IS a former PCW Champion, and at 7 feet and nearly 300 pounds, anybody wishing to mess with the PWC's Chief of Security is going to have to bring their 'A' game... and pray it'll be... ENOUGH!
