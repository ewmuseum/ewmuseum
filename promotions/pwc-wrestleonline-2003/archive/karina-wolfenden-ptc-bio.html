Karina Wolfenden Biography Nicknames The Boarding Beauty, K-Wolf Hometown Albany, New York Birthday 22/12/79 Height 5'10 Weight 153 Fed Awards Runner-Up in PWC's Last Man Standing. Biography Amongst the populous of the world are many walking contradictions. One of them, a young girl from Albany who’s never quite seemed at ease with her place in life. A millionaire father while she can’t stand watching the greed of the planet. A six-figure trust fund while she riots alongside anti-capitalists. A second home on the snow while never visiting her family’s chalet in the Swiss Alps. A PWC contract while she looks like a strong wind could bruise her. Miss Karina Wolfenden can not be summed up in words, for she can not even sum herself up in her own head. All she knows is what she loves and what she hates... With an un-canny ability to use what she hates to fund what she loves. She doesn’t need to be in PWC, she doesn’t need to fight on it’s tatty canvas pulled tight over it’s degrading ring. For Karina, there is no stress in wrestling, it’s not in her blood, she’s not tied to it, she does it because she loves it. Wrestling, the under-appreciated Extreme Sport. How is wrestling on the Pro Wrestling Circuit any different to soaring up into the Alpine air with the snowy slope below you, kicking your legs back to feel the edge of your board through the gloves that hug your hand? How is the thrill of flying from the top rope any different to opening up the throttle on your bike and feeling that brief second where inertia tries to best you before you catch up to your machine? People wrestle for money. People wrestle for fame. People wrestle because they know nothing else. Karina wrestles because it’s fun. It’s risky. It’s edgy. It’s a test. It’s unpredictable. It’s challenging. It’s skilful. It’s a rush. It’s a sport. It’s a hobby - There, said it. Whether on the mountain, on the road, or in the ring, Karina is Karina. And that’s all she wants to be. Other Fed Awards 13th in the EW 24/7 Rankings Wrestling Style Inventive, Aerial, Evasive, Spot Wrestler Finisher Move The WolfTamer Finisher Description Reverse Huricanrana from the top turnbuckle. Finisher Setup Move Skinned Flying Squirrel Setup Description With her opponent dazed and facing away from her, Karina makes her way to the top rope and leaps off to land on their shoulders, facing forward with their head clamped between her knees. From here she then whips her upper body forward and twists her hips Other Moves The Mayday Riot - Running Bulldog/Diamond Cutter Hybrid applied while running past an opponent from behind. Sinful Indy Air - With a her opponent facing her, Karina leaps with her legs together from the top rope, pushing them out to her side while keeping them touching to deliver a push-off dropkick to the victim’s chest that allows her to land on her feet. Can be done as a Spring-Board move too. Frontside 360 Tailgrap - Standing in front of a stunned opponent, Karina leaps up, spinning to her right and rests her left hand on the top of her oponents head while kicking her right leg back behind her to catch the victim with a toe kick to the chin before continueing around to land back on her feet facing the same direction as before. Frontflip 180 Method - Springing from the top turnbuckle, Karina executes a frontflip to land on her opponents shoulders, facing them. Then, twisting her knees to force them to turn, she moves them around 180 degrees so she's facing back towards the buckle she leapt from before pushing herself off (using both hands on their head to push herself off) them to hit them with a variety of moves such as a Dropkick to the face or a high DDT. The Luscious Jackson - Running at her opponent, she flies at them as if for an Aerial Clothesline, Flying Forearm etc., but instead wraps her arm around their neck and swings through, kicking her legs out behind her and dropping them with an impressive Sleeping Neckbreaker. Teardrop Explodes - (Used as a very opportunist move, and is often hit out of nowhere if the slightest chance presents itself) A very quick rotation 450 Splash, not getting much height but huddling her frame up into a tight ball until the very last second before almost exploding out. Other Regular Moves - Handvault over their head into an Edge-O-Matic, Crescent Kicks with both feet in sequence, Flip-Over Neckbreaker, Hiptoss Slam, Russian Legsweep/Reverse DDT Hybrid, Victory Roll, Teasing a Victory Roll into a Reverse Huricanrana, Drop Toe Hold > Rolling Senton > Standing Moonsault, Tornado Bulldog.
