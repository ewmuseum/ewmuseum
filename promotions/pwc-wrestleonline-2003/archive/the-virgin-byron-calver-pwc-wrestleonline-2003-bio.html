'The Virgin' Byron Calver Biography Nicknames 'The Virgin' Hometown Boise, Idaho Birthday November 4, 1979 Height 5'10' Weight 206 Biography It wasn't quite meant to be like this originally, but one could probably have assumed that from Byron Calver's current position in life...It's not often that somebody comes along with the stigma of being 'the proudest virgin in professional wrestling.' How did this come along? What had led him from the calm sheltered suburban life of a Christian youth in Idaho to inflicting pain upon other men in the ring with armbars, leglocks, and steel chairs? The answers stemmed from his upbringing, it seems..Byron was born into a religious household, his parents Joshua and Emily both Roman Catholic and eager to instill what they considered properly beliefs into their impressionable child's mind. By the age of 4, he knew his ABCs, his 123s, and his Anger, Lust and Gluttony, and as grade school neared, the actions of his parents grew in magnitude upon the personality of young Byron... They meant well, they really did, they'd heard how much the youth of the 80s had been demoralized from those of previous decades and they weren't willing to take the risk that their firstborn son was to end up just a degenerate member of society, detached from the Church to the point of venial sin..They wouldn't have that for their child. So, instead of risking exposure to the perversions of his potential classmates that could scar his mind, they kept a vigilant watch over his activities, keeping him mostly separate from other youth outside of the children of couples of similar devotion to their faith and away from the horrors of the media.. In essence, the Calver parents brought their son up in a sort of decelerated social stream, as he was introduced to concepts that needed to be addressed only vaguely at first and with the basic moral tenets they wished him to follow, before expounding when they felt he was mature enough to understand. This, of course, was no easy task, and Byron was kept from the normal educational system until about halfway into the typical high school season, when he was enrolled into St. Marguerite Catholic Academy in Boise, a hope on the parents' part to make sure that he wasn't around too many corrupting influences at once, which may pose enough ethical hazard to extend beyond his grasp and reduce him to the level of the common thug or pervert... It worked, for the first two years or so. Byron's moral standards had been carefully ingrained into him through positive reinforcement methods, and the parents' fears were starting to subside a little as their son made a reasonably good transition into mainstream academics.. However, something went terribly wrong as he neared the end of his Grade 10 year: St. Marguerite had been hit hard by a dropoff in enrollment which had seen steady losses for the school in the course of the 80s, and by 1995, these losses had spread to the economic balances, forcing the once-healthy academy into closure once more and leaving the Calvers with a tough choice: Pull Byron out of the social environment in the midst of his adolescence, or transfer him into the public system with its questionable moral caliber.. With trepidation, they opted for the latter, fearing that a removal from society could stunt his social growth, and hopeful that maybe the people at Gerald Ford High School wouldn't be as bad as they thought...This hope lasted for the better part of a year. It was subtle at first, he'd been careful to seem like part of the crowd without bringing up his religion, just as they said to do, and nothing of real note happened at first...Seeing this seemingly good omen, Josh and Emily finally decided that it was time to teach their son the broader scope of relationships, and in particular the specifics of dating. Indeed, Byron seemed to catch onto this quickly as well, though awkwardly as most kids tend to be on their first dates...It was the third date where things got hairy. Byron wasn't too shabby in the looks department, and come the fall of 1994, he'd managed to catch the blue eyes of a cute brown-haired girl in his Physics 12 class, Sarah Beckham, and they made plans to go to the movies (naturally a PG-rated film) on Friday night. He was a little more excited about this date than the previous ones, which were more or less just trial dates in preparation for the moment when he would actually have an interest in the girl, and they seemed to be paying off as things had been going swimmingly, Sarah and Byron making what he felt to be an instant connection... However, about halfway into the film, she started to make moves toward a different type of connection, her mild cuddling to the side and light kisses narrowly more flattering than distressing Byron as he remained comfortable...She had picked the upper deck of the theater for a reason, and that became all too clear as she pulled him tighter, contact between their upper bodies becoming a little too close for his liking suddenly, and as her hands slid down toward the waistband of his jeans, Byron's mind could only flash back to the lessons he'd been taught by his parents: Don't lie, don't steal, don't take the Lord's name in vain or use profanity, don't do things that your Sunday school minister wouldn't approve of, and smack dab in the middle of it all at this moment, SAVE YOURSELF FOR MARRIAGE... He panicked, pushing away shocked and disappointed simultaneously by her actions, leaving the theater in a rush to get home...But he couldn't just tell his parents about it. He knew their thoughts about sex in modern society, it was the root of so much evil to them that they would be disappointed in him no matter of his managing to avoid it in the end...They might ground him, or worse...pull him out of the system. He thought of his friends, the people who cared about him, he couldn't just leave them..Lying wasn't right, but his parents had told him that hurtful truth could be just as bad as a harmless lie. At school, however, the truth couldn't be avoided, and Byron started to come home complaining of bullying problems, bumped and bruised a good amount. Hardpressed to explain it, he went for a condensed version of the truth; that his meaner classmates had mocked him for being a virgin, and when he defended his life choice, they responded with violence. Josh and Emily were heartbroken for their 'little boy' Byron, and they didn't have the heart to take him out of the system so near to a high school diploma because of others' persecution..They needed to find an alternate method of dealing with the problems, and the school was being of no use regarding the treatment he got and would likely continue to get. Even if Byron made it out, he'd still be a walking target with his reputation, and without a University degree that he'd never be able to go for with the influences of campus, he'd never get a job worth beans...Desperate to help Byron out, Josh called up an old family friend with a wrestling background, Trent G. Zimmer, and the two men set out to teach Byron how to fight. The early lessons were rather harsh for the now-barely-17-year-old senior, but he learned quickly and the bullying subsided to a controllable level with some moves shown here and there when necessary, until finally he got his diploma with the class of 1997..There wasn't much he'd be able to do industry-wise though, coming out of school and into fast food, retail or one of many other bad jobs, and they recognized this. However, Byron had precious little adaptable skill than his religious knowledge, which would never carry out the family name.....And his wrestling. Byron Calver might have been a cautious boy in high school, but where 'The Virgin' had become a term to fear hearing, in his independent federation bookings he tended to make it an issue himself. He had seen too many people fall prey to STDs and pregnancies already now, and so he took up the cause of abstinence through his wrestling.... Perhaps, he could make a difference... Perhaps, he could show them the light. Wrestling Style Technical, with a good deal of extreme experience in addition Finisher Move Abstinence Finisher Description Modified Figure Four Leg Lock, squeezing opponent's legs together in process Finisher Setup Move Ain't No Safe Way Setup Description Forward Russian Leg Sweep
