Chryste Biography Nicknames The Pretty Hate Machine, The Anti-Savior Hometown Rome, Italy Birthday 6-15-85 Height 5'9' Weight 140 Biography Chryste is the alleged Princess of several Italian organized families. Her father, who was alleged head of the Dons of the Families, was recently murdered, thus leaving her at head of the house. A marriage was arranged recently for a “head” of her household, which was to the son of a Japanese Billionaire, the alleged head of the Yakuza (Japanese Mafia) in an attempt to unite the two factions. Chryste in turn hates this, and her only true dream at one time was Music, however, playing the type of music they do, popularity in the “Mainstream” world is pretty much impossible, and she refuses to stray from what she loves. So in a stunt for publicity, her fellow bandmates enrolled her for a tryout with the DWO, which she passed and soon found herself fighting in the league. After winning the Intercontinental Title, and being accepted into the Circle of Rage, Chryste was starting to pull away from her musical love, and become engulfed in the addiction created by Callisto Bane, known as Dominant Wrestling. She was stuck, under the 'protection' of Callisto and Draven, as then superstar, Jade, hunted her like a dog, claiming her to be the 'next' God, who would die at her hands, instead of tainting the holy land with her impurity. She entered the GTT on a whim, not really undertsanding what she was getting her young, inexperienced self into, but she had her heart set on full intentions of coming home the victor. After a heart filled attempt against Inphino Blitz, in which she shocked the world by almost winning, Chryste was set in her mind that she could, infact succeed as a wrestler. In reward for being the PTC's prime hope in the GTT, Chryste was awarded a World Title shot, against Adam Stryker. She won, through disqualification, and a huge brawl soon broke out, in which Chryste was kidnapped by an unseen foe. A week later, Chryste was found dead in an alleyway, hours away from the shows location. Nearly a month later, however, Chryste walked out onto the stage at the DWO Morbid Intentions main event, alive, but unwell. She had been diagnosed with a severe amnesiac condition, as well as the constant testing she has been put under to insure that she is, in fact, Chrystal Fellini. Within the month after, Chryste's protector, Draven, had seemed to of disappeared from his duties to protect her, and she traveled home to Rome with her friend Cera, unseen. As of late, she has been rarely seen in the world of wrestling, if seen at all. Rumor has it that while not fully 100% cured from her amnesiac state, she has been resuming work on a side project band, as well as a second chance at her marraige with Kazuyin Kyoshin. Other Feds DWO Other Fed Awards DWO: Intercontinental Champion(2x), Tag Team Champion. Babyface of the Year. Feud of the Year. Rookie of the Year. Stable of the Year. GTT3 Bracket 1 Champion. Wrestling Style Amatsuotome Jadoku (A Custom form of Martial Arts based on fluid movements and vital strikes. Think Jackie Chan meets Steven Segal. Translated to Celestial Venom.) Finisher Move The Chrystalfixion Finisher Description Chryste locks her opponents in a crucifix maneuver, rolling them back to the mat. Instead of an attempted pin however, she repeatedly elbows them in the side of their head until submission or KO occurs. Finisher Setup Move Downer Setup Description Arm Wrench into a jumping Hook Kick. Other Moves Cleansing - A sleeper type maneuver, where the opponents arm is trapped, by having Chryste's shoulder beneath it, and bent back at the wrist, held by one hand, while the other hand is used to press her thumb behind her opponents jaw, and into the pressure point beneath the ear. 'The Chaos Effect' - With her opponents face down, she straddles their back, locking them in a full nelson type maneuver, and pulling back like a camel clutch. She shakes them back and forth, screaming in their ear as she works on the back. Diabolica Divinitus - A scissors sweep, where the opponent is brought to his knees only, instead of to the mat. Chryste follows up by spinning on her knee, and slamming her shin into the victim's throat, and falling atop him for the pin. Temptation of Chryste - Chryste moonsaults back off the turnbuckle, locks her legs around the opponent, and slips between their legs to pull them down and over for the pin. Nightfall - Reverse judo flip toss (monkey flip) Wheelbarrow suplex, Chryste has the opponent lying on the ropes or the turnbuckle and grabs the legs. then she slides underneath and props her feet against their midsection, rolling back and sending them back first into the mat. optional damage can be done if Chryste jumps on the opponent's midsection after execution of move, also both legs can be hooked for a pin S.I.D.S. (The Seriously Inflicted Depressive Shit)-with the opponent on his back Chryste locks her legs around their arms and folds them up slanted so that she may hammer into the kidneys with forearms, fists, and elbows Infernal-Chryste, getting a running start, hops into the air, kicking her opponent in the chest, and then spins around, hitting a back kick to the throat of her opponent. (running corkscrew moonsault double kick onto a vertical opponent) Blitz - An Irish Whip YO-YO snap kick to face combo, where she repeatedly yanks the opponent back by his arm straight into her kicks, and ending with a stepover the yanked arm and nailing a calf kick lariat straight in the face. Kiss of Death - Chryste grabs her opponent and locks them in a huge kiss, before pulling them back by the hair and spitting in their face. She follows up rapidly by slamming a front elbow strike into their nose. Open handed kidney strikes. Basic kicks and punches in the martial arts form of Karate. Several moonsault and splash style maneuvers using the ropes for her momentum. Dropkicks (from the ground or the turnbuckle.) Chryste has been known to create spur of the moment moves of very odd nature to even up the score of size and power. Entrance Music 'Automatic Lover' by Theatre of Tragedy
