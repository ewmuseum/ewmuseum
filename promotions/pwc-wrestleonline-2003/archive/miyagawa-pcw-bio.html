Alexander Miyagawa Biography Nicknames Miya; Miya-Jobber Hometown Anaheim, California Birthday 27-08-1980 Height 5'10' Weight 220 Fed Awards PWC Comissioner Biography It all started off with a supermarket job sending him slightly loopy and an unfortuanate win in a state lottery syndicate. That all happened so long ago now that it seems like a lifetime ago. He fought in WwOwW2000 for a very brief time, before being picked up by the then fledgling PCW as an opener. His first PCW match was during the early part of November 2000, where he switched Brian Hanson's themetune to 'Mmm Bop'... starting a legacy of comedy. He went on to lose that match after Jon Kano threw Ming at him, while Lackostress got the pinfall on Hanson. His second match pitted him against Dan Hollywood and Clyde... he would go on to feud with Clyde on and off for the rest of his PCW career. Alliances 1 would create the first ever Asylum stable, with Miyagawa aligning himself with the already existing Trashcan Man-Joker tag team. They would team up with Meanstreak on the night, and come third placed overall, but afterwards the team stayed together and that team in one form or another is still with us two and a half years later. Miyagawa would interfere in Kid Wonder versus Sereph causing another rift that never had a conclusion. Following a number of defeats at the hands of Jake Keeton, he eventually wins the PCW Cruiserweight title after winning the Kid Wonder rumble. Kid Wonder was supposed to fight him for the title, but instead smashed him over the head with it and gave him it. Something that Miyagawa never forgave him for. He would lose that title some two weeks later after a successful defence to... Jake Keeton, who had just returned from injury. He took on an apparentice in Phoenix and taught him how to fight, having been in PCW for coming up to six months. Phoenix eventually joined Asylum after The Joker retired from wrestling never to be seen or heard from again. Right now though, the biggest break of his career was about to happen. In a World Title Tournament, he was placed against the former champion (the title had been stripped from him) in the second round. He beat his first round opponent in under a minute and then faced The Reverend, Jon Hull. After a gruelling bout, Miyagawa stepped out victorious with Phoenix's aid. Miyagawa would later have a go at Phoenix, something which Phoenix remembered a month later. His next round was against none other than Jake Keeton. Keeton had lost the Cruiserweight Title by this time. He would have to face first Keeton and then Meanstreak afterwards, who had already won his third round match. He defeated Keeton for the first and only time before taking Meanstreak right to the limit before the PCW Hall of Famer reversed Therapy into his own finisher and pinned Miyagawa for the three count. This didn't daunt Miyagawa as this was the start of bigger things. He went on to win a number one contender's match for the world title, defeating Meanstreak, Slade and Howard Porter in the process. He went on to lose to Frisco, due mainly to being distracted after the death of his father two days before. Phoenix attacked him out of jelousy along with Montrose although Miyagawa did fight Phoenix and Montrose both out of PCW. For the next few months his heart wasn't really in it. Asylum disbanded for the time being and PCW closed for three weeks. He came back from the closure as a new man, he teamed with Kensuke Suzahara, a former Fedwars enemy to take the PCW Tag Team titles for a brief title reign. Because of his new stable 'The Elements' suddenly everyone seemed to want to be Asian for the next month. He was however, quickly muscled out of The Elements and found himself in a different stable, 'Old School' which quickly had a PPV named after them, entitled 'Back To Old School'. He teamed with Alan Kriegman, Jon Hull (the man who pretty much made him) and former Asylum enemy Ed. The team had a steady run until Jon Hull retired once again, although Miyagawa did retire Kensuke Suzahara out of PCW at that very PPV. Alliances 2 was coming up and the decision was made to reform Asylum... former members Jeremy Howard and Ed signed up along with newcomer Timo Bolambo. They ended up being victorious, Jeremy Howard winning the event itself, while Miyagawa was eliminated fifth from last. The year would end with Miyagawa dating a young Russian lady named Natalia. Natalia and Alexander were married after a whirlwind romance of only three months after she found out that she was pregnant. A feud with Ivan Stanslav would quickly follow, but ended abruptly when Ivan jumped ship to OSW. He went on to win the TV title in the early part of Febuary 2002 and then fought in the March Madness battle royale for the PCW World Title. Boda would win after the White Mexican eliminated both himself and Miyagawa at the same time leaving Boda alone at the end in the ring. His title reign would last exactly three days as Miyagawa defeated him for it on the following Tuesday Turmoil after the chaos from the infighting in Asylum caused Miyagawa to pin Boda after both were laid out. Boda would go completly crazy on Friday Fusion and throw Natalia through a wall while Miyagawa was cutting an interview in the ring. Miyagawa rushed to her aid, but was threatened with firing if he left the building before fighting his Television Title defence against Akiyama in the main event later that night. Miyagawa stayed, and lost the match due to a beating from Boda at the end of the match, losing his Television Title to Akiyama in the process. A rematch for the World Title on the next Turmoil ensued and Boda won the belt back after the same happenings occured, but with Boda ending up covering Miyagawa. Boda versus Miyagawa would end up being on the headline of the next two PPV's with Miyagawa retiring Boda at the second one. Miyagawa then took some time off to look after his still injured wife and new born baby. He returned with a new look Asylum consisting of Anarchy and the P.W.A. and immediatly started making people's lives hell. Anarchy won the Exteme title and P.W.A. were looking good for the Tag Team titles when Natalia saw Anarchy kiss Miyagawa on the cheek as she left the stable to be with her family. Natalia immediatly left Miyagawa and took thier baby son, Christopher with her. Miyagawa couldn't do anything, as she claimed that he was a violent man in the courts... something that they were all together too ready to believe since his profession was being a professional wrestler. P.W.A. eventually got sick of him moping around and not being generally helpfull... or selfish as they liked to think and took matters into thier own hands. Nerezza smashed Miyagawa's skull fifteen times into a brick wall, causing his skull to shatter. He didn't take being injured lightly. He took the quickest form of surgery, a solid metal skullcap attached to the outside of the head along with thirty pounds of spinal supports installed into his back and took his skills to OSW for five weeks. However, the extra weight became impracticle and he left, in order to get the regular surgery performed. That was December 2002 and now the surgery is finished, he is back to his old ways once again. He is however a man who will no longer trust anyone. Kid Wonder, Phoenix, Natalia, Nerezza... they all stabbed him in the back eventually, and he expects that if he lets his guard down someone will again. He stands alone, and his pride prevents him from going back to PCW or OSW... the P.W.A. are still in PCW and Natalia of all people is thier manager. As for OSW... he just let the side down over there. So he looks for somewhere completly fresh, somewhere with new enemies and allies. But no one closer than an ally. Other Feds PCW, OSW, EW, GWF Other Fed Awards PCW World Champion, PCW Television Champion, PCW Extreme Champion, PCW Cruiserweight Champion (4-Time), PCW Tag Team Champion (w/ Kensuke Suzahara as The Elements), Team Winner PCW Alliances 2, Winner Kid Wonder Rumble, Joint Runner-Up March Madness 2001 Wrestling Style Suicidal, Luchador Finisher Move 1) Therapy; 2) Five Elements Frogsplash Finisher Description 1) Tazzmission; 2) Five Star Frogsplash Entrance Music 'The Last Song I'll Ever Write About A Girl' by The Ataris
