PWC TV card for 21 June 2003

21 June 2003

PWC TV Card for June 21st LIVE from the Blue Horizon in Philadelphia, PA More Matches to be Announced PWC Television Title Match VS. Hoyt Williams (c) vs. Warpath Warpath makes his return to PWC TV with a huge opportunity: To grab a bit of PWC gold. Can the Keyport Killer defeat God's Gift to Wrestling? Tune in this week to find out! Three Way Tag Team Dance Cage Match For the PWC Tag Team Titles vs. vs. Sinister Steven Lee & Ariath vs. J-Con & Clark Carlisle vs. 2GVC Neither Sinister Steven Lee's great performance at Last Man Standing nor words of warning to Clark Carlisle apparently have gone unnoticed the higher ups in PWC, especially CEO Ron Starr's personal lawyer and #1 Contender for the PWC World Heavyweight Title, Clark Carlisle. Lee, who claimed to be on the prowl for the PWC World Heavyweight Title, apparently will have to settle for his first shot at PWC gold being for the tag team titles, as he has been paired with the enigmatic, and some would say, untalented Ariath, to take on not on the PWC World Tag Team Champions, 2GVC, but Starr Power members Carlisle and Jonathan Conspiracy as well. Couple this all with a steel cage, and you will have yourself what promises to be one bloody affair. Also this week on PWC TV, PWC World Heavyweight Champion, Jim Axtell, will give an exclusive interview to our own Jason Maddux to discuss his recent victory over Penance and his plans for the future, El Spiffy! will address the rumors that his nemesis Ebola III has been signed with PWC and a new PWC superstar will make his debut. So tun in this week at 10 pm EST for all the wild action on PWC Television!
