Totakoxe Salvador Biography Nicknames Tota, The One in Demand Hometown Buenos Aires, Argentina Birthday 1st December 1977 Height 6 ft. Weight 230 Biography Failure, death and social enemies have held a grip of Totakoxe's life for so long... Born on a cold December night of 1977 in the capital of Argentina, the youngster came into the world to a pair of loving parents - Jose and Maria Salvador. When he was three years old, his baby brother was brought into the world. However, only several weeks into Pablo’s life, his mother was found dead. Distraught by the death of his wife, Jose Salvador went missing for three days, leaving the hospital to take care of the 3 week old Pablo and the 3 year old Tota. Although seemingly oblivious to the happenings around him, Totakoxe was mentally scarred and would continue to be for his next few years of life. A single father – not any normal single father, brought up Tota and Pablo. They were brought up by a father in the wrestling business. He was constantly on the road with his two children touring with him. They had no set home and lived on a bus the majority of the time. This isolation has followed Totakoxe throughout his life. “Where’s mommy?” he asked day after day. “She’s at home resting, son” Jose would often reply. Truth was, Jose asked himself that very question day in, day out. He missed his wife just as much as his children did. His wrestling career underwent a downward spiral as Jose tried to juggle raising two kids and wrestling. Finally, the pressure became too much for him to handle and he left the wrestling business in Argentina. Now seven years of age, Tota couldn’t wait to return to their home. “Mommy will be at home, mommy will be at home!” he said anxiously to his four-year old brother, Pablo. Unfortunately Jose did not hear this and the return home was a miserable occasion for the 3 Salvadors. That very day, Jose broke his own heart for the final time as he explained to his children just what had happened to their mother. Years went by as the Salvadors lived it large on the money Jose had saved through wrestling. Pablo was Tota’s best friend and Tota was Pablo’s best friend. Outside the triangle of family, the two teenagers had no friends. In their minds, they didn’t need or want friends – they had each other and that’s all that had mattered to them. When Tota became 16 years of age, Jose was struggling to keep control of both him and Pablo. He enlisted the help of various babysitters but none of the long list could control the rebellious duo. Through Jose’s list of links to wrestling federations, he sent Totakoxe to the United States of America. One of Jose’s long-time friends had taken the task of making sure Tota went to school to receive a thorough education. Meanwhile, in the South American country of Argentina, Pablo was under the constant control of his overcautious parent. The years went by and Totakoxe Salvador failed in school. He spent his days in school daydreaming about girls, cars and alcohol. He was a rather attractive teenager and this was reflected in the amount of attention he received from the ladies. However, still wary of the death of his mother, he was frightened of becoming attached to a girl in case the same scenario would arise. He lived an isolated life. A life locked up inside a run-down apartment in the heart of L.A. He still had no friends but now the loss of his family was getting the worst of him. Little is known about Pablo and Jose’s life in Argentina while Totakoxe was in the US of A. Tota attempted a full-time job as a factory worker but was fired only a week into the job for picking a fight with a fellow immigrant. What could Tota succeed at? What was the one thing his family had excelled in the past? WRESTLING. Totakoxe joined a well-known wrestling academy known as Primetime Wrestling Academy. Only several weeks into his training, his father and younger brother paid a visit to him in Los Angeles. They unleashed a huge surprise to Totakoxe, which was that they had moved from Argentina to stay with him! Jose also announced that he would train Totakoxe to the top of the wrestling world. Several weeks passed and Tota was becoming more and more of a wrestler. With the help of his father, he was deemed a success by the PWA and was soon packing his bags to move to PWC. Albeit a small federation, Tota recognised the potential of this fed and despite having to move across the country to wrestle there, jumped at the chance. He is still awaiting his debut in PWC, but the company has high hopes for him… Other Feds PWA, ToW, eVe, 360° Other Fed Awards PWA Coming Storm Champion, #30 WrestleOnline Rankings (Jan/Feb '03) Wrestling Style Technical/Submission Finisher Move Tota Destruction Finisher Description A camel clutch on the opponent while on the mat in the center of the ring. Finisher Setup Move The Last Chance Setup Description A sleeping neckbreaker. Other Moves General Moves: A DDT. A swinging neckbreaker. A double-arm DDT. A kneebreaker followed by a figure-four leglock. A piledriver. A double underhook suplex. Side walk slam. An array of suplexes (ie. Vertical, German) 'Dislocator' (Trademark move) - He grabs the opponent by the head/hair and executes a shoulderbreaker on them. Usually followed by a submission move. + Other moves that are generally done (ie. DDT, atomic drop etc.) Turnbuckle Moves: Tota will rarely climb the turnbuckle for a high-risk move but when he does he will do the following; Elbow drop. Leg drop. Flying clothesline. With opponent in the turnbuckle; Mount the turnbuckle and throw 10 hard punches to the side of the opponent's head - played along with the crowd. Tree of woe, followed by forearm strikes or kicks. He may also lock the opponent’s legs around the bottom ropes and thrust forearm strikes/knife edge chops toward the opponents chest. Submission Moves: An ankle lock. A figure-four leglock. Torture rack - if opponent is light enough to lift onto his shoulders. A sharpshooter. 'Chokechain' (Trademark move) - Tota grabs his opponents head (ie. the position of a stunner) but keeps their neck held tightly against his shoulder as he continues to apply pressure to their neck, thus choking them. *This move can be easily reversed* 'Time To Cry' (Trademark move) - Tota gets the opponent face down and holds their arms behind their neck causing immense pain to the opponents shoulder, neck, arm and head. This is generally followed by a double underhook suplex to further the damage. Entrance Music 'Hero' by Enrique Iglesias
