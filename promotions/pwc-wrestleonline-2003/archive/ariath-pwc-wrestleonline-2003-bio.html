Ariath Biography Nicknames The Puppet, The Doll, 'Paula's Man' Hometown Seattle, Washington Birthday September 24, 1977 Height 6'2' Weight 246 Biography After being divorced by his wife, Andrea, he turned to drugs and alcohol to cope with his problems. A man with a history of misdeamors, especially during his days as a minor, Jonathon Norton was going off the deep end fast. Though he had visitation rights, he hadn't gone to see his kid for months. Paula saved him from going to prison alongside her mother, with whom he was having an affair at the time. Obviously attracted to her, and afraid of her threats that she will put him back in jail if he screws up, Jonathon agrees to all the rules she lays down for him, assumes the identity of 'Ariath,' and follows Paula across the country. Back home, his wife has begun the process to take away all of his visitation rights. Paula Michelle Smith on Jonathon Norton: 'He's an idiot. But he's big, and he's easily controlled.' Wrestling Style Brawling, power moves, untechnical fist-fighting Finisher Move 'Bad Day' Finisher Description Sets opponent on top rope, while standing on second rope, then jumps backwards, pulling them down into a ball-buster. Finisher Setup Move 'Seattle Silencer' Setup Description Grabs opponent by jaw so that his hand covers their mouth, and pulls them into a headbutt. Other Moves Standing clothesline Series of haymakers Vertical Suplex Piledriver Jumping DDT Stomp while opponent is down Entrance Music 'Your Disease' by Saliva
