'Sinister' Steven Lee Biography Nicknames Sinister Hometown Lost Hills, CA Birthday July 17, 1981 Height 6'3' Weight 263 Biography You want to know about my past? Well so does everyone else, but you don't see me telling them either! My past is just that, MY past. It's done, and over with. What I did back then means nothing with what I will do now, in the present. I'm not here to have my past looked at and judged, I'm here to make my future a better place for me when I make it there. Other Feds PWA, PURE Other Fed Awards PWA Coming Storm Champion Wrestling Style Technical Brawler w/ Cowardly Heelish Tendancies Finisher Move S-Force 5 Finisher Description DVD to Toss Up Brainbuster Finisher Setup Move Unseen Misfortune Setup Description Top Rope Full Nelson Slam Other Moves Various Punches & Kicks, Sleeper Hold, Back Drop, Russian Leg Sweep, Front Russian Leg Sweep, Closeline, Dropkick, DDT, Snap Suplex, Belly to Belly Suplex, Belly to Back Suplex, German Suplex, Reverse DDT, Top Rope Missile Dropkick, Double Arm Underhook Suplex (Top Rope or Standing), Implant DDT (Top Rope or Standing) Trademark Move: Adrenaline Rush (With wrestler staggering on the opposite side of the ring, Steven Lee positions himself in a crouch waiting for wrestler to make it completely to his feet. Once wrestler turns around, Lee takes off in a dead sprint towards wrestler, and dives towards him, wrapping him up with his arms, and placing his shoulder into wrestler's gut, bringing him to the mat. (Spear)) Set-Up Move: Unseen Misfortune (With wrestler dazed, Lee places them up onto the top turnbuckle of the nearest corner with wrstler facing out. Lee then climbs to the second turnbuckle and applies a full nelson, and when he has it securely locked in, he climbs to the top rope, and stands wrestler up as well before jumping off backwards, and rotating ninety degrees, and slamming wrstler to the mat hard, while Lee lands on his side, only slightly affected by the fall. (Top Rope Full Nelson Slam)) Secondary Finisher: Melancholy Cry (With wrestler laying belly down, and Lee standing to the left of him, Lee goes down on his left knee. With his free right leg, then, he positions it over wrestler's body, and plants his right foot just away from wrestler's body, not letting his leg touch wrestler's back. Lee then takes his right arm, and wraps it around the front of wrestler's head, and pulls back applying pressure to the nose, face, along with his back. Then with his free left arm, Lee chicken wings wrestler's left arm, and begins tweaking it every which way while pulling back on wrestler's head while Lee's right leg blocks wrestler's back from going any farther, causing wrestler's back to stretch out while his face is being crushed, and his left shoulder being pulled out of socket. (Modified Dragon Sleeper Camel Clutch)) Primary Finishing Move: S-Force 5 (Lee picks wrestler up onto his shoulders with them belly down, feet out to Lee's left side, and the head over his right shoulder. After holding wrestler there for a few seconds, Lee tosses wrestler's legs out and up over his head, and he grabs ahold of wrestler's head with his right arm. Before letting wrestler get parallel to the mat, Lee position's himself under wrestler who is still in mid air, and Lee drops down to his back, bringing the now verticle wrestler down onto the top of his head. (DVD to Toss Up Brainbuster)) Entrance Music 'Sinner' by Drowning Pool
