Chris Campbell Biography Nicknames 'C.C.', 'Jackknife', 'Newbie' Hometown North Hampton, Massachusetts Birthday 6/21/80 Height 6'1' Weight 205 Biography So they give me this little box to describe myself. Wow... You know, until I sat down to try and write this thing up, it hadn't dawned on me just how far I've come. I actually made it - I'm a pro wrestler. I've wanted to be one ever since I was a little kid. Usually it's the kinda thing that goes away, like wanting to be a pirate or something. With me, it never went away. I guess I was lucky enough to have parents that actually supported me when they realized that it wasn't just some phase I was gonna grow out of. I wasn't stupid, though. I took my dad's advice and decided against putting all my eggs in one basket, and in 2001 I graduated from Westfield State College. I'll spare you the details of what school was like for an 18 year old English major who dreamed of becoming a sports entertainer, but lemme just ask you this: Would YOU have taken me seriously? Anyway, during college I spent summers training, and it definitely paid off when I signed up for the Primetime Wrestling Academy about 3 weeks after graduation. It paid off by showing me about a tenth of what it takes to break into this industry. El Diablo - yeah, he made us call him by his ring name. I guess it's something that just stuck - was just what I imagined a drill sergant at boot camp would be like. A total ballbuster, but him and Tempest did everything they could to shape us into talent worthy of airtime. My big break didn't come until the start of 2003 when I got a call from this guy called Ron Starr. I didn't know who the heck he was, but I knew a talent scout when I heard one - Diablo and Tempest told us there were dozens of indy league feds out there that always tried to leech rookies into contracts before they were ready - and I was about to hang up, until he introced himself a little better. Ron Starr was the Phantom Booker. I had heard that he was planning on making a comeback to the industry, but all I knew were the initials of his new project: PWC. He explained everything to me, that 'Pro Wrestling Circut' would be a pretty small time show (on account of the money being scarce) until interested picked up, which he assured me would happen in due time. Then he said that some guy named Ashton Grace had to drop his spot at the last minute. Then he said to me, exact words... 'You interested in taking his place?' The legendary Phantom Booker, who I now knew as Ron Starr, had actually ASKED me...asked ME...to join his new promotion. I never stopped to think that Mr. Starr was just the kind of guy Tempest and Diablo warned us about: a small-time fed owner claiming that his project will be the next big thing, making offers to unprepared students promising us the world (just sign on the dotted line, disregard that paragraph about ''soul ownership''). Never thought about just what it was that this Ron guy wanted from me. Never thought about his well-known reputation as career vampire, sucking as much as he can out of his 'superstars' before tossing aside whatever's left and finding new meat to exploit. None of that ever entered my mind. Here was THE Phantom Booker, and he personally asking me to come work for him. What would you have done?
