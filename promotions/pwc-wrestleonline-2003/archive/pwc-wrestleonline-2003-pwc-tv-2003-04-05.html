PWC TV Results for April 5th, 2003 Live from the Gymnasium at the College of New Jersey in Trenton, NJ Outside the College of New Jersey Gymnasium, at the entrance to the employee’s parking lot, a faded-maroon ’94 Accord pulls up to the security gate. Sitting within the booth, in a wooden chair leaned all the way back, with his legs kicked up on the desk, a rather chubby guard catches sight of the vehicle out of the corner of his eye. Sighing, the man reluctantly pulls himself away from the mini-television, slowly rises to his feet, and walks outside. He takes one quick look at the car, rolls his eyes, and hurriedly makes his way over to the driver’s side window, which promptly rolls down. Security guard (dismissively): Look, pal, this parking lot is for PWC employees only. Sitting behind the wheel of his car, Ariath just stares up at the guard with a blank, confused look. The guard points back towards the main road. Security guard: Why don’t you just hit the road, chief? Ariath (stammering): B-b-but I’m a wrestler. Security guard (scoffing): Yeah? And I don’t like donuts. Quit wasting my time. Ariath: But I AM a wrestler, I’m Ar- Security guard: Okay. So… if you’re an employee, then where is the placard for your car? The security guard looks down at the bewildered wrestler, a smug, humored look on his face. Ariath stares at the inside of his windshield for a long moment, before finally returning his gaze to the guard. Ariath: Decal? The guard starts laughing, and actually has to lean against the car to support himself. After a few moments, he finally catches his breath. Security guard: Now I know you’re screwing with me. Shooing Ariath away with his hands, the guard starts heading back for his station. Security guard (muttering): I can’t believe I missed some of ‘The Three Stooges’ for this dumb ass. His mouth slightly open, his eyes wide like a deer caught in headlights, Ariath just stares straight ahead for a long time. Just as the annoyed security guard is about to come back out and speak with him once again, the wrestler finally comes to his senses. Ariath carefully turns the car around, and starts driving off. In his wake, the disgusted security guard glares after the Accord, and then angrily shakes his head. . . . . . . . . . Staying outside the gymnasium, the PWC TV camera’s view is flooded by another pair of headlights. When they pan off to the side of the shot as the vehicle turns, we see that they belong to a Ford Explorer. As it’s tyres crunch the gravel, the car comes to a stop, the passenger side door’s window showing none other than PWC’s ‘Boarding Beauty’, Karina Wolfenden. The sound of two thousand or so fans inside cheering is heard in the distance as the door opens and Karina drops down onto the gravel, her knee seeming now fully heeled. She’s decked out in Skechers sneakers, bright orange cargo pants and a black strappy top, her sandy blonde and pink hair falling loose around her face. Straightening her clothes out, she then turns to the driver who’s now shutting off the engine. Karina: Thanks, Max... The travel budget never quite cuts it on rent week anymore. Her hands find themselves a warm home as they plunge into her deep pockets, stepping backwards away from the car as the smartly dressed driver drops down to the ground with the same crunch. Max Wolfenden, Karina’s older brother, is dressed in a v-neck grey jumper and smart black trousers. His brown hair is smartly, and conservatively, styled... The Wolfenden offspring certainly taking different style routes. Max walks around the front of the car and sighs, looking as if he’s going to carry on a conversation started in the car. Dutifully, Karina waits for him. Max: I wish you’d just take my money, Kari. At least then you could get another bike. I won’t tell dad if you don’t want, but I’d sleep easier knowing you had it. Looking as if it isn’t the first time she’s rejected the offer, Karina’s hands remain firmly planted in her pants’ pockets. Her head shakes, mane of shiny hair following it back and forth and bright green eyes looking in gratitude. Karina: I know, but I made my choice, Maxy... It was this, or being a Wolfenden. I chose this, and I have to stick to it. Taking your money would... Well, it would just make everything I resolved all for nothing. Frustrated, Max tries to find the middle ground. Max: At least let me talk to him, will you? I know he’s never been the warmest of people, but when he heard you got hurt he was... Well, he was really worried. Standing her ground, Kari again reflects the offer away. Karina: I have to do this by myself, and I can’t back down... No matter how hard, otherwise I don’t think I could look at myself. If dad wants to back down, then he knows where I am. But I’m sick of having to make choices for him, I’ve made my last one and I’m sticking with it. Impressed by his sister’s stubborn side, Max smiles as he shakes his head in defeat. Conceding with good will. He holds his hands up, signifying his retreat. Max: Okay, I get you. Just know that I’m here if you need anything. Her pink lips smiling, Karina nods to him. She knows it, and she’s grateful. Karina: I know, and I appreciate it. At least one Wolfenden understands me. Humble, Max shrugs it off. Max: Well, it isn’t easy, but... He pauses for a second and the siblings then chuckle, seeming perfectly at ease with one another despite the family’s problems. Max: Well, good luck with everything tonight. Don’t hurt those guys in there too bad... He goes to head back to the car but Karina is quick to leap in front of him and playfully block his path. Karina: And where do you think you’re going, big brother? Seeming confused, Max doesn’t get a chance to reply before Karina turns him back towards the building and pushes him reluctantly towards it. Karina: You’re here now, might as well stick around. Not really having much of a say in the matter, Max goes with the flow. Max: I..... I.... I don’t really have a say in this, do I? The smile on Karina’s face says it all. Even just a car journey with her brother has perked her up, she now knows she’s not alone. They may have fell very different distances from the Wolfenden tree, but the bond they have is holding strong. Karina: Have you ever? Max rolls his eyes and moves under his own steam now. Karina, spring in her step, ceases to push him and joins his side. Happy to be in one another’s company, the Wolfenden siblings head towards the venue, al Karina’s anxiety seemingly lifted. . . . . . . . . . Axtell reels backwards, his arms flailing wildly, and falls to the ground near the curb to the nearby street. Vehicles begin to stop to watch the action as Penance stalks his prey. Mark: Where are McGriff and the rest of the security detail for Christ’s sake?! Where are the police?! WHERE’S THE GODDAMN NATIONAL GUARD!? SOMEONE HAS TO STOP THIS MONSTER!! Bang: Look at them rubbernecks out there! Indeed there are many motorists who have stopped their vehicles and are watching in horror as the mammoth Penance yanks Axtell to his feet again. Jim can barely stand on his own as Penance rears back to connect with another right hand. Suddenly Axtell springs alive from the dead and fires a weak right hand that has little effect on Penance. The Masked Monster shakes his leather clad head a few times and then brings his arm back again. The groggy Axtell, his eyes glazed over, winces in pain as he launches another blow at Penance. Jim howls in pain as he lands on the battered knee which was injured earlier in the night. Penance stays on the attack, coming down across Axtell’s back with a clubbing forearm smash. Jim tumbles out into the street, the headlights from the stopped vehicles illuminating the pavement. Penance steps into the would-be spotlights, a sinister grin able to be made out under his mask. He scoops Axtell up and presses him over his head, walking around with him aloft like some sort of trophy. Many of the observers outside scream and shout at Penance, begging him to put Axtell down. Mark: What’s he gonna do with Axtell?! Jim comes to life once again, wriggling and flailing about until he falls to the ground behind Penance. Axtell rolls several feet away from the behemoth before struggling to his feet, hobbling on his bad leg. Penance spins around and beckons for Axtell to come at him as he flexes his humongous muscles. The people cheer for Axtell as he grits his teeth in determination. A low growl begins to erupt from Jim’s chest, then turns into a full out battle cry as he charges at Penance with every ounce of energy he has left. Mark: TAKE HIM OUT, JIM!! Bang: I wanna be saved, Malloy! Axtell limps across the pavement toward the waiting Penance, his lips curled and face mangled. He lunges at Penance, reaching for the Masked Monster’s throat. Penance takes one step forward and reaches out with his massive arms, catching Axtell in mid-air and thrusting back up into a gorilla press position. This time, however, Penance wastes no time as he pivots on his foot and hurls Axtell through the air like a Scud missile. Mark: NOOOOO!!! Jim Axtell shouts in horror as he is thrown through the windshield of a Chevy Blazer that is parked in the street watching the action. Upon impact, the glass shatters and almost explodes from the SUV. Axtell’s body goes through the glass and the opening that remains, ending up sprawled out inside the front seats of the vehicle. The people standing watch are silenced with shock as Penance beats on his chest victoriously. Mark: OH MY GOD!! PENANCE JUST LAUNCHED JIM AXTELL THROUGH THE WINDSHIELD OF THAT VEHICLE!! The Prophet snickers as he peers over the hood of the Blazer, satisfied with his beast’s handy work. The camera moves in to view Axtell, and finds nothing but a bloody mess. The body of Axtell is nicked and cut up by the shattered glass, blood dripping from numerous lacerations on his body. Mark: Penance and Solomon have gone too far this time!! They are trying to end Jim Axtell’s life! Bang: Good! Den my tax dollars can stop going to support his family!!! Mark: (ignoring Bang) We’re out of time, folks! We need some medical personnel out there NOW!! Bang: Praise Solomon for my salvation!
