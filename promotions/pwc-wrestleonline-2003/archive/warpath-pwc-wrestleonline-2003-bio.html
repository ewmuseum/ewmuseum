Warpath Biography Nicknames 'Path Hometown Keyport, NJ Birthday 11/12/78 Height 6' 7'' Weight 280 Biography Unlike most professional wrestlers these days, Warpath experienced a childhood filled with not tragedy and loss, but with happiness and wealth. Growing up in a small town, financially secure, and happy, Warpath was shaped by events that took place as a late teen, early adult. At the age of 14, Warpath's mother grew ill, and was forced to stop working. Though his father had a job, he didn't make enough money to support the lifestlyle that they were accostomed to. The cost of living got to be too much for him to pay for alone, so the family was forced to move out of the sub-urban town on Keyport. Decent places to live in New Jersey are hard to come by, especially when you are put in this kind of situation for the first time. Warpath's father was searching alone, with a growing child and a dying wife at home to worry about. After a few stints in small apartments and a few living room couches, they were made an offer that was hard to refuse. A house, 2 bedroom, 1 1/2 bathroom, all necessities provided, for a CHEAP price. Warpath's father jumped at the opportunity and gathered what little funds that he had to put the down payment on the house. A move he would eventually regret, but suitable for the time being. With the cost of supporting his wife great, and the stress level rising, Warpath's father was at the breaking point for any human being. He was a broken man, his physical and mental health were both horrible, and none of this was going unnoticed. Warpath, then 15, didn't deal with it well. His grades dropped and school didn't go so well. Constant fighting and trouble had him on probation and he was a top candidate to be sent to Coastal Learning Center, a correctional school in Jersey. At 16, Warpath's mother died, and things only got worse. Now in Coastal, he didn't get along with anyone, he didn't fight much, but certainly had no friends. That is probably what molded 'Path to be the man he is today, it is not often you will see him pursue a partner, and that is most likely because of friends as a child, or lack there of.
