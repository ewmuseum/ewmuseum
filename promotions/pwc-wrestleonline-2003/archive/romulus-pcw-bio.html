Romulus Biography Nicknames Emperor; Lord; Soverign Ruler; Master... take your pick. Hometown Hails from Ancient Rome... Birthday Unknown. Height 5'8' Weight 182 Biography Romulus and Remus founded Rome in the legendary tome. The person who is currently in PWC certainly does believe that he is Romulus, one of the two founders of Rome. If that is true or not has never actually been determined. But he does have the credentials... an American passport made out to Romulus... which means that a birth certificate must exist... somewhere. The date of birth on the passport doesn't seem to exist, and is yet to be questioned, so that only adds further to the mystery. Anyway, Romulus' modern life begins when he joined Primetime Championship Wrestling back in September 2000, going on to form a major stable called 'The Roman Empire' along with tag team partners Collosus and Brutus. He went on to win the Television title, and then just seemed to vanish after losing to Chef. When Ron Starr (Then the Phantom Booker) annouced the PWC Roster Romulus' name was amongst it... where the founder of PCW and OSW found his old roster member is unknown... but it was probably somewhere close to Ed's hiding place.
