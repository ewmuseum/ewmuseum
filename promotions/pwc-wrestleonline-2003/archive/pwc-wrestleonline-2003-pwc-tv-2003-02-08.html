PWC TV Results for Saturday, February 8th, 2003 Live from the National Guard Armory, in Atlantic City NJ The show fades in from black to reveal plain white walls reflecting the harsh strip lighting from overhead, giving an already clean and crisp seeming environment and even more austere and antiseptic feel. In the lower right hand corner of the screen is a small PWC logo with the words “Earlier Today.” A wall mounted digital clock shows confirms the graphic, as it shows a time of just after 4 PM. Below the clock is a glass fronted wall, behind which is seen a middle-aged woman … probably in her late thirties, though with a weary expression that could easily be the face of a woman twice her age … in the white uniform of a nurse. She looks up with an expression of barely concealed boredom as a figure approaches the desk. Wearing a pair of black baggies, a leather bum-bag around his waist, and a black sleeveless t-shirt that exposes his arms to view, a long haired man … the auburn hair tied neatly back into a plaited ponytail … smiles at the nurse, removing a pair of sunglasses, his bright green eyes glinting reflecting the humour. Leaning against the counter, the man speaks. Man: “Hi there, my name is Jay Phoenix, I have an appointment with one of the doctors for a physical” Without matching the smile, the nurse just stares up at the man, know revealed as Jay Phoenix. Nurse: “Which doctor?” A massive grin appears of Phoenix’s face as he waves his finger negatively in front of the nurse. Phoenix: “Hey, what do you mean ‘witch doctor’? Just because I am Native American does not mean that I am here to see a shaman you know!… though if my Grandfather was here that would be a shock!” Once again the nurse doesn’t miss a beat, her face remaining impassive as she stares up at Phoenix, his attempt at humour obviously lost on her. With a sheepish look on his face, Phoenix reaches into the bum-bag and pulls out a folded piece of paper, handing it to the nurse as he tries to explain. Phoenix: “ … erm … I am from the Professional Wrestling Circuit, and I have a session booked with Doctor Carter, full physical, insurance purposes … ma’am ..” Taking the paper from his hand, the nurse scans it, before giving pointing down an adjacent corridor, explaining where Phoenix is to go. Nurse: Oh … a ‘wrestler’ is it? Well, Doctor Carter can be found in the Orthopaedic department, just follow that corridor until you come to a junction, turn left and keep walking until you go past the Morgue … For the first time the nurse actually smiles ... or at least her face makes some attempt at movement. Phoenix visibly shudders at this, and grabs the paper back, rushing down the corridor as he shouts back over his shoulder. Phoenix: Thanks ... I am sure that I can find it from there! Rushing down the corridor, staying to the opposite side as he passes the morgue, Phoenix arrives at the Orthopaedic department, where there are a few chairs arranged around a table, magazines scattered over it. A buzzer on the wall near the only door has a small sign above it, stating that it should be pressed for attention, which Phoenix does, turning to start to sit down on one of the chairs, but being interrupted as the door is opened almost straight away. A greying man in a white knee length jackets smiles at Phoenix, as he reaches out to shake his hand. Doctor: Good afternoon Mr Phoenix, sorry if I startled you but you are actually my last appointment for the day, so we don’t have to keep you waiting. If you could follow me we can get started … a few physical tests to run first, but they shouldn’t be a problem, and then we will run some x-rays and a CAT scan. You should be out of here in a few hours. With that the doctor holds the door open for Phoenix to enter, following behind him. As the door closes an elderly looking man … his bald head lolling to one side as a small amount of drool drips down from his lower lip … is pushed past in a wheel-chair, what is visible of his arms and legs looking to be as thin and frail as sticks. The orderly … completely attired in blue, including a surgical hair net and mask that almost completely obscures his face from view … stops and looks at the closed door through which Phoenix has just passed. Reaching up to adjust the mask, the face … and the cold smile that plays across it … is revealed for less than a second, but long enough to see just how out of place the figure actually is.
