Jim Axtell Biography Nicknames 'The Axphyxiator' Hometown Papillion, Ne Birthday 3/31/75 Height 5'11' Weight 240 Fed Titles First Ever PWC World Champion Biography Jim Axtell was born on March 31, 1975 to Joseph and Margaret Axtell. He lived a fairly normal childhood in Papillion, Ne. His father was a teacher at Papillion-LaVista High School and his mother was a secretary at the Sarpy County courthouse. They lived the typical middle class life, not lavish but without much want. Jim was never the biggest or strongest kid, but he had a drive that lead him to success in many areas in his childhood. “That kid has a motor that just won’t quit running,” said Jim’s high school football coach. “He may not be the best player on the field but he gives 150% and that makes him great.” His outstanding effort at the inside linebacker position earned him All-State Honorable Mention his junior and senior years, and gave him hopes of continuing his football career in college. He received several offers from smaller schools around the area, but like most high school football players in Nebraska, he wanted to play for the Big Red. Jim decided to take his chances and walk on at the University of Nebraska in hopes of making the team and perhaps earning a scholarship. The first practice came around and Jim was pumped up like never before. The players started to do some running drills on the Memorial Stadium field turf. As the defense went through some pass defense drills, Jim made a cut on the turf and heard a pop in his knee. He immediately went down in pain, a torn ACL the unfortunate reason. Jim was devastated. His hopes of playing football for the Nebraska Cornhuskers was dashed away before it had even gotten started. Surgery and months of excruciating and intense rehab followed, with Jim determined to make his knee stronger than ever. Due to his high level of motivation and dedication Jim recovered from the injury much sooner than most people, but not soon enough to be able to continue his football career. Now 20 years old, Jim was forced to push that dream aside and focus on the life ahead of him. Jim returned home and got a job at a catalog redemption warehouse as a forklift operator and truck unloader. He made decent money and he enjoyed the work he did. His supervisors constantly praised Joey for his work ethic and enthusiasm. Jim continued to stay in shape over the years, working out at the local Gold’s Gym. At the age of 24 he met Kami Palmer, a gorgeous blonde woman who worked as a dental assistant. The attraction was instantaneous and the two dated for well over a year before they finally got married in October of 2000. Life was good for the married couple, as they soon bought a house and a dog, a German Shepherd they named Kima. Life seemed to be going as planned for Jim….but something was missing. That unfulfilled dream he once had. The urge for competition. His motor was still running at full speed, but the tires were spinning. The job at the warehouse was treating him well, and he was making decent money. There was just that empty spot that wasn’t being filled. Jim began training at a local gym in the art of grappling and submission, preparing to test his medal in the arena of bare knuckle fighting. Many of the local bars in town had amateur fight nights where men and women would pit themselves against each other in the “Ultimate Fights”. Feeling he was ready to compete, Jim finally had his first fight, a successful bout in which he forced his opponent to submit to one of his favorite moves, the triangle choke. Several other victories would follow for Jim, and his intense training and determination made him a formidable opponent. One night after a successful fight Jim was approached by an indy promoter about joining his wrestling organization. Jim was hesitant at first. Although he was a wrestling fan he didn’t want to have to uproot his family and change his entire life around for something that wasn’t a sure thing. He declined the man’s offer but it still remained in the back of his head as an option for the future. Jim continued to train and perfect his grappling and submission style, chalking up victory after victory in the local scene. Meanwhile his home life was better than ever. Kami had completed some classes to become a certified dental assistant, which came with a substantial increase in pay. The offer from the indy promoter still crept into Joey’s thoughts, but they were more frequent as the days went by. Finally Jim decided to give the wrestling business a shot. After talking things over with Kami and getting her blessing, Jim began to submit tapes and applications to various wrestling promotions around the nation, one of them being Old School Wrestling. Could he use wrestling to fill the empty space that had been inside of him since his football dream ended? Jim seemed to think so.
