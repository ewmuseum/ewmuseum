PWC TV, 7 June 2003

7 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PWC TV for June 7th LIVE from the Esby Gymnasium, Glassboro, NJ. As PWC TV comes on air, we see that the cramped dressing room of The Revival is almost full. In the centre sits a rickety old cheap white plastic patio table and three matching chairs. In each chair sits either Hoyt Williams, Eric Sagar or Propaganda. Circled around them are nine of their followers, all of them clean cut young men in casual business dress; khakis and a nice white polo shirt. The camera catches a glimpse of Eric Sagar’s very visible black eye which shines like a homing beacon on his flabby, bulldog like face, after his beating from Limerick last week. The bitterness is very apparent in the room, you can almost feel it peeling off the walls and dropping onto your skin. “The Fat Preacher” Eric Sagar and “Your Personal Jesus” Hoyt Williams are in the middle of a conversation. Hoyt Williams: I have a question. Eric sits rubbing his throat. Eric Sagar: I’m a bit thirsty. Could someone get me some coffee? Eric avoids the question to put his needs first. Hoyt Williams: Get me some too. Anyone else want some? A Follower: Yes, please. Mr Williams. Another Follower: I would too, please. Hoyt Williams: Okay, Larry and John get us four coffees. Larry: Okay, Mr. Williams. The two disciples of The Revival walk out of the room. Hoyt Williams: Now, where were we? Eric Sagar: You were about to ask me something. Hoyt Williams: Ah Yes, So is McGlockan back tonight? Eric Sagar: Yes. He should be. Hoyt Williams: I bet he’ll be smug. I hate him! And I hate UnHoly Underground Eric Sagar: Me too, but at least Jesus loves him. God knows why. The preacher growls as he leans over the table. Hoyt Williams: I mean the way Limerick won that is just unacceptable and immoral. Hoyt is condeming the actions of the small Limerick who miraculously, pulled through and beat the odds by completing and winning the Revival gauntlet. Eric Sagar: THAT CHEATING LITTLE HEATHEN SHALT PAY FOR HIS ACTIONS! PAY IN FULL FURY OF THE REVIVAL! WE SHALT TAKE HIM IN OUR GRASPS AND CRUSH HIM! WE NEED TO SWEEP THEM BOTH OUT OF PWC! LIKE THE RED SEA DID TO THE PHARAOH'S TROOPS! DROWNING THEM IN A SEA OF PUNISHMENT! Hoyt Williams: Yes, quite… We need to teach them a lesson! Eric Sagar: But how? Silence fills the room. Both men lean onto the table, Eric Sagar rubbing his grey heavy moustache and Hoyt stroking his beard into a tip. No ideas. Eric Sagar: Where’s my coffee? “The Fat Preacher” barks like an inpatient bulldog wanting his food. Eric Sagar: How long does it take to get some coffee. Who di— Hoyt Williams: I know, how abo— Hoyt pauses. Hoyt Williams: No… No, never mind. Eric Sagar: Go on. Hoyt Williams: No, it wouldn’t work. Eric Sagar: Are you sure? Eric Sagar is chasing up this theory Hoyt had, like a provoked dog barking at a cat up a tree.” Hoyt Williams: Yes, it wouldn’t work. I’m sure of it. Eric Sagar: Well… Eric sighs. Eric Sagar: It looks like we’re going to do it the only way it can be done. Propaganda and Hoyt nod there heads as Eric Sagar continues. Eric Sagar leans into the table, hovering his chest just off the surface, his tie dangling down on the table. He screws his face up, he erects his top lip. He snarls like a bulldog ready to strike. Eric Sagar: We’re going to have to hunt them down and beat them like dogs! Eric Sagar snarls. A few seconds of silence in admiration for Eric’s gritty growl. Eric Sagar sits back comfortably into his very uncomfortable seat. Propaganda: A-Men. All three men stand up, then Hoyt realises. Hoyt Williams: Haven’t the other two got back yet? The remaining seven followers shake their head. Hoyt Williams: Where could they have possibly gone? . . . . . Our shot fades into the interior of the Esby Gymnasium in Glassboro, New Jersey where the small but loyal crowd are their usual vocal selves as they usher in a night of PWC action with cheering. All their favourites are displayed on their signs and t-shirts, with everyone keen to get on camera and encourage their chosen wrestler. Mark: Hello and welcome to Glassboro, New Jersey! Bang: You got the "Hell" bit right, bub. Moving away from the crowd, we are shown the PWC announce duo of Mark Malloy and Bang Davenport, behind them a row of fans pulling all manner of faces for the camera. Mark: We're back where PWC began on February 1st this year, and what a show we have for you tonight! Bang: Yeah, we gots Special Delivery so ya mights as well turn off now! Mark: *Whisper* Stop losing the audience... Bang: *Whisper* Stop bein' bookin' them Pizza guys then... Bangs: And we gots us a new Champ! Mark: We'll see... But a certainty tonight is that Jim Axtell will defend his PWC Title against none other than Angelo Deville! Bang: By defend, ya means lose! Mark: When better than now to start the action packed show?!?! Bang: 2 minutes ago? Mark: ...I don't know why I bother... . . . . . The scene fades backstage, to a locker room we go, the presence of Steven Lee is there. He is lacing up his wrestling boots as his right foot is propped up atop a chair, his left foot on the floor, his boot already laced up. He is ready for his match with Kohana against Special Delivery. He can be heard, talking to himself. Lee: The charades are done for. I'm tired of pretending to be someone I'm not, and in a few minutes everyone will know that it was all an act for me to further my plan. Lee finishes lacing up his right boot, and puts his right foot down onto the floor as he stands up straight. He hops around a few time, working himself up for the match. Lee: Kohana should have known better than to trust me the way he did, and now he will pay the price for his actions. The outcome of this would have been the same, he just chose the path less traveled, and he will learn from his mistakes. Lee pops his neck, moving it back and fourth, left to right. He paces back and fourth now, looking at his door occasionally as he thinks to himself. A knock is heard. Lee stops, and looks at the door. He smiles. The knock comes again. Steven moves a few steps closer to the door. The knock gets louder now. Lee: Hold your damn horses! Lee finally approaches the door, and gets ready to open it as it comes flying open. Kohana standing on the other side with an obvious look of disgust on his face. Kohana: What took you so long? Plotting something against me? Lee's smile fades as he seems to begin to wonder if Kohana overheard him or not. Lee shakes his head as he slams the door behind Kohana as he enters the locker room. Lee: Why would I do such a thing like that? I have no reason to be like that, and if I did, why would I have saved your job for you? I would have just let you lose, and then I would have been done with you, but no, I saved it in order to keep you around. Why? Because I... Lee chokes on himself some, and fakes a hiccup as not to look suspicious. Lee: Because I like you. Kohana looks strangely at Steven as he turns his back and walks away. Kohana walks towards Lee. Kohana: What is your problem? Getting choked up over the fact that you want to be friends with someone like me? I understand that I am a likable guy, but damn, you don't need to be crying or anything. Lee, still with his back turned, has no choice but to smile, but omits the laughter he'd like to let go of. Steven then does his best to wipe the smile off of his face as he turns around to look Kohana in the face. Lee: I'm sorry, it's just a very emotional subject to talk about. First I saved your job, then we become friends, start hanging out and all, and now we are getting ready to wrestle together as a team. I promised myself I wouldn't cry. Lee smiles, Kohana disgusted with the sarcasm he knows of. Kohana: That was a bit uncalled for. Steven shakes his head. Lee: Yeah, and so was your lame speech a minute ago. I hiccuped, big deal, not like I'm crying or anything. Can we stop talking about it now though? We got better things to think about at the moment. Like our match. Kohana nods his head. Kohana: The resident pizza boys. Why do we even need to bother with them anyway? Lee: Gotta start somewhere don't we? It's our first tag match, why not get a W in our first one, rather than shooting big and end up losing? Kohana nods in agreement as Lee turns towards the door. Kohana: So what's our strategy? Lee smiles to himself, keeping his back to Kohana. Lee: Communication. Keeping each other fresh with frequent tags. Anything we can do to keep ourselves from losing to these punks should get us a win. Kohana: Kinda vague, but I guess it'll work. Lee: I'm sure I know the outcome of the match already. I don't... I mean, we don't have anything to worry about. You can trust me. Kohana shakes his head as Lee turns back around to look at him face to face. Kohana: I guess it's been a couple weeks being around you. I can trust you, but don't prove me wrong! Lee turns back around again and heads towards the door, Kohana has a cheesy looking grin on his face, obviously not trusting Lee as much as he says he is. Lee: Oh I won't prove you wrong... Trust me! Lee flings the door wide open, and it bangs off the wall as it's opened, Kohana follows right behind Lee as they head to the ring for their match. . . . . .
