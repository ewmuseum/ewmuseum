Ashley Winters Biography Nicknames The Tragedienne Hometown Baltimore, Maryland Birthday September 30, 1982 Height 5'7' Weight 149 Biography Ashley Winters was born into a model family, in more ways than one. Her mother was a model. Her father was a model. Her two sisters were models. And, from the time that Mrs. Winters felt that first kick, plans were made for their new little girl to enter the business as well. Unfortunately, things did not turn out as planned. Oh, Ashley was beautiful alright, but not beautiful in the stick-thin sense that characterized the supermodel industry. Ashley could not have known the dissapointment that overtook her parents then. Their lives had been in the modeling industry. To have a member of their family deemed not good enough, to them, seemed their own failure…and their daughter’s. Simply put, they snapped. They forced the poor girl to wear a mask at home, so they would not be “scarred” by her “ugliness”. This message at home was greatly contrasted by the one she got in public and at school, when she took the mask off. Throughout middle and high school, Ashley was loved by her peers. She excelled in school, collecting a 4.4 GPA and valedictorian honors, was the class president, and was pursued by all of the boys in the school. And then, when she got home, she was both mentally and physically abused by her parents. The contrasting opinions drove the girl to the depths of mental disorder. She craved acceptance in any way she could get it. Her parents shipped her off to college at the University of Maryland, and then were promptly arrested. Suspecting something wrong at home, her closest HS friends had gone to the police. Mr. and Mrs. Winters are still serving time to this day for assault, battery, and child abuse. In college, Ashley began looking around for companionship. However, as the school was only a stone’s throw from her hometown, by now, everyone knew the story of the girl in the mask. She was shunned, ostracized from virtually all groups, until she met a few caring souls in the theater department, and fell in love with acting. Her roles began to take her mind off of her past. She joined the club gymnastic teams, and soon had twin loves: gymnastics and acting. As she hung out with the acting troupe more, she gained a third, in the form of professional wrestling. One day, on a Caliban whim, she sat down to watch with her friends, and was hooked. She became so obsessed that she went to a nearby wrestling school, and pleaded with the trainer to train her. Despite her slight build, he was impressed with her athleticism, and agreed. Now, the Tragedienne has arrived in the Pro Wrestling Circuit. Will her need for acceptance drive her to the top, or be the thing that cripples her chances at success? Wrestling Style Ashley uses an aerial and submission based game, due to her size. She peppers her wrestling moves with gymnastic maneuvers, designed to fluster opponents, show off to the fans, and add power. Other Moves Signature Moves: Fair is Foul: Ashley hooks her opponent, and delivers a vertical suplex. She holds on, rolls backwards, pulls her opponent and herself to her feet, and drives him/her down with a DDT. Foul is Fair: The exact opposite of Fair is Foul: A vertical suplex chained into a DDT. To Be Or Not To Be: Winters, using her gymnastic acumen, executes a full split, then punches her opponent as hard as she can in the groin. Whirligig of Time: Ashley executes a picture-perfect cartwheel, kicking her opponent in the face as she does so. Much Ado About Nothing: A real crowd pleaser. Winters grabs her opponent in a wristlock, twists the arm, and begins a ropewalk, walking from corner to corner. She leaps, feigning a strike,then lands, and kisses her opponent, then slides between his legs holding the twisted arm, using it as leverage to pull the opponent over in a bridge. Alternate Setup Moves: Setup Move for Female Competitors: The Taming of the Shrew Ashley sits her opponent on the top rope facing the middle of the ring, and steps outside to the apron. She then springboards into the ring, and takes her opponent off the turnbuckle with an Ace Crusher, then climbs back up top, ready to perform A Midsummer Night’s Dream. Setup Move for Heavier Opponents: The Comedy of Errors Designed to embarrass and fluster her heavier, larger opponents. Ashley bounces off the ropes, and baseball slides between the opponent’s legs, catching him with a low blow on the way through. She then pops up behind, and rolls over his back into a DDT, from where she goes up top to execute A Midsummer Night’s Dream, only with her opponent in a standing position after he rises. Alternate Finisher: All's Well That Ends Well Ashley’s second finisher, this move can be pulled off from anywhere, rather than requiring the setup that A Midsummer Night’s Dream needs.
