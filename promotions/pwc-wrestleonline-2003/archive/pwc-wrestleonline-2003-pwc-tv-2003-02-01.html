PWC TV for Saturday, February 1st, 2003 Live from the Esby Gymnasium, in Glassboro, NJ The scene opens to the view of a camera moving through a dark, cinderblock-walled hallway, as the slight chant of a crowd can be heard echoing throughout the blackness. Chanters: P-dubya-C!!! P-dubya-C!!! P-dubya-C!!! P-dubya-C!!! P-dubya-C!!! The camera winds around a corner of the hallway, continuing forward, the chanting growing louder and more feverish all the while. Chanters: P-dubya-C!!! P-dubya-C!!! P-dubya-C!!! P-dubya-C!!! P-dubya-C!!! Suddenly, the sound of a deep bell being rung is heard as the collective voice of the crowd suddenly erupts into a huge burst of excitement. Another bell tolls, as the camera continues to surge forward. The sound of a guitar begins to roll along with the bells, the chords unmistakably being those of AC/DC’s “Hell’s Bells,” which only serves to cause the crowd to pop even louder. Turning one last corner, the camera approaches a set of double doors, which have bright light pouring from the jams at which they meet, as well as from beneath them. Just as the first crescendo of the song hits, the camera pushes its way through the doors to reveal a packed but small gymnasium full of exceptionally loud fans, who go ballistic at the site of the cameraman signalling the beginning of the show. The scene shifts and begins taking samples of the crowd, who are going absolutely wild and singing to the music. Among the samples shown are shots of over-stimulated frat boys in the front row behind the steel barricade near the ringside area, a group of hot and likely slutty college chicks there to see men with bulging muscles and form fitting tights, and finally a group of 30 year old “wrestling” purists, who certainly are showing “restrained skepticism” being at the first ever show of the new promotion. Finally, after several more rapid fire shots of the crowd, the camera settles on the ringside announce position of the PWC commentator team. Mark Malloy: HELLO EVERYONE AND WELCOME TO THE DEBUT TELECAST OF PRO WRESTLING CIRCUIT TELEVISION!!! I AM MARK MALLOY AND I AM JOINED HERE AT RINGSIDE BY THE LEGENDARY BANG DAVENPORT!!! WE ARE COMING TO YOU LIVE FROM ROWAN UNIVERSITY’S ESBY GYMNASIUM IN GLASBORO, NEW JERSEY, EXCLUSIVELY ON WGTW TV-48! AND FOLKS, TONIGHT NOT ONLY ARE YOU WITNESSING THE DEBUT OF PWC HISTORY, BUT I HAVE A FEELING TONIGHT COULD VERY WELL BE A BENCHMARK IN THE HISTORY OF SPORTS ENTERTAINMENT!!! Bang: Dat it will be too!!! I got a feelin’ PWC is gonna spin the wrestlin’ world around more than a coupla second-cousin’ playing spin the shine jug!!! Mark: (casting a bizarre eye at Bang) We have an action packed show on tap for you tonight, and from what we are told, we will find out the identity of the owner -- or owners -- of the Pro Wrestling Circuit!!! Bang: Dat is good an' all, but really, no one really cares about the farmer… we only be carin' 'bout the corn he grows!!! Mark: What the heck is that supposed to mean?!? Bang: If da in-ring scrapin’ ain’t good, it don’t make no nevermind who the owner is!!! You dun got it now dere college boy?!? Mark: (sarcastically) Yes, now I do. Now. I mean, how foolish of me not to have understood that crystal clear metaphor. (changing gears and tones) On that note, we are about to head to the ring for the first ever match in PWC history!!! Bang: An’ it’s darn tootin’ ta be a barn burner! Mark: It most certainly is. What better way to kick off the PWC legacy than to have a triple threat match with three of the hottest rising stars in wrestling today!?! Bang: Ya got that right, Mark! These three young stallions are rip-roarin’ and rarin’ ta go! Mark: Let’s not delay any longer then, and send it up to Bob Kirby for the introductions! The crowd is buzzing with anticipation as the ring announcer climbs into the squared circle. As Kirby steps to the center of the ring the crowd begins to roar in anticipation. Bob: Ladies and gentlemen….it’s now time for the FIRST EVER MATCH IN PWC HISTORY!!! The audience erupts into a gigantic, wrestling-thirsty pop. Soon thereafter “Hard Workin’ Man” by Brooks and Dunn hits over the speakers, bringing more cheers from the crowd. After a few seconds Porky Jack Bunson comes walking into the gymnasium from behind the curtain. He begins his journey to the ring, lumbering down the wooden ramp after passing between the two stacks of televisions on either side of the passageway that serve as a poorman’s “tron.” The video screens play clips of Porky Bunson in action. Bob: Making his way to the ring, from Chicken Lick, Kentucky… He weighs in at 271 pounds……”HARD LUCK” PORKY BUNSON!!! The crowd of 1300 or so people explodes for the popular Porky Bunson. Mark: Porky Bunson is a big part of the future of the Professional Wrestling Circuit!!! Regarded as one of the greatest wrestlers to come out of the Primetime Wrestling Academy, Bunson is here to prove that he has what it takes!!! Bang: Hot diggity! This feller be from ‘round my neck of the foothills!!! Bunson makes his way to the ring, slapping hands with the rabid fans at ringside as he does so. He climbs into the ring and does a little jig as his music plays. Finally his music fades and gives way to “More Hate Than Fear” by One King Down. The fans continue to cheer as Jimmy Nguyen appears from behind the curtain and walks toward the ring. Bob: And his opponent…..from Philadelphia, Pennsylvania and weighing 182 pounds…..JIMMY NGUYEN!!! The fans pop loudly, not only for the popular Jimmy Nguyen, but the fact he is from Philadelphia which is only 10 minutes away from the site of tonight's contract. Mark: Here is another great talent to come out of the PWA!!! Jimmy and Porky were the top two students in the recent crop of talent and are well regarded as future megastars!!! Bang: There sure be a buzz 'round these two, and I don’t reckon it has to do with poo on the bottom of their boots!!! Mark gasps. Nguyen gets to the ring and climbs through the ropes, the crowd cheering him loudly. He briefly locks stares with the much bigger Porky before playing to the crowd. Mark: These two men had a friendly rivalry down in PWA, as both of them were extremely popular and well regarded. It will be fun to see that competition continue here in PWC!!! Porky and Jimmy stand in opposing corners, both looking toward the entrance to the gymnasium. “Assfault” by DryKillLogic hits over the speakers and the crowd erupts into a charged chorus of boos. Bob: And their opponent…..hailing from Portland, Oregon and weighing 195 pounds… He is “THE MODEL CITIZEN” CHANDLER TSONDA!! Tsonda appears from behind the curtain and looks out at the crowd with a look of disgust. He turns his nose up as he makes his way to the ring. Mark: One more of the talented wrestlers that will carry the torch for PWC, although there isn’t much known about this one. We certainly have our share of blue chippers, don’t we Bang? Bang: I don’t know ‘bout no blue chippers, Mark, but I DO know ‘bout some cow chippers. Mark: Ugh… Bang: Is it just me, or does this Chandler feller be kinda sweet on himself? Chandler wrinkles his nose as some of the fans reach out to touch him. He quickly makes it to the ring and slides in under the bottom rope, brushing his arms and chest off disgustedly. Mark: Certainly seems that way! The referee comes around and checks all three men for illegal weapons and then calls for the bell. Mark: Young referee Bobby Howard will be in charge of the action for this inaugural match. He’s an aspiring wrestler himself, but he just missed the cut here. Bang: Sure don’t look like he’s got much to him. I kinda think a stiff breeze could knock the poor kid over. The bell sounds, causing the crowd to climb to a fevered pitch. They know they are witnessing history unfolding before their very eyes. In the ring, Porky and Jimmy begin to circle around backwards, sizing each other up. Chandler stands in a corner jawing with some of the fans at ringside. Nguyen begins to dance back and forth in a rhythmic fashion as Porky wiggles his fingers, gearing up for action. Bunson moves in for a tie-up, but Nguyen deftly wriggles out of the way and connects with an elbow to the back of Porky’s head. Bunson lurches forward and then spins around, meeting the bottom of Nguyen’s boot courtesy of a martial arts kick to the face. Mark: Nguyen is well versed in several styles of martial arts, Bang. Bang: Well I’ll be my sister’s uncle, that there Japan-man is isn’t he? Mark: (sighing) It’s gonna be a long night. Porky clutches his head and shakes it a few times, cleaning out the cobwebs. Nguyen continues to move about in a dancing motion, hands ready in a defensive position. Bunson collects himself and begins to circle the ring again. He passes by Tsonda and bumps into him, which Chandler responds to by shoving Porky in the back. The crowd boos as Bunson whirls around raises a fist to deck Tsonda, only to have the former model duck through the ropes and cover his face. Mark: Chandler Tsonda doesn’t look all to eager to get into the action. Bang: Reminds me of this yeller striped skunk we had down home. Every time we wanted to kill that critter fer lunch it would run and hide in the outhouse. Back in the ring, Porky sighs and turns his attention back to Jimmy. The two square off once again, with Porky moving in for a tie-up. As was the case before, Jimmy dances around Porky and throws an elbow at the back of his head. This time, however, Porky ducks the attempted elbow and sees Jimmy stagger backward and end up right in front of him. Bunson scoops Jimmy up off the mat and slams him down hard. Bang: Can’t fool a country boy twice!!! Porky wastes no time and drops a leaping elbow on Jimmy that connects across his chest and then climbs to his feet. Porky pulls Jimmy up and whips him into the ropes. The 267 pound Porky Bunson flies through the air as Nguyen bounces off, landing with a huge flying shoulder block that sends Nguyen down to the mat again. The crowd goes wild for the big move. Mark: What a maneuver by Bunson!! He’s a pretty big man to be pulling something like that off. Bang: Yeah, I reckon he could tie a plow around his neck and handle the back forty by his lonesome. Bunson quickly scrambles over to Jimmy and makes a cover. One… Two… Th-Kickout by Nguyen! Mark: What in the hell is Chandler Tsonda even doing out here if he’s not going to wrestle?! Looking at Tsonda, we find him on the outside of the ring in a face to face encounter with a female fan at ringside. He runs his hands along his body in an effort to impress the attractive young brunette. The woman cracks a smile as the rest of the fans boo Tsonda unmercifully. Tsonda snarls at the other fans and snorts as he rolls back into the ring. Mark: This guy is so wrapped up in himself that I don’t even know if he remembers that there is a match even taking place. In the ring Bunson and Nguyen are standing toe to toe, trading blows. Mark: I don’t know if Jimmy Nguyen wants to continue to trade shot for shot with the much stronger Bunson much longer! Porky connects with a solid right hand, which Jimmy counters with a vicious knife edge chop. They exchange evenly for a while before Jimmy ducks underneath a Bunson fist and connects with a spinning heel kick to Porky’s jaw. He staggers back and walks right into a sleeper hold from Chandler Tsonda. Mark: Well, well….Chandler Tsonda has finally decided to join this match. And at an opportune time. Bang: He dun picked his spot but good! Chickenshit, but smart. Mark: I don’t think you can say “chickenshit” on Channel 48. Bang: I didn't hear no beepity-beep… Did you? Back in the ring, Bunson flails his arms and tries to escape from the sleeper hold of Tsonda, but Jimmy Nguyen joins in on the attack with martial arts kicks to Porky’s midsection and legs. Mark: A rather smart strategy by these two smaller wrestlers, taking out the much bigger Porky Bunson. Porky reaches back with both hands and grabs the head of Tsonda, ripping him up and over from behind him and tossing Chandler to the mat. The crowd pops. Mark: What a show of stength!!! Bang: He dun be cornfed I do declare!!! Porky charges forward and nails Jimmy with a huge lariat, causing Nguyen to do a back flip before landing face first on the mat. Both Nguyen and Tsonda, however, spring to their feet and charge at Bunson. They back him into a corner and begin to pound on him with right and left hand shots. Mark: These two cruiserweights are going to have to work as a team in order to wear down this good ‘ol boy Porky Bunson!! Bang: When you rassle cows and bulls yer whole childhood ya tend to get strong as ‘em. That’s whatcher seein' from Porky Bunson right now! Porky continues to get rocked in the corner by Chandler and Jimmy. The two men then double Irish whip Bunson to the opposite corner. Porky crashes into the turnbuckles and his head snaps back. Tsonda and Nguyen exchange quick glances before Nguyen runs over and gets on all fours in front of Bunson. Tsonda runs and springs off of Nguyen’s back, nailing Porky with a flying side heel kick to the chops. Mark: Poetry in Motion maneuver from Chandler Tsonda with an assist from Jimmy Nguyen!!! How the heck did they pull that out of their hip pocket?!?! Porky staggers forward and is taken down with a drop toe hold by Nguyen. He hits face first as Tsonda hits the ropes and comes off with a Rolling Thunder somersault that he finishes off with a leaping Swanton to Bunson’s back. The crowd boos as Tsonda cockily gets to his feet. Meanwhile Jimmy kneels down and barks some verbal punkage into the ear of Porky Bunson. Mark: Ummm…..I have a sneaky suspicion that something fishy is going on here!!! Bang: Heh, that could be mah breeches, Mark. I be wadin' in da creek out yonder a'fore da show. Nguyen gets to his feet and exchanges another look with Tsonda, then both men pull Bunson to his feet. Nguyen spins around and nails Porky in the gut with a back kick, then shoots himself off the ropes. As Bunson is doubled over Jimmy jumps and comes down with a well placed heel to the base of Porky’s skull. Mark: A NEW KIND OF FREEDOM from Jimmy Nguyen to Porky Bunson!! Immediately after Porky hits the mat, Chandler runs over to the corner and executes a split-legged moonsault on Bunson. Mark: THE MODEL CITIZEN!! Bang: I dun think this Chandler feller is sorta queer lookin'. I better keep an eye on my flock o' sheep. Mark: These two look like they’ve been working together! They are communicating with eye contact alone!!!! Bang: Maybe they dun be makin' one anotha squeel like pigs in the barn. Jimmy and Chandler begin to put the boots to Porky, stomping away with fervor as the crowd erupts into a chorus of boos. Nguyen pauses momentarily to hock a huge wad of spit on Porky’s back, sneering in contempt. He then nods to Chandler and the two of them kick Bunson to the outside. Mark: This is terrible!! Jimmy Nguyen and Chandler Tsonda have formed some sort of partnership and are beating the hell out of Porky Bunson!! This has premeditation written all over it!! Bang: I know what ya mean, Malloy. Them Mediterranean’s are sumbitches!!! Porky writhes in pain on the gymnasium floor as Chandler walks over and grabs a steel chair from ringside. He folds it up and raises it high over his head. Referee Bobby Howard admonishes Tsonda but to no avail, as Chandler brings the chair crashing down hard across the back of Porky Bunson. The referee calls for the bell. Winner by Disqualification: Porky Bunson Mark: What in the hell is going on here?! The first match in PWC history just ended on a disqualification!! Bang: Guess they didn’t know this be a HAAAARRDCOOOOORE match-up. Bob Kirby: (from ringside) Here is your winner, as a result of a disqualification, PORKY BUNSON!! The crowd cheers for a second before lavishing the gym with more intense boos. As Chandler waffles Porky with the chair again we spot Jimmy over on the side of the ring setting up a table. He makes his way over to where Chandler and Porky are and helps Bunson climb to his feet. They drag Porky over to the table and roll him on top of it. Jimmy gets up and stands on the table, then carefully pulls Porky up, cinching him in a front face lock. Mark: Oh God no!! Bang: I get the itchin’ that we’s gonna see some wood a-shatterin’!!! Screams come from the crowd as Jimmy’s face contorts into a mask of rage. He snarls as he drives Porky’s head down through the table and to the floor with a DDT!! Wood splinters fly everywhere as Bunson and Nguyen lay amidst the rubble. Mark: GOOD GOD!! JIMMY NGUYEN JUST DDT’D PORKY BUNSON THROUGH THE TABLE TO THE FLOOR!! Bang: Why in tarnation would he be doin' that, Malloy? Mark: It’s obvious that he’s jealous of Porky Bunson!!! He wants to prove that he was the top student in PWA, not Porky!!! Too bad for him he just made himself look like an asshole!!! We move in closer to the shambles that remain of the table. Porky lays there unmoving, blood pouring from his forehead. Mark: PORKY IS BUSTED OPEN!!! THIS IS AN OUTRAGE!!! Chandler celebrates as he pulls Jimmy to his feet. The two look down at their handy work and grin with satisfaction. The fans at ringside hurl debris and expletives at the pair as they continue to boo and jeer the two men. Nguyen and Tsonda scoff at the fans and then head toward the back. Mark: Ladies and gentlemen, Porky Bunson wins the first ever match in PWC history although it turned out to be a tainted victory as he was brutally attacked by Jimmy Nguyen and Chandler Tsonda!!! Those two have apparently formed some sort of working relationship before tonight and launched a vile plan to take out Porky Bunson!!! Bang: Them two boys sure ‘nuff dun a number on my good ‘ol boy, Porky!! Mark: We need to get some medical attention out here for Porky Bunson!!! We will be right back after these messages!!! The cameras settle in on the broken and bloodied Porky Bunson, as the scene fades to commercial. . . . . . . Commercial: Tonight, after PWC TV… Are you worried that your significant other is not being faithful? CHEATERS can help! CHEATERS helps you exercise your right to know! You can catch CHEATERS tonight at midnight, right after PWC TV, right here on WGTW TV-48! . . . . . . The scene fades in from commercial to the parking area outside the Esby arena as a Jeep Renegade pulls up in front of a pair of sealed gates. A security guard, wearing a black jacket with three initials … PWC … in white lettering on the left hand side above the heart, glances at the vehicle, trying to see who is inside. The guard doesn’t look all that physically imposing, in fact on a closer look it is obvious that he is probably not long out of High School … if in fact he is yet … and that this is probably just a part time job. Clutching a clipboard to his chest, the guard approaches the vehicle, walking around to the driver’s side where the window is starting to come down. The guard speaks, his voice cracking slightly, his nerves evident. Guard: Can I take your name and ask what you are doing here tonight? The unseen figure answers from inside the vehicle, his voice betraying the fact that he is male, a soft accent … unidentifiable … yet strongly evident. Voice: What I am doing here tonight is probably the same as quite a few guys, and that is to try to get in to see the man who is running the show and-- The security guard interrupts, a hint of arrogance appearing as a grin appears on his face. Guard: Well, unless you are on my list I am afraid that you aren’t seeing anyone tonight, especially not as the show is underway!” The headlights of the car go out, as the driver’s door opens, and a pair of feet are seen stepping out of the vehicle to clack hollowly on the pavement beneath. As he stands up and closes the door behind him, the figure is still slightly blocked by the Jeep, though the angle of the guard's face shows that the driver is taller than he is, though he only stands at about five foot eight or nine. The unseen driver, a hint of amusement audible in his voice, asks a question of the guard. Voice: …And do you know everyone on that list then, kid? Guard: Erm… No, I suppose that I don’t, but I do recognize YOU and I know that you are NOT on the list, so I am afraid that I am going to have to ask you to turn around and leave, as I have very clear instructions on who to let in and who not to. The unseen driver reaches out and takes the clipboard from the guard, turning it around and seemingly reading it before speaking again. Voice: You are right, kid; my name isn’t on this list, but how about you get on the radio that I see attached to your belt and ask someone inside if I could go in and have a chat with your boss? The guard looks slightly unsure about this, as he was obviously hoping that his job would simply entail letting the right people in and the wrong people turning around at his request, but his hesitance is solved when the unseen driver reaches out, unclips the radio, and hands it to him. Taking the radio, the guard speaks into it. Guard: Erm, Mr McGriff, I have someone here who wants to see you … shall I let him in? After a few seconds the radio emits some static before an irate sounding voice comes over the ether, the voice of PWC Security chief, Jack McGriff being heard from inside the building. The crowd cheers at the sound of the voice of the former PCW Tag Team champion. McGriff: Listen pal, we are pretty busy in here you know, so I REALLY don’t have time for visitors right now… If he isn’t on the list then get rid of him, capice?! Before the guard can answer, the unseen driver reaches out and takes the radio from his hand, speaking into it himself, a small laugh in his voice. Voice: McGriff, I think that your tin badge out here got the wrong end of the stick... I am not here to see you, though catching up could be fun. When I said I wanted to see the boss, I was talking about ‘The Boss’, so what do you say -- can I come in? McGriff: Hey, who is this? As the figure steps around the front of the Jeep, coming into full view as he stares over at the building to which he is trying to gain entrance, he smiles, now standing revealed. Voice: It is Phoenix, Jack… Jay Phoenix The crowd from within the arena can be heard in the distance, exploding into a loud pop for the former UWF and ProWC superstar, and perhaps most impressively, runner up in the first ever Global Title Tourney. The radio goes quiet for a few seconds, then squawks back to life. McGriff: OK Phoenix, come on in. Phoenix throws the radio back to the guard with a grin as he climbs back into the Jeep and revs it up as the kid scrambles to open the gates before Phoenix drives through them. . . . . . . Mark: Welcome back to PWC TV!!! Holy crow, Bang, that was Jay Phoenix!!! Bang: I remembers me a couple of Phoenixes, but they wasn’t that there fella. There was the one in Asylum with me. Trashy and Mee-er-gow-er…(the crowd pops at the mention of the great PCW stable) and that other guy who wore that there towel around… Mark: That was Fenix… Either way, this guy dwarfs both of those men!!! The question is, what is a superstar the likes of Jay Phoenix doing here?!?! Bang: I reckon he be here to… Suddenly Bang is cut off by the sounds of “Quiet” from the Smashing Pumpkins hitting the arena’s PA system, instantly bringing the crowd of 1300 people surging to its feet. Bang: What in tarnation?!?! Mark: THAT’S THE PHANTOM BOOKER’S MUSIC!!!! IS HE THE OWNER OF PWC?!?! Bang: I DUN THINK WE GONNA FIND OUT IN TWO HOPS OF A FLEA ON A HAIRLESS DOG!!! Mark: (rolling his eyes) Whatever the hell THAT means… The cameras focus on the entryway in anticipation, as the televisions stacked to each side of the threshold come to life, with images of the mysterious, stocking mask wearing, man who built Primetime Championship Wrestling and Old School Wrestling appearing on the screens. The crowd’s anticipation whips into a fevered pitch. Mark: THOSE ARE IMAGES OF THE BOOKER!! Suddenly, the crowd explodes into an even louder roar of approval. Bang: LOOKY DERE!!! The camera focuses in on the darkness of the entryway, as a man…THE man, steps out of the darkness and out onto the makeshift ramp way, putting all debates about the ownership situation in PWC to rest. Mark: THAT’S HIM!!! THAT IS THE PHANTOM BOOKER!!! Bang: HOWSA CAN WE BES SURE ITS HIM?!?! I WON’T BELIEVE IT UNTIL BESSY THE BLOODHOUND PICKS UP HIS SCENT!! Mark: I DON’T NEED A DOG TO TELL ME THAT IS THE MAN!!! THE PHANTOM BOOKER IS HERE IN PWC!!! On the rampway, the Booker, wearing his trademark stocking cap over his face to hide his identity, a faded, black, Primetime Indy Circuit T-shirt, and a pair of faded blue jeans stands, looking out over the crowd, which down to the man, is on their feet and roaring in approval. After nodding his head to acknowledge the crowd, he suddenly sprints into the ring, diving beneath the bottom rope, sliding on his stomach to nearly the middle of the stained canvas. Quickly rolling to his back, he does a kip up, bounding to his feet, much to the delight of the crowd. He then raises his arms high to the sky and spinning to face each corner of the arena!! The crowd is eating it up. Mark: LISTEN TO THAT OVATION!! WHO KNEW THAT 1300 PEOPLE COULD MAKE THIS MUCH NOISE!!! Bang: YA AIN’T NEVER BEEN TO THE NAKED HOG SPRINTS IN COLDWATER, HAS YA? Mark: Thankfully, no… The Booker climbs the turnbuckles in one corner of the ring and raises his arms up high. Flashbulbs sparkle throughout the dingy, antiquated gymnasium catching the moment for all of eternity. Hopping down from the turnbuckle, Booker steps into the center of the ring and makes the cut motion across his neck, as if to tell the production workers to cut his music, which they do immediately. This only causes the crowd to cheer louder, out of respect for the man who has been so important to the industry in recent memory. Mark: It’s only fitting that a man like the Phantom Booker is right back here in New Jersey, in front of a small, knowledgeable crowd, who knows and appreciates his work… Bang: Since when’s Jersey folk considered know-ledge-Able? Back in the ring, the Booker simply stands there soaking in the admiration from the crowd. He slowly nods his head in acknowledgement as he pulls a microphone out from his back pocket. He begins to raise it to speak, but the crowd bursts into a chant. The Crowd: T-P-B!!! T-P-B!!! T-P-B!!! T-P-B!!! Mark: This is almost surreal!!! The Booker continues to nod in appreciation for the ovation from the crowd. Again he tries to raise his microphone to speak, but before he gets a word out, the chant once again gets louder. Mark: Would you listen to that crowd!!! The Booker holds his free hand up in front of him with his palm facing outwards, motioning for the crowd to quiet down. Meanwhile, he raises the microphone towards his mouth. TPB: (choked up) Thank you… The chant breaks into a single roar from the crowd, which causes the Booker to put his free hand on his heart, as if to signal to the crowd that they are “too kind.” Nodding his head in appreciation once more, he again raises the microphone to speak. TPB: Thank you….You have NO idea how much this means to me. Mark: (sounding a bit choked up himself) And YOU, Booker, have no idea how much you have meant to all of us! TPB: I have to say, I stand before you a man humbled by not only your applause, but by circumstance as well… Mark: Circumstance? The Booker nods his head, as some members of the crowd seem a bit confused. TPB: Yes, that’s right, I stand before you, in this glorified bingo hall, at a third rate, state funded school, upon this stained and torn canvas, between these threadbare ropes, the owner of the Pro Wrestling Circuit. They are not pleased by the Booker’s less than sterling description of their school and gymnasium. The Booker paces around the ring a bit like a caged animal. A noticeable tension builds in his voice, as he continues. TPB: Throughout my time in this industy, I have done nothing but build. I have built not one, but TWO multi-million dollar sports entertainment franchises… Bang: I once built me a trough for pigs… Mark: SHHHHHH!!! TPB: (continuing) I have built hungry young talent, without a pot to piss into, into multimillion dollar celebrities, driving fancy cars, starring in movies, and living in mansions in Beverly Hills. And what do I have to show for it? The Booker looks out to the crowd for an answer, but they only stare back in bewilderment. TPB: …Not a damn thing. I allowed my generosity and desire to allow all to profit from my hard work cloud my judgment, and in the end, jackals like Tyler Nelson and Jane Reagan took what I built and twisted its vision, while the superstars I made turned their backs on me. Well, let me assure each and every person in this arena and all the fans at home, that I will NEVER let something like that happen again!!! The crowd seems divided between people who are confused by what the Booker is talking about, and those who are shocked by his matter of fact statements. He just saved O’Neil’s Road Warrior Hawk lookin’ ass. Mark: And Vendetta isn’t happy about that at all! Angered, Vendetta eyes O'Neil sprawled out on the canvas and pulls him to his feet. Instead of hitting another move, however, he elects to toss him into the corner instead, in a prime position to make a tag to Deville. Seeing this, Toomes smacks himself in the head, then throws his hands in the air, screaming angrily at Vendetta. Clay ignores his screaming partner, instead beckoning Angelo to step into the ring. Bang: What’s the moron doing?! Mark: It looks like he wants a piece of Deville so bad, he’s letting him tag in!!! Bang: That idiot! He had O’Neil beaten! He’s throwing the match away! Smiling deviously back at Vendetta, Deville slaps O'Neil's shoulder and steps through the ropes. Charging at Clay, Angelo catches him with a few right hands, rocking him back...until he is caught by the arm and pulled into a head-and-arm suplex that lands him on the side of his neck. Vendetta crouches down, taunting Deville now as he waits for him to climb to his feet. Mark: Vendetta just caught Deville hard with that suplex, landing him directly on the back of his head! Angelo staggers to his feet, taking another lunging swing at Vendetta. Clay ducks the blow and goes around the back, catching Deville in a release German suplex that gets full rotation, landing Angelo on his stomach. Mark: Another amazing suplex from Vendetta! Bang: Dang it! How does he do that? Climbing on top of Deville's back, Vendetta begins driving punches into the back of his head. He doesn't realize, however, that Angelo is slowly slouching his way back until Clay is standing directly above his neck. Then, quickly grabbing the back of Vendetta's legs, Deville stands up. Holding his opponent in electric chair position, Angelo swiftly drops him neck first across the top rope. Mark: And Vendetta gets caught across the rope!! Bang: Yeah, let’s see him suplex his way out of dem apples!! Vendetta bounces back, holding his throat in pain. Grabbing him from behind, Deville positions him in a pumphandle. Using it to pull Clay off the ground, Angelo shifts his body and drives him into the mat with a piledriver. Bang: Deville is smart as a coon, and crafty as a fox!!! Mark: It was a beautiful move, without a doubt, but must you endlessly shill the guy? Bang: Duh. Of course. Pulling Vendetta up, Deville traps him in an Argentine backbreaker, holding him there for a few seconds. Then, with a fluid motion, he drops Clay out of the hold right into a sit-out DDT. Rolling Vendetta over, Angelo makes the pinfall, hooking the leg. Bang: And another great move! This one could be it! Mark: Vendetta will be hard pressed to kick out of that one! One... Two... Th...No! Mark: But he does! Vendetta kicks out! These guys are laying it all on the line!!! Vendetta is again hoisted to his feet, as Angelo presses him over his head, only to drop him straight down into a jawbreaker. Clay recoils backward, holding his mouth, and Deville whips him to the ropes. He doesn't catch Sebastian Toomes running out to the center of the ring apron and slapping Vendetta on the shoulder for the tag as he hits the ropes, though, but the referee sure does. Catching Clay off the ropes with a spinning backdrop driver, Deville continues on with the match, not knowing that Toomes is now the legal man. Mark: Vendetta just got a blind tag to Toomes! Bang: And shoot, Deville don't even know it! Hoisting Clay up again, Angelo whips him across the ring, but as soon as Vendetta hits them he grabs the top strand and uses it to stop his momentum. Seeing his opponent slide underneath the bottom rope to the outside, Deville follows after him, oblivious of Toomes approaching him from behind. Rushing in with a waistlock, Switchblade runs Angelo into the ropes, before pulling him back with a roll-up pin. Bang: Aw…it would be a shame for it to end like this! One... Two... Th...No! Bang: Dat was as close as first cousins in courtin’ month!! Deville kicks out, staggering to his feet still in shock from the unexpected roll-up. Toomes manages to capitalize on his opponent's surprise by quickly delivering a boot to the midsection and cutting him down with a double arm DDT. Rolling Angelo over, he hooks the leg for another pin. Mark: And Toomes immediately takes advantage of Deville’s stunned state! DDT into a pin! One... Two... Thr...No! Mark: That was a millisecond away from being three! Pulling him up to his feet, Toomes quickly shoves Angelo's head underneath his arm. Raising his other arm in the air, he directs a shout toward the fans at ringside before hooking Deville's other arm behind his head. Lifting him up, Sebastian attempts to swing Angelo around to deliver the Future Shock, but Deville manages to block the attempt by intertwining his leg with Toomes'. Bang: This right here is one of dem clash of the titans. Mark: Sure, if you want to look at it that way… Bang: I just don’t know who’s going to win! Shoving Deville away, Toomes charges at him, just barely ducking a clothesline tossed by Angelo, who is running forward as well. Spinning around, Switchblade runs back toward Deville, who bounces off the ropes. With a quick motion, Toomes catches Angelo in a sleeperhold, walking along with him to center of the ring off his momentum. Mark: Toomes with the sleeper! Bang: Angelo’s going for the ropes! Seabass cranks in on the sleeperhold, cutting off the flow of blood to Deville's brain and causing him to drop to a knee quickly. He reaches out his arms toward the ropes, but his grasp doesn't come close to saving him from the hold. Mark: He’s not making it! Switchblade has him trapped too far toward the center! Just when it looks as if he's about to fade, Angelo delivers an elbow to Switchblade's stomach, causing him to slacken his grip. Taking this opening, Deville spins around and lifts Toomes up in a fireman's carry, then swiftly deposits him to the mat with a side bodyslam piledriver. Both men lay sprawled out on the canvas, looking as if they're down for the count. The referee begins the ten count. Mark: Both men are down! This match is anyone’s game! Bang: Yee haw!!! This is more entertaining than raising a barn, right here! One... Two... Three... Four... Five... Both men stirring, they begin to crawl over to opposite sides of the ring. Vendetta and O’Neil are standing on the ropes, reaching their arms out for the tag, as their partners slowly but surely make their ways over. Mark: Who’s going to get to their corner first? Six... Seven... Eight... Bang: Looks like they’re getting there at the same time! With simultaneous lunges, both men make the tags to their respective corners. And simultaneously, both of their partners enter the ring. Simultaneously, both Clay Vendetta and Jason O'Neil pause, looking down thoughtfully at their injured partners. And simultaneously, they both raise their heads and exchange the briefest of looks between each other. Mark: These two men are caught in a standoff on opposite sides of the ring… Then, in unison, both men jerk their partners to their feet, hook their heads and arms, and drive them down with stereo brainbusters. The crowd explodes! Bang: What?! Traitors!!! Mark: VENDETTA AND O’NEIL HAVE JUST TURNED ON THEIR OWN PARTNERS!!!! Bang: They’d be strung up if they was caught doin' that anywhere south of Indiana! The cheering crowd are on their feet as Vendetta and O'Neil stand up, looking down at their partners. Toomes and Deville are out, unconscious or damn near to it after being dropped on their heads. As the crowd cheers, Jason and Clay look at each other again...and then almost at the same time the same thought dawns upon them. Leaping across the ring, Vendetta jumps on top of Deville, and O'Neil throws himself across Toomes. The ref drops to the mat, counting two pinfalls at the same time. Mark: WHO IS THE REF COUNTING FOR?!?! Bang: I DECLARE I GOT NO CLUE!!! One... Two... Thre................No! The crowd roars as both men kick out. Mark: DOUBLE KICK OUT!!! DOUBLE KICK OUT!!! Bang: DEM DOGS STILL GOTS SOME BARK LEFT!!! Both Toomes and Deville kick out at the very last possible moment, drawing an excited gasp from the Glassboro crowd. Vendetta and O'Neil slap the canvas in frustration. Clay begins delivering stomps to the body of Deville, while the Scarecrow pulls a dazed Toomes up to a kneeling position, setting him up for a powerbomb. Then, suddenly, the crowd lets out another gasp… Mark: WHAT THE!??!? Bang: GOOD GAAAAAWWWWDD!!! The cameras turn to the entryway as another figure emerge from the back...a huge awe-inspiring figure, at that!!! Bang: WHO THAT BIGGUN BE?!?! Mark: I DUNNO, BUT LOOK AT THE SIZE OF THAT GUY!!! Standing almost seven feet tall, with nearly 400 pounds of muscles rippling, a monster of a man walks down to the ring with a purpose and climbs up onto the ring apron with an effortless jump. His face shrouded by a studded leather mask, he casts quite the impressive aura as he steps over the top rope, drawing the attention of the combatants in the ring. Mark: I DON’T KNOW WHO THE HELL THAT MONSTER IS OR WHAT HE WANTS, BUT HIS INTENTIONS CAN NOT BE GOOD!!! Clay Vendetta, the man nearest to the giant intruder, barely has time to react as a powerful hand shoots out and grips him around the throat. Lifting him off the ground with ease, the masked man slams him down viciously, landing him on the back of his head and neck. Vendetta folds from the impact, ending up laying face down on the mat. The crowd is in a confused frenzy. Mark: OH MY GOD!!! HE JUST TOSSED VENDETTA LIKE A DOLL!!! Bang: He just got dag-gone broken in half! The referee calls for the bell. Mark: THE REFEREE HAS JUST THROWN THIS MATCH OUT!!! WHAT A CRYING SHAME!!! Bang: DAT BIGGUN AIN’T DONE!!! Back in the ring, Jason O'Neil has more time to react than Vendetta, and as soon as the man turns his gaze to focus on him, O'Neil is ready in a fighting stance, having dropped Toomes to the mat as soon as this new menace decided to show himself. The unnamed giant advances on him slowly, appearing to be almost sluggish, and O'Neil leaps in, delivering a few hard punches to the midsection before leaping back out of range. The man stops in his tracks at this, gazing at the Scarecrow in silent contemplation. Mark: HA!!! THAT MONSTER IS GONNA THINK TWICE BEFORE MESSING WITH JASON O’NEIL AGAIN!!! Bang: YOU BE BEST NOT TO ASSUME DAT, I DO RECKON!!!! Then, with almost unbelievable speed for his size, the masked man lunges, across the ring one moment and on O'Neil the next. Grabbing O'Neil by his arm, the monster tosses him into the corner like a rag doll, and is already raining down punches upon him before he can bounce back out of the corner from the initial impact. Over and over the left and right hands connect with Jason's head and body, and with the speed and power behind them it isn't long before he can no longer defend himself against the flurry. Mark: MY GOD!!! I HAVE NOT SEEN A SUCH A PACKAGE OF SPEED AND POWER SINCE, DARE I SAY IT… Bang: O’NEIL DUN GOTS A HEEP OF TROUBLE!!! Pulling the unmoving O'Neil out of the corner, the unknown monster casually presses him above his head. With an almost nonchalant motion, he tosses him toward the center of the ring, rushing in after him and catching him with a hard downward punch to the jaw before he hits the ground, thus adding an extra emphasis on the impact with the canvas. The crowd eats up this unnecessary violence, starting a chant of "Holy shit!" in appreciation. The Crowd: HOLY SHIT!!! HOLY SHIT!!! HOLY SHIT!!! HOLY SHIT!!! Bang: HE SMACKED O’NEIL LIKE COUSIN RUFUS SMACKS HIS MULE BEFORE MOUNTING HER!!! Mark: I AM NOT GONNA DIGNIFY THAT WITH A COUNTER COMMENT!!! Satisfied with the beating he has delivered, the behemoth decides to turn his attention away from O'Neil to focus upon Angelo Deville, who has just now gotten to one knee, still breathing heavily from earlier exertion. Deville puts a hand up in hopes of stopping the oncoming giant, but it provokes no response in the masked man, who wraps his hand around Angelo's throat as soon as he is in range. Bang: RUN ANGELO!!! RUN!!! Mark: THIS GUY IS A MONSTER!!! SOMEONE CALL THE COPS!!! His eyes bulging out of his head as the hand squeezes around his neck, the panic is apparent on Deville's face. Thinking quickly, he delivers a swift low blow, which succeeds in causing his assailant to break his grip. The masked man keels over from the shot, allowing Deville to scramble backwards and get to his feet. Mark: DEVILLE IS FREE!!! Bang: A KICK TO THE PEAS!!! A KICK TO THE PEAS!!! Immediately Deville begins to lay in with forearms, reaching over the big man's head to slam them down across his upper back. A brutal uppercut ends Angelo's offense, catching him under the chin and sending him stumbling back to the ropes in a daze. He bounces back, his legs rubber, and is immediately pulled down in a powerbomb set-up by his adversary. Mark: THAT BEAST FEELS NO PAIN!!! Bang: HE KINDA REMINDS ME OF… Mark: WHERE THE HELL IS SECURITY?!!?! Hoisting Deville up in crucifix position, the masked monster turns around to face the center of the ring. Taking a few steps forward, he tosses Deville up and forward... Then increasing his pace to a run, the man snatches Angelo out of air and impacts him to the canvas with a sit-out piledriver. The Crowd: OOOOOOOOOOOOOOoooooooooooo!!!! Deville’s head bounces off the mat with a sickening thud. Bang: TARNATION! Mark: CALL THE NATIONAL GUARD!!! CALL GODZILLA!!! CALL SOMEONE!!!! Bang: LOOOOOOOOK!!!! The behemoth gets up, surveying the unmoving Deville... And *CRACK*... A steel chair swung by Sebastian Toomes collides with his back. The crowd cheers. Mark: TOOMES WITH THE CHAIR!!! A look of self-praise is painted on Switchblade's face as he grins evilly from ear to ear. Until the man turns around to face him unfazed, that is. Dropping the chair, Toomes backtracks, holding his hands out in front of him as he apologizes frantically. Mark: THAT MONSTER FEELS NO PAIN!!! TOOMES IS IN DEEP TROUBLE!!! Bang: RUN SEABASS!!! RUN!!! Turning to flee, Toomes gets all of two steps away before a hand on his shoulder jerks him back toward the center of the ring. Mark: THAT CREATURE HAS TOOMES!!! WHAT IS THE MEANING OF ALL OF THIS?!?!! In an instant Toomes is up in a crucifix powerbomb position on the masked monster's shoulders, and an instant later he suffers the same fate as Deville, being planted with a vicious powerdriver. The Crowd: OOOOOOOoooooooooooooooooooo!!! Toomes is down and out. Mark: WHO IS THIS BEHEMOTH!?!? WHAT THE HELL DOES HE WANT?!?! Bang: I DUNNO, BUT I AIN’T STICKIN’ AROUND IF HE COMES OVER YONDER!!! Whatever they're screaming, however, they're all doing it at the top of their lungs, as the small venue is filled with more volume than one would think it could contain. Mark: THIS IS AN ABSOLUTELY SHOCKING TURN OF EVENTS!!! THIS BEAST HAS COME FROM NOWHERE AND TAKEN OUT FOUR OF THE TOP WRESTLERS IN THE WORLD!!! WITHOUT BREAKING A SWEAT!!! Bang: WHO DO HE BE?!?! Bang: WHO IN THE NAME OF UNCLE JESSE IS THAT!??! Mark: I HAVE NO CLUE!!! FOLKS WE ARE OUT OF TIME!!! WE WILL SEE HERE ON WGTW TV-48 NEXT SATURDAY NIGHT!!!
