Move Set Regular Tag Match Moves 1.WAAAAAAAAAAAASABI!!!!-Gunner body slams his opponent to the mat, standing over them and spreading their legs as Neon climbs the turnbuckle. Neon then looks to the crowd, then to Gunner, and in Dudley Boy fashion yells 'WASAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAABI!!!' before dropping a leg-drop nutcracker. 2.The Thumbtack Jack-Gunner lifts the victim in a single leg pick-up over his shoulder, Neon coming up from behind and hooking a DDT as they both drop to the mat. 3.Twin Shurikens-Double superkicks by Gojoh and Neon, Neon's being the more pretty of the two seeing as how Gunner has bad knees 4.'LET ME SHOW YA HOW IT'S DONE!'-In the midst of a match-up, Neon will yell to Gunner,'WATCH THIS' and immediately hit the hapless victim with a beautiful moonsault press from the top...this eggs Gunner into busting out his OWN moonsault press to 'show him how it's done', Gunner moonsault being a bit more rugged however 5.The House Of Pain-Gunner hooks a figure four leg lock on the victim, Neon going to the top and unleashing one of his many high-flying moves on their downed body. 5.The Deathsmack-With the victim laying face down, Gunner grabs an arm and a leg and Neon does the same. They both then power the opponent up and over, slamming him to their back with extreme velocity more always coming.... :) Hardcore Tag Moves-Meant to be used for ANY type of Extreme match they face off in 1.HOT WASAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAABI!!!!-(Requires a kendo stick or some kind of pole)-The more extreme version of their pet move, WASAAAAAAAAAABI!! They go through all the motions just like the regular version, but instead they yell 'HOT WASAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAABI' and Neon performs the leg drop portion with a kendo stick or type of pole between his own legs to crash into the opponents testicles. 2.The Tokyo Streetfight Special-(No weapons needed, but kendo sticks or trashcan lids can be implemented)-Neon and Gunner get the opponent weakened but standing, each man taking turns striking the major extremities of the body until the opponent is downed with stinging kicks. They start with the arms, then work their way to the legs so they are down on their knees, then finish with two strong kicks to each side of the face 3.Flaming Con-Chair-To-(Two WOODEN chairs and some sort of flammable substance)-After a thorough beatdown of their opponent, Gunner and Noen get a set of wooden chairs and douse them in some sort of flammable substance. After setting the chairs on fire somehow, Gunner and Neon procced to deliver a con-chair-to with their flaming chairs 4.Fall From The Heavens-(One ladder and a metal, wooden, or glass table)-Gunner sets the table up, beating the opponent down until they are able to be laid upon the selected table. The ladder is positions close to it, Neon climbng all the way to the top before leapfrogging over the top and dropping a FULL CHEST style splash, legs and arms fully extended back, on his opponent while putting him through the table as well 5.Barbed Wire Express-(A string of barbed wire)-The barbed wire is put over the chest of Gunner, the victim already set in the corner as Neon whips Gunner into the victim...crashing his barbed wire enwrapped chest into the opponent.
