Matthias Kuntz-Hunt Biography Nicknames 'Das Dangerous Technik' or/and 'DDT' Hometown Hamburg, Germany Birthday April 7th 1969 Height 6'5' Weight 290 Biography Matthias Kuintz-Hunt was born into a German working class family on April 7th 1969 in Hamburg, Germany. His father, Karl, is an executive within his father’s (Matthias’ grandfather’s) contracting company (the biggest in Northern Germany, third largest in Germany) and his Mother, Kristina, works as a head nurse at the local hospital. He also has an older brother called Andreas who is five years his senior, who continues the family tradition of working in the family business alongside his father and grandfather. Sister Gina is two years younger and works as a freelance photographer for the numerous local newspapers. Although the family construction company has brought wealth to Matthias' life, he has decided Matthias - or Matt for short - is married to Manchester-born Stephanie and has two children; Carl (4) and Jessica (7). The three of them currently reside in a New York suburb after deciding to move to the United States, as Matt’s wrestling career made the leap across the pond from Europe. At grammar school at Lons-Sherber Gymnasium (German grammar school) in Hamburg, Matt excelled in his favourite subject; English. He was the polite and well-mannered student, but often had his moments where he would act the class clown to get attention. He was also the sportsman, although he only had eyes for one sport in particular and it wasn’t wrestling…hell, wrestling wasn’t even known of in Germany until the late 1980’s! Matt’s main love in life was football, or soccer, and often used his size advantage (he was 16st at aged 17) on the pitch. Of course, people debated whether he should chase his dream of following in his hero’s - German football captain Franz Beckenbauer - footsteps and become a professional footballer. By the time he left the grammar school, Matt was a hefty 17st and stood at 6’4” and had his heart set on playing professional football. However, the top German clubs in the area turned him down because of his abnormal bulk and his dreams of playing the field were slowly fading quickly. At that point - mid-1988 - Wolfgang Hunt's (Matthias' Grandfather) construction company had branched out into other European countries, such as Austria, Switzerland and England. At this fathers request, Karl and his family were to move to England so he could work in the Manchester branch and basically, spead the news about W.Hunt Construction. This is where Matthias' life took a different direction from where he had expected. Once he was on English soil and following a period where he and his family got to grips with the local wife of life, Matthias looked at a number of English football clubs to trial at. Using his vast knowledge of the game, he noticed that the English style was much more physical than his local German approach. So, with this in mind he thought he would be at an advantage and knocked on the doors of certain teams in London area. But his dream were dashed once more, but it wasn’t the language barrier as Matt had excelled in the English subject at school and spoke the dialect fluently. He was simply too big to be a footballer, despite his young age and talented potential, and to save time and money on other clubs of the country, officials advised that he should change the direction he wanted to go in. At 20, Matt was obviously crushed and seeked a new career path. But what would he do? He didn’t want to return to Germany, he had tasted the English environment and wanted more, but what exactly? He obtained a working pass and worked in a supermarket. Although his family was slowly coming into the wealth, his father wanted Matthias to earn his own money and pay his dues...just like any man...and Matthias had no problem with the theory. He wasn’t paid that much a week, but what he did earn was enough to show his father and family that he was determined and focused enough to graft and work hard. After a few months of stacking shelfs and huffing and puffing in the back stores, he noticed an advertisement for a new wrestling school that was soon opening in the area. He hadn’t given the idea much thought as he’d always had football as his career path. But this appealed to him and after hearing the news that he was “too big for football”, was he too big to be a wrestler? He enquired and enrolled in the school for a costly fee that took away nearly half of his weekly wage, but Matthias didn’t mind and made the necessary sacrifices. Course, he wanted to show Karl that he could pay his own way - just like Karl did at his age. He slowly but surely worked on his physic and in-ring style, while suffering taunts about his nationality and often suffering racist remarks. In the early stages of the learning curve, Matthias decided that his name was 'too German' and didn't want the added attention. Of course, he was ashamed of himself for hiding his nationality and heritage, but he felt that a booker wouldn't hire a young man called 'Mattihas Kuntz-Hunt'. So he lost the 'Kuntz' and shortened 'Matthias' to 'Matt' However, it didn't stop the abuse... Instead of reacting face-to-face, Matt took the abuse and worked his frustration off in the ring and gym…it soon paid off and after six or so months in the school, he was up for independent booking by the local small organisations. However, the small-time promoters often ignored his wrestling ability and prodded at his nationality, for the entertainment factor. Matt began to tour on the indy scene, receiving abuse inside the ring and outside…but this only pushed his determination even further. As the years rolled by, Matt began excel while trying his best to ignore the taunts and provocation from those working alongside him. He rose up the ladder in the English wrestling scene and got recognition for his technical wrestling ability…as well as his nationality. It was during a tour of the Greater Manchester area, where Matt met his wife, Stephanie in January 1993. After a whirlwind romance, they married in Salford, Manchester on August 31st 1993 - Stephanie’s birthday. Two years later, in November 1995, baby girl Jessica was born and Carl soon followed in April 1998. While Matt was making changes to his personal life with the marriage and births of two children, his goal of becoming one of the best in the business did not stop for one second. With the family settled in Manchester and Matt being a big name in the English wrestling scene, he felt it was time for a change and pondered about the hop across the large pond to the North Americas. The family stood by his decision and his mind was made up, the Hunt’s were coming to America! After being directed by some Manchester contacts, Matt enrolled into a well-known and ever-developing wrestling academy. This meant that he had to start 'at the bottom' - but that wasn't a problem, it just gave Matt the extra drive. He proved people wrong once before and he was determined to do it again. After honing his in-ring ability and verbal skills in the academy, Matt was drafted to the now-defunct wrestling developmental terrority - Canuck Heat Zone. Course, this meant that he had to go to Canada while Stephanie and the children decided to stay in the NEw York area - where they first moved to upon arriving. This was to test their relationship, as it would turn to out to the longest that the family has ever stayed apart. In this developemental terrotry, Matt was announced as the CHZ Commissioner - which turned out to be a major shocker to the terrotry's small following - moments after losing the CHZ World Title match against Jake Schultz.. This would kick off a rivalry between the two, which looked to run a good few months. However, due to financial reasons, the terrority closed within a few months of opening and Matt was left with his future to consider. Fortuantely, he was approached by organisers of RUSH wrestling. A small, tigh-knit company which thrived on the fact that they were 'the little organisation that could' and soon enough, Matt got himself into a heated rivarly with GOD - a 'robot' that was constructed by a Dr Herbert Jenkins, who proclaimed that his creation was the perfect physcial specimen. Matt took offence to this moniker and set out to prove that GOD was nothing more than a fake and anything but perfect. Matt beleived that he was the 'greatest wrestler' there is and although he was a sound worker in the squared circle, many felt that his ego was slightly ballooning. He also had a tendacy of getting into trouble, purely by shooting his mouth off with such claims as the one noted above. Arrogance started to show itself, as well as some cowardice...as Matt kidnapped Dr Jenkins and tormented him for three weeks. Although he has shown a somewhat comedic side - only to himself, that is - he displayed henious and distasteful scenes as he whipped, beaten, spraypainted and threatened his life (Matt would often have his German shepard dog at his side and claimed that his pet would tare Jenkins to peices at a click of a finger). However, as Matt's tormenting and 'fun' continued...GOD simply vanished of the face of the earth. Nobody knows where he disappeared to, even to this day, and nobody knows why. To end this saga once and for all, he simply released the Doctor...knowing full well, that he would be scarred for life. While he rallied the he forced GOD out of RUSH, Matt set his sights on the RUSH Network Title and it wasn't long before he defeated then-Champion Livewire on Adreneline - RUSH's weekly show. The 'Hunt-Effect' - as he calls it - was at work again, as Livewire disappeared soon after losing the Network Title. Now he was in his own fantasy land as he claimed to have pushed Livewire out of RUSH as well. As the PCW-RUSH merger was broadcasted continued, Hunt defended his title against numerous opponents - but he came face-to-face with Gideon Daray Poe, who proved to be his toughest nut to crack. In December 2002 and early January 2003, Poe and Hunt embroiled themselves ina heated feud for the Network Title, where the challenger often came close to winning the strap. However, Matt showed cowardice as another side to his 'new' character and often got himself disqualified to save his title reign. As the feud was developing, Matt suffered an injury and took the time to heal back in his homeland in Europe...and 'promising' Poe that their rivalry would resume when he would return in mid-Jan. When he did return, he came back with a new name and some inherited wealth from his grandfather - who had since passed away. To show his family the respect and loyality that he felt he lacked in his early days, Matt renamed himself Matthias...brought back the Kuntz (mother's madien) and stuck with the Hunt...now being known to the North AMerican wrestling indutry as his real birth name - Matthias Kuntz-Hunt. However, no sooner had Matthias returned that the PCW/RUSH merger ended and all RUSH contracts were deemed void. That meant that all RUSH roster members and their contracts were being released with immediate effect in late January 2003. While many speculated and discussed the return of RUSH as a separate entity, a statement was finally released in early April 2003 that RUSH would not be re-opening it's doors and ending a legacy that had lasted for a good eight months. As nobody questioned his reign at the RUSH Network Champion as a result of the release of his contract, Matthias assumed that he was still the titleholder and still has it in his possession to this day. Something that should be questioned is Matthias' future in this business. It's all well and good being a titleholder of a defunct organistation and thriving in the supposed limelight of that reign, but he needs a new home to showcase his talents and reintroduce himself to the community before he is labelled as a has-been, even before he got started... Only time…as well his focus, determination and drive…will tell.
