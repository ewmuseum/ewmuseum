Magnum Wolf Biography Nicknames The Alpha Male, The Unit, Magz Hometown Tucson, Arizona Birthday October 22, 1975 Height 6'6' Weight 270 Biography After a quite devestating loss at the FSW PPV Genesis to Super Duper Masked Jobber King and John Jupiter, Magnum Wolf sort of fell out of the 'tough guy' lifestyle. For unknown reasons, Magnum began to run his own Private Eye business. At first, Magnum employed the veteran Sports Entertainer Virgil (of Ted DiBiase fame) to be his assistant. Soon, the on-screen antics of Virgil and Magnum brought them both new found fame. The calls started to come in to them to move to a new stable that was forming. Magnum dropped the PI business for television, but he never let it go outside of FSW. After the closing of Five Star Wrestling, Magnum Wolf found himself in an interesting position. No one was too interested in 'The guy who talked into dildos' in the wrestling world. He was snubbed by many of the top federations, and finally decided to just go back to his PI business full time. He went searching for a new partner, because paying Virgil for doing nothing is just TOO MUCH on a PI's salary. Enter: Beretta Coyote. Johnathan Christianson, by birth. He was a struggling actor who played Don Johnson's stunt double on Miami Vice. He stumbled into Magnum Wolf's offices one day after a drinking binge. After a few hours of arguing over who the better Ninja Turtle was, Magnum offered Johnathan a job. Only problem was that he couldn't be John Christianson. That's not cool enough. After much debate, and suggestions like 'The Cool Guy', which was thrown out saying that no one in their right mind would use that name, they finally rested on a mutation of Magnum's own name. Beretta Coyote. What would they call this company? Magnum's Gumshoe Services. The two went a few months, solving a few minor cases, and making enough to live on. Soon enough, they knew they needed something else. But didn't know what. Until one day, a man came into their offices wearing a pair of stockings on his head. Once Beretta and Magnum were able to control their laughter (about an hour later) the man explained that he was with a small wrestling company known as Pro Wrestling Circuit. He was interested in employing them... really mostly Magnum... and would pay a few hundred dollars a night. While not a whole lot, it was enough to pay the rent by doing one night's work. That, and it would make it seem less weird that Wolf was always wearing his wrestling gear. An agreement was made, and the rest is, as they say, history. Other Feds LCW Mercury, Extreme Online Wrestling, American Wrestling League, Five Star Wrestling Other Fed Awards Hall of Famer in each of them, other than FSW... voted best roleplayer of the year two years running in EOW ('97 and '98), best character in AWL in '00, Feud of the year in EOW in '99, 14-time EOW Champion Wrestling Style Technical/Power hybrid Finisher Move Magnum Force, Point of Authority Finisher Description (Magnum Force) Inverted Implant DDT (Point of Authority) Sit-out Fire Thunder Driver Finisher Setup Move The Alpha Driver Setup Description Gorilla Press into high angle michinoku driver Other Moves Alternate Finisher- The Large Package (a cradle pin where Magnum ends up kneeling over the face of his opponent, and pulling the legs back so that the knees meet the shoulders) Tiger Suplex, Dragon Suplex, Cradle DDT, Impact DDT, Snap Suplex, Tiger Driver '91, Modified STF, half crab, Brainbuster Suplex, Reverse Russian Leg Sweep, Meltdown Entrance Music 'Hungry Like the Wolf' by Duran Duran
