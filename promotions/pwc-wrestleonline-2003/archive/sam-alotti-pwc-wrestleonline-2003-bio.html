Sam Alotti Biography Nicknames Sammy Hometown Sometime here, sometimes there... Birthday 16/05/82 Height 6ft1 Weight 137 Biography It wasn’t really a troubled beginning, rather one of those things you would rather push to the back of your mine and try and ignore for as long as possible. My father was quite the spectacle, and probably the reason I left home so quickly. He was not a bad man, nothing like the general stereotype of a father that most will whinge on about. He was just over protective to the tune of way too much. The kind of guy who would follow you on dates or sit in the same restaurant and pretend it was a coincidence. He was an entertainer by nature, quite a funny guy when he wanted to be, just way too clingy for my liking. My mother, on the other hand, was perfect. Kept the write distance most of the time, but was there when she was needed. Sweet, kind, generous and most definitely perfect in every way, she was the mother that we all dream of, like the TV sitcom mother whom we all admire. God I miss her. Where was I? Oh, schooling was cool; it was filled with lads if you know what I am saying. Heh, It was not a problem, not a great activity but it happened, there was no way of actually avoiding it and so I did it. I tried as hard as I wanted and achieved what was within my reach. Dating was the only problem. Don’t get me wrong, it all came easily enough, I was never bad at getting the lads, it was just keeping my dad away from them for long enough to put the moves on them you know? I had to get away, I left home at eighteen and moved as far away from them as I could, at the time I moved about fifteen minutes away, but that’s what you get while working for McDonalds. I had always looked at America with wide eyes, the tans you could get from the beaches were like nothing you could get in England and the people were great. Then there was Hollywood, the glamour, the glitz, and the harrowing stench of cash! I had to be a part of it! I left Maccy D’s soon after and got a job doing as much as I could, as often as I could. I soon got my plane ticket and rented a cheap flat in Florida, and when I say cheap, you better believe it! It was bottom of the range crap, but it was liveable. I handed out leaflets for a while, it was a job at least, but I wanted fame. Now what was there for me? I couldn’t act, sing or dance and even skating was a problem. So what did American’s do when acting was a problem? They applied to the amateur dramatics association, otherwise known as World Wrestling Entertainment! Well, that didn’t work. Apparently the English woman was out of fashion at this time of the year, and in fact, this century. But I still wanted my fame. I could fight and made sure that I could. A cheap gym membership and several promotional tapes later, and I were spreading my name around the world of wrestling. It seemed I could do no wrong. I joined a small place and talked aimlessly about how good I was; it did seem to work like some miracle cure for low self-esteem. I wasn’t a bad wrestler either; working hard at everything as it was all there was to do. However, as I got bored of fighting beefed up steroid junkies, I had to get somewhere were women had the up most value in society. I had to make it big. So, after sending out even more tapes to even more people, I had got nowhere.
