Angelo Deville Biography Nicknames Crimson Angel, Angie Hometown Kemptville, Ontario Birthday May 17, 1978 Height 6'2' Weight 259 Biography Nineteen-seventy-eight was a banner year for the Deville family. Lorenzo and Carla finally purchased their dream-house in the country, Lorenzo was spending far less with the business, and the couple gave birth to their first child, Angelo. Everything was going perfectly for the self-made millionaire family, with their hardships done and their plans finally coming to fruition, and things only seemed to be getting better. A happier family you’d be hard-pressed to find. The Deville Family, on the other hand, was in ruins. With Lorenzo dedicating less and less time to his organisation and delegating more and more duties, the focus of power shifted and wavered, settling unequal and jagged on many of his closest friends and advisors. Matters that once would’ve been too unimportant or non-sensical to hear the light of day became major bones of contention. Petty fights began breaking out among the respected heads. Some even began no-showing important meetings and working on their own. Lorenzo Deville still had the major say in the inner-workings, and his hand fell hard, fast, and without mercy where need demanded, but his chokehold on the industry began to relax, bidding anarchy from its depths. His time ruling his city took a backseat to that spent with his wife and child, and gradually his power lay only in his name, not in the respect and loyalty of those beneath him. Lorenzo had no intention of giving up either family, or letting either suffer in any way, but by the time Angelo was five years old, he was a figurehead of only one. Michelangelo (Michel) Salvatore was the one to step up in his pseudo-absence and take over the day-to-day operations. The two had been close since starting their own minor syndicate in their teens, and the only difference between the two lay in their greed and ambitions – Salvatore’s were far greater than Deville’s. Salvatore had always been jealous of Lorenzo’s charisma, which was the sole factor in determining which eventually ran everything and named their Family. He always told Michel that it didn’t matter, that the two of them were on the same plane, but he never believed it, and nor did those following their greatness. It was The Deville Family. Salvatore didn’t mind, as Lorenzo told him he never planned on having children, and would likely grow tired of running everything before he turned thirty-five. This would pave the way for Salvatore to take over without bloodshed, presuming Deville’s hot nature didn’t get him killed before the day of his retirement came. Contentedly, he played second-fiddle to Lorenzo, anxiously awaiting the day for his turn in the spotlight. The birth of Angelo Deville consumed that hope in perpetual darkness. Lorenzo made it clear upon his son’s birth that Angelo would one day become Don. Salvatore raged. Salvatore’s following raged. There was nothing that could be done. Killing the infant heir would definitely bring Lorenzo’s full attention, and he wouldn’t quit until he had skinned the culprit alive. Killing Lorenzo himself would never work, since even if he was able to catch the man unaware – which he couldn’t even fathom – rumours would abound and his people would scatter. Salvatore would be suspect, at least, and with the loyalty Lorenzo inspired among his men, Michel feared his burial would come within days of Deville’s. (Yes, I'll finish this...
