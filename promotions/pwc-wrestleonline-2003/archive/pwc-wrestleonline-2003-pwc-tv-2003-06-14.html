PWC TV for June 14th LIVE from the Richard Stockton State College, Pomona, NJ. PWC TV burst to life with a split screen shot of the two combatants for the PWC World Heavyweight Title at Last Man Standing. On the right walks the Champion of the World, Jim Axtell. He wears a Gold’s Gym t-shirt, blue jeans, and a determined look on his face. The PWC World Title belt is wrapped around his waist as he strides rather easily down the corridor, not showing too many lingering effects from his knee injury. On the left side of the picture is the Masked Monster, Penance, striding powerfully toward the camera with ‘The Prophet’ Jacob Solomon at his side. The hulking figure of Penance is menacing as his leather mask-covered face seemingly shows no emotions. PWC officials and backstage hands alike step aside as the Dark Atoner and his handler stalk down the corridor. Mark: There they are, fans! Those two men will do battle tomorrow night for the PWC World Heavyweight Title! Bang: An it’ll be ‘bout time that there Axtell gets the whoopin’ he right deserves. Mark: These men are on their way out here to the ring RIGHT NOW!! The split screen continues to show Axtell, Penance, and Solomon walking as if on a mission before it switches to the packed arena on the campus of Richard Stockton State College in Pomona, New Jersey. The fans are going nuts on the eve of the second pay per view in PWC history as “Straight Out of Line” by Godsmack rips over the PA system. The camera pans around the small arena, taking samples of the rabid fans. One shot is of a group of fans wearing leather S&M masks and passing out Neo-Eden pamphlets. Moving on from them we see a pair of young, salivating, gay men with large blown-up posters of Magnum Wolfs package. Quickly moving on from the ratings killers, we switch to the ringside announce table where Mark Malloy and Bang Davenport are standing by. Mark: Welcome folks to this pay per view-eve edition of PWC Television!! I am Mark Malloy, and with me as usual is Ba- Bang: The Bang-er of mothers, daughters, sisters, and anyone else thats in mah family that wants ta git some! WHOOO~~!! Malloy buries his head in his hands as “Quiet” by the Smashing Pumpkins hits over the PA, bringing the crowd to a fury of boos. Mark: I am almost grateful that Starr is coming out here after that….announcement. Bang: What? I like to go with what I know, ya know? Ron Starr, the CEO of PWC, emerges from the back and walks out between the two stacks of televisions. Images of Starr doing various dastardly things, including some clips of him planting PCW stars with the TPB-DDT, are shown as he saunters toward the ring. The Masked Monster steps over the top rope and then steps on the bottom rope. He pulls up on the middle rope and allows Jacob Solomon to step right through. The Champion rolls his eyes at Starr who looks at both of the competitors in the ring.
