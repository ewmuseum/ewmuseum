Rhett Locke Biography Nicknames The Albino Hometown Truth or Consequences, NM Birthday The second day of the second month, of the year 1976 Height 6'0' Weight 225 Biography A really long fucking time ago, we were born in Truth or Consequences, New Mexico. It was some big town, tourists pouring in for what we assume was just for the name. You know, they'd stop in Roswell and say 'Hmm, Truth or Consequences? Sounds cool.' Fucking faggots and their assumptions. Truth or Consequences is, beyond a shadow of a doubt, the biggest cesspool of shit we've ever stepped foot in. Soon, thank God Almighty, we were put up for adoption. For the longest time, no one wanted us. Until we were about seven, when the Raiyn family adopted us. Fuck the Raiyn's...and Corg. And we guaran-fucking-tee that's all you'll ever get out of us about our relationship with that God forsaken family. A lot of the shit after the adoption is a blur. Fuckin'... Cocksucker (Corg) was born, and reality meshed with fiction for a while. We had no idea who the hell we were. Padded walls surrounded us, doctors injected some fucking blue shit into us like heroin. Like we got high off of it or something like that. Finally we got out, no longer seeing perception... But fuck, you wanna know about Rhett Locke of the present right? Well, that little bastard nabbed a couple of OSW titles and dominated. Shortly after leaving and pissing off the entire worldwide OSW fanbase. Who gives a shit though? Not us. We could've watched soap operas and been just as content as watching some russian bang some teen pop-idol bitch reject. PIW sucked. We won the Hardcore title...it still sucked. So, we toured the garbage feds of Japan. Puroresu sucks dick anyway, even more than lucha libre. Fucking chinks and wetbacks jumping all over the place, with a clear good and a clear evil. Bullshit. The only clear good lately has been Jesus, and half of the world doesn't believe he exists. So now we're here. Whoopdy-fucking-shit right? That's what we said. We only came here to fuck up Ron and now we suddenly have some ninny running around attacking us everywhere we turn. Eh, he'll get skullfucked. Just like the rest. That's our story, and we're sticking to it. Other Feds OSW, PIW Other Fed Awards OSW: International and World title. PIW: Hardcore title.
