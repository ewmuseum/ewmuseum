The scene opens up backstage to show Jim Axtell with a sad look on his face. He is on his feet and is wearing a sports tracksuit with matching top and pants. He runs his hands through his spiky brown hair and looks left. The camera follows this glance to show Totakoxe Salvador standing alongside Axtell. The Argentinean looks worse for wear as the kidnapping of his father has taken its toll on him. However, the camera quickly shoots back to Axtell as he speaks. Jim: I don’t think you should come out to ringside tonight, Tota. The camera zooms out to show the figures of both men. Tota: Listen, Jim, of course I’m upset at what those, those… (BLEEP) did to my dad. Hell, I still don’t even know where Jose is… Tota stops mid-way through his sentence and covers his face with his large hand. Axtell is caught off guard and consoles his friend with an arm around his shoulder. Jim: That, that right there is why I don’t think you should accompany me tonight. You’ll be driven by emotions and fuelled by the desire to exert revenge on 2GVC and you’ll do something that you’ll regret further down the line. Salvador removes his hand from his face, his light blue eyes releasing several teardrops. They run down the contours of his face and he wipes them off. Tota: You don’t get it, Jim. I want to be there… I HAVE TO BE THERE! Jim: Why? I don’t want to see 2GVC hurt you more than they have already and I don’t advise you to come out to ringside tonight. Tota: 2GVC couldn’t possibly hurt me more than they already have! The Axphyxiator takes a step back from Totakoxe. Tota: Jim, you’re a good friend. You don’t want me hurt and I respect that. But I NEED to go out there tonight. Absolutely nothing can stop me from finding Jose and paying those dirty, dirty Viet Congs back! Jim: Well, bud, I guess I can’t stop you. Totakoxe’s frown quickly turns into somewhat of a smile. Jim: You can come out with me tonight, but I can’t guarantee I can watch out for you – you gotta be real careful! Tota: Oh, don’t worry, amigo, don’t worry – I’ll be fine. The scene closes on Totakoxe Salvador and Jim Axtell walking down the corridor. . . . . . . . . . We switch to the car park of the arena, where Jay Phoenix is walking into the arena, with his sports bag slung over one shoulder. Standing there waiting for him, are the two members of the Roman Empire, Romulus and Colossus, dressed in ceremonial toga attire. Romulus: Greetings Phoenix, we welcome you back to Harlem on behalf of your insurance policy. Phoenix looks at the duo and shakes his head. Phoenix: I didn’t realize your policy came with free lackies. Romulus: Ah, for your barb cuts deeply, I am sure on some great level. However, you might be glad to know that it does in fact not break the cease fire we have called some two weeks ago on PWC Television. How may I ask is your knee? Phoenix: It’s fine. Romulus grins deeply. Romulus: It’s far from fine isn’t it? The left one is giving you great pain still after that chair attack by Colossus some time ago. Phoenix shakes his head. Phoenix: I said, it’s fine. I’m not ruling myself out of the match at the pay per view. Is that what you want to hear? Romulus grins ever more deeply. Romulus: Why do you lie to me, Jay Phoenix. You see, I know for sure that your knee is in exactly that condition which I described. Because I have an insurance policy. You should know by now that I am all about insurance. Romulus waves his hands towards a closed door. Romulus: May I introduce… The door opens and out walks Phoenix’s masseuse from a few weeks ago… dressed in a toga. Romulus: Roman Empire member Valeria Messalina, the very best in massage that the Roman Empire has to offer. And also, makes rather good aperitifs when she sets her mind to it. Valeria stands beside Romulus and Colossus. Phoenix: But the ceasefire?! Romulus: Did not the Americans gather intelligence on the Soviets all throughout the cold war? That was not a breach of any cease fire. Phoenix looks red with rage. Phoenix: I WILL NOT STAND FOR THIS!! Romulus looks at the end of his fingers and then back at Phoenix. Romulus: Now now, Jay Phoenix. I do suggest you calm down. That little outburst certainly raised your blood pressure, and that cannot be good. Phoenix doesn’t respond, he simply barges through the Roman Empire and through the open doorway, leaving the three there. Romulus: Thank you Valeria, that will be all. . . . . . The scene opens to the interior section of the Apollo Theatre, where 2900 screaming fans go bananas, as “Getting’ Hot in Here” by Nelly hits the PA system and lights and sirens go off around the arena. Cameras pan around the arena, revealing a predominantly African American crowd on hand, here to not only take in some quality wrestling action, but to watch their local television affiliate, WMXS-TV 22, sign a deal to carry PWC programming later in the show. After a bit more panning, to further put home the idea that the crowd is stoked for tonight’s festivities, the scene shifts to the ringside position of the PWC announce team. Mark (Only just being heard over the boos): Is he trying to start a riot or something? Romulus: Now I just want to make a statement about Jay Phoenix. You know, that Native American? Mark: We look set for this first match of the evening to get underway. Colossus double axehandles Gojoh to the side of the head and down to the canvas. Neon climbs to the top of his turnbuckle, obviously aiming to try to fly to the other side of the ring and impact Romulus onto this bulbs, when suddenly Colossus grabs him and flings him out of the ring and just over the crowd barrier into a bunch of burly black men. He gets quite confused when the crowd begin to cheer this. Mark: Phoenix is climbing the Empire’s own turnbuckle! Sure as he says, Jay Phoenix climbs to the top of the turnbuckle on the opposite side of the ring (and now behind) Colossus and leaps off in a flying cross body, hitting Romulus square in the chest and shattering the light bulb board. Phoenix rolls over onto the ring apron and stands on it, outside the ropes. Colossus turns about and sees Romulus amidst a pile of broken bulbs and Phoenix near by, and charges the Native. Mark: He’s in for it now! Phoenix pulls down the top rope just as Colossus comes close, and the giant goes tumbling over the ropes. Just to make sure, Phoenix jumps off the turnbuckle with an elbow drop onto the bigger guy. Gojoh scrambles over to where Romulus is and makes the cover… one… two… three!!!
