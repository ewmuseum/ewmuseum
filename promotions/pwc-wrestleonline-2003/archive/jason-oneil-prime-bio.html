Jason O'Neil Biography Nicknames J.O. Jace, Hometown Berea, Ohio Birthday 2-11-74 Height 6'2 Weight 252 Biography I’ve seen it all. Blood. Sweat. Tears. Death. Pain. Smiles. Doesn’t matter. I’m truly a scarecrow. Over and over the images dance in my mind. And yet, I feel nothing. No remorse. No sympathy. No empathy. I’ve felt more pain than most men, yet, I’ve never shed a tear. I’ve seen people die before my eyes. I’ve seen shattered necks, broken legs, and the way the face contorts in excruciating pain. And I feel nothing. I've lost everything. My wife. My daughter. My parents. My brother. My self respect. And still, I feel nothing. Pain, is all I know, and I welcome it. I crave it. I need it. Without pain, life is not what it should be. Joy is fleeting; anger is true. It's pure in it's feeling. There is no gray area. Anger is what drives me to be who I am. Retribution is coming to those who nearly destroyed me. Kimbusa, Caldera, and the others that let this happen to me, you will be entering your own personal nightmare, you will feel pain, and suffering, unequalled in the history of humanity. It makes me the scarecrow. And that makes me whole. Now can you handle...that? (for a detailed bio about O'Neil's life go to www.angrygeneration.com/oneilbio.shtml) Other Feds FSW, GCW, PCW, EHWF Other Fed Awards PCW Extreme Champion (Pete) 3x GCW Tag Champs (Twizz, Reaper, Bryan Mayhem), 3x EHWF Tag Champ (Mayhem, Reaper, BPP) Wrestling Style Technical/Submission/MMA Finisher Move The Rapture Finisher Description O'Neil picks his opponent up for a fishermans suplex, but instead of going over with them, he turns them, and drops them down in a modified powerbomb, a la the Ki Crusher. Finisher Setup Move Boot to the stomach Other Moves Common Submission Autumn Harvest - O'Neil gets behind his opponent and grabs the opponents arms in a double arm grapevine. He then backs into the turnbuckle, and puts his right foot behind him onto the second turnbuckle. He pushes off, driving his opponent chest first into the mat, and rolls so that he ends up bridged, while still holding the opponents arms (American Dragon's Cattle Mutilator) The Recurring Nightmare - Crossface Chicken Wing. Children of the Corn - O'Neil stands op top of his opponent, who is on their stomache. He pulls them up as if to put on a Camel clutch, but wraps his right arm around his opponents throat from the left side, and grabs his opponents left arm with his left arm. He then wrenches back as a camel clutch. Think modified Dragon Sleeper, but more painful. No Moment - The victim lies on their back on the mat, with O'Neil standing over top of them. He picks up the victim’s legs as if he were getting ready for a wishbone legsplit, but he suddenly spins around so his back is facing the opponent, while holding onto the victim’s ankles. O'Neil then steps over so the victim is back on their stomach, and he has the victim’s feet/legs locked under his armpits, with their lower body going under his legs, but their upper body and head are still on the mat (essentially a Boston crab of Walls of Jericho, except O'Neil faces the opposite direction). He then reaches down to the opponent, still lying on their stomach, then grabs their arms and picks them up into the air, suspending them. While suspended, O'Neil swings the victim back and forth, putting extreme pressure on the back, up through the upper neck of the victim. - (done soley to smaller, opponents) Common Power Hammer On DDT - O'Neil delivers a kick to the gut, doubling over his opponent. He then backs into the ropes, and runs at his opponent, delivering a Strong DDT to the top of his opponents head. Rolling Suplex - First hits a Belly to Back, then locks arms, and throws down for a gut wrench Suplex. After that's executed, he picks up opponent for a Northern Lights Suplex - Usually a 'hulk up' or Hot Tag move. Scarecrow Bomb - Lifts opponent for a powerbomb. Drops opponent down, yet remains standing, and locks arms. Lifts opponent again, for a double powerbomb, but flips victim around, and drops them into a Rikishi Driver. - Will go for pin 100% of the time after this move. Common Top Rope Starry Night - Shooting Star Press. Wind Gust - Top Rope Corkscrew Plancha - mostly used on opponent on the outside. Common Moves Suplex Belly to Belly Suplex. Belly to Back Suplex. Butterfly Suplex. Pancake Suplex. Northern Lights Suplex. Side Salto Suplex. Brainbuster Suplex. Bear Hug Suplex. Short Powerbomb DDT Reverse DDT Standing Russian Leg Sweep STRO (think Rock Bottom, without the lift, sweeping the opponents legs out behind them.) Dropkick Rolling wheel kick Standing Sidekick Leaping Roundhouse kick yakuza Kick Asiatic Thrust European Uppercut Knifehand chop Adheres to strict Ethics code - Will not cheat under any circumstances to win. Will not use chairs, belts, etc. to pick up a win, and if known that a 3rd person did, he will not go for pinfall, letting his opponent recover, beating him on his own merit. Hates Cheaters. Entrance Music 'Scarecrow' by ReHaB for normal shows. 'Last Man Standing (Scarecrow Remix)' by DJ Anonymous for PPV.
