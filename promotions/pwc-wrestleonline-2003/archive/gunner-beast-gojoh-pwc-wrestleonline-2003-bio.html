Gunner 'Beast' Gojoh Biography Nicknames The Beast, Hardcore Monster, King of the Deathmatch, Mutilator, Sack-Man(Due to the black sack he carries around) Hometown Yokohama, Japan Birthday May 17, 1956 Height 6'6 Weight 283 Fed Titles -stare- Fed Awards -grumble- Biography COMING!! Other Feds Numerous Japanese Hardcore Federations, independent and professional. Other Fed Awards Countless deathmatch titles, trophies, and accolades ranging from the late 60's to the present. A Cult hardcore legend around the world. Wrestling Style Brutal Brawling, Japanese Deathmatch AKA 'Garbage' Wrestling, Dirty Tricks, Suprising amount of other styles when he wants to Finisher Move The Violent Urge Finisher Description Gunner assaults his victim with a barrage of chops, punches, forearms, and headbutts, choosing to end the massacre by locking one hand about his opponents throat while lifting the victim's leg up as well. He then lifts them up with the choke and performs Finisher Setup Move The Beast Breaker Setup Description Gojoh lifts his victim into a Canadian backbreaker, holding them up as he roars at the fans before spinning them over and SLAMMING their chest across his knee. Other Moves Strengths-HIGH threshold for pain...years upon years of hardcore fighting has tempered his body to take pain and his mind to block it out. Very knowledgeable in his older age, doesn't make rookie mistakes. Powerful brawling style can stop most in their tracks. Unpredictable when he has to be with his move-set, and even more so in a hardcore match. Weaknessess-Terribly bad knees. His left knee is bad, but his right knee is incredibly torn up, often wears a heavy brace over or under his gear. At risk whenever performing ANY kind of top rope move, especially his pet move in serious cases, the moonsault. Dosen't know when to call for aid at times. DA BLACK SACK!!! For your info, Gunner carries a black sack to the ring at all times with him...this sack houses hardcore items and there is NO TELLING what could come from this bag. There have been stories of what has come from the bag, but that was overseas and cannot be confirmed. I encourage matchwriters to use DA BLACK SACK~! in hardcoe or even regular match action, PULL WAHTEVER YOU WANT FROM IT...cause nobody knows what's in there! What Has Come Out Of DA SACK~! 1. Hard Plastic Bongo Bat-Used on Chris Campbell, cracked it over his frickin skull three times. (3/15/2003) 2. Gold Edition of MC Hammer's 'You Can't Touch This'-Used on Colossus, he didn't touch it...but his head sure did. Not like anyone'll miss that CD anyways. 3. Dell CPU, 1984 Edition-Used on Colossus, DUDE, you got hit with a DELL! *Cheesy smile* More to come... Normal Match Favorites -Shackles of the Beast-Chaining overhead belly-to-belly suplexes -The Gunner-sault-Moonsault, although not very pretty. Used only when he REALLY wants the win, due to his bad knees, or when egged on by the fans or Neon. -Heaven's Mandate-Overhead Press into a shoulder slam -Mutha F'N Choke-Cross arm camel clutch submission -Mutha F'N Bomb-Release Powerbomb (A'la Hardcore Holly) -The Yokohama Spike-Irish Whip, tosses opponent into air, catches them on the way down with a DDT -Figure 4 leglock -Countless headbutts, punches, bites, chokes, kicks -Yakuza Kick -Corner Splash -Snake Eyes -Release German Suplex -Low Blow -Choke with tape from fists -Corner beatdown -Side belly-to-belly slam -Short Clothesline -Spinebuster -Butterfly Lock -Fujiwara Armbar -Standing Leg Crab More to come...
