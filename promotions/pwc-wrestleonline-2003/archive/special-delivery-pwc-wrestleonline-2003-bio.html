Special Delivery Biography Biography These two are Pizza Delivery boys. They are juniors at Richard Stockton College. Sammy and Chuck were never the top students of their class. They were never the sports stars or the ladies men. In fact when it came to popularity they were a few eggs short of a dozen. Growing up going to Mays Landing High was tough for the boys. Growing up as geeks they never really had the chance to make many friends but they did always have each other. Chuck was always slow and dumb. He was the kid that the bullies would throw stuff at and he would react much later. Surprisingly enough though, Chuck was incredibly strong. Being so large he always found a spot as the schools Right Guard on the football team. In fact he was so strong that as a bet after a game he won a six pack of beer for lifting the back end of the school bus. Sammy was never really a sports guy, well he was but he wasn't a fan of football, baseball or hockey. Sammy was on the schools gymnastic team. He always felt at home after landing a triple back flip off the uneven bars. In fact, Sammy still to this day holds the record at Mays Landing High School for male gymnast. He proudly displays them for all to see although he gets defensive when reminded that no other guy has joined the team other them him. After high school these two childhood friends found that college was tougher then they though. Both having little ambition they decided to go to RSC. Together they live in Chuck's basement downstairs from his parents. Since being a fat linemen and a not so manly gymnast weren't the keys to many of the ladies hearts at MLHS the guys decided that something had to change. After not having a date between the two of them in 3 years, although Sammy denies it, the two noticed that girls liked one thing, wrestlers. In an attempt to attract lady friends the two went through wrestling school together. Although not having the money for it at first, the boys were sure that Sammy's rich father would help them pay their loans. Sammy's father who is the biggest defense lawyer in all of Mays Landing was not as eager to help. Embarrassed of his underachieving son and disgrace of a friend, Mr. Bello refused to lend the boys the money they needed. Now, the boys work both their wrestling job and at Cousin Mario's pizza shop to help pay off the bills. It's a tough life for them sometimes but Sammy claims that chicks dig the smell of pizza. So armed with handfuls of bread sticks, and Chuck's 1989 Ford Station Wagon named Polly, they are ready to bring service with a smile to the wrestling ring. As long as you hold the anchovies.
