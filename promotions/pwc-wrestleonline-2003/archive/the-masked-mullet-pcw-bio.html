The Masked Mullet Biography Nicknames Mullet, Clem Hometown Lemmon, South Dakota Birthday April, 1968 Height 6'2' Weight 157 Biography Clem came from a small town, called Lemmon, South Dakota. He always dreamed of being a wrestler, though, he was always deemed too small, so he got a job close to the action. He joined the wrestling world as ring crew. He happily worked along side the wrestlers, constantly the butt of jokes, due to his looks, and redneck ways, but he still felt like he was living his dream. He joined CCW, as ring crew, and stayed there for quite a while. At least, until his dream came true. An injured Cianan, CCW champion at the time, and owner of CCW, as well. Became injured, and his increasing paranoia caused him to look for some 'muscle.' no one applied, exscept Clem. Cianan begrudgingly took him on, and gave Clem a shot at being a >wrestler, which Clem has never forgotten and to this day thanks, and is loyal towards his 'dream maker' Cianan. As The Masked Mullet, he struggled to over come adversity, beating opponents like Bojangles The Klown, and Devyl, and eventually winning #1 contendership to a Hardcore Title. The title shot never came. However, he did make quite a name for himself being as a 'hardcore icon.' After struggling and adversity, he managed to beat several people, and win the newly re-instated Light Weight Champion, over the likes of Kyle 'Velocity' Lamen, Kensuke Suzahara, and Jesse Ramey. After a small, and rather painful experiance in the MET Tournament, (and losing to Lilith in a house show). He sank back into obscurity. (RUSH history to come.) Other Feds CCW, RUSH, OSW (MET) Other Fed Awards CCW Lightweight Champion (Final), RUSH Syndicate Champion (Final) Wrestling Style Brawler, Old School Flyer Finisher Move The Mullet Drop Finisher Description A swandive headbutt off the top rope. Finisher Setup Move The Mullet Buster Setup Description Emerald Fusion Piledriver (Shoulder Mounted Sit-Out Piledriver) Other Moves Sod Buster - a Diving Sunset Flip Powerbomb (Either from the top rope, or over the ropes, to an opponent standing on the appron) Ball Breaker - Mullet catches the opponent running in, and propells them upwards, in a flapjack like motion, and as they go up, he grabs their hips, and drops to a knee slamming them down into an inverted atomic drop. Ranch Hand (-le Arm Bar)- The victim is sitting, the attacker straddles the one of the victim's shoulders and grabs thier arm. The attacker grabs their arm and pulls up, trying to hyperextend the victim's elbow across the attacker's body. a.k.a. the step over arm bar. Old School Fistin' - 2nd Rope Fist Drop South Dakota Stampede - A running spear-like headbutt, (usually from as far as possible) Thunder Beute - Over Head Face First Powerbomb: (Used for only REALLY light opponents, or women.) Mullet sends a swift kick to the stomach of his opponent, and grabs around the waist and he lifts the victim up so they are sitting on the attacker's shoulders facing the opposite direction. Then Mullet falls backwards so the victim lands face/chest first on the mat. Note: This is done in a smooth motion, with no delay to get the opponent on his shoulders, a very brutal move. The Redneck. (A.K.A. Reverse Calf Branding) - Mullet sets up on the second rope, with the opponent, standing and stunned, he places his knee on the back of the opponent's head, and leaps off the rope, riding them down, and smashing their face into the canvas. Other used moves: Rolling Neck Snap: Opponent's lying on their stommach, Mullet runs through, and grabs their head, yanking it back, as he rolls over the opponents back. Normal & Grappling Note: Headbutts are the main comeback offence, his head is very tough, if there's an oppening to smack his noggin' into his opponent's he'll take it. Headbutts, Snap Suplex, Lariats, Clotheslines, Reverse Elbows, Knee Drop, Running Yakuza Kick (He has cowboy boots on, heh!). Figure Four Leg Lock, Hurricanrana, Spinning Toe Hold (A-la Terry Funk), Drop Toe Hold, Snapmare, Bulldogs, Mircacle Exstacy Bomb (Chokeslam/Powerbomb Combination), Knife Edge Chops, Bear Hug (Note: This rarely works.. and is only done when he gets too caught up in the moment.) Piledriver and The Wheelbarrow Suplex High Flying Flying Elbow Drop (Savage Style.), Drop Kick, All sorts of Lariats, and Body Presses, and planchas. Note: Mullet is an old school high flyer, so he rarely does anything fancier then a plancha.) Pinning Grapples: Flying Crucifix, Forward Legsweep Cradle, Victory Roll, and The Sunset Flip. (All of these can come from anywhere, at any time, to end a match quickly, or get a near fall.) Entrance Music The Phantom Mullet by Five Iron Frenzy
