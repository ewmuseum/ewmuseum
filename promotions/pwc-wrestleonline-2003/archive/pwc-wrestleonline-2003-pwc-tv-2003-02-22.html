PWC Results for Saturday, February 22nd, 2003 Live from the Ferragamo Center in Hammonton, NJ “Okay….gotta be suave and persuasive. Just work it like the runway at the Met in ’93.” As we open up with the shot, we can see that none other than Chandler Tsonda is standing in front of a large door. However, this door has special meaning, because on its exterior label reads, “Ron Starr.” Chandler: Just be smooth. You can do this… With a deep breath, Chandler reaches for the door just as it opens inward, revealing the face of Ron Starr, who takes a look out at the former model. As he recognizes Chandler, he opens the door wider, putting an arm around him. Ron: If it isn’t half of the next tag team champions! As Starr leads him into the office, Chandler gulps and continues in, taking a seat in a chair across from the owner’s seat. There is an overly excited and fake tone to Starr’s voice, to which Chandler can only meekly reply. Chandler: Uhh…yeah….about that… Ron looks around the room, grabbing a fresh new PWC pen and handing it to Tsonda as he sits down across from Chandler. Ron: Here, want one of the new official PWC pens? They write like magic and- Trying to be polite-a characteristic he’s not very used to being-Chandler cuts Ron Starr off, a thing not many men would have the balls to do, but it’s more out of fear than anything else. Chandler: Listen, Ron…ehh…Mr. Starr, I was just coming to talk to you about that tag team title shot you so…generously gave after Jimmy’s little challenge to Mr. TV Evangelist. Laughing insincerely at the joke, Starr doesn’t let Chandler get another word in, continuing to try and encourage the man who must face Penance in the ring. Ron: Well, I think you guys deserve it after all. I mean, after you guys almost got both your asses kick….well, you deserve a shot and personally, I think you guys are going to annihilate the big lug. The owner’s insincerity in trying to get Chandler to help take care of Penance is very obvious, as is Chandler’s fear to explain his dilemma to the man who will be paying him for fighting this match. Chandler: Umm…I really appreciate it….it’s just that I’m a little under the weather. I beat a former OSW’er last week in a brutal match, and I’m feeling a little sore. So, maybe if you wanted to take it a little easy on us this week, I’m sure Salvador and Axtell are free! The smile, fake as it was, has totally faded from Ron Starr’s face as he stares a hole right through Tsonda from across the table. His voice is stone cold and doesn’t waver a bit as it cuts through the falsity that was in the room before. Ron: I don’t care what you have to do. Hairspray, a giant net, or if you two can somehow figure out to… Starr chuckles a bit, then continues. Ron: …actually beat that monster….but you will put a show on tonight….for those tag team titles! Gulping again, Chandler tries to start another weedy rebuttal, but before he can articulate on his cowardly plans, Ron Starr cuts him off once again. His voice is only more uncompromising as he lectures at the man across the table. Ron: You will wrestle for those titles…or you will find yourself selling your pathetic little Gucci shoes outside the arena for your room and board tonight. Before he can even ponder a response, a gut instinct of Chandler’s makes him blurt out accusingly at the man who holds his employment in the grasp of his palm. Chandler: These are not Gucci, idiot. They’re Prada. P-R-A-… “Ladies and gents, one half of your new tag champeens is now in the building!” While Chandler has been in the midst of walking himself towards a quick firing, neither man has detected the presence of a more fashionably dressed than usual Jimmy Nguyen. Sliding across the room, he sits himself down in the second chair across from PWC’s owner. Chandler tries to make himself invisible, after that last outburst towards the owner. Jimmy: Chandler, man, how’s it going? Aren’t you friggin’ pumped to win those tag titles? I mean, PWA’s greatest graduate, the most fashionable man in this whole place, and an owner with an eye for talent…what a triad! And just like that, Starr’s smile reappears right back on his face, this time even greater. However, compared to the owner, Jimmy Nguyen looks like the Cheshire Cat. He leans back in the chair, thinking himself the absolute portrait of coolness. Ron: Jimmy…I’m glad you feel that way. Chandler and I were just talking about how to celebrate once you two have rid me of the problem known as Penance. Chandler now gives a weak smile, as he puts his head into his hands, although neither Ron nor Jimmy seem to notice or care. Jimmy turns to his tag partner and pronounces his happiness loudly. Jimmy: Dude, you’re ready to face Penance? And the way you’d been acting all week, I thought you’d wuss out on me. Mr. Starr, I can’t thank you enough for making Chandler see the light. Now me, I knew all along that we could win, but Chandler still had his doubts. This means there’s nothing left but to go lay a giant beating on that freak, eh ol’ buddy? The other half of 2GVC nudges Chandler, whose head rises from his hands long enough for him to give another weak smile and an exclamation of his “new found confidence.” Chandler: Yeah, I guess I just saw the light of the Starr… Ron and Jimmy both chuckle at the forced joke, as Nguyen stands up to shake hands with the owner. Chandler leaves in the same fashion, but Ron’s smile looks a bit more devious as Chandler shakes his hand. As they close the door behind them, Starr appears in the doorway and calls out. Ron: Good luck on those tag titles, champs! As he hears this, Chandler begins to shake his head, rubbing his hand on his forehead in frusteration. However, as 2GVC walks down the hallway, Jimmy’s all smiles. Jimmy: Dude, light of the Starr? That was a good one, I wish I’d thoughta that! Chandler just continues to shake his head, muttering to himself as 2GVC makes their down to the locker room. Chandler: We’re so screwed… . . . . . . The scene shifts to the parking lot, as a black Renegade Jeep pulls up alongside a young security guard at the backstage entrance to the building that is hosting this week’s installment of PWC. The security guard looks into the driver’s window, and when he recognises who it is gives a smile as he waves the vehicle though. Guard: Evening Mr Phoenix, won’t be stopping you this week, as I realise that you are with the PWC now. Without looking at the guard, Phoenix replies as he drives past. Phoenix: Not quite kid, nearly but not quite. Parking his Jeep, Phoenix steps out, grabbing a thigh length black leather jacket that he puts on over the black Levis and round neck shirt that he is wearing, and pushing some loose hair away from his face he walks in through the backstage door, almost immediately bumping into Jack McGriff, head of secrity for PWC. McGriff: Oh, hi there Phoenix, wasn’t expecting to see you tonight, not after … well you know. Phoenix sighs. Phoenix: Yeah I know Jack, after Starr told me that he wouldn’t hire me. McGriff: Yeah, sorry to hear that Jay. Phoenix: I don’t understand how the medical came back that bad, I mean I feel great, I feel better than I have in years. I even tried doing what Ron said and looked into private insurance, but with everything else that I am responsible for I just can’t afford to pay it! McGriff sighs. McGriff: Tough break man. Phoenix thinks a moment, rubs his chin, looking beyond McGriff into the building, he sees PWC CEO Ron Starr still standing in the doorway, where he just saw off Jimmy Nguyen and Chandler Tsonda. Quick as a cat, Phoenix, slips past McGriff, and scoots inside the building, calling out the Starr. Phoenix: Listen Ron, is there no way at all I can persuade you … Starr’s smile drops as he sees Phoenix. He then raises a hand and interrupts the native American warrior. Starr: Listen Phoenix, I know what you are going to say, and as much as I can understand your desire to compete still you are going to have to face it that if I cannot get you insured, even with all of my contacts in this business. Your wrestling days are over. Phoenix listens, the words sinking in slowly, and then with a sigh he replies. Phoenix: … maybe you are right Ron, maybe it is time I walked away then. I know that this is a lot to ask, but is it possible to get a few minutes air time please? I want to make sure that I say goodbye to the people that have supported me all these years. Starr thinks for a moment. Starr: Sure Jay, I think that can be arranged, hang around and we will get it sorted. Starr turns and walks back into his office, closing the door behind him, leaving a desolate Phoenix alone with his thoughts. . . . . . . . Cameras shift around the arena, taking samples of the crowd. Cameras focus in on several people holding up posters and signs supporting supporting both Angelo Deville and Clay Vendetta, reinforcing the fact that Hammonton is a primarily Italian-American town. Bang: I can’st help it, Malloy. I gots me a hankerin’ for some violence. “Determination Part 1” by God Forbid begins to play throughout the building, drawing a shower of boos as two men walk out from the back side by side, pausing to address the crowd with glares of disgust. Bob: Introducing first, the challengers…at a combined weight of 377 pounds, Jimmy Nguyen, Chandler Tsonda…SECOND GENERATION VIEEEEET COOOOOONG!!! Loosening up in the ring, Chandler leans back against the far corner, not looking to be as pumped as his partner, who is jumping from foot to foot on the other side of the ring. Mark: These two are a disgrace to the entire human race. How dare they make a mockery of poor Porky Bunson. Bang: I hate to break it ta ya, but ole Porky made enough of a mockery o’ himself. He ain’t got no hillbilly guile. Mark: Bah!!! The only solace I can take is that I’m positive that they’ll be entering a world of pain any minute now. “Antichrist Superstar” by Marilyn Manson hits the PA as the lights in the arena dim, prompting the strobe lights near the entrance to the backstage area to begin flashing. In the ring, Nguyen looks defiantly toward the back, but Tsonda seems to appear even more jittery than before. The jeering gets even louder in amplitude as the fans anticipate the arrival of the champion. Mark: This is not going to be a pleasant affair, by any measure. Bang: Course it ain’t…this is wrasslin’, Malloy. Ain’t no room for pleasantness! Bob: And the champion, being accompanied to the ring by “The Prophet” Jacob Solomon…from the Neo-Eden Compound, weighing in at 385 pounds….PEEEEEENAAAAANCE!!! Stepping forth from the back, the 6’9 behemoth lets out a roar as he spreads his arms in a crucifix position. On each shoulder rests a PWC tag belt, the poor lighting still managing to glint off the surface of the straps. Walking out beside him is a rather jovial looking Jacob Solomon. Rubbing his hands together sinisterly, the Prophet leads the way down to the ring to a chorus of boos. Mark: I think the fans may hate these two even more than 2GVC! Bang: Naw, don’t be crazy. That’s impossible…ain’t it? The lights come back on as the two men reach the ring. With one effortless leap, Penance bounds from the ground up onto the ring apron and then easily steps over the top rope. Climbing up the ring steps, Solomon steps through the ropes and joins his protégé in the ring, looking smug as his gaze takes in both members of 2GVC. Mark: Really, though, who is the crowd supposed to cheer for. Take your pick, it’s either the freak or the assholes. Bang: Well, ya see Malloy, in a case like this, I think it comes down to a case of who gots more HILLBILLY GUILE~! Tsonda tries to pull his partner back into the corner to talk strategy, but Nguyen waves him off, instead opting to walk up chest to chest with the monster…actually, chest to navel would be a better approximation. Jimmy doesn’t seem to care about the height difference at all, as he stands looking up at the masked monster as if to challenge him. Bang: See, here we go right off the bat. Jimmy’s showin’ some classic hillbilly guile right here. Mark: Do you mean the complete and utter ego-induced stupidity that goes along with challenging a man nearly over twice your size? Bang: Yeah, that’s what I’m talkin’ bout! Mouthing some words to the big man, Nguyen lifts an arm in the air and points toward himself with his thumb, continuing to speak before ending his unheard monologue with a confident smile. Feigning a turn away, Jimmy whips back and lays a vicious open handed slap across the upper chest of Penance, a loud smack echoing through out the arena. Bang: Whooooooo! That’s some the Ric Flair slappin’ right there! Mark: Shut up, Bang. No WWE references on our show, damn it! Turning away, Jimmy laughs cockily, calling out to ask Tsonda in the corner if he saw that. Chandler slaps his forehead as he screams for Nguyen to turn around. Listening to his partner, Jimmy obliges…to see Penance showing no signs of injury from the attack, or even that he felt the blow at all. Mark: Nguyen is in TROUBLE! Bang: Guess that guile wasn’t much help after all. Mark: Is it ever? Bang: No, I guess not, really. Nguyen tries to backtrack from the monster, but before he can get anywhere a huge right hand connects with his jaw. The Crowd: OOOOOOOOOOOOoooooooooooooooo!!! Spinning 180 degrees, Jimmy’s eyes roll back up into his head as he collapses onto the canvas, unmoving. Mark: THAT WA S A ONE PUNCH KNOCK-OUT!!! Bang: Shiiiiiit....Jimmy just got dog gone punched outta his boots! Nguyen lifts his head up from the mat, but can make no attempt to rise. The camera focuses on him to reveal his eyes are glazed over as he looks up at the referee with an expressionless gaze. Mark: My god…I have never seen a punch back that much power before in my life. Nguyen can’t even stand after that one! Bang: Penance is the Lennox Lewis of tha P-Dubbya-C…and Solomon is the Don King! Penance advances slowly on the downed Nguyen, but before he can bend over to grab a hold of him, Jimmy is pulled out of the ring. Dragging his partner out by the wrist, Chandler positions himself under Nguyen’s arm and begins to walk him to the back, shaking his head in disbelief. Bang: Wha?! Where they going? Mark: They’re getting out of dodge, and after what we have just seen, I don’t blame them. The fans begin to boo loudly at the departing challengers, but Tsonda waves them off as he concentrates on getting Jimmy to the back. They disappear behind the curtains as the crowd chants. The Crowd: “YOU’RE A PUSSY! *clap, clap, clap clap clap* YOU’RE A PUSSY! *clap, clap, clap clap clap*” Bang: Boooo! Rip-off!!! Mark: What are you complaining about, Bang? You’re paid to be here. Just think if you were one of these fans who doled out their hard earned money to see this show!!! Bang: You’re right…sucks ta be them! Chandler comes backtracking out from the curtain just seconds later, with a startled look on his face. The reason for his surprise is made clear as out from the back step Jim Axtell and Totakoxe Salvador, the looks on their faces just a bit smug as they effectively chase Tsonda back toward the ring. Mark: IT’S JIM AXTELL AND TOTAKOXE SALVADOR!!! Bang: Aw naw…they don’t need to be showing their faces out here! Mark: THEY ARE FORCING TSONDA AND NGUYEN BACK TO THE RING!!! Dropping Jimmy to allow himself more speed, Chandler just barely avoids being caught by Axtell’s outreached hands. His partner isn’t so lucky, however, as Salvador delivers a boot to the head of the already out of it Nguyen. Mark: Tsonda abandoning his partner in his escape!!! I wonder how Nguyen will feel about that tomorrow!!! Bang: Hey, it’s better than Chandler get outta thar than neither of them. That’s basic survival…you city folk’d never understand. Climbing up on the apron, Chandler warns Axtell, raising a fist from his elevated height in an attempt to keep him at bay. Bang: Chandler, look out behind ya! Tsonda’s expression goes from one of determination to horror, however, as a massive hand cups his head. The crowd lets out a cheer at the anticipated brutality as Penance drags Chandler over the top rope by his skull. Mark: Axtell didn’t quite get ahold of Tsonda, but I’m sure he won’t be disappointed when he sees what will happen to him in the ring alone with Penance!!! Bang: I think we’re goin’ ta see Chandler modeling a full body cast after this big’un gets done with him!!! As Tsonda lands butt first on the canvas, he rolls away in pain. Mark: Chandler using his speed…that’s his best hope at this point!!! Bang: I hate it when people play hard ta get…like this one time I was tryin’ to get inta my cousin’s pants an… Mark: (interrupting) Please…just stop right now. When he does so, he is met with a corkscrew body press from Tsonda…that just bounces right off his chest, as if he didn’t even feel it. Mark: Penance reacted to that move as if Chandler were nothing more than a fly! Bang: This don’t look too good fo’ Chandler. Tsonda lands on the canvas hard, holding his head in pain as he attempts to get to his feet. Unfortunately, he doesn’t move fast enough, as Penance scoops him up and climbs to the second rope, holding Chandler horizontally across his chest. Then, falling backwards and throwing Tsonda back over his head, he launches the Vietnamese grappler nearly the entire distance across the ring. Mark: Super fall-away slam!!! Bang: I ain’t never seen no one tossed that far before! Mark: Spinning inverted DDT!!! On the outside, Salvador slams Nguyen’s head into the ring post one last time before rolling him into the ring. He meets O’Neil halfway his feet and rocks him with a left hand, followed by another, and the Scarecrow starts sending back a few of his own, trying to fight to his feet. They go shot for shot for a minute, vying for the advantage, until O’Neil gets his other arm up to block Deville’s shot and drills him square. Angelo staggers back under the assault, unable to answer, until O’Neil whips him into the ropes. He comes charging back and finds Jason waiting with his head lowered, then hurtles through the air from a backdrop! Mark: I’ll bet ya Angelo wishes he would’ve taken more precautions earlier... Bang: Fer what? Mark: To make sure O’Neil didn’t escape his pals! I don’t think Deville had this in mind tonight! O’Neil presses his advantage, grabbing Deville’s hair and pulling him to his feet, but the next instant he’s crumpled forward, holding his groin, and the referee is shouting exasperated warnings at Deville! Mark: Damn that Deville! Does he have no scruples? Bang: Yup, lots of ‘em... An’ dey’s fightin’ fer ‘im too! O’Neil stagger to the ropes and grasps hold with one hand, his other placed firmly on his aching groin. He winces in pain, trying to regain his composure before Angelo can take advantage. Deville meanwhile is hunched in the opposite corner, breathing heavily through sick smile, almost smirking at his dirty work. O’Neil sees this and grits his teeth before moving back towards Deville. Mark: Time’s up! That barely even fazed O’Neil! He’s on a mission! Bang: Ta get fisted! O’Neil be in a world of pain! Mark: You mean Deville more like it, look! Bang: Dang! Deville is up in the corner, and O’Neil is right on top of him, pounding away with closed fists square on the jaw. The crowd is fully behind him, urging on his every blow, and it seems to feed the Scarecrow the extra energy he needs. He stops punching and hits an Irish whip, sending Deville crashing into the opposite turnbuckle stomach first, falling back out for O’Neil to pull him down in the reverse DDT. There is a boom as they hit the canvas, the crowd roaring up while both men roll to their sides. O’Neil is the first get back to his feet, turning and stomping away at Deville’s back again, moving him towards the ropes. Mark: O’Neil setting the pace and tone here, he’s got Deville right where he wants him! Crawling like a dog! Bang: An’ he be cheatin’ again Mark, he be cheatin’ I swear! Mark: Are you watching the same match as I am? O’Neil picks up Deville now and locks him up in a grapple, then takes him down with a snap suplex. He vaults to his feet and brings Deville up again, switching behind for a German suplex. He does this again, the crowd really roaring up now behind him. He tries for a third but Deville has recovered and counters, moving behind before delivering a German suplex of his own. Instantly the crowd is sapped of their happiness, booing as Deville gets to his feet, looking very smug even in his disoriented state. O’Neil is breathing hard on the mat and Deville adds insult to injury by kicking at O’Neil lightly with his boot before spitting on him. Mark: Now that was unnecessary! Bang: I don’t see nuttin’ wrong wit’ it, after all dis cheatin’! Deville grabs hold of a leg and pulls O’Neil to the ropes. He places O’Neil’s ankle on the bottom rope, hanging it there, before jumping and landing down hard on the leg, sending jolts of pain shooting up O’Neil’s body. He sits up in shock and grits his teeth while the referee tells Deville to break the hold. Deville ignores him and brings the leg higher, placing it on the second rope now. Deville calmly gets on the apron on the outside and looks out, smiling again before grabbing the leg and dropping viciously to the ringside area. O’Neil howls in pain this time, while the crowd begins to boo loudly at Deville, who’s sauntering around with his arms outstretched in reference to his greatness. Mark: This is sick! Deville has no care for anybody or anything, it’s simply disgusting! He could’ve popped O’Neil’s knee out! Bang: He dun be fixin’ him right up for the slaughter. Mark: I hope Starr is watching this, and I hope Deville gets fined for his blatant attempt to injure O’Neil. Bang: How ‘bout all dem closed fists from O’Neil? More fines, ya reckon? O’Neil is laying in the centre of the ring now, clutching his leg in pain and refusing to be baited to the outside. Deville walks up the steps slowly and climbs back into the ring, still smiling at him. He moves over and grabs the leg viciously, then drops an elbow across it, O’Neil wincing and screaming out and battering uselessly at Deville with his arms. The Universal Champion does it again and again, trying to cripple the leg. Finally he halts, the crowd in a state of uproar at this man, though they likely wouldn’t call him one. Deville pulls the legs and spins round, falling into the figure four leglock, clamping it shut as O’Neil begins to grit his teeth and think of a way out of it. Deville sits up meanwhile and looks at him, arrogant in his victory. Bang: Deville dun worked dat leg over like dey be related! Smart rasslin’! Mark: Oh no, this doesn’t look good at all... O’Neil is in severe pain. C’mon Scarecrow! You can escape! Bang: Well back at the farm the only way to loose a cow is to break its leg… Mark: That’s fine Bang, that’s fine. Bang: But it’s true, you’ve got to grab it by… Mark: I get you! O’Neil’s not a cow, and this isn’t the farm! Bang: ...what?! The referee is looking in O’Neil’s direction, probing for signs of wanting to quit, and is met with violently head-shaking and cries to let the match continue. He won’t give in. While the referee continues to check, Deville decides to add a bit of leverage, holding onto the ropes and making O’Neil thrash out in pain. The referee turns, but cool as a cat and sinuous as a viper Deville lets go, idly looking away, no hint of malice except for the braying crowd in the background who saw the entire thing. Mark: What a complete cheater! Come on O’Neil! Bang: I dun never saw no cheatin’ there... It only be illegal if ya get caught! O’Neil winces and digs deep as he turns the move slowly on its head. Deville tries to hold it in place but O’Neil manages to flip them over, Angelo now in pain as the crowd cheers, the karma coming right back to hit him in the face. The pain is only passing though as Deville grabs the ropes, and O’Neil releases the hold, grasping to the remnants of his strict code. Mark: Now there’s a gentleman! There’s a sportsman! Even with everything Deville has done, O’Neil still follows the rules! Bang: You dun be a fruit, Mark... Dat’s all I gots to say. Deville pulls himself to his feet and dodges the hobbling O’Neil, who is heavily favouring his right leg. Deville launches himself suddenly and brings a forearm crashing into the side of his head. O’Neil is waiting though and pulls him in, taking him over with a belly to belly. As he does so his leg crumples from beneath him though, and Deville only gets halfway over in the arc before being dropped on his feet, no harm done. Mark: No! Deville’s despicable actions have taken their toll on O’Neil’s leg. It’s not strong enough for him to hit any of his trademark suplexes. Bang: What I dun say? Smart rasslin’! An’ not illegal, neither, like closed fists! Deville laughs at the weakened O’Neil before bringing the boots crashing into his knee, focussing more on the leg, making O’Neil suffer. He moves quickly to the ropes and holds on tight but Deville continues stomping anyway, taking as long as possible before the count is up to stop him and the referee physically interjects himself. Letting himself be cowed, Deville moves back and pulls O’Neil towards the centre of the ring, then scoops him up in his arms and breaks him over his knee! Hooking the leg, Deville reefs back for all he’s worth! Bang: Anudder win fer Angelo! Mark: O’Neil won’t give up... He can’t give up! 1... 2... ...Shoulder up! Deville slaps the canvas in rapid succession and bitches about slow counts, then climbs to his feet and pulls O’Neil up with him by the back of the tights. O’Neil stumbles on his bad leg, but Deville yanks his arm down between his legs before he can stumble, then whips him up overhead, holds him there for a second, and plants him with a pumphandle piledriver! The wound from earlier opens up and begins leaking his blood onto the canvas as Deville makes a sloppy cover. 1... 2... Bang: O’Neil be out cold! ...Kickout! Mark: NO! He kicked out! Frustrated, Deville rolls out of the ring to the outside. McGoosen follows him, lecturing him about maintaining decorum and not throwing the match away, but Deville spares him only a smirk as he folds up a steel chair and makes to re-enter the ring. The ref is steadfast in staying in Deville’s face, cutting him off from the ring, but the Crimson Angel solves that problem by casually shoving him out of the way with one hand. The crowd suddenly explodes into a chorus of cheers. Mark: What the?! Bang: Aw fer... Mark: Wh–?! Oh my god! Look! Clay Vendetta is already halfway down the ramp, charging at full speed with a look of angry purpose painting his face. As the crowd begins a “Clas-sic” chant, Vendetta reaches ringside and charges for Deville, who has spotted him by now and is waiting with the chair cocked back. Bang: More cheatin’ by yer “good guys”... Mark: Maybe Clay is finally responding to Angelo’s challenge! Or maybe he’s just sick of all the cheating! Bang: Maybe he dun got gay wit’ O’Neil! McGoosen scrambles out of the way completely as Deville swings the chair hard at Vendetta, who somersaults underneath it easily and pops up behind him, hooking his head from behind and pulling him straight up in the air, making him let go of the chair, then drilling him to the floor with an inverted brainbuster! Bang: Dis be 2 on 1! DQ! Angelo wins! Mark: No way! McGoosen is letting it go on! O’Neil is barely up on his knees in the ring as McGoosen asks Vendetta to leave ringside. Vendetta picks Deville up and tries sending him into the ring, but Deville counters the move and whips Vendetta the other way, sandwiching the ref between him and the guardrail. Flimsy as a wet piece of paper, the referee predictably falls to the ground, unconscious and near dead. Bang: Hoowee! Now da real fun begins! Mark: Vendetta’s still up! Clay bounces off the referee and turns in one motion, striking back at Deville with a kick to the midsection that doubles him over, leaving him prone for a knee to the face. As Angelo stumbles back, holding his nose, Vendetta is on him again and this time manages to force him into the ring, where O’Neil is now waiting. O’Neil just stands there, though, staring at Deville as if waiting for him to recover. Mark: What’s he doing? Finish it, O’Neil! He’d do it to you! Bang: You uppity cityfolk are all such darned hypocrits... Vendetta shouts at O’Neil to get moving, then searches around suddenly, snatching the chair up and throwing it into the ring to him. O’Neil catches it mechanically and looks at it long and hard, a look of confusion marking his dilemma plainly. The referee is still down, but Deville is back on his feet now, crouched halfway across the ring like a cornered rat. Vendetta is shouting bloody murder at O’Neil, pointing frantically at Deville and miming chair shots... Mark: Bah! He can’t do it! Even with everything Angelo has done to him, he can’t bring himself to cheat! Bang: He dun been cheatin’ all night! An’ hit Deville wit’ a chair jus’ last week! O’Neil continues staring at the chair, and Deville bolts across the ring in a flash, jumping up and dropkicking it into the Scarecrow’s face, where it hits with a clang and goes flying to the side. O’Neil crumples where he stands, falling to the mat like a heap of empty clothes. Mark: NO! He should’ve done it! Damn you, O’Neil! You had him! Vendetta begins rolling to the ring, but suddenly Toomes is charging down the ramp like a bull, eyes locked on the Classic one as if ready to fry him with lasers. Clay hears the booing and turns around to see Seabass just as he gets to his feet inside the ring. He shouts something, but the word dies half out of his mouth as Deville runs up to him from behind and plasters him across the back with the chair, spilling him over the top rope to the outside, arching his back and hurt, at Toomes’ mercy. Mark: What the hell is Toomes doing out here? There’s no love lost between him and Deville! Bang: I reckon dere be less ‘atween him and Vendetta, though... Or if I was you I could dun pretend it be noble, an’ say it be ta even da odds! Mark: Shut up. Toomes stomps away at Vendetta’s face and neck as Deville stalks back to the blood-drenched O’Neil, brandishing the chair with malice. O’Neil, even with his eyes closed, is trying to struggle to his feet. His injured leg just won’t work, though, and he’s more than a little disoriented besides. He stumbles forward on hands and knees, and there is a sickening crack as Deville brings the chair down into the back of his head like he’s chopping wood. The crowd gasps as one. Mark: HE HIT HIM WITH THE EDGE OF THE CHAIR! THAT’S A CONCUSSION FOR SURE!!! Bang: An’ what O’Neil dun deserve! Unconscious, O’Neil crumples to the mat and Deville throws the chair out to Toomes on an impulse. Sebastian snags it out of the air and begins jabbing it into Vendetta’s ribs, almost teasingly, playing with him like a cat does a mouse. Mark: ARE THOSE TWO DIRTBAGS WORKING TOGETHER?!?! Bang: Dey’s gots a common goal, Mark. Grabbing O’Neil’s ankles in his hands, Deville plants his feet in his groin and locks in the Soprano. The Scarecrow is already unconscious, however, so he hangs there limply, feeling no pain, as Deville tries to neuter him. Bang: DA SORPANO!!! I DUN BE OVER!!! Mark: THERE’S NO REF!!!! Bang: Mark... Trust me. It be over. On the outside, Vendetta grabs the chair and wins the tug-of-war with Toomes, then swings it wildly at Seabass’ body, chasing him away enough for Clay to get his feet under him. Dropping the chair, he lunges at Toomes with a clothesline. Ducking smoothly underneath, Toomes grabs hold of him from behind and runs with him, driving him facefirst into the ringpost! Bang: WHAT A GREAT NIGHT!!! Mark: PATHETIC NIGHT IS MORE LIKE IT!!! VENDETTA MAY BE BUSTED OPEN, BUT HE ISN’T DONE YET!!! McGoosen gets shakily to his feet on the outside and starts when he sees what’s going on in the ring. Stumbling forward on his unsure referee’s footing, giving his head little shakes as if he can’t see or think clearly, he rolls into the ring. Mark: NO! Stay down! Bang: (smirking) Oh, how da mighty ‘ave fallen. Toomes mounts Vendetta’s chest and grabs a fistful of hair, then pounds his other fist mercilessly into Clay’s exposed face, spurting blood from his mouth and nose. O’Neil is still straddled atop Deville’s feet, limp as a blind 80-year-old quadriplegic. McGoosen rolls to his feet, sees it, and doesn’t even bother raising his arm before calling for the bell. Mark: But he cheated! He cheated! Bang: Clay dun throwed the chair into da ring... An’ Clay dun knocked out da referee... The bell rings as Toomes continues his beating on Vendetta outside the ring, savagely pounding him without any of his usual thoughts for style and showmanship, and Deville refuses to let the unconscious and soaking red O’Neil down. Winner: Angelo Deville Mark: WHAT A DESPICABLE WAY TO END OUR TELECAST!!! WE WILL SEE YOU NEXT WEEK ON PWC TV!!! GOOD NIGHT EVERYONE!!! Bang: Da American way! The PWC TV logo materialises in the centre of the screen as Toomes and Deville continue relentlessly, unwary of any revenge they may be calling down on themselves... Mark: WE ARE ABOUT TO LOSE OUR FEED!!! WE WILL SEE YOU NEXT SATURDAY NIGHT ON PWC TV!!! GOOD NIGHT EVERYONE!!! Large styrofoam cups and various other debris begin flying through the air…..as the scene fades to black… . . . . . . Or does it? . . . . . . . The scene fades up and a nervous looking Ron Starr is shown exiting the Ferragamo Center, with Jack McGriff at his side. McGriff: Don’t worry, Mr. Starr. I will found out who the guy was and take care of him. Starr: You’d better, McGriff, because I am not paying you to… Suddenly, the ringing of mobile phone causes Starr to jump nearly out of his skin. He and McGriff look at one another, then Starr, with shaky hands answers the phone. Starr: (cautiously) Yeah… Starr’s face changes from one of worry to a big smile. Starr: Yo dude!!! You got my message!!! McGriff looks at Starr as if to say “Who is it?” but Starr just turns away from him. Starr: Yeah……..yeah……Oh, man that is great news!!!! You know where the place is?….. Of course you do!!! Starr gives McGriff a thumbs up, who stares back at him, unsure of what is going on. Starr: Great…great….Jordan, you are a life saver man!!! See you next week!!! Starr closes his mobile phone and turns to McGriff…as the © 2003 Pro Wrestling Circuit logo appears at the lower, center portion of the screen… Starr: Crime Dog….We got ourselves a bonafide, African American superstar!!! ….as the show fades to black.
