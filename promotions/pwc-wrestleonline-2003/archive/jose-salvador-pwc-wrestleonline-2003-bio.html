Jose Salvador Biography Nicknames Señor Salvador Hometown Buenos Aires, Argentina Birthday 11th March 1959 Height 5 ft. 10 Weight 190 Biography Jose Salvador didn't live an easy life by any means. The ex-wrestler, turned single unemployed father had trouble keeping control of his two teenage sons. Things, however, are turning a lot brighter for Jose now... Born on a cold December night of 1977 in the capital of Argentina, Totakoxe Salvador came into the world to a pair of loving parents - Jose and Maria Salvador. When he was three years old, his baby brother was brought into the world. However, only several weeks into Pablo’s life, his mother was found dead. Distraught by the death of his wife, Jose Salvador went missing for three days, leaving the hospital to take care of the 3 week old Pablo and the 3 year old Tota. Although seemingly oblivious to the happenings around him, Totakoxe was mentally scarred and would continue to be for his next few years of life. A single father – not any normal single father, brought up Tota and Pablo. They were brought up by a father in the wrestling business. He was constantly on the road with his two children touring with him. They had no set home and lived on a bus the majority of the time. This isolation has followed Totakoxe throughout his life. “Where’s mommy?” he asked day after day. “She’s at home resting, son” Jose would often reply. Truth was, Jose asked himself that very question day in, day out. He missed his wife just as much as his children did. His wrestling career underwent a downward spiral as Jose tried to juggle raising two kids and wrestling. Finally, the pressure became too much for him to handle and he left the wrestling business in Argentina. Now seven years of age, Tota couldn’t wait to return to their home. “Mommy will be at home, mommy will be at home!” he said anxiously to his four-year old brother, Pablo. Unfortunately Jose did not hear this and the return home was a miserable occasion for the 3 Salvadors. That very day, Jose broke his own heart for the final time as he explained to his children just what had happened to their mother. Years went by as the Salvadors lived it large on the money Jose had saved through wrestling. Pablo was Tota’s best friend and Tota was Pablo’s best friend. Outside the triangle of family, the two teenagers had no friends. In their minds, they didn’t need or want friends – they had each other and that’s all that had mattered to them. When Tota became 16 years of age, Jose was struggling to keep control of both him and Pablo. He enlisted the help of various babysitters but none of the long list could control the rebellious duo. Through Jose’s list of links to wrestling federations, he sent Totakoxe to the United States of America. One of Jose’s long-time friends had taken the task of making sure Tota went to school and got a thorough education. Meanwhile, in the South American country of Argentina, Pablo was under the constant control of his overcautious parent. The years went by and Totakoxe Salvador failed in school. He spent his days in school daydreaming about girls, cars and alcohol. He was a rather attractive teenager and this was reflected in the amount of attention he received from the ladies. However, still wary of the death of his mother, he was frightened of becoming attached to a girl in case the same scenario would arise. He lived an isolated life. A life locked up inside a run-down apartment in the heart of L.A. He still had no friends but now the loss of his family was getting the worst of him. Little is known about Pablo and Jose’s life in Argentina while Totakoxe was in the US of A. Tota attempted a full-time job as a factory worker but was fired only a week into the job for picking a fight with a fellow immigrant. What could Tota succeed at? What was the one thing his family had excelled in the past? WRESTLING. Totakoxe joined a well-known wrestling academy known as Primetime Wrestling Academy. Only several weeks into his training, his father and younger brother paid a visit to him in Los Angeles. They unleashed a huge surprise to Totakoxe, which was that they had moved from Argentina to stay with him! Jose also announced that he would train Totakoxe to the top of the wrestling world. Several weeks passed and Tota was becoming more and more of a wrestler. With the help of his father, he was deemed a success by the PWA and was soon packing his bags to move to PWC. Albeit a small federation, Tota recognised the potential of this fed and despite having to move across the country to wrestle there, jumped at the chance. He is still awaiting his debut in PWC, but the company has high hopes for him. Jose remains Totakoxe's manager to this date and is hoping to mix his vintage wrestling style with Tota's submission style to make a new star. Management Style Provides Tactical Advice/Moral Support Finisher Move No Finisher Setup Move Yes Other Tactics Will only attack opponent if he is attacked. If attacked, Jose will fight back with; - Defensive strikes/Reversals - Slap to the face - Vertical Suplex - Clothesline He will only attack when the referee is distracted and will do nothing to severely injure the opponent, only daze them. If attacked by two or more opponents, he will do his best to avoid the situation. Not out of cowardice but through wisdom. If someone injures his son, Tota, in a match-up or elsewhere, Jose would see it fit to punish the attacker... not through violence but through mind games and outsmarting the opponent.
