Penance Biography Nicknames The Great Purger, The Dark Atoner, The Masked Monster Hometown Neo-Eden Compound, New Mexico Birthday Unknown Height 6'9 Weight 385 Fed Titles PWC Tag Team Champion: February 8, 2003 to April 5, 2003 Fed Awards First ever PWC Tag Team Champion, #12 WrestleOnline rankings (Jan/Feb '03) Biography In these times of moral decadence and utter disregard for one's fellow man, where it seems as if nothing is sacred, there still exists such a thing capable of evoking pure terror in the minds of men. Drawing up this fear from the deepest recesses of the soul, this thing brings it to the forefront of the psyche, where it takes root and dwells. Nurtured by hollow feelings of guilt, such horror soon becomes accompanied by empty premonitions of a violent atonement that is yet to come. This sense of unease lingers even after thoughts shift to more pleasant topics. Once introduced into the mind, it can never be removed. It becomes as inherent as the most basic of human instincts. One can try to deny it...try to tell themselves that it is nothing more than drivel and tripe, but doubts will always lurk. Preventing certainty, clouding judgment, infesting throughout the consciousness. What is it that could weigh so heavy on the hearts of men? The answer to that question...is Penance. Wrestling Style Powerhouse/Shootfighting Finisher Move Ideological Crucifixion Finisher Description Picking his opponent up into a crucifix powerbomb position, Penance tosses them up into the air, rushing forward and catching them with a vicious piledriver in one fell swoop Finisher Setup Move Sinfall Setup Description Penance lifts his opponent up in gorilla press, before dropping them into a kneeling tombstone piledriver Other Moves Axe kick Bearhug Canadian backbreaker Charging big boot Fallaway slam delivered from the second turnbuckle Fisherman suplex into an Orange Crush bomb Flying body press Inverted DDT with a 180 degree rotation Lifting chokehold Lifting dragon sleeper Powerbombs of all variations (normal, running, diving, sit-out, chokehold, tiger) Powerslam Press slams Pumphandle slam Running lariat Short arm chokeslam Side suplex Sidewalk slam Spinning Death Valley Driver Surfboard Tilt-a-whirl gutbuster While he relies on the moves listed above, much of Penance's offense is centered around a shootfighting based approach. His big punches are his most lethal weapon, carrying enough impact behind them to turn most of his opponents into quivering, unconscious wrecks. He enjoys slamming his opponents to the mat with spears and takedowns and simply tearing them apart with mounted punches. If he can back them into a corner, he will not hesitate to mercilessly beat them down into the mat. Elbowstrikes and kneelifts provide other main facets of his striking offense. Penance is very intense when engaged in combat, and will often get carried away in the bloodlust of battle, which, when combined with his massive size, proves to be too much for most to stand up against in brawling situations.
