A battered, dented bumper pulls to a quick stop in front of the camera as PWC TV comes on the air. As the view starts panning out, we can see that the whole front of the maroon car looks like a crinkled piece of paper, and the hood has a nasty bend long-ways down the middle. Drawing further away from the vehicle, as the headlights die, we can see the driver’s side door fling open. As the interior lights respond, playing their part of dim halo, we can see Paula Michelle Smith behind the wheel, with Ariath off to her right. Giggling happily, Paula takes the keys out of the ignition, and hops out of the car. As her feet meet the asphalt, she is already shoving the keys into a little handbag. Coming around the front of the car, Ariath shuts her door behind her. Paula: I have been driving for only a few months, and I am already ‘better than you.’ There is something dark about her last three words, and she immediately starts laughing. Closing his eyes and making a big show of it, Ariath quickly joins in, and continues laughing even after Paula has stopped. When he finally opens his eyes, pretending to wipe a tear, he finds his gaze resting on Paula’s outstretched handbag. The mirth quickly vacates his facial features. Paula: Surely, you do not expect me to carry it. Reluctantly, he takes the handbag, and starts walking towards the entrance. Paula (mocking): What a pretty purse, for a pretty lady! Ariath sighs, and doesn’t look back as Paula hustles to catch up with him. Paula (musing): You are as much a pretty lady as Karina Wolfenden. She shudders at the thought of the K-Wolf. Paula: What the heck is with that hair? Pick one damn color and stick with it, please. Ariath largely ignores his mistress’ rantings. Paula (continuing): And the clothes, and the make-up… and that damnable obsession with pink. Seriously, anybody can look almost-decent if they BUY their appearances. But I see right through all that, to her ugly, bony… … inner-skankiness. They get to the door, and Paula throws a grandiose gesture towards it. Paula: Ariath, would you be a gentleman for once in your life? Ariath moves towards the door. Paula (mocking again): That purse really brings out the color of your eyes… As Paula’s puppet opens the doors for her, the couple are met by the sight of Karina Wolfenden standing guard over the corridor in front of them. A stern look on her face, Karina’s hands are buried deep in her pockets, she isn’t budging. Paula looks to Ariath with displeasure. Paula: I cannot say the first impressions of this place are very appealing, can you? Ariath just shakes his head, grunting at Karina. Un-moved by the pair’s attempt to dismiss her, Karina just focuses her attention on Paula, bluntly and directly addressing her. Karina: We need to talk... Her emerald eyes slowly drift over to the menacing figure of Ariath who stands beside Paula, his fists clenched. Karina: Preferably somewhere that doesn’t allow animals. Taunting him with a flash of a smile, Karina knows he won’t respond without an order from Paula. The young woman tries to brush Karina aside. Paula: I believe I have nothing more to say to you, Miss Wolfenden. Karina’s hands come out of her pockets as she adopts an open stance, showing she has nothing to hide and no ulterior motives. Her body language is in no way aggressive, it’s more passive. Karina: Look, about all that stuff you said last week, abou- Before she can finish, Paula sharply cuts her off, seething. Paula: You do not need to remind me what I said last week, Miss Wolfenden. After all, I lived it. Now, remove yourself from my path, I have more pressing engagements. PMS just walks right past Karina, blanking her. In desperation, wanting to try and resolve things, Karina grabs Paula’s upper arm with her gloved hand. Paula: Unhand me this instant. Karina looks right through Paula’s eyes with sincerity. Karina: I want to hel- However, before she can even finish her plea, Ariath jumps into defence mode, barging Karina away from Paula. PMS’s puppet pushes Karina against the wall, his forearm over her throat so that the boarder’s face turns red. Paula: What you really want to do, Miss Wolfenden, is leave me alone. Come, Ariath. Obeying the command, Ariath retreats away from Karina and she grabs her throat and coughs, watching the couple leave but not seeming as if she’s giving up.
