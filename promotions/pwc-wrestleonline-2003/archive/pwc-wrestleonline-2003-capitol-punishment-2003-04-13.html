Capitol Punishment 2003, 13 April 2003

13 April 2003

Capitol Punishment Sunday, April 13th, 2003 Sovereign Bank Arena, Trenton, NJ Live and Only on Pay Per View Lightening crashes, briefly illuminating the darkness of a cold, slate-stone room to reveal the cold steel lines of the ultimate penalty the United States justice system has to offer… …the electric chair. Slowly the image fades out to a clip of Magnum Wolf nailing an Electric Chair Drop on an unwitting opponent. That scene is quickly interrupted, however, as the gleaming blade of a guillotine falls from above, towards the neck of a soon-to-be unfortunate victim. Before the blade can strike the killing blow, however, the scene fades out to Totakoxe Salvador nailing Chandler Tsonda with a devastating guillotine leg drop. Finally the scene shifts to the wells of syringes attached to a lethal injection machine, as their deadly chemicals are released through a series of clear tubes, on a one way trip to the veins of a condemned individual. That scene quickly fades to the Masked Monster, Penance, delivering a devastating pile driver to Sebastian Toomes. The beast roars out in rages, as the shot fades to black, as the words “Capitol Punishment” suddenly stamp themselves onto the screen….as the scene once again fades to black. . . . . . . . . The scene opens up on an unknown location somewhere in the backstage area of the Sovereign Bank Arena. Along the white walls are flyers and posters advertising not only this Pay Per View but also the one to come, Last Man Standing. The concrete floors of the area are the origin of footsteps that is picked up by the microphone. The footsteps continue while the camera remains still, and at the same angle. Suddenly the shadow of a man is cast onto the concrete floor. From outside the confinement of the camera steps a man… but not just any man – One half of the PWC Tag Team Champions, Jimmy Nguyen. His figure takes up the majority of the camera as he stops his walk. His calm facial expression immediately changes to an awkward smile as he stands still. His focus is on something beyond the camera. Jimmy: So glad you could make it, this evening. Jimmy chuckles at his own statement and after herding no response from off-camera, speaks once again. Jimmy: You know, I haven’t forgotten about your little rendezvous with me last night! Nguyen’s face changes to express anger. This time, a voice comes hurdling towards him. Voice: Ah, so you remember? Nguyen is slightly taken aback by the question and walks closer to the person off-camera. As Jimmy walks towards him, the camera zooms out, showing none other than Jimmy Nguyen’s biggest rival… Totakoxe Salvador! The Argentinean has his dreadlocks tied back and looks less than content with ‘bumping’ into Jimmy Nguyen. However, cheers from the fans seep through backstage. Jimmy: Bring it on, Tota, bring it on! Tota: Save it for the match, Nguyen. Jimmy Nguyen edges closer to Totakoxe Salvador, the Viet Cong standing just slightly taller than ‘The One in Demand’. Jimmy stops a meter or two away from Salvador. Jimmy: You know, I SHOULD save it for the match but what the heck, I’ll enjoy beating you… RIGHT NOW! Tota: Calm down, calm down… ASSHOLE! Totakoxe’s words make Jimmy Nguyen turn bright red in anger. His face scrunches up even more as the fans start an ‘Asshole’ chant. Tota: Well that makes A LOT of people who think you’re nothing but an asshole! Jimmy: You’re all talk, Salva-bore! Jimmy Nguyen is still pissed off at Salvador and slowly creeps closer towards him. He is now within only inches of the man he is set to face in only a short time. Tota: We can fight now if you please. But if things turn ugly, Trenton and I would hate to see you forfeit that little Tag Team Title match that’s scheduled later. The Trenton, New Jersey fans burst into uncontrollable cheers. Jimmy, on the other hand, turns his back to Totakoxe and whispers into himself. Jimmy: I forgot about that damn Title match! Tota hears the slight murmur of Jimmy’s voice. Tota: What was that, Jamesy? Jimmy spins on his heels and face Totakoxe. He is quick to cover up his statement, as he retorts with an on-the-spot comment. Jimmy: I was just saying how I’d love to kick your ASS RIGHT DAMN NOW! Tota: Oh gee, that’s smart. Obviously at the end of his tether, Jimmy protrudes his forehead and sends it against Totakoxe’s. Totakoxe is not moving back, away from the fight and is doing his best to withstand Jimmy’s head, which is pushing ever so slightly forward. Suddenly, Jimmy swings his arm back, ready to punch Salvador but someone stops his hand. The camera fails to pick up the person but shows Jimmy turning round to face the man. Trying to find out who it was, the camera pans to the right to show Chandler Tsonda, holding Jimmy’s clenched fist in his hand. Chandler: Save it for the match, Jimmy. Jimmy: No way. We’re going at it… RIGHT THIS MINUTE! Eager to calm Jimmy’s hot temper, Chandler tries to encourage him to wait. Chandler: Look at it this way. You wait until the match, you can finish it quickly with minimal damage and THEN we can go on to successfully defend the Tag Titles. Think about it. Jimmy twists his neck, looking at Totakoxe, then back at Chandler Tsonda. Jimmy: You’re right - he’s not worth it. I’ll wait until the match and get it over and done with A-S-A-P! Chandler releases the grip of Jimmy’s clenched fist, obviously comfortable that nothing between Jimmy and Tota will happen until the match. Totakoxe laughs at 2GVC as they walk off. Tota: It’s all the same to me. See you later, amigo! Jimmy turns his head back and shouts obscenities at him. Jimmy: Yeah, I will (bleep) see you later, you mother(bleep)… Chandler: Calm it, Jimmy. As the Tag Team disappear from the range of the camera, it remains on Totakoxe who is also starting to walk off in the opposite direction. Tota: Definitely under the thumb! He quietly laughs into himself, walks off the camera and gets ready for both his and Nguyen’s BIGGEST match of their wrestling career. The scene fades on the poster, on the wall, displaying the advertised card for Capitol Punishment but more importantly, Totakoxe Salvador vs. Jimmy Nguyen! . . . . . . . . . . . The scene shifts to the inside of the Sovereign Bank Arena in Trenton, NJ, as pyros, yes pyros, shoot off from the ringposts, ceiling and entryway and “Panama” by Van Halen hits the PA system. Cameras scan the arena taking samples of the sell out crowd of 7000 fans, who are absolutely going bonkers, with supporters of Angelo Deville, Sebastian Toomes and Clay Vendetta each getting plenty of air time. After continuing to show supporters of each of the three men who will battle it out for the PWC World Heavyweight Title, the scene shifts to the ringside position of the OSW announce team. Mark Malloy: Hello everyone and welcome to the Professional Wrestling Circuits first-ever Pay Per View, Capitol Punishment!!! I am Mark Malloy and I am joined here at ringside by my legendary partner, Bang Davenport, as well as 7000 crazed PWC fans, who are here to not only for this historical event, but to witness the crowning of the first ever Pro Wrestling Circuit World Heavyweight Champion!!! Bang: Indeed dey do be, Markus, but the reality is they might as well kept themselves home because any fool knows that Sebastian Toomes is gonna add P-dubya-C gold to his ever growin’ collection!! Mark: Well, not to take anything away from Bang’s choice, or the highly capable Angelo Deville, I am pulling for Clay Vendetta, who will finally prove himself tonight as the great competitor that he truly is. Either way, however, this is CEO Ron Starr’s show and in sticking with the formula of success that has made him a success in this industry, we are going to treat our Pay Per View fans at home with plenty of non-stop action, beginning with Paula Michelle Smith taking on Sam Alotti in a match where the loser leaves the Pro Wrestling Circuit forever!!! Bang: How about instead of the loser leaving the Circuit, the loser gots to rub the bunions on my feet? Mark: I bet they would rather leave PWC. Either way, this party is ready to get started, so lets send it up to the ring announcer, Bob Kirby. The scene shifts to the ring where ring announcer Bob Kirby, dressed in a tuxedo, stands ready to introduce the participants. Bob Kirby: The following match is scheduled for one fall… ‘The Click’ by Good Charlotte comes on over the PA, and the crowd immediately starts booing. The curtain leading to the back slowly parts, and Paula Michelle Smith casually walks out and to the head of the ramp. She is wearing her purple prom dress, and a faded-blue duffel bag hangs from her left shoulder. With her right hand, she reaches up, pushes her hair off her face, and glares out at the crowd. Bob Kirby: … with a ‘loser leaves town’ stipulation… Mark: That’s right! In the next few minutes, the PWC dream will come to an end for one of these ladies. Bob Kirby: Now making her way to the ring… Paula readjusts the strap of the duffel bag, and then starts walking down the ramp, her head high, and a slight swagger in her step. Bob Kirby: Weighing in at 103 pounds… standing at 5 foot, three inches… Paula arrives at ringside, and quickly walks over to the stairs. Bob Kirby: ‘The Punk Princess’… Paula Michelle Smith! The young girl clambers up the steps, walks out to the center of the apron, and carefully enters the ring in between the top two ropes. She walks over to the center of the ring, sets the bag down, and then turns her attention on the entrance as she fits a derisive sneer onto her face. Paula’s song slowly fades, but is quickly replaced by The Distiller’s ‘Young, Crazed, Peeling.’ A good portion of the crowd starts cheering as Samantha Alotti pushes aside the curtain, and hobbles to the top of the ramp. Bob Kirby: Now making her way to the ring, weighing in at 137 pounds, and standing at 6 foot, 1 inch… she is- Paula (snatching the microphone away): Samantha, Samantha, Samantha. The young girl walks forward, and – glaring down at Alotti – leans on the ropes. Paula (mock concern): You are not looking exceptionally well, dear. A sizable outpouring of loathing immediately descends upon the ring. Mark: I don’t think anybody forgets the images we saw last night, after Sammy was run down in the parking lot. Bang: She shudda looked both ways afore steppin’ inta da road. Sammy slowly starts making her way down towards the ring, and the extent of her injuries becomes immediately apparent. Her right arm is drawn in towards her body protectively; the bulk under her shirt hints at bandages and wrapping; her right knee is supported with a brace; and yet more bandages cover a large portion of her right leg; and she leans heavily upon her left leg, whose ankle looks a lot less swollen. Paula (not even trying to sound friendly): You know, dear, there is no shame is throwing in the towel. While I commend your spirit and bravery for even showing up, your health is much more important than this. Making very sluggish progress towards the ring, Samantha struggles to walk as normal as possible. Paula: Especially with your right leg injured like that. (Sighing, as though distraught): To think that you will not be able to use a large part of your offensive arsenal… Mark (disgusted): If the whole point of running Alotti down was to take away the potency of her arsenal of kicks, that is simply horrible. Paula: I suppose you could always kick with your left leg… but how well can your injured leg support your ever-increasing weight? Samantha stops walking once she gets to ringside, and – as she looks up at Paula – her eyes narrow angrily. Her left hand, which falls loosely down around her hip, slowly tenses up, and her hand clenches into a tight, white-knuckled fist. Paula, meanwhile, pushes herself away from the ropes, and gestures as if to welcome her opponent into the ring. Samantha doesn’t budge. Paula: I know that we do not exactly get along, and that you may not value my opinion. But if I can just offer some advice: the best thing to do would be to simply lie down. I promise I will not let you suffer… too much. Mark: Oh, what a bitch… Bang: You dun said ‘bitch.’ Mark: Indeed I did, because this is, after all, a pay per view, and anything goes. Bang: Even cousin humpin’? Mark sighs. The young girl flashes her rival an insincere smile as Samantha cautiously approaches the ring. Alotti pulls herself up onto the apron, a painful, difficult process, and then rolls under the ropes. She slowly rises to her feet, and – judging by the determined look on her face – she has no intention of letting Paula off so easily. Paula: Honestly, Miss Alotti… I am deeply concerned for you. Notions of pride, self-respect, dignity, and honor might be telling you to fight it out, but these feelings are going to get you seriously hurt. Mark: She’s already seriously hurt, thanks to Paula. Bang: Da police say she ain’t a suspect. Air-e-ath’s car was bang’d up ‘cuz he rear-ended somebody dis morning. Mark: Oh please. I see right through the whole ‘car accident’ thing. Alotti takes a menacing step towards Paula, who backs up just enough to put the duffel bag in between them. Samantha keeps advancing, and – as Paula holds her ground – she finally comes within mere inches of the small girl. Alotti leans forward, so that she towers above her adversary, and then glowers down at the girl. Paula (unphased): And just to prove that I am a good sport about all of this… With a juvenile smile plastered on her face, Paula reaches out, and gives Alotti a “good-natured” locker-room-style punch to her injured right shoulder. Samantha, wincing in pain, immediately twists away. Paula (not losing a beat): … I brought you a bag full of consolation prizes. (Motioning to the duffel bag): After all, no loser deserves to leave the show empty-handed. Gently shaking her arm, Samantha turns back around to face Paula, her teeth bared in anger. Paula (suddenly completely pleasant): So what do you say, Miss Alotti? In answer, Samantha swiftly slaps Paula across the face. The crowd pops as the young girl stumbles to her left. Paula quickly regains her footing, and reaches up to gingerly touch her right cheek, which is already stung red. Paula (completely emotionless): I will make you sorry for that. Smith suddenly tosses the microphone up, towards Samantha’s face, and – as Alotti instinctively throws up her hands to protect herself – Paula quickly dropkicks her right knee. Sammy swiftly goes face down, and Paula quickly rolls up to her feet. Referee Gil McGoosen, meanwhile, pulls the duffel bag out of the ring, and then signals for the bell. As the match officially begins, Paula drops a knee onto the back of Alotti’s right leg. The injured woman lets out a pained yelp, and starts trying to struggle up. Paula steps back, pointing and laughing hysterically at her rival’s misery. Mark: She has no interest in wrestling. Her fascination with suffering is disturbing. She needs help. Bang: Looks like right now, Alotti be da one in need a help. Samantha finally gets to her hands and knee, careful to keep her right knee off the mat. Paula steps forward to deliver a soccer-ball style left kick to the ribs along Sammy’s right side. Alotti, her face twisted in agony, rolls onto her back. Mark: Alotti has a uphill battle ahead of her. Bang: She shudda listen’d ta Paula and givin’ it up. Mark: Samantha has too much heart to do that. Bang: Too bad she dun have two good legs! Paula jumps into the air as high as she can, and comes down in a sitting position on Sammy’s stomach. After landing, she quickly motions for McGoosen to start counting. Mark: One… two… Samantha throws her left shoulder up to break up the count. Paula, still sitting on her opponent, spreads her legs just a bit, to expose the right side of Alotti’s abdomen; the young girl immediately starts slamming the heels of her palms into that area. Left, right, left, right, each blow bringing tears to Sammy’s eyes. Mark: Paula just relentlessly targeting Samantha’s injuries. Bang: Ain’t she smart? Alotti finally puts her left hand on the middle of Paula’s back and shoves for all she’s worth. The young girl, obviously caught off guard, flops forward. Alotti immediately starts rolling to her left, trying to get away from her opponent. Paula, meanwhile, pushes herself back to her feet. Just as Paula regains her footing, Samantha gets to the ropes, and then starts crawling towards the turnbuckle. Paula, embarrassed at having been so easily dislodged, whirls around, her face drawn up in a snarl, her eyes dripping with venom… and then her face slowly softens as she watches Sammy try to get to the turnbuckle. A satisfied smile spreads across her face. Paula (shouting): Crawl like the dog you are! Crawl, bitch, crawl! Alotti finally reaches the turnbuckle, and Paula quickly makes her way over there. Pulling the woman up by the hair, Paula helps her to stand, only to swiftly kick the outside of her right knee. As Alotti starts to lean towards her right, Paula grabs her hair once again and uses that to pull Sammy’s face into a rising knee. Mark (shrill, upset voice): This is just wrong! Bang: What is? Mark: Paula has ducked Alotti at every damn turn! Time and time again, Paula has found some way to avoid a legitimate wrestling contest with Samantha. It just pisses me off! It’s not right. Meanwhile, in the ring, Samantha slumps into the turnbuckle. Paula gives her a pitying look, and then slowly raises her left hand. With no protest from Alotti, Paula viciously slaps her across the face. Bang: Paula returnin’ da insult from afore! Mark snorts, but doesn’t have anything to say. Paula grabs Alotti’s hair, and pulls her head back as far as the turnbuckle will allow. Climbing up onto the bottom rope, Paula finally gets enough height to be able to look down at Alotti. Paula (screaming): I told you to give up! I tried to tell you how hopeless it was! You should have listened, you stupid whore! Paula lets go off the hair with her left hand, and rudely backhands Alotti across the face. Suddenly, with a surge of energy, Alotti grabs Paula on each of her hips, and shoves her backwards, much to the delight of the crowd. Smith loses her footing on the bottom rope, and barely manages to get the heels of her boots on the ground as she lands; she immediately is forced to back-pedal, and finally just stumbles and falls onto her back. Mark: It looks like Paula’s insults went too far! Paula rolls backwards, coming up to her feet, just as Alotti pushes herself away from the turnbuckle. Alotti lowers her head, putting her left shoulder out in front, and drives Paula into the mat with a spear, much to the crowd’s delight. Paula, dazed, rolls onto her side, but Alotti just lays on the mat, her heavy breathing providing her only form of movement. Mark: Sammy may have mustered up all her energy for that move… I don’t know how much she has left. Bang: And what she gonna follow it up with? She can’t kick… can’t goes up’on ta da top rope… Mark: I don’t like it, but you might be right. Paula actually gets up first, and slowly walks over to the downed Alotti. With a lot of effort, the young girl manages to get Samantha back up onto her feet. Hobbling wickedly, Alotti reaches out and pushes Paula away from her. Angered, Paula steps forward, simultaneously swinging her left arm in a wild haymaker. Alotti expertly blocks the incoming punch with her arm, lashes out to grab Paula’s wrist, and slowly ducks under the arm, bringing Paula into a hammerlock. The spin almost causes Samantha to keel over, but she firmly plants her left foot into the mat to stay standing. Pulling up hard on the hammerlock, which causes the young girl to squirm, Alotti slowly stabilizes herself. Mark: It looks like Alotti is setting up her ‘Ray of Sunshine’… except it’s much slower going than normal. Indeed, Alotti finally kicks out with her left leg, catching the back of Paula’s knee. At the same time, she pulls rearward on the hammerlock, causing Paula to fall onto her back with her arm still bent behind her. Alotti comes down on top of Paula, which obviously doesn’t do wonders for her bruised body. Nevertheless, it is a pin. One… …two… ….thre-! No!! At the last possible moment, Paula just barely manages to get her right shoulder off of the canvas. Mark: With her arm bent behind her, and a heavier body pressing down on her, Paula had to do an awful lot of wiggling to get out of that. Bang: Didcha say sumthin’ ‘bout bodies pressin’ together? ‘Cuz at my last family reunion- Mark: Just stop, right now. Alotti, still holding onto the hammerlock, rolls Paula facedown onto the mat. Coming up on top, Samantha just sits on top of the young girl, obviously trying to recover some strength and energy. Mark: Paula was 1/10 of a second away from being out of a job! Bang: T’wasn’t dat close. Mark (excited): She almost had to take her own ‘consolation prizes,’ whatever the hell those might be. Back in the ring, Alotti slides off of Paula and – still holding on the hammerlock – pulls the young girl off of the mat. Paula squeals in pain, and her right hand shoots towards her left shoulder. As an angry look crosses her face, she reaches her right hand behind her and starts blindly swiping at Samantha. Alotti quickly lets go of the hammerlock, grabs Paula’s waving hand, and uses that to spin the young girl around. Alotti wraps Paula up in a DDT-like position, but quickly swings it around into a forceful neckbreaker. The crowd, recognizing the move, pops again. Mark: Paula’s felt that before – ‘Stars in ya eyes!’ As soon as Alotti hits the mat, which is just a moment after Paula, she immediately curls up into a ball. Her eyes shut tight, her face scrunched up in pain, Alotti bends her left arm back so that she can press her hand against her lower back. Mark: Her shirt is obviously hiding the full degree of her injuries… but this has to be related to last night. Paula, meanwhile, slowly rolls over onto her stomach, and carefully pushes herself onto her knees. Mark (obviously disappointed): This is twice now that Alotti has hit a big move, only to see Paula get up first. Paula, still shaken up, cradles the back of her head with both of her hands as the crowd starts up a chant. The Crowd: ‘SA-MAN-THA! SA-MAN-THA! SA-MAN-THA!’ Mark: Maybe responding to the positive energy from the crowd, Alotti is starting to stir... She’s up to her feet… looking a little shaky… but she’s up! Alotti warily approaches Paula, and grabs the young girl under the chin, meaning to pull her back up. Paula, however, lashes out and chops the inside of Samantha’s right knee. As Sammy starts to buckle, Paula scrambles around to Alotti’s left in order to put Alotti in between herself and Gil McGoosen. Once she is obscured from his vision, Paula wraps her arms around Alotti’s good leg, and quickly bites its thigh – hard. As Samantha yells in pain, and pulls her leg away, Paula scampers to her feet and then backs up a few steps. Mark: I can’t believe what I just saw! Bang: I did na see it. Mark: Paula should be disqualified, and if that means she is done in PWC, so be it. Alotti, a look of shock on her face, turns toward McGoosen, and points to her leg. Alotti (loud enough to be heard): Hello! She totally just bit me! McGoosen looks at her leg critically, noticing a very faint hint of a red mark where Samantha is pointing. Of course, there are bruises and scrapes all over both of her legs, so he is obviously uncertain. He quickly looks over at Paula, who is standing with her hands on her hips, and her chest puffed out suggestively. Alotti, obviously appalled at this display, can find no words, and manages only to gesture weakly at her opponent. Mark: Alotti is outraged, and so am I!! McGoosen obviously notices Paula’s body, and she is more than happy to use that!! Bang: What? Can’t a girl jus’ stand dere? McGoosen finally turns away from Paula, and gives Alotti a subtle shrug. He then motions for the match to continue. As Alotti starts to say something, McGoosen raises his hand. McGoosen (barely audible): You could be right, but I can’t prove it. So you might be wrong. Mark: This is outrageous! Bang: He’s jus’ bein’ fair… callin’ it down da middle. For all he knows, Alotti coulds be lyin’. Mark: You know damn well she isn’t lying!! Alotti, still focused on McGoosen, is ready to argue her case further when Paula suddenly starts running towards her. Paula, with a little bit of speed worked up, lands a diving clothesline, and Alotti immediately goes down onto her back. Paula springs to her feet, rushes off the ropes perpendicular to Alotti, and comes back, baseball sliding into the right side of Samantha’s body. Alotti, with a loud, pained protest, flips onto her side. Paula quickly grabs her, pulls her down onto her back once again, and goes for a pin. McGoosen making the count… 1… 2… Mark: NOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!! 3!!! Winner: Paula Michelle Smith Bang: Bye-bye, Sammy girl!!! Mark: Have a little class!!! As soon as the bell rings, Paula stands up, and starts jumping up and down excitedly while raising her arms triumphantly in the air. The crowd, of course, starts booing. Bob Kirby: And the winner of this match, by pin-fall… Paula Michelle Smith! In the ring, Samantha has curled into a fetal position, her arms wrapped about herself protectively. So preoccupied with the pain wracking the right side of her body, she doesn’t even seem to realize she has lost. Mark (worried): I think Samantha Alotti is badly hurt… Bang: And her P-Dubya-C career is over! Paula, still bouncing around giddily, makes McGoosen raise her arm. After letting him hold it there for a few seconds, she tears it away, and walks over to Alotti. Casually, with complete disdain for the hurting woman, Paula pokes and prods Samantha with the tip of her boot. Finally, with a gleeful smile, Paula delivers one swift kick to the middle of Alotti’s back. Mark: Just leave her alone already!! Paula hustles over to the ropes, slides out of the ring, and searches around ringside until she locates the duffel bag. Before heading back to the ring, she collects a microphone from the ring announcer’s table. After that, she carefully sets the duffel bag on the apron, and then slides back into the ring. She collects the bag once more, and then walks to the center of the ring, where Alotti still lays writhing in pain. Unzipping the bag, she pulls out a T-Shirt, airbrushed with ‘The Punk Princess.’ She drapes it over Alotti. As Paula lifts the microphone to her lips, the crowd starts booing loudly. She tries to wait them out, but they won’t get quieter. Paula (screaming into the microphone): SHUT UP! JUST SHUT UP! SHOW SOME DAMN RESPECT!! Mark (disdainfully): She has no right to use the word ‘respect.’ The crowd still continues booing, maybe even getting a bit louder. Paula: I AM TRYING TO COMFORT THIS POOR WOMAN! DAMN YOU PEOPLE! Bang: Ya… hasn’t Alotti suffer’d enough… without dese people makin’ her lie dere longer? Finally, giving up on trying to be heard over the crowd, Paula turns the bag over and dumps the rest of its contents onto Alotti: a rolled-up PMS poster, a box of tampons, and an autographed picture of Paula. Smith also drops the duffel bag onto Samantha. Mark: Samantha doesn’t deserve this. This is sickening. Paula: HAVE A NICE TRIP HOME, LOSER-BITCH! With that, she spikes the microphone down onto Alotti, and – once again raising her arms victoriously – exits the ring, and walks towards the back, a happy bounce in her step. Mark: (disgusted) This is an outrage. We need to head to a commercial. We will be right back, right after these messages. . . . . . . . . . . Commercial: PWC’s Last Man Standing, Sunday, June 15th, 2003, Live and Only on Pay Per View. Call your local cable company or satellite provider to order today!!! . . . . . . . . . . The show backstage we see the interior of Karina Wolfenden’s small but homely changing room. A faint ambient beat plays just in the background and the room is lit by several groups of candles rather than the harsh electric lights. Mark: Welcome back to Capitol Punishment!!! We’re rapidly approaching Karina’s return match against Magnum Wolf here at Capital Punishment, and with no rules to hold them back, we might not see both of them next week. Bang: So that be there end o’ Karina then! Through the flickering orange light we can see Karina looking at herself in the mirror, her face blank, almost as if she’s trying to get the measure of herself. Quietly, and in a reflective manner, she gives herself a pep-talk. Karina: Not long to go now, girl. You’re living by Magnum’s rules now, you’re living in a world he created. Karina’s own words seem to anger her, the thought of Wolf always managing to stir up emotion within herself. Karina: He made this life for you, and now you get to show him that some things you just don’t mess with. Despite the focused and determined look on her face, her words remain in monotone. The candlelight means her face’s shadows are dynamic. Still studying her own face in her reflection, Karina reaches up and pulls the pink flash at the front of her hair from her face, her dazzling green eyes still sparkling in the scarce light. Karina: It’s not about winning or losing, it’s about letting people know that you won’t have your world invaded by the maliciousness of others. No 3 count can change what has happened, but the person who did it can be changed. Continuing her reflective, solemn monologue, Karina pulls on her fighting gloves, imagining Magnum Wolf being on the other end of them shortly. Finally looking away from her own face, her eyes wander to her right knee, the heavy strapping clearly showing under her black cargo pants. Karina: ...No mater the cos- Her solitary moment is broken as there is a knock at her door, breaking her focus. Closing her eyes and sighing at the untimely interruption, she doesn’t answer it’s knock, just wishing whoever it is will go away. “Karina?” Then, in one word, her whole demeanour is shattered. The gritty determination she was showing slides away from her as the voice of her father speaks to her through the door. She doesn’t respond, just walks slowly to the door as he tries the handle, luckily she always locks the door. Lawrence: Karina, are you in there? Her body is as close as it can be to the door without touching it, debating whether to open it or not, for the first time tonight a look on uncertainty creeps in. His mere presence having this effect on her. Lawrence: Look, I got sent the ticket... I don’t know if it was from you or that slimeball I hired, but I’m here. Slowly, her right hand creeps towards the lock, hovering above it. Lawrence: Kari, we need to talk. I’m going to be out there watching you, maybe afterwards we can.... I don’t know, just something? Her hand opens, surrounding the lock but not turning it. Lawrence: I guess you’re not here... Well, maybe I’ll catch you around later.
