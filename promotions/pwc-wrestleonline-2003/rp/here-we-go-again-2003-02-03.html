Here We Go Again

3 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: day after yesterday Fitting snugly inside the large archway leading into the main living room, Angelo Deville lounges arrogantly in nothing but a towel, leaning his forearms against the doorframe for support and leering suggestively at the lithe forms of Kathryn and Erica entangled on his sofa. His blonde hair is almost brown with wetness, and there is a glistening sheen about his entire body, betraying the hasty towelling job following his shower. His normally golden skin has a reddish tinge from the scalding water, which gives his already engorged and rippling muscles the appearance of bursting through the skin. Blue eyes like a pair of melting glaciers scan the bisexuals hungrily, and they squirm and giggle as he swaggers into the room with lusty purpose. 'You girls look as if you've been up to something,' Deville intones in a quiet and falsely admonishing tone. 'Something naughty.' Another giggle wracks them, pressing and rubbing their bodies against one another as they kiss, pretending not to notice Angelo's approach. They're both draped in sheer robes that do nothing to hide their perky figures, and Kathryn's red curls seem to fuse with Erica's raven tresses in an erotically suggestive way. Curled up into each other's arms on the plush, black leather sofa, the girls give the impression that they've been doing this forever, and never intend to stop. 'Playing hard to get, are we?' His voice is amused, with just a hint of scandal as he quickly breeches the intervening distance, sparing not a glance for the grandfather clock on his left, the entertainment centre on his right, or any of the paintings or furnishings that add an air of coziness to the room. 'Well I've got a cure for that,' Deville smiles, one hand moving deftly to the knot of his towel as he comes to a stop, looming over the coy couple before him. Finally they turn towards him, grinning like mirror images of the Creshire cat, and a low purring sound ululates from Kathryn. Erica's right hand strays from her mate's body and covers the one untying Angelo's towel, gently pulling it away and relieving it of its duty. One of Kathryn's hands floats in the same direction, but lands in the middle of the towel instead, caressing slowly and deliberately. 'Sebastian Toomes has nothing on me,' Angelo chuckles, throwing his head back to laugh at the heavens themselves. 'Nothing can stop this.' The double-entendre sails straight over the girls' heads, but Deville doesn't bother trying to explain. There are more important things afoot. Or acock, rather. 'Fuck!' Angelo shouts, still looking at the ceiling, as the familiar chiming of the doorbell slices through the mood like O'Neil to the front of a congo line. He begins to take a step back, and both girls moan in exaggerated anguish. He looks down at their pleading eyes and sighs. 'Ignore it,' Kathryn whispers, stroking as if her life depends on it. 'It's not important,' Erica chimes in, her voice husky with lust. She gives a final tug at the knot and the towel begins to fall away, much to their delight. Snatching it back around his waist, Deville eyes them regretfully. 'I can't,' he states simply, looking to them for understanding. 'Nobody is supposed to get beyond the outer gate without my permission.' His voice is dull and angry, and realisation dawns on Kathryn and Erica's faces, though it is quickly replaced by concern. They exchange a worried look, then bound up off the couch as Deville refastens the towel. Erica bolts from the room as the doorbell rings again, seemingly harsher and less patient, and returns a moment later with Deville's custom-made pistol dangling from her thumb and forefinger as if it might corrupt her to hold it properly. Deville snatches it away and gives them a sympathetic look. 'Stay here,' he commands and stalks off, leaving them clutching each other worriedly, lost in the scent of soap and something else – something deadly. In the three months they've spent living with the mobster, no one has ever rung the bell unannounced. Getting past the ominous barbed-wire fence surrounding the estate in order to do so would be nearly impossible. As one they crane their necks towards the door, listening intently. Deville arrives at the reinforced set of steel double-doors and cocks the hammer of his pistol as he steels himself to use it. Cautiously, he glances out the one-way windows that flank the doorways like guards, and curses under his breath. A large man in a black, gray pinstriped suit and a fedora occupies the porch with his back to the door, a most arrogant stance for an invader. Arrogance doesn't bely deadliness, Deville reminds himself as he silently unclasps the lock and raises the gun to shoulder level. He holds his breath, grasps the doorknob in his right hand, then whips it open with a fury and leaps through, pressing his weapon against the other man's head before he has a chance to even think of turning. With his free hand Deville knocks the fedora off the man's head with a casual backhand, then grabs him by the shoulder and spins him around, replacing the gun at the bridge of his nose. 'Who the f–' Deville begins in a low growl, but his words catch as he recognises the startled face staring cross-eyed at the pistol. He gawks in blatant disbelief at the stocky man standing an arm-length away, grinning now despite the circumstances. As a measure of his confidence, he turns his eyes away from the gun and locks them on Deville's own, which falter only a moment before becoming rock and ice once more. 'Ang–' the large man begins in a heavy, rumbling voice. 'What the fuck do you want?' Angelo's own musical voice, low now and saturated with venom, overrides his. The recognition has made his manner harder than it was before, not softer. The larger man jerks upright, smile running off his face like sludge down a drain. His jaws ripple as he clenches and unclenches them, working his hands in much the same way, as if trying to wring invisible necks. 'Alright, then,' the rumble is intense now, calculating. 'You have three seconds to put that thing away,' he warns in a level tone, his face growing darker with each passing word. Though his hands are empty, his demeanour promises violence and danger, and his entire body has the appearance of a deadly weapon. 'Or I'm gonna shove it up your ass.' 'You might find that a bit difficult,' Deville snarls, pressing the barrel harder against his nose, 'without your filthy head.' His opponent shows no sign of relenting, however, and silently mouths the word 'one,' his mouth curling into a cruel and self-important smirk. 'Two,' he mimes again, and still neither man moves, like flesh statues preserved to prevent them from destroying one another. But the stillness is of their own design, and the impending violence each has promised is only a twitch away. The world is deathly quiet around them, no birds or insects to be seen or heard. Only a faint breeze stirs them on the polished marble-blue porch, a porch that has embraced spilled blood many times before and will many more to come. It seems to rock beneath their feet as time draws near, as if eager to hold it again. 'Three,' the man whispers, giving voice to the final toll. Everything happens in a blur, quicker than the twitch of an eye. Deville's gun is off the man's head in an instant, finding its way around his massive back as Deville hugs him tightly. They rock back and forth, roaring laughter and slapping backs like men who have not seen each other for a very long time, and seek to make up for what was lost with volume and violence. 'You son of a bitch!' Deville bellows, staggering back, at the same time the other shouts, 'You asshole!' This only serves to get the laughter going as hard as before, but eventually it subsides to chuckles and they regard one another with affection. 'Goddamn, Johhny,' Deville exclaims, shaking his head. 'I've got a fuckin' gun to your head and you still don't back down?' His voice is incredulous, but his eyes betray that he knew it all along. 'Jesus Christ, man, you must be here to kill me.' Deville laughs again, outwardly, but again his eyes reflect the insincerity. What is he doing here? Three months of nothing, and now he's suddenly here, bypassing the gate. 'You think you'd still be alive, if that were the case?' Johnny grins in their shared knowledge that he likely wouldn't be, and claps a hand down on Angelo's shoulder. 'It's good to see you, even if you're still a suspicious asshole.' He barks another laugh as Deville smirks and raises the gun halfway back to his head. 'Don't you tempt me!' Deville shouts, as if struggling to keep the gun down with all his will. Another shared laugh followed by an awkward silence. Johnny picks his hat up off the ground and dusts it off, then replaces it on his head. Just as Deville opens his mouth to ask what inspired Johnny to show up, the latter speaks all in a rush. 'Salvatore wants you to come back,' his voice is flat and emotionless, yet somehow pleading. 'Today. Right now.' Deville can do nothing but gape. It has been three months since he stormed out of Don Salvatore's office, vehemently vowing never to return. Except to kill him, of course. Deville never closed the door on that. Never would close the door on that. 'Fuck Salvatore,' he snarls, unconsciously rubbing his gun in eager anticipation. 'Angie...' Johnny trails off, his brow furrowing with concern. 'That son of a bitch left me to die, Johnny. And then when I didn't, he sent someone else to try and get the job done.' The words manage to slip through his gritted teeth somehow, quiet and intense with rage. 'He should thank his lucky stars I'm leaving him alive.' 'You wouldn't survive him a week,' Johnny warns, never elaborating on whether or not he'd be part of those hunting him down. You probably fucking would, wouldn't you, John? The man notices the sudden narrowing of Deville's eyes and stammers, 'You were in trouble with the law, Angelo. Don Salvatore was worried you'd get caught and give everything up.' 'Oh, so now I'm a fuckin' rat? I can't be trusted not to crack under the oh-so-harsh scrutiny of the fuckin' law? Both of you can go fuck yourselves.' Deville ends the tirade by turning on his heel and grabbing the door to slam it shut, but Johnny is beside him in an instant, one firm hand wrapped around his upper arm. 'I never thought it, Angie,' Johnny swore, peering straight into Deville's eyes, baring his soul. 'Never for an instant.' The hard stare continues for a long moment, then Angelo sighs and nods, and the other man's grip relaxes. 'Salvatore didn't either, I don't think... He just couldn't take the chance. Think about it... How many counts of murder did you have to scam your way out of? And police officers, no less.' The man grins in spite of himself, and its double appears on Angelo's face. 'Don't you try to sweet-talk me, Johnny... Not now.' 'We miss you, Angie,' Johnny continues, the grin replaced by a humble look of sincerity. 'All of us. You belong. You're part of the Family. It just isn't the same without you.' He suddenly looks abashed, and adds to maintain his manhood, 'No one else sucks enough to lose to me at pool, and I wanna win, damnit.' Deville ignores the pathetic cover-up, staring hard at his longtime friend. No explanation of how he got past the gate. No reason for taking three months to come see me. No chance of Salvatore sending someone to beg for my return. 'Not anymore, Johnny. Don Salvatore made that decision all those months ago, and I don't see what's changed. I'm sorry. I'm out.' Deville's voice really is tinged with regret, and he seems ready to break down or explode, one or the other. 'Goddamnit Angelo!' Johnny suddenly cries. 'Shelve your pride for one minute – just one minute! – and come talk to the man! Just talk! We need your help, you selfish prick!' There is no embarrassment or cover-up this time, only a red face and angry huffing. 'So you need my help, do you?' Angelo says slowly, thoughtfully. Now it makes sense. 'You have something better to do, towel-boy?' Johnny teases, poking one fat finger against Deville's bare chest. Deville only grins and leans forward for confidentiality purposes. 'Actually, yeah,' he whispers, pausing to let doubt register on Johnny's face. 'I've got two whores inside, both bisexual, who were positively melting before you brought danger to the mix. They'll be bursting by now.' Johnny struggles to peer over Angelo's shoulder into the house, hopping and bobbing with a child-like expression. 'Bullshit,' he sneers, seeing nothing. 'Alright,' Angelo laughs, in a decidedly better mood. 'Here's the gameplan.' He pauses for effect, then continues, 'you and I are going to go in here and scramble their bisexual brains, and then we're going to play a game of pool. You win, I come talk to Salvatore. I win, you go tell Salvatore to fuck himself for me, to his face.' The grin on his face is the biggest it's been all day, and Johnny's wince does nothing to make it falter. 'Okay,' Johnny agrees hastily, his mind on the women now instead of his true purpose for being here. 'You're on.' The two step into the dimly lit house, closing the door behind them, and Kathryn and Erica burst into the hallway, twin expressions of worry on their face. Johnny lunges forward to begin what he takes for comfort, but Deville puts a restraining arm on his shoulder and pulls him close. The laughter, the affinity, the closeness, all of it is gone from Deville's eyes, leaving emotionless nothing, like a doll's. 'We'll have fun now,' Deville whispers in a monotone for their ears alone. 'But when we leave here, if there's anyone in the backseat of your car...' Deville trails off, and Johnny shrugs uncomfortably, trying to push past him to the girls once more. Angelo holds him firm, his eyes and voice never wavering in the slightest. 'If anything even smells wrong... I'll skin you alive.' Johnny stares in horror at the honest conviction in Angelo's face, then flinches as Deville laughs and turns around to face Erica and Kathryn. 'Girls!' He shouts, seemingly overjoyed, as if he didn't just threaten to kill his friend. The other three are all staring at him uncomfortably. 'A good friend of mine has dropped in unexpectedly. A good friend of yours, too, shortly.' He pauses to share a mischievous and suggestive grin around, then places a hand on each girl's chest and gently pushes them backwards into the living room. 'Now where were we?'
