Hoyt Goes One On One With The Great One.

22 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/22/02 The room smells of perspiration as Hoyt Williams paces back in forth in his sardine can apartment in New Jersey. Hoyt’s wearing a pair of dirty jeans and a sweat stained wife beater T-shirt that’s been on his body for the last forty eight hours. His voice is loud as he shouts out to the heavens. “I call you Jesus and you call me Hoyt and for the last twenty-two years that’s the way we’ve done it being completely honest with each other. Well Jesus this is Hoyt talking to you brother to brother. My dare brother Jesus I seek not to sound vein but why is the gold not cast upon my waist yet?” Hoyt shouts as he paces back in forth at maniacal pace and motion. Hoyt has conversation with god every single night sometimes they talk and other times they argue just like any other family. Tonight they are arguing as Hoyt is trying to understand why he hasn’t won the TV title. “I do as you say lord I have always done as you said. Is this a test much like the one given to the mortal Job? Father with all do respect I have passed all the tests on my path and as the second coming of your image I feel insulted. I’m down right angry with you God if its something I have done just give me a sign to guide me in the right direction as you have done in the past.” Hoyt looks around for a sign before returning his focus to the heavens. God has guided Hoyt’s life from day to the current time in PWC. He knows his mission is not a simple one but one he accepts with vigor. He trust the path laid in front of him but at times he questions it but always follows through “I ask you God to give me the title this Saturday night for all the world to see!! I ask you in the name of Jesus and all that is right with the world. You have the power and glory now and for always I just ask you to shed a little light in my direction.” Hoyt says, almost sounding as if he’s now pleading with somebody. It’s not easy being the second coming of Christ especially if you know what happened with the first coming!! Hoyt’s anxiety if one of his many human traits that drag him down. Soon the keys to the kingdom will be his and all the doubters will be punished. “Just give me a sign God….just give me a sign that all is well and that you hear me tonight” Hoyt shuts and is then interrupted by a noise. Hoyt freezes to listen again for the miracle and again its heard. Three pounding knocks from the ceiling above him. Hoyt smiles and shakes his head in delight. “OH THANK YOU GOD OH THANK YOU JESUS I HEAR YOU LOUD AND CLEAR!!!” Hoyt rejoices as he yells praise at the top of his lungs. “Hey asshole I’m trying to sleep.” A distant voice yells from the ceiling above. Hoyt ignores the remarks from the idiot upstairs and lays down to go to sleep now that his question has been answered. =w=
