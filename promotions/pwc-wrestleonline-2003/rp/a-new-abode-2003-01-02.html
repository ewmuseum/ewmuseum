A new abode

2 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/02/03 The event was over. Every single speculation ever conjured and uttered had been answered, yet more queries had been spawned from tonight's event. It was what everyone wanted really, especially Gideon. It allowed him to play the role of the puppet master to the people, instead of Jeremiah playing the role and using Gideon as his puppet. Gideon himself thought he left his mark well on the night. Warpath was weak in the eyes of him, mentally weak, not physically. Gideon was aware of the fact that he himself was no small man, but then again neither was WarPath, and he could easily toss Gideon around like a rag doll on a good day. Yet mentally, mentally WarPath was attending nursery school in the mentally strong area, while Gideon was a professor teaching at Yale on it. Everyone has a weakness and a fear. Gideon knew all too well about fears, and he knew even more about finding out other peoples and manipulating them with them. 'I really can't believe you!' croaked the old voice of Jeremiah O'Barr, as he tried desperately to keep up with Gideon's pace as he walked through the almost desolate arena. 'What in the name of God were you thinking when...' 'I thought you weren't a holy man' replied Gideon calmly as he continued walking through the aisles of seats, his feet brushing past empty cans and wrappers of candy and various other high cholesterol foods. 'I am many things you don't know about and that you will never know about Gideon!' 'Thank goodness for that, I guess there is a God after all' Jeremiah stopped in his tracks, his long white hair and beard were messy and untidy after the stressful night he had been through in trying to make sure everything he wanted went to plan, Gideon saw that it didn't however. 'Don't take that tone with me dear boy! I tried very hard tonight to ensure...' 'To ensure I wouldn't be a major factor. I know, but you didn't so lets just end this discussion at that shall we?' Gideon himself then stopped in his tracks and looked around the small venue that tonight held place to the first PWC live televised event, and in a completely unusual and rare act by him.............he grinned. 'I mean look at this place. Look at what we can do here' 'This place is a rotting infected cesspool, and if we do things your way all that we can do is rot away with it into nothing. If we did things your way we will dissolve into the ether!' Gideon sighed slowly and walked slowly down the aisle of scattered steel chairs towards the small stained and battered ring, and slid underneath it slowly, smothering his face into the mat slowly and inhaling deeply through his nose before grinning again. 'Smell that old man?' Gideon asked. 'It smells of filth!' 'Exactly!' Gideon's eyes widened and lit up as he uttered the word. 'This ring, this venue, this establishment reeks of filth, putrefaction, disease, plague, sadistic ness and nothingness.' 'What is your point boy?' Gideon rose to his feet, brushing himself off slightly as he did so. 'It's abandoned, neglected, isolated, unaccepted and unwanted. Yet it's trying whatever it can to salvage something positive from the world.' Gideon then slowly turned and looked menacingly at Jeremiah. Whatever positive feeling he had been feeling that had forced him to smile and cause his eyes to light up had now vanished. He was now as deep dark and demented as always. 'Remind you of anyone, old man?' Jeremiah reared his lips back with his trademark sly grin, before chuckling annoyingly. 'Your more foolish than I thought you........' 'Whether you like it or not old man!' Jeremiah stopped suddenly at the violent and loud nature that Gideon interrupted him with. 'This is our home now. Can't you see that? Everything that I am and represent is reflected in this place. This is the closest I've felt to having a base, a salvation, a home in a long frickin' time. It's a feeling I don't experience very often because I had it snatched away by an old worthless menace like you. So please forgive me if I try to enjoy and savour this moment!' 'You'll never have a home you fool! You'll never be accepted and will never lead a normal life as I long as I still draw breath through these withered and aching old lungs! Whether you like it not BOY! You are mine, end of story, and you are my property until I deem the time is right for me to release you.' Gideon slid under the bottom rope of the ring and stormed towards Jeremiah, his enchanting blue eyes were now on the verge of exploding into a fiery crimson red of rage. 'Go on! Attack me! Maim me! It's what I want, it will prove everything I've said!' Gideon then stopped slowly in his tracks, running his hand through his long black hair as he did so. 'Your nothing but a killer, a cold hearted killer. I admit I morphed you into that, but oh no, you've taken it further from there! You kill what you deem evil for your own pleasure and to gain what you call some form of purity in your worthless life! Well face facts, your a murderer, a misanthropic demon who lives for the need to try and overcome what he can't...ME!' Gideon stood there, emotionless for a few solid minutes simply staring at the ground and the surrounding areas of the venue, before turning again to Jeremiah and smiling wickedly. 'Now......... that wasn't very nice, was it old man?' With that, The Shade turned briskly and walked off, leaving the Elderly Evil standing there, an expression of fury and confusion on his face, as his attempt at breaking down his protégé was countered and thrown right back at him.
