The Biggest Night in PWC History [16]

13 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: day after yesterday Step Ripping inside like a gunshot, I feel it. Anticipation. I’ve felt it my entire career – my entire life – as I’ve moved from dangerous obstacle to dangerous obstacle, never faltering, never stumbling, never falling. It’s always there, burning like the memory of a lost passion, as familiar to me now as either of my hands, or any other part of my mind or body. Every time I hear my name mentioned on the television, or the radio, or even in hushed whispers as I walk down the road, it’s there. Every time I laugh laudably at a press conference, or talk trash about someone, or set foot into the squared circle, I feel it. Every time I’m involved in something – anything of any importance – I feel it lifting me above and beyond my opponent... Above and beyond opposition. Anticipation is my friend and ally, and though it’s seen everything in my life time and time again, it sticks around and helps me through... Preparing me, enticing me, exciting me. Step Except now. I’m here in the PWC, what everyone – myself included – dubs as the next big thing in professional wrestling. I’ve Clay Vendetta on one side of me, Sebastian Toomes on the other, and Ron Starr overseeing the three of us, battling for a title no one else has ever held before... Fighting for the right to shape the federation in our image... Struggling to overcome one another and show that we’re the best, shout that we deserve it, and prove that no one else could pull the job off like us. I’m sitting here today with all of that on my plate... And my dearest friend, my ally, is gone. Step Clay Vendetta is useless. How can I build an exciting feud around this? Is there anyone in the world anywhere who believes a defunct smurf can unhinge and overcome Angelo Deville? Even if the guy was a good wrestler, all we ever hear about is how great of a wrestler he is. We don’t see it, we just hear it ad nauseum. And then when he loses, we hear about the politics... About how there’s no way he’d lose to anybody in a perfect world, and he got screwed by people behind the scenes, jealous of his skill and trying to hold him down. I could go on and on about how ridiculous that is, and how it’s because Clay really just sucks, but that would assign the grunt way too much credit. Hey, Clay? You’re tired of getting screwed by people manipulating the situation behind your back, operating illegally and unethically in the shadows to make sure they win? You want real competition, honest and pure...? Something you can excel at where being an uncouth midget idiot isn’t a disadvantage? Get the fuck out of wrestling, halfwit. There’s a bingo hall down the road. In this world you can’t succeed being one-dimensional like your name is Penance, unless, well, you’re a giant goat-molester like Penance. You, “Classic”, are short and small. You’re also boring. And simple. And frankly, you’re not very easy to work with in the ring. Every move I make I feel like it’s ten, just because it’s against you. Who would’ve thought carrying an extra 75 lbs would be such a hassle? Step And then there’s Toomes. Does he like kayfabe? Yes. Is he here, reading this? No. Is he a threat? No. Do I care? No. Step This match is a struggle on a level I’ve never felt or even experienced before. The three of us held PWC and the World in our hands, soft and malleable to our will, poised to shape it around who we are, what we are, and what we can do. Our feud should’ve gone above and beyond the hype, and it did for a while, but now we’re living off of it. The World could’ve been ours. The World should’ve been ours. The World would’ve been my home. Step So long since I’ve had a home, a castle to lord and fuss over. Something beyond a simple trophy for show, or a carnival prize to taunt and barter. Something to build and carve in my image, something that will become me... Something that will not outcast me from myself. The Universal Title was fun, and so was the Golden Turnbuckle Tournament. Both provided intense competition like nothing else I have felt before, challenged me beyond what I thought possible, and probably still do. Abducted on a swirling rampage of promotion and destruction, the belt showed me the rest of our Universe... It brought me to the glowing heart of PCW, to the depths of PTC, and to the lurking shadows of OSE. It gave me all I wanted to see and more, took me everywhere, brought me to everything. But it was empty. There was nothing beyond what I put into it, and even that was a struggle. I was hoping this would be different. Step Right now I should be marching to a triumphant payperview victory, lording over the biggest night in PWC history, filled to the brim with intrigue and anticipation. Step I can feel myself moving, roaming like a nomad without a cause to fight for or a home to defend, knowing I’m headed for something better simply because it couldn’t get worse. Step The PWC will be mine. Step Time for the albino hype to darken and die. Time for the unsung hero to fade into dust. Time for the Deville to claim his domain. Step People keep asking me if I’m going to win this match at Capitol Punishment, if I think I’m going to leave the payperview as the first-ever PWC World Champion. I always just answer with a shrug of my shoulders, not wanting to get into it. This is too complicated a matter for a simple yes or no answer, but... Yes, I think I should win... But I don’t deserve to. Neither does Clay. Neither does Sebastian. None of us deserve it. Step But I’m going to take it, and there isn’t anyone employed in PWC who can stop me. The World Title will be mine, and PWC will be my home. I just wish there was even the smallest hint of my dearest old friend, anticipation, to see me through it. Instead, I walk blindly and apathetically toward my token prize, joined only by contempt. Contempt for Toomes, contempt for Vendetta, and contempt for myself for not giving them the same treatment I gave O’Neil. Step Of course I’ll be World Champion. No one else is fit to reign like I am. But it feels like shit. I feel like a nobody doing it, stealing it on account of my opponents’ incompetence instead of my prowess. Hell, I feel like one of you. This should be a battle... This should be a war... Step Instead it’s a robbery. Step The Biggest Night in PWC History? Not for me. This is the most ashamed I’ve ever felt in this sport, overcoming my opponents without really defeating them. I’m better than them, and I could beat them regardless, but I’d have a more interesting and insightful fight with figments of my own imagination. And there’d be a lot more build-up going into the match, too. Step But this is how your first World Champion has been made... Step Don’t like it? Step Step up and do something about it. Step Because after Capitol Punishment, after the Biggest Night in PWC History is in the books and you’re all staring with unrestrained awe at your undeserving champion, you can bet your ass I’m going to. Step And who knows? With a little bit of luck and somebody to work with, maybe my old friend will finally return. Step Maybe this will finally feel like home again.
