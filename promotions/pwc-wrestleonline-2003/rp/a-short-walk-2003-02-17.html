A Short Walk

17 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/17/03 He felt the need to vomit. He could feel the mixture of stomach acids slowly climbing his esophagus which caused a tingly, burning sensation. His eyes narrowed as he stood outside City Hall in Philadelphia, staring up at the cloth he now despised so much. He began to think about how much he hated the way those colors went together. The blue, the red, and the white just looked horrible all on one flag. And to think that it’s said that so much unity is credited to this stupid cloth that is now fully outstretched due to the wind. As he pulled his gaze away from the flag, he turned away from the building, beginning to walk away form the building. “Red and gold go together better.” He quietly muttered to himself. The thick layer of snow that covered the ground made a crunchy, packing sound when he stepped and his breath was clearly visible in the air when he exhaled. A large black overcoat covered his body, keeping him warm in the cold air. As he continued to move forward his thoughts move toward how much this country has done for him. Nothing. Nothing at all. He’d be nowhere right now if it weren’t for what he worked incredibly hard for and accomplished by himself. He thought about how blind he had been to all of it and if it hadn’t been for Chandler, he’d probably still be oblivious to it all. He told him about how the United States was set up that only true Americans could be successful. That’s why he did what he had done. He knew that he couldn’t let it be like this. Being a Viet-American meant that he was being oppressed and held back just like all the other minorities. He was beginning to hate this country that he had lived in his entire life. He wished he was a full-blooded Viet and lived back in Vietnam, then things would be different. The snow continued to crunch under his feet as he came to a small vendor booth. He glanced inside for a second seeing a small Asian man wearing a large heavy winter coat. He sits quietly watching a compact portable television his eyes almost glued to the screen. Jimmy’s eyes then glanced upward, seeing two flags pinned to the back wall of the small, wooden, booth. The red and white of Japan and the red, white and blue of the United States, hanging next to one another. Jimmy scoffed at the sight, spitting a large wad of phlegm onto the snowy ground. “Sickening.” He mouthed under his breath. Originally he wouldn’t have noticed… and if he had, he probably wouldn’t have cared. But now that kind of stuff just made him angry. For a moment he actually pondered going over to the man and saying something, but then he realized that would just be a waste of time. Other people had their opinions, although wrong, they still were entitled to them. As his walked on, his mind rested on Porky Jack Bunson for a split second. He was glad the guy got what he had coming. He didn’t feel sorry at all that the guy couldn’t walk anymore, he deserved it. To think that back in the academy he used to have tons of respect for that man. He chuckled quietly to himself at his complete foolishness at the time. But now there were bigger, more important things to worry about. Penance, mainly. He couldn’t understand what was wrong with Chandler. He was always confident in himself but suddenly he had been acting strange. His mouth which normally never stopped running was almost completely silent on the subject of Penance and the tag team titles. He seemed almost worried or even afraid. Jimmy shook the idea of Chandler being afraid out of his head. Chandler was never afraid, he was the who convinced Jimmy that they were unstoppable. Jimmy shook that idea out of his head as well, assuring him that Chandler was just as confident as himself and that they were going to win the tag team titles. Sure, Penance may have destroyed Porky… but he was nothing. Many people thought he was this great power when in reality he was as frail as a newborn. But Penance was a power in his own right, a dangerous one at that. But Jimmy was convinced they could handle it. Penance didn’t know what he had coming. His mind was spinning in circles with everything and everyone that was after him and Chandler. First it was Porky and Axtell, then Penance and now Totakoxe. Axtell was nothing, Chandler took care of him, quick and easy. But Tota had to come and stand up for Axtell. Tota butted in on something he shouldn’t have. Jimmy’s hands clenched into tight fists as the thought of Tota now angered him. His eyes narrowed as he passed a few random pedestrians making their way around the city. He knew what he had to do. He had to get rid of Tota.
