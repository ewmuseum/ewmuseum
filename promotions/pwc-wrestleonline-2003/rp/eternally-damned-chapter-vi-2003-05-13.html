Eternally Damned: Chapter VI

13 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: A very emotional day If you want the full mood of the roleplay please listen to 'I'll Be Missing You' by Puff Daddy. This was my inspiration for this roleplay. It’s just about 10:00pm. Everyone’s gone home. The sky is mourning the loss of Hessian, as it’s crying on his grave. It’s just going dark, but in the rain a figure stands alone wearing a long black leather trench coat. The figure is alone. The figure is cold. The figure is Atlanta Johnson. He’s wearing what was an immaculate suit, but now it’s soaking wet. His expensive Italian leather shoes have mud and grass on their soles. He stands with his arms hugging his waist due to the coldness. His figure is very distinguishable against the landscape. His emotionless face is staring at the grave. He’s having a little think. We journey into his mind. Hessian 1971 - 2003 'To Be Or Not To Be… We All Die Anyway' Poor man. He was a true great of the ring. Agreed he may have killed the odd person. Took the odd life. But he only did it because he was confused. He needed to see the light. If only he had… maybe God might have not been so angry with him. Maybe God might not have struck down upon him with vengeance and furious anger. I hope others learn. But Hessian wasn’t just a great wrestler. He was a great man. A great, great man. I wish him the best of luck in the after life. You told me your dreams: to be remembered as a legend. To me you are. You’ll always be a legend. You wanted to get to the top. Through my talents I will fulfil your wishes. Propaganda kneels down on the grave and clasps his hands together to pray. Our Father, forgive Hessian. He came into heaven ignorant of your existence. But he was troubled. He never got the chance to learn the ways of God. Just when I was about to, he was took from grasps. Now you have him lord. Lord, Jesus, take care of my friend. He will be a good servant to you up in heaven. He’s good hearted. 'A-men.' Atlanta sighs as he gets back up to his feet and brushes off the mud and grass from his knees. He ganders around the sky. I can’t believe you’re gone. He stares at a cloud. I can’t believe you’re not here anymore. Not only will you be remembered. You’ll be missed. 'Brother Hessian…' Atlanta hollers into the dark sky. 'I know you’re listening.' 'I’m sorry about the funeral. It was a complete disaster, the eulogies were poor. They never knew you…. So here’s my eulogy to you.' 'Brother Hessian, thank you, thank you for the light you brought into my world. The glory you helped me achieve. The skills you taught me. The new ways of life you opened up to me. Thank you for all the help you gave me. The friendship you offered me. We were from different worlds. But you took me in. Hull plotted to betray you, you showed him that I must show you respect.' Atlanta’s bottom lip starts to quiver. His face starts to get all prickly and his head starts to feel weak. What’s this new sensation? An Emotion? 'That I did. I respected you more than anyone. For my blunders I apologise, but my friendship will be yours forever.' Propaganda glares over the grave one more time. 'Rest In Peace, friend' For the first time to his memory, he displays a real emotion. A tear. 'I’ll miss you.'
