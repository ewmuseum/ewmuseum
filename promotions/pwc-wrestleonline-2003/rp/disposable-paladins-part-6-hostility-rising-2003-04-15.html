Disposable Paladins - Part 6 - Hostility Rising.

15 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 15/04/04 Part 6: He'd taught and disciplined himself over the years to control, withstand and cope with many things. Getting ungrateful and unwanted looks was one of them, yet there was something about getting them from the police, the officers of the law that made it that little bit more annoying. But he couldn't blame them. Sure, half the people in here couldn't catch a cold, but they had enough brain cells to at least take an accurate shot in the dark at who he was. They'd heard the stories, the tales. He was Gideon Daray Poe, he was the man who Martin Bauerman had tailed for months, he was the man working the serial killer case with that very man now, he was the man who did their job better than them, he was also the man sitting on a desk chair with his black boots crossed and resting comfortably on Bauerman's desk with an eagle staring sadistically at anyone who so much as looked at him the wrong way. Again though, he couldn't blame them. He did their job better than them in the eyes of most people. In the eyes of the press, in the eyes of society, in the eyes of God? Well God's got a pretty sick sense of humour, so he might as well add his name to that list. Inside the Chief's office, not that he didn't need to look, he sensed Martin Bauerman was going one on one with the chief in a verbal bout that had a no time limit and no DQ on curse words. 'I wanted someone to help you on this case! not a circus freak!' 'You told me to get someone, so I did!' 'So you hired the guy who keeps our workers in the morgue working overtime on the weekends?!' 'It's more than you've done so far chief!' 'Are you raising your voice to me?!' Truth be told Martin Bauerman had been raising his voice to him for the last 15 minutes. Bauerman almost found the question funny and almost shot back with 'Is that a taupe you're wearing' if they were going to start asking questions they blatantly knew the answers to. Instead he settled for 'Damn right I am'. Before any further verbal lashings and pink slips could be thrown about however, the door swung open harshly and was slammed shut just as quickly. 'What the fuck, speak of the devil. Come on in why don't you!' bellowed the chief, the veins just at the sides of his temples seemingly growing a rapid pulse beneath his hot crimson flushed skin. Poe didn't bother answering him, instead just crossed his arms and rested his forearms on the top of a vacant seat next to Bauerman's. '4 days. Give me four....' He saw Bauerman shooting daggers at him from the corner of his eyes and rolled them in response before sighing slightly. 'Give me AND him 4 days' '4 days for what, to figure out your elbows from your assholes?!' 'We'll have the John Doe found out and eliminated in 4 days' 'This isn't a fucking execution! You lay a hand on him and....' 'And what?!' snapped back Poe, causing the chief to recoil slightly at the harsh growl in the tone of which the reply was uttered. 'You want to be the first man to slap him on the wrist for being a bad boy? Get a frickin' grip on what your dealing with here. This isn't a serial graffiti artist on the loose here, the closest thing to graffiti this person has come close to is spraying the alleyway walls with his victims blood. If I see the man, I'm not going to go by the rule book unlike you, you has-been fu...' 'OKAY!' chimed in Bauerman, right on cue as always as he grabbed Poe by the forearm and escorted him physically from the chiefs office, leaving him to stir in Poe's harsh yet factual words. It wasn't until the two reached the desolated hallway that they spoke again. 'Get off' Poe snapped and yanked his forearm from Bauerman's grip. He hated the way he was being dragged away by him like a father will drag away his son after just saying an obscenity to his mother. 'Stop right now!' Poe tried to walk away but was shoved instantly into a coffee machine. 'STOP!' 'So book me!' Bauerman let fly right a vicious right hand in response that connected with the side of Poe's face with a stinging snap that made his face recoil ten times more forcefully than the chief's did at the sound of Poe's wolf growl moments ago. Poe had no doubt he'd wanted to do that for a long long long time, and decided to just bear it and let him have that one for free, but that was the last one he'd let him have on credit. 'You happy now? In four days I lose my job and you get locked in jail' 'I wasn't serious when I said book me for swearing at your....' 'DON'T EVEN THINK FOR ONE MINUTE I WON'T HAUL YOUR ASS IN ON YOUR PAST CRIMES IF WE FUCK UP!' Poe let his head tilt down to face the floor letting his long black hair flow over his face like a protective shield. 'We can get him in four days. Then you can have your purple heart or whatever the hell it is they give you city boys, you can have your face in the paper and a handshake off the mayor along with your wife getting hornier than ever over the attention her husband is getting. You'll get laid every night for three weeks and you'll feel like a fucking God amongst your peers.' Poe finally lifted his head up and let his hair fall to the sides of his face. 'Then I'll gallop off into the sunset become nothing more than a crumpled up memory. You'll feel a slight inkling of guilt for about a month, but hey, fuck it, I'm just a nobody anyway, I'm used to this shit, so you'll get over it. So will I' Bauerman didn't fill up with rage, he didn't lash out again. Heck, for all Gideon knew he could be getting a pleasant hard on right now over the thought of getting laid for three weeks straight non stop from his wife if they catch this guy. A real pant buster he'd suspect, yet he got the slightest of feelings that it was just a harsh but refreshing briefing on the reality and consequences of what would ensue sooner than later. 'That's how it will end huh?' 'That's how it usually ends.' 'Happily ever after?' 'I don't do fairy tales' 'Well you just better start praying that you can become a knight in shining armour over the next four days to catch this evil king and save every princess on the streets who he's tailing' Poe straightened his long black leather trench coat and stood up straight. 'I don't do the lead roles. That will be your job, that will be your objective with the publicity. I'll just be the second man, like always, and that's fine by me. Gideon began walking down the hallway, his soft footsteps beating in rhythm with Bauermans somewhat not calm heartbeat ringing through his ears. He loved how he was so positive, so sure. Although he would never likely admit it, not even on his deathbed, he felt reassured by that man. 'You better be right' he whispered to the wind. 'Remember to smile for the camera's' shouted Gideon as he left through to pale white double doors. '.......Sarcastic lil' shit' he whispered again, this time letting a smile creep across his face. 'Urrrgh, sir?' came a camp light voice. 'What is it Melvin?' asked Bauerman without even looking. 'Everything okay, I heard..' 'You heard nothing' '..Yes sir' 'Melvin?' 'Yes sir?' '.....Don't ever call me sir again' '...Yes si-sure thing' 'Fucking kids' Bauerman sighed.
