PWC TV Commercial 6

5 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Originally aired on WGTW TV-48 in Philadelphia, PA at 1.21pm EST Sunday, January 5th. The thin bloke spins around, the cloak twirling in the air as he goes to sit down on his chair. You think that it's a perfectly normal chair until you see that it's made out of what appears to be white marble. Well… probably marble effect plastic, but hey garden centers carry a hell of a lot of gear these days. The chap does not too best please, his toga ruffled and the crown of leaves upon his head looking slightly disheveled. The scene is nicely decorated with what looks like borrowed office pot plants placed around the floor with a white blanket held up behind the chair to try to make the guy in the toga look even more Roman than he currently is. The guy runs his hand through his thinning hair as he looks towards the camera. "I'll give you a brief history of time. You see time is a thing that is always too short, but in this case it's the wait that was too long." He chuckles to himself for a few seconds. "Always funny how the time is too short but the waiting is always too long. Much like life. You see sometimes I just sit here and think that this is in fact a crown of thorns upon my scalp and that the next crucifix to be carried up the hill is mine; such is the burden of a Roman Emperor." He clears his throat a little as another man in a toga walks on carrying a large palm leafed fan; however he quickly gets waved away before he can start to do anything. "God I hate fans." Retorts the man in the toga. "The Roman Empire was a great thing, it spread far and wide bringing wise and just rule to the unruly. It calmed brutal Europe and brought peace to millions. Then that accursed child was born. Then those damn easterners invaded. Then I died." He pauses for a few seconds to let that last sentence sink in a little bit more than the rest. "But I was reborn to a man who knew no real life, no real meaning until I took power, as I always did, back in the old days and I brought the rule of the Roman Empire back to Primetime Championship Wrestling for a brief time where I fought with some of the finest ring Gladiators I ever knew, until I was finally beaten unjustly by a Gaul going by the name of 'The Chef'. Never trust a Gaul, they always stab you in the back." Another roman walks on with a bowl in his hand, contained in the bowl appears to be a type of salad. "Would you like another Caesar Salad, sir?" He gets summarily waved away again. "I already ate two Brutai." Says the guy on the plastic/marble seat. "Well not this time, I shall bring the rule of the Roman Empire back to this day and age, and where better than to start it from something small, something tiny that will endear in the hearts of millions. Here in the Pro Wrestling Circuit I shall create the New Roman Empire… a Roaming Empire… and I too will have a month named after me… just like Caesar and Augustus… it will be called Romember and it will indeed be to Remember… but I need a warrior, a fighter… no I need a Gladiator who will kill all opposition to the Empire. It is time for the Christians to be thrown to my very own lion, but first I must find a big cat to lead… but let's face it… how can they manage without me?" Fin.
