Putting the Mask Back On (Part 1)

22 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April, 22 2003 The setting was a small Independent Federation, out stationed in Ontario Providence of Canada. A show had just finished, in the gym of a high school, and the wrestlers gave out the last autographs, before retreating to the locker rooms. The fans moved through the gym doors, jabbering on about the high spots of the matches, and how cool, so and so was, the usual for the people who visit such shows as this one was. Of course, there were some that were disappointed that it didn't match up to the spectacular PPV that PWC had just had. Sometimes, people don't get that there are levels to independent wrestling federations. Not all had money backing the, or high profile names, who contribute. When the last fan left, the doors to the gym were closed, and the wrestlers and the Ring-Crew workers moved from their hiding in the locker rooms, and started to clear the area. A few of the moving stiffly, due to the injuries that they'd gotten that night. It was the same old, same old on the Indy scene. Slowly, a ring crew worker, ducked under the 18x18 foot ring, and pulled out a box of tools, flipping it open with a smirk, as he pulled out a beer, popping the tab, and taking the first sip of the night. It was time to relax now; this was the easy part, quite relaxing and no worries at all, aside from when he'll get to bed. 'This it the life..' He muttered to himself, after another sip of beer, setting the can by his feet. Reaching once more into the tool box, and grabbing a monkey wrench by the head, flipping it up, and over in mid air, and snatching it by the handle, before hopping up onto the ring apron. The wrestlers smirked, and watched him, as they folded up the chairs, and set them aside. A of the wrestlers took to cleaning the floor of the debris left by civilization, all happy to have worked, and be doing their part. Then of course, there were the ingrates, those who thought they'd be stars in a matter of months, who had snuck out the back, for an easy lay, with the ring rats. They deserved a shake of the head, there was nothing anyone could do about them, and their own ego would crush them. Clem Jenkins, the ring crew worker, looked up, from unscrewing the ropes from the turnbuckle, and with a gapped toothed grin, he flashed the wrestlers a thumbs up, for their work for the evening, quite proud of them. 'Hey! Clem! We read the good news on a site!' One wrestler called out, Cecil VanDrake. A kid with a blue blood gimmick, of course, he was the worst worker of the bunch, but he tried hard, and had a passion for the business. 'Good news?' Clem just blinked, his head tilting to the side, as he reached up, and scratched the mop of a mullet that resided on his head, a brown eyebrow raised in confusion. 'What in th' hell 're ya goin' on about?' This had caught the Redneck's interest, as he saw some of the guys, huddled around a small lap top computer, scrounging the wrestling sites, to see if they had gotten any recognition lately for their works. Clem leapt off the ring apron, and cocked his head to the side, tucking his hands in his back pockets, as he gave a bit of a yawn. 'Did 'ah win 'n award or sompthan'?' Clem drawled on, in his southern way, as he took a look at the screen, then the boys looking at it, only a handful actually over twenty. 'Dude! You were just signed to PWC! That's pretty big! It's a stable job, dude!' Utterly Awesome Austin, grinned, as he slapped the ring crew worker on the shoulder, flashing a big grin. UAA's gimmick is to basically, that he is a bad rip off of PCW's current world champion, Totally Tubular Tommy, a gimmick he did rather well. 'PWC? What in the world? Aww.. Skillz! He must'a talked to 'em fer me!' Mullet straighten his slacking posture, and scratched the back of his neck, forgetting all about the grease on the back of his hands. 'What's wrong man?' UUA, turned, glancing at the older Ring Crew worker, his smile, slowly was curving down into a frown. 'You don't want to work there? Hell! You can tell them to give me your place!' It was a tease, and he nudged the smaller man with his elbow, sending Clem back peddling a few paces. 'Aww, guys. S'not that t'all! Ah'm just gonna miss ya knuckleheads!' Mullet guffawed in his way, and grabbed UAA in a headlock, starting to muss his hair, once more forgetting about his greasy hands. The youth scrambled, trying to get out of the hold, throwing his hands up. 'Hey! All right! All right! I give! I give!' Clem just released the youth, another chuckle emerging from his chest, as he shook his head, giving them another gap-toothed grin, 'Y’all best get back to work! Afterwards, we'll go get some beers! And sodas fer' ya youngins!' A bit of a wink, knowing that that would motivate them to get the gym cleaned up, right quick. Though, Clem was rather honest. He had liked his vacation, it paid far less money, but he was happy with less stress, especially after that RUSH/PCW angle that happened. He was wasted after that, and saddened that RUSH never got back on his feet, after that, he and his friend, Vanilla Skillz, just roamed around with a local Indy Fed, taking it easy for a while. Skillz wrestled, or announced and Clem just did what he did best, aside from wrestle, and that, was being ring crew. It was hours before he got back to the motel room, that he shared with Skillz. But, he just smiled, and shook his head. Skillz had been sick all week, with the flu, and couldn't compete, even though he was so close to home. Unintentionally, Clem held his breath, as though it would protect him from getting sick, as he staggered through the door, just a bit buzzed, he knew better then too drunk. There was another show tomorrow. 'Yoo-hoo! Skillz'? Ya home, hombre?' He grinned, as he plopped down on the unmade bed, and slowly pulled off the cowboy boots, yawning, and stretching, then once more, holding his breath. A small moan came from the bathroom. 'You okay there, hombre?' Mullet blinked, as he scratched the scruff on his cheek. 'Yeah, but dayum! I ain't been tossin' up like that since I heard the Big Tymer's first single, yo?' Vanilla Skillz, the self proclaimed 'greatest white rapper on the face of the earth,' muttered, as he staggered out of the bathroom, his face a lovely tinted green, as he wiped his lips on the back of his arm, shaking his head. 'Who 're the Big Tymer's?' Clem blinked, looking to his friend, and former manager. 'Err, don't worry about it, Home Slice.' Skillz brushed off the question, and plopped onto the other bed with a bit of a moan, draping his arms over his eyes, and giving out a sigh. 'Ah' read an Internet site today Skillz.' Clem started. 'Yo!?' Skillz sat up a bit, a cheesy little grin, on his face, as he looked on his redneck friend, and former tag team partner, 'Was it one of those nasty Asian ones? Dayum! That Bukkake stuff is whack!' That got a blank look. 'Err... Never mind, what'd ya see, Dawg?' Skillz deftly tried to change the subject, giving a nervous little chuckle. 'Seems that ah've been hired ta' PWC.' Clem said lightly, with a smirk tugging on his lips. 'Ya did? YO! THAT'S THE SHIZ-F'N-NIT!! WORD!' Skillz sat up, and went for a high five, but it never came. 'Yo! Don't leave me hangin’, Dawg!' Clem just shrugged, as he scratched his cheek, 'Ya know, I just dun' know if ah'm ready to go back yet.' This was honesty, he knew he could count on Skillz to make him feel better. Skillz may be odd, and idiotic, but sometimes, there was a golden nugget, that always seemed to make sense somewhere with in all the non-sense. 'Dawg, ya can't just go 'round mopin' all day! This is the time ta' go back! And begin to crack 'does heads and skulls, then the world'll be seein' yo' name on the walls!' Skillz bobbed his head, imagining the beat, but then stops, and pinches the bridge of his nose, 'Ugh.. That sucked. Aiight, no rappin' while I have the stomach flu.' 'Thanks. Maybe yer' right, Hombre. Ah've been away far too long, ah think people have fergotten what ah've done in RUSH and CC-Dubya. Maybe ah'll remind em just what ah c'n do.' 'Yo, just do your best dawg.' Skillz nods, with a small smile, 'Word.' 'Alright, Ah'll do it. After tommara' we'll leave fer South Dakota, ah gotta start gettin' prepared. Now ah'm get some sleep, Hombre, there's another show tommara’, they need that ring ta be up after the high school basket ball practice!' With that said, Mullet threw his shirt off, and tossed the covers to his bed back, sliding in and made himself comfortable, before Skillz turned out the light, before retiring to his own bed. The scene fades out, but the anxieties were still there, despite encouraging words.
