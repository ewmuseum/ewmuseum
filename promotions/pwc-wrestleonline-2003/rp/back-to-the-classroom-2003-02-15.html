Back To The Classroom

15 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 15th February 2003 “The mood is about to change” said the orange wrestling midget. Or at least he would have had he been here. But alas, we are currently following the adventures of Romulus; the man who seeks to restarted the twice failed Roman Empire (once a few centuries after Christ was born and died, and once two years ago in Primetime Championship Wrestling). We arrive at our present location via a motor vehicle as our mother drops us off. We step outside the car in our nicely pressed school uniform, hoping that no enraged gunman decides that his parent’s didn’t love him enough and try to gun us down. We turn to mother who has her window rolled down. “Have a good day at school” she says, we don’t really care because at present we hate school and everything about it. Perhaps if we were forced to work a worthless nine till five job for several years before qualifying for school we might appreciate it better. But still, enough of the melodramatics as we wave goodbye to our mother and make our way towards the iron school gates, as we mingle with the other children. We look forward in anticipation because today we have history, and what’s more, we have a guest speaker who apparently knows a lot about the Roman Empire… this term’s class project. Through the hassle and bustle of the school we trip and scratch our way through the army of children huddling through that one gate. That one gate with security cameras and barbed wire over the top of the fence. Perhaps once the war on Iraq is finally declared, they might just be able to quickly change this place into an interment camp for all American-Arabs just like what happened with the American-Japanese back in 1942. We would think that if we were several years older and weren’t trying to kill Saddam with the safety of our own bedroom and action men. We eventually get into the school building and are faced with its usual look… peeling paint, rusting window frames and out of date text books. Anyone would think that the President is spending all his money on pointless wars, and not enough on education. But if you were to say that then doubtless he’d say something about the poor Iraqi children… forgetting for a second that he’s hoping to bomb them flat dead. We make our way to our desk in the class-room; thanking the heavens that the chairs are now metal… picking splinters out of your ass is most unpleasant. Times passes, as does morning registration… luckily for our first lesson there is no need to move… we keep our form tutor and this room for the lesson of the day… History. “Now children”, begins our teacher, “You already know about the fate of Rome, but what about the start… who can tell me the names of the two brothers who legendarily founded Rome?” That prissy bitch, Samantha Tailor sticks her hand up. Perhaps when I get a little older and better at forging gun licenses that I should go purchase a shotgun with my mother’s credit card and give the walls a decent lick of Samantha’s Brain Paint. “Romulus and Remus” says the prissy bitch from two rows ahead of me. I almost dare to scream ‘BLAM’ at her, but manage to calm myself down. “Indeed children, Romulus and Remus… now I would like to introduce our guest speaker today, Romulus!” she points towards the door as a murmur amongst the students goes up. I can’t believe they’re getting another one of those crappy actors in costumes again. Last year’s Genghis Khan was awful… he had a Chicago accent. In walks the fellow, dressed to the nines in Toga and gold… ivy crown around his head and for some reason a pair of eyes akin to David Bowie. He looks around at the children carefully before smiling and leaning backwards against the blackboard. “I bet all you junior citizens think I’m just another actor, don’t you? That the American School System is going to new lengths in boring lessons?” This gets the best response ever in a history lesson. Several nods. What? You were expecting people to jump to their feet and cheer? Have you any idea how boring History actually is? Romulus smiles to himself more than anyone else. “Well my name is Romulus. I was born before Ancient Rome and founded it. I then died. I was then reborn and now I am back trying to restart the Roman Empire once again.” The teacher begins to look worried; after all, she thought she was hiring one of those crappy actors. “But I have several tasks for you to do… firstly…” And that is where the teacher interrupts the roaming Roman… “Tasks? I’m the teacher, I have the lesson plan already worked out… you can’t set tasks!” Romulus looks over towards her with a sense of authority and triumph; already. “I am the man who will rule this world with an iron fist… I can do whatever I want. First task, crucify this woman!” More murmurs and the teacher probably thinks she’s having to deal with a complete nut now. One of the children sticks their hand up… all I can think about is crucifying that prissy bitch a couple rows in front of me. Perhaps I’m having a Clyde attack or something. “Yes, what do you want little Citizen?” says Romulus to the child with her hand up in the air like she just don’t care… “Well… crucifixion… it’s a little… well… gothic, don’t you think? Romulus is taken aback by this statement; obviously he had not factored gothic into his world domination equation. “Gothic… what’s gothic?” says the rather confused former founder of the Roman Empire. “Being gothic is well… like wearing black leather and studs and piercing and other stuff… oh and talking about crucifixions too… it’s because they’re kind of like an alternative to Christianity.” Romulus looks at the child with utter interest. “Alternative to Christianity… but that’s Judaism, surely? So the Jews now worship something that I, the founder of the Roman Empire invented? Interesting… so these gothic Jews, are they about much?” says he who would want to lead mankind back towards Romanism. “No, you’ve got it all wrong, Jewish people aren’t Goths… well… I guess there must be some… but it’s not the same.” Finally I stick my hand up to ask this twit in a costume something. He points at me to say something. “Yo, Roman dude… why exactly do you wear a dress all the time? Don’t your legs get cold?” Romulus looks at me with utter contempt before finally answering; “No… because I am not a Pict.” He looks around, almost expecting laughter…. But none comes forward. “A Pict… they live on Britannia north of the Celts… surely, you must have heard of them?” People shrug all around him. Just them the unexpected happens, the door swings open and standing there wearing an almost entirely leather bodysuit is the class rebel. Wyde. The teacher takes one look at him and starts to go off on one. “Wyde, you know you cannot wear non school uniform in here, you’ll have to go home to change.” Wyde eyes her up as he puts his black leather schoolbag onto someone else’s desk and opens it. “Nice tits, miss.” He says as he pulls something out of the bag. “Kiss them goodbye you evil bitch” He also says as it is revealed that the thing he pulls out of the bag in a small pistol, some type of old police revolver it looks like. Immediately the Roman idiot in training slaps the gun out of his hand grabs him in a reverse headlock and drops backward onto the wooden floor, there he locks the child into some type of wrestling move. I don’t watch wrestling; it’s all fake anyway so I’m not entirely sure what the heck it is. Teach quickly dials the number for security and after a few seconds a fat out of breath man in a uniform and a donut in hand bursts through the door. And then the bell for next lesson finally rang. Only one more to go and then I get to go on a break… and then I might actually kill someone.
