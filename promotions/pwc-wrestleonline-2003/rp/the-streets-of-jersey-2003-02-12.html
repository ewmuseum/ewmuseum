The Streets of Jersey

12 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/12/03 It’s a tiny apartment that sits above the Atlantic Adult Video Store two blocks west of the Showboat in Atlantic City. The apartment is tiny with one room that encompasses the bed, kitchen, and living area. There is a tiny room to the right that houses a small bathroom and shower. Hoyt sits on his bed and looks out at the dark city night. His face looks like it needs a shave and his eyes look tired. It’s 2am and the city is alive and so is Hoyt’s mind while his body begs for rest. Insomnia has been common for Hoyt since he moved to Jersey. Every little noise from a car, drunk, or whore, echo’s in Hoyt’s head like it was broadcast on a bullhorn. The filthy city has been rained upon for the last few days. The moon shines down on the puddles in the street and offers an illuminating glow that contrasts well with the neon signs on the buildings surrounding the area. Even with all the rain the filth never stops. The bums beg, the hookers sell, and the Johns buy. “HELL!” Yells the frustrated and exhausted soldier of God. If a biblical rainfall that cleaned these streets were to happen Hoyt would understand. How can one city dabble so heavy in the devils delight. Men walking into a bookstore to be pleasured by other men in small back video booths. “Fag.” Hoyt whispers under his breath as he watches a man walk into the video booth below his apartment. What happened to the simple times when a man loved a woman? A woman cleans, and loves, and a man works. A woman’s purpose is to serve her man and carry their baby. Now the world is spinning so fast its distorted. “God please let me sleep.” Pleads Hoyt to the man upstairs. Hoyt watches a slut showing her tits to a prospective john. I bet she doesn’t have any trouble sleeping at night figures Hoyt. Why are the bad admired in our society while the good are persecuted and crucified? Sam Alotti is a perfect example of the scum who is cheered. She was asked to do something that was needed to be done. She refused and that outlaw sprit is reward with admiration? “Dirty Whore!” mutters Williams as he lies back down on his soft bed. The hypocrisy is enough to make a man go crazy. How can anybody sleep with the injustices taking place on our very streets? More so in our wrestling federations, Christ wouldn’t stand for it why should we? =w=
