Timeline - 03/06/03 : New Surroundings

5 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/06/03 Karina Wolfenden stands in marvel as she admires the view before her. She's surrounded by her vast new home, open plan to the very truest sense of the word... But right now her eager eyes are decorating, each gaze adding colour and texture to the walls, each blink moving in furniture. 'Perfect,' is the only word she can muster, and in her head it's the only apt word. Of course, to everyone else, it just looks like a dirty, derelict old warehouse... But not all are blessed with the vision of the Wolfenden girl. This is just another chapter in the riddle that cannot be solved, that riddle being Karina herself. The sane may question why a girl brought up in the lap of luxury is standing proudly with the keys to this rather unappealing property in her pocket... But nobody can see the world like the K-Wolf sees it. 'Home, sweet home,' Karina warmly whispers to herself as she hops onto the creaky spiral staircase, speckled with rust, that leads to an unsafe looking upper level. Maybe instead of a letterbox, this place needs a 'Warning' sign. But then again, how many early twenty-somethings with no real job, or desire to have one, can say they live in a vast expanse of a home... Such is this, or such as this will become? 'Now,' announces Karina, with a sparkle in her already lively emerald eyes, 'This HAS to be my bedroom.' Ever the individual, only Karina Wolfenden could pick out a decrepit room with broken floorboards, and a suspicious puddle in the corner, as her boudoir... And as the visibility is just about zero from the morning light catching the plentiful airborne dust, it can't even be seen if her choice has all 4 walls. Fulfilling the role of intrepid explorer, Karina overcomes the stench that is sure to be lingering, and marches on into the room, eyes only for the outside wall that is practically one giant... Not to mention dirty and chipped... Window. 'Morning sun,' she admires, transfixed as the 10am sun beams over the buildings opposite and bathes her in it's warmth, 'This place gets better and better.' Whichever Estate Agent brokered this deal must be thinking he's firmly in God's good graces, having managed to sell, or rather 'offload', this piece of land... Or maybe he didn't realise the young woman actually intended to LIVE here... And even if she did announce the fact, he would have surely laughed it off as a polite joke. Exiting her soon-to-be bedroom, the K-Wolf takes a moment to sample the bird's-eye view of the old warehouse. Each square metre of the floor, walls and ceiling needing a lot of TLC to make the place habitable. But being viewed through the eyes of Karina, each section of crumbling building is being claimed.. But maybe it would be best to show people her new abode AFTER she's finished renovating. 'And who knows?' she questions herself with, as if in a daydream while eyeing a spot further along the upper section, 'Maybe this could be Paula's room?' Karina had to mention her eventually, but surely PMS couldn't be the reason for Karina's bizarre purchase? If there was indeed ever a reason for Karina doing anything. Maybe things would work out well, and Karina can help Paula... For sincerity can breach the most stubborn of defences... And one thing is for certain... Both the girl, and the building, will take a lot of work.
