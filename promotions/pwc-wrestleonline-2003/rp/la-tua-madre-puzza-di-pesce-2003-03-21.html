La Tua Madre Puzza Di Pesce

21 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 21st March Romulus shifts in his seat uncomfortable with his current stance. He looks over the coffee table in the waiting room towards Colossus, who is reading the sports section of USA Today, and isn’t paying any attention to the man who would be the emperor of Rome. He looks to his left towards an old dear who is clutching a Mills and Boon novel with intensity and seems to be devouring every single word. It is an unusual site for Romulus, because he is in fact not wearing a toga, but instead seems to be wearing what could be called almost modern clothing… although it does look like Huggy Bear’s favorite suit if you catch my drift. He crosses his legs quickly before finally uncrossing them and then crossing them again. Suddenly a secretary calls out “Mr. Romulus, the doctor will see you now” from behind her oak desk… the Roman leader gets to his feet and walks towards the large leather clad door, almost like an up market padded cell and opens it, glancing towards him at the giant reading about last night’s hockey scores in the paper. He looks ahead and enters. “Pwease, pwease, take a seat by all means” says the doctor from behind a pair of glasses, a university education a rather cheap looking suit and another oak desk. Romulus closes the door behind him rather quickly and sits down on a chair opposite this Herr German doctor. “My name is Romulus. But you can call me sir.” The doctor raises an eyebrow and starts to write things down in a little notepad. “It’s good to see that you’re writing that down, you wouldn’t want to forget something important like that.” The doctor continues to write something down on the little notepad. Romulus taps his fingers impatiently waiting for this twenty buck an hour quack to actually say something constructive. “My namen is Doctor Scheuss” he prattles on… “I believe you were sent here by a friend of yours, one Colin Ossous. Is that correct?” Romulus grins for a second. “Colin Ossous? I guess that makes me Ron Ulous then.” The doctor writes that down again. “Oh come on, you didn’t have to write that down.” The doctor writes that down too. “Look I’m not going all that crazy here, I just happened to lose it when I heard I might be fired.” The doctor writes that down also, and Ron Ulous seems to be getting the hump big style. “Look, I am not mental.” States the Roman Dictator. “Just because I happen to believe that I am Romulus, the man who founded Rome, killed his own brother and then ascended to heaven to become a god, does not mean that I am crazy. Now a man who wears feathers and dances the fandango on certain days of the year, now that’s a crazy man.” The doctor writes for a good minute continuously after that outburst. Romulus stands up and snatches the notepad from the hands of the doctor and starts to read it. A look of surprise hatches on his face. “YOUR SHOPPING LIST?!” Screams Romulus. “You’ve been writing down your shopping list during this? Damn, I should throw you to the lions already you worthless piece of Gaul!” The Roman lobs the notepad back at the doctor and turns about to storm out. “Same time next week?” says the Doctor, Romulus spins back around and holds a finger to upwards the Doctor before simply saying “Vaffanculo” back to him. He kicks the door open and storms through the waiting room, Colossus puts the paper down and gets to his feet. The secretary stands up annoyed, “You can’t slam the door like that!” she shouts at the Roman Master, who turns to her, stops and smiles at her for a second. He looks at her and says “Scopatrice, Fatti i cazzi tuoi” in Italian, causing Colossus to laugh for a few seconds, before the duo depart the waiting room.
