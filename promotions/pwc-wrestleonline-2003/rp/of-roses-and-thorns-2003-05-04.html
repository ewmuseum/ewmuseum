Of Roses and Thorns

4 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/05/03 Roland soared gracefully through the eternal black abyss that was this cold night. The faint flickering glimmer of nearby streetlamps emphasised his rare unadulterated beauty as he swooped underneath them before ascending gracefully back up towards the stars. He squawked with an enchanting tone. He was talking in a language while still soaring above the flat based rooftops that only one person could understand. A language that only one person wanted to listen to. He was calling to the one person who would heed his call. Gideon's pale wet hands grasped at the top of the rooftop as he hauled himself up over it as Roland called to him. Roland squawked again as he fluttered just in front of Gideon as he rose to his feet. His long black hair drenched with the unforgiving New Jersey rain hung in front of his face, yet his enchanting blue eyes still pierced through and glittered under the moonlight like blue gems. Gideon tilted his head sideways with an inquiring look on his face as Roland continued to talk fluently to him. He rubbed his arms slowly over his black fishnet top as he did so, no trench coat over him tonight. He knew it would not keep him warm anyway under these harsh conditions. Gideon gave Roland a sarcastic uncertain look as if to ask 'are you sure?'. Roland himself squawked deeply himself as if replying back with his own sarcastic response. Gideon smiled at this, and gestured his head forward. 'Then show me my old friend. Lead the way as you have done so in the past. Be my torch....be my eyes.' Roland fluttered his wings one last time before taking to the skies again and soaring just in front of Gideon. 'Let us fly my friend' he said while smiling and taking chase after Roland. Gideon's boots thumped against the solid stern rooftops as he sprinted across them. His deep rhythmic breaths echoing through the night as he vaulted off the edge of a rooftop and soared across the air before landing firmly on the edge of another one like a black cat with enough lives to spare not to care about the dangers of gravity and logic. Ahead Roland kept taunting him as long time companions would, squawking his challenges and criticisms as he led the way. Every time he did so Gideon let out a rare smile, before continuing to give chase. The rain continued to beat down as Gideon hurdled over assorted items of junk before vaulting off the ledge of another rooftop and latching onto an antenna while using it to swing himself across to the rooftop opposite. 'You're getting slow in your old age my friend' Gideon shouted ahead of Roland, causing the eagle to soar ahead even faster. Gideon gave chase one last time and sprinted at full pelt across the obstacle ridden rooftops like an Olympic hurdler, his long black wet hair swaying from side to side as the wind swept underneath it while he leapt from rooftop to rooftop, clearing gap after gap. Each jump, each cleared gap made him feel like he was taunting and cheating death. He felt immortal, unbreakable, untouchable. He felt alive. Gideon pushed his right foot off one final ledge and landed squarely on the rooftop that Roland had come to a standstill upon. Gideon stood there for a few moments like a frozen image neglected by society. Moments passed, and then he began moving, walking purposefully towards the ledge of the apartment block he stood high atop of before standing at the very edge of the ledge as Roland soared up to him and perched himself proudly on his shoulder. Beneath them in a window opposite, sitting and dwelling in another apartment block no doubt mostly ridden with ferocious demented drug abusers and slimy hookers, sat three people, to Gideon they were a representation of something pure existing in something so wrongfully diseased and soiled. Three enchanting roses blossoming amongst a bed of thorns and nettles. A husband. A wife. A young child. Gideon's face turned to an expression of mournfulness and distant pleasantness. The usual stern calm expressions that usually clouded and veiled his face had now all but slipped off his handsome face and smashed beneath him as he looked on at the happy family sitting down at a small table eating dinner. He wondered if they had heard of him through the grapevine., The dark paladin who wanders the streets hopelessly, searching for a soul that once belonged to him. Serving justice to gain some form of purity in his plagued life. He doubted it. He hoped they didn't anyway. Carefully he lowered his body and sat cross legged on the ledge. Now he and Roland were moulded as one creature, perched witchingly atop of their guard post. 'These are who we protect' whispered Gideon. 'This is why we do it, for these people.' Gideon smiled slightly as he continued to stare aimlessly at the family, who somehow still genuinely seemed happy with their living conditions. 'One day we'll have a life. A real one, one like that. One not shrouded and dominated by possessions and beliefs force fed down our throats by the media and government. One filled with hope, purity, and love. One day Roland... one day.' The rain then hammered down some more as The Paladin and his companion continued sitting down, perched on their post, unflinching as they stood guard. Guarding and protecting the three flowers who were blossoming in a bed full of thorns.
