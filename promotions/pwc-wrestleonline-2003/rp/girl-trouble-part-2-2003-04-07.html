Girl Trouble Part 2

7 April 2003

Date: 4/7/03 Special Delivery Girl Trouble Part 2 Sammy Bello is seen leaning on the bright green counter belonging to Cousin Mario’s pizza place. His large nose is dripping with perspiration and he has a half smile on his face. In his hand he holds a dirty wash rag, obviously used for wiping tables down. He seems slightly irritated as he watches another person in a Cousin Mario’s uniform scramble to place toppings on an already made pizza. “No, No, No!” Sammy says irritated again as he watches the person put the toppings on, “Cheese, toppings, and then more cheese. That is how you make a perfect pizza.” The irritability in Sammy’s voice falls flat as the new trainee turns around. No wonder why Sammy’s irritability doesn’t last long, he’s training a girl…a rather attractive girl. She is rather tiny with small blue eyes that sparkle from the fluorescent lights of the restaurant. Her bleach blonde hair is tied back in a pony-tail and she smiles at Sammy who in return smiles back. “I’m sorry Sam. I’ll be sure to get it next time.” She says as she bats her long eyelashes at him. Sammy lets out a long sigh and returns an unsure and awkward smile. “Don’t apologize Katherine, just do it right next time.” Sammy tries to do his best to sound tough and authoritative but he can only let out a little squeak as he falls silent staring into her eyes. “Yes, Sir!” Katherine says as she salutes Sammy, “I will be the best damn pizza girl Cousin Mario’s will ever see.” She smiles again and then turns around and begins to continue to work on the pizza. Sammy turns away and walks over to the sink off to the side away from Katherine. There hunched over scrubbing pizza plates, is Sammy’s best buddy Chuck D’Andrea. “Wow, I am in love Chuck.” Sammy says as he motions to Katherine. “Yep sure you are tough guy.” Big Chuck says without even looking up from his work. “Who’s it this time? Mary-Kate or Ashley?” “Can’t you see her? I mean just look at her beauty, she is the most precious thing on the planet.” Sammy says with a sigh as he stares over at Katherine who continues to work on her pizza. “More so than the Olsen twins?” Big Chuck says with a chuckle as he places his dish rags on the outside of the sink to allow them to dry. Chuck now takes a look over at her and than looks back at Sammy. “Ehh, she’s not bad.” Sammy is taken back at the comment made by his best friend. “Not bad? Not bad? How dare you insult such a perfect creature such as Katherine Rode?” Sammy’s voice trails off again as Katherine looks over towards Sammy and waves. He slumps over and leans against the sink with a dumbfounded smile. Chuck snaps his fingers to break Sammy out of the trance. “Katherine Rode? As in Kiley Rode’s sister?” Chuck says with a surprised tone. “Umm? Maybe?” Sammy adds confused. Chuck smiles at Sammy who seems to forget who Katherine’s elder sister is. “Dude, she was like prom queen of our senior class don’t you remember?” Sammy smiles as his mind wanders back into a fantasy of the past. “She was captain of the gymnast club with me too. Wow, Kiley, what an amazing girl.” A loud crash snaps Sammy out of it as he glances over at Katherine who has dropped the pizza she was working on onto the ground. “Well obviously she doesn’t have the grace of her sister. And besides you are 20 and what is she? 17? 16? Jailbait much?” Chuck says as he smashes Sammy’s dreams of Katherine. Sammy’s face falls down to a scowl as he attempts to defend himself. [withheld — a passage held under the museum's content rule] “None the less, maybe since I couldn’t get Kiley I can get Katherine.” He smiles as he rushes over to her. Big Chuck just leans back on the counter and smiles. Sammy bends down and begins to help Katherine pick up the pizza. He helps her throw the pizza out and together they begin to quickly put a new pizza together. “Don’t worry stuff like that happens to everyone when they are new.” Sammy says reassuringly. “Well, I hope it stops soon. I can’t loose another job I need to pay for prom.” Katherine says without glancing at a blushing Sammy Bello. “So, you’re a senior in school than?” “Yep, few more months and I am out of here. Goodbye Mays Landing High and hello world!” “Yeah, I went to Mays Landing High too.” Sammy says trying to keep a dull conversation from going dead. “Yeah I know.” Katherine adds with a smile. “You were a gymnast with my sister.” Sammy bursts with pride that he is remembered for his amazing talents on the mat. After all he still does hold the uneven bars record in the school. “Geez, I must have made a pretty big impact on that school to be remembered after all these years.” Sammy says with a huge smile on his face. “Well, I just remember my dad always coming home from Kiley’s meets and cursing about some gay guy on the team and him ruining the whole event. And then he put a big “X” through your picture with the team.” Sammy’s face drops to a scowl as Katherine continues. “And then there was that letter he wrote to the school about getting you off the team. And then your record banned…and then there was the time that he…” Sammy interrupts, “I get the point, but I still don’t understand why it would bother him that much?” “Well, he’s just a big homophobe. And I recognized you from the picture yesterday so I went home to check and it was you. Funny how ya can’t miss that nose right?” She laughs as Sammy turns around and rubs his larger than normal nose. “Who ever said I was gay?” Sammy says in a hopeless defense. Katherine smiles, “It’s ok if you are. I have an open mind and besides a guy able to do a split makes me wonder a little bit right? She chuckles and slaps Sammy on the shoulder but he is reluctant to laugh. “Wow, I am appalled that you would consider me gay. I mean with a body like this I have woman crawling all over me.” Sammy brushes some dust off his uniform. “I can see that.” Katherine adds with a chuckle. “Where are they?” her wiry smile causes Sammy to blush again. “It’s an off day. I mean I always keep one day open so I can make sure that I am prepared for 6 days of monster lovin.” “Aww, you’re so cute.” Katherine taps Sammy on his nose as his knees begin to get weak. “Thanks…Katherine.” Sammy is almost on his back from the attention he is receiving from this girl. “Aww, you can call me Kitty. That’s what all my friends call me.” She glances at the clock on the wall as she puts the pizza in the oven. “Well, look at the time. My shift is done.” She takes off her apron and goes over to wash her hands. She notices Chuck standing next to the sink and smiles. He turns to Sammy who is floating on a cloud and nods. He then turns to the back and walks away. “Well, if you need a ride home I can…” “Nope, it’s all taken care of.” Kitty says as Sammy hands her the soap. “And well, even though today is my off day…I guess maybe if you wanted…we could go to a…movie tonight…I guess I could pencil you in. How about 8?” Sammy’s face is now bright red. This is the first time EVER he has asked a girl out on a date and he is unsure what has taken over him. “AWWW, you’re so cute I wish I could take you home with me. But I am seeing someone.” Sammy almost falls over from the monster rejection. “If anything happens though I will be sure to keep you in mind though. See ya tomorrow Bello.” Kitty walks out the back door as Sammy stares. As the door slams loudly closed the depression that Sammy feels begins to overwhelm him. He falls over onto the floor as Chuck walks over to his buddy. “Air traffic controller this is Air Bello, we have a problem. I repeat we have a major problem, crash landing now…” Chuck makes airplane whistles and then a loud explosion. Sammy glares at him with disdain as he lifts himself to his feet. “Relax tiger.” Chuck adds, “I have some Cheesy Bread in the back with rejection written all of it, what do you say?” Sammy pats his old buddy on the back as they begin to walk. “I say that sounds good right about now man.” Together they walk into the back prepared to fill their stomachs. Fade to black.
