Eternally Damned: Chapter II

13 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Early In The Morning 'Knock, Knock, Knock!' 'Coming' Eric Sagar hollers. He looks at the clock at the side of the bed. 04:18 it flashes at him. Sagar sighs. 'Knock, Knock, Knock!' He plods out of bed and puts the silky red night gown over his stripy blue and white pyjamas. Before he answers the door he straightens up his wig. 'Knock, Knock, Knock!' ‘Yes, Yes.' Eric Sagar yaps with annoyance. He opens the door. 'Sweet baby Jesus and the orphans, brother Propaganda, you’re a state!' Eric Sagar splutters staring at his friend, Atlanta stands leaning on the door frame with the bags under his eye tugging down on his face, a very rough unshaven garden of sharp bristles spouting out of his chin and upper lip, his hair flowing in scruffy random paths. His light grey jogging pants with stringy rips on the knees and thighs and a tatty black polo neck shirt with the buttons undone. 'What in the name of Mary mother of Jesus is wrong?' Eric Sagar asks, staring into Propaganda’s eyes, his eyes almost shut behind his thick glasses that are staggering crooked on his nose. Silence. 'Come in.' Eric Sagar demands pointing at the expensive leather couch. Stumbling Propaganda drops himself on the couch and slowly twists himself into seating position. Eric Sagar sits opposite to him. 'I’m going to it.' Propaganda groans. Eric Sagar leans forward. 'Going to what?' 'The funeral, Hessian’s funeral.' 'WHAT!?' Eric Sagar jumps to his feet. Propaganda blinks. 'YOU’RE GOING TO HESSIAN’S FUNERAL!? DO YOU NOT REMEMBER WHAT HE DID TO YOU!?' Eric Sagar is fuming. His face is red. Propaganda’s face is still emotionless. 'BROTHER PROPAGANDA! HE WAS SATAN’S MERCENARY! THAT’S WHAT THEY CALLED HIM! HE DOESN’T EVEN DESERVE A PROPER SEND OFF!' Eric Sagar roars. He starts to pace up and down the room in anger. 'HE WAS A SATANIST! SCUM! PURE SCUM! HE BETRAYED YOU! HE MURDERED! And when he murdered did you think he felt sorrow?' Eric Sagar leans into Propaganda. 'Do you? Not one bit. YET WHY MUST YOU GO AND MOURN THIS VILLAIN! THIS SPAWN OF SATAN FOR WHAT? LETTING YOU CARRY HIM THEN DROPPING YOU TWENTY FEET!' Atlanta looks to the side, as if he was ignoring him. 'When you fell twenty feet did he help you? Did he Jehovah! He’d have let you die. In fact he would of preferred that. I say let him die… alone!' Eric Sagar barks. He sits down and puts on a very serious face. 'If you ask me the human race is better off without him, I’m glad he’s rotting in hell! If you want to go, fine, go. But you’ll disgrace me!' Propaganda leaves a cold blank stare burning a whole through Eric Sagar’s eyes. Propaganda stands up. Storms off. Slamming the door behind him.
