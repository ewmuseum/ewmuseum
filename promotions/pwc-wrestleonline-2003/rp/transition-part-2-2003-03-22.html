Transition Part 2

22 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9th November He didn’t know how long it had been since he lay dazed in the middle of the ring in a crumpled heap. Hours, days, weeks, months, he couldn’t tell. He kept his eyes shut, not wanting to open them and see what environment he was now in. He had a throbbing pain shooting through his left leg and his chest felt incredibly tight and almost suffocating. (I wonder if I’m in heaven, maybe if I open my eyes there will be lots of naked chicks standing over me covered in beer….yeh and smothered in chocolate….not dark chocolate though I don’t like that.) He opened his eyes hoping he would see something appealing, or at least something familiar so he wouldn’t feel out of place or have that feeling of being alone that he had become so accustomed to. This time his eyes opened easily, the crusted blood had been washed away allowing for his eyes to slide open without any trouble. He moved his head from left to right cringing as he did due to the stiffness that came with not moving for what he presumed was quite a long time. A frown crept over his face, he was clearly not happy with what he saw. Instead of his Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles wallpaper he hoped to see he was met with the dull emotionless beige wall, which seemed to staring right back at him. (What type of hell hole is this…wait…What if I’ve been asleep for 53 years and I’m stuck in an old peoples home…NOOOOOOO!!!!) He looked round and saw wires coming from his arms and chest which joined him to some type of machinery. Probably something checking on his vitals he thought to himself. He saw a letter lay on the covers beside him. He recognized the ever-distinguishable PIW logo imprinted on the front. He reached over for it, his arms feeling like huge trees as he fought against their immense weight. He picked the letter up and after minutes of fiddling managed to open the envelope and retrieve he letter from inside. (What could this be?) He unfolded the letter and began staring into it intensely as he tried to focus his eyes on the print. Dear Employee Due to the injuries suffered during your last match in PIW we unfortunately have to release you from our roster due to you being unable to compete in the next 8 weeks. As part of your contract and under the conditions of your release you CANNOT join any of the following wrestling promotions for the next two years. They are as follows: !Action Wrestling 21st Century Wrestling Old School Wrestling Fans Wrestling Organization Icons of Wrestling Alliance Wrestling Corporation We are also sorry to announce that your trademark name given to you by PIW is copyrighted to us and you will also be unable to use that. You are free however to use the name you had before becoming a member of PIW. Thanks for your work (Aaaaahhh shit!)
