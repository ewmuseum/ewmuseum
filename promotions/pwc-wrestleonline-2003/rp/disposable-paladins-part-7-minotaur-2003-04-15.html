Disposable Paladins - Part 7 - Minotaur

15 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 15/04/03 Part 7: 'Come on over here and talk to me' She was as smitten as anything, right now he had her eating out of the palm of his hand and they'd barely been talking for 3 minutes. Yet she wouldn't admit it, but as sure as he was wearing an Armani shirt she knew she couldn't help it. 'Why, guys who hang out in alleyways aren't really a turn on for girls like me?' she playfully replied, and got what she had hoped for. That smile. His smile. A smile that would make a Cheshire cat blush. She was sure she could even see her reflection glimmer off his stone white teeth. 'You think I'd wear a suit like this to hang out in an alley?' He twirled around slightly letting her see the suit in all it's glory. He wasn't shy about showing off his assets, and she liked that. She responded by placing her palms on her hips and stretching one leg forward. 'I dunno, are you sure you're a nice guy?' 'I'm anything you want me to be' She blushed at that comment and didn't even try to hide it. Was she a hooker? Yes. Was she proud of that? Not really. Was she going to miss out on the chance to earn from one guy what she wouldn't even earn in a week? Hell no. Plus, the chance of the foreplay was a nice change to her. It made her feel like she could still wrap men around her little finger without having to start off with 'It's $50 for an hour'. 'And you're sure your not a serial killer?' 'Positive, but even if I was do you think I would tell you?' He held out his hand and she took it and shivered slightly at how his grip was so firm, yet so warm as well even under the cold night. 'What did you say your name was?' '.....Minotaur.' 'Heh, I like it. It sounds--!' Silence. The faint sound of a struggle and muffled yelping, but overall, just picturesque silence. He liked that. Having her squirm before him in a hush calmness made him grin from ear to ear with that Cheshire cat grin again. 'He will fall! You understand!' he shouted to her. 'Theseus will fall, even if he doesn't know it yet! He is Theseus....... he is the one.....Gideon wants to be Theseus, then Thesues HE SHALL BE! 'The Perfect Drug' by Nine Inch Nails began blaring, well, at least the enharmonic chorus of it on his cell phone which Tanya had programmed in. He shot his head up and nearly forearmed her in the temple in the process. Things had moved fast between them, real fast. Yet he was learning to handle it, and wanted her with him, even though he blatantly refuses to admit it openly yet, but she's been good enough to pick up on the crystal clear hints he's been making. He reached over her, still a little unused to having someone else in bed with him, and answered the phone. 'Yea?' 'Gideon?' It was Bauerman, which meant the news was either bad or apocalyptic. 'Yea, what's the problem?' There was always a problem when Martin Bauerman called. 'We've got another one!' 'Dead?' It was a stupid question, but he asked the question with such force Bauerman replied almost immediately. 'Now, yeah, but she wasn't completely when we found her. 'Did she talk?' he shouted. 'Yeah, but look you better get here right now because...' 'What did she say Martin?' Gideon wasn't just yelling now, he was pleading. 'A lot of stuff we're still trying to find out, and......' He already knew what he was going to say. 'He knows your on the case.' '...........' 'Look Gideon, if you can....Poe?' The annoying voice of the stuck up bitch who usually only bothers to give you the time of day when she wants to inform you that 'the number you have dialled has not been recognised' starting spouting her head off repeatedly. Tanya could sleep through Gideon's yelling, but not that. She awoke, looked around found the cell phone, told the woman to 'Get laid' and turned it off. Then she looked to the other side of the bed, the quilts had been ruffled and Gideon's clothes were gone. 'Jesus, he gets called out more at night than a damn on call doctor' she said and sighed before rolling back to sleep. If the killer knew about Gideon, then he sure as hell would now about Tanya. She hadn't noticed or felt the knife Gideon had left her under her side of the bed, he thought it might help reassure himself everything was safe back there. The thought of purchasing an alarm obviously escaping his trail of thought. Either way, now Gideon, and everyone he knew, Jeremiah (not that he'd care what happened to him), Tanya, Trent, Vex and Maynard were all at risk. Himself possibly being the prime target was the least of his concerns.
