Seeking Revenge

11 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-11-03 And here I was once again, left out in the open of the world without a job. I had known this position very well and had it happen to me many times over, but I couldn’t understand it this time. So sudden it came rushing in on me, capturing me like a towel soaks up water. I was so stunned by the things that had happened that I didn’t even come to my senses on coming back to the States to reside in my old home. It didn’t matter either way though, because little did I know that when I signed my contract with that dreaded place that they gained control of my estate and I was left with nothing. All the while the glamour and the spotlight I was being thrown into struck me; and the fact that I had become World Champion made me even blinder to the things that were happening to me. Even when I was being informed of the things that were happening, I would still shrug it off like it was really nothing and that everyone was just out to bring me down. Oh how I wish I could go back now and just take that all away, maybe I wouldn’t be left in the position I am now had I been a little more open to the things that were happening. I had so much before all of this, and yet I was so willing to throw it all away to pursue the dreams I had always wanted to achieve. The fact that I am so intelligent in my career is what brings me down the most. I could have seen these things had I not been so sightless and willing to do anything to get that last run in of my career. And it would have been my last run had I not fallen so behind in my debts. That corporation not gained access to the deeds to my estates all over the American and Canadian nations, but they also achieved the codes to all of my bank accounts and stole all of my money in one of the biggest scandals of my life. I couldn’t believe it; what I had worked so hard for had been stolen from me. The things I have saved since the time I started in this career, at the age of sixteen, something I had been putting away for a little over ten years was taken away from me so quickly and I was left with nothing. All I had was the money in my pocket and the clothes on my back, and not even that would have lasted me very long. Then that phone rang. On the other end was my lawyer and little did I know, but I was still employed. All the while, I was wondering how this could be. I was so mad that I was happy I was out of that dirty business and then I come to find out that I was being drug back into this thing because of another one of those clauses in my “iron clad” contract. I was so furious, and where would they have me go? Then a smile came across my face as a picture of a man began to flash through my mind. Ron Starr, the alias of Phantom Booker, and then I remembered that part of my contract. I was so shaded by the things that had occurred that I had forgotten complete about my ultimate insurance policy. That promotion was a branch of the growing smaller promotion in Primetime Wrestling Circuit, a promotion ran by the man I was seeking to destroy more than anyone else in this business and knowing I had this contract in the bag began to build my drive for wrestling once again. This time though I wouldn’t be worried about the flash and the glamour. I have my “special” intents. My career starts anew and my fire for wrestling begins to burn even more now than it has ever done before in my life. Ron Starr, your time has come and I’m here in PWC to stay. You’ve created a monster over many years and this time, it’s going to take more than a half-ton of bricks to get rid of me. Your time has come!
