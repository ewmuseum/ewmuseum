Enter the Roman

2 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2nd Gebruary 2003 Location: An Econolodge, somewhere in New Jersey. Time: Some point in the afternoon. Sunday 2nd February 2003. Sitting on the edge of one of the two cheap motel beds in this room is a man wearing a toga. Not exactly standard wear for someone in a cheap seedy motel, although we do live in interesting times. The man in the toga is obviously the Roaming Roman, Romulus and he appears to be on the telephone. You can tell this by the obvious anxiety in his voice as he speaks down in. “What exactly do you mean you didn’t place my advert? I already paid you! What do you mean that you don’t accept denarius? I’d have been glad to receive two asses, never mind a single denarius.” He hangs up the phone after looking at it confused for a few moments. “That’s weird… he hung up after I mentioned payment by two asses. Some people I just don’t get.” Romulus reaches for a small black object next to the telephone… most people might call it a television remote… but who knows what the man from ancient Rome will think of it. “Ah, a television remote. Another marvelous Roman invention.” He points it at the television and switches it on, sparking it to life… right in the middle of a fight during the NHL All Star’s game. “Excellent, I did not realize gladiatorial combat was still televised during these times.” He watches intently seeing the two hockey players fight for a few moments before one knocks the other to the floor. All of a sudden Romulus’ hand shoots out towards the television clenched in a fist. He twists it this way and that, then sticks his thumb out and points it downwards. “That will show that brute for not putting up enough of a fight!” Then neither hockey player attacks the other… “DAMN THEM FOR IGNORING THE ROMAN EMPEROR! I SHALL DESTROY THEM WITH ALL MY LEGIONS! THEY SHALL KNOW PAIN!” shouts Romulus as he leaps to his feet. He does however quickly sit back down again; although he does still look furious. “That is the worst gladiatorial combat ever… no lions… not even any tigers… and what on earth is that white stuff that they’re sliding around on… and add to all of that, those swords are far too long and far to blunt!” He places the television remote back down by the telephone and picks up a book that was lying next to him… the amazingly titled “Dummies Guide to World Domination” by the author who knows the most about world domination… Bill Gates. “This Gates would have made a fine general… I would never have thought about sneaking a piece of equipment in with more expensive equipment as standard to eliminate another free piece of equipment in order to make your own piece of equipment the market standard… oh wait… didn’t we do that with the Greeks?” He looks down at his toga and shrugs before leaning back onto his bed and continuing to read his book. Suddenly and abruptly there is a knock at the door. Romulus looks up from his book before shouting “COME!”. In walks an Eastern European looking gentleman wearing a suit and looking rather embarrassed. “Excuse me Mister R. Ominous but I’m afraid that the cheque you made out is rather… shall we say unique.” Romulus looks at him waiting for him to say what was so unique about it. “Well go on, what’s wrong with it then?” The Eastern European man continues slowing… “Well you wrote it out for… denarius… and I don’t know what a denarius is, but I know we only take dollars around here. Romulus nods at the man and grabs a wallet from beside the television remote, removes several notes from it and holds it out for the man to take. “You know what they say… when in Rome…”
