PWC TV Commercial 1

25 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Originally aired on WGTW Channel 48 in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania on December 25th, 2002 at 2:47 am EST. A black screen and silence leads away from an infomercial for Penco Technical Institute, where you too can now learn the skills that today's employers are looking for… Lighting crashes, briefly illuminating the stained canvas of a battered wrestling ring, as the sounds of of the guitar at the beginning of Alice in Chains "The Rooster" begins to strum in the background. A streaking bolt of lightening surges into the ground beyond the ring, starting a small fire. The fire manages to illuminate the area just enough, to reveal an old, derelict gymnasium, littered with trash, decaying training equipment, and the hint of broken dreams of many men and women who wanted to be wrestlers, along with the failed hopes of would be promoters. "It is here where the dreams of many who wish to step into the squared circle and make a grab for fame and glory begin," croaks a deep voiced narrator. The scene shifts to the blood and sweat stained ring, where two "shadows" come to life and begin to grapple. One shadow throws the other into the ropes, then catches his opponent in the belly to belly suplex position after the unfortunate victim bounces over the ropes. Letting out a scream of rage and aggression, the grasping shadow spins, then releases his opponent up and over the ropes, sending him crashing to the filthy and unforgiving floor below. The triumphant shadow raises its arms in victory and lets out a scream of jubilation. "No matter what the cost or pain, young men and young women keep coming to places like this hoping for their big break, no matter what the costs to themselves, their families or their bodies. The camera shifts to the form of the defeated warrior, who lies unmoving among the remains of rusted chairs and broken glass, as his figure dissolves into nothingness. "On Saturday, February 1st, 2003, stars will be born, hopes will be dashed, and a legend… Out of the darkness, appears a man wearing a stocking over his head to hide his identity. "…will allow the world to look into his eyes for the first time, so that all can see the fire that burns within." With that, the image of the masked legend fades to black.
