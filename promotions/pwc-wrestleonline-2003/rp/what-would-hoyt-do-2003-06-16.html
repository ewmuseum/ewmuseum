What Would Hoyt Do?

16 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 16th June 2003 Eric Sagar wonders the street of central Atlantic City on a bright, hot, roasting summers day. He’s got a grin like a Cheshire cat. He has a hop and skip in his stride. A giggle in his throat and a gleam in his smile. Eric Sagar is wearing all white except his black tie and his expensive black Italian leather shoes. He looks like an angel as the material of his suit is so bright. A rather plump angel. As he waddles along with a definitive swagger he sees a man in a long black gown, a ruff unshaven beard and a long winding side burns touching his shoulders. The skull cap confirms it; approaching 'The Reverend' and honorary R.I.S.P. member, is an orthodox Jews. Orthodox Jews make Eric feel sick in the stomach. Make his stomach wrench up in a whirlwind. Normally he’d say something under his breath, but he’s in such a good mood he feels he can’t let it pass. He asks himself 'What Would Hoyt Do?' So he pauses and waits for the Jew to take a step closer then he holds his hand out. 'What can I do for you, sir?' The Jew asks. 'Shut up, Kyke!' Barks Sagar. 'You are an ignorant moron, a moron I say! And you will rot in hell for eternity for your SICK beliefs. [withheld — a passage held under the museum's content rule] All Jews are paedophiles. It’s a fact. God is waiting to grill you. You and all of your people are a joke. A SICK joke!' The Jew is flabbergasted. He can’t speak. He’s almost tearing up. He glances at Eric Sagar to see he has a grin. A cold grin. An Evil Grin. A sick grin. '…' Speechless. 'GET OUT OF MY SIGHT YOUR SICK! SICK! SICK! SICK!' Eric Sagar yelps in his harsh tone. The Jew wonders off in a zigzag, obviously disturbed by Mr Sagar’s line of thinking. Eric Sagar however struts on. He’s brought good into the world. As his shouting and balling had caught quite a crowd. He hasn’t noticed yet but a few, white youths are following him with a jolly chuckle on their faces. They’re listening to Eric Sagar’s words and are following him. He then approaches a group of three old ladies. All of them have the blackest black skin possible. As dark as a dark horse in a dark shadow. 'There’s more colours here than in a pack of Jelly Beans.' The Fat Preacher mutters to himself. 'What Would Hoyt Do?' The ladies are in the middle of what seems to be a jolly conversation. All of them are wearing bright dresses and shirts. They look like something off a Lilt advert. 'Excuse me, ladies!' Eric Sagar balls loudly with his chest out, ready to make an announcement. 'Yuss, young lad.' One of the three ladies say in a very thick and broad Caribbean dialect. 'When are you going home?' Eric Sagar asks. 'In about fifteeeeeeeen minutes, woi?' The old lady says politely. 'No, home as in where you come from. To Africa. Instead of littering the streets with your worthless jibberjabbering and let the decent hard working folk enjoy the privileges we get for being the hard workers. For being the people that made this country. For being the people that wages pay for this country to run. For being American!' This roaring speech has got a few more followers for the Preacher. 'U-S-A! U-S-A! U-S-A! U-S-A! U-S-A! U-S-A! U-S-A!' Chant the followers. 'TESTIFY!' Hollers another. The women just walk off angry and shocked. 'THAT’S RIGHT WALK AWAY! MAKE THE MEN THAT OWN YOU THEIR TEA THEN GO BACK TO AFRICA! GO LIVE IN A HUT! MADE OF STRAW! STRAW I SAY!' He bellows at them as they storm off. 'FOR THIS IS THE LAND OF THE FREE! FREE FROM THE COMPRESSION OF CORRUPT MINORITIES! AND TAKE YOUR MUSIC WITH YOU! IF I WANTED TO LISTEN TO A NEGRO TALK FAST I’D GO TO THE COURTHOUSE! AMEN! AMEN! AMEN! AMEN! AMEN!' Eric Sagar’s bright red cherry face is in complete contrast to his bright white suit. 'R.I.S.P! R.I.S.P! R.I.S.P!' The followers chants fade with the transmission.
