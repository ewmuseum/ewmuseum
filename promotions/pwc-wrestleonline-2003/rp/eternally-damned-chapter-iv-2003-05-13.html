Eternally Damned: Chapter IV

13 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: In a very dark time Atlanta 'Propaganda' Johnson parks outside the Gethsemane Cemetery, it’s a dark morbid place. On it’s own, it’s own street, like an outcast from the other buildings. The sky is a watery grey, but no rain, the light is blocked out by the huge ghoulish shadow of Nerezza, crouching on the peak of the Cemetery building scanning the grim Boston skyline with his lifeless eyes. Atlanta pauses, he takes a deep breath and looks at the cemetery, the home of death. Hessian should feel right at home here. Hesitantly Propaganda steps out, he strides along the weathered sidewalk with his head held high and taking deep breaths in. He tries to give off the impression he’s here because Hessian was an old co-worker. The cemetery gates are daunting, patterns and pictures made out of bent jet-black steel with withered plants, dying lizards, and two sinister eyes at the top of the gates. Propaganda takes a moment to stare at them. He opens the demonic gates which let off a howling screech as they drag along the stone floor of the Cemetery walk way. Inside the Cemetery, it’s bleak, dark, dreary. On the passage to the actual building there are graves and slumping dark turquoise trees splattered around. Cautiously he follows the path, it’s all silent, then there’s a rustle in the tree. Propaganda looks up, to see staring at him is the albino, Rhett Locke. Perched in a tree, wearing all white. Rhett stares Propaganda in the eye. Propaganda stares back. Atlanta’s eyes may be focused on Rhett. But once again his mind runs wild. All the terrible things Hessian did to him, crucify him, break Eric Sagar’s neck, attempt a genocide on his faith. Then all the things Eric Sagar said. Perhaps Sagar was right. Propaganda turns his back on Rhett and walks down the pathway, when he’s about a foot away from the dreadfully demonically daunting doorway a huge gust of wind picks up, slamming the gate doors together, shut, then for a very brief second the wind picks up a few piles of discarded leaves, rotting trash and a few dead rats carcass’ form the shape of a figure behind the gate, this figure is tall, sporting a cape and with a demonic presence to the figure, the figure is holding the palm of his hand up as if to say 'stop' the wind drops. Propaganda’s in disbelief. 'Brother Hessian?' Propaganda splutters. Propaganda turns round and heads to the funeral. Thanks to Matt and Alex
