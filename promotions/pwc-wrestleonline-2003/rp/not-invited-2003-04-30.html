Date: 04.30.03 03.01.03 Hey I bet you thought I was going to be the 'Black Star' on that fateful night in March. Especially after you saw that archive film of me hurting up KroW with my 'Conspiracy Theory'. I bet you thought I would be the 'Black Star' putting PWC on the map. Well know you know I am that Black Star, but back then things didn't look so good. I mean of all people Frankie Bones shows up, and just ruins what was supposed to be the biggest thing to hit the Pro Wrestling Circuit to date, but no... it's just Frankie Bones. Who the hell is Frankie Bones?! Do you know how high the ratings would've risen had they got in contact with me in time? Do you know that the Apollo Theatre would've been sold out beyond any Showtime At The Apollo could've ever done. I'm through talkin', they're just lucky I wasn't allowed in the arena, when I went gambling in AC.
