Quicker Than...

22 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/22/03 I remember. “Oi! Yeh’ve got ter be kiddin’ me, McKinley! Yer pubbin’ this ‘lil runt? Yeh go through a new lad ever’ week!” “Póg mo thóin, yeh bastard, an’ git inter the cage.” That bastard was named Rufus O’Riley. With a snap of suspenders against flesh, the stout, red-haired Irish man stepped up onto the bar and through the threshold of a cage-like structure. There I stood, young and energetic, my hair so long it nearly blinded me when I fought. I remember the way it’d flare up as I exhaled deeply. I remember the smells of stale ale and sweet Bailey’s. I remember. With a crack of the neck, O’Riley shut the door behind him as all the patrons surrounded the cage and threw paper bills onto the counter. “Fiyve the wee buggar downs ‘em in t’ree minutes!” Three? Oi, sucker’s bet. “Yer nuts! Dempsy’ll drop ‘em in terdy!” Yep. That’s more like it. “Oi! Awright, lads! Get to it!” The proverbial bell. Heh, the poor bastard. There he stood with his dukes raised… so unaware of the ferocity that lay before him. I almost felt SORRY for him. I still shudder at the thought of the first punch that broke his nose into five pieces. Almost sorry. When your farm’s in trouble, you’ll just about whore yourself out to anyone. You can’t have emotions when it stoops to that level, mind. You just can’t. It’ll break you. “Oi feck! Yah see tha’?! Christ, O’Riley, yew alive?” Mmm… that stung the ‘ole paw. Hurt him more, though. That’s all that counts. ‘Ole Riley hit the cage with that blast, his supple fat spreading and taking shape that the criss-cross design of the cage. His eyes bruised almost immediately, his nose completely flattened, the blood oozing from the would-be nostrils nearly drowning him as he hampered to breathe through his mouth. Shocked and confused, he staggered upright and, sickening as it was, laughed. Sadistic motherfucker… Only O’Riley could laugh after getting his bridge obliterated. “Fark this!” Smart. “Yer givin’ up awready, O’Riley?” “Heh! Tha’ was shorter ‘an a limerick!” …Limerick… Heh… so it stuck. I remember.
