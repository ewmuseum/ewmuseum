The World Torture Series - Part Two

20 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Unknown … SLAM! The door closed once again, causing the blinding artificial light to be blocked from the room. Jose was still face down on the concrete floor, his nose dripping blood and his eyes dripping tears. Was this still a dream or had it become real? The answer was unknown. The PWC Tag Team Champions were once again back in the room, no doubt ready to dish out even more punishment. ”Hey Jimmy, you bastard, leave him alone!” – a feeble and unsuccessful attempt that I had aimed at Jimmy Nguyen to stop the pain. There was no use shouting any more. No amount of expletives and yells were enough to warrant 2GVC to stop this… this punishment to my defenceless father. They couldn't even see me, let alone hear me! Jimmy Nguyen and Chandler Tsonda were both standing on their feet, marvelling in their ‘artwork’. With hands upon their chins, they carefully studied the piece before deciding their next task. “Grab that out of your bag, Chandler” Jimmy said. Without even hesitating to think, Chandler reached into an old, dusty backpack and lifted out a red cylinder object. “Do you want to do it or shall I?” Whatever it was they had up their sleeves, it would no doubt end in suffering for my poor father. Jimmy Nguyen, the man I hated the most in the entire universe, offered to be ringmaster of the ceremony by taking the object from Chandler’s hands. Once in his own, greasy, blood-soaked hands, he took a flame and set it against the object. He couldn’t help himself from shouting. “Let there be FIRE!” The object became visible… It was a candle – the flame immediately caught onto the wick of the candle as it slowly burned down the red wax. ”Jiiiiiimmmmmyyyyyy…. Jiiiiimmmmmmyyyyy!” – I shouted in a haunting manner, hoping to put off Jimmy from his latest plot to hurt my dad. He looked around the dimly lit room, searching for the origin of the voice that must have reached him in some manner. “Did you hear that, man?” Jimmy asked Chandler with a worried expression on his face. “Hear what?” Obviously only Jimmy had heard my shout. ”NGUYEN, you put that down and come face me… one-on-one!” – maybe if he’d heard my last shout, he’d hear this and leave my father alone. Jose had taken enough of this already! Jimmy just shrugged and brushed the noise aside. My new shout also fell on deaf ears. I realised then that nothing could stop 2GVC from doing this and that nothing could stop this dream, if these images hadn’t become reality by now. Jose wiggled his legs and groaned through the gag as he was slowly regaining consciousness. “Looks like the hard man is awake again” Chandler mockingly said. Jimmy slowly walked closer to my dad and turned the red candle upside down. The red wax reached boiling point due to the flame and it slowly liquidized. ‘AGHHH’ A collection of hot wax hit my father’s back. The yell heard was from me, not from my father. He was obviously taking this a lot better than I was – and I was only watching the punishment, not taking it! Another drop or two of boiling wax collided on my father’s naked back with a splash. It quickly burnt his skin and formed a solid mould of wax on the area it fell on his back. The look in 2GVC’s eyes was evil… like they were enjoying this sadistic event… as if it was a ritual for the sadists… like they were at one with the devil! I wanted the pain to stop. I couldn’t watch it any longer – I was slowly beginning to lose my composure. My father, however, was dealing with things a lot better – he was both mentally and physically stronger than I. But, no matter how hard I tried to take my eyes away from the scene, I couldn’t. My eyes felt like they were forced open by matchsticks and constantly fixed on the image of 2GVC torturing my father. Reality had become one with imagination and they could not be separated. 2GVC finally stopped dripping the hot wax onto my father’s back. I breathed a sigh of relief as they headed towards the exit of the small, cramped, unknown location. I prayed to the higher power that this would mark the end of the torture. Little was I to know, that this was far from over. “Oh, before we go Jimmy… one last thing” Chandler said, smiling evilly and pointing towards something out of my view. “Ah, why very nice choice, Sir” Jimmy remarked, sarcastically and rather cheekily. Nonetheless, he walked towards the location and took a hold of the object that Jimmy was pointing at. As Jimmy Nguyen walked back into my view, I saw a small metal bar tightly grasped in his right hand. ”STOP IT RIGHT NOW!” – I shouted, sounding like a spoilt little child. Jimmy brought the metal bar crashing down onto Jose’s back once… twice… and a third time. Once again, though, Chandler could not have enough of the punishment and pointed the isolated back of Jose’s head… …I TRIED TO CLOSE MY EYES… …But I couldn’t. My eyelids repelled each other… …I TRIED TO LOOK AWAY… …But my eyes remained focused on what was happening… SMACK! SPLASH! The metal bar hit Jose on the back of the head with high velocity and blood began pouring out of the gash created. The two men left the man to basically bleed to death. …I TRIED TO CLOSE MY EYES… …But my brain wouldn’t let me… …I TRIED TO LOOK AWAY… …But I wept a tear… ”WHAT IS NEXT? WHAT THE HELL IS NEXXXXXXXTTTTTTTT?!?!” – I shouted. It was audible to myself but to no other. This had to end and END FAST! My tear hit the floor at the same time as one of Jose’s tears. When would this be over? WHEN?!
