Date: May 7, 2003 What else was to be expected from a man such as himself? He knew he had no chance in Hell of making it on his own, so he decides to take the easy way out and make a cling-on of himself. That long hared freak just followed me to PWC. What was I to do about it though? Well I happen to think I've done a whole lot already, but that's not all I planned to do. 'He really thought he got away with that whole shit pudding gag I'm sure! But leveling him in his match the other night was just the beginning.' I said, thinking out loud. There I was, standing on the alter, or what was left of it, in this church I had found out in the middle of no where, left in almost ashes, for the taking. Although I admit, it's shit for living quarters, I have that arranged already. I wasn't looking for a place to live when I found this, I was looking for a place with a certain feel. How much closer to Hell can you come than a burnt down church? The place was perfect. Nobody had even touched it after the fire. Bibles still sat, burnt in the backs of the seats. The organ still functioned properly, and had yet to be removed from the outlines the fire made around it. 'This is the perfect place to be to get my sinister urges flowing. Kohana won't stand a chance, and by the time I finish with him, he'll wish he never came to PWC.' I began to scheme as I looked around, still amazed that nobody had taken the opportunity that I just had at claiming this place. I had found the perfect place to seek the evil that lay dormant deep inside. The evil that lurks in me had been chained down in Japan, a place I wish I will never have to return to. Now with the claiming of this church, which I have deemed the House of Evil, I have the perfect place to go when I need that little bit of encouragement to coax the evil out of me. 'Some people will think that I couldn't get any more sinister, well then they were stupid! And for those who don't see the big deal, saying that I'm not evil enough, and that they wanted to see me be more sinister...' I pause for a moment to let out a sinister smile, and a chuckle to go along with it. '...Well then, I guess I have no other alternative than to give them what they want.' My smile still plastered on my face as I take a seat on a burnt stool. I could already feel the energy emitted from the property inside of me. My hatred for those who don't believe in me, and for those who wish to defy me seems to have climaxed even more with my surroundings inside of the brunt walls that used to be a church. 'With this, I now have an even bigger advantage over Kohana, the little tag along. He WILL wish he never followed my footsteps when I'm finished with him, no matter what it costs me, I will make him realize, following ME was a big mistake!' I emphasized as I pounded on the bench I was sitting on, making it crumble a little. And to be honest, as I sat in that church that day, I began to wonder why certain things in life happen the way they do. Why does Kohana have to be such a little bitch? That's just a minor detail I wish I knew. But as long as I had my very own temple, I knew I would not be alone in thought any longer. 'The time is now!' The words come out of my mouth, but I had no clue as to where exactly the did come from. I didn't fret over it, however, and I began to lose myself in thought over what I should do next, in my fight with Kohana. He may want to fight me, just 'cause we were in PURE together, or because he doesn't like my attitude. But I was going to think of something more to make him think about before he finally steps into the ring with me. I will make sure he has all the more reason to take that very first swing at me. I will give him a damn good reason to step into the squared circle, so I can kick his ass! 'Evidentially them Natives don't know about biting off more than they can chew.' I said to myself, thinking out loud for the last time as I stood up, and walked towards the exit. I wouldn't want to overload myself in just one day. Maybe I'll come back tomorrow. Yeah, tomorrow sounds good... Really good!
