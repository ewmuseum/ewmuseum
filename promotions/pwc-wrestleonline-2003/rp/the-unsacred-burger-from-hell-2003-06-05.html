The Unsacred Burger From Hell

5 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5th June 2003 (After a recent post on the forums I thought about doing a roleplay going back to the essence and do it in script. Here you go.) Propaganda and Eric Sagar swing the double doors open, to see a rather quiet Burger King, then again it is Wednesday noon it wouldn’t be booming. Stalls across the walls with people in enjoying their burgers. There’s a light blue coat of pain splashed over the walls, with cardboard cut-outs of various Burger King mascots. Some are painted on the windows that overlook the hushed fast food restaurant. Eric Sagar: Burger King, hey? Eric Sagar stands stroking his heavy moustache. On looking the counter with only two workers standing behind the counter. One; a fairly attractive young woman, with long blonde hair and very small in height, the other; a spotty young man with greasy brown hair flowing wildly from under his box-like hat. They’re both wearing their stripy uniforms. Eric Sagar: Brother Propaganda, you sit down. I will order us our meals. Propaganda storms over to a stall, with no expression on his face at all. Eric Sagar waddles over to the counter. Spotty Burger King Worker: Hello Sir, how can I help you? Eric Sagar leans over the counter and squints at the lighted menu above the counter, not being able to read it, he flicks his hand underneath his usual immaculately clean and perfect grey suit, without a crease to be found. He reaches into the top pocket of his silky white shirt and grabs his glasses. He pulls them out and puts them on Eric Sagar: (Reading slowly and cautiously) Flaming… Tower… Burger… Eric Sagar slams his eye lids down then draws up in a long blink. Eric Sagar: Is that some sort of sick 9/11 joke? Spotty Burger King Worker: No sir, it’s a spicy bu— Eric Sagar: I don’t care. I’ll have, two of these… (patronising tone) 'Whoppers' and some 'Fries.' Spotty Burger King Worker: Coming right up sir, that’ll be $4.68, please. Eric Sagar: (Roaring in an outrage) $4.68! $4.68! …! DO YOU THINK I’M MADE OUT OF MONEY!? 'The Fat Preacher' rummages around his pockets and drops a handful of coins on the counter. Eric Sagar: This best be ambrosia! After a few five seconds of waiting. Eric Sagar starts to talk. Eric Sagar: So, son. Are you a religious boy? Spotty Burger King Worker: No. The preacher leans forward his eyes almost bulging out of his skull. Eric Sagar: NO!? The Burger King employee thinks fast. Knowing he’s about to get an earful. Spotty Burger King Worker: I mean… Yes. Yes. Very religious. Religious parenthood and everything. Eric Sagar: Oh good, because I’ll be able to taste the touch of a lowly no good Satanist on my food and if I do, I’ll be highly displeased. You hear me? I am not an easy man to please. Spotty Burger King Worker: (Mumbling) Tell me about it. Eric Sagar: WHAT!? Spotty Burger King Worker: (stammering) Umm… Well we’ll try our best to please you, we’ll make sure of it. Voice from the back: TWO WHOPPERS AND FRIES!!! Then four boxes dart down a metal slide thing from behind the till assistants. The Spotty worker grabs the four boxes and sweeps them into a small, brown, paper back with the Burger King logo on. He slams it on the counter in front of Eric Sagar. Spotty Burger King Worker: Two Whoppers and two fries, I hope you enjoy your meal and have a nice day. Eric Sagar: I hope I do too. Eric Sagar picks up the burger, goes to the stall where 'The Power Himself' Propaganda is waiting patiently glaring at the empty seat opposite at him. Eric Sagar: Here we are. Eric Sagar hands Propaganda his burger and he opens his. He lifts up the cardboard burger box and takes out the burger that doesn’t look as big as it on the advert or as appetising, but he opens his mouth wide, rap his teeth around the bum. He takes a chunk out. He throws it around his mouth. Swallows it. He stands up. His eyes start to bulge. His face starts to turn red. Steam starts to flow out of his ears. The pupils in his eyes turn red. Eric Sagar: WHAT IN THE NAME OF BABY JESUS AND THE FARM ANIMALS IS THIS!?!?!?!?!? Eric Sagar throws his Whopper across the room. Eric Sagar: THIS IS THE MOST DISGUSTING TRIPE I’VE EVER TASTED! 'The Fat Preacher' is drawing a lot of attention, his voice is booming throughout Burger King. He approaches the counter. Eric Sagar: YOU! YES, YOU! He beckons over the spotty Burger King worker. Eric Sagar: WHAT IS THAT YOU JUST SERVED ME!? BECAUSE I ASSURE YOU MY LAD, THAT WAS NOT EDIBLE! IT WAS FAR FROM IT! I’M SURE THE PENIS OF SATAN WOULD TASTE NICER THAN THAT! I DON’T KNOW WHAT EVIL BEING YOU HAVE HOVERING IN THAT KITCHEN BUT IT PREPARED THAT! Spotty Burger King Worker: (stuttering quietly) J… J… John prepared it. Eric Sagar: GOD IS LOOKING DOWN ON YOU! JUDGING YOU! JUDGINING YOU AND ALL OF YOUR COLLEAGUES AS SINNERS! CHILDS OF SATAN! YOU! YOU MY LAD WILL PERRISH! PERRISH IN THE PITS OF HELL AS THE DEVIL STABS YOU RAMPANTLY WITH HIS TRIDANT! AND EVERYONE WHO WITNESS’ MY— Spotty Burger King Worker: Would you like a refund, Sir? Eric Sagar: WORDS OF GO… Yes, I would like a refund.
