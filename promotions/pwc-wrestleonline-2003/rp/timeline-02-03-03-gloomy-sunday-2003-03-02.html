Timeline - 02/03/03: Gloomy Sunday

2 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/03/03 As Karina’s tired eyes open, the display from her digital bedside clock brands itself onto her eye. 11:43, later than she ever sleeps and half the day already gone. Sorrow masks her face, the path her mascara ran when mixed with her tears still dried onto her cheeks. ‘Ugh, what’s the point?’ Karina groans rhetorically just as it looks like she’s about to rise, before just pulling the covers back over herself. The events of last night don’t just weigh heavy on her mind, they squash it flat. When she woke up yesterday morning she was Karina Marie Wolfenden, daughter of Lawrence and Sandra, not a care in the world and living a life of leisure... An existence oh so different to that of the Karina who woke with a splitting headache this Sunday morning. A sad eye glances out of her bedclothes, sheets huddled to Karina as her only defences, and look once more to the clock where the date reads “MAR 02”... Rent day was yesterday. Would the money even be in her account? Would her cards work? What did she have left? Behind the clock stands the bottle of Vodka she used to drown her sorrows, the impressive volume gone explaining why it’s this late that the Boarding Beauty has arisen. Then, she finally decides to venture out of her comfort zone and a foot swings out and onto the floor. ‘Here’s to the first day of the rest of my life.’ she quips, putting a depressed perspective on the positive saying. Tossing the sheets aside, Karina sits on the edge of her bed, a thin pillar of light that funnels through the small gap in the curtains cutting across the thigh of her pyjama bottoms. With her head throbbing and body aching in accordance, Karina slowly rises and walks to the window, turning away and squinting as the curtains are ripped open, getting hit of the lighting change over with. Shying away from the midday sun that only intensifies the sharp pain running through her skull, Karina heads into the shadows that darken the path to her en suite bathroom. Bare feet tingling as their soles touch the cold tiles, Karina looks into the mirror. Eyes that sparkled the previous day now look dull and muted, bright shiny hair now dull and frizzy. ‘I look how I feel.’. Her head shakes in self pity as Karina turns on the tap and makes a bowl with her hands, splashing the water up into her face before shaking it off... And act that only aggravates her throbbing head. The water washes away the black tear streaks, but not the sadness, that remains. Standing, looking into the mirror, the Boarding Beauty looks a shell of her former self... No optimism, no determination, not even a look that says she wants to persevere. With a hollow exhale, she troops back into the bedroom, her one ray of hope being nullified as she looks at her Answerphone where the red digital display proudly boasts 0 messages. She’s now an orphan, no parents to speak of and only her meagre PWC earnings to survive on. Right now, for the first time in her life, she hates herself. The situation is of her making. Never one to second guess any of her actions, Karina now finds herself asking so many things about what she did last night... Her self-assured persona eroded by fear. Should she of just left PWC? Why did her father talk to her that way? Was there some compromise she could have suggested to avoid all this? ‘I guess what’s done is done.’ she commentates, leaning with her elbows on the windowsill, staring out into the brightness of the outside world from her shadowed den of self-doubt. Should she be angry at her father? After all, he was duped, tricked and manipulated by Magnum Wolf. Shown a highly edited tape to showcase the wrongs of PWC... But why would he take Magnum’s side over hers? Why wouldn’t he listen? Well, it’s all academic now, she made her decision. Her father is no longer in her life... ...But Magnum Wolf is. On Saturday she will once again be in the same building as him, the man who broke her family apart through lies and misinformation. The bank account of Magnum Wolf may have $15,000 extra in it for what he did, but Karina is going to make him pay for every last cent of it. He expected her to run, he expected her to quit, he expected her to surrender. But she didn’t. As low as Karina feels, as much as she regrets not trying harder to talk Lawrence around... She held firm. Karina Wolfenden is a wrestler now.
