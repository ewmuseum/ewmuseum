Me Against The World

20 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 20th April 2003 The bright lights of New York City surrounded the balcony of the 25th floor apartment. A cool night breeze blew away as the leaves of a single pot plant began to flutter in the direction the wind was blowing; a dark silhouette could be seen moving behind a sliding door. Standing still the shadow could be seen fumbling around with something in front of it’s body; moments later a small black rectangle could be seen in mid air flying across the room… moments later the sliding door began to open. Each and every venison blind fluttered softly as the wind entered the apartment and the figure stepped onto his small balcony. A soft orange glow shone through the opening of the door as the figure turned and closed it slowly behind him, making sure not to make a sound. It wasn’t as though the sound would’ve mattered, after all a busy city bustled below and noise during the late hours of the night would be expected… but all the same the figure made sure the door was slid shut, carefully and quietly. Bowing his head the shadowed man placed something in his mouth and clicked his wrist; a small clinking sound was soon followed by a click as the man flicked the flint of a gas lighter. A blue flame began to shudder as a small amount of light was cast over the mans face as he lit a cigarette, the lighter revealing his identity for a split second before soon being shut off as the soft glow of a lit cigarette took its place. Pacing a few steps along his short balcony the man turned his back and removed the cigarette from his mouth… a thick cloud of grey smoke could faintly be seen being blown into the night sky. Digging his spare hand in his pocket the man gazed up towards the stars that studded the sky and raised the cigarette to his mouth. As he drew the pale moonlight shone over his face and revealed a rather revered looking PWC Superstar. With a slight cough Charlie Loc exhaled another cloud of smoke before turning to the side and leaning forward over the balcony; the light was little but it cast enough to show the Vietnamese native deep in thought. How could this be? A man known for reacting before a thought could buzz through his brain deep in thought and concentration. It was the year 2003 after all and change had shown itself more than once with a lot of action throughout the short period that it had been. But then again…. Just as the thought of wondering what the man was thinking could cross Charlie began to talk to himself. His voice was soft and near silent as the wind whistled louder but he could hear himself and that was all that mattered. “Back again, can’t even keep my own promises.” His comment gave little insight to what was running through his head but if he was the Charlie Loc of old, he’d soon reveal his true colors and motives. “I guess it’s just fate…” Virtually he answered his own question. The small red light of the cigarette trailed to his mouth and glowed brightly for several seconds before being lowered and dimming; Charlie hung his head over the balcony and looked at the city below. People bustled around below like ants… just the way they seemed. The city never sleeps and it was oblivious to the fact that Charlie appeared to never sleep himself. People who think are often known not to sleep much as much can weigh down a mind or float through a persons head as they try to fall asleep. Perhaps the thoughts or emotions running through his head were to much to bear… an old wives tale was that a cigarette can clear all stress, Charlie was on to believe that. Exhaling the Superstar coughed, “Have to quit smoking if I’m back in the business… if I’m back home” His head shook and the smoke swayed as he looked up towards the stars once more. Everything that was running through his head could be narrowed down to one word… change. As he gazed up at the stars a look of home sickness could be seen deep within his eyes as the pale moonlight shone directly into them; life was much different to Saigon and it’d be hard to become accustomed to America once more… but the way he looked it was something he had to do. The moonlight continued to dance across his dark face as he took another draw from the cigarette and held in the smoke; amidst the look of home sickness demons could be seen burning brightly behind his eyes. He had left wrestling and his demons behind but they were returning once more. “This is my last chance…” he muttered softly into the moonlight. “I have to prove myself to be the best, to lead a crew once more” The weight had been shifted off of his shoulders when he left a crew in Saigon suddenly it had been dropped back on again… only this time it wasn’t as he was thinking. Perhaps the jet lag had messed with his brain…. Perhaps he was going insane as it was his first night away from Vietnam… Perhaps he hadn’t been informed of what was going on… The way Charlie was thinking, the way he was talking, the body and facial language he was giving made things look like he was the leader of 2GVC. He had no clue and his demons were playing tricks with his mind, “For once I’m the one who’s been there done that, for once I’m the one who is wiser” His English had improved and his thoughts suddenly became clear as his intentions remained clouded; he was the eldest and most experienced of all members in 2GVC, the way he looked at things he was to help them out just as the elders of his crew had helped him. “I’m not going to be like them…” his voice trailed off as the wind began to howl. His hair fluttered behind him as he squinted his eyes and took a short draw from his near finished cigarette. Exhaling a small puff of smoke Charlie wrapped his jacket around him tightly as he began to pace towards the opposite end of the balcony; hanging over he looked down towards the city below. “This is serious… this is my final chance” he took his last draw from the cigarette as the wind howled violently and the noise of the city below drifted upward. “I can’t fail… I won’t fail!” Exhaling the final draw from his cigarette he closed his eyes and tilted his head upward; everything was finally beginning to fall into place. Finally after several seconds he opened his eyes and leant over to the pot plant next to him, spitting into the base before wiping his nose. “They had better not fuck up…..” As his voice trailed off into the darkness he opened the sliding door and turned to flick the cigarette butt off of the balcony before closing the door shut behind him; pulling the venison blinds across the glass and closed as the light flickered off inside. The cigarette butt trailed down 25 floors to hit the pavement below… as it hurtled down it glowed in the darkness due to the wind; New York City was his home once more and although he didn’t want to be there Charlie wasn’t going to do anything to jeopardize that. The way Charlie had looked at things… he was allied with two men he hardly knew. Two half-casts… He was playing with them. But he was playing for himself.
