Girl Trouble Part 1

6 March 2003

Date: March 6 Girl Trouble Part 1 The scene fades from black showing a room in incredible disarray. Clothes are all over the floor, posters of teen stars such as Britney Spears and Hilary Duff are hung up on the walls and ceiling, a play station 2 is plugged into the television and still emits the red light of it being left on from the previous night. A loud rumbling can be heard from inside the room, but that’s not something worry about…probably just another California earthquake. The children that reside in this room are used to them by now…well they would be used to them by now. If this was California, and if the room belonged to children. No, this lovely domicile is the home of Chuck D’Andrea and Sammy Bello and that low rumble, that’s Chuck snoring. It used to keep Sammy up for hours, but after a year of living together he’s gotten used to it. So used to it in fact that he sleeps soundly now, so soundly that he has slept in today, and today of all days is Cousin Mario day. Now, Sammy and Chuck are always late for work, its become a common joke at the pizzeria that if you show up 15 min late your pulling a Special Delivery catchy right? But as of late, the boys have been on an attempt to provide something more of a different more ethical approach to work. I mean being such big wrestling stars now ladies have been calling in left and right to just get a chance to have their pizza delivered by the tag team tandem themselves. Ok that last part is a lie…Mario, the owner of Cousin Mario’s decided to tell them that every order called in was by sexy single 21 year old women. And our hero’s having as many brain cells as they do testicles have yet to come to an understanding that there are no hot women calling for them. None the less, it serves as a motivation factor for Special Delivery and sometimes…that’s all you need. “AHHHHHHH! WAKE UP WE ARE LATE!” cries Sammy as he jumps out of bed. He is wearing only his Sponge Bob Square boxer shorts and is suffering from the all male problem of “morning wood” He notices it and screams as he attempts to scurry into the bathroom before Chuck notices his good morning salute. “Huh? What time is it?” a groggy Chuck asks Sammy who in return is startled and trips over the strewn play station 2 chord. Sammy falls face first into the wall and into the cleavage of the Britney Spears poster. He lets out another girly scream as he falls over and crosses his legs carefully avoiding any damage to Sammy II. “It’s 11:30 Chuck we were supposed to be at work 11:00! We are majorly late.” Sammy says as he carefully reaches for his pizza pants and pulls them on over his boxers. Chuck half smiles and looks at his watch, he than pats his tummy as a loud grumble fills the room. “Yep, it must be work time…she’s hungry.” Chuck says with a grin. “Ya know I had a great dream last night Sammy” “Chuck, we don’t have time for this we are late. REAL LATE.” He throws on over his head a wife beater tang-top and then begins to button up his Cousin Mario shirt. A sense of urgency covers his face while Chuck has yet to even get out of his make shift bed of dirty cloths and a sleeping bag. “I dreamt we were back in high school and it was prom time again.” He smiles at the thought of his dream. “Not that dream again…did you dream you took Lizzie McGuire to prom? What is it the fifth time you have dreamt that in the past month?” Sammy’s some what mocking tone hints that there is even a touch a jealously. Perhaps Sammy wishes he had those dreams. “She was so great…she looked good, and danced great…and smelt great…and we ate pizza…man it was a great night.” He falls back into his pillow of greasy t-shirts and smiles as he stares at Hilary Duffs picture on the ceiling. Sammy snaps his fingers as he walks over to Chuck. “Ok newsflash FATS! First her name isn’t Lizzie McGuire its Hilary Duff, two she never went to prom with you…you never even went to senior prom, third she is only 16 which means she is way beyond jail bait and forth…she so would have gone with me over you.” Sammy smirks as Chucks happy dream is ripped to shreds. “Well,” Chuck says with a sigh, “she is a fine looking tweener. And if you want to be blunt Romeo, you didn’t go to prom either.” He returns Sammy’s smug look as he rises out of his bed. He begins to look for his pants as his tightly whitey underpants begin to stretch from him bending over. Sammy who is used to the sight but still somewhat revolted tosses him his monster size work cloths. “I would have gone to prom…I was going to go to prom…I asked Charlotte Bloom. You remember her?” Chuck nods. “Prettiest girl in the sophomore class, she was ready to go with me too.” Sammy begins to drift off into never, never land. “But than she decided to go with that stupid Pat Lloyd. You’d think he was the captain of the football team or something.” “He was,” Chuck adds sending a blow to Sammy’s dream, “and SGA president, and Varsity Wrestler and…” “Point taken, he was a little cooler than me so what though, look at me now, I am sexy, charming, and personable and I even have great teeth.” He smiles revealing a grimy sensation of teeth not being brushed. He frowns and continues. “I have a steady job, great friends, and enough publicity of my face on TV that will get any girl jealous. My phone should be ringing off the hook!” He finishes his speech to a slow sarcastic clap from Chuck. “Might I remind you that you haven’t had a date since…Charlotte Bloom back in high school.” “Not true! I have plenty of girls, so many that any minute now this phone will ring and a girl will call me asking to go on a date with the now famous pro wrestling SAMMY BELLO!” And just like it was on cue, the phone rings. The two begin to search for the phone through all the clutter and Sammy is able to get to it first. “It’s my house!” chuck yells as he grabs Sammy who takes the phone and ducks under the giants legs. Sammy answers the phone. “Hello?” his face lights up. He stares at Chuck with wide eyes. He points to the phone and mouths…”it’s a girl” “Yes this is Samuel.” more silence as Chuck watches with a keen eye. “Oh… than why did you call me?” Sammy’s face of compete delight has sunk to the lower depths of sadness. “Thanks, bye.” He hangs up the phone and throws it up in the air as he falls down into the makeshift bed that Chuck was sleeping in. “Well?” Chuck asked eager to find out who was on the phone. “Was it Charlotte?” Sammy’s lips curl into a pout, “No, it was Mario’s daughter Lori…work is cancelled because of the snow…and she only called me because Bello comes before D’Andrea. Chuck lets out a bellow of a laugh which rocks the room again as he condoles Sammy with an arm around his back. “Relax hot sauce; your lady in shining armor will rescue you one day.” He says with a serious tone of friendship. This doesn’t cheer Sammy up though, “And with my luck, she’ll look like THE VIOLATOR!” BLACKOUT
