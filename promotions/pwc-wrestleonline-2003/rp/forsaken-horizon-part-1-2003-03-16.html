Forsaken Horizon - Part 1 -

16 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 16, 2003 Jimmy’s eyes concentrated on the stout, shirtless, elderly Asian man that stood in front of him. His short gray hair was short and slightly curly where he wasn’t balding. He wore a pair of khaki pants that looked to be two sizes two big as they hung around his lower half. “Why have you come here?” He questioned, his arms folded across his wrinkled, yet muscular, chest. Jimmy’s hands were jammed in the pocket of the blue pair of ‘Dickies’ he was wearing, his eyes still on the man. “Well, it’s a long story. I basically have a match coming up. A very big match.” The old man looked puzzled as he cut Jimmy off in mid sentence. “Yes and…” Jimmy sighed, and then finished his sentence. “You don’t understand. This guy destroyed Chandler and I last time. Like freaking killed us. By himself, nonetheless!” The man folded his arms once again as he took a slow, deep breath. “Why are you here then, exactly? You haven’t been around for months and you show up now? I do not understand.” His eyes were fixed on Jimmy. The skylights on the ceiling of the small room let just enough sunlight to ensure visibility. “I’m sorry. But I really need your help Grandfather. I need training or discipline or whatever. I need to beat him.” The man's hand quickly darted upward, pressing against Jimmy’s chest as if to stop him from moving. “You need nothing. You want everything.” Jimmy sighed. “Ugh, Okay. So… I want to beat him. We want to beat him.” Jimmy pulled his arms from his pockets and brought them to fold across his chest. “I need to prove that I’m not just a cheating bag of hot air. I need to show what I can truly do.” Jimmy expression was now that of desperation. His grandfather gave him a stern look, and then motioned with his hand for Jimmy to follow him. “Come with me.” _____________________ Jimmy’s eyes shot open as he sat cross-legged on the floor of the small room, his hands resting on his knees. His gaze shifted to his right without his head moving the slightest bit. He saw his grandfather sitting in the same position he was with his eyes closed. He was deep in concentration. Without warning Jimmy lets out a large, sigh of annoyance and turns his head toward his grandfather. “What is this crap? This isn’t helping me? We’re sitting here concentrating, that’s not going to help me win.” His grandfather’s eyes slowly opened as his head turned toward Jimmy. His facial expression was blank but it was obvious he was displeased. “This is stupid. I’m getting up.” Jimmy began to uncross his legs and prepared to stand upward. “Sit.” Ordered his grandfather, his tone stern and demanding. Jimmy paused as he was partially standing now. “What? Why?” He question. “Just sit.” His grandfather’s voice was a little louder now. “Concentrate.” Jimmy scoffed as he sat back down muttering quietly to himself. “No talking. Quiet.” Jimmy was ordered once again as he repositioned himself and let out a large sigh of disappointment. __________________ “Stay still. This improves discipline.” Grandfather said as he walked past the oddly positioned Jimmy. Standing completely still, Jimmy balanced a wine glass filled with red wine on the top of his head along with two more on his hand that were outstretched to his sides. He was perched atop what looked like a small piece of tree stump, which he could only stand on with one foot, the other leg outstretched in front of him. “I’m still not getting this. And if I get any of this wine on my white shirt, you’re paying for it.” Jimmy complained. “Silence!” His grandfather screamed as he brought what looked like a small whip to the calf of the leg Jimmy was standing on. In a scream of pain Jimmy immediately dropped both glasses in his hands and reached down toward his calf where he was hit. Upon leaning over the glass perched atop his head tumbles downward, spilling all over the back of his white, button-up shirt. “Ahh! That hurt! And now my shirt! It’s ruined!” Jimmy yelled obviously annoyed and angered. “You should not have opened your mouth. Not a smart move.” Jimmy stood upward, pulling his fist back as if getting ready to hit his grandfather. Before anything else could happen, Jimmy found himself on his back. Courtesy of a sweep kick from his grandfather. “Never attempt to strike me.” The old man warned. “Now…” He said as he looked down toward the fallen Jimmy. “Onto the next thing.” He clasped his hands together with a slight smirk on his face which caused Jimmy to worry. “What? What’s so funny?” Jimmy’s tone was that of nervousness. His grandfather turned and began to walk away as Jimmy still tried to get his attention. “Grandfather!?”
