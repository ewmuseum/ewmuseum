Five Simple Questions [5]: Why Am I?

25 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 25th, 2003 Why? It is a question that I ask myself every waking moment of everyday that I pretend to be something that I’m not. It can be asked when pertaining to virtually every aspect of my life. Yet, it cannot be answered by anyone except for the man who will never tell. Sucks to be uniformed doesn’t it? Well, welcome to my world everyone. You won’t enjoy your stay, so I may as well not act as if everything in here is alright. What really gets under my skin though is that I’ll never know why things in my life can’t be alright and normal. I’ll never know what made him lead me down the path of becoming a professional wrestler, rather then a college football player and scholar like I originally planned. I’ll never know what made him offer me as a deal sweetener when agreeing to be a sponsor of the PWC and let the man formally known as The Phantom Booker into my strange, twisted, and unimportant life. All that I know is that I have to deal with it. So I’ll try my best to hold my head up high, despite the fact that the ideas he has filled it with weigh it down. I’ll try the hardest I can to do what I’ve been assigned to do and make you all hate me by acting as if I despise you and the true superstars that you support. I just hope that you all can realize that the man you hate isn’t me. If only I knew who me really was so I could tell you.
