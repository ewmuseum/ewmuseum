The Upstairs Downtown Cafe At Three In The Morning.

14 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/14/03 When you can’t sleep nothing beats an all night breakfast café. You get the largest cross section of America and all walks of life in one tiny dive. Stories that can make you cry, laugh, or run in sheer terror. Freaks, sheiks, hobos, doctors, drivers, saints, and Satan all visit for the 3.99 breakfast special. The Upstairs Downtown all night café is no exception. The walls were probably painted in the late 70’s with the pee green style of the day. After thirty years of grease fires and chain smokers the color has diminished even further into the realm disgusting. The floor looks like its seen it’s better years but nobody seems to care. The clock on the wall ticks away at a half past three in the morning as in walks Hoyt Williams wearing black sweat pants and a tank top. Anywhere else he’d be under dressed, and out of place for a cold February night in New Jersey, but at the Upstairs Downtown café nobody even takes a second look. Hoyt walks past the longhaired hippie whose eyes are glazed over like an Easter Ham. He takes his stride up to the front counter where the first dollar ever made is taped to a glass display case that sells addiction and gum. Behind the counter is an over weight, over worked, café owner whose dreams have long faded like the paint on the walls. Her charm and frankness cover up her depression and emptiness that even Christ would have a hard time filling. “Do you have anything healthy on your menu?’ Inquires the large framed wrestler of the PWC. “Yea sweetie let me get Juan to cook you up a sushi platter laid on a fresh lettuce leaf.” Retorts the gum smacking café owner with more then a hint of sarcasm in her voice. The overweight cop laughs at Fran’s response as he sips his black coffee. Once a top recruit in the police academy the years and opportunities have fled like a roach in the light. It’s a bad career move to pull over the mayor and give him a DWI. Those who don’t play the politics end up at the Upstairs Downtown café at three in the morning. If he had his life to do all over again he would have sold out a long time ago. If heaven is ran like mortal life then the good will be fucked out of their cloud and wings. The humor of Fran keeps Officer Mike coming back night after night. Someday they will marry but the cards aren’t their just yet. “I’ll take the breakfast special then.” Smiles Hoyt as he strangely feels somewhat in place in the dingy dinner. Fran gives Hoyt a wink as she writes down the order while smacking her gum. The thoughts of jumping Hoyt’s bones race through her mind like a Kenyan in a marathon. The fact that she’s old enough to be his mother doesn’t even enter the thought process. “I’ll bring it right out to you darling.” Smiles Fran with a welcoming warmth as she bats her eyes. The flirtatious behavior goes right over Hoyt’s head, as he’s too tired to understand anything. Mike the cop catches the comment and shovels a Krispy Cream in his mouth to ease the pain that goes hand and hand with jealousy. Hoyt smiles at a sign on the wall that reads, In God we trust but all other have to pay. Nothing like quaint wit to bring a smile to your face at three am at the Upstairs Downtown café. Hoyt takes a seat in a back booth where he can observe all the happenings of the active diner. A knot ties up his stomach as he watches Juan the balding, over weight, illegal, short order cook serve up Hoyt’s food with a cigarette in his mouth and filth on his dirty wife beater shirt. “Now I know why it’s only 3.99.” Mutters Hoyt under his breath. A man sitting in an opposite booth catches Hoyt’s eye. The man who is in his late thirty’s is toying with his greasy fry’s dragging them through a pool of ketchup. His eyes never leave the cesspool of heart stoppers and tomato paste. Hoyt can tell the man is hurting and sees him as an easy target to get a donation towards his missionary. All the man seeks is forgiveness and understanding something Jesus bathes in. This will be an easy sell for a pro like Hoyt. The man’s mannerisms and clothing tell Hoyt what his troubles are. They stick out like a black guy at a hockey game and there is no hiding them. From the smell of cheap perfume to the lipstick on his shirt’s collar this man has been cheating. Further proof is the gold band on his finger that bonds him to his wife in a symbol of marriage. “Don’t you throw the queen of diamond boy she’ll hurt you if she’s able. You know the queen of hearts is always your best bet.” Hoyt yells over the ambient café noise to the stranger lost in guilt. The man raises his head and looks into Hoyt’s eyes. “The Eagles? Desperado??” Responds the man in low tone and a soft smile. “You know your tunes well. How true are those words?” Asks Hoyt as he moves in for the kill. The sinner just shakes his head in agreement as he moves his eyes back to the ketchup for a second then back to Hoyt. With the mans attention, Hoyt starts to work him with hand gestures and subtle head movement. It’s amazing what you can accomplish just by knowing how to hold yourself. The things they just don’t teach you in school are the tools needed to succeed. “Can I ask you a question my friend?” “Sure, I guess.” Responds the stranger who is about to get lighter in the checkbook. To bad Sam Alotti wasn’t this easy. “If you died tomorrow would you be sure you were going to heaven?” Hoyt asks the man with direct eye contact and seriousness. Before the man can answer Fran slams down some runny eggs and bacon and points to a sign on the wall. “No cuss’n, fuss’n, or preach’n.” reads the sign. Hoyt smile at Fran who again winks at him with lust. His mission to save has been abruptly ended. The sign is the law in the Upstairs Downtown Cafe. He forgets about the man and digs into his eggs. Now if only Sam Alotti and Sublime knew their role in life like these strangers do at the Upstairs Downtown café at 3am. =w=
