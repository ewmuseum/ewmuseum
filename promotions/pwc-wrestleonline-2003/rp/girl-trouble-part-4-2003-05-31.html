Girl Trouble Part 4

31 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 31,2003 Girl Trouble Part 4 The scene fades from black showing PWC’s very own pizza boys in the midst of a match…Sammy flies off the ropes and dives at former PWC star The Violator but is awkwardly slammed on his back after a nasty reversal. The picture freezes showing Sammy’s face of horror and pain. The camera then pans out showing a non injured Sammy Bello sitting on a gym mat. Next to him, eating a slice of the ever popular Cousin Mario’s pizza is his road buddy Chuck D’Andrea. They are both in athletic warm ups, that both display Cousin Mario’s logo as well as PWC’s. These warm ups are part of the new ideas to get PWC’s name around New Jersey…even though Chuck and Sammy had to make the warm-ups themselves, with iron on computer patches. Behind the two boys, is a very large, burly woman. She has her arms folded and she is glaring at the two out of shape wrestlers. The woman walks over to Chuck and snatches the pizza from his mouth. “Snack time is over fatty.” “But, I wasn’t finished with that.” Chuck said sadly as he watches her throw the half eaten pizza into the garbage. “Listen you two lazy loafers. It’s time that you two start to get into shape. I have watched your matches and you make disgraces of yourselves in the ring.” Chuck turns to Sammy and whispers into his ear, “Wow, what a spark plug…maybe I should take her back to my place and get her to mellow out a bit. If ya know what I mean.” “Excuse me?” The woman interrupts Chuck who dumbly stares at her blankly. “Look lady,” Sammy says as he raises to his feet coming face to face…urr face to boob with the gym trainer. “Chuck and I are in perfect shape. I can do the uneven bars for hours and you need plenty of strength for that. Look at these pythons!” Sammy flexes his tiny arms at the woman who shows no sign of either laughter or fear. She just looks at Sammy with a raised eyebrow, and then turns to the TV screen that still shows a frozen face of Sammy in unbearable pain at the hands of the ravaging retard. “You see how you look there? If you let me do to you, what I feel like I should do to you right now. You will always have that face.” Chuck snickers at this comment and the woman just turns and swiftly moves a hand towards Chuck who has risen to stand. In one fluid motion she locks hair burly hands around Chuck’s Italian penis and squeezes. Chuck lets out a howl and slumps down to his knees. The woman lets go…as Chuck’s face curls into a series of humiliating grimaces. Sammy just smiles at the lady, after taking a step back. “Point taken. But, what do you think you can do to help us…uhh win?” “Well, first off, you need to set a permanent work out regime. You would think that being around real wrestlers would make you want to be one. After plenty of exercise we will work on eating right. This means, no more pizza.” Sammy and Chuck cringe at this. “No more, buffalo wings” Tears begin to fall down the eyes of Chuck. “No more, bread sticks.” Sammy falls back grabbing his heart in pain. “It’s time to make eating right, your top priority. You see boys; the key to success is a very simple equation. Eating right, plus constant exercise will equal wins.” Chuck and Sammy are still destroyed by their new diets. “But what can we eat?” A broken Sammy Bello asks. “Healthy food such as salads, cheeses, and selected meats.” Sammy smiles and looks at Chuck. “Hey, we make great salads at work.” “Stop, you will not to place a single Italian salad in your mouth from now on. The salads made at Cousin Mario’s are just as unhealthy as his pizza’s are.” Sammy and Chuck fall onto their backs staring up at the ceiling. They both wonder in their minds what they ever deserved to do to get into this awful mess which now has taken away everything they loved and replaced it with healthy food, running, and exercising. The exercise trainer steps over the two boys lying on the ground. She extends her hand to both of them and with great ease she pulls both boys up to their feet. “It’s time to start.” She says with a big and somewhat evil grin. The camera fades to black and music begins to play in the back round. “Eye of the Tiger, the ever popular theme from Rocky begins to play as the camera fades back in. In almost a video montage it shows clips of Special Delivery doing their work out. First it shows Special Delivery running throughout the town of Mays Landing. Their gym trainer leading the way with Sammy closely behind her…a moment passes and an out of breathe Big Chuck then follows in pursuit. The next show shows Chuck doing sit ups. Sammy is sitting on Chuck’s feet and keeping count. The camera pans up and shows a piece of pizza dangling in front of Chuck, and every time he sits up he attempts to bite it. The next shot shows Chuck doing bench presses with Sammy. While Sammy is being lifted by Chuck he is pressing up his own weights, although the total only is about 50 pounds. The next shot shows the infamous Rocky scene with Chuck and Sammy punching meat hanging a freezer. Sammy throws a sift punch on a hanging piece of lamb and reels back in pain. He turns away but cowers in fear as the infamous trainer makes a face at him to once again punch the meet. He continues to punch the meat with tears in his eyes. The camera switches shots again with Sammy and Chuck finishing their work out at the top of their stairs to their house. The woman comes into the shot and gives both boys a hug. A big 3 way hug ensues. “Excellent workout boys…I will see you bright and early tomorrow for the same thing.” Both boys moan as they open the door to the house. They walk in and close their door behind them. Chuck turns to Sammy… “Whose idea was it to get a female trainer?” “Well, she certainly has a nice rack…” The camera fades to the PWC/Cousin Mario Starr Treatment promotion logo.
