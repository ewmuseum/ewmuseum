Racial Tolerance

8 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10th June. 3:00am Eric Sagar’s bedroom, three o’clock in the morning. The room is dark, settling in an empty black. Eric Sagar is asleep, snoring loudly, sending gushes of air through the room. Slowly a creeping and creaking can be heard, this doesn’t awaken Eric Sagar. There’s a flick, from under the door of his room; a small bit of light escapes, hardly lighting the room. As if the lights in the hallways have just been turned on. 'The Fat Preacher’s' sleep is not disturbed. A raking noise, can be heard as if items were being scraped across a table. Then some more creeping can be heard. The door handle on Eric Sagar’s bedroom door starts to turn with an unoiled meow. Eric Sagar grunts in his sleep. The door opens ever so slightly with a squeal. In it sneaks a big 6’7' bald black man, wearing all black, from gloves to baseball cap. He’s carrying some dirty scars splashed randomly across his face - the only bit of his cocoa brown skin which can be seen. He has a black bag over his back, which jingles as he walks. It’s obviously full of stolen items he’s stealing from Eric’s house. He heads over to Eric Sagar’s dresser, on the surface of it alone it’s got; piles of valuables, all of Eric Sagar’s major and vital documents, all of his money which isn’t in the bank or down the back of his designer leather sofas, his credit cards and keys. With one clean swoop, the man drags all of the items into his bag, one coin drops to the floor making the quietists of clinks. Eric’s eyes shoot open, he shoots up out of bed to see the silhouette of this man. 'GET AWAY!' he roars. Lunging out of his bed to his dresser, he knocks the receiver of his phone off the hook and meshes a few numbers in an attempt to lunge for his drawer, he opens it and tries to grab the magnum revolver he has kept hidden in there. He drops the weapon that’s shining like a knight from his unsteady arms. Only for the robber to stand on it. Eric Sagar looks all the way up at this huge man. He switches his lamp on to make sure he isn’t seeing things. 'Don’t hurt me!' Eric Sagar weeps, cowering on his knees. 'SIT ON DA FUCKIN’ BED MUVVAFUCKA!!!' The man roars with harsh venom. 'But…' 'I SAID SIT ON DA FUCKIN’ BED!' The man demands, picking up the revolver and aiming it at the Fat Preacher. 'Ya done best be no doin’ no stupid shit! As Imma’ blast ya!' 'But I am just a poor elderly, preacher. I would never dream of doing you any harm. Especially if you have a gun and are burgling me.' Eric Sagar howls like a wounded puppy. 'Ya’ tryna’ be funny muvvafucka? You dun’ think I wont pop ya’? I will!' The man barks bitterly. 'I don’t dispute that, but why are you doing this?' Eric Sagar enquires. Cautiously the man approaches the dresser, still holding the gun squarely pointed at Eric Sagar’s head. 'To get into da fuckin’ circus. Whotchu think?' The man sarcastically growls. 'On da paper chase.' 'I assume that means to earn your bread for you and your family. But that isn’t the way my brother.' This triggers some interest from the burglar. 'Whut?' 'Yes that’s right. God is watching you. He’s judging you and he knows you better than anyone. Even you. He knows your full potential. He knows that you are not just a thief. You have intelligence, you have friends. You have Jesus. Jesus loves you, my friend.' The man draws up an antique Victorian wooden chair, he sits down to think, drooping the gun down on the floor. 'Jesus… loves me?' He questions. 'Yes. He loves you. He loves all of us. As we’re all good people.' 'Really?' He stammers. 'Yes. Jesus loves you.' There’s a few seconds of silence. 'GET HIM!' Eric Sagar roars, startling the man. 'What?' The man blinks, then the door of Eric Sagar’s bedroom is ripped open and 'The Power Himself' Propaganda launches himself at this back, snapping his neck forward with a lariat. Mounting him, then pummelling with vicious rights and lefts full of venom. 'Damned Coloured. They’re all the same, thieving, bags of worthless scum, all of them. If we lit him on fire he’d burn due to all the grease off the KFC megabucket he just ate. Well he’s going to join where the rest of his people deserve to go. PRISON! For coming in here holding an unlicensed weapon and pointing it at me.' Eric Sagar cackles as he put the phone back on hook. He picks it up again and dials 911.
