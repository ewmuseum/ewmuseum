A Ride Through Your Card

31 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-31-03 Welcome, ladies and gentleman, to this bitter little existence that we know as reality. If by any time, you are frightened by the perks of this ride…too bad. Take a look around, perhaps over to your left where you can see a visual of this week’s PWC television card. It may be a bit blurred, but as your cart moves forward, you will notice a visual break-down of the card, or whatever parts we feel like making fun of. If you look straight ahead, you’ll see a stalker with nothing better to do than try out women’s lingerie on his head taking on a man who steals eagles: Now THERE’s a real criminal. Neither of these two men, nor that poet guy who’ll undoubtedly get involved, are of any consequence to you, fans. You’re, of course, here to see the jewel of PWC, but you’ll have to wait, as we’ve got to go through the slums to reach the heights. Oh dear, you may want to ask the children to look away as we progress on to this next representation, fans. Those two mounds of flesh are just unfortunate results of inebriated breeding by semi-humanoid creatures and we apologize if your young children are a bit frightened. That’s often the case with those Italian fellows who are always trying to hang around the studio. Near those two blubbery behemoths, you’ll also find a snippety lawyer and the definition of a wash-up. That guy with the messed up hair would be Double C, the new attorney for our ever-so-powerful owner, the one who needs three guys backing him up to beat up a simple nig…well, to beat up some push-overs. These two debuted last week, with ol’ Ronny’s backing and find themselves, not surprisingly, facing the most useless pair in…well, in a realm that doesn’t include the Leprechaun and Jake Keeton, of course. Fans, you may again want to cover your children’s eyes unless you want them turning into colossal losers such as one of our next competitors. He’s pathetically hopeless, so much that he’s now becoming a perennial loser to the females of this federation. His opponent? A figurehead for the new powerful woman in the federation, but one that, like all women, is using her twat to get ahead. What a surprise, our poon hungry owner hired a whore for his mid-card. Wonder how she got the job? At the risk of death by boredom, we’ll move on to our next stop. I…ahh…Zzzzz…WAIT! Ack, just move on, the Keeton rays of boredom and the meaninglessness of this belt are corroding your brains! Oh right, now we’ve got the man called a ‘greasy wop’ by his own fan, Bang Davenport. The proclaimed first PWC champion: Angelo Deville. Sure, he’s a tough guy. Hell, 2GVC even has a few, but no more than that, ounces of respect for the stupid mook. A gangster that tied Jason O’Neil up in a closet somewhere is scary, sure, but look who our future champ is fighting: Ms. Botched Dropkick herself, Karina Wolfenden. Now, in all honesty, we’re not really sure if she didn’t give some tongue incentive to get hired after what we’ve seen from her. A semi-main event that’s worthy of perhaps a minor league fed, but not for one that’s now reaching the New York metropolitan area. Finally, folks, we’ve reached the pinnacle of this little ride. Our main event showcases firstly the team of Jim Axtell and Totakoxe Salvador. Our narrator has dissed them so many times that it’s really not even needed any more, just to simply tell you that they obviously won’t be winning this main event. Penance, a beastly face, true, but also someone of beastly proportions. However, the solo tag champion can’t walk out another show with four men at his throat and win. And, thus, we come to the gem of PWC. Two men who deserve this main event spot they garner this week. Jimmy Nguyen and Chandler Tsonda find themselves, meriting such a spot, in the spotlight of the federation, putting on the big kahuna of a match this week. A shining crystalline apex to an otherwise mediocre field. Say, shouldn’t these two be here every week? All those on this ride who think so raise your hands. Yeah, that’s what I thought.
