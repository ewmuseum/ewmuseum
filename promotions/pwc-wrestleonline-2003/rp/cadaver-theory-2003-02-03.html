Cadaver Theory

3 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/02/03 'Was that as good for you as it was for me ladies?' asked Trent mockingly as he lit up a cigarette. 'Like carrying a dead horse for a solid 3 minutes!' shouted Lennon back. 'Song is 4 minutes actually sweetheart, and if anyone was carrying anyone, it was my lyrics carrying your 1970's Black Sabbath style bass lines.' replied Trent grinning while he exhaled a cloud of smoke. 'Screw you both, my drums held both your asses together' came the monotone voice of Maynard, who's original name was Mayton which he enjoyed having as much as a root canal. So, just like the other members of the band who were die hard Tool fans, he took his respect for them one step further and legally had it changed after the lead singer Maynard James Keenan. 'I think Gideon may have something to say about that' chipped in Trent, who always made it a hobby to remind Maynard that he didn't have to have his name changed in respect for one of his favourite vocalists, Trent Reznor from Nine Inch Nails. Gideon placed his black X-Cort guitar in it's case and sealed it shut before looking up at his three remaining band members who he had played with since college. 'If you ask me.....' He stood up straight and pulled his guitar case from up off the floor. 'All 3 of you suck cock as bad as Trent does' 'We all got to earn a living honey' Trent replied sharply before all 4 members broke into laughter. There were 4 people Gideon trusted in this world. Three of them were standing in the small garage at the back of Gideon's childhood home which was now abandoned and almost derelict. Lennon, Maynard and Trent. The fourth person was the owner of the reflection that was staring mysteriously at him from the mirror near the door of the garage, himself. 'So what time are we leaving?' asked Lennon. '4:30 ' replied Gideon. These band practices were not often, and were made a special occasion when they did occur which was rare to say the least. The four man collective known as Cadaver Theory had once had high hopes of supporting Pantera as they created their own style of music through high school and college which they classed as a fusion of early Pantera and Tool. Yet, things change. Trent owned his own music store in the centre of New Jersey, not far from the area in which PWC would spend most of their time touring. Maynard worked for an underground record label known as Dark Era Records and was in charge of arranging booking dates for several up and coming bands across the country and signing new acts. Lennon himself married at the age of 28 and amazingly has stayed with his wife ever since. What was even more amazing was that his wife became a millionaire by the age of 27 after her father, an oil contractor died and left her all his worldly riches. What was even more amazing than that was the fact that Lennon knew he was in love with her without knowing that if he married her he would marry into an amazingly rich family. 'I'd have married her when I was 25 if I'd known and killed her father by the time I was 26!' was a favourite quote of his whenever the subject became a topic of conversation. As for Gideon. Well, in a nutshell, he travelled the country studying martial arts, followed by travelling to China and Japan to continue his education in the arts, in the process winning an underground martial arts tournament held in a derelict Monastery in China hosted by a set of Shaolin monks who's dangerous styles had been outlawed. Then as he planned on returning to America, he got poisoned by a twisted old man. Easy enough to believe huh? Yeah, it took 8 shots of Jack Daniels each to eventually convince Trent, Lennon and Maynard. The fact that these 3 men didn't treat Gideon any differently made them even more special to him. The fact that all 3 men, including Lennon with his 6 figure total in his bank account all lived in New Jersey at the heart of the PWC operation meant that Cadaver Theory would once again be in a social radius of each other. For the first time in a long time Gideon was excited and had a few very small plans for the future, both for himself and Cadaver. 'Anyway gentleman, we best be off' said Trent. He and the others came to help Gideon pack after they looked on in shock at the debut of PWC Television and seen their old band mate and friend make his huge impact. It was meant to be a surprise, however the surprise backfired when they discovered that Gideon's possessions were basically the clothes on his back. Lennon himself however, paid for the flights to and from Bangor. Yet with all his riches he still felt most comfortable sitting on a hard cold floor with a 4 string Ibanez bass in his grip dressed in his black torn denim jeans and black tank top. 'Where is it you're staying again?' asked Maynard. 'Some motel out on route 45' 'The old fucker found you a place yet then?' asked Lennon as he referred to Jeremiah. 'Yeah, some old abandoned house, lot like the one out of Fight Club. I've seen homeless people with more respectable accommodation. ' 'Yeah, and I've seen homeless people with a better dress sense' replied Lennon on Gideon's usual dress sense. The four members of Cadaver Theory slowly laughed. Gideon had almost forgotten how it had felt to truly and humorously laugh, he almost felt like the muscles used to do it were shaking the dust off themselves after not having done it for so long. Minutes later Trent opened the door of the small cramped garage and strolled outside, the basking sunlight making the eyes of all four men retract and sharpen, especially Gideon's as he followed the three men outside. The sun itself almost seemed to shy away from the sight of him as he looked at his 3 companions shove each other jokingly while walking over to their large red pickup. 'Roland' he called gently. Soaring out from the depths of the garage came the bold and mighty eagle himself and as usual, perched himself casually on Gideon's shoulder. Make that 5 people he trusts in this world. 'Time to take on the world one more time old friend.' Gideon looked ahead and saw Lennon sitting in the back while Maynard and Trent duelled in a game of scissors, stone and paper to decide how would drive. 'This time though, we've got a few allies backing us up.' 'HA! Paper beats stone, eat shit!' yelled Trent triumphantly while Gideon placed his guitar in the back. His journey through PWC was just beginning, and he already knew it would be a long hard, yet hopefully enlightening one. Yeah, who was he kiddin'?
