Timeline - 01/01/00 : Morning Has Broken

17 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 17/05/03 Karina Wolfenden is sitting, knees hugged tightly to her chest, on an unremarkable patch of grass... A patch of grass, however, that will soon be bathed with historical light. The ground still damp and the morning air chilly, Karina doesn't mind these minor distractions, for she is about to witness something truly special. On the Fijian island of Taveuni, dawn was rapidly approaching, ushered in by the first light of the new millennium. Back at the village, the celebrations that had been going on since midnight could still be heard, the first people to experience the 3rd millennium not being able to think of anything better to do than drink. On a planet of 6 billion men and woman, the populous of this island were blessed, could say they had experienced something nobody else on their world had... And most treated it as a novelty. Such beauty, such majesty, such history... Lost in an alcoholic blur. Karina knew she was lucky, a trip to this tiny speck on the globe at this precise time was beyond the financial means of most... And was booked years in advance. The child of the 80s, the girl of the 90s, will soon become a woman of the noughties... Although she hated that term. Soon, achingly soon, the first light of the year 2000 will highlight her bare toes in soft amber as the horizon gives birth to the low Sun... And her startling green eyes are open to it all. News stations will relay a feed all around the globe, anyone with a TV set can see it for themselves... But they'll have to watch through glass, they'll have to watch it as pixels, imprisoned by plastic casing. They won't feel the same shiver up their spine, the same uncontrolled, minor adjustment of their pupils as light floods into their world. This was something you had to experience first hand. Her time in the 20th century had been spent in a bubble, protected by her parents... Shaped, moulded, influenced. She was indeed her father's daughter, but this was the dawning of a new age in more ways than one. For too long she had been repressed, feeling she had to fit in with their image of how the Wolfenden's only daughter should be. Karina would never be who they wanted her to be, and in trying, she could never be who she wanted to be either. What better time than now to make a New Year's resolution... A New Century's resolution... A New MILLENNIUM’S resolution. Already, the sky bordering the horizon was highlighted, the dawn was approaching first, and when it did... Karina Wolfenden would be Karina Wolfenden. She was lucky, she was privileged, and it was time for her to become independent to boot. No longer would she be imprisoned within a family ideal, no longer told how she should dress, how she should look, act... Think. When the sun rose, she would be her own woman. This wouldn't just be a new day for the planet, it would be a new life for Karina. Here, on the 180 degree meridian, she would be reborn. Amidst the beautiful foliage of this garden island, with the birds singing their morning chorus in her ears, with the sharp breeze blowing her hair, a new woman was ready to emerge from the cocoon of her youth. 'First off, this is getting cut.' she decides, running her cold hand through the long, mousy hair that falls down her back. 'I think I'll take it short, choppy... Throw some colour in.'. The fine strands fall between Karina's fingers, in front of her plain face as she makes herself over for the millennium. Then, she saw the light... Literally. 'It's.... It's....' mutters Karina softly to herself, in awe of what her wide-open eyes are seeing, witnessing, 'It's.... Beautiful....'. Morning had now broken upon the island of Taveuni, and the first light of the 3rd millennium was creeping it's way up Karina's body, leaving everything it passed over brighter, more vibrant... On an increasingly shrinking planet, Karina had found her own little piece of Heaven in the Pacific Ocean, and she wasn't going to waste it. What Karina decided on before the sun rose would indeed be done, to remind her of what her eyes were seeing. As long as she lived the life she wanted, that feeling would stay with her. There was no way Karina Wolfenden could go back to Albany, back to her parents and continue to be the shell of herself, the shell that was in danger of becoming who she really was. You can't witness such majesty, such an awe inspiring sight, without it having some effect on your life. And it had a far reaching, timeless, infinite, life-changing effect on this young woman... ...It made her Karina Wolfenden.
