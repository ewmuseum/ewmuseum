Disposable Paladins - Part 1 - Chasing Ghosts

19 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 19/03/03 Part 1 ::' Again, back to our leading story here tonight, another body of a young woman has been found severely mutilated by police in the downtown New Jersey area. Police officials are reported to have little to no leads on the case, or any solid reason as to support why the killer is targeting young females. Here is George Willard live at the crime scene where-':: Bauerman turned the television off with the remote and rubbed his forehead with the palm of his hand roughly as he poured himself another strong drink. He took a slow deep breath before drinking the entire content of the glass before loosening his tie out some more and undoing the top button of his white shirt as he sat down on his black leather couch. 'Slip up' he muttered to the four walls of his living room, hoping he wouldn't wake his wife and kids. 'For the love of God slip up' The scene then gently fades to black. Our scene reopens with Bauerman sitting in a small stuffy office, the sound of computer keys hacking away in the background and telephones ringing constantly cringe through his ears as he glances across the desk opposite him which is smothered in paperwork. 'Alright Bauerman!' came a stern voice followed by the sound of a door slamming shut. 'You better have something for me and it better be good!' 'You know just about everything that I know 'Stop feeding me your bullshit Bauerman!' 'All due respect Chief Calderidge, but I think you wouldn't need to put up with my shit if you were to put me back on the street' Chief Calderidge sat forcefully down in his plush black leather chair as the rooms dingy light bulb shone glisteningly on his bald head. 'Not unless you have someone to go in with, not someone from this department cause Lord knows all these people have their heads wedged so far up their ass they can taste what they had for lunch last night through their own shit!' Bauerman sighed and quickly contemplated what he was about to get into, what he was about to suggest, and who the man who played a large factor in all of this was. 'I may know a man' 'Who?!' snapped Calderidge. 'G...' Bauerman stopped hesitantly before continuing. 'Gideon Poe' There was a tender long silence before it was broken by Calderidge and his lung busting deep yell. 'Are you going AWOL on me Bauerman?!' 'No sir.' 'You've been chasing this phantom for how long, 8 or 9 months? Not a shred of evidence. Hell, we even searched through the only half descent fingerprint we have of the guy. What did the results come up as Bauerman?!' 'That he's....' 'DEAD! THAT'S RIGHT! DEAD! Gideon Poe may as well be a figment of your imagination as well as a fictional paladin to these people. He makes us look like a laughing stock by doing our job better than we do. Hell, if he was sitting before me right now I'd give him your badge!' Calderidge reached over his desk and retrieved a long Cuban cigar from a oak varnished box and slowly lit it up, engulfing the room in it's deathly strong aroma. 'Okay, but if you find this so called Shade, by all means use him. It'll at least make for good press. Hell, might even scare this killer away...HA!'
