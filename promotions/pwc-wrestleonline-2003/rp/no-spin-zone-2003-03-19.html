No Spin Zone

19 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/19/03 How can a man of God support a war that will take the lives of thousands? When does religion turn a blind eye to murder and violence? What justifies killing one man to save another? These are questions that have plagued the mind of Hoyt Williams for the last week or so. While his father was out capturing the PCW world title our president was preparing a speech to declare war on Iraq. Hoyt Williams who is wearing a suit and tie is ushered to the New York set of the No Spin Zone on the Fox News Channel. Hoyt shakes hands with the make-up caked host Bill O’Reilly and the plump fashion nightmare known as Natalie Maines. He sits down in the seat and the lights quickly fade up as the show comes out of break. “In the back of the books segment tonight our topic is Natalie Maines, lead singer of the Dixie Chicks, who is finding out that sometimes saying you're sorry doesn't make much of a difference. Radio stations nationwide are boycotting the Dixie Chicks, even though Maines publicly apologized for telling a London audience last Monday: 'Just so you know, we're ashamed the president of the United States is from Texas.' Why did Maines put down the president? And are fans being too hard on this Dixie Chick? Joining us in the Studio is that Chick, Natalie Maines. On the other side of the desk is conservative professional wrestler Hoyt Williams who has spoken out publicly on Mrs. Maines is comments saying she should be deported. Lets start with you Mrs. Maines what prompted you to make such a stupid comment?” Bill says into the camera like a trained professional. “Well to be honest Bill it was a stupid place to make a personal comment.” Natalie retorts. “Mrs. Maines the comment was out of line personal or not. People aren’t spending money to hear you rant on a soapbox they want to hear you sing you’re a singer not a politician. Hoyt, what do you think of this?” Bill says cutting off Natalie’s follow up answer. “Jesus died on the cross to pay for our sins. Natalie needs to pay for her sins by being banned and deported forever.” Hoyt responds. “Yea that’s real American!” Natalie cuts in with sarcasm. “I have to agree with part of what you said and disagree with the other. Mrs. Maines I hope the American public continues to boycott your music but I also hope it’s not permanent. I think America needs to send you and others like you a message. Damage will be done and I hope you gals can pull though this and survive it. As far as being deported Mr. Williams come on she’s not funding terrorists she said a stupid comment, which she’s apologized for.” O’Reilly snaps back with an arrogant tone and presentation to him. “She needs not to worry about her comments here on earth because when she gets to heaven she’ll never get past the weight limit first of all. Secondly God doesn’t like foreigners and people who don’t support good Christians like President Bush. I’m sorry to say this, but Natalie is going to hell.” Hoyt responds with the same arrogant tone in his voice that O’Reilly had. “Why don’t you go f(BLEEP)k your self!” Natalie yells before Bill has the mics shut off. Bill face turns bright red as Natalie and Hoyt mic’s cut out. You can see the two yelling across the table at each other with Bill using his arms to try to calm them down. “That is ridicules Mr. Williams and both of you need to calm down. Were out of time anyway Natalie I’m going to give you the final word.” Bill yells losing his normal cool. “I’d just like to say I’m sorry for my comments and wish the troops the best of luck.” Natalie says while eyeballing Hoyt. “I think that wraps things up lets take a break.” Bill says as the camera cuts to a break. =w=
