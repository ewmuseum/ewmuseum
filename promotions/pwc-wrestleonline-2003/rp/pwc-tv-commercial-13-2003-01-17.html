PWC TV Commercial 13

17 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Originally aired on WGTW TV-48 in Philadelphia, PA at 2:25pm EST Friday, January 17th. The scene opens inside a dark and gloomy setting. It is hard to make out shapes or colours, but it seems that we are inside a conical environment, as it is darker and tighter near the top, whereas (as the camera pans around in a circle) the base seems to be wider. The only light, muted as it is, comes from a low fire that has been banked, so that only the ashes … and the rocks that surround them … glow faintly red. A hand suddenly reaches out from the shadows and lets a handful of grey powder fall onto the ashes … that suddenly explode in blue flames, filling the area with blinding light. The scene fades gently back down from blinding white, through the spectrum until it reaches a comfortable medium … and everything becomes clear The flames from the fire are burning gently, a normal … natural ... red and yellow, and from their light we can see that we are inside a tent that appears to be made out of a thick hide, stitched together to form a whole piece. Sitting cross-legged in front of the fire is a solitary man. He is dressed in just a pair of brown trousers, which also seem to be made out of natural hide. His body … lithe and extremely muscular, evidence of many hours of exercise … is sweat covered, his face coated in red paint that covers his eyes like a mask that runs from one cheek to another, and as he looks straight into the camera, his green eyes almost seeming to pierce the soul framed as they are by the long auburn hair that flows down to his waist level. He begins to speak. “Once upon a time, there was a man who had it all. Talent, ability, experience. A body that was at the peak of physical perfection. This man was at the pinnacle of his sport, winning titles and accolades wherever he went, time after time. He flew high … he lived his dreams … the flames of desire burned bright within him … and then he crashed, he fell from grace …the dreams shattered and the fire went dim. Injuries took their toll … rivals broke his body as defeats broke his spirit … “ The fire starts to burn down, the flames getting lower as the light starts to slowly dim. “ … but now, one last time, can the impossible become reality? Can this fallen man fly again … once more live his dream … … will he rise from the ashes?“ The man settles back, his form and features fading into the twilight as the flames die further until everything blends together into darkness.
