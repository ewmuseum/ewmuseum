I used to believe...

7 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/7 I used to believe in things. I used to believe that everything happened for a reason. That there was a plan for us all. But when a life pans out the way mine has, one has to hope that no one would plan something this macabre. So if there is a higher being, if there is something up there looking out for his children, planning out their lives; he must have looked over me. My life has no plan. It is simply an endless string of events that have lead me down the path of my life. Events that have lead to an overgrown cynicism that controls me. A cynicism that has lead me to lose all belief, in everything. All I believe in these days is myself, and lately that has been faltering. I used to have good intentions. I tried to do what's right. Especially when it came to wrestling. Occasionally I would let greed get the best of me, but it was a rare occasion. These days that drive to do what's right is simply gone. I only want to do what is right for me. After years of looking out for others, I decided it is time to get what's mine. But it ain't easy. I'm so glad Ron Starr, Clark Carlisle, and J-Con have come along. A goal easily obtained isn't a goal worth gaining. And if there is one thing I like more then obtaining a goal, it's destroying someone to get what I want. I cannot wait until I stand over three beaten, broken bodies in a ring covered in blood. Breathing heavily as the mist of spent blood spreads from my lips. A massive warrior with heaving chest, standing over his victims. I cannot wait for this moment, because in those few choice minutes, I finally believe again. I believe I have a purpose. I believe that I am here to destroy and maim. I believe that I AM in control, and if there is a plan for these three, I'm disrupting it. I make them believe...
