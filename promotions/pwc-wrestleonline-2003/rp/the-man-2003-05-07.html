The Man...

7 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11:17am 10th May 2003, 6:46pm 10th May 2003 and 8:57pm 10th May 2003 The scene is black, silence, it’s like this for 15 seconds. Gently some light opera music starts to play. Then with a roaring yelp and crash a clip of Propaganda hitting the Exodus on Vampir Nosferatu, Vamp’s back slamming against the ring canvas and bouncing out of the ring. 'The man who inflicted a new sort of violence on Vampir…' Then a clip of Lillith being hurled at a rope then being met by an ungodly big boot to her face. 'The man who gave Lillith the boot…' The scene shows Eric Sagar planting the drugs in Nick Casey’s gym bag and then shows Propaganda slamming a chair on the back of ANC’s head while he’s not watching. 'The man who outwitted Nick Casey.' A clip of Propaganda setting Aimz on fire in there inferno match. 'The man who showed Amy Campbell the light…' Various brutal clips from the Propaganda v Hessian match at Old School Entertainment’s New Years Revolution flick through with pace. Then ends with Propaganda screaming while being attached to a cross. 'The man who took the extreme nature of Hessian to its limits.' A clip of Propaganda hitting the 11th Commandment on 'Smart Ass' Matt Register airs. 'The man who helped rip the OSE Tag Titles from Ronin’s possession.' Next is a clip of Propaganda and Hessian talking in their locker room at Old School Entertainment’s Head On Collision. 'The man who was part of a unified alliance between Heaven and Hell!' The opera music is getting increasingly louder, as the volume ascends a more demonic crazy feel comes to it! We see Propaganda holding up the OSW International Title high in the air in victory after beating Rhett Locke for it. 'The man who promised to strike down upon the evil in wrestling with VENGEANCE AND FURIOUS ANGER!' A clip of Propaganda standing with the OSE tag title around his waist. 'Sent from Heaven…' Propaganda hitting a clothesline. '…To show you the light.' Propaganda looking up to heavens. 'Gods Ultimatum…' A clip of Propaganda fighting and winning in the four way match with Frankie Bones, Trashcan Man and Ivan Stanislav. 'The Power Himself…' All the clips that have played, play again, in reverse, but very, very, fast in pace with the music as the singer chants. Then a cracking noise can be heard, the clips stop, the music stops, all that can be heard is the whistling of the wind. 'But is he here?' Then in the centre of a screen the PWC TV graphic loads up. 'Tune in tonight, to find out.'
