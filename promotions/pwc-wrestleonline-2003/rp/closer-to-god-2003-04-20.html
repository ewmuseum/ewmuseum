Closer To God

20 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 20/04/03 I rubbed my thumbs against the rest of my fingers to check if they reacted to it's touch. The response was negative. The bitter cold New Jersey night had turned them into hard cold stone sculptures that weighed down at the side of a figure who all the way up here looked like a demonic statue who represented 'all that could have been' in peoples scheduled dull routine lives, The missed opportunities. The burden they carried from dwelling in denial every day. The faint glimmer of satirical hope in a world where evil seemed to be the constant puppet masters to the few people who still believed in Purity, Good, Just, and Righteousness and what they were defined as in Webster's Dictionaries. I was that statue. I was that representation. Not by will, not by choice, but by unwanted, but accepted expectations. The rain stopped it's gentle occasional picking of spine tingling drops and stopped for a dramatic pause before thundering down it's arsenal of moist cold soldiers onto the pillaged earth, and onto me. Up here, on the roof of the chapel, I smiled faintly as the rain soothed down onto my head. The sound of worn down tyres on age old motors skidding across the unforgiving roads as they tried to cooperate with the sudden turn in harsh weather rung out beneath me like relentless gunfire on a blood stained battlefield. I let my now wet hair sag down over my face. These were the sounds, the sights, and the images that I came here to see, to here, to be greeted by. I needed to be reminded. Of what? That iniquity still exists outside of the three letter promotion I'm expected to fight the good fight in, and that I do gladly. Yet for myself, and no one else. I need to be reminded that the never ending battle between good and evil still runs ferociously and carelessly outside of the squared circle. Why? So I can convince myself to live in a denial along with the rest of this species? Certainly not., It's more of case of not to fall into the same trap that the others have. The people that rely on me as a so called 'Paladin' can keep their beliefs, their possessions and their denial ridden lives. They can use me as their symbol for hope if they wish, they can use me as their excuse to carry on living in denial. I won't take that away from them. Why? Simple. Up here on this church roof, amongst the other sculptures used to fight off evil, the symbolical crosses and the atmosphere of strong beliefs. I feel closer to God. Yes, I believe in him, and me the big man don't exactly see eye to eye. Yet up here I realise. one thing. The people who constantly use me as their new found hope and in the process become just another burden on my shoulders. They all want what I will son have when I leave this plagued earth. A place in heaven. Why? I thought the answer to that was simple. I've already spent my time in hell.
