Dropping Bombs

2 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 2, 2003 For the past few weeks I had manipulated Kohana so much, that I had basically had him to a point of trusting me. I could tell he still wasn't convinced enough about me, but at this point in time, he didn't need to be one hundred percent sure about me, because I was one hundred percent sure it was time to fuck him over. I was sitting inside of some family owned café, and really I didn't even know why I was here. About seven other people were sitting in as well, at different tables of course, but they must have been regulars as the waitress called them by name, and they to her. The smell, well, it wasn't as bad as it could have been, but the place looked run down. 'Excuse me, but can I get my food?' I impatiently asked the waitress, who was wiping down a table across the café. She looked up at me and gave a kind of angered look towards me. I was already pissed that it was taking thirty minutes to get my food, I didn't need some nasty red headed café whore giving me a look like I was in the wrong. However, the waitress finished cleaning up the table, and walked towards me. 'Your food will be here when it's ready... K?' She said, ending it with the sarcastic tone I was expecting. I wasn't in the best of moods when I had came here, by the way, but I was in an even more pissed off mood now. I didn't have the time to fly back to California to plot out the rest of my plan inside the House of Evil, so I figured a quiet little place like this would be sufficient. I was dead wrong. My stomach was growling, and I needed something to eat before my brain would function correctly. 'Listen here. I've been waiting here for thirty minutes on a damn chicken fried steak with mashed potatoes. The only thing I've had to eat today were those three packets of sugar I just downed about five minutes ago to try and hold back the hunger until my food got here. And to tell the truth, I don't like you one bit.' I sternly said towards the waitress, who gave more than just an angered look this time, she gave a look of disgust. 'Oh, and not only is your service shitty, your seating is too. My legs stick to this damn vinyl shit you got covering these booths.' I continued on in my rant, but the waitress interrupted me. She held her hand up, and shook her head, like she wanted to say something. Her mouth was wide open, but no words were coming out. Finally, she got something. 'Listen you son of a bitch, I don't care if you don't like me, this establishment, or the food when you get it, I'm just here to get paid. So leave me alone!' She exclaimed, almost in tears it seemed. Maybe it was my anger, or maybe it was just because I was hungry, but I had no choice but to retaliate. I ripped my legs off of the vinyl covering for the booth, and stood up, looking the waitress eye to eye. 'Well I'm guessing you should stick to the street corners then, because with that attitude, you won't be getting many tips, and you'd be better off naming your own prices for the random people wanting to come fuck you, or whatever their sick minds desire.' I had inched my head forward as I yelled this, my face was practically in her's by the time I had finished. The rest of the people in the café had begun to stare by now, although I couldn't tell all that much, the poor lighting was making it hard to see at times with all the flickering lights. 'I... I...' The bitch had tried to say something, but she just ran off in tears across the checker board black and white tiled floor, her heals clicking as she ran. 'What the hell did you do that for?' A voice questioned from across the café. I smiled at the gesture that anyone would try to stand up for such a low life as that waitress, I looked for where the voice came from anyway. I called again. 'Why in tarnations did you do that?' I shook my head and sat back down to wait on my food. The sound of a chair scratching on the floor, backing away from a table, echoed through the café. 'Listen, you are wasting your time if you want to talk, or even if you just want to beat me up to stick up for your beloved Flo. So just sit your ass back down, and go about your business. I'm waiting on my food, I'll eat, then I'll leave.' I said to whomever was approaching. Footsteps grew louder as they approached my booth, I continued not to look at who was coming my way. I was too worried about eating, and worrying about how to punish Kohana this week on TV. I stared out the window, the footsteps stopping beside my booth. The person had set down. I decided to look. 'Holy shit!' I exclaimed, looking at the God forsaken looks of the man. His eyes grew wide, wondering what my problem was. 'Gots a problem sonny?' He asked as I forced myself to calm down, and to at least look at the man while he was talking. I was trying to say something, but my mind was in overload thinking what could have happened to make a man as ugly as that. 'I'll take that as a no I s'pose.' He said, answering his own question. 'Naw I jus figgered I'd come over here and discuss that lil ol lady with yas.' He said with a smile plastered all over his disfigured face. I once again forced myself to look the man in the eyes. 'Why bother?' I said, looking away as soon as I finished. The man looked like his face had been run over my a motorcycle a dozen time, and then threw the dead skunk on the side of the road on top of his head for hair. I wasn't hungry anymore, but I was still wishing the guy would leave. 'Well, Flo has been here awhile. And alls I know is that she's only been on them there corners twice, because this job was better for her. Now look what you've gone an dug up. I'm ashamed of you young'n. So I spect you better eat, pay, and leave before the other boys get angry with ya.' The man said, I wasn't paying too much attention, trying to keep myself from vomiting. I did, however, nod, and then the elephant man walked away. Whispering to myself, 'Damn what an ugly bitch.' I whispered with a smile as I turned to look out the window. A noise startled me, however, and I looked to find Flo setting my food down, a runny makeup faced whore she looked like now. I began to laugh, and she ran away crying again. I was feeling a bit better now, so I decided to take a few bites before paying, and leaving. 'What the fuck?' The words just came from nowhere as the taste of the food surprised me. I picked the plate and silverware up as I dropped a twenty onto the table, and I headed out the door into the dark streets. I went and sat down on a bench, sitting just outside the café I was just in, and began to eat the rest of my food, all the while plotting to myself about this Saturday. 'Should I do it early, or wait till later in the match?' I asked as I began to question myself on what to do. 'I got the match set up, now I just need to right plan. I also can't do anything too suspicious that would warn Kohana of eventual danger either.' All the thoughts running through my head at this time were indescribable. 'Why am I even worrying about it? Kohana is just a little bitch, he won't realize what is going on until it happens.' I said, reassuring myself in the situation. I set down the plate from the café beside me on the bench, and then I stood up. 'I guess there is always another time for this, if it is needed.' I said as I began to walk away. I turned the corner, and pulled my keys out of my car. It was getting late, and I needed to get some sleep anyway, guess I had better quit for the night. I then got into my car, and headed off.
