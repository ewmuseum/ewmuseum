Disposable Paladins - Part 8 - Scardy Cat

15 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 15/04/03 Part 8: 'What did she say he said his name was again?' asked Poe as he drew his black boots from off Bauerman's desk as he rested back and cradles his hands behind his neck while massaging it slightly. 'Minotaur?' Bauerman replied loading a file on his computer. 'Melvin has found a lot of stuff on what this Minotaur represents'. 'Him and his mythology theories, he reminds me of someone' 'Who's that then?' 'Beats the shit out of. What's he got?' Poe got up out of the chair and looked over Bauerman's shoulder at the file which he had brought up on his monitor. 'Minotaur, half-man, half bull. Exiled by King Minos and placed in a labyrinth for it to hide in where it fed upon seven youth and seven maidens annually sent from Athens.' Bauerman took a deep breath after reading the small article before scrolling down for the rest of it. 'That explains all the killings of the females, but this is where you come in Gid.' 'What do you mean?' Bauerman pointed at the monitor. 'The hero, Theseus, aided by King Mino's daughter Ariadne, slew the monster with it's own horn' Gideon rolled his eyes slowly before walking back over to the chair had he been resting in. The station at this time of night was thankfully empty and scarce of any life. 'So now I'm- I don't know- a fucking mythological version of Arnald Schwarzenager? I'm Theseus and he's Minotaur? This psycho wants to play role-play with peoples lives while casting me in a leading role? Fuck that!' 'Whether we choose to accept this or not, we're playing by this fuckshots rules. The ball is in his court. He wants to make things exciting, he wants the media in on this. He wants to be famous, let's try and stop him from getting what he wants. Gideon ran his hand over his jaw line, he felt slight grains of stubble running over his worn hands like sandpaper over wood. The case in a nutshell at the beginning seemed mystical to say the least, but now it had spawned into a plot that would give movie directors and authors like Spielberg and King wet dreams. Then somewhere along the dark plagued lines that wove through and made up the cloth and canvas that this facade was being played on, Gideon had somehow sewn himself into it. 'So what role do you play?' asked Gideon, who only asked the question to kill the tentative silence. 'None, I hope. Fact of the matter is he somehow knows your working on this case, and wants to use you as a stepping stone for publicity.' 'No shit, who else is in danger of being cast into this move by psycho director?' 'No other roles play any significant part, oh, except Ariadne, but let's face it, you don't know that many woman.' Bauerman chuckled, it was laugh that was harmless in tone, but simply added emphasis to the cold sharp horror that Gideon had just realised. '...Gideon'? Martin asked, slightly alarmed at the shade of pale Gideon's skin had turned. 'Tanya' he muttered. Tanya was Adriane. Tanya was the missing link. As if on cue, the phone besides Bauerman rang. Gideon thrust his hand out without bearing thought and yanked the receiver up. The voice on the other end greeted him with sadistic approval. 'Greetings Gideon' Gideon clenched his eyes in horror and frustration as Bauerman looked on with shock and confusion. 'You have her?' he inquired through gritted teeth. 'Of course' 'What do you want?' 'Just to know old friend. Just to know.' 'Know what?' 'If you want to play again?' Gideon doused some of the sweat away off his forehead while trying to work out these vile clues. 'Play what? What do you mean?' '.....Scardy Cat....' Gideon's eyes opened as the hysterical laughter on the other end rang out through his ear like cold sharp daggers. Bauerman's face had long since grown impatient and was now ready to explode and grow hostile to gain answers. The phone hung up. The laughter ceased. 'What the fuck was that all about? What did he say?!' Gideon looked deep into Bauerman's eyes, his own were filled with trauma. Bauerman knew he would likely never see that look in his eyes again. 'He's back.' 'Who?!' 'Sit down Martin. It's about time I got this off my chest.'
