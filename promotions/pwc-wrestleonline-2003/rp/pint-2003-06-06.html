Date: 6/6/03 Two eyes opened, two pupils dilated, two pairs of teeth spread and let out a silent scream as the light increased his already sensitive headache. With a grimace, he raised his aching arms and shielded his lids before rolling over and attempting to rest, the ringing in his ears refusing to leave him. The beeping of a heart monitor, the scent of the sterility, and the light chatter of conversing doctors would prevent that. Groaning, he gave up hope and sat up, taking the time to adjust the dressing on his forehead. Rubbing his eyes, he blinked twice and stared across the room, meeting the gaze of a yawning, graying young man. “Argh,” he said between his teeth, “the fark are you doin’ here?” Limerick gave him the peace sign and a huge, cheesy smile. With a deep sigh, McGlocklan rolled the sheets off his torso and swung his legs off the side of the iron-like bed. His two bare feet met the ice-cold floor, sending a shiver up his spine. Attempting to stand would prove useless… as would asking the kid for help. So there he sat, on the bed, his two bare feet on the ice-cold floor. “How long ya been sittin’ there?” Limerick lifted his hand and extended 3 fingers. “Three hours? Christ… How long I been layin’ here? Four…? Mother of fu…” He trailed off a second before questioning his partner. “Where are my clothes?” Hospital gowns… how defaming and inappropriate, he thought to himself. Limerick motioned at the seat next to him. Sure enough, his clothes were there, folded neatly. Black t-shirt, jeans, rosaries… the proverbial ‘goods’. McGlocklan looked on as the kid folded his arms and crossed his leg in a slightly defiant matter. “Well? Let’s have ‘em.” Limerick made a clicking between his teeth, the dreaded ‘tsk, tsk’ sound. Rolling his eyes, McGlocklan grabbed hold of the table at his side for balance and motioned for them to be thrown. “If you think I’m stayin’ here over night, yer off yer nut, kiddo.” Sighing, Roan reached over and flung them over, not before flipping his friend the bird. Nodding with another eye roll, Mick caught them and dropped them on the bed. Tearing the IV from the outside of his hand, Mick struggled with the bracelet before sliding the rosaries over his shoulders. “Awright, I know yer envious and all, but turn yer head for a minute.” Pulling the knot at the back of the robe, Mick looked on as Limerick spat at the ground and left the room. Sliding on his clothing, Caleb staggered across the room, his hand sliding across the wall for minimal support. Opening the door, he grabbed hold of his partner’s shoulder and walked down the hall, leaning on him for balance. “Ergh… I need a fuckin’ pint.”
