The Customer is Always Right (Part 2)

17 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/17/03 The Customer is Always Right Part 2 The scene opens up where we left it off, our heroic tag team of pizza delivery boys standing face to face with Sammy’s dad. Mr. Bello is standing firmly in the door with his hands on his hips as he stairs sternly at Sammy. Sam has always been intimidated by his father. “In!” shouts Mr. Bello as he points at Sammy to entire the house. In Sammy does walk followed by Mr. Bello and Chuck at the same time. Unfortunately this scenario doesn’t work well and Chuck’s large body prevents Mr. Bello from getting in the door. “Out!” Mr. Bello shouts in response causing Chuck to jump and almost drop the pizza. “But, but…” stammers Chuck as he motions the pizza toward Mr. Bello who scowls angrily at him. He never has been a big fan of Chuck. Chuck slowly begins to walk out of the house when Sammy stops him. “No, he’s staying dad we are working now.” “Working?” Mr. Bello’s voice seems rather tense, “You call delivering pizzas for minimum wage working? I made more money shoveling snow in 1970 then you do working at that shit-hole place. What the hell is the name of it?” He looks at the box that Chuck is holding. “Cousin Mario’s” Chuck pipes in adding more heat to the already red face of Mr. Bello. Sammy just stands there with his head hung low. “Did I say you could talk FATS?” The insult hits Chuck hard as he sucks in his gut and joins his best friend with his head hung low. “No ambition, no drive, no motivation, is that what you want to be for the rest of your life? MY SON THE PIZZA BOY!” Mr. Bello’s hands are up in the air as his graying hair flaps madly. “My son would rather work for that grease ball goomba then go to college and become something great like me; does that sum it up right Samuel? “Hey we are Italians too, dad, so leave Cousin Mario alone!” Sammy says with a new found vigor. “How am I supposed to feel Samuel? I had such big plans for you we could have been lawyers together Bello and Bello lawyer firm. Father and son…we could have been rich. But instead, you want to make and deliver pizza’s wasting away in Elephant man’s basement.” Sammy frowns upon the comment made by his father at Chuck as both Sam and Mr. Bello watch Chuck for his emotion. Chuck is obvious to the comment though and notices everyone staring at him. Confused and feeling threatened Chuck blurts the first thing that comes to mind. “We are pro wrestlers now.” Sammy slaps his hand to his head as Mr. Bello looks at Chuck and then Sammy, and then back at Chuck. “Speak up boy…elaborate more…” Mr. Bello asks Chuck interested. “Huh?” Chuck asks, the big words boggling his mind. “You don’t need to say anything Chuck.” Sammy says trying to defend his friend and change the subject. “I am done talking to you for the moment, I am talking to your waist of so much space friend…well speak up boy.” Chuck smiles, “We got a job working for Ron Starr and Primetime Wrestling Circuit. He is gonna give us money to put those wrestling skills that Sammy and I trained for last summer to good use. He even said that we might even get some girls to maybe even talk to us.” Chuck blushes thinking about the blonde double D girl that will someday put her arms around him. His smile slowly rises along with his bulge in his pants. Mr. Bello notices first with a horrified expression and Sammy in return snaps his fingers in front of Chuck snapping him out of the day dream. “DUDE!” Sammy cries motioning to Chuck’s proportionately sized boner. Chuck does his best to turn around and hide it…after a moment of readjusting he turns back around, his cheeks bright red. “So let me get this straight, some guy actually hired you to…wrestle for him…on T.V.?” Mr. Bello starts laughing hysterically. “Those wrestling lessons you boys took over the summer actually got you a job? Have you even paid for them yet?” he laughs even harder basically doubling over in pain from the laughs. “Well son, I wash my hands with you then.” He takes a deep breathe to prevent another burst of laughter. “Do what you want; you and the human tank can get married for all I care anymore. We’ll if you don’t mind I have a job to get to now, more important children to watch out for and yes, and a pizza to eat.” He takes the pizza from Chuck and hands them a twenty dollar bill. “No worries,” he says motioning to the money, “you can pay me back at Thanksgiving.” Mr. Bello opens the door for the two boys…and forcefully pushes them out back into the snow. The door slams behind them with a thud and they walk back to Polly in silence. After getting into the car and starting her up Sammy turns to Chuck. “I hate my dad.” “He is a good guy, Sammy.” Chuck seems to forget the harsh comments Mr. Bello made about him. “Chuck, he ripped you apart in there.” Chuck thinks to himself for a moment, “Hey Sammy?” “Yeah, man?” “You have siblings?” “No, dad just made that up, and it’s a Sunday. He doesn’t even work on Sunday. Great parenting right?” “Thanksgiving?” Chuck asks curiously bouncing from one topic to another. “Isn’t that kinda far away?” “Yeah, I think that was his point. So honestly Chuck, is the Customer Always Right?” Both boys absorb that comment as Chuck backs out of the driveway and begins to drive back towards Cousin Mario’s Pizza. They would probably get back and Mario would send them home because of the snow. Back to Chuck’s place…back to home…for both of them. “Hey Sam?” Chuck pipes in one last time. “Yeah, man?” Sammy answers with his head resting on the torn plush seating of Polly. “I bet your sister is hot.” Chuck says with a smile as Sammy only shakes his head. They both look at each other and laugh. BLACKOUT
