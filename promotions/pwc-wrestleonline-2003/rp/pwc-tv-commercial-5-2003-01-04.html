PWC TV Commercial 5

4 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Originally aired on WGTW TV-48 in Philadelphia, PA at 12:16 am EST Saturday, January 4th. The scene, nothing more than black hole greets us, and then slowly begins to shake on it's axis. Slowly the darkness obscuring our vision slowly lifts greeting us with another dark, yet gloomier scene. The annoying repetitive sound of dripping water blends in with the surroundings. Then slowly the soft faint whistling of "Burn" by The Cure fades softly in from the background. The camera moves slowly in, the scene around us nothing more than a haunting chamber of solitude and isolation. The drum and guitars then softly fall in as the lyrics combine with the melodic riffs of the song. "Don't look don't look" the shadows breathe. In the centre of the cold dark dungeon rests a young man slouched on the damp wet floor. His long black hair smothering his face prevents us from seeing his true identity. "Whispering me away from you" As the camera cautiously draw in closer, we see the man wearing nothing more than a pair of black leather pants. His feet bare and damp and as his hands rub up and down slowly against his arms in an attempt to keep warm. There is no doubt this man is a fine athletic specimen, his body toned and chiselled out of henge. Yet it is ablaze with scars from battles of a time now past. "The end is all that's ever true. There's nothing you can ever say Nothing you can ever do... " He runs his hand up and down his arms slowly, smoothing out the scars that remind him of his brutal past. "Every night I burn Waiting for my only friend Every night I burn Waiting for the world to end" Slowly "Burn" by The Cure begins to fade out as the camera circles the man sitting hunched on the cold hard floor of the this spiritual entrapment of a prison. Once the song has completely faded out, a voice this time fades in, uncannily resembling that of Vincent Price. "When a man, who once had everything to live for..." The camera continually circles the man slowly. "Has everything snatched away from him in one vile swift motion. When his hopes, dreams, goals, sanity, even life are smashed up before him. What could there possibly be left for him to do?" Slowly the mans arms start to move, in an almost mechanic fashion. "When suicide from this cruel is earth is deemed as a sign of ultimate weakness, yet death is the only thing that makes him feel free and taste salvation. Does he embrace death? Does he seek it? Does he crave for it? Or does he set out to leave a mark on this cruel earth that has robbed him of everything? Or yet...... does he somehow walk through the long dark tunnel that is his life, seeking some light at the end of that tunnel?" Slowly, as the man begins to rise, we notice dozens upon dozens of small strings attached to him, his arms, legs, head, neck, waist, all connected to the numerous strings being used to pull him up....to make him move... to control him. He's nothing more than a puppet. "Does he try and break free of the chains that bind him? Slowly and defiantly the man clenches and flexes mightily, the veins shrouded by his scabbed over scars begin to pulsate and throb as he begins to breath deeply at expending all this strength. The camera then slowly retreats backwards as the man shoots his head buprights and into the air letting out an almighty roar at the top of his exasperated lungs before eventually stopping. He then slowly falls back to his knees, his long black hair once again smothering his face. "If only it was that simple. So when the chains that bind us and infect us with vile putrid evil seem unbreakable, what is there left to do?" The scene of the dark man kneeling down tired and beaten the begins to fade away and dissolve into the ether, before a new scene returns to us. A bare naked foot comes crashing down in front of us, black leather pants surrounding the legs. It is without a doubt the man we just seen try and break free of whatever unholy spiritual chains binding him. "He fights..... fights and salvages whatever piece of a soul he has left and tries to obtain some form of purity and sanity, by doing what he deems good.... be doing what he can." The almighty drum beat of "Dead Souls" by Nine Inch Nails then begins to blast through in the background, as the a pair of hands reach down near the naked feet that we see and begin to put a pair of traditional black boots on them. Someone take these dreams away That point me to another day The pale cold hands with various Stirling silver rings on them then lace up the black boots, as the camera begins to rise upwards. "A duel of personalities That stretch all true reality" Slowly, we focus once again on the mans chiselled and battered body, as a thin black fishnet top slips gracefully over it, faintly shrouding the scars that infest his body. "When figures from the past stand tall And mocking voices ring the hall Imperialistic house of prayer Conquistador's who took their share " The mans arms then slip through the sleeves of a long black leather trench coat that reaches all the way down to his knees. He then adjusts the long collar around his neck, which is assorted with numerous tribal tattoos. "They keep calling me" Slowly the man takes a few steps forward, the view of his face always kept smothered by his long black hair. "Keep on calling me" Then out of the caliginous shadows , a bold and mighty eagle soars out and towards the dark figure and perches itself proudly on his shoulder. "CALLING ME!" Slowly "Dead Souls" by Nine Inch Nails fades out, as the Vincent Price voice over fades in to replace it. "When The Shade takes flight, society and humankind will have no choice, but to face the brutal realisation of this................... Victims...." Then for the first time, the dark figure speaks slowly in a deep mature voice, finishing off the sentence. " ...Aren't We All?!" A bolt of lightening then strikes down violently, as if on a dramatic yet sadistic cue, as the scene fades to static.
