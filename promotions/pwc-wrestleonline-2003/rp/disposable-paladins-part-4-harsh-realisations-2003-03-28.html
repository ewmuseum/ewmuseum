Disposable Paladins - Part 4 - Harsh Realisations

28 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 28/03/03 Part 4 The pictures in this case file rivalled that of the Marquis De Sades photo album. Simply judging by these graphic pictures Gideon knew that this killer enjoyed the maiming of others, and that was something that he doubted he would find written in the paperwork of the case file. He wanted to believe that he was strong enough emotionally to walk away from this, to say 'Fuck you' to Bauerman and toss the case file right at him and disappear into the sunset. Yet he knew he couldn't, and he stopped kidding himself that he could do that 30 seconds after he began pondering over it. He didn't know what the drive was this time though. Maybe it was the fact all the victims were extremely young and female. Maybe it was the fact that Tanya was a lot like these females, young, energetic, did the same things as them, went to the same places as them. Maybe it was the fear that one day he'd wake up and read her name in the obituary. Maybe it was the dread that Bauerman would knock on his door to break the bad news to him. Maybe it was just the fear that he didn't want any extra added pain on his already weak conscience. 'It's 3:15' came a gentle voice through the tense darkness in the room that Gideon was sitting in. The derelict home that they were basically squatting in wasn't exactly homely. Yet the sound of her voice would warm any room, and he never stopped being glad to hear it instead of Jeremiah's. he was glad to be able to hear it at all after how she suffered over the past week. 'I know' he replied and quickly shuffled the pictures up and slid them into the case file. He didn't want to take the risk of having two people in the same bed having nightmares over this killer. 'Well you've been staring at that case file for the last 3 hours and don't even try hiding the pictures from me' He turned around slowly to finally look at Tanya standing there wrapped in a long nightgown. 'I looked at them when you went out earlier, try hiding them somewhere a bit better than your athletic bag' she winked mischievously as she leaned against the frame of the bedroom door. Gideon simply turned his head and ran his hands through his long black hair. 'Come on, come to bed, staring at the pictures for hours on end won't do you any good' She was right, but he was too tired to admit it to her right now. 'Get some rest, I'll be there soon' He replied back to her as he drank some of the red wine he had barely touched all night. 'Yeah, you said that hours ago' He hadn't even officially agreed to help Bauerman out on this case and already it was causing friction in the only relationship he had been a part of in years. Being poisoned by an old guy isn't really a turn on either. She slowly walked over and placed both her hands on his shoulders and began massaging them slightly, Gideon didn't stop her either. 'PWC causing you stress is an understatement, me, you, the fans, we all know that. Now this case is hardly shaping up to be the best stress reliever for either of us. But if you're willing to carry the extra baggage, then so am I' He sat back gently and breathed deeply, he wasn't used to this much understanding from someone. 'Just choose carefully' she whispered before walking off back to the bedroom. 'I already have' he whispered.
