Girl Trouble (Part 3)

25 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/25/03 Special Delivery 4/25/03 Girl Trouble Part 3 (Talk Is Cheap) Choppy vision and slurred speech fill Chuck D’Andrea’s mind as he suddenly is awakened from his ever common naps. Chuck smiles awkwardly and looks around, there are people everywhere, staring at him. Chuck begins to sweat, as a wave of panic rushes over him. “Oh god, I have dreamt about this moment before. Once I look down I am going to be naked and everyone is going to be laughing at me.” Chuck’s horrible thoughts end though once he quickly takes a nervous glance down at his crotch; pants and shirt are on, and in tact. “Hey, it’s not a dream. I even remembered to dress myself today.” But where am I?” Chuck slowly looks around the room. The people whom he assumed where staring at him are paying attention to a man standing in the front of the room. The man continues to wave his arms and speak loudly. “Wow, I even came to class today. I am batting a thousand.” Chuck looks down at his desk and rummages through papers and notes he has taken for the class. The book is empty, except for the occasional I HEART MARY KATE scribbles here and there. Chuck looks to his left and sees his best buddy Sammy Bello sitting next to him. Sammy has all his notes sprawled out over two desks as he frantically jots down each and every note the professor says. “Jeez, since when is he in a hurry to learn so much?” Sammy looks over at the nonchalant Chuck and widens his eyes. Sammy mouths something at Chuck but, the big man is unable to understand his friend’s words. “What, is he trying to tell me? Between this old mans monotone lectures and him not talking like a normal person I can’t hear pick up a damn thing he is saying.” Sammy, frustrated turns and scribbles something down on a piece of paper. He then holds it up for Chuck to see… “Finals in 2 Weeks! MUST PASS! Oh, so that’s why he is…FINALS? IN 2 WEEKS OHH MAN! Why didn’t I know this? Why was I already to start my afternoon nap at 9 in the morning?” Chuck frantically begins to open up his notebook and attempt to write any sort of notes down, but like a small child of nine with Attention Deficits Disorder he finds himself totally smitten with his new object of affection. “Wow, hold the phone girlfriend. Who is that fine piece of work?” Chuck begins to stare at a girl sitting 3 desks down from him. Her laptop is open and she is typing away notes and study tips listening intently to the lecture being given. “I think I am in love…I wonder if she would go on a date with me” Chuck stares at the girl who is oblivious to the infatuated wrestler. Sammy looks over at Chuck and begins to wave his arms wildly. But Chuck is unable to snap out of his trance. “She’s so pretty…I wish I could only hold her…only for a moment.” And on that line, the camera switches over to Sammy whose thoughts now narrate us. “What the hell is that fat fool doing? Did I not just tell him that we have finals in 2 weeks? And no I don’t mean the final four in basketball. All he thinks about are girls…girls, girls, girls. Geez that boy has a one track mind!” Sammy continues back with his notes for class…but once again is distracted by his partners staring problem for the girl. “I think he is just jealous that I have a date tomorrow night and he doesn’t. Ok, it’s not a date…but still it’s one girl that I have that he doesn’t. Chuck needs to realize it’s ok if he doesn’t have a girlfriend right now and I do. I won’t be mad at him.” Sammy scribbles down another note and then sits back in his desk again. “Well, ok maybe work doesn’t really count as a big date with a girl. And who cares if Katherine has a boyfriend in high school. What do I have that he doesn’t have? I am older, smarter, stronger, and on TV! What girl doesn’t want a guy that’s on TV? Even if it is late night Saturday night TV…” As Sammy’s thinking ends the professor dismisses class and in a mad rush students rise to their feet and begin to exit. Chuck is determined to meet this new girl that he has become an on looking fan of…but he finds himself stuck in his desk. “Look at Chuck now. He is so overly obsessed to meet this girl he has gone and gotten himself caught in the desk again. And look at that, she’s gotten away from him. SAMMY BELLO 1 CHUCK D’ANDREA 0!” Chuck doesn’t even bother to try to wiggle out of the desk. He silently waits for Sammy to walk over to him and help him and his new desk attachment to his feet. He looks wounded over to the girl who still, hasn’t even given him a short glance to make Chuck’s morning. Sammy pats his long time friend on the shoulder and together they make their way towards the maintenance room. Where hopefully Chuck will be able to remove himself from his new college 12 by 7 desk appendage. Without a word being said from either member of Special Delivery, the scene fades to black.
