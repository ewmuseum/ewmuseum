All Rhodes Lead to Rome

2 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2nd March 2003 Location: New York Docks Date: Friday 28th 2003 Time: Around 11am. The cargo ships merrily unload their cargo as two figures stand there watching them. One is a thin, short figure whilst the other is an almost giant of a man. Both are wearing long black jackets and trying their best to be unnoticed as the hustle of the docks moves around them almost like an army of worker ants. The camera pans around the front and reveals it to be two men that you will now surely know… Romulus and his new compatriot, Colossus in the Roman Legion… or the New Roman Legion, whatever you prefer to call it. “So what have you been up to, old friend?” says the shorter man to the massive hulk to his right. Colossus smiles down, no rage in his face… almost a shocking thing to see for a wrestler of his size and stature. “Doing this and that… still been Colossus, but in unusual circumstances.” He replies carefully, his English speaking slow, yet well spoken all the same. The smaller man nods carefully, deep in thought as more dock workers pass by them carrying crates containing something of substance. “That’s always good to hear… but you managed to circumvent my question completely… what have you been up to? Something worthy of Rome, I pray on Jupiter’s name I take?” says the small runt of a man. The larger man smiles and walks away towards an open crate of bright red apples. He reaches over and takes one before rubbing it onto his jacket and taking a bite. “Colossus… are you actually going to answer me?” says the PWC wrestler trying to get an actual response from his former stable mate. The giant turns around, still eating his apple and nods towards the smaller man. He quickly finishes off the apple and throws it over the side of the dock and down into the water. “Of course I will Romulus, but first you have to understand some rules… I’ve been getting by with what I’m already doing, and frankly how you used to treat me and Brutus back in Primetime wasn’t fair. Hell, you were meant to be pushing us as a tag team, and who was the only one who achieved success was you.” Says the giant, obviously having thought up this whole argument on the way to this meeting. Romulus looks worried for a few moments, not really knowing what to say in response. “I don’t know how to tell you this, but Brutus is… well… he died of a heart attack over a year ago. I always wondered why you never made the funeral.” Colossus looks shocked, but it stalls him for only a few moments. “I wasn’t in the country a year ago… hell; I wasn’t even in the country three days ago. Venezuela… that’s where I’ve been. Working on Venezuelan Gladiators… as the main Gladiator… named Colossus… of course.” He says, finally admitting where he’s been all this time. Romulus looks at him… and suddenly lunges forward and hugs his huge bulk… well, ok so his arms don’t actually go that far around Colossus’ massive bulk, but he tried to hug him. The giant pushes him away with a frown. “Hey, I don’t do that sort of stuff… leave that to those gay Centurions.” Says Colossus, to which Romulus openly laughs, his first sign of real happiness since meeting once again with Colossus. “I knew it… I KNEW IT!” says Romulus out loud and loud enough for several people to hear, or at least they would if the noise of the docks wasn’t already at a defining level. “Knew what?” replies the man named after one of the seven wonders of the ancient world. “Why of course, you were a Gladiator… you never stopped being Roman… this is brilliant, I thought I would have to bring you back around to your heritage, but all this time you never actually forgot it! Thank you Colossus for still being you… you could not have made me happier. Well if you gave me world domination on a plate, strictly speaking that would make me happier but still, this will do just fine.” Says the Roman stick man with the David Bowie eyes. “This brings us back to what I was saying before you mentioned Brutus… it cannot be like it was before… I want a say in what we do these days. You might have aspirations of being Emperor, but I have aspirations of a Senate.” Romulus seems to ponder Colossus’ words for a few moments and then sticks out his hand in agreement. “Very well Colossus, outside of the ring we are a Senate, we discuss all actions… but in ring you are still a Gladiator, doing exactly what the Emperor says to do… just like in the days of Augustus… oh and if we ever get to name a month, then its Romember… ok?” Colossus smirks slightly and shakes Romulus’ hand in open agreement. “Think about it… now all we have to do is take over the world, right?” says Colossus to the man who would rename November. “That’s the plan” says that man right back at him.
