An Anonymous Letter

12 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/12/03 Our scene opens to show the interior of a less than extravagantly decorated office with nicely chosen paintings along the walls and a fairly comfortable feeling to it. Dusk is visibly approaching as seen through a shot of the New Jersey sunset as it peaks through an open window. We focus on the sunset until the window is closed by dry, white hands. We gaze through the rest of the office when the indistinct figure of a remarkably overweight man with high forehead, melancholy dark eyes, a large generous mouth, and impressive large white teeth, intersects our line of vision and is spotted fumbling and rifling through the papers on the wooden desk of the office suspiciously. A large sheet of white paper with fancy black embroidering is easily picked up by the camera lens that is slung over the obesity of a man’s shoulder. The lens adjusts to its fine print and we are able to make out the contents of this specific letter. It reads: 'Dear Mr. Starr, It has been brought to my attention, as a result of its improved significance to an individual of my worth, that in light of the recent legal mishap that has taken place as a result of the Pro Wrestling Circuit’s lack of expertise and authority with regards to the legal profession, your fine establishment is, in fact seeking legal council to act on your behalf. In spite of the of the humiliation that your company is inevitably suffering on account of having hired an alleged leprechaun, if that is the politically correct term for those things, I personally, am willing to put my firm on the line as representing your federation, the Pro Wrestling Circuit and take on the formal role of lawyer for the institution that you’re building. And I don't negotiate with just any client Mr. Starr, my previous clients are the likes of those with inconceivable prosperity in this day and age. I expect no less from the Pro Wrestling Circuit. But that’s enough about me, Mr. Starr we ought not stray for the real issue here. Your appointing me as the attorney to the Pro Wrestling Circuit would not only be among every single one of your lucrative and well-liked business decisions, plentiful as they are, but I’m also sure that once you glance over my impressive résumé it will become obvious that a fervent worker such as myself, will only provide more of the wealth we both are so deserving of. Now, realize Mr. Starr that I get paid for seeing that my clients have every break the law allows. I have knowingly defended a number of guilty men. But even the guilty never escape unscathed. My fees are sufficient punishment for anyone, and I do mean anyone, Mr. Starr. Nevertheless, I’m sure that in cooperation you and I can put our heads together and settle on a sufficient amount of compensation for my service to you. Provided that you’re willing to go through some toil for a viable cause. My secretaries await immediate response from you or your counsel. Sincerely, ...Well, now why don’t we save a proper introduction for a time convenient for the both of us. Until then, Mr. Starr I look forward to conducting business with you. with you.' Our shot of the conclusion of the letter flinches when the squeak of the office door rattles the fat man in possession of the camera. We vaguely make out an inaudible gasp while the camera cuts to the fuming red face of the CEO of the Pro Wrestling Circuit, Ron Starr with his arms crossed and the eyes surrounded by his glasses becoming bloodshot. Our “fearless” cameraman looses all of his valor in the instant that he finds Ron Starr’s face is glaring back at him and his video camera plummets to the ground. One can only imagine that Chubby only got so far before security was over him like the backside of his hand, but you can’t blame a guy for trying, right? Even if the guy is ridiculously large...Who knows? Perhaps, he’s bringing the news of something or someone equally as great?
