For What's Been Done

29 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Sunday What a bunch of bullshit. Shaking his head slowly and snickering under his breath, Deville turned his attention from the small glowing screen and finished fastening his left boot, corded arm muscles rippling as he pulled as tight as he could. The night hadn’t been exactly what he expected for the first show out of Capitol Punishment, but then, the entire last week of his life had become a soap opera from hell. There’re other kinds? Laughing softly, he began tugging on the other boot. It was considerably dismaying that Starr didn’t recognise the blatant idiocy of the payperview main event, and allowed the impromptu generic-fest to go on, but even that paled in comparison to how he wanted to ignore the fact that Deville was unjustly eliminated from the match. Instead of making that crybaby Jim Axtell defend his newfound title against the man who deserves it, Ron Starr was running around like an eighth grade bully, minus the wit and charm. What the hell are you doing in the ring, “boss”? Have a little fucking class. His heel stamped into the bottom of the boot, and lithe fingers flicked deftly through the laces, tying them into a series of intricate knots running up the bottom of his leg. Before yanking them tight and finishing up, he cast another admonishing glance at the monitor and screwed up his face as if his tongue had suddenly become a lemon. What the hell? Reaching up to the screen, he twisted one of the knobs sharply to the right, and pandemonium swelled from the tiny speaker. “BLACK HAZE~!” Mark was screaming like a little girl, as if anyone actually cared. Straightening up, Deville’s eyes bulged as the now-blind J-Con ducked a clothesline from Rhett, clearing a path straight to Starr. An ear-shattering cheer split the arena as Starr took it straight in the face and crumpled to the ring like a bitch. “THE BOSS IS OUT!” Mark’s shrill marking continued, for the benefit of all the blind viewers watching at home. “THE BOSS IS OUT, BANG!!” With a grin wide enough to show all of his teeth, Deville pulled his left arm across his body with his right, stretching. “Serves you right, ya fuckin’ cocksucker.” He had been watching the show from his unremarkable locker room since dealing with Magnum Wolf earlier on in the night, and the mediocrity of the product without him had been grating on his last nerve. Where the hell does that moron get off not booking me for matches? Finishing his stretch, he pulled up his wrestling trunks and let them snap back against his waist. He turned and regarded himself in the full length mirror leaning against the wall, peering through the sheet of mildew coating it to his reflection. Topless, his trunks and boots the only clothing covering his Apollonian body, he nodded appreciatively at himself. Those blue eyes blazed with the ferocity of a waterfall; he was ready for battle. Stupid doesn’t even begin to describe not giving me what I want... But if he thinks keeping me off the card will keep me out of the ring, stupid will never be a harsh enough word. Boos poured from the tiny monitor as the bell rung out, signifying the end of another Starr-studded-screwjob. Deville made a point of ignoring the commentators’ scripted and line-towing orgasming, then almost tuned himself into the commercial, since it would have to be better than anything else he had watched that night. Penance thinks he can continually interfere in my business and walk away from it without a scratch? Cost me the Heavyweight Championship of the World with his cowardly and opportunistic “dominance”...? Without retribution? Rotating back to the television screen, Deville’s body contained the imminent explosiveness of a coiled spring. His jaw set and his fists clenched, every inch of him preparing for war. Well, we’ll just see how much the goofy sonofabitch likes having my attention trained on him... We’ll see how well he copes with interference in his fuckin’ matches. Leaving the television on, Deville began making his way to the door, a soldier on the march. Without breaking stride, he throttled the doorknob and whipped the door itself back against the wall with a shuddering boom, then stalked into the hallway. “So I’m a nobody, huh?” Magnum Wolf’s quiet voice penetrated the empty room without challenge, bouncing off the walls uselessly as the door swung silently towards shutting. Deville’s face was suddenly back at the door, a perplexed and overly curious expression marring it. Pushing the door back, he waded into the room as if into a trap, eyes darting wildly about him. “A nothing?” The television asked again, in Wolf’s eerily serious voice. Grinning at his own paranoia, Deville takes up a smug position in front of the monitor, arms laced over his wide chest. Yeah, that’s right, Mags. You’re just an overrated piece of talentless shit, like Penance. “All I ever do is talk, is that it, Angie?” Answering his own question, Wolf stood up and began muttering under his breath. His hand darted up and hit the eject button of a small VCR Deville had failed to notice before. You certainly don’t wrestle, or do anything that would make anyone but a spoiled little bitch nervous. Don’t throw a tantrum about it, just take it like a man for once and fuck off. Rocking back and forth on his heels, Deville’s smugness could only grow. “You think I should do something to you, do you?” Tucking the tape into his coat, Mags cast a searching eye about the darkened room he was in, scanning for god only knows what. I only wish you could, you pathetic waste. “Something that’ll grab your attention?” A haunting laugh erupted from the apparently demented man’s lips, but Deville continued to no-sell. Yeah, beyond throwing yourself at my feet to be trampled. What the hell is wrong with you, Wolf? Trust me, you don’t want anything to do with me. If you had even the barest glimpse into my life, could conceive even the faintest glimmer of what I could do to you, you’d– Angelo’s mind went dead, followed immediately by the television. Frozen in place, Deville’s blank eyes lay waste to the television for several long moments, his mind trying to convince itself it didn’t just hear what it did. The denial is useless, of course, and eventually his mind kicked back into gear enough for conscious thought. “Maybe I’ll just deliver another copy of this tape to the police.” That’s what Magnum said. He’s sure of it. But... But... He may as well have started flying as tried to think, his mind turning to useless jelly once more. There’s no way that Magnum Wolf was saying what it appeared he was saying. It was just... Impossible. But as he slowly digested the statement, pieces of the puzzle began to fit together. Magnum Wolf taping him with the fish, and always running around with that camcorder. Magnum Wolf’s jealousy over Angelo’s success in FSW, and now PWC. Magnum Wolf always being around when Deville was stalking the back halls. Magnum Wolf’s constant comments about video cameras. The reward for a conviction, and Magnum Wolf’s greed. Magnum Wolf's suicidal admission. But he couldn’t be that stupid. No one could be that stupid. Not Starr, not Penance, not Wolf... Not anyone. But someone had been, and he knew it. Reaching up to his bruised face, he still bore the painful evidence of it, physically as well as mentally. Blinking couldn’t wash the disbelief from his eyes, but slowly it faded to acceptance. Then murderous rage. Magnum Wolf?! Magnum fucking Wolf?! He would kill the fool. Even as his mind swirled incoherently, he knew that as well as his own name. Magnum Wolf would die screaming at his hands, and Deville would have a monitor pressed to his face as it happened, so he could watch his own torturous death as well as feel it. Right now! Trembling with outrage, Deville turned to the door, still slightly open. He’s still in the fucking building...! Right! Fucking! Now! He couldn’t bring himself to storm out of the room, though. Rivetted in place as if a part of the floor, he could only stare hatred at nothing. No one betrays me... No one fingers Angelo Deville! Is he a fucking lunatic? What the hell is– The thought was cut off by another, one of infinitely more importance. The one that had held him fast. Still trembling, his face softened to the point of unspeakable anguish, then his knees buckled and he crumpled to the floor. Kathryn and Erica. Their names tore his soul apart, followed closely by images of their normally beautiful and smiling faces, charred black now and regarding him accusingly. Regarding him with as much betrayal as he felt from them before finding out what now appeared as inarguable truth. Kathryn and Erica, who he might have even loved, innocent and loyal, dead now because of him. No! the voice was cold steel, raging against the forge. Because of Magnum Wolf. As if discovering he could move it for the first time, his head arced slowly up from the floor, shadows dancing across his already blackened face. In slow motion he rose to his feet, straightening fluidly and solidly, even the memories of the trembling seemingly gone. Kathryn and Erica were still at the forefront of his mind, but their faces were darkened masks of sympathy now, urging him to justice. Supporting him toward vengeance. They would forgive him where he would not forgive himself. In a mocking parody of his body, his mind churned forward roboticly. He will pay for what he has done. For all that he has cost me. The Title. His freedom. The only people he felt he could trust, after robbing him of even that security and turning him against them. He will pay for all eternity. ...But he will not die. Death is much better than he deserves, and more mercy than he will receive. Striding from the room, a predator on the prowl, Deville uttered not a single sound. There would be plenty of time for noise later. Plenty of time to scourge the world with his agony some other time. If he had his way, Magnum Wolf would live forever... Screaming vainly for what he had done. He didn’t care if he ever fought another match, or won another title, just so long as he laid hands on Magnum Wolf... Just so long as he could make him pay for what he had done. For what they both had done.
