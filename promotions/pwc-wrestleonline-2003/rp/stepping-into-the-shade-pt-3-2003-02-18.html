Stepping into The Shade Pt:3

18 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 18/02/03 'Nothing quite like getting your head stitched eh?' 'Nothing like getting a gash the size of a canyon in your head from Jake Keeton getting the urge to see if the colour of steel blue went well with my head' 'If that's the case' muttered Trent while stitching Gideon's wound up slowly. 'The guy can't colour code for shit' Poe chuckled slightly while downing some of the bottle of Jack Daniels in his right hand as Trent began to finish stitching Poe back together. In the background, the sound of some cartoon on the television blared through the air in between the hysterical fits of laughter of Vex and Maynard. 'So, that old shit still his usual self?' asked Trent while trying to talk about anything than what he was about to do. 'SHIT TRENT!' Gideon yelled as Trent yanked the last piece of stitching tight with brutal force before taking the bottle of JD from Gideon's hand. 'Well, is he?' he asked quickly trying desperately to change the subject. 'He's been pretty subdued lately, he's stayed out of my way. I think he's finally getting sick of my bullshit' Gideon muttered while rubbing his head gently. 'Think it's because he's more afraid of that WarPath more than you, and seeing as you and him are never really far apart he's keeping safe distance' 'No shit' Slowly the sound of footsteps coming down the stairs in a slight jog caused all four men to divert themselves from what they had been focusing their attentions on and stare at who was coming down the stairs. They could all tell by the pace on the steps that it was way too quick to be that of Jeremiah. Then all queries were answered. Out of the corner of his eye Poe could see Trent's jaw fall agape slightly and heard the remote crashing to the floor as it fell out of Maynard's hand. Her navy blue hair was soaking wet and clinging to her neck mightily while the rest of her body glistened under the small lamp light's. The small towel wrapped round her body didn't leave much to the typical horny male's imagination either as she slowly looked in at the four men, smiled gently, Gideon suspected it was a forced one, and then took off down the hallway. The silence that followed was awkward for Trent, Vex and Maynard, but highly amusing for Gideon. 'Did you hire a maid?' asked Maynard. 'If so I want the number of the agency!' chipped in Trent. 'Lying bastard! You told us you had no hot water' bellowed Vex as he banged the back of the couch he was sitting on to further add power and emphasis to his point. It was quickly banished however by the three idiotic confused looks shot at him from the other three. 'What? Well he said he didn't!' 'That isn't a maid though is it Gideon?' Trent's voice had a slight element of excitement in it. The type of excitement a child would have after just confirming what his big Christmas present is. 'No, it's not' 'And you didn't kidnap her?' inquired Maynard, it was the serious tone in his voice that made it seem even more amusing. 'She know about, well, the old bastard and you and-well...' Ven stumbled around all the words in his limited storage of grammar to try and find the right words, but thankfully Gideon saved him from any further ordeal. 'She knows everything, and she understands. It's along story how she got here, but she needs a place to stay. She went through a lot, she's been through a lot, and she thinks she's got a hell of a lot more to go through. She's like me, we can relate to each other.' 'But she's not one of those chicks that sticks to you like a booger all the time is she?' inquired Trent as if this final question was the most serious of them all. 'More like duck tape sweetie. Booger's are easier to flick away' came the neutral toned voice of the young woman known as Tanya from outside the hallway. Not many people could make Trent turn red, and not many women could make his heart skip a beat without shaking all their female assets over him while spinning round on a pole. Yet she did, and did with such ease that it made Gideon stare at Trent hard to try and install the image on his face forever into his head for future comic relief. 'I like her' chuckled Vex approvingly. 'I like her a lot!' 'Me too' Gideon whispered, and double checked that nobody heard him, and smiled gently once he confirmed that nobody did.
