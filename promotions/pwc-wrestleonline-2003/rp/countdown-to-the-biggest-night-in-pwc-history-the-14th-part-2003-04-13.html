Countdown to the Biggest Night in PWC History - The 14th Part

13 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04.13.2003 What a change for Jonathan Conspiracy. Not too long ago, he made a one-time only appearance in IOW to job to his brother Romey Ransom, to try to help his brother 'get over', and many moons ago he lost his final PIW Match to Rune Winters, and sat home without a job for what felt like years. But Kendra and Ron's agreement led JCON back into a squared circle, and the pizza-pizza boys paid dearly for that union to ever come about. And now 'The Only Black Wrestler That Matters', is ready to prove himself tonight in Trenton, New Jersey. He's ready to show not just Frankie Bones, but Bones' mom, Bones' dad, Bones' dog that Bones loves to sleep with, Bones' cat that he does the same too, and Bones' girlfriend (aka pillow from puberty) that the only 'Black Star Power' in the PWC belongs to Jonathan Jerome Leary. So when you hear 'Guilty Until Proven Innocent' by Jay-Z and R. Kelly, please don't boo this man. Cheer for that fact that you are going to witness the best wrestler enter the ring, black or white, bar none. If you want to boo him because he's buddies with Ron Starr or Clark Carlisle, that isn't a valid reason. If you want to boo him because he 'sold out', then you don't know what 'selling out' really is. If you want to boo him because he's black, then fuck you, and I hope you die via choking on a brusselsprout. If you want to cheer for him because he's the greatest thing to happen to the PWC, then you just might get his autograph on the biggest night in PWC history, for a small fee. #1 Contender to the World Championship, here I come. Next stop, PWC World Championship, Toomes, DeVille, Vendetta... pay close attention to what happens to Bones tonight, because somewhere down the line, it will be you. :::Jonathan Conspiracy::: :::The Only Black Wrestler That Matters::: :::::::::::::::Fade Out:::::::::::::::::::
