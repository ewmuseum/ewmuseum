In Dreams.....

28 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/28/03 Alone in the darkness... A cold sweat... Masahiro Zangetsu suddenly sat up in shock a cold sweat running down his face. 'Is someone there?' His voice echoed through the apartment. The apartment that had previously been barren now had a few residents. A telephone sat off the hook in the corner. A tv stand held a small tv that simply played one channel.. The channel was WGTW 48, The TV itself was only 13 inches and black and white. The simple mat that Masa had previously slept on now replaced with a cheap looking metal bed. A sleeping bag covered Masa as he sat drenched in cold sweat alone in the darkness.. I suppose not the man thought to himself as he pushed out of the bed and walked to the bathroom. The light clicked on as he stared himself in the face. The scar stretched along his face, along with the scar stretched something that shocked Masa... A single tear dripped from his dead and useless eye. The other eye widened in shock as the tear fell from Masahiro's chin... 'my.... my eye....' His words shattered the silence as he stared himself in the mirror... Slowly Masa leaned forward looking directly into the mirror... 'How....' Slowly as Masa stands in the dim light his second eye begins to slowly but surely open. Shock filters through his mind as the light penetrates and the world becomes full for the first time... Suddenly Masahiro sits up in his bed... The room is still the same as the realization sinks in... His hand goes to his eye as tears begin to flow from his good eye.... The dream world shattered and the real world filled with pain slowly oozes back in... 'Father, I hate you.....'
