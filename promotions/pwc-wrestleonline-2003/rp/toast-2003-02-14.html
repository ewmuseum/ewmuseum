Toast

14 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 14/02/03 Sam didn’t like losing. It came as quite a shock really, a nasty kick to the system. It took her off guard and nagged at the back of her head with a rage unquenchable. Those few moments when she lost momentum, that thing inside her head that drew her to the top rope, it killed her chances of victory in one instant. Maybe she just got carried away, it was in her nature after all, but that didn’t make it right. She knew it was a character flaw, a problem she had always had. Her first appearance in the ring, live on TV, and she was taken apart by some biblical monster. She had been beaten down and nearly broken, but not enough… oh no, never enough. Things were looking up. Her hair had not played up since and she had regained her aura of happiness that flickered brightly around her at all times. She didn’t care for the beating given to her by Hoyt, but being saved by that hunk of a lad Sublime genuinely excited her. He said there was nothing in it, but she knew there was. There was no sense in what he did if he didn’t want her, but in all honesty, who DIDN’T want her? Maybe she was getting carried away again, but she didn’t dare contemplate that. Things were too good to ruin them by thinking too deeply about them. Tagging with him was a great experience for Sam; she enjoyed the thrill of having someone with her to help with the elimination of Hoyt from the rumble. She may have been able to do it alone, as she noticed her domination of the early periods of her match. But that didn’t matter. Sublime had a match with Hoyt and she with that nice Karina lass. Maybe she would accompany Sublime, pay a little visit to that hypocrite Hoyt. It was something to think about at least. She was quite happy at facing Karina as well. She was facing the same uphill struggle against the overall suppression of the supposed male superiority of the business and she wanted to put on a good show. It was understandable; really, it put some excitement into Sam, who now had a purpose to show off. She ran a thin hand tentatively through her neat hair and sighed loudly at nothing. Her button-like brown eyes stared deeply into the thin blue carpet, which was struggling to hide the floorboards. The room was calm and her eyes kept closing and fighting their way open in a useless attempt to fight off the urge to sleep. Slowly, she began to succumb and, with the darkness of the room, her eyes closed gently. Her head flopped backwards against the comfortable sofa and she lay still, not asleep, but still. “I’m Home!” The sudden burst of noise and the brightness of the door being flung open announced the arrival of Sarah, who forced her way into the room with a robust statement pointing out the clear and plain obvious. They were on speaking terms now, she had apologised to Sam about her outburst and things were sailing smoothly again, well, as smoothly as things are ever going to get in their small flat. “Surprisingly, I have noticed that fact” Replied Sam in a deadpan voice she kept only for special occasions or for insulting Sarah “I was trying to get some sleep, can you not, like, respect my needs and all that?” Sarah’s face morphed into a frown and she placed her bags of shopping on the thin carpet, crossing the arms that were holding them firmly across her chest. “I thought you would be, like, interested in what I had gotten you” Said Sarah, the excitement creeping back into her voice with renowned vigour. “It’s not forty winks is it?” Questioned Sam, fully knowing what Sarah would reply with. “I didn’t see you as a fan of the Rolling Stones, thought they were a bit old for your liking. That Mick Jaggar is sooo last century, like, totally out of it, girl” “I knew you would say that” She smiled at the innocence of Sarah, who would still be babbling if it wasn’t for Sam’s interruption “Forty winks is an English expression regarding, like, sleep. The Rolling Stones album is called Forty Licks” The look on Sarah’s face was priceless. It was an oddly strange concoction of shock, relief and confusion. She was glad that Sam was not a Rolling Stones fan, because they were not happy enough for her liking. “…No, its totally not sleep, girl, don’t you know that you cant buy it? I don’t know what your like in England, but they don’t sell it over here. You will totally love what I got you, like, take a look at this!” She began to rustle in the closest bag to her left foot, it was plain and hence gave no clue as to its contents, yet Sam could but imagine the horrors available in a place quite as big as this. Knowing Sarah, it could be anything from a new brand of eye shadow or some new robotic dog or something; for someone with next to no money, she could sure shop. Sam rubbed gently at her eyes and sat upright, she didn’t want a surprise at the moment; things were hectic and she just wanted a nice regular schedule so she could settle down and begin to get on with the rest of her robust, high action lifestyle. Suddenly, with a burst of energy, Sarah emerged from the bag cradling a large box in her chubby little arms. It read ‘Toaster’. “You like it?” Sarah motioned with a loose hand towards the box, her face beaming a smile brighter than some stars. “It’s a toaster.” She brought out the deadpan voice again “What the hell would I want with a toaster?” “You can, like, cook toast in it and so totally make an awesome cheese on toast” She was hurt and it showed through like a gash on skin. She had clearly become somehow wrongly attached to this toaster “No you cant. The cheese would fall off the toast and get stuck on the filament” “Oh… well you can make toast in it” Sam, despite being an optimist, found it hard to find a bright side to yet another cooking implement in the house. They could make toast using the grill anyway. She presumed that it must be the American way of saying ‘your skinny, put on some damn weight, bitch’. Sarah was definitely not skinny, very American in build, is our Sarah. She could see her expression turning into a pout, so she had to break in with the smile again. “Its great! We can, like so totally have proper toast now!” I suppose life was full of those little sacrifices.
