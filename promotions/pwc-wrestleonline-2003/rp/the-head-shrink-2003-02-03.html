The head shrink.

3 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/03/03 A dull room with wood paneling, a few scattered piece of furniture, and a dark colored carpet is where we find Hoyt William. A few paintings decorate the wall but nothing that provokes any emotion. To put it mildly the room is rather bland. Hoyt is seated in a leather chair and a young woman of about thirty-five sits across from him. The women’s hair is as dark as tar, yet her skin has an almost bright glow to it. She is dressed in a gray business suit that clings to her curvy body. She stares down at a notepad through her black wire framed glasses. In her hand is a pencil that she’s waving in the air. You can almost feel the tension in the air. Hoyt sits silent in a pair of slacks and a white dress shirt. He stares at the young lady waiting for some sort of initiation to begin. The room reeks of Old Spice emitting from Hoyt’s body, which disturbs the young women slightly. The uncomfortable silence is broken by the women’s soft voice. “Last week we we’re talking about you debuting in the ring. How did that go?” Asks the woman with a soft, sensitive tone. Hoyt takes a few moments to gather his thoughts before answering the simple question. A smile cracks his face as he finds the right words to express his feelings. “Fabulous. I want down to the ring and called out anybody who thought they had a chance against me. Needless to say somebody challenged and got beaten.” Hoyt responds with an air of cockiness to his voice. The young head doctor adjusts her legs to a crossed position. She looks Hoyt directly in his eyes searching for something she has not yet identified. Maybe a clue, a suggestion, a mannerism that would lead to a break through in her patience condition. “You sound confident in your victory? How would you have felt if you lost?” Probes the doctor with an inquisitive look upon her face. Hoyt’s cocky smirk disappears like the smoke from a match in the open sky. The thought of defeat never crossed his young mind. After years of working hard to be the best, defeat has become just a word. Something uttered to scare one into training harder. A word used to provoke a negative feeling just as the boogieman does to small children. “I don’t lose, it’s just something I don’t allow.” Hoyt strikes back with a hint of anger. The lady doctor now knows she has struck a cord with the young Adonis. She now knows the path she must blaze to get to Hoyt’s egotistic root cause. It’s going to be a long road but at least the direction is set. The navigation is the doctors job and she’s ready to drive. “Well what if you were defeated?” Snaps back the doctor. Hoyt adjusts has ass in the seat trying to find a comfort zone. He didn’t come to a shrink’s office to be provoked into anger. He came here for answers to his panic attacks. Is being badgered by somebody your paying really the answer. “It never would happen.” Hoyt responds in a defensive tone. The eye contact is now lost as Hoyt has shifted his eyes to the floor. The sure tell signs of conversation defense are show when Hoyt slouches back into his chair and crosses his arms. The doctor takes note of actions and sighs coming from Hoyt. “How can you be so sure that you wouldn’t lose?” Asks the doctor not changing her mild mannered tone. Hoyt’s arms remain crossed but he sits up attentively and looks the doctor in the eyes. His face is that of a little boy about to tell his mother a secret. He leans forward to make sure nothing is lost in his whisper. “Because I have God in my corner.” Whispers the large man in complete seriousness. The head doctor stares into the eyes of Hoyt looking for any element of levity. She finds none and feels the need to find out what exactly Hoyt means. “You mean God is here with all of us, and through faith you were able to obtain a victory?” Asks the Shrink. “No, I mean God told me I was the chosen one. Why do you think my body is perfect?? Why do you think my father is famous? Why do you think I do no wrong? I’m invincible because God is my manager. I’m your personal Jesus. I am Hoyt Williams the son of Satan, but soldier of God, and brother of Jesus.” Yells Hoyt with passion and conviction in his voice. “So it’s God who allows you to have panic attacks and rage?” Retorts the doctor with a hint of sarcasm in her voice. Hoyt stands up like somebody just stuck an unwelcomed finger in his asshole. His face turns a bright red which contrasts his blonde hair. He shakes his head looking for something profound or hurtful to say. “A don’t have to take this carp. I’m a savior and undefeated.” Stammers the angry Williams boy. In one quick motion Hoyt’s out the door leavening the doctor with a blank look on her face. She jots down some notes in her notebook before the thought of lunch overcomes her mind. The end of another interesting session.
