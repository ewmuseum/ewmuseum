Eternally Damned: The Final Chapter: Chapter IX: Born Again

20 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: A Landmark In The Holy War …Propaganda rolls off of his bum onto his knees and stands up with a bit of difficulty. After all he is over seven foot and over three hundred pounds, standing up in a straight jacket and an empty tranquilliser dart stuck in your neck isn’t the most easy of thing to do. 'You’re joking?' Propaganda bellows in fury at the voice of his manager. 'I would not laugh about this occasion, brother Propaganda, I’m deadly serious. You have gone insane and I’ve had to have you committed. Knowing you would not come easily I had to have you tranquillised.' 'The Fat Preacher' Eric Sagar informs his client. 'Pardon?' Propaganda barks in disbelief. 'Well I had to call in a contact of mine who works at this mental hospital…' Eric Sagar tries to explain. 'No, No, I understand that, not that it’s understandable, but I understand it. It’s just where are my glasses? I can hardly see. Where are you?' Propaganda queries squinting his eyes. Like a mole who’s just been flushed out of his whole and put into the arctic. 'Glasses have sharp edges. You could do damage to yourself or others. That’s why you’re here. You’re mentally unstable.' The Fat Preacher informs Propaganda casually as if it was part of a polite conversation. 'That’s what I don’t understand! I’ve done nothing wrong.' Propaganda barks snidely. 'I beg to differ, Brother Propaganda. You were on the verge of defecting, thanks to the devil. The devil may be the source of all that is evil, the organ grinder in all that is unholy but he is not stupid.' Eric Sagar sighs in a mellow tone. 'What are you trying to say?' Propaganda asks bluntly. 'Satan killed Hessian. HE TOOK HIS LIFE TO GET ONE OVER ON YOU AND YOUR BELEIFS! HE WANTS TO CONVERT YOU! YOU’RE SUCH A POWER THAT HE WANTS TO CLAIM YOU! YOU ARE THE POWER HIMSELF! HE’S SENT YOU IN AN EMOTIONAL DOWNFALL, SPIRALLING INTO DESPAIR, HALTING TO A LANDING IN A PIT OF SORROW AND DEPRESSION!' Eric Sagar roars. 'S… S… S… Satanism… took…' Propaganda gulps in horror. 'Satanism took my friend?' 'I’m afraid so, Satanism and all that is morally incorrect in the world, my brother.' The preacher sighs. 'So I suggest you convert your despondency into anger. Into hatred for Satan and his followers, all of those led astray. Convert your anger into a fist of rage to drop on their fearful hearts.' Propaganda nods his head. 'Together me, you and any companions with morally correct intentions such as our good friend Hoyt Williams shall join to create a force not to revive Wrestling, but to revive humanity in general. I’ve had enough of this disbelieving world.' Eric Sagar clamours. 'Follow my commands brother Propaganda, God told me to lead you into a better future, I shall point the finger in the right direction. Just follow my command.' Propaganda nods his head. 'I will come and get you in a minute, I’ll just leave you a few minutes to think.' Uh-huh yeah, Uh-huh yeah, Uh uh-huh yeah, Uh uh-huh yeah, Uh uh-huh yeah, Uh uh-huh yeah, Yeah, Hear my cries, Hear my call, Lend me your ears, See my fall, See my error, Know my faults, Time halts, See my loss, Know I'm lacking, Backtracking, Where I met you, Pistol packing, Itchy finger, Trigger happy, Try to trap me, Bad rap, Wiretap me, Back stab me, Break the faith, Fall from grace, Tell me lies, Time flies, Close your eyes, Come with me. Propaganda huffs his nose, blowing out all thoughts of morally incorrectness. Come with me, Yeah, Come with me, Uh-huh yeah, Yeah. Propaganda stands up perfectly straight, like a soldier at attention, waiting to face the war against the Devil. You said to trust you, You'd never hurt me, Now I'm disgusted, Since then adjusted, Certainly you fooled me, Ridiculed me, Left me hanging, Now shit's boomeranging, Right back at you, Think long-ranged, Narrow minded, Left me blinded, I co-signed it, Shit backfired, But I'm bouncing back, I grinded, Not many would bear the pressure, You comprehend me, You musn't end me, You offend me, It's trauma, Feel the drama, Come with me. Propaganda rubs his thumbs across the palms of his hands, mopping up the sweat of hate for the wrong way of life. Yeah, Uh-huh, Heh-heh Come with me, Don't be afraid, Yeah, Come with me. I close my eyes, And I see, You… standing there, I cry, Tears--of sorrow, I die. Uh-huh, Yeah. Propaganda leans his head back slamming memories of his old friend Hessian to the back of his mind. Fuck my enemies, Fuck my foes, Damn these hoes, Is stepping on my toes, Back up off me, Take your hands off me, Give me room to breathe, I'm not hearing it, I'm not fearing it, I'm up to my ears in it, Bullshit, I'm destructive, Some women find that seductive, Some say it's lunacy, But luckily I've been moving on, I ignore you, Sorry if I call you, I neglect you, Don't mean to disrespect you, Can't you see... I love you dearly, And not sincerely, But you annoy me, You can't avoid me, I'm here to stay, Forever, And ever in a day that’s never, I can't let you go, I can't forget it, Why you did it, I won't permit it, I won't acquit it, I want to fight you, I'll fucking bite you, Can't stand nobody like you, You can't run, You can't hide, No surprise, Close your eyes, Come with me. Propaganda starts to snarl at Satanism. Come with me. Propaganda rotates his neck round, warming up for his battle. Come with me. Propaganda does one jump on the spot. Come with me. RAAAAAR! Then in an amazing display of strength, Propaganda lets off a roar and rips the straight jacket off of him, tearing it, ripping his arms up like an automatic double door on performance enhancements. COME WITH ME! Propaganda heads to the door and kicks it open with his foot, sending the lock on it ricocheting across the corridors. I'ma take you with me! I'm here to stay, Forever, And ever and a day, That's never, Come with me.
