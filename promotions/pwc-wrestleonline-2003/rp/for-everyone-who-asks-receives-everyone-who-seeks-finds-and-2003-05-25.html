'For everyone who asks, receives. Everyone who seeks, finds. And the door is opened to everyone who knocks ~ Luke 11:10'

25 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 25th May 2003 Propaganda is propped up on his rickety old arm chair with a tatty cotton cover, glaring out of a window on looking a street, you can tell it’s heading to summer as it’s bright. Outside you can hear the joyful chuckles of charming children, chanting rhymes whilst skipping and having water fights. The birds are in full chorus, chirping in a cheerful chanter. The smell of freshly cut grass fills the air and tickles the taste buds with it’s tangy presence. Propaganda has his window open, letting a herd of fresh air run free throughout his rather untidy apartment. He’s tapping his fingers in a catchy rhythm on the fraying arm of the chair. Propaganda is in a pair of black pants and a plain grey jumper. Ring, Ring, His phone gargles from the work top. Propaganda lifts his three hundred plus pound body out of his chair and steps over to the hideous cocoa brown kitchen area, where he picks up the portable house phone. '678905.' He answers. 'Hello brother Eric, what can I do for you?' Propaganda asks like a servant to his master. 'The ranch? Why? All right.' The scene fades to black. We see Propaganda and Eric riding a horse. On looked by a marvellous hilly landscape, which is clashing with the bright sun. The whole area is peaceful, flooded in fresh blades of glistening emerald grass. Propaganda is wearing a pair of black jeans and a white polo neck T-shirt. Like always Propaganda’s hair is yanked back in a cover of gel. Eric Sagar is looking the part, with brown leather cowboy boots, a grey suit, white shirt and tie. Looking immaculately neat, like always. Both are riding mighty chestnut brown horses. Strolling around an open field. 'Beautiful isn’t it, Brother Propaganda?' Sagar sighs in marvel, glaring into the distance with one hand grabbing the horse reigns firmly the other stroking it’s main. Propaganda swivels his head a round. 'Yes.' Propaganda grunts with the usual emotionless expression on his face. 'The beautiful acres of green country side, where only us and the singing birds can be heard.' Eric Sagar chirps with a chuckling green on his face. 'I’m glad I bought it, and for half a million it’s a bargain. This ranch has everything I needed. A peaceful alternative, to my hectic life as preacher and your manager, in PWC. And there are some thugs and hooligans in PWC. So many nasty pieces of work, don’t you think?' Propaganda takes a nice long inhale, smelling the wild aroma of Eric Sagar’s recently purchased ranch. 'Yes.' Propaganda exhales. 'Such as those corrupt Paddy Heathens. The 'Un'Holy Underground. It’s funny they call themselves Underground as that’s where they’ll be after we’ve finished with them, six feet underground but their souls will be deeper. In the very core of hell, brewing like a glass of their savoured and idolised stout. Which they worship as if it was a divine ambrosia.' Eric Sagar growls in a bitter bite of bad blood. Propaganda nods his head in agreement. 'Very true.' 'We shall show them and their unholy race of people.' Sagar barks harshly. 'We shalt strike down upon them with VENGEANCE AND FURIOUS ANGER! Like Sampson pulled the pillars from under the palace, you shalt pull the pillars from under The 'Un'Holy Underground, but you shall be left standing. THEY QUESTION OUR MORALS!? THE PADDY HEATHENS SHALT FALL AT OUR KNEES OF A TRUE, RIGHTEOUS WAY OF LIFE!' Propaganda nods his head affirmatively. ''For everyone who asks, receives. Everyone who seeks, finds. And the door is opened to everyone who knocks ~ Luke 11:10' Eric Sagar quotes with a red face. 'However, those who sin are punished and get shown the door! They shall be shown the door, and be thrown out into a life of hellish slave labour for the Devil.' Eric Sagar cackles. 'A-Men.' Propaganda chants.
