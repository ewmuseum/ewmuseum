Let us pray.

2 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/02/03 It’s a Baptist church on the dark side of Atlantic City, New Jersey. The ceiling is high and comes to a schoolhouse point at the top. The walls are white and filled with pictures of the stages of the cross. One end of the room is the entrance, and the other is a red stage with a podium. A cross is the centerpiece above everything else. The room is filled to capacity with black men and women dressed in there finest. It’s Midnight mass and the place is jumping with more energy then any local drunk tank or casino. Men and women are on there feet with there hands extended to the lord listening to the preacher, preach. The preacher man sticks out like a duck on Wall Street, as he’s the only white person in the place. Not only is he the only white person but also he stands a solid 6’6” with not even a once of fat on him. His white robe covers his perfect body like icing on a cake. His forehead is soaked with sweat as he preaches with all he’s got. “I said somebody give me an a-MEN!!” Shouts Hoyt Williams with conviction and power. “Amen!!” Replies the parishioners with might. Hoyt Williams wipes off his forehead with a white towel as he starts to pace around the red stage. “Tonight I was in a gymnasium full of sinners. Most of whom haven’t listened to the words of JE-SUS in ages. Sinners hell bent on destructing their bodies with booze, women, violence, drugs, and obesity.” Thunders Hoyt to crowd. The congregation shouts out random “Amen’s” as Hoyt delivers his sermon with passion. As he’s on the stage working his magic, the thought of his father Duke Williams sitting on the coach drunk flashes into his head. The images of his wayward father drive his preaching to the next level. You can feel the heat trickle down your back like Christ is in the room with you. “I’m a man of 22 and I have given my life to Christ.” The crowd cheers so loud the roof shakes. “Tonight I spread the word of Jesus to those parasites looking for the host of Christ. When I was done my brothers I had the crowd doing jumping jacks for the lord! Training there out of shape temples back to a Mecca of worship. Let me tell you brothers and sisters it was a site to behold.” Hoyt reflects. Again Hoyt wipes the sweat from his brow produced from the production he is putting forth. It was indeed a great night for Mr. Williams. But will the stories in tomorrow’s paper reflect that or will they talk of how great Duke’s kid did. Will the shadows continue to cover Hoyt’s life? “A missionary in the world of wrestling some would say is a waste. My brothers and sisters I can tell you it is not. It is a mission I’m on from the spoken words of Jesus himself. You see my brothers; I was feeling bad and ready to give up on life. Ready to end it all. Ready to just give it all up. I was lying there down and out. Yes I was. Then I head a voice tell me. You know what it said?” Asks Hoyt as the crowd yells out random thoughts. “No” responds the crowd. “I’m going to tell you what it said. IT said Hoyt get your butt out of bed and preach the words of the lord. Do so in a wrestling ring where you can reach the most people. Save your families name and make it right again. Do so in the name of GOD!!!” Hoyt almost passes out as he yells. “AMEN” says the crowd in unison. “I can tell you this my brothers tonight when I was in the ring at least one man felt the good book. The holy Bible affected his outlook like none other. One man was touched by his Personal Jesus like never before. The good book hit him so hard he was laid out cold. IT was a miracle my brothers and sisters. A miracle of chirst.” Duke Williams whispers with a smile on his face. Little does the flock know that he’s being very literal in his sermon. “Thank you and God Bless YOU. AMENNNNNA” screams Hoyt. The choir starts to sing as the collection plates are passed around. Hoyt heads to the back of the church were he waits to collect his share. He shakes hands and pats as he walks to the back. It should be a good night of work, both in the ring and in the church.
