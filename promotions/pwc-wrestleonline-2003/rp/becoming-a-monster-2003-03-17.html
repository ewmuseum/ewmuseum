Becoming A Monster...

17 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-17-2003 “Man…this is gonna be great! We’re gonna be stars! Can you imagine us on all those New Jersey TV sets at ONE time?” To accuse Neon of not having youthful exuberance about the world would be a lie no matter who made the accusation. His excited voice could be heard in the background like soft music in a convenience store, the humble home of Gunner Gojoh knowing no peace with Neon’s constant verbiage. The focus is within the back bedroom of the home, soft slices of bright light seeping in through the window as they strike a mattress devoid of a bed and a 20 inch TV in the corner, the light coat of dust settled upon it giving the impression that it gets no use. The aroma is that of fresh berries, the scent emitting from a lit stick of incense being held in place by a wooden Buddha figurine. This meager room is sanctuary for the Olden Warrior, Gunner Gojoh. He is in a calm trance, sitting in a butterfly position on the soft grey rug with his eyes closed. His breathing is slow and steady, his mouth and nose allowing in only slight spurts of air before releasing it again. ”He’s a monster….an utter monster…” Gunner’s calm thoughts are invaded by the 6’9, 385 pound monster known as Penance as he decapitates a faceless victim with a clothesline in an imaginary ring. The Masked Monster lets out a roar to a crowd that isn’t here, his body pulsating with destructive energy. Gunner’s face tightens, trying to push the monster from his head ”He’s broken everyone there is to offer…” No matter how hard he tries to block him out, thoughts of Penance continue to break through as the monster places his unrelenting grip upon another set of faceless victims. Penance releases a roar that would scare a lion, powering both victims up to the heavens before sending them plummeting to hell with chokeslams. The self-proclaimed “Great Purger” stands over the two broken bodies, no remorse for the destruction as he throws his arms out and roars with dominance. ”The odds are impossible…the stakes are so high…” Gunner can now see himself in this horrible nightmare, wanting to move but being helpless bound by the arms between the top and middle rope. The strength of his 287 pound frame is not enough to break him free, witnessing in horror as Penance places his death grip around the throat of his first faceless victim in this nightmare… Neon. Neon kicks and screams about, his wild flaming red braids in a tussle from trying to break free as Penance’s soulless eyes stare right into Gunner’s incubus black pupils. ”He is an indiscriminate monster….he will hurt those you love.” And that is indeed the case as Penance powers Neon up, holding him by one hand in the air as his huge frame flexes with power. Gunner tries to fight his imprisonment in the ropes….screaming at the top of his lung, but no sound coming out. Nothing can save Neon as Penance DRIVES his youthful protégé’ into the mat. With his superiority proven, Penance takes his huge black boot and places it on the chest of Neon, the usually exuberant youth laid out spread-eagle with his eyes wide open…. Yet with no life… The way a corpse would look. ”He will destroy you…..your on borrowed time.” Penance grins, extending his index finger and pointing to the captive Gunner. He then takes that finger and runs it across his enlarged throat, the symbolism there saying more then words. With that, the Masked Beast slowly stalks towards Gunner with his hand opened at the ready, ever nearing his throat as Gunner continues to try to fight the ropes. Just as he feels the tips of Penance’s fingers nearing his trachea, Neon’s voice seems to come from nowhere as he exclaims,”YO OLD MAN…..SNAP OUT OF IT!!” Gunner’s eyes jolt open, sweat beading down his scarred forehead as he finds himself back in the humble room he started in, the incense burned all the way to the tip. His head then turns to the right, finding Neon standing in the door way with a confused look on his handsome face. The cocky Asian holds up two open up-shirts, one emerald green and the other sky blue, asking in indecision, ”What you think? “Green Lantern” green,” he playfully holds the shirt up to his chest and “models” it before finishing, “Or “bad boy” blue? I wanna look blazin’ for this meeting we got with that wasabi guy.” Neon stops with his questions as he takes notice of Gunner in a fearful trance, the youth showing concern as he asks, “You okay Gun?” Gunner’s eyes are filled with a cocktail of gripping fear and beastial fury, the old warrior responding in a lost tone, “Look…you go ahead. I need to tend to some business.” Neon wants to ask Gunner again, wanting to really know the problem…but he stops himself cold, knowing Gunner’s nature as he turns and leaves his presence. Rising to one knee, Gunner walks over to the sliding slit closet on the right wall, shoving it open as the closet reveals very few clothes and a shoebox sitting on the top shelf. He grabs the shoebox, usurping the top as his eyes gaze upon a small metal key sitting in the contents of the cardboard box. Taking it within his grasp, Gunner eyes it in a lost trance, saying aloud, ”The only way to destroy a monster…is to become one….” The scene fades as Gunner’s maddened eyes keep their gaze upon the key, glimmering in the light as it’s purpose is yet unknown.
