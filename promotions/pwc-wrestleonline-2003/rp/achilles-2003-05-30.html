Achilles

30 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: ... Why had he returned. That constant sly thorn always prodding in my side, protruding it's jagged self savagely further and further into my skin and resting there while my blood trickled out. I thought I had set myself free of him, banished him from my life. The one man who actually caused me to lose sleep at night just over the thought of him. Not that I even slept much these days. He had no business here. He had no need to be here. His lies had already started. That polite and genuine happy go lucky facade he had mastered so well was already slipping, and it was because of me. I was the only one who could see through his mask, his disguise. He was a wolf in sheep's clothing, but I was tearing through that second skin of his before he had a chance to deceive anyone else with it. Was I having doubts over what I was doing? No. Yet even though we had only just started the mind games, I was already too deep and too far involved to turn back now. I couldn't let him get the upper hand, I had to stay on him. But why? What was that going to achieve?! Stay on him 'til what? He has enough and leaves? HA! Fat chance, he never gives up, he never lets go. He's like a dog with a chew toy. A bear clawing at a blood stained wound. He was like that then, and he still is now. He was like it when he was bathing in the ecstasy of gold in RUSH, and clung to it for dear life every time I gained on him and his precious possessions. Politics, bribes, cheating. He used it all to hold me back then, but this time it's different. He's in nobody's back pocket now. It's just me and him. Then why am I so worried about him? I already know the answer, but I still ask myself, hoping I may be presented or discover a new answer, but it never comes. I've seen what he can do and what he's capable of when pushed, and I was pushing him right now. We'd been through a lot together. We always ended up travelling down the same roads. I respected him as much as I feared him though. Yes, I feared another human being. You're paladin has shown weakness. You're paladin ahs feelings and an Achilles heel. It goes by the name of Matthias Kuntz Hunt. So fucking shoot me! I'm human, sorry if it surprises you, but I'm afraid you're just going to have to live with it, just like I have. You know he would regard me as Dismas. Dismas, one of the men who was crucified with Jesus. The thief who repented his sins and asked God for forgiveness before he was crucified. The good thief. Well, something like that anyway. He would try and manipulate me, try to make me like him. Yet I was always plagued by a conscience unlike him, and I would always turn to the 'good'. The good paladin. Well I'm sick and tired of being held down by you Matthias. I'm not running anymore, and I'm not hiding. You carry on being the thorn in my side, and I'll carry on being your Dismas. You're one weakness. I know I'm your main fear and worry, just like you are mine. Let the games begin? No. They began a long time ago Matt. We were just to busy killing each other to realise and notice it back then. The games will come to an end soon though Matthias. I promise you that. Sweet dreams.
