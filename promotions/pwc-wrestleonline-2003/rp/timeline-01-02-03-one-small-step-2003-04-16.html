Timeline - 01/02/03 : One Small Step

16 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 16/04/03 As the door slams shut behind her, Karina Wolfenden looks out into the New York night on the 1st day of February. Eyes glimmering with anticipation and leathers pulled on, she wants to take a few seconds for herself before setting off into the unknown. ‘Time to get the 411 on this PWC place.’ she says in a lively manner, eager to see what her first wrestling promotion is like. Her expectations are pretty low, she doesn’t expect luxurious surroundings, but then again Karina’s always been more about people than environments. She does a final check of her rucksack. As usual she’s travelling light with only a few items of clothing packed. Then, as if to seal the deal, she zips it up and slings it over her right shoulder, almost in one motion threading her left arm through the free strap as it flings around. Running her thumbs under the shoulder straps so the bag falls into place, she’s now ready. ‘Karina Wolfenden - Professional Wrestler.’ is proudly declared by the boarder with both pride and splendour, even posing dramatically as she proclaims it. Upon hearing how she sounds Karina breaks into a chuckle, liking how it sounds. ‘Has a good ring to it.’. Zipping her leathers up as she heads down the steps, Karina then breaks off and leaps up onto the hand rail, dropping the 10 feet or so down to the gravel where her feet land with a soft crunch. Recoiling up from her landing crouch, she’s now standing beside her baby. Her pride and joy. A red Ducati 748, not just A Superbike... THE Superbike. When she hit that age, her father had said he’d buy her whatever car she wanted. While most daughters would have fainted at the prospect of driving a Porsche or a Merc, Karina politely shrugged the offer off. For a few seconds Lawrence would of then wondered what he could spend the money he’d be saving on, until she pulled a picture of her beloved out of her pocket and placed it in his hand like an order. It was the debate that followed in which Lawrence realised Karina wasn’t one to give in when there was something she wanted. After all, she got the bike, doesn’t she. ‘Ready to roll?’ she asks the machine playfully as she pats it’s side like one would horse, as if to ready up her metallic steed. The slender frame of Karina looks out of place on the powerful machine in the physical sense, but there seems to be a strange kind of chemistry between woman and machine that makes them appear natural together despite first appearance. The calm of the February evening I then ripped asunder by the Ducati’s engine, the roar almost sounding out like a warning call to all of it’s vehicular kin on the roads to beware, it’s marking it’s territory. Without even looking back, her hand reaches behind and plucks her distinctively pattered helmet off the back of the machine. Red, black, silver, same as her leathers. The red of the bike, the silver of the frame, the black of the tyres. The scene is so colour co-ordinated that it is truly a sight to behold. There seems to be an order to this. First the hair gets tied back, then the dyed streak gets flicked out, then the mirrored sunglasses go on, then the helmet... Then, she’s ready. Ahead of her is the 1st night in PWC’s history, the night she can say she was at if the company does indeed take-off like many say it will. But, then again, this is Karina... She just hopes it’s fun. The Federation’s creator, along with his mystique, is a stranger to her, he’s just the guy who made the call that she accepted. On the night when many hope their careers will be thrust into the spotlight, where many hope their livelihoods will be salvaged, when some want to witness the birth of wrestling’s future... A young woman from Albany sets off to have some fun. And sets off very loudly. The tyres screech as they finally hit the road surface, then in the blink of an eye, the only daughter of Lawrence Wolfenden is nearly out of sight, off to begin her own wrestling story.
