Countdown To The Biggest Night In PWC History (Part #5)

7 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/7/03 Special Delivery Presents: Countdown To The Biggest Night In PWC History (Part #5) The scene fades from black showing an office room. In the center of the room is a large desk. Papers are scattered in clusters on the desk along with a small calculator as well as empty pizza boxes. Pictures of children are hung on the wall as well as new paper clippings the person is obviously proud of. A name tag is neatly placed in the center of the desk. It reads: Cousin Mario. Behind the desk sits none other than Cousin Mario himself, owner of the ever growing pizza chain wildly popular to junk food eating teenagers of New Jersey. He is going over notes on papers in his hands as a knock is heard on the door. “Come on in boys.” Mario says as he waves two people into the office. Those two people just happen to be PWC’s own Special Delivery. “Welcome boys, please sit down.” Mario notions for Chuck and Sammy to take seats in the two chairs placed in front of his desk. “What’s up boss?” Chuck says with a lopsided smile. He knows that these meetings occur normally between workers and their bosses but Mario has always been rather intimidating to work for. “Wait, let me guess.” Sammy interrupts Mario before he can speak, “Chuck and I are employees of the month again?” Sammy smiles at Chuck who returns the smile. The both then look at the Cousin Mario plaque on the wall showing the employees of the month, whom of which has been Bello and D’Andrea for the past 3 months. “Boys, you do know that ever since you have been showing up on PWC TV the business has started to boom.” Mario smiles and lets the compliment settle. “You guys fight with the Cousin Mario’s name on your shirts and in return I give you all the free pizza you want.” “And to give to fans boss, they love that!” Chuck says with a smile as Sammy pats him on the back. “I even thought it was a great idea to market your boss, Mr. Ron Starr by making him his very own pizza style. The Starr Treatment Pizza has been a best seller for 2 months now boys.” The boys nod and listen on with curious expressions. They are unsure where Mario is planning on going with this meeting. Panic begins to fill both boys’ minds and sweat can be seen on their brows. Usually these meetings consist of a five minutes of great job and don’t eat while you’re on the job and then both parties can go upon their business as usual. The longlivity of this meeting has Special Delivery rather worried. “Yep boss. Cousin Mario’s will soon become the most popular pizza place in all of New Jersey!” Sammy says proudly with a twinge of nerves in his voice. “Better yet, the east coast!” Chuck adds in sensing Sammy’s urgency and panic. “No, even better the whole country will…” Sammy begins to finish his sentence but is interrupted by Cousin Mario once again. “Yes boys, but I have been doing some thinking.” He pauses to let that comment settle in as both boys shift awkwardly in their chairs. “Every Saturday we stay open late so people can come in and watch you two on PWC TV. Watch you guys wrestle and represent Cousin Mario’s while at the same time I give out half priced pizza. Well after watching Saturdays…less than well…proud performance of you two I did some thinking.” “Oh no!” Sammy shouts as he throws his head into his hands. “I knew it would happen Chuck, we are getting the can!” Chuck grabs on to Sammy, “Little buddy I doubt that is what is happening, right boss?” Chuck is as nervous as Sammy as they both look at Cousin Mario with puppy dog eyes. “Relax boys, you are not getting fired.” Both boys give sighs of relief. “However, I was considering what Ron Starr was talking about you two…we need to make you a little bit more marketable. And what better way to do that than PWC’S first major PPV Capital Punishment. First off you boys have a major match against three other teams, Death Match Samurai, Total Violation, and 2GVC.” “We know boss, and we have been training real hard!” Sammy says with loads of enthusiasm. “Yep, eating right and working out.” Chuck adds with a smile as Sammy nods his head in agreement. “Yes, well I don’t think eating left over Chinese food and running outside half naked to grab the paper for your dad is a workout Chuck.” Mario adds with a concerned look of disappointment at the team. “Do you know what it would do for the business if you two walk out of Capital Punishment with PWC’S Tag Titles? What a major upset that would cause?” “That would be wonderful wouldn’t it Sammy?” Chuck begins to daze off into his own fantasy of winning the tag titles. “Man o Man I wish that we could win those babies.” Sammy adds as he two joins Chuck in LALA land. Cousin Mario gets red in the face as he snaps both boys out of their dream sequence. “Well, it’s a long shot but you can! First off I want you too to be in the gym for the rest of the week working out. Sammy, work on those uneven bars and practice your flips off the vault horse.” Sammy smiles proud that his gymnast abilities can possibly help Special Delivery to a victory. “Chuck I want you to start lifting weights again. Pump the iron as much as you can, imagine it’s that waste of space VIOLATOR. I also want you eating nothing but fatty foods Chuck. You’re to big now to start loosing weight with this important of a match coming up. You think that those two 4 Eastern narrow eyed fools in this match are going to be able to do anything to you with brute strength? You are a 350 pound pile of flesh. Get it up to 375 for safe measure.” Both pizza boys’ faces are now filled with smiles again hopeful of possibly winning tag team gold by doing things they do best, eating. “And remember, teams that play dirty loose, but teams that don’t get caught playing dirty win. What else did I forget?” Mario looks at his pad with the list of things he wanted to tell Special Delivery. “Oh yes, free pizza for the entire roster on Sunday night and lastly, make those Pizza trashing fools Total Violation wish they never messed with Cousin Mario. I mean Special Delivery! NOW GET TO WORK!” Special Delivery jumps up from their seats and out the door. Excited and pumped up, ready to give 110% to secure a win at the Pay Per View. Once they leave Cousin Mario sits alone in his chair. “Those boys have what it takes to be champions.” Mario says as he leans his head back in his chair. Once the door to his office clicks shut though another smile crosses his face. “Who am I kidding? Those two have as much talent as I do at dancing. They are gonna get their asses whopped 3 times full. But at least they will make me money doing it!” Cousin Mario laughs as he pulls out a stack of hundred’s from his desk and throws them up in the air. The Camera fades to black.
