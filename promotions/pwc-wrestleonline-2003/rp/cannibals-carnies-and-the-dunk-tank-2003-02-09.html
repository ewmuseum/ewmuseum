Cannibals, Carnies, and The Dunk Tank.

9 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/02/03 'This is simply asking for trouble, you understand the chances of us getting savagely murdered by some deranged locals are in the 90% range!' croaked the shrill voice of Jeremiah O'Barr. His voice trembled slightly as he uttered the words, a sign of how cold the night was. '80% unfortunately' Gideon replied calmly walking several yards ahead of Jeremiah, who had been in a mood since he and Gideon had no other option but to walk back through the stabbing cold night back to their cheap motel after Gideon ruined any chance of them getting a ride back. 'Why did you have to kill it? Answer me that at least you young fool!' 'It annoyed me, and it was a means to an end' 'An end to what?' 'That guys narrow mindedness' 'The guy was a pig farmer, he has every right to be narrow minded, what were you expecting from him?!' 'Some sort of open mindedness at least' 'You sliced the throat of one his pigs in the back of his pick up Gideon! He was willing to give us a ride back to the motel, which would have prevented us from walking along this horrific route, which so far is just sand and desert under this ungodly night!' 'I prefer walking, live with it'. Roland, who sat perched on Gideon's shoulder calmly rubbed his head gently against Gideon's cheek in agreement. 'Roland likes the temperature too' 'That bird can ......' Before Jeremiah's harsh insult could be fully utilised, both he and Gideon stopped dead in their tracks, the sight which had seemingly appeared out of nowhere now caught their eyes with blistering effect. 'What in God's name is that? It can't surely be what I think it is, can it?' asked Jeremiah in a nervous tone. Gideon smiled menacingly at the sight before breaking into a slight chuckle and nodding to Jeremiah's question. 'The circus is indeed in town Jeremiah.' 'How did we miss it?' 'We're in the middle of nowhere as you said, it's not like we were on the lookout for it. Plus the lights are off and it's closed, it's barely noticeable from afar in this light.' 'Closed, thank goodness for that at least' 'No, come on, surely you went to the circus as a child' 'My childhood was not filled with all the pleasantries that all you typical Americans enjoy' Gideon merely shrugged and took a deep breath while smiling. The raw dry stench of the desert and sand under the cold night circulated through his nose right through his lungs and down to his stomach, making him shiver slightly before he took off towards what could only be described as a Carnival Of Sorrow from it's mere sight. 'What, may I ask is this circus doing in the middle of the desert?!' Gideon was already walking towards the circus as he answered the question leaving Jeremiah only one option, to follow him unless he wanted to walk back to the motel by his lonesome self. 'They're carnies Jeremiah, you think this so called good state is going to let some throwback gypsies invade their precious cities and towns filled with expensive houses and white picket fences with the stench of freshly mown grass swirling through the air? Hell no, they have no choice to come here by the looks of it. They're outcastes, just like you and I' Just as he finished the sentence, a shrill scream came soaring through the air causing Gideon and Jeremiah to stop dead in their tracks. 'You were saying Gideon?' Gideon ignored Jeremiah's comment, and drew his attention to the assortment of caravan's parked outside the entrance to the gateway of hell's carnival where the scream originated from. 'Okay, time to leave, if that isn't an omen then I don't know what is' snapped Jeremiah in a mixture of anger, nervousness and due to the sharp cold temperatures. Yet his pleas fell on def and ignorant ears as The Shade took off towards the arrangement of carnivals. The sound of more screams and cries drawing him near. 'Gideon, I am telling you NOW that...' 'You don't tell me anything anymore!' Gideon shouted as he turned around to face The Elderly Evil. 'You may have control over me from poisoning me like the sick twisted lonely bastard you are, but you lost every right to seek my help, guidance, trust and demands the moment you sank that poisonous dart into me!' Jeremiah gave his usual sly sick grin, the one he always wore when Gideon grew angry at him. It gave him an even stronger feeling of how in control of the young combat machine he was. 'I've grown sick and twisted, but not by your wishes, but by my own decisions. Now shut up and come with me or walk off alone back to the motel on your own, and to be quite honest I hope you take the latter option because nothing would please me more than to walk back up this road in 20 minutes and to find your body mangled and hacked like a rotted piece of meat by some of the local wildlife!' Jeremiah's smile faded instantly and was immediately followed by him waddling along to catch up with Gideon. The screams grew louder as The Dark Duo grew closer to the camper vans, nothing to signify immediate death was blooming in the area, more of a domestic scream. But we know how they can escalate, and with Gideon's self worth low as it is the last thing he needed was the death of some young woman hanging over what little of his conscience was left. Yet he didn't need to try and hone in from where the trouble was coming from for long, as one of the doors of the camper vans violently burst open followed by a young woman being tossed out onto the hard dusty sand gritted ground. 'Good for nothin' whore!' came a deep Southern voice as the girl crawled helplessly crying in tears. 'Get her Joel' came another deep southern voice, this time of a female. 'Don't yew worry Martha!, I will' 'Right ya' are Joel!' Gideon looked on in somewhat calm confusion, never the one to usually spawn out of his constant calm, depressed and mysterious state. Soon a podgy old man in his late forties, a broad sly smile on his face and a large buck knife in his hand came stumbling out. 'Now, this isn't really fair now is it?' spoke Gideon softly from the darkness. The man identified as Joel and the young girl turned their heads sharply to greet The Harvester Of Macabre himself as he continued to speak in his deep mature calm voice. 'This young girl has been tied up, abused by the look of it as well. Now where is the sadistic fun in this Joel? I mean come on, surely you yourself enjoy mutilating someone who's a challenge at least?' Joel smiled revealing a set of mustard yellow teeth, his hair brown and slicked back not from gel but from grease and his stubble grew in vast random areas on his face. 'Hehe, your an out of Towner ain't ya fella?' 'So are you and your people for that matter Joel' 'Ahhh now that's where yer wrong fella. Ya probably think we'res just some old foolish carnies don't ya?' Gideon simply nodded while Joel simply chuckled manically. 'Gideon...' spoke Jeremiah softly. His beckoning was ignored. 'Well yous a wrong there outsider!' Gideon sighed and walked a few yards in towards the crazed man, who responded by raising his fists in an almost comical fighting stance. 'Back outsider! Back wayfarer!' Gideon stopped dead in his tracks and rolled his eyes carelessly, as Jeremiah continued his badgering to him. 'Gideon!' 'What is it old man?! snapped The Shade as he turned his head to face Jeremiah, who simply summoned him with his index finger to come closer, which Gideon hesitantly did. 'What?!' 'We need to leave here, now' 'Why?' 'Either we leave here now or you kill these people' 'You know I'll never kill for you' 'Okay, but before you put that moral into further action, allow me to draw your attention to the following.' Jeremiah then took Gideon's arm and led him away and then pointed out to a small figure hunched behind where the mobile homes sat , his head down and making various grumbles as he gnawed away on something. Under the moonlight Gideon was barely able to make it out until Jeremiah confirmed what he unwontedly suspected it was. 'It's a human arm!!' Gideon simply stood frozen for a few seconds, watching the vile cannibal tear apart the human arm with his bare teeth. The sight of crimson wet blood around his mouth appeared dark and caliginous under the moon light. 'Oh you sure know how to pick them Gideon! Cannibal eating Carnies!' Gideon ran his hand through his long black hair before walking back to where he originally stood and faced Joel, only this time there were a few more unwanted faces greeting him. A podgy old woman wearing a tattered yellow dress, her legs covered in bruises and her face smothered with cheap sloppy make up and greasy long blonde hair. Two young boys stood there two, not much younger than Gideon. They two like their supposed parents Joel and his wife were large and obese as they stood there in identical blue shirts and trousers. Then there stood Joel, smiling menacingly as he wielded his buck knife against the throat of the young girl. 'Okays now folks, yews here interrupted ours dinner tonight' Joel snickered at the young girl as he spoke the last few words and licked her neck sickeningly, slobbering all over her as if she really was a piece of meat. 'Well as a Chinese proverb once said, Heaven will give you a life and earth will give you a grave. Now let her go Joel' Gideon spoke with his usual came psychological charm. 'Let's play them at The Dunk Tank Joel.....' Joel turned to his wife and grinned slightly as whatever psychotic thought ran through his head before chuckling madly. 'Good idea Martha, yew go and a get it ready with tha boys, and take the 12 gauge!' Martha nodded while clapping loudly as the two boys and her equipped themselves with the 12 gauge from inside before walking off out of sight. 'Now, what are you gonna be putting on the line in a game like this?' Gideon had no clue whatsoever of what was going on, or the potential of the repercussions that could ensue if he was to adopt his usual methods of poetic justice. No, right now he was playing on the carnies turf and playing by their rules, whatever those rules might be. He just had to play along, and if that failed.....well, he hadn't thought that far ahead yet. 'You can have all three of us' announced Gideon positively, gesturing with his head to Jeremiah and Roland. He could hear Jeremiah breath a deep sigh of shock, but he thankfully held it in, probably assuming Gideon had a plan, he strongly wished he had. Joel stood there for a few moments pondering the offer, before sternly nodding. 'Okay, we could use three of yews for a good feast. Now come on, let's go!' Joel then dragged the young girl with him while gesturing to Gideon and the rest to follow him, where their fates would be decided upon not a winner takes all card game, not a game of Russian roulette, but.....The Dunk Tank! 'If I die here I'll tear your heart out in hell' snapped Jeremiah as Joel and his two sons loaded Martha atop of the small seating above The Dunk Tank. She was excited and giggling, the thought of being the main attraction again like she probably was in her younger, perhaps more attractive days were being relieved. 'If that happens and we die here, I'll make sure you hit the burning hot floor of hell hard before I do old man' Jeremiah ensued snapping and croaking his insults and empty threats, yet Gideon easily ignored them. He was focusing on the young girl who's life was at stake as she stood tied to a nearby pole sobbing. Reality hits hard when we least expect it. It hit Gideon right now, this girls life was at stake in a game of The Dunk Tank. 'What a God awful way to lose your life' he muttered under his breath. 'All because of a lost game of The Dunk Tank'. He sighed and looked up at Joel who was now ready and satisfied. 'I have a pistol' whispered Jeremiah. Gideon couldn't help but laugh hysterically. 'So that's why you've been walking with that limp' 'Silence you fool! One needs to be armed in this environment!' 'Look, you have your staff right?' Jeremiah tilted the head of his long staff, which was a term he preferred to call it other than a rather tall walking stick seeing as it was 2 feet taller than him, and pulled it at the top revealing the shining silver of the tip of a long blade. Gideon simply nodded. 'Okays folks, we's about ready for ya, I'm a sue you understand the rules?' 'I do' 'Okay, step right up sir and test your skills on The Dunk Tank' yelled Joel as he led Gideon a few yards in front of him before placing a ball in his hand. 'You just let fly of that sucker on the target, and if ya hit it and Martha falls in, you'll a win. But if ya miss...' 'I know what will happen' spoke Gideon softly as he looked at the large circular lid of The Dunk Tank resting next to it. Joel then came closer to him and whispered in his ear. 'You think she's a virgin?' Gideon looked sharply at Joel with an intense look before Joel replied. 'Their flesh tastes even sweeter if they are' he giggled before walking away. Who would have though carnies could adopt the skill of trying to psyche you out? 'Ready when you are chief!' yelled Joel one last time. As he did Jeremiah cut in and began whispering to Gideon. 'Your count or mine?' 'Mine' replied Gideon. 'Very well...' '5....' Jeremiah stood up straight and took a deep breath. '4....' He slowly began to unsheathe the tip of the blade from his staff. '3....' 'Hope for both our sakes this works...' '2.....' 'Come on folks, take your shot' yelled Joel. 'Hurrrrrrrrrrry.....' '1...................' The tension seemed to be suspended above them heavily for the brief few milliseconds that ensued before Gideon gave the green light for the sketchy and rough plan of evacuation and termination to begin. 'NOW!' Jeremiah unsheathed the long sterling majestic sword from his staff as Gideon tossed the small ball up into the air as high as he could. As he did Jeremiah tossed the sword to him which he caught quickly and briefly took aim at a confused and frightened Joel. Gideon winked at him sadistically before throwing the sword powerfully towards him as it spun through the cold air before eventually the sound of it lodging itself into Joel's main organs was all that could be heard before he fell to his knees, blood spurting out of his mouth almost instantly. 'JOEEEEEEL' yelled a horrified Martha. Gideon looked up just in time to see the ball coming down and caught it in the palm of his hand. He then quickly took aim and let fly of the ball sending it soaring through the air much like the sword as it too hit it's destined target. Poor old Martha barely had time to react as the small platform gave way underneath her quickly and violently sending her crashing mightily into the pool beneath her. 'Get the girl!' yelled Gideon. 'No, she's worthless and...' 'DO IT!' The eventual urgent tone in Gideon's voice was enough to get Jeremiah to waddle over to the girl and begin untying her as the two shocked sons of both Martha and Joel just seemingly looked at the body of their father. Then they looked up at Gideon......... He was already wielding the sword from their fathers fallen body, and gestured for them impatiently to step forward. Yet what happened next surprised even him as he watched on in horror as the two looked at each other before running over to their fathers fallen body and began gnawing at his deceased flesh. 'Sweet Jesus....' 'GIDEON!' came Jeremiah's voice. Gideon turned to see he had managed to untie the girl and was leading her over to a battered and unusual looking stretch limo amongst an assortment of other strange motor vehicles. Within minutes of some of the other angry cannibal eating carnies coming outside Gideon had hot wired the car and began reversing out of the car park as quick as the tires would take him, knocking over various trash cans as he did so. 'What in the hell was all that about?!' yelled Gideon to the girl resting in the back with Jeremiah. 'You tell me' she replied. 'Well, who are you?' he asked. 'Get me out of here then I'll tell you' It sounded like a good deal to him. Sooner than later all three of them were speeding off into the dark night, leaving whatever perils and evil they had just encountered behind them to rot in their own hell. “Another day at the office” Gideon muttered quitely to himself while smiling ever so slightly while looking in the windscreen at the young girl.
