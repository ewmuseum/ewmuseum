PWC TV Commercial 7

7 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Originally aired on WGTW TV-48 in Philadelphia, PA at 11:15pm EST Tuesday, January 7th. Darkness becomes light, the sudden change in luminosity momentarily blurs your vision. Music is followed by the voice that comes from a lone figure standing in the center of a wrestling ring, a bright spotlight masking his identity... -If there's one thing in this world that I must do -It will be, prove you wrong -Make you take back the lies -And bring the truth to the surface -Clean my slate and erase -All the black you've tried to -Tarnish my name with -This is conviction -What I have inside you'll never possess -And because you never understood how I lived -You try to disrespect -I paid my f##king dues -And everything I have in this life -I've made from nothing -I've done this all on my own -That's more than you can say for yourself -You disrespect the way I live -You take apart what I say -You try to judge me -Now I'm judging you -This time I'm judging you -The harder you try and break me -The harder I push on -If there's one thing in this world that I must do -It will be, prove you wrong -You never believe in this -And you never would admit your fault -You've always had everything handed to you -But your luck has finally run out -I knew from the beginning -You had no heart of soul -I've always seen through your lies -And I'll always hate you for what you are -You disrespect the way I live -You take apart what I say -You try to judge me Now I'm judging you "Remain Nameless" by Hatebreed A song can be interpreted in so many ways. Who am I? I ask myself that everyday, but why's it matter. I'm my own person, from the shadows I step into the spotlight once more, but is it truly the spotlight? I've done things in this business and beaten would be legends. I made me, no matter what so many others would like to think. The plague is gone and I will never again allow myself to play second fiddle to an over rated, talentless, manipulator, or anyone else for that matter. The only thing I can do is use God given talent and the skills I've honed and sharpened over the past seven years and bring a purity back to an almost forgotten art for known as wrestling. What was done in the past will remain there, dead and buried just like careers will be once I reclaim my former glory and rise above it. What's yet to come in the future, has yet to happen and lies in the hands of fate. Who am I? Why's it matter? Static...
