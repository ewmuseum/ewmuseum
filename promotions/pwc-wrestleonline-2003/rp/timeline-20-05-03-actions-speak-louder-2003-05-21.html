Timeline - 20/05/03 : Actions Speak Louder

21 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 21/05/03 As Karina Wolfenden spins around, she has no time to duck the pale blue chair that's swinging down onto her forehead. The force her colossal Roman opponent exerts through the weapon making it bounce back off her perspiration glazed cranium, be it a bounce that didn't soften the blow. With control over her own body relinquished, Karina feels her legs buckle under her. The image of the muscled Roman loses clarity, slips away from her. 'Cheater!' a purist at ringside heckles, feeling aggrieved by Colossus' rule breaking. The jeer that is erupting around Karina is dulled by her clouded mind, her senses dampened and bearings lost from the chair shot. Her eyelids quiver as they remain close, protecting the emerald orbs that make up the K-Wolf's eyes from the harsh lights above. Each step Colossus takes around the ring shakes the mat, which in turn resonates through Karina's whole body as she reaches up to her forehead. Karina gently probes where the chair hit with her right hand, while her ears are subjected to the voice of a jubilant Romulus, rejoicing as he sees from ringside what Karina is now feeling with the fingertips that emerge from her black fighting gloves. Her blood. 'Ah, jeez,' she lethargically bemoans, for a second worrying more about the cosmetic than the physical. Through her still blurry vision, she sees the crimson that's embedded itself into the grooves of her digits, as if she's about to give her fingerprints. Despite her vulnerable position, down on the canvas, Colossus isn't concentrating on Karina... Seeing blood he's already toasting his victory. Karina's head drops to the side, where she sees she isn't the only person laying prone in the ring... The referee still isn't up. Filtering through the rabid boos, the sound of the celebrating Romans finds it's way into the K-Wolf's ears, which now hear crisp and clear. 'Time to ruin the party,' she thinks, stealthily getting up behind the giant Roman. Smaller, weaker, and still a little dizzy from the chair shot, Karina isn't in a position to physically fight back... But where Colossus has the brawn, the brains are in the custody of Wolfenden. 'Ehem?' Karina coughs, reminding her opponent that she was still there. Colossus turns, not sensing any threat from the Boarder... Although now he was starting to wonder why the bell hadn't rung. With a devious smile on her face, Karina uses the shiny leather surface of her right glove to extract the blood from around her cut. With none of life's liquid soaking into the waterproof surface, it's then wiped across the furrowing brow of the colossal Roman. 'Didn't you know?' questions the smiling K-Wolf as her eyebrows shot up, 'I'm a blood donor.' Not getting the joke, Colossus looks to Romulus, confused, the Roman Emperor frantically pointing to where his forehead was smeared in Wolfenden's blood. Realising her plot, Colossus snarls and clenches his giant fists, turning to confront her... But the K-Wolf was gone. The referee, however, is in her place. The crowd rise as one in their cheering as the bell rings. 'The winner of this match..... Karina Wolfenden!' announces Bob Kirby, with gusto. Wearing the grin of a woman who just won a match by mind rather than muscle, Karina slides back into the ring she'd only just deserted... All traces off blood now wiped from her forehead and the pink flash of her fringe concealing the cut. With the protesting Romans being ushered away, Karina looks out into the 300 strong crowd and salutes them, thanking them for their support... Their support then takes on a different form as their heads turn towards the short aisle. Standing there, his eyes locked onto her tired body through the mask's eye holes... Penance... Along with Solomon. The words spouting from the mouth of the Prophet were lost in a murmur, Wolfenden only concentrating on returning Penance's stare. Physically she was no match for Penance, but her gaze was just as potent as the Dark Atoner's. Solomon continues to rant, but his words are mixed in with the boos of the crowd, formulating nothing the K-Wolf can take in. Karina is lost in her own world, a place where only she and the Masked Monster inhabit... There's no fans, no Prophet, no ring. Then the time comes for the stare down to end, and Karina extends her right hand, whipping the crowd up with the motion. 'KA-RI-NA!' they repeat, anxious to hear her words towards the detested duo standing in the aisle. But as the mic slaps into her palm, her retort to the Prophet's words isn't vocal.... ...Karina gives them the finger. 4 days away from the biggest match of her career, the K-Wolf's lone digit said more than any press conference could.
