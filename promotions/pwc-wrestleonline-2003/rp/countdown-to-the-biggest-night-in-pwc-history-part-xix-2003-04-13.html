Countdown to the Biggest Night In PWC History (Part XIX)

13 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 13, 2003 Well, here I was..... I was right there, at Capitol Punishment, ready for action before a live Pay-Per-View audience.. ...In the unlikely event that someone got injured. I guess I couldn't blame Mr. Starr for not putting me onto the show; I had spent the last month or so against Ariath, and all the real options were exhausted, we'd had a match where he lost by DQ, so we could have had a no-disqualification match..But we'd had the relative equivalent before then. We could've fought in a tag match, but I'd beaten him there too before, and besides, goodness knows they'd have rather seen Sam fight that soulless monster alone in the hope I shared in, that she could stop her misbehaviour before she got out of hand... There was nowhere to go from here for me that the fans hadn't already gotten a look of on free TV, so there was no reason for me to wrestle for the fans who'd seen all I had to offer against him now. It was simple as that..I had to at least come around though, in case Ariath decided to get involved in the match, maybe I'd get some time on the show then... ...Maybe wasn't much to hope for. I was on the outside, looking in through the magic of television at the first pay-per-view I was ever able to attend as a wrestler, the start of a new, stellar promotion, unable to add anything much of my own to it. What could I do, but just sit back and hope for the best, that things wouldn't stay like this? Hopefully, Paula would have to leave, just quit bringing her involvement into the federation, and even if not, it meant she would be able to claim the personal victory it had all stemmed from...It would be over, one way or another, and even if it didn't work out, I could move on my way... ...Alone again. Wouldn't it just figure to be left alone, without a friend once more so soon upon my arrival? I told her before, how it was taking a risk to do this sort of thing, but she was determined here. We were trained wrestlers, we had been through enough to know how to fight our battles, and Paula..Paula was just a mere minor, who had never even received her training to fight... I guess it's what Sam wants to prove. If she couldn't beat someone whose wrestling skill amounted to catfights and cheating in front of this sort of large, she wasn't going to make it in Ron's eyes as anything great; Anything more than...the mere thought bothered me.....A shapely body to fight only in the lowest type of match, with some other 'not-cut-out-for-the-big-time' girl in some sort of muddy substance. She'd never get a real shot at anyone afterward; She might as well go away... Leave me alone to fend for myself. Not like it really would be a change; My life had been one clique's disassociation after another. Kids, teenagers, even wrestlers...They all seemed to have that Survivor mentality, that life's always easier if you've got enough friends. I'd missed the boat every time, I knew not this easy life they spoke of..There was nothing left to lose from an emotional perspective, so I just let it slide when things went wrong... Maybe this would be another of those times. Maybe I'd be alone. But if I was alone, I'd at least be in a familiar setting; I'd began here this way, hadn't I? It did feel awfully bad though...Just about every other wrestler here was having a match, except a few guys.... ...Axtell, but he'd probably fight whether it was safe or not and get some PPV airtime, no matter what Starr did to stop him... ...Penance, but he'd fight whether it was safe or not for everyone else, lord knows Starr wouldn't waste the opportunity to give the people some brutality... ...That left who? The Roman Empire, Hoyt Williams and Jake Keeton. Not exactly the best set of people to be around by any stretch: a pair of perverse anachronisms, a man whose morals are perfect only to himself and an arrogant former champion in the big leagues...All three of them preachy in one way or another, not the sort of people I could get along with here by any stretch. No fun to be had tonight, not even a match to wrestle..I could handle it, I'd at least get to see the show without paying, I could just watch and try to forget that the next big break chance out there, the show everyone else in the industry'd be looking at, was going on not any number of miles away by satellite, but a matter of mere hundreds of meters away...Where new stars were going to be made, past champs would be fighting against relative unknowns, people's careers made and broken by their performances.. Performances which I wouldn't even have the chance to put on, because here in the Sovereign Bank Arena, my world was right outside my door, the cheering crowd audible without even having to have my set on, the matches' outcomes being greeted with more reaction than I ever heard outside of the rare try-outs I got, and all this happening around, just like all my life before it.. ...Without me.
