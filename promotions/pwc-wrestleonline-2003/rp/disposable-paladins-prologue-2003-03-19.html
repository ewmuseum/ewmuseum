Disposable Paladins - Prologue

19 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 19/03/03 In the face of adversity, our true colours will always shine and our inner strength will surface to it's peak. Well, that's what we've been fed by books , religion and movies since we can all remember. Yet a certain select few people, all linked by some bizarre reason are all about to be tested by adversity and evil at their most potent. Some are friends, some are lovers, some are unwanted companions and some are bitter enemies. Yet when it comes time for all their inner strength and trust to fully shine, they are all going to have to work and pull through together. Isn't life just fucking grand? The following, are the characters who's lives we are going to play Big Brother over and monitor and follow over the course over the next few days, and who's lives will change dramatically when it all is said and done, whether it be for better or worse is still undecided. Gideon Daray Poe- Professional wrestler/martial artist. In his mid twenties and now resides in New Jersey as a fully fledged member of the PWC roster. Travelled across the country and Japan and China developing his style of martial arts and wrestling style. Also a talented guitarist, but a man who's feelings are often guarded from others. Jeremiah O'Barr - 83 years old. Poisoned Gideon as he was set to leave Japan and return to America. He uses Gideon as his forced companion and provides him with a small antidote every once in a while to help keep him alive. He has sworn to give Gideon the full antidote once he feels satisfied Gideon has done everything he wants from him. Tanya Washburn - The current and first in a damn long time lover of Gideon Poe. Gideon rescued Tanya from a set of cannibal eating circus workers. Romantic huh? Although her and Gideon's relationship is currently moving slower than a 33k modem connection, she is determined to get him to open up to her and the world and force Jeremiah into giving Gideon the antidote immediately due to a theory that this will help Gideon finally live a normal life with her. Martin Bauerman - Long time cop in Maine, now transferred to New Jersey. Bauerman has been hell-bent on catching Gideon after discovering he was behind a set of serial killings of various paedophiles, rapists and serial killers that had eluded the police for several months. Now that Gideon himself is in New Jersey, Bauerman is convinced that this is his last chance of catching Poe after failing on his last few attempts when he and Gideon met face to face. Melvin Peterson - Partner of Martin Bauerman. Young fresh recruit who would rather crawl over hot coals than so much as bend a rule in the department. Often scared and nervous on the job, he looks for Bauerman for inspiration. Vex Lennon - Long time friend of Gideon and the bassist of his old band Cadaver Theory . He, along with the other members of the band moved to New Jersey together a number of years ago. Now married and incredibly wealthy after his wife's father, and oil contractor, died and left every bit of change to her. Maynard Sanders - Former drummer of Cadaver theory, Maynard now works for Dark Era Records, an underground label that houses a lot of alternative music bands around the New Jersey area. Was one of Gideon's first high school friends and helped form Cadaver Theory with him as the drummer. Trent Scott - Vocalist of Cadaver Theory. Trent now owns his own music store in New Jersey and has done for the last several years. While all three members of Cadaver Theory know of Gideon's relationship with Jeremiah, Trent is often the one who still acts the most violent towards it, and is hell-bent on killing Jeremiah himself when he fully recovers Gideon.
