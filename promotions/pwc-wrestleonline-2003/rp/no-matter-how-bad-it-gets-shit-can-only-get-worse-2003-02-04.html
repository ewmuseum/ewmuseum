No matter how bad it gets, Shit can only get worse.

4 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02-04-03 There I was, living large and loving every second of it in Five Star Wrestling. I was in line to become the first Television Champion. I was wrestling with the likes of Politically Incorrect, Mike Marvelous, Timo Bolamba. Then one day, the whole thing came to an end. The dream abruptly cut itself short. Why, you ask? Why, indeed. Magnum Wolf is found standing outside the Firstar Arena in Cincinnati, OH. He has thus far ignored the fact that there is no 'FIVE STAR WRESTLING' sign anywhere to be seen. He's also somehow ignored the fact that the parking lot is as empty as Pamela Anderson's breasts after the implants were removed. He walks up to the door, and yanks on it. Hard. It doesn't budge. 'Who the hell locks the doors on a night like tonight?! FSW IS HERE, DAMN YOU!' yells Magnum at the door that impedes his entry to the arena. Wolf continues to bang on the glass door, yelling loudly for someone to open it. Presently, a little old man comes hobbling into view from the inside of the arena. Clearly, he's the janitor for the place, and is quite annoyed at being interrupted during his nightly chores. The old guy manages to get to the door, and opens it just wide enough to talk out of it. 'This here Arena's closed tonight. Can't ya tell by this parking lot being as empty as Pamela Anderson's breasts after the implants were removed?!' the old man hollers in the face of Wolf. Magnum's brow furrows, wondering how the old guy knew that phrase... it seemed so familiar... but quickly dismisses it as he growls a response at him. 'CLOSED? How can it POSSIBLY be closed when Magnum Wolf is supposed to wrestle tonight?' 'Well, I don't know what a B.B. Gun Panther is, and I have no idea why he's wrestling or who the hell he's wrestling, but this arena is closed tonight. Somebody cancelled their booking for the place, and now I'm stuck cleaning this whole god damned place!' 'Cancelled the booking, eh? What about my job?' asked Magnum quizzically. 'Job? You want a job? Well, hell, you could help me clean this place!' Magnum just stares at the old man, then points to his gym bag which is PRISTINE, and marked with the FSW symbol. He then points to his clothing which is all very high priced stuff. Indignantly, he turns his nose up to the old man, and turns around to head back towards the car he left in the parking lot. I didn't get it at the time. I didn't understand what was happening. Maybe that's what I get for keeping my head so firmly planted in my ass for so long during my FSW run. Maybe if I had paid attention, I would have had time to find a new job. Well, at least a new job that would pay the bills. All of them. In the same month. Unfortunately, this was the beginning of a new life for me. Not one I necessarily wanted.
