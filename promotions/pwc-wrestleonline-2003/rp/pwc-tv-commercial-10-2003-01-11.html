PWC TV Commercial 10

11 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Originally aired on WGTW TV-48 in Philadelphia, PA at 10:15pm EST Saturday, January 11th. “Hello?” The sound of tapping on the cold glass of a camera lens as it tries desperately to focus on the blurred shape in front of it. “This thing working?” The blur backs away, as the figure attempts to get a grasp of the situation. The voice is female, with a slight tinge of English midlander mingled with the Californian overtones. Slowly the camera begins to focus, the operator fighting furiously with the controls until it finally focuses in the poorly lit room. One ray of light that escapes the drawn curtains illuminates the slim female figure that bounces up and down on the spot, clapping her hands manically. “I am so excited! Look! Look at me! I’m on the TV!” Still she jumps up and down on the spot, flailing her arms around the side of her head. “Yeah, I’m hardly what you expected to turn up next, huh? I’m not exactly your scary she-monster or the big bad beast from the depths of the black lagoon. God! I’m on TV!” She still struggles to gather the ability to string together a cohesive sentence as she stops jumping and moves towards the far wall of the darkened flat. A small wooden-framed mirror is hung and, as she approaches, she begins to struggle furiously with the mental image of ugliness. A shapely hand is thrust hard through her neat, shoulder length auburn hair as she glares into the reflective surface of the mirror. “Damn! My first TV appearance and my hair is a mess! That is so just like me!” Slowly, she turns back to the camera and lets of a dazzling smile, seemingly illuminating the dark room with the sheer white of her perfect teeth. She is quite a specimen of woman, apparently. “So, Who am I? I doubt you have heard of me, only those of you with an ear to the ground like. I haven’t got a clue about this whole big-time business, its hard enough to see the light of day, you know what I mean?” The strange look she received from the camera operator seemed to make something click, and her expression altered quickly. “Oh! No, no, no! Don’t look at me like that, I’m not a vampire!” She giggles and, seemingly to prove something to the people, she crosses the room and opens the curtains, wincing back in the sunlight that glares into her eyes through the small PVC window, still giggling uncontrollably. “Nah, I haven’t been around, don’t try looking for little old me in the record books or anything, I’m just a little English girl trying to make it in the big old wide US of A. I’m not like your big stars or your up-and-coming names like Porky Jack Bunson, I’m just a little girl.” She looks around again, seemingly losing interest with her current pass-time. She crosses the room and slowly begins to pace backwards, stretching out her long legs. She is hardly the build you would expect for a wrestler, tall and slim with little muscular build. She wears knee high black boots and a short skirts, as well as a baggy pink t-shirt adjourned with the words “I R WOMAN” in black across the chest “Your still here? Oh, sorry, I thought we had finished. Well, you want to know more? No? What the hell is it that you do want?” her patience is clearly running thin now; she appears to be totally bored of speaking to nothing. “Well, I am hired by the Pro Wrestling Circuit, you want me to say something macho, hard, making me seem like a tough guy? Screw that, I’ve got a job, and I will be on TV! Woo!” She giggles again and claps wildly as the camera fades.
