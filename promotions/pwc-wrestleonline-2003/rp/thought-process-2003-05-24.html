Thought Process

24 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-24-03 People often say that when you give something time that every problem that comes along will be fixed as everything falls into place like a puzzle. That’s not always true though. Sometimes people have to make some changes in that for themselves. A puzzle really isn’t the greatest word to explain a life, because when you’re putting together a puzzle you always come up with some missing pieces or you come up with that ominous piece that just doesn’t fit anywhere. If you’re going to try and explain how life can be so easy, then you need to pick another way of explaining it, because you’ve already ruined the entire point of your speech. Me? I’ve sat and thought about this a lot, and I’ve analyzed everything that has ever happened to me in the past. The only summation that I can come to is that life is like a game of solitaire. There is a chance you’re going to win; and then again, there is a chance you’re going to lose. You always take that chance of losing though when you decide whether or not to draw the next card or not though. The people in your life can easily be recognized as the cards that are not in use. That one big pile that just lies in front of your face, but you have no knowledge of what’s on the other side. You could without a doubt reach down and draw the card, then again, you could simply pick the people that walk in and are apart of your life. Yet, no matter what we do, we always fail in some way or another. Very often do we get so frustrated with ourselves and the cards that lay before us, that we lose the match in the end and have to start all over again. Then you have the only problem with this situation being? The fact that our lives are not made to be dealt over again and a new game to proceed. We are merely left having to pick the cards back up after only tossing them about in a fit of rage. Now that my friends is life, take it as you will, or don’t. I’ve come to the realization that I will take it for what it is, and that is all there is to it.
