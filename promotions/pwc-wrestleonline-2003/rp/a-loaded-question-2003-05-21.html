A Loaded Question

21 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05-21-03 'Why did you call Ariath off?' Mark Malloy asked me, in what was apparently his most curious, obnoxious tone of voice. I refused to answer. Ariath, in all his stupidity, asked me the same question. He got his answer, but it was not the truth. The honest answer... Well, the thing of it... It just so happens to work out that... No. No, Paula, do not lie to yourself. If to nobody else, at least be honest to yourself. Picture what happened, and remember how you felt. How you really felt. Ariath then drapes Karina's throat on the middle rope and drives his knee into the back of the Boarder's neck, pulling the rope up with his hands. You should have enjoyed that. Somebody was suffering. Somebody was in pain. Is that not what you traveled all the way across the country to see? Is that not what you wanted from the start? But staring at the dumb woman, it was not pleasure that flooded your mind. It was the softness of pity... And something else, something darker. It was that moment of horror, when your father was... taking you... sexua... It suddenly occurs to me that I need to find something to wear for this weekend- NO! Do not turn from this! Stop running, girl, and confront this. When you tried to protest, your father started to strangle you. That is what flashed into your mind when Ariath was using the ropes to choke Miss Wolfenden. And how did that make you feel, Paula? To see a big man choking a smaller, vulnerable woman? How did I feel? In that moment, I hated Ariath more than I could ever hate Karina Wolfenden.
