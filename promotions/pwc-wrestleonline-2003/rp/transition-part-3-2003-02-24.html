Transition Part 3

24 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10th November He opened his eyes slowly and let the light enter his eyes until his surroundings were clear to him. (Damn!!!! Still here, if only I had been dreaming.) The same boring, glum environment was all he could see. The same stupid walls, the same stupid machines…. the same stupid letter. At first he thought he might have dreamt that small bit, but deep down he knew he hadn’t and now the evidence was staring him directly in the face. (What am I to do now? Where can I go?) Just before he had fallen asleep he was at least given a small bit of good news. The hot (Boy was she hot) nurse with the frizzy blonde hair and tight little ass had told him the extent of his injuries. Apparently he had suffered two cracked ribs, a small fracture of the fibula and some heavy bruising to his upper torso. Like I said it was only a “small” bit of good news. He still hurt like hell, but it meant that he could continue to do what he loved and that was to wrestle. Where though? He loved the spotlight that came with wrestling in a big time fed, the fans chanting his name, the fans wearing his t-shirts and waving his merchandise in the air as he strutted down the aisle. Images flooded back into his mind. He saw himself lifting a PIW belt high in the air, he saw the wonderful smile which flowed vibrantly from cheek to cheek. (Those were the good times…. now what?) Before he read that letter, that dreaded letter he always thought he would continue on in the limelight after his injury. He had always dreamed of showing up in the fWo and fighting alongside Kid Cool, or squaring off against the likes of KVC and the Boston Strangler. Now that was ruined, it looked like he was confined to the indy leagues. The dismal abyss, where he would no longer be cheered, he would no longer be noticed. He would cease to exist. He felt himself being overcome by sleep as his eyes fluttered and he passed into the other world. (Many a dream later) His eyes opened slowly. Someone was in his room. (Who are u? What do you want? Damn it I can’t see properly, it’s all blurry…Who are u?) He blinked repeatedly as he tried to clear his vision, but instead a liquid filled his eyes making it even harder to see. He could make out a flowing red flame like hair on the person stood before him. (NO…. it cant be!!) The person was dressed in black from head to toe, that much he could make out. The man leaned in closer pressing his face up against him; he could smell his breath heavily against him. “Life’s a bitch” the person muttered in a nasty voice “Life’s a bitch” It all happened so quickly; suddenly something was pushed up against his face, covering his entire head. He fought, but in his weakened state there was no way he stood a chance. The pillow filled his mouth and blocked off his nostrils. There was laughter. Then there was nothing.
