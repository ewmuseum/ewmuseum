Orangutans are Skeptical of Changes in Their Cages

18 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 18th May 2003 We walk right into Miyagawa’s usual nightly obsession these days. He seems to think that the phrase “if you can’t beat them, join them” applies to comic book artists. The shame of this is that drawing is something that Alex really cannot do well. He cannot do well at all. You can easily tell this by the fact that the noise underfoot is the crunching of screwed up balls of paper thrown over-arm across the room prior to entry. We approach Miya from behind as he stands in front of a story board… a story board which seems to involve hiring thugs to kidnap Alex Ross to force him into drawing a Miyagawa Comic strip for Stan Lee. Then kidnapping Kevin Smith and forcing him into making a Jersey Movie based around the adventures of a certain Japo-American. In other words, a slightly better plot than the Ultimate Warrior hitting on Santa with fun and games with S and M. Another screwed up ball of paper thrown behind and the artist formerly known as Alexander Miyagawa reaches for his phone. He bundles in the number quickly and hits the dial button. Placing it to his ear he waits tentatively for a pick up. “Hello, I’d like to speak to Mister Lee please. Yes, I’ll wait. It’s Alexander Miyagawa.” He pulls the phone away from his head and looks at it annoyed. “Well I guess I was expecting that.” He puts the phone back on it’s stand and starts to put away his pens and pencils. Suddenly he stops and ponders for a moment. “That’s it… I need to become a real life super-hero. Go out, save people from muggers and so forth.” He ponders a little more and then comes to a realization. “Second thought, I’ll skip that. It’ll be just my luck to trip over the cape and get shot on the first attempt. I need a drink.” He reaches towards a small square fridge he has in the room and opens it with a gentle tug after unlocking it’s holding mechanism. He pulls the bottle of vodka out of the side compartment and unscrews it, taking a swig, only to find the bottle empty. “Damn it… what is this, piss off Miya day or something?” He slings the empty bottle back into the fridge, mainly due to a lack of a better place to put it. He wanders over to a nearby table and picks up a copy of today’s daily paper before slouching in an armchair opposite the television before beginning to flick through the pages. He gets to an advert and stops. ‘Speed Dating’ it reads. “That’ll work.” Says Alex out aloud. “My name is Alexander Miyagawa and I am a professional wrestler. I saw Sex and the City, that line will so totally get me phone numbers. And that is the sort of thing I need to get on with my life. I’m doing this. I’m breaking out of the cage that Natalia has shoved me into.” He nods at himself triumphantly before sitting the paper down and retrieving the phone from the other side of the room. He dials the number on the advert carefully. “Hi, I’d like to register for the speed dating please.” He says into the phone carefully. “No, I’m not from New York… originally from California. Alexander Miyagawa. Professional Wrestler.” He pauses for a second. “No seriously, Professional Wrestler. Divorced.” His eyes squint for some reason, something that is explained by the next line. “No, no prior convictions. Ok good, so you’ll send the details over of the next meet? Good.” He pulls the phone away and turns it off, satisfied with the result; and places the phone back on it’s stand. Miyagawa sits back down on the armchair and continues to read the paper again.
