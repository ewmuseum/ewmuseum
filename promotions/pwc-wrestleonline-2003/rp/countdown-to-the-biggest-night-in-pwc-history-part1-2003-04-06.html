Countdown to the Biggest Night in PWC History (Part1)

6 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-6-2003 ”STAND YOUR ASS UP RYO!!” 8 days until I make my FIRST pay-per-view appearance ever. 8 days until I prove to the world that I’m the true star of the show. 8 days until I get my chance to FINALLY attain a title to call my very own….and what am I doing just 8 days before the pinnacle of my career… Getting my ass beat to a pulp by my monstrous mentor, Gunner. I often crack on Gun-Gun, calling him “old man” and citing the fact that when he “cums” dusts shoots out….but no jokes came from my mouth as he beat the shit out of me with forearm shot after forearm shot as I laid on my hands and knees, a beaten mess. The pain was unlike anything I ever felt before….and trust me, I’ve felt plenty of it for my age. I’ve been put through flaming tables, piledriven on thumbtacks, even thrown off of balconies….but what made this pain that much more unbearable…. Was that it was coming from someone I loved. ”STAND THE FUCK UP RYO!! WHAT KIND OF MAN ARE YOU?!” Gunner’s face was filled with beastial rage as he punctuated his sentence with a boot to my face, my neck jerking back as my body fell to the dingy canvas of the ring set up in the backyard of Gunner’s home with a sick thud. I remember being trained in this ring by the very same man merely two years ago….and the VERY first thing he told the sixteen year-old youth that would soon become his tag team partner, was a simple and effective quote. ”You have to learn how to take pain, before you can ever hope to give it.” That quote raced through my head the entire time that Gunner beat the piss out of me, my body never synching with my mind as one always wanted to quit…yet the other would never let it. I pushed myself up from the mat with my hands, my sweat being dried by the cool breeze that came with our outdoor training. I hoped to at least get to all fours so I could regain whatever energy I could… I should have known better…. Gunner mounted my back like a mule, gripping my emerald green braids tightly as he looked into my eyes. My lifeless, incubus ink eyes stared right into his furious brown pupils, my body to weakened to be intimidated by his glare. The pain shortly followed as I expected as he struck the sides of my face in rapid succession with closed fists, the shots sounding off like guns as he ended every word he exclaimed at me with a crushing blow. ”YOU…ARE….NOTHING!!! QUIT…NOW…WHILE…YOUR…BONES…ARE…INTACT!!!” His words were prudent and made absolute sense to me…but it could NOT be done, no matter how much my body ached. If I laid on the mat and stayed down, I’d lose Gunner’s faith and respect in me…he only beat me like this to make me stronger, no matter how twisted and sick that may sound. I felt the slight trickle of blood leaving my bottom lip, making crimson droplets on the already filthy canvas. That’s when I felt the full force of Gunner’s palm against the back of my head as my face SLAMMED to the mat. My head rattled violently as my eyes would battle between forcing themselves open and slamming shut. I only had to choices here as my palms pressed against the mat… Stay down, or tell Gunner “fuck you” and get back up… Fuck you Gunner. I’m too cocky to be kept down. Too bright to not shine. Hell, there’s no ceiling, glass or otherwise, that can keep me grounded. So the only way I could prove that to the very same man who was handing me my ass on a lunch tray….was to get back up to my feet. I pressed my palms to the mat and force my broken body up, supplementing them with my spaghetti knees as I was now on all fours like an old dog. And just like a dog, Gunner did not hesitate to reel back and kick the living shit out of my ribs when the opportunity presented itself. Don’t get me wrong, I love Gunner like a father….although I’d never tell him that. The man is wise and a hell of a mentor…but you don’t acquire the nickname “Beast” unless your able to become a monster between the ropes. Anyways, back to my pain here… ”STAY THE FUCK DOWN RYO!!” He must have been out to emulate what he painted on his face…..a red devil. Damn it if he wasn’t doing a good job. Those kicks burned, Gunner’s shins felt like raw iron as they smashed against my ribs time after time…but I would not go down. I took whatever energy I could muster and finally made it to one knee, my eyes meeting his defiantly as if to say, ”Hit me again.” I stood there on that knee, bracing myself for whatever he was ready to hit me with….what he would hit me with though was definitely a surprise. An open hand. An open hand extended in aid. I grasped the hand of my mentor as he pulled me to my feet, I stumbled back as he grasped me with his strong hands. A small grin came across his usually hardened face as he looked to my beaten body…..impressed I guess. ”Hehe….that was fun? Let’s do that again sometime.” He punctuated the sentence with a pat on my sore back, my face wincing as he exited the ring. For all the excruciating pain Gunner put my body through….it was all worth it. I am strength, speed, youth, looks, and charisma personified. I am the man destined to steal the show come Sunday night….I’m the cocky son of a bitch everyone else will hate, but all the fans will congratulate. I am Neon. And no ass-whipping in the world will EVER let me forget that.
