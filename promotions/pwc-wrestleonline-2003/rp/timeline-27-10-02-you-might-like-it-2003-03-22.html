Timeline - 27/10/02 : You Might Like It

22 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 22/03/03 ‘Try it.’. The words that have sparked off many a downward spiral for many a person. Words uttered by friends, wanting you to join in with them, as if it validates their choice. ‘You might like it.’ usually follows, planting the seed of curiosity that will only grow with the help of fertiliser... Time. ‘If you don’t, you never have to do it again.’ is the sealer, why not give it a go, it’s only once. ‘Try it, you might like it. If you don’t, you never have to do it again.’ they’ll tell you. Words that could start the 20 a day smoking habit that will lose you a lung in your 40’s. Words that could lead to your first drink, before you’re getting through a bottle of Scotch a day as if it’s water. Words that could begin your descent down the drugs chain and leave you robbing old ladies to score. Words that could talk you into that first sexual experience and a teenage pregnancy. ‘Try it...’ Karina’s friend told her, ‘You might like it.’. At first Karina shrugged it off, it wasn’t her scene. For a girl already doing the opposite of what was expected from her, she wasn’t going to break character anymore. ‘If you don’t, you never have to do it again.’ swayed her, after all, what was there to lose? Reluctantly, she agrees. Although, showing a rare bout of nerves, she doesn’t want him there. If she’s going to try this, like they asked, she wants it to be a moment for just her. Karina Wolfenden alone will decide, although before she even begins, she’s working out how to say it wasn’t for her. ‘Try it, you might like it. If you don’t, you never have to do it again.’ is the phrase that leads people to many places, in the case of a young woman from Albany, New York who’s personal ad would begin something like ‘Energetic rich kid who rides...’, it has led her the stand in the middle of an old wrestling ring in an even older gym. If she’d have asked her father, he’d of probably built her a gym to use, or signed her up to an exclusive health club to use theirs. But, that’s not her style, she lacks the social graces of the generation that succeeded her, she’s out of that circle. ‘Why am I even here?’ Karina questions herself, words barely being heard anywhere outside her thoughts as she looks down at the patched up canvas. ‘What the hell, might as well have some fun while I’m here.’ she continues with the optimism we more associate her with. Her bright green eyes scan the structure, while many would just she ropes and floor, Karina sees angles, levels, elevation and jumps. Through the eyes of the 20-something, the ring becomes a playground through the eyes of a child. Bending her knees and pushing up and down, she gets a feel of the surface, which she decides will take some getting used to. The feeling of the boards below mixed in with a certain amount of give make it dynamic to her own movement... An odd feeling that will soon become as natural as the snow under her feet. ‘Still prefer the powder.’ she mutters to herself, missing her chosen environment already. Walking over to the ropes with a spring in her step, starting to get a feel for the ring, she grasps the top stand in her gloved hands and drops back. Hopping up onto the bottom rope, she bounces lightly on it while letting the top take the weight of her 160 pounds leaning back. Then her feet leap up and land on the middle rope, and transferring her weight from her arms to her legs, she springs off the middle rope with back flip, for the first time hearing the sound of herself landing on the canvas. A smile crossing her pink lips, Karina is like kid in a sweet shop. All around her she now sees things she wants to try. Skipping to the ropes and ducking out under the middle stand, still shaking from her somersault, she now looks to the corner. Having never seen a wrestling ring first hand, and hardly ever on TV either, Karina had never noticed how the ring forms a ladder between the post and the turnbuckles. Without a second thought, she heads up to the top, her natural balance holding her steady on the top with no fear of the drop. Crouched, gloved hands holding the ropes beside the buckle, Karina looks down to the worn canvas. It’s not even 5 feet down, there shouldn’t be any reason why she isn’t already flying through the musky air and onto the mat, no reason why dust isn’t flying into the air as her feet crash onto the canvas. The kind of adrenaline rush that has started to leave her on the slopes has returned. The shiver she used to feel up her spine when she opened up the throttle on her bike is back in full force. The buzz is there. Sucking in a sharp, deep breath Karina thrusts her body upwards, leaving the top rope shaking as she soars up into the air, whipping and contorting her body around like a cat with that natural instinct for landing on it’s feet. She doesn’t see white snow below her, but it’s all the same... High elevation and a solid landing. Twisting in the air, Karina almost seems supported by some unseen force, like watching a synchronised swimmer held in position by the surrounding water’s buoyancy. Gravity puts itself out for Karina, deciding to come back into force when she’s finished. A perfect landing. It’s almost as if the rats that the gym surely plays host to should hold up little 10.0 signs. She’s tried it, and even she’ll admit she likes it. Only having used one rope and one turnbuckle, the options appear endless to her, so much more she can try, so many more she can utilise. ‘I could get used to this.’ comes the pleasure finally being expressed. The first steps on a long road have been taken, and she hasn’t even reached the foot of the hill that lays before her yet. If Karina Wolfenden didn’t like it, she’d never have to do it again... 5 months later and she’s doing everything she can not to have to stop. ‘Try it, you might like it. If you don’t, you never have to do it again.’ started Karina Wolfenden in wrestling, and now Magnum Wolf, anyone else in PWC, or even her own father will stop her.
