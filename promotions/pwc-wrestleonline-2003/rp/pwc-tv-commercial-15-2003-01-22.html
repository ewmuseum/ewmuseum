PWC TV Commercial 15

22 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Originally aired on WGTW TV-48 in Philadelphia, PA at 11:39pm EST Sunday, January 22nd. What makes the perfect person? Black spiked hair. Perfect build. Chiseled body. 5’11, 195-pound frame. Know who it is? He’s not a monster in the typical sense, nor is he your average brawling type, but he will make sure that he gets the job done of making you bleed. Hell, while he’s at it, he’ll put on a show like never seen before, but not because he likes you. Because he’s skilled at putting on a show. Think you know who it is now? Got an idea of who he might try to align with? Well, he’s got all the money he needs and power’s not an issue for someone with ties to famous folks all across the globe. What might one with access to money and power want? Would someone join a company just to inflict pain and suffering upon those who are inferior to him? You damn straight he would. The camera fades in and shows a dim profile of the man, but there’s enough light to identify his facial features. His unblemished dark earthy colored skin, jet black hair, and the sneer that often resides on his face can identify him to some, but not to all. “Hmmph…look at this little shack. Stupid owners ask ME-me, a man deserving of abundant hype- to do a little bit so that the fans can check out exactly who they’re going to see in a PWC ring and they give me….this?!” With a look back at his surroundings, which are partly covered by the same darkness that he presides in at the moment, and spits ungratefully down towards the dingy floor. “So…check me out. Look at my every graceful move. No man of this caliber has ever set foot near the grimy rings that you’ve accustomed yourself to. My past is not one of sorrow, or of the poverty that some of the less worthy roster members have gone through, but it is an illustrious one.” This callous individual runs a hand through his upturned raven hair, then once again begins to spin the rest of his tale. “It was a rather perfect life for an even more perfect individual. The modeling industry was at its pinnacle and you sheep were buying our goods almost faster than our beautiful sweat shops could produce.” Chuckling at his own humor, the former model continues, “But no one could deal with the fact that I was the top! And thus, now I find myself at the bottom, amongst you destitute and penniless rejects.” Sighing as he thinks of the current situation, this cocky and unfeeling man steps forward into the small amount of light, and his entire face becomes illuminated. “Know this face, know this man, because even in this pathetic excuse for an industry, I will succeed, and you will still envy me. February 1st…we’ll meet again.” With that, he turns back into the dark back sector of the room and the clip-clop of human feet hitting cement is the last sound that echoes as the camera fades
