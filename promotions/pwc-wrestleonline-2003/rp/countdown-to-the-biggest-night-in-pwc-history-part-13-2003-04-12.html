Date: 4/12/03 Otherwise known as: Early Assessments and Advice The scene opens to show Clark Carlisle’s radiant face within about 3 feet of a wide-angle camera lens. Only his boyish face and broad shoulders are discernible, yet there’s something about the huge smile on Clark’s face that averts the attention from these essentials. He begins to speak directly into the camera using his recognizably supercilious accent that doesn’t faze the camera’s imperturbable lens. “Hello, previously loved- ones! It’s me Clark! I know that receiving partial notification from me excites you but relax, because I have nothing else to give to you. You probably figured that I’m letting you back onto one of my estates, or re-associating myself with you all. Hahaha, you might’ve even thought that I was SORRY!” Clark’s breaks off his parley to his family and friends so that he might commence laughing. “Hell, I’m not exactly sure where you are and I don’t really care. However, I do care that you all are no doubt monitoring my progress within the Professional Wrestling Circuit, my newfound residence. As you know, there are hardly two nights left before the ‘biggest night in PWC history’ and even if I tried, I wouldn’t be able to find a knife large enough to cut through the amount of exhilaration that I’m feeling. Imagine, hundreds of thousands--- okay, I’m getting ahead of myself, approximately a thousand people, strolling idly around an arena filled with concession stands from which to buy merchandise of their favorite PWC stars, bolstered to completely unreasonable prices. All that, in addition to what those hefty New Jerseyans in particular, can’t get enough of...food. I can see Mr. Franklin’s face already.” Clark pauses and lets out a brief snicker. “Oh, I’m sorry. I promised to keep this story applicable to you. All the same, my day began at a place you're probably familiar with now Honey, the “bargain basement” shopping mall here in Trenton.” Clark shows his perfect, white teeth to the camera. “It’s amazing the depths I’m willing to go to for the almighty dollar, isn’t it? Well, during my excursion I noticed something that pushed my disgust much farther than each one of you. I do not consider underage public displays of pubic hair tasteful. In fact, it really has nothing to do with age—although the underage bush is way more disturbing. I do not find any public showing of one’s genital hair to be arousing or appropriate. So jean-makers, parents, police, whoever can help me with this dilemma, crack your whip, take out your handcuffs, grab some lube—sorry, I got off track—help me curb this horrific fashion trend. And to all the ladies out there… you don’t need to show my your bellybutton, or your stomach, or in this case your eyebrow colored bunch-of-fun in order to attract or please me. Real men, like me want a woman that give it up with a little convincing, cooks, cleans and most of all respects me. And the latter is pretty hard to get in return when I’m introduced to your pooky before I know your name. I mean I’ll still sleep with you, but don’t expect to get my home phone number...come on. My ex-wife barely got that far, isn‘t that right Honey?” He backs away from the camera and lifts the skin-tight wifebeater he’s wearing above his torso, baring his finely chiseled chest. “And God knows she’s craving some Carlisle lovin’ now. So, though today took a toll on my upset stomach, no thanks to the citizens of New Jersey, I’m sure that things will go smoothly tomorrow night. I’ll be coasting while keeping tabs on Mr. Starr and Mr. Conspiracy respective matches. And despite that, it’s common knowledge that I thrive on pressure...besides, when has money ever been bad incentive?”
