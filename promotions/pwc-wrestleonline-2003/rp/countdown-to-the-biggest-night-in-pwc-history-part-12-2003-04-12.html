Countdown to the Biggest Night in PWC History (Part 12)

12 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/12/03 In a poorly lit room, fairly small, and fairly empty, Warpath sits alone. There is a light bulb hanging by its wire in the center of the ceiling, and a TV/VCR on a shelf in the corner, and aside from a metal fold-up chair and 3 cardboard boxes, the room is barren. Warpath searches through one of the three boxes, hesitating every so often to analyze whatever it is he is searching through. 'Ahhh.' Warpath moans to himself in frustration, continuing to search. He slides the first box out of the way and pulls up the next. He shuffles through the contents for a few moments before pulling out a video cassette labeled, '2/1/03'. Warpath pops the cassette into the the VCR, presses play and sits back. He watches on as Gideon Poe sneaks him in week one, fury building in his facial expression. He continues to watch as Poe foils his plans and makes him look like a fool in front of all spectators. 'Five...And that is when it started. That was your first mistake, Poe. The countdown started at five, and it was you that started it.' Warpath says to himself, as if he knows someone is listening. He shuffles through that second box again, and again he pulls out a video. This time it is labeled, '3/1/03'. Warpath watches again as this time, the tape is of the March 1st show, where Warpath enlisted the help of an outside force to strip Roland away from Poe. The small clip Warpath shows is of himself, this time, standing at the entrance of an arena, and the arrival of the 'help'. Warpath presses the pause button, white lines appear across the picture, and he again begins to speak to himself. 'Four...the second stop on in this countdown, was on me. After being taken advantage of by your mind games week in and week out, I enlisted help. It was the second tick on your countdown. Again, you knew nothing of this 'master plan' and played out your part perfectly.' Instead of searching through a box, this time, Warpath goes right back to what is in the VCR. He fast forwards for a short while and soon begins to play again. This scene is of Warpath, again, but this time he is running scared. He sprints up and down a hallway, running from the voice of 'The Shade' Gideon Poe. Warpath begins to anger as he watches, but he controls himself. 'Three, Poe. You turned my plot against you right around and into my face. It turned out for the better, though, because in my fear, I think I had you scared. I think that in having me afraid, you grew fearful yourself because you no not how to control any kind of power. I think in you taking advantage of me, you took advantage of yourself, and allowed me to get inside YOU.' Warpath goes into the third cardboard box and rummages around before pulling out another tape. The label reading '3/22/03' this time. He pops it in and it plays. In this clip, Warpath takes the upper hand. He comes from backstage, annihilates a few fans, and then hits home on Poe. He threatens the safety of Poe's girl, and eventually punishes them both. 'Two...Poe...Two. Just one more tick after this. You are almost at your final stop of this countdown. This time, Poe, you couldn't use your 'intellect' and 'wit' to foil my plans, you felt my wrath, and got a taste of what is in your future. I got personal with you Poe, but unfortunately, you returned the favor the next week when you through me off the building. That which does not kill you shall make you stronger, so Poe, you should have killed me, because I am stronger and I am coming for you.'Warpath turns the TV and VCR off and sits with his arms on his knees. 'The last tick, Poe, is yet to come. Your last count is coming, and it is coming fast. This 'Countdown to Extinction' has started, progressed, and will soon come to an end. We are going to end this thing once and for all, and in the end, I will have your heart, soul, and your FUCKING EAGLE.' Warpath stands up from his seat and leaves the room, closing the door behind him.
