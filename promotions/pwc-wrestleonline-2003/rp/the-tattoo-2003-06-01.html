The Tattoo

1 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1st June 2003 A tattoo parlour, pretty plain. A clean hygienic feel to the light lime green coat of paint spread on the plastered walls. It’s a converted apartment, there’s a lobby, with chairs and a small coffee table with a selection of outdated magazines splashed over it. There is no one in, just a one man. He’s pretty chubby, a loose fitting white T-shirt and snug jeans, a selection of obscene piercing visible and tattoo’s all over his body. He has thin curly blonde hair that’s getting a bit long. His hair flows out wildly like gape vines. He’s cleanly shaven, then again he’d have to be with the amount piercing he has. The door creaks open. The man looks over. 'Hi welcome to Troy’s Tattoo’s.' The man scoffs. 'I’m Troy, how can I help.' In the door stands a very unsure Eric Sagar and an emotionless Propaganda. Propaganda is in his usual ring attire, Eric Sagar in his immaculately neat suits. Which contrast to his black-eye that is planted under his eye after his clash with Limerick this past Saturday. 'Yes, hi, I’m here to enquire if you could do my friend a tattoo, a religious based tattoo. None of that…' Eric Sagar pauses to eye up the ruffian he sees before him. '…Grimey garbage.' Troy just shakes his head. 'All right' he turns to Propaganda 'Do you have a specific idea of what you want? Or do you want to see a booklet?' 'Booklet' The Power Himself voices surely. 'All right' he says producing a leather A3 folder, 'Check through there, see if there’s anything you fancy.' Troy hands Propaganda the folder, he flicks through with Eric Sagar crouched over his shoulder. 'Urgh!' Sagar groans at a picture of a naked woman. 'Kuh!' Sagar heaves at a picture of the devil. 'Huh?' Sagar batters at an odd pattern. After a few minutes of discussion and banter the duo finally make up their mind. 'Excuse me, you.' Eric Sagar beckons Troy. 'Yes sir how may I help?' Troy politely asks, with a false smiling covering the urge to hit 'The Fat Preacher' 'Never hit a man of God, that’s what momma kept saying' he tells himself. 'We’ve decided.' Sagar commands. 'And what would you like?' Troy queries. 'Jesus, in that lettering.' Sagar demands throwing open the tattoo booklet and pointing to a very medieval, but classical font. 'Nice choice, that ones a popular one. Where would you like it?' Troy follows up. Sagar batters his eyelids, he hadn’t thought of that. 'On the underside of my right forearm.' Propaganda bellows. 'Right forearm, why, Brother Propaganda?' Eric Sagar stammers, a bit confused. 'Because when I raise my hand in victory, I will be able to show the world Jesus is raising my hand, Jesus is in my corner and Jesus will keep with my through and through.' He remonstrates with pride. 'A-Men, Brother Propaganda, A-Men!' 'A-Men'
