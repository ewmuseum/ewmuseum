Disposable Paladins - Part 9 - Old Friends Die Hard

15 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 15/04/03 Part 9: 'We were about 9 or 10. His name was Peter. We had been friends for about 2 years solid. Best friends in fact. We were thick as thieves.' 'Bauerman sat on his desk listening intently as Gideon sat there, hands clasped tightly in their palms while his head hugn to face the floor as he spoke. 'Anyway, we used to play games, just like any boys would. Scardy Cat was one of them. You'd have to scare the other person as bad as you could. We'd keep points and everything, and somehow I always used to scare Peter the worst. But it was still just a game, I knew that, and he knew that.' Gideon ran his hands through his long black hair and took a deep breath before he continued. 'So one day, in school, after swim practice or something or other. I showered as usual, then walked back to my locker. I opened it, and there it was. This hand,. Not a real one of course, a fake one, but still, there was one there resting in my locker. He had covered it in ketchup or something or other, y'know, to make it look like blood.' Martin nodded, He genuinely felt sympathy for Gideon right now. He never thought he'd feel true sympathy for a man held been trying to catch for the last 10 or so months. 'I, well, I freaked. I screamed, I yelled, I fell back and landed on my ass. That hand, that fucking hand. It looked like it was trying to reach out and grab me. Everyone laughed, no surprise there huh? I looked up again, looked at the hand and realised what it really was. Then I heard Peter. He was laughing too.' Gideon sat up and rubbed his eyes with his broad hands. This tale had been a long time in coming out, and unlocking all these painful memories from the box he had stored them in at the back of his mind was proving to be a real test of human mental strength for him to retrieve them all. 'He was in tears, I mean he was breaking down into hysterics. But I wasn't laughing. I was ready to kill.' 'What did you do' asked Martin. 'I ran up to him, I grabbed him by the back of the neck. I knew he was afraid of the water. When he was about 4 he nearly drowned in couple of inches of water in the bath or some shit like that. He had been petrified of it ever since. So I grabbed him and hauled him to the pool. He was screaming even while I was dragging him, begging me, pleading with me not to. But I wasn't listening, and so I chucked him in.' Gideon grabbed a bottle of water from the desk next to him and took a swig before continuing. 'He couldn't swim. I just watched. Some other kids who had seen enough eventually jumped in and helped him out. When I seen how pale he was, how he was shivering, not from the cold, but from fear, that was when i realised what I had done. As they led him past me, he stopped and looked at me. He said 'You sick uncaring fuck. One day I'll make you pay' Then Gideon stopped. 'And now he is.' Martin nodded. ' You can't blame yourself though' Bauerman said sympathetically. Gideon didn't bother replying, didn't even bother arguing. There was a simply reason to why he didn't too. He already was blaming himself, and there was nothing that Martin n Bauerman or this psycho from Gideon's past claiming to be Minotaur could say or do to prevent him from feeling the way that he did. Bauerman continued his emotional pep talk, but it fell on def ears. Instead Gideon just stared blankly at the screensaver on one of the computers, a picture of a seemingly grotesque creature with a mans body and a bulls head. Something about it intrigued him, almost shouted at him, teased him by it's mere presence flickering on the computer monitor. Bauerman continued to wail away, yet Poe's captivation with this still imagine only grew. 'This guy Peter will call us soon.' He wouldn't. 'They always call' Not all of them do. 'Then when he does, we'll be tracing him.' He wouldn't call. This one would never call. 'Are you even listening to me?!' Gideon continued looking at the screensaver. 'What was that creature Minotaur made up of?' Gideon finally asked. 'The one the psycho thinks he is?' Gideon nodded. 'Ummm, half man and half bull.' Gideon dropped the ice pack from his hand that he was holding onto his head. 'Who's computer is that?' Gideon pointed over to the screen saver with Minotaur on the front. 'That's Melvin's......why?' Melvin Peterson..... 'Peter' he said softly. 'Peter Meleverson. That was his name!' Peter Melverson.... Melvin Peterson The squirmy young timid looking rookie who stuck to Bauerman like a fly around shit. The man who Gideon had never even looked twice at. If there is a God... he's got one sick sense of fucking humour.
