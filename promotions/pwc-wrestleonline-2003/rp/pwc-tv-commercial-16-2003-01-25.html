PWC TV Commercial 16

25 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Originally aired on WGTW TV-48 in Philadelphia, PA at 10:30 am EST Saturday, January 25th, 2003. The sun beats down brightly on the home at [detail withheld] in Papillion, Nebraska on this rather chilly winter day. A wind whips the cold air causing it to sting against the skin as it hits. In the front yard of the home there is a “SOLD” real estate sign, and a single car parked in the driveway that is backed in with the trunk open. The front door to the split level home comes open and out walks a man that is familiar to some, but still relatively unknown to most. His short brown hair and goatee are neatly trimmed as usual, but the normally warm expression on his face is replaced with one of trepidation. He walks with a slight limp on his left knee and carries a suitcase down to the Dodge Stratus parked in the driveway. The hooded sweatshirt he wears reads ‘Property of the University of Nebraska Athletic Department’ on the front and has the name ‘Axtell’ printed across the back. Following close behind him is a beautiful blonde woman, bundled up in a heavy winter coat. “Is that everything, Jim?” she calls out with sadness in her voice as she starts to pull the door shut behind her. Kami Axtell turns and awaits the reply from her husband. Jim Axtell tosses the suitcase in the trunk and slams the lid shut, pausing a second before looking up to his wife. “Yeah, I think so.” A tear begins to roll down Kami’s face as she slowly pulls the door to the home shut for the last time. She buries her face in her hands and begins to sob, bringing Jim up from the car to comfort her. “Come on now, Kam,” Jim says soothingly. “We’ve talked and talked about this. I know you don’t want to leave, but this is something we have to do.” Kami looks up at her husband, face quivering and eyes full of tears. “YOU have to do this! I don’t WANT to leave!!” She sobs some more and buries her face in Jim’s shoulder. He caresses her head and holds her close as he looks up toward the sky. “This is my last shot, boo. OSW didn’t want me back after my knee injury and there weren’t any other promotions knocking down the door to sign me. When this opportunity came along, yes, I had to take it. PWC is my last chance to make it in wrestling and I’m not going to let it pass by.” Jim gently pulls Kami away from his body and holds her softly by her face, looking deeply into her eyes. “I love you more than anything, you know that right?” Jim asks Kami. She nods her head as she wipes some tears from her face. “But we had to sell everything and leave our whole life behind, Jim!” “I know,” says Jim sympathetically. “It has to be like this. I promise you that when we get to New Jersey and I get rolling in PWC that I’ll make everything right with you. I’m going to PWC to show the world that I have what it takes to be the best in professional wrestling. I’ve never quit on anything in my life, Kami, and I’m not going to quit on this. Jim Axtell is going to take the PWC by storm and then you’ll have everything you ever dreamed of.” Jim smiles warmly which brings a halfhearted smile from his wife. Jim wipes the remaining tears from Kami’s face and then leads her down to the car. He walks her around to the passenger side and lets her in, shutting the door behind her. He moves around the car and opens the passenger door, but takes one last look at his former home and things of all he is leaving behind. After a deep breath Jim climbs in the car and turns over the ignition, beginning his long drive to New Jersey and the Primetime Wrestling Circuit.
