Eternally Damned: Chapter V

13 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Time To Pay Respect Hessian rests peacefully, his arms crossed over his chest. Buried in his ring attire, the way he would have liked it. Jesse Ramey is crouched next to the open top coffin, paying his respects in silence. On the bottom half of the coffin is a wreath of black flowers with a cold chain spiralling around the circular shape of the wreath. The wreath has a note attached: 'Ready to kick your ass in the afterlife, Dave' Manuel Labour quietly stands by the coffin and places a replica OSW Word Title belt on the corpse. Labour is wearing a rather old suit, which by the sweet and sour chicken stains, is obviously rented. Manuel sits back down. On looking the coffin are Rush Mason who’s looking happily retired in his Hawaiian style T-shirt, baggy denim shorts and sandals. He’s talking to Angelo Deville who’s wearing a very stylish black suit. 'Hey, Mason, I never expected to see you here.' Deville comments in a hushed tone. 'Yeah, you doing a eulogy?' Mason queries. 'Naw, I aint big on that sorta’ stuff.' Deville comments. 'I’m just here to pay my respect, ya’ know. The best way I can.' 'And how’s that?' Rush asks again. 'Slap around any dick who tries to be a fucking smart ass, this guy made a lot of enemies, we’re here to remember, not be a prick!' Deville grunts. 'You had any work yet?' Rush jokes. 'Actually, yeah, that prick Kreigman tried to take a leak on the corpse. So I slapped him about a bit. The guys got no class!' Deville barks. 'Well I’m off to find the Bobby Stormbringer voodoo doll, later.' Rush kids. In attendance today are some very big names in wrestling. Everyone looks in an immaculately neat dress. Taking a seat ready for the service are the likes of Jason Marah, John Critchton, Akage Kigami wearing a white suit, Shawn Stewart sporting a blue pin stripe suit and Gideon Poe holding up a piece of card with 'You Know That Shit’s Hessian' plastered on. On the front row Dark Gimmick is huddled in a corner next to Magnum Wolf, Deadhead and DarkStar. 'Do you know who I blame?' Deadhead asks rhetorically in a snide tone. 'Jews, fucking cheap skate tools!' 'It’s his own fault.' Dark Gimmick yaps interrupting him. 'He deserved it, he’s useless. Just like his father. I’m ashamed to say it was all started by me. I’m glad he’s dead, come to think of it. His coffin will probably rot before they get in a grave. He stank.' Dark Gimmick stands up to look at the coffin. 'YEAH! YOU’RE NOT SINGING ANYMORE!' Dark Gimmick roars, as everyone turns silent. All the heads spin to look at Dark Gimmick. 'Hey! You stupid boner. Sit down and shut up!' Deville tells off the skeleton. Dark Gimmick sits down. There is silence for a few seconds until. 'Hey we were almost, very nearly, in a stable…. Almost.' DarkStar boasts to the three. 'May I ask a question?' Magnum Wolf interrupts a very boring conversation. 'Who was this Hessian character?' 'What?' 'What?' 'WHAAAAT?' 'Hey, C’mon, stop it with the silent treatment.' Magnum says while unbuttoning his jacket. 'Check out my package.' Magnum insists while untucking his shirt. At the podium a stumpy man with a big nose and a horseshoe like curtain of grey hair around the back and sides of his head. He stands at the podium. 'Awe of She' by A.S.H. plays very softly in the background. 'May you all please take your seat for the service and be silent, please?' The man insists rather sternly. There’s a shuffling of feet and a two second chatter. Then silence. 'Thank you, I’m Mr Belfanew and I will be taking this service in memory of the life of Hess—' Mr Belfanew is interrupted as the door of the cemetery swings open. In the entrance stands Atlanta 'Propaganda' Johnson in his neat suit and his hair gelled back. Despite that he looks rough, his eyes are bloodshot, there are bags under his eyes, he’s unshaven, he’s got his shoulders slouched and his glasses are ever-so-slightly crooked. There’s a slight gasp. No one would expect him to be here, they assume he’s here to cause trouble. The only trouble Propaganda’s planning on causing is with his emotions. Angelo Deville from the front row stands up. 'Hey Prop, you aint here to cause trouble are ya’?' Deville grunts, heading to Propaganda with his arms spread as if he was about to have a scrap. Atlanta just stares at the superstar with a blank stare. After a few seconds he just shakes his head and takes a seat at the very back corner of the room. Discarded from the rest of the room. There is a very hushed whisper of 'What’s he doing here?' from someone seated. 'This will be tough' Propaganda thinks to himself. Taking a big gulp. 'As I was saying' Mr Belfanew starts again. 'I’m Mr Belfanew and I will be taking the service in remembrance of the former professional wrestling juggernaut, Hessian. We shall start with eulogies from his fellow co-workers. First up is.' Mr Belfanew looks at the list on his podium. 'Gaz Green.' Gaz 'The Jester' Green makes his way from the audience to the podium. 'Hello. Hessian masterminded P.W.A so being his studen-... Bitch I at-least should pay my respects to the man! So Hessian, if you’re in Heaven or if you’re in Hell, I know you’re enjoying yourself. Rest In Peace.' Gaz pays his respect and heads back to his seat. There’s an applause. 'There was no need for the profanities! He went out of his way to use it.' Propaganda thinks. 'Thank you, Mr. Green' Mr. Belfanew express’ whilst taking his place back in front of the podium. 'Next, is James Edwards.' James 'lunar' Edwards steps up to the podium. 'I’m going to make this short and simple' James states. 'I never met the man, I hardly saw the man, but, I know this...' Everyone sits on the edge of their seat waiting for a speech praising him. But they get. 'I'm glad I didn't tag with him as I'd be dead now, rest among the stars big man.' Edwards empties his heart the best he can to a very unenthusiastic applause. 'Athiest moron, he knows nothing. I’m sick of his sort.' Propaganda howls harshly in his head. 'Uh. Thank you, Mr. Edwards. Next is a Mr. Azala Zameer.' Mr Belfanew introduces. But there is nothing. Azala doesn’t appear. 'Where is he?' Belfanew Then, once again, the cemetery doors swing open. 'Celebration' by Kool and The Gang busts out of the portable speaker, held by one of the hands of 'The African Nightmare' Azala Zameer. Who seems to be in a very party like mood. Due to the language barrier, he’s come. Not too dressed for the occasion. He’s in a bright yellow polo neck T-shirt and blue jeans. In his other hand he has about 15 chocolate bar wrappers. 'MOWWY MIME MWATE!' he roars at Mr. Belfanew, his speech distorted because of the amount of Chocolate and caramel crammed in his mouth. 'The Snack Daddy' makes his way to the podium. 'MI MONT TO MAY MESSIAN MAR MRBILLIANT! MOW METS PARTY! MAW MOI MAY MOTTIMHAM! MEEZE MONT MURT ME MOOBY-MOO! MI MAR MAV MOORE MENDS MIN MY MARCAR! MOT MOO MOGING MOOO MO!? MEH!? MEE' Zameer splutters while nodding his head to the music. He picks up his speakers and walks out of the cemetery with portable speaker in hand. 'Mary Mother Of Jesus! What was that? Damn brain-dead foreign cretin. Damn him.' Propaganda growls in his mind. 'Oooookay then.' Mr. Belfanew drags on unsure of what to do now. 'So now to the next eulogy. Asa Fountain.' In a flared suit, Asa Fountain strides up to the podium. Clears his throat. 'Hey folks. Now I’d like to show my respect to the late, great, Hessian.' Fountain prattles. 'The man was a 2 Time US Champ back in NTICW, he made a fool out of the great The Josh and he beat the current PCW Champion, Totally Tubular Tommy, in a US title tournament. Amongst many of his great achievements. Put yo’ hands together, for the big man up in heaven. Hessian!' A loud applause for Fountain’s tribute. 'He did better things than that.' Propaganda ponders in a grunting manner, staring out the window to see an endless block of graves. As 'Awe Of She' by A.S.H. finishes, 'Still In The Dark' by Daisuke Ishiwatari starts to play, lightly in the background. 'Now.' Mr Belfanew states. 'Stone Burns is to speak.' As Belfanew steps down, Stone Burns steps up, doing his suit nice and proper. 'Hi.' Stone Burns greets. 'Well Hessian, was great… I knew him well. We enjoyed many good times. I often liked watching him win matches. In PWC.' There’s a few raising of eyebrows and concerned 'huh'ing. 'I mean… ummm… OSW. Right? Yeah. OSW. We were great friends. I liked it when he won his matches. I knew him well you know and… REST IN PEACE, LITTLE G—' he looks at the open coffin. '…BIG MAN!' A rather cautiously slow applause is handed out. 'What a fool. What a total fool.' Propaganda’s mind barks silently. 'All right then.' Belfanew groans. 'Now we’ve got… Rush Mason to come up to the podium and speak.' Rush Mason steps up to the speaker’s podium and overlooks the mourners. 'Now this isn’t much of a Eulogy, but Hessian reached the massive feat of winning a 10-man battle royal. Even if he and Drake Maxwell conspired to make me lose. Rest in peace you cheating…' Rush sees Angelo Deville standing up angrily. 'Great man. Thanks everyone.' Mason rapidly walks to a seat as far away from Deville as possible. 'What was the point? Them independent blacks are all the same… there I go, sounding like Eric again.' Propaganda thinks to himself. 'Thank you Mr. Mason.' Mr. Belfanew blabbers. 'Next up we have a Mr. Calvin Riley.' There’s a few groans from the audience from those who know Riley and how much of a jackass he is and how boring he is. 'Ladies and gentlemen. Hessian taught me, like a great teacher should. He taught me well. He taught me to be more of a monster inside the ring then a gentlemen. I’ll really miss him. He was great. NOT! HA! HA! SWERVE! SWERVE! SWERVE! I’M EVIL! I HAVE TO BE! Now my mission for global perfection is a step closer now that a freak like him has died.' WHACK! Deville clonks the annoyance that is Calvin Riley with a back hand. Picks him up by the back of his pants, carries him to the entrance then hurls him out. Riley lands on the hard concrete path with a bang. 'Thank You, Lord.' Propaganda meditates. 'Finally.' Mr. Belfanew who’s starting to sweat due to what a disaster the funeral service has been. 'The last two eulogies will be by Tempest and the great Ivan Stanislav.' The two step up to the podium to an applause. Ivan in his army uniform as respect to the dead, looking very prestige and honourable. And Tempest in a purple and black colour scheme suit. Tempest clears he throat with a very discreet cough. 'I’ll miss Hessy-poo. He was a great man. A Man who loved to spattered with pudding, a man with a sense of humour, a man with compassion for the howling souls in the night… that’s why he killed so many of them.' Tempest pauses to cast a black rose onto the coffin 'I hope you don’t meet all your tag partners in Hell. Now Ivan.' 'Thank you.' Ivan thanks his girlfriend. 'Now I would like to start off by saying I was and still am to this very second undefeated against Hessian. But Hessian is dead, now it’s a shame, I do concede, but if he was a good communist he’d be alive today. But he isn’t. So it just proves that another capitalist has fallen to the red army…' Ivan’s communist preaching bores Propaganda. He drifts away while Ivan hauls on. 'This has been a complete disaster. He didn’t deserve this. Granted he may have killed in masses. But everyone deserves a good send-off. Everyone here are just here for cheap publicity. Except me, I’m here for a friend. I’m the one who tagged with him… where are the others? …Well they’re dead. They have an excuse. I’m sick of this. I just want it to end.' Propaganda contemplates to himself. Then wakes up from his dream. 'So in conclusion, as always, Communism triumphs once again. Thank you.' A very quiet applause can be heard. As Ivan soaks it up, then him and his partner take their exit. Mr Belfanew gets to his feet. Then he makes his way to the podium. 'I’d like to take you all to the graveyard for the burial. You are not obliged to stay.' Mr Belfanew announces. Everyone stands up to stay in this awful service or to leave as the scene fades to black.
