Rhymes for the forgotten...

24 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 24/5/03 The emotions contained within his scarred shell representing his body were battling each other in a deadly battle for dominance, a battle that was going to reach a cataclysmic fever pitch here tonight. In front of them all. In front of his friends, in front of the fans. Yet he cared not. And why should he? Very few even cared for him now. 'You okay?' asked Maynard as he twiddled his drumsticks through his fingers with such ease that he made it look as though it was going out of style. Trent looked up from the floor as Vex opened his eyes and both followed to where his gaze was aimed at. 'Gideon?' asked Maynard again. The Shade continued to let himself become transfixed by the dark amber liquid occupying his shot glass a little longer before raising it and knocking it down in his throat allowing all it's scorching humid nectar to gurgle and slide down his throat, ever so slightly causing him to clench his eyes as he did so. 'Yes' he finally answered as he turned and slipped on his thin fishnet top completing the rest of his attire which was black leather pants and boots. He then brushed his black hair with fresh crimson dyed streaks out of his face and turned to his three band mates. 'Hand me my axe' Trent nodded slowly before reaching behind him and lifting Gideon's guitar from behind the couch and handing it to him. He then slowly slid the black leather strap over his shoulder and let the black body of the guitar hang around his back. The long neck of the instrument stuck up diagonally behind his shoulder like a proud warriors sword. 'So, I guess we're ready then ladies' spoke Vex as he got up and grabbed his bass guitar. 'No vocal harmony warm up for you tonight Trent' he asked sarcastically. 'Bite shit and die you Axl Rose wannabe' replied the vocalist with amusement in his voice. 'Well then gentleman, lets rock this shit house bar in the dead centre of nowhere!' All four men then placed their clenched fists against each others before letting out a triumphant yell. Their audience was waiting. In single file the four man army walked. At the head, Trent, black hair tied back in a ponytail as he led him men through the narrow backstage area. Behind him walked Vex, carrying his bass at his side like a Gladiator would carry his spear as he walked up the pathway to the coliseum. Trailing a few yards behind him was Maynard, still twiddling his drumsticks at his side like a lone range cowboy would swirl his side arms before setting to unleash bullets seeking death from his six shooter at a gunfight at dawn. Behind them all, at the very back, trailing a few yards just behind Maynard was Gideon. Guitar slung over his back with the neck poking up like a Samurai's sword. Deep blue eyes cold as ice. The four warrior's walked up their everlasting green mile, and stepped forth before their coliseum crowd. Cadaver Theory were not here to entertain. Cadaver theory were here to unleash every positive and negative energy in their bodies. Their expressions and stage presence alone gave out the message that if you weren't going to listen to them ... they were just going to play harder and faster. Maynard sat, Trent stood, Vex and Gideon plugged in. All set. No four count to lead them in. No introduction. Just the harsh aggressive sound of Gideon bellowing out a harsh vicious rip roaring riff that immediately set the crowd into a crazed frenzy. Trent then bellowed his deep enchanting vocals, Vex used the bass as a blistering machine gun as he pummelled the strings while Maynard pictured his symbols and snares as the face of every enemy he ever encountered just to have more reasons to beat them harder and faster. Gideon stood tall, spouting his poetry and emotions through 6 strings that he made talk. This was harsh poetry in alternative endless motion. This was his salvation. 2 hours later things had changed dramatically. A crowd of young youths who no doubt expected lyrics about 'killing your parents and shooting your brothers and sisters' had now fallen moronically silent with arms folded over their chests, the odd few shouting obscenities at the industrial band. Trent looked at his three band mates, the overhead lights glistened of their sweat soaked bodies making them glimmer like lost golden angels. Oh how there were anything but. 'That's it' Trent said, and dropped his microphone. He was right, their set was up. Yet one of them wasn't done. Gideon wasn't done. 'Not yet' he said softly, hardly expecting any of them to hear him, but they did. He then walked up to the microphone and strummed his guitar to check it was still in tune. Trent stood at the side curiously, no alarm or confusion on his face. They wanted shock factor. He had 20 plus years of hidden secrets he was about to unleash in a form that would no doubt go down as being described as 'fucked up'. He'd give them shock factor. He strummed and picked at his guitar, and Maynard and Vex knew exactly what to do, and added a small melody line and drum beats onto the piece, unsure of what to expect and what Gideon was doing. 'Ring around the roses A pocket full of poesies Ashes, ashes We all fall down...' Everyone fell silent as his deep hollow voice racked through the nursery rhyme. 'Ring around a dead rose A coffin with a dead Poe Ashes, ashes We all die now.' The inhabitants of the club took a step back, as did Trent. Gideon looked up, his eyes almost glazed over and oblivious to his surroundings, and who or what he was doing and what he was becoming. 'There was a crooked man Who walked a crooked mile He found a crooked sixpence Inside a world that was so vile' The crowd looked at each other with shock and horror, while others simply fell transfixed under Gideon's power. His words echoed through their own past misfortunes. 'He bought a black witches cat It's eyes dead as a spouse And they all died painfully In a little crooked house.' Gideon continued pulsing through riff after riff, his eyes growing more demonic, his words escalating in sadistic nature, the crowd more and more falling to his command. 'Mary had an ill lamb It's infection would infest and grow And everywhere that Mary went She'd carry the disease upon the unknown' The crowd were his, and he now held them in the palm of his hand. 'It came with her to school one day Which was never written in the rules It made the children writhe and die Face down in Crimson pools.' With that he stopped. They all stopped. He un-strapped his guitar and let it drop with a crunching distorted thud against the cold hard floor of the stage before walking off, the crowd only now awaking from the hypnotic trance he seemed to hold them under with from his demonic nursery rhymes. He then disappeared backstage, calm as ever. His three band mates followed him. 'What the hell was all that about?!' bellowed Trent and grabbed Gideon by the shoulder and turned him around, the words echoing through the backstage area. 'Jesus Gideon, that some new shit you been writing?' asked Vex. Gideon looked at his band mates thoughtfully. 'I just wanted to scare some people tonight. Pass on some of the shit I've been through to them.' 'Through fucking nursery rhymes?!' shouted Maynard. Gideon simply shrugged and nodded. 'Was a good a method as any. Everyone remember their childhood. Everyone loved their childhood. They were the best days of everyone's lives. What better way to scare them than twisting the very words that their adoring parents and guardians would use to sing to them softly in the night to send them off to sleep and dream.' 'What is this all about Gideon?' Trent asked caringly. 'Tonight I didn't want to be a fucking hero! I didn't want to be a God damn Paladin! I didn't want to be the man bound to do right under everyone's eyes and morals. Tonight I wanted to be something else!!' They all looked at him, to his surprise, with sympathy. 'Now it's out of my system. Now I can go back to being the adoring anti-hero I have no option and choice in being.' He said with faint sarcasm. Yet it was all true, and they could see through his attempts at trying to sugar-coat the harsh truth. 'I'll see you guys later.' With that Gideon turned, and his band mates knew they need to give him his space. they'd been through much worse and stranger situations together, and this was just another one that they would no doubt remember in years to come with strange smiles on their faces. But until those days and memories would come, what they had just witnessed had scared them all to their core.
