Transition Part 1

19 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 19th Feb He lay motionless on the mat, flat on his back, staring up at the bright white lights and the huge crowd that packed out the arena. His body was covered head to toe in pearls of sweat. He thought to himself. (If I move will there be a body shaped wet patch where I lay?) Kind of a stupid thought but it was all he could do to try and block out the pain he was feeling through the majority of his body. He hadn’t suffered a beating like this since the time he was beaten by a group of thugs because he wouldn’t hand over his Big Mac and packet of cigarettes. (They say cigarettes will kill you, but that’s ridiculous!!) He tried to open his left eye but met a small resistance as the dried blood broke away. He could make out a beautiful brunette standing over him, taunting him. At first he wasn’t sure who she was, apparently when you get hit over the head a lot of times, you kinda lose your awareness or something like that. Then he remembered (Lisa that bitch. Money grabbing, good for nothing, pair of breasts on legs. If I ever get my hands on her again I’m going to grab her by those silicon enhanced mounds and squeeze them till her head pops!) He tried to push himself up but found he had no strength what so ever. By now a swarm of officials and medical staff were knelt round them. He only knew cause he could hear their muffled speech swirling round him like and out of control tornado. Dave, Steve and Pete were there. He had no idea who they were, but they told him who they were that many times it was as if he had known them all his life. (Leave me alone ya idiots I’m fine! Ugh…. What do I mean, I can’t move, I cant feel my legs…or my arms for that matter.) He could hear the crowd booing immensely. He hoped they weren’t booing him; they were what kept him going. He hoped and prayed they were booing that sick monster that had put him in this mess. (Aaaaaagggggh!!!) He lifted his head about 3 inches from the canvas…then it went black. That was the last time he had been in a wrestling ring. That was 4 months ago. That was PIW.
