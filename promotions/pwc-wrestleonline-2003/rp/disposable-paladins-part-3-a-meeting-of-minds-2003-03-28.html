Disposable Paladins - Part 3 - A Meeting Of Minds

28 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 28/03/03 Part 3 Things had not worked out the way Martin Bauerman had hoped for at all. He had flashbacks of Newspaper headlines running through his head. 'Dark Figure claims the life of another criminal' 'The Shade serves justice once again' People loved it, they felt safe. That's what a cop, a good cop at least, always wants. People to feel safe, but feel safe because their protecting them, not some overnight Batman who shows up on the scene and starts taking out paedophiles, rapists and serial killers. He knew Poe didn't want the recognition, and he knew he had his own motives for the killings. Yet that was why Bauerman wanted to catch him so bad. Pick his brain for a few hours. Their game of cat and mouse had slowly but surely turned into a respectable one, but nevertheless Poe had made Bauerman and his department look like a laughing stock. In the end, Poe's killings died down before fading away completely. Bauerman grew tired of the Bangor area and requested a transfer to New Jersey. He and his family had been there for 6 months, had settled in nicely and lived in a nice area. He was closing cases as quick as ever before, and was getting a lot of respect from the department. They even gave him a partner, Melvin Peterson. Young kid, never even dreamt of bending a rule. Melvin was young though, and often scared. Bauerman was sure he'd soon learn the tricks of the trade. But then....... then the shit began hitting the fan. PWC began opening their operation in New Jersey, and everybody who was anybody somehow knew who was on the roster. Half the department were wrestling fans, and they all knew the tales of Gideon Poe. They even knew, that he himself was on the PWC roster after he made his startling debut on the opening show. So, Gideon Poe was in New Jersey. No swift justice killings yet, but this time Bauerman wanted him caught before any could happen. Then things changed. Now he needed Gideon, needed his help on catching this killer, this madman, this psychopath. He had come face to face with Gideon many times. The way he looked dead into his eyes as he leapt out of the window of Winterbourne Asylum having manipulated Jacob Riley into slashing his own vocal chords apart with a razor blade. He'd openly admit it scared him to the point of cold flustered sweats. 'You should be going home to those two women, not pondering over them' came the voice of Sue the cleaner. Bauerman looked up from the picture of his wife and child and chuckled. 'Someone's got to protect them Sue, you know that' 'Well let someone else do it for tonight, it's past midnight. You can protect them better when your near them than instead of just sitting in here all night.' Bauerman laughed and drank some the water from his Evian bottle. 'Don't worry Sue, I won't be long' Sue shot him a mocking suspicious glance before laughing herself 'Okay, time for me to go anyway. You're not the only one who has people to protect you know' 'Oh no?' 'Hell no sweetie, I'm off home to protect my cats from that vicious frickin' dog next door who keeps terrorising them.' 'Don't you have a husband as well Sue?' 'Don't remind me' Bauerman laughed again as Sue left and headed downstairs and waved him goodbye. He looked at the photo of his family once again as he went on one of his guilt trips. 'Thought she'd never leave' came a deep voice causing Bauerman to turn around violently and stare into the darkness. Then out of the shadows came the owner of the voice wearing his trademark long black leather jacket and long black hair. 'Gideon!' The Shade himself simply smoothed his hair out of his face before drawing the blinds of the window behind Bauerman. 'There are no snipers Poe' 'No reason for me to take precautions then' Bauerman slowly stood and faced Gideon as he himself turned to greet him. The two then shared a moment as they both pondered over their last few encounters over time. 'Been quite a long time Gideon' The Shade simply nodded. 'I know why you're in New Jersey, it's because of this PWC venture, I didn't suspect you were tracking me down so don't worry' 'That was never my intention' 'Small world huh?' 'Try a fucked up one' 'I'll agree with you on that' 'You ran into the others as well I understand?' 'Lennon, Trent, Maynard, yeah.' 'Look Bauerman, I doubt we're going to delve into pleasantries so lets just avoid all the civil bullshit. Cut to the chase.' Bauerman nodded while smiling slightly. 'You know why you're here' 'Serial killer, young women and children the main targets. Suspected to be a white male between late twenties and early thirties. No signs of rape yet mainly because of suspicion of impotency. Lives in the New Jersey area and yet no signs of a clear pattern, trend or motive for his killings. ' Even Bauerman was slightly surprised by how much had gathered and picked up on. 'Then again Martin, what would I know? That was a wild guess, tell me, was I close?' 'Cut the sarcasm Gideon, this is serious stuff. I'm at a fucking loose end here and I'm going against all my morals bringing you in here!' 'I didn't even say I wanted n yet!' 'Oh come on Gideon let's cut schoolyard antics! You won't be able to walk away from this, just like I won't until it ends and that bastard is behind bars or 6 feet under! Then again let's face it, you only seem to deal with the latter!' Poe lashed out at hearing the final comment and sent Bauerman stumbling back into his chair. It was unusual for him to lose his cool like that, but under the circumstances stress could overcome a man easily. 'Don't ever....EVER think I get a kick out of doing this bullshit Bauerman. Anyone with the smallest, even faintest shred of sanity and a heart would do EXACTLY the same thing. I know you've thanked me in the prayers you utter at the back of your mind for ridding this cesspool of a world from the people you fear you can't protect your wife Joan and your daughter Lucy from!' 'You leave my family out of this!' Bauerman shot up and grabbed Poe by the collar of his trench coat and slammed him up against a filing cabinet sending a set of files stacked at the top falling and scattering all across the floor. 'What do you want Poe?!' 'I want you to wake up!' 'No, what do you really want, me to admit it!' 'Okay Bauerman, admit it!' 'FINE! I need you, I need you on this case and --!' 'You KNOW what I want you to admit!' 'FINE!' Bauerman moved away from Poe and ran his hand through his short auburn hair before looking back at Poe. 'I'm scared.......... I'm scared Gideon. I'm scared that no matter what I do, nothing is going to make a difference. This uphill battle I've been fighting for the last 15 years isn't getting any easier. Corruption, blackmail, you name it, everything goes on here. I'm sick of trying to be the believer that good will always prevail over evil....... I'm just flat out fucking terrified!' Bauerman banged his fist against his desk in a fit of rage as Poe finally processed everything that he just confessed to him. 'I'm not going to say keep doing what you do. Just do whatever you can to protect the people who matter to you Bauerman. Protect your daughter, protect your wife, because I'll be blunt, this world is never going to be lenient to anyone.' Martin simply took a deep breath and nodded slowly, before eventually walking to the edge of his desk and picking up a folder and handing it to Poe. 'It's the case file, look over it, tell me what you think. If you want nothing to do with it, fine, I'll understand, disappear into thin air like you usually do. Yet if you do want to help me, which I'm pleading with you to do, then, well, I'm sure you'll contact me.' Poe took the case file and nodded slowly. 'Give me a few days' 'Okay, but we don't have much time' Bauerman then looked Poe up and down and extended his hand to Poe who looked at him as though he had just pointed a gun to him. He then eventually grasped it and shook it firmly before walking past Bauerman and past the several other work desks. 'You going to jump out a window as usual?' asked Bauerman with a faint smile on his face. 'Actually ......I thought I might use the elevator' spoke Poe before disappearing into the darkness at the end of the hallway.
