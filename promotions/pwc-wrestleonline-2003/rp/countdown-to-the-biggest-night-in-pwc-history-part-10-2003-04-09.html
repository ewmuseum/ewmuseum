Countdown To The Biggest Night In PWC History (Part 10)

9 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9th April 2003 A Roman dressed man looks up from a library computer screen, with a slightly puzzled look on his face. He turns to the giant next to him, who seems to be getting away with wearing jeans and a t-shirt for the time being, anyway. The big guy takes a loud suck from his slurpee before finally saying “What?” to the Roman Legion leader who sits next to him. The annoyed Roman turns to the bigger man and responds. “I can’t concentrate on this with you drinking that. Besides, you shouldn’t be drinking in here.” The big man just chuckles at him. “Why are you so bothered, you’re just trying to make PWC in EWR version 3. Hey, you gave me a hundred in brawling… thanks!. “ Colossus frowns at something. “I’m not uncharismatic. I resent that.” Romulus turns to him and shakes his head solemnly. “That is not important. You know what is important?” Colossus looks around and shrugs to the world as if it were watching this; “Dunno. Perhaps getting the next issue of the Ultimates. Hey, I know, I should track down Stan Lee and force him to add a Roman Superhero to the United Nations team. Perhaps in Ultimates: Europe. Maybe Thor could lead them.” Romulus seems outraged at the suggestion… “Thor cannot exist as his religious system does not exist. Apollo and Jupiter and so on are the only true gods, the Asgard and the others are the false gods!” “Even the Go’uld” says Colossus, directly at the camera, before winking. Romulus looks towards the camera and then back at Colossus. “Who are you talking to? And stop pretending to be charismatic!” shouts the founder of Rome. Colossus shrugs. “Ok, now this is what is really important. Two people is not a stable. Two people is a tag team. We need to seek out and employ more people to wrestle for us as part of our team. Perhaps we should offer a staff discount on the toga’s or something to try to attract more people” says Romulus to Colossus, who is paying more attention to his slurpee than anything else. “Got any Reese’s Peanut Butter Cups on you?” Romulus covers his head with his hand as Colossus says that, having paid no attention to what the Roman leader just said. “I do have an idea though… what if we got the togas to be made out of something that doesn’t make your ass chafe quite so much?” Romulus’ eyes narrow at the suggestion. “As the kids these days would say, that was way too much information for me, Citizen. Now to the business. What we need is to become celebrity. We need to be famous, we need children to wear our t-shirts. We need women to want their husbands to be us, we need to… Colossus, where the hell are you?” While Romulus was talking, Colossus went to chat up a particularly nerdy looking girl at another table in the library. Actually, if she removed the glasses, you’d imagine that she would like quite pretty… rather like Fred from Angel, in fact. The camera goes back to Romulus. “God, I knew I should have tracked down Unstoppable Eugene instead. At least he’d have waited till I finished the sentence before going away and doing his own thing. Hell, even Macabre Payne would have pretended to listen to me for the first couple of months.” The legion leader shakes his head. “We need some victories. That’s what we need. I may not be booked at the pay per view… but that was because we resolved the problem with Phoenix prior to it. But that doesn’t mean that I’m going to take this lying down.” Romulus turns around to see where Colossus was and he appears to be gone. “Well that’s just great.” Meanwhile, Colossus was off counting down the minutes to his personal (and the young lady’s) biggest night in PWC history.
