Date: 2/24 Close your eyes for a moment and take in the atmosphere. The lighting…dim, sets the mood The Music a mix of many Jazz Artists float through the air, most of it recorded and pumped in through quality speakers, but later on Mark Carter will take center stage and fill the clubs live band quota for the week with his own unique brand of smooth sounding Jazz. Jazz is a hard kind of mood music to classify…it's good for a variety of things, social drinking, social conversation, private parties, pleasant music to kill yourself by too…there's nothing like slitting your wrists to the sound of Chet Baker is there? And if your that kind of person Jazz can be that mood enhancer to fuck by. Yeah, Jazz…the all purpose life enhancer. Various Pictures decorate the walls, famous Jazz Legends gaze out from behind the frames, forever immortalized by a picture worth a thousand words, Each particular photograph has been autographed, bring the Jazz Club that much more prestige…after all any Neanderthal who walks the city streets can find a picture of Louis Armstrong and frame it…but an autographed picture…that speaks volumes of your taste doesn’t it? Sharp dressed waiters move graceful across the floor taking orders quietly and quickly, after all this isn’t the place for a blond haired, big-breasted blond or brunette destined for a leading role in the next major porn video, young men tend these tables, young men who recognize this form of servitude not as a job…but as an art, no questioning, no repetition, done right the first time because the customer is ALWAYS right. Seated above on the second floor that overlooks the stage is one of the Clubs regulars, he never speaks much during his nightly visits, for he is a man of few words when he chooses to relax, many nights he will simply nurse a drink and relax to the soothing music, other nights he may indulge in the pleasures of the flesh and procure the talents of one of the women who frequent the club…not so much an act of prostitution as an act of mutual consent of pleasure for one night…yes even sex done right and in the proper environment can be done with class. Long silver hair tied back in a black band seems to shimmer in the dim light, the brightest thing amidst the crawling shadows of the room, clad in an Armani suit unbuttoned slightly, hands gloved in the finest black leather lay folded on the table, posture relaxed, comfortable, if one could see his eyes they would find him to be most relaxed. Gloved fingertips adjust a pair of black sunglasses, in the dim light these seem to be out of place one would think, but if the man is worth as much as his suit then exceptions can be made, if you have the cash then people seem to be more willing to accept your little quirks and oddities. Marcus Divone has the cash, enough to buy the little Jazz club a hundred times over, but he’s settled for half ownership, a partnership that secures the clubs financial well being, in turn Marcus has a place to come and relax, or carry out business… Tonight it’s about the business. Rolling up his sleeve Marcus checks his Rolex…Timex became a joke around the same time he stopped fucking those groupie sluts that followed wrestling promotions around down south. Now, just like everything in his life, women are among the “finer” things. 10 pm…well to be precise in one minute it will be 10 pm…and to be even more precise in one minute his business arrangement will begin. His eyes watched the front door, his watch chimed softly alerting him it was time to begin, focused on the front door he was surprised when he felt a presence behind him, Clad in a brown trench coat and a bowler hat he effectively covered up his face but could not hide the unruly locks of blond hair that spilled out and hung in his face. (So) Marcus thought with an inward grin (He likes to make an entrance.) 'What do you want?' The man offered from behind Marcus, shortly after taking a drag of his cigarette. He was calm, collected...shrouded in mystery. 'I'd like to think,” Marcus began seemingly unfazed'That this conversation can be about what 'We' want...and not so one sided as what my needs are...please be seated' Soon he'd found a seat across from Marcus, and red pupils flashed from beneath the brim of his bowler. 'We're busy, Marcus...it's rather late.' Inwardly Marcus chuckled, his sources had been correct...this man wasn't alone in his head. 'My apologies,' he said. Not really putting any feeling into it, 'But I think you can make time for this bit of business...no lets not call it business...as a matter of fact let's play a game...a word association game...are you interested in playing?' 'Sure...' He offered, 'That is, if you'll stop wasting our time and get to the point.' 'By the time we're done my friend I think you'll find your time has been well spent...but now, on with the game.' Marcus said with an almost playful smile. 'First word...Ron. ...Second Word...Starr. Add one and one and tells me what pops into your head' '...We don't know what you're talking about.' The man said, placing the cigarette between slightly parted lips. (Bluffing) Marcus thought to himself (Which would be acceptable if this were a poker game). 'Perhaps I am mistaken then...which I admit does not happen often...but tell me my friend, why do I feel that I am not mistaken? Why do I feel that one of us is not being honest?' 'We have a feeling you'll tell us,' he offered. If the bowler on his head hadn't created such a shadow, you'd see the man grin. 'So get to the point, cock sucker.' A hardening around the edges of his eyes only momentarily is the only sign of his anger, slowly a smile spreads across his face 'Very well, we'll get right to the point.' Reaching into his coat pocket he withdraws a small black tape recorder and holds it up in the air...reaching down he picks up his glass and takes a small sip of whatever he's drinking pressing play at the same time. The static sound of a tape player's footage rolling, obediently before the words were spoken: 'We've decided...we're gonna take Ron out. For too long he's been some...some, for too long he's been running around, shooting the shit with his buddy Seabass. For too long he's been bragging he made me... Wrong fuck face. You didn't make us...and now, we'll prove it.' When the message has finished Marcus presses the stop button with a soft click...there is a tone of finality to it The man offered a nonchalant shrug, 'That voice could be anyone’s. Just because the voice was hoarse, you instantly assume it's ours.' The man offered, adding a bit of retort shortly after. 'Fuck you, and your game.' Marcus gave a theatrical sigh as he slid the recorder back into his pocket 'You shouldn't be worried about what I think my friend...now if it was myself in your position, and let me say I'm glad I'm not...but if it was I sitting there I would be much more concerned about what Mister Starr will say when this turns up on his desk...I think Mister Starr wont like this game, don't you agree?' 'Who cares what Mister Starr likes? As far as we're concerned, Mister Starr may like the game 'Fuck Marcus in the ass with a spiky, metal dildo'. I don't give two fucks and a cancer stick.' He obviously wasn't going to budge. Suicidal? Most likely. (Amazing) Marcus thought (This pissant has no idea how quickly I could crush him) 'Now I think my friend you do yourself a discredit...you snub my words, my warnings...when the simple fact is I want to help you...' he trails off his face an unreadable mask; but his mind delivering another move in the mental game of chess he was currently engaged in, 'How long do you think you'll last on your own my friend?' 'Christ Jesus, would you get OFF our dick!?' He flicked the rest of the cigarette at Marcus' face. 'Besides, you can't fucking offer us anything we don't already have. So go to Hell, cock sucker, we don't fucking need you.' With a deft movement the black gloved hand plucked the remains of the cigarette from the air, 'I can assure you...there is a reservation for me in Hell, one I will gladly accept once my duties are done here my friend...but my duties are far from finished whereas yours...' he lets the words trail off 'Besides, my friend. I'm not offering you something you already have...I'm offering you something you want...with a one hundred percent chance of success margin.' 'What do we want, swami man?' He offered simply, not bothering to show concern or care for his situation. It was most likely that he didn't have the concern or care to show. 'For one second let us pretend we're on a level-playing field,' Marcus said his voice remaining calm. Calm, like an AIDS virus slowly yet surely overtaking it's victims body. 'You are a man with a plan...you want this plan to succeed...yet you alone stand against an impenetrable wall that you cannot hope to get through alone...if help was available, wouldn't you take it? Especially if it meant success?'. He stared into the faintly glowing red orbs as he added, 'Now I know this is difficult for you, but for one moment stop acting like the best part of you ran down the inside of your mothers legs and think about your future.' '...What the fuck's in it for you?' Under his glasses his eyes would appear to gleam as he spoke. 'Money...Power...and toppling a tyrant who doesn't deserve what he rules. Together, my friend, we can be a formidable force...you alone however stand very little chance...you might as well piss into the wind and let it come back and slap you in the face. Because without my help, my friend, that is exactly what will happen.' 'Fine...let's get started, then.' And just like that without admission, withoutdeclaring a winner of the verbal contest…two men began to plan, a plan that would rock the PWC on its foundation…and when the time was right…topple it like the brittle house of cards it was.
