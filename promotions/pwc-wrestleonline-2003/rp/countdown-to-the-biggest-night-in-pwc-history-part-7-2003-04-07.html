Countdown to the Biggest Night in PWC History (Part 7)

7 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/7/03 Magnum Wolf blinked hard at the man sitting across from him. The guy was wearing this suit that just SCREAMED fag to Wolf. Then again, any guy who was clean cut and well dressed screamed fag to Magnum Wolf. They were sitting in a highly decorated office filled with books. Wall to wall books. A few little plaques here and there demanded respect from those reading them. It had been four or five weeks now (Wolf was losing track of time) since Karina Wolfenden had roped him into the match they'll be involved in at the Pay-Per-View. His aggravation with the situation had not been calmed at all over that period of time. Rubbing his temples, Wolf shakes his head slowly. 'There's no way that this lawsuit can lead to an injunction or something against the match Karina and I have?' Wolf somberly and slowly asked of the man, presumably his lawyer. The lawyer simply shook his head, and threw his arms into the air, 'You don't get it, Magnum. Your lawsuit is with MAX Wolfenden. Karina has nothing to do with the proceedings! Furthermore, Ron Starr has been absolutely NOT HELPFUL in any line of questioning involving the cancellation of that match. And why the hell should he be?!' Papers fly across the little drab office as Magnum swipes them out of his way. As the papers float downward to the floor, Magnum stares at them. His eye twitches slightly as he looks up from the floor. 'What in God's name is wrong with you? I've been telling you all month that there is NO WAY I want to go into my PWC Pay-Per-View debut in an exhibition match against the resident Chyna. Some bitch waltzes into a wrestling federation because she felt that she had to rebel against daddy and just gets a shot at a former FIVE STAR WRESTLING star like Magnum Wolf?! Fuck that! Fuck Starr for signing it!' Everything about Magnum shows the level of frustration he's come to. The usually calm and jovial man has lost all composure now. How telling it is of his true persona. He turns his head slightly to the right, and looks down. He's still shaking his head... but now it's a shake of disbelief, 'He came to me so long ago. Back when he was still going by the persona of the Dumb-Ass in a Stocking Cap. Plucked me from the top rankings of Primetime Central's federations, and told me that I was being handpicked... that I was being GROOMED FOR STARDOM...' Magnum has been raising his hands to the heavens. Slightly higher with each item he lists... but as he gets to the end of the list, his hands fall. They slap the table, causing the lawyer to jump slightly. '... for naught.' Once again, punctuating each word the hands raise higher and higher, as though he's praising a God he doesn't believe in, 'Then he shows up again. Tells me to ignore the calls from OSW. Ignore the calls from PCW. Ignore the calls from RUSH... as though he had to tell me that one... he said that PWC was going to open in Trenton, New Jersey. Guilt tripped me about owing him my career. Told me that if I refused him, he'd black ball me in PCW and OSW. WHAT CHOICE DID I HAVE, RICH?!' The lawyer, Rich as it were, just stares blankly at Wolf, with an 'I don't know' look on his face. Magnum isn't deterred in the least. 'He told me that I'd be a star. That FSW was nothing but a flop... and that they were fools not to notice my talents sooner. But believe me when I say that it was PWC, or one of the other indy circuits for the rest of my life.' His look of disbelief quickly becomes a look of disgust, as the left side of his mouth curls into a sneer. He half chuckles now as he uses his index finger to tap the desk as he speaks, punctuating important phrases or words with a tap. 'Then SHE showed up. Karina FUCKING Wolfenden. Some snobby little prissy BITCH who treats my livlihood like a game. A mere hobby. What right does she have to come into PWC? I worked my ASS off to be here, Rich. What did she do? Tickled Ron's fancy, and worked for peanuts because DADDY was giving her money. If you want to be a woman in this business, FINE! Go out there, get breast implants, and look pretty for the fans! BEND OVER, WIGGLE YOUR MAMMARIES... whatever it is that women do to work their magic. DO THAT. But DON'T come into my federation where I work my ass off, and try to treat it like a game.' Magnum's face flushes red slightly, as he realizes his cool has been lost for quite some time now. His demeanor fades back to passive, and his smirk returns to the corners of his mouth. With one swift motion, Wolf returns to the seat that he had angrily crawled out of just moments ago during his tirade. He replaces a few papers strewn around the desk to a pile, and taps his fingers to the beat of 'The Imperial March' on the oaken table. His eyes lit up as though he had just thought of something. 'She doesn't matter anymore, though.' Beat. 'If she decides to come to Capital Punishment, I'll remove her once and for all. The target is obviously the legs. Or perhaps she'll grow some smarts I never imagined she'd grow, and accept my offer to cancel the match. After, of course, I offer her to dropping of her brother's lawsuit. Either way, I'm making money, baby!' Wolf's smirk soon degenerates into a grin, and he chuckles to himself as the man who looks like his lawyer, Rich, looks on at him. Through all the anger, and all the aggravation caused, his plan still seems to be coming together. Despite all of the setbacks, and the lost matches, Magnum is still in the best condition possible for his match at Capital Punishment. Happy. A small wink from the whacky private eye.
