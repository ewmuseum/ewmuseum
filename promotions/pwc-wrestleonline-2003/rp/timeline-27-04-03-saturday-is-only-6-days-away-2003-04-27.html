Timeline - 27/04/03 : Saturday Is Only 6 Days Away

27 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 27/04/03 As she finds ways to amuse herself in the Albany night, Karina Wolfenden is, to put it bluntly... Bored. After Capital Punishment she was given a show off, 'Family Time', which she was grateful for... But she joined PWC for a reason, fun, and sitting on the bench this week wasn't it. Just a week after she was flying off rails, over ropes, from turnbuckles, from ladders... She was left waiting for a tape of PWC TV to wing it her way. But it wouldn't be the same, she wouldn't be part of the action while looking through a TV screen at it. No plasma screen, surround sound, cinema system set-up could even bring the footage close to resembling the 1st hand experience. The week Karina spent with her family as if they'd never ever had a fallout since she was born was a nice little release from what she'd been through over the past 2 months. No having to check prices to the nearest cent, no having to arrange bills in the order of which she'd be least late paying... But just as when she was a child, it just wasn't 'her'. She just wasn't a girl born to play happy families. Why does she stay then? As much of a free spirit that she is, it would be a bit rude flying off in the middle of 'Family Fortnight' to hit the slopes in Austria or wherever, or even take a ride down the east coast for a few days to see what fun she may find. No, for them, her family, she'll stick it out for another week. After all, her mother had been caught in the middle of the Wolfenden family squabble for weeks now, she at least deserves this time with her daughter. 'I haven't seen you in years...' Karina says affectionately, wandering out under one of the beacon-style lights that are dotted around the Wolfenden estate. In her hands is cradled a skateboard, showing it's years but carrying a classic grace all the same. 'Let's see if you get better with age.'. She glides the board down onto the smooth concrete and tentatively places her right foot on it, the black rubber of her sneaker's sole sticking to the surface like glue. Almost immediately the feeling comes back to her, like riding a bike, and she's off... Nothing special, just riding around the paths that wind their way through the ornamental gardens bordering her family home. In an Evanescence t-shirt over a long sleeve grey top, coupled with baggy black jeans she isn't dressed like the high society who last occupied these lawns and paths at last night's garden party. 'This'll pass an hour or so,' she sighs, 'but it's no hitting a WolfTamer...' is then added in resignation, accepting that this would only be a short and cheap fix. They don't tell you about wrestling withdrawal symptoms when you sign up. With a casual grace she follows the paths through the chilly twilight, her pink fringed hair being pushed back tight along the flanks of her head. Shifting her weight in the blink of an eye, Karina effortlessly executes a perfect kickflip, as if she'd been practising every day of her break. For the first time on her ride, a smile breaks out across her pink lips, a small bit of pride welling up at scoring the trick with such ease. But it was no Sinful Indy Air, both the ring and slope varieties. Like a Heroin addict having to settle for a half-dose of Methadone, Karina just isn't fulfilled. Out of the blue she then busts out a heelflip, almost as if she was hoping an element of surprise would make it all the more enthralling, even to her. But alas, it's still a poor substitute. What's a casual skate against the sheer adrenaline of opening up the throttle on her Ducati, what's a kickflip compared to racing through soft, powdery snow at breakneck speed before watching the ground rotate below her as she soared off a ramp... ...And what was a heelflip next to tucking her body in close and spinning through the air to hit the Teardrop Explodes. For some unbeknown reason, Karina checks her watch, as if checking the time is going to make it Saturday already... All it does is remind her it's only been 5 minutes since she last checked. But there's one thing to give her heart, to get her through the fake comfort she'll display... ... Saturday is only 6 days away.
