A Night In This Thing Called Life

10 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/10/03 Laying on the bed that night... His ribs still aching with pain. The scared face smiled for the first time in ten years. Blood soaked his sheets as his head wouldn't stop bleeding. Backstage that night Vendetta had asked him if he was all right after the match. He of course replied yes.. This is the best I have felt in years Clay... Well good Winky.. Those were the only words the two had spoken but they meant a lot to Masahiro Zangetsu. To Clay it may have been a simple gesture but to Masahiro it was the first person in his life time that actually noticed his pain and worried about his health.. More than that though... This was the first time he was seen as a hero.. And flying through the air eliminating himself but destroying Sebastian Toomes in the process... He had heard a louder roar at that moment than at any time during his cage fighting days... Masahiro Rolled over slightly in his bed. The bed itself was a simple mattress with a white sheet in an empty room. The room was one of three in the small New Jersey apartment.. A Kitchen a living room and a bathroom.. All were empty except the living room that played two parts, doubling as Masahiro's bedroom. One other item joined Masahiro in this barren dark room... A bottle of cheap vodka. His hand reached out in the dark and gripped the neck pulling it close and taking swig before pouring a good amount onto his bleeding forehead. Sebastian Toomes may have cost me a match but I cost him his ego... And that is all he has in the end.... The man's stomach suddenly growled in fury as he sat up in pain.. Ok, ok I'll go get something to eat.. The man stood up slowly pulling on the only clean pair of pants he had left of four pairs. A tight black PWC shirt followed as he slipped on his wrestling boots and pulled down the pant legs over them.. Well isn't this fun.. I can get a huge roar from a crowd but I can't afford a pair of sneakers.. Masahiro walked into the bathroom and opened up the vanity mirror grabbing a wad of cash and sorting through it. Pulling a five out and setting it back down he muttered to himself slowly. Fifty bucks left till the end of the week huh... Masahiro sighed and stared his own one eye visage in the mirror. Winky.. heh... A quick spin on his heel and Masahiro was out the door. His neighbors constantly stared as if they thought he wouldn't catch them looking. The scar across his face was an attention grabber so he didn't care. Out his door it was quite a run to the nearest place of food service. Never will this be a painful memory.. Success or not I will always look back on this night and smile.... Masahiro turned around a street corner and slowed his pace as he stepped up to the glass door of a McDonald's.. A step through the door and for the first time since he left the arena he felt warm.. Welcome to McDonald's can I take your order? A cheese burger small fries and a small drink.. $3.67 sir. Masahiro handed the girl the five dollar bill and got his change. seconds later the girl handed him a cup and his food. Domo.. Huh? I apologize.. Thank you.. Masahiro got his drink and sat down in the playroom area to watch the children play. A strange calm came from being near children. As it was he was never allowed to experience the things he watched and maybe that was why. The gleeful smiles of joy filled him with warmth. As he ate slowly he suddenly felt a tug at his arm and turned slowly to see a small oriental boy staring up at him. Your... Your Masahiro Zangetsu aren't you? Masahiro smiled warmly and nodded his head. I TOLD YOU GUYS! Three other children all talking suddenly ran over and stopped next to the oriental boy. Are you really him? Yes I am.. We have watched PWC TV both times it's been on.. Your our favorite.. Our dads all like Magnum Wolf, Clay Vendetta, and Jason O'neil. But we think your the best.. Uh Mista Zangetsu.. Think we could get your autograph? Masahiro opened his one functioning eye in shock and nodded yes to the smallest boy as all four ran off. Masahiro quickly finished off his food and took a sip of his drink before the boys could return. He stood up slowly and kneeled as the boys ran up. Who should I sign these to. Shin Tommy Mark Jimmy Masahiro took the PWC headshots of himself from the boys and signed each of them.. I take it you boys went to one of the events? We were there last week for the Frog splash. Yeah we waited an hour after the match to see you but the guys in back said that you barely stand cuz of blood loss.. Oh I'm really sorry about that kids but the backstage man was right.. I really couldn't stand until three hours after the doors closed and only Clay stayed to see if I was ok.. This is the reason why we put disclaimers on the show though. One of the children cringed a moment as Masa raised his working eyebrow and raised his hand to his forehead. His hands came away red as he reached for a napkin and covered it over his forehead. Sorry about that.. It really was great meeting you kids but I should probably get home and take care of this. Masahiro laughed and shook all the kids hands before standing up and grabbing his tray. The long walk home his faced was framed with a smile wider than the last. And as he slid into his bed that night he thought one last thought before falling asleep.. I Have Fans.. Who Appreciate My Efforts....
