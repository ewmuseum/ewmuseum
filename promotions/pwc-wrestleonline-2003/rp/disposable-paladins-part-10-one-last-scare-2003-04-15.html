Disposable Paladins - Part 10 - One Last Scare

15 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 15/04/03 Part 10: 'Sweet sweet Adriane' he whispered through his hoarse claspy voice. Whatever charade Melvin Peterson had created of him being a young gullible man who wasn't ready for the harsh realisations that reality would bare for him, let alone what true police work would bare on him had been shrugged off like a jacket. Just like a snake shedding it's skin. 'If you thought that I was cynical, psychotic, even crazy, I would not blame you my child. I would forgive you, for it is society that has wound it narrow minded web over your eyes, preventing you from opening your mind to what really lurks in this world.' Tanya fidgeted in the chair and continued to wrestle with the rope used to tie her feet and hands together. He didn't seem to mind that she was doing this right in front of him. In fact he seemed to enjoy watching her struggle. 'I admire your determination' he said while twirling a long knife through his palm, an indication of the fate that would await Tanya should she eventually brake free of her entrapment. She took the hint for what it was worth, and relaxed her muscles while seething under the duct tape that covered her mouth. 'I bet you want a motive?' inquired Melvin. His usual greasy hair was now bone dry and bearing down scruffily across his fringe. His usual clean presentable face was now riddles with early stages of stubble. It was hard to imagine Melvin and puberty ever meeting each other acquaintance, but he had obviously hidden their relationship extremely well. 'Yet I feel that this world would be far more less complex without motives, wouldn't you agree?' Tanya simply stared with hatred in her eyes. 'Maybe not. Either way, let's just say that me and your significant other are playing a little game. A game that he took far a few many a year ago. A game at which I am about to bring to an abrupt end.' Melvin aka Peter could see his words were having no immediate or lasting effect on Tanya, and so he loomed over her slowly before leaning in to her. His hot breath brushing against her cheeks as she turned her head.. 'I could hurt you, badly. If I wasn't waiting to plan my next move on lover boy I'd gut you open like a pig right now and watch as your entrails spilled across the carpet. I'd dissect you methodically before burying you underneath these very floorboards. Nobody would find you for years. Then if and when they eventually did you'd be nothing more than just a bag of bones.' Tanya's eyes watered slightly with tears as Peter grinned while twirling the knife gently across her throat and down her arms. 'Shhhhh' he whispered sadistically. 'Don't cry.' She clenched her eyes in fear and misery. 'Don't be a scardy cat..... don't be like Gideon' Bauerman pulled up outside Peter's house. as quietly as he could. He and Gideon then sat out there for a few tense seconds before Gideon yanked at the passenger door. 'NO!' Bauerman yelled. Gideon turned to meet his eyes with confusion. 'You wait here' Gideon looked at him with an impatient look that reeked of the fact that would leave the car as soon as Bauerman was out of eye and earshot. Bauerman slowly rolled his eyes, before nodding. 'Fine. You take the back. Gideon didn't reply, and he and Bauerman slowly exited the vehicle. As Gideon walked to the back of the grey and seemingly malevolently fabricated house, he felt the fear clamp tightly around his head and heart like a vice. Yet he walked on. He walked back into a childhood nightmare that he had carelessly forgotten. But one another tortured soul had carried on living nearly 15 years. ' You know he never used to be like he is know.' Peter whispered. 'I mean fucked up beyond all recognition.' Tanya looked away, her eyes avoiding Peter's and looking sharply as the wind swept through the window and alarmed them both. Peter turned his head sharply before storming over and slamming it shut. 'Thought I closed this.' He looked back at Tanya, and her eyes facing the walls that were shrouded in shadows. 'Anyway, where was I?' Peter walked back just in front of her on a small rocking horse especially designed for children. 'Ah yes. He was never like he is now. I mean even I was shocked when I seen him face to face and in the flesh. I mean damn, that guy has issues now that even I couldn't have dared imagined existed,' Tanya avoided his eyes, Peter had already grown frustrated and angry when that happened. He longed for eye contact from her. 'Come on now my little peach!' Tanya looked over Peter's shoulder with emotionally charged eyes. 'Don't shy away from me like that!' Tanya couldn't pry her eyes away. 'Look at me when I talk to you!' She fixated on it, in disbelief and uncertainty. 'God damn you!' He rose from the rocking horse. Tanya's eyes grew wide..... 'Do I have to start tonight's festivities early on you?!' 'The festivities have already begun...' Peter stopped, and dropped the knife on the floor in the process. All the while Tanya's eyes never left Gideon;s since he got in through the window. '...Old friend.' Peter turned around slowly, his eyes welling up as fear and smothered them. From behind him a cold stone face emerged, A face from a past he wanted to end. 'No...' Peter whispered. Gideon kept coming towards him. 'No!' he yelled and withdrew the gun. Gideon stopped. The gun blast rang through the house. Peter's body flinched violently before he fell to his knees.. Martin Bauerman had shot him right in the side of the chest. Gideon looked up at Bauerman, his eyes unerving even though seconds ago they had been filled with the calm knowledge that he was about to die. He remembered the images of his traumatic life flashing past him in seconds before Bauerman had shot him. He remembered not feeling a thing.... 'Get her untied!' yelled Gideon while pointing to Tanya, the shock still evident in his voice. Bauerman obliged and quickly went to work while Gideon knelt next to Peter. 'What about him?' Bauerman asked while untying Tanya. 'I'll take care of this' replied Gideon. Peter was resting against the floor of his room. It was only then that Gideon noticed the decoration of the room. Pictures of animated football and baseball players graced the walls. An old rocking horse lay next to Tanya who was being untied. Various baseball cards and Action Men dolls occupied the shelves along with various comics. Along with the evident sculptures of mythological creatures, most of which were of half man and half bull. He had forgotten of Peter's fascination with them as a child. 'Jesus...' Gideon muttered while looking around in disbelief. 'You never left that day that our friendship ended, did you?' Peter shook his head slowly while sobbing. 'All I wanted...' he winced while whispering with tears streaming down his face. 'Was one last scare.' Gideon nodded slowly. 'Well you got it, old friend.' 'End it Gideon.' Peter whispered, and slid the gun to him with hopeful and wishful eyes. 'I'm already broken. Don't let them break me even more.' Gideon lifted the gun, he himself was choked up with all the events. 'Peter. You killed in an attempt to get back at me. You took away the lives of people who had nothing to do with me.' Peter sobbed some more. 'You'll have more peace in hell,,,than you will have on this earth with me still occupying it.' Peter nodded approvingly. 'Goodbye old friend.' The gunshot rang out. Peter's arm fell limp. Bauerman and Tanya looked up in shock. Gideon rose to his feet like a phoenix from the burnt soiled ashes of a past life. They finally knew it was over.
