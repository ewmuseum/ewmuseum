Five Simple Questions [2]: What Am I?

25 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 25th, 2003 I can’t help but think that I am a shell of my former self, molded by my father into the puppet that he has always wanted me to be. I feel as if I’m trapped inside of a cage that cannot be unlocked. I beg and plea for him to let me out, but that would mean that he would actually have to be a father for once and act as if he cared about me. This, followers, is an impossible dream. A dream that has long been crushed by his hateful actions towards me. People view me on television and think that I’m a merely a wrestler, or sports entertainer as we’re called these days. The truth is that I, as ‘The Investment’ that is, am so much more, or so I’m told. I’m tomorrow’s superstar. I’m my father’s way out of debt and back into luxury’s lap. I’m dead on the inside. On the outside, however, I am a machine simply because I’m bound to be that way by a signed contract that I was ordered to put my signature on by the man who insists on making my life a living hell, more so then it was when I was just his son rather then his son and the pawn in his sick game which most would call his life. In the end, what I am isn’t definite, at least not in my eyes. Maybe you should ask my father. He seems to know everything, doesn’t he?
