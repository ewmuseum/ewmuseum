Disposable Paladins - Epilogue

15 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Same as it was last time. 'So everything is fine?' asked Bauerman as he and Gideon stood face to face just outside the police station. 'As well as I can be expected.' 'Good.' 'Got your face in the picture I see' Gideon said while smirking. 'You deserved the credit' replied Bauerman with guilt etched all over his face. 'No. It wouldn't have been possible without you.' Bauerman nodded reluctantly before reaching inside his coat and withdrawing a package. 'I took the liberty of getting you a little something. Something I think you deserve, something you should have been given a long time ago.' Martin handed a confused Gideon a small brown folder, which he opened cautiously before reading through it's contents carefully. 'A social security number? An actual record?' 'An actual life' Martin Bauerman chirped in. 'You finally exist' Gideon took a deep sigh before handing him the package back. 'Burn it' he said. Bauerman looked confused. 'I don't mean to seem ungrateful Martin, but at the end of the day I've become used to being nonexistent. It's just the way I like things now I guess.' 'A paladin?' Gideon smirked. 'A Disposable Paladin you could say. We both are.' Martin nodded before grabbing the brown folder and tearing it in half straight down the middle before proceeding to tear the rest into shreds. 'Thank you Gideon' He extended his hand, and this time Gideon didn't hesitate and shook it. 'Look after everyone that you need to' Bauerman said. 'Be thankful their still with you' 'I am...... and I will' He replied. Bauerman nodded, before turning and walking off back into the warm comfort of the police station. Gideon watched him going in, before turning back to the taxi Tanya was waiting in for him. Tanya...the woman he had saved. the woman he and Martin Bauerman had saved. 'Disposable paladins' he said to himself one last time. 'Aint that the truth.'
