PWC TV Commercial 8

9 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Originally aired on WGTW TV-48 in Philadelphia, PA at 7:15pm EST Thursday, January 9th. The scene fades into the a city streets scene. There is a gritty feel about the film you're looking at, almost as though it's dirty. The darkness almost hides the fact that the film is in black and white. A peice of paper floats down the street, and rustles in the wind. A booming voice comes on over the scene. "These are desperate times." The scene cuts to a homeless shelter, where you can see hundreds of filthy rag-covered people laying on the floor, with little poor kids running in and out of the adults. Giggling. "Times when men and women alike are beaten in the streets." Now, we watch as a scene showing a woman being attacked by three or four big men is replaced by another scene of a man in the same sort of situation. The four men have the lone man on the ground now, and are kicking him in the ribs. The man on the ground coughs loudly, spewing red liquid everywhere as his eyes roll up into his head. "Times when you fear for your child's life as he walks down to the bus stop." A small child stands with his mother, at the door of their suburban home. She hands him his lunch, and shoves him on his way lightly. The boy takes off running towards the bus at the end of the walk... but before he is able to reach the sidewalk, a man dives out of the bushes, and snatches the kid. His mother watches in horror, screaming for all she's worth, as her son is stolen. "Times when someone has to step up." Presently, we find a fight going on with someone dressed in a pair of tight blue jeans and a white shirt buttoned halfway up fighting off a gang of 5 men. He ducks one punch, and throws one at the man to his right, sending him crashing into the wall. Then he quickly turns, and blocks another punch by grabbing one assailant's arm. He wrenches the arm, and tosses the man over his back, and to the floor. As he thrusts his hands into a karate stance, the scene freezes. "Times which call for a hero such as..." > Suddenly, it sounds as if the needle of a record player has been drug across a 45. "BURT REYNOLDS?!" The same man from the last video, dressed in the same blue jeans and white collared shirt buttoned halfway up stands in the shot now, with a smaller man dressed in a white suit with a t-shirt under it kneels down in front of him. In block letters that are blue and metallic, the letters "MGS" appear above their heads. "Hungry Like the Wolf" plays in the background now, as the scene fades out, and you return from commercial break to the show you were watching.
