Countdown To The Biggest Night In PWC History (Part 4)

7 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-7-2003 ”Hey Gunner, WATCH THIS!” The olden warrior, Gunner Gojoh, watched on from the emerald green grass as Neon pranced around in the dingy and battered ring that was “The Ring of Pain”. The ropes looked gnawed and stringy, exposing pieces of the underlying metal. The mat was a cocktail of dirt, blood, and sweat; all of those elements ground in from decades upon decades of training. Gunner trained numerous “would-be” Deathmatchers…only a few survived the brutality of his training…. And his greatest prodigy stood high and mighty atop the turnbuckle in his very ring. Neon fixed his goggles to his face, pointing out to a crowd that wasn’t there as he leaped backwards from the turnbuckle. The rotation was excellent, Neon smoothly turning in mid-air as his body streaked past the shimmering sun in mid-move. His chest was parallel with the mat as he made his descent, finally crashing to the canvas as the ring rattled violently. Hopping back up to his feet and pointing to his mentor, Neon’s yell was filled with exhilaration as he said, ”Beat that old man! Come on, show me what your workin’ with!” Gunner turned from the young Neon, running his fingers through his thinning, wild hair as his head slowly looked up to the powder blue sky. It seemed as if Gunner’s mind was elsewhere….lost in his own thought with his broad shoulders facing the youth, making a proverbial wall between them. ”I don’t do that anymore Neon….I can’t do that anymore,” Gunner replied humbly, wiping the sweat from his brow with his calloused palm, feeling like a bag of rocks ran across his forehead. “Gunner, this ain’t the time for that bro. Usually…you’re the one who acts like the teacher, now it’s my turn. The biggest night in our lives is coming up. The chance to prove we’re more then just cult favorites in Japan….isn’t that what we wanted Gun-Gun? But we’ll NEVER prove how good we are if you start doubting yourself and saying you CAN’T! That’s not the Gun-Gun I know. That’s not the man I grew up with. That’s not the man I begged to be trained by,” expelled Neon with an unusually heartfelt spiel. Gripping the ropes and opening them up for Gunner’s hopeful entrance to the ring, Neon asked his mentor once more, ”Gunner…please. For me at least…can you show me how it’s done?” The moment was tense…neither man wanting to budge. The Beast’s feet were ground into the grass, staked in the dirt like an immobile scarecrow. Neon held steady, hoping deep inside that Gunner would push his fear aside and come between the ropes… And that he did. His steps were slow and steady until finally reaching the apron, entering the ring via the pathway Neon crated for him. Slow, deliberate steps were taken towards the nearest turnbuckle as Gunner climbed slowly…rope by rope. First…. Second…. And finally to the third. Now mounted on the very top rope in a crouched position, Gunner looked about as he was slowly reminded of the altitude and the sheer feeling of elevation being on the top rope. His lungs filled with higher, fresher air as his body rose to a vertical base until his 287 pound frame stood tall upon the top turnbuckle. Neon watched on, a slight smirk overcoming his face as he took witness of his mentor…. And he witnessed his mentor as he flipped from the top, Gunner’s rotation rugged and without grace as the height of his leap saw his feet touch the sky. His body plummeted to the mat, Gunner putting his chest and knees out as his body SLAMMED to the canvas…the moonsault complete. His knees were wracked in pain, stinging “on and off” as if someone was flipping a switch inside him. Yet he refused to let that pain show through to a clapping Neon, who remembered at that moment WHY Gunner was part of the reason he wanted in this cruel business. ”Beautiful man….but nowhere near as good as mine,” playfully complimented Neon as his clapping continued. Gunner slowly rose up to his feet, merely nodding his head in appreciation of his prodigy’s praise as he said to Neon, “Just wait till your past 30…” Gunner proved what was needed….that if he wanted to, he could still do it. And if he has the will to win, when it really counts….then victory at the biggest night in PWC history is very much attainable, no matter how old the beastial warrior has become.
