Last Night In Japan

2 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/2/03 Thought's raced through his mind as blood splattered up off his opponent's head. Damn him... Damn him to hell.. Damn him for spoiling what could have been a break... Fists pounding repeatidly into a precise position will break bone, mangle cartilidge, and end life. These are the goals of a Japanese Cage Match. Masahiro crouched over his opponent still driving fist after fist into his skull. Men filtered into the ring lined with barbed wire and steel. Words came at him from far away like screams in the night. Masahiro! Stop! You've killed him.... The last words sinking in further than anything he had ever heard. His fists stopped pumping as he stood up covered in brain matter and blood. Wha... what have I done? Two men grab Masahiro by his arms and escort him to a set of black double doors. In the dark room behind the doors only breath can be heard for a few moments when suddenly a splash of water sounds out as Masahiro stands drenched. Masahiro what the hell was that. You have NEVER let things get that far. I... I don't know... Murder isn't something new to the one eyed demon or.. Dokugan Oni if you will. But thats another story for another time. What the hell drove you to that Masa.. What is wrong with you? Sebastian Toomes and Chris Campbell... Who? Nevermind that.. I have to go.. Tell the 'Boss' I won't be back to Japan for quite sometime.. a hesitant silence fills the room as the second man finally speaks up You know he won't allow that Mr. Zangetsu. You make him way too much money to just let you 'walk away' I don't care if he likes it.. Let him chase me if I mean that much. I have more important matters to deal with. The dark room illuminates as the door opens and the shaved head of Masahiro Zangetsu turns back. You two should really get out of this whole 'Yakuza' Thing.. It will kill you sooner or later..
