To Think or Not To Think

13 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-13-03 Yeah, the unthinkable has happened, but why in the world would people even think of blaming me for taking the offer that lay in front of my face. An open contract set up just to walk into the promotion and work as I pleased, but I’m not that greedy. As much as I don’t like Starr, I go by his rules for the most part. I’ve also found myself having to explain to people just how in the world that clause found its way into my contract that if PURE closed that I would be offered a spot in PWC automatically. How stupid can people really be? The two promotions worked together pretty much. I mean take a look at some of the people who were on loan from PCW, that was the same thing I was under, only mine contract wasn’t as known to the public as everyone else. I’m rather sneaky I guess you could say. When it comes to things like this, I’d much rather keep it under my hat rather than spread the word throughout a community of people. I’m the type of person who likes to keep his fans on their toes and by staying hidden for several weeks after PURE closed, that just kept them wondering where I was and if I would show up anywhere again. Sitting back, I would analyze my contract and wonder if I should take it, or just rip it up and be on my own once again, seeking employment on the free agency market. After all, I could have easily gotten a place back into Old School Entertainment, I did have a history there, but I didn’t want that. From the looks of it I would have ended up in that Fight Division and that isn’t where my expertise lies. Then I took Primetime Championship Wrestling into consideration, one of the only promotions I haven’t been affiliated with and one of the longest running, but then I thought to myself, what purpose would I play if I was to ever set foot in one of their rings? I didn’t have a history with any of their workers and most likely I wouldn’t have survived with all of the conflict surrounding my name in that region of the wrestling world. Then I turned back to that contract once again, and began to analyze it. The beginning of Primetime Wrestling Circuit wasn’t much of an attraction for me during my stay in PURE, but once I saw the likes of Ron Starr entering; I knew I had to come into the PWC. The history between Starr and myself runs thicker than blood and if only you people knew just exactly what type of hell he’s put me through. Well, you will, soon enough.
