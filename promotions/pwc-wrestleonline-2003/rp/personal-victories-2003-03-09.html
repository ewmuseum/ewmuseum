Personal Victories

9 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-9-2003 'WEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEE ARE THE CHAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAMP-YUUUUUUUUUUNS MY FRIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIEND!!!' The gritty Boardwalk Hall locker room was more then just a locker room that night...it was a shrine of celebration as three men absorbed their victory that night. PWC TV saw the debuts of the Leprechuan, the return of the thought to be dead Frankie Bones, and hard-hitting ladder match action from five men over just the MERE TV title contract. But all those happenings could now overshadow the physical and internal victories for three men.... Masahiro Zangetsu, Gunner' Beast' Gojoh, and Ryo 'Neon' Kazaki.... Sitting in two steel folding chairs, both lop-sided and wobbly but serviceable, were Gunner Gojoh and Masa Zangetsu, both men dripping with sweat and spent for energy....yet smiles were tattooed on the warriors faces as they simply sat hunched over, looking to each other. In the background, young Neon pranced about like a king....a king to be celebrated by the world and beloved for his efforts, pouring rivers of bubbling Sprite from seperate cans over his braided sky blue hair, closing his eyes and absorbing the win as he continued to sing off-key in his unfitting Queen rendition, 'WEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEE ARE THE CHAMP-YUUUUUUUUUUUUUUNS........of the wooooooorld...' Normally, Gunner would shut Neon up during his overzealous victory spiel...but Gunner was too happy, too enthralled that he made it to 'the States' and did what he thought he couldn't do....get the fans behind him. His black face paint was already rubbing and peeling from the cocktail of salty sweat and tears, pieces of it already strewn in little chips on the concrete floor. Masa’s grin is one of when a general conquers a stronger, larger army…that feeling of knowing your planning was superior to that of your opponent, and he looked like a fool in the process. These two met, eyes to eye in this case, Gunner saying, “So you developed a strong mind to go with that strong body....you’ve really changed Masa.” Masa grinned, modestly accepting the praise from the olden warrior seated beside him that was Gunner. Leaning back against the cold steel chair, Masa reached down and took hold of a unopened bottle of Naya water, unscrewing the cap as he took a slight sip to refresh himself. “I’m impressed as well Gunner, I thought your knees would’ve took you out by now….old man,” cracked Masa as Gunner playfully sneered in return. “My God…DID YOU SEE ME! I MADE A FORMER OSW CHAMPION LOOK LIKE MY BIZNITCH! And that Campbell kid couldn’t even hold my jock! THE STATES AREN’T READY FOR ME!! Just give me a week guys….I’ll be getting title shots out my ass,” oozed Neon with confidence in the background, steadily pouring more and more Sprite over his head while dancing about in youthful exuberance, Neon’s trail marked by the Sprite puddles on the floor. Tonight was a personal victory for the three warriors in that room. For one, he made his impact and showed off skills that the world is yet to even see the beginning of. For another, he proved he still had what it took….fighting age and an aching body. For another, he bested two men who were thorns in his side….using his brain and his body, a duo few dare to take advantage of. Victories get not better then personal ones. “This is only the beginning though, old man. I hope your prepared for this place….it’s more cutthroat then those Deathmatch fed’s, I’ll tell you that much,” warned Masa as he downed more of his water, his one eye flashing an annoyed stare at the young Neon in front of him, the kind a parent would give a child when he acts out in public. Neon could care less as he broke in his rendition of a Cryp Walk of sorts to celebrate his victory. Gunner simply shook his head in shame as he tossed his empty water bottle at Neon’s leg, stopping the young spirit in his tracks as The Beast exclaimed in a scolding tone, “Would it hurt for you to ACT like an adult for a change?” “Psssh, I’m not the one who wears kiddie make-up to the ring GRANDPA! Don’t be a Neon-Hater…,” he retorted with a defiant smirk, bobbing his head to an imaginary beat in his head as Masa snickers quietly while looking to Gunner, the Beast folding his arms and releasing a deep breath before saying, ”If the bible-thumpers, fake racial icons, Italian mobsters, masked monsters, or undead thugs don’t kill me….he will.” But Masa nor Gunner could truly be mad about Neon’s self-celebration….the One-Eyed Demon and The Beast wanted to jump about and do their own personal victory dances….Neon was the only one who was brave enough to act on it. However, it didn’t matter how the three chose to celeberate…one thing was certain… Victory, for that night…was theirs.
