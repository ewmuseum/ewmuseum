So it begins.

7 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/7/03 It’s a small basement in the room of an apartment building that has been converted into a war room/meeting room for the Revival Independent Socialist Party of New Jersey. There are about fifteen white, clean cut, protestant males sitting around a dry erase board tacked up on the back wall next to the please turn off the lights when leaving hand written sign. The man armed with the black dry erase marker drawing up wartime strategies is Hoyt Williams. Hoyt is dressed like just about everybody else wearing carpenter jeans, a polo t-shirt, and a clean buzzed haircut. As a matter of fact the only thing that makes Hoyt stand out besides his perfect posture and riveting body is the PWC title hanging over his shoulder. His hand’s fly around and do half the talking for him as he rants on about how and why we they are the chosen people. The crowd is mesmerized by his words and hypnotized by his hands as he continues on about how they will suppress their enemies on their path to utopia. “We shall not be forced into hiding by the greedy Irish. They are only jealous of our greatness because we already have the gold at the end of the rainbow.” Hoyt thunders at the crowd. Hoyt takes the TV title off his shoulder and holds it high about his head for everybody in the room to see. For it is the TV gold that makes RISP’s claims even more relevant and truthful. It is the TV title that makes Hoyt better then 99 percent of the PWC roster and he has the proof to back it up. “Unlike other tainted belts that the PWC has I defend our gold more then anybody. I give this title more then just monetary or status value but something a lot more. For I was chosen by God himself, high up in the heavens, to hold this gold. Why, you ask?? Because we are the soldiers in the army of my father and your lord who will wash this land clean like the great floods of the past.” Hoyt screams as he paces around the back of the room. His eyes are on fire and his voice is like gravel as he lays down the facts. His soldiers of God listen with great enjoyment and fulfillment as Hoyt gives them exactly what they want to hear. Lonely men with a lack of purpose in life are perfect targets for the hate and self-gratification Hoyt preaches and seeks. “Alone we are nothing but victims of modern day bureaucracy caught in a web spun by “equality” which is nothing but a buzz word that mean lets favor the bad guys. Lets give our jobs, scholarships, and dreams to the less deserving simply because they are not from America or God’s chosen children. Well my brothers I for one am sick or being taken advantage of but I alone cannot fight the lord’s fight. But together we can succeed and take back his land and rebuild the garden of Eden.” Hoyt yells at the men like a father yelling at his son. The deal is now sealed Hoyt has the men in the palm of his hand and his army grows. More foot soldiers mean more recruitment opportunities. Friends have friends who know others who will join once the seed has been planted. “So let me hear you where you stand?” Hoyt asks the rhetorical question. “RISP” Thunder the men. “What?” Hoyt asks. “RISP…RISP…RISP” the men start to chant louder and louder till it becomes deafening. Hoyt smiles as he joins in on the chant. =w=
