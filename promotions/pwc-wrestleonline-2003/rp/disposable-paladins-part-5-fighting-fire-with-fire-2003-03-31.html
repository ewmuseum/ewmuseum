Disposable Paladins - Part 5 - Fighting Fire With Fire

31 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 31/03/03 Part 5 'Get those bastards away from there!' yelled the tense voice of Martin Bauerman. 'Now Jerry! If the press get any photos here I'll wipe my ass with your pay checks for the next month, NOW MOVE!' The red and blue sirens of the nearby police cars at the latest crime scene shone on his face through the dark cold night, the red sirens adding emphasis to the anger and rage in his eyes. 'I want this alley cordoned off NOW!' 'Bauerman!' bellowed a random voice amongst the numerous shade of white coats that were the on hand forensics. 'I'm coming, what is it?' Then he saw him, seemingly appearing out of nowhere as usual. Amazing really, he could still blend in amongst the numerous shades of imperial pure white coats which donned the forensics. The ill sensing wind sweeping his long black hair out of his face as he stood looking down at what was nothing more than a massacre. 'Glad you could make it' shouted Bauerman as he ignored the forensics and walked over to Gideon. He felt somewhat relieved that he was here, even when Gideon didn't answer him or look up to him. Bauerman looked down to the desecrated body, at least what was left of it anyway. 'Not a pretty sight is it?' 'How long has she been here?' '2 hours roughly, passer by called it in. Not really something you want to find on your way home from work' 'No shit' Both men stared meaninglessly down at the victims body, the bright flash of the reporters cameras highlighting their pale sorrowful expressions under the bleak night. 'We've found the other arm!' shouted on the few detectives on hand at the crime scene. Bauerman thrust his hands behind his head and rested them there as he scrunched his face up in dismay and grief. Yet Poe did nothing. No drastic facial expression, no violent mood swings. He just stared. Stared deep into those bulging red eye sockets looking up intriguingly at him; and let's face it, you at least expect eyes. Gideon had seen some gruesome happenings in his time, he'd been behind most of them. Yet he'd always viewed the aftermath, and he'd always grown use to that vacant stare. The intoxicating white abyss of the sockets staring accusingly up at you, all with the same look, a look that blamed you. They all read 'You could have stopped this, I'm here because of you, you heartless coward. May my demise weigh on your already heavy conscience.' He'd grown use to that stare. Now though, now it was gone, and in a sick vile way, he was glad the killer removed the eyes leaving nothing but the hollow emptiness behind those eyes that had viewed the lunacy in the killer look up at him. He now officially knew he was too drawn in to turn back. 'Who's the trench coat?!' came a reporters voice. Bauerman caught the owner and leapt at him like a wild savage animal. 'Get out of here?!' 'Is he helping you on the case?!' 'OUT OF HERE NOW!!' Bauerman turned sharply and hoped Gideon had taken the initiative, He had. He was gone. Forensics, police, they barely even knew he was there. He doubted they would even remember he was there by the time they were shutting the body up in the morgue and even before the ink had dried on the 'Jane Doe' signature which would probably be attached to her foot. At least that was still attached to the body. 'Never thought I'd be happy for that bastard to do that' he uttered. There and then, Bauerman knew that even up against the killer, this plague, he at least had The Shade by his side. One sick nobody against another. In his mind the odds were now even. Now things would really take shape.
