Timeline - 08/06/03 : A Time For Reflection

8 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/06/03 Karina Wolfenden leans forward, hands on the sink basin taking the majority of her weight. Closely, she inspects her own face, the visage she has carried for near 24 years... She'd known every square inch of it before, now it's changed. Her nose is still the same, with the prominent bridge that leads down in a slope to the rounded tip. She'd always liked her nose, it suited her. Karina's finger now runs along her top lip, creating a pleasant tingle. Not as full or luscious as she might like, but she's happy with them... Again, they just suited her. Exploring her own face in this reflective time, Karina's finger now twiddles with her hair... Winding the golden stands around before releasing them. Shiny and with natural volume, she has no complaints... Even the multiple and highly frequent dye-jobs couldn't taint her mane. Casually, Karina's eyes drift away from her hair until they meet their own reflection. Most people have said the first thing they notice about Karina is the sparkling green of her irises, which given how brightly she dresses surely says something about them. Unlike some, there's no small hint of brown, or no speckles of blue... They're pure emerald green, spectacular and stunning in equal measure. Shadowed by her brow, they glow majestically, and can't help but attract the full attention of those she converses with. It's nice for someone to look longingly at a pair of eyes for a change. This was her face, this was Karina Wolfenden... But it wasn't the Karina Wolfenden of yesterday, or the day before... Or any other day of her life so far. Paula Michelle Smith had changed that. Last night, Karina didn't want to see what was done to her face... She'd felt the burning of cut skin when probing with her finger, but she didn’t want to view it herself... Not then. For over an hour she tried to drift off into slumber, but the images she imagined would be her face wouldn't allow Karina to sleep. For someone who'd never been image conscious in her life, Karina suddenly became scared of what she'd look like. And now she could see it. The route Paula's nail took as it slashed across Karina's cheek was left for all to see, etched into her face. She has made no attempt to soften it with foundation, or try to lessen it's harshness with concealer... Karina wanted to see the worst, see it raw. So with the blankest expression that we've ever seen adorn the face of the K-Wolf, Karina gets to knew herself again. Gone was a blemish-free face, and with it went any chance Karina had of helping Paula, of rescuing her from the past she has had to endure. PMS wasn't beyond help, she just didn't want it... Especially from Karina. All the emotion, all the persistence, the research, the small hours spent awake... And what was the result for Karina? A scar across her cheek, and Paula becoming even more distant. Karina's right cheek is now a reminder of her failure, a reminder that she maybe used the wrong approach, or uttered the wrong word in a conversation that lingered in Paula's brain, growing into doubt. It's there to tell Karina every day that despite all that Paula Michelle Smith has had to endure, the thought of being aided by her was even worse. It was the ultimate slap across the face, and it left it's mark.
