American History X

27 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 27th February 2003 “Don’t concern yourself with trivial matters, that Phoenix will get exactly what the good lord Jupiter deems appropriate for his misdeeds against the Empire.” Says the toga dressed man in the library as he takes back his library card. The clerk raises an eyebrow above her plain Jane features and mother’s knitted sweater. “But I asked you if you knew about our telephone renewal service?” she replies with a hint of plain Jane ‘What the fuck’ in her voice. The Roman master shrugs and walks past her, humming a merry tune… probably something by Queen, since they’re quite good. He wanders down what appears to be a random aisle, past a kid reading a Ultimate Warrior comic next to a shelf of well out of date cheap comics and newspapers. “Ah, a bondage fan are we?” says the Roman quietly without the child hearing. Especially since the child is currently transfixed to a picture of Santa Claus being strung up against a wall in his underwear whilst being gagged as the Ultimate Warrior looks on with a grin. Onwards the Roman walks down towards the history section. He finally arrives in a dusty corner of the library with several dozen shelves on American history. He looks around in dismay trying to work out where exactly the Roman history books would be placed. He finally notices in the corner of the bottom shelf there is a card marked ‘Foreign’ and three books. “And people wonder why George Bush is an idiot about places that aren’t in the fifty states.” The master of the two piece toga and cape effect crouches and pulls out an extremely dusty copy of the ‘History of the World Part 1: Not America’ He smiles with glee and stands back up again, taking it to a nearby table and placing it down with a firm thud before dragging up a chair and taking a load off. He starts flicking through the book until one thing suddenly dawns on him… no matter what he reads about, it’s about that things impact on America, not on what actually happened. “The Roman Empire… did nothing in or to America” his eyes squint for a second and he frowns. “Let’s get to changing that, then shall we?” he says as he gets up and leaves.
