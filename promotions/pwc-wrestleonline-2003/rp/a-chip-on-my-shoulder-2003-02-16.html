A Chip on My Shoulder...

16 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: A few days after the FSW closure A day had passed. Still no word from Five Star Wrestling. Who in the hell did Chip and Orion think they were? We all sweated our asses off for their greater good, and now they decide to just leave us out on a limb? I jobbed to Jack Brooze for the love of God. JACK BROOZE. Not just once... THREE FUCKING TIMES! But I digress. I headed home after going to the arena and finding no one there. Slept the night away, and woke up to find Ella making breakfast. This was going to be a day to remember. This was the beginning of the end of the Magnum Wolf that FSW was home to. Pride? Pffffft. BZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZ! My arm swung hard to my right, nearly smashing my alarm clock into peices. My mind swimmed as I tried to wake up. The little bits of red light said that it was 7:45, but there was no damned way it was 7:45. As my mind's peices began falling into place, a smell came to my nose that brought back the memories of mom. THUD! Falling out of bed sucks. I scrambled to my feet quickly, and looked down. Boxers. T-shirt. Sure, why not? A voice floated up the steps. Damn that woman for being so damned cheery in the mornings, 'Honey... are you ok?' Ah, fair Ella. The girl who stuck by when the going got tough. The girl who DEFINATELY stuck by when I was in Five Star. The girl who may not stick by when she finds out that something may have happened to FSW. A pattern began today. It seemed that everytime I thought of FSW, I began muttering to myself. To be totally honest, I didn't even have a clue what I was muttering about. 'Godfuckingpeicea... whodoyouthink... sonuva...' The smell again. OH MAN! This girl knew how to cheer a guy up. There was the faint smell of egg in the air... and the dominant bacon grease odor... even the smell of bread being toasted. A BELT. No, not the things made of leather used to hold your pants up. A Bacon, Egg, Lettuce, and Tomato sandwich. These damned sandwiches were food of the Gods. Slowly, I made my way to the door of our room, glancing back at the FSW bag that sat next to my bed. I once again found myself muttering all the way down the stairs. 'EveryonewantstoseeSTREAK... noonecaresaboutmagnum... GRRRRAH!' As I came to the bottom of the stairs, the smells grew even stronger. Enveloping all of my senses but touch as I walked into the kitchen. Ella stood in the kitchen wearing nothing but one of my FSW sweatshirts, and even that was barely on because the neck was big enough to lay across her shoulders. Standing over the stove, flipping bacon. Is there anything sexier than a near naked chef? Yeah, I agree. A naked chef. But the point remains. My eyes darted from her body up to the food she was cooking, and watched hungrily as the bacon sizzled. 'Morning, sweets! I noticed you got home last night, but I didn't want to wake you. You seemed sort of uneasily sleeping,' chirped the petite brunette standing in front of me. 'Uh, yeah, well I got the night off. Good behavior and all that...' I stammered weakly, hoping she would go for the story. She always went for my stories. She wasn't the kind to distrust anyone. Especially not me. 'Oh. Well, how long are you home?' I looks down at my feet. I hated lying to her because she would believe me no matter what I said. There was never any challenge. I searched for a good answer, and none came. Best way to handle this? Dodge the question! 'Chip O'Meara hasn't called, has he? Or perhaps Paul Orion?' HA! That was spectacular, Aaron! You are truly a genius. Thanks, Aaron. You're welcome Aaron. But my congratulations didn't last long. 'Why?' DAMN! Those women and their investigative skills! 'No reason.' AHA! A masterful response! I could win awards for this stuff. Now... would she go for it? Ella just sort of shrugged, and said, 'Whatever. Want a BELT?' 'DAMN STRAIGHT!' As I sat down, and watched Ella walk over to the table with one of the best sandwiches ever invented, I couldn't help but wonder what was taking them to tell us the deal. I'll call Timo. He'll know what's up. He was always on the inside for FSW stuff. 'You gonna eat that, or just stare at it?' A grin covered my face all of the sudden. This was the kind of girl I could live with for the rest of my life. She was probably the only one who would keep me around when I didn't have a job...
