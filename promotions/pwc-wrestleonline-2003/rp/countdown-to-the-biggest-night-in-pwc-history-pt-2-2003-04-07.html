Countdown To The Biggest Night in PWC History (Pt 2)

7 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07/04/03 The faint systematic hum of the cheap overhead lights and X-Ray machine wailed in the background of the confines of the EMT's room at the PPV venue. In the far corner examining several X-Rays was doctor Dr. Edward Samson. 'Well, it's worse than we thought' he muttered while squinting at two X-Rays in his left and right hands. Sitting on the small bed just in front of him with his back turned was a man, who, according to these X-Rays, shouldn't even be within a 5 mile radius of a wrestling ring, let alone competing in one, and certainly let alone competing in one with a Stairway To Hell stipulation. 'I take it some of the other on hand doctors have informed you of the condition your in?' The man wearing the long black leather trench coat shrugged while not taking his eyes off the eagle who sat perched on his right hand which he held just in front of his face. 'Yes doctor, he is aware of the severely fatigued situation his body is in' chirped in Jeremiah who sat in the far corner of the room. 'Liar' Poe whispered just loud enough for Jeremiah to hear him, resulting in him shooting him an adamant stern look which Poe ignored. 'Gideon.....' the doctor said while walking over to him and kneeling just in front of him. He looked like a father nestling down to give his young son a lecture on the harsh realities of life but of what hope it can still bear for him. 'One wrong knock on the head tonight, one wrong fall on the back of your neck tonight, and that's it. Career over. There will be no miracle comebacks and physiotherapy to help you. You'll be on your own and isolated from the sport. PWC don't give out health and injury insurance Gideon, you know that. That is why I am giving you my expert opinion, that you don't compete in the match tonight.' Gideon didn't look at him, instead he just kept his gaze fixated on Roland, pecking his head gently every so often as the eagle caressed his cheek with his wings. 'The match is a big draw, they'll put it on again. Live to fight another day kid.' No look. No reply. 'Are you even listening to me?' Before an answer could be given, referee Gil McGoosen knocked and poked his head around the door. 'Err, Gideon, it's been half an hour now. We really need to take Roland ready so we can prepare him for the match.' '.......Fuck off' No harsh tone, just blunt and to the point. Gil shot a surprised look, before looking around the room for support. He found it from Jeremiah. 'Allow me Mr. McGoose, Gideon, come now...' 'Touch him, go ahead. I'll point and laugh as he pecks your shallow eyes out.' Jeremiah stopped dead in his tracks and took a step back as Roland contorted his head to meet Jeremiah's. Jeremiah had no doubts that the eagle could understand Gideon perfectly. Finally, Gideon sat up. 'One wrong chairshot, one wrong fall. End my career? So be it? Bail out of the match and let that bastard have the joke on me? Make a fool out of myself? Run with my tail between my legs? Sorry, doc, no dice!' Dr. Samson removed his glasses and rubbed his eyes before getting to his feet and walking away before shaking his head. 'Fine, your decision. I've given you m expert opinion, I can give you no more.' Gideon ignored the comment before pointing at Jeremiah. 'You know full well I'll die a thousand deaths before I let you have any overruling voice over what happens to Roland.' Gideon then looked to McGoosen. 'I said fuck off!' McGoosen didn't even stop to look for support this time, and instead quickly exited the room. 'Capitol Punishment. First ever PWC Pay Per View. I'm not a man with much pride, you know that'. Gideon pointed at Jeremiah. 'But 'Path ahs put Tanya, Roland and myself through enough pain and misery. Tonight it ends! Tonight isn't about being a part of the first God damn PPV this company is airing. It's about brining an end to a nightmare that I should have brought to an end a long time ago! To hell with expose.... to hell with glory.... to hell with being put in the history books. I'm tired of waiting to see what he's gonna do next. Tonight I end it. Tonight I end him, and I've got no fear of dying to do it in the process. ' Gideon looked at Jeremiah, who's face was ready to overflow with anger and rage. He didn't want Gideon to fight tonight. Why? because Gideon would go all out, no doubts about it. He'd be the show stealer without even trying. He'd get recognition, and once he got recognition the would slip out of his wrinkled wretched grasp. he himself, would KILL Gideon before that happened. 'I'll see you after the match' Gideon muttered, before exiting the room leaving Jeremiah to stir and wallow in his own anticipation of what would happen tonight. He too had seen the X-Rays. He vowed he would kill Gideon before he gained the true spotlight... Yet if WarPath did what he knew he was going to do tonight... ...he wouldn't have to. This was the biggest night in PWC history. It would also be the biggest night of Gideon Poe's career. If he wasn't careful though, it would be the biggest for all the wrong reasons.
