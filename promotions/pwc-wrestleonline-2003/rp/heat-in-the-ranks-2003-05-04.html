Heat In The Ranks

4 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4th May 2003 “How did it feel to pick up your first win in the Pro Wrestling Circuit, Loc?” Charlie Loc looked over at the reporter and went to reply, another reporter cut him off. “What are your plans for 2nd Generation Viet Cong?” He scratched his head and looked around; Charlie went to answer, another reporter managed to cut him off. “Charlie, is chicken illegal over in Vietnam?” He was being assaulted by questions from the paparazzi. The room started to buzz as more and more reporters jumped in with their own questions, none of them getting the faintest hint of answer as Charlie stood there stunned. Covering the mic with one hand Charlie coughed to the side. “Excuse me Charlie; is there anyone you dislike here in PWC?” Charlie looked confused, trying to think of an answer. A reporter stood up and flailed his hand in the air; Charlie Loc stared at the reporter as if acknowledging him. “Loc, what brings you back over to the States?” “Certain Issues,” he replied sternly. “Do you not have the resources to tackle these issues back in Vietnam?” The female reporter glared through her glasses as Charlie managed a smirk. “I like to know the job is done properly” Reporters all around the room bowed their heads to note his reply; leaning to the side Charlie let out a soft cough. “Did you study the English Language at a University or College?” An irrelevant question broke the silence as Charlie turned back. “Does it matter?” his reply was short and to the point. “No Charlie… it doesn’t matter, but…” amazingly enough he cut a reporter off. “Then don’t bother…” his voice trailed off as the reporter shuffled in his seat nervously. Another reporter shot his hand up; Charlie looked over and glared as if he recognized him. “What are the limitations on your Visa?” The reporter hovered his pen over his paper awaiting an answer. “Same as every other immigrant.” The reporter rubbed the hair on his chin and grinned from ear to ear, “How does it feel to be playing follower to…” he paused for a brief moment, “two half-cast Vietnamese?” A dreaded silence took over the conference room as Charlie Loc froze up. He began to shake and breathe violently before slamming his hand down hard on the table; everyone sat on the edge of their seats waiting for Charlie Loc to reply. “I do not play follower… to ANYONE!!” His voice rose at his last word; the reporters in the front row of the conference room jumped. “2nd Generation Viet Cong called ME in for back-up…” angrily he paused for a brief moment, “This is how it should be portrayed!” Charlie began thumping his chest with his fingers; everyone in the room sat scared heeding his every word. “Vien Phuc Loc is a product of Vietnam… 2GVC are three powerful people with a name” “Never am I ruled” he glared across the room; his gaze stopped on the single reporter the sparked his outburst. “Never do I follow” He began to breath deeply as he eyed out the single reporter; his glare was one of the deadliest looks known to man. Everyone in the room sat frozen with fear as Charlie Loc couldn’t contain himself from lashing out, finally he calmed. “This Is Over.” Charlie Loc pushed himself up and out of his seat before walking down the small stage. As he walked past the reporter questioning him, he was sure to ram a shoulder into him. He exited the room and disappeared out of sight as the conference room buzzed, the media director was left to give excuses for Charlie’s outburst.
