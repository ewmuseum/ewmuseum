Stepping into The Shade Pt:2

12 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/02/03 She woke up again, this time without a cold sweat and a pounding headache. She looked out of the window to her right to see the sunrise slowly begin to occur before her. She threw the bed sheets off her, just noticing a set of deep blue and purple coloured bruises on her wrists. 'I was tied up' she said to the four walls and no one else as thoughts of a fat obese man came flooding back to her, licking her neck, running a cold blade across her neck. 'Cannibals' she said while holding back a few lurking tears. Everything there and then came flooding back. How she was set to be a culinary cuisine for a cannibalistic family of circus workers. How he came, the dark man, how he placed her life on the line in a game of The Dunk Tank. How he was so sure, so certain. Every miniscule detail came soaring back to her with brutal force, and even she had a hard time trying to comprehend and believe it. She stood up slowly, dizzy and groggy, yet she managed to find a set of clothes folded neatly beside her. A pair somewhat baggy black denims, a pair blue vans and a black Nine Inch Nails T-shirt. She eagerly put them on in an attempt to keep warm. As she eventually did, something caught her attention. Well, captured it was a more accurate assessment, for she was almost instantly hypnotised by the sound. She walked out of the bedroom and across a long hallway. Ahead she could hear the guitar being played even louder as she made her way to the balcony it was originating from. She tip toed over while staying close to the wall until she finally got a clear view. There he was, his back to her with his long black hair hanging down to his shoulders, wearing a black fishnet top with black leather pants and boots, perched calmly on a small stool playing a melody that could easily captivate and ignite the imaginations of the souls of the dead. 'The clothes fitted nicely I trust?' he asked as he stopped playing, catching her off guard. 'Uh.....' He stood up and turned around slowly removing the guitar from around his shoulders. 'Uhhh, yeah, thank you' He nodded while smiling a bit, obviously slightly amused at her nervousness. She could tell he was a man that didn't smile often though. 'You a big fan?' she asked while holding out the NIN logo on the T-shirt he had given her. 'Definitely' he replied while nodding, this finally caused her to give him a heart-warming smile that almost, just almost, made him blush. She suddenly stopped smiling, and her face changed to that of grimace and confusion. 'What is it?' he asked with clear concern in his voice. 'Old..............The old man' she replied while thinking back to the encounter she had with him. Gideon's face immediately turned to anger as he gritted his teeth and ran his hand through his long black hair before resting the guitar down against the wall. 'Who is he?' Gideon turned to her and simply stared for a few moments. 'You remember how you got here?' She nodded slowly. 'Think that was hard to believe? Wait till you hear how I got here.'
