Hook, Line, and Sinker

25 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 25, 2003 'I'm not a total jerk off? Is that all he had to say?' I questioned myself as I continued to drive around in this, the early hours of the morning. The dash was glowing an ominous orange color, which was shining off my face, causing a sinister effect. I looked at the clock, glowing the same orange color... 3:17. It had been a few hours since I visited him in jail. 'Damn he was pissed!' I said, looking back at what went on during my visitation. It was surprising that they were keeping him over night too. Or at least I would assume that's what they were keeping him in for. Hopefully no longer, I have a plan I need to carry out. 'Surprising what these donut eating freaks will do when they see a minority running around like that. They didn't even bother to ask any questions, they just picked the poor fool up, and slapped him into handcuffs.' I said with a laugh as I stopped at a red light, or at least I thought it was a red light. The alcohol from earlier was taking it's toll on me. I hadn't drank like that since before coming back to the states from Japan, that God forsaken country! 'Oh well, I did what I had to do to keep this carnival ride going, despite the injuries that were necessary to keep the night going.' I was still amazed that the man was even taking a chance with me. OK, I may have acted like a fucking school girl at times, making it seem as if I was being fake, but he didn't question what little 'friendship' I'm trying to pretend we have together. 'He sure is falling for it though. I don't think I will be having any troubles with continuing this.' I said before hammering down on the gas pedal as the street light turned green. I was still perplexed at how long I continued to drive around. I had a perfectly good hotel room to return to. A nice cozy bed I could sleep in as well. 'Why the hell am I still driving?!' I asked forcefully, as if to find an answer from someone, something, else. I wasn't getting anything, so I was forced to answer myself. 'I have no clue!' I said, with an almost sad tone of voice. I wasn't for sure either. Everything was going according to plan, everything was sitting in the palm of my hand to deal with, and yet, I was almost saddened by the fact that I should go back to my hotel room, and sleep. It was like being alone kept me from being myself at any particular point in time, even in such a case as right now. I shook my head as I switched lanes, and turned left at the next light. 'Hell, might as well get some sleep.' I moaned. A yawn followed shortly after. The drive, was beginning to take some effect on my body, as if to say I was being stupid for just sitting for so long. The orange glow was etched into my vision, however, as I stopped the car, turned it off, got out, and locked it up. I tried to focus on where I was walking, while approaching the front door to the hotel, but the bright orange lights blurred my vision. I sat down on the bench just outside the hotel to rest, and also to try and get my vision back. But why was I even there? 'Shit, I've got to stop asking myself so many questions.' I said to myself, the sound muffled a bit as my face rested in the palms of my hands. 'I sure as hell don't care that I am here.' Of course I didn't. I didn't care much for anything in the world. Wrestling, cash, and the occasional bit of having a bit of fun, or as others would recognize as my kind of fun... being 'sinister'. Isn't that really all that matters? 'The fact that I can't see straight will soon fade.' It's not the first, and won't be the last time I had tunnel vision, and a bunch of floating dots in my line of sight. I'll give in a few minutes, it will go away. 'My days of being single don't bother me, although a 'soul mate' would be appealing.' I hadn't seen the sight of my arm around a woman in years. Me holding hands with a woman, well it's been a while too. I've been by myself for this long, what would a few more years hurt? 'And money, why I even gave that excuse to Kohana earlier was beyond me. I had loads of it given to me from... It's not like I care how much I spend look what kind of car I... I fucking blew ninety bucks Friday afternoon without a care in the world where that money would end up, all for the fact that I knew what I wanted to do with it.' I'm pretty sure I have a good idea of what I'm doing anytime I spend my own cash, so why bother worrying about that either? 'I sure as hell don't give a damn what happens to Kohana when I finish with him either.' I reassured myself some more. I should hurt myself for even thinking for a second that I might care what happens to the little reservation no sensation. All I cared about was that I get him lured into my trap, and then tear him apart. And that's about all I needed to worry about at the moment too... Getting Kohana to fall for ever single trap I set for him. After that, other things will make the list of needing to be dealt with. 'Until it's done... Nothing stops me from finishing the job!' I said to myself as I regained my full use of vision, stood up, and proceeded into the lobby of the hotel. Nothing stands in my way from demolishing Kohana until I'm through with him... NOTHING!
