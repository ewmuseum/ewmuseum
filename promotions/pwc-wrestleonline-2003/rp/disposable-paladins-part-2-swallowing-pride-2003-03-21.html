Disposable Paladins - Part 2 - Swallowing Pride

21 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 21/03/03 Part 2 'Okay, one chilli dog with mustard, no onions, and one meatball sandwich with extra sauce....' 'With onions asshole!' came the voice of Lennon from the backseat of his car. 'Ahhh you fucker, make up your damn mind!' shouted Trent before letting out his anger by running and kicking the curb violently, causing himself to yell in slight agony from the impact. This spawned hysterics from both Maynard and Vex Lennon who sat in the car watching and chuckling. 'One chilli dog with mustard AND onions, one meatball sandwich, and one contaminated shit filled cheeseburger for myself. 'Got it in one chief' chipped in Maynard. Trent then disappeared into a small cafe while Maynard and Lennon calmly looked out of the windscreen at the typical dead streets of New Jersey. 'So you heard from Gideon much lately' asked Maynard. 'Nah, which is understandable. I mean the guys got a lot of shit going on with PWC and....' 'Speaking of which boys' came a voice through the car window followed by the trigger of a gun pulling back. Martin Bauerman had no intention of using it, but he knew exactly how to get what he wanted just by waving it about in a threatening way. That was the most Martin Bauerman had ever had to do with a gun, wave it about. He had fired a total of 15 bullets in his career as an officer of the law. Three of those bullets had claimed the lives of three criminals. The rest, well, to Bauerman, if they didn't kill the criminal, those bullets may as well have never been fired. Lennon immediately froze while Maynard simply opened the glove compartment quickly and withdrew a sidearm of his own which he aimed at the head of Bauerman. 'Touché bitch' said Maynard smugly. Before anyone could react the sound of another gun being armed was heard. 'Try check mate asshole'. Maynard looked out of the corner of his eye to see Bauerman's partner Melvin Peterson with a gun to his head. The nervous tone in Melvin's voice indicated this was as close as he had ever been to being like the people he seen on 'Cops' every Thursday and Maynard couldn't help but laugh. 'Put the water pistol away sweetheart' shouted Maynard. Melvin stuttered a while before the feeling a cold crisp blade was positioned strategically across his throat. 'Try game set match sweet thing' came the voice of Trent, as he sadistically licked the ear of Melvin causing him lose breath rapidly as he clenched his eyes and sobbed slightly. 'Okay Okay' came Bauerman's voice as he held his gun high in the air. 'Let's calm things down here.' 'From what I saw from the window over there, you drew first. So go ahead Bauerman, be John Wayne! But as soon as you pull that trigger I'm gonna get all Michael Myers on your boyfriends ass over here.' 'Look, that won't be necessary. All I want is a few answers to a few questions, but having dealt with you guys in the past this is the only way I usually get a response out of you.' Trent looked at Peterson, his mouth agape and breathing heavily. Eventually though Trent let him go and shoved him away before placing his blade back into his boot. 'I want to know where your friend is' asked Bauerman as Peterson quickly joined him at his side. 'Narrow that down for us' spoke Lennon, still seething that Bauerman had caught him off guard only moments ago, 'You aren't that popular' 'Yeah, bite shit and die Bauerman. You want to find Gideon, go do some policing like you pigs SHOULD be doing right now.' 'Easy Vex' spoke Trent. 'Look I know he exists, don't insult my intelligence guys, I've seen him and what he's capable of. I seen the damage he did to Jacob Riley up in Winterbourne Asylum, I know that he's a dangerous man, but I know that he's a descent enough man............. and I need his help' Trent knew what this was all about and sighed slightly before looking up at him. 'Those killings?' Bauerman simply nodded. 'Our guys are like blind men in a dark room looking for a black cat that's not there. We're clutching at straws Trent, we're scraping the barrel for resources and the only way I'm allowed back on the street with this case if I get someone who can definitely get this job done. I can't think of anyone who happens to be inhabiting the New Jersey area other than Gideon who can help me on this' 'Why the hell should he help you Bauerman, how do we know this isn't a frickin' set up?!' 'C'mon Maynard, give me some credit. What if it's your younger sister we turn out of a gutter next?' A silence fell between all five men before it was broken by Lennon. 'Look, we'll let him now that you want to talk to him, but nobody goes to Gideon, he comes to them, if ya get my drift.' Bauerman took a deep sigh of relief before nodding. 'Yeah, and guys?' All three men looked at Bauerman simultaneously. 'Thanks' 'Just make sure you catch the bastard' 'I won't stop hunting, put it that way' Bauerman and Peterson then slowly took off down the street. 'Well' asked Lennon looking up at Trent, who looked down confused. 'Get my sandwich bitch!'
