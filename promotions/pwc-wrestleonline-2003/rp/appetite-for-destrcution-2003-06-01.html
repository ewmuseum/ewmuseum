Appetite for Destrcution

1 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/01/03 As he walked calmly and methodically away from the wild savage roaring flames that yelled and screamed in a dry roar behind him, an unforgiving image of destruction caused by the dark architect who's steel blue eyes glistened in the dark as his companion remained perched on his shoulder proudly, Jeremiah's skin began to burst out into goose bumps, and he was thankful he wore his long robes on this cold bleak night. As he continued to stride forward he didn't seem of this world, and if it was anyone other than Jeremiah O'Barr looking at him they would have prayed to the good Lord himself that he was not from this world. The harsh brutal reality that this ...this..demon was raised in a society that they also inhabited would have repulsed them and scared them to their fragile foundations. Jeremiah thought he was hiding his shock and sudden fear rather well though. 'You fool of a boy!' he snarled, banging his long staff down against the ground for emphasis. 'Do you realise what you have done?' 'Made the New Jersey fire department earn their pay checks' he replied in his dry sarcastic tone while not even stopping in his stride to shed a glance to the Elderly Evil who still in these modern times controlled him from the shadows they both inhabited. 'What do you want, FAME?! RECOGNITION?! THE SPOTLIGHT?!' Gideon walked past Jeremiah which caused Jeremiah to grimace due to the fact that he now had to break into a run just to keep up with Gideon. 'You want the world to see you beat Matthias?! You want the world to see you overcome you're one true Achilles heel? Well guess what? THEY DO NOT CARE! Not about your quest to purify your soul, not about Hunt, and certainly NOT ABOUT YOU!' This time The Shade did stop and turn around to look straight down his unwanted mentor. 'They never have Gideon, and they never will. People like us, we can not be accepted, we never can!' 'Do you think I seek acceptance? Do you think I seek fame, fortune and recognition from the very people who expect me to be their paladin?!'. The loud aggressive tone his voice took seemed to share the same wild untamed aggression that the flames in the background possessed. 'Just remember you made me this way, you made US this way. You live with that harsh fact, while I live with the harsh burden that is YOU!' Gideon then turned around sharply and continued to walk away, Roland the eagle still perched proudly on his back. 'He can get to you! He can get inside! Maybe if he did then maybe you'd learn! Maybe if he did then maybe you'd wake up and listen' Jeremiah's taunts fell on def ears as Gideon and Roland disappeared into the shadows of the New Jersey night.. Yet he made deliberately sure to whisper the last one. 'Maybe I should help him..... help him make you realise'.
