Showtime At The Apollo

27 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05.27.03 03.29.03 It definantly was Showtime at the Apollo, as I sat back awaiting my BIG debut. One that would go down in the short history of the Pro Wrestling Circuit, as the biggest thing to hit the federation since... since... well it was going to go down as the biggest thing to hit the federation, period. I toggled back and forth with the idea of not signing the contract, and letting Ron go with his backup plan of making Frankie Bones, 'The Black Star', just because I didn't want to deal with being called an 'Uncle Tom', but the visit I got from Kendra Newman went REAL WELL, no and I don't mean it like that. Needless to say, I'm sitting back here awaiting my moment. And that moment would arrive, as I was whisked away to my ringside seat for my eventually debut. Ofcourse I had to sit through a Paula Michelle Smith versus Samantha Alotti match, but in all honesty, it wasn't half bad. I mean that PMS is a looker, but I can't really get into much detail there, after all she's just 17. (I believe she's 18 now though ;)). Then it happened... Ron, Clark, Jack, Kendra and company were all in the ring, and things seemed to be going good, until that god awful 'Exile' song started playing. Frankie Bones came out and ruined everything; that is ofcourse until Starr, Carlisle and McGriff pulled a fast one on him, so I decided to take a closer look, and believe you me, I LIKED WHAT I SAW! Kendra's ass looked BETTER IN PERSON! Hey don't sweat it, I'm following Veronica's rule's, I can't look but I can't touch. As long as I do that I'll be fine. But what I was really doing in the ring, was showing everyone that there could only be one 'black star' in the PWC, and that man was their very own, Jonathan Conspiracy. Not some New York-wanna be named Frankie Bones. I was NYC's own, and they would love me for it. What black man listens to Slayer anyway? I mean really. Bones stayed up late nights to watch Tales From The Crypt, I deprived myself of sleep to watch some ghetto black people attempt to perform in the very place I made my return to wrestling. I was performing for the very people that had no dreams, and took it upon themselves to boo the ones that just wanted to get on TV. I don't blame there for it, but damn these people are fical. Just moments ago, I hopped the guardrail and entered the ring, and they cheered me because they thought 'the brothers' would stick together. They thought I was coming to 'my homies' aid. Well in the simplest term possible; 'real recognize real', and Slayer's #1 fan wasn't real. And now look, I have a contract, and he has a contract. Thank you Kendra Newman for keeping another black stereotype alive. 'Black on Black Voilence'. 'Let the regression of black people continue.' Until then Jonathan Conspiracy will live and fight for the only color that matters... GREEN
