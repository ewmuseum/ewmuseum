The Story Until Now

19 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03\19\03 Flashbacks... A tear in the fabric of life that is wide enough to make you go back to it time and again... 'Memories of the past.. Memories of a life left long ago...' Masahiro's voice came soft in the darkness as his eyes opened and he looked around the room slowly.. 'This room reminds me of the 'Cell'. The room they hid us in before the fights..' His voice broke at the end.. Flashbacks like this happen a lot when you see the world through one unfiltered eye.... A dark room... The smell of sweat.... One man stands alone in the room taping up his fists.. 'The One and only... MASAHIRO ZANGETSU!' A chorus of cheers sounded through the underground area. This was where they put us.. This was the Cell... As the door opened light flooded in and a ten foot by ten foot cage stood before him.. No padding on the floor... Barb wire wrapped polls in the four corners and the cage itself lined with razor wire... 'Gideon Poe..... Can I trust him?' Masa looked up at the ceiling.. 'Does it matter? This match is only going to make Starr more money' Masahiro shook his head in disgust.. 'Pizza delivery boys, Idiots dressed like romans... and a fool who thinks he is Jesus...' Flashbacks are funny... Come to you from no where... Like a fist to the skull... The lights flashed everywhere as Masahiro reared back.. Fans cheered as he stood straddled on the man below him.. Both of them bleeding profusely.. Masa from his forehead and shoulders.. The man beneath him from his neck... His chest... In fact, the blood was covering most of his body now so it was hard to tell where it started... As Masa straddled the man his fist reared back.. The just over 100 people in the large basement screamed with intense fury all of them cheering the idea of Masahiro killing this boy... The boy was no older than 20.. He must have stepped into this venue not knowing what he was in for and he was paying for it... Paying with his life... The fist came crashing down on the table in Masahiro's apartment. The memories of that boys last moments fading from his mind. What was bringing back memories of that boy.. Was it Campbell? Chris Campbell was 23 at best... Only 4 years younger than Masahiro himself. Physically anyway.. 13 years ago Masahiro stepped into a death match for the first time and was forced to mature... '14 years old... The promoter decided with my skill I was ready... Then again... The promoter was out to earn money from my blood...' Masa uttered the words as he grabbed a glass from the dish rack and filled it with water.. After a quick drink he turned on his heel.. Flashbacks are the last drops of blood from a stone.. The last memory.. The young boy dropped with Masa fist. Blood splattered up onto Masa's face as the kid choked out his last words.. 'Please Stop'... Masa felt for him but knew that the young boy would die either way.. 'Mercy is the best I can do for you young one..' The words slipped from his lips as the sound of a crack rang out.. Silence followed but only for a few moments.. Cheers filled the 'arena' as Masa stood up the blood splattered across his face like war paint. 'That was when I met him... Ron Starr' Masahiro sighed slowly.. Flashbacks... 'Hey.. Zangetsu was the name right?' The man stared at Masa through his glasses. 'Hai' Masa's voice came strong. 'What can I help you with..?' The man smiled lightly. 'Your talent. You see I'm a recruiter for a man by the name of The Phantom Booker..' Masa's voice answered immediately 'Primetime Championship Wrestling and Old School Wrestling.. I know of him..' The man smiled 'Ahh good.. Well He asked me to find the best all around athlete in Japan.. and quite honestly.. You worked a crowed into cheering for death.. I'm impressed and I suspect he will be too..' Masa turned away snorting out snot and blood. 'all right well what's your name first..' The man grimaced and look Masa in his one good eye. 'Ron Starr..' 'Well guys I would tell you the rest, But if we don't leave now we won't have the time to work out' Masa laughed under his breath. Across the room Gunner Gojoh and Neon Kazaki sat.. 'Interesting story Masa... You've done a lot since we last saw you.' Gunner stood up as Neon yawned heavily... 'Yeah dude... you BORRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRED me!'
