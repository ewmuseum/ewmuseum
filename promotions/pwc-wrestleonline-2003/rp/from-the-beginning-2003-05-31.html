From the Beginning...

31 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-31-03 Back here again, I find myself wondering why in the world I am where I am. How did I get to the place I am right now? From the start of my wrestling career to now, a span of close to nine years and what have I accomplished that is worth merit during this entire time? From the beginning when it wrestling was all that mattered anyway because you were living in Japan and working for a living. That point in time when you had just jumped off being one of the smartest in your high school class, but you weren’t smart enough to throw yourself right into college, he had to shoot for the bigger things, or at least what you thought were the bigger things in life. It’s been your entire life, this business, and you’ve never longed to escape it. Why? Because it is your life, once you’ve had the chance to walk out into that ring and experience the rush that you receive from pulling off one of the biggest and greatest maneuvers that crowd has ever seen before. That is a feeling that can never be replaced, but it gets to troubling sometimes. Making you feel so awful inside that you really didn’t want to work in the business ever again, or at least have taken a brake for some time, only those never last as long as you would expect them to. Then you get older, you find yourself caught up in so much more than your typical work ethics and you’re now being brought into the wonderful world of relationship and finally come to realize what they meant when they always told you that “women are the root of all evil in this world.” You always refuted that comment as well, because you were always raised around “girls”, then you met that one girl who you entrusted the most in life and then you were broken in half. That’s half of your life gone there in the blink of an eye, so what comes next in life? I mean, you’re not the carefree kid you once were and now you’ve got more responsibility to worry about, or at least you believe you do. So, where do we go from here? Do we continue on with our building of the career or do we just stop? It’s not such a hard decision to make, is it? You’ve done everything you’ve ever really wanted to do in your line of work, but you just don’t consider yourself being on the level of respect that you want to be. After all, the world does revolve around respect, and everyone wants some of it. You’ve achieved that stint of fame in nearly everything you’ve done, but you lack that element of respect from certain people you wish to have it from the most, so you decide to continue your career in hope that you will gain what you seek most. Now, you’re stuck between a rock and a hard place, because you my friends are stuck for life in the choice you have made. This is your life, good luck in it and God speed.
