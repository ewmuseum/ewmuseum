Stepping into The Shade Pt:1

12 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/02.03 She rose violently drenched in a cold sweat. Her navy blue dyed hair clung to her toned skin due to its dampness. She looked around the room sharply, the darkness smothered it unmercifully, she couldn't even see the colour of the sheets she was laying beneath. 'Rest easy my child' quirked a shrill voice that made her turn sharply to the direction it came from. Eventually a small candle nearby was lit, revealing a an old face riddled with wrinkles and small black eyes. 'Wha-' 'Please, be silent my child. There is a matter I feel we need to discuss' Her head throbbed unmercifully as she tried to remember what happened to her. All she had were visions of a dark man, tall, mysterious, depressed, handsome. These were the only thoughts and images she could conjure as she clutched at the strands of fading memories of how she reached here. Why couldn't she remember anything?! 'Ah yes, I gave you a little home remedy to help you fall asleep, a slight episode of amnesia is often a side effect due to it's potency.' 'Him...... the man....saved me' 'Oh yes, he has habit of doing that. That's why I came here to talk to you.' She rubbed her head slightly before looking out towards him. 'Now, my disciple has obviously taken a keen liking to you, rescuing you was the biggest give away of that' All these comments only confused her more as she tried to piece together the small fragments of the events of last night. 'Be warned young lady, it is by his wishes alone that you are still alive and here right now. It is by his demands that you stay here and heal. Yet I warn you, if you approach him in any manner other than one filled with gratification for him saving your filthy plagued self, then...' Drugged, tired and semi conscious as she was, she knew when she was being threatened, and she knew when she was being insulted. Her eyes glared even through the darkness and she swung forwards at him, she was too tired to speak, but her physical attempt at payback was just as weak as she missed making any form of contact with the Elderly Evil. His vile chuckles were what began to fill the dark air next. 'Oh my dear girl, you are just like him when I first took him under my wing. He was just as pathetic and...ARGH!' The girl reared her head up as the old man yelled in pain. The candlelight showing his small shadow being manhandled by a much taller shadow. The taller shadow lifted his small light defenceless body up into the air and tossed it beyond and away from the shadows,. The old mans voice was heard yelling as he soared through the doorway and down the hall. 'I-I It was you....you saved-' 'Be quiet, you need rest' came his stern voice. As he approached her his deep blue eyes lit up the darkened room more than any candle of this world could. She slowly leant back flat on her back as he pulled the sheets up onto her. He was right, she needed rest, but she also needed to know who he is. 'Who are you?' she managed to squeeze out of her tired aching lungs. 'Nobody' He was true in his statement 'Just a shade' he said with a hint of light sarcasm in his voice. To her eyes, he was without a doubt a shade in the dark abyss of the room. Yet as he left, she could tell this man was so much more, and whether it was fate or just plain luck, she knew there was a reason why the two met. There was a reason why she met him. That thought alone made her fall back to sleep with a warm feeling of reassurance inside her even in the harsh bitter cold temperature of the room.
