Timeline - 17/05/03 : Breaching Emotional Defences

20 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 20/05/03 An exhausted Karina Wolfenden collapses back onto the stiff, orange plastic chair in her changing room. The adrenaline that was pulsing around her body having dwindled, the full effects of her match now consume her body. Her lower back is aching, something which the unyielding, firm plastic of the seat is doing nothing to help. Coupled with that, the skin across her throat is sore, mild friction burns from being pulled across the plastic coated ropes. But these are just physical inconveniences. Karina was always quick to heal and when she awakens tomorrow they'll be mere memories. What she'll remember Sunday moring will not be the pain of Ariath's stiff, un-stylised attacks, and it won't be that 'Victory Rush' she always got every time she hears the 3 count come down... What Karina would remember was a girl from Seattle, and how she was helped by her. Karina will tell you herself that she isn't the most 'Open' of people. She doesn't like people intruding into her life and always keeps her defences up. Defences disguised as icy apathy, and an air of un-caring. These guard her well in life, care-free being her choice of lifestyle... But every now and then, something breaks through. Paula Michelle Smith had breached Karina's emotional security. And now, in the bleak and featureless surroundings of a tiny changing room in New Jersey, Wolfenden is left wondering what to do next. It wasn't too late to walk away, just blank the fact that Paula broke out of her enforced character to come to Karina's aid... Ignorance could truly be bliss for Karina. But she couldn't. She just couldn't. Karina remembers every word Paula spoke to her when they 'Met' the night of Karina's return to PWC. In her memory there was no Ariath there, restraining her in a Full Nelson. There were no surroundings, no light, nothing... Just Paula and her stare, and her words... And her revelations. It would be easy to pass off what Karina felt, what she feels, as pity. But pity is a sad emotion, and insulting emotion. Paula Michelle Smith is a girl who would spit pity back in your face. What's getting to Karina is that she can't name what she feels, she can't think of any classification for this emotion. Someone like Karina always knows what she's getting into, she isn't naive... She needs the 411 before throwing herself into a task... But this time she was flying blind, and where this uninformed flight would take her, she didn't know. All Karina knows for sure is that Paula broke out of her shell on Saturday night, and with her emerged a glimmer of hope... And now she must make sure that the young wrestler isn't sucked back into her toughened casing. This could be dangerous, and it could end in pain and heartbreak for Karina, but she can't just leave it now. Karina has the ways and means to help, but that doesn't mean she'd be any use... The desire to help is what would drive the K-Wolf. Some things weren't about upbringing, weren't about dollars in the bank... They were about people, about being human. 'Thank you, Paula.' Karina speaks softly, 'Thank you for helping me.' If only Paula were there to hear her, as this isn't just Karina talking, this is her raw emotions expressing their gratitude. All the reasons Paula has for detesting a woman like Karina, all their differences... Yet Paula called Ariath off, she couldn't watch him destroy the woman who reached out to her... Maybe the first to ever do so. 'And now, let me help you.' If only it would be that easy.
