The Customer is always right Part 1

16 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/16/03 Special Delivery The Customer is always right Part 1 Cold, cold, cold and yes more cold thought Big Chuck D’andrea staring out the window of Cousin Mario’s pizzeria in Mays Landing. It was a slow Sunday evening, even slower with the weather that already beginning to hit all of the East coast. Chuck wasn’t in his normal perky mood, and the lack of a big dumb grin seemed to show it. “Cheer up ya big galoot.” chimed an overexcited Sammy Bello from the other room. “Why?” replied Chuck with more unhappiness “We are the laughing stock of the school after last night.” Out from the kitchen walks Sammy Bello his big nose greased up from sweat and the heat of the oven. He smiles at Chuck and walks over to him patting him on the back. “Hey respect is earned, you know who said that to me?” asked Sammy. “No,” Chuck said looking at Sammy, “who did?” Sammy scratches his black hair for a moment. He obviously has forgotten who said the quote. “Say are you hungry?” Chuck pipes in disturbing the awkward silence. “Naw man, I had a whole pie at that high school today before work.” Sammy adds as he grabs his stomach. He tries to make it look fat but is unable to causing Chuck to chuckle. “Give it up midget; you’ll never have a keg like this!” Chuck pats his stomach as an older Italian looking man walks out from the kitchen. He glares at the two pizza boys and begins to shout. “Hey you too I got an order here that needs delivering.” “Ok Mario, we’ll be right in.” adds the slightly happier Chuck. “He’s a good guy.” He adds with a smile as Sammy nods in agreement. “Yeah, he is gonna give us free pizza to give out at the shows to help promote Cousin Mario’s.” “You saw how those fans at the show today loved it. They seemed to like us a lot.” Chuck adds through a smile. “Who doesn’t like free pizzas; you would be a fan of ours too had another team given out free pizza.” Sammy and Chuck both smile as they walk into the kitchen. Sitting on the counter are two pizza boxes. They both look at them, concerned about which one to grab. Mario stares at both of them and Chuck’s mind begins to blow a fuse. They both know that they have screwed up more than a few times. “Both of them,” Mario says with a little smirk. “I figured that this order won’t be too hard for you guys to screw up. Just go ahead and deliver the two pizzas to 55 Boardwalk Avenue. That shouldn’t be to hard should it boys?” “Not at all, boss!” Sammy says as he salutes Mario. The two grab their jackets and run out to the car. The wind has obviously picked up as Sammy let’s out a yelp as the cold bites him on his large nose. The get inside the official Cousin Mario’s delivery vehicle all smiles. Not because the car is a beautiful ford mustang spray painted Green, Red and White to show the pride of Italy. And not because Mario pays for gas to make sure that the boys get their deliveries on time. But because, the car isn’t anyone of those things. It’s more like a 1989 ford station wagon. And it’s not painted colors for Cousin Mario’s. It’s painted stainless steel because that’s what it is made out of. But Chuck doesn’t mind it much, it gets him from point A to point B…sometimes…it has had a history of stalling, or crashing, or falling apart. But that didn’t matter to the boys. Polly was their pride and joy. Chuck inserts the key and begins to start her up. As he turns the engines whines and putters out. “Come on baby” Chuck says encouragingly to the car. His voice is unsure and his breathe can be seen from the cold. He turns the key again; it whines a little more and putters out again. “Third times charm, we love you Polly come on.” Sammy shouts as the car fails to start once again. Chuck turns the ignition once again and the engine whines longer and louder. Loud bangs can be heard as the engine slowly begins to spin and start up. “That a girl!” Chuck adds proudly as he pats the steering wheel. “Bring on the heat Polly and Chuck... let’s roll!” Sammy jokingly adds as the two leave Cousin Mario’s parking lot. As they begin to drive down the street Sammy scratches his head thinking about who the order is for. “Hey Chuck” “Yeah Sammy?” Chuck replies curious. “What was the address for delivery again?” Sammy’s voice is vibrated from the cold. “Something Boardwalk Avenue maybe?” Chuck is reluctant to remember it off the top of his head. “55 Boardwalk Avenue, Chuck?” “Yeah, that’s it!” Chuck chimes in as a light bulb goes off in his head. “55 Boardwalk Avenue.” “Dude! You know where that is?” Sammy says with a touch of concern. “Umm on Boardwalk Avenue. Umm next to 54 and 56?” “Man, that’s my old man’s place!” Sammy says as his nerves jump. “Well at least we know how to get there…and hey maybe he’ll give us a lil extra tip. That’s always a positive.” Chuck says oblivious to the facts that Sammy is obviously worried about. “Sometimes, Chuck, I wonder how your brain is able to keep the rest of your fat body alive.” Sammy says irritated. “Hey!” Chuck says insulted, “is that a fat joke again?” “Yes!” shouts Sammy as he wipes snot dripping from his nose. “You know how my pops feels about us working at Cousin Mario’s. He still thinks I could be a lawyer like him.” “And just wait till he finds out you’re a wrestler now too.” Chuck says not realizing it’s only adding fuel to Sammy’s fire. “I wish I could just take these Pizzas and shove them in his face.” Sammy says with anger. “No Sammy, remember the customer is always right.” Chuck says it so innocently that it eases Sammy’s mind. But only for a mere second. “Well I guess its time for a family reunion because here we are.” “Next house Dumbo” Sammy says as he crosses his arms with a scowl. Chuck eases Polly into the driveway and shuts off the car. He looks over at Sammy with a smile. “For Mario” Chuck says with a smile. “No, not for Mario. For our new boss, Mr. Starr.” Sammy says with a smile. “I’ll eat to that.” Chuck adds as he opens up the pizza box and inserts a slice of pizza into his gaping mouth. “I’m sure your pops won’t mind.” “We’ll let’s go find out.” Sammy says as he exits the car pizza boxes in hand. The two walk up the stairs to the front door and ring the doorbell. The door slowly opens as they come face to face with Mr. Bello. BLACKOUT
