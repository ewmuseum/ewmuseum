Countdown To The Biggest Night In PWC History (Part #3)

7 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07/03/03 After the events of PWC TV just 2 days ago, and considering that the biggest night of her career is rapidly approaching, Karina Wolfenden would be forgiven if she decided to have a lazy week. Maybe rest her knee, lounge around the apartment, put her feet up. Well, this is Karina we’re talking about, so rather than just putting her feet up, she’s put her whole body up. 11,000 feet up, that is. ‘Hmmmmmm...’ she muses, looking down at the ground below that she left 20 minutes ago, ‘I have the feeling that some might be preparing just a little differently.’ just makes it from her pink lips, torn to shreds by the sound of the aircraft’s engines. She’s right, she does have a unique approach to things. Unique, and maybe not all that sensible. The last thing someone with a notoriously vulnerable knee like Karina’s would be advised to do is fall from 11,000 feet to the ground in a mere 5 minutes. But, she had it booked to relieve pre-event stress, and she certainly wasn’t going to let Magnum Wolf and Angelo Deville ruin her fun. Yes, jumping out of a plane and free-falling for a minute is fun. “ARE YOU READY TO SKYDIVE?!” is then bellowed into her ear by her instructor, it really doesn’t need to be phrased as a question, given he’s the kind of guy who’d reply to a negative by pushing them out anyway. She’s been said to ‘live on the edge’, never more true than now. The door opens, and all that separates her from the sky is herself. Wind rips across her, pushing the suit tightly across her front, making her squint even though her eyes are covered by goggles. At this height the elements are furious. “YES!” she screams back, jubilation in her voice, she’s getting the adrenaline rush that her pursuits offer to quench her craving. Nervousness is reserved for PWC, she has none to offer this pursuit, she’s having a ball. On Sunday she’ll be fighting Magnum Wolf, but at this moment in time she’s battling the physical forces besieging the plane, stepping out onto the strut, closer to the vacant sky. Her right hand clutches the support bar on the wing, the same thing keeping the plane in the air now keeping Karina flying with it. ‘Who needs a gym when you have this!’ she thinks to herself, dismissing the conventional. You learn nothing about yourself lifting weights or jogging on a treadmill. Gym’s are sterile, the atmosphere provided by the air conditioning. This is living. As if caught up in a hurricane, the vicious air tries to pluck her prematurely from the aircraft, but just as she has to battle the world every day, she gives as good as she gets, limbs keeping her glued in place. This isn’t the first time she’s done this, you can’t fling yourself out into nothingness and feel gravity in all it’s glory just once. This time, she has no chaperones, no trainers diving with her... The stabilisers are off. ‘READY! SET! GO!!!!’ is expelled in glory from her lunges, immediately lost in the sky as she lets go of the wing support and drops. No longer feeling the struts under he feet, the only force she feels is the air being pushed away from under her 153 pound frame, thankfully, the strapping she concealed under her pants is doing it’s job, her right knee playing nice. Arms and legs move out, back arches, making herself symmetrical to avoid being sucked into a roll, that’s her last conscious thought for the next minute. From here on it’s sensory auto-pilot. So many sensations are overcoming her, at home once more with her good friend air. It’s almost as if she resents being born a land-dweller, if reincarnation was her friend she’d come back as an Eagle, soaring through the skies out of habit. Eventually, she has to stop taking it all in, not that the brain is capable of processing even a fraction of the experience, her altimeter has to be checked. A crash landing here won’t be one she can walk away from. ‘Time to hit the brakes.” she mouths, almost saddened that she has to slow her descent. In perfect time, both hands move behind her neck, line of symmetry retained. It feels like pulling the plug, one sudden yank seemingly changing the world entirely. The chute opens above her, immediately filling with the air it’s catching. Sharply she’s jolted upright, causing her to let out a winded ‘Ugh!’. After the winds ravaging her, after feeling raw gravity, now she feels the true meaning of peace. She’s taking a stroll through the realm of the birds, gliding down to her own part of the planet. 4 minutes feel like eternity, if she believed in heaven it would be this. As with all good things, it must come to an end. The ground she watched getting closer and closer is about to catch up to her, growing broader on her eye’s horizon. Is there any way she can turn around and float back up? But alas, she now measures her landing, not having had much success with them lately. This is her PPV preparation, not the cause of her missing it. Things go perfectly to plan, she may as well have dropped an inch into a valley of feathers. A 11,000 foot drop having the same sensation as a footstep. ‘Okay...’ she says, exhilarated, laying on her back and looking up at the sky she just fell from, ‘Someone fly me back up there!’. PWC has changed many things in Karina’s life, but it hasn’t changed Karina. This is how she prepares for PWC’s biggest night, and by the look on her face she’s in the best possible condition. Happy.
