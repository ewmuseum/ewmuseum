PWC TV Commercial 2

28 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Originally aired on WGTW TV-48 in Philadelphia, PA at 11:16 am EST Saturday, December 28th. The wind blows a steady pour of rain sideways. As the cameraman uses a white cloth to clear the water off his camera, you see a shirtless male with a green cap with 'John Deere' written in yellow lettering. His skin soaked as he holds a rusted, metal shovel in his left hand and stabs it into the ground with tremendous impact. Mud splatters on his muddy, rain soaked blue jeans as he tips the bill of his cap to the camera and lets the shovel stand sticking into the ground. Propping his elbow on the shovel handle, he has the look of a man who has been blue-collared most of his life. He looks at the camera, scratching his shoulderblade with his filthy hand as he smiles showing a full set of yellow stained teeth. 'I bet you're wonderin'. Why in Sam Hill is this big ol' Kentucky Man doin' at the side of a Craphouse while it's rainin' somethin' crazy?....' His thick, southern accent would melt a woman's heart. Unfortunately, he is very unattractive. Unshaven and filthy, he keeps his smile but seems more serious as he continues. 'You see Folks, I got hired to do a job before I come into the P dubyah C. To many of you, I might seem to be as wanted as a skunk in a shed of domestic dogs, but I tell you....I'm not a man out for trouble. All I ever wanted was to live my life in peace.' He sniffles and looks off to the side, perhaps thinking of something important in his life. He spits to the ground and turns back to the camera. '.....I wasn't allowed the luxury n' stuff that City Folk were given. No siree, Bob. You talk about scrapin' the bottom of life. Been there the last few months. Lived it and I did it with pride. And so when you see me out in the fields or out in the squared circle, you remember that Ol' Porky Bunson is just a guy a lil' down on his luck.' Porky removes his elbow from the shovel handle and walks forward. The deliberate steps of a man on hard times. The rain gaining speed yet he no-sells the weather and continues talking. 'See, there's not a dash gone thang wrong with a hardworkin' man. And Ol' Hard Luck has got a mission to fulfill in good ol' P dubyah C. You will find that out, yes sir. Ol' Porky Buns is gonna light up the P dubyah C on February 1st like a firecracka under a cloudy night. With the Good Lord willin', you best believe I'm no joke. That's a fact. Thank ya'll kindly for listenin'.' Porky Bunson turns away from the camera and grabs the shovel as he digs into the ground as the camera fades away.
