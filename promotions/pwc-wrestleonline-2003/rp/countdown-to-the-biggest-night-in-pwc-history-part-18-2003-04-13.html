Countdown to the Biggest Night in PWC History (Part 18)

13 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 13, 2003 Standing in the gorilla position backstage before the show, Jacob Solomon smiles. Looking out through the curtains, he can see the fans are already streaming in and filling the seats of the Sovereign Bank Arena. They flock in, oblivious, just like the sheep they are, he thinks to himself. It had taken a long eleven weeks to reach the position they were currently at, and things had not quite gone as expected. In fact, that would seem to be a gross understatement. The opposition they had met was intolerable in its resolve, so much so that it require a drastic reevaluation of strategy. What had been predicted as a quick route had turned into a moral stand-off, led by one insufferable little pest that no amount of effort could seem to do away with. By forcing them to deal with him, one annoying little cockroach had set them back six weeks, at least. It was long overdue for them to get back on track. Yes, a gross understatement indeed. But tonight, that would all change. The rodent…the venomous little plague rat had been exterminated. Finally they could move on, and tonight it was time to take things to the next level. The gap that had been created between their estimated position and their current one could be bridged in one fell swoop, provided they took action immediately, and that was exactly what he had planned. By the time this night was over, the political playing field that was the PWC would be radically altered, and the pundits would be left with their jaws dropped, wondering how they had not seen it coming. If only they knew… Solomon thinks, chuckling sinisterly as he turns away from the curtain, walking back in the direction of his locker room. These pathetic souls have ignored their shepherd for long enough. It is time to lead the lambs to slaughter...
