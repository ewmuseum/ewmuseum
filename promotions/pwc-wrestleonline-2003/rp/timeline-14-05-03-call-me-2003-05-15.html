Timeline - 14/05/03 : Call Me!

15 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 15/05/03 Just for once, Karina Wolfenden wanted to wake up to a trouble-free day. A new dawn with no niggling problems on the horizon. But, alas, it wasn't going to be. In the few months that the wrestling world has known Karina, she's been changed, morphed... Effected. When the Boarder first arrived on the scene at the Pro Wrestling Circuit's inaugural show, she was a breath of fresh air, a refreshing change... She was something new. Amidst a locker room full of former legends, rising stars and big names going small-time... Karina was the odd one out. She was just there for the fun of it. She cared about little, and wasn't going to allow anything to intrude on her perfect existence. How quickly things change. 'I'm starting to regret giving them my real number.' Karina quips dryly as the phone rings for the third time this morning, and for the third time she declines to answer. It isn't even screening, for she knows someone from PWC will be on the other end of the line, or leaving a message. She's right, the familiar voice of one of PWC's employees imprints their words digitally on the answering machine, saying the exact same thing as the other two... And also using the same neutral, inoffensive words... Even though they're angry with her, and the K-Wolf knows it. 'Call me!' is chirpily spoken over the message leaver's last words in a fake happy, mocking tone. Karina's face matches her 'Have a nice day now!' voice, creating new creases in her young face as she smiles falsely, then relaxing her features with a sigh. If she'd known that opposing the match would be this much hassle, she'd of only voiced her opinions a few minutes before the bell. Wise to the world and it's workings, Wolfenden knows in the cut-throat business that is wrestling, especially in the fledgling PWC, most things are of greater importance than a wrestler's feelings... Especially one the owner doesn't really care for to begin with. 'Maybe they'll get the message soon?' Karina ponders over her coffee as she perches on one of the stools at her kitchen diner to read the morning paper, 'Don't call me, I won't call you.'. She hoped her lack of a reply would just have them give up, all she wanted was to have her breakfast in peace... This is what you get for trying to help a girl, the urge to pull the phone from the socket. In most cases, someone trying to back out of a match would be called a coward, Magnum Wolf sure was when the shoe was on the other foot. But to fight Paula would be to destroy her chance of helping Paula... When Karina was gripped in Ariath's Full Nelson, when PMS was berating her... Karina felt no anger, she felt sorrow. Why attack the person their past has made them, when you can help them deal with the cause? Wolfenden has had it easy in life, she's grateful for that... But she knows many haven't. 'Finally, silence!' she mutters, relieved at the break between ringtones. Turning the page and taking a sip from her coffee, everything seems to be peaceful, relaxed again... ...Until the phone rings once more.
