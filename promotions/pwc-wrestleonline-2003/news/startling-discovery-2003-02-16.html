Startling Discovery?

16 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Rumors are flying that Rush Mason is out as the PWC's African American superstar signee, and former IWO and PCW star, Rock Startling is in. "We have it on good sources, that Rock Startling was seen eating dinner in Philadelphia with Ron Starr," said The News' John Owens. "Startling looks to be the man Starr will sign." PWC officials have denied the report. "Mr. Starr has stated, and I quote, that he would 'prefer to sign the most annoying black man on earth, Marlon Wayans, to a contract, than Marlon Starling,'" said PWC spokesperson, Mark Malloy. "Mr. Starr would also like to say, that his hatred for Mr. Wayans stems from the fact that he was cast in the Dungeons and Dragons movie, singlehandedly ruining a life long dream of Starr's that a movie based on his favorite game would be cool."
