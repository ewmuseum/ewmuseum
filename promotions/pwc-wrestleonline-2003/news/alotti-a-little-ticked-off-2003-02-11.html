Alotti a Little Ticked Off

11 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sam Alotti, who faces her second PWC match this week, has reportedly been 'angry and upset' at incidents following her hiring to the Pro Wrestling Circuit. Sammy, as some had dubbed her, was not available for comment but close friend and flatmate Sarah decided to represent her. "She's like, totally not happy with things that have been going on, you know?" Blurted the angry woman "She's been asked to suck off the president, beaten by some lunatic Christian and thrown over ropes like some, like, erm, rag doll or something, you know?" "People wont talk to her and those who do ask her to suck them off! Where's the, like, humanity in that?" When questioned of why Sam wasn’t available for comment, we were greeted by a resounding confusion of answers "She's, like, in bed taking a shower, like, totally!" It was at this point when Sam, who had been doing some shopping, returned and frog-marched Sarah inside, cursing her for 'meddling in things she does not understand. We, of course, will bring you more on this as it develops.
