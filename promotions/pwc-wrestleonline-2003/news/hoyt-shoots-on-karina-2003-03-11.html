Hoyt Shoots on Karina

11 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Always one to enlighten us with his interpretation of God’s will, PWC’s Hoyt Williams is said to have spoken out recently on fellow roster member Karina Wolfenden. But while Magnum Wolf, and even PMS, have troubles with the Boarding Beauty herself, Hoyt has something else about her riling him... Her music. After the events of PWC TV5, Karina’s attitude has noticeably changed, becoming more focused and less care-free. She has also changed her theme to “Sterilise Me” by Pillbox, a Title of song that doesn’t sit well with Hoyt. “When the Lord churned out the first Baby Maker 00001 model, which some call “A Female” he didn’t put an off switch in there, if ya know what I mean?” he explained, “After a while the batteries go flat, but until then they’re supposed to be ripe for the birthing.” Hoyt, known for taking the Bible and putting his own spin on it, continued. “When it’s time, I’ve got my fleet of swimmers guided by the Lord himself ready to pass on my wisdom, and I don’t want them running into a dead end. What next? A theme sung by the ‘Morally Corrupt Street Abortion Clinic Choir’?” Swamped by her own problems away from PWC, Karina was unavailable for comment. But with her best friend in the Circuit, Sam Alotti already having problems with Hoyt, this could be another unwelcome distraction for the troubled Boarding Beauty.
