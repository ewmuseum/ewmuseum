Big In Japan

25 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It has been reported that none other than PWC's Karina Wolfenden has been invited to the land of the rising sun to compete in a special woman's tournament, the aim of which is to showcase woman's wrestling from all around the world. While stars like Tessa Windsor, Brianna Washington and Tempest are all locked down with exclusive contracts, Karina's provisional PWC contract, which is still binding, has no such obstacle in her way. "I'm stoked... It'll be a great experience for me," commented the K-Wolf, "A trip to the Far East and some matches thrown in free.... What's not to dig?" While Wolfenden seems eager, some of PWC's management are less keen on letting their fan-favourite loose on Japan. "Japan?!?! You're kidding, right?" remarked an un-named source, "That girl could injure herself yawning... I've seen the kind of matches they have over there, and we'll expect her back in a full-body cast." Given that in her short tenure in PWC Karina has suffered a knee injury that required several shows off, as well as back pain following Last Man Standing that required a week's rest, PWC officials can be forgiven for their worry... Not liking the prospect of one of their most popular stars coming back on crutches... ...But if Karina is keen on something, history shows they won't have much of a choice. "I'm not one to tie myself down, " added Karina, "But if they want me to commit 100%, Starr had better make up for his past actions."
