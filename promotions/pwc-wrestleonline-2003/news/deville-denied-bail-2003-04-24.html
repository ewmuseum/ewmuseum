Deville Denied Bail

24 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After failing to appear for his hearing on Monday morning, Angelo Deville was temporarily denied bail by the Honourable Justice Hearn. After hearing from the prosecution that police reported Deville unable to appear for unspecified reasons, the judge ruled that a plea for bail would be held this Friday morning at 9am. "And whatever the police are doing that is prohibiting Mr. Deville from his rights and rendering him unable to appear," he added afterward, "had better stop right now." His Honour was unavailable for comment, as were any members of the Trenton police department we tracked down, but Rob Murphy, the lead prosecutor, was more than delighted to share a few words about the situation. "The charge is one of the most serious we know, so I regret I can't comment much at the present time, but there are some things I am at leave to discuss." 'Some things' turned out to be really nothing at all, as he could report very little of what had happened with Deville following the arrest. He didn't know why Deville wasn't there, nor any of Deville's lawyers. "The man's lucky to have eluded us this long. It just shows that even the most careful slip up eventually. "The case against him is airtight. We received a video tape from the night in question showing Mr. Deville ambushing Mr. O'Neil and hogtying him, then dumping him into the back of a car and driving away. We cannot say who provided the tape, but everyone should rest assured that it does exist. Angelo Deville murdered Jason O'Neil, and he is currently in custody for the outrageous crime." He refused to substantiate rumours that more charges were pending, but other sources said the fuzz were deliberating on pressing for extortion, racketeering, and a whole list of other charges. Deville was arrested live on payperview this past Sunday at Capitol Punishment, during the main event he was scheduled to be a part of. Charged with murder and taken into custody on the spot, that is the last anyone on this side of the fence has seen the Crimson Angel. Deville, of course, was unavailable for comment, but we're sure the outspoken mobster will have plenty to say in court tomorrow morning. Will the Deville be released back into the community, or is this the last we've seen of his antics for ten to twenty? PWC officials offered no comment.
