Women in Wrestling

17 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
By Anonymous This past week on PWC TV displayed what a small amount of the woman on the wrestling scene are able to accomplish. It shows that women aren’t just there too look pretty, stand on the aprons and be candy for the fans in bikini matches. They aren’t just there for catfights and too draw an extra few old perverts who want to see a busty blonde. This week's PWC show proved that a women can wrestle. This history of women in wrestling hasn’t been the best, they started off by being valets in the 80s and women wrestlers could hardly hit a headlock. People then saw women in a new light as the 90's rolled in and glamorous females enrolled in the wrestling business seeing it’s potential as a money gaining industry. They strolled down to the ring with their wrestler dawning his championship all glammed up and helped him retain his championship. Soon that was all to change in the mid to late nineties in Philly the role of the woman in the wrestling ring took a turn for the worse. Not only did some wrestlers do the above but male wrestlers used them as objects to get to there opposing. Kidnapping, sexual abuse and pornographic material was used to introduce more males to the gates. Some females where spanked by other women sometimes-even men, stripped down to their very tight small underwear and bras. This era in wrestling destroyed wrestling for women in the indy scene as many women who could wrestle where forced into some of they above by promoters. After a while the mainstream couldn’t stay away. This pushed indies into a bigger plunge hole on exposing women having porn models appear as wrestlers. Having them go near the full way, having buck naked matches teasing the crowd of hungry perverts to come and see naked women fight. Meanwhile mainstream used holidays, Thanksgiving, Christmas and Easter to dress women up and fight in this style matches like Santa Strips and Gravy Bowl match. So women found this degrading an unknown insider from the wrestling business has this too say about this kind of women wrestling. “I believe it is just plain wrong to exploit women the way they are because quite honestly it takes away from the belief that women could actually do something decent in the ring. Women are always being overlooked as true competitors simply because they are being used as sex toys rather than actual competitors.” Today there is a new breed of hard working women wrestlers in the world of wrestling PCW has the likes of Tessa Windsor, Teresa Tomas, Brandi, Brianna Washington, the OSW has Tempest, and promotion PWC has Sam Alotti and Karina Wolfenden. All these women are the revolution all these women give the industry a good name and send it one step forward. There are still some women wrestlers who get money off of the T and A style gimmick but they aren’t what we are interested in.
