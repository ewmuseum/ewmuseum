Loc Taking Monkey Roids?

26 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
One of PWC's newest superstars, The Vietnamese Viper, Charlie Loc, is reportedly taking steroids laced with monkey hormones, as opposed to the more traditional, but equally illegial, bull enhancement steroid concoctions. "He wanted to be able to jump higher and move quicker," said an anonymous source, who claimed to be Loc's dope connection. "To that extent they have worked, but the side effects are his cravings for bananas, and well, his appearance... He is beginning to look a bit like a chimpanzee." The 'pusher' claims despite the fact that he warned Loc that the pills were untested and had to be smuggled from India, Loc has demanded a second monthly supply of the drug. Loc, of course, poo-pooed the report claiming it was just another fabrication of the American media to make Asians and Vietnamese in general, look ridiculous. "I suppose they will next be accusing my people of eating monkey brains," said Loc. "Proposterous I say!" PWC spokesman, Clark Carlisle, said that while it had no comment on this specific rumor, that the company in no way, shape, or form, condones the use of performance enhancing drugs by its superstars. We will bring you more on this breaking story as it develops.
