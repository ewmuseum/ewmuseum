Tota Over Training?

18 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
In recent light of the card for this weeks PWC TV being issued, word is filtering through from reliable sources that Totakoxe Salvador is training extra hard to make sure revenge is indeed his this Saturday. 2GVC member and bitter rival of Totakoxe, Chandler Tsonda looks to take on the Argentinean one-on-one this Saturday night and this has apparently caused Salvador to train harder and longer. "We see him arrive at about 7am and he doesn't leave until 8pm that same night" says the anonymous yet highly reliable source. "He only stops for a one hour lunch break at noon. I don't know how he can do it but I do know what fuels him to do it!" Of course, we here at PWC took the initiative and indeed found Totakoxe Salvador leaving the training facility at 8pm alone. We tried to catch an interview with the Argentinean but he said that he was late and had to leave. The rumour could be true or false. No-one knows at this stage but we will keep you posted. No matter how reliable a source is, the truth is never clear. The only concern we have is that Totakoxe will burn himself out before the match has even begun. Is Tota really training 12 hours a day? If so, is Chandler preparing in any way like this? We may never know but one thing is for sure, a lot of answers will be found this Saturday night, on PWC TV.
