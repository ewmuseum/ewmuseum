New PWC Commercial Airs

8 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A new PWC Commercial aired late Saturday night, hyping the arrival of a new wrestler. --------------------------------------------------- The scene opens upon a mildly dingy but apparently well-lit room, the walls captured in black and white coming out as merely a shallow shade of grey, as it pulls across them slowly, benches littered with the debris of someone having been in the room for extended periods of time: Voice: I've been biding my time... A small towel, its white stained brownish-yellow from sweat; Voice: Working hard, trying all that I can do to make an impression, to take my wrestling to the next level... A black shirt, left in a crumpled heap a few feet to the right against the wall, as an "O" can be made out in clear white lettering upon it; Voice: Waiting for the opportunity to battle for a larger audience... A paper plate on a bench in the middle of the room as the camera speeds up somewhat, turning past another wall, scraps of what appear to have been fettuccine noodles remaining on the surface as well as a plastic fork; Voice: Waiting on opportunities... A pillow in the next corner, the impression of someone having slept upon it clearly indented within the fabric. Voice: But they never came. The rotation starts to slow up, as the final wall of the room comes into view... Voice: Some guys said they thought I just didn't have the skill to compete with their men... A wall broken by an opening, a short walkway to another room, where a man sits on the corner of a bench, hands brought upward and under his chin in thought...Evidently this is his voice narrating. Voice: Others told me I was just not good enough on the microphone to make the fans care like they should.. The view pulls slowly through the hall and into the next room, the man in sight now recognizable as the one from last week's commercial as well, dressed entirely in a pair of light grey speckled wrestling tights, his upper body muscles glimmering in the illumination of the room's sole lit bulb. Voice: Still more told me that I wasn't clean enough as a worker, that I'd end up injuring the guys the fans come to see... The figure looks toward the camera with a heavy glance, speaking the words clearly himself. Guy: They were lying to me, patronizing me. They didn't want to say what was really bothering them... He looks back toward the walls with a smirk, as the view is now entirely within the new room. Guy: That's the way the world works. If you don't have the guts to tell the truth, just throw them a line to keep them in the game, maybe they'll change their ways then... The smirk slowly tilts upward, into a grin from the youthful face of the unknown. Guy: But you'd think they'd realize it by now..I won't change. Not for the other wrestlers..Not for the fans...And sure as hell not for those greedy promoters. The view of him rotates further, now in profile as he shifts his hands onto the bench behind him. Guy: Maybe now...Now, I might get my shot, my opportunity after all this time..My chance to take all of those guys who told me I wasn't cut out for it... Reclining, the man places his hands behind his head as the scene pulls upward slowly..Only to be interrupted by his head once more, popping upward for a sit-up. Guy: ...And make them believers. The scene continues its elevation, speedily passing by the wall to show the sole fluorescent light glowing, just like the previous week... "HE IS COMING". And fades to black. Only for a few more words to appear, same font, but in white as opposed to the black surrounding it now. It only lasts a mere second before fading out, but the message is clear.. "THIS FEBRUARY." ------------------------------------------------ Who is this superstar? Is he a new face, or an old standbye making his return to the squared circle? Make sure you tune into PWC TV to find out!
