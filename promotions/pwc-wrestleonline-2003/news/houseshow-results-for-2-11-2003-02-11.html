Houseshow Results for 2/11

11 February 2003

Pro Wrestling Circuit House Show Results for February 11, 2003 Live from JC Shaner Elementary School, in Mays Landing, NJ Report sent in by: Dave Tomlinson of Egg Harbor, NJ What a great night of wrestling I had the opportunity to witness over in Mays Landing at the old Shaner School. There was a crowd of 350 on hand and it was pretty live. The merchandise stands looked busy as well, with Toomes and O’Neil T-shirts selling well. The show started with ring announcer Bob Kirby heading to the ring to remind fans that tonight’s show was being sponsored in part by Modell’s Sporting Goods. He explained some of the ground rules to the fans, then we were on our way. Simply Cool Jimmy vs. Chris Campbell The show started off nicely, as Simply Cool Jimmy came out and strutted his way to the ring, as Jimmy only can. Campbell came out to good heat. The two tied up a few times, with Jimmy coming out the better man in a few amateur move sequences. Jimmy then flung Campbell into the corner, causing the rookie to hit the turnbuckles hard. Jimmy went to follow up, but Campbell begged off. Jimmy let up, turned around and lit up a cigarette, which allowed time for Campbell to hit him with a low blow. Simply Cool Jimmy doubled over, then got hit with a face buster for his troubles. Jimmy rolled out of the ring, but when he got to his feet and grabbed the apron, Campbell nailed him with a baseball slide. On the outside, Campbell whipped Jimmy into the railing, then the ring steps. Campbell then took Jimmy and rolled him into the ring. Campbell went up top and hit a leg drop. Campbell then hit the Campbell Special DVD and finished off Simply Cool Jimmy. Average Joe vs Magnum Wolf Average Joe came out to no reaction from the crowd. Surprisingly, the cowardly Wolf came out to a decent pop. Average Joe came out fast, beating Wolf to the canvas with a series of punches. That was the end of Average Joe’s offense, however, as he tried for a running splash in the corner, but Wolf got out of the way. Wolf proceeded to smash Average Joe’s head into the turnbuckle a few times, then nailed him with a sidewalk slam. Wolf pulled up Joe and hit him with a running powerslam into the turnbuckles. With Joe helpless, Magnum picked him up and hit him with Magnum Force, for the win. An Impromptu Riot After the Wolf match, a shocking turn of events took place, the man dubbed as the “Keeper of the Masked Monster Penance”, Jacob Solomon came towards the ring, demanding time to speak. PWC security, led by PWC Security director, Jack McGriff, was quick to come out and to stop Solomon. Solomon and McGriff had words, with Solomon asking McGriff why he was afraid to let him speak. The former PCW Tag Team Champion then informed Solomon he was afraid of nothing. Solomon said if it was true, that the 6’10” McGriff could prove it tonight by facing Penance in the ring…one on one. McGriff though about it for a moment, then with egging from the crowd and his security staff, he agreed. Intermission During the intermission, PWC officials took up donations for Porky Bunson, who they said lay paralyzed from the waist down in the hospital. 2GVC vs AFL-CIO After the intermission, Jimmy Nguyen and Chandler Tsonda, collectively known as 2GVC came out to face local favorites, AFL-CIO. AFL-CIO looked woefully out of shape from their PCW days, with both Plummer and Carpenter looking well over 300 pounds each. 2GVC looked great in this match, showing teamwork, speed and technical skills, that the former PCW champions could not keep up with. Tsonda had tons of heat with the crowd, and Nguyen, while not a crowd favorite, brought the crowd to its feet on three occasions with three high flying moves, eventually pinning Plummer with his End of Sound 450 splash. All in all an impressive win for 2GVC. Romulus vs. Masahiro Zangetsu Romulus came out to huge heat. God, the crowd hates this guy. Masahiro came out to a decent pop. This match started off quickly as the former PCW TV champ attacked Masahiro as soon as he entered the ring. Romulus then laid into Masahiro by stomping on him several times with his sandles. Romulus went for the Roman Solution, but Masahiro got to the ropes. Romulus moved in to attack Masahiro immediately after being forced to break his hold, but got a chop to the neck, followed by a series of kicks, finally dropping on a spinning roundhouse kick. Masahiro went up top, but Romulus rolled out of the ring. Masahiro cared little, simply crossbody blocking Romulus to the floor on the outside. Both men were down. After a moment, both men staggered up, but Romulus used the olive leaves he wears as a crown to scratch at Masahiro’s eyes. The Roman then laid into Masahiro on the outside with a series of knife edged chops, then rolled himself into the ring. Hoping for a count out, Masahiro slide back into the ring, still having trouble seeing. Romulus tried to take advantage, but got super kicked for his trouble. Masahiro made the cover to pick up the win. Main Event Jack McGriff vs. Penance McGriff came out, and while still imposing, looked a bit out of shape. Penance came out with Solomon, and sheesh is this guy huge. He looks bigger in person than he does on TV. McGriff came out and attacked Penance with thundering right hands and knees to the midsection. They had no effect, however, as the one man tag team champion practically threw the 300-plus pound McGriff across the ring. McGriff came back across the ring, but was dropped by the most devastating clothesline I have ever seen Wasting little time, Penance stormed across the ring and grabbed McGriff, gorilla press slamming him into a backbreaker. This lead to his infamous crucifix powerbomb into piledriver, finishing off McGriff in just over a minute. It was perhaps the most impressive performance I have ever seen. Post match, Penance roared and left the ring with Solomon, who told fans at ringside that they had just witnessed invincibility. McGriff needed medical attention, but eventually left under his own power, to a decent pop from the crowd. All in all it was a good show and I am glad I went. Biggest Pops 1.
