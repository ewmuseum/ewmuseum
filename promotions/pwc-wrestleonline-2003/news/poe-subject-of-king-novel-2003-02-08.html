Poe Subject of King Novel?

8 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
As of late, popular rumour has it that fellow Bangor, Maine native of Gideon Poe and best selling author Stephen King has plans of introducing a character in his latest book, which many believe is largely based on Gideon Poe himself. It has become no secret as of late that the two men are close friends with both being Bangor's largest claims to fame. The book, so far untitled, is supposedly set to feature a dark mystical character and follow his rise through a mental depression as he battles spiritual evils in his childhood home. So far artists compositions of the character uncannily resemble Gideon Poe and many are already speculating a role in the book to be taken by Gideon's own twisted manager Jeremiah O'Barr and Poe's own long time companion Roland the eagle. What roles exactly these people and creatures will, if at all, take on in the book are still very much unclear. Yet many people are even going as far to guarantee that the book will be titled "The Shade", which of course is a popular alias that Gideon Poe goes under. Although whether any of Poe's foes in PWC will be used as a base for any other characters in the novel is still undetermined, yet very doubtful. Although we were unable to get any word from Gideon Poe or Mr. King himself, we will keep you updated on any news if and when it becomes available.
