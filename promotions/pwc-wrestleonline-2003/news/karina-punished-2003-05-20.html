Karina Punished

20 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Following the events of PWC TV just last night, a shocking Main Event has been scheduled for this week's show, and a match that is as close a match to the "David v Goliath" formula as you will find, despite the gender of one of the combatants. After Karina Wolfenden refused to raise her hands to Paula Michelle Smith in what was scheduled to be their match, and in defiance of PWC CEO Ron Starr, the Circuit's owner has thrown her in at the deep end in a match that can only be described as "Punishment".. And that's "Punishment" in the truest sense of the word, for this Saturday's Main Event reads... Main Event Singles Match Karina Wolfenden v Penance That's right, after refusing to fight her chosen opponent last week, Karina will have to fight with every bit of strength, speed and heart she has... And even that might not be enough against the Monster that has stamped his physical dominance over the Pro Wrestling Circuit since day one. "Let this be a lesson to anyone in this company who thinks they can defy me," read the memo released by Ron Starr following the match announcement, "I run this place, I say who fights who... And if you don't want to fight in a match... Then I can always find one you'll HAVE to fight in. Good luck, Karina. You'll need more than that." Despite the mammoth task ahead of her, when contacted about the news, Karina Wolfenden seemed un-fazed by the challenge, "Bring it on!" being her only words. Are those words the fighting spirit that Karina need to pull off one of the biggest upsets in wrestling history? Or are they just a sign of naive over-confidence? Saturday we will see. Penance vs. the K-Wolf, by order of Ron Starr, only on PWC TV.
