Talent Gets Cut

28 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This evening, PWC owner Ron Starr - as he so gracefully put it - "Made a few more apartments available for rent" by releasing Byron Calver, the Deathmatch Samurai and Total Violation from their contracts. "I didn't care, the fans didn't care... Not that they matter... So I fired them," coolly remarked Starr, "Problem with that?" Word from the Circuit's management is that others could follow in the path of the 5 released stars in an attempt to free up funds for a new signing spree. With such established talents as Angelo Deville, Rhett Locke and J-Con on the roster and on large contracts, Starr seems keen to conserve money wherever he can, and especially on contracts so those not pulling their weight are walking a thin line.
