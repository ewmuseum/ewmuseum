Pro Wrestling Circuit House Show Results for May 20, 2003 Live from Paul Robeson Community Elementary School, in New Brunswick, NJ Report sent in by: Warren Nash of Branchville, NJ What a great night of wrestling I had the opportunity to witness over in New Brunswick at the old Paul Robeson School. There was a crowd of about three hundred on hand and it was pretty lively. The merchandise stands looked busy as well, with Axtell and Penance T-shirts selling well. The show started with ring announcer Bob Kirby heading to the ring to remind fans that tonight’s show was being sponsored in part by Quaye’s Sporting Goods. He explained some of the ground rules to the fans, then we were on our way. He also said that this would be the house show that the Alliance Division rules would be in effect at for this week. Hence why I’m sending this in, as I guess it could be important. Simply Cool Jimmy vs. Jonathan Conspiracy The show started off nicely, as Simply Cool Jimmy came out and strutted his way to the ring, as Jimmy only can. J-Con came out to decent heat. The two tied up a few times, with Jimmy coming out the better man in a few amateur move sequences. J-Con then flung Jimmy into the corner, causing the rookie to hit the turnbuckles hard. J-Con went to follow up, but Jimmy begged off. Jimmy slid out of the ring, turned around and lit up a cigarette, which allowed time for J-Con to nail him with a baseball slide. On the outside, J-Con whipped Jimmy into the railing, then the ring steps. J-Con then took Jimmy and rolled him into the ring. J-Con grabbed him and hit a power-bomb hard. J-Con then went up top and hit the Conspiracy Theory and finished off Simply Cool Jimmy. Ryo “Neon” Kazaki vs Charlie Loc Neon came out to a reasonably good cheer from the crowd. The cowardly Loc came out to a fairly decent boo. Loc takes a butterfly suplex from Neon. Brainbuster suplex by Neon, who apparently is in the mood to bust some brains. Hooks the leg for a two count. Loc kicks Ryan Kazaki in the gut to reverse the momentum. Stiff chop lights up Neon. Running knee lift from Charlie Loc. There's a two count on the pin. Death valley driver by Loc, Neon is down and hurt. Pinfall attempt gets a 2.9999. Big backdrop on Kazaki, executed well. Neon pulls a mule kick out of nowhere. Loc takes a quick lariat. Hooks the leg for a two count. Neon hits a great swinging DDT on Loc. Pin : 1 - 2 - 2.9999999. Vertical suplex by Loc. Big forearm by Loc, ripping a page from the NOAH playbook. Neon ducks a clothesline attempt. Fallaway slam by Loc. Powerbomb on Loc. Loc gets whipped into the corner. Neon charges in, but into a pair of raised boots. Loc uses a roll up, with feet on the second rope! The referee hasn't seen it: 1....2....3!! Even I booed my ass off for that one. Old Friends/Enemies Jake Keeton walked out to the ring like he owned the place and started going on about how he’s so great. Maybe he is, but I’m yet to see it. Anyway, then out walks Alexander Miyagawa, the guy who used to job to him regularly, and the current PWC commish. Anyway, the two start talking back and forth and Keeton suddenly announces a challenge to Miyagawa right there and then. Miyagawa takes off his t-shirt and throws it to the outside and the two get ready to fight! Keeton walks into a high dropkick from Miyagawa. Miya misses a clothesline. Stiff high kick on Miya by Keeton who has evidently been watching some old PURE tapes recently. The referee bumps after catching a wild right hand and is down. Tiger suplex on Keeton, right from out of the Miyagawa playbook. Hooks the leg, but the referee is still out. Jake Keeton takes the advantage after a i-block-your-punch-you-don't-block-mine routine. Keeton uses a basement dropkick to the knee. Super frankensteiner on Miyagawa, who hit hard. Hooks the leg for a two count. Back heel kick off the second rope, Miya goes down. Hooks the leg for a close fall. Alexander Miyagawa avoids a Keeton avalanche. Flying cross body off the top rope! I'll give a 0.8 on the Steamboat scale for that effort. Cover, but there's a last second kick-out. Keeton kicks Miyagawa in the gut to reverse the momentum. The bell rings, and this match has come to a time limit draw. Alexander Miyagawa offers a handshake to Jake Keeton...and he accepts it! Intermission During the intermission, PWC officials asked the crowd to report any sightings of Angello Deville to the head office at the front of the hall. Kohana vs. Gideon Poe After the intermission, Kohana came on out to face Gideon Poe. The entrance was quick, and they went straight into it. Kohana strikes Poe. The Native American hits a sloppy bulldog off the ropes. There's a two count on the pin. Kohana gets taken down out of nowhere. Poe snap suplexes Kohana. Kohana gets caught with a short powerbomb from Poe. There's a two count on the pin. Wicked suplex out of the corner from Poe, executed well. Pin : 1 - 2 - 2.9999999. Big dropkick by Poe, who got good elevation. Kohana powers out of a headlock. Powerslam from Kohana on Snow. Hooks the leg for a two count. Flapjack from Kohana on Poe. Pin, but Poe is out just before the three count. Big forearm by Kohana.. Poe takes a weak kick. Kohana misses a clothesline. Kohana gets caught with a short powerbomb from Poe. Poe plants Kohana with an exploder suplex. Kohana can barely stand. Here it comes - Cadaver Theory. 1....2...3, it's finished. The Revival vs. Special Delivery Miyagawa came out first to introduce the Alliance Division teams and to announce this as an official league match in the division. Hoyt bodyslams Sammy. Another bodyslam by Hoyt. There's a two count on the pin. Tag to Propaganda. Props \ Hoyt whip Sammy into the corner. Hoyt Williams whips Propaganda in for a hard clothesline to follow-up. Cover for a two count. Weak slam from Propaganda. Sammy pulls a mule kick out of nowhere. Super frankensteiner on Propaganda, who hit hard. Cover for a two count. Tag to Big Chuck. Powerslam from Chuck on Propaganda. Pinfall attempt gets a 2.9999. Chuck slams Propaganda down. Penance would be proud, no doubt. Propaganda reverses a waistlock. Uninspiring brawling from Propaganda. Tag to Hoyt Williams. Chuck receives some punishment. Tag between Chuck and Sammy. Running knee lift from Hoyt. Romulus and Colossus come running down the aisle with chairs! Hoyt and Sammy continue fighting, unaware of the intrusion. Romulus slides in and blasts Sammy with a chair to the head! The Roman climbs out of the ring, the damage done! Sammy can barely stand. Crucified and Saved! 1....2....3. Romulus and Colossus aren't satisfied! They decide to continue the assault on Sammy and Chuck, stomping them into the mat until referees pull them away! Main Event First Blood Match Karina Wolfenden vs. Colossus Both headed out, Colossus without Romulus and Karina with a big pop with people expecting this as the sort of warm up match for her main event this Saturday night on TV. Full nelson slam on Karina. Karina counters an avalanche with a raised foot to the face. Colossus takes a kick to the chest, and staggers back. Flying elbow from Karina, barely hitting the target. Colossus walks into a spinning heel kick, evidently feeling that the 'duck' tactic is over-rated. Colossus pulls a mule kick out of nowhere. Spear by Colossus. Massive lariat, apparently Colossus loves the things. Running knee lift from Colossus. Karina takes the advantage a series of blocked punches. Colossus takes a knee lift from Karina. Slingshot clothesline by Karina. Colossus charges, Karina moves, and the referee is conveniently placed to get knocked out. Colossus quickly grabs a chair...and hits Karina with it, drawing blood! Karina recovers quickly, and hits a low blow...then wipes the blood off her head and smears it on Colossus and exits the ring. That has to be at least a 0. 7 on the smart tactic scale. The referee recovers, sees the blood....and calls for the bell, awarding the match to Karina! After the match, Penance marches straight down to ringside and Solomon runs his mouth off at Karina, after the end of it Karina gets a mic and instead of speaking to him, just shows him a raised finger!!!! Solomon has to almost pull Penance back. Overall a good show, and some excellent finishes. Biggest Pops 1. The Raised Finger 2. Karina’s Tactic 3. Miyagawa when he accepted Keeton’s challenge 4. Poe's Victory 5. Special Delivery's antics Biggest Heat 1. Arrival of Penance 2. Colossus busting Karina open 3. J-Con 4. The Result of Neon vs Loc 5. The Revival’s Victory
