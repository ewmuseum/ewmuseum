Ready... Set... Draw!

4 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Atlantic City, NJ- Despite the Pennsylvania Lottery representative’s mysterious absence, the draw for the first eight competitors in the Last Man Standing tournament went off without a single hitch! ...Well, at least in the eyes of Ron Starr and Clark Carlisle. Would it appear the same to one special member of the roster? Who would find themselves moving one step closer to Gold? Click the link to find out. The Television Title has been fought over to death by Jake Keeton and Hoyt Williams during the past month, and this week, like all the others before it, promised to be different. Could the Television Title finally find itself a champion? How about with a little bit of divine intervention? Only this week’s PWC TV has the awe-inspiring answer to the much-asked question: What Would Hoyt Do? Also... Is Magnum Wolf really stupid enough to finger the mob? Trouble in the 2GVC camp? Can Chandler Tsonda impress the importance of his tag team title on The Masked Mullet? Could PMS having Karina Wolfenden "tied up" live up to Byron's wildest fantasies? Will the first-ever PWC Commissioner please stand up? How many must fall sacrifice to Penance’s monstrous appetite for destruction? ...And what will our beloved champion, Jim Axtell, have to say about it? Only one way to find out...
