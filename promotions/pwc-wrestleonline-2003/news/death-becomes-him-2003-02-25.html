Death Becomes Him

25 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This past Saturday night on PWC TV, we witnessed what many speculate was the attempted murder of Jason O'Neil at the hands of Angelo Deville. After distracting the Scarecrow with a dead carp painted in his likeness, Deville knocked him unconscious and carried him out to the parking lot like a bag of garbage. Dumping him into the trunk of a car and the possession of two shady-looking individuals, he told O'Neil in no uncertain times that it was time to end the games -- that it was time for O'Neil to die. The trunk slammed shut and the car sped away, leaving Deville to pronounce that "O'Neil sleeps with the fishes." When the time for the main event came, however, O'Neil charged into the ring from the crowd and proceeded to beat Angelo from turnbuckle to turnbuckle. Even though it wasn't enough to put him over the Crimson Angel, O'Neil somehow escaped the murderous and diabolical plot, apparently thwarting Deville's plans to thin the number one contenders to the PWC World Title to three. We tried contacting Jason to find out exactly what happened and how he got away, but he was unavailable for comment. Deville, on the other hand, was candid as usual when I reached him by phone. "What the hell are you talking about?" He demanded when asked about his actions on the show. "I wasn't even there." After pointing out that thousands of witnesses saw him there repeatedly, including seeing him live for roughly twenty minutes in the main event, he became a little more cooperative, but not very. "Oh... That show... Right. Well, as always, I dealt with the Scarecrow and showed him once again that he isn't fit to be wrestling me. What else do you want to know?" Then, becoming appalled when I mentioned fish and car trunks, Deville broke in angrily. "What are you -- an idiot? You make me out to be some kind of mobster...! I'm a respectable wrestling champion and businessman who couldn't be bothered playing arts and crafts with fish or trying to kill the competition outside of the ring. Didn't you see the show? I do it well enough inside the ropes." I pointed out that Magnum Wolf released video tape evidence of him scribbling marker around the fish's eyes. Silence. "Magnum Wolf better watch where he's pointing that... thing... of his..." He grated quietly after a long moment's pause. "Or it'll get detached from his body. If he's still jealous about FSW, maybe I'll have to remind him who calls the shots. But seriously... Do you really believe anything that Magnum Wolf shows you? The guy's nothing more than a demented pervert who can't even succeed in getting rid of one woman, no matter how he lies or who he hires. A woman!" "Like you can't get rid of Jason O'Neil?" I asked, feeling brave and stupid all at once. "If I tried to do away with O'Neil," the scolding in his voice seemed to make the receiver warm in my hands, "he'd be dead by now. If you want to badger someone we know got away with something, why don't you track down OJ Simpson? My sources tell me he's Ron's controversial black superstar, and I wouldn't put it past him." He stops to snicker. "Black superstar... What an oxymoron. But I digress... Let's cut right to the chase here. If I want someone dead, they die. Period. Tune in this week and see. Or, if you're in a real fuckin' hurry, quote anything I've said to you today -- I'll make sure you find out personally, goombah." Then the line went d-- ...quiet. After contacting the police, who said they'd look into matters, my journalistic integrity demanded I release this to the masses. My life has been threatened, and unless I misunderstood his words, so has the life of someone who will be at the show this week. Is Angelo Deville going to try to kill his nemesis Jason O'Neil... Again?
