Williams to Honor Shuttle Crew

6 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PWC wrestler and out spoken evangelist, Hoyt Williams, will be holding a special prayer service for the astronauts of the Columbia. The service will be held at the first church of Christ in Atlantic City, New Jersey and is open to the public. Service starts at 6pm with a fitness routine geared towards the lord. "It's a terrible tragedy seeing seven people die in a blaze above the skies," said Williams, from his Atlantic City apartment. "My thoughts and prayers are with the families and friends of those astronauts. However we must remember that God punishes those who do not believe. There was a Hindu and an Israeli on board and I'm sure their lack of faith was the cause." Despite the fact many reporters took offense with his comments against the Indian-American and Israeli astronauts, Williams continued his 'sales pitch.' "Fat free, rice paddy, snack cakes will be served after the commencement weightlifting ceremony," shilled Williams. "Come feel the warmth of God and remember those who have perished. Don't forget Hoyt works directly with God on a pro bono basis. So all free will donations are accepted. If God wanted us in space he would have given us wings. Please make plans to join me, and believe, before it's too late for you like those seven fallen heathens. Williams has set up a hotline for info regarding the prayer vigil. For more information call 888-2-Save Me.
