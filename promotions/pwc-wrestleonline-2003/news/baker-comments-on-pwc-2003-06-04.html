Baker Comments on PWC

4 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Indy superstar 'Unbelievable' Bryan Baker had plenty to say about the rumors that he was close to signing a contract with the Pro Wrestling Circuit, when reporters caught up to him at small indy show in surburban Winnipeg. "Well, can you blame PWC for wanting to sign me?" queried Baker. "There is no question that I could step in and help their promotion take the step to the next level." Baker wasn't revealing specifics of his discussions, if any, with PWC officials. "I think it would be premature to detail any discussions I may have had with PWC at this point," said Baker. "Right now, I can't say either way if PWC fans will get to be the man who has so much talent that you have to 'see it to believe it.' Baker, 22, hails from Calgary, Alberta, Canada, and is known by knowledgable wrestling fans as not only a death-defying risk taker, but for his near flawless mat technic. We, of course, will continue to monitor this story and bring you more when it develops.
