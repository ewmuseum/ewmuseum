Phoenix to Address Crowd

20 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Word has it that former UWF and ProWC superstar, Jay Phoenix, is going to make some sort of statement this Saturday night on PWC TV, fueling speculation that he may retire. Phoenix, who had sought a spot on the PWC roster prior to failing a physical examination, confirmed that he would be in Hammonton for this Saturday night's show, but refused to comment on the purpose of his presence. PWC officials continued to state that while they like Phoenix and respect his work in the past, that he is damaged goods. "Jay Phoenix was a great competitor, but medical reports do not lie," said PWC spokesman, Mark Malloy. "It would be a risk for us to allow him to compete, and more importantly, a risk to his physical well-being." What will Jay Phoenix have to say Saturday night? Tune into PWC TV to find out!
