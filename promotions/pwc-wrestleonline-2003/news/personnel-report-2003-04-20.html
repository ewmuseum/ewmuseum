Personnel Report

20 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PWC spokesperson Mark Malloy had plenty of personnel issues to report on at a Sunday morning press conference following the airing of Capitol Punishment. Here is a summary of the press conference. Both Sebastian Toomes and Clay Vendetta are expected to miss 9-12 months with leg and head injuries caused by the viscious attack by Penance at Capitol Punishment. More detailed medical reports on their conditions will be known later in the week. Angelo Deville will be arraigned on first degree murder charges on Monday afternoon, in New York City Superior Court. Malloy had no other information available. Sam Alotti's contract has indeed been terminated. Malloy confirmed the signings of Rhett Locke and Charlie Loc. Warpath has several nagging injuries which will keep him out of action for three to four months. Police and PWC officials are clueless as to exactly what happened to Byron Calver at Capitol Punishment, but are actively investigating the situation. Calver is in stable condition at Mercer County Community Hospital, recovering from his injuries as a result of the "crucifixtion" he suffered." Malloy promised a second press conference with more information to come Wednesday of this week. We will, of course, bring all of the details of that briefing as it occurs.
