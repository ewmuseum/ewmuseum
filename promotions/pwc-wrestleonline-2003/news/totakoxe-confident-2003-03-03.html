Totakoxe Confident

3 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After an early morning training session, PWC resident interviewer, Jason Maddux, caught up with the now established PWC member, Totakoxe Salvador. Maddux reported that the good friend of Jim Axtell is confident that he will recover in time for the proposed tag-team match against their bitter rivals, 2nd Generation Viet Cong. It had earlier been reported that Jim Axtell had been hospitalized after his defeat to Penance. The recorded interview directly proved this theory. "Jim Axtell may be in hospital at the minute but he will be fit for the match this Saturday night. Nothing can keep Jim away from the ring, he proved that himself. I mean he even took on the monster Penance without fear," said the Argentinean. "Let's just say the worst comes to the worst, how will you break it to 2GVC that the match is off?" Big Jay asked. "If the worst comes to the worst? The best thing for me to do now is to think positively. If he is unable to make it to PWC TV this Saturday, you can count on me competing one-on-two against those Viet Congs," replied Salvador. In response to Jason's statement that "Axtell is more important than a shot at the tag titles”, Salvador said, "It's not about titles! It's more personal than simple materialism now! I will not let 2GVC get away without an attempt to halt them on my part. I know that Axtell would want me to take on Nguyen and Tsonda if he can't make it. Mark my words, though, Axtell will be there this Saturday and everyone will be aware of his presence!" After those words it is reported that Totakoxe left the training arena to pay a visit to Axtell in the hospital. Will Axtell be fit for the next edition of PWC TV? Will Salvador be digging his own grave by facing 2GVC alone if the worst DOES come to the worst? Only time will unveil the answers to these questions and only time will tell how far out of control the hatred held between the team of Salvador and Axtell and that of 2GVC will spiral!
