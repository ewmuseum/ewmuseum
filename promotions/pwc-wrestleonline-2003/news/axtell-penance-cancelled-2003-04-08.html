Axtell/Penance Cancelled!

8 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This breaking story has just come down from CEO Ron Starr's office! In a statement issued by Starr, read by his legal council Clark Carlisle, the scheduled match between Jim Axtell and Penance has been cancelled. "Due to the recent severe injuries suffered by Jim Axtell, and acting on the advise of his own doctors, the match between Mr. Axtell and Penance scheduled to take place this weekend at Capitol Punishment has been cancelled," said Carlisle. "It is in the best interest of both Mr. Axtell and PWC that he stay at home and rest his injuries so as to not further them....and subsequently raise our insurance rates. PWC is a growing corporation and cannot afford to have money taken from its pockets by greedy insurance companies that wish to double our rates if Mr. Axtell wrestles. Perhaps we can reschedule this match in four weeks when Mr. Axtell returns to action, if he does indeed return." After getting word of this shocking announcement, Jim Axtell was understandibly upset. "They did WHAT?! They can't do that!" exclaimed Axtell over the phone. "This isn't over!" As of now it does appear to be over, however. The match between Axtell and Penance, a chance for Axtell to finally exact revenge on the Masked Monster, is off. No word yet on a possible replacement opponent for Penance, or if he will be added to the Tag Team Title match at the PPV.
