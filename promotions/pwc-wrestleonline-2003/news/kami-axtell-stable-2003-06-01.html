Kami Axtell Stable

1 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After what looked to be a serious and tragic situation last night on PWC TV, we have received word that Kami Axtell is resting comfortably at a local hospital. "Mrs. Axtell is doing just fine," said Dr. Sigmond Fraud. "In the heat of the altercation she had last night, her heart rate and blood pressure increased dramatically. This caused some discomfort in her chest and stomach area, giving her the illusion of trouble with her baby. After we were able to get her to calm down, the pain went away and everything was back to normal. She and the baby are both fine and should be out of the hospital either late today or early tomorrow." After hearing the good news, the PWC World Champion Jim Axtell was relieved. "I was so scared last night," said Jim from the same hospital his wife is in as he recovered from the beating he took from Penance and Porky Bunson. "I thought that we were going to lose our baby. If that would have happened...." Axtell paused, shaking his head slowly. "I don't know what I would have done." When asked why Kami was even at the event, Jim had this to say. "I don't know why she was there. I guess she thought she had to confront Porky face to face. She's a strong willed woman, you know. If she sets her mind to something she's gonna do it whether I like it or not." A coy smile crept across Axtell's face. "I guess that's one of the things I love about her." As this sage between Jim and Kami Axtell and Jacob Solomon, Penance, and now Porky Bunson continues, we look forward to the upcoming match at Last Man Standing. There is a slight roadblock on the horizon for Mr. Axtell, however, in the form of Angelo Deville this Saturday night.
