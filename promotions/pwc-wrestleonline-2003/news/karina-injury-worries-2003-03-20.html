Karina Injury Worries

20 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After the injury she suffered during her match with Magnum Wolf at PWC TV, PWC officials requested that Karina Wolfenden submit herself for a medical on Wednesday afternoon. Karina, keen to shrug off the injury and continue, had not been on contact with the Federation about her condition so was asked to be checked over by PWC’s medical team. “Her knee was as we expected,” commented PCW’s head physician, “She’s bandaged it up tight, iced it regularly and kept her weight off it. It’s still tender, but if she continues with her course of painkillers and doesn’t do anything stupid in the next 10 days, she could be in the ring again as soon as next week’s PWC TV.” Even though her knee wasn’t troubling them, her weight was. Karina was listed at 164 pounds, and that weight was accurate 11 days ago, but during her check up she weighed in at only 153 pounds. “The weight loss is quite worrying, especially given how in-active she has been since Saturday night.” said the physician. “She said she’s been eating normally, but I couldn’t help to notice how tired she seemed, and how pale her complexion looked.” With Karina becoming increasingly detached from PWC away from the cameras, nobody really knows just how she is coping with the recent events in her life, not even her closest friend in PWC, Sam Alotti. Karina is expected to still be at PWC TV this weekend, against medical advice, so hopefully an insight into her current state can be gained then. PWC officials with give updates as they have them on the state, both physical and mental, of their Boarding Beauty.
