Tag Team Titles Still On Line

10 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
As we have seen from this week's press announcements from PWC, several Pay Per View matches are in jeopardy or have already been cancelled. However, despite questions concerning certain competitors' health, there is one match that WILL go on: The Tag Team Title Match. Despite pleading from the exhausted tag champions, 2GVC, Ron Starr has said that the fans will get the two title matches they paid for. In fact, Starr went so far as to say that if two 2GVC members do not show up for the match, it will continued as planned, and the winner will STILL receive the tag team belts. At the news of this, a telephone call was placed into the hotel of Chandler Tsonda, whose first response was a loud groan. However, after this, Tsonda quickly switched gears, mumbled something about "this being a perfect time for it" and then abruptly hung up. What exactly this is a perfect time for, we have no idea, but knowing what the tag champions are capable of, there's no limit to what they'd do to get out of defending in their current state. More can be found out on the champs' status on this week's edition of PWC TV!
