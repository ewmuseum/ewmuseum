PWC Going National?

23 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Rumor has it that PWC officials are currently negotiating with the Primetime Network, to fill the professional wrestling programming void left behind when the RUSH promotion closed earlier this year. "We know for a fact Clark Carlisle has been negotiating with Primetime Network officials for some time now," said Wrestlesmack.com reporter, Nate Nichols. "Its just a matter of time." PWC officials denied the report. "We neither have the resources or the production equipment to make such a move at this time," said Carlisle, when contacted via telephone. "Perhaps online wrestling sheets should spend more time reporting facts and less time creating fables." Is Carlisle telling the truth? We will find out in the days to come.
