Rumors Fly About Tag Match

20 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Rumors are abundant backstage and flying after an alleged run-in between Jacob Solomon and Chandler Tsonda. The rumors all are similar that they mention some sort of bribe paid from the side of 2GVC to Penance's manager. After Tsonda's less than joyous reaction to the news that he had to face Penance with tag team partner JImmy Nguyen, he didn't seem too eager to face the beast. However, no one can discern whether this rumor has any truth, and if so, what exactly is the truth? The stories go from a paid "hit" on another PWC'er to a bribe to throw the match to a contribution to the Neo-Eden Compound's following in return for immunity from Penance's wrath. However, no one on the side of 2GVC will confirm it and, as usual, Solomon and Penance were nowhere to be found during the work week. Fans will be waiting with baited breath, though, when the first match of the night opens. In the first title defense in PWC's history, the Second Generation Viet Cong will take on the monster known as Penance and the rumors will certainly be put to rest through whatever means. However, will we see Penance and the Vietnamese tag team declare a temporary alliance, or is the press distorting this far beyond what the facts are? We'll know Saturday night.
