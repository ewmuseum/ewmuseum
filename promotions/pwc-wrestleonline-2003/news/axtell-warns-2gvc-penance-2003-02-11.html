Axtell Warns 2GVC; Penance

11 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jim Axtell, who just narrowly missed a chance at being one of PWC's first two tag champions last week when Porky Bunson was unable to defeat Penance in the main event battle royal, will continue to fight his enemies...alone. The Nebraska-born Axtell will fight on in the memory of Porky Bunson's short-lived, yet promising career, whether or not he's got back from anyone in PWC. "I will not let those two get away with this," said Axtell, referring to Chandler Tsonda and Jimmy Nguyen. "My wife and I are working on things, but once I reach Pomona, my every thought will be directed towards taking out the Second Generation Viet Cong. But, I want that masked freak known as Penance to know, that I have not forgotten what he did to Porky, and his day will come...whether it be sooner, or later!" That was part of statement Axtell gave some of PWC's media who were wondering about his stature in PWC now that he has no true allies, with Clay Vendetta focused on his own problems, mainly the PWC Heavyweight Title. Nguyen and Tsonda, who make up the 2GVC forces, made their "debut" under that name last week before Nguyen's match and are said to be planning "something special" for Porky and Axtell that will "be a ceremony to remember," as a backstage PWC source put it. We can only watch and hope that 2GVC is stopped this week on PWC TV.
