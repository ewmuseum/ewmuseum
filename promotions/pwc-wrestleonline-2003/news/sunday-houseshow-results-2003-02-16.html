Sunday Houseshow Results

16 February 2003

House Show Results for Sunday, February 16th, 2003 by Kenny Simmonson of Margate, NJ About 65 brave souls braved the driving snow in Southern New Jersey to make it out to PWC’s House Show Sunday afternoon in the gymnasium of Atlantic City High School, and found themselves treated to an absolutely marvelous show. Every fan in attendance was given a new PWC Rancid T-shirt, which only leads me to believe after last nights edition of PWC TV, that Rancid is gone or one his way to being gone from the roster. That’s cool, because I didn’t like that guy anyway. Match #1 Special Delivery vs. AFL-CIO I almost couldn’t believe my eyes when those two pizza delivery guys came out and began distributing pizza to the fans. With only a few fans able to make it to the show, my brother and I were able to scarf two pieces each, which ruled. Then the Pizza guys climbed into the ring, and stood there. When AFL-CIO came out of the back along with Union Boss Peroni, I was shocked to find out we were going to see the Pizza guys, who were announced as the team of Special Delivery, consisting of Sammy Bello (the little guy) and Chuck D’Andrea (the fat one,) were actually going wrestle. Bill Plummer started off with the fat guy, little Sammy Bello. Union Boss Peroni was at ringside telling Bello and D’Andrea they were not real Italians. Plummer used his size to throw Bello down a bit at the start, throwing the sub-200 lb Bello into the turnbuckles in each corner after locking up. On the fifth time, Bello baseball slide through Plummer’s legs, and rolled him up and got a two count. Plummer kicked out and got to his feet, but Bello was quick, drop kicking him to the back of the legs, knocking him to the ground. In the corner, Mike Carpenter of AFL-CIO begged for the tag, while Sammy used one of his towels to choke the much bigger Plummer. Referee Bobby Howard took away his towel and forced the break. Plummer charged Bello off the break going for a lariat, but Bello ducked it and kicked him in the back, towards his partner, Big Chuck D’Andrea I the corner, who hit him with a big right hand. Bello headed to the corner, tagged in D’Andrea, then the pair picked up Plummer and superplexed him. Bello left the ring, and D’Andrea hit the prone Plummer with a series of standing elbow drops, which seemed pretty tough coming from a fat guy like that. D’Andrea then ran back and forth from the ropes and went for a standing splash, but Carpenter, pulled his partner to the corner, leaving no one home when the fat man landed. Carpenter tagged himself in and went at D’Andrea with right hands, driving him into the corner. D’Andrea took them, then returned with a massive headbutt…then another of his own, leaving Carpenter on la-la land, Carpenter staggered into the corner and caught a big splash from D’Andrea, which practically shook the whole ring. Carpenter was down and out. D’Andrea dragged Carpenter to the middle of the ring, then went to his corner and tagged in Bello. Bello climbed to the top rope, then D’Andrea pressed him to the sky, and launched him into a huge frog splash, onto Carpenter for the 1-2-3 and the victory. All in all, Special Delivery looked better than I thought they would. Rating: ** Match #2 Simply Cool Jimmy vs “The Virgin” Byron Calver Calver came out to a mixed reaction from the crowd. I, like many people, am not sure what to make of this guy. His opponent came out a moment later in the form of Simply Cool Jimmy, who appeared to have what could only be described as a skanky go-go dancer with him. Jimmy took the microphone and said, as only Jimmy can, that he brought Coco to the ring as a gift to Calver, because he definitely needs to get himself laid before he explodes and kills himself. Calver did not take very kindly to this, and used his shirt to cover up Coco. During his attempt to cover her up, Simply Cool Jimmy attacked him from behind. Jimmy kept Calver on the canvas with a series of kicks and punches, until Calver rolled out of the ring for a breather, only to find himself flashed by Coco and her lumpy, saggy breasts. I nearly lost my lunch. Calver, on the other hand, looked blinded. Simply Cool went to the outside and whipped Calver into the guardrail, then followed with the obligatory throw into the ring steps. Jimmy climbed back in the ring, and went up high, attempting to splash Calver at ringside. Calver rolled out of the way, causing Jimmy to land hard on the floor at ringside. Calver picked up Jimmy and rolled him into the ring. Once in the ring, Calver, picked up Jimmy and nailed him with a snap suplex for a two count. Again, he nailed him with a snap suplex, then followed it with a northern lights suplex. With Jimmy out of it, Calver locked on a figure four, which he calls Abstinence, according to the program, and Jimmy tapped out. Nice debut for Calver, who looks to be a straight laced, geeky, baby face, in my guestimation. Rating: ** Chandler Tsonda After the Calver/SPJ match, out came Chandler Tsonda, much to the displeasure of the crowd. He came to the ring and told fans that no one in PWC is more sexy or wrestles better than he does. He said come next Saturday night, he and his partner, Jimmy Nguyen, who he claimed was not here because he was looking over a new movie script, would be the PWC Tag Team Champions. Tsonda spritsed his hair with a bit of the spray he used last night, and went on to brag about his victory over Jim Axtell last night, which prompted me and my fellow fans to begin a chant of “Clairol Fag.” It also prompted someone else to take offense, as out from the back came Totakoxe Salvador. Salvador said where he comes from, only bendejos use hair spray, and that Tsonda may have technically “won” last night, the fans know that Axtell was the real victor. Tsonda tried to verbally fire back, but Totakoxe told him to save it for today’s main event, when it would be him and Tsonda going at it, one on one. Match #3 Warpath vs. Trailer Park Trash The pencil thin Trailer Park Trash came to the ring, in a flannel shirt and greasy jeans with a cigarette hanging from his mouth. No one likes this moron. A few moments later, out came Warpath, who looks really impressive in person. TPT came out and slapped Warpath across the chest, which only pissed off Warpath, who nailed him with a huge right hand. Warpath picked up TPT and nailed him with a backbreaker, then dumped him to the ground with a gorilla press slam. Warpath picked up TPT, flung him into the ropes, then nailed him with the Nuclear Strike clothesline. He then grabbed him with a double underhook, and powerbombed him down with the Countdown to Extinction for the win. All in all, a dominating performance for Warpath, even if it wasn’t exciting. I bet this guy will be huge in six months. Rating: * Match #4 Jake Keeton vs. The Great Destroyer of Humanity After a brief intermission, we came back to find Jake Keeton in the ring, sitting on a chair. Keeton explained that his complete domination of Rancid was easier than he expected it and now he had his eyes on a bigger prize: The PWC Television Title. He explained how no one deserved it more than he did since he was the most gifted grappler in PWC, and he hoped that PWC officials recognized his superior talents and gave him what he deserved. Apparently what PWC officials felt he deserved was a match with the 150 lb goth, The Great Destroyer of Humanity. Keeton told him to not even bother coming to the ring, because he was not worthy to be in the same room with a ring general like himself. The Destroyer still made his way to the ring, where he was immediately met a chair to the skull, courtesy of Keeton, which knocked out the Great Destroyer of Humanity, causing Keeton to lose by DQ. Keeton then stomped away on the Goth a bit, and told fans that they could expect more of the same until he starts getting some respect. The fans did not like Keeton at all. One cool note, was when Keeton left, he ripped the Rancid shirt my brother had out of his hands and hocked a looger on it, then stamped it into the floor. I suppose you had be there to appreciate it. Main Event Totakoxe Salvador vs. Chandler Tsonda Boy the fans really heat Tsonda almost as much as they like Salvador. The two men exchanged amateur holds, but Tsonda kept breaking their grapples and rolling out of the ring to delay. Finally he got back into the ring, and Salvador powered him down to the mat with an armdrag takedown, into a shoulder lock submission. Tsonda begged out, and got a free hand onto the ropes. Salvador let Tsonda up, and went to move in again, but Tsonda begged off. Salvador looked to the crowd for support in his desire to whoop up on Tsonda, but got raked to the eyes when he turned back to Tsonda. Blinded, Salvador stumbled around the ring a bit, until Tsonda bounced off the second rope with a spring board bulldog. Tsonda went for the cover, but got two. Tsonda nailed the downed Salvador with a couple of standing elbow drops, then a leg drop from the second rope. Again he got two on the cover. Tsonda laid the boots to Salvador, then picked him up and nailed him with Beautiful Dreamer front Russian Leg Sweep. Tsonda posed for the crowd, who threw things at him, then he went up top for the Model Citizen, his split legged moonsault….only to find no one home, as Salvador rolled out of the way. Both men were down, but got up around the same time. Tsonda went for a clothesline, but instead got caught in a sidewalk slam from Salvador. Salvador, picked up Tsonda, and nailed him the Last Chance, then immediately followed with his Tota Destruction, Camel Clutch finisher. Tsonda looked like he was about to tap, but Jimmy Nguyen flew out of the back, sprung to the ring apron, then off the top rope, landing spread eagle style on the back of Tota’s neck, immediately causing the DQ. Nguyen began laying the boots to Tota, who was completely taken by surprise by the original attack, and was joined shortly thereafter by Tsonda, much to the dismay of the crowd. The two continued to beat down Salvador, with the crowd growing more unruly, until Jim Axtell surged out of the back, with a chair in hand. Axtell hit the ring, causing both Nguyen and Tsonda to stop kicking Salvador and turn their attention to him, just in time to catch a chair shot to the skull each. Both men crumpled in a heap and rolled out of the ring, much to the delight of the crowd. Axtell patrolled the ring, a bit and jawed out at 2GVC, who shouted back at him as they slowly backed the aisle and into the back. The crowd continued to cheer loudly as Axtell helped Salvador to his feet, and the PA announcer announced Salvador the winner….Only to explode into a chorus of confused emotion, when both Tsonda and Nguyen came rolling back out of the back, unconscious. Everyone was confused about what the hell was going on, including Salvador and Axtell, until the massive Penance came out of the back with Jacob Solomon and stood over the unmoving Tsonda and Nguyen, roaring and beating his chest. Axtell pointed his chair out at Penance, and beckoned the beast to come to the ring. At this point, the crowd, though small, was in a frenzy. Penance paid Axtell little mind, holding both his tag team title belts up high, then he and Solomon simply turned and went back into the tunnel, to huge heat from the crowd. Rating: ***1/2 All in all it was a great show. I was disappointed like everyone else that Penance did not come down to the ring to face Axtell, but I realize a tease when I see one. Biggest Pops 1. Axtell when he beckoned Penance to the ring. 2. Salvador when he challenged Tsonda. 3. Special Delivery when they gave out free pizza. 4. Axtell when he saved Tsonda. T-5. Coco’s hideous, flopping, breasts. T-5. Byron Calver’s victory Biggest Heat 1. Penance not coming to the ring to face off with Axtell. 2. Nguyen’s interference. 3. Chandler Tsonda. 4. Jake Keeton 5. Warpath Axtell and Salvador signed autographs after the show for every single fan in attendance, and Byron Calver came back out and signed several autographs as well. I got autographs from all three. I am so happy I braved the tons of snow that continues to fall as I write this, to watch PWC live.
