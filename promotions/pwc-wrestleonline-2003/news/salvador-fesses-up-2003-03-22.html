Salvador Fesses Up

22 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The truth has finally been revealed. When we here at PWC caught up with Totakoxe Salvador in the early hours of this morning, Friday 21st March, the Argentinean told the truth to set the record straight. "Did I train for 12 hours every day for a week? The answer is yes. I like to train and what better reason to do so with my match this weekend with Chandler Tsonda" he said. Only a few days ago, rumours were circulating that Salvador was training frantically to get as fit as possible for this weeks PWC TV. "It wasn't a case of over-training, more the fact that I know it'll be a hard-fought match and want to be at my fittest for it" said Tota at 7am this morning. He also went on to talk about the 'plan' that 2GVC seem to be plotting. "I heard what 2GVC have allegedly said. I WILL be taking any precautions necessary to make sure their plan will not have success!" "Whatever they have up their sleeves, I will be keeping a close eye on them all Saturday night. Their spiteful behaviour has gone far enough and I am going to try to put an end to it. Hopefully I will take the first step this Saturday!" The 'One in Demand' Totakoxe Salvador then walked away, into the training facility to get his last 12 hour workout before the showdown against Chandler Tsonda. Totakoxe Salvador... Chandler Tsonda... THIS SATURDAY ONLY ON PWC TV!
