PMS Seeking Karina Match

6 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
News has reached PWC that Paula Michelle Smith is eager to secure a singles match against Karina Wolfenden in the very near future. Paula, never one to ignore someone that grates her, made her feelings on Karina perfectly clear on Saturday when she labelled Wolfenden a "Spoiled Brat", as well as continually denouncing her as "Weak" and "Feeble" during the K-Wolf's Last Man Standing 1st round match against Big Chuck D'Andrea. Upon leaving the show at Atlantic City's Boardwalk Hall, Jason Maddux reported that Paula was ranting on about teaching the 'stupid-haired woman' a lesson, or words to that effect, and that she planned on meeting with CEO Ron Starr to secure her desired match. "I think it's fair to say that Karina isn't on Paula's Christmas card list." commented Jason, "I wouldn't be surprised if she gets her way either, that girl was pretty determined." PWC officials have confirmed that neither woman has been booked for this coming Saturday, which is expected to be when Paula meets with Starr to request the match... So could we see Karina vs. PMS next Saturday? Not if the K-Wolf has anything to do with it. "No." was her rather abrupt answer when asked if she wanted to settle her issues with Paula in the ring, and before abruptly hanging up when further pressed on the matter. So, the question begs to be asked... What happens if Paula gets her match and Karina doesn't change her mind, which she isn't exactly prone to doing? For answers, be sure to tune into PWC TV this coming Saturday to see how things develop.
