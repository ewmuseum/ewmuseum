CCV on PWC TV Main Event

10 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It was announced Sunday that on PWC TV, coming at you this Saturday night from Mays Landing, NJ, the main event would pit "Classic" Clay Vendetta against Angelo Deville in a "Sleeps With the Fishes" match. The match, a nod to the "Sicilian message" sent from Virgil Sollozzo to the Corleone Family in The Godfather, will surround the rings with aquariums filled with water and electric eels. The wrestler who throws his opponent into the tanks will win the match. On Monday, we caught up with Vendetta, who had thoughts on both the match and his opponent. "It's a joke," Vendetta said, speaking about the match. "If the world needed definitive proof that Ron Starr is nurturing a crack habit, this is it. 'Sleeps With the Fishes'? Electric eels? And we're trying to start a credible promotion here? C'mon." Asked how the match came about, Vendetta was dumbfounded. "I have no answers. Either Deville or Starr knows that Angelo can't take me, one on one, in the ring, so that's why we're getting this gimmicky crap. Either way, this is about as far from 'Classic' wrestling as it gets." When one reporter remarked how the match was an ironic tribute to the vanished Jason O'Neil, Vendetta became angry and withheld official comment. "I'm not going to make any accusations before I have all the facts," Vendetta snarled. "Whatever happened, though, is a travesty, and I WILL find answers." Vendetta had some final remarks for Deville before cutting off the interviewers: "Know what? Gimmicks, no gimmicks, classic wrestling or trips to the aquarium... I'm gonna win no matter what you decide to throw at me. Deville, you'd better get used to the taste of defeat; this is just an appetizer for what I'm going to do to you at Capital Punishment. You know, it's like the late, great Dr. Seuss would've said: Deville can't beat me on the mat, Deville can't beat me with a bat, Deville can't beat me over a tank Deville... you take that to the bank." We'll find out this Saturday night... tune in to PWC TV for the first ever "Sleeps With the Fishes" match and all the other exciting Pro Wrestling Circuit action!!
