Spotlight: Karina Wolfenden

1 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
with Jason Maddux It was another day with another dollar to be made and I, Jason Maddux, was taking my seat and anticipating the arrival of Karina Wolfenden, the woman who I had been told by my superiors at the offices of the Pro Wrestling Circuit to interview. She had chosen a charming café’ in inner Harlem, which was a huge upgrade from the dark and dingy hotel room that I had interviewed Gideon Poe in a few days before, but there was still a question inside of me that until answered would make me nervous. As Ms. Wolfenden stepped through the café’s front door, her reddish hair bouncing with each step, and sat down in the chair across from me, I knew that now was the time to ask. Jason Maddux: “I've heard about your reputation, Ms. Wolfenden. Apparently you're extremely reluctant to speak to reporters of any kind and haven't since you've been signed by the Pro Wrestling Circuit. What made you call this interview?” Karina Wolfenden: “A certain 'higher up' thought I needed to be more personable to the press, hence our little chat here today. Supposedly I'm a 'marketable face'.” I made a mental note at that point not to ask her anything extremely personal, simply because it was obvious that even sitting before me with the idea of answering questions made her irritated. I figured, as she placed an order for a cup of coffee, that I would begin the interview with an ice breaker. Maybe then she wouldn’t view me as the enemy. Maddux: “How are you feeling lately Ms. Wolfenden?” Wolfenden: “I can't honestly say things have been peachy, but I'm here and doing what I want to do. That's good enough for me.” Unfortunately, the curiosity that ran through my mind overtook me at that moment and I blurted out the next question without even thinking. The instinct within me, the same instinct that tells all reporters to pry and not stop until answers were given, was too powerful to resist. Maddux: “Let's get to the point, shall we? The world wants to know, are Magnum Wolf and Karina Wolfenden an item?” Wolfenden: “The day even a single part of me even 'likes' that man, will be the day I take up cordless bungee jumping.” Apparently that wasn’t the only instinct running through me. Maddux: “Really now? So you’re not seeing anyone then? Maybe I could give you my number…” Wolfenden: “You could, and then I could pass it on to my trashcan.” Maddux: “You can’t blame a man for trying…” Judging from the look on her face she could. Maddux: “Ahem. Anyway, back to the interview. How does it feel to be one of the only women in the Pro Wrestling Circuit and a very successful one at that?” Wolfenden: “Well, I’m still new to this thing. But, it’s fun to do and it always helps when you enjoy your work. As for being a woman in the business? You learn to ignore certain people’s opinions and just do your own thing; it’s all you can do.” Maddux: “Do you feel that you’re representing the women across the globe every time you pick up a microphone or step into a wrestling ring?” Wolfenden: “I represent Karina Wolfenden out there. I could, and wouldn’t, want to represent anybody else.” Maddux: “Well what kind of job do you feel you do when you’re representing Karina Wolfenden, as you so eloquently put it, especially when you’re doing it against members of the opposite sex?” Wolfenden: “An opponent is an opponent. It could be Magnum or it could be Paula. I just go out there, do my thing, catch some good air, and try to have fun.” Maddux: “Considering all that you’ve been through, that’s a pretty positive attitude to have, I suppose. But while we’re on the subject of Paula Michelle Smith, or P.M.S, would you mind telling me what you think about her?” Wolfenden: “She hasn’t exactly had the best of starts to her life, but I don’t think it can excuse some of her actions. If anything, I’m walking proof that how you’re brought up shouldn’t affect who you grow to become. I have my hands full with a certain private eye though, so as long as she steers clear of me, she has no reason to worry.” Maddux: “So you don’t consider her a threat to you? What about to the PWC promotion as a whole?” Wolfenden: “Anyone can be a threat to anyone if they so desire. Only she’ll know if she’s any threat to me.” Maddux: “Where do you see Karina Wolfenden in the future?” Wolfenden: “Still doing what I love, wherever that may be.” Maddux: “You seem like a very laid back individual Karina, which is why I’m asking you this next question. Who, if anyone, is on Karina Wolfenden’s hit list? Wolfenden: “I wouldn't say I'm exactly the type to have a "Hit List", but if someone tries to mess with those I hold dear, they can expect to answer to me. Magnum Wolf falls into that category. Do what you want to me, I'm tougher than I look, but try to get to me through my family, and you'll see what this girl can do.” Maddux: “In my last interview with Gideon Poe, I touched on some rumors regarding the size of one of his body parts and asked him about it. Now I’d like to ask you a question of the same nature. What does Karina Wolfenden prefer to do … spit or swallow?” Wolfenden: “You’ll be spitting two of your private parts out of your mouth in about five seconds if you don’t change the subject.” I gulped, the idea of my Johnson coming out of my mouth freaking me out completely and utterly. Maddux: “Understood, Ms. Wolfenden.” I spoke attentively before coughing. Maddux: “Someone needs a Mydol…” She raised her eyebrows, signifying the fact that she heard me. Wolfenden: “Someone will need a band-aid unless they move on quickly.” It was déjà vu as the freaked out feelings returned and I once again gulped. I never knew a woman could do this to me. Maddux: “I guess we should just wrap this up then. Thank you for your time.” Wolfenden: “You’re welcome, for the most part.” Maddux: “Maybe we can get together and do this again? Say over dinner?” Wolfenden: “Sure! We can have dinner!” She exclaimed lightly, smiling flirtatiously as my eyes lit up. Wolfenden: “Just won’t be with each other.” Damn, tricked again by the opposite sex. Maddux: “I read you and I’m now starting to think that I should get out while I can?” Wolfenden: “You’re about two questions too late for that as it is, but you may as well play catch-up now.” Maddux: “Ok. Once again, thank you for your time.” Someone want to remind me why I took this job again?
