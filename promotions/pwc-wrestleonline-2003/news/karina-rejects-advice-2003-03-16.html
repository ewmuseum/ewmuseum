Karina Rejects Advice

16 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After suffering a knee injury in her PWC TV match against Magnum Wolf, that caused the referee to stop the contest, Karina Wolfenden has been actively avoiding any questions as to the severity of the injury. From the footage shown as she collided with the guard rail, it appeared that she suffered a twisting of her right knee, a diagnosis supported by the visible swelling post-match. So far Miss Wolfenden has not approached any PWC staff or officials about taking this Saturday off, so she is still expected to be on TV despite the medic’s advice to take time off. Karina has been presented with a Doctor’s note that would give her 4 weeks off, but this has not yet been presented to anyone at the Pro Wrestling Circuit. “She seemed adamant about not taking time off,” stated the medic who gave her First Aid following the incident, “Her knee is going to be a soft spot and prone to swelling for the next few weeks... So it’s going to take more than just a bandage to get her back in wrestling shape.” With all that’s going on in Karina’s life at the moment, an injury that could put her on the shelf would be the last thing she needs. Especially given that her current contract is appearance based, and the Boarding Beauty will not be earning the full amount if she has to miss a show. Ironically, the contact Karina rejected from Ron Starr on the grounds that it was “degrading” would have given her both a higher pay-out per show, and 100% of her usual fee if she had to miss a show through injury. If Karina is unable to continue in the ring for the next few weeks, could we see her swallow her pride and accept the deal Starr put on the table in order to pay her way? PWC officials are hoping to hear from Miss Wolfenden in the coming days to clarify the situation.
