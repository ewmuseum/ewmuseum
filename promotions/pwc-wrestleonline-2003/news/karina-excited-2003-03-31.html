Karina Excited

31 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Despite her less than cheery disposition over the past few weeks, PWC’s Karina Wolfenden was uncharacteristically eager coming off of PWC TV’s Harlem show on Saturday. With a return match against Magnum Wolf at Capital Punishment, as well as facing Angelo Deville this coming Saturday, the ‘Boarding Beauty’ is finally seeing things fall into place wrestling-wise. “Getting for face Deville is a pretty great honour,” she said excitedly when told about the match. “I didn’t see it coming, but to be in there with one of the best, doing what I want to do, is the most I can ask for.” After giving up so much to be in PWC, a match against Deville seems to be a good reward for her determination. Having only been in the business for 10 weeks, to face off against the ex-Universal Champion will be a huge jump for Karina, and one she’s eager to take. “Here is where I want to be, and I’ve said I’d wrestle injured, healthy, whatever.” she continued, “I’m glad to see what I put in is starting to pay off, makes everything I’ve done seem all the more worth it.” After weeks of gloom and troubles for Wolfenden, things seem to be coming good for her. But can she pull out the upset of the year so far in PWC? Be sure to watch on Saturday to see first hand.
