PWC Tops on WGTW TV-48

4 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Pro Wrestling Circuit Television is the highest rated program on its only current television affiliate, WGTW TV-48. According to regional Nielson ratings, PWC TV is averaging viewings in over 40,000 local households each and every Saturday night. "I'd be lying if I said we were not pleased with the numbers, PWC TV has brought in," said WGTW Spokesperson Harrison Gerschwitz. "Revenues are up, and local advertisers which target a young demographic have been calling to pick up advertising time during the telecast." While Gerschwitz confirmed that the station is seeing "spillover" ratings which have increased the viewership of Cheaters, which airs just after PWC TV goes off the air, he stopped short of committing the station to a second PWC television program. "Somewhere down the road, we can consider the magazine-esque show that has been pitched to us by PWC, but for the time being, we would like to monitor the ratings on Saturday night, then take it from there," said Gerschwitz. PWC spokesperson, Mark Malloy, stated the report came as little surprise to PWC officials. "We knew we would come out of the chute strong," said Malloy. "There was really never any doubt."
