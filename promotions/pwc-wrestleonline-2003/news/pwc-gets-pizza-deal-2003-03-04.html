PWC Gets PIzza Deal

4 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Cousin Mario's two most popular delivery boys will be getting some extra TV time. Cousin Mario's Pizzeria the place both Chuck D'Andrea and Sammy Bello otherwise known as Special Delivery are employed and represent has decided to offer the boys’ time in their new Cousin Mario's commercial. Even more news with this commercial is that the boys will be advertising Cousin Mario's new pizza style: The Starr Treatment Pizza. This pizza named after PWC's CEO is twice the size of a large pizza and comes with whatever you want on it. The idea came from Mario himself, owner of the numerous pizzerias in honor of the massive endorsements that Sammy and Chuck have gotten the pizza place thanks to PWC TV. When reached for comment Cousin Mario eagerly put, "I am excited to be taking my business up to new leaves, and with PWC and Cousin Mario side by side The Starr Treatment Pizza Monstrosity will become the number one junk food for not just all of PWC but for all of PRIMETIME TELEVION." Cousin Mario was then carried away by numerous employees as he continued to cry about money. The stars of the new commercial Chuck and Sammy were unable to be reached for comment and neither was PWC CEO RON Starr...more news on this yummy breaking store as we receive it.
