Tampax wants PMS

4 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Over the last week, PWC CEO Ron Starr and his lawyer Clark Carlisle have held several meetings with officials from Tampax, the feminine product company. It seems that Tampax would like to hire Paula Michelle Smith, or PMS, as an official spokesperson. Starr is insisting that he be the one to broker a deal between the two since Smith is under PWC contract. "I think it's only fair that if the fine people from Tampax, the maker of the best feminine napkin around by the way, want to use my talent as their spokesperson that they should go through me to do it," said Starr rather smugly. Starr's legal council, Carlisle, chimed in for further details. "There are many considerations when undergoing negotians of this nature. Ms. Smith is property of Pro Wrestling Circuit, and my client as owner has negotiating rights when it comes to endorsement deals such as this." When asked if they were close to brokering a deal, the Tampax representatives had this to say. "Mr. Starr has been gracious with his hospitality. We hope to have a deal reached in the near future. Ms. Smith is a natural spokesperson for many reasons, and not just the obvious. She can identify with a new demographic that we hope to reach, which is the 15-20 year old female wrestling fan. We feel it's an untapped market that we can expose." Paula Michelle Smith could not be reached for comment on these negotiations, in part because she probably knows nothing about them. We will bring you updates on this story as they occur.
