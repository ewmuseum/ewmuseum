Karina Denies Rumour

30 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Following the rumours being spread about her "relationship" with fellow PWC wrestler Paula Michelle Smith, Karina Wolfenden today hit back... And sounded less that pleased in doing say. "Yeah, we're lesbians, sure..." mocked the K-Wolf sarcastically, "..Maybe whoever wants to hide in the shadows whispering rumours should come out and speak to our faces? I've never backed down, and I won't now." Worried that the remarks being bounded around could jeapordise the fragile bond that has formed between Kari and PMS, she was determined to bring an end to them. "...And if I can't get the the person who started it... I'll just have to go for those spreading it." With this flat-out denial, will that be the end of the gossip?
