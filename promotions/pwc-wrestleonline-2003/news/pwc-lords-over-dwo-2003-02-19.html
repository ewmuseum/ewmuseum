PWC Lords Over DWO

19 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This past Sunday night, Pro Wrestling Circuit's resident mobster at large and undisputed Primetime Central Universal Champion, Angelo Deville, made a revolutionary appearance on Dominant Wrestling Online television to face Syko Arsonist for the Universal Title. The special telecast aired only for this contest, at the order of the Bane family, and was attended by every current DWO Champion. Lining the ramp like an honour guard, they watched and cheered in loyal support of Syko, pushing him to unseat the unsavory Deville. Kaiowas, reigning DWO Heavyweight Champion of the World, even involved himself in the match while the referee was distracted, saving his friend from a world of pain by untangling him from the ropes. In his home, with more than 80 000 people jammed into the arena supporting him unconditionally, and with six champions watching his back, it seemed like everything was being stacked to see Syko Arsonist crowned as only the second-ever Universal Champion -- the only man in history other than Deville to lay hands on it and wear it with pride. Emotions were high, and so were expectations that DWO would have the honour of cradling arguably the most prestigious title in our sport today. The original couldn't quite come around to their way of thinking, however, overcoming the odds and foreign landscape to prove the merits behind his championship. Locking in his finishing maneouvre, the Soprano, after a vicious and bloody battle, marked the downfall of the first Universal challenger since Xavier Kannon at Primetime Championship Wrestling's payperview extravaganza, Alliances. As Syko Arsonist, on the verge of passing out from the pain, gave in and tapped out, Angelo Deville's continued reign was ascertained until his next defense, despite the disapproving taunts and screams of those around him. The champ was given no time to celebrate, though, as "The Scarecrow" Jason O'Neil popped out of the crowd and uncharacteristically decimated his long-time rival with a steel chair. He continued his gallavanting up on the ropes, pointing at the fallen Deville and shouting to the capacity crowd, eyes flashing dangerously. O'Neil, who has been struggling with Deville since their days together in the Primetime Wrestling Academy almost a year ago, is currently entangled with the man in their present home. This Saturday night on PWC TV they are slated to battle it out one-on-one in the main event. They will meet alone for the first time since their Five Star Wrestling battle over the FSW Primetime Championship, which Angelo won. We called Jason to find out if this encounter would be any different, but he was unavailable for comment. "He's nothing but a hypocritical coward," the outspoken Deville told us with a wry smirk when we tracked him down at his restaurant, A Little Taste of Hell. "And jealous of my supreme success. I'm surprised he'd go against his moral code," he pauses to snicker loudly, "and smash me with a chair, but I guess it's finally getting through his thick skull that he can't handle me by himself. Although, if he's still harbouring any delusions to the contrary this Saturday night, I'll happily maim and extort them out of existence, along with his fledgling career." Wanting to get off the subject of things mysteriously disappearing, we inquired as to his thoughts on the Universal Title match, and whether he was worried about losing at any time, or if he was surprised that he had won. "What the hell are you talking about, goombah?" He demanded, his voice rich with scorn. "Of course I fuckin' won..." Another pause while he pointed at the Universal Championship Belt adorning the wall, hung like a big-game trophy. "Why do you think I have this title in the first place?"
