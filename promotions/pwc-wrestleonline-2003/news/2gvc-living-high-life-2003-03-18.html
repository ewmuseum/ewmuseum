2GVC Living High Life

18 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Last week, Chandler Tsonda and Jimmy Nguyen, Second Generation Viet Cong, pulled the wool over the eyes of Jim Axtell and the Mays Landing fans fans. They executed a surprise upon Axtell, who was said to be furious when he regained consciousness after Penance's attack. He tried to rush back into the building, but EMT's stopped him, saying he would just risk further injury. However, despite not actually capturing the tag titles, 2GVC spent the night in a much different way. The two (and an entourage of some scantily clad, probably paid, escorts) gallavanted around the town, hopping from bars to strip joints to clubs. During this time, though, it was not all fun and games, as 2GVC reportedly began talking, in drunken stupors, about their plans for this week. "The one with the gel in his hair said that they had something real special planned against Tozadoke or somebody like that," the owner of Duke's Late Night Fun Room was quoted as saying. "They kept talking about how this week was going to be even better." This is more bad news for Totakoxe Salvador, if it happens to be true. Seeing what kind of "surprises" the duo has provided PWC with, the Argentinean must be on full alert, ready for anything at any time. But the question will be what exactly 2GVC has planned for this week's edition of PWC TV...and it can only be answered by tuning in this Saturday night!
