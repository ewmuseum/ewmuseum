Warpath Speaks Out

20 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After a routine physical by PWC doctors, our very own Jason Maddux caught up with the infamous monster of PWC, Warpath. He refused to sit down for a formal interview, but agreed to answer a couple questions. When asked about being matched up against rival Gideon Poe on PWC TV once again, he replied quickly and anxiously, "Yea well, I don't like the guy, but squaring up with him every week works for me, seeing as I have more opportunities to kick his ass." Aside, Maddux whispers quickly, "He always owns you." Warpath hears it and takes a step toward Big Jay, but Maddux stops him short with a more serious question. "In your ongoing feud with Gideon Poe," Maddux begins, cautiously and deliberately, not to offend the beast again, "who do you feel has the upper hand? Which one of you has proved to be superior, week in and week out?" Warpath's eyes open wider, his nostrils flair, and his lip begins to quiver. He looks right into the eyes of Maddux, to the ceiling, and then back at Maddux, who is attempting to stand as strong as possible. He takes two deep breaths, and replies, "You know, Jason, that came to my mind as well, and the answer I came up with angered me greatly. After looking at tapes of all of the previous PWC TVs, I feel that from the very start, Gideon Poe seems to have the advantage. As much as I regret to say it, FOR NOW, Gideon Poe has the upper hand on me." Warpath looks to the ground and awaits further questioning. Maddux asks, "'Path, with the PPV coming up, do you have anything planned, anything special or out of the ordinary for your rival Gideon Poe?" Warpath chuckles and responds, "Ha, it's funny you should ask, there certainly is a little something, and you will find out in good time. That’s all I can say on that." Maddux looks at Warpath inquisitively, and tries to get more. "C'mon, this week, next week, when is this going down?" Annoyed, Warpath answers, "No, really, that’s enough." Desperately this time, Jason Maddux almost begs, "C'mon, 'Path! Just a clue, give me something!" Now angered, Warpath's body flairs as he answers, "NO! What the hell else do you need me to say, what part of NO do you not f*cking understand?! Something’s going down, and it’s going down hard, on Poe. That’s all you will need, now stay the hell away! I got real tired of you, real fast!" Warpath storms away and out of sight. "This Warpath has something up his sleeve, and as on the edge as he is, I fear what he may be up to..." Maddux says to himself.
