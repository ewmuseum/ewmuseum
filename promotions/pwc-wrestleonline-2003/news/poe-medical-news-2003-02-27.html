Poe Medical News

27 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Earlier today it was officially revealed the extent of Gideon Poe's injuries sustained at the hands WarPath last week on PWC TV. Doctor Mick Sanchez, who treated Poe's injuries, had the following to say to PWC reporters. "What you seen happen to Mr. Poe on PWC didn't even come close to justifying how bad and severe these injuries are. The severe trauma his head suffered that night was shockingly alarming to say the least, and we ourselves had to view the footage to establish what exactly caused all of this. Upon his arrival here, Mr. Poe was unconscious and we immediately established he had a concussion. He awoke the following night with no recollection whatsoever of what had happened to him the nigh before or how he arrived in the hospital." Doctor Sanchez went on to say "Eventually however his memory did return, and he was, to nobody's surprise, extremely hostile. The impact suffered to his midway through the night from his head connecting with the steel chair thanks to Jake Keeton rendered him almost barely conscious, and we are still amazed he was able to continue to even walk for the rest of that night. Yet the lead pipe shot to the back of Mr. Poe's head was the final straw, and the young mans head basically said "no more". When asked if Poe was cleared to return to PWC TV, Doctor Sanchez had this to answer. "Right now Mr. Poe isn't fit to even fit to walk round the corridors of this hospital, although all our examinations have shown no sign of any further damage, yet his head has still not yet fully healed. He however has discharged himself from the hospital. Yet as I have already told him myself, if he gets just one full force blow to his head right now before it heals, the consequences will be even more damaging in the long term." PWC CEO Ron Starr had nothing to say on the matter, but has thus not booked Gideon Poe or WarPath for this weeks PWC TV, yet rumours are already flying that Poe is hell-bent on turning up this week, and WarPath apparently already has a plan hatched to dispose of Poe once and for all if he does show up. Are there any truth in these rumours? There's only one way to find out, and that's by tuning into PWC TV this week.
