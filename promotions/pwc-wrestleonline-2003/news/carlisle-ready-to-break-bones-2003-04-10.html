Carlisle Ready to Break Bones

10 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With the PWC’s first ever Pay-Per-View less than 5 days away, final tactics are being put in play and the PWC’s newest stable has not neglected in doing so. Clark Carlisle, who is not scheduled for in-ring action spoke with reference to his own interests for the Pay-Per-View, at a press conference for the Pro Wrestling Circuit today. Below is a brief excerpt. WrestleOnline.com writer: Mr. Carlisle? Are you a little jealous that Jonathan Conspiracy was given a potential shot at the World Title so close to the beginning of his tenure in the PWC? And that you were overlooked, because he‘s black? Clark Carlisle: Why...me? Jealous? Of course not, you intolerant bigots! I’m dealing with a full deck of race cards here, and even if Ms. Newman saw fit to send me on a free ride like she did with her ill-mannered, baby daddy-- my added commitments to WMXS completely congest my schedule. Thus, a match would be impossible to pull off, even with my extraordinary capabilities. In fact, I plan on showing Ms. Newman just how appreciative I am of her booking preferences at Capitol Punishment when I’m ringside watching Mr. Conspiracy put an end to Frankie Bones short-lived rebellion. Though these events have yet to take place, one can only assume that on account of the abundance of tension among every member of this assertive club Capitol Punishment live in Trenton, New Jersey will be an encounter for the ages.
