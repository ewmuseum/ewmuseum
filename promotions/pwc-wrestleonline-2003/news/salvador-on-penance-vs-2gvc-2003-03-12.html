Salvador on Penance vs 2GVC

12 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PWC interviewer, Jason Maddux caught up with Totakoxe Salvador to find out his thoughts towards the scheduled Tag Team Title match this Saturday night. Penance will be defending his titles against the team of 2GVC, who are bitter rivals of the Argentinean and his friend, Jim Axtell. When asked his thoughts about the match, Salvador replied, "Let's make this clear, neither 2GVC nor Penance are great friends of ours. Who the titles belong to has certainly no revelance to me. Penance destroyed Porky Bunson and made an effort to do the same to Axtell. Penance will not live this down, as he will find out soon enough! As for 2GVC, let's just say they'll get their just desserts this Saturday!" "What about Axtell's beef with Penance? Where do you come in there?" Maddux asked. "Jim is understandably upset at both his and his wife's treatment at the hands of Penance. He will do his best to make sure Penance will not get away with this. Where do I come in? I'm Axtell's amigo - I'll be watching his back 24/7 in his struggle with Penance. In the meantime, though, I have my mind set on 2GVC" Tota said. "Penance versus 2GVC! I'm certainly hoping for all three men to get a taste of their own medicine and not forget PWC TV this weekend" he said as he walked away, leaving Maddux to ponder what he meant by this. The truth will be unveiled LIVE, this Saturday night, only on PWC TV!
