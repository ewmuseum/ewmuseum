Deville Claims to be Innocent

11 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“The people who think I killed Jason O’Neil are fools,” Angelo Deville told a small press conference this morning after professing his virtues for some time. The gathering was called in honour of Deville’s charitable donation of a new sports complex to his small hometown of Kemptville. The public is pleased with the generosity, but only those taking the act at face value. Anyone willing to do the smallest bit of digging would have discovered that this “charity” actually pads the pockets of three of Deville’s cousins, provides a close friend of his with the political momentum to win their county, and gives him a vessel to “wash” money through, since he has at least partial ownership. These things were glossed over, however, and Deville lured people away from that line of thinking and questioning by discussing his other career. “I wasn’t even in the same country as O’Neil when he got flipped off the Brooklyn Bridge, spending quality time with my family. How could I have done it? Why would I have done it? As you all know, I am a kind, noble, and venerable humanitarian, with a word and heart of gold, even if I am a little modest most of the time.” He paused to allow for laughter, glaring around at the cowed and frightened faces, and after a moment it came. “I’m hardly a cold and calculating murderer. “These accusations stem from the ludicrous assumption that I’m somehow “made”, or affiliated with the mob. I don’t know how those rumours started, but allow me to reassure everyone that I’m as forthright and forthcoming with my private affairs as the inexorable George W. Bush, and my aspirations are equally pure.” He intoned grandly, a far cry shy of reassuring, then went on in a scandalised and puzzle tone. “Why point the finger at me, while ignoring the man who seems to be capitalizing from his demise? Why accuse a charitable businessman of impropriety when that same scurvy lowlife is trumpeting his affiliation with the darker side of society? How can you possibly ignore the man who is trying to become and replace me, the man who falsifies reports by “experts” on organised crime? “Obviously, I’m talking about Clay Vendetta, or, as he’d rather be known though he tries to hide and deny it, Clayton Battaglia.” One of the reporters started to interrupt and ask questions at this point, but a couple of burly men in grey suits moved quickly to his side, where they loomed over him threateningly. Needless to say, his questions went unanswered, and unasked a second time. “If you’ll pardon my French, Vendetta is a conniving piece of dogshit. He’s the very worst kind of person there is: greedy and delusional to the point of death.” Deville shook his head slowly, as if disbelieving, for a long time before continuing. “Everything he does is to win the PWC World Title, a trophy the likes of which he has never before held... And rightly so. Despite his constant claims to the contrary, he has no intention of doing so with technical ability, because he has already gone that road over and over again, and every time he’s found himself staring at a dead end. It’s his talent that holds him down, not what propels him, which is why he’s never made it anywhere. “But it seems he’s finally realised this, and so now he’s taking alternate routes to get where the real cream of the crop end up by default and necessity. He’s trying to cheat his way to the top by any means he can think of. If people would actually connect the dots from time to time, they might find a design... One so sinister as to make “Classic” Clay Vendetta blush. “Notice how he doesn’t pay Toomes any notice any more? Like everyone else who’s run across the man’s path, Battaglia knows Toomes isn’t a threat. Seabass is just a mouth and an anus, and what goes in and out of one is the same as the other. If he’s still even around at Capitol Punishment I’ll be amazed, and so will Clayton, which is why he despised being paired with him. Which is why he was jealous of the other world title faction. Battling with Toomes was not going to get him anywhere... It would certainly never deliver him to his prize. “The man who would, however, was already occupied. Jason O’Neil was standing directly in my crosshairs – figuratively speaking, of course – and it would be me going over him for the title here, just as it happened before. Vendetta and Toomes were in the match, sure, but the title fight was always Angelo Deville versus Jason O’Neil. Vendetta wanted to change that. Vendetta needed to change that. But how? “Simple. All he had to do was get rid of O’Neil, then thrust himself in my path.” More grey-clad men rush in to keep the peace. “Befriending O’Neil was easy. All he had to do was keep up his moral bullshit and he had O’Neil eating from his palm. They had an affinity based on being “technical greats held down somehow for unknown reasons,” and their relationship blossomed. Time went on, and I continued having fun with O’Neil, scaring him to the point where he thought he would die... All for the sport, of course. And then, while I’m here in glorious Kemptville and O’Neil is in New Jersey, he does. “Ask yourself honestly, if O’Neil thought I was trying to kill him, and that I’d go to any length to do it, would he have followed me behind a building into thick and unescapable shadow? Would he really rush off in search of his death? Would he charge after an enemy, unarmed, beyond the sights and sounds of the public eye? Of course he wouldn’t... “But he’d meet a friend there. “Classic Clay Vendetta killed Jason O’Neil, and now he’s trying to frame me for it and cover it up by “looking for the real killer.” Hell, why not? It worked for OJ. The only problem facing him now is that I’m still alive and standing, and I know exactly what he’s up to. He won’t catch me unaware in the shadows, he has to face me man to man. “This week, as a matter of fact, we are booked in a match wherein Vendetta will mockingly try to do away with me as he so callously did O’Neil. It’s time to find out how much skill this ambiguous asshole really has, and what he thinks he can do with it. “You can’t play this game like I can, Classic. You’re not ready to take it to this level. You want to beat me on my terms? You want to emulate the life you’ve dreamed up for me to live? You want my attention this bad? You’ve got it, kid. This week, just like O’Neil last week, Clay Vendetta sleeps with the fishes.”
