Solomon Scoffs at Match

1 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
While not usually open to the media, PWC officials were surprised when they recieved a press release from none other than the Prophet Jacob Solomon. It contained some harsh words concerning both the opponents and the partners of his protege Penance this coming week on PWC TV, where the masked monster is scheduled to team with Jonathan Conspiracy, Clark Carlisle, and Jimmy Nguyen to face Jim Axtell, Totakoxe Salvador, Rhett Locke, and Frankie Bones. The press release reads as follows: "When PWC officials informed me that Penance would be competing in an eight-man tag this weekend, my first inclination was to think that it would be Penance decimating eight hapless souls, working together in a pathetic attempt to bring him down. When they corrected me, saying that Penance would be on a team with three other men, I could not stop myself from laughing in their faces. Who do we have on the other side of the ring? Rhett Locke? That foul-mouthed little cur…his greatest accomplishments include playing hide and seek on a mountain for an hour until his competition eliminated itself and beating Tom Walczak. Of course it sounds more impressive when you call him the winner of last year’s Ascension match and a former OSW World Champion. When you look at these things for what they really are, however, they seem to lose most, if not all of their luster. Rhett Locke is an angry little man with an attitude problem and a fuck-the-world mentality. If his mental state were not so far gone, I would consider taking him under my wing for guidance, but this fool is beyond helping. Fortunately, it is never to late to purge the wicked… Then we have the supposed “Great Black Superstar” Frankie Bones…a man who died and came back to life, not unlike another false idol of the masses. This Saturday night,t would be in his best interests to watch his step, lest he get sent to the grave once more. Mr. Bones may be able to use his strength to bully around little Jonathan Conspiracy, but what recourse will he have when he comes up against the power of the Dark Atoner? If he thinks he…or any other man, for that matter…can even come close to matching the strength of Penance, he will find out the hard way just how sorely mistaken he is. Who else is there, Totakoxe Salvador? He was thoroughly demolished last week. I’m surprised there’s anything left of him to drag to the ring this coming Saturday. We already know how frail Salvador is, for how can we help but hear him bemoaning his poor injured neck backstage. I sincerely do not know what right he has to whine. He knew full well what he was getting into when he stepped into the ring with Penance. The Dark Atoner has no tolerance for crybabies and neither do I. Speaking of crybabies, that brings me to the last of the men on this team of infidels, our hailed World champion…that THEIF, Jim Axtell. He is the fitting head of this team of misfits, and you can rest assured he will be the prime target of Penance in this match. Our sights will never leave him for a second. Axtell’s lackeys will be swatted aside like the flies they are, and once they are disposed of, there will be nobody left to save him. He’ll be isolated in the ring, by himself, with nowhere to turn, and no way out. This Saturday night, Penance will exact vengeance upon him the likes of which haven’t been seen since Sodom and Gomorrah. And when it is over, and the life is crushed out of that annoying little ant, the people will rejoice, for justice will have been served in favor of righteousness everywhere. So there you have it. Four men, who on their own would be nothing to the might of Penance. Together they might serve as somewhat of a work-out, but even that is doubtful. As far as I’m concerned Jonathan Conspiracy, Clark Carlisle, and that annoying pest Jimmy Nguyen can stay at home this Saturday night. There’s no need for them to show up. Penance is more than capable of taking on their scheduled opposition by himself. I doubt they could do much more than get in his way, anyway. Mr. Nguyen can tell them from first hand experience what happens when you get in the way of Penance. Heh heh heh. So what more is there to say? I have addressed the issue thoroughly. Take this word and preach it to the masses. It is spoken law the second it has left my mouth. Rejoice in the coming destruction this Saturday night. Rejoice and be glad!" Will Penance be able to make good on the claims of his spokesman? While they seem quite preposterous, one hesitates to put anything past the masked monster. And will Penance be able to work as a cohesive team with his partners this Saturday night? The results, as they say, will speak for themselves.
