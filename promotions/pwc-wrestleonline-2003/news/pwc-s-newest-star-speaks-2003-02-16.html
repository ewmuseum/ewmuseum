PWC's Newest Star Speaks

16 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PWC’s latest signing has expressed his delight towards the up-and-coming indy federation. PWC interviewer, Jason Maddux caught up with Totakoxe Salvador and the Argentinean expressed his feelings. “My first impression of PWC wasn’t the best, if I am to be brutally honest” the Argentinean said, “I saw that it did have a lot of potential but watching it at home, I felt it lacked a little something.” However, when asked how he felt after witnessing PWC first-hand by Maddux, Tota could do nothing but praise the fed. “On Saturday, though, my opinions about Pro Wrestling Circuit spun on their head. I was shocked, amazed and delighted to say the least at the show PWC puts on. Its struggle to stay alive fuels the desire in the hearts of the roster to put on a memorable performance each and every time they are on camera. The response I received from the fans was immense and the atmosphere created by the loyal PWC fans cannot be beaten” He went on to say. “Do you think PWC will contend with the huge PCW or OSW?” Maddux quizzed ‘The One in Demand’. “At the moment, I am glad I’m a member of PWC. It’s not all about the sports entertainment factor and it’s certainly not flooded with money-hungry performers, except maybe Angelo Deville. The hearts of everyone playing a role in PWC is in the right place at the minute and I hope this will continue for a long time to come. Maybe PCW and OSW are drawing in huge television ratings but PWC emits a huge amount of entertaining wrestling” replied the energetic wrestler. “As for the television deal that PWC CEO, Ron Starr, is desperately seeking to land, I can only see it broadening the horizons for PWC. I am all for this television deal but I hope it will not result in the growth of backstage politics or money-hungry and power-hungry mobs,” the handsome foreigner said in relation to the WMXS TV-22 television deal. Maddux shot yet another question at Salvador, momentarily catching him off guard. “What do you have in store for Jimmy Nguyen and Chandler Tsonda?” the PWC interrogator asked. Pausing for a few seconds Salvador replied with a simple statement. “Nguyen and Tsonda will find out what’s in store for them this Saturday night” he said. With that, Jason Maddux let Totakoxe Salvador out of his grasp.
