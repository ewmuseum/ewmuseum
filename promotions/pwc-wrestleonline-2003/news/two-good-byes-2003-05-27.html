Two Good Byes?

27 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This Saturday will see an entire PWC TV devoted to the 2nd round of the Last Man Standing tournament. 14 PWC stars remain in the competition after a hard-fought first round that saw the likes of Charlie Loc and Angelo Deville be eliminated. Those through are... Rhett Locke, The Masked Mullet, Jimmy Nguyen, Chandler Tsonda, Karina Wolfenden, Paula Michelle Smith, Clark Carlisle, Jesse Ramey, Magnum Wolf, Gideon Poe, Jonathan Conspiracy, Frankie Bones, Romulus & Totakoze Salvador. ...But the 3rd round of the tournament will feature 8 of those names, so the questions begs to be asked... How? Currently, there is talk of 2 of the 14 receiving byes into the 3rd round via the random draw process... But with such a prize, can those in charge be trusted? All will be revealed on PWC TV this Saturday.
