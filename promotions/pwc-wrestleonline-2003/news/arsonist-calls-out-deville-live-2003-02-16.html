Arsonist Calls Out Deville LIVE!!!

16 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After this week's main event on PWC TV was over, and Angelo Deville and Jason O'Neil made their way to the back, the crowd was shocked, when Syko Arsonist made his way to the ring. Luckily, our cameras were there to capture the whole scene as it unfolded. -------------- Mark: What the hell is this? Bang: Put da mirror down, Mark... You gonna never figure it out. Mark: ...on the ramp! Fool! Striding down the wooden ramp is a large, heavily built man with fiery shoulder-length hair, a couple of strands swaying down in front of his eyes as he moves. A dark jagged line mars his light Hispanic face, running unabated from his left eye down to his lips, disappearing beneath the red goatee. A trenchcoat obscures most of his body, but faded black jeans jut out from the bottom, moving quickly and purposefully toward the ring. Mark: That looks somewhat like Syko Arsonist... But he’s not on my card... Bang: Who? Mark: Syko Arsonist... From DWO. He recently won a mammoth battle royale for a shot at He’s here for Deville! Deville’s Universal Title! Bang: I reckon Deville don’t know yet? Without music and fanfare to announce his presence, the crowd wonders if the man is here to wrestle legitimately or if this is just some clown trying to get on television. They mill aimlessly and mumble among themselves, and those that recognise him enough to cheer are far outnumbered by the ones who simply stare. Glaring around at all of them, the man rolls underneath the bottom rope and flips up to his feet. Mark: But even if that is why he’s here, he can’t just storm into our show! Unless this is another surprise of Starr’s! Bang: I dun like surprises. As the man pulls a microphone from his pocket, the burly form of Jack McGriff appears in the entryway, and though his arms are folded calmly over his chest, it still seems as if they’re on the verge of strangling someone. The man in the ring raises a cautionary finger to him and pulls the microphone up to his lips, smiling crookedly. Man: It would be best if you used that empty space in your skull to do something useful: like step away from the entryway so I can go about my business. The veins in McGriff’s forehead look as if they’re about to explode, but he doesn’t move from his spot. Bang: Dat boy dun gone crazier ‘an a pet coon! Mark: Why’s he just standing there? He must think this is Ron’s doing as well! The man in the ring lowers the microphone down to his side and looks around with a crooked grin on his face. He turns his attention back to the entryway, where McGriff stands like a pitbull ready to pounce, and raises the microphone to his lips. Man: Some of you seem to know who I am, and others don’t. That’s all well with me, I’m not here for that formal bullshit. I’m Syko Arsonist and that’s all you need to know. Bang: Spit it out, we gots a show to run! Mark: Be careful what you wish for... If what I hear is right, that guy’s got more than a few screws loose. Syko: What I am here for is to get what is rightfully mine. There’s a man, if you can call him that, backstage that has something that will belong to me. There’s a man backstage that has spent the last three months piloting only his dick! Scatters of jeers and curious looks come from the crowd as the man in the ring glares at the entryway. Syko: You all know who he is, and you know why I’m here. Angelo Deville, get your Armani-rip off-suit-wearing ass out here, and give me my damn match for the Universal Title! Mark: He must be crazy! He didn’t just call out Angelo Deville!? Bang: Ya dun right, he do got some screws loose! Syko: You spent the last the three months stroking away while that Universal Title just collected dust. For those fools with their heads crammed up their asses, I recently won the Primetime Central No Limits Battle Royale. I threw countless world class competitors out of my ring to earn a chance at the gold. Now I’m the number one contender for that title and I want my shot. Right... NOW!! Bang: World class cum-pet-a-tors? Somebody better dun tell ‘im this here be da PWC! There is a brief moment of turbulence as the crowd mills like a frenzied anthill, craning to peer over one another at the entryway. Syko Arsonist waits impatiently in the ring, arms folded in imitation of McGriff. There is a loud crackling, then Sympathy for the Devil is pounding off the rafters, inciting the marking fans into an uproar. Mark: What the?! Some people are actually cheering! Bang: They dun hate Deville sumpin’ fierce, but they dun hate invaders more! Angelo Deville saunters out onto the stage in a dashing Armani suit, perfectly tailored in white silk with hair-thin grey pinstripes tracing its length. His blonde hair is slicked straight back, perfect to a fault, and the smirk twisting his lips is mirrored in his pale, crystal-clear blue eyes. The Universal Title rests prominently on his left shoulder, a trophy, like the skin of a ferocious animal adorning the hunter who conquered it. Bang: Deville dun look like he could buy Syko a hun’nerd times over! My sister, too... When I be done wit’ her. Mark shudders. Deville strolls over to McGriff and nods to the man while gently stuffing a small fold of bills into his breast pocket, then turns his attention on the ring, and the man seething inside it. He shakes his head slowly, in pitying disbelief, then swaggers down to ringside with a regal air. Syko watches through narrowed eyes as Angelo ascends the ringstairs, steps through the ropes, and moves to share the centre of the ring. Mark: There’s no way these two egos can share the ring at the same time! They’re gonna explode! Bang: Like ma after eatin’ chili? Arsonist is the taller of the two, roughly two inches Deville’s senior, yet their builds are remarkably similar. Laughing icy blue meets bubbling cauldron green in a delicate dance of wills, which is only broken when Angelo snatches the microphone from Syko’s hand and pulls it up to his lips. The crowd quiets as he turns from the man with a sneer, then flourishes his free arm towards him and addresses the masses. Angelo: Let’s hear it for Syko Arsonist! Mark: What? Deville pauses to give Arsonist an uproarious golf clap, slapping the microphone into his wrist a whole three times, a drunken expression on his face. Syko’s mouth tightens, but he makes no move. Mark: Ah, I see... Now it makes sense. Angelo: The man who managed to outlast such all-time greats as Donny Diamond, Julius Corbin, and KrazE in a cluster(bleep) battle royale! Bang: KrazE?! Dat boy be from my hometown! We dun exiled him when we found him examinin’ the chickens wit’ more ‘an his finger! Dat sorta thang be alright for sheep an’ rooster, but chickens be fer eatin’! Mark: Can’t... Dignify... With... Response...! Angelo: The man who would have won the Golden Turnbuckle Tournament and perhaps stand before you today as Universal Champion, if only guys like Inphino Blitz and I hadn’t bothered to enter! Mark: Syko’s not liking this one bit... Angelo: The man who would be your next Universal Champion! Deville finally turns back to the man he’s been toasting, a wolfish grin spread across his face. Angelo: If only I wasn’t smarter, stronger, faster, more cunning, and ten times as skilled as him! Mark: Here it comes! Syko lunges forward suddenly, snatching at the microphone in a rage, but Deville whips it behind him and raises a restraining hand. Syko stops, glowering, and Deville brings the microphone back to his mouth. Angelo: Ladies and gentlemen... Once again... He flourishes grandly once more, this even more exaggerated than the first. Angelo: ...Syko Arsonist! Another thumping golf clap against the mic, then Deville sneers and holds it out to him. Syko grabs at it again, but Angelo lets it fall to the ground and turns around, extending his arms out to the side as if to absorb more of the massive mixed reception. Mark: Deville’s nuts! Turning his back on the man after saying all that! Heck, even I’d attack him! Bang: I reckon Angelo wouldn’t take the time ta insult you. Syko Arsonist seizes the microphone up from the mat and brings it to lips trembling with fury. Syko: Think you’re a funny man, do you, Angelo? Well I can point out a joke as well as you can... Your reign as Universal Champion, for instance. Deville starts and turns around slowly, one eyebrow cocked threateningly, as if daring the man to continue. Syko’s smile returns. Bang: He dun talking shit?! Who he reckon he be – Toomes? Syko: You’re afraid to put your crown on the line, because you know you don’t have what it takes to defend it. You’re afraid. Sure, you won the GTT, but it killed you. Sure, you’re Universal Champion, but what does it mean hanging impotently around your waist? Mark: Angelo’s story is that administration has bogged down the championship... Bang: Whatchu mean, “Angelo’s story”? Dat man be pure an’ noble as newfallen snow! Syko: How many times have you defended your title, champ? A whopping zero? Impressive. Arsonist offers the mic with a snicker, but Deville walks past him to the far side of the ring and orders one of the stagehands to throw up an extra. Catching it deftly as he turns back, Deville speaks into the microphone with the same mocking patience. Angelo: Ever hear of Xavier Kannon? Former PCW CEO and World Champion? I’m not surprised you haven’t heard of him, though, wallowing under Callisto’s boot in the DWO. All the same, he’s accounted the best in the world by many. I gave him a shot, in his home, and pinned him as easily as your drunken sister. Mark: That’s not exactly true... Deville was in cahoots with Tyler Nelson at the time, and you’d be right to wager The Greediest Player in the Game secured his bet in that match. Bang: Yeah, but he dun won, right? Syko: You and how many of your friends? Angelo: Jealous, are you? But hold on... I’m glad you said that. Mark: (suspiciously) Why? Angelo walks over to Syko and puts an understanding hand on his shoulder, which is promptly knocked off with a growl from the challenger. Angelo: At the root of it is the reason why I will not defend my Universal Title to you tonight, live on PWC TV! The audience boos themselves hoarse at this announcement, and Deville rides it out with extreme patience. It is Syko that finally waves them down. Mark: Well, that’s a huge letdown for everyone! Especially Syko! Let’s see how he takes this... How bad he wants it. Syko: Because you’re scared! Angelo: No, it’s because I could hear the excuse being made even before I saw your snivelling face here tonight... Even before I watched video after video of you jobbing away your career, garnering only the respect you earned – which is to say none. I can even hear it now, after you lose, defaming our good name at the Pro Wrestling Circuit. “He cheated... PWC stacked the odds... The refs were biased... Their ring is an inch bigger... I broke a nail...” And so forth and so on, forcing me to come kick your ass again, in front of your home crowd. Syko: Face the facts, Deville... You’re scared. You’ve already peaked in this business, and now it’s my time to shine. Go dredge in the slums with the other has-beens. Stop putting off the inevitable. If you don’t agree to give me my shot right now, maybe I’ll just take it by force... Bang: WHAT?! Arsonist takes a menacing step forward, and Deville laughs in his face. Angelo: You see that large and incredibly handsome individual standing up there? Deville turns and points at McGriff, who takes his cue and begins flaunting his muscles. Syko looks at him with the same blatant disregard he shows Deville. Angelo: If you even lay one filthy finger on me here tonight, he and I will beat you within an inch of your life... And then we’ll get serious. Mark: Oh my god! We could see Jack McGriff in action here tonight! Bang: Mark. Mark: Yes? Bang: I didn’t say yer name, I dun called ya one. Syko continues sneering as he covers the short distance separating him from Angelo, coming so close that their chests touch. His eyes pierce into Deville’s, searching, and he simply stares back impassively. Syko: I’m taking that title tonight... Arsonist’s hand floats up to the title and pats it affectionately, earning him an indignant look from both Deville and McGriff. Syko: No matter what you and your big gorilla have to say about it... Mark: He’s not giving up! He wants that title! Bang: I dun think he forgot he has to beat Angelo a’fore he takes it. Angelo shakes his head slowly and sighs, as if trying to explain something to a child who just doesn’t want to understand. Angelo: Callisto wouldn’t like that too much, I’m guessing. Arsonist’s eyes go wide with shock at the name, and Deville gives a small, satisfied smile. Mark: Callisto Bane is the owner of the DWO! Syko’s fed! Angelo: I said I didn’t want to have to come chase you down and whoop you again in your home fed, and I meant it. Which is why we’re going to forego the first round... Mark: Waitasecond... Bang: Still think he be afeared? Angelo: Which is why I spoke with Callisto personally and arranged to have the match on your turf. Among your friends. With your referees. The fans boo once again at being deprived of the match, but Mark is practically having an orgasm at the acceptance of the challenge. Arsonist simply throws back his head and laughs. Syko: You’re going to come to the DWO to lose? Ha! Suits me fine! When – next week on Travesty? Mark: That’s DWO’s weekly show, for any who are interested. Bang: How in da hell do ya know all this, Mark? Ya sit up late at night readin’ ‘bout all dese places, don’t ya? Ya ever been outside? Mark: At least I can read... Angelo: I never go anywhere to lose, “Psycho”. I’m storming the DWO to kick your belligerent ass and teach you some respect... Tomorrow night on a special show, just for us. Mark: WHAT?! Deville’s defending his Universal Title tomorrow night! On DWO television! Something tells me I should be offended, but... Deville offers a grin that threatens to swallow the universe whole, and Arsonist steps back from him, shaking his head and laughing in disbelief. He rubs his chin thoughtfully for a moment, marvelling at the prospect, then points a mocking finger at Angelo’s chest. Syko: Tomorrow night then, Deville... Tomorrow night your title becomes mine... Tomorrow night you discover why I am The One and Mother(bleep)ing Only Syko Arsonist! Deville gapes at him for a moment, eyebrows drawn down in an incredulous furrow. Angelo: You (bleep) your mother? Arsonist’s fist strikes out like a flash of lightning, erupting on Deville’s face with a thunderous crack. Mark: HOLY SHIT! DEVILLE’S DOWN! DEVILLE’S DOWN! Bang: SUCKER PUNCH! SUCKER PUNCH! Angelo never saw it coming, and recognition still eluded his eyes as he crumpled to the mat in a heap. Syko looms over him now, eyes burning with the desire to do more, but Jack McGriff is already halfway to the ring. Mark: Syko better get out of here! Even if McGriff can’t beat him alone, Deville won’t be down long! Bang: Who’s side you be on, Mark?! Dat boy deserves ta be lynched, darky or no! Syko slowly retreats a couple of steps, giving ground grudgingly, but then he turns and dashes from the ring, easily hurdling the guardrail and disappearing into the screaming crowd. Deville climbs up to his hands and knees, watching after him with deadly intent as McGriff slides into the ring with surprising grace and takes up a guard position. Mark: SYKO ARSONIST JUST COLD-COCKED ANGELO DEVILLE! Bang: An’ run away like a scalded dog! Mark: AND TOMORROW HE HAS TO STEP BACK INTO THE RING WITH THE UNIVERSAL CHAMPION, FOR A CHANCE TO TAKE HIS TITLE! LIVE, ON DWO TELEVISION!
