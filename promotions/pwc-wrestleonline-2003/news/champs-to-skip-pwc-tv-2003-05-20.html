Champs To Skip PWC TV

20 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After last week's humiliating beatdown that commenced with the debut of former RUSH Syndicate Champion El Spiffy!, 2GVC was said to be licking their wounds, milking "injuries" sustained in the romp by Spiffy! and The Masked Mullet. Sources close to the three champions of the Alliance division have even speculated that the champs, barring Charlie Loc, whose LMS match will occur this match, although against a random opponent, will totally no-show PWC TV. 2GVC has not been known for tremendous courage while holding the titles but they have never totally sidestepped a challenge. However, the Mullet, while taking his fair share of beatings from Tsonda, has recently become just as much a thorn in the side of Chandler. With Mullet now having another man to be an annoyance towards Tsonda and the rest of 2GVC, will Chandler and Jimmy risk another embarrassment and take a personal day on Saturday? Of course, everything is just talk as of now, but more will be revealed as we near PWC's hit Saturday-night show.
