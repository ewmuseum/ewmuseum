Magnum Wolf Sounds Off

9 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
We caught up with an irate Magnum Wolf shortly after the main event of PWC TV6 came to an end. I attempted to interview him, and produced an obscenity laced tirade. "You want to know what I think of that match?" screamed Wolf. "What match? That wasn't a match, it was a god damned spectacle. A five man ladder match? Ron promised me that it would go off smoothly, and not to worry about a thing. That's what I get for trusting that sombitch, I suppose. You know, this isn't FSW. I won't stand for being nothing more than a tool for some chump coming in from one of the "big feds". Fuck PIW. There's a reason it's closed. Fuck that four foot nothing Leprachaun. Fuck this whole night. I would say fuck Jake Keeton as an announcer... but at least as long as he sits there, we don't have to listen to his boring ass stand in the ring and yell 'I R WRESTLER! I R GOING TO BE CHAMPION!' Get out of my face." Obviously, Magnum Wolf is not pleased with the way that The Leprachaun effected the main event. Even going as far as insinuating that Ron Starr set this stunt up. How will this outburst effect his job? Will he be focused for his match with Karina Wolfenden next week? Tune in to PWC TV to find out!
