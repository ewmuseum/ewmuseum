Karina Gets Her Match

10 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PWC CEO Ron Starr wasted little time responding to Karina Wolfenden's request for a match, by booking the boarding beauty in a one on one matchup against fellow female worker, Sam Alotti. "Fans like to see hot girls pull each other's hair and slap each other around," said Starr. "It's just ashame that Pomona is not zoned to allow mud wrestling, because that would make a good thing that much better." Wolfenden was quick to respond to the announcement. "I am pleased that I will finally be given the opportunity to showcase my talents," said Wolfenden. "I am sure Sam and I can put on a show to prove that women do belong in the ring." We will bring you more on this story as it develops.
