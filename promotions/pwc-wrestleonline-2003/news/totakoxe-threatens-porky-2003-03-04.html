Totakoxe Threatens Porky?

4 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Rumours are flying amok that Totakoxe Salvador has sent Porky Bunson a threatening letter, informing him that he will remain in hospital for the rest of his life so that Salvador himself can continue to shine in the PWC. Of course the sources of this rumour may be wrong and we believe that to be the case. However, nothing can be ruled out at this stage. Apparently, Porky Bunson recieved a letter on the 4th March which was unjustifiably harsh and cruel towards the paralyzed PWC wrestler. These sources did not quote anything from the letter but strongly suggested that it was informing Bunson that he will never appear in PWC if this anonymous person had anything to do with it. It has been suggested that 2GVC were behind this atrocious and foul letter but a stronger case has been pointed towards Totakoxe Salvador, who has seemingly made a name for himself in the absence of Bunson. When asked about the situation, Salvador reportedly said, "Of course I had nothing to do with that letter. Porky and I were both in PWA together and I couldn't say a nicer word about him. To wish such cruelty onto someone is appaling behaviour and I tell you right now, I did NOT send it!" He is also reportedly extremely upset at the accusations. Bunson remains paralyzed from the waist down and fears for his future in wrestling but more importantly, his future outside of his hospital bed. Bunson has denied that Salvador would be capable of such foul play and told the sources that Salvador would pay regular friendly visits to him in the hopsital. Jim Axtell was not on hand to comment. The question remains, though - who sent the letter? Was it 2GVC up to no good yet again? Or was it indeed Salvador, hoping that Bunson wouldn't threaten his future in PWC? Or could it have been someone else from within PWC, hoping that 'Hard Luck' Bunson would receive even more bad luck? The answers are not yet clear. However, New Jersey police are trying to track down the letter and of course, we will bring more news once we receive it.
