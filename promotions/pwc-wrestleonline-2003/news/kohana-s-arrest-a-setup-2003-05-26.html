Kohana's Arrest A Setup?

26 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Last night during PWC TV Steven Lee and Kohana were seen at a bar playing pool. Rather early on in the evening, a short, fat, middle-aged man caused a big scuffle with Lee during one of the wrestlers' games. The fight didn't last very long for a barroom brawl, but the situation was serious enough for the bartender to call the cops. Luckily for the instigator, Kohana jumped on Lee and prevented him from rendering the guy comatose. Unluckily for Kohana, it was just as the cops arrived on the scene. The polce mistook Kohana for the troublemaker and arrested him immediately, ignoring the fleeing Lee. It has been rumored that Lee and the middle-aged man had an agreement prior to the event, and that Lee was seen outside the bar sometime later paying the man off. Jason Maddux questioned Lee about these allegations this morning, and Lee had this to say, "Me? Set Kohana up like that? I saved the man's job last week, and you think that I would set this up, causing him to go to jail? You are too underpaid, and too over dressed to be standing here asking me these kind of dumb ass questions! Good day!" Although some of the story may be true, and some of it may be false, another eyewitness claims to have seen Lee get out of his Trans Am in front of a local jail early this morning. No PWC officials have spoken about these events, and it is still uncertain if Kohana has made bail, or has been released from jail at this time. More on this to come once the information is available.
