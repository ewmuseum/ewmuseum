Who Is Karina Wolfenden?

3 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello, PWC fans. I'm Mark Malloy, and as spokesperson for the Pro Wrestling Circuit, I'm pleased to introduce you all to a new feature our promotion has launched, entitled "Who Is?". Over the coming months I'll be profiling our unique roster for you, letting you get to know them and what they've accomplished throughout their careers. So, for those who tune in at 10pm religiously every Saturday night, or those who have just discovered the Circuit and are wondering just who are these wrestlers and how did they get here, we're kicking it off with one of your favourites. That's right, in this inaugural edition of "Who Is?", I'll be detailing none other than PWC's Boarding Beauty... The K-Wolf... Karina Wolfenden. I hope you enjoy. -Mark Malloy . . . . . Of all the employee who turned up at PWC's 1st show in Glassboro, New Jersey, Karina Wolfenden was one of the most intriguing. There were the Toomes, the Devilles and the Vendettas who were known throughout the wrestling world as some of the best from this 'Generation'. Then there were the likes of Romulus and Jake Keeton, returning to spotlight after years away... There was even Hoyt Williams, son of the legendary Malice Man... ...And then there was Karina. She was the unknown, the girl who amongst a locker room full of guys wanting to make a name or win a Title for themselves, was just there to have fun. Surrounded by a group who were wrestling to pay bills, support families or because it was all they could do, Karina was the odd one out in more than one sense. Karina wasn't a classically trained wrestler, she hadn't any background both professionally or amateur, she wasn't out there to make a wage or become a star... No, the K-Wolf as she would become known, just did it because she liked it. Due to the life that she came from, evident right away from her arrival on her red Ducati superbike, her presence was bound to rub some the wrong way... The worst case of which being Magnum Wolf. They say first impressions count, and when Karina met Magnum for the first time, it was clear that they were never going to get along. Frustrated by her casual approach to the business, and her seemingly uncompromising attitude, Wolf set himself the mission of driving Karina out of the Pro Wrestling Circuit. The weeks that were to follow, when Wolf put his plan into action, would change Karina's life more than even Wolf could have imagined. But while Magnum set about creating his trap, Karina finally got to show what she could do in the ring... And the results 'sealed the deal' with the PWC fans, they were now well on the side of the K-Wolf. With little time spent learning traditional holds and techniques, Karina's wrestling style reflects her character perfectly... Energetic, unpredictable and straight to the point. Many times a commentator will mention that a good wrestler always knows where the ropes are, usually so he can use them to force a hold to be broken... In Karina's case she uses the ropes to her advantage in a much more offensive manner. She sees the ring how a skater would view a skatepark, full of angles and levels, with plentiful places to get good air. With cat-like agility Wolfenden can fly off any rope or turnbuckle in the blink of an eye, snapping her powerful legs out with well targeted kicks. She was a lot more lethal than her looks would have suggested. Flying around a ring is one thing, but that would not be her greatest advantage inside the squared circle. While her 'never say die' attitude and impressive stamina would be to her advantage, it was Karina's background in other sports that completed the wrestling package. Skateboarding, Snowboarding, Skydiving, Motorbikes... Everything Karina found fun was dangerous. Her 153 pound frame may look slender and feminine, but it's a body capable of taking a hard knock or two... And when you're being slammed down onto a PWC mat, knowing how to take a fall is a handy bit of knowledge. However, despite the fans she had gained from her matches, Karina had failed to impress Magnum Wolf who pressed on further with his campaign to rid PWC of the K-Wolf. After paying off fans backstage to make PWC appear dangerous and violent, Magnum was on hand to record everything... And then send a drastically edited version to Karina's father, Lawrence. The paternal instincts of her already over protective father then kicked in, leading to a tense and emotional confrontation in front of the PWC cameras... And Magnum found he had achieved something even greater than ridding PWC of Karina... ...Her father had rid himself of her. Disowned after the headstrong Karina refused to give in to her father's demands, Karina had little left except PWC. Stripped of her substantial funds and forced to sell her beloved Ducati, she'd gone from wrestling for fun to wrestling for money, but refused Ron Starr's offer of a more lucrative contract due to what she would have to do in return. With PWC now her sole focus, she threw herself into it with gusto... Getting her match with Magnum Wolf. Once in the ring with the man who'd plunged her life into despair, the K-Wolf gave everyone a glimpse of just what she could do. The potency of her attacks impressed many, each kick seeming more vicious and every aerial move done with both greater impact and grace... But it didn't end well for her. Just as it looked like she was rallying and preparing to put Wolf away, Karina's airborne style displayed it's downside when she crashed into a guardrail after leaping from the opposite set... ...Her right knee was damaged and the match had to be stopped. That match marked the beginning of Karina Wolfenden's lowest ebb in the Pro Wrestling Circuit. Physically unable to get revenge on Magnum, she wasn't even emotionally up to it either. Always looking close to breaking point, and on painkillers for her injuries, Karina could of easily been sucked into a downward spiral... But the desire to make Wolf answer for what he had done kept her going. But it was going to be far from easy. When she was cleared to wrestle once again, Karina was then thrown one of PWC’s best... Angelo Deville. Could it be that her hard work and sacrifices were paying off? Although Wolfenden was never in it for the fame or he recognition, being able to face a man as skilled as Angelo was a proud moment for her. It did not come about by chance, however. As with most of the bad events in Karina's PWC career, Magnum Wolf was behind this one too. Cashing in on a favour he earned from Ron Starr, Wolf chose not to take a personal reward, and instead asked for Karina v Angelo to be made. Then Magnum Wolf sat back and watched Karina think she'd earned the match... Letting her enjoy the moment leading up to the match so he could get even more pleasure from bursting her bubble. She didn't "earn" the match, she got it so Wolf could hit her where it hurts most - her pride. The match went ahead, and Deville chose to be less than classy by mocking the challenge of the K-Wolf, giving her 5 minutes to try and beat him before a finger would be raised against her... But true to form, just when Karina was about to hit the famous WolfTamer, Deville went back on his word. One fall from the top turnbuckle down to the floor later, and the right knee of Karina was once again injured... An injury that wasn't helped by the following Figure 4 Leglock that Angelo applied, just to prove his point. In one night she'd gone from earning a shot at Deville, to being humiliated, beaten, injured and mocked. Not many would of blamed Karina for walking away from the Pro Wrestling Circuit and back to her family, but by now people were starting to see that Karina Wolfenden was far from how they pictured her in the beginning. As long as Magnum Wolf was still strutting around, Karina wouldn't be leaving... Because despite his attempts to weasel out of it, she had him at PWC's first ever PPV, Capital Punishment. But, true to form, Magnum had yet another trick up his sleeve... One that would eventually backfire on him. While Magnum couldn't draw a reaction from the injured Karina backstage, he managed to get one from her brother Max... One punch was all Magnum needed to bargain with, and he made his offer. If Karina called off their PPV match, he wouldn't press charges against her brother... She didn't need long to think and accepted he deal to protect Max. It looked like that was it for Karina. Injured, depressed, alone, and without a match on PWC's biggest night... She only had one shot left, and she took it. One day before Capital Punishment, Magnum began arranging her replacement... An open challenge to anyone, to fight him live on PPV. However, Wolf was hardly the most popular man in the back after what he put Karina through, and nobody answered his call. Frustrated, he said the words Karina was waiting for. Anyone, any match. That was her cue, and before Magnum could even argue, the woman from Albany had accepted the challenge. Wolf v Wolfenden was back on, but with no disqualifications and no count-outs. After a roller coaster of emotions, stretching 11 weeks, Karina finally had what she wanted... Magnum Wolf one on one. As was his style, Wolf again tried to get into her head, arranging to Lawrence Wolfenden to be at ringside for the event. Could her father be what was needed to put her off? The match between the pair at Capital Punishment was a back and forth, brutal and draining contest. Magnum using his strength and size advantage to throw the K-Wolf around at will, while Karina was flying from anything that could take her weight in an attempt to put her tormentor away. This was Karina's chance to show that she belonged in PWC, in the ring... Just as it was Wolf's chance to disprove all her claims. Both wanted to be right, they wanted to be right so badly that neither could be. Following a WolfTamer off a ladder through a table that ended the exhausting and career threatening bout, both were counted down for 10. Having inflicted so much punishment on each other that neither could rise to their feet and beat the count. Magnum was right in part, Karina couldn't pin him no matter what she threw at him... But it was Karina who won far more that just a match that night. It was one of those rare moments when everything just clicked. Her father, having watched his only daughter give everything she had to try and beat the man who tried to tear apart the Wolfenden family, realised that this was Karina at her most vibrant, at her best. How could he deny her that? At Capital Punishment, as Magnum Wolf came to in the ring, father and daughter left through the crowd. In the end, Karina's determination, pride, and unbreakable will ended up winning her back the same person it had lost her before. The PPV was the last time the Pro Wrestling Circuit saw Karina Wolfenden. after being given a week off to spend with her family, she was not present last week. But just as it looked like things were back to normal for Karina, just as it looked like everything had fallen back into place around her care-free life, the words of Paula Michelle Smith seem to place her in the cross-hairs of another wrestler... ...Will Karina's idealist world yet again be intruded upon? Nobody knows what lays ahead for Karina, and even the Barding Beauty herself isn't one to look further than the day's end, so to see where this unique wrestler's drive takes her, you'll have to keep watching the Pro Wrestling Circuit every Saturday. . . . . . I've been Mark Malloy, and I'll be back shortly with more of the stories our diverse and talented roster have lived. So until next time, keep enjoying our product and don't forget to order Last Man Standing, live only on PPV, June 16th! -Mark Malloy
