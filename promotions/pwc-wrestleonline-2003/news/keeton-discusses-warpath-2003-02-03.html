Keeton Discusses Warpath

3 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The day after PWC's Television debut former two time PCW Cruiserweight Champion, and Advantage Wrestling World Champion Jake Keeton caught up with his former enemy and new found fan, PWC Color Commentator Bang Davenport to give his thoughts on the comments made by Keeton's tag team partner for the second edition of PWC TV. "Ol' Jake sure did get a lil bit redneck when he heard what Warpath had ta say." Bang stated as he chewed his toe nails. "I reckon he was mad enough ta spit nails. Talkin bout solvin problems but I didn't see one a them there arithmetic books." When Jason Maddux, PWC Interviewer, approached Keeton for the English translation of what Bang was trying to relay Keeton had this to say. "This guy is nuts," said Keeton. "Not only is he proclaiming himself as the first World Heavyweight Champion, but he must have been completely delusional when he stated no one in PWC can out think, out muscle, or out wrestle him." Keeton said with a cocky smirk as he continued. "I don't doubt that he could out muscle the majority of the guys in the locker room, and after his actions this past week he doesn't appear to be able to out think an ignorant fan and his twelve year old kid, but when it comes to wrestling I'm sure that quite a few of the boys in the back, myself included, could wrestle circles around him." When Maddux reminded Jake that he and Warpath would be partners Keeton smiled. "I know that, and I look forward to it, as long as he sticks to using that muscle and lets me do the thinking and wrestling we'll be a great team." replied Keeton as he flashed the PWC cameras an arrogant grin. If anything further develops with this situation we'll be sure to inform you.
