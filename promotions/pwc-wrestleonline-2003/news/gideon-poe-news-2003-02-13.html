Gideon Poe News

13 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
That's right folks, Hot Topic has finally announced that it has a new spokesperson to lead them into a new era! After PCW's Darkstar recently turned his back on Hot Topic and threw down his position as spokesperson without a second thought, the company was in desperate need to find a new young fresh face to fill The Mourning Star's position. Nevertheless, they found the new spokesperson for their ever popular brand in the form of PWC's very own Gideon Poe, which was announced on Hot Topic's official website much to the delight of their loyal customers and public. Poe, who apparently was slightly reluctant at accepting the position at first, eventually accepted the offer. Since he did, the response from Hot Topic's customers has been nothing but positive. "I mean if others want to turn their back on us, so be it, it's no skin off our nose. At least with Gideon we know that won't happen without good reason. The guy tells it like it is and doesn't bullshit or beat around the bush. He's just what this company needs in a spokesperson." "He's the perfect man to represent Hot Topic and speak up for it right now. He fits in perfectly with it's image and no doubt he'll tell it like it is as spokesperson." Rumours also have it that PWC CEO Ron Starr is more than pleased with having one of his roster members associated with such a strong name with an even stronger following of customers, and no doubt hopes this will draw in those very people to watch PWC TV to see their new spokesperson in action every week. Yet the highlight of this huge announcement was witnessed as Poe gave his first statement about accepting the position as Hot Topic spokesperson on a local underground radio show in the New Jersey area, using the opportunity to not only get his feelings about accepting the position out to the numerous listeners, which was very positive, but also used it to get out his thoughts and feelings on his current bitter enemy in PWC, WarPath. "If the people want to know what their new spokesperson has to say, they can tune into PWC TV. I'll do all my talking in the ring where I always do it, and I'll do it against that brain-dead steroid-ridden freak WarPath. Then when it's all over, he'll be nothing more than a Dead Topic." Strong words from Hot Topics new spokesperson and PWC's very own Gideon Poe.
