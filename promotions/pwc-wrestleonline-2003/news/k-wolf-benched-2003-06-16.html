K-Wolf Benched

16 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After the punishment taken during the course of Last Man Standing on Sunday night, Karina Wolfenden has been blocked from competing in the ring this coming Saturday at PWC TV. After an attack by Ariath, followed by 2 hard-fought matches against Paula Michelle Smith and Clark Carlisle respectively, Karina was barely able to stand by the time the show went off air... But was still resisting any attempts by PWC's medical personnel to 'ground' her. "To say she was as stubborn as an Ox, would be unfair to the Ox," commented the unfortunate medic who treated her, "It was like talking to a brick wall... But less productive." It was only when PWC officials stepped in and told Karina that they were refusing to book her for this Saturday was the situation finally resolved, much to the annoyance of Miss Wolfenden who will however still be in attendance for the show. Not one to happily sit out on the sidelines and watch the fun go on, Karina had her own interpretation of events. "Okay, so it hurt a little.... Goes with the territory though, or so you'd think," muttered the frustrated wrestler, "I thought by this age we were meant to be able to look after ourselves?" she then added, still looking a little worse for wear after the previous night, but in much better condition physically. With Karina forced to stay out of the ring this week, albeit against her will, be sure to tune in Saturday to see what the Boarding Beauty will do to keep herself entertained.
