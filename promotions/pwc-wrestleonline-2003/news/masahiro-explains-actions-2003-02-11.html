Masahiro Explains Actions

11 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
One of the most talked about moments from last Saturday's edition of PWC TV was when Masahiro Zangetsu hurled himself from the top rope onto Sebastian Toomes, who was on a table in the ringside area, not only driving Toomes through a table, but eliminating himself from the Tag Team Battle Royale in the process. Our Jason Maddux caught up with Zangetsu after PWC TV and asked him why he did it. "Baka Jan!" screamed Zangetsu. "I did it for a reason. Life is about Respect. Respect is earned by proving to others you respect yourself and you respect them. I showed faith in Vendetta doing what I did. I showed self respect in what I did!" When asked to elaborate, Zangetsu clenched his fists. "When I leapt off that turnbuckle I knew what I was doing," seethed Zangetsu. "I was paying Toomes back for the chair shots he gave me earlier in the night and more important than paying Toomes back, I was giving those fans in that arena what they wanted to see. Your "Straight Shooting" style works better when your fully informed damu ketsu." With that Masahiro Zangetsu spat on the ground and the interview abruptly ended.
