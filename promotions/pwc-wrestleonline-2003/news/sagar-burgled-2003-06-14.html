Sagar Burgled

14 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PWC's Southern Preacher Eric Sagar was involved in a burglary earlier in the week. During the early hours of the morning his house was robbed. While sleeping heavily after an early night of supposedly spreading the word of God and bringing joy into the world, when his house was broken into. Eric Sagar accuses Andre Roberts of breaking an entry, attempted burglary disturbing the preacher's sleep, holding him hostage at gun point, possession of an unlicensed fire arm, threatening him and assault. "The heathen broke into my house and went through my items like a tornado taking anything of a value of more than five American dollars! I think he wanted to wake me up so he could frighten me." Eric Sagar claimed "He then pulled out a pistol and hit me with the butt. He held me at gun point. I reasoned with him. Luckily I was able to knock the phone off hook and press 5, the speed dial for my good friend, Propaganda's cell phone. He heard everything and I was able to risk my life long enough for him to sneak in and save me." When asked if he felt he was lucky, Mr. Sagar replied "Never, I knew I would get through it alive and okay as God is with me." When asked for a comment off Mr. Roberts' lawyer, Mr. Theo Sedlmayr, Esq. for Cutler & Sedlmyer LLP insisted that his client was denying all charges accept breaking an entry and attempted burglary.
