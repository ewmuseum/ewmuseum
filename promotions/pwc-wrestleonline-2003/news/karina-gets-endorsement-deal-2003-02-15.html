Karina Gets Endorsement Deal

15 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hot on the heels of Gideon Poe's announcement that he is to became a spokesperson for the clothing chain "Hot Topic", K2 Snowboarding have revealed that PWC's own Boarding Beauty, Karina Wolfenden has agreed to endorse their products. The company, which operates off of www.k2snowboarding.com was quick to praise Miss Wolfenden upon announcing the deal. "Karina was a real breath of fresh air." stated the head of on-line marketing, "She's a regular customer who told us she had just started wrestling on-camera, and said that she'd endorse our products if we wanted. Naturally, we jumped at the chance." Already the Boarding Beauty has funded a photo shoot where she models some of the company's wares, to act a promotional material for K2 who were quick to confirm she has waived any fee or discounts. "In many ways they're just like the PWC," spoke Miss Wolfenden when reached for comment. "Both are pretty cool small companies who can use a break. Some fans have asked where I get my gear from, so now I can just point to my shirt." Upon hearing the news, Karina's main detractor, PWC's very own Magnum Wolf scoffed at the deal. "When did wrestling rings become sandwich boards?" the Private Eye sneered, "We're here to wrestle, not to slap logos all over our clothes... If fans wanted that they'd read the local free-ads." With the fledgling PWC already having two of it's popular stars with advertising deals, as well as the prospect of a TV deal in Brooklyn, the Pro Wrestling Circuit appears to be finding it's feet in it's early days.
