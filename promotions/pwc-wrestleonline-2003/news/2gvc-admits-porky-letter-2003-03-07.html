2GVC Admits Porky Letter

7 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After the previous rumour that Totakoxe Salvador had sent Porky Bunson a threatening letter, news is developing that the letter was NOT sent by the Argentine. Bunson declined an interview but allegedly said, "I knew it wasn't Salvador. He be a good guy!" Bunson, when he received the letter, was shocked that someone could be so vicious. The New Jersey Police Department had somehow tracked the letters down to an abandoned house within Atlantic City. Knowing full well the address of Totakoxe Salvador, he has been ruled out as a culprit especially after a disturbing revelation by 2GVC. Chandler Tsonda and Jimmy Nguyen, collectively known as 2nd Generation Viet Cong, admitted to sending the letter, saying, "We thought it would not only be funny but scare the shit out of Porky's lifeless body! And it worked, better than we could ever have imagined!" 2GVC received a formal caution from the police. It just goes to show that 2GVC are grateful that Penance put Porky in hopsital and further emphasizes the disgraceful behaviour of the duo. We here, at PWC, are glad this case is finally closed and are relieved that Totakoxe Salvador did not threaten 'Bad Luck' Bunson. When asked about the situation, Salvador simply and smugly told those who accused him, "Told you it wasn't me" before leaving. Will the accusations towards Salvador add more fire into the mix for the proposed Tag Team Match this Saturday night? Will Salvador have to face 2GVC alone as his tag partner, Axtell, has been diagnosed as having bruised and cracked ribs among other injuries? Tune in this Saturday night to find out...
