PWC — Column

Marking Out #3

25 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello everyone, and welcome to the third edition of Marking Out. A lot has happened since I last checked in with you, including PWC’s apparent signing of an African American Superstar in an attempt to help secure a TV deal with WMXS TV-22 officials out of Harlem. While most people believe from the conversation we witnessed at the close of last Saturday nght’s telecast of PWC TV was with none other than Jordan X Shabazz, its my believe that the “Jordan” Starr was speaking to was actually the representative or agent of someone else, perhaps a Jonathan Conspiracy or even a Ruben Ross. I suppose we will find out one way or another this Saturday night when PWC hits the Apollo theatre. I have to say that both Magnum Wolf and Karina Wolfenden have performed admirably thus far and their feud has been entertaining to say the least. Props to a veteran like Wolf for helping a rookie talent like Wolfenden get over. He has proven himself to be nothing but a class act and positive influence backstage since coming aboard with PWC, despite rumors he would be just the opposite. I was one of the people who had serious doubts about the decision to bring in Jake Keeton when it was announced, but he is another guy who has proven his worth. He is an asset to the organization, and works hard both in and out of the ring. I am really looking forward to the Jim Axtell/Penance match this week, and do not believe it will be as much of a walkover as many people think. Jim Axtell is one of the toughest competitors I have had the pleasure of knowing during my time in wrestling, and has proven himself in the past against other ‘monsters’ such as Hessian and Graphic Violence. I think if Penance and Jacob Solomon take him lightly, they will be making a very, very serious mistake, one that Axtell just might hammer home, both figuratively and literally. As many of you problem have expected, Rancid has been released. He was not only having troubles in the ring, but outside the ring as well, as he never really meshed in with the rest of the guys in the locker room. There has been no change in Porky Bunson’s condition, and he is still unable to move from the waist down. I suppose its in God’s hands if Porky is ever walks again, lets alone wrestle. Totakoxe Salvador is a real breath of fresh air in PWC. His energy and talent are not measurable, and PWC officials have high hopes he is one of the new faces that will lead the company into the future and to prosperity. I like the work that Romulus and Jay Phoenix have been doing, but is it just me, or is Phoenix longwinded on the stick? PWC officials like the effort Sam Alotti is putting in, but feel her character is a bit thin, with not a lot of room for growth. They, as well as I, am looking forward to seeing this character become more than just a valley girl who wrestles. This past week was a quiet week for Sebastian Toomes and Clay Vendetta. PWC officials want to see more than they have been getting from these two guys. I, for one, am sure they will respond. I can confirm that former PIW superstar has been signed, and PWC officials are really excited about him. I am sure that fans will be pleased with this addition to the roster. That’s all for this week. I’ll catch you on the flip side with another edition of Marking Out!
