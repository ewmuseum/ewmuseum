Boarding Beauty Not Happy

9 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It's been reported by several people backstage at the Pro Wrestling Circuit that Karina Wolfenden is deflated at not having had a match yet. The Boarding Beauty, as she's become known, was the only active wrestler excluded from the Tag Title rumble on PWC TV this past Saturday, and is not slated to make an appearance in the ring this coming show either. "I think she just wants the chance to show what she can do," commented one of the backstage crew that the personable Miss Wolfenden has befriended. "She's new and she knows that, but with people like Magnum Wolf thinking she's not up to the task, a match will be her chance to shine and show off her style." Karina has not commented on the matter herself, seeming somewhat evasive to reporters. But, one member of PWC has been very vocal on the matter, one Magnum Wolf. "Good," he grunted with distaste for the Boarder in his voice. "You don't see me playing on some mountain in Switzerland and living off Daddy's money... So why should I have to see her in a wrestling ring?" Already a fan favourite, the small following PWC has attracted are anticipating Miss Wolfenden's first match, to see how her Extreme Sports background can translate into the squared circle. "Karina rules!" exclaimed one fan outside the National Guard Armoury, in Atlantic City following PCW TV on Saturday. "She just does what she wants... So let her wrestle, man!" Despite the fact that her Father, Lawrence Wolfenden, will be in attendance at this Saturday's PWC TV show, it's not confirmed that Karina will turn up herself. Some she has talked to mentioned that she was considering attending an anti-war protest in London's Hyde Park on Saturday afternoon, not something that would hint towards her showing up at the show. More on these stories as they develop.
