2GVC Sets LMS Plans

10 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
We saw Saturday night that 2GVC (brilliantly, they most likely think) tricked the drunken pair of The Masked Mulet and El Spiffy! into leaving themselves very much revealed outside the arena. The bait was an Alliance Titles shot at Last Man Standing, which Spiffy! and Mullet have been pestering 2GVC about for weeks. It's now official that 2GVC has accepted the challenge for Last Man Standing, and will defend against the duo. However, which of the men from 2GVC (or whether it will be all three) is unclear from the statement they released. "Sure, we'll give the sock puppet-toting pair of numbnuts their shot." That was Chandler Tsonda's response to questions about whether 2GVC would actually give a shot to Mullet and Spiffy!. "I mean, if they were moronic enough to go out into the night in their....well, if they were that dumb, the least we can do is kick their ass for a national audience at the Pay-per-view." So, the match is now set for LMS, but we can only speculate on what 2GVC has planned for next week's PWC TV and then the pay-per-view
