Is Vendetta Mobbed Up Too?

2 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It's no secret that PWC superstar Angelo Deville has extensive "family ties", if you will... his links to organized crime and the Mafia are about as flaunted as they can be by The Devilish One while still maintaining the vow of omerta, or silence. However, our insiders recently spoke with noted Mafia expert Jeffrey Scangarello, who said that the ties between PWC superstars and the Mob quite possibly extend further than Deville. "Of course, the FBI is keeping extensive tabs on Deville, who is noted around the community for doing a stunning job keeping his hands clean, especially considering the amount of 'wet-work' he does personally," said Scangarello. "But it's also widely rumored that Clay Vendetta, real name Clayton Battaglia, has some ties he's not mentioning as well." When pressed, Scangarello said he didn't have much hard evidence to go on, but had a few hunches. "Vendetta inherited a large sum of money from his father, that much is known... it's the reason he's able to maintain such a comfortable lifestyle despite the drop in paycheck heading to PWC. However, Vendetta never has spoken about his father or the source of the money, and though Battaglia is a common name in Italy, there is at least one major and two minor crime families using the name." But how, if at all, do the two men's ties affect their status in PWC? Scangarello explained: "If the rumors are true, Vendetta's worked to estrange himself from that way of life, it seems. However, if it's true that there are connections, you can bet that given his reputation, Deville will work them for information and to gain every psychological edge possible." With Deville demanding "tribute" from Vendetta a week ago, and the recent intrigue surrounding Deville and O'Neil, time will tell whether the heat between Deville and Vendetta will leave one of them "sleeping with the fishes"!
