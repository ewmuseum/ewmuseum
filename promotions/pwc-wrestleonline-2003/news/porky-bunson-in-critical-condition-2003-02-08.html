Porky Bunson in Critical Condition

8 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PWC fan favorite, Porky Bunson, was rushed to the hospital following Saturday night's broadcast of PWC TV, after he suffered what appeared to be a spinal cord injury at the hands of the beast known as Penance during the main event. "We have no word on his condition, other than it is critical," said PWC spoksesperson, Mark Malloy. "It would be improper to speculate now, but we ask that all PWC fans keep Porky in their thoughts and prayers during his time of need." Fans at ringside claim Porky screamed about being unable to move his legs after suffering several high impact moves by Penance. We, of course, will continue to monitor this story and bring you updates as they become available.
