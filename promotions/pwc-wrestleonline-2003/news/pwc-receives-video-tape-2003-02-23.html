PWC Receives Video Tape

23 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Earlier today, PWC officials recieved a clip of what appears to be the final tape that Magnum Wolf sent to Lawrence Wolfenden. We already saw two segments being taped on air on PWC TV involving a drug dealer, and a harassing fan. This clip came to us with a number one written on the tape, so I expect we will be recieving more soon. Water splashes on the floor of a damp bathroom. The sounds of "Figaro" bounce off of every corner of the room as someone in the middle shower stall is belting out the opera classic. The little "REC" with the red dot next to it is on in the corner of the screen. Slightly in the way of the camera's lens is a shower curtain. Apparently, Magnum is hidden in one of the opposite showers. Suddenly, a blonde haired head pops out of that middle shower, and looks around as he slowly stops singing the song he had begun. He checks his watch, then smiles slightly as he whips open the shower curtain. Angelo Deville stands in the shower, fully dressed, and holding a very large fish. He whips out a Sharpie, and begins to scribble around the eyes of the poor thing. Angelo: You always were the foolish hero, Jason. Stepping in to try and stop me even when you didn't understand what you were stepping in. Well, you sonuvabitch, you've really stepped in it now. Wolf: *Whispering* Lawrence Wolfenden, I'd like to introduce you to Angelo Deville. He's one of the number one contenders to the PWC world title. He also is suspected of having mafia connections. Deville finishes with one eye on the fish, which is now covered with Sharpie in a design which is very reminiscent of Jason O'Neil's face paint. He grins widely at the fish, and snarls at it slightly... Angelo: In FSW, you were at least smart enough to use the law. Now you're on MY turf. Going by MY rules. And when the time comes, Jason... Wolf: *Still whispering, slightly surprised himself* He appears to... uhhh... be threatening the life of that dead fish. And yes, he is painting on a carp. Rumor has it that he's got it out for your daughter, too. Angelo continues to trash talk the little fish... but his voice sounds funny near the end of this speech. It also loses synchronization with the movement of his mouth. Angelo: Believe me Jason, this will only be over *the voice suddenly changes to Magnum Wolf impersonating Deville* is when I decide to go after that preppy boarding bitch! After a few extra seconds past the words ending, Angelo's mouth stops moving. Obviously, Magnum did a bit of post-work on the tape. Wolf: *still whispering* Is this really someone you want your daughter to be working around? The scene cuts off abruptly, as Angelo Deville wraps the little Sunburst masked fish inside a newspaper, and walks out of the bathroom whistling "Figaro" once again... All of these scenes we're seeing are obviously being set up by Magnum Wolf in order to get Karina pulled out of PWC. Will Lawrence see through the Private Eye's plot, or will he be in attendance next week to remove Karina from her position in PWC?
