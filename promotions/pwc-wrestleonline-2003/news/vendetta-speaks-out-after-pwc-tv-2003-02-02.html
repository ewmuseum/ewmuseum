Vendetta Speaks Out After PWC TV

2 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Outspoken as always, the newly-signed PWC superstar "Classic" Clay Vendetta was on hand after the first edition of PWC TV, giving his comments to reporters. "I'm thrilled with the way things went down on the first show," Vendetta said. "It's certainly a change of pace for me, but this is what wrestling's really about in my book." On his former fed, OSW, Vendetta had a few surprisingly nice things to say. "OSW really gave me my first big break, so I'll always be grateful to them, to an extent. I've still got a lot of respect for Ivan, Tempest, Kid Wonder and all the others who've respected me backstage. I think they understand why I had to jump on this opportunity, and I'd still relish the opportunity to work with any one of them in the future." Vendetta was quick to temper those comments with the sort of bile he showed in his "shoot" diatribe on PWC TV, though. "But God, was it nice to actually be able to get out there and say what I've always wanted to say about Jane Reagan. That's right, Jane, when I win my first PWC title at Capitol Punishment... and despite what's being termed an "unclear situation" after that big goon's interference in MY match tonight, I WILL be wrestling for that title, I promise you... I'm gonna dedicate it to you, Jane, and you can stick that up your tight little ass for holding me down." As for the rest of the roster, Vendetta had a mix of respect and contempt. "O'Neil, from what I've seen, is a decent guy and a hell of a wrestler. As for the rest, though? Sebastian Toomes is, was, and always will be a piece of trash, and I fully expect to show that to the world over the course of the next few weeks. Deville's a punk... I'm sure he's parading around with his big head thinking he's so ballsy for trying to pick a fight with me; well, Angelo, you may be a paisan, but if you're dumb enough to get in my way, I'll bury you deeper than Hoffa." As for the masked monster at the end of the main event? "Stupid thug," Vendetta said, "sticking his nose where it doesn't belong. Jumping me by surprise at the end of a match, he got lucky. He won't be so lucky if he tries a stunt like that again." Finally, reporters questioned the whereabouts of Vendetta's girlfriend, Niki Weathers, and Vendetta was somewhat vague: "We're still not sure about what Niki's role will be, from a managerial standpoint. We're still working out where we want to be in that regard." Vendetta vehemently denied rumors that their relationship was on rocky ground, however, stating that "just because you're not seeing Niki doesn't mean she's not seeing plenty of me." At that point, Vendetta left the arena. Given CCV's history of bluntness with interviewers, though, if he has anything further to say I'm sure you will hear it shortly.
