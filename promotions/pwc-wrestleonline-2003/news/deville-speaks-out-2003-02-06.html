Deville Speaks Out

6 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Angelo Deville, PWC's alleged mobster at large and the Primetime Central Universal Champion, is finally back in the spotlight after a three month hiatus. Not the burning attention of international sports entertainment acclaim -- this is PWC, afterall -- but the harsh interrogative lights of the law. While he has no criminal charges pending at the moment (which I, for one, don't think will last another week), a joint civil suit was launched yesterday by two students of the college where PWC TV debuted. Their names are being withheld in an attempt to retain what little anonymity remains to them after their cruel treatment Saturday night. Both have reportedly left school, fearing not only ridicule from their peers but retaliation from the Crimson Angel himself. "Dude, the guy's a lunatic," one of the youths sputtered over the phone in a worried and no more than half-sobre voice. "I think he might've, like, killed us if those chicks weren't there." He refused to say more, insisting his phone was tapped, but I managed to discover the amount of the joint claim before he hung up, sobbing. When I tracked down Mr. Deville at his restaurant, A Little Taste of Hell, and asked him what he thought of the $100,000 lawsuit, he simply sneered... Seemingly at me more than the lawsuit. "Who gives a shit what those moneygrubbing morons are asking for? They're not getting shit," Deville growled emphatically, backing me around the large room. "Is this really news to you? Is this the best thing you can think to come and ask me about?" I tried shaking my head to placate him, but he rolled right over me. "Why not ask about my long-awaited return? Why I'd choose PWC over PCW, OSW, and fWo? You think people are more interested in a lawsuit than that? How about my thoughts on the gigolo and his classy sidekick... My plans for the Scarecrow... What I intend to about that gigantic masked freak and his dominatrix." Fumbling, I tried to ask some of those very questions, but he raged on, barely even noticing me now despite grabbing my tie and choking me with it. "None of that's important to you fools in the media, right? All you care about is that some assholes want to take advantage of my wealth. You make me sick." Dragging me, coughing and clawing, to the front door, he paused at the last moment and pulled my face close to his. "You want to know what I think?" His eyes burned into mine, threatening to char my insides. "I think you'd better stay the fuck outta my way..." Without warning he tossed me through the window beside the door -- his own window! -- then spat unconcernedly on the shards of glass covering my writhing form. "...before something bad happens to ya."
