A Black Male's Blackmail

6 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Mays Landing, NJ- This week on PWC TV, Frankie Bones played his part in helping PWC inch closer to getting another regional television deal. Question is, is Ron Starr willing to pay Frankie's asking price in order to close the deal? Also this week on PWC TV, Ron Starr is making a list and checking it twice, PMS made an impact early and often and Karina Wolfenden hit another bump on her already tough road en route to making her dream of being a big time professional wrestler come true... ...and did we mention that when the night was over, either Angelo Deville or Clay Vendetta would be sleeping with the fishes... literally?!?
