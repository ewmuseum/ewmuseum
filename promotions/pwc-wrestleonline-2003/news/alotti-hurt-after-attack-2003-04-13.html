Alotti Hurt After Attack

13 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Last night, on PWC TV, Samantha Alotti was found seriously injured in the parking garage. She was rushed to emergency care, and released six hours later. Our own Mark Malloy was there, waiting, ready to get an interview. Mark: First off, how are you doing? Alotti: Hello, I got run over with a car... (after a sigh): I guess it could be worse. Like, I have cuts and bruises and stuff all down my right side, and my right arm and leg are, you know, banged up and all. It so hurts, but it could be worse." Mark: Will you compete tonight? Alotti: Like, totally. I'm not going to let her just do this and, you know, get away with it? Mark: 'Her'? You saw who attacked you?! Alotti: ... Well, no. Bu-bu-but I so know who was behind it. Going off of these statements, authorities paid an early-morning visit to Paula Michelle Smith's apartment, armed with a search warrant for her home and Ariath's vehicle. First of all, no evidence linking PMS to the assault was found without the apartment. As for Ariath's car... Apparently the wrestler got into a semi-serious fender-bender on the way home from PWC TV last night. Because the car, which also looked like it had been freshly cleaned, was too damaged to supply any concrete evidence, the charges against the pair have been dropped. Paula Michelle Smith had this to say: "This is an insult, and an unwanted distraction. Is it not bad enough that I was involved in a car accident last night? On top of which, Ariath's neck has been sore since the incident, and I must worry about that. I have enough things to do without moronic bitches throwing groundless accusations at me. Of course, if she is desperately trying to get out of our match tonight, I cannot say that I blame her." So, despite Alotti's injuries and Paula's latest brush with the law, the 'loser leaves town' match goes on... Catch this, and much more, on 'Capitol Punishment,' live and only on Pay Per View!
