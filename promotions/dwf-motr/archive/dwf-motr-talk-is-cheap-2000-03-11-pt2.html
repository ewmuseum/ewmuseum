SJ: And speaking of good guys, it is odd but Dave Pietka got a lot of cheers last week! JV: Florida is his home state, and it looked like he was out to kiss a little ass there too.

Gabriel Demorte take to the ring in a three-way matchup that could be a preview of the violence to come at Total Annihilation!
JV: Demorte is brilliant! He burns scrolls, he puts glue in eyes. This guy will do anything to win and I predict that he’ll do what he needs to do on this very card.
SJ: We’ll have to see about that. Roll clips please… =========================== “HEAVY MENTAL” DAVE PIETKA =========================== Voice: Well, this is turning out to be a GRAND OL' TIME! Excuse me while I toss around some cray paper and inflate some balloons with all the hot air coming outta Demorte!
((The scene fades into the Candress Estate in South Beach, home of former IHWA Commentator and frequently lusted-after woman "Alluring"
Cassandra Candress... along with the behemoth known only as "Marz" Candress, Cassandra's brother. Inside, the large behemoth is sitting in a chair while Cassandra applies a solvent to her brother's eyes... YES, the shades came off, but Cassandra is standing right in front of Marz, blocking our view. "Heavy Mental" Dave Pietka is sitting right next to
Marz, while "Double D" Devin DeMasters is treating his wounds...
although not as severe as the ones on her bodyguard. Pietka is flinching at the sting of the bactine against his open flesh.))
D. Pietka: OWWWW! That hurt, Devin! D. DeMasters: Well, if you didn't squirm around while I did this, it wouldn't hurt as much! ((looking at Cassandra)) I don't get it, Cassie. He can take hits from ladders, wooden boards, and anything wrapped in steel wire... he'll never let someone hold the fact that they made him say "I Quit"... he'll never back down from a single person... yet he can't stand the stinging of a little bit of bactine!
C. Candress: Tell me about it. I heard my older brother here was the same way. Now look at him. You could press a hot coal against his skin and he won't flinch...
D. Pietka: Yeah, well that rat bastard Demorte thought of something new. Because he THOUGHT I was some kinda rookie! Because he THOUGHT
Marz is here to help me! Well, that punk-ass bizz-nitch much be the rook... I've been in this damn business for close to five [bleep]in'
years. Marz is here for Devin... but he's free to help me out when I ask him. After all... it's my name on his paycheck!
C. Candress: What paycheck? D. Pietka: The paycheck that I... OH, wait... that's Nick Demola's name! Heh... sorry, my mind went wondering. I'd like to know how long
Demorte's been at this. Total Wrestling Machine? Didn't know they came equipped with glue spray! You know... there are reasons he did... OW!
D. DeMasters: Sorry, hun. ((Pietka stands up, a little fustrated, and looks at DeMasters. She jumps back, startled a little, causing her to bounce a bit... The bactine bottle and rag still in her hand, while Pietka looks at her.))
D. Pietka: You know... there's such a thing warning me! High Pain Threshold or not, that stuff stings like a bi... D. DeMasters: Like a WHAT? D. Pietka: Like... like a... oh, damn... Like a... bastard? ((DeMasters smiles, and pushes Pietka back into his chair.)) D. Pietka: Whew. Anyway... You know why Demorte did this to you, Marz. He's SCARED! He's AFRAID! He's PETRIFED! Of all of us! You, especially. He thinks that if I'm without you, I'll be easy pickings. NEWS FLASH,
DEMORTE! I won the match at the first Battleground, NOT Marz... The
Wicked Existenz is getting their fair shot at what is truly there because we worked for it, NOT because Marz Candress threw his hand into your face and decided to make it a permanent ornament! But he just might, because you have to remember something. Before that glue went into his eyes, Gabriel... GOD, what a faggy name... Before it when in there, YOURS was the last face he saw! And while he may have been hankering for a hunk of LockHEART!... you KNOW he's gonna want to tear off a piece of your ass, bronze it, and hang it next to the other torn pieces of never-shoulda-been wrestlers and bystanders in his room.
C. Candress: DAMN IT! Marc, can't you just sit still! ((Marz jerks his head, growling very loudly. Cassandra just blows it off.)) C. Candress: Get over it, Marc... I'm your damn sister. I'll call you whatever I want! D. Pietka: Marz is here for DEVIN'S protection. When I'm busy in the ring, I can't kick YOUR ass and save hers at the same time... just can't be done. In fact, Demorte... if you would have kept your paws on the matter at hand, WHICH WAS ME AND THE EXISTENZ, Marz would have left you alone. He was busy enough having some fun with Vincent Lockheart, all big and bad, tossing down Tarot Cards on the bodies of his quote- unquote victims. Lemme let you in on a secret, Vinnie... The Death Card in Tarot represents change. CHANGE, DAMN IT! As in Change outta those ratty clothes and get into some untorn duds. And one thing that's gonna change is you, your face in particular... see, I bought myself a new pair of Cumberland Boots, and they's just ITCHING to have your forehead- print on their heel!
D. DeMasters: All done! ((DeMasters is very quick to sit in Pietka's lap, and he doesn't mind... as her sudden descent caused her to bounce again. Cassandra finished with Marz's eyes, and placed his shades back on before he could open his eyes. She pulls up a chair, and sits next to her brother.))
D. Pietka: But there's another card in the Tarot, Lochness. It's called "The Fool", representing irrationality and carelessness... which is exactly what you are showing towards everyone. Hell, if you wanna play with swords, I'll play with swords. I have a tasty fifty-six inch
Scottish Claymore and an antique Military Dress Sword I can poke you with. You see... I KNOW I'm gonna breeze back Demorte, he's not a real big problem. If he wrestles like he talks, he may as well not even show up at all, I'll humiliate him that badly. Hell, it was HIS carelessness that's keeping our match unadulterated, which is a plus for me. But YOU, Lockandkey, I want YOU! I always go in head first and brains last against the big Gothic pain and/or death freaks. They're always the first to go. You see... I DESTROYED Jedah Talikite in a career ending match, and now the phreaker is scared to go anywhere NEAR me! Now... I'll take you out. I'm not afraid! I'M NEVER AFRAID!
FEARLESS, FRANTIC, AND F[bleep]IN' NUTS, REMEMBER? Bring on your skunkfists and your Memnock Mambos!
C. Candress: Um... Dave... I think they're called Stinkfists and Devil's Dances. D. Pietka: Yeah, I know... but a guy like Lockup, who thinks of nothing better to do that blindsides my boys and toss around Tarot Cards, you can't take him seriously! Just like DePostmortem, anyone who thinks they are better than me, funnier than me, and takes out a guy who is of absolutely no threat to anyone except a couple of groupers at ringside because he's S[bleep]ING HIS PANTS at the thought of getting manhandled by him. Only people worth taking seriously HERE is the Wicked Existenz!
And now that poor Mark Marleau has been thrown into this... after being manhandled by the Anti-Social Sublife, before joining me... the fatality numbers are gonna be thrown through the roof and thrown all the way down into the basement again. But secretly... I'm praying I get into the ring with Lockheart! I hope he kicks the crap outta Locke and grinds Marleau into a bloody mess, because I want to take that Black
Flag and dye it white with FRIGHT! Mo-fo thinks he's intimidating anyone... NEWS FLASH! Often Imitated, Sometimes Duplication, NEVER
Intimidated!
D. DeMasters: So the three-way's gonna be a piece of cake? D. Pietka: You know it, sweetheart. Marleau will be going down, and the quote-unquote Total Wrestling Machine's gonna find that there's a little virus out there. It infects both organic and inorganic, human and mechanical... and that virus is called the HEAVY MENTALITY
SYNDROME! Worse than Melissa, Trojan Horse, and whatever the weekly
Flesh-Eating virus is this time. So bring what you got! Bring your spray cans of glue, bring your Tarot Cards, bring your swords... I'LL
GIVE YOU A BEATING TEN TIMES THEIR WORTH! C. Candress: Dave... we're all in the same room. ((Pietka slumps back into his chair, and he and Marz exchange a glance. Pietka shrugs.)) D. Pietka: Well... I don't live with her, so I can deal with it. ((Fade out.)) =========================================== “TOTAL WRESTLING MACHINE” GABRIEL DEMORTE ============================================ [ Gabriel Demorte. The Total Wrestling Machine. Week after week in
DWF he has continued to prove that he is a man prepared to be the fly in everyone`s ointment, to rock the boat, to make everyone sit up and take notice. Just ask Dave Pietka and his bodyguard Mark Candress, who just last week felt the brunt of Demorte`s aggression. He`s a tough man alright. And now he`s with freelance wrestling report Toxic
Shock...]] TS: Mr. Demorte, thank you for allowing me to interview you. However,
I would like to begin by objecting strongly to the tactics you undertook last week against Mark Candress, bodyguard of Dave Pietka.
Was it necessary to spray glue in his eyes? [ Demorte looks at him with an expression that blends a satisfied smile with utter contempt for the question ]
[ He pauses to think...and replies ] GD: Only in wrestling could an act that would have put me in prison been allowed. Only in wrestling can I push the boundaries of violence against my competitors. You haven`t seen anything yet.
TS: That doesn`t answer my question. GD <snapping>: I don`t damn well feel like answering your question little man. Let me tell you something - Candress would have been more than happy to interfere in that match to help his team. That isn`t exactly fair, so excuse me if I take a pre-emptive strike and equalize things for my team.
TS: So it wasn`t just a simple case of revenge for the attack by Pietka and friends the week before? GD: Of course it was. Any man who is wronged and doesn`t do something to balance the deed is no man. People say leave it to God, or karma or whatever. Well it`s time to take attention because I damn well am living divine retribution, I damn well am your bad karma come back to get you. Nothing you can do can stop it.
TS: Aren`t you afraid of a return attack from Pietka? GD: Pietka can kiss my sweaty b***s. Of course he`s going to try a return attack. I`ll give him that much credit at least. He`ll probably try to bring his little friends with him as well.
TS: Anti Social Sublife? GD: Yeah, that`d describe them. TS: Before we go on to talk about your upcoming match with Pietka and
Marleau, do you have anything to say to Frank Anthony & Jeremy Locke for basically being the worst tag partner ever?
GD: Like sorry? Not a chance. I said, and keep on saying to the authorities that I`m not a tag-team wrestler, and I refuse to wrestle in tag-teams. Don`t trust them. People turn on you. Just look at what
Anthony did to Locke.
TS: So the loss doesn`t bother you? GD: It wasn`t even a match I was involved in. I just turned up to show Pietka and Candress that I`m the man to look out for.
TS: So your thoughts about the upcoming three way match? GD: My thoughts? I hope Marleau, the most over-rated, over-loved wrestler to ever step in the ring, just stays out of the way so I can carry on with ripping off Pietka & Candress`s heads...damn, bring sub- life along and rip their heads off as well...and shove each and every one on top of a ring post. Yeah, that`d be damn pretty.
TS: But do you have any worries of fighting in a three way match? GD: My first match in DWF was a four way battle, and I won that. Way
I see it, three people means there is one less person to worry about me.
TS: Don`t you mean one less person for you to worry about? GD: I know exactly what I said. TS: So you`re confident of victory? GD: This fight will not only give me victory in this match, but also in my semi-final match against Pietka.
TS: Why`s that? GD: Because after this Tuesday, he`ll be lucky to walk again, let alone wrestle. [FADE] SJ: Well we know that at least Dave Pietka and Gabriel Demorte are ready to show their wares in the ring this Monday night!
JV: Monday night? Thought it’d be Tuesday. SJ: Whatever night we get to it, we’ll get to it. Either way it’ll be great action! And our next match will have lots of great action as well. It’s for the fourth semi-final spot in the Southern Title tournament. Vincent Lockheart and Jeremy Locke.
JV: And this is where Lockheart will end this punk’s miserable career once and for all! SJ: This clip from Jeremy Locke is actually the debut of the son of
Frank Anthony’s friend and former DWF great Matt Mitchell as a reporter.
JV: I just hope Locke doesn’t do anything stupid to him, or there’ll be Hell to pay! SJ: Let’s go to this… ============= JEREMY LOCKE ============= I had no idea it was going to be like this. A rookie reporter, called up by Nick Demola to do some DownSouth Wrestling Federation show or something as a favor to my dad Matt. Brian Mitchell, assignment number one -- Jeremy Locke. Interview the enigma. Capture the moonbeam.
Why me? Why was it I who had to go trailing this guy through the streets of every major city in America? Some reporters get to go out galavanting around Mexico, Canada, and all the best little whorehouses in Texas and I got this nutcase? I guess Demola thought that I could defend myself, being the son of the two time
DWF International Champion and all, but I don't know. The blond hair, the flowing trenchcoat... I had seen flashes of this man in the night, and in my nightmares... then I felt a chill from behind.
JL: Young man... I turn, ever wary of a flying kick or a forearm shiver. Instead, I get a set of eyes digging through my skull.
JL: You follow the Hunter of the Shadows... Almost a question, but more of a statement. BM: Y... y... yes... ssssir. I... I... JL: My Dear Brother sent you here, did he not? BM: Y... yes. JL: To interview me for the DWF. To attempt to penetrate the thoughts of a mind too advanced to grasp, correct?
BM: W... w... well, I guess... JL: Go. Go far. Go fast. Go now. The long, muscular arm of Jeremy Locke points me the direction that he wishes I leave to. But I must stay.
BM: Mister Locke... Ummmm... I have to ask you... JL: Do you not understand? Do you not comprehend? Go. Go far. Go FAST. Go NOW! I cannot move. I'm frozen in fear. No way I'm leaving now, I've come so far. JL: Very well. Determined young man. Please God... tell my kids I love them. JL: ... what would you like to know? BM: Huh? JL: Please tell me you aren't deaf. What would you like to know? Elation! I have survived! BM: Thank- JL: Do not thank me or this is over. What do you want to know? I shakily pull out my list of questions. I look through them. Why are you in the DWF? Soft question. Are you out to destroy Nick Demola? GOD these questions suck!
JL: Are you going to ask me anything? BM: Yes... Think. Think. Think. BM: Are you really so simplistic that you can only focus on one thing at a time? Or is there some other reason for carrying on this childish feud with your half-brother?
Oh shit... I said that?!?!?! JL: Hmmm... Oh shit... he's looking me up and down. God I'm glad I wore brown pants... JL: I must say, that takes guts. I admire that. He admires it... good... JL: But... [The camera shows Brian's head smashing against the wall. He loses consciousness and Locke looks to the camera.]
JL: A victim. A victim is what I've been all my life. And I must take apart the one man who has kept me at that status. Is this about revenge? Yes. Cold blooded revenge. My life is ruined. A life for a life.
[The dark man looks down to the ground.] JL: Not dead. He'll be back. Gall will get you nowhere. [He now positions himself in front of the camera and glares into it.] JL: I have had a busy time lately, traveling all over the South fighting a hick and a few degenerates.
[He looks to the sky.] JL: But I am focused. And as the Fates as my witness, I shall walk in the DWF champion and walk out with that very same belt. Why? Because it angers my half-brother so. It would anger him if I actually WON the whole thing, but that is for later.
[The man disappears into the shadows with one swift move.] JL: Learn it... the Word has spoken. The cross is set. But this time, the tables are turning. For the carpenter's son will no longer be the martyr. The crown of thorns is won by King Nothing, and he shall be enclosed in death for eternity.
[The camera looks into the night sky.] JL: When you see the shadows of the night, you will understand. [The camera fades to black.] JV: Where the HELL does this guy get off slamming Brian Mitchell’s head into a wall??? SJ: Jeremy Locke has a temper, that’s for certain. And Brian is a bright kid. He should know better than to be so uppity with someone who is twice his size.
JV: Locke will pay! I guarantee it! SJ: Ladies and gentlemen, that’s it for this week. For Juan Vasquez this is Stuart Jennings saying See You At BattleGround!!!
