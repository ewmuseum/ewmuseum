Night One Controversies and...

Jul 13, 2000, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
If you missed Night One of War of Attrition - Master of the Ring
III, you missed... The Wasp pushing forward in the tournament in what just might be his very last event in wrestling.
The battle between two friends and a MotR veteran, as "Candyman" Damian Rose, Mad Goat, and Sabbath all did battle. Ace of Hearts and Vandal Gomez sticking it out one-on-one for most of their match, with Randall Crane involved as well. And what some people would call a "shocker" ending.
Simon Bach, Knick Man, and Hades having probably the match of the night against each other, with a close call ending.
Jeremy Locke and Kieran Erotikka starting their match without the third man in the match, Rick Styles. But Styles would make his appearance... in a controversial ending that proves only one thing... sins of the past truly do come back to haunt people.
Andrew Tucker taking over his match with Tracker and F.U. Walters, but suffering ultimate damage from Styles and crew. And the crowning of the final DWF and MotR Tag Champions, with a shocking revelation that puts to end a FOUR YEAR LONG angle in the independant federations.
