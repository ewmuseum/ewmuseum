MotR IV - Tradition - Night 2 Promo

27 February 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Many people are already calling Master of the Ring IV one of the great supercards ever... And we're only halfway done. Now comes Night Two. Surprises have been shown in the announcement of the guest enforcers for the Eight Man Elimination Match between Team N.E.W. and the Masters of the Ring. Also, with Chris Hopper's interference in the award ceremony of "Cowboy" Ken Curtis, a Texas Death Match has been signed between those two superstars.
I guarantee there will be one more shocker. It'll get Night Two kicked off in a huge way. So here's the lineup. The card will be out tomorrow night, right on time. Get on the mailing list by writing to [detail withheld] if you haven't already. NIGHT TWO - Wednesday, February 28th, 2000 (with special guest color commentator... DWF GREAT, ROBERT MULA!!!)
1) EIGHT TEAM ELIMINATION TAG MATCH Team Sychosys, Burt Clifton/Mike Masters, The Suicide Blondes, American Idols vs. Precious Moments, JoJo The Indian Circus Boy/Homeless Joe, Dichotomy, Desolation Row - In what is shaped up to be one of the most memorable tag matches on any card in recent memory, a couple of younger teams try to hang with the veterans here. Much has been said about the return of Team Sychosys (will
Joe show, especially after his anti-violence rhetoric of recent weeks?),
Precious Moments and the team of JoJo and Homeless Joe... as well as defending MotR champs Desolation Row, but not much has been said about the other teams. Anyone can win in this match. The only thing that is certain is the action will be fast, furious, and brutal. The final two teams left will advance to the final match later in the night.
2) MotR IV SEMIFINAL #1 "Kichigaijimita" Kuroi Tenshi vs. The Wasp - Tenshi advanced after outlasting the dirty tactics and overall insanity of his first round matchup. The Wasp advanced by the skin of his teeth over
Mitchell Prose and The Warlock. What will each man have left in his tank for this one? And can The Wasp keep his three year dream of winning a Master of the Ring title alive?
3) MotR IV SEMIFINAL #2 "Cream of the Crop" Mike Waldrop vs. Elijah Flynt - Two young generation wrestlers square off in what is being dubbed the "Cinderella bracket". Waldrop outlasted three of the top wrestlers in the world to get here, and Flynt outlasted three legends himself. One of these men will keep the fairy tale going... which one will it be?
4) MotR IV SEMIFINAL #3 Nytro vs. Victor V. Hill - After two BRUTAL first round matchups, these two men would normally be counted out of the rest of the festivities. But they've proven they're tough before. Nytro has a huge size advantage. Can Hill overcome that to advance? Or will Nytro keep terrorizing the MotR field?
5) THE ROAD AHEAD FINAL "Top Gun" Ben Tuckwell vs. Max Pain vs. Ketiak vs. "High Stakes Soldier" Jake Cannon - In a last minute addition to the Master of the Ring card, these four men will compete in the official final matchup of the tournament they've worked for months on. One of these young wrestlers will make their name known permanently here at Master of the Ring IV?
6) RETIREMENT MATCH Spikyjim vs. Loaded - These two men have been through the ringer in the IWC, and have settled on Master of the Ring IV as a place to end their feud entirely. If you want to see bad blood, if you want to see brutality... just watch this match. It's two guys proving themselves at its finest.
7) RETIREMENT MATCH Valtharius the Mad vs. Nick Demola - This is the big sendoff for the great Valtharius the Mad. And against his own best wishes, Nick Demola has decided to go ahead and wrestle on the card against one of his archrivals. The last matchup between these men ended in a controversial win for Demola a little less than a year ago. Now the feud is rekindled in Valtharius's final match. Can he go out with a win?
8) TEXAS DEATH MATCH "Cowboy" Ken Curtis vs. "Too Cool" Chris Hopper - After interrupting Curtis' award ceremony on Night One and having some derogatory things to say about the Cowboy, Hopper now faces a brutal Texas
Death Match against a pissed off legend. Can he establish his name at THE top of the wrestling ladder with a win here? Or will Curtis beat some sense into him?
9) MotR IV TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP MATCH - TRIPLE JEOPARDY MATCH Final Two Teams from Eight Team Elimination Match earler - The two teams who survive the eight team elimination tag match will have to battle in a match formerly dubbed a "Demola Rules Match". There will be no tagging in or out, all men (or women) in the ring at the same time. There are three ways to win: Knockout, Submission, or by tying your opponent up in the leather strap hanging 15 feet above the ring and dragging them to all four corners. To get to the strap you must use a ladder. Only tables, ladders, and chairs are allowed. This is the matchup that the WWF stole a variation of for their tag teams... but nobody does it like MotR.
10) FOUR ON FOUR ELIMINATION TEAM SUPERBOUT MATCH Team N.E.W. ("Iron Man" Eddie Jacobs, "Cold Blooded" Chris Courtade, Andrew
"Flash" Tucker, "Money Driven" Mike Sebastian) vs. The Masters of the Ring
("Top Dog" Rick Styles, Doc Holliday, "Crimson" Joe Reed, "The Last American
Badass" Alex Martinez)
- The first ever Team SuperBout Title will be determined here. The Sun has nothing on the heat in this match. The four men who have been announced on one side comprise four of the top names in the history of wrestling. Bar none. The four men on the other side of the ring are just as respected and are also four of the top names in the history of wrestling. To call this a barnburner would be an underestimation. This is gonna burn the whole fucking farm down.
11) MASTER OF THE RING IV FINAL - MASTER'S CELL MATCH The three winners of the Semi-Finals - The match that is the signature match of Master of the Ring. A cage completely encloses the ring and ringside area. There are four tables set up, one on each side of the outside of the ring. There are two ladders set up in opposite corners. No weapons allowed. Elimination style. In the end there will be only one.
