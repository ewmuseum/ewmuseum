Final Card Lineup

May 10, 2000, 12:00:00 AM

Here is the final lineup for the four-day pay per view... DWF/MotR War of Attrition - Master of the Ring III. The first day is a purely DWF show, with the exception of the first match, which will determine who runs the entire pay per view. Day 1 - DWF War of Attrition - If This Ain't Country MCI Center - Washington DC 1) Nick Demola vs. Big Dick McGee (for the final rights to the DWF name and Commissionership over the ppv) TV Title First Round Matches (Robert Mula and Matt Mitchell are in the second round already): 2) “Playboy” Aidan Madison vs. Brian "Thunder" Storm vs. Cyric Logan 3) Jim "THE CRUSHER" Johnson vs. “Hollywood Starfucker” Levi Ash vs. Dillon De Stride 4) El Sangre del Noche vs. Mark Marleau vs. Project X 5) Spikyjim vs. Wes "The Eliminator" Coleman vs. Sheik Huseni Khartoum DWF/MotR Tag Title Match - 4) Anti-Social Sublife vs. The Kings of Hardcore Loser Leaves Wrestling Forever - 5) The Axmaker and Desolation Row vs. Damian Orenthal Anderson and The Eclipse (Master's Cell Match) The second day is reserved for purely the Master of the Ring Tournament. It goes by "whoever pins first, wins" rules. Day 2 - Master of the Ring III First Union Center, Philly 1-6) Six threeway first round Master of the Ring III matches 1) Wasp (unknown) vs. Boltz (SSCW) vs. "Roman" Thomas Lawrence (AWMC) 2) Mad Goat (UEW) vs. Sabbath (unknown) vs. "Candyman" Damian Rose (UEW) 3) "The Revolution" Randall Crane (unknown) vs. Vandal Gomez (EMWC) vs. Ace of Hearts (UEW) 4) Simon Bach (EMWC) vs. Knick Man (WWO) vs. Hades (IWF/WOW) 5) Andrew "Flash" Tucker (UWF) vs. F.U. Walters (FA) vs. Tracker (UEW) 6) "Top Dog" Rick Styles (UWF) vs. "Extreme Goddess" Kieran Erotikka (BLAZE) vs. "Nightshade" Jeremy Locke (DWF) The third day is when it all "comes together". Semifinals of the TV Tournament, Semis of the MotR III Tournament. And two HUGE matches to settle some feuds. Day 3 - Come Together FleetCenter Boston 1-2) Semifinal TV Tourney Matches: Robert Mula vs. Winner 1 vs. Winner 3, "The King of Hardcore" Frank Anthony vs. Winner 2 vs. Winner 4 3-5) Three second round MotR III matches 6) Levi Ash vs. Jeremy Locke (no matter WHAT Ash does in the TV Title Tourney) 7) Morgan De Sade vs. Vincent Lockheart vs. ??? (and you know who you are...) The fourth day is the final. The last "battles" if you will. Day 4 - The Epic Clash MSG NY, NY 1) Television Title Final 2) MotR III threeway Final - Master's Cell Match 3) MotR II Final - Master's Cell Match - Ricky Deathwish vs. Menelaus vs. Bad Eye McBaine 4) Demola Rules - Jason Keening vs. Alex Martinez 5) Ladder Match - Setzer Van Strife vs. James Pillow 5) DWF Atlantic Storm Title Match - 30 Minute Ironman Match - Bob Muretic vs. Mike Waldrop vs. Darryn LaSalle 6) DWF Southern Title Match - Elimination Style - Dave Pietka vs. Tommy Grayson vs. Gabriel Demorte vs. Chris Courtade 7) DWF World Title/MotR Ultimate Master Match - MotR III winner vs. MotR II winner vs. Southern Champ vs. Atlantic Storm Champ vs. Television Champ Lineup for rp shows DWF/MotR Talk is Cheap I - Friday, May 26 DWF/MotR Talk is Cheap II - Friday, June 2 DWF/MotR Talk is Cheap III - Friday, June 9 DWF/MotR Talk is Cheap IV - Friday, June 16 Three weeks to write... with a preview show in the middle (Fri. June 30) to keep things hot, of course... and then... WAR OF ATTRITION - From Mon. July 10 - Thu. July 13! Thanks to all for applying!
