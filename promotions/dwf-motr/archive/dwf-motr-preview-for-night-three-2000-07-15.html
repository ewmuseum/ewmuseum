Preview for Night Three

Jul 15, 2000, 12:00:00 AM

(ooc: Night Three will be tomorrow night. Storyline-wise it's going to be because a trip from New Orleans to Atlanta via trucks takes a few hours, and we're not sure we could get the gear up in time. Real life-wise, it's because I couldn't get the day off today and will be spending tonight/tomorrow night finishing up the card)
War of Attrition - Master of the Ring III has been a card to remember so far... From the controversies surrounding the absolute indecent acts of
Rick Styles in the first round, and the apparent fix of the tournament in his favor.
To The Wasp making his way through the tournament as the overwhelming fan's choice. To Simon Bach showing why he belongs with the big boys. To the mystery of The Unknown. Who is this man? And where the
Hell is he going to come from?
At Night Three all questions will be answered... and some surprises lie in wait. - Can Robert Mula stop Johnny Axis from reaching the Ultimate Master's Match at the end of the night? And therefore stop the unleashing of Axis' "Unknown"?
- Can Mike Waldrop, after months of feuding with Bob Muretic, finally take home the Atlantic Storm Title? Or will another young star on the rise, Darryn LaSalle, spoil the party?
- With Nick Demola pulling out of the Southern Title Match, can Chris Courtade possibly beat two members of the SAME STABLE to win the DWF Southern Title? Or will Dave Pietka, the champion, prove to be too much for the young man yet again?
- Can the rookie Menelaus, after making such a splash in MotR I, finally win a tournament (namely MotR II) and defeat the Greatest Indy Wrestler Ever in Ricky Deathwish and one of the most feared men in the history of wrestling, Bad Eye McBaine?
- Who will come out on top? The surprising Wasp, who has really dominated his third of the bracket? Simon Bach, who has done every dirty tactic imaginable to advance, and got his ass handed to him by Ace of Hearts after their second round match? Or Rick
Styles, the chosen champion. The one who has been handed the title even before it is decided?
- How about James Pillow and Setzer Van Strife? At Master of the Ring III they will be fighting for the MotR II SuperBout
Title, which will be hanging high above... with only a ladder available to get it. Which of these two legends will come out on top?
- And then there's Jason Keening and Alex Martinez... in a Demola Rules Match. Keening is Demola's long time enemy, and
Martinez made it clear on Night One that he wants NO PART of
Nick Demola's help in this match. With Demola as the ref, what fate lies waiting for them?
- And then... the Ultimate Master's Match. Will the Unknown be revealed? Will someone nobody expects jump out and win it? Will Rick Styles be handed THIS title as well... that is, if he makes it past The Wasp and Simon Bach? The winners of the
Television Title, Atlantic Storm Title, Southern Title, MotR II, and MotR III Matches will battle to vie for the overall winner... the man who will be named the Ultimate Master and the last DWF World Champion.
- What other surprises wait? I GUAR-AN-TEE that this card will be talked about endlessly, as it already is. But Night Three will put everything together in one nice, neat package.
