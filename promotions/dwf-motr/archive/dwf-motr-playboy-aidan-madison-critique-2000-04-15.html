"Playboy" Aidan Madison (Critique)

Apr 15, 2000, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
RSPWF, It has been a while since I've posted a flash on RSPWF so I'd be thankful if somebody could just comment on this. Tell me what is good about it, tell me what is sucky about it. It doesn't matter to me. Any help would be cool. Thanks!
By the way, this flash is for Nick Demola's DWF. -------------------------------------------------------------------- [The scene opens up to show a beautiful shot of a river. As the camera zooms out, you can see that a light snow is falling and that you're in a park. You can feel the brisk wind breaking through your hair and the sight of snow in the middle of Spring makes you wonder where you are at.]
[The camera turns left about 90 degrees to show a man sitting on a park bench. He is all bundled up. He has a big winter coat on, a scarf, gloves, and boots. Everything you need to endure weather like this. The camera man begins to walk up to the man and you can see that it is none other than
DWF's "Playboy" Aidan Madison. His blue eyes seem to sparkle as the snow goes by them and they are fixed on a boat going down the river.]
[A puff of wind comes blowing through the area making Aidan Madison's continually growing blonde hair blow to it's side. Aidan Madison wipes off his cheek what seems to be a tear. He looks into the camera with sad eyes that seem to burn through you with depression. He then begins to speak.]
[AM]:"From day one in the DWF, I knew I was going to have a tough time. I knew I was in a federation that had some of the best wrestlers around. But,
I didn't let that fact get me down. My close friends, Nick Demola himself, told me that I'd do fine. That I'd be a success story in the DWF. But, everything they said I'd do, I've done the total opposite."
[Aidan Madison REALLY is crying now. A few tears roll down his cheek and fall off to the ground. The snow begins to fall harder and at a more steady pace. The ground around Aidan begins to get covered. Aidan looks up at the sky and looks at the peaceful snow falling down. He cracks a tiny smile. He begins to talk again as he continues to look up at the sky.]
[AM]:"I can remember when I was just a kid when I use to go outside and play basketball in my driveway. I would pretend that I was in the World
Championship. No time left. Score tied. I was at the foul line and if I made the shot, we won the World Championship. But, if I missed, we would go into
Overtime and eventually loose.]
[Aidan moves his hands and arms in the motion of shooting a basketball.] [AM]:"I released the ball and usually missed ten times in a row, but I would yell 'JUST PRACTICE!' I would step up the line for the fifteeth time or so and finally nail one. I jumped and down, I screamed. I acted like I was running around with a big trophy. Thank God for those second chances in your imagination."
[Aidan Madison makes a fist and hits the bench he is sitting on.] [AM]:"It is just too bad that in the world of wrestling we don't get second chances. And, if we do, I must have used mine already because I'm getting NO
WHERE in the DWF."
[Aidan Madison closes his eyes and takes a deep breath, calming himself down. He re-opens his eyes, sticks his tounge out and trys to catch snow on it. After a few moments, he begins to speak again.]
[AM]:"When I first came into the world of e-wrestling, I was quickly noted as one of the best rookies to ever hit the scene. I guess it all got to my head because all I've done since that time is fall, fall, and fall some more. But, an ending is just the beginning of something new. Right now my record in the DWF is somewhere around 'no wins' and 'five losses.' But, I'm forgetting about that. I'm wiping the slate clean."
[Aidan Madison stands up off the park bench, wipes the snow off him, and begins to walk down the street as he continues to talk.]
[AM]:"Yeah, Yeah. I know I do have a match this week. But, truthfully, what is the point? I don't really care who it is against. I'm not even going to mention his name. All the people who are listening to me right now have to know is that
I'm sick of losing. And I'm sure that you are sick of SEEING me lose. So, tonight, I'm going to try my hardest to win this match. I know there is a chance to lose and I'm keeping that in the back of my mind."
[Aidan Madison stops walking, turns around, looks into the camera and speaks with a firm voice.]
[AM]:"And if another lose DOES happen, there is always next week." [Aidan Madison smirks and walks away as the camera fades to black.] [name withheld] Handler of: "Playboy" Aidan Madison [DWF] #12 On RSPWF's Mid-Year Best Rookies
