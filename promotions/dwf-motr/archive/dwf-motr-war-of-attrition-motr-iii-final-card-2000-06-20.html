War of Attrition — Master of the Ring III Final Card

Jun 20, 2000, 12:00:00 AM

The DownSouth Wrestling Federation and Master of the Ring Inc. ( http://members.xoom.com/DWFrasslin ) present... WAR OF ATTRITION - MASTER OF THE RING III DAY 1 – For Whom The Bell Tolls From The Astrodome in Houston, Texas Thursday, July 13, 2000 Announcers – Stuart Jennings, Matt Mitchell, Cris Brothers
Six threeway first round Master of the Ring III First To Pin
Wins matches.
1) Wasp (unknown) vs. Boltz (SSCW) vs. "Roman" Thomas Lawrence (AWMC) 2) Mad Goat (UEW) vs. Sabbath (unknown) vs. "Candyman" Damian Rose (UEW) 3) "The Revolution" Randall Crane (A1 Promotions) vs. Vandal Gomez (EMWC) vs. Ace of Hearts (UEW) 4) Simon Bach (EMWC) vs. Knick Man (WWO) vs. Hades (IWF/WOW) 5) Andrew "Flash" Tucker (UWF) vs. F.U. Walters (FA) vs. Tracker (UEW) 6) "Top Dog" Rick Styles (UWF) vs. "Extreme Goddess" Kieran Erotikka (BLAZE) vs. "Nightshade" Jeremy Locke (DWF) The backstories are many... can Wasp in his last event possibly win it all? Can Goat and Candyman, despite their apparent admiration, get it on in the ring? Can the upstart Ace beat
Vandal Gomez, from the biggest federation on the planet, EMWC?
With the past wars between the men involved, can anyone escape
Bach/Knick/Hades alive? Will Tucker get his fight with Styles?
Or can Walters, Tracker, Locke, and Erotikka play spoilers? And what else can happen in this tournament?
DWF/MotR Tag Title Elimination Match (special refs Junya Kontani and El Sangre del Noche) 7) Desolation Row vs. The Eclipse vs. Thomas Hunter & Jason Allen vs. The Kings of Hardcore This is the last match for all eight of these competitors. Desolation Row and The Eclipse have feuded for four years, and it comes down to this match. Thomas Hunter, son of The Row's
Black Demon, wants final revenge for his awful childhood. Jason
Allen wants to make The Row rue the day they kicked him out of
Death's Minion. And The Kings of Hardcore just want to kill someone.
DAY 2 - Survival From The Superdome in New Orleans, Louisiana Friday, July 14, 2000 Announcers – Stuart Jennings, The Axmaker, Damien Orenthal
Anderson Two TV Title First Round Matches 1) Jim "THE CRUSHER" Johnson vs. Johnny Axis 2) Mark Marleau vs. Havoc Hate (DWF Legends Match with Nytro as Special Guest Referee) The late entry Axis has a chance to unify the ACWA and DWF
Television Titles. But will Jim Johnson "crush" him before he can? And the final clash between two true indy legends happens here. Will Mark Marleau cap off his career with a strong showing here? Or is it Havoc Hate's time to defend his win from
MotR I?
Three Second Round MotR III Matches 3) Winner of MotR Match 1 vs. Winner of MotR Match 2 4) Winner of MotR Match 3 vs. Winner of MotR Match 4 5) Winner of MotR Match 5 vs. Winner of MotR Match 6 What matchups will we see here? Who will be the cinderellas and who will be the big names left out of the second round?
Semifinal TV Tourney Matches: 6) Frank Anthony vs. Winner TV Match 1 7) Robert Mula vs. Winner TV Match 2 The final match of Frank Anthony? Or just a stepping stone to another piece of gold. And the cookie cutting Robert Mula might have a walkover if the survivor of Marleau/Hate is as torn up as expected.
DAY 3 – The Ultimate From The Georgia Dome in Atlanta, Georgia Saturday, July 15, 2000 Announcers – Stuart Jennings, Big Dick McGee, Juan Vasquez 1) One-on-One Television Final Who makes it past all the obstacles and places in this match, with the right to possibly shock the world and go on to the
Ultimate Master's Title later in the night?
2) DWF Atlantic Storm Title Match - Elimination Lumberjack Match -"Wildcard" Bob Muretic vs. "Cream of the Crop" Mike Waldrop vs. "Cajun Sensation" Darryn LaSalle This feud has been MONTHS on the front boiler. Waldrop and
Muretic damn near killed themselves at DWF Total Annihilation, and with LaSalle's help Waldrop got a bit of revenge for his loss. But now all the cards are on the table and only one man can walk out the last DWF Atlantic Storm Champion.
3) MotR II Final - Master's Cell Match - Ricky Deathwish vs. Menelaus vs. Bad Eye McBaine It has been a year in the waiting, and now it's time. Can
Deathwish, the man revered as many as the greatest ever, win the tournament he was expected to take? Could last year's rookie sensation Menelaus end his career with the gold? Or will Bad
Eye McBaine just stomp right through everyone?
4) MotR III Final - Master's Cell Match – Winners of Semifinal Matches from day before After two previous matches in the two previous days, who's going to have the energy to take this win?
5) DWF Southern Title Match - "Heavy Mental" Dave Pietka vs. "Moral Degeneracy" Tommy Grayson vs. Nick Demola vs. Chris Courtade With the DWF President installing himself into this match, is there some dirty trickery coming? Can Pietka and Grayson keep the title in the Wicked Existenz stable, or will Courtade finally take it home?
6) SuperBout I - Ladder Match - "Simply The Best" James Pillow vs. "2 Smooth" Setzer Van Strife Two of the greatest INWI champions of all time battle for the
SuperBout Championship... hanging high above in the rafters.
Who will prove he is the best? 7) SuperBout II - Demola Rules Match - "Screaming Drillbit" Jason Keening vs. Alex Martinez (guest ref Nick Demola) Demola has made no bones that he wants Keening to lose and be destroyed. But when asked about possibly recieving help from
Nick, Martinez said, "DeMola can have his grudge on his own time, not on mine. He <bleeps> with me, and he gets <bleep>ed up, got that?" With those strong words, how is this match going to end up playing out?
8) Ultimate Master/DWF World Title Match - MotR III winner vs. MotR II winner vs. Southern Champ vs. Atlantic Storm Champ vs. Television Champ - STAGGERED ENTRY, NO DQ. TV champ and Atlantic
Storm champ start. Two minutes later the MotR II winner enters.
Then the MotR III winner. Then the Southern Champion. Only after everyone is in the ring can the eliminations begin.
After all the battles is the ultimate battle. The possible combinations in this match are infinite. Will a rookie use this as his launchpad to superstardom? Will an old vet take it home as his final trophy? Or will this title end up in the hands of someone already at the top of his game, as proof of his greatness? If Nick Demola has his way Rick Styles will win it... but what effect will this "mystery person" who will be installed into this match have on the plans of mice and men...?
