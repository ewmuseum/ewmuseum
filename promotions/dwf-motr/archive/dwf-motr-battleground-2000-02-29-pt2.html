BattleGround for 2/29 (Part 2 of 4)

Feb 29, 2000, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
SJ: And now, ladies and gentlemen, we’re FINALLY ready to kick off with the first of three great matches tonight! The ladder is being set up and Mitchell Harris Butler is in the ring, so let’s go to him now!
[DING! DING! DING!] [The camera shows a slender man in a tuxedo standing in front of a huge unfolded ladder in the middle of the ring, with a scroll hanging from the rafters right above the peak of that ladder.]
MHB: Ladies and gentlemen… this match is the first of two Ladder Match
Battle Royals set for tonight! The winner will be the man who climbs the ladder and retains possession of the scroll, which is an invitation to take part in the semi-finals of the Southern Title Tournament next week! Your referee for this match is Brian Hunter!
[The crowd pops as the former Black Demon does a martial arts display in the middle of the ring.]
MHB: Introducing first… {One by Metallica starts blaring out of the p.a. system. The lights dim and blue stobe lights start flashing the entrance way. Small smoke fills near the floor. Then from out behind the curtian struts the "Million Dollar Icon" Ash Bombay III. Bombay is wearing black boots, black parachute pants, white sash. He is wearing a white
Superman shirt, and is sporting a brass walking cane. He points to "his adoring public" as they boo him heavly. Bombay makes a few "thank you, thanks you" gestures to the crowd as he continues struting to the ring.
Once in the ring he blows a kiss to the crowd and bows for everyone}
MHB: In the ring, hailing from Oklahoma City, Oklahoma, and standing at 5 ft 11 inches, weighing in at 225 pounds, he is, "Mr. Fame", the "Million Dollar Icon', L.A. BOMBAY!!!!
{Crowd boos more as Bombay blows more kisses to the crowd as Bombay's entorage of Christian Blytch and The Fence joins Bombay in the ring.}
MHB: And his opponent… [The "Adults Only" mix of White Zombie's "Super-Charger Heaven" opens up on the amplifiers as Robert Mula strolls out onto the runway at the
DWF arena. The former "White Knight" is dressed in his usual attire:
dark blue biker shorts, black boots and fingerless wrestling gloves, and his trademark forest green t-shirt with "TIME TO DIE" scrawled in with black lettering.]
MHB: And now, weighing in at three-hundred pounds, out of Norfolk, Virginia- here is, Robert Mula! [Mula stops and scans the crowd for a moment, as the audience emits a variety of sounds. He then continues to walk to the ring and slides in under the ropes. He walks to his chosen corner of the ring to take his shirt off, tosses the shirt aside, and sets down on one knee in that corner, staring intently to where his opponent is at.]
MHB: And their opponent… ["Break Stuff" by Limp Bizkit plays. A lone spotlight hits the entryway, as we see a track-star like teenager in black regailia, a girl with an impressive figure, and a rather large man. The shorter man wearing a black T-shirt with the words "I do what the voices tell me to do" imprinted on the front, black Kick-boxing pants with "HEAVY MENTAL"
written on the back, a Black Overcoat that stops at his knee-caps, and black boots. She's wearing a leopard spotted long-sleeved, transparent,
Blouse-like top with a tight-fitting black sports bra underneith,
Leopard spotted transparent tights with low-cut black Rio-style Bikini bottoms, black boots, and Custom-Made Oakleys with Holographic Lenses each having a "D" in them. The large man has a "Security, PUNK!" T- shirt on and jeans, along with Ray-Bans similar to the first guy's.]
MHB: Accompanied to the ring by "Double D" Devin DeMasters and "Marz" Candress. From Coral Springs, Florida... Weighing in at 170 pounds. The
Self-Proclaimed Thorazine Posterchild... "HEAVY MENTAL" DAVE PIETKA!
[The three make their way down, but Pietka rushes ahead. He starts thrashing to the music, laughing hysterically... and then stopping suddenly. His face completely blank and emotionless as he steps towards the ring. DeMasters flirts with a few crowd members, before "Marz" gets in the way of their groping hands. Pietka suddenly gets a smile on his face, and goes "Heavy Mental" on a fan, and the fan seems to join in on the fun. Pietka gets into the ring, and becomes all serious like.
DeMasters enters the ring, and "Marz" rolls in. Pietka hands his overcoat to Marz, then knocks fists with the big guy. He kisses
DeMasters before she leaves, and Pietka just waits... looking the same as always... Fearless... Frantic... and F[bleep]ING NUTS!]
MHB: And participant number four… [The lights in the arena dim a little, as blue strobe lights begin to go off through out the place. The fans stand up in a frenzy, getting ready for the next entrance. Slightly Imperfect's "Study Humans" begins to blast through out the arena. The fans go crazy for they know who is coming out.]
[MHB]: "Now coming to the ring, hailing from Toronto, Canada, weighing in at 130 pounds and standing 5'8'', he is the only "Playboy" of wrestling. . . HE IS. .
_"PLAYBOY" AIDAN MADISON!! _ [Aidan Madison waste no time from running behind the curtains and running to the ring and getting ready.]
L.A. BOMBAY VS. ROBERT MULA VS. DAVE PIETKA VS. AIDAN MADISON - DWF Southern Title Quarterfinal Four Way Ladder Match Battle Royal [DING! DING! DING!] SJ: Mula is across the ring from Pietka and Bombay and Madison are in opposite corners… JV: What is that IDIOT Mula doing??? [Mula puts his head down and charges right into Dave Pietka with a leaping lariat that sends Pietka backwards into the corner. Mula then follows up with a series of headbutts on the staggered Pietka!
Meanwhile, Bombay charges at Madison for a clothesline, but Madison leapfrogs him and hits him with a spinning heel kick from behind that sends Bombay’s face into the turnbuckle directly to the right of Mula and Pietka.]
SJ: Quick start to this match and to the action in the DWF! JV: My boys Pietka and Bombay are being taken down already? It’s a fix! SJ: Already you’re going with the “fix” talk? JV: Yeah! Isn’t it obvious? [The ladder is situated next to the ropes at the top part of the ring. Mula attempts to whip Pietka into the opposite corner, but Pietka reverses the whip and turns Mula to the left so that he crashes into the corner opposite where L.A. Bombay and Aidan Madison are fighting.
Bombay is getting leveled with a 10 count of mounted punches from Madison.] SJ: The little men now have control… JV: Little men? SJ: Well yeah. Pietka and Madison are actual lightheavyweights, which is rare in the day and age of 237 pound weight class limits.
JV: But I still wouldn’t call a guy named “Heavy Mental” a little man if I were you. SJ: You might actually have a point, Juan. [Madison gets off of Bombay as Pietka whistles out loud. Pietka then whips Mula towards Bombay and Madison, and they team up to double clothesline the big man and send him crashing to the mat!]
SJ: A double team on Mula? [Pietka then runs over and lays a few kicks in on Robert Mula, and
Bombay and Madison join him as they kick him and roll him right out of the ring!]
SJ: What’s up with that? What is this, “Triple Team Robert Mula Day”? JV: He’s a moron. He deserves a beating. SJ: If every moron out there got beat up, you’d have been paralyzed long ago. [Mula lays on the ground at ringside and hits the floor in anger and disgust. Meanwhile, in the ring Pietka comes up behind Bombay, slides around him, and hits him with a nasty Backspin DDT! All the while,
Aidan Madison measures up the frustrated Robert Mula.]
SJ: The little man is going to fly! JV: He’s insane! These damn luchadores are going to kill somebody with these stupid high flying moves one of these days!
[As the fans chant “MA-DI-SON! MA-DI-SON!” Mula gets to his feet. Madison springs off of the ropes and… CRASH!!!!!! … MULA CATCHES HIM! With an amazing amount of power, Robert Mula catches Aidan Madison and drives his head into the ringpost!]
SJ: My Lord! What a show of power by Robert Mula! JV: Maybe he killed that little kid! We can only hope… SJ: That’s sick! JV: It was kinda funny though. [Mula immediately grabs Madison and tosses him into the ring. Pietka is in the ring delivering an Asai Moonsault onto a helpless L.A. Bombay in the meantime.]
SJ: Bombay really isn’t showing too well here… JV: It looks like he didn’t prepare for this match well enough. I wish he’d show a little better, but if you don’t prepare against talent like this…
SJ: … you get your ass whipped. JV: Pretty much. [Mula throws Madison into a corner and attempts a kick, but Madison springs up to the top rope and flips in midair with a moonsault bodyblock! Both men hit the mat hard… and meanwhile Dave Pietka goes to the outside and under the ring.]
SJ: What is he doing??? Under the ring??? JV: Do you REALLY want to know what he’s doing? SJ: Whatever it is, I’m sure this sick man will make it dastardly! [As Madison gives Mula a couple of stiff knife-edge chops in the corner, L.A. Bombay begins to slowly come to a knee. And Dave Pietka comes out from under the ring, sliding a table out to the ringside area and throwing a metal bucket, a fire extinguisher, and the top half of the metal ringsteps into the ring before stepping in himself!]
SJ: Oh no! There’s going to be some real mayhem out there now! JV: This is where Pietka is at home! In an absolute no-holds-barred brawl! He’s going to turn this into his living room!
[L.A. Bombay attempts to ambush Pietka, but Pietka sidesteps him and lets him bounce off the ropes. Bombay comes off the ropes and Pietka picks up the fire extinguisher… then NAILS Bombay with the fire extinguisher right in the face!]
[CROWD: WE WANT MUR-DER! <clap, clap, clapclapclap> WE WANT MUR-
DER! <clap, clap, calpclapclap>]
SJ: It takes a special breed to be a DWF fan, that’s for certain. JV: A special breed of sick puppy! These people want to see poor, innocent L.A. Bombay killed!
SJ: Juan, I don’t think they care who dies… [Madison leaps up to the top rope… and comes down on Robert Mula with a bulldog headlock onto the metal ringsteps in the ring! Meanwhile,
Pietka crawls outside and sets the table up…]
[CROWD: TA-BLE! TA-BLE!] SJ: Mula is down and opened up over his right eye and now Dave Pietka is going to sacrifice someone to the Table Gods!
JV: Gotta love Stu’s catchphrases… the only announcer with more signature sayings than The Rock.
[L.A. Bombay staggers to his feet in the ring next to the ropes where
Dave Pietka now stands on the ring apron. Aidan Madison, meanwhile, attempts to put the ladder in the middle of the ring and climb up, but
Robert Mula sneaks over and knocks the ladder over, crotching Madison on the top rope.]
SJ: A bit too early to go for the win here… JV: How the Hell is someone going to win this match? You climb up the ladder and three guys are going to knock you off!
SJ: The winner will be the one who can fight the hardest… JV: … and get the luckiest. [Pietka grabs Bombay’s head from behind and runs with him… then slams his face into the turnbuckle, sending Bombay falling backwards and to the ground. Pietka then signals for his big bodyguard Marz Candress to toss the set-up table into the ring, which he does with minimal effort over the top rope. Pietka springs into the ring and sets the table in the top right hand corner of the ring. Mula then works on setting the ladder up in the middle of the ring, dazed the whole time.]
SJ: Pietka is so focused on dishing out punishment that he may end up losing the match if he’s not careful!
JV: Shut up and watch the master at work! Poor, poor L.A. Bombay’s about to get his career ended here!
[Pietka hits Bombay with a clothesline that lays him on the table and climbs to the top rope. He raises his arms in the air, flies off, and…
CRASH!!!!!! … DRIVES L.A. Bombay through the table with a perfect Frog Splash!!!] SJ: MY GOD! MY GOD! HE MUST BE DEAD! JV: He’s not DEAD… [Bombay lays motionless in the scraps of table as Aidan Madison holds his sore crotch on the ring apron and Robert Mula slowly staggers up the ladder…]
JV: Then again, maybe he is… [Dave Pietka sees Mula climbing the ladder and immediately gets under it and starts shaking the thing!]
SJ: Pietka’s shaking the ladder! Mula’s losing his balance! JV: He’s standing right over L.A. Bombay! If he falls off on Bombay he’ll kill him! [Pietka continues shaking the ladder and Mula looks down to Bombay…] SJ: You don’t think… JV: He can’t… he doesn’t have the balance. He just CAN’T! [Mula takes one look down and leaps off! He flips in the air and… CRASH!!!! … lands right on top of L.A. Bombay with a Somersault Senton off of the ladder!!!] SJ: INCREDIBLE! A man that size isn’t supposed to be able to have the balance, presence, or athleticism to do that, but Robert Mula has all three!
JV: Forget that idiot Mula! What about poor Bombay? SJ: He’s dead. He’s alive. Who really cares? [Mula lays on the ground and is still dazed. Bombay is just flat out unconscious. Madison is recovering from his earlier crotching… and now
Dave Pietka is climbing the ladder to attain the scroll!]
SJ: THAT was his plan! He shook the ladder so it’d stay upright for when he wanted to climb!
JV: Brilliant! Dave Pietka is incredible and he’s going to take this match hands-down! [Pietka climbs the ladder. He gets up to the second-to-last rung and is about to grab the scroll… but Aidan Madison recovers! He bounces off the ropes and flings himself against the ladder and tips it over, sending Pietka crashing to the ground!]
SJ: That ends that. Aidan Madison with a desperation move and we’re at square one again! JV: Square one… except that everyone’s probably got permanent injuries, unlike at the start of the match.
[The four men crawl around the ring, trying to get their winds back. The fans in the arena all turn to the entryway…] SJ: What’s going on over there? [The fans boo as a rather pale man, with long dark brown hair tied back in a pony tail comes sprinting down to the ring!]
SJ: It’s GABRIEL DEMORTE! What the Hell is he doing here?!?!? JV: He’s here to ruin the show! He’s sick of these pretenders to his throne! [Demorte leaps into the ring and sets the ladder up quickly, then sprints up the ladder while the four men on the ground get their winds, and grabs the scroll!!!]
SJ: GABRIEL DEMORTE HAS THE SCROLL?!?!?! JV: Does that mean he wins? SJ: Of course not! I don’t think… [Demorte climbs down fast and stands on the ring apron. He pulls a lighter out from under his tights and faces the fans as he holds the lighter up to the scroll!]
SJ: HE’S GOING TO BURN IT! JV: He’s going to ruin the show! I love this guy! I can’t believe it! [Demorte cackles as he holds the lighter up… but from behind Dave Pietka starts to get to his feet.] SJ: Pietka’s up! Pietka’s up! [The other three men all climb to their feet as well, but Pietka gets to Demorte first! He turns Demorte around before he can burn the scroll and proceeds to punch the “Total Wrestling Machine” in the face repeatedly, knocking him off the apron and to the floor below!]
SJ: He stopped him! Pietka stopped him! JV: So does Pietka win now? What’s going on? [Demorte screams as Pietka jaws at him from the ring. Security comes out and ushers Demorte out as Marz Candress sees to it that Gabriel
Demorte leaves the ringside area. While all this is happening, Robert
Mula hits Aidan Madison with a running lariat that makes the smaller man flip in midair. L.A. Bombay is still barely up with help from the ring ropes.]
SJ: Demorte’s finally gone! But what happens to the match? JV: I don’t think these guys care! They just want to beat each other to a bloody pulp! [A ring attendant picks up the scroll and makes his way into the ring under orders from the referee. He climbs up the ladder as Mula gets hit from behind by Dave Pietka with a dropkick.]
SJ: So the scroll’s going to be put back up and all is good… JV: And nobody will knock that poor kid off the ladder. I don’t know why he looks so frightened up there.
SJ: Wouldn’t you be scared if you were in the ring with these insane men? JV: Not really. No. But I’m 6’8, 300 pounds… so… [Pietka gives Mula a couple of chops to the chest, and Madison hits
Pietka from behind with a clothesline. The ringside attendant ties the scroll to the rope over the top of the ring as L.A. Bombay staggers closer to the ladder…]
SJ: Hey Juan. I don’t think Bombay knows where he is. JV: He’s probably got amnesia. As long as he doesn’t start acting like Kevin Nash, I don’t care.
SJ: But he’s sorta looking at the ring attendant a little strangely… I think he thinks the kid is an opponent!
JV: You don’t think… nah… [As Pietka, Madison, and Mula fight in a corner… L.A. Bombay grabs the kid on the ladder by his underarms!]
SJ: NO!!!! JV: DON’T DO IT BOMBAY!!! [L.A. Bombay lifts the poor kid in the air… and sends him CRASHING to the mat with a vicious Power Bomb!!!]
SJ: There’s a SUSPENSION coming for that one! Or at least a fine! JV: This guy’s gone nuts! I can’t believe this! [Bombay grabs the steel chair in the ring and unfolds it on the ground. He then turns Robert Mula around in an attempt to suplex Mula onto the chair… but Mula blocks it.]
SJ: There goes Bombay’s offense for the evening. [Mula kicks Bombay in the stomach and spins him around. He then kicks him in the back and points at the chair on the ground…]
SJ: You don’t think… JV: Electric Chair Powerbomb time! No!!! [Mula flips Bombay in the air so that Bombay’s face looks down to the mat. He then leaps up and DRIVES Bombay straight into the chair with an inverted sitdown Power Bomb he calls the Electric Chair Powerbomb!]
SJ: That’s it… someone has to get some medical attention for L.A. Bombay. He’s gone. JV: Watch out for Pietka from behind! [As Mula stands up from the move, Pietka bounces off the ropes, leaps, and hits Mula with a modified Asai Moonsault that he calls the Pietka- sault!]
SJ: Here come the big moves! This match is coming to an end! [Aidan Madison begins to move to Dave Pietka, but he is overtaken by a sprinting, returning Gabriel Demorte! Demorte hits Madison with a clothesline, then comes up from behind Pietka and hits him with a series of punches to the back of the head!]
SJ: What is HE doing back here??? JV: I don’t think his stay will be long… look who’s jumping in the ring. SJ: Oh man. Business is about to- JV: Don’t go stealing lines now. [Marz Candress, Pietka’s GIANT bodyguard, steps over the top rope, grabs Demorte by the neck, and throws him over the top rope and to the outside with minimal effort!]
SJ: WHAT POWER! How the HELL can you beat that guy??? JV: You can’t! That’s why everyone’s happy he’s just a bodyguard…
[Candress punches Demorte back to the locker room as Aidan Madison recovers in the ring. He attempts to go as quickly as he can up the ladder and to victory!]
SJ: He’s almost there! What an upset this would be! JV: Can he do it? Can this little man do it? SJ: Little man… you better watch your mouth too, Juan! JV: I’m three times his size. I’ll say what I want. [Madison reaches the top rope… but Mula pushes the ladder over at the last second! Madison flies off the ladder, over the top rope, and
CRASHES to a halt on the guardrail on the outside of the ring!!!]
SJ: MY LORD! HE’S DEAD! HE’S DEAD! Get him some medical attention! JV: Get a hold of your aneurysm, Stu. [Mula sets up the ladder and gets up two steps of the ladder… but Dave
Pietka catches him! Pietka climbs up to the first rung of the ladder and hooks Mula’s head under his arm, and ties his arms up in it as well…]
SJ: NOT Heavy Mentality! NOT off the ladder! [Sure enough, Pietka leaps off the top with Mula and sends him CRASHING into the mat with the Double Arm Leaping DDT that Pietka calls Heavy
Mentality!]
SJ: That’s it! Mula’s unconscious! Madison’s unconscious! Who else can stop Pietka??? [Bombay slowly gets to his feet…] JV: L.A. Bombay! He’s still in this match! SJ: No. No way. He can’t… [L.A. Bombay charges at the unsuspecting Dave Pietka…] SJ: Bombay’s going to knock him out and win this match! Bombay’s going to win it! [Bombay charges and… at the last second Pietka sees him! He backdrops
Bombay over the top rope and to the floor!]
SJ: That’s it! Bombay’s out! Pietka’s climbing! [Pietka gets to the ladder and climbs up slowly… he finally gets to the top… AND GRABS THE SCROLL!!!!]
[DING! DING! DING!] MHB: The winner of this match and advancing to the Semi-Finals… “HEAVY
MENTAL” DAVE PIETKA!!!!!
SJ: Pietka did it! Come Hell, high water, or insane run-ins… JV: He really outlasted everyone there! He deserved that win for certain. SJ: And coming off the heels of that great match we have another one for you! Perhaps not as many shocks will come from this match, but it’ll be interesting to see how Gabriel Demorte does after his appearance on the last match.
JV: Demorte ruined it all! I love this guy! SJ: You would. JV: What’s that supposed to mean? Sent via Deja.com http://www.deja.com/ Before you buy.
