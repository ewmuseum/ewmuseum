Two MOTR IV Call-Outs, December 2000

28–29 December 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
To: Wasp & Dante Culpepper From: The One and Only... Title: "Lurking In The Shadows." ---- [A familiar voice breaks from within the RSPWF shadows.] Voice: Dante Kreig? [A sadistic sounding laugh can be heard coming from the darkest corners of the RSPWF.] Voice: The Dark Wolf?!? [More laughing can be heard as the mans voice booms throughout the newsgroup.] Voice: You've got to be f'n kidding me right?!? [Again more laughing can be heard.] Voice: 'Cause you don't have a snowballs chance in hell of getting past the one and only... [A dark and ominous figure steps from within the shadows of RSWPF. Dressed from head to toe in black, the man glares towards the cameras lens. This man, the one and only... Ace of Hearts.] Ace: I hate to pour some "Raid" on your parade there Wasp... but it seems as though you and this punk Dave Kreig seemed to have overlooked the fact that the MotR has once again signed the one and only to its talent roster! [Smirking, the self proclaimed second coming of the GOD of Wrestling, cocks his thumbs back towards himself. With an arrogent as hell look planted boldly upon his handsome facial features, Ace steps back into the darkness as he has accomplished his goal of making his presence known to the two false prophets.] Ace: Bear this little fact in mind there Wasp... when you go around boldly making predictions that your ass can't Kash! [The laughter returns yet again as the scene slowly begins to fade around you.] ---- See you bitches in the ring... only this time its in my back yard! Calgary, Alberta, Canada! I'm bringing this one home Waspy! So put the women and children to bed, 'cause I'm coming to hunt... ...and I always get my prey! Master of the Ring IV Participant -=*=- Ace of Hearts -=*=- [ENTER STAGE RIGHT!] [The Wasp, with that RIDICULOUS looking mustache (!!!) and tired old leather pants calls to attention a press conference at RSPWF Headquarters, where he is a MAINSTAY... A LEGEND! The conference room is filled to the brim with such celebrities as Brian Blottie, Mr. [name withheld] (All 13 of them), and even President [name withheld]. Jessica Falcon eyes a big set of hairy balls being eaten by [name withheld], nearly distracting the man of the hour from responding to the latest ludicrous, HATE-FILLED comments by the Ace of Hearts and the rest of the contingents of what will no doubt be the most exciting MOTR tournament yet.] -=== SLAM~! ===- [The Wasp pounds his fist on the press conference podium, scaring only Shawn Mihalek, who gets up and races out of the room with his brand new corduroy Jordache jeans soaked in piss. [name withheld] takes this opportunity to flip Shawn the finger with one hand, while holding various Fleer Rugby rookies cards in the other that he has been comparing with Pat. The rest look on in wonder at the size of this man's mustache, extending at LEAST three inches past each side of his face.] The Wasp: It is time I address this community as a whole... [He becomes frustrated at the lack of attention span among the patrons of this crowd. One would think the only person paying attention is [name withheld], until they realize that he is sitting up straight in his chair with one eye closed and one eye opened, sound asleep.] The Wasp: With the assertion that the most elite group of our kind lurk here among us, I have come to the realization that none of you are worthy of my presence. However... (Smiles) I will give each of you -ONE- memory to tell your grandchildren... (Takes in the sight of this most... (ahem) astute crowd) That you saw The Wasp in all his glory, at the most profound moment in his career. I am -ONE- step away from becoming the Master of the Ring, and YOU ALL sit by and idly watch me be disrespected. Not one of you had the BALLS... [Looks at Brian Blottie with a twisted frown on his face, as Brian springs out of his chair at the mention of balls...] The Wasp: ... (Looking away, same look on his face) to do the -RIGHT- thing and explain to Ace of Hearts and even each other, how you are (pounds his fist) -LOOKING- at the (pound) LICENTIOUS (pound) TRENDSETTER of pain and -SUFFERING-! That this coming year is the year The Wasp Grand -SLAMS- the world of Professional Wrestling. I -AM- already the Master of any ring. I -AM- the Pro Bowl player that will sweep Wrestlebowl. I AM THE PROPRIETOR OF JUSTICE, THE AMERICAN WAY AND HUMANITARIANISM~! [He smiles, nodding vigorously in the general direction of the crowd expecting them to agree, but they are paying more attention to the pizza man, who is on his way to [name withheld]'s office.] The Wasp: (Realizing no one cares) This is unbelievable. It is obvious the level of intelligence I am dealing with here, so I will reserve questions at this time. But until I address this (waves his hand around, to be sure -EVERYONE- knows he is addressing them) plethora of (gritting his teeth) -AMOEBA- again, I want to convey one last message. If you are -IN- MOTR IV, I offer no apologies for the level of violence and pain I am going to inflict on you... And if you are not, (Pokes his finger roughly into his chest) -= I =- am the -REASON- you didn't make the cut. (He becomes animated, pointing to himself) -= I =- am the measuring stick that you didn't measure up to. (Scoops something off the podium, and smirks) I -AM- the reason... =-- [ He turns his hand, allowing sand to sift through his palm to the floor. ] --= The Wasp: Your opportunity slipped -right- through your grasp... (Evil, EVIL grin) Your mind is in the hunt... But your heart is not with your prey! [The Wasp turns and leaves, taking cat calls from various alumni. The Press Conference ends as [name withheld] springs from his chair, grabbing [name withheld]'s favorite Rugby rookie card. He takes off toward the exit, damn near running [name withheld] over, who is pulling his hair out, yelling "JOIN THE SWA!!!".] [EXIT STAGE LEFT!]  >[ENTER STAGE RIGHT!] > >[The Wasp, with that RIDICULOUS looking mustache (!!!) and tired old leather >pants calls to attention a press conference at RSPWF Headquarters, where he >is a MAINSTAY...
