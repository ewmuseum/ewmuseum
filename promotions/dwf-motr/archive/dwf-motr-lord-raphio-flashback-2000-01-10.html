Scene: A man who dons the cap of pure evil, Lord Raphio, is ready to fight Nick Demola in the old DWF. This roleplay is about two years old, but it's still pretty damn good.
This is why the DWF was so excellent. Most of the fed did roleplaying with the brackets and dialogues and all that. But then there was Lord
Raphio, Nytro, and Nick Demola. We had to go and do things differently. Raphio and Demola especially tested the boundaries of damn near sci-fi writing. When we worked our angle together, we felt we could do whatever we wanted. So we pushed the limits. And this is one of the results. I'll be damned if I don't have any of my old stuff, but I have all of Raphio's.
God I love the memories. I may just return to writing in this format for shits and giggles from now on. It's what I love to do.
Anyways, if you want to mail the guy who handled Lord Raphio, it's actually my brother [name withheld]. So you can write me and I'll relay any comments to him.
[DWF] Lord Raphio: Frayed Ends of Sanity -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- “Keep far from the man who has the power to kill, and you will not be filled with the dread of death.
But if you approach him, offend him not, Lest he take away your life.” -Sirach 10:13 His overalls, once a denim blue, now donned a coating of rusty crimson, too much to merely be called spots on the stained clothing.
Those who still could speak lain in the street, screaming for mercy and vainly holding their torn appendages in place as they slowly lost consciousness. Up above the sun shined brightly, sending gentle waves of heat up from the sidewalk pavement. More than likely, not far from the busy city street, children were playing in a field of tall country grass, their skin baking to a golden brown in the light of the sun- it was a good day to die.
“Yes… I am coming.” The man said plainly, his face pale and devoid of expression as he snapped a new clip into his weapon. Looking up, he saw more of the troublesome obstructions, soul-less blurs that kept him from his master.
Though the gun’s barrel still glowed an iron-forged red from the many rounds fired off, his aim was still true and steady, sending several volleys in the direction of his distractions. The police officer couldn’t tell by seeing the gun that the rounds had exploding heads, but he could feel it as his right lung felt the stinging pain of exposure amidst the quickly evacuating remains of his ribcage. He had no time to scream before being blown against the police car, covering it too in a coating of crimson.
Looking quickly around in a fit of confusion, he blindly followed the sound of the voice that only he could hear. Media helicopters followed his movements as he methodically made his way to the outskirts of town, occasionally pausing only to grab another clip from his belt.
Suddenly, three police cars raced ahead of him, blocking his path as the screeched to a stop. The six officers quickly jumped from their cars, taking cover behind them.
And the man came closer. They drew their weapons, quickly switching off their safeties. (~Come to me, my child…~) And the man came closer. Summoning up their years of academy training, the officers took aim at the man’s head, adrenalin numbing the world around them. (~Come to me…~)
And the man came closer. “Fire!” The officers squeezed the triggers without hesitation, sending the payload to it’s intended target. (~ You need not fear them, trust in me…~)
And the man stopped, raising his hand ahead of him. All was silent except for the humming of media choppers and the penetrating sound of metal falling to the ground, six times. It rung in the ears of the policemen, like the sound of a water drop tortures the man who receives its impact for days while bound and chained, no longer able to scream for lack of strength and lack of sanity.
For a moment the man looked down, and laughed when he saw the metal casings lying harmlessly at his feet. He continued walking, firing a round deep into one of the policemen as he continued to cackle madly, the surviving policemen diving for cover.
The man walked another hundred feet or so, then stopped, staring at the road at his feet. “I am here.” He stated. A voice, coarse as metal filings, seemed to bellow from the very wind. “I knew you would come…”
The dark gray concrete began to shift, rumbling in a low tone. Without further warning, the earth yawned open before the man, swallowing the very ground itself as it spread from its epicenter like a ripple in water, leaving a hole forged in the darkest midnight.
Throwing down his red-hot gun, the man jumped inside. Slowly the ground began to re-appear, constructing itself back to that single point, and in a flash of light all was as it once was- the concrete undisturbed, the ground unscarred, the gaping chasm vanished from existence.
******************* He knelt before the altar, whose neon flame cast green shadows on the wall as its tongues cackled and burned in an almost harmonic cycle. Two points of dark red light, like the fire of hell itself contained, shone from behind the burning tabernacle, intense enough to be seen on the other side.
Low and coarse came a voice from behind the altar, which made the very earth tremble at the sound of it. “ You have arrived, I see.
Do you have it?” Reaching in his blood-stained pocket, the man pulled out a crystal, which glowed green in the light of the engulfed altar. “ The diamond Uraj, my sovereign. The ancient artifact which you have requested.” Although the mans’ delusional mind did not allow him such insight to believe it, he could see that the refraction of light within the crystal was not pure- this was no diamond. But it was, it had to be. The ancient diamond of Uraj, through which the power of the universe flowed… at least, that was what the master had told him.
The twisted demon behind the altar jumped to his feet, in poorly acted surprise. “You found it! For a moment I thought you would not.” Stepping in front of the altar, the man could see the demons’ disfigured, scarred face for the first time. It almost looked like a man…
“Give it to me.” The demon said, his resonant voice shaking the crude cavern walls of his blasphemic palace.
Without hesitation, the man reached his arm out to the demon, the crystal in his outstretched palm.
Seizing the crystal, a demonic laugh issued from the hell child. “ Very good… Now you shall have the reward I promised you.”
He smiled, rising to his feet, visions of the immense power that would be soon granted to him floating in his head. Dreams of power, and conquest, of vengeance and –
The flow of thoughts suddenly became interrupted, cloudy. At first he knew not what, but that he could not see. Within a few seconds, it became apparent to him that pain was the feeling which interrupted his vision of power, as he felt his first layer of skin rise to a boil. His very blood heated to a misty vapor, sending to his brain which was somehow still alive the only thoughts he would have for the rest of his existence- pure, unrestrained torment. Convulsing on the floor, he could feel the cells that held his arteries rupture, intensifying the spread of his burning blood to throughout his body.
He could feel his innards turning molten, his bones, his skin. There was no time to reason within his mind how he could possibly still be alive, and still feeling his restless boiling. The only small comfort that came to him was the knowledge that, eventually, his mind would cease to function and he would be free of his torture. After what felt like hours, his seemingly endless burden of pure torment began to shift towards numbness, then to nothing at all…
… and the last thing he heard was the deep, hearty laughter of the lord of darkness. “Heh… the ‘Uraj diamond’. Humans are even stupider than I had previously thought…” The demon in the black beret stated, crushing the ‘diamond’ into a powdery dust between his thumb and forefinger.
“… Nick Demola… I wonder what your remains will look like…” He stated as he walked away from the sizzling, quivering puddle of a peach and red swirled liquid.
