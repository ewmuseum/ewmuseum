DWF/MotR — Card

BattleGround for 2/29 (Part 1 of 4)

Feb 29, 2000, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Sounds of guitar plucking can be heard on the dark screen…] [As the guitar goes on, a voice says, “Turn it up” and the green DWF logo covers the screen.]
[The sound of Ronnie Van Zandt screams out the lyrics to “Sweet Home
Alabama” as pictures of DWF stars flash on the screen.]
#Big wheels keep on turning,# #carry me home to see my kin# #singing songs about the southland.# #I miss ole bamy once again and I think it's a sin.# [Pictures switch off, from little Aidan Madison executing a 360 degree
Frog Splash, to the masked El Sangre del Noche hitting a twisting plancha, to Jeremy Locke hitting a double spinning leg lariat, and coming to L.A. Bombay hitting a catapult blockbuster.]
#Well, I heard Mister Young sing about her.# #Well, I heard ole Neil put her down.# #Well, I hope Neil Young will remember# #a southern man don't need him around anyhow.# #Sweet home Alabama where the skies are so blue.# #Sweet home Alabama, Lord, I'm coming home to you.# [More pictures now flash on the screen, starting with Gabriel Demorte hitting a reverse neckbreaker, to Robert Mula hitting a German Suplex with a pin, finally to Mike Waldrop hitting a Death Valley Driver.]
#In Birmingham they love the Gov'nor.# #Now we all did what we could do.# #Now Watergate does not bother me.# #Does your conscience bother you, tell the truth.# #Sweet home Alabama where the skies are so blue.# #Sweet home Alabama, Lord, I'm coming home to you.# [Pictures flash of Frank Anthony hitting a superplex, going to Vincent
Lockheart hitting a heart punch, to Dave Pietka locking on a modified bow and arrow submission, and finally to Mark Marleau hitting a series of punches to a poor opponent’s face.]
#Now Muscle Shoals has got the Swampers# #and they ve been known to pick a song or two.# #Lord, they get me off so much,# #they pick me up when I'm feeling blue, now how about you.# #Sweet home Alabama where the skies are so blue.# #Sweet home Alabama, Lord, I'm coming home to you.# #Sweet home Alabama where the skies are so blue.# #Sweet home Alabama, Lord, I'm coming home to you.# [Pictures of the countryside of Alabama roll by the screen, before finally closing on a picture of the DWF logo with a confederate flag under it.]
[The camera pans the small capacity crowd, which is in an absolute uproar! The crowd is all on its feet as the camera keeps skipping over the two men in the ring… a small, unathletic looking man named Stuart
Jennings, and a huge tanned hispanic named Juan Vasquez.]
[The fans go crazy as the camera pans past them. Signs in the crowd include, “Respect the Shadow,” “Finally the Dogg has brought wrestling to Birmingham!” and “Marleau for Prime Minister!”]
[The camera focuses on the announcers in the ring. Stuart Jennings bows and Juan Vasquez brushes off the crowd as the crowd screams out their chant of “D-W-F! D-W-F!”]
SJ: Ladies and gentlemen! Welcome to the first show of the DOWNSOUTH WRESTLING FEDERATION!!! [The chant only gets louder as Stu signals for them to quiet down a bit.] SJ: We’re here from Seibert Auditorium on the campus of Samford University in Birmingham, Alabama! [The crowd breaks in to a random screaming fit and a “Let’s Go Bulldogs!” chant.] SJ: I’m Stuart Jennings and with me as always, for better or worse, is
Nick’s best bud (which is why he has the job)… “El Rey” Juan Vasquez.
[The crowd boos the heel and Juan just looks at them disgustedly.] JV: Don’t be jealous of me. Don’t be scared. I truly am this perfect. No reason to boo out of fear.
[Juan smiles and the crowd keeps getting louder.] SJ: Juan, enough lame lines. We’ve got one Hell of a card tonight. We’ve got two four-way matches, the first involving L.A. Bombay… [Loud boos rain down throughout the arena at Bombay’s name.] SJ: “Heavy Mental” Dave Pietka… [Even louder boos come down.] SJ: Robert Mula… [The boos turn into cheers for Mula.] SJ: And the little man that could, “Playboy” Aidan Madison. [The crowd goes nuts at the extreme star.] JV: You all are idiots! Do you think Madison stands a chance? He’s got nothing! Pietka, Bombay. THOSE are names you should be proud of!
Names you should be cheering! [The crowd boos.] SJ: Enough cheap heat. We’ve got our second match right after that. Involving “The Total Wrestling Machine” Gabriel Demorte… [HUGE booing going on.] SJ: “Cream of the Crop” Mike Waldrop… [More booing.] SJ: El Sangre del Noche… [HUGE pop and “IN-SA-NO!” chants going on.] SJ: And… well… we don’t know… JV: We know that that sack of crap Mark Marleau is out of commission because he slipped and fell!
SJ: Slipped and fell onto a crowbar being held by two of Nick’s cronies, eh? JV: Whatever. No matter why, he’s out of the card tonight. Looks like it’s going to be a three man battle for that spot in the semis tonight!
[The arena goes completely dark.] SJ: What is THIS now? JV: I don’t know… whatever it is I’m getting out of here! [The whaling of the guitar riff to open "Sleep Now in the Fire" by Rage
Against the Machine deafens those in the arena as the entire arena becomes silver in color. A mysterious man in all black walks down the aisle and to the center of it and then stands there with his hands crossed at his waist level.]
SJ: IT’S LOCKE! IT’S LOCKE! #The world is my expense# #The cost of my desire# #Jesus blessed me with its future# #And I protect it with fire# #So raise your fists# #And march around# #Don't dare take what you need# #I'll jail and bury those committed# #And smother the rest in greed# #Crawl with me into tomorrow# #Or I'll drag you to your grave# #I'm deep inside your children# #They'll betray you in my name# #Sleep now in the fire# [As that part of the song ends, an explosion of lights appears at each side of the area where the wrestlers enter. When the lights fade, standing on the top of the ramp is the blonde haired, trenchcoated dark warrior. The man standing in the middle of the ramp has disappeared.
Jeremy Locke then proceeds to walk very slowly towards the ring, looking only at the ring and ignoring all others.]
#The lie is my expense# #The scope of desire# #The Party blessed me with its future# #And I protect it with fire# #I am the Nina# #The Pinta# #The Santa Maria# #The noose and the rapist# #And the fields overseer# #The agents of orange# #The priests of Hiroshima# #The cost of my desire# #Sleep now in the fire# [Locke immediately leaps onto the ring apron with one stellar bound, then flips over the top rope and into the ring. He bounces off of each rope and then kneels in the middle of the ring in a trance-like state.]
#For it's the end of history# #It's caged and frozen still# #There is no other pill to take# #So swallow the one# #That made you ill# #The Nina# #The Pinta# #The Santa Maria# #The noose and the rapist# #The fields overseer# #The agents of orange# #The priests of Hiroshima# #The cost of my desire# #Sleep now in the fire# [As the song goes out to hard metal, Locke leaps onto the top rope and sits on the turnbuckle, glaring across the ring and getting a microphone handed to him.]
JL: The shadow bows for no mortal. The game may be at its gravest, death may be imminent. But the shadow remains.
[The fans pop huge as Locke just keeps his head bowed as he sits on the top turnbuckle.] JL: This is your game, Frank Anthony. Your game, with a rotten stench hanging overhead. Falls Count Anywhere, but it will not be I who takes the fall.
JL: You should not take what I do personally, young brother. Take it as business. Take it as a message to our brother… as a symbol of what is to come for him.
[He leaps down into the ring and looks into the camera.] JL: Tonight, Frank Anthony. This is your game. But it is my time. My show. If you would like, you can walk into the ring and fall and end this on your own. But if you do not end this on your own, I will be forced to end it. Easy way, hard way… either way it’s my way.
JL: When you see the shadows of the night, you will understand… [The arena goes dark once again, then the lights flash back on. When the lights come back on, Locke is gone.]
[The camera now cuts to Stuart Jennings and Juan Vasquez seated at ringside.] SJ: Quite strong words by the reigning DWF World Champion… JV: Where did he go? How does he do that! SJ: Well, regardless of that we have a whole card to get through to- wait. Wait! There’s something going on backstage! Let’s cut to
Scheme Gene Dunderhead in the backstage area!
[The camera cuts to a small bald man standing in front of an overturned stack of cartons and a big, buff blonde woman with a bruise under her eye and a scroll in her hand.]
SGD (breathing heavily): Stu… Juan… I tried to get back here… to interview… Frank Anthony…
SJ: Slow down Gene! It’s alright. What happened? SGD: He… hit Michelle… SJ: Michelle Magdalena? Nick Demola’s manager and Frank’s ex-fiance? SGD: Yes… SJ: Why was she there? [Michelle steps in front of Gene, rubbing her eye with her left hand.] MM: I was here to hand Frank a scroll that Nick wanted him to read on the air. SJ: What did that scroll say? MM: It talked about how great the DWF is, how Nick Demola is the Ruler of All Wrestling, how Frank is going to tear Jeremy Locke apart…
SJ: No WONDER he didn’t want to read it! MM: But he’s under personal contract to us! He has to read this! JV: So that ROTTEN Anthony hit you on your beautiful face? MM: Yes Juan. He hit me. He gave me a bruise right under my eye. JV: What a piece of crap! SJ: Oh cut out the buttkissing already! [Gene steps in front of Michelle.] SGD: If you want someone to interview this psycho, get someone else because I’m not doing it!
[The camera cuts away from the backstage area and back to the announcer’s table.] JV: I have half a mind to find that idiot and beat his head in! SJ: With the mood he’s in I’m not sure even I want to talk to him… and he’s one of my closest friends!
JV: Why is he being so difficult? He’s got a great job with security. Why doesn’t he just accept it?
SJ: And be treated like a slave by his older brother Nick Demola? Fat chance. He’s got self-respect.
JV: And that self-respect is just going to get him put in a hospital one of these days. SJ: Well… after tonight he may be put in a hospital from the sheer brutality that will emanate from his matchup with another dangerous man, his own half-brother Jeremy Locke.
JV: A lot of people are saying how there’s a lot of common respect between Locke and Anthony, but I think that when the chips fall they’ll end up kicking the Hell out of each other if for no other reason than the fact that the Southern Title semi-final berth is in the balance.
SJ: You might actually be right for a change on that one, Juan. JV: For a change? Sent via Deja.com http://www.deja.com/ Before you buy.
