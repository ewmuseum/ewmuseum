BattleGround for 2/29 (Part 4 of 4)

Feb 29, 2000, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
SJ: And now, after all that turmoil, we come to the final match of the night! And what a backstory there is on this fight, ladies and gentlemen! Jeremy Locke and Frank Anthony, both brothers to Nick
Demola and both full of hate for said president, will now fight it out to see who advances in the Southern Title Tournament, with the loser staying home next week.
JV: Another masterful plan by Nicky D. And he’s got Frank by the short and curlies with that contract of his!
SJ: It’s despicable. He’s forcing Frank to expel someone who he actually considers his friend in favor of a man who through the years has screwed him!
JV: Screwed him? SJ: Screwed him! JV: Well it seems to me that Locke is the one that’s looking like a two-dollar Hollywood and Vine merchant tonight. He’s got the deck stacked against him and this may be step one in a final plan to rid the
DWF and Nick Demola of this bastard once and for all.
SJ: Ladies and gents… two weeks of talking, two years of fighting, and three lifetimes of backstories are about to come to a head here. Dave
Pietka and Gabriel Demorte are already in the semi-finals of the tournament, and one of these men will join them. Let’s go to Mitchell
Harris Butler for the introductions to this main event matchup.
MHB: Ladies and gentlemen… this is a Falls Count Anywhere Match with the winner advancing to the semi-finals of the Southern Title
Tournament next week! [The fans go buckwild for the first intro.] MHB: Introducing first… #Looooooooooooooooooong...# #...Neck bottle# DS: Hailing from Dallas, Texas, at 6'6, 300 pounds...he is "The King of Hardcore," Frank Anthony! #Let go of my hand# #Hey jukebox don't start playing that song again# #'Cause there's a girl at home who loves me, # #You know she won't understand # #Long neck bottle, # #Let go of my hand# [Frank comes out holding a bottle of Jack Daniels in his left hand. He takes a gulp of JD, and then when people go to reach out for his hand, he grabs it and pours the alcohol on it. Eventually, people almost get riotous reaching with their hands...]
#Hey barroom mirror on the wall# #Go stare at someone else # #Don't show the world # #The fool I am# #Just keep it to yourself # #Long neck bottle, # #Let go of my hand# #Hey jukebox don't start playing that song again# #'Cause there's a girl at home who loves me, # #You know she won't understand # #Long neck bottle, # #Let go of my hand# [Frank steps up to the ring apron and takes a giant swig of the alcohol, then puts the bottle in the corner of the ring.]
#Dance floor, seems you're underneath my feet# #Everywhere I turn # #I'd waltz right out of those swinging doors, # #But that step I just can't learn # #Long neck bottle, # #Let go of my hand # #Hey jukebox don't start playing that song again# #'Cause there's a girl at home who loves me, # #You know she won't understand # #Long neck bottle, # #Let go of my hand# #'Cause there's a girl at home who loves me, # #You know she won't understand # #Long neck bottle, # #Let go of my hand# [Frank goes in the ring and points at a young guy in the crowd. He then finds a youngin' in the crowd, grabs the bottle... and starts pouring the hard liquor on him! You hear the parents squealing as Frank
Anthony jumps in the ring and squats in the corner, ready for his opponents' entrance.] MHB: And his opponent… [The lights of the arena go dark.] [The whaling of the guitar riff to open "Sleep Now in the Fire" by Rage
Against the Machine deafens those in the arena as the entire arena becomes silver in color. A mysterious man in all black walks down the aisle and to the center of it and then stands there with his hands crossed at his waist level.]
#The world is my expense# #The cost of my desire# #Jesus blessed me with its future# #And I protect it with fire# #So raise your fists# #And march around# #Don't dare take what you need# #I'll jail and bury those committed# #And smother the rest in greed# #Crawl with me into tomorrow# #Or I'll drag you to your grave# #I'm deep inside your children# #They'll betray you in my name# #Sleep now in the fire# [As that part of the song ends, an explosion of lights appears at each side of the area where the wrestlers enter. When the lights fade, standing on the top of the ramp is the blonde haired, trenchcoated dark warrior. The man standing in the middle of the ramp has disappeared.
Jeremy Locke then proceeds to walk very slowly towards the ring, looking only at the ring and ignoring all others.]
#The lie is my expense# #The scope of desire# #The Party blessed me with its future# #And I protect it with fire# #I am the Nina# #The Pinta# #The Santa Maria# #The noose and the rapist# #And the fields overseer# #The agents of orange# #The priests of Hiroshima# #The cost of my desire# #Sleep now in the fire# [Locke immediately leaps onto the ring apron with one stellar bound, then flips over the top rope and into the ring. He bounces off of each rope and then kneels in the middle of the ring in a trance-like state.]
#For it's the end of history# #It's caged and frozen still# #There is no other pill to take# #So swallow the one# #That made you ill# #The Nina# #The Pinta# #The Santa Maria# #The noose and the rapist# #The fields overseer# #The agents of orange# #The priests of Hiroshima# #The cost of my desire# #Sleep now in the fire# [As the song goes out to hard metal, Locke leaps onto the top rope and sits on the turnbuckle, glaring across the ring and waiting for the proceedings to begin.]
“NIGHTSHADE” JEREMY LOCKE VS. “THE KING OF HARDCORE” FRANK ANTHONY - Falls Count Anywhere Southern Title Quarterfinal Match [DING! DING! DING!] [The two men begin the match by circling around the ring, looking over at each other and waiting for whoever is going to do the first move.]
SJ: These two men don’t want to fight, Juan! They’re united against a common enemy! This is just a way for Nick Demola to get rid of them both in one fell swoop.
JV: Brilliant, ain’t it? SJ: It’s sick! Nick Demola wasn’t supposed to be an active part of this federation, but already he’s sticking his nose into the DWF just like he always has.
JV: What’s that supposed to mean? SJ: You know exactly what that means! Last time he said, “I won’t have a part of the fed,” he ended up main eventing himself on a pay per view!
JV: But it’s what the fans wanted. SJ: And the fans want these two men to rip Nick’s head off, so why doesn’t he just come out here and get his rear end whooped like a man!
[Finally after all that waiting both men walk to the center of the ring. Locke lifts his arms for a collar and elbow tie-up, but Frank gives him a quick boot to the ribs and a couple punches to the face that sends the blond man staggering into the corner.]
SJ: And now the thought of winning that title has Frank kicking and punching Locke. That Southern title would mean the world to Frank
Anthony, seeing as how he’s such a proud southerner himself.
JV: I never understood how people could be proud of being from the south. It’s like celebrating diarrhea. And who says that even if
Frank got past Locke, which he should, that he could beat either
Demorte or Pietka later in the tournament? SJ: If he fights like I know he can, he can beat anyone. JV: Thank you Mister Inspiration… [Frank immediately wallops on the thinner Locke with a series of rights and lefts to the body… but Locke instantly turns the tables, putting
Frank in the corner and giving him a sharp Spinning Heel Kick that dazes Frank and leaves the King of Hardcore holding his jaw!]
SJ: Locke retaliates! This man truly does not know how to stay down! JV: He’ll wish he learned how after Nicky D gets through with him. [Locke whips Frank into the opposite corner hard, sending reverberations of the thud of Frank’s back against the turnbuckle charging throughout the arena. Frank staggers out of his corner as
Locke charges… and Locke hits him with a double spinning leg lariat he calls the Kiss of the Shadows!!!]
SJ: That HAS to be it! Anthony is done! He’s made quick work of him! [Locke covers.] ONE!!!!! TWO!!!!!! NO!!!!!!!!!!! SJ: He’s up! Frank Anthony is up! JV: It couldn’t have ended that quickly. Nicky D wouldn’t have picked
Frank if he thought he’d lose like that!
[Immediately Locke throws Frank out of the ring and to the concrete floor below… where he then measures up the staggered Texan. Locke runs from the opposite side of the ring… and LEAPS over the top rope and onto Frank Anthony with an amazing Springboard Somersault Plancha!!!]
SJ: I can’t BELIEVE that a man that size can do those moves! Locke is inhuman! [The fans chant “D-W-F! D-W-F!” as Locke and Frank both stagger to their feet. Locke clotheslines Frank over the guardrail and to the front row of fans, much to their delight!]
SJ: Now this is getting into Frank Anthony territory. An all out brawl that takes place all over the arena. Locke might be wise to keep it closer to the ring.
JV: This is all a part of the master plan! These guys are going to
KILL each other out there!
[Locke immediately goes for another Spinning Heel Kick, but Frank ducks it and instead gives Locke a low blow while his legs are split!]
SJ: Frank Anthony with a desperation move! JV: That’s more like it! Dirty tactics are always better! [Frank then takes control as many thought he would in this match. He lifts Locke up and throws him down into the guardrail throat first, then picks up a folding chair at ringside!]
SJ: Oh man… what’s he going to do with that chair now? JV: DWF chairs are very sturdy, we saw to it that they were. [Anthony lifts up the chair… and brings it down HARD onto the back of the slumping Jeremy Locke!!!]
SJ: You could hear THAT from Frank’s home in Dallas, Texas! JV: And I bet it put a smile on his twisted friends’ faces too. This guy is insane. He should be put in a loony bin!
SJ: You might actually be right about that one, Juan. JV: I’m right about everything, Stu. You’ll learn that as we work more together. SJ: I’m sure. [Anthony then sets the chair upright, leaning on the guardrail. He lifts up Locke and punches him with a couple low blows to the stomach… then he drops to the ground and executes a drop toe hold so that
Locke’s face SMASHES right into the chair!!!] SJ: GOD ALMIGHTY! Locke is going to be a bloody mess after this match! JV: Nicky D’s plan working to perfection Stu. Perfection. [Locke crumples down to the ground in a heap and Frank immediately picks him up by the hair and drags him further into the crowd.]
SJ: Frank Anthony is insane! And only God knows what he might do to someone in this environment! He’d hit his own grandmother in a brawl.
That’s how he is. He completely loses his mind! JV: That’s just what we’re hoping for… and Frank would hit his own grandmother for fun, not just in a brawl.
SJ: Point taken. [Anthony tells a group of fans to move out of the way, hooks up Locke, and delivers a crushing suplex onto the concrete floor! Locke stays motionless on the ground as Frank points at a table and sets it up just under the balcony overlooking an exit to the arena!]
SJ: Oh God no… what is he planning on doing with that table? JV: Better yet, what’s he planning on doing with BOTH of those tables??? [Frank, with a look of glee on his face, sets up a second table on top of the first one, and then goes over to Locke and smashes his face into the concrete a few times, causing blood to gush from his forehead.]
SJ: Oh my God. Locke is busted open! Locke is busted open! JV: This is what DWF is all about. It’s about two men giving it their all for the biggest prize this federation has to offer!
[Frank begins to drag Locke up the steps to the balcony… when all of a sudden the fans’ heads all turn to the entrance ramp.]
SJ: Oh no! What is SHE doing here??? JV: SHELLEY’S BACK!!! [The big, buff blonde bodyguard of Nick Demola… and Frank Anthony’s ex- fiance… Michelle Magdalena runs quickly down to the site of all the mayhem, with crowbar firmly in hand.]
SJ: Who is she out here to support though? JV: Neither of them. She’s here to support Nick Demola! [She runs up the stairs and comes to the scene where Frank Anthony has
Locke grasped in for another suplex… this time off the top of the
BALCONY and through BOTH TABLES!] SJ: He’s going to kill them both! JV: One can only wish… [From the side of Frank, though, Michelle taps him on the shoulder. She has the crowbar in hand and seems to want to hand it to Frank…] SJ: That’s not such a smart move… JV: Frank’s insane! Get out of there Michelle! Get out of there! [Frank spots his ex-fiance with a crowbar in her hand… and LEVELS her with a vicious Lariat!!!]
JV: FRANK HIT HER! What a piece of crap! SJ: How could he have known she was going to hand him the crowbar? He thought she was going to hit him with it. He had no other choice!
[Frank looks down at Michelle and chuckles… and from behind Locke gets to his feet…] SJ: Frank better watch his back! JV: Here comes Locke, sneaking from behind like the coward that he is! [Locke gives Frank a stiff kick in the back, and then hooks him up for an inverted DDT…] SJ: He’s going to drive his head into the concrete! JV: No he’s not! Look! He’s moving towards the balcony’s edge! [Locke stands at the edge of the balcony, with a squirming Frank Anthony’s face dug into his armpit…] SJ: THEY’RE BOTH GOING TO DIE RIGHT HERE!!! JV: This can’t be!! [Locke leaps with Frank under his arm and… CRASH!!!!!!!!!!!! FANS: D-W-F!!! D-W-F!!! … BOTH MEN GO THROUGH THE TABLES!!!! They lay in a heap, with Locke happening to have landed on top of Frank through the mess!!!]
SJ: HOLY COW! THEY’VE GOT TO BE DEAD! JV: Get up Frank! Get up! [The referee goes down and makes the count…] ONE!!!!! TWO!!!!!! THREEEEEEEEEEE!!!!! [DING! DING! DING!] MHB: The winner of this match, and advancing to the semi-finals next week… “NIGHTSHADE” JEREMY LOCKE!!!!!!
SJ: Get some help for these men, damn it! [Two stretchers get pulled out of the back… but Locke is already staggering around and going towards the ring. Frank, meanwhile, lays absolutely decimated on top of the scraps of tables.]
SJ: Incredible… incredible. I can’t believe Locke is WALKING to the ring! [Locke slides into the ring and jumps onto the top rope. He sits on the top rope trying to gather himself as the crowd chants “D-W-F! D-W- F!”]
SJ: Juan, you’re not saying anything now, are you? JV: … SJ: Well for my disconcerted colleague Juan Vasquez, this is Stuart
Jennings saying goodnight from the campus of Samford University in
Birmingham, Alabama! We’ll see you next week for more DWF action!
