[The camera cuts into a crowded room filled with flashing photographers
(yes they have all their clothes on) and reporters with their little notepads and their little pencils at the front of the room.
Immediately in front of this crowd is a raised podium, about six feet off the ground. On the podium is the green logo of the DWF. A green curtain extends from one side of the room to the other behind the podium.]
[As cameras flash, the balding, short announcer of the DWF makes his way to the podium. Stuart Jennings, glasses on his face and papers in his hands, taps the mic a few times to see if it’s working. He then speaks into it with his strong tenor voice.]
SJ: Ladies and gentlemen, welcome to the official press conference for
DWF Total Annihilation I – Bad Moon Risin’. I am your Emcee for the evening, Stuart Jennings and it is my honor and privilege to introduce the stars of the DWF to the entire world.

I am excited to be a part of this as it truly gets off the ground.
[Another round of polite clapping.] SJ: But enough of all that. We have a virtual bevy of superstars to get to. So with that, let’s get on with the show!
