“HEAVY MENTAL” DAVE PIETKA VS. “TOTAL WRESTLING MACHINE” GABRIEL DEMORTE - DWF Southern Title Match, Master’s Cell Match =========================== “HEAVY MENTAL” DAVE PIETKA =========================== Voice: Lesse... I won my first match... my second match... my third match... ANYONE ELSE CARE TO POINT OUT THE THEME HERE?
((The screen fades into Grand Prix, located in Dania, Florida. Seeing that the DWF has come down to Miami, it seemed only fitting he have a good time at home. He? "Heavy Mental" Dave Pietka, wearing a gray
Mossimo shirt and jeans... and alone. He walks up the parking lot, after locking his car up, and stand in front of a gate. On the other side of this gate are the two thrill rides Grand Prix is known for. The
Sky Coaster and The Blender. The camera pans to where Pietka is pointing... The Sky Coaster. Three people are getting attached to the cord that will lift them up 250 feet before they pull the ripcord that causes them to plummet at 60 miles per, coming just ten feet from the ground. Pietka just laughs.))
D. Pietka: MAKE WAY FOR THE DWF'S WORK! MAKE WAY! A simple benadiction for these three poor girls who are about to plummet. May God show them the love they so richly deserve, even though there won't be much of them left to love when their bodies slam onto the cold, hard, unforgiving earth! WAAAAAH HA HA HA!
((People just look at Pietka, as he continues with his rant. Pietka walks towards the entrance just as the girls begin their ascent. As
Pietka enters, the sounds of inside nearly drown everything else out.
The video games, the roller-coaster simulator, the laser tag arena. Pietka quickly makes his way outside, walking towards the Sky Coaster.)) D. Pietka: What was it that I said once? "Give me your hand and I'll gladly accompany you on my fall from reality." As I think about it, it holds true here. Here I am, in my home-state. Obvious advantage... Here
I am, coming off a winning streak that isn't about to be broken. Yet another obvious advantage. And here I am, about to become the first
REAL DWF Champion. The greatest advantage of them all. What seperates me from the two soft pink bags of flesh that have to come so close to greatness only to have their lifelines severed before they reach a safe distance to descend... if that I hold not only the desire, the will, and the spirit... but I'm just plain [bleep]ing crazy! HELLO, MCFLY!
ANYONE HOME? It's gonna be me, Gabby, and that poor guy, whats-his- name, who got caught in the middle of all this in a STEEL CAGE WITH
WEAPONS! Sure, sure... I can't have my Existenz with me at ringside, I can't even have Devin come down. LIKE I NEED IT! I managed to get through all of my matches without FLAW! Well, except that last one...
not about to say I won that flat out... but I DID win it. So Marleau can whine and cry and pout all he wants about it. The fact that my hand was in the right place at the right time only goes to show that I'm the only man deserving enough to wear the DWF strap!
((Pietka walks up to the Sky Coaster booth, and the people nod to him. An attendant walks up and tells Pietka to step into the holes of the harness he needs. After he's suited up, he approaches the gurney to be suited up
Already on the stand are "Double D" Devin DeMasters and "Marz" Candress. Pietka walks up the steps, and kisses DeMasters before walking to a position where Marz was in the middle.))
D. DeMasters: Where were you? D. Pietka: Brutal Traffic. This place is a hotspot at night, remember, babe? Well, maybe Brutal isn't the right word. When I say Brutal, I often refer to Gabby's interviewing habits. And while his promos can be considered the top reason that depressed stock brokers and middle-aged horses commit suicide, I have to give the devil his ounce of due. He managed to pull a fast one of Marz... but you got his ass back, big guy! Nothing like a little ignited hair-spray in the face to say "YOU
[bleep]ED WITH THE WRONG GUY!"
D. DeMasters: He's all talk. I haven't seen him come out on top over you at all, not even once. Total Wrestling Machine, my sweet ass. Marleau? Well... he just got shoved into the wrong place at the wrong time because the little bitchy bastard brother of Nicky Demola decides that he can't take the pressure anymore. Little Boy Locke wants to even the score with the family... so he forfiets his place. I would say that it was a bright move, because now he won't have to worry about a chance of fighting you.
D. Pietka: Ain't that the truth, baby. One less person to worry about him! I'M THE ONLY MO-FO THAT HAS TO BE WORRIED ABOUT! What Gabby never seems to understand is that he can spray glue into your bodyguard's eyes, and it just gets a big wet bite taken outta his ass at his own expense. In fact, he's damn right LUCKY, for lack of a better term, that we're fighting in a steel cage... and, yet not. FACE IT, DEMORTE!
You got this far out of PURE AND SHEER LUCK! I am a true champion, and
I've proved it so far by winning the last three way, and now... well...
I'm about to show why the DWF should have handed me the belt just as soon as they opened. YOU, GABBY, DIDN'T, DON'T, AND WON'T EVER QUALIFY as a champion because of two things...
((Pietka, DeMasters, and Marz are told to fall forward as the blue support bar falls. The gurney is rolled out from under them, and they are given last minute instructions. Pietka rants through all of this.))
D. Pietka: ONE... Existenz or NOT, I am the GREATEST athlete in the DWF. I did not need Devin or Marz to help me to win my spot for the belt. The fact that Marz kept you from burning the scroll was something he did on his own volition, so don't be using the pissy-ass excuse that
Marz would be happy to help me, because he's here for Devin's protection.
((They start to be reeled upwards towards the top of the tower... again, Pietka is still ranting.)) D. Pietka: TWO... The Wicked Existenz RULES this damn federation. The Anti-Social Sublife taking the Tag Team Championship... and then ME becoming Top Kick! WE have the brains while all you have are some biceps you just kiss too much. And as for Marleau, what the hell makes him even think he has an
opportunity of coming CLOSE to winning that belt! And if by some fluke you manage to have Marleau pin me and then you pin Marky-Mark...
because we ALL know you can't beat me... I guarentee you I will be breathing down your neck and beating down every other part of your anatomy until that belt is wrapped around my waist. But that's not gonna happen! I will be champion... I will walk away with...
((Pietka's ranting becomes softer and softer as he reaches the top. We can still see him waving his arms around as if he was ranting still.

DeMasters blowing the camera a kiss, and we see Marz with his usual scowl... and the shades still on his face. Fade to black.))
============================================ “TOTAL WRESTLING MACHINE” GABRIEL DEMORTE ============================================ [ Gabriel Demorte doesn`t like Dave Pietka. Why should he after Pietka arranged to have the Total Wrestling Machine`s face to be burnt off. For the last week his face was in bandages while the burns healed. And then there as the match they fought at the last card with
Marc Marleau. Pietka was taken to the limit, but Demorte still had fight left in him. Despite have his face burnt at the beginning of the match, he came several times very close to winning the match that would be a preview of the PPV. Now
really, are you going to bet against a man like that? We join Gabriel
Demorte, the Total Wrestling Machine, in front of the DWF logo. Hey, it`s what he says, not where he is, okay? ]
I`d be the damndest liar if I said I didn`t want the DWF Southern Title. I`d be the damndest liar if I send the recognition and money it would bring meant nothing to me. Yet there is something that means a lot more to me than that.
[ Demorte looks deep into the camera with his burning dark eyes ] Showing Dave Pietka exactly what his colon looks like. You think I`m going to let you get away with burning my face Dave? Do you? You think I`m just going to queue up and wait for you to try and make my face look like a particularly unappetizing pizza, then say, thank you very much Dave, why don`t you do it again please, and while you`re at, take the damn DWF Southern title as well?
LIKE HELL I WILL! Dave, when you set fire to my face, you set fire to me. You started an inferno of ever growing vengeance, that I promise to you, on the life of my mother, my brothers and my father, is going to come back and consume you until you fall at Total Annihilation. It`s not just going to beat you, it`s going to destroy you. You`ve let the fuse of a firework, and it just won`t let you go. It`s going to blow, and take your hand, your arm, your torso, your head...everything...and scatter them to the wind.
[ Demorte looks to the side, breathing slowly, calming down ] Bring Candress, bring Demasters, bring the goddamn demons of hell itself... ...I`m going to breeze through them all.... ...then I`m going to get you... ...and as I look into your eyes... ...and you realise that there is no escape... ...you`re going to beg for forgiveness Dave... ...but Dave... ...forgiveness is something I keep no stock of... ...pain though... ...I`ve got all the pain in the world... ...and I`m going to give every last ounce to you.
