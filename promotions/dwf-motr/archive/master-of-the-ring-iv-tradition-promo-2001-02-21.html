Master of the Ring IV - Tradition PROMO

21 February 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Just a reminder... the card is Monday, February 26th and Wednesday the 28th. It's going to be huge with some neato surprises. After the card lineup I have 15 questions that will need to be answered at
MotR that make this card even more intriguing.
And now Master of the Ring IV - Tradition will take place from... The Saddledome in CALGARY, ALBERTA, CANADA! NIGHT ONE - Monday, February 26th, 2000 1) MotR IV FIRST ROUND FOURWAY ELIMINATION MATCH Morgan De Sade vs. "Walking Contradiction" Sabbath vs. "Sex Pistol" Jaimé Roberts vs. "Kichigaijimita" Kuroi Tenshi - You want chaos and destruction? Here you are. Master of the Ring IV will start off with a matchup that might just be the most brutal matchup of the entire card. MotR veteran Sabbath knows what it is to wrestle on the MotR stage. De Sade is one of the top names in the game. Roberts is a forlorn ex-playboy who is going through a time of misery, and will not let up. The mysterious Tenshi is the wildcard in this matchup.
2) MotR IV FIRST ROUND FOURWAY ELIMINATION MATCH The Warlock vs. "Insomniac" Mitchell Prose vs. Higor "The Rebel" Rosa vs. The Wasp - The men in this fight, for whatever reason, are not considered to be "all there". Whether it's drinking, insanity, or twisted brilliance... all four men are unpredictable. MotR III second place finisher The Wasp comes back to the event to take on the underappreciated Warlock and two other solid names in Prose and Rosa.
3) MotR IV FIRST ROUND FOURWAY ELIMINATION MATCH "Mr. Excitement" Alex Extreme vs. "Cream of the Crop" Mike Waldrop vs. Tommy Lionheart vs. "Simply the Best" James Pillow - As Alex Extreme's moniker says, this matchup is all about Excitement. From the veterans Pillow and Extreme to the two top rookies from last year in
Waldrop and Lionheart, this matchup may be the best first rounder in MotR history. All four men have something to prove, and it's sad that only one man can advance.
4) MotR IV FIRST ROUND FOURWAY ELIMINATION MATCH "Hot Stuff" Brian Von Braun vs. Elijah Flynt vs. Steve Houston vs. Johnny
Axis - Four old schoolers do battle here, as the boobirds will be out in force for three of the men, with Flynt garnering a lot of fan support. Johnny Axis has the second best record in MotR history. Steve Houston's last MotR appearance was over a year and a half ago to announce his retirement. This match looks to be stellar if you like the old style of wrestling.
5) MotR IV FIRST ROUND FOURWAY ELIMINATION MATCH Heidi Leick vs. Ace of Hearts vs. Nytro vs. "The Elite Sensation" Eric
Emerson - Sex appeal is what ties all these wrestlers together. Though in much different ways. Heidi Leick, the one woman in the field, must contend with
Ace, one of the top men from MotR, Eric Emerson, who fancies himself a stud, and Nytro, who fooled everyone into thinking he was gay just so he could get in the tournament under the nose of his part-time enemy Nick Demola. And we all remember what happened the last time a woman wrestled on the fifth match of the first night of an MotR card. More controversy coming?
6) MotR IV FIRST ROUND FOURWAY ELIMINATION MATCH Boltz vs. Vik Ventressa vs. "Badboy" Randy Acorn vs. Victor V. Hill - This is just going to be a brutal brawl. Four guys who can fight with anyone are in this match, and it's chaos waiting to happen. All of these four men have been through the wars in their careers, and now they finally get to go head to head against each other in this battle.
7) SPECIAL AWARD PRESENTATION To "Cowboy" Ken Curtis - Ken Curtis has graced the ring with a style and flair all his own for many years now. This is his chance to shine. The people at Armbar.com and Big
Dick McGee will join us in a special celebration of the Cowboy's career, which will culminate in an award ceremony that is sure to leave tears in the eyes of all Curtis fans everywhere.
8) DOUBLE PINFALL NO DQ FALLS COUNT ANYWHERE TAG MATCH "Mr. Main Event" Chris Douglas & Tyrone Parker vs. Power & Honor - Power & Honor, Gabriel Whitecross and Jason Keening, are a force that can't be denied... but their matchup on MotR IV might prove to be too much to handle. Sworn Keening enemy Chris Douglas (look to MotR I for more history with these guys) has vowed to defeat Keening once and for all. And in the brutal setting they have embarked on here... all four men may be stopped once and for all.
9) SUPERBOUT IV - 60 MINUTE IRONMAN RETIREMENT MATCH "Bad Eye" McBaine vs. "Britain's Nightmare" Alexander Lawson - The first match of both of these men was against... each other. They started their careers together, and now they will end their careers together. This matchup will go a full hour, with special stipulations every fifteen minutes. And there will be a winner. The winner of this match wins the six year long feud between them and breaks the tie that they've established through the years of fighting.
NIGHT TWO - Wednesday, February 28th, 2000 (with special guest color commentator... ROBERT MULA!!!)
1) EIGHT TEAM ELIMINATION TAG MATCH Team Sychosys, Burt Clifton/Mike Masters, The Suicide Blondes, American Idols vs. Precious Moments, JoJo The Indian Circus Boy/Homeless Joe, Dichotomy, Desolation Row - In what is shaped up to be one of the most memorable tag matches on any card in recent memory, a couple of younger teams try to hang with the veterans here. Much has been said about the return of Team Sychosis (will
Joe show?), Precious Moments and the team of JoJo and Homeless Joe... but not much has been said about the other teams. Anyone can win in this match.
The only thing that is certain is the action will be fast, furious, and brutal. The final two teams left will advance to the final match later in the night.
2) MotR IV SEMIFINAL #1 Winner of De Sade/Roberts/Sabbath/Tenshi vs. Winner of Warlock/Prose/Rosa/Wasp - After matches that are sure to drain all the men involved, someone is going to advance to this match. And the only thing for certain is that it will be brutal. The most brutal bracket of all three lays right here.
3) MotR IV SEMIFINAL #2 Winner of Extreme/Waldrop/Lionheart/Pillow vs. Winner of Von Braun/Flynt/Houston/Axis - No matter who advances, this match will be a contrast of styles to the ultimate degree. The high-flying, risk-taking highspotter against the old school tough guy will be the highlight of this match.
4) MotR IV SEMIFINAL #3 Winner of Leick/Ace/Nytro/Emerson vs. Winner of Boltz/Ventressa/Acorn/Hill - Another contrast of styles will highlight this matchup. In this one you have pretty boy (or girl) sex appeal against rugged, brutal toughness. 5) RETIREMENT MATCH Spikyjim vs. Loaded - These two men have been through the ringer in the IWC, and have settled on Master of the Ring IV as a place to end their feud entirely. If you want to see bad blood, if you want to see brutality... just watch this match. It's two guys proving themselves at its finest.
6) RETIREMENT MATCH Valtharius the Mad vs. Nick Demola - This is the big sendoff for the great Valtharius the Mad. And against his own best wishes, Nick Demola has decided to go ahead and wrestle on the card against one of his archrivals. The last matchup between these men ended in a controversial win for Demola a little less than a year ago. Now the feud is rekindled in Valtharius's final match. Can he go out with a win?
7) MotR IV TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP MATCH - TRIPLE JEOPARDY MATCH Final Two Teams from Eight Team Elimination Match earler - The two teams who survive the eight team elimination tag match will have to battle in a match formerly dubbed a "Demola Rules Match". There will be no tagging in or out, all men (or women) in the ring at the same time. There are three ways to win: Knockout, Submission, or by tying your opponent up in the leather strap hanging 15 feet above the ring and dragging them to all four corners. To get to the strap you must use a ladder. Only tables, ladders, and chairs are allowed. This is the matchup that the WWF stole a variation of for their tag teams... but nobody does it like MotR.
8) FOUR ON FOUR ELIMINATION TEAM SUPERBOUT MATCH "Iron Man" Eddie Jacobs, "Cold Blooded" Chris Courtade, Strictly Business vs. "Top Dog" Rick Styles, Doc Holliday, "Crimson" Joe Reed, "The Last
American Badass" Alex Martinez
- The first ever Team SuperBout Title will be determined here. The Sun has nothing on the heat in this match. The four men who have been announced on one side comprise four of the top names in the history of wrestling. Bar none. The four men on the other side of the ring are just as respected and are also four of the top names in the history of wrestling. To call this a barnburner would be an underestimation. This is gonna burn the whole fucking farm down.
9) MASTER OF THE RING IV FINAL - MASTER'S CELL MATCH The three winners of the Semi-Finals - The match that is the signature match of Master of the Ring. A cage completely encloses the ring and ringside area. There are four tables set up, one on each side of the outside of the ring. There are two ladders set up in opposite corners. No weapons allowed. Elimination style. In the end there will be only one.
15 Questions (in order of their place on the card): 1. Who the HELL is "Kichigaijimita" Kuroi Tenshi? The man under a mask has been destroying people in the weeks leading up the event... but nobody knows who he really is. Will we find out?
2. Can Mitchell Prose get some sleep? Can The Wasp finally win MotR? Prose's insomnia seems to only have one cure... victory. But The Wasp is in the same first round match, and his story is just as compelling. From being rejected on the first two cards, to reaching the finals on MotR III... can he complete the cycle?
3. With the recent events involving Tommy Lionheart... will he actually show up for the match? Big Dick McGee has threatened Lionheart with a lawsuit if he doesn't show.
4. After Nytro pulling the wool over everyone's eyes with his "Grace Gaylord" persona, can he actually follow it up with a win? Or will Ace of
Hearts take what he says is his destiny and win the tournament?
5. Ken Curtis is being presented with an award... but rumor has it someone won't just let it rest with that. 6. Will the issues between Douglas/Parker and Power and Honor actually be settled on this card? 7. Same goes for McBaine and Lawson. Can they go out with a bang? And what the hell will their stipulations for the Ironman match be? 8. Can Robert Mula commentate with his mouth full of cookies? 9. With the dissention between teams in the tag title match, can anyone even SURVIVE to make the Tag Team final later in the night? 10. Whose career will be over? Spikyjim or Loaded? Someone's losing their career. 11. And can Valtharius the Mad finally overcome his nemesis, Nick Demola, in his final match? 12. A huge question, said simply: Who will win? Team N.E.W. or the Masters of the Ring? 13. And who will be the guest ref for that match? 14. Who will win the Master of the Ring IV tournament and join Jason Keening, Bad Eye McBaine, and Rick Styles in the ranks of tournament champions? 15. And what do the terrible twosome of Nick Demola and Frank Anthony have in store for MotR? Their goal is to ruin the card... can they get away with it?
