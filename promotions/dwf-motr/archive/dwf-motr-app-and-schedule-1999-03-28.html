NOTE: I'm not sure if the message was sent the first time I tried, so here I go again. The schedule for the four night card looks like this... Night One (DWF War of Attrition -- Unfinished Business) ------------ Full DWF Reunion Card Night Two (MOTR Qualifyers - Non DWF) ------------ Tag Team Match 1 Tag Team Match 2 Qualifyer 1 Qualifyer 2 Qualifyer 3 Qualifyer 4 Qualifyer 5 Qualifyer 6 Qualifyer 7 Night Three (MOTR First Round) -------------- Battle Royal (2 Qualify) Tag Match 1 Tag Match 2 First Round 1 First Round 2 First Round 3 First Round 4 First Round 5 First Round 6 First Round 7 First Round 8 Night Four (MOTR Final 3 Rounds) ------------- Quarter 1 Quarter 2 Quarter 3 Quarter 4 Four Way Challenge Match 1 (DWF) Four Way Challenge Match 2 (Invite) Semi 1 Semi 2 Tag Tourney Final Final There will be seven singles qualifiers and two tag qualifiers from the DWF. The Battle Royal on Night Three will be for all the guys who lost in their qualifying matches to take part in. Roleplaying wouldn't begin for another couple weeks. There would be a roleplay show sent out twice a week on Tuesdays and Fridays, so only 2 rp's a week will be needed. The card will probably not take place until late May/early June, so I'd have tons of time to write these matches and make it the biggest pay per view of all time.
