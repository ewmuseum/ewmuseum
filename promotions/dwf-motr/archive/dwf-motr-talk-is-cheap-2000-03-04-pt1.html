It's pitch dark out, except for a lamp post which illuminates the parking lot and some surrounding lush green grass and bushes. There is a cherry red, 1969 Corvette Stingray parked alone in the lot. Mark Marleau stands beside the driver's side door of the car with his hand in his pocket, apparently searching for keys. A second later he pulls out the keys and opens the door. Just as he is about to step into the car, another man walks up. DWF Ringside Announcer Stuart
Jennings reaches out his hand to Marleau...]
SJ(solemn smile): Long time no see Mark... [Judging by the look on Stu's face, he doesnt really know what to expect. He does indeed know the man he has approached, but he also knows that knowing this man makes him even more unpredictable...]
[Marleau looks down at Stuart's hand, and then back up to his face...He reaches out his hand and shakes Stu's...]
MM: How ya been Stu? [The look on Stuart's face becomes even more puzzled. The man who stands infront of him is one of the most dangerous men in wrestling.
He has no friends, and hundreds of bitter enemies...yet he just shook hands with him. Who's the crazy one?]
SJ: I'm breathing...how bout you? How's the wounds? You looked alright out there tonight to me...
[Marleau can see the uncertainty in Stuart's eyes. He knows exactly why it's there, it's nothing new, and yet Marleau has even suprised himself tonight. He wonders why he hasnt snapped at Stu, but even still he doesnt...]
MM: I'm alright...legs a little sore... [Marleau lifts his pantleg up a bit showing a white cast that fuses his leg and foot together...]
MM: But I'll be alright for next week... [Stuart begins to calm down. However, the feeling of wonderment does not escape him as he looks at the large man. Six foot two and one half inches, two hundred and forty pounds of incredible athleticism, with the shortest fuse he's ever seen.]
SJ: Big match you missed tonight...see that new kid Waldrop? Nineteen years old...pretty impressive...
MM: Some would think so... SJ: Not you though I suppose... [Marleau just smiles...] SJ: He had some unpleasant words for you though... MM: They all do Stu...they all do... SJ: What...Mark Marleau at a loss for words? Never thought I'd see the day... [Marleau smiles again...] MM: You want me to give the kid some cheap heat Stu? Tell him that he's too young? Tell him that he's good, but he's not great, because to be great you gotta beat great and until you beat Mark Marleau you havent done that?
That's what he wants Stuey...that's what they all want, hell that's what _I_ wanted when I was his age...
SJ: You talk like you're an old man...what are you, Twenty Nine? MM: Twenty Eight...I've been around...I've already given him something to laugh about just by saying his name...
SJ: That you have...but if not to trash talk and trade insults...then what IS Marleau's purpose here?
MM: To win Stu... SJ: But you've done plenty of that already... MM: It's what I do best...I'm not here to teach anybody anything... I'm not the great superstar returning to give something back to the sport who's done such good for him...
I'm not the wise man come back to teach the new generation how to survive... And I'm not here to put some chump kid in the spotlight just because he had one lucky match.
SJ: I see.. MM: The thing you people have to understand is that I've worked for every last thing I have. I've scuffled and I've struggled just to be viewed as the best by some and average by others. So if Mike Waldrop or whatever the hell his name is has what it takes to even get close to my mountain peak...well I guess I'll see him when he sees me then wont I?
[Stuart takes the hint and changes the subject...] SJ: What about Lockheart? Did you mean to help him out? Or did he just happen to be there when you got a small taste of revenge...
MM: He was just there Stu... SJ: Well, reports are saying that he's not too happy about it... MM: Oh? SJ: Yeah...claims he didnt need your help... MM: Maybe he didnt, who cares? I didnt intend to help the sorry son of a bitch...he just happened to be there. If I was him, lord knows I wouldn’t be happy either, but that's sometimes the way things happen.
If Vincent Lockheart has a problem with Mark Marleau, that's perfectly fine, he wouldnt be the first, but it wouldnt take much for me to drop him like the snivling sack of shit he is...
SJ: Most people think that stepping into a feud with Vincent Lockheart is like walking through the valley of the shadow of death...
MM: Maybe it is Stu...maybe it is... SJ: So what do you plan to do? MM: I plan to strut right on through the damn "valley of the shadow of death"...you know why Stu?
SJ: Why? MM: Cause I'm the meanest son-of-a-bitch in the whole damn valley. [With that, Marleau gets in the car and drives off.] Sent via Deja.com http://www.deja.com/ Before you buy.
