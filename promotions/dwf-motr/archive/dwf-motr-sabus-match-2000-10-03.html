Sabu's Match (aka — You Want It? You Got It!)

Oct 3, 2000, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
NOTE: I just wrote this in 15 minutes. Consider this my one and only reply to Sabu's months of stupid posts. DWF for life. http://DWFrasslin.tripod.com/ Our first card since returning is out on Saturday. Get on the mailing list. We pick up the action live, as K-Dogg and Midian are in the ring awaiting their opponents...
SJ: Hello folks! This is the voice of the Darkside Wrestling Federation, Stuart Jennings. And I'm alongside the man who just doesn't know when to quit, and probably should before he gets fired,
Juan Vasquez.
JV: Very funny quip, Stu. Any funnier and you'll overload your brain. SJ: Another poignant analysis from Mister Vasquez. JV: You don't need to go to the big words, Stu. SJ: Well, there's no big words to describe what we're about to see here. K-Dogg and Midian, after weeks of publicly begging the president of the DWF, Nick Demola, for a slot in the DWF are now getting their tryout match.
JV: If they win, I quit. SJ: You promise? I might have to actually root for these guys then. JV: Root for them. You'll be the only one doing it. SJ: True. If you want proof of how deep in the crapper pro wrestling has gone evidence is standing right in that ring.
[K-Dogg stands in the ring, jumping up and down like an idiot, while
Midian stands next to him adjusting himself.]
SJ: I give these guys about zero chance here. JV: Less than zero. And if they win... SJ: If they win then they join the DWF. JV: Oh God. SJ: Let's go to Mitchell Harris Butler for the announcements of these men's opposition tonight!
MHB: Ladies and gentlemen, this match is set for one fall! The referee for the match is Paul Pierce!
[The lead DWF official waves to the crowd from a corner of the ring.] MHB: In the ring to my right... the men who are attempting to become a part of the DWF roster... K-DOGG AND MIDIAN!!!
[The crowd gives a slight booing to the two men as they jump around the ring.] SJ: No heat for these guys. JV: No surprise there. MHB: And their opponents... #Looooooooooooooooooong...# #...Neck bottle# MHB: First, hailing from Dallas, Texas, at 6'6, 300 pounds...he is "THE KING OF HARDCORE" FRANK ANTHONY!!!
#Let go of my hand# #Hey jukebox don't start playing that song again# #'Cause there's a girl at home who loves me, # #You know she won't understand # #Long neck bottle, # #Let go of my hand# [Frank comes out holding a bottle of Jack Daniels in his left hand. He takes a gulp of JD, and then when people go to reach out for his hand, he grabs it and pours the alcohol on it. Eventually, people almost get riotous reaching with their hands...]
#Hey barroom mirror on the wall# #Go stare at someone else # #Don't show the world # #The fool I am# #Just keep it to yourself # #Long neck bottle, # #Let go of my hand# #Hey jukebox don't start playing that song again# #'Cause there's a girl at home who loves me, # #You know she won't understand # #Long neck bottle, # #Let go of my hand# [Frank steps up to the ring apron and takes a giant swig of the alcohol, then puts the bottle in the corner of the ring.]
#Dance floor, seems you're underneath my feet# #Everywhere I turn # #I'd waltz right out of those swinging doors, # #But that step I just can't learn # #Long neck bottle, # #Let go of my hand # #Hey jukebox don't start playing that song again# #'Cause there's a girl at home who loves me, # #You know she won't understand # #Long neck bottle, # #Let go of my hand# #'Cause there's a girl at home who loves me, # #You know she won't understand # #Long neck bottle, # #Let go of my hand# [Frank goes in the ring and points at a young guy in the crowd. He then finds a youngin' in the crowd, grabs the bottle... and starts pouring the hard liquor on him! You hear the parents squealing as Frank
Anthony jumps in the ring and squats in the corner, ready for his partner's entrance.] MHB: And his partner... [Soft violin music starts playing.] [Hard drum beats kick in from out of nowhere as Don Henley’s “The Garden of Allah” starts blaring.] MHB: Hailing from Verandia III… weighing in at 285 pounds… here is LORD RAPHIO!!! #He said "Now that I have your attention #I got somethin' I wanna say #You may not want to hear it #I'm gonna tell it to you anyway #You know, I've always liked you, boy #'Cause you were not afraid of me #But things are gonna get mighty rough #Here in Gomorrah-By-The-Sea" #He said "It's just like home #It's so damned hot, I can't stand it #My fine seersucker suit is all soakin' wet" #And all the hills are burning #The wind is raging #And the clock strikes midnight #In the Garden of Allah [The lights of the arena flicker on and off quickly… and Lord Raphio, complete in his red overcoat with flaming red, long hair stands in the middle of the ring. He stares a hole through his opponents and cuts the music off with a wave of his hand.]
LORD RAPHIO AND FRANK ANTHONY VS. K-DOGG AND MIDIAN - DWF Tryout Match [DING! DING! DING!] SJ: I fear for their lives. JV: I don’t. I just want them dead. And the two men that Nick Demola has chosen to represent the DWF are known for putting people out of action for a very long time.
SJ: He has brought Lord Raphio out of over a yearlong retirement for this matchup. And Frank Anthony hasn’t been seen in many months after his win over Jeremy Locke at Total Annihilation I in April. That match took a lot out of him. You have to wonder if these two men are going to be at their peaks.
JV: Lord Raphio is always at his peak. And Frank Anthony has never been at a peak of anything… except a buzz when he’s drunk. And he sure looks loaded tonight!
[Raphio stands in the ring as K-Dogg and Midian charge Frank Anthony. They beat on The King of Hardcore in the corner as Raphio just stands in the middle of the ring, looking at them.]
SJ: What is Raphio doing? JV: He doesn’t seem to be all too interested in getting involved in this matchup, that’s for sure.
[K-Dogg and Midian grab Frank Anthony, one arm apiece. They whip him right into Lord Raphio… who stops Anthony from hitting him by lifting him in the air by his throat!]
SJ: RAPHIO IS TURNING! JV: He really IS evil if he wants these two idiots in the DWF! [Raphio gently sets Frank Anthony down.] JV: Thank God! No turn here! SJ: I swear. I know Lord Raphio is evil, but that would be just plain wrong. [Raphio pats Frank on the back and points to the outside of the ring. Frank slides out under the bottom rope as Raphio shoots a glare at K-
Dogg and Midian.] SJ: Oh God. Oh God. JV: God has nothing to do with this. This is going to be an unholy sacrifice! [As Frank Anthony pulls two tables out from under the ring, K-Dogg and
Midian attack Raphio! They deliver punches and boots to the stomach and head of Raphio… but to no affect! Raphio just laughs as they hit him with all their power!]
SJ: It’s like Lord Raphio is just wrestling a couple of little children! JV: He’s not showing them any respect right now! Have I mentioned how much I missed this guy?
SJ: In the right context he’s amazing. In the wrong context… he’s just sick. [The two men start to pant from punching themselves out… and Raphio grabs each man by the face! He lifts them both high in the air overhead!]
SJ: WHAT STRENGTH! What INHUMAN strength here! JV: Of course it’s inhuman. It’s LORD FUCKING RAPHIO for God’s sakes! [Frank Anthony slides the tables into the ring under the bottom rope as
Raphio just SLAMS the two opponents to the mat with authority!]
SJ: Did anyone think they stood a chance? JV: Not me, Stu. Not anyone with half a brain. SJ: I heard there was one internet reporter who thought they could win…
JV: But I don’t know. Doesn’t he always talk out of his ass?
SJ: I hope he’s kidding when he reports the things he does. If not, he’s the biggest idiot I’ve ever seen.
JV: Well… not as dumb as the guy who signed K-Dogg and Midian up for this match. SJ: True. Whoever agreed to this is even stupider than that reporter. Either that or he doesn’t think much of these men.
[Raphio points to the tables as Frank sets them up, one table on one side of the ring, one table on the other. As Frank sets the tables up,
Raphio lifts both K-Dogg and Midian on his shoulders and sets them up on opposite turnbuckles.]
SJ: Oh God. What is going to happen here? JV: Something career ending hopefully! [Raphio points to Frank again, who climbs up to the top rope where K-
Dogg is sitting. Raphio climbs up to where Midian is sitting as well.]
SJ: You don’t think- JV: FLAMING DEATH AND THE HARD STUFF! They’re going to hit their finishers through those damn tables!
SJ: This could kill these men! [Frank Anthony hooks up K-Dogg in a suplex position on the top rope as
Lord Raphio lifts Midian in a Tombstone Piledriver position and stands on the top rope.]
SJ: This is brutal! JV: Finish the job, boys! Finish the job! [Both men leap off, and… SMASH!!!! SMASH!!!! … Frank Anthony puts K-Dogg HARD THROUGH THE TABLE with his superplex finisher, The Hard Stuff as Lord Raphio piledrives Midian THROUGH THE
TABLE with Flaming Death!]
SJ: That has to be it. My God. That has to be it. [A large DWF flag, showing the green logo of the Darkside Wrestling
Federation is thrown in the ring. Raphio stacks both K-Dogg and Midian side by side with each other and drapes the flag over them as he and
Frank Anthony stand on the two men.]
ONE!!!!! TWO!!!!!! THREEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEE!!!!!! [DING! DING! DING!] [The DWF’s theme song, “Money Ain’t A Thang” by Jay-Z and Jermaine
Dupri kicks on the loudspeakers as the fans chant “D-W-F!!! D-W-F!!!”]
MHB: The winners of this match… the team of FRANK ANTHONY AND LORD RAPHIO!!! [Raphio calls out to the back for something as Frank Anthony leans on the top rope and drinks some beer he got from a fan at ringside.]
SJ: Thank God! They’re out! JV: Was there ever any doubt? Did anyone think these guys were good enough to make it into the DWF?
[Two attendants from the back come out… bringing body bags with them.] SJ: Oh man. And Lord Raphio isn’t done yet! JV: Like I said, it’s not enough to win. You have to destroy your opponents to prove a point!
[Raphio opens up the bags and unceremoniously dumps both K-Dogg and
Midian in those bags.] SJ: Well, now that this is said and d- [Raphio begins to violently kick the body bags with the two men inside of them!] SJ: Hey now! This is too much! JV: It’s never too much! Never! [Raphio slings both men over his shoulders and walks out of the ring and to the back. He grabs a cameraman and tells him to follow him in the backstage area.]
SJ: What is he planning now? JV: Only God knows. No. God has nothing to do with this. [Raphio begins walking in the halls of the back area, as Frank Anthony staggers and catches up with him and speaks into the camera.]
ANTHONY: Now you boys have gone and pissed us off! Oh, we’re gonna teach you a lesson in manners and respect! Take this back to your buddies and choke on it!
[Raphio, still with K-Dogg and Midian on his shoulders, and Anthony kick open a door that says “boiler room” on it.]
SJ: Oh no. You don’t think- JV: They’re going to burn these idiots alive! YES! [Frank Anthony hops ahead and over to the large boiler in the back of the room. He opens up the grill of the boiler, revealing a gigantic flame inside of it.]
SJ: Fans, we have to get out of here! This is inhuman! JV: NO! STAY! I want these men’s deaths to be televised worldwide as a point to all the idiots who think they are some kind of smart asses.
To the boys who think that they can just run rampant and piss all over other people. To the boys who push their stupidity on others. This flame’s for YOU!
[Lord Raphio stands before the boiler with a tiny smirk on his face.] SJ: Fans, we really have to go! For Juan Vasquez this is Stuart Jennings saying goodnight! JV: NO! DON’T GO! [The camera fades to Raphio holding K-Dogg right outside of the furnace.] Sent via Deja.com http://www.deja.com/ Before you buy.
