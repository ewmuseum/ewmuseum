Selection Show Press Release

May 7, 2000, 12:00:00 AM

To: All Members of the Public and the DWF/MotR mailing list in particular Fr: The Offices of the President of the DWF, Nick Demola, and the
C.E.O. of MotR Inc., Big Dick McGee, respectively
Re: War of Attrition - Master of the Ring III Selection Show At 8:00pm eastern standard time on Tuesday, May 9th the DWF/MotR will announce the final card for the supercard titled War of Attrition -
Master of the Ring III, set to take place on July 10-13.
Stars who are already involved in the tournament include all the DWF roster, such as Southern Champion Dave Pietka, Atlantic Storm Champion
Bob Muretic, Tag Champions The Insiders, and top contenders Mike
Waldrop, Darryn LaSalle, Gabriel Demorte, Tommy Grayson, and Chris
Courtade. Also included are the stars of Master of the Ring, who will be taking part in two SuperBouts and the Master of the Ring Tournament itself.
SuperBouts include James Pillow taking on Setzer Van Strife in a ladder match and Jason Keening taking on Alex Martinez in a "Demola Rules
Match". Participants in the tournament include Rick Styles, Andrew
Tucker, Mad Goat, Simon Bach, Sabbath, Kieran Erotikka, and others.
Also included on this card is the long-awaited final of Master of the
Ring II, featuring Ricky Deathwish taking on the shocking rookie
Menelaus and EMWC/LWC superstar Bad Eye McBaine. There are only three spots left in the tournament and a total of 28 applications were turned in. Some great men will be left out, but three will make it into the elite field of 18 who will embark on the
Master of the Ring Tournament.
Who will they be? Come into #DWF on the IRC server Starchat on Tuesday, May 9th at 8:00pm eastern to find out. ps -- Refer to http://members.xoom.com/DWFrasslin/ for more information or contact [detail withheld] with any questions or if you'd like to be added to the mailing list for this event.
