Updated Total Annihilation I Lineup

The DownSouth Wrestling Federation Sunday, April 2, 2000 From Hecht Athletic Center on the campus of Miami University in Coral
Gables, Florida, outside of Miami.
Total Annihilation I – Bad Moon Risin’ MAIN EVENT DWF Southern Title Match- Master’s Cell Match- “Heavy Mental” Dave Pietka vs. “Total Wrestling Machine” Gabriel Demorte
Special Referee - Chris Courtade
- These men have been going at it for a month now. And now it comes to this, for the biggest prize in the DWF. A cage encloses the entirety of the ring and the ring area. Only one referee rules over this... and that is Chris Courtade. All types of goodies are under the ring, and one table is set up on each side of the ring. The only ways to win are via pinfall or submission in the middle of the ring. All managers, bodyguards, and valets are barred from ringside.
DWF Atlantic Storm Title Match ‘Cuff Match “Cream of the Crop” Mike Waldrop vs. “The Wildcard” Bob Muretic - THE hottest feud going right now, steeped in personal hatred. These two men have put the cuffs to use with each other so many times that they might as well fight it out with the cuffs as a major part of the match. There will be two poles on opposite corners of the ring, each with a pair of handcuffs on them and the keys to those cuffs on them as well. The cuffs are there to be used as weapons. Only tables and chairs are allowed as other weapons. Any other weapons brought in will bring an immediate forfeiture of the match and title to the opponent.
The only way to win is by pinfall or submission, or aforementioned forfeiture. Loser Leaves Town Match- Barbed Wire Weapons Match- “The King of Hardcore” Frank Anthony vs. “Nightshade” Jeremy Locke - This hatred runs so deep that Locke gave up his spot in the Southern Title Tourney to chase Frank Anthony, his own half-brother, out of wrestling for good. Now he gets his chance. Four poles stand tall, one in each corner. They each have a particular weapon on them. The only ways to win are submission or knockout. That’s it. Falls Count
Anywhere.
DWF World Tag Team Title Match Anti-Social Sublife vs. ??? - The opponents for this great young tag team are yet unnamed, but we will all find out what surprise the front office has cooked up for these rebels as the show goes on.
Havoc Hate vs. Junya “Kamikaze” Kontani - These two got into it a bit at the last BattleGround when Kontani refereed Hate’s match and showed favoritism to his opponent. Now they meet in the ring to settle their score.
“Playboy” Aidan Madison vs. Robert Mula - These two men have had TREMENDOUS showings recently. Now they meet up at Total Annihilation to see who moves up the ladder, and who comes careening down it. Sent via Deja.com http://www.deja.com/ Before you buy.
