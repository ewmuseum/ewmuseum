=================== ANTI-SOCIAL SUBLIFE (Albright and Clarkson) =================== [Two o'clock in the afternoon on the streets of Durham, New Hampshire. This small college town, covered in the purity of dirty snow and New
England Attitude, has never fit Adam Clarkson too well. When he took the invitation from Nick Demola to come to Bulldogg University and escape the path of self-destruction he was surely headed on. Purple hazey memories of a two-year party in Monterrey give him all the impetus he needs to continue.]
[But this? This setup with a man who he can't even talk to... who he doesn't want to have anything to do with. Nick Demola has a great wrestling mind and has created dozens of champions, but it must have scrambled up here. Terin Albright is his partner? In a team called Anti-
Social Sublife? This isn't the most keen opening he's ever seen, but he'll take the opening and bust through it like the good ballcarrier that he is. Terin never spoke about it... so it was up to the good soldier, Mister
Number One In The Bulldogg University Class of '99, to be the leader.]
[Approach the table with caution. Good. What is that book he's reading? And why does he wear that trenchcoat everywhere?] [Terin's eyes never budge from the paperback he holds. Thin glasses lay on top of his nose as his hair, for once, is cleared from his face.]
[A clearing of Adam's throat and still nothing.] AC: Excuse me Terin. [Still nothing moving on Terin's Albright's person, save the eyes which jet across the pages at a speed that seems to be proportional to light and a finger that flips the pages like a mindless machine.]
AC: Terin. We really do have to talk about the DWF. [Still nothing. Adam's face turns a flustered red, but he breathes in and out deeply twice and sits at the opposite end of the small, circular table from Terin.]

[Nothing.] AC: Damn it Terin! This is my big break! I don't know if Pietka's toking up a bit of that bong action or what, but he's teamed us together in this stable and for better or worse it WILL WORK! I will
