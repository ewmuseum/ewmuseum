The Darkside Wrestling Federation presents… The Exodus Tour! BATTLEGROUND Sunday, October 18, 2000 From the Kresse Center on the campus of the College of Charleston in
Charleston, South Carolina
NO DISQUALIFICATIONS - “Nightshade” Jeremy Locke vs. Ngaio Ndaiye Jeremy Locke is one of the hottest names in pro wrestling. And as the pissed off half-brother of Nick Demola, he is one of the most loved wrestlers in DWF history. The men who run the federation have put him into the first matchup of the new DWF era against a rookie, Ngaio
Ndaiye, whose wrestling skills some say are “questionable”. We will have to see how Locke deals with his “unorthodox” fighting methods in this fight.
TELEVISION TITLE MATCH - Johnny Axis vs. Sabbath The newly crowned TV Champion Johnny Axis, hot off his tournament win at Master of the Ring III, will defend the gold for the first time against one of the greats of the sport, the suicidal “Walking
Contradiction” Sabbath. Can Axis outsmart and outwrestle the unpredictable superstar? Or will Sabbath’s death defying maneuvers prove to be too much for the champ?
WORLD TAG TEAM TITLE MATCH - Desolation Row vs. Anti-Social Sublife A four year guessing game was concluded at Master of the Ring III when
FIRESTORM of Desolation Row was revealed to really be Alex Demola… longtime missing brother of Nick Demola. Now The Row are tag team champions and must take on the two most recent graduates of Nick’s prestigious Bulldogg University in Anti-Social Sublife.
ATLANTIC STORM FOURWAY CLUSTERFUCK TAG MATCH - “Wildcard” Bob Muretic/“Cream of the Crop” Mike Waldrop vs. Mark Marleau/Valtharius the Mad vs. Robert Mula/Reverend White vs. “Kodiak Cop” Maxwell Creed/Boltz Four teams of random partners will climb into the ring in a DWF original match… the Fourway Clusterfuck! One man from each team is in the ring at the same time. The first man to win a fall takes the title! Muretic and current champion Waldrop have just come off the most bitter feud in DWF history, while Mark Marleau is bitter that
Valtharius the Mad is one of the greats of all time. How will White and Mula, with their contradicting styles, be able to coexist? And
Creed and Boltz?
WORLD TITLE MATCH - “Cold Blooded” Chris Courtade vs. “Heavy Mental” Dave Pietka This feud did not come to an end with Courtade’s victory at Master of the Ring III. Courtade took the title by pinning Pietka’s stablemate… but has yet to defeat the longtime DWF Champion himself. This is the moment of truth. In the main event Courtade must prove he has what it takes, as he takes on the leader of Wicked Existenz… who must prove that he is still what he claims he is. The true DWF Champion.
