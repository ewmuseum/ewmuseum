Total Annihilation I — Bad Moon Risin' (Announcement)

Mar 13, 2000, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The first DWF supercard in two years is going to occur on Sunday, April
2nd and will be put on head-to-head with Wrestlemania. It will feature the resolution of feuds that have been long brewing, a couple of matches that promise to be absolute tear-apart brawls, and three new titleholders crowned.
Here is the lineup. Go to http://members.xoom.com/DWFrasslin for more information on the DWF. The DownSouth Wrestling Federation Sunday, April 2, 2000 From Hecht Athletic Center on the campus of Miami University in Coral
Gables, Florida, outside of Miami.
Total Annihilation I – Bad Moon Risin’ MAIN EVENT DWF Southern Title Match- Master’s Cell Match- Mark Marleau vs. “Heavy Mental” Dave Pietka vs. “Total Wrestling Machine” Gabriel Demorte - These three men have been going at it for a month now. And now it comes to this, for the biggest prize in the DWF. A cage encloses the entirety of the ring and the ring area. Three referees are in the area to make sure that nothing fishy happens. All types of goodies are under the ring. This is elimination rules, so the last man standing is the man who will win.
DWF Atlantic Storm Title Match ‘Cuff Match “Cream of the Crop” Mike Waldrop vs. “The Wildcard” Bob Muretic - THE hottest feud going right now, steeped in personal hatred. These two men have put the cuffs to use with each other so many times that they might as well fight it out with the cuffs as a major part of the match. There will be two poles on opposite corners of the ring, each with a pair of handcuffs on them and the keys to those cuffs on them as well. The cuffs are there to be used as weapons. Only tables and chairs are allowed as other weapons. Any other weapons brought in will bring an immediate forfeiture of the match and title to the opponent.
The only way to win is by pinfall or submission, or aforementioned forfeiture. Loser Leaves Town Match- Barbed Wire Weapons Match- “The King of Hardcore” Frank Anthony vs. “Nightshade” Jeremy Locke - This hatred runs so deep that Locke gave up his spot in the Southern Title Tourney to chase Frank Anthony, his own half-brother, out of wrestling for good. Now he gets his chance. Four poles stand tall, one in each corner. They each have a particular weapon on them. The only ways to win are submission or knockout. That’s it. Falls Count
Anywhere.
DWF World Tag Team Title Match Anti-Social Sublife vs. ??? - The opponents for this great young tag team are yet unnamed, but we will all find out what surprise the front office has cooked up for these rebels as the show goes on.
Havoc Hate vs. Junya “Kamikaze” Kontani - These two got into it a bit at the last BattleGround when Kontani refereed Hate’s match and showed favoritism to his opponent. Now they meet in the ring to settle their score.
“Playboy” Aidan Madison vs. Robert Mula - These two men have had TREMENDOUS showings recently. Now they meet up at Total Annihilation to see who moves up the ladder, and who comes careening down it. The MAJOR questions heading into Total Annihilation: 1) Who can rise up from the midcard in the Madison/Mula match to open the show? Both men have MORE than impressed in recent weeks with their action, but have failed to get that one big win to push them over the top. Will it happen here? What happens to the person who loses?
2) Can Junya Kontani, with a year layoff, actually challenge Havoc Hate in their match? Smart money says don't bet against Kontani. But then again, Havoc always finds a way to win. Always.
3) Who will be the tag team to face Anti-Social Sublife for the gold? SO many rumors are floating out there, and all we know is that it will be a team that is not on the roster yet. That's all. That limits it to... oh... five hundred thousand teams or so.
4) Will Locke and Anthony simply kill themselves before the match at Total Annihilation? Or will they wait to get into the ring to do it? These guys are headed for pure destruction, and whoever the winner ends up being might not be any better off than the loser who has to "leave town".
5) How will this VERY personal Waldrop and Muretic issue end? Something tells me it won't end at Total Annihilation... but it'll take a gigantic step in either direction. Whoever wins takes the Atlantic
Storm Title, but I don't really think that it matters to either man.
6) Can Pietka do it again? Dave Pietka won the three-way matchup against Demorte and Marleau on the last show... but in a cage with no help and no false entrances allowed, can he pull it off?
7) What about Chris Courtade and Vincent Lockheart? What affect will these men have on Total Annihilation? Something tells us that we're going to see what they're up to in short order.
