Time is running short to be a part of this. It's going to be a small roster to start (about 10) and then we will grow to include more.
The DownSouth Wrestling Federation is a part of the Alliance of Champions headed by the handlers of LOCO and King Apples and involving the new federation headed by the handler of Setzer Van Strife. This could be an opportunity you've been waiting for.
So get back to me if you can't fill out the app right now and tell me of your interest. Thanks.  [name withheld] The DownSouth Wrestling Federation F.A.Q. 1) Mission Statement 2) Rules 3) Cards 4) Participation a) Roleplays (amount, show) b) In-rings c) Strats d) Angles 5) How do we operate? 6) Titles 7) Fictional Faces 8) In closing Mission Statement This Federation has been designed to enhance the careers of wrestlers, provide enjoyable entertainment, and give a non-corrupt, easy going area for wrestlers and their handlers to compete in.
Based in the South region of the United States (Virginia south and Mississippi River west), the DWF is a part of the Alliance of Champions and together with its sister federations comes up with one Alliance
World Champion... someone who can truly be called the top of the wrestling world. The founder/CEO of the DWF is Nick Demola, one of the most experienced wrestlers out there. His goal, his only goal, is to have the best brand of wrestling and the best talent to participate in that brand of wrestling.
In real life terms, the fedhead is [name withheld], with an e-fed career spanning about 100 feds and three and a half years. You ask it, he knows it pretty much.
Rules The DWF will allow whatever characters you want, but be aware that no matter how "impossible" it is to beat them, if you don't roleplay your guy is dead. You may have an 8 foot monster, but be aware that I'm a prick about powerful guys and I love to knock them down.
You can only submit one singles wrestler in the DWF. Only the best characters and apps with the best quality will be chosen.
DON'T use another player's wrestler, unless there has been an OK by the other party and by the President. Furthermore, don't ask for your wrestler to hosptialize another wrestler unless there has been consent from the other party and the President.
DON'T attack announcers or any of the DWF staff, unless there has been express permission by the President. You can attack as many no name jobbers as you want.
Cards ALL matches are written by Nick Demola and take place three matches per week in a Tuesday card called "ACTV". ACTV also has the matches of the sister feds in the Alliance of Champions, as well as one match for the
Alliance World Championship. Participation Roleplays, In-rings, Strats, and Angles fall under the Participation category. Roleplays will be the deciding factor as to who wins... but the other things will definately tally points for you.
- Roleplays There is one DWF roleplay show, called "Talk is Cheap". It occurs on Friday nights at 11:59pm sharp. If you want to not job horribly, roleplay as much as possible. For any shot to win, do it every week.
They are weekly and there is no limit to how many you can do per week, though they will be graded on quality, not quantity. Use only your own characters or DWF announcers... no using other characters.
-In-rings Strongly encouraged. Don't have to be long, just have to add to the feud. Will help in your chances of winning.
-Strats Again, strongly encouraged. They will be used to their fullest extent and will help your chances of winning.
-Angles While Nick Demola will be GLAD to help with angles in the fed, it's up to you to get the ball rolling. Each person will be on a constant communication basis with the prez... communication is the key to a great fed. Likewise, any questions or any e-mail address requests will be responded to promptly.
How does the DWF operate? DWF is a ''booked'' federation. Meaning the match results are predetermined by the DWF council (basically, Nick Demola himself).
Roleplaying is the key to sucess in this supercard. Angles play a big part too, but if you roleplay better, you win. Period. Strats will have a minor effect and are used more as an outline for how a handler wants the match to run and how a handler wants his wrestler to get put over by the writer.
Titles DWF DownSouth Title - The main title of the federation. The holder of this title is the top man in the DWF.
DWF Atlantic Storm Title - The secondary title of the federation. Relaxed rules and loose DQ's are the stipulations for any matches for this championship. Also, there is always a special circumstance to every Atlantic Storm title match (such as a ladder match, cage match, etc.). Title will be determined at a later date.
Fictional Faces and Places DWF Founder/President: Nick Demola DWF Vice President/Financier: Big Dick McGee (not an active DWF wrestler, and doesn't show up often, but if you punk him around there will be consequences.)
DWF Backstage Interviewer: "Scheme" Gene Dunderhead DWF Ringside Announcers: Stuart Jennings - Face/Jim Ross-type Matt Mitchell - Color Man, Favors nobody... just good wrestling Juan Vasquez - Heel/Jerry Lawler-type DWF Ring Announcer: Mitchell Harris Butler In closing (out of character): I'm putting a lot of pressure on myself to make sure that this federation is the best damn federation on the planet. And I can damn well guarantee that you're going to have one of the best experiences of your careers here. If you're in the fed, always know that I will answer any mail, any question you have at any time.
Thanks.
