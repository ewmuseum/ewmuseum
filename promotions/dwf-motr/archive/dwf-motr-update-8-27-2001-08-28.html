DWF UPDATE 8/27 1) Mail to all 2) Roster 3) Next Card 4) Last Card 5) Next Supercard 6) Current Rankings 7) Weekly Awards 8) Handler Reminders (rp/strat deadlines) 9) Website and E-Mail Info 1) (ooc) MAIL TO ALL It feels so good to reopen. I love this fed. Remember to send me all the rps and strats to [detail withheld] by Sunday September 2nd. I've divided up the singles wrestlers into three eight-man groups. These groups will be fighting each other for the next three weeks in qualifiers to reach the World Title semis and finals at the pay per view. This gives people a chance to start some feuds, get some heat, and get acquainted with the system. Also, tag team qualifiers will begin next week. To get everyone introduced to the fed, I've included one member of all five tag teams in the DWF into the qualifiers. If you're a tag handler, and you don't want to advance in the singles tournament whatsoever and want to focus on tags, put that in the strat. If you want to take a risk and move forward in the singles area, that's fine too. If you deserve to move on, you will whether you're a tag wrestler or singles unless you state you don't want to. Which I completely understand. And the site will be fully updated with wrestler profiles later today. Which reminds me... remember, cards come out every two weeks and all roleplays will be put on the cards. Also, matchwriters are cool people and I dig them a lot. I love when people volunteer to write. :-) The card is below. We rule.
