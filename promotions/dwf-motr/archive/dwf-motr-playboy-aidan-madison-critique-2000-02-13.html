"Playboy" Aidan Madison (Critique)

Feb 13, 2000, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
RSPWF, This is my intro flash into Nick Demola's DWF. He plainly put to make it short and to the point, for this is just a little thing to show the other members of the DWF who we are and everything. Enjoy!
--------------------------------------------------------------------- -[ . . . A picture of "Playboy" Aidan Madison fades into the screen. It shows him standing victorious over a falling opponent in a past federation. A new picture comes into view showing the same thing, then another, and another. The picture begins to go by so fast that you can't really make out what they are off. . . ]-
-[ . . . Suddenly, the theme music to the show "Rugrats" begins to play. A picture of Angelica from "Rugrats" comes onto the screen and stay there. From the corner of the screen you see "Playboy" Aidan Madison walk on. He is wearing a Blue, Old Navy Tek Vest, cargo shorts, sandels, and his famous Nike Golf Cap that never seems to leave his head. He looks at Angelica and smiles. He then looks at the camera and begins to speak. . . ]-
[Aidan Madison]: "This sucks! I got a new home for myself, the DWF, but no girlfriend to come along with me! I mean, dude, why won't Angelica come out from hiding and admit her true feelings for me? I know deep down inside that she likes me, and I _KNOW_ that she knows that I'm totally in love with her.
Only if I knew." "Anyways, President Dude of the DWF has told me to cut a little promo to introduce myself to the DWF. So, umm, let me introduce myself. I'm "Playboy"
Aidan Madison, I like Angelica from Rugrats, and I wrestle. Well, I guess the fact that I wrestle is kind of apparent, so let me think of something different."
-[ . . . Aidan Madison looks into space and ponders for about 5 minutes. At about this time, the television producers cuts him off the air and go to something else. What a way to introduce yourself. . . ]-
[name withheld] Handler Of [ "Playboy" Aidan Madison ] - [DWF] -[0-0-0]- #12 Best Rookie on RSPWF's Mid-Year Awards "I had every intention of not letting another man into the fed, but I like your roleplay and you've got a neat character." - Nick Demola
