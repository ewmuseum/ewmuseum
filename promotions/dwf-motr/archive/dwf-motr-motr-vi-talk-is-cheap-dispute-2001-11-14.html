MotR VI: The Talk Is Cheap Dispute, November 2001

14–15 November 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
LOL. This is stupid how Andy's flash was the only one singled at. Trying to turn this situation into an EMWC vs. AVW thing is downright retarded, so please let those who are trying to provoke another such argument.. just let it die. The fact is, in the five plus years that I've been doing this, I've noticed that the best flashes always have a hint of OOC comments behind them, because that's what get handlers to react. Now, is Kinsey were to come out and say "Jase is a fuckin' tool", that, of course, wouldn't be appropriate. But if Luke Kinsey as a character wants to disrespect AVW, then that's fair game. Afterall, he's a _wrestler_... and they're inclined to disrespect their competition. One of the funniest Rock lines IMO was when he mocked Jericho for competing with "Juventud"... it's a similar situation here. If the AVW people (this is not a generalization. I'm referring only to the people who brought this topic up, and am in no way blaming AVW as a fed) want to try and warp this into another fed war, then they're just leeching off of a topic that they shouldn't. Not everyone in the world is against AVW, no matter how nice it would be to believe. Not everyone in the world is for AVW, either though, and the amount of those indifferent (myself included) is extremely high. But, don't go out and ruin the MOTR tournament over something as trivial as a roleplay. This tournament doesn't need controversy, and Andy shouldn't be getting ragged on for writing that roleplay. It was completely appropriate... and this is coming from someone's who both written, and been on the receiving end of, inappropriate things. It's the last MOTR... let us all enjoy it. If some of you have a problem with Andy's work, hit him up in e-mail, and I'm pretty sure he won't mind. Peace. ~Maz Handler of: 'Vindicator' V.D. Vitrone [MOTR].
