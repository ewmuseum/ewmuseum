Master of the Ring VIII Lineup

Aug 3, 2005, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
HELLOOOOOOOOOOO E-WRESTLING AFFICIANADOS! Master of the Ring VIII's lineup is about to be announced, but before that... let's go over a couple things.
A) The website is down. I mean, it's up... but my server won't let me get to it. So ignore anything on there. B) I was waiting on a couple things, but I decided to say fuck all to that and just release the lineup. Strongest top to bottom in MotR history, methinks. NIGHT ONE MotR VIII First Round - Scott Starring vs. Oeste Neblinoso, Jr. - One member of one of the best tag teams out there today (and maybe one of the best of all time), Scott Starring tries to see his way to his dream of being a singles superstar. He meets up with a bonafide legend from Mexico trying to break into the United States in Oeste Neblinoso Jr. Both men feel they have something to prove, and feel the need to break free from preconceived notions of their skills. Neither man will want to slip up in the first round in what might be the most exciting match of the round.
MotR VIII First Round - Adam Mitchell vs. Rage McLane - The dastardly Adam Mitchell returns to the ring to show that his underground legend status is well-earned. He faces off against a man who is double tough, but still green in the ring in Rage McLean. Can Mitchell make a triumphant return to the ring? Can McLane defeat the demons that threaten to devour him?
MotR VIII First Round - Tommy Danger vs. Stevie Erickson - Tommy Danger, the man ranked as the number one wrestler of the year last year, steps into an MotR ring for the first time. He is the top name in the game, and history tells us that men with that position usually fare well at
MotR events (see: Thunder, Brody; Styles, Rick; Courtade, Chris). Stevie
Erickson just wants a shot to make himself a star, and he's getting the biggest chance at it right off the bat.
MotR VIII First Round - "The Rich One" Steve Greedy vs. "Sex Pistol" Jaime Roberts - Two veterans of Demola-operated promotions and tournaments past step into the ring for this one in Steve Greedy and Jaime Roberts. Both men have earned reputations as top names in the game since the last time we saw them, taking their previous experiences and using them as catapults to stardom.
Now they look to take the next step to the top by winning the Master of the
Ring Tournament in what may be the most hotly contested first round match of them all.
MotR VIII First Round - "Vile" Vince Viper vs. "Outlaw" Morris Bass - The aging, yet extremely dangerous, Vince Viper returns to MotR for the first time since his agonizingly sick Tanning Bed Death Match against Vandal
Gomez that almost cost MotR its pay-per-view license. He meets the often-underrated and always double tough Morris Bass, who brings a laundry list of credentials with him to the ring. Two balls-out tough veterans clawing for a chance at the MotR title.
MotR VIII First Round - El Savaje vs. "Suicide" Spike Nelson - This one's just going to be insane. The smallest, and (possibly along with Viper) the most sadistic man in the tournament, El Savaje, steps into the ring with the 19 year old athletic phenom Spike Nelson. Savaje wants nothing less than the destruction of his opponents. Nelson wants nothing less than to become a star. There will be blood, there will be violence.
Will it be the crowd pleaser or the gut queaser who gets the duke and moves on? Grudge Match of the Century - CAGE MATCH Nick Demola vs. "Top Dog" Rick Styles - What? What is this you say? Demola has announced a match against Rick Styles for Master of the Ring VIII, though many can't believe Styles would sign on to compete in a Demola-operated event after their heated exchanges over the years. There must be something more to this.
Team SuperBout VIII - MotR VIII Tag Title Match - The Outlaws vs. The Ghost Dancers - Night One ends with a classic battle between two teams of people who hate each other. Brent Maverick and Doc Holliday comprise one of the great teams of all-time in The Outlaws. Of course, on the other side of the ring is a team that can rival them in greatness, Patrick and Jason Keening, The Ghost
Dancers. The question is, who will end up on top in this blood feud? And will Holliday overcome his MotR jinx, or will Jason Keening keep up his stellar record at MotR events?
NIGHT TWO SPECIAL CHALLENGE MATCH! Participants will be announced on NIGHT ONE! MotR VIII Standing Six - Starring/Neblinoso Jr. Winner vs. Mitchell/McLane Winner MotR VIII Standing Six - Danger/Erickson Winner vs. Greedy/Roberts Winner MotR VIII Standing Six - Viper/Bass Winner vs. Savaje/Nelson Winner Pentagon Pain IV - For the MotR VIII Cruiserweight Title - "Lonewolf" David Rheaume vs. "Hardcore Pop Star" Brandon Spears vs. Junya
"Kamikaze" Kontani vs. "King of the Streets" Paolo Ruas vs. Jonah "Ice"
Keller vs. Scimitar - Rheaume is the younger brother of Nick Demola, and has already lost in one Pentagon Pain match. Spears and Ruas are both promising indy stars who have just not broken through. Keller is the most crafty, disturbed man in this match. Kontani is the superstar here... the one playing the role that Bilal
Masai and November did on their way to victory in previous Pentagon Pain matches. And Scimitar? He has only one win in his ENTIRE career... but the most devastating finisher in wrestling. You can never count anyone out in what is sure to be another insane spotfest from HELL! Six men, five losers, all bringing one weapon to the ring apiece. You can't miss this!
SuperBout VIII - IRONMAN MATCH "Cool Hand" Luke Kinsey vs. "2 Smooth" Setzer Van Strife - In what will be the legendary Van Strife's final match ever, he takes on MotR VI winner Kinsey for the first time. The respect these men have shown each other leading up to the signing of this match is matched only by their overpowering need to claim final victory. For Kinsey, a win over Van Strife in his going-away match would solidify his legendary status. And for SVS, there is only one way to end a great career... with a win. Who comes out on top will be determined in the match that ONLY MotR does right, the 60 Minute
Ironman Match.
MotR VIII Final - TRIPLE THREAT MASTER'S CELL MATCH Standing Six Winners - The semi-final winners will take part in a match that has created memorable moments, and created legends as well. A cell with tables on either side and chairs in all four corners. Three men. Pinfalls and submissions only. No DQs. Only one survives to join the ranks of Keening,
McBaine, Styles, Hill, Von Braun, Kinsey, and Frost as Master of the Ring!
