Master of the Ring III

Apr 29, 2000, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
To apply for MotR III, please visit the website at http://members.xoom.com/DWFrasslin The four day card is just getting formed. Roleplaying will occur on four weekly shows starting on May 26. The actual card will be from July 10-13. Here's the card, complete with the superstars who have already been signed. DWF War of Attrition - Master of the Ring III Day 1 - 1) Nick Demola vs. Big Dick McGee (for the final rights to the DWF name and Commissionership over the ppv) TV Title First Round First To Pin Wins Matches (Robert Mula and Frank Anthony are in the second round already): 2) “Playboy” Aidan Madison vs. Brian "Thunder" Storm vs. Cyric Logan 3) Jim "THE CRUSHER" Johnson vs. “Hollywood Starfucker” Levi Ash vs. Dillon De Stride 4) El Sangre del Noche vs. Wes "The Eliminator" Coleman vs. Spikyjim 5) Mark Marleau vs. Havoc Hate vs. Frank "The Falcon" Peregrine DWF/MotR Tag Title Elimination Match - 6) The Eclipse (MotR Tag Champs) vs. The Insiders (DWF Tag Champs) vs. Anti-Social Sublife vs. The Kings of Hardcore Day 2 - 1-6) Six threeway first round Master of the Ring III First To Pin Wins matches. Already signed are Andrew "Flash" Tucker, Wasp, and Mad Goat. 15 spots remain so apply NOW! Day 3 - 1-2) Semifinal TV Tourney Matches: Mula vs. Winner 1 vs. Winner 2, Anthony vs. Winner 3 vs. Winner 4 3-4) Three one-on-one second round MotR III matches 5) Weapons On Four Poles Match - Levi Ash vs. Jeremy Locke (no matter WHAT Ash does in the TV Title Tourney) 6) Crucifixion Match - Morgan De Sade vs. Vincent Lockheart vs. mystery man... Day 4 - 1) 3-way Television Title Final 2) DWF Atlantic Storm Title Match - Bob Muretic vs. Mike Waldrop vs. Darryn LaSalle 3) MotR II Final - Master's Cell Match - Ricky Deathwish vs. Menelaus vs. Bad Eye McBaine 4) MotR III Final - Master's Cell Match 5) DWF Southern Title Match - Dave Pietka vs. Tommy Grayson vs. Gabriel Demorte vs. Chris Courtade 6) SuperBout I - Ladder Match - James Pillow vs. Setzer Van Strife 7) SuperBout II - Demola Rules Match - Jason Keening vs. Alex Martinez 8) Ultimate Master/DWF World Title Match - MotR III winner vs. MotR II winner vs. Southern Champ vs. Atlantic Storm Champ vs. Television Champ - STAGGERED ENTRY, NO DQ. TV champ and Atlantic Storm champ start. Two minutes later the MotR II winner enters. Then the MotR III winner. Then the Southern Champion. Only after everyone is in the ring can the eliminations begin. Sent via Deja.com http://www.deja.com/ Before you buy.
