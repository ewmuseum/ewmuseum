I don't ordinarily give out info ahead of time. Two reasons for that. First, I want people to come in based on if they want to. If I release names ahead of time, people might get scared off if it's good competition, might come in if they feel it's easy competition, or something else. Second, I like the suspense of it all. But I'll make an exception here and mention a couple names in the tag scene. Girlz on Film, Suicide Blondes, the Alpha Brothers, the Crazed Animals, the Kings of Hardcore, and the staple of MotR tag action... the two time tag champs Desolation Row. I want to see who's reading this post, so I'm going to give you an idea of what the tag scene is going to look like this time around. I always try to invite as many teams as possible. I think that you can measure the greatness of a fed by the time they take, and the talent they get, for a tag division. So every time out I invite eight teams, and they take part in various multi-team matches to determine the MotR tag champs. This time I want to invite twelve. How's this for a concept? BattleGround Arena is being built by MotR in Verdun, France. One of the cool innovations to this wrestling-only arena is that it will have the capability of keeping two pre-set rings underground, right under the center of the arena floor. So when we need to run a multiple-ring match, the other rings will simply rise above ground for the matches, and drop down when the matches are over. Yes, it takes a bit of a suspension of disbelief. But this IS the first ever wrestling-only arena. So it could happen. Anyways, back to the point. These other rings will be raised up for a three ring, 12 team matchup to determine who moves on into the four man mini-tournament for the MotR Tag Titles. The 12 teams will be randomly split into three groups of four. Those groups will have an assigned ring, and assigned corners for each team in that ring. During the match, one man from each team will be the legal man in the ring. All participants will be able to move freely between rings, trying to gain a pinfall or submission on any other legal man in the match. Now, the reason that each team is assigned to a ring, and a specific corner, is that the only way you can tag your partner is in your assigned corner. So, for example, if you're in Ring #1, and you look way over yonder at Ring #3 and see someone who's unconscious, and you want to pin them... by all means, go ahead and try. But if you get caught in Ring #3, you're going to be two rings away from making a tag, thereby putting your team in jeopardy of losing. The ways to advance to the mini-tourney are simple. You have to score a pinfall or submission on any other legal man in the match. Once you score that pinfall or submission, you are in the mini-tourney and you and your teammate can leave. Also, the team that gets pinned/submitted is eliminated from the match and their time at MotR V is done. What it boils down to is this... 12 teams in the match, then the First pinfall/submission happens, and the two teams involved in the fall leave. One moves on to the mini-tourney, one is done for the show. Thus making 10 teams left over. 10 teams in the match, then the second pinfall/submission happens, and the two teams leave. 8 left over. 8 in the match, third pinfall/submission, two teams leave, six left. And now you've got six teams left... all fighting in three different rings (and keeping their assigned corners for tags in mind). These six teams are fighting for the last pinfall to reach the four team mini tournament. That'll be chaos. When the match is over, the way the mini-tourney will go is this. First team to score a fall vs. the fourth team to score a fall. Second team vs. third team. Then the winners of those matches fight in that MotR staple, the Triple Jeopardy Tag Team Match, which I'll describe later. Yes. This will be a BITCH to write. I figure that I'm going to have an army of referees (maybe even lumberjacks) to keep double teaming and illegal entry to a minimum. In fact, that'll be the only way to get disqualified... if you enter the ring illegally and a ref catches you, you're out of the match. Nobody gets credit for beating you, and the field dwindles down by one. I'm figuring that this match is going to be a damn fiasco. Which is why I'm calling it... Tag Team Fiasco. Go fig.
