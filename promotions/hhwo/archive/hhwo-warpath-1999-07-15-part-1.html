Warpath! (15 July 1999), Part 1

16 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The screen shows nothing but total darkness as "Nocturnal" by Mortification begins to play. Just as the serene background noise of crickets and other nocturnal creatures is heard, a deep and mature male voice begins to speak...]
VO: "Some say that there is such a place in existence, where heroes are born..." [Suddenly after those brief words two thick thumping notes of bass drum and guitar are played together as a quick video clip of "Super Rad" Billy Kane nailing an unsuspecting opponent with his devestating finishing manuever, "The Smash Hit". The clip quickly disappears as fast as it came as the noise of the crickets and nocturnal creatures becomes louder and the man continues to speak...]
VO: "...and legends are made." [Once again the thunderous boom of the synchronous bass drum and guitar pounds twice, and another video clip quickly appears, this time showing a beaten and fatigued man, "The
Showstopper" Simon Lebec, holding the HHWO World Title above his head in sheer glory. Once again the slip quickly dissipates as the noise of the creatures begins to fade into the background and the voice continues to speak...]
VO: "A place where both evil..." [The growing volume of the tremenous bass drum and guitar banging out another two heavy notes plays once again as a video clip of "2 Smooth" Setzer Van Strife executing his infamous "Smoothsault" finishing manuever onto an unfortunate and broken opponent now crippled face down on the mat. The video clip, like the other, quickly fades away back to nothing but total darkness and the sound of the unknown man's voice...]
VO: "...and good..." [As the calming sound of the crickets and other small creatures is eliminated, the sound of the ground-shaking bass drum and guitar continues to swell louder and more furious, coupled with another short video clip of "Spirit Warrior"
Jin Tao nailing an unsuspecting opponent with the "Tao Special". The clip once again fades quickly, but now another heavy guitar riff begins to play with the deep bass drum and guitar...]
VO: "...brutally compete inside of the squared circle. Such a place does exist..." [As the video begins to fade, "Headup" by the Deftones erupts over the PA system. The camera begins to pan around the sold-out Gund Arena in Cleveland, Ohio, while blue, and silver fireworks and pyrotechnics explode throughout the arena.
The fans are standing on their feet, screaming in excitement and holding up their homemade signs. As the camera continues to make it's way around the arena, various signs can be seen more clearly than the others...one fan imparticular is holding up a white sign with gold letters saying "WELCOME BACK HHWO!", while another fan holds up another white sign with "R.I.P SIMON
LEBEC!" in red letters...] [The ring is decorated with a custom blue, black, and silver
HHWO ring canvas, with "WARPATH" ablaze in big bold letters in the center of the canvas. The black entrance ramp is adorned with a blue, silver and black flaming Rebirth logo, while a blue and silver large neon Warpath sign hangs above the wrestler's black entrance curtains. The camera continues to pan around the arena, glancing at the enormous videotron and finally making it's way over to the ringside commentator's table where Lance Marshall and the infamous Marcus Haynes are sitting...]
[Lance is a well-mannered and mild-tempered middle-aged man wearing a pair of thin reading glasses to correct his somewhat impaired vision. The spotlight from above seems to gleam on the top of his balding head, as well as his wide smile. Lance is attired in a grey polo shirt with a blue and silver HHWO logo on the pocket, while his brown pants and black dress shoes are somewhat hidden by the table. Marcus on the other hand, is an egotistical and ignorant man who seems to enjoy getting on people's nerves. Overwhelmingly not a fan-favorite,
Marcus smiles from ear to ear, soaking in every last boo the fans throw at him. Marcus' long, thin, black hair drapes across his back while he scratches at his blue-colored goatee. He is casually dressed in a black t-shirt with a blazing red and gold HHWO logo on it, and a pair of blue jeans and tennis shoes. Both men smile as Lance begins to speak...]
LM: LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, WELCOME TO THE SOLD-OUT GUND ARENA IN CLEVELAND, OHIO, WHERE YOU WILL WITNESS THE RETURN OF THE HOMETOWN HEROES WRESTLING ORGNANIZATION!! WELCOME TO HHEO WARPATH!!!! [The deafening pop coming from the crowd overwhelms Lance's voice, forcing him to pause and grin evn more. After taking a quick glance at the fans surrounding him, Lance looks over at Marcus, and grins again, then once again begin to speak...]
LM: Ladies and gentlemen, as always, I am Lance Marshall and seated next to me is the always interesting Marcus Haynes!
MH: Boy oh BOY am I glad to be back Marshall! LM: So am I Marcus. MH: Yeah, well no one asked you Marshall, so shutup! [Lance grins for a moment, and looks at Marcus...] MH: I don't swing that way Marshall! Quit staring at me! LM: Yeah folks, we're DEFINATELY back! What a lineup we have for you tonight too! In tonight's MAIN EVENT we will see the powerhouse DRAKE take on one of the sport's top atheletes, PEDRO KOZARO!
MH: Oh man, Kozaro is gonna stomp a mudhole in that big oaf! LM: We will also hear from HHWO President Wayne Michaels, who has an announcement to make concerning the vacant HHWO
National and HHWO Rock and Roll Title.
MH: He should just give both titles to Lawrence Williams! LM: I don't see that happening Marcus. But speaking of Lawrence
Williams, he'll be in action tonight when he clashes with the largest man in the HHWO, "Soul Reaver" Trevor Copeland.
MH: That's gonna be great. The bigger they are, the harder they fall. That should be my saying for tonight. Cause both
Drake and Copeland are goin DOWN!
LM: We also have a HARDCORE MATCH between "Brutal" Brian Floyd and "Shotgun" Shawn Crimson. There are NO DISQUALIFICATIONS and FALLS COUNT ANYWHERE!
MH: That's gonna be great! Crimson is gonna bleed Floyd like a stuck pig! LM: That match certainly will not be for the faint of heart. We will also see the debut The End when he takes on Kenneth Morlock, and a TRIPLE THREAT MATCH between Chad
Nowell, Christian "Spotlight" Madison, and "Spirit
Warrior" Jin Tao! MH: I hope Tao gets the crap kicked outta him. LM: Time will only tell the outcome Marcus, and I can't wait to get started! MH: Well it looks like we don't have to wait much longer! LM: Folks it looks like HHWO ring announcer Tony Palmer is ready to announce tonight's first match and get the action-packed nigth started, so let's not waste any more time!
MH: WOOHOO!! __ ____ ____ ________ _________________________________ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ __ \ \ \ / /_/ / /_/ / //| / / / / / / SINGLES MATCH / / __ / __ / // |/ / /_/ / / The End vs Kenneth Morlock / / / /_/_/ /_/___/|__/\____/ / _______________________________/ \/ \/ Writer: Wayne Michaels TP: Ladies and gentlemen, our first match is set for one fall with a twenty minute time limit.
[The lights drop, abruptly replaced by blacklight. bringing an eerie silence over the arena. The noise of the crowd is quickly extinguished by the opening notes of "The End" by the
Doors...]
TP: Coming to the ring tonight from Parts Unknown, he stands
6'6" tall and weighs in at 265 pounds ... Ladies and gentlemen ... THE END!
[The jumbotron over the entrance portal awakens, a gray swirl growing from the center of the screen as Jim Morrison's voice echos throughout the arena.]
# This is the end, beautiful friend # # This is the end, my only friend, the end # # Of our elaborate plans, the end # # Of everything that stands, the end # # No safety or surprise, the end # # I'll never look into your eyes # # Again # [The End steps through the entrance portal, barely noticeable in the blacklight. He wears all black - boots, wrist tape, long tights, robe, and mask. The camera zooms in on him, showing that there are no openings of any kind on the mask. He stands motionless in the portal for a few moments, apparently observing the crowd.
The words 'THE END' appear through the smoke on the jumbotron; The thin, shadowy font making them nearly indistinguishable from the smoke itself...] # Can you picture what will be # # So limitless and free # # Desperately in need # # Of some stranger's hand # # In a desperate land # [He slowly begins walking to ringside, the crowd surprisingly silent as he walks past them. His stride is long, but he clearly isn't in much of a hurry. Upon reaching ringside, The End stops again, bowing his head...]
# Lost in a roman wilderness of pain # # And all the children are insane # # All the children are insane # # Waiting for the summer rain # [The End raises his head and shrugs off his robe, letting it drop to the ground. Grabbing onto the top and second ropes, he pulls himself up onto the ring apron. He steps between the ropes and takes a few steps into the ring, slowly lowering himself into a kneeling position, As the music begins to fade,
The End turns his head to Tony Palmer, his hand outstretched in a request for the microphone. Palmer responds, hesistantly, backing away from The End as soon as the mic is out of his hand.]
TE: "Confringam illos nec poterunt stare cadent subtus pedes meos" [The End reaches behind him and sets the microphone down on the ring apron, his face never turning away from Morlock. In the background, the music slowly fades out, allowing the crowd to break free from their somewhat hypnotic state, albeit reluctantly...]
MH: What the hell was that?! Gibberish?? LM: I believe he was speaking Latin... TP: And his opponent...weighing in at 240 pounds and standing at
6'2...He hails from the Dirty South...Memphis Tennessee...
["Turn The Page" by Metallica starts to play over the PA
System. On the Jumbotron the letters TNL appear on the screen...]
TP: He is...."THE NEW LEGEND" KENNETH MORLOCK! [On the Jumbotron you can now see clips of Kenneth Morlock doing various things. One picture has him in a leisure suit, one picture has him in the ring wrestling and one picture has him giving an interview. "Turn The Page" continues to play as all of the sudden the lights begin to flash from blue to white and a huge dark blue stobe light flashes on the entrance ramp.
Suddenly a huge explosion is heard in the arena and "The New
Legend" Kenneth Morlock walks out from the curtain. Kenneth
Morlock is wearing his dark blue leg tights that read "REAL"
down one leg in black letters and "DEAL" down the other leg in white letters. He wears blue wrestling boots that read "SSS" on each of them in black barbwire letters...]
[Kenneth Morlock stays on the ramp pacing back and forth for a few seconds until another huge explosion is heard around the building. Suddenly "The New Legend" Kenneth Morlock begins to walk towards the ring. Morlock slowly walks down the aisle taunting the fans with fake high fives and casual brush offs.
The fans begin to boo and fuss as Kenneth Morlock walks up the stairs to the ring and smirks at the fans. Morlock enters the ring and begins to laugh and spit towards the fans as "Turn The
Page" ends...]
LM: The fans definately don't seem to care for Morlock too much, and I feel like for Morlock, the feeling is mutual.
MH: Of course the feeling is mutual, the fans are dumb! Look at The End, just standing in the ring motionless like some sort of idiot. KILL HIM KENNY!
[Morlock begins moving around, anxious for the match to begin, while The End calmly stands in the ring, seemingly staring at Morlock...DING! DING! DING!]
LM: Here we go! The first match of the evening is now underway! MH: Woohoo! [Morlock charges at The End from the get go, but The End stops him in his tracks with a big spear, sending both men crashing to the mat abruptly...]
LM: The End starts this match off with a great high energy manuever. I have the feeling this is gonna be a very intense matchup between these two competitors.
MH: You say that for every match Marshall. LM: Well, maybe it's true. MH: [mocking Lance] Well, maybe your an unoriginal bastard! [As both men scramble to get back to their feet, Morlock quickly begins to hammer on The End with a succession of punches to the head. Morlock backs The End up, then thrusts him into the ropes. The End ducks a clothesline by Morlock and comes back off the opposite set of ropes...]
LM: FLYING CLOTHESLINE! The End almost took Morlock's head clear off his shoulders with that one!
MH: I don't think that's possible. Your head is on your neck, not your shoulders. LM: Well, Marcus, how 'bout we find out? You just sit there and I'll see if I can take your head off your shoulders.
MH: Shutup Marshall. [The End brings Morlock to his feet and throws him into a standing headscissors before lifting him up and Powerbombing
Morlock back to the canvas...]
[BIG crowd pop...] LM: WHAT A MOVE! The End now definately has the momentum going his way for the time being. MH: But not for long! [The End once again brings Morlock to his feet, but this time Morlock begins to fight back with a barrage of blows to the midsection of The End. Morlock then lifts The End up into the air and brings him back down with an atomic drop...]
MH: OH MAN! That's gotta hurt! LM: Morlock is battling his way back now. [Morlock once again tosses The End into the ropes, but The End reverses, tossing Morlock into the ropes. Morlock ducks a clothesline and fires back off the ropes with a HUGE clothesline of his own sending both The End and himself over the ropes to the outside...]
[BIG crowd pop...] LM: Oh man, things can really get dangerous outside of the ring on this concrete floor! MH: Just the way I like it! 1! 2! [Morlock laughs at and mocks the crowd as he brings The End to his feet. Morlock puts The End into a front facelock and continues to yell at the crowd before DDTing The End right onto the conrete floor with incredible force...]
LM: DDT ON THE CONRETE FLOOR! MH: Haha! He almost buried The End's head into the concerete like a flamingo buries it head in the sand!
3! LM: Umm...you mean an ostrich? MH: NO! I mean The End you stupid moron! Do you see any ostriches around here? Jeez... 4! 5! [While The End lies face down on the cold, concrete floor,
Kenneth Morlock walks around the outside looking for something...]
6! LM: Uh oh, I wonder what Morlock is looking for. MH: He probably wants my autograph. LM: I highly doubt that Marcus. 7! MH: Shutup Marshall. LM: These men may both be counted out if they don't get back into the ring! [Morlock smiles ear to ear as he grabs a folded table and quickly sets it up on the outside, and sets it right up against the side of the ring...]
[HUGE crowd pop...] 8! MH: Atta boy Kenny! Tables RULE! LM: Morlock will soon be disqualified if he doesn't get back in the ring instead of messing around with that table!
[Morlock brings The End to his feet and slides him into the ring under the bottom rope, then follows him back into the ring...]
LM: That was close. MH: Oh who cares. I think that ten count is the dumbest rule in the world. Let me beat the crap out of each other wherever they want!
LM: That's called a Falls Count Anywhere match Marcus. MH: Shutup Marshall! [While Morlock turns around to taunt the crowd, The End gains his composure, and waits for Morlock. As soon as Morlock turns around he is greeted with a swift boot to the midesction followed by a HUGE brainbuster...]
[BIG crowd pop...] LM: The End has turned the tables in his favor! He took advantage of Morlock's egotistical flaws!
MH: Those stupid fans! They distracted Kenny! LM: It looks like Morlock's mistake has cost him this time. MH: Shutup Marshall! [The End quickly covers Morlock...] 1! 2! LM: Kickout by Morlock! MH: Come on Kenny! [The End brings Morlock back to his feet and puts him in a standing headscissors before piledriving him back to the mat...]
[BIG crowd pop...] LM: The End seems to be strategicly doing damage to Morlock's neck and head. MH: What a cheater! LM: What!? How's he cheating Marcus? MH: Shutup Marshall.
