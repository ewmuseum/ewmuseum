There is no other purpose in all of e-wrestling that takes precedent over having fun. HHWO gives the handlers the chance to do as the yplease with their character in a negotiable and exciting environment. FUN! FUN! FUN! |砝 |砝|砝 |砝|砝 |砝|秤秤|秤秤秤秤秤秤秤秤秤秤秤秤秤秤秤珮 | |-| || |-| || |/\| || || | Cards/Events : |_| |_||_| |_| \_/\_/ |____|_______________________________/ HHWO will hold one card per week, and one flash show per week, and will have pay-per-view's bi-monthly, with supercards once a month during month's without pay-per-views. 1. HHWO Warpath: This is HHWO's weekly card that will contain on average 5 to 7 matches. This is where angles and fueds will form. 2. HHWO Shockwave: This is HHWO's weekly flash show which will recap past matches as well as preview upcoming matches and occasionly have special guests. 3. HHWO National Title: This is the big gold, the prize everyone is striving for, only to be worn by the best of the best of the better than the best. 2. HHWO Rock and Roll Title: While not the major title, this is the next best thing. The HHWO RNR Title commands respect and is always well-earned.
