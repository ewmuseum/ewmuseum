HHWO Legends 98 Lineup

23 August 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Where Heroes Are Born...And Legends Are Made" [HHWO Legends 98] Sunday, August 30th Sun Devil Stadium, AZ *LIVE* Only on PPV! [3-Way World Title Match] "Confident" Lawrence Williams -vs- Creed -vs- Paul "statutory" Clark <Which man will walk away the first ever HHWO Heavyweight Champion of the World? Who will be eliminated first? Will accused pedophile Paul Clark get double-teamed and taste defeat for the first time? Can Creed survive this brutal 3-Way dance and walk away with his first World Title? We will soon see!> [3-Way United States Title Match] Fang -vs- Jack Vars -vs- Vic "The Hammer" Hudson <Vic Hudson has already defeated Jack Vars in singles competition, but can he succeed with another body in the ring? Can Jack Vars overcome all odds and leave the ring wearing the HHWO World Title? Or will Fang keep his undefeated streak alive and capture the gold? At Legends 98 we will find out!> [#1 Contender: World] "TPS" Brock Carden -vs- Knick Man <Two fan-favorites will lock up in the ring to see who will recieve the first shot at the newly crowned HHWO World Champion on the following Friday Night Frenzy. Which one will be able to pull out the win? Find out at Legends 98!> [Grudge Match] "Gorgeous" Cyrus Greene -vs- "The Headliner" Scott Wright <The two most egotistical and conceded personalities wandering HHWO will finally square off in the ring after weeks of heated fueding. Can the official "HHWO Cyrus Greene Champion", Cyrus Greene, defeat Wright? Or will Scott Wright pull out the win, and become the HHWO Cyrus Greene Champion?> [3-Way Steel Cage/Weapons/No DQ Match] "Rage" Robert Hearsch -vs "Homeless" Joe Boxcar -vs- "The Punisher" Alex Fury <Three of the most extreme and fearless wrestlers HHWO has to offer will collide inside of a steel cage! No disqualifications, and various weapons will be added into the steel cage! The winner of this match can truly call himself "King of Extreme".> [Grudge Match] Steve Houston -vs- Mystery Opponent <Steve Houston, still lacking a mark in the win column, will face an opponent to be named that night. Who will the mystery opponent be? Can Houston finally rack up his first win, or will the mystery opponent prevail and force Houston to continue his current path?> [Grudge Match] Brass Knuckles -vs "Iron Man" Eddie Jacobs <Newcomer Eddie Jacobs will make his long-awaited and he steps into the ring against one of the roughest and toughest around...Brass Knuckles! Will Jacobs be able to make a successful debut? Or will BK dish him his first loss?>
