HHWO Rebirth Promo

24 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[SCENE: Nothing can be seen. But the sounds of a man panting and seemingly walking through tall grass are heard. The swish sound is heard because of the man walking through the grass. He could be panting because he's scared. Then, a sound rattling the grass is heard in the background, and the man stops, his breathing taking over all the audio. Over all of this drama, a voice-over of a rough man is heard.] VO: "Fear . . ." [After a few minutes of pause, the man continues to walk through the walk, seemingly walking faster. The voice continues, now whispering.] "Fear . . ." [Once again, we hear the man come to a stop because of the sound in the near distance. The man begins panting even harder, now beginning to run across.] "Your heart pounds; your lungs race. You can't avoid it . . ." [Now, the sound begins to come closer, now seeming to run after the man. The man screams and starts running. Footsteps from the back are heard, the man continuing to yell.] "When you fear, you must think: What did _YOU_ do to 'cause it? Why are you fearing something that could harm you? Were you thinking when you committed this act? Whichever it is, you must now deal with it, for _you_ are the one that caused it. Take responsibility for your actions. And most importantly. . ." [A big thump is then heard, and screaming follows this. It seems as if the man is being torn apart. He continues to let out bloodcurdling yells, not being able to avoid the pain nor defend himself.] ". . .DON'T burn your bridges . . . for if you do, you must accept the consequences. And believe me . . ." [The sound of a lion purring is then heard, and we now know that the man was being chased by the lion. The lion now roars, sending the scene into all whiteness and bringing the HHWO Rebirth logo onto the center of the in all black text.] ". . . the consequences could be _deadly!!_" [Howling in the background is heard.] "It's HHWO: Rebirth! November, 15th, 1998. LIVE from the Gund Arena in Cleveland, Ohio. Be there!!!"
