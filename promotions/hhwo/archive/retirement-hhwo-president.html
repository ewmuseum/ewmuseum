Retirement: the HHWO President

8 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Well fellas, yes it's true...I'm retiring. I've only been in this game for 5 short months, and I'm already calling it quits. No grand finale. No big last dance. This is it, right here, I'm done. It's been fun guys, and itwas great while it lasted, but to be as corny as possible..."All good things must come to an end". I'm not going to bore you all with my pathetic history in e-wrestling, but I just want you all to know that I don't regret any of it. Why? Why am I retiring? I feel it's just time...I just have lost interest in handling anyone, as well as running a fed. There are much more important things in my life right now that I don't want to get into, and as a matter of fact, there's much more important things in ALL of your lives, just think about it. While I may be retiring, I'm not gonna crawl under a rock and die or anything, I'll still be here...but not as active. Who knows, maybe one day, I will be back to share in this addicting and exciting habit, but until then, the show must go on! I'm gonna leave ya with a list of people I would like to thank, adios! Danny-boy Rushing Adam Moss Chr...I mean Creed ;P The infamous Tony Scampini My mentor, [name withheld] My mentor, [name withheld] [name withheld] My fellow EMWC compadres [name withheld] Jason Darin Sean Cox Aaron Barham Jay Ralston And all the others I may have forgotten or have supported me in any way, shape, or form. It's been great... Peace and prayer, ~ the HHWO President -HHWO President "The Disciple" Eric Wayne (EMWC, OWF, GFWA) -Formerly CoW, IeWC, EMWO, RMWA, and countless others... "Wildstorm" [ring name] -former APwC World Champion
