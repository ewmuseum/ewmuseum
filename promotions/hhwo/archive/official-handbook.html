Application [What is the HHWO?] HHWO stands for Hometown Heroes Wrestling Organization, and is run by myself and [name withheld]. HHWO will strive not only for fierce competition, well-written and exciting matches, and the constant and zealous pursuit of making e-wrestling better, but it's main focus is what e-wrestling is all about...fun. HHWO will be a place for you the handler to continually grow in the development of your abilities and creativity of your character, and build strong angles and interesting ideas in a coherent environment. The HHWO will prove to be one of the most exciting and memorable federations to come around in a long time, because this is where the true heroes and superstars of e-wrestling compete. [How does HHWO work?] HHWO depends on the active involvment of it's participants to keep it running smoothly and efficiently at it's highest level of potential possible. HHWO will use a closed flash packet system, meaning that all flashes will be compiled into a periodic packet or mail and then distributed to everyone in HHWO or on the HHWO mailing list. [Flashes] Flashes will have the most influence on whether or not you win or lose a match. You must flash at least once for every match that you are assigned to. Spamming is not only frowned upon but is restricted. Spammers will have their flashes disregarded and also be punished. Flashes will be graded on quality, not quantity. [In-Rings] There will be spots on every HHWO card for e-wrestlers to conduct an in-ring roleplay. It could be before during the pre-show or during the card. In-Rings will help you get a slight push and are great for angles and fueds. They can also get you matches signed easier because of an angle or fued. [Strats] Strats are _NOT_ required for a match, but they do help and can be what tips the scale in a close match. They also help the writers write the match more efficently and quickly when you provide a list of moves, in order, that you would like to perform. [Cards] HHWO Cards will be broadcasted via e-mail as well as the RSPWF newsgroup. There will be one card per week for now entitled Friday Night Heat. Friday Night Heat will be broadcasted live every Friday (obviously) around 7 PM. It will showcase our major talent as well as many upcoming stars and newcomers to HHWO. We will try our best to make sure that every card is not only well written and exciting, but also on time every time. [Titles] HHWO will have two singles competition belts to be won and defended: HHWO World Title, and the HHWO United States Title. HHWO World Title: The HHWO World Title is the big prize of the HHWO, which only the best of the best can attain. It will be defended when appropriate, at most pay-per-views and sometimes the main event of the weekly card. The World Title is the most respected and admirable belt, and is to be treated in that manner. HHWO United States Title: The HHWO US Title is the minor of the two titles, but is still a highly respected belt. It will be defended at pay-per-views as well as the weekly card. [Pay-Per-Views] HHWO will hold a monthly pay-per-view event, and an annual pay-per-view event that will be _THE_ wrestling event of the year. The monthly pay-per-views will be where the World Title is valiantly defended, and where many angles and fueds come to an end or go through the roof. At the annual mega pay-per-view, the intensity and excitement will grow ten fold. It will be where heroes are crowned and where legends are made. [HHWO Website] There will be a HHWO website made as soon as possible. We can guarantee you that it will be of professional quality and updated on a daily basis. Check out http://members.xoom.com/HHWO/ [HHWO Online Magazine] There will be a HHWO Online Magazine published on the HHWO website. The magazine will feature articles by the staff on the wrestlers and the HHWO, as well as articles by you, the particpants and wrestlers of HHWO.
