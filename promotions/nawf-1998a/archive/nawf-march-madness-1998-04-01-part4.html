March Madness

1 April 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
JL: Now we are on to the match the everyone has wanted to see, this should be one hell of a brawl. JG: I agree, I've been waiting all week for this one, the winner of this should really give an edge to their respcetive stable...Let's go to the ring! Grudge Match *On for Life* Cannibus "The Fatal One" versus Sinister Shakur "The Prophetic" :: The bass line to "Victory" by Puff Daddy, Notorious B.I.G and Busta Rhymes blares over the loud speakers. The crowd goes wild as they see Cannibus "The Fatal One" walk out from the locker room area. He looks at the fans and smiles. As he enters the ring he is wearing his usual entire and seems to be very confident. He walks over to a corner and sits on the top rope, patiently waiting for his opponent to enter the ring. :: :: You can hear the words, "Are you ready to die, or do you want to live forever. Tell Me." At this time the music blaring is "Only Fear of Death" by Tupac Shakur. Again the crowd goes wild, cheering on as Sinister Shakur "The Prophetic" walks down the aisle. He is looking directly at Canniubus "The Fatal One," directly into his eyes. As he enters the ring he takes off his robe. He has a rope around his neck, one that has a noose and a knot on it. This is uncharacteristic of him. Cannibus looks surprised. :: :: Ding, Ding, Ding :: JG: This looks to be a very interesting match-up. Both men have had some harsh words to each other and both seem to know alot about each other. Look at them, it is as if they are both peering into each others souls. They are just circling each other. JL: Both of them just need to fight and get this match going. The fans came to see some beef and they dont want to watch two people circle around each other like ballerinas. JG: Damn, you really dont know too much about these guys, do you? The mental part of wrestling is very important, and right now both men are playing the mental game. Here they go, they both lock up. JL: But what do you know, they both break free. Both of them look in excellent condition. Oh yeah, did you get a chance to see Sinister's newest tattoo. I think it says ILM whatever that means. JG: Good point, maybe we could find that out later. Look at Cannibus, he is just screaming at Shakur, calling him a bitch, a fake, and a wanna-be. I dont think Shakur is going to take this verbal abuse, especially to his face. JL: Uh oh, here comes Cannibus with a clothesline that almost took Shakur's head off. Cannibus is just laughing now. That caught Shakur by surprise. JG: Look who just came out of the locker room area. It is a stablemate of Cannibus'. None other then former champion Devil. What in the hell is he doing here? It does not look good for Shakur with a man like this out at ringside. JL: What is Devil saying? I heard him mention Malik, but I do not know why? Does he have an infatuation with Malik Mustafa? In the ring both men are back on their feet. Here is Shakur gaining the advantage as he body slams Cannibus hard onto the canvas. That was showing some strength and quickness on the part of Shakur. JG: Cannibus is going to the outside to chat with Devil. But wait, Shakur is on the top rope. Both Devil and Cannibus do not see him. Oh my!!! Shakur just nailed Devil with a double ax handle off the top rope. JL: Look at Cannibus, he picked up the chair from the outside area. Devil is on the ground and Shakur doesnt see Cannibus with the chair. JL: What?!? Cannibus just tagged Devil in the face with the chair. Devil's head is busted open. Cannibus and Shakur are just looking at each other now. Both men nod their heads...very professional. Cannibus picks up the bloody Devil and throws him back in the ring. JG: Maybe we should get some help out there for Devil. Both of these men are fatal and destructive. But who wants to get involved with something like this. JL: Meanwhile this match is still going on. But both men have dragged Devil into this. Devil is out cold, he has no idea what is going on. Look at Cannibus with that chair. He just nailed Devil in the head again. He could have a fractured skull. Blood is all over the canvas now. JG: Shakur picks up Devil and brings him up to the top rope. Why are they doing this? Devil was going to be a vice-president, maybe even president. If people thought that Cannibus and Shakur were not genius', they need to watch this pay per view again. JL: Oh no! Shakur just nailed Devil with the Black Raven. This could be it. Devil is just lying their on the ground, not moving at all. We only get a spasmic jerk from him every once in awhile. Cannibus drags Devil up and nails him with a heart punch as Shakur goes over to the corner and grabs his noose. JG: No, they cannot lynch him. I thought the NAWF was finished with those matches and those actions. They all started when Malik Mustafa lynched Jonathan "The Ripper" O'Malley and continued to the matches between Millionaire Mike and "Chicken Hawking" Hustler. JL: Yep that is exactly what they are doing. Cannibus and Shakur slip the rope around his neck and Shakur is just dragging him around the ring. Cannibus takes the rope from Shakur and ties it to the top of the ring post. Look at Devil, he is just hanging there, with only his toes touching the ground. Oh My, that could kill him. Get somebody out here. JG: Damn, Shakur and Cannibus, Cannibus and Shakur...whatever way you put it, those are two names that you will be hearing for awhile. A deadly duo. Cannibus is calling for a microphone. Before he begins to speak. He embraces Shakur. Cannibus "The Fatal One": Three letters for all you bitches. I - L - M. Coming strait at all you motherfuckers. :: He hands the microphone to Shakur. :: Sinister Shakur "The Prophetic": You will here from me later. But to all you motherfuckers who want to mess with me? Think about it. Dont talk about me, I wont talk about you. It is as simple as that. :: Both men walk out of the ring as "Victory" blares again. The crowd looks shocked and ectastic at the same time. Officials are helping to untie Devil. :: JL: well, I can safely say that not many people expected that, it looks like Devil's stable has quickly fallen apart as Pain has won all of the head to head matchups tonight. JG: Yeah, but did you hear how they were talking about this I-L-M, is it possible that they will no longer be in Pain? JL: Well only time will tell, but right now it is time for the main event, "The Best of the Best" *Main Event* The Best of the Best Battle Royal Lasher vs. Marcus Millenium vs. Chaos vs. Skye Daniels vs. Tony Morris -The Television Champion, Tony Morris walks down to the ring, followed by the United States Champion, Skye Daniels...Then the newly crowned Hardcore Champion, Chaos walks down to the ring with Rebecca, followed by the former I.C. Champ, Marcus Millenium in his last match in the NAWF. Finally, the World Champion, Lasher comes down with The Lovely Deirdre. All five men enter the ring as the bell sounds... JL: We're off as Chaos and Lasher quickly go after each othe and are trading punches...Daniels and Morris are teaming up on Millenium in the corner. JG: Yeah, I don't expect much from Millenium after the beating that he got from Lasher and Mystikal early tonight...Lasher picks Chaos up and nails him with a reverse atomic knee drop...Now Daniels comes over to help Lasher with Chaos. JL: Lasher just nailed Daniels, I guess he wants Chaos all to himself...On the other side of the ring Morris is choking Millenium with the bottom rope...Lasher picks Chaos up and powerslams him to the mat. JG: This is some match, Lasher goes over to Chaos but Chaos reaches up and gouges Lashers eyes and then naisl him with a jawbreaker...Morris and Daniels picks Millenium up and they both whip him into the ropes...Millenium ducks under the double clothesline attempt he turns around and hits a double DDT! JL: That's something, he has to be working on instinct right now, on the other side Lasher and Chaos are choking each other and they both fall out inbetween the ropes...The referee is trying to get them back in but they're not listening. JG: Lasher and Chaos are fighting all the way up the runway now, Chaos slams Lasher into one of the spotlights, Lasher turns around and hits Chaos with a kick to the midsection...Lasher just nailed Chaos with a Voodoo Drop on the concrete! JL: These two are incredible...Lasher is on top of Chaos choking him...Wait! Rebecca just jumoed on top of Lasher and is hitting him...They're not really affecting him but he has let go of the chokehold...Now Deirdre grabs Rebecca by the hair and gets her off of Lasher!!! JG: Yeah, it's a catfight...Look at those two going at it...NAWF officials have come out to break this up, they are having a tough time breaking these two women up...Lashe is trying to get Deirdre off and Chaos is up and trying to get Rebecca off. JL: The referee has ruled that both Lasher and Chaos are disqualified, so that leaves three wrestlers left...Lasher finally gets Deirdre and brings her to the back..In the ring Morris picks Millenium up and hits him with an atomic drop and Daniels drop kicks Millenium over the top rope. JG: That leaves two Pain members left and only one of them can be crowned best of the best...The slap hands and they are ready to go...They tie up and Daniels pushes Morris into the ropes...Daniels nails Morris with a right. JL: So much for friendship. Daniels whips Morris into the ropes...Daniels goes for a dropkick but Morris holds on to the ropes and Daniels crashes to the mat...Morris picks Daniels up and goes for the Doomsday DDT...Daniels blocks it and counters with a back body drop. JG: What back and forth action we have here...Daniels grabs Morris and whips him into the corner...Daniels takes a running start and splashes Morris in the corner. I think Daniels is tasting victory here. JL: Daniels grabs Morris and throws Morris over! Wait, Morris is holding on to the ropes! Daniels doesn't see it, he thinks he won the match. Morris pulls himself up and over the ropes back into the ring. JG: Morris is standing behind Daniels, Daniels oesn't know he's there...Morris dropkicks Daniels and Daniels goes flying over the top rope. That's it the winner of The Best of the Best is Tony Morris! What a match. JL: Daniels doesn't know what happened and the referee is tring to explain it to him...Morris is celebrating in the ring...Daniels goes back into the ring and confronts Morris...They're staring each other down...Now Daniels puts his hand out and morris shakes it and they embrace. JG: Great show of sprotsmanship in a hard fought match. Well folks, we're exhausted here. What a night. We hoped you enjoyed yourself, For me and my partner Joey Langford we'll see you later... PaiN 1997-March 31, 1998 Rest Eternally
