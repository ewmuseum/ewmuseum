Joey Langford: Hello everybody and welcome to San Antonio, Texas for NAWF's March Madness...As you can see the Alamodome is jammed packed for the second straight night. [name withheld]: That's right Joey we have a big card tonight including the main event know as "Best of the Best" as all five singles champions will be in the ring together in a mini battle royal, plus we have the match that most people are talking about...Once best friends, Sinister Shakur and Cannibus will go at it one on one. JL: That's not all we also have a big steel cage bout with the return of Devil going against Pain member Joshua "The Unseen" Curry. Also, the tag-team titlea will be on the line when Lasher and Mystikal will defend their titles against Marcus Millenium and Overpower. JG: This has got to be one of the best cards we have seen in a long time here, but there are a couple of changes, due to the retirements of Scott McMichael and King, Chaos and Death Master both win by forfeit. JL: That's right, plus with Chaos' win that means he gets a shot at Lasher's World Title, we are ready to go with our first match tonight between Handsome David Parker and "The E.Y.Con" Nick Machiavelli. Let's go to the ring... Handsome David Parker vs. "The E.Y.Con" Nick Machiavelli JL: This should be a very good matchup between two newcomers to the NAWF but both men are very experienced in the ring. JG: Both men tie up in the middle of the ring and Parker throws him to the ground...Parker does a little posing for the crowd. JL: Great, another showboat, just what this fed needs...The tie up again and this time Machiavelli and Nick mocks Parker by doing a little posing of his own. JG: They tie up one more time and Parker backs Machaiveeli into the ropes...The ref is looking for a clean break but Parker nails Machiavelli with a right...and another right. JL: Parker whips him into the ropes...Parker goes for a clothesline but Machiavelli ducks under it and comes off again and hits a flying body press...Quick cover, one...t kickout by Parker. JG: Machiavelli picks up Parker and nails him with a belly-to-back suplex...Now Machiavelli picks Parker up and puts him in a headlock...Parker backs him into the ropes and whips him in...Machiavelli comes back with a shoulder block...Machiavelli runs off the ropes and Parker nails him with a knee to the midsection. JL: Parker picks him up and hooks him up for a suplez...He nails it...Now Parker is looking for some applause from the crowd but only gets a round of boos...Parker picks Machiavelli up now and throws him out of the ring. JG: Parker follows him out and picks Machiavelli up and slams him on the outside...Parker picks Machiavelli up and tries to whip him into the guardril but it's reversed by Machiavelli JL: Machaivelli picks Parker up and throws him back into the ring...Machiavelli follows him in...Machiavelli picks Parker up and whips him into the turnbuckle...Machiavelli takes a running start and nails him with a splash in the corner. JG: Machaivelli picks Parker up from the mat and nails him with a double armed DDT...That could be it, he goes for the pin...one...two...three! That's it Your winner is "The E.Y.Con" Nick Machaivelli. JL: Very impressive match by both men, but now it's time for our match for the tag team titles, let's go to the ring... *Tag Team Title Match* Lasher and Mystikal (Champions) vs. Overpower and Marcus Millenium JL: We're ready to go here as it looks like Lasher will start it for his teams and Millenium will start for his...They both look at each othe, Millenium gives Lasher a smile and flexes...Lasher responds with a boot to the midsection...Lasher follows with an axe handle to the back as Millenium falls down to one knee. JG: Lasher picks Millenium up and whips him into the ropes he comes off and is met with a clothesline from Lasher that takes Millenium over the top ropes and outside the ring... JL: The referee is talking to Lasher as Mystikal grabs Millenium and runs him into the steel stairs...Now Mystikal picks him up and throws him back into the ring. JG: Lasher grabs Millenium and drags him to the corner and tags in Mystikal...Mystikal comes in the ring and both he and Lasher start to pummel on Milenium in the corner. JG: Mystikal picks Millenium up and nails him with a DDT...He goes for the cover...One...Two...Overpower comes in and breaks the pin attempt...The ref is warning Overpower as Mystikal drags Millenium to his corner. JG: That should be it but Lasher isn't going for the pin...He tags Mystikal and he's going up to the top rope...Lasher grabs Mystikal and rocket launches him onto Millenium...that should be it...One...Two...Three!
