March Madness

1 April 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Millionaire Mike vs. Slash JL: Well we got match for you and boy oh boy what a match it is, as Slash takes on what I think to be the greatest athlete to ever step into the squared circle, Millionare Mike and man, he is lookin pumped tonight dont ya think Stan? This is Millionaire Mike's first match back since he left the NAWF, and returned as a member of PaiN. JG: Well I dont know Chris, Slash is lookin pretty good, and they lock up for the first time and Mike kicks him to the midsection and Slash looks hurt he is holding on to his stomach. Now Mike picks him up and throws him out of the ring and Mike is going out after him, I'm tellin you Chris, Mike is a force to be reckoned with, damn look at him, he is completely killing Slash, who is one hell of an athlete. Millionaire Mikee now sets him up on a table, this is amazing and oh no!!!!! Mike elbowed Slash right through the table, this isnt a falls count anywhere match or a table match but I guess Mike is making it one and the referee is obliging him. JL: And now Mike is throwing Slash back into the ring and he covers 1..2..kickout by Slash. Mike gets Slash to his feet and an irish whip by Mike and WOW!!!!! a devastating clothesline by Mike. Millionaire Mike is a force, and boy oh boy, what a force, Slash has not gotten a move in yet. And now a vertice suplex by Mike, he is now setting Slash on the second rope, could this be PAY DAY, no he Superplexes him off the top rope, and Slash isnt even moving, I think hes dead. JG: NO Stan, hes breathing, Mike get Slash to his feet again, he picks him up and POWERBOMB, could it now be time for the PAY DAY, no he is putting Slash in the corner and nailing him with a series of vicious fists to the face and Slash is not lookin too good and finally after all the punishment, Mike is covering 1..2..no, Mike picked Slash up off the canvas, come on ring the bell ref, its obviously over, and now its time and..........PAY DAY, its over for Slash and 1.....2......3 ......Mike wins, Mike wins!!!!!!! JL: That was an impressive win by Millionaire Mike, I thought Slash would have given him a tougher match then that. JG: Wait a minute, I hear some music in the background The lights go off as "My Only Fear of Death" by Tupac Shakur is blaring in the background. Two men make their way to the ring. Along side of them is some white guy that represents the NAWF. He looks scared, as if he doesnt want to be there with them. The crowd seems excited as they cheer on the two men who are coming to the ring. One of them is the former Vice President of the NAWF, Sinister Shakur (formerly The Infamous Sinister). The second man is a NAWF legeng, The "Chicken Hawking" Hustler. Both of them are wearing traditional ring attire, and seem to be in a hurray. White Guy: Please take this time to witness the newest game show of the NAWF. The NAWF/ Pai(\) Pyramid. I am sure you have all seen the game show, "20,000 Dollar Pyramid." These two men are going to be playing a game similar. There are three clues that they have to answer. If they get them all, which they better do, they get to go shoot some pool, if they dont, then they have to listen to some country music. Hustler: Bring this shit on, I am ready. White Guy: You have 30 seconds to do this. You better not fuck up. Hustler: Bitch, we won't. ((The timer begins to run as Sinister Shakur smiles when he sees the tree clues.)) Sinister Shakur: "King Snake" Hustler: A fucking kingpin? Sinister Shakur: "Lasher" Hustler: A consigliere? Sinister Shakur: "Tony Morris" Hustler: Ummmmmmm... a student? Sinister Shakur: "Nick Machiavelli" Hustler: Oh shit! People You Want in your Family, watching your back. ((Ding Ding Ding Ding Ding)) Sinister Shakur: "Step to me and let me suck your dick" Hustler: Fuck you bitch! Sinister Shakur: "Oh, do me up the ass" Hustler: Yo, what happened to the normal Sinister? Sinister Shakur: "I am coming out of the closet" Hustler: Go ahead and do so. But stay the fuck away from my little brother. Sinister Shakur: "Oh shit, I never should have fucked with Pai(\)" Hustler: Damn, you should have said that first. Things that Devil says regularly. ((Ding Ding Ding Ding Ding)) Sinister Shakur: "I knew Mr. MaLiK "The Grim Reaper" Mustafa" Hustler: So did I bitch, what is your point? Sinister Shakur: "Hustler is going to teach me how to wrestle" Hustler: I didnt say that to anyone. Sinister Shakur: "I've been hanging out in Cali with Pai(\)" Hustler: You should, you are the don right? Sinister Shakur: "Sinister Shakur schooled me to some knowledge" Hustler: Damn, this shit is too damn easy. Things Niggaz Be Saying to get the Pussy! ((Ding Ding Ding Ding Ding)) Hustler: What did I tell you? You stupid motherfucker. Yo, Sin, lets go play some pool, so I can bust you the fuck up. We are unstoppable. ((The two men then exit the ring area as their music blares.)) JB: Well, I must say that was really interesting, you can see that the time off hasn't changed these guys in the least. JL: You're right Josh, it looks like these guys are ready for a war...let's go to our next match!
