March Madness

1 April 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*Intercontinental Championship Match* Eldar "The Crusher" Markovich vs. "The Chicken Hawking" Hustler JG: Well Folks..this one is the one we have been waiting for..Everyone knows that Double M left the NaWf not to long ago..and that leaves the Intercontinental Belt vacant at this time. JL: Your right...but are we sure that Double M gave us the real reason why he left the NaWf?? Everyone knows that Hustler was going after him very soon..maybe he ran off before he got his ass beat...Who knows.. JG: This has been a heated rivalry all week..who is going to come out on top..who will walk away with the Intercontinental Title tonight..Will Crusher win his first belt here in the NaWf or will Hustler take back the belt he never lost?? JL: Well...here comes Crusher..This man has been through alot since he first showed up in the NaWf not too long ago..what has he had..three title shots already and this is his fourth?? I mean alot of people might think he doesn't deserve all of this..but he is alot better than most would think.. JG: Your right..Crusher is going to be more than Hustler can handle maybe..Now..here comes "The Chicken Hawking" Hustler...this man has been through alot in the NaWf..he is one of the most respected men to ever come through the NaWf..look at Hustler..he looks like he is ready to kill someone.. JL: Both men are now in the ring..there's the bell..Hustler and Crusher lock up..you can see that Hustler has the upperhand at first..he throws Crusher off the ropes..Hustler ducks..Crusher stops over him and knees him in the back.. JG: Crusher pulls Hustler up..Crusher grabs him and snapsuplexs him to the mat...Crusher pulls Hustler up again...he grabs him from behind..but Hustler reverses it and belly to back suplex by Huslter..Hustler is up quick..he starts stomping on Crusher...Hustler grabs Crusher's leg..but Crusher nails him in the face with his boot.. JL: Crusher is up quick..he grabs Hustler and pulls him up..he throws him off the ropes...powerslam by Crusher..Crusher goes for the pin.. 1 2 No kickout by Hustler JG: Crusher pulls Hustler up again..he throws him to the turnbuckle..Crusher goes running in but Hustler moves out of the way..Hustler then grabs in and gives him and inverted DDT..that had to hurt right there..Crusher is on the ground holding his head... JL: Hustler pulls Crusher up...he grabs him for an atomic drop..but Crusher reverses it and turns Hustler around and catches him with a swinging neck breaker..Crusher has really come out tonight ready to fight... JG: Crusher pulls Hustler up..throws him off the ropes...but Hustler catches himself..Hustler then runs in and clothslines Crusher down to the mat..Hustler picks Crusher up and gives him a reverse neckbreaker..He goes for the pin... 1 2 Thre...no...How did Crusher kickout.. JL: Hustler pulls Crusher up...wait..Crusher blocks it and turns him around..and grabs Hustler's waist..Hustler elbows him in the head..he grabs him and Hustler Cut's him...Crusher is out.. JG: Hustler turns Crusher over and goes for the pin... 1 2 3 JL: It's over....we have a new Champion.."The Chicken Hawking" Hustler is the New Intercontinental Champion...He never lost the title before now he has it back..what will come of this?? JG: Well, we have the steel cage up so let's get to the next match *Steel Cage Match* Devil vs. Joshua "The Unseen" Curry JL: Devil has already made it inside the cage and Curry is circling the ring...He finally enters the ring and Devil attacks him right away... JG: They closed the door and Devil is all over Curry with kicks to the head...Now he picks Curry up and throws him face first into the steel cage. Devil doesn't looked like he's slowed down from that time off. JL: I agree Josh, Devil sets Curry up for a piledriver but Curry bloacks it and counters with a back body drop...Now Curry picks Devil up and whips him into the ropes...Devil comes off and recieves a clothesline from Curry. JG: Curry in control now as he picks Devil up, oh low blow from Devil and follows with a DDT...Devil asks for the door to be opened and walks to it Curry catches him and the ref closes the door again. JL:Wait a minute, Sinister Shakur and Hustler have come out and they are just standing at the top of the runway. JG: I don't know if they have something planned or they are just trying to play with Devil's mind, or maybe a little bit of both. JL: Curry whips Devil into the ropes, he goes for a back body drop but Devil stops and nails him with an axehandle to the back. Devil is going up to the top rope now...He gets there but Curry is up and nails him...Now Curry is on the second turnbuckle and nails Devil with a superplex. JG: Great move by Curry but that took a lot of him also...Both men are slow in getting up...Both are up but their legs are shakey...They start exchanging punches...Curry is getting the upperhand but Devil grabs his tights and throws him into the cage JL: Devil is starting to climb the cage as Curry is down on the mat...he gets to the middle as Curry is starting to get to his feet...Devil is at the top as Curry sees him and is climbing the cage. JG:Devil gets one foot over but Curry grabs his other foot before Devil can put that one over on the other side...Curry is reallly pulling on that leg and Devil is forced to come back in. JL: Curry climbs up and grabs Devil...he wraps his arms around Devil's waist and he nails and german suplex!!! they both crash down to the mat after that...It looks like they are both out. JG: What an incredible move by Curry...He's the first one to move...Curry is crawling to the cage...He's slowly climbing the cage...He's half-way up now as Devil is starting to come to. JL: Sinister Shakur and Hustler have big smiles on their faces now but they haven't made a move to ringside. JG: Well, now they're leaving. Maybe it was just to play with Devil's mind after all. JL: Devil is now crawling to the cage door and asking for it to be opened...Curry is at the top of the cage and is starting to climb over. Devil is almost at the door!! This is going to be close. JG: I dunno Joey if I was Curry I would just jump off...He's on the other side of the cage now and sees that Devil is almost at the door. JL: No he's climbing over to the door? What is he doing? Devil is almost out of the cage? Curry swings around to the side where the door is...OH!!! Curry just kicked the door in Devil's face!!! JG: Devil falls back in the ring and looks to be out cold...Curry falls down to the floor, that's it!!! Joshua "The Unseen" Curry has won the match and ends Devil's undefeated record in the NAWF. JL: Wow, what a match and we still have two more matches to go!!
