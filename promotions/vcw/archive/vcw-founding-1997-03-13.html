I myself have had a lot of experiencing running and particpating in e-feds. I am the former DPW Co-President and well as the Co-Creator of the Dawg Pound of Wrestling. This will be the arena in which your wrestler will battle it out every week to take his spot as the #1 guy in VCW. Try and picture it now, as you walk down that aisle and just behind the arena doors you cna hear all the happenings and going-ons of a late night casino. Right now we are looking at having the VCW Heavyweight Championship title (not World, this is a regional) and a Television champion. With 2 titles the league will have much competition for each belt and there will be no 'jobbers' with gold around his waist. Our other title will be VCW Heavyweight Tag Team titles...for tag teams of course. =========================== Cards/Supercards =========================== We will be having two weekly cards called Tuesday Night Torment and Casino Nights held on Friday nights. As for PPV's we will not be having any. We will be holding supercards from time to time but in order to give each supercard a special feel most of them will be spaced during the course of the year.
