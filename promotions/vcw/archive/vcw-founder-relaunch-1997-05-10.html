VCW "Looking For QUALITY Members and Writers", 10 May 1997

10 May 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The VCW has been going for 2 monthes now and is still going strong, but I'd like to geta few more good members for this federation. Writers are welcome to, but I'd like to see a sample of your work before I hire you, stuff around 8 or 9K is prefered. If you don't hear from me, you are not in the VCW. But if you are excepted, you'll be put on the mailing list and booked to a match at the earliest free date. V C W _______________________________ Vegas Championship Wrestling _______________________________ Hello, I'm [name withheld] ( [detail withheld] ) here with a new federation . I am hoping to bring a new federation that will be on the cutting edge of E-wrestling today. Are plans are to have 2 weekly cards and frequent Updates with all kinds of hype promoting your wrestlers and bring up to speed on that latest happenings around the VCW, and also expect houseshow results to also be included from time to time. I have had alot of experience running and particpating in e-feds. I Co-Founded the EUWF. Founded Mentally Challeged Wrestling (a parody fed) and was the VP of the NWWF for a short time. I have also written for numerous feds such as WEW, MCW, EUWF, NWWF and has been successful in numerous feds. ================== League Feel/Concept ================== Our federation, the VCW - Vegas Championship wrestling, is a regional league based out of Las Vegas, Nevada. The VCW will be a small fed located out of a casino in Vegas called The Golden Palace. This will be the arena in which your wrestler will battle it out every week to take his spot as the #1 guy in VCW. Try and picture it now, as you walk down that aisle and just behind the arena doors you cna hear all the happenings and going-ons of a late night casino. The fun doesn't stop there, who knows where these brawls could lead to on any given night. Heck, you could find your self spilling out into the Casino duking it out with your hated rival. Being slammed against the slot machines and getting a piledriver on the craps table! ===================== Titles/Championships ===================== We look to make sure every belt here in VCW has meaning to it, this means we will only be carrying the amount of titles that this fed can support with plenty of competition to go around. Right now we are looking at having the VCW Heavyweight Championship title (not World, this is a regional) and a Television champion. With 2 titles the league will have much competition for each belt and there will be no 'jobbers' with goldaround his waist. Our other title will be VCW Heavyweight Tag Team titles...for tag teams of course. =========================== Cards/Supercards =========================== We will be having two weekly cards called Tuesday Night Torment and Casino Nights held on Friday nights. As for PPV's we will not be having any. We will be holding supercards from time to time but in order to give each supercard a special feel most of them will be spaced during the course of the year. Casino Nights Golden Palace Casino VCW Arena Las Vegas, Nevada Approx: 315 Sunday Nights @ 12:00 (Midnight) PST Announcers: TBA Late Night at the Casino is when the stakes are raised and when your gamble really will hurt. Every Friday wrestlers will step into the powder blue ring, with the golden-shine VCW on the middle of the mat grabbing the golden-yellow ring ropes and look out into the crowd. The lower floor level and up into the balconies surrounding the ring area. Big angles very encouraged for this card and hopefully cut-in interviews will also be involved, possibly even commercials to hype your wrestle with their 'style' of merchandise. Wednsday Night Warriors Golden Palace Casino Cards are held in the middle of the casino Las Vegas , Nevada Approx:Whoever is in the casino at the time Tueday Nights @ 11:00 PM PST Annoucers: TBA You'll actually start looking forward to Tuesdays, because of the VCW's Tuesday Night Torment. Torment will be action packed with 5 PBP matches on the card at the very least and some dark matches. Havoc will run rampid throught the Golden Palace Casino as the ring will be set up between the craps table and the slot machines. So you can either hit the jackpot or be hit with the jackpot! Wrestlers might even have some time to do some gambling before their match as well. ==================== Winning and Losing =================== At VCW all winners will be chosen on the the _BEST_ role player is. If you win in RP's you will win your match up. Now, we do not encourage spamming so to cut back on that we have a 1 RP a day rule. If you have more than one fued going on your are allowed 1 RP a day per fued as well. Now that you know this will be RP based, it's up to you if you win or lose. Impress and impress your peers and you will quickly climb up the ladder at Vegas Championship wrestling. So if you are ready sign on please fill out the application below! ==================== Appliacation ==================== SEND APPLIACATION to [detail withheld] Your name: e-mail address: Wrestler: Height: Weight: Hometown: Theme Music: Style: (Aerial/Power/Tech etc....) Heel/Face: Bodyguard/Manager/Valet: Looks/Description of Manager, etc.: Wrestler's Ring Attire: Personality/Attitude: Looks/Physical Description: Wrestlers Primary Style of Wrestling: At least ten moves your wrestler does. (Be sure to list alot of moves in your wrestlers style) Finisher Move: Submission or Regular: Description of Finishing move: ======================== T A G T E A M I N F O ======================== (NOTE: You must fill out singles app for both tag team wrestlers) Tag Team Name: Members of team: Heel/Face: Manger/Valet: Description of Manager: Tag Team finisher: Description of Fin: SAMPLE ROLEPLAY......Send anyone you have laying around.
