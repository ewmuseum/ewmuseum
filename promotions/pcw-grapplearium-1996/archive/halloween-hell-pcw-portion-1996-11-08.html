As requested by several of members of the PCW Talent Roster, I have decided to post PCW matches from EWA's Halloween Hell on here. Look for more regular PCW Cards and such on here. The schedule is usually Tuesdays and Sundays. If you have questions, just email me, and I'll be more than happy to answer them....except the fact that PCW is closed. Well, without further Ado....welcome to the Psychotic. ============================ EWA Halloween Hell PCW Television CHAMPIONSHIP 10 Man Come as You Are ROYAL RUMBLE! ============================ by: [detail withheld]@telerama.lm.com ============================ Rogers - Thank you, EWA! Hello, fans. I'm PCW Broadcaster Paul Rogers, and joining me as always is the Superstar of Wrestling, the one and only Steve Sampson. Steve, tonight we have three great matches to show to the fans of the EWA and PCW, starting out with this one. The Ten Man Come as You are Royal Rumble. Sampson - Amen to that, Pauley. These ten guys are ready to fill the void that lowlife scum Damian Long created when he put Terry Storm out of wrestling. They're ready, willing, and able to destroy each other. Personally, I don't like anyone in the match, with the possible exception of TnT. Rogers - Well, let's get up to EWA Ring announcer Joe Blow for the announcements of our first two competitors. Blow - Ladies and Gentlemen, this contest is a Come as You are Royal Rumble for the Psychotic Championship Wrestling Television Championship. It is officially sactioned by the office of PCW President Owen McDonald. Introducing the wrestlers who drew Numbers 1 and 2, respectively. (Super Blue Ninja strides down the ailse and into the ring. He is in a gi top and Thai fighter shorts.) Blow - From Osaka, Japan. Here is the Super Blue Ninja. (T.R. Ela walks into the ring, fists taped and carrying a handcuffs.) Blow - From Colorado. Here is T.R. Ela! Sampson - Dunkin Donuts is going down! HAHAHHAHAHAH! Rogers - You don't know that Steve. He could be the man who wins this, or it could very well be Super Blue Ninja. *DING, DING* And we're underway here, as Ela charges at Ninja, who has his back turned! Ela hitting him viciously with that nightstick. Ninja goes down and into a fetal position to try to defend against the blows. Sampson - Well, it's about time. Ela's back to his roots, beating people with a nightstick and all. He looks about as at home as he ever has. Rogers - Ela moves over to a corner and yells at some fans. Ninja gets up slowly. *WHACK* Down with the Boken stick. Ninja sets it up in the corner, and whips Ela into it. NO! Ela reverses, and Ninja is stunned! Ela with a clothesline to the back of the head, and Ninja once again down on the mat. Ela picks him up and suplexes him. Ninja looking worse for wear here. Sampson - Worse for wear? The man is getting an ass kicking that people will NEVER forget. Ela's finally showing some kind of spine. Rogers - We're getting down to *10* Seconds to *9* go until our next *8* man comes into the match, and we're all *7* waiting with baited breath. *6* Sampson - I thought that was Ela's underwear. I guess I could be wrong though. 3 2 1 *DING* (Soul Skinner comes to the ring, dressed in steel toed boots and taped fists.) Sampson - SOul Skinner? Awww, Man. How did that dildo get in this match? He couldn't carry Courage's jock. This guy is bad, bad, the worst. Rogers - He's in there, Steve, and he's pounding away on T.R. Ela. Ela now firing back. Both men's fists are being soiled with blood from the other's forehead. Ninja is using this time to...OH MY! Super Blue Ninja just drove that boken up into Ela's unmentionables. OH WOW! Skinner just put one of those steel toed boots to Ela's face. Is Ela ever bleeding now. Sampson - Get a glove!! We need a bloody glove, dammit!! Ela has to frame somebody. It just happens that Skinner gave him the blood to use. I love it. Rogers - And now, Soul Skinner is over onto Ninja, and he's pounding him. Ela's in pretty bad shape. Ninja now, with a hard shot to Skinner, and another. Ela back up, and he puts the boots to Skinner. This is a pretty vicious beatdown that Skinner is recieving. *DING* (TnT Races to the ring, in a flack jacket, jeans and combat boots.) Rogers - TnT in, and what a beautiful back suplex on Ela to get him off Soul Skinner. Skinner shows his thanks by beating on TnT with Ela, as if it was a setup! Ela and Skinner all over TnT, but now Ninja comes in and helps out TnT. TnT up and firing off some heavy rights to Ela. Ninja talking with Skinner about something. Sampson - Probably asking about how many pounds of poop per square inch there are in each others shorts. You know, I hear Ninja used to be called Stinky Brown Gaijin, but my sources have their bad nights, too. Rogers - Ela now beils TnT over. Ninja and Skinner coming up from behind on Ela. OH MY! Ninja just clotheslined Skinner! Ninja picks him up, and Soul Skinner has been eliminated! Sampson - Just an old fashioned butt whipping. The way I like it. He should have tossed Ela over, though. That would have made my freaking night so much better. *DING* ("Rude" Craig Jayhawk makes his way to the ring. Jayhawk is in full Rudey regalia.) Sampson - Oh Dear God, no. Not Mr. Fancy Pants. Why does he have to be here now? Why couldn't he be in an EWA match tonight? WHY! Rogers - Calm down, Steve. Jayhawk is a full time PCW competitor, and as such, welcome in this match. Jayhawk immediately goes after T.R. Ela!!! You know, Ela has said some dispariging things about Jayhawk recently, and it looks like Craig is trying to take out his revenge here tonight. Jayhawk tosses TnT off of Ela and begins to pound him. Look at this! TnT pulls Jayhawk off and shoves him! They're arguing. Sampson - Now if I was Ela, I'd try to take advantage of this! This is too good. I'd try to either get some rest or go to work on them while their backs are turned. Rogers - Ela goes off to work on Super Blue Ninja, who was getting a rest. Ela and Ninja trade punches. Jayhawk and TnT still yelling at each other. OH MY! What a ruse! Jayhawk and TnT were just drawing Ninja and Ela in! A beautiful double drop kick almost sends both Ela and Ninja out of the ring. Sampson - Fair play in the PCW. Bah. That just plain sucks! Rogers - Jayhawk and TnT whip Ela into the ropes, double clothesline! Jayhawk now with a beautiful cross body on Ela, as TnT schoolboys him. Ela's down. *DING* (Thorn walks out to the ring, carrying his crown of thorns (Barbed Wire Crown)) Sampson - Ladies and Gentlemen, business just picked up. This is my first time to actually see this guy up close and personal, and he's a big guy. Look at him! Rogers - Exactly. Thorn hits the ring, and immediately slams that crown of thorns down on Ninja's head. Ninja may be under a mask, but even that can't hide the blood being shed. TnT now pounding on an injured Ela. Ninja bleeding profusely. Jayhawk looks at Thorn for a few seconds, and then flies at him! Jayhawk and Thorn beating each other. Sampson - It's the trail of tears!!! Thorn'll kill him. Jayhawk must have skanked too much, because he's crazy for even flying at Thorn! Rogers - I dunno. Jayhawk has alot of courage. Ninja finally getting that crown off, but he's lost too much blood to mount any offense. Thorn slams Jayhawk down, and then picks up Ninja. He's out of there! Sampson - such is life! Ninja just couldn't hang. *DING* ("Cool" Chris Courage runs into the ring, wearing jeans and a t-shirt) Sampson - Oh God. Look at this. Rogers - Courage pointing at Thorn. Thorn looks at him, and laughs. Courage flies at Thorn! Jayhawk from behind! OH MY! I heard something in Thorn snap! Thorn grimaces, but picks up Courage and chucks him out of the ring! Unbelievable! Sampson - If you didn't know that was going to happen when Courage messed with Thorn, then you're a singular idiot. Rogers - Thorn back to work on Jayhawk, but he is limping quite severely. TnT and Ela still brawling away. Ela with a piledriver on TnT. Jayhawk with a powerslam on Thorn. Thorn screams in pain! Jayhawk puts the boots to him. Ela comes over and helps out Jayhawk. Thorn getting beaten by two men at once now. TnT rises to his feet and pops Jayhawk one! Sampson - It's finally every man for themselves in there! It's about time! Rogers - TnT now with Jayhawk! Gutbuster! TnT locks in the Superbomb! Jayhawk grimacing. Ela looks over at TnT and continues to kick at Thorn. *DING* (Psychrobat comes racing into the ring. The Ringmaster is several yards behind him) Sampson - JESUS! MANIAC ALERT! This man has more jump to him than a room full of jackhammers! D-Day Drew Number 10! Rogers - And Psychrobat returns to the ring, and he clotheslines TnT, making him release the Superbomb. Psychro slides under the bottom rope, leaps to the top, and fires off a beautiful moonsault onto the prone Jayhawk. Ela over to pick Jayhawk up. Jayhawk goes over the top....NO! Ela is infuriated. Ela over on TnT, though. Thorn has the time to stand up. Thorn looks at Psychrobat, but then fires his own body into TnT! TnT falls over the top But NOT to the floor Sampson - He has a Lucky Rabbit's foot. That was just way too close. Rogers - TnT gets back in the ring, and he fires off a couple of left hands to Psychrobat. Psychrobat races out of that danger, and into an Asai Moonsault! He knocked TnT down. Thorn jamming that crown on Jayhawk's head! Sampson - He wants to make him king! That's all. Nothing too vicious. *DING* Rogers - Please. And Dr. D-Day is the last man to enter the ring! One of these gentlemen will be your next PCW Television Champion. D-Day hits the ring and begins to pound on Thorn. Ela and TnT are still going at it, and now, Jayhawk is trying to get a hold of Psychrobat. Sampson - He'd have a better chance getting a greased up hog. Or his girlfriend. Interchangeable parts there. Rogers - Wait a minute! Psychrobat just jumped to the floor. Where the hell is he going? He just eliminated himself from this match. Jayhawk shrugs, and helps TnT lift Ela up. D-Day over to make the save! Sampson - Why hasn't Ringmaster left ringside yet? And why in the hell is he smiling? His protege lost! Rogers - DEAR GOD! Psychrobat is on the Scoreboard!! How the hell did he get up there?! SENTON SPLASH FROM THE SCOREBOARD! He just flattened Jayhawk. Thorn comes over and chucks the Rudey out of the ring. We're down to Ela, Thorn, D-Day and TnT. Thorn pulls D-Day off and OH MY!!! HE LOCKS IN THE CROWN OF THORNS OUT OF NOWHERE! D-Day in serious trouble! TnT and Ela look on...Ela now clips Thorn! Thorn is in agonizing pain from that left leg! Sampson - It's a miracle he held on that long. If I didn't know better, I'd say that Ela was trying to hurt Thorn. Rogers - D-Day trying to regain his composure. TnT comes up from behind Ela! ELA'S GONE!! TnT used a flying forearm to knock Ela over the Top! Ela is furious! Ela has just yanked down the top rope, as Thorn sent TnT into the ropes! TnT is gone! Ela and TnT now brawling on the outside! Thorn and Dr. D-Day are left. All this comotion has given D-Day some time to recover. Sampson - It's not going to help. He's propped against the ropes, and is still hurting from the crown of thorns. Rogers - Thorn over with a clothesline! *DING, DING* Blow - Ladies and Gentlemen. The winner of this match, and NEW PSYCHOTIC Championship Wrestling Television Champion.......... Blow - THORN! ("Stalker Song by Danzig plays as Owen McDonald hands Thorn the brand new PCW Television Championship belt.) Rogers - Give it up for Thorn. He put up one hell of a fight, and so did everyone. I can almost assure you that Dr. D-Day is going to have another shot at that title soon. Sampson - I don't think anything is settled between Ela and TnT, either. They went at each others throats. Psychrobat, he's just crazy. Rogers - Fans, we'll be back! ------------------------------------------------------------------------- Watcher Relm on IRC "what is.... is" Mother Askani "As far as I'm concerned, reality can go take a flying fuck at a rolling doughnut." The main Event of the PCW Portion of Halloween Hell... ========================== EWA Halloween Hell PCW WORLD TITLE Weapons Cage Match Loser gets 10 lashes! Dark Destroyer (C) vs. The Unknown ========================== by: [detail withheld]@telerama.lm.com ========================== Rogers - Fans, we're back, and joining me at this time is Psychotic Championship Wrestling President, Owen McDonald. Mr. McDonald, it is a true honor to have you doing commentary on this match with us. McDonald - My pleasure, Paul. This should be one fine contest between two of the best wrestlers in PCW. I'm really looking forward to this. Rogers - As are we. I'm sure that Dark Destroyer has put his problems with the PCW aside, and will be here tonight. Hasn't he Owen? McDonald - Uhm, *ahem*, This should be a great match. Unknown is a great wrestler, as is his opponent. Rogers - Uh, Okay. Let's get up to Joe Blow with the introductions to this pivotal match! Blow - This contest is scheduled for one fall, and is a special Weapons Cage match, for the Psychotic Championship Wrestling World Heavyweight Championship! The loser of this match will recieve ten lashes with a kendo stick! Introducing first, From the Other Side of Eternity.... (The Music "Just Another Victim" by House of Pain/Helmet rattles off the walls of the arena, and the fans immediately begin to boo.) Blow - ....accompanied to the ring by the Shadowkeeper. Here is the challenger, THE UNKNOWN!!!! (The Unknown and Shadowkeeper come to the ringside area, but instead of Unknown getting in the ring, he waits in the ailse, ready to strike.) Blow - And his opponent, making his home in Grey Mountain, Arizona..... ("Step Back" by the Rollins Band begins to play as the fans immediately begin to rumble with anticipation.) Blow - ....He is a four time former EWA World Heavyweight Champion, and the Current Psychotic Championship Wrestling Heavyweight Champion....here he is! DARK DESTROYER!!! (The fans Explode with cheers, as they await Dark Destroyer's arrival. After a few moments, no one appears. The crowd begins to murmur with questions, as Joe Blow once again gets on the mic.) Blow - ....Here he is! DARK DESTROYER!!!! ("Step Back" continues to play, but still no one comes out from the back.) McDonald - This is terrible. This is tragic. We're going to lose so much face over this. Excuse me for a moment, Mr. Rogers. Rogers - By all means, Owen. Fans, this is incredible. Dark Destroyer seems to have decided that he was better than PCW tonight, and he's not shown up. (Owen makes his way to the ring, and begins to talk with Joe Blow.) Blow - Ladies and Gentlemen, the winner of this match, as a result of a ..... WHOA! (Suddenly, Dark Destroyer shoots out from under the ring, and attacks Unknown, who had his back to the ring in celebration.) Rogers - IT'S DARK DESTROYER! He showed up after all! This is great! Fans, we got us a world heavyweight title match. Here comes Owen McDonald. (Dark D and Unknown finally get in the cage, and the door is shut.) McDonald - Oh thank god, Dark Destroyer showed up. God forbid that Jim Russ have more ammunition against my regime. (Confidence returning to his voice) This should certainly be a great matchup, Paul. These two men are extremely similar in style and build. Unknown is much faster, but Destroyer has a definite weight advantage. Rogers - OH MY! Unknown reverses a whip into the cage, and Dark D hits hard. Unknown grabs a pair of brass knux and is pounding away on Dark D. Unknown up, and he raises Dark D to his feet. DDT! Cover by Unknown!!! 1............. 2................... 3....NO! Dark D just barely escaped that one. Unknown scoops Dark D up. Backbreaker by the Unknown. McDonald - Some fine wrestling ability being shown by the Unknown. If ever you doubted this man being a fine athlete, then you should kick yourself for being wrong. Dark Destroyer needs to make a comeback and soon, or we're going to see a new champion. Rogers - Unknown picks Dark D up. Tombstone coming up. NO! Somehow Dark Destroyer was able to turn the momentum over, and delivers a Tombstone Piledriver of his own. Dark D over and begins choking out Unknown. Picks up the Unknown and throws him into the cage. Dark D now with the knux, and he begins pounding Unknown on the bridge of the nose! That's gonna break his nose. McDonald - This is the kind of action that is synonymous with Psychotic Championship wrestling! Fine efforts being put forth by both men. Of course, Dark D is a former four time EWA Champion, so he is most definitely a great athlete. Rogers - Dark D now with a reverse neckbreaker. Cover by the champion! 1......... 2................. NO! Not quite! Dark D looks over at PCW official Bob Sanford and questions the count. Wait. Look at this! Soul Skinner, Dark Dreamer and Blood Fist are making their ways to ringside. What in the hell are they doing there? McDonald - They have no business being around ringside. If Mr. Skinner wants a shot at Dark Destroyer or Unknown, he should prove himself worthy. This is only going to aggrevate the competitors, and myself. Rogers - They seem to be encouraging the Unknown. Shadowkeeper is giving them hell for being out there. They've got Dark D's attention in the cage. LOOK OUT! Oh My! Unknown up and hits Dark D with a serious chair shot to the neck. Soul Skinner and his entourage cheering now. McDonald - Thank Goodness. Here comes security. It's about time. Mr. Skinner is definitely getting a reprimand from my office in the morning. Rogers - And Unknown with a piledriver on the chair! Cover! 1......... 2.......... 3.....NO!!!! Dark D barely gets his shoulder up. OH MY! Security just clotheslined Skinner. The second security man kicks Blood Fist in the stomach and DDT's him. Dark Dreamer scurries back up the ailseway. Who in the hell were they? McDonald - Don't look now, but one of those security gaurds is coming over. (The security gaurd sits down at the desk and puts on a headphone set) Sampson - Happy Halloween! HAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA! McDonald - Mr. Sampson! What in God's name are you doing? Sampson - Making sure that no one interferes. Besides, do you really want that idiot Skinner within ten feet of a ring? Rogers - Oh MY! While you two were bickering. Unknown brought the chair down on Dark D's knee! Dark D grabbing at it, but Unknown locks in a half crab! Sanford asking Dark D if he wants to submit! Dark D is shaking his head! Unknown sits down on the crab! Dark D.......... REACHES THE ROPES! How in god's name did he do that? Sampson - It's easy if you're almost seven feet tall. Dark D is the champ, anyway. He wants to hold onto that title in the worst ways. Unknown has tasted the gold himself, but he's too weak in the head to be champion again. McDonald - This is fine intestinal fortitude being shown by the champion. Love him or hate him, you have to respect the man. You also have to give credit to the Unknown, because he has put up a fine effort in and of himself tonight. Rogers - Unknown picks Destroyer up and whips him in. Reversal by the wounded champion. BIG SPINEBUSTER!!! What a move by Dark D! It took every last ounce of energy he had to do it, though. Look at the limp from that chairshot, as well. Sampson - It's going to be hard for the ghost with the least to hang on. Sure, he did a great move, but Unknown has just pounded him into the mat. Rogers - Dark D grabs a table! How in the hell did that get in there? He sets the table up, and places Unknown on the top rope. Sampson - This could hurt, I think. Rogers - OH MY WORD!!! Dark D just put Unknown through that table with the Flatliner from the top! Dark D is screaming about his knee, and Unknown is just plain out of it! Dark D moves over for the cover. 1............ 2.......................... 3................ NO!!!!!!!!! HOW IN GOD'S NAME DID HE ESCAPE THAT? Dark D slowly rises to his feet, and Unknown is beginning to stir a bit more. Dark D over to the chair, and he returns the earlier favor with a chairshot of his own. Unknown covers up. Dark D raises the chair again. FIREBALL! Unknown caught Dark Destroyer with a fireball, and Double D is on the mat clutching his face. Unknown gets to his feet....lifting Dark Destroyer up. BLACKOUT!! Blackout by The Unknown! COVER!! 1....................... 2......................... 3..............NO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Dark D got his foot on the ropes! This is just amazing. Sampson - It suprises me that Dark D is even still breathing at the beating he has taken tonight. Same goes for Unknown. This is incredible. McDonald - Incredible? Mr. Sampson, you above all people should know that the PCW is not incredible. It's Psychotic! Sampson - Save your promotions for someone who cares, Owen. Rogers - Unknown over to Dark D again...bearhug. Dark D is fading very fast! Wait a minute, what's that commotion from the back? OH MY GOD!! Steve Stone is running down the aisle!! He just hit the outside referee!! Luckily he can't get into the cage! (The sound of bolt cutters is heard, as Stone gets the lock off the cage) Rogers - Stone in the ring now, and he grabs the chair. *WHACK* and down across the Unknown's back. Stone sets the chair down! OH GOD! He just did the 9-1-1 Face First Powerbomb on Dark D onto the Chair! Oh the carnage!! Stone now with the kendo stick. He's lashing both men ten times! Look at that psycho! Sampson - Brings a tear to my eye. McDonald - I have a decision to make, gentlemen. If you'll excuse me. Rogers - Stone continues his onslaught.. Unknown is finally able to roll out of the ring, Stone has turned his attention from the battered champion, however. He's still pounding on Unknown! 911 on Unknown onto the stairs! Incredible! Sampson - Tsk. Tsk. Such violence. Rogers - Dark D up. He taps Stone on the shoulder. Stone turns around and catches a chairshot!!!! Dark D puts the chair down, Flatliner onto the chair. Listen to the crowd going nuts! Sampson - whoopie. Ghostie is able to hold onto his title via a psychopath named Steve Stone. Blow - PCW President Owen McDonald has informed me that since Mr. Stone had no business getting involved in this match, and did not assist either man, this contest is declared a NO CONTEST! The title cannot change hands on a No Contest, so your PCW Champion is Dark Destroyer! Rogers - Contraversy seems to follow Steve Stone, and tonight was no exception. The Champion is regaining his composure and asking Blow for the microphone. Let's get up to the ring with the champion. DARK DESTROYER - <smile> Still heeeeerrrre. Why? <shrug> Call it renewed interest. Call it a new attraction, but you STILL call ME the MAIN ATTRACTION, folks! [Crowd pops] DARK DESTROYER - First let me apologize to Steven Spector. <deep breath> Man ... I'm sorry I skipped out on you, Edge. I lead you straight into a trap that was designed for me. I was on the verge of abandoning my interests in PCW because they didn't happen to be in my price range anymore. Call me greedy, but old habits die hard you know. [Brushes through his short, yet thick brown hair.] DARK DESTROYER - Someone appeared in PCW on Saturday and went on record as saying ... I "... chickened out". I "... decided I was bored". I "... saw greener pastures". Two out of three ain't bad, Singmaster. Oh yeah, you spew those tunes while 'Bat goes out there and dances. That's what I call shaking your moneymaker. Ringmaster, don't be so rapid in assuming that it's MY job to prove I can beat 'Bat! Been there, done that, and returned to do it again! [Pop] DARK DESTROYER - TWICE I brought your sky surfer down to earth! TWICE I made 'Bat realize that his place in this sport was behind me! You want me to think back to the match that never happened? Ok, I did. 'Bat never showed because he was told not to. Now YOU think back to November! Revive the memory of 'Bat LOSING the EWA World Heavyweight Title in his FIRST defense, and it wasn't much of a defense, believe me, 'cause you weren't in there with us. No, 'Bat isn't the greatest EWA World Heavyweight Champion of all time and in fact he doesn't even approach earning that label. But he DOES earn my respect! He confronted me with the fight of my life at EWA's Showdown. It was his opening to redeem himself. He failed. He didn't gain the EWA championship, but he gained the EWA champion's respect. That was as close as he'd ever get. Three different intitals don't alter that. I've got NOTHING to prove to either of you! YOU are the ones with this issue tearing your insides apart! YOU have something to prove to ME! So let's seal the deal, 'Bat! Come on out here and shake my hand, and let's jump into round three! (Psychrobat and the Ringmaster make their way to the cage from the back. They pause shortly before entering the ring, and look at Destroyer. Finally they get into the ring.) Rogers - This could be interesting. Ringmaster has made it no secret that Dark Destroyer isn't on his favorite people list right now. Ringmaster now saying something in Indian, it would appear. Psychrobat walks over to the waiting Dark Destroyer. Sampson - PUNCH HIM! Rogers - Ringmaster...oh wait a minute. This is a setup! Look at the Ringmaster! He's behind Dark D!! Psychrobat extends his arm to Dark D! They've shaken hands! LOOK OUT CHAMP! (Dark Destroyer turns around and sees Ringmaster behind him. Ringmaster is holding his whip in both hands, but releases the grip on the tail with his right hand.) Rogers - Well, wait a minute...maybe they're not here to fight Dark D. Maybe they're here to see him regain his form. Sampson - That's bull. You know Ringmaster would have hit him with that whip in another second. Rogers - Psychrobat and the Ringmaster are walking out of the ring. Dark D raises his arms in victory to the crowd, and listen to the cheers. Fans, it's been a blast. For Steve Sampson, PCW President Owen McDonald and the entire PCW Staff, this is Paul Rogers. Enjoy the rest of Halloween Hell.
