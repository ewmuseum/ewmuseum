PCW — Regional Promotion — Our first card (2 matches)

8 February 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Here's our first card. To join, write to me or to [detail withheld] for all the info. -------------- {As "Bad to the Bone" plays and as slow motion shots of wrestling action in a rather dilapidated and blood-stained ring are shown, the words "PCW Northwest Wrestling" appear on the screen.} {Two men are seen at a desk about 10 feet higher than the ring. They're in the area called the crow's nest, where interviews take place and where the announcers sit. The ring is behind them as they sit at the desk. Eugene Curtis: Hello everyone, and welcome to our first edition of PCW Northwest Wrestling! Wrestling is back in Portland and back in the Northwest. And, fans, we've joined the online world through our working agreement with FASW! Now, the Northwest Legend, the man who will be joining me each-- Larry "The Chainsaw" Gaines: Hey, there Euge. You want action? You want blood? You want a hotdog and a pepsi for $5.95? Well, PCW is the place where the wrestling is in your face! Eugene: I should have called you the man who will be _interrupting_ me each week, not the man who will be _joining_ me. Anyway, let's get down to the ring for our first match with our promoter, Owen McDonald. {Camera then zooms to the ring, visible behind the announcers' shoulders.} {An elderly man steps through the ropes and into the ring, then fumbles for the house mike...} Owen: Welcome to Northwest Wrasslin here at the Grapplearium in Portland. Our first match tonight is one fall, and is part of the PCW Championship Tournament. {Opera music begins.} Coming down the aisle, from Beverly Hills, weighing 240 pounds, Francis Quincy Cleveland! {Frans boo and throw paper as he steps through the ropes} Larry: This man has class, but I'd still kick his ass. Eugene: Careful, Chainsaw. The wrestlers do come up here for interviews. Owen: And his opponent. {Theme from _Leave It to Beaver_ plays.} He weighs 302 pounds, from parts unknown, here is "5150" John Doe! {Steps through the ropes.} Eugene: What do you make of his outfit, Larry? He's dressed up like a family man from the fifities. Coat, tie, even a pipe? But the name "5150" is police code for the criminally insane? He doesn't look insane. What gives? Larry: You don't have to _look_ insane to _be_ insane, Gene. You should know that. Eugene: Whatever. {Bell rings.} Well, Larry, Doe is just standing there fiddling with his pipe, and Cleveland is walking slowly over to him. Doe isn't paying attention. He's-- OUCH! Out of nowhere, Doe jabs his fingers right in the Adam's Apple of F.Q. Cleveland. Cleveland's keeled over! A boot to the midsection! [... the bout continues; Doe overpowers Cleveland, whose corner nurse's "bandages" turn out to be soaked in chloroform ...] Eugene: Doe, hooking up Cleveland for a power bomb! The Nurse, again, comes over and grabs Doe by the hand. He releases the hold, Cleveland falls limply to the mat. She's saying someting, we can't hear, and they both leave the ring. Cleveland is still out. [Cleveland is revived, storms to the interview desk, and challenges Doe to a stretcher-match rematch, which Doe accepts. The broadcast moves to the second match.] Eugene: ...And now down to the ring for our second match of the night: The Warden versus "Hollywood" Johnny Dreamer. Let's go to Owen McDonald. This match is one fall, and is part of the PCW Championship Tournament. Coming down the aisle, from Hollywood, weighing 237 pounds, Hollywood Johnny Dreamer! {He steps through the ropes} Larry: Or, as I like to call him, Dreamdust. Owen: And his opponent. He weighs 240 pounds, from parts unknown, here is The Warden! [The two wrestle a hard-fought, back-and-forth bout, trading finishing maneuvers, until both men's shoulders are down for a simultaneous three-count.] Eugene: Warden begins to rise...Dreamer rolls him up. 1...2...he kicks out. [...] He picks up Cleveland. He hooks the leg for the pin...1...2...Warden shifts weight, Dreamer down...1...2...Dreamer shifts again, now Warden's shoulders are down...2...and Warden kicks out. He climbs to the second rope, hooks him up for a belly to back superplex, lifts him up and...down. Pinning predicament. 1...2...3! {Warden raises both arms in the air.} Owen: Your winner, who will now advance to the next round... {Referee comes over to Owen, whispers something to him.} Owen: The referee has informed me that both men's shoulders were down for the 3-count. Therefore, this match is a draw! They will wrestle with a 10 minute time limit next week to determine who will advance in the tournament. If no one wins in 10 minutes, neither one will advance. The winner would face Silencer in the next round! Eugene: Aaaack! {Camera shot moves from ring to Crow's Nest desk to show that Eugene has been startled by none other than the Witch King! Witch King: You didn't play my interview on your update. Well, I want you to play it. Now. {Video begins} {emerging from the shadows, sitting on a dark thrown, and wearing full black dress royal attire, and a Golden Crown} Witch King: Ahhh... the PWC tournament for the title. And the Witch King's chance to showcase his talent here in the FASW, you have not seen the Witch King in action, sure you think that you have but you have seen this bad luck and the bad jokes that have been plagueing the Witch King, you haven't seen the Witch King who, in other federations, has held three world titles, a Galactic title, an intercontentinal title, and currently holds a world title and is the current Commissioner's Cup Champion in the NWC. No you haven't seen everything that the Witch King is capable of. But thanks to the PCW you will get to see that, because in the tournment I plan on destroying everyone and winning the title. Billy, you seem to be the frount runner, that will soon change, as well it will change with every other ranked wrestler in the tournament. You will all learn to accept the darkness and to bow down to your King, the ONLY King in the FASW and future PCW Champion, the Witch King. {The Witch King is over taken by the shadows once again} {Video ends, shot of announcers at desk now.} Eugene: Well, that was a very nice interview, Witch...Where did he go? Larry: I don't know. Maybe you were hallucinating. Eugene: Well, that's all for tonight's edition of PCW Wrestling. Next week, Crater will face the Ultimate Gladiator, newly entered Witch King will face Killer Hulk, and Warden will have a 10 minute rematch with Hollywood Johnny Dreamer! So long everybody, from the Grapplearium! PCW Episode #1
