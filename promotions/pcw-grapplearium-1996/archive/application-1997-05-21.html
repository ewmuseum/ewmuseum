[PCW] Application

21 May 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PSYCHOTIC CHAMPIONSHIP WRESTLING! ------------------------------ Wrestler's Name: Height: Weight: From: Entrance Music: Looks: Attitude: Describe personality, motivation, etc.: Experience?: Ring attire: Finishing move: Describe: Do you have a manager or valet? What are your advantages and/or disadvantages? (Optional) A - D- Wrestler's style Primary style - Secondary style - What are at least ten, if not more, of your favorite moves? Please indicate your agreement to the following: "I understand PCW is a roleplaying league. Not everyone can win all the time, but those who roleplay well will have their opportunity to shine and amass victories. I understand that if I don't roleplay, I may become the unfortunate victim of periodic roster trimmings. I agree to live with all this." I agree (sign your name here): Okay, then. ENJOY THE PCW! %%%%%%%%%%%%%%% USUAL REMINDERS %%%%%%%%%%%%%%% Thanks to Curt for doing this originally. This rocks, and almost every fed uses it now :) NOTE: I can't emphasize this enough: if you want angles done, make sure the person writing that card gets them! WHAT TO SEND? - TO GET MATCHES: Challenge, Challenge, Challenge! - TO WIN: Roleplay, Roleplay, and (did I mention?) Roleplay! - ALSO IT HELPS TO: Do Crow's Nest (Grapplearium) AND In Ring (Tuesday FEAR) interviews! Just leave space for the announcers to ask questions (or even write them yourself). You fill in the answers or whatever and we'll do the rest! - AND IT NEVER HURTS TO: Send in a STRAT! That's short for STRATEGY. A strategy would include how you want your guy to go about wrestling the match, or any sneak attacks/double crosses that you want to see happen. This is not required every time...just when you want something special to happen. It REALLY helps improve the angles and plotlines. We can't always follow your suggestion to a "T," but we will try. %%%%%%%%%%%%%%% ROLL OF CHAMPIONS %%%%%%%%%%%%%% SINGLES: -------- PCW World Heavyweight Champion --> "Hardcore" Matt Harris PCW Columbia Valley Champion --> The Extremist PCW Television Champion --> "Maniac" Joe Colosi TAG TEAMS: ---------- PCW WORLD Tag Team Champions --> Crusher Tech %%%%%%%%%%%%%% BOOKERMAN SPEAKS! %%%%%%%%%%%%%% PCW: ROLEPLAY RULES *** 3 RPs a week, 1 Character Dev. RP (doesn't count toward matches), and 1 Crow's Nest *** Angles/Strats will count for the match when the decision comes down the wire. Only if it's the only thing that will help determine who wins will it factor into the decision. Just have fun and STAY ACTIVE! :) [The document's own staff and writer directory, and its list of submission addresses paired with named handlers, are withheld here as out-of-character business credits, per the museum's privacy rule; the promotion's own roleplay rules, application, and roll of champions above are unaffected.]
