[PCW] New Year's Bash, PCW portion

2 January 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The promotion's own portion of a joint New Year's pay-per-view shared with the EWA and DPW. Backstage, interviewer Paul Rogers previews the Tag Team Championship challenge to come:] Paul: "Good evening gentlemen, I am Paul Rogers, PCW interviewer...You two have quite a match ahead of yourselves today. Barry Hart and Jack Kelly." Mark "Tech" Stephens: "This is not singles. This match is BARELY wrestling, this is a caged baseball bat match, this is not an athletic contest, this is a barbaric street fight." Steve "Crusher" Kingston: "And that's just how I like it!!" Mark: "You see, Crusher Tech got it's first big break here in PCW and this is where we will be staying. Of the three federations which we currently hold the World titles, PCW is by far the most precious." Paul: "Well there you have it, Crusher Tech, the PCW, EWA, and CHAOS!!! World Tag Team Champions, but will they still have the distinction of PCW champion after this night? We'll just have to wait and see..." ---------------------------------------------------------------------- PCW COLUMBIA VALLEY TITLE MATCH Drago vs. The Prophet Gaines: "Drago has held the title since defeating Mr. Deadly a few months back, who has not been able to regain it from Drago, who he is still at odds with." Owen McDonald: "Our next bout is set for a 60 minute time limit, and is for the PCW COLUMBIA VALLEY TITLE! First, the challenger...on his mission from God...please welcome...THE PROPHET." [The two wrestle a full hour-limit main event; Drago retains.] Owen McDonald: "Here is your winner, at 34:13...AND STILL PCW COLUMBIA VALLEY CHAMPION...DRAGO!" ---------------------------------------------------------------------- [A gimmick "Luck of the Draw" Wheel of Torture stipulation match also aired on this card; its own result is not preserved clearly enough in this corpus to structure.]
