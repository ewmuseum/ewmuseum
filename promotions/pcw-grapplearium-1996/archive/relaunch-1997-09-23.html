Pacific Coast Wrestling: BACK under original management!

23 September 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Pacific Coast Wrestling: BACK under original management! [Owen McDonald's history is narrated in full: an elderly Northwest promoter, the fictional founder-figure of the Grapplearium, whose promotion is now returning to its original name and its original operator after a run under a second name and a change of hands.] This time, Owen will resist the urge to go national. This time, Owen will only sign small names. Young rookies and washed-up veterans who come cheap. Owen knows you don't need overinflated egos with million-dollar contracts to entertain people. Good old-fashioned action puts asses in the seats. And action...is what Owen will provide. All he needs... -----------------------------> ... is YOU. <----------------------------- The timeline: - Apply now, or at least send a note, to [detail withheld]. Application below. - I want to sign wrestlers by Sept. 26 and start roleplaying at that time. This is a regional fed, so while I might bring in big stars for VERY special occasions, the permanent employees will be rookies, or veterans past their prime. This is your chance to build your new wrestler from a rookie up to stardom and eventually the big leagues. - I want to hold the first card Oct. 3. Cards will appear every friday night, and will contain three live matches and four darks. - I'll be doing the booking and mailing of roleplays. - Cards will take place at the Grapplearium in Portland. Wins will be decided on roleplays, strats, reps...and STORYLINE (booker's creative decisions). The best won't always win...but the best WILL have the best record. [The application form itself follows the same shape as the promotion's standing template, asking for a wrestler's name, look, biography, and finishing move.] FINAL INSTRUCTIONS *** Okay, now go back through, and take out all the instructional lines. The ones that begin with "***." Including this one. We don't need those. Then mail the sucker to [detail withheld]. Be sure and cc: a copy to yourself for future reference. Good luck, and may the best wrestler win! [A second, brief post the same day endorses the relaunch and encourages sign-ups.]
