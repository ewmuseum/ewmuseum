[PCW] LIVE! At The Grapplearium 11/10/96

10 November 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PCW: LIVE! At the Grapplearium November 10, 1996 1. PCW Television Championship: Champion vs. "Master Of Pain" Barry Hart 2. The Return of Johnny Coles: Johnny Coles vs. Soul Skinner 3. PCW Tag Team Championship: Devil's Disciples vs. Crusher Tech 4. Debut of Hardcore Harris: Hardcore Harris vs. "Cool" Chris Courage 5. Debut of Christian Kingsworth: Christian Kingsworth vs. Iceman 6. Tag Team Explosion: Sin & Salvation vs. Gangsta's Paradise 7. The Return of Kevin Jade: Kevin Jade vs. Dr. D-Day 8. One Last Time...In A Steel Cage: T.R. Ela vs. Ultimate Maniac *Anyone who interferes will be suspended until March ---------------------------------------------------------------------- PCW WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP MATCH Devil's Disciples (champions) vs. Crusher Tech (challengers) Owen McDonald: "Our next bout is tag team action scheduled for one fall, this will also be contested for the PCW WORLD Tag Team Titles. First, the challengers, hailing from the city of Boston...they are Steve "Crusher" Kingston, and Mark "Tech" Stephens, they are, CRUSHER TECH!" Owen McDonald: "And their opponents, they hail from...The Road, at a total combined weight of 566 pounds, they are accompanied to the ring by Sexy Mama, and are the PCW WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS...they are Iron Head Harley, and Chopper Davidson...the DEVIL'S DISCIPLES!" Gaines: "Kingston has nicely come in and done what his partner could not, and that is control the Disciples...but CHOPPER CLOTHESLINES HIM OVER THE TOP ROPE! Iron Head in the meantime with a SUPERPLEX ON KINGSTON! Now Chopper is up on top...TOP ROPE LEGDROP! Thats the CHOP SHOP DROP! 1...2.....3............." Owen McDonald: "Your winners...and STILL PCW WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS... THE DEVIL'S DISCIPLES!!!" ---------------------------------------------------------------------- DEBUT OF HARDCORE HARRIS Hardcore Harris vs. "Cool" Chris Courage Jim Russ (in his own weekly aside) narrates the squash: "Harris powerbombed this scrub, gave him numerous reverse neckbreakers, hit him with a Doctor Bomb, top-rope DDT, hell, he hit him with his entire arsenal." Harris, a 6'7", 260-pound New York City native, dominates from bell to bell in his debut. ---------------------------------------------------------------------- ONE LAST TIME...IN A STEEL CAGE T.R. Ela vs. Ultimate Maniac Owen McDonald introduces Ela as "a former 2 time PCW Television Champion" and Ultimate Maniac as "a former PCW Television Champion, and a former member of the Den of Destruction." Both men climb to the top of the cage together. Gaines: "Both men are climbing the wall...both men reaching the top of the cage at the same time! Now they are going over...and ULTIMATE MANIAC JUMPS! THERE GOES ELA!" Gaines: "Both men hit the floor nearly at the EXACT same time..." Owen McDonald: "Here is your winner at 11:19 into the match...THE ULTIMAAAAAAAAATE MAAAAAANNIIIIAAAAAAC!!!" Gaines: "Ela is walking over to Ultimate Maniac..and...THEY SHAKE HANDS! THAT is well deserved respect!" ---------------------------------------------------------------------- RETURN OF J. COLES! Johnny "Cool" Coles vs. Soul Skinner Jim Russ's own aside admits he skipped watching most of the match, but reports: "Coles dominated from start to finish, and kicked some ass... Coles won, I do know that much." Winner - Johnny "Cool" Coles ---------------------------------------------------------------------- TAG TEAM EXPLOSION! Sin & Salvation vs. Gangsta's Paradise Jim Russ, introducing the newly arrived Sin & Salvation (Gary Grayson and Caleb Temple): "Sin & Salvation destroyed the two Gangsta's...Temple got Nibs in his finisher, The Last Rites, which is a Scorpion Deathlock, and one of the stupid fuckers gave up." Winner - Sin & Salvation ---------------------------------------------------------------------- THE RETURN OF KEVIN JADE "King of Excuses" Kevin Jade vs. Dr. D-Day A chaotic comedy finish: Iron Man Jack Kelly interferes against Jade at ringside, and Dr. D-Day capitalizes. "That's when Dr. D-Day got up, and...hit his FINISHER, The Landmine... 1-2-3....Dr. D-Day wins!" Winner - Dr. D-Day ---------------------------------------------------------------------- DEBUT OF CHRISTIAN KINGSWORTH Christian Kingsworth vs. Iceman Iceman dominates early, but a fan named Funklord seizes a chair meant for Kingsworth and the match breaks down. Owen McDonald: "Your winner of this bout as a result of a countout... CHRISTIAN KINGSWORTH!" ---------------------------------------------------------------------- -MAIN EVENT- PCW TELEVISION TITLE MATCH THORN (champion) vs. Barry Hart Owen McDonald: "And his opponent, hailing from Detroit, Michigan...he is the CURRENT PCW Television Champion...he is...THORN!!" [Barry Hart, "The Master Of Pain," plays to the crowd with a running comedy routine before the bell, then wrestles a competitive match that turns on one lapse in concentration.] Gaines: "Barry Hart now coming up onto THORN...and NAILS him with a DDT! Barry Hart goes for the cover...1 2 3!!! NO!!!" Gaines: "...he is talking to some lady in the front row." Irvine: "Do you see the hooters on that chick!? BARRY HART IS THE MAN!" Gaines: "Not right now, THORN comes up behind him...BRIDGING BACK SUPLEX! 1 2 3!!!" Gaines: "A beginners mistake cost a veteran in Barry Hart the match..." [Thorn retains the Television Championship.]
