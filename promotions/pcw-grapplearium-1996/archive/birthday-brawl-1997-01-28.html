[PCW] Birthday Brawl

28 January 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Fade in on the PCW World Title. A picture of Dark Destroyer, the champion who parted ways with the promotion in December. A voice-over narrates: "In November, the champion and the PCW parted ways, and PCW has been without a World Champion since then." Tonight's Bunkhouse Brawl, sanctioned by the PCW Board of Directors, will crown a new champion.] George Jefferson: "Ladies and Gentlemen...Your new Psychotic Championship [Wrestling World Heavyweight Champion is about to be crowned in the Bunkhouse Brawl.]" [The broadcast's own surviving text is damaged by a capture defect: five of the card's eight parts end mid-sentence, spliced onto an unrelated Google Groups thread listing, and the Bunkhouse Brawl's own finish -- along with a Television Championship challenge from T'n'T earlier on the card -- is lost to it. The promotion's own later broadcasts and a rival federation's own contemporaneous interview both credit Christian Kingsworth as the wrestler crowned that night; commentary elsewhere on the card separately credits the Rednecks with winning the Tag Team Championship the same evening. Neither result is stated in the surviving text itself.] ---------------------------------------------------------------------- PCW COLUMBIA VALLEY TITLE MATCH, FALLS COUNT ANYWHERE Drago (champion) vs. Thorn [Thorn, the reigning Television Champion, challenges up a weight class for Drago's title in a hard-fought falls-count-anywhere bout.] Ring Announcer: "The winner of this match at 29:59.........AND _STILL_ PCW COLUMBIA VALLEY CHAMPION...DRRRAAAAGOOOO!!!" ---------------------------------------------------------------------- [Elsewhere on the card, "Iron Man" Jack Kelly is credited by a broadcaster during the Bunkhouse Brawl with injuring promoter Owen McDonald, and commentary gives credit "to the Rednecks for becoming the NEW PCW World Tag Team Champions" -- the tag match itself, and its opponents, do not survive.]
