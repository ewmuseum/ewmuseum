[PCW] Spring Showdown

18 May 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
SPRING SHOWDOWN -- Pittsburgh Civic Arena, Pittsburgh, PA Card: 1. [Main Event] PCW World Heavyweight Title Match: Captain Midnight vs. "Hardcore" Matt Harris vs. "Iron Man" Jack Kelly 2. [PCW Columbia Valley Title Match] The Extremist vs. "American Nightmare" Gary Grayson 3. [PCW World Tag Team Title Match/Caged Elimination] Crusher Tech vs. Bigger Than Hollywood 4. [PCW Television Title Match] "Maniac" Joe Colosi vs. Dimension (plus undercard matches involving MLWO- and non-PCW-billed talent, not this promotion's own story) ---------------------------------------------------------------------- PCW TELEVISION TITLE MATCH "Maniac" Joe Colosi (c) w/Pamela vs. Dimension w/Virginia Owen: "...weighing in at two hundred eighty three pounds...he is the current PCW Television Champion..here is...'MANIAC' JOE COLOSI!!!!" [An even contest between the two.] Owen: "The winner of the match at 11:07...and STILL PCW Television [Champion...Joe Colosi.]" Joe (announcer): "Colosi retains his Television title.." ---------------------------------------------------------------------- PCW COLUMBIA VALLEY TITLE MATCH The Extremist (c) vs. "American Nightmare" Gary Grayson Owen McDonald: "...coming to the ring...he is your PSYCHOTIC CHAMPIONSHIP WRESTLING COLUMBIA VALLEY CHAMPION, he is.....THE EXTREMIST!" Owen McDonald: "...led by Tommy B and FOLLOWED by THORN.....here is, the challenger, BUT the man with the belt.............THE AMERICAN NIGHT....MARE............GARRRRYYYY GRAYSON!" [Thorn is at ringside already carrying the physical championship belt when Grayson comes to the ring; the match ends without a fall.] Owen McDonald: "Uhh...Fans, this match has a result of a NO CONTEST, therefor the EXTREMIST is still Columbia Valley Champion, and he gets the belt back, physically as well." ---------------------------------------------------------------------- PCW WORLD TAG TEAM TITLE MATCH, CAGED ELIMINATION Crusher Tech (challengers) vs. Bigger Than Hollywood (c) Gaines: "Crusher Tech, the world tag team champs' entrance music..." [A hard-fought elimination match inside a steel cage.] Owen McDonald: "Your winners of this match at 45:13 into the match.... [Still PCW and DPW WORLD Tag Team] CHAMPIONS...CRUUUUUUUUSSSHHHHHEEERRRRR TEEEEEEECCCCHHHH!!!!!" ---------------------------------------------------------------------- [MAIN EVENT] PCW WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT TITLE MATCH Captain Midnight vs. "Hardcore" Matt Harris vs. "Iron Man" Jack Kelly [A chaotic three-way. Midnight pins Harris for what is announced as a disqualification win, then refuses the title, throwing the belt and microphone from the ring and demanding the match continue.] Captain Midnight: "HARRIS! Get back out here!...Midnight doesn't fall on a technicality! This belt hasn't been won yet!...We...end it...NOW!" [The two brawl to the finish.] Brian Nelson: "Harris picks up Midnight and hoists him up to the top turnbuckle!...OVERHEAD BELLY-TO-BELLY SUPERPLEX!...HARDCORE CUTTER!!!" Owen McDonald: "Ladies and gentlemen...the winner of this match via PINFALL...and NNNNEEEEEWWWWW PCW WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION.......... 'HARDCORE' MATT HARRIS!!!!!" [Steve Sampson, who had earlier challenged Gaines for this same title and lost, storms the ring after the bell and attacks the new champion with a chair before being pulled away by security.]
