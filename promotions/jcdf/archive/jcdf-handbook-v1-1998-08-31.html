Table Of Contents ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ 1) Introduction. 2) How this fed works? 3) Card. 4) PPV's 5) Titles 6) Application 1. Introduction ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Hello and welcome to the JCDF. I know, i know there are many new feds on the net, but this is going to be diffrent. How? You get to win every match! And not only win, but win against one of the biggest stars in e-wrestling, "Mr Main Event" Chris Douglas. Read the rest of the handbook, and i hope you'll like it and join. 2. How this fed works? ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ I know, i know you must be asking yourself "How can Chris Douglas compete so many matches? Will he want to join the fed that will job him every day?". Well, not a problem. The JCDF has many Chris Douglas's clones, nad Midget Chris Douglases, so you can beat them. Of course, if Douglas will join, he will be the loser of the main events. How to win a match? ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ To win a match, all you have to do is RP and trash talk Douglas. Humiliate him as much as you want in your RP's, that's fine with me. Also, it never hurt to send a strat. In the strat, i expect you to write how your wrestler will humiliate Chris Douglas (like sticking a goat's horn in his ass). This will make this fed much more fun! 3.Cards ~~~~~~~ There are two cards a week in the JCDF: Tuesday Night Crush - The minor card of the JCDF. Something like 4-6 summerized matches. This card will be for the mid - carders, and they will face mostly midget Douglas's. Saturday Night Humiliation - The big card. 4-8 matches, in which the superstars of the JCDF will humiliate Douglas's clones. In the main event, if Douglas himself will join this fed, will be Chris Douglas losing to someone. 4.PPV's ~~~~~~~ We'll have only 4 PPV per year: The Big Throw: 30 man big Battle Royal. All Participants beside Chris Douglas will be declared as winners after they'll toss Chris Douglas over the top rope 30 times. King of losers - Chris Douglas will lose to 8 man in the same night, to be diclared as "King of Losers". Unsurvival Series - teams of 4 Douglas's clones and midgets facing teams of 4 JCDF superstars. In the main event, Chris Douglas will lose to his one clone. Douglasmania - Just a regular PPV's, all of them include Douglas's clones/midgets loses. The main evet will feature Douglas losing to a famous celebrity. 5.Titles ~~~~~~~~ Since the only matches are with Chris Douglas's and his clones/nidgets, there is no way you can lose a title. So, every man that will win a match will get a title. The title will be named after the most humiliating move you excuted on Douglas during the match. Ex: The most prestigious title in the JCDF right now is the "Goat's horns in Douglas A**" Title. 6. Application ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ (boring stuff) Your name: Your E-mail: IRC nickname: Character name: Height: Weight: Alignment: Ring Style: Manager and description(If any) 10 favorits moves: Background: gimmick: Advantages/Disadvantages: (Fun stuff) Past experience with beating Chris Douglas: Weapons he'll use on Douglas: Special moves he'll humiliate Douglas with: Special things he'll do after beating Douglas: That's it i think. for any more info e-mail me.
