AWF : Mid-South Kings

August 28, 2001, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I don't like to bore people with the crappy letter bull, so I'm going to make this short and sweet.
I've ran a few feds before that either I've lost interest, or my wrestlers just didn't feel like doing anything. I eventually lost all interest in running a fed all together.
Recently, I was given the chance to write two matches for a fed I've been involved with since 1999, and for the first time since my first fed was open, I felt I could write a whole card again.
Now, I have had this fed built for six months, and I'm ready to debut it, but I need your help. I need the competitors to make this great.
Even if you don't feel like joining, but know someone looking for just the right fed, send them the link.
I will work with you all on angles, storylines, matches, whatever you like. I have loved it when I've been given that opportunity, so I am willing to share it with anyone joining AWF.
So, check us out at: http://geocities.com/awf_2k1/awfindex.html and e-mail us at [detail withheld] And thanks for your time.
