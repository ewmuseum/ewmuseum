[NACW] Press Conference

14 August 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
If you like what you see, drop an e-mail to [detail withheld] and ask for a handbook. [We fade in to the Boathouse in Norfolk, Virginia. There is actually a decent turnout for the press conference that will start off the new era of
North American Championship Wrestling.about 550, 600 people. To some, that may mean failure. However, considering the size of the Boathouse, that's good: it means that the room is safely filled. There is a podium on one corner of the small venue. In the very center sits the NACW ring, very basic. Black ropes, white padded turnbuckles, white canvas with the NACW letters in plain black lettering, and a black ring skirt with "NACW" in the center of each skirt in plain white lettering. Much like the old NWA
Mid-Atlantic ring from 1983. Suddenly, we fade down to ringside, as Dan Mazin and Steve White sit at the table. White is wearing a basic gray and white suit with a black tie, and is in his early-40s with graying black hair. Mazin is in a black NACW polo shirt and jeans, is in his mid-30s, and has dark brown hair. White begins to speak.]
SW: Ladies and gentlemen, it is great to be back in action! While it's a press conference, North American Championship Wrestling has returned with a furious vengeance, and we're going to showcase the best in old-school wrestling with new school attitude. And speaking of attitude.
DM: Steve, I got sick of your jokes a long time back, so don't start. SW: Funny, I'd say the same for you. DM: Yeah, but you missed me. SW (rolling his eyes): Anyway, my name is Steve White, and joining me as usual is Dan Mazin. We'll kick things off in just a few minutes, but right away, NACW has taken the wrestling world by storm again.
DM: Yeah, but look at the guy they hired as the commissioner. Jeremy Rhodes? I mean, granted, he was an okay wrestler, but why him? SW: Jeremy Rhodes was hired to keep order here in NACW, and now that he's retired, he can bring more time to that position.
DM: Whatever. That guy thrived on chaos, and the boss puts him in power? I don't get it. SW: Either way, he will announce the signing of several wrestlers to NACW contract tonight, but we already know two men who are ready to roll.
Long-time NACW veteran Vaggy Magoo . DM: Oh lord . SW: . will go up against Stephen C. Bourdo. Bourdo has been making a name for himself in N.E.W., and looks like he's ready to shake things up here in
NACW. DM: As long as Bourdo takes out that cabbage-munching freak, I'm fine. [The grinding sounds of "Spit It Out" by Slipknot begin to play.] SW: And that music means our commissioner is on his way! Let's go to the podium. [The camera angle shifts to the podium. No special lights or anything, but "Supreme Being" Jeremy Rhodes makes his way up to the podium to a respectable round of applause. Rhodes looks nothing like most commissioners, opting for a black Slipknot T-shirt, which isn't tucked, and a pair of blue jeans, as well as a pair of army boots. Rhodes makes his way to the podium as his music cuts and he speaks, with his soft British accent.]
SBJR: Ladies and gentlemen of the press, as well as you wonderful fans here in Norfolk, I'd like to welcome you to the first step to the return of North
American Championship Wrestling. This press conference is to introduce you to some of the wrestlers we've signed, as well as to start our drive to recruit some of the best talent we can get. To kick things off, let's meet a man who has made a big impact on Chicago, and we'll see if he can do the same for the rest of North America . "Windy City" Dan Stevens!
[A large screen lowers, as a promo begins to play. Fade into a colorful apartment. Gray Carpet, and pictures of a handsome man framed professionally in seemingly the perfect spots. We turn to the right and find two neon green sofas, and a small patch of bleach blonde hair sticking out and slight noise and the words "NACW" in blue letters on a thirty-two-inch Sony Television.
The letters than disappear, and a six-foot-four man wearing a "Chicago Owns You" Black and White T-Shirt, and Red And1 Basketball Shorts paces slowly towards the camera.
He then stands by one of his pictures, and then speaks.] DS: Hello, NACW. Welcome, to your windy city nightmare! [Winks, and then puts a broad smile on his face.] Dan Stevens is the name, and I plan to stick around for a while. Mr. Russell gives me the call, I respond and wha-la! The "windy city" man himself has arrived. I owe it to the city of Chicago, and to my family, to give everyone under contract in the NACW a nice little taste of.
Sweet Home Chicago. Wrestling is my goal in life now; it's my career choice. I've never failed at anything. [Dan smiles once again.] And don't expect me to let my city down. [The big screen rises, as the camera angle shifts back to Rhodes.] SBJR: That's just some of the talent that is making its way here. Now, I'd like to announce the signing of Bilal Masai. However, Mr. Masai was unable to be here today, and he apologizes for his absence. As you can see, though, NACW will have something for everyone, but we'll also stick true to our morals. We promise that WRESTLING will be the entertainment, and our action will be wrestling that you can enjoy. And now, a man who will prove me right on that statement, Brian J. Blottie!
[The press rises to their feet, looking expectantly at the entrance area. But, strangely, no one comes out. Jeremy Rhodes begins to fidget a bit, and looks about ready to say something into the mic. Suddenly, a man in a very nice suit, white hair, and a white mustache begins to wheel a TV/VCR combo down the middle of the aisle, through the press core. The reporters begin to buzz, as the man arrives at the front of the room, unrolls an electrical cord, and plugs it in. He turns on the TV, and presses a button next to the
VCR section. The TV springs to life, and we see a very clean-cut, very young looking face on the screen. The young man's short brown hair and clean-shaven look almost screams dignified, and the wicked grin that spreads from ear to ear makes you wonder just what this man may have planned.]
Young man: Ladies and gentlemen, I'm sorry I couldn't be there. I had some urgent business to take care of, but I did find the time to record this video for you to become familiar with me. For those who do not know, I am
"The True Living Legend" Brian J. Blottie. Also, I am THE former NACW World champion. Sure, there were other champions before me, but I am the man that
MADE that title. [Notes are scribbled on pads of paper fervously, as these reporters are obviously taking down all of this for their internet reports later.]
"TTLL" BJB: You maybe wondering, "Why did Blottie agree to come back to the fed that screwed him out of the World title?". Well, Jeremy called me. As some of you may know, me and Jeremy go way back. And he basically begged me to save this pathetic excuse of a wrestling federation one more time. Plus,
I was bored. I mean, when you're a multi-millionaire running a business that is pulling in more money a day than you sad, pathetic souls will pull in the rest of your lives, you get a little antsy.
So, I'm joining NACW again. I will win the NACW World title again. And I will make each and every one of your lives that much easier to deal with as I show you how it's done.
That's all you get Jeremy. Jeeves. Stop the tape. [The white haired man quickly hits another button, unplugs the TV, and wheels it back up the aisle, and out the door.]
SW: That will be a man to contend with. We know Brian J. Blottie well. DM: Blottie certainly isn't a man to mess with. SW: Let's get back up to the commissioner. SBJR: Okay, you know, I'm sick of crap like that . urgent business my ass. Brian J. Blottie, you put on your show here at this press conference, but if you ever . and I mean EVER . no-show one of my shows again, I guarantee you, you will be eating pavement tout suite, mon ami. You're not getting by that easy. Here, you're relying on wrestling. And that leads me to my next announcement. When NACW gets rolling, our title situation will be determined in a simple manner: a ten-person double-elimination tournament.
The winner gains the NACW World title, and the runner-up gains the NACW Television title. And thanks to the stunt Brian J. Blottie just pulled, he just made himself the #10 seed, which means he'll be opening cards instead of main-eventing them. AND. your first opponent in this tournament will be the NACW's first female wrestler, "The Tomorrow Girl" Natalie Rose Rogan!
Let's see if your ego can handle being beaten by a woman, Blottie. [Rhodes takes a sip of water, trying to calm down.] SBJR: Now, I know you are here for action, and that's what we'll deliver. So, let me take it down to the men who can call it much better than I, Steve White and Dan Mazin. Thanks for coming. [Fade back to Mazin and White. Mazin is visibly upset.] DM: Blottie at #10? My name placed last?! What the hell kind of federation did we bring back?
SW: One with some order, it looks like. Jeremy Rhodes is here to do business, not let the inmates run the asylum.
DM: Oh God, I forgot how many clichés you spout each show. SW (ignoring Mazin): Let's get things rolling here tonight! Drew Fortesque, it's on you! ["The Inevitable Return of the Great White Dope" by the Bloodhound Gang (thanks for the idea, Scampo) begins to play over the Boathouse sound system, as the lights dim and blue and white lights flash to the beat of the song.]
DM: Vaggy gets an entrance?! SW: Quiet, Dan . DF: Ladies and gentlemen, this bout is scheduled for one fall, and it is an exhibition match with a fifteen-minute time limit! Introducing first, from
South Miami Beach, Florida, weighing in at 198 pounds . "The Cabbage Man" . VAGGY MAGOO! [Vaggy ends up stumbling out of the entrance to an actual pop from the crowd. Vaggy is also gnawing in some cabbage, which he occasionally offers to some members of the crowd. Vaggy is dressed in dirty purple sweatpants and a fresh NACW shirt that someone apparently gave him . and he seems to be walking sideways. Either way, Vaggy slides into the ring and gets a pop from the crowd as he raises the half-eaten cabbage head up in the air. The lights come up and the music cuts, as Vaggy asks for (and receives) a microphone.]
DM: Oh no . Vaggy Magoo: Um, hi. [The crowd gives a good reaction, which Vaggy seems shocked by.] Vaggy: Vaggy asked Jeremy Rhodes for some cabbage earlier today, and you know what he did? [The crowd is quiet, some looking on in disbelief.] Vaggy: Vaggy don't know either. Vaggy went and asked Steve White for cabbage, and you know what he did?
[Some members of the crowd shout out "WHAT?"] Vaggy: He wasn't there. But worst of all is when I asked Stephen C. Bourdo for cabbage. He . he . he give Vaggy tomato! Vaggy pissed! Vaggy gonna whip you up, and get more cabbage!
[The crowd gives a nice reaction, most laughing, as Vaggy continues gnawing on the head of cabbage, but not before passing the mic back to Fortesque.
Vaggy stands in his corner as Fortesque prepares for the next announcement. The sounds of Steppenwolf's "Born To Be Wild" fill the Boathouse.] DF: And his opponent, from Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada, weighing 235 pounds . STEPHEN C. BOURDO!
[Through the curtain steps Steve Miller's super hero, and a damn fly man, Stephen C. Bourdo, dressed in his one of a kind ring attire. He's got the purple boots, the purple tights with the ScB logo on the rear, and the unforgettable cape reading with the SCB logo on the back in sequins.]
DM: I'm just wondering, does anyone screen these guys for psychological competence BEFORE they sign their contracts?
SW: I'm starting to wonder the same thing. [SCB poses with his arm in the air, with a stern look on his face, apparently trying to intimidate the audience. Most don't buy it, laughing it off. He begins to strut down the aisle with a purpose. That purpose is probably to attempt to impress us with his mighty fine physique. SCB makes it to the ring, where he bows for the fans. He finally steps into the ring, and stands in his corner, where he waves and poses for the fans.]
SW: Referee Mikey Johnson is trying to check Bourdo, who is refusing . and Vaggy sprints across the ring! He just threw that drool-covered half-eaten cabbage head right into the face of Bourdo! Vaggy is pounding away as
Bourdo! [Mikey kicks the cabbage head out of the ring and avoids the wrestlers, who fall to the mat with a resounding crash, Vaggy pounding on Bourdo, and calls for the bell.]
DING! DING! DING! DM: Vaggy gets the first NACW match, an entrance, and now some offense? Who is this man, and what did he do with Vaggy Magoo?!
SW: I don't believe my eyes! Vaggy is flailing away at Bourdo, who is trying to cover up with his forearms!
DM: Vaggy is showing offense! In the three years he's been with NACW, he's never done that!
SW: Well, he seems to be tiring out . Vaggy doesn't have that much stamina. DM: I don't want to know how you know that. SW (ignoring Mazin): Vaggy's punches are getting slower and slower here, and Bourdo drives a forearm smash into the skull of Vaggy, and Vaggy falls off. [Bourdo gets up, a little woozy, as Vaggy slowly recovers on the mat. Bourdo gets his head together and picks up Vaggy, but then sends him sailing ass over head with a release German suplex which folds Vaggy in half, much to the dismay of the Boathouse crowd.]
SW: PUNISHING German suplex there by Bourdo, who just chucked Vaggy halfway across the ring.
DM: You know, the way Vaggy landed, he could probably lick his butt clean if he raised his head.
SW: Well, he is folded up with his feet near his shoulders, but let's not get graphic. DM: That's right, I forgot; we can't have any fun with you around. [Bourdo helps Vaggy to his feet and goes for a butterfly suplex, but Vaggy somehow manages to backdrop Bourdo.]
SW: Vaggy isn't out of this match yet! DM: Odd, he's usually out in a minute and a half, and I don't know how he's moving after that German suplex.
SW: Vaggy gets Bourdo standing . [Bourdo, woozy, stands in the center of the ring as Vaggy bounces off and connects with a forearm right to the face of Bourdo. Bourdo falls to the mat, and Vaggy lands a fistdrop, then covers.]
ONE! TWO! TH. SW: Kickout! Bourdo is out of that pinning attempt. DM: No kidding. It was just a forearm and a fistdrop. SW: Vaggy is getting Bourdo up, and pops him with an uppercut! Bourdo is rocked! [Bourdo stumbles backward, near the ropes, as Vaggy charges.] DM: Vaggy coming in . SW: AND BOURDO PULLS THE ROPES DOWN AS VAGGY LUNGES! [Vaggy flies over the lowered ropes and plunges out to the floor. As Vaggy gets to his feet, Bourdo runs off the ropes and leaps onto him with a tope con hilo, to a crowd pop.]
SW: Bourdo has wiped Vaggy Magoo out, and in the process, has sent himself into the crowd! DM: Bourdo got some air on that one . [Bourdo climbs back over the railing, and gets met with a Vaggy headbutt. Vaggy smashes Bourdo in the face with the point of his elbow, then rolls him into the ring.]
SW: What in the world is Vaggy about to try . DM: He's leaning against the ropes . [Vaggy, with the help of the ropes, backflips into the ring . and lands gut-first onto the knees of Bourdo.] SW: Vaggy gets smashed with two knees in the gut . and Bourdo lifts him up! [Bourdo picks up Vaggy in an inverted fireman's carry, and then flings himself backwards, driving Vaggy face- and chest-first into the mat.]
SW: There's the Bourdominator! And now, Bourdo is calling for the end of this match! DM: Bourdo turning Vaggy over, and this match is all but over. [Bourdo climbs up to the top turnbuckle, and connects with an ultra-stiff frog splash, staying on position for the pin.]
ONE! TWO! THREE! DING! DING! DING! DF: Ladies and gentlemen, your winner of the match at a time of 4:14 . STEPHEN C. BOURDO! SW: A four-minute exhibition here in the Boathouse. Vaggy Magoo may just be ready to take on a full-time schedule if he can polish up the skills he showed here.
DM: Yeah, but with what Bourdo just showed, he is a man to deal with. Only problem is . Vaggy Magoo of all people roughed him up.
SW: Well, Vaggy has gotten better lately, and I think we haven't even seen a tenth of what Bourdo can do inside the ring . what . we just got a message here.
DM: Why do you always get the message? SW: Because I'm not biased. DM: Right, right, whatever, what's it say? SW: It says here that the legal staff has came to terms with "The Bomb" Ian Bradley, a former NACW Television champion, as well as "The Natural One" Trey McKool! Bradley's a big signing, and from what we understand, McKool is a self-trained rookie.
DM: We're just letting anyone in, aren't we? SW: Well, as long as they can wrestle. DM: That hasn't been proved to me so far. SW: You'll see, Dan. And as for us, we are completely out of time. Thanks for viewing this press conference, and we'll be in business soon!
[With that, we fade out.] -- THE ONE AND ONLY (thank God)... [name withheld] AMAZING HANDLER OF: "The Dream" Michael Bailey Yes, even you can AOL IM me. I'm [detail withheld].
