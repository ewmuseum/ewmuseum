THE NACW REPORT [detail withheld] News, rumors, Match of the week, Top roleplays, and more. ----------------------------------------------- News ----------------------------------------------- Welcome to the first NACW Report! I'm your commissioner, Pato. I'll be writing this report weekly as a summary to the happenings of the NACW. We'll have many features week after week, so get a look at what will be our weekly magazine. Now, for the REAL news. -The NACW has recently released Jeremy Houston, also known as "Hell's Angel." The reason being was the he was truly Ian Bradley, under a hidden identity, who was previously released on bad terms. The Television title will be claimed in a three way between Chris Widmayer, Rage Idol, and Brimstone. -World Champion Alan Soriano is opening up a new federation, called the XCA. Already signed are Jason Deegan, Brian J. Blottie, Brandon Bailey, Lost Metal, and more. -The NACW world title match at High Stakes is looking to be a classic, with Russell, Blottie, and Soriano all working their asses off. -NACW recently signed Rage Idol, straight out of Justin Russell's school as the first graduate. He looks to be ready to make big waves. -The NACW recently released the world title history. Here it is. NOTE: The HWA World title and the NACW World title may cross paths, as a result of a slow unification between the federations. -The RSPWF top 100 cruiserweights came out. Three NACW stars made the list. 44. "El Diablo" Eddie Deegan 57. "Superbeast" Billy Classon 64. "The Lobo" Jeremy Rhodes. -------------------------------------- Rumors -------------------------------------- -Rumor has it possible future world champion Justin Russell may have a big announcement at High Stakes -It seems that Jimmy is being severly warped by Brian Blottie. Scotsman, we hear, is not amused. -------------------------------------------- NACW Wrestler of the Week -------------------------------------------- Now, this isn't chosen by Justin or a committee or anyone matters, but by me. Pato. Hi. This is my opinion. Now, the wrestler is... "CRAZY" CHRIS WIDMAYER! Yeah, I like him. He's quite improved since he's entered the NACW, and I get a kick out of every flash I see. Those Scottish flashes were hiliarious and HAD A POINT TO THEM! ------------------------------------------- NACW match of the week ------------------------------------------- No doubt, the match of the week is the 9 man elimination, as written by... ME! ~NACW~ MAIN EVENT! Nine-man, three-way elimination match Chris Kitching, Brian J. Blottie, and Jason Deegan v. Alan Soriano and The Blood Brothers v.
