It's just one of those things I forgot to place in the handbook (and most of the questions are stuff I forgot to put in there as well). Hopefully, this will answer any questions you may have about joining NACW...because I've been asked these questions a lot.
Question: If I apply, does that mean I can't run a certain angle because of the "old-school" attitude?
Answer: No. Something I really forgot to clarify is that as long as I like the angle, I'll run it. What I mean by "Old School Wrestling, New School
Attitude" is simple: no ridiculous matches, no three-way matches, no battle royals...if it's not one-on-one, a tag match, or a six-man tag match, it's not happening.
Question: How will this fed be run? Answer: As far as style, I'd like to think that this will be a combination of an independent with a national TV contract as well as the NWA circa
1984-1988 (in other words, more like Crockett). Question: How are matches decided? Answer: One of the things I'm amazed I forgot. Basically, it's 85% roleplaying, 10% strat, 5% angle. What I mean by roleplaying is by entertainment value. If you can keep me reading all the way through without skimming through it (see The Gremlin's RPs as an example), you'll be in good shape. Just remember...boring guys don't get pushes in real life. You could talk for 35 minutes and go lose, because you've put the fans to sleep.
Question: What makes you such an authority on entertainment value? Answer: Because I am. I know how a wrestling crowd thinks...I've seen it happen. Most anything I predicted as far as not getting over didn't work.
Question: What if my question isn't covered here? Answer: Well, ask me. I'll answer any question I get. You can either AOL IM me at [detail withheld], send an e-mail to [detail withheld] , or hang around in #rspwf and wait for a schmuck by the name of TigerStratus to show up and annoy him. Question: Why do Pato and Blottie shill your fed? Answer: Because they bugged me enough to reopen it. They also like this federation. Question: Will Pato really give me a blowjob if I sign up? Answer: Take that one up with him. ;) Question: I lost the handbook. Where can I get another one? Answer: E-mail [detail withheld] and ask for one. Your request will be processed in three to five business days (actually, whenever I get around to it, which is usually by the end of the day).
Hopefully, that covers everything. -- THE ONE AND ONLY (thank God)... [name withheld] AMAZING HANDLER OF: "The Dream" Michael Bailey Yes, even you can AOL IM me. I'm [detail withheld].
