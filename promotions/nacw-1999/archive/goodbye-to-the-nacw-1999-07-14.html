Goodbye to the NACW

circa July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Well the day has come to say goodbye to another fed. I don't know the history of the fed at all, nor do I care. But when I was in it, the fed head was Justin Russell. Now Im going to take this Opportunity to thank Russell for running the fed. Even though I jobbed a lot, for reasons I don't know seeing as my rps were in the top level of the fed, I still enjoyed the fed. Mostly because it allowed me to start a new gimmick that was well taken by the other wrestlers. It also showed me how not to run a fed. All of the suits also had wrestlers, who were all in the main event, thus ruining the trust factor of the ones who just joined the fed. Well Justin maybe if there is a next time things will be different, with better luck. Thanks.. Voo Sent via Deja.com http://www.deja.com/ Share what you know. Learn what you don't.
>I've been in this fed and I hated evey minute of it, you're right the >bookers all had wrestlers and they always made themselves win, even if our >RPs were better that theirs. Meh. There was one booker, believe it or not. Then myself, Alan, and Brandon wrote cards, and I was the "commissioner" which more or less meant that I told Justin who I thought was the better roleplayers of the week. Justin booked everything, m'dear. The top of the card featured Brian J. Blottie, who was a simple handler in the federation, Alan Soriano, who handled and wrote matches, Justin's character who was only in the top of the card to work toward his retirement, which was announced, and lately Jason Deegan, my character who I used to put over the hardcore and tag team division for the sake of the league for most of my stay and Chris Kitching, who was nothing but a handler. Chris Widmayer was in line for a massive push, himself only a handler. The Flying Scotsman was in line for a massive push, himself only a handler. Lost Metal was to be pushed, I think, had he not quit. Himself only a handler. Tell me... who were those bookers that always made themselves win? And who the hell are you, anyhow?
