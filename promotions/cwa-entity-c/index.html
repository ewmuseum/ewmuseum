Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Cyber Wrestling Alliance announced itself on 16 August 1997 as a hybrid of “Simulated and Role-Played matches”: dark matches would run as simulated logs, “so at least you get something more then YOU LOST,” and live matches would carry commentary and role-play alongside the sim. Its own opening post asked for new original wrestlers, a vice-president to handle flashes, and a second writer to share the card-writing load, and pointed readers to a homepage still under construction on a self-hosted address.

Its own president followed up over the next several days. A 19 August post claimed a prior history running through “the NCWA,” “the AAWA,” and a brief stint in the AWA before this launch, and two more posts on 20 August kept the recruitment drive going, one of them promising to welcome and encourage newcomers to e-federations specifically.

Its last surviving document, on 25 August 1997, is a single roleplay: an exhibition match staged in Norfolk, Virginia and framed explicitly as a recruiting showcase, called by a broadcast team of Chris Figoro and Anastasia Osborne with Ted Fitsgeral on introductions and Tim Linch as referee. Deadpool beat The Shamus, a veteran of the defunct All Star Alliance, by submission with a hold billed as “the Dead Lock”; The All-Star Wrestler, identifying himself as one of that older league's own founders, then confronted Deadpool at ringside and dropped him with a reverse DDT after Deadpool took a swing at him. The broadcast closed on a countdown to the promotion's first announced pay-per-view, Cyber Combat, with every belt on the line.

Whether Cyber Combat ever happened isn't in the surviving record; no document later than 25 August 1997 survives.
