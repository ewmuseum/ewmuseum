Cyber Wrestling Exhibition Match

25 August 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The lights dim, thousands of fans are screaming from the event which happened just a posting or two ago. A middle-age sharply dressed man enters the ring with the microphone. The ring is black with black ropes and the letters CWA painted in red. A solitary spotlight shines on the well dressed man) Ted Fitsgeral: GOOOOD EVENING WRESTLING FANS! Tonight coming to you live from Norfolk, VA we have a special exhibition match for you tonight. This match showcases the talent from the Cyber Wrestling Alliance, who is now accepting new talent at: http://www.bestweb.net/~bobbafet/cwa.html . (The camera pans over to a broadcast booth where a slim black curly haired man sits with a tall lean woman with cat-like features and long black hair. They are the CWA broadcast team of Chris Figoro and Anastasia Osborne) Chris: Thank you for tuning in with us tonight. We have a very exciting match for you tonight showcasing the newest member of the Cyber Wrestling Alliance vs. a member of the former All Star Alliance. Anastasia: That's right Chris, the All Star Alliance was disbanded in 1989 and this is probably the only event where we would see one of their members. Chris: The purpose of tonights event is to showcase the new Cyber Wrestling Alliance and hopefully recruit some new members. Anastasia: Let's go to Ted Fitsgeral for the introductions (Camera cuts to Ted) Ted Fitsgeral: The following contest is scheduled for one fall with a twenty minute time limit. The official for this match is Tim Linch. It is a non-sanctioned match and therefor ANYTHING GOES....fans are you READY? (the crowd cheers) then fans THE TIME HAS COME TO GET IT ONNNNNNNNNNNN!!!!! Chris: This place is erupting Michael Panzer eat your heart out. Ted Fitsgeral: First coming down the aisle weighing in at 260 pounds, from the once proud All Star Alliance here is.....THE SHAAAAAMMMMUSSSSSS (Aqualung by Jethro Tull blasts through the arena. A man who is slightly overweight appears through the curtain. He has long red hair and strange grey eyes with green trunks. He walks slowly to the ring ignoring the fans, who are neither cheering or booing. The crowd is just stupified at who this person is) (Cut to broadcast booth) Chris: Very interesting reaction, wouldn't you say Anastasia? Anastasia: I don't know what to make of this myself. The guy looks like a pervert not a wrestler. Chris: Now watch what you say next stace. Anastasia:Well *laughs* reminds me of the time I...hey...wait a minute...look chris..over there.. Chris: Where? Anastasia:There by the runway coming towards us isn't that.. (A man in an Armani with a blue mask with white stripes joins the booth) Chris: The All-Star Wrestler? What the hell are you doing here? All-Star: Well hello to you also. As you both know I am one of the wrestlers who started this league and I want to know who the HELL booked this ol coot to be in our exhibition match? Anastasia: I don't know. All-Star: Well, I think I will sit in on this one..mind? Anastasia: SInce you help cut the checks....no. (Cut back to ring) Ted Fitsgeral: And his opponent...coming down the aisle from parts unknown..he is the shining star in CWA right now..the master of the DEAD LOCK.....here comes... DEADPOOOOOOOOOL!!! (Ted leaves the ring. A man in a red mask with two black spots covering his eyes and red trunks walks through the curtains. He is carrying a wooden staff he runs to the ring) Anastasia: Now he has a BIG stick! Chris: Stop it..you are going to be a horny old woman someday. Anastasia:Someday? Ha! Chris: Ok...Deadpool runs into the ring and is met by a SERIES OF STOMPS by the Shamus. The bell rings and this match is officially under way. Anastasia: Shamus wasting no time here chris. Chris: None at all...Shamus picking up Deadpool by the mask and whipping him into the ropes and hits with a HUGE CLOTHSLINE sending Deadpool flat on his back. Anastasia: Why is Shamus breathing so hard already? All-Star: Cause he's past his friggin prime. Anastasia: ? Chris: Shamus slow to pick up Deadpool and it costs him Deadpool with a EUROPEAN UPERCUT..he follows it with a series of rights....grabs Shamus by the hair and SLAMS HIS FACE AGAINST THE TURNBUCKLE...again..and again and again. Anastasia: The fans are going wild. Chris: Deadpool sends Shamus into the ropes and hits him with a SPINNING LEG KICK...ow! Anastasia: Sweet chin music my ass..thats broken chin visit to the hospital. Chris: Deadpol picking up Shamus in suplex formation in the middle of the ring and he's just holding him there.. Anastasia: What a display of power. Chris: what's he doing...he's walking slowly towards the ropes and he DROPS SHAMUS OUT OF THE RING...the Shamus goes hard to the concrete floor and lands right on his knee. Anastasia: The Shamus is doin a lil dance of pain right now. Chris: Deadpool breaks the ref's count and rolls out of the ring. Shamus still down on the floor grimmacing. Deadpool looking for something..looking..he grabs that wooden staff. Anastasia: Wait a minute Chris..thats not wood....THAT"S METAL! Chris: Deadpool goes over to Shamus and OH MY GOD...HE JUST SMASHED THE METAL STAFF ON SHAMUS KNEE!!! Anastasia: We are seeing the battle of wounded knee right here folks. Chris: Shamus is on the verge of tears. Deadpool picks up his limp body and rolls him back to the ring. After putting the staff down, Deadpool re-enters the ring. He climbs on to the ropes and is looking into the crowd..looking right at the ALL-STAR. Deadpool: I NEVER MISS PUNK! Anastasia: What was that all about? All-Star: Never mind. Chris: Deadpool grabs Shamus left leg..crosses it over for a figure four and..wait a minute..holding that lock he flips Shamus over into a boston crab type hold..its..its..THE DEAD LOCK...the Shamus is howling and the fans are going through the roof! The Shamus taps out three times and Tim Linch signals for the bell! DING! DING! DING! Chris: Deadpool is not letting go. Anastasia: He wants to make sure this pervert never wrestles again. Chris: Linch calling for the bell again. DING! DING! DING! Chris: Deadpool finally releasing the hold. He is just looking down at the Shamus and STARTS KICKING HIS KNEE....the ref pushing Deadpool aside...Deadpool seems to be laughing. Anastasia: Hard to tell beneath that mask. Chris: The kicks continue..hey All-Star where are you going? Anastasia: He's left the booth and is headed towards the ring. Chris: All-Star in the ring now...both masked men are staring at each other..Deadpool saying something...I can't quite make it out...Deadpool turning around to leave.....wait he TAKES A SWING AT THE ALL-STAR WRESTLER...All-Star ducks under...Deadpool goes into a spin he swung so hard..All-Star catches him and A REVERSE DDT ...Deadpool is out like a light. Anastasia: What a match and this was against a washed up pervert... Chris: Well fans that is all the tim we have..we would like to remind you to please come to: Http://www.bestweb.net/~bobbafet/cwa.html and sign up your wrestler today. The application is a cut and paste email one but it is worth the time believe me. Right now the countdown is on till our very first Pay-Per-View: CYBER COMBAT. All belts will be on the line.....for Anastasia Osborne, Ted Fitsgeral, The All-Star Wrestler, and all of CWA I'm Chris Figoro saying GOODNIGHT!
