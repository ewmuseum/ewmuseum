U.W.O. — classified advert

c. 1994
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. The museum serves it whole because pretending it didn't happen would be a worse dishonesty than being embarrassed by it. See Curation.
What this is One entry from “Dit Dar Dit,” the reader-message/classifieds column of Stosser issue 11, a UK Atari ST STOS-BASIC diskmag — the entire surviving record of U.W.O. The disk image this was recovered from carries a 22 April 1987 stamp, and the column's own contents rule that date out: one message in it wishes the magazine “good luck in '94,” another announces issue 12 for “20th March,” a third advertises STOS source code for the Atari STe, a machine that did not exist in 1987. The issue is early 1994. See the U.W.O. wing.
Redacted This document carries redactions: the advertiser's own real name and home mailing address, a private individual's, removed per the museum's privacy rule. Logged in the accession digest and, since 2026-09-02, in the workshop redaction ledger.
