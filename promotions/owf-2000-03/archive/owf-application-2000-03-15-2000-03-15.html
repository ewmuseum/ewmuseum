-=- The Online Wrestling Fedaration -=- ~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~ President: [detail withheld] ~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~ Thank you for considering online emplyment with the OWF. This is a Role Playing Fantasy Fedaration,We allow both fake and real wrestlers. All cards are handwritten and will include at least five matches,and one interview. The cards occur on Tuesday's and Saturday's,and on special day's there are Pay Per View
Events on Sundays! Also we have Surprise House Shows every once in a while to keep people and story lines on their toes.
~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~ Rules of the OWF ~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~ 1.There will be no TOSing of any sort,if you quit or get fired and you keep receiving mail,it is not the President's fault as he removes your name as he receives news of your departure
2.There will be a 20 line minimum on RolePlays,And you must do them once a week..if you fail to make it 20 lines,the RP will not count,if you fail to do at least one a week without notice of being on a vacation,you will be ejected from the fed.
3.Swearing must be censored due to America Online Terms Of Service 4.No Racist Comments like Gay-Bashing,Religion Bashing and stuff like that,also due to America Online Terms Of Service
5.If you use another Person's Wrestlers or Manager in your RP without written consent by both you and the other person,you will lose your next three matches reguardless of RPing or not,however you must still RP to remain in the fed.
6.I go for Quality Over Quantity,so putting the words to your theme song will lose to an in depth description of the wrestler
7.Do not complain if you lose,you lost because you didn't RP as good as the opponunt. 8.It is allright to fight Jobbers in your RP's but they must be made up,no real life wrestlers even Gillberg or The Brooklyn Brawler
