Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

All American E-Wrestling survives as one page: its own launch announcement, "NOW OPEN!!! Get your applications in now!!!", last updated 3 June 2006. Every substantive page it links to — Roster, Current Champions, Rules, Title Histories, Rankings, a television show, pay-per-views, house shows — lived on a second GeoCities account, geocities.com/WV_RiffRaff/, that this holding never reached; none of that material survives here.

Its own credited designer is thanked by handle only ("The Devil Bunny"), and its own commissioner is named only by role, the page's own contact address redacted per the privacy rule. No roster, card, or title of its own survives.
