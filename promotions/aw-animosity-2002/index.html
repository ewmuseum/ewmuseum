A Primetime Central member fed that opened in September 2002 under a Board of Directors, filled its first roster from NGW and NTW and announced a Pyramid headquarters with an underground arena for tryouts, then came back under a new owner in 2003 with pay-per-views of its own and announced a return in August 2004. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

AW, Animosity Wrestling, appears on Primetime Central in September 2002. Its notice of 21 September asked for volunteers to fill a Board of Directors that would settle all business by majority vote: a Chairman who acts as president, founder and owner, Directors of Operations, Television, Graphics, News, Talent and Finances, with the Director of News charged with promoting AW through PTC News. On 22 September PTC's news carried three bulletins under its banner. Its owner, at the NTW headquarters, was on the phone to superstars under contract at NGW and NTW and to the owners of DWO, WIW and EPW; a roster of 16 was set to open, with a ceiling of 25 to 30 and a limit of 35; a draft from the NGW and NTW lists was promised; and the Pyramid headquarters was to have an underground arena for tryouts, training and meetings.

Two profiles followed under its -AW- banner, Mark Blaze on 23 September 2002 and AJ Storm on 27 November 2002. A profile of Time Bomb posted to PTC for EPW in February 2003 recalls the fed in the past tense, as the place NGW merged into and where he went undefeated.

A second run followed under a new owner. From September 2003 he advertised Animosity Wrestling on PTC's forum from a site of its own, writing that it was not the same e-fed it used to be and that nothing involving it before 1 June 2003 had been in his hands. It held a pay-per-view, Recoil, that month and another, Night of Glory, at the end of November, and closed the year with a special of its best matches, calling 2003 a new beginning for a federation that rose from the ashes. After a summer break he announced its reopening at a new address on 1 August 2004, with its first returning signings ten days later.

Primetime Central (PTC)

Member of AW (Animosity, 2002) (2002)
Primetime Central's own news carried its bulletins and profiles under the -AW- banner from 22 September 2002, reporting that its owner would do everything it takes to bring Animosity Wrestling to the top of PTC, that the owners of DWO, WIW and EPW were already in close relations with it, and that its Director of News would promote it through PTC News; PTC's channel line-up in a later capture lists it as Channel 10, Featuring AW.
