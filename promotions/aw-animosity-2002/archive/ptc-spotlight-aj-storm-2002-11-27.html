Move List Wrestling Style: High Flying Technical Set Up Move: Eye Of The Storn (Flapjack powerbomb from ropes) Finisher: Tsunami Twister (Dragon corkscrew moonsault from top turnbuckle) Other Moves: High flying moves Corkscrew dropkick 450 Spinning heel kick Flying lariat Spear Top rope spear Impact moves Flapjack Spinebuster Running knee drop Hard Clothesline Multiple german suplex Tiger suplex pin Brainbuster Technical/ Submission moves Ankle Lock submission Texas Cloverleaf Rolling armbar drop History The story all begins in St Katrina's orphanage in New York, as we cant start the story before that, because we honestly know nothing about AJ until here. He was brought to the orphanage and left there as a baby, no one knows his parents, or how he got there. AJ was left in the capable hands of the nuns in the Orphanage, as no one was interested in adopting a baby at the time. AJ Stayed with the nuns throughout his first 12 years, especially one, named Sister Mary. Sister Mary looked after AJ throughout his childhood, raising him as she was her own, when AJ began to walk, she was there, when AJ forst went to the potty, she was there,throughout all of his early childhood, sister Mary was always looking out for him, and making sure he was never in trouble. AJ figured out at an early age that these nuns, although special, were not his parents, and quickly decided that he would find them when he was old enough to be able to do things for himself, it was when he was 8, AJ thought he could go alone, where obviousl the nuns thought he couldnt, as he was in fact still only an 8 year old child. This led to a lot of attempted escapes from the orphanage from AJ, for 4 years. In fact, in his last year at the Orphanage he attempted to break out on no less that 67 occasions, involving elaborate plans and sometimes violent conduct when he could not escape. (Once, after AJ had managed to set off fire alarms throughout the whole of the inner New York City District simultaneously through his PC to create a disraction as he hid in the laundry basket which was just on its way out, while holding a carved knife he had made from a piece of wood and a flint. AJ was caught and almost arrested for violent assault with a deadly weapon) After the most drsatic attempt at escape, the Orphanage were finally fed up of his temper and they actively tried to find a foster family further away from New York. Eventually, a family from Chicago decided they wanted to adopt AJ, namely a man and wife prison warden, who had dealt with the most vicious thugs in history. John and Brenda Storm. They thought that as they had an unruly kid here, and with their reputation in the prisons, they could bring him up and reform him. Obviously they took the hard knock life of schooling, and regulary beat AJ when he was unruly, and made sure he was always on the straight and narrow, John even enrolled AJ into wrestling college as he wanted to harness the anger and potential he saw in the young AJ. This is where AJ found himself, he could beat people up and do things to release the anger and frustration he felt at them nuns holding him back when he wanted to find his parents, now AJ was truly happy.... At least until his foster parents were both killed by an ex con in the middle of the night, when he was at university at 18. This tragic event devestated AJ, the family he thought he had were now dead, the only people he ever respected, namely his foster parents, were gone, AJ was alone. This period of mourning escalated to AJ becoming hooked on drink and drugs, and it was while in rehab, after being picked up by some tramp while wasted in the gutter stuck in his own saliva and vomit, that AJ found Tia, the councillor at the rehab clinic...She was the most gorgeous woman he had ever seen, and if she saw past his addictions, he was sure he was in with a shot. It was during a session of 'find yourself' that AJ realised if he could just begin to wrestle again, he might be able to beat the addictions, and become once again a man.... and with some training, and a lot of dedication, AJ finally on his 20th birthday managed to join the PCW, all the time becoming stronger as his life began to take shape. He even managed to get a date with Tia, and then they got serious, and Tia left her job to become AJ's manager and supervisor to keep him on the straight and narrow. This leads us to the current day. AJ left PCW for pastures new, and decided to have a while out of the sport for media commitments, this was until he got the call to come out of retirement and face once again the challenges of wrestling, with the promise that he will get to the top, then find out what happened to his parents and what happened to his foster parents, bringing the killers to justice Description : AJ has chiselled good looks, with long shoulder length scraggy hair in purple and blue colour, he is clean shaven, with bright blue eyes and a cracking smile which lights up every woman. AJ is cheeky, and colourful, always smiling, and happy, yet his nasty streak is legendary, he sometimes snaps if people push him too much, and makes rash decisions. His toned slim body is not heavily beefed up, it is a nice shape, and it is highlighted by the black and purple tinted t-shirt he wears to the ring, with the Storm Motif he has in the centre of it (A purple circular badge with lightning striking a tree) His pants are always cargo pants or khakis, in dark colours, and he wears black shoes with lightning bolts on the soles of the feet in purple.
