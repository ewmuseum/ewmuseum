the barron® the Barron Real Name: Victor Von Barron Handler's Name: [name withheld] Hometown: Coburg, Germany Height: 6' 1" Weight: 210 Age: 28 Finisher: The money maker (A hanging vertical suplex dropped straight down into a shoulder breaker Manager: None but always travels with at least 5 security guards Theme Song: "Barron Theme" Quote: "Bow to the Money" Email:[name withheld] Titles Won: none History: Earlier Career The Barron was born to a very wealth land owner in Coburg, Germany. The Barron never saw much of his father or mother while he was young; he was constantly in the care of nannies and caregivers. He was raised with a silver spoon in his mouth, and never had to work a day in his life. The Barron’s parents died when he was 20, and thou he went to the funeral he never shed a tear. In all reality he was glad the fortune was all his. The Barron’s family fortunes exact worth is actually unknown to anyone but the Barron family. The money is so wide spread and tied up in so many ventures that it is almost impossible to get an exact amount; rumor is though that the richest man in the world might not be Bill Gates. One day The Barron was watching TV when something caught his eye it was a commercial for a wrestling program, he was instantly drawn in. Suddenly he decided he want to be a world champ himself. He immediately got on the phone and contacted a few people and next thing he knew he was talking to different world champs from different fed’s around the world. When the Barron told them he wanted to buy there world title. Every one of them told him the same thing….. “You can’t buy this title you have to earn it.” For the first time in his life the Barron had been told NO, but it only strengthened his resolve. He hired the best trainers in the world, and bought the best equipment. He has worked night and day for his new goal like a man possessed, and maybe just maybe he learned to respect the men of this sport…. Then again maybe not. CWF-Pro The Barron signed on with the cwf.pro in 1998. However getting a big push for the IC title. His push was cut short and he only lasting a 2 months, he was released. No word on where he went to after he left the company.
