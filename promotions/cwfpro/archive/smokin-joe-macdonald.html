smokin joe macDonald Smokin Joe MacDonald Real Name: Joe MacDonald Handler's Name: Joe Hometown: Aberdeen, Scottland Height: 5' 11" Weight: 188 Age: 37 Finisher: Smoke Out (Sleeper) Group Affiliation: MacDonald Clan (leader) Manager: none Theme Song: "Smokin Joe Theme" Quote: "Let's Rock!" Email:Joe Titles Won: CWF-Pro Intercontinental Champion (2) CWF-Pro Royal Champion (2) CWF-Pro Crown CWF-Pro Hall of Fame TDW Champion TDW International Champion TDW No-Limits Champion TDW United States Champion History: Earlier Career He signed on as a rookie in 1998, He was scounted by r@ven CWF-Pro Joined the cwf.pro in late 1998 and was the last man to have held the cwf.pro Crown untill it was return in 2006. Had some good feuds with his cousin James MacDonald. After 2 years Smokin Joe left the cwf.pro. He was upset about the way Draven and Pres Burns "killed off" Draven's Image in the match with Capt Sexy in late 1999. Pres Burns took the heat sinec Draven was gone from the cwf.pro. Draven told no one that he was leaving nor the way he was leaving. Smokin Joe and Pres Burns would butt heads over the subject for a few months, untill he left in early 2000. joined the TDW. He then became the TDW Champion. There has not been any word from him since 2001. He only lasted 6 months in the TDW Exwrestler Story: Smokin Joe: Yeah, I remember you. Been a while, hasn't it????? To tell the truth, there isn't really too much to tell. When Pres Burns decided to close things down a couple of years ago, I figured that was it, so I called it quits. A few months later, I was approached by Beastman and the TDW, so I figured I'd give it one more try. I had a good run with TDW (which seems to have ended permanantly), and now I'm retired for good. Like I said, nothing much to tell. No hard feelings, no vendettas, No bitterness. I've just come to the point where I'm 31 years old with too many personal things going on. I had to downsize some of the things that make up my life. Unfortunately, e-wrestling is one of them..
