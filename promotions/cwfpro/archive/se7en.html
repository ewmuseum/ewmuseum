se7en® Se7en Real Name: Drake J. Dawson Handler's Name: [name withheld] Hometown: Thunder Bay, Ontario, Canada Height: 6' 8" Weight: 290 Age: 34 Finisher: Nevermore (boston crab) Manager: none Theme Song: "Harvester of Sorrow" Metallica Quote: "Time for the Se7en" Email:[name withheld] Titles Won: CWF-Pro Champion (2) CWF-Pro Intercontinental Champion CWF-Pro Royal Champion CWF-Pro Tournament Terror Winner CWF-Pro World Tag Team Champion (2) LNAPWF Interstate Champion NWC Champion History: Earlier Career This was Seven's 1st efed. He signed with the cwf.pro as a rookie. R@ven found him at another efeds wrestling event trying to break into the sport. R@ven offered him a job and brought him to Pres Burns. CWF-Pro He came to the cwf.pro as a rookie brought in by R@ven in 2000. Pres Burns has stated in an shoot interview that He has turn out to be a good man and friend. Seven would later join up with Ang, Hero, Major D, Ninja X and the Johnson boys as a young group backstage, more like road buddies. After the cwf.pro closed up in 2001, he went off and started his own efed the EOW for some time. EOW The efed lasted about 2 months. Hero and Keith were the only two that went to the efed. Hero has stated in a shoot interview that the EOW was a grave yard efed. Meaning there was not many wrestlers in the efed, more like 10 guys. Keith would become champion and lasted untill it shut down CWF-Pro 2004 He would return in 2004 after Pres Burns and Beastman reopened the cwf.pro for a short time, but after a fall out with Pres Burns, Seven was released. He clamed in a shoot interview that Beastman (who was booking the matches for the weekly show) was dogging him each change he got. Seven also said that he did have a breif run in the TDW where he and Beastman had a big blow out and didn't trust Beastman at all. It would come down to Pres Burns having to release one or the other. After a month. Seven and Pres Burns meet again and talked out there problems. Seven said that talking with Burnsy was a good thing, however would never talk things out with Beastman. WWA After almost a year of unknown absense from ewrestling. After leaving the cwf.pro in 2004. Se7en debuts on WWA's Meltdown, making immediate impact were his real name was known as Drake J. Dawson and went on to take off his mask, to no longer be known as a strange wrestler behind a mask. Never winning any titles in the WWA. Se7en and foe, now allie, EkoSyde(now Enigma) compete in a Buried Alive match. If Se7en wins, he gains control of Enigma, if he loses, vice versa. Se7en wins and EkoSde become one of Se7en's 'Soldiers'. Se7en sustains an injury caused by a bad fall. He unofficially announces his retirement. Se7en returns to the WWA, immediatley fueding with Venny LeTrav. Se7en teams up with Enigma and Joey K (not joey of the cwf.pro) to form the 'Forsaken Souls' Se7en announces retirement after several absences from WWA's Meltdown and Fiesta. Reason why is unknown, but it was not due to injury. Forsaken Souls split up. WWA 2nd run About Seven. Evil, Sadistic, Cheating, a Mastermind of Offense, demented, crazy, insane and just about every other bad word there is in this world. He has an unbelievable attacking arsenal and a solid defensive style. He's very quiet and keeps to himself, hardly shows emotion and is very tormented by his past. He looks and acts like he should be in a horror movie. He also lives in a place in his mind called 'The Seventh World'. Seven would later leave the efed for unknown reasons in 2006. He has not been seen since. (picture credit to the wwa efed)
