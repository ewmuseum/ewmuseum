His main fight was with Sanchez after he became the 1st National Champion. It was always Sanchez vs Mercury. They have been in a feud for over 2 years. When the CSW opened Mer was the 1st National Champion. He lost and after some time the CSW close. He went his own way. UCWF Mer met up with Sanchez in the UCWF and did feud with him. After the UBH was released, Mer was also released with the group CWF-Pro Then Mer came to the cwf.pro after a few months the UCWF closed. Mer had changed. Burns wanted to have a big war between Mer and Sanchez in a series of matches. He then was released after he refused to fight Insyders: Kevin Hall. No one has seen Mer since 1998. .
