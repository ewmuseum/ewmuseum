CWR Jimmy Miracle the man of Miracles. Jimmy Miracle will go down in CWR's history books as one of the greatest performers ever seen. In his promo's and in the ring, he was funny, charismatic, creative and full of direction. Along with his ever present sidekick Big Kev the one legged midget Miracle used mind games to get to his opponents. From his Anti-hardcore Revolution to being WCWO president to making Destroyer's life hell this talented young wrestler was involved in some of the most memorable stories of CWR. Unfortunately his time in CWR was cut short when his handler [detail withheld]. RIP Jimmy Miracle and [name withheld]. You will never be forgotten! The CWR Arena was later renamed the Jimmy Miracle Arena by Natas in December 2001 in his honor. Jimmy Miracle's return In 2003 some fans say CWR reached a new low when Jimmy Miracle rejoined the ranks of the CWR! It was not the real Jimmy Miracle however, is death was very real - but an imposter claiming to be him arrived! The eiree part though is the imposter's likeness, both in character and appearance. 10/29/00 Jimmy Miracle wins CWR Title and declares himself the unified CWR/WCWO Champion (picture and history credit to the cwr efed)
