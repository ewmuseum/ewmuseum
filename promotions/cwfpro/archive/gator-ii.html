gator II® CWF-Pro CLASSIC WRESTLER Gator II Real Name: Jack Gator Handler's Name: [name withheld] Hometown: the Swamps near the gulf Height: 6' 0" Weight: 161 Age: 24 Finisher: Gator Press (running powerslam) Manager: none Theme Song: "Nothing Else Matters" Metallica Quote: "I am the one and only GATOR!" Email:[name withheld] Titles Won: WWX US Champion (3) WWX World Tag Team Champion (2) History: Earlier Career Gator #2 started out in the WMX efed where he wrestled his whole career. WMX History not really known. He was a former US and Tag champion there. That is all he told the cwf.pro when in talks CWF-Pro in 1998, Pres Burns and R@ven found him in a far off efed called the WWX. The WWX was his "friend's efed." R@ven talked with him a few times. He was intrested in a career in the cwf.pro. However He saw that we had a "gator" on the roster already. He said that it would be a bit to much and would make the fans and roster two slided. The company's idea was to have them feud for a few weeks and then run them as a tag team. This is where the CWF-Pro's gator was not really doing anything and started having his "family problems" thus causing his retirment. Gator 2 would meet with the cwf.pro 4 times, untill he would not agree to a contract and walked away from Pres Burns. They however shake hands are would leave the matter on the table. Gator 2 and Pres Burns are still good friends and still talk with each other now and then. Gator 2 has retired after the closing of the WMX efed in 2005.
