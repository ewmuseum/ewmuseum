Email:Rob Titles Won: OCWF Champion OCWF American Champion History: Earlier Career History unknown He came in during the winter of 1998. He joined the other Harts on the CF gang (wave 2) He lasted one month and was released after he started a backstage fight with Mike Powers. He clams he went to an efed called the OCWF and became world champion as Shane Matthews. No word if this is ture. Exwrestler Story: Rob "the Rock" Hart: Now this is possibly the biggest mistake Pres. Burns ever made. He got rid of Rob Hart. Now lets get to the story. Now Rob didn't know about the CWF. He just finished a contract with another organization which he just got bored of. He did everything possible and wanted a change of pace. He received a letter from the CWF to join the Hart stable. You know, to build some heat. So I said why not. So I get in the fed and right away I feel as if I'm not wanted. The leader of the stable didn't know me or like me. I was being put in matches that I had no chance of winning. I was a jobber. Nothing more. Then I asked him about my situation and BAM. He starts to tear into me like I'm a nobody. Here I was, putting in my hard work and putting in my time and he says that "Maybe you shouldn't be here" I've been World Champ in every fed I've been in. I've been the main event. And still this pres. says that I wasn't good. Then he called me a little boy! If I could get my hands on that son of a bitch I'd make him think twice about calling anyone a boy. So I packed the bags, changed my name (cause this a$$hole ruined Rob Hart's career) and started a new life in the OCWF, the best damn fed out there. I'm known as Shane Matthews. The one and only. I've won the American title, I've won the World title. So this is what I have to say to you Burns. You lost one hell of a star. And here I am today to let you know.
