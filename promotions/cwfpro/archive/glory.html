He had loving parents but they were dirt poor. When they finally got their first TV, he always loved to watch the WWF and the local indy feds that came on. Glory was always the athlete growing up. That's one of the things he did for fun. In high school he played football, hockey, baseball and after school boxing. Obviously he couldn't pay for college and Glory was just shy away from getting a sports scholarship to a couple of colleges. Since he couldn't go to college he asked his old boxing coach if he could train him for competitions. Glory went pretty far making enough money to help pay his own rent and help keep his parents on their feet. When Glory got fed up with the hard life of boxing, he looked into somehting different. And that was wrestling. He along with his best friend (aka WEAPON) joined a local wrestling club and started training. Glory and WEAPON did really well and excelled. Later they went on to join local Indy feds. After becoming local heroes through that a bigtime wrestling promoter went to one of Glory's matches and hired him on the spot to join the TWF. After a brief stint there, Glory left because of troubles with the president of that fed. Glory then went on to join the GFWA where he really developed stardom with the fans. After the GFWA closed down Glory and many of the GFWA stars went to the ACWF where he did really well there as well. Yet Glory never won a World Title. He had one chance in his career so far against the legendary EVIL which will go down in the recordbook as one of the best bouts in history. After things started slowing down in the ACWF Glory said his goodbyes to seek more fertile ground. He then left to join a couple other feds like the CWF-Pro and the IRWC. AFter not finding his place in those feds he was invited to go to New England Wrestling. Glory excelled there and finally won his first World Title. He didn't keep it very long as he had some troubles with his knee (after being in a car accident awhile back) and forfeited it to take some time off from the sport. Months later, Glory made his return by going to the then super popular, Championship Wrestling Federation. While there, Glory struggled tooth and nail sturggling through multiple losing and winning streaks to finally defeat the feared Archbishop Apocalypse for the CWF World Championship. After leaving CWF, Glory went on to work with management of New England Wrestling as a booker for the federation for over a year. However, wrestling called out for Glory once again. This calling was the Master of the Mat tournement. Once there, Glory virtually swept the competition (in the process eliminating 3 favored CWF superstars from the comeptition). However, Glory lost in the final round of the competition after a grueling and bloody bout. Glory's last stint was with the famed Fans Wrestling Federation in which Glory quickly quit after a few months because of rediculous working conditions. Another noteable federation Glory wrestled in was the ASWF, which is acclaimed for running over 10 years CWF-Pro Glory came in and joined up with SGT USA and his cousin The American Weapon along with Sledgehammer to be TEAM USA. After awhile the team died off and TAW was released. SGT stayed on for some time and then retired. Hammer went for along time and out lasted the whole team. Glory retired after SGT did and started his own efed. He has not been seen since. Until He made contact with Pres Burns in Oct of 2008. He return for a one shot deal and won the cwf.pro title which was long over due, in a series match where the titles moved around alot. Glory now works back stage and will soon be managing. Exwrester Story: Glory: There isn't much to say about the reason why I left CWF. My time there was fun but I felt it was my time to move on. I wasn't going anywhere in the fec creatively and there didn't much point to remain in the fed. Though my time there, it was a good atmosphere most of the time except how you started getting into out of character inter fed battles that's my story I guess. (picture credit to IWO efed)
