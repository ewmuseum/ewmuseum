He had won world titles in each of his indy efeds (see his title history) PWF Love Wrestled all over the net untill he signed on with the PWF in 1998. There he became a superstar. The Talks went on for few weeks and before he was going to come in during the early 2000 run, he had death in the family (his dad) and took time off. After 2 months there was still no word from him. Rumors have it that he retired after the death os his dad Bruce Love. he has not been heard from him since.
