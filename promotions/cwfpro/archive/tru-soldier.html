Tru had alot to give to this sport, it's too bad he left. Exwresteler Story: Tru Solider: The Man, The Myth, The Legend, Tru Soldier, has returned to CWF to tell his story. The CWF had never experienced someone as good as me, and Pres Burn's didn't know how to handle someone with my extraordinary talents. I came in with two others wrestlers to form the Young Bloods. I quickly grew too big for the group and broke out by myself. After my success continued, and I held four belts, Intercontinental, Royal, Canadian, and Hardcore, at once. I was one of the biggest names in CWF, but I never got the respect I deserve. In one night, I lost all of my belts, to four opponents that were below me, which at first made me mad, but then I was given a shot at the World Championship, which made everything alright in my book. But I never did get that match at the next PPV. The next couple of TNF's, I wasn't scheduled for any matches, so I trained for my upcoming title match. I still made some appearances, about two a week, but without a match, there wasn't much for me to do. The next thing I knew, my shot at the World Title was taken away. It seemed to me that the Pres was protecting his little bitch, Mike Powers. He knew Mike could never beat me, and he wanted to make sure he had someone he could control with the World belt. This was too much bull[bleep] for me, so I moved on to much bigger and much better things. I'm out. I was as good as anyone in the CWF, and better than most, especially Mike Powers. When I first came to CWF, every rp was about 5 to 10 lines. Every other fed I was in, you had to rp at least 50 lines to be competative. That is why I was able to succeed so quickly, basically no one was on my level. When I finally did get my shot at the world belt, the prez starts pulling some bs about me not rping twice a week. I rped on monday, then friday, then the next wednesday after that, but the prez said he started the week on tuesday on something and that my rps weren't enough. He can have his twice a week rule, but what is really stupid is that he doesn't even consider length. Mike Powers would rp everyother day with 5 lines. I did every 3 or 4 days with 50 or 60 lines. Which is better? I got mad at the Prez, wrote him an e-mail where I basically cussed him out, but I still didn't get fired. I had to write another e-mail where I cussed at him some more, and quit. For the record, I was not fired. After the CWF, I got into some much better feds, where 150 line rps are the norm. In conclusion, CWF had some good wrestlers, maybe even some on my level, but I felt that the prez was full of shit, keeping his friends on top. Some of the people that got pushed definitley did not deserve it. And anytime the Prez received critisism, he becamse angry and pulled his whole "I've been doing this for three years. No one knows more about e-feds then I do. I am the greatest Prez ever." Maybe I didn't handle the situation in the best of ways, I'm out. .
