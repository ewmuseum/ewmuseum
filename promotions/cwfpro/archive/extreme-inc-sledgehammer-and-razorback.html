Extreme Inc (Sledgehammer & Razorback) Real Name: Brock Sledgehammer & Mark Razorback Handler's Name: [name withheld] Hometown: Denver, CO Height:(sledge) 6' 8" Height:(razor) 6' 9" Weight:(sledge) 250 Weight:(razor) 270 Age:(sledge) 36 Age:(razor) 34 Finisher: Double backsuperplex off top topes Group Affiliation: Team USA Manager: none Theme Song: "Extreme Inc Theme" Quote: "Time to get EXTREME!!" Email:[name withheld] Titles Won: CWF-Pro World Tag Team Champions (7) IWF World Tag Team Champions History: Earlier Career Sledgehammer signed as a rookie with the cwf.pro in 1998. CWF-Pro Sledgehammer came into the cwf.pro in a singles career. After wrestling in the cwf.pro for about 2 months. He then became apart of the TEAM USA with Glory, the American Weapon & SGT USA. After a few weeks the group broke up when TAW was released. Sledgehammer out lasted all three of them. Glory stayed for some time and then made his own efed. SGT retired soon after. The TEAM USA ended with in 2 months of it starting. Sledge would then leave after being in the cwf.pro for one year. Tag team return Sledgehammer then return a month later and brought in his brother Razorback to be in the tag team ranks. They mainly feuded with the Dragons and Insyders. After 1 year of ewrestling with the cwf.pro again Sledgehammer & Razorback retired and has not been seen since 2000. They did return for the big April 2008 TNF show in a ladder match. Exwrestler Story: Extreme Inc: This is Aaron I was the handler of Extreme Inc. (SledgeHammer and RazorBack) I held the Tag titles numerous times and was even voted best tag team once but never got my acceptance typed out so it was given to Double Dragons I think. Anyways My time in the CWF was very fun. I remeber when you guys did this before. I saw how some ex-wrestlers hated Burns cause he made them follow rules or get out. Well I followed the rules most the time. Burns and I got along well and even when some idiots where posting some porn on our boards and trying to hack our site I help take care of a few of there emails!! :) I left because I just didn't have time to dedicate myself to the CWF much like alot of you guys do. I am in Collage and almost done. Along with the fact that I am over 21 and spend lots of times out partying I just couldn't find the time a lot. I did recently talk to Prez and say that the site was doing great. I asked him if there was room for another Extreme Inc. return but he said no that my time was over in the CWF. I respect this I mean there comes a time when you just have to move on. Now if Prez wanted me to come back and help the site, wrestle just some matches (ie. Hogan, Savage) or wrestle full time I would accept cause I loved the CWF. But if not then I will still stop by the site and check out how things are going cause it is the best damn fed around. I am not to long winded so I think this will be it. "Beat me if you can Survive if I let you!!!" .
