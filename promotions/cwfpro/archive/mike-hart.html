mike hart® Mike Hart Real Name: Mike Hart Handler's Name: Mike Hometown: Hartown, Canada Height: 6' 2" Weight: 199 Age: 35 Finisher: Hartbomb (powerbomb) Group Affiliation(s): CWO, Canadian Foundation (leader) Manager: none Theme Song: Hart Theme Quote: "I am the hart of the Canada" Email:Mike Titles Won: CSW Royal Champion CSW World Tag Team Champion CWF-Pro World Tag Team Champion CWF-Pro Canadain Champion History: Earlier Career Mike was a rookie when he joined the csw in 1997 CSW In 1997, He started out in the CSW from www.prowrestling.com. There he became the CSW National champion. He did join the CWO gang in the CSW with Denny, Heartbreaker, Deem Hart and others. After the fall of the CSW. Mike would bounce from efed to efed not really doing much but matches and jobs. He did wrestle in the UCWF, TPW, SIW, and others CWF-Pro He came to the cwf.pro after talking with his friend Miguel Sanchez and Denny. He came in and had a feud with a "fake" Mike Hart or as he was later called Mike Hart II over who was the real Mike Hart. After beating Mike Hart 2, Hart stuck around for about 6 months and retired from ewrestling. Mike has stated in a shoot interview that he loved his time in the CWF-Pro and was glad he retired there on that note. He also said that he did like the 2 Mike hart storyline. Mike Hart has not been seen since 1998. Mike Hart: If you want an interesting story about why I left the CWF then I have it for you. It is a crazy business we have ourselves in here. A business with many ups and many downers. I remember back in the day when I sat and waited for my next match to happen. I still remember my first win, my first title, and each and every World Title reign. Along the way Mike Hart became a legend of the sport. Some may disagree but almost everywhere I went people knew what and who I was and that I am a force to be reckoned with. Eventually though something happened and I lost my drive. It's funny though because this was about the same time that Pres. Burns recruited me into the CWF. He knew I was a big name that was an eloquent and creative speaker. He knew that Mike Hart could and should be the headliner of his promotion. Should be if not for a certain someone that had a "brotherly claim" on the spot. Now, I'm not going to say that I lost a match that I didn't deserve to lose, but I am going to say that I won a few I didn't deserve to win. I got my first three wins in the CWF on name alone. I don't know if it was some sort of signing bonus or what but they were even over some of the CWF "big timers". I immediately got stuck in some angle with some punk trying to imitate the magnificent one (me) and it was perhaps the most boring feud of my career. It was labeled the Real Mike Hart (me) against the Fake Mike Hart. The fake Mike Hart never role-played once from the time I joined that fed and the pres. decided that it should take outside interference for me to win the retirement match. I just grinned and bared it though and kept on going. Miguel Sanchez was champion at the time and my record was a perfect 9 and 0. So I figured I deserve at least a little consideration for a title shot. Doing what I do best, I goaded Miguel into a war of the words and well, opened up that proverbial can of whoop ass on him but only in the role-playing room. It was turning out to be a pretty intense rivalry, the few times Miguel graced the role-playing room with his presence. Do you think I got a title shot though, no way. I was being groomed to be the Canadian Champion but I didn't want that. I have more championships than most men in this business and another small time belt was not my first choice. I didn't go after Miguel to get myself a name, I did it to try and salvage what little respect was left for that title. You know what though, that is a title beyond repair. I mean no offense to some of the guys who have held it because there were some good role-players in the CWF. But for a guy like Miguel Sanchez to hold it that many times with three line role-plays, that is not right. So I packed my bags after doing a few jobs and left the CWF. I don't even know if the CWF is still going or what but if it is way to go guys. Just try and get the president to do something about the most worthless World Title in E-Wrestling, believe me you need it. Like it or not, that is the way Mike Hart sees things..
