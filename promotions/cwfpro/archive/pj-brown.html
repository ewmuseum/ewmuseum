Pj went on to other efeds and has not been seen since 1998. PJ Brown: the name's Brown....PJ Brown. And yes, I did serve a short but ultra illustrious term in the CWFpro, the subject of this putrid documentary. I have been asked to narate, nay, tell the story of my CWFpro career. Now since all the PJmaniaks worldwide....all the millions apon millions of those people who live and breath the two aforementioned letters that forever define perfection, just can't live without my piece of the story, I shall grant you all that little wish, and tell of a CWFpro that once had in its hands the God of Wrestling. It was a sunny fall afternoon on Mnt. Olympus and I was, like many other days, playing a game of chess amongst the clouds with Hermes. He had wagered me his shoes that I couldn't force him to checkmate, but I did. However instead of giving me his shoes he gave me the gift of wrestling which he had stolen the previous summer from my half brother Heracles. Now then, apon hearing of my gift a Pres. Burns dialed my holy cell phone and offered me a merge contract with his fledgling organization. I felt that it would be a good opportunity for me to test out this gift to see if Hermes was attempting another trick, for if these silly mortals were any match for me the gift truly must be bogus. I arrived at the CWFpro soonafter in mid fall, near the equinox if I'm not mistaken, and i rolled easily through all opponents like I had prophesied, and the gift truly was as it appeared....I was the God of Wrestling. I quickly de-associated myself with a group known as the UBH, I had associated with to appear to fit in, but I soon grew weary of there pettiness. I won the IC Title countless times and took the Tag Titles by myself, and to tell you the truth did it all with ease. However throughout my career Pres. Burns insisted I 'job' to wrestlers I clearly needed to make a conscious effort to loose to. I dropped my belt to a mortal from the Celtic islands names Smokin' Joe MacDonald, and my Tag titles to a tag team that has since slipped my mind. Soon, however, I would regain the IC Title, with ease again, and was set to face a man named Draven at a Paid Per View performance. The Pres. insisted that I NOT ONLY loose to this Draven character, but that I do likewise to a man named Machine of War, who had a smoking problem and surely could not defeat one of my god-like toenails let alone myself. It was a travesty, throughout the land the PJmaniaks hollered that the match was a fraud, that a man as great as I could never possibly loose to a man such as MoW, who, though he may be greater than many mortals, was truly not PJ Brown. And so, after the match, with the voices of my PJmaniaks enforcing in my mind the course of actions I must take, I chose to quit the world of wrestling and the CWFpro, and return to my home in Olympus. But to this day, despite the attempts of Pres. Burns to cast a visade over my greatness, every man women and child alive and every soul gone or floating aimlessly in this desolate land, still consider me the God of Wrestling. .
