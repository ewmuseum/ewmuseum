He was again released for the same reasons Exwrestler Story: The Man with No Name: About the end of last year....I was going across these E-fed Lists..and I saw an add...It Was a banner of The CWF-Pro. Well..I went to it....and looked around and thought it was a decent fed....So...I sent in an app. Well, I got my reply....And in it...the Pres. said ok..but then he said this "Hey..I need someone to do a mystery guy...) so I thought about it...and said..Sure why not. Well get in the CWF-Pro...with my new guy. TMWNN. He's a jobber at this point, and starts making comments..that he is superior to the rest. Then he finds The Don....he gets together with him....Well...after about a month....My band starts doing alot of practicing.....and well...since my guy was mysterious anyway...I didn't RP....alot....every once in a while...but not alot. So..one day..I got on the net...and The Pres. emailed me...and said "You Need to RP" I said OK...But then I made the mistake of saying. "You have a Problem with me?" Which must have made him mad...so he fires me. I think "what?" But ya know ...no biggy...well 2 months pass....and yes..during that time I DO try to get back in. I see him on the net..and ask to talk. Well..we talked for about a min, and I say nothing bad at all. But after about a min....The Pres. sends me an ICQ message...saying that it is a waste of his time to talk to me.....(I have pple who can prove that) anyway...so i get all upset..and start talking to my friend on ICQ...(I will not state his name.) So, I am mad....about 2 weeks later..I get back on with him...and we start talking..well...we resolve our differences ....and well..ever since...I guess we have been Neutral to one another...But...the thing is...even though...I don't want back in...I just want to know...was saying "You Got A Problem With Me?" Wrong? or is it just the way he took it....I will probably never know....but...you know...That's how the world turns....well...I would ike to thank everyone..who helped me with this interview.... .
