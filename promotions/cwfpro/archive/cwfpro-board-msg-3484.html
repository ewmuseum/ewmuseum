the cwf.pro roleplay board: Cwf.pro Efed timeline(updated) [ post a reply | the cwf.pro ] Cwf.pro Efed timeline(updated) Posted by Pres Burns on 12/19/2001, 8:52 pm [detail withheld] CWF.PRO TIMELINE:1997: -Pres Burns(loose cannon), R@ven, Jimmy B(Jim Boss), Migual Sanchez, Jenny Love, Beastman, Hammerman, the Insyders:Kevin Hall & Scott Nash(Mike Powers), Jackson, White Zombie, the Double Dragons:Billy & Jimmy Lee, Legend, Rico Suave and Pj Brown all walk out on the NWA efed(which was ran by JL Black aka Shad Roberts) and start Building the cwf.pro efed -All the founding father join the cwf.pro: Beastman, Hammerman, Sanchez, Jenny, R@ven, Jim Boss, Jackson, Double Dragons, Insyders, White Zombie, Gator, Draven, TTB, and a few others -After winning the Royal title to be the 1st champion..Triple X leaves the efed for unknown reasons -the UBH gang lead but Sanchez join back together in the cwf -The 1st TNF show of the cwf.pro -the 2nd TNF all the belts are given out in a battle royal for each title. World, Tag, IC, Royal, and the Crown -Celitc Chaos turns on Gator and leaves his gang(the Kill Army) to join Sanchez in the UBH -Jock Joins -the Insyders break up. Scott Nash injures Kevin Hall in a match and UBH splits into two. One lead by Sanchez the other lead by Beastman. Beastman’s gang is called the UBH Blood Brothers-Mr. Ravishing & Ace Stevens show up for a PPV one timer -Justin K aka Rock Hard gets fired -Bane get's fired -1st PPV “the Beginning“, Sanchez loses the World title to Gator, after Gator puts on the Gator Streach. Jenny throws the match and G wins the title. -The next night gator coins the word ICON as he gives that name to Sanchez on the TNF -Tank wins the world title from Gator at a PPV. Then 3 nights later loses it on a TNF to Beastman -the UBH teams join together again and does the Insyders -Jackson retires after losing the title to Sanchez at the World Series of Wrestling I1998: -the Insyders retire and turn into the Preacher -Furnace is fired & stripped of the royal title. Jock tells the cwf that he will walk out if Furn is not rehired. Jock is fired and stripped of the world title. Legend quits and gives up the IC title. The next Night a big battle royal is on. Beastman wins the world title for a 2nd time. White Zombie wins the royal belt and Jackson wins the IC title. -3 nights later Jock & Furn show up on the CSW 2 efed. -the CSW 2(ran by JL BLack) goes to war on the cwf -CSW 2 closes and a week later there page is taken down by Geocities for slander on the cwf.pro ower Pres Burns-Lost Soul starts problems back stage about Jock returning. And is asked to leave the efed. -Pres Burns turns heel/bad guy -Rico Suave is made to retire after 3 weeks of not Role playing. To hold his head high as he leaves. The Cwf works out a deal to make him look good as a they show him retiring.-Ashley N the 1st female wrestler in the cwf.pro joins -Draven and the Crow switch places -Smokin Joe joins -Gator retires -Ultimate Man (called the biggest heel in e-wreslting) faces Migual Sanchez. Two be legends in e-wrestling fight and Sanchez beats him. Ultimate Man retires at this TNF show and shanks Sanchez's hand -Todd the Godd and Strike the bros from Hell retire -Draven turns from the crow gimmick to the Corp with Pres Burns.-the Preacher retires -Draven turns back into the crow gimmick -PJ Brown quits after losing two matches at the World Series of E-wrestling II.-Mike Powers joins the cwf.pro. Once known as the Insyders and the Preacher, he makes this gimmick hoping it works.-the Dragons split up -Billy Lee becomes Badboy Billy Lee -Jimmy Lee Becomes the Superstar Jimmy Lee -Draven falls to his death in a HELL IN THE CELL match with Capt Sexy as Cap superkicks him off the cage and dies in the fans. the cwf.pro gets a bad rap from the fans, news, wrestlers and everyone. -Smokin Joe is fired. -the don and the 1st family join -Mike Powers throws the US title from Billy Monroe's efed in the trash on a TNF 1999: -The UBH ends -Dragons rejoin together-Ceo joins-Mr. Tuesday Night Justin Hart retires -the don gets fired -Erick White joins Sanchez in the bigdogs as Jackson is injured. 4 weeks later they beat Erick White down and Jackson rejoins Sanchez. Erick White is fired by the cwf for not rping -deballegde goes on in the ring when the lights are out. Don't ask -Beastman & hammerman retire -Wolverine retires -The Cwf.pro closes for 3 months on Oct 12 1999 for a break, The Cwf.pro has a PPV called the Ending where Powers beats Sanchez for the world title-CEO & Enrique Vasques retires -reopens on 12/31/99 for the Y2K PPV everyone returns 2000: -TDW efed starts by Beastman-Powers beats Sanchez and retires him -Jackson & Sanchez come back for 3 months, they have a feud with some rookies the Young Blood (Tru Soldier, Archery, & Big Nasty)-the UBh is restarted with Sanchez(lead) Powers, Dragons. -Sanchez quits his UBH and Powers takes over. -UBH is over -the cwf gets a news board things pick up -Tru Soldier gets fired -The cwf.pro gets a backyard Wrestling fed started with (Beastman, Brad Burns, Mike Powers, Migual Sanchez, Amber burns, Hammerman, Todd the Godd and Pres Burns)-Sgt Majors Semper Fi gets fired when he gets upset over WZ world title win -White Z gets injured in a match with the Punisher & Adog. He leaves -CEO returns -CWF-Pro becomes the cwf.pro -Alex Draven(Draven's bro) Joins to take it out on Pres for his death -Mike Powers retires -Amber joins -Brad joins and 3 weeks later Chris Burns joins -the cwf gets new message boards In side the web falls. -the cwf.pro is offered $2000 to sell to a .com site, Pres Burns turns it down.-Powers becomes a 6 time world champions -Major D Joins -Hero & Ash K joins -Brittany Michaels joins 2001: -Amber takes over -Crow returns as Gemmie, he gets fired 3 weeks later -XR gets fired after only being together for 5 weeks. XR was CEO, Sanjour, Mr. Hardcore, & Dark Angel. Ceo remains on the cwf.pro roster -Powers retires after losing the title to Major D -Pres Burns wins a lawsuit over another efed for stealing his logos -Jenny Love turns on Sanchez and joins Pres burns in his Corp with CEO -cwfpro.com is up -MD picks fights with Loose Cannon -Loose cannon wins the world title but gives it up and the title is won back by MD -CEO gets fired -Brittany gets fired -"SuperSexy" Keith Preston joins -Lucas Hollywood walks out on Super to join an efed -Sanchez becomes the Commish -Ang joins -R@ven brings in seven -Amy Hall joins -Brittany returns -ninja x gets fired for being an ass -Ang wins the world title to become the only female champion -Powers returns -Ang & Brittany win the tag titles to become the only female tag champions -After losing the tag titles, Brittany retires -SuperSexy brings out his body guard -Adam X returns -Jim Boss turns into Jimmy B -The Johnson Boys are released -Enrique Vasques returns-Enrique Vasques gets fired-Angelica wins the world title and becomes the 1st female world champion-Beastman returns-Ash K returns-Brad Burns returns-Mike Powers returns-the Double Dragons retire-the NB is born-the New UBH is born-Amy Hall wins the world title and becomes the 2nd female world champion-the TDW and Cwf.pro join as one efed.-The TDW goes to war with the cwf.pro having TDW wrestlers vs cwf wrestlers-all the tdw and cwf.pro titles are joined in matches-Ang retires-mike powers returns-Ash K returns-the cwf.pro sells shirts-the cwf.pro & TDW closes-the cwf.pro becomes a full byw fed-Seven starts an efed and more than 70% of the cwf.pro wrestlers join.
