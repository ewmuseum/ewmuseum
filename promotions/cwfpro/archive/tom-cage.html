Whats there to say about Cage other than he is just Brutal and unrelenting to the core. Only one word best describes him....Legend Cage grew up on the Streets of South East Dc not much is knwon about this time in his life other than this is where he recieved his unmatchable tolarance for pain. CSW In 1997 Cage enlisted in CSW After a year went by Cage felt he was being that he was underrated in the E-feds Listing so with out notice and unprovoked He ventured out to TPW ( Total Package Wrestling) to prove to everyone that he was number 1. Upon entering the locker room of TPW via Friends of his (Lord Of Illustions, and others) this is where he found Terminator-X Ranting and Raving about how he is the best and TPW backs down from no one Well at the time Cage Was the CSW National Champion he took all of this as an insult after weeks of pleading with CSW officals to sign the match of Champion Vs Champion (CSW vs TPW) he was denied. The next TPW Broadcast was where he took it upon himself to try to get X to agree to a match Whereever or whenever After dozens of attacks,run ins, and verbal lashings from ring side the match was not signed and felt that it would be to graphic for anyone. CSW closed its doors not to long after that so came The IECW era this E-fed was kind rundown most of the Wrestlers in it were knockoffs of real pros (Austin, Hart, Hogan) on December 12 1998 Cage recieved his first World Heavyweight title for 9 months he remained Undefeated until its doors closed Cage bounced around for a little while after that Finding Homes in Both the UCWF Fueding with Miguel Sanchez and SIW (Slaughterhouse Internet Wrestling) this part of Cages life was plauged with hurtles Pain Killers and Booze were how he wanted to live his life after the death of his girlfriend during a car crash. SIW with all the major talents relocating to the bookers were back dating matches trying to find just one angle they could push there An angle was found It seemed they had Hired to of the worse enemies they could have and an angle was already ready for them all the had to do was sign it (Terminator-X Vs Tom Cage) this match was pushed for months untill it was signed for December 26 1999 The match lasted for 29 minutes both fighters (at this point neither one was wrestling) were torn to shreds the mat was blood soaked before it was finished in the End X had Defeated Cage cleanly for the pin hopped up on painkillers and refusing to leave Cage Grabbed the SIW title belt And Handed it to Terminator-X It seem that Cage was choosing to Show sportsmanship after the grueling battle after the belt was handed over and X's Arm was raised Cage struck repeatedly hammering the SIW champion Over and over again with what seemed to be a lugwrench and then hanging him above the SIW Arena after this PPV Cage was Barred from ever fighting in SIW again never recieving a rematch With X. UCWF At this time the new E-fed UCWF was coming into its own with established Stars such as Miguel Sanchez, Loose Cannon, Lord of illusions etc) Cage needing some place to vent frustration joined biding his time waiting for the proper moment before he would strike. In early Febuary 2000 Cage won the UCWF Championship from Sanchez Back and forth for months it went, till Cage was Injured and forced into an early retirement (side note was never beaten for the UCWF Championship during Second Rein) CWF-Pro Cage Vanished for years turning up from time to time in crowds but never wanting to fight In early 2007 he has reamarged in CWF-Pro how will this change everything only time will tell. He would lasted about 3 months and was released by the company. Cage has not been see since. Some rumors have said that Cage started his own efed called Thunder Wrestling.
