billy monroe Billy Monroe Real Name: Billy Monroe Handler's Name: Billy Hometown: Monroe, Ohio Height: 6' 4" Weight: 170 Age: 25 Finisher: Super Stunner (stunner off the top ropes) Manager: none Theme Song: "Monroe Theme" Quote: "Can you beat the best!" Email:Billy Titles Won: CSW National Champion TPW United States Champion IWF Champion (4) UCWF Champion CWF-Pro Canadian Champion History: Earlier Career He came into the csw as a rookie in 1997 CSW He started out in the CSW from www.prowrestling.com in 1997. He would only win the National title. After losing the title he left for other efeds Indy efeds He then went on to the TPW after the CSW. Then came to the UCWF then a few others. CWF-Pro He came to the cwf.pro (1998) and had a few good months. He was released after getting upset that Miguel Sanchez was the champion and how Pres Burns was a piss poor President. IWF IWF was an efed he started during his time in the UCWF in early 1997. He then retstarted the IWF after his release and Mike Powers joined it. Powers then walked out on him and took the IWF US title with him, which Powers still has to this day. Monroe was upset and his efed closed with only 5 members. He also crowned himself the IWF Champion a number of times. Monroe has not been seen since 1999. Exwrestler Story: Billy Monroe: I filled out an app for CWF Pro and 3 weeks later I was accepted. When I joined I was with the Bigdogs, but that wasn't the group for so one night I was talking to Beastman and I was going to join the Factor. I told Beastman for the Tuesday night show we could have a match Jackson and Miguel vs.Beastman and a mystery partner(me Billy Monroe). So that night I e-mailed the pres. and said I have a match for Tuesday Jackson and Miguel vs. Beastman and a mystery partner(me Billy Monroe). He e-mailed me back and said I didn't sign this match. I asked him on ICQ why can't we have the match. He said because he done had me doing something Tuesday. So then I said what about next Tuesday he said no. We had little argument about it which everyone has arguments. The Final words said was I said We are what makes this e-fed and we should atleast choose some of our match. He replied and said It's my e-fed I can do what I want. I don't have to take this your fired. I never said one curse word or even anything really out of line. He has a short fuss. So then I started my on e-fed IWF. When it first started I asked Burns if he wanted to have a PPV IWF vs. CWF. He said no. I said OK. The other day I asked him ever look at my website. He said no and I probably wont. I left it at that. So then the unexpected happened. I got a e-mail from my US Champ Mikey Powers saying he was going on to bigger and better feds that also had a Tuesday Night Show like mine. Well guess what it was CWF Pro. Am I mad at burns for firing me.not really. Am I mad at burns for taking my talent not really. The thing that makes me mad is he takes little feds like mines talent and sugarcoats a contract and then in 6 weeks fires them. Do I dislike pres. burns.Not really. Do I dislike R@ven. NO. Do I dislike the CWF period not really.I am just a guy that got pres. burns on a bad night and the short fuse. It hank you for your time its been a thin slice on heaven boys..
