Alfonso Sharp Mr. Hardcore Alfonso Sharp Real Name: Alfonso Sharp, Jr Handler's Name: Alfonso Hometown: Miami, Florida Height: 6' 3" Weight: 246 lbs Age: 26 Finisher: Sharpshot (DDT) Group Affiliation: XR Manager: none Theme Song: "Shape Theme" Quote: "I'm HARDCORE DAMNIT!" Email:Alfonso Myspace: Alfonso Titles Won: CWF-Pro Intercontinental Champion (2) CWF-Pro Royal Champion (2) CWF-Pro Canadian Champion CWF-Pro Extreme Hardcore Champion (2) CWF-Pro World Tag Team Champion CWF-Pro Hall of Fame TDW International Champion (3) TDW No-Limits Champion History: Earlier Career He was counted by R@ven in a far off efed. His history is unknown CWF-Pro Signed on with the cwf.pro in 1998 as Mr. Hardcore Alfonso Sharp. Later after a few months he droped the "Mr. Hardcore" name and was only known as Alfonso Sharp. After he and CEO lose the tag titles to the Burns Boys, he was very upset along with Sanjour. He and Sanj would start the XR stable in a way to gain power backstage and band together in a way to stop the guys from winning matches and titles they they seen didn't need them. basicly running the efed the way they want. With Adam X, CEO, Alfonso Sharp, Messiah & Sanj they formed the XR. After a few run ins with Pres Burns and the roster. Alfonso Sharp left the cwf.pro for the TDW after he and the rest of the group (not ceo) were released. TDW He won a match over Beastman for controll over the TDW. Had a good run there. Pres Burns has stated that "I have never had any bad feels with the guy. That is why I put the IC title on him before we closed along with the TDW International Title." Alfonso Sharp went off to other efeds after the closing of the tdw/cwf.pro Alfonso Sharp himself said that "that he was very shocked that Pres burns didn't job him out during the cross over" He did shank burns' hand after the show. Alfonso Sharp was in talks about a 2004 cwf.pro return, but things never did pan out. No word on what happen to him after 2004. Alfonso Sharp made his return in March of 2008. He has became apart of the roster. Exwrestler Story: Mr. Hardcore: My stories a Simple one, Myself and my "Crew" Were pretty upset that Brad Burns a guy who could barely write 2 sentences is getting the World title handed to him, while guys like myself, Sanjuro and the others that were fired were busting our asses trying to make the Cwf-Pro the greatest federation ever. Were busting our asses and not getting any respect. Instead myself and Ceo lost the Tag titles to the Burn Boys? Give me a break. I can beat them both in a handicap match. And since we spoke up, we were fired. Guess he (Prez BUrns) doesn't like being told what to do. So I and my friends were fired, I"ve retired from Efed Wrestling since then. Doesn't seem so fun anymore. But I get to retire as a Ten Time World HEavyweight Champion and holding most of the Cwf-Pro's titles, except for the Canadian and World title. NOt too bad for a guy everyone thought would be a nobody. I hope to proved them wrong, but don’t get me wrong I loved my time in the CWF-Pro. It was one hell of a ride. .
