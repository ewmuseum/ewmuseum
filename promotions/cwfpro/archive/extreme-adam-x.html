extreme adam-x® Extreme Adam-X Real Name: Adam Extreme Handler's Name: [name withheld] Hometown: Unknown Debut: 1998 Height: 6' 5" Weight: 190 Age: 35 Finisher: Starbreaker (shooting starpress) Group Affiliation: XR Manager: none Theme Song: "Adam X Theme" Quote: "Time to get EXTREME!" Email:[name withheld] Titles Won: CWF-Pro Champion CWF-Pro Intercontinental Champion (3) CWF-Pro Royal Champion (4) CWF-Pro Extreme Hardcore Champion (4) CWF-Pro Hall of Fame IWF Internet Champion IWWA Intercontinental Champion TDW International Champion History: Earlier Career Adam jumped from efed to efed not making it big anywhere. CWF-Pro Adam signed with the CWF-Pro in 1998. He won his 1st title The Royal title from Machine of War. This was a title to build up a young/rookie wrestler. Adam then went on to won the extreme hardcore title. Then making his mark by wining the IC title From the great legend Gator. Adam's rise to main event level took off fast. It was a time when the CWF-Pro needed new top stars. With the lose of other wrestlers leaving. Adam X and others were rushed to the top at a fast pace. Adam did however handle it very well and then became the CWF-Pro Champion by beating the Icon Miguel Sanchez. XR The XR or Xrteme Restiance was a stable that Sanjuro started along with CEO, Vasques, Mr. Hardcore Alfonso Sharp, and Messiah in 2001. This gang would be his downfall. It mainly was a way for Sanjuro to get power in the politics backstage. It is rumored that Adam was not apart of this and only went along for the ride to belong in a stable. With in 7 weeks of there newly formed group. All members were released. Adam did however shake hands with Pres Burns and said he would return one day. The others all walked out with nothing to say. CEO did however stay for about another 2 weeks to drop the CWF.Pro. TDW Adam signed on with the TDW. Where he became there Inertnational Champion. Many have rumored that it seems his love for the sport had died off. He did leave the TDW after a short run and did retire from ewrestling. Return to the CWF-Pro Adam and Pres Burns were in talks about a return in 2004. However there talks lasted for a few days and nothing was signed. He has not been seen since 04. exwrestlers' story: Adam X: Okay, as I'm sure most of you know, I was Adam-X in CWF-Pro. I had a pretty good run, even holding the World title. My buddy Sanjuro and I formed a group called the HeartBreak Hotel, and started a fued with the Burns Boys. We started haveing problems when we let Dark Angel become part of our group. Also at the time, we were told if we joined Beastmans fed,TDW, to say goodbye to our push. I joined TDW under another name, and tried not to refer to my past in CWF. I had a slight disagreement with Pres Burns, over someone elses Rp's. Then another member of the fed started emailing guys threatening letters, claiming to be me, when in fact it was Dark Angel doing the letters. Pres Burns and I talked several times about the issues, and I told him that I'd leave voluntarily, with no hard feelings between either me or the fed. Hopefully, someday I can return to CWF, and start over. CWF was probably the best fed I've ver been in. I took a break from efeds after leaveing the CWF, but Adam-X is eyeing a return to the mat wars........
