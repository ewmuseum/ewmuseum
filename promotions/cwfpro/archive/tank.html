tank Tank Real Name: Sean Tanker Handler's Name: [name withheld] Hometown: Unknown Height: 8' 0" Weight: 309 Age: 42 Finisher: Tank Bomb (powerbomb) Manager: none Theme Song: "Tank Theme" Quote: "I am the god of wrestling" Email:[name withheld] Titles Won: CSW Champion NWA Champion CWF-Pro Champion NWO Champion (2) CWF-Pro Hall of Fame History: Earlier Career Tank was a rookie when he came too the csw and became the 1st world champion CSW In 1997, He started out in the CSW from www.prowrestling.com. He is one of the few ewrestlers that started there. With guys like Mercury, Miguel Sanchez,Machine of War, Powler, Austin 9:11, Facing Death, Ace Stevens, Billy Monroe, Loose Canon, Mr. Ravshings and a few others. After losing the CSW title. Tank left the csw and went on to other efeds. CWF-Pro Tank came to the cwf.pro as a big name but soon after he won the CWF-Pro title. His name didn't help him do well. He was just a name value. After he lost the cwf.pro title to Beastman. He was released. He later came back again but was released for not roleplaying again. At the start of the CSW 2 he was there 1st champion again. But died off. He left and started the NWO efed, but it was shut down with in 3 months. Tank has not been seen or heard from since 1998. Exwrestler Story: Tank: I came into the CWF-Pro when it was brand new. I saw a wonderful potential in the CWF-Pro and its talents. When I got here the name that stood in my mind was Miguel Sanchez (My Enemy through and through). I wanted to get him very badly. But as time went on in the CWF-Pro, I just kind of lost the anger towards him. I later went on to win the World Heavyweight title, but I lost it to Beastman. The reason I believe I lost was at the time the CSW (Cyber-Space Wrestling) was re-opening under a man named Shad Roberts(JL Black). I had become very close to him, and as a personal favour I rejoined CSW. But without realizing it I believe I might have angered the powers that be in the CWF-Pro. This of course was a mistake. I did not realize that it was that easy to have my named ruined in that federation. I never wanted that. But later on, after the CSW and CWF-Pro war was raging on, I showed up on Tuesday Night Fight (The CWF-Pro's program). On that note I was fired immidiately by CSW. Which was fine, because the CSW closed down months after I was fired. I then was back into CWF-Pro, but I was unhappy with what had happen earlier in CWF-Pro and CSW. So basically my ties with both feds had been burned. Which I felt was all my fault. I then went into Heavy drinking, which eventually ended me up in the hospital for rehab. I got out about 8 months ago. I recently visited the CWF-Pro website and saw that it was still open. I then started to train. I thought to myself "I may be 44 years old, I may have won many titles, and I may have been a all time great in the sport. But one thing has not been finished. I need to tie the wound between myself and CWF-Pro" So as I thought that I got up off the couch. (At that time I was way out of shape. And I was slow.) I went back to wrestling school. I enrolled in Dick Murdoch's "School of Hard Knox". I went through hell just trying to keep up with the younger, more agile and quicker guys. At one time I also thought "I can't do it anymore" but along came my with Denise. Who stayed with me thru and thru. She had to remind me that I never ever quit,, and that I never ever say die. So I took her advice and tried even harder. I then graduated from The School. I am litterally in the best shape of my life right now. I am now 289 lbs, thats down from 331 when I was last in a CWF-Pro ring. A few days ago I contacted President Burns and we had a nice conversation. I believe that I might be coming back to the CWF-Pro, if it is the right time. I hope to see you all in the ring. Thank you for your time.
