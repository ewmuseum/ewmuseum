Email:[name withheld] Titles Won: CWF-Pro Champion CWF-Pro Intercontinental Champion (2) CWF-Pro Royal Champion (3) CWF-Pro Canadian Champion (2) CWF-Pro Extreme Hardcore Champion (3) CWF-Pro World Tag Team Champion IWWA World Tag Team Champion TDW World Tag Team Champion History: Earlier Career Amy Hall a girl that was born for this sport. She was trained by her dad Kevin Hall. Came into the cwf.pro as a rookie. To most she is merely a normal 20 year old gothic girl; wanting to make her name in the wrestling business...yet to those who TRULY know her...she is a 452 year old Vampire hailing from a very highly-respected mansion in Marseille, France. In the Year 1551; Aria was taken away from her family and lifestyle and dragged into the world of immortality - Not exactly happy with what her creator had forced her to become, she snuck away into a sort of slumber - hoping a sort of death might happen...yet over the four hundred years in which she slept, the sounds of the world were calling out to her louder than they had ever done...upon her awakening the world did not sound as it once did, more fearless...more ruthless...better even. She was to find the world was worshipping new Gods and Godesses...much different to the ones of the fifteen hundreds. Normal men and women were having mortals falling at their feet, begging them to come into their lives; These men and women were the wrestling superstars of today and Aria decided she was to become the new Goddess of the Mortals. Hence, her being here today... CWF-Pro Amy came into the cwf.pro as a rookie in 2000. She got a job in the cwf.pro because of her dad Kevin Hall one half of the Insyders. She won just about all the singles titles here in the cwf.pro. Now goes by the name of Aria. After a big fight with her dad about her image. She and her Dad are not on speaking terms anymore. She did have a breif run in the TDW while wrestling for the cwf.pro in 2000. The 2nd woman to become the cwf.pro champion. After the cwf.pro shut down in 2001. She left for the IWWA where her friend Lela Lane was wrestling. IWWA After leaving the CWF-Pro. She signed with the IWWA where she became a tag team champion. She lost contact with Kevin Hall and she has not been seen since 2003. She did however contact Pres Burns in 2004 about backing a return during the Beastman/Pres Burns era but nothing was ever signed.
