Exwrestler Story: Lost Soul: I remember the day very well that I got fired. It was my birthday Sept. 20 and I just got home with my friends. We got on-line so they could check the CWF. But low and behold my first email was from the pres. The first line read You services in the CWF are no longer needed, you are terminated. At the time I felt screwed over. But as I thought I deserved it. It all started in late summer when Furnace got fired which eventually brought down Legend and the Great One The Jock. I was becoming friends with Jock and we Instant Messaged each other quite a bit. This event made me think about leaving CWF. But I stayed. Jock did offer me a spot in the CSW's nBh but I turned it down to stay loyal to Pres. Burns After that event things got better in the CWF after a short sabbatical I returned I won the crown twice and then became the pres.'s first bodyguard. Everything was good but Sanchez/uBh/Bigdogs were in control and If you know me well you know Sanchez is the reason I came here. So I thought what was the only gang that ever was able to beat the uBh? That the KILL ARMY. Bringing back the army would stop them. I emailed everyone Gator, Sebek and others. At this time I hooked up with Joe Macdonald when he returned. We had some success before the split. Joe and my own inuring relationship ended. I needed someone else. I needed I asked the press If Jock could come back. He told me If I asked again he would fire me. And what did I do I asked again. So I was canned. The worst thing about it was how badly I was later treated. The day after I was fired at two house shows I lost to the entire roster. I hope you don't ever make the same mistake as I did. So basically don't be a jerk to the pres.
