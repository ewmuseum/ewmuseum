steve spice® Steve Spice Real Name: Steven Spicer Handler's Name: Steve Hometown: Little Rock, AR Height: 6' 6" Weight: 238 Age: 34 Finisher: Spice Bomb Manager: Sally Spice Theme Song: "Perfect Strangers" Deep Purple Quote: "Time to SPICE IT UP" Email:Steve Myspace: Steve Titles Won: CWE Champion CWF-Pro Royal Champion CWF-Pro Hall of Fame History: Earlier Career He was a rookie when he came to the cwf.pro. Steve singned with the cwf.pro in 1998. He would lasted for more than 8 months. CWF-Pro When Steve came in he did so well Burns has said that "He was going to push him all the way to the cwf.pro title." He started slack on the showing up for events (not RPing) and then he was released and also stripped of his Royal title. Spice then return and was rehired. Spice would remain on for only 2 months and was released again for the same reason as before. He had a great feud with Sanchez. Then after he had left, Sally Spice stayed. She joined with Sanchez & Jenny to replace Sarah Love. Then she was beatin in a match to leave the cwf.pro by Jenny Love. Pres Burns haa tryed to patch things up but Spice will not have it. Steve Spice did return in 2006 for a shoot Interview with Dawg. He also was put into the Hall of Fame in 2007. Steve would later return parttime and win the CWE title, but after losing the title he again left the cwf.pro and has not return since. Exwrestler Story: Steve Spice: i joined the CWF Pro a little over a year ago. I expected to be there for quite awhile. we started out with the whole crew, raid, xanadu, etc. and of course, my girlfriend sally. ... we started with a feud with the Canadian foundation. although I out red most of them, I still was on the losing side. I didn't say anything, because I thought that was just the way things went... maybe it is, I still don't know.losing caused me to start recruiting people left and right for X RAIDED, but there were no takers. anyway, after around a month, I was finally recognized as what I should have been, and that was a strong, irresistible force in CWF. as a matter of fact, I would say I was one of the best. around that time, I got my first title.. I believe it was the royal title. stevie and sally spice were becoming superstars. fans all over the world came to see us. we were the most popular couple in the CWF.. unfortunately, I made the fatal mistake of going on vacation for 2 weeks. the kicker here is that I told the pre I was going on vacation and I probably couldn't RP for 2 weeks... and he replied "OK, don't worry about it man" in a message.. I also mailed him when I got to California saying I probably wouldn't be able to RP. he never replied, but I know he got it. about a week in, I get to the page and I find on the PPV that I won the royal title... I thought finally I was getting the recognition I deserved. it was probably a week after that that I went back to the page and found I had been stripped of the title and fired,and SALLY WAS WITH MIGUEL SANCHEZ!!!!!! ... I emailed him asking what was up.. this was on a different address, but the title was "this is stevie spice from CWF" I didn't get an answer. when I got home I was upset and emailed him again, but this time I was pissed. he replied and said he didn't know you were gone, which I knew was a lie. I began to suspect a conspiracy.i almost quit right there, but he promised to give me sally back and say the sally with miguel was a fake, as in Sarah love..i came back and started rping like id never left, and I was my same flamboyant self. no one in the locker room cared much for me, but I didn't care, since sally and I were doing *other* things. I started talking about a world title shot, around this time, furnace approached me with an offer to join up with him and jock, since they were friends. I took him up on that cuss its good to have people watch your back, and jock was probably the best rper at the time. I constantly stated my respect for him before that. we talked a few times and we joined up. we kicked ass, but we started feuding with the big dogs. after that, for some reason furnace was fired, jock, the world champion was "fired"(the quotes on that ill get to in a moment") they went to CSW and goaded my to join.. I didn't. I said I would continue to fight for them in the CWF. after a week of constant "blackheart style" rping(blackheart meaning my friends got screwed, so IM gone stay here and kick everybody's ass just to piss you off") I found out I was fired. I was like "What!!!!!" I rpd. i asked legend, who was also in the jocks crew why I was fired and he said"lack of rp".. I mailed the pres., and he never replied.. I promptly went to CSW and bashed the hell out of CWF for not giving me a reason for the firing. I was pissed, like furnace.. and like jock seemed to be... and the CSW cards with constant bashing of the pres.?? well I won the title in my first week, and the fed got dumb, so I quit. a few months later, I returned to the webpage, and I found SALLY WITH SANCHEZ AGAIN!!!! I mailed the pres. in words of venom, profanity laced messages after he promised he wouldn't do it again, since sally was my girlfriend. I also found R@ven badmouthing me, and I was a regular butt of jokes on the PPV cards.. but jock wasn't...I went back again later, and found nothing.. then just recently, sanchez mailed me. i of course asked him if he knew why I was fired. he said that JOCK..my partner JOCK, and my other partner LEGEND,the guy who said i lacked RP, amongst others such as the biggest piece of trailer park trash vie ever laid eyes on, TANK had all come in contact with burnsie selling me out. they said i gave out the RP address. what a load of crap. i mailed jock, and used nice respectful words so I might get a reply.. i got NOTHING!!!!!!! i came back again and R@ven thought that my contact with sanchez meant i wanted back in.. well I personally DO hate pres. burns. he had NO REASON to fire me the FIRST TIME!!! and he broke his promise that i at least could keep the name sally spice.. apparently this whole rift was a on spicy just to get me out. burns never liked me. he always wanted me out. jock must have thought i was a threat, and i would have taken his belt. its true. if we handout teamed, i would have beaten him for that belt. tank is just scum, and so is legend. finally, the hall of shame.. IM on there, furn is on there.. but jock isn't???? theirs more proof right there. CWF is a federation that screwed me, and brought back wrestlers to lie about it.. me giving out the RP address is BULL$#iT. that is my story, and the TRUE version. anything else from anyone else is a load of CRAP!!!!!!! burns, if you read this, vie been to the page. badmouth me all you want to, it docent change the fact that i was one of, if not the best you've ever had. i guess ill find someplace else that will treat me with respect. and as far as jock goes, your not the only friend that turned on me. the last one got me kicked off aol.. you just got me out of a bad situation... .
