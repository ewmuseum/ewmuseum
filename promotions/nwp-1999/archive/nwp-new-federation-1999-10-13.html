New Federation — Limited Open Spots!

13 October 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Greetings RSPWF, I feel it's time for me to get back into the fedheading role, and that's exactly what I'm doing now. Northern Wrestling Promotions has been born, and it is a regional, angle-based fed with a limit of 20 singles, and 8 tag teams.
I have already recruited and accepted several handlers, so if you wish to take part, act soon. The handbook is attached, and the webpage is also up at [
http://northernwrestling.hypermart.net ]. Thank you, - [name withheld]

Attachment: NWP Handbook.txt Northern Wrestling Promotions Version: 1.0 [1] General [2] Rules [3] Roleplaying [4] Shows [5] Booking [6] Titles [7] Rankings [8] Staff/Personalities [9] Format [10] Application ======================================== ===1=== GENERAL ===1=== NWP (Northern Wrestling Promotions) is a regional fed based in Southern Ontario and some parts of Northern USA. The actual headquarters are based in Mississauga, Ontario - a suburb of Toronto. Although we may take the very rare trips further, the bulk of the cards and events will take place in Southern Ontario and parts of the Northern United States like Detroit and New York. The president out of character is [name withheld]. Harpreet has been in e-wrestling for over a year and a half and in his tenure, he has participated in the UEW, WWO, and other feds handling Prince Judife and the tag team of "The Good, The Bad, and The Ugly". He also has quite a bit of fedheading experience, as he ran the TWL for a year. Keep in mind that NWP will usually have a roster cap of 20 singles and 8 tag teams to insure that everyone keeps involved and gets matches regularly. ==2== RULES ==2== The first and primary rule of the NWP is simple. Have fun! Now for some of the disciplinary ones that we hopefully won't have a problem with. - SWEARING: Keep it relatively clean. If you must, use [BLEEP]s to show your wrestler's frustration or anger. For example: "I am getting so f[BLEEP!]ng bored reading this handbook!" Get the idea? Basically, if they can't see it on daytime TV, don't use it. In the end, the president will make the decision about whether or not profanity was improperly used. - ROLEPLAYING: Just remember -- quality over quantity. Only 1 RP a week -- don't bother doing more. If you want to get more involved, write matches or do in-rings! - RACISM: Shouldn't even be an issue. Not tolerated. Period. - IN-CHARACTER: Don't go out of character! Remember, we're pretending that this is real. And to do so, we can't use words like "jobber", "heat", "heel", "face", etc. You won't see these words used in WWF or WCW, because they're not words that wrestlers would use! The same applies for you -- don't use them! - COMPLAINTS: Make them fairly! If you must, bring them up without accusing me of being biased, etc. =====3===== ROLEPLAYING =====3===== You'll get one chance per week to roleplay -- take advantage of it! For those of you who want to do MORE, you can't. But you CAN do in-rings, and they're heavily encouraged. Now, the NWP will have one flash show a week - a "preview" to the weekly show. This flash show will come out Sunday, with the card itself on Monday. Because there is only one chance to roleplay, take advantage of it, but keep in mind that roleplaying alone is not the only way wins and losses are decided. Strats also play a factor, but angles supercede EVERYTHING. You must understand this. Remember to use the format shown in Part 9 of this FAQ! ==4== SHOWS ==4== The NWP will feature ONE wrestling card per week: "NWP Spotlight!" - The big weekly show. Will usually feature 6 matches (occasionally more) plus in-rings, angles being furthered and everything else a big show features. Spotlight is hosted by Thomas Hunter & Wilbur Jones, and takes place live every Monday night from 8PM to 10PM on CTV Sportsnet and TSN in Canada, and ESPN in USA. Currently it is only featured locally on those stations (Southern Ontario and Northern USA), but there are chances for increasing the viewer range. Deadlines: Strats- Thursday at 8PM EST Roleplays- Saturday at 8PM EST "NWP Sub-Zero!" - This is the weekly update. It'll come out soon after the card is released, and will feature news, next week's card, the roster, rankings, injuries, rumours and more. Deadlines: None "NWP Flash Forward" - The flash show -- all the roleplays for the week will be in this puppy. It takes place Sunday night, and is aired later that night on the NWP stations. It is fully commentated by the NWP interviewer, Deadlines: Roleplays- Saturday at 8PM EST NWP House Shows: When the need arises, the NWP will air the results of the house shows it regularly puts on. This is more of a spur-of-the-moment thing and won't take place regularly. NWP Pay-Per-Views: NWP PPVs won't occur very often. Expect 4-5 throughout the course of the year. This way there will be enough time to get angles fully developed and keep the PPVs special. January - "Canadian Corruption" April - "Brawl at the Border" July - "No Retreat, No Surrender" November - "Northern Exposure" ===5=== BOOKING ===5=== Roleplaying is quite important. Staying active is essential! Doing strats regularly also help your chances of winning, but angles are the most important factor. If there is not an essential angle that needs to be played out, the winner or loser will come down to roleplaying, and to a lesser extent, strats. But keep in mind - a loss or two won't kill you! Records aren't important when it comes to deciding title shots and other related topics. For this purpose, I will not give a formula or percentage to decide winners or losers. ===6=== TITLES ===6=== NWP will have 2 singles, and 1 tag team title officially recognized, at least for the time being. With such a small fed, 2 singles titles should be enough to keep everyone involved, and depending on how things go, a third will be added later. -NWP Greater North Championship (often referred to as the Heavyweight Title) This is _THE_ title in the NWP. The man holding this title is the top in the federation. This is usually defended on the large PPVs, but should be defended once a month. The title defenses will be a BIG deal, and will not be given out very easily. No weight limit or minimum, anyone can attempt for it. -NWP Television Title The secondary NWP title. Again, no weight limit and fairly angle-based, probably won't change hands too often, either. The winner of this isn't considered the number one contender to the Greater North Championship, although it can be used as a stepping stone. -NWP Tag Team Titles The titles that only the best tag team in the NWP can hold. The same as the Greater North Championship, except it's for tag teams. ===7=== RANKINGS ===7=== There are two types of rankings: win/loss and contendership. Win/loss does not factor in greatly for anything, because it's strictly if you won or lost, not counting angles or anything. Contendership is where you really stand, and it wouldn't matter if you had lost all your matches. Both will be listed on NWP Sub-Zero. As I said, winning or losing is NOT that important! What matters more is running a good angle. =========8========= STAFF/PERSONALITIES =========8========= OUT OF CHARACTER: President/Booker: [name withheld] Match Writers (thus far): [name withheld] [name withheld] [name withheld] Ryan H. This means all action is in SQUARE BRACKETS, with the person's initials preceding. For example, since the commentator names are Thomas Hunter and Wilbur Jones, here's how a snippet of a match could look like. ==== WJ: Come on! X is dominating Y! [X sets Y in the corner before spinning him around and knocking him down with a lariat. The crowd explodes.] TH: What a devastating maneuver! He'll be down for a while after that one. ==== NOTE: Initials are before what the person says, and ALL action is described with square brackets. THIS APPLIES TO ROLEPLAYING AS WELL!!
