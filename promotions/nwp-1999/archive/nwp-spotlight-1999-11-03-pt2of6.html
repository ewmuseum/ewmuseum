TH: Yes, sir! Fans, it is now time for the inaugural match in NWP, and this one should be a doozy! WJ: A doozy is right Tommy. We get to see two of the craziest guys in this fed go one on one in one of the strangest matches there is. TH: We are going to see El Puerco and XeRo go at it in an "Asylum Match". The rules are quite simple in this one fans. The first one to put the other in a straight jacket... wins! WJ: And if I have my way, we're going to see that crazy XeRo strapped up, and taken away to the loony-bin. TH: Well you have no say in the matter Wilbur. El Puerco will be the judge of the matter here tonight. [Scene switches to a shot of the ring announcer, Bobby McCarville standing in the center of the ring.] TH: Well it looks like we are ready to go. [BIGGER CROWD POP!!!] BM: Introducing first... [The Mexican National Anthem comes up over the speakers, and the crowd gives off a mixed ovation.] BM: He stands at 6 feet tall, and weighs in at 233 pounds. He hails from the beautiful country of Mexico, and is accompanied to the ring by Davis Jefferson! Ladies and gentlemen, here is... E L P U E R C O ! ! ! ! ! ! ! [The music gets louder as flashy blue and red lights flicker in the entrance way. The curtains then part and out steps the masked luchador himself, El Puerco. He is then followed by Davis Jefferson, who is decked out in a suit this evening. El Puerco wears a blue, red and purple glittery mask, with long purple tights which have "EL" down one leg, and "PUERCO" down the other. He also has blue fingerless gloves on his hands. He and his manager walk down the aisle, and the luchador shouts out a few Mexican phrases as they enter the ring.] WJ: Whoohoo! The most luchalicious man in profressional wrestling is here in NWP. What a way to kick off this fed. TH: El Puerco is an impressive athlete. As is his opponent, so this should be a great one. WJ: His opponent is impressive!? XeRo couldn't... {{{DON'T {BEEP} WITH THE MASTER OF {BEEPING}!!!}}} [HUGE POP!!!] TH: It's XeRo! [All the lights go out in the arena. "Breathe" by Prodigy Blasts over the speakers. A blinking strobe light slowly fades on the enterance way. A smoke cloud engulfs the enterance way as purple and green lasers circle the arena. We can see a figure in the enterance way with his arms raised. When the drum beats of the song start to hit, flames shoot up infront of the cloud and the house lights come on. Suddenly "Livn' La Vida Loca" by Ricky Martin starts to play over the speakers. ] WJ: What the hell is this!? TH: I'm not sure what's going on here. There is a lot of smoke on that stage, but I think I see something moving in there. [As the smoke clears we see a Midget wearing a sombrero and dancing around with Chalupas in both hands. He has churros all around his belt and borritos along the rim of his hat. There is a guy in a large yellow chicken suit behind him doing a chicken dance El Puerco is looking at them weird. ] WJ: Can you belive this? After all that stuff XeRo said about going "hardcore on Puerco's ass"!? XeRo has got to be the lamest of the lame! TH: I find this funny! Besides, you know XeRo... this will have a purpose to it. WJ: What? To Promote Taco Bell? [ The midget throws the Chlupas into the crowd, he begins to walk down the aisle, tossing the borritos from his sombreo along with the chicken . The chicken continues to do his dance around the ring as the midget climbs into the ring and asks for the mic. The music fades as he speaks. ] M: Greetings El Puerco! Tis I... Your long lost cousin La ParticalManga! WJ: La Particalmanga? M: Chu Miss me? ['Particalmanga' jumps up and hugs EP around his neck, weighting him down. The midget's sombrero goes fling off to reveal his Green mowhawk.The chicken slides into the ring behind EP, takes off his head...] [FACE POP!!!] TH: THERE HE IS! THERE HE IS! XeRo is right behind El Puerco! [The midget jumps off of El Puerco. El Puerco enraged by the midget jumping on him kicks him out of the ring. He turns around and XeRo blindsides him with the chicken head! Puerco drops to the mat rubbing his head as XeRo goes to turnbuckle and raises the head for the crowd to a large pop.] TH: XeRo just popped Puerco with that chicken head! WJ: That cheater! Disqualify him right now ref. DING!!! DING!!! DING!!! TH: This is a no DQ match Wilbur! Anything goes in this one. WJ: And look what Puerco is about to do! [With XeRo up in the corner of the ring showing off for the fans, Puerco sneaks up from behind and DROPKICKS him over the top rope and to the outside of the ring. XeRo staggers to his feet in the large chicken suit, and El Puerco charges the ropes... and hits a beautiful corkscrew plancha on XeRo!] TH: What a move from El Puerco! WJ: The man is fantastic Hunter. [El Puerco now grabs XeRo by the neck and lifts him to his feet. A hard irish whip sends XeRo crashing into the steel guard rail. El Puerco takes a few steps back, then charges in with a cross body press against the steel railing.] WJ: He ain't letting up here either! LET'S GO MEXICOOOOO! [With XeRo stunned, Puerco pushes him aside and walks over to the time keeper's table where the straight jacket is located. Puerco grabs the jacket from the table and spins around towards where he left XeRo, but is met with a spear tackle from the crazy man himself. XeRo then starts pounding away with fists of fury on the masked luchadors skull.] TH: XeRo is pounding away with those fists! He isn't letting up here either! WJ: Disqualify him ref. Those are closed fists! TH: For the second time, this is NO DQ!!! WJ: Oh yeah, I forgot. [XeRo grabs El Puerco by his mask and the luchador drops the straight jacket. XeRo with a few hard right hands that stagger Puerco, and now he grabs the ring bell from the table beside them. He raises it over his head and... ... Puerco kicks him in the stomach which causes him to drop the bell! XeRo doubles over and Puerco grabs him by the head, and nails a DDT on the concrete!] WJ: I can't believe that fool would stoop so low as to hit a man with the ring bell. TH: Those guys will do anything for a win here. Each man wants to be the first winner in NWP. WJ: Well in my opinion, XeRo is the first loser! [With XeRo down on his back, El Puerco climbs up on top of the time keeper's table. He then leaps and soars through the air, and NAILS a splash onto XeRo's chest area! Davis Jefferson is cheering his client on with every move, and Partical Man looks awfully upset with how things are going thus far.] TH: Vicious splash from Puerco! But that padded chicken suit XeRo is wearing might have reduced some of the impact. WJ: That cheater shouldn't be allowed to wear that! [El Puerco now feels a little momentum as he lifts XeRo up to his feet, and nails a hard right hand which sends some feathers flying. Puerco now lifts XeRo up and drops him throat first across the steel guard rail on the outside. XeRo clutches his throat in pain as Puerco walks over and grabs the straight jacket once again. When Puerco gets close to XeRo, XeRo performs a kip up out of nowhere and then nails Puerco with a superkick.] TH: Where did that come from!? He nailed that kick out of nowhere! WJ: I think he was cheating again Thomas. TH: He wasn't cheating! Sheesh. [XeRo now grabs the straight jacket out of Puerco's hands, and wraps it around El Puerco's neck. He then pulls back and starts choking the masked man for all it's worth. The fans react wildly, cheering on every XeRo move.] TH: XeRo has that straight jacket wrapped around Puerco's neck, and now he is dragging him around the outside of the ring! WJ: Now _THAT_ is cheating! TH: I guess it is. But remember this is no disqualifications Wilbur. He can do that as much as he wants. It's all legal! [XeRo releases the choke hold and tosses the straight jacket into the ring. He then lifts El Puerco up and rams his forehead into the steel post a few times before tossing him into the ring. XeRo then grabs a chair from ringside and tosses it in the ring as well.] WJ: What is that fool doing!? He's littering the ring with these objects! TH: I have a feeling El Puerco isn't going to enjoy all these objects either. [XeRo hops into the ring and wipes the sweat from his face. He then lifts El Puerco back up to his feet and nails a back-breaker. XeRo then feels the rush as he grabs the chair and climbs to the top turnbuckle.] WJ: What is that fool doing with that chair!? TH: I'm not sure, but he is up on the top rope with it and El Puerco is down on the ground! [XeRo raises the chair into the air and the crowd cheers him on. He measures out El Puerco and then leaps through the air with the chair looking for a splash... but El Puerco raises a foot and connects with the chair, which in turn SMOKES XeRo in the face!] TH: Oh my! That chair went right into XeRo's face! I think he may be busted open with that one. WJ: Yes! Yes! I love it. Bust him up some more El Puerco! [Indeed XeRo has been busted open, as there is a sign of blood trickling down his forehead. El Puerco gets up to his feet and pulls XeRo up by the hair. He sends the XeRo into the ropes, and when XeRo comes off Puerco catches him and nails a tilt-a-whirl backbreaker. XeRo screams out in pain as his back lands across Puerco's knee.] TH: El Puerco has the advantage now and he is going for that straight jacket. WJ: Lock that moron up and let's move onto the next match! [Puerco brings the straight jacket over to XeRo, and tries to put it on him. Unfortunately for Puerco, XeRo's chicken suit is too large, and the jacket can not fit over it.] TH: What a smart move by XeRo! That jacket can't go over the chicken suit. Puerco is going to try and find a different way to get it on him! WJ: Well what he is doing right now might work! [El Puerco pulls XeRo up and tries tearing away at that chicken suit. He grabs XeRo's right arm and rips the suits piece right off of his arm, sending feathers flying everywhere. XeRo comes to his senses and realizes what is happenning. He quickly grabs Puerco and delivers a short-arm clothesline. XeRo then climbs on top of him and starts pounding away with a flurry of punches.] TH: XeRo didn't take too kindly to Puerco ripping his suit! WJ: Get that fool off of him! [XeRo quickly lifts Puerco up to his feet and whips him into the corner. He then charges in and nails a handspring elbowsmash which rocks El Puerco. XeRo then accends the turnbuckle, grabs Puerco by the neck, and delivers a punishing tornado DDT. El Puerco lays out cold in the ring as XeRo hops the ringropes and grabs the timekeeper's bell.] WJ: What is that moron going to do with the bell!? TH: I'm assuming he'll use it on Puerco, don't ya think? Duh! [XeRo slides under the bottom rope and approaches El Puerco. He gives him a couple swift kicks, and then places the ring bell on El Puerco's face. XeRo then quickly accends the top rope and looks out to the crowd with a grin on his face.] TH: The fans are going wild Wilbur! They want to see El Puerco knocked out cold here! WJ: Get up Puerco!!! [With the bell on El Puerco's face, XeRo leaps from the top rope with a flying elbowdrop . . . . . ....DING!!!] TH: HE NAILED IT! EL PUERCO JUST HAD HIS BELL RUNG!!! WJ: This isn't good! Get up El Puerco!!! [XeRo kicks the ring bell off of El Puerco's face, and rubs his shoulder a bit as it appears he injured it with that move. There is a bit of blood soaking through El Puerco's mask, so it is obvious he was busted open with that move. XeRo though grabs Puerco by the mask and pulls him back up to his feet. He sends the luchador into the ropes, and XeRo himself goes to the opposite side. Puerco bounces off his side and XeRo springboards himself off his ropes... and hits a SPRINGBOARD CROSS BODY PRESS!] TH: El Puerco is taking a beating here right now. It's going to be hard for him to come back. WJ: He can, and he WILL! [With both men bloody and beaten, XeRo senses that he has to finish El Puerco off soon. He brings El Puerco up to his feet and kicks him in the stomach. Puerco doubles over and XeRo grabs him and goes for the XeRo DrOp, but Puerco reverses the move and hits a german suplex on XeRo.] WJ: Yes! Yes! He escaped his finisher! TH: A smart move by Puerco to avoid the Drop! [Puerco now quickly sends XeRo into the ropes, and when he comes off he catches him with a hurricanrana. Puerco then grabs the chair that was laying in the ring from earlier, and begins bashing XeRo in the stomach with it!] TH: He is brutally assaulting XeRo with that chair! WJ: Puerco is just repaying the favor from the ringbell incident. Hit him a couple of times for me too! [Puerco tosses the chair to the side and sets XeRo up on the top turnbuckle in the corner. Puerco then climbs up as well, and now both men are positioned up top. El Puerco looks out to the fans with his bloody mask, and then grabs ahold of XeRo. He tries lifting him up for a superplex... but XeRo blocks it! He tries again... but no luck.] TH: XeRo is blocking the superplex! He won't let Puerco hit it. [XeRo retalitates with a couple hard right hands, and then reverses the hold. He then lifts El Puerco up... and SUPERPLEXES HIM BACKWARDS TO THE OUTSIDE OF THE RING AND BOTH MEN LAND ON THE STEEL STEPS!!!] TH: DID YOU SEE THAT!? OH MY! THAT WAS AMAZING!!! WJ: BOTH MEN ARE DEAD!! THEY BOTH LOSE!!! CROWD: NWP!!! NWP!!! NWP!!! TH: XeRo and El Puerco are both laying on those steel steps, and both men's bodies are mangled and twisted. WJ: Incredible! What a way to open the fed! [Both men look to be out cold after that awesome superplex, but amazingly XeRo begins to come to, and sits up. The fans cheer on wildly as XeRo gets up to his feet and wipes some of the blood from his forehead. He then slowly grabs El Puerco and rolls him into the ring. XeRo slides in after him and looks down at the straight jacket which lies in the center of the ring.] WJ: Is he going to strap it on or what!? TH: Now would be the best time of any! [XeRo shakes the cobwebs off as he lifts El Puerco to his feet and kicks him in the stomach. El Puerco doubles over and XeRo then grabs him, and nails THE XERO DROP to the delight of all the fans in the Air Canada Center.] TH: XERO DROP! XERO DROP! IT'S ALL OVER!!! WJ: Awe damn! [The bloody XeRo then goes down and grabs the straight jacket. He looks out to the fans and then kneels down and puts it over El Puerco's head, pulls it down over his body, and tightens the strats to secure it.] TH: IT'S OVER!!! IT'S OVER!!! XeRo has put El Puerco in the straight jacket!!! WJ: I didn't hear the bell though! TH: That's because it is sitting in the middle of the ring you goof. WJ: Oh yeah! BM: Ladies and gentlemen, as a result of putting his opponent in the straight jacket, the winner is... XERO!! ["Breathe" blasts over the speakers as XeRo climbs to the top turnbuckle and raises his arms. His face is covered in blood, as is the chicken suit that he wears. The crowd is going nuts and cheer his name over and over.] TH: Wow, what a match THAT was. WJ: Damn that XeRo. I was hoping HE'D be going to the loony bin. TH: No, sir! It'll be El Puerco heading to the asylum for this whole week. WJ: Anyway, what's going on now? TH: Let's go backstage. We have an interview ready, I believe... [Scene fades in on a parking lot full of cars. At first glance, we see many expensive cars such as BMWs and Mercedes, along with some small cars. From the back, a ahite stretch limo pulls up. The limo is decorated - across the first door, we see "Roman" Thomas Lawrence" and behind that a small picture of a chariot of some sort. As the limo comes to a stop, after a few seconds, the limo driver gets out and walks back to the last door and opens it. Out steps the rookie to wrestling and NWP, "Roman" Thomas Lawrence. Lawrence is dressed very nicely in a nice gary suit with Rockports and a stylish tie supporting his name. As he steps out of the limo and begins to walk towards the entrance, he is swarmed with reporters. In the back, the NWP interviewer Nick Demola lags behind the crowd. As Lawrence continues walking, Demola begins yeling out questions over the crowd.] ND: HEY! Toga boy! You got a problem? Got piledrived by the Trojan Horse or something? TL: Who the hell said that?! Just for their info, I _AM NOT_ the one with the problem. I believe you are mistaking me for yourselves. I follow and live by Roman ways, meaning I am _SUPERB_ to all other beings, INCLUDING ALL OF YOU! [Blabbing over the crowd, Nick demola continues...] ND: You think you're something? You think you're BETTER than the GOD OF WRESTLING? [Lawrence now figures out who is asking these questions.] TL: Better? I believe saying I am better would be an _UNDERSTATEMENT_! Not only am I better than you, I am _SUPERB_ to you, and for the matter, everyone else in NWP. And for the people who are daring enough not to take my word for it, I will go out and prove it. This week, I will conquer some man by the name of Lawrence Williams, how dare he take my last name like that, and next week it'll be a gauntlet match. My path of destruction will lead all the way up to Northern Exposure where I will capture the coveted Greater North Title, while everyone else in NWP watches in _AWE_. ND: Let's get this straight, rook. I've had more belts than you've got pubic hairs yet. So if you want to talk smack to someone, I'm not even going to waste my time. Talk to Gene here... [Nick Demola shoves a small, bald, and chubby man dressed in a suit towards Lawrence and takes off back into the arena.] TL: I've talked enough for now. You'll hear me _AGAIN_ later tonight. If Demola cannot be found, don't blame me for his disappearance. I guarantee you that if I get a hold of him, he will disappear for good! [On that, Lawrence shoves his way through the crowd of reporters and walks quickly into the arena. Possibly after Demola? The scene switches back to the announcers' table.] TH: Well, I guess we'll just have to wait and see what he'll do here tonight. WJ: Yup. WJ: As we prepare for the next match, here's a pre-recorded segment from "El Dub" Lawrence Williams! [The scene opens up to a shot of a group of children playing Red Light, Green Light in the street. An orderly row of children stares down one little boy, who, in this order, covers his eyes, turns around, and yells out "Green light!" Simulataneous with the yelling, the row of kids breaks apart as the kids begin a frenzied sprint towards the one child who yelled. Suddenly, the child screams "Red light!", and all but one unfortunate child stop. They laugh at the little girl who hangs her hand and walks off the street and takes her place on the curb. The yeller is about to start the game over again, when a car comes down the street, generating a mating whale-like groan from all of the kids who scurry off the street. However, the car pulls into the nearest driveway to the camera as the kids reform in the preceding spots. The car's engine shuts down as the drive opens the door and sticks a leg out. "Green light!" The man emerges and upon sight, it's the man whom we all--hopefully--know: Lawrence Williams. "Red light!" Lawrence steps out of his car and slams the door shut as he yells out to the girl who didn't stop in time.] LW: You out already? [The girl painfully nods as Lawrence chuckles to himself and proceeds to walk up the driveway and onto the porch upon which the camera crew stands. One look at the camera crew and Lawrence, as if almost reminiscing before us, dressed in a black two-piece suit, black dress shoes, and golden wristwatch, speaks for the very first time in almost three months.] LW: Wow. It's been a while, gentlemen ... a *long* while. ["REDLIGHT!" This yell catches Lawrence's attention as he turns to his left to watch the game, as if the camera crew isn't even there.] Those kids--they're playing that game we used to play: Red light, green light. [smile] I remember playing that game for hours upon hours. It was always fun ... til that one kid decided he ain't feel like playing anymore. [The camera almost on cue with Lawrence's speech, zooms in on the girl who refuses to play again now that the previous game was over.] When that person quit ... it was pretty much detrimental to the game-playing mood. You ain't wanna play anymore. [Lawrence looks back towards the camera, creating another close-up of himself.] Well... in the game of professional wrestling, I was that one that quit. That's it: I quit. [Another quick zoom-in on the girl who refused to play again; now she begins walking towards what is assumingly her house. The camera focuses in on Lawrence again.] Yeah, I quit. I couldn't take it no more. The constant starting and stopping--the green lights an' the red lights--were getting to me. My whole persona was driven by notoriety. Once the red light was given, my notoriety was no more--so I hatta quit that portion of the game, jus' as that little girl hatta do when she couldn't stop on red light. [Lawrence moves past the cameraman and heads up onto the porch, where he begins shuffling around with his keys.] [pause] And jus' like her, I couldn't take the notion of defeat--so I quit, unknowingly sufferin the biggest defeat of my career. [Lawrence finds the proper key, sticks it into the keyhole, and turns the doorknob. However, before he steps in, he turns around.] But one of Newton's law of motion states that for every action ... [dramatic pause] there's an equal an' opposite reaction. An' this reaction ain't gon' be what you used to witnessin'. There's no more "confidence" built upon false images of godliness. There's no more 'straight outta Compton' mentality used in order to incite some kinda' intimidation in my opponent. Nah.. None of that. None of that conqueror, cocksure, confident garbage. What you seein' right now is what you gonna get. Me. Jus' Lawrence Williams. _"El Dub"_ Lawrence Williams. [The camera begins zooming out while fading out as Lawrence steps through the door. However, before all audio and visual is lost, two words are uttered from one of the kids as the game has been restarted: "Green Light!"] TH: Well, looks like it's about time for a bettle between two highflyers. This should be a great match, don't you think Wilbur? WJ: Only if "Delicious" Dave Davidson wins. Did I ever tell you I wrestled him one time? TH: No, you didn't. WJ: Well, to make a long story short, he beat me after he cheated. Of course, the only reason I _EVER_ lost is because of outside interference. But then again, if I were wrestling myself, I would probably have to win by cheating too. I was just _THAT_ good. TH: OK, sounds like fun. lets get on with this match!
