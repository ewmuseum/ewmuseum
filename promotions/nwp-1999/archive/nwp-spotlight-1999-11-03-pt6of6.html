Spotlight!

3 November 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
BM: The next competitor hails from Atlanta, Georgia, and weighs in at 275 pounds. Here's "DIVINE" DALLAS GREENE! ["Larger than Life" by the Backstreet Boys cues up. "Divine" Dallas Greene steps out from behind the curtain and begins sprinting down to the ring. He is wearing a pair of blue wrestling boots, black knee-pads, blue wrestling shorts that say "Divine" on the back in red writing and a "Divine Creation" t-shirt. He gets in and goes right after Knight.] BM: And the fifth person in this contest... hailing from Scotland and weighing in at 245 pounds. He is GARRETT SELCOUTH! [The hard lyrics of the song "Focus," now begin as the NWP fans alter into a stirring curious atmosphere. The fans turn their attention towards the curtain entrance area and all of a sudden, a hand pops out and pulls back the red entrance curtains. The well defined body of Garrett Selcouth walks out as a mixed crowd erruption occours. Following behind the focused Garret, in a very slow pace, is none other than his manager, Patrick Jaeger. He takes his time, and slides into the ring, but gets attacked by Blades almost immediately after doing so.] TH: Here we go! Blades and Selcouth battling, with Knight and Greene on the other side. [Blades gets Selcouth, and scoops him up, taking him down with a Powerslam. Without giving Selcouth a change to recover, he follows up with a series of elbow drops to the side of Selcouth's face as he lies on the ground.] WJ: Come on Selcouth! TH: Blades is really giving it to him right now. Greene and Damon Knight are still exchanging blows on the other side. [Greene tries a Vertical Suplex, but Knight is able to counter, kicking his way out of it and then taking Greene down with a Rocker Dropper.] TH: That, right there was a Knight Light! And it just took QUITE a bit out of Greene. WJ: Good for him. Shows just WHO isn't Larger than Life. [Greene gets up, but Knight goes into the ropes and does a Springboard Moonsault right onto Greene, taking him right back down.] WJ: That's the way, baby. Knight all the way. [Knight turns around to pick Greene up, but Blades rushes him from behind and takes him down with a belly-to-back suplex. Selcouth is up by now, and he drops a cheap elbow on Greene before getting up and locking up with Blades again.] TH: Selcouth doesn't care about fighting. He'll win at any cost. [Selcouth manages to get an eye rake in, and as Blades covers his eyes, Garrett takes him down with a Twisting Facelock Bulldog. Everyone is down except Selcouth, and he walks over to Greene, picking him up.] WJ: Selcouth has to be the freshest guy in there right now. Even though the match hasn't been going for too long, Blades got double-teamed earlier, and Knight's taken a little bit of punishment as well. [Without warning, Selcouth takes Greene down with a Split-Legged Piledriver into a Bodyslam. Greene is down, and shows no signs of getting back up very quickly.] WJ: The Guerilla Heartthrob!! That was Garrett's Selcouth's finishing maneuver, and it's just knocked Greene damn near unconcious! [Selcouth smiles, standing over the body of Greene. As he savours the moment, Blades comes in and knocks him to the other side of the ring with a devastating clothesline. Damon Knight also gets up around this time and scoops Greene up. He sends him towards the ropes, and Greene comes back wearily.] TH: Greene is REALLY out of it after that Guerilla Heartthrob. [He manages to duck under a clothesline, but gets an elbow from Blades, who was rearing back to hit Selcouth. Greene doubles over, and Knight scoops him up. Before he gets a chance to do anything, Blades knocks hiim backwards, sending Greene down on top of Knight. Selcouth also gets up and takes down Blades from behind.] TH: This is crazy! Everyone's taking down everyone else. [Selcouth gets Greene up and sends him into the ropes. Again, Dallas manages to duck, but he gets caught afterwards by Blades who sets him up for a back body drop. Blades gets him up, but gets speared by Selcouth running in. Knight was behind Blades, and he catches Greene before he hit the mat, and kept him going in the air, sending him out of the ring!!!] WJ: Whoa! BM: Ladies and gentlemen, DALLAS GREENE has been eliminated from this match! TH: Greene is DOWN! Blades tried taking him out, but it was Knight who got the job done in the end. But Dallas is really hurt right now, and he'll be carried out of here. WJ: Who cares about that! Let's find out our next two people. BM: The next participant hails from Grand Rapids, Michigan. He weighs in at 269 pounds... here is "GORY OREO" FOSTER CRANE!! ["Sabotage" by the Beastie Boys starts up, but there's no sign of Crane.] BM: Ahem. Here is... "GORY OREO" FOSTER CRANE! [Still no sign of him as Knight celebrates in the ring, standing in the corner. Blades and Selcouth are quite angry at each other and begin really nailing one another.] WJ: WHAT is up with this? TH: I don't know! But I think they're going to announce the next pers-- [Suddenly, the lights go out. They come back in just enough time for everyone to see Crane pop out from under the ring, ripping a hole through the mat!] TH: THERE HE IS!! IT'S THE "GORY OREO"! [Knight looks shocked as Crane heads straight for him and puts him in a full nelson. He jumps up and falls forward, sending Knight's face into the mat. It's "The Unthinkable"!] WJ: Pretty interesting entrance, that's for sure! TH: And that move sends Knight DOWN! WJ: Where's the next man? BM: Introducing the next participant in this match... he's from Stockholm, Sweden. Weighing in at 275 pounds, here is "The Swedish Stalker" Shalatna Merski! [The Jumbo-tron switches to a shot of a car -- it's the inside, and it appears as if Merski is driving somewhere.] TH: Fans, I've just been informed Shalatna had a problem getting to the arena. He's on his way here right now! WJ: When's he going to get here then-- [We're interrupting by a HUGE crash sound -- it comes from the Jumbo-tron, and a car is trying to run Merski off the road. We get a shot from the front bumper, and it's the face of a quite angry person. After continous bumps, he finally swerves drastically, sending Merski flying off the road and into the darkness. Shortly after, we hear a loud crash, and as the camera struggles to get a view, we see the car in flames. The person driving the car looks over quickly before speeding off.] TH: Wow! Fans... I'm not sure what to make of this. This does not look good! We'll try to keep you updated and find out what's going on out there. WJ: That was pretty strange. But what happens now? Is Merski eliminated? BM: Ladies and gentlemen, the next participant... TH: I guess so! BM: He hails from Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania and weighing in at 275 pounds. Here is "DOOMSDAY" VADEN DUGGERT! ["Hells Bells" by AC/DC starts up as Duggert steps out from behind the curtain and walks down to the ring with a menacing look on his face.] TH: That leaves only TWO people to be announced, and one of them are going to be announced right NOW! BM: The next participant, from Lawton, Oklahoma and weighing in at 275 pounds, he is "BRUTAL" BRIAN FLOYD! ["Took My Place" by Third Day begins to play over the arena sound system. White lights strobe randomly around the crowd, then come together suddenly as Brian Floyd appears from the wrestler's entrance and begins to make his way down the aisle. Brian wears a serious, determined look on his face. He slaps fan's hands along the way as they cheer him and shout words of encouragement. Clothing wise, Brian has on a plain white t-shirt, his navy blue wrestling tights with a large, white cross on the buttocks of them and the word "BRUTAL" running down each pant leg, black elbow pads, knee pads, and boots. As he gets within ten feet of the ring, he breaks into a full-out sprint and does a head first slide under the bottom rope, then hopping up from the mat with ease, arms raised in the air. He gets in the ring, and instantly gets into a fist fight with Duggert.] TH: Duggert and Floyd, the two newest members have decided to lock it up, and they're hammering away at each other! WJ: Six people in the ring right now! One person still to be announced. Looks like everyone's found a dance partner. [Duggert and Floyd continue to battle, while Knight - who is just beginning to recover from the 'Unthinkable' - and Selcouth are going at it. Crane and Blades are on the other side, and Crane just took him down with a Brainbuster!!] TH: This match is just madness! [Duggert and Floyd hit each other with a double clothesline, and both are down. Garrett Selcouth comes over and picks up Vaden Duggert and sends him into the ropes. Selcouth clotheslines him, trying to send him over the top rope and Duggert falls over, but clings onto the bottom rope.] TH: Duggert isn't eliminated quite yet! WJ: Look at Floyd! He sees what happens, and he's going after Selcouth! [Floyd runs up behind Selcouth and gets ready to tip him over the top rope, but Selcouth moves out of the way and grabs Floyd's arm, sending him over the top!] TH: He lands on Duggert and takes HIM out as well!! BM: Ladies and gentlemen, BRIAN FLOYD and VADEN DUGGERT have been eliminated!! TH: We're ready for the final person, and the ring is still packed! BM: The final participant, from Tombstone, Arizona and weighing in at 230 pounds. He is "The Last Real Man"... DOC HOLLIDAY! [The memorable opening to "Blaze Of Glory" by Jon Bon Jovi opens up, and a pair of beautiful ladies, wearing stylish tuxedo-wear arrive at the top of the aisle, to the whistles and applause of the males in the crowd. The two ladies hold open the curtain... but no one steps out!] TH: Not this again! It looks like Holliday doesn't want to come in here tonight. [Knight and Blades lock up on one side of the ring, and Knight gets the advantage, suplexing Blades backwards towards the middle of the ring. Selcouth and Crane have also linked up and begin exchanging blows.] WJ: Blades is down in the middle of the ring. [As Knight turns around to take a breather and lean on the ropes, we see Holliday jump out from behind the crowd and onto the apron!] TH: IT'S HOLLIDAY!! IT'S DOC HOLLIDAY! [Knight tries to move back, but Holliday catches him and puts him straight up, suplexing him onto the outside!!] BM: Ladies and gentlemen, DAMON KNIGHT has been eliminated from this match-up! TH: What about Holliday?! He went down too! WJ: But he hadn't entered the ring yet! So his elimination doesn't count. [Holliday then rolls into the ring and goes to work on the fallen Blades.] TH: 4 men are left in the ring right now. And after the next elimination, we're going to switch to a pinfall or submission style elimination! [All 4 men retire to individual corners for a breather. Holliday does the same, but then charges out and starts attacking Selcouth.] TH: Garrett's getting what was coming to him! WJ: Holliday's just making sure everyone knows not to mess with him. And he's doing it quite well right now! [Holliday gets Selcouth in position and sends him down with a tilt-a-whirl DDT, his "Arizona Airbreaker"!] TH: That just HAD to hurt! Selcouth is down, and Holliday just looks over him with a grin on his face. [Blades and Crane are going at it on the other side of the ring, and Blades tries a suplex, but Crane reverses it into a brainbuster! Holliday goes over and clips Crane's knee from behind, sending him down. At this point, Selcouth somehow gets up and starts walking up behind Holliday.] WJ: Watch out! [Selcouth spins him around and sets him up for a bodyslam over the top, but Doc hooks him in a small package type maneuever and rolls him THROUGH the ropes. Both men land on the outside, with Doc on top.] TH: They went through the middle rope, so they're both legal right now! Doc's on top, and that doesn't bode well for Selcouth. [Without hesitation, Holliday starts pounding away on the fallen Selcouth, who's underneath him. Meanwhile, inside the ring Crane and Blades are in a lock-up. Blades gets the upperhand and gets Crane up, ready for a powerslam over the top rope!] TH: Say bye-bye Mr. Crane!! [Holliday gets off Selcouth and grabs the ankle of Blades from outside the ring. Blades slips, and Foster falls on top of him.] WJ: Wow! That just HAD to kill off a lot of fight left in Blades. TH: Yep. He's been in from the start, and here's Crane, picking him up and getting ready to hurl him over the top rope -- THERE HE GOES! [Holliday ducks to avoid Blades, so Adrian goes down and lands on top of Selcouth, hurting both men even more!] BM: Ladies and gentlemen, ADRIAN BLADES has been eliminated from this match! We will now switch to a pinfall or submission ONLY elimination match. TH: And two of the three men in this match are OUTSIDE the ring. [Holliday gets an exhausted Adrian Blades off Garrett Selcouth, and then slides the injured Selcouth back into the ring. Crane attacks the fallen Selcouth, but Holliday charges him and sends him towards the other side ropes. Crane rushes back, and Holliday catches him in a sky-high powerbomb. He walks Crane towards Selcouth, and lands him so the back of his head lands on Selcouth's groin.] WJ: ... Ouch. TH: I would not want to be Crane NOR Selcouth at this point! WJ: They're both down, and it looks like Holliday is going to the top rope! [As a default, Crane is on top of Selcouth, and the referee begins to count... 1! 2!! 3!!!] WJ: He's gone!! Selcouth is eliminated! TH: But look how much damage he took... can't blam-- [Thomas is interrupted by Holliday, who flips off the top rope, landing on Crane with a Reverse 450 Senton Splash!] TH: ACES HIGH!! THERE'S THE ACES HIGH!! [Holliday hooks the leg, and the cover... 1! 2!! 3!!!] TH: WOW!!! [The bell rings as McCarville gets the microphone.] BM: Ladies and gentlemen, here is your winner, he will be the FIRST of three participants at the PPV to vye for the NWP Greater North Title... "THE LAST REAL MAN" DOC HOLLIDAY!! [Holliday starts celebrating as if he just won the superbowl.] TH: Boy, how did he do it?! He managed to win the match without taking any big punishment. Holliday's certainly a sneaky little wrestler. WJ: He's also my favorite to become first NWP Champ! TH: Regardless of what he IS and HOW he managed to get the Number 10 spot, we're out of time. Thanks for tuning in, fans! We'll see you NEXT week!
