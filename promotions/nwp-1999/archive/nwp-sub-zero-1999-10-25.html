Sub-Zero!

25 October 1999

[A black screen. We can see nothing, and hear nothing. All we can do is anticipate.] VO: Northern Wrestling Promotions. [A logo slowly comes onto the screen...] ___ ___ ___ /\ \ /\ \ /\ \ \:\ \ _\:\ \ /::\ \ \:\ \ /\ \:\ \ /:/\:\__\ _____\:\ \ _\:\ \:\ \ /:/ /:/ / /::::::::\__\ /\ \:\ \:\__\ /:/_/:/ / \:\~~\~~\/__/ \:\ \:\/:/ / \:\/:/ / \:\ \ \:\ \::/ / \::/__/ \:\ \ \:\/:/ / \:\ \ \:\__\ \::/ / \:\__\ \/__/ \/__/ \/__/ VO: You haven't seen ANYTHING like it!! [The logo begins to freeze, and the following words appear underneath the logo.] VO: Today, the NWP is born. Be prepared, because after this, the world will no longer be the same. You won't ever think, breathe, or eat the way you do right now. Enjoy the show. [The voice slowly fades out, and Metallica's "Trapped Under Ice" starts up in the background.] # I don't know how to live trough this hell Woken up, I'm still locked in this shell Frozen soul, frozen down to the core Break the ice, I can't take anymore # [As the song continues, a logo comes into the screen as the NWP logo fades out...] _______ ______ _______ _______ _______ _______ ( ____ \|\ /|( ___ \ / ___ )( ____ \( ____ )( ___ ) | ( \/| ) ( || ( ) ) \/ ) || ( \/| ( )|| ( ) | | (_____ | | | || (__/ /_____ / )| (__ | (____)|| | | | (_____ )| | | || __ ((_____)/ / | __) | __)| | | | ) || | | || ( \ \ / / | ( | (\ ( | | | | /\____) || (___) || )___) ) / (_/\| (____/\| ) \ \__| (___) | \_______)(_______)|/ \___/ (_______/(_______/|/ \__/(_______) # Freezing Can't move at all Screaming Can't hear my call # [The word Sub-Zero begins to shake, and finally explodes, sending pieces of ice flying across the screen, until we are eventually back to a pitch-black screen.] # I am dying to live Cry out I'm trapped under the ice # [Now we slowly fade into a studio. At first glance, it seems similiar to any other studio, but it's far from that. For this is the studio of Northern Wrestling Promotions -- what could be the hottest new federation around. In the center of the studio is Bobby McCarville, the NWP interviewer and Sub-Zero host. He is dressed sharply, with a navy blue designer suit, and his hair slicked straight back. He shuffles some papers on his desk as the camera moves around the studio, getting a good look at the desk Bobby is sitting at. The desk is black, with a dark blue "NWP" logo engraved on the front. Behind Bobby is a single huge screen which currently has nothing but an NWP logo on it as well. After getting another close-up of the desk, the camera moves in and settles in on Bobby's face.] BM: Welcome fans! I'm Bobby McCarville, the NWP's interviewer and Sub-Zero host, and this is the first ever NWP footage you will ever watch, and it'll certainly be quite interesting! Tonight, we'll inform all the curious fans out there about what's going on with Northern Wrestling Promotions. This will be a considerably shortened version of Sub-Zero, as we only have the roster, and announcement of the FIRST ever NWP card for you! So without further ado, let's show you the current Northern Wrestling Promotions roster. At the moment, it looks like there will be quite a few additions in the near future, but at the moment, it looks something like this. [A graphic shows up behind Bobby, and it's the only thing visible to those watching at home:] Adrian Blades "Homeless" Joe Boxcar (B.U.M.) Devon Case "Gory Oreo" Foster Crane "Delicious" Dave Davidson El Puerco "Mr. Excitement" Alex Extreme (The Masters of Disaster) "Brutal" Brian Floyd "Divine" Dallas Greene "The Last Real Man" Doc Holliday "Devastating" Damon Knight "The Child of Fire" Kossuth Larry (B.U.M.) "Roman" Thomas Lawrence "The Swedish Stalker" Shalatna Merski James "Simply The Best" Pillow "Hot Stuff" Scott Richards Ryan Rumble (The Masters of Disaster) Garrett Selcouth "The Ladies Man" Vince Taylor Lawrence Williams XeRo [The graphic slowly dissolves.] BM: In my humble opinion, it's quite an impressive one! And let's not forget a HUGE part of the new NWP commentating crew. He is one Nick Demola, making his return to the wrestling world! Northern Wrestling Promotions are very excited and are looking forward to working with Mr. Demola, along with all the talent listed. Nick will be the federation's new interviewer during NWP Spotlight, and will also be performing other tasks previously assigned to myself. Not that I'm bitter or anything. [Bobby tries to hide the envy in his eyes.] BM: Anyhow, moving on, let's discuss what's going on with the next few cards! So all the fans now, the first EVER NWP card will take place on November 1st, from the Air Canada Centre near our home in Toronto, Ontario, Canada. I will go through the line-up for that in just a moment. This card will be an NWP Spotlight, and so will the week following -- November 8th. After that, the first PPV will take place in late November, but more information on that will be released in the coming weeks. [Bobby turns around, and faces another camera.] BM: Earlier today, Marcus Johnson, the NWP President and Commissioner released his plans for deciding the first EVER NWP Greater North Champion. The concept will be as follows: - On November 1st, the main event will be a 10-man gauntlet/battle royal (rules explained in the line-up). The winner will given Spot #1. The people that aren't in this 10-man will be in the second week's match. - On November 8th (the second weekly show), the first match will be the other 10-man gauntlet/battle royal with the winner being given Spot #2. Later that night, the main event will be the other 18 participants in a Royal Rumble for Spot #3. - The main event for the first PPV ("Northern Exposure") will be a 3-way Ladder match for the title. [Bobby turns back to the other camera.] BM: Certianly an interesting way to decide our new champion. Now, let's get on to the first card -- the one that will take place on November 1st! ================ Taking place LIVE from the Air Canada Centre in Toronto, Ontario, Canada on November 1st, 1999, we're looking forward to an ACTION-PACKED night! In the opener, we'll see an old rivalry re-kindled, as EL PUERCO will square off against XERO! Johnson has ordered an "ASYLUM" match -- meaning the winner is the first person to get their opponent into a STRAIGHT JACKET! The loser must spend an entire week in an asylum!! Both of these men are known for their strange style, and this match should certainly prove to be one for the books! The second match of the night will feature two self-proclaimed chick-magnets going at each other! "THE LADIES MAN" VINCE TAYLOR will square off against "DELICIOUS" DAVE DAVIDSON! We'll find out who the more talented one REALLY is. After that, we'll see El Dub, LAWRENCE WILLIAMS going up against "ROMAN" THOMAS LAWRENCE! Williams has a storied wrestling career, while Thomas is trying to make a name for himself. And he could definitely do that with a big win here! Next up, we'll see James "Simply The Best" Pillow step up against Devon Case! Two of the finest atheletes in the wrestling industry will go at it in what should be one of the best matches of the night! Then, we'll see the NWP's tag division in action! B.U.M., consisting of "HOMELESS" JOE BOXCAR and the giant LARRY will take on "MR. EXCITEMENT" ALEX EXTREME and RYAN RUMBLE, collectively known as THE MASTERS OF DISASTER! I, for one am certainly looking forward to this one! In the main event, we'll see a match to decide the first of 3 challengers for the Greater North Title at Northern Exposure! The participants of this match are: Adrian Blades "Gory Oreo" Foster Cane "Doomsday" Vadden Duggert "Devastating" Damon Knight "Brutal" Brian Floyd "Divine" Dallas Greene "The Last Real Man" Doc Holliday "The Swedish Stalker" Shalatna Merski "Hot Stuff" Scott Richards Garrett Selcouth The match will go down like this: 3 people will be chosen to start the match off. Every time ONE person is eliminated, TWO more will come into the ring. This will continue until there is only one man left standing. Elimination can occur via an over-the-top elimination only! However, the last TWO men must be eliminated via pin or submission!! The winner will get the first of three spots at the PPV for the Greater North Title!! ================ BM: And that's all for this week's "Sub-Zero"! In the next issues, there will be a lot more to discuss, but now you're informed, and let's look forward to the first ever NWP card -- "SPOTLIGHT!!"
