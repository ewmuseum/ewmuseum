Flash Forward

3 November 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Another match that will certainly be interesting is the only tag match of the evening -- B.U.M, consisting of "Homeless" Joe and Larry will take on this team -- the self proclaimed Masters of Disaster! _________________________________________________ | ___| | | |___ | | |_ | Masters of | _| | | _|LASH | Disaster | DRAWRO|_ | |_|_______|___________________________|_________|_| [The scene opens on Toronto, Canada at nightfall. The lights of the city shine brightly reflecting the vibrance of the large city. The camera zoom forward toward the city below. The shot dissolves to the Hard Rock Cafe and inside of it where Alex Extreme and Ryan Rumble, the Masters of Disaster, are having a few drinks and some dinner. Extreme, a tall dark haired gentlemen with a goatee, is wearing black suitjacket, a purple mock t-shirt, and a pair of blue jeans. Rumble, a taller muscular tanned man with long blonde hair, is wearing a black dress shirt and a pair of black dress pants. The two are sitting at a table having a few drinks while waiting for their food to arrive.] Alex Extreme: Now this is more like it. Betty Ford was nice and all but it lacked action. There was no nightlife at all. [Ryan gives him a funny look.] Ryan Rumble: Um, it's a drug rehablition center. It's not supposed to have any nightlife it's supposed to get you clean and sober. [Extreme's eyes and his attention trail off following a young blonde female waitress who walks across the room.] AE: Yeah right, uh huh, clean and sober. Boy I'd love to get clean and sober with her. R: Man, I just can't take you anywhere can I cousin? AE: Hey, I can't help it! It's been a couple months since I got some. It's not like the Betty Ford Clinc is a place to pick up some nice women or anything. [Rumble looks at him and rolls his eyes as Extreme gives a chuckle and grins. At this point a attractive brunette haired woman walks up the table and straight up to Ryan.] Woman: Hi, my name is Raquel. You're cute. Call me. [With that the woman drops a piece of paper down on the table in front of Rumble, giggles, and wanders off. As the woman walks away Ryan shakes his head in disbelief while his cousin Alex's jaw looks like it just busted through the floor of the restaurant.] AE: How the hell do you do that? R: Do what? AE: 'Do what' he says. Unbelievable. On a scale of 10 that girl rated a 2000 and all he can say is 'do what.' Argggghhh... [Extreme hangs his head in shame.] R: Call me old fashioned but I just don't believe in one night stands. [Alex does a double take and with a dumbfounded look on his face.] AE: We can't be from the same family. You sure you're not adopted? R: Look I know you like life in the fast lane but I just don't finding it that appealing. I like a drink and women as much as any other guy but I just like to take it a step or two slower than you do. AE: I got it! It must be that you lived in Wherever all that time in cheeseeaterland. R: Don't got there. AE: Oh really? And why's that Ry? R: Okay you asked for it. How many games have the Bears taken from the Pack the last few years. [Extreme stays silent for a moment with a look of utter anguish on his face while Ryan has a smile that could glow for miles.] AE: You know sometimes I really hate you. R: I know. So tell me cuz, what do you know about this team we're gonna fight? AE: Well, I know Homeless Joe. I met him a couple of times. We've never wrestled before though. He's a pretty good guy though and damn good wrestler from what I've seen. I have no idea who this Larry guy is though. To be honest Ry, I don't much about these guys at all. R: Oh that's just great Alex. Glad to see that once again you're so damn prepared. AE: Well you can't prepare for a team if they never existed before. We're just gonna have to be careful and take a longer time in the feel out process than usual. Besides, a loss now means nothing. Now if the tag team titles were on the line it would be a different story but one loss isn't gonna kill us. [ A red haired waitress comes up to the table with the two cousins food. She sets down their dinners, looks at Ryan, and hands him a piece of paper.] Waitress: Call me. [The waitress happily walks off. Extreme hangs his head in disgust and dismay.] R: Don't say a word. Just shut up and eat your damn food. AE: God sometimes I really hate you. [Fade to Black.] BM: There's a team that's definitely one of the most interesting ones I've ever seen! Now, we heard from "Roman" Thomas Lawrence, but now let's hear from the man that will be squaring off against him. "El Dub" makes his return to the wrestling world, and he had quite a bit to get off his chest. _________________________________________________ | ___| | | |___ | | |_ | "El Dub" | _| | | _|LASH | Lawrence Williams | DRAWRO|_ | |_|_______|___________________________|_________|_| [The scene opens up in an incommodious wrestling training gym in Los Angeles, California. A wrestling ring is found in the center of the gym, with several rows of folding chairs on each of the ring's four sides. Littered around the ring are crushed paper coke cups, squashed popcorn bags, and balled-up programs which inform the spectators of the evening's entertainment. As the camera gets a low- angle shot, it begins panning the vacuous gym as a shadow in the center of the ring is seen for the first time. The shadow seems to be the silhouette of a man standing as still as the leaves on a tree during a windless night. As the camera zooms in, the figure is revealed as "El Dub" Lawrence Williams, who is standing next to one of the sets of ropes with both arms on the top rope, looking out into the empty rows of chairs. As if he knows the camera is upon him, he begins to speak.] It all started right here, y'know? [Knowing that no one behind the camera knows what he speaks of, Williams steps forward finally letting the camera get a detailed view of his attire and facial expression. Decked-out in a long-sleeved gray turtle- neck, dark gray dress slacks and black dress shoes, Lawrence seems as if he were the promoter of the preceding show.] My professional wrestling career started right here-- in this very gym. Wasn't no more than fifty people in the audience. Some were little kids at their first wrestling show ... others were eldery men looking to get away from it all ... some overly anxious teenagers ... some people from the so-called lower socioeconomic class attended the show. [Lawrence grabs the ropes again and pans the room with his eyes. After a long, look-into-the-past glimpse of the room, he continues.] But to think ... I went from fifty to fifty thousand. I went from *having* to wrestle for money to jus' goin' out an' havin' a good time, knowin' that my check's in the mail. [Lawrence takes his right legs and puts it through the top and middle rope, eventually stepping out of the ring and out onto the concrete floor. He stops and looks around the gym again.] Why does this matter? Where does this relate to the Northern Wrestling Promotion? It's simple, really. This is where I started ... I started out as a wrestler lookin' to make it big--an' I eventually did. But somewhere along the line, I let the money get to me, an' on came the arrogance-- the attitude that has ruined so many careers. I fell off the track of success an' endin' up losin' focus. I made excuses when I lost. I played off the fact that I was gettin' beat by wrestlers that would *never* stand a chance against Lawrence Williams. But they weren't wrestlin' Lawrence Williams--they were wrestlin' _"Confident"_ Lawrence Williams, a man created by the money an' destroyed by that very same creation. [Lawrence turns around to face the camera.] But no more. No more am I gonna taunt the crowd ... no more am I gonna taunt my opponent. No more ... None uh that. It's just me, "El Dub", goin' out there an' doin' what made me big in the firs' place--wrestlin'. An' if you're "Roman" Thomas Lawrence, that's the last thing you wanna hear. You don't wanna hear the fact that the *real* Lawrence Williams will be appearin' at the Air Canada Centre durin' your match. You don't wanna hear the fact that the wrestler who over five times within a span of two months two years ago is makin' his comeback from retirement against you. Naw.. You don't wanna hear that. [Dramatic pause.] But you are. You hearin' every word I'm sayin'. Are you shakin' in your boots? No. Are you callin' Johnson with news of your retirement? No. Are you plannin' to leave the country? No. But am I serious? Am I dead serious about the words I speak tonight? Yes. An' that's the truth, 'cause the truth always can, always has, and always _will_ hurt. [Fade.] BM: As I said, Lawrence had quite a bit to get off his mind there. He'll be squaring off against another Lawrence, Thomas Lawrence in what should be an amazing match! Speaking of amazing matches, Devon Case and James Pillow should definitely have one of those! Two of the most talented wrestlers around will square off, and let's hear what James has to say about that match. _________________________________________________ | ___| | | |___ | | |_ | "Simply The Best" | _| | | _|LASH | James Pillow | DRAWRO|_ | |_|_______|___________________________|_________|_| [The camera opens up to Outside on the city street. The camera slowly pans around as cars zoom by on the road. The sound of a siren in the distance grows louder with each passing second until a squad car appears in the distance, quickly getting close. It then turns down the road as the camera pans back to show a sidewalk below and a line of stores. The camera slowly moves closer to one of the building and then pans upwards to the sign above the entranceway as the siren dies out and everything is silent with the exception of the occasional sound of a car zooming by. This is broken as we hear the thumping sound of boots on concrete,"Thump, thump.." The sound stops as the camera pans down as we see the footsteps belong to none other than James Pillow.] [Pillow is garbed in a pair of black sweatpants, and an unbuttoned Boston Red Sox jacket. Under the jack he has on a plain, tight white T-shirt. James also has on a pair of Black Timberland boots and a pair of black oakley sunglasses. He glances down and runs a hand through his hair before he begins to speak..] JAMES "SIMPLY THE BEST" PILLOW: "The Devious One"... [James merely grins..] JP: Just another youngster trying to see if he can measure up. Well I got news for ya kiddo.. [James tilts his head slightly, the sun gleaming on his sunglasses..] JP: You're still a little to short to come on this ride... [He lifts his head back up and chuckles..] JP: Case, I know all about ya, ace. I've seen you grow from a naive rookie to the <smirks> "Devious" individual you are today. You've improved quite a bit, and have garnished quite a name for yourself. But I have news for ya, Sport.. [James takes a step forward stepping off the sidewalk, he then stops..] JP: Everytime I step into the ring the measuring stick just becomes that much higher and come the 1st I have a feeling you're gonna come up just a tad bit.. [James holds his hand up, his thumb and his index finger held slightly apart..] JP: Short. . [James starts to walk down the side of the road..] JP: Ya know kiddo I'm not impressed by many... [James stops and takes his sunglasses off, squinting at the light from the sun..] JP: You're on the long list of those who don't impress me. Ya see they're are two kinds of people in this world. Those who get stomped.. [James puts his sunglasses back on and smirks..] JP: And those who do the Stomping. .And on November 1st I'm gonna turn the "Devious" one into just another one of my bitches. 'Cause ya see kiddo, you may be one of the hottest young stars around but you're still only second best when compared to.. [James tips his sunglasss and cocks his left eyebrow slightly and chuckles.] JP: Simply The Best... [James turns away once again and continues to walk as the camera watches as the scene slowly fades to black..] BM: We'll definitely find out if James really IS the best on Monday night when he steps in with the Devious one. B.U.M will also be making their first appearance as a tag team. The unlikely duo of "Homeless" Joe and Larry will be trying to get past Ryan Rumble and Alex Extreme, who we heard from earlier. Let's see what's going on in the lives of B.U.M... _________________________________________________ | ___| | | |___ | | |_ | B.U.M | _| | | _|LASH | | DRAWRO|_ | |_|_______|___________________________|_________|_| (The following is entitled "The Beginning" - #1) [The screen is black. A sound clip of a crowd chanting "Homeless Joe" begins to play quietly. The chanting crescendos..] Crowd: HOMELESS JOE! HOMELESS JOE! HOMELESS JOE! [there is a flash. The light slowly dissolves, revealing a video clip from "Homeless" Joe's final in-ring. He is waiving to the crowd and blowing a kiss. The chanting decreases in volumes, allowing us to hear a voice over of the Bummiest Bum himself..] VO: "August 2nd, 1999 I walked out of the GSWP arena and out of wrestling period..." [With a tear in his eye, Joe exits the arena and the clip fades out. The chants gradually fade out..] VO: "Wrestling had caused me to forget who Homeless Joe was. My desire for fame is what brought my career crashing down." [The clip of "Homeless" Joe and Mo 'Diamond' Jackson's break up is shown. Joe punches Mo in the face and storms out of the ring. The clip fades out] VO: "After Mo and I chose to go our separate ways, things went down hill...I became a 'has been.' That's when I knew I needed a break.." [There is a flash, unveiling an aerial view of beautiful New York City. The camera fades into Grove Place Alley] VO: "Functioning as a depository for the garbage of both Fulton Street and Livingston Street in New York's busy downtown shopping district, Grove Place, a dead end on equally obscure Hanover Place, is a candidate for the worst street in New York City, if not the entire US. This is my home..." [the camera pans around. There are newspapers, cans, and even a wrecked car lying in that alley.] VO: "It's not much to look at, I know...but this is where I learned the art of wrestling. These are my roots...I decided to start at the beginning again. You see, every weekend the bums of New York get together for a fight night...just a little thing we started doin' back when I was first starting out as a bum. Our weekly fight night is what helped me become the wrestler I am today" [The camera fades into an alley where Joe is fighting some unknown homeless guy. Joe nails him with a New York Style Knuckle Sandwich, causing him cry out in pain. Joe pins the guy and wins.] Joe: "Alright! Who's next?!" [a giant hobo steps out of the crowd of bums. Joe smiles...] J: "LARRY!" VO: "It was Larry, my old dumpster-mate...I hadn't seen him in years. What blew my mind most about him is how BIG he had gotten...I mean, he was always tall, but now... He had the body of a monster..." [Larry has no emotion across his face. He's not angry, not happy, just...well...blank] VO: "Well, apparently Larry wanted a match for old time's sake...so we fought...for what seemed like hours.." [Several clips of Joe getting smacked around by Larry] VO: "...and in the end, I was the one with my shoulders down. I couldn't believe it. I'd always beaten Larry back in the day..." [Larry extends his hand to Joe, who is lying on the ground. Joe accepts...] VO: "While shaking the hand of my old friend, I knew I had come across something big...and I don't just mean Larry. It was a perfect plan, me and Larry...together...the possibilities were infinite..." [the screen fades to black as they shake hands.] VO: "On that day, B.U.M was born..." [The scene fades out] BM: They certainly were born, and they'll be seen LIVE in action on Monday. Now, let's get to the competitors in the 10-man Gauntlet match. First up, we'll hear from a man who does most of his talking in the ring. Here is Adrian Blades! _________________________________________________ | ___| | | |___ | | |_ | Adrian | _| | | _|LASH | Blades | DRAWRO|_ | |_|_______|___________________________|_________|_| [As the scene opens up we find ourselves in what appears to be a insignificant, bland office, the quality of the taping is not that good...but things can clearly be made out, we see the new signee of the NWP, current BACW superstar... Adrian Blades. For some people that simple name might ring a bell, for others he might just be another name because they have not heard of him, his long dark hair is combed backwards, tied underneath the baseball cap over his head sporting the logo of the Red wings, just a plain baseball cap.He adjusts the cap a bit and awaits the signal that will tell him when to start talking, waiting for the chance to give out his very first interview or 'promo' if you will. In the background, even though the camera-man doesn't really focus that sharply on it, we can see a few photo's hanging, a few from when he became regional collegial amateur wrestling champion, a few pictures from him with managers that have guided his career over the past few years, and a few closeups of himself with a celebrity or two, no really big names however. Blades straightens himself up a bit and we can see he is wearing the "Blades, Even The Name is Extreme" t-shirt...something that no doubt will be a hot commodity in due time around the NWP merchandise stalls whenever a show hits a town, he takes off his cap once again and brushes his right hand through his dark hair, straightening it out a bit and cooling off his noggin before he puts his cap back on, he then is given a signal by a man we cannot see, and he begins to speak, starting his very first interview here in the NWP] AB: "Well I guess that this is a hello to everyone watching me right now. I am really not the greatest guy in the business at giving these 'introduction interviews' "Hi I am so and so and I plan to do this and that" because I do most of my talking inside of the squared circle, and not out of the squared circle. For all the people that know me, I am Adrian Blades, there is no need for a more detailed introduction, because the simple fact of the matter is this, the name Adrian Blades, will still strike fear and/or respect into the hearts of the people that have wrestled me, because those people know that regardless of what I do inside that squared circle, regardless of what the circumstances are and regardless of who opposes me, I go out there and I provide everyone with a damn good accounting of myself...that's the story of Adrian Blades for the people that know me. For the people that do not know me, again my name is Adrian Blades, and just like the people that know me will certify, in due time my name will be a word that you use frequently to give a description of a certain event or happening, and more then likely you will find my name when you look at your win/loss record, at the bottom of your loss record, because that is where it ends up all of the time. I stand six feet eight, and I weigh close to three hundred pounds, I have nothing more to prove in any of the federations where I have been before, I have already proven to everyone in every single federation that I have been a part of, that I am in the real deal, that I am not someone is content with taking a backseat to anyone, I am the number one guy in every single federation that I have ever been a part of, and this federation will be no different, regardless of what people might say". [He pauses a second, he says something to one of the guys standing behind the camera, he is speaking a rather quiet voice so it cannot be made out what he is saying, two seconds later we see an unfamiliar hand coming into the screen hanging Blades what appears to be a medium sized bottle of Mineral water, he smiles and tips his head to whomever just handed him the bottle and he continues to speak] AB: "I know that some people want credentials when you come into a federation as "the new guy" I also know that some people feel that you are nothing unless you have beaten them or unless you have wrestled in one of their matches, I have been in barbed wire matches in Japan, where I left after about a four month stay, I've destroyed the very best that the TWL can put on television...often destroying two men at the same time...and most recently I took a 25 feet leap from a tank through a table, just in order to become the Heavyweight champion in another federation, I have seen what extreme is, and I have taken everything the other federations could possibly throw at me..so now it is time for me to come into to the NWP and to start showing people what Adrian Blade is really made of" [Blades pauses, he takes a hold of the bottle of mineral water that was standing close to him and he twists off the blue plastic cap, he places the cap back onto the desk that is standing before him and takes a sip of the water, he takes three more sips before he continues to speak] AB: "But I also that a lot of you guys are going to look at me and say 'yeah well that's all fine and dandy what you did in the past, but what in the hell are you going to do in the NWP in order to draw some attention to yourself'..well allow me to explain to each and every single one of you. It would appear as though our federation leaders like special stipulations, and they would like to let people wait as long as possible to see who will become the NWP Heavyweight champion for the very first time, they decided to determine the three men who will be fighting for the Heavyweight title in the ladder match, by having three separate shows, and three separate fights, I am involved in the first one. Come November 1st there is going to be battle royal of sorts, and I am going to be involved along with a few other no doubt fantastic athletes, however there is one problem for all the other participants in the battle royal or whatever the NWP wants to call it, cause come November 1st, on a Monday, there is not going to be a fair fight, they are not going to get their shot at becoming the NWP Heavyweight champion...and there is just one reason why that is, and you are looking at him right now. [Blades grins for a second, he continues] AB: "Whether I am the first guy to enter the ring, or whether I am among the last to enter the ring, as soon as I plant my foot on the apron people are going to be flying over the top rope, and the last two guys are going to be pinned in the center of the ring, no sweat at all. [He grabs a hold of a piece of paper that was laying in front of him, he looks at the camera and he begins to list the people that will step in the ring with him come November 1st..] "Gory Oreo" Foster Cane "Doomsday" Vadden Duggert "Devastating" Damon Knight "Brutal" Brian Floyd "Divine" Dallas Greene "The Last Real Man" Doc Holliday "The Swedish Stalker" Shalatna Merski "Hot Stuff" Scott Richards Garrett Selcouth AB: "Gentlemen, there is not going to be a chance in hell that you win the shot to fight in the Three Way Dance come our first Pay Per View, so please, do not get your hopes up, because you will only be disappointed. It's fairly odd, that I know a whole lot of people in this federation, yet the people that I am stepping in the ring with are all new to me, with one exception "Devastating" Damon Knight...people if you do not believe me that I am indeed as good as I claim to be, just ask Damon, because Damon saw me rip through people in the TWL, the almost exact same federation as this one, he saw how I was only able to be defeated when people ganged up on me, he can tell you that Adrian Blades never suffered one loss cleanly, and he can tell you that I was indeed not only the BEST TWL Television champion ever, but that I was also the uncrowned Heavyweight champion, but this time I am not going to go uncrowned, because gentlemen, come November 1st, people are going to see a new and improved Adrian Blades, an Adrian Blades that is going straight to the finals of the tournament, there is just no other way..." [After these words Blades kind of waves to the camera, signaling for them to stop recording, he takes another sip from his water as the scene slowly fades to black] BM: So, it appears as if Adrian is pumped for Monday. Let's see who else is ready -- Shalatna Merski is up next...
