He is "The Ladies Man" Vince Taylor and he'll be going up against "Delicious" Dave Davidson this very Monday. _________________________________________________ | ___| | | |___ | | |_ | "The Ladies Man" | _| | | _|LASH | Vince Taylor | DRAWRO|_ | |_|_______|___________________________|_________|_| [The scene fades into an old and grungy looking gym. The walls are tattered and stained brown, and its obvious that this gym has seen its share of fighters. In the center of the room is a wrestling ring. All around the ring are brown punching bags that have been worn in by being hit with punch after punch. This is the kind of place that guys who want to get a real workout come to. The men that don't find the need to use the high-tech equipment of today, but instead go about it the good old fashion way. One of these men is off in a corner jumping rope on his own. The camera approaches and you see that this man is Vince Taylor. Vince is wearing a simple pair of black mesh shorts and a tight fitting t-shirt. He is sweating profusely and it's obvious that he's been working out for quite a while. Vince acknowledges the camera and speaks, continueing to skip rope.] VT: Hold on one second for me, would 'ya. [Vince starts to skip the rope at a quick pace, and then comes to an abrupt stop. He walks over to the old ring and sits down on the apron before continueing.] VT: I just couldn't resist. I told myself and the world that I was done, out of the game. But like so many others, as soon as I got out of wrestling...I got sucked right back in. While I was sitting around, watching the sport from the difference, I got to look at what was going on. What did I see, you ask? Well I saw the true state of wrestling, and it wasn't a pretty site. I saw men who came out and wrestled for fame, who wrestled 'cause they were forced to, and yes I admit I saw guy wrestling for money and women. And I don't know why fans continue to pay their own money to come and watch these people. The wrestlers aren't out there for them, there out there for themselves, and themselves only. [Vince smiles oddly at the camera.] VT: But guess who had a change of heart? Yours truly. I was sick of being known as the kid who had the talent but didn't have the drive. Or the kid that could be great if he cared what happened in his matches. I was sick and tired of hearing that I was wasted talent. But for once in my life I did something about it. I didn't piss and moan, instead I took my problems to one place...this gym. I've known for a long time that this was my knack, my true talent. Wrestling. Not fighting, not brawling, but true wrestling. And not until recently have I put forth the effort to become great. And now the NWP gets to see just how great I can become, and that the sky is my limit. And the first man to get a first hand account of this will be "Delicious" Dave Davidson. ["The Ladies Man" pauses and shakes his head.] VT: I gotta be honest when I tell you that I've never even heard of this guy. That I don't know how good he will really be in the ring. But I also gotta tell 'ya that I really don't care how good he is in the ring, 'cause no matter how you are, I'm still gonna come out and give my all just to pick up a win over you. I'm not gonna walk in here to the NWP and expect to people to lay down for me and likewise, don't anybody here think that I'm gonna lay down for them. So Davidson, regardless of what you think is night and come into the ring, and do what I do best, there is no way in hell that you can compete with me. Some people would call that being cocky, but if you can back it up then you can be cocky. And when the time for talk is over with come Monday, are you gonna be able to back it up Dave? I know for sure that I'll be able to. "The Ladies Man" is back in his element. I'm back to what I love, and there is no animal, beast, or person, especially "Delicious" Dave Davidson, that will stop me from rising to the top this time. Dave, you might as well check your aspirations of making a name for yourself by beating me at the door, because they'll never happen. I'm so determined, that there's only one possible outcome of this match. That outcome is me walking back to the locker room with one more win under my belt, a lot more fans behind me, and two more beautiful women on my arms. [Vince gets up from the ring apron, and starts to walk out of the gym as the scene fades to black.] BM: Well, Vince certainly seems prepared for his match against Davidson. We'll hear from Dave in a bit, but first here's one of the stranger characters in Northern Wrestling Promotions. He may be stuck in a little bit of a time warp, but here he is -- "Roman" Thomas Lawrence! _________________________________________________ | ___| | | |___ | | |_ | "Roman" | _| | | _|LASH | Thomas Lawrence | DRAWRO|_ | |_|_______|___________________________|_________|_| [Scene fades into a dimly lite room. Tables, short and small, fill the room. Every last table is full, with people nicely dressed. Some in suit and ties, and others in nice pants and shirts. Continuously, we see people moving around - waiters and waitresses as well. Now, we finally get the picture - we're in a restaurant. Suddenly, "Roman" Thomas Lawrence walks into the picture, with an attractive woman following close behind, as well as a waiter. Lawrence is dressed nicely. He is wearing a sand pair of Dockers and a hunter green button down shirt, all looking very nice. The woman also wears a pair of Dockers, khaki, and a red and white shirt. As they enter the picture, the waiter seats them at a small table for two. Along with seating them, he fills their glasses with water and asks them what they would like to drink. As they answer, the waiter walks off.] TL: My favorite restaurant, I must say, but their services are lacking poorly. Can't these people get some decent waitresses and waiters? For God's sake, they charge the highest prices in town, and they don't pay a thing for rent. We're in an old, shutdown factory! Melanie, you deserve better than that... ML: Cool down honey. To you, I deserve better, but to all of these other people in here, I'm just another person. I'm sure these people hire the cheapest people they can find so they can bring in more money. Don't worry about it hon. TL: Yeah, right now you're a nobody, but after Monday and Northern Exposure, I won't be a nobody anymore. People all across the north, and even people down here in the south will know that I _AM_ the greatest person to ever walk the face of this earth and they will recognize me as the greatest. ML: Baby, lets just get off the subject of being nobodies and eat. Did you bring me to The Spaghetti Warehouse to pay foty dollars for spaghetti and be mad, or to be happy and pay forty dollars? [Melanie flashes a smile at Thomas.] TL: I guess you're right, but you know I'm going to have to talk about my upcoming match and this illiterate bas- ML: Yes, go ahead, and watch your language... TL: OK, suits me... ML: Thank you. TL: Well, as the fans know, I am facing some illiterate _MAN_ by the name of Lawrence Williams. At first glance, it's a normal name, and as a matter of fact, his first name is my last. But when you see the man in action, you totally change your outlook on the man. I mean, how many people do you see that call themself "El Dub?" What kind of crap is that? Yeah, I could walk around calling myself "Tee El" but I don't, and for one simple reason. If I walked around doing that, what kind of impression would you get of me? You'd think I was some freaking redneck straight from the woods. But I'm not a [BLEEP!]'ing redneck! ML: THOMAS WILSON LAWRENCE! TL: Excuse me... ML: Watch it... I sleep with you, remember?! [Mel flashes another smile. This time, stern.] TL: Like I said, I'm not a redneck! Sure, maybe I went to Georgia Tech, but I'm far from a redneck. If you saw a redneck in Ancient Roman times, he would be immediately executed, and I would not want to be that person. Now Williams, I would like to see what accomplishments you have made in this sport. I'm sure very few... if _ANY_. So now you ask me about myself? Well, to tell you the truth, I haven't accomplished a single thing, but you know what? I've never wrestled before. Unlike your ancient old ass... excuse me hon. [Mel looks at Lawrence.] TL: You know, I remember a certain phrase very well. It goes a little something like this: You can't teach an old dog new tricks. To make that very complicated phrase of meaning to you, it means that you're too old to be wrestling. Do you _REALLY_ want to step into the ring with a true superstar this Monday, or do you not? Of course you don't. Hell, no one on this earth wants to. If you have studied your history, you should know that the Romans were superior to all beings back during late BC and early AD times. Some people say that now, they aren't, but it looks as if we will come to find out this Monday, when a true superstar steps into the ring against a noble redneck. [A man starts to walk by Lawrence, but suddenly stops and turns around.] MAN: Are you Thomas Lawrence? TL: Yes, I am. And who are you? MAN: My name is Brian Chadwick. I live in New York, down here on business. I've seen some commercials on television about Northern Wrestling Promotions, and you compete their, right? TL: You could say so. Compete just isn't the right word to use. I was thinking more along the lines of dominate, desicrate, and rule. As for you, go back up north! We don't need anymore of you yankees down here! BC: Exvuse me? TL: You heard me, now leave. I may live in North Dakota sometimes, but I don't know _ANYONE_ who considers North Dakotans yankees. Yankees are people from New England... now go home! BC: I will. Sorry to bother you... [As the man walks off, we hear him say, "What an asshole." Immediately, Lawrence jumps up and turns around.] ML: Calm down.. if he lives up north, he'll be able to see what you can do in the ring this Monday. Don't show him now... [Lawrence straightens his tie and takes one last look at the man. He sits down with a face full of anger.] TL: These people just do not know what they're missing. They have the chance to eat with a soon-to-be legend, and they don't even acknowledge who I am, but that's alright, because then everyone will finally find out how _STUPID_ they really are. And for those who do recognize me, good for you. You are amongst the first to talk to and recognize the man that will be known for making NWP and wrestling as a whole. I just hope that someday, NWP will move south, where are of the true wrestling fans are, so some decent people can see me. ML: There isn't a date planned where you will come south already? I thought that you said the would come south and that the date was already planned for the first trip. TL: No, there is no date planned, but I'm sure we will come south sometime. If we don't, it looks like I may have to become a tyrant and force NWP to go south. I'm sure our good friend Marcus Johnson will take us south, because I'm sure he doesn't want his wrestling federation to go into tyranny... does he? Of course, he may want NWP to be in the hands of a superstar, because it would do this federation a lot of good I believe. I _KNOW_ what change I would make right at the beginning - it would no longer be Northern Wrestling Promotions, but Southern Wrestling Promotions. But, I'm not going to blab on and on about what _I_ would do. Instead, the _GOD_ of wrestling will show what he has this coming Monday in Toronto, Canada. Not my favorite place to go, but it'll do for a start. Lawrence Williams, you _WILL BE_ conquered. Eros is dead... Mars reigns... [On that, the waiter walks back into the picture with thier drinks and appetizers. Lawrence, with a stern look in his eyes, begins to eat and asks the waiter for a beer. Scene fades to black as Lawrence and his wife begin to eat.] BM: Prestige certainly seems like an important factor in the life of Thomas Lawrence. This Monday will really be his chance to gain some of that prestige. Now, onto one of the most promising wrestlers in the NWP. Dave Davidson will be taking on Vince Taylor, who we heard from earlier tonight. And let's find out what the Delicious one is up to. _________________________________________________ | ___| | | |___ | | |_ | "Delicious" | _| | | _|LASH | Dave Davidson | DRAWRO|_ | |_|_______|___________________________|_________|_| [The scene opens in a small apartment. The carpet is an off color white, like a beige, but it doesn't appear to have been bought at such a shade. There is a small tv sitting on a brown fake wood tv stand it is on and is tuned in to what appears to be Nick At Nite. The walls are a shade of beige a little lighter than the carpet. Sitting a couple feet from the tv is a folding chair, on which Delicious Dave Davidson is sitting. He's wearing a pair of navy Abercrombie jogging pants with a gray stripe down the sides and a white wife beater along with a pair of navy Oakleys. His hair is tied back into a ponytail and he has an expression on his face which makes him look a little upset.] Dave: Here I am, sitting in my apartment watching Nick At......... [Dave catches himself before he says it, picks up the remote and flips the channel.] Dave: Well, I'm watching ESPN, the damn cameraboy must've changed the channel or something. But I'm not here to talk about how great a channel ESPN is, yes I could go on for days about their award winning Outside The Lines program, or there daily SportsCenter, or even their tremendous job of covering not one, but many sports. However, today I'm going to focus on the profound impact I've had on the lives of millions, much like ESPN, but to a far greater extent. I'm here, of course, to talk about my wrestling career. [Dave picks up a can of Diet Coke sitting next to him and takes a drink.] DD: Ahhhh, the wonders of Diet Coke. I don't know how they make it so good, and I'm not gonna ask any questions. But now onto my NWP wrasslin debut, which will obviously occur in their big premier show. The schedule makers must've been a bit confused and forgot to put me in the main event, but hey we all make mistakes, we're only human. Well, all of us except for those aliens who come to earth and suck out your brain to install a tiny pod in there where they can live and walk around among humans gathering all our secrets. But that's a tale for a whole different day and place, right now I'd like to discuss the NWP for a minute or two. Now many fans, ok none, are asking me, "Dave, why the NWP?" Well my naive fans, it's quite obvious, I need the money. Ya see I decided to take a little risk and invest every penny I had on a little invention that was going to revolutionize the world as we know it, I'll go slowly here, it was called a "Steam Engine." It was, basically, from what I understand an engine, that runs on steam. Well, this door to door salesman made this seem like the greatest invention since the industrial revolution so I emptied the ol' bank account and gave him what I had. However, a couple months ago I got a call, and I was informed that the test "Steam Engines" were a miserable failure and they were sorry, but they had lost all my money. So I said thanks for telling me, and hung up the phone a little disappointed, I just hope they get those things to work some day, for the sake of every person alive. [Dave takes another sip of Diet Coke.] DD: Anyway, enough about how I got here, let me just explain some things for those two or three fans, or even wrestlers, who forgot who I am or somehow were living in oblivion and never heard of me. You see, the Delicious One isn't simply the best, he isn't merely devastating, and he doesn't just claim to be exciting or hot stuff. I, Delicious Dave Davidson, am the single greatest athlete on the face of the planet, and I'm the greatest wrestler to ever lace up a pair of ring boots. Alright, actually my mom laces them for me, but I tie them! This Monday, one night after Halloween, The Delicious One will get to show Vinny Taylor that first hand. [Dave clears his throat.] DD: Vinny Taylor, self proclaimed Ladies Man. Ya know what Vinny, the last time you were with ladies was when you accidentally went to the bowling ally on ladies night because you wanted to bowl, but then figured out it was ladies night and they wouldn't let you bowl, so you had to go home, because you couldn't bowl, that's the last time YOU were with ladies. I mean c'mon, it's not like that ever happened to me. Anyway, Vinny, Monday night I will show every NWP fan exactly who and what Delicious Dave Davidson is, a mega-talent, with a ton of heart, pure innate ability, and the best looking face in all of sports today. [Dave reaches for his Diet Coke lifts it up and then looks a little sad.] DD: DAMN! This was my last Diet Coke, what am I gonna do? Cameraman: Why don't you just go to the store and buy more Diet Coke. DD: That's why I'm paying you. You're not just a great cameraman you're also a genius! With novel idea's like that why are you still just a cameraman? CM: [sarcastically] Boy Dave, I don't know. DD: Well enough of you hogging my limelight, I gotta finish up this interview and head for the store to get some Diet Coke. Let me just leave all the fans and "competition" with these words. I've come to NWP not just to wrestle, or for money, but to bring wrestling back to glory. The glory of all of our heroes. Today wrestlers try to make their name by cracking their skull in half and hitting an opponent with their brain, I have never done such a thing. I'm not here to please those of you who are only watching to see a pair of idiots damn near kill themselves. Delicious Dave truly is a hero in every sense of the word and with my return I can promise you the wrestling world will never be the same, because I'm Delicious Dave, and I am, and always have been, The One And Only Rave! [After this Dave proceeds to get up and run to the door exiting the apartment and run out muttering something about needing more Coke.] BM: As I said, Dave is a very promising athlete, and the match between him and "The Ladies Man" will certainly prove to be an interesting one.
