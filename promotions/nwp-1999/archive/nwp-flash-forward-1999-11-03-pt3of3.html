Flash Forward

3 November 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
_________________________________________________ | ___| | | |___ | | |_ | "The Swedish Stalker" | _| | | _|LASH | Shalatna Merski | DRAWRO|_ | |_|_______|___________________________|_________|_| [We are somewhere in Sweeden. The streets are dark, and the air is crisp. No one is on the streets. The silence seems somewhat odd. Like, something is destened to happen. The cameraman mutters a curse word. He continues to walk. He is now near a forest. The leaves are turning from green to all different colors. A street light casts an eerie light below. In the shadows from the light we can see the outline of a figure. He looks to be around six, six. He has a cigerate in his mouth. He sees the camera. He says something. We can notice a think Sweedish accent. He lets out an evil laugh. Now the man steps forward. He has on a full Army suit. He slowly, steadly looks into the camera. He drops his cigerate, and stomps it. He looks into the camera. His icey blue eyes locked on the camera nice. He then begins to speak.] M: "Tis' a night like any other Sweedish October nights, ya? Nothing feels better, no?" [The man has not taken his eyes off the camera.] M: "I just had a cigerate. Nothing feels better then a drag of a smoke, on a cool night. I feel my blood warming up with each puff. My skin tingles inside. My pulse races. I feel a high. Just like I did as a sniper... Untill.. Untill that dreadful day." [The man tries to fight back the tears.] M: "I missed the enemy, I hit my own man. They thought it was a mistake. Oh yes, it was an afull mistake. One I paid dearly for. I was ridiculed, I was forced to leave my country. Yet, now I am back. Back where they all said I did it on purpose. They messed with Shalatna Merski and now they'll all feel the backlash of the mistake. For I am now rejuvinated. But one thing I forgot to say, I have snapped!" [The man kicks a nearby bush.] SM: "I am sick of this torment I have felt! Tis' not fair! I was not at fault, it was his fault. He wasn't suppose to be there. Yet he was. So now he is at fault, no? Oh well. Tis' now time to put that torment behind me, and concintrate on this wrestling federation, the National Wrestling Promotion. Tis' my first fed and I will showcase my military skills on all of thee' asses of the En Double Pee wrestlers." [Shalatna lights up a new cig.] SM: "My time to leave has come, so I will. But you musen't forget who I am, because thee will be holding the name of En Double Pee All Star. Now leave me now! NOW DAMN YOU!" [Shalatna kicks the bench as the camera fades to black.] BM: Well, someone certainly appears to have an attitude problem. On that note, we'll hear from another participant in the main event on Spotlight. Here's the Scotsman, Garrett Selcouth! _________________________________________________ | ___| | | |___ | | |_ | Garrett | _| | | _|LASH | Selcouth | DRAWRO|_ | |_|_______|___________________________|_________|_| [Scene: A private gym located inside the Jaeger household, in North Berwick, Scotland. Flashing onto the screen simultaneously are numerous small video clips of a new face, Garrett Selcouth either lifting weights, on a mat sparring, hitting a speed bag, or jumping rope. The simultaneous video clips stop as our screen fades straight into the relaxed yet concerned face of Garrett Selcouth. Along his side, talking with one of the trainers is his manager, Patrick Jaeger. Garrett is attired in only skin tight white spandex shorts, along with black aerobic shoes, and his hands taped for protection. Patrick on the other hand is attired in his formal black George D. Anthony tuxedo, black penny-loafers, and trademark "Guerrilla Heartthrob" tie. A white towel is lifted up from the bench which Garrett sits on to wipe the dripping beads of sweat from his forehead. The always talkative Patrick Jaeger turns around towards Garrett and nods. The person who Patrick was talking to then nods back, just as he walks off. Patrick now takes a seat next to Garrett and begins to speak.] PJ: Are ye nervous th're champ? Ah can see 't en yer eyes. Yer worri'd that ye aint gonna' repr'sent what ye f'ght fer. Well Garrett, get away wit' ye an' listen te me. Yer one o' th' finest athletes wh'ch ah seen come out o' these p'rts. And don't let 'em blasted bloody veter'n's tell ye differ'nt. Ye hear me? GS: Fer th' first t'me 'n my life Patrick, 'm nerv'us. Ah been trainin' fer weeks now fer th's comp'tition and ah'm still treblin' 'n my knees and breathin' heavy und'r my bre'the. Ah know that ev'ry new comer 'sually feels th' same way but, why me? PJ: 'Cos. it's just nat're Garrett. 'T'll all be ov'r with in do time my fr'end. In due time. [The towel which Garrett used to wipe the sweat off his head is brought up one more time and swept across his fore-head for a second time, clearing off all moisture and wetness. Just as Garrett does this, he stands up from his seat and gets back to working out. Patrick though continues to sit and just ponders at the intense and thurough body work out and devotion Garrett has for what he does.] PJ: That could've been me. Ah could've been th' one trainin' te become one o' th' fines' wres'lers in th' world t'day. Ah could've been th' lad, th' lad preparin' fer a chance te become th' Gre't North'rn Champion of th' North Wrestlin' Promotion. If ah had only been prepared bef'rehand, I woulda' been th' one right now. But, 'm not. And ah will not let th' same hap'en te Garrett. Ah will not let 'im fail in his tasks, and ah will not let 'im be defe'ted by mere junk'es. He 's a lad o' great sign'ficance, and ah, will be th' one to brin' that sign'ficance out o' him. [The scene cuts away from Patrick and fades to black.] BM: I hope you caught most of that, because I sure as hell didn't. Anyhow, let's see how Mr. "Last Real Man" is feeling and how he'll approach this match. _________________________________________________ | ___| | | |___ | | |_ | "The Last Real Man" | _| | | _|LASH | Doc Holliday | DRAWRO|_ | |_|_______|___________________________|_________|_| [SCENE: An empty arena... specifically, the Air Canada Centre in Toronto, Ontario, Canada. There's no wrestling ring set up here, as the Leafs are in town... the place is set up for hockey. A man walks along the boards, in the stands. A man who wears the regalia of an Old West gambler... a fine black jacket, a silk ruffled white undershirt, billowing out from his sleeves, collar, and chest, and fancy black pants. A man walking with a limp, using a mahogany, hand-carved cane to support himself. A man with long, wavy, sandy-brown hair, flowing over his shoulders and down his back, from underneath a black western-style broad-rimmed hat. A man with an extremely handsome, clean-shaven face... angular as if an aristocrat, yet somehow, there is a quality there that makes this face seem disingenuous... a look enhanced by a single shiny gold tooth along the bottom row of his teeth. His name is Doc Holliday, and if one didn't know that before, they know now, as his name is printed in a graphic along the bottom of the screen. Holliday sits down in a front row seat, kicks his feet up onto the boards, and begins to speak in his baritone voice, HEAVILY accented in a Southwestern style.] Doc Holliday: Th' name's Holliday... yeah, Doc Holliday. If ya don' know me yet, yer sure ta know me soon. On account of ah'm th' Last Real Man onna Planet Earth. [Holliday laughs, a sneering, derisive sound that echoes across the empty Air Canada Centre.] DH: Lemme tell it to ya lak this. Times were, a hun'nerd fifty years ago, thet men was men an' womenfolk knew they place. A time whar th' law took a back seat ta keepin' th' society intact. Ever'body had a role, a niche, a part in th' overall society. Thar was good guys an' bad guys, heroes an' villians, bit playahs an' major playahs... an' all wuz right with th' worl'. Real men blazed trails, explored territory thet weren't nevah explored b'fore. Real men came an' went as they pleased, an' pursued life any way they chose ta pursue it. A good time. But in this heah censh-ry, things done went an' got all wrong. Alla bleedin' hearts out thar cried an' whined thet they wuz gittin' treated wrong. Worse yet, some jackasses in Washin'ton an' Ottawa ack-shally LISTENED, an' they done put th' clamps down on ever'thang. Alla litigation, an' laws, an' red tape... purty soon, people wuz lettin' governments run they lives fer 'em. An' so, alla real men thet walked th' earth, did as they pleased, fought our wars an' made our society great... died out one by one. All but me. [Doc stands up, puffs his chest out, and nods to himself.... an AGGRIVATINGLY cocky, self-assured nod, as if to indicate that he knows better than everyone else.] DH: Yup, it's hard livin' in this hell-hole they call nineteen ninety-nine. Especially when yer a real man lak me. But yer not, so don' worry. It's hard seein' how men done rolled ovah an' let womenfolk take ovah they lives. Them addle-brained females is th' ruination of us all, actin' lak they's a equal to a man. But hell, ain't no REAL men lef' but me, so hell with it. It's hard seein' th' police walkin' round with a iron fist lak they run th' place. Times were a man could settle his issues with another man... if two men wan'ned ta fight, they did... if they wan'ned ta shoot it out, they did... an' if they wan'ned ta rob, steal, an' loot, then they could live thet life, an' take they chances. But NOW, they'll arrest ya fer crossin' a street inna wrong place. They'll take yer car away jus' fer enjoyin' a man's drink, then havin' ta drive somewhar. They tryin' ta make ever'body happy when they should know DAMN WELL it cain't be done. Let people live lak they wanna. An' times used ta be when th' bettah man won any contest. Especially th' contest o' life. Th' strong took as they pleased an' th' weak an' skittish folk were either protected by th' strong or weeded out. An' thet's th' way it should be. Whut th' HELL's th' damn point o' bein' bettah'n ever'body else an' excellin' in every field... if they jus' gonna cut corners so th' weak an' incompetent kin be on a even field with ya anyway! Dagnabbit, if they's one thing ah cain't stand, it's lookin' round an' seein' alla these weak an' helpless folk... an' people go outta they way ta help 'em move up ta be somethin' they ain't. It's one thang if'n yer ol' an' ya already lived a full life... it's yer due fer people ta help ya. But people thet're jus' natch-rally flawed an' useless, oughta STAY thet way! On account of REAL men were MEANT ta dominate. An' ever'body else should git in they place in line they BELONG ta! So... if yer th' Last Real Man On Earth... livin' in this day an' age, whut do ya do? Do ya try an' run th' worl'? No... alla them crooked politicians ain't got no heart nor backbone. If ya got legs ta walk on ya don' slither with th' worms. It's they fault we ended up lak this anyhow. Do ya go adventurin' across th' globe, darin' any challenge nature kin give ya? No... damn computers made it so they wasn't no unexplored places left fer a man ta conquer. Nobody's impressed no mo' by wil' terry-tory, on account o' they done seen it all by satellite pictures an' ovah tee vee. Do ya go out, an' show th' worl' jus' whut they's missin'? Hell, yes. An' thet's whut ah'm heah ta do. [Holliday stands up again, and starts heading down the boards once more, towards the benches.] DH: In this heah NWP, ya got all kinds. People from ever'whar, thet claim ever'thang. Ya gots bums an' knights an' extreme ones an' ladies men an' confident people an' children o' fire an' romans an' so on an' so forth... but they all add up ta nothin'. On account o' they all's jus' a big collective... a group o' bodies with th' same mind. They let th' establishment do alla they thinkin' fer 'em. Not lak a Real Man, thet kin think fer hisself. But y'all gonna see whut ah mean this week. [Doc's arrived at the benches now, and he looks over the ice.] DH: Ah came heah ta this heah arena ta scope th' place out. An' ah done saw thet they froze th' place up. Ice ever'where. Damn Canucks. Don' even know how ta maintain a proper arena. Sure ah knows it's hockey season... hell, ah saw Tie Domi onna way in, an he begged me not ta bust 'im one. So ah didn't, hell, ah knows how ta have mercy on chumps thet hafta associate in a cream-puff so-called sport jus' so they look tough in compary-sin. But hell, DOC HOLLIDAY is in town! Ya'd think they couldn't melt this damn ice fast enough ta git me in heah ta sell tickets fer th' REAL toughest sport on Earth! Alla these heah dang Canady-ins is alla same anyway. All 'bout they piss-thin beer, this sorry-ass hockey, an' whinin' 'bout how terrible th' USA is. Hell, chumps, we KNOW how terrible th' USA is, an' yer worse so shaddap. When people use th' phrase "hell on Earth", ah knows they been ta Canada. So this comin' Monday, ah'm'a show y'all ass-backward SCRUBS up heah in this sorry land whut a Real Man looks lak. On account o' Real Men used ta walk th' Earth... but they ain't NEVAH been one in Canada! Up till th' day Doc Holliday decided ta come up heah an' give ya one night... one night ya could say a Real Man walked yer grounds. An' thet's all ah wants ta hear ya say. [Holliday reaches his foot out, and kicks the penalty box door shut.] DH: So don' go lookin' fer two minutes fer hookin'. Don' beg fer two minutes fer in'nerference. Don' cry cuz ah didn't git a five-minute major when ah whup yer ass. Yer in a Real Man's territory, punks. An' th' only thang ya got ta look forward to 'bout Monday, izza fact thet it'll be Tuesday soon. An' once yer away from Doc Holliday, ya kin celebrate. Ya lived through it... thet's more'n SOME men kin say. So celebrate th' day... ah know ah will... cuz... ...EVERY DAY is a HOLLIDAY! [Doc's trademark phrase echoes through the empty building... as Holliday nods his head to himself again, we fade out...] BM: No such thing as modesty when your name is Doc Holliday, that's for sure! Now, wrapping things up, here's another strange character -- he's the "Gory Oreo" Foster Crane! _________________________________________________ | ___| | | |___ | | |_ | "Gory Oreo" | _| | | _|LASH | Foster Crane | DRAWRO|_ | |_|_______|___________________________|_________|_| [Fade... fade... fade... fade into a shot of a man sitting on a chair in a dark room. All is silent except for the sound of each breath the man takes. There is a dim light hanging from the ceiling of the room, which forms a silouette around the man in the chair. We can see he is a well-sized man, who is leaning forward with his face in his hands. Suddenly the light in the room gets somewhat brighter, and we can make out some features of the man. He is wearing a white cut-off t-shirt, and a pair of blue jeans. On his shoulder we can see a tattoo of what looks to be a bloody oreo cookie. The man with the odd tattoo has short brown hair which is rather messy, but can not see his face because it is hidden in his hands. Speaking of his hands, the right one has a very distinct feature to it. Actually, it is missing one distinct feature. This man is missing his pinky finger. This man, he is... "Gory Oreo" Foster Crane.] "Gory Oreo" Foster Crane: Don't ever think that it won't happen to you... because it will. [Foster raises his face out from his hands, and peers towards the camera with his beady brown eyes.] FC: Have you ever had a dream in life? Something which you have strived for since you were born... something which you spent your entire life working on... something which you wanted to achieve more than anything in this world? I once had a dream. But it was crushed by a mere accident. A simple error which occurred in something totally unrelated to my dream. My dream was crushed by what some would call "the unthinkable". And listen when I tell you this... the unthinkable can happen. [Suddenly Foster throws his right hand up in the air. He does not look at his obscure hand, but rather stares into the camera with a look of rage in his eyes.] FC: This demented, horrid, awful thing is what cost me my dream. One freak accident while making supper at home crushed all my hopes and ambitions in highschool. And this was what caused me torment and anguish during the end of my highschool years... [He lowers his hand back to his lap, and stares at the camera with a cold look.] FC: They don't call me "Gory Oreo" because it sounds cool. No, you see a so-called "friend" thought it would be cool to shake my hand after suffering my mishap. I bled... it dripped... and it landed on that object. Thus the name I have had to this day. But you see, it is something that I am not offended by. It is the total opposite of this horrid thing that is at the end of my right arm. The name has that bit of irony, that bit of oddness that makes people stop and think. I'm sure right now you are thinking about it. You are wondering if there is more to the story. You want to know more. You NEED to know more... But you don't get to know more. [Foster smirks and then changes the subject of his talk.] FC: On November 1st, 1999 I enter a new dimension of the sport that has been my life. Northern Wrestling Promotions first card from the city of Toronto. The first card of the fed's existence. And the first time I get to unleash my terror on nine other men in quite a long time. Nine men which won't know what to expect, and will see the true meaning of the words pain, sorrow, and anguish. It is kinda sad how these men have worked their entire lives to get to a point where they will be someone, but will now have to deal with a large obstacle in their paths to achieving their dreams. The obstacle being me of course. [He shakes his head.] FC: I learned that it is pointless to put your life into achieving a goal, and the rest of NWP will soon learn that as well. No matter how hard you try, and how hard you work... something will pop up and stop you from getting what you want. And for the entire roster here in NWP, I am here to stop their hopes and dreams. Everybody wants to be a World Champion, or in this case the Greater North Champion... and everybody in that ring on November 1st is going to be trying to reach that point. Except for me. My job is to stop people from reaching their goals. My job is to crush their dreams. My job is not to win titles, it is to stop other from winning them. So I would like to send a little message out to those other nine men involved, and tell them this one simple thing.... You don't know me. You don't want to know me. And you will never know me. I don't care if your name is Damon Knight, Dallas Greene, George Bush, or Jesus Christ. I will torment you... I will hurt you... and I will destroy you. You will then think to yourself, "Where did this guy come from?" or "Why didn't I hear about this guy from before?". [Foster smiles and then leans forward.] Then you will come to the realization that I am something which you have never dealt with before. And then you will remember my words which I am about to speak... ...the unthinkable can happen. And it will! [Foster then puts his face back in his hands as the light in the room dims, and we go to the darkness we started in. Fade... fade... fade... fade back to black.] BM: And that's about all for tonight, folks! Until I see you again, have fun, and keep watching Northern Wrestling Promotions!
