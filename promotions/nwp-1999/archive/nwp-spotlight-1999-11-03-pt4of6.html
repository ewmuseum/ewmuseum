_______________________________________________________________________________ | \ | \ \ / / _ \ / _ \ \ / / | / | | \| |\ \ /\ / /| |_) | SINGLES MATCH | (_| |\ \ /\ / /| |/ | | |\ | \ V V / | __/ James Pillow vs. Devon Case \__ | \ V V / | /| | |_|_\_|__\_/\_/__|_|_________________________________________|_|__\_/\_/__|_/_|_| |_Written by:__[name withheld]______________________________________________________| [The camera cuts to ring where Bobby McCarville is ready to introduce the competitors.] BM: Ladies and Gentleman introducing first, Weighing in at 237 pounds. He hails from Newport, RI. Here he is, the man that is sometimes called "Es T Bee", but whatever way you put it he is "Simply The Best", here is JAMES PILLOW!!!!! [A blue strobe light blinks on to the right of the entrance ramp, a mere millisecond later a gold one blinks to life on the left side shinning through each other as the house lights dim slightly. The opening beats of "Limelight" by Rush kicks in as the strobe lights tilt so they are shinning straight into the air as gold fireworks fire off of the top of the entranceway shooting straight up and others fire from the bottom firing high into the arena at a diagonal angle. The camera then begins to move closer onto the entranceway at a side angle. The camera continues to zoom in on the curtain over the entranceway at the same angle, an arm then pushes through the curtain and slowly parts it as the crowd rises up to there feet. A heavy drum beat in "Limelight" hits as a barrage of pyrotechnics shoot off from all angles.] *WOOSH!* *WOOSH!* *WOOSH!* *WOOSH!* *POP! POP!* *BANG!!!* *POP! POP! POP!* *BOOM!!!!* [The figure then steps through the entranceway into the smoke exuded from the fireworks. The figure then places one foot out from the smoke and then the rest of him follows as we see whom the man is. He stands directly in front of the Smokey entranceway as it slowly dies down behind him, his head looking down at his feet, he slowly raises his head up as it is none other than James Pillow! His usual arrogant grin is perched upon his lips. As soon as his head is raised at an even level with the crowd and the ring down the rampway another fast paced burst of pyrotechnics shoot off behind him and he begins his walk towards the ring. James is garbed in a a pair of long black tights with "SIMPLY THE BEST" written up the right leg and "ES T BEE" up the left, as well as a black vest with "SIMPLY THE BEST" on the back in diamonds and gold chips and a pair of gold boots which go up to the middle of his calf and have "STB" written on the sides. James slides under the bottom rope and goes to the middle of the ring, the lights shine off the of his vest as another burst of fireworks shoot off from the ringpost. James slides under the bottom rope and goes to the middle of the ring, he grabs the mic from the ring announcer and takes his sunglasses off as the lights come up and the music fades away..] JAMES "SIMPLY THE BEST" PILLOW: I came to the arena today and the big bosses wanted me to get interviewed by Nick Demola here tonight.. [James rolls his eyes slightly and laughs..] JP: I about died in laughter at the thought of _Me_ being interviewd by Demola. The man that was...Well... [James hesitates for a second..] JP: My personal little...Bitch, for the lack of a better term over in the INWI. Where I basically walked over his now crippled ass on two... [James holds up two fingers..] JP: Seperate occasions, and if Nick wants to wheel his worthless carcass out here I'll gladly do it a third time and make sure his head is the only body part he'll be able to move! [HEEL POP!] JP: Yeah, yeah..cheer for me all ya want. I don't give a flying <Bleep> about what you hypocritical sons of bitches think! [The crowd's cheers quickly turn to jeers as James stands unshaken. He abruptly drops the mic, waiting for Devon Case.] BM: And his opponent... [The lights dim down in the arena as: "Seek and Destroy" by Metallica, begins to vibrate over the arena speakers. The lights slowly illuminate to a dark blue hue, that casts over the realm like a blanket. Fans in and around the aisle and entrance way file together, for a closer look. As they gather around, the music hits its hardcore edge to the explosions of deep blue fireworks] _BANG_ BANG_ BANG_!! [Then suddenly a familiar voice rings out over the arena speakers straining over the music.] VO: YOU KNOW WHO THE REAL MAN IS! [Cones with blue sparkles spray across the entranceway and various decks across the arena to even a large, and now familiar, HEEL POP! Suddenly the... "Devious One" ...appears from the back wearing a black t-shirt and midnight blue pants with the letters, "DDC" embroidered on the sides in black. He struts down to ringside, not acknowledging any fans , boo or cheer regardless. He climbs into the ring, pauses, and sits a top the nearest turnbuckle.] BM: Weighing in at 225 pounds and standing at an even 6 foot tall... he hails from Las Vegas, Nevada. Thhhheeeee ONE! Thhhheeeee ONLY... ... DEVON CASE!! TH: Well this should be one of the most technically sound matches we've seen all night. WJ: Tom, that's an understatement, this may very well be the best technical match in NWP history. TH: Wilbur this is the first show, there's only been a few matches up to this point. WJ: Shut up Tom, at least I don't think they're hot. DING! DING! DING! TH: There's the bell and this match is starting. WJ: Really Tom, I thought it meant the cookies were ready. TH: Pretty funny Wilbur. WJ: Yeah, I thought it was. [Case and Pillow start the match with a stare down in the center of the ring and a shoving match ensues. Case pushes Pillow, only to be pushed back by Case, who pushes Pillow, who pulls back with all his might and pushes Case to the ground.] TH: Ohhhh, Case is eating canvas. WJ: Tom, have you noticed their last names? Pillow and case, Pillow Case, it's like the things you put on pillows! TH: Good point Wilbur. [Case gets up and dusts himself off as the opponents give each other another cold stare, suddenly from out of nowhere Case flies halfway across the ring in a flash and nails Pillow with a flying cross body block. He immediately hops up as does Pillow and hits him with a snap suplex that is immediately followed by an elbow drop onto the downed Pillow.] TH: WHOOO, Case came out of nowhere with a flourish! WJ: I've always thought that kid had nothing but talent, he can't lose tonight! [Pillow hops up quickly after this only to be met by a wicked chop from Case that resounds through the building.] WJ: Owww, I could feel that from here. [Case keeps the momentum picking up Pillow and putting him down with a scoop slam. He follows this up with an attempt at a quick leg drop but Pillow quickly moves out of the way and hops up quickly. Immediately after hopping up, a grounded case sweeps Pillow's legs out from under him causing him to fall back to the ground. They both hop up immediately and just glare at each other.] TH: Wow! What a tremendous set of moves, these two men are putting on quite a show tonight. [The two men suddenly rush back at each other and lock up, case seemingly wins and locks onto Pillows waist from behind and goes for a German Suplex which he releases in hope that Pillow will land on his back, however Pillow does a full flip and lands square on his feet and, as Case hops up nails him with a drop kick to the back.] WJ: Ohh, Case did not see that one coming, I was for Pillow the whole time! TH: Of course you were Wilbur. [Pillow loses no time in picking up Case and hitting a picture perfect vertical suplex which he holds for about 5 seconds. Case lands with a ton of impact.] TH: Pillow is now firmly in control and wrestling an amazing match. [Pillow once again picks up Case but this time Case jabs a finger to the eye of Pillow and hits a drop kick to the left knee of Pillow.] TH: Another sudden turn around by Case, what a see saw battle this has been. [Case attacks the knee of Pillow who is on the ground stomping on it and kicking it repeatedly.] WJ: Case is vicious, he's got the killer instinct it takes and that's why I think he'll when this match!!! [Case picks Pillow up and flings up into the ropes and as Pillow comes back Case nails a drop toe hold into a single leg boston crab.] TH: He calls that move the Case Closed, and what a move it is! WJ: Right now Pillow is wishing he were born without a knee, that move hurts. [Pillow is on the ground in pain and crawling trying tio get to the ropes, which he does after nearly 20 seconds in the hold, Case however doesn't let go. 1! 2!! 3-- NO! Case releases the hold and takes a step back before taking the knee of Pillow and propping it on the bottom rope, he then runs into the opposite rope and with a full head of steam jumps, putting all of his weight on the knee of Pillow.] TH: Case is trying to decimate that knee and take away the amazing aerial arsenal of James Pillow. [Case turns his back on the downed Pillow and raises his arms to the fans who boo very loudly, at the same time Pillow hops up and is obviously trying to take the weight off of his left knee.] TH: Pillow is limping and Case is showboating. WJ: He's showboating with good reason he probably broke Pillow's knee, Pillow's done, what'd I tell you? [Case turns back to Pillow and grabs his head in the position to do a snap or vertical suplex, however as he goes for it Pillow blocks with his leg. Case tries again and is blocked again. Before Case can attempt a third time Pillow reverses it into a Northern Lights suplex.] TH: What an incredible reversal by Pillow, that took everything he had. [Pillow hobbles up and over to Case whom he picks up and nails him with a fallaway slam, as Case lies on his back Pillow limps over and stomps on Case repeatedly until Case rolls to the outside of the ring.] TH: Oh no, this could get ugly. WJ: Let's just hope it doesn't get as ugly as you Tom. TH: Yeah, good one Wilbur. [Pillow makes an immediate move and slingshots himself over the top rope and nails case with a cross body block that takes Case down hard.] TH: Oh, incredible move by Pillow. [Pillow hops back into the ring and seems to have injured himself even more.] 1! 2! 3! [Case struggles up to his feet.] 4! 5! 6! [Case crawls back up into the ring and is greeted by Pillow who picks him up only to receive a kick to the groin from Case.] WJ: Oh great move by Devon Case! TH: WHAT!? He kicked him in the, well you saw where he kicked him, that's not a great move. WJ: Speak for yourself Tom, that was a picture perfect move in my book. [Case, now with the advantage once again, grabs Pillow, picks him up, and powerbombs him to the ground, and immediately pins him. 1! 2!! 3... NO!!] TH: No! Pillow kicks out after that great move by Case! [Case looks a little surprised and picks up Pillow and gives him a German Suplex with a bridge into a pin. 1!! 2!! 3... NO!!!] TH: No! Pillow kicks out again! What amazing heart by Pillow! WJ: Shut up Tom, he's merely avoiding the inevitable. [Case is beginning to look a little frustrated and takes Pillow to whip him into the ropes, however after hitting the ropes Pillow's leg collapses and he falls down. Case seeing this starts laughing and, mocking Pillow, begins to limp around the ring.] WJ: HAHA look at that, he's making fun of Pillow right in his face and Pillow's over there on the ground crying. TH: You're a sick sick man Wilbur. [As Case hobbles around mocking Pillow, the latter suddenly hops up and at full speed runs at Case who is now near the ropes, and clotheslines Case clear out of the ring. Case lands hard on the ground and as he slowly gets up Pillow bounces off the far ropes comes back and flips over the top rope landing on Case at the ground.] TH: WHAT AN AMAZING MOVE! WJ: But? His knee? [Pillow picks up Case and throws him back in the ring, he then hops onto the apron and lands slingshots himself over the top and onto Case with a somersault senton splash. Pillow hops right up after this and climbs to the top rope where he waits on Devon Case, as soon as Case gets up Pillow nails him with a dropkick from the top rope.] TH: Pillow is now suddenly dominating this match, what an amazing turnaround! WJ: I told you Pillow would win this thing, C'mon Pillow! TH: Fans, I won't even comment. [Pillow then waits on Case to get up again and right as Case gets up Pillow goes for his STBKick but as he does Case grabs the ref and pulls him into the kick, and the ref falls in a heap.] TH: Oh my God, the ref is out! What a kick by Pillow, unfortunately he missed his mark. WJ: Genius move by Case, he's showing why I picked him to win this match TH: [sigh] [The crowd suddenly erupts as Nick Demola runs down to the ring and quickly hops up and in holding a crowbar which he swings at James Pillow who deftly avoids it falling to the crowd, Demola can't stop the swing and hits Case with a full swing, Case drops immediately.] TH: What has Demola done! He came down here to knock out Pillow over what he said earlier, but instead almost decapitated Case. WJ: I told ya Pillow was gonna win. [Demola stands a little shocked at what he just did, and as he does Pillow nails him with a perfectly place STBKick, as he does Demola falls down and rolls out of the ring. Pillow grabs the ref and shakes him, waking him up, he then picks up case nails the SimplyBest-Plex and the ref slowly begins a count. 1!! 2!! 3!?! 3!!!! DING! DING! DING!] TH: Yes, Pillow got him, Pillow gets the win! And believe me no one is more pissed off than Nick Demola who just helped a man he hates win this match. WJ: What'd I tell ya Tom, I knew Pillow would get this win. I just had this gut feeling. TH: Of course you did. The only reason he really won was because of that botched interference from Nick Demola. Nick came down to 'even the score' for the words Pillow said about him earlier, but ended up doing nothing but HELPING him! WJ: Who's coming out now? TH: Let's find out... [The lights in the arena go out, completely. Suddenly, white and blue spotlights begin to swirl all around the arena. While the blue and white spotlights swirl around the arena, strobe lights begin to flicker above the curtains. The opening of "F#!* ME, F#!* YOU" by Mase featuring Mysonne begins to play, the following is then seen over the jumbotron:] SUPERSTAR [Afther the text is shown over the jumbotron, smokes begins to rise from under the steel grating ramp. The fans rise to there feet in anticipation to who is going to make their way out from the back. As the break from "F#!* ME, F#!* YOU" begins to play, the following appears on the jumbotron:] SPOTLIGHT [The strobe lights continue to flicker, slowing down, then speeding up, the blue and white lights continue to swirl around the arena, slowing down, then speeding up, the smoke continues to rise when out of nowhere, over the music and reaction from the crowd the following words are heard spoken in a hardcore ghetto voive:] VO : "DEVASTATING" DAMON KNIGHT MUTHA(BLEEP)A!!! [Following that, the following appears on the jumbotron:] MR. ATTITUDE [With that on the jumbotron pyros shoot out like sparlers, however they continue to run. Though all of the pyros and what-not, you are unable to see if anyone has walked out.... after a few moments though the pyros, smoke, and lights emerges... "Devastating" Damon Knight! Damon Knight is african american. Damon Knight has braided hair down to his shoulder's which is worn in a corn-roll style. He has deep dark brown eyes. His left ear is piereced which he wears a black pearl in. On Damon Knight's left arm he has a tatoo of a burning cross, with barb wire going up and down it. Damon Knight has a tatoo of a black panther over his upper left chest muscle, were his heart is located. Damon Knight wears black timberland boots. Damon wears baggy black carpenter jeans, and a white beater. Damon Knight walks to the ring with the spotlight on him.] [Damon hops on the ring apron, then sling shoots himself into the ring. He takes his shirt off and throws it into the crowd. He sets on the top turnbuckle to await the ring bell, this draws a HUGE heel pop for Damon Knight. Damon's music dyes down, the lighting also returns to normal as Damon Knight grabs the microphone and begins to speak.] DK: Yo, as y'all can all tell by lookin' at tha men that we are, tha new guns is all up in tha mix. Your lord and savior is all up in tha mix. Who is this man? Your role model? Tha man ya' wants ta' be? Tha man that is gon' take ya' to tha top? [He pauses before looking up with a serious look upon his face ands shaking his head "yes."] DK: Yep, y'all are right. I'ma take ya' to tha top, while droppin' all of y'all on my way up. (Chuckles) Do y'all expect less? Nope. I think not. So what'cha gon' get? Nothin' less. Tha ultimate male, tha new age in wrestlin' has arrived in this piss-pot organazition. Y'all now know that Damon Knight is in tha mix, y'all now know that y'all have a new number one, and y'all now know that stepping in tha ring, my ring, will prove ta' be, "Devastating." [The crowd boos as Damon Knight does his trademark "smirk."] DK: Y'all can set back there and boo all y'all want too. [HEEL pop.] DK: (Yelling over crowd) Tha reign ain't even started and y'all is already hatin'. (Shakes head) Y'all need to set back, shut up, and veiw the speciment that god spent too much time on. [Knight lowers the microphone as he look around at the crowd.] DK: I personify perfection, I show class, and I wouldn't show any of you koons and hill-billy bitches shit! So calm your happy asses down. I know it's hard to resist when tha ultimate male is standing in front of y'all sluts. [HEEL pop.] DK: Y'all shouldn't set there and get used to this picture soon(Grin), 'cause not only will I wear tha NWP Greater North Title, but I will continue to be your reason for coming. [Damon Knight lowers the microphone from his mouth as the fans begin to boo at Knight's arrogance.] DK: Com' on now... [Damon pauses for a moment, having a devious look on his face, allowing all of the fans in the audience to get a better understanding of Knight, all though a look.] DK: I'm not here to play nice. I'm the knight and shinning armor of NWP, and ya' know what, truthfully ... i'ma guru to you peasants. Y'all can keep taking those snapshots 'cause soon y'all might not be blessed wit' tha presence of tha top dawg, unless you earn some respect, pups! [Knight pauses once more due to the "pop" once more from the crowd.] DK: Everyone in the back can try to match fire with fire (shaking head), but mine is hotter, and you'll get burnt. I'm the only man that matters, and soon i'll be the only man standing in the main event ... believe that. [Damon Knight walks around the ring for a few moments.] DK: NWP(Smirk), I'm Lots of action...Twice the satisfaction... and if i'm not the main event ... I'm DEFINITELY the MAIN ATTRACTION!! [A Hardcore Rap Beat plays over the loudspeakers as the crowd erupts in boos as Damon Knight makes his way to the back area.] TH: Well, are we ready for another match? WJ: What's up next? TH: The first EVER NWP tag team contest!
