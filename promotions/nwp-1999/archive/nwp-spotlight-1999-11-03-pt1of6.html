"Mr. Excitement" Alex Extreme flying off the top rope while Rumble holds their opponent down. XeRo running around like a maniac, carrying a little kid on his shoulders. And "The Last Real Man" Doc Holliday flipping off the top rope with his Aces High.] # It's the Eye of the Tiger, It's the thrill of the fight, Rising up to the challenge of our rival, And the last known survivor Stalks his prey in the night, And he's watching us all With the Eye of the Tiger # [And as the song fades out, this logo slowly comes on...] ____ __ ___ __ __ /\ _`\ /\ \__/\_ \ __ /\ \ /\ \__ \ \,\L\_\ _____ ___\ \ ,_\//\ \ /\_\ __\ \ \___\ \ ,_\ \/_\__ \ /\ '__`\ / __`\ \ \/ \ \ \ \/\ \ /'_ `\ \ _ `\ \ \/ /\ \L\ \ \ \L\ \/\ \L\ \ \ \_ \_\ \_\ \ \/\ \L\ \ \ \ \ \ \ \_ \ `\____\ \ ,__/\ \____/\ \__\/\____\\ \_\ \____ \ \_\ \_\ \__\ \/_____/\ \ \/ \/___/ \/__/\/____/ \/_/\/___L\ \/_/\/_/\/__/ \ \_\ /\____/ \/_/ \_/__/ [And then we're RIGHT in the middle of the Air Canade Centre. The crowd is small, but VERY lively. As the camera pans around, we get a view of the many signs the fans have brought in. They're all battling for position, jumping up and down in front of each other, trying to get into the camera's path. And then, the view abruptly changes to the entrance, where pyros are rocketing up at an alarmingly fast rate, and all we see are fireworks. As they slowly die down, the view goes down to ringside, where Thomas Hunter and Wilbur Jones are waiting at the commentating booth. The two of them are sitting side by side, but are quite contrasting in appearance. Jones is a larger individual, over 6 feet tall and a solid 220 pounds, while Hunter couldn't be bigger than 5 foot 8. The camera moves right into the two of them.] TH: Welcome! Welcome to the first NWP wrestling show. This is a night that you may NEVER forget! WJ: But if they're lucky, they will. TH: This sarcastic man is Wilbur Jones, and my name is Thomas Hunter. Anyhow, not only will we see somoeone get taken away to an asylum tonight, we'll also witness the first competitor for the NWP Greater North Title awarded here tonight. It'll be one of the men in our 10-man main event! WJ: That should be an interesting match, actually. 10 good wrestlers in the same ring. TH: Definitely. Not to mention the other great talent we have lined up -- James Pillow, Devon Case, Dave Davidson, Vince Taylor, Lawrence Williams, Thomas Lawrence, the Masters of Disaster, B.U.M. and more. WJ: It better be good! Northern Wrestling Promotions needs to make a statement to the wrestling world. TH: Hey, it looks like "The Buldogg" is up in the ring, ready to do an interview. This'll be keeping him busy all night. I mean, there are TWO interviews scheduled for this segment. WJ: Take it away, Nick! [The camera focuses up on Nick "The Bulldogg" Demola, who is holding the house mic, ready for the interview.] ND: Ladies and gentlemen, at this time allow me to introduce to you... the self-proclaimed "Last Real Man"... DOC HOLLIDAY! [The memorable opening to "Blaze Of Glory" by Jon Bon Jovi sounds out over the public address system, and a pair of beautiful ladies, wearing stylish tuxedo-wear, arrive at the top of the aisle, to the whistles and applause of the males in the crowd. The two ladies hold open the curtain... and much of the crowd erupts into boos, as the treacherous man known as Doc Holliday appears at the top of the aisle. The man is decked out like an 1880's millionaire. A fine black jacket with ruffled white undercoat, and black pants, gives Holliday the appearance of a dapper Old West socialite. He brushes back his long, wavy, light-brown hair on both sides, and an evil grin forms on the angular, clean-shaven face of the self-styled "Last Real Man"... and as he grins, the light gleams off his gold tooth.] TH: I've seen Holliday in action before. Unlimited potential, but MAN, is he full of himself! [One of the ladies hands Holliday his black hat... the other hands him his mahogany cane... they take him by the arms, and the three strut down the aisle to the HEEL POP of the capacity crowd. Holliday stops a few times to mock some of the fans in the aisleway, laughing at some, threatening others. As they reach ringside, the trio circles all the way around the ring, as if on parade! Holliday eventually heads up the stairs, arriving at the side of the ring nearest to Demola (who is impatiently tapping his foot, as most interviewers would during Doc's long, drawn-out intro). The women leave ringside, and Holliday walks down the apron, and into the ring... as soon as he's in the ring, a gold spotlight hits him, and he starts dancing and showing off! The music soon fades...] TH: The guy just about killed half his interview time just getting to the ring! WJ: Style points, I gotta say it, style points. ND: So, I hear that you're a great wrestler... even a champion of some kind... [Doc just grins knowingly, and nods his head up and down in a really self-satisfied, IRRITATING manner. When he speaks, he does so in a baritone voice with a HEAVY Southwestern accent.] DH: Yup, ya done heard right. Doc Holliday is not only th' Last Real Man on this heah ball o' crud ya call Earth... but he's also TH' NWP WORL' HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION!! [BOOOOO!!] DH: Yeah, ah won it by default, on account o' noone else within' two hunn'erd fifty miles could shine up mah boots. Them NWP officials knewed it too, so's they done gone an' made Doc Holliday th' champeen already, an' signed up nine BUMS ta git inna ring ta make a fair fight fer mah firs' title dee-fense! [Demola just shakes his head, and glares at Holliday.] ND: Yeah... uh huh... kid, look at me. I'm the greatest champion you'll EVER lay eyes on. I was winning titles in Baghdad before you were in your daddy's bag. Now who the Hell do you think you are that you're going to say all that shit about yourself when you're looking "The GOD of Wrestling" in the eyes? [Holliday just waves at Demola with the back of his hand, as if he were a mosquito.] DH: PUNK, if ya was a damn God, you'd'a used yer godly power ta make yerself look lak Doc Holliday, instead o' lookin' lak th' south end of a northbound mule. Now ah know ya wanna be lak Doc, jus' lak ever' Doc Holliday wannabe in this heah crowd. At least ya gots standards. As far as bein' a God, Doc Holliday would NEVAH claim ta be th' "God O' Wrasslin'" on account o' ah don't LOWER mah status fer nobody! An' as far as you talkin' 'bout mah daddy's sack, don' talk about yer gran'pa thet way, boy. [BBBBOOOOOOO!! A small "KICK-HIS-ASS-NICK" chant starts up in the back.] ND: You're really starting to... DH (interruputing): Ah'll be startin' ta whup yer ass in a minute, mah son, but since ah'm yer daddy ah'll have mercy an' go on ta talk about th'... ND (interrupting): ... DO NOT interrupt me! You better know your... damn, that bastard stole THAT line from me too! Know your place, kid! This is a federation of talented wrestlers, and you're making me sick. [Nick throws down the mic and walks out disgusted. Holliday points at the mic and looks at Demola expectantly, as if he honestly expects Demola to pick up the mic and give it to him. When it becomes clear that won't happen, Doc flips the mic up into his hand with his left foot, and goes on, as the crowd jeers him.] DH: Now thet ah gots mah ring ta mahself, it's time ta tell alla you no accounts, scrubs, an' boot-lickers exactly whut Doc Holliday expects from ya, as th' new champ-een 'round these heah parts! Firs' off, when ah talk or wrassle, ah demand absolute SILENCE! SHUT YER PIEHOLES! [BBBOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!] DH: An' second off, ah expec' full obee-dee-ince! When yer champ-een tells ya ta shaddap, ya bettah shaddap. Else ah'm'a hafta go out thar an' whup hell outta each an' ever' one of ya! AN' THET GOES DOUBLE FER YOU PUNKS INNA BACK!! [Doc points down the aisle menacingly, then turns around, and pulls some kind of package out of his suit jacket.] DH: Now, now... since each an' ever' scumbag runnin' his lip out heah inna crowd obviously wants ta be lak Doc, ah decided, thet as th' Last Real Man onna planet Earth, ah kin set a good example fer alla you bums thet paid yer hard-earned money ta come out ta th' show an' daydream about bein' a somebody lak Doc Holliday, instead bein' o' a worthless nothin' lak yerselves. [Amidst the jeering crowd, somebody stands and throws a drink at Holliday... Doc swats it out of the air, but it splashes all over him anyway. Even THIS does not stop the man, or even make him pause, from running his mouth even more.] DH: Is thet any way ta treat yer champ-een? Ah even brought y'all a gift! Seein' how y'all wanna be lak Doc, ah brought somethin' ta help ya do thet. 'Cause ta make an improvement in yer life, ya gotta distract yerselves from realizin' whut a pack o' no account jackasses y'all really are. An' so... ah brought a whole truckload o' these! [Holliday undoes the package, which is seen to be a rolled up T-Shirt. He unfurls it... it's a white T-Shirt with a phrase clearly printed in bold black print on the front... "I MARK OUT FOR MYSELF"] DH: Now you too kin feel lak yer somebody wearin' Doc Holliday's shirt. Mind ya, it won't MAKE ya a somebody... fact is, ah'm th' Last Real Man they IS, an' alla yer women knows it, so yer ALL outta luck thar! But mebbe y'all kin Mark Out For Yourself, lak ah do, an' have at least ONE meanin'ful experience in yer life aftah all. Fer ONLY thirty bucks a pop! [BBBOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!] TH: Doc Holliday may have the biggest ego I've ever SEEN! Somebody get Nick back out there, his alloted interview time is up! WJ: No WAY! Let him stay, this is great! TH: We have more interviews scheduled... he's hogging all the time! DH: Now thet yer all RUSHIN' ta th' souvenir stands, ah guess ah kin take mah leave... [Doc walks to the ropes to leave. And now, from the back of the arena, a chant starts. A chant, that to followers of Doc Holliday's career, is fairly familiar. The hated chant that follows him everywhere he goes. He hears it now, and his expression turns sour, as it grows in size... "holl-i-day-sucks... holl-i-day-SUCKS... Holl-I-Day-SUCKS... HOLL-I-DAY-*SUCKS*... HOLL-I-DAY-*SUCKS*... HOLL-I-DAY-*SUCKS*!!!..."] DH: Aw, hell, since yer all chantin' mah name anyway... AH MIGHT AS WELL STAY OUT HEAH ALL NIGHT!! [Doc moves back into the center of the ring, and the crowd boos like mad!] DH: So, lak ah wuz sayin', each an' ever' one of you scrubs out heah is... [Abruptly, the Jumbotron blinks into life, and the cameras cut away from Holliday ranting in the ring to a interview scene with Nick "The Bulldog" Demola, the new NWP superstar Garrett Selcouth, and his manager, Patrick Jaeger. All three have smirks on their face, though Garrett, with the more serious facial expression of the three. Nick Demola is attired in casual street wear, with Patrick in his managerial tuxedo. With Garrett, attired in his traditional wrestling wear. Inside the ring, Doc Holliday looks shocked as Nick Demola begins to spout questions towards the likes of Garrett and Patrick.] ND: Hey rook, you brought your entorauge with you? GS: Aye, o' course ah' brought Patrick wi' me. Th's 'es the lad 'ho br'ught me into the worl' ah' Proffesional Wrahslin'. Reason bein' 'cos since we wh're l'ttle lads, 'is family treated me like 'ah were royalty. Not only that thoo,' since day one me an' 'im cl'cked into fr'endshp, an' from ther' we stu'k together through thick an' th'n. From day one t'll nao. ND: Yeah. That's nice and all. Just what the Hell do you think a rookie can do in a federation like NWP? Or are you just going to do the sign of the cross and pray the big bad bullies don't break your neck? [Patricks cuts in on this one as Garrett doesn't look too pleased.] PJ: Ach, lad! Garrett 'ere 's a fine athl'te an' I be damned 'f ye tell'n 'im diff'rnt. Garrett and I are goin' te' sh'w ye' all teh'nite wh' we really are. We'r' goin' te' show ye' wee lads how te' light up ah' arena. Ye jus' watch and learn, laddy. ND: Talkin' tough there youngsta. But let's get serious. How 'bout that Ryder Cup? JUSTIN LEONARD RULES! GS: Mr. Demola. Please nao, yer aggorance 's much ign'red and frankley. Patrick an' I, well, we.. PJ (cutting off Garret): We don't apprec'ate 't.. [With that, Demola tosses Patrick the mic and walks off into the shadowed off screen picture.] PJ: Why, th't bleedin' Jessie, who mig't he th'nk ye' are? Some blasted lassie? Well, th'ts th' last t'me he get's en' interview wi' us. VOICE FROM OFF SCREEN: Yer _DAMN_ right it is! [Doc Holliday storms in, having just marched all the way from center ring. A look of anger has supplanted his typical cocky facial expression. He points a threatening finger right at Patrick and virtually snarls at him.] DH: PUNK, ya done whut should nevah BE done! Ah didn't come alla way up heah ta NWP ta have rookie scrubs thet wear SKIRTS cut me off when ah gots thangs ta say! Ya bettah clear on outta dodge, an' RUN th' hell outta MAH fed, 'afore ah hit ya so hard, th' only athletic compy-tishin yer EVAH gonna be fit ta compete in is th' damn Caber Toss... AS TH' DAMN CABER!! PJ: Mr. Holliday. If ye ple'se. My wrestler, nor ah had cut ye off.. We were mer'ly sched'led fer 'n int'rview an' well, a int'rview 's what we reci'ved. GS: Aye, it's not or fault tha' ye cont'nually talk with'ut break. DH: WHUT?! Kid, ah could slap yer face clean off an' ya'd end up havin' ta talk out yer ass th' rest o' yer life. Wouldn't make no diff'ernce, on account of you just spewed mo' ((**BLEEP**)) than ya kin fin' in a sewer. 'Ceptin' fer thet SEWER ya call SCOTLAND... ya kin find PLENTY O' ((**BLEEP**)) THAR!!! [Doc finishes his words and then, just as quickly slaps and pushes Patrick Jaeger out of his chair making him fall flat on his back. This enrages Garrett Selcouth as Doc turns around to walk away. Just as he does so, Garrett gets up from his seat, spins Doc around, and then simultaneously kicks him in the gut and lifts him up for a Body Slam Through Split-legged Piledriver. Just as Garrett hits his manuever, he gets up and peers down at the downed body of Doc Holliday.] GS: This 's what ye asked fer, and what ye s' r'ghtfully desrved. Remem'er my face, remem'er my name, and ah will meet ye inside the r'ng.. Tonight. [The ice-cold Garrett Selcouth and Patick Jaeger walk off screen.] [Doc slowly... ever so slowly... rolls up to a seated position. He's bleeding from the forehead, and seems to be very dazed... his eyes have gone glassy. After a moment, he SNAPS out of it, and his eyes grow cold as a look of rage has filled his features. Doc tries to get up but fails, and so he slides away, almost slithering as a snake does... muttering under his breath. We only catch the end of what he mutters...] DH (muttering): ... las' mistake you'll evah live ta make... [...and then he's gone, out of the picture. We fade to commercial.] [Slowly, the screen fades from black to a black backround. The background is outlined in gold, and there is a picture of a yellow jacket directly in the center. Out walks the NWP star "Roman" Thomas Lawrence.] TL: Ah yes, the _ONE_ and _ONLY_ college for anyone to attend - Georgia Tech, located in Atlanta, GA. I could care less if I wrestle in a federation that's based in the northern United States and Canada. If you want to learn, go where I went, Georgia Tech. Not only is it the best, but right now, their football team is ranked number seven. It's the best _ANY_ way you look at it! [Scene fades to black.] TH: Where did Lawrence learn his math? 7th ISN'T the best last time I checked. WJ: Probably learned at Georgia Tech. TH: Anyhow, maybe we should get down to our first matc-- ["It's All About the Benjamins" by Puff Daddy comes on over the PA system as some fans come to their feet awaiting what they know will follow. On the big screen the words "Ladies Man" flash in gold, followed by a picture of Vince Taylor talking to three beautiful women. Then "Millionaire" flashes in gold, followed by Taylor counting a stack of money. Then the man himself, Vince Taylor, comes out from the back. A mixed reaction. Vince is wearing a nice white casual suit, with his matching white hat. On his face are his usual gold framed sunglasses and his diamond stud earring. Vince stands at the entranceway and gazes out at the fans with a sly smile on his face.] TH: Well, I guess we're going to have some comments from Mr. Taylor! [Vince starts to casually walk to the ring, stopping occassionally to give a fan a high five. He gets to the ring and steps right in. He goes to the center of the ring, grabs a mic, and removes his sunglasses.] VT: I gotta admit that I couldn't stay away. I missed stepping into the ring and dishing out the punishment that no man can take. I missed being in the hunt to have that gold around my waist. But there is one thing that I didn't miss one bit, and that's the big chip that used to rest on my shoulder. [The crowd seems dumbfounded as if this is a different man then who entered the ring just minutes ago, and a frenzy of confused people chattering. Vince continues while pacing around the ring, and working into a frenzy.] VT: You heard me right people, "The Ladies Man" is no longer out here wrestling to get more women into bed. "The Ladies Man" is no longer out here so I can put more money in my pocket. I'm out here for one reason... you people that are sitting in the seats. I'm here to give my blood, my sweat, and my soul every night, right in the center of the ring, in order to show every god damn person sitting in the crowd the night of their life. [HUGE face pop from the crowd.] VT: And all of that starts right here tonight when I finally get to make my way back to the squared circle, and prove that I've got something that can't be overcome. To prove that I've got the drive that could stop Jesus Christ himself. I'm right here tonight to show the fans that I'm wrestling to give them a good time, and go down in the history books as the most exciting, most whoever laced the boots up. [Another BIG face pop.] VT: So I guess that leaves only more thing for me to do tonight, and thats to simply go out and what I do best...wrestle. Go out, give every drop of sweat in the body, and leave it in the ring. And I can tell you something else that I'm gonna leave in the ring, Dave Davidson. Dave, you call yourself "Delicious." When I look at you I see one thing, somebody who so badly wants to be what I once was. Well maybe I do have a new attitude and maybe I'm not out here to piss the fans off any longer. But there's one thing that I can still do better than anybody on this planet, and that's walk away with a lady on each arm. [HUGE pop.] VT: I was just like you when I started out in this sport. So you can come out here and show your ego off. But I've been there, done that, and you can bet that I'll know every mistake that you're going to make in the ring. There's only way that this match can finish, and that's with me walking out here with a notch on belt after just one night of NWP competition. [Vince pauses and thinks to himself.] VT: Am I the best wrestler in the world today? No, nor do I claim to be. But show me the best, and I'll sure as hell give him the fight of his life. So Dave Davidson, are you ready for the _new_ Vince Taylor? Are you ready for somebody with a fire lit under them that has fully rejunvenated him? Are you ready for the wrestling ability of me along with the backing of every person in this arena? I don't think you are, and I can guarantee that you'll never know what hit 'ya when I walk down that aisle, drive the ladies wild and put give these fans the thrill of a lifetime. [Vince drops the mic as the fans give him yet another huge face pop. Taylor walks back to the locker room, while giving fans high fives the whole way.] TH: Well, the fans have certainly taken a shining to him. WJ: That's nice. CAN WE PLEASE GET TO A MATCH?!
