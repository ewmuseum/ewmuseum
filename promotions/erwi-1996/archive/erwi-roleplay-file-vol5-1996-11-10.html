His HOW World Championship belt is laying on the bench in front of him. He's looking down upon it intensely. After several minutes, he looks up to address the camera.) What's up, people. You see this, (Points to the belt.) laying right here? This is my present and I will never give it up. (Walks over to the right where there's a huge advertisement for When Worlds Collide stuck on the wall.) But this, (Points to the advertisement.) this is my future and I'm not about to give that up either. (Walks back over to where his belt is lying.) One round of the International Championship Tournament is complete and there are four rounds left to go before we're down to the final two. I fully intend to be one of those two. I've worked too damn hard and too damn long for me to let anything or anyone stand in the way of what is my destiny. I know I have a long road ahead of me but I'm willing to go that extra mile to get the job done. (Looks straight into the camera.) Iceman, this Saturday we hook up for the very first time but definitely not the last. I've been studying your every move over this past week and a half and I have to admit that you're about as tough as they come. You don't have many flaws but you have just enough for me to exploit to come out on top. You're one of the top athletes in the ERWI, there's no doubt about that, but as Chris Abenetoire and Ice Century found out, so am I. So what it's gonna come down to is who's got what's in here (Points to his heart.) to advance to the next round. I don't know about you, Iceman, but I've got a whole lot of it so you're gonna have to damn near kill me to put my shoulders to the mat for the one, two, three and as for submitting, you can just forget about it 'cause the words 'I quit' aren't a part of my vocabulary. The bottom line is, Iceman, you're in for one hell of a long night. (Picks up the HOW World Championship belt and drapes it over his shoulder.) See ya at Slamfest. (Walks off.) ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ 7. War Hawk and Thunderbird THUNDERBIRD: (He and WarHawk are at a local mall doing an autograph-signing and picture-taking session. Several hundred fans have packed the mall just to get a glimpse of the hottest young superstars in the ERWI. After several hours, the session is brought to an end. Thunderbird and WarHawk get up, wave to the fans to a roaring ovation, and begin a stroll through the mall as security holds the fans back. Thunderbird proceeds to address the camera.) Damn, I can't believe that it's six o'clock already. I guess time really does fly when you're having fun. (WarHawk nods his head in agreement.) Now that the fun's over it's time to address some more serious issues here. First and foremost is the subject of the Diamond Crushers. We've heard what they had to say about us recently and we took offense to some their comments. Fellas, what's the problem? Did you guys wake up on the wrong sides of your beds a couple of days ago? I mean what beef have we ever had with you for you to be talking down to us like that. If the aim of your little tirade was to get us riled up, well, you succeeded and, for you, that's not such a good thing. You see, I'm already ticked off at my early exit from the International Championship Tournament and you just added fuel to the fire. Not very smart, Crushers. All you've done was make us more determined and motivated to make our first appearance at a PPV in the ERWI a rousing success. So I guess we should thank you for that and you, (Points to the camera.) you should really feel sorry for yourselves 'cause you're gonna get an ass-kickin you'll never forget. Tell 'em, 'Hawk. WARHAWK: Partner, you took the words right out of my mouth. Crushers, we never had any intention on taking you lightly and we know that we had to be in the best conditions of our lives to take on a team of your caliber. I never said anything to the contrary; did you, Thunderbird? (Thunderbird shakes his head.) So what I don't understand is where you guys came from with these fairy tales. This time, I think you bit off a little more than you can chew. (Takes a stick of gum out of his pocket and pops it into his mouth.) When Worlds Collide is an appropriate title for this upcoming pay-per-view because when we four collide the tag team scene in the ERWI will never be the same. You wanna know why? (Pauses.) It's because Thunderbird and myself, on that day, are gonna start setting the standard for all other tag teams in the ERWI, present and future, to live up to. And it all begins with you, Crushers. (Smiles devilishly.) Congratulations. (Takes his sweater off and wraps it around his waist.) Speaking of tag teams, I've got a match this Saturday with one half of the current World Tag Team Champions. Eraser, I expect one hell of a match out of you and you better expect the same from me. Good luck to you. And, by the way, don't think that this is the last time that we'll ever meet. You and your partner have a couple of belts that we'd like to be wearing one day so keep on the lookout for this dynamic duo. We're definitely one to watch. Check y'all later.
