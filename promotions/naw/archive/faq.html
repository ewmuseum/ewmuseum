F.A.Q.'s

April 2003

F.A.Q.'s Q. When did NAW open? A. NAW opened in late March of 2003. Q. How do I join NAW? A. This must be the easiest question I've ever heard of... just click Wrestler Application and just fill out the application and the NAW staff will go over your application. So remember to add as much details as possible! Q. Why can't I apply as Stone Cold or the other pro wrestlers? A. The reason why is because this is an original efed, we do not like to call people in this efed as pro wrestlers. And if you do, your application will be denied. Q. How do I contact the staff if I will be out for a few days/weeks/months? A. You can contact the staff by clicking here . Remember to give us the reason why you will be away from NAW and for how long. Q. How do I get warnings? A. You get warnings for complaining about a match you lost or complain to the staff or big mouth the staff or other fellow members in NAW. Below is the warning procedures. 1st Warning - You will be told directly from the president of NAW. 2nd Warning - Suspension (number of days determined by president/v. president) 3rd Warning - Fired Q. How do I become a card writer or a staff? A. You can contact the president by clicking here . Remember to add what position and why. Q. I've been here for the longest, why can't I become a main eventer? A. Why? The answer is quite easy, you must be an outstanding RPer or have a good character that sticks in with NAW. If you change your gimmick every week or two that does not show the staff much about you. Q. I want to quit, where do I go to tell the president? A. You contact the president or the staff by clicking here . If you post anything about quitting on the RP Board, you will be in serious trouble, so DO NOT do it.
