Thursday Night Anarchy Results: 03/03/03 April 3rdth, 2003 Joe Louis Arena, Detroit, Michigan (Before Anarchy starts up, a video of last week's Battle Royal is shown, Cereal with the victory, the video fades away into E-Dogg's office.) E-Dogg: What can I say? Cereal won the Battle Royal, and tonight he will be facing TraV for the Heavyweight championship. What a big night it will be. NAW is rising every week, and this is only our second event. Renegade: Oh yes... NAW is shaping up like we've planned it to be... (The screen changes to the Anarchy opening video, and then pyro's goes off in the Joe Louis Arena, which is sold out and live from Detroit, Michigan. NAW commentators, Ed 2 Tite and Rich Extreme are seated at the announce table.) Ed 2 Tite: Welcome to Thursday Night Anarchy at the sold out Joe Louis Arena in Detroit, Michigan. As E-Dogg said, tonight will be a big night for the company. NAW will have serveral titles up on the line. Who do you think will win the Heavyweight championship Rich? Rich Extreme: Hum... I haven't seen TraV in action before, so I'm going for Cereal. Cereal kicked major ass last week. Let's see what happens this week. Rising Star Championship Mystic J vs Weaponary Standard Match (As Rich and Ed continue talking, "Fine Again" by Seether hits the P.A. and Mystic J walks out into the ring.) Patrick McDonald (ring announcer): This match is for a one fall! And it is for the NAW Rising Star championship! Introducing first from Philadelphia, Pennsylvania... Mystic J! (The fans goes crazy over Mystic J.) Patrick McDonald: And the opponent... Weaponary! (Weaponary slides into the ring and Mystic J starts it up with kicks to Weaponary.) Rich Extreme: Wow... Mystic J is doing great. (While Mystic J takes a look at the fans Weaponary gives a hard punch to Mystic J and quickly gets to his feet. Weaponary throws Mystic J to the turnbuckle and begins stompping on Mystic J. Senior ref. David Erikson pulls Weaponary back and warns him. While Weaponary is pulled back Mystic J charges at Weaponary with a clothesline from hell. Weaponary gets up and Mystic J get a hold of a piledriver. Mystic J gets Weaponary up and sets him up for a jacknife powerbomb.) Ed 2 Tite: What a nice jacknife powerbomb by Mystic J! Rich Extreme: Weaponary slowly gets up, tries to throw a punch, Mystic J blocks and gives a hard kick to Weaponary! (Mystic J picks Weaponary from the back and runs to the other side of the ring with a running powerslam. Weaponary gets a hold of the ropes to get himself up, Mystic J charges at Weaponary like a rhino for a huge big boot. Mystic J covers Weaponary.) 1... 2... 3... Patrick McDonald: And your winner, and NEW NAW Rising Star champion... Mystic J! Winner: New NAW Rising Star champion, Mystic J (Senior ref. David Erikson holds Mystic J's arm in victory. Patrick McDonald hands Mystic J the Rising Star championship title, Mystic J looks at the belt very clearly for a few minutes then slides out of the ring and walks backstage.)
