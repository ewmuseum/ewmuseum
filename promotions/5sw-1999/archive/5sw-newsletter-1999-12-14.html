Remember, you may think that these are opinions, but everything I say goes ... - So XTC won the battle royal and was rewarded with the number one seed in the World Title tournament, which gives him a nice advantage. Can anyone step up and beat him in the tournament? The Killa? Pheonix Kidd?? - On the subject of XTC, what the hell do those letters stand for?? - A little suggestion to Jonathan Rock ... cut down on the crack, weed, or whatever you're smoking because it will do nothing but hurt you inside the ring. - Wanna here of a couple of guys I think I have the potential to go far in this company's Tag Team Division ... The Army Men. I believe that they have yet to show us their true abilities.
- This one goes out to the man who comes out of this tournament as the champion ... keep your eye on WiggeyD, because he can and will take the title away from you if he truly wants to.
- Using references to The Undertaker, "Are Your Ready" for a themesong, and even crotch crops!?! Is the Pheonix Kidd in the wrong company, or does he just suffer from an extreme case of "gimmick infringment"?
- God's Gift decides to interupt almost the entire 5SW staff, including President Mike Williams, on the first ever 5SW event?? I guess he was hanging out with John Rock, because he had to have been smoking something to have done that!!
- One other thing about God's Gift. If thats your name ... shouldnt that mean you're good??!! - Gutter Scum ... Gutter Scum? Please enlighten to me as to why someone would want to be known as that by hundreds of thousands of people! - Is it just me, or does anyone else think that in that picture we've seen that Lance Wild looks more of a lover than a fighter? - You know what really ticks me off more than anything? The people who every other word out of their mouth is a curse. - The Fantastic Four? If you ask me, it is the fantastic "ONE"! I mean, Overdose and Surge are alright, but they are from from being fantastic ... maybe a word like "mediocre" fits each of them a little better. Now, thats not to say they camt be top stars in the future, but as of right now I see nothing special out of them.
- Now its time for my TOP 5 5SW Wrestlers for the week. 5. Lance Wild 4. Jonathan Rock 3. The Killa 2. The Pheonix Kidd, and ... 1. XTC - Okay, on to another section of my column, which will also be a weekly staple. "Michael O'Dell's Fearless Forecasts". In The Tournament Matches ... Derek Irons will land a minor upset over Doom. Overdose will also land an upset by beating Surge. Lance Wild will beat War. God's Gift, receiving a push I dont feel he deserves will upset Jonathan Rock. And, in the biggest match of the night ... The Pheonix Kidd will beat The
Killa. As for the other matches ... frankly you all dont deserve to be mentioned by someone as great as me ...maybe next time
Well boys and girls ... thats all the words of wisdom I have for you today, meaning your hero must leave. Dont fret too much, I'll be back next week so make sure you tune in at the same Perfect Time, same Perfect Place, same
