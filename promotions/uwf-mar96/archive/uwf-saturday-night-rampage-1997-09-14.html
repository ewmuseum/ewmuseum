UWF Saturday Night Rampage, 14 September 1997

14 September 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Part 1] [Fade up from black, to the scene of a packed chamber. The camera opens on a crowded press conference room, and around the room can be seen many reporters, some who are familiar, such as PWI reporter Bill Apter, and some who are not. The reporters talk amongst themselves and the cameras pick up words from a few, who seem to ask the others why they are at the conference. None of the reporters seem to now the answer other than the fact that it is a conference from ‘Modern Day Warrior’ Mike Stewart.] [Suddenly, a man in his mid 30s walks into the room wearing a black suit and some of the fans know the man as Stewart's agent, David Pitt. The man walks up to the makeshift stage in the front middle part of the conference room and then up to the podium on the middle of that stage. He clears his throat before speaking. All of the reporters quiet down as he speaks.] DP: Ladies and gentlemen, as most of you know, you've been called here today by one of the up and coming stars of the professional wrestling world, the ‘Modern Day Warrior’ Michael Stewart. Mr. Stewart has called you all here today to make an announcement that may take some of you by surprise, and some of you it may not as he has made this announcement before. But I hope that Mr. Stewart's fans will understand his announcement and decision and why he's making it. So, without any further adieu, here is the ‘Modern Day Warrior’ Mike Stewart! [The reporters all begins to talk loudly and cameras begin to flash as Stewart makes his way through a side door hand in hand with his girlfriend Michelle Anderson. Stewart is decked out in a nice-fitting black Armani suit, but he doesn't look to be his normal happy self. Anderson is wearing a beautiful black business suit with a somewhat short skirt, but she also doesn't seem to be happy. Stewart walks up to the podium and shakes hands with Pitt, who steps back and stands next to Anderson. Stewart gives a smile to the cameras and then they all begin to quiet down.] MS: Ladies and gentlemen of the press, the reason you have all been called here today is because I have decided to announce that I will be retiring from the wrestling ring. [Some of the announcers begin to talk amongst themselves, while some others have already figured out the announcement and are not all that surprised.] MS: The reason I am making this announcement is because of a reoccuring neck injury that I suffered almost a year ago in a scaffold match in the AEWA with Heavy Metal. As most of you, this injury had forced me to retire once before in my career, but I was then told that it was fine. Well, after suffered a back injury in my match at Battle Klash II with Joe Reed, I had felt some stiffness in my neck again and I was examined by doctors once again. After seeing those doctors, they told me that I had to retire due to this injury. Now, since I have already been mis-diagnosed before, I decided to see two other doctors and seek their opinions. I felt that it could possibly be another mistake... but this time it wasn't. All of those doctors told me that if I didn't quit wrestling that I could have major back problems around the age of 40 and that I may even be paralyzed. [Some of the reporters buzz as the thought of paralization of Stewart.] MW: As many of you know, I do have a wonderful future... [looks at Anderson and then back out to the reporters] ...and I didn't want to jeopardize not being able to spend time with my children in the future, so I have heeded the advice of those doctors and I will be stepping away from the ring. Also, as some of you know, I have recently not been fairing terrible well in the ring, certainly not as well as I have done previously. I would like to blame this on my injury, but I've always been one to believe that excuses never solved anything and that everyone should stand up for their own actions. With that, I would like to thank all of the fans that have stood by me, and I would also like to thank all of the federational presidents and promoters who gave me the options of displaying my talents each and every week out in the ring. I do feel blessed with the happiness that I had while I was out there, and I thank everyone for that, and with that, I'd like to thank you all for coming today. [Stewart then nods to the reporters, shakes hands with Pitt, and grabs Anderson's hand and walks out the side door as Pitt follows them out. Fade into the opening for Saturday Night Rampage. As "Let's Get Rocked" by Def Leppard is heard, fast paced clips of UWF shows start to flash by, each with the name of the wrestler at the bottom, as the logo jumps onto the screen, and then flickers on and off before disappearing for a second. Alex Kidd executes the Somersault Bulldog, followed by a Diamondback Death Drop from Sebastian Jericho, and then a Kentucky Backsmasher brought to us courtesy of 'Crimson' Joe Reed. The fabled Superman Spike onto the steel chair plays, as does the Pride Breaker, Pride Press, Pride Bomb, and Pride Buster in succession. The DOA Full Nelson flashes by, as does the SnakeBite, Retro-Rocker, and the Wildflyer Moonsault finish up, and a strobed picture of Alex Kidd raising the Championship Belt at Summer Heat Wave. Fade from this up to the Corestate Center in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania. While the many cameras in the building pan across the crowd repeatedly, fans jump up and down trying to be seen. As always, a usual array of colourful signs are visible, some reading “Stewart sucks, Pride 4 life!”, “Crystal for UWF President!”, and “Bischoff in da house. Paul Reed, watch your wrestlers’ contracts!”. David Rogers, Amy Marshall and Sam Steeeley are seen at ringside, each with a headset on. Dave’s wearing his usual outfit, consisting of a suit and tie, the jacket having a small UWF logo on the pocket. Sam’s more casual, wearing a “Pride is Forever” t-shirt and a pair of jeans. And Amy is wearing a white t-shirt with black vest, black skirt, and has her hair up in a ponytail. The crowd cheers as fireworks shoot out at the wrestler entrance, and the lights fade up and down a few times before Dave opens the show with his usual greeting.] DR: Hello everybody, and welcome to Saturday Night Rampage. As always, I’m David Rogers, and joining me is Amy Marshall to my left, and Sam Steeley to my right. As you saw at the top of the show, the mass exodus out of the UWF continues with the announcement that ‘Modern Day Warrior’ Mike Stewart has decided to retire from the sport. SS: Just like an AEWA wrestler, he loses a few matches and runs away crying. AM: Sam, why don’t you try to show a little bit of respect? Mike Stewart is a great competitor, and the sport will miss him. But you can’t expect him to jeapordize his future, including his personal life just to keep on with his professional life. I don’t blame Mike one bit, but I am sad to see him leave. DR: Agreed. But there is even more bad news to report on this front. At a house show this past week at the Blue Horizon boxing club in Philadelphia, Kelly Maloney took on his fellow family members Brendan and Neal in a handicap match, or at least he was supposed to. Instead, Kelly came out and delivered a fantastic speech to the crowd, and offered the following challenge: He would retire from competition in the UWF if he had the chance to wrestle his brother Brian in a one falls match. In return, if he defeated Brian then all three of his family members would have to leave as well. SS: I always said Kelly was stupid, but this takes the cake. Either way the match went, he would be out of the UWF. AM: Well, Brian came out and agreed to the challenge, and the two original members of the Maloney Brothers fought in what many at the event called the best match of the night. Twenty-six minutes the match went, and Kelly ended up with the win, forcing Brian, Neal and Brendan all to leave the UWF. So because of that match, the UWF is now without all four Maloneys for the first time in it’s history. Like them or not, they were and are some of the best competitors we’ve ever seen. DR: Once again, agreed. So in the last few weeks we’ve seen Kelly, Brian, Brendan, and Neal Maloney, Ace Malone, Blaze, and Bishop have all left the league for various reasons. SS: What is it about leaving the UWF? Are we not good enough for them? AM: Sam, calm down. Just because a number of athletes has left, that does not mean that there aren’t great stars still around. SS: You’re right, the Pride is still here. AM: If you think so... Let’s hear from the Reformer, who has some words about the Fraternity now that Bishop is gone. [Camera fades into a lonely, deserted area that is very dark and creepy.] The Reformer: [in the most calm voice ever heard from this man] Philadelphia, Pennsylvania!!! My hometown. The place will go crazy as I walk out that door and go to the ring. There is no better place to be then in Philly, and that is why I represent all people from the great city of Philadelphia. But what I have to say might not sit well with some fans, OH WELL, These are my feelings, and now I am going to say whats on my mind. UWF, This is addressed to every single wrestler here. Most of you have probably noticed that I haven’t been here talking to you people. Well, the reasons for not doing that are simple, I have been hiding out, watching and waiting to strike. Looking at wrestlers styles, abilities, talents, manoevers as well as their good sides and their bad sides. I have been the right hand man of the Bishop, but now that he is gone, it is my duty to lead the Fraternity of the Stone. And this I will do, since he is gone the Reformer is in charge, and now that I have done my studying and watching of all these wrestlers, It is time to start my reign here in the UWF. It is time to climb into the top 5 ranks and it is time to get the UWF World Title around my waist. I might be reformed but I still can play dirty, and right now, I am going to do what ever it takes to get that belt around my waist. The silence is over and now the Reformer is going to start doing what he does best, Kick @ss!!!! Brewster Cogburn, tonight I start with you, as my journey to the World Title begins. It has been a long time since I have been in the singles ring. I had many tag matches in the past couple of months, then the battle royal at the PPV. But now I make my return to the singles ranks. And I will take out each and every one of you people that get in my way. Cogburn, tonight is your downfall as I will destroy everything that you stand for. All you abilities, all you talents, they will do you no good as I rip you apart piece by piece!!! I will be the UWF World Champion! The Reformer has spoken! [Cut back to ringside. The fans begin to cheer as Tom Davis enters the ring with a microphone, and a smile on his face.] ===================================================== ‘FABULOUS’ FRANK WHITE versus ‘OFFICER’ MARK DURMAN ===================================================== TD: Ladies and gentlemen, welcome to Saturday Night Rampage! The first match of the night is scheduled for one fall. First, coming to the ring at this time, the PWA World Champion... ‘FABULOUS’ FRANK WHITE! [White and his bodyguard walk out to ringside without music, but they are booed soundly. The bodyguard makes like he’s going to strike a few rowdy college fans, but White holds him back and instead shakes his finger at them, then points to the gold title that he’s got around his waist. White steps into the ring, just as “Hair of the Dog” starts to play.] TD: And his opponent... From the KO Corral, and accompanied to the ring by Miss Demeanor and Jack Moran... ‘OFFICER’ MARK DURMAN! [Durman walks down to the ring with a “Corral or nuthin’, BOY!” t-shirt, and Jack Moran walks out in front of him with Miss Demeanor at Durman’s side. He steps through the ropes and stares White in the face, standing just as tall. White begins to smirk at him and talks trash, causing Durman to shove him.] DR: Is it me or does Frank White seem almost bored with the whole match? SS: He did say that he wasn’t going to wrestle until the UWF gave him some better competition. I’d say we’re still going to have to wait a while. AM: Mark Durman is a former United States Champion! SS: Uh huh. FORMER. [White grabs Durman in a headlock, but the Corral member throws him into the ropes, taking a shoulderblock off the rebound. White laughs at Durman, then grabs him by the hair and whips him into the corner. He delivers several knife-edge chops, and whips him cross corner into the corner, and runs at him with a lariat. Durman sidesteps the hit and reverses positions, and gives White a few shots to the head. He steps onto the second turnbuckle to drive more fists into his head, but White grabs him and atomic drops him hard, then kneelifts him in the stomach. Durman grabs him and tries to set him up for a bearhug, but White sends him crashing headfirst into the turnbuckle, and then hits him with a low blow.] DR: That will be sore in the morning. SS: Seems sore right now. [Durman doubles over in pain, and White executes a powerbomb to the mat and hooks the leg. Durman kicks out, but is placed in a chinlock and then stretched out on the mat in a bow and arrow hold. He manages to get to the ropes, and White jumps up and drives a knee into the shoulderblade of Durman, and grabs his head in a clawhold. Durman starts to get energized and lifts White up into a fireman’s carry, then samoan drops him into a pin attempt. White kicks out, and then grabs Durman by the throat and starts to choke him, but Durman kicks him off, then gets to his feet. White comes running at Durman, but Durman avoids the tackle and grabs him in the Bloody Gloves(full nelson).] AM: He’s got it! White’s going out! [This was true, until the bodyguard to ‘Fabulous’ Frank White reached into the ring and tripped Durman, knocking him to the ground with White on top of him. White uses a modified bridge to keep the shoulders to the mat, and the referee counts Durman out.] TD: Here is your winner, as a result of a pinfall... ‘FABULOUS’ FRANK WHITE! [White rolls out of the ring and retrieves his PWA belt and holds it up as if he’d defended it, then walks out after getting into a heated argument with those college guys again.] AM: Geez, why doesn’t he just strum the belt like a guitar? SS: Because he’s not an idiot? DR: Fans, in just a few moments, UWF President Paul Reed is going to make an important announcement about the AEWA. According to what I’ve heard, he will be responding to some of Bob Freeman's recent comments. He’s also coming out to talk a little more about War in the Desert too. SS: Yeah, Freeman is full of crap. He still thinks his Team AEWA has what it takes to stop our guys? Give me a break. We beat him in the battle royal, we’ll do it again. [As “Hail to the Chief” rings out, Paul Reed walks down the ramp and after speaking to a few of the fans at ringside steps into the ring, the crowd popping for him madly.] AM: Paul Reed appears to be ready in the ring now, and he’s actually getting quite a nice reaction from this crowd. Let’s hear what he has to say. [President Reed is wearing a nice-looking brown suit, with a red tie that says “UWF” in big lettering. He begins to speak.] Reed: Ladies and gentlemen, I am here tonight to make an announcement concerning the next pay per view from the UWF. War In the Desert will take place on Tuesday September 30th, 1997, and will eminate once again from Sun Devil Stadium in Pheonix, Arizona. The main event has been announced, and it will be Alex Kidd against ‘Crimson’ Joe Reed for the UWF World Heavyweight Championship. Also on the card, four of the UWF’s top tag teams will engage in a four team war games style elimination match. Last year this type of match was held at Beach Blast, and featured the World and United States Tag Team Champions as well as the top two contending teams. This time however, it will feature the United States Champions, as of this time which are Working Class. As well, the three top contendors to the US championship will also be involved, although they are yet to be determined. These are the only matches scheduled so far, but I will continue to update you all as more are signed. On another note, I would like to thank you for your continued support over the past few months. There are countless people trying to close this company down, but as long as you fans are on my side, we will _never_ quit the fight. At this time I would like to respond to Mister Freeman's recent challenge to me. This is absolutely ridiculous, folks. He seems to think that _I_ should get into the ring with him. [A slight pop from the crowd.] Well, let me make this clear: I don't acccept your challenge, Freeman. Why should I? After Team UWF got through reclaiming what is rightfully theirs, I really doubted you'd come looking for more. I guess I was wrong. Just stop your whining, Freeman, and put your money where your mouth is. If your Team AEWA is so good, _prove_ it. We are going to find out tonight, when Brett Greene and Alex Kidd, admittedly two of the greatest enemies the UWF has ever seen, join forces to face ‘Crimson’ Joe Reed and Serge Annis from the AEWA. I would also like to comment on the recent formation of our Team UWF. With all of our wrestlers fighting together, against the AEWA, I don't see how _anyone_ can stop us. Alex Kidd won back the World Title, Freeman, and let me make this clear-- you started this entire war. If it wasn't for you and 'Crimson' Joe Reed, the UWF wouldn't have had _any_ problem with the AEWA. Well, you asked for a war, and you've got one." Anyway, fans, I won’t bore you any longer with my problems, but I just wanted to clear those things up. Thank you for-- [AC/DC's "It's a Long Way to the Top" suddenly begins blaring over the public address system, drowning out Reed’s voice. A large man emerges from behind the curtain, dressed completely in black, with long white hair hanging in front of his face. He climbs into the ring and grabs the microphone away from Reed.] AM: Who is this guy?!? Man: [yelling] You don't come into my home and trash the place without considering the consequences. If you want to play the game of capture the flag, then consider yourself at a loss, because the AEWA is about to come into your house and kick some ASS. Everyone in this damned federation is on the Road to Nowhere and THERE IS NO WAY IN HELL that you'll make it. Remember, it’s a long way to the top if you want to ROCK N' ROLL! [Without any warning, the man punches President Reed in the face. Reed stumbles backwards into the corner, and the ring is instantly swarming with security guards, who quickly slam the man into the corner and handcuff him. Paul Reed leaves the ring, while the man is carted off by security in cuffs.] SS: WHOA! I wasn’t sure at first, but after getting a good look at him, I’m positive-- that man is Randall Osbourne, the AEWA’s North American champion. I knew he was crazy, but this is just plain ridiculous. AM: What the hell was that guy thinking? He actually punched Paul Reed in the face! There’s going to be some more retaliation because of this, that’s for sure. I cannot believe the AEWA has allowed this man to come here and assault the president like that! DR: Well, it looks like the security guards have taken that maniac away, and Paul Reed is being helped back to the locker room. I don’t think Reed was hurt very badly, but he’s definitely upset. SS: The AEWA is going to _pay_ for this little prank. Anyway, I hate to change the subject, but isn’t it time for our next match yet? ================================ TOM LANDIS versus MARC STEVENS ================================ TD: The following contest is scheduled for one fall, and has a twenty minute time limit. First, making his way to the ring at this time and accompanied by Crystal Cutglass, from Charlotte, North Carolina... MARC STEVENS! [“Don’t You Tread On Me” by Metallica begins to come over the PA system, and the crowd instantly starts to boo. Marc Stevens walks down the ramp followed by his valet, and walks the aisle surrounded by unfriendly fans. Stevens tells a few where to ‘get off’, then enters the ring and stands on the turnbuckles, daring Tom Landis to come out.] TD: And his opponent... From Chicago, Illinois and representing Legion, weighing in at 243 pounds, accompanied to the ring by Tara ‘Sunburst’ Marshall... TOM LANDIS! [The crowd reverses direction and a huge pop is delivered, while “We’re All Together Now” by Slam Jam hits the airwaves. Landis comes out with Tara Marshall on his arm, and slaps hands with excited Legion fans all the way down to the ring. He enters, and Marc Stevens immediately strikes with a flurry of punches.] AM: He couldn’t even wait until the bell rang! Stevens is nothing more than a vulture. [Stevens rocks Landis with several right hands, but Tom returns the favour, giving as good as he gets before dropping the Asylum’s leader with a legsweep. Landis gets up on the turnbuckle and pumps his fists, getting the crowd into it. He gets down and attempts to place Stevens in an armlock hammerlock submission, but the crafty opponent manages to drive Landis to the mat with a standing dropkick. They trade shots for the next minute, and Stevens then goes for a back suplex. Landis counters with a swift go-behind and uses a spin kick to take Stevens to the mat.] DR: Tom Landis has amazing agility, he always has. SS: Yeah, but he’s too gullible. Remember last Saturday? [Tom Landis whips Stevens into the ropes and prepares to meet him with a clothesline, but Stevens ducks and catches him with one instead, and the force of the blow sends Landis sprawling out to the floor. Stevens, not feeling particularly sportsmanlike, exits the ring too and smashes Landis headfirst into the ringsteps, and uses a fistdrop off the steps onto Tom. He pulls Landis up by the hair and a kick to the midsection reduces the Legionnaire to his knees. A forearm to the small of Landis’ back is enough to allow Stevens to push him back into the ring, and then he climbs the ropes to perch on top for a second, then comes down with a flying legdrop. Landis rolls out of the way in time, and Stevens crashes to the mat. Outside the ring, ‘Sunburst’ gets the crowd to start a chant of “Legion!”, which they do. Tom feeds off the crowd’s energy and gets to his feet first, then grabs Stevens and hits a snap suplex. Crystal Cutglass gets up on the ring apron to distract the referee, never a good sign for the face in the match.] AM: Come on, get her down from there! DR: I think- oh my! Tara Marshall has obliged her sister’s request! SS: She just pulled Crystal off the apron! [True, Tara had grabbed the leg of Ms. Cutglass and pulled her off the apron, but Crystal landed on her feet and the two managers had ‘words’. The crowd explodes with cheers as they get into a shoving match, and to the chants of “Catfight!”, ‘Sunburst’ and Crystal Cutglass have a small tussle at ringside. The referee leaves the ring(wouldn’t you?) and seperates the two, telling each to return to their own corner. Meanwhile Tom Landis places Marc Stevens on the turnbuckle and slams him down to the mat by way of a belly-to-belly superplex. He covers Stevens, but a kickout at two from Stevens prolongs the match. Next Tom takes Stevens down in a heap with a cartwheel kick, and starts to position Stevens for his finisher, the the Chicago Thunderbomb. Instead, Marc counters it with a snap suplex, and tells the crowd to shut up. Stevens goes for another, but Tom counters it with a vertical suplex and throws his enemy out of the ring. Landis bounces off the opposite ropes to get up a head of steam, and then catapults himself over the ropes with a somersault flying headbutt, knocking the two into the guardrail.] SS: Landis is nuts! He could have killed himself, or worse yet, killed his opponent. AM: It’s all in the name of the game, Sam. DR: And it was quite effective, by the looks of it. [Landis gets to his feet first again, and tosses Stevens back into the ring, while himself staying on the ring apron. He waits for him to stand, and then leaps onto the middle of the ropes for a springboard frankensteiner, and instead comes crashing down in a wicked powerbomb from Stevens. Too exhausted to cover him, Stevens lies next to Landis, each man breathing hard and trying to get back up. Tara encourages Landis to work to the ropes, while Crystal screams at Marc just to get up. Slowly each man works back up, and Stevens rushes Landis with an axehandle blow. It knocks him to the corner once more, and Stevens tries a cross corner whip but Landis reverses, sending the man from Asylum to the corner and flattening him with an avalanche. An inside cradle, and “Under Pressure” starts to play again.] TD: Here is your winner, as a result of a pinfall... TOM LANDIS! [Part 2] [As his music plays, Tom Landis stands up and raises his arm in victory, while Tara Marshall joins him in the ring. Stevens rolls out to the floor, and storms off to the locker rooms while Tom and Tara continue to celebrate.] SS: This is disgraceful, them carrying on like this. AM: Give me a break Sam, name one person who doesn’t celebrate when they win? And who can blame them, that win was tough, but well earned. DR: Precisely. Both men should be congratulated on a fantastic showing. And now- what? Oh no. Ladies and gentlemen, standing by at this time is the team known as Black Ice, and their new manager, Randolph Chalmers III. [The scene: A posh dressing room backstage. This is no ordinary dressing room, what with the beautiful textiled carpeting, incredibly expensive workout equipment and reclining leather chairs, 2 big screen TV's... you get the point. Black Diamond and ‘Iceman’ Greg Smith are standing right in the middle of the room, loving their new surroundings as the man that paid for them, Randolph Chalmers IIIrd, begins to speak, bringing back memories for old time UWF fans.] RC3: I left the UWF a long time ago, but I am as always a prudent businessman. When I see a commodity available with the strength [Big D. flexes], intensity [Smith stares right at the camera], and pure marketability of Black Ice, I buy very quickly. I only work with the best, and these two men are the BEST! Besides, how could I turn down a chance to make Legion look like even bigger fools? BD: Why did I do it? Just look at the accomodations I get now. [Big D. laughs] I'd have been an idiot to stay in Legion and ride coach everywhere I went. So Legion, it's time to pay the piper. It's time for you to answer to Black Ice. And anyone that's got tag gold, quit being pussies and sign the contracts. GS: Tom Landis, how I hate that name so... you paid the price, no pun intended, for unmasking Mr. Chalmers here last week. You lost the match to Black Ice, and your closest friend Randolph Chalmers IIIrd. I don't know what's with you Legion losers... you piss off Pride, you pissed off Chalmers Corp, and you _REALLY_ piss off Black Ice. I guess it's just in your nature. Well, in the end we stomped over you losers, and soon world tag gold will come out way, right RC? RC3: These two men are, and I guarantee this, future UWF World Tag Team champions. And when I guarantee something, you can take it to the bank. My bank! [Chalmers breaks down into diabolical laughter for a moment, so Greg Smith steps up to speak again.] GS: Ya know Tara, you should have picked me over Tom when you had your chance. You could have had a piece of not only one of the best wrestlers, but one half of the best tag team around! Black Ice baby. Tom, if you think you've pulled something big on us in the past, I think we just out-did ya by about 10,000 times! Ha ha! BD: Point blank. We are the best team EVER assembled. No one is going to stop us. Every match we have is just one step closer to the Hall of Fame. And you can bet on it. RC3: With their pure ability and my managerial skills, no one can stop us! But now, on to other business. Greg, you said something about me trying to get you a co-host position with Ashley Wilder...? [Fade out as all 3 discuss Ashley's, uh, merits. Return to a shot of the arena, where unannounced the Executive Aide, Kenny Carroll, and Reggie Martin walk down the ramp to huge pops, and head straight to the ring. They acknowledge the cheers from most of the crowd, but obviously are looking somewhat distracted. The EA picks up a house mic once they get in the ring.] EA: Well, by now, most of you have either seen Heat Wave or know what happened. Vladimir Karensky and Julio Sanchez were able to successfully steal the US tag titles from us, and now Karensky is parading them around like he won something. [Heel pop for the mention of Working Class. Carroll takes the mic.] KC: Now, it seems to be the trend in wrestling that when something like that happens, when somebody wrongs you. You go all out to destroy your enemy. Damn the rules, damn your conscience, just end up on top. [Crowd cheers. So far, this is sounding pretty good.] KC: I'm sorry, but we're not like that. Karensky, not a day goes by that we don't think about how pleasant it would be to destroy you for what you've done to Julio, and what you've done to us, and what you've said about the U.S. But we're not about to get dragged down to your level. These fans know that you didn't earn those titles, and no matter what you say, all you have is two empty, meaningless pieces of leather and metal. Someday very soon, Karensky, Julio is going to realize that, and when he does, you're going to be very sorry. [Martin takes the mic.] RM: But right now, it seems all there is out there for us to do is revenge. Revenge on Working Class. Revenge on Anarchy. Revenge on the Darknights. And even revenge on the Pride. That's right, Brett, I haven't forgotten. But we never joined the UWF to get revenge. We didn't become wrestlers to get revenge. We did this because we loved it, and we took pride in giving the fans our best every night, with the sole goal of being the top competitors in the sport. [The EA takes the mic, and pauses before continuing.] EA: Competing for competition's sake just doesn't seem to exist anymore. And none of us can justify spending time away from our families to be dragged down to the level of teams like Working Class and Anarchy. It is for that reason, that all three of us have decided to retire from active duty as wrestlers. [Surprised pop from the crowd at this announcement. Carroll takes the mic.] KC: But before we go, we want to remind everyone what the UWF used to be about. We don't want our last match to be a bitter war with a former friend. If they'll agree to it, we want our last memory of the UWF, and your last memory of us, to be another classic match between the Grapplers of Politics... and Lights, Camera, Action. [Crowd pops, remembering the history between the two teams.] RM: LCA, you always brought out the best in us. We've had ideological differences, but when we stepped through the ropes, we showed everyone what two great teams can do in the ring. What we want is everything you have, one last time. You'll get the same from us. After that match, whether we win, lose, or draw, we'll leave, knowing we set the right example for everyone else. EA: Devon, Frankie...how about it?
