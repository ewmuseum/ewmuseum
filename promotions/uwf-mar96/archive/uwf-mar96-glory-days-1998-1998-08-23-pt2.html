From the reports we have recieved you have claimed thet you are god's gift to the UWF among other things. Though many of us here know that when you say UWF you mean the Universal Wrestling Federation, we feel that just by saying the UWF, many people and especialy young children will think you are referring to the United Women's Foundation. We have watched some of the Universal Wrestling Federation broadcasts and were appalled at the activities you and your escort, one Miss Tawni, have done on camera and what have claimed to do off camera. In addition to that, we feel you having Tawni flaunt around like a sex object is demeaning to her and to women worldwide. It's men like you who push the women's movement back another 20 years. Sincerly, Mary Beth Vanderburg. Chairperson: United Women's Foundation... [Osbourne pulls off the glasses and folds them neatly to set them in his pocket. He then folds the letter and sets it back into the mail bag with the rest of the letters. He smiles.] RO: I appreciate the concern but let me give you the lecture. Tawni is not my slave. She's not my property at all. She's with me at her own choice. She's with me because she wants to be and any action she does, she does of her own free will. She does what she does because she wants to. I don't tell her what to do. She's as free and liberated as anyone. In fact she read the letter and she thought she'd do something special for you. Tawni. [Tawni begins to walk down the ramp, untieing the belt on her robe. She gets the straps undone and pulls away the robe revelaing an EXTREMELY skimpy black bikini, setting the male segment of the audiance off with a wild pop. She walks down the ramp like a model, turns and heads right back up, putting the robe on.] RO: Like I said she does what she wants. Tawni, do you have any words for the Foundation. Tawni: Maybe. Hold open the mailbag. [Osbourne opens up the mailbag as Tawni reaches into her robe pocket. She fumbles around for a second and finally pulls out a book of matches. Both Osbourne and Tawni smile as Tawni opens the book and pulls out a match. She strikes it and watches as it is set a flame. She then tosses the match into the mailbag, setting all the hate mail on fire. Osbourne quickly sets the bag aside on the ramp, safe away from anyone.] RO: That's my girl. But back to the matter at hand. [Tawni hooks her arm with Osoburne's as they both watch the flaming pile of mailbag.] RO: Tonight, it's first blood. Somehow that's very appropriate for a couple of guys like me and "Crimson" Joe Reed. And you know, I don't think that matters. You want to know what matters? Of course not. Nothing matters to anyone here. But I'll tell you what matters to me. I watched a tape of the Brawl from the Mall shortly after the show ended and I went back to my hotel room. I sat there and watched quietly as the Lady in Red picked up a video of "Wrestling's Bloodiest Matches". I'll be damned if she calls me lucky again. I'll be damned if _ANYONE_ else calls me lucky again. Over and over I here things that somehow state that I can never be "Crimson" Joe Reed's better. All I ever here is that somehow I can never be or become "Crimson" Joe Reed's equal. I'm always the guy who got lucky one time. [Tawni's expresion slowly goes from joy to worry as Osbourne slowly makes him self more agitated. As her expression changes, she slowly pulls away from Osbourne, who doesn't seem to notice as he is engrosed in his growing aggravation.] RO: There have been four encounters between Joe Reed and I. The first was a tag team match that ended with a no contest. Then there was that infamous bloody match in February of 1998 where I beat him for the AEWA World Title. Thats supposedly when I got pretty damned lucky. Then in early April of 1998, I showed up as his his mystery challenger for the EMWC Television Title. Things were going good and apparently everybody thought I was going to have another lucky day. That was up until the Lady in Red decided to hit me in the back with a steel chair to get Reed DQed. Then there was the Brawl in the Mall. Sweet Madness beet Reed and the LiR. So what's the record? For me against Joe Reed in any sort of match, it's three wins, no loses and one draw. How is it that I'm only lucky? Only lucky? Help me to understand! Not once have you beaten me Joe. Explain that to me! I'm sure you have the answers. [Tawni's expression goes from worry to fear.] RO: Your blood is on my hands Reed. And I'd gladly do it again if the situation were to arise. And I know that given the chance you'd do it to me, because yes, your hands are stained with my blood as well. I like everyone else, is quick to point out that I bit a piece of your face off. I remember. I always remember. I had it bronzed. As a matter of fact I have it on a chain and it's hanging around my neck. But no one else remembers. All I have to do is point to my temple. Remember that Reed? Thats where you took the end of a broken table leg and dug into my skull. You're not so innocent Reed. You'll do what you have to do to hurt me, but you have to remember that I'll try to do it to you first. Because I can. Because I want to and because I will. [Osbourne momentarily pauses to rub his temple as Tawni looks on in fear.] RO: Explain this to me Reed. Why are you made out to be such the hero? You're just like the rest of us? You break bones. You crack ribs. You're only cheered by the same fans who turned their backs on me so long ago. The Era of Madness began with you and me and I'll declare that tonight, win or lose, the Era of Madness ends. I will no longer settle to be considered your inferior. I will be an equal. If I have to make you bleed then the crimson liquid that runs through your veins will flow freely. The man they call the underdog won't be the Madman. The man you face tonight won't be the guy who has the fractured personality that switches from place to place. Tonight you face the meanest and angriest Randall Osbourne ever. My hands will be stained with your blood again. [SLAP! From out of nowhere, Tawni pushes away her fear and slaps Osbourne across the face hoping to get him back down from his agitation.] Tawni: _RANDALL_! Calm down! You're hysterical! RO: I'm not hysterical. I'm only bloodthirsty. [With that Randall Osbourne turns and walks away to the entrance and disappears with no music playing, leaving the audience and Tawni a bit suprised at the man's sudden change.] DR: Fans, we'll be back in just a minute. [Fade.]
