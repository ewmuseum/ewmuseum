Introducing first, on his way to the ring... [The limo pulls to a stop at the side of the aisleway, and the driver exits the car, walks to the back and opens the driver's side rear door. Stepping out of the limousine, of course, is Edmond Winston IV who tips a polite hand to the jeering crowd. He is wearing his usual Doc Martens, cargo pants, and a light yellow polo shirt.] DH: ...from Winston-Salem, North Carolina and currently residing in Martha's Vineyard, Massachusetts... EDMOND WINSTON THE FOUUUUUUUUUUURRRTH! [EW4 rolls into the ring and gets to his feet, looking back to the entrance with defiance and yet at the same time a calm, calculating look.] AM: Winston might look like a million bucks, but he's still got to be feeling the effects of the Youngblood doublecross from last week. Alex Extreme tried to put up a fight and look what it got him. DR: ... AM: What's wrong? DR: Sorry, I was waiting for Sam to jump in there and call Alex Extreme a washed up porn star. [As the crowd's hatred dies down a little, "Bleed for Me" by the Black Label Society hits the speakers and now the arena erupts into a huge series of pops!] DH: And his opponent, hailing from Dallas, Texas... "DOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOMSAYEEEEEEEERRRRRRRRRR" KYYYYYYYYYYYYYLLLLE LLLLLLLLLLLLLLEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEE! [The Doomsayer enters the arena dressed in black tights, trimmed with dark blue and his kneepads and boots are also in the dark blue colour. Fingerless gloves complete the outfit, along with a t-shirt with the MBC logo on the front. Once the introductory screams of "Bleed for Me" give way to the chorus of the song, Lee walks down the ramp eyeing his opponent in the ring. Edmond glares back at Kyle, both men realizing the gravity of the situation and what's at stake. As Lee approaches the ring, his music fades, and then gets replaced by the introductions of the guest referee for the match. The lights drop out all around the arena and an eerie guitar piece comes in with the bells, cueing a massive heel pop from the crowd.] DR: It's rare that a referee is brought out after the participants of a match, but then again this referee happens to be the current heavyweight champion of the world also. AM: And that's what makes this match even more interesting. Augustine hates both of these guys, and if Kyle can pull off a defeat of Winston tonight, it'll be these same three guys in the Gold Rush main event. [The drums kick in as fireworks are set off, and "Hell's Bells" by AC/DC starts to play full blast as the audience releases a huge heel pop, save for the wild cheering of the Children of the Evolution! The CoE have managed to shove their way up to ringside as usual, and a familiar banner is hung by the ringsteps that reads "Youngblood Law applies HERE". Augustine emerges from the back, dressed in black jeans and boots, with a white "Youngblood Law" t-shirt. Augustine also has the championship draped over his shoulder, and makes sure to show it off to both Lee and Winston before entering the ring and flipping both men off.] AM: I think that's how Augustine likes to signal for the bell. DR: It wouldn't surprise me. [True enough, the bell rings and almost immediately Lee opens up on Winston with a series of punches, rattling Big Tobacco until he's off-balance and stumbling into the nearest corner. But a rake of the eyes turns that tide, and a pair of hard chops has the Doomsayer gasping for breath. A whip to the ropes and Edmond goes for a shoulderblock, but Kyle counters with a leaping forearm that knocks the businessman to the mat to a huge pop from the crowd.] DR: The early-going is all Kyle Lee, and I think you might be right about last week's attack still impacting EW4, Amy. SS: Amy right? That'd be a first. AM: Yippee, he's back. [Augustine admonishes Lee as he stomps away while Edmond holds the ropes, and gets right in the Doomsayer's face. Kyle looks like he wants to pop Augustine, but holds off and grabs Edmond again for an irish whip. Now it's Edmond's turn to counter, sending Kyle into the mat by the back of the head off of an attempted backdrop. A sharp kick to the gut and Edmond quickly grabs a thigh, smashing it repeatedly with his knee before hooking on a single leg crab. Kyle crawls quickly to the ropes, and Augustine starts to put a count on the former world champion. A slow count, reaching four after about an extra half minute. The audience lets Augustine know what they think by showering the guest referee with boos.] AM: Uh oh, I was afraid of this. He's so intent to keep the belt that he's going to let Edmond walk all over Kyle, even with the hatred he has for Winston. DR: No question it was a slow count, and now it's the Doomsayer on the receiving end of this match. [Kyle crawls to the corner, but is staggered thanks to an earringer and Edmond starts to whip him cross-corner only to smash him with a short-arm elbow. An atomic drop leaves Kyle on rubber legs, and Edmond hooks him for a suplex but it's Lee who after blocking hits a Northern Lights suplex of his own into a pinfall attempt. 1! 2! Shoulder up! Augustine raises two fingers in the air, as Lee slowly rises with Edmond on his tail.] AM: Wait a second, that count... it was fair. SS: You expect any less? AM: Well... yes. [Kyle hooks Edmond with a double underhook and tries to hit a butterfly suplex, and Winston grabs the ropes to block it and sends Lee crashing to the outside apron with a backdrop. The Doomsayer stays on the apron though and blocks a punch from Edmond, hooks him and attempts to suplex Big Tobacco all the way to the cement floor. EW4 again blocks it, and a sharp uppercut frees him and allows Edmond to pieface Lee to the ground! HEEL POP!] SS: Now that's a message sent and delivered. Kyle Lee just isn't in the same class as Edmond Winston IV. DR: Winston to the apron, is he setting up for his patented running superkick? AM: He tried, but Kyle sidestepped him! Neckbreaker on the floor! [Winston is groggy on the outside, as Lee sits up with a satisfied grin on his face. He starts to pull Edmond up, but with a desperate shove EW4 knocks Kyle into the side of the ringsteps headfirst! _CRASH!_ And now an unbelievably loud round of boos starts up, as Lee slumps to the floor next to Winston, neither man able to get up right now.] DR: A desperate Winston now trying to buy himself some time, but Augustine's already up to four with his count. AM: Oh no, he's the type of guy who'd declare it a double countout all so he doesn't have to defend that belt against two men at the PPV. Somebody get up and break that count! [Augustine continues to count as neither man seems capable of rising back up... 7... 8... 9... 10... The crowd boos, until it realizes that Augustine is still going. 11... 12... Winston rolls back into the ring with Lee a few seconds behind.] DR: Clearly Michael Augustine just wants to see both men destroy each other, with these extra long counts. SS: Hand it to him, he's one of the most intelligent champions we've ever had. AM: Intelligent isn't all that far from psychotic, at least in his case. SS: Whatever works for him. [Edmond rises to his feet before Kyle and tries to lock on his cobra clutch sleeperhold, but the Doomsayer senses what is coming and backs right up into the corner, squashing EW4 and freeing himself quickly. A pair of knife-edge chops complete with "Whoo!"s from the crowd softens Edmond up to be put on the turnbuckles for a superplex attempt, but another rake of the eyes slows him down. Edmond starts to turn around, pulling Lee up and into a side headlock...] DR: High risk situation here, I think Edmond Winston is trying to set up for a bulldog all the way to the floor! Fist to the ribcage by the Doomsayer- AM: FINAL TEMPTATION! [Lee's reverse superplex leaves Edmond curled up in a fetal position and clutching his stomach, as Kyle grabs the ropes and slowly drags himself back up. Signalling for the Gone To Texas, he brings Edmond to his knees and starts to pick him up... But Edmond traps his opponent in a triangle choke, and quickly brings Lee to the mat!] DR: Submission hold applied by Big Tobacco, Lee needs to find a way to escape or he'll be unconscious very soon... And Kyle to the ropes! [Augustine demands that Winston release his submission hold, but the New Era leader ignores him until Augustine physically drags him off of his foe. Now EW4 rises and gets right in the face of the guest referee... And takes a swing! Augustine ducks, and to a tremendous heel pop flattens Winston with it! Augy cradles his hand, having used the one that was injured by the Waterford Crystal all those months ago as it's now a race between Winston and Lee to get up.] DR: What an equalizer, Michael Augustine may have turned the tide of the match himself! Rollup by Lee! ONE! TWO! KICKOUT! AM: Edmond still has a bit of fight left in him, unfortunately. [Lee rolls to his feet, grabbing Edmond in a headlock but Winston grasps for the ropes and tries to get free. Now it's Lee who refuses to let go, and just like before Augustine physically tears him away from Winston. The two have a tense staredown... Before the Doomsayer ducks a punch from the referee and nails Augustine between the eyes! HUGE POP!] SS: DISQUALIFY HIM! HE PUT HIS HANDS ON A REFEREE! GONE TO TEXAS! AUGUSTINE IN POSITION... ONE! TWO! THREE! [The crowd roars to life as "Bleed For Me" comes over the PA system, and Kyle Lee rolls to the side of the ring, arm raised in victory.
