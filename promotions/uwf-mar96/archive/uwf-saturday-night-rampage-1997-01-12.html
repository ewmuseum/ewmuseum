[Part 1] [Voice of Amy Marshall] This past Tuesday night, Serge Annis and Bishop's ongoing fued came to a head as they confronted each other with words...Bishop came out early on, and promised to watch the back of the Epitome of Evil...Serge responded shortly before his match by stating that he didn't need the 'petty services' of Bishop...Bishop proved his word to be true when he defended Serge against the nWd, and the two men took on Raekwon and Shaolin and held them back...Shortly after, the nWd challenged them to a two on two, no rules tag match against Bishop and Serge, who both agreed...Tonight, we'll have news concerning these two men, and also a strap match between Youth Gone Wild and Chronosphere... [The opening for Rampage is shown, while Tina Turner's "Simply the Best" is the background music. The camera fades up on the inside of GM Place in Vancouver, BC. David Rogers and Sam Steeley are at ringside, while the fans around them are going crazy.] David Rogers "Hello fans! Welcome to another edition of Saturday Night Rampage, the predecessor to Shotgun Saturday Night! I'm David Rogers, and with me is Sam Steeley." Sam Steeley "And all you fans out there get to gaze upon yours truly. Talk about lucky people." DR "Only those who don't have TV's Sam. We have an explosive lineup scheduled, and it starts with another debut." SS "My god, there's a lot of them lately Rogers. What's Paul Reed done?" DR "You mean president Reed? He's brought in a steady stream of talented newcomers, that's what. And this man that we are about to see is no different, his name is Arch Angel." SS "I've heard of this guy before. "The Purifier from the Astral Plains" they call him. Here's a hot tidbit for you people to chew on, it seems that the alliance between Desperado and Stephan LeRoc now also includes this man, Arch Angel. They're rumored to be calling their stable "Judge, Jury, Executioner". And who would be their manager, but Luscious Lydia Anderson." DR "Gee, there's a connection." ========================= ARCH ANGEL VS JOB HOLLY ========================= To start the entrance of Arch Angel, the music heard was "Smells Like Teen Spirit" by Nirvana. As the music started, small white lights crept up around the arena, and Arch Angel walked down to the ring. He stepped through the ropes, and stood in the center of the ring. He looked up, and a blinding white flash filled the building for a moment, and then the bell rang. Job Holly was still wincing from the white light, and was unable to defend himself against three elbowsmashes in the corner. Arch Angel lifted Job Holly over his head and gorilla pressed him to the mat, then landed a legdrop across the jobber's throat. He turned him over and started to pin Holly, but pulled his opponent off his shoulders by the hair. A couple of slaps from Arch Angel, and then he set Holly on the top rope. Arch Angel himself stood on the second rope, and delivered a razor's edge from an elevated position. He called this move the Purification, and then covered Holly, hooking the leg. A three count came, and Arch Angel was victorious in his debut. WINNER: ARCH ANGEL DR "This guy is kind of a reverse Darksider, isn't he?" SS "He's domineering!" DR "He's seen the movie "Michael" a few too many times. I mean, Arch Angel?" SS "Maybe he laughs at the name David Rogers. Anyways, you were talking about the Darksider. He's got a message to deliver to Serge Annis, as well as Bishop." ````````````````````````````````````````````````````````````````````````` (camera fades in on total darkness; a match is lit and lights a candle) Darksider: Bishop, I have given you my hand in friendship and you throw it away. None of these fans can save him from the beating he's gonna take." ============================= TOM LANDIS VS STEPHAN LEROC ============================= "La Marsellaise" came over the speaker system first, and the procession of the former French Connection walked down the aisle. Lydia Anderson led the way(which explained the catcalls coming from the stands), while Marty "The Manipulator" Stevens and Stephan LeRoc followed. LeRoc got into the ring and waited for his opponent while a chant of "USA" resounded throughout the crowd. Then ZZ Top's "Under Pressure" came over the loudspeakers, and the entrance of two members of Legion was marked by a thunderous pop from the crowd. Tom Landis and his manager, Tara "Sunburst" Marshall, walked out of the dressing rooms and to the ring, where LeRoc was waiting. He kicked Landis in the face as the legionnaire entered the ring, and then dropped several fists onto the top of his head. An elbowdrop caused Landis to lie motionless on the mat, while Stephan went to the top rope. At this point Hotspot appeared on the video wall. 'Hotspot': "Hey Leroc, stop losing and listen up!!! You have a partner, Desperado. Yeah, you two are _real_ tough. I got a partner... how about you against us at the next Pay Per View? Not that I didn't enjoy kicking in your ass at Holiday Havoc, but now I've got to kick yours and Desperado's arce's in! Stop looking at the Sillicone Slut over there and you think long and hard about my little offer. Oh, and watch out, you're about to get beat." Landis superplexed LeRoc down, but it was LeRoc who landed on top of the legionnaire. LeRoc again climbed to the top, and a jack-knife splash would have ended the match, except that Landis was playing dead and raised his knees at the last second. LeRoc rolled around in pain, and Landis got back on his feet. He taunted Lydia and Stevens, causing the crowd to pop big time. LeRoc managed to get back on his own feet, only to be snap mared over. LeRoc was able to regain the control in the match when he thumbed Landis in the eyes, and then clawed at his face. Sunburst cried out when that happened, and Lydia was quick to laugh at her for it. LeRoc hiptossed Landis before taking his legs out, and when Landis was back on the mat, he executed a kneelock submission hold. Landis was next whipped into the corner, and he got hooked into an armbar submission hold. He released Landis who was dizzy, and delivered flying headscissors to again bring the former world champion down. A crucifix by LeRoc drained more of Landis's strength, but Tom was able to get back into the match with a double underhook backbreaker. This drew a gasp from the crowd, and a second backbreaker resulted in an armdrag takedown. An armbar submission stepped over the line, because LeRoc again trapped Landis in flying headscissors. LeRoc was again all over Landis and executed legdrops, headbutts, and a northern lights suplex. The cocky frenchman covered Tom Landis and expected to get the easy pin, but a kickout shocked him. LeRoc irish whipped Landis to the ropes, but Marty Stevens pulled the ropes down and Landis fell out to the floor. LeRoc was quick to follow and rammed him headfirst into the steel steps, but himself felt steel as well when Landis reversed a whip into the ringpost. Marty Stevens attempted to KO Landis with the quebec flag, only to have it stolen by Landis and smacked across his own backside. Landis seemed to be back in control, when: SS "Look out! It's the Badboy!" DR "What's Acorn doing out here?" Randy Acorn aka The BadBoy, fresh from the locker rooms greeted Landis from behind with a knee in the back. He picked up a chair and drove it onto Landis's back, then concentrated on the leg. He put Landis on his stomach and then slapped on the Newark Knife for a full two minutes. The referee saw none of this as Stephan LeRoc was occupying his attention, and Acorn rolled Landis back into the ring once Tom's leg was pretty much useless. LeRoc knew the time was right and simply covered Landis, getting the victory. WINNER: STEPHAN LEROC LeRoc and the gang decided to call it a night, and left while Landis continued to roll around in pain. Acorn hopped into the ring and stole the microphone from the ring announcer, drawing much heat for it. RA: You know what Tommyboy. You may have thought by ignoring my presence in the UWF that I might just go away and then you wouldn't have to worry about me. Well, I didn't forget about you and I'm sure as hell not going away. This was just a little reminder that you still have a contract that needs to be signed and oh.....what's this?? [Randy reaches back into his pocket and pulls out a wrinkled piece of paper] Oh my god, here's that contract that your signature's been dodging for so long. Well, here's your chance to sign it, are you going to?? [Randy laughs at Landis as Landis tries to get at Randy but he falls from the pain that the Newark Knife has caused on his legs] Well, it doesn't seem like you're in the wrestling mood or the signing mood for that matter today so maybe that little girl that walks you down the aisle over there would like to sign it for you. Well, it's up to you, right now, I'm leaving because I have better things to do then sit here and wait for the ambulance to get you. Randy throws the mike down and hops out of the ring. As he walks past Landis, he kicks him one more time and then throws the contract down into his lap. Acorn walks out with a smile on his face while taunting as many fans as possible along the way. Sunburst and the referee had to help Landis walk back up the aisle, and his leg was supported by the referee. Tom was careful to take the contract with him though. DR "That is despicable. Randy Acorn is scum if he has to draw attention to himself like that." SS "Landis stuck his nose in the Badboy's business at Holiday Havoc. He tried to steal Randy's interview time." DR "That was Tom's time and you know it." SS[with fingers in ears] "Lalalala, I can't hear you." ````````````````````````````````````````````````````````````````````````` [Camera picks up the Twin Ninja's arguing in a hall way on Jan 1st, an hour after Alex Kidd's victory party. The GOP entered first to "America The Beautiful" and received a half pop from the crowd, or at least it's republican members. Then Padded Room made their way out to the ring. David Rogers was stunned to hear a polite pop for the duo from Asylum. DR "What is happening? Padded Room have fans? I guess when it's them or the nWd, you take the UWF faithful wrestlers." Marc Maniac and Maxx Karnage stormed the ring, and a brawl between all four men ensued. Kenny Carroll took Marc Maniac over the ropes with a ropeflip, but Reggie Martin was less fortunate, being ropeflipped out to the floor(and that's a long way down) by Maxx. Inside the ring Kenny had Maniac in an armlock, and then dropped a leg onto it. Maxx was beating up on Reggie, and put him in a boston crab on the floor. Kenny Carroll finally had his attention drawn to his partner by the Executive Aide. He exited the ring and tore Maxx off Reggie, bulldogging him onto the floor. He checked on Reggie, and helped him to his feet. Marc Maniac pulled both GOP'ers back into the ring by the hair, and tried to smash their heads together. Instead they blocked it and whipped Marc to the ropes, delivering a double clothesline that sent him over the ropes. At this time, Maxx crawled back into the ring and Reggie left, allowing the match to finally begin. An abdominal stretch from Carroll only got him hiptossed, and Maxx quickly dropped him with a back suplex. He resorted back to normal tactics and tossed him to the floor, then came out to meet him. Carroll attempted a hotshot, and was met instead with a lariat. He was gorilla pressed onto the guardrail, and then Maxx rolled him back into the ring. Carroll kept on rolling and got over to Reggie, bringing a fresh(er) man into the ring. Reggie stomped Maxx in the stomach, then executed a powerslam. This got the crowd into the match as they chanted "U-S-A", spurring the GOP on. It also prompted the Twin Ninjas to come out, and Matt Richards seemed to be in scouting mode. Chris Nelson however did not, he instead was attempting to get his partner to leave. Eventually though Nelson gave up and also started watching the match. Meanwhile, Maxx made it to his own corner and tagged in Maniac, and again a fresh(er) man entered. Maniac shoulderblocked Reggie, then placed him in a chinlock deathlock submission hold. Reggie struggled to the ropes, and took Maniac out with a modified fireman's carry over the top rope. Reggie came off the ropes and flew at Maniac with a NEWTron bomb, but missed and hit the cement. Maniac pushed Reggie back in through the ropes and tagged Maxx in, and both members of Padded Room lifted Reggie up in a gorilla press. Kenny Carroll stood on the top rope, and Maxx was the first to take a missle kick in the face. Carroll quickly dumped him over the ropes and Marc Maniac was left in the ring against both of the GOP. Reggie was able to help Kenny double clothesline Maniac, and then he watched as the Voting Block sealed the win for the GOP. C O M M E R C I A L [Part 2] ==================================== RICKY JOHNSON VS DESPERADO-NO DQ'S ==================================== Desperado headed to the ring before Ricky, and the crowd was still semi-surprised to see Lydia Anderson escorting him and Billy the Kid to the ring. "Misery" played over the loudspeaker, and the crowd booed at Desperado. Ricky Johnson was obviously their choice, and when "The Distance" by Cake came on the speakers, an electrified pop spread from the crowd. Out walked both Ricky and Chris Scottsdale, who slapped hands with the fans all the way to the ring. Desperado taunted Ricky from inside the ring, and Lydia mockingly blew both Scottsdale and Johnson kisses. Ricky rushed for the ring and slid under the ropes, causing Luscious Lydia to head for the hills. It was Ricky who started out well, with an elbowsmash and superkick. It led to a spinebuster, and the crowd could sense a quick ending. Instead, Desperado countered a backdrop driver with a backwards kick. A single leg takedown from Desperado set Ricky up for a bridging back suplex. He attempted to rake Ricky's face and was slapped instead. An avalanche by Ricky turned the tables but a samoan drop brought the match back to Desperado with a counter into an elbowsmash. A crucifix by Desperado brought him right back into a samoan drop by Johnson. They continued to go back and forth as Desperado slammed him with a side suplex. He double axehandle smashed Ricky, and pushed him to the floor. Billy the Kid took it upon himself to attack Ricky, but took a blow to the midsection rather hard. Desperado tried to sneak up on Ricky, but Ricky turned in time. Desperado grabbed Lydia and held her in front of him, and Ricky turned away in disgust. Johnson re-entered the ring and waited for Desperado to do so. Desperado got into a shoving match with Chris Scottsdale and only a hair pull by Ricky brought him back to the squared circle. Ricky had Desperado under control and was on his way to getting him in the Gut Breaker when he took an elbowsmash to the face. Desperado pitched him off the top rope and tossed him down to the mat and steadied himself on the top rope. Chris Scottsdale turned the tables on him, literally as a back suplex from the turnbuckle through a table sent the two to the floor(think Mankind-Shawn Michaels).
