Landis tried pulling out of his tailspin with a double reverse chinlock, but Chronosphere countered it with a facerake and threw Landis over the ropes. He came back in at the five count, and Chrono tried to grab him with a DDT right away, but Tom Landis countered it with a backdrop. Back in the swing of things, Landis executed a legsweep before getting pummeled by Chrono. 'Sphere nailed him with a pair of dropkicks, then a slingshot splash and finally cradled Landis with a German suplex. Again Tom got out of the pin, and Chronosphere attempted a moonsault, but Landis rolled out of the way and then cinched in an armlock hammerlock submission. Chrono broke the hold and Landis caught him in an inside cradle of his own, but Chrono kicked out at two and threw Chronosphere out of the ring and jumped onto him with a twisting elbow, then threw him back into the ring and dropped him on his head with a piledriver, but Chronosphere still managed to kick out just before the three count. Finally, Landis executed a Soviet suplex on Chronosphere, and gave the sign for the Chicago Thunderbomb. He executed the front-layout tigerbomb on the Asylumite, and not even Marc Stevens, who rushed out to the ring at the last second, could save him from being pinned: 1, 2, 3.
