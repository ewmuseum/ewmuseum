UWF Tuesday Night Thrashings, 23 September 1997

23 September 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Fade up from black to an exterior shot of Madison Square Gardens, followed by clips from Saturday night. ‘War Machine’ by KISS starts up, and fast clips of the Kyle Hatcher-Daniel Kidd contest roll.] DR: New York City, the city that never sleeps. After this weekend at MSG, neither will the UWF. On a night that Kyle Hatcher finally found his answer with the Pride... [After seeing the entire Pride attack Hatcher, clips of Twister’s match against the Twin Ninjas are shown.] DR: The CWAA tag champions, Twister, proved that they are a force to be reckoned with... [The Fabulous Ones interfere, causing the Ninjas to exit the match.] DR: And the Fabulous Ones made Chris Nelson and Matt Richards sit up and take notice... [Now a montage from the Jericho-Riley match rolls.] DR: The world was treated to two outstanding matchups that should receive many a vote for match of the year. Sebastian Jericho and Jack Riley took each other to the absolute limit in a submission contest... [cut to Serge Annis and Joe Reed struggling over the baseball bat] and then the UWF’s ‘Epitome of Evil’ battled ‘Crimson’ Joe Reed in what was a brutal contest, and one that raised the standard for streetfights everywhere. [Cut to clips of the TNT parodies from Steve Armstrong and Ginger, then interrupted by a shot of Shawn Vexton executing the Pride and Pain.] DR: Tonight, Steve Armstrong and Shawn Vexton will do battle, with the good name of Ashley Wilder hanging in the balance. Plus the debut of a mysterious new force named Entrophy, and Eddie Fantastic faces Tom Landis in his first real test since coming back to the UWF... [Fade into the opening for UWF Tuesday Night Thrashings. We can hear “Hard To Make A Stand” by Sheryl Crow in the background as clips of UWF wrestlers and their finishers flash by, three at a time on a mirrored cube. The first three show the Sledge Hammer Submission, the Kentucky Backsmasher through a table on the floor, and then the Reformation reverse DDT. A slow dissolve on each side shows new shots, of the double Superkick by the Twinjas, and Deadly Intentions attack their partner Robbie Greer, and then finally the Bulldog Supreme from Brewster Cogburn to Ricky Reno. The next dissolve melts the screen, and into view comes the UWF Championship belt by itself, hung up over the ring from last month’s PPV. The music begins to trail off as the image dissolves again, into the parking lot of the Pepsi Arena in Albany, New York. Row upon row of cars are shown, and finally two large mobile editing studio trucks come into view. The red truck bears the familiar UWF logo, and the dark blue truck has some sort of weird looking symbol that looks like a W and an F put together. Cut to another camera angle, inside the UWF truck. In front of a large editing bay, Ashley Wilder is seen, wearing a white Pride t-shirt and a black UWF nylon pullover. She smiles for the camera as always, and pulls down a lever slowly as as the background music fades out.] AW: Hello Wilder Zone fans, I’m your goddess, Ashley Wilder. As you obviously can see, Thrashings is a little bit different this week. It would seem that some silly twit double-booked the Pepsi Arena tonight, with our show and some other wrestling federation. We were able to reach a deal though, as the matches for TNT went on as scheduled earlier today, and this worldwide leader in sports entertainment group is in there right now. Oh well, this way I can make sure that ‘Dartboard’ Steve Armstrong doesn’t sneak in another of those segments that he’s so fond of. I’m totally in control now, and there’s nothing the UWF can do about it. As Dave Rogers said at the top of the show, Armstrong is going to be given a one-way ticket to the hospital tonight, with a little less pride and a lot more pain. Shawn Vexton is in the groove lately, and he’ll steamroll little Stevie later. I haven’t even had a chance to see the matches tonight, because I had the chance to talk to one of the employees from this other federation. I tell you, she’s one smart cookie. A little obvious with the hair colouring, but still a great person. We’re going to start things out tonight with the first appearance of a man who goes by the name of Entrophy. In case you missed what he had to say a couple of weeks ago, he named Joe Reed, Alex Kidd, and Serge Annis as part of his triangle of pain. What are his motives? Let’s find out. ================================ ENTROPHY versus BLOCKY JOBAVIA ================================ Blocky came out with the entire Nation of Stagnation, including Jobrooq, D-Lo Job, and Joba Mujobba. The crowd began a “Blocky sucks!” chant, which angered the NOS member enough to start crying in the ring. While this happened, smoke billowed out from under the ring and engulfed the squared circle. When it cleared, a large man wearing a dark blue mask with a black cross over the face stood behind Blocky, who was totally unaware. The man spun Blocky around and grabbed him in a belly to belly suplex, then grabbed his leg and dropped an elbow to the inside of Jobavia’s thigh. He twisted Blocky into a figure four leglock, which according to the brief bio notes was called the End. Blocky was unfortunately close to the ropes and caused the break, but this good luck hit a brick wall with an irish whip into the ropes, leading to a wicked spinebuster, and the pinfall over Blocky Jobavia. WINNER: Entrophy AW: I don’t know about you people, and really I don’t care, but Entrophy looks about as good as he’s going to get, with a win over Blocky Jobavia. This guy doesn’t have it, and he’ll be gone in a month, guaranteed. There is however a silver lining to that dark cloud, and here he is... Bobby Rain! [Standing in front of the camera, and behind the enormous UWF Logo is the newest addition to the ranks of the Universal Wrestling Federation in Bobby Rain who is wearing a pair of faded and ripped blue jeans, black work boots and a brand new UWF T-Shirt, his brown hair dyed blonde with the original color of his roots beginning to show is covered by a black bandana as well as a short one and a half foot black pearl rod with a pewter dragon head on the tip. Standing next to Bobby Rain is the absolutely drop dead gorgeous red haired golden brown body of Rachel Kennedy who is wearing a blue blouse and a black mini-skirt.] Bobby Rain: You know it's great!! After all those years of toiling down in the bush leagues in the New England Pro Wrestling and the Ultimate Championship Wrestling I FINALLY get my call to head straight for the Universal Wrestling Federation! No more starving for nights on end as I try and earn my keep around the locker room! No more will I, Bobby Rain the best man for the job in any line of work be cleaning the wrestling ring for a few extra bucks!! For you see ... as my dear Rachel Kennedy here will tell you ... things are a changing ... AIN'T THAT RIGHT RACHEL?!? Rachel Kennedy: That's right Bobby ... from now on we're taking the high road! It's about time that a real woman of my absolute beauty be given the treatment that I should of been receiving since I became involved in the wrestling industry! Why Bobby ... you and me will indeed become the premier duo here in the UWF! Bobby Rain: YOU HERE THAT?! Rachel says that the time has come for me to get down and dirty with the big boys from the UWF! And you know something ... I just might do that! With Rachel in my corner and Lord Almighty looking out above the skies will be free for the dreaded storm cloud to spread a little RAIN out on each and every one of the UWF Superstars parade!!! Rachel Kennedy: Haha ... and we sure wouldn't want to Rain on anyone's parade! Bobby Rain: But we will ... and that's the best part about it! There ain't nothing to stop the deadly duo of this poison ivy beside me and the acid that I carry. From now on the UWF forecast calls for Rain ... and plenty of it! [Cut back to Ashley in the truck.] AW: There’s a real cruiserweight. With Dan Kidd as the champion, and Mr. Rain coming in to add class to the division, we could be alright afterall. [From inside the Pepsi Arena, the camera picks up the sound of glass breaking, followed by a strangely familiar guitar beat. The sound of the crowd exploding with cheers puzzles Ashley.] AW: Hmm, wonder what that’s all about? Oh well, on to another of tonight’s great matches. Working Class was out in style, in fact I’m told that they demanded two of America’s defenders to face them in the ring, and got them. Let’s go to the footage. [As the Russian march dies down, Vladimir Karensky walks stiffly to the center of the ring, while Julio "stands guard" on the ring apron. Standing guard consists mostly of insulting the fans and trying to get them not to chant "USA!" Karensky asks for a mic and is promptly given one.] VK: Ever since the Working Class became champions in the UWF, we have come to a decision. That decision is to bring honour to our United States titles by defending them against the best competition the UWF has to offer, which is why we chose the team we did to wrestle tonight. We mulled it over for a long time... should it be the Twin Ninjas? [Heel pop!] Twister? [Big face pop!] Or maybe a return match with the GOP? [Earth-shattering pop that only the possibility of a return match can bring.] There are many teams that might merit a shot, but none have showed the skill of the team I'm about to introduce... a team that defends the United States and everything it stands for. Without further adieu, here are: The Man They Call Jobber and the Idiot, Del Jobkes! [“The Star Spangled Banner” begins to play, as the crowd lets loose with a huge pop, not believing that it is the team Karensky spoke of. All hopes were dashed when indeed, The Man They Call Jobber comes out first, and is followed by a USA flag-waving man wearing a mask.] TD: Here are their opponents... THE MAN THEY CALL JOBBER, AND THE ‘IDIOT’ DEL JOBKES! ============================================ WORKING CLASS VERSUS TMTC JOBBER/THE IDIOT ============================================ As soon as the bulbous TMTC Jobber hit the ring, both Julio and Vladimir started to lay knees into him, while the Idiot Del Jobkes enters the ring without so much as a whimper. He tries to help his teammate by punching Sanchez from behind, but Julio spins around with a kick to the jaw, leaps up and grabs his neck by Julio’s legs, and frankensteiners him down. Vladimir meanwhile continues to kick away at the larger jobber, then pulls him up by the head and grabs him in a side headlock. Julio bounces off the ropes and dropkicks TMTC Jobber straight on the top of the head, and climbs to one turnbuckle, as Karensky does to the other. A double flying headbutt results in the three count, a celebration for Working Class, and a major headache for the fans in the crowd. WINNERS: Working Class [Cut to the truck, and Ashley is watching an old episode of Seinfeld. Kramer slides into Jerry’s apartment, which knocks into George and sends him to the floor.] AW: God what a loser. [mocking] Vandelay Industries, Vandelay Industries. Idiot. [Notices the red blinking light on the camera] Oh, uh, what a great match. I’m sorry, but even with Working Class in it, those two punching bags made me lose all interest. Here’s somebody who can keep my interest though- [A new camera shot fades in on a dark purple lighted room.] Reformer: The night is here and tonight, I will continue to lead the Fraternity of the Stone right to the top of the UWF. Tonight, my opponent is Fabulous Frank White. Well Mr. Fabulous, I will make sure that you go back to where you came from, as I destroy you and all that you were meant to be. The Reformer will be on the top of the UWF in no time. It is only a matter of time until I get that World Championship around my waist. I have paid my dues and have waited, and once I destroy you, I am asking the President for a match with the champion for the belt. I feel that I deserve a shot and Tonight, I will prove to the wrestling world, why I deserve that shot at the UWF World Belt. So Mr. Stone, you have made the biggest mistake of your short career as I will dismantle you. The Reformer has spoken!!!! [The camera fades out.] AW: Not him! I was talking about the man who’s going to wipe the floor with him, Fabulous Frank White. ========================================== FABULOUS FRANK WHITE versus THE REFORMER ========================================== As the Reformer made his entrance to the ring, Frank White’s bodyguard kept up his post outside the ring, simply watching, while White and the Reformer faced off, nose to nose. The second the bell rang they began to trade shots, and the Reformer decidedly came on top there, rattling White with multiple fists and then clotheslined him out of the ring. Redeemer, another member of the Fraternity of the Stone, walked out and stood at the top of the ramp, surveying the scene. The bodyguard helped White up, but both took a flying clotheline from the Reformer, who leapt off the ring apron and sent White’s bodyguard toppling into the crowd, and White’s head struck the concrete floor. He got the crowd to begin chanting for the Fraternity, and then dropped Frank White with the Reformation on the floor, then rolled under the ropes and took the win by countout. The Reformer got up and saw Redeemer, who walked back to the dressing room silently. WINNER: The Reformer AW: Now that’s a travesty of justice. Frank White is a talented, well built, handsome young man, and the Reformer is the opposite of all those things. These referee’s really need to take some sort of refresher course, they obviously don’t remember how to officiate a match. [The director, off camera, says something to Ashley in a low voice] What about it? Oh come on, it’s my show. Do we really have to roll- [Cut to a backstage shot, earlier today in the Pepsi Arena.] PP: I'm here with Luis "The Kid" Garavello... Kid, you got a hard match with Underground tonight and- LG: No, you must be reading the last week's schedule, Peter. PP: No, no, you will face Underground again. LG: AGAIN? Geez, the committee has been making strange decisions recently... But that's ok. I beat this guy once, I can beat him again... Underground, I hope you're ready to be put 1-2-3 on the mat again! Stevens, Chronosphere, if you try to interfere, you'll have yours too!! PP: And how about the Cruiserweight situation? You are ranked #2, below Daniel Kidd and... LG: That's right, the so-called "REAL champion"... Kidd, I have nothing for you but SPITE!! You hide under your Pride fellows boots... I want you in the ring, next week... For the #1 position! If you're man enough, come without your allies... And I'll keep KO Corral out of the ringside too. ============================================= LUIS ‘THE KID’ GARAVELLO versus UNDERGROUND ============================================= Luis came out without any other Corral members, only the fans backed him up with cheers of “Kid!”. Underground was seconded by the entire rest of Asylum, Chronosphere, Marc Stevens, and Crystal Cutglass. Unlike last week, the match was mostly dominated by Underground, because the Asylum managed to prey on Luis’ despair over having no backup. Every time Luis tried to rally around the fans with an aerial manoever, Underground was there with either a block, a counter, or a roll away. Luis finally took a bad spill off the top rope when he tried for a flying forearm to Underground’s back as he stood at ringside, but Stevens pushed Underground out of the way and the Kid hit the guardrail face first. Marc rolled Luis into the ring as Underground slid in and bounced off the ropes with a somersault jumping splash, and hooked Luis’ leg to get the cover. WINNER: Underground [Cut back to the truck. Now the crowd inside the arena’s are beginning to chant three letters: “E - C - W!”] AW: What’s going on in there? Is Moe Owens trying teach those morons the alphabet? Underground finally gets the duke, and Garavello gets the shaft. I love it. Next in the ring is Eddie Fantastic, the comeback kid, against Tom Landis, the Legion’s main loser. Landis opened his big yap before the contest, and for the sake of fairness, let’s listen to the bigmouth. [Cut to the half empty arena, a short time before TNT begins. Tom Landis is out in the aisleway and signing autographs, while Tara ‘Sunburst’ Marshall is posing for pictures with members of the audience nearby.] Fan #1(a fifteen year old boy): Hey Tom, what do you think of Eddie Fantastic, now that he’s back? TL: About the same as when he was here the last time, not an awful lot. Fantastic is slime, pure and simple. He cheated his way to Tim’s U.S. title right after the first Gold Rush pay per view, but he’s not going to do it again. Fan #2(an even older boy, wearing a “Pride” t-shirt): Aren’t you worried that the Empire’s gonna get him? TL: You’re wearing a Pride t-shirt, but I’ll let that one go. Tim’s got backup this time, and the rest of us aren’t going to let Fantastic’s crew get the best of him again. They’re the only reason he lost it in the first place, and we haven’t even seen if the Painkiller and the other tools are back. Fan #3(a young girl with a worried look on her face): Tom, you’ve got to be careful. Eddie Fantastic cheats, and he’ll try to cheat against you. TL: [bends down to look at the girl face to face] That’s okay, little lady. I’ve seen guys like Eddie come along before, and I know how to handle him. I’ll be fine. [Tom signs the girl’s program, and she runs off into her mother’s arms. He stands back up and faces the camera.] TL: Fantastic, tonight you and I are set to go one on one for the first time in a long while. I still owe you from all the way back at the first Midsummer Massacre pay per view, when you decided that after teaming with me, that you’d unceremoniously dumped me off the team. I’m finally going to get revenge for that night, and you’re going to see what two really hard fists feel like. You say your’s are harder than two dollars worth of jawbreakers? Well, if that’s true then mine are harder than the kidneys of anybody that eats two dollars worth of jawbreakers. =================================== TOM LANDIS versus EDDIE FANTASTIC =================================== Landis came out first to the cheers of the crowd, and Tara Marshall received one of the louder pops of the night, and both repaid the crowd by hi-fiving and shaking hands right down the ramp, and all around ringside. The first strains of Darth Vader’s “Imperial March” played, and Eddie Fantastic came out, arms raised and heel pop throbbing. Fantastic and Landis spent the first few minutes of the match reacquainting themselves with each other, and it was mostly lockups and moves that neither could benefit from. Finally after a stalemate to that point, Eddie launched a major offensive when he caught Landis with a lariat from behind, knocking him out to the floor. Vince DaVinci, Fantastic’s manager, tried to hit Landis with brass knuckles but the Legionnaire blocked it, then dumped him on his butt with an atomic drop. It gave Eddie Fantastic the opportunity to blindside Landis, and after rolling Tom back into the ring Eddie drove him into the mat with a sharp belly to belly suplex. He covered Landis, but Tim Vexton rushed out and landed an elbow into the back of Eddie’s skull, disqualifying him but extracting a small measure of revenge. Landis and Sunburst leaves soon after the bell, racing after his stablemate. WINNER: By DQ, Eddie Fantastic [Cut back to the control truck. In the distance, church bells and thunder can be heard, and another pop rises as well.] AW: Didn’t realize it was getting rainy out. One more match to go through, and I can’t wait. Armstrong, your number’s up. And now, Dead Man Walking speaks his last words. [Camera opens up on a corner of Armstrong's locker room. Armstrong is slumped up against his locker, wearing his usual motiff of baggy shorts, flannel shirt tied around the waist, black UWF hat turned backwards, and a black shirt with white lettering saying "Ashley Wilder: All Business, For a Crisp Fifty Dollar Bill!" Armstrong stares directly at the camera, his Riot Squad and his valet, Ginger, are nowhere to be found.] SA: Where do I begin? Paul Reed finally acknowledges my great talent and has me headline a TNT card. It's about time, I begin to get respect in this league. So far I have been absolutely perfect, and I plan to keep it this way. Also, Reed finally gives the match I want. Vexton... Your motto is "Pain is temporary, Pride is forever." Well Vexton, I will show you that pain will be forever when your lying face up looking at a hospital's ceiling, while a doctor tells you that you will never walk, never talk, never blink, and never wrestle AGAIN. To put it in layman's terms, I will simply break you. You will be in pain for the rest of your miserable life, or should I say the rest of your life I allow you to have. [Armstrong pauses while taking a look around. Armstrong partially gets up and reaches for something out of the camera's view. Armstrong brings back a 1950’s toaster with a Pride sticker on it. Armstrong flashes it to the camera, before setting on the ground next to him.] SA: One final note Shawnie. You maybe shaking of fright in your little pink panties, cause you know the Riot Squad will be present at ringside. Well to make the match more even for you, the Riot Squad will not be at ringside. You know to make it an even more equal match, Ginger won't be at ringside either. That way you have nothing to complain, bitch, moan, whine about after your shoulder's are placed firmly on the mat for the count of three. Also, you may be wondering why I have this piece of trash toaster with me. Well like this decript thing here beside me, and I'm not talking about Ashley, is just like the Pride. Old, rusty, and long past it's prime. And you know what happens when these ancient things try to do something?! They break! [Armstrong stands up and picks up the toaster. Armstrong throws it across the room. The toaster smashes against the concrete wall, shattering into a tiny, million metal shards. Armstrong walks over to the debris, smiling to himself as he hears the metal screech and crumple as he walks on it.] SA: This toaster and the Pride both will share a similiar fate. Just as easily as I destroyed this stupid 'Pride-endorsed' toaster, I will destroy Vexton and any member of the Pride who decides to interfere tonight. That's all I have to talk to you about! Now get the hell out of my locker room! [The camera man turns around to leave, but the Riot Squad are standing in the doorway. The camera man starts to back up, but Armstrong stops him. Armstrong spins the camera man around to face him.] SA: Remember! "Pride is temporary, but the pain I give is forever!" [Armstrong spins the frightened camera man back around, so he now faces the Riot Squad. The closest Riot Squad member punches the camera man dead in the face. The camera crashes to the ground, knocking the feed out. Cut back to Ashley, who’s leaning out the door of the truck, talking to somebody. She nods, and “Owwwwww, have mercy!” is heard before Ashley shuts the door again.] AW: What a cool guy! He said he’s going to introduce me to a couple of his friends. One’s named Jack and the other’s named Manny or something. Maybe we’ll hit the town tonight. Let’s just show Armstrong getting the boots put to him. ================================================ ‘HARDCORE’ STEVE ARMSTRONG versus SHAWN VEXTON ================================================ Armstrong came down to the ring first, without the Riot Squad or his ‘friend’ Ginger. He got on the microphone and verbally abused the Pride and Shawn Vexton, then told him to get out there so he could finish the job that God started. As “Lakini’s Juice” began to play, Blocky Jobavia came out to the ring a second time, this time dressed up in a Pride t-shirt and wearing Shawn Vexton’s ring outfit. The crowd really let Blocky have it, this time for not being a Pridesman(instead of usually being who he is). Armstrong wasted no time at all, launching himself over the top rope to the floor, and hitting “Vexton” with a flying cross bodyblock. Armstrong scraped him off the floor, threw him into the ring, and hit him with the Riot Drop, a flying somersault legdrop. He covered for the win, if you could call it that. WINNER: Steve Armstrong [Following the contest, Armstrong was celebrating his win when the four man assault team known as the Pride rushed the ring and began to decimate Armstrong, until the Riot Squad rushed down to save their leader. By this time Steve’s down in a bad way, and the Pride escapes through the crowd, smiles wider than ever. Cut to Ashley, who’s smiling just as much as the Pride was. Before she can start to speak, the truck starts rocking back and forth. She looks out the window, and gets a shocked expression on her face.] AW: I don’t believe it! There’s two guys out there with shaved heads and skirts. They’re actually throwing themselves up against the side of the truck, and spinning themselves in circles. And they have some kind of belts with them. I guess that does it for tonight, we’ll see you on Saturday Night, and then next Tuesday night the UWF brings you War in the Desert. Goodnight from Albany. [The camera follows Ashley as she walks out the door of truck and says to somebody “No, I’m sorry I don’t know where your smile is.” Fade down to the rankings for the week.] UWF RANKINGS -HEAVYWEIGHT
