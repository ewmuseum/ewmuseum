Weekend WallBangers, 13 June 1998 (Hour 1)

13 June 1998 the post on Google Groups

[Camera fades open to the outside of the Hartford Civic Center in
Hartford, Connecticut. In the parking lot we see hundreds of fans lined up to go inside, as well as a few still grilling out and drinking beer. The camera fades to white, then another snaps back in to the inside of the Hartford Civic Center, the crowd is screaming and yelling as the pyrotechnics go off all around the arena. On the UWF
Wallcam we see the UWF logo come up in flames, spin around three times, and settle in in a flash of fire. The camera pans around the crowd, showing all the signs around the arena. We see a "Free Derek
Monk" sign, a "Go churn your own butter Yoder" sign, a WTF 3:16 sign and many many others. The crowd is screaming and yelling as the camera continues its circuit around the arena, until it settles on
Jason Max and Wil Greene sitting at the UWF Broadcast booth. Jason
Max is wearing a tuxedo, with a light blue cummerbund and a light blue bow tie, while Wil is wearing a semi-black jacket and a pair of faded blue jeans. Both men look up as the camera reaches them and wave.
Jason starts to speak] Jason Max: Welcome everybody, to the UWF's newest show, Weekend
WallBanger's!!! We have a great night of...
[Creeping Death by Metallica begins to play as the camera moves up to the entrance way. We see Viper walking down to the ring grinning, with the UWF US title strung over his left shoulder, and a big grin on his face]
Wil Greene: Looks like Viper has something to say [Cory Wells runs out from the back, grabs a microphone and enters the ring. Viper gets in the ring and struts around for the fans a little bit before finally settling down and walking over to Cory]
Cory: Well Viper, what can we do for you out here tonight? Viper: It's simple Wells, I have this fuckin match tonight with the
"American Emblem" Gunlock… He's been runnin his fuckin mouth all week after his big "I'm a sorry loser" speech on Turbo Tuesday Night...
Sayin how he might be better than me, and how he's gonna take this title from me... Well I'm here to answer him Wells...
Cory: Well go ahead then Viper. [Viper looks at Cory strangely for a second, shrugs, and grabs the mic from his hand as he pushes Cory out of the ring]
Viper: Alright Gunny, one thing I HAVE to address is your little comment about how you've held as many titles here as me... Boyo, you haven't even BEGUN to come close to The Snake or what I've accomplished... You held the Amateur Title and the Unified Title, and, well... shit, thatz it isn't it??? Gunny, I'm a former TV champion, a former Amateur Champion, a former WORLD
Tag-Team Champion, a former Unified Champion, and a CURRENT United
States HeavyWeight Champion... And you say you've matched my achievements??? You've got a LOOOOONNNGGG fuckin way to go before ya do that boyo...
And then of course ya say how ya won the amateur title from Manchester Mauler AND JiNx, and ya expect me to think it was a
HUGE deal... Gunny, three days BEFORE I VACATED the Amateur title, I beat the fuck outta Mauler in a one-on-one match defending the amateur title, and I won it from JiNx and Ethan Maguire in a cage match... I think ya better get yer fuckin facts right before ya open yer mouth, it'll help ya not to make such a fuckin fool outta yerself...
Now, Gunny, you tell yer sister that she can be sure that there is at least ONE of us UWF wrestlers who gives a fuck less about her, or whoever she does… See Gunny, I beat you for this title 2 weekends ago, I put yer ass down in the middle of the ring... Nothing is gonna change that Gunny, yer mine, I can beat you anytime I'm ready, and it would take MAJOR help for you to take this title from around my waist
But who knows Gunny, both Waggy and Chrissy Carter have been vowing that I won't hold this US title for long, maybe THEY'LL help ya... Yea right, and maybe Carter will actually get a little wrestling talent too... Gunny, this will be the second and last time we meet for this belt, because after I finish with you, yer gonna wish you'd decided ya liked the Unified Belt and had gone fer that instead
'Nuff Said
[Viper throws the mic down and walks off grinning to the crowd and slapping hands here and there... The camera pans back to Jason and
Wil]
Jason: Well, Strong words from our US champ towards his opponent later tonight, Gunlock! Now, let's get straight to the acti...
[You're Never Gonna Get It by En Vogue starts to play as a beautiful woman walks out of the entranceway and heads towards the ring. The woman has long, curly black hair, big, soft brown eyes, and very full lips. The woman walks down towards the announce table, sits down and grabs a headset]
Woman: Jason, get out of here, you're fired from hour one of WallBanger's, this is MY show now. Jason: The hell you say! I wasn't informed of this, and just who are you? [The woman throws the headset down, stands up, and proceeds to beat the everloving crap out of Jason Max. She ends the beating with a big powerbomb down to the concrete. The woman smiles and sits back down as she puts her headset back on. She watches with a smile as the
EMT's come down and cart Jason Max off.]
Woman: Now, my name is Delilah Morris, and I'm taking over for Jason
Max, on hour one of Wallbangers. I'm trusting no one has any problems with that?
[The woman glares at Wil, who just grins, holds his hands up, and turns towards the ring] Wil: Glad to have you aboard Ma'am. Think we could get to our first match now? Delilah: Sure Wil, let's send it up to Rod Stunk for the official announcements. Rod Stunk: Ladies and Gentlemen, the following contest, the first ever on the new UWF Weekend WallBanger's show, is scheduled for one fall. Coming to the ring first, hailing from Calgary, Alberta,
Canada, standing at 6 foot 5 inches tall, and weighing in at 255 pounds. He's the self-proclaimed Master of the DDT, here is, The
CCCRROOOWWWWWW!!!![A thunderstorm starts to pound over the sound system as the Crow walks to the ring focusing entirely on his match.
The crowd gives out a slight cheer as he makes his way down] Rod Stunk: And his opponent, from Montreal, Quebec, Canada. He stands 6 foot 3 inches tall, and weighs in at a robust 296 pounds, please welcome, The DDDRRAAAGGGOOONNNN!!!!!!![Bullet with Butterfly
Wings begins playing as Dragon steps out of the entranceway. Dragon glares at The Crow as the crowd cheers for him. Dragon walks quickly to the ring and steps in.]
Wil: Rod Stunk out of the ring now, and the referee Jimmy Sulsanto giving the instructions. Jimmy motions to the timekeeper.
[Bell Rings] Delilah: These 2 men looking daggers at each other as they circle. Now they move together, and a lock up, Crow quickly turning and throwing Dragon with an armdrag. Crow holds on to the arm though, and quickly turns it in to an armbar. Dragon swings his legs out wide, spins around and kips up, now Dragon underneath, and he has Crow in a hammerlock. Crow going for a back elbow, but Dragon ducks, Dragon sets him up for some kind of throw, but Crow flips up and over his back.
Wil: Crow jumps, and a standing drop kick to the side of Dragon's head sends Dragon tumbling to the mat. Crow hits the ropes, here he comes back, and Dragon drops underneath as Crow jumps over. Crow coming back now, and Crow with the leapfrog and he keeps going, but
Dragon hits the ropes on the other side, these two men flying towards each other now, but Dragon with a big clothesline that flips Crow over!
Delilah: I love to see two men kill each other, it makes me very happy! Wil: Well, I'm glad you're pleased Ma'am, in the ring Dragon up quickly, he grabs Crow's arm a whip to the ropes, no, reversed by The
Crow. Here comes Dragon back, Crow with the back body drop that sends
Dragon crashing down to the mat, Crow wastes no time, as he turns around and drops a leg across Dragon's neck. Crow going for the pin now...1...2.Dragon kicks out with plenty to spare.
Delilah: Well, Crow trying to win the match, which is always smart, but you have got to wear someone down a lot more than that before you can end it. The Crow now, hits the ropes, and here he comes back.
Crow with a senton backsplash, but Dragon moves out of the way! Crow hits his back hard, and is clutching his kidney's in agony.
Wil: Not too smart a move by Crow there, Dragon is taking this time to regain his breath a little, and now he looks like he's ready for some more fighting. Dragon picks Crow up, he sets him up in a front facelock, and a snap suplex! Dragon to his feet quickly, and he's laying the boots in to The Crow now!
Delilah: That's what I always say, kick them when they're down, it's a lot more fun than doing it when they are standing!
Wil: Ummm, yea well now Dragon has been pulled away by the ref and is receiving an admonishment for his behavior. Dragon smiles at the ref and pleads his innocence. The ref backs off, and Dragon heads back for The Crow. Dragon picks him up, and a whip to the ropes. Crow on his way back, and Dragon catches him in a bearhug type maneuver.
Dragon with an over the top belly to belly suplex that sends Crow flying! Dragon wastes no time as he heads over to The Crow. Crow up the hard way a scoop and a slam by Dragon.
Delilah: I think Dragon is smelling victory, or maybe that's just the
Crow's underarms? Wil: I wouldn't know Ma'am, but either way, Dragon is getting fired up here. He picks Crow up again, and a kick to the gut, Dragon hits the ropes to the side as Crow bends over in agony, and Dragon with a
kneelift! Crow flies back down to the mat, Dragon hits the ropes, and he comes back with a kneedrop to the top of Crow's head. Dragon with the cover...1...2..Crow get's the right shoulder up.
Delilah: And look at Dragon whining to the ref, like it changes anything. He'd better pay attention to The Crow, and the fact that he's sneaking up behind him!
Wil: That he is Delilah, and The Crow with a handful of tights, and the roll-up...1...2..Dragon just barely kicks out, and he is upset and embarrassed by that! Dragon right over on to Crow, Dragon picks him up and whips him to the ropes, Thesz Press! And Dragon is pounding on
Crow! The ref over pulling Dragon off now, but Dragon jumps right back on to him!
Delilah: Wow, he's pissed. I guess I'd be pissed to, if you could take my name and make it mean "worm"
Wil: Well, uhhh, yea. But Dragon getting his bearings back now, he picks Crow up, a whip to the ropes. Crow coming back now, and Dragon with a kick to the gut! There's the signal! Dragon picks him up, and
Dragon's Edge!!! This one is over! Dragon with the lazy cover...1....2...3…[Bell Rings]. And Dragon goes down in the UWF history books as the first win recorded on UWF Weekend Wallbangers!!!
Rod Stunk: The winner of this bout as a result of a pinfall, The
DDRRAAGGGOOONNN!!!!![Bullet with Butterfly Wings starts to play as the
Dragon celebrates a little in the ring before dropping to the outside and walking out]
Delilah: Well, a good showing by Dragon in this match against fellow
Canadian Crow. But why, if Dragon beat The Crow tonight, will Crow still be getting a US title shot next Tuesday?
Wil: I have no clue Delilah, I don't make the matches, just call 'em. Speaking of calling 'em, we have Rod Stunk in the ring for the announcements for the next match.
Rod Stunk: The following contest is set for one fall, with a 10 minute time limit. Coming down to the ring first, standing 6 foot 4 inches tall, and weighing in at 275 pounds! He hails from San Jose,
California! Killa...... Karl...... KKKOOOOKKKKOOOOO!!!!!! [Dangerous by Busta Ryhmes starts to play as Killa Karl struts down to the ring dancing and generally making an ass out of himself. The crowd boos mightily as he starts talking trash to various fans in the aisleway.
Karl gets to the ring and dances around on the apron for a little while, until a thrown hot dog with mustard and ketchup catches him right in the face. Karl gets in the ring after that and just stands there]
Rod Stunk: His opponent, making his UWF debut! Being accompanied to the ring by Cassandra, he hails from "The City of Brotherly Love"
Philadelphia, Pennsylvania! Weighing in at 231 pounds and standing 6 foot 3 inches tall, he is the man who puts the HARD in hardcore! Rick
TTTAAAYYYLLLOOORRRRRRR!!!!![War Machine by Kiss blares out over the sound system as Cassandra comes out of the entryway and flirts with the camera for a few seconds. Taylor comes out a few seconds behind her and they walk to the ring together. Taylor stops halfway and drags his thumb across his throat. The crowd explodes in applause as they continue to the ring. When they get there Taylor holds the ropes open for Cassandra to go through, then he enters and drags his thumb across his throat again to another huge round of applause from the fans. They parade around a few more times, the Taylor holds the ropes for Cassandra to exit]
Wil: Wow, this newcomer looks like he'll be making some waves real quick here in the UWF. The referee Marc Rasputin checking in with both opponents, and there's the signal to the timekeeper[Bell Rings].
Delilah: Rick Taylor is studying his opponent with a smirk on his face as Koko is circling him scowling fiercely. Taylor looks around at the crowd with a grin and shrugs his shoulders. Taylor now circling too, and now they lock up, Koko goes for a quick kick to the gut, but Taylor catches his leg, and a dragon corkscrew leg-whip!
Koko goes down holding his knee in agony. Taylor quickly over, he picks Koko up, a whip to the ropes, and a big powerslam on Koko!
Wil: Taylor overwhelming Koko here, Taylor with a pick up and a whip in to the turnbuckle. Taylor following right behind and Taylor hits a huge clothesline on to Koko in the corner. Koko falls face first out of the corner and Taylor going up to the top rope! Koko slowly to his feet, and Taylor with a big moonsault that lands perfectly on Koko.
Taylor with the cover...1...2..Koko gets his foot on the rope and
Taylor up quickly in surprise.
Delilah: Taylor is doing to this boy, what is known on the streets as, whuppin his ass! Koko hasn't even seen what offense might look like in this match!
Wil: Yea no kidding, and now Taylor deciding to show off to the crowd a little bit. He's up on the turnbuckle doing the thumb across the throat, and the crowd is eating it up. But here comes Koko charging
Taylor! Taylor with a backflip off the turnbuckle and he lands behind
Koko! As Koko is turning, Taylor with a savate kick right to the side of Koko's head! Koko goes down hard, and Taylor quick to take advantage. He grabs Koko, and Taylor again with the thumb across the throat! I think he's signaling for his finisher! Taylor with a whip to the ropes, here comes Koko back, and a big SpineBuster Slam!
Taylor with the cover...1...2...3!!! [Bell Rings]. Taylor wins his debut match with ease! Rod Stunk: The winner of this match, as a result of a pinfall, Rick
TTTAAAYYYLLLOOORRRRR!!!!!![War Machine again starts as Taylor gets up on all 4 turnbuckles and draws his thumb across his throat! The crowd goes wild each time, until Taylor gets down, jumps to the outside and walks to the back arm in arm with Cassandra, slapping hands on the way]
Delilah: Well, he certainly looks to have a future here. Wonder how attached he really is to the Cassandra chick?
Wil: Delilah, we do have a job to do you know. Our next match is our first of four title matches tonight. This one pits Our Unified
Champion Grave against his main contender so far, Cage. Though this will be Cage's last match for a while, as he is taking some personal time off to take care of personal matters. Let's show you how Grave ended up as the Unified Champion!
