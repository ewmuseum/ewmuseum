[The manager hands Reno a set of keys to the room, and after grabbing them he picks up a suitcase and walks over to his partner, "Romeo" Matt Richards who's waiting by the elevator.] SRR: Bad news man, this stupid hotel lost our suite. We're stuck with some crappy room on the fifth floor. RMR: WHAT? How the hell did that happen? SRR: I dunno, but it sucks. RMR: I'll betcha Smith had something to do with this. That big dumbass really has some sort of problem with you and me, he's threatened by our manhood. Afterall, a guy that big can't be very large, if you catch my drift. SRR: I know the big oaf from ACWF. He always strikes out with the ladies. But this is bullcrap. If he's got a problem with us he should step to us like a man would. As it is he's wearing our damn belts, him and Scott. RMR: They're not getting away so easily this weekend. They've screwed us twice now, they took our shot at Gold Rush and then walked out on us in New York. I'll be damned if the third time's the charm, nobody does the screwin' but us! [The elevator doors finally open, and the disgruntled NFC steps on board. As the doors close, we get a glimpse of three young ladies on the elevator with NFC. Richards and Reno give each other the eye and smirk as the doors close. Fade to black.]
