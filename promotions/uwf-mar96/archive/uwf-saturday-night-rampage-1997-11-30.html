UWF Saturday Night Rampage, 30 November 1997

30 November 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Part 1] [Fade up from black to a starry image of a night sky. As the camera slowly begins to rise above the ground and pan upwards, the vision of stars and blackness fill the screen entirely. In the middle of the picture, a blurry edged square starts to replay images of battle royals past. We begin with the image of Greg Smith tossing bodies over the ropes, some faces familiar and some not. On top of the picture, the voice of Sam Steeley cuts through.] SS: All through the history of the Universal Wrestling Federation, great battle royals can be remembered. From the very first battle royal staged back on UWF: Open Season, to the Road to the Gold event at Summer Heat Wave this past August, the greatest wrestlers in the federation have risen to the occasion... [A clip of Alex Kidd hoisting the United States Championship belt over his head after winning a battle royal, and Apocalypse doing likewise with the World Title, as fellow-Chalmers Corporation member Brett Greene holds the U.S. Title while grinning from ear to ear.] SS: Battle royals have been fought to fill vacant titles, as special events, or to name representatives in inter-league tournaments... [An image featuring former UWF'er Takezo Musashi, Scott Daniels and Brett Greene becomes visible. We see Daniels hoist Musashi up on his shoulders, and at Hotspot's urging, Brett pushes both out to the floor, winning the Ring Kings battle royal and getting the berth in the TNM World Title tournament.] SS: UWF battle royals have also had it's share of upset winners... [Cut to clips of Blaze winning a battle royal on Sunday Slams, and then of Randolph Chalmers himself besting a number of opponents in another.] SS: As well as cemented the careers of some wrestlers as legendary... [Clips from Heat Wave are shown, as Alex Kidd becomes one of the final two men in the match, and then goes on to defeat Heavy Metal from the AEWA.] SS: Tonight, another test of endurance awaits twenty men. At stake are three positions in the upcoming SWT, the Supreme Wrestling Tourney. Will it be newcomers... [Clips of Xavier Lee Jackson, Bobby Rain, Jack Riley and Kensei Tokugawa play.] SS: ...established superstars... [Clips of the Legion and Pride members, Eddie Fantastic, Randy Acorn and the Reformer play.] SS: ...or seasoned veterans... [Clips of 'Crimson' Joe Reed, Brewster Cogburn, Randy Acorn, Bishop and Raekwon play.] SS: ...who will come away victorious? Find out tonight, on UWF Saturday Night Rampage! ["All That You Are" by Econoline Crush leads the opening title sequence for Rampage, and the faded out screen starts to show more images of high impact wrestling. Three sides of a cube continually show clips, from Alex Kidd executing the Somersault Bulldog, to Eddie Fantastic with a belly-to-belly suplex, to the Reformer's reverse DDT. The cube starts to spin on it's axis now, and all sides gradually show the UWF logo. Now the cube begins to zoom in, until only one side is seen. The logo shatters, and reveals an exterior shot of the Mobile Civic Center, situated in Mobile, Alabama. Cut inside the building, to a high shot from up in the rafters, which pulls back to reveal the entire arena, which at capacity of just over 9,200 people manages to fill the arena with huge cheers as fireworks are set off to signify the beginning of the show. New angles appear every few seconds, each displaying handmade signs held up by members of the crowd -- "No Black Ice tonight... RCIII, p.o'ed?" -- "Twister sucks!" -- "Bring Back Amy!" -- and showing thousands of screaming fans. Fade down to ringside, where the new broadcast team for SNR is seen. Sam Steeley is the only returning face, as he is now seated next to Jennifer Davies-Parker, late of UWF Slam 'n' Eggs. Sam's wearing his leather jacket over a white "Bite this Chalmers, look who's employed!" t-shirt, and Jennifer is wearing a black t-shirt with the words "Presidency from Hell: Day 7", which goes well with a devilishly short skirt.] SS: Hello feebs, welcome to Saturday Night Rampage. As you can see by this lovely creature beside me, President Raekwon's sweeping changes have continued. David Rogers is no longer with the promotion, and I hear Amy Marshall was offered the position of being the president's personal secretary. JD-P: She refused; [dry, british sarcasm] I can't understand why. If this is what the UWF is going to be like from now on, I can't see many of it's employees sticking around. With him as president, maybe the AEWA was right. SS: What, are you crazy? Things are running sooo much smoother now. And the President likes blondes. JD-P: More and more like the American government every day. Fans, we've got a great show lined up for you, despite the setbacks, and it gets started with the much awaited announcement from Legion. For this interview, we take you to the ring with Moe Owens. [The crowd release an extremely large face pop as "Morning Glory" starts up. The curtains part, and out come the men of Legion. Tom Landis is the first man seen, wearing a pair of bluejeans and a white tank top bearing the same message as Jennifer Parker-Davies' t-shirt earlier. Tara 'Sunburst' Marshall is next, and she is wearing a glittering red dress with "Legion" across the front in white sequins. Youth Gone Wild marches out next, in his rock-band-of-the-week t-shirt(it's Greenday for those keeping track at home) and cutoff jean shorts. Tim Vexton appears right after Wild, walking out in a pair of Champion mesh shorts and a Seattle Sonics team jersey. Visible on his injured leg is a cast that comes up to his calf, smaller than the one we saw on him last time. Finally, Alex Kidd comes out wearing his wrestling gear of black Legion pants and white boots. Kidd is wearing his white 'ENGLAND' soccer jacket. He claps as many hands as he can, and is followed by the lovely Alicia Powers as always, whom is dressed in a green short sequined dress. All members get to the ring, and circle around Moe Owens.] MO: Now, tonight is the special battle royal for the three spots in the Supreme Wrestling Tourney. Originaly, Tom Landis, you were supposed to compete alongside of these two men, Alex Kidd and Youth Gone Wild. Your thoughts? TL: Nobody's more surprised than me that I'm not included in that battle royal Moe. Look at the lineup. Most of the former World Champions that are still around are in there. Alex, Wild, Cogburn, even Reed. But I guess our president thinks too much of himself to let another former champion in there, so there's not much I can do. But I'll say one thing, [mockingly] President Raekwon, Legion won't stand by and let you get away with this for too long. It's a little late for me in this battle royal, but two Legionnaires are better than none. Sun: Tom and myself are going to be out at ringside with Alicia, cheering on Alex and Youth to victory. Because there's no victory like a Legion victory! [The fans in attendance give a wild pop, but then again, Sunburst is female and the fans are rabid dogs.] And one more thing, as kind of a personal note, don't worry Amy, your job will always be safe, no matter what Raekwon thinks. You'll be back, I'm sure of it. AK: See Moe, Raekwon can keep Tom Landis out of the battle royal, but he can't keep Legion from winning it. He still has to contend with myself and the Wild Thing over here. Not to mention twenty two other disgruntled employees of his, that would love the opportunity to wipe that arrogant look off his face. [Large face pop] Later tonight, whether it is Pride, Joe Reed, Acorn, even Chris Scottsdale, Legion are out to win it all tonight and earn the right to represent UWF, in the tournament! YGW: [addressing the crowd more than Moe] Is it just me, or does Raekwon think he's god's gift to wrestling? [Some of the fans agree, but the majority boo Raekwon] I say if he's so good, why hasn't he been champion? Where's the credentials, Raekky? You don't deserve to shine this man's boots [over-exagerates as he points to Landis], much less take his place in the main event. MO: Recently, the Reformer has added a special challenge to his match against World Champion 'Badboy' Randy Acorn. He wants it to be a lumberjack match... with you four being his lumberjacks. What do you guys say? AK: Well, I think I can speak for everyone else here. Reformer, I'm glad that you are finally getting your shot. And I'm even happier to see the style of match, because this way, you won't be screwed out of that fair title shot that has avoided you for so long. You know you can trust us, and we know we can trust you. YGW: 'Cause everyone knows that if there's one thing Legion does, it's stand for the truth. Hey, if you can't trust Legion, just who can ya trust? TV: It would be a pleasure to see a man like the Reformer paste the ring with that lowlife Acorn. And to see it up close and in person would be twice as sweet. Reformer, Legion accepts. MO: As of late, the Pride has been really doing a number on Legion. Perhaps because of the number disadvantage, but the fact is this: Pride have been stomping all over Legion lately. AK: Thank you for putting it in such kind words Moe. MO: No probl... oh... sarcasm. Sorry guys. TL: Actually Moe, you summed it up better than we ever dared to. That's exactly what they've been doing, kicking our tails. Of course it's taking the odds in their favor to do it, but that's beside the point. AK: Let the Pride think what they think. It's better we just ignore that. But Legion has had some weaknesses lately. And it is not because the Enforcer is hurt. Trust me, this guy can do more damage when he's injured than Raekwon can do on his best night. TV: Thanks for the vote of confidence, Wonderkid. I just want the Pride to hear one thing: I'm going to be back at 100 percent real soon. You want a piece of the Enforcer? Then come get some. MO: So what is this big surprise you guys were talking about then? [Alicia takes the question, for the first time.] AP: I believe Tara and I should answer that one. One of the reasons that Pride have been running over us lately, is because of poor booking. We aren't mad or disgruntled though, so don't worry. But fact is, Legion matches were running one after the other. So everyone was tired. And Pride could then stomp over us. It is that, and other small little booking decisions, and contract signing that has gotten Legion into trouble. Sun: Exactly. When was the last time you saw all of Legion in the same building? Between Alex's commitments to the LWC, Youth's charitable contributions and appearances, Tom's troubles with the Chalmers Corporation, and Tim's rehab, Legion hasn't stood united in quite a long time. AP: Now, unlike Tara, I don't know much about contracts. So one woman is looking after all four member's contracts. Well, with the work rates of these four men, it can become a tedious and tiring job. Mistakes are made. And in the end, we pay for them in the ring. SS: She's got some nerve talking like that. As if she gets into the ring on a regular basis. Sun: That brings us to our major announcement. We've decided to handle the problems of over-booking. MO: Well, what is it? TL: I was the one that got nominated to announce this, so here goes. Ladies and gentlemen, please welcome the newest member of Legion, Mr. Nelson Taylor! SS: Who? JD-P: Beats me, I've never heard of him before either. [With that, a moderate face pops erupts for a somewhat overweight man, who's wearing a standard business suit and shakes hands with any of the fans who are willing. Taylor is carrying a stylish leather briefcase to the ring with him, and he's all smiles as he, well, waddles down to ringside. Landis moves over to hold the ropes open for this new associate, who graciously accepts as he enters the ring. Both Kidd, Wild, and the two ladies offer him a wave as he is handed the mic by Moe Owens.] NT: Thank you very much for that introduction, Tom. Well, to get right down to the nuts and bolts, Legion needed a guy to handle all the little details, behind the scenes wheeling and dealing, and so on. And they found it in me. You cannot perform at peak physical condition if you're worried about booking a flight to Detroit or signing contracts for hours after the match, so that's why they needed some with a business background. Heck, I've worked for some pretty dynamic companies as a consultant... I just finished a tour of duty with Microsoft, but let me tell you no one holds more of a monopoly on their industry like Legion does on wrestling! Let's see what you can do with two guys who are staring you in the faces. Right Chris? [In the ring, Tasker brings the sparring partner crashing to the mat with a simple trip, then hops to his feet and gives an enthusiastic thumbs up to Sanders.] BS: Exactly. He said it all, didn't he? Armstrong evades a lockup and instead uses a waistlock suplex, which draws a huge round of boos from the crowd. It doesn't hurt the LiR much however, and she ducks a Western lariat and nails Armstrong with a reverse neckbreaker. The crowd releases a huge heel pop as a woman makes her way to the ring, dressed in a tight purple bodysuit with the words "Time To Get Even Wilder" written down the thigh of the suit.] JD-P: That's ASHLEY WILDER! SS: What's she doing out here?!? [Ashley makes no false pretenses about why she's come out, as she walks to ringside and stands on the floor, close to where the LiR and Armstrong are once again locked up. The Lady in Red grabs Armstrong in a headlock but is lifted up into a back suplex, and Armstrong pauses for a little bit of time to taunt Wilder. As he does, Ashley gets up onto the apron and grabs the LiR's leg, pulling her out of the move. The moment the Lady in Red lands on her feet, she backdrops Armstrong over the top rope, and with Ashley backdropping him as well, he hits the floor, eliminated!] JD-P: What a move! What a stunning move by the ladies of the UWF! SS: That was not fair! Steve didn't have a chance, he got trapped. JD-P: And of course the fans feel reeeeeeeeeal sorry for him too. [Armstrong is pulled back to the locker room by a couple of members of the Riot Squad, as Ashley and the LiR hi-five each other, and then Ashley warns the LiR to duck. Joe Reed's manager does, and Ashley flattens an oncoming Randy Acorn with a fist square in the face. Ashley leaves the ringside area to a hail of pops, while Joe Reed comes over to help the Lady in Red pound Randy Acorn into the mat. Bishop has silently been waging a war with Bobby Rain in one of the corners all this time the match has been going on, and gaining the considerable advantage. Rain tries a low blow on the Avatar, and even that works against the Rainmaker when Bishop picks him up in a fireman's carry position, then bucks Bobby over the top rope to the floor.] SS: Another casualty now, this time courtesy of the man afraid of Serge Annis, Bishop. You know, he doesn't even deserve to be called Bishop. He should be named after the chess piece he really resembles, Pawn. JD-P: That's just sad. [Scott Daniels nails Joe Reed with a springboard dropkick, after dangerously climbing out to the apron and jumping onto the top rope. Daniels then works on the Lady in Red, clotheslining her into the corner and then a flurry of kicks to her midsection by Hotspot. If he wasn't happy with the boos he was getting before, he sure has to be now. Alex Kidd gets tied up with Brewster Cogburn on the ropes, and Youth Gone Wild arrives on the scene to execute a spin kick, knocking Cogburn out to the floor. The Legionnaires on the floor(Landis, the two managers, and Nelson Taylor) errupts into cheers on the floor, and Alex now starts to tangle with Chris Scottsdale of all people. Alex ducks a clothesline, but is snared with an armbreaker to the mat. Wild suffers a similar fate thanks to Kensei Tokugawa, who executes a dragon screw and then knocks Wild to the mat with a short lariat.] SS: The Samurai's having no trouble with Wild. I'm not surprised, the last battle royal he was in he almost won it. He cleared out half the field before Jimmy Dangerous of all people caused him to lose. That's why they've teamed up, they respect each other's abilities. JD-P: Oh brother. Hey, Raekwon's in trouble! RAEKWON'S IN TROUBLE! [Some of the wrestlers in the battle royal have teamed against the President and are working on eliminating him, the same strategy usually seen on a 400+ pound man. Xavier Lee Jackson, Sebastian Jericho, Bishop and Eddie Fantastic all grab a hold of Raekwon and try to dump him over the ropes, but he fights with all he has not to go. Kensei finishes with Wild and rolls under the bottom rope, then proceeds to pick up the Kendo stick he left behind in the Daimyo interview and returns to the ring with it. He heads for Raekwon and cracks it over his head, which allows the other four men to dump him out to the floor. However Raekwon, incensed, grabs the nearest tights he can find, those belonging to Xavier Lee and Chris Scottsdale, and pulls each of them over the top rope, into elimination. Raekwon curses loudly at the remaining competitors, especially Tokugawa.] JD-P: That's cleared up the field, wouldn't you say? We've got Alex Kidd, Youth Gone Wild, Bishop, the Reformer, Scott 'Hotspot' Daniels, Sebastian Jericho, Eddie Fantastic, Joe Reed and the Lady in Red, Tim Greer, Randy Acorn and Kensei Tokugawa in there! 12 men are left out of twenty. SS: Wait, it's about to get cut down to 11, 'cause here comes Alex Kidd! [Alex, victim of a sneak attack by Eddie Fantastic, is set up on the top rope following a hard roundhouse right. As soon as he's set up, Kensei Tokugawa runs to the corner, and Eddie drops to his knees. Tokugawa leaps, springs off Eddie's back and nails Alex flush in the face with a beautiful kick to the head. The Wonderkid falls off the turnbuckle to the floor, another victim. Kensei doesn't have time to celebrate though, as Eddie doublecrosses him with a low blow and a quick throw to the floor as well. Half the people gone, the remaining participants start to break down into one on one situations. Wild and Eddie hook it up, while Jericho and Scott Daniels, Bishop and the Reformer, Tim Greer and the Lady in Red, and Joe Reed and Randy Acorn do likewise. Reed and his manager stay close to one another and offer help when they can, as Reed ducks and the LiR clotheslines Acorn at one point, then Reed takes the legs out from under Greer. Fantastic and Wild are renewing the Empire-Legion rivalry as they pound on each other, but Eddie lands a good elbow to the head of Wild and takes over, executing a devastating hotshot on the top rope.] SS: A good old-fashioned fight. I love it. And three of those people are going to represent the UWF, what better way to decide who than by survival of the fittest? JD-P: And I thought Darren Ball was full of cliches. [Thanks to a doubleteam by Reed and the Lady in Red, Tim Greer is tossed out of the ring after missing a Twister kick and then a double whip to the ropes and double backdrop. On the other side of the ring Eddie stands over Wild, just slapping the downed Legionnaire now. But in a split second the face of the battle royal changes, as Sebastian Jericho lends a land by a clothesline from behind, knocking Eddie over the ropes. He extends a hand, but Wild refuses due to the nature of the event. Jericho seems to say "Fine.", and then begins to kick Wild when he's down(literally). Randy Acorn charges the U.S. Champion but is cut off with a double clothesline from Bishop and the Reformer, each of whom take time out of their own lockup to knock the champion down. Bishop then strikes first on the Reformer, knees him in the stomach, and sends him over the top rope with a diving shoulderblock.] [Part 3] JD-P: The Reformer's gone! He's got a shot at Acorn at Live and Interactive, but he doesn't get the win tonight! SS: Good riddance. [Bishop walks towards Joe Reed, but instead grabs the LiR and nails her with a jumping DDT, crushing her into the mat. Reed is absolutely enraged over this, and climbs on Bishop's back, applying a tightened sleeperhold. While this occupies their attention, Randy Acorn quietly crawls over and picks up the LiR, then dumps her out to the floor as the crowd sighs unhappily.] SS: She's out, the amazon's out! Ha ha ha! JD-P: Too bad, she'd have made a fine representative to- what am I saying? She hates the UWF! [The crowd is going into a frenzy as Reed has Bishop worked down to one knee, and Scott Daniels comes over with a dropkick to both men. Daniels isn't one to stay with one opponent for long, as he surprises Acorn with a shot to the back, followed by a vertical suplex. Sebastian Jericho picks Youth Gone Wild up and prepares for a powerbomb out to the floor, but as Wild is picked up and sits on his shoulders, manages to flip Jericho over the ropes in a frankensteiner, eliminating one of the champions in the match. The second champion soon gets thrown out as well, as Acorn is held by Daniels while Joe Reed works him over with punches to the body. Behind this Bishop rises to his feet, takes a long hard look, and bounces off the opposite ropes before shoving the entire group backwards with a shoulderblock that starts a chain reaction. It's raining wrestlers at ringside, as first Daniels and then Acorn fall from the ring, leaving the three representatives left - Youth Gone Wild, Bishop, and Crimson Joe Reed.] SS: Ring the bell, ring the bell! JD-P: No way, someone's gotta win this thing. [Each man realizes at the same time that they've already gained the spot in the tournament, and collapse into a corner. There's Bishop, who's already won a battle royal earlier this year to win the IEWF/LWC Championship; Joe Reed, who won the reverse steel cage battle royal at Luck of the Draw; and Youth Gone Wild, the perenial favorite and all around likeable guy. Wild glares at Reed, as does Bishop. The smallest and tallest men in the ring gang up on Reed, and as Bishop lifts Reed up in a gorilla press, Wild dropkicks the knee of Bishop out, both falling to the mat. Wild, now safely able to utilize his high risk talents, climbs to the top rope and leaps onto Bishop with a Wildflyer, then does the same to Reed. With Legion spurring him on from ringside, Wild climbs again and jumps off with a somersault corkscrew splash that hits Reed like a ton of bricks. Wild pulls Reed to his feet and moves to eliminate the AEWA Champion, but again like a Pheonix from the ashes, Bishop rises to his feet and grabs Wild by the legs and sends him crashing to ringside. Joe Reed and Bishop remain, but not for long. Bishop grabs Reed by the throat and lifts him up in a Chokeslam position, but sets him up on the top rope and executes a swift Cathedral DDT instead. Reed, having absorbed this much punishment, is easily taken out, leaving Bishop as the sole remaining wrestler.] JD-P: BISHOP WINS! BISHOP WINS! SS: What a miscarriage of justice this whole thing has been. I can't believe President Raekwon will stand for this. JD-P: He doesn't have a choice, he's got to abide by the decision of the referee. TD: Ladies and gentlemen, here is your winner... BISHOP! Because they were the final three entrants, the spots in the SWT are awarded to YOUTH GONE WILD, BISHOP, AND 'CRIMSON' JOE REED! [The crowd explodes with the most thunderous pop of the night as the Cathedral Remix comes over the arena. On the floor, Reed is congratulated by a woozy Lady in Red, and Wild is put up on the shoulders of the other Legion men and carried off. As the celebrations continue for a few minutes, the lights start to flicker, and off they go again.] JD-P: Oh god, not Exodus again. [Even though the arena is still submerged in darkness, the fans continue to scream and cheer anyways. Suddenly, a spark is seen, and then the ropes around the ring light up like a Christmas tree, or more aptly like a ring of fire. Bishop can be seen squinting in the middle of it, and over the loudspeaker we can hear maniacal laughing.] SS: That's Serge's handywork! We're out of time, goodnight idiots!
