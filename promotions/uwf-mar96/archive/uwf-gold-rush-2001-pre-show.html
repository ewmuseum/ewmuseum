I came back healthy the freak avoided me like the plague. It still rubs me the wrong way I never got a chance to get back in the ring with him and if he *ever* steps foot back in this business he is going to have to deal with me... but I digress.
[Sampson cracks his knuckles and adjusts his tie.] LS: The belt means a lot to me, it's been degraded badly since I lost it. People don't have enough respect for what it means and the work that goes into winning and retaining it. I'm going to show Chris Courtade what being a champion means and restore that belt to where it belongs.
DR: Then it's on to the world title? LS: Without a doubt. DR: Since you're still bothered by Caliban not giving you a return, then you must be furious with Dan Kauffman. By the way you reacted after
Satin pinned Dan you weren't happy with that result.
LS: I wasn't. Sure, it might have been good for a laugh, the paper number one contender got pinned by a girl cause I kicked his ass so bad he couldn't kick out, but I wanted the win. I wanted to show Dan
Kauffman that he's in this particular main event due to dumb luck. Dumb, blind luck that is going to run out very soon. I am going to snuff the newfound arrogant smirk right off his fat little face. Dan tried to walk out on SNR and pretend like a didn't exist. If he didn't talk about me the problem might go away. Not going to happen, the Dan Kauffman thing isn't over until I say that it is. It's not over until he look up at me and realizes who the better man is.
DR: You sure don't like to lose do you? LS: No champion does. A man who accepts defeat well only does it because it's all he knows. I'm no loser, no second fiddle to anybody on this planet. I'm the man Alexander Karelin pulled out of the amateur world championships with a fake ass injury to avoid. His first loss would have happened then, not by some technicality in the Olympics. They built that man up to be a machine, a wrestling machine. When Amy called me the
Mechanical Animal she hit the nail right on the head. I am *the* wrestling machine, straight and simple.
DR: One last question. LS: Shoot. DR: How far will you go to win the UWF World Heavyweight Title? LS: All the way. I breathe it, I eat it, I sleep it, I obsess over it. David in this business we talk about a glass ceiling a lot. Everybody fights so hard to maintain their position, which is fine, that's the way of life in wrestling. Well, I'm about to shatter the ceiling, I'm about to invade the ivory tower that our current crop of main event stars has felt so comfortable in for so long. When I shatter that ceiling, things are going to change around here for good. When I shatter that ceiling, the UWF we know will change. When I shatter the ceiling, the status quo will no longer be acceptable. When I shatter the ceiling, my "Legacy"
will be firmly cemented and things will never be the same. Take a good long look, cause sitting right across from you is the man that will lead this company for a long time to come. Today North America... Tomorrow the world.
[With that Sampson smiles, shakes David's hand one more time and exits. Fade to a gold UWF logo, and then to black.]
