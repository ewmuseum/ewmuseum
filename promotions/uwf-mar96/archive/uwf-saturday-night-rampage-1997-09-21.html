UWF Saturday Night Rampage, 21 September 1997

21 September 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Part 1] [Fade up from black to the UWF logo. The logo spins for a moment, then shatters as a montage of last weekend and earlier this week plays. “D’You Know What I Mean” by Oasis is heard in the background.] DR: What a week the UWF saw. The return of two legends[shows Serge Annis chokeslam Reed out of the ring, and Eddie Fantastic unmask]... a new United States Champion[shows Tim Vexton holding the U.S. title over his head]... and a new show[shows Jennifer Parker-Davies and Darren Ball commentating from Sunday Morning Slam N’ Eggs]... One question remains... what will happen this week to top it? [Cut to more clips, and the lineup for tonight’s show is superimposed over top.] DR: This week: the fued between the Twin Ninjas and Twister culminates in a best two of three falls contest, Sebastian Jericho and Jack Riley face each other one more time in a submission match, and Serge Annis locks up with AEWA World Champion ‘Crimson’ Joe Reed... Plus a whole lot more, tonight on Saturday Night Rampage: Madison Madness! [Fade into the opening for Saturday Night Rampage. As "Let's Get Rocked" by Def Leppard is heard, fast paced clips of UWF shows start to flash by, each with the name of the wrestler at the bottom, as the logo jumps onto the screen, and then flickers on and off before disappearing for a second. Alex Kidd executes the Somersault Bulldog, followed by a Diamondback Death Drop from Sebastian Jericho, and then a Kentucky Backsmasher brought to us courtesy of 'Crimson' Joe Reed. The fabled Superman Spike onto the steel chair plays, as does the Pride Breaker, Pride Press, Pride and Pain, and Pride Bomb in succession. The DOA Full Nelson flashes by, as does the SnakeBite, Retro-Rocker, and the Wildflyer Moonsault finish up, and a strobed picture of Alex Kidd raising the Championship Belt at Summer Heat Wave. Fade into a shot of the legendary electronic billboard at Madison Square Gardens in Times Square, New York City, which flashes “Tonight, UWF Saturday Night Rampage. Main Event: Serge Annis versus ‘Crimson’ Joe Reed.” Another fade brings us to the inside of the arena, and various pans across the screaming audience let some of the more excited audience members get on camera. Tons of signs, merchandise and posters can’t help but be seen, and then we come down to see the three announcers at ringside. David Rogers and Amy Marshall talk to the fans before the show starts, but Sam is busy trying to hustle a free bag of popcorn from one of the female vendors. She slaps him and walks away, just as Dave gets the cue to begin.] DR: Hello everybody, welcome to Saturday Night Rampage. We’re here in New York City, the city that never sleeps. SS: And it never bathes either, from the smell of things. AM: UWF Dignitary Sam Steeley, ladies and gentlemen. Can you believe the lineup tonight? Joe Reed and Serge Annis? Twister against the Twin Ninjas? Sebastian Jericho versus Jack Riley? SS: I hope the Hammer breaks Jericho’s head open. What a waste of skin that guy is. DR: To make matters interesting, the only way that either Jack or Sebastian will be winning tonight is by making the other man submit. Somehow I can’t see either man giving up. AM: Both men are very proud, neither having given up previously in their careers. It’s going to get real painful before either man feels inclined to submit to the other. DR: The first match of the evening is a special six man tag team matchup. One has to wonder about the team assembled to take on the three men from Asylum though- Robbie Greer and Deadly Intentions. Greer’s been an enemy of the Asylum ever since he arrived on the scene, but where do the former ACWF tag champions fit in? SS: I don’t know about that Rogers, but Greer better not screw up tonight. I talked to Bobby Nash and Stevie Ramirez earlier, and they told me that if Greer loses the match for them, then there’s going to be some deadly consequences to pay. ============================================================================= DEADLY INTENTIONS/ROBBIE GREER versus MARC STEVENS/CHRONOSPHERE/UNDERGROUND ============================================================================= TD: Ladies and gentlemen, this match is scheduled for one fall and is a special six man tag match. First, introducing team number one, accompanied to the ring by Crystal Cutglass, the team of Marc Stevens, Chronosphere, and Underground... ASYLUM! [As “Don’t You Tread On Me” by Metallica comes out of the speakers, the entourage from Asylum walks down the ramp and receives a mighty heel pop. Cutglass leads the way, and according to the New York crowd, looking pretty damn good tonight.] TD: And their opponents... First, from Boston, Massachusetts and accompanied to the ring by Jenny Shards... ROBBIE GREER! And his teammates, the team of Bobby Nash and Stevie Ramirez... DEADLY INTENTIONS! [“Whiplash” by Metallica replaces the other heavy metal song, and Shards and Greer walk out to a loud pop from the crowd, shaking hands as they walk to the ring. The team of Deadly Intentions follows far behind, and are booed by the crowd. Greer exchanges a few brief words with the tag team, and then enters the ring to start out against Chronosphere.] SS: Oh great, another chance to see Robbie Greer get pasted. Why couldn’t Paul Reed give Deadly Intentions a better partner? [Greer takes Chrono down with an armdrag, and holds him there in an armbar. Chrono uses the ropes to leap to his feet, and reverses the armbar, turning it into a standing wristlock. Greer runs and leaps up onto the top rope, does a somersault in midair and lands on his feet, back with Chronosphere at a disadvantage. Chrono somersaults on the mat over to his corner and tags out, while Underground comes off with a nasty clothesline on Greer. He takes Greer by the legs and bends him into a standing figure four leglock, but Greer manages to use his leg strength to spin Underground out of the hold and to the mat. Greer executes a kip-up to his feet and goes for the tag to Ramirez, who busies himself with Jen Shards, as she begs him to tag in. Greer is caught from behind with a back suplex, and Underground drops an elbow across Greer’s throat before tagging in Marc Stevens, the man who hates Robbie Greer the most. Stevens pulls Greer up and winds up with a haymaker, but Greer blocks, gives one of his own, and dropkicks him into the corner. Greer runs at him with a handspring elbow to the face, and crawls over to Bobby Nash, the big man. Nash strikes up a conversation with Stevie Ramirez, ignoring the pleadings of Jen Shards as Robbie Greer is dragged back into the middle of the ring and powerbombed to the mat. Stevens tags Chronosphere in, who executes a moonsault and tags right back out to Marc. The DOA full nelson is turned into a dragon suplex, and Robbie Greer’s shoulders remain on the mat for three.] TD: Ladies and gentlemen, here are your winners as a result of a pinfall... ASYLUM! [Asylum hi-fives each other and walks away, leaving Greer in the ring with his partners. Enraged with losing, Nash and Ramirez begin to yell at Greer, and then kick him in the sides. Nash hauls him up and uses a backbreaker, but holds him in place as Ramirez climbs to the top rope. He jumps off and lands the flying kneedrop, completing their finisher which they call the Deadly Consequences.] AM: This is too much, somebody get out there and help Robbie out! [The crowd gasps as they see help arrive in the unlikely forms of Marc Stevens, Underground and Chronosphere. The members of Asylum converge on the ring and clear the two tag wrestlers out, and help Greer to his feet, then allow him to lean on them for support on the way back to the dressing rooms.] DR: What is going on? Robbie Greer assisted by Asylum? AM: They’ve been the bitterest of enemies for the longest time, but it seems as though things may have been patched up. Has the world gone topsy turvy? DR: I have no answers to that Amy, but we have to continue on with the show. At this time we must apologize for a scheduling mixup that’s occured. Originally the Masters of Motion were supposed to face the Fabulous Ones in the opening contest, but somebody [looks at Sam, who’s wearing a tag that says “UWF Travel Co-ordinator”] forgot to make travel and hotel reservations for the boys from the bay. So unfortunately, it doesn’t look like we’ll be seeing the Fab- [“Beautiful Girls” by Van Halen starts up abruptly over the PA system, cutting Dave off and causing him to speculate as to who is making an entrance. Ten second later a beautiful oriental woman walks out from behind the curtain, holding a microphone and makes her way to the ring.] AM: What’s this? SS: That’s Ms. Miki, the valet of those two oriental guys that call themselves The Fabulous Ones. AM: What’s wrong with you? SS: I’m just doing my journalistic duties by researching the talent of the UWF. AM: No you’re not! Your thinking with your other member! [Ms. Miki talks to the crowd as she walks around the ring.] Ms. Miki: Hello everybody!! [The crowd--mostly guys--give her a huge applause as she waves and smiles.] It looks to me like we have a few more women in the crowd and the viewing audience at home this week, no doubt due to the Fabulous Ones arrival here in the UWF. Before I bring out the men every woman dreams about, let me say this... If you’re bored of your fat, ugly, out of shape, beer guzzling, old fart of a husband or boyfriend, then start your VCR’s and get your cameras ready for a feast for the eyes! Then, after were gone you can rewind it and watch it over and over again. Now, with no further ado here are “The Universal Heartthrob” Agito Nakajima and “Sweet” Sho Satsuma--THE FABULOUS ONES!!!!! [Agito and Sho come out from behind the curtain, wearing their wrestling gear, carrying two folded tables, as “Kiss of Death” by Dokken plays over the PA, followed by Mr. Tsuburaya who’s wearing a black Armani suit. Ms. Miki leaves the ring and sets next to Sam Steeley at the press table. He gives her a headset rather eagerly.] Ms Miki: Thank you studly! SS: Who me? Ms. Miki: Yes you! AM: Oh brother. Ms. Miki: I heard what you said last week after we came out. SS: What did I say? Ms. Miki: You called me Ms. Miko, not Ms. Miki. SS: I’m sorry! [Sam blushes with embarrassment] But, I got it correct this week. Ms. Miki: That’s okay, most men get tongue tied when they’re in my presence. [Agito, Sho, and Mr. Tsuburaya have entered the ring and set up the press tables.] DR: Excuse me Ms. Miki, but what is this all about? You came out last week and did a lot of talking, what are you doing now? Ms. Miki: Hold tight you’ll see. [Ms. Miki leaves the press table and starts to talk.] Ladies of the UWF I promised you a special treat. This is your chance to give a rub down to two of the most fabulous men in wrestling history. Who wants too? [Lots of women start raising their hands. Miki points out four of them and they climb the barrier and enter the ring. Agito and Sho lay down on the tables as the women from the crowd begin to give them each a rub down.] Is that great or what? A dream come true for four lucky women! AM: This has got to be stopped, they’re going to get us in trouble with the FCC. This isn’t a brothel! [Ms. Miki hands the microphone to Mr. Tsuburaya.] Mr. Tsuburaya: Son ban wa fans of the UWF! I have a few things to say to the Twin Ninjas and the Pride! [Just then a ninja--somebody in a black ninja outfit--comes out from behind the curtain to the back room.] What do we have here? It can’t be...[Then a ninja comes out of the crowd and climbs over the guard rail.] Hey, boys we have two ninja’s who want to play. Do your thing! [Agito and Sho get up off the tables, fold them up and slide them out as Ms. Miki helps the women out of the ring and Mr. Tsuburaya leaves, but keeps talking. The two ninja’s then enter the ring from either side.] DR: An impromptu match... I guess now we can see if they can wrestle as well as they talk. [Agito and Sho rush the two ninjas and pound them with forearm smashes.] Mr. Tsuburaya: The Twin Ninjas, what can we say? Your self proclaimed dominance in the UWF tag team ranks will now come to an end... we have arrived! [Sho throws one ninja into the corner as Agito throws the other into the ropes. The ninja comes out and Agito does a legsweep takedown on him. Then, Sho follows up with an kneedrop to the back of the head.] Mr. Tsuburaya: Sho! What is that fabulous move called? [As Agito throws the flattened ninja out of the ring Sho leans between the second and third rope. Mr. Tsuburaya holds the mic up to his face.] Sho: That would be called the MOJO. Yeah, it’s a cajun word. We thought we’d name some of our moves, since we have so many, after things other than Japanese. [Sho turns around as Agito clotheslines the ninja left in the ring. Agito then bounces off the ropes and drops an elbow to the ninja’s chest. By this time the ninja who was thrown out of the ring has gotten to his feet. Sho notices him and bounces off the opposite ropes charges across the ring and jumps between the second and third ropes, head first, at the hapless ninja slamming into him. The momentum carries the ninja into the guard rail *BANG*] Mr. Tsuburaya: Suicide dive... That was so sweet! [Sho gets up and runs over to Mr. Tsuburaya who holds the microphone for him as Agito picks up the ninja in the ring and puts him in the Inverted Body Vice.] Sho: You see that Twin Ninjas... Pride? [Sho looks into the ringside camera.] Do you see the move that will end your pathetic reign in the UWF! There’s a little pipsqueak by the name of Valachi running around here who wears a shirt that says “Pain is temporary, but Pride is forever!” We’ll when Agito puts you in that move... [Sho points into the ring where Agito is performing the Inverted Body-Vise and the ninja is screaming in pain]... you’ll feel pain FOREVER! [Agito drops the ninja as Sho peels the other one off the guard rail and throws him back into the ring. Sho screams something at Mr. Tsuburaya.] Mr. Tsuburaya: Everybody get ready for the “Panku”. In english that means flat tire. [The dazed ninja stands up in the middle of the ring trying to clear his head. Agito and Sho run and bounce off of opposite ropes and deliver a clothesline that hits the ninja in the back and the chest. This keeps him standing upright. Then, Agito and Sho bounce off the opposite ropes again. This time Sho goes low from behind as Agito delivers a clothesline that sends the ninja flying over Sho and through the ropes. Agito and Sho exit the ring.] Mr. Tsuburaya: Arigato! Agito: When you’re this good, it’s hard to be humble! AM: Obviously for you it is. SS: Hush, Red. [Agito drops the microphone and with Sho, Ms. Miki and Mr. Tsuburaya head back to the locker room to a huge ovation as “Kiss of Death” plays over the PA.] AM: About time they left. SS: I was hoping Ms. Miki would stay longer. DR: Ahem, yes, well... Since we have a little more time left than we had expected, let’s take you to comments from the until now silent World Tag Team Champions, the team who won the belts at Summer Heat Wave, Anarchy. [Cut to a prerecorded segment. The scene is some sort of club. Several people, dressed in black outfits, wearing assorted tattoos and piercings, are mulling around the club. At one end is a stage, where some woman with a dozen noserings keeps screaming into a microphone. Not screaming words; just screaming. She finishes, bows to the audience, and walks over, to where Zombie of Anarchy is seated.] Zombie: An excellent job. I really felt the primal urges of life's despair. Woman: Thank you. When will your next performance be? Zombie: Soon. Very soon. Already, the names of our next victims are being tossed about like kittens in a burlap sack. Twister... Twin Ninjas... Working Class... not knowing adds to the anticipation. Woman: My sister wants to join the Guild. She works in a department store, and is already planning on bringing in some mannequin body parts for you to use on your next performance... Zombie: [Shaking his head] No, no, no. Don't tell us! [Suddenly picks up a chair and slams it against a table, breaking the chair into two pieces.] I feel much better now. Look, we appreciate the Guild's help with supplying us with the tools to destory our opponents, and even I like the metaphor of using mannequins as weapons. But MadDog and I are working on being spontaneous. Don't tell us... just show up, and surprise us. Woman: Ok. But she can come, can't she? Zombie: Of course. The more the merrier... [Just then, a large man with purple hair walks up to the pair.] Man: Hey, Zombie! Your partner is outside, throwing full bottles against the walls. Says he is working out his agression against his inner child, or something like that. But I think the neighbors are ready to call the police. Zombie: [Sighing] Come on. MadDog is brilliant, but often is too willing to tempt fate. Why risk jail for random acts of destruction when others will accept your actions with joy? [Fade back to the announcer’s table.] SS: It’s good to see the champs aren’t getting away from what brought them to the dance. AM: It’d be even better to see them leave the UWF for good. SS: Aw, somebody’s just upset ‘cause she missed the chance to jump on the bandwagon. You know, if you’re nice to him MadDog may still agree to go out with you? AW: Not on your life, Sam Steeley. DR: Up next is a match which has intrigued insiders ever since it was signed- Brewster Cogburn against ‘Slick’ Ricky Reno. Reno may not have a cowboy mentality, but what he does have is the same type of desire that helped Brewster get into the RSPWF Hall of Fame. SS: Yeah, I think Ricky Reno is the second coming of Brewster, as long as he doesn’t fall into the same trap of going soft like Brewster. [Cut to Brewster Cogburn in the dressing room. He's still in his street clothes.] BC: Reno! You got the distinct displeasure of gettin' in the ring with me when I'm good an' mad and ACHIN' FOR A FIGHT -- BOY! I'm gonna tan your hide and make jerky outta yer flesh! And Tim Vexton -- I believe that's my gold you've got around your waist. Give me a shot at that US title and I'll take it just like I did the last time! [Brewster goes quiet for a moment, then looks away.] BC: That's enough talkin' -- I didn't get ta where I am by jawin' up a storm. [Brewster turns and walks away. Fade into a new scene, Ricky Reno sitting in the stands after a house show. It’s not a big arena, at most one that could hold 1000 people. Reno studies a copy of Pro Wrestling Illustrated, then notices the camera.] RR: Hey idiots, what do you think you’re doing, trying sneak up on me like this? All I’m doing is catching up on a little background information on my opponent this weekend, Brewster Cogburn. Listen to this: [Reno begins to read from the copy of PWI.] RR: "Of all his skills, Brewster relies more on his brawling techniques than anything else. He possesses a nasty right hook and a wild haymaker, but when provoked or inclined to do so, he can also match technical holds with the best of them." [Reno stops reading.] RR: Well well, Mr. Cogburn. It seems as though you’re regarded highly as a fighter, not a wrestler. Either way, I’ll have you outmatched, and on the losing end of a Texas Cloverleaf. Say, what’s this? This is interesting too. [Reads the last paragraph of the article.] RR: "With the flood of rookies as we end this decade, Brewster says he looks forward to tangling with many of them in the future, but one in particular stands out. Brewster says that of all the so-called newcomers, he’d like to eventually face Terry ‘Hulk’ Bollea, stating that from what he’s seen, Bollea has tremendous potential as a technical marvel." [Looks up at the camera in shock.] RR: Bollea, technical? How old is this thing?!? [Reno turns the magazine around to see a very young Ric Flair engaged in combat with an even younger Ricky Steamboat, staring back at him from the cover.] RR: 1979!?! Geez Cogburn, how old are you exactly? Tell you what- you get your scrawny, creaky bag of bones into the ring, and I'll put you out of your misery quickly. It's more than I'd do for a lesser man. [Fade to the ring.] ============================================ BREWSTER COGBURN versus ‘SLICK’ RICKY RENO ============================================ TD: Ladies and gentlemen, the following contest is scheduled for one fall. First, making his way to the ring at this time, from Las Vegas, Nevada and weighing in at 255 pounds.... ‘SLICK’ RICKY RENO! [“Sharp Dressed Man” begins to pump out of the PA system, and the crowd begins to give the approaching Ricky Reno a pretty loud heel pop. Reno walks down the aisle with a cocky expression on his face, and nearly gets into fight with one of the fans lining the aisle. Reno steps into the ring and heads for his corner, while “Hair of the Dog” comes on next.] TD: And his opponent, accompanied to the ring by Jack Moran, from Austin, Texas... BREWSTER COGBURN! [Brewster and Jack walk out to a loud pop from the fans, but Cogburn is about as hospitable to the fans as ever, which isn’t much. He concentrates on his opponent, and as he steps through the ropes Reno tries to attack him with a kneelift.] AM: That didn’t take long, they’re already into it. [Reno goes for a full nelson and tries to lock it in, but Brewster Cogburn blocks it and hits Reno with an earringer, then slaps him in the face. This only makes Ricky madder, and in his anger Brewster takes him down with a legsweep. Cogburn throws Reno to the ropes and misses with a clothesline as Reno ducks, then comes up on the other side and grabs Brewster with a clawhold. Cogburn struggles to break the hold, and does so after a low blow to Reno’s groin. Reno reverses a whip into the corner, and charges at Brewster with a knee.] DR: So far this match has been pretty even. Neither’s paying a lot of attention to the rules, that’s for sure. SS: Rules are made to be broken. [Cogburn chops Ricky Reno enough times to get out of the corner, which gets the crowd cheering. Reno starts to chop Brewster in return, and they trade hard knife-edge chops to the delight of the crowd, who love a great fight. Cogburn comes off the ropes for a flying forearm, but Ricky Reno counters it with a duck-down move and then takes Cogburn down with a go-behind, locking him in a front facelock. Brewster grabs the ropes after a few seconds, then kneelifts Reno and places ‘Slick’ Ricky on the turnbuckle. He drives Reno into the mat after executing the Bulldog Supreme, and then covers him with one hand. Reno kicks out as the count nears three, and Cogburn is shocked. He pulls Reno to his feet by the hair, and a full nelson nearly gets the submission. Reno hooks his leg around Brewster’s and takes him down to the ground, but finds himself in a standing wristlock moments later. Reno again struggles to get out, and a whip from Brewster, not to mention the Corral leader springing off the ropes, gets Reno down after a flying clothesline. Reno stands as Cogburn comes off the ropes again, but Reno manages to give his opponent a tilt-a-whirl suplex, but Brewster doesn't sell it at all. Cogburn elbows Reno again, then delivers another Bulldog Supreme, and this time there is no kickout for the Las Vegas native.] TD: Here is your winner, as a result of a pinfall... BREWSTER COGBURN! SS: He didn’t even get a chance to use the Texas Cloverleaf! I tell you, Brewster Cogburn is scared of Reno’s finisher. AM: No, Brewster’s been in enough fights to know how to avoid a personal finisher. Reno on the other hand doesn’t, he took the Bulldog Supreme twice. [Reno rolls out of the ring to clear his head of all the cobwebs, while Brewster celebrates his win by puffing on a Cheroot. He walks up the ramp hearing the cheers, but doing his best to ignore them. As the camera pans on the crowd, it stops on a familiar face in the front row. Youth Gone Wild has just made an appearance, and is sitting front row, sharing his popcorn with the young fan on his left.] DR: Well, it appears we have a very interested observer for this next match. I'm going to get a quick word with him. [Rogers stands up, takes a house mic over, and greets YGW.] DR: Wild, given Legion's troubles with the Pride, and your personal issues with Kyle Hatcher, I guess I'm not surprised to see you down here. But what are you rooting for? Do you want Hatcher to pull this off and earn his way into the Pride? YGW: You know, Dave, when I heard about this, I just couldn't watch from the back. I mean, wrestling four matches in one night to get into the Pride, is like trying to break INTO the mental institution and giving yourself electroshock therapy. I mean, if you want my opinion, the two of them deserve each other. I figure Hatcher's going to get killed tonight, and I wanted to be front row center to watch it. And hey, if by sheer dumb luck he takes out a couple of Brett's boys...then this goes from a good night, to a GREAT night. [With that, Wild sits down, exchanging high fives with his neighbors, as Rogers returns to the broadcast table.] ============================================= -------PRIDE GAUNTLET MATCH #1------- ‘PRIMETIME’ KYLE HATCHER versus DANIEL KIDD ============================================= TD: Ladies and gentlemen, the following contest is scheduled for one fall, and is the first match tonight in the special Pride Gauntlet. As you know, should Kyle Hatcher successfully defeat all four members of the Pride in one evening, he will be officially inducted into the Pride. And now, introducing his first opponent, from Leeds, England and weighing in at 225 pounds, accompanied to the ring by Cameo... DANIEL KIDD! [As “Lakini’s Juice” by Live starts up, Daniel Kidd walks through the curtains and down the ramp, carrying his cruiserweight title in his hands as usual. He takes the time to remind the fans that he, not Chris Scottsdale is champion, and receives a few boos, a big heel pop, and even laughter at his claims. Cameo just receives catcalls, pure and simple. Daniel climbs into the ring and stands on the ropes, daring Hatcher to come out. The response is “Dr. Feelgood”, which replaces the Pride’s theme on the speakers.] TD: And his opponent... From Cincinnati, Ohio and weighing in at 236 pounds... ‘PRIMETIME’ KYLE HATCHER! [Hatcher comes running out, with a look of determination on his face, eager to get the challenge underway. He slides under the ropes into a good oldfashioned bootstomping from Daniel, as Cameo goes right to work cheering Dan on. Hatcher seems to actually be cheered by the crowd, who would love to see the Pride defeated tonight, one after another.] SS: Hey, Hatcher’s under the weight limit for cruisers. AM: And? SS: Doesn’t that make this a title match? AM: Maybe, _if_ Daniel had the belt. [Kyle Hatcher takes the kicks from Daniel and still gets back to his feet, then goes for an enzuigiri to the face. Daniel takes a piece of it, but not the full blow and leaps into a Frankensteiner, taking Hatcher to the mat. Youth Gone Wild stands up and mockingly gives Dan a ‘golf clap’ for it, which makes everybody in the crowd around him burst into laughter and cheers. Daniel runs into the ropes and drops Hatcher with a football tackle, but Hatcher manages to duck a flying forearm, then mount the ropes and come down on top of Daniel with a flying cross bodyblock. He catches Daniel and tries to hook the tights for the pin, but Dan kicks out first. Daniel attempts another football tackle, but Kyle Hatcher counters it with a kick to the head, a kick to the thigh, and a kick to the ankle.] SS: Sweet Shin Music! [From here the crowd starts to chant “Prime-Time”, with Wild leading the pack. Hatcher looks around and smirks, then grabs Dan by the head and uses it as a battering ram into the corner. From there a sunset flip off the top rope gives Hatcher a two count, and Hatcher traps Kidd on the mat with leg scissors. Daniel suffers through it for a few seconds, and then Hatcher tries to end the match with the Doctor Bomb. The Pridesman blocks it, takes Kyle down with a faceslam, and uses a knee to the back to reduce Hatcher to his knees. The pseudo-champion takes Kyle Hatcher down with an enzuigiri to the face, and gets an octopus hold on him, but makes a mistake as he tries to go for a Frankensteiner from the top rope. As he does, Hatcher jump off the ropes himself and executes a stunning superbomb onto Dan, which again makes Wild jump up, and he makes a big production over cheering for Hatcher. Cameo shoots a look at Wild that would melt steel, and he grins and waves back.] SS: He can’t be allowed out here. DR: He bought a ticket like everybody else. SS: Raided his piggybank, did he? [A small "Kyle Hatcher" chant is being started, again the work of a certain Legionnaire. Inside the ring Hatcher hits Daniel Kidd with a senton, and as he pins Daniel he pulls the tights. The referee sees it and breaks up the attempt, which gets Kyle livid.] [Part 2] SS: I like Kyle Hatcher, but he’s not Pride material if he gets caught at that. AM: Yes, he should hide it like any good Pridesman would. SS: Exactly. AM: You’re hopeless. [Hatcher grabs Daniel and places him in a painful boston crab, then lets go of the legs and jumps into the air, coming down on Daniel with a sharp knee to the back. He grabs Daniel in a chinlock and digs his knees into the Kidd cousin, hoping to make him submit.] AM: He did it! He’s got Daniel Kidd ready to say “I quit”! [Instead, the crowd gasps as the rest of the Pride pours out of the locker room, and hits the ring. Brett Greene grabs Hatcher and rips him off Daniel, DDT’ing him into the mat and instantly knocking him out. Outside the ring, Youth Gone Wild watches with a somewhat smug look on his face, as if to say “I figured as much”. Scott Daniels climbs to the top rope and comes down on Hatcher with a flying elbowdrop, and Shawn Vexton pulls him back to his feet while Brett and Scott help Dan to his feet. And when I look at people like 'Crimson' Joe Reed headlining cards... when I see Alex Kidd t-shirts being sold to the kids... when I see people actually buying toasters because they say the word 'Pride' on it... it makes me sick. Paul Reed firing Serge Annis may have been a work... but the actions of The Epitome of Evil leading up to my termination were not. I am set to take back my rightfull place in the UWF. Ruling on the Apocalypse's throne of bones... ruling over the thousands upon thousands of worthless souls of British Wonderkid fans... ruling an organization that has given the word true meaning to the word evil. And with ruling such a place, comes great responsability. And as the UWF's saviour, the one who destroyed Joe Reed's legacy as UWF champion... my first priority is to rid the UWF of the plague of locusts called the AEWA. [The crowd cheers madly, but Serge does not seem to care or even awknowledge the fans' existence.] SA: In the holy ground of wrestling... Madison Square Gardens, Serge Annis will sentence the Crimson one to eternal damnation!!! In the holy ground of wrestling, Serge Annis will take the AEWA back with him, through the inner regions of my mind... and they shall crumble to dust. In the holy ground of wrestling, I, Serge Annis shall set loose the inner demons of a paradox version of Hell I call home... AND MAKE SURE.... that the Crimson one will NEVER disrespect... SERGE ANNIS' UNIVERSAL WRESTLING FEDERATION!!! [Serge swings at Moe Owen's hand and connects with the mic thus knocking it out of Moe's hands and flies to the floor. Moe gets a scared look as Serge' calm blue eyes begin to fill with an inner rage, and an all-too-familiar sadistic grin comes across the Epitome of Evil. Moe runs to the ropes and dives through them and narrowly avoids landing on his head and miraculously gets his two feet on the floor first. 'Some Days It's Dark' plays as Serge Annis stands in the ring grinning madly as the lights drop.] DR: Well, I guess this means we’re set for the main event! ======================================= SERGE ANNIS versus ‘CRIMSON’ JOE REED ======================================= TD: Ladies and gentlemen, the following contest is scheduled for one fall, and is tonight’s main event. First, standing in the ring at this time, from Oakville, Ontario, Canada and weighing in at 290 pounds... ‘THE EPITOME OF EVIL’ SERGE ANNIS! [Flames again spew forth from the turnbuckles, surrounding Serge inside the ring. Serge stands there and watches the fire intensely(perhaps a method of preparation for the match?) while the hardcore crowd half cheers him for being UWF, and half cheers him for the frightening new attitude. In other words, Serge has the crowd solidly behind him, not something he wanted though.] TD: And his opponent... From Lexington, Kentuck- [Tom Davis is interrupted from his introductions as Joe Reed sneaks out of the crowd, bat in hand, and enters the ring from behind, attacking Serge with the Louisville Slugger. He jams the butt of the bat into the small of Serge’s back, dropping Serge to one knee, and then cracks it broadside across his shoulderblades. The Lady in Red climbs out of the crowd too, standing in Reed’s corner as the bell rings.] DR: What a dirty sneak attack from Joe Reed! I thought he’d have the decency to come out and face Serge Annis like a man. AM: You thought wrong Dave. Joe Reed is a piece of human trash, and he’s proved it for sure tonight. [Reed puts the bat in front of Serge’ throat and begins to choke Annis out, while driving several knees into the back area once more. The referee gets the bat away from Reed to start the match, and Joe bulldogs Serge the short distance to the mat, then rolls him over and feeds him numerous closed fists to the head. Reed picks Serge up and delivers the Kentucky Backsmasher to the Lethal Protector, then tries to cover him for the pin. Somehow, even with all the damage withstood, Serge Annis kicks out mightily, throwing Joe a good three feet into the air and then rolls to his feet, standing defiantly.] SS: He’s alive, he’s alive! Serge Annis is up, and he’s pissed! [Serge indeed was, grabbing Reed’s head and smashing it into the ringpost, then sets up on the top rope and executes a belly to back superplex down to the mat, and gets up while the crowd chants his name. Annis gives the crowd a look that would melt ice, which silents only a few people in the first row. Annis bounces off the ropes and drops a leg across Joe Reed’s throat, and then tries to cover him. However, Serge lifts Reed’s head and shoulders off the mat at the two count, and a forearm shot seemingly puts Reed out cold in the middle of the ring. Now Serge gets that ‘look’ again, and climbs to the top rope. Facing the crowd and with his back to the ring, Serge Annis leaps off and performs a move only seen once before, and never in the UWF...] DR: A MOONSAULT BY SERGE ANNIS! AM: How the hell did he get up high enough to do that? Sure, his form wasn’t great, but he managed to catch enough of a flip to really take care of Reed. SS: He also injured himself, Serge doesn’t look like he can get up right now. [Serge, having come down partially on his own legs, struggles to stand up with the help of the ropes, and has to lean on them for support. Joe Reed meanwhile, isn’t moving at all, and suddenly another enemy of his arrives, and stands behind the Lady in Red.] SS: Hey look, that’s Los Muertos! Why’s he out here? [Unsuspecting, the Lady in Red feels a tap on her shoulder and turns around, shocked to see Los Muertos. In her shock, she’s unable to do anything as Muertos plants a big kiss on her, drawing the loudest laughter of the night! Muertos grins at her and points at the downed Reed, then escapes into the crowd, leaving the LiR stunned at ringside.] SS: That was great! Somebody finally shut her up! Hahaha! DR: That’s not going to be good for Los Muertos when he finally gets Reed inside the ring. Joe’s going to be upset. AM: And that’s a shame. [Reed finally begins to stir, as Serge manages to drop a fist onto the temple of Reed and covers. The referee counts, but Reed kicks out as Serge didn’t hook the leg, and both men try standing again, a process that takes a couple of minutes. Serge is up first, but Joe manages to be standing soon after, then goes to work again on the back of the Epitome. He delivers a few forearm shots to Annis’ back, then looks at the LiR and wonders why she’s in a state of shock, unmoving. Annis grabs Reed and yanks him over the top rope by the head, and then bounces off the opposite ropes. Like a freight train, Serge barrels over the top rope and catches Reed with a cross bodyblock, albeit one that sends both men to the ground in equal amounts of pain.] DR: Serge Annis is actually destroying himself in the process of going after Joe Reed. What a match this is turning into. AM: Is it me, or does Serge look more muscular since the last time he was here in the UWF? SS: I think he’s being working out ever since he left. Boy does it show. And competing in the IIWF doesn’t hurt either. [Reed finds the bat at ringside, and while still laying on the floor cracks Serge in the head with it, knocking Annis for a loop. Reed finds the strength to push Serge onto the ring apron, and drag a table over so it’s touching the apron. Reed climbs up and pulls Serge up by his head, then to a raucous cheer from the crowd, executes the Kentucky Backsmasher out of the ring, through the table!] DR: That could have ended the careers of both men! What a devastating shot. [Reed stands at about the six count and tries to roll back into the ring, but Annis grabs him by the leg and keeps him out, then amazingly works to his feet and drives Joe Reed’s skull into the ground with the Epitomizer, Serge’s new version of a chokeslam. Neither man can answer the ten count, and the referee declares the match over, but the two men continue to batter each other with fists, not caring about the non-decision in the contest. Officials swarm from the back and try seperating the two men, without much luck.] TD: Ladies and gentlemen, as a result of a double countout, the referee has declared that this match is a DRAW! DR: I’ve never seen such brutality before, they simply don’t care about their own welfare. They just want to see the other man incapacitated permanently. Folks, we are out of time tonight, be sure to watch Slam N’ Eggs tomorrow morning! Goodnight from New York City! [The cameras continue to follow the action as Serge and Joe Reed battle their way up the ramp, with the Lady in Red following with a chair behind them. Fade down to black.]
