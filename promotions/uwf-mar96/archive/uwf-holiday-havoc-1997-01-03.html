UWF Holiday Havoc, 3 January 1997

3 January 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Free For All] [Fade up from the Viewers Choice logo to a UWF logo, half covered in snow. The logo shakes the snow off, and explodes, revealing the capacity crowd of the Skydome in Toronto Ontario, Canada. Amy Marshall is standing at the entranceway to the aisle, decked out in a stunning red strapless dress that matches her fire-red hair.] Amy Marshall "Welcome to the Free For All, and hello Toronto! This is it, the countdown is on for Holiday Havoc! Are you ready?!?" [The crowd bursts into applause, cheers, and other such popping activities] AM "Tonight you will see Kelly Maloney defend the world title against the British Wonderkid, Alex Kidd![A loud pop for Alex] You will see Serge Annis and Brewster Cogburn duke it out in a lumberjack match![A major pop for the two men mentioned] And you will see Scott "Hotspot" Daniels take on Stephan LeRoc![A third cheer, but this one is mainly heel heat] The Universal Wrestling Federation is going to take you into the New Year! We're starting later than usual as so the event will coincide with the beginning of 1997! Let's go to a prerecorded tape featuring Alex Kidd." [Not only the home viewers see the tape, as it is also played on the closed circuit tv screens as well.] *Interview taped Dec. 25th, 1996, from London, England.* [Peter Phillips is sitting on a couch in a home. Alicia Powers is sitting in a chair. Alex Kidd is sitting next to Phillips on the couch, holding Alicia's hand. A plaque-shaped object is sitting on the coffee table, but is covered up by a cloth. A Christmas tree is seen in background with several presents under it. Kidd looks very serious.] Peter Phillips: "Hello UWF fans, I am Peter Phillips here in Alex Kidd's own home in London, England on a special interview assignment for Holiday Havoc. First, Alex, thank you for inviting us all into your own home." Alex Kidd: "Oh, it's no problem. My pleasure really." [Kidd smiles] PP: "Alex, when the fans back in the US see this, RINGLORD will be over. How do you think you will of did?" AK: "I can't promise victory, nor can anyone else. But I can assure everyone that I tryed my very best and I hope I did not let down a single person." PP: "Now Alex, why is it you requested this special interview?" AK: "Well, it wasn't really me who asked for it to be today." [Kidd looks at Alicia and smiles.] Alicia Powers: "I asked the heads at UWF to have this special interview today to be before RINGLORD, so Alex can take the proper time to properly train for Kelly Maloney." PP: "All right. Alex, rumours are still abound as to your contractual status in UWF. Right now you are still currently signed to UWF, but what about after Holiday Havoc?" AK: "Well, I will be honost. I have been looking around and have been offered numerous contracts to other feds. I haven't accepted any yet because I feel I am not done in UWF. I'm in UWF to conquer the childhood dream of once and for all becoming a World Heavyweight champion. I was U.S, but it wasn't quite the same. And if I can't beat Kelly Maloney for it in UWF, I may consider to try to accomplish the dream elsewhere because I have had ample opportunity in UWF." PP: "And what about Kelly Maloney. He has stated that he thinks you are too heavily concentrating on the RINGLORD and not the title match on the 31st. He even was rumoured to of said that if you think RINGLORD is the biggest match of your life, you are wrong." AK: "Kelly said that? Good for him. He can't really understand the pressures of RINGLORD on me. I am telling everyone that RINGLORD is the biggest match of my carrer as a wrestler. But my match with Kelly Maloney is definately the biggest match ever in UWF for me. I've concentrated very hard about our match Kelly. I started training for you the day after you beat Tom for it. And other people are complaining that I am getting the UWF World title shot despite not being number one contendor. The number one contendor is Chamoflage Chameleon if I am not mistaken?" PP: "That is correct." AK: "Well I hope everyone remembers back to November ninth when I beat the Chameleon. I gave him his only loss in UWF so far. And I intend on doing so to Kelly Maloney." PP: "Speaking of Kelly's undefeated singles carrer, he feels that no one is respecting him despite the undefeated streak." AK: "Kelly, you have a great record for sure. But for the book, I have had more losses than you have had matches, and I've doubled the number of that with wins. I respect you very much Kelly. You are a very hard working guy, and usually respectable. I'm awknowledging it Kelly and I am planning on countering it. You'll see what I mean in the ring." PP: "Some say you have had too much going on in the last month Alex for you to focus directly on the UWF World title, especially within the last week." AK: "Last night I wrestled Mark Durman, and I'll be honost, I didn't think once about my match with Kelly. This Saturday I have a tag match with Tom, I have RINGLORD tonight, and in six days my match with Kelly. And that is just wrestling Peter. I've had a family death recently to deal with too. But I'm trying not to let everything get to me. I am taking one thing at a time, and now it's time for Kelly Maloney." PP: "On a different note Alex, you won a very prestigous award in the RSPWF awards for your Sumersault Bulldog as "Best Finisher"." [Peter holds up the plaque and hands it to Alex.] "What are your comments on that?" AK: "Well, I really want to thank all who voted for me and the move. I think YGW and Tom should also deserve part of this award because lord knows in practices, we developed the move very well. Without those lumps on their heads, it wouldn't of been possible." [Alex chuckles and looks at camera.] "I'm just kidding Tom! You know, I heard that Kelly doesn't even know what the somersault bulldog is! apparently he thought it was a headlock or something. Well Kelly, I'll tell you this. You'll know what it is when I hit you with it in Toronto." PP: "Alex, starting just last night, you began perhaps the busiest and most stressful week of your career. Are you ready for Kelly Maloney?" AK: "Well...I'm as ready as I can be... but I of course am going to train as much as I can. Review old tapes. DO what I can. In fact after Saturday, no one's going to see hide nor hair of me, because...." AP: "I'm going to make sure Alex gets enough time for training in and nothing else." AK: "You heard the lady." PP: "Alex, is there anything else you want to say?" AK: "First to Kelly Maloney, have a merry Christmas. Second, don't think for a second that I am underestimating you. I know you and what you are all about, and I owe Tom one for what you did to him. I owe my fans one for letting them down all the way back to Gold Rush, and most of all, I owe a 15 year old dream one because I want to be the next UWF World champion, and I'll be damned if I don't try my hardest!" PP: "Thank you Alex for inviting us into your home on this joyous holiday. For all of you fans back in US with your families, I'm Peter Phillips on the job." [Phillips, Kidd and Alicia stand up and Alex and Peter shake hands while Alicia holds the BEST FINISHER award.. fade to black.] [Fade back to Amy Marshall, who is now standing backstage in the locker room area.] AM "I should point out that Alex has not been seen since that interview, and it has not yet been confirmed that he has arrived at the Skydome. We'll keep you posted as that story develops. Let's head to the ring for the first match of the Free For All, Ricky Johnson and Desperado. Hello ladies and gentlemen, welcome to the Free For All." SS "And welcome to Ricky Johnson's Nightmare. You saw what they did to him on Saturday." SS "Not all of us." =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- TOM LANDIS VS PSYCHOSIS3 =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- Psychosis3 came out to a rousing chorus of boos, and it didn't help matters that Smash was with him. Since he was last in the Skydome for Wrestlemania VI (Demolition beat Haku and Andre for the tag titles for you historians), he had severly dropped in popularity. Psychosis3 climbed to the apron, and ropeflipped himself into the ring. Then "Under Pressure" by ZZ Top blasted on, and the crowd cheered like mad. Tara "Sunburst" Marshall came out first, leading Tom Landis out to the ring. They shook hands with Legion fans lining the aisle, and Landis burst into the ring. The bell hadn't rung when Psychosis3 launched himself at Landis, catching him with a spin kick that would have made Savio Vega proud. Landis found himself a victim of a belly to belly suplex, and the Psychosis executed a tilt-a-whirl backbreaker. He tried for a snap mare but Landis threw him in the direction of the ropes. P3 came back and missed Landis with a shoulderblock, then took a double axehandle chop to the back. The former Heartbreaker's temper rose, and a hotshot took P3 to the mat. Landis attempted a back suplex but instead was nailed with a backwards kick. Landis hopped up, avoiding a dropkick to the knee and grabbed Psychosis3 in a DDT. Instead, P3 backdropped Landis, though he did a complete flip and landed on his feet. A jumping side kick from P3 slowed Landis, and then a missle kick from the top rope sent him to the Skydome floor. A luchador in the true meaning of the word, Psychosis3 dove over the top rope and elbowed Landis. Tom was on rubber legs and P3 tossed him back through the ropes. Landis struggled to his feet and caught P3 as he tried to come off the top rope. A belly to back superplex from Landis evened the match up, and each took a few seconds before getting up. Psychosis3 executed a springboard moonsault that delighted the crowd, and Landis barely kicked out of a cover. Psychosis3 whipped Landis into the ropes but was clotheslined, then took Landis's cartwheel kick and a Widowmaker. The crowd counted along as the referee counted, but P3 kicked out at two. A double clothesline again stopped the match for a while, and then a series of reversals on an armbar led to a Landis savate kick that was ducked by his opponent. Psychosis3 frankensteiner'd Landis to the mat, and then dropkicked him in a sitting position. His speed took over as Landis couldn't keep up. A flying headbutt landed on the Legionnaire, and then a slingshot legdrop followed. Landis kicked out again, and rolled to the floor. Instead of paying attention to his opponent, he tried to recover. Unbeknownst to Landis, Psychosis3 set himself up for a spectacular Asai moonsault to the floor. He connected completely, and then went for a powerbomb on concrete. Instead Landis backdropped him, and then ducked as P3 dove into the ringpost. Landis and P3 barely(and I mean barely)avoided the countout, crawling in by their fingertips. P3 set Landis up for a swinging neckbreaker and instead was rudely side suplexed. P3 whipped Landis into a turnbuckle but was rocked with a kick to the midsection. Landis grabbed him in a full nelsonand attempted a suplexed backwards, but P3 latched on by his legs to Landis rolled him up. A quick two count, and then Landis enzuigiri'd him in the back of the head. P3 went for a jumping side kick, connected, then climbed out to the apron. He launched himself to standing on the top rope and dropkicked Landis, all in a matter of a few seconds. Landis was covered, but put his leg on the ropes to break the count. P3's undoing was going up to the top rope, and coming off with a tumbleweed that missed Landis entirely. Landis climbed up top, executed a mero-sault and covered, for the three count and the win. WINNER---TOM LANDIS--- DR "Whoa! Landis came out on top, but just barely. That was a pretty exhausting match they fought." SS "Landis cheated again. Reggie was thrown over the top rope by Brian, and he and Neal double dropkicked Julio into the corner and then double backdropped him. Martin came in to a football tackle, front facelock, and swinging punch. He ducked a superkick and tagged in his partner, Kenny Carroll. Carroll shocked Brendan with a flying clothesline, and then tied him up with a bow and arrow submission hold. Brendan struggled out, and then was placed in a La Tapatia(surfboard where the wrestler is lifted into the air by the arms and legs). Brendan couldn't get out of it, but Brian helped him by kicking Carroll in the head. Reggie rushed in to help, and an allout six man war was waged. Brian and Neal worked over Reggie, while Brendan took on Julio. Carroll tried to help out Reggie, but after Brian dropkicked Reggie he lifted Reggie and guillotine dropped him onto the top rope. Julio whipped Brendan towards the ropes but had the move reversed, which sent Julio, Brian and Neal over top to the floor leaving Carroll and Brendan in the ring. Kidd climbed the ropes, and executed a moonsault onto Luis from the top rope. Garavello was almost in lala land, and yet when Kidd threw him back into the ring Luis kicked him in the back of the head. Kidd slumped to the mat and was easy pickings for The Dive, but at the last second pitched Luis off the top rope onto his head. Kidd stood in position and came off the top turnbuckle, hitting the champion with his Shooting Star Press. Luis was stunned, but still put his foot on the bottom rope to stop the count and save his belt. Kidd dragged Luis into the middle of the ring and applied the figure four leglock, with nowhere for Luis to go. Nowhere, except to reverse it on poor Daniel who wasn't in a position to let go immediately. He eventually did, but not until after he had partially wrecked his knee for the duration of the match. He limped into the corner, and Luis did the same in the opposite corner. They stared at each other, then came to the center of the ring and locked up once more. Luis knee'd Daniel in the midsection, then set him up for a powerbomb. Instead of following through, Daniel managed to take his legs out from under him, set him up and then slingshot him right into the cornerpost. Luis's head struck the metal, and it was lights out as Daniel rolled him up from behind, kept Luis' shoulders down, and pinned him for the title.
