You never _DID_ tell me what you're doing here at this time. Fop: Just seein' an old friend, takin' care o' a bitta business. Ya know the deal. I ain't been here in a while. Moe: No. [Moe doesn't know what to say... to say he's flustered is an understatement. He's a poor liar.] Moe: Listen, I'd better let you go! You've got to get ready for the big falls count anywhere match. [Rogers grins.] Fop: Nah, Moe, I'll give ya five minutes.. Moe: Uhhh, no... no, you go! I wouldn't want to be blamed for your poor showing... err... not that I think you _will_... in fact, I hope you don't! [Rogers looks at Moe, and seems to assume that he's lost his marbles. Cut back to ringside.] _______ ___ __ | _ \' _| |_.--------.-----------------. |. 1 / _| _| | \ |. _ \__| |____|__|__|__| \ |: 1 \ \ |::.. . / MAIN EVENT \ '-------' FALLS COUNT ANYWHERE WAR \ | Scott "The Fop" Rogers & "Playboy" Ronnie D \ | vs. "The Epitome of Evil" Serge Annis & \ | Youth Gone Wild \ |_____________________________________________________\ This match, by far the favorite of the Brawl from the Malls, was as wild as previous falls count anywhere matches have been. Serge Annis and Youth Gone Wild, the unlikeliest team you'll ever see, started things out with a direct assault on the Fop, while Ronnie D took his own sweet time to arrive. When he did, it wasn't long before all four men were battling in the aisle, and Wild grabbed D in a headlock for an eyeburn up the entire length of the escalator railing. A bulldog onto the floor of the second level left D rattled, and Annis took a different way, throwing Rogers into the crowd and blasting him with chairs. He threw Rogers into an open elevator car and followed, so while the doors shut we caught up again with Ronnie D and Youth Gone Wild. Ronnie, in complete control after smashing a birdcage over the head of Wild at the pet store, tried to apply a figure four leglock in the middle of the mall. Wild tipped a display of catfood over onto Ronnie and then scrambled to his feet, peppering the Playboy with shots to the head. When the doors of the escalator opened, Annis had Rogers slumped over his shoulder, and ran right out with a powerslam. But at the last second Rogers slipped away and shoved Serge through the window of a toy store. Glass everywhere and Annis sporting some brand new cuts, the heavyweight champion grabbed a pair of plastic hockey sticks and snapped each across his opponent's forehead. It was the Fop's turn to take control and he did so, pulling Serge down to the food court nearby. The proprietor of the Orange Julius wasn't happy as Rogers overturned the huge drink machine across the back of Serge, and the sting of orange into those cuts was almost enough to have him start screaming. Youth Gone Wild on the other hand was screaming as Ronnie D subjected him to the worst punishment of all... by dragging him into the music store just as they were playing the new Backstreet Boys album. Wild began to sob, but still blocked several punches and threw Ronnie into the wall of new releases. A diving shoulderblock missed though, and as Wild stood stunned and unaware, several copies of Ricky Martin's CD fell on him. Ronnie grabbed Wild and threw him behind the counter, then attacked with the price gun. Lucky for Wild he wasn't using all his ear piercings today, because Ronnie surely would have made a new hole to stick the tag on with. Serge meanwhile, slumped across a table in the middle of the food court, took a plastic tray to the head and then an irish whip into several more tables. Annis falls ass over teakettle, and soon after Rogers picks Annis up by the bloody head and directed him towards the hairdressing salon. About a dozen women screamed at the sight of the two and ran out of the store, while Rogers tried to throw Annis into the shampoo chair. Annis resisted and poked Rogers in the eyes, sat him down in it instead and started to choke him while running cold water over his head at the same time. A low blow saved the Fop, but not his coiffure. Annis himself splashed some water on his face, but Rogers added more agony to the wounds with sticky hairspray, blinding Annis. Back to the lightweights, as Ronnie D grabs Wild and tossed him on top of a bench in the middle of the mall. A kneedrop to the chest stunned Wild and a second knee, this time to the stomach, knocked the wind out of him. Ronnie tried for the cover, but is unable to keep him down. They headed into the huge department store we saw the Fop and Moe Owens in earlier, and Ronnie cracked a mannequin in two over the back of the Legionnaire. Back in the hair salon, Annis grabbed the advantage by pulling down the rack of hair supplies on top of Rogers, then tossed one of the chairs underneath the hairdryer on top of the pile. Rogers crawled out, and Annis slammed him into the wall a few more times before throwing him out into the open area of the mall. Rogers stumbled down towards the department store as well, and Annis followed closely, after grabbing a steel chair from the Florida branch of the Chair Shop seen back in Toronto(like that wasn't planned). Rogers takes a swift chairshot to the back of the legs, and then one across the head. As he stumbled into the store, he pulled himself back onto his feet with the help of a display of sweaters. Only a few feet away, Youth Gone Wild swung desperately with a purple umbrella at Ronnie D, and when it connected the Playboy hit the ground, out cold. Wild looked at the umbrella, but then pulls back the shield to discover... a baseball bat? Wild stomped away on Ronnie as the Fop keeps running from Serge, towards the umbrella stand. As Annis stands in front of Rogers, he grabbed an identical purple umbrella to the one Wild had and swung it across Serge's skull. When it snapped in half and doesn't harm Serge, Rogers looks stunned and checked it, obviously not finding the weapon he'd planted. Wild meanwhile stood behind Rogers and tapped him on the shoulder. Rogers turned around to catch the blunt end of the baseball bat in the stomach, and then Wild threw the bat to Annis. The Epitome of Evil smashed Rogers in the ribs and then knocks him down. The champion scampered away again, underneath a table of pants and then stands up, overturning them on top of both Wild and Annis. He headed for the shoe department as Ronnie begins to stir, and the Playboy choked Wild with a pair of bluejeans after regaining his senses. Annis stalked after Rogers once more and followed him to the shoes department where he scrambles around, looking for something else. He finds a wingtip and grabs it, as Annis grabbed him by the throat. Rogers digs into the shoe and produced a package of salt, throwing it into the eyes of the beast to avoid an Epitomizer. Youth Gone Wild goes airborne off of a sales counter and tackled Ronnie onto the floor, peppering him again with punches. They rolled around trading shots until they knock into a stand of negligées in the women's department. The stand, and dozens of packages of nighties collapsed on top of the two wrestlers, and after Wild pushes the stand off of himself, he stood up with a ripped package and a negligée half over his head! He pulled it off, and began to choke out the Playboy with it. Rogers meanwhile had Annis in a headlock and dragged him over to the same area where their partners were, and slammed him on top of the display of negligées. Rogers searched amidst the packages for a few minutes, before finding what he wanted... a pair of brass knuckles stuck in the matted hair of Youth Gone Wild! He snuck up behind the Wild Pack leader and sent him to the floor with a forearm to the back of the head and then ripped a pair of brass knuckles off of him. Wild grabbed his head in pain as a few strands had been ripped out with the knucks, but as Rogers turns to use them, an enraged Serge Annis stood behind him. A hand to the throat, and an Epitomizer through a table of golf shirts laid out the champion, and Wild scored the victory for his team. Following the match, "Sexy Baby" Kyle Backwood emerged on the scene with a belt and began to whip Serge, choking him down with it and then doing the same to Wild. Rogers and Backwood left Serge down and out, with Ronnie D scrambling to get away as well. WINNERS: Serge Annis & Youth Gone Wild SS: That guy is a dead man walking!
