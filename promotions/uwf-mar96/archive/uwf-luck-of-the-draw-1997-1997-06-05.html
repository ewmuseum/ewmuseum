Brendan Maloney was next closest and hurled himself at both of them, sandwiching the two against the cage wall. Chris Scottsdale and Crimson Joe Reed traded punches, and Scottsdale stunned the former AEWA champion with a palm blow to the temple, and planted him with a german suplex before executing the Texas Cloverleaf to make sure Reed couldn't climb the cage wall. AM: That's an odd strategy. BD: Not really, if he can't walk he, he can't climb. Thus he can't get out of the cage. Bishop saved Joe Reed by pulling Scottsdale off and then dropped a knee into Reed's back himself. Bishop and Scottsdale hit Crimson Joe Reed with a double bodyslam before Greg Smith came to Reed's rescue, flattening Scottsdale with a running elbowsmash and set him up on the turnbuckle. Scottsdale attempted to slip out of the cage but Dimento pulled him down off the ropes and then shocked him with a brain buster. Julio Sanchez tried to help Scottsdale out by taking Greg Smith down in a russian legsweep, and Bishop had Joe Reed in a bearhug in the center of the ring. Joe Reed thumbed Bishop in the eye and then delivered a headbutt before Randy Acorn took the Avatar of Extreme down with his inverted DDT that he called PrideKiller. He picked him back up and whipped Bishop into the ropes, who ended up taking Brendan Maloney and Alex Kidd out from behind with a double clothesline instead of bouncing off the ropes. SS: Keep your head up Kidd! Haha! DR: Black Diamond, since you are going to take on the winner of the match, do you have a preference as to who wins tonight? And Joe Reed needs a lesson more than anyone else, I wouldn't mind pasting the floor with him. As Reed and Acorn walked towards Bishop, both were plowed into by the Avatar with a shoulderblock followed by a double underhook backbreaker, and right behind them Dimento and Brewster Cogburn got into a scrape. Cogburn tried to put the Darknight'er on the mat with a bridging back suplex, but Dimento beat him to the punch with a headlock takedown and then his partner from the lottery match, Jack Riley, made his first substantial move of the match as he helped Brewster up and then the two hit Dimento with a double football tackle into the cage. Cogburn dropped Dimento with a German suplex, and all Dimento could do was stun the Sheriff momentarily with an elbowsmash to the head. He whipped Cogburn into the ropes and went for a backdrop, but Riley grabbed his head and snapped it backwards, sending Dimento to the mat headfirst. Psychosis came up behind the rookie, and applied a half nelson into a side suplex on the Hammer, but once he was down, Riley brought Psychosis down to his level with a single-leg takedown. Oddly, Kenny Carroll and Chris Scottsdale engaged in a fistfight, even more odd considering Scottsdale is teaming with Carroll's old partner, Reggie Martin presently. DR: I guess Carroll is just testing Scottsdale to see if he's a worthy partner for Reggie. SS: You fool, he's doing it to win the match. Carroll went to the top rope, but Scottsdale was the cause of a fall this time, instead of the recipient. Scottsdale put Kenny in a front facelock, but the Conservative one replied with an abdominal stretch. Dimento tried to use that move to attack both men, but Carroll let go first and used Scottsdale's legs to batter Dimento in the face and then force him into the corner. Dimento came back with a rake of the eyes to Carroll, and he whipped Scottsdale into the ropes, both men taking clotheslines from others on the way to the ropes. Chris took Chris Nelson down with a standing wristlock, and Devon Taylor moonwalked over and stomped on Nelson's midsection. Nelson somehow reversed the armlock and took Devon out with a Yakuza kick to the face, and then hiptossed Scottsdale over. Psychosis having been attacked by Brewster Cogburn from behind, Devon Taylor and Jack Riley went at it, Riley knocking Taylor down with a flying bulldog and a hotshot. Mike Stewart hung back mostly up to this point, helping out Alex Kidd and Kelly Maloney when he got the opportunity. Joe Reed found him from behind and clipped his knee out, wrenching the leg with a toehold. Scottsdale made it out of Dimento's grip and sent him right into Demolition, then ran to the aid of Stewart. Reed saw him first and waistlocked him to the mat, and beat him with a series of punches to the face. BD: Boy, I'd like to teach him what the UWF's all about. With so many men in the match, help was usually not far away and Sterling Justice caught Psychosis from behind in a dragon suplex, which was followed by a legdrop from Sterling's stablemate Luis Garavello. Kelly Maloney had thus far spent most of the match taking chokeholds and other illegal tactics from Raekwon, who dropped him to the mat with a side suplex, but Alex Kidd came out of the corner with a bulldog headlock to knock Raekwon senseless. Chris Nelson took Luis down hard with a reverse neckbreaker, and Sterling Justice came to Luis' rescue with a chokehold, one that was furthered by Jack Riley into a double chokeslam. AM: BOOM! Down he goes. BD: I've never seen a double chokeslam before. Wow. The KO Corral reunited and went after Raekwon and Joe Reed, leaving Nelson and Riley to battle out. Riley made the mistake of trying to pick Nelson up off the mat with a Northern Lights suplex, but Chris Nelson came out of it with a backdrop driver. Psychosis grabbed Nelson with his hands and pulled his hair to get him out of the way, and dropped several knees on Riley's stomach. Keeping a low profile throughout the match at the moment was the battle between Jimmy Dangerous and Greg Smith. Each time they bumped into each other they turned around and locked up, splitting the advantages equally. BD: Everyone's giving so much in this match, they aren't stopping at anything to win. I have to remember though, they're doing that so they can come after me! SS: Well, someone's gotta...
