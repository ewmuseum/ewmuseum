Peter Phillips is also there, perhaps taking some time off from his Real Audio broadcasting duties to make this interview.] PP: Brett, in just a moment you'll be stepping out there into the Road for the Gold Battle Royal. What does this match mean to you, and how will you handle yourself in there? BG: There's always an element of luck in a battle royal, especially one with the weird kind of elimination rules this one has. The only thing you can trust in is yourself... and that's what I'm going to do. I'm going to spend every ounce of energy I can to stay in that ring, by hook or by crook. I may have some fan support now, but make no mistake, I intend to win... by hook or by crook. See you later, Peter. [Brett walks away as the introductions for the match seem imminent.] PP: Fans, we'll be back in just a minute with the beginning of our third hour here in Hawaii! [Fade to black.]
