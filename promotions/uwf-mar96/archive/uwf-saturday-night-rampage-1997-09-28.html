UWF Saturday Night Rampage, 28 September 1997

28 September 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Part 1] [Fade up from black, into a montage from the past week’s action. Narrarated by Dave Rogers, and with “War Machine” by KISS playing in the background. Shots of the Jericho-Riley, Serge-Reed, and the DI/Greer-Asylum matches roll.] DR: Another week, another shocking turn of events. Last Saturday night the UWF rocked the Big Apple, presenting not one but two candidates for match of the year. Not to mention a shocking assist by Asylum to their hated archrival Robbie Greer. Tonight we will talk to Asylum, to find out exactly what is going on and why they helped. [Clips now of Sunday Slam n’ Eggs roll, as the Unknown hits a top rope doctor bomb, Brett Greene and Joey Valachi attack Stevie Smith, and Running Eagle Feather makes Jimmy Dangerous submit to the Indian Pain Dance.] DR: Then on Sunday, the Pride practiced what they preached, a new sensation hit the Skyweight division, and Running Eagle Feather added another notch to his belt. Tonight Brett Greene faces Stevie Smith, who is bent on revenge for last Sunday. [Fade into clips from Thrashings, the debut of Entrophy, Frank White versus the Reformer, and Tim Vexton attacking Eddie Fantastic.] DR: And this past Tuesday saw the UWF roll into Albany. We saw a mysterious enigma by the name of Entrophy, possible trouble in the Fraternity of the Stone, and the United States Champion extracting a measure of his own revenge on a man who’s been a thorn in his side for well over a year now. Tonight Eddie Fantastic meets Shawn Vexton, Tim’s brother, while the members of Legion are set to conduct their first in-ring interview in a while. This and more tonight on Saturday Night Rampage, live from Buffalo! [Fade into the opening for Saturday Night Rampage. As "Let's Get Rocked" by Def Leppard is heard, fast paced clips of UWF shows start to flash by, each with the name of the wrestler at the bottom, as the logo jumps onto the screen, and then flickers on and off before disappearing for a second. Alex Kidd executes the Somersault Bulldog, followed by a Diamondback Death Drop from Sebastian Jericho, and then a Kentucky Backsmasher brought to us courtesy of 'Crimson' Joe Reed. The Sledge Hammer Submission is shown, as are the Pride Breaker, Pride Press, Pride and Pain, and Pride Bomb in succession. The DOA Full Nelson flashes by, as does the SnakeBite, Riot Drop, and the Wildflyer Moonsault finish up, and a strobed picture of Alex Kidd raising the Championship Belt at Summer Heat Wave. Fade into a shot of the Marine Midland Arena in Buffalo, New York. Outside, a large gathering of the Wild Pack is out front, chanting the name of the hometown favorite, Youth Gone Wild. Another fade brings us to the inside of the arena, and various pans across the screaming audience let some of the more excited audience members get on camera. Tons of signs, merchandise and posters can’t help but be seen(and neither can the usual [Fill in the name] 3:16’s. Each camera pans across the crowd until it rests on the announcers’ table, where Sam Steeley, David Rogers, and Amy Marshall sit. Each has on their UWF wear, Amy and Sam at odds over the Legion and Pride merchandise. Dave sits in the middle(literally and figuratively) with his UWF logo cap and baseball jacket.] DR: Good evening fans, and welcome as always to Saturday Night Rampage! We’re in Buffalo tonight, otherwise known as Youth Gone Wild’s backyard. SS: That explains the fans here. Have you ever seen such an ugly bunch? AM: Sorry Sam, I’d like to get out of here alive tonight. It’s a shame that Youth isn’t scheduled for action tonight, but he will be here with the other members of Legion for a special interview. SS: Oh yeah, there’s a think tank. DR: Also tonight we’ll be bringing you the debut of a new cruiserweight here in the league, by the name of Bobby Rain. SS: I’ve seen him in the past working for these small time promotions. Not as small as the AEWA mind you, but smaller than us. I have quite an interest in the match. AM: The only thing you have an interest in is his manager, Rachel Kennedy. Isn’t that right? SS: I uh- no. DR: Before we get to the action, I’d like to convey the news that a list of the best tag teams in the world came out this week, and the UWF didn’t do too bad. In the top 200 teams, believe it or not, even Scott Daniels and Tim Vexton placed. SS: What? But they’re not a tag team. AM: For a few matches they were Sam. DR: They placed as #187, and other teams on this list included the current UWF World Tag Team Champions Anarchy at #130, former ACWF Tag Champions Deadly Intentions right at #100, Lights, Cameras, Action in at #51, CWAA Tag Champs Twister was in there at #49. The Pride had the Twin Ninjas in there, ranked #35, and although they’re out of the league now, each combination of the Maloney Brothers made the list all the way up at #16. SS: The Maloneys? Good to see Brian, Neal and Brendan are getting the recognition they deserve. AM: Uh, you’re forgetting one. SS: Nope, don’t think so. DR: What about Kelly? He and Brian made up the first team, and he’s the only Maloney to wear World Title gold. Let’s hear from the newcomer, Bobby Rain, and his manager. [Sitting on the bed of a hotel room in the Holiday Inn not too far from the Marine Midland Arena in downtown Buffalo, New York is Bobby Rain. The newest addition to the UWF is wearing a tye dyed blue and white t-shirt, faded blue jeans being held up by a black belt decorated with silver bars, his dyed blonde hair been taking over by its natural brown color. Also in the baize colored apartment is the beautiful redhead Rachel Kennedy who in a casual state is wearing a pair of black jeans, black riding boots with silver buckles and a blue t-shirt enhancing her quite noticeable bust.] Bobby Rain: In just a few short days... this coming Friday on the twenty-seventh of September... I... Bobby Rain will make my ILLUSTRIOUS DEBUT against the man called Double J. Well I hate to "rain" on your parade Jesse ... but the time has come for one Bobby Rain to show the entire UWF... just what a real man is made of. Rachel Kennedy: It's a shame Double J, the stars will not be in your favor for this Friday night when Bobby Rain makes his debut in front of thousands at the Marine Midland Arena. The clouds will circle and the tear drops of heaven will flow as Bobby Rain cleanses the ring of all that is unworthy of his presence. Bobby Rain: You know Jesse... ole Dubba J, the UWF knows that they have placed their future into the hands of Bobby Rain. And you know as well as I do that Bobby Rain ain't one to disappoint... THAT'S WHY I am future UWF Cruiserweight Champion... and THAT'S WHY I, Bobby Rain, heir to the UWF Crown... will indeed come out victorious in our match. Rachel Kennedy: Sorry Double J ... but when you are getting your little southern butt kicked around the ring... try and remember... we LOVE you! Bobby Rain: We love EVERYBODY!!! And on Friday night Jesse ... we're gonna show ya just how much we love ya ... ain't that right Rachel? Rachel Kennedy: [devilish grin] You got that right Rain man. Bobby Rain: And Jesse... just remember something... I'm Bobby Rain... AND YOU'RE NOT! ========================================= DOUBLE J JESSE JOBBES versus BOBBY RAIN ========================================= TD: Ladies and gentlemen, the first match this evening is a debut contest. First, coming to the ring at this time... DOUBLE J, JESSE JOBBES! [Jobbes comes walking out to the ring with a microphone in hand, signing his own theme song, “Jobbing Tonight”. The crowd covers it’s collective ears, and Jobbes gets angry, then announces his new manager to come out. The Jobby Tonk Man strides out, the black paint from his ‘sideburns’ running down the side of his face. Mockabilly even decides to come out, and the fans just keep on groaning at the country trio.] DR: I can’t believe Jesse Jobbes would align himself with those two. SS: Hasn’t he always? DR: No! SS: Gee, I can’t even remember Jobbes being liked by the fans. TD: And his opponent... Hailing from Long Beach, California and weighing in at 220 pounds, accompanied to the ring by Rachel Kennedy... BOBBY RAIN! [The lights start to turn multicolored as Rain and Kennedy make their first appearance, walking down the ramp smiling brightly to “Nothing Else Matters” by Metallica. Kennedy has the male section of the crowd mesmerized, as Rain continues to smile at Jobbes until he hits the ring.] DR: There goes the Rain Man to work already. SS: Huh? AM: Sam, eyes on the match. [Bobby Rain starts off with a short jab to Jobbes, to get him mad. Jobbes does, and tries to fight back but Rain blocks every attempted shot. Rain helps Jobbes get him in a headlock, but then swiftly dumps him on his head with a back suplex.] SS: Now that was great! Rain caught that idiot by total surprise. DR: I’ll say this, Bobby Rain obviously has much talent. But his attitude is not going over well with the crowd, listen to the jeers. SS: He’s a real man, he doesn’t care what these idiots think. [Rain gets up and looks around, smiling as always. He helps Jobbes back on his feet, dusts him off, and plants him with a clothesline. The Jobby Tonk Man shouts instructions to Jobbes, as does Mockabilly. All the help only confuses Double J, and Bobby Rain climbs the ropes and takes him down with a flying dropkick that puts him over the top rope. Rain gets up and offers to help Jobbes back into the ring, and naively the youngster accepts. Rain holds the ropes open for Jobbes, then snaps them upwards, grabs him by the head, and executes the Rainmaker, a fisherman suplex. He holds the cradle, and Jobbes is counted out.] TD: Here is your winner, as a result of a pinfall... BOBBY RAIN! [“Nothing Else Matters” plays again, and Rachel Kennedy enters the ring to join Rain. In the process of crossing the ring, she walks right over poor Double J, then raises Rain’s arm. They walk out hearing the boos from the crowd, but it doesn’t deter them at all.] DR: And Bobby Rain debuts with a win over Jesse Jobbes. Impressive display from the newcomer, even if Double J did most of the work. SS: What do you mean, Rogers? DR: He had Jobbes doing all the work, and then surprising him with counters left right and center. SS: That just proves he’s a master technician. AM: And a master sneak. DR: Up next is a match that ‘Stylin’ Stevie Smith asked for personally. SS: Who? AM: Stevie Smith, formerly of the ACWF and Saturday Night Fever, the tag team that he and Danny Derrick used to form. SS: Oh, the scrub league. AM: Stop it Sam. Last Sunday Smith’s old partner Derrick matched up against Valachi, and came out on the losing end. Then Smith came out and helped Valachi and Brett Greene in a three on one beatdown, but found himself taking a pounding when the Pride turned on him. SS: So now Brett gets to take Smith apart completely this time. I like it. =========================================== BRETT GREENE versus ‘STYLIN’ STEVIE SMITH =========================================== TD: The following contest is scheduled for one fall. First, making his way to the ring at this time... ‘STYLIN’ STEVIE SMITH! [As a self-sung song called “Stevie’s So Sexy” begins to play, the crowd groans the same way they did when Jesse Jobbes came out. Smith bringing former French Connection manager Marty ‘the Manipulator’ Stevens out didn’t help, and they were booed down the ramp and into the ring.] SS: And this dork thought he was Pride material? I don’t think so. What’s he ever done that’s so special? AM: He caught a glimpse of himself in the mirror I suppose. A funhouse mirror at least. TD: And his opponent... From Baton Rouge, Louisiana and weighing in at 285 pounds, representing the Pride... BRETT GREENE! [As the crowd actually pops a little for “Lakini’s Juice”, Brett Greene comes out to the ring wearing a trademark “Pain is Temporary, but the Pride is forever” t-shirt. Greene looks out at the fans and a hint of a smile forms, Greene enters the ring and Smith lunges right for him.] DR: Whoa! It looks like Brett might have a few problems with Smith afterall! SS: Nah, this won’t last long at all. [Smith jumps on Greene with a forearm blast, then tries to stun him with an inverted DDT but gets twisted to the ground with an armdrag. Greene gets him in an armbreaker, then executes an octopus hold on ‘Stylin’ Stevie. Smith fights to get out of the hold, and actually manages to struggle to the ropes and hiptoss Brett out of the ring. The Pridesman lands on his feet though, and pulls him out by the legs, then winds up and blasts Stevie with a punch to the face. He drops him in a hotshot on the guardrail, and returns him to the ring while taunting a member of the Wild Pack, all at once.] SS: Man he’s got style. AM: He’s got nerve. That’s it. [Greene steps back through the ropes and says something to Stevie, no doubt nothing complimentary. He kicks Smith in the side of the head, pulls him to his feet and jams a knee into his midsection, then delivers the Pride Bomb, rolls Smith over and hooks the leg. Pinfall, ‘nuff said.] TD: Here is your winner, as a result of a pinfall... BRETT GREENE! [Brett rolls from the ring and picks his t-shirt up, then comes back in to place it over Stevie Smith’s laid out body. A Big Leg Drop from Greene adds some more insult, and then Greene grabs his t-shirt from the ‘unworthy’ one. He leaves to an incredibly loud heel pop.] DR: Well, give him points for self-confidence. AM: I’d rather ignore him totally. DR: That could work too. In just a few moments the CWAA Tag Team Champions Twister are set to face the Fabulous Ones, but first let’s take you to comments from Redeemer. Last Tuesday he appeared during the Reformer’s match with Frank White, but nobody knows why. [Scene: A desolate plain. As the camera pans the landscape, it comes across the figure of the Redeemer, staring out into space with his arms folded. He is dressed in all black, except for his trademark red mask. After a brief pause, he speaks without looking at the camera, as though he is talking to no one in particular.] Redeemer: Bishop. My old friend, my mentor, has felt the wind at his back once again. A betrayal? Perhaps, but we speak of the one man against whom I could never hold a grudge. He is gone, and for the moment, I remain. [Pause.] Redeemer: And what am I to do here? I see old enemies... Reed, Annis... but my friend the Reformer has other ideas. He has taken upon himself the mantle of leadership, and set his sights on the UWF World Heavyweight Championship. And as the only remaining Fraternity member, I must assume that I am to help him on this quest. [Pause. The Redeemer places a hand on his chin.] Redeemer: Oh, don't misunderstand, Reformer. I believe you can do it. I have seen you in action, and where others look past you to men with flashier costumes and better press agents, I see a man who has quietly destroyed all who came his way. Alone, you would be formidable... with me, you would be unstoppable. [Redeemer clenches a fist, as if trying to grasp a difficult concept.] Redeemer: But, things are not that simple anymore. Bishop led, I followed. You are my friend, Reformer, but I do not owe you my soul. Nor do I select targets based on what they wear around their waist. Alex Kidd and his band of fools, a pleasant diversion to some, an annoyance to others, but an enemy deserving of our attention? I am not sure. I am sure that I have walked away from too much in my life to cast our union aside lightly. Perhaps you still retain the fire that caught Bishop's attention, and made us comrades. I would hate for us to be enemies, but my loyalty is not quickly or lightly given. Reformer, you have claimed to be worthy of my loyalty. [Redeemer finally turns to face the camera.] Redeemer: We shall see, my friend. We shall see. [Cut back down to ringside.] DR: Strong words from one of the oldest members of the Fraternity. SS: I’d love to see him and the Reformer go at it, they both suck but they’d be good enough to injure each other permanently. AM: But where’s Bishop? He hasn’t been seen for weeks. He left the UWF, but it’s more important that we at least know what happened to him. SS: Let him go, I never liked him anyways. [HELLO BABY!!!! HA!!! can be heard over the PA as a loud guitar kicks in and “Good Enough” by Van Halen starts to play, followed by Ms. Miki coming out to the ring with microphone in hand.] Ms. Miki: Hello UWF fans!!!!! I want to bring out for you the most fabulous tag team on the face of planet earth. Ladies get ready because you’re not going to believe your eyes. I know you’re used to those fat, flab ridden, sweathog looking, old men you’ve spent your entire life with, but you don’t have to look at that anymore. I know you think it’s just another ugly wrestler who thinks he’s good looking, like all the wrestlers in the UWF, but NO not these two. Now, lets get to the good stuff!!!! [“Kiss of Death” by Dokken plays over the PA as “The Universal Heartthrob” Agito Nakajima and “Sweet” Sho Satsuma the Fabulous Ones come down and into the ring followed by Mr. Tsuburaya who’s sporting a black Versace original. Ms. Miki hands the mic to Mr. Tsuburaya as Agito and Sho flirt with the ladies in the crowd.] Mr. Tsuburaya: Kon ban wa to all the fans of the UWF. It seems to be the time for awards throughout the e-wrestling world, so I have a few of mine own, Tsuburaya Enterprises, to give out. [Just then an oriental man in street clothes comes out with three small statues of wrestlers on plaques and hands them to Mr. Tsuburaya.] Mr. Tsuburaya: The first award goes to the tag team combination who will be destroyed by The FABULOUS ONES very soon. They hail from a group of bakayaro losers that call themselves the Pride. They are Chris Nelson and Matt Richards the TWIN NINJAS! [The crowd gives a huge heel pop.] Mr. Tsuburaya: You can pick them up at anytime! Now onto award number two. This is the MEANINGLESS BANTER AWARD and it goes to... [Sho pulls an envelope from his tuxedo jacket with tails, and hands it to Mr. Tsuburaya who opens it]... ZOMBIE AND MADDOG--ANARCHY. I’ve never heard so much nothing talk in my life. Can anybody understand what they’re saying? [The crowd laughs] You two walk and talk like rejects from the dark ages. Wake Up!!!! It’s the twentieth century and the FABULOUS ONES are on top of the world. What you see in this ring [Mr. Tsuburaya points at the Fabs.] is the finest tag team ever to step into the squared circle, so why don’t you pull your tails out from between your legs and give them a title shot. Hmm, maybe your scared, or maybe you can’t understand english? Damn, I didn’t think about bringing a gibberish translator so you could figure out what I said. [Sho and Agito join in the laughter.] Mr. Tsuburaya: Now, for the final award. It goes to the guy who has the worst fitting nickname...FABULOUS FRANK WHITE. Sorry, but I don’t think you’re fabulous and neither do the fans of the UWF. [Some boo but most continue laughing] For instance, ladies in the crowd tonight I want to here your applause for true Fabulous things. First, “Sweet” Sho Satsuma? [Sho takes off his tuxedo jacket and starts posing and the woman errupt in cheers] O’kay, o’kay, now how about Frank White? [The women in the crowd go silent as a few boo.] Now, “The Universal Heartthrob” Agito Nakajima? [The stadium roars with applause as Agito strips off his robe and poses for the crowd, the ladies eat it up.] I rest my case! Hey, there solid gold, if you don’t like looking at the Fabulous Ones who are on the trophies than melt them down and turn them into neckless’ if you like. Ooh Anarchy, you could probably use them to buy a cow or sheep for food. Maybe, then you could make a coat out of the fur. It’s up to you. Arigato! Tom Davis enters the ring.] ================================== THE FABULOUS ONES versus TWISTER ================================== TD: Ladies and gentlemen, the following contest is scheduled for one fall. Currently in the ring at this time, from Nagoya, Japan, accompanied by Ms. Miki and Mr. Tsuburaya, the team of ‘Sweet’ Sho Satsuma and the ‘Universal Heartthrob” Agito Nakajima... THE FABULOUS ONES! With a total combined weight of 560 pounds, led to the ring by Kelly Perich, the team of ‘Tornado’ Paul Langford and Travis Conley... TWISTER! Kelly herself gets a fair share of applause, just like Ms. Miki before her.] DR: Twister certainly is popular up here. It should be interesting to see who has the crowd’s favour at War in the Desert, in the four team War Games match. You have to figure Twister will be popular there too, but as of late the crowds have started to support the Twinjas again, mainly because they’re pro-American, unlike the Fabulous Ones. They lock up and Sho is whipped into the ropes, then executes a sunset flip over the ‘Tornado’ into a pin attempt. An early kickout at one, and Langford hiptosses Satsuma over, and the Fabulous One lands on his feet, reverses it, and hooks his leg against Langford, rolling him through into a leg grip. Langford gets out of the move, catches Sho with a knee to the face, and then heads for the top rope, coming off with a flying fistdrop. Sho works ‘it’ for the crowd, allowing the referee to miss seeing Agito choke Langford out while standing in the corner. Sho Satsuma catches Paul Langford in a guillotine choke when he returns to the match, and tags in his partner. Satsuma is whipped into the ropes again, but leapfrogs Conley and tags in Nakajima. ‘The Universal Heartthrob’ gets an abdominal stretch on Travis Conley after a facerake, then throws his opponent out of the ring. Conley swings wildly but misses each time, and Sho peppers him with leg kicks before sending him back into the ring. Agito goes up top and comes down with a kneedrop, but Conley rolls instinctively out of the way, causing the bigger member of the Fab. Ones to strike his knee off the mat. Conley places Agito in a figure four leglock, and he’s close enough to tag in Paul Langford, who kicks the knee repeatedly in an attempt to injure it. He takes over and uses a figure four sleeperhold, but soon releases the hold and places Agito Nakajima on the turnbuckle for a superplex. He slams him off the mat, and gets Agito in a side headlock when Sho dives over the ropes in a somersault dropkick that catches Langford in the face. Nakajima goes for a waistlock suplex, but Paul Langford counters it with an elbowsmash and serves one up to Sho as well. Conley enters again and all four men go at it, while the referee tries to regain control. In the process, he gets in the way of the Panku, taking a clothesline from each side by the Fabulous Ones. Twister mount an offensive strike with a Hart Attack clothesline on Sho, but Agito gets behind Langford and executes a dragon suplex. This leaves both Sho and Langford down while their partners fight it out, and Sho is able to crawl over to Paul and cover him as Ms. Miki and Mr. Tsuburaya revive the official and urge him to make the count. Blindly he does, and the bell rings.] TD: Ladies and gentlemen, the winners of the match as a result of a pinfall... THE FABULOUS ONES! [As “Kiss of Death” by Dokken starts to play, Agito exits the ring and pulls Sho out with him. They, along with Ms. Miki and Mr. Tsuburaya, walk back up the ramp and laugh at Twister, who regroups and stares back out at the winners. Agito makes the worldwide recognized motion of “I should have a belt”, and Sho raises his arm while trying to shake the dew off the lilly.] DR: That was a fantastic contest between the two teams, and in just a little while we’ll also see the Twin Ninjas against Working Class. They are the other teams in the pay per view match for the United States Tag Titles. But right now let’s take it to Amy Marshall, who’s ready for a big interview. [Cut to the ring where Amy Marshall has now joined Tom Davis, but quickly replaces him as Tom leaves after giving her the mic.] AM: How are ya'll doing? [Crowd pop] I want to bring out now, the 'Fab' Four of the UWF, give it up for Legion! ["Until It Sleeps" by Metallica loudly blasts it's way onto the speakers and the crowd goes wild. Tim Vexton walks out from behind the curtain, with the U.S title strapped around his waist. Tim is followed by Tom Landis, who is as always accompanied by Tara ‘Sunburst’ Marshall. Tom’s dressed in street clothes, a pair of jeans and white sleeveless t-shirt, topped off by a Chicago White Sox cap. Tara is also in street clothes, wearing a pair of jeans herself(and the debate rages, who looks better in jeans, her or him?) and a “Legion” t-shirt knotted in the middle and exposing her midriff. The UWF World champion, Alex Kidd follows next, accompanied by Alicia Powers, and Alex is wearing ring attire of red trunks and boots and has the UWF World championship around his waist. Alex is also sporting his Union Jack 'ENGLAND' jacket. Youth Gone Wild brings up the rear, and claps hands with just about every fan in sight. The fans roar when they see Legion, but Wild’s entrance doubles the intensity of the pop. A chant of “Youth - Gone - Wild!” echoes for a few minutes in the arena, and Wild tries to greet every fan possible. The four men (and two ladies) get to the ring, and all raise their hands simultaneously for the crowd. The cheers are deafening, as the crowd shows their support for the stable. Tim's music cuts, and Tom Landis and Tim Vexton stand on opposite sides of Amy Marshall.] AM: Tom and Tim, last we saw of you, was on Tuesday where Tom lost the match against Eddie Fantastic, courtesy of your interference Tim. Comments? [Amy holds the mic out to Tim first.] Tim: Amy, a year and a half ago I sent Eddie packing. Now that he's back, I'll stop at nothing to get rid of him again. That fact that Tom lost the match in unfortunate, but it's over. Tom understands the way I am and that I did what I felt I had to do. [Amy turns to Tom and offers him the mic. Tom takes it with a flourish.] Tom: Eddie, you made a huge mistake in coming back here. You haven’t got a clue what Legion’s been through since you sulked off last year, you don’t know how much we’ve suffered through. But what you do know is that Tim Vexton is out for your blood, and where Tim goes, Legion goes too. One match in the grand scheme of things doesn’t mean anything to me, but that attack satisfied Tim just enough, until the day that he finally gets you in the ring. AM: One more question for you Tim.
