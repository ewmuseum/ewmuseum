DR: Well, quite the announcement from Kyle Backwood, who is putting fifty thousand dollars of his own on the line! But we've got the main event up next, between Ronnie Paris and Daniel Kidd!
["Lakini's Juice" begins to play, starting the usual chorus of boos for the stable everyone loves to hate. Most of the Pride, sans Joey Valachi, walk out onto the ramp, though Dan Kidd is noticeably not wearing his wrestling gear yet.
The lovely Cameo leads the way, followed by Dan and then the tag team champions, both of whom are smiling like the cat the swallowed the canary.]
AM: What do these guys have planned for this fake title match coming up? They're going to ruin the main event! DR: That's what they do, Amy. They ruin main events. SS: At least they've refrained from ruining my Uni. AM: So far. [All the Pridesmen, and one Prideswoman, enter the ring almost at once. PP: Mr. Daniels... we do have a match to get underway. What's your purpose out here? SD: Well, Petey, the Pride has decided that a title versus title match between our Dan Kidd and Ronnie Paris is so momentous, it should wait until the main event.
PP: But there are only ten minutes left in the program! [At this, Scott smacks his head in mock dismay, and the other two Pridesmen pretend to be disappointed in him.]
SD: I _knew_ I forgot to tell you something... the Pride bought an extra hour! [Shocked pop from the crowd, but an overwhelmingly in favour one!] That's right,
Rampage is about to go into it's third hour... the Pride Hour! Randy really is a generous deal-maker, I'll tell you what.
[Peter Phillips can barely speak, he's so stunned, and most of the crowd joins him in that sentiment. The Pride members all look on gleefully as Scott grabs hold of the mic, with little resistance from our intrepid interviewer.]
SD: Let me just run down the lineup of this show for our faithful viewers at home. There is, of course, the big title versus title main event. Daniel, would you care to comment on that one?
