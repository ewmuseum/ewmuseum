[Part 1] [Fade up from black to a white background, with the words "Last Week" written in red, and the font seems to be written in a watery font that gurgles and bubbles. The words evaporate, and clips from a week ago roll: Ashley Wilder coming to the ring during a Steve Armstrong interview... Armstrong ordering her to apologize to him... Ashley with a hurricanrana on Armstrong... Ginger appearing from in the crowd and attacking Ashley from behind, the two women wrestling on the mat... Armstrong, poised to smash Ashley with a chair... Youth Gone Wild coming to her rescue and taking Armstrong down... Ashley and YGW standing side by side in the aisle as Armstrong threatens to reveal her secrets once and for all. As the fast paced theme music for Saturday Night Rampage, "All That You Are" by Econoline Crush, another title shows up, much like the first. This one however reads "This Week... Stay Tuned..." The screen switches to a black field for a second, and the opening title sequence for Rampage plays. The words "Saturday Night" fly in from the top right of the screen in a scripted font, while Rampage comes up from the bottom in big block letters. The title screen is replaced by a three sided cube, each square continually show clips, from Alex Kidd executing the Somersault Bulldog, to Eddie Fantastic with a belly-to-belly suplex, to the Reformer's reverse DDT. Finally we come to rest at the announcers' table, at which sits Sam Steeley with yet another new co-host. Two weeks ago it was Jennifer Parker-Davies, last week supermodel Rebecca Romijn filled in. Sam's partner this week is none other than the blondest of them all, Cameo(though we suspect it's a bottled colour). Sam as always has a leather jacket over a Pride t-shirt, while Cameo stuns the crowd by wearing a baby version of the new Pride "Boo me" t-shirt, barely covering her shapely and bountiful cleavage. She also has on a pair of skimpy red spandex shorts, white puffs of cotton around the bottom of the shorts, in the middle of her thighs. She's feeling festive, and so is Sam sitting next to the bombshell.] SS: Hello again, idiots around the world, Sam Steeley here for Saturday Night Rampage. And the President of the UWF has really outdone himself this time in finding me a co-host. I suffered through a week with that Slam 'n' Eggs nobody, then last week I was stuck with a brainless supermodel that likes little boys, but this week I have a lady next to me that oozes class. You all know her, so give her the respect she deserves... CAMEO! [The crowd gives her the respect she deserves alright, that being none. Catcalls, boos, and heel pops fill the arena, and Cameo smiles.] C: Thanks for the introduction Sam. I'm no stranger to UWF broadcasting, I did have my own show for a while. Sunday Slams, and then the Sunday Morning Supershow. SS: Wasn't that little pipsqueak Moe Owens your sidekick? C: [answering very quickly to hide the truth] No. We have to listen to him on Tuesday Thrashings, let's not bring him up tonight. SS: A great plan. This show is going to be explosive, because of two reasons. [catches a glance at Cameo's chest] Okay, four reasons. C: Sam, why are you so happy to see that? I thought you liked her. SS: I did, until she started hanging around with that Inbreeding Gone Wild character. What is it with him and dumb blondes? C: Don't worry about me being swayed by the little boy's charms, I've got an IQ higher than the temparature of any male that looks at me. SS: [again takes a glance at the physique of his broadcast colleague] Well I am feeling a little warm right now, now that you mention it. Anyways, Raekwon as of late has controlled the UWF with an iron fist, which we can tell with tonight's schedule. Let's take a look. C: Yeah, it's all the Pride merchandise. SS: Come again? C: Well it's so hard to find good help that all the guys decided to unload the packing crates at the warehouse themselves.
