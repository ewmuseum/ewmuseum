The two look over a few steps up, see the other... and trade a few hard shots! Neither can be shaken from their grasp, though, and Elliott gets a bit ahead by kicking down towards the champ's face. The Thrillseeker then starts up to the roof in earnest, with Goldstone in hot pursuit. Elliott has that crazy gleam in his eye, which is causing Tawni to yell out warnings and Scud to grin like a cat. Elliott makes it to the top very quickly, and it's soon apparent what he was trying for.] DR: The trap door! Elliott wants to fight on _top_ of this cage! AM: Goldstone looks ready to oblige, he's letting Elliott open it up! [Indeed, the champ lets a few seconds roll off the clock as Elliott first pops open the trap door, and then climbs out onto the cage roof. Elliott allows Goldstone the same courtesy, not attacking him until he's through the door... but right at that very moment, he blasts the champ with a shot to the temple! Goldstone staggers back, but regains the advantage with a knee to the gut. When Elliott keels over, he takes advanatge by planting him into the mesh with a rocker dropper! The move scratches up Elliott's face, blooding him, and basically sickening the weak-stomached in attendance - but really pleasing the rest! Goldstone doesn't let up, walking over the precarious mesh to scoop Elliott up in a bodyslam attempt... but Elliott slips loose, and in one motion scoops Goldstone up and hurls him down, THROUGH the trap door to the mat!] DR: GOOD GOD! That must have been a fifteen foot drop! [Elliott looks down at his opponent, shrugs, and dives in the hole after him, using his body as a projectile!] SS: So is that! Somehow, somehow out of that sick, stupid move, Elliot is on top for a pin! [Despite what we're sure are at least minor injuries, Elliott is on top of Goldstone and has the presence of mind to go for a pin. Dale Simpson counts: 1...2...3!] DR: Elliott gets the shot! A bloodied, bruised Tommy Elliott has a Skyweight title shot at L and I 2! AM: However drew the next number is going to have any easy time, comparitively. [Scott Ingraham opens up the door, waiting for the third man to show up. Meanwhile, Tawni and Scud walk over to greet the Thrillseeker who is just leaving the cage. Elliott walks out, limping a bit, and leans slightly on Tawni for support. As he does, the vixen shoves Scud hard in the back, into the cage, and the door is locked! Surprised pop!] SS: BWA HA HA! Scud is the mystery man! DR: Look at him, he's bewildered... I don't think he even knew! Tawni must have signed him up without his consent. AM: Poor, poor Scud. [A groggy Goldstone gets to his feet, shakes out the cobwebs, and hears the bell ring to signify a new three minutes. He then looks outside the ring, where a terrified Scud is looking for some means of escape in the walls. Seeing none, he looks up and notices he still open trap door, so he does what any coward would and starts to climb. Goldstone is now back in control of his faculties, and he'll have none of it, as he leaps at Scud with a death defying plancha, nailing him head on and dropping him off his perch on the wall!] DR: Scud was trying to escape, but Goldstone wants to beat him quickly and get on to the next man. SS: "Man" is kinda a strong term to use in referring to Scud, isn't it? AM: Poor, poor Scud. [Scud is, miraculously, not really hurt, but he is very shaken. That's putting it mildly, he's scared shitless. Goldstone senses this, and doesn't want the poor bastard to wet himself, so he picks him up and gently rolls him back into the ring, looking to end it peacefully. With little resistance from Scud, he hooks him up for a full nelson cradle, and pulls through with it.] DR: Detonation! Scott Goldstone calls that the Detonation, and here he can call it a win. AM: Wait, Goldstone lets go at two? What gives? SS: Woo hoo! Tawni gives! [Sam, like many of the males in the audience, have noticed what distracted Goldstone. Tawni has lifted her skirt up to near scandalous levels. The cameraman, being red-blooded, has zoomed in to catch a good shot of what can almost be seen... Goldstone, meanwhile, has momentarily forgotten the match and is walking over, almost trancelike, to get a better look for himself. After a moment, though, Goldstone clues in to the distraction, and turns away... to find that Scud is nowhere to be seen!] AM: That libido of Goldstone's has got him into trouble befrore, it may have again. SS: One question... where the Hell did Scud go? DR: That's a good question... and as he clock continues to tick down, our video crew is trying to find out. Wait, I'm told we have a shot now... roll it! [The current picture, of a baffled Skyweight champ looking for a scrawny reporter, is replaced by tape feed with a caption that reads "moments ago". As Goldstone can be seen walking away from the ring, Scud gets up, thanks his lucky stars, and scrambles under the ring to hide! As we cut back to the live feed, we find that Scud is unfortunate - that feed just played on the Unitron, as well!] DR: Goldstone knows he's down there! That's not gud for young Billy McKenzie. AM: I've said it before, and I'll say it again. Poor, poor Scud. [Goldstone peeks his head under the apron, and milliseconds later a frightend Scud pops out, and makes for the door. Goldstone moves to intercept, and soon has him cornered against the closed outer door. Just when things look dark for Scud, however, a man hops out from the crowd and runs over to threaten Scott Ingraham into opening the door! Scud tumbles out to the floor, and Goldstone steps into the doorway, confused... and takes a chair shot right to the head!] SS: It's Desire! Elan Desire has a master plan! [Scud scampers off to safety, while "GQ" blasts away on Goldstone with the chair, opening up a cut on the champs head. Scott Ingraham and Dale Simpson gather up some nerve, advancing on the irate rookie and ordering him to cease his attack... but Desire is too enraged to stop, so he swings around and levels Ingraham with the chair! The bell rings franticaly, as Simpson backs off a bit while a group of security guards and officials run out to control Desire.] DR: That man is out of control! Not only has he injured Scott Goldstone, but he hammered a defenseless referee. Get him out of here! AM: Scud and the Daimyo faction are out of sight, now, and the three minutes are now up. Do you know what that means? Scud McKenzie gets a Skyweight Title shot, next week on Rampage. SS: BWA HA HA HA! [The officials have now cleared out Desire, with some effort, and others have escorted Scott Ingraham backstage for treatment. Meanwhile, Scott Goldstone lays out cold in the doorway of the cage, bleeding profusely. Tom Davis makes the following announcement.] TD: Ladies and gentlemen, "GQ" Elan Desire has been disqualified from this match! We will therefore proceed directly to tonight's final challenger! [If Scott Goldstone were conscious, we're sure he'd be cursing his luck right now. Because "Lakini's Juice" has begun to play over the sound system. The "Cardiac Kid", Joey Valachi, emerges to a largely positive response, and he seems quite amused with the condition of his opponent. He chuckles a bit and points, saying "This should be tough!", as he makes his way to the cage, where Goldstone is now showing only the faintest signs of motion.] DR: Elan Desire didn't care about wins or title shots... he just wanted to hurt Scott Goldstone. And he's succeeded. AM: Joey Valachi is going to roll all over Goldstone in this state... let's just hope he's merciful. SS: Hey, he would have kicked "Wet Fuse"'s ass anyway, what's the big deal? [Valachi gets to the doorway, and picks up Goldstone very gingerly, with mock care for his physical welfare. He drags the semi-concious champ to the ring, rolling him in and following to place him right in the center. Then, after a bit of showboating that's obligatory of the Cardiac Kid, he grins widely and puts one foot over Goldstone's chest. Dale Simpson reluctanty starts counting. One... Two... It's all over!] DR: In a sad display, Joey Valachi inserts himself into the Live and Interactive 2 title shot... which I know guess will be a Triple Threat match. AM: It could have been worse. At least he didn't try to hurt Goldstone more. DR: There is that. ["Lakini's Juice" begins again, and Valachi heads out of the cage to celebrate his "hard fought" victory, wile the fans release a mixed pop. The Pride's Pride is behind him, at least.] SS: It's Goldstone versus Elliott vs Valachi at L and I 2, and next week it's... [snicker]... Scud vs Goldstone: The Title Shot That Should Not Be." DR: That's it for Saturday Night Rampage, and what a show it has been! Until Tuesday night, then, this is Dave Rogers signing off for the UWF... Happy Holidays, everyone, and have a great weekend! [Fade out, as "Impressed" begins to play, and a shot of Joey Valachi hamming it up in the aisle can be seen.]
