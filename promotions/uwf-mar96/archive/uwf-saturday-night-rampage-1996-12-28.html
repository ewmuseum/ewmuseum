UWF Saturday Night Rampage, 28 December 1996

[Announcer] Family squabbles are normal, it seems like everyone has a fued between relatives. But Alex Kidd and his cousin Daniel have been at odds almost immediately upon Daniel's arrival to the UWF. Daniel has betrayed his older cousin repeatedly. He has done so by verbally abusing Alex's manager and girlfriend Alicia Powers, signing on with Alex's bitter enemy Randolph Chalmers III, and attacked him on many different occasions. Even in the ring things haven't been settled. Alex and Daniel have both scored wins over each other, but tonight each has brought backup. Alex brings to the ring with him his best friend, and fellow Legionnaire Tom Landis. Daniel counters with former Chalmers Corporation member and current associate Officer Mark Durman. This is sure to be a classic matchup tonight when these four men seek to destroy each other. Tonight, on Saturday Night Rampage. [As black and white images of UWF superstars flash by, "Simply the Best" by Tina Turner plays in the background. The camera fades up to the Corel Center in Ottawa, Canada. Sterling Justice, the aforementioned title contendor last month came out with fire in his eyes. Before the last card, he had suffered a losing streak since that pay per view match, and was determined to keep the win streak alive tonight. Psychosis3 was looking to do the same thing, keep alive his modest win streak of 2. He was soundly booed, came out looking calm, and wanted to continue his streak, currently at two wins. They met in the ring and despite the height difference, stood face to face(or face to pecs as this case was). The bell rang and Psychosis3 latched onto Justice and pulled him to the mat in a modified armdrag takedown. Once there, he Garvin stomped his way around Justice and then went up for a flying splash. Justice rolled away quickly and P3 hit hard, knocking the wind out of him. Sterling picked his foe up by the hair and stretched him out with an abdominal stretch before slamming him back-first into the mat. A cover only led to a kickout by Psychosis3, and when he returned to his feet, he trapped the Eyes of Justice in spinning headscissors and rolled him up with a cradle. Sterling kicked out forcefully and then side suplexed Psychosis3 with a thud. He then scooped P3 up and gorilla pressed him for the crowd to see, and then hurtled him over the top rope into a long conference table. It heaved under the weight of P3 but did not break(hey, only a cruiserweight). Sterling exited the ring, but wasn't quick enough to catch P3 hurt. The Xerox of Psychosis stood on the table and actually plancha'd off, onto Justice. The tide was turned, and it continued that way when Justice took a dropkick into the stairs. Sterling crawled back into the ring and P3 followed, by a slingshot legdrop over the ropes and a cover. Sterling kicked out again and P3 tried a different approach. He grabbed Justice's legs and executed a boston crab for a minute, then let go and mounted the ropes once more. A flying elbowdrop landed on the back of Justice's head, and Sterling blacked out. Supremely confident(as all Asylumites are), Psychosis3 wasted some time by taunting the fans. By the time he refocused on his opponent, Sterling had revived and began to stir. P3 climbed the ropes again but this time Justice grabbed him and hauled him off, DDT'ing him from the top rope(but Sterling's feet never left the mat so it wasn't an actual top rope DDT) before whipping him off the ropes. Never give a luchador speed, as Sterling learned from a cross body block that sent both men crashing to the arena floor. Justice got up before his opponent, and dropped a few knees into the throat of P3, then dragged him back in throught the ropes, delivered a forearm to the back and placed him in position for the Unconditional Surrendor. Justice jumped up and down to maximize the damage, and P3 had no choice but to submit or be crippled for life. WINNER: STERLING JUSTICE Sterling didn't let go immediately, simply because he was focused on his opponent and not the bell. The referee talked him into letting go and he did, dropping Psychosis3 to the mat slumped over. DR "Wow, I've never seen him that into a match. If only he was like that against Kelly Maloney, he might be world champion right now." SS "Hah! That was a fluke, even Job Holly wins now and then. Justice is a glorified punching bag, hired to take dives." DR "Sam! That's not true. Sterling Justice is a top contendor in the UWF, how do you explain the battle royal victory?" As I was saying before, the next match tonight is between Desperado and Running Eagle Feather." ==================================== DESPERADO VS RUNNING EAGLE FEATHER ==================================== The next thing the crowd heard was "Misery" by Soul Asylum, and they took it rather badly. The crowd booed for Desperado and Billy the Kid, going so far as to throw garbage. The team of two ignored the fan response and slid into the ring as the music changed. "Hair of the Dog" thundered and the fans gave Running Eagle Feather a mixed response(ah, blessed neutrality). He rushed out to the ring with Jack Moran, and stood toe to toe with Desperado. Before the bell rang they were already slugging it out, and Desperado beat REF to the ground. Eagle Feather responsed by monkey flipping Desperado twice, angering the mysterious cowboy. REF missed kicking Desperado, and was viciously dropped with a lariat. REF rolled to the floor to get away from Desperado, but was soon joined by him. Desperado grabbed Eagle Feather's head and rammed it into the guardrail. That knocked REF senseless and Desperado held him while Billy the Kid came at him with a roundhouse right. At the last second REF ducked and Desperado was flattened, as Billy the Kid looked on. Jack Moran warned REF that Billy the Kid was behind him, and a back heel kick dropped the manager as well. Both men rolled in under the count of the referee, and REF covered Desperado. A kickout, and REF executed a sleeperhold on Desperado, who struggled. He reached the ropes and choked Eagle Feather out with them, escaping from the submission hold. Desperado was still a little light on his feet, and when REF whipped him into the ropes he reversed it, and Eagle Feather hit the referee in the corner. Both men crashed to the mat, and Billy the Kid charged the ring. He grabbed REF and delivered a version of the Austin 3:16, then revived the referee and left before the official was wise to his actions. Desperado miraculously seemed to be 100 percent again(wow, a miracle!), and hauled Eagle Feather into position for the Nightmare. He delivered and Running Eagle Feather received, and the referee, albeit groggy, counted to three as Desperado won. WINNER: DESPERADO DR "I smell a setup. He wasn't all that groggy, he was playing possum." SS "He won didn't he? That's all that counts in this league." DR "Sick man Sam, sick." ======================================= CAMOUFLAGE CHAMELEON VS RICKY JOHNSON ======================================= The Camouflage Chameleon came out first, to the theme music of the nWd(and Sycho Sid of the WWF as well). The few fans that liked the nWd cheered for him, but mostly the crowd just booed. The boos turned to cheers when they heard "The Distance" by Cake. Ricky Johnson was using his friend Chris Scottsdale's music to come out, and when he hit the ring he engaged the Chameleon in hand to hand combat. Johnson tried to position him for a northern lights suplex but had it blocked, and instead caught CC with an earringer. A forearm smash-belly to belly suplex combo was effective, but it didn't get the pin. The Chameleon kicked out and threw Ricky off, only to suffer a second suplex. He tossed CC out of the ring and waited for six seconds to pass before he came back in, and Ricky pulled him by the head. He avalanched the Chameleon, but was stunned by a european uppercut. An earringer and a kneelift changed the complexion of the match, and Ricky was inverted powerbombed. A full nelson subdued Ricky until Johnson got his foot to the ropes to break the hold. CC tossed him over, but Ricky didn't waste time, coming back in immediately. He stunned CC with a kick to the midsection, then grabbed his head and slammed it to the mat. He delivered a couple of chops before a short arm scissors, and then reverse DDT'ed the nWd member. He covered him but wasn't able to hold the count, and the Chameleon kicked out at two. CC got up, only to be felled with a swinging neckbreaker. Ricky was ready, and set CC up on the turnbuckle for the Gut Breaker. He was all set to deliver, when all lights in the arena turned red. Then in the middle of the ring a noose lowered from the ceiling. Desperado's music began to play and then thunder and lightning kicked in. Once the crowd and Ricky Johnson's attention was gotten, and the announcer's speculated as to what was going on, wind and old western showdown music played instead. On the video wall a huge picture of a desert with a cactus was displayed. The bass voice of Desperado began to speak: "Who do you think you are telling people to start listening to what you say? You try to throw your weight around to threaten other people. This is where you learn that you have no weight to throw around in the UWF." Then all of a sudden from opposite sides of the ring Desperado and Billy the Kid slid in the ring beneath the ropes and each manoever'd on an opposite side of Ricky. Johnson tried to defend himself but eventually it became a two on one beat-down. The Camouflage Chameleon tried to join in, and Ricky was kicked and beaten by all three. DR "Someone give him a hand, please! Anyone! Aw no, not them." Street Gang decided to make an entrance early, and rushed the ring. The crowd feared the worst, that Ricky would be at a six on one disadvantage. Strangely enough, Ramon, the Darksider, and the Darknight Express came after the Chameleon, eager for revenge on the nWd in general. Tom Landis also came out, to help Ricky Johnson with Desperado and Billy the Kid. The arena crowd watched as the Chameleon saw the Street Gang and hightailed it through the crowd, and Landis took out Billy the Kid from behind. Ricky managed to get in a few shots at Desperado, and Johnson and Landis cleared the ring of the pair. WINNER: BY DISQUALIFICATION, RICKY JOHNSON DR "There is definitely bad blood between Ricky Johnson and Desperado, I can't wait for that match on the Free For All." SS "Johnson's bitten off more he can chew with that one. Desperado is one mean sunnuva-" DR "Family show Sam! Who's up next?" SS "The Maloney clan against Street Gang. This should be a heckuva battle." ============================= THE MALONEYS VS STREET GANG ============================= "Harold of the Rocks" by Primus echoed throughout the arena and Rock Ramon, the Darksider, Dimento, and Demolition, as well as their manager Frank Friday and bodyguard Executioner, emerged from the dressing rooms. The six men didn't get cheered, but there weren't that many boos either, considering the opposition. "Teenage Kicks", the anthem of the Maloneys kicked in, and massive heel heat was given to the four family members. Kelly led the way, holding the world title up for all to see. Behind him were Neal, Brendan, and Brian, all dressed in wrestling attire. When all men involved were at ringside, the match began. Elected to start for their teams were Dimento and Kelly, the world champion. Kelly took an elbowsmash to the face, and then was double teamed by both Darknights once an incoming Neal was tossed back out of the ring. Kelly was bodyslammed by Demolition and then Dimento legdropped him. Dimento tagged out to Demolition and Kelly was still at the mercy of his opponents. Kelly tried to slip out of the ring following a Sammartino backbreaker but couldn't get the hold on, and got nailed with a double kneeling headbutt. Kelly was whipped off the ropes and managed a shoulderblock before lunging and tagging in Neal. Neal fared much better, as he came off the top rope in a flying sunset flip and then executed a scorpion deathlock. He dropped an elbow on Demolition, and a quick tag brought in Brendan, the smallest and lightest of the Maloneys. Neal gutwrenched Demo first, then allowed Brendan to get his opponent with a jumping elbowthrust. The Darksider tried to storm the ring but wound up on the floor after a double clothesline from Neal and Brendan. Neal left the ring and Brendan followed up on Demolition with an enzuigiri to the face, then a dropkick. He mounted the ropes and jumped off with a flying bodypress, catching Demo off guard. He covered him for the pin but Rock Ramon came in and stomped on his head. Demolition crawled over to the Street Gang corner and tagged in the Darksider, who stepped over the ropes towards an unsuspecting Brendan. Brendan turned around right into a chokeslam, one that knocked him senseless. The Darksider gave the sign for the RIP piledriver, but as he lifted Brendan up Kelly dropkicked him over, and Brendan had the Darksider in a bridge that he couldn't hold. The Darksider and Brendan crashed to the mat, the youngest Maloney being in a position to pin the darkman. He kicked out, and the Darksider whipped him off the ropes into a scoop slam. Brian and Kelly came into the ring and double dropkicked the Darksider from behind, rescuing Brendan. Brendan and the Darksider each tagged out, and Rock Ramon and Brian came in. They locked up and Brian showed no ringrust as he used a single leg takedown and then a half boston to weaken Rock's legs. Ramon fought back with a couple of temple shots, and then trapped Brian in his old finisher, the STF. Brian was close to the ropes and broke the hold that way, and crawled to the outside apron. He slingshot himself over with a clothesline that connected with Ramon, and tried for the pin. Rock kicked out, and Brian tagged in Kelly for an attempt at the Maloney Sandwich. As Brian clipped Rock from the back, Kelly came off in a bodypress. Unfortunately, the Darksider got into the ring and he caught Kelly first. He plucked him out of midair, slid him into position and delivered the RIP Piledriver. Kelly was unconcious, but Brian came to his rescue and took the Darksider momentarily out of focus with a shoulderblock into the corner. Brendan meanwhile dragged Kelly's form to their corner, and when the referee's attention was turned to them, Brendan slapped Kelly's hand to tag in. Rock was still the legal man in, and was the victim of Brendan's surfboard. He struggled, and was only able to escape when Dimento grabbed Brendan's legs out from under him and dragged him to the floor. DR "We have to take a break, stay tuned!"
C O M M E R C I A L [Announcer] To a champion, pride and respect are everything. A champion wants to be known and remembered for fighting valiantly, and defending against all challengers. At Holiday Havoc, the champions of the UWF all face uphill battles in their fight for immortality. Kelly Maloney, the current world heavyweight champion, has agreed to put the gold on the line against the two original stars that fought it out for the title when the league started. Should he best both Serge Annis and Alex Kidd, he will carve a name for himself comparable to other great champions. Youth Gone Wild, the United States champion, faces not one but two challengers to his throne, when he meets former champion Officer Mark Durman and longtime UWF'er Rock Ramon. He must defeat two contendors, a feat that he has already accomplished once before. Luis "The Kid" Garavello, the Cruiserweight Champion of the UWF, will do battle with Daniel Kidd at Holiday Havoc. Kidd has proven not only to be a dangerous competitor in the ring, but outside as well. With the Chalmers Corporation behind him, Luis will have to watch his back twice as hard. Both tag team champions, world and U.S., face opponents with experience as champions. Raekwon and Shaolin will take on the Masters of Motion, a tag team which has beaten them before. The Twin Ninjas face former US tag champions the Darknight Express, two men who want revenge in the worst way. Will the champions be able to keep their precious gold, or will there be a change at the top of the ratings when Holiday Havoc is over? There is only one way to find out: ORDER HOLIDAY HAVOC TODAY! Miss Demeanor also accompanied the team, which collectively gained heel heat, but very little cheering. Then "Under Pressure" by ZZ Top ripped the lid off of the Corel Center and two thirds of Legion came down to the ring. Alex Kidd, Alicia Powers, Tara "Sunburst" Marshall, and Tom Landis emereged from the curtain leading backstage. Along with miniskirts, Sunburst and Alicia each wore a t-shirt that read "YGW: Best High Flyer - 1996 RSPWF Awards" The crowd seemed to be mainly pro-Legion and the fans went wild(had to get that last part of Legion in there somehow) for the fan favorites. DR "Say, isn't that Ricky Johnson?" Daniel used a facerake to throw Alex over the ropes right away, causing a loud resounding boo from the audience. Daniel was not shy about fighting on the floor, but neither was Alex. Alex missed a dropkick, but hit his younger cousin with a flying forearm. A duck down saved Daniel from a short lariat, and he scampered back into the ring, sucker punching Alex as he followed. A soviet suplex by Daniel proved ineffective as Alex came back with a hiptoss, and Alex tagged in Tom Landis. A double forearm to the back by the Legionnaires and then Landis backdropped Daniel. Landis pumped his fists in the air to cause the crowd to cheer, and he double leg cradled Danny for two (landis's new favorite move ever since he lost the world title by it). Kidd kicked out, but a tiger suplexed caused him to get pinned for another two count. Randolph had enough of this and called the referee over to discuss Landis's "severe breaking of the rules". In the meantime Durman snuck in and put a knee in Landis's ribs, injuring him. Alex took a kneelift off the apron, and then Daniel was able to tag out when the referee returned to the match. He and Durman double elbowsmashed Landis, leaving Landis easy pickings for Durman's brand of justice. He caught the former world champion with flying kneedrops, chops to the neck, and then a figure four taught by the master, RCIII. Alex returned to the apron having recovered, and Landis was able via the ropes to reverse the figure four. Durman let go quickly and Landis tagged out to Alex, leading to a backdrop into sunset flip by Landis, and immediately after, a flying cross bodypress. Alex turned Durman over into a Boston Crab but couldn't hold the cop down. Instead he turned to the strategy that led to his singles victory against Durman, a Thesz Press followed by a dropkick. Alex went for a rarely used standing moonsault but instead was kneelifted in the groin(another Chalmers trademark). Daniel tagged into the match and attacked Alex from behind, clamping on a sleeperhold. Alex struggled, then managed to make the hold a little les painful as a side headlock, and finally whipped Daniel off the ropes. He was taken down with a shoulderblock though, and Daniel started to yell at the Legion fans in attendance. Members of Legion don't take kindly to that, and Landis climbed to the top rope and dropkicked Daniel over the ropes. The referee didn't notice(or did he?), and Landis simply tossed Kidd back into the ring to Alex. Alex covered Daniel but only received the two count, then tagged Landis in. Landis was a house of fire, as he bodyslammed Daniel, then mounted the ropes. A mero-sault from Landis almost proved to be the winner, but Durman came in(like a good heel) and saved Kidd from the pin by dragging Landis off and kneeing him in the face. Daniel managed to get to his feet and superkicked Landis against the corner, knocking Landis silly. Daniel monkey flipped Tom, then got him in a small package, stealing a two count. Landis kicked out, and tried to crawl to his corner but Durman tagged in and kept him away from Alex. He got Landis in the Bloody Gloves, and Tom was going nowhere. Only a punch to the head from Alex saved him, and Durman took it hard. Daniel came in but was met with a spinning mule kick, and then Landis gave him the Widowmaker. Daniel was out like a light, but Durman was the legal man. He stood up at the wrong time and was caught with a Somersault Bulldog, knocking out the other half of the team. Landis covered Durman(being the legal men) and Legion reigned victorious. WINNERS: ALEX KIDD AND TOM LANDIS DR "Yes! Alex and Tom are two of the most beloved wrestlers in the world, and that's why.
