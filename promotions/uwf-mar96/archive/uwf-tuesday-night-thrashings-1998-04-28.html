UWF Tuesday Night Thrashings, 28 April 1998

April 28, 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Fade up from black to the UWF's Tuesday Night Thrashings, as we see footage from earlier tonight of Shawn Vexton of the Pride. He's all by himself, which by itself is strange. Shawn stands in the ring wearing street clothes and is holding his World Tag Title belt and a piece of paper.]
SV: First off I want to thank Scott Daniels, my best friend, for giving me the honor of doing this. It was Scott's idea, but he let me come out here and tell all of you the great news. You see, this piece of paper is my dear brother's contract. [Shawn rips the contract into many small pieces] I've waited a long time to say this.
Tim, Scott has decided that _YOU'RE FIRED_! [Major boos from the
Legion friendly fans] That's right, what you did Saturday night was the last straw and now you can go back to working two bit abandoned wharehouses in Asia. You're finished in the UWF. Bye bye!
HAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA! So, now that the Pride have done away with Legion's "enforcer" [said mockingly], we all sincerely hope that the rest of you will fall in line and do what's right.
[Shawn drops the mic and hams it up for the crowd before exiting the ring while the opening credits for Thrashings come on the screen.

He wears a pair of black "UWF" sweatpants and a matching t-shirt, neither of which serve to hide his less than athletic physique. Standing next to him is Tawni, one half of the
Nasty Habits, Jimmy Dangerous' valets. She's wearing thigh-high black wet leather boots with a tight miniskirt, and a wet leather half top with a zipper down the front that leaves little to the imagination. Her blonde hair is held back in a ponytail, and Moe is noticeably nervous around her. Nevertheless, our fearless host marches on.]
MO: Hello fans, welcome to Thrashings, the most explosive weekday show in wrestling. I'm Moe Owens, and as you can see my special guest this week is none other than Tawni, one half of the Nasty
Habits. Tawni, thanks for joining me.
T: Thank you for having me, Moe. MO: For those of you wondering what happened to Ashley Wilder, my usual co-host, for the third straight week she hasn't shown up for the start of the show. It seems this week Ashley didn't make it from the hotel to the arena. Strangely enough, neither did Steve Ryott.
But enough about those two, let's move on with the show. As you saw at the top of the program, the Pride has struck yet another blow to
Legion, as it appears Tim Vexton, the longtime "Enforcer" for Legion, has been fired.
T: Tim's a friend of that cutey Alex Kidd, isn't he? MO: Uh, yes Tawni, he is. T: Then I'm sorry for Alex's loss. MO: O-kay. It wasn't enough that the Pride shocked everyone Saturday night with the acquisitions of Ronnie Paris and the new tag team No
Fat Chicks to the fold, they had to go and fire Tim Vexton as well?
Scott Daniels and his band of thieving scoundrels have to be stopped. T: The Daimyo will stop them Moe, hey, we have done it before. Remember Kensei's night of revenge? MO: I do, and I'm sure the Pride does as well. Let's take a look at our opening contests this evening, the first one being a no disqualifications handicap match between the Darknight Express and those hapless pretenders, Dejobberation X.
______ /_ __/ ______________________________________________ / / | / /_____ ___\ / / |/ /_ __/ The Darknight Express ___\ /_/ /| / / / versus ___\ /_/ |_/ / / Dejobberation X ___\ /_/_______________________________/ The Darknights, having just been screwed out of the World Tag Titles this weekend, weren't in a good mood tonight and took it out on the four man squad from. Triple J came first and was plowed with a double trashcan shot to the head, and the New Age Inlaws suffered a high impact version of the Total Destruction out on the arena floor.
Guyna took the worst of the damage though, to the crowd's delight, as the Express decimated it with several batterings with objects, and finally Dimento scored the pinfall on her with a chair aided elbowdrop. As DJX's manager, X-Blac, doubled over in pain from too many rapid crotch chops, the Express celebrated and vowed revenge on the Saviors.
WINNERS ~~~ The Darknight Express in 6:41 ~~~ MO: A display of reckless abandon from the Darknights, who should be the World Tag Team Champions right now.
T: Sweet Madness are the next champions, Moe. MO: The tag team scene is becoming increasingly confusing as a whole raft of teams are at or near the top. Next we have the singles debut of Kore Wrench, the competitor from the neo-CWAA.
______ /_ __/ ______________________________________________ / / | / /_____ ___\ / / |/ /_ __/ Kore Wrench ___\ /_/ /| / / / versus ___\ /_/ |_/ / / Jobbio Vega ___\ /_/_______________________________/ Kore Wrench came down to the ring to a surprisingly accepting crowd. Max Robertson didn't appear with him and neither did McNultey or
Anderson, leaving Kore to do things right. Jobbio tried to use his martial arts background on the ex-Preacher, but Kore landed a mighty gorilla press and then landed a legdrop to score the pinfall.
WINNER ~~~ Kore Wrench in 1:20 ~~~ MO: Kore Wrench, in both of his appearances in the UWF, has been a powerhouse. I'm not sure there's anyone that's as pure a brawler as he is, anyone that could match him in the ring.
T: Given the chance, Randall Osbourne could take him out. MO: Now that would be a great match. Oddly enough, we've got Sweet
Madness up next. ______ /_ __/ ______________________________________________ / / | / /_____ ___\ / / |/ /_ __/ Sweet Madness ___\ /_/ /| / / / versus ___\ /_/ |_/ / / J.O.B. 2000 & Public Enema ___\ /_/_______________________________/ Another handicap match, Sho Satsuma and Randall "Madman" Osbourne took it to Hawked and Minimal first, putting the JOB 2000 down with a spiked Michinoku Driver. Their manager, Nummy, helped the JOB to the locker rooms as Public Enema, fresh(except smell-wise) from their loss to No Fat Chicks on Rampage, carried a coffee table to the ring and tried to outmuscle the Madness. Sho Satsuma made Jobby Grunge pay with a hiptoss over the top rope onto the coffee table, which didn't break. Soon after, Rocco Rocked took a powerbomb over the top rope from Osbourne that smashed him and his partner through the coffee table, and Sweet Madness won by countout.
WINNERS ~~~ Sweet Madness by countout in 3:32 ~~~ MO: I sense a theme with Public Enema. T: I told you Sweet Madness will be the champions, one day very soon. They'll make the Pride look like puppy dogs, and Randall loves to kick puppy dogs in the head.
MO: Somehow it's as if we already knew that. "Wasted Youth" Judas
Steele, who we saw on Saturday with a challenge to Youth Gone Wild, was in action today against Jobrooq, who's abandoned the Nation of
Stagnation.
______ /_ __/ ______________________________________________ / / | / /_____ ___\ / / |/ /_ __/ "Wasted Youth" Judas Steele ___\ /_/ /| / / / versus ___\ /_/ |_/ / / Jobrooq ___\ /_/_______________________________/ Judas Steele, as in his match with Youth Gone Wild, took to the airways against Jobrooq and had an easy time with the longtime UWF
'employee'. Steele smashed Jobrooq into the turnbuckles and then executed a wickedly fast snap suplex, setting up for the Stardust
Press and the win.
WINNER ~~~ "Wasted Youth" Judas Steele in 4:14 ~~~ MO: Judas is talented, and his first match with YGW could have gone either way. Lots of people would love to see a rematch.
T: Is it time for the Danger Zone yet? MO: [sigh] Yes. [Cut to footage from today. "Welcome to the Danger Zone" by Kenny
Loggins plays over the PA system. The cameras search around the arena swinging up towards the upper concourse level, searching for the Danger Zone set, until... in the upper corner of a roped off area of seats, a fifteen by fifteen foot cyber-optics wall flashes the words "Danger Zone". Across the top are JD's different t-shirts, and down the left side are the AEWA National tag titles, and down the right is Randall Osbourne's poster of him punching President Reed in the face. Standing in front of the wall are Jimmy Dangerous' two personal assistants, Tawni and Shawni.]
Tawni: Hiya, everybody! [Both women wave to the crowd as the male portion cheers wildly. The two vixens are wearing black wet leather half tops, with a zipper down the middle and black wet leather mini-skirts. Tawni is wearing black wet leather, thigh high boots, and Shawni is sporting black high heels to accent her amazing gams.]
Tawni: I'm Tawni, and this is... Shawni: Shawni. And we're the... [Tawni holds to microphone out to the audience who chant... "NASTY
HABITS!"] T: With no further ado about nothing, here is your host with the most. S: The man who saved us from playing with little kidds. [Tawni shoots Shawni a look.] T: You do mean little Dan Kidd, right? S: Yes, I do. Didn't we do this last time? T: Hey, I've got to make that clear. Alex is a cutey, who I suspect is a wild one in the sack?
S: Yeah, probably so, but what about the Madman? T: [smiling] He's like the energizer bunny...he goes on, and on, and on, and on... [As Tawni makes the motion with her hand of over and over, the video screen behind them flashes the cover of the bootleg tape with Randall
Osbourne and herself.]
T: And on, and on, and on... Voice: Enough! [Then, from behind the wall Jimmy Dangerous walks out to a huge heel pop. He's wearing a black shirt with the words "To fear is divine, to ignore is to be destroyed!", blue jeans, and black Reeboks.]
S: The world's most dangerous man... T + S: Jimmy Dangerous! JD: Well, thank you my sweets. It took you long enough. [JD sits down in one of the seats, kicks his feet up on the seat in front of him, and the Nasties set behind him.]
JD: Today, it's my honor to have one of the greatest cruiserweights in the world grace my set. He comes from Japan, and is a close personal friend, and has a challenge to boot. He's the CIWF cruiserweight champion, here's the ""Ruler of the Airways", the
KABUKI KID! ["Hotter than Hell" by KISS plays loudly as the video screen wall changes to a scene of the Kabuki Kid leaping off the top turnbuckle towards the crowd. As the scene continues it's evident he's about land on a prone wrestler on a press table. The Kabuki Kid connects with a kneedrop to Tiger Claw's head, breaking the table in the process and sending both combatants to the floor.]
JD: Now, that's going to leave a mark. [Then, an oriental gentleman steps out from behind the screen. He stands five foot, ten inches tall, with no hair and face pant on.
The paint is black with a red and white eagle on it. The wings of which cover his eyes and go down his cheeks, with the neck and head residing on his forehead. He's very stocky for a cruiserweight, with muscles that seem to want to pop out of his skin. He's wearing blue jeans, sandals, and a white tank top. Around his waist is the CIWF cruiserweight title. The crowd gives him a mixed pop. Some of the crowd recognizes him, but most do not. He sets down next to JD.]
JD: Thank you for coming. KK: Arigato, right back at'cha. JD: You asked for some time and here it is. Go with it. KK: I'm here to challenge the best cruiserweight the UWF has to offer, in my quest to prove that I'm the best to ever grace the squared circle. I've wrestled some legends and some great up and coming lightheavies, but that is not enough. In order to be the very best, a warrior must face and defeat the very best, that is why I've come to the UWF. This is the first stop in my tour of the major organizations to prove this point.
JD: So, who do you feel is the best cruiser in the UWF? Is it Joey
Valachi? KK: No, Jimmy-kun. He has a lot of ability, but he's bastardized it with his dishonorable antics and association with idiots. He's learned to cheat like no other, but in the process has forgot how to be a great athlete. I feel Valachi-san, is no competition.
JD: Interesting point. How about Chris Scottsdale, the cruiserweight champion? KK: A fine young warrior with a lot of ability. He's earned the right to be the UWF champion, but not the right to face me.
JD: [smiling] I've got it. What about one of my personal favorites... Steve Armst... uhm Ryott?
KK: Once again, a great athlete, only the best seem to be in the UWF, but his outside the ring distractions would make him an easy victory for the "Ruler of the Airways". I'm looking for a challenge.
JD: [looking bewildered] What of Daniel Kidd and Ronnie Paris? KK: [Kabuki shoots JD a look] Last Saturday, they fought the match of their careers, so they say. If that's the best they can do, I'd have to say that a four year old child could defeat the both of them at one time. Neither man is worthy.
JD: Uhmm, I'm running out of names. What about Vortex? KK: What about Vortex-kun? JD: Is he up to par? KK: No! [JD sits with a look of intense thought.] JD: Good god, you're making this difficult. KK: He's the best the UWF has to offer. [Tawni then leans over and whispers something in JD's ear. Suddenly,
JD's eyes light up and a smile comes across his face.]
JD: I think I've come up with the million dollar answer. Is it Youth
Gone Wild? KK: Hai, it is. [the crowd unleashes a huge ovation] He has beaten the best the UWF has thrown at him time and again. He has devoted his life to his profession. He has shown to me the warrior spirit
I'm looking for in an opponent.
[Kabuki Kid stands up.] KK: I, the Kabuki Kid, King of the Cruisers and Ruler of the Airways, challenge Youth Gone Wild to a match. Warrior to warrior, man to man, and the squared circle will be our battle field. Youth-kun, do you accept? I await your answer.
[Then, "Hotter than Hell" bursts over the PA once again as Kabuki
Kid, JD, and the Nasties leave the set. Fade back to Moe and Tawni.]
MO: You are aware Alex Kidd is married, right? T: Yes, but when was the last time we saw his little girl, Alicia? MO: Another Daimyo-ite, Kensei Tokugawa, is in action against Max
Robertson next. ______ /_ __/ ______________________________________________ / / | / /_____ ___\ / / |/ /_ __/"The Professional" Max Robertson ___\ /_/ /| / / / versus ___\ /_/ |_/ / / "Samurai" Kensei Tokugawa ___\ /_/_______________________________/ [Max Robertson's "Gregorian Chants" remix begins to play, complete with assorted professional wrestling sound effects. "The
Professional" appears and is wearing his CWAA Television Belt and has the CWAA Heavyweight belt slung over his shoulder. Kore Wrench appears a few steps behind him, and is wearing his CWAA 6-man tag belt. Max grabs a microphone and both men slide into the ring.]
MR: Last week on Saturday Night Rampage Kensei Tokugawa reminded me of a few things. I had almost forgotten about his attack against me last summer during my CWAA Television Title bout. Alright then, Mr.
Tokugawa, I accept your challenge. I have been looking forward to this for some time. Good ole JD doesn't understand how talented his henchman is. You know Kensei, it's too bad you're stuck over there.
A talented wrestler like yourself could go far with the NCWAA. But I'll let you in on something: When you tried to take my TV Title away from me that night, I overcame and won the match. I don't know about you, or the rest of your little club, but I'm here to win. The truth is, honor _IS_ winning. That's the Professional Way. That's the only way. I'll see you in the ring, so get your butt out here right now.
Robertson was accompanied by Kore Wrench, but McNultey and Anderson came out as well for the beginning of the match. Kensei Tokugawa came out by himself, and he and Robertson put on a technical showcase that had both on top of the match at various points. As Anderson accidentally tripped Robertson instead of Tokugawa, the Professional one explodes, ordering the team to vacate ringside. As they do,
Kensei levels him with a heart punch and lifts him up for the Shinzo
Mahi. Desparately, Max yelps for Kore to enter the ring and help him out, and reluctantly Kore does. They begin to attack Kensei two on one, but Dangerous runs in for the save.
WINNER ~~~ Max Robertson in 13:50 ~~~ T: Kensei was robbed, and everyone associated with the CWAA are going to be paying the price to the Lethal Protector.
MO: It wasn't too long ago that Serge Annis went by that name. But I think it fits Kensei Tokugawa's role in the Daimyo to a T. Don't look now, but the Pride is up next.
______ /_ __/ _________________________________________________ / / | / /_____ ___\ / / |/ /_ __/ Daniel Kidd & No Fat Chicks ___\ /_/ /| / / / versus ___\ /_/ |_/ / / Vortex, Stevie Smith, Cosmos 3000___\ /_/__________________________________/ This match was more of a continuation of the debut of No Fat Chicks, as Reno and Richards fought the duration of the match while Daniel stood outside the ring. They were accompanied to the ring by the
Sweet Saviors, title belts in hand. The Pride was booed out of the building for their earlier announcement involving Tim Vexton, and the trio of luchadors tried to take advantage of that hatred of the crowd. It didn't work, as Reno and Richards pulled the doubleteam moves out of nowhere. At one point Cosmos suffered a tilt-a-whirl backbreaker from Reno, right into a flying legdrop from Richards, who hadn't lost a step from his Twinja days. The match ended as Reno locked in the Texas Cloverleaf, which he now calls the Chic Magnet, on Smith. However as the Pride celebrated the win, the Daimyo contingent of Kensei Tokugawa, Jimmy Dangerous, Randall Osbourne, Sho
Satsuma and the Kabuki Kid attacked the ring and ended up stealing the world tag belts, walking off as the Pride was left to regroup and charge after them without luck.
WINNERS ~~~ Daniel Kidd & No Fat Chicks ~~~ T: When the Daimyo strikes, people sit up and take notice. MO: Well that goes without saying. Deez Nutz Brown against Robert
Greer was supposed to be the next match. Here are words from Robert
Greer.
[Camera cuts to the locker room of Robert Greer. Greer is sitting in a blue chair. He is wearing blue jeans, a black Korn T-shirt and black wrestling boots. He looks ready for his match against Deez
Nutz Brown. He looks at the camera and speaks.]
RG: Well, Deez Nutz, tonight is the night. Tonight a fight you in that squared circle. I've just gotten back and I've barely gotten any fights accept when I'm with my brother. I wanna say right now that I am not a tag team wrestler. My brother used to be. He used to be one of the Twisters, when they were "Tornado" Tim Greer and
"Cyclone" Anthony Jarvis, one of the greatest tag teams in wrestling history in my opinion. Now I don't want to take the place of
"Cyclone", 'cause I can fight tag, but I choose not to, cause I wrestle best one on one. [Greer brushes his hair back with his right hand, and takes a drink of a nearby Powerade sports drink.]
RG: I've been here in the UWF over a year, however I still haven't won a title. When I came into this federation, I was on the top of the heap in wrestling. I was WAIWA World Heavyweight Champion. I was IWF King of the Ring. I was a good bet to bring into the UWF.
However, when I came in, they put me in the Cruiserweight category, and let me stay there. I don't care about how they organize their wrestlers, cause I'm not a cruiserweight wrestler. No matter what anyone says, I am one of those guys who need to try to be the best in his sport. At a time I was, and now I plan on doing it again.
[Greer takes another gulp of Powerade.] RG: Valachi, you beat me and I'm out of the tournament. I understand that. I also understand that with a little effort, I can recieve a shot at one of these titles. Now it's time. Deez Nutz Brown, Robert
Greer is back on track. He has a goal. Tonight's goal is to get the one, two, three on you, Brown. I know you have had a few good matches in your career, and a few wins as well. But tonight won't be one of them if I have something to say about it. Tonight is the first step to me returning to where I was a year ago. To be the UWF
World Heavyweight Champion.
[Greer stands up and leaves the camera's view. Camera fades out.] ______ /_ __/ ________________________________________ / / | / /_____ ___\ / / |/ /_ __/ Robert Greer ___\ /_/ /| / / / versus ___\ /_/ |_/ / / Deez Nutz Brown ___\ /_/_________________________/ Greer came out pumped, ready for the match with the colourful newcomer. Unfortunately, Deez Nutz didn't appear at all, and the match was thrown out.
WINNER ~~~ No contest ~~~ MO: With luck we'll get to see this match sometime in the future. But that brings us to tonight's main event, and it's a match where the fans were dying to find out who Chris Scottsdale would defend the
Cruiserweight Championship against. Watch this folks, it's unbelievable.
______ /_ __/ ________________________________________ / / | / /_____ MAIN EVENT ___\ / / |/ /_ __/ Chris Scottsdale ___\ /_/ /| / / / versus ___\ /_/ |_/ / / Mystery Cruiser ___\ /_/_________________________/ [Cut to footage of earlier today. Chris Scottsdale awaits his challenger in the ring, the belt over his shoulder. Randolph
Chalmers walks out to his usual chorus of boos. He steps in the ring and grabs the house mic.]
RC3: Being a man of my word. [boos and some minor garbage thrown in the ring] I have brought with me tonight, the greatest cruiserweight to grace a UWF ring. And here he is now.
["War Machine" by KISS begins to play as Chalmers begins the introduction.] RC3: Ladies and gentlemen, your next cruiserweight champion of the world. From Las Vegas, Nevada... Bllllaaaaacccccckkkkkk
Diiiiiiiiiiiiiiaaaaaaammmmmoooooonnnnnndddddd!!!!!!!! [The crowd is stunned as the seven footer walks down the ramp. But no one is more stunned than Chris Scottsdale. As Diamond gets in the ring
Scottsdale can be heard on the house mic.] CS: That guy's over 300 pounds, how can he be a cruiserweight? [As if reading Scottsdale's mind, Paul Reed walks out to a bevy of cheers, and quickly enters the ring.]
PR: Mr. Chalmers, there is no way in hell that this guy is a cruiserweight. He can fight tonight, but it won't be for the title.
And you my friend, are facing a big fine for this little stunt. BigD: Hey, I've been in the gym. I've lost a lot of weight. RC3: Let's bring out a scale and weigh this man, he's a cruiser and
I'll prove it. [A member of the ringside crew walks out from the locker room, carrying a large scale. The scale is brought to the ring and Diamond steps up on it as the crowd guffaws, fully expecting the result to be over 300 pounds. Chalmers and Reed both view the reading and Chalmers tells the crowd the result.]
RC3: 225 pounds. See, he's a cruiser. PR: I don't know how you did this, but you'll pay. The title match is on. [Reed leaves the ring, and Scottsdale gets on the microphone.] CS: Alright, I'll fight this mutant and I'll kick beat him 1... 2... 3... But something is fishy here and I don't like it. The champion was right to question the result, and in seconds he was in major trouble, Diamond grabbing him and clubbing him to the mat.
Big D. worked Scottsdale's back over, assaulting it with sidewalk slams and side suplexes to crush him into the mat. Diamond applied a version of Agito Nakajima's inverted body vice to further weaken
Scottsdale, but the Cruiser champion put up a fight after escaping it, chopping Diamond down to size with a dropkick to the knee and a flying bodypress for a near pinfall. But Diamond kicked out, and as
Scottsdale ducked a clothesline, the corporation giant hooked him into a Snakebite and scored the incredulous win to capture the
Cruiserweight Championship. WINNER ~~~ And new Cruiserweight Champion, Black Diamond in 14:55 ~~~ MO: I'd really like to know how much Chalmers paid to get the belt, because no scale can mask the fact that Black Diamond weighs over three hundred pounds.
T: The scale said 225. MO: The scale was rigged! T: Send Jimmy after Diamond, he'll win the cruiserweight championship. MO: He's over the limit too. T: Closer than who we have as champion now. MO: True enough. Well that does it for Thrashings this week, except to bring you the note that because of the new champion in Black
Diamond, the announced contest between Scottsdale and Steve Ryott has been called off. Goodnight from Kansas City.
[Fade.]
