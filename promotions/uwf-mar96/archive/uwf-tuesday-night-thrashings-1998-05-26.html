UWF Tuesday Night Thrashings, 26 May 1998

May 26, 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Fade up from black to the Universal Wrestling Federation logo on a background showing a lightning storm. The crack of thunder and lightning is intermixed with verbal exchanges between Serge Annis and
Scott "Hotspot" Daniels last Saturday on Rampage.
Serge Annis: Now, I know for a fact that some people in the backstage aren't happy about me wearing this title out here... I worked fifteen damn months for this, and I have spilt more blood than anyone else.
I've earned this... If anyone has the guts to come out here and take this title away from me right here... right now... then step on out into the isleway and get in this ring!
[Eventually Scott Daniels and a host of Pridesmen emerge from the locker room.] David Rogers: The Pride! The Pride are going to stand up to Serge
Annis! Sam Steeley: No! Guys! That's suicide! Don't do it man! [Daniels gets a microphone and starts to answer the challenge.] Scott Daniels: Annis.... the almighty Serge Annis... yeah, I've got a problem with you flashing that stupid IIWF World championship around here in the UWF... because this ain't the goddam' IIWF, that's why! Half of the locker room would agree with me. This is the
UWF... The name Serge Annis no longer strikes fear into the hearts of anyone. When Bishop died, pal, Serge Annis went down with him!
[At the mere mention of Bishop, Annis' eyes seemingly bulge out of their sockets. Serge's face begins to turn red, as he is getting incensed at
"Hotspot".] Scott Daniels: Where the hell have you been lately? Oh, here's a better question... who the hell cares? I sure don't... and I am willing to bet everyone in that locker room are saying the same thing... you sold your goddam' soul... all because of a stupid promise you made to that dead whore of a mother you have!
[Annis tries to attack Daniels for the comments, but the Pridesman turns the tables.] Scott Daniels: Okay Kidd, get in. There's your opponent. [Cut to footage of Annis demolishing Alex Kidd and most of the Pride to boot, as Daniels inches up the ramp with a grin on his face.]
Voice-over: Tonight, Serge Annis and Scott "Hotspot" Daniels lock up in the main event, as the IIWF Heavyweight Champion faces off with the leader of the Pride and the self-proclaimed flagbearer of the
UWF. Who will survive this historic clash? Find out tonight, only on Tuesday Night Thrashings!

Thrashings, the midweek classic from the UWF. I'm Moe Owens, and along with me is the lovely Becky Carlisle.

As it turned out, Tim did in fact make it to the arena, but just as Rampage was going off the air. His car apparently had been hi-jacked by the Pride, which is hardly a surprise to anyone that they'd stoop so low.

[Apparently someone has missed their sound cue, as there's a bit of dead air while no entrance music plays. Suddenly, however, we see the "Samurai" come careening out through the curtains, doing well not to lose his balance. Tim Vexton bursts out after him, running full bore into the kendo master with a clothesline. The fans begin popping madly as they start to realize what's happened, while Vexton tries to pick Kensei up and only gets an elbow in his gut for the trouble. He's then thrown towards the ring, stumbling and somehow stopping before hitting the apron. Kensei keeps right on going at him, looking for a "Spear" like football tackle. Vexton, instead of dodging, just prepares for the brunt of the move and gets ridden down hard. Kensei tries to throw in some martial arts style thrusts from above, but Vexton is doing a very good job of covering up his neck and chest.]
DR: This match looks like it started in the back and just wound it's way out to ringside! AM: It looks like a referee is just now showing up on the scene to keep these two from killing each other.
SS: Hey, now there's something I could cheer for. AM and DR: SAM! [Scott Ingraham has indeed trotted out to officiate this, as soon as it becomes an official match. Meanwhile, Kensei has given up on the thrusts and has got back up to his feet, allowing Vexton to do the same. The truce doesn't last long, though, as both men charge at each other pell mell. Vexton gets the upper hand, driving a knee into the Samurai's chest to hobble him up. Tim then grabs around his neck and drives down, bouncing Kensei's head off his knee. The
Samurai is rolled into the ring, so our timekeeper immediately takes advantage and rings the bell.]
AM: This is a legal match now, although I'm not sure legal is a good word to describe the battle we're going to see.
DR: Vexton goes up to the apron... he's looking to catapult himself over the ropes, it looks. Big splash coming up!
SS: And he lands right on the Samurai's knees! Hah! [Vexton falls to the side, doubled over in pain, which gives the
Samurai a chance to get the early advantage. Getting to his feet, he lays in a few kicks to the midsection of Vexton to keep him from trying to get up. Kensei then goes off the ropes and comes back to nail Vexton right in the face with a low-level dropkick, drawing a sympathy pop from the crowd. Giving up the chance for an immediate cover, the Samurai goes into a ready stance as Vexton struggles to remember his name and where that bicuspid just went. Finally, Tim is able to force his way groggily to his feet, only to be levelled as the Samurai finally pounces to hit a leaping savate kick. This time, he does follow up with a cover.]
DR: We have one... two... and a kickout! How resilient is Tim
Vexton? SS: I dunno. Why don't you ask Amy, she seems to spend a lot of time in the men's locker room after shows. AM: You ever get your ass kicked by a woman, Sam? SS: Not without paying for it. [Vexton is immediately whipped into a corner... HARD. The Samurai takes a bit of time and then follows him in, but in desperation the
Enforcer gets a boot up to knock the Daimyo member right on the jaw.
Catching a second wind, Vexton surges forward with a clothesline to knock his opponent over, and then raises his arms in triumph for the crowd. The response seems to get him back in the match... but he's also not paying attention to the Samurai, who spins him around and fires in a stiff shot to the jaw. Vexton gives one back as good as he got it, and the Samurai steps it up with an even harder shot.
Vexton rears back with all he has in him, and fires a big roundhouse right... flooring the Samurai!]
AM: No one wins a slugfest with Tim Vexton! SS: And just like any Japanese import, the Samurai finds out his
American counterpart has more punch.
DR: Nice analogy from a man that was named Nissan's "Customer of the
Month" for May. SS: Shut up, Rogers. [Vexton isn't wasting any time now, as he picks the Samurai right back up and starts twisting him into an armwringer. Kensei tries to fight it, eventually just going with the momentum and reversing into one of his own. Tim shows quite a bit of agility in flipping to relieve the pressure, and then lining up the Samurai for a short-arm clothesline. Trying to capitalize right away, the only free
Legionnaire heads right for the ropes and moves quickly towards the top one, while the Samurai tries to get his breath back. Tim has now made it to the top, and is preparing to fly off, but he is briefly distracted by a drunk fan yelling at him. When he does finally take to the air, it's a fraction of a second too late... the Samurai gets a foot up while he's in the air, and there's nothing Vexton can do but take the shot to the chin.]
SS: Looks like Tim Vexton just got some free dental work! DR: This has been a rough encounter so far, and you get the feeling these two could go all night.
SS: I know I can. They don't call me the "Sixty Minute Man" for nothing. AM: Who, exactly, calls you that Sam? [Scott Ingraham begins to put a count on both men, as neither shows any immediate signs of movement. Scott counts fairly slowly, though, so he's only to four when the Samurai starts getting up, and Vexton is soon to follow. The Samurai charges right away with a clothesline, which Vexton deftly ducks, and then he prepares for a counter-attack on the rebound. The Samurai has other plans, however, as he grabs on to the ropes while Vexton fires a dropkick that's not even close to the mark. Vexton gets up a little slowly, giving the
Samurai a chance to go in for the kill.]
DR: Heart punch by Kensei! We know what this means... we're about to see the Shinzo Mahi! AM: Tim Vexton is in a lot of trouble right here, as- SS: [interrupting] Hold on, we have company! DR: Him again? We've already seen him tonight, we don't need a repeat for Thrashings. [A loud round of boos, which attracts the Samurai's attention, breakout for the emergence of Pride associate Ronnie Paris, who as usual wears both of his fake titles around his waist and the "King of the Cruisers" crown on his head. Paris, unfortunately, has a mic.]
RP: Hold on there, big guy! Both of you have some business to conduct, that business is with the Pride, and business is about to pick up! [The Samurai makes a motion for Paris to come to the ring and give him a try.] Not so fast there, Tonto! You still have a match, don't you?
[Paris smirks as the Samurai realizes his mistake a split second too late, and is rolled up from behind by Vexton, who decidees to keep rolling through into a bridge. The pin looks tight, very tight, and it holds for: 1...2... Kensei kicks out with authority, pushing
Vexton forward and right into Scott Ingraham, knocking him out!]
SS: Referee down! Referee down! [Vexton stumbles back towards the center of the ring as Paris continues to look on, and runs right into the Samurai. Kensei attempts a whip to the ropes, but Vexton is quick to reverse it, and as he comes back towards the Enforcer, we see him setting up for...]
DR: The Spinebuster! Vexton has this match won with that move, but the referee is down! SS: And here come more Pridesmen! [From the front row, our tag team champions suddenly appear, Scott
Daniels with a chair in hand. The two quickly roll into the ring, and Vexton sees them a bit late. He rushes over to confront his enemies, and ends up getting waffled by the chair! Another round of boos errupt as Daniels and Shawn Vexton start making ready to leave.]
AM: This is totally heinous, what a farce this match has become! RP: [Who, BTW, still has that mic] No, no, guys. Let's make it two for one! [With that, Scott and Shawn look at each other, and then make a beelin for the rising Kensei Tokugawa, flattening his head with the chair too. They then drag him over to drape him on top of Tim, and exit the ring just as Scott Ingraham shows some signs of waking. The
Sweet Saviors run to meet up with their fellow Pridesman and get safely out of the way when these two wake up. Ingraham, meanwhile, just sees a cover and makes a very slow count.
One... Two... Three!] TD: Ladies and gentlemen, your winner, and moving on in the tournament, the "Samurai" Kensei Tokugawa!
[A round of boos for the cheap decision ring out, as neither man has shown signs of regaining conciousness quite yet.]
DR: That was a total miscarriage of justice, planned and perpetraited by the Pride. SS: And what better revenge against Tim Vexton? I thought that was great! AM: You would. DR: The Samurai moves on in the tournament, but I'm not sure he'll like the way he did it. Back to you, Moe and Becky.
