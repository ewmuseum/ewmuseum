19 September 1998

[Fade up from black to past images, projected onto a brick wall like old home movies. The quality of picture is substandard, but over it is a voice-over by Moe Owens. The clips roll of the many confrontations between Alex Kidd and Brett Greene, age old enemies. Behind the voice-over, LL Cool J's "Phenomenon" is heard.] MO: Alex Kidd and Brett Greene are two of the most recognizable names in UWF history. Between the two of them, they have won four Heavyweight championships of the world and four United States Championships. But from these similarities, contempt and hatred has been born. [Cut to clips from early 1996, as Alex Kidd and the other members of Legion stand in the ring, and the masked Humanity makes his way to the ring... Humanity unmasks to reveal a younger Brett Greene, selling out Legion to the Chalmers Corporation... Alex and Youth Gone Wild battle it out in a steel cage, as Brett walks up with the blonde wig and baseball bat that led everyone to believe Wild had attacked Kidd...] MO: When Brett organized the Pride, the hatred between these two men reached new limits. It had now gotten personal... [Cut to clips from the first days of the Pride... Brett standing with Scott "Hotspot" Daniels outside the ring as Alex and Legion look on... The Pride interrupts the wedding of Alex Kidd and Alicia Powers, causing havoc... Brett takes the UWF Championship from Alex at AEWA Extreme...] MO: The summer of 1997 was the culmination of bad blood between the UWF and AEWA, and yet through it all, the bad blood between Alex Kidd and Brett Greene continued. But a third force, "Crimson" Joe Reed, entered the picture, a man whom neither Greene or Kidd liked. This advance by the AEWA forced Alex and Brett to work together, which bred an uneasy alliance. [Cut to clips of Team UWF assembled in the ring, front and center being Alex Kidd and Brett Greeene... Alex and Brett battle in the "Road to the Gold" Battle Royal at Summer Heat Wave, and inadvertantly hit each other...] MO: Things started to cool off, until the fall of 1997 when Brett Greene did something that nobody ever expected... he left the Pride. Brett and Scott Daniels waged a war between each other, while Alex refocused on his career and defended his second world championship until October. A new conflict errupted however, when Legion was taken over by the Chalmers Corporation. Brett made it quite clear that his opinion of Alex Kidd degraded by the way he was allowing RCIII to control his career. [Cut to clips of Legion being told what to do by the Corporation... Alex runs in and attacks the opponents of Chalmers associates... Chalmers hands the contracts of Legion over to the Pride... Alex pins Brett with the help of the Pride to take the U.S. Title from Brett...] MO: It had become a question of Alex Kidd's mettle, which Brett claimed was tarnished. When Alex and Legion were finally freed, thanks in no small part to Brett Greene, Alex became embittered and lashed out at the fans, at his stablemates and at Brett. [Cut to clips from Glory Days 1998... Alex hits the Somersault Bulldog on Sebastian Jericho to win his third World Championship...] MO: Now, the roles are reversed... Brett Greene, once the hated Pridesman, is now the fans' choice... And Alex Kidd, beloved Legionnaire and British Wonderkid, is the despised one, looking to keep his title at any and all costs. Tonight, these two meet in a titanic clash, in what is possibly the most important match the two have ever faced off in. Coming up, on Saturday Night Rampage. [By this time, Paris, Vexton and Valachi have come halfway down the ramp to ringside, and Ricky Johnson has spotted them. He throws Bolton's half dazed carcass back into the ring and slips back in to safety. As Ricky Johnson and the Pridesmen on the outside of the ring, Dwight Bolton starts to get to his feet, and rolls Ricky up from behind in a small package. Ricky kicks out, and plows Dwight with a forearm smash and then sets up for a powerbomb. Dwight happens to be near the ropes, and Paris holds his legs down so Ricky can't powerbomb him. Instead Hotstuff releases Bolton and takes a swipe at Paris, who ducks.] SS: Hey, keep your mind on the match, idiot boy. He's not cut out for Legion. AM: Actually he is. Who else but Legion has to deal with multiple Pridesmen in a singles match? [Ricky turns around and catches Dwight with a kneelift to the face, then sets him up for a fisherman suplex. He nails it and cradles Dwight, but Ronnie distracts the referee while Valachi sneaks into the ring and dropkicks Ricky in the face, on an almost 90 degree drop from the top rope. Johnson releases and clutches his face in agony as Joey Valachi slips out of the ring, and Paris allows the referee to get back to the match.] AM: That's totally unfair! SS: That's Pride. DR: That certainly is. WAIT A MINUTE! Here comes Tom Landis and Youth Gone Wild! [The crowd explodes into full cheers as Ricky's partners in Legion, Illinois native "Heartbreaker" Tom Landis, and Youth Gone Wild race out to ringside to negate the impact of the other Pridesmen. Dwight Bolton sets Ricky up in the corner and drives his shoulder into the gut of Hotstuff, then he pulls him out and drops Johnson with a modifed facebuster.] SS: That's the Pride Breaker! Scott Daniels taught Dwight his finishing hold! AM: If he did, Bolton's a horrible student. He hardly even applied it right. [Bolton goes for a cover, but Ricky kicks out and rolls to the Legion corner where he talks to Landis and Wild. Paris complains to the referee about an unfair advantage, but Dale Simpson effectively tells him to sit on it. Bolton heads for the corner but receives a kick to the gut to double him up, and a vertical suplex drops him to the canvas. Bolton rolls out to the floor, where he's surrounded by the other three men for protection.] AM: Come on, get him back in the ring. He's just stalling. SS: Leave him alone, Red. Bolton could have a real serious injury after that Johnson tried to break his neck. AM: I don't think he's got an injury. Malfunction maybe, but not an injury. DR: Wait a minute, Youth Gone Wild's in the ring. Ricky Johnson's out, and grabs Bolton- FLYING BODYPRESS FROM WILD, RIGHT ONTO THE OTHER THREE! AM: And here's Tom Landis from the other side of the ring! We've got Ronnie Paris, Joey Valachi and Shawn Vexton brawling with Youth Gone Wild and Tom Landis outside the ring, but Ricky Johnson has Dwight Bolton in a sleeperhold out on the floor! SS: Get some order, you idiot Simpson! [Ricky throws Bolton back through the ropes and then climbs to the tope rope. He comes off with a flying clothesline, and proceeds to grab Bolton for a powerslam, but steps to the middle turnbuckle and executes the Heat Seeking Missle to a loud response. But before he can get the three count, another Pridesman comes through the crowd, hopping the boards.] DR: That's Daniel Kidd! Dan Kidd is out here- DDT on Johnson! Ring the bell, we have a disqualification. TD: Ladies and gentlemen, here is your winner AS A RESULT OF A DISQUALIFICATION... "HOOOOOTSTUUUUUFF" RIIIICKY JOOOHNSOOOOOOON! [Landis tries to climb into the ring, but is pulled back by Shawn Vexton and hammered with a forearm to the back. Youth Gone Wild likewise is teamed up on by the swelling masses of the Pride, and inside the ring Bolton and Kidd are kicking the life out of Hotstuff.] SS: NOW THIS IS THE PRIDE! AM: Get some help! Get- oh my god! Dan Kidd just attacked Dwight Bolton now! Look at Paris and the others, they don't know what to do! DR: I think they were under the impression he really was a Pridesman, but Dan Kidd obviously has some other agenda! [Dan lays into Bolton now and pummels him before applying an anklelock hold, causing Dwight to scream in pain. Dan slides out of the ring and heads back into the crowd as Paris and the others get in the ring and help Dwight back to the locker room, dazed completely. After they leave, the members of Legion start to get up, and walk out as well.] DR: And the battles between Pride and Legion start anew. SS: Yeah, and the Pride kicked ass! [As the crowd noise dies down from the confusion, a man gets up out of his seat and hops the railing, stretches, and climbs into the ring. He's wearing a pair of blue jeans, and a sleeveless white "Buckwheat!" half t-shirt, as well as some Ray Ban sunglasses. He looks at the jeering crowd, who recognize him as Sam Willis. He picks up the house microphone, as security comes closer, and he backs up.] DR: Wait a minute! AM: Dammit no! Why is he here again? SS: The guy has guts, I'll give him that. SW: "Whoa, whoa... there's not going to be any trouble, guys... just take it easy. There are no gimps for me to stumble into and knock over right now." [Sam laughs uproariously, while everyone else seems unamused.] SW: "Rough crowd." DR: That's disgusting. [He runs his hand through his dirty blonde hair, then strokes his stubble beard a bit.] SW: "But then again, that's sort of the attitude everyone's copped towards me... I get the feeling I'm not welcome here in the UWF." [A lot of fans yell, "My, aren't we perceptive!" or something to that extent.] DR: What on earth is he talking about? He isn't in the UWF, he's never signed a contract. AM: Oh this guy is psychotic, he has to be. SW: "Because I had an accident and knocked over your former champion you turn the cold shoulder towards me? Sheesh. Hey, speaking of which, why wasn't I asked who _MY_ inspiration was? Everyone else got to answer that question except me." AM: Where is security? This guy can't be allowed to just strut around whereever and whenever he wants. DR: He keeps talking like he's got a contract. [More boos.] SW: "But, being the kind, considerate guy that I am, I'll tell you right now, _AND_ show you some bonus footage. My inspiration is of course..." [He grins.] SW: "Sebastian Jericho. Never before has there been a man so co-ordinated that he could sit still and fall down at the same time. What a-- ow, [bleep]!" [Sam is nailed with a large can of Coke, which causes him to stagger backwards.] SW: "Son of a [bleep]!" [He wipes the Coke off his face, and tries to regain his composure.] SW: "Anyway... [sigh] the footage. I'm running the show around here now... I'm the legendslayer, I took out your champion... roll the footage..." [He waits a minute. Nothing happens.] SW: "Roll it! Roll it! Roll it!" DR: Don't do it, whoever's back there in the truck. Don't give in to his demands. [Still nothing happens.] SW: "Roll it!" [Finally, some the footage he wanted shows up on the videotron. Sebastian Jericho is giving his interview.] SJ: [on Unitron] "And sometimes, just like in other types of accidents, some that happen in pro wrestling are more consequential and more serious than others." [Suddenly, a man wearing a pair of faded blue jeans and a sleeveless, black "I Graduated With a 4.0... Blood Alcohol Level" t-shirt hops the boards. He's got dark-rooted, unkempt blonde hair, and a stubble beard.] DR: [on videotron] Who the hell is that? What's he doing?!? AM: [on videotron] For god sakes, where is security? SJ: [on videotron] "And as much as it saddens me to say this, this is the last you'll see of--" [The man enters the ring, a microphone in hand. Sebastian Jericho is visibly angry.] SJ: [on videotron] "What the hell do you want? I'm trying to do an interview here." SW: [on videotron] "All I have to say to you is one thing..." [The man pops Seb with a right hand, then shoves him backwards and out of his chair with his foot! The fans explode into boo's! Sam, in the ring NOW, is cackling, and slapping his knee.] SW: "Oh, God, _THAT'S_ wrestling. The guy just fell right over! He must've had a few in him before he came out... heh heh... let's see that again!" [The footage rewinds, and Seb is decked, then shoved back out of his chair once again. The fans in attendance are hurling trash into the ring.] SW: "Can we- hey, what are you doing? Get off of me!" [Security grabs Sam, and against his wishes, escorts him back down the aisle, as he kicks his legs violently and shouts.] DR: That man hopefully will be thrown in jail now, and we won't have to see him again. AM: Sam Willis is a certifiable nutcase if he thinks he's under contract here. Fans, we're going to take a break, stay tuned for hour number two. [Fade down to black.]
