[Fade up from black, right into the Molson Center in Barrie, Ontario. The crowd is cheering already, in anticipation of something, though they aren't quite sure what. And suddenly, without warning, the lights go out in the arena. Total darkness, except for a few lighters lighting in the crowd, and some flashbulbs going off, but as for the ring, its plunged in total darkness...] DR: The arena is bathed in total darkness fans, as we await the mystery wrestler who's to debut tonight! Slater looks around, before removing his sunglasses and placing them in a pocket on his vest. He rubs the goatee on his chin as he raises the mic to his mouth.] KS: Wow, and to ever think I'd get an ovation like this in Canada... [Some of the crowd laughs at the joke, some cheer louder. Slater gives them time to calm down before continuing.] KS: You know, it feels kinda weird being here. But, hey, here I am. You see, I was sitting around the house the other day, and I get a call, from one newly reinstated President of the UWF, one Michael Beeby, and him and I start talking. And we talk about this, that, the Red Sox, the Rangers, wrestling, and he asks me, "Kev, isn't one match a week a little slow for you?" And I'm like, well, yeah, I have been feelin' a bit frisky lately, like I want to get some more competition, and don't get me wrong, there's tons of competition in the other place I work, but I wanted a little more, to expand my horizions. So, next thing I know, through my fax machine, comes a UWF contract. Now, I've been following the goings on here for a while now, and, well, the offer Beeby made me, I just couldn't refuse. So here I am. Now, I know most of you know me, and my style, so I'll spare you all and make this short. I'm here, I'm here for a while, so basically, everyone in the back should get used to it. Tonight, I get to debut, in style, yadda yadda yadda. Basically, I get to make my UWF debut tonight, in this ring, against some guy named Taurus. Now, its real important to make a good first impression, so what I'm gonna do tonight, basically, is give all of you a nice little show. Simply because I can. That, and I *love* to make a good first impression. So for tonight, my friends, get ready to sit back, relax, cheer your favorite, and watch me make my start on taking the UWF for a walk...
