AM: Marissa Monet and Pizzazz haven't been seen for a while now, and obviously Serge Annis feels the need to stoke the hatred between the two teams by taunting an already wound up Shadoe Rage. [A burst of mixed pops for the Prophets of Rage, who blow through the portal and head for the ring, before the PA guy can even cue up their music. Shadoe and Derek ignore the crowd as they roll right into the ring. Fire burning in their eyes, they want Hands of Death, and it shows.] DR: Alright, Shadoe and Derek Rage have joined us here in the ring, and they are awaiting the arrival of the Hands of Death. AM: Serge Annis called Shadoe and Derek to the ring, and you know that the Epitome of Evil has to have evil intentions in mind to do a thing like that. SS: Serge Annis ALWAYS has evil intentions in mind guys. Hello, he's the EPITOME of Evil! DR: Be that as it may, I can't help but think that the disappearances of Marissa Monet and Pizzazz, and the sudden appearance of Serge Annis is hardly any coincidence. AM: I don't think anyone's seen The Hands of Death all day! I didn't even think they were in Cleveland tonight. [And out go the lights, and here comes the flames! The lights in the arena drop, prompting everyone to rise out of their seats in anticipation. The four ringposts explode with ten foot spouts of flames, as does the Unitron, creating a massive wall of pyro. The red glow illuminates the sea of fans, and the pissed off IIWF World Tag Team Champions Forever, storming about inside the ring calling on the Hands of Death.] DR: The arena is bathed in a sea of fire, and the Prophets of Rage are utterly incensed! [After the impressive display of pyro, the lights remain out. There is no "Haunted" remix, and there is no red crimson spotlights. Just... dark.] SS: This is creepy... [As a sea of Zippo lighters from the Annis faithful begins around the arena, the original World Champion's low, gruff voice is heard speaking over the PA.] SA: Death in darkness... [A small red crimson spotlight slowly illuminates the center of the ring. Shadoe Rage is pacing back and forth, in and out of it. The rest of the arena is still black.] SA: I guess that is the beautiful thing about the darkness. Anything can happen. Darkness can veil the most horrid of atrocities and the most heinous of crimes. Darkness can also be a haven for imposters to hide out in a failed attempt at being bad and fearsome. But the thing about true darkness is that it easily casts light upon those imposters that lay claim to it... The posers to the throne of black are drawn out and exposed for the weak cowards that they are. Those imposters do not possess a dark soul. They instead attempt to hide their pink, fleshy hearts by painting them black in a hollow, shallow attempt at darkness. And that is your weakness, Prophets. You hold something dear to those pink fleshy hearts of yours, and thus, makes you weak... A weakness which neither myself nor Caliban are afraid to exploit. Because you see... WE are darkness. We are what you wish you could be... [The red crimson spotlight begins to move. The camera follows it as it goes through the crowd, and then up the stairs of the lower bowl of the arena... Up... up... until we illuminate a balcony of the skybox. And standing there, with microphone in one hand, and Pizzazz's throat in the other is Annis... grinning. Beside him is Caliban, who is holding Marissa Monet in much the same fashion.] SA: Gentlemen... *WE* are evil... [The lights return to normal as Serge grins wider. Shadoe can be heard screaming profanities from the ring as we can now see the blood and welts on both women... Dog collars clamped around each of their necks, and attached to them, the very same rusted gritty chains that Caliban brought back with him from Berwick, Nova Scotia.] DR: What the Hell?!? What are they doing up there?!? SS: Being evil Dave, pay attention. SA: So while you enter the final stages of preparation for Rise to Power, Prophets... forget about these two distractions. Bury those bright, loving hearts of yours and get down to business, or else the Hands of Death shall run through you like lightening through the rain. You call yourselves Prophets...and a prophet is nothing more but the puppet of a higher power. The message you spread from now until Rise to Power shall be nothing but sorrow. We are the darkness. We are the higher power...and we manipulate you like puppets on strings...or men in chains... Remember this Prophets... [Serge squeezes harder on Pizzazz's throat, who gasps for breath. The man's face is blank...as if this is act of cruelty is nothing more to him than drawing a breath or a blink of the eye. Both Annis and Caliban step to the ledge of the balcony, with the chained Monnett and Pizzazz in suit. Serge speaks one last time, and tosses the mic away...] SA: No joy... Only sorrows... [As the crowd is oddly quiet, Annis and Caliban do the very deed they said they would. They both chokeslam Marissa Monet and Pizzazz over the balcony, and drop them both downwards! ______________CRAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAASH~!______________] AM: OH MY GOD... THOSE EVIL BASTARDS! THEY JUST DROPPED THOSE TWO WOMEN TO THEIR DEATHS! SS: Oh stop being so dramatic, Amy. The nice big table over there broke their fall. They're perfectly fine! [As The Prophets of Rage seemingly explode in the ring, Caliban flashes Annis an ugly smile. While the Alaskan Enigma seems satisfied with the act of chaos he has just partaken in, judging by that blank look on the Epitome's face, the suffering has only just begun.] AM: Wait a second. those chains that were attached to Monet and Pizzazz. they still have the other ends of them! DR: Please, no. [With a simple nod that says it all, Annis and Caliban both reach down and pick up the other ends of the chains, and rear back on them with all of their incredible might. That might is enough to jerk both Monet and Pizzazz up by their necks from the shattered pieces of table!] AM: They're hanging them both those chains and collars! This is vile! DR: They did the same thing to the Romanis on Meltdown! SS: I think it is pretty clear what Annis and Caliban have in store for The Prophets of Rage at Rise to Power! [The two women dangle and sway, hanging limply from their tethers, no doubt a result of that ten foot fall from the balcony through the production table. The Prophets quickly depart from the ring and leap over the retaining barrier into the crowd, making a beeline for the stairs and up to the mezzanine level. As Monet and Pizzazz symbolically hang above the broken table, help arrives underneath in Alex Kidd, Ryu Osawa, Paul Wong and Stephanie Harper. The faces clamor to get them down, but just as Wong is almost within reach of Monet, Caliban cruelly pulls her higher! Annis returns the favor as Kidd and Harper try to reach Pizzazz, yanking the grievously injured woman out of reach!] DR: Oh what a fitting way to head into Rise to Power. Just another reminder of why Edmond Winston IV, Marshall Law, and everyone that takes advantage of it needs to be stopped. This makes me sick. What a cowardly display of violence. AM: Rise To Power we strike back, Dave! This is going to be the last time we have to suffer through that damned Law! DR: Let's all hope so... Goodnight from Cleveland! [Fade on Pizzazz and Monet dangling like broken puppets, and the cold expressions of the Hands of Death, laughing it up... Fade to black.]
