Finally, Sam Steeley, the leather jacketed Pride fanatic, appears to a fairly large ovation, startling even the never-at-a-loss-for-words Sam. The three walk to their broadcast position on the stage, to the left of the entrance, and sit down. The crowd continues to cheer, and Dave talks over them.] DR: Hello fans! We're in Greensboro for another exciting edition of Saturday Night Rampage, and- SS: And Scott Daniels is going to take the UWF Title from that pansy Alex Kidd! [The crowd, upon hearing Sam's prediction, lets loose with an earth shattering pop! Who could have possibly believed this a few weeks ago? SS: I don't care what these morons out here think, just as long as you all remember who's been on the Pride's side since they started here. _Me_! Tim begins to walk down the ramp towards the ring, and does so with barely the trace of a limp now, suggesting that his recovery from the car accident is progressing well. His damaged arm and shoulder is still in a sling, but other than that minor detail, Tim appears to be in fairly good shape.
