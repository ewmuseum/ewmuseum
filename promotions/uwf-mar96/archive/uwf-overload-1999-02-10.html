[Fade up to the UWF studios, as the music slowly fades down and out. Even though the studio is darkened, we can see that the set is completely different. As the lights slowly come up, gone is the basement feeling with the wood panelled backdrop, the leather couch and chair, and the coffee table. In it's place is a more modern look, with video monitors on both the left and right sides of the set, while in between is a section of gridwall with UWF merchandise hanging, including t-shirts featuring Genocide, the Pride, "Erotic" Erin McCoys and the Fabulous Ones. In the forefront of the set is a desk with the UWF logo placed right in the middle of the front of it. At the desk sits Moe Owens, wearing a nylon windbreaker with the UWF logo on the front.] MO: Hello everyone and welcome to an all new edition of UWF Overload! We're back and we've redecorated again, as you can tell. I'm Moe Owens, ready to bring you all the news, action highlights and gossip that is the Universal Wrestling Federation. And fans, standing by with a report live at the Memorial Center in Peterborough, Ontario is my co-host and everybody's pal, Becky Carlisle! [Cut to a split screen, as we can see Becky Carlisle on location, a packed crowd behind her. Becky's dressed in a blue miniskirt and white blouse combination, which shows cleavage as only Becky can. Her golden locks are tied up into a ponytail. She's not all that thrilled to be coming from a remote location, we gather.] BC: Hey Moe, how come I'm the one who had to take this assignment? [Moe shrugs.] MO: I'm the executive producer for Overload, I need to be here in the studio. BC: Why'd you get rid of my couch?!? MO: Demographics for males 16 to 40 skyrocketed, but everyone else seemed offended by... well, you sprawled out on this couch each and every week. This is the best solution. BC: [muttering] Wanna bet? [speaking clearly now] Well, alright, fine. Just as long as my dressing room and hot tub are intact. MO: [changing the subject] Hey Becky, how were the matches tonight? You've got the report on the newcomers. BC: Yes I do. [Silence.] MO: Care to share? BC: Maybe. MO: Well... maybe we'll get back to you later then... BC: Wha- [Becky's image dissapears, and a full studio shot of Moe is seen.] MO: Alright, let's take a look at what happened this past weekend on Rampage. [The videoscreens to each side of Moe begin to display a horizontally scrolling version of the Rampage logo. An image of "Wild Thing" Kevin Slater in mid-jacknife splash appears.] The show started off with a major bang as "Wild Thing" Kevin Slater showed up, revealing himself as the mystery superstar that was scheduled to compete. He went on to defeat Taurus quite easily, and made a splash in his first ever appearance. [Next an image of Youth Gone Wild being clotheslined by Edward Love as Elan Desire stands ready with a steel chair appears.] But in the main event, it was Heavyweight Champion Youth Gone Wild that ended up the target of a conspiracy as Edward Love turned his back on Wild, allowing "GQ" Elan Desire to gain the pinfall and advance in the Reed Cup tournament. Desire used a steel chair not only on Wild, but on his own partner "Badboy" Randy Acorn as well! Desire went on to challenge Acorn to a scaffold match, which we'll see this Saturday Night in one half of the main event. The other half of course pits Edward Love against Youth Gone Wild for the World Championship, as a result of the battle royal a few weeks ago at Brawl from the Mall 2. [Again the image changes, and we see Sam Willis arm in arm with "Erotic" Erin McCoys in one picture, while Scott Rogers nails Desire with a weapon in the other.] Elan Desire was all over Rampage this past week. Scott "The Fop" Rogers got a small measure of revenge over Desire for costing him the battle royal, with an attack backstage in the locker room. Rogers went on to lose to Daniel Kidd in a match with very shady circumstances, in fact all it took for Daniel to defeat the Fop was a one finger push to the mat. Rogers and Kidd have both claimed that it was not a fix, that it was in fact the brawl backstage that took everything out of Scott Rogers. Here's the man who gave the Fop his first loss, Daniel Kidd. [The outline of a face can be made out, dimly, in very poor light. A band of light, as if from a crack in an opening door, slowly crosses the screen, eventually illuminating the man's face for a moment before it passes on. The man is Daniel Kidd.] DK: They said I was a mystery. My allegiance uncertain. [Another flash of light, another glimpse of his face.] DK: As soon as I fought Scott Rogers, the rumours began. They said it was a fix, a sellout, a ruse. They assumed the worst. [This is a damn cool effect, and my words don't do it justice. Still, the band of light swings back one more time over Dan's face.] DK: But then you saw the clips, the attack from Elan Desire. You saw why the Fop flopped. You all saw. [In a near-blinding flash, the studio lights all turn on, to reveal a generic UWF backdrop in a generic promo filming room. Kidd stands, looking quite serious, with his hands on his hips.] DK: Now, there's light. You can see everything... most certainly, you can see that I am _NOT_ a member of Genocide. I am not a member of anything. I am my own man, and you would do well to remember it. [The camera slowly pans down to reveal Dan's t-shirt, one of those jobs with the iron-on lettering. It reads "Evil Ain't my Name". And certainly, we can trust the shirt, can't we? Fade back to the studio.] MO: Meanwhile Elan Desire proceeded to opt out of the main event, or so we thought. Randy Acorn faced Edward Love and Youth Gone Wild by himself, until Love and Desire brought their plan to light. Desire knocked Wild and Acorn both out and left them laying in the middle of the ring, but as his back was turned, Sam Willis proceeded to come out and steal his manager! "Erotic" Erin McCoys turned her back on Desire, who took a fireball to the face when he tried to stop the new duo's exit. [Now an image of "The Samurai" Kensei Tokugawa with the United States Championship belt appears.] News from Japan shocked the UWF, as Kensei Tokugawa defeated "The Natural" Kyle Backwood to become the U.S. Champion on the very first UWF Eurasia show. Kyle wasn't even scheduled, and made the impromptu challenge before eventually losing to the Samurai. Kensei is scheduled to put the title on the line this weekend in a return bout with Kyle Backwood, who's said to be very dissapointed with his performance and is already looking ahead to Rampage. Here are some comments from the former champion. [Cut. "The Natural" Kyle Backwood is seen coming out of the airport pulling a little cart, with his luggage on it. Backwood wearing black jeans, a Perry Ellis blue shirt, a black "diesel" ball cap, and black shades. Backwood seems to be very disappointed, walks out... and begins walking to the door, when a UWF camera crew charges up to Backwood, as he stops. Backwood takes off his sunglasses, folds them and puts it in his pocket. Backwood then looks at the camera, with a cold face, no emotion, no anything, and begins to talk.] KB: Nothing last forever, not a marriage, not happiness, and not a "Reign." I have the utmost respect for "The Samurai" Kensei Tokugawa the new United States Champion. He beat me clean, and for that I respect him. I'm not going to complain that I went into the match injured, or hurt or anything like that. Tokugawa, you had your shot, and jumped at the opportunity, you won _MY_ United States title. You know, you got the oppurunity to take my title and that's fine, you did, but in fact, I don't remember my shoulders being pinned to the mat, are my lips uttering the words "I quit." I never gave up, I never surrendered, so in fact, "The Reign" never died, just I don't have the title to prove that. I guess Joe Reed said it best, that title puts a _HUGE_ target on the champion, and I was the hunted. Kensei, rolls are switched, and you're the hunted. So round one ended controversially, just as round 2 will begin. Saturday Night Rampage, February 13th, 1999. 24 hours before lovers engage in epic tradition, we engage in bitter bloodshed. Two men enter, one title stays where it should be... and we knock each other from piller to post, from corner to corner... all over the damn arena. Kensei, it's nothing personal.. it's buisness, you understand that? I know you're an international superstar, and great... whatever, whatever makes you happy, and floats the boat is fine with me, but that isn't some Japaneese or Mexican title, that's the Universal Wrestling Federation United States title. You can go overseas, and be the legend you are, but St. Valentine's Eve... you're in _NORTH AMERICA_, it isn't Bangkok anymore Dorothy, we are not in Bangkok no more. So bring _YOUR_ belt, and bring your little wrestling tights and everything you need to step in the ring. Kensei, there is no place like home, and home is where the heart is... home is where I take what's rightfully mine, rightfully _BACK_. [Backwood is seen leaving the airport as we fade back to the studio.] MO: Kyle Backwood feels a profound sense of loss with the title changing hands, but he gets an opportunity to right the wrong this coming weekend. Also on the first ever UWF-E show, Tara "Sunburst" Marshall wrestled to a time limit draw with japanese sensation Mitzi Sukkora while "Crazy" Chris Hurt, the Darknight Express, Randall "Madman" Osbourne and "Agony" Michael Augustine were all successful. UWF-E will run two shows this week, one in Japan and the other in Korea. Look for a full report on Rampage. One of the wrestlers that will be overseas this week is Michael Augustine, who will return to the UWF after this week. Here he is. [The camera opens on a wrestling ring, and the thousands of screaming fans surrounding it. [Ryott starts to give a sly smile.] It looks like I did what no other UWF superstar, EMWC superstar, or AEWA superstar was able to do... I put out of commission the Lady in Red.
