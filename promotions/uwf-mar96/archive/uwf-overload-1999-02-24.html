What is Ted Bruce going to do after our match? We'll find out the answer soon enough. Could be that you and me go out and get a beer after the show, which you'll have to buy for me. [Jake grins.] Or... it could be that you'll say I never beat you. We'll find out after Overload. See you then, Ted. [The camera fades out.] ------------------------------------------------------------------------ Jake Sterling versus Ted Bruce ------------------------------------------------------------------------ Ted Bruce came out first to the ring, and was cheered mightily, proclaimed as a hero by the ring announcer. Jake Sterling followed, and was cheered just as much, more for a hard work ethic than anything else. What followed was a tremendous battle that saw many ups and downs, both men getting the advantage at various points throughout the contest. Sterling kept to his gameplan and wrestled a brilliant technical match, opting at one point to wait for Bruce to re-enter the ring after a backdrop over the top rope, rather than take things to the floor. Bruce turned things around after his patented "chainsaw chop", a tornado knife-edge chop off the ropes. Sterling was then worked over with Bruce's extreme style of wrestling, including various suplexes. Ted Bruce nailed a neck snap from behind on Sterling and then worked the crowd by motioning like he was revving up a chainsaw, which seemed to get them pumped. He pulled Sterling to his feet, threw him to the ropes and ducked his head. Big mistake, as Sterling made him pay with backhand punch that rattled Bruce, then a kneelift into a swinging neckbreaker. Again they traded advantages, and after Bruce attempted an inverted front layout suplex, Jake out of nowhere got him with the Sterling Driver, and scored the victory. Both men were given a standing ovation after a brilliant match, and shaked hands afterwards. Winner: Jake Sterling MO: A thrilling encounter between two of the newest and brightest stars to hit the Universal Wrestling Federation, Ted Bruce and Jake Sterling have long and illustrious careers ahead of them I'm sure. BC: Sportsmanship, bah. What's it ever gotten anyone? MO: I can't answer that, but it makes for quite a segue to this next man. Scott "Hotspot" Daniels. [The camera cuts to the inner offices of the UWF Towers in downtown, Toronto. It is a board room, with a giant desk in the middle of it, complete with comfy chairs and pitchers of water. One man sits alone in the room... He is Hotspot. Scott Daniels is wearing a Pride eye chart t-shirt. Scott looks pretty bored, as he runs his hands through his slick brown hair, of course, tied back in it's usual fashion. Around the room, some of the UWF's greatest moments are plastered up on the wall in the form of comemorative photos. Such as: A shot of the original 90,000 in the Pontiac Silverdome for UWF's original Gold Rush... A shot of the ring being held in the air by four ziplines, with Scott Daniels, Dan Kidd, Shawn Vexton and Joey Valachi all sitting in the ring, smiling... "Crimson" Joe Reed verbally lashing out at the UWF, with trash being thrown at him... Serge Annis dressed up in a giant black chicken costume, complete with the short lived KFC title around his waist, after losing a stipulation match to Youth Gone Wild... Serge Annis chasing down Brett Greene, and into the women's washroom(back when it was a big deal)... Paul Reed posing for a picture, dressed in his tuxedo from the '98 Uni's, standing proud with his family... And an assortment of others. There is a large clock directly behind Daniels' head, and it shows 2:15. Daniels looks at his watch, as he seems to be getting a little more visibly annoyed by someone's apparent lateness. Hotspot mumbles out loud...] SD: Oh, come on... he said to be here at two, sharp... son of a... Geez... [The camera fades away... ...and fades back in on the same shot... Scott is still sitting, although he's now leaning back in his chair, with his feet up on the desk. He's looking back at the clock, albeit it is upside down for him. He slowly bends his head back down and shrugs his shoulders...] SD: Stupid piece of... they said they'd be here.... God damnit... Ugh... [Fade away... ...Fade back in... The clock now says 3:35... Scott Daniels still sits at the table. His greasy hair is now loose, as he plays with the hair band. He's humming the theme to Hawaii Five-O out loud... He looks back at the door.] SD: Do do do dooooo.... Where are they? I'm Scott F'Ing Daniels... I deserve much better than this... [Scott looks to the camera and awknowledges it's existence. He pours himself a glass of water from the pitcher.] SD: Look at this! Warm pitcher water? Damnit, I deserve at least Perrier or Evian... Anything instead of this tap water... Geez, it tastes like Lake Ontario... [Scott Daniels leans back in his chair, as the camera fades, yet again... ...fade back in at 4:23... Scott Daniels is standing in front of one of the pictures... he scratches the rear end of his blue jeans, as he looks on... Fade out... ...fade back... the clock is now at 4:55... Scott is sitting back at his chair, and is busy scratching the word "HOTSPOT" into the wooden oak with his keys... He grins as he crosses the final T. Just then, the door swings open, and in walks a secretary... Scott quickly leans forward to cover the vandalism, and then pulls papers over it.] Secretary: I'm sorry sir, you'll have to wait just a few more minutes... Mr. Beeby is still in a board meeting. SD: Board meeting?!? What about me? I've been in here over three damn hours waiting for that fool! Sec: I'm sorry sir, but your patience is appreciated... So please, just sit still a little longer, and I'm sure Mr. Beeby will be right along shortly... SD: You go back there and tell him that I'm Scott Daniels. I've got places to be, people to see, toasters to sell. I don't have time to sit around and waist my afternoon, trying to fit into his schedule, and all because I was asked to be here. I don't even know why I'm here! Sec: Well sir, just keep waiting... [The secretary turns to leave...] SD: And least get me some decent water. And a chocolate bar would be sweet too. [The secretary rolls her eyes and shuts the doors... Scott leans back in his chair, and uncovers the papers, grinning at his dirty work... Fade away... ...fade back in... The clock is now 5:26, and Scott is busy twidling his thums, grumbling to himself...] SD: ...who the hell does he think he is... no one keeps Scott Daniels waiting... [Fade away... ...fade back in. The clock is now at quarter to six... Scott lays, slumped over in his chair, his head collapsed on his arms, and is fast asleep. The doors in the background open, and in walks two men. The first, is UWF President Michael Beeby, clad in a three piece suit. Beside him stands the UWF's legal man, Mr. Crosby. Crosby drops a breifcase on the table, creating a loud thud which quickly awakens Hotspot. Daniels rubs his eyes as Beeby and Crosby sit down.] MB: Thank you for joining us here today Scott... SD: What the... I've been waiting in here for four goddamned hours! MB: Sure sure... down to business... SD: Good, because I have a few things I want to clear up with you. MB: Oh really? Such as? SD: First things first. You know it, I know it, and any one with half a brain, excluding the AEWA fans, know that I was robbed of the UWF World Title several weeks ago... And I want to know what you intend to do about it? MB: Well Scott, you lost the match via pinfall, so there's not much the championship comittee can do for you in terms of contendership. All I can say is to get back in the title hunt, and maybe earn another shot in a few months... SD: I've bene here three damn years, I think I've earned a title shot! MB: But you weren't active all three years. SD: Sheesh... Okay, second thing. Let's take Gold Rush. I can't help but notice, you haven't talked to me about anything. I haven't had any contracts passed on to me. In fact, not a single phone call, no communication, no opponent, no match. What's the deal? MB: Well Scott, we just haven't been able to find an opponent... of your stature... and you can be rest assured that the championship comittee are doing their best to find you some competition. And when we do, we'll let you know. Now... on to business... SD: You mean that wasn't business? MB: Mr. Crosby...? [Mr. Crosby stands up and readjusts his glasses. He opens up his briefcase and pulls out some forms, and hands them to Hotspot.] Mr. Crosby: These, sir, are lawsuits... Over seventeen of them to be exact. All stemming for your little "scene" last week where you infringed on over ten different "characters". Well, it turns out some of them are suing you, and in some cases, twice. You're in hot water Mr. Daniels. [Daniels shrugs and passes the papers back to Mr. Crosby.] SD: Hey, what do I care? You're the one that has to sort this stuff out, not me. It's your job, not mine. MB: True Scott, but since you are the leader of Pride, and are held personally liable for the Pride's actions, all of the blame is placed on you. Therefore, as of this moment Scott, Mr. Crosby here shall be taking a dividend off of each of your paycheques untill these lawsuits are paid for and settled. SD: But that could take... MB: Months? I know. Perhaps next time, you'll know better. [Mr. Crosby closes his briefcase after putting the papers back. Mike Beeby rises.] MB: And so on that note Scott, please reconsider things ahead of time, before you run your mouth off again and jeopardize the credibility and the good name of the UWF. That will be all, have a good day. [Beeby and Crosby turn and exit the room. Scott Daniels just looks on with wide eyes, in shock over what he just heard.] SD: ...I waited.... four hours... for -THAT-?!? Jeopardize the promotion?!? What the hell is he on?! [Scott gathers his stuff and rises.] SD: All right Beeby... we'll play it your way. You want to play Mr. Responsibility... then Hotspot will match you. [Scott leaves the room... but before he closes the door, he turns and sees the camera. He has a smug look on his face.] SD: Hmph. Good day, indeed. [Fade to black, as Scott closes the door. Fade back to the studio.] BC: You tell 'em Scott. Give him the gears! MO: Loyalty, thy name is Becky. Jack Banks met the Watchguard in a battle of the behemoths, and how the ring held up under all the weight I'll never know. ------------------------------------------------------------------------ Jack Banks versus The Watchguard ------------------------------------------------------------------------ Jack Banks was the picture of determination in his approach to the ring, his eyes never leaving his opponent. The Watchguard, a massive man in his own right, stood ready and waiting until the bell rang. A flurry of punches from Banks opened it up, but the Watchguard stood his ground, and didn't back down at all until an errant shot to the stomach doubled the Watchguard up. Banks rocked him backwards with a clothesline, but then on a second attempt ran into a sidewalk slam. The bodyguard of Miss Miki rolled back to his feet, and again they traded punches. Banks got the advantage after a while, and hit the Watchguard with a spear tackle into the corner. It didn't take him down, but off kilter enough for an assault on the right leg of the bigger man, and Banks draped it over the rope to expose the knee. Banks exited the ring, and somehow it escaped the referee that he picked up a chair and ground it into the knee of the Watchguard, softening it right up. Later on, the Watchguard managed to turn the tide and military pressed Banks into a hotshot along the top rope, causing Banks to choke and gasp. The crowd favorite, the Watchguard executed a huge powerbomb in the center of the ring, but it couldn't keep Banks down. They continued to brawl, and the match went into it's tenth minute the same way the match had began, with a fistfight. Shortly after, the referee had been knocked down. A man of honour, the Watchguard went to check on the downed official, leading to the spear from Banks that finally took him off his feet. Banks dragged the referee over effortlessly, draped an arm across, and scored the pin. Winner: Jack Banks BC: Congratulations Watchguard, you just got _JACKED_. MO: Seriously though, another great match from two up and coming stars, the Watchguard was only defeated after going to check on the referee and let his guard down. BC: When will people learn those zebra shirts are a dime a dozen? You take one out, another will come along. MO: I don't know whether to pity you or be frightened. But now we have a chance to listen to one of the fiercest legends in the history of the UWF, "The Epitome of Evil" Serge Annis. [Fade. The camera shows the UWF logo in a basic UWF interview area. No one stands in front of it... but the voice of "The Epitome of Evil" Serge Annis is heard.] SA: Too many questions surround the birth of the Genocide... Not enough answers are out there for the people... And it is those people that question myself and Scott Rogers... But who are they to question? Are these not the same people that questioned Genesis in the past? Not everything is face value... Deep down inside, Genocide has an inner mask... and only those that embrace the darkness... my darkness... can see that. And for everyone else... They just stand, watching... waiting for Serge Annis to strike... [The camera starts to turn around... slowly...] SA: But they will be dissapointed to find Serge Annis is not there... Maybe Scott Rogers is... but Serge Annis is not... [The camera does a complete 180 degree turn... in the darkness, and several meters away from the camera, the Epitome sits in his ring attire, sitting on a giant production box for equipment... He is well concealed in the dark however.] SA: But "The Epitome" has always been there... watching... waiting... As I said, those that look right past the shadows... the darkness... look past Serge Annis. And it is when they make that cardinal mistake... that's when they'll pay. For Scott Rogers and Serge Annis certainly are a team... And tonight, Langford and Conley will defend the UWF World Tag Team Championship against us... Now, I'll admit that tag team wrestling is not my forte'... but when you combine the destructive power and nature of Genocide... nothing can stop us. Twister... Prepare to be casualties of a great war... because when it's over, and the lights are out... Genocide are bringing those tag team belts back with them... [Fade to black.] ------------------------------------------------------------------------ World Tag Team Title Match: Twister versus Genocide ------------------------------------------------------------------------ Annis and Rogers came out to without a doubt the loudest heel pop of the night. The team of Conley and Langford had a tough going of it in the opening of the match, as they were pounced upon before the opening bell. Annis threw the larger Conley over the top rope early and split him up from Langford for most of the match. The referee wasn't able to keep control over everyone, so he stuck to the two inside the ring, Scott "The Fop" Rogers and "Tornado" Paul Langford. Paul was able to get the better of the Fop with a technical barrage with a little aerial mixed in, and could have ended the match if it weren't for a poke of the eyes and a low blow by Rogers during a sleeperhold. About seven minutes into the match, Annis and Conley finally got seperated, and climbed onto their respective corners. Conley took the hot tag and came after Rogers with a vengeance, but in typical Genocide fashion, Rogers turned the tide and brought in the Epitome of Evil. Serge unleashed a volley of forearm shots to the face of Travis Conley, setting him up for a wicked lariat by the Fop from the apron. Paul Langford used a little of Genocide's tactics against them, coming in and attacking from behind with the referee's back turned. A dropkick to the knee chopped Serge down, and this allowed Conley to tag out. Now in legally, Langford slapped on a texas cloverleaf and held Serge down for a minute and a half, but Annis dragged himself to the Genocide corner, opponent and all. The Fop chopped Langford down from behind after the tag, then caught him with the Prunes and Prisms inverted bearhug. This was broken up with a lariat by Travis Conley, but Serge took care of Conley with another throw over the top rope, then along with Rogers executed a double chokeslam on the "Tornado", then scored the pinfall for the victory, and shocked the world by claiming the World Tag Team Championship! Winners: And new World Tag Team Champions, Genocide! MO: A major upset for sure, but when you think about the history of Scott Rogers and Serge Annis, maybe it's not. BC: Two highly regarded singles wrestlers, and now tag team champions. MO: Whatever it is that binds Annis and Rogers together, it works for them. They're polar opposites in terms of personality, but each has pure evil coursing through their veins. BC: Which brings us to the main event. MO: What? How do you mean? BC: That Kyle Backwood has to be one sick son of a [bleep]. Talk about evil. Nobody can be that nice. MO: Maybe no-one _YOU_ date. ------------------------------------------------------------------------ United States Title Match: "The Reign" Kyle Backwood versus "Sweet" Shawn Vexton ------------------------------------------------------------------------ Seeing as how this was Canada, Kyle Backwood was treated to another tremendous welcome by the crowd. But Shawn Vexton had at least the respect of the audience, getting a large reception of his own. This one degenerated into a wild brawl early, Backwood taking things to the floor and Vexton keeping them there. The champion clocked the challenger with the belt, and the challenger returned the favor a litle later by ringing his head off the post with the timekeeper's bell. The blood was flowing freely as well, and during this match the new tag team champions, Scott Rogers and Serge Annis, came out to take a closer look at Backwood for themselves, scouting their opponents in a week's time for the Reed Cup. They didn't get involved though, despite a lot of crowd noise directed at them. Backwood took over close to the end by escaping the Pride & Pain and turning it into a torture rack backbreaker, then allowed Shawn to recover just enough to get in position for the Sweet Delivery. He nailed it, and "Sweet" Shawn went down for the 1-2-3. Winner: And still U.S. Champion, "The Reign" Kyle Backwood. MO: What a way to cap off the evening of action, Kyle Backwood retaining the title over a hardnosed Shawn Vexton. BC: His nose isn't the only thing hard about him. MO: Oh come on, we're almost at the end of the show for crying out loud! BC: What? I meant his bicep! MO: On that sentiment, we're out of time. Goodnight everybody! [Fade.]
