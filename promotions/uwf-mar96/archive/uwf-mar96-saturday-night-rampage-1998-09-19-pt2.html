The lights go out completely as once again a pale light and a grey mist form on the entrance way. As the mist disappears, the Reformer is left standing there as the lights come up and the crowd gives him a heel pop. He has a mic in his hand and begins to talk.] R: Yo punk, hey Kidd, I am talkin to you! [Kidd looks around, until he realizes where the Reformer is.] R: Yah, thats right, look over here you bumbling idiot. So I see that you wanted to talk trash about me last week during Rampage huh? You were in a little interview and decided to talk about me. You could have left it alone. But no, you gotta say that the Legion actually did a good thing to not let me into their precious group. Well buddy, you want a piece of this action, you come get me, freak. AM: He's still going after Alex Kidd and Legion for their comments last week! R: 'Cause you know that I won't back away from no little piece of trash in this federation. You call me washed up and what not and you just don't know me. You keep your mouth shut for so long, then decide that turning on your people will be a good thing. What were you thinking fool. You had it made. The fans, the chicks, Legion. You come out here and criticize how things are being done around here with Joe Reed and Serge Annis. Well I didn't see you stepping up to the plate to take a swing. I however was the man that got up to take that swing. I took them on. But where were you? DR: He speaks a little truth in there. AM: As much as Alex Kidd ticks me off lately, I can't help disagreeing with the Reformer. He had it made with the chicks? He's married for god sakes! DR: This match has ground to a halt ladies and gentlemen. R: Well you come back and complain and then you get yourself a championship match. Well hot damn, ain't you special? You win the belt and you think you are the cream of the crop. Well you ain't [bleep]! If anybody is washed up buddy, it's you, Mr. Wonderkid. I can put some Wonderbread in the ring that would wrestle better then you. If you want some of this, you don't need to sing it, you can just bring it punk. I am waitin for you! Come on Kidd, come get me. [Alex, completely having forgotten about Brett Greene, looks at the Reformer with hatred, bewilderment and disgust. Almost as if unsure what he should do, Alex finally exits the ring to a chorus of boos, and heads up the ramp for the Reformer. As the seven footer glares at Kidd and prepares for him, the grey mist forms again and the lights go out. When they come back up, the Refomer is gone, and Alex is heading towards nothing. Realizing he's about to be counted out he sprints back to the ring, and is caught with a knee to the face by Brett Greene, who then suplexes Alex over the ropes back into the ring. Alex rolls onto his stomach and then gets to his knees, but Brett is there and starts to feed him a series of punches to the face, then grabs him by the head and snapmares him over into a chinlock. Alex struggles, gets to his knees again, and jumps up, snaps Greene down in a jawbreaker and then crawls to the corner.] DR: What a shocking appearance by the Reformer, and now the match has gone back in favour of the champion! [Alex Kidd hits a swinging neckbreaker on Brett Greene just as Greene was getting up, and goes for a cover. Greene escapes, and Alex pounds the mat in frustration. Alex goes to the corner and starts to taunt Greene, which gets the big man irate. Greene gets up and Alex comes at him with a dropkick, but Greene grabs Alex by the legs and slams him down to the mat with incredible force. Alex lies on the mat in pain, and Brett looks at the crowd, then motions for the Brett Bomb. The audience explodes with a round of cheers, and Brett lifts Alex up into position.] DR: This is it, it's the Brett Bo- No! No! No! Alex Kidd counters in midair with a rana, and Brett Greene is on the mat! Did you see the force his head hit with? AM: Alex covers! One! Two! Three! NO! Jim Junas says Brett kicked out! He kicked out! What intestinal fortitude by the challenger! [Alex, now shocked as well as frustrated, grabs Brett by the hair and drags him on the mat over to the corner. He climbs to the second turnbuckle and executes a flying legdrop onto the back of Brett's neck, then turns him over and makes another cover. Again Brett escapes, this time putting his leg on the ropes. The crowd raises the roof with a loud cheering session, and Alex grabs Brett by the hair and the tights, pulling him back onto his feet and leans the former champion in the corner. Alex walks halfway across the ring and runs in with a Stinger splash, but Brett dives out of the way and Alex knocks himself senseless. Brett, half in desperation and half sheer instinct, hits Alex Kidd with a deadly enzuigiri kick to the back of his head.] DR: He flattened the champion! COVER HIM! AM: Brett has Alex in a scorpion deathlock! Alex Kidd is in the center of the ring! No escape! No escape! DR: It's over! All he has to do is wait for Alex Kidd to either pass out or submit! AM: I don't think you'll ever see him submit. [Alex, in a terrible position, fights through the pain, the exhaustion and the heat to get his arms underneath himself as Brett wrenches right back on his legs. He manages to push up slightly and tuck underneath, rolling right through on Brett, until Brett's shoulders are on the mat and Alex is sitting on the mat. Kidd struggles to his feet and drives a knee into Brett's back, then leans back into a surfboard. However he can't hang on, and Brett escapes. Alex comes right back with a forearm smash, and pulls him into the corner. He hooks on another full nelson, but climbs to the second rope as he does. The crowd gasps as he comes off with a bulldog, as Brett remains hooked in the full nelson!] SS: God damn what a move! DR: That has to be it. One! Two! Three! It's over! [The acoustic version to "Morning Glory" begins again, to loud heel pops from the audience. Alex rises slowly to his feet as he's handed the championship belt, while Brett Greene remains on the mat, hurting from the full nelson bulldog. Alex climbs to the turnbuckles, holding the belt up for the crowd.] DR: Fans, we're out of time! We'll see you next week on Rampage, goodnight everybody! [Fade to black.]
