[Fade up from black to grainy images of two years ago, the main event of the

Serge manages to grab Wild and haul him up into a chokeslam on a set of steps, and then hurls Wild to the floor, making the cover for the win.
Another burst of light shows images recently of both men, from Gold Rush 1998. Serge grabs Joe Reed and lifts him above his head, and Wild executes the
Wildflyer onto Steve Ryott. The screen fades to black, and in plain white letters "1998 ... Tonight ... Wild vs. Annis 3!". Fade to the opening graphics.]
