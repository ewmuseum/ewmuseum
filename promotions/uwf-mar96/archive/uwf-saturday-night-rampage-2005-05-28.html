[The camera switches to an exterior view of the Staples Center. Specifically, the parking lot and surrounding areas. The thunderous roar of an engine, along with screeching tires and loud honks from other drivers, can be heard from far off in the distance. All of a sudden, a cherry red 1965 Mustang convertable speeds its way into the lot and skids into the only parking spot remaining, one right next to the building and clearly marked for a disabled individual to use. The "Undefeated Shining Star of UWF," the one and only Trey DaMann, jumps out of his seat (no, his seatbelt was not on) and slides down the front hood in his pinstriped black and gold Armani suit with a huge smile on his face. His 6'5", 265 pound frame easily lands on its feet as he immediately looks in the air and snaps the fingers on his right hand. Just as quickly, five men in dark suits run into the frame and begin working. Two use black spraypaint to cover the blue lines on the ground, while two others remove any traces of the "Handicapped" insignia. The last man rapidly screws a large sign into the nearby wall, one that clearly reads "Parking for the New UWF World Heavyweight Champion." Trey beams as he looks at the wall, nodding to the workers for another job well done. He clicks the car alarm and proceeds into the arena. Trey begins to address the camera filming him, while not looking directly in it.] TD: Thank you for being on-time to record history... and also thank you for not bringing one of those annoying backstage interviewers with you. Unless they're an unbelievable piece of eye candy, they're utterly useless and I really have no use for them at all. [Trey displays a shameless grin.] TD: Oh, but back to the history part. For those of you who watched Mayhem, or whatever that show was called...and of course you did because your undefeated shining star of UWF was on it, you know that tonight will be the night that I, Trey DaMann, finally capture my long-sought after UWF World Heavyweight Championship. [Trey begins to speak in a hurried/excited manner.] TD: Let me reiterate. I, Trey DaMann, am from sunny and beautiful Southern California. The Staples Center is in my backyard and what better storybook ending could be written than the hometown hero, the greatest wrestler in anyone's lifetime, and the man who has never been defeated in his 20-year life, finally bringing home the biggest prize in the business, the UWF World Heavyweight championship. [Trey flashes his huge and trademarked Trillion Dollar Trey DaMann Smile!!! upon hearing what he had just said.
