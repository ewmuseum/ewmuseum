Kidd drags the referee over next to Jericho, and he's covering again! One! Two! Three! That's it! [Tom Davis stands up and announces the decision.] TD: Ladies and gentlemen, here is your winner and _NEW_ UWF Heavyweight Champion of the World... For the record third time, "THE BRITISH WOOOONDERKID" AAAAAAAAAALEEEEEEEEEEEEX KIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIDD! [The crowd lets loose a mighty heel pop as Kidd rolls off of Jericho, who hasn't moved since the Bulldog. Alex gets up and rips the championship belt out of the hands of an official, and hoists it over his head. Referee Simpson meanwhile tries to revive Jericho, but appears to be having troubles. At ringside, Youth Gone Wild is back up and looks at the ring with concern. Forgetting all about Kidd and the title, Wild heads over to the side of the ring where the now former champion lays in a crumpled heap. He too attempts to revive Jericho, but again Jericho lies motionless. The emergency medical technician team comes down the ramp with a rolling stretcher, and Alex Kidd looks back at Jericho. He exits the ring but with a very concerned and distraught look on his face, and he backs up the ramp, the belt hanging at his side.] DR: [somber] Fans, this looks bad, very bad. We've gotten word that Sebastian Jericho is in fact conscious, but seems to be temporarily paralyzed from the neck down. It looked like he suffered a stinger from the Bulldog, and as you all know a neck injury is very serious and should be taken care of very gently. AM: LOOK OUT! [Amy's referring to the entranceway, as Kensei Tokugawa barrels out of the locker rooms and down the ramp with his kendo stick firmly in his hands. He nails Alex Kidd right in the back of the head with it, and drops the new champion like a sack of potatoes. Tokugawa heads to ringside and nails Youth Gone Wild with the stick as well, putting Wild facedown and nearly unconscious. Tokugawa walks back up the ramp and picks up the world title belt, as Kidd and Wild remain on the ground. Tokugawa says something to the crowd, and the nearest camera picks it up.] KT: Boku no kobashi, omae no kao! AM: What does that mean? SS: It's japanese for "My fist, your face." AM: I thought I recognized it. DR: Kensei Tokugawa has struck out of nowhere, but we're out of time fans, but please stay tuned to UWF television for the very latest news on Sebastian Jericho's condition, as well as that of Max Robertson who earlier this evening suffered a possibly career threatening injury as well. Good evening from Hawaii, we'll be seeing you. [Cut to a long shot on the ring as Sebastian is still taken care of by the EMTs, while Kensei Tokugawa stands over Alex Kidd and Youth Gone Wild both with his kendo stick. Fade to black.]
