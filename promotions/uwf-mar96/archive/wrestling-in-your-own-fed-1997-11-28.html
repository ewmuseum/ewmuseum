wrestling in your own fed

28 November 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Depends on how it's done. I run the UWF, and at the moment I've got two tag teams and two singles wrestlers of my own entered. As the handlers who have been with the UWF from the beginning will tell you, I very rarely push my own characters. One belongs to the major face stable, which means on occasion he goes along for the ride in tag, six man or eight man matches. Another was picked up by a new stable and really is rp'ed now by another handler, and as for the two tag teams, one has a losing record and the other is always around, but never in the thick of things. The only reason they're currently at the top of the rankings is they've been around since the beginning, and the ratings system is weighted towards quantity of wins and quantity of matches. Of course there was that one world title reign, but I'm not the one that came up with it, it was the handler of the champion at the time who wanted to get rid of the character. Any time characters of my own have been champions, it acted more as an in-between for other champions, and these reigns have never been longer than a month, tops. At least I hope none of the handers mind me having these guys around...
