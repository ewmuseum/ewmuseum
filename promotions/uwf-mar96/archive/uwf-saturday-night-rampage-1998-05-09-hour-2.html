As he frees himself, Reed connects on a Doctor Bomb to put him on the mat, and tries for a pinfall. Alex interrupts the count once more and grabs Reed by the hair, setting up for a T-Bone suplex. The champion climbs to the top rope and perches again, readying himself for another attempt at a Somersault Bulldog.]
DR: Here comes Alex, he looks to be in control as Diamond slowly gets up. BULLDOG! He only used a regular version of the move, but it's just as effective as Black Diamond is out like a light.
SS: Kick out dammit! AM: Joe Reed breaks what was a sure pinfall, and this crowd is going crazy! They want Joe as champion, and nobody else will do. [Alex Kidd gets up and executes a swinging neckbreaker on "Crimson" Joe Reed, then executes a falling headbutt on Black Diamond as he stands over the unconscious form of him before simply falling onto him. As he does, Reed exits the ring and climbs right to the top rope, waits for the champion, and...]
DR: SOMERSAULT BULLDOG BY JOE REED!?!?!?! SS: Damn that wasn't pretty. AM: Joe Reed just took a page out of Alex Kidd's book and used the award winning finisher against him! Joe wasn't too pretty in his execution, but he landed it with all of the force he needed. Alex looks like he's in trouble now.
[After the Somersault Bulldog, the crowd explodes with a huge wave of applause. Reed gets to his feet and raises his arms, tired, and grabs Diamond by the head to pull him up. Diamond slowly rises, and Reed delivers a forearm shot before trying the Backsmasher again. Diamond blocks it and guts Reed on the top rope, then as he leans half in the ring and half out, Diamond grabs Reed and positions him for a springy version of the Snakebite. Just as he goes to deliver it, the crowd explodes with a series of heel pops as the Unitron comes to life.]
DR: What's going on? Why is everybody looking this way? SS: [big smile] They must be looking at me, I am afterall a Uni winner. AM: Wrong! SS: What? AM: Look behind you, on the jumbotron. [On the Unitron are the members of Sweet Madness, "Sweet" Sho Satsuma and Randall "Madman" Osbourne. All that can be seen is some trees in the background and the upper-body of both men. Sho has his black hair pulled back in a pony- tail with a lock of hair hanging in front of both ears.
He's also sporting a shirt that reads "I bought some toasters and all I got were the UWF World Tag Titles!". Randall stands next to him with a the same shirt on, looking back over his shoulder.] SSS: Well, hello Sour Saviors! How's your waist feel without the titles? [Scott "Hotspot" Daniels and "Sweet" Shawn Vexton look up at the Unitron with murder in their eyes as the wrestlers in the ring come to a stop, and cautiously look up as well. Alex seems to be enjoying what he's watching.]
SSS: Thought that you might want these back? [Sho and Randall both hold up the titles in the air. The crowd gives a mixed pop as the Sweet Saviors start yelling at the jumbotron.]
SSS: [Looking over at Randall] We stole these things from you Tuesday night, then started thinking, what a bunch of bakayaro's we were. [Looking remorseful] So, we figured that we should be the nice guys and give the belts back.
[Scott and Shawn start waving at the Unitron to bring it on. Suddenly, they're cut off by Sho.]
SSS: However, before you can get these back, we have a little present for you. [Suddenly, under the Unitron, coming out from behind the curtains is a Federal
Express man. He's carrying a box that takes both of his rams to carry.]
SSS: Great, there he is, on time. [The FedEx man gets to ringside and hands the box to a now angry Shawn Vexton. All the while in the ring, Alex is laughing up a storm. Shawn and Scott cautiously look over the box.]
SSS: I promise you on my Japanese honor that it's not an exploding box. It really is a present from Sweet Madness. Go ahead, open it. [Shawn yells up at the Unitron: "[bleep] YOU!"] SSS: Hey, wait a minute. If you don't open your gift, you'll never see these belts again. [Shawn, against his better judgement, ever so cautiously starts to peel off the tape to the top. The crowd patiently waits to see what's in the box.]
SS: No! Shawn, don't do it! AM: Do it Shawn! Do it! SS: What's wrong with you? You know it's a trick. AM: That's exactly why I want them to open it. [Shawn gets the tape off without the box opening up. Then, Scott pulls Shawn back and says something to him. Then both men peel the mat up from around the ring, and lay it partially over the box as not to have anything hit them should something shoot out.]
SSS: Good lord, you act like you can't trust us. [Scott quickly flings open the box top and dodges behind the mat. Nothing happens. Slowly Scott and Shawn sift through the packing foam. The first thing they pull out is two pairs of heavy chain link duty pants. Both Sweet Savior members look bewildered.]
SSS: Go on, continue. SS: What in the world is that for? AM: Nothing. I can't believe this, absolutely nothing. SS: What do you expect from the Daimyo? They don't have a brain to share between the eight of them.
[By this time both Scott and Shawn have pulled out two pair of heavy duty jackets, gloves, and two whips. All the clothes are made of chain link.]
SS: What the hell's with the whips? DR: All I know is they've ground this match to a halt and the crowd is getting restless. SSS: Go on guys, there's one last thing. It's at the bottom of the box. [Angrily, Shawn throws the clothing on the floor and flips the Unitron the bird.] SSS: I'll never understand Pride mentality. You give them a present and they turn on you. [Scott leans over and quickly punches his hand to the bottom of the box. He pauses, now with a strange expression on his face, then pulls his hand out. The crowd roars to life as they see Scott's hand.] SS: What happened?! His hand is covered in blood! [Though Sam and the crowd are all riled up, Scott and Shawn remain calm. Shawn then puts his hands in.] SS: NO! [When Shawn pulls his hands out, he's holding a few slabs of bloody raw meat.] SS: What? SSS: I'm sure you're asking yourselves, what does that mean? Well, Randall and
I thought that since you liken yourselves to a pack of lions, it would fit your style if we treated you like one.
[Shawn slams the bloody meat down, and starts yelling at the Unitron again. Then, the scene on the jumbotron pulls back to reveal Sho and Randall holding the belts on a man made rock ledge over looking a bunch of trees and a fence in the distance. Past the fence people can be seen looking on.]
SSS: You see here. We're at the local zoo. [The cameraman, walks past the duo and looks over the edge. About ten feet down five lions lying around in the grass beneath the trees can be seen.]
SSS: [Yelling at the lions] Here you go Pridie, Pridie, Pridie! [The lions notice the two wrestlers and the cameraman on the ledge and stand up looking at them. One starts to roar as the cameraman pulls back to show Randall and Sho.]
SSS: Be sure an use your gift to get these back. [On que the two members of Sweet Madness pitch the belts over the ledge down into the lion's den. They start laughing as the jumbotron goes black.]
SS: What the hell did they do that for? They chucked the belts in a lions den at the zoo! AM: [chuckling] That was pretty good. SS: Shut up! [Scott and Shawn are livid at ringside and immediately head up the rampway towards the back. In the ring and huge smile can be seen on Alex's face.
However with the Saviors gone and everyone else distracted in the match, "Crimson" Joe Reed leaps into attack, spinning Alex Kidd around and grabbing him for the Kentucky Backsmasher, and delivers it to tremendous cheers. He hooks the leg for the one...]
SS: Diamond, get him! [Two...] AM: He's got it! [Three!] DR: This crowd has errupted as "Crimson" Joe Reed captures the United States
Heavyweight Championship!
[The roar of the crowd drowns out Reed's music, as Chalmers throws a fit and
Diamond picks himself up off the arena floor. They leave immediately, Diamond throwing the Cruiserweight title over his shoulder. As he walks up the ramp,
Luis Garavello suddenly charges out at him and catches Diamond with a dropkick in the face, further added punch with the downward angle he goes on. Diamond rolls to the bottom of the ramp, and Luis escapes through the crowd to a burst of cheers.]
SS: What was that about? AM: Luis must be fed up with that walking stiff holding a belt he doesn't deserve and legally shouldn't have.
[Diamond is helped up by RC3, but is groggy as he stumbles backstage. In the ring, Alex sits in the corner in stunned disbelief, while Joe Reed has his arm raised by referee Ingraham and is presented the title. He lifts it up over his head, and the noise dies slightly to hear the official result.]
TD: Here is your winner, and NEW UNITED STATES HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION... "CRIIIIIMSON" JOOOOOOE REEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEED! [Reed puts the belt around his waist and raises his arm again, then turns to shake the hand of the former champion, but by this time Alex has already left the ring. He walks up the ramp without so much as one word, his head hung low while the crowd celebrates the new champion.]
DR: What a turn of events this evening! We'll see you later fans! [Fade down on "Crimson" Joe Reed, the new champion in the ring.]
