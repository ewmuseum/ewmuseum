[The scene fades backstage to the dressing room of Leanna Love. Leanna wears a white, tank top and blue, track pants, her blonde hair styled in a messy ponytail. [The smile on Lolita's face is immediately replaced by a gasp and a look of horror.] Lolita: Nina Grimsson!?! I didn't know she baked. Leanna: Me neither but she's been doing it to keep busy, while she gets herself well. [smiles] And let me tell you, the girl is an excellent cook. I've never tasted a cake quite as good as hers before. Hell, I think she could even give you a run in the cooking department.

In fact, you should really look into getting the recipe. I'll bet that Nina would even help you so that it turned out as good as the one that she made for me.
Lolita: [smiling tightly] That's...nice. Leanna: I think I have a piece left over, if you really want to try it. Lolita: [grumbles under her breath] Like hell I would. Leanna: What? Lolita: [covering quickly with a smile] Nothing. [Leanna begins to look through her nearby sports bag.] Leanna: I'm sure that it's in here somewhere. I put it with the rest of my stuff. I think... Leanna: Found it! [Leanna pulls out a brown, paper bag and reaches into it, producing a piece of aluminum foil-wrapped cake.]
Lolita: [with zero enthusiasm] Yay. [Leanna hands the cake to her sister.] Leanna: Go on, taste it. Lolita: [with a wan smile] Um...maybe later. [wanting to hear about anything other than chocolate cake] Anyway, how about that match with
Bliss tonight?
Leanna: I'm expecting a tough fight. Bliss is good. She's a former women's champion and one of the top wrestlers in the women's division.
So, I'm really looking to put my all into this. A victory over her could do nothing but help catapult me in the singles ranks, not that I'm forgetting our tag goals.
Lolita: [smiles] Of course not. Leanna: I'm still itching to get a shot at the women's tag titles. Not to mention getting the Coven in the ring again too.
Lolita: Yeah, we both owe them for what they did to us. Leanna: And Nina too. Lolita: [pauses] Yeah. Right. [under her breath, sarcastically] Can't forget everyone's favorite wrestler and chef.
Leanna: Regardless, the Coven is the last thing I should be thinking about tonight. Bliss is too good for me not to give her my full attention. She's just the type of wrestler to hand me a royal ass kicking, if she caught me sleeping. I should know. She and I are both martial artists and I know full well how she's going to be approaching this.
I think that's one of the reasons that I respect her so much. It takes a certain type of person to excel in the martial arts. And if Bliss can do it, that says a lot about her as a competitor and a person. I just hope that the rumors are true and that she and Marshall are no longer conducting themselves as they used to. I heard stories about how bad The
Bod Squad
used to be. Lolita: I don't think you've got anything to worry about there. Tara
Marshall is a very good friend and an old stablemate. I trust her. Plus, from what I've seen, I don't think either she or Bliss is anything like they used to be. They really have turned over a new leaf.
Leanna: I hope so because, if Marshall does show up, I have no qualms about beating her ass, friend of yours or not. I'm not letting anything stand in the way of me performing to my best, in this match.
Lolita: You'll do well. Trust me. Bliss may be this tough martial artist but you can beat her.
Leanna: [smiles] Thanks for the vote of confidence. Anyway, I had better get going. I promised that I would cut a quick promo before the match.
Lolita: Well, I'll see you later then and good luck tonight. Leanna: Thanks again. [As Leanna leaves the room, Lolita looks down at the box of cookies in one hand and the cake in the other. She lifts the cake, glaring at it.]
Lolita: Nina, you could never top me, with your Easy Bake Oven goods! Never! [Lolita sniffs and tosses the cake in the trash bin before stalking off. Fade into the opening of the show, as Aerosmith's "Just Push Play" rolls over the graphics.]
