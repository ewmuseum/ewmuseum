UWF Tuesday Night Thrashings Recap, 23 July 1997

23 July 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Part One] [Fade up to clips from the end of Saturday Night Rampage this past weekend while 'Misled' by Celine Dion plays. Legion file into the ring, and then are jumped by both Black Diamond and the masked man he was supposed to face. A Snakebite to each is shown in slow motion, while Ashley Wilder's voice starts a voiceover.] AW: Saturday will be remembered for many things, but the most shocking of all was the turning of Black Diamond against his friends in Legion. Just earlier that night, Alex Kidd fell for a trap laid by Brett Greene, who seemingly offered his and the rest of the Pride's hands in a peace offering to take out the AEWA. [Clips from the interview with the two stable headliners play, and another slow motion starts as Alex is jumped by Greene.] No sooner had Alex agreed to take it up with his allies, Brett viciously attacked from behind and put Alex down for a moment, insulted his enemy once more and left him in the ring. Alex was not stunned for long, and vowed not to be such an easy target. [Cut back to the intense look on Black Diamond's face, which freezes onscreen.] The man supposed to be his foe for the evening turned quickly into an ally of Big D., who left Legion laid out in the middle of the ring, the second time of the night for Alex Kidd. [Clips of Julio Sanchez roll, including his face to face encounter with Vladimir Karensky.] As well, Julio Sanchez also took a step towards betraying his fans as he reluctantly agreed to a partnership with the russian wrestler with an attitude against capitalism. [Cut to a short clip of Kenny Carroll locked up in the ropes with Marc Stevens] Tonight the Grapplers of Politics were on the schedule, and one had to wonder if Julio and Vlad would also be near the Eiffel Tower. And in the main event, Tim Vexton and Scott Daniels battled over the United States Championship, and you won't believe the finish! Stay tuned, the update is next. [As 'Shades of Grey' by Billy Joel starts, fast clips of the UWF's roster fly by, three at a time. Marc Stevens catching someone in the D.O.A. full nelson, Stephan LeRoc taking out an opponent with the Pounding Headache, and Blaze performing a spectacular moonsault. Then the screen peels away to reveal more images, of the Balanced Budget from the Grapplers of Politics, the Clothesline Cradle from the Twin Ninjas, and a Cathedral DDT by Bishop. More images replace the old ones, and eventually we are left with images of the Somersault Bulldog, Pride Bomb, and Kentucky Backsmasher all at once. The music fades out as a camera picks up a small, lingering crowd from the UWF event held earlier this evening. They still hold up some signs, and the one centered out is a picture of Joe Reed being trampled on by the UWF logo, and the caption "Eat your Hart out, Joey." A couple more passes over the crowd, and then we come to a desk located just at the base of the Eiffel Tower, and just outside the wrestling ring. Ashley Wilder sits there alone, obviously without Dave Rogers after the attack in Spain by the Epitome of Evil. She's wearing a stunning red crop top and has a baseball cap that says "Can't Get Any Wilder than this!". She smiles for the camera, and begins.] AW: Hello fans, welcome to the Wild Zone. Or at least that's what I'm pushing for. Until a name change, it's actually Tuesday Night Thrashings. As was explained on Rampage this weekend, due to both a scheduling difficulty and a huge timezone gap, the card was held earlier and we are presenting a recap of the action. What a night it was too. Talk about surprises. Oh well, let's get right to it. The card opened with some words for Joe Reed, who arrived in style. Who'd have guessed the hick would know a fashionable entrance. [Cut to footage subtitled "Earlier Tonight"] AW: I've been notified that someone has a message... [Just then, the makeshift jumbotron comes to life, and the screen shows the view of Paris from inside the top of the Eiffel Tower. The camera pans to the left, and the crowd boos as "Crimson" Joe Reed and the Lady in Red appear. Joe has both belts over his shoulder.] LiR: This is a great view. Then again, the view is always good when you're on top. Like Joe here in the UWF. CJR: [Joe heads over to the edge, and waves below] And everyone down below looks like a bunch of ants. Again, just like the rest of the UWF. I see that Serge finally realized what I've been saying all along; that the UWF is a second-class fed that treats the most talented wrestlers with the least amount of respect. Meanwhile, talentless bums like the Pride keep running the fed, and the spineless executives let them. Listen, I spent twelve years getting to this point in my career. I've wrestled more matches, suffered more pains, and spilled more blood than anyone else in this godforsaken fed. Someday, someone's going to come along and take these belts {Holds up the UWF and AEWA titles} from me. But it's going to take a great man to do it. LiR: Bishop? At one time you might have, but not now. Alex Kidd? Not until he grows up. The Pride? Don't make us laugh. CJR: And as long as the UWF keeps pushing those losers over the really talented wrestlers, I'm going to keep this title. At least the AEWA gives the title shots to the wrestlers who actually challenge me, men like Mister Crowley and "Superman" Mike Stewart. Just one more difference between the best [holds up the AEWA belt] and the pretenders [holds up the UWF title]. [The crowd boos, and we return to Ashley.] AW: Like I said, he knew how to make a first impression. With that so-called 'Lady' in Red, I'm wondering just which one of those two it is that has the fashion sense, if you catch my drift. Reed's got a match with Bishop at Midsummer Massacre, but apparently some stipulations have been added to the match. The president I'm being told will actually be here live at the end of the show to talk about the pay per view. Paul Reed, Alex Kidd is in town, so watch yourself on the drive over here. Anyways, Bishop's got the shot at the champion, and tonight his stablemate in the Fraternity of the Stone took on Serge Annis. Redeemer went up against the Epitome of Evil, and both spoke before the match. [Cut to a shot of Serge Annis making an entrance to the ring and snarling at the french fans, while in one corner a message recorded by Redeemer rolls.] Redeemer: Annis, my friend, tonight I take a break from the Fraternity's wars to indulge myself. I have always known that mine would be the hands to annihilate you. This is not Bishop's battle I fight. Throughout the WCeW I wanted to crush your throat, but that would not have been fair, now would it? Now...I fight by my own rules...and I have no rules...except one. Serge Annis... YOUR FATE IS CARVED IN STONE! [The corner shot fades, and the camera pans up from the ring, up up and further up the Eiffel Tower. The camera then switches to a camera already at the top where Moe Owens stands with 'The Epitome of Evil', Serge Annis. This interview is shown on the video screen for the fans down below.] MO: "Fans, a reluctant Moe Owens standing by with Serge Annis. After what you di--" SA: [interrupting] "What the hell do you mean... reluctant?" MO: "Well, given your history with announcers and interviewers, not too many people wish to be assigned an interview with you." SA: "Oh well. Who needs you then?" [Annis pushes Moe away, and Moe backs up, and being ever so graceful as always, he nearly falls over the end of the Eiffel Tower. Moe, after regaining his balance, quickly exits the scene. Annis holds the mic and calmly looks into the camera.] SA: "Many people ask me am I sorry for what I did to Dave Rogers? Hell no I'm not, for I have no reason to be sorry. He had the audacidy to say that I was out of line... well, if he's going to make comments like that, then to hell with him and his opinion, for he did not respect mine. It is plain and simple: The old Serge Annis is back! You can screw that Lethal Protector crap... because that is not who I am. That is the UWF trying to capitalize on the name of Serge Annis... and now I refuse to allow that, unless it is *MY* given and promised UWF World Title shot! But no... Bishop receives it. Well, Bishop is now in my way again. What do I do? Hmm... well, first of all I take out his hench men. And then... it's just like the old nursery rhyme: Humpty Dumpty sat on a wall... Humpty Dumpty had a great fall.... and all the king's hench men, and all the king's hench-horses won't be able to put their pieces back together again. Bishop, I guarantee you this: You have your UWF World title shot to worry about... *BUT* I'm going to see to it that you go through hell to get that title. Why? BECAUSE YOU DON'T DESERVE to have it! I do... and you'll pay dearly for that... and tonight, it will start with your good friend, Redeemer." [Annis walks out of the camera's shot and the camera man pans down, over the ledge and zooms down for an over head view of the ring.] ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Redeemer versus Serge Annis ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Cut to footage of the match between Serge and Redeemer, and Serge has the Fraternity member in a headlock. Redeemer throws him into the ropes and suffers a shoulderblock, but remains standing. Serge does it a second time, and then a third time. Still the big man from the Frat stays on his feet, though he wobbles. Annis climbs to the top rope and comes off with a flying clothesline, but Redeemer ducks and Serge hits the mat hard. The crowd cheers as Redeemer kneedrops Serge and then drags him into the middle of the ring, executes a legdrop and covers Annis. A kickout from Serge gets the crowd in a lather, chanting for Redeemer to put him away. He pulls Serge up by the face and goes for his version of the chokeslam, but Serge grabs Redeemer likewise and the two behemoths stand in the middle of the ring, each with a hand wrapped around the other's throat. As they stood there trying to outwill the other, Chronosphere took out the referee with a flying spinkick from the top rope, and Marc Stevens grabbed Redeemer and spun him around, out of the chokehold. While this was going on, a third man slipped in, grabbed Serge and DDT'ed him. It was Error Macro, the shortlived Asylumite that only lasted a match in the UWF, and after the Descrambler DDT he held up two fingers, in conjunction with Godzilla Blanchard giving the sign for one. The three men escaped unharmed, and after a short time Redeemer left upon hearing the match declared a no contest. Serge was out a little longer, but woke up and was unhappy to say the least, vowing revenge. WINNER: No contest due to interference [Cut back to Ashley] AW: And another Asylumite of the past comes back and gives Serge a lump on the noggin'. What are they up to? Not even god knows, he's afraid like the rest of us. Afterall, give a group of inbreds knives, and you won't cross them. Same deal for Asylum. Speaking of them, they were present later on at the card, as Stevens and Chronosphere were on hand for an interview with good ol' Moe Evans. [Ashley gets corrected by the director] Uh, yeah. Owens, Moe Owens. [trailing off] Like I care what his name is... [Cut to the ring as 'The Great Southern Trendkill' by Pantera starts up, and the Asylum walks to the ring, now being Marc Stevens and Chronosphere with Crystal Cutglass. Stevens is wearing a Metallica t-shirt, and black jeans, as Chronosphere is in his black "Asylum 4 Life" tights, and a black Pantera t-shirt. Crystal is wearing a blue bikini top, covered by a plain shirt, and short blue jean shorts. They enter the ring to a huge heel pop, and grab the microphone from the ring announcer.] MS: Well, Well, Well... [crowd gives another heel pop]... You all thought that the Asylum has went away... But we didn't... We just lost a great member. You all know who I'm talking about... [Crowd chants "Nah-Nah-Nah-Nah. Hey Hey Hey. GoodBye.", which is tough to do in french accents]... Shut the F$#@ up!!! We're trying to pay tribute to the Asylum's greatest member ever, and you @$$&%*? fans gives us $%!@ about it. [Crowd boo's)... Now,as I was saying before I was interrupted by you stupid F$#@'s... The Asylum lost their greastest member, Psychosis... But don't you fret... 'cause we got a new member coming soon... but he won't be the new leader of the Asylum... [Stevens chuckles]... Guess who is... [Crowd Boo's]... That's right, I am you F$#@'s!!! But right now, I wanna deal with two subjects... Stephen LeRoc... [Crowd cheers wildly for the frenchman]... You piece of $%!@!!! You stole my Cruiserweight Title from me... Now, I'm gonna come and take it back from you sooner or later... but until then... protect ya neck!!! [Crowd boo's, and Stevens gives them all the finger.] MS: Next... What's with this Anti-UWF $%!@??? You don't like the UWF, get the F$#@ out of here. Joe Reed, Greg Smith, and all you other losers, the Asylum could take you losers. Or should I say... the NEW Asylum. ['The Great Southern Trendkill' by Pantera starts back up, and the Asylum, or rather, the New Asylum goes backstage to an incredibly loud heel pop. Cut back to Ashley Wilder at the interview desk.] AW: Okay, so I was wrong. Mosey wasn't in there, so what? I don't have to be right all the time, I'm too damn cute. They faced the Grapplers of Politics a short time later, and before we go to the end of that match, let's see what the political posterboys had to say. [The original G.O.P. is sitting on a bench outside the American Embassy in Paris. Outside, a small protest is going on, as approximately a dozen Frenchmen carry placards decrying the U.S. for some reason or another. They appear depressed.] RM: Focus, huh? Easier said then done. KC: It's one thing to disagree... to fight, even. But to see Julio embrace that misguided jackal Karensky... EA: Tonight, we can't show Julio the error of his ways. But we can take out some frustration on Stevens and Chronosphere. Bad night to be you guys. Very bad. RM: It wasn't our intent, but the best team in the history of the UWF is together again. KC: And we're not in an accommodating mood. [The GOP go back to watching the protest, and we can hear a muted comment questioning the wisdom of the Marshall Plan as the camera fades. We fade up on later later in the card, and both teams are standing in the ring, for the match.] ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Marc Stevens/Chronosphere versus The Grapplers of Politics ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Early in the match Chrono and Stevens had Reggie Martin and Kenny Carroll reeling with great doubleteaming manoevers, including a spectacular double flying fistdrop and a backdrop attempt by Chronosphere that delivered Reggie right into position for a powerbomb by Marc Stevens. Reggie kicked out, came back with the support of the crowd(republicans in France?), and rocked both with dropkicks, then tagged in Carroll who bodyslammed Marc and then along with Reggie put Chrono down with a double clothesline over the top rope to the floor. Stevens was prepared and nailed with the Balanced Budget, but as Carroll went for the pin it was broken by the GOPer, at the sight of Vladimir Karensky and Julio Sanchez standing in the makeshift aisle. As the Executive Aide screamed for the pair to concentrate on the match and not the uninvited guests, Stevens snuck up behind Carroll and locked in the DOA full nelson, which was perfect to hold him in place for a dropkick by Chronosphere from the top rope. He scored a pinfall on Carrolll as Stevens threw Martin out of the ring to the floor, where Vlad wasted no time to pounce and starting beating on Reggie, the 'Right-Wing' GOP'er. Kenny took to the air in saving his partner and tackled Karensky with a plancha over the top rope. Julio till this point hadn't moved in position to attack his former comrades, but when Karensky went down he grabbed Kenny by the head and rang him into the ringpost. As the crowd booed for the international pair, Vlad and Julio put the Grapplers of Politics down with a spike piledriver inside the ring on Carroll, and then Julio executed the Cuban Leglock on Reggie before exiting. Julio seemed to have a look of regret on his face, but Vlad hustled him away before he might have acted upon it. WINNER: Marc Stevens and Chronosphere The Asylumites stayed in the ring to celebrate a little too long, and Serge Annis came out wielding a chair and grabs a mic. after the carnage is done and bodies are layed about the ring. Annis: "THAT... is for you Asylumites... why? Because of one simple reason: I will take you out time and time again." [Annis points to the ref, referring to why he didn’t attack until after the match] "THAT... is so I wouldn't disqualify you idiots... I got rid of your leader Psychosis, yet you all claim that one shall rise from Psychosis' ashes so to speak, and destroy Serge Annis. Well listen up Asylum... YOU CAN NOT TAKE ME OUT! It's proven! Why don't you all stop riding on the name of Serge Annis, and make a name for yourselves? Oh... wait. That's because you can't. WELL I DON'T CARE!!! I'VE HAD IT WITH YOUR PATHETIC RUN INS. Don't you morons realize... it's over?" [Annis leans over and shouts into Chronosphere's ear.] Annis: "IT IS OVER! I WON. NOW GO HOME." [Serge stands up] "Go anywhere, just as long as it is far from me. You have no more business with Serge Annis... I am cutting off all connections with you." [Annis bends down and shouts into Stevens' ear.] Annis: "YOU LOST. IT IS OVER." [Annis rises again] "You can tell your Godzilla that when you awake. You can tell your Error Macro when you awake. I'm sure they'll get the idea. I got nothing left to gain from Asylum, why? Because I got rid of Psychosis for*EVER*. I have nothing left to prove to you idiots, because after a year you should have realized that it is a pathetic cause." [Serge stands up on the second rope and looks down at all the bodies that lay in the ring, then Annis 'accidentaly' drops the mic on Chronosphere's head and leaves the ring with an evil grin on. The french crowd release a heel pop, despite Annis threatening to hit some fans. Cut to Ashley, who holds up a piece of paper.] AW: Those american 'patriots' got carted off by the french medical team and were released after being treated for slight concussions and other such minor things. On the plus side, I was there and got one of the attendees to give me his phone number. Ah, the french, so romantic... uh, anyways, things weren't civil between the two sides before, and they're downright explosive now. It seems they’re heading to a match at Midsummer Massacre, but hey, I don’t want to step on the president’s toes and give everything away. And of course, Serge Annis took care of the Asylum, at least for now. Another of the hot topics around the UWF water cooler is the situation with the Maloney clan. You have Neal, the new Unified Champion after defeating some reject from the ACWF. Then there’s Brendan, his family member and backup inside the ring, and of course Brian Maloney, the financial guru that led to their commercialization. You have to respect someone who can earn money like that. But then the black sheep of the family, Kelly Maloney, who used to be world champion incidentally, tried to take the Cruiserweight Championship away from Stephan LeRoc on Saturday. Neal stopped that from happening, and now poor Kelly’s cryin’ the blues. Stephan LeRoc, fan favorite suckup that he is, sent a note to the offices for Neal that said “Neal, all I can say is thank you! I don't know your reasons for helping me but if you ever need help I will remember that I owe you one. Steph.”. Well, oh wonderful champion, don’t bother to strain your brain over this one. He wasn’t helping you, he was stopping his cousing from winning. What a tool. Neal was in action against Chris Scottsdale tonight, so let’s hear from the hopeful challenger before we show you the match. [Cut to a shot of Chris Scottsdale standing in the middle of the local Paris market. As the local people walk by him unaware of who he is(ah, pro wrestling, unassuming worldwide), he speaks into the camera to deliver a message to Neal Maloney.] CS: Apparently the good people that run the UWF and the ACWF noticed that I have been working as hard as ever in order to be one of the best wrestlers today. Otherwise, why else would I get the shot at Neal Maloney? In the past few months, I have had my chances at the United States belt, but I never came away with the title. Now, this time there will be three men to contend with in the match. I am positive that somewhere along the line Brian and Brendan will make some attempt to interfere. Don't think that for one minute I will let you prevent me from winning the match. I know how you operate. I've seen the tapes, and I know my history. So, when I step into the ring with Neal, the three of them will wish they were in Scottsdale, Arizona, instead of in Europe. Losing to me. [Fade out to the ring, and the Maloney’s heated entrance to the squared circle. Even in this country, a sellout is still considered a sellout. Or in this case, three sellouts. Before the match starts, Brian takes the microphone and tells Chris that if he had bothered to read all of the contract he’d realize that he wasn’t facing Neal tonight, but Brendan. Not only that, he goes on to say, but IF he beat Brendan, then he’d get a Unified Title shot. Scottsdale is upset and complains to the referee, but the footage cuts forward to the start of the match.] ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Neal Maloney versus Chris Scottsdale ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Brendan controls the opening minutes of the match simply by the element of surprise, setting the pace with a couple of flying elbowdrops onto the downed Scottsdale. However, the former Cruiserweight Champion comes back and rallys with a reversal of a cross bodypress into a pin attempt, and from there Scottsdale manages to land a couple of kicks to the midsection and then a fallaway slam. He climbs to the top rope, but as Brian distracts the referee as was expected, Brendan gets back into the match and climbs to the top, meeting and stunning Chris with a shot between the eyes and then a belly to back superplex. Brendan covers, but to the shocked delight of the crowd Chris still manages a kickout and rolls into a neutral corner. While he is there he takes time to recuperate somewhat, but Brendan comes right at him with a stinger splash. Chris ducks, then hooks his arm around Brendan’s neck and a leg around his thigh, drops him face first to the mat and rolls him into a cradle. Brendan escapes at a two count with a reversal into a pin on Chris, just as Kelly Maloney appears suddenly at ringside. The referee is unable to see Kelly push the cradle over, and Chris holds on this time for the win. It’s too late before the other Maloney’s can do anything to stop it, and Kelly runs away to the locker room, smiling and laughing at evening the score with his family members. Scottsdale celebrates with the fans at the shot at the Unified title, though he had been hoping for the belt itself, and the rest of the Maloneys argue with the referee, who didn’t see anything and refuses to change the decision. WINNER: Chris Scottsdale. [Cut back to Ashley, who’s sitting at the desk reading a newspaper. She looks up and sees the director flailing his arms in vain, trying to tell her she’s on.] AW: What? Neal however better watch his back, or he could suffer a loss the same as Brendan this weekend. But he’ll have lost more than a match, he’ll have lost his gold. At least Joe Reed’s promised that the ‘Iceman’ will get a world title match, and that’s going to happen this Saturday night. And another man who’s been given the nod at a title match by Reed is Blaze, who coincidentally happened to be in action tonight. Against none other than Tommy Boy Landis, another Legion loser. Blaze lands on the ring apron and tries to execute a springboard dropkick to Landis, but loses his balance and goes downward, crashing into the ‘Iceman’. He lands on top of Greg Smith, drawing a huge pop from the crowd. Blaze gets up quickly and re-enters the ring, but Smith gets up and looks mighty pissed off. As Landis bounces off the ropes he grabs his leg and Tom goes to the mat face first, and is a victim of a sharp DDT from Blaze. Blaze gets up and asks him what the hell he thinks he’s doing, and is knocked flat out cold with a roundhouse right. Landis is barely able to cover, and the referee counts Blaze down. Before anything else happens, Smith enters the ring with a chair and blasts each man with it, laying them both out. The man identified as Ace Malone(so far at least) runs out to a huge boo and tries to save Blaze, but he too takes a shot with the chair followed by Icepick powerbombs to all three men. Smith exits the ring and grabs the microphone, then delivers the following epitaph to his and Blaze’s friendship. Iceman: "Arghhh! Geez Blaze, what the hell is with you? From now on Blaze, you and that idiot, Ace Malone can just stay the hell out of my business, from now on! And Landis, don’t think for a moment that we’re through with, Tommy boy.” [Iceman shouts as he knocks over the cameraman inside the ring with his palm, and we come back to a live shot of Ashley Wilder at the broadcast desk.] AW: Greg Smith decides to go it basically alone in his quest for UWF gold. Good for him, he doesn’t need anyone. Not Blaze, not Scott Daniels, not even Randy Acorn from what I’ve been hearing backstage. Oh well, save that for another show. [The crowd gives another mixed reaction.] SD: "You were in AEWA. You were a champ. Wow. Doesn't say much at all. You were in UWF once... and you left. You left after a very, *VERY* brief time. Why? Because you couldn't cut it. And now your back, and you want admittance into the Pride? Well, first of all I have to tell you admmitance into the Pride is *C-L-O-S-E-D*. You know what that means? You are not in Pride my friend!" [Crowd gives a large, heated mixed reaction and Daniels grins.] SD: "The only members of Pride are myself, Brett, Shawn and Dan. I'm sure your asking what about the Twinja's and Valachi? Well, they are not Pride. But they deservedly consider themselves Pride Associates, because that is exactly what they are. Pride Associates. But you Kyle Hatcher, after *TWO* matches in UWF, want admittance into the Pride. We caught the clip of you calling yourself the next Pridesman... well we got news for you. You ain't even *CLOSE* to Pride!!!" AW: [doing a voiceover live while the footage from earlier continues to play.] At this point, the crowd seemed to be divided. Half hated Daniels, the other hated Daniels for not admitting Hatcher. Try and figure out these french fans. SD: "Who says we can trust you Hatcher? You've been here a week and expect admittance into the BEST stable in wrestling? AFTER competing in AEWA? Forget it! You’re dumber than I thought! We had a saying a while ago Hatcher... 'Don't come to us, we'll come to you.' And that was only if you were good enough! Check your answering machine pal, and see that it's blank. Why? Because you are not good enough to be Pride. Can we really trust you? Or is it just another AEWA pathetic trick? Prove yourself in _REAL_ wrestling competition, UWF or ACWF and we will see if you are even good enough to be considered an associate. Until then Hatcher, all we have to say is this... don't call us. We will call you. And if you keep this up Hatcher, you won't be called at all." [Scott walks down right to the ring, all the while blowing kisses to his french 'fans'. Cut to mere minutes later, and Tim Vexton coming out to a mixed pop. He stares at Daniels and the U.S. title, which he still considers his. Cut to the first few moments of the match.] ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Scott ‘Hotspot’ Daniels versus Tim Vexton ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Daniels grabs Vexton by the tights and hammers his back with a forearm, then takes him over with a waistlock suplex. He gets back up and goes to the top rope, then comes down with a fistdrop. Vexton rolls out of the way and lets ‘Hotspot’ crash and burn, then covers him with a quick attempt. Daniels kicks out and is kicked in the ribs, then Vexton pulls him up by the hair and tries to DDT him right away. Daniels drops to his knees first and crawls to the corner, but the Enforcer follows and continues to hammer with fist after fist. Daniels is in heap, and Vexton stands over him and tells Scott that he wants ‘his’ belt back. Daniels responds with a kick to the groin, and then stands up to execute a waistlock takedown and then a legdrop off the ropes. Another cover, but Tim kicks out again and starts to get the crowd behind him, although he didn’t ask for it. They start to chant ‘Enforcer’, and he looks out surprised at them. It also distracts him long enough for Daniels to execute the Pride Breaker, and then he takes Vexton and dumps him out of the ring where he is almost counted out. He just barely returns in time and Scott tries to suplex him back into the ring, but he lands standing behind him and rolls him up with a reverse cradle. Daniels kicks out and is taken out of the ring with a clothesline, where Scott lands with a thud. He looks around and whistles, which is a signal for the rest of the Pride to come out. Daniel Kidd, Shawn Vexton, and Brett Greene emerge from different spots in the crowd, and stand next to ‘Hotspot’ as Tim Vexton looks on in the ring. Each goes to a different side of the ring, and start to climb into the ring. AW: [Another live voiceover over the footage] And just as Tim Vexton seemed to be outnumbered, look what happens. Just as Vexton tries to fend off Daniel Kidd with a forearm to the head, Alex Kidd, Tom Landis and Youth Gone Wild rush down the aisle. Alex grabs Brett, YGW likewise with Shawn Vexton, and Landis latches onto Danny Boy. Hotspot is suplexed into the ring while the others start to pound the Pridesmen outside the ring, and the crowd goes crazy at the apparent reunification of the original men in Legion. Greene, Kidd and Shawn Vexton grab Daniels from inside the ring and take off for the hills, while the British Wonderkid and his teammates join Vexton inside the ring. They all hi five each other as the crowd roars with delight, and then the footage fades on the sight of the four men, while ‘Morning Glory’ blares. We now see Ashley Wilder still sitting at the desk but joined by UWF President Paul Reed. AW: I promised him, and I delivered. Paul Reed is here with news on Midsummer Massacre. PR: Thank you Ashley, and hello to all the fans watching wordwide. I hope you are enjoying this format for Tuesday Night Thrashings, which by the way will remain like this for the rest of the summer, due to conflicts on the originating networks. Anyways, I do have news concerning this month’s pay per view, Midsummer Massacre. As mentioned previously it will originate from Mexico City, at the Azteca Stadium. The main event has already been announced as Joe Reed versus Bishop for the World Title, but now the match has the added stipulation of being a steel cage ladder match. A ladder will be placed inside the ring, and to win one of the two will have to climb up and retrieve the belt, then escape the cage. Whoever exits the cage with the title will be crowned winner and UWF World Champion. In another match, Sebastian Jericho will face Damien Nightfall in a dog collar match. It is obvious that neither man is willing to stand by the rules in a regular match, so a dog collar match is somehow appropriate after the debacle of last Saturday night. And the Grapplers of Politics will face Julio Sanchez and Vladimir Karensky in a tag team match. Despite my better judgement to hope that Julio and the GOP will find a way to remain allies at least, I have been compelled to sign this match. As it stands it will be a regular tag match, but as always, things could change and this could wind up a different style all together. And finally, Kelly Maloney has asked that he and two partners of his choice meet his brothers Brian and Brendan, and cousin Neal. No word yet on who the teammates of Kelly’s will be, but we will keep you updated if anyone steps forward. There will be more matches added, and as they are signed we will announce them. Thank you fans, and Ashley, a commendable job on stepping in and doing this show solo tonight. AW: Thanks Paulie, I aim to please, in every way imaginable. Like he said, more matches to announce later, but we’re done here tonight. Thanks go out to the french officials who made this event possible, and tune in Saturday night for Rampage, as Joe Reed defends against a man who deserves the title, Greg ‘Iceman’ Smith. Goodnight, and sweet dreams. [Fade out]
