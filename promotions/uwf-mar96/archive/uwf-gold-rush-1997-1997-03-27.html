Greene, in a daze, attemped to slip Alex into a sleeperhold but the champion turned it into a wicked neck snap on the ropes, after using his legs to slip out onto the apron. Greene fell back grabbing his throat, and Alex started after the other members of the Pride. Landis and the others restrained him, and Alex re-entered the ring to a hair pull by Greene. Greene pulled Alex back over the ropes by the hair until only his legs were balanced across the top rope, and then let go of him. Alex hit the mat headfirst, knocking him out. Greene dragged Alex into the middle of the ring and executed the modified camel clutch on Alex(for those who haven't seen it before, it's a camel clutch in an almost upright position). Alex was unconscious, so the referee signalled for the bell, awarding the first fall to Brett Greene at 15:00 past. [A mighty heel pop] AM "Oh no, Greene's ahead on falls. Come on Alex!" Greene wouldn't release the hold, and the referee laid a count on him to release. He did so, only at the last possible moment, and then executed an inverted atomic drop on the troubled champion. Alex got into a rhythm of chopping Greene and executing spin kicks, alternating between the two. Greene came out staggering, but then leapt at Kidd and DDT'ed him to the mat. Greene then wiped the sweat off his brow and flung it at Legion, causing a stir. Wild and Landis tried climbing into the ring, but Black Diamond(who'd a figured him to be the coolheaded one) held the two back as he knew it would cost Alex a fall. Greene got his shoulder up and was victimized with a flying frankensteiner from the top rope. Brett Greene went for the pin and got a two count before Alex put his feet on the ropes, then stomped a mudhole in Alex. He raised his arms in a sign of superiority, and front slammed Alex. Then he executed the one move that would have done anyone in, the PrideBomb. A hook of the legs and Greene had the edge in pins, 3 to 2. The fans tried desperately to get Alex back in the match, and a chant of "Wonderkid" seemed to do the trick. Greene hoisted Alex up in a torture rack backbreaker, but Alex was close to the turnbuckles and hooked his legs around the top one. He pulled Greene over and bulldogged him, sending Greene into blackness. Alex pulled Greene over to the middle of the ring and added insult to injury by executing the same modified camel clutch on the master of it. Greene woke up somewhat and got onto his feet, but Alex put him out again with a stunning Somersault Bulldog off the apron! He pushed Greene back into the ring and covered Greene, getting an easy three count. At this point, Alex came off the ropes and was tripped by Hotspot.
