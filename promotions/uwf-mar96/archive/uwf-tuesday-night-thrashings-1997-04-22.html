Smith then dropped the Reformer with a kick to the midsection and a Russian legsweep, and ran into the ropes. Another one count from Smith and then a second spinebuster slam left the Reformer in between consciousness and unconsciousness. Smith got right in the Reformer's face and proceeded to taunt him, then got up and stood on the middle rope, all the while calling for the Deep Freeze. Instead he used a pair of Doctor Bombs on the Reformer, then again covered him fully expecting the pin. Instead the Reformer kicked out, more energized than ever and causing the Iceman to retreat to the floor for the time being. The Reformer obliged him by climbing out too, and Smith went for a kick to the thigh. The Reformer caught the leg with his hand, spun Smith around and executed an atomic drop. The Reformer attempted a backspin DDT onto the concrete, but Smith escaped it and threw the Reformer into the ringpost, then caught him with an enzuilariato as he staggered away from it. Smith rolled the Reformer back into the ring and tried a front-layout suplex, but the Reformer blocked it, then blocked a Boston crab after being tripped. He grabbed Smith by the throat and raised to his feet, then a swinging neckbreaker put Smith on his back. The Reformer used surprising agility with a dropkick to the knee, but Smith sat up albeit groggily. At the urging of Mad Dog, the Reformer went to work on the Iceman with a football tackle into a backspin DDT, then pulled him up and irish whipped him for a clothesline. Smith also suffered a jawbreaker but amazed the crowd by putting the Reformer in a quick power bomb. He covered the Reformer once more but had it reversed, and Smith was rolled up for two. Greg Smith kicked out, and as he did Bishop emerged from the locker rooms, chair in hand. Mad Dog didn't notice him, and neither did the Reformer. Bishop came out and slammed the chair across Mad Dog's back, then grabbed the Chalice out of his unconscious hands. The Reformer meanwhile saw Bishop and tried to grab the chair out of his hands, but Bishop held on. A struggle ensued, and eventually Bishop let go of the chair, causing the Reformer to take the chair in the forehead. Meanwhile the Iceman had made sure the referee saw none of this, and after Bishop reclaimed the chair and had left the ringside area, Smith gave the sign for the Icepick Powerbomb.
